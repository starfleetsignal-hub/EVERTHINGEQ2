## Notes

- These are an important part of the strategy for defeating [[Crusader of Rok Nilok]].  They come in a group of 6, and each of the 6 drops a [[Ghostly Channeler's Focus]], a charm that can be used from your inventory.  This charm is needed for dispelling/preventing a buff that the Crusader casts on itself.
