## Notes

- ![](images/Pinch.jpg)Grotto feasters start combat by casting Pinch, a curse that debuffs casting speed and inflicts major crushing damage every 5 seconds. Be ready to interrupt it (it has a slow casting time) or cure it. Aside from Pinch, these things are relatively weak.
