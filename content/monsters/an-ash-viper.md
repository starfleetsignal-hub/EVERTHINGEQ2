## Location

- Levels 21-23 can be found in the south, along both sides of the [[Torrent River]] near the entrance to [[The Commonlands]]. One such location is {{waypoint -573, 14, -80}}.
- Levels 24-25 can be found north-east of [[Hidden Beach Path]].
