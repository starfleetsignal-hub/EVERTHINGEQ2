## Notes

- Possible races are Dark Elf, Half Elf, Human, Kerran, Ratonga.
- Mechanically speaking, there are *three* different kinds of Darkblade scouts...
  - First, the kind in the netherot chanter room (6 - 7 assassins)
  - Second, the kind wandering around the middle of the main Darkblade room (always lvl 7 human assassins)
  - Third, the kind in the main Darkblade room in linked groups with other Darkblades (7vv-9even assassins)
