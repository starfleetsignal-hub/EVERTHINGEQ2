## Notes

- Serilian nobles can be multiple races, with class depending on race
  - Bugbears and Kobolds are Berserkers
  - Fungusmen are Defilers
  - Goblins are Warlocks
- If you stay out of range and wait, you can eavesdrop on the conversations the Serilian nobles have with each other.  Nothing plot relevant, but it may be amusing.
