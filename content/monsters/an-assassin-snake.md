## Notes

To spawn the assassin snakes, first clear all humanoid mobs within the zone. When the zone is empty, four NPC [[Seagull (Cove of Decay)|seagulls]] will spawn on the rocks between the shoreline and the zone entrance (see seagull link for exact locations). If you hail a seagull, the bird will fly slowly to the water's edge and spawn an assassin snake, which promptly eats the seagull.
