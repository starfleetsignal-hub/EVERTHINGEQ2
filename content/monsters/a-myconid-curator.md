## Notes

- There are technically 2 different types of myconid curators.  One is lvl 57-58 and on a shared spawn with myconid mages and warriors.  The other is 58-60 and spawns throughout the myconid tunnels.
- Possible classes are Brigand, Defiler, Fury, Guardian, Inquisitor, Mystic, Swashbuckler, Templar, Warden
