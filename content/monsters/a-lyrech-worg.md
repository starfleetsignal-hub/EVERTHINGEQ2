## Notes

- Two of these are linked with Zieran the Hunter, regardless of whether he is in [[Zieran the Hunter (Wood Elf)|Wood Elf]] or [[Zieran the Hunter (werewolf)|werewolf]] form.
- During the daytime, they are on [[The Lyrech (Faction)|The Lyrech faction]] and cause faction hits if killed.
- At night, they are always agro and do not cause faction hits when killed.
