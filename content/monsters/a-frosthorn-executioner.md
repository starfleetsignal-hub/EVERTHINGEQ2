## Notes

- Frosthorn executioners and nightgores are triggered spawns - after clearing the packs of Frosthorn shadowgores/stormcallers/vindicators from around {{waypoint 115, 24, -542}} and {{waypoint -10, 23, -438}}, a group of executioners and nightgores will spawn.  Killing the southern bunch of executioners/nightgores has a chance to spawn [[Bron Frosthorn]], the northern bunch has a chance to spawn [[Bloodrage]].
- Possible classes Berserker, Warlock
