## Notes

1. Click **a mute skull** in [[Malice's Narthex (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Malice's Narthex]] straight ahead from the zone-in point and go through its dialog to spawn the Librarian.
1. Kill and loot [[A bubble of hatred|bubbles of hatred]] until you receive [[Dried glob of hatred]] in order to be able to active the transporter.
1. Click the transporter pad at {{waypoint -154.24, 12.43, 215.01}} to activate, then click again to port to the balcony.
1. Hail the Librarian to activate.
