## Notes

- The levelmod of the scarabs depends on how many there are in the linked encounter - e.g. a group of 3 scarabs, they will all be one up, a group of four will be evencon, and a group of five will be a mix of evencon and one down.
- These occasionally cast a self buff called Armored Carapace that is a regenerating ward much like the ones Shamans cast.
