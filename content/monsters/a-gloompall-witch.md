## Notes

- Possible classes Fury, Guardian, Warlock
- The Gloompall witches are the first part of a ring event.  After killing all the witches, [[A Gloompall crone|Gloompall crones]] spawn, and after the crones are killed, [[The Gloompall Hag]] spawns.  After the Hag is killed, there seems to be timer of about one hour real time before the witches respawn.
