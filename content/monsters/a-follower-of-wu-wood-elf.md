## Notes

- This unique Follower of Wu is actually a *triple* agent, infiltrating the Wu camp for the Nybright family while actually working for the Thexians.  She's actually [[Lynn Q'Tentu]], a Dark Elf.
- There is no faction hit for attacking her, and the other monks aren't 'social' with her and won't attack players attacking her.
