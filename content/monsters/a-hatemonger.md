## Notes

It keep spawning and despawning. When he does so, he temporarly becomes unattackable.

The **[[A hatemonger|Hatemongers]]** come in groups of 3's and are attached to [[A scorn reaper]] somewhere nearby.
When [[A scorn reaper]] stetches out his a group of [[A hatemonger|Hatemongers]] will come out of the ground somewhere nearby, not always in the same place, and not always right near the summoning [[A hatemonger|Hatemonger]].
Kill the [[A scorn reaper|scorn reaper]] and his 3 [[A hatemonger|Hatemongers]] will go away.
