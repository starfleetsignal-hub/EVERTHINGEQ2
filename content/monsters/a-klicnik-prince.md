## Notes

- To spawn these, find 'a larvae infested carcass' and clear all the [[A klicnik warrior|klicnik warriors]] from around it, and a klicnik prince may spawn. Kill it for a chance at spawning [[King Klicnik]].
- Sometimes instead of spawning a single klicnik prince, four linked heroic kliknick princes will spawn.
