## Notes

These appear only during the quest [[By Hook or by...]].  Two of them spawn in a group of five with [[Asajj An'duuth]] and two [[A wicked assassin|wicked assassins]].  As priests, they will heal Asajj An'Duuth, and should probably be targeted first.
