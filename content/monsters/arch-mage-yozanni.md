Arch Mage Yozanni is located inside the tower on right side upper ledge and is spawned by defeating [[Magmatron|Magmaton]]. He has a curse (Fighting the Flames) you have to cure with the brittle magma gems gotten from Doomfire Sparks in the zone, which respawn in case you run out. The adds he spawns also drop the gems. He will randomly spawn adds you have to kill to remove his damage reduction.
