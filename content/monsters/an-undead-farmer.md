## Notes

- The ruins in the Fallow Farmlands have three different locations for a small ring event.  Clear out groups of [[An undead farmer|undead farmers]] and [[An undead settler|settlers]] - the individually spawning undead are not involved.  Clearing a group of farmers and settlers causes a group of 3-4 undead explorers to appear.  Clear the explorers for a small chance to spawn a group with [[A Rotting Pioneer]].
