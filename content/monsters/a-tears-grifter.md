## Background

The Tears' grifters serve the [[The Court of Tears (POI)|Court of Tears]], the smallest of the [[The Courts of Maj'Dul|Courts of Maj'Dul]]. They operate in the shadows, assigning dirty work to any willing adventurers.

The grifters offer repeatable quests that take place within small instances around [[Maj'Dul]] called '[[A Maj'Dul Residence|Maj'Dul Residences]]'. These quests are randomly assigned, and are on a 30-minute cooldown whether you accept or decline them. They reward a varying amount of [[The Courts of Maj'Dul|Court Tokens]], which can be turned in at [[The Tower of the Moon]] for a faction increase with a Court of your choice.
