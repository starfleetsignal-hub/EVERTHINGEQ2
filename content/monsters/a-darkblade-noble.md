## Notes

- These are on a shared spawn with the other monsters in the room ([[A Darkblade brigand|Darkblade brigands]], [[A Darkblade fury|furies]], [[A Darkblade maiden|maidens]], [[A Darkblade scout|scouts]], and [[A netherot chanter|netherot chanters]], but are a comparatively rare spawn.
