## Notes

- Level scales to the tier of the zone, not to the level of the person fighting it.
- Will never be in the first four rounds of the Void Tempest ring event, only the 5th, and is still a rare spawn in that slot compared to [[Dark seepage]]s and [[Dark horror]]s.
- Has knock back; will also KB on death (as do all of the 5th round mobs).

## Drops

![The odd patterns of a void gear hammer.](images/Shadow_Juggernaught's_Disenthralled_Impact_%28Equipped%29.jpg)
**Void gear** is obtained from the corpses of [[Shadow brute]]s.  The gear is highly distinctive, incorporating circles and hollowed orbs in strange linkages.

All tiers of the same item have the same appearance.  The tier 1 hammer looks exactly like the tier 8 hammer when equipped, for example.

### Items

- Blade of the Shadow Brute - scouts; warriors, crusaders; druids
- Longbow of the Shadow Fiend - scouts; warriors, crusaders
- Shadow Bonesplitter - scouts; warriors, crusaders
- Shadow Eddy Staff - fighters; mages; priests
- Shadow Juggernaught's Impact - fighters; priests
