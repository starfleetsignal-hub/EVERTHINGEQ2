## Notes

- Level range for deep spawn trawlers at Wayunder Lake is 63-64, at Timorous Moor it is 64-65.
- At Wayunder Lake trawlers are on a shared spawn with deep spawn anglers and seers; at Timorous Moor they're on a shared spawn with deep spawn anglers and beachcombers.
