## Notes

- Part of the zone-wide event to gain access to the inner ring of fire and pyramid with [[Terror]]. Spawns upon placing [[A Thulian caller]] corpse into the center of the sacrificial well. Once all three caller corpses have been dragged into the center and all three flightflyer's have been killed, a heart of fear appears in the center that you and any group members can now click and use at the two braziers outside the tower in the Northern area of [[Terrock's Bite]] to gain access to the inner ring.
