## Notes

- Despite the name, this is a golem type mob.
- This monster was once a part of the Mage subclass selection quest, back in the days when adventurers had to quest for their adventure professions instead of starting out at Level 1 as a specific class. Now, he wanders strangely-stitched, nonaggressive and useless, killed only by adventurers searching in vain for a little slice of AA experience (which he doesn't provide).
