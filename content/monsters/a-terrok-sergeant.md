## Notes

- When an adventurer (or group of adventurers) passes through Bloodforge Gate, four [[A Terrok gate enforcer|Terrok gate enforcer]]s and one Terrok sergeant will spawn down the hallway on either side, then run towards the gate.  If they don't aggro, they'll stop running and stand guard on the far side of the gate.
