## Location

Graverobbers are only up at night (20:00 - 05:00) and will immediately despawn at 05:00. Their spawn locations are surrounding (especially behind) mausoleums at: {{waypoint -30, -8, 111}} {{waypoint -80, -11, 38}} {{waypoint -80, -11, -24}} {{waypoint 46, -3, -29}}. Their placeholders are the rats, skeletons and zombies immediately surrounding the mausoleums.
