## Notes

- After using the [[Willow reed flute]] inside the orc tents, two linked leafy defenders will spawn and attack the tent.  After 'killing' it they'll remain up for a little while, and you can kill them if you're desperate for XP, spading something for EQ2i, working on Treant L&L, or just feel like being a jerk to a treant who just helped you.
