## Notes

- To spawn these, you must be on the quest Remnants of Butcherblock.  Clicking on most tree stumps in the zone (some of the burnt ones in the drake area don't work) will cause one of these to spawn.
- Should you run from it instead of fighting it, it will despawn instantly once aggro breaks and the tree stump that spawned it won't spawn another one.
- Classes include (but may not be limited to): Berserker, Coercer, Fury
