## Factions

- -100 faction with **Gloompall Fairies**

## Notes

- Possible classes Fury, Guardian, Warlock
- The Gloompall crones are the second part of a ring event.  The event starts with [[A Gloompall witch|Gloompall witches]].  After killing all the witches, the crones spawn, and after the crones are killed, [[The Gloompall Hag]] spawns.  After the Hag is killed, there seems to be timer of about one hour real time before the ring event restarts and the witches respawn.
