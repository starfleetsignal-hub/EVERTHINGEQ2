## Notes

The mob appears both in the upper cavern and in between the second and third cavern levels.  There are two groups in the upper cavern; one by the skeleton of the thing that ate [[The Devoured]], and one by the second big gnomish device to be blown up.  Right behind that gnomish-device group is a little passage in the wall that leads down to a cave that holds another group of them.

In the upper cavern, the behemoths spawn in groups of three.  In the back cavern, they come in pairs.
