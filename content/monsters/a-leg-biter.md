## Notes

- These only spawn during the quest [[Watch Your Step in the Thundering Steppes, Part III]].  Every time you enter the Village of Thundermist, a pair of these will spawn and attack.
- They also appear on Antonica Road just south of Qeynos Tower 1.
- If you run away and they de-aggro, they will de-spawn as soon as they return to their spawn location.
