## Notes

- Part of the [[Igidishi]] encounter.
- May appear as a shadowy bear, drake, frog, or wolf.
- Spawn from portals all over the zone. As the portals open, a dark swirling cloud appears for a brief time. Possible locations include:
  - At the base of the stairs of the [[Basilica of Hate (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Basilica of Hate]] at  {{waypoint -16.17, 27.39, -142.97}}
  - East of the stairs of the [[Basilica of Hate (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Basilica of Hate]] at {{waypoint -54.02, 30.56, -192.93}}
  - In [[Malice's Narthex (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Malice's Narthex]] at {{waypoint 44.04, 0.44, 232.98}}
  - At the base of the stairs that lead up to [[Rectory of Confession (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Rectory of Confession]] at {{waypoint 122.15, 19.89, 15.49}}
  - In the tunnel that leads from [[Wrath's Anvil (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Wrath's Anvil]] to [[The Abbey (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|The Abbey]] at {{waypoint -200.47, 11.07, 87.34}}
  - South of the fountain in the [[Amphitheater of Rancor (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Amphitheater of Rancor]] at {{waypoint -8.91, 11.59, 3.28}}
  - Southwest of the organ in the [[Amphitheater of Rancor (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Amphitheater of Rancor]] at {{waypoint 7.50, 7.98, -38.67}}
