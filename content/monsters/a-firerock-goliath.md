## Notes

- The first Firerock goliath you kill gives AA.
- On the bridge across [[Deadview Pass]], a group of 5-6 non-heroic firerock giants spawns.  Sometimes a non-heroic Firerock goliath will spawn with them; it gives AA and will update any quest requiring you to kill goliaths.
