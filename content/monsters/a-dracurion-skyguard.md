## Notes

- These things swoop down from the sky and immediately attack the nearest [[A Danak protector|Danak protector]], usually losing to them. They will only attack PCs if there is no Danak protector near them to mess with agro, and they will despawn on their own in about 2 minutes real time if not killed. No faction changes.
