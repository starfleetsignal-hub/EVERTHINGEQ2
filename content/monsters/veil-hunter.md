## Notes

A Veil Hunter isn't a monster in the usual sense.  During the mission [[Entering the Veil]], you will take control of a Veil Hunter and use it to kill [[A spirit scamp|spirit scamp]]s that are invisible to humanoids.  Veil Hunters are named "Veil Hunter (Player)".
