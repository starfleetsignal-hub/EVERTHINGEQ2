## Notes

- Always comes in a linked encounter of one [[A Sathirian squad leader|Sathirian squad leader]] and three [[A Sathirian tracker|Sathirian trackers]], all evencon heroics.
- The group that patrols between Jinisk and The Tabernacle is lvl 74.  For some reason, the Sathirian trackers in that group are not agro and have no faction hits (the squad leader, however, is agro with faction hits)
- The second group patrols between The Tabernacle {{waypoint 111, -117, 536}} and [[Dalnir's Wheel]] {{waypoint 358, -118, 798}} is lvl 76.  The trackers in this group are agro with faction hits.
