## Notes

- These are guards that can be used to locate other NPCs.
- Rather than attack you, a Hua Mein guard will kick you back to where you zoned into The Sundered Frontier from.
- They will be aggressive to any player who has not yet completed the quest [[Battle of Ages Past]].
