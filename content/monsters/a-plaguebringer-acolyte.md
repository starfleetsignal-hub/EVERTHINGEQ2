## Notes

- To spawn these, clear all the [[A restless Caltorsite|restless Caltorsites]] from around the 'foci of Decay'. Either one 18^ or four linked 18 Heroic plaguebringer acolytes spawn. There is a small chance that [[Priest of the Plaguebringer]] will spawn after killing it/them.
- Possible classes Inquisitor, Necromancer, Shadowknight
