## Locations

- In the [[Valley of Sacrifice]], across the road from the [[Grove of Stones]] {{waypoint 187, 9, 22}}
  - This group will sometimes spawn as Heroics, with [[A Hill Cat Patriarch]]
- At the passage from Valley of Sacrifice to [[Spirit Valley]] {{waypoint 121, 11, 84}}
  - When you pass between the stones they can appear and attack, even if they are grey con
- In the [[Splintered Plain]] by the entrance to [[Three Toes Valley]] {{waypoint 490, -16, -104}}
  - If [[A Hill Cat Matriarch]] is up, the group will have [[A hill cat prowler|hill cat prowler]]s instead
