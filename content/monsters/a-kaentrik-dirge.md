## Notes

- Can be human, half elf or wood elf
- May spawn as 56-57 heroic (no arrow) grouped with [[An Aravu naga]]
- Kaentrik dirges do not give any faction hits (good or bad) if killed.  Kaentrik troubadors do.  It's unclear why, and possibly a design oversight on SOE's part.
