**Dopplegangers** have the ability to assume the form of anyone they see, including adventurers. The shape-changed monster can even speak as your friend! However, this is only a seeming. When it attacks, it will have the same attacks, defense and procs as your friend, but will use them as a level 74^^^!

## Things Dopplegangers Say

*"Some how I dropped from the group."*

*"LFG invite plz"*

*"How did I get in here?"*

*"I got lost back there. Why did you leave me behind?"*
