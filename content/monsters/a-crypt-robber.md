## Notes

- Part of a linked encounter with [[Ronicus the Red]] (21^), and [[Rustam the Vile]] (21even).  There are always 3 crypt robbers - one inquisitor, one dirge and one warlock.
- The warlock is a half elf, the other crypt robbers are human.
