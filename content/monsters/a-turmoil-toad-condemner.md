## Notes

- Part of the ring event to spawn [[Angst]]. Kill all of the turmoil toads located in the [[Turmoil Boneyard]], including the ones spawned by clicking on the headstones. Once you click the final headstone and all the other toads have been killed Angst will spawn in addition to one more turmoil toad.
