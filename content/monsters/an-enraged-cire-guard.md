## Notes

- This mob is involved in the spawn cycle for [[Thimzem Ci'Re]].
  - First group is two [[A Ci'Re apprentice|Ci'Re apprentices]] (32vv), two [[A Ci'Re guard|Ci'Re guards]] (32vv), one [[A spirited tome|spirited tome]] and one [[An enchanted tome|enchanted tome]] (both 32)
  - Second group is two [[A replacement Ci'Re apprentice|replacement Ci'Re apprentices]] (33vv), two [[A replacement Ci'Re guard|replacement Ci'Re guards]] (33vv), one [[A spirited tome|spirited tome]] and one [[An enchanted tome|enchanted tome]] (both 33)
  - Third group is two [[An enraged Ci'Re apprentice|enraged Ci'Re apprentices]] (34vv), two [[An enraged Ci'Re guard|enraged Ci'Re guards]] (34vv), one [[A spirited tome|spirited tome]] and one [[An enchanted tome|enchanted tome]] (both 34)
  - After the third group is slain, there is roughly a 50% chance that Thimzem Ci'Re will spawn
