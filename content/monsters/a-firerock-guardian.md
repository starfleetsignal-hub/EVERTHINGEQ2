## Notes

![](images/A_pile_of_bones.png)To spawn these, left click the "a pile of bones" that can be found scattered around the Valley of the Ancients.  As soon as you click, the piles of bones will animate into Firerock guardians.  They do not automatically agro, and a player can quickly move back after activating them to avoid being immediately attacked.
