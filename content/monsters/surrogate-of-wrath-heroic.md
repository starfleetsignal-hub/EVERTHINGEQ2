## Notes

- Slaying [[Surrogate of Contempt (Heroic)|Surrogate of Contempt]], [[Surrogate of Rage (Heroic)|Surrogate of Rage]], [[Surrogate of Rancor (Heroic)|Surrogate of Rancor]], [[Surrogate of Venom (Heroic)|Surrogate of Venom]], and [[Surrogate of Wrath (Heroic)|Surrogate of Wrath]] spawns [[The Head of Hate]].

To kill the Surrogates permanently, kill the Surrogate. Where it fell will be a book. Right-click on the book and choose **Create**. The Create option gives you the recipe "Destroy Vessel", which permanently kills the Surrogate once crafted. The recipe requires:

- 10 Ire Dust (harvested from barrels in zone)
- 10 etherium
- 10 golden ember
- 10 deadly mushroom
- 50 Celestial Candle
