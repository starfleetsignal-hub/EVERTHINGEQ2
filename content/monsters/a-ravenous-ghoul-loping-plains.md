## Notes

- Ravenous ghouls can be found around the [[Reconstructed Ulteran Spire]] and the plains south of Somborn and [[The Breathless Hollow]].
- They seem to be on a shared spawn with the [[A risen bandit|risen bandit]]s and [[A risen thug|risen thug]]s.  The risen bandits and thugs have a fear ability, so be careful when fighting them.
