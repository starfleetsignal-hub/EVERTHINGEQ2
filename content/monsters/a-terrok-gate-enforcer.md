## Notes

- When an adventurer (or group of adventurers) passes through Bloodforge Gate, four Terrok gate enforcers and one [[A Terrok sergeant|Terrok sergeant]] will spawn down the hallway on either side, then run towards the gate.  If they don't aggro, they'll stop running and stand guard on the far side of the gate.
