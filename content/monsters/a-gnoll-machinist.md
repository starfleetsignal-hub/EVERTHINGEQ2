Gnoll machinists spawn as part of the following quests.

- [[Mysterious Machine]] - spawns a lvl 8 Guardian gnoll machinist when you examine the machine at {{waypoint 676, -34, 500}}
- [[On the Move]] - spawns a lvl 10vv Berserker gnoll machinist, linked with a 10vv Assassin [[A gnoll guard|gnoll guard]] and a lvl 10vv Coercer [[A machinist assistant|machinist assistant]], at various places in the zone
