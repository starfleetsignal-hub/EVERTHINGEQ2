## Notes

- The Darkfall Gorge bridge has an odd spawning cycle similar to a ring event.  The dwarves that normally patrol it will be replaced by kobolds - it's unclear exactly what triggers the replacement.  If all the kobolds are killed, the bugbears will spawn *immediately*, and if the bugbears are in turn all killed, the dwarves will spawn immediately.
