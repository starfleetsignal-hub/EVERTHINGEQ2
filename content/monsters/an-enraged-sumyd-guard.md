## Notes

- Involved in the spawn cycle of [[Erbmys Sumyd]].
  - First group is three [[A Sumyd apprentice|Sumyd apprentices]] (32vv), two [[A Sumyd guard|Sumyd guards]] (32vv), one [[An enchanted tome|enchanted tome]] (32vv), one [[A spirited tome|spirited tome]] (32vv), and one [[A clay guardian|clay guardian]] (32^)
  - Second group is three [[A replacement Sumyd apprentice|replacement Sumyd apprentices]] (33vv), two [[A replacement Sumyd guard|replacement Sumyd guards]] (33v), one [[An enchanted tome|enchanted tome]] (33vv), one [[A spirited tome|spirited tome]] (33vv), and one [[A clay guardian|clay guardian]] (33^)
  - Third group is three [[An enraged Sumyd apprentice|enraged Sumyd apprentices]] (34vv), three [[An enraged Sumyd guard|enraged Sumyd guards]] (34vv), one [[An enchanted tome|enchanted tome]] (34vv) and one [[A spirited tome|spirited tome]] (34vv)
  - After killing the third group, there is roughly a 50% chance that Erbmys Sumyd will spawn.
