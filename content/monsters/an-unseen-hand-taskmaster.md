## Note

- Killing this mob causes any remaining [[A circle initiate|circle initiates]] to attack you
- If you talk tho this mob, she will tell you to clean up the place.
  - If you do not do it, she assumes you are not a new initiate and you are attacked by all the circle initiates
  - If you clean up the place, she says you can go into the back room and talk to the bosses
    - [[Fippy Darkpaw, the 4th|Fippy]] will offer you his quest whether you talk to the taskmaster or not
