## Notes

- Although they're marked 3 Up Heroic, they don't have the usual modifiers that Obulus Frontier mobs have, and will be dead in a split second in combat.
- Only interactable during a specific step on the quest 'Bridge to Success' - even then they have to be left clicked to activate into attackable mobs.
- They're tiny little and the same color as the ground - hard as hell to see.
