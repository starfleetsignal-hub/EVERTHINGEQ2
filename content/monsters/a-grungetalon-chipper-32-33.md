## Notes

- These monsters come in the formation of one [[A Grungetalon chopper|Grungetalon chopper]] (32^-33^ Brigand), one [[A Grungetalon chipper|Grungetalon chipper]] (32-33 Berserker) and one Stonemunch troglodyte (either [[A Stonemunch crusher|crusher]] or [[A Stonemunch pulverizer|pulverizer]]).
- Despite having 'Grungetalon' in its name, this goblin is a Boombottom as seen by its title, smaller size and red skin (Grungetalons are larger than Boombottoms and have gray skin).
