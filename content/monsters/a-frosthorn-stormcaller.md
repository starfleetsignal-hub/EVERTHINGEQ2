## Notes

- There are technically two different mobs both called a Frosthorn stormcaller.
  - Type One is 42vv-43even Heroic agro/social and linked with its fellow Frosthorn minotaurs.  It can be found in [[Jagged Plains]] by the ice slabs at  {{waypoint -8, 23, -435}} and {{waypoint 100, 27, -550}}.
  - Type Two is 44even solo, neither social nor agro.  It wanders freely around the edges of the Jagged Plains, and is on a shared spawn with some animal mobs.

Can be charmed by Coercers.
