## Notes

- There are only ten bloated soul spawn points at [[Mound Lake]].
- Bloated soul leeches are on a 5 minute respawn timer.
- There is one found in the center of [[The Pot]], which is the placeholder for [[Muckwallow]].
- Similar to the [[A soul leech|soul leeches]] found in the southern part of the Torrent River, walking over a bloated soul leech will afflict the player with [[Soul Drain]].
