## Notes

- During LU54, Sinking Sands was de-heroiced and raised to the mid 50s in level.  LU55 re-lowered the zone to high 40s/low 50s.  SOE neglected to relower the level of groups of raptors, although they *did* relower the level of the wandering solo raptors. This is the reason for the raptors' odd level range.
