Shares its spawn point with [[A Blackfurl lubber]], [[A Blackfurl medic]], [[A Blackfurl oarcaller]], [[A Blackfurl scout]].

## Locations

- On [[Blackfurl Beach]] and on the second floor of the central ruins during the day, in the shared spawn configuration. For some reason the shared spawn Blackfurl scouts don't use class abilities in combat.
- In the castle foyer, during the day.
- Where the water runs through the castle, just outside the castle walls, one static spawn on each side of the castle.
