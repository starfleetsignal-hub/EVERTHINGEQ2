## Notes

- This monster is spawned during the [[The Teachings of Yoru]] quest by [[Cythan]] as a preliminary test of the character's abilities. A succession of these will spawn ending with a [[A Spirit Master]]. The quester has only 5 minutes to slay all the spawns.
