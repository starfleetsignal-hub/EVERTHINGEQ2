## Notes

- The patrons of hate are a straight up fight with one small twist - when they die the immediately spawn in place [[A manifestation of rage]]. This manifestation is also a straight up fight it just has a fair amount of hit points. Not a bad fight overall - provided you are prepared for the immediate second spawn. Also, the Manifestations do not despawn and do not go to where the Patron was pulled from. Where the patron died is considered the manifestations spawn point and he will just hang around.
