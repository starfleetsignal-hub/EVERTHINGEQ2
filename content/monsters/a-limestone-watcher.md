## Notes

- The watchers are not attackable until you disturb each of the four skeletons in in the walls around the alcove.
- Distubing *a skeleton* will spawn a level 26 [[A brittle bones|brittle bones]], which you must kill.
- As soon as the fourth skeleton dies, the limestone watcher will become attackable.
