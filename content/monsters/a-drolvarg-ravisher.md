## Notes

- Kylong Plains drolvarg class varies based on the appearance of the drolvarg, not the name of the mob.
  - Red armored drolvargs are always berserkers
  - Orange furred drolvargs are either guardians or assassins
  - Blue furred drolvargs are always inquisitors
