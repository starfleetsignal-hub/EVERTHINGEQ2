## Notes

- These spawn along with [[An ambushed gnome]].  If they're killed before they kill the gnome, the gnome turns into [[Gimdimble Fizzwoddle]] and offers a Heritage Quest.  If the gnome is killed, kill them anyway and the gnome will respawn along with them.
