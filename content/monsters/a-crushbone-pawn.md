## Notes

- Technically speaking, there are three separate types of monsters called 'a Crushbone pawn'
  - One is found in [[Valley of the Great Swarm]] {{waypoint -375, 24, -56}} - lvl 7-8,  agro but not social.
  - One is found south of [[Orc Hill]], in the camp and by the ramp leading down off the hill. {{waypoint 186, 61, -204}} - lvl 8, agro & social
  - One runs randomly underneath Kelethin, around its outskirts - lvl 9-10
- Possible classes for all types are Assassin and Guardian
