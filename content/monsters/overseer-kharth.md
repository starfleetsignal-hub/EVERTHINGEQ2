Overseer Kharth is the final mob of a Public Quest: Frenzy of the Mange that occurs in the [[Central Farmlands]] of Antonica near [[Farmer Walcott]] {{waypoint -100, -18, 47}}. According to the May 14, 2013 patch notes, *"Antonica burrow public quest is now active when Nights of the Dead, Erollisi Day, Bristlebane Day, Brewday, Heroes' Festival, Frostfell, and Qeynos city festival are not active."* Rumor is that this PQ is broken, specifically, that even when at the correct level, the reward chest from killing Overseer Kharth is unlootable (https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/antonica-pq-how-does-it-work.531339/).

<nowiki/>
The PQ: Frenzy of the Mange manifests itself as 4 farmers standing in a row in front of a pacing lieutenant of the Qeynos Guard. At some point, gnolls will begin attacking the farmers and the lieutenant, essentially beginning an extended ring event that proceeds as described below. Your mission is to defeat the gnolls and try to spare the lieutenant and farmers from being killed. (Credit to https://eq2.zam.com/wiki/EQ2_Quest:Frenzy_of_the_Mange for much of this information).

1. Defeat 10 gnoll lookouts
1. Defeat 20 gnoll vanguard soldiers (^^ difficulty)
1. Defeat 45 elite gnoll lancers (^^^ difficulty) or 30 elite gnoll lancers (^^ difficulty)
1. Defeat Overseer Kharth
