## Notes

- To spawn these, left-click the skeletons in the cubbyholes. Each skeleton will spawn one brittle bones
- You must kill 4 brittle bones in each alcove to make the [[A limestone watcher|limestone watchers]] attackable.
- Possible classes Assassin, Guardian, Inquisitor
