## Notes

- There are 3 different palace sweepers, determined by the color of their robes:
  - [[A palace sweeper (Blades)]] - Blue Robe
  - [[A palace sweeper (Coin)]] - Yellow Robe
  - [[A palace sweeper (Truth)]] - Red Robe
- These do NOT spawn on the Skygazer plateau but instead spawn on the first plateau as you enter the city, about midway between the gates and the first staircase.

## Related Quests

- Drops a [[Service Entrance Key, Truth|Service Entrance Key]], which starts the quest [[A Thief in the House of Truth]].

- bad faction with **The Court of Truth**
