## Notes

- Involved in the spawn cycle of [[Et'sipe Ymeresh]].
  - First group is two [[A Ymeresh apprentice|Ymeresh apprentices]] (32v), one [[A Ymeresh guard|Ymeresh guard]] (32v), one [[A spirited tome|spirited tome]] and one [[An enchanted tome|enchanted tome]] (both 32)
  - Second group is two [[A replacement Ymeresh apprentice|replacement Ymeresh apprentices]] (33v), one [[A replacement Ymeresh guard|replacement Ymeresh guard]] (33v), one [[A spirited tome|spirited tome]] and one [[An enchanted tome|enchanted tome]] (both 33)
  - Third group is two [[An enraged Ymeresh apprentice|enraged Ymeresh apprentices]] (34v), one [[An enraged Ymeresh guard|enraged Ymeresh guard]] (34v), one [[A spirited tome|spirited tome]] and one [[An enchanted tome|enchanted tome]] (both 34)
  - After killing the third group, there is roughly a 50% chance that Et'sipe Ymeresh will spawn.
