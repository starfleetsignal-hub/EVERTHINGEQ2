## Notes

Upon claiming and examining this token, you will receive a pop-up window that states:
Thank you for attending SOE Live 2012! As a special token of our appreciation please accept this claim reward! What would you like to do?
Accept Reward!
Save for later!
If you choose to accept the reward, you receive the message:
You may now hire the vampiric mercenary from either Qeynos or Freeport mercenary dens!
In addition, you receive the following from a reward pop-up:

- [[Dashenti's Cloak of the Gathering]]
- 2 [[Dashenti's Dagger of the Gathering]]

The mercenary may then be hired from one of the following locations:

- [[Qeynos Harbor]]
  - Within the Mercenary Den underneath Fish's Alehouse and Inn {{waypoint 799, -253, 34}}
