## Notes

- When used, your character tosses a bee hive at the target's head. This is a "fluff" effect for fun (kind of like throwing snowballs during [[Frostfell]]), not an actual attack.
- This is one of several options to choose when you complete the [[Bristlebane Day Snack Snatcher: Enchanted Lands]] races.
- This is a consumable charge, similar to fireworks charms.
