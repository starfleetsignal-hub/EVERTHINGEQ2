## Notes

This item can only be obtained via the [[Marketplace]] and the cost will vary, based on the value of [[Daybreak Game Company, LLC|Daybreak]] Cash.

- These tickets are sold in increments of 1 to 20 and all members of a guild can accumulate them to unlock the ability to purchase the hall.
- Excess tickets can be sold for [[Status points|status points]].
- As of 2016, the hall this unlocks requires a total of 350 Highhold Guild Tickets.
- See the [[Highhold Guild Hall]] page for images and more details.
