## Notes

See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity does not have a [[Guild Level]] minimum.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

Courtyard Landmark Statue: Domination is a very large statue amenity.

- When this amenity is purchased by a guild leader, it is automatically placed in the central courtyard of the T3 and T4 [[Freeport-style Guild Halls]].
- It can **not** be moved.
