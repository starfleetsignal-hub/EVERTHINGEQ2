## Notes

The Troglodyte Ribcage is a breastplate for Berserker, Guardian, Inquisitor, Paldin, Shadowknight, and Templar. It is Fabled and Attunable. Adds 12 strength and 12 stamina. Mitigation is 114 level 10 to use. This item is obtained by fighting the Troglodyte Champion in the small arena on the right side of the the Bloodskull Valley zone.
