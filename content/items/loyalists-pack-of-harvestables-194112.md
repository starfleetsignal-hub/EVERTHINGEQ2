## Notes

- If you R click and open the examine window, you will see a list of possible [[Harvestables (All)|tradeskill resources]] that you *might* get.
  - What you receive is random, but stacks of common resources are 100 items per type. For example: 100 [[Leonid root|Leonid roots]], 100 [[Ragged leathery hide]], 100 [[Stringy meat]].
  - Along with common resources you *might* get a small quantity of rare resources. For example, 5 [[Araucaria lumber]].
- Only [[Free-To-Play#Membership Plans|All Access (paid)]] subscribers can purchase this item for **10** [[Loyalty Point Token|Loyalty Point Tokens]].
