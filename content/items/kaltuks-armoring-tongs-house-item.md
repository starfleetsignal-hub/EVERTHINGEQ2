## Notes

The Favor of the Artisan will be imbued into the user of this item, granting a temporary bonus to crafters of the 20th rank or higher. You may not use the favor more than once per hour.
You can buy the weapon version again from the [[Shady Swashbuckler XVIII]] at the docks in [[Nektulos Forest]].<br>
