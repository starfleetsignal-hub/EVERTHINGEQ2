## Notes

Teleportation Pads make it easy to magically travel from one spot to another in any house. Simply place a pad and right click on it to name its location (eg. Bedroom or Kitchen). Once at least two have been placed in a house, step on the pad and you'll be asked to select another location by the name you've given it.

- This item can now be purchased during the [[City Festival|City Festivals]] each month.
- Only 4 (2 bags) can be placed in a home.
- There is a [[Guild Hall Amenities|Guild Hall Amenity]] that serves the same function, so these **can not** be used in guild halls.
- These teleportation pads have become very popular when players completely alter the perceived layout of a home or in very large homes like the [[Teneberous Island Refuge]], because they offer both design flexibility and fast travel.
- Many players use these to "break out" of the confines of homes. For more on that topic, see [[Housing]].
