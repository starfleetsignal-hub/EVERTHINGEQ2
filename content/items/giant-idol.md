## Notes

Examine the first idol you get to start the language quest, [[Words of a Giant]]. Find and examine 5 more to complete the quest. Completion of this quest grants you the ability to understand and speak [[Krombral]], the common language of all giants.<br>
Each idol you find and study will give you a **Studied Giant Idol** in its place while you are on the quest, [[Words of a Giant]]. These studied idols sell the same as the regular ones.<br>
