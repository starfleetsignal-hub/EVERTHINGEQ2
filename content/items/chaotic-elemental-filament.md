- Crafting component in tradeskill quests from the [[Heralds of Oblivion Timeline|Heralds of Oblivion]] prelude event.
- Primary component in crafting [[Inscrutable Rune Building Blocks]]
- If farming for crafting purposes:
- Each Planar Rift spawns 5 elementals, the first 4 elementals drop x2 filaments, and the final 'boss' elemental drops x3, for a total of x11 filaments per complete Rift cycle.  x55 filaments per quest if you are running those concurrently.

Once prelude event is over these can be found roaming freely on the ground in [[Rage of Cthurath]] overland zones.
