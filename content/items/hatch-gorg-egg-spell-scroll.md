## Notes

A **pet gorg** is a fluff pet: something that follows the player around, doing nothing but looking good.  This aviak stands a little taller than waist-high, and will give replies in [[Volant]] when hailed.

The whole pet's name is ended with a question mark.  Considering a gorg is a lizard and the pet is an aviak, a question mark is suitable.  (The replies are indicative of an Ugly Duckling scenario.)

- "Gorgity gorg gorg!"
