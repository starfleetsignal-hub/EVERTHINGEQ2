## Notes

See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity can only be purchased when your guild reaches [[Guild Level]] 100.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

A clickable banner that grants a [[Guild Buff]]. It boosts your primary stat based on the Guild level (for example 418.5 at Guild level 100)
