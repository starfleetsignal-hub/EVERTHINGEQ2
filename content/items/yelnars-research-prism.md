## Notes

This prism can be used to access a [[Skyshrine Tradeskill Apprentices|skyshrine tradeskill apprentice]]. The apprentice has a variety of recipes, but a few recipes are for [[Housing|furniture]] items with high [[Rent Status Reduction]], making them highly desirable to players who enjoy [[Decorating Houses|decorating houses]].

### **Warning!**

This item has two phases in your inventory that eventually grants one [[Tradeskill Apprentices|tradeskill apprentice]]. The steps are easily confused and the items are easy to misplace.<br>
**Do not click on and "use" the Prism to make the Teleportation Stone it if you want to sell or trade the Prism!**<br>
It is very easy to accidentally equip this in your characters charm slot, so if it goes missing while trying to use it, check your character's charm equipment slots for it.<br>

### How to Obtain

The quest to obtain this item, [[Etch the Stone]], is part of a raid instance, which makes it difficult for non-raiders to obtain. The stone *is* tradeable and can be found on the broker on rare occasions, but it typically sells for a hefty sum.

### How to Create the Apprentice

1. Right click the Prism and choose Use from the options.
1. This item now becomes the [[Yelnar's Teleportation Stone]]. It will NOT teleport you anywhere, ever. Instead the stone is what you click on to place [[Apprentice Elder Yelnar (Dracurion Mastercraftsman)]] in a house to begin the research process.
- Icon changes that can cause confusion:
- The item looks like a simple diamond-shaped stone in your inventory, but the icon switches to a blue bubble icon once you have consumed it.
- If consumed correctly (and not anciently equipped) it grants [[Yelnar's Teleportation Stone]] (blue bubble icon), which is the actual [[Tradeskill Apprentices|tradeskill apprentice]] you can place in a house and use to research various recipes.
