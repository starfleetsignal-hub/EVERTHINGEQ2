## How to Obtain

To obtain complete the following quests from the frog spirits then talk to [[Tiptup Strongheart]] in [[Tupta]]:

- [[Fallen But Not Forgotten]]
- [[The Spirit Sees All]]
- [[Not Really Deep or Murky, Actually]]
- [[The Old Sting of Betrayal]]

He currently will not show a quest icon indicating this item can be obtained from him.
