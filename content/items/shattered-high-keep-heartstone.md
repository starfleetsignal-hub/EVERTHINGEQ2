## Notes

This item can be used by those with a tradeskill of level 30 or higher who have purchased the [[Tradeskill Apprentices]] content from [[Daybreak Game Company, LLC]] and who have researched the [[High Keep Heartstone (Recipe Book)|recipe]] needed to "[[High Keep Heartstone (Item)|repair]]" it.

Repair turns it into a [[Restored High Keep Guildhall Deed]]. The deeds are auto-consumed to add to your character's contribution to unlock the [[Guild Achievements|guild achievement]] that allows your guild leader to buy the [[Restored High Keep Guildhall]] for the guild if so desired.
