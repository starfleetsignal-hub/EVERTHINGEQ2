## Notes

![Right click a hireling to open the Options. You must have at least one Mannequin amenity to set the Uniform. (Click to enlarge)](images/Hireling-options-window.jpg)
See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity does not have a [[Guild Level]] minimum.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About the  Guild Hall World Market Broker Hireling

A Guild Hall World Market Broker is a NPC.

- It functions just like a normal [[Broker|broker]] NPC.
- The broker fee for shopping within a hall is 20% (it is 20-40% elsewhere).

### Hireling Options

R click and choose Customize from the list. You can set the following"

- Name -Like character names, you can not choose a name with a copyright (e.g. Luke Skywalker) or one that is obscene.
- Race - choose any of the playable races for the hireling
- Sex - choose to make the hireling female or male
- Uniform - you must have at least one [[Uniform Mannequin]] amenity to set a uniform.
