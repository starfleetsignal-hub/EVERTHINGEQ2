## Notes

from [[Burnt Recipe Page 2]]
Crafting this requires:

- 1 Brick of bronze ore
- 1 Brick of silvril ore
- 1 Brick of thalium ore
- 2 Bricks of iron ore
- 1 [[Magmatic Crystal]]
- 15 smoldering coals[1]

All of these items can be looted in Deep forge zone or bought from broker. The item in question is crafted in the forge room of [[The Deep Forge]].
[1]These can be bought from an NPC near forge for 3g 33s
