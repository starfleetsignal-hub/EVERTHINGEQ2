## Notes

This item typically sells for 100SC ($1.00 US) though it goes on sale from time to time.

- The precise real-world value of items like this is hard to quantify, due to promotional offers that include Station Cash (SC); for more info on SC see the page about the [[Marketplace]].
- This will appear like a charm in your inventory if you purchase it and it must be consumed in the house you want to expand the item count in.
- You must *always* be inside a home you own; you can not use it in homes owned by others under any circumstance. You can not use it in guild halls (though that happened briefly when it was "bugged")
- Applying [[Personal House Item Limit Expander (200)|Personal House Item Expander (200)]] from marketplace will overwrite it and only add additional 100 items.
- This item ***can*** be combined with player crafted [[Dimensional Pocket Expander]] for a total of 200 additional items.
- This item cannot be applied to a house where [[Personal House Item Limit Expander (200)|Personal House Item Expander (200)]] from  marketplace was already applied.

**Other Expanders**
As of 2014, there are two expanders that can expand the limit in a house.

- [[Dimensional Pocket Expander]], which is player made from a rather difficult to obtain recipe, and add 100 items.
- [[Personal House Item Limit Expander (200)]], which is also from the [[Marketplace]], and adds 200 items.
