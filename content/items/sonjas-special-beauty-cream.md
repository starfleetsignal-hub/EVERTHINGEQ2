## Notes

- When placed, right-click and **use beauty cream**. Grants "Beauty Cream", which shapechanges caster into a Ykeshan pirate for 6 hours.
- Text when used: "You feel pretty ... oh, so pretty, and witty, and bright ..." are lyrics from "I Feel Pretty", a song from *[West Side Story](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Side_Story#Musical_numbers)*. Ironically, you are now a troll.
