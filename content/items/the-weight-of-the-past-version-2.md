## Book Text

A selection of poems in the djinn's favored style, the short form. Do these carry insights into the minds of the djinn?

Never sing again<br>
Songs bring pain, death and bondage<br>
Silence is precious

I found a garden<br>
Full of dust, ashes and weeds<br>
It is my home now

Though the Plane is gone<br>
Chains still bind those who fled it<br>
Linked to its torment

Come, sit beside me<br>
Fear nothing but night's shadow<br>
You are my anchor

Spare me your anger<br>
Too long have we gone on thus<br>
Now it is too late

In the long ago<br>
I lived in another Plane<br>
I grieve for its loss

Bring me to water<br>
Where I can drink my fill, then<br>
I will be refreshed

In his tiny mind<br>
He rules Maj'Dul, a city<br>
Of nothing but thieves

Far above the world<br>
The Citadel soars freely<br>
Dangling its chains

Why are you crying?<br>
There is pleasure without pain.<br>
Are those tears of joy?

Can you speak with me<br>
And use only words of truth?<br>
Yet you speak of love...
