## Notes

See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity does not have a [[Guild Level]] minimum.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

The guild hall versions of the tradeskill stations exist to assure a guild can have crafting tables without the need to ask guild members to use their personal status to directly buy the station.
*The above also prevents stations being removed if a member leaves the guild as they might be with stations purchased by individuals.
