## Notes

Required to purchased a large residence (2 types) in Maj'Dul: [[Affluent Maj'Dul residence]] or [[Large Maj'Dul residence]].

It can be purchased from one of the merchants inside the buildings for one of the courts by anyone who has sufficient faction in [[Maj'Dul]] with **one** of the following: The Court of Truth, The Court of Coin, or The Court of Blades

**To buy this licence, the minimum positive faction required with one of factions in Maj'Dul is 40,000**.

- If you loot or buy the following tokens from the [[Broker|broker]], you can speed up your faction gain. If you buy them, make sure they are from one of the two factions you are *not* trying to increase faction with before you turn them in to, since all three are opposed to each other.

Tokens: [[Coin insignia token]], [[Blades insignia token]], [[Truth insignia token]]

- In order to buy each type of residence (by size), the separate residency licenses are required. Buying this expensive residency license will not give you the freedom to buy the other two house types (small or medium).
