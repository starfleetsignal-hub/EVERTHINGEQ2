## Notes

To purchase this item from [[Vhishall]], a "quest unlock" is needed.
The unlock requires the completion of *one* of the following quests from [[The City of Freeport Timeline]]:

- [[Destructive Tendancies]]
- [[Dismal Portents]]
- [[Forging Alliances]]
- [[The Animus Agenda]]
