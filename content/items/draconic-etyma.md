## Notes

This currency is awarded during Tears of Veeshan Tradeskill Quests and Vesspyr Isles and is used at merchants.  Amongst other things they are good for Runes and War Runes and their associated recipes.

- Find the merchants in [[Falinpol]]:
  - tradeskill recipe merchant [[Sllithzes]] at {{waypoint 154, 187, -121}}
  - tradeskill fuel merchant [[Sissrath]] at {{waypoint 147, 187, -118}} (Special fuels)
  - adventure merchant [[Delvakin]] at {{waypoint 1, 197, 1}}
