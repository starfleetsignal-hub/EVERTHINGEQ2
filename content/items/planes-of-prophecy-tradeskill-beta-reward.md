## Notes

If you achieved both objectives on the Beta server, this reward is accessed by typing /claim to open the [[Claim]] Window.

- The item is a "crate" style reward that must be unpacked and it is found with under the Beta tab of the Claim window.
- Any character on your account can access and claim this reward, as it is not bound to a specific character.
