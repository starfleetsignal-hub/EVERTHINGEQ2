## Notes

- This item is a reward for the [[Scale Break]] quest, but afterwards you can purchase multiples from [[Vhishall]] in East Freeport.
- This item is very similar to the carpenter made item [[Small Fir Double Shelf]], though the carpenter made item offers [[Rent Status Reduction]].
- Aside from the adjusted size (this is smaller), it is otherwise identical to the carpenter made [[Classy Cedar Double Shelf]] which also offers Rent Status Reduction.
