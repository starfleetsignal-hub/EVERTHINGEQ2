## Book Text

"**Treants**," by various authors -- Being a collection of the thoughts and feelings evoked by these fascinating forest creatures.

The woodlands are filled with a mysterious beauty. Some of that beauty is due to the unseen influence of mana. Infused with mana, sentient beings known as treants are the guardians of the woods. This is their story.

"It nearly ripped my arms off." -- Griffin Barkstripper, Baubbleshire, after mistaking a drowsy treant for a willow and attempting to remove its bark to brew some tea. Lesson: A treant's bite is worse than its bark.

"Are those...eyes?"<br>-- overheard in the Orcish Wastes, followed by a high-pitched scream. The Orcish Wastes are the known habitat of a darker form of treant that is preoccupied with exterminating any other being in its path. They apparently view everyone else as the cause of the decay in their lands.<br>Lesson: If it has eyes, it can see you.

"A single treant can breathe life into a forest."<br>-- Daryann Stormrider, wood elf of the Willow Wood. The breath of the treant may be a source of renewing the concentration of mana within a forest, although this is not proven.<br>Lesson: Wood elves know the woods.

"Arbos is father and mother to all trees."<br>Anonymous dryad, OakmystForest. In mythology, Arbos is often cited as the tree from which all other trees sprung. Apparently, this relationship extends to the sentient treants as well.<br>Lesson: Even a potential enemy has parents.

"Put that out!"<br>-- an eldery treant to a camper lighting an improper campfire. Treants may be living beings, but they are still made of the same fibers as other trees. Generally, if you are in a deciduous forest, the treants there will appear as deciduous trees rather than coniferous trees.<br>Lesson: Make sure your fires are completely out before abandoning a campsite.

"It's magic!"<br>-- a Runnyeye goblin surprised by a treant, immediately before being crushed by its roots. Treants are very strong physically, however they are also well-known magic users. In some areas, magic-wielding treants outnumber other treants three to one.<br>Lesson: This could be magic!

"Mmm. Jumjum juice."<br>-- Xanuusus, a treant in the old Northern Karana Plains. As with most sentient creatures (and some that are not), jumjum is an excellent way to make friends. There is no guarantee that this will work with all treants, but it could be worth a try.<br>Lesson: Sometimes, it is better to give than to hoard but it may be impossible to tell when.

"Zzzzzzz..."<br>-- a sleeping treant in Nektulos Forest. As they age, treants tend to become less mobile. It could be the result of their sap flow slowing, making them more sleepy. Eventually, treants pause for longer and longer naps, finally losing all mobility and turning into regular trees.<br>Lesson: Not every great oak sprang from a normal acorn.

"Sharp axes make for small treants."<br>-- a Bloodskull lumberjack battling treants in the Orcish Wastes. Sadly, many treants have been turned into kindling by the orcs who continue to clear large swaths of land. While a lone orc is no match for a healthy treant, the orcs send in vast numbers of lumberjacks when they suspect a treant is living amongst the trees in their way.<br>Lesson: Even the oldest and strongest can be felled by strategic planning.

I hope this information provides you with enough basic information about treants that you will be able to learn more about them on your own. Remember to walk carefully through the forests and beware of all big sticks!
