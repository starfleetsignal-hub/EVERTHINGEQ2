## Notes

This weapon is a ground spawn near Karatun Hold, the giant camp near Highton. It spawns in several locations (see the talk page for known locations).  You can bring this spear to [[Warlord Gadar Gwalnax]] at {{waypoint 1470, 165, -2910}}, a Gwalnax Brigade sarnak near Chardok. Also, disabling flora in options will remove the branches from the landscape making the spear more apparent.

## Rewards

Experience<br>

Repeatable.
