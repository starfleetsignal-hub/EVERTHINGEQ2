## Notes

[[Monk pebble|Monk pebbles]] are used to obtain titles from the [[Tower of the Four Winds]].  First, you must obtain access to the tower.  Then you can begin collecting the pebbles.  The pebbles can be obtained through the different trials available in the tower, which come in a Solo or a Heroic variety (the Heroic versions grant more pebbles).  Monk Pebbles can also be dropped by mobs inside the trials, although this is rare.  The trials each have their own 3-day success lockout timer.

Once you have enough pebbles to purchase a title, visit [[Brother Huang Mantian]] in the first floor of the tower.

**Trials by Floor**

1. [[Trial of Qin Lo: Solo (Quest)|Trial of Qin Lo: Solo]] (5 pebbles) / [[Trial of Qin Lo: Heroic (Quest)|Trial of Qin Lo: Heroic]] (10 pebbles)
1. [[Trial of Bo Fen]] / [[Trial of Lin Vo]]
1. [[Trial of Sagacious Ting]] / [[Trial of Jiang Anlan]]
1. [[Trial of Sun Kai]] / [[Trial of Li Zhong]] (*Heroic only*)
