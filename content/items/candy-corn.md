## Notes

- Candy Corn acts as a currency to buy some of the items sold by [[A Nights of the Dead merchant|Nights of the Dead merchants]].
- All [[Nights of the Dead]]  recipes, like those found in [[Celebrations of the Dead]], require a treat dropped from monsters during the celebrations. This item counts as a treat.
  - To learn more about the various treats that can be used for crafting and find tips and tricks to gather more, see the [[Nights of the Dead Treats (Crafting Materials)]] page.
- Candy Corn can be obtained by various means. One simple way to get candy corn is to trade in various [[Nights of the Dead Treats (Crafting Materials)|various treats]] to [[Grabby Gigglegibber]]. Monsters all over Norrath drop the treats during the festival.
- You can also get Candy Corn by completing [[Nights of the Dead]] quests. [[Ghost Hunting]] in [[Antonica]] and [[Commonlands]] can reward several Candy Corn per race.
