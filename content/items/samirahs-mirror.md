## Notes

**Escape of the Djinn Master* transports players (within its range) to the plaza in [[The Shimmering Citadel]] directly between the [[Scriptorium]] and the mirror to [[The Poets Palace]]. It only works if you are in [[The Pillars of Flame]], [[Maj'Dul]] or [[The Sinking Sands]].
