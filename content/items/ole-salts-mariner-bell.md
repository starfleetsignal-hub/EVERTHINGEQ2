## Notes

See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity does not have a [[Guild Level]] minimum.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

This amenity functions like [[Travel in Norrath|mariner's bell found on docks]], offering more specific locations within cities than the globe. For example, you can travel directly to the [[Qeynos Capitol District]] instead of just the harbor.

- Fun fact: this bell looks like the original bells that were replaced with the travel updates that changed the look to a less worn version.
