## Book Text

**by the Djinn and for the Djinn**

As with most poems by the djinn, credit is not given to the individual writer.<br>Many djinn prefer the stylized short poetic form, especially the ones which end with an ironic twist.

**"Reflections"**

Reflections in the mirror<br>
Through which I see the past<br>
But do reflections bind me<br>
To a house that's made of glass?

The sky was once our homeland<br>
Our refuge and domain<br>
Now those lands are closed to us<br>
But reflections still remain

Our challenge is a hard one<br>
Our power must not wane<br>
We would not have it said of us,<br>
"Only their reflections remained."

**"Short Forms"**

Though the djinn love poetry in all its forms, the short form is a favorite.

Sometimes, the djinn will converse in this short form to see who can carry on the longest conversations without lapse.

Two snakes slithered by<br>
One smiled at me, and so<br>
I have let him live

Do you find that there<br>
Are more stars in the night sky<br>
Since the Rending?

"Truly," said the man<br>
"Have I been blessed in life; but..."<br>
"What blessing is death?"

Give me your hand, friend<br>
I will fill it with riches<br>
Do not sink with them
