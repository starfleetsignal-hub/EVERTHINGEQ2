**City Tokens** may be sold to [[City Festival#Festival Merchants|festival merchants]] for festive rewards or sold to [[Status items#Where to Sell Status Items|status merchants]] for status points for both you and your guild.
If a city token is sold to a status merchant you will receive 250 status and will provide 25 status to any [[Guild]] under level 95 (for leveling) **OR** coin only 1g 25s.
To be able to sell a city token to a status merchant it must be in your inventory and not your currency window. To remove city tokens from your currency window left click on the city token icon and drag it to an empty bag slot.
City tokens do not have to be in your inventory to be sold to festival merchants.
**Note:** Not all faction merchants will offer status for City Tokens. Below is a list of merchants who will award you faction for selling them City Tokens.

- [[Guild Hall Amenities#General|Commodities Exporter Hireling]]
