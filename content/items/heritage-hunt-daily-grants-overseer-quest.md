## Notes

- *For many years, this quest scroll was bugged and the quest could be run infinitely once the initial scroll was purchased from [[Arabella F'arquharson]].*
- As of June 2026, the "1 charge" property of this quest is now adhered to by the Overseer system. After the initial purchase, players are now properly awarded an additonal copy of the charged mission scroll into inventory upon completion of the mission, allowing them to continue the quest indefinitely as normal, but with the added step of having to actually scribe the new charge of the mission between each 24hr run.
