## Notes

This can be obtained in one of two ways:

- It is an 11 Year Veteran Reward, but the free deed is limited to **one** per account.
- It can be purchased from the [[Marketplace]] using Daybreak Cash (real world money) at any time.
  - The second option allows for ownership of multiples of this house per account, but like all other [[Prestige Housing]], it is one (of its type) per character.
- Redeeming the above grants access to the [[Isle of Refuge Prestige Home]]; see the details on how this works on the [[Prestige Housing]] page.
