## Notes

If you have completed any Epic 2.0 Quest series you can now purchase the Guise of Pursuit for your alts. This bauble will give you a Cae’Dal illusion and maxes the following factions, as long as you are wearing the illusion.

1. The Circle of the Unseen Hand
1. The Order of Rime
1. Othimir of Velious
1. Zou’Lidelas
1. Disciples of Circling Destiny
1. Bastion of the Tranquil
1. Shields of Maldura
1. Clan Skleross
1. Legion of Danak
1. The Sel’Nok Brigade
1. Guktan Guard

This illusion can be cancelled, so that quest lines associated with these factions can still be done if desired. You may purchase the Guise of Pursuit from Sarya Val’Sara in Nye’Caelona.<br>
Only a char that has finished the Epic 2.0 Quest series can purchase it, but it's Heirloom so you can give it to alts via shared bank. If you wanna give it to chars on a other server you have to either do all the factions there as well or give it to a char and use a [[Server Transfer Token]].
