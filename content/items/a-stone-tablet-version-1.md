When examined, the stone tablet reads:

This is a stone tablet of great density. There are words scratched into the tablet. The script is not a normal one seen in Norrath. They appear to be words of pure magic.

When you try and decipher the words:

We have located the correct stone monolith of Tumek'Un. The creature will not speak to us.  We shall acquire more glyphed stones and hide them within the urns of ashes around the statue cave.  The stones will call the Toht. We shall hide and await the expedition. Am sending Akdalin back to plains in flight.
