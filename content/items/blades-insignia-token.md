## Notes

This can be turned in to a Consul at [[The Tower of the Moon]] for 250 faction with either the Court of Coin or the Court of Truth.
All insignia tokens from Maj'dul can be sold to other players on the [[Broker]].
If you buy tokens off the broker make sure they are from one of the two factions you are *not* trying to increase faction with before you turn them in to, since all three are opposed to each other.
