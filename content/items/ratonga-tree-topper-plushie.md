## Notes

- This plushie is an homage to a very special departed player, Cloudrat, who was one of the guild leaders on the [[Server Types|Public Test Server]]'s guild, Heroes of Test.
- Cloudrat was responsible for setting up and maintaining numerous [[Items that teleport|travel hubs with items that teleport]] for players to use via the [[Housing & Leaderboards]] system.
