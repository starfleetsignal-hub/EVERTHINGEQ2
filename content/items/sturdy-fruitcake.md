## Notes

Has an animation of you throwing the fruitcake, and it causing the target to fall to their knees (sort of like a Feign Death) or to emote a soundless "gutcramp" or "wince."  Sturdy Fruitcakes can only be crafted while the Frostfell Live event is active, but can be stored/carried year-round for use. After being hit, the target will receive a Sturdy Fruitcake.
