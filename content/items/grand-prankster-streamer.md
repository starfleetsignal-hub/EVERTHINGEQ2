## Notes

- This item can be purchased **only** during [[Bristlebane Day]] from an one of many merchants throughout the world. See the [[A Bristlebane Day merchant]] for a list of merchants to visit.
- This item requires an unlock before you can buy it. To unlock it, complete [[A Plethora of Pranks]], which is an [[Achievements|achievement]] that must be completed during the live event.
