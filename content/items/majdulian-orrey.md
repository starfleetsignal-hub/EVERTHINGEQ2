## Notes

See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity does not have a [[Guild Level]] minimum.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

This is a very large amenity that looks like a globe with astronomy equipment attacked to it.

- It is animated and rotates.
- It is identical to the orrey seen in [[The Tower of the Moon]] in [[Maj'Dul]].
- This is one of the few strictly decorative statues that can be resized and moved around the guild halls.
