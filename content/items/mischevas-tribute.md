## Notes

- This is one of *two* special currencies during the event.
- Mischeva's Tribute is a currency that can only be gained from completing quests given by [[Roslyn Mischeva]] during the annual event, [[Heroes' Festival Timeline|Heroes' Festival]].
- The Tributes are dropped after defeating "patchwork" bosses across Norrath. These battles are difficult and require open-participation in large groups. The Tribute currency [Loot DB first appeared](http://www.lootdb.com/eq2/item/2001428185) in Norrath in 2014.
- **Use**

These tokens may be used to purchase [[Heroes' Festival Merchant Items|special event-themed items]] from [[A Heroes' Festival merchant|Heroes' Festival merchants]].

- The Tribute currency is used to buy limited house items and is primarily used to by powerful weapons and armor, while the other currency ([[Token of Heroism]]) is from completing simple solo quests.
