## Notes

The prequel quest is the [[The Aqua's Aura]], which requires that it be done in each of the other vasty deep zones first.  So, if you are simply going to Cella for the first time (for example to get the [[Epic Repercussions]] update) you won't be able to take [[A Sortie Of Sorts]].  Therefore, if you want the head, plan on doing all three zones.
