## Book Text

**"Nhjela's Poem," by Nhjela, poet-in-residence of the Poet's<br>Palace in the Shimmering Citadel.
**

In our sleep we wander<br>Away to distant shores<br>Our eyes see things they've never seen before<br>We hear beautiful music<br>Speak a different tongue<br>In dreams, we do not walk, we do not run<br>We fly

Wings of night enfold us<br>Letting dreamers soar<br>Far over the fields and through the sky<br>Higher than the mountains<br>Over sleeping seas<br>Graceful as a night owl on the wing<br>We fly

Rising high above all<br>Arms flung open wide<br>Caressed by the cold wind that rushes by<br>Silent as a soft rain<br>Swift as waterfalls<br>Dark as shadowed pools beneath the moons<br>We fly

Tumbling...ever soaring<br>Turning...ever dreaming<br>Day turns to dusk, then to night, then to dawn<br>And we fly

Golden wings enfold me<br>And my spirits rise<br>High above the hills and over shores<br>Gentle is the morning<br>Waking in the sky<br>A citadel of love is what we've found<br>And we fly
