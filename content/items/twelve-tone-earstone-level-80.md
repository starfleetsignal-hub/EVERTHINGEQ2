## Notes

- The Silence's Echo effect is automatically conferred upon a hit of [[Night Strike VII]] or [[Misfortune's Kiss VII]] and lasts for 24 seconds.  The effects include:
  - Increases Casting Speed of caster by 25%
  - Increases Melee Weapon Range of caster by 100%
  - Increases range of all combat arts by up to 15m.
