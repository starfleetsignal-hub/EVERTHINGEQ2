## Notes

- The items in this recipe book can be crafted *all* year, provided you have materials from the [[Bountiful Brewday garden|Bountiful Brewday gardens]] that are *only* visible during [[Brew Day]] when you equip the [[Improved Ale Goggles]], a reward if you complete the quest, [[Ale Goggles]].
- If you run out of the holiday-themed materials, check the [[Broker]].
