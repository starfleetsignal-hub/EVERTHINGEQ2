## Notes

Once you completed the Kunark Ascending Timeline for adventurers and have completed the [[Frontier Tamer]], [[The Frillik Tide (Achievement)]] and [[Kunark Ascending (Achievement)]] achievements, it's possible to purchase this "The Essential Kunark Guide" - Alt Bauble from [[Sarya Val'Sara]].  You do not need the [[Underdepths Saga]] achievement.
This Bauble gives alts:

- Grants access to train with all Ascension Masters
- Grants the ability to utilize Dalnir's work chest key - *which means you don't need to do [[Greenmist Timeline|Greenmist HQ Timeline]] for the signature quest, but you have to do it for the proc!*
- **Grants understating of the following languages:**
- [[Di'Zokian]]
- [[Draconic]]
- [[Sathirian]]
- [[Froak]]
