## Notes

- There are many theories on what exactly needs to be in your inventory when hailing Gynok. You will need to have either the [[Bone Bladed Claymore]] or the [[Electrified Bone Bladed Claymore]]. You will also need the book [[The History of Gynok Moltor]] or the [[Paw of Opolla]] from completing the heritage quest [[A Source of Malediction]] (exactly which is needed is unknown).
