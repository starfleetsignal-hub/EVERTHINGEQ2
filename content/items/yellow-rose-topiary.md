## Notes

This item is from [[Romantic Gifts to Craft VII]]. The recipe yields 3 total when successfully crafted and 1-2 otherwise.

- The Hard Candy or Love Notes needed are only available during [[Erollisi Day]].
- Though it is made at a [[Work Bench]] it can be made by anyone with at least level 10 [[Artisan]] fairly easily.
- As long as you have or can buy the Hard Candy or Love Notes, it can be crafted all year.

## Supplies

- 3 [[Basic Coal]]
- 1 of either: [[Erollisi Day Hard Candy and Love Notes]]
- 1 [[Root (harvest)]]
- 1 [[Lead cluster]]
