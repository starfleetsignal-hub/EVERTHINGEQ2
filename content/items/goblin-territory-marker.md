## Notes

This page refers to the inventory-only, No-Trade items that drops randomly. To avoid confusion, an explanation of a similar visible item is explained here too.

- Examining the first one of these opens the quest [[Claiming the Goblish Tongue]], which can be completed by one of two methods. You can kill more goblins until they randomly drop enough markers (this can be time consuming) **or** by clicking on actual statues while on working on [[Kunark Ascending Crafting Timeline]].
- Collecting all of these completes [[Claiming the Goblish Tongue]] and grants knowledge of the [[Goblish]] language, which is required for many quests.
- *See the [[Claiming the Goblish Tongue]] page for more tips and tricks on completing this task by either method*

### Confirmed Drop Locations

The following goblin tribes drop the markers:

- Blood Bone goblins in [[Stormhold]]
- Necrosis goblins in [[The Condemned Catacomb]] and in [[Antonica]] near the Catacomb entrance
- Aqua Goblins in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
- Gleam and Glimmer Goblins near the back door of [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] and outside in [[The Thundering Steppes]] by that door
- Mist Grinnin goblins in [[Tombs of Night]]
- Runnyeye goblins in Enchanted Lands and [[Runnyeye]]
- Fog Grimmin goblins along the coast in [[Enchanted Lands]]
- Sootfoot goblins in [[Lavastorm]]
- Cinderfoot goblins in [[Solusek's Eye]]
- Icegill goblins in [[Permafrost]]

## Alternate Completion Method

For the [[Kunark Ascending Crafting Timeline]], players can examine tiny, visible statues to complete the quest, [[Claiming the Goblish Tongue]]. In order to do so, at least one of these body-drop markers must be obtained and examined.
