## Notes

1. Hail the [[NPC|New Combine Flightmaster]] at {{waypoint 1277.37, -540.25, 304.83}} on the [[Great Divide]] dock to travel to [[The Withered Lands]].
   - Alternate access to [[The Withered Lands]] via [[NPC|a Combine griffin trainer]] in [[Cobalt Scar]].
1. Hail [[NPC|a Combine horse trainer]] at {{waypoint -3539.15, -781.47, -61.09}} to travel to [[Alivan]].
   - To unlock this location for travel it requires you to first hail the horse at [[Alivan]]
1. Hail the [[NPC|Dracurion Flight Master]] at {{waypoint -556.76, -685.68, 363.52}} to travel to [[Skyshrine: The City of Dracur]]
   - To return to [[The Withered Lands]] hail [[NPC|a covert shadow-talon drake]] at {{waypoint -13.01, 1.47, -14.39}}
1. Enter the [[Skyshrine: The Forbidden City of Dracur]] portal at {{waypoint -34.02, -0.41, -8.52}}
   - Requires +50,000 [[Claws of Veeshan (Faction)]] for this portal to appear.
   - Completion of [[Skyshrine Timeline]] quests required.
1. Purchase the [[Pennant of Skyshrine Loyalty]] from [[Quartermaster Zrek]] at {{waypoint 49.02, -0.98, -295.72}}
