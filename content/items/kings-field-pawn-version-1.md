## Notes

You will need to collect four Pawns to place on the chessboard in the "Games Room" in order to spawn [[Screwloose the Mad Tinkerer]].

The Pawns can be found in or around these places:

- In the fountain, near the zone-in at {{waypoint 8, 0.73, 1.55}}
- In the moat, close to [[Jessa Quellborn]] at {{waypoint 100, -10, 138}}
- In the kitchen, on the shelves
- Near a glass on the bar near [[The Sadistic Bartender]]
