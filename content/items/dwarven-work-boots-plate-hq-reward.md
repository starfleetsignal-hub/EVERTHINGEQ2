## Notes

### Multiple Versions

There are 4 different boots available, one for each armor type, but the quest rewards only one (apparently at random). To change the one you got for the one you want, see the [[Shady Swashbuckler]]. He appears in several places but the easiest to get to is on the docks in [[Nektulos Forest]]. Sell him your boots, then buy the one you want.

### House Item

Examining this equipment, while it is in your inventory and not equipped, gives you the option to turn it into a house item called, [[The Legendary Dwarven Work Boots]].
