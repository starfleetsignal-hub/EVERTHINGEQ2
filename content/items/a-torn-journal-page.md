## Notes

When examined, it displays:

This appears to be one of the missing pages from Ernie Gimblelock's journal.  It is torn and smudged.

When you read what you can of the page, it displays:

I have learned that the Amygdalan statues are a place of sacrifice.  I watched as one beast was placed upon the dais and sacrificed in the name of Cazic-Thule.  He almost seemed proud of the act about to be carried out.
