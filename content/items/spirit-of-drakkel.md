## Notes

- After you have completed all eight [[Legend Hunting]] quests on the Kael Drakkel server, your account will be flagged so you can purchase the adornment for your characters on other servers.
- This adornment cannot be purchased on new TLE servers as of the opening of the Wuoshi server on June 9, 2026.
- You can purchase the adornment from the Lore Token Merchant [[Ronald Gleneed]] in East Freeport {{waypoint -108, -38, 66}} or Qeynos Harbor {{waypoint 905, -25, 77}} at no cost. This merchant also offers a limited selection of cosmetic items.
- The bonuses granted by the adornment scale based on level. On TLE servers, the Ability Doublecast and Fervor bonuses are minimal, and there are no Crit Bonus Overcap or Weapon Damage Overcap bonuses.
- The adornment has no effect on PvP servers.
- You must have an [[All Access Membership]] to create a character on the Kael Drakkel server and complete the [[Legend Hunting]] quests.
