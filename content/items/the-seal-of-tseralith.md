## Notes

- Looting this item is an alternative method of entry into [[The Vault of the Fallen]], an instance within [[Fallen Gate]]. The other method is completion of the quest [[Searching the Depths of Fallen Gate]].
- Immediately upon looting, the item will disappear and in-game text will appear in your chat window stating: "The seal crumbles to dust and the door to the Vault of the Fallen is open to you."
