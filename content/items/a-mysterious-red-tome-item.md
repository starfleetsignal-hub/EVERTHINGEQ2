## Book Text

As translated from The Di'Zokian Language, which is required
in order to read the Tome.

[Page 1]<br>
BLANK

[Page 2]**The  edict of Di'Zok Leader Warlord Kel'Nor**

[Page 3]<br>
It is Warlord Kel'Nor's opinion that any aspiring military strategist needs to know the following terms.

[Page 4]<br>
**- Adversary** - Any person, animal, or party that refuses to follow Warlord Kel'Nor's orders.<br>
**- Ballistic** - A Ballistic is anything that Warlord Kel'Nor wants fired in the air at any adversary.<br>
**- Cache** - A cache is a supply of items that Warlord Kel'Nor feels are necessary. Like a cache of ballistics for throwing at an adversary.<br>
**- Dazzle** - A form of attacking with something like fire or spells that can blind the adversary. These are very effective when attached to the cache Warlord Kel'Nor's ballistics.

[Page 5]<br>
**- Emplacement** - A prepared position for attacking from or repairing any ballistics under Warlord  Kel'Nor's command.

**- Fallout** - Particles that can some times break off of ballistics and or pieces of a foot soldier that might have fired Warlord Kel'Nor's weaponry incorrectly.<br>
**- General Orders** - Any standing order that must be followed at all times. For instance, never get in Warlord Kel'Nor's way.<br>
**- Hazard** - A condition that can lead to the causing of injuries due to being in Warlord Kel'Nor's way.

[Page 6]<br>
**- Incident** - What they will tell your family happened after you got in Warlord Kel'Nor's way.<br>
**- Joint** - An operation that contains activities in which two or more divisions are under Warlord Kel'Nor's control.<br>
**- Key Terrain** - Any location that Warlord Kel'Nor decides to take, that will grant him an advantage.<br>
**- Liaison** - The liaison is the only person that Warlord Kel'Nor will allow to travel between his camps to make sure that everyone is following his orders.

[Page 7]<br>
**- Manifest** - A list usually carried by the liaison that can contain specifics of Warlord Kel'Nor's ballistics.<br>
**- Neutral** - An opponent of Warlord Kel'Nor's that may not be considered directly an Adversary.<br>
**- Objective** - The goal which Warlord Kel'Nor is fighting towards.<br>
**- Pace** - The pace is the speed with which Warlord Kel'Nor is moving towards his objective.

[Page 8]<br>
**- Quick Response Force** - Warlord Kel'Nor's special team that handles his special assignments.<br>
**- Raid** -An operation usually run by the Quick Response Force that is a temporary seizure that may be necessary to obtain Warlord Kel'Nor's objective.<br>
**- Sabotage** - An act which the Quick Response Force is known for doing after a raid has taken place.<br>
**- Target** - A target is a designated area or object that has been chosen by Warlord Kel'Nor.

[Page 9]<br>
**- Unit** - Warlord Kel'Nor commands many small units that make up a larger unit.<br>
-** Validate** - Units have to be validated before they can join Warlord Kel'Nor's command.<br>
**- Warning** - Knowledge of impending danger. The sound of Warlord Kel'Nor's foot steps will be all the warning you need to get out of his way.<br>
**- X** - Marks the spot to where Warlord Kel'Nor has planned his offensive.

[Page 10]<br>
**- Yield** - A yield is what is attained from Warlord Kel'Nor's directions. Raids are expected to have high yields.<br>
**- Zone of Action** - An area decided by Warlord Kel'Nor that is sub-divided from a larger area, where an offensive action will take place.
