## Notes

- This recipe book is obtained by typing /claim, but it is available only to those who purchased either the Collectors or Premium edition of Terrors of Thalumbra.
- Though the recipes are level 1, the materials to craft the items come from the area outside of [[Maldura]].
- The items match the [[Prestige Housing|prestige house]] that was also included with the Collectors and Premium editions: [[Dhalgar Precipice of the Deep]].
