## Book Text

We Will Be Free Again!<br>
A Froglok Unity Song

This is a song that all Frogloks know.  It has been with our people for thousands of years, since the time we were first enslaved by the foul Sebilisians.  It unites us, and steels us in our darkest hours.  Many a Froglok has been beaten to near death for singing the song in the face of a captor, but in response, all other Frogloks will lift their voices to a higher swell.  They may take our freedom, but they will never take our song!

They came in the night with their hordes of swordsmen,<br>
Oh ho!  Oh ho!<br>
To slay and enslave these valiant freemen,<br>
Oh ho!  Oh ho!<br>
And though we stood and fell in the name of valor,<br>
Oh ho... Oh ho...<br>
They slapped us in chains in that most fateful hour.<br>
Oh ho... Oh ho...<br>

And the mother Frogloks cry!<br>
And the childer Frogloks scream!<br>
And they march us to our fates,<br>
Through their iron shod gates.<br>
Oh ho... Oh ho...<br>

But though the nights be long and dark,<br>
Oh ho... Oh ho...<br>
And our chambers dank and stark,<br>
Oh ho... Oh ho...<br>
We will not lose hope and weep,<br>
Oh ho!  Oh ho!<br>
For Mithaniel guides us through the deep.<br>
Oh ho!  Oh ho!<br>

And though the mother Frogloks cry!<br>
And though the childer Frogloks scream!<br>
Mithaniel steels us with his light,<br>
In the guidance of the right.<br>
Oh ho!  Oh ho!<br>

Forgive your captor, brother Froglok,<br>
Though he may beat you til you bleed.<br>
For he knows not the comforts of Mithaniel,<br>
And will face great punishments for his deeds.<br>
Oh ho... Oh ho...<br>

Listen to the mother Frogloks cry!<br>
Hear the childer Frogloks scream!<br>
And let our people's suffering,<br>
Drive our spirits to rise again.<br>
Oh ho!  Oh ho!<br>

Long comes the day of liberation...<br>
Oh ho... Oh ho...<br>
Some days an impossible destination...<br>
Oh ho... Oh ho...<br>
But when Frogloks rise and cast their shackles to the breeze!<br>
Oh ho!  Oh ho!<br>
Let the wicked bow and tremble upon their cursed knees!<br>
Oh ho!  Oh ho! <br>

And let the mother Frogloks cheer!<br>
And let the childer Frogloks sing!<br>
For the Frogloks will be free again!<br>
And the right shall know its ken!<br>
Oh ho!  Oh ho!<br>

(All together)<br>
This is the right of the Froglok<br>
This is the due of the brave<br>
Though this day may end in misery<br>
We will be free again!<br>
(Solo singer)<br>
Aye we will be free again!<br>

(all together)<br>
Yes, we will be free again!<br>
Let evil tremble on its knees!<br>
For we will be free again!<br>
We will be free again...<br>
We Will!  Be Free!  Again!<br>
Oh ho!
