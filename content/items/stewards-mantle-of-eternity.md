## Notes

- The beneficial effects of Dauntless Devotion all have a duration of 15 minutes.
- The specific effect is selected randomly each time the cloak is used.
- The individual beneficial effects are as are as follows:
  - **Steward's Fervor**: Increases WIS and INT of caster by 200.0
  - **Steward's Fealty**: Increases Potency of Caster by 12.0%
  - **Steward's Zeal**: Increases the Ability Modifier of the caster by 1,500
  - **Steward's Adoration**: Increases the Critical Bonus of the caster by 12.0%
