## Notes

When worn these goggles allow the wearer to see [[Brew Day]] harvest nodes.

The harvest nodes look like shrubs and are called **bountiful Brewday garden**

The [[Bountiful Brewday garden|bountiful Brewday garden's]] can be found in the following zones:

- [[Antonica]]
- [[Commonlands]]
- [[Greater Faydark]]
- [[Darklight Wood]]
- [[Timorous Deep]]
- [[Frostfang Sea]]
