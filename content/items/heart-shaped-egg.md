You will receive this egg the first time you receive best reward crate [[Love Will Lead You Back Proactive Participant Reward Crate]] from completing [[Love Will Lead You Back]] Public Quest.

To hatch this egg and receive the familiar:

- Keep completing [[Love Will Lead You Back]]  Public Quest  in  [[The Commonlands]] and **do enough to earn the best reward crate** [[Love Will Lead You Back Proactive Participant Reward Crate]].
  - the lesser reward crates [[Love Will Lead You Back Active Participant Reward Crate]] and [[Love Will Lead You Back Passive Participant Crate]] do not advance hatching.
- After you finish each PQ and earn Proactive crate examine your egg.
- It will take at least 5 completed PQs where you earned Proactive crate. No upper limit, after 5 PQs it's random when your egg hatches
- So grab those yellow and red roses and hand them to one of the undead lovers plus move along with PQ and help with purple, black roses and attackers.
  - Tip: a combination of about 18 hand-ins, along with squashing 2 purple and 2 black roses, and killing 2 attackers (by using roses on them) consistently yielded Proactive reward in testing. Show up to PQ early for best chance of  being proactive enough.
