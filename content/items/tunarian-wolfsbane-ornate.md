## Notes

- Wolfsbane is used during the fight against [[Mayong Mistmoore]] in [[Mistmoore's Inner Sanctum]] to prevent wolf adds from spawning. He will say something like "get that foul plant out of my sight..." about one minute into the fight. At that point he will select a random person among those that have a Wolfsbane in their inventory and take (destroy) it. You will need one Tunarian Wolfsbane for each pull and kill attempt that lasts longer than this.
- There are three sources of the Tunarian Wolfsbane:
  - Players can receive it as a quest reward from the last quest in the Tunare Deity Quest Line [[Faith (Quest)|Faith]]. You will receive one wolfsbane, and this can not be repeated.
  - Groups defeating [[Wuoshi]] will find five Tunarian Wolfsbanes. This can be repeated.
  - Groups defeating [[Malkonis D'Morte (Freethinker Hideout)|Malkonis D'Morte]] will get three Tunarian Wolfsbanes. This can be repeated.
