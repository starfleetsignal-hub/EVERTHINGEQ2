Completing any of the 6 treasured timed quests which reward certain amount of encrusted planar key. Unpacking these keys will reward following:
One of the following keys:

- [[A polished elemental stone key]] (common, roughly 90% chance)
- [[A polished sandstorm pearl key]] (uncommon, roughly 10% chance)
- [[Polished Magma Crystal Key]] (rare, roughly 1-2% chance)

AND a [[Moonstone crystal fragments]] (can be sold to guild commodities vendor or city merchant for Status Points)
