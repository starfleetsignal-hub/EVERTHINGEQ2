from [[Burnt Recipe Page 5]]
Crafting this requires:

- 1 [[Brick of Bronze Ore]]
- 1 [[Brick of Silvril Ore]]

- 2 [[Brick of Iron Ore|Bricks of Iron Ore]]
- 1 [[Magmatic Crystal]]
- 5 [[Smoldering coal|smoldering coals]][1]

All of these items can be looted in [[The Deep Forge]] or bought from broker. The item in question is crafted in the forge room of [[The Deep Forge]].
[1]These can be bought from an NPC near the forge for 3g 33s.
