## Notes

[[Grant Overseer Quest: Heritage Hunt (Daily)|Grant Overseer Quest: Heritage Hunt [Daily]]] item requires:

- Membership
- Character level 120
- Overseer level 10

As an Overseer quest, it is account-wide and, though lists only one charge, is (currently) repeatable with a permanent x1 charge in the Overseer mission list. This mission does not count against the daily mission quota.

This item will not show up on the vendor if you currently have the related Overseer mission in your list.<br>
<sub> *This mechanic is similar to NPCs who give Overseer missions - you cannot obtain the mission (or initiate relevant dialogue) while some part of the mission chain is already active/on cooldown in your Overseer mission list*</sub>
