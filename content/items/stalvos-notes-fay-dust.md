## Text

*Sub-component: Fay Dust:*

*Fay dust is quite miraculous, it has natural healing properties and is also imbued with small traces of magic. In theory this means unlimited potential. In practice it means it can be used as a sort of 'power source' when unlocking the latent properties of other substances. It can be scraped quite easily from the dried wings of a dead fairy or sprite.*
