[[Shadow brute]]s can appear at Void Tempests in any zone. Their level, and accordingly corpse drops, scale to the tier of the zone they are in. This was part of a [[World Event]] for [[LU46]], but the weapons can be purchased from Loyalty Merchants in Qeynos and East Freeport using [[Loyalty Point Token|Loyalty Point Tokens]].
