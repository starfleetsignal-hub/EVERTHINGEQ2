## Notes

This stone will NOT teleport you anywhere, ever!

- This item is what you will find in your inventory *after* you consume the [[Yelnar's Research Prism]] charm.
- While the prism is a simple diamond-shaped icon in your inventory, the icon for this apprentice is a bright blue bubble.
- It is very easy to accidentally equip the prism in your character's charm slot while trying to convert it into this apprentice, so if you do not find the stone, check your character's charm slots of the prism.
- Once placed in your home it becomes the [[Tradeskill Apprentices|tradeskill apprentice]], [[Apprentice Elder Yelnar (Dracurion Mastercraftsman)]].
