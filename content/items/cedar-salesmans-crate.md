## Notes

If you are not familiar with [[Salesman's Crate]], you may want to read about the general topic of [[Sales Display|sales displays]] that can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]].

- All salesman's crates are Master Crafted (made from a rare material) by those who take up the [[Carpenter]] tradeskill. No matter which tier of rare wood is used to make any salesman's crate they all look the same when placed.
- Anything that can be sold on the [[Broker]] can be placed in this type of Sale's Display because it is a general-use sales container.
