## Note

This is acquired during, and required for, the [[Warlock Epic Weapon Timeline]] quest, [[The Will of Kyrtoxxulous]].

## Lore

"It would take several hours to digest the entirety of this book, however there is one passage you take note of that catches your interest."

""Followers of the Pestilence Lord often cite tribute as an effective way to gain favor with Kyrtoxxulous. Diseased or poisoned corpses, idols and bowls of spoilt food are often placed around makeshift shrines to show a demonstration of faith. Prayers of divine intervention on behalf of the devotee should be made daily at these shrines until an answer is given.""
