## Notes

- This does not work with the orange celestial gate adorn wearing both will cancel this buff.
- This is the buffs description of the adorn when you wear it not the tooltip one you get when you first get the adorn. This is a more precise and accurate description of what it does:
  - On a combat or spell hit this spell may cast Leach on caster.  Lasts for 2.0 seconds.  Triggers about 3.0 times per minute.
    - Inflicts between 3 and 4 times the casters current health in damage on target.
    - Heals the caster for 10-15% of Max Health
    - Cannot be modified except by direct means
    - This effect can only trigger once every 30.0 - 45.0 seconds.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means
