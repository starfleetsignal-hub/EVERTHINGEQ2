## Notes

- You must be in [[The Sundered Frontier]] for this item to work.
- This item also acts as a red-colored torch

I'm not sure whether this item has changed or not.  The current status of the item is not the way I remember it though.  Here's what it looks like today (click to see full size):
![Three items, leaf, gift, and the skill in my knowledge book.](images/Jintus_leaf_gift_and_skill.jpg)
My recollection is that you broke off a piece each time you wanted to travel and you used the 1-charge item to travel.  Now you use the base item and the "break off a piece" item gives you the skill permanently.  So I guess we no longer need to carry it.

Once you click the item, it becomes an ability.  Check your ability tab, and click there to complete.
