## Notes

![Female dark elf before and after drinking Holy Moly Water](images/Holy_Moly_Water_result.jpg)
Right-click the placed bottle to drink it.  It will cast *Holy Moly Water* on you, giving you the illusion of a random player character race.  In ugly clothes. And possibly gender-changed.
