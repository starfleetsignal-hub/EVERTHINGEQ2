## Notes

These tokens may be used to purchase [[Heroes' Festival Merchant Items|special event-themed items]] from [[A Heroes' Festival merchant|Heroes' Festival merchants]].

- This is one of *two* special currencies during the event. This currency is used to buy most (but not all) of the [[Housing|house]] items.
  - This type is an option for completing regular quests, while the other ([[Mischeva's Tribute]]) is from defeating the "patchwork" bosses and completing the various achievements. The Tribute currency is used to buy limited house items and is primarily used to by powerful weapons and armor.
