## Notes

- Qho is the NPC that many people love to hate, because he offers a *very long* quest series that can be extremely time-consuming if his quest is the only one you are working on in a given zone. For this reason, it's wise to visit him in The [[Village of Shin]] where he swims in a pond at {{waypoint -13.25, 9.87, -108.48}} as soon as you create a new character; doing so will make it easy to stop and harvest (the purpose of most of his quests) as you progress through various zones for adventure quests.
- Qho's quests do have rewards that are of great benefit to those who take up a tradeskill or who like to harvest though. They include items that provide harvesting bonuses like:
  - The [[Cloak of the Harvester]], the [[Artisan's Pack Pony]], and the [[Artisan's Fully Trained Pack Pony]].
- A grown-up Qho is also available as a non-elite, fighter mercenary when you have completed [[A Gathering Obsession Beyond The Grave]]. Find him wandering around {{waypoint -256, 0, -82}} in [[The Village of Shin]]. See the [[Qho Augren (Mercenary)]] page for details.
