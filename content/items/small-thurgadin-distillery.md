## Notes

- Though this *can* be placed in a [[Housing|home]] or [[Guild Halls|guild hall]] as it is, it can *also* be used to craft the larger, more ornate [[Mechanized Thurgadin Distillery]] (and then [[Super Mechanized Thurgadin Distillery]]) from the recipe book, [[Brewday High-tech Distilleries]].
- The quest this item is from can be repeated annually.
