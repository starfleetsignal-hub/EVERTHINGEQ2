## Notes

This prism can be used to access a [[Skyshrine Tradeskill Apprentices|skyshrine tradeskill apprentice]]. The apprentice has a variety of recipes, but a few recipes are for [[Housing|furniture]] items with high [[Rent Status Reduction]], making them highly desirable to players who enjoy [[Decorating Houses|decorating houses]].

### Warning!

This item has two phases in your inventory that eventually grants one [[Tradeskill Apprentices|tradeskill apprentice]]. The steps are easily confused and the items are easy to misplace.
**Do not click on and "use" the Prism to make the Teleportation Stone it if you want to sell or trade the Prism!**
It is very easy to accidentally equip this in your characters charm slot, so if it goes missing while trying to use it, check your character's charm equipment slots for it.

### How to Obtain

The encounter that rewards this item takes place in the [[Dracur Prime (Advanced Solo)]] instance, it could be difficult to obtain at level, however it can be done at higher level with gray mobs and the prism *is* tradeable, it can be found on the broker occasionally.

In [[Skyshrine: The City of Dracur]] speak to [[Cek'Arctor]] who is pacing around {{waypoint -16, 1, -16}}. Pick either the response "I'm interested... go on." or "Just skip to the task...". Then choose "Tigloth sounds the most dangerous." This will offer you quest [[Collapsing the Capitol]] and prompt [[Cek'Arctor]] to open portal behind him, which will take you to zone called [[Dracur Prime (Advanced Solo)]].

You do not have to complete the zone, but you need to go to {{waypoint -407, 0, 25}} to trigger a fight between a few NPCs, observe the fight and once done target and hail the lone wounded survivor (there is no name or feather above him, but it's Apprentice Overknight Deshniak). Choose the first option to stay and help him fight more attackers coming for him. Be ready for 4 waves of level 93 and 94 mobs to come as soon as you finish conversation.<br>
When combat is done Deshniak will have a name above him, talk to him and he rewards you with [[Deshniak's Research Prism]].

### How to Create the Apprentice

1. Right click the Prism and choose Use from the options.
1. This item now becomes the [[Deshniak's Teleportation Stone]]. It will NOT teleport you anywhere, ever. Instead the stone is what you click on to place [[Apprentice Overknight Deshniak]] in a house to begin the research process.
- Icon changes that can cause confusion:
- The item looks like a simple, red, diamond-shaped stone in your inventory, but the icon switches to a rough red stone icon once you have consumed it.
- If consumed correctly (and not anciently equipped) it grants [[Deshniak's Teleportation Stone]] (rough red stone icon), which is the actual [[Tradeskill Apprentices|tradeskill apprentice]] you can place in a house and use to research various recipes.
