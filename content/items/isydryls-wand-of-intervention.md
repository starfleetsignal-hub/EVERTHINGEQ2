- This item is used against [[Selrach Di'Zok]] in [[The Temple of Kor-Sha]]. When Selrach reaches about 58% health, he begins speaking prepares to cast an AoE. You must use the Wand of Intervention at that time, otherwise it will kill everyone in the raid.
