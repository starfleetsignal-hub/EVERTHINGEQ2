## Arcanna Puzzle

### Notes

You must follow this entire thing, in order. If you make a mistake, you have to start over from the very beginning. The only thing you don't have to re-do, is get new instances, you can re-use your instances.

- If you are on the correct step, you will get an orange message and glow blue.
- If you are not on the correct step, you will NOT get a message, and you will glow red, and you get to start back to [[Arcanna'se Spire: Repository of Secrets (Heroic)]].
- October 2025: You appear to have do all three instances either during the same play session or within 6 hours, as any longer causes you to lose the ability to click on the items. See [this EQ2 forum thread](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/amenophas-livingspirit.576557/), which correlates with my experience trying to complete this puzzle. (I did Forgotten Sanctum the previous evening. I did Repository of Secrets and Vessel of the Sorceress the next day. When I got to the steps in this puzzle inside Forgotten Sanctum, I could not click on any of the items.)

### Steps

1. [[Arcanna'se Spire: Repository of Secrets (Heroic)]]  {{waypoint -66, 12, 168}}
   1. Clear the zone of all the named.
   1. Click on each of the following items:
      1. {{waypoint -69.099998, 3.850000, 35.459999}} - Helm - South circle, in the mortar/pestle room
      1. {{waypoint 11.055545, 8.943695, -145.113480}} - Shield - North, in the room middle of the map, that's square
      1. {{waypoint -42.250000, 4.906156, -73.869995}} - Scepter - in the conservatory-ish area, in the middle of the walkway, on a desk
      1. {{waypoint 152.005356, 4.058685, 26.35832}} - Dagger - South west, in the Patches room, in the left hand corner, in a book on the floor
      1. {{waypoint 4.047741, -1.015934, -86.072929}} - Chest piece- Main entrance room, on the floor behind the statue
1. [[Arcanna'se Spire: Forgotten Sanctum (Heroic)]]
   1. Clear the zone of all the named.
   1. Click on each of the following items:
      1. {{waypoint -389.390015, 9.537946, -63.840000}} - West, purple room, scroll on floor
      1. {{waypoint -555.443054, 10.897234, -91.054527}} - East, green/pestilence room, gloves on ledge.
      1. {{waypoint -544.183044, 13.067001, -183.672119}} - North East, red/fire room, spear in fire pit
      1. {{waypoint -584.930359, -5.770000, 35.231251}} - South East, earth/tree room, book on ledge
      1. {{waypoint -327.226074, -5.772963, 43.783306}} - South West, Lachina/magic room, bowl on ledge
1. [[Arcanna'se Spire: Vessel of the Sorceress (Event Heroic)]]
   1. Clear the zone of all the named.
   1. Click on each of the following items:
      1. {{waypoint -458.342896, 30.580000, -22.569763}} - South, Back of big room, helm on floor
      1. {{waypoint -463.128021, 34.625038, -95.593552}} - Near zone in, armor piece (chest) in the window at entrance
      1. {{waypoint -513.903442, 30.811754, -49.950428}} - East, Ankh on floor
      1. {{waypoint -472.897797, 36.694641, 20.671486}} - South, very back room, flute on ledge

When you click the last one, you should get an achievement reward window pop up right away with the mount.
