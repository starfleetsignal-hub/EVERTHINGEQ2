## Notes

Text when Examining this item:

- The edges of the parchment are torn and jagged as though ripped from a much larger document.
- The parchment looks familiar and you realize it's part of the same note Charles Arker had asked you to track down. You remember the words on the other piece which, together with this one, spell the message: "The horse sentries will ... that we can eliminate the wilderbears ..." Odd. Why would the Tunarians want to eliminate the wilderbears themselves? Perhaps its time to take down a few more Tunarians and find out what they know!
- You carefully roll the parchment and stow it away.

Text when Examining this item after killing 3 [[A Tunarian protector|Tunarian protectors]]:

- The edges of the parchment are torn and jagged as though ripped from a much larger document.
- The message from the parchment scraps is now: "The horse sentries will prove difficult ... we can eliminate the wilderbears to ..." Eliminate the wilderbears to do...what? The note sounds less like a Tunarian plot, but it's obvious there is still more of it to be found. Could the last piece of this note be on one of the Tunarian beastmasters?
- You carefully roll the parchment and stow it away.

Text when Examining this item after receiving the quest update from [[A Tunarian bearmaster]]:

- The edges of the parchment are torn and jagged as though ripped from a much larger document.
- The note says: "The horse sentries will prove difficult to avoid. I hope that we can eliminate the wilderbears ... Charles..." it looks like Charles Arker has some explaining to do. You aren't sure whether he's betraying the trust of the Overlord, or if this note indicates something much simpler.
- You carefully roll the parchment and store it away.
