## Notes

**All of the Heroes' Festival weapons named after the event monsters share identical appearances for all levels, following a pattern. For example, Darathar's Ethos is identical to Trakanon's Ethos and Roehn Theer's Quarrel is identical to Djinn Master's Quarrel.**

- This weapon can only be purchased during the annual [[Heroes' Festival Timeline|Heroes' Festival]] event.
- It is one of many [[Heroes' Festival Merchant Items|Heroes' Festival merchant items]].
- This item can also be equipped and used by Beastlords.
