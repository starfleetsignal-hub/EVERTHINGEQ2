## Note

- This item can be used to craft a [[Mirror of Reflected Achievements]] and [[Guild hall trophy pedestal]].
  - The [[Mirror of Reflected Achievements|mirror]] allows you to change between two Achievement profiles once placed in your home. (As of December 2014 the recipe has been removed from "Reflections of the Grandmasters" recipe book.)
  - The [[Guild hall trophy pedestal|pedestal]] is used to mount [[Trophy|trophies]] looted off of named epics in various Raid Zones.
- The crafter has to buy the recipe [[Reflections of the Grandmasters]] for the [[Mirror of Reflected Achievements|Mirror]] and [[Reflections of the Grandmasters, vol. II]] for the [[Guild hall trophy pedestal|pedestal]] from his tradeskill society which requires 40k Faction with the tradeskill society.
- Note:  The reflective smoldering shard is drop-item from small chests similar to recipe chests in Tier 8 instances like Sebilis.  It is NOT hand-crafted.
