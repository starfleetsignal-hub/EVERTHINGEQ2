The Crunchy Gingerbread Archon comes with a bow tie, smile, and two gumdrop buttons. When he is not calling forth his gumdrop horde, he is healing and dishing out frosting lashings on your mobs. Crunchy Gingerbread Archon is the mercenary to have for the holiday battles.<br>
He was purchased from the Rogues Gallery. It is yet unknown if he will be available after the Frostfell holiday.<br>
To purchase Crunchy, I used the Rogues Gallery vendor. After the purchase and contract, Ientered a new door in the gallery which took me to a Frostfell decorated zone where I met my Crunchy for the first time. His opening line was a joke (literally).
