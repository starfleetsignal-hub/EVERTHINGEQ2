## Notes

- The Harvest Storage Bank Box is created by examining a [[Disassembled Storage Box]] which is a reward from the quest [[Tutorial: Learning to Harvest]].
- The Harvest Storage Bank Box can only hold [[Harvestable]] items.
- This item use to weigh 9999 stone but when item and equipment weight were removed from the game, this item was useful to carry around in your inventory as a normal container; although it can only hold harvestables.
- It is no longer possible to place this item in a bag slot in your inventory. You may carry it (empty) within a bag, but you can not use it as a bag.
