## Notes

This door functions like [[Furniture]]. Once placed in a [[Housing|player-owned house]] it can be used to travel to a [[Guild Hall|guild hall]].

- Anyone who encounters one of these doors can click it, but it will *always* send them to the hall of the [[Guild]] they belong to, *not* the guild hall of the player whose house the door is in/the guild of the person who owns the door.
- Though this is a LORE item and you can only have a single door in your character's personal inventory, you *can* buy multiple doors; this is because players can now own several houses at one time.
- If one of your characters has a great deal of [[Status points|status]], you can place this in the house of another character who has less.
  - To do so, buy it while on the character who has status to spare, log on to the character whose house needs a guild hall door, and set the first character's (used to purchase the door) [[Housing#Access|access to Trustee]].
