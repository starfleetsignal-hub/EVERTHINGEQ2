## Notes

This is one of many prizes you can choose from the races during [[Bristlebane Day]].

- This pet is based on a beloved monster one can battle in Norrath: [[The Meatbeast]].
- Around Bristlebane Day a mount with a similar look can usually be purchased form the [[Marketplace]].
- Beastlords should keep an eye out in various locations in Norrath during Bristlebane Day, as they can tame a special pink version of [[The Meatbeast]].
