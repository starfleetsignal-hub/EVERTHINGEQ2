## Notes

Has an animation of actually throwing a snowball, frequently hitting the other person in the head and causing your character to issue the emote "Sorry!". Snowballs are only available during the [[Frostfell Timeline|Frostfell season]], but many players save them throughout the year to surprise people with.
The snowball harvest nodes are approximately on a 1 one minute timer.
