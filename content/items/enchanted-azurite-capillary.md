## Notes

These are used for the [[Gogas Afadin (Quest)|Gogas Afadin]] quest (subquest of [[The Symbol in the Flesh]] HQ).

If you have sufficient [[Adorning]] skill, you might also wish to provide two [[Illusionary Powder|Illusionary Powders]] (which could come from Tier 7 Treasured, Master Crafted, and Legendary items). If not, consider buying them or paying the Adorner for them.

This combine does not return any resources (i.e. Powders) to the Adorner.

Note: This item is crafted at a Chemistry table.
