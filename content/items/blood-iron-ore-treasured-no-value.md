## Notes

- This is the rare material used in Deathfist Common Recipes and Deathfist Rare Recipes to make the various [[Deathfist Armor|Deathfist Armor Sets]].

## Drop locations and rates

- Most orcs in Zek, the Orcish Wastes, have a 0.18% chance to drop.
- Most trash in Deathfist Citadel have a 1% chance to drop.
- [[A Vallon miner|Vallon miners]], [[A Vallon quarry sentry|quarry sentries]] and [[A Vallon quarry elite|quarry elites]] have a 2% chance to drop.
- Most names in Deathfist Citadel have an 8% chance to drop.
- Emperor Fyst has a 25% chance to drop.
