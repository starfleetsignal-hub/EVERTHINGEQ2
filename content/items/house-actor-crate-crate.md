## Notes

This is the result of using the [[House Actor Crate (Recipe)|recipe]] to craft [[House Actor|house actors]].

- The crate is clicked to grant the house actor.
- House actors are NPCs that can be customized by the owner.
- The *unopened* crates stack, but the actual house actors do *not* stack, hence the separate items for the crate and the actors.
- See the [[House Actor]] page for details.
