## Notes

If you are not familiar with [[Potion Cabinet|Potion Cabinets]], you may want to read about the general topic of [[Sales Display|sales displays]] that can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]].

- All potion cabinets are Master Crafted (made from a rare material) by those who take up the [[Carpenter]] tradeskill. All potion cabinets look the same no matter what tier of rare wood is used to make them.
- They hold a variety of [[Potion]] and [[Poisons]] made by [[Alchemist|Alchemists]] and [[Totems]] made by [[Woodworker|Woodworkers]].
