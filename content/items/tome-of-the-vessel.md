## Text

*The sacred vessel of she who cannot be named shall lie forever buried in the sands below the gaze of Akh Na'ba. May it never be unearthed nor used, for the power it once contained should not be ushered into this world again.*<br>
*Though she is long forgotten, the power of the river of Fyr'Un has not been erased. Only within a few enchanted containers can the water of the river be stored.*<br>
*The Vessel of Fyr'Un once contained the unliving waters of the dead river. To fill it again would surely be folly. Woe to those who take the empty vessel to the pools of worship, for they are on the path of the forbidden.*<br>
*Should the four pools once more give unlife to the Vessel, the spirit of She may awaken. I, the humble chronicler, must again offer my extreme caution to any who are attempting this.*<br>
*Oh folly reigns, for to read this text is to have done the unthinkable! The pools give their dark radiance to those who speak the Writ of Akh Na'ba, but to do so can only lead to doom. For the safety of Ahket Aken, it has been sundered into three.*<br>
*Woe fall upon he who seeks the sacred Writ, for the protectors of it have taken oaths never to relinquish their parts, even after death. Tirik'rah, nor his sages, nor his scribes shall ever yield their pieces!*<br>
*Speaking aloud the combined trinity can only be done below the watchful visage of Akh Na'ba. He who does shall find his spirit lifted to the very sky by the will of [[Anashti Sul|Anashti]].*<br>
*Seek the blessing of Akh Na'ba at the peak of the soul's ascendance. Know that the keepers shall be watching and those found unworthy will be punished.*<br>
