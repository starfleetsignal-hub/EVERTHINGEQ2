## Notes

Upon **[[Banners of Thalumbra]]** quest turn in and accepting the [[Bottomless Dhalgar Stein]] it will grant you the [[Dhalgar's Last Call]] ability which is a Recall just like a city or guild recall excepting it takes you to a Bar that you choose to set your recall to. Visit a Bartender and select the 2nd choice "Actually, I'd like to talk to you about returning to this bar' option.  Default location is [[Aidan]] in [[Maldura]], Other known locations are the Bartenders: [[Zleerty]] in [[Karuupa Jungle]] and [[Mohsen]] in [[Takish Badlands]], there are many others as referenced [[Stein of Recollection (Crate Reward)|here.]]
