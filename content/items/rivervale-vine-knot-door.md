## Notes

- This door has decorative scroll work on both sides.
- During this event you can also buy [[Rivervale Bramble Door|Rivervale Bramble Doors]] and and [[Rivervale Vine Wreath Door|Rivervale Vine Wreath Doors]], which are less ornate than this.
- In 2013 all doors were added to the [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|Building Blocks]] category in the game and have a separate item count from other furniture when placed in [[Housing|player housing]].
- This is a round door that would be easy to use in a house like [[Vale of Halfpint Delight]].
