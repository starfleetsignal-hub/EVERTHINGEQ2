## Notes

- The other two Frostfell fuels referred to in these notes are [[Elven Magic]] and [[Holiday Cheer]].
- All Frostfell fuels can *only* be purchased during the annual [[Frostfell]] world event from fuel vendors in the [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]], but with the addition of a collection of recipe that allows players to make special [[Frostfell Crafting Stations]] for their homes, it can be used year-round.
- Though the description states these fuels are used for decorations, they are actually used to craft **all** types holiday-themed items, including gear like [[Candy-Striped Buckler|Candy-Striped Bucklers]], and [[Appearance|appearance clothing]] like the [[Snappy Blue Helmet]].
