## Notes

Rumored to have been made for an associate producer for EverQuest II -- Jen "Kirstie" Gerull -- after a statement on an EQ2 radio broadcast stream [http://eq2sday.mypodcast.com/2009/02/EQ2sday_Feb_3_2009-184247.html](http://eq2sday.mypodcast.com/2009/02/EQ2sday_Feb_3_2009-184247.html) that she wanted a pink unicorn with red eyes and hearts.

To see what the mount looks like, paste the item-code into your chat window and examine it in your Dressing Room.
