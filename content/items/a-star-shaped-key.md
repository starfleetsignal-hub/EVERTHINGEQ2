## Notes

- Required to advance through the Overlord's Throne Room to defeat [[Overlord Baylzuthak]]
- The rune covered chest is with the 2 [[A Gorehorn Sentry]] guards in front of the 4 safes.
  - WARNING: A short while after you kill the guards/chest, a large group of 6 mobs will spawn, approach, and attack.  They consist of [[Vault Curator Ashuk]], [[Assistant Curator Skint]], a [[A Gorehorn boar handler]], and 3 [[A warboar ravager|a warboar ravagers]].
