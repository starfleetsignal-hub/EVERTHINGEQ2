## Notes

This little cube will cause a screech to go off when you enter your house (every few minutes). Received from: [[Havras Scutter]] in the [[Village of Somborn]] within the [[Loping Plains]] if you grab the red cube from behind Havras' feet AND you have [[A lost necklace]] in your inventory (five <s>minute</s> second respawn).
