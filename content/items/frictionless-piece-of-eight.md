## Note

![Oh noes!!! You dropped it!!!](images/Oh_noes!!!.png)
This is a 100% "joke" item. You do not actually receive it, however it triggers a link in chat and on screen announcement like other Fabled items do.<br>
Like many 'joke' items associated with the Gigglegibber Goblins from various events throughout the year, this item has very significant stats with non-existent level requirements...<br>
... the only catch, of course, is that it has no slots that can be equipped on any player character.<br>
In addition "looting" it is followed by announcement that you dropped it and it has fallen through the cracks in the floor.
