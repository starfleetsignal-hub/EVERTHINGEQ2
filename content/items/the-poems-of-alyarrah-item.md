## Book Text

Compiled by the Editors

Dervin poet Alyarrah Mahaat was a celebrated poet whose works captured the attention of the Caliphs of Maj'Dul.
Though she was the Court of Coins' official Court Poet for only a short while, several of her works are still in circulation.

"**The Two Gardens**"<br>
For many of the nomadic Dervins, the imagery of a garden or oasis is a powerful symbol of life and restoration.

Near the edges of the Known World<br>
Are the two gardens of the gods<br>
To the East is<br>
the Garden of Fulfilled Dreams<br>
And in the West,<br>
the Garden of Your Desires

Between night and day, we visit<br>
One or the other as we sleep<br>
And happiness lays somewhere<br>
between them<br>
Not all in one<br>
Nor all in the other<br>
Somewhere in the sweetly scented<br>
lands between

Come, sit beside me in my dream<br>
We will stand at the crossroads of<br>
The gardens of the gods<br>
To decide whether we<br>
have been fulfilled<br>
And so to travel East<br>
Or whether we have but desire<br>
And so to travel West

If you are with me,<br>
know that I go East<br>
For all my dreams are fulfilled<br>
And yet<br>
And yet, if you would not go East,<br>
Then I would follow you West<br>
Into the Garden of Your Desires

"**The Bard's Tale**"<br>
The djinn supress music in all its forms whenever they can.
To the Dervins, music is simply another form of expression. Many of Alyarrah's poems are set to music.
In lands occupied by the djinn, the Dervins may simply tap out the beat of the song rather than sing it.

We celebrate our lives in song<br>
And tales of days of old<br>
With words that sound a clarion<br>
Or whisper from the soul

Those who have gone on before<br>
Their words we still can hear<br>
Through each lark's trill and<br>
stream unstill<br>
Distant refrains unfold

Our music is in poetry<br>
And magic is our myth<br>
Their old songs and our new entwine<br>
The silver with the gold

Many seasons may have passed<br>
And yet we can't forget<br>
Their touch, their voice,<br>
their soft caress<br>
Their stories yet untold

"**Desert Nights**"<br>
This appears to be a fragment of what is surely a longer piece.

Warm sands of the desert<br>
Golden, silky sands<br>
Pour through my fingertips<br>
When cupped into my hands

See you the tiny tracks<br>
Crossing the starlit dunes?<br>
See you their mystic pattern<br>
Like ancient, unknown runes?

You will learn much just by<br>
Sensing what is around<br>
Feel the sand beneath your feet<br>
A silent battleground

The salty tang of the sea<br>
Breathe in the scent of sweet dates<br>
The cries of the wild fill the air<br>
Listen! Be still, and wait.
