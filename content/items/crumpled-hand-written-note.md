## Notes

When examined, the note reads:

To my friends and comrades...<br>I'm afraid this will be the last you will hear from me. The Yha-lei have brought me to some underwater lair, and I cannot be sure where I am, how deep I am, or what their plans are for me now. However, I have taken what is probably a mortal wound, and it is likely that I will not live long enough to find answers to any of those questions.

I am assuming that the creatures took several item from me while I was unconscious, since I cannot find them on my person. First and foremost, they have taken my weapons... my feather-adorned longbow, and my elven rapier. It stands to reason that they would disarm me, but I cannot say where they have been taken.

Also, I lost a couple of personal items. Whether the creatures took them or they simply fell loose I cannot say. One was my helm, which could have come off anywhere, although I believe it was still on me when I was brought to this place. The same for my Omen's Defender clasp... I think it might have fallen off under the weight of my water-soaked cloak.

Whomever comes across this note, if you can, please retrieve my items and return them to my brothers in Omen's Call. I would like them to know what has become of me, and I would hope that the items that once served me well could also serve another in my place.<br>Farewell to you all... it was an honour serving with you.<br>-Cursis Skyguard
