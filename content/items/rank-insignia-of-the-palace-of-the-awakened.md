## Book Text

**-The Palace of the Awakened- Soldiers andOfficers: Rank Insignia**

**Rank 1:**<br>
Green Trinity:<br>
The rank itself is signified by a green ribbon holding the bronze trinity.<br>

This is the lowest rank<br><br>Notes: I found this rank insignia on a Blacktalon initiate within the Palace of the Awakened.

**Rank 2:**<br>
Lavender Trinity:<br>
The rank itself is signified by a lavender ribbon holding the bronze trinity.<br>

This signifies a soldier of the same rank as the Green Trinity but on active duty in the Home Guard.<br><br>Notes: I found this rank insignia on a Blacktalon palace guard.

**Rank 3:** Trinity: The rank itself is signified by a white ribbon holding the bronze trinity. This signifies a soldier within the Home Guard with a practicing specialty.   Notes: I found this rank insignia on a Blacktalon disciple.

**Rank 4:**<br>
Green Thrice:<br>
The rank itself is signified by a green ribbon holding the bronze thrice.<br>

This signifies a commanding soldier's second in command within the Home Guard.<br><br>Notes: I found this rank insignia on a Blacktalon coadjutant.

**Rank 5:**<br>
Lavender Thrice:<br>
The rank itself is signified by a lavender ribbon holding the bronze thrice.<br>

This signifies a commanding soldier in command within the Home Guard.<br><br>Notes: I found this rank insignia on a Blacktalon palace knight.

**Rank 6:**<br>
Thrice:<br>
The rank itself is signified by a white ribbon holding the bronze thrice.<br>

This signifies an active soldier of rank sergeant of higher.<br><br>Notes: I found this rank insignia on a Blacktalon partisan.

**Rank 7:**<br>
Trident:<br>
The rank itself is signified by a white ribbon holding the bronze trident<br>

This signifies the rank of captain<br><br>Notes: I found this rank insignia on a Blacktalon vanquisher.

**Rank 8:**<br>
Green Disc:<br>
The rank itself is signified by a green ribbon holding the golden disc.<br>

This signifies a member of the High Guard of lieutenant rank.<br><br>Notes: I found this rank insignia on a Bloodscale enforcer.

**Rank 9:**<br>
Red Disc:<br>
The rank itself is signified by a red ribbon holding the golden disc.<br>

This signifies a major of the High Guard.<br><br>Notes: I found this rank insignia on a Bloodscale high priest.

**Rank 10:**<br>
Blue Disc:<br>
The rank itself is signified by a blue ribbon holding the golden disc.<br>

This signifies a major general of the High Guard.<br><br>Notes: I found this rank insignia on a Bloodscale shadowslayer.

**Rank 11:**<br>
Disc:<br>
The rank itself is signified by a white ribbon holding the golden disc<br>

This signifies a lieutenant general of the High Guard.<br><br>Notes: I found this rank insignia on a Bloodscale dreadknight.
