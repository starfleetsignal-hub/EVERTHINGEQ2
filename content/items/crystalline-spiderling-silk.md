## Notes

Can only be gathered during and after the quest [[Dream Weaving]]. Once you've right clicked and gathered silk, there's a 24 hour timeout before you can gather again.<br>
Needed to craft [[Rallic Pack]] and [[Blessed Coldain Prayer Shawl]] .
