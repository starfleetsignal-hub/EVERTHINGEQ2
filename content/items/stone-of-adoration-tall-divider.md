## Notes

- This item is from a crafting book that is only available during the [[Erollisi Day]] holiday event.
- Crafting it requires any (one) of the [[Erollisi Day Hard Candy and Love Notes]] only looted during the [[Live Events]], though you can buy more candy or notes from the [[Broker]].
- Though the name has TALL in it, it is a much *wider* wall than the [[Stone of Adoration Narrow Divider]].
