## Notes

As soon as this item is purchased a window will open that allows you to choose from several (4 or 5) high level, high stat equipment items normally sold by [[Noble Toowik Mclure]], that suit your typically character's adventure class. The selection is always comprised of heroic item of legendary quality or better.

If you are lucky, you can choose an item that normally costs more than double the amount in Loyalty Point Tokens. For example, you may get to choose a legendary weapon that normally costs 7 LPT or greater at the 5 LPT cost.
