## Notes

You can use these tokens to buy items from the merchant, [[Koros Splinterlimb]]. In order to buy items from Koros, you also need a specific amount of positive faction with [[Far Seas Supply Division (Faction)]]. Koros, and several NPCs who give quests that reward these tokens, are located in at [[Outsiders' Landing]] near the [[Village of Shin]] on the [[Isle of Mara]] .
