## Notes

"Zannas, just as I've foreseen, things have gotten really bad here on the western front.  We only received half of the ogre conscripts that were promised to us.  The generals had to detach an infantry unit from the Kelethin attack force in order to bolster our position.  Even with less the numbers than original planned we were able to make land north of the Lesser Faydark and were pretty well dug in to halt the Kaladim response to the west."

---

"We were able to stop the initial surges of dwarven warriors from entering the Lesser Faydark, but our victories were short lived.  Something stirred the normally timid fae creatures into battle!  We begun to be attacked from behind by satyrs, unicorn, brownies, and other vile beasts!  At the same time the dwarves made another desperate push led by the King Stormhammer himself.  We had no choice but to break and flee!  I'll try and find a place to hide for now.  You better be out looking for me!" -- Tessa

---

"Where are you?!  You and your buddies had to have been dispatched to Faydwer by now.  Get me out of here!  I've convinced the aquagoblins of the region to take me in.  With my magic I was able to divine where a large school of fish was hiding.  This was enough to persuade their king to shelter me for now."

---

"I need to get out of here, I can't take their cackling and slimy paws any longer!  You better find me!  None of my translocation spells to Neriak are working and the word is Neriak has closed itself off from the surface!  What is going on?!  I've decided I need to find a more permanant residence... the only two options for a Teir'Dal in this area are Mistmoore Castle and Crushbone Keep.  I'll have to weigh these options carefully."  -- Tessa N'Mare

---

"I've enlisted the aquagoblins in carrying notes for me to inquire of a permenant residence, but these stupid beasts end up destroying the paper, eating it, or carelessly diving into the water with them.  I've taken to enchanting messages on gems for the goblins to deliver but most of them just keep the gems as jewelry!  If I don't hear word back from my messages in the next week I'll just show up on their doorstep.  If you don't find me with the aquagoblins then I should be at...."   (The rest of the message is soaking wet and unreadable.)
