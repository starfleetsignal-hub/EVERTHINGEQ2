## Notes

The merchants you can visit to spend these is only around during [[Erolisi Day|Erollisi Day]]. Visit [[Steven]] or [[Douglas]] to spend your tokens.

- This coin can be earned by completing various quests and it's also a reward option for completing the [[Hearts a'Flutter]] races.
- Unlike some special currency, this is **not** heirloom, which means you can sell it or buy it using the [[Broker]].
