## Notes

At the behest of pleas of the crowd from SOELive 2012, beginning with [[Chains of Eternity|CoE]], [[Momo (NPC)|Momo]] will now accept payment of 5 blue shards for 1 white [[Primal Velium Shard]]. Offering, albeit at a steep price, raiders with a glut of these blue shards a method to obtain white shards (for replacing yellow adornments), without doing solo/heroic content.
