## Notes

[[Velious Winter Rewards: January Treasures]][http://eq2.zam.com/wiki/Velious_Winter_Rewards](http://eq2.zam.com/wiki/Velious_Winter_Rewards) contained Frost the Ice Wolf Pup. After you have summoned Frost you may right-click him and cause him to emote by choosing such things as *Dance*, *Sing* or *Train*. While Frost will happily follow you from zone to zone, he does not fight or alter your abilities - he is just a fluff pet.<br>
Scribing this Spell Scroll gives the ability [[Call Ice Wolf Pup]].
