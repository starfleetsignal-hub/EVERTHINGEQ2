## Notes

This resource only appears as part of the Bristlebane Day holiday event. It is harvested from [[Jester's Gardens]] found throughout [[Antonica]], [[The Commonlands]], [[Darklight Wood]], [[Timorous Deep]], and [[Greater Faydark]].

It is used in the various recipes from the book series: [[Perfectly Serious Items to Craft]] ... sold by the [[A Bristlebane Day merchant|Bristlebane Day merchant]], who is only available during the [[Bristlebane Day]] holiday.
