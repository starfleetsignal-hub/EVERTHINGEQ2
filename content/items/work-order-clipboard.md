Only allows you to "pick up" the writ, you must still go to a writ starter npc to start the writ quest.

- [[Acquisitions Expert Kyri Velkyn]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
- [[Fhara Hanford]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]]
- [[Juvena]] in [[The City of Freeport]]
- [[Peregrine Helgreth Iceweaver]] in [[Frostfang Sea]]
- [[Prime Stockmajor Grelishh]] in [[Timorous Deep]]
- [[Yono Sylnu]]	in [[Greater Faydark]]
