Bubonian mobs are [[A bubonian fetor]] or [[A bubonian retch]] found in [[Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands]]

Bubonian mobs are ALSO available in  Plane of Disease: Outbreak [Solo] which is the ONLY location for Pusling  Love Note drops.  ***Note:  If mobs are gray, even 130 Adventure Level toons will die from the toxic detriment unless they mentor down.***  This is necessary to get the Springview Healer Mask drop to allow progress in the instance.  BOTH Bubonian and Pusling are required for the Erolisi Day  'Love is magic in the Planes of Prophecy' achievement.
