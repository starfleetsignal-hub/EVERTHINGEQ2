## Notes

Though categorized as such and though it *will* show up in the Currency Tab on the [[Understanding Your Character|character window]], this is **not** a currency.

- Instead, it unlocks the ability to "buy" the rewards from the [[Days of Summer]] quests for *zero* copper.
- Doing the quest series on a single character on an account unlocks the items for **all** characters on that account.
- Most rewards will *only* show up for characters at level 120, so be sure to return to Merchant [[Pas Yu]] if your characters are not yet able to collect them all.
