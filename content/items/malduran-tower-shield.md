## Notes

Make the Malduran Tower Shields at the fire pits next to Gwynvyn at the Maldura Forge.
You will need:

- 5 x Splitiron Ore
- 15 x Umbrite
- 15 x Bornite Nodule
- 25 x Thaumic Coal (from the fuel merchant)

Once made, Deliver them to Quartermaster Lysslyn (near the entrance)
