## Notes

See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity does not have a [[Guild Level]] minimum.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

This works just like any [[The Norrathian Express|mailbox]], but the guild leader or a trustee can choose the appearance of any of the city mailboxes found major cities throughout Norrath.

- For example, it can be set to look like those seen in [[The City of Freeport]], [[Kelethin]], [[The City of Paineel]] and so forth.
