Purchasing this item requires completing the quest: [[Tears of Veeshan: Falling Tears]]

- [[Essence of a Dragon Knight]]
- [[Essence of the Dreadscale]]
- [[Harrowing Hoard]]
- [[Klandicar's Hoard]]
