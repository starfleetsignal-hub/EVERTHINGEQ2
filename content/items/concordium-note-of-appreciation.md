Notes of appreciation can be turned in to [[Mage Fulcrum Gearshift]] up the red portal at the [[Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos]] or [[Scientist Dally Scrumplecog]] at the [[Academy of Arcane Science|Academy of Arcane Sciencein]] [[North Freeport]]. You can choose between many different Rewards. The prices are between 1 to 5 notes.
