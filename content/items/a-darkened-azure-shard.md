## Notes

1. If you did not receive *a darkened azure shard* from the mail or from reward claims then right-click and 'use' one of the four [[Terraporter|terraporters]] found in The Thundering Steppes. The item will automatically be placed in your inventory. Terraporter locations are:
   - {{waypoint 548, -1, -507}}
   - {{waypoint 710, -2, -414}}
   - {{waypoint 1023, -1, -247}}
   - {{waypoint 1267, -3, -208}}
1. Examining *a darkened azure shard* will prompt the quest, [[A Darkened Shard]]. Alternatively, if the item is in your inventory and you zone into [[The Thundering Steppes]], the quest will be prompted again.
   - Once the quest is accepted, further examination of the shard will display a 3D model of a [[Terraporter]] in front of the character for a few seconds.
