## NOTE

This extra rare wood is used exclusively to craft [[House Actor Crate (crate)]]

There is a **50% chance of getting etherwood lumber upon harvesting a rare [[Bubinga lumber]]** from wood nodes in [[Vesspyr Isles]], [[The Withered Lands]], [[The Eidolon Jungle]], [[Obol Plains]] and [[Cobalt Scar]].
