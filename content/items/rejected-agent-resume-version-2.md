## How to obtain

- Most agents can be converted into these resumes. Those who cannot be converted will have that spelled out in their examine window.
- Right click on unwanted or duplicate agent and select to convert. The resulting Rejected Agent Resumes will be added to your currency window.
- Conversion according to quality of agent:
  - Treasured agent yields 2 resumes
  - Legendary agent yields 3 resumes
  - Fabled agent yields 4 resumes
  - Celestial agent yields 5 resumes

## Merchant

[[Old Michem]] located in [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -258, -56, 60}}, or in [[Qeynos Province District]] at {{waypoint 980, -26, 86}} sell a variety of items for these resumes.

For example:

- 250 of these can be traded for certain recipe books
