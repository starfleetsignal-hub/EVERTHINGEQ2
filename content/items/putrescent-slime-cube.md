## Notes

A fluff pet with a *hail* and *poke* option that does nothing! This blubbering cube of slimy jelly just follows you around. It overrides other fluff pets when summoned but not the deity pet. When it is up you may be able to summon a second fluff pet (depends on the pet). It was added in 2018.
