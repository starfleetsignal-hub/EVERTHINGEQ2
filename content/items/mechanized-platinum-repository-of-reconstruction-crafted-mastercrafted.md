## Notes

Summons an NPC [[Mender]] bot for 5 minutes. Repairs with this bot are more expensive than those by NPC menders.<br>
Also, casting and recasting times are effected by your individual toon's stats.
Recipe calls for 5 [[Xegonite cluster]], but 3 are returned. Fuel is 10 [[Pristine repair material]] @ 60g, for 6p in fuel total.
