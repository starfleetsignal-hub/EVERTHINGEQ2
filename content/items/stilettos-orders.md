## Notes

These orders are not labeled as Lore, but they mostly follow the rules of being Lore. You can place them in your characters bank, but not the shared bank. You can't mail the orders, but you can put them in to the guild bank.

If you have accepted or completed the quest given by this item then you no longer receive the drop in the future (if you try to take them, they simply delete).

If you have the orders on the broker you cannot recieve another set of orders from Stiletto. Unknown if this applies to haveing orders in Guild Bank or not.

### Examine Dialog

"As stated in our previous meeting, this information is not to be shared with anyone.  The sailing vessel, 'The Kedge's Lament', set out to sea with a very peculiar item on board over a decade ago from Freeport.  It seems this ship was hired by a merchant family in Freeport who did not weather the Shattering well and was in dire need of money."

"Evidently the family sold this object to a mysterious buyer who wished to remain anonymous.  Apparently the buyer wished the item to be delivered to the southern part of Karan.  However, the ship did not return to Freeport and was never heard of again.  This intrigues me greatly.  I want you to find the whereabouts of this ship and learn the fate of this object."

"Look for a small bag made of an exceedingly thin but strong material.  It was important enough that the captain himself would have been in possession of it.  Return to me with this bag and its contents and I will pay you the remaining half of your money.  Your kind has failed me in the past, do not test my capacity to forgive any further, you will not appreciate it.  -- Master T'Lys"
