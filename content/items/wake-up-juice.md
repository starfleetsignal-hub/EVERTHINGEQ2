## Notes

Drinking a Wake-up Juice will remove one level of drunkeness. Ie. you will go from "Very Drunk" Status to "inebriated" status. Drinking a second Wake-Up juice will remove inebriation.
When used, a dialog box appears with the text "This brewed sludge of a coffee drink contains hot pepper seeds, chili powder, onions and mustard seed."
along with 2 choices:

- Drink up!
- Save this drink for another time.
