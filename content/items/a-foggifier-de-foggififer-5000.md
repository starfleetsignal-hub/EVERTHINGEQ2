## Notes

This item lets you turn on and off the FOG effects in your house.
Received from: [[Havras Scutter]] in the [[Village of Somborn]] within the [[Loping Plains]]
if you grab the red cube from behind Havras' feet AND you have [[A lost necklace]] in your inventory (five <s>minute</s> second respawn).
