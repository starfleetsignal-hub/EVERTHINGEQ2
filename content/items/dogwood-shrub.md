## Notes

- This item is both used to craft and it can be [[Decorating Houses|placed in your house]].
- Unlike the many other [[Plundered House Items|plunder decor items]], you must be at least level 100 in your craft and have one of the two quests this is used for, in order to gather it. The item is Heirloom.
  - Because of the above, be sure to gather extras while you have the quests.
