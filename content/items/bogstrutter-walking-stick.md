## Note

This effect does not stack with [[Earring of the Solstice]], [[Spirit of the Wolf]], and mounts, but it ceases to give any benefit once you have reached 45% run speed through combined effects not counting AAs and gear. For example, 45% warden's SoW (fully AA buffed) + 10% [[Journeyman's Boots]], + 35% Bogstrutter Walking Stick will give a total of 55% run speed.
