## Notes

See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity does not have a [[Guild Level]] minimum.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

A chest item that grants a training dummy item in your inventory when you click on it.

- The lid will open when the dummy is transferred to your inventory.
- The chest offers a selection:-
  - Solo Training Dummy
  - Group of Training Dummies
  - Heroic Training Dummy
  - Epic Training Dummy
- The dummies can then be placed anywhere in the guild hall for fighting and skill practice
