**NOTE**: This item is treated as a mount and will be located in your mount window, but does **NOT** increase your speed! Neither can you use this mount while you're already mounted or cast a different mount while this one is active. Also, the appearance of this mount is visible in the preview window, but does not appear when you cast it. This may be either intentional or a bug. In other words, right now this 'mount' is only useful for its Featherfall effect.
