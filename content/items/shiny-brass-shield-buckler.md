## Heritage

In [[EverQuest]], this shield was a fairly common drop from the **Orc Trainer** in **Crushbone**. While the drop was not uncommon, getting the trainer to spawn was a sometimes frustrating camp.

## Note

This item may be turned into a House Item called [[The Legendary Shiny Brass Shield]], by examining it in your inventory.
