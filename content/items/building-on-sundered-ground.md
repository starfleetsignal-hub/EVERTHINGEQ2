## Notes

- <s>This recipe book was only available in 2015 during a prelude event for the Terrors of Thalumbra expansion.</s>
- <s>Though it was only available to obtain by normal means in 2015, it is a tradeable recipe and it *might* be available via the [[Broker|broker]].</s>
- The recipes in this book are all rough-textured stone [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]] that require looted [[Arcane Umbra Gravel]] as a component.
