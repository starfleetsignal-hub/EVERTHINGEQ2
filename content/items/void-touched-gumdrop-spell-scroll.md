## Notes

The gifts given by [[Santa Glug]] change each year, so this may not be available every year.

- This **gift** is NO TRADE but it can be sold to an NPC for gold.
- It can also be purchased for 3 [[Frozen Token of E'ci]] from [[Garsleblat Gigglegibber]] and [[Giggawat Gigglegibber]].
