## Notes

- Crafting this item may result in multiple items of 2-4, depending on your success and the item.
- This item is from a special crafting recipe book that was only offered with the purchase of the Collectors and Premium editions Terrors of Thalumbra expansion.
- The items all match the [[Dhalgar Precipice of the Deep]] (the special edition [[Prestige Housing|prestige house]].
- This item is considered part of the [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|Building Block item category]], which has its own item limits in [[Housing|housing]].
