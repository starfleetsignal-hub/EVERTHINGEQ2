## Notes

If you have lost or otherwise misplaced your glowing azure shard you can purchase a new one at any time from [[Gumgullet]] for 10 copper at any time if you have completed the quest [[A Darkened Shard, Part 2]]. For a quick reference on how to reach the [[Lower Tunnels]] to purchase another glowing azure shard see the zone guide for the [[Sundered Splitpaw: Upper Tunnels|Upper Tunnels]].
