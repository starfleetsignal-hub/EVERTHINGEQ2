## Notes

1. Click in the closet located either in Frostfang Sea (close to the Cairn of the Huntress, middle of the city) , Great Faydark or in Qeynos Harbor.
1. Go down to the islands using the rainbown.
1. click on the pudding on the table on the island to the left.
1. Go to Mr. McScroogle and make the quest he offers. When finished click on the sack of coal.
1. Speak to Queen Bunny and do the quest. When finished click on the snowglobe.
1. Speak to Gardy Ex-Giftgiver and do first quest. When finished click on the present.
