## Notes

**Veasna says to you**, *"Let me see what you have there."*<br>
**You say to Veasna**, *"Here you are, just as you said. It's the same bracer, but it feels energized somehow... stronger."*<br>
**Veasna says to you**, *"Exactly! The ideal is becoming real."*<br>
**You say to Veasna**, *"Becoming? There's more?"*<br>
**Veasna says to you**, *"There is. We can only draw on some of the potential at a time, and so these gems unlock the power of the item they are used on in stages. In this case, I estimate that this bracer has two more stages to go before it reaches its full potential. Here, take another yellow gem, and bring those bracers one step closer to their ideal."*<br>
**You say to Veasna**, *"Thank you Veasna."*<br>
**Veasna says to you**, *"Remember, right click on the yellow gem and then click on your upgraded bracers."*
