## Notes

![The yellow arrow points to the Screeching Noise box you must click to get this item.](images/Blanket_of_fog_location.png)

- Creates a blanket of fog in your house or guild house. This effect has a warmer, yellow-green tone than [[Thick Blanket of Cold Fog]].
- You can click the cube to get the fog house item every few seconds
- You **must** have **A lost necklace** in your inventory
- If you keep **A lost necklace**, you can reuse it to get more fog every year.
- On first loot one also receives [[A Foggifier De-Foggififer 5000]] which is LORE (so place in house/dungeon to get more).

## How to Obtain

1. Complete the [[The Hedge Hollow]] and loot [[A Gleaming Chest]] to receive [[A lost necklace]]
1. With **A lost necklace** in your inventory, travel to the [[Village of Somborn]] within the [[Loping Plains]]
1. Travel to /loc {{waypoint -311, 12, -49}}
1. Click on the [[Screeching Noise Box]] (a red cube) near [[Havras "Hack" Scutter]]
