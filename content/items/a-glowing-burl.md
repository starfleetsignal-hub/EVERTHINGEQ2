## Notes

1. Right-click the **Glowing Burl** to pop up a message:
   - *The rings of growth within this burl glow with arcane energies, drawing your eye and calling for your touch.*
1. Select **Grasp the burl tightly.**
1. A projection of [[Balin Grove the Elder (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Balin Grove the Elder]] will appear to speak with you and offer the quest, [[The Siege of Zek: Rise of The Sanctifiers]].
