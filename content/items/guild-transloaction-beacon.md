## Notes

See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity does not have a [[Guild Level]] minimum.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

A statue that new members can click in order to add the [[Call to Guild Hall]] ability to their Knowledge "book" (press K to open).

- Functions just like the [[Call]] to various home cities, but teleports the caster to their guild's hall.
- The ability itself has a 15 min cool-down before it can be reused.
