## Notes

Text when Examining this item:

- The edges of the parchment are torn and jagged as though ripped from a much larger document.
- You can barely make out some writing on this scrap: "The horse sentries will ... that we ..." There is very little to go on in this note, but it's obvious the Tunarian horse sentries are involved. You decide you will hunt them down until you retrieve more information.
- You carefully roll the parchment and stow it away.

Text when Examining this item after acquiring the piece from the [[A Tunarian horse sentry|Tunarian horse sentries]]:

- The edges of the parchment are torn and jagged as though ripped from a much larger document.
- You line up the parchment pieces. The newest piece adds enough new words that you can read: "The horse sentries will ... that we can ... wilderbears ..." The Tunarians are using the wilderbears for something? They have various animal trainers. Perhaps slaying the wilderbears will force a change in plans?
- You carefully roll up the parchment and stow it away.
