## Notes

- This item is identical to [[Freeport Heroes' Festival Band Flier]] (and the Qeynos version) but the text on it can be personalized if it is blank.
  - The process to personalize the note is similar to the creating a [[Player-written books|player-written book]], though the format is not as long.
- This item is from a crafting book that is only available during the [[Erollisi Day]] holiday event.
- Crafting it requires any (one) of the [[Erollisi Day Hard Candy and Love Notes]] only looted during the [[Live Events]], though you can buy more candy or notes from the [[Broker|broker]].
