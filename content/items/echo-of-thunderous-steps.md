## Notes

This and eleven other paintings that can be chosen from the package [[An heirloom painting]] refer back to the original EQ game. See the info and all choices on the page for [[An heirloom painting]]. You will **not** be able to examine this item or preview it in the dressing room feature until you enter your character's house and the list of choices opens.

- This painting was a gift to those who had a legacy EQ account. It and can be chosen from one of many when you open **An heirloom painting** via the /claim system. If you had an EQ account when EQ2 launched, type /claim to see if you still have one waiting.
- All players can also "buy" **An heirloom painting** (and then choose this option) by spending [[Loyalty Point Token|Loyalty Point Tokens]] at [[Noble Tark Validus]].
