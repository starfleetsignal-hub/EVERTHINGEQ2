## Notes

This tapestry is rewarded for doing any **one** of [[Majah L'Asi]]'s repeatable quests eight times. His quests are:

- [[Service of the Blades]]
- [[Service of the Coin]]
- [[Service of Truth]]

**Note:** After the fifth time of doing the above listed quests you will receive the respective tapestry:

- [[Court of the Blades Tapestry]]
- [[Court of the Coin Tapestry]]
- [[Court of Truth Tapestry]]

[[Majah L'Asi]] will only offer the quests to you if your faction with all of the courts is below 30,000.
