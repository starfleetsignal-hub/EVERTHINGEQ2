## Notes

- The recipe *[[The Tender's Secrets Volume 1]]* for this scroll is a single-use rare drop in [[The Emerald Halls]] and can also be obtained from the Far Seas vendor once you have sufficient Tradeskill faction. The rare can be found during Far Seas tradeskill group missions. The possessor, if not a Scholar, must engage a Scholar to make it using the [[Commission System]] system.
- This scroll allows any character, level 65 or higher,  to cast [[Fire Seeds]]. The scroll has 10 uses.
- This spell can only be crafted as Adept III.
