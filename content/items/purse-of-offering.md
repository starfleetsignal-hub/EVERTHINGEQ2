## Notes

This quest starter offers four quests. Accepting any one of them will remove the Purse of Offering from your inventory. You can be on multiple quests at the same time.

Examining the Purse of Offering opens a text window: *This is a very small purse, worn and tattered due to years of neglect. There appears to be writing scrawled along the length of the purse's drawstring.*

- Read the writing. (Option 1, opens second text window)
- Ignore the writing. (Closes the text window)

Second text window:
*The words on the drawstring are so faded they cannot be deciphered by a casual glance. With some squinting, blinking and a lot of guessing, you have an idea what the letters spell out.*

- Read the scribbled writing. (Offers the quest [[Memorial of Darkness]])
- Read the scratched writing. (Offers the quest [[Memorial of Hatred]])
- Read the smudged writing. (Offers the quest [[Memorial of Power]])
- Read the faded writing. (Offers the quest [[Memorial of Vengeance]])
- Ignore the writing. (Closes the text window)
