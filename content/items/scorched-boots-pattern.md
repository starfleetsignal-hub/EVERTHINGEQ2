This item, along with three gems that drop off of trash in [[Veeshan's Peak]], is needed to buy the level 80 Fabled set armor for [[Berserker|Berserkers]], [[Swashbuckler|Swashbucklers]], [[Warden|Wardens]] and [[Wizard|Wizards]] from [[Avess Ryiss]] in [[Danak Shipyards]].
