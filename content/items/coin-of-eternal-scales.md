## Note

Examining this item starts a mini-event: You are accused of murdering a god, [[Lanys T'Vyl]], daughter of [[Innoruuk]]. However, [[Druzzil Ro]] goes to your defense and accepts full responsibility for [[Lanys T'Vyl]]'s death. You are cleared of all charges and set free, being granted access to [[Myrist, the Great Library]] and rewarded with the [[Book of The Plane of Knowledge]]. The Coin disappears after this mini-event.
