- This amenity does not have a [[Guild Level]] minimum.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

A NPC that grants flags (to guild members) that can be placed in the world, allowing guild members to travel to that location (e.g. a raid zone). It provides highly efficient point-to-point travel when time is of the essence, by eliminating the need to use [[Travel in Norrath|standard means of travel]] or the often "buggy" [[Call of the Veteran]] ability.

### Hireling Options

- Uniform - you must have at least one [[Uniform Mannequin]] amenity to set a uniform.
