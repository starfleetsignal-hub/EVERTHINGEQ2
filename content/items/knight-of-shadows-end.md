## Notes

The **Knight of Shadow's End** is a "fun spell" pet that can be purchased for 400 points on the Marketplace. The pet is compatible with summoned / charmed pets and deity pets, allowing all three to display simultaneously. It will not display concurrently with other "fluff" pets.

The pet is very, very small, approximately the size of a full-size Fae / Arasai torso, and uses an all-black texture.
