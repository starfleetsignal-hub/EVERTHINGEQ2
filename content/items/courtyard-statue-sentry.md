## Notes

See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity does not have a [[Guild Level]] minimum.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

Courtyard Statue: Sentry is a large statue amenity.

- When this amenity is purchased by a guild leader, the a statue is automatically placed along the path between the entrance and center courtyard area. of the T3 and T4 [[Qeynos-style Guild Halls]].
- This statue has 4 different locations that must be purchased **separately** in the north, east, south and west.
- It can **not** be moved.
