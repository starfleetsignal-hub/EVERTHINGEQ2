## Notes

- This [[Appearance|appearance]] item can be equipped by characters of either sex. On females it renders as a dress. On males the top/pants is similar to the [[Crimson Festival Garb]], though the coloring on this item is pink.
- This item is crafted using [[Erollisi Day Hard Candy and Love Notes]], which can only be looted during [[Erollisi Day]].
- The candy is tradeable, so check the broker if you run short during the rest of the year.
