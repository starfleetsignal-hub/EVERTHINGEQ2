## Notes

- This is a smaller, stationary plushie. It is one of many possible quest rewards. You can also get a house pet version of this item, [[A Strange Cabbage]], that is larger and moves around your [[Housing|home]].
- This is one of many options from the quest, [[Snoogle's Presentation]], which can be repeated once every two hours during the event.
