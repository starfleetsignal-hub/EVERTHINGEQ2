## Notes

- This fun "fluff" pet (it just follows you around the world) may be purchased or received as a chest drop during [[Brew Day]] celebrations.
- Because it is a fluff pet and not a pet used by various "pet classes" ([[Channeler]], [[Necromancer]], etc.) anyone can buy and use this pet.
- This item should **not** be confused with the identical looking [[Wild Boar (House Pet)]] (a house item) available only during [[Bristlebane Day]].
