## Notes

If you decide you no longer want to ride it, this mount *can* be placed in your [[Housing|home]].
*To do so, press **C** to open the character sheet and click on the Mounts tab. Drag the icon for this mount to your bag. R click it in your bag and, when the window opens asking if you want to turn it into a house item, choose yes.
*After turning it into a house item it can be manipulated just like other house items when you are in [[Decorating Houses|decorator mode]].
