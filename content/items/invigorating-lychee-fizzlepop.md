## Notes

Advanced Provisioner Volume 90 contains the recipes for the Provisioner's Supply Cart. Every player may get one of three (random) drinks from the cart per day, including the Invigorating Lychee Fizzlepop. These drinks act in the same way as the berries from the old world event Blessed Sapling, and share a 24-hour cooldown timer with it.
