## Notes

The Greater Spirit currency is used for purchasing heroic-level equipment from [[Drinal's Steward]] in [[Obol Plains]].  While the essences dropped from the Bosses from each heroic instance can be exchanged for jewelry, the Greater Spirits are exchanged for armor and weapons.
