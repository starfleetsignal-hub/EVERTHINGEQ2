## Notes

These items can be found by looking for the red sparkling mushrooms near the [[Lujien Fields]]. Examining the mushrooms will pop up a window that reads

*An Ethernere Mushroom. I should stuff it into some [[Ether bat meat]] for [[Deema Mertshak]].*

- *Stuff bat meat.*

Choosing "Stuff bat meat." will replace every Ether bat meat and Ethernere mushroom pair with one [[Tainted Bat Meat]] until you run out of either.
