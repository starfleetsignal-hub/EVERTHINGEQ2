## Requirements

- You must be level 80 in order to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Examine the Icebox to receive one of the mini-quests
1. Kill 7 of the necessary creature
1. Examine the box for another of the same quest
1. Repeat the above four times, until that quest is no longer available

Inspect the box a final time to receive [[Necromantic Research - Delivery]].

## Notes

Note that the Neophyte may not have a feather up (he didn't for me anyway).
The mini-quests can be done in any order.
This series of quests is a little tedious, but there are no timers and you don't need to cycle through all of the quests before you can get the next round.

The reward is a very nice lvl 82 chestpiece.
