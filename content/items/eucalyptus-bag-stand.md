## Notes

If you are not familiar with [[Bag Stand|Bag Stands]], you may want to read about the general topic of [[Sales Display|sales displays]] that can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]].

- All bag stands are Master Crafted (made from a rare material) by those who take up the [[Carpenter]] tradeskill. No matter what tier of [[Rare Wood]] is used to make them, they look the same when placed in a house.
- This sales display holds a limited scope of items that are *typically* [[Tailor]] made, like [[Backpacks]].
