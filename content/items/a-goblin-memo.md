## Text

*I have grown fond of the smelly halfling history. The fur toes have such a comical life, worthless other than to laugh at. In honor of their humorous ineptitude I began to create and name my moppets after fur toes of old ages. But just like the fur toes, my dolls have lacked sensibility and run off, scattered in the smelly town, most with little spark to retain life. I will retrieve them as soon as I complete this note to remind me to do so. My memory falters back to cursed times. - [[Grumblerutt]]*
