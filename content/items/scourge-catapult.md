## Notes

This is an exact replica of the damaged catapult that you are required to repair for the [[Rubble Chunkin']] quest, in [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Solo)]].

When placed in a house or guildhall you will only see the base framework. Click on this and it will arm itself (as shown in the picture) and fire a boulder!
