## Notes

See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity does not have a [[Guild Level]] minimum.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

- A very ornate version of the globes found on docks that are used to [[Travel in Norrath|travel in Norrath]]. This amenity is used for instant travel to any locations accessed by the globes found on docks throughout Norrath.
