## Notes

- Players can buy this "Gifty Bag" from Marketplace for 300DB and open it by Santa sleigh in [[Qeynos Capitol District]] or [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]].
- This results in a reward [[The Spirit of Giving]] for the player  who opened the bag and a separate Santa Glug's Lil' Helper who runs around the city and drops 10 presents for others to pick up. Those presents will have a name of a player who opened the "Gifty Bag" in their name ( Xxxx's Generous Gift).
