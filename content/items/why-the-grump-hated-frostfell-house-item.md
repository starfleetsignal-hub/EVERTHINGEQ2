## Book Text

Every goblin in Gibberville liked Frostfell time a lot<br>

BUT...<br>

Grimagus<br>
Grabblerouser<br>
Gigglegibber<br>
Did NOT.<br>

Grimagus hated Gifting Day! That whole Frostfell time!<br>
When gifting and gabbing and giggling gave cheer.<br>

It could be he thought his own gifts weren't quite right.<br>
It could be, mayhaps, that his funds were too tight.<br>

But the reason most likely - and let us be blunt -<br>
Was the horrible youth he had spent as a runt.<br>

He thought of the times when he was quite so small<br>
That other Gigglegibbers called him a Goofball.<br>

Grimagus moped in the corner or they tossed him around<br>
And he peeped not a word; he would not make a sound.<br>

His lunch coin would fall from his pockets with ease<br>
As the other Gigglegibbers taunted and teased.<br>

Though now he is rich and owns the whole town<br>
Those were sad times when they called him a clown.<br>

Grimagus grew into a mischievous teener<br>
And wider and richer and quite a lot meaner.<br>

Over time, Grimagus outgrew being a chump.<br>
In fact, all the Gigglegibbers now called him the Grump.<br>
He was grumpy and snarly and bossy most days<br>
Especially during the goblins' few holidays.<br>

Holiday time always made the Grump bleak.<br>
He remembered those days when he was young and weak.<br>

And the festive time he hated, hated most of all<br>
Was Frostfell time, when folks tall and small<br>
When folks young and old, and folks far and near<br>
Would exchange little presents and be of good cheer.<br>

"They're buying their presents," he said now with a growl.<br>
"Gifting Day is tomorrow! That day, oh, most foul!"<br>

HE pounded his fist and he cried with a sneer,<br>
"There will be NO Gifting Day! Not no-how, this year!"<br>

He thought of the elves, with their boxes and bows.<br>
He thought of them singing and twinkling their toes.<br>

The thought of those elves made him quite sneery.<br>
They were chipper and sparkly and impossibly cheery.<br>

"They've never been sad, never gone without gifts..."<br>
"And that is something I simply must fix."<br>

"I'll stop Frostfell time!" his eyes glinted with glee.<br>
"I'll start in the cities! I'll make them all flee!"<br>

The Grump plotted how he could bag up the goods<br>
That were sure to bring Frostfell time cheer to the 'hoods.<br>
"I'll take all the feasts and baubles and toys"<br>
"That were meant for all the good girls and boys!"<br>

The Grump found himself empty boxes and bags.<br>
He then bundled up in some tatters and rags.<br>

And he raced through the villages. He races through the 'hoods.<br>
He swiped and he stole and he took what he could.<br>

He took the wrapped presents. He took the cooked feasts.<br>
The Grump even took the pies of minced meats!<br>

And as he was leaving, his sack bulging with stuff,<br>
The adventurers shouted, "Enough is enough!"<br>
"We know you're unhappy because of the past"<br>
"When the other Gigglegibbers made you an outcast."<br>

They gathered around him and he braced for a fight.<br>
"Don't be mad!" said one. "We will put the past right."<br>

"Though you were put down and all your presents got broke,"<br>
"Is that a reason to take from innocent folk?"<br>

Snarled the Grump, "Do not judge my sad childhood."<br>
"But," said one tiny elf, "You used to be good."<br>

And deep down inside, the Grump felt himself sway<br>
He had once been good; what had made him this way?<br>

The Grump stared at the small group and realized at last<br>
That they weren't being mean; they were all just downcast.<br>

And he knew deep inside he was no Frostfell-hater!<br>
He'd hidden his heart like a prestidigitator!<br>

He'd give back the presents. He'd give back the feasts.<br>
He'd even return all the pies of minced meats!<br>

The littlest elf extended her hand and invited the goblin to visit her land.<br>
"Come stay with us!" said the elf, "We'll help you stay true!"<br>

And that's exactly what<br>

Grimagus<br>
Grabblerouser<br>
Gigglegibber did,<br>
too.<br>
