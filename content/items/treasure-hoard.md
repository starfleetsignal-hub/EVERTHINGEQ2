## Notes

- This item can be purchased for **10** [[Loyalty Point Token|Loyalty Point Tokens]], allowing players to place them in multiple Standard [[Housing|houses]].
- It opens!
- The bonuses from having multiple Treasure Hordes does NOW stack. Each player house is able to reach a 100% reduction.
