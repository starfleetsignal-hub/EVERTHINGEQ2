## Cost

Server Transfer tokens can be purchased via the [[Marketplace]] for 2500 [[Station Cash]] or $25USD.

## Notes

If you want to transfer characters from one account to another, [[Phone Support|Customer Service]] will ask you to purchase a token and then create a transfer request to move your character to another account. Once the transfer is completed the token will be removed from your inventory. If you do such a transfer and your character remains on the same server; your guild affiliation will not be removed.
