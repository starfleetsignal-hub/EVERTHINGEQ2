## Notes

- This item requires materials from [[Bountiful Brewday garden|Bountiful Brewday gardens]], which can only be seen during [[Brew Day]].
- If you have the materials, you can craft it all year though. If you run low on materials, check the [[Broker]].
- If successfully crafted this recipe produces 3 of at once.
- This is one of many Brewday items with a hewn stone theme. Other examples include: [[Hewn Stone Bench]] and [[Hewn Stone Bridge]].
