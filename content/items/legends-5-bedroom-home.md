This is a Legends of Norrath Everquest II Loot card, titled "Legends 5 Bedroom Home"

When claimed, it awards the following item:

[[A tax-free Freeport residency license]] awards the [[Magical Academy Housing]] prestige home.

[[A tax-free Qeynos residency license]] awards the [[Concordium Tower Magical Manor]] prestige home.
