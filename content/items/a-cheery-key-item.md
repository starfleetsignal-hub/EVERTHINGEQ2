## Notes

- Required to see and unlock the chest that holds [[A Cheery Loom]].
- The recipe to make this is only sold during [[Frostfell]] to crafters with level 90+
- The key **must** be crafted in [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]] **during** the event, as the chest to loot it will only appear while the event is live.
- See the [[Frostfell Crafting Stations]] page for more details.
