## Notes

This item is only available during the annual [[Heroes' Festival Timeline|Heroes' Festival]] and requires a special, event-only currency: [[Mischeva's Tribute]]. It is one of many [[Heroes' Festival Merchant Items]] that can be used in [[Housing|housing]].

- To buy this item you must first defeat the event-only version of [[Darathar (Heroes' Festival)|Darathar]].
