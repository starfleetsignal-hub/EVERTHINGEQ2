## Notes

- This house item can be converted to the wearable [[Dwarven Ringmail Tunic]] by examining it.
- Contrary to popular belief, this furniture item is not a piece of armor on a stand, but rather a chest. As of [[LU36]], this item's appearance no longer shares the same model as the t7 crafted  [[Xegonite Chest]]. Instead, it uses the model of an [[Exquisite Chest|exquisite treasure chest]].
