## Examined Text

This ancient tablet is made from an unknown type of stone. It bears what some call the *Runes of Chaos*, however that has never been proven to be the true name of the sigil. The tablet looks as if it is just part of a larger tablet. When you touch the tablet you feel a great sensation of arcane energy flow through your body.

The center of the tablet has a raised hemisphere made of gold. Onto that hemisphere is engraved the image some refer to as the sign of [[Innoruuk]] while others think it is the sign of [[Veeshan]]. Aside from that there can be found five slots, four of which contain gold disks bearing unknown symbols. The fifth slot is vacant.
