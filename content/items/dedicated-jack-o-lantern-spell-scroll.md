## Notes

This pet **does** override your Deity pet and may override other fluff pets.

A fluff pet with a *poke* option that does nothing, this bouncing jack-o-lantern says only "!" or "..." when hailed. Otherwise it just follows you around. It overrides other fluff pets when summoned but not the deity pet. When it is up you may be able to summon a second fluff pet (depends on the pet). It was added in 2012.
