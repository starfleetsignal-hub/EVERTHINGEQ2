## Notes

See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity does not have a [[Guild Level]] minimum.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

A swirling portal that allows players to instantly travel to [[Housing|player housing]].

- This can be used to enter any home that:
1. Your character owns
1. Any house to which you were granted entry access (e.g. as a Friend or Trustee)

The above can only be carried out if the [[Standard Housing]] has its upkeep paid or any [[Prestige Housing]] (which never has any upkeep due).
