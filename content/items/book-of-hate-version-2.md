## Notes

- Formerly a reward from the quest, [[The Battle of Cauldron Hollow (Evil)|The Battle of Cauldron Hollow]], a world event.
- This book cannot be read ... it is appearance only.

### Buffs

When you attempt to open the book in your home, it infects you with the **Knowldge of Hate**  ![](images/Knowldge_of_Hate.png)

- This buff increases the damage of all divine and mental spells for all classes by 2%.
