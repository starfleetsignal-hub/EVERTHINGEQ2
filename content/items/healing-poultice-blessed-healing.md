This item will not show up in your inventory if you are on the quest [[Blessed Healing]]. It will applied directly against the four needed for the quest instead.
Requires the following to craft one:

- 1 [[Tunarian Trumpetleaf]]
- 1 [[Leonid root]]
- 2 [[Handama leaf]]
- 5 [[Effulgent Candle]]
