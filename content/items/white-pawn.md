## Notes

- This item is extremely small when placed in a home of guild hall. Depending on the location of the item, you may need to enlarge it using Decorator Mode (press CTRL + E to activate it) after placing it, and make adjustments in order to see it.

- 5 [[Effulgent sandpaper]]
