## Notes

- You can use Lore and Legend Tokens to purchase items from the Lore Token merchant [[Ronald Gleneed]] {{waypoint 1193.35, -533.12, 466.50}} at [[Thurgadin Harbor]] in the [[Great Divide]]. Most of the items are either cosmetic weapons and armor, cosmetic mounts (without stats) and house items. However, there are three crafting potions you can purchase:
  - [[Potion of Bountiful Harvests]] (increase rare harvests by 2% for 6 hours)
  - [[Potion of Critical Success]] (increase critical success by 5% for 10 minutes)
  - [[Potion of Success]] (increase success chance by 5% for 10 minutes)
- Ronald Gleneed can also be found in Freeport {{waypoint 108, -38, 66}} and the Qeynos harbor {{waypoint 905, -25, 77}}, however he has a limited selection of items and does not require Lore and Legend Tokens. However, to purchase items from the merchant in Freeport or Qeynos, you must complete the [[Legend Hunting]] quests on the Kael Drakkel server. Players who have completed the Legend Hunting quests will have their account flagged so they can purchase these items for other characters on other servers, including the Time-Locked Expansion (TLE) servers.
- The [[Spirit of Drakkel]] adornment does not require Lore and Legend Tokens. After completing all of the [[Legend Hunting]] quests, you can buy the adornment from Ronald Gleneed on any server at no cost. Note that this adornment can be equipped on TLE servers but will not have any effect on a server with PvP enabled.
