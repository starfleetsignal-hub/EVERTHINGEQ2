### Arena

The [[Arena]] was retired from the game, but this pet can still be used to practice combat if your guild has the [[Creature Conjurer]] as an amenity in the [[Guild Halls|guild hall]].

**Abilities**

| Name | Cost | Casttime | Recasttime | Duration | Range/Target | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frost Bite | 18pwr | instant | 3secs | 10secs | 22m, faced enemy | inflicts 26-39 cold damage and roots enemy (50% break) when bite terminates |
| Rending Sleet | 112pwr | 1.5secs | 10secs | 10secs | 22m, faced enemy | inflicts 286-429 cold damage |
| Frozen Embrace | 75pwr | .75sec | 12secs | 10secs | 35m, faced enemy | inflicts 177-265 cold damage, slows by 75% |
| Glacial Barrier | 75pwr | instant | 60secs | 10secs | self | 9 second stun (100% break) on enemies who inflict physical damage on you (3secs epics?) |

The Frozen Maji can deal a tremendous amount of damage and can slow and stop enemies from moving.  When under tricky physical attack, the Glacial Barrier can allow a retreat to snipe the enemy down.  This author isn't sure whether Frost Bite can be cancelled to get an instant root or not.
