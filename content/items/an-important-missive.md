*This missive is written in a very elegant and august script. It is addressed to [[Ruenok G'Koz, the Rook Lord|Ruenok G'Koz]].*

*[[T'Haen the Lost|T'Haen]] has foolishly lost control of his brood. This incident has broken the veil of secrecy that our Master has worked so diligently to maintain. Obviously, He is very displeased and wishes you to deal with this breach of contract swiftly and mercilessly. Silence T'Haen and wipe his ilk from the pages of history. - Falon*
