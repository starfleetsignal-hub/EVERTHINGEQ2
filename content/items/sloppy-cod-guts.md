## Notes

![Put bucket in inventory slot, put guts into bucket.](images/Six_sloppy_fish_guts_in_bucket.jpg)
![Go up the blocked pass nearly to the avalanche area, click the rock on your left to spawn The Wooly Beast.](images/Put_fish_guts_on_rock_to_spawn_The_Wooly_Beast.jpg)
This item is used to spawn [[The Wooly Beast]] a Level 8^ solo yeti monster in Frostfang Sea. Read the note in the cave by Dolur Axlebeard for the lore of spawning this creature. You don't need to read the note to spawn him.<br>

## Steps for Spawning the Wooly Beast

1. Click on *a stinky bucket* next to [[Arrin Clelland]] {{waypoint -294, 50, -864}}.
1. Place the [[Sloppy fish gut bucket]] in a bag slot then kill iceclad cods for [[Sloppy cod guts]]
   - Placing the bucket in a slot will trigger cods to drop guts
   - The fish guts will auto-fill the bucket as you collect them
   - You need at least four fish guts
1. Go to {{waypoint 304, 54, -252}} and click on the rock.
   - The bucket and the guts will disappear; return your bag to its slot.

Drops are similar to the other nearby nameds such as the [[Crushinator]]. Example [[Lustrous Bracelet]]
