## Notes

After placing this altar in your home, a worshiper may right click on the altar and select to commune with the deity. This action gives the worshiper a deity-specific version of the "Blessing of the Ancients" buff, described below.

Note that this buff does not stack with (but is a considerable upgrade to) the "Blessing of the Ancients" buff provided by the [[Altar of the Ancients]] house item, which came as the Terrors of Thalumbra expansion pre-order award.

Truthbringer's Communion

- Duration: 24 hours
- Description: Grants the target Blessing of the Ancients, improving experience gain, potency, ability modifier, experimentation success chance and primary attributes.
- Effects:
  - Increase Combat XP Gain of target by 10.0%
  - Increase Tithe Gain of target by 10.0
  - Increase AA XP Gain of target by 10.0%
  - Increase Tradeskill XP Gain of target by 10.0%
  - Increase AGI, INT, STR and WIS of target by 369.6
  - Increase Potency of target by 31.9%
  - Increase experimental success chance by 3.2%
  - Increase Ability Modifier of target by 3687.2
