## Notes

A few things to note while having this active are that the shapeshift abilities of the Warden do not visually override the effect of the eye. So, for example, if you have the gnoll form activated and use the warden's ability to become a wolf you will get the stat bonuses of the Warden shapeshift while still maintaining the visual appearance of the gnoll.
Secondly, if you happen to find a new piece of armor and want to see what it will look like on your character but don't want to end your time as a gnoll, remember you can always go to the options tab of the character screen and select "hide my illusion form." This will disable the audio and visual effects of being a gnoll and thus allow you to see how the new piece of armor will look on your character. Additionally, the visual effects of this item do not stay while on a mount.
