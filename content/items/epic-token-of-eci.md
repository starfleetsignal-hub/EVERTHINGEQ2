## Notes

- All boss mobs in Icy Keep: Retribution drop one or more Epic Tokens of E'ci.  Occasionally trash mobs will drop one as well.
- Icy Tokens of E'ci can be used to buy items from [[Ryni Frostfoot]] near the zone entrance of Icy Keep: Retribution.
