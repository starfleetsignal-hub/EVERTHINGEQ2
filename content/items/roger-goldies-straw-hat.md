## Notes

This is a yellow swashbuckler-type hat with an orange feather and rag (the part people apparently think is hair, but isn't).

The name and appearance is a nod to both Gol D. Roger, and Luffy's red-rimmed straw hat from the Japanese manga "One Piece".
