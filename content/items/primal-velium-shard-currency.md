## Notes

At the behest of pleas of the crowd from SOE Live 2012, beginning with [[Chains of Eternity|CoE]], [[Momo (NPC)|Momo]] now sell these, at the cost of 5 [[Pure Primal Velium Shard|Pure Primal Velium Shards]] (blue shards). Offering, albeit at a steep price, raiders with a glut of blue shards a method to obtain white shards (for replacing yellow adornments), without doing solo/heroic content.
