May be combined with other Basic Damage Effect Objects to create Combination Damage Effects:

- Creates [[Prismatic Power]] when combined with [[Elemental Power]]
- Creates [[Ethereal Force]] when combined with [[Physical Force]]
- Creates [[Venomous Blast]] when combined with [[Venomous Power]]
