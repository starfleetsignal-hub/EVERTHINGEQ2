## Notes

This is a replica of the [[Temple of Life]] in [[North Qeynos]], including the building's rotation, lightning and glowing ripples. The quest to obtain it, [[Fishing for Keys and Clues]], can be repeated to obtain multiple replicas. (The other reward from this quest, however, is lore, and you can only obtain one.)
