## Notes

As soon as this item is purchased a window will open that allows you to choose from several (4 or 5) high level, high stat equipment items normally sold by [[Noble Toowik Mclure]], that typically suit your character's adventure class. The selection is always comprised of heroic items.

If you are lucky, you can choose an item that normally costs more than double the amount in Loyalty Point Tokens. For example, you may get to choose a fabled weapon that normally costs 5 LPT at the 2 LPT cost.
