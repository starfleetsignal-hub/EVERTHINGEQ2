## Notes

Starts and updates the [[Thulian]] language quest.

- Lizardmen in Sinking Sands do not drop this item.
- Lizardmen in [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]] can drop this item.
- The Amygdalan in [[Tenebrous Tangle]] can drop this item.
- Wailing Spirits in [[Cauldron Hollow: The Witches' Cove]] can drop this item.
