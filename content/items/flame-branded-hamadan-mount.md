## Notes

In 2019 only players with a [[Free-To-Play|paid membership]] could "buy" this for *zero* [[Daybreak Cash]] through the [[Marketplace]] during the [[Scorched Sky]] live event.
*This may or may not be available after 2019 for future Scorched Sky events. Please confirm and remove this line as needed.*

- This is one per character, **not** per account
- If your character is at least level 35 this can be equipped like a ground mount, but you will need to be level 85 in your adventure or tradeskill for it to be used as a flying mount.
- This can be converted into a house item, [[Flame-Branded Hamadan (House Item)]]
