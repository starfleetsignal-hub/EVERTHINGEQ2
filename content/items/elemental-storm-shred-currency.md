## NOTES

After completing [[Against the Elements for Freeport]] ***or*** [[Against the Elements for Qeynos]] quest (depending on your alignment), you can continue hunting [[An elemental tempest|elemental tempests]] with their 5 elemental spawns for further rewards.
These rewards are in the form of collection members, for the collection: [[Raging Elemental Scintilla]] and by killing the [[An elemental tempest|elemental tempest]] at the end, you will also receive this new currency, which may be used to buy house items from certain merchants: For those aligned with Qeynos, it is [[Mage Fulcrum Gearshift (Elemental Storm merchant)|Mage Fulcrum Gearshift]], who is located near [[The Concordium Tower]] at {{waypoint 706, -20, 169}} in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. While in [[Freeport]], you need to visit [[Scientist Dally Scrumplecog (Elemental Storm merchant)|Scientist Dally Scrumplecog]] outside the [[Academy of Arcane Science]] at {{waypoint -10, -8, -90}}.
