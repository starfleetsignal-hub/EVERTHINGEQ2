## Notes

The mount was a gift from [[Daybreak Game Company, LLC|Daybreak]] to players with a [[Free-To-Play|paid membership]], available for **free** through the [[Marketplace]] during the [[Scorched Sky]] [[Live Events|live event]] in 2019. It may or may not be available during future events.

- To covert a mount into a [[Housing|house item]], press **C** to open the Character sheet, click on the Mount tab, place your mouse over the mount, R click to open the dialog window that asks if you would like to covert it to a house item.
