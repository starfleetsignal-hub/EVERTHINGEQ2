## Notes

The recipe for this is from [[Ironforge Exchange Basic Recipes]], which also includes the following items:

- [[Simple roll of fabric]]
- [[Stack of Coldain Plates]]

Though it is an Ironforge Exchange item, any crafter (around level 15) can get this the recipe book easily.

- If your character is from a good-aligned city, you need to do a series of quests for [[Envoy Tami Swifthammer]].
- If you are from an evil-aligned city you must do quests for [[Commissioner Varla Z'Velran]]. At the end of the quests for Varla, the recipe book is looted from a table at {{waypoint 61, 154, -130}}.
