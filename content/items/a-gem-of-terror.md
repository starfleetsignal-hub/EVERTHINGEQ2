This gem evokes terror in those that carry it. Such gems have only been known to originate in the [[Plane of Fear]]!

## Notes

Killed for about 45 minutes in the Cave of Fear and it finally dropped. -1042.01, 2.85, 853.67

Obtaining the item is a random update. A few people will be very unlucky and kill for 45 minutes or longer while a few others will get very lucky and have it drop on the first or second group of mobs after entering the cave.
