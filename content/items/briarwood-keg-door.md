## Notes

- This is a working door (can be opened and closed) only available during Brew Day and purchased using 30 [[Beer Token|Beer Tokens]].
- This door looks like the front face of a keg. For a more traditionally-shaped door available during the event, see the [[Barrel Room Door]].
- This door fits well in the entrance of the underground area in the [[Vale of Halfpint Delight]] (a [[Prestige Housing|prestige house]].
- This door matches the [[Carpenter|player-made furniture]] items with "vale briarwood" in the descriptions.
