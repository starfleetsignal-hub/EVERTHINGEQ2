## Notes

This recipe book only has a single recipe and an *ultra rare* material, known as a [[Primordial Fragments|primordial fragment]], must be *harvested* to craft it.

- This recipe is a [[Lore]] item, so you can only have one.
- The fragments needed to make it are **no-trade** and can not be gathered by a [[Artisan's Fully Trained Pack Pony|pack pony]] or [[Tradeskill Alternate Advancement|gathering goblin]].
