This item, along with three gems that drop off of trash in [[Veeshan's Peak]], is needed to buy the level 80 Fabled set armor for [[Bruiser|Bruisers]], [[Dirge|Dirges]], [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]] and [[Warlock|Warlocks]] from [[Avess Ryiss]] in [[Danak Shipyards]].
