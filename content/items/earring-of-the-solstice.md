## [[Master Artisan Red Shinies]]

As of [[LU46]], characters that have completed [[The Proof of the Pudding]] and received their earring can see [[Master Artisan Red Shinies]]. Each collection completed from them can be turned in for a reward.
This was later amended to not require **wearing** the earring, **carrying** it in your inventory, or even to have the buff effect **active** to see the red collections! **(See talk page)**
