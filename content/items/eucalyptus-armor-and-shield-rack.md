## Notes

If you are not familiar with [[Armor and Shield Rack|Armor and Shield Racks]], you may want to read about the general topic of [[Sales Display|sales displays]] that can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]].

- All armor and shield racks are Master Crafted (made from a rare material) by those who take up the [[Carpenter]] tradeskill. No matter what tier of rare wood used to make these sales displays, they all look the same when placed.
- They hold a variety of armor and shields of any type, typically made by [[Armorer|Armorers]], [[Tailor|Tailors]] and [[Woodworker|Woodworkers]].
