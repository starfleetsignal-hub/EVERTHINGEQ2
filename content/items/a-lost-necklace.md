## Acquisition

Obtained from [[A Gleaming Chest]] in [[The Hedge Hollow]] maze (only available during [[Nights of the Dead]]).
**NOTE:** The chest only appears if you have completed the prerequisites **over and above that required merely to access the zone** (see the above linked page!).

## Uses

This item serves two purposes if you keep it in your inventory:

- It is used to pick up house items in the Village of Somborn.
- It must be in inventory to start the encounter with [[The Headless Horseman]].

The order in which you do the above matters. Go to Somborn first if you want the house items, because The Headless Horseman quest consumes the necklace.

- Note:  you can reuse the same necklace in Somborn every year, as long as you have it (ie, you don't lose it, or use it to aggro The Headless Horseman).
- Tip: to locate it again each year: press **C** to open your Character sheet and type the item name in the search box below your character's "paperdoll" portrait. A list of items with this name or similar and their inventory locations will appear in the chat window.

### Use in Loping Plains

If you have this item in your inventory, you can collect [[Housing|house]] items multiple times by clicking a box.

1. Travel to the [[Village of Somborn]] in the [[Loping Plains]].
1. Locate the small red box on the floor at {{waypoint -309.49, 11.56, -48.52}} near [[Havras "Hack" Scutter]]'s feet.
1. Click the box to receive house items that you can use to [[Decorating Houses|decorate]] or sell on the [[Broker]].

The first time you collect the box, you get:

- [[A Foggifier De-Foggififer 5000]] - while the blanket of fog (see below) comes from a tiny mound, this looks like a fog machine and it can be turned off/on etc. at will.

You can also collect unlimited numbers of:

- [[A blanket of fog]] - a tiny mound that creates a fog effect when placed
- [[Screeching noise box]] - looks like the box you're collecting, makes a horrible screech when you go near it in a house or guild hall

The box respawns every few seconds and you can continue to collect the [[Screeching noise box]] and [[Blanket of Fog (Treasured)]] indefinitely while the [[Nights of the Dead]] is live.

### Use in Nektulos Forest

- At least one person in your group must have this item in their inventory in order to make [[The Headless Horseman]] attackable.
- Offering the necklace to [[The Headless Horseman]] consumes it.
