## Notes

- In order to buy this cloak, you need 30,000 faction with the Far Seas Supply Division.
- Purchased with 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]].
- The faction needed to purchase this cloak is from the [[Far Seas Supply Division (Faction)]], rather than the Far Seas Trading Company. Because the special currency that's needed to buy some items in the Isle of Mara is called Far Seas Trading Company Token, these two factions are easily confused.
- To check your faction, press C to open the Character window, click on the Factions Tab and set the drop down to [[Shattered Lands]].
