## Notes

- When you kill [[King Klak'Anon the Mechnamaximus]] he will always drop this thing onto the floor. ![](images/Floor_energized_clockwork_gear.gif)
- 1 person may click it to receive the Energized Clockwork Gear.
- Use it on the [[Master Clockwork Protocol]] once he puts up the defense shield (sometime <25% hp) to bypass the shield and kill him.
