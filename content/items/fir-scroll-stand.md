## Notes

If you are not familiar with [[Scroll Stand|Scroll Stands]], you may want to read about the general topic of [[Sales Display|sales displays]] that can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]].

- All scroll stands are Master Crafted (made from a rare material) by those who take up the [[Carpenter]] tradeskill. No matter what tier of [[Rare Wood]] is used to make them, they look the same when placed in a house. Only the quantity of (limited-scope) items they can hold changed with the tier,
- This sales display holds a limited scope of items that are [[Scholar]] made, like spell scrolls, combat art runes and distillations, Master spells, and recipe books.
