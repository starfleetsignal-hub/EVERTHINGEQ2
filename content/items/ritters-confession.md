## Notes

This is acquired during, and required for, the [[Warlock Epic Weapon Timeline]] quest, [[The Will of Kyrtoxxulous]].

## Lore

"So, you've figured out the truth about what really happened and now you've come and found my grave. I bet you're looking to take all the treasures I took with me to the afterlife! Well, too bad! I'm not buried here and you're never going to find me. I won't leave you completely empty-handed, however."

"Let me allow this letter to act as my full confession. Okay, so I didn't really kill Gherzhas like I told everyone. (Sorry to disappoint you, Samantha). Truth be told, that crazy old warlock was long gone by the time I went looking for him. The dagger I showed you was some cheap knock-off I bought from a traveling merchant. Oh, and the ashes were my morning breakfast's campfire!"

"You country bumpkins were all too eager to believe my story about killing him in, "the most amazing epic battle ever fought between two men!" Ha! I'm almost surprised no one else took credit for killing the warlock when he suddenly stopped tormenting your two horse town. Karana help you if he ever returned. Between the stories of his deeds and that dagger of his, I'd never want to tussle with the guy! Better luck next time!"

"Ritter Shortshank, "Hero" of Everling Village"
