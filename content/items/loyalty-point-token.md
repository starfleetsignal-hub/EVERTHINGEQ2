## Notes

This is a special currency earned by completing any two [[Daily Objective|Daily Objectives]]. It was implemented July 23, 2013.

- All account levels can gain loyalty point tokens, however there are certain restrictions on what free accounts can spend tokens on. For instance, free accounts can not purchase the [[A small bag of platinum coins|bag of 500 platinum]] reward.
- Loyalty Point Tokens are shared across an entire account so if you gain a token, all characters on the account "gain" a token and if you spend a token all characters on that account "spend" a token.
- See the [[Daily Objective System]] for details on earning and spending these tokens.
- To see the types of items you can purchase with this currency, see:
  - [[Loyalty Point House Items]]
  - [[Loyalty Point Equipment Items]]
  - [[Loyalty Point General Items]]
