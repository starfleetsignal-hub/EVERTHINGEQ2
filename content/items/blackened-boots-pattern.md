This item, along with three gems that drop off of trash in [[Veeshan's Peak]], is needed to buy the level 80 Fabled set armor for [[Guardian|Guardians]], [[Troubador|Troubadors]], [[Mystic|Mystics]] and [[Illusionist|Illusionists]] from [[Avess Ryiss]] in [[Danak Shipyards]].
