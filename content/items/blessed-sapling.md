## Notes

- <s>This item is only available for purchase until LU58.</s>
- Note: Picking a berry from one bush will lock all other bushes. You may only pick one berry per day per character.
- The three possible magical fruits are:
  - [[A sweet berry]] (increases xp gain)
  - [[A refreshing berry]] (increases movement speeds)
  - [[A flavorful berry]] (doubles duration of food and drink)
