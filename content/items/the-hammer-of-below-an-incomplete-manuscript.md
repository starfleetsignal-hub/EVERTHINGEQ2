This item is needed for the collection: [[The Hammer of Below (Collection)|The Hammer of Below]]

## Obtain

- Examine a chest in [[Stormhammer Keep]] in [[Kaladim]] at {{waypoint -101, 46, -81}} to spawn the book.
- Harvest the book, located around {{waypoint -195, 53, -200}} in the Hidden Nest area of [[Kaladim]].

## Notes

Collecting the book will allow you to harvest the remaining pages of [[The Hammer of Below (Collection)|The Hammer of Below]].
