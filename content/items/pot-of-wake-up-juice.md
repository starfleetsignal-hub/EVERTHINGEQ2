## Notes

- This is a ten-charge version of [[Wake-up Juice]], which removes the blurry screen effect if your character consumes alcohol.

When examined, a dialog will appear, letting you know this item contains 10 drinks.<br>
you are given 2 choices:

- Pour the drinks
- save for later

if you opt to pour the drinks you are given a stack of 10 [[Wake-up Juice]] (along with 10 popup flash messages letting you know about each one, thanks soe...)
