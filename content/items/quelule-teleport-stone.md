## Notes

- You must be in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]] for this item to work.
- If you Examine the item, a dialog will pop up.

![Dialog when examining Quel'Ule Teleport Stone](images/Quel'Ule_Teleport_Stone_break_off_a_pebble.jpg)
Select "Break off a pebble..." to receive [[Quel'Ule Teleport Pebble]] in your inventory. The original
item will remain unchanged.
