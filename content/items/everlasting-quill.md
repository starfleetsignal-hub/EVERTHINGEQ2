## Notes

Mobs in [[Sebilis]] and [[Chardok]] drop this item, however, the [[A Sathirian researcher|researchers]] in the library of Sebilis are reported to be the best targets (because of the association of researchers with books and quills). Patrons are also known to drop them.
Quill drops from the following mobs:

- random mob in [[Sebilis]] Library
- [[Hierophant Prime Kulgarn]] in [[Sebilis]]
- [[A Gorowyn hierophant]] in the [[Chardok]] Library
