Note: This is not a normal fish.  You will need a [[Collapsible Fishing Pole]] and a fishing skill of 400 to catch these fish which only spawn in the lava pits in the Skyfire Mountains.  It has a special use for Provisioners to create Fiery Magma Infusion and Magma Fish Reduction.
