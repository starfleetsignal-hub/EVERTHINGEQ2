## Book Text

This book is titled "The Lyresmith Poetry Collection - Children's Rhymes".  It is a collection of rather strange children's rhymes.

The following poems are more like songs than actual poetry.  Sung by children all over the world, it's hard not to smile when hearing a little one reciting the verses.  Though their choice of lyrics may seem a bit strange, the happy kids sing them with all their heart.  These are just a few that I've collected from the youngsters themselves over the years.

"Ring Around the Tower"<br>Ring around the tower,<br>Crackling full of power.<br>Shadows, shadows,<br>Norrath falls down.

"It's Raining, It's Pouring"<br>It's raining, it's pouring<br>The shadowed man is snoring. <br>Lopped off your head, <br>Now you're dead, <br>You won't wake up next morning.

"In a Tower, In a Wood"<br>In a tower, in a wood<br>Shadowed man by a window stood<br>Saw a halfling walking by<br>Knocking at his door, he was about to die

Help me! Help me! the halfling said<br>Wicked troll wants to eat me dead.<br>Little halfling come inside.<br>Take my hand and you I'll hide.

"Here Comes a Candle"<br>Here comes a candle to light you to bed<br>And here comes a shadow to chop off your head<br>Chip, chop<br>Chip, chop<br>Chip, chop<br>Chip, ch...

"Open, Shut Them"<br>Open them, shut them<br>Open them, shut them<br>Give a little clap, clap, clap<br>Creep them, meet them<br>Creep them, meet them<br>Put them in your lap, lap, lap

Know them, need them<br>Know them, need them<br>Right up to your chin, chin, chin<br>Bad old shadows<br>Bad old shadows<br>Open wide and let them in!
