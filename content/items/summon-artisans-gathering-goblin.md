## Info

- This [[Tradeskill Alternate Advancement]] ability from the Prestige line is a tiny goblin ability one can cast.
  - For the [[Kunark Ascending Crafting Timeline]] skill of the same name that replaces the summoned goblin with a tree and see [[Summon Artisan's Gathering Goblin (Harvesting Buff)]]

- After he appears, click the goblin to choose which materials (by [[Harvesting Timeline|by tier]]) to "send" it to get. The goblin will remain by you/follow you unless you cancel the spell manually.
  - Even if you manually cancel the goblin to reduce screen clutter while doing other things, he is still gathering!
  - The goblin has a small chance to return *with* rare materials.
- Recast him after a couple of hours and hail him to collect what he harvests.
- If it seems like you have a long recast timer, longer than you expect (12 hours maybe?), then you might want to be sure you have used the Commit button on your AA configuration.
- A similar minion spell, [[Artisan's Pack Pony]] and the upgraded [[Artisan's Fully Trained Pack Pony]] serve a similar purpose, but only the upgraded pack pony brings back rare materials and seasonal materials (e.g. [[Nights of the Dead Treats (Crafting Materials)]]). See the [[A Gathering Obsession Timeline]] for details on obtaining those.
