## Notes

- During this annual event, you can also buy (blue) [[Azure Stained Glass Square|Azure Stained Glass Squares]].
- This is one of several tiles that look stained glass (most are from [[Erolisi Day]] and [[Nights of the Dead]]).
- In 2013 all tiles were added to the [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|Building Blocks]] category in the game and have a separate item count from other furniture when placed in [[Housing|player housing]]
