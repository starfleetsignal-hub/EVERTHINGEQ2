## Notes

This is similar to the bouncy blocks used to play through the [[Bristlebane Bounce: Sinking Sands]] quest. If you place it in a [[Housing|house]] or [[Guild Hall|guild hall]] everyone can use this to bounce in the air. Lining up several allows player to bounce from one area to another.
