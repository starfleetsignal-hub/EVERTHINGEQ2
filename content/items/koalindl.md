## Notes

Examining one of these fish in the Pool of Jahnda (the pool under [[The Temple of Life]] in [[North Qeynos]]) will give you [[A koalindl scale key|a koalindl scale key]]. This key can be used to open a chest in [[The Down Below]] at {{waypoint -205, 0, -25}}. See [[Fishing for Keys and Clues]] for more information.
