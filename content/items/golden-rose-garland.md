## Notes

- This item mounts to a ceiling (or doorway) and has petals that fall down from the garland.
- This is one of many reward options, depending on successful you are at the race.
- Additional garlands and other holiday-themed decor can also be purchased with standard coin (silver, gold, etc.) and [[Erollisi Day]] coins and some can be crafted. See the [[Erollisi Day]] main page for full details about how to obtain these items too.
