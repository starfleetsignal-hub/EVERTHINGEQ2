## Notes

- This crate is a reward from the daily Flamebearer 'Untold' dungeon missions in [[Rage of Cthurath]] for each of the original 8 Cthurath-era [Untold Heroic] dungeons.
- The number (and quality!) of items awarded from each crate varies significantly with the chosen untold tier difficulty.
