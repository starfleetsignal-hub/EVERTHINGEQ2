## Notes

These runes are used to acquire the [[Serilian]] language spoken by Kobolds and Bugbears, however, unlike most other language quests, [[Language of the Dust]] is not started by examining one of the runes. You must speak to [[Lingualogian Roosey]] at {{waypoint -636, 175, 1094}} in the [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]] to start the quest.
You must be higher than level 24 to get this quest from the NPC.
Once started, all the runes you have held on to (you did keep them, right?) are now usable to advance the quest. You need 7 runes to complete the quest.
