- Heirloom item - Can be shared with alternate characters on the same account, allowing alts to skip the step of finding the 26 runes during the quest.

## Notes

Saves you time lost from hunting down all the runes in the quest [[To Speak as a Dragon]] if you've done it before on a character on the same account.

You may only buy this tablet with a character who has already completed and collected all the runes for the [[Draconic]] language. After doing so, you can place the tablet in Shared bank space to let other characters on the same account make use of it to study the runes at the appropriate point while on the [[To Speak as a Dragon]] quest (meaning, when the [[Sage of Ages]] asks you to seek out the runes, you can examine and use the tablet instead).
