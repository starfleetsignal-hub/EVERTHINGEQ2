## Notes

- This candelabra gives off a fair amount of light (not all lights do).
- This item was once sold in many of the [[Freeport]] village zones when they still had player housing.
- Though it's an item that has been in the game for a very long time and many new lights have been added since, it remains one of the most popular decor choices for both its colorful candles and the quality of light it gives off.
