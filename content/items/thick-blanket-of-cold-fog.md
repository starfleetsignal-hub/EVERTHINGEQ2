## Notes

This house item creates an effect that looks like a white fog.

## Obtain

1. Complete [[An Exalting Experience!]] and several of the quests from this year's Frostfell celebration first, so you can see the cube.
1. Enter [[The Icy Keep: The Return (Heroic)]] (within the [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]] instance).
1. Click the small red cube at {{waypoint 112, 21, -237}} in [[The Icy Keep: The Return (Heroic)]].
