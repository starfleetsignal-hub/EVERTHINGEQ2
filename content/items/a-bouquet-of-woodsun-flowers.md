## Notes

- This item was returned to the game with the [[LU64]] Qeynos revamp after a hiatus.
- Before the Qeynos revamp this was a reward from the quest [[Tribute Flowers]], part of the old [[Qeynos Racial Timeline#Wood Elf series|Wood Elf Racial timeline]].
- You can acquire this along with other former items from "good"-aligned racial quests, including the [[Green Wisp]] and [[Selkie Mushroom]]. These and other retired items are sold by [[Hegrenn]], the Qeynos furniture merchant.
  - She is near the [[Broker]] NPC, located inside [[Sneed Galliway's Trading Post]] (near [[The Claymore Plaza]]).
