These parts are ingredients to craft a [[Spidermech Aggressor]] familiar.

This box of part has a chance to drop in main chest reward from Tinkerfest PQ [[Quest: Hack 'n Slash]]. It will give you a random part that you do not have yet, no duplicates.

Besides these 4 parts you also need total of 140 x [[Ginormous scrap metal]] as fuel. These are mined from wreckage left after PQ. You can only mine 5 times from each wreckage (bountiful harvest helps double the amount of scraps received)

Recipe to craft the familiar is [[Spidermech Aggressor Blueprints]] bought from [[Myron]], one of 2  Tinkerfest merchants.
