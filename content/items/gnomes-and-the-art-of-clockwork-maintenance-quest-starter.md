## Trivia

- The title of the book is likely a reference to the [Robert Pirsig](http://robertpirsig.org/index.htm) philosophical novel, *[Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zen_and_the_Art_of_Motorcycle_Maintenance)*, the title of which was inspired by *[Zen in the Art of Archery](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zen_in_the_Art_of_Archery)*
