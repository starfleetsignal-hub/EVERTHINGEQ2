## Notes

See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity requires [[Guild Level]] 77 minimum.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

Grotto Sailing Ship looks like a sailing pirate ship.

- When this amenity is purchased by a guild leader, it is automatically placed in the underground dock area of the T3 and T4 [[Freeport-style Guild Halls]] or [[Qeynos-style Guild Halls]].
- It can **not** be moved.
