## Notes

If you are not familiar with [[Weapon Racks]], you may want to read about the general topic of [[Sales Display|sales displays]] that can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]].

- All weapon racks are Master Crafted (made from a rare material) by those who take up the [[Carpenter]] tradeskill.
- This sales display holds a limited scope of items that are typically made by [[Weaponsmith|Weaponsmiths]] and [[Woodworker|Woodworkers]] like weapons, bows and ammunition.

## Recipe

- Level: 23
- Class: Carpenter
- Technique: Sculpting
- Device: Woodworking Table
- Components
  - 1 Raw Fir
  - 3 Raw Ash
  - 2 Raw Boiled Leather
  - 1 Raw Carbonite
  - 6 coarse sandpaper
- Produces: [[Fir Weapon Rack]]
