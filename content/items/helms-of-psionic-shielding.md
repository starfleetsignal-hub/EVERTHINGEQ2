This is the basic helm recipe. The crafted helm can be upgraded to a mythical version. You need to gather four components. **Note:** can only be performed once as the materials come from *achievements* and are *not* dropped by mobs.

1. Defeating [[Mastersmith Naghig Dakgu]] in [[Furnace of Ages (Challenge Duo)]] give the achievement [[Stoking the Forge]] which yield a [[Soaked Coal]].
1. Defeating [[Tuatog the Tidal Lord]] in [[Abyssal Aqueduct (Challenge Duo)]] gives you a [[Purified Cave Water]]
1. Defeating [[K'zan the Automaton]] in [[Antechamber of the Automaton (Challenge Duo)]] give the achievement [[Automation at it's Best]] which yield a [[Mote of Gleaming Aqua Metal]].
1. Defeating [[Gen'Ra]] in *Challenge Mode* to get the achievement [[Gen'ra and the Warcouncil's Demise (Challenge)]] which yield a [[Vial of Ancient Tavalan Blood]].

The helm can only be crafted ([[Commission System|commissioned]]) by an armorer, and can only be created once per character as the components come from achievements, not chests.

After the helm is created, there are two more steps to create the mythical version.

1. Defeat [[Overlord Talan]] in *Challenge Mode* to get the achievement [[Overlord Talan's Demise (Challenge)]] which yield [[Talan's Prized Hydra Scale]].
1. Defeat [[Psyllon'Ris]] in *Challenge Mode* to get the achievement [[Psyllon'Ris' Demise (Challenge)]] which yield [[Pair of Ancient Gold Flecked Rubies]].

With the last two ingredients in you backpacks, examine the helm twice to upgrade it.

The first upgrade yield a **Hydra-Scale *nnn* of Psionic Shielding** depending on [[Class]].

The last upgrade yield a mythical **Indestructible Hydra-Scale Helm of *nnn***, depending on class.
