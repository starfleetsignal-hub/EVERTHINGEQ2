## Notes

- A glowing skull that does nothing but follow you around, it is the epitome of a fluff pet. It overrides other fluff pets when summoned but not the deity pet. When it is up you may be able to summon a second fluff pet (depends on the pet). It was added in 2011.
- Gives the ability [[Summon an Eerie Essence]]
