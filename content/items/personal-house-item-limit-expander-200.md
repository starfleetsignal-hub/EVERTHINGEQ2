## Notes

**This item will overwrite [[Personal House Item Limit Expander (100)|Personal House Item Expander (100)]] from marketplace if you  applied it previously. However it can be combined with  a player crafted [[Dimensional Pocket Expander|Dimentional Pocket Expander]] for a total 300 items added**
This item typically sells for 250SC ($2.50 US) though it goes on sale from time to time.

- The precise real-world value of items like this is hard to quantify, due to promotional offers that include Station Cash (SC); for more info on SC see the page about the [[Marketplace]].
- This will appear like a charm in your inventory if you purchase it and it must be consumed in the house you want to expand the item count in.
- You must *always* be inside a home you own; you can not use it in homes owned by others under any circumstance. You can not use it in guild halls (though that happened briefly when it was "bugged")

**Other Expanders**
As of 2014, there are two expanders that can add 100 items to the limit in a house.

- [[Dimensional Pocket Expander]], which is player made from [[Danak Arcana Volume II]] a faction purchase in Danak
- [[Personal House Item Limit Expander (100)]], which is also from the [[Marketplace]].

**Combining Expanders**
Though it's in the item description above, there is often considerable confusion about how to combine expanders, so here is a detailed description:

<s>You must **always** use a smaller expander before a larger expander</s>. You can now use marketplace and crafted expanders in either order, crafted one before or after using a marketplace expander.  In order to combine expanders they **can not** be the expanders with the same name. In other words, you can only combine one of each type from the Marketplace or one player-made expander with one from the Marketplace.

- <s>Though it's unclear if it was intended or a "bug," it may be true that you must use the player-made expander first, so it's advised.</s>  No longer the case in 2020-ish.
- As of March 2014 all three expanders are *not* "stackable", meaning you **can not** combine more than two, making the absolute highest added limit 300. To do so, you combine the 200 SC version with **<s>either</s>**  **only** the player-made expander (adds 100) <s>**or** the SC expander that adds 100 items.</s>
