## Notes

- Having this coin in your inventory allows you to access [[Crow's Resting Place, Thieves Guild]] by clicking on a statue at Loc {{waypoint -144, 0, -74}} in the qeynos sewer zone [[The Down Below]].
- [[Crow's Resting Place, Thieves Guild]] is required for many of the [[Swords of Destiny Timeline]] quests.
