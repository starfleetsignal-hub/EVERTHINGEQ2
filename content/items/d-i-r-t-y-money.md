## Notes

This coin is used by the Dedicated Individuals Recovering Technology of Yesteryear or D.I.R.T.Y. It can be exchanged for items with Jimbo McDaniel at the encampment above the Sinking Sands dock area.<br>
Collect [[D.I.R.T.Y. Money]] doing [[D.I.R.T.Y.]] quests in [[The Sinking Sands]] and turn in to [[Jimbo McDaniel]] to purchase reward items.
