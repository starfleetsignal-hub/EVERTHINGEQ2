## Notes

- This recipe book includes three items that make a matching [[Appearance]] armor set: [[Supple Hart Leather Chest Wrap]], [[Supple Hart Leather Leggings]], and [[Supple Hart Leather Gloves]].

To craft any of the items from this recipe book you will need materials dropped by monsters *only* during this holiday or you must purchase them from other players selling off excess supplies, using the [[Broker]].
*See the [[Erollisi Day Hard Candy and Love Notes]] for a list of interchangeable materials.
