## Book Text

The Riddlemaster's Tome

I.<br>
At night they come without being fetched<br>
And by day they're lost without being stolen.

II.<br>
Runs over fields and woods all day<br>
Under the bed at night sits not alone,<br>
With long tongue hanging out,<br>
A-waiting for a bone.

III.<br>
Round as a marble, blue as the sea,<br>
Unless I am brown or green maybe.<br>
Smile at me and I shine from my pane,<br>
Frown at me and down comes my rain.

IV.<br>
I never was, am always to be,<br>
No one ever saw me, nor ever will,<br>

And yet I am the confidence of all<br>
Who live and breathe on this global ball.

<br>
Answers<br>
I. Stars<br>
II. A shoe<br>
III. An eye<br>
IV. Tomorrow
