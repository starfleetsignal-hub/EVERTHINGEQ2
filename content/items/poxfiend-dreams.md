## Book Text

**Poxfiend Dreams**<br>
a collection of verse by Ranylle Leafborn

Wings of a dragon<br>
Venom of a manticore<br>
Face of my nightmares

Most confused of beasts,<br>
Wings, horns, venom, and chitin,<br>
Are you bat or wasp??

Oh poxfiend horror!<br>
Wings, claws, stinger, teeth, and horns,<br>
Unsettling my dreams.

Beating in the dark<br>
Not two but four leather wings<br>
The poxfiend cometh.
