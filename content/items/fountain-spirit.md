### Arena

The [[Arena]] was retired from the game, but this pet can still be used to practice combat if your guild has the [[Creature Conjurer]] as an amenity in the [[Guild Halls|guild hall]].

#### Ratings

- Strength*
- Toughness***
- Focus ****
- Resilience***
- Speed *****
- Health 563
- Power 475
- Melee: weak

**Abilities**

| Name | Cost | Casttime | Recasttime | Duration | Range/Target | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dampen | 30pwr | .5sec | 5secs | 10secs | 20m, faced enemy | inflicts 100-122 cold damage, decreases mitigation vs cold and magic by 200 |
| Aqueous Armor | 75pwr | instant | 5secs | 300secs | 20m, faced friend | ally/self gets buff giving +100 max health, +200 mitigation vs magic, +400 mitigation vs fire |
| Rejuvinate | 225pwr | instant | 10secs | 10secs | 20m, faced friend | ally/self gets 78-96 health and dispels 24 levels of noxious effects (desc says impairments, effects says beneficial effects?) |
| Restore | 112pwr | instant | 12secs | 10secs | 20m, faced friend | ally/self gets 129-158 health and power |

The Fountain Spirit is very fast and has powerful spells.  It can refuel and repair allies in combat and do a good amount of damage with spare mana using Dampen.  Make sure to always Aqueous Armor every team member you find.  The Spirit is probably only a viable choice in battles of larger teams.
