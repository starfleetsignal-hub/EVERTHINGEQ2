## Note

This is acquired during, and required for, the [[Warlock Epic Weapon Timeline]] quest, [[The Will of Kyrtoxxulous]].
Clicking on the grave of Yasinia Simond will spawn [[A guardian apparition]], which drops a small chest that contains this item.

## Lore

"I've been instructed to distill a particularly corrosive poison called Siv'ajauhar's Kiss. According to the recipe supplied to me by he who shall not be named, the chief ingredient needed will be a type of poison only found on the continent of Kunark. Once a significant portion of Siv'ajauhar's Kiss has been made, I am to hide it in a secure location until further instructions are given."
