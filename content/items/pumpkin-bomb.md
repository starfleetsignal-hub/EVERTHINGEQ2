## Notes

- You must buy Pumpkin Bombs (2 [[Candy Corn]] per bomb) from [[A Nights of the Dead merchant]].
- Throwing and being hit with these is meant to be beneficial, good fun for both parties as described below.
- The trick to getting the rare mount is to **only** throw 25-50 bombs per day. It takes roughly 800 bombs to get the mount. You can throw bombs anytime during the year, but can only purchase them during NotD. Throwing 800+ in one go does nothing but waste bombs, as the loot algorithm is set this way.

## Purpose

- Throwing these bombs is required for the [[Pumpkin Pulper]] achievement and **several** [[Nights of the Dead]] collections.
- Getting hit with these bombs causes a random collectible for this event to spawn in your inventory. Some of the collectibles are quite rare, so getting hit with bombs is helpful!

## Use

When you throw these bombs at another player,  several *mysterious pumpkins* (very small) appear in their vicinity.

- The target (or other nearby players) can collect the tiny, mysterious pumpkins to receive various collectibles.
- Meanwhile, you will earn credits toward various achievements depending on the number of pumpkins you throw and some collectibles in your inventory. The player who throws the pumpkin bombs **can not** collect pumpkins after throwing the bombs.

### Rewards for Picking up Pumpkin Pieces

Those who pick up the pieces of exploded pumpkin bombs get collection pieces:

- [[Deadly Remnants]]
- [[Frightful Fabric]]
- [[Crunchy Candied Crickets]]
- [[Ghoulish Masks]]
- [[The Great Haunt]] - this is a meta-collection; all of the collections above reward you with a collection item that goes into this final collection. The rare collection item, **Jack's Lantern**, is also from the Pumpkin Bombs.

### Tips and Tricks

- Meet up with friends or guildmates and toss pumpkins bombs at each other to complete the collections easily.
- Loiter near a travel hub, bank, broker, etc. and other players can bomb you easily in passing. The best spots are generally near the merchants for the event. See the [[A Nights of the Dead merchant]] page for locations.

## Rewards for Throwing Pumpkin Bombs

Throwing the Pumpkin Bomb at players can reward the following:

- [[Werehunter's Survival Kit]] (collection pieces)
- [[Frightfully Frosted Cupcakes]] (collection pieces)
- [[Ancient Desert Urn]] (house object)
- [[Sanguine Fountain]] (house object)
- [[Silk Lined Coffin Bed]] (house object)
- [[Baktor, the Voracious]] (rare mount)
