## Notes

Krono are purchased from Daybreak and can be sold or traded among players. When consumed, a Krono adds 30 days of [[All Access Membership|All Access]] membership time to the account of the player that consumes the Krono.

- All Access membership can be paid for by purchasing a Krono with standard in-game [[Coin|currency]] from the [[Broker]] or directly from another player. The in-game cost for a Krono varies based on the server type. They will typically cost millions of platinum on standard servers. The cost on Time-Locked Expansion (TLE) servers will be lower due to economic conditions for that server type.
- Krono is account wide, and can be exchanged in both EverQuest and EverQuest II. For example, a player can purchase a Krono using in-game currency in EverQuest, and then sell, trade or consume that Krono while playing EverQuest II.
- All Access membership can be gifted to other players by purchasing a Krono. It can take up to 72 hours for it to appear in your inventory, but once it does, open a trade with the person you want to give membership to by right clicking on their character while logged into the game.
- You can purchase a Krono and sell it on the broker for platinum. You can choose how much to sell it for, although you should check the current pricing on your server before deciding on the amount. There are no broker transaction fees associated with selling a Krono, regardless of the type of sales container it is placed in.
- Due to limits on the maximum price an item can be listed for on the broker, it is unlikely you will find Krono listed on the standard servers. Currently, Krono sell for several billion platinum and those transactions can only be done directly from player to player. If you wish to buy or sell Krono on a standard server, it's recommended you use the auction channel.
- When you purchase a Krono, it can take up to 72 hours for it to appear on your character, although typically it takes about 24 hours. This is intended as an anti-fraud measure, so do not be concerned if you purchase a Krono and it is not immediately available.
- Krono are not refundable and they never expire.

For more information about purchasing and using Krono, refer to the [Krono FAQ](https://www.everquest2.com/krono) page on the official website.
