## Notes

This recipe is used to craft [[House Actor Crate (The Crate)|house actor crates]]. The recipe only needs to be purchased one time to craft unlimited numbers of the house actors.

- It is one of many [[Loyalty Point House Items]].
- House actors are NPCs that can be customized by the owner.
- See the [[House Actor]] page for details.
