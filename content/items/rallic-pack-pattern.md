## Notes

- Examine it to get the [[Rallic Pack Recipe]]
- It's also needed to craft the [[Rallic Pack]]
- If you don't receive the pattern when you search the stunned grimling runner, delete the [[Grim Running]] quest from your journal instead of turning it in. You can then reacquire the quest from [[Skrit]] to try again.
