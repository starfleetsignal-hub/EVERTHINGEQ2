## Notes

To purchase this item, you must obtain at least *Kindly* (20k positive) [[Faction]] by participating in the [[Dragon Attack!]] event.

- Participating in slaying the dragons attacking the spires results in +1500 faction per kill.
- Crafting during the event adds +500.
