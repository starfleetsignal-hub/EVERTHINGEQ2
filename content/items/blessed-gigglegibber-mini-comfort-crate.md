## Effects

Touching the placed crate will cast *Bristlebane's Touch* on you.  The spell makes you look like various monsters.  The illusion changes with each zoning, and lasts until cancelled or death.

### Possible illusions

- Boarfiend
- Burynai
- Centaur
- Conjuror's [[Earthen Avatar]] (Adept version)
- Djinn
- Goblin
- Kunark goblin
- Nightblood
- Di-zok
- Yha-lei
