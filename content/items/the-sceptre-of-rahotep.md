## Notes

The actual reward is the [[Scepter of Rahotep]] which becomes a mountable house item after examining it in your inventory.
Note the subtle spelling difference between the house item **Sceptre** and the equipment item **Scepter.** The house item uses the British English spelling while the equipped item uses the American English spelling.
