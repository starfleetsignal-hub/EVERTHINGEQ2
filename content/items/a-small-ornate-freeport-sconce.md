## Notes

- This item is a reward for the [[Mysterious Designs]] quest, but afterwards you can purchase multiples from [[Vhishall]] in East Freeport.
- This item is very similar to the carpenter made item [[Fulginate Militia Sconce]], though the carpenter made item offers [[Rent Status Reduction]].
- It gives off very little light.
