## Effect

The machine will hunt anyone within the same housing zone it is in, and can be placed in guild halls. It will drop the target's hit points to ≈ 10% of the current max (non-combat insta-damage) and knock them down (5 second stun). It has a green glow when it has a named entered, and a red glow when it is actively hunting. It returns to where it was placed once its target has been "eliminated"
