## Notes

- This is part of the Building Block category and has matching pieces like columns, tiles, railings and more.
- In 2014 the item count (or limit) in all housing was split to have two item counts: one for regular furniture and one for anything in the building block category.
  - The item count varies by the size of the home, but the item count for furniture and all Building Blocks will always be equal. For example, a home that can have 500 furniture items will allow for 500 items in it with the Building Block category tag too, for a total of 1000 placed items.
