## Notes

![The Cherry loom when placed in player housing.](images/Cheery-loom-placed.jpg)

- This tradekill station can *only* be used to make [[Frostfell]] items.
- It can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]] or in [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]] and used year round if you have stocked up on Frostfell materials and Frostfell fuels like [[Elven Magic]], [[Holiday Cheer]], or [[Holiday Spirit]].
- This crafting station is looted after players with a tradeskill of 90+ craft [[A cheery key]] during Frostfell.
  - For details on this and the other two player-made Frostfell stations see the [[Frostfell Crafting Stations]] page.
