## Notes

- As the [[Return of the Gods Timeline|hand of the Gods]] became more prevalent in Norrath, the engraving on the idol became clear (as relating to the Void storyline).
- Examining the idol will make clear another message, as if you hear a voice speaking to you: "Within thickets of fiend and flesh, through a tunnel beyond death... I call to you."
- This item has a ring sparkle effect once placed in house. you can view it ![here](images/Image:_The_Idol_of_Everling_%28sparkle%29.gif), slight warning, its a 100k .GIF file

## Hidden Quest

- This item permits the owner to start [[The Rune of Sunder]], a forum lore quest.
