## Notes

***The recipe for this item "cannot be commissioned."***
To craft the backpack (a level 10 Artisan "binding" combine), you will need:

- 1 [[Emerald Clovers]]
- 1 [[Sudsy Stein]]
- 1 [[Pewter Cup of Imbibing]]
- 1 [[Maj'Dul Ale Rack]]
- 3 [[Basic Filament]]

The stein, pewter cup and ale rack are other recipes from [[Brewday Accoutrements to Craft]].
