In theory, the Aviak is a fast well rounded fighter with a lot of denial abilities.  Chaining the stuns will allow 10 seconds of stun, making the Hatchling an ambusher or disabler of enemies.  The Hatchling's damage is extremely low and the dps ability is terrible as well as the attack abilities being on longer cooldowns than most other pets; it is probable that the aviak hatchling is underpowered or trash tier as of LU47.
