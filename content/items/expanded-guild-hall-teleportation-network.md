## Notes

See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] page for a list of all amenities.

- This amenity has a [[Guild Level]] minimum of 100.
- This amenity is available for all types of [[Guild Hall|Guild Halls]].

## About this Amenity

This amenity functions like the [[Bag of Teleportation Pads|Teleportation Pads]] used in players housing, but they are *only* for use in the guild hall.

- The guild version of the teleportation pads can have multiple pad locations to make instant travel between locations is the hall easy.
- The pads can also be used to [[Housing Breakout Guide|"break out"]] into [[The Commonlands]] or [[Antonica]] if your guild has a T3 or T4 hall.
- Grants a maximum of 16 pads.
