## Notes

After "buying this house, players need to use in-game currency (gold, silver, copper) to pay "upkeep" and enter the house. To learn more, see the [[Standard Housing]].

In order to "buy" this house must have [[A Basic Maj'Dul Residency License]] (which only grants access to this 2 room home) from one of the Courts ([[Faction|factions]] in Ma'dul) to purchase this home.

### Maj'dul Houses and Faction

This housing can be purchased by anyone who has sufficient faction in [[Maj'Dul]] with **one** of the following:

- The Court of Truth
- The Court of Coin
- The Court of Blades

If you loot or buy the following tokens from the [[Broker|broker]], you can speed up your faction gain. If you buy them, make sure they are from one of the two factions you are **not** trying to increase faction with before you turn them in to, since all three are opposed to each other.
Tokens:

- [[Coin insignia token]]
- [[Blades insignia token]]
- [[Truth insignia token]]

## Quests

This is a solo instance of a standard Maj'Dul housing instance. These are used extensively in the quests given by the [[Tears grifter|Tears grifters]].
