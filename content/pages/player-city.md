A player city is simply any of the major cities you can choose for your character at the time of creation. They are most easily distinguished by the plethora of [[Standard Housing|standard housing]] available in them and guild halls *within* the city limits. Likewise, many [[Live Events]] have some form of event-NPC within these city limits or very near them in and adjacent zone (e.g. Darklight Wood--adjacent to Neriak).

## Locations

- [[Freeport]]
- [[Gorowyn]]
- [[Kelethin]]
- [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
- [[New Halas]]
- [[Qeynos]]

Though players can technically buy a house in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]], it is **not** a player city because you can not it as an city to travel to or adjacent to when you create a new character or to after you leave the [[Overlord's Outpost]] or [[Queen's Colony]]

## Alternate Terminology

- Because of the game's early history, which sent players directly to one of them, they are alternately referred to as "starter cities".
- Because of their centralized amenities like banks, brokers, and so on, they may also be generically referred to *major cities*.
- Some players may refer to them as "hubs".
