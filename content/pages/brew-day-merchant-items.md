The items on this page are sold by [[Balin Copperfoot]] and [[Digret Runebrand]] during the [[Brew Day]] live event in mid-March.<br>

All items that can be placed in [[Housing|housing]] or worn can be viewed using the "dressing room" window by holding down CTRL and L clicking on the item icon while in-game. Most of the items sell for [[Beer Token|Beer Tokens]]. See the [[Brew Day]] page for hints on getting Beer Tokens.

## New in 2026 on [[Balin Copperfoot]]

[[Balin Copperfoot]] in [[Bar of Brell]] at {{waypoint 332, -21, 139}} sells only new items in current year.<br>
