These quests were all started by examining a scroll, Starting a Tradeskill Profession, which was automatically in the inventory of all new characters.

- [[Seeking a Tradeskill Profession (Qeynos)]] - starter for good characters starting in [[Qeynos]]
- Seeking a Tradeskill Profession (Freeport) - starter for evil characters starting in East [[Freeport]]
- Seeking a Tradeskill Profession (Kelethin) - starter for good characters starting in [[Kelethin]]
- [[Seeking a Tradeskill Profession (Neriak)]] - starter for evil characters starting in [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
