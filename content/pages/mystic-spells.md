### Levels 1 to 9

- 1 - [[Velium Winds]] - Deals instant cold damage and reduces the movement speed of the target.
- 1 - [[Rejuvenation]] - Instantly heals some of the target ally's health. This also increases the target ally's maximum health for a short duration.
- 2 - [[Haze]] - An impairment that decreases target's attack speed and offensive melee skills.
- 3 - [[Plague]] - Deals disease damage over time to the enemy. Additional damage is dealt if the enemy is a being of shadow.
- 3 - [[Ritual Healing]] - Replenishes a large amount of target ally's health. This also temporarily increases target ally's maximum health for a short duration.
- 4 - [[Premonition]] - An augmentation that increases the health and power of the mystic's ally.
- 5 - [[Runic Armor]] - A group augmentation that increases physical damage resistance and grants a replenishing ward against physical damage to the mystic's group.
- 6 - [[Cure]] - Cures noxious, trauma, elemental and arcane impairments on the target ally. This includes poison, disease, slashing, piercing, crushing, heat, cold, magic, mental and divine effects.
- 6 - [[Summon Food and Water]] - Summons food and drink for the chosen ally.
- 7 - [[Rejuvenation II]] - Instantly heals some of the target ally's health. This also increases the target ally's maximum health for a short duration.
- 7 - [[Deteriorate]] - An impairment that decreases the enemy strength and stamina.
- 8 - [[Revive]] - Resurrects a fallen ally. That ally has resurrection sickness for a while. This spell cannot be used in combat, but can be used on allies that are not part of your group.
- 9 - [[Velium Winds II]] - Deals instant cold damage and reduces the movement speed of the target.
- 9 - [[Enduring Breath]] - An augmentation which grants an ally the ability to breathe under water.
