[[Nights of the Dead|Nights of the Dead 2008]] added several NPCs to [[North Qeynos]], [[West Freeport]], [[Kelethin]], [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]] and [[Gorowyn]].  They were all references to characters in scary movies.

- **[[Ann Wilkes]]** - Annie Wilkes of "Misery"
- **[[Annabelle Lekter]]** - Hannibal Lecter of "Silence of the Lambs"
- **[[Ardath Bay]]** - Ardeth Bay of "The Mummy" (the 1999 version) - this was also     used as an alias by Boris Karloff's Mummy, which is how it got into the 1999 Mummy
- **[[Bruise Hotep]]** - Bruce Campbell starred in "Bubba Ho-tep" (the only actor mentioned)
- **[[Carol N'Freeling]]** - Carol Anne Freeling of "Poltergeist"
- **[[Chuck Le'Ray]]** - Charles Lee Ray, Chucky of "Child's Play"
- **[[Damy Thorn]]** - Damien Thorn of "The Omen"
- **[[Dave Kessluhr]]** - David Kessler of "An American Werewolf in London"
- **[[Edward Hyde]]** - Edward Hyde of "Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde"
- **[[Frederick Kruhgar]]** - Freddy Kruger of "Nightmare on Elm Street"
- **[[Jack Torrents]]** - Jack Torrance of "The Shining"
- **[[Jayson V'rhees]]** - Jason Voorhees of "Friday the 13th"
- **[[Jonathon Har'kur]]** - Johnathan Harker of "Dracula" and possibly Jonathan Parker of "Shocker"
- **[[Karl Den Hamm]]** - Carl Denham of "King Kong"
- **[[Melony Danyells]]** - Melanie Daniels of "The Birds"
- **[[Mykel Miers]]** - Michael Myers of "Halloween"
- **[[Norm Baites]]** - Norman Bates of "Psycho"
- **[[Pazuzu]]** - the demon voice of "The Exorcist"
- **[[Phrau Blucher]]** - Frau Blücher of "Young Frankenstein"
- **[[Pynn Head]]** - Pinhead of "Hellraiser"
- **[[Reigen MacKneel]]** - Regan MacNeil of "The Exorcist"
- **[[Rose Woodhouse]]** - Rosemary Woodhouse of "Rosemary's Baby"
- **[[Shaun Rom'zomcom]]** - "Shaun of the Dead" (romzomcom stands for **rom**antic **com**edy with **zom**bies)
- **[[S'mara Mooregan]]** - Samara Morgan of "The Ring"
