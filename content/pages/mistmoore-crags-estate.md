![](images/Mistmore-crags-estate.jpg)
**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** 7 Year Veteran's Reward **or** purchased with [[Loyalty Point Token|Loyalty Point Tokens]]
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** huge, spooky vampire castle
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** ground mounts only

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Mistmoore Crags Estate is a replica of [[Castle Mistmoore]]. A smaller house with a similar look, [[Freeblood Lair]], is only available to those who have unlocked the Freeblood character race

This is a spacious 5 room home with a balcony, 6 [[House Vault|vault slots]] and no upkeep cost. The deed to this home is [[A blood-written contract]].  It was introduced in GU58 on October 12, 2010 as a 7-year [[Veteran Reward]].  It can now also be purchased from a [[Loyalty Merchant]] for 30 [[Loyalty Point Token|Loyalty Point Tokens]]. Though you can purchase additional contracts for this house it is still only **one** per character.

The home can be accessed from either [[South Qeynos]] {{waypoint 735, -20, 199}} or [[South Freeport]] {{waypoint -112, -18, 270}}.
