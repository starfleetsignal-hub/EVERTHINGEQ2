**Crushing** is a [[Skills|skill]] used by all [[Crushing Weapons]]. This skill is of particular importance to [[Mage|Mages]], [[Priest|Priests]] and [[Fighter|Fighters]]. This skill demonstrates how well you use hammers, maces, staves, and even your own fists and affects the chance that you will be successful when landing a blow with a weapon that uses the crushing skill.

The Crushing weapon you can use depends on your adventure level and class, but not on your Crushing skill:

- Crushing (Equipment) - Equipment that modifies the **Crushing** skill.
- Crushing Skill Adornments - Adornments that modifies the **Crushing** skill.
- [[Crushing Weapons]] - Weapons that depend upon mastery of the **Crushing** skill.
