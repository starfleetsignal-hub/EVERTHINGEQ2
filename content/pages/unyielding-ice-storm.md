## Effects

- Inflicts massive cold damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts heavy cold damage on targets in Area of Effect every 5 seconds.
- Decreases Haste of targets in Area of Effect by 15.0.
- Slows targets in Area of Effect by 50.0%.
- Decreases Mitigation of targets in Area of Effect vs. elemental damage.
