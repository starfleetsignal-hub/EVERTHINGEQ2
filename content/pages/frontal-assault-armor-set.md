---

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

[[Bad'Ushra]] in [[Moonfield Hamlet]] will exchange [[Mark of Manaar|Marks of Manaar]] for Legendary armor. The head, chest, and hands slots are expected to be filled with instance drops and are offered only as appearance items so characters may obtain a consistent look.

The only armor piece in this set that actually shows are the slippers. See the [[Demonplate (Armor Set)|Demonplate Set]] for appearance.
