Goblish is the common tongue of all goblins (and minotaurs).

- The ability to speak and understand Goblish is gained by completing the quest, [[Claiming the Goblish Tongue]]. The quest requires the examination of a random No-Trade drop, but can be completed by adventurers at various levels or by those working on the [[Kunark Ascending Crafting Timeline]].

## Quests Requiring Goblish

- [[Saving Soles]]
- [[Defender of the Faith]]
- [[Requesting Blessing]]
- Most of the [[Kunark Ascending Crafting Timeline]]
- [[Get A 'Shroom]]
- [[Secrets of Runnyeye]]
