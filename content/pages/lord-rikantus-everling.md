**Lord Rikantus Everling** exists in game in several locations:

- In [[Nektropos Castle: Tribulation]] he appears as [[Rikantus Everling]], a ghost who admits that his soul has been cursed and divided.
- In the countryside of [[Antonica]], a man named [[Rikantus]] guards the cemetery and his quest line is designed to ultimately lead you to [[A Calling in the Forest]], which was formerly the access quest for [[Nektropos Castle]].
- In [[Maj'Dul]], a man named [[Antiquitor Kantus Mor'Tael]] appears under one of the many tents of the city and plays an important part in the [[Peacock Club Timeline]].
