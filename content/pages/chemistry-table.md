**'Chemistry tables**' are used by [[Alchemist|Alchemists]]. In addition to each of the traditional tradeskill zones, free-standing chemistry tables can be found in the following locations:

## Qeynos

- [[South Qeynos]]: The Herb Jar  {{waypoint 651, -18, 238}}
- [[Qeynos Harbor]]: Echoes of Destiny   {{waypoint 793, -23, 99}}

## Freeport

- [[North Freeport]]: Torlig's Herb and Potions, Academy of the Arcane Sciences
- [[East Freeport]]: Poison Shop {{waypoint -134, -46, -47}}
- [[West Freeport]]: Backalley Brewhouse and [[Coalition Crafter's Association (POI)]] (the crafting area, not just the instance) at {{waypoint 33, -12, 80}}.
- [[South Freeport]]: Block and Tackle

## Faydwer

- [[Kelethin]]: Crafting House, Tunare's Pages
- [[Steamfont Mountains]]: Explosive Discoveries
- [[Loping Plains]]: Village of Somborn. Use the [[Magical cauldron]] next to [[Ihriel the Sorceress]].

## Gorowyn/Timorous Deep

- [[Timorous Deep]]: [[The Wards]] {{waypoint 2747, 124, 1122}}

## Neriak

- [[New Foreign Quarter]]: {{waypoint 88, 24, 108}} and {{waypoint 77, 24, 103}}
- [[Neriak Down Under]]: {{waypoint -161, -3, 280}} and {{waypoint -176, -4, 260}}

## Kunark

- [[Teren's Grasp]]: [[Stealth and Skullduggery]] (first building on right as you enter the outpost via the ramp)
- [[Fens of Nathsar]]: on [[Sathir's Span]] in one of the buildings

## Other areas

- [[Haven]]: [[Ekroosik's Replicators]] at {{waypoint -59, -32, -211}} and {{waypoint -53, -32, -205}}
