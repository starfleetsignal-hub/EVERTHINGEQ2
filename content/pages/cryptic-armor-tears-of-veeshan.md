## Description

Cryptic Armor is the initial entry-level dungeon-dropped armor available to solo players in the [[Tears of Veeshan]] (ToV) expansion.

More importantly, Cryptic Armor contains a special 'yellow gem' slot that can be infused with a special [[Glimmering Yellow Gemstone (Item)|Glimmering Yellow Gemstone]], which increases the raw stats of the armor item itself (as opposed to just putting a single yellow adornment with a single stat into a single yellow adorn slot).
*(The yellow gemstones can be purchased for 6 [[Draconic Etyma]] and 5p each from merchant [[Delvakin]], or crafted by players for 5 [[Draconic Etyma]])*

Players can see the status of the current 'gem slot' upgrade by examining ToV-specific armor pieces and noting the level at the top of the examination window

Cryptic Armor is awarded to players empty with 0 'gem slots' filled and can be upgraded with an additional yellow gem up to 4 times per piece of armor.
*Players looking to see what the upgraded stats for each of the 4 steps on a piece of armor will be can search the wiki here for the specific name of the item, appended with (0 gem), (1 gem), (2 gem), (3 gem), or (4 gem), example "[[Cryptic Last Resort Spaulders (0 Gem)]]"*

Players are given a quick how-to tutorial for upgrading 'colored socket' armor with matching colored gems via the quest [[The Real Ideal]] given by [[Veasna]] (after receiving an in-game mail from Veasna first).

## Where to obtain

Cryptic Armor is initially obtained exclusively from the [[Ornate Crate of Goods]] that drops from the final boss of each of the 8 [Advanced Solo] dungeons located on the [[Vesspyr Isles]] (or in the adjacent [[Temple of Veeshan]]).  Each Ornate Crate dropped from the final boss *guaratees* a random piece of Cryptic Armor matching your armor type every time (mages only get cloth, warriors only get plate, etc), with the possibility of additional loot (adorns, accessories, recipes) in addition to the armor piece.

## Alternate Source

As players run and re-run the solo dungeons, they will begin to accumulate copies of armor they already have, or loot pieces with sub-par stats they they'd rather not wear.

The merchant [[Zaszea]] @{{waypoint 32.03, 197.38, 26.17}} (standing right next to [[Veasna]]) will allow you to trade-in those 'extra' Cryptic pieces for other more-preferred Cryptic Armor pieces *(but only of the same slot)*. The unwanted gear will need to FIRST be converted into a slot-specific trade token via a consumable [[Zaszea's Armor Exchange]]. This process will return a generic trade token matching the slot of the item that was just consumed.  It takes **7** tokens of a single slot to purchase specific armor pieces from Zaszea - so if you are trying for a specifc piece, it can be a slow process.  The upside, is that the [[Ornate Crate of Goods]] guarantees an armor piece drop each time, so you may end up getting the piece you're looking for before you actually hit the '7-runs' mark.  Having this exchange system in place also means you'll never have to run a dungeon more than 7 times when trying to get a specific piece of Cryptic Armor. (Now, the accessories/recipes/adorns in the Ornate Crate, that's a different story...)

*While Zaszea does also do trade-in tokens for the upgraded Heroic Dungeon and Raid versions of ToV armor (Arcane and Potent Armors respectively), players CANNOT 'trade-up' from Cryptic to the other two more advanced type, nor can they trade-down to each other. Like Cryptic armor, you'll have to earn your Arcane and Potent armor pieces first and trade those exactly as you do with Cryptic (break down pieces first, needs x7 tokens, etc)*
