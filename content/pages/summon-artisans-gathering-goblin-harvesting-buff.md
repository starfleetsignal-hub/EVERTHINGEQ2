## Info

- One obtains this from the quest [[Tree of Growf]] if one is willing to "free" their gathering goblin during the quest.
- The quest has warnings telling you you will "lose" the goblin, but doesn't specify how the skill will change.
- The skill remains in your Knowledge book (press K to open it) but is changed.
- Casting speed bonus of 100% or better reduces the base harvest speed by 1/2, yielding 0.75 sec/ harvest node.
