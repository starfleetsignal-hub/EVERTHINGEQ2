The table below details the **Tier 1 Adornments** organized by the slots to which they apply:

**Lesser** adornments appear with no highlighting. They require 1 [[Ulteran Powder]] and 6 [[Lapis Lazuli Fragment|Lapis Lazuli Fragments]].  Up to 3 [[Lapis Lazuli Fragment|Lapis Lazuli Fragments]] are returned per craft.

**Greater** adornments appear with brown highlighting. They require 1 [[Infusion of Earth]], 2 [[Ulteran Powder|Ulteran Powders]], and 6 [[Lapis Lazuli Fragment|Lapis Lazuli Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Ulteran Powder]] is returned per craft.

**Superior** adornments appear with red highlighting. They require 1 [[Refined Mana]], 2 [[Infusion of Earth|Infusions of Earth]], 3 [[Ulteran Powder|Ulteran Powders]] and 6 [[Lapis Lazuli Fragment|Lapis Lazuli Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Infusion of Earth]] is returned per craft.

For a simple, alphabetic listing of tier 1 adornments, see Tier 1 Adornments.
