### POPULATION / ZONE PROGRESSION

*Chelsith: The Ancient Vault* [Group and Solo]

- Players with an existing lockout for these zones should be able to enter them again (this will reset the zone).

### CHARACTER ACHIEVEMENT TEMPLATES

- AA Templates: If you are not on your currently committed profile when you gain experience you will revert to that profile. This will cancel the build process if one is in progress.
- You can no longer commit an AA template while flying.

### QUESTS

- Darkness Ascending: Tear in the Drakelands - Exile faction characters will now receive a cloak reward upon completion of this quest.
