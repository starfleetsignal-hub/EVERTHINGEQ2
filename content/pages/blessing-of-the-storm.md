## Effects

- When damaged with a melee weapon this spell has a 10% chance to cast ***Wind Gust*** on target's attacker. Lasts for 3.0 seconds.
  - Stuns target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 9.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 1.0 second.
- On a melee hit this spell has a 25% chance to cast ***Storm Shock*** on target of attack.
  - Inflicts massive magic damage on target.
