**Subjugation** is the [[Skills|skill]] used by all Spells that mesmerize, charm, stun, stifle or root the target or targets. This skill is of particular importance to [[Enchanter|Enchanters]] and other [[Mage|Mages]].

"This is how well you use spells to command pets and control monsters." An increase in this skill decreases the odds that a spell that uses this skill will be resisted.

- Subjugation (Equipment) - Equipment that modifies the **Subjugation** skill.
- Subjugation Skill Adornments - Adornments that modifies the **Subjugation** skill.
- Subjugation Spells - Spells that depend upon mastery of the **Subjugation** skill.
