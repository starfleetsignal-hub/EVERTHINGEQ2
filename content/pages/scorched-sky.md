## Overview

Scorched Sky is the live event that begins in late June/early July. While in-game, click on the EQ2 radial button and choose the Calendar to check the exact dates. It was first introduced to EQ2 in 2018.

Devotees of Fennin Ro and his son, Solusek Ro are celebrating the cleansing and tempering power of flame in various locations. Players will receive an in-game mail inviting them to test their skills against flame-themed creatures. See the first official announcement for more lore in the History section below.

## Important Event-Related Clarifications

![An example of a Fireworks Launcher of the Festive Fireworks quest. These can be various colors.](images/Fireworks-launcher-quest.png)

- Buy the sparkler for 10 copper from either [[A Sparkler Merchant]] in an major [[Player City|player city]] **or** any [[Ember Merchant Assistant]] celebrating in several locations.
- [[Ember Merchant|Ember Merchants]] are conveniently located near the MOBs that can be killed to earn [[Coin of Ember]] and they will sell *all* available items
- [[A Sparkler Merchant]] can be found in the cities and sell *limited items* (designed match the limits of the TLE servers)

## Currency

The event's currency [[Coin of Ember]] is awarded by event's quests and also dropped in small chests by fiery elementals (they scale to level of player approaching them) around celebration camps.

Two Ember merchants are located among the Devotee of Flame celebrants. See the Locations section above for their waypoints. ![Examples of two building blocks sold during the event. Blocks are sold in packs of 8 or 15.](images/Scorched-sky-building-blocks-examples.png)

### A Sparkler Merchant

[[A Sparkler Merchant]] can be found in all of the [[Player City|player cities]]. They sell **limited** items because they were once the only NPCs for this event on [[Server Types|TLE servers]].

Players should buy a [[Festive Sparkler]] for 10c. The item is required to get and complete the [[Festive Fireworks]] quests.

## Quests

#### Unlimited Repeatable

The following can be repeated as often as one likes any time the event is live:

- [[Festive Fireworks]] - 6 separate quests in 6 major player cities. Can be done one at a time in any city  as long as you are not killed by guards. To begin you must **first** obtain [[Festive Sparkler]] for 10c from NPCS. See the [[Festive Fireworks]] page for details.

#### Annual Repeatable

Once a year players can complete the following:

- [[The Heat Is On]] - See [[Limora Roamhill (NPC)|Limora Roamhill]] at {{waypoint 37, -39, -13}} in [[Darklight Wood]] (near the [[Sableflame Crater]]).
- [[Feats of Burning Devotion]] - Speak to [[Devotee Aborasah]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -1479, -229, 2}}
- [[Brimfire and Flame]] - started from Shattered Brimfire Tablet, a random drop from any of Scorched Sky mobs
- [[Combusting at the Seams]] - started by [[Chandrima]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] at {{waypoint 266, 188, 435}} near [[Highland Outposts]]
- [[Thready for Anything|Thready For Anything]] (new in 2024) - offered by [[Harland Brimpebble]] in any version of [[Doomfire:Ro's Sweatshop|Doomfire: Ro’s Sweatshop]]

### Overseer quests

## History

- The first Scorched Sky celebration was held from Thursday, June 28, 2018 at 12:01 AM until Monday, July 9, 2018 at 11:59 PM.

### Announcement/Lore

The following announcement summarized the event as such:
*The Scorched Sky Celebration has begun, and worshipers of Fennin Ro and his son, Solusek Ro, can be found celebrating their gods across all of Norrath! During this special celebration, the devotees of Flame are offering a chance for all to rejoice in the primal power of Fire and Flame, with no grudge towards those who have embraced other deities. They believe that within the flames all mettle is tempered, both literally and figuratively. That which is weak, distracting, and fleeting will be burned away in battles against fire elementals, leaving only the strong, pure, and resilient.*
