**POPULATION / ZONE PROGRESSION**

*The Dreadcutter: The Pirate Kings [Raid]*

The scout cursed by the ogre witchdoctor should not be teleported unless there are not enough valid teleport targets.

The pirate adds should no longer occasionally stop spawning.

*Eidolon Jungle*

Shriekgloms in Eidolon Jungle will no longer alert other Harrowing Horde to your presence at such great distances.

**ITEMS**

Shiverback King Loop no longer has the same effect as the Shiverback King Stud. It now has an effect that is more appropriate.

Torment is now useable by brawlers and Beastlords.

Ethereal end line rings have been given their own effect and have been slightly improved.
