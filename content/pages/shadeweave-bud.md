![shadeweave bud, a harvestable node from Obulus Frontier](images/EQ2_shadeweave_bud.png)
Found only in [[Obulus Frontier]], this plant node spawns semi-randomly around the foot of large-trunk trees throughout the swampy forests in the north part of the zone.  The node always yields a rare (randomly one of either a [[Shadebark]], [[Shadeweave root]], [[Shadow quartz]], [[Shadowstone ore]], [[Leathery shadeleaf]], or [[Luclinite nodule]]); there are no common harvests from this node.

- There are 12 nodes total, but each node spawns in a random spot each time (so if you've harvested 12 in a relatively short span of time, that's all of them; come back in...)
- Respawn time for an individual node is 1 hour, at which point it will be respawned at a random spot throughout the swamp or nearby ocean cliffs
