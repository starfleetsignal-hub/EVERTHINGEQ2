## Default Number of Slots

- EQ2 subscribers start with [7 slots per region](https://www.everquest2.com/faq#Q3).
- Free-to-Play accounts (F2P) start with [2 slots per region](https://www.everquest2.com/faq#Q20).
- The "per region" means that you can have a full complement of characters on each of the server "regions", i.e., for a paying member, 7 on Test, 7 on the US servers, and 7 on the EU servers.
