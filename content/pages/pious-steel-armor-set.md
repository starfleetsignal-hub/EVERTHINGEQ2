- (2) +10 WIS
- (3) Applies Runic Recovery II
  - Reduces reuse time of benefical spells by 2 percent.
- (5) Applies Burst of Healing.
  - When target casts a healing spell this spell has a chance to cast Burst of Healing on caster Lasts for 15.0 seconds. This effect will trigger an avcerage of 2.0 times per minute.

This armor may be worn only by: [[Cleric|Clerics]].
These pieces are available for purchase from [[A mysterious Quellithulian]] in [[Moors of Ykesha]] at the [[Dropship Landing Zone]] {{waypoint 1680, 452, 894}}
