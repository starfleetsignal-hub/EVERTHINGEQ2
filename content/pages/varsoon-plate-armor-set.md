**Varsoon Plate armor set**

- [[Spaulders of the Immortal Guard]]
- [[Vambraces of Torment]]
- [[Thimzem's Breastplate of Undeath]]
- [[Barbed Legplates of Brutality]]
- [[Overseer's Sabatons]]

These drop from various named mobs in [[The Ruins of Varsoon]].<br>
All pieces in this set have the same appearance and coloring.<br>
There are no bonuses derived from wearing multiple members of this set.
