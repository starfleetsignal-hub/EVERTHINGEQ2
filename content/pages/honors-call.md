## Effects

- Applies ***Honor's Thunder*** on termination. Lasts for 9.0 seconds.
  - Stuns targets in Area of Effect.
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 27.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 3.0 seconds.
- Applies ***Marr's Heroism*** on termination.
  - Heals group members.
- Summons a limited pet to aid the caster.
