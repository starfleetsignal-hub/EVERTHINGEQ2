## Effects

- Applies ***Divine Exodus*** on termination. Lasts for 6 seconds.
  - Inflicts 15% of max health in divine damage on caster instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Increases AE Autoattack of caster by 20%.
- Increases Multi Attack of caster by 20.
- Enlarges caster by 100%.
- Increases Crit Chance of caster by 100.
