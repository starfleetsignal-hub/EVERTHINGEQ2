The tables below details the **Tier 9 Adornments** organized by the slots to which they apply:

**White Slot Adornments** are created by [[Adorner|Adorners]].

- **Lesser** adornments require 1 [[Void Powder]] and 6 [[Underfoot Fragment|Underfoot Fragments]].  Up to 3 [[Underfoot Fragment|Underfoot Fragments]] are returned per craft.
- **Greater** adornments require 1 [[Infusion of the Void]], 2 [[Void Powder|Void Powders]], and 6 [[Underfoot Fragment|Underfoot Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Void Powder]] is returned per craft.
- **Superior** adornments require 1 [[Distilled Mana]], 2 [[Infusion of the Void|Infusions of the Void]], 3 [[Void Powder|Void Powders]] and 6 [[Underfoot Fragment|Underfoot Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Infusion of the Void]] is returned per craft.

For a simple, alphabetic listing of tier 9 adornments, see Tier 9 Adornments.
