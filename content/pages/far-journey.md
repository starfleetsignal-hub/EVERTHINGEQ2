The Far Journey has sailed in many capacities on the seas of [[Norrath]]. She formerly transported survivors of the storms of [[The Shattering]] to the [[Isle of Refuge]]. She now appears in game in two places. When characters first start the game, they see her sailing between the [[Queen's Colony]] and [[Qeynos]] as well as the [[Outpost of the Overlord]] and [[Freeport]], transporting survivors of sea disasters. Later in the game, players learn that the Far Journey transports supplies between [[The Village of Shin|Island of Mara]] and various ports of call for the [[The Far Seas Trading Company (Faction)|Far Seas Trading Company]].
