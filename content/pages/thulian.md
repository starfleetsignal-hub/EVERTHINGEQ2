This is the language of the followers of [[Cazic-Thule]]. Some [[Category: Lizardman|lizardmen]] and [[Category: Amygdalan|Amygdalan]] (nautilus-heads) will only speak this language. The ability to speak and understand Thulian is gained by completing the quest, [[Fearful Words]].
