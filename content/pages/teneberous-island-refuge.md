**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** Purchased from the [[Marketplace]]
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** large, airborne isle
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** none allowed

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Teneberous Island Refuge is a an island, floating in the air a'la those of [[Tenebrous Tangle]]. The island is essentially a blank slate, making it easy to build anything you desire using [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|blocks]]. Though bare of trees and major plants, it does have (mostly) grassy fields. A small pond catches water that flows from a tiny island just above the main isle.

## Cost and Item Count

*Prices for all Marketplace items is subject to change at any time. Item limits have been increased in the past and can change at any time.*

- Cost: 1350 Daybreak Cash unless you have an [[Free-To-Play|All Access]] membership, which grants a 10% discount as of 2016.
- Base Item Limit: 900 and 900 [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]]

## Notes

This house does not have a typical area to [[Housing Breakout Guide|break out]] into, since it is open, but you can use a [[Bag of Teleportation Pads]] to reach the tiny upper island and build on it too.
