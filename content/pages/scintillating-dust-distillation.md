![Scintillating Dust Distillation](images/{{PAGENAME}}.jpg)
This recipe allows an [[Alchemist]] to refine several lower level dusts into a higher one.

- **Class:** [[Alchemist]]
- **Recipe Level:** 60
- **Workstation:** [[Chemistry Table]]

## Ingredients

- 3 [[Lambent Dust|Lambent Dusts]]
- 10 [[Flamerought candle|flamerought candles]]

## Yields

- 1 [[Lambent Dust]]
- 1 [[Scintillating Dust]]
