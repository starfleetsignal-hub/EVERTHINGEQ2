![](images/Hooluk_in_a_Hat.jpg)

A fluff pet that can be summoned by activating one of the Hoo'Loh hats. The hats are rewarded to you when you finish the quest [[Blood of the Brood]]. The pet will not help you in fights or perform other tasks for you; it is simply decoration.

Casting the pet will override any other fluff pets, including Deity pets, except for [[Zygomyd Sporeling]].

It will NOT override Elemental of Karana if you cast Hooluk first and then resummon Elemental of Karana

## Things This Pet Says

- "How many bites does it take to get to the center of a Freeport hard candy?"
- "Who?"
