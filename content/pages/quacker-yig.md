![](images/Quacker_Yig_vis.jpg)
Quacker Yig is another one of those pets that follow you around and don't really do anything but give you a friend to talk to while soloing. (He also quacks!)  To get him you must complete the quest [[Anas Mechanica Arcana]].  Upon completion you will receive the [[Anas Mechanica Case]].  Just use it and you will get your very own Quacker Yig to follow you around.

Quacker Yig does not override your Deity pet, but does override other fluff pets.

- "Ruffle my feathers... I can't reach this one annoying spot on the back of my neck..."
