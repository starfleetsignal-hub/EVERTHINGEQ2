The Ability Modifier stat increases the amounts of all abilities.

### Ability Mod Caps

Ability mod caps at +100% (Bumped up from historical +50%) of the base amount (factoring in Potency and primary stat +%) of the ability.

Some abilities have no cap at all. Many AAs are uncapped, and Strike of Consistency is uncapped.

The "base amount" includes taunt amount, all DoT ticks, and healing done.

Reactive Caps seem inconsistent. Mana cloak (Power Replenish), for example, cannot gain from ability mod. Others determine their cap by the total of their triggers.

### Application

The distribution of the amount varies depending on the type of ability:

DoTs with an "initial hit" explicitly defined in the tooltip will frontload the ability mod.

DoTs without an initial hit will evenly distribute the ability mod across every tick.

Reactives will divide the ability mod by the base number of triggers and apply to each trigger.

Lifetaps will split the ability mod across the healing and damage portion evenly

AOEs will assume 3 targets, dividing the ability mod by 3.

Combinations of the above will have combinations of the application. AoE Lifetaps only gain 1 damage per 5 points of ability mod, for example.
