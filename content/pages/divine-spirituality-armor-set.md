**Divine Spirituality armor set**

- [[Divine Spirituality Coif]]
- [[Divine Spirituality Vambraces]]
- [[Divine Spirituality Chestguard]]
- [[Divine Spirituality Bracers]]
- [[Divine Spirituality Gloves]]
- [[Divine Spirituality Leggings]]
- [[Divine Spirituality Boots]]

---

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

To be able to buy this armor set from [[Keortor Talyse]] (outside the entrance to [[The Infernal Forge of Ages]] in [[Solusek's Eye]]  at {{waypoint -68.56, -626.30, -58.67}}) you will need to obtain an armor component (dropped in the [[Kingdom of Sky]] raid instances) and three gem components (dropped in the [[Kingdom of Sky]] heroic instances) per item, i.e.:

- [[Divine Spirituality Chestguard]]: 1 [[Relic Chain Chestguard]], 3 [[Pristine Ruby]]
- [[Divine Spirituality Boots]]: 1 [[Relic Chain Boots]], 3 [[Flawless Jade]]
- [[Divine Spirituality Bracers]]: 1 [[Relic Chain Bracers]], 3 [[Flawless Turquoise]]
- [[Divine Spirituality Gloves]]: 1 [[Relic Chain Gloves]], 3 [[Flawless Red Beryl]]
- [[Divine Spirituality Coif]]: 1 [[Relic Chain Coif]], 3 [[Flawless Amber]]
- [[Divine Spirituality Leggings]]: 1 [[Relic Chain Leggings]], 3 [[Flawless Azurite]]
- [[Divine Spirituality Vambraces]]: 1 [[Relic Chain Mantle]], 3 [[Flawless Rose Quartz]]

This armor may be worn only by: all [[Scout|Scouts]], [[Berserker]], [[Defiler]], [[Guardian]], [[Inquisitor]], [[Mystic]], [[Paladin]], [[Shadowknight]], [[Templar]].
