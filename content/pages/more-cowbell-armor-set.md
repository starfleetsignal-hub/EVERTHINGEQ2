**More Cowbell armor set**

- [[Cowbell]]
- [[More Cowbell]]

---

- (2) Applies ***The Cowbell Tolls for Thee.***
  - On a hostile ability cast this spell has a 30% chance to cast The Tolling of the Cowbell on target of spell.  Lasts for 3.0 seconds.  This effect normalizes based off of a three second triggering event.
    - Inflicts 432 - 721 disease damage on target instantly and every second.
    - Decreases Combat Mitigation of target by 188.6.
    - This effect can only trigger once every 12.0 - 18.0 seconds.
