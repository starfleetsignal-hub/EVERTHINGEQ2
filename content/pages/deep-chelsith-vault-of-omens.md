## Overview

Deep Chelsith is an instance that was introduced as part of Sept 6, 2019 "The Vault of Omens" event, which was the prelude-event to the [[Blood of Luclin]] expansion.<br>*[Official Announcement (Pre-2023 forums)](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/sharpen-thy-wits-and-prepare-thyselves-mythic-passage-begins-soon.590487/)*

Appropriate level adventurers and tradeskillers (110+) were mailed [[A Vigilant Sanctum Key|a Vigilant Sanctum Key]], which summoned them to [[The Duality's Vigilant Sanctum (Mythic Passage)|The Duality's Vigilant Sanctum]] an area that gave them their corresponding event quests.

Adventurers were then sent to the Deep Chelsith to [[Light Amongst Shadows: The Vault of Omens|investigate]] strange happenings potentially involving the Shissar.

*Tradeskillers were sent... [[Piercing the Darkness: Carving A Legacy|on a different errand]].*

## Location

Deep Chelsith dungeon instance entrance is at {{waypoint -809, -52, 1205}} in the [[Jarsath Wastes]] zone and was designed primarily for level 110 pre-Luclin characters.

- *Swim there or speak to [[Brind]] on the Jarsath docks next to the travel bell to be teleported there.*
  - [[Deep Chelsith: Vault of Omens (Solo)|Deep Chelsith: Vault of Omens [Solo]]]
  - [[Deep Chelsith: Vault of Omens (Heroic)|Deep Chelsith: Vault of Omens [Heroic]]]
- The quest [[Light Amongst Shadows: The Vault of Omens]] article has a decent walkthrough of the dungeon itself and is applicable to both [Solo] and [Heroic] versions.

*This dungeon entrance is also already home to Kunark-era [[Chelsith: The Ancient Vault]]*
