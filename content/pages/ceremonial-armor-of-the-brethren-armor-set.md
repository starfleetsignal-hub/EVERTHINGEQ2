![](images/Ceremonial_Armor_of_the_Brethren_%28Armor_Set%29.png)<br>
Plate appearance armor that can be worn by all classes. There are no bonuses derived from wearing multiple pieces of this set.<br>

Sold by [[Quartermaster of the Brethren]] (Merchant) in [[Nektulos Forest]] Requires completing the Bloodlines Chronicle quest [[A Small Sampling]] in order to purchase.<br>

- Note that this is the armor set for **Evil** characters doing the Bloodline Chronicles quests. **Good** characters should refer to the [[Ceremonial Armor of Marr (Armor Set)|Ceremonial Armor of Marr]].
