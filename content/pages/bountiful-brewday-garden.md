Bountiful Brewday gardens are shrubs that only appear during the annual [[Brew Day]] festival. If you have your graphic effects turned up, these shrubs also have a green particle effect.

## Overview

![](images/001brewday_garden.jpg)

- In order to see them, you need to equip the [[Improved Ale Goggles]], which are a reward from the quest, [[Ale Goggles]].
- They are (primarily) found around the entrances to [[Bar of Brell]].
- Harvest these shrubs to gather ingredients required to craft items found in the themed recipes, like [[Brewday Accoutrements to Craft]].
- Materials gathered from these shrubs can be bought and sold using the [[Broker|broker]].
- This shrub and it's harvested crafting materials are also used for the (one-time-only) quest, [[Valla Brewhammer's Special Brew]].

- [[Darklight Wood]]

## Materials

The following ingredients can be harvested from a bountiful Brewday garden.

- [[Brell Barley]]
- [[Golden grapes]]
- [[Noble Hops]]
- [[Wild yeast]]
- [[Emerald Clovers]] (a rare item; you will get far fewer clovers than other materials)

The rarity of the clovers is impacted by many factors, including [[Tradeskill Alternate Advancement]] you may have chosen and any gear you may have equipped that grants additional bonuses.
For example, someone with the [[Cloak of the Harvester]] equipped will gather more than someone who lacks the cloak; someone with the cloak who is also a max level [[Carpenter]] (with the right AA) will gather even more than the first person.

### Artisan's Pack Pony and Gathering Bonuses

- If you completed the quest [[A Gathering Obsession Beyond The Grave]], you can have your [[Artisan's Fully Trained Pack Pony]] gather the ingredients for you automatically. The Fully Trained Pack Pony also has a much higher success rate to return with the rare [[Emerald Clovers]].
- If your character has a taken up a [[Tradeskill]] and advanced well, you can use your [[Tradeskill Alternate Advancement]] to gain various gathering bonuses, like an increased chance to harvest rare materials or a chance to harvest more materials each time you interact with the Bountiful Brewday gardens.
