These notes are pieces of information regarding the resistances and weaknesses of the various mobs found within the [[Chamber of Immortality]], the final encounter for the [[The Ruins of Varsoon]].

- [[Society of Varsoon research note A|A]] - "... the [[Extraplanar invader|extraplanar beings identified as Invaders]] have been observed to be particularly vulnerable to slashing blades."
- [[Society of Varsoon research note B|B]] - " ...the [[Extraplanar invader|extraplanar beings identified as Invaders]] have been observed to be especially vulnerable to cold based attacks."
- [[Society of Varsoon research note C|C]] - "...the [[Extraplanar denizen|extraplanar beings identified as Denizens]] have been observed to be especially vulnerable to piercing weapons."
- [[Society of Varsoon research note D|D]] - " ...the [[Extraplanar denizen|extraplanar beings identified as Denizens]] have been observed to be especially vulnerable to heat based attacks."
- [[Society of Varsoon research note E|E]] - "... the [[Extraplanar lurker|extraplanar beings identified as Lurkers]] have been observed to be especially vulnerable to slashing blades."
- [[Society of Varsoon research note F|F]] - "... the [[Extraplanar lurker|extraplanar beings identified as Lurkers]] have been observed to be especially vulnerable to magic based attacks."
- [[Society of Varsoon research note G|G]] - " ...the [[Extraplanar aggressor|extraplanar beings identified as Aggressors]] have been observed to be especially vulnerable to crushing hammers and clubs."
- [[Society of Varsoon research note H|H]] - " ...the [[Extraplanar aggressor|extraplanar beings identified as Aggressors]] have been observed to be especially vulnerable to divine based attacks."
