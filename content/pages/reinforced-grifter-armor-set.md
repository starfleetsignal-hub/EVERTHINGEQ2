**Reinforced Grifter armor set**

- [[Reinforced Grifter's Coif]]
- [[Reinforced Grifter's Birnie]]
- [[Reinforced Grifter's Handguard]]
- [[Reinforced Grifter's Chausses]]
- [[Reinforced Grifter's Boots]]

All pieces are obtained from the guild city raids, after turn in of the Fine (for head, feet and hand slots) and Ornate (for leg and chest slots) chainmail molds.<br>
There are no bonuses derived from wearing multiple members of this set.

This armor may be worn only by: [[Assassin|Assassins]], [[Brigand|Brigands]], [[Defiler|Defilers]], [[Dirge|Dirges]] and [[Troubador|Troubadors]]
