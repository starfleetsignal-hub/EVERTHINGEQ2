![The Gwenavyne as it appeared prior to The Shattering.](images/Gwenavyne.png)
**The Gwenavyne** was a pre-Shattering ferry that transported people across the frigid lake that separated the city of [[Halas]] from the rest of the world.  Its pieces now lie at the bottom of the waters in [[Everfrost]] waiting for [[A Deeper History of the Truthbringer|brave adventurers]] to discover.
