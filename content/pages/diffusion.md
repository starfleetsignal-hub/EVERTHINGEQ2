## Effects

- On a damage spell hit this spell will cast ***Healing Diffusion*** on target of spell. Lasts for 10 minutes.
  - While this buff is active, the next damage spell cast by the follower that successfully lands on a target will grant the follower a buff that increases all healing by 5% of the damage dealt to that target.
  - Grants a total of 1 trigger of the spell.
  - Will trigger off of cold, disease, divine, heat, magic, mental or poison abilities.
