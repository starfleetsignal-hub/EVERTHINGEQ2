## Effects

- On death this spell will cast ***Seething Vengeance*** on caster. Lasts for 10 seconds.
  - Applies ***Vengeance's Departure*** on termination.
    - Decreases threat priority of target by 24 positions.
    - Heals caster for 100% of max health.
      - This effect cannot be critically applied.
  - Wards caster against damage.
  - Heals caster.
  - Increases Haste of caster by 15.
  - Increases Multi Attack of caster by 15.
  - Increases Crit Chance of caster by 25.
  - Increases DPS of caster by 50.
  - Increases Casting Speed of caster by 50%.
  - Grants a total of 1 trigger of the spell.
