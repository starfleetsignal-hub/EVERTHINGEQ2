"Census" is the web api data database of Everquest 2 and other [[Daybreak Game Company, LLC|Daybreak Games]].<br>

Websites like [eq2wire](https://u.eq2wire.com/) or [Dragon's Armory](http://dragonsarmory.com/) use the Database to that database to dynamically create Information about toons and items.<br>
On this wiki the items and quests have a link to the census database to show Information about items and quests.<br>
The Census Information is used on this wiki as well to create Articles of Items and Achievements, The articles will be updated/added to the wiki by Chillispike's Bot

Your Character Information is updated on census when you log out with your toon.

More Information about census can be found at:

- [Everquest 2 Forum](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?forums/developer-announcements.297/)
- [eq2wire forum](https://forums.eq2wire.com/forums/census-everquest-ii.268/)
