## Effects

- Summons a pet Tunare's guardian.
  - This is an Earth Elemental Fighter and appears to be identical to the  summoned pet [[Earthen Avatar]]. The pet will scale to caster's level, and will taunt off everything but a fighter class of equal level. It also uses several special abilities to inflict magic damage on opponents.
