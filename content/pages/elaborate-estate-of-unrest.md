**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** purchased from the [[Marketplace]]
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** huge, [[The Estate of Unrest]]-themed
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** flying and ground mounts

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- You must get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- It has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- You can get a [[House Portal|portal]] to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]. Portals allow for quick travel between locations.
- You can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

**You can easily tour any [[Marketplace]] house by doing the following:**

1. Click on the EQ2 button in the bottom left corner of your screen
1. Chose [[Housing & Leaderboards]] from the list
1. Click on the Buy Houses tab
1. Locate the house you want to look at on the list and you can click the Tour button to see an empty version.

## About This House

![The bookcases can be clicked to enter the basement.](images/Unrest-secret-door.jpg)
Elaborate Estate of Unrest is a very large house based off of the [[The Estate of Unrest]].

Both this house and the vacant version have a secret door at {{waypoint -17, 4, 139}}. Click the bookcase to enter the creepy basement!

This version has some of the items seen in the (normal) zone still within; the [[Vacant Estate of Unrest]] is identical, but it is a completely empty version. Important to note though: the furniture within is in *fixed* locations, meaning it can not be moved in any way or packed in a moving crate!

On entering, you will find yourself at a pathway leading up to this house. To get to the house you must pass a fountain and hedge maze. The grounds around this house also features a gazebo.

The house is in a state of disrepair; the wall paper is faded and torn and areas of the floor have exposed planks.

Using a [[Bag of Teleportation Pads|bag of teleportation pads]], you can break out of the normal limits of this home. See the [[Housing Breakout Guide]] for details.
