The title "the Gladiator" is obtained for the number of kills in the [[Arena]].

For obtaining 5000 Arena Kills (using only Arena Champion Pets) you can purchase from the Arena Master outside the Arena in Maj'Dul the title of The Gladiator and a special Trophy Flag that can be placed in your home.  The title is a suffix - ie appears as **Your Name the Gladiator**.
