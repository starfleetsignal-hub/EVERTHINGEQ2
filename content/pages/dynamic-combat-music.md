Dynamic Combat Music is part of [[Rise of Kunark]], EverQuest II's fourth expansion since launch.

The basic idea behind this addition is to make the music reflect more of the situation during game play.

There will be different in-game combat music if the fight is going for you or your group/raid or against you.

This game addition was shown during the "Rise of Kunark" Panel at the SoE Fan Faire 2007.
