**Sturdy Warplate armor set**

- [[Sturdy Warplate Pauldrons]]
- [[Sturdy Warplate Chestguard]]
- [[Sturdy Warplate Bracers]]
- [[Sturdy Warplate Gauntlets]]
- [[Sturdy Warplate Greaves]]
- [[Sturdy Warplate Sabatons]]

Pieces in this set have the same appearance and coloring.<br>
No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

This armor set can be worn only by [[Guardian|Guardians]], and is rewarded for:

- [[Custom Fitted Armor - Part I|Custom Fitted Armor]] (evil)
- [[Ian's Forgetful Family - Part I|Ian's Forgetful Family]] (good)
