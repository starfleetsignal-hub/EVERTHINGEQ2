## Items that teleport the player within a zone

- Moves to the Hua Mein area while within [[Sundered Frontier]]
  - [[Jin'tu's Gift]] - charm, equip then use to scribe transport spell into 'Abilities' tab.
  - [[Jin'tu's Leaf]] - charm optional, 'broken off' of the Gift, use to teleport (same as scribed spell).

- Moves to the spire area while within [[Stonebrunt Highlands]]
  - [[Quel'ule Teleport Stone]] - charm, use to teleport.
  - [[Quel'ule Teleport Pebble]] - charm optional, broken off of the stone.
