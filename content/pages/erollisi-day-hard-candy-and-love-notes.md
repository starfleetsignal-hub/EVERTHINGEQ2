During the [[Erollisi Day]] celebrations, certain mobs body-drop love notes or hard candies. These items can be used as components in E-Day crafting recipes (sold by [[Steven]]), or turned into [[Erollisi coin|Erollisi coins]] by [[Liannya Heartswell]] to buy items from Steven or [[Douglas]]. If you save them for crafting, [[Heartgar MacInnes]] will convert them all into *barbarian love notes* for easy storage.

If you are trying to keep track of all the Candy and Love Notes for the Achievements, here is a ![Printable Erollisi Day Candy and Note Tracker for 2021](images/File:Erollisi_Day_Candy-Note_Tracker.pdf). It has all the candies/notes needed for the 14 achievements, with slots for 12 characters, and some location information.<br>
