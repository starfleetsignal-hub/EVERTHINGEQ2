![A frozen moment of Love](images/Love_%28Pet%29.jpg)A pet **Love** is a swirling vortex of red hearts that follows you around.  It does nothing except look pretty (the definition of a fluff pet).  Pet Loves are received by completing the quest, '[[Expedition to the Barren Sky]]' and casting the spell from either of the engagement ring rewards.

This pet does **not** override your Deity pet, but may override other fluff pets.
