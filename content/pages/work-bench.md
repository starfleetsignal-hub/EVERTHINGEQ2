**Workbenches** are used by [[Jeweler|Jewelers]], [[Tinkerer|Tinkerers]], [[Transmuter|Transmuters]] and [[Carpenter|Carpenters]]. In addition to each of the traditional tradeskill zones, free-standing workbenches can be found in the following locations:

## Qeynos

- [[North Qeynos]]: The Jewel Box, Sneed's Trading Post
- [[South Qeynos]]: Maile's Exchange, The Herb Jar
- [[The Elddar Grove]]: Antonica's House of Pottery

## Freeport

- [[South Freeport]]: Within the Block and Tackle at {{waypoint -218, -56, 190}}.
- [[West Freeport]]: On the 2nd floor of the Coalition of Tradesfolke at {{waypoint 24, -12, 96}}. Also down in the basement workshop [entrance at {{waypoint 17, -19, 85}}] of the Coalition of Tradesfolke at {{waypoint 24, -12, 96}}.

## Gorowyn/Timorous Deep

- [[Timorous Deep]]: [[The Wards]] {{waypoint 2791, 121, 1235}}

## Neriak

- [[New Foreign Quarter]]: {{waypoint 104, 22, 87}} and {{waypoint 106, 22, 83}}
- [[Neriak Down Under]]: {{waypoint -182, -2, 290}} and {{waypoint -180, -4, 255}}

## Obol Plains

- [[Cardin Ward]]: {{waypoint -333, 15, 6}}

## Other Zones

- [[Kelethin]]: Crafting House, Tunare's Pages
- [[Steamfont Mountains]]: Tinkerer's Building, Finbert's Mechanmagical Wonders
- [[Loping Plains]]: Village of Somborn, inside the building next to the forge near [[Melora Kisbacs]] the Weaponsmith
- [[Haven]]: [[Ekroosik's Replicators]] at {{waypoint -19, -32, -199}} and {{waypoint -30, -32, -205}}
- [[Fens of Nathsar]]: on Sathir's Span
