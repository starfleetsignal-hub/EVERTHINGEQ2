## Effects

- Applies ***Blind Eye***.
  - Draws considerable attention away from the caster for a short duration.
  - Decreases Threat to targets in Area of Effect.
- Applies ***A Makeshift Costume***. Lasts for 6 seconds.
  - Shapechanges caster.
- Increases Crit Chance of caster by 30.
