| **Zou Kunnen** |

| **Goddess of Magic** |

The former goddess of magic, upon her death, control over her plane was transferred to [[Druzzil Ro]]. Very little is known about Zou Kunnen, although the Glaufaye consider her to be their creator.

She has not yet appeared in EQ2.
