The last known Tier'Dal (Dark Elf) racial language spoken before the sealing of [[Fallen Gate|Neriak]].

## How to Learn This Language

- Buy a [[Thexian Language Primer]] from
  - [[Eredykil Zum'tok]], [[Gorowyn]]
  - [[Martok K'Fauxx]], [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
  - [[Scribe Hilja Saraste]], [[Kelethin]]
  - [[Krishana]], [[The City of Freeport]]
  - [[Dannon Ramsdell]], [[Frostfang Sea]]
  - [[Phyrace J'Despath]], [[Wanderlust Fair]] in [[Darklight Wood]]
  - [[Garglari]], [[Kylong Plains]]
- Reward for completing [[Symbols of the Damned]] (good) or [[Mysterious Artifacts]] (evil) in the [[Bloodline Chronicles Timeline]]
