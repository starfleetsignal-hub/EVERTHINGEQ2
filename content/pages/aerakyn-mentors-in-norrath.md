All races have a quest series in the cities, started by speaking with their "mentor" NPCs. The following quests can only be done by Aerakyn race characters.

## Overview

Aerakyn mentor quests can be found in both [[Qeynos]] and [[Freeport]], depending on character alignment.

- Qeynos mentor is [[Lonce Jellysmots]], located near the [[Clothspinners]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]] {{waypoint 336, -22, -181}}.
- Freeport mentor is [[Psellic]], located on the eastern end of [[Champion's Coliseum]] {{waypoint 169, -4, 20}}.
