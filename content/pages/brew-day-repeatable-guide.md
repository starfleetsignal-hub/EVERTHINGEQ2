**This walkthrough was originally added in 2017.**
*This walkthrough covers only all of the repeatable quests. For full event details, especially for some of the non-repeatables that are a requirement for this guide, see [[Brew Day]].*

## How to Use the Walk Through

- This walk through is designed to complete all repeatable quests in an efficient manner. You are not required to complete them in this order.
- If you follow the guide in order (down the page) you should be able to cover many steps for several quests in each zone. For this reason, the steps are listed by the zones you should be in as you progress.
- To make it easy to jump to a section if you had to stop and start again, zones you go to more than once have a number in the section title. For example, Bar of Brell 2 indicates it's your second time entering the Bar of Brell for a round of steps.
- Wherever useful this guide relies on portals and evacs, if you cannot use these methods there are often slower alternatives given in the guide.

*Get the quest [[Beers for Brell!]] from the [[Avatar of Below]] near the northern Qeynos Tower around {{waypoint -328, -14, 67}}.

## [[Bar of Brell]] 1 - Enter near NQ Gate

1. Get the quest [[Ale Goggles]] from [[Gilin Rockbreath]].
1. Get the quest [[Brell's Everlasting Brew]] from [[Rasel Alechaser]].
1. Get the quest [[Rummage for Answers]] from [[Tellar Blackswell]] (second alcove) and examine the [[Pristine Note]] he gives you.
1. Get the quest [[Snoogle's Presentation]] from [[Snoogle Blitzed]].
1. Buy [[Brewday Accoutrements to Craft VIII]] from [[Balin Copperfoot]] (optional for people who want the recipes).
1. Get the quest [[Thurgadin Ice Brew]] from [[Oldar Foambeard]] (last alcove).
1. Go down into the [[Serilian Bar]] {{waypoint 339, -27, 144}} to do [[Drinks Are On The House!]] from [[Sam]].
1. Buy a [[Dwarven Ale]] from [[Mraugl Stonecrusher]].
1. Give the [[Dwarven Ale]] to [[Oldar Foambeard]] for "Thurgadin Ice Brew".
1. Go upstairs into the [[Private Lounge]] {{waypoint 348.34, -14.49, 153.39}} for "Brell’s Everlasting Brew".
   - Serve him: **Coffee** -> **Ales**, *count* until his tone gets aggressive -> **Coffee** -> 1 **Ale** less than before and then Gnomish Spirits until he glows green to talk to.
1. Leave the Lounge and the bar to...

## Random Location

*From [[Feerrott]] **evac** or take the horse to the [[The Drowning Causeway|Drowning Causeway Station]].
*From [[Jarsath Wastes]] use the **Druid Ring** to Feerrott and then **evac** or take the nearby horse to the [[The Drowning Causeway|Drowning Causeway Station]].
*From [[Mystic Lake]] leave through the nearby door to [[Village of Shin]] then use the **bell**.
*From [[Steamfont Mountains]] use the **Druid Ring** to Feerrott and then evac or take the nearby horse to the [[The Drowning Causeway|Drowning Causeway Station]].

## [[Feerrott]]

*Collect a **Small Lizard Toy** after drinking [[Snoogle’s Special Flask]] at the cave entrance on the docks {{waypoint 73, 4, -92}} for “Snoogle's Presentation”.

## [[The Thundering Steppes]] 1 - Via bell

1. Talk to [[Gurag Bloodwart]] in the cabin at the end of the docks {{waypoint -573, -14, -487}}, **with and then without** wearing [[Ale Goggles (Equip)|Gilin's Ale Goggles]] for “Ale Goggles”.
1. Get the quest [[Pressed into the Brewcrew]] from [[Captain Gimdahr]] next to the dock {{waypoint -461, -25, -387}}.
1. Collect **pure water** from underneath the docks for “Brell's Everlasting Brew” and then **take the griffin** to [[The Village of Thundermist]].
1. Collect a **Flying Pig** after drinking [[Snoogle’s Special Flask]] in the ceiling of the big barn for “Snoogle's Presentation” {{waypoint 562, 2, -126}}.
1. Tip [[Betsy]] in the small barn {{waypoint 593, -1, -62}} and then run into Prew Drowser’s House to grab Prew’s Hops {{waypoint 549, -0, -67}} for “Brell's Everlasting Brew”.
1. **Evac** or **take the griffon** back to the [[Dead River Docks]].

## [[Isle of Mara]] - Via bell

***Wake up** [[Jyndeth]] who lies in the dock area “Pressed into the Brewcrew”.

## [[Antonica]] 2 - Via bell

1. Collect **pure water** from underneath the docks for “Brell's Everlasting Brew”
1. **Fly** or take the griffin to the back entrence of [[Blackburrow]] {{waypoint -1029, -41, 1173}}.

## [[Blackburrow]] - Enter through the "underwater" backdoor

1. Pour a **Sample of Stout** from the Blackburrow Stout keg in the brewery {{waypoint 20, -40, 57}} for “Beers for Brell!” *(Breaks Invisibility!)*.
1. **Go out and evac** (**Only** if you’re a goodie!) or use a druid ring portal to [[Greater Faydark]] and enter the Bar there.

## [[Bar of Brell]] 2 - Enter near NQ Gate or from Greater Faydark

1. Talk to [[Gergy Gigglegibber]] and buy him an [[Ale]] from [[Mraugl Stonecrusher]] for “Beers for Brell!”.
1. Talk to [[Oldar Foambeard]] for “Thurgadin Ice Brew”.

## [[Timorous Deep]] - Leave the Bar to there

- *Good Characters leave to [[Greater Faydark]] and then take the bell.*
1. Talk to [[Smurg Fleshreaper]] on the overlook behind the beach {{waypoint 2160, 21, 1167}}, **with and then without** wearing [[Ale Goggles (Equip)|Gilin's Ale Goggles]] for “Ale Goggles”.
1. Talk to [[Tarcha Niba'dn]] in the nearest tent at the docks {{waypoint 2342, 20, 1340}}, **with and then without** wearing [[Ale Goggles (Equip)|Gilin's Ale Goggles]] for “Ale Goggles”.

## [[Nektulos Forest]] - Via bell

*Collect **pure water** from underneath the docks for “Brell's Everlasting Brew”.

## [[Moors of Ykesha]] - Via bell

*Grab **Oldar’s Armor** from the crane platform {{waypoint 1748, 472, 901}} and dispatch the 2 clockworks that spawn for “Thurgadin Ice Brew”.

## [[Sinking Sands]] - Via airship if it is there, if not use the bell

*Grab **Oldar’s Shield** from the excavation pit {{waypoint -1352, -178, -737}} and dispatch the 2 monkeys that spawn for “Thurgadin Ice Brew”.

## [[Maj'Dul]] - Via carpet

*Talk to [[Amorous Ali]] and [[Amavi Kar]] {{waypoint -217, 160, -89}} just left inside Maj’Dul for “Beers for Brell!”.

## [[The Commonlands]] - Via carpet and bell or portal

1. Collect **pure water** from underneath the docks or in the lake for “Brell's Everlasting Brew”.
1. Collect a **Pink Elephant** after drinking [[Snoogle’s Special Flask]] standing near Pride Lake {{waypoint 432, -47, -333}} for “Snoogle's Presentation”.

## [[Enchanted Lands]] - Via bell or spire

1. Collect a **Strange Cabbage** after drinking [[Snoogle’s Special Flask]] from the tree nearest the docks {{waypoint 58, -1, 15}} for “Snoogle's Presentation”.
1. Talk to [[Ayndrew Celladar]] just inside the building on the dock {{waypoint 49, 2, 42}} for “Rummage for Answers”.
1. Get the quest [[Bog Mountain's Eruption]] from [[Maisy Sweetbread]].
1. Click the nearby a **halfling pony** and jump when you see a Minotaur on your left.
1. **Tip** the Minotaur [[Charvis Drunkhoof]] and then take his Brew from the **Brewing Bucket** for “Beers for Brell!”.
1. Kill 12 [[Runnyeye spellflinger|Runnyeye spellflingers]] (etc.) for “Bog Mountain Eruption”.
1. Enter the [[Runnyeye Hoard Pits]] and destroy the barrier with the movable **Boom Keg**.
   - Kill [[Ntemk Darktongue]] downstairs and then grab the **4 goblish stones** for “Rummage for Answers”.
1. Go out click the nearby a **halfling pony** to take you back to the docks.
1. Turn in [[Bog Mountain's Eruption]] to **Maisy Sweetbread**.
1. Turn in [[Rummage for Answers]] to [[Ayndrew Celladar]] in the building on the dock.

## [[Butcherblock Mountains]] - Via bell

1. Talk to [[Glug Irontoe]] at the docks near the ramp that leads up {{waypoint 691, 24, 603}} for “Beers for Brell!”.
1. Take the griffin up to the [[Highland Outposts]] {{waypoint 335, 189, 501}} and take the quest [[Deep Within Hammerhall]] from [[Steinsister Daglara]].
1. Take the griffin towards [[Greater Faydark]] and jump off near [[Kaladim]] to find and talk to [[Tumpy Irontoe]] for “Beers for Brell!”.
1. Enter the [[Hammerhall Barrel Chambers]] {{waypoint -379, 158, -523}}, kill all ghosts and then [[Brewmaster Thifgur]] to pick up the **Hammerstein** for “Deep Within Hammerhall”.
1. **Evac** back to the docks.
1. Use [[Liquid Courage]] on the docks and then talk to [[Tumpy Irontoe]] for “Beers for Brell!”.
1. **Wake up** [[Stormcoat]] who lies somewhere on the beach for “Pressed into the Brewcrew”.
1. Do the Race [[Brewday Beer Guardin': Butcherblock]] from [[Otengrad Brauer]] at the beach {{waypoint 242, 30, 270}}.
   - The golden drinks give a short runspeed buff, so construct paths between them, max reward with 90 goblins.
1. Fly up and turn in the quest [[Deep Within Hammerhall]] to [[Steinsister Daglara]] at the [[Highland Outposts]].

## [[Kylong Plains]] - Via druid ring

1. Use the **sokokar** to go to [[Kunzar's Edge]].
1. **Wake up** [[Tryh]] who lies on the area around the [[Kunzar's Edge]] encampment for “Pressed into the Brewcrew”.
1. Use a **portal** or fly to [[Teren's Grasp]] to take the spire there.

## [[Antonica]] 3 - Via spire

1. Talk to [[Krihz]] in the watermill in [[Windstalker Village]] {{waypoint -1899, -36, -613}} for “Pressed into the Brewcrew”.
1. Talk to [[Dala Plateheart]] in the same watermill, **with and then without** wearing [[Ale Goggles (Equip)|Gilin's Ale Goggles]] for “Ale Goggles”.
1. Kill the [[Animated hops]] in the fields around [[The McQuibble Farm]] {{waypoint -1846, -22, -201}} for “Pressed into the Brewcrew”.

## [[The Thundering Steppes]] 2 - Via spire

1. **Evac** to the docks
1. Turn in the quest [[Pressed into the Brewcrew]] to [[Captain Gimdahr]] next to the dock.

## [[Antonica]] 4 - Via bell

1. Grab **Oldar’s Pants** from [[Qeynos Castaway's Isle]] {{waypoint 237, -17, 875}} and dispatch the gnoll that spawns.
1. Turn in the quest [[Beers for Brell!]] to the [[Avatar of Below]].

## [[Bar of Brell]] 3 - Enter near NQ Gate

1. Turn in the quest [[Ale Goggles]] to [[Gilin Rockbreath]].
1. Talk to [[Rasel Alechaser]], **use** the [[Prayer to Brell]] and pick **A Wooden Stein**.
   - Turn in the quest [[Brell's Everlasting Brew]] to him.
1. Turn in the quest [[Snoogle's Presentation]] to [[Snoogle Blitzed]].
1. Turn in the quest [[Thurgadin Ice Brew]] to [[Oldar Foambeard]].
