![The Fearful Retreat as seen from a flying mount.](images/Fearful-retreat.jpg)

**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** Looted from Legends of Norrath, Drakkinshard card packs
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** large, foreboding
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** ground and flying mounts

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Fearful Retreat is a replica of the [[Shard of Fear]]. It features creepy eyeball trees, pools of water that smolder, and various minor flames. The sky is raging with red clouds.

## Notes

- You **can not** buy this house from the [[Marketplace]], because it is random loot from Legends of Norrath (LoN).
  - Players with [[Free-To-Play|All Access]] accounts get five free LoN card packs per month (as of 2016).
- Even though you can not buy it outright, this house can be toured by visiting general portal locations in Freeport and Qeynos (locs given above)
