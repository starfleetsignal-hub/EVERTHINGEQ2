## Key NPCs

*This page is is generic to all key quest turn ins.*

First, unpack your keys.

The key turn-in NPCs are not always up ; however, they always follow the same path, and they are trackable. Find them, then hail them to receive your rewards.

### a polished elemental stone key

- This is the common key received (~95% chance).
- The turn-in NPC is a Kerran female, [[Laalah the Wanderer]], Master Archeologista, who wanders a path that starts in [[Esoterra Gardens]] {{waypoint -491, 31, 743}}, wraps around to the coastline via a slope at {{waypoint -674.21, 20.35, 832.86}} then heads towards [[Zivier's Reach]] {{waypoint -381, 16, 867}}, where she de-pops.
- Laalah rewards lvl 103 solo-loot type items and merc accolades, all Heirloom. It's useful to note that some of these rewards are secondary slot items that can be cracked with **Extract Planar Essence** to give [[Planar Weapon Essence|Planar Weapon Essences]].

### a polished sandstorm pearl key

![Baaltus the Wanderer popped at this time,however it was immediately after i killed Tintinnal he popped up at the foot of the hill in amphitheater of song, heading up towards metetherial plains](images/Time.png)

- This is the semi-rare key received (~2% chance).
- The turn-in NPC is [[Baaltus the Wanderer]], Seasoned Architect, a dwarf who wanders a path starting at [[Amphitheater of Song]], then heading north through the [[Metetherial Plains]] towards the [[Aetherscar]].
- Rewards are mostly planar infusers, with an occasional bit of heroic-type Fabled loot.

### a polished magma stone key

- This is the rare key received (~1% chance).
- The turn-in NPC is a male ratonga, [[Squeekir the Wanderer]], Associate Archeologist, who wanders in the sandy clefts of [[Pakiat Bluffs]], first appearing at {{waypoint -715, 69, -267}} and de-pops at {{waypoint 669, 13.41, -893}}.
- Rewards Fabled-quality loot.
