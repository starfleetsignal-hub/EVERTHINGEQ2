| **Xul'Varien** |
|  |
| **Lord of Pestilence** |

The former Lord of Pestilence, Xul'Varien was replaced by [[Bertoxxulous]] at some unknown point in time. He is associated with the Xulous, an ancient civilization and creators of the [[The Necropolis of Lxanvon Vol. II (Collection)|Necropolis at Lxanvon]], who eventually were corrupted and consumed by Bertoxxulous.

[[Https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/category/races/xulous/ Source]]

He has not yet appeared in EQ2, but is mentioned in the collection [[Departed Deific Efforts]].

He is also mentioned in [[Anashti Sul]]'s Deity Quest in the following exchange:

Avatar of the Forgotten says to you, “Hmm… I do not feel the presence of Xul’Varien, but instead I sense this new being — Bertoxxulous, if I have heard correctly, who is evidently the new Lord of Pestilence."

This implies that Bertoxxulous took over Xul'Varien's role at some point while Anashti Sul was banished to the Void.
