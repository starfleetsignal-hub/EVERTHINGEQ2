## Effects

- When damaged, this spell will cast ***Shock of Faith*** on target's attacker.
  - Inflicts divine damage on target.
  - Grants a total of 10 triggers of the spell.
  - This effect can only trigger once every 0.1 seconds.
- Will absorb all damage. This spell will absorb a total of 10 attacks.
