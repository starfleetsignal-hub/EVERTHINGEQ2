**Disruption** is a [[Skills|skill]] used by all Spells that cause direct damage to the target or targets. This skill is of particular importance to [[Mage|Mages]], [[Priest|Priests]], [[Crusader|Crusaders]], and [[Bard|Bards]].

"This reduces your target's resistance to your Disruption spells." Mechanically, it decreases the odds that you will be fully resisted when casting a spell.

- Disruption (Equipment) - Equipment that modifies the **Disruption** skill.
- Disruption Skill Adornments - Adornments that modifies the **Disruption** skill.
- Disruption Spells - Spells that depend upon mastery of the **Disruption** skill.
