## Ranges

**Artisan Level Range:** 10-19<br>
**Recommended Minimum Adventurer Level of Harvesting Zones:** 8<br>
**Minimum Harvesting Skill Required:** 20<br>
**Trivial Score (Maximum Skill Gain Possible):** 95

## Zones

[[Antonica]], [[The Commonlands]], [[Greater Faydark]] (Tunare's Glade, Combine Spires, Crushbone area, Upper Emerald River area), the western half of [[Darklight Wood]] and [[Frostfang Sea]].
