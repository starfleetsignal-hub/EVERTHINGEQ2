*This event occurs close to April 1st each year. See the [[Bristlebane Day]] page for more information about the live event. Check your in-game calendar for the event start and end dates, which change each year.*

## About

During the end of March and beginning of April, players can join the fun on [[Bristlebane Day]]. Check your in-game calendar for exact dates.

As of 2025  this holiday has **2 merchants**: [[Hilarity]], who sells only items newly added in the current year, and [[Hijinks]], who sells all the items from previous years. Hilarity and Hijinks stand near each other and can be found near the crafting areas of the following locations:

- [[Darklight Wood]] at  {{waypoint -409, -63, 232}}
- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint 39, -12, 91}}
- [[Neriak, City of Hate]] at {{waypoint -145, -5, 263}}
- [[Frostfang Sea]] at {{waypoint -345, 176, -58}}
- [[Timorous Deep]] at {{waypoint 2746, 123, 1148}}
- [[Greater Faydark]] at {{waypoint 458, 93, 325}}
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 339, -21, -100}}
- [[Haven]] at {{waypoint 53, -32, -179}}

To check your event currency while in-game, press **C** to open the character sheet and click on the Currency tab.
