## 3 Birds On, 3 Birds Off

Before the raid, determine what the Courage score will be for each peafowl based on the number of players in the raid (see the [[Poacher Paol#Strategy|main page]] for specific numbers). Take that Courage score and subtract one to find the maximum number of players in a "hiding group". Assign each member of the raid into one of three hiding groups (tagging the players with the numbers 1-3), up to the maximum previously calculated. If there are any extra players (because there aren't enough spots in the three hiding groups), tough luck, those players are the designated sacrifices. Paol heals for each sacrificial death, so be prepared for a longer fight, but she doesn't repeat her one-shot attack script.

For example, a raid force of 24 players would have a Courage score of 10 and a maximum group size of 9. Dividing all 24 players into 3 groups is 8 per group (less than the maximum of 9) and all players can hide behind a peafowl. A raid force of 16 players would have a Courage score of 4 and a maximum group size of 3. This means 9 players can hide while the remaining 7(!) are sacrifices that will die every round.

Shortly after the six peafowl arrive, tag each of them with a different number 1-6. Ensure that each raid member recognizes which peafowl they will be using.

When Paol performs her attack at 85%, hiding group 1 hides behind peafowl 1, hiding group 2 hides behind peafowl 2, and hiding group 3 hides behind peafowl 3. Any extra players remain in the middle and accept their fate. This leaves peafowl 1-3 only one Wounds point away from death, while peafowl 4-6 are still Healthy. Killing Astra at this point partially heals peafowl 1-3, but the exact amount is unimportant.

When Paol performs her attack at 75%, hiding group 1 hides behind peafowl 4, hiding group 2 hides behind peafowl 5, and hiding group 3 hides behind peafowl 6. Again, extra players remain in the middle. This leaves peafowl 4-6 only one Wounds point away from death, while 1-3 are partially injured. Killing Astra at this point partially heals peafowl 4-6, but completely heals peafowl 1-3.

Continue alternating between peafowl 1-3 and peafowl 4-6, always hiding behind the Healthy birds and ignoring the partially-damaged birds. The three peafowl used in a given round protect their players while the three peafowl unused regain their strength. Repeat this pattern until 15%.

When Paol performs her attack at 15%, this is the final attack. The health of the peafowl is no longer important, as they won't be used again. Quickly reassign the raid into six hiding groups. Three of the peafowl should be Healthy and can support a number of players equal to their Courage, while the remaining three peafowl are partially injured and can only support a number of players equal to Courage minus Wounds. For this final round, the sacrificial players can join one of the six new groups and survive, sparing the raid from several percentage points of heals and the time of re-damaging Paol. Finish off Paol, and claim your prize.
