This guide is the new starting area for the new Dragon race called the Sarnak. The starting area appears to be 3 .The first island is called Chrykori Isle, which this particular Quest Guide covers.  The main point of this guide  is to give you an overview of what to do here. After viewing the opening movie, you are placed at the southeastern section of the island here. Right near you is [[Tykor Gi’lok]], who has your first quest to work on.

1. [[Poaching the Poachers]] (Level 1), given by [[Tykor Gi’lok]].
   - Kill 4 [[Haoaeran poacher|Haoaeran poachers]] before they affect the food supplies in this area. Reward: Scaled Armguards and 27 Copper Coins.
   - [[The Haoeran poachers]] can be seen right behind [[Tykor Gi’lok]]. Kill 4 of them and then return to [[Tykor Gi’lok]] for your reward for Quest 1. He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Curious Ore (Quest)|Curious Ore]] (Level 2), given by [[Tykor Gi’lok]]. Collect 3 Curious Ore Samples along the beach wall inland to the west of here and then bring them to [[Tykor Gi’lok]]. Reward: Reinforced Miners Sack (6 Slots) and 1 Silver Coin.
   - After this, you can also talk to [[Taroche Dry’Zok]] in the small hut here (a provisioner) for this quest.
1. [[Stuffed Gorger Claws]] (Level 2), given by [[Taroche Dry’Zok]].
   - Kill the Grit Gorgers in the waters to the northwest of here and then return to [[Taroche Dry’Zok]] with 4 Grit Gorger Claws. Reward: 56 Copper Coins.
   - You can now work on Quest 3.The small pond is just behind [[Tykor Gi’lok]] to the northwest of [[Taroche Dry’Zok]]. Kill the [[Grit Gorgers]] in this pond until you obtain all 4 Grit Gorger Claws.After this, go due west across the pond where you will see Haoaeran Diggers working on the Curious Ore. Kill these enemies as needed so you can mine the Curious Ore until you can obtain all 3 Curious Ore Samples.After you have all 7 items (4 Claws and 3 Samples), you can head east back across the pond and first talk to [[Tykor Gi’lok]] for your reward for Quest 2. He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Report to the Primary]] (Level 3), given by [[Tykor Gi’lok]].Take the Curious Ore Sample to [[Primary Conzuk Zum’ha]], who is located at  to the north of here at the top of a nearby ramp. Reward: Acid-carved Bracer, 1 Silver Coin and 7 Copper Coins.
   - Now, talk to [[Taroche Dry’Zok]] for your reward for Quest 3.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Delivery to a Veteran]] (Level 3), given by [[Taroche Dry’Zok]].
   - Deliver the Specially Prepared Bait to [[Citizen Niba’Ouz]], who is fishing just east of here.Reward: 1 Silver Coin and 3 Copper Coins.
   - As the quest suggests, head due east from [[Taroche Dry’Zok]]. You will see [[Citizen Niba’Ouz]] fishing at the other side of the water here.Talk to him using the phrase that you have some special bait for him for your reward for Quest 5. If you ask him what he is doing, he will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Fish of the Chrykori Isle]] (Level 3), given by [[Citizen Niba’Ouz]].Obtain 3 Chrykori Saltfin Fish by fishing in the waters here and then return to [[Citizen Niba’Ouz]]. Reward: 26 Copper Coins.
   - Now go in the water right by [[Citizen Niba’Ouz]] until you see a white writing that says “”.Get close enough to the writing and then right click on it and select “Fish”. Continue to do this until you have all 3 Chrykori Saltfin Fish (note that you can fish at the same area several times).After this, return to [[Citizen Niba’Ouz]] and talk to him for your reward for Quest 6. He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Bigger Fish to Fry]] (Level 3), given by [[Citizen Niba’Ouz]].
   - Obtain a Bull Saltfin Fish by fishing in the same area as before and then return to [[Citizen Niba’Ouz]]. Reward: Choice of Saltspray Slicer or Sharkblade and 59 Copper Coins.
   - Again, fish in the water right by [[Citizen Niba’Ouz]] in the “” until you get a Bull Saltfin Fish (note that this may take several tries as it took me around 5 attempts).Once you have this fish, go back and talk to [[Citizen Niba’Ouz]] for your reward for Quest 7. He mentions to you that the biggest fish around is Vethka the Gorger and that no one has ever caught this fish and to let him know if you ever find him.At this point, you should only have Quest 4 left to do. From [[Tykor Gi’lok]], head due north and you will soon see a ramp going up a hill.Take this ramp to get to . After you arrive, you can first talk to [[Primary Conzuk Zum’ha]] for your reward for Quest 4.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Disrupting Haoaeran Studies]] (Level 4), given by [[Primary Conzuk Zum’ha]].
   - Go to the cave that is both northwest of  and north of the [[Timeworn Pools]] and kill 4 Haoaerans and also obtain an Inscribed Pottery Sample of what they are digging up and then return to [[Primary Conzuk Zum’ha]].Reward: 63 Copper Coins.
   - Just south of [[Primary Conzuk Zum’ha]], you will see [[Civ-Parser Di’Xin]] in a small hut. Talk to him for this quest.
1. Chrykori Relics (Level 3), given by [[Civ-Parser Di’Xin]].Search the shores near  and then return to [[Civ-Parser Di’Xin]] with 6 Uneartherd Relics. Reward: 65 Copper Coins.
   - After this, head northwest from [[Civ-Parser Di’Xin]] and talk to [[Varkori Riz’Lul]] (Chrykori Village Chef) for this quest.
1. [[Hopefully the Meat Isn’t Timeworn]] (Level 3), given by [[Varkori Riz’Lul]].
   - Go to the [[Timeworn Pools]] to the west of Chrykori Village and kill the Briny Lizards in this area and then return to [[Varkori Riz’Lul]] with 4 Briny Lizard Meat. Reward: 55 Copper Coins.
   - You can now first work on Quest 9.Head back down the ramp and head to the Docks (see Zone Map if needed, but should be close to end of the ramp). From the Docks, look south along the beach and around the pool at the edge of the water for the Unearthed Relics.Once you find one, right click on it to collect it and put it in your inventory. Keep doing this until you have all 6 of them.Please note that a lot of the relics can be found at the very north end of the pool. After this, head back up the ramp to  and talk to [[Civ-Parser Di’Xin]] for your reward for Quest 9.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[The Ends Justify the Means]] (Level 5), given by [[Civ-Parser Di’Xin]].
   - Go to the [[Timeworn Pools]] to the west of  and kill the Haoaeran Scavengers and then return to [[Civ-Parser Di’Xin]] with 4 Scavenged Relics.Reward: Layered Scale Mantle, 1 Silver Coin and 5 Copper Coins.
   - At this point, you have Quests 8, 10 and 11 to work on.
   - All 3 are either at [[Timeworn Pools]] or just north of [[Timeworn Pools]]. Thus, you should now travel to find the [[Timeworn Pools]]. Note that on your way west, if you have your zone map opened that there are some quest givers just west of the southwest corner of the first pool that you already know about.For now, ignore these quests but keep in mind for later. Thus, continue due west until you get to the area that has what appears to be a large tear drop on the map, which is [[Timeworn Pools]].You can now work on Quests 10 and 11 here. At this area, kill the Briny Lizards and the Haoaeran Scavengers until you have all 8 items (4 Briny Lizard Meat and 4 Scavenged Relics).After this, look at your zone map and you will see a star symbol to the north of the [[Timeworn Pools]]. Head over to this star symbol and you will find the cave for Quest 8. Kill the Haoaeran Excavators and/or the Haoeran Taskers here until you have all 4 kills. Be careful as the Taskers will attack you on sight.After this, enter the cave and you should see an Excavated Debris on the ground. Right click on this Debris to gather and put it in your inventory for the Inscribed Pottery Sample you need for Quest 8.After this, you can head back southeast to . Once here, first talk to [[Primary Conzuk Zum’ha]] for your reward for Quest 8.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Disrupting Haoaeran Rituals]] (Level 6), given by [[Primary Conzuk Zum’ha]].
   - Go to the [[Timeworn Pools]] to the west of  and disrupt the strange Haoaeran Ritual that is going on south of the large statue in the Pool.Reward: Earring of Zum’Ha, 1 Silver Coin and 20 Copper Coins.
   - Now talk to [[Varkori Riz’Lul]] for your reward for Quest 10. He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Sandy Eggs]] (Level 5), given by [[Varkori Riz’Lul]].
   - Search the Tortoise Nests in the Dunes to the west of  and return to [[Varkori Riz’Lul]] with 6 Tortoise Eggs. Reward: Layered Scale Gauntlets and 60 Copper Coins.
   - Now talk to [[Civ-Parser Di’Xin]] for your reward for Quest 11.After this, talk to [[Tertiary Mak’Ki]], who is standing right next to [[Primary Conzuk Zum’ha]] for this quest.
1. [[Don’t Get it in Your Eyes]] (Level 5), given by [[Tertiary Mak’Ki]].
   - Go to the [[Timeworn Pools]] to the west of  and kill the Crusty Rustbeasts in the area and then return to [[Tertiary Mak’Ki]] with 5 Acid Sacs.Reward: Acid-inscribed Ring and 66 Copper Coins.
   - You can now head west from  and start to head back to the [[Timeworn Pools]]. About half of the way there, you should see the Timorous Tortoises wandering about.When you see this, stop and start to look for the Tortoise Eggs for Quest 13. When you see one, right click on it to gather it into your inventory.Continue to gather the Tortoise Eggs until you have all 6 needed for this quest (you will encounter no resistance in doing so). Then continue west until you reach the [[Timeworn Pools]].If you look at you zone map, you will see a 2nd smaller pool to the west of the main pool that is in the shape of a backwards letter C. Here in this smaller pool are the Crusty Rustbeasts.Kill these enemies until you have all 5 Acid Sacs you need for Quest 14. After this, head slightly south from the bigger Pool (you may have to go around a stone barrier.You should soon see some Haoaerans at an Altar Site (3 Haoaeran Ritualists and 1 Power Infused Haoaeran). This will be your first big battle test as the Ritualists are all Level 6.If you can, try to try one at a time to kill all 3 of them. If you are successful, the Power Infused Haoaeran will say that you will pay for killing the Ritualists, but the Ritual is cut short and he quickly dies.After this, you can now return east back to  (use zone map as needed). Once you arrive, first talk to [[Primary Conzuk Zum’ha]] for your reward for Quest 12.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Further Assistance to]] (Level 6), given by [[Primary Conzuk Zum’ha]]. Report to [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]], who is located in Trythec Loft to the southwest of  (up a wooden ramp).Reward: None.
   - Now talk to [[Varkori Riz’Lul]] for your reward for Quest 13. After this, talk to [[Tertiary Mak’Ki]] for your reward for Quest 14.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Don’t Get It in Arozik’s Eyes]] (Level 6), given by [[Tertiary Mak’Ki]].
   - Deliver the Crusty Rustbeast Acid to [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]], who is located in Trythec Loft to the southwest of  (up a wooden ramp).Reward: 1 Silver Coin and 7 Copper Coins.
   - After this, head towards what looks to be a triangular shaped icon on your zone map that is southeast of the [[Timeworn Pools]] area. Right where the Haoaeran Diggers are located (from Quest 2 above), you should see a wooden ramp.Take this wooden ramp until you reach the area of Trythec Loft. Head to the hut in this area and first talk to [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]] for your rewards for Quest 15 by selecting the “Primary Zum’Ha directed me to report to you” talk bubble.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Haoaeran Poisons]] (Level 6), given by [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]].
   - Kill the Skittering Monitors in the Trythec Loft area and then return to Secondary Arozik Sky’ Ki with 6 Poison Glands.Reward: 65 Copper Coins.
   - After this, talk to [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]] again for your reward for Quest 16. Then talk to [[Bio-Parser Zo’Dok]], who is also in the hut for this quest.
1. [[The Filament Strangler]] (Level 6), given by [[Bio-Parser Zo’Dok]].
   - Lead the Skittering Monitors to the Filament Strangler Plants and then collect 3 Filament Encapsulated Monitors and then return to [[Bio-Parser Zo’Dok]].Reward: 60 Copper Coins.
   - After this, you can also talk to [[Craewen Ku’Vi]] in the hut for this quest.
1. [[Pluck Them of Their Pride]] (Level 8), given by [[Craewen Ku’Vi]].
   - Kill the Haoaeran Turf-Hunters just south of the hut in the Trythec Loft area and then return to [[Craewen Ku’Vi]] with 10 Pride Feathers.Reward: 1 Silver Coin and 15 Copper Coins.
   - You can now work on these 3 new quests (Quests 17 to 19).
   - First, kill the Skittering Monitors in this area until you have all 6 Poison Glands for Quest 17.To do Quest 18 is a little harder. You will have to begin attacking a Skittering Monitor and then as you are fighting, try to walk over to one of the Filament Strangler Plants (this is harder than it sounds as when in combat you cannot just normally walk as it stays in the fight with the enemy).If successful, the Plant will grab the Skittering Monitor and quickly transform it into a Filament Encapsulated Monitor. Right click on this new Monitor to gather it into your inventory.Repeat this process until you have all 3 Monitors that you need for Quest 18 (please note that some of the Skittering Monitors wander themselves into the Filament Strangler Plants and turn into the Filiament Encapsulated Monitors. Thus you could just wait until 3 of them did this and pick them up more easily).This now leaves Quest 19 to do. The Haoaeran Turf-Hunters are all Level 8, so you will have to be careful in combat (I was still only at Level 7 here).Kill these Turf-Hunters until you have collected all 10 Pride Feathers. Once you have all 19 items (6 Poison Glands, 3 Monitors and 10 Feathers), you can go back to the hut and first talk to [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]] for your reward for Quest 17.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Scout Scouting]] (Level 7), given by [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]].
   - Venture south from here and try to locate the Missing Scout.If he is dead, retrieve his Log Book and then return to [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]]. Reward: Layered Scale Barbute and 64 Copper Coins.
   - After this, talk to [[Bio-Parser Zo’Dok]] for your reward for Quest 18.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[It Can’t ALL Be Interesting Work]] (Level 7), given by [[Bio-Parser Zo’Dok]].
   - Go to the Jungle just south of here and then return to [[Bio-Parser Zo’Dok]] with 5 Trythec Roots.Reward: Steel Belt Buckle, 1 Silver Coin and 12 Copper Coins.
   - Then talk to [[Craewen Ku’Vi]] for your reward for Quest 19. She will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Ku’Vi's Revenge]] (Level 8), given by [[Craewen Ku’Vi]].
   - Kill the Haoaeran Turf-Hunter leader named Valcryn and then return to [[Craewen Ku’Vi]] with his Head and Pride Feathers. Reward: Layered Scale Breastplate, 2 Silver Coins and 37 Copper Coins.
   - You can first work now on Quest 22.
   - Head due southwest to the end at the mountain here and then go west along the path by the mountain to the end of this path. Kill the Turf-Hunters as you go and at the end of this path you will find Valcryn.Kill him to obtain his Head and Pride Feathers. Once you have killed him, head back to the hut where the quest givers are located.Once here, head due south to find the Dead Sarnak Scout for Quest 20 that is guarded by 2 Satiated Raptors. Kill the Raptors and then right click on the Dead Sarnak Scout to retrieve his Log Book. Now, turn around in place and you should see 2 of the Trythec Roots here very close to the Scout.Right click on these Roots to gather them into your inventory. Head further south and search the area for more of the Roots.When you have all 5 Roots, you can now return to the hut to the north (you can also first head towards the ocean and see the scenery heading east along the cliffs if you want). Once back at the hut, first talk to [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]] for your reward for Quest 20.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Haoaeran Orders]] (Level 8), given by Secondry Arozik Sky’Ki. Go to the Jungle due south from here and then return to [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]] with the Haoaeran Orders.Reward: 1 Silver Coin and 10 Copper Coins.
   - After this, talk to [[Bio-Parser Zo’Dok]] for your reward for Quest 21.
   - Then talk to [[Craewen Ku’Vi]] for your reward for Quest 22.
   - This now leaves you with Quest 23 to work on. Head due south back to the Jungle area where you found the Dead Scout earlier.Toward the southern end here and by the mountains to your right, you should soon see several Dead Spirocs guarded by Satiated Raptors. Kill these Raptors as needed and then right click on any of the Dead Spirocs to obtain the Orders.Once you have the orders, head back north to the hut and talk to [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]] for your reward for Quest
   - He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Decoder Glyph]] (Level 8), given by [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]].
   - Kill the Haoaeran Turf-Hunters to the southwest of here and then return to [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]] with the Decoder Glyph. Reward: Pyrrin Amulet, 1 Silver Coin and 11 Copper Coins.
   - Now head back to the Turf-Hunters to the southwest of the hut and kill one of them to obtain the Decoder Glyph.Once you have the Decoder Glyph, return to [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]] for your reward for Quest 24. He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[War]] (Level 8), given by [[Secondary Arozik Sky’Ki]].
   - Go to Pyrrin Roost to the west of Trythec Loft and ascend the ladders to find and speak with [[Tertiary Dih’Ha]]. Reward: None.
   - If you check your zone map, you will see that Pyrrin Roost is shown as 2 Crossed Arrows.Head to that symbol (keeping your zone map showing). When you get close to the symbol, you should see a path that curves upward.Take this path to the top of the plateau. Off to your right, you should see a rope ladder.Walk into the ladder and you will begin to climb up it to the next small area where the next ladder is located. Walk into this 2nd ladder and head up to the next small area.Once you are at this 2nd small area, you can talk to [[Gerwen Di’Ki]] for this quest.
1. [[Weapon Break]] (Level 9), given by [[Gerwen Di’Ki]]. Go to Pyrrin Roost below and spray the Acid on 5 Haoaeran Spears.Reward: Layered Scale Greaves and 58 Copper Coins.
   - Then talk to [[Bezok Myli’Ki]] for this quest.
1. [[Prison Break]] (Level 9), given by [[Bezok Myli’Ki]].
   - Go to Pyrrin Roost below and free 5 of the Captive Sarnak from their Jailors.Reward: Standard-issue Soldier’s Ring, 1 Silver Coin and 1 Copper Coin.
   - Now, continue to climb up the next ladder to the 3rd area, which is much larger than the 1st two areas. Then take the next ladder to the 4th and final area.Once here, first talk to [[Tertiary Dih’Ha]] for your reward for Quest 25 (be sure you use the talk bubble that Secondary Sky’Ki sent you). She will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Killing the Advance]] (Level 9), given by [[Tertiary Dih’Ha]].
   - Climb toward Thulwun Station East to the west of here and uncover the hidden Haoaeran Advancers and kill 7 of them and then return to [[Tertiary Dih’Ha]]. Reward: 1 Silver Coin and 62 Copper Coins.
   - Then talk to [[Tanzikla Hir’Ki]] for this quest.
1. [[A Reverse Supply Run]] (Level 9), given by [[Tanzikla Hir’Ki]].
   - Climb toward Thulwun Station East to the west of here and destroy 5 of the Supplies that the Haoaeran Advancers are hoarding in front of the gates and then return to [[Tanzikla Hir’Ki]]. Reward: 98 Copper Coins.
   - Once you have Quests 28 and 29, head west for a ways and you will find the Haoaeran Advancers at the mountain’s edge off to your right.
   - Kill these Advancers until you have all 7 kills. While doing this, right click on the barrels they are guarding to destroy them until you have done this to 5 different barrels.(Note that there are only 3 each of the enemies and barrels in this area off to the right. You may need to head due southeast to the other side from here to find more along a 2nd wall).Once you have all 12 items (7 Dead Advancers and 5 Destroyed Barrels), you can head back east and first talk to [[Tertiary Dih’Ha]] for your reward for Quest 28. She will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Reinforcements of a Sort]] (Level 9), given by [[Tertiary Dih’Ha]].
   - Take the Revitalizing Tonic and use it on 3 of the Wounded Sarnak Soldiers and then return to [[Tertiary Dih’Ha]]. Reward: Braided Strangler Filaments, 1 Silver Coin and 65 Copper Coins.
   - Then talk to [[Tazikla Hir’Ki]] for your reward for Quest 29.She will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Stealing their Steel]] (Level 9), given by [[Tazikla Hir’Ki]].
   - Obtain 5 of the Weapons from the fallen Haoaeran Lightfoot enemies and then return them to [[Tazikla Hir’Ki]].Reward: Layered Scale Stompers, 1 Silver Coin and 53 Copper Coins.
   - After this, return west back to the Thulwun Station East area to work on Quests 30 and 31.
   - As you first enter this area, off to your right, you will see many Dead Haoaeran Lightfoot enemies.Right click on these enemies to search them until you have all 5 Weapons needed for Quest 31. In this same area, you can also do Quest 30.You will see many Chrykori Defenders that are lying on the ground. Highlight the Defender and then go to your inventory and right click on the Revitalizing Tonic and then select the *Use* function to revitalize the fallen comrade.Repeat this process until you have done this 3 times. When this is done, you can return east and first talk to [[Tertiary Dih’Ha]] for your reward for Quest #*She will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Sever the Head]] (Level 10), given by [[Tertiary Dih’Ha]].
   - Follow the canyon north out of Thulwun Station East to the cave at the end.Once here, enter the cave and kill Assault Captain Phezzryn and then return to [[Secondary Shai’Dn]] in Thulwin Station. Reward: Choice of Chrykori Standard Cleaver or Seaspray Bow, 3 Silver Coins and 68 Copper Coins.
   - After this, talk to [[Tazikla Hir’Ki]] for your reward for Quest 29.
   - You can now work on Quest 32. Head back to where you healed your comrades earlier and continue in a north direction all the way to the end.At the end here, you will find Assault Captain Phezzryn surrounded by several other allies. Try drawing them one at a time to kill them.Once only the Captain is left, go ahead and kill him as well. (Please note that there is another quest here in this area to free a Captive Griffon.The quest is given by an item seen on the ground. I inadvertently declined the quest and I could not get the item on the ground to reappear again as it appears that it only shows up after killing the Captain the first time.In addition, the quest says that the Captain is in a cave. However, there is no such cave at the end of the path here.)After killing the Captain, head back south to the main area where you earlier did Quests 28 and 29. Once in the main area, head west down the pathway in that direction.You will soon see [[Secondary Shai’Dn]]. Talk to him for your reward for Quest 32.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[A New Threat?]], given by [[Secondary Shai’Dn]].
   - Take the path down to the south and investigate the beach area.Once here, meet up and talk with [[Standard Hir’Vin]]. Reward: None.
   - Before doing this new quest, you should now go back and work on Quests 26 and 27.Head back to where the 4 ladders are located and go all the way back down to the bottom. Once at the bottom, head south to Pyrrin Roost.You will see several Captive Sarnak here. Kill the enemies as you go and then talk to each of the Captive Sarnak to free them.Continue to do this until you have freed a total of 5 Sarnak for Quest 27. As you do this, you should also see Spears that are resting upon 2 pieces of wood that look like an X.Right click on the Spears to apply the Acid for Quest 26 until you again have done this a total of 5 times. Once this is completed, head back north and up the ladders 2 times to the quest givers.First talk to [[Gerwen Di’Ki]] for your reward for Quest 26. Then talk to [[Bezok Myli’Ki]] for your reward for Quest 27.This now only leaves you Quest 33 to work on. Climb back up the remaining 2 ladders and head back to [[Secondary Shai’Dn]].From his position, head due south until you reach the cliff. Once at the cliff, turn left and head east down the path to the beach area.At the very bottom, you will see [[Standard Hir’Vin]]. Talk to him for your reward for Quest 33.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Wrecking Crew for the Wrecked Crew]], given by [[Standard Hir’Vin]].
   - Kill 8 Blackshield Pirates on the southern beach here and then return to [[Standard Hir’Vin]].Reward: 3 Silver Coins and 65 Copper Coins.
   - Now head south to the beach. Kill the various Blackshield Pirates until you have all 8 kills (note that in doing this you may want to consider heading east along the beach to finish mapping out the area here) and then return to [[Standard Hir’Vin]] for your reward for Quest 34.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[Blackshield Orders]], given by [[Standard Hir’Vin]].
   - Locate the Blackshield Orders among the Blackshield Pirates on the southern beach.Also, kill Captain Gorth of the Blackshield Pirates and then return to [[Standard Hir’Vin]]. Reward: 3 Silver Coins and 64 Copper Coins.
   - Head to the beach and then head west to the 2 tents.Captain Gorth is here along with 2 bodyguards. Kill them first by drawing one at a time and then kill Captain Gorth.Once he is dead, head to the Crates here where you will find the Blackshield Orders (right click on them to collect into your inventory). After this, head back and talk to [[Standard Hir’Vin]] for your reward for Quest 35.He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[A Job for a Secondary]], given by [[Standard Hir’Vin]].
   - Bring the Blackshield Orders to [[Secondary Shai’Dn]], who is located back up at Thulwun Station East.Reward: None.
   - Now head back up the path here and talk to [[Secondary Shai’Dn]] for your reward for Quest 36. He will then have this quest for you.
1. [[The Problem Concerning Pirates]], given by [[Secondary Shai’Dn]].
   - Speak with the Griffon Tamer to the west of here and then fly over to the Gorowyn Beach Outpost and find and talk to Primary Sha’Ki, who is dealing with the Pirates. Reward: None.
   - Note that if you take the Griffon ride, it will fly you all the way to another island here.Since the northern part of this first island is still not mapped out, it is highly suggested that you map this part out first before doing Quest 37. Check your zone map and head all the way back east on the island to where you first started.You can then take a swim all the way around the northern and western coasts on the island to reveal most of the remaining map here (there will still be a section in the northwest that will not be mapped as yet). Then swim back to the eastern coast to get back to land (if nothing else, your Swimming skill will go up quite a bit for doing this).After looking around for a little while, I could not find a way to map anything else on this island. Thus, you can now head back to Thulwun Station East and talk to the Griffon Tamer to the west of [[Secondary Shai’Dn]] (climb a ladder in the tower to reach him).He will ask you where you want to go.
