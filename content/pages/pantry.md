If you are not familiar with pantries, you may want to read about the general topic of [[Sales Display|sales displays]] that can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]].

- All pantries are Master Crafted (made from a rare material) by those who take up the [[Carpenter]] tradeskill.
- This sales display holds a limited scope of items that are typically made by a [[Provisioner]], like [[Food]] and [[Drink]].
