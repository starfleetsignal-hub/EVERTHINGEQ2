## Effects

- Applies ***Fury of the Storm***. Lasts for 5.0 seconds.
  - Throws target back
  - Inflicts massive magic damage on caster.
  - Stuns caster
  - Epic target gain an immunity to Stun effects of 15.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 1.7 seconds.
