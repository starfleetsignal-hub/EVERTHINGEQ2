desc: The Alliz Evol Ew are a savage tribe of lizardmen that inhabit the jungles of [[The Feerrott]]. They worship the influence of [[Cazic-Thule|Fear]], which directs them to defend the island and its monuments from any invader. They are cousins to the cannibalistic Alliz Tae Ew. The Evol Ew have a smaller population than the Tae Ew, and have no presence within [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]]. For this reason, they may appear to be weaker than their cousins, yet [[Alliz Evol Ew (House Item)|according to their folklore]] their role in the world is much different. For more information on the Evol Ew and the Tae Ew read the tomes, [[Alliz Evol Ew (House Item)|Alliz Evol Ew]], [[Alliz Onu (House Item)|Alliz Onu]], and [[Alliz Tae Ew (House Item)|Alliz Tae Ew]]. However, these tomes are written rather cryptically and have never been properly deciphered.
