The Far Wind was a ship similar to the [[Far Journey]], one of the boats that regularly served as refugee transport to the [[Isle of Refuge]]. It was captured by orcs near [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] and all crew members were taken as slaves or slaughtered.
