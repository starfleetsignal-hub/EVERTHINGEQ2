## Spell Effects

- Inflicts 4400 - 5378 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3 seconds
- Stuns targets in Area of Effect
- 5% chance to dispel when target takes damage

## Note

Can be cast by Named in Plane of Magic.
