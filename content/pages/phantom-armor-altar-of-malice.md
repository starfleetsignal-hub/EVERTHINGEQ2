## Overview

Similar to [[Cryptic Armor (Tears of Veeshan)|Cryptic Armor]] from the [[Tears of Veeshan]] expansion, Phantom Armor is the initial (Solo) tier of armors in the [[Altar of Malice]] (AoM) expansion's armor progression system.

As with ToV armors, AoM armors can be augmented with colored gems to increase the armor's raw stats. For the AoM expansion these gems are referred to as Malice Gemstones and come in 5 colors, Yellow (Solo), Blue (6-man), Green (advanced 6-player), Red (T1 raid), and Purple (T2 raid)

All Phantom armors are initially obtained by the player with 0 gems inserted.  Phantom Armors require a [[Yellow Malice Gemstone]] to be upgraded, and each piece can be upgraded with up to 4 gems each.

Players wishing to see what the stats of a specific piece of Phantom armor will be after inserting a gem can search the wiki for the name of the armor piece, appended with (0 Gem), (1 Gem), (2 Gem), (3 Gem), or (4 Gem), depending on the number of gems they want to see inserted.<br>*Example: if a player has a fresh pair [[Swift Phantom Chain Leggings (0 Gem)|Swift Phantom Chain Leggings]], and wants to see what the stats will be after inserting two yellow malice gems, then they simply search the wiki for "[[Swift Phantom Chain Leggings (2 Gem)]]"

## How to obtain

Players obtain Phantom armors via Altar of Malice [Solo] dungeons, [Advanced Solo] dungeons, and frequently from the 'Exceptional Chests' of named Overland MoBs in AoM zones

## Alternate Source

As players run and re-run the solo AoM dungeons and kill additional Overland MoBs, they will begin to accumulate copies of armor they already have, or loot pieces with sub-par stats that they'd rather not wear.

Just as with ToV armors before them, players may trade unwanted and duplicate AoM armors for other pieces of AoM armor they may be seeking. [[Shioni]] {{waypoint 1195.23, 25.13, 1140.01}} (in the Phantom Sea docks tower) will happily exchange any armors you do not need for ones that you do.  There is a cost, of course... each armor piece requires x10 Malice Shards of a matching color to purchase. Yellow Gemstone armors require Yellow Malice Shards to purchase, Blue Gem armors require Blue Malice Shards, etc.  Phantom armors are exclusively yellow gem/shard armors.

In order to obtain [[Yellow Malice Shard|Yellow Malice Shards]] (or [[Blue Malice Shard|Blue]] or [[Green Malice Shard|Green]]), players need to break down an unwanted piece of Phantom armor into a yellow shard. Shioni sells consumable [[Malice Gem Shard Cannibalizer|Malice Gem Shard Cannibalizers]] for 25g each. Each cannibalizer breaks down one piece of AoM armor and returns an appropriately-colored shard.  **Fair warning** (and this warning is also on the Cannibalizer itself in-game) you will NOT get back any Malice Gemstones you've spent on a piece of armor if you choose to have it consumed for trade, so be aware of that if you decide to break down pieces you've already put gemstones into.

In addition to breaking down unwanted pieces of AoM armor, players can also earn Malice Shards themselves both at the end of AoM dungeons and by completing certain repeatable and non-repeatable quests within AoM dungeons.  This trade system is considerably more flexible than the previous ToV system, as it does NOT require traded pieces to match the same slot, as all armor slots for trade in a given tier use the same color Malice Shards, regardless of slot.  All that matters is the gem color of the armor and that it matches the shards.

*NOTE: While Shioni does also handle trading for both Blue and Green Malice Gemstone armors in addition to Yellow, players cannot 'trade-up' yellow malice shard armors for blue or green malice shard armors via this system.  Each color armor can only be traded for other armors of the same color.*

## List

While the armors of the solo tier of Altar of Malice armors are collectively referred to as "Phantom Armors", only HALF of the armors actually bear the name 'Phantom' in them, and that is the melee half of the armors.  The caster half of the armors bear a different name depending on the armor type.
