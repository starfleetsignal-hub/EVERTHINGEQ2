## Effects

- Applies ***Rupture*** on termination.
  - Inflicts heat damage on target.
  - Decreases power of target.
- Mesmerizes target.
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Mesmerize effects of 1 minute 12 seconds and duration is reduced to 8 seconds.
- Dispelled when target takes damage.
