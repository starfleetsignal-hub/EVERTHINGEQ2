## Notes

- This is the faction required to purchase the tradeskill gear and harvesting mounts in [[The Village of Shin]] and [[Tranquil Sea]], mounts such as the [[Pack Unicorn]] and [[Far Seas Pack Pegasus]].
- This faction is of specific interest to those who [[Craft]].
