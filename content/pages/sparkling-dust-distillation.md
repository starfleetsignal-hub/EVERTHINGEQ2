![Sparkling Dust Distillation](images/{{PAGENAME}}.jpg)
This recipe allows an [[Alchemist]] to refine several lower level dusts into a higher one.

- **Class:** [[Alchemist]]
- **Recipe Level:** 20
- **Workstation:** [[Chemistry Table]]

## Ingredients

- 3 [[Shimmering Dust|Shimmering Dusts]]
- 3 [[Tallow candle|tallow candles]]

## Yields

- 1 [[Shimmering Dust]] (IF Pristine)
- 1 [[Sparkling Dust]]
