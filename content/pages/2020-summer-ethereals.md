The event began on live servers on June 24, 2020.

Peculiar Coins are the currency used to purchase ethereal rewards. These coins are awarded on a weekly basis and reset every Thursday at 12:01 AM pacific time. The following chart shows how the weekly coins are awarded based on weekly quests completed.

| Quests Completed |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heroic Weekly in All Expert Zones | No | No | No | No | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Heroic Weekly in All Challenge Zones | No | No | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes or No | Yes or No |
| Heroic Weekly with at least one zone completed at normal heroic difficulty | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes or No | Yes or No | Yes or No | Yes or No |
| Big Baddies | No | Yes | No | Yes | No | Yes | No | Yes |
| One or more Solo weekly quests | Yes | Yes or No | Yes or No | Yes or No | Yes or No | Yes or No | Yes or No | Yes or No |
| Total Coins | 24 | 48 | 48 | 72 | 52 | 76 | 60 | 84 |

The number of shards needed to increase the level of each jewelry piece is shown in the table below. Each shard costs 3 Peculiar coins. Cumulative coins includes the 100 coins to purchase the base item.

| Item Level | Shards | Cumulative Coins |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | 130 |
| 2 | 20 | 190 |
| 3 | 40 | 310 |
| 4 | 80 | 550 |
| 5 | 120 | 910 |
