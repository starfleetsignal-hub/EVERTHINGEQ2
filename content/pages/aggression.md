**Aggression** is a [[Skills|skill]] used by all Taunts and is important for [[Fighter|Fighters]].

This is how well your taunts will affect monsters. This skill affects the chance that a taunt will be resisted.

- Aggression (Equipment) - Equipment that modifies the **Aggression** skill.
- Taunt (Spell Type) - Spells that depend upon mastery of the **Aggression** skill.
