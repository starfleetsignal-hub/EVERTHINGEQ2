**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** Purchased from the [[Marketplace]]
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** large, [[Maj'Dul]]-themed
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** none allowed

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Maj'dul Vacation Suite is a replica of  in Maj'Dul.

- You can easily tour any [[Marketplace]] house by doing the following:
1. Click on the EQ2 button in the bottom left corner of your screen
1. Chose [[Housing & Leaderboards]] from the list
1. Click on the Buy Houses tab
1. Locate the house you want to look at on the list and you can click the Tour button to see an empty version

This house twists and turns from its main entrance to a final (non-working) door that looks similar to the entrance.

## Cost and Item Limits

*Prices for all Marketplace items is subject to change at any time. Item count limits have changed in the past and can change at any time again.*

- Cost: 1050 DC unless you have [[Free-To-Play|All Access]] membership, which grants a 10% discount as of 2016.
- Base Item Limit: 900 decor items and 900 [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|Building Blocks]]
