**[[Sister Katey Dalwynn]]** currently appears only in [[New Halas]] ([[Frostfang Sea]]).  She offers a quest during the [[Erollisi Day]] celebrations, but can be found there year-round.  Historically, she appeared in other locations.

- [[Sister Katey Dalwynn (Antonica)]], in [[Antonica]] as part of the [[Erollisi Day]] celebrations every year prior to the introduction of [[Frostfang Sea]] in LU56.
- [[Sister Katey Dalwynn (Everfrost)]], in [[Everfrost]] as part of a live event ([[LU53]]) focused on the Shard of Love.  Removed with LU54.
