Characters can have difficulty finding appropriate level creatures (i.e. MOBs **Mobile Objects**) to capture in their [[Familiar Cage|Familiar Cages]]. You must be engaged in combat to capture a MOB. Too large a difference in Adventure level can create problems for Characters:

1. Too low an Adventure level for the MOB and the Character is killed so quickly there is no opportunity to capture the creature.
1. Too high an Adventure level for the MOB and the MOB is killed so quickly that there is no opportunity to capture the creature.

These tables are sorted into ascending Adventure order to assist people finding appropriate creatures. There will be some overlap for the Beastlord class. Due to the number of enteries the tables had to be split off to independent pages. Use the Table of Contents below to access an appropriate table.

Even though many creatures may have the 'bag' flag (i.e. symbol) visible it does not guarantee they can be captured in [[Familiar Cage|Familiar Cages]]. Unfortunately over the years inconsistancies have crept into EverQuest II. Only some named and/or Heroic MOBs can be captured while others can not. Similar inconsistencies can be found within classes of creatures.

These tables are a guide to some of the available creatures. Creatures that can not be captured are listed but their names will be crossed out (i.e. [Strikethrough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strikethrough)). Due to the size of the file of the combined tables they have been split into individual pages.
