A **phylactery** typically refers to a charm or amulet designed to protect someone from danger. A **phylacter** refers to a person acting as a protector. In game there are several types of phylacteries.

Several monsters named with "phylactery" exist, including:

- [[Praetor of the Phylactery]], a named monster in [[Charasis: Vault of Eternal Sleep]]
- [[A monolithic phylactery]], an elemental monster in [[The Bonemire]]

Several instanced zones named with "phylactery" exist, including:

- [[Miragul's Phylactery: Scion of Ice]]
- [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Anathema]]
- [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Crucible]]

Also, several quests exist that involve "phylactery," including:

- [[Phylacter of Truth]], which takes place in [[Maj'Dul]]
- [[Phylactery Find]], which takes place in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
- [[Claim the Phylactery]], which takes place in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
- [[Hold on to the Phylactery]], which takes place in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
- [[The Search for Vel'Arek]], part of the [[Guardian Epic Weapon Timeline]]
- [[Draco Mortuus vos Liberatio]], the [[Heritage Quests|heritage quest]]
- [[From Within the Phylactery]], which takes place in the [[Moors of Ykesha]]
- [[Leave No Di'Zok Behind]], which involves harvesting phylacteries in [[Kylong Plains]]

Finally, several "phylactery" items exist in game as either quest rewards or items used to further a quest's progression:

- [[Rile's Phylactery]], an item used during the quest [[Claim the Phylactery]] and the item that starts the quest [[Hold on to the Phylactery]]
- [[Phylactery of the Darslayer]], an item found in [[Charasis: Vault of Eternal Sleep]] and used to spawn [[Dominus Ganak (Kunzar Jungle)|Dominus Ganak]] in  [[Ganak's Old Crypt]]
- [[An ornate phylactery]], which is crafted during the [[Heritage Quests|heritage quest]] noted above, [[Draco Mortuus vos Liberatio]]
- [[Unglued Alchemist's Amulet]], the reward from the quest [[Safely-Kept Soul]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]]
- A book titled [[On the Origins of the Phylactery]]
