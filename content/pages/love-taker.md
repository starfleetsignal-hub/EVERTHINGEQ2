Love Taker is a prefix title from completing the [[Passions of Kunark]] [[Achievements|achievement]] available during [[Erollisi Day]].

To set this title after you earn it, press **C** to open the character sheet. Click the Options tab, Located the drop-down arrow next to the titles and choose it from the list.
