**Grassgalor** the fluff pet is cast from [[Chokéball: Grassgalor]], the reward from the quest [[Grassgalor (Quest)|Grassgalor]], which has you capture the named mob [[Grassgalor (Named)|Grassgalor]].  This scaly pup does absolutely nothing - the epitome of a fluff pet.
