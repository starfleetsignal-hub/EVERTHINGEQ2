![SOE Live](images/SOELive-sm.jpg)
Formerly known as SOE Fan Faire, SOE Live is a gaming convention sponsored by [[Sony Online Entertainment]] (SOE) that offers players of SOE games the chance to meet other gamers and prominent SOE personalities. Attendees  participate in a number of panel discussions and game-themed live events.

The official site for information and countdown is http://www.soe.com/soelive/
