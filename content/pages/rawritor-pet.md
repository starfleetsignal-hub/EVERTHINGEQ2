**Rawritor** the fluff pet is cast from [[Chokéball: Rawritor]], the reward from the quest [[Rawritor (Quest)|Rawritor]], which has you capture the named mob [[Rawritor]]. This tentacled kitty does nothing but follow you - a perfect example of a fluff pet.
