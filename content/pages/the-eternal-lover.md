The Eternal Lover is a suffix title for completing the [[Love Is Eternal]] [[Achievements|achievement]] during [[Erollisi Day]].

To set the title after you ear it, press **C** to open your Character sheet. Click on the Options tab and use the drop-down arrows to choose the title.
