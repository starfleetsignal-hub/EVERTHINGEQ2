The language of the pygmy race of the [[Tranquil Sea]] and is required to complete [[A Tale of Two Trails]] and allow you to speak with Nuwat Pickletrade at the Merinep outpost.  Can be learned by doing the quest [[Handle With Care (Tranquil Sea)|Handle With Care]], which is in a series started by the [[Shattered Seas: Refuge Return]].
