Eva Corunno'thes was the High Priestess of Growth and appears twice in the storyline:

- [[Eva Corunno'thes (Antonica)]] - as part of the [[Return of the Gods Timeline]], she sends you to [[The Battle of Cauldron Hollow (Good)|The Battle of Cauldron Hollow]]
- [[Eva Corunno'thes (Greater Faydark)]] - as part of the [[Tunare Timeline]], from Echoes of Faydwer, she gives you quests to earn [[Tunare]]'s trust.

As it turned out, Eva was actually [[Firiona Vie]] in disguise. She has since revealed her true identity and abandoned the Eva Corunno'thes guise.
