Research Assistants have moved into many cities in Norrath. These hardworking men and women will do the research to upgrade a spell or combat art from journeyman to adept or from expert to master. They will assist you by researching one spell at a time per character -- ***all characters on your account can now be researching different spells concurrently*** -- but will only help those adventurers level 20 or higher. Research Assistants can be found in each of the six neighborhoods and villages of [[Freeport]] and [[Qeynos]], as well as in [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]], [[Haven]], [[Gorowyn]], [[New Halas]], and [[Kelethin]]. You can ask any guard in those areas to point you in the direction of a Research Assistant if you can’t find them.
