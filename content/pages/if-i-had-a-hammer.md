This quest introduces players to tradeskilling and the crafting areas in the cities. It rewards the player with the book for that city's tradeskill society. A different version of the quest exists for each city, but they are not restricted, so all six can be completed by all players, and all six will appear in the player's journal upon completion.
Note: in many locations, you may need to zoom the viewpoint in to see the clickable recipe (a paper) and coal (small sack) to update the quest. You must also click items in the order they appear in the Journal to get the updates. If you've done steps out of order, there is no need to delete the quest. Instead repeat the steps in order again, based on the quest Journal. Press J to open your journal.
