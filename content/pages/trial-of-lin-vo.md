## Notes

The **Trial of Lin Vo** can refer to the solo or heroic version of the Trial of Lin Vo quest or the related zone instance.

- You can choose between solo and heroic instances for this trial.
- Mercenaries are disabled in the solo instance but permitted in the heroic instance.
- There is no lockout timer for failing either instance, but the success lockout timer is 3 days.
- The solo and heroic instances are on separate lockout timers.
- The reward for the solo instance is 15 [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]].
- The reward for the heroic instance is 30 [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]]
- [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]] can be turned into [[Brother Huang Mantian]] for titles.
- A Full List the [[Fallen Dynasty]] trials can be found on the [[Tower of the Four Winds#Trials|Tower of the Four Winds Trials]] article.
