combat mit: 242k

spawn requirement: need 1 of 3 diffrent items, can have different people use each item to summon him doesn't have to be all items same person. He spawns adds grimling hero support in 5 different locations 1 at a time and speeds up closer he is to death. locations are 2 spot snear entrance, 1 behind the tree near fire pyre, 2 up the hill towards (2nd solo/ heroic named loc).
