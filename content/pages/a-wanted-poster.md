There are also Wanted posters appear in many zones and trigger small single quests to kill a specific mob or a number of a type of mob.

- [[A wanted poster (Ant)]] -- in [[Antonica]]
- [[A wanted poster (SFM)]] -- in [[Steamfont Mountains]]
- [[Wanted: Ithari K'Xyrae]] -- in [[Darklight Wood]]

There is a [[Slayer status poster]] in each player city, referred to sometimes as a wanted poster, that tells a player how many of each category of monster he has slain, including orcs, goblins, undead, vampires, et cetera.
