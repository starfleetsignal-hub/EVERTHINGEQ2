Suffix title earned for completing 20 [[Heritage Quest Timeline|Heritage Quests]].

- You must talk to the following NPC to earn this title:
  - Qeynos - Sage [[Indis Surion]] (green portal) in the [[Concordium Tower|mage tower]] in [[South Qeynos]]
  - Freeport - Sage [[Navarius Orvalis]] in [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] in [[North Freeport]]
  - Kelethin - Sage [[Piritta Silvarri]].
