## Overview

Advanced tradeskill recipes may be obtained from various mobs around Norrath. They can also be purchased from NPC vendors, saving players loads of money for a little bit of quest/faction work.  Levels 80+ require more extensive work but with average player sold recipes in the thousands of platinum versus the few gold pieces it costs from the NPC vendors, the work is worth it <i>especially for new and returning players</i> that do not have that kind of money.  These recipes are typically no-trade but are occasionally heirloom and cannot be sold on the broker.
