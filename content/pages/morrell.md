Morrell was rumored to be one of three moons that orbit [[Norrath]].

**Theory:** According to the model in The Tower of the Moon in Maj'Dul, a 3rd moon is located around the southern pole of Norrath and some how does not orbit. Rumor had it that this moon was named Morrell, after "Morrel-Thule," the God of Dreams.  However, when the question was raised in the [Lore Panel](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LHFr_I0gnJk) at Fan Faire 2008, the developers stated they had not discussed a third moon.  The geostationary orb under Norrath on the model is believed to be artist liberty. This was further confirmed by the lack of a third moon visible on [[The Vigilant Orrery]].
