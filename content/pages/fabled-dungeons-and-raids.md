## Overview

Fabled dungeons and raids were first introduced with [[Update:67|Update 67]], with higher level versions of original zones from the Desert of Flames expansion. Over the years, this was expanded to include dungeons from other expansions, such as the Kingdom of Sky and Echoes of Faydwer. With Update 100, they also released Fabled versions of classic dungeons from the original release of the game, namely [[The Fabled Ruins of Varsoon]] and [[The Fabled Temple of Cazic-Thule]].

Fabled dungeons have higher level monsters and bosses, and drop loot that is comparable with the expansion they were introduced with. For example, [[The Fabled Den of the Devourer]] has loot that is similar to gear dropped in other Tears of Veeshan dungeons because that was the current expansion when it was introduced.

Fabled dungeons are identical to their original counterparts in terms of layout, and most of the named bosses within the zone are the same. However, these new higher level bosses can have some new tricks up their sleeve. This is particular true for raids, where you will find boss fights to be more challenging than their original versions. Bosses which used to be be straight-forward fights can now cast curses and have new attacks which need to be countered by the raid.

New quests and collections are also available in Fabled dungeons, and quest rewards correspond with the expansion the Fabled dungeon was introduced. For example, some level 95 Fabled dungeons have missions which award [[Draconic Etyma]]. Level 100 dungeons can have missions which can award [[Far Seas Ferrin]].

Fabled dungeons have minimum level requirements and may be restricted to players with [[All Access Membership|All Access]] membership. They may also require the player to have purchased the expansion associated with the dungeon. Members who have the current expansion will have access to all Fabled dungeons because each expansion grants access to content from all prior expansions.

## Entrances

Fabled dungeons can be entered at the same location as the original dungeon is it based on. However, there are also additional portals which were added at convenient locations, based on the level of the dungeon.

### Bonemire

There is a Teleportation Orb located at the [[Drednever Crash Site]] in [[The Bonemire]] at {{waypoint 34.26, 43.62, 520.02}}. These fabled dungeons are for characters who are level 95+.

- [[The Fabled Deathtoll]]
- [[The Fabled Den of the Devourer]]
- [[The Fabled Halls of Fate]]
- [[The Fabled Laboratory of Lord Vyemm]]
- [[The Fabled Mutagenic Outcast]]
- [[The Fabled Sanctum of the Scaleborn]]
- [[The Fabled Three Princes]]
- [[The Fabled Vaults of El'Arad]]
