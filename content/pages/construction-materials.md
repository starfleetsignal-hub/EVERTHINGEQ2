This is a series of quests added in [[LU48]] in preparation for the appearance of [[Guild Halls]].

## Getting the Quest

Hail the Dockside Organizer [[Director Jasmine]] on the docks of [[Antonica]] or the Dockside Organizer [[Director Hezreal]] on the docks of [[The Commonlands|the Commonlands]].

You are asked to choose which node type you wish to harvest from:

- [[Ore Nodes|Ore]] -- [[Hard Metal|Hard Metals]] and [[Loam|Loams]]
- [[Gemstone Nodes|Gemstone]] -- [[Soft Metal|Soft Metals]] and [[Gem|Gems]]
- [[Wood Nodes|Lumber]]
- [[Root Nodes|Roots]]

You are then asked to choose which tier you wish to harvest. Harvesting in higher level zones will increase the amount of personal status which is rewarded for completing the quest.

- [[Harvestables (Tier 1)|Tier 1]] (1+) *Oakmyst Forest, the Sprawl, Greater Faydark, etc.*
- [[Harvestables (Tier 2)|Tier 2]] (20+) *Antonica, Commonlands, Greater Faydark, etc.*
- [[Harvestables (Tier 3)|Tier 3]] (90+) *Thundering Steppes, Nektulos Forest, Butcherblock Mountains, etc.*
- [[Harvestables (Tier 4)|Tier 4]] (140+) *Enchanted Lands, Zek, Steamfont Mountains, etc.*
- [[Harvestables (Tier 5)|Tier 5]] (190+) *Everfrost, Lavastorm, Feerrott, etc.*
- [[Harvestables (Tier 6)|Tier 6]] (240+) *Sinking Sands, Lesser Faydark, etc.*
- [[Harvestables (Tier 7)|Tier 7]] (240+) *Tenebrous Tangle, Loping Plains, etc.*
- [[Harvestables (Tier 8)|Tier 8]] (340) *Kylong Plains, Jarsath Wastes, etc.*

See main [[Harvesting]] article for recommended harvesting zones.

## Lore

I have volunteered to help the guild hall construction project by collecting materials that are needed to help progress the work.
I need to collect materials for the guild hall construction project.
I need to collect redwood lumber for the guild hall project.

## Rewards

Completing this quest rewards personal status and guild status. Completing it multiple times rewards a certificate, statue, and character title.

### Personal Status

Completing this quest awards personal status appropriate to the level of harvested material. 10% status is also rewarded for your guild.  Status listed is approximate; actual reward varies based on character level to quest level.

- Tier 1 = 222 status
- Tier 2 = 438 status
- Tier 3 = 842 status
- Tier 4 = 1594 status
- Tier 5 = 3870 status
- Tier 6 = 7395 status
- Tier 7 = 11205 status
- Tier 8 = 15311 status

### Other Rewards

Completing this quest 10, 20, and 30 times grants different rewards:

- 10 completions = House item: [[Certificate of Service to the State]] for Evil citizens, [[Qeynosian Commendation for Material Assistance]] for Good citizens.
- 20 completions = House item: [[Miniature Replica Statue: Domination]] for Evil citizens, [[Miniature Replica Statue: Resolution]] for Good citizens.
- 30 completions = suffix title, [[The Guild Hall Constructor]].
