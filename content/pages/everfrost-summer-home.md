![The tour preview of this home.](images/Everfrost-summer-home.jpg)
**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** Only with the purchase of Collectors Edition or Premium Edition of Destiny of Velious expansion
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** icy, large
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** none
- Maximum number of placed items prior to expanders: 900 ( item count confirmed 07/27/2024 to be 900, despite tooltip on deed saying 400)

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Everfrost Summer Home is icy!

- Fun fact: this was the first [[Prestige Housing|prestige house]] offered with an expansion upgrade; all rent-free housing prior to this was looted from Legends of Norrath loot cards.
