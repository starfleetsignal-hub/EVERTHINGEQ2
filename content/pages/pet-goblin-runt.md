![A pet goblin runt](images/Tribal_Goblin.jpg)
The result from buying the [[Friendly Tribal Goblin Runt]] ability from either [[Mage Fulcrum Gearshift]] or [[Scientist Dally Scrumplecog]], this fluff pet is reminiscent of Kunark goblins.  It has neither speech nor special abilities - except, like The Fonz, the ability to look cool all day.

This pet does **not** override your Deity pet, but may override other fluff pets.
