**Glyph of Restoration armor set**

- [[Hearthwarmed Belt]]
- [[Hearthwarmed Bracelet]]
- [[Hearthwarmed Ring]]
- [[Shimmering Swiftlace Shadow Waist]]

---

- (2) +10 In-Combat Power Regeneration Per Tick
  - +20 In-Combat Health Regeneration Per Tick
    - Reduces cast time of all spells by 3 percent.
- (3) Applies Self Preservation.
  - On a successful combat attack this spell has a chance to cast Self Preservation on caster. This effect will trigger an average of 1.8 times per minute. If a melee attack is used, only the primary weapon can trigger this effect.
    - Heals caster for 100
    - Increases power of caster by 100
  - When target successfully casts a hostile spell this spell has a chance to cast Self Preservation on caster. This effect will trigger an average of 1.8 times per minute.
    - Heals caster for 100
    - Increases power of caster by 100

## Notes

There are actually several Glyph of Restoration sets with the same name and set effects. Items from a different set will not contribute towards the set bonus in another set. In other words, you will not get a two set bonus for just wearing both a Hearthwarmed Belt and a Inner Power Coil, since they belong in different sets.
