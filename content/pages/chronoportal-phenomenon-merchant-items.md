To purchase items, visit [[Erben Delki]] inside the Chronoweave while the event is live.
For Chronoweave locations (which can change annually) see the main page for [[Chronoportal Phenomenon]].

- Players can purchase items with [[Ancient Platinum Coin|Ancient Platinum Coins]] during the event, which typically occurs during the end of March. See the in-game calendar for exact dates.
- To see items before you buy them while in-game: hold down CTRL, place your mouse over the item icon, and L click.
- Unlike most other live events, this merchant does **not** have event-themed recipes to sell.
