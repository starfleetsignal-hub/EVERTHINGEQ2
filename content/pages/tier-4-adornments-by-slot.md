The table below details the **Tier 4 Adornments** organized by the slots to which they apply:

**Lesser** adornments appear with no highlighting. They require 1 [[Ethereal Powder]] and 6 [[Opal Fragment|Opal Fragments]].  Up to 3 [[Opal Fragment|Opal Fragments]] are returned per craft.

**Greater** adornments appear with brown highlighting. They require 1 [[Infusion of Water]], 2 [[Ethereal Powder|Ethereal Powders]], and 6 [[Opal Fragment|Opal Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Ethereal Powder]] is returned per craft.

**Superior** adornments appear with red highlighting. They require 1 [[Purified Mana]], 2 [[Infusion of the Void|Infusions of Water]], 3 [[Ethereal Powder|Ethereal Powders]] and 6 [[Opal Fragment|Opal Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Infusion of Water]] is returned per craft.

For a simple, alphabetic listing of tier 4 adornments, see Tier 4 Adornments.
