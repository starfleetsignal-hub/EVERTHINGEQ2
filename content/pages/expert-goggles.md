For Expert collections in [[Echoes of Faydwer]] zones, use [[Gnomish Collection Goggles]] from collection quest [[Faydwer Collections]] or [[Kunark Expert Adventurer Goggles]] from collection quest [[Kunark Collections]]

**the [[Earring of the Solstice]] replaces those goggles*

For Expert collections in [[Rise of Kunark]] zones, use [[Kunark Expert Adventurer Goggles]] from collection quest [[Kunark Collections]]

For Expert collections in [[Runnyeye: The Gathering]], use [[A Gigglegibber Courting Hat]] from collection quest [[Relics of the Goblin Hero]]. **Also Gnomish Collection Googles work.**

For Expert collections in [[The Shadow Odyssey]] zones, use [[Signet of the Ethernauts]] or [[Talisman of the Ethernauts]] from collection quest [[Relics of the Ethernauts]]
