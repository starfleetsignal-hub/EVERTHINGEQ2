*See also [[Hall of the Champion]] for the adventure instance zone.*
![](images/Hall-of-the-champion.jpg)
**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** This was a reward from the (now retired) Recruit Rewards Program
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** medium, arena
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** none

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Hall of the Champion (Prestige House) is a arena with a central platform and seating above, though there is no (normal) way to get to the seating area. Two prominent scupltures in the shapes of wings can be seen from the entrance.

## Notes

- You **can not** buy this house from the [[Marketplace]], because it was from the Recruit Rewards program.
  - The program to recruit new players to play game was [retired in July 2015](http://eq2.zam.com/story.html?story=36184). If you did not earn a deed for this house prior to that date, you can no longer get this house (as of Jan. 2016).
- Even though you can not buy it without a deed, this house can be toured by visiting general portal locations in Freeport and Qeynos (locs given above)
