**Spiritweaver's Purified armor set**

- [[Spiritweaver's Purified Coif]]
- [[Spiritweaver's Purified Spaulders]]
- [[Spiritweaver's Purified Hauberk]]
- [[Spiritweaver's Purified Wristguard]]
- [[Spiritweaver's Purified Cuffs]]
- [[Spiritweaver's Purified Chausses]]
- [[Spiritweaver's Purified Boots]]

---

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

This armor may be worn only by: [[Mystic|Mystics]].

- With the exception of the Hauberk, all of this armor may be purchased from [[Rotuk]] at the Thurgadin Docks in [[Great Divide]].
- With the exception of the Hauberk, all of this armor may be crafted from recipes found in [[Far Seas Strategic Pricing (3rd Edition), Volume I]]
- The Hauberk is obtained from the [[Thickened Crystalline Hauberk]] mold, which is dropped from [[Grand Restorer Lingwal]] in [[Hold of Rime: The Fortress Spire]]
