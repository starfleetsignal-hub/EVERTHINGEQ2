## Effects

- Applies ***Tranquility's Balm*** instantly and every 2 seconds.
  - Increases power of group members by 5%.
- Stuns caster
- Stifles caster

## Quote

"Tranquility lives within each of us, to touch that essence may overwhelm you, but it will also rejuvenate you." - [[Quellious]]
