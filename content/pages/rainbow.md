The term **rainbow** appears in several places in game. It may refer to:

- the in-game visual effect when a player is near a [[Scrying]] point
- the rainbow hued armor armor pieces rewarded from the quest [[Destroy the Corruption]], which takes place in [[Oakmyst Forest]]
- the [[Rainbow Tinkerfest Firework|tinkered firework]] which creates a rainbow effect
