![On the left, the spiral stairs that lead to the cellar. On the right, the front door.](images/Freeblood-lair.jpg)
**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** purchased from the [[Marketplace]] only if your character is a [[Freeblood]]
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** medium, spooky vampire house
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** none allowed

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

In order to buy this house you **must** have the [[Freeblood]] race unlock from the [[Marketplace]] and the character buying the house must also be a Freeblood.

Freeblood Lair is similar to the [[Mistmoore Crags Estate]], without the sprawl. It features 4 rooms on the main level, right off the entry. A spiral staircase leads to a cellar. Conventional stairs lead up to a balcony that overlooks the [[Loping Plains]].

## Cost and Item Count

*Prices for all Marketplace items is subject to change at any time. Item limits have been increased in the past and can change at any time.*

- Cost: 900 Daybreak Cash unless you have an [[Free-To-Play|All Access]] membership, which grants a 10% discount as of 2016.
- Base Item Limit: 900 and 900 [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]]

## Notes

With a [[Bag of Teleportation Pads]] you can [[Housing Breakout Guide|break out]] to the beach area seen from the balcony or into the wooded area seen nearby.
