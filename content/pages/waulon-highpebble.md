**Waulon Highpebble** is a high-ranking member of The Far Seas Trading Company. He once oversaw the [[Isle of Refuge]], directing young adventurers to either [[Qeynos]] or [[Freeport]]. He was reassigned to [[Queen's Colony]] and [[Outpost of the Overlord]], and later reassigned as the Dockmaster at [[Butcherblock Mountains]].
