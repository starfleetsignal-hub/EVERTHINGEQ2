The language spoken by [[A four-armed seeker]], who is a member of the Akheva race in the Tranquil Sea. It is also spoken by Akhevan mercenaries around [[Castle Highhold]]. Akhevans are also plentiful in the [[Blood of Luclin]] expansion.

Completing the collection [[Akheva Runes]] allows you to learn this Akhevan language. See [[Fleshless Tongue Untied]] for the necessary steps.
