Two quests appear in game with the name **The Aqua's Aura**:

- [[The Aqua's Aura (Conservatory)]], which directs you to [[Vasty Deep: The Conservatory]] to complete the quest
- [[The Aqua's Aura (Vestigial Cella)]], which directs you to [[Vasty Deep: The Vestigial Cella]] to complete the quest

Neither quest is repeatable.
