The **Leaky Drake** was a for-profit venture of the [[The Far Seas Trading Company (Faction)|Far Seas Trading Company]], carrying trade goods. It went afoul of pirates in uncharted waters during an ill-fated trip between [[Qeynos]] and [[Faydwer]]. One survivor remains from the ship's final voyage, but little knowledge remains of the ship's final days other than what has been gleaned from the few pages remaining and legible in [[The Final Days of the Leaky Drake|Captain's Log]]. The current location of the wreckage of the Leaky Drake is unknown.
