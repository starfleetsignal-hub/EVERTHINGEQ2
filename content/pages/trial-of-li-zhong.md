## Notes

The **Trial of Li Zhong** can refer to the heroic quest Trial of Sun Kai or the related zone instance.

- There is only a heroic version for this trial.
- Mercenaries are permitted in the zone instance.
- There is no lockout timer for failing the instance, but the success lockout timer is 3 days.
- The reward for the heroic instance is 65 [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]]
- [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]] can be turned into [[Brother Huang Mantian]] for titles.
- A Full List the [[Fallen Dynasty]] trials can be found on the [[Tower of the Four Winds#Trials|Tower of the Four Winds Trials]] article.
