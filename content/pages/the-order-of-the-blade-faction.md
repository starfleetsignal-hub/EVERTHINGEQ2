desc: These fierce maidens of the cold blade are rumored to be the children of the [[E'ci (God)|Ice Goddess, E'ci]]. They believe that only through intense combat and challenge can true honor be obtained. The Order of the Blade is composed solely of valkyrie and is located in central [[Everfrost]], protecting [[Icespire Summit]]. They will act aggressively to any that stray too close to [[Icespire Caverns]], but [[Everfrost Timeline|may warm up]] to an adventurer that is willing to help them out. Due to shrinking territory after the near total destruction of the northlands post-[[The Age of Cataclysms (Lore)|Age of Cataclysms]], their ages long struggle against their neighbors, the Frosthorn Minotaur, has become more vicious.
