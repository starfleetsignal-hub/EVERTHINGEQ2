**Wildhide armor set**

- [[Wildhide Shoulderpads]]
- [[Wildhide Bracers]]
- [[Wildhide Boots]]

---

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

This armor may be worn only by: [[Bruiser|Bruisers]], [[Fury|Furies]], [[Monk|Monks]], [[Warden|Wardens]].

[[Falakhar]] in [[The City of Paineel]] will exchange [[Mark of Manaar|Marks of Manaar]] for Legendary armor.The head, chest, hands, and legs slots are expected to be filled with instance drops and are offered only as appearance items so characters may obtain a consistent look.

This armor is purchased as follows:

| Slot | Cost | Zone |
|---|---|---|
| Shoulders | 25 [[Mark of Manaar\|Mark of Manaars]] | [[The City of Paineel]] |
| Forearms | 15 [[Mark of Manaar\|Mark of Manaars]] | [[The City of Paineel]] |
| Feet | 15 [[Mark of Manaar\|Mark of Manaars]] | [[The City of Paineel]] |
