**The Archive of Ik** is a fan-site dedicated to the Iksar race. In addition to forums for [[Iksar]] to discuss items of interest, the site contains a large collection of Lore about [[Iksar]] history. Archive of Iksar is now defunct, we recommend the below website.

- [The Athenaeum](http://www.lorelibrary.com) - a website dedicated to all things Iksar from all EverQuest versions.
