This is an annual event, usually running in September and October for 9 consecutive weeks, with a new quest and new rewards released each week. Previously known as Days of Summer, officially renamed to Panda, Panda, Panda! starting in 2021.<br>
It is not marked on calendar, it is advised to watch [Announcements](https://forums.everquest2.com/index.php?forums/announcements.11/) section of the official forums in early fall.

## Requirements

- The quest series can only be completed by those with an [[All Access Membership]], paid traditionally or with [[Krono]].
- Previous seasons' quests in the series can be completed at any time. They are no longer limited to the period when this event is active.
- The quests must be completed in the order they were originally released. You will not be able to skip forward to select only certain quests in the series.
- This quest series is *not* level bound, but as you travel to locations with enemies starting in the 50+ level range, completing this on a character at that level or above is significantly easier.
- This event and some of its rewards may not be available on some [[Server Types|server types]], such as the Time-Locked Expansion (TLE) servers.
- Although the quests were initially unlocked by the week, players can now complete them in succession after the last one was introduced. *Originally there were a total of 9 weekly quests, with a new one available on Tuesdays.*
  - In the journal, the reward is listed as *Days of Summer 2017: Week (x)*, but completing each series unlocks a variety of items, many of which are only visible to characters by their class and level. For example, characters under 100 won't see *any* adornments on the merchant's list, because they all require that level. Likewise, a mage won't see items suited to tanks, and so on.
  - **Warning:** Some players have reported that entering Skyshrine does not update the quest [[The "Travels" of Yun Zi - Ice to See Velious]], even though the level requirement to enter that area was waved for this series. Using an appropriate adventure level character is advised.

## Quests

- All quests start in [[The Sundered Frontier]] on [[Spire Island]].
- *See [[The "Travels" of Yun Zi Timeline]] for the full list of quests in the series.*
- **Completing the quests on one character unlocks the ability to purchase items from the merchant account-wide.**
- The quests for this event require travel to many locations. Though they are not difficult, they can be time consuming.
- It's much easier to complete them on a higher level character with good gear, in case you "aggro" any NPCs as you travel.
- One phase of the quests will take you into [[Skyshrine: The City of Dracur|Skyshrine]]. The level requirement to enter has been *removed* for this quest, but getting to the zone in point is difficult if you've never been there or without a flying mount.

## Merchant Items

The original merchant [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]] is no longer a merchant. Items sold are split in between 2 merchants in order to highlight new items better:

- all older items were moved to [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2294, -199, 2506}} in [[The Sundered Frontier]].
- all new items in current season are sold by [[Tie Shan]] at {{waypoint 2292, -199, 2505}} in [[The Sundered Frontier]].

Once you complete these weekly quests all characters on your account can buy items from merchants on the Spire Island in [[The Sundered Frontier]]. The items are one per character. For example, you can only buy one [[Days of Summer Decoration Pack 2]] on your main character, but you can buy it again on any and all alts. You will not be able to unpack the same pack if you already bought on that character.

The items you can buy are:

- Potency [[Adornments|adornments]] for primary, secondary, ranged and 2H weapons
- Boost a single ascension class to level 5.  *Tip: best to use that on a ascension class you didn't started leveling yet*
- Orange [[Adornments|adornments]] to boost Ascension spells
- An armor set, suited by basic adventure class stats (e.g. a set suited for "tanks")
- [[Appearance]] gear pieces
- A manticore mount (lion body, wings)
- Several [[Decorating Houses|decorative house items]], some of which are normally sold for [[Marketplace|Daybreak Cash]]
- A [[Mara Estate]] [[Prestige Housing|prestige house]] that looks like the *entire* [[Isle of Mara]], without any NPCs or items in buildings.
- A [[Familiars|familiar]]
- A [[Mercenaries|mercenary]]

## Archived

- Days of Summer is an annual event which typically begins in August. The following message was posted on the official forums  [Update Notes: Tuesday August 1, 2017](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/update-notes-tuesday-august-1-2017.579973/#post-6453664).

*The Days of Summer have arrived in Norrath! Visitors to the spire island in the Sundered Frontier can meet Yun Zi, The Great Traveler, who will offer weekly quests that unlock Days of Summer rewards. Reward eligibility is account wide, so once a quest has been completed on a single character, all characters on the account will be eligible for those rewards.*
