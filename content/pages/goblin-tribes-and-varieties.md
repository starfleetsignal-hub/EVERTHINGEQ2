There are many different kinds of Goblins, with special adaptations to their environment leading to some extreme differences in appearance, and many tribes within some of the types.

### Surface-Dwelling Goblins

- **Sapswill** on the island of [[Queen's Colony]]. While hardly civilized like the Gigglegibbers, the Sapswill tribe is basically friendly, and take great joy in a simple, peaceful life.
- **Muckflick** in the [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
- **Sandscrawler** in the deserts of [[The Sinking Sands]] and [[The Pillars of Flame]] of the Desert of Ro. **Sandcrack** goblins in [[The Sinking Sands]] are believed to be a sub-caste of the Sandscrawlers, performing jobs considered too filthy or repugnant, even for a Goblin.
- **Sootfoot Talent Society** in [[Lavastorm]]

### Water-Loving Goblins

- **Fog Grinnin** in the West coastal areas of [[Enchanted Lands]].
- **Globbsunk Goblins** in [[Butcherblock Bay]] on the coast of the [[Butcherblock Mountains]]. Found on and around the island called [[Globbsunk Cove]], these would appear to be a low order of Goblin, as they do not seem to have a tribal name and do not affect any faction.
- **Icegill** in [[Everfrost]]
- **Deep Spawn** in [[The Timorous Moor]] and [[Wayunder Lake]] of the [[Loping Plains]]

### City-Dwelling Goblins

- **Gigglegibber** are believed to be the only tribe of essentially civilized goblins. Although greedy and, if opportunity presents, larcenous, they generally refrain from blatantly unlawful behavior and can be found in every city on [[Norrath]]. Their primary endeavor is the running of the Gigglegibber Goblin Gamblin' Game.

### Subterranean Goblins

- **Bone Blood** in the earthen tunnels beneath [[Stormhold]]
- **Runnyeye** in [[Enchanted Lands]] and [[Runnyeye]]
- **Grobins** in the [[Shroomba Subterrane]] of [[Greater Faydark]]
- **Grungetalon**, mostly consisting of miners and laborers in the depths of [[Kaladim]]
- **Boombottom**, the bomb-loving goblins nearer the entrance of [[Kaladim]]
- **Necrosis** on the island just north of [[The Tower of the Oracles]] in [[Antonica]]. Although some goblins appear on the island and wander the water, the bulk of this clan remains underground.
- **Gleam and Glimmer Goblins** within [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] and just outside guarding the back door entrance within [[The Thundering Steppes]].
- **Gobblerock** in a subterranean hideout near the gates to [[The Sprawl]] in [[The Commonlands]]
- **Mist Grinnin** in the [[Tombs of Night]] cave system, though they used to have larger groups above ground along the Torrent River a bit north of the [[D'Abth Bridge]]
