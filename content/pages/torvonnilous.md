| **Torvonnilous** |

| **Lord of Greed** |

The Lord of Greed, Torvonnilous personifies the essence of greed. He sits in his Demi-Plane of Greed, plotting new ways to expand his already vast expanse of wealth. His person is embodied as a human man, decorated in resplendent jewels, rings, amulets, fine silks, and an obsidian cane, topped with a platinum dragon’s head. Torvonnilous borders between neutral and evil. It is somewhat hard to tell, as he truly cares about none but himself.

[[Https://loreofnorrath.wordpress.com/2021/06/28/torvonnilous/ Source]]

He has not yet appeared in EQ2. A title bearing his name, however, is available in the "[[An ancestral scroll|An Ancestral Scroll]]" Veteran Reward item.
