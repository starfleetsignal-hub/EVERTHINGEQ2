**Limestone Amulet** can refer to two different necklaces:

- [[Limestone Amulet (no-trade)]] the old level 17 NO TRADE reward from the quest "[[Piecing Together the Clues]]" in [[Darklight Wood]]
- [[Limestone Amulet (attuneable)]] - the level 16 tradable necklace dropped by [[Ayamia the Rat Queen]] in [[Vermin's Snye]]
