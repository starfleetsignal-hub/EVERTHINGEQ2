Skyshrine Capital Guild Hall is a prestige [[Guild Halls|guild hall]].

## Overview

Unlike other guild halls, the the prestige halls give players the option to use [[Marketplace|Daybreak Cash]] to purchase a hall.

The halls still have upkeep costs like regular halls, though the coin cost is only for amenities, rather than the hall itself.

## How to Obtain

![Edited for Emphasis: the guild window area to check the ticket count.](images/Guild-window-skyshrine-count.jpg)
The guild must be at least level 30.

One or more members of the guild must purchase [[Highhold Guild Ticket|Highhold Guild Tickets]] from the [[Marketplace]].

- To "unlock" the ability to buy the hall, the guild needs a total of 400 tickets.
- Tickets can be purchased 1 at a time or in groups of 20.
- Each added ticket is tracked through the guild roster (press U) in the achievements tab.
- Excess tickets can be sold for [[Status points|status points]].

### Locations

This hall can be accessed at the same bell as the largest [[Freeport-style Guild Halls]] and large [[Qeynos-style Guild Halls]]:

- [[The Commonlands]] dock, northeast corner at {{waypoint -1055, -144, -683}}
- [[Antonica]] dock, southeast corner at {{waypoint 441, -37, 823}}

### Base Cost

The following are the associated *base* costs for this hall. Any [[Guild Hall Amenities|amenities]] added will have additional costs and most amenties also have an upkeep cost as well.
**Purchase Cost** 10000 status
**Upkeep Cost** 2000 status

### Amenities

Though the above are the base cost, the cost can be a mix of coin (platinum, gold, etc.) and additional status. See the [[Guild Hall Amenities]] for the costs of each.

- This hall has an option not available in other halls: a dragon that grants a small bonus to guild members.
  - As of fall 2016, the bonus is a 200,000 experience vial that is granted once per week.

## Style

This hall features a round entry courtyard with a huge courtyard and smaller outbuildings off to the sides. A large, gold dragon statue is prominent in the huge courtyard. While looking up, players will see the open sky many drakes flying around.
