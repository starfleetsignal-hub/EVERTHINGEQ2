## Effects

- On a hostile spell hit, this spell will cast ***Soothe*** on caster.
  - Heals caster for 10% of caster's max health.
    - This effect cannot be critically applied.
  - Increases power of caster by 10% of caster's max power.
    - This effect cannot be critically applied.
  - Grants a total of 5 triggers of the spell.
  - Will trigger off of heat abilities.
