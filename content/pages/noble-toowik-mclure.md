These NPC share the same name, but you must visit the appropriate one for your alignment.

- [[Noble Toowik Mclure (Qeynos)]] - The loyalty merchant in [[Qeynos Province District]] who sells loyalty rewards to citizens of Qeynos, New Halas, and Kelethin.
- [[Noble Toowik Mclure (Freeport)]] - The loyalty merchant in [[The City of Freeport]] who sells loyalty rewards to citizens of Freeport, Gorowyn or Neriak.

This NPC has the same name in **both** [[Qeynos Harbor]] and at the [[The Harbor Exchange]] in East [[Freeport]].

- He sells the **same items in both** cities.
- See the [[Loyalty Point Equipment Items]] for a list of items sold by this NPC.
