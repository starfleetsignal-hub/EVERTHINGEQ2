According to [[Marshal Ralem Christof]], the **Ewer of Sul'Dae** is a chalice of the gods that will either give you eternal youth, or turn you into an undead creature. It is mentioned during the quest, [[A Final Note (Quest)|A Final Note]]. According to [[Antiquitor Kantus Mor'Tael]], the name Sul'Dae is [[Elvish dictionary|elvish]], and "One word is of life and one is of death". According to [[1001 Tales of Maj'Dul]], the ewer is the "... vessel that sprang life to the [[Fyr'Un]], the river of Life that ran through the celestial worlds". The ewer is said to reside in the ancient elvish city of [[The Living Tombs|Ahket Aken]], which currently resides under the [[Sinking Sands]] as [[The Living Tombs]].

[[Phar'Ahkt]] says of the Ewer : "The Ewer of Sul'Dae bleeds the river of life. The great flow, the [[Fyr'Un]], winds through the land of [[Anashti Sul]]. The river runs free with her gift of eternal life. The ewer is a direct connection to this immortal flow. It is the ewer we shall drink from to taste the gift of Anashti Sul."

According to [[Seeker Abdeef]], the Ewer was removed from the city by [[Zombie Fadid|Fadid]].
