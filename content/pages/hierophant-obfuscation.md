## Effects

Absorbs x% of same-level spells: 1.5% / 3% / 4.5% / 6% / 7.5% / 9% / 10.5% / 12% / 13.5% / 15%

The percentage shown is vs. a same-level effect - i.e. if you are level 45 then the percentage shown applies to a level 45 effect. This would imply that the percentage is higher or lower depending if the effect is lower or higher level than you.
