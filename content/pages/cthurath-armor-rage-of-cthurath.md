Rage of Cthurath dungeon armors come in three tiers, denoted by their [[Resolve]] stat.  610, 620, and 630+.
While quest reward and crafted armors are present at [[Resolve]] 600, 610, and 620 - this guide if focused primarily on dropped/looted equipment.

## Prefixes and Suffixes

- The Prefix of dropped armors denotes the base [[Resolve]] of the item.  All items within the same prefix class will have the same resolve stat and overall stat distribution.

- Suffixes of dropped armors will lie primarily in 3 different areas of stats - **Melee DPS**, **Caster**, and **Tank/HP** stats.  Characters are encouraged to mix/match as needed for specific desired stats.

## Imposing (Resolve 610)

'Imposing' equipment comes from the loot chests of named enemies in Cthurath's 3 primary overland zones [[Yon Gorroth]], [[Oogothl Sprawl]], and [[The Unknown]]

Imposing gear bears 3 different Suffixes "of Open Skies", "of Rolling Hills", and "of Towering Peaks".

- These suffixes provide the same stat to specific slots of gear, regardless of armor types.
  - * 'Open Skies' is moistly melee stats*
  - * 'Rolling Hills' is mostly caster stats*
  - * 'Towering Peaks' is mostly tank/survivability stats*

(Ex: [[Imposing Barbute of Towering Peaks]])

- Not all suffixes are available in all slots of gear.

## Mordant (Resolve 620)

'Mordant' equipment comes almost exclusively from [Solo] and [Signature Solo] dungeon content from the following 9 dungeons:<br>

- [[Oogothl Sprawl: Coral Roost (Solo)|Oogothl Sprawl: Coral Roost [Solo]]]<br>
- [[Oogothl Sprawl: Ver'Bohten Atoll (Solo)|Oogothl Sprawl: Ver'Bohten Atoll [Solo]]]<br>
- [[Zon Zobboz: The Graftwerk (Solo)|Zon Zobboz: The Graftwerk [Solo]]]<br>
- [[Zon Zobboz: The Chimeric Chain (Solo)|Zon Zobboz: The Chimeric Chain [Solo]]]<br>
- [[The Unknown: Sacrificial Pursuits (Solo)|The Unknown: Sacrificial Pursuits [Solo]]]<br>
- [[The Unknown: Edge of Oblivion (Solo)|The Unknown: Edge of Oblivion [Solo]]]<br>
- [[Gerion: Dominion of Pain (Solo)|Gerion: Dominion of Pain [Solo]]]<br>
- [[Gerion: Ark of Ascension (Solo)|Gerion: Ark of Ascension [Solo]]]<br>
- [[Gerion: Rage of Cthurath]]
- *While the 620 chest and legs do rarely drop from end-of-dungeon bosses, the [[Rage of Cthurath: Rune a God Thing (Daily)|[Signature Daily]]] is generally the more reliable source for 620 Chest/Legs upgrades.*

Mordant has 3 primary Suffixes *"of the Umbrith"*, *"of the Warpwolf"*, and *"of the Grathok"*

- Umbrith: Deals primarily with Melee and associated stats.
  - *Expands upon the previous 'of Open Skies' suffix*
- Warpwolf: Deals primarily with Caster and associated stats.
  - *Expands upon the previous 'of Rolling Hills' suffix*
- Grathok: Deals primarily with Tank and other survivability stats.
  - *Expands upon the previous 'of Open Skies' suffix*

(Ex: [[Mordant Helm of the Grathok]])

In addition to the 3 primary 'Mordant' suffixes, quested Mordant equipment rewards from [[Gerion: Rage of Cthurath]] instances bear the suffixes *"of Torn Flesh"*, *"of the Rattled Mind"*, and *"of Broken Bones"*. Items with these suffixes carry identical stats to the 3 primary suffixes above and can be used interchangably.

- Torn Flesh = Umbrith
- Rattled Mind = Warpwolf
- Broken Bones = Grathok

(Ex: [[Mordant Helm of Broken Bones]] *compare to Helm of Grathok above*)

### 'Free' Blue Adorns

There is a final set Mordant equipment suffixes, *"of the Tendril"*, *"of Oblivion"*, and *"of the Spiral"* that have an uncommon drop rate (~ 1 in 10) from any of the bosses in a [Solo] dungeon.  What makes these items special is not necessarily the suffix itself, rather the fact that these items come pre-slotted with a random Cthurath-era blue adornment exclusive to dungeon drops.  There are 5 + 1 such adornments, and are generally considered upgrades to the Tishans's blue adornments - especially if a player has not had a chance to run Cthurath-era 135 [[Chrono Dungeons]] yet.

- [[Lost Time Actaea Stone]]
- [[Lost Time Bromi Stone]]
- [[Lost Time Coronis Stone]]
- [[Lost Time Ismene Stone]]
- [[Lost Time Orphne Stone]]

The most important of these blue adorns is

- [[Evie the Astrologer]].

Which is 1/3 of a set *(including [[Arnaut the Scribe]] & [[Viol the Botanist]])* that gives significant statistic boosts.  Evie is obtained primarily via [Solo] dungeons, though sometimes rarely in [Heroic]<br>*([Heroic] dungeons, instead, drop [[Cthurath Armor (Rage of Cthurath)#Upgraded 'free' Blue Adorns|an upgraded set of blue adorns]] including the 'Botanist' blue adorn.)*

The suffixes, themselves, like the quested suffixes above, are identical in stats to the 3 primary Mordant suffixes, and can also be used interchangably with those.

- Tendril = Umbrith
- Oblivion = Warpwolf
- Spiral = Grathok

### Unbound version

Equally rare to the 'blue adorn' versions of Mordent equipment drops, are the 'Unbound' versions of regular Mordent armors that can drop.  These unbound versions of certain equipment slots trade all of their raw Critical Bonus stat for Critical Bonus Overcap stat.  This can be useful if you are significantly over your current Critical Bonus cap.  ***Just be careful that in swapping you don't accidentally end up with a lower Critical Bonus than when you started (it's a large amount you're trading away to get that cap raised!)***

- Not all equipment slots have an equivalent Unbound version that drops (There are 25 total possible drops across the different armor type/suffix combinations). Primarily only:
  - Head
  - Shoulders
  - Feet
  - Cloak
  - Charm
  - Earring
  - Ring

(ex: [[Unbound Mordant Helm of the Grathok]])

## Creature Killer (Resolve 620)

'Creature Killer' equipment is identical in stats to Mordant armors of the same slot and type.  And drops from [Heroic] Overland named bosses. (Where 'Imposing' drops from [Solo] Overland nameds).  *There are 4 named [Heroic] encounters in each of the 3 Cthurath overland zones, for a total of 12 encounters.*

Like Mordant equipment, Creature Killer equipment has three suffixes, each corresponding to an equivalent suffix in the Mordant/Imposing lists above.

- 'of Seeking' = Umbrith
- 'of Trapping' = Warpwolf
- 'of Surviving' = Grathok

## Dominion (Resolve 630)

'Dominion' armors are largely to 630s, what Mordant was to 620s, but with a few final stats properly fleshed out.

- Dominion armors will be sourced almost entirely from [Heroic] versions of the previous dungeons.
  - While Legs and Chest do drop very rarely from [Heroic], [Untold Heroic] will be your more likely source for those two slots.
- In order to run Cthurath [Heroic] dungeons successfully, it is *highly recommended* that a player attain a minimum [[Resolve]] rating of 13,020 via 620 equipment.
- Dominion armors follow the same format as Mordant armors, in terms of suffixes, with the base stats of the armor starting at a higher amount than the previous Mordant.
- Stats are fully focused on Ubmrith, Warpwolf, and Grathok suffixes - no more Torn Flesh, Rattled Mind, or Broken Bones cluttering up the loot tables.

### Upgraded 'free' Blue Adorns

- The rare 'Tendril', 'Oblivion', and 'Spiral' armor/weapon drops will still make the occasional appearance.  But in [Heroic] dungeons, they have upgraded versions of the blue adorns.
  - [[Torn Reality Actaea Stone]]
  - [[Torn Reality Bromi Stone]]
  - [[Torn Reality Coronis Stone]]
  - [[Torn Reality Ismene Stone]]
  - [[Torn Reality Orphne Stone]]

The most important of these upgraded blue adorns is

- [[Viol the Botanist]].

Which is 1/3 of a set *(including [[Arnaut the Scribe]] & [[Evie the Astrologer]])* that gives significant statistic boosts.  Viol is obtained via the Cthurath-era [Heroic] dungeons

### Unbound-ed version

Like Mordant's 'Unbound' version of gear, Dominion has its own (slightly more lettery) 'Unbounded' version.  The biggest difference is that these Unbounded versions do not drop - they must be converted from specific Dominion pieces, via a consumable [[Matter Mallea]].

- The upside, is that players now have much more choice where and when to convert excess Critical Bonus into Critical Bonus Overcap.  It's not without its downsides, though...
  - [[Matter Mallea]] can only be used on specific pieces of level 135/630 gear.  The item description will indicate this ability to be converted at the top of the examine window should you come across a piece that can be converted.  (This conversion is permanent. You have been warned!)
  - The matter [[Matter Mallea]] you use must also match the slot-type of the item you are trying to convert.  You cannot use a 'necklace' Mallea on a pair of boots, for example.
  - [[Matter Mallea]] is Heirloom-only, so you must have an alt who knows the correct recipe and able to craft the matching Mallea you need for the conversion.
  - The recipes are uncommon drops (and also Heirloom-only) from all of the Cthurath-era 'invasion' PQs scattered across Norrath (including The Unknown)
- The good news, is the list of convertable 'types' has been expanded, as well the number of choices from each type as well.  Where Mordant only had 24 options for Unbound armors, Dominion has 74 viable items for Unbounded conversion.
  - In addition to the previous slots available for conversion, Unbounded Dominion armor adds several new slots to choose from:
  - Head
  - Shoulders
  - Forearms *(added)*
  - Hands *(added)*
  - Pants *(added)*
  - Feet
  - Cloak
  - Charm
  - Neck *(added)*
  - Earring
  - Ring
  - Waist *(added)*
