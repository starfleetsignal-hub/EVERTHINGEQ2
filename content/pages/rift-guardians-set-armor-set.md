- (2) Applies ***Focus: [[Winds of Healing VI]]***.
  - Increases base healing of [[Winds of Healing VI]] by 20%.
- (4) Applies ***Focus: [[Instinct V]]***.
  - Adds 7 Double Attack and 10% Accuracy to [[Instinct V]].
- (5) Applies ***Wildly Uncontrolled Healing***.
  - When target casts a healing spell this spell has a chance to cast Wildly Uncontrolled Healing on the group. This effect will trigger an average of 2.0 times per minute.
    - Heals group members for 275
    - This effect may trigger off each target of an AE heal.
  - On a successful spell attack this spell has a chance to cast Wildly Uncontrolled Healing on the group. This effect will trigger an average of 2.0 times per minute.
    - Heals group members for 275
    - This effect may trigger off each target of an AE heal.
- (6) +10 Heal Crit Bonus

This armor may be worn only by [[Warden|Wardens]].
