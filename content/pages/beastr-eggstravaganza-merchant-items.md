*See the [[Beast'r Eggstravaganza]] for more info.*
This event typically occurs in late March or Early April. Check your in-game calendar for actual dates.

## About

Unlike most [[Live Events]], this one has fairly simple items. The event skips the typical themed currency, allowing the exchange of captured eggs pets for other eggs that can be kept as house pets.
The eggchangers can be found in the following locations:

- [[Darklight Wood]]: {{waypoint 519, -38, -230}} (Down the road from the [[Griffin Tower]] in this zone)
- [[Freeport]]: {{waypoint -82, -37, 8}} (In the small merchant courtyard near the guild registar)
- [[Frostfang Sea]]: {{waypoint 86, 145, -63}} (Behind the Carin of the Huntress shrine)
- [[Greater Faydark]]: {{waypoint 197, 111, 143}} (Near the bank in Old Kelethin)
- [[Qeynos Province District]]: {{waypoint 624, -15, -350}} (In the grove area, rather than the city)
- [[Timorous Deep]]: 2,215 55 1094 (near the Gorowyn Beach Station)
