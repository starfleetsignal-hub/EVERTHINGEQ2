These spells/combat arts either never get updated or do not get updated between 91 and 125.

- 'Non-upgradeable' mostly *(though not always)* refers to earning new ranks of the spell at higher levels *(ex: Intercept I vs Intercept II, etc)* - it is not referring to possible expert/master/grandmaster upgrades of the spell itself. Many of the abilities listed below will have expert/grandmaster upgrades for the level they *do* exist at.
- in the "most" cases you will get a new Version of the spell/combat art after 10 or 15 levels up to 125
- in some cases you get an Version of the spell/combat art at between 60 and 80 and the next upgrade past 110
- *Note : For EVERY class, the Mastery Strike is level ~5 and as such can be upgraded to Ancient quality extremely fast.*
