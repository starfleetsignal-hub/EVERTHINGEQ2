![Top: the "awakened" area of the house. Bottom: the "sleeper" area of the house.](images/Kerafyrm-lair.png)
**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** by completing the [[Fabled Time Traveler]] achievement during the [[Chronoportal Phenomenon]] event.
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** huge, the entry level looks like the [[Sleeper's Tomb]] and the blue area is thought to be a hail to Everquest.
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** flying and ground mounts

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- You must get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- It has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- You can get a [[House Portal|portal]] to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]. Portals allow for quick travel between locations.
- You can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

This house is comprised of two very different styles of enormous, dungeon-sized rooms. Each room has varied levels and platforms.

The "awakened" entry and first area is earth tones with gold accents and bright, colored crystals affixed to the building's walls. The second "sleeper" area is blue and gray tones. Players can travel between these two areas by clicking on portals named "awakened" to travel to the entry or "sleeper" to travel to that area.

Players can see a [video tour](https://youtu.be/9lGHgataR5I).

### Break Out

Using a [[Bag of Teleportation Pads|bag of teleportation pads]], you can break out of the normal limits of this home.

Though this house doesn't have a traditional in/outside boundary, a few locations have been noted to break out of the common limits of this house.

See the [[Housing Breakout Guide]] for details on how breakouts work. See the specific [Kerafyrm's Lair breakput guide](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/house-preview-kerafyrms-lair.588981/) on the official forums.

### Building Blocks

There are no building blocks to match this home at this time.

## Cost and Item Count

- After completing the achievement, locate it in your bag, then travel to one of the two locations to "buy" it at no cost.
- 900 items and 900 [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]]
