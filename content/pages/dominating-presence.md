![Dominating Presence effect of named Monster Galadoon](images/Effect_DominatingPresence.jpg)
The less of you actively engaged in combat against the buffed-named the greater it dominates the battlefield!
Effects:

- Gains offensive power with each increment. Max increment count is 23, should one be foolished enought to attempt this alone...
- If no increments are displayed, enemy presence is at maximum and caster gains no advantage.

## Note

Also party-members which are dead (e.g. by using a [[Exothermic Lifestone]]) before the encounters starts, count as not "actively engaged in combat".
