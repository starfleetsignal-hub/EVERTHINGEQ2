- (2) Applies ***Focus: [[Autumn's Kiss VI]]***.
  - Increases the base heal amount of [[Autumn's Kiss VI]] by 30%.
- (4) Applies ***Focus: [[Death Swarm VII]]***.
  - Increases base damage of [[Death Swarm VII]] by 30% and reduces target's melee crit chance by 5%.
- (5) Applies **Wildly Uncontrolled Healing**
  - On a successful spell attack this spell has a chance to cast Wildly Uncontrolled Healing on the group. This effect will trigger an average of 2.0 times per minute.
    - Heals group members for 275
    - This effect may trigger off each target of an AE heal.
  - When target casts a healing spell this spell has a chance to cast Wildly Uncontrolled Healing on the group. This effect will trigger an average of 2.0 time per minute.
    - Heals group members for 275
    - This effect may trigger off each target of an AE heal.
- (6) +8 Spell Crit Bonus<br>+4 Heal Crit Bonus

This armor may be worn only by: [[Fury|Furies]].
