### GENERAL:

- Fixed a bug where not all of your points spent would load from an AA mirror.
- Granted a free respec on all trees to all players.

### POPULATION / ZONE PROGRESSION

*Sirens Grotto Raid*

- Caella the Pearl encounter - Siren Controllers are slower to react after spawning and have less health. Entrancer Lisha now waits a while after casting Numb Senses to cast her Consume Vitae AOE.
- Entrancer Lisha will no longer charm pets with Siren’s Melody.

*Siren’s Grotto: Alluring Embrace* [Heroic]

- Health has been reduced on a number of NPCs.

*Drinal’s Castle Raid*

- Fitzpitzle should now properly advance to his rooms when defeated.

### ABILITIES / ALTERNATE ADVANCEMENT

*Warden*

- When turned into a group effect, Instinct's damage should now stack when applied from multiple players.

### QUESTS

A Helping Hand - Access to the Scarstone world bell, and destination, in Cobalt Scar is now granted upon quest completion.

### ITEMS

- Shoulders awarded from the quest The Rock in Cobalt Scar have been changed to match progression. They have been upgraded.
- Dagger of the Archmagus will no longer proc in PVP combat.
- Cloak of Thule and Cloak of Justice will no longer proc in PVP combat.
- PVP jewelry set bonuses will now activate more reliably in battlegrounds.
- Briny Deep will now proc off of ranged attacks.
- Indestructible Being will now proc off of ranged attacks.

### BATTLEGROUNDS

*Ganak*

- Stealth/Invisibility run speed mods will no longer affect your speed when carrying the flag.
