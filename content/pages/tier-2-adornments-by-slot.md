The table below details the **Tier 2 Adornments** organized by the slots to which they apply:

**Lesser** adornments appear with no highlighting. They require 1 [[Aether Powder]] and 6 [[Coral Fragment|Coral Fragments]].  Up to 3 [[Coral Fragment|Coral Fragments]] are returned per craft.

**Greater** adornments appear with brown highlighting. They require 1 [[Infusion of Air]], 2 [[Aether Powder|Aether Powders]], and 6 [[Coral Fragment|Coral Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Aether Powder]] is returned per craft.

**Superior** adornments appear with red highlighting. They require 1 [[Processed Mana]], 2 [[Infusion of Air|Infusions of Air]], 3 [[Aether Powder|Aether Powders]] and 6 [[Coral Fragment|Coral Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Infusion of Air]] is returned per craft.

For a simple, alphabetic listing of tier 2 adornments, see Tier 2 Adornments.
