The Nights of the Dead Devotee is a suffix title given for completing the [[Nights of the Dead Devotee (Achievement)]].

- The associated achievement can *only* be completed during the annual [[Nights of the Dead]] event.

## Setting Titles

To set suffix and/or prefix titles *after* you earn them:

1. Press **C** to open the Character sheet
1. Click on the Options tab
1. Locate the drop down to see all of the titles you have earned and select the title you want to display.
- You can see the title displayed with your character's name at any time zooming out and by pressing F1 to target your own character.
