**Swimming** is a General skill possessed by all classes of all races.

## Notes

- This skill determines how much breath you have underwater (unless you're an Iksar or Froglok, which breathe underwater) and how fast you can swim.
- Leveling swimming is very easy; find any pool of water deep enough to submerge yourself, and swim around until satisfied.
- Walking through shallow (up to waist-high) water can also level swimming skill.
- Your swimming level cap increases by 5 for each adventure level you gain.

## Skill Cap

The skill cap for swimming is based off your adventure level only.
