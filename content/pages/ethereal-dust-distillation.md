- **Recipe Level:** 80
- **Workstation:** [[Chemistry Table]]

## Ingredients

- 3 [[Smoldering Dust|Smoldering Dusts]]
- 4 [[Ethereal candle|ethereal candles]]

## Yields

- 1 [[Ethereal Dust]]
- 1 [[Smoldering Dust]]
