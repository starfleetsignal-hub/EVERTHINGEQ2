**Stormbringer's Venerable armor set**

- [[Venerable Cap of the Stormbringer]]
- [[Venerable Shoulder Pads of the Stormbringer]]
- [[Venerable Tunic of the Stormbringer]]
- [[Venerable Bracers of the Stormbringer]]
- [[Venerable Gloves of the Stormbringer]]
- [[Venerable Pants of the Stormbringer]]
- [[Venerable Moccasins of the Stormbringer]]

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

This armor may be worn only by: [[Fury|Furies]].

These pieces are available for vendor purchase from [[Muram Bladebender]] at {{waypoint -102, -1, -36}} in [[The Fortress of Drunder]] at [[The Great Forge]].

The pieces of this set can also be crafted by level 90 [[Artisan]] who have purchased the recipe [[Ancient Armor of War]] from [[Brontis]] in [[The Great Forge]].

Note that the [[Venerable Tunic of the Stormbringer]] only drops from a mob. It can not be purchased or crafted.
You can create it from a [[Irradiating Hauberk]] which drops from [[Warmaster Deynka Packlasher]] in [[Tower of Tactics]]

After today's patch (11.10.2011), some of these items can also drop from mobs in Drunder:
•Grolla will now drop shoulders
•Warmaster Korok Hai will now drop legs
•Dvuul Ripclaw will now drop boots
•Hamslammer has a chance to drop forearms or gloves
•Rageborne MacKulla has a chance to drop forearms or gloves
