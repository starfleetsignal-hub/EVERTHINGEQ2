Shadowed Heart is a prefix title that you can earn by completing the [[Love Found in Innothule]] [[Achievements|achievement]] during [[Erollisi Day]].

To set a title you earn, press **C* to open the character sheet. Click on the Options tab and use the drop-down arrows to choose a title.
