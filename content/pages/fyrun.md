Fyr'Un (or "The River of Life") is the river that flows from [[The Fountain of Life]]. The river is empowered by the goddess [[Anashti Sul (God)|Anashti Sul]] (who originally introduced the undead onto Norrath) and is said to hold the power of eternal life. The undead in [[The Silent City]] feed upon this power as they await the return of Anashti Sul from her exile. The name Fyr'Un is derived from [[Elvish dictionary|Elvish]] words meaning "Fyr" (life) and "Un" (all of).  According to [[1001 Tales of Maj'Dul]], the [[Ewer of Sul'Dae]] is the "... vessel that sprang life to the [[Fyr'Un]], the river of Life that ran through the celestial worlds".
