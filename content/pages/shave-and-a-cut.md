## Notes

- Allows you to re-customize your character's visual appearance
- Make sure to remove illusions any active illusions, especially race changing ones.
  - Be sure that "Hide Illusions" option is not checked
- Any hat/help/hood/head cover is viewed in the tiny paperdoll preview. Best to remove/hide while previewing
- The presets are stored in <code>EQ2ROOT\saved_customizations</code> as <code>.ecf</code> files. You can freely move/share/post these.
- The charm item is consumed and removed from inventory only when you "Accept" a new look. You can purchase, activate, play around, save your new profile, and hit cancel to apply later.
- There is no longer a 1 week re-use restriction
