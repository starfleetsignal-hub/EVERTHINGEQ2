Future Echoes is a quest found on the EverQuest Worlds Mobile App. The app and quest were introduced at SOE Live 2013.
Quest Steps

The quest involves finding and scanning (with your mobile device) a series of EverQuest-lore related images.
Quest Rewards

Complete the quest to learn the rewards.
