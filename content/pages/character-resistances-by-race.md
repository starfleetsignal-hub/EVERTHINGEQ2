The following table is the racial differences in resistances, which scale with level.  It's a comparison of the resistance numbers of the characters with no equipment.
the difference between a 1 and a 6 should be about 4.7% mitigation unarmored it seems.
They are relative ratings.  1 is still a racial increase every level i guess, just small and losing relative mitigation against equal level mobs.

Fae, Arasai, Humans and Barbarians total to slightly higher numbers, i believe.
Sarnaks seem to use some different kind of system than the "Start low, get more and more disparate while increasing, as you level" .  The one i leveled to level 10 started at all scores of 9 which is very high compared to other starting for the old races and remained at 9 up to level 10, where such a score is probably somewhat low.  If they remain at 9 throughout the game it would of course be very weak and bad because other races get 70-130 and such by tier 3 inherent.  i'm still looking into it.
