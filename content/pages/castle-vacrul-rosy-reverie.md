[[Kartur]] {{waypoint -28, 0, 47}} Pick up items from around the zone for his display cases. Periodically during the fight he will check his master's inventory and call a specific item he's looking for. Place that item in the case to remove the offensive buff he gets. If it stacks to 5 he will kill the party. Also has a knockback/daze trauma AOE effect and calls in thrallic footman adds.![](images/Karturbuff.png)

[[T'valla Rilayne]] {{waypoint 72.81, -0.70, 7.46}} Alternatively calls in waves of adds that are either bats or vampires based on what it calls. Seems tank n' spank.

Fireplace opens up {{waypoint 70.88, -0.75, 21.64}}

[[Vhenex the Werefather]] with adds [[Sister Herex]] and [[Brother Shen]] {{waypoint 104.19, -15.46, 113.75}} clear the room of adds. Maintains a combat mitigation buff if any of his pack are alive. Kill [[Brother Shen]] first, [[Sister Herex]] and [[Vhenex the Werefather]] last.
Platforms in this room remove the red beam, but cannot 'see' past the platform to get the adds on the other side's platform. Not sure of function of this, except that it clears the giant red beam in the room. Killing the were trio also removes the beam from the room.

Hail [[Lyrez O'Scholoran]] to make him attackable n kill him.

- Black Rook on A-4 move straight ahead to H-4. White Rook takes Black Rook. (In the solo, move this to the furthest square opposite its position)
- Black Bishop at B-2 move to E-5, take White Knight. White Queen takes Black Bishop. (In the solo, move this on a diagonal to take the White Knight)
- Black Queen at F-1 to G-2 for checkmate. (In the solo move the Black Queen ONE square on a diagonal to take the White King).

Spawns [[The Black King]] Chess adds spawn. Stay on board to maintain encounter. [[A black queen]] requires player to be on black square to receive damage.

[[Roseblood and Thornblood]] Epic x2 linked encounter. Room will start ticking health damage as soon as you step into it. Use water breathing to negate the damage of the room. Surrounded by several linked *a thallic reaper* mobs that have buff of Sanguine Mind - increases AE, strikethrough and accuracy by 50%. Roseblood is easier to kill. Thornblood has a 50% damage reduction buff that can be dispelled.
