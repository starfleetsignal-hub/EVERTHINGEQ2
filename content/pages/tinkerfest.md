Celebrated by Gnomes throughout [[Norrath]], the **Tinkerfest** holiday occurs for several days in June (moved from end of July in 2022 with the introduction of [[Summer Jubilee]]) and exalts the craft of [[Tinkering]] and the joy of Clockworks. During Tinkerfest, Gnomes hold festivals celebrating tinkering and all things gnomish, frequently exchanging gifts with family members and celebrating their heritage. Once celebrated regularly, it has only been recently resurrected [[The Shattering|post-shattering]].

## Overview

Speaking [[Gnomish]] will be handy for this holiday!  [[Tik-Tok]] is not required but can also be helpful.

Celebrations and celebrators can be found wherever gnomes are found. To find out more about this summer celebration, speak with any Celebrations Coordinator NPC. However, the central location to all Tinkerfest celebrations is in the [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]]. To make your way there, many "Unstable Tinkered Portals" can be found throughout Norrath to transport you to the party headquarters. Before you go, make sure you stop by a [[Language Sellers|merchant who sells language primers]] if you don't speak Gnomish, as you need it for some quests!

- This event offers steampunk-themed cosmetic clothing and accessories, mad machines to [[Decorating Houses|decorate]] with, and robot pets to follow you everywhere you go.
- Be sure to do [[Great Balls of Fire]] quest early on, it can seem like the quests "stop" if that one is missed.

- The PQ [[Quest: Hack 'n Slash]] drops parts for a familiar, and a Green Adornment with Fervor, plus two limited-use recipes.  There are many other goodies for decorators and a Mythical armor piece for Head slot that features the Accuracy stat.
- Tinkerfest's [[Aether Racing|Aether races]] are considered "the hardest" - give them a try, the Achievement, [[Furiously Fast]], really feels like you've accomplished something.
- Go prepared:  your tinkerer will need more [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog|Shiny Tinkerfest Cogs]] to buy all the blueprints this year, assuming they don't have them yet. [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog|Shiny Tinkerfest Cogs]] can be picked up from the ground in Steamfont and other locations with portals to Gnome HQ listed below.
- Tinkerfest Cheer - have you taken a close look at the effect given by the Gnomish spirits?  Go look, you may want to spread more cheer this year.

## Event Currency and Merchants

### Currency

![A Shiny Tinkerfest Cog as it appears on the ground before it is picked up.](images/Shiny_tinkerfest_cog.png)
This event has *two* types of currency.

- The common type is [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog|Shiny Tinkerfest Cogs]]. These are very easy to obtain, because you can simply pick them up where you find celebrating gnomes around the [[Unstable Tinkered Portals]]. It is used to buy the majority of items sold my merchants.
- The more valuable [[Platinum Tinkerfest Cog|Platinum Tinkerfest Cogs]] are rewarded for completing specific quests. Because they are more valuable and used to buy only the very powerful items like the the [[Swift-Kicking Pedipowered Leaper]] mount and the [[Boostbarrel Jump-Pack]] mount. In 2025 they are added as rewards from [[Summer Jubilee]] content white Tinkerfest is active.
- Tips:
- Before spending the Platinum version of the cogs, check both merchants for items that require them!
- The cogs have a fast respawn time, but if you feel that you're being trampled in the rush of party-loving gnomes, check out one of the alternate locations.

### Merchants

Both merchants are located in [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] at {{waypoint -579, 171, 995}}. They are standing next to each other amidst the celebrating Gnomes.

- [[Myron]] - has all new items added in the current year
- [[Tarly]] - has previous years' tinkering recipe books and items. Many items are the same as craftable versions found in the Tinkerfest blueprints, while some are unique to the vendor.

**See the [[Tinkerfest Merchant Items]] page for a full list of items sold by Myron and Tilly.**

#### Tinkering and Tinkerfest

You don't have to be a [[Tinkering|Tinkerer]] to enjoy the event, but **do** need the skill to use the [[Tinkerfest Merchant Items|Blueprints sold]] during the event. The good news is this event offers some opportunity to help speed up the leveling of the Tinkering skill.

- If you have your Tinkering skills leveled up to 400 or 450, you can use [[Tinkered Clockwork Brain Blueprints|Clockwork Brain]] recipe to cheaply finish up to 500.  Otherwise, the pressure on Amber will make it very expensive, or time consuming to harvest more.
- Tinkerfest recipes seem to have a better chance to give skillups. See the  [[Tinkering]] page for more ideas.

## Cog-Collecting Macro

![Sample "Harvest Tinkerfest Cog" Macro](images/Harvest_Tinkerfest_Cog_Macro.png)

You can make a [[Slash Commands|macro]] for harvesting Tinkerfest [[Shiny|purple shinies]] and [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog|Shiny Tinkerfest Cogs]]. Simply walk up to either a shiny or a Shiny Tinkerfest Cog and click the macro!

1. Press **O** to open the Macros window.
1. Find a blank macro slot and click on **<click to edit>**
1. Add a Command Step: **/target_nearest_npc**
1. Add a Spell/Ability step, then from your Knowledge Window, drag over the Ability **Collecting**
1. Add a Spell/Ability step, then from your Knowledge Window, drag over the Ability **Gathering**
1. Give your macro a title. I like to drag a [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog]] onto the icon picture myself.
1. Click **OK**
1. Drag the new macro to a hotbar for easy access.
