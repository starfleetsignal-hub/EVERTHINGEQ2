## Effects

x% greater approach to maximum defense: 2%

Increases defense skill for the Animist's pet. Note that this means, practically, only a small increase in the avoidance of the pet. (Make sure to use [[Possess Minion]] to check how much you are altering the pet's abilities with defensive stances before investing in this ability very heavily.)
