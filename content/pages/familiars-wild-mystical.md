This table is a guide to some of the available creatures and is sorted into ascending level order to assist finding appropriate creatures. Other types of creature are available from the [[Familiars Wild/Mob List|Index Page]].

Even though many creatures may have the 'bag' flag (i.e. symbol) visible it does not guarantee they can be captured in [[Familiar Cage|Familiar Cages]]. Unfortunately over the years inconsistancies have crept into EverQuest II. Only some named and/or Heroic MOBs can be captured while others can not. Similar inconsistencies can be found within classes of creatures. Creatures that can not be captured are listed but their names will be crossed out (i.e. [Strikethrough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strikethrough)).
