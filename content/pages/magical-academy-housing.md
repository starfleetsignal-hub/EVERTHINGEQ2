**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** See Notes section below
**Available Location:**

- [[The City of Freeport]], *inside* the [[The Academy of Arcane Science]], take the elevator down to {{waypoint 17, -43, -146}}
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** see About section below
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** none allowed

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- You must get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- It has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- You can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- You can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

![This loot card is from the Legends of Norrath Ethernauts pack.](images/Tax-free-licence-loot.jpg)
Over the years, major confusion about this house has carried on due to a single loot card rewarding a *choice* between this and an equally large house in Qeynos. The issue is further addressed in the Notes section below.

Magical Academy Housing is identical to the large Freeport house at [[3 Compassion Road]], but because it is a [[Prestige Housing|prestige house]], it has *zero* standard coin or status upkeep.

Though it behaves in all manners like a prestige house, once you are inside it can have its walls, floors, and ceilings changed just like the all other standard Freeport homes. See the [[Standard Housing]] page for details.

It is *not* the same as the [[Arcane Academy]] prestige house from the [[Marketplace]], which looks like the interior of [[The Academy of Arcane Science]]!

## Item Count

*Item counts have changed once already and can change again at any time.*
Unlike all other prestige homes which have a 900 item limit, this mimics the large, [[Standard Housing|standard housing]] for this zone and has the following limits:

- 700 items and 700 [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]].

## Notes

This house is one of two choices when you get a *A Tax-Free Housing Licence*. The other choice is [[Concordium Tower Magical Manor]] in the [[The Concordium Tower]].

- *A Tax-free Housing License* is looted from Legends of Norrath, Ethernauts card packs
- You **can not** buy this house from the [[Marketplace]], because it is random loot from Legends of Norrath (LoN).
  - Players with [[Free-To-Play|All Access]] accounts get five free LoN card packs per month (as of 2016).
- Even though you can not buy it outright, this house can be toured by visiting location in Freeport (given above)
