## Effects

- When damaged with a melee weapon this spell has a 5% chance to cast Thule's Terror on target's attacker. Lasts for 15 seconds.
  - Makes target afraid.
  - Roots target.
  - 15% chance to dispel when target takes damage.
  - Resistibility increases against targets higher than level 116.
- When damaged this spell will cast ***Fear Spines*** on target's attacker. Lasts for 2 seconds.
  - Inflicts poison damage on target.
  - Inflicts poison damage on target every second.
