desc: Deep within the fiery abyss of the volcanic lands dwell the Guardians of Thyr, cruel fire giants. From an underworld they call Thyr they act as a frontline defense of their master, the red dragon, [[Lord Nagafen (Nagafen's Lair)|Lord Nagafen]]. The Guardians of Thyr occupy the lower levels of [[Solusek's Eye]], protecting the heart of the dungeon, [[Nagafen's Lair]]. While the above paragraph, taken from the in-game factions tab, states that the 'underworld' they live in is called 'Thyr', the fire giants' leader is named [[Flame Lord Thyr]]. Perhaps this is a development oversight or perhaps the leader of the faction assumes the name 'Thyr' upon their ascension to ruler.
