## Overview

A Key component of the [[Chrono Dungeons]] system, these *consumable* tokens are required to convert qualifying [Heroic] dungeon instance lockouts into a upscaled 'Chrono' version of the same lockout.

## How to Obtain

The currently available tokens for purchase are:

- [[Chrono Dungeons: (Level 130) (Version 1)]] - [[Nemok Time-Touched (Splendor Sky Aerie)]]
- [[Chrono Dungeons: (Level 130) (Version 2)]] - [[Nemok Time-Touched (Western Wastes)]]
- [[Chrono Dungeons: (Level 135)]] - [[Kehar Time-Touched (Yon Gorroth)]]

## Usage

- One token converts the entire chosen instance.
- The player activating the token must be IN the instance they wish to convert.
  - Best practices dictate that it is best to wait for the entire party to be IN the zone before consuming a token, as it technically transports you and your party to a new instance of the same dungeon, but with the properly level'd enemies.  If all party members are not present, it can cause shenanigans for players trying to enter/exit the instance, preventing them from being able to access the correct upgraded instance.  *Just be patient while waiting for others to zone-in.* ;-)
- Currently only one token is required per-party-per-instance.
- Each player has their own 24 hour USAGE cooldown per token, but can be the *recipient* of unlimited token effects from others.
