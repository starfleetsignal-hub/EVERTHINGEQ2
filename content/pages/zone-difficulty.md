The difficulty of a zone can vary significantly. The following terms are used to give an idea of what you should expect when attempting a specific zone.

## Public

This zone is open to all, whether grouped or not. It will contain areas of assorted difficulty, both Soloable and Heroic, spread across the level range indicated. A new [[Instance]] will fork if the zone contains more than 100 players.

## Solo

This zone is [[Instance|Instanced]] and is designed for 1-2 players within the level range indicated.

## Group

This zone is [[Instance|Instanced]] and is designed for a full group of 6 players within the level range indicated.

## Epic x2

This zone is [[Instance|Instanced]] and is designed for 2 full groups (12 players) within the level range indicated.

## Epic x3

This zone is [[Instance|Instanced]] and is designed for 3 full groups (18 players) within the level range indicated.

## Epic x4

This zone is [[Instance|Instanced]] and is designed for 4 full groups (24 players) within the level range indicated.

Generally speaking, x4 Raid zones are some of the most difficult and challenging content in the game, and should not be attempted lightly.
