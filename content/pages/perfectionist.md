- [[Assassinate]] and [[Decapitate]] only go down to 10 minutes instead of 7.5 minutes.

The math is (Base Reuse Speed/1.Reuse Reduction %)=New Reuse Speed. So the math (in seconds) for Sniper Shot (15 minute reuse) would look like this:

900/1.50=600
