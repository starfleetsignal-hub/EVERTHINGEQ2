**Catalogue Creature** is an ability you receive the first time you accept a Catalog Quest. To use it, target the creature directly. It will not work if you are targeting another player who is targeting the creature (known as [[Indirect Targeting]]). This ability will not work at any range. You must be able to see and target the creature within 15 meters of you.
