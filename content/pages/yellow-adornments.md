**Yellow Adornments** can be added onto items to augment their stats.  Items that have *Yellow Adornment* slots are typically, but not solely [[Legendary]] items.

- Sold for 5 [[Mark of Manaar]] by vendor [[Elendra]] in [[Paineel]]
