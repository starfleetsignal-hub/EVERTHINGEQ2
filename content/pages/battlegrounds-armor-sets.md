These armor sets can be purchased from the [[Scion of Baast]], a [[Sisters of War|Sister of War]] located in all major [[Cities]].  The Qeynos Sisters are in Qeynos Harbor.

- Plate fighter - [[Challenger's Dreadnaught (Armor Set)]]
- Leather fighter - [[Challenger's Kejaan (Armor Set)]]
- Plate healer - [[Challenger's Warpriest (Armor Set)]]
- Leather healer - [[Challenger's Wrathcaller (Armor Set)]]
- Chain healer - [[Challenger's Dark Voodoo (Armor Set)]]
- Chain dps - [[Challenger's Brutallic (Armor Set)]]
- Cloth w/o pet - [[Challenger's Deathdealer (Armor Set)]]
- Cloth w/ pet - [[Challenger's Devastation (Armor Set)]]
