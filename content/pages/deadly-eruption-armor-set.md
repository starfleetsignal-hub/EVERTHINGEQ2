This is the Fabled shard armor set (referred to as Tier 3) introduced in [[LU51|GU51]] for [[Predator|Predators]] (). However, it can also be worn by all other [[Scout|Scouts]], [[Berserker]], [[Defiler]], [[Guardian]], [[Inquisitor]], [[Mystic]], [[Paladin]], [[Shadowknight]], [[Templar]].

The Molds for these drop in the X2 raid zone [[Ward of Elements]]. You have to buy the Patterns from [[Assilus Poisonbolt]] designated as <Elemental Pattern Quartermaster> in exchange for the Molds and some [[Void Shard|Void Shards]]. The actual armor pieces are available for vendor purchase from [[Borden Chargehammer]], designated as the <Elite Void Shard Armor Quartermaster> in [[Lavastorm]] at the [[Shrine of Thunder|Docks]]. The price is the Pattern and the respective Tier 2 shard armor piece. The Bracers are actual drops in [[Ward of Elements]] and [[Kurn's Tower]].

NOTE: As of February 17, 2013, no Patterns are needed to be purchased from [[Assilus Poisonbolt]]. You can purchase the armor pieces straight from [[Borden Chargehammer]] in exchange for the Mold, 5 to 12 [[Void Shard|Void Shards]] (depending on an item), and 9p 60g.

These pieces are upgrades from the equivalent Tier 2 pieces from the set: [[Ravaged Vigor (Armor Set)]]
