## Overview

[[LU131]] Gerion's Reckoning added a new optional activity to harvesting in [[Rage of Cthurath]]'s 3 overland zones: [[Yon Gorroth]], [[Oogothl Sprawl]] and [[The Unknown]].<br>
After fully harvesting a node there is a chance that a visible critter will spawn. Player then has to use a new ability [[Sweep for Critters]] to pick up a collectible.

## Requirements

To take part in the fun you need to:

- Complete crafting signature questline ending with [[Destination Unknown: Additional Tasks]]
- Purchase [[Critter Collecting Starter Kit]] from [[Assistant Flamebearer Silverpot]] at {{waypoint -894, -52, 1006}} in [[The Unknown]] for 50x [[Token of the Formless Crafter]]
  - unpack this starter kit to receive ability [[Sweep for Critters]] which auto scribes into your knowledge book, plus instruction book [[Void Guide to Critter Collecting: Volume I]], which can be read from inventory  and placed in player house.

## Collecting critters

Add your new ability to a hot key or your harvesting macro and you are ready to go and collect critters. This really is a new way of collecting special collectibles, just with a fun animation of you catching various bugs into a net.<br>

After a harvesting node is fully harvested it has a chance to spawn a critter, the looks of them vary by node and sometimes by zone. It takes a second or two to spawn a critter so don't run off just yet.

- Watch out for blue text in chat window "You notice a critter emerge from the disturbance you created! If you are swift, you may be able to catch it!" and a visible critter large or small spawning in the spot where node was.
- Use your [[Sweep for Critters]] ability to pick up a collectible.
- You need to be in range to pick it up.
  - In case of fish nodes, which by default have rather large harvesting range, if you are on shore and can reach the shoal of fish node, then you will be able to pick up the collectible too.

## Collections by node
