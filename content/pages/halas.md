Racial homeland of [[Barbarians]]. Halas is placed deep in the frozen heart of [[Everfrost]]. It was attacked and conquered by the [[Rallosian Army]], but eventually retaken before it was finally destroyed by the [[Shattering]].  Its ruins can be found under the waters of the [[Icegill Lagoon]] in Everfrost.

[[New Halas]] was released as part of LU56 on May 25, 2010, as a good-aligned starting city.
