**D**edicated **I**ndividuals **R**ecovering **T**echnology of **Y**esteryear

The group of (mostly) gnomes investigating and excavating the ancient airship in [[Sinking Sands]]. Many quests and events circled around this group over the summer of 2008, that eventually lead to the launch of [[The Shadow Odyssey]] and GU50.
