Many EverQuest II zones are referenced by common, player-accepted abbreviations. However, some zones have no common abbreviation and others are difficult to abbreviate. This list compiles abbreviations the EQ2 community at large most commonly uses.

- WFP - [[West Freeport]]
- NFP - [[North Freeport]]
- SFP - [[South Freeport]]
- FP - [[The City of Freeport]]
