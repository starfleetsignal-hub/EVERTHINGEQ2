Everquest2 Logger Links (in short EQ2LL) is a tool that will parse your logs for loot and collection items info and offer you the possiblity of uploading the data to [EQ2LL website](http://www.eq2llinks.com/) where you can make searchs to find which mobs drop the item of your dreams and which zone can harbor those eluding collection items.

Can be runned alone or as plugin for [[Advanced Combat Tracker|ACT]].
