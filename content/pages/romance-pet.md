![A frozen swirl of Romance](images/Romance_%28Pet%29.jpg)
A **romance** is swirling vortex of purple hearts.  It follows its caster around and does nothing: the ideal fluff pet.  It is cast from [[Romance in a Jar]], a reward from the quest [[Sacrificial Trust]] during [[Erollisi Day|Erollisi Day 2009]].

Casting this pet does **NOT** override your Deity pet.
