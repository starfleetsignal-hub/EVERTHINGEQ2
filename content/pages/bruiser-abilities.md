## [[Bruiser]] Abilities

At level 70:

- 1 point of agility gives approx .025% avoidance.
- 4 points of agility gives approx .1 % avoidance.
- 1 point of defense gives approx .15% avoidance.
- 1 point of parry gives approx .17% avoidance.
- 1 Point of deflection gives approx .23% avoidance.
- 1 point of stamina gives 5 Health.
