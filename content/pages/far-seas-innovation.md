This event may occur more than once in a group tradeskill mission given by the Far Seas Trading Company on the Island of Mara. It usually occurs after quite a few successful event counters, and likely several Inspiration events as well. In most cases a rare tradeskill item is deposited invisibly into your inventory, such as [[Battle Warped Wood]], but it is possible if it happens more than once that a deposit will not be made. You get the same sudden burst of progress that you get from a normal Inspiration event.
