**Silent Fist Initiate armor set**

- [[Mantle of the Silent Fist Initiate]]
- [[Gi of the Silent Fist Initiate]]
- [[Armwraps of the Silent Fist Initiate]]
- [[Handwraps of the Silent Fist Initiate]]
- [[Leggings of the Silent Fist Initiate]]
- [[Sandals of the Silent Fist Initiate]]

All pieces in this set have the same appearance and coloring.<br>
There are no bonuses derived from wearing multiple members of this set.

This armor can be worn by only [[Monk]], and is obtained through the armor quest series, [[Selwyn's Errands - Part I|Selwyn's Errands]].
