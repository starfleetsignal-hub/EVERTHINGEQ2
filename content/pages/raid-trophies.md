## Overview

Since the release of [[LU51]], **trophies** drop from several slain Epic x2 or x4 named that can be placed in guild halls and appears as a replica representation of the monster itself. To create a trophy, loot a crate, trophy house item, or head from the named that is killed and purchase or craft a [[Guild hall trophy pedestal]], or craft the trophy.

- NOTE: Many newer raid mobs will drop the trophy instead of a crate, and the pedestal is not needed.
- NOTE: All of the Blood of Luclin and Reign of Shadows raid mobs drop the head, then need a recipe book from the tradeskill merchant in either Recuso Tor or Echo Caverns to CRAFT the trophy. Requires a shadowed material, 200 of a common material, and a guild hall trophy pedestal, for BoL trophies, and 50 common shadowed material, 150 rare shadowed material, and 500 common material, or for Xerkizh The Creator its 50 rare shadowed material, 150 common shadowed material, and 150 rare shadowed material for RoS. NO PEDESTAL is required for RoS trophies
- NOTE: All of the Blood of Luclin raid bosses drop the head ONLY in the challenge version of the zone. Cannot be gotten in the normal version.
- NOTE: Unlike BoL, RoS heads drop from normal versions.
- NOTE: The Masquerades of Divinity trophies are not a drop, but a purchase from the merchant at the respective God's camp. They cost 50 Minted Celebration Token and require that you have killed the instanced version of the Avatar. They are also restricted to the limited time event.
