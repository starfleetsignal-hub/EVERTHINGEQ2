## Overview

Matter Mallea are a mechanic introduced in [[Rage of Cthurath]] that allows player to make a one-way conversion of certain pieces of in-game designated Level 135 Heroic, Untold, and Raid armors (Resolve 630+) [[Cthurath Armor (Rage of Cthurath)#Unbound-ed version|equipment pieces]] into another form.

For the Cthurath expansion, that is primarily to allow players to convert excess blue stat Critical Bonus armors into much-needed additional orange stat Critical Bonus Overcap "Unbound" version of the same piece.

Specially designated armor pieces from the following slots may currently be converted:

- Head
- Shoulders
- Chest
- Forearms
- Hands
- Legs
- Feet
- Cloak
- Charm
- Ear
- Necklace
- Ring
- Wrist
- Waist
  - *...(Basically, everything except Primary/Secondary/Ranged)*

## How to obtain

Matter Mallea are currently crafted by players, from uncommon recipes that drop from the Cthurath-era [[Invaders of The Unknown]] Public Quests scattered across lvl 110+ Norrath zones (and The Unknown)

- Each Matter Mallea can only be used on the matching slot it is crafted for.  You cannot, for example, use a Necklace Mallea to convert a pair of boots.
- The following recipes books are currently in-game and contain the recipe to craft the associated slot Mallea:

  - [[Matter Mallea - Pauldrons (Recipe Book)]]
  - [[Matter Mallea - Breastplate (Recipe Book)]]

  - [[Matter Mallea - Legguards (Recipe Book)]]

## Fair warning

- Be aware that both the recipe AND the resulting item are **Heirloom-only**, so players will need to have their own access to both a crafter character to make them AND a character high enough crafting/adventure level to survive the forays into the Public Quest instances necessary to obtain the recipes drops themselves. (This can be a single character for all of the above or multiple characters spread across a single account.)
- The matter malleas **CAN be commissioned** from a crafter with recipe.
  - each Matter Mallea crafted will require 25 x [[Adventurer's Void Shard]] that should be supplied by the owner of gear, plus some common current harvests and fuels.

## How to use

**VERY IMPORTANT** - before using Matter Mallea right click on piece of gear you want to convert and remove all adornments in case you want to use them  on new piece. You are creating a brand new item so all adornments will be lost if you don't remove them.<br>
Once you have the proper Matter Mallea in inventory click on it and select "Use" to bring up **ALL** eligible equipment it can be used on. Make sure you are truly selecting gear you want to convert, the process is not reversible. Click the gear piece from list to convert it.
