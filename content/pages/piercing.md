**Piercing** is a [[Skills|skill]] used by all [[Piercing Weapons]] and is of particular importance to [[Mage|Mages]], [[Scout|Scouts]], [[Crusader|Crusaders]] and [[Shaman|Shamans]]. This skill demonstrates how well you use daggers and spears and affects the chance that you will be successful when landing a blow with a weapon that uses the Piercing skill.

The Piercing weapon you can use depends on your adventure level and class, but not on your Piercing skill:

- Piercing (Equipment) - Equipment that modifies the **Piercing** skill.
- Piercing Skill Adornments - Adornments that modifies the **Piercing** skill.
- [[Piercing Weapons]] - Weapons that depend upon mastery of the **Piercing** skill.
