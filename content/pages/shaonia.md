**Shaonia** was the daughter of [[Antonius Bayle IV]] and [[Lady Shae]] of Felwithe.

The firstborn child (and therefore rightful heir to the throne of Qeynos) of Antonius the Fourth, Shaonia was removed to [[The Isle of Erollisi]] by her mother, Lady Shae, in secret. It was only later in her life that Shaonia was reunited with her father, who left Qeynos to be with her and her mother.

Given Shaonia's known direct lineage to the [[Bayle]] line, and [[Antonia Bayle]] having grown up on Erollisi and having proved to the Council that she was indeed a Bayle by blood, Shaonia can reasonably be assumed to be a direct ancestor of [[Antonia Bayle]].
