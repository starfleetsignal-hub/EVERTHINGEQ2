## Effects

- Applies ***Haze of Quellious*** on termination. Lasts for 2 minutes.
  - Decreases Haste of target by 50.0
  - Decreases DPS of target by 25.0
  - Slows target by 87.5%
- Mesmerizes target
- Dispelled when target takes damage
- Epic targets gain an immunity to Mesmerize effects of 4 minutes 30 seconds and duration is reduced to 30.0 seconds.

## Quote

"Tranquility, once realized, can be taught, even to those who are unwilling." - note affixed to the collar of [[Giang Yin]]
