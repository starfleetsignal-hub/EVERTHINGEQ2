## Effects

- Summons a pet forest spirit.
- On a hostile spell hit this spell may cast ***Nature's Touch*** on caster. Lasts for 6.0 seconds. Triggers about 1.0 times per minute.
  - Heals group members.
  - Heals group members every 3 seconds.
- When a combat art is used this spell has a 5% chance to cast ***Nature's Touch*** on caster. Lasts for 6.0 seconds. This effect normalizes based off a three second triggering event.
  - Heals group members.
  - Heals group members every 3 seconds.
