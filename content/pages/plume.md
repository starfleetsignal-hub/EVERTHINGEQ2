## Intro

In June of 2023, with the start of Summer Jubilee, a new inventory slot called Event Slot was added to character window. It is a space to equip Plumes, a new type of gear earned exclusively from holiday and live events. When in group, the bonuses given by your equipped plume will be amplified, depending on  the number of players in group or raid who also have a plume equipped. The power gained by your Plume is based on the tier of your Plume, so even those who have not unlocked the same tier will contribute to the amplification effect.

Summer Jubilee introduced 3 types of plumes, each with 3 tiers.

- Ability Doublecast boost : Striking Plume of...
- Crit Bonus boost: Critical Plume of...
- Max Health Percentage boost: Robust Plume of...

Complete the achievement [[A Jubilee for You and Me]] by completing the solo dungeon called Triad of Elements to unlock the ability to purchase your first set of plumes, as well as a recipe that will allow you to craft some lower tier versions of the plumes to share with your friends. Triad of Elements [Solo] is only available on servers which have unlocked The Shadow Odyssey and can be accessed through a celestial aperture portal located in the Moors of Ykesha.

On TLE servers a Plume recipe can be purchased with [[Perennial Plumage]] currency obtained by participating in Summer Jubilee events.
