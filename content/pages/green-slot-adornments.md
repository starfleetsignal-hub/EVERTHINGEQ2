## Notes

Green Adornments were first introduced to EQ2 in the [[Chains of Eternity]] expansion.  Like other types of adornments, green adornments are added to equipment that has a slot of the same color; at the present time, green slots are found only on Neck and Finger slot equipment.

Green adornments can be obtained in a variety of ways. Recipes for some green adornments can be obtained from [[Tradeskill Apprentices]]. In the [[Tears of Veeshan]] areas, both the raw ingredients and recipes for green runes (also called stoneshards) can drop from ToV solo, group or raid content. In [[Terrors of Thalumbra]], recipes for green adorns must be purchased from the Deepforge Founders faction merchant, while the primary raw ingredients are obtained from ToT solo, group or raid content.

Some quests reward green adornments, and sometimes a green adornment itself may drop in chests from solo, heroic, or raid content.

## Slots

Green adornments can be used on Neck and Finger slots only

## Where to get

Green adornments can be obtained

- from quest rewards
  - [[Nebulous Reality Anchor]] from [[Shore Defense]]
  - [[Dreary Scuttler Chitin Shard]] from [[Unkempt Desires]]

- crafted (adorner recipes) in 4 qualities (Soul Traps)
  - Common (doesnt require dropped astral material)
  - Crude (requires [[Crude astral material]] )
  - Refined (requires [[Refined astral material]] )
  - Prime (requires [[Prime astral material]] )

- dropped from chests
  - solo/heroic
  - raid
    - [[Will of the Shiverback King]] ([[Baroddas (Epic)|Baroddas]])
