**Ministration** is the [[Skills|skill]] used by all Spells that Heal, whether Self, Friend or Pet. This skill is of particular importance to [[Priest|Priests]].

"This is how well you use spells that heal and cure." This skill affects the power cost of spells that use this skill.

- Ministration (Equipment) - Equipment that modifies the **Ministration** skill.
- Ministration Skill Adornments - Adornments that modifies the **Ministration** skill.
- Ministration Spells - Spells that depend upon mastery of the **Ministration** skill.
- [[Ministration (Spell)|Ministration]] - Tier 1 (level 1) Inquisitor spell.
