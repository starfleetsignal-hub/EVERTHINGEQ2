**Blessed Brace of Piracy** is the name of two separate armor pieces.  Both are rewards for the quest [[Printing Pressganger]].  Presumably, this was a design oversight.

- [[Blessed Brace of Piracy (Chain)]] - Shaman armor
- [[Blessed Brace of Piracy (Leather)]] - Druid armor
