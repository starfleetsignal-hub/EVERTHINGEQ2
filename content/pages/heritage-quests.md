An extended quest which traditionally rewards an item reminiscent of the original EverQuest.  Similar quests which reward items not associated with the original Everquest are called Signature Quests.

After completing 5 Heritage Quests, a character is eligible for "[[The Treasure Hunter]]".  After 10, "[[The Lore Seeker]];" after 20, "[[The Relic Keeper]];" and after 30, "[[The Artifact Raider]]."  When the requirements are met, these titles are available at your character's primary library/Mage Guild Tower in his/her home city.

See the [[Heritage Quests Timeline]] for more information.
