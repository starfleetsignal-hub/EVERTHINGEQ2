![Pyre Starte (part of effect)](images/Effect_PyreStarter.jpg)
Effects:

- Inflicts heat damage on target
- Inflicts heat damage on target every 2.4 seconds.
- Inflicts additional heat damage on target every tick.
- Will kill target und up to 6 other within 10m of target if left uncured.
