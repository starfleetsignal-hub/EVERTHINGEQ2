**Forest Scion's Purified armor set**

- [[Forest Scion's Purified Cap]]
- [[Forest Scion's Purified Shoulder Pads]]
- [[Forest Scion's Purified Tunic]]
- [[Forest Scion's Purified Bracers]]
- [[Forest Scion's Purified Gloves]]
- [[Forest Scion's Purified Pants]]
- [[Forest Scion's Purified Moccasins]]

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

This is the [[Destiny of Velious|DoV]] Armor Set (Tier 1) for [[Warden|Wardens]] that can be obtained via the [[Primal Velium Shard Mission System]].

These pieces are available for vendor purchase from [[Rotuk]] at {{waypoint 1358, -540, 503}} designated as the <Armorsmith> in [[Great Divide]] at [[Thurgadin Harbor]].
They can also be created by crafters.

Note that the [[Forest Scion's Purified Tunic]] only drops from [[Grand Restorer Lingwal]] in [[Hold of Rime: The Fortress Spire]] via [[Thickened Crystalline Hauberk]].
