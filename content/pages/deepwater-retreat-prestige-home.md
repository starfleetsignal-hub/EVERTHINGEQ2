**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** Only with the purchase of Collectors Edition or Premium Edition of [[Chaos Descending]] expansion
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** large, multi-level zone with ramps and waterfalls
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** flying mounts work

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- You must get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- It has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- You can get a [[House Portal|portal]] to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]. The portal allows quick travel between each location.
- You can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button.

**You can easily tour this by doing the following:**

1. Click on the EQ2 button in the bottom left corner of your screen
1. Chose [[Housing & Leaderboards]] from the list
1. Click on the Buy Houses tab
1. Locate the house you want to look at on the list and you can click the Tour button to see an empty version.

## About This House

![](images/Deepwater-fixture.png)
Deepwater Retreat (Prestige Home) is a water-themed Prestige Home. It looks as though it is within a bubble, deep within the sea. Waterfalls pour from level to level and sea-themed details like coral and the shadow of swimming sharks can be seen in the distance.

It has a multi-level ramp system, with large, flat areas for building or adding home decor on each "floor," which are really open platforms and regions within, like the pools. Players can travel to each floor by clicking on the pale blue, spinning portal fixture (seen on the right).

### Break Out

Using a [[Bag of Teleportation Pads|bag of teleportation pads]], you can break out of the normal limits of this home.

Though this house doesn't have a traditional in/outside boundary, a few locations have been noted to break out of the common limits of this house.

See the [[Housing Breakout Guide]] for details.

### Building Blocks

Those who purchased the Collector's or Premium version of the [[Chaos Descending]] expansion can also [[Claim|/claim]] the [[Deepwater Retreat Recipe Book]]. The book has [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]] (and tiles, etc.) that match the style of this home. Most notably this building block set includes animated waterfall parts. Most notably, the blocks include waterfall parts.
