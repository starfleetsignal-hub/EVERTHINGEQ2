**Wooly armor set**

- [[Wooly Shoulderpads]]
- [[Wooly Bracers]]
- [[Wooly Boots]]

---

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

This armor may be worn only by: [[Bruiser|Bruisers]], [[Fury|Furies]], [[Monk|Monks]], [[Warden|Wardens]].

[[Bad'Ushra]] in [[Moonfield Hamlet]] will exchange [[Mark of Manaar|Marks of Manaar]] for Legendary armor. The head, chest, hands, and legs slots are expected to be filled with instance drops and are offered only as appearance items so characters may obtain a consistent look.

This armor is purchased as follows:

| Slot | Cost | Zone |
|---|---|---|
| Shoulders | 75 [[Mark of Manaar\|Mark of Manaars]] | [[Moonfield Hamlet]] |
| Forearms | 50 [[Mark of Manaar\|Mark of Manaars]] | [[Moonfield Hamlet]] |
| Feet | 50 [[Mark of Manaar\|Mark of Manaars]] | [[Moonfield Hamlet]] |
