Overview of the legendary [[The Shadow Odyssey]] shard armor sets acquired by the [[Void Shard Mission System]]. The cost of each piece, in [[Void Shard|Void Shards]], is detailed in the table below.

For tier 3 TSO armor sets dropped in Ward of Elements, a raid x2 instance, see [[TSO Fabled Shard Armor Sets]]. For fabled TSO armor sets dropped in raid x4 instances, see [[TSO Fabled Sets]].
