Professor Cogglesworth is a gnome sage of some renown. In the course of his dealings with adventurers, he has compiled a journal detailing some of the encounters these brave souls have had with persons and creatures of great power. While the professor has gone to great lengths to compile accurate information, it should be noted that certain entries may not contain all pertinent information necessary when facing these exceptional entities. Consider yourselves warned.

The figures described in this compendium are said to possess great wealth that is worth the risks associated with confronting them. Generally speaking, only weapons of exceptional quality will have any effect against such formidable foes.
