This refers to a non-combat secondary instance that's *couched* within the non-combat known as the [[Rogues Gallery]]. The entry point looks like the [[Magic Door to the Guild Hall]]. The door first enter the [[Rogues Gallery]]. The Holiday Mercenary Gallery door can be found at {{waypoint 5, -25, 36}}

## Mercenaries

*See the individual mercenary page for details or [[Mercenaries]] page for rarity, status point value, etc.*

### Cookie Mercenaries (2015)

These all have the tag, "Cookie Mercenary" and may be available when the annual [[Frostfell]] event is live. They were initially added in 2015.

- [[Molasses Gingerbread Archon]]
- [[Sulky Gingerdread Archon]]
- [[The Ninjabread Man]]
- [[Delicious Gingerbread Archon]]
- [[Sugared Gingerbread Archon]]
