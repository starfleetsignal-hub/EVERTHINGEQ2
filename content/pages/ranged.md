**Ranged** is a [[Skills|skill]] used by all [[Ranged Weapons]] and is of particular importance to [[Scout|Scouts]] and [[Fighter|Fighters]]. The ranged skill demonstrates how well you use bows and thrown weapons and affects the chance that you will be successful when landing a blow with a weapon that uses the Ranged skill.

The Ranged weapon you can use depends on your adventure level and class, but not on your Ranged skill:

- Ranged (Equipment) - Equipment that modifies the **Ranged** skill.
- [[Ranged Weapons]] - Weapons that depend upon mastery of the **Ranged** skill.
