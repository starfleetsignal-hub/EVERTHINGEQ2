This recipe allows an [[Alchemist]] to refine several lower level dusts into a higher one.

- **Class:** [[Alchemist]]
- **Recipe Level:** 70
- **Workstation:** [[Chemistry Table]]

## Ingredients

- 3 [[Scintillating Dust|Scintillating Dusts]]
- 5 [[Smoldering candle|smoldering candles]]

## Yields

- 1 [[Scintillating Dust]]
- 1 [[Smoldering Dust]]
