Upgrading **Accessories** ([[Boreas Signet Ring|Boreas Ring]], [[Boreas Penannular Cuff|Boreas Cuff]] / [[Corona Signet Ring|Corona Ring]], [[Corona Penannular Cuff|Corona Cuff]] / [[Aurora Signet Ring|Aurora Ring]], [[Aurora Penannular Cuff|Aurora Cuff]]) works with the use of [[Forgotten Badge - Rings]] and [[Forgotten Badge - Wrists]].

There are three Stages of Upgrading the Accessoires:

- **Stage One**: Combine one Forgotten Badge with either [[Boreas Signet Ring]] or [[Boreas Penannular Cuff]] to recieve the 1x Upgraded Version:
  - [[Gamma Corona Signet Ring]]
  - [[Gamma Boreas Signet Ring]]
  - [[Gamma Aurora Signet Ring]]
  - [[Gamma Corona Penannular Cuff]]
  - [[Gamma Boreas Penannular Cuff]]
  - [[Gamma Aurora Penannular Cuff]]
- **Stage two**: Combine 2 of the Forgotten Badges to recieve [[Remembered Badge - Wrists]] and [[Remembered Badge - Rings]] and add these to your **Gamma Upgraded Accessory** (From Stage One) to recieve the 2x Upgraded Version:
  - [[Beta Corona Signet Ring]]
  - [[Beta Boreas Signet Ring]]
  - [[Beta Aurora Signet Ring]]
  - [[Beta Corona Penannular Cuff]]
  - [[Beta Boreas Penannular Cuff]]
  - [[Beta Aurora Penannular Cuff]]
- **Stage three**: Combine 2 of the Remembered Badges (So 2 Forgotten Badges each) to recieve [[Lustrous Badge - Wrists]] and [[Lustrous Badge - Rings]] and add these to your **Beta Upgraded Accessory** (From Stage Two) to recieve the 3x (and final) Upgraded Version:
  - [[Alpha Corona Signet Ring]]
  - [[Alpha Boreas Signet Ring]]
  - [[Alpha Aurora Signet Ring]]
  - [[Alpha Corona Penannular Cuff]]
  - [[Alpha Boreas Penannular Cuff]]
  - [[Alpha Aurora Penannular Cuff]]
