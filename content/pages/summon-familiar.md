## Familiar Types

- **Animated Tome:** Increases Damage Spell Crit Chance of caster by 2.3% (see [[Summon Animated Tome]])
- **Drake:** Decrease Reuse Speed of caster by 2.0% (see [[Summon Drake]])
- **Gargoyle:** Decrease Hate Gain of caster by 4.0% (see [[Summon Gargoyle]])
