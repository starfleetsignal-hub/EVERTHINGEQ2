In real-world application loam are particular forms of soil. In EQ2 it is a crafting material gathered by mining rocks in various zones. They are particularly useful in alchemy.

- All materials have a [[Tier|tier]] (T1, T2, etc.), which primarily describes a level range in relation to both the zones they are found in while [[Gathering|gathering]] them and their use in [[Tradeskilling|tradeskilling]].

### Tradeskill Uses

- Alchemist - Used in Fighter Combat Art Distillations, [[Potions]] and [[Poisons]]  (All Tiers)
- Jeweler - Used in Scout Runes (all Tiers)
- Provisioner - Used in a few odd Drink recipes.
- [[Tinkering|Tinkerer]] - Used in nearly all Tinkering recipes.

## Rares

![Rare Loams](images/Sprite_loam_rare.png)

### Harvestable Names

See the [[Harvesting Timeline]] for a full list of zones that have these materials.

- [[Solidified loam]] (T1 1-9 experts)

- [[Malleable loam]] (T3 20-29 experts)
- [[Ductile loam]] (T4 30-39 experts)
- [[Fused loam]] (T5 40-49 experts)
- [[Alkali loam]] (T6 50-59 experts)
- [[Spongy loam]] (T7 60-69 experts)
- [[Silicate loam]] (T8 70-79 experts)

Note: There is no rare loam for tier 9. T9 Scout and Priest combat arts use the [[Gem#Rares|rare gem]] [[Ulteran diamond]] and the [[Soft Metal#Rares|rare soft metal]] [[Kaborite cluster]] will be used for Mage and Fighter.

Note: There is no rare loam for tier 10. T10 Scout and Priest combat arts use the [[Gem#Rares|rare gem]] [[Black star sapphire]] and the [[Soft Metal#Rares|rare soft metal]] [[Osmium cluster]] will be used for Mage and Fighter.

Note: There is no rare loam for tier 11. T11 Scout and Priest combat arts use the [[Gem#Rares|rare gem]] [[Sapphire bixbite]] and the [[Soft Metal#Rares|rare soft metal]] [[Lumium cluster]] will be used for Mage and Fighter.

### Tradeskill Uses

- [[Alchemist]] - Used in [[Fighter]] Expert Combat Art Distillations (All Tiers)
- [[Jeweler]] - Used in [[Scout]] Expert Runes (All Tiers)
- [[Tinkering|Tinkerer]] - Used in some dropped recipes.
