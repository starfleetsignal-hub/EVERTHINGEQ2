This residence's floorplan is [[A three room house, version 2 (Freeport)]]. This is the same as [[A three room house, version 1 (Freeport)]] except that it is flipped, putting the front door and the back bedroom to the East.

**Features:**

- Large front room with vaulted ceiling and exposed beams.
  - The beams can be floored (using carpets) to create a loft.
- Spacious bedroom to the rear.
- Upstairs open-air patio.
