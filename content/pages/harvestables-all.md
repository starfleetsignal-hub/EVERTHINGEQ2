The table below details all the harvestable items currently in game. For more information about harvesting itself, including information on how to harvest, see [[Harvesting]].

## Notes

1. With [[LU37]] all imbuing items became just "materials". For instance, Glowing Flowers are now [[Glowing material]]. The icon for imbuing materials are the per-tier icons that were used for the Stones. Stones in your inventory have been renamed. Older Flowers, Scales, and Teeth have not been renamed, but they can still be used.
1. [[Destiny of Velious]] zones ([[Great Divide]], [[Eastern Wastes]]) have special nodes with harvests used in various quests and recipes in that expansion:
   - icy rock - gives [[Pristine Flame Opal]], [[Pristine Onyx]], [[Pristine Nephrite]]
   - frosty den - gives [[Molkor Hide]], [[Manticore Mane]], [[Brontotherium Meat]]
   - chillblossom bloom - gives [[Chillblossom]] (Eastern Wastes only)
   - school of iceclad swordfish - gives [[Iceclad Swordfish]]
   - school of iceclad cutlassfish - gives [[Iceclad Cutlassfish]]
   - frozen parsnip plant - gives [[Frostbitten parsnip]] (Great Divide only)
1. The following harvests are missing from this table: [[Shadebark]], [[Shadowstone ore]], [[Shadeweave root]], [[Shadow quartz]], [[Leathery shadeleaf]], [[Luclinite nodule]]. They come from a special harvest node [[Shadeweave bud]]. It is a rare spawn only in the [[Twark]] and [[Warslik's Wood]] areas of [[Obulus Frontier]] (Kunark Ascending expansion). Crafting recipes for this expansion use these rares along with common harvests and fuels from previous expansion [[Terrors of Thalumbra]].
