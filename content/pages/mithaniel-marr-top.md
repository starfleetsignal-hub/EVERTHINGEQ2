Mithaniel Marr, the Truthbringer, governs the Plane of Valor. Mithaniel is the ultimate [[Paladin|paladin]] who wears a full suit of shining silver plate armor and wields a glowing longsword. Mithaniel Marr, who owes his existence to [[Tarew Marr]], leader of the Triumvirate of Water, is allied with [[Erollisi Marr]] and [[Karana]]. He counts [[Innoruuk]], [[Cazic-Thule]], and [[Bertoxxulous]] among his enemies. He has a friendly rivalry with [[Solusek Ro]]. [[Http://eqlive.station.sony.com/library/deities.jsp Source]]
