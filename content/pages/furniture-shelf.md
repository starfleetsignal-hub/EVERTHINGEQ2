![A furniture shelf, placed in a house. No matter what tier of rare wood used to make them, they all look the same when placed.](images/Furniture_Shelf_Sales_Display_Placed.png)
If you are not familiar with furniture shelves, you may want to read about the general topic of [[Sales Display|sales displays]] that can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]].

- All furniture shelves are Master Crafted (made from a rare material) by those who take up the [[Carpenter]] tradeskill.
- They hold a variety of [[Furniture]] items (also primarily) made by Carpenters.
