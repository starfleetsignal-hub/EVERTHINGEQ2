This is a Quest NPC who can be found in different locations near any of the 6 cities.

- He starts [[Tin Metal Protection]] for [[Bristlebane Day]] at the end of which he'll reveal himself to be [[The Grand Prankster]].
- You *_should_* go to an area that suits your alignment. For example, evil-aligned characters should choose [[The Commonlands|the Commonlands]], [[Darklight Wood]] or [[Timorous Deep]]; this is because the quest will send you into a city you may not be able to enter without being killed by the city guards. The same holds true for good-aligned characters.  **If** one can avoid the Epic or more aggressive guards, one may complete this quest in any city of their choosing.
  - [[Blat Berisen (Antonica)]] - in the [[Qeynos Plains]] in [[Antonica]], just outside the [[South Qeynos]]-Gate {{waypoint 279.82, -14.37, 384.80}}.
  - [[Blat Berisen (Commonlands)]] - in [[The Commonlands|the Commonlands]] just outside the [[West Freeport]]-Gate {{waypoint -1104.53, -59.94, 54.98}}.
  - [[Blat Berisen (Darklight Wood)]] - in the [[Indigo Draw]] in [[Darklight Wood]], just outside the [[K'Rais Tunnel]] leading into [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]] {{waypoint -622.36, -55.32, -64.91}}.
  - [[Blat Berisen (Greater Faydark)]] - at the [[Green Knoll]] in [[Greater Faydark]], just down the ramp that leads into [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 554.97, 44.58, 45.02}}.
  - [[Blat Berisen (Timorous Deep)]] - in [[Timorous Deep]] on the path leading down to [[Gorowyn Beach]] {{waypoint 2336.36, 20.34, 947.97}}.
- There is no Blat Berisen in [[Frostfang Sea]], even though [[The Grand Prankster]] can be found just north of [[Erollis Dock]]. {{waypoint -402.77, 24.76, 147.01}}. However there is [[Garple Gigglegibber]] {{waypoint -374.9, 30.67, 163.91}} that offers an additional quest called [[Blat Bound]] to seek out [[Blat Berisen (Commonlands)|Blat Berisen]] in [[The Commonlands|the Commonlands]] (evil aligned) or [[Blat Berisen (Antonica)|Blat Berisen]] in [[Antonica]] (good aligned).
