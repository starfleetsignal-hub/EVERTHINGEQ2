**Tunare's Wrath armor set**

- [[Crystal Gift]]
- [[Ring of Tunarian Grace]]

(2) +15 int +8 disruption

- Applies Raw Power
  - On a successful hostile spell this spell has a chance to cast **Raw Power** on caster.  Lasts for 30.0 seconds.  This effect will trigger an average of 1.8 times per minute.
    - Increases the damage of group members next spell or combat art by 10%

This armor may be worn only by: [[Mage|Mages]].
