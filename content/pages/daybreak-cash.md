## Daybreak Cash

Daybreak Cash (DBC) is the premium virtual currency used in the Marketplace across all games operated by [[Daybreak Games]], including *EverQuest II*. It can only be acquired using real-world money and is used to purchase cosmetic items, account services, expansions, and other premium content.

The current approximate conversion rate is: **$1 USD = 100 DBC**

Conversion rates for other currencies may vary depending on their exchange rate to the US dollar. Purchases are typically made through the Daybreak website, in-game Marketplace, or supported platforms.

## Usage

DBC is used for:

- Marketplace items such as mounts, illusions, housing decorations, and appearance gear
- Account services like name changes, server transfers, and race/class unlocks
- Expansion packs and adventure packs
- Promotional bundles and limited-time offers

DBC is account-bound and cannot be traded or gifted to other players.
