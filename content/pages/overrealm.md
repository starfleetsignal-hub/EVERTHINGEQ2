The **Overrealm** collectively refers to the three outdoor zones of the [[Kingdom of Sky]]:

1. [[Tenebrous Tangle]]
1. [[The Barren Sky]]
1. [[The Bonemire]]

To access the Overrealm lands, one may quickly and easy collect a [[Spire Teleportation Shard]] and use the [[Ulteran Spires]].
