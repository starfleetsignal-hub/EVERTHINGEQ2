## Effects

- Decreases Haste of targets in Area of Effect.
- Decreases Mitigation of targets in Area of Effect vs elemental damage.
- Roots targets in Area of Effect.
- Stifles targets in Area of Effect.
- Epic targets gain an immunity to Root and Stifle effects of 3 minutes and duration is reduced to 20.0 seconds.
- Resistibility: 56% Harder
  - Increases against targets higher than level 89.
