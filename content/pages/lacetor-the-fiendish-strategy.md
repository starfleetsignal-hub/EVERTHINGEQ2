## 24 Flags on the Shore

Before pulling Lacetor, clear out all of the trash on the eastern shore of the lake. This spot is larger, flatter, and more open than any of the crevices near Lacetor, so it makes a better fighting spot.

The prime difficulty with this fight is the curse **Left in the Cold**. If even one player is within 7 meters of the target, the curse cannot be cured, and the curse has a very short cure window. At first, only one player at a time will have this curse, but it grows to two at a time and later three at a time. If two cursed players are within 7 meters of each other, the raid is doomed. Both players have an ally within 7 meters, preventing any cures, and neither player can move away from the other. The setup therefore involves designating an assigned spot for every member of the raid, with sufficient distance that no two players are within 7 meters of each other.

Designate a spot to be the center of your chosen arena and mark it will a flag. This is where the main tank stands throughout the entire fight. Plant six more flags around the center, with each flag 8 meters from the main tank and 8 meters from each other, forming a hexagon. These six flags are where the six top melee players stand. (Only 7 meters is strictly necessary, but 8 adds a bit of buffer without adding too much distance.) Plant a second ring of flags around the melee ring, again with each flag 8 meters from the melee ring and 8 meters from each other. This second ring is where most of the raid will stand (apologies to the scouts and fighters in this ring who are out of melee range and rather useless). Place a few more flags outside the second ring to accommodate the most-ranged of mages, again 8 meters away from every other flag.

Once all the flags are placed, evenly distribute the healers throughout the second ring. Healers should be spread out away from each other to increase the range of cures. Healers are unable to cure themselves of the curse, so another healer needs to be in range. Loosely divide the field into four quadrants, one for each raid group. Each group should stick together to increase the utility of heals, wards, buffs, etc.

With all raid members standing in their designated place, the main tank can run up the hill to pull Lacetor. The tank then runs back to their designated flag in the center of the field. It doesn't matter much where Lacetor stands; it is much more important that the tank is still 8 meters away from the inner melee ring. The bonestorm adds don't hit hard, and they don't move far from their initial spawn, so there's no reason for the main tank to attack them. All other players should focus on killing the bonestorm adds and only damage Lacetor when all adds are dead.

When multiple curses land at the same time, it is possible that there aren't enough healers within range. Once the curse has landed, it is safe for healers to leave their flags, cure the curse, and then run back to their flags. Between curses, it is safe for melee dps to be in close to Lacetor or one of his bonestorms, so long as they run back to their flags before the curse lands.

It's a slow and painful fight, but this strategy guarantees that Left in the Cold never lands on two stacked players, which would be an incurable raid wipe.
