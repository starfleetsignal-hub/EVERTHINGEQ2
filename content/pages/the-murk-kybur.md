[[The Murk Kybur]]
Zone: Forgotten Pools
Health Points: 8.3 Million
The Murk Kybur is a rare named spawn in the forgotten pools. He appears after Aeri'ondi the Infectious is killed at {{waypoint -17, -23, -114}} He has one ability of note Corrosive Murk. This spell deals ~20000 noxious damage instantly and every second to the entire zone. To avoid this damage you need to be within 5 meters of him. It has a decent casting time so you have time to move in to avoid the damage. Moving in also cures the detriment if you don't make it in time.He casts this ability about once every 30 seconds. He needs to be tanked on the ground as the water puts a bad noxious detriment on you as well.
