**Makoto Shoda** is a teacher of the martial arts and a follower of [[Quellious]]. He has appeared in game in several locations.

- Prior to the [[Return of the Gods Timeline|Return of the Gods]] he was known as [[Sensei Makoto Shoda]] and resided in [[The Elddar Grove]].
- As the Gods were returning, [[Sensei Makoto Shoda]] spent a brief period of time on the docks of [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] but soon disappeared and did not return to his dojo. Apparently he spent considerable time searching for enlightenment and returned with [[LU42|game update 42]].
- He is now known as [[Master Makoto Shoda (Fens)|Master Makoto Shoda]] and starts [[Monk|monks]] on their [[Monk Epic Weapon Timeline|epic path]], while wandering the beach in the [[Fens of Nathsar]], not far from [[Omen's Call]].
- With the current unrest regarding the fate of [[Erollisi Marr]], [[Master Makoto Shoda (Ant)|Master Makoto Shoda]] has also been seen spending time in [[Antonica]] near the druid ring.
- [[Master Makoto Shoda (EF)|Master Makoto Shoda]] plays an integral part in the ritual to open the gate to the [[Shard of Love]] on the North end of the [[Everfrost]] docks.
