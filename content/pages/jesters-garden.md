![Gather materials used to make Bristlebane day crafted items from this bush.](images/Jesters_garden.jpg)
This bush only appears as part of the [[Bristlebane Day]] holiday event. It appears as a light green bush shrouded in a sparkling red particle effect.

## Locations

- [[Antonica]]
- [[The Commonlands]]
- [[Darklight Wood]]
- [[Timorous Deep]]
- [[Greater Faydark]]
- In the outdoor areas of [[New Halas]]

## Materials

There are five resources harvested from this special node:

- [[Ettin-Headed Coin]]
- [[Ogre Tutu]]
- [[Highelf Funny Bone|High Elf Funny Bone]]
- [[Erudite Hairpiece]]
- [[Halfling Stilts]]

The resources are used in to make items found in recipe books you can purchase from [[A Bristlebane Day merchant|A Bristlebane Day merchants]].

## Notes

A similar-looking bush appears **only** on April 1st, but it has a purple particle effect.

- See the [[Jester's Superior Garden]] page for details.
