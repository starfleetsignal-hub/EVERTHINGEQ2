Shiny is a colloquial (slang) term used to describe the [[Collection Quests Timeline|collection]] items one finds on the ground while out adventuring, exploring, or harvesting.

## About

The term refers to the the shiny glow around the "?" marks scattered randomly on the ground that players can target to pick the collectible up.

- Players use their [[Collecting]] skill to pick the items up, so it may take a few tries to pick them up on a new character with low skills.
- Because it's colloquial term, there is debate about whether the plural is shinys or shinies, though the latter follows the common pluralization of words ending in Y in American Enlgish.
- Shinies come in many colors and types and in some cases, you must complete collections or specific achievements to see those with unique colors.
  - Most [[Live Events]] have purple shinies.
