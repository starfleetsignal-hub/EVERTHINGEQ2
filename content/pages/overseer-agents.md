With the [[Overseer Feature]] introduced to Everquest II different agents can be acquired to run [[All Overseer Quests|Overseer Quests]].

Leveling was introduced with [[Update:114]]. The current maximum level is 80 *[As of Mar 2026]*. <br>
Each new season unlocks for a player's entire account at levels 11, 21, 31, etc...
