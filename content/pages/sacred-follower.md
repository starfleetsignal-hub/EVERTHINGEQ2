### Notes

- This spell is granted to the priest from [[Litany of Combat]] in Priest Shadows AA line, along with [[Battle Prowess]] and [[Casting Expertise]]. Only one of these spells may be active at a time.

- This spell appears to have no effect on a Channeler's Construct.
