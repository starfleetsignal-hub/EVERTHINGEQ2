## Introduction

This page describes the various housing customizations available for [[Qeynos Housing|housing in Qeynos]]. Ones marked "default" are those initially displayed. Default options are *not* considered "purchased" and much be paid for if you wish to switch back to them from another option.

Purchase costs are listed under each option in the tables below. Options must be purchased individually on a per-"room" basis (note that some "rooms" actually encompass multiple rooms, especially for flooring). All options, once purchased, cost 10s to set.
