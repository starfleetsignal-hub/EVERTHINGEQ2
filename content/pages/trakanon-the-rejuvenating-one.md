In order for the zone-in in Jarsath Wastes under the red temple to be active, one must have completed or be on the mythical Epic 2.0 step to kill Trakanon.

First fail condition: Not having one of each ascension class.

The script that follows is byzantine: Toxic life prevents instant-death from Basilisk Bite for its duration if cast after he first emotes unleashing it and before he does, for which one should have the panda adornment that extends toxic life's duration and the tome of the ascended that reduces its cast time so that at least one thaumaturgist in each group is needed to keep it constantly up by casting in rotation; and this is only the second fail condition of which many more follow: adds, stoneskin, ports, tank-swaps, contagious curses, mana-drain death, equipment damage, stifles, certain ascension spells wiping the toxic life buff, specific classes called to do specific actions at specific times... But you are four MML (mentored max level) toons: Burn the hell out of him before the script kicks in. Collect your achievement, trophy, mythical epic, and go home.
