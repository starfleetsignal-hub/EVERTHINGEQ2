**"Goblin Firepit"** are "quest-specific" crafting stations, usable by all [[Tradeskill]] classes for crafting items. They were introduced with the [[Kunark Ascending]] expansion.  There are different appearances for the Goblin Firepits, however they function the same.  Free-standing Goblin Firepits can be found in the following locations:
