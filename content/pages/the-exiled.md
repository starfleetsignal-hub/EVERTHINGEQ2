*the Exiled* is a suffix title received after completing any [[Betrayal|betrayal]] quest of [[Qeynos]], [[Freeport]], [[Kelethin]], or [[New Halas]].

- When you have it, it's always shown and cannot be replaced by another. You also gain access to the [[Haven]].
- You will lose it when you become a citizen of any city (except [[Maj'Dul]])
