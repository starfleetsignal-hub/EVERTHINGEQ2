**Ceremonial Armor of Marr armor set**

- [[Ceremonial Boots of Marr]]
- [[Ceremonial Bracers of Marr]]
- [[Ceremonial Epaulets of Marr]]
- [[Ceremonial Gauntlets of Marr]]
- [[Ceremonial Leggings of Marr]]
- [[Ceremonial Tunic of Marr]]

Sold by [[Quartermaster of the Hand]] (Merchant) in [[Nektulos Forest]]<br>
Plate appearance armor that can be worn by all classes.<br>
Must complete the Bloodlines Chronicle quest [[Symbols of the Damned]] in order to purchase.<br>
NOTE: I was able to purchase the armor after having completed ONLY "The Missing Scouts" (still on "Luring Out the Evil", in fact). (Aegin, Antonia Bayle, 6/17/13)

There are no bonuses derived from wearing multiple members of this set.

Note that this is the armor set for Good characters doing the Bloodline Chronicles quests. Evil characters should refer to the [[Ceremonial Armor of the Brethren (Armor Set)|Ceremonial Armor of the Brethren]].
