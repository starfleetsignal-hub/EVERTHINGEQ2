The language of the [[Froglok|Frogloks]] within the RoK.
Can be learned by doing the quest [[Finicky Frogloks]] from [[Jerome]] in [[Fens of Nathsar]].

The ability to speak **Froak** is needed for the following quests:

- [[The Will of Kyrtoxxulous]]
- [[A Scheduled Meeting]]
- [[Removing the Darkness From Within...]]
- [[Beastlord Epic Weapon Timeline]]
- [[The White Dragonscale Cloak]]
