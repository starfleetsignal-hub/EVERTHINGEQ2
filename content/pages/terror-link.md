- Resistibility: 78% Harder

## Effects

- Decreases Mitigation of target vs arcane damage.
- On a hostile spell cast this spell cast ***Terror*** on caster. Lasts for 6 seconds.
  - Makes target afraid.
  - Roots caster.
  - 5% chance to dispel when target takes damage.
  - Grants a total of 5 triggers of the spell.
- Does not affect Epic targets.
