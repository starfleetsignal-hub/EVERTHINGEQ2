**Dark seepages** first appeared during [[LU46]] as part of the lead-in to The Shadow Odyssey, and they disappeared when that expansion went live.  Years later, a few dark seepages were encountered during a specific quest in the Vesspyr Isles.

"Dark seepage" is the correct name - unlike many other SOE monsters their name does not contain 'a'.
