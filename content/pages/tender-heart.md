Tender Heart is a prefix title you can earn when you complete the [[Hearts Across Faydwer]] [[Achievements|achievement]] during [[Erollisi Day]].

If you earn this title you can set it by pressing **C** to open the Character sheet. Click on the Options tab and use the drop-down arrows to choose a title.
