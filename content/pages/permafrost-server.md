## Server Merger Info

**Upcoming Server Mergers**

- 11 November 2015: [[Nagafen (Server)|Nagafen]], [[Freeport (Server)|Freeport]], and [[Permafrost (Server)|Permafrost]] servers merged creating [[Skyfire (Server)|Skyfire]]. The merging servers were taken down for the merger on 11 November 2015 at 7am PST and the new [[Skyfire (Server)|Skyfire]] server came on-line at 07:40am PST on 12 November 2015 .  Where duplicate Guild and Character names occurred, names were maintained as with past server mergers where the older Guilds and Characters kept their names and the younger Guilds and Characters had a 'z' added to the end of their names.
