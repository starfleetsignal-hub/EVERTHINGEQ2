## Effects

- Inflicts disease damage on target.
- Inflicts disease damage on target every 12 seconds.
- Heals caster for a small percentage of max health.
- Heals caster for a small percentage of max health every 12 seconds.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Increases power of caster instantly and every 12 seconds.
- Decreases the target's effectiveness of worn armor vs noxious damage by 15%.
