## Norrathian Days of the Week

Norrath utilizes a ten-day week:

1. **Feastday** (roughly equivalent to Earth's Sunday, a day of rest)
1. **Darkday** (roughly equivalent to Earth's Monday, the first day of the work week)
1. **Burnday** (the day trash is burned)
1. **Soulday** (a day of remembrance, many lives were lost throughout the Shattering, and many survivors' descendants feel honor-bound to weekly remembrance)
1. **Windday** (fresh winds and weighed anchors fill the bustling port cities with the raw stuff of industry, traditionally caravanned inland on this day)
1. **Steelday** (a day of full-blown productivity among craftsmen, the forges are lit early on this day)
1. **Spryday** (teamsters rush to deliver newly produced wares to merchantmen and the docks today, general considered the busiest day of the week)
1. **Moorday** (dockworkers load cargo onto ships destined for far-away lands)
1. **Brewday** (with much of the week's work done, attentions are turned to the local traders and merchants)
1. **Mirthday** (a final day of work, then a night of fun!)

## Seasons

The three seasons of Norrath are imbued with a more agrarian understanding of life.

- **Decay** (cold, damp, and windy - little of what's exposed to the weather survives)
- **Growth** (a time of renewal, Ro seems to find his heat as Norrath blossoms)
- **Harvest** (the peak of the warm season, when foodstuffs are gathered and stored away against the Decay)

## Norrathian Months

1. **Deepice** (Decay) - The doldrums of winter, all the world seems encased in cold and shadow.
1. **Grayeven** (Decay) - Dawn breaks on the wintry scene, life stirs and finds its legs.
1. **Stargazing** (Growth) - The clarity of the cold night offers an unhindered glance deep into the heavens, as the warming days make the out-of-doors habitable again.
1. **Weeping** (Growth) - Rain and wind sweep across the lands, a tempestuous mix for mariners, from which new life finally springs on land.
1. **Blossoming** (Growth) -Norrath in full flower, the air warm and sweet at last.
1. **Oceansfull** (Growth) - The tides are most favorable for mariners as what's left of Luclin reaches its perigee.
1. **Scorchedsky** (Harvest) -The heat feels closest as it ripens the fruits and grains of the fields.
1. **Warmstill** (Harvest) - The days are still very long and the climate yet favorable for returning home from far-away adventures.
1. **Busheldown** (Harvest) - When the last fruits of the growing season are gathered in and stored away.
1. **Lastleaf** (Harvest) - Feasts abound as Norrathians celebrate the bounty of the far-distant gods' favor.
1. **Firstchill** (Decay) - Winter signals its coming as the days grow long.
1. **Deadening** (Decay) - Life settles indoors and deep in-ground yet again.
