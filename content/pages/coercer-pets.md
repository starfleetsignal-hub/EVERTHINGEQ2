*See Coercer Pets for a simple, alphabetic list of pets.*

As seen in the official forums, this is the coercer page much talked about [here](http://forums.station.sony.com/eq2/posts/list.m?&topic_id=169754). This is just a start; please help by entering your favorite pets, especially on the lower tiers, as information on these areas is the most useful for new players. The coercer pet information here is ordered alphabetically by zone so it's easier to find what you want.

If you are not an Erudite with Aura Sense, consider getting a pair of Thaumatoscopic Goggles at level 65 (these are not usable below level 65). These will enable you to see which mobs are casters etc. when in an unfamiliar zone. (The goggles are currently described as head slot leather armor, but that's incorrect, the item is actually cloth armor).

Note that this page strictly applies to pets created with [[Charm]].  Pets which are charmed retain their original script and roster of abilities, whereas pets created by [[Possess Essence]] may or may not resemble the live mob.  For more on [[Possess Essence]] and the abilities your pet will have, see [[Coercer Pet Strategies]].

See Also: [[Coercer Pet Strategies]] and strategies listed in [the SOE Forums](http://forums.station.sony.com/eq2/forums/show.m?forum_id=75).
