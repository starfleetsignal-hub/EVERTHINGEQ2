### Description:

Miragul's Immeasurable Menagerie is a zone that is progressed through endless rounds.  Each round is broken into a smaller series of rooms.  The rooms follow the pattern: 2 normal rooms, boss, 2 normal rooms, boss, 2 normal rooms, boss, end of the round boss.  Each boss drops a shiny nearby.  The boss at the end of the round drops an ornate or exquisite chest.

### Location:

The entrance is in **Splendor Sky Aerie** on a platform above **Nemok Time-Touched** the **Crono Dungeon Merchant**.  (407.02 309.64 938.03) It is a portal that is mouse-over labeled "*Depart for the Celestial Conveyance*."

### Level Restriction:

You must be **Adventure Level** **130** to enter.

## Rooms

### Boss Rooms

1. Excavated Zavith'loa
   1. Smeltga Falkyr - Her main ability is a severe knockback.  It is frontal only, so when she says "Heat Wave!" just go behind her.
1. Midst of Fordel
   1. Skyggind Shalkyr - Her ability is Enveloping Shadows, a debuff.  Do a heroic opportunity to dispell.
1. Shell of the Dark Fathoms
   1. Oseaniska Salkyr - Her main ability is Washout, a power drain and stun, Do a heroic opportunity to dispell.
1. Torden Asunder
   1. Frosta Kalkyr
      1. She will summon adds in the icy ring around the center platform.  You need to stand next to the frozen add to melt them before they break out.  If you don't do this, then they will become another add you have to deal with.
1. Nexus Encore
   1. Divinhild Malkyr
      1. She creates new constructs (mobs) in the center portal.  You need to jump through the center portal through the light and above the hole to either clear one that hasn't fully formed yet or destroy one that is.
1. Chunk of Decay
   1. Virsyk Dalkyr
      1. She will shout "Plague!" or something similar and one of the four posts in the far corners of the room will activate.  A ... Plague Carrier will spawn at the post and start coming towards Virsyk.  You must agro it and kill it very close to the post it came from.  Killing it away from the post will just allow another one to spawn.  Letting it get to Virsyk will spawn a different creature.

## Power Ups

Mobs in these zones can drop power ups.  Clicking on them will provide a different buff for 3 minutes based on the color

1. Red - Crux of Cylor - Immune to daze, melee, flurry, doublecast, and potency buff
1. Purple - Prions of Phylax - Damage Shield
1. Green - Exeter of Eltier - Heal
1. Yellow - Orates of Osienne - Damage reduction if Scout, immune to root and mez
1. Blue - Axiom of Alkantar - Power increase, caster can't be interrupted, immune to fear
