[[The Shattering|Pre-shattering]], **Highpass Hold** (sometimes referred to as "High Hold Pass")  served as the main route of travel between the cities of [[Qeynos]] and [[Freeport]]. Plagued by the presence of both [[Orc|orcs]] and [[Gnoll|gnolls]] at the former north and south entrances, the pass managed to attract travelers from all areas of [[Norrath]] due to the rumors of knowledge about such legendary items as the [[Polished Granite Tomahawk]] and the [[Shiny Brass Halberd]], which have since been re-discovered. In fact, many a [[Rogue]] sought out the denizens of the pass in search of information about the famed weapon, [[Fatestealer]], the location of which has been lost to time.
