Shattered Heart is a prefix title earned by completing the [[Love on the Shattered Seas]] [[Achievements|achievement]] during [[Erollisi Day]].

If you earn this title you can set it by pressing **C** to open the character sheet. Click the Options tab and use the drop-down arrows to choose a title.
