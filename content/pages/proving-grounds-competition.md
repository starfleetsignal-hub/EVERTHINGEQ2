The Proving Grounds are a cross-server hub introduced with [[LU103]] on Tuesday May 9, 2017 (formerly the [[Battlegrounds]] PvP arena). The Proving Grounds feature timed PVE competitions as well as solo and duo instances.

- note: The Proving Grounds were removed in 2019** https://forums.eq2wire.com/threads/proving-grounds-gone.230715/

<br>

## Entering the Proving Grounds

Entry portals to the [[Proving Grounds Lobby]] are located in the major cities, with merchants and quest givers being located near the portals:

- [[Freeport]]: In North Freeport across from the T2 guildhall {{waypoint -48.57, -20.05, -263.24}}.
- [[Frostfang Sea]]: On the docks near [[New Halas]] {{waypoint -350.31, 30.31, 228.56}}. -- *This is the easiest portal to access, because it's right by the bell*.
- [[Gorowyn]]: On the [[Timorous Deep]] docks {{waypoint 2404.16, 18.77, 1392.43}}.
- [[Greater Faydark]]: In the city of [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 309.77, 109.04, 143.77}}.
- [[Neriak]]: Near the gate to [[Timorous Deep]] {{waypoint 43.16, 17.49, 11.77}}.
- [[Qeynos]]: In [[Qeynos Harbor]] near the [[The Clock of Ak'Anon|Clock of Ak'Anon]] {{waypoint 749.20, -21.30, -62.90}}.
