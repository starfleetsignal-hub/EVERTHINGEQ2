The language of the Avian, Vultak, and Blacktalon creatures within the [[Kingdom of Sky]]. Can be learned by doing the quest [[Words of a Feather]] from [[Turk Whooyip]] in [[The Barren Sky]] on [[The Whisperwind Isle|Whisperwind Isle]] at {{waypoint -592, -48, -439}}.

The ability to speak Volant is needed for the following quests:

- [63] [[Expedition to the Barren Sky]]
- [70] [[Culture of the Aviaks]]
- [70] [[Might of the Aviaks]]
- [65] [[Seeking a Hiding Feather]]
- [63] [[Toys to Play With]]
- [65] [[A Papango on the Wing]]
- [67] [[Another Aerial Attempt]]
