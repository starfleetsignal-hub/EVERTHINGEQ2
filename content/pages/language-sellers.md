All player languages (except for PvP specific ones) can be bought in [[Qeynos Province District]], [[The City of Freeport]], [[Kelethin]], [[Gorowyn]] or [[Neriak, City of Hate]]. The sellers in Qeynos and Freeport do not sell all the language primers while the [[Kelethin]] seller sells all (except Gorwish) and the [[Gorowyn]] and [[Frostfang Sea]] sellers sell all the languages. These merchants are carry the tag, *Linguist*.

## Notes

It is a good idea just to go to [[Eredykil Zum'tok]] in [[Gorowyn]] (she's actually on the docks, so avoiding guards if you have bad faction is very easy) considering she sells all of the basic language primers.
