![A pet gazer](images/Pet_gazer.jpg)
A pet gazer has seven eyes, one mouth, and a desire to follow you everywhere.  The ability to summon this fluff pet is the reward from the collection quest, '[[Abomination Teeth]].'

This pet does **not** override your Deity pet, but may override other fluff pets.

## Things this pet says

- "Grargh."
- "Grr.  Argh."
- "Har har har.  Ok, so... joke's over.  Where's my food?"
- "I've got my EYE on you, buddy."
- "Say, buddy, got a spare eye?"
