Although many languages can be purchased from merchants in the districts of Qeynos and Freeport, some languages can only be obtained as the reward from specific quests. Some quests in the game require you to speak a particular language in order to start them or to complete them.

[[LU37]] made significant changes to the way Language Quests work. See the [[Auto update]] page for details.
