These are the notes associated with the patch from [Friday, June 7, 2013](https://www.everquest2.com/news/server-downtime-notice-06-07-2013-eq2).

### GENERAL

Pets with auto assist on should correctly follow the player's lead.

### MERCENARIES

Mercs will now only display their first name in raid / group UIs.

### POPULATION / ZONE PROGRESSION

*Sanctum of Fear*

- The zone bosses in Sanctum of Fear once again drop loot and give exp.

*Thundering Steppes*

- "The Cove of Decay: A Treaty for Treasure" instance should now be accessible again without getting kicked out after zoning in.

*Charasis: Maiden's Chamber*

- The gate opened after slaying Sandstorm will now stay open even if you leave the zone and return at a later time.

*Siren's Grotto: Dissension [Raid]*

- Gen’ra fight: Aquatic Curses happen less frequently.
- Warcouncil members have less health.
- Urchins should stick more closely to the player they are stuck on.

### ITEMS

The ground mount version of the Cevern Vastspan can now have another ground appearance mount used with it.

### TRADESKILLS

- Tradeskill Apprentice components from Siren's Grotto bosses now drop 100% of the time, up from 25%.
- Tradeskill Apprentice components bought from Charsin Andolar have been reduced to 25 Mysterious Blue Coins + 12,500 Status, down from 100 Mysterious Blue Coins and 50,000 Status.

### QUESTS

- Mysterious Stones - Spearhead now has the proper quest icon when ready to complete the quest.
- The Heart of Qeynos - Each time you give a Palemoon Antidote away, one is now removed one from your inventory.
- The Eagle and the Hawk - Field Marshall Vishra now updates this quest, even if the player is on any of his subsequent quests.
- Long Live the Queen - Darcnir now only requires one stein of Kazon's Rum Barrel Red Ale.
- Speaking to Threeko on the quest An Ugly Bounty now updates the whole group.
- Proof in Writing - Players that leave The Concordium Vault before defeating the tomecreepers can now respawn them by reexamining the bookcase
