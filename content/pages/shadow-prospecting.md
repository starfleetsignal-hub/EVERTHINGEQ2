## Overview

Shadow Prospecting is a special kind of resource harvesting done exclusively on the moon of Luclin and it's accompanying zones (*See [[Blood of Luclin]] (BoL) and [[Reign of Shadows]] (RoS) expansion info for list of all zones on the moon*)

Normal teir 12 harvest nodes in Luclin zones are occasionally replaced with the 'shadowed' version of the same node and result in 'shadowed' versions of the Luclin resources you'd get from the normal nodes. Both normal harvests AND rare harvests could have shadowed versions)

It was originally added as a way to artificially create a tier of harvested materials above 'rare' that were then used in more elite recipes, yielding more-sought-after items from crafters - but players saw it more as an artificial time-sink that did not add much value to the game versus the time required to actually utilize it and the mechanic was abandoned for any future expansions beyond RoS.

## How to start

The ability to Shadow Prospect was originally awarded to players about [[Piercing the Darkness: Gifts from the Great Beyond Part III|halfway]] through BoL Tradeskill Timeline. Eventually, the [[Shadow Prospecting (Item)|Shadow Prospecting]] training book was added to a vendor and could be purchased by any player of sufficient tradeskill level in their main trade, regardlesss of progress (or lack thereof) in the TS sigline.

Once learned, the skill is slowly raised over time as you harvest more and more shadowed nodes around Luclin, much like any other harvest skill - albeit MUCH more slowly, since the shadowed nodes are so rare, and extremely difficult to find under normal circumstances.

## How to find nodes

The thing that made the mechanic such a time-sink, was that under normal circumstances, players cannot actually 'see' which resource nodes had been replaced with 'shadowed' versions, unless they had special tools in their inventory.  Without those tools, players would normally have been harvesting massive about of seemingly normal resources nodes with only the 'hope' of obtaining those rarer-than-rare shadowed resources. Thankfully, with the introduction of the mechanic, also came the mechanic for 'finding' these rare shadowed nodes more easily.

- [[Shadowed Staff of Luclin]] - While normally invisible to a player's senses, special tools could be utilized to make nodes visible for a short time. The main tool was the [[Shadowed Staff of Luclin]], which was earned as the final reward of the BoL [[Blood of Luclin Crafting Timeline|Tradeskill Signature Timeline]]. The tool has a click effect that allowed harvesters to 'see' Shadowed Nodes for the next 30 minutes, but the staff effect had a 2hr cooldown - this meant that players would activate the staff effect, then spend the next 15-30mins 'looking' around (while still under the effect of the staff) for a nice cluster of Shadowed nodes all close together - they had to look for clusters of shadowed nodes because once they started harvesting the first one, the staff effect would begin an immediate 30second countdown until expiration (regardless of how much time was still remaining), meaning players would have to wait roughtly 2hrs just to 'try again'.  Harvesting that first node meant they had less than half a minute to get the other shadowed nodes around them before the effect expired and they had to wait for the extremely long cooldown.

- [[Glass Orb of Swirling Shadows]] - Ironically, the staff itself requires shadowed components in order to be completed.  This is where the [[Glass Orb of Swirling Shadows]] comes in.  The orb is a rare harvest from normal Luclin resource nodes and has a single charge of the same 30 minute effect the staff has, including the unfortunate 'the effect ends 30 after harvesting a shadowed node' aspect.  So, players had to spend a significant amount harvesting regular nodes for just a *chance* at getting the glass orb - which was LORE on top of that, so you couldn't 'stock up' on them, you had to use it immediately or risk missing another glass orb from a harvest because you were already carrying one - and using those glass orbs to spend the time hunting down the required resources to eventually complete their own staff... to use to collect both shadowed resources and additional glass orbs (since orbs didn't have a cooldown, the staff did)

So get out there and start Propecting... you'll be doing it for awhile. ;-)
