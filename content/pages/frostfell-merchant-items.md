*This page refers to an event that occurs in December. Check your in-game calendar for dates. For more information see the main [[Frostfell]] page.*

## About

The items listed below are sold by **both** [[Giggawat Gigglegibber]] at {{waypoint 139, -9, -1240}} and [[Garsleblat Gigglegibber]] at {{waypoint 125, -3, -1154}}.

- The items sold by both of these merchants is identical and includes the tradeskill recipes, a variety of smaller [[Housing|house]] items for general event-themed decorating (e.g. [[Cheerless Snowwoman]], garland, decorative gift packages), and some snow globes once given out by [[Santa Glug]].
- See [[Frostfell Merchant Items (Gerbi)]] for a *different* selection of items that sell for [[Frozen Token of E'ci]] *only*.
- The keys needed to unlock chests and craft [[Frostfell Crafting Stations]] are **not** sold by these merchants, but only available while in the crafting instance for the quest, [[Frostfell Decoration Committee: Permafrost!]]
