![Kelethin, City of Trees, Home of the Feir'Dal and Fae](images/Kelethin.png)
Set amidst the emerald canopy of the grandest forest on all of [[Norrath]], the tree city of Kelethin has blossomed into a thriving center of activity. Kelethin was formerly the city of the wood elves, but became the city of the Fae in the aftermath of the War of Fay. Hidden high in the trees of [[Greater Faydark]], Kelethin is built into the trunks of enormous trees, which provide a strong foundation for a network of platforms, bridges, acorn-shaped buildings and even acorn-shaped elevators! As shadows linger over [[New Tunaria|Felwithe]] and the [[Clan Crushbone (Faction)|Crushbone orcs]] continue their quest for dominance, the lighthearted Fae flutter amongst the platforms of Kelethin to promote peace and harmony with nature.

Kelethin was released in the Echoes of Faydwer expansion.

## Geography

Kelethin is built directly into the trees of [[Greater Faydark]] and as such, is not a separate zone. Players can seamlessly travel between the platforms of the city to the leafy earth of Greater Faydark without the need of a loading screen.

- Without a leaping or flying mount, players can access Kelethin via acorn elevators, or by the ramp at {{waypoint 475, 71, 112}}.
- The [[Globe of Swift Travel]] located at {{waypoint 340, 109, 124}}.

### Teleportation Pads

![The travel map for the city teleportation system in Kelethin. Shown here, leaving the teleport pad at Jysolin's Pub.](images/Kelethin_Teleport_Pad_Map.png)
Players can either use the ramps to move around the various regions in this city or utilize the local, city teleportation pads to quickly reach the several platforms.

Below is the name of the teleport pads (in bold) as they appear on the map, along with pad locations. Some points of interest are beneath each pad name:<br>

- [[Kelethin First Regional Bank]] {{waypoint 301, 109, 109}}.
  - Close to this pad: the bank and a mender NPC. A broker is up the ramp to the northwest and [[Linguist|linguist]] is up the ramp to the northeast.
- [[Old Kelethin Acorn Lift]] {{waypoint 46, 96, 23}}
  - This is the most common point of entry for new characters, as it is close to a fairly safe road from [[The Nursery (POI)|the Nursery]]. A broker NPC is just up the ramp in the north, under a leaf-awning.
- [[Near Residential Aerie]]{{waypoint 447, 92, 169}}
  - On this platform is the [[Citizenship Registrar]], a merchant that sells house pets, and [[Tinkering|tinkering trainers]].
- [[Far Residential Aerie]] {{waypoint 440, 108, 449}}
  - Close to the larger player housing and not too far from [[Silver Leaf Inn]].
- [[Jysolin's Pub]] {{waypoint 119, 112, 398}}
  - Near the acorn with the [[Advancement Counsel]] and [[Order of Arcane]] NPCs.

## Politics

Characters of good or neutral alignment are welcome here. The merchants will not deal with those of evil alignment (or exiles), however the guards will not attack, except for the ones around the [[Fae Royal Court]].
