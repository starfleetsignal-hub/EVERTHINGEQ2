## Description

The daily adorning task is an infinitely repeatable quest that you may carry out once every 18 (real world) hours. The quest offers you the opportunity to increase your [[Adorning]] skill for merely the cost of the [[Fuel]]. All the other required materials are provided to you free of charge.

[[Londiar Inygad]] also offers a [[Daily Tinkering Task]] and a [[Daily Transmuting Task]] now.

## How To Start The Quest

![Londiar Inygad looks the same in all the cities.](images/Londiar_Inygad_%28New_Halas%29.png)
To begin the daily adorning task, speak to [[Londiar Inygad]] who is located in any of the major cities throughout Norrath. (He must be a doppleganger of some sort!)

- [[Freeport]]  {{waypoint 23.8, -12.5, 93}}
- [[Gorowyn]] {{waypoint 2790, 121, 1239}}
- [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 457, 91, 332}}
- [[Neriak]] {{waypoint -184, -3, 275}}
- [[New Halas]] {{waypoint -392, 176, -157}}

The daily adorning task may also be obtained from [[Dercin Marrbrand]] in

- [[Myrist, the Great Library]] {{waypoint 651, 411, 17}}

## The Daily Tasks

The quest you receive varies depending upon your skill level for adorning and is not based on your adventure or tradeskill level.

Each task requires you to create a version of a "[[Faded Adornment of Guarding (Greater)]]" [[Adornment]]. You are provided with the [[Infusions]], [[Fragments]], and [[Powders]] required to make the adornment as well as a temporary recipe book that you must scribe each time you start the quest. The recipe also disappears from your knowledge once the daily task is completed.

You are provided with enough materials to perform 11 combines even though the quest only requires 10 combines for completion. So make sure to do all 11 combines for maximum skill ups!

**The quest you receive is based on your skill level:**

- [[Flickering Adornments for the Guard]] - Adorning Skill 25 and Lower
- [[Glowing Adornments for the Guard]] - Adorning Skill 25 to 75
- [[Sparkling Adornments for the Guard]] - Adorning Skill 75 to 125
- [[Glimmering Adornments for the Guard]] - Adorning Skill 125 to 175
- [[Luminous Adornments for the Guard]] - Adorning Skill 175 to 225
- [[Lambent Adornments for the Guard]] - Adorning Skill 225 to 275
- [[Scintillating Adornments for the Guard]] - Adorning Skill 275 to 325
- [[Smoldering Adornments for the Guard]] - Adorning Skill 325 to 375
- [[Ethereal Adornments for the Guard]] - Adorning Skill 425 and greater

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience. Amount Received Scales to your Tradeskill Level. See Discussion
- 85s 64c (300 Adorning Skill)
- 1 [[City Token]]
- +300 [[Faction]] with your local Tradeskill Guild.

## Notes

The adorning daily task is an easy way to complete some of the [[Daily Objective]] tasks.
