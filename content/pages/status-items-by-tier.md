## Status Items (Tier 20)

Starting with the [[Planes of Prophecy]] expansion, and each subsequent expansion, new sets of expansion-specific status loot was introduced  They can be obtained from sentient enemies in the overland (as well as instances).
All Tier 20 items have the same level 200 guild-benefit cap.
