The following is a list of possible items players may find while opening giant clamshells during the [[Oceansfull Festival]] [[Live Events|live event]]. These items are *gifts* that are not purchased from a vendor as is common with other live events.

Not all items listed are available each year. The list of items available changes year to year, with some items coming back and some new ones being added.

## Othmir Tribe Blessings buff

- Stand with the othmir group at the celebration sites. You will automatically start dancing. After two repetitions you will receive the **Othmir Tribe Blessings** buff, which changes the loot table for the shells you loot for the next 10 minutes.
- To get a **standard loot table** from shells, **including any new items**, you need to open shells **without** this buff. Cancel it in your Maintained Spells window.
