No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

This armor may be worn only by: [[Berserker|Berserkers]], [[Guardian|Guardians]], [[Paladin|Paladins]], [[Shadowknight|Shadowknights]].

There are two different sets of Marshall's Armor, differing only in appearance.  The gold appearance set is obtained from the Frostfang Sea quests, the red appearance set from the Greater Faydark and Timorous Deep quests.
