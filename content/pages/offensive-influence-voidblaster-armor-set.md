- (2) +10 Int +10 Str
- (3) Applies Scathing Magic.
  - On any successful attack this spell has a chance to cast Scathing Magic on target of attack. This effect will trigger an average of 1.8 times per minute.
    - Inflicts 1170 magic damage on target

## Notes

There are actually several Offensive Influence sets with the same name and effects. Items from a different set will not contribute towards the set bonus in another set. In other words, you will not get a two set bonus for just wearing both a Crusading Bangle and Onslaught Mantle, since they belong in different sets.
