Craft these Improved Barricades using the forge next to Quartermaster Roberta Robbins.<br>
NOTE: If you are crafting these for the [[Feeling Defensive]] quest, you cannot multi-craft them.
<br>
They must be crafted one by one.
<br>
<br>
Items needed for one barricade are:

## Primary Component

- 1 cadmium ore

## Build Components

- 1 leonid root
- 2 larix lumber
- 5 effulgent coal
