**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** see notes about quantities below
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per account (free) **or** per character from the [[Marketplace]]
- **House Style/Theme:** island
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** ground or flying allowed

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Isle of Refuge Prestige Home is an island that is a replica of the (now retired) [[Isle of Refuge]] starting zone.

## Notes

To own this house you need an [[Isle of Refuge Prestige Home Deed of Ownership]], which can be obtained in one of two ways:

- It is an 11 Year Veteran Reward, but is limited to **one** per account.
- Can be purchased from the [[Marketplace]] using Daybreak Cash (real world money) at any time.
  - The second option allows for ownership of multiples of this house per account, but like all other [[Prestige Housing]], it is one (of its type) per character;
- Style
- This house is actually an island that looks like the retired [[Isle of Refuge]] zone, without any NPCs or monsters.
- It comprises most (but not all) of the island as it appeared before the zone was retired with the tower intact, the ship at the dock, and the rustic building in the beach. The tree house is not attached to this zone.
- Those wishing to recreate the old zone for the sake of nostalgia may want to check the [[Marketplace]] for a bundle of NPCs and creatures that match the retired zone.
