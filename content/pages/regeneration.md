Regeneration is a magical effect, found on variety of equipment, that increases In-Combat Health Regeneration Per-Tick of the caster by x, where x is the amount of extra hit points regained each [[Tick]].

This was one of the effects converted to a [[Blue Stats|Blue Stat]] (we express it as <code>hregen</code>)
see In-Combat_Health_Regeneration_(Equipment) for items with this effect.

Note: not all items with this type of effect were converted to blue stats, the few things that had a regen effect part of a bigger effect were not converted.

See Regeneration for items with this effect.

This can also be shortened to "regen" and refer to the numerical value representing how fast your character regains health or power (in-combat or out-of-combat regen numbers both exist, and are viewable on the Stats tab of your Character page).
