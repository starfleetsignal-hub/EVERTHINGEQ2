The **Festival of Unity** is an annual [[Player Events|player-run event]] on the [[Antonia Bayle (Server)|Antonia Bayle]] server.  It was first held in 2007, after a number of [[Roleplay]] storylines that left characters emotionally exhausted.  To remind the valiant defenders of Qeynos what they fought for, the leaders of several good-aligned roleplaying guilds collaborated to hold a Festival to celebrate their unity of cause and of ethos.

Held in late May or early June, the week-long Festival features games, contests, and events like those at a medieval faire&mdash;archery contests, jousting, storytelling&mdash;and many others of a more modern spirit, like the famous Feyiron Chef cooking competition.  Most contests are contested randomly, using the /roll command, to even the playing field; the skill comes in describing the results of the random rolls.
