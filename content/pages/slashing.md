**Slashing** is a [[Skills|skill]] used by all [[Slashing Weapons]]. This skill is of particular importance to [[Scout|Scouts]], [[Crusader|Crusaders]], [[Warrior|Warriors]] and [[Druid|Druids]]. This skill demonstrates how well you use all types of swords and axes and and affects the chance that you will be successful when landing a blow with a weapon that uses the Slashing skill.

The Slashing weapon you can use depends on your adventure level and class, but not on your Slashing skill:

- Slashing (Equipment) - Equipment that modifies the **Slashing** skill.
- Slashing Skill Adornments - Adornments that modifies the **Slashing** skill.
- [[Slashing Weapons]] - Weapons that depend upon mastery of the **Slashing** skill.
