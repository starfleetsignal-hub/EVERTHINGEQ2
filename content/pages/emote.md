**Pre-defined Emotes:**
Anyone can see.
eg. /flee ({Character} tells {target] to run away)(Comes with a voice as well)

**Custom Emotes:**
Only your city fraction can see.
eg. /emote says %t sucks!! ({Character} says {target} sucks!!)
