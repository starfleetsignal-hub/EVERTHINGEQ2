A **hot zone** is a [[Instance|dungeon]] of [[Norrath]] that is selected daily to offer a higher than normal chance of dropping Exquisite [[Chests]]. Hot zones first became active in game on August 28, 2009 on servers in the European Union and on September 5, 2009 (Labor Day weekend) in the United States.

Under normal circumstances there are three different sets of Hot Zones per day determined by a rotation. The current set of three available **Hot Zones** are advertised in the main EverQuest II menu (i.e. not the Character Menu), in the **Zone Info** tab under the **Hot Zone** tab.

#### Access to [[Skyshrine: City of Dracur|Skyshrine]]

To get to Skyshrine you must first travel to the [[Great Divide]] selecting the [[Thurgadin Harbor]] location using a [[Travel in Norrath|Travel Globe]] from any dock, or the [[Fast Travel]] option if available.

Once you land on the Thurgadin dock, there are *two* griffon tamers on this dock; after arriving on the Thurgadin dock, locate the [[Griffin Tower|New Combine Flightmaster]] {{waypoint 1277.85, -540.25, 305.06}} and hail them to travel to the [[The Withered Lands]].

- The first time you enter [[The Withered Lands]] you will **not** have access to travel quickly by hailing the a Combine horse trainer at the [[Mount Station]] {{waypoint -3539.79, -781.54, -62.47}}. You must manually travel this zone and add each Mount Station (the first time) and the journey is loaded with "aggro" both on the ground *and* in the skies.
- If you have already visited the Mount Stations, hail the trainer and select [[Alivan]] {{waypoint -460, -710, 410}} as the station.

When you arrive in [[Alivan|The Village of Alivan]], locate the landing pad of the Dracurion Flight Master {{waypoint -556.58, -685.67, 363.36}} and fly to [[Skyshrine: City of Dracur]].

### Earlier Content

The hot zone appears in the bottom right corner of the welcome screen as you enter the game. You can bring this screen up later by typing <code>/we</code> or <code>/welcome_info</code> on any chat line or by pressing <code>CTRL + L</code> (default key setting) while in game. Also in game, you can enter /df  for dundeon finder or access this screen through  EQII button. The hot zones are on one of the tabs at the bottom of that screen.<br>
