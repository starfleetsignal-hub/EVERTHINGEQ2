## Effects

Increases Wisdom: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 28 / 32

Wisdom improves all of your [[Resistance|resistances]] against spell damage.  Note that [[Wisdom]] also influences a [[Cleric|Clerics]]' maximum [[Power]] pool.
