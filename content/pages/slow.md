In the EQ2 Wiki, **slow** is used to refer to reduction of movement speed (from spells and CAs such as [[Snare]]).

**Slow** is a term that has a varied past within the EQ community. In the original everquest, it was usually used to refer to reduction of attack speed (as opposed to **Haste**, which was an increase of attack speed). In the EQ2 Wiki, this concept is always spelled out as an reduction (or increase) of attack speed.
