## Calculating Effect

The Skill Increase will change based on the Adventure Level of the defiler. However if you check your Shaman Advancement Tree in-game, you can calculate what the rankings will give you with the following formulas:<br>
s = (current rank value - next rank value) / (current rank - next rank)<br>
Skill Increase = (rank*  s)
