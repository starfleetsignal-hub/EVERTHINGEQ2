All housing instances in Maj'Dul display "a Maj'Dul residence" when you enter them, but if you examine the purchase UI window, the true name is revealed.

## For Quests

All of these housing instances are also used extensively in the quests given by the [[A Tears grifter|Tears grifters]] in the [[Maj'Dul Timeline#Court of Tears|Court of Tears quest series]]. The doors to these special instances are different from the normal doors for residencies, and become highlightable only for those that currently have a [[Maj'Dul Timeline#Court of Tears|Court of Tears mission]].

The generic term used in most quests refers to:

- [[A Maj'Dul Residence]] -- The instance used for quests given out by the [[A Tears grifter|Tears grifters]].
