A collection of recipes which can be used to upgrade equipment which drops in [[Agnostic Dungeons|agnostic dungeons]]. These recipes can be purchased from [[Koros Splinterlimb]] in [[The Village of Shin]]. Each book costs 15 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]] and requires +40,000 faction with the Far Seas Supply Division.

To craft an upgraded version of an item, you need the item and a [[Sliver of Arcanna'se]] which is obtained by salvaging other items which drop in agnostic dungeons. The ability to salvage is a tradeskill prestige ability which only available to crafters over level 90, and these are very rare.
