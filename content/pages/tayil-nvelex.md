Tayil is rumored to be the lover of the Overlord, [[Lucan D'Lere]]. She is without doubt his most trusted agent.

**TRIVIA:** She is the [[Dark Elf (Character Race)|Dark Elf]] female seen in the Intro Movie; while on a spying mission in [[Qeynos]], at the side of [[Lucan D'Lere]] in [[Freeport]], and attacking the warrior in the final scene. If the information in the movie is to be trusted, she is a [[Necromancer]].

She appears three times in-game:

- [[Tayil N'Velex (Outpost of the Overlord)]] in [[Outpost of the Overlord]]
- [[Tayil N'Velex (Circle of Elders)]] in [[The Circle of Elders]]
- [[Tayil N'Velex (Tenebrous Tangle)]] in [[Tenebrous Tangle]] in the [[Hidden Refuge]]
- [[Tayil N'Velex (TSO)]] in Lucan D'Lere's house during the quest [[The Shadow Odyssey: Prologue]]
- [[Tayil N'Velex (Tribunal Prison)]] in a cell asking to be let out during the quest [[Elements of Destruction: Visitation Day]]
