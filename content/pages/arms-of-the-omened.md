## Overview

Arms of the Omened, (in addition to [[Armor of the Omened]]) is the colloquial term for a set of [[Resolve]] 90 equipment that drops from the [[Deep Chelsith: Vault of Omens]] [Solo] and [Heroic] dungeons.

- This dungeon instance was introduced with the Sept 6, 2019 "The Vault of Omens" update, as part of the [[Blood of Luclin]] expansion 'prelude event'.
- [Official Announcement (Pre-2023 forums)](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/sharpen-thy-wits-and-prepare-thyselves-mythic-passage-begins-soon.590487/)

Players are more familiar with this event in modern times as part of the Pre-requisite quest to enter Luclin for the first time at Adevnture/Tradeskill level 110.
