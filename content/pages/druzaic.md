This is the language of magic. Some ghosts, golems and elemental NPCs will only speak this language. You can learn this language by completing the quest [[Words of Pure Magic]].

The ability to speak Druzaic is needed for the following quests:

- [[The Lost Legend of Lavastorm]]
- [[Coin Operation: The Sanctum of the Scaleborn]] -  (Quest 3)
- [[From Hands of Stone]] -  (Quest 15)
- [[An Ayonic Journey]] - Troubador epic weapon
- [[Don't Wand-er Off Just Yet...]] - Quest reward is used in [[Miragul's Planar Shard]]
- [[Conjuror Epic Weapon Timeline]]
- [[Diviners and Conquer]] (Part of LU100 questline)
