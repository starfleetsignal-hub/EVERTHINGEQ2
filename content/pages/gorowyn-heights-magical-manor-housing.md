**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** See Notes section below
**Available Location:**

- [[Gorowyn]], in the hallway at 2,700 123 1,125 near broker/banker
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** see About section below
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** none allowed

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- You must get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- It has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- You can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- You can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Gorowyn Heights Magical Manor (Housing) is identical to the large Gorowyn house at [[Timorous Heights]], but because it is a [[Prestige Housing|prestige house]], it has *zero* standard coin or status upkeep.

This house is in a odd location. Unlike the other homes which have doors inside the smaller, upper residential area with the banker, broker, the tradeskill area and other NPCs, the entrance for this home is just outside the upper residential area.

After you use the portal in the lower massive cave (2,507 13 1,217) to travel to the top platform, the door is in the wall, right where the hallway turns to take you into the upper residential area. An image at the top of this page shows the door, with the banker area in the distance.

- This house style is popular because with a [[Bag of Teleportation Pads]], you can [[Housing Breakout Guide|break out]] and build anything using [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]].

## Item Count

*Item counts have changed once already and can change again at any time.*
Unlike all other prestige homes which have a 900 item limit, this mimics the large, [[Standard Housing|standard housing]] for this zone and has the following limits:

- 700 items and 700 [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]].

## Notes

You can not buy this house outright. Instead, you must "loot" a deed (*a tax-free Gorowyn residency license*) from Legends of Norrath (LoN).

- The item, *a tax-free Gorowyn residency license* is looted from Legends of Norrath, Draonbrood - The Anarchs card packs
- You **can not** buy this house from the [[Marketplace]], because it is random loot from Legends of Norrath (LoN).
  - Players with [[Free-To-Play|All Access]] accounts get five free LoN card packs per month (as of 2016).
- Even though you can not buy it outright, this house can be toured by visiting location in Gorowyn (given above)
