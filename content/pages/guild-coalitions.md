The following information is from [Guild Coalitions](https://www.everquest2.com/guides/eq2-guild-coalitions) which got added with the [[Rage of Cthurath]] expansion in 2025.

Let’s start off with a short synopsis of what this system is all about.

Joining a coalition guild is just like joining any other guild. A guild coalition is a personal choice which you decide per character.

Your character will remain in your current guild, and you will also be able to join another guild with that same character. You would be able to set one of the guilds the character is in as the active guild, which would then display that guild tag. That actively set guild is the one that gains status, achievement progress, and such, until the character changes their active guild again.

What you get with Guild Coalitions:

- Normal guild command structure with access rights and permissions.
  - You can toggle the Coalitions UI window with /togglecoalition
  - You can change your active guild or coalition with /set_active_guild "Guild or Coalition Name"
    - Progress for Guild achievements and similar systems is only applied to your active guild or coalition.
  - Most /guild <command> actions are available for coalitions with /coalition <command>
- Access to a guild coalition hall which contains all the same possible amenities as a guild hall.
- Chat channels and who commands recognizing coalitions like current guild chat.
- Mail allowing you to send messages to coalition/coalition officers, like current guild mail.
- Guild coalition banks, like current guild banks, with access rights defined by the same means.
- Limited to 1 coalition per character.

This guide is by no means all-inclusive at this time and will be updated with new information as it becomes available.

edit: The above is not quite correct. You cannot belong to two guilds, exactly. At the location where you can form a guild there is a new NPC to form a coalition. The only requirements to form a coalition is you must be a member of a guild and have the 60 silver fee. You do NOT need a group to form a coalition. You can name the coalition whatever you want within the # of character requirements (minimum 4, I don;t remember maximum) and it can NOT be the same as an existing guild.

You cannot be a member in two guilds, You can be a member in a guild and a coalition. Yes, coalitions function pretty much exactly like a guild, but they are NOT the same.

If there are two guilds, guild A and guild B and you are a member of guild A, you cannot then join guild B. However, you can form a coalition and invite others to join it, and they can join whether or not they also belong to a guild. Also, you could accept an invite into a coallition that someone else formed.

1 guild

1 coalition.

Coalitions are available on Origins and other TLE, but you must own the Rage of Cthurath expansion, even though the expansion is not yet active on Origins. You cannot invite a person to join your coalition unless they own the expansion, and of course you cannot form a coalition without owning the expansion.
