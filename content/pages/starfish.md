The QSS Starfish was a ship of the Qeynos Navy. Surviving crew members disagree on whether the pirate attack on the ship was the result of [[Sarnak]] discoveries or a conspiracy of the Freeport Navy, with [[Iksar]] playing the part of attackers. Although the debate continues, at least more of the crew and passengers survived the attack on the Starfish than the [[Samwell Brighton|sole survivor]] that was rescued (by the Starfish) from the wreckage of the ill-fated [[Leaky Drake]].
