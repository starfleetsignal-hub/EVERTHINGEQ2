desc: The Sullians are a community of fairies that have been driven from their subterranean homelands and now fight for survival within [[Nektulos Forest]]. The now find themselves in a war of resources with a fellow fairy community of Asilian Fairies. The Sullians have a peace treaty with the Asilians that prevents them from taking outright action against them. For this reason, [[Loyalist Erityire]] is constantly seeking the aid of adventures to covertly thwart any Asilian advances in Nektulos. The Sullians also have a presence in northern [[Darklight Wood]] and are led by [[Queen Dyleena]].
