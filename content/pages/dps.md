- abbreviation for **Damage Per Second**, as a standardized measure of the ability to deal mayhem.
- also refers to a [[Roles in a Group or Raid|role in a group or raid]].

## Notes

The in game DPS Stat in the character window is not your actual Damage Per Second but rather a modifier to your DPS. If you want to know your true DPS you will either have to use a parser or do some math by hand.
