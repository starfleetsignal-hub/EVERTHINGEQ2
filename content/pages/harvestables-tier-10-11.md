NOTE: Due to the way recipes were added for levels 96-99 and later for recipes of level 100, the tier system is now easily confused, as there is no cut-and dry pattern. The items here listed at T10 are found in one zone, while T11 is in other (below). Please bear with the regular editors here (or lend a hand) to work this out!

## About T10

When the materials for this range was added the max level was 99 (I think - checking this craziness)

### T10 Ranges

**Artisan Level Range:** 96-100 (used for all crafted items levels 96-105)
**Recommended Minimum Adventurer Level of Harvesting Zones:** ?
Note: the harvesting minimum/trivial scores only effects your chance of getting rares as of 2013
**Minimum Harvesting Skill Required for Rares:** 500
**Trivial Score (Maximum Skill Gain Possible):** 886

### T10 Zones

The following zones are accessible via [[Travel in Norrath|the globe found on major city docks]]:

- [[Tranquil Sea]]
- [[Phantom Sea]]

## About T11

Though the materials say "used to craft level 100-109 items", this zone has few items to harvest and the items are for only one level of recipe book (100).

### T11 Ranges

**Artisan Level Range:** 100+
**Recommended Minimum Adventurer Level of Harvesting Zones:** 95
Note: the harvesting minimum/trivial scores only effects your chance of getting rares as of 2013
**Minimum Harvesting Skill Required for Rares:** ?
**Trivial Score (Maximum Skill Gain Possible):** ?

### T11 Zones

All outdoor zones associated with the [[Terrors of Thalumbra Crafting Timeline]]
