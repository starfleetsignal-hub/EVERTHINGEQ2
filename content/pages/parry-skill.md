**Parry** is a [[Avoidance|avoidance]] [[Skills|skill]] used by all classes. This skill is of particular importance to [[Fighter|Fighters]]. It gives you a chance to ward off a physical attack. This raises your parry roll chance, which has a chance of being a riposte that counterstrikes the attacker.<br>
*In oder to raise the skill you need to get hit by melee weapons.*<br>
*Your chance to parry is based off your parry skill relative to the attackers offensive skill. Then on a successful parry you have base of 20% chance to riposte which is normalized by your weapon delay. [source](http://forums.station.sony.com/eq2/posts/list.m?topic_id=454758&post_id=5070183#5070183)*<br>
*The difference between contested and unontested is that contested is allways based on your skill/Level and the mobs level that is hitting you.*

- contested Parry
  - Parry (Equipment) - Equipment that modifies the **Parry** skill.
  - Parry Skill Adornments - Adornments that modifies the **Parry** skill.
  - *Buff (Spell Type) - some buffs increase or decrease the **Parry** skill too*

- uncontested Parry
  - Uncontested Parry - Equipment, Spells or AA's gives increases your chance to parry a physical attack the same % of times regardless of the attackers offensive skill.
  - Parry % Adornments - Adornments that give uncontested Parry
