**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** [[Luxurious Kromzek Keep Deed of Ownership]] is either (raid) looted *or* purchased with [[Loyalty Point Token|Loyalty Point Tokens]]
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** huge, hall-style
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** ground only

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Luxurious Kromzek Keep is a huge house. The entry is a snowy walkway up into a classic hall. After walking down a long hallway and entering a main room, smaller rooms (which are still huge, mind you!) can be found on each side and in the back.

## Cost and Item Count

*Prices for all Loyalty items is subject to change at any time. Item limits have been increased in the past and can change at any time.*

- Though it is a raid drop, this house can be purchased from [[Noble Taig Rhul]] with 60 [[Loyalty Point Token|Loyalty Point Tokens]]. Those who have an [[Free-To-Play|All Access]] membership earn double points (as of 2016).
- Base Item Limit: 900 and 900 [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]]
