## Important Quest and Merchant Clarifications

![An example of a Fireworks Launcher. These can be various colors.](images/Fireworks-launcher-quest.png)
**This page refers to the same quest which is given in multiple locations during the [[Scorched Sky]] [[Live Events|live event]].**

- These quests replace older quests that were once titled *Summer Fireworks* (e.g. [[Gorowyn Summer Fireworks]]). Instead of buying a sparkler from the Marketplace (as it was in the past), the event NPCs now sell the sparkler.
- To get these quests buy [[Festive Sparkler]] for 10 copper from either [[A Sparkler Merchant]] in an major [[Player City|player city]] or any [[Ember Merchant Assistant]] celebrating in several locations outside of cities.
- [[Ember Merchant Assistant]] merchants are conveniently located near the MOBs that can be killed to earn [[Coin of Ember]] and they will sell older items.
- [[A Sparkler Merchant]] can be found in the cities and sell *limited items* (used to match the limits of the TLE servers).

### List of Quests by Zone

The process for this is pretty straight forward and easy in all zones. Buy the sparkler, hail the [[A Fireworks Coordinator]] to get the quest, click on fireworks around the city (near the quest-giver) until it auto-completes.
