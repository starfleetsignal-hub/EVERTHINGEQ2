| **The Mother of All Wyrms** |

Veeshan is the great crystalline dragon who rules the [[Plane of Sky]]. She needs no allies but considers Brell Serilis an enemy. Veeshan is known as the Mother of All Wyrms and this title includes all of dragonkind such as the drakes and wyverns. When the universe was young, Veeshan traveled throughout the cosmos depositing Her children on worlds She deemed worthy. She would then strike the planet with Her massive claws so that the other deities would know She had laid claim to that world. The continent of Velious on Norrath bears Her mark, known as the Scars of Veeshan.
