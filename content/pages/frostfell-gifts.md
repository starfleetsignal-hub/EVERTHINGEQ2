*For a categorical listing of Frostfell gifts (including images) see Frostfell Gifts.*

Each (player) character on [[Free-To-Play|all accounts types]] can receive 1 gift from [[Santa Glug]] every 18 hours during [[Frostfell]]. (I was able to collect 1 gift on the same character every 5 minutes. I have collected 10 in a day. When Santa Glug visits a major city, he will spawn at some point, and run toward an exit, leave packages in his wake. Typically there will also be an area where multiple packages are dropped in addition to the others left along his trail. The packages despawn after a while but it is at least 30 minutes before they despawn. I was able to collect many packages because I would sit at one and wait until it became clickable despite having received the message that I had already received a present that day and could not carry anymore. This took about 5 minutes between packages. TRUE as of Dec 2020.)  These tend to be house items, food/drink, or festive attire. Most of the items are not NO-TRADE, so they can be traded around including bought and sold on the broker. No trade items can usually be sold to a merchant NPC.
