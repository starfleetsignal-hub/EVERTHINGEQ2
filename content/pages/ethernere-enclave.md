![The Housing & Leaderboards tour preview of this home.](images/Ethernere-enclave.jpg)

**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** Only with the purchase of Collectors Edition or Premium Edition of Tears of Veeshan expansion
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** island
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** ground or flying allowed

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Ethernere Enclave is an island that's identical to the [[Vesspyr Isles]] island, Aeterna Gardens. [[Bag of Teleportation Pads]].

- If you purchased the Collectors or Premium versions of the expansion type /claim. This item is easy to miss, because it is *inside* a reward crate with other Tears of Veeshan expansion items. You must unpack the crate to get the deed!
- If you did not purchase the Collectors or Premium versions of the expansion at the time the content was new, you can no longer buy access to this home.
