This is the language spoken by most Sarnak NPC's and mobs. The language of the Sarnark player race is [[Gorwish]]

Players learn this language by completing the quest [[Bones, Bones, Everywhere Bones]],  which is in a series started by the collection [[A Mysterious Red Tome]].
