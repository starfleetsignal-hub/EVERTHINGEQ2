## Intended Purpose

This list was compiled for two main uses:-

### 1. Quest Purchase

A merchant sells from two locations the Overseer Quests from all past seasons:-

1. In [[Qeynos Province District]] harbour on the raised platform [[Stanley Parnem]] Administrator Agent {{waypoint 972.56, -20.69, 90.64}}.
1. In [[Freeport]] harbour on the dock [[Stanley Parnem]] Administrator Agent {{waypoint -246.67, -56.06, 58.30}}.

Catch is the Overseer Quests from all seasons are thoroughly mixed in together as they are listed in strict alphabetical order. This web page is designed to assist finding the appropriate quests.

## Warnings

The Reward Crate for each quest can be 'Examined'. Select the Reward Crate in the Overseer window's quest list to open the Overseer Reward window. Then Examine the 'Overseer Reward Crate'. Buried in the list of items will be the Overseer Agents that may be rewarded.

When completing an Overseer quest there is a chance of receiving a bonus reward crate. Before accepting any rewards this bonus crate can be examined for a list Overseer Agents just like a standard 'Reward Crate'.

And then there are the catches with rewards and these tables.

- The rate at which Overseer Agents drop (are rewarded) varies so widely that some Oversee Agents are rare.
- The list of Overseer Agents rewards listed in a crate may not be accurate and unlisted Overseer Agents can be rewarded.
- Some Reward Crates do not list any Overseer Agents as possible rewards but they may still drop Overseer Agents.
- To track which Overseer Agents are rewarded by what crates the Type of the rewarded Overseer Agent is added to the table. That is why the table is sparsely populated with this data.
