These merchants were removed in the [May 24, 2022 patch](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/update-notes-may-24-2022.604958/).

<s>The Inheritance Merchant, Fracture, can be found in either Freeport or Qeynos. This merchant sells unlockable [[Heritage Quests Timeline|Heritage Quest (HQ)]] rewards for zero copper. The shop inventory is the same in either city.</s>

- <s>[[Fracture (Freeport)]] at {{waypoint -246, -55, 114}}</s>
- <s>[[Fracture (Qeynos)]] at {{waypoint 900, -25, 59}}</s>

After completing a Heritage Quest on the **Kaladim** (previously Fallen Gate) [[Server Types#Progression (Time-Locked)|Time-Locked Expansion (TLE) server]], you will receive an [[Inheritance Voucher]] which is a permanent bonus that allows **any** character on your account (on **any** server) to purchase the respective heritage item an unlimited number of times.

For example, completing the [[Hadden's Earring]] quest, you will get a voucher to purchase the [[Fishbone Earring]] on any character in the future.

Additionally, updated versions are available at levels 90 and 100.  Some of the slots are modified, such as the Greater Lightstone becoming a charm.
