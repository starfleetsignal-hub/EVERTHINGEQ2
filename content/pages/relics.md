## Overview

Relics are unique items that bear extra effects (or stats) above what the normal item of that item's rarity would have.
Relics are identified by their rarity.  In place of the standard rarity tag, relic items have a "<Rarity>, Relic" tag instead.
![an example of the Relic tag in-game, in this case a 'Mythical, Relic'](images/Voided_Gloves,_Star_Forged_Relic.png)

Currently, they come in several varieties:

- Fabled, Relic
- Mythical, Relic
- Ethereal, Relic
- Celestial, Relic

## Restrictions and Limitations

While characters may possess any number of Relic items in their inventory, a character may only **wear** two Relic items at the same time across all equipment slots, regardless of individual Relic rarity.

## How to obtain

- The majority of Relics in-game are obtained from the loot chests of Named and Boss enemies in [Solo], [Heroic], and [Raid] instances.
- Occasionally, Relics are also rewarded as part of Adventure or Tradeskill 'Signature' questlines.

## Upgrading your Relics

While not all Relics can be upgraded, generally in later Expansions (and/or Game Updates), looted relics of Fabled quality will have a Mythical version it can be upgraded to.  This is usually accomplished by bartering the Fabled relic to a specialized vendor for each expansion, in exchange for the upgraded Mythical version.
