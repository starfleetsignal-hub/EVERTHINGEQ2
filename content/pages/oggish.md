The language of the Ogre. Can be learned by purchasing an [[Oggish Language Primer]] from

- [[Eredykil Zum'tok]], [[Gorowyn]]
- [[Martok K'Fauxx]], [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
- [[Scribe Hilja Saraste]], [[Kelethin]]
- [[Krishana]], [[The City of Freeport]]
- [[Dannon Ramsdell]], [[Frostfang Sea]]
- [[Phyrace J'Despath]], [[Wanderlust Fair]] in [[Darklight Wood]]
- [[Garglari]], [[Kylong Plains]]
