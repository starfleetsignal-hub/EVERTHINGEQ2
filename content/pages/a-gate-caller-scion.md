**Gate Caller scions** are NPCs that act as travel agents for players at the various [[Ulteran Spires]]. Hailing one will place a [[Spire Teleportation Shard]] in your inventory. When the spires activate (every 5 minutes), and if you are in range of the spires, you will be transported to your destination. If you change your mind destroy the [[Spire Teleportation Shard]] or move out of range of the spires.

Gate Caller scions may be found in the following zones:

- [[A Gate Caller scion (Antonica)]]
- [[A Gate Caller scion (Thundering Steppes)]]
- [[A Gate Caller scion (Feerrott)]]
- [[A Gate Caller scion (Commonlands)]]
- [[A Gate Caller scion (Nektulos Forest)]]
- [[A Gate Caller scion (Enchanted Lands)]]
