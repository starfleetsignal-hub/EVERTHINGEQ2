**Find 'em a Find, Catch 'em a Catch!** refers to a quest held during [[Erollisi Day]] in [[Norrath]]. Two versions of this quest exist:

- [[Find 'em a Find, Catch 'em a Catch! (Qeynos)]] - for good-aligned adventurers
- [[Find 'em a Find, Catch 'em a Catch! (Freeport)]] - for evil-aligned adventurers

Exiles can do either version of this quest, but as of Erollisi Day 2015, Qeynos is easier - the guards are lower level and easier to avoid.

The title of this quest is an allusion to the song "Matchmaker" in the musical Fiddler on the Roof, where one of the lead characters sings the line "Matchmaker, Matchmaker, make me a match! *Find me a find! Catch me a catch!*."
