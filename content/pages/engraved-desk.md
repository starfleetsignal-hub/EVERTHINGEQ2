**Engraved desks** are used by [[Sage|Sages]].
You can carry a desk anywhere you go:

- Starting at level 30 [[Portable Desk]]
- Starting at level 60 [[Portable Desk v2.0]]

In addition to each of the traditional tradeskill zones, free-standing engraved desks can be found in the following locations:

### Qeynos

- [[North Qeynos]]: Sneed's Trading Post
- [[South Qeynos]]: Mage Tower

### Freeport

- [[North Freeport]]: the Oracle, Academy of the Arcane Sciences
- [[East Freeport]]: Freeport Observer, Trader's Holida {{waypoint -129, -38, 25}}
- [[West Freeport]]: [[Coalition Crafter's Association (POI)]] (the crafting area, not just the instance) at {{waypoint 26, -12, 86}}.

### Faydwer

- [[Kelethin]]: Kelethin Research Library, Jeweler House (near the <Scribe>), Crafting House (Tunare's Pages)
- [[Steamfont Mountains]]: near the Lingualugian
- [[Loping Plains]]: Village of Somborn [[Philip Glaslow]] across from the [[D'Venhz Rectory]]

### Gorowyn/Timorous Deep

- [[Timorous Deep]]: [[The Wards]] {{waypoint 2736, 124, 1140}}

### Frostfang Sea

- In the crafting area, just beyond the broker in [[New Halas]] .

### Neriak

- [[New Foreign Quarter]]: {{waypoint 120, 20, 71}}, {{waypoint 138, 20, 71}} and {{waypoint 128, 20, 59}}
- [[Neriak Down Under]]: {{waypoint -165, -3, 274}} and at {{waypoint -190, -4, 268}}

### Kunark

- [[Teren's Grasp]]: [[Arcane Refuge]] (first building on your left as you enter the outpost via the ramp)
- [[Fens of Nathsar]]: on Sathir's Span in one of the buildings

### Obol Plains

- [[Cardin Ward]]: {{waypoint -322, 14, -5}}

### Other Zones

- [[Haven]]: [[Ekroosik's Replicators]] {{waypoint -45, -34, -225}} and {{waypoint -54, -33, -221}}
