Allied with [[Karana]], [[Erollisi Marr]] and [[Rodcet Nife]], and enemies with [[Bertoxxulous]], Tunare, the Mother of All rules the [[Plane of Growth]]. Tunare appears as a very attractive older humanoid female wearing flowing robes and a crown of vines and leaves. Tunare is very protective of what she considers to be one of Her finest achievements, the good elf races of [[Norrath]]. As part of an agreement with [[Brell Serilis]] and [[Prexus]] and in response to the arrogance of [[Veeshan]] in claiming Norrath for her own, Tunare created the High Elves and the Wood Elves of Norrath.
