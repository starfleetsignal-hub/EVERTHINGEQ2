**Blackburrow** is a now defunct Everquest II server. On 8 December 2010, residents of Blackburrow endured a downtime of nearly 24 hours in order to merge into the existing [[Everfrost (Server)|Everfrost]] server.

*If you are a resident of this server, please feel free to add your Guild and Player Name to the sections, below!*

See also Blackburrow Guild Heraldry pictures.
