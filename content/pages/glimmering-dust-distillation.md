This recipe allows an [[Alchemist]] to refine several lower level dusts into a higher one.

- **Class:** [[Alchemist]]
- **Recipe Level:** 30
- **Workstation:** [[Chemistry Table]]

## Ingredients

- 3 [[Sparkling Dust|Sparkling Dusts]]
- 4 [[Gel candle|gel candles]]

## Yields

- 1 [[Sparkling Dust]]
- 1 [[Glimmering Dust]]
