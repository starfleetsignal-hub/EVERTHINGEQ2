![](images/Mara-estate-prestige-house.jpg)
**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** deed purchased from [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]] after completing all of the [[Days of Summer]] quests.
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per account (free) **or** per character from the [[Marketplace]]
- **House Style/Theme:** entire zone, with empty buildings
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** ground or flying allowed

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Mara Estate is a is a blank replica of the [[Isle of Mara]].

## Notes

To own this house you need an [[Mara Estate Deed of Ownership]], which can be obtained in one of two ways:

- The quest series from [[Days of Summer]] must only be completed by **one** character per account to unlock this deed and each character on an account can own one.
- Style
- This "house" is a zone with empty buildings identical to those found in [[The Village of Shin]], though without the NPCs
- The style of the buildings is reminiscent of real-world, ancient Chinese architecture.
