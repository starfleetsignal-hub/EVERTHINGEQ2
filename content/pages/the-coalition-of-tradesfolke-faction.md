desc: The Coalition of Tradesfolke is the tradeskill faction within [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]]. As with all factions within Freeport, they only have as much freedom as [[Overlord Lucan D'Lere]] permits, and as such they do not have unlimited trade freedom. They are known for having aggressive trade tactics, such as exclusive partnerships by means of intimidation, as evidenced by a quest series in the [[Demands to the Ravens|Frostfang Sea]]. The Coalition has no official leadership, but is rather an alliance of the most powerful artisans and businessmen within Freeportian territory.
