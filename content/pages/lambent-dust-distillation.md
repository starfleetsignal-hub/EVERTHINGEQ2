This recipe allows an [[Alchemist]] to refine several lower level dusts into a higher one.

- **Class:** [[Alchemist]]
- **Recipe Level:** 50
- **Workstation:** [[Chemistry Table]]

## Ingredients

- 3 [[Luminous Dust|Luminous Dusts]]
- 8 [[Lambent candle|lambent candles]]

## Yields

- 1 [[Luminous Dust]]
- 1 [[Lambent Dust]]
