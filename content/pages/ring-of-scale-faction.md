The Ring of Scale comprises those dragons who wish to directly influence mortal affairs. They have split off from the more conservative Claws of Veeshan, who have no love for the beings they considered lesser than themselves but prefer to live removed from them. Because of their great power, knowledge, and willingness to interfere in worldly affairs, the Ring of Scale has powerful enemies.
