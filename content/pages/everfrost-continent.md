![An image of Everfrost as appears on the world map.](images/Everfrost1.png)
Everfrost is the remnant of the Everfrost Peaks from northern [[Antonica (Continent)|Old Antonica]] that separated from the supercontinent in the events of the [[The Rending|Rending]]. Most of the northlands were lost during the Rending and the further events of the [[The Age of Cataclysms (Lore)|Age of Cataclysms]], with the continent of Everfrost and [[Erollis]] being the only two surviving snow-covered regions. Being a small continent, Everfrost only contains one zone, just called [[Everfrost]].

![An image of Everfrost as appears on the world map, zoomed in.](images/Map_everfrost.jpg)
Many of the same creatures that inhabited Everfrost Peaks remained on the landmass, such as ice giants, valkyrie, and minotaur. However, the barbarians, also native to the north, were forced to flee the land as [[Halas]], their capital and main population site, was totally destroyed when the [[The Shattering]] brought it to ruin. The main theme of the [[Everfrost Timeline|timeline]] within Everfrost is the barbarians reclamation of their lost land.

The great ice giant citadel of [[Permafrost]] also resides On Everfrost. It is home to the Kromise, where they actively defend it with the aid of their goblin armorers, the Icegill. Also found on Everfrost is the valkyrie faction known as the Order of the Blade, who are locked in an eternal struggle again the Frosthorn Minotaur.
