**Ebon Strongbear** was the guildmaster of The Steel Warriors during the reign of [[Antonius Bayle IV]].

Ebon was a master of combat, and trained the young Ruler of Qeynos during his childhood. It was said that Ebon could change into this armor faster than most men could saddle a horse.

The Qeynosian naval vessel [[Q.S.S. Ebon Strongbear]] was named after him.
