**Stormbringer's Reconstructing armor set**

- [[Stormbringer's Reconstructing Cap]]
- [[Stormbringer's Reconstructing Shoulder Pads]]
- [[Stormbringer's Reconstructing Tunic]]
- [[Stormbringer's Reconstructing Bracers]]
- [[Stormbringer's Reconstructing Gloves]]
- [[Stormbringer's Reconstructing Pants]]
- [[Stormbringer's Reconstructing Moccasins]]

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

This is the [[Destiny of Velious|DoV]] Armor Set (Tier 2) for [[Fury|Furies]] that can be obtained via the [[Primal Velium Shard Mission System]].

These pieces are available for vendor purchase from [[Cramnon Spirittalker]] at {{waypoint -685, -340, 2755}} designated as the <Armorsmith> in [[Eastern Wastes]] at [[Ry'Gorr Keep]].
They can also be created by crafters.

Note that the [[Stormbringer's Reconstructing Tunic]] only drops from a mob. It can not be purchased or crafted.
You can create it from a [[Hauberk of Coagulated Primality]] wich drops from [[Idol of Rallos Zek]] in [[The Temple of Rallos Zek]]
