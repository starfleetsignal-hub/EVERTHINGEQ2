The Gather of Bones is a quest that appears twice in game as city task quests from the quest givers for [[The Dismal Rage (Faction)|The Dismal Rage]] at the [[Temple of War]] in [[North Freeport]]:

- [[The Gathering of Bones (Commonlands)]] from [[Convert Y'Ral]] offers kill tasks for [[A tortured soul (The Commonlands)|tortured souls]] in [[The Commonlands]]
- [[The Gathering of Bones (Fallen Gate)]] from [[Occultist Levonicus]] offers kill tasks for [[A blighted patron|blighted patrons]] in [[Fallen Gate]]
