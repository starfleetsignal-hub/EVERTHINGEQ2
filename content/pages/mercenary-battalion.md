The most recent mercenary that you hired which is unlocked to hire anywhere and is at least rank <strike> 5 </strike> 2 grants you a persistent buff. The buff is granted whether or not that mercenary is active or suspended.

In the past, the buff varied based on the archetype of the Mercenary hired - this was simplified after Reign of Shadows. All archetype 'Mercenary Battalion' effects now give similar amounts of the following stats:

- Fervor
- Fervor Overcap
- Crit Bonus Overcap
- Ability Mod
- Max Health %
- Ability Doublecast

Four factors determine the *amount* of each stat that the buff provides:

- The most important factor is the rank of the mercenary. A rank 20 mercenary buff is more than twice the amount of a rank 5 mercenary buff. In fact, a rank 20 Treasured mercenary will outperform a rank 5 Celestial mercenary.

- The second most important factor is which of the following eras (or mercenary 'seasons') the mercenary originates
  - *In ascending order of power:*
  - [[Shattered Lands]]
  - [[Blood of Luclin]]
  - [[Reign of Shadows]] (Includes [[Mercenaries#Season 4 Mercenaries|Mercenary Season 4]])
  - [[Visions of Vetrovia]] (also includes [[Renewal of Ro]] and [[Mercenaries#Season 5 Mercenaries|Mercenary Season 5]])
  - [[Ballads of Zimara]] (Effect listed as "Antiquitas", includes [[Mercenaries#Season 6 Mercenaries|Mercenary Season 6]])
  - [[Scars of Destruction]] (Effect listed as "Zimaran", includes [[Mercenaries#Season 7 Mercenaries|Mercenary Season 7]])
  - Season 8 Mercenaries**  (Effect listed as (*aptly named*) "Season 8".)
      - *As of the release of [[LU129|LU129 "Fear of Eternity"]] (Aug 2025), season 8 mercenaries are currently only available via the Marketplace from the purchased mercenary 'gambling' lootbox [[Season Eight Monsters Mercenary Crate]]. This effect name and mercenary availability may be updated with future content releases.*

A Shattered Lands mercenary provides about half the stats of a Blood of Luclin mercenary  A Blood of Luclin mercenary provides about a third of the stats of a Reign of Shadows mercenary.

- The third factor is the rarity of the mercenary (Treasured, Legendary, Fabled, or Celestial). At the same rank, a Celestial mercenary buff is about 23% better than a Treasured mercenary buff.

- The least important factor (5.8% gain) is your mercenaries' total contribution, which is based on the total number of mercenaries that you have unlocked that have reached a rank that is a multiple of 5 (5, 10, 15, 20, etc). The difference between having a single unlocked mercenary (contribution = 1) and a virtual army of unlocked mercenaries (contribution = 100) is a maximum gain of 5.8%. Contribution of each mercenary can be checked by hiring the Mercenary and right clicking the in-game avatar and selecting "Check Contribution".
