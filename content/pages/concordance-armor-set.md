**Concordance armor set**

- [[Mantle of Concordance]]
- [[Coat of Concordance]]
- [[Bracers of Concordance]]
- [[Gloves of Concordance]]
- [[Leggings of Concordance]]

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

This armor can be worn by only [[Troubador]], and is obtained through either [[Custom Fitted Armor - Part I|Custom Fitted Armor series]] or [[Ian's Forgetful Family - Part I|Ian's Forgetful Family series]].
