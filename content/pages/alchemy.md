Alchemists create Spell Scrolls for [[Fighter|Fighters]], along with [[Potions]] and [[Poisons]].

## Recipes

Journeyman spell recipes and non-mastercrafted Poisons and Potions are learned by scribing "Alchemist Essentials Volume #" scrolls, which can be bought from a Tradeskill Recipe Salesman. Expert spell recipes and mastercrafted Poisons and Potions are learned by scribing "Advanced Alchemist Volume #" scrolls, found as drops from mobs or bought from the broker. For spells, the number of the Volume corresponds with the level of spells that are taught.

Below is a table of the necessary primary components and fuel needed for the Alchemist to create a spell scroll. Common components used vary from tier to tier, as well as the amount of fuel needed.

NOTE: "Rough" materials will show in your recipe book as "raw". For example; "raw malachite" is actually "rough malachite".

| Tier 3 (20-29) <br>(90) | Pliant Loam | Malleable Loam | Tallow Wax Candle |
| Tier 4 (30-39) <br>(140) | Supple Loam | Ductile Loam | Gel Wax Candle |
| Tier 5 (40-49) <br>(190) | Bonded Loam | Fused Loam | Beeswax Candle |

This spell scroll can only be crafted by Alchemists who have scribed "[[The Tender's Secrets Volume 3]]"; a rare recipe book which drops from [[Tender of the Seedlings]] in [[The Emerald Halls]]. The recipe requires a legendary ingredient that drops off of non-named mobs throughout the zone called [[Seed of Growth]]. The alchemist can craft up to 25 [[Poisoned Seeds of the Tender]], usable by [[Rogue|Rogues]] or [[Predator|Predators]]. This rare book (along with other rare recipe drops from the zone) used to be no-trade, but can now be found on the [[Broker]].
