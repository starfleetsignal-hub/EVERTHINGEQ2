**Cute Mode** gives over-sized heads ("bobble heads") to all humanoid players and monsters. Advanced cute modes (cutemode2, cutemode3) alter limbs as well as the head.

Cute mode is automatically turned on when you log in on [[Bristlebane Day]] (April 1st).

To turn cute mode on/off, type **/cutemode**.
