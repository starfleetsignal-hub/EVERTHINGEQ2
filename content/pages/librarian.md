**Librarians** make regular appearances in many places in game. The following are malevolent, usually attacking most people who enter their literary domain:

- [[A Librarian (Nektropos Castle)|A Librarian]] guards the now abandoned books of [[Nektropos Castle]]
- Several high elf [[A librarian (New Tunaria)|librarians]] guard the books in the city of [[Felwithe]]
- [[The Arcane Librarian]] in [[The Vaults of El'Arad]]
- [[Librarian Zimor]], a ghostly librarian in [[The Crypt of Agony]]

There are, however, some **librarians** who hold no ill will:

- [[A crushed librarian]] tends the books in [[Stormhold]] and offers the quest [[A Key to the Past]]
- [[Librarian Dalmas]] in the [[Concordium Tower]] (red portal) in [[South Qeynos]]  participates in [[A Tour of Antonica]]
- [[Librarian Dungil]] in the [[Concordium Tower]] (blue portal) in [[South Qeynos]]  participates in [[An Ancient Desert Power]]
- [[Librarian Mervyn T'Xiett]], a dark elf librarian in [[Library of K'Lorn]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]]
- [[Librarian Motius Feldarn]] a human librarian in [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] in [[North Freeport]]
- [[Librarian Zabnolique]] an erudite librarian in [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] in [[North Freeport]]
