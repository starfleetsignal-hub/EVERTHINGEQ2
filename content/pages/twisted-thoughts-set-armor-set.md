- (2) Applies ***Focus: [[Asylum VI]]***.
  - [[Asylum VI]] decreases arcane resistances by an additional 800.
- (4) Applies ***Focus: [[Breeze V]]***.
  - Increases group's spell and combart art casting speed by 15%.
- (5) Applies ***Crippling Magical Affliction***
  - On a successful hostile spell this spell has a chance to cast Crippling Magical Affliction on target of spell. Lasts for 30.0 seconds. This effect will trigger an average of 2.0 times per minute.
    - Decreases Mitigation of target vs magic and mental damage by 1373
    - Inflicts 482 - 804 mental damage on target
- (6) Applies ***Focus: [[Enraging Demeanor III]]***.
  - Increases the recipient of [[Enraging Demeanor III]]'s base Spell and Combat Art damage by 10%.

This armor may be worn only by: [[Coercer|Coercers]].
