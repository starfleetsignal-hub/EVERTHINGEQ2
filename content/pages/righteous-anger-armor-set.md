This is the TSO Armor Set (Tier 2) for: [[Cleric|Clerics]] ( ) that can be obtained via the [[Void Shard Mission System]].
These items are sold by merchants in [[Moors of Ykesha]] at [[Dropship Landing Zone]] {{waypoint 1680, 452, 894}}. They are the group of [[A mysterious Quellithulian]]s. These specific items are sold by the Greater Shard Armor Merchant. They may also be purchased from a player crafter who has scribed [[Far Seas Strategic Pricing Manual, Volume IV]].

These pieces are upgrades from the equivalent Tier 1 pieces from the set: [[Pious Diatribe (Armor Set)]]

This armor set can be further upgraded to Fabled shard armor set (Tier 3): [[Chaotic Energy (Armor Set)]]
