At 82 AAs, Beastlords can put 82 points in their Beastlord AA tree can get the end-of-line Tame Exotic Warder ability, thus opening up four Exotic families of Warder. (Easily attainable by level 50)

1. Starts in Alternate Advancement, Beastlord tab
1. First Row (Beastlord) - Requires 5 points allocated to unlock Second Row.
1. Second Row (Beastlord) - Requires 5 points allocated to unlock Third Row.
1. Third Row (Beastlord) - Requires 5 points allocated to unlock Fourth Row.
1. Fourth Row (Beastlord) - Requires 5 Points allocated to unlock Fifth Row.
1. Allocate xx points in Beastlord Rows (First through to Fith Rows)
1. Fifth Row (Expertise) - (from the bottom (i.e. above Tame Exotic Warder) is the Sentinel's Fate row. Requires 10 points in Sentinel's Fate to unlock Tame Exotic Warder.
