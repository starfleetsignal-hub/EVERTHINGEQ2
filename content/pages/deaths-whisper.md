The long lost language of the Dead.

Odd when you consider the bulk of undead always spoke in common if they were humanoid.
There may be more to this in a later timeline or it is simply to gain Achievement Point XP.

Learned by completing the quest [[The Whispers of Death]] in [[The Sinking Sands]]
