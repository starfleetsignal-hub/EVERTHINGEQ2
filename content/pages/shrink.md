The following are items which can be purchased or won from [[Legends of Norrath]] as well as items in EQII that will allow a person to shrink their character's size.

## LoN Loot

| Item Name | Obtain | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [[Badge of Thyr]] | Claimable item from Exterminator Mark V Tournament Pack | Shapechanges caster; if "show illusion form" is turned off, it simply **shrinks the caster 40%**. |
| [[Badge of the Kromise]] | Claimable item from Steamwork Mender Tournament Pack | Shapechanges caster; if "show illusion form" is turned off, it simply **shrinks the caster 40%**. |
| [[Legends of Norrath Cloak]] | Claimable item from Oathbound booster pack | Reduces maximum falling speed of caster and **shrinks caster by 50%**. |
| [[Potion of Deflation]] | Claimable item from Storm Break booster pack | 15 charge potion that **shrinks the caster by 50%** for one day. |

### Everquest II

| Item name | Obtain | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [[Toadstool]] | [[Carnivorous Plant]] | Claimable item for players who purchased the retail version of [[Kingdom of Sky]] expansion. Applies Toadstool when activated. Lasts for 10 minutes. **Shrinks caster by 35%.** Enlarges caster by 50% |
| [[Mystic Moppet Billy]] | [[Veteran Reward - 4 year\|Veteran Reward]] for accounts of 4 years' minimum age | Applies Mystic Moppet Disguise when Activated.<br> ***Shrinks caster by 40%**. <br>*Shapechanges caster into a Form of a mystic moppet.<br> *This effect cannot be cast during combat. |
| [[Fungoid Souvenir]] | Guide Event Reward | Applies Fungoid Fascination when Equipped.<br> ***Shrinks caster by 30.0%**. |
| [[Cloak of Mischief]] | [[Bristlebane's Beloved]] | May apply Mischief Personified when activated.<br> ***Shrinks caster by 30%<br>Decreases Hate Gain of caster by 10%**. |
