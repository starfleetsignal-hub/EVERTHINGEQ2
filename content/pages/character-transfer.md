Character transfer between servers for EQ2 is automated. In most cases, one may simply purchase a [[Server Transfer Token]] from the [[Marketplace]] then activate the server transfer token in game.

Character transfer between accounts for EQ2 usually does not require contacting [[Phone Support|Customer Support]].

Please review the following stipulations and make sure that all prove true:

- Account must be open, active and in good standing for a minimum of 60 days.
- The source account must have enough station cash to pay for the transfer.
- Your Game Pass has to be active for at least 31 days in a row.
- Character must be level 10 or higher.
- Destination account must have the equal amount or more expansions as the source account and an available character slot.
- All items on the consignment ([[Broker|broker/fence]]) that your character owns must be completely removed, including money and containers.
- All items owned by the character must be completely repaired.
- No [[The Norrathian Express|mail]] should be present in the mailbox for the character, regardless of read status. Please file a petition to remove any ghost mails if any.  Use the command /clear_mailbox to delete all mail.
- The character should be clear of all experience debt.
- Both the Source and Destination Accounts must have Two Factor Authentication enabled.
