This is the Fabled shard armor set (referred to as Tier 3) introduced in [[LU51|GU51]] for [[Warrior|Warriors]] ( ), [[Crusader|Crusaders]] ( ), [[Cleric|Clerics]] ( ).

1. The Molds for each armor piece drop in the X2 raid zone [[Ward of Elements]].
1. You have to buy the Patterns for the armor piece from [[Assilus Poisonbolt]] designated as <Elemental Pattern Quartermaster> in exchange for the corresponding Mold and some number of [[Void Shard|Void Shards]].
1. The actual armor pieces are available as vendor purchase from [[Borden Chargehammer]], designated as the <Elite Void Shard Armor Quartermaster> in [[Lavastorm]] at the [[Shrine of Thunder|Docks]].
   - The price of each piece is the corresponding Pattern and the respective Tier 2 shard armor piece.
1. The Bracers are actual drops in the zone.

These pieces are upgrades from the equivalent Tier 2 pieces from the set: [[Holy Bastion (Armor Set)]]
