These quests are similar in scope to Heritage Quests, but the rewarded items are new to EverQuest II rather than being familiar items from EverQuest.

Signature Quests were first introduced in the [[Kingdom of Sky]] expansion, and commonly involve a series of quests ultimately rewarding a big item. Many of the intermediate steps also might reward very fine items.

- For a list of the major SQ timelines, see [[Signature Quests Timeline]]
- For a listing of these types of quests, see Signature Quests (all/most quests in a timeline are marked 'Signature,' so there are many more quests than there are timelines/rewards)
