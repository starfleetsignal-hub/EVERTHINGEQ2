Cthurath, the Consumer, is the inscrutable deity that lords over [[The Void]]. They are the primary antagonist of the [[Rage of Cthurath]] expansion. Their singular goal appears to be the consumption of all, returning the universe to a state of nothingness. They are worshipped by many creatures found within the Xebrith Tendril of the Void, such as the Lamia and Nightblood.
