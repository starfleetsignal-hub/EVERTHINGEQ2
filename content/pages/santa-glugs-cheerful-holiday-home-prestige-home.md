![Top: the view from the entry globe. Bottom: view from the table globe.](images/Santa-glug-cheerful-holiday-home.png.png)
**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** Purchased from a "12 Days of [[Frostfell]]" merchant, located outside of the instance only during Frostfell. To buy the house you use event tokens that are purchased (for real cash) from the [[Marketplace]].
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** a humorous, [[Frostfell]]-themed house
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** flying and ground mounts

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- You must get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- It has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- You can get a [[House Portal|portal]] to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]. Portals allow for quick travel between locations.
- You can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

![Located at the edge of snowglobes, this tiny tower is used to travel between globes.](images/Santa-glug-home-travel.png)
This house is decorated in a [[Frostfell]]-theme and looks similar to the [[Qeynos Housing]], **but** it comprised of three snowglobes locations on the counter, mantelpiece, and table. The twist is that you are tiny and *inside* the snowglobes, looking out at [[Santa Glug]]'s cheerfully decorated holiday house.

Players can travel between the areas by clicking on a small tower with a snowglobe on it.

The view from the counter is of the fireplace and tree. The view from the mantle includes the door, which is donned with wreathes and the side of the tree. From the table globe, the side of the fireplace is visible, as is a stairway, the front counter, and front door.

### Break Out

Using a [[Bag of Teleportation Pads|bag of teleportation pads]], you can break out of the normal limits of this home.

Though this house doesn't have a traditional in/outside boundary, a few locations have been noted to break out of the common limits of this house.

See the [[Housing Breakout Guide]] for details on how breakouts work. See the specific [Santa Glug breakout video by Ocarinah](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/house-preview-kerafyrms-lair.588981/).

### Building Blocks

Though not specific to this house, the [[Glacier Tile]] (and related blocks) look like chunky ice and can be made using Frostfell harvestables. During the Frostfell event, players can also buy ice themed tiles from the merchants in the [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]] using event currency.

## Cost and Item Count

- After completing the achievement, locate it in your bag, then travel to one of the two locations to "buy" it at no cost.
- 900 items and 900 [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]]
