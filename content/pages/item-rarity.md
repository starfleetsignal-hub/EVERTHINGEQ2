## Item Rarity

Item rarity describes the relative value, power, and availability of equipment and other items. While rarity tiers tend to remain consistent, rules associated with specific items have changed over time. For example, many older collection items—such as *An empty Paineel bath*—had their restrictions removed. Items once limited to specific characters were reclassified as **TREASURED**, making them fully tradeable and eligible for broker sales starting in 2013.

For this reason, it's wise to check items stored in your bank, broker, house vault, or housing. If you no longer need or want them, you may now be able to sell them on the broker or transfer them to your alts.

*Not to be confused with [[Item Flags]], which describe how items behave (e.g., trade restrictions, equip requirements).*

## Item Rarity Reference

| Rarity | Description |
|---|---|
| **No Label** | Items without a rarity tag are considered **COMMON**. These are typically vendor trash and are sold to NPC merchants. |
| **UNCOMMON** | Slightly rare items found in small chests or monster loot. Usually of little value but can be sold to NPCs. |
| **HANDCRAFTED** | Crafted using common harvested materials. Not found as loot. Can be crafted or purchased from the broker. |
| **TREASURED** | Dropped by monsters or given as quest rewards. Includes rare harvestables and gear. Often tradeable and worth selling on the broker. |
| **MASTERCRAFTED** | Crafted using rare materials. Superior to **TREASURED** but below **LEGENDARY**. Not found as loot. |
| **MASTERCRAFTED LEGENDARY** | Crafted tier between **MASTERCRAFTED** and **LEGENDARY**. Includes some adornments. |
| **MASTERCRAFTED FABLED** | Crafted tier between **LEGENDARY** and **FABLED**. Includes some adornments. |
| **LEGENDARY** | Dropped from stone **LEGENDARY** chests or earned via quests. Sometimes better than **MASTERCRAFTED**. Can be sold on the broker. |
| **FABLED** | Dropped from steel **FABLED** chests. Includes high-tier gear and **Master I** spell scrolls. Highly valuable on the broker. |
| **MYTHICAL** | Extremely rare items from epic quests or encounters. Among the highest item classes in the game. |
| **CELESTIAL** | High-level item, spell, or gear with powerful properties; often obtained through endgame content or pre-orders. |

## Note

If an item with a high rarity tag cannot be equipped, consumed, upgraded, placed in a [[Housing|house]], or otherwise used by your character, it is likely considered "junk loot." For example, a crab claw marked as **FABLED** may have no functional use. In such cases, it's best to sell the item to a merchant.
