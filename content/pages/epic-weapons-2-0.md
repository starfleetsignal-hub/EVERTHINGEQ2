## Notes

- If you have lost or destroyed your Epic 2.0 weapon, or want a house version, seek out the [[Shady Erudite]] on the docks in [[Nektulos Forest]].
- You can now purchase your Epic 2.0 Apprentice spells by speaking to one of the following:
  - [[Alhuurn Khalen]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]] {{waypoint 178.49, -4.96, -25.87}} -- *You will need to have completed the [[Chaos Descending Timeline|CD Adventure Sig Line]].*
  - [[Maesy Colmiter]], [[Recuso Tor]], [[The Blinding]], {{waypoint -523.29, 61.29, 243.43}}
  - [[Maesy Colmiter]], [[Sanctus Seru]], {{waypoint -359.30, 89.66, 61.86}}
- You can upgrade your Epic 2.0 spells using the recipe book [[Awakened Abilities]] (or finding a crafter who has those recipes scribed).
  - Each level of the spell must be scribed before the next higher level can be scribed: Journeyman, Adept, Expert, Master, Grandmaster, Ancient.
  - Each level of the spell will require rares harvested from [[Shadeweave bud|shadeweave buds]] in [[Obulus Frontier]] (or obtained from the broker, or from [[An Obulus Frontier Garden]], etc.)
  - Epic 2.0 spells also require [[Lesser Fragment of Planar Energy]], [[Superior Fragment of Planar Energy]], and [[Greater Fragment of Planar Energy]]. These were originally obtained from Public Quests in [[Kunark Ascending]]. It is currently much easier to obtain these now from performing Charged Overseer Missions from Seasons 1 and 2. Charged Overseer Missions may be obtained from [[Stanley Parnem]] <Administrator Agent>.
    - Journeyman -- requires a [[Lesser Fragment of Planar Energy]], [[Superior Fragment of Planar Energy]] or [[Greater Fragment of Planar Energy]]
    - Adept -- requires a [[Lesser Fragment of Planar Energy]], [[Superior Fragment of Planar Energy]] or [[Greater Fragment of Planar Energy]]
    - Expert -- requires a [[Superior Fragment of Planar Energy]] or [[Greater Fragment of Planar Energy]]
    - Master -- requires a [[Superior Fragment of Planar Energy]] or [[Greater Fragment of Planar Energy]]
    - Grandmaster -- requires a [[Greater Fragment of Planar Energy]]
    - Ancient -- requires a [[Greater Fragment of Planar Energy]]
