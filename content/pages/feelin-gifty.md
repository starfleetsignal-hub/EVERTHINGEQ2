First appearing in 2005, completing one of these quests rewards the player with the ability to light up their [[Frostfell Tree]]. Only one of the quests can be completed per character, i.e. if you completed 'Feelin' Gifty in Qeynos' you will not be able to complete any of the other quests as they will not be offered. Additionally, the completed quest does not appear in the World Events category of the quest journal, rather in the city in which they were completed:
