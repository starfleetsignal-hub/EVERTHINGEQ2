## Overview

![Use a scrying stone here!](images/A_Light_in_the_Forest.jpg)
Training tomes are books that you can find from [[Scrying]]. They initiate quests with the same name as themselves. There are 72 different training tomes, 18 for each class archetype. Each class can only complete the 18 tomes related to their archetype.

- [[Fighter]] - Marshall's
- [[Mage]] - Arcanist's
- [[Priest]] - Diviner's
- [[Scout]] - Outrider's

## Starting The Quests

1. Obtain a training tome, by either scrying or by purchasing them off other players via the broker.
1. Kill a set number (5, 10, or 20) of certain types of monsters in 5 minutes.
   - It would probably be easiest to complete each set of 3 tomes (neophyte, apprentice, etc.) at the same time.
   - When you start the quest, the tome is taken away, so if you fail to kill the appropriate amount of monsters within the time limit you will need to find another tome and start the quest completely over.
1. Experience and a unique quest journal entry are the rewards for completing a training tome quest.
