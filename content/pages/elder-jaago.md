The former [[The Ashen Order (Faction)|Ashen Order]] monk, **Elder Jaago**, appears in two locations in game. When first approached by monks working on their [[Monk Epic Weapon Timeline|epic weapon quests]], he appears in the jails of the basement of [[Elder Jaago (Sebilis)|Sebilis]]. Once the bones that were removed during his torture are retrieved from the monsters of the zone and buried in [[Elder Jaago (Elddar Grove)|Elddar Grove]] he appears a second time and confirms that he is at peace with his final resting place.
