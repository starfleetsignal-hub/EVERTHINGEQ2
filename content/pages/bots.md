The term **bot** has several different meanings within the context of EQ2 and the EQ2i wiki. It may refer to various types of monsters, usually clockworks such as:

- [[Assessment bot|assessment bots]] in the [[Moors of Ykesha]]
- [[An excavation bot|excavation bots]] in the Moors of Ykesha
- [[A security bot|security bots]] in McScroogle Corp. Headquarters, encountered as part of the [[Frostfell]] celebrations
- a [[Mechanized Platinum Repository of Reconstruction]], commonly referred to in game as a "mender bot" by players

Bots also refers to the automaton user accounts at EQ2i designed to complete repetitive, otherwise manually intensive editing at the wiki. EQ2i has information specifically related to bots that users may wish to read:

- a [[EverQuest 2 Wiki:Policy/Bots|bots policy]] detailing the process for requesting a bot and requirements for creating one
- a specific template for labeling user accounts that are bots
