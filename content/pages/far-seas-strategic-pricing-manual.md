Far Seas Strategic Pricing Manual refers to the following four volumes containing [[Void Shard]] recipes.

- [[Far Seas Strategic Pricing Manual, Volume I]] -- which contains Tier 1 Shard Armor recipes
- [[Far Seas Strategic Pricing Manual, Volume II]] -- which contains Lesser Shard Jewelry recipes
- [[Far Seas Strategic Pricing Manual, Volume III]] -- which contains Greater Shard Jewelry recipes
- [[Far Seas Strategic Pricing Manual, Volume IV]] -- which contains Tier 2 Shard Armor recipes
