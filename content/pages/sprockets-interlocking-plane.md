**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** Only with the purchase of Collectors Edition or Premium Edition of Planes of Prophecy expansion
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** floating aerial platforms in gnomish/tinkerer-style
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** ground or flying allowed

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- You must get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- It has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- You can get a [[House Portal|portal]] to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]. The portal allows quick travel between each location.
- You can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button.

## About This House

Sprocket's Interlocking Plane is comprised on one huge floating platform with two identical buildings on it and several smaller floating platforms. To travel between them, players need a flying mount or the owner can place [[Bag of Teleportation Pads|housing teleportation pads]] for visitors to use.

The entire space is styled in bronze tones and the buildings have spewing smoke stacks. It has a "tinkerer" theme that makes it look like it was built by gnomes. The only thing that can be seen from the platforms is blue sky, clouds, and sun, giving one the sense that you're very high up in the atmosphere.

## Building Blocks

- The [[Sprocket's Interlocking Plane Builder's Kit]] is a recipe book with [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]]-like items that was also included with the purchase of the Collector's or Premium edition of the Planes of Prophecy. The recipe book includes tiles, walls, stairs, roof pieces and more.
