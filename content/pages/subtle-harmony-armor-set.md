- (2) +120 Primary Attributes
- (3) Applies ***Lyricist's Harmony***.
  - On any successful attack this spell has a chance to cast Lyricist's Harmony on the group. Lasts for 10.0 seconds. This effect will trigger an average of 1.8 times per minute.
    - Increases STR, INT, AGI, and WIS of group memebers by 200.0.
    - Increases Spell and Combat Art damage of group members by 650.
- (5) Applies ***Greater Mistarget***.
  - On any successful attack this spell has a chance to cast Greater Mistarget on target of attack. Lasts for 5.0 seconds. This effect will trigger an average of 1.8 times per minute.
    - Target will have their chance to hit with a weapon lowered by 4.0%

This is the TSO Armor Set (Tier 2) for: [[Bard|Bards]] ( ) that can be obtained via the [[Void Shard Mission System]]. .

These pieces are available for vendor purchase from [[A mysterious Quellthulian]], designated as the <Greater Shard Armor Merchant> in [[Moors of Ykesha]] at the [[Dropship Landing Zone]] {{waypoint 1680, 452, 894}}. They can also be created by crafters.

These pieces are upgrades from the equivalent Tier 1 pieces from the set: [[Lyrical Composure (Armor Set)]]

This armor set can be further upgraded to Fabled shard armor set (Tier 3): [[Volcanic Symphony (Armor Set)]]

Note that the Void Shard costs in the above table are for vendor purchase. If purchased from a crafter, Hauberk and Spaulders cost three void shards less, all others will cost two void shards less.
