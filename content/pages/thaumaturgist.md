## Spells by Level

**1**: [[Sanguine Encasement]]

- Applies Sanguine Encasement to qualifying pets
  - Converts all abilities to disease damage
- Increases Fervor gained by 1.1-4% depending on spell level
- Converts all abilities that do not depend on weapon damage type to disease damage

**2**: [[Septic Strike]]

- Inflicts disease damage

**3**: [[Toxic Life]]

- Increases noxious damage done to target by 2.25-5.17% up to 50 stacks depending on spell level

**4**: [[Withering]]

- Inceases noxious damage to targets in AOE range by 2.25-5.17% for 25 seconds depending on spell level

**5**: [[Sanguine Shelter]]

- Replaces [[Sanguine Encasement]]
- Applies Sanguine Encasement to qualifying pets
  - Converts all abilities to disease damage
- Increases Fervor gained by 1.3-5.7 depending on spell level to ancient
- Converts all abilities that do not depend on weapon damage type to disease damage

**6**: [[Virulent Outbreak]]

- Inflicts disease damage on target encounter for 8 seconds

**7**: [[Necrotic Caress]]

- On a melee hit this spell will cast Necrotic Caress on target of attack.  Lasts for 4.4-9.5 seconds
  - Reduces Melee Multiplier by 0.09-0.21 up to 50 stacks every 1 second depending on spell level

**8**: [[Blood Contract]]

- Direct Disease Damage + Disease (DOT)
- Direct Heal + Heal (HOT)

**9**: [[Tainted Mutation]]

- Potency decease on target
- When target falls below 3-4% health this spell will cast Tainted Mutation on target. Lasts for 20.0 seconds.
  - Increases Fervor of caster (Ancient+ spell level only)
  - Reduces target's health to 1.

**10**: [[Sanguine Embrace]]

- Replaces [[Sanguine Encasement]] and [[Sanguine Shelter]]
- Applies Sanguine Embrace to all qualifying pets
  - Converts all abilities to disease damage
- Increases fervor gained by 1.6-11.2% depending on grade
- Converts all abilities that do not depend on weapon damage type to disease damage
  - Celestial level grants 10.3 Ability Doublecast

**10**: [[Revocation of Life]]
8 seconds duration

- Death prevent
- Increases power & heals of caster
- Disease Damage + Disease DOT

**11**: [[Anti-Life]]

- Inflicts x - y disease damage on target
- Anti-Life deals more damage to targets with higher health
- Anti-Life will trigger a combo off any ascension combo starter.
- Anti-Life does not start any ascension combos.

**12**: [[Blood Parasite]]

- On a death blow this spell has a 5% chance to cast Blood Infusion on target. Lasts for 17.6 seconds
  - Increases Fervor Overcap of caster by 0.9-2.1, multiplied by increment stacks
  - Increases Crit Bonus Overcap of caster by 9.0-20.7, multiplied by increment stacks
  - This effect can only trigger once every 5.0-7.0 seconds.

**13**: [[Bloatfly]]

- Enemy - Duration 12.3sec+
- Summons a limited pet to aid the caster

**14**: [[Dessication|Desiccation]]

- Enemy - Duration 15.8sec+
- Inflicts x-y disease damage on target
- Inflicts x-y disease damage on target every z seconds
- Decreases Combat Mitigation of target by 227.8+

**15**: [[Bonds of Blood]]

- Self - Duration 14.1sec+
- On a hostile ability cast this spell has a 10% chance to cast Blood Infusion on caster. lasts for 28.2+ seconds
  - Increases Fervor of caster by 0.9+
  - Increases Ability Modifier of caster by 2,250+
  - This effect can only trigger once every 6-9 seconds.
