The table below details the **Tier 6 Adornments** organized by the slots to which they apply:

**Lesser** adornments appear with no highlighting. They require 1 [[Vision Powder]] and 6 [[Pearl Fragment|Pearl Fragments]].  Up to 3 [[Pearl Fragment|Pearl Fragments]] are returned per craft.

**Greater** adornments appear with brown highlighting. They require 1 [[Infusion of Daydreams]], 2 [[Vision Powder|Vision Powders]], and 6 [[Pearl Fragment|Pearl Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Vision Powder]] is returned per craft.

**Superior** adornments appear with red highlighting. They require 1 [[Coalesced Mana]], 2 [[Infusion of the Void|Infusions of Daydreams]], 3 [[Vision Powder|Vision Powders]] and 6 [[Pearl Fragment|Pearl Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Infusion of Daydreams]] is returned per craft.

For a simple, alphabetic listing of tier 6 adornments, see Tier 6 Adornments.
