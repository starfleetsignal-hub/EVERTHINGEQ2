**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** Purchased from the [[Marketplace]]
**Available Locations:** only in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]], entered through a door at {{waypoint 917, -274, 108}}
**Quantity Limit:** one per character
**House Style/Theme:** huge, [[Thurgadin]]-themed
**[[Mount]] Use Within:** none allowed

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Thurgadin Grand Hall is a almost like an extension [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]]. The door to enter this house is directly behind area with the [[Banker|banker]], [[Broker|broker]], and [[Destiny of Velious Crafting Timeline|crafting NPCs]] for this city. On entering, you will find an enormous room with 4 smaller rooms flanking each corner of the central court area. Beyond the main room, a carbon copy of the [[Remembrance Park]] area in Thurgadin can be found, complete with the same benches and plants.
