**Appellation:** The Great Dreamer

**Forebear:** [[Antonius Bayle VI]]

**Successors:** [[The Circle of Five]]

**Time of Rule:** [[The Age of Cataclysms]] to [[The Age of Destiny]]

**History:** No official information of any substance has been offered from any official EverQuest II source regarding this ruler of Qeynos.

It can be assumed, as following the rule of Antonius the Seventh the rule of the Bayle line was interrupted due to the lack of a verifiable claimant, that Antonius did not abide by the will of the Council to bear children, or those children did not survive into their twenties as the traditional age of assumption of rule.
