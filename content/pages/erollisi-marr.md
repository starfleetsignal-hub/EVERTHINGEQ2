This sister of [[Mithaniel Marr (God)|Mithaniel]] and daughter of [[Tarew Marr|Tarew]] is allied with Fizzlethorpe [[Bristlebane]], [[Mithaniel Marr (God)|Mithaniel Marr]], [[Rodcet Nife]], [[Tunare]], and [[Quellious]]. Her enemies are [[Innoruuk]], [[Cazic-Thule]], [[Rallos Zek]], and [[Bertoxxulous]]. Followers of Erollisi Marr, the Queen Of Love, cling to the belief that love conquers all. It should be pointed out that while love is a generally peaceful concept, Erollisi worshippers are not pacifists. They would like to live in a world where everyone loves everyone else and violence does not exist but they are not naive enough to think that Norrath is that world. They are passionately devoted to people, places and ideas and are more than willing to fight and die to preserve those things. The dream of every follower of Erollisi is to die in the selfless defense of someone or something they love. Many paladins hear the true calling of their hearts and follow Erollisi Marr.

![Erollisi Marr](images/Erollisi_Marr.png) ![Erollisi Marr and two of her winged guardians.](images/Erollisi_Marr-02.png)With the release of the instance [[The Shard of Love]] it is found out at the end of the instance that the Gods of Fear, Hate, War and Disease have won a battle and killed Erollisi Marr by attacking The Planes of Valor. Her brother, [[Mithaniel Marr (God)|Mithaniel]], asked for her assistance in fending off the forces of evil. But in doing this, the four evil deities took the chance to attack and kill the God of Love. At the end of the Shard of Love, you fight Mithaniel Marr, The Sorrowful who is standing in a room with the body of his beloved sister in a coffin. He eventually snaps out of his trance of self pity and sadness after you fight him to 50% health and releases his sister from the world of Norrath, telling her to go find peace and love in another time and place.

[Source](http://eqlive.station.sony.com/library/deities.jsp)

In truth, Erollisi Marr was not dead. When battling [[Ullkorruuk]], she had taken the opportunity to seal them both, figuring that such sacrifice would be worth it if it meant keeping Ullkorruuk imprisoned. The statue of Erollisi in New Halas was used as the seal, but eventually tampering with the statue ended up releasing both deities.

Later, after [[Innoruuk]] had been betrayed and killed by [[Lanys T'Vyl]] during the conquest of the Planes, the Plane of Time began to destabilize. Erollisi made the difficult choice of restoring her sworn enemy to life, so that balance could be maintained. Despite now having upper hand, [[Innoruuk]] ended up taking out his wrath not on Erollisi, but on those responsible for the entire mess: [[Ullkorruuk]], [[Terris-Thule|Terris Thule]], and [[Saryrn]], imprisoning them all on Plane of Hate. Same fate would've awaited Lanys, if she hadn't already been killed by adventurers.
