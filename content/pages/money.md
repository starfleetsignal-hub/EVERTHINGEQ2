## Money in Norrath

[[Norrath]] uses a straightforward monetary system consisting of four denominations: copper, silver, gold, and platinum. Each tier is worth 100 of the one below it:

- 1 silver = 100 copper
- 1 gold = 100 silver = 10,000 copper
- 1 platinum = 100 gold = 1,000,000 copper

For example, 2 platinum = 200 gold = 20,000 silver = 2,000,000 copper.

Most players—and the game itself—abbreviate currency using the first letter of each denomination. For instance, 5 gold, 27 silver, and 18 copper is written as:

- Player shorthand: **5g27s18c**
- Game display: **5g, 27s, 18c**

## Moving Coin

To move money between your inventory, bank, or broker:

- Click and hold a coin denomination while dragging.
- To move a single coin, hold the **Ctrl** key while clicking and dragging.
- To move a specific amount, hold the **Shift** key while clicking and dragging. A window will appear where you can enter the exact number of coins to transfer.

This method also works at bankers to make change. For example, if you only have platinum stored in the bank, you can shift-drag a lower denomination (like copper) and the game will automatically convert it as if you had enough of that currency.

For visual instructions on coin-splitting, see [[Bank|bank]].

## Spending Coin

Coin is used throughout Norrath for a wide variety of purposes, including:

- Purchasing items from NPC merchants and faction vendors
- Buying gear, spells, and consumables from the broker
- Paying for housing upkeep and decorations
- Repairing damaged equipment
- Crafting components and recipe books
- Auctioning rare items to other players

Some services or items may also require additional currencies such as status points, loyalty tokens, or itemized gear for exchanging, but standard coin remains the most widely accepted form of payment across the game.

## Meta-Currency

In addition to standard coin, Norrath features special currencies that serve unique purposes:

- **[[Daybreak Cash]]** – A premium currency that can only be purchased with real money. It is used in the Marketplace to buy cosmetic items, account services, expansions, and other premium content.
- **[[Loyalty Point Token]]** – Earned by completing Daily Objectives. All players can earn these tokens, but [[All Access Membership|All-Access Members]] receive bonus tokens. Loyalty tokens can be spent at special vendors for unique items, housing goods, and consumables.
