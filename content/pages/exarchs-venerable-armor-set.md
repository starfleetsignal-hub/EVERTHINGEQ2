**Exarch's Venerable armor set**

- [[Venerable Barbute of the Exarch]]
- [[Venerable Gauntlets of the Exarch]]
- [[Venerable Greaves of the Exarch]]
- [[Venerable Pauldrons of the Exarch]]
- [[Venerable Sabatons of the Exarch]]
- [[Venerable Vambraces of the Exarch]]

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

This armor may be worn only by: [[Templar|Templars]].

The pieces of this set are crafted by level 90 [[Artisan]] who have purchased the recipes, [[Ancient Armor of War]] from [[Brontis]] in [[The Fortress of Drunder]].
