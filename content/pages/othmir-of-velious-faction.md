## Faction Merchants

- [[Fynn]], located at [[Fina's Retreat]] and requires a minimum of 10,000 faction.
- [[Laurel]], located at [[Nipik's Haven]] and requires a minimum of 30,000 faction.

How to boost this faction:

1. Run the repeatables in **Fina's Retreat** (tradeskill/merchant area) in Great Divide.

- Speak to Narp for [[Crustacean Critters]] which gives +200.
- Speak to Runt for [[Slippery as an Eel]] which gives +200.

2. Run the repeatables in **Nipik's Haven** in Eastern Wastes.

- Speak to Klipklap for [[Precariously Placed Package]] which gives +500.
- Speak to Fiyt for [[Cutting the Ranks]] which gives +200 *and* a selection of house items.
- Speak to Minos for [[Fishing Fishmen]] which gives +200
  - <sub>May suffer from slow respawn rate; however, respawn rate not an issue during completion of other Nipik's Haven quests.</sub>
- Suggest completing Nipik's Haven quests in above order, farthest first; which reduces back & forth travelling and assures respawn of beasties. Almost the entire beach respawns in the time it takes to complete the quests near Kael Drakkel and fly back, so you can do Minos' quest 3-4 times per round to gain faction even quicker. Took a little over 4 hours of grinding to reach max faction doing this (after having completed all other quests at Fina's Retreat and Nipik's Haven).

3. Whether Great Divide or Eastern Wastes, the above noted quests are repeatable as soon as they are turned-in (formerly dailies).
