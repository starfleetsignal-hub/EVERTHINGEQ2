(6) Applies ***Hardened Skin***

- When target takes any damage this spell has a 5% chance to cast Hardened Skin on caster. Lasts for 8.0 seconds.
  - Increases Mitigation of caster vs crushing damage by 50
  - Increases Mitigation of caster vs slashing damage by 150

This leather armor may be worn only by: [[Brawler|Brawlers]].
