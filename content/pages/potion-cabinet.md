![A potion cabinet placed in a house. All potion cabinets look the same no matter what tier of rare wood is used to make them.](images/Potion_Cabinet_Sales_Display_Placed.png)
If you are not familiar with potion cabinets, you may want to read about the general topic of [[Sales Display|sales displays]] that can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]].

- All potion cabinets are Master Crafted (made from a rare material) by those who take up the [[Carpenter]] tradeskill.
- They hold a variety of [[Potion]] and [[Poisons]] made by [[Alchemist|Alchemists]] and [[Totems]] made by [[Woodworker|Woodworkers]].
