- (1) Applies ***Fire Within.***
  - On a hostile ability cast this spell has a 15% chance to cast Fire Within on caster.  Lasts for 6.0 seconds.
    - Increases Flurry of Caster by 10,000.0.
    - Dispelled when target hits with a melee attack
    - This effect can only trigger once every 60.0 - 300.0 seconds.
- (2) Applies ***Fire Within.***
  - On a hostile ability cast this spell has a 15% chance to cast Fire Within on caster.  Lasts for 10.0 seconds.
    - Dispelled when the target casts a hostile ability.
    - Increases Ability Doublecast of caster by 10,000.0.
    - This effect can only trigger once every 180.0 - 300.0 seconds.
