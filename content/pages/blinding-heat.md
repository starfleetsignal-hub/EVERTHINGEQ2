![Blinding Heat effect](images/Effect_BlindingHeat.jpg)
Effects:

- Forces targets in Area of Effect to clear their selected target.
- Targets in Area of Effect will have their chance to hit with a weapon lowered by 50.0%
- Decreases strikethrough chance of target in Area of Effect by 50.0%
- Blurs vision of target in Area of Effect
- Decreases Mitigation of targets in Area of Effect vs elemental damage by 1,000,000
