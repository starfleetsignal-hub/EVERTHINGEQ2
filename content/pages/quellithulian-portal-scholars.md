## Notes

This faction appeared during the live event for the [[15th Anniversary Event]] in 2019.

- The event spanned 12/06/2019 1:59am PST through 11/07/2019 2:00am PST.
- During the event, players were asked to help Quellithulian Portal Scholars building monuments in [[The Commonlands]] and [[Antonica]].
- This faction appears under the *Shattered Lands* group in the faction drop-down list on the player character sheet (press C).
- In order to buy most of the event items, players had to attain positive faction ranging from *Amiable (+10k)* to *Ally (+40K)*.
  - The quickest way to gain faction during the live event was to attend many of the [[Dragon Attack!|Dragon Attacks]] at various spires.
