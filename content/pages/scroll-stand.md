If you are not familiar with scroll stands, you may want to read about the general topic of [[Sales Display|sales displays]] that can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]].

- All scroll stands are Master Crafted (made from a rare material) by those who take up the [[Carpenter]] tradeskill.
- This sales display holds a limited scope of items that are [[Scholar]] made, like spell scrolls, combat art runes and distillations, Master spells, and recipe books.
