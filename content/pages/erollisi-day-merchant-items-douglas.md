## About

Douglas is a merchant that appears during the [[Erollisi Day]] festival in two locations:

- [[East Freeport (POI)]].
- [[Qeynos Harbor (POI)]]

Some items may be duplicates of those you can chose from the [[Hearts a'Flutter]] races or items from retired quests.

- A second merchant, [[Steven]] also sells event items.

To view these (and other items) using the in-game "dressing room" window before you buy them, hold down CTRL on your keyboard and L click on the item's icon. The dressing room will open and show a preview of house items, petamorph wands, mounts, appearance gear, dungeon-maker goods, and (most) other items.
