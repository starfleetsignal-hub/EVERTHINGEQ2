The language of the [[Fae (Character Race)|Fae]]. It is similar to [[Feir'Dal]] since the fae have lived with the wood elves for many generations. It can be learned by doing the quest, [[Learning Faerlie]], from [[Miella Maarsitotts]] up in the trees of [[Kelethin]] in [[Greater Faydark]].

- Faerlie is often confused with [[Fayefolk]], which is the language of Fay creatures (Treants, Fairies, Dryads, etc.).
- Evil characters are able to learn Faerlie.
