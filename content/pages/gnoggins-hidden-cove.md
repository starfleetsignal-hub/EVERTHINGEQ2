- This is a instanced zone for [[Gnary a Joke to Tell]].
- It's a solo zone that scales to your level.
- The quest can be repeated annually and the zone can only be accessed after you hail [[Captain Gnoggin]].
- [[Sirus Bis Nes]] is a named crocodile you can defeat in this instance for [[Alternate Advancement]] points.

## Monsters

Though there are roaming crocodiles, snakes, and other creatures, only two will give you want you need to update the quest:

- Static, wooden crates that must be targeted and "attacked" to open them. These don't attack back so they may be a better choice non-mele classes.
- [[Mimic|Mimics]] (monsters that look like chests) to release the **a joke shop curiosity** that you must collect for updates to the quest.
