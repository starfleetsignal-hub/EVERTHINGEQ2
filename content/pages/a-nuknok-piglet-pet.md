A boarfiend summoned from a [[Nuknok Piglet Leash]], this fluff pet stands a little shorter than a Gnome.  It does nothing except follow its caster around.

This pet **OVERRIDES** Deity pets and the [[Hatch Gorg Egg|gorg pet]], and vice versa.
