**Backpacks** are leather-based containers that characters can use to carry most of their inventory. They can also be used to increase space in [[Bank|bank slots]] or in the storage (the vault) you can access when inside [[Housing|player housing]].

- They are primarily made by [[Tailor|Tailors]], but you start with a few small backpacks, some are quest rewards or can be purchased from NPC merchants.
- At one time, they added less weight to your character (a mechanic that could slow movement) but this was removed from [[Strong Box|strong boxes]]. As a result, both items serve the same purpose.
- Each player-made (crafted) backpack also has the same number of slots as player-made strong boxes of the same Tier.
- Due to market fluctuations and item availability, it's wise to search the [[Broker|broker]] for both backpacks and strong boxes now that they function in the same manner. At times, both backpacks and strong boxes with the same number of slots can vary greatly in price and you may save a great deal of coin.
