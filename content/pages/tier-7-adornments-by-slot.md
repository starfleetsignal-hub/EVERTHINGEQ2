The table below details the **Tier 7 Adornments** organized by the slots to which they apply:

**Lesser** adornments appear with no highlighting. They require 1 [[Illusionary Powder]] and 6 [[Lunar Fragment|Lunar Fragments]].  Up to 3 [[Lunar Fragment|Lunar Fragments]] are returned per craft.

**Greater** adornments appear with brown highlighting. They require 1 [[Infusion of Illusion]], 2 [[Illusionary Powder|Illusionary Powders]], and 6 [[Lunar Fragment|Lunar Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Illusionary Powder]] is returned per craft.

**Superior** adornments appear with red highlighting. They require 1 [[Hardened Mana]], 2 [[Infusion of the Void|Infusions of Illusion]], 3 [[Illusionary Powder|Illusionary Powders]] and 6 [[Lunar Fragment|Lunar Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Infusion of Illusion]] is returned per craft.

For a simple, alphabetic listing of tier 7 adornments, see Tier 7 Adornments.
