**Londiar Inygad** is the man who hands out the [[Daily Adorning Task|Daily Adorning Tasks]], [[Daily Tinkering Task|Daily Tinkering Tasks]] and the [[Daily Transmuting Task|Daily Transmuting Tasks]]. Somehow, he can be found in all the major cities of Norrath.

His name is an anagram for Daily Adorning.
