## Overview

All throughout [[Norrath]], money-loving goblins that call themselves Gigglegibbers are selling lotto tickets for 10 silver a piece. Bored? Tired? Feeling naughty? Well, step right at up and take a chance at being rich! The rules are as follows:

- Six numbers are chosen ranging from 1 to 36 (top row). If your 6 numbers match the winning numbers (bottom row), you get the jackpot!
  - Match 2 numbers and you win 10 silver.
  - Match 3 numbers and you win 50 silver.
  - Match 4 numbers and you win 2 gold 50 silver.
  - Match 5 numbers and you win 25 gold.
  - Match all 6 numbers and you win the jackpot!
- The jackpot grows larger as more people play.
- Tickets for the game cost 10 silver each.

## Details and Statistics

- Whenever someone wins the jackpot, 6 new different target numbers from 1 to 36 are chosen randomly by the game and a new jackpot is started at 10p.
- Each time you buy a ticket, 3s 70c is added to the jackpot.
- Because the tickets are sold for 10s, the average return ignoring a jackpot is about 5s 26c.
- The long term average return of the game (taking into account the 10p starting jackpot and the amount invested into it for each ticket) is about: 9s 01c.
