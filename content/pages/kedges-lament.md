The wreckage of **Kedge's Lament** lies in the [[Cove of Decay]], her shattered hull the constant target of salvage divers from Bloodsabers who comb the shores as well. It is believed that the ship was originally discovered based on [[Stiletto's Orders|a rumor]] that [[Master T'Lys]] heard that resulted in him sending [[Stiletto|a hired scout]] searching for clues to the whereabouts of information related to the fate of the Legendary [[Manastone]]. One wonders how the divers and the treasure hunters avoid the massive bulk of the [[The Octagorgon]] as they attempt to find treasure from her shattered remains.

**Captain:** [[Captain Ulssissaris]], now a skeletal undead haunting the beach of the cove.<br>
