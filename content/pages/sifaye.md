Sifaye are fairy-like children of Tunare, Goddess of Growth, who inhabited the Wakening Land. Upon learning of the gods' retreat from Norrath, the Sifaye wielded the Scimitar of Emerald Dawn to snip a flowering bud of the Tree of Growth. This bud would be known as the Bloom of Growth, which Tunare entrusted to the dragon Wuoshi. The dragon keeps the Bloom in the shade of the Tree until Rallos Zek's giant wage their attacks, at which point he secrets it away to a hidden place in the Lesser Faydark.

According to Tunare, the Bloom of Growth will always be entwined with the essence of the Sifaye, who sacrificed themselves to preserve a remnant of Tunare in the gods-bereft world.
