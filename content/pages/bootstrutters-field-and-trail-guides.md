Bootstrutter's Field and Trail Guides were written by the gnomish explorer Hasten Bootstrutter. As his supposed cousin [[Hoggle Bogstrutter]] states, "Boostrutter's field guides have proven to be a wealth of undiscovered knowledge and undiscovered riches. This is why the great libraries pay highly for the offer of such books." Below is a compilation of Bootstutter's field and trail guides:
