Apprentice Elder Yelnar is a [[Tradeskill Apprentices|tradeskill apprentice]]

In order to research the recipes this apprentice offers, the crafter must be at least level 90 or above.

## About Yelnar

The quest to obtain this [[Tradeskill Apprentices|tradeskill apprentice]] is [[Etch the Stone]], which is part of a raid instance. It difficult for non-raiders to obtain.

The stone to access this apprentice *is* tradeable and can be found on the broker on rare occasions, but it typically sells for a hefty sum.

- Completing Etch the Stone rewards [[Yelnar's Research Prism]], which must be used to convert it into [[Yelnar's Teleportation Stone]].
  - Yelnar's Teleportation Stone is the actual item used to place the apprentice. While the *prism* is tradeable, the *teleportation stone* is not, because the stone is the actual no-trade apprentice!
- Should you pick it up to move it you can find it in your inventory, Yelnar's Teleportation Stone is a bright blue bubble icon.
- This tradeskill apprentice has a few recipes for [[Housing|furniture]] items with high [[Rent Status Reduction]], making them highly desirable.
- Unlike the standard tradeskill apprentices, you can not coach this apprentice or complete tasks to speed up the research process. The process for these recipes is (typically) much longer than those found on standard tradeskill apprentices.

## Recipes

The recipes one can research is dependent on your general crafting class, rather than your (final) crafting class.

### Craftsman Items

Craftsmen (Carpenters, Provisioners, Woodworkers) can research recipes for the following Draconic Knowledge items:

- Food:
  - [[Draconic Battlepriest's Mending Ale]]
  - [[Draconic Defender's Victory Mead]]
  - [[Draconic Miscreant's Intoxicating Chocolates]]
  - [[Draconic Oracle's Visionary Soup]]
- Wood:
  - [[Channeling Totem of the Archmagus]]
  - [[Cleansing Totem of the Dragon Seer]]
  - [[Dragon Sinew Strung Bow]]
  - [[Unbreakable Dragonbone Guard]]

- Furniture:
  - [[Arcane Dragon Statue, Version 1]]
  - [[Benevolent Dragon Statue, Version 1]]
  - [[Drakota Statue, Version 1]]
  - [[Fierce Dragon Head Statue, Version 1]]
  - [[Noble Dragon Head Statue, Version 1]]

### Outfitter Items

- Weapons:
  - [[Arm of the Draconic Battlepriest]]
  - [[Cleaver of the Draconic Defender]]
  - [[Dirk of the Draconic Miscreant]]
  - [[Sceptre of the Draconic Oracle]]

- Armor:
  - [[Barbute of the Draconic Defender]]
  - [[Boots of the Draconic Miscreant]]
  - [[Gauntlets of the Draconic Battlepriest]]

- Cloth:
  - [[Epaulets of the Draconic Oracle]]
  - [[Gloves of the Draconic Battlepriest]]
  - [[Shroud of the Draconic Miscreant]]
  - [[Skullcap of the Draconic Defender]]

### Scholar Items

Scholars (Alchemist, Jeweler, Sage) can research recipe Draconic Knowledge items:

- Potions:
  - [[Mighty Elixir of Deftness]]
  - [[Mighty Elixir of Fortitude]]
  - [[Mighty Elixir of Intellect]]
  - [[Mighty Elixir of Piety]]

- Charms:

  - [[Totem of the Draconic Defender]]

  - [[Totem of the Draconic Oracle]]

- Jewelery:

  - [[Draconic Defender's Shackle]]
