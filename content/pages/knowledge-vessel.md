**Knowledge vessels** are people in the world of Norath listed as important to the researches of the inhabitants of [[The Void]] on a scroll in [[The Obelisk of Lost Souls]]. The full list of knowledge vessels are :

- [[Fiddy Bobick]]
- [[Markus Jaevins]]
- [[Tobin Starpyre]]
- [[Milea Clothspinner]]
- [[Kyle Bayle]]
- [[Vurgo]]
