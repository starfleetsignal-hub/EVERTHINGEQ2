Tishan's Lockbox provides you with free gear appropriate for your class. This gear serves as a baseline for the equipment that you'll need to begin the quest timeline for that expansion. The lockbox is available for the following expansions:

- [[Tishan's Lockbox (Plane of Magic)]] (Level 100 gear, [[Resolve]] 15)
- [[Tishan's Lockbox (Myrist)]] (Level 110 gear, [[Resolve]] 35)
- [[Tishan's Lockbox (The Blinding)]] (Level 110 gear, [[Resolve]] 145
- [[Tishan's Lockbox (City of Fordel Midst)]] (Level 120 gear, [[Resolve]] 195)
- [[Tishan's Lockbox (Svarni Expanse)]] (Level 120 gear, [[Resolve]] 275)
- [[Tishan's Lockbox (Raj'Dur Plateaus)]] (Level 125 gear, [[Resolve]] 385)
- [[Tishan's Lockbox (Splendor Sky Aerie)]] (Level 125 gear, [[Resolve]] 445)
- [[Tishan's Lockbox (Sodden Archipelago)]] (Level 130 gear, [[Resolve]] 465)
- [[Tishan's Lockbox (Yon Gorroth)]] (Level 130 gear, [[Resolve]] 600)
