**Aaranae Acrimae** has been found in two locations known to date.

Strategy: Aaranae summons adds. She has an AOE, the longer the fight goes on the shorter the break in between AOEs is. If the fight exceeds 7 minutes (possibly nerfed as of LU61) the AOE will become far more powerful and one-shot most of the raid.
