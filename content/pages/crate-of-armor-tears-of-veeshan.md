## Overview

*"Crate of Armor" is the shorthand for the four different crates of armor (Cloth, Leather, Chain, and Plate) awarded from [[Tears of Veeshan]] dungeon missions given by [[Onerind Fe'Dhar]] and [[Anex]] on the [[Vesspyr Isles]]*

These crates award players the direct upgrades to legendary [[Cryptic Armor (Tears of Veeshan)|Cryptic Armors]] (yellow gem).

- Legendary "Arcane" armors (blue gem)
- *and the more powerful* Fabled "Potent" armors (green gem)

Upon completion of the mission, players may CHOOSE which one of the four crates they wish to be awarded (this can be useful for players farming for an alt, as all the armor pieces throughout the progression system are heirloom).

- [[Crate of Cloth Armor]]
- [[Crate of Leather Armor]]
- [[Crate of Chain Armor]]
- [[Crate of Plate Armor]]

## How to acquire

The crates are rewards for completing dungeon missions involving ToV [Heroic] dungeons

Speak to [[Onerind Fe'Dhar]] and [[Anex]] in [[Falinpol]]([[Vesspyr Isles]]) to acquire the proper missions.<br>
- Onerind gives players the unlimited-availability repeatable dungeon Missions for each [Heroic] dungeon<br>
- Anex gives players the 'Daily' Missions for each [Heroic] dungeon (only 1 per day, but awards more [[Draconic Etyma]]). The specific mission chosen cycles in a predictable order each day at 1am server time<br>

Both NPC's mission awards the same Crate of Armor, based on the dungeon the mission directs you to; meaning if you are looking for armor for a specific slot, it will be easy to know which mission to take as it will be the same slot(s) every time for that specific dungeon mission's reward crate.

*Beware: These NPCs also give players the [Advanced Solo] missions, so make sure you are properly indicating which difficulty you wish when speaking with them, it's easy to accidentally pick up the solo versions without noticing, as many of them share the same name general name.*

## What they give

Crates of Armor award either Arcane or Potent armors, based on the difficulty of the dungeon the mission is assigned for.

*Note: Most dungeon's Crate of Armor also has a small chance to award players a special Crate of Ornate Goods that contains a dungeon-specific Fabled "Arcane" or "Potent" armor piece that sacrifices the 'green' STA stat for an extra 'blue' stat. The usefulness of this specific Fabled piece varies from class to class as the blue stats gained may or maynot have a use to your charatcer (melee and caster stats frequently mixed together on that piece)... your mileage may vary*  Not all instances have a 'Fabled' version of an armor drop, but those that do will show it in the list of possible rewards when examining the quest rewards.

### Potent Armor

Potent Armors are primarily acquired from the Crates of Armor in the three Temple of Veeshan [Heroic] instances, as those zones they are designed to be more difficult.

The three Temple of Veeshan instances' crates are also the crates awarded from the raid reward [[High Keep Mission Crate]], ensuring that raiders are only receiving "Potent" armors.

## Armor Trade-ins

As with [[Cryptic Armor (Tears of Veeshan)|Cryptic armors]], duplicate or subpar-stat Arcane and Potent armors may be traded in to the merchant [[Zaszea]] @{{waypoint 32.03, 197.38, 26.17}} in Falinpol.  Each individual piece must be broken down, via a consumable [[Zaszea's Armor Exchange]] (purchased for 5c). This process will return a generic trade token matching the slot of the item that was just consumed.  It takes **7** tokens of a single slot to purchase specific armor pieces from Zaszea.  Trade tokens come in Cryptic, Arcane, and Potent form, depending on the item that was consumed.

It should be noted that while Zaszea handles the trade-ins for all three lower-tier ToV armor types (Cryptic, Arcane and Potent), players CANNOT 'trade-up' from one armor type to another more advanced type, nor can they 'trade-down' to another type.  Arcane only buys Arcane, Potent only buys Potent, etc
