During the quest [[Draco Mortuus vos Liberatio]], you encounter the dragon called **Do'ellin** three times:

1. [[Do'ellin the Young]], at the base of the waterfall on the [[Isle of the Ravasect]] in [[The Bonemire]]
1. [[Do'ellin (subdued)]], in the same spot after you bring [[Do'ellin the Young]] to 10% health or less
1. [[Spirit of Do'ellin]], on [[Gazer Isle]] in [[Tenebrous Tangle]], behind the temple ruins near [[Sinephobis]]
