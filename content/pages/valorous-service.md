## Effects

- Applies ***Marr's Heroism*** on termination.
  - Heals group members.
- Wards caster against (scaling) points of all damage.
- 100% chance to intercept all damage on target. On a successful intercept caster takes 55% of attack damage, target takes 50% of attack damage. Spell ends after 10 successful intercepts.
