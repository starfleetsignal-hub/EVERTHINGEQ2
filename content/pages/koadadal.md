**Koada'Dal** is the language of the High Elves. It can be learned by buying a [[Koada'Dal Language Primer]] from one of the following vendors:

- [[Scribe Hilja Saraste]], [[Kelethin]]
- [[Breego Sunshimmer]], [[Teren's Grasp]], [[Kylong Plains]]
- [[Tumpy]], [[Crow's Resting Place, Thieves Guild]]
- [[Martok K'Fauxx]], [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
- [[Eredykil Zum'tok]], [[Gorowyn]]
- [[Dannon Ramsdell]], [[Frostfang Sea]]
- [[Master Scribe Phonetia]], [[Qeynos Province District]]
- [[Phyrace J'Despath]], [[Wanderlust Fair]] in [[Darklight Wood]]

The language can also be learned by completing the quest [[Knowledge of the Past]], a sub-quest of the second step in [[Wrapping It All Up]], from the [[Peacock Club Timeline]].  (Note:  This quest requires the language, but it is not needed to learn the language.)

## Quest Lines Requiring This Language

- [[A Poem to the Past]], a subquest in the [[Swords of Destiny Timeline]]
- [[Wrapping It All Up]], from the [[Peacock Club Timeline]]
- The [[Wizard Epic Weapon Timeline]]
- the [[Channeler Epic Weapon Timeline]]
