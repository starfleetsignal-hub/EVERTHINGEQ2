![A Zygomid Sporeling](images/Zygomid_Sporeling.jpg)
A mushroom that follows you around, the **Zygomid Sporeling** is a fluff pet cast from the [[Summon Zygomyd Sporeling]] ability.
It is the reward for completing the Arena in [[Sundered Splitpaw: Gladiator's Triumph]].  It does not speak, but it can be "poked" causing it to release spores (a simple graphics effect).

This pet does **not** override your Deity pet, but may override other fluff pets.
