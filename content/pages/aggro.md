- **Aggro**: Abbreviation for **Agression**, also known as **Hate**. Hate is the method used by the EQ2 monster AI to determine who the monster will try to kill. The monster keeps a table of hate points and attacks players in that order. Damage, Healing, Buffs and Debuffs all increase Hate. Some spells and combat arts have special effects that lower Hate.
