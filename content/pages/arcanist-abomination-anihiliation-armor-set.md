**Arcanist Abomination Anihiliation armor set**

- [[Earring of the League Arcanists]]
- [[Signet of the League Arcanists]]
- [[Bracelet of the League Arcanists]]

---

- (3) Applies ***Arcanist Abomination Anihilation***.
  - On a successful hostile spell this spell has a chance to cast Abomination Anihilation on target of spell. This effect will trigger an average of 1.8 times per minute.
    - Inflicts 1130 divine damage on target.
    - If target is vampire

This jewelry is a quest reward for the following quests in the [[Loping Plains]]:
