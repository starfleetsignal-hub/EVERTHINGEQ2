- (2) Applies ***Focus: [[Soulrot VIII]]***.
  - Increases the damage of [[Soulrot VIII]] by 80.
- (3) Applies *****Minion's Resilience*****.
  - Increases pet health by 1250 and all resists by 625.
- (4) Applies ***Focus: [[Lich III]]***.
  - Increases the damage amount of [[Lich III]] by 45.
- (5) +5 Base Spell Damage
  - Applies ***Voidchanneling***.
    - Applies Voidchanneling to qualifying pets
      - On any successful attack this spell has a chance to cast Deathly Netherbolt on target of attack. Lasts for 15.0 seconds. This effect will trigger an average of 2.0 times per minute.
        - Inflicts 497 disease damage on target
        - Inflicts 497 poison damage on target
        - Decreases Mitigation of target vs all noxious damage by 1248.
- (6) Applies ***Dimensional Force***.
  - Increases Crit Bonus of both pet and caster by 10.0

This armor may be worn only by: [[Necromancer|Necromancers]].
