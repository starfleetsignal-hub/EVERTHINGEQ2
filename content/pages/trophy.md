A **trophy** is an in-game item that can be placed in a [[Housing|house]] or [[Guild Halls|guild hall]]. Two types of trophies can be acquired:

- [[Raid Trophies]], which drop from named monsters in various raid zones in [[Norrath]]
- Lore and Legend Trophies, rewarded when players complete Lore and Legend quests after learning about specific types of monsters in game
