## Effects

- Applies ***Final Judgment Immunity***. Lasts for 30.0 seconds.
- Inflicts magic damage to target equal to 50% of the casters total Health. **Final Judgment** damage cannot exceed 50% of the target's maximum Health.
- Must be hated by your current target.
- The Tribunal do not allow for judgment to be handed down on a being that has already had judgment done upon them recently.
