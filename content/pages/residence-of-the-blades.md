**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** Looted from Legends of Norrath, Priestess of Anarchs card packs
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** medium, [[Maj'Dul]]-themed
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** none allowed

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- You must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- There are **no** recurring upkeep costs ("rent") of any kind.
- You can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- You can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Residence of the Blades is similar in style to the various faction buildings found in Maj'Dul. It has pale orange and cream tiles and large, fixed support columns.

The entry is on the lower level and two stairwells bear the back take you to a second floor that overlooks the first. The rear wall opposite of the entrance has climbable areas on the side which can be used to reach a small balcony on a third level.

## Item Count

*Items counts have changed once and can change again at any time.*

- 900 items and 900 [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]]

## Notes

- You **cannot** buy this house directly from the [[Marketplace]], it is random loot from Legends of Norrath (LoN) loot packs. However, you can purchase a [[Legends of Norrath Loot: Season Two]] pack which gives you a chance to obtain the deed. Rewards from the loot packs are random and it is not guaranteed you will receive a deed.

- Even though you can not buy the deed outright, this house can be toured by visiting general portal locations in Freeport and Qeynos (locations given above)
