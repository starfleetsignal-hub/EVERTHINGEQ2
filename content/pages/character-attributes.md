There are five **Character Attributes** which are fundamental to the capabilities of any character: Strength (STR), Stamina (STA), Agility (AGI), Intelligence (INT) and Wisdom (WIS).

## Scope

- **Strength**: Strength represents your character's physical strength. It affects a Fighter character's ability to deliver damage. Strength also affects the maximum Power pool for [[Fighter|fighters]].

- **Agility**: Agility is a measure of your character's nimbleness and dexterity. It affects a Scout character's ability to deliver damage. Agility also affects the maximum Power pool for [[Scout|Scouts]].

- **Stamina**: Stamina is your character's fortitude. It affects your maximum Health pool with 1 point being 10 health. Basically the more Stamina the more Health you have and the more hits you can take. Stamina is very important to everyone.

- **Intelligence**: Intelligence represents your character's powers of reasoning and overall intellect. As Intelligence increases, the damage for mage spells (min - max) increases as well. Intelligence also affects the maximum power pool for [[Mage|Mages]].

- **Wisdom**: Wisdom is a measure of your character's spiritual strength and willpower. Wisdom also affects the maximum power pool and potential damage for [[Priest|priests]].
