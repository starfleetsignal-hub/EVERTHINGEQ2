**Account Flags** in *EverQuest II* are internal markers used by the game to record permanent or semi-permanent progress on a player's account. These flags act as a **gating tool** — controlling access to certain items, features, zones, or quests. They are automatically set or updated when the player completes specific objectives, owns expansions, or meets certain account-wide conditions.

## Overview

Account Flags serve as a method for the game to track what content a player has unlocked without relying on per-character progression. Once a flag is granted, it typically applies to all characters on that account and sometimes even across all servers (depending on the flag type).

Common uses for Account Flags include:

- Granting access to expansion or adventure pack content.
- Unlocking special merchants or quest lines.
- Allowing account-wide access to unique features, such as [[Mercenaries]] or [[Mounts]].
- Managing entry into restricted or progression-locked zones.

## Function

Account Flags are usually set automatically when certain milestones are reached. These can include:

- Completing a signature or heritage quest.
- Purchasing or registering an expansion or feature pack.
- Receiving an achievement that is considered "Account-WIde"
- Logging in during special events or claiming promotional rewards.

Once the flag is stored on the account, any new characters created afterward can benefit from it (if the flag applies globally). For example, unlocking access to a zone such as Western Wastes or gaining access to Panda x3 rewards would apply to all characters once the account flag is set.

## Examples

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Quest Progression** | Certain signature quests, such as Visions of Vetrovia, will grant an account flag to state you have completed the signature line. |
| **Event Participation** | Seasonal or one-time events (like the *Days of Summer* or *Panda Quests*) set account flags for ongoing reward eligibility. |

## Persistence and Visibility

Account Flags are stored server-side and persist through character deletions, server merges, or renames. Players typically cannot view these flags directly in the UI. However, they can sometimes infer flag status when:

- A quest giver offers or withholds a dialogue option.
- A merchant allows or denies purchase of an item.
- A zone portal displays an “Access Denied” or “You do not meet the zone eligibility requirements” message.

In rare cases, customer support staff may confirm whether an account flag is active if access issues arise.

## Viewing Your Flags

To view your Account Flags, open your Character window (C) and go to 'Account Flags' in the left-hand side.
