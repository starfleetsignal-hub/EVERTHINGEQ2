**Warlord's Unyielding armor set**

- [[Warlord's Unyielding Barbute]]
- [[Warlord's Unyielding Breastplate]]
- [[Warlord's Unyielding Gauntlets]]
- [[Warlord's Unyielding Greaves]]
- [[Warlord's Unyielding Pauldrons]]
- [[Warlord's Unyielding Sabatons]]
- [[Warlord's Unyielding Vambraces]]

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

This is the [[Destiny of Velious|DoV]] Armor Set (Tier 1) for [[Berserker|Berserkers]] that can be obtained via the [[Primal Velium Shard Mission System]].

These pieces are available for vendor purchase from [[Rotuk]] at {{waypoint 1358, -540, 503}} designated as the <Armorsmith> in [[Great Divide]] at [[Thurgadin Harbor]].
They can also be created by crafters.

Note that the [[Warlord's Unyielding Breastplate]] only drops from a mob. It can not be purchased or crafted.
You can create it from a [[Thickened Crystalline Breastplate]] wich drops from [[Grand Restorer Lingwal]] in [[Hold of Rime: The Fortress Spire]]
