**Woodworking tables** are used by [[Carpenter|Carpenters]], [[Woodworker|Woodworkers]]. Some recipe books still list these stations as "sawhorses." In addition to each of the traditional tradeskill zones, free-standing woodworking tables can be found in the following locations:

## Qeynos

- [[North Qeynos]]: Fhara's Furnishings, Sneed's Trading Post
- [[South Qeynos]]: Maile's Exchange, Bag n'Barrel
- [[Qeynos Harbor]]: The Harbor Cache
- [[The Elddar Grove]]: In Range

## Freeport

- [[North Freeport]]: Temple of War
- [[East Freeport]]: Hallard's Bolt & Arrow, Armor by Ikthar, The Emporium
- [[West Freeport]]: Gurb and Ironstar's or [[Coalition Crafter's Association (POI)|Coalition Crafter's Association]] (the crafting area, not just the instance) at {{waypoint 30, -12, 96}}.
- [[South Freeport]]: Block and Tackle

## Faydwer

- [[Kelethin]]: Tunare's Pages
- [[Loping Plains]]: Village of Somborn, next to [[Konrad Sebestyen]] the Lumberjack

## Gorowyn/Timorous Deep

- [[Timorous Deep]]: [[The Wards]] {{waypoint 2787, 122, 1256}}

## Neriak

- [[Indigo Hollow]]: {{waypoint -20, 20, 30}}
- [[Neriak Down Under]]: {{waypoint -167, -3, 295}}

## Kunark

- [[Teren's Grasp]]: [[Hall of the Crafters]]
- [[Fens of Nathsar]]: on Sathir's Span between two of the buildings

## Other Zones

- [[Haven]]: [[Ekroosik's Replicators]] at {{waypoint 18, -32, -163}} and {{waypoint 13, -32, -170}}
- [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]]: on the ice floes
