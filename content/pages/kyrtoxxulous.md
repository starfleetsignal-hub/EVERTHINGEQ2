The Father of Pestilence, Kyrtoxxulous is the ruler of the Demi-Plane of Pestilence. Little else is known about him at this time.

He has not yet appeared in EQ2, but is mentioned in various quests and items, most notably during the [[Warlock Epic Weapon Timeline|Warlock Epic Weapon Questline]].
