The Isle of Mara is a home to a quiet little village, a trading port, and deep dark secrets. It is accessible from any [[Travel in Norrath|Globe]] you find on any dock in the world.

The [[The Village of Shin|Village of Shin]] is home to the great tradeskill lineages of Augren, Ventur, and Nurwin. The Monks of the [[Tower of the Four Winds|Four Winds]] over look the village from their tower. The Far Seas Trading Company has set up a small port city across the river.

Up the river leads to a calm forest area, leading to a series of ancient ruins guarded by timeless sentinels, and even deeper to tainted lands and submerged ruins overrun with dark adversaries.

If you travel to the area behind the water fall, you'll discover an additional zone.

## Quest Series and More

- Various Tradeskill daily missions: This zone became the home of the [[Far Seas Supply Division (Faction)]], which offers a variety of quests, gear, and other goodies of note to those who take up a [[Tradeskill|trade]]. You will find NPCs in the area who give a variety of tradeskill quests.
  - NPCs for the faction also sell a wide variety of items to those who have adequate faction and [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]].
- Gather quest series: The [[A Gathering Obsession Timeline]] centers around this zone and it's easy to love/hate NPC protagonist, [[Qho]].
- Collection quest: Those who take up carpentry will find the pages of the [[Nurwin Family Secrets (Recipe Book)]] in this zone, which offers recipes that look like the furniture seen in the zone.

## Zone History

- At one time, this zone was part of an "adventure pack" ([[Fallen Dynasty Timeline|Fallen Dynasty]]) that was sold independent of the normal expansions, in a manner not unlike DLC (Downloadable Content) seen in contemporary games and sold through websites like Steam.
- Years later the content with included for free with the [[TSO|Shadow Odysessy]] expansion; access was granted to all accounts and the zone's use was built upon.
