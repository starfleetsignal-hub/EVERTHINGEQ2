- When target takes any damage this spell will cast Six Feet Under on target. Lasts for 20.0 seconds
  - Grants a total of 1 trigger of the spell
  - Inflicts 1639 disease damage on target
    - Certain creature strengths only
    - If under 20% Health
  - Inflicts 1549 disease damage on target
    - If target is stronger than Standard
    - If under 8% Health
  - Inflicts 1549 disease damage on target
    - If target is Weak
    - If under 40% Health
