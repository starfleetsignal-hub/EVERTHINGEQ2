A worried halfling appears in game on occasion when things flare up around the state of [[Erollisi Marr]]'s fate. One such halfling appeared in [[A worried halfling (SQ)|South Qeynos]] as the fate of Erollisi first began to unfold. Another now appears in [[A worried halfling (Ant)|Antonica]] just outside the [[North Qeynos]] gates, as more information has unfolded about the [[Shard of Love]].
