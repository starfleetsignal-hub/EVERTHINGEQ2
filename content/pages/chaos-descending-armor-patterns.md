Heroic and Raid instances drop various tiers of armor patterns. These are progressive: before you can get the T2 armor, you must already have "unlocked" that slot by obtaining a T1 armor pattern for the same slot, and trading it to [[Ambar]] in the Myrist [[Merchants' Gallery]] at {{waypoint 663.77, 411.69, -168.22}} along with the slot-appropriate gems, plat, and status.

Alternatively, you can earn an item unlock (eg. [[CD Armor Voucher: Deathless Earthcrafted Chest]]) whch grants you access to that item only. These can be purchased from [[Radek]] in the Myrist [[Merchants' Gallery]] at {{waypoint 667.01, 411.69, -170.68}}.Once you have unlocked an item, either buy buying it with a pattern or via unlocker, you can purchase these items with any character on the same account from Radek.

## Note

Having acquired any of the Deathless, Destruction, or Enlightenment armor via a pattern for a slot will allow you to obtain any of the next tier's armor for that slot with the appropriate pattern. (i.e., you don't need the T1 Enlightenment leg armor to get the T2 Enlightenment leg armor, you could instead to get T2 Deathless leg armor, etc.)

As of June, 2019 you no longer need to have all of the lesser patterns to get an upgrade.

## Gems

Gems are obtained by completing missions:

- Daily Heroic and Event Heroic mission reward 2 gems.
- Weekly Event Heroic Missions reward 3 gems.
- Weekly T1 and T2 Heroic Missions reward 5 gems.
- Weekly T1, T2, and T3 Raid Missions reward 15 gems.

**Gem Slots**

- [[Celestial Alexandrite]] -- *helm*
