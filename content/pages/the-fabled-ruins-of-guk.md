The Fabled Ruins of Guk is a series of instances in [[Plane of Magic]] that were introduced as part of the "Return to Guk" update (LU107) and based on the original Guk dungeons from [[The Shadow Odyssey]].  Access these zones via the [[Plane of Magic]]

- [[The Fabled Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen (Solo)|The Fabled Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen [Solo]]]
- [[The Fabled Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen (Heroic)|The Fabled Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen [Heroic]]]
- [[The Fabled Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen (Tradeskill Mission)|The Fabled Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen [Tradeskill Mission]]]

<br>*completing the above gives access to*

- [[The Fabled Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors (Solo)|The Fabled Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors [Solo]]]
- [[The Fabled Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors (Heroic)|The Fabled Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors [Heroic]]]

<br>*completing the above gives access to*
