## [[Paladin]] Abilities

**Attributes**

- MAXIMUM = (player-level)*15+20 with diminishing returns over *10+20
- Level 50 = 770
- Level 60 = 920
- Level 70 = 1070
- Level 80 = 1220
- Level 95 = 1445

**Health**

- 1 STA = 5 health

**Power**

- 1 STR = 2.5-2.6 power
- 1 WIS = 1.7-1.8 power (up from 0.8-0.9)

**Offense**

- 10 STR = 1.25-1.4% increase in CA damage
- 10 INT = 1.25-1.4% increase in spell damage

**Defense**

- MAXIMUM Mitigation or Resistance = (player-level)*150
- EFFECTIVE Mitigation = (mitigation)*(item-level)/(player-level)
- Level 50 = 7,500
- Level 60 = 9,000
- Level 70 = 10,500 (4,000 before diminishing returns)
- Level 80 = 12,000 (4,560 before diminishing returns)
- Level 95 = 14,250

**Avoidance and Resistance Modifiers**

- 10 AGI = 0.5% additional total avoidance
- 10 Defense = 3% additional total avoidance

**Haste**

- MAXIMUM = 200
