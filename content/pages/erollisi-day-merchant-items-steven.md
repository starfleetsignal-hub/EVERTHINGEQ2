- Steven is a merchant that appears during the [[Erollisi Day]] festival and sells only new items introduced in the current year.
- [[The City of Freeport]] in front of [[Seafarer's Roost]] around {{waypoint -210, -56, -4}}
- [[Qeynos Province District]] in front of [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn]] around {{waypoint 921, -23, 65}}
- The second event merchant [[Douglas]] has all the holiday items from previous years.

Items can be purchased with standard currency or [[Erollisi coin|Erollisi Coins]].

- [[Crimson Owlbear Chick]] for 20 x [[Erollisi coin]]
- [[Kamapor Cloak of Love]] for 10 x [[Erollisi coin]]

- [[Razzmatazz Nethernaught Ray Plushie]] for 5 x [[Erollisi coin]]
- [[Rose Door of Paixao, Left]] for 5 x [[Erollisi coin]]
- [[Rose Door of Paixao, Right]] for 5 x [[Erollisi coin]]
- [[Shy Violet Fae Drake]] for 20 x [[Erollisi coin]]
- [[Tholuxe Paells House Curtains]] for 5 x [[Erollisi coin]]
