**Stoves and Kegs** are used by [[Provisioner|Provisioners]]. In addition to each of the traditional tradeskill zones, free-standing stoves and kegs can be found in the following locations:

## Qeynos

- [[North Qeynos]]: Removed from Voleen Bakery
- [[South Qeynos]]: Lion's Mane Inn and Tavern, Food on Foot
- [[Qeynos Harbor]]: Fish's Alehouse
- [[The Elddar Grove]]: Antonica's House of Pottery

## Freeport

- [[North Freeport]]: Jade Tiger Inn
- [[East Freeport]]: Seafarers Roost, Hallard's Bolt & Arrow
- [[West Freeport]]: Backalley Brewhouse, Blood Haze Inn and [[Coalition Crafter's Association (POI)]] (the crafting area, not just the instance) at {{waypoint 45, -12, 85}}.

## Faydwer

- [[Kelethin]]: Library; Joleena's Restaurant; Tunare's Pages
- [[Steamfont Mountains]]: Ak'Anon Inn

## Gorowyn/Timorous Deep

- [[Timorous Deep]]: [[The Wards]] {{waypoint 2761, 126, 1221}}

## Neriak

- [[Neriak, City of Hate]]: [[Neriak Down Under]] {{waypoint -191, -4, 261}} and at {{waypoint -178, -4, 266}}

## Kunark

- [[Teren's Grasp]]: [[Hall of the Crafters]]
- [[Fens of Nathsar]]: on Sathir's Span in one of the buildings

## Obol Plains

- [[Cardin Ward]]: {{waypoint -300, 12, -38}}

## Other Zones

- [[Haven]]: [[Ekroosik's Replicators]] at {{waypoint 34, -33, -176}} and {{waypoint 40, -33, -155}}
- [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]]: on the ice flows
