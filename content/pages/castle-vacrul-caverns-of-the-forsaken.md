- [[Syperia the Shackled]] {{waypoint -154, -73, 264}} has a dot/daze trauma detriment. spawns *bloodless chains* shackle adds

- *an allelopath blightsnail* use to lift curse

- [[Zarrakon]] {{waypoint -287, -82, 221}} monlogues, then flies away leaving linked encounter [[Bonewretch the Expelled and Skelegore the Regurgitated]]. Has knockback and ports player to pits of bile. Each has different detriment.

- Take door 'to the craw' {{waypoint -32, -63, 195}} Go up the stairs.

- [[Mazza Zanzeer]] {{waypoint 20, -15, 172}} Has a zombie pet Porzo Kel'Kanna. Summons several Sullian fayefolk adds that cast damage AoE DOT + Potency trauma detriment on the player.

- Go back down the stairs all the way to the bottom. Watch reanimated Vishra run off and kill the two vampires standing there. Go through the "Leave the craw"(5,-58,156) door to "The Royal Blood Halls". Proceed through the halls to "Cruor Keep" Kill all the royal guard vampires to spawn the named.

- [[Lysander Mistmoore]] {{waypoint -109.90, -45.56, 0.25}} Casts a damage AoE DOT+ daze arcane on player. Animates the gargoyles around the room to call in as adds. Allowing multiple gargoyles to become active will cause a party wipe.

- If the 'watchful eye' mobs makes it to the chasm beyond the hallway, each will spawn a colony of several bat adds. Root and snare effects work on these.

- Stand on the red dais {{waypoint -189.38, -61.68, -74.29}} and click the 'Dumbwaiter delivery' statue to take you across.

- [[Vorigan Mistmoore]] Wanders around area {{waypoint -192.31, -62.22, -165.58}} Hail to start fight. Calls in adds.
  - He has 2 blood pools that will place a curse of Sanguinated Sewage on the player.
  - He gains counters of Glutton for Punishment that increases offensive and defensive capabilities with each increment. Dispel works on these, but they stack quickly.
  - Periodically, he'll spin around and drop blood boils on the floor. Step on one to receive a 'detriment' Vampiric Bloodburst. It will heal him for each player that has afflicted themselves, but it also dispels 200 levels of hostile effects (Glutton for Punishment). >>>Sanguinated Sewage curse prevents the heal to the named and the noxious damage to the player while removing his Glutton for Punishment stacks.
  - Periodically, he will cast Vampiric Hemorrhage. This is a curable trauma that has a huge KB effect until it's cured or expired, but can be protected against with the Sanguinated Sewage curse from the blood pool.
- TLDR Version: Mob starts spinning, step in blood pool, get curse, step on as many blood boils as possible. Mob starts casting Vampiric Hemorrhage, get hit by spell, curse is removed. Repeat. Kill adds in between or ignore them and kill named.
