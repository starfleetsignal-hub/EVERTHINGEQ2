## Overview

This timeline gives details about the Account unlock items as part of the Renewal of Ro expansion.

*Available after completing the [[Renewal of Ro Timeline#Renewal of Ro Signature Quest|Renewal of Ro Adventure Signature Line]]*

Items will unlock once obtained by one character on each account. You must have the necessary currency in order to see the items for purchase.
