- [[Sea salt encrusted helm]]

- [[Sea salt encrusted gauntlets]]
- [[Sea salt encrusted legplates]]

Pieces are random drops by all mobs in [[The Thundering Steppes]].<br>
Pieces in this set have the same appearance and coloring.<br>
No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.
