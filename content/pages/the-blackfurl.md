![](images/Blackfurlpirateship.jpg)

The Blackfurl berths just off the shores of the beach nearest [[The Forest Ruins]] outside of [[The Baubbleshire]] and [[The Willow Wood]]. The name of the ship is speculative based on the name of the pirates combing the beaches and invading the ruined mage tower and surrounding keep in the forest clearing. The crew of this ship frequently dies at the hands of [[The Basalt Watcher]] within the keep, yet this hasn't stopped their pillaging efforts.
