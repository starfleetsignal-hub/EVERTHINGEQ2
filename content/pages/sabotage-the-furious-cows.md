This is one of a group of quests in series 1 of a 3-series quest line in the [[Freeport Sabotage Quest Timeline]].

## Starting the Quest

Speak to [[Gil McMartin]] in the Commonlands (-1030, -50, -11) outside the West Freeport gates.

## Steps

1. Kill infected cows.  They are found in a wide area south of the crossroads (before the Dreg camp), and also northwest of the crossroads (before the East Nomad camp).
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

Coin
