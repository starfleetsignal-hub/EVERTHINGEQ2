Many utilities exist that are designed specifically to help [[Guild|guilds]] create, customize, and manage information about their confederation.

**IMPORTANT:** these links are provided as a guide to users only; we don't endorse or support any of these sites or services.

## Guild Hosting Services

Guild Hosting is provided by companies that tailor web applications to gamers. Their hosting plans typically offer out-of-the-box functionality with popular features such as content management, forums, rosters, polls, and image galleries. Some services offer game-specific features. These services range from free to ad-free service on a monthly, quarterly, or yearly charge.

- [BattleStandards](http://battlestandards.com)
- [Guild Launch](http://www.guildlaunch.com/) ([features list](http://www.guildlaunch.com/order/compare.php))
- [Guild Portal](http://www.GuildPortal.com/) ([features list](http://www.guildportal.com/Features.aspx))
- [Gamers Within](http://gamerswithin.com)  ([terms of service](http://gamerswithin.com/index.php?Action=terms))beta service offering

## Content Management Systems

### Nuke

- [http://phpnuke.org/](http://phpnuke.org/)
- [http://www.postnuke.com/](http://www.postnuke.com/)

The different flavors of Nuke support Forums, polls, articles, reviews, users, and many other features useful for Guilds to create their own system just for their own guild.

### Joomla

- [http://www.joomla.org/](http://www.joomla.org/)

Joomla is an open source content management system. It can be used for everything from simple websites to complex corporate applications. Joomla claims to be easy-to-install, simple-to=manage, and reliable. A large support network exists on the internet for Joomla.
