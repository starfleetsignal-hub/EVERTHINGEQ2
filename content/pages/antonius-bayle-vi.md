**Appellation:** The Great Healer

**Forebear:** [[Antonius Bayle V]]

**Progeny:** [[Antonius Bayle VII]]

**Time of Rule:** [[The Age of Cataclysms]]

**History:** Nothing of any substance has been reported by any official SOE resource regarding this ruler of Qeynos. It can be assumed that he, like his predecessors, obeyed the will of the Council of Qeynos and wed and bore at least one male son, who was named Antonius in keeping with tradition.
