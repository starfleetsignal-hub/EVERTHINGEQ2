The ooze crawler replaces your tank, mage or scout pet. It will not act in addition to the primary pets.

## Effects of Ooze

The ooze crawler will have the following AoE effects:

- Applies Ooze Slime. Lasts for 10.0 seconds.
  - Decreases Damage Per Second and Attack Speed of targets in Area of Effect by 10.0
    - If level 10 or higher
  - Slows targets in Area of Effect by 20 %
    - If level 10 or higher
  - Decreases Subjugation, Disruption, Ordination, Ranged, Focus, Slashing, Piercing and Crushing of targets in Area of Effect by 25
    - If level 24 or higher
  - Causes target's spells to become more easily resisted by 5 %
    - If level 38 or higher
  - Causes target's combat arts to miss 10 more often
    - If level 38 or higher
  - Reduces target's spell and combat art damage by 5 %
    - If level 52 or higher
  - Targets in Area of Effect will reduce the chance for their primary weapon's autoattact to affect multiple targets by 25 %
    - If level 52 or higher
  - Decreases Riposte Damage of targets in Area of Effect by 25 %.
    - If level 66 or higher
  - Reduces radius by 15 %
    - If level 66 or higher
  - Reduces Double Attack chance of targets in Area of Effect by 25
    - If level 66 or higher
  - Must be engaged in combat

These effects were recorded with a level 80 Necromancer.
