**Housing Type:** [[Prestige Housing]]
**Obtained:** Only with the purchase of Collectors Edition or Premium Edition of Terrors of Thalumbra expansion
**Available Locations:**

- [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -115, -18, 271}} for evil-aligned characters
- [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 734, -19, 199}} for good-aligned characters
- **Quantity Limit:** one per character
- **House Style/Theme:** island
- **[[Mount]] Use Within:** ground or flying allowed

## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- You must get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- It has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- You can get a [[House Portal|portal]] to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]. The portal allows quick travel between each location.
- You can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button.

## About This House

Dhalgar Precipice of the Deep is an island topped with natural stone tower. At its peak you will find a [[Maldura]]-themed dwarven head carved in the stone.

Though looks like most of the tower was formed by ocean winds, it does have a areas with tile floors and walls. To reach some of those areas, you will need a flying mount or a [[Bag of Teleportation Pads|bag of teleportation pads]]. See the [[Housing Breakout Guide]] for details.

The lower area has one round room inside the tower and three platforms that reach out into the sea. The upper area (accessed by flying mount or teleportation pads) has a round room with one large platform and two small that offer views of the sea and the areas below.

## Building Blocks

- The [[Dhalgar Home Builder's Kit]] is a recipe book with [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]]-like items that was also included with the purchase of the Collector's or Premium edition of the Terrors of Thalumbra. The recipe book includes tiles, walls, stairs, roof pieces and more.
