The **Festival of Discord** is an annual [[Player Events|player-run event]] on the [[Antonia Bayle (Server)|Antonia Bayle server]]. The festival began in 2007 as a [[Roleplay]] event celebrating the darker deities.

Traditionally held in early November, the festival runs for seven days and features social events and contests in the [[Commonlands]].
