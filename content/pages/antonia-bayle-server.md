**Antonia Bayle** is an [[Roleplay|RP Preferred]] server, named after [[Queen Antonia Bayle]] of Qeynos.  It also happens to have one of the larger server populations which attracts people in itself. If interested in the roleplaying, try hanging out in the taverns in town and you should meet others interested in more active roleplaying. There is a custom chat channel, RPEvents, for announcements and information about roleplaying events. You can also check out the [Guide and Player Events forum](http://forums.station.sony.com/eq2/index.php?forums/guide-and-player-events.24/) for social events being hosted by the guides or players on all servers, including Antonia Bayle.

*If you are a resident of this server, please feel free to add your Guild and Player Name to the sections, below!*
