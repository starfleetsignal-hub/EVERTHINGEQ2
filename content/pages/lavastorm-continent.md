![An image of Lavastorm as appears on the world map.](images/Lavastorm6.png)
Lavastorm is the remnant of the Lavastorm Mountains from [[Antonica (Continent)|Old Antonica]], which was located in the northeast corner of the supercontinent. During the [[The Rending|Rending]], the Lavastorm Mountains were ripped from its neighboring regions and most, if not close to all, of it's own mountain-ranges were utterly destroyed, sinking into the newly created seas. Today it is composed of just one zone, [[Lavastorm]], with a [[Mount Solusek|giant volcano]] at its center holding the dungeon, [[Solusek's Eye]], within it.

![An image of Lavastorm as appears on the world map, zoomed in.](images/Map_lavastorm.jpg)
The environment of Lavastorm is ash-laden and coursing with lava. Every creature found here has adapted to the unforgiving ecosystem, by either absorbing fire into its very essence as with the [[A magma worm|magma worms]], or through thick-skin as with the Sootfoot Goblins. Beings of pure flame, flame spirits, have also risen from the pooling, effervescent lava.

Lavastorm has been blessed by [[Solusek Ro (Deity)|Solusek Ro]], god of the sun, and by extension [[Fennin Ro]], god of fire. Many POIs found here pay homage to the two, in particular Solusek. Furthermore, there existed an ancient order on Lavastorm that used to inhabit the [[Temple of Solusek Ro]]. The Burning Prince so greatly empowered these followers that when their order eventually collapsed the remnants went on to form the current great magical communities of the world: the Order of Flame, the [[The Lost Legend of Lavastorm|Burning Dead]], the [[Library of Light]], the Academy of Arcane Science, and the Concordium.* - (Source: [[Pallasa Rargon]])*

A major theme of Lavastorm is the plight of both the Sootfoot Goblins and the Order of Flame against the forces of the Void. Additionally, the aftermath of a great battle between the drakota and the dragon-lord [[Lord Nagafen (Nagafen's Lair)|Nagafen]] can still be seen throughout the northern parts of the zone. Nagafen survived the engagement and currently resides deep within [[Solusek's Eye]].
