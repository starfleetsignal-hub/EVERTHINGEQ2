**Feral Strength armor set**

- [[Breastplate of Feral Strength]]
- [[Pauldrons of Feral Strength]]
- [[Bracers of Feral Strength]]
- [[Gauntlets of Feral Strength]]
- [[Greaves of Feral Strength]]
- [[Sabatons of Feral Strength]]

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

This armor may be worn only by [[Berserker]]s, and is obtained by completing the [[Ian's Forgetful Family - Part I|Ian's Forgetful Family series]] in Qeynos harbor or [[Custom Fitted Armor - Part I|Custom Fitted Armor series]] in East Freeport of the [[Armor Quest Timeline]].
