![A jewelry box sale's display. No matter what tier (level rare wood) used, all jewelry boxes look the same when placed in a house.](images/Jewelry_Display_Placed.png)
If you are not familiar with jewelry boxes, you may want to read about the general topic of [[Sales Display|sales displays]] that can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]].

- All jewelry boxes are Master Crafted (made from a rare material) by those who take up the [[Carpenter]] tradeskill.
- This sales display holds a limited scope of items that are typically [[Jeweler]] made, like jewelry, baubles (charms), adornments, transmuted components.
