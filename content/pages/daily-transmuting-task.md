## Description

The daily transmuting task is a infinite repeatable quest that you can perform once every 18 hours. The quest offers you an opportunity to skill up your [[Transmuting]] skill for free.

[[Londiar Inygad]] also offers a [[Daily Adorning Task]] and a [[Daily Tinkering Task]] now.

## How To Start The Quest

![Londiar Inygad looks the same in all the cities.](images/Londiar_Inygad_%28New_Halas%29.png)
To begin the daily adorning task you must speak to [[Londiar Inygad]] who is located in any of the major cities through out Norrath. (He must be a doppleganger of some sort!)

- [[Freeport]]  Loc {{waypoint 23.8, -12.5, 93}}
- [[Gorowyn]] Loc {{waypoint 2790, 121, 1239}}
- [[Kelethin]] Loc {{waypoint 457, 91, 332}}
- [[Neriak]] Loc {{waypoint -184, -3, 275}}
- [[New Halas]] Loc {{waypoint -392, 176, -157}}
- [[Qeynos]] Loc {{waypoint 345, -17, -87}}

The daily transmuting task may also be obtained from [[Dercin Marrbrand]] in

- [[Myrist, the Great Library]] {{waypoint 651, 411, 17}}

## The Daily Tasks

The quest you receive varies depending upon your skill level for transmuting and is not based off your adventure or tradeskill level.

Each task requires you to use your transmuting ability on the items provided. You are provided with a chest that you can unpack to recieve 10 items which you can transmute.

**The items you receive is based on your skill level:**

- - Transmuting Skill 25 and Lower
- - Transmuting Skill 25 to 75
- - Transmuting Skill 75 to 125
- - Transmuting Skill 125 to 175
- - Transmuting Skill 175 to 225
- - Transmuting Skill 225 to 275
- - Transmuting Skill 275 to 325
- - Transmuting Skill 325 to 375
- [[A worn pair of boots]] (95 Treasured) - Transmuting Skill 425 and greater

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience. Amount Received Scales to your Tradeskill Level.
- 1 [[City Token]]
- +300 [[Faction]] with your local Tradeskill Guild.
