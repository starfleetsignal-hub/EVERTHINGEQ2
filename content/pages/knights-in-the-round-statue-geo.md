This is a master list of the the statuettes needed for the quest [[Knights in the Round]], part of the [[Swords of Destiny Timeline]]. The list is ordered according to geographic location, for efficieny purposes. Regardless of where you choose to start, this quest-step will be tedious and will take you across the entire [[Norrath|world]].
