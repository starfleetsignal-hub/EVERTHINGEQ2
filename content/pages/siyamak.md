This game character exists several forms:

- [[Siyamak (Maj'Dul)]] -- The level 64 epic X 4 dragon form of the NPC Barakah in [[Maj'Dul]].
- [[Siyamak (NPC)]] -- The NPC in [[Maj'Dul]].
- [[Siyamak (Pillars of Flame)]] -- The level 64 ^^^ epic X 4 dragon in [[The Pillars of Flame]].
- [[Siyamak of the Night]] -- The level 64 epic X 4 dragon in [[The Pedestal of Sky]].

## Notes

For the quest [[Capturing Day and Night]], you can receive a quest update by killing either [[Siyamak (Maj'Dul)]] or [[Siyamak (Pillars of Flame)]].
