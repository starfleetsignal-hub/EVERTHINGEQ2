**Rolled Discordmail armor set**

- [[Rolled Discordmail Coif]]
- [[Rolled Discordmail Coat]]
- [[Rolled Discordmail Gloves]]
- [[Rolled Discordmail Boots]]

All these pieces are obtained from the guild city raids, after turn in of the Fine (For Head, Feet and Hand slots) and Ornate (for Leg and Chest slots) chainmail molds.<br>
There are no bonuses derived from wearing multiple members of this set.
