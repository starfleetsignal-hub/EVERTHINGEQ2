The table below details the **Tier 5 Adornments** organized by the slots to which they apply:

**Lesser** adornments appear with no highlighting. They require 1 [[Spectral Powder]] and 6 [[Ruby Fragment|Ruby Fragments]].  Up to 3 [[Ruby Fragment|Ruby Fragments]] are returned per craft.

**Greater** adornments appear with brown highlighting. They require 1 [[Infusion of Nightmares]], 2 [[Spectral Powder|Spectral Powders]], and 6 [[Ruby Fragment|Ruby Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Spectral Powder]] is returned per craft.

**Superior** adornments appear with red highlighting. They require 1 [[Natural Mana]], 2 [[Infusion of the Void|Infusions of Nightmares]], 3 [[Spectral Powder|Spectral Powders]] and 6 [[Ruby Fragment|Ruby Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Infusion of Nightmares]] is returned per craft.

For a simple, alphabetic listing of tier 5 adornments, see Tier 5 Adornments.
