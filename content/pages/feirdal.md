**Feir'Dal** is the language of the Wood Elves.
Can be learned by purchasing a [[Feir'Dal Language Primer]] from:

- [[Scribe Hilja Saraste]], [[Kelethin]]
- [[Eredykil Zum'tok]], [[Gorowyn]]
- [[Martok K'Fauxx]], [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
- [[Master Scribe Phonetia]], [[Qeynos Province District]]
- [[Dannon Ramsdell]], [[Frostfang Sea]]
- [[Phyrace J'Despath]], [[Wanderlust Fair]] in [[Darklight Wood]]
- [[Breego Sunshimmer]], [[Teren's Grasp]], [[Kylong Plains]]
