Update Notes for May 14, 2013

This downtime occurred at 7:00 am PDT on the US servers, and at 9:00 PDT on the EU servers.

### GENERAL

- Click-to-cure should now use the robust remedies that are in your bags.
- Pet back off will now remove auto-assist.

### MERCENARIES

- Quest mercenaries should no longer overwrite your hired mercenary if they die.

### ACHIEVEMENTS

- The Triumph "Destiny of Velious Over Achiever" should once again be awarding to players who have already killed the required bosses.

### AMENITIES

- Bells should no longer require a 6 person group to zone to Cobalt Scar.

### POPULATION / ZONE PROGRESSION

*Cobalt Scar*

- Mangla the Repeller in Cobalt Scar should reset properly, now, if not defeated.
- World travel bells should no longer require all players of group to have completed "A Helping Hand" or "Rot in the Tides" to zone to Cobalt Scar.
- World travel bells should no longer zone the entire group when one person chooses to zone to Cobalt Scar.

*Siren's Grotto: Tavalan Abyss* [Challenge Heroic]

- Overlord Talan's Tavalan Mark of Death should no longer target player pets or mercenaries.

*Sirens Grotto: Dissention*

- Caella of the Pearl: Coerced combine guardians are limited to a maximum of 9 in the zone at a time and they no longer memwipe.  Murky Poison and poisonous secretion can now be cured as curses as well as cured in the waterfall.
- Entrancer Lisha: Enslaver Eels have less health.  Enslaver eels have an obvious visual effect when immune to taunts.  Enslaver Eel Debilitating venom will no longer overwrite itself causing an incurable penalty to trigger.

*Sleeper's Tomb: Unearthed*

- Ancient Sentinel: Touch of Velium II in challenge mode now has a much weaker heal component.

*Antonica*

- Antonica burrow public quest is now active when Nights of the Dead, Erollisi Day, Bristlebane Day, Brewday, Heroes' Festival, Frostfell, and Qeynos city festival are not active.

*Plane of War*

- The Enraged War Boar now drops a curious new item!

### ITEMS

- NO-EXPERIMENT should now only be visible on the intended items.
- The Wooden Wash Basin will no longer provide free rent.
- Tavalan Drowning Doll charms: the melee proc for the set bonus has been changed. *Instead of damage it now procs critbonus, mana on the wearer and a group heal.
- Added Modulated Armband of the Hendin Shadow Savant and the Modulated Armband of the Fate Revisionist to their item set.
- Ethereal Corslet of the Silver Reaper is now flagged as Ethereal.
- Ethereal Buckler of the Silver Reaper now has block chance.
- Siren's Grotto: Dissension - All loot for for Gen'Ra, Diviner Glerin and Overlord Talan normal mode fights has been upgraded.

### ABILITIES / ALTERNATE ADVACNEMENT

*Beastlord*

- Enhance: Savage Howl should now apply properly.

*Guardian*

- Taunting Assault now casts faster in PVP combat.

*Templar*

- Divine Fate no longer can be dispelled.
- Reverence can no longer trigger procs.
- Sanctuary and Sanctuary II now properly share a reuse group.

*Warlock*

- Dark Aggravation now states how many targets it can hit and now uses the disruption skill.
