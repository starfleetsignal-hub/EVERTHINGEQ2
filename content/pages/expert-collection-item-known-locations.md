The following information details the known locations of spawn points for expert collection items.

- You must obtain and equip either the [[Gnomish Collection Goggles]] or [[Kunark Expert Adventurer Goggles]], which are collection rewards from normal, but exceptionally-difficult-to-complete, collection quests.
- See the Expert Collections category page for list of all collections of this type.
