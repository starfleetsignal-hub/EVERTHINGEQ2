## Overview

Graftwerk cosmetic armors and weapons were introduced shortly after the launch of [[Rage of Cthurath]] expansion; and consist of the following categories:

### Armors

- Cloth - 'Grotesque' Graftwerk *armors*
- Leather - 'Gruesome' Graftwerk *armors*
- Chain - 'Hideous' Graftwerk *armors*
- Plate - 'Dreadful' Graftwerk *armors*
- *Certain individual pieces also have an 'Epic <piece>' visual alternative, ex: 'Epic Grotesque Graftwerk Robes'*

### Weapons

- Weapons - 'Vicious' Graftwerk *weapons*

## How to Obtain

These cosmetic Armors and Weapons are obtained from [[Zon Zobboz Heritage Wardrobe Crate|Zon Zobboz Heritage Wardrobe Crates]] unpacked from [[Zon Zobboz Heritage Crate|Zon Zobboz Heritage Crates]] (Currently only available/Marketplace (159 Daybreak Cash) *(or higher)* )
