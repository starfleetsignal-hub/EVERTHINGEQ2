## Notes

Every [[Ascension]] ability has a synergy Ascension ability of another class. If both of these abilities are applied within a short duration to the same target, an extra effect will be applied based off of the ability that finished the combo.<br>
Those combos work mostly vice versa.<br>

- Example for [[Glacial Freeze]] and [[Septic Strike]]
  - [[Glacial Freeze]] then [[Septic Strike]] --> Septic Strike surges
  - [[Septic Strike]] then [[Glacial Freeze]] --> Glacial Freeze surges

When combo triggered you get the following text in the chat log:<br>
**Your {spell name} surges with power.**

The Combo doesn't trigger when any other Ascension spell is cast. For example:

1. player A casts [[Glacial Freeze]]
1. player B casts [[Etherflash]] *one second after player 1 started to cast*
1. player C casts [[Septic Strike]] *once player 1 finished casting*
1. combo failed

## Known Simple Combos

Note: ALL COMBOS ARE REVERSIBLE, THE 2ND SPELL WILL BE THE ONE THAT SURGES.

The following **Simple Combos** are tiggered from 2 Ascension classes:

- [[Glacial Freeze]] < -- > [[Septic Strike]]
- [[Etherflash]] < -- > [[Virulent Outbreak]]
  - Resulting Effects:
    - Etherflash surges - [[Etherflash]] will deal normal damage as well as give a 2nd cast that deals disease damage.
    - Virulent Outbreak surges - [[Virulent Outbreak]] will deal normal damage as well as give a 2nd cast that deals magic damage.
- [[Ethershadow Assassin]] < -- > [[Brittle Armor]]
  - Resulting Effects:
    - Ethershadow Assassin surges - [[Ethershadow Assassin]] will deal 30% more damage.
    - Brittle Armor surges - Stacks of [[Brittle Armor]] will grant 50% more combat mitigation reduction per stack.
- [[Stone Hammer]] < -- > [[Blood Contract]]
- [[Telluric Rending]] < -- > [[Tainted Mutation]]
- [[Implosion]] < -- > [[Terrestrial Coffin]]
- [[Erosion]] < -- > [[Wildfire]]
- [[Ethermancy]] < -- > [[Scorched Earth]]
- [[Septic Strike]] < -- > [[Glacial Freeze]]
- [[Granite Protector]] < -- > [[Levinbolt]]
- [[Fiery Incineration]] < -- > [[Bloatfly]]
- [[Elemental Amalgamation]] < -- > [[Mudslide]]
- [[Revocation of Life]] < -- > [[Cascading Force]]
- [[Withering]] < -- > [[Frozen Heavens]]
- [[Stone Hammer]], [[Blood Contract]], [[Telluric Rending]], [[Tainted Mutation]], [[Implosion]], [[Terrestrial Coffin]], [[Erosion]], [[Wildfire]], [[Ethermancy]], [[Scorched Earth]], [[Septic Strike]], [[Glacial Freeze]], [[Granite Protector]], [[Levinbolt]], [[Elemental Amalgamation]], [[Mudslide]], [[Revocation of Life]], [[Cascading Force]], [[Withering]] or [[Frozen Heavens]] --> [[Anti-Life]]
  - *Note: any starter, followed by [[Anti-Life]], but [[Anti-Life]] can't start any combo*
- [[Dessication]] < -- > [[Domain of Earth]]

The combos can also be chained together to cause multiple surges:

- [[Etherflash]]  -- > [[Virulent Outbreak]] --> [[Etherflash]]
  - Will cause Virulent Outbreak and the 2nd Etherflash to surge.
