For the short version see Expansions and Adventure Packs.

EverQuest II was originally released on November 9th, 2004 and now has 19 expansions along with 4 adventure packs. The game went completely [[Free-To-Play|Free-to-Play]] with the release of the Age of Discovery expansion on December 6th, 2011.

Adventure Packs are also listed here, although they were not full expansions. They were intended to serve as additional downloadable content, purchased separately from the original game and subsequent expansions. Eventually the Adventure Packs were included with newer expansions released for the game. There were three Adventure Packs released in 2005 and 2006. The fourth, and last, Adventure Pack was released in 2015, nearly nine years later.

New and returning players only need to purchase the current expansion. It will include access to all previous expansions and will also include access to all of the Adventure Packs. Free-to-Play members who have not purchased any expansions will only be able to access content up to Planes of Prophecy. This effectively limits the player to a maximum level of 110 and does not allow access to the areas introduced in later expansions.

## Expansions

#### Desert of Flames

- Released: September 13, 2005
- Levels: 45-60
- Maximum AA: None
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Update: [[Update:13|LU13]]
- Zones: [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]], [[The Pillars of Flame|Pillars of Flame]]
- Desert of Flames was the first expansion and introduced two large overland areas based on the desert of Ro from the original EverQuest. The maximum level was increased to 60. There were a number of new dungeon instances added for groups and raids. The release of this expansion coincided with  significant changes to the combat system and other core game mechanics, and several new spells were added for each class. The main quest series for the expansion was the [[Peacock Club Timeline|Peacock Club]] which concluded with raiding [[Godking Anuk]].

#### Kingdom of Sky

- Released: February 21, 2006
- Levels: 55-70
- Maximum AA: 50
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Update: [[Update:20|LU20]]
- Zones: [[Tenebrous Tangle]], [[The Barren Sky|Barren Sky]], [[The Bonemire|Bonemire]]
- Kingdom of Sky was the second expansion which took the players to the [[Overrealm]] and introduced the [[Ulteran Spires]]. The maximum level was increased to 70 and the [[Alternate Advancement]] system was first introduced. The main quest series for the expansion dealt with searching for the stolen [[Claymore Timeline|Qeynos Claymore]]. It culminates in several raids against the powerful dragons of the region, including [[Lord Vyemm]] and the undead dragon [[Tarinax the Destroyer]].

#### Echoes of Faydwer

- Released: November 13, 2006
- Levels: 1-70
- Maximum AA: 100
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Update: [[Update:29|LU29]]
- Zones: [[Butcherblock Mountains]], [[Greater Faydark]], [[The Lesser Faydark|Lesser Faydark]], [[Loping Plains]], [[Steamfont Mountains]]
- Echoes of Faydwer was the third expansion and it introduced a significant number of new areas and dungeons to the game, along with a completely new leveling experience for new players. New characters could start in [[Greater Faydark]] and be citizens of the city of [[Kelethin]]. A player could start a new character and level from 1 to 70 entirely in the new content introduced with the expansion.
- <br>This expansion also began the return of certain [[Deity Timeline|deities]] to Norrath and the ability for players to select which deity to follow. The main quest series for the expansion was [[Swords of Destiny Timeline|Swords of Destiny]] in which the player recovers [[Soulfire]], twin sword of the Qeynos Claymore. This concluded in a confrontation with the vampire lord [[Mayong Mistmoore (Instanced)|Mayong Mistmoore]].

#### Rise of Kunark

- Released: November 13, 2007
- Levels: 1-20, 65-80
- Maximum AA: 140
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Update: [[Update:40|LU40]]
- Zones: [[Timorous Deep]], [[Kylong Plains]], [[Fens of Nathsar]], [[Kunzar Jungle]], [[Jarsath Wastes]]
- Rise of Kunark introduced a new starting area for level 1-20, a new player race, the Sarnak and their capital city of [[Gorowyn]]. Unlike the evil cities of [[Freeport]] and [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]], Gorowyn tolerates characters of good alignment. All players can visit and even live there, if they choose.
- <br>The maximum level was increased to 80 and a new level 80 spell was added for each class. The prominent feature of this expansion was the introduction of [[Epic Weapons]], powerful class-specific weapons which could be upgraded to Mythical versions by raiding [[Veeshan's Peak]], the stronghold of [[Trakanon (Monster)|Trakanon]] and many other dragons.

#### The Shadow Odyssey

- Released: November 18, 2008
- Levels: 50-80
- Maximum AA: 200
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Update: [[Update:50|LU50]]
- Zones: [[Moors of Ykesha]]

The Shadow Odyssey introduced one large overland area, based on the Innothule Swamp from EverQuest, and 18 different instanced dungeons. These heroic dungeons were designed to scale with the player from level 50 through 80, offering rewards based on the average level of the group members. It also introduced the ability to purchase equipment using a currency earned from dungeons and raids, called [[Void Shard Mission System|Void Shards]].
<br>The main quest series was called [[The Shadow Odyssey Timeline|The Shadow Odyssey]] which concludes with confronting [[Valdoartus Varsoon|Varsoon]] in the [[The Palace of Ferzhul|Palace of Ferzhul]]. Unlike the main quest series in previous expansions, players did not have to raid to complete the quest. There were a number of raid zones, with the players ultimately challenging [[Anashti Sul (God)|Anashti Sul]] the goddess of undeath, and [[Ykesha]] in the depths of Guk.

#### Sentinel's Fate

- Released: February 16, 2010
- Levels: 75-90
- Maximum AA: 250
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Update: [[Update:55|LU55]]
- Zones: [[The Sundered Frontier|Sundered Frontier]], [[The Stonebrunt Highlands|Stonebrunt Highlands]], [[The Hole]]
- Sentinel's Fate introduced two new overland zones that were originally part of Odus in EverQuest, along with a large new contested dungeon called The Hole. The maximum level was increased to 90, and 12 new heroic dungeons were added. The main quest series was [[The Footsteps of Dartain Timeline|The Footsteps of Dartain]] and a raid quest line that begins with [[Tending to Toxxicology]] and ultimately leads to a confrontation with [[Roehn Theer (Palace of Roehn Theer)|Roehn Theer]], the Godslayer.

#### Destiny of Velious

- Released: February 22, 2011
- Levels: 85-90
- Maximum AA: 300
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Update: LU59
- Zones: [[Great Divide]], [[Eastern Wastes]]
- Destiny of Velious introduced two new overland zones, the city of [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain|Thurgadin]] and 10 heroic dungeons. This expansion also introduced flying mounts to the game, and a new main quest series called [[The Fallen Swords Timeline|The Fallen Swords]] where players challenge [[Rallos Zek (Plane of War)|Rallos Zek]] in the [[Plane of War]].
- <br>Additional dungeons were added to Velious with [[Update:63|LU63]] which introduced [[The Withered Lands Timeline|The Withered Lands]] and [[Skyshrine: The City of Dracur|Skyshrine]], increasing the maximum level to 92. This update, which came after the release of Age of Discovery, also made some changes to the AA system and reduced the amount of experience needed to earn AA points.

#### Age of Discovery

- Released: December 6, 2011
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Update: LU62
- Age of Discovery was the eighth expansion, but did not contain any new zones, heroic or raid content. Instead, it added a number of new features to the game:
  - The Beastlord, a new scout class based on the original EverQuest class.
  - [[Feature: Mercenaries|Mercenaries]] which players could hire to join them in adventures.
  - [[Tradeskill Apprentices]] to research new spells and items.
  - [[Reforging]] to change the stats on equipment and add particle effects.
- This expansion also included an update for [[The City of Freeport Timeline|Freeport]] and introduced a new series of quests. Many of the features which were included with Age of Discovery later became available for individual purchase on the Marketplace. They are not included with Free-to-Play memberships. In late 2020, the ability to reforge items was included with the base game and does not need to be purchased separately.

#### Chains of Eternity

- Released: November 13, 2012
- Levels: 90-95
- Maximum AA: 300
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Update: [[Update:65|LU65]]
- Zones: [[Obol Plains]], [[The Eidolon Jungle|Eidolon Jungle]]
- Chains of Eternity was the ninth expansion which introduced two new overland zones and nine new heroic dungeons. The maximum level was increased to 95, and new Prestige and Tradeskill Prestige abilities were added. Instead of increasing AA points, new Prestige abilities would be earned as you leveled beyond 90, with a new point earned for every 20% of a level.
- <br>The main quest line for Chains of Eternity is [[Shades of Drinal Timeline|Shades of Drinal]] which begins at level 92. The quest has players explore the afterlife in the Ethernere. The two overland zones resemble the [[The Feerrott|Feerrott]] and [[Loping Plains]], but with a very different look and all new creatures.

#### Tears of Veeshan

- Released: November 12, 2013
- Levels: 95
- Maximum AA: 340
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Update: [[Update:68|LU68]]
- Zones: [[Vesspyr Isles]]
- Tears of Veeshan is the 10th expansion, and continues the main story arc through the afterlife in the Eternal Broodlands. This expansion introduced a new class, the Channeler and added new Dragon AA abilities. The maximum number of AA points was originally increased to 340, and then to 350 with the release of a subsequent update. The overland zone is similar in layout to the [[The Barren Sky|Barren Sky]], and includes nine new dungeons.
- <br>The main quest series starts with [[Tears of Veeshan: The Eternal Broodlands|Tears of Veeshan]] and concludes with the [[Age's End: Final Monolith|Age's End]] quests. This expansion completed the story arc that began with the Kingdom of Sky expansion and the theft of the Qeynos Claymore. [[Age's End: Shattered Fate]] is the final confrontation with [[Kerafyrm (Lore)|Kerafyrm]], the reformation of Luclin and leads to the eventual return of players to the moon with the Blood of Luclin expansion.

#### Altar of Malice

- Released: November 11, 2014
- Levels: 95-100
- Maximum AA: 350
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Update: [[Update:69|LU69]]
- Zones: [[Tranquil Sea]], [[Phantom Sea]]
- Altar of Malice was the 11th expansion, increased the maximum level to 100 and added new Grandmaster and Ancient tier spells. This expansion also included a new playable race, the Aerakyn which could be purchased through the Marketplace. There are two large overland zones, with the story centered around the Isle of Refuge (which has largely been destroyed) and Kithicor forest from the original EverQuest.

#### Terrors of Thalumbra

- Released: November 17, 2015
- Levels: 100
- Maximum AA: 350
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Zones: [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]]
- Terrors of Thalumbra was the 12th expansion and offered a new underground zone and dungeons for level 100 players. It also introduced additional [[Agnostic Dungeons|agnostic dungeons]] which were available to lower level players. This expansion also included a new [[Deity Tithe System|deity system]] and introduced the [[Equipment Infusing|infusion system]] to improve the stats on equipment. The main signature quest series for the expansion was the [[Terrors of Thalumbra Timeline|Underdepths Saga]].

#### Kunark Ascending

- Released: November 15, 2016
- Levels: 100
- Maximum AA: 350
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Update: LU102
- Zones: [[Obulus Frontier]]
- Kunark Ascending was the 13th expansion and featured a return to Kunark, with a completely new series of Epic quests for a new class weapon. Unlike the original epic weapons, these new weapons are based on class archetype (Fighter, Mage, Priest and Scout) rather than individual subclasses. The expansion also introduced the [[Ascension|Ascension classes]] which become available after completing the [[Kunark Ascending Timeline|Kunark Ascending]] signature quests.

#### Planes of Prophecy

- Released: November 28, 2017
- Levels: 100-110
- Maximum AA: 350
- Free-to-Play: Yes
- Zones: [[Plane of Magic]], [[Coliseum of Valor]]
- Planes of Prophecy was the 14th expansion and introduced the various planes such as the Plane of Magic, Plane of Innovation and Plane of Disease. The maximum level was increased to 110 and the maximum Ascension level was increased to 15. Mercenaries could also be trained up to level 10. The main signature quest line for the expansion was [[Planes of Prophecy Timeline|Legacy of Power]]. Prior to the release of this expansion was the first [[Days of Summer: 2017|Days of Summer]] series of quests from [[The "Travels" of Yun Zi Timeline|Yun Zi]]. These quests have since become a standard feature of the game, with each year introducing a new collection of quests.
- <br>This expansion changed some aspects of gameplay in fundamental ways. The amount of experience needed to advance each level made it impractical to use the traditional leveling method of adventuring in dungeons or using tradeskill writs. Instead, players are effectively required to complete the signature quest lines for both adventuring and tradeskill professions to advance their character. Dungeons become available to players at level 105 and require completing parts of the signature quest line; however, access is unlocked on an account-wide basis.

#### Chaos Descending

- Released: November 17, 2018
- Levels: 110
- Maximum AA: 350
- Free-to-Play: No
- Zones: [[Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands]], [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]], [[Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wind]], [[Myrist, the Great Library]], [[Vegarlson, the Earthen Badlands]]
- Chaos Descending was the 15th expansion and introduced four new overland zones and more than ten new solo and heroic dungeons. This expansion also introduced the ability to level up mounts and equip them with gear that increase the rider's stats. The maximum Ascension level was increased to 20 and new spells were added. The maximum Mercenary level was also increased to 20. The signature quest series is [[Chaos Descending Timeline|Elements of Destruction]] which begins in [[Myrist, the Great Library]] for players who are level 110.

#### Blood of Luclin

- Released: December 17, 2019
- Levels: 110-120
- Maximum AA: 350
- Free-to-Play: No
- Zones: [[Aurelian Coast]], [[Sanctus Seru (City)|Sanctus Seru]], [[The Blinding]], [[Wracklands]]
- Blood of Luclin was the 16th expansion and the maximum level was increased to 120. There are four new overland adventure zones along with new solo and heroic dungeons, all based on [[Luclin]], one of the moons of Norrath. This expansion introduced the new [[Overseer Feature|Overseer]] system which allows players to send agents out on adventures who return with various kinds of loot, including armor, weapons and crafting supplies. The main signature quest line is [[Blood of Luclin Timeline|Shattered Dawn]] and begins in [[Kylong Plains]], where you speak with [[The Duality]] and travel to [[The Blinding]] on Luclin.

#### Reign of Shadows

- Released: December 15, 2020
- Levels: 120
- Maximum AA: 350
- Free-to-Play: No
- Zones: [[City of Fordel Midst]], [[Echo Caverns]], [[Savage Weald]], [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]], [[Shadow Haven]], [[City of Shar Vahl]]
- Reign of Shadows is the 17th expansion which continues the story on Luclin, adds six new overland zones and nine new solo and heroic dungeons, as well as new raids. A new player race, the [[Vah Shir]], was introduced which is based on the same race from the original EverQuest. The [[Alternate Advancement]] system was changed significantly for the base class and subclass trees, updating abilities to be more relevant to the current game mechanics and new Prestige abilities were added. The main signature quest line is [[Reign of Shadows Timeline|Reign of Shadows]] which starts in the [[City of Fordel Midst|City of Fordel Mist]].

#### Visions of Vetrovia

- Released: December 1, 2021
- Levels: 120-125
- Maximum AA: 350
- Free-to-Play: No
- Zones: [[Svarni Expanse]], [[Karuupa Jungle]], [[Mahngavi Wastes]], [[Forlorn Gist]]

Visions of Vetrovia is the 18th expansion, set in the uncharted island of Vetrovia in the Shattered Seas. You begin in the Svarni Expanse, home of a colony of centaurs. This leads you to the dense jungles of Karuupa, the Manghavi Wastes and ultimately Forlorn Gist, all to discover who occupies mysterious Castle Vacrul, a foreboding fortress built high in the mountains. This expansion includes new upgrades to all of your spells and new tradeskill recipes. As with the other recent expansions, leveling is primarily through completing the adventure and tradeskill signature quest lines.

#### Renewal of Ro

- Released: November 30, 2022
- Levels: 125
- Maximum AA: 350
- Free-to-Play: No
- Zones: [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]], [[Takish Badlands]], [[Sandstone Delta]]

Renewal of Ro is the 19th expansion, set in the southern part of the continent of Ro. You will begin in the Raj'Dur Plateus, which has a similar look to the desert in the [[The Pillars of Flame|Pillars of Flame]]. You explore the area in search of the Elddar city of Takish'Hiz. From there, you head to the Takish Badlands and Sandstone Delta to unlock the secrets of the cursed ruins. This expansion does not include a level cap increase, but it does add a new epic class spell, 5 new Ascension spell levels, and a new adventure and tradeskill signature quest line.

#### [[Ballads of Zimara]]

- Released: November 29, 2023
- Levels: 130
- Maximum AA: 350
- Free-to-Play: No
- Zones: [[Splendor Sky Aerie]], [[Zimara Breadth]], [[Aether Wroughtlands]]

Ballads of Zimara is the 20th expansion, visiting the Home of the Hooluuk - Splendor Sky Aerie. You are to save the Hooluuk from an invasion of Metallic Djinni. This expansion includes new game-mechanics like [[Researchers]], using Status and Platinum to research Advancements, Mounts and more.

#### [[Scars of Destruction]]

- Released: November 2024
- Levels: 130
- Maximum AA: 350
- Free-to-Play: No
- Zones: [[Sodden Archipelago]] , [[Western Wastes]]

Scars of Destruction marks the 21st expansion of EverQuest 2. From discovering the Swamps and Plateaus of Sodden Archipelago to fighting alongside Lucan D'Lere in Western Wastes - The war for an ancient Dragon Relic wages on. Even the Othmir make their well-earned comeback. In this expansion, players can use [[Researchers]] to upgrade their Gear and even their Tradeskills.

#### [[Rage of Cthurath]]

- Releases: December 2025
- Levels: 135
- Maximum AA: 350
- Free-to-Play: No
- Zones: [[Xebrith Tendril]]; The Unknown, Yon Gorroth, Oogothl Sprawl

Rage of  Cthurath is the 22nd expansion for EverQuest II, plunging players into the chaotic Void where the forces of Cthurath threaten Norrath. It raises the level cap to 135, adds new zones within the Xebrith Tendril like [[Yon Gorroth]] and [[Oogothl Sprawl]], introduces heroic dungeons, public quests, and advanced progression systems for both adventurers and tradeskillers.

## Adventure Packs

#### The Bloodline Chronicles

- Released: March 21, 2005
- Levels: 50-55
- Zones: [[The Tombs of Night|Tombs of Night]], [[The Crypt of T'haen|Crypt of T'haen]]
- Bloodline Chronicles added several new zones in [[Nektulos Forest]] which were primarily themed around vampires. It added new spells and introduced destructible environments to the game. To advance through certain areas, players would need to damage walls which would allow passage through caves or reveal areas where monsters would be hiding.
- <br>The adventure pack included both group dungeons and a raid for characters around level 50. When this adventure pack was original released, the level requirements were 35-45, however the minimum level was recently increased to 50. This was primarily for the benefit of the Time-Locked Expansion servers. These dungeons are unlocked during the middle of the server's progression through the Shattered Lands content, so raising the level to 50 ensured more relevant rewards for players.

#### The Splitpaw Saga

- Released: June 28, 2005
- Levels: 20-50
- Zones: [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den|Splitpaw Den]]
- The Splitpaw Saga adventure pack is themed around gnolls in the Thundering Steppes, and included solo, group and raid content. It introduced some new features, like dynamic level scaling (where the level of monsters would scale based on the level of the player or group in the instance) and movable objects such as wooden planks and explosive barrels. These were used to solve simple environmental puzzles where the player would need to use these to access some areas of a dungeon. One of the more popular dungeons was the [[Sundered Splitpaw: Trial of Harclave|Trial of Harclave]], a solo instance where players would be buffed and fight heroic gnolls and other monsters.

#### The Fallen Dynasty

- Released: June 14, 2006
- Levels: 55-70
- Zones: [[The Village of Shin|Village of Shin]], [[The Mystic Lake|Mystic Lake]], [[The Forsaken City|Forsaken City]]
- The Fallen Dynasty adventure pack was released prior to the Echoes of Faydwer expansion and served as the prelude to the gods returning to Norrath. It added new solo, group and raid content up to level 70. It included [[Nizara, City of the Nayad]] which was a challenging heroic dungeon, and a raid zone called the [[Antechamber of Fate]] which tied into the story about the gods. The raid boss [[Chel'Drak the Ancient Lord|Chel'Drak]] dropped a rare mythical item which could only be obtained once on the server.

#### Rum Cellar

- Released: April 28, 2015
- Levels: 100
- Zones: [[Far Seas Distillery]]
- Rum Cellar was a "campaign" style adventure pack with a pirate theme and included a number of solo, group and raid dungeons. It was designed for players who were level 100, and required they owned the Altar of Malice expansion. There were new collections, achievements and many rare loot drops, including an elite mercenary and a mount. The main quest series begins with [[Averting the Rumpocalypse]] in the [[Phantom Sea]].

[[Lure of Darkness]]

- Released: April 08, 2025
- Levels: 130
- Zones: [[Spiral of Vul (Contested)|The Spiral of Vul]]

Lure of Darkness was released as an add-on in content for Endgame players, adventuring into the [[Spiral of Vul (Contested)]] , with content ranging from Solo players to Heroic I and Heroic II Bosses. The player can interact with Goblins to follow a Quest through the Spiral, rewarding them with a Set of Appearance-Only Armor and access to new Goblin [[Researchers]] and Merchants, selling Upgrades for Crafters and Adventurers.
