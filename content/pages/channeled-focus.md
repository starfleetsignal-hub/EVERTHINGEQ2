## Effects

Effects Rank 1

- Reduces the power cost by 100%.

Effects Rank 2

- Increases Attack Speed of raid and group members (AE) by 33.0
- Increases Ability Casting Speed of raid and group members (AE) by 33.0%
- Improves trigger chance by 50%.
- Reduces the power cost by 100%.
