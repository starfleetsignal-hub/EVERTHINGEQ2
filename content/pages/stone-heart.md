Stone Heart is a prefix title from the [[Thalumbra Lovers Do It Deep]] [[Achievements|achievement]] that can be completed during [[Erollisi Day]]

To set a title after you earn it press **C** to open the character sheet. Click on the Options tab. Use the drop-down arrow to choose the title you wish to display.
