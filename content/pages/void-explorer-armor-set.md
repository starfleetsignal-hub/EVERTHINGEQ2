  - On a hostile ability cast this spell has a 18% chance to cast Greater Overflow on the group.
    - Heals group members for 390
    - Cannot be modified except by direct means

  - On a combat hit this spell may cast Greater Healing Wave on the group.  Triggers about 1.6 times per minute.
