This recipe allows an [[Alchemist]] to refine several lower level dusts into a higher one.

- **Class:** [[Alchemist]]
- **Recipe Level:** 40
- **Workstation:** [[Chemistry Table]]

## Ingredients

- 3 [[Glimmering Dust|Glimmering Dusts]]
- 6 [[Luminous candle|luminous candles]]

## Yields

- 1 [[Glimmering Dust]]
- 1 [[Luminous Dust]]
