Suffix title earned by giving 100 [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]] to [[Brother Huang Mantian|Huang]] in the Whistling Fist Clan's Tower on the Isle of Mara. You earn the pebbles by completing the quests called The Trials of  (for example, [[Trial of Qin Lo|The Trials of Qin Lo]]). By completing these repeatable quests you earn (from 5 up) pebbles. There is a 2-day lockout on the instanced trial zones and there are at least 5 trials found on scrolls within the tower. Also, pebbles can randomly drop from mobs inside the instances but it is rare.
