- Effects
  - Caster has a 10% chance of having one of their weapon's auto-attack affect multiple targets they are facing, who are within range of weapon.
  - Gives caster a 20% chance to strikethrough a successful avoidiance check.
  - Increases Melee Weapon Range of caster by 100%
