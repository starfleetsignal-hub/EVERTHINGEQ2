## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

Storm Tower Isle is an island with a beach. Above the beach on a craggy hill, is a tower with a giant skull at its peak. Though the tower figures prominently, there is plenty of room to build unique stuctures in the area surrounding it using [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]].

## Weather

- Due to a bug the weather was removed from this for a time, however, it was later restored. It does *not* have a weather toggle like [[Galleon of Dreams]], but the rain on this island is "smart" rain; it will not pass through anything you build that is made from solid items, like [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]]. If you have an older system or slower connection, this island is *not* recommended, as the rain can cause considerable lag for some players.
