| **Xev Bristlebane** |
|  |
| **God of Gambling** |

Xev is the Prince of Fortune and the god of luck.  He is the younger twin brother of the more notorious Fizzlethorpe Bristlebane, the god of mischief.  Some believe that Xev does not truly exist, and is simply one of Fizzlethorpe's many disguises, characters, or personalities.  During the Age of Turmoil, Xev had some followers in the trade route city of High Pass as well as some of the travelling gypsies of Antonica.
