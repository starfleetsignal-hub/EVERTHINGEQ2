- Reduces reuse time of all spells by 3 percent.

(5) Applies ***Grave Elemental's Affliction.***

- On a hostile spell hit this spell may cast Grave Elemental's Affliction on target of spell.  Lasts for 30.0 seconds.  Triggers about 1.8 times per minute.
  - Inflicts 965 - 1,608 heat damage on target.
    - If conjuror
  - Inflicts 965 - 1,608 poison damage on target.
    - If necromancer
  - Decreases Mitigation of target vs elemental and noxious damage by 1,519.
  - Will trigger off of poison, mental, magic, heat, divine, disease or cold abilities.

<br>
This is the TSO Armor Set (Tier 1) for: [[Summoner|Summoners]] ( ) that can be obtained via the [[Void Shard Mission System]].

These pieces are available for vendor purchase from [[A mysterious Quellthulian]], designated as the <Shard Armor Merchant> in [[Moors of Ykesha]] at the [[Dropship Landing Zone]] {{waypoint 1680, 452, 894}}. They can also be created by crafters.

Once purchased, this armor can subsequently be upgraded to the TSO Armor Set (Tier 2): [[Voidcry (Armor Set)]]

Note that the Void Shard costs in the above table are for vendor purchase. If purchased from a crafter, each piece costs one void shard less.
