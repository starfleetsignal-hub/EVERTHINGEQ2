## Effects

- Increases Hate Gain of caster by 100.
- Increases Mitigation of caster vs elemental, noxious and arcane damage.
- When damaged with a melee weapon this spell will cast ***Word of the Tribunal*** on target's attacker.
  - Inflicts massive divine damage on target.
