![An image of Karan as appears on the world map.](images/Karan1.png)
Karan is the western remnant of the former super-continent of *Antonica* that broke apart during [[The Rending]]. It is primarily controlled by the forces of Good, with [[Qeynos]] standing brightly along its westernmost side. The name 'Karan' is a slight-abbreviation of [[Karana]], the god of storms, and is thus called in tribute to the [[Diety]].

There are several major landmarks on Karan that can be attributed to storms and the power of the elements. Karan receives frequent thunderstorms, bringing with them rainwater, which keeps the land a lush green. *The Knights of Thunder*, a prestigious group of knights responsible for the building of [[Stormhold]] in eastern [[Antonica]], were headquartered on Karan and built their fortress in honor of the storm god. [[The Thundering Steppes]] zone, situated in the southern part of the continent, is a living tribute to Karana, as the area is gifted by constant thunderstorm. Formerly, The Thundering Steppes were called the *Plains of Karana*, and were some of the most fertile land on [[Norrath]], since storms not only bring destruction, but life as well.

![An image of Karan as appears on the world map, zoomed in.](images/Karan2.png)
Karan is composed of [[The Thundering Steppes]] in the south, [[Antonica]] in it's center, and [[The City of Qeynos]] on it's west. The Thundering Steppes is a green landscape pocketed by meteor craters left by [[The Shattering]]. In the Steppes exist native centaur, ancient gnolls and a recent incursion of settlers from Qeynos. Antonica is a plain of rolling hills interrupted by sharp mountain upcropings. The Sabertooth Gnolls can be found lurking in any darkened corner that The Qeynos Guard have turned a blind a eye. Crowning Antonica's western side sits [[The City of Qeynos]], home of [[Antonia Bayle]], [[The Temple of Life]], and shines as the beacon of righteousness on Norrath.

Central themes of Karan include the legacy of [[Varsoon the Undying|Valdoartus Varsoon]] and the [[War of Plagues]] that ravaged much of the populace, the fallout of [[The Age of Cataclysms (Lore)|The Age of Cataclysms]] and the stabilization of the continent by the forces of Qeynos in the aftermath of centuries of destruction and chaos.
