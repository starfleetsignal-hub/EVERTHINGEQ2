**Malkonis D'Morte** appears in several locations in game:

- [[Malkonis D'Morte (Living Tombs)]] - the NPC appearing as part of the quest [[From Hands of Stone]]
- [[Malkonis D'Morte (Gates of Ahket Aken)]] - the level 69 Epic x4 named
- [[Malkonis D'Morte (Freethinker Hideout)]] - the level 75 Epic x4 named

He also appears as a [[Malkonis D'Morte's Head|decapitated head]] (after killing him in [[Freethinker Hideout]]) that offers the quest [[The Head of Malkonis D'Morte]] upon examination.
