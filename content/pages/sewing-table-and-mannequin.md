**Sewing Table and Mannequins** are used by [[Carpenter|Carpenters]] and [[Tailor|Tailors]]. They may be referred to as a "Loom" or simply a "Sewing table" in recipe books. In addition to each of the traditional tradeskill zones, free-standing looms can be found in the following locations:

## Qeynos

- [[North Qeynos]]: Clothespinners, Fhara's Furnishings, Sneed's Trading Post
- [[South Qeynos]]: Maile's Exchange, Bag n'Barrel
- [[Qeynos Harbor]]: Beside the Chronomages, under an awning at {{waypoint 886, -25, 22}}

## Freeport

- [[North Freeport]]: Freeport Fine Clothiers
- [[East Freeport]]: The Emporium
- [[West Freeport]]: Fine Leather & Hide or [[Coalition Crafter's Association (POI)]] (the crafting area, not just the instance) at {{waypoint 29, -12, 106}}.
- [[South Freeport]]: Block and Tackle {{waypoint -208, -56, 189}}

## Faydwer

- [[Kelethin]]: Tunare's Pages, a house marked ?Tailoring? on the map of the city
- [[Steamfont Mountains]]: near Fizza Cogsworth
- [[Loping Plains]]: Village of Somborn, next to [[Vinatori]] the Leather and Cloth merchant
- [[Butcherblock Mountains]]: Butcherblock Bay, next to [[Trader Darmlin]]'s tent

## Gorowyn/Timorous Deep

- [[Timorous Deep]]: [[The Wards]] {{waypoint 2815, 121, 1250}}

## Neriak

- [[Neriak Down Under]] {{waypoint -177, -3, 286}} and {{waypoint -178, 3, 334}}

## Kunark

- [[Teren's Grasp]]: [[Arcane Refuge]] (first building on your left as you enter the outpost via the ramp)
- [[Fens of Nathsar]]: on [[Sathir's Span]] in one of the buildings

## Obol Plains

- [[Cardin Ward]]: {{waypoint -394, 15, 65}}

## Other Zones

- [[Haven]]: [[Ekroosik's Replicators]] at {{waypoint 61, -25, -172}} and {{waypoint 64, -25, -183}} (take a ladder up at {{waypoint 52, -31, -171}})
