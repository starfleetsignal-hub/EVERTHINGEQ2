[[Expert]] is a quality of spells/combat art, better than "Adept" but inferior to "Master". The spell or combat art scrolls are mastercrafted items that require a rare harvest to make and are made by one of the [[Scholar]] tradeskill classes ([[Sage]], [[Jeweler]], and [[Alchemist]]) depending on archetype.
