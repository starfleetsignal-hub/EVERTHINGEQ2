Many quests and areas exist in game using **gorillas** as a central feature. This includes any number of actual attackable gorillas as well as several quests, including:

- [[Garbing Us in Gorillas]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
- [[Made From Real Gorilla Chest]] in [[Timorous Deep]]
- [[Huggable Iksar]] in [[Shard of Fear]]
- [[Gorililililas!]] (particularly difficult to spell and pronounce) in [[The Sundered Frontier]]
