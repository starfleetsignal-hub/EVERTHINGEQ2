The table below details the **Tier 8 Adornments** organized by the slots to which they apply:

**Lesser** adornments appear with no highlighting. They require 1 [[Sacred Powder]] and 6 [[Emerald Fragment|Emerald Fragments]].  Up to 3 [[Emerald Fragment|Emerald Fragments]] are returned per craft.

**Greater** adornments appear with brown highlighting. They require 1 [[Infusion of Reality]], 2 [[Sacred Powder|Sacred Powders]], and 6 [[Emerald Fragment|Emerald Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Sacred Powder]] is returned per craft.

**Superior** adornments appear with red highlighting. They require 1 [[Crystallized Mana]], 2 [[Infusion of Reality|Infusions of Reality]], 3 [[Sacred Powder|Sacred Powders]] and 6 [[Emerald Fragment|Emerald Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Infusion of Reality]] is returned per craft.

For a simple, alphabetic listing of tier 8 adornments, see Tier 8 Adornments.
