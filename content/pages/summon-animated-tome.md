## NOTES

This spell is obtained by spending Alternate Advancement points in [[Kineticist's Intelligence]].  Increasing ranks in [[Kineticist's Intelligence]] will increase the rank of [[Summon Animated Tome]], [[Summon Drake]], and [[Summon Gargoyle]].

Sorcerer pet effects were modified significantly with the release of the [[Reign of Shadows]] expansion/update.
