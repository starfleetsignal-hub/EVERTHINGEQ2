## Effects

- On a hostile spell hit this spell may cast **Preoccupation** on target of spell. Lasts for 20.0 seconds. Triggers about 2.0 times per minute.
  - All combat arts and spells suffer a 25% longer reuse time and 100% longer casting time.
- When a combat art is used this spell has a 10% chance to cast **Preoccupation** on target of combat art. Lasts for 20.0 seconds. This effect normalizes based off of a three second triggering event.
  - All combat arts and spells suffer a 25% longer reuse time and 100% longer casting time.
