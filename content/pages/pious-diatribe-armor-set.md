- (2) +10 WIS
- (3) Applies ***Runic Recovery II***
  - Reduces reuse time of benefical spells by 2 percent.
- (5) Applies ***Burst of Healing***.
  - When target casts a healing spell this spell has a chance to cast Burst of Healing on caster. Lasts for 15.0 seconds. This effect will trigger an average of 2.0 times per minute.
