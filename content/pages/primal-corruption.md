This is a mercenary ability (e.g. [[Shallall'Dene]])

- Increases Agi of group members (increases with level)
- Increases Str, Agi, Wis and Int of group members (increases with level)
- Adds +% to base avoidance (increases with level)
- Increases in-combat power regeneration of group members (increases with level)
- Increases Max Power of group members (increases with level)
- Increases Mitigation of group members (AE) vs elemental, noxious and arcane damage (increases with level)
- On any combat or spell hit this spell may cast Battle Fury on target.
  - Increases ability casting speed of target (increases with level)
  - Increases attack speed of target (increases with level)
  - Increases multi attack chance of target (increases with level)
