- (2) Applies ***Focus: [[Act of War]]***.
  - Increases the trigger chance of [[Act of War]] by 3% and removes the concentration cost.

  - Improves the effects of [[Tenacity]] and makes it groupwide.
  - Increases the concentration cost by 1.
- (5) Applies ***Greater Salubrious Aura***
  - When target casts a healing spell this spell has a chance to cast Greater Salubrious Aura on target of spell cast. Lasts for 30.0 seconds. This effect will trigger an average of 2.0 times per minute.
    - When target takes any damage this spell will cast Greater Reflexive Restoration on target
      - Heals caster for 457
      - Grants a total of 3 triggers of the spell.

This armor may be worn only by: [[Inquisitor|Inquisitors]].
