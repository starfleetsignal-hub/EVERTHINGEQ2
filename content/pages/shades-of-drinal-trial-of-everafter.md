Speak with Lady Lae Croae in The eidolon Jungle, near the Spire to Feerrott @ 63.70, 13.70, 167.80.   Then exit back out to the jungle an follow the path, as you wonder along an Assassin will attack, this will be the first of three. (first: -111.71 5.56 46.43    second: 57.63 -9.20 130.01   third: -98.60 -3.46 329.82  ) Continue along the path until all have attacked.  Go back to Lady Lae. She will progress you to talk with the Drinal Steward.
