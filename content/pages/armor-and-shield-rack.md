![An armor and shield rack placed in a house. No matter what tier of rare wood is used to make these, they all look the same when placed.](images/Armor_and-Weapon_Rack_Placed.png)
If you are not familiar with armor and shield racks, you may want to read about the general topic of [[Sales Display|sales displays]] that can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]].

- All armor and shield racks are Master Crafted (made from a rare material) by those who take up the [[Carpenter]] tradeskill.
- They hold a variety of armor and shields of any type, typically made by [[Armorer|Armorers]], [[Tailor|Tailors]] and [[Woodworker|Woodworkers]].
