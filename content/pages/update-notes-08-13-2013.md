### POPULATION / ZONE PROGRESSION

**Cobalt Scar**

- Yeppa's Last Stand: The othmir have moved their tradeskill forge to a less exposed location.

### CHARACTER ACHIEVEMENT TEMPLATES

Auto-consume items will now auto-consume after the AA Template is committed.

### PLAYER HOUSING

Mannequins: Resolved an issue where a player would be unable to select an outfit for a hireling after moving items between houses.

### TRADESKILLS

The Hydra Helm recipes can no longer benefit from material conservation effects.

### ITEMS

Dawnbreak should proc much more reliably.
