## Society

A rebel pack from the Splitpaw clan that have gone cannibal, dwelling in the [[Drowned Caverns]] off of [[Splitpaw Den|Sundered Splitpaw]]. Just like the Splitpaw, they are caste-oriented. For further information see Underpaw Gnolls.
