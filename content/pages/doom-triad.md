Raiding Guilds often refer to this encounter as the **Doom Triad**, but they are actually three linked Named mobs located in the [[The Laboratory of Lord Vyemm]]. Because they are linked, the individual mobs do not really have drops, so any known drops are listed, below, here.
Kill order is Ravager, Prophet, then Reaver.
