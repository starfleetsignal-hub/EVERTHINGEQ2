The **Bane of Prexus**, owned by  the Seafury Buccaneers of [[Freeport]]. Like her [[Qeynos|Qeynosian]] sister, the QSS [[Valiance]] (staffed by the same captain and crew), she remains one of the most attacked of the ships still on the waters today. She regularly patrols the waters near the suspected [[Kunark]] continent in hopes of discovering more information about the mysterious [[Iksar]] currently plaguing the Norrathian seas.
