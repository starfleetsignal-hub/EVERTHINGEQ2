Group cures target your group instead of an individual and Remove a type of detrimental effect from the members of your group who are in range of the cure.

## Group Cures by Spell Line

- **Arcane**:
  - Templar ([[Cure: Resolve]])
  - Inquisitor ([[Flagellant]])
  - Mystic ([[Fading Spirit]])
- **Noxious**:
  - Defiler ([[Cure Mail of Souls]])
  - Mystic ([[Fading Spirit]])
  - Fury ([[Toxic Abolishment]])
- **Elemental**:
  - Fury ([[Toxic Abolishment]])
  - Warden ([[Cure: Verdant Sigh]])
  - Inquisitor ([[Flagellant]])
- **Trauma**:
  - Templar ([[Cure: Resolve]])
  - Defiler ([[Cure Mail of Souls]])
  - Warden ([[Cure: Verdant Sigh]])

### Additional Group Cures of Note

- Defiler ([[Voice of the Ancestors]]):
  - Cures all hostile control effects on the party. The Defiler can cast this while under the effects of Fear, Stuns, Mesmerizes, or Stifles.

## Group Wards by Spell Line

Presuming you are looking to cure a group of Detrimental Effects, you probably also would like to know what class can Ward and Increase Resists against said effects for their group.

- **Arcane**:
  - Templar ([[Protective Faith]])
- **Noxious**:
  - Mystic ([[Prophetic Guard]])
- **Elemental**:
  - Warden ([[Favor of the Untamed]])
- **Trauma**:
  - Mystic ([[Runic Mark]])

### Additional Wards/Buffs of Note

Mystic ([[Spiritual Seal]]): Hitpoint Buff for Group vs. Noxious
