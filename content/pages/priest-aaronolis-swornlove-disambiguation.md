**[[Priest Aaronolis Swornlove]]** currently appears only in [[New Halas]].  He offers quests during the [[Erollisi Day]] celebrations, but can be found there year-round.  Historically, he appeared in other locations.

- [[Priest Aaronolis Swornlove (Antonica)]], in [[Antonica]] as part of the [[Erollisi Day]] celebrations every year prior to the introduction of [[Frostfang Sea]] in LU56.
- [[Priest Aaronolis Swornlove (Kelethin)]], in the city of [[Kelethin]] in [[Greater Faydark]] as part of the world event announcing the discovery of [[Shard of Hate]].
