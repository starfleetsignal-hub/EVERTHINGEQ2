## The Golden Path: The Legend of Roger Goldie

For new players, figuring out how to approach the literally thousands of quests available in EverQuest II can be overwhelming. For a guided path through leveling, SOE's "Golden Path" is currently represented by the Hallmark quest series, [[The Legend of Roger Goldie Timeline|The Legend of Roger Goldie]].

The best new player experience in starting a new character is offered by [[Darklight Wood Timeline|Darklight Wood]], [[Greater Faydark Timeline|Greater Faydark]], [[Frostfang Sea Timeline|Frostfang Sea]] or [[Timorous Deep Timeline|Timorous Deep]]. In these zones, a solo player can easily achieve level 20.

![](images/Ole'_Man_Silvers.jpg)
**Ole' Man Silvers**

From your starting city, the first step onto the Golden Path is to locate [[Ole' Man Silvers]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]. Silvers will start you along [[The Legend of Roger Goldie Timeline|The Legend of Roger Goldie]] quests:

1. [[The Legend of Roger Goldie: The Adventure Begins]]
1. [[The Legend of Roger Goldie: Some Like It Hot]]
1. [[The Legend of Roger Goldie: Silvers versus the Volcano]]
1. [[The Legend of Roger Goldie: Sandblasted]]
1. [[The Legend of Roger Goldie: Quality Time in the Overrealm]]
1. [[The Legend of Roger Goldie: Booty Abound!]]

## More Soloing Opportunities

If you are looking for a less structured guide for leveling, our [[Soloing Timeline]] provides an easy-to-use reference so you can determine which zones are appropriate for your level. By clicking into a given zone, you can then also get more information about quest-givers and quests within that zone.
