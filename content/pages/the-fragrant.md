Suffix title earned by giving 150 monk pebbles to Huang in the Whistling Fist Clan's Tower on the Isle of Mara. You earn the pebbles by completing the quests called The Trials of  (for example, The Trials of Qin Lo). By completing these repeatable quests you earn (from 5 up) pebbles. There is a 2-day lockout on the instanced trial zones and there are at least 5 trials found on scrolls within the tower. Also, pebbles can randomly drop from mobs inside the instances but it is rare.
