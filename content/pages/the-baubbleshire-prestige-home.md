## General Prestige Home Info

Like all Prestige homes:

- you must first get a deed first and then travel to the location and "buy" it for *zero* coin.
- it has **no** recurring upkeep ("rent") of any kind.
- you can get a portal to place in other [[Housing|homes]] or (if you have access) your [[Guild Hall|guild hall]]
- you can travel to it quickly if you already own it; press **C** to open the character sheet, click the Housing tab, locate the house on the list and click the Enter button

## About This House

The Baubbleshire (Prestige Home) is a near replica of the [[The Baubbleshire]] zone, adjacent to the [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. The original location was the portal location for the smallest (two room) Qeynos-style homes. The buildings sit on the outer edges of the area, connected by a network of tiny islets with arched bridges. In the distance, the waterfall and trees can be seen. The dock is also present, though without the travel bells or globe.

The major differences in this version:

- The two building that used to host the banker, broker, and other NPCs are empty and altered slightly to look like a house with a single-door entry point.
- Most of the "hobbit holes" in the hillside are open and without doors.
