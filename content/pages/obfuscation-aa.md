## Effects

Casting and reuse speed increase: 12.5% / 25% / 37.5% / 50% / 62.5% / 75% / 87.5% / 100%
Combat art increase: 1% / 2% / 3% / 4% / 5% / 6% / 7% / 8%

Note that getting a 100% increase to reuse or casting time halves the original casting/reuse time - not makes them "Immediate".
