## About the Items

There are numerous rewards that one can get while in this instance. They are all [[Plundered House Items|plundered house item]]. A plundered house item is simply a non-crafted furniture item you can place in a character's [[Housing|house]]. If you don't care for [[Decorating Houses|decorating homes]], these can also be sold on the [[Broker|broker]].

Several different methods are used in the various riddles of the slain Scholar Primarch within the [[Library of Erudin]], all of which result in a different reward. Each item has a similar theme: they all have poorly hidden corpses!

In order to get a reward you have to solve the riddle correctly.
