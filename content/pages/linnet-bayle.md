**Linnett Bayle** was the wife of Qeynos ruler [[Antonius Bayle III]] and mother of [[Kyle Bayle|Kyle]], [[Antonius Bayle IV|Antonius IV]], and [[Kane Bayle|Kane]].

Based on recorded testimony [[From the Trial of Kane Bayle|from the trial of Kane Bayle]], we know that Linnet was sympathetic to Kane following his indictment on high treason to the throne, and advocated his extradition to internal family justice, a plea that was ultimately denied. Linnet's maiden name and family line are unknown.

*See: [[From the Trial of Kane Bayle]]*

### Apocrypha

It has been suggested in at least one [online forum](http://www.sagafamily.org) that Linnet was unfaithful to Antonius III and bore Kane from an affair with the Captain of the Guard. No canonical  evidence has been located to date supporting this insinuation.
