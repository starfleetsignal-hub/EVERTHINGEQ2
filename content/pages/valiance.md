The **Valiance** remains one of the most attacked of the Qeynos Sailing Ships still on the waters today. Her port is Qeynos Harbor and she regularly patrols the waters near the suspected [[Kunark]] continent in hopes of discovering more information about the mysterious [[Iksar]] currently plaguing the Norrathian seas. The Valiance captain and his crew also serve on the [[Bane of Prexus]], a ship berthed in [[Freeport]].
