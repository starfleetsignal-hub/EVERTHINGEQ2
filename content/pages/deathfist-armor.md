Named Monsters in [[Deathfist Citadel]] have a chance to drop special Common and Rare **Deathfist Armor Plans**.

These armor plans may be used by [[Armorer|Armorers]] and [[Tailor|Tailors]] at the [[Blood Iron Forge]] within the [[Deathfist Citadel]] or at the [[Blood iron forge (House Item)]] to create Deathfist Orc armor.

The Common plans contain recipes for armor for feet, hands and head. The Rare plans cover chest, shoulder and leg armor.
