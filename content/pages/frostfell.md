It's [[Frostfell Time]] in [[Norrath]]! Song fills the air, Norrathians bustle to purchase that special gift and scramble to fix their winter feasts. Those Frostfell Elves are out in force to bring cheer and gifts of the season to one and all. Come check out [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]] for joyful snow-laden adventure and rejoicing. No matter what you do, make sure you visit [[Santa Glug]] every 18 (real-world) hours - he has a special Frostfell gift just for you!

Frostfell is a [[Live Events|Live Event]] that begins in December and is similar to real-world Christmas. It has numerous quests, unique collection quests, exciting achievements, special craftables, fun games, and more. Frostfell was first introduced to EverQuest II in 2005.

## Frozen Tokens

The Frostfell currency is [[Frozen Token of E'ci]]. They can be exchanged within Frostfell Wonderland Village for valuable items like vanity armor and weapons, a variety of ice and holiday-themed house items, and more.

### Earning Frozen Tokens

There are many ways to earn Frozen Tokens, however notable ways include:

- Completing [[Frostfell Timeline|Frostfell quests]].
- A specific quest, [[Raiding the Gifty Storehouse]], allows you to turn in extra quest items to [[Gixen]] on the dock in [[Enchanted Lands]] for Frozen Tokens. Rather than turning in the quest, you can re-enter the quest instance and farm additional quest items ad nauseum.
- Once you progress far enough in [[Frostfell Timeline#The Icy Keep|The Icy Keep questline]], you can farm [[The Icy Keep]] instance for Frozen Tokens in addition to completing [[Covering Your Snow Tracks!]] for extra tokens while you're there.

### Recipes

Recipes follow a general pattern for the types of items you will scribe from them:

- "Frostfell Feasts and Decorative Crafts" are for a variety of house-only items, but contain all Frostfell-themed decorative food.
- "Frostfell Outfits You Can Make" are for [[Appearance|appearance clothing]], holiday-themed weapons, and some house items.
- "Frostfell Scholarly Pursuits" are for icy-themed [[Housing Item Limit and Building Blocks|building blocks]], decorative windows with snowflakes, and candles.

Each year the recipe scrolls have the same category title, followed by Roman numerals to indicate their year edition. For example, [[Frostfell Outfits You Can Make III]] is an older edition than [[Frostfell Outfits You Can Make IV]]. The previous years' recipes are available each year.

Just need to see which books you might be missing? See the Frostfell Recipe Books category page.

### Frostfell Crafting Stations

Located in [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]]:

- [[Frostfell Work Bench]] {{waypoint 330, -24, -1516}}
- [[Frostfell Stove & Keg]] {{waypoint 76, -35, -1623}}
- [[Frostfell Loom]] {{waypoint 129, -18, -1521}}

As of in 2013, players can make crafted Frostfell items year-round by crafting keys that unlock chests with the special holiday-themed tradeskill tables. For more info on how to get the recipes and make the specialized tables see [[Frostfell Crafting Stations]].

Though the player-made holiday tables can be used year-round, the materials and fuel needed to make Frostfell items can only be acquired from [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]], so stock up while the event is live! The fuels needed from the Village are: [[Elven Magic]], [[Holiday Cheer]], and [[Holiday Spirit]].

### Harvesting Presents

*Presents* are the harvest nodes in [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]]. When harvesting, it is important to recognize which presents you are harvesting from. There are three types of presents and they correspond to a specific Frostfell tradeskill station:

- The large red present contains components for the [[Frostfell Work Bench]]. These are used to make house items and holiday-themed weapons and shields, like [[Candy-Striped Buckler of Doom]].
- The stack of presents with a tiny *red* present on top contains components for the [[Frostfell Stove & Keg]]. These are used of decorative all food and drink items, like [[Stuffed and Roasted Fowl]].
- The stack of presents with a tiny *blue* present on top contains components for the [[Frostfell Loom]]. These are used to make items like [[Appearance|appearance gear]] and stockings to hang in [[Housing|player housing]].

Those who have completed the full [[A Gathering Obsession Timeline]] to get the upgraded [[Artisan's Fully Trained Pack Pony]] ability can summon it and send it out to gather seasonal harvests during this event.

## Other Activities

Don't forget - Frostfell is for having fun! As you explore the cities and Frostfell Village, you will find buckets of snowballs that you can click to get a pile of 20 [[Snowball|Snowballs]]. Target a mob or your fellow players and give them a good whack! You can also craft [[Sturdy Fruitcake|Sturdy Fruitcakes]] to pitch at your fellow Norrathians. If you are in need of more fruitcakes and aren't in the mood to craft, there is an unlimited stash behind [[Garsleblat Gigglegibber]] that you can click for 20 fruitcakes.

## Santa presents to pick up

![one of presents dropped by Santa Glug in Freeport or Qeynos Capitol](images/One_of_presents_dropped_by_Santa_Glug.png)

1. [[Qeynos Capitol District]] and [[Freeport]] have a visiting Santa running around dropping presents about every 2 - 2.5 real time hours.
   - This is announced by audible sleigh bells ringing and a message in chat *"The ringing of sleigh bells fills the air. Are you listening?"*
   - He drops 10 presents in randomized locations and anyone passing by can pick them up.
   - picking up a present named To: You <From: Santa Glug> gives a random gift. Possible items are: a simple snowman or snowwoman, a pet present, a few titles, Snowy Dwelling House deed, a flying mount, [[A cask of Santa's Secret Cider]], [[Blizzard Blue Gumdrop]], collectibles.
1. Players can buy a [[Gifty Bag]] from Marketplace for 300DB and open it by Santa sleigh in [[Qeynos Capitol District]] or [[Freeport]]. This results in a reward [[The Spirit of Giving]] for the player  who opened the bag and a separate Santa Glug's Lil' Helper who runs around the city and drops 10 presents for others to pick up. Those presents will have a name of a player who opened the [[Gifty Bag]] in their name ( Xxxx's Generous Gift).
