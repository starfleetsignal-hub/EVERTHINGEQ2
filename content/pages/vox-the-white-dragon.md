There are 2 encounters with the ghostly spirit of Vox, the White Dragon.

- [[Vision of Vox|The Vision of Vox]] is the contested mob in [[Permafrost]].
- [[Spirit of Vox|The Spirit of Vox]] is the version inside [[The Crypt of Vox]] and only becomes aggro if you click on the orb without the proper ritual preparation!
