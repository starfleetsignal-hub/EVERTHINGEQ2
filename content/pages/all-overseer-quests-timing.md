## Introduction

This list is primarily designed to identify the Overseer Agents that a Character is missing from their roster of Overseer Agents. The list of Overseer Agents currently available (not in cooldown or captured) for a Character is in strict alphabetical order when 'No Filter' is applied.  By comparing equivalent side by lists of Overseer Agents it is possible to page down and identify a missingin Overseer Agents when the lists get out of alignment.

Once a missing agent is identified, look up their season and details as these will determine which Overseer Quest must be run
