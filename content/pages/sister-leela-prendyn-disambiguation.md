**[[Sister Leela Prendyn]]** is a member of the Sisterhood of Erollisi. She currently only appears in [[New Halas]] ([[Frostfang Sea]]), but appears there year-round.  Historically she appeared in other locations:

- [[Sister Leela Prendyn (Antonica)]], in [[Antonica]] as part of the [[Erollisi Day]] celebrations every year prior to the introduction of [[Frostfang Sea]] in LU56.
- [[Sister Leela Prendyn (Everfrost)]], in [[Everfrost]] as part of a live event ([[LU53]]) focused on the Shard of Love.  Removed with LU54.
