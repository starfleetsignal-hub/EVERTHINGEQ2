## Overview

Loyalty Point Equipment Items are items that can be purchased with [[Loyalty Point Token|Loyalty Point Tokens]].

- The tokens are a special currency that come from completing [[Daily Objective System|Daily Objectives]].

### Free-to-Play Limitations

Players with [[Free-To-Play]]  accounts can earn Loyalty Point Tokens, but members with All Access (paid) memberships earn double tokens. All Access members can buy all of the items sold by the merchants, while some items can not be purchased by Free-to-Play accounts.

For example, the [[A small bag of platinum coins|bag of platinum coins]] (with 500 platinum) can only be purchased by players with All Access (paid) memberships.

To quickly identify which items are restricted for free-to-play accounts, place your mouse pointer over the item while in-game. When the item details window appears you will see the statement, "You must be a member to purchase this item" at the bottom of the window. See the example image of this exact action.
![Edited for emphasis: a note about restricted items appears in the item details for Free-to-Play accounts. (Click to enlarge).](images/Loyalty-f2p-limit-example.jpg)

### [[Noble Toowik Mclure]], Loyalty Outfitter

After you earn one or more of the tokens they can be spent by visiting Noble Toowik Mclure, a Loyalty Outfitter merchant.

- This NPC *only* sells equipment items, including enhancements for equipment.

Noble Toowik Mclure has identical items for sale in each location. Visit the location that matches your character's alignment.

- [[Noble Toowik Mclure (Freeport)]] near the banker (under the giant tent) in [[East Freeport|Freeport]] at {{waypoint -225, -56, 89}}
- [[Noble Toowik Mclure (Qeynos)]] near the broker in the [[Qeynos Province District]] at {{waypoint 901, -25, 49}}
- See the Related Topics section at the bottom of the page for additional merchants with different items.
