> **Gi:** A lightweight, two-piece garment worn by barefooted<br>
> martial-arts participants, consisting of loose-fitting pants<br>
> and a wraparound jacket with cloth belt.

The following items have a "gi" appearance. They do not appear merely as tunics or robes or chest armor.<br>
This table is presented as an at-a-glance listing of Gis within Norrath, and where/how you might go about obtaining one for your toon.
