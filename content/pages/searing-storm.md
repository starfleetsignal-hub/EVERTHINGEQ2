![Searing Storm effect](images/Effect_SearingStorm.jpg)
Effects:

- Inflicts heat damage on target in Area of Effect
- Decreases DPS of targets in Area of Effect by 100 per increment.
- Decreases Weapon Damage of targets in Area of Effect by 25 per increment.
- Kills target at the 10th increment.
