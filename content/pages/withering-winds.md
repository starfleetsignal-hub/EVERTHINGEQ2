![Withering Winds effect](images/Effect_WitheringWinds.jpg)
Effects:

- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 25.0%
  - Damage of this effect is harder then normal to mitigate.
- Reduces Potency of targets in Area of Effect by 3000% per increment.
- Kill target at the 10th increment.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means
