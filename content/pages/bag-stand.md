![All bag stands no matter which tier (rare, harvested wood) they are made with have this appearance when placed in a house.](images/Bag_Stand_Placed.png)
If you are not familiar with bag stands, you may want to read about the general topic of [[Sales Display|sales displays]] that can be placed in [[Housing|player housing]].

- All bag stands are Master Crafted (made from a rare material) by those who take up the [[Carpenter]] tradeskill.
- They hold [[Backpack|backpacks]] and a variety of items (primarily) made by [[Tailor|Tailors]].
