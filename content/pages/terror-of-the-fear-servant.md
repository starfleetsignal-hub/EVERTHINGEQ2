## Effects

- On a combat hit this spell may cast ***Thule's Terror*** on target of attack. Last for 15 seconds. Triggers about 1.8 times per minute.
  - Makes target afraid.
  - Roots target.
  - 15% chance to dispel when target takes damage.
  - Does not affect Epic targets.
  - Resistibility increases against targets higher than level 116.0
- On a hostile spell hit this spell may cast ***Thule's Terror*** on target of spell. Last for 15 seconds. Triggers about 1.8 times per minute.
  - Makes target afraid.
  - Roots target.
  - 15% chance to dispel when target takes damage.
  - Does not affect Epic targets.
  - Resistibility increases against targets higher than level 116.
