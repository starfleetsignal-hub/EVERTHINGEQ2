## Related Quests

- [[Whispers of Tyranny: Shadowbane and Shadowveined]]
- Level 128 Epic X 2 Tank and Spank, Lvl 128 SK With healer merc soloed him.
- Equip the [[Shadowbane Phantastic Gaffi]] from your inventory (given by Nabihan at start of quest)
- Fight [[Emperor Ssraeshza]] up to 40% and then wait for all his dialog to conclude
- Speak to [[Head Scholar Nabihan]] and receive [[Nabihan's Soul Gem]]
- Use [[Nabihan's Soul Gem]] on [[Emperor Ssraeshza]] which makes it unattackable.
- right-click [[Emperor Ssraeshza]] and remove the Staff.
- Inspect [[Staff of the Sacrum Va]] and select "peer into the orb at the head of the staff".
