This is a mercenary ability (e.g. [[Shallall'Dene]])

- Increases Wis of group members (increases with level)
- Increases Int of group members (increases with level)
- Increases Max Health of group members (increases with level)
- Increases mitigation of group members (AE) vs noxious and arcane (increases with level)
- Increases mitigation of group members (AE) vs physical damage (increases with level)
- Increases mitigation of group members (AE) vs elemental damage (increases with level)
