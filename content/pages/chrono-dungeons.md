## Overview

Chrono Dungeons are a feature introduced with the expansion Ballads of Zimara.<br>Initially, dungeons from The Shadow Odyssey, Sentinel's Fate and Destiny of Velious were available as Chrono Dungeons.

*Players who do not already have the Chrono Dungeon feature activated may unlock it account-wide from a Chrono Merchant in-game (or  - Note: Players who are members will get the normal -10% Discount if purchased directly through the marketplace, there are conflicting reports over whether players receive the same discount or not if purchased from the in-game vendor instead... proceed with caution.*

Subsequent expansions have added additional previous content to the Chrono Dungeons roster.

| Expansion | Level | Previous Content |
|---|---|---|
| Ballads of Zimara | 130 | The Shadow Odyssey<br>Sentinel's Fate<br>Destiny of Velious |
| Scars of Destruction | 130 | Age of Discovery<br>(Skyshrine and Drunder) |
| Rage of Cthurath | 135 | Tears of Veeshan |

## How to use

One person in the group needs a [[Chrono Dungeons: (Level X)|Chrono Dungeons bauble]]. *The bauble can be bought from chrono dungeon merchants in various zones and is only applicable to the era of dungeon for which it is sold.*

- Once in the regular dungeon instance, only one person needs to use the Chrono Dungeons bauble, which will then transform the regular instance into a 'Chrono version' at the appropriate level.
- Trash mobs become [Chrono Dungeon Level]+1, Bosses become [Chrono Dungeon Level]+3
- While every individual character can only use a single Chrono Dungeons bauble per every 24 hour "Mission Timers" timeframe, each player in a group may be the *recipient* of unlimited numbers of bauble-uses from others.
- Certain dungeons that qualify for the rotating 'hotzone' status award double the reward coins of a normal non-hotzone dungeon.
  - When attempting to re-run the same dungeon as a Chrono, standard dungeon cooldown/reset rules still apply.

*NOTE: While the levels of mobs in the dungeon are raised, the dungeon drops/collection shinies remain unchanged.  i.e. Cthurath-era Chrono dungeon, while 135, will still only drop the original lvl 95 ToV-era rewards.  The goal of running Chrono dungeons is NOT the loot drops, rather the currency rewards at the end after the final boss that are then used to buy current-era gear/adorns from the Chrono dungeon merchants themselves.*

### Repeatable mission rewards

- The character who used the Chrono Dungeons bauble will get the "Chrono Dungeon Commander" daily mission.
- All characters in the group will get one mission out of three possible ones depending on the availability of the mission timers:
  - Chrono Dungeon Companion Primary: [Level X]
  - Chrono Dungeon Companion Secondary: [Level X]
  - Chrono Dungeon Companion Tertiary: [Level X]
- All characters in the group will get additionally the "Chrono Dungeon Companion Weekly: [Level X]" mission once a week.

All missions will reward you with 'Fragment' coins based on how many nameds are defeated within the zone.
It starts with 3 nameds and raises for additional defeated nameds per instance.

Players may track the server-wide reset timers for different reward tiers by checking the "Mission Timers" tab of the quest journal, under the "Chrono Dungeons" dropdown at the top *(or by simply typing 'Chrono' into the search at the bottom)*
