Summer Jubilee is an annual event that runs during the [[Tinkerfest|Tinkerfest,]] [[Scorched Sky]], and [[Oceansfull Festival]] live events. During this event, jubilation parades can be seen in all 6 major cities,  special currency can be spent at [[A Jubilation merchant]]  and event dungeons with daily missions reward gear and event currency.

## New in 2026

Schedule for the event:

- Tinkerfest — June 11, 2026 to June 25, 2026
- Scorched Sky Celebration — July 2, 2026 to July 15, 2026
- Oceansfull Festival — August 6, 2026 to August 20, 2026

- Wuoshi, Varsoon, and Kael Drakkel servers - Start earning Mithril Jubilation Medals during Tinkerfest and continue earning them through Scorched Sky and Oceansfull Festival!
  - NOTE: Wuoshi will not be able to attain Jubilee achievements due to expansion availability.

## Plumes

New item slot was introduced in 2023: Event slot. This slot accepts [[Plume|Plumes]].<br>
When grouped, your Plume will be amplified by how many others in your group or raid also have a Plume item equipped. The power gained by your Plume is based on the tier of your Plume, so even those who have not unlocked the same tier will contribute to the amplification effect.<br>

Complete achievement series of [[A Jubilee for You and Me]] and [[A Jubilee Spectacularly]] by completing the [[Triad of Elements (Solo)]] to get your plumes and unlock ability to purchase recipes for lesser versions of those plumes to share with your friends. Be aware that some crafted plumes are tradable, but some are not and will need to be crafted using commission method.

## Event Currency

Every year uses new currency: [[Copper Jubilation Medal]] in 2022, [[Silver Jubilation Medal]] in 2023, [[Gold Jubilation Medal]] in 2024, [[Platinum Jubilation Medal]] in 2025, **[[Mithril Jubilation Medal]] in 2026.** Players who still have older jubilation medals can exchange them with [[A Jubilation merchant]] for current medals, for a fee.

Jubilation Medals are earned by:

- Completing live event quests for Tinkerfest and Scorched Sky
- Uncommon loot via opening clams during Oceansfull Festival
- Uncommon loot from killing end bosses in the following event dungeons:
  - Innovation: Tinkerer's Trial (Tinkerfest)
  - Doomfire: Ro's Sweatshop (Scorched Sky)
  - Awuidor: Trench of Terrors (Oceansfull Festival)
- Killing the end boss in the [[Triad of Elements (Solo)]] jubilee event dungeon.
- Level 120+ Daily Mission for each event's dungeon end boss (can be updated in either solo or heroic)
- Some [[City Festival]] and [[Moonlight Enchantments]] quests will now also award Jubilee coin while the event is active.

## TLE Servers

On TLE servers which have not unlocked The Shadow Odyssey expansion, event currency is obtained by gathering collectibles in the capital cities. In addition to [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog|Shiny Tinkerfest Cogs]], you can also collect [[Mithril Jubilation Medal]] and [[Perennial Plumage]].

The [[Perennial Plumage]] currency is used to purchase the [[Basic Plumes for All]] recipe book which allows TLE players to craft the Plume event items without having to complete the [[Triad of Elements (Solo)|Triad of Elements]] dungeon. Note that the recipe book is the only thing which can be purchased with this currency.

## Achievements

- [[A Jubilee for You and Me]] (introduced in 2023)
  - Complete [[Triad of Elements (Solo)]] 10 times. This enables you to purchase the recipe book to craft Inspired plumes and you can buy the Enduring tier of plume from the Jubilation merchant.
- [[A Jubilee for You and Me II]] (introduced in 2024) achievement to earn the NEW 2024 plumes and plume recipes.

- [[A Jubilee for You and Me III]] – (introduced in 2025) achievement to earn the new 2025 plumes and recipes.

- [[A Jubilee for You and Me IV]] - (introduced in 2026) achievement to earn the new 2026 plumes.

- [[A Jubilee Spectacularly]] (introduced in 2023)
  - Complete [[Triad of Elements (Solo)]] 20 times. This enables you to purchase the Genuine tier of plume from the Jubilation merchant. This is the highest tier of plume currently available.

- [[A Jubilee Spectacularly II]] (introduced in 2024)
  - achievement to earn the NEW 2024 Plumes and Plume recipes

These achievements are per-character and not account wide. The crafted Inspired plumes are the lowest tier; they are freely tradeable, can be crafted by any tradeskill class and sold on the broker. If you're only concerned about your main character having the highest tier plume, then you can craft the lower tier plumes for your other characters. However, if you want them to have the highest tier plumes as well, each will need to complete these achievements.
