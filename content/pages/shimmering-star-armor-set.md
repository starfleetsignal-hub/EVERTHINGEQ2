- [[Helm of Shimmering Stars]] *(See Note 1)*
- [[Shimmering Star Pauldrons]] *(See Note 1)*
- [[Shimmering Star Breastplate]] *(See Note 1)*
- [[Shimmering Star Vambraces]] *(See Note 1)*
- [[Shimmering Star Gauntlets]] *(See Note 1)*
- [[Shimmering Star Legplates]] *(See Note 1)*
- [[Shimmering Star Tonlets]] *(See Note 1)*
- [[Shimmering Star Round Shield]] *(See Note 2)*
- [[Shimmering Star Sai (Version 1)|Shimmering Star Sai]] *(See Note 3)*

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

**Note 1** - This **armor** may be worn only by [[Berserker]], [[Guardian]], [[Inquisitor]], [[Paladin]], [[Shadowknight]] and [[Templar]].

**Note 2** - This **shield** may only be equipped by , [[Berserker]], [[Guardian]], [[Paladin]] and [[Shadowknight]]s.

**Note 3** - This **weapon** may only be equipped by , , [[Berserker]], [[Defiler]], [[Guardian]], [[Mystic]], [[Paladin]] and [[Shadowknight]]s.

This complete set of armor, and matching weapon and shield, is dropped by [[Xiggalg the Fearfiend]] in [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]]
