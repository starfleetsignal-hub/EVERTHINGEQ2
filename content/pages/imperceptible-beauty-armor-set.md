**Imperceptible Beauty armor set**

- [[Breastplate of Imperceptible Beauty]]
- [[Mantle of Imperceptible Beauty]]
- [[Bracers of Imperceptible Beauty]]
- [[Gloves of Imperceptible Beauty]]
- [[Boots of Imperceptible Beauty]]
- [[Loincloth of Imperceptible Beauty]]

This set can be purchased from [[Sanya Farshore]] during city festivals.
There are no bonuses derived from wearing multiple members of this set.

## Notes

- These armor pieces give the look of bare skin, with the exception of the Breastplate and Loincloth. Equipping the Breastplate will only give male characters a truly bare chest; female characters will wear the default sleeveless blouse. The Loincloth will reveal the "underwear" all characters have by default. For male characters this displays ragged looking pants and a ragged skirt for females.
- The armor in this set is for appearance-only and has no stats. See the [[Appearance]] gear page for an explanation of how to equip these items **and** a list of alternate crafted and quested gear that has a similar function.
- If you can not wait for the City Festivals and have [[Daybreak Game Company, LLC|Daybreak]] Cash (DBC) burning a hole in your pocket, you can buy a set with an identical invisible appearance, [[Bare Necessity Crate]], from the [[Marketplace]].

Many of the items in this set were added or available during [[LU46]] and the [[World Event]] to rebuild the Spires during [[Update:53]].
