The language of the Sathirian Iksar within the RoK. Can be learned by doing the quest [[Feathers for Sethis]], which is in a series started by the collection [[A Mysterious Black Tome (Collection)|A Mysterious Black Tome]].

The ability to speak Sathirian is needed for the following quests:

- [[More Territory, More Problems]]
- [[The Saga of Yasva V'Alear]]
- [[The Broken Hand]]
- [[The Heart of Treachery]]
- [[They Have Searched an Age for a Dark Champion...]]
- [[Ill Omen (Mission)|Ill Omen]]
- [[Artifacts of Life]]
