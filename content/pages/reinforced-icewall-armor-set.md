**Silent Fist Initiate armor set**

- [[Reinforced Icewall Boots]]
- [[Reinforced Icewall Bracers]]
- [[Reinforced Icewall Breastplate]]
- [[Reinforced Icewall Gauntlets]]
- [[Reinforced Icewall Helm]]
- [[Reinforced Icewall Legplates]]
- [[Reinforced Icewall Spaulders]]

All pieces in this set have the same appearance and coloring.<br>
There are no bonuses derived from wearing multiple members of this set.

This armor is identical in appearance to all other **plate** armor sets (of all levels) sold by [[Gerbi Frostfoot (Frostfell)|Gerbi Frostfoot]] in the [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]] during the annual [[Frostfell]] holiday [[Live Events|live events]].
