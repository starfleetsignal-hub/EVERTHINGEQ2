Anashti Sul, the original Prime Healer, was sentenced to non-existence by the other gods when she unwittingly released undeath upon the inhabitants of early [[Norrath]].  [[Rodcet Nife]] would be later appointed to take her place.[http://forums.station.sony.com/eq2/posts/list.m?start=3&topic_id=411844](http://forums.station.sony.com/eq2/posts/list.m?start=3&topic_id=411844)
