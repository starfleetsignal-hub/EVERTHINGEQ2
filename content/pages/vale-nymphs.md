**Vale nymphs** can be found throughout the [[Rivervale]] zone and are required to kill for various Book Quests.

Some have been infected by the corruption that runs through Rivervale, and some are still pure and fight against the corruption.  Killing the blue-skinned corrupted dryads raises your faction with the Guardians of the Vale and the Seamist Faeries.
