This quest is initiated by examining the item "Green Hood Ration Recipe". This item is dropped on monsters in Zek. You must gather the following items in Zek:

5 Fish (Sharks or needlers near docks)
5 Deer Meat (from deer in Zek)
5 Bear Meat (from bears in Zek)
