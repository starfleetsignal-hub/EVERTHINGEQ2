There are 6 pattern sets, each of them shared by 4 classes:

- Shadow (for Coercers, Defilers, Paladins and Assassins)
- Vampiric (for Bruisers, Brigands, Mystics and Illusionists)
- Dark Void (for Berserkers, Dirges, Inquisitors and Necromancers)
- Void Infused (for Guardians, Rangers, Wardens and Warlocks)
- Undying (for Conjurers, Templars, Shadowknights and Swashbucklers)
- Everliving (for Monks, Troubadors, Furies and Wizards)
