Loyalty merchants sell items purchased with [[Loyalty Point Token|Loyalty Point Tokens]]. These are awarded by completing [[Daily Objective System|Daily Objectives]]. Players with [[All Access Membership]] earn double tokens for completing the daily objectives.

#### Loyalty Merchant

Tark Validus sells miscellaneous items, many of which were originally available as part of the Veteran Rewards system. He also sells legacy titles that were available during past events, such as [[The Guild Hall Constructor]] title.

- [[Noble Tark Validus (Freeport)]]
- [[Noble Tark Validus (Qeynos)]]

Items of particular interest to most players would be the [[Orb of Concentrated Memories]] which resets your adventure vitality once per week, and the [[Hammer of Adept Hands]] which resets your tradeskill vitality once per week. He also sells [[Betrayal Token|Betrayal Tokens]] which can be used to easily change city alignment and class where applicable.

#### Loyalty Realtor

Taig Rhul sells various housing items and deeds to tax-free housing for [[A blood-written contract|Mistmoore Crags Estate]], a [[A Skyblade Skiff deed of ownership|Skyblade Skiff]] and [[Luxurious Kromzek Keep|Krozmek Keep]].

- [[Noble Taig Rhul (Freeport)]]
- [[Noble Taig Rhul (Qeynos)]]

#### Loyalty Outfitter

Toowik Mclure sells armor, weapons and jewelry. He also sells adornments, infusers and some types of appearance-only equipment.

- [[Noble Toowik Mclure (Freeport)]]
- [[Noble Toowik Mclure (Qeynos)]]
