desc: A secretive and silent group, the membership of the Marked seems to be entirely ratongan. While it is unknown what goals this group pursues, they will without a doubt bring trouble to the other races of [[Norrath]]. Currently, the Marked are synonmous with the Black Magi, a ratongan gang active in the [[The Sprawl|Sprawl]] on the outskirts of [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]]. It is possible that there are other groups within the Marked, but none other than the Black Magi have ever been confirmed. The Black Magi are involved in a territorial gang war with the Dervish Cuttrhoats and the Dreadnaughts in the Sprawl. Peculiarly, the Black Magi along with the Guttersnipes, a former Freeportian street gang, are involved in a bizarre summoning ritual in the [[The Invasion of the Vale|Shattered Vale]] of [[Antonica]].
