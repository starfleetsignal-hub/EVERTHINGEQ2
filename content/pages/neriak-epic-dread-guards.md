The Dread Guards of [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]] have their names randomly assigned with every game reset.  This is a list of their spawn spots, since those never change.

They also come in three "stances": on foot, on wargback, and on horseback.  It is unknown at this time if (like certain mobs in the [[Fens of Nathsar]]) they can dismount and have mount and guard fight separately.  As of the [[Tears of Veeshan]] expansion, the level of the Neriak Epic Dread Guards has been raised massively.
