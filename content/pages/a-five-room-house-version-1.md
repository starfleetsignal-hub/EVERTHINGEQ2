This residence's floorplan is [[A five room house, version 1]].

**Features:**

- Enormous front room divided into 3 living areas.
  - Small garden area connects the main room to the basement stairs.
- Basement with secluded storage room
- Second-floor loft with sitting area overlooking part of the first floor
- 3rd-floor open-air patio.
