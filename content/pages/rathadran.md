**Rath'Adran** appears several times and in different forms at varying points of the game:

- [[Rath'Adran (Zek)]] -- The quest NPC at the fort entrance by the docks in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]]
- [[Rath'Adran (The Fanged Sea)]] -- The NPC that appears during the quest [[The Crossing of the Fanged Sea]] in the instance [[The Fanged Sea]].
- [[Rath'Adran (Monster)]] -- Rath'Adran changes form into a [[Drakota]] during the quest [[The Crossing of the Fanged Sea]] and becomes attackable.
