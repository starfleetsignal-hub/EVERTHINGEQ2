**Ornate Crate of Goods** is the reward a player receives for killing the end-boss of any [[Tears of Veeshan]] Advanced Solo (or Duo) instance. The reward for completing the instance is selected randomly from the content of the crates rather than giving the player his or her selection.  Armor rewards are only granted to classes capable of using that armor type; e.g. mages will only have a chance to receive the cloth armor rewards, plate tanks and clerics will only have a chance to receive the plate armor rewards.

## <Zone-Specific Accessory>

- Fractured Hive - [[Stonehive Conqueror's Gem]] (Ear)
- Nexus Core - [[Tiny Fractured Core Shard]] (Charm)
- Highkeep - [[Graknus's Ring of Command]] (Finger)
- Pickclaw Depths - [[Bolgin's Beer-Battered Choker]] (Neck)
- Stratum of the Protectors - [[Eye of the Silver Reaper]] (Finger)
- Chamber of Stasis - [[Hoops of the Mad Priestess]] (Ear)
- Halls of the Betrayer - [[Engraved Aerakyn Horn]] (Charm)
- Laboratory of Mutation - NONE

## Ornate Crates (Zone-specific)

In addition to the above-listed container-items, each zone's Ornate Crate will primarily award randomized equipment for specific slots.
Each player will only receive armor types that are appropriate to their class (mages only get cloth, tanks only get plate, etc)

The Ornate Crate from Nexus Core [Challenge Duo] ONLY drops its accessories and no other items
