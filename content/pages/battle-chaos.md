## Effects

- When damaged, this spell will cast ***Shock of Faith*** on target's attacker.
  - Inflicts divine damage on target.
  - Grants a total of 5 triggers of the  spell.
  - This effect can only trigger once every 0.1 seconds.
- Increases STR and STA of the caster.
- Decreases Defense of caster.
- Increases Slashing, Crushing, and Piecing of caster.

**note:** the numbers of this effect scale to your level when you cast it.
