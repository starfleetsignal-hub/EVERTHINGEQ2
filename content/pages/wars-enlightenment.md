## Effects

- On a kill, this spell will cast ***War Wisdom*** on caster. Lasts for 1 minute.
  - Increases AE Autoattack of caster by 5%.
  - Increases DPS of caster by 5%.
  - Increases Crit Chance of caster by 5%.
  - Increases Multi Attack of caster by 5%.
