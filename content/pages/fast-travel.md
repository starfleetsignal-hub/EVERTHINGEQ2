![Fast Travel](images/Fast_Travel_Icon.png)
Fast Travel is an [[All Access Membership|All Access]] feature where players can instantaneously travel to any major location on the map without using a Globe of Swift Travel found in major cities and docks. It is similar in functionality to a player having both the [[Translocate]] and [[Circle of Growth]] spells, allowing direct travel to any spire or druid ring. There is no cost and no cooldown period, you can use it as often as needed.

To use this feature, simply open your game map (the default is to press the **M** key) and there will be an icon in the upper left. Clicking on this icon will open the Fast Travel window which gives you a map of all major regions (zones) in the game.

Once you've selected a region, such as the [[Enchanted Lands]], another map will open for that specific area which will show the same icon at each location you can travel to. This typically corresponds to either a Combine or Ulteran spire, a druid ring or a dock area.

## Notes

- This feature is only available to players who have an active subscription.
- There are a few areas you cannot Fast Travel to, such as the [[Tranquil Sea]].
- Fast Travel is not available on the Time-Locked Expansion (TLE) servers.
- Using Fast Travel does not trigger a cooldown on your recall ability (e.g.: [[Call of Qeynos]])
