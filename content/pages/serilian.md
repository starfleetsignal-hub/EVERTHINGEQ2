The language of the Kobolds and Bugbears within the [[Echoes of Faydwer]]. Can be learned by doing the quest [[Language of the Dust]] from [[Lingualogian Roosey]] at the [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]].

[[Ratonga (Character Race)|Ratonga]] can learn the language simply by talking to [[Lingualogian Roosey]].

The ability to speak **Serilian** is needed for the following quests:

- [[A Couple Ringlets Missing]]
- [[Ritsy's Lucky String (quest)]]
- [[The Staff of the Observers]]
- [[The Stormhammer (Quest)]]
- [[The Bellywhumper Harvest]] (74 repeatable)
- [[Little Whiskersnapper]] Timed quest in Obulus Frontier
