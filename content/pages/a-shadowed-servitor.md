**Shadowed servitors** are currently found in four locations known to date. These are:

- [[A shadowed servitor (Thundering Steppes)]]
- [[A shadowed servitor (Enchanted Lands)]]
- [[A shadowed servitor (Zek, the Orcish Wastes)]]
- [[A shadowed servitor (Obelisk of Lost Souls)]]

Historically, shadowed servitors were also found in [[A shadowed servitor (Antonica)|Antonica]], [[A shadowed servitor (Commonlands)|The Commonlands]], and [[A shadowed servitor (Nektulos Forest)|Nektulos Forest]].  It is unknown why they disappeared.
