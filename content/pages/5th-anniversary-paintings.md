On November 24th, 2009, to honor EQ2's 5th birthday, 10 new paintings were available from city faction vendors. These paintings are:

- [[A Moment with Lucan D'Lere]]
- [[Avenging the Crabs of Jarsath]]
- [[Destroyer of Ordanach (painting)|Destroyer of Ordanach]]
- [[Humble Beginning, Higher Calling]]
- [[Love's Last Arrow]]
- [[Prayer of the Righteous]]
- [[Shattered Soul]]
- [[The Blade Singer]]
- [[The Enchantress]]
- [[The Hand of Hatred]]

All of these paintings are available from the city merchant for 55,385 status points and 9g 76s 33c.

- One of the Freeport city merchants for paintings, [[Valokis]], is located at {{waypoint 43, -12, 173}} in [[South Freeport]] by the gate to [[West Freeport]].
- One of the Qeynos city merchants for paintings, [[Jowling]], is located at {{waypoint 656, -13, 240}} on the second floor of The Herb Jar shop in [[South Qeynos]].
