![](images/Station_Forge.jpg)

**Forges** are used by [[Armorer|Armorers]], [[Carpenter|Carpenters]], [[Weaponsmith|Weaponsmiths]]. In addition to each of the traditional tradeskill zones, free-standing forges can be found in the following locations:

## Qeynos

- [[North Qeynos]]: Call to Arms
- [[South Qeynos]]: The Tin Soldier
- [[Qeynos Harbor]]: Hall of Steel

## Freeport

- [[North Freeport]]: Temple of War
- [[East Freeport]]: Armor by Ikthar, Hallard's Bolt & Arrow
- [[West Freeport]]: Gurb and Ironstar's **and** Hide or Coalition Crafter's Association (the crafting area, not just the instance) at {{waypoint 34, -12, 100}}.
- [[South Freeport]]: Block and Tackle

## Faydwer

- [[Kelethin]]: Gearheart's Forge, Tunare's Pages
- [[Steamfont Mountains]]: Bloomhege Provisions
- [[Loping Plains]]: Village of Somborn, next to [[Horatio Mercuria]], the Mender
- [[Butcherblock Mountains]]: Butcherblock Bay, next to [[Trader Darmlin]]'s tent

## Gorowyn/Timorous Deep

- [[Timorous Deep]]: [[The Wards]] {{waypoint 2805, 121, 1263}}

## Neriak

- [[Indigo Hollow]]: [[Forge of the Blue Flame]] at {{waypoint -35, 13, -5}}
- [[Dockside Markets]]: {{waypoint -120, 8, 299}}
- [[Neriak Down Under]]: {{waypoint -160, -2, 290}} and {{waypoint -188, 3, 327}}

## Kunark

- [[Dreg's Landing]]: just up from the docks
- [[Teren's Grasp]]: east of the entrance to [[Hall of Teren Rysis]]
- [[Fens of Nathsar]]: on Sathir's Span between two of the buildings

## Other Zones

- [[Haven]]: [[Ekroosik's Replicators]] at {{waypoint -32, -33, -192}} and {{waypoint -40, -32, -192}}
- [[Solusek's Eye]]: entry from Lavastorm, in the area with the Drednever Expedition force (the special wurmsteel-only forge is in a special instance)
- [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]]: not a real forge, but *an ancient draconic forge* used only for [[Bone-Clasped Girdle]] (BCG) and [[The Wurmslayer]]
