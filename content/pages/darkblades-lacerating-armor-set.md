**Darkblade's Lacerating armor set**

- [[Darkblade's Lacerating Coif]]
- [[Darkblade's Lacerating Spaulders]]
- [[Darkblade's Lacerating Hauberk]]
- [[Darkblade's Lacerating Wristguard]]
- [[Darkblade's Lacerating Cuffs]]
- [[Darkblade's Lacerating Chausses]]
- [[Darkblade's Lacerating Boots]]

No special bonus is gained by the wearing of multiple pieces of this set.

This is the [[Destiny of Velious|DoV]] Armor Set (Tier 2) for [[Assassin|Assassins]] that can be obtained via the [[Primal Velium Shard Mission System]].

These pieces are available for vendor purchase from [[Cramnon Spirittalker]] at {{waypoint -685, -340, 2755}} designated as the <Armorsmith> in [[Eastern Wastes]] at [[Ry'Gorr Keep]].
They can also be created by crafters.

Note that the [[Darkblade's Lacerating Hauberk]] only drops from a mob. It can not be purchased or crafted.
You can create it from a [[Tunic of Coagulated Primality]] which drops from [[Idol of Rallos Zek]] in [[The Temple of Rallos Zek]]
