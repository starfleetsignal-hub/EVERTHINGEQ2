The Ticking Tock is a disaster gone right. Created by the father of [[Ebik Wobblecog]], it left [[Queen's Colony]] docks only to sink immediately to the floor (of course, it was made of iron). However, because of the pre-shattering "miscalculation" curse laid upon the Wobblecog family, Ebik's father prepared for this eventuality by making the ship completely watertight and thus the Ticking Tock became the first "submarine" in Norrath.
