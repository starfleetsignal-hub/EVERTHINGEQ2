Quint Cerlius M.E. is active in two different locations: the [[The Thundering Steppes|Thundering Steppes]] and [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]]. In the Thundering Steppes he is a representative of the Far Seas Trading Company, while in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]] he is teamed up with the Green Hoods. His exact allegiances/motives are unknown.
