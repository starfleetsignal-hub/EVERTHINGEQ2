A Patchcraft Trasher is a kind of Patchcraft monster popping up in different zones and locations during [[Bristlebane Day]]. Killing them counts for the quest [[Plethora of Patchcraft Parts]] and the achievement series starting with [[Patchcraft Killer]] (killing 25 ), up to [[Patchcraft Executioner]], killing 20,000 of Patchcraft monsters.

You can find them in the following zones. See individual monster page for information on locations.

- [[A Patchcraft Thrasher (Antonica)]]
- [[A Patchcraft Thrasher (Splendor Sky Aerie)]]
