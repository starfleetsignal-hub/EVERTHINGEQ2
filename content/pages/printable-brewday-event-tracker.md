These tables are designed to help you keep track of your Brewday Event progress for multiple characters.

If a quest is Indented, it means that you have to do the quest above it first.

### Downloadable / Printable PDF

This ![Brewday Event Tracker](images/File:Brewday_Event_Tracker.pdf) is in PDF format and can track 12 characters. Click on the PDF icon on that page to view it. From there you can download it, or print it directly from the little icons at the top of the page.<br>

### Printable Table

The table below can track 15 characters.

It can be printed by pressing CTRL + P to bring up the print menu. On the right hand side, change Pages from All to Custom, and enter 2 in the line that appears below that. This will print only the table.

If you are using Firefox the page/table might not look right in the Print Preview. Close the print dialog and click [here](https://eq2.fandom.com/wiki/Printable_Brewday_Event_Tracker?printable=yes) to get a printable page.
