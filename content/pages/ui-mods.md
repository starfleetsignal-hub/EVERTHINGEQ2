The user inteface ([[UI]]) in [[EverQuest II]] can be customized to fit your style.

## External Links

- **[EQ2Interface](http://www.eq2interface.com/)** "Downloadable modifications for the user interface. Also offers forums discussing creating custom UIs."
- **[EQ2MAP](http://maps.eq2interface.com/)** "EverQuest 2 Modular Atlas Project (EQ2MAP or MAP) is an interface modification that provides user made zone maps as well as user submitted Points of Interest (POIs)."

## Popular UI Mods

### Fetish

The Fetish line of UI mods was designed by Fetish, aka [Zonx, The Green Troll](http://www.eq2interface.com/forums/member.php?action=getinfo&userid=12531|), and is downloadable at [EQ2Interface](http://www.eq2interface.com/|).

- [Fetish Nightfall](http://www.eq2interface.com/downloads/fileinfo.php?id=3563http://www.eq2interface.com/downloads/fileinfo.php?id=3563) - the most popular version
- [Fetish Classic](http://www.eq2interface.com/downloads/fileinfo.php?id=3631|) - the second most popular version
- [Fetish Themes](http://www.eq2interface.com/downloads/fileinfo.php?id=3750|) - alternate skins for Fetish

### ProfitUI

ProfitUI was originally designed by [Profit](http://www.eq2interface.com/forums/member.php?action=getinfo&userid=44893) in 2005. After Profit left the game, the interface was kept alive by the community on [EQ2Interface](http://www.eq2interface.com/). Development was then taken over by [gm9](http://www.eq2interface.com/forums/member.php?find=lastposter&t=10776) with the first ProfitUI Reborn having been released in March 2006, now the most popular EQ2 interface modification after EQ2MAP.

- [ProfitUI Download](http://www.eq2interface.com/downloads/fileinfo.php?id=3948) - Original download post, stops at version 1.8c - last version posted by Profit is here: [version 1.8d](http://www.eq2interface.com/forums/showthread.php?t=4746&page=1).
- [ProfitUI Reborn Download](http://www.eq2interface.com/downloads/info4277-ProfitUIReborn.html) or [ProfitUI Reborn Auto Updater](http://www.eq2interface.com/downloads/info4633-ProfitUIRebornUpdater.html) - ProfitUI Reborn, development continues
- [ProfitUI Reborn Portal Page](http://www.eq2interface.com/portal.php?uid=135011) - further ProfitUI Reborn related downloads and information
