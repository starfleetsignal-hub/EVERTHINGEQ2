This is a suffix title for competing in the [[Bristlebane Day Snack Snatcher: Enchanted Lands]] races five times.

- This can only be obtained during the annual [[Bristlebane Day]] event.

## How to Set a Title

1. To set any title you have earned, press **C** to open the Character window.
1. Click on the Options tab
1. Use the drop down arrow for the the type of title to select the title you would like to display.
- You can view the displayed title by pressing F1 on your keyboard.
