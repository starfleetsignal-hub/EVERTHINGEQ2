The original **EverQuest**, launched in early 1999. Everquest 2 takes place 500 years after it.

Referred to ingame as the Age of Turmoil, Cazic Thul revealed that it is an alternate past of Norrath. Despite this, various artifacts, and even individuals such as Firiona Vie, and Al'Kabor, still exist.
