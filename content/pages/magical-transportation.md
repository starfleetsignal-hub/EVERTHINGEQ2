Aside from tangible items and animals, there are many magical methods of movement also.

- Teleportation (Spell Type), [[Evac]] like spells, includes the various City "Call of" spells

Both new with [[EoF]]

- Portal (Spell Type), cast by [[Druid|Druids]] for one way gates to the [[Druid Rings]] around the world.
- Translocation (Spell Type), [[Sorcerer]] spell line for single/group one way movement to [[Wizard Spires]]

See [[Teleport and Portals Trainers]] for who you need to visit to start using these new travel methods.
