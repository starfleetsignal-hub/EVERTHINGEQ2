This is the Fabled shard armor set (referred to as Tier 3) introduced in [[LU51|GU51]] for [[Bard|Bards]] ( ).

The Molds for these drop in the X2 raid zone [[Ward of Elements]]. You have to buy the Patterns from [[Assilus Poisonbolt]] designated as <Elemental Pattern Quartermaster> in exchange for the Molds and some [[Void Shard|Void Shards]]. The actual armor pieces are available for vendor purchase from [[Borden Chargehammer]], designated as the <Elite Void Shard Armor Quartermaster> in [[Lavastorm]] at the [[Shrine of Thunder|Docks]]. The price is the Pattern and the respective Tier 2 shard armor piece. The Bracers are actual drops in the zone.

These pieces are upgrades from the equivalent Tier 2 pieces from the set: [[Subtle Harmony (Armor Set)]]
