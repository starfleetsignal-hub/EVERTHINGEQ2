Introduced with [[Echoes of Faydwer]], **Horse Stables** are, like [[Griffon Tower|Griffon Towers]] and [[Magic Carpet|Magic Carpets]], a form of rapid transit within a zone.

To use a **Horse Stable**, speak to the [[A stable master|stable master]] and choose the destination you wish to go to. Destinations are unlocked by speaking to the stable master at each location, so you can only go to destinations you have visited previously.
