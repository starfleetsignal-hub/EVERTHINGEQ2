Naturalist may refer to one of two prefix titles, each earned in a different manner:

- by gaining +10,000 (Amiable) faction with the [[The Tunarian Alliance (Faction)|Tunarian Alliance]] in [[The Elddar Grove]]
- by completing the strength AA line on [[Druid|Furies or Wardens]].
