## Notes

- The quest changes who you talk to, and where you go depending on city, but the end results are the same.
- There is no [[New Halas]] version of this quest instead [[Garple Gigglegibber]] will offer a prequest called [[Blat Bound]] to find [[Blat Berisen]] in either [[Antonica]] or [[The Commonlands|the Commonlands]].
- Once [[Blat Berisen]] transitions to [[The Grand Prankster]], this quest will not be accessible for that years season.
