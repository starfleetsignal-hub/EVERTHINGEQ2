## Overview

This event began September 22nd 2020. Though no firm date for the next expansion has been announced, this is a prelude to the expansion.

Unlike most prelude events, this one does not have a specific faction to gain. Instead, quests can be repeated to earn the special currency, [[G.L.E.E.S.H. Mark|G.L.E.E.S.H. Marks]]. The monsters you fight for the adventure quest have a chance to drop chests with the event currency.

### Event Location

To participate, travel to [[Teren's Grasp]] in [[Kylong Plains]].

All of the NPCs with quests are located close to *a sokakar tamer*, near {{waypoint 1975, 481, -865}}.

- The adventure quest requires travel to multiple locations: [[Commonlands]], [[Antonica]], [[Bonemire]], [[Feerrott]], [[The Sundered Frontier]].
- The crafting quests use Tier 2 fuels, with crafting resources *any* tier, combined a resources for the event.

### Achievement

Completing 25 tradeskill quests unlocks the [[G.L.E.E.S.H. Supporter]] achievement, which grants the suffix title of the same name.

### Tradeskill Quests

![A special resource for the event is gathered from special nodes.](images/Solidified_etheral_filament.png)
The tradeskill quests can be repeated. All recipes are automatically scribed and completed at standard crafting stations. The recipes are level ten but use generic, gathered resources from any tier, and a special resource only available during the event.
Fuel can be purchased from [[Gabin Baerea]] at {{waypoint 2193, 483, -730}}. The special resource, [[Solidified ethereal filament|solidified ethereal filaments]], are gathered 3-at-a-time from the purple, floating nodes that shimmer, also found in Teren's Grasp region.

After getting each quest, click the box near the NPC to reveal what you're being asked to craft to reveal what what you are supposed to make.
![The check box to disable use of Supply Depot resources. Click to enlarge.](images/Disable_supply_depot.png)

- [[Woodworkers Cutting the Workload]]
- [[Tailors on the Task]]
- [[Smithing the Hours Away]]
- [[Provisioners Fuel the Forces]]
- [[Sage Knowledge Applied]]

Tips:

- The recipes use generic resources, including rare resources if you are not careful!
  - To avoid using valuable resources, check the Disable Supply Depot box in the crafting interface and remove any rare resources from your bag. Remove resources from the depot and place them in your bag.
- The "any meat" resource includes fish.
- If you bring supplies to the location of the event, you can use the summoned crafting stations if you have the [[Alternate Advancement|Tradeksill AA]] that grants the ability to do so. Alternately, bringing the player-crafted portable crafting stations like the [[Portable Cooking Set]] will save time.

## Event Announcement/Lore

The [Announcement on the Daybreak website](https://www.everquest2.com/news/eq2-expansion-prelude-2020-haven-bound) summarizes the event as such:
*"Luclinites are determined to reclaim the ancient city deep in the heart of the moon, Fordel Midst, often referred to as Shadow Haven. It's a big undertaking and although they cannot yet use the Combine Spires to come to Norrath, there's nothing stopping us from helping them! Join G.L.E.E.S.H. (Guild of Luclin Enthusiasts and Experts Supplying Help) at Teren's Grasp in Kylong Plains, as they work tirelessly to provide the Luclinites with much needed physical and magical materials necessary for the reopening of the city. They're offering an extensive list of items in exchange for your assistance. There's something everyone can do, no matter how experienced!"*
