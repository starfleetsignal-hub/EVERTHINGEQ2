**Freeportian Studded armor set**

- [[Freeportian Studded Helm]]
- [[Freeportian Studded Mantle]]
- [[Freeportian Studded Wristguards]]
- [[Freeportian Studded Gloves]]

These pieces are rewards for completing the [[Outpost of the Overlord]] introductory quests.<br>
All pieces in this set have the same appearance and coloring.<br>
There are no bonuses derived from wearing multiple members of this set.
