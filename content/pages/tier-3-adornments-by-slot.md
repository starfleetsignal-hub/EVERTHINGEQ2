The table below details the **Tier 3 Adornments** organized by the slots to which they apply:

**Lesser** adornments appear with no highlighting. They require 1 [[Phantom Powder]] and 6 [[Jasper Fragment|Jasper Fragments]].  Up to 3 [[Jasper Fragment|Jasper Fragments]] are returned per craft.

**Greater** adornments appear with brown highlighting. They require 1 [[Infusion of Fire]], 2 [[Phantom Powder|Phantom Powders]], and 6 [[Jasper Fragment|Jasper Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Phantom Powder]] is returned per craft.

**Superior** adornments appear with red highlighting. They require 1 [[Clarified Mana]], 2 [[Infusion of Fire|Infusions of Fire]], 3 [[Phantom Powder|Phantom Powders]] and 6 [[Jasper Fragment|Jasper Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Infusion of Fire]] is returned per craft.

For a simple, alphabetic listing of tier 3 adornments, see Tier 3 Adornments.
