![](images/Adornments_small_white.gif)
![](images/Adornments_small_yellow.gif)
![](images/Adornments_small_red.gif)
![](images/Adornments_small_green.gif)

NOTE: all adornments that give health have been upgraded to give STAMINA instead!

### Crafting information

**White Slot Adornments** are created by [[Adorner|Adorners]].

- **Lesser** adornments require 1 [[Frozen Powder]] and 6 [[Frozen Fragment|Frozen Fragments]].  Up to 3 [[Frozen Fragment|Frozen Fragments]] are returned per craft.
- **Greater** adornments require 1 [[Infusion of Ice]], 2 [[Frozen Powder|Frozen Powders]], and 6 [[Frozen Fragment|Frozen Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Frozen Powder]] is returned per craft.
- **Superior** adornments require 1 [[Frozen Mana]], 2 [[Infusion of Ice|Infusions of Ice]], 3 [[Frozen Powder|Frozen Powders]] and 6 [[Frozen Fragment|Frozen Fragments]].  Up to 1 [[Infusion of Ice]] is returned per craft.

For a simple, alphabetic listing of tier 10 adornments, see Tier 10 Adornments.
