## Overview

Armor drops follows the same pattern as [[Tears of Veeshan]] ([[ToV Gear|Armor progession overview]])

- Solo/Adv Solo/some Overland Named will yield [[Phantom Armor (Altar of Malice)|Yellow Armor]].
- Easy Heroics will yield Blue Armor.
- Harder Heroics will yield Green Armor.
- 1st tier raid will yield Red Armor.
- (presumably) 2nd tier Raids will yield Purple Armor.

Yellow armor with four gems is slightly better than blue armor with no gems. The same goes through each tier (i.e 4g-AoM-blue is slightly better than 0g-AoM-green, 4g-AoM-green is slightly better than 0g-AoM-red and so on)

## How does AoM armor compare to ToV

Roughly speaking..

- 4g-AoM-yellow will have better STA and MIT than 3g-ToV-potent but slightly less blue stats.
  - [Strong Phantom Pauldrons](http://u.eq2wire.com/item/index/965046410)
  - [Potent Stout Vambraces](http://u.eq2wire.com/item/index/1997055415)

- 3g-AoM-blue is better than 3g-ToV-potent:
  - [Obsidian Knight's Pauldrons of Raptorial Agility](http://u.eq2wire.com/item/index/3883336992)
  - [Potent Stout Vambraces](http://u.eq2wire.com/item/index/1997055415)

- 4g-AoM-blue is better than 4g-ToV-potent & 1g-ToV-energized:
  - [Obsidian Knight's Pauldrons of Raptorial Agility](http://u.eq2wire.com/item/index/2002502340)
  - [Overcharged Potent Stout Vambraces](http://u.eq2wire.com/item/index/315726839)
  - [Energized Reflexive Spaulders](http://u.eq2wire.com/item/index/708231455)

- 0g-AoM-green is better than 1g-ToV-energized, will have more STA/MIT than 0g-ToV-mystical but less blue stats:
  - [Engraved Bone Pauldrons of Unliving](http://u.eq2wire.com/item/index/118256120)
  - [Energized Reflexive Spaulders](http://u.eq2wire.com/item/index/708231455)
  - [Mystical Plate Pauldrons of the Dragonsaber](http://u.eq2wire.com/item/index/218975478)

- 4g-AoM-green is better than 1g-ToV-mystical but it's muddier when comparing to 2g-ToV-mystical:
  - [Engraved Bone Pauldrons of Unliving](http://u.eq2wire.com/item/index/1293004030)
  - [Mystical Plate Pauldrons of the Dragonsaber](http://u.eq2wire.com/item/index/438642742)
  - [Mystical Plate Pauldrons of the Dragonsaber](http://u.eq2wire.com/item/index/462981219)
