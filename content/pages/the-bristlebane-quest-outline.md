- The old [[Tin Metal Protection]] quest, along with all of its subquests, are now referred to by this series title (per the devs notes in Testing and Feedback forums). Please be patient as this info must be tested for various zones/alignments. Info below is incomplete until testing is done, but retained here for possible use in this quest's info.

## When Location Matters

If you are doing the [[Tin Metal Protection]] quest, you *should* go to an area that suits your alignment; However, as long as one avoids the Epic and more aggressive guards, one may venture into any city of one's choice to complete this quest.

For example, evil-aligned characters should choose Commonlands, Timorous Deep, or Darklight; this is because the quest will send you into a city you may not be able to enter without being killed by the city guards. The same holds true for good-aligned characters.

If you are any other phase of the quest series or working on the quest, [[Rescuing the Princess - A Bristlebane Tale]], you can enter the zone by clicking on the book at *any* of these locations.

### Evil-aligned

- [[The Commonlands]] Near the ruined towers west of the West Freeport gate {{waypoint -1104, -59, 55}}
- [[Darklight Wood]] - immediately outside the gate for [[Neriak]]
- [[Timorous Deep]] -Gorowyn Beach {{waypoint 2336, 20, 947}}

### Good-aligned

- [[Antonica]] not far from the gate for [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 280, -14, 396}}
- [[Greater Faydark]] Near the Green Knoll entrance to Kelethin {{waypoint 563, 44, 24}}
- [[Frostfang Sea]] - speak to [[Garple Gigglegibber]] on the Erollis Dock at  {{waypoint -374, 31, 164}}, who gives you the quest [[Blat Bound]].
