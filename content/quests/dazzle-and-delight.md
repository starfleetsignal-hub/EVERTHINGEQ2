## Notes

- requires 32,500 [[House Vahla (Faction)]]

## Steps

1. Kill 14 flickering ignus in the [[Amphitheater of Song]] near {{waypoint 207, -154, 933}}. ![flickering ignus](images/Flickering_ignus.jpg)
   - *Note: they are the blue wisps*
1. Travel to {{waypoint -178, -11, 1136}} and deposit the cores around the island and the ![yellow sparkles](images/Yellow_sparkles.jpg) sparkles.
   - Deposit the first pair at {{waypoint -119.39, -27.50, 1194.36}}
   - Deposit the second pair at {{waypoint -117.55, -17.57, 1155.22}}
   - Deposit the third pair at {{waypoint -133.03, -10.17, 1126.36}}
   - Deposit the fourth pair at {{waypoint -179.89, -8.89, 1112.39}}
   - Deposit the fifth pair at {{waypoint -221.23, -12.21, 1122.68}}
   - Deposit the sixth pair at {{waypoint -225.68, -17.81, 1163.35}}
   - Deposit the seventh pair at {{waypoint -182.40, -22.20, 1194.82}}
   - *Note: NOT group-wide updates, individual clicking required.
1. Return to [[Mindfold Matriarch Taneesha]] at {{waypoint -300, 581, -663}}.

## Rewards

- At least 90g 72s
- +850 faction with **House Vahla**
- -850 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -850 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
