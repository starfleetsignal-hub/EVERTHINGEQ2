## Steps

![](images/Dead_cleffyl.jpg)

1. Search for clues in the western [[Drukyna Marsh]]:
   - Click on ***a dead cleffyl*** at {{waypoint -579.98, -46.51, 56.37}} for a first clue.
   - Click on the ***strange supplies*** at {{waypoint -617.24, -49.14, 105.58}} to get another clue.![](images/Strange_supplies_%28clue_2%29.jpg)
   - Click on the ***etched stone*** at {{waypoint -643.45, -47.06, 64.32}} to get the final clue.![](images/Etched_stone.jpg)
1. Follow the clues to an aluxob camp at the wall up to the [[Pakiat Bluffs]] and speak to [[Atkuki]] {{waypoint -680.33, -38.85, -9.93}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Aetherial Sketch]]
