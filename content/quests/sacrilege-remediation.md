## Notes

[[Derlak]] appears in camp on [[Nightscar Steppes]] when you are on [[Ward Removal]] quest from [[Vadreem]]. This quest, Vadreem's [[Portal Seeker]] and Sarkan's [[If I Only Had a Brain]] can be done at the same time in [[Oblivion's Bask]] area.

## Steps

1. All items needed by Derlak are found among lamias in the [[Oblivion's Bask]] plateau {{waypoint -49, 136, -234}}
   - Remove 8 smashed steles *(Ground Spawns)*
   - Remove 8 defiled icons
1. Return to [[Derlak]] in [[Nightscar Steppes]] camp.

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Defiled Icon of Mischief]] - house item
  - [[Smashed Stele of Valor]] - house item
