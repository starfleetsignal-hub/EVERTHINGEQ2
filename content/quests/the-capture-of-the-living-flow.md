## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- This is a subquest of [[A Water Tight Case]], and a sub-subquest of [[A Crusade to Faydwer]].
- While you can only carry one [[Living flow urn, empty]], you can carry an unlimited amount of the quest reward, [[Living flow urn, filled|living flow urn, filled]]. To save time, do not deposit 'living flow urn, filled' each time you receive one. Rather stock up four and then deliver it to either the [[Urn of Devotion]] or the [[Urn of Purity]].

## Steps

1. Obtain 3 *magic waters* (uncommon update) from [[A water spirit|water spirits]]. {{waypoint -796, -11, -1347}}
