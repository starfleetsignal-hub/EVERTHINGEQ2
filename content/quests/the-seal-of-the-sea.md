## Notes

- This quest may be started without completing the previous quests in the [[Peacock Club Timeline]], i.e. you can pick it upon your first visit to the [[The Silent City|Silent City]].

## Steps

1. Inspect ![a raised block](images/Image:a_raised_block.jpg) left of the back door. {{waypoint 147, 31, 158}}
1. Inspect ![a tower chest](images/Image:a_tower_chest.jpg), found on the east tower that stands between the two entrances to the Silent City. {{waypoint 102, 44, 318}}
1. Re-inspect the raised block at the back door.
1. Within 35 seconds to return to and inspect the tower chest.
1. Examine [[A lockbox]] in your inventory.
1. Obtain 6 gems from the nearby mobs in the [[The Halls of War (The Silent City)|Halls of War]]. The name of each gem corresponds to the name of the mob:
   - [[A Faro' Nuk hyas (Silent City)|a Faro'Nuk hyas]] - a Hyas Gem
   - [[A Sulite tjat (Silent City)|a Sulite tjat]] - a Tjat Gem
   - [[A Sulite arbiter (Silent City)|a Sulite arbiter]] - an Arbiter Gem
   - [[A Faro' Nuk mercenary (Silent City)|a Faro' Nuk mercenary]] - a Mercenary Gem
   - [[A Faro' Nuk tower guard]] - a Tower Gem
   - [[An Anuk' Sul augur (Silent City)|an Anuk' Sul augur]] - an Augur Gem
1. Inspect the lockbox again.

## Rewards

- 6g 19s 1c
- [[Seal of the Sea]]
