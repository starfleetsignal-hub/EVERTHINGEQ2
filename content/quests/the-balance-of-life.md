- *This quest can be obtained only by characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

All monsters found in [[Antonica]].

1. Kill 6 [[A sea turtle|sea turtles]].
   - Found around {{waypoint 136, -41, 56}} by the Qeynos gates.
   - All six can be found at along the [[Coldwind Coastline]], especially around {{waypoint -688, -38, -508}}.
1. Kill 3 [[A red tailed hawk|red tailed hawks]].
   - Found in front of [[The Tower of the Oracles]], and at the gnoll camp across the bridge.
   - If need be, kill the wild animal placeholders to spawn more hawks.
1. Kill 6 [[A darkclaw crab|darkclaw crabs]] at the beach around {{waypoint -2362, -36, -355}}.
1. Return to Daryann for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least: 11s 13c
- One of the following:
  - [[Willow Wood Blade of Service]]
  - [[Willow Wood Bow of Service]]
  - [[Willow Wood Hunting Blade]]
  - [[Willow Wood Maul]]
  - [[Willow Wood Walking Staff]]
