## Steps

1. Speak to Kerr Wanderroot when you are ready to begin
1. Protect Kerr while he enchants the broken stele.
   - **Quest appears to be functional again but there are two Kerr Wanderroots which merge back into one when the event is finished.**
1. Speak to Kerr Wanderroot again
1. Speak to [[Brint Thornwood]] {{waypoint -2164, -650, -460}}
1. Speak to [[Leif Tenderton]] {{waypoint -2399, -674, -179}}

- You must do this solo; it seems that with someone in your group, it fails. Put the person outside the group unlocked encounters and completed it the first time.

- You can use a mercenary. After Kerr finishes his chant, Suspend your mercenary and the quest will complete.
- **Quest appears to be functional.  It is no longer necessary to suspend your mercenary to complete this quest.**

- At least 1p 25g
