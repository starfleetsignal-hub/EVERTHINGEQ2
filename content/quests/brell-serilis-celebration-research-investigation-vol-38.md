## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of Brell Serilis has asked for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Gather the requested supplies
   - Collect 3 [[Golem Core (Level 130)|golem cores]] found in [[Aether Wroughtlands]]
     - *Note: drops in [[Chests|small chest]] from a silveroid sentinel (found in the [[Adja-Beryl Berms]] area)* {{waypoint -393.50, 130.17, 351.08}}
     - *1-2 drops per group member*
   - Gather or purchase [[Aether Ore|aether ore]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas
     - *Common harvest from BoZ rough ore*
     - *Can buy off broker*
     - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to [[Herald to the Duke of Below]] {{waypoint -241, 212, -56}} in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
