## Notes

Daily crafting mission. Reset timer 18 hours. Quest **expires in 2 days.**

Journal entry: In order to help support the continuing population of explorers in the area, I must help generate a local revenue stream.

When accepting quest you receive [[Recipe Scroll: Supply Mission Materials]], which auto scribes 4 recipes needed for crafting dailies.

Materials needed:

- 30 [[Ilmenite Ore]]
- 12 [[Fox Pelt]]
- 18 [[Larch Wood]]
- 30 [[Zimaran Coal]]

## Steps

- Harvest 30 x [[Ilmenite Ore]]
- Harvest 12 x [[Fox Pelt|Fox Pelts]]
- Harvest 18 x [[Larch Wood]]
- Craft 6 x [[Scrimshaw Carving Tools]]

Quest auto completes upon crafting the last item.
