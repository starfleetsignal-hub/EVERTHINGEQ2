## Steps

1. Collect 10 White Spiderling Chitin.  This is an auto-update of [[A white spiderling]] that has a close to 100% drop rate.
1. Collect 6 White Spider Silk.  This is an auto-update off any spider in the zone with a drop rate of roughly 25%
1. When all the above have been collected, speak to [[Garion Dunam]] in [[South Qeynos (POI)|South Qeynos]].

## Rewards

- [[Minor Elemental Ointment]]
- At least 2s 86c
