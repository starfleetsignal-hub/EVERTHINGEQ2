## Background

- This is not really a quest, but is required to access the surgeon's quests in the [[Tenebrous Tangle Timeline]].
- Do the following whenever you need [[Surgeon Kweezil]] if he is absent from the Hidden Shelter.

## Steps

- Kill all Scaleborns in the treehouse at {{waypoint -70, 0, -40}} on Temple Grounds island. You must kill them all before they respawn (6 minutes).
- When no Scaleborns are alive, [[Captive froglok surgeon|a captive froglok surgeon]] will run out of the treehouse and down the river to the waterfall.
- You can now find [[Surgeon Kweezil]] at his spot in the Hidden Shelter.

## Rewards

Access to the following quests:

- [[Restocking the Supplies: Toxxilac]]
- [[Restocking the Supplies: Rodcet Aloe]]
- [[Restocking the Supplies: Life Root]]
