This quest is repeatable.

## Description

"Sajas Goldbeam, a fae stationed at Green Knoll, has asked that I sow death and mayhem amongst the Crushbone forces below the city of Kelethin. This distraction will buy my allies enough time to plot and carry out their strikes." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill 8 Crushbone orcs
1. Return to Sajas

## Rewards

- At least 2s 57c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Sajas Goldbeam: "Are you volunteering to help us battle this orcish scourge even further, [Player Character]?"
- Player Character: "I am!"
- Sajas Goldbeam: "I knew you would bloom within this heat, my mighty friend! Go beneath the city and battle the Crushbone orcs. They threaten our city and while some tactical strikes are being carried out and others devised, we must cause as much disruption and mayhem as we can."
- Player Character: "The ones that cross my path will not live to tell the tale!"

Sajas has asked me to sow death and mayhem amongst the Crushbone forces below the city of Kelethin. Slay Crushbone orcs.

- Sajas Goldbeam: "You do not know the joy it brings me to see you once more, [Player Character]. Tell me, have you sown more death and mayhem amongst the Crushbone beneath Kelethin?"
- Player Character: "I have!"
- Sajas Goldbeam: "Praise the Mother of All! Thank you, again. Your service and sacrifice is greatly appreciated.

I've spoken with Sajas Goldbeam.

Sajas Goldbeam praised The Mother of All, Tunare, when I returned to him victorious, having sown death and mayhem amongst the Crushbone forces below the city of Kelethin.
