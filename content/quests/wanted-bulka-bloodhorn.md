## Steps

1. Kill [[Bulka Bloodhorn]] inside the tunnels leading into The Mines of Meldrath, at {{waypoint -1456, 95, 970}}. Facing the door to the instance, take the tunnel to the right.  His placeholder is a white [[A Bloodhorn vestal]] (43-44^).
1. Talk to [[Waynil Garwater]] at {{waypoint -562, 174, 919}} for your reward.

## Reward

- At least 8g 67s
