## Steps

I must make my way past the Temple of Scars, defeating all of the Awakened Legion that I encounter along the way.

1. Defeat [[Tel'koran Crimsonmaw]] in battle.
1. Find Charayan.
1. Inspect Charayan's body (you will get his aegis).
1. Defeat [[Theldek the Stinger]] in battle.
1. Defeat [[Dagarn]] in battle.
1. Use a portal to get back to the entrance.
1. Return to [[High Commander Vyrin]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 48g 35s 24c
- +3000 faction with **Claws of Veeshan**
- [[Enduring Drakiz Head Statue]] (a house item)
