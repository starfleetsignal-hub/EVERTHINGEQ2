## Steps

![A fractured stone slab on which statues are placed.](images/Afracturedstoneslab.jpg)

1. Kill [[Emperor D'Vinn (Acadechism)|Emperor D'Vinn]] and examine [[The Tools of Vhalen]] that drop in his chest. If D'Vinn is a grey con, he does not drop the item.
1. Go to [[The Bells of Vhalen]] in [[Antonica]] and play the bell. {{waypoint -1300, 38, 45}}
1. Speak with [[A vision of Vhalen]]. He sings you an epic poem, and gives you a scroll with the copy of the text, [[Knights in the Round (Scroll)|Knights in the Round]].
   - The complete text of the scroll (with clues) can be found [[/Song|here]].
1. Collect 50 (yes, 50) [[Knights in the Round/statue geo|statuettes of the Knights of Marr]] scattered across [[Norrath]].
   - For the most efficient way to locate that statuettes, based on geographic location, click [[/statue geo|here]].
   - For an alphabetical list of the statuettes click [[/statue alpha|here]].
1. Place the statuettes on a fractured stone slab in [[The Ruins of Val`marr]] in the [[The Commonlands]]. {{waypoint 522, -48, 293}}
1. Speak with [[A vision of Sir Vallius Bayle|a vision of Sir Vallius Bayle]].
