## Description

[[Garby Gingham Gigglegibber]] can't believe he got pranked by [[Tobel Patadash]] and now he wants to get him back with a prank of his own design. Never being one to turn down a chance to play a prank, I've agreed to help Garby create a pie of his own to give to Tobel. I must say, however, Garby's ingredient list looks a bit odd. I'm not sure how this is going to turn out.

## Steps

![a pile of dirt](images/Pile_of_dirt.jpg)

1. Collect the following items:
   - Harvest 3 [[Pile of foul dirt|piles of foul dirt]] from the [[Foul Stench]] in [[Enchanted Lands]] (underwater near the entrance to [[Runnyeye]]. {{waypoint 86, 10, -852}}
   - *Wasp guts* from wasps in Enchanted Lands. {{waypoint 106, -1, -50}}
   - 4 *bugbear parts* from Bugbears in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]. {{waypoint 143, 233, -204}}
   - Harvest 4 [[Wild apple|wild apples]] from [[Bush Nodes|mystical natural gardens]] found throughout the Enchanted Lands.
     - Wild apples bought on the broker or traded from others will also update the quest.
1. Return to [[Garby Gingham Gigglegibber]] and give him the pie ingredients.
1. Cook the [[Uncooked Gigglegibber Pie]] in the fire next to Garby.
1. Show Garby the cooked [[Gigglegibber Pie]].
1. Offer the Gigglegibber Pie to [[Tobel Patadash]].
1. Return to Garby.
