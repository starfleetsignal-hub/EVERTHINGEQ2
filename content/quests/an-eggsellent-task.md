## Steps

1. Go to the [[Chamber of the Diviners]] and inspect a chest at {{waypoint -252.4, 84.4, 277.6}}.
1. You need to acquire the keys that will unlock the chest. Kill the following:
   - 2 [[A Scaleborn diviner|Scaleborn diviners]]
   - [[A Scaleborn oracle]] or [[Oracle Tuunza]] - either will give two updates.
1. Unlock the chest.
1. Return to [[Gribbly the Gallant]].

## Rewards

Choice of:

- [[Eggshell Studded Belt]]
- [[Horn-tipped Longbow]]
- [[Membrane Stitched Duffle Bag]]
