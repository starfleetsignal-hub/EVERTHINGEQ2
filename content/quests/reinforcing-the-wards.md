## Description

The Quel'ule scholar Azira has requested assistance in creating more warding spells to strengthen the wards around Quel'ule.

## Steps

1. Craft 5 warding spells.
   - *Note: since there is an engraved desk nearby, you could opt to bring 15 [[Bamboo shoot]], 10 [[Sumac lumber]], and 5 [[Ethereal incense]] along and complete this quest on the spot.*
1. Return to [[Researcher Azira]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2500 faction with **Researchers of Quel'ule**
