## Steps

1. Collect 8 Pyre Tongue Shards in [[Wurmscale Barrens]]  {{waypoint 159, 135, 738}} . Red crystals at edges of pools of magma. They are very abundant; you should have no trouble finding them.![a Pyre Tongue](images/A_Pyre_Tongue.jpg)
   - *Note: They are not trackable and may be difficult to see unless you zoom in.*
   - *Once again, Level of Detail Bias to the rescue!  Turning this up can make a LOT of difficult-to-see harvestables viewable from much further away. And it has very little effect on FPS or lag.  (Options - Display - Model Detail, the first slider.)
1. Return to Klok Grissuc
