## Description

*I've agreed to help Lady Koivuu, the Royal merchant at the Court of the Fae by filling a cask with honey and killing a few bees to quell their unchecked population. I should head West of the Court of the Fae to the honeybrood hive area to complete this task.*

There are 3 quests that require you to kill Honeybrood Bees. It would be a good idea to get all 3 at the same time: [[Learning about Neurotoxins]] from [[Uric M'Ots]] in [[Camp Nybright]], and [[Refilling the Stores]] from [[Lady Koivuu]] and [[It Pays To Bee Nice]] from [[Soneej Viivi]], both in [[The Fae Court]].

## Step

1. Gather 6 bits of honey from Honeybrood Bees to fill a [[Honey cask]].
1. Kill 8 Honeybrood bees to lower their population.

## Notes

- Beehive area is -111, -54, 417
