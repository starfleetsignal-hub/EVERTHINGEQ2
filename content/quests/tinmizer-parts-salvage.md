## Description

Lieutenant Kresic has commissioned me to collect various Tinmizer parts located within Nu'roga.

![](images/Tinmizer.png)

## Steps

1. Collect 6 Tinmizer Parts within [[Nu'roga]], located in three spots in the zone, each with a five minute respawn:
   - {{waypoint 16, 63, 3}}
   - {{waypoint -60, 129, 39}}
   - {{waypoint 14, 1, 44}}
1. Return to Lt. Kresic for your reward. Note: The shard quest must be picked up individually at Omen's Call; it is not shareable.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]]
