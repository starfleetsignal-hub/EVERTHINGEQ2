## Steps

1. Save 3 sifay budlings from corruption.
   - Attack the non-aggro sifay budlings. Each party member needs to do so individually. After you defeat them, they will de-spawn and an NPC version will spawn that you can lead them back to the queen.
   - Only 1 budling can be lead back to the queen at a time.
1. Return to [[Queen Caleuh]] after escorting the 3rd one, for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
