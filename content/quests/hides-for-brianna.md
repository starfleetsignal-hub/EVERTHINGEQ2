## Steps

1. Kill rumblers in [[The Rumbler Caves]] for 15 updates
   - The Rumbler Caves are accessed from *a sink hole* in [[Dead River Basin]] at the bend south of The Village of Thundermist around {{waypoint 575, -21, 180}}.
1. Return to Brianna.

## Rewards

- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Acolyte's Plate Greaves of Devotion]] (Clerics)
  - [[Bandit's Chain Leggings of Agility]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Beguiler's Leggings of Intelligence]] (All Mages)
  - [[Expert's Leg Wraps of Ability]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Preserver's Leather Pants of Wisdom]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Sentry's Legplates of Strength]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Soothsayer's Chausses of the Insightful]] (Shamans)
