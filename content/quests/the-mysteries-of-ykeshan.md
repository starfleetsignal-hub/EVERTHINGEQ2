## Steps

1. Gather samples of [[Ykeshan]] from around the [[Moors of Ykesha]]:
   - Loot 12 *Book of Ykeshan Writing* or *Scroll of Ykeshan Writing* from the corpses of Ykeshan mobs outside [[Entrance to Guk]]. These are uncommon drops. {{waypoint 607, 49, -336}}
1. Return the samples to [[Linguobot]].

## Rewards

- Proficiency in [[Ykeshan]].
