## Steps

Zone into [[Ssraeshza Temple: Inner Sanctum (Heroic)|Ssraeshza Temple: Inner Sanctum [Heroic]]]

1. Kill [[Kesa'Tra Xon'Xiu]]
1. Kill Rhag'Vozgath and Rhag'Yalzzen, linked Epic x4 that spawns near death when you kill [[Arch Lich Rhag'Zadune (Epic)]] a x2.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 16g 20000 status
- 15 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- One of the following:
  - Crate of Armor (Chain, Cloth, Leather or Plate)
