The goblins are trying to establish a trade relationship with the Cae'dal elves in Nye'Caelona. It seems the elves have found the slugs are effective in keeping the borer beetles in check, and would like to trade for some more.

## Note

Upon accepting this quest, you will receive [[Slug Call (Recipe Book)]] which will automatically scribe the [[Slug Call]] recipe to your recipe book.

The recipe for this quest can **only** be crafted at a [[Goblin Workbench]] in the area. One is found at {{waypoint 72, -164, -722}}, near Oogthor.

## Supply List

Because these must be crafted in [[Twark]], and the required materials cannot be harvested in [[Obulus Frontier]], bring the following (minimum) supplies:

- 1 [[Malduran lumber]]
- 3 [[Splitiron ore]]
- 3 [[Umbrite]]
- 5 [[Thaumic Coal]]

## Steps

1. I can use a goblin workbench to make the slug call
   - Craft a [[Slug Call]]
1. Wild forest slugs are found in the Obulus Frontier
   - Capture a wild forest slug and deliver it to Lyraquel in Nye'Caelona
     - Use the Slug Call on [[A forest slug]] found in the vicinity of:
       - {{waypoint 14, -196, -515}}
       - {{waypoint 82, -200, -498}}
       - {{waypoint 94, -213, -419}}
       - {{waypoint 188, -235, -293}}
       - {{waypoint 385, -229, -234}}
       - {{waypoint 359, -198, -499}}
     - Deliver it to [[Elder Lyraquel|Lyraquel]] in [[Nye'Caelona]] at {{waypoint -204, 91, -209}}
1. Oogthor is in the main goblin encampment of Twark
   - Deliver the payment back to [[Oogthor]]
