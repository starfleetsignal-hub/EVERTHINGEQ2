## Notes

- You must dismount before stepping on the pad to start the race.
- While racing: aim to fly through the blue and green spots floating in the air to increase your speed, but dodge the spots with red lightning around them, because they will slow you down.
- If you click the big machine next to the quest-giver, you can see race results.

## Steps

1. Speak to the [[Aether Racing Referee]] located on the docks at {{waypoint -381, -23, -421}}.
1. Step on the the teleport pad to start
1. Complete the course in the designated time period
1. Return to [[Aether Racing Referee]]

## Rewards

**The rewards for completing the races varies depending on the number of times it is completed.**
Unlike the typical event reward, this mount is a hover disc, somewhat similar in appearance to the [[Veteran's Elemental Mount]] (minus the particle effects).
Depending on who spawned this track, completion will reward one of the following:

- [[Green C.R.A.S.H. Disk]] (Guides)
- [[Purple C.R.A.S.H. Disk]] (Customer Service/Developer staff)
- [[Gold C.R.A.S.H. Disk]] (Community Relations staff)

Racing against the volunteer coordinator and interacting with them afterward may reward you with the following:

- [[Purple C.R.A.S.H. Pad (Mount)]] (Customer Service/Developer staff)
