**Notes:**

- In order to get to get down to the [[The Forgotten Waterway]], you have descend the central stairs, [[The Descent into Darkness]], to click a chest at {{waypoint -53.77, -0.15, -11.96}} to open the secret door at {{waypoint -71, 0, -20}} allowing access to the lower grotto area.
- You should notice a red shimmering walkway on the South wall that crosses the water to the ledge where Monika is.  The walkway is invisible, and the 'red shimmer' may not show.  The invisible walkway starts near the boat's hitching post at {{waypoint -24, -10, 8}} and follows the wall around the left to the top of the center wall, and continues across the to far ledge.  You may have to jump from the invisible walkway to the ledges.
- Use the newly received [[Enchanted Whistle of the Freethinker]] in the corner {{waypoint 3, -7, -40}} to summon [[Monika Davissi]].
- To exit the [[The Forgotten Waterway]], click the chest at {{waypoint -57, 1, -22}} to reopen the secret door.

## Step

1. Go to [[The Cells of Oblivion]] and click on a shackled skeleton hand. {{waypoint -254, 4.9, -40}}
1. Inspect a book on a table in [[A'Nstasi's Experimentation Chamber]]. {{waypoint -251, 4.9, -15.67}}
1. Return to the [[The Forgotten Waterway]] and use the [[Enchanted Whistle of the Freethinker]] on the ledge in the corner {{waypoint 3, -7, -40}} to summon [[Monika Davissi]].
1. Return to the jail and escort [[A captured elf]] {{waypoint -254, 4.9, -40}} safely from the jail to the main entrance of the dungeon.  Everything will aggro and try to kill your rescue.  Respawn is too quick to clear the whole route, but you can clear most of the route before the rescue.  Speak to any captured elf to have it start following you.  Guide the captured elf to the zone's front door.
1. Speak with [[Chardasus Divok]] in the [[Village of Somborn]].
