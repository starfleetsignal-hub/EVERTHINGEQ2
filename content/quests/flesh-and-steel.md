## Steps

1. Kill 50 clockworks in [[Klak'Anon]].
1. Kill 10 [[A treespirit thumper|treespirits]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]]. {{waypoint -66.71, -16.76, -282.77}} (Lower Grove)
1. Kill 7 brownies in Lesser Faydark. {{waypoint -500, -55, -260}} or {{waypoint -351.75, -3.41, 5.19}}
1. Kill 4 unicorns in Lesser Faydark. {{waypoint 200, -25, -130}}
1. Kill 6 fae drakes in Lesser Faydark. {{waypoint 750, 70, -205}} or {{waypoint -180, -52, -188}}
1. Return to [[Tychus Zeksworn]].

## Rewards

- +5000 faction with **Rallos Zek**
- Unlocks the Blessing and Miracle Pair: [[War's Enlightenment]] and [[Battlegod's Furor]]
- Pet: [[Warrior of Zek]]
