## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
- Although not marked 'Heroic' in the quest journal, this quest requires killing heroic mobs.

The most efficient method of increasing your faction with a court is to clear the tower of guards from an opposing faction, **without taking control of the guard tower** for your faction. Instead, wait for the towers to repopulate for the current faction and repeat clearing the towers. The coins dropped by the guards you kill can be turned in at [[The Tower of the Moon]] which will award you faction.

There are quests towards the end of each faction's timeline which require you to kill the tower captains for each opposing faction. These quests only require you to kill the captains and do not require you to take control of the tower.

Ownership of the guard towers will reset whenever the game server is restarted. In practical terms, this means that towers will revert back to their original factions after a game update or weekly server restart.

If you are over level 60, this quest can only be completed once.

## Steps

1. Kill any [[A Blades tower captain|Blades tower captain]] or [[A Truth tower captain|Truth tower captain]] at the top of a [[Guard Tower]] controlled by the respective faction.
1. Click on the corpse of the slain tower captain. The tower is now under control of the [[The Court of the Coin (Faction)|Court of the Coin]] and will repopulate with [[A Coin guard|Coins' guards]].

## Rewards

- -500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- +500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
