## Steps

Note: Mass production will not work.

1. Craft 5 [[Recording Device|Recording Devices]] at [[Work Bench]] using [[Jeremy's Recording Device Schematics]]
1. Craft one [[Sonic Tranquilizer]] at the [[Work Bench]] using [[Jeremy's Sonic Tranquilizer Diagrams]]
1. Tranquilize 5 [[A Daar sentinel|Daar sentinels]] on [[Daarspire]] and implant the [[Recording Device|Recording Devices]].
   - Use the [[Sonic Tranquilizer (Item)|Sonic Tranquilizer]] from the quest helper, then right-click the sentinel to implant the device.
1. Return to [[Jeremy]] {{waypoint 142, 157, -168}}

## Materials Needed

- 2 [[Amethyst]]
- 16 [[Rhenium ore]]
- 6 [[Swamp ash lumber]]
- 30 [[Effulgent coal]]

- At least 1p 25g
- 10 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- [[Daar Sentinel Model]] (House Item)
