## Required Materials

*In addition to the materials harvested for the Requisition, you will need:*

- 1 [[Jumping Creeper]] -- *rare*
- 50 [[Coruscating Coal]]

## Steps

1. Harvest Materials:
   - 20 [[Slow Creeping Root]]
   - 25 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
   - 45 [[Hardened Zelniak Pelt]]
1. Craft a [[Precarious Rockhopper Statuette]].
1. Return to [[Fivalt]].

## Rewards

- [[Research Mission Reward Crate]]
- 8 [[Pico]]
- At least 1p 25g 7,700 status
