This quest is part of a 6-quest series; the first five can be done in any order.

## Steps

1. Visit [[The Pot]] {{waypoint -1029, 0, -532}}.
1. Visit [[Mound Lake]] {{waypoint -530, 6, -417}}.
1. Visit [[Black Water Lake]] {{waypoint -462, -3, -993}}.
1. Obtain a grinnin frill by killing any type of [[Category:Mist Grinnin Goblins (Group)|mist grinnin goblin]]. (rare drop) {{waypoint 68, 1, -967}})
1. Return to [[Kleron Asana]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Acolyte's Wrought Plate Barbute]] (Clerics)
  - [[Bandit's Light Iron Chain Helmet]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Beguiler's Embroidered Cowl]] (All Mages)
  - [[Expert's Fine Cloth Skullcap]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Preserver's Cured Cap]] (Druids)
  - [[Sentry's Hammered Helmet]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Soothsayer's Bound Coif]] (Shamans)
