## Materials Required

- 12 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
- 12 [[Humming Azurite]]
- 12 [[Luclizite Cluster]]
- 12 [[Radiant Algae]]
- 25 [[Coruscating Coal]]

## Steps

1. Create a Recuso Core sampler (table right next to quest giver works)
1. Look for 6 "grumblings" around the quarry area and take core samples. ![A Grumbling](images/A_Grumbling.png)
   - *The grumblings only spawn as you are approaching their location and will de-spawn in approximately 10 seconds.*
   - *They are located around the quarry walls as pulsing crystals on the ground.
   - *Get very close to the crystal or the reading will fail. It is recommended that you stand on top of a crystal to ensure you are close enough.*
   - *Do not attempt to target the grumblings, this is a pointless waste of time. Just use the Recuso Core Sampler when in position.*
   - *It takes the tool a few seconds to give a reading.*
   - The Grumblings have static spawn locations that do not move. There's an exact number of spawns that you need. Locations are:
     - {{waypoint -34, 158, -21}}
     - {{waypoint -72, 179, -51}}
     - {{waypoint -38, 165, 106}}
     - {{waypoint 95, 156, 136}}
     - {{waypoint 208, 123, 97}}
     - {{waypoint 153, 108, 26}}
   - *On initial quest release the grumblings did not refresh after they de-spawned. At 16/12/2023 it is confirmed they do respawn after a reasonable time.*
1. Keep an eye out for [[Jiggly Mushroom|jiggly mushrooms]] in the same areas, as you will need to collect 5 of them.
   - *Picking up the mushrooms requires 500 in Gathering. You can skill-up from attempts if your skill is too low. (450 with the Far Seas Trade Skill Prestige "Experienced Reaping" which adds 50 to all of your harvesting skills.)*
   - *These look like bright orange gum drops. (Depending on your display settings, these may appear as bright yellow)*
   - *Harvest tracking works. Shows up as Odd Looking Mushroom.*
1. Return to Foreman Gibbons.
1. Seek out [[Erdani Ruilep]] {{waypoint 367, -36, 730}} within the [[Luclin Nexus]].

## Rewards

- At least 4p 46g 96s 59c
- [[A Grumble]] (house item)
- RoS Flag: Savage Weald Unlock
- Ability to Fly
