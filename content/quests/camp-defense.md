## Steps

1. Gather materials for bonfires and barricades near the beach:
   - Gather 5 bones:
     - {{waypoint 4455, -1089, 1909}}
     - {{waypoint 4474, -1090, 1901}}
     - {{waypoint 4510, -1092, 1953}}
     - {{waypoint 4501, -1093, 1977}}
     - {{waypoint 4519, -1092, 2011}}
     - {{waypoint 4560, -1096, 2018}}
     - {{waypoint 4548, -1095, 2064}}
   - Gather 6 [[A piece of driftwood|pieces of driftwood]] for bonfires:
     - {{waypoint 4420, -1088, 1678}}
     - {{waypoint 4438, -1088, 1714}}
     - {{waypoint 4490, -1091, 1726}}
     - {{waypoint 4526, -1092, 1754}}
     - {{waypoint 4489, -1090, 1891}}
     - {{waypoint 4563, -1093, 1838}}
     - {{waypoint 4608, -1093, 1906}}
     - {{waypoint 4553, -1095, 2013}}
     - {{waypoint 4545, -1094, 2029}}
     - {{waypoint 4486, -1091, 2052}}
     - {{waypoint 4439, -1090, 2097}}
1. Erect a barricade near the south edge of the camp - click the white sparkle effect {{waypoint 4267, -1097, 2073}}
1. "Light" [[A pile of driftwood]] from your inventory to places bonfires that burn 20 undead othmir.  You'll have 2 fire starters.
   - The bonfire will appear around you when placed; use your position to place the fires effectively
   - Speak with Merrik if you need another pile of driftwood (they will burn out eventually)
   - Placing the fires at the entrance to [[Yeppa's Last Stand]] {{waypoint 4279, -1096, 2053}} is fairly effective, giving you 20 updates in a couple of minutes
   - It is not necessary to place two; one can suffice
   - You can speed the process by body-pulling mobs to the fires; they start burning, turn grey, and attack the othmir defenders
   - You can speed it up a if you kill the undead ones that got past the bonfire to trigger to next wave
1. Speak to Merrik

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
