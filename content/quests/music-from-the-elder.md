## Steps

1. Speak to [[Elder Zet]] at {{waypoint -860, 82, -457}}.
1. Travel to {{waypoint -613, -49, 201}} and use [[Pakiat Divining Device (Music From the Elder)|Pakiat Divining Device]].![ghostly book](images/Ghostly_book.jpg)
1. Read the book.![The book](images/The_Story_of_the_Crimson_Rebec.jpg)
1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (Music From the Elder)|Pakiat Diving Device]] again
1. Travel to {{waypoint -898, 296, 980}} (on the floating island) and use [[Pakiat Divining Device (Music From the Elder)|Pakiat Diving Device]].
1. Read the book
1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (Music From the Elder)|Pakiat Diving Device]] again
1. Speak to [[Elder Zet]] at {{waypoint -860, 82, -457}}.
   - Answer the questions. (2-3-1)
   - It was painted with blood.
   - He was betrayed by the town and it was revenge.
   - Powerful artifacts can be created by traumatic events.
