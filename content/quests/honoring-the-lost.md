## Steps

1. Click 6 **Memorium Candles** at the [[Memorial of the Wayward]] upstairs on the west side of the [[Myrist Mezzanine]] at {{waypoint 336, 15, -13}}.
1. Speak with [[Rourre]].
1. Collect **Rourre's Urn** from the shelf behind [[Rourre]] at {{waypoint 342, 17, -14}}
1. Take the transporter downstairs on the first floor, at {{waypoint 257, -19, -287}} to travel to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]]. Enter [[Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wind]] through the portal at {{waypoint 714, 412, -380}}.
