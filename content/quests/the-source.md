## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A Rilissian cabalist|Rilissian cabalists]], found at {{waypoint -1441, -105, 1806}} and {{waypoint -1632, -107, 1693}}.
1. Return to [[Oracle Vorzalka]] in [[Bathezid's Watch]].

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Exarch's Blessed Sacrament Plate Greaves]] (Clerics)
  - [[Executioner's Truesteel Legplates]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Illuminate's Still Water Leg Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Magus's Glowing Gossamer Leggings]] (All Mages)
  - [[Outrider's Blacksteel Chain Leggings]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Stormcaller's Spring Weave Leather Pants]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Witchdoctor's Primal Wrath Chausses]] (Shamans)
- +5000 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
