## Description

"Amber Walker, a provisioner at Green Knoll, promises to make a delicious meal out of the spider meat I provided her earlier. She just needs me to gather cedar star nuts for her." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

![Cedar Nuts](images/ForagingintheGroveQuestObject.JPG)

1. Gather 5 cedar star nuts from glowing bushes under [[Kelethin]]
   - The bushes are not tab-targetable, and can be found anywhere under Kelethin
1. Return to [[Amber Walker]]

## Rewards

- At least 76c
- 3 [[Nut Encrusted Spider Flank|Nut Encrusted Spider Flanks]]
- [[Kelethin brazier]]
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Fighter's Earring (Version 2)|Fighter's Earring]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Earring (Version 2)|Priest's Earring]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Earring (Version 2)|Scout's Earring]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Earring (Version 2)|Sorcerer's Earring]] (All Mages)
