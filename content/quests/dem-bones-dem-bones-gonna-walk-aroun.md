## Steps

1. Find out what happened to [[Criggle Cloudpuff]] on the [[Isle of Discord]].
   - Fly to the [[Isle of Discord]].
   - Collect the glowing bones in front of the entrance to [[Ascent of the Awakened]] at {{waypoint -181, -166, -284}}. (This is down at the bottom of the island. From the landing place on the Isle of Discord, you should face north and carefully walk off the cliff, ensuring that you will land on the out-cropping below. A Leaper mount is a great help here, as you will not take any falling damage.)
1. Return to [[Clarissa Cloudpuff]] on the [[Isle of Desolation]].

- At least 14g 81s 38c
