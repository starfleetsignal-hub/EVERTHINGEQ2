## Starting the Quest

You have received a letter from [[Queen Antonia Bayle (Queen Antonia's Chambers)|Queen Antonia]] asking you to come and speak with her at [[Queen Antonia's Chambers]] in [[North Qeynos]] in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 491, 5, -21}}. This series of quests runs in parallel with the quests that begin with [[Lilly Ironforge]], starting with [[Ironforged Requests]]. You must complete both the Queen's quests and Lilly's quests to proceed further into the quest line with [[The Eyes Have It]].

## Notes

- Scales with player level, requires level 60+
- Although [[Best of Intentions]] is what begins the series of Qeynos city quests at level 60, it does not directly send you to Queen Antonia.
- If you deleted Queen Antonia's letter, or haven't received it, speak with Messenger Tibeld in Qeynos Province District, or Jerol in The Village of Shin. They can provide you with the letter if you're eligible to receive it.
- Check your journal to make sure you've completed both [[Closing the Black Market]] and [[Sink or Swim]] if you don't receive the letter. Both of these quests are prerequisites to continuing the Qeynos city quest timeline.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Antonica]] to seek the Queen's missing runner
   - The corpse of the missing messenger will be found on the [[Qeynos Highway]] almost exactly in the center of Antonica. {{waypoint -1201, -20, 244}}
   - As you approach the body, you will be ambushed by 3 [[A stalking timber wolf|stalking timber wolves]].
   - Right-click to search the body and discover a map.
     - NOTE: Map is not an item in your inventory.
1. Look for [[Outrider Lidsey]] at {{waypoint -1186, 16, 306}}. She will tell you of gnoll incursions and give you the missing report.
1. Return to [[Queen Antonia Bayle (Queen Antonia's Chambers)|Queen Antonia]] and listen to her meeting with the Council of Ten.
