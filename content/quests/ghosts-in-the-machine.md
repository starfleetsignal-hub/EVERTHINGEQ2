It turns out that the senile gnome mentor in eastern Freeport, Professor Flimflam, didn't need all those odd clockwork bits I provided to him, as he had already made a necro-clockwork! Unfortunately though, his necromechanoid is running on a low charge. He has asked me to lead it into The Graveyard and obtain some souls to fuel it.

**Note Mercs can be used**

## Steps

1. Click on Flimflam's Necromechanoid and tell it to "Follow Me!"
1. Equip Flimflam's Incorporeal-Visual Activators in order to see the ghosts
1. Use any Freeport Bell to enter The Graveyard
1. defeat 10 A Knight of Truth ghosts (double click or right click and select attack)
1. Return to Professor Flimflam

## Rewards

- At least 19g 93s 2c
