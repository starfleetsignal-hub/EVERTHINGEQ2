## Steps

1. Find:
   - Somborn Weapons Crate {{waypoint -819, 7, 22}} It's in the Bummer Gang Camp near Wayunder Lake.
   - Somborn Tools Crate {{waypoint -454, -16, -154}} It's in a Bummer Gang Camp near the river.
   - Somborn Medicine Crate {{waypoint 152, 45.55, -330}} It's near the Reconstructed Ulteran Spire in a broken wagon.  Be careful of the gremlin trickters and muses (lv 63-64).  They will swarm you if you go for the crate, but can be picked off one by one if you can't handle them all together.
     - *NOTE: The crates have a respawn time of about 15 seconds.*
1. Return to Retainer Bacchante.

  - [[Woven Werewolf Hair Circlet]]
