## Notes

- When starting this quest a mount's ability to fly is restored.
- Djinn may drop a [[Glass Orb]] to initiate or progress the language quest [[Words of Air]].
- Loot the various metallic Djinn to progress [[Lore and Legend: Djinn]].
- Side-quest [[Splendor Recycling]] also requires mobs in [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion (Solo)|Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion [Solo]]], but is not offered until this quest is complete.
  - **Note:** However if you leave at least **12** Maedjinn trash alive you will be able to use the same instance to do the later quest.

## Steps

1. ![Hoot Totem](images/Coursequill's_Hoot_Totem.jpg)Destroy hoot totem at western end of [[Raptor's Landing]] {{waypoint -753.77, 186.34, 165.30}} (group-wide update)
1. Destroy hoot totem at eastern end of [[Raptor's Landing]] {{waypoint -855.41, 174.51, 210.34}} (group-wide update)
1. Kill a metal-skinned djinn attacker.
1. Speak with [[Vofres]] at {{waypoint -849.02, 175.86, 191.86}}
1. [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion (Solo)|Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion [Solo]]] near {{waypoint -795.30, 174.52, 194.89}}
1. Speak to [[Miranna Ameema]] near the entrance at {{waypoint -801.78, 84.09, 269.76}}
1. Do what is necessary to stop the metallic djinn incursion:
   - Talk to [[Kusala’Din]] at {{waypoint -744, 63, 407}} to spawn [[Magmalatorr]].
   - Talk to [[Kusala’Din]] at {{waypoint -566, 79, 667}} to spawn [[Tkesh’Tura]].
1. After killing the first two nameds ([[Magmalatorr]] and [[Tkesh’Tura]]) you will get the first update.
1. Continue doing what is necessary to stop the metallic djinn incursion:
   - Kill 3 [[A Zakir Rish Conduit|Zakir Rish Conduits]] to receive a Glass Orb. Some possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -527, 81, 692}}
     - {{waypoint -766, 61, 539}}
     - {{waypoint -776, 84, 610}}
     - {{waypoint -763, 62, 542}}
1. ![Teleport to Molted Island](images/Teleport_to_Molted_Island.jpg)Use the crystal at {{waypoint -793.09, 84.70, 622.25}} to teleport up to [[Molted Island]], and kill [[Grand Conjurer Kusala'Din]].
   - protip: wedge yourself with a rock behind you to limit being tossed around. eg. {{waypoint -1034, 175, 650}}
1. Speak with [[Miranna Ameema]] at the [[Aether Breach]] {{waypoint -1083.33, 181.71, 753.22}}
1. Enter the breach (Zones you into [[Zimara Breadth]]). The portal in [[Zimara Breadth]] is at {{waypoint -1089, 181, 736}}
1. Speak with [[Miranna Ameema]] nearby at {{waypoint -687.72, 228.98, -752.21}}
   - NOTE: If you exit the instance back to Splendor, Miranna is also near the portal to Zimara breadth and you can complete the quest there.
