## Steps

1. Kill the [[Enraged dwarven Briarstone]] that spawns at {{waypoint 562, -29, 208}} when you approach.
   - **Note:** The ghost does NOT spawn every time, so you may have to kill the [[Enraged dwarven Briarstone]] several times!
1. Speak to [[Hube Flintwrit]] who will spawn in the same location.
1. Return to Thadus.

## Reward

- At least 2s 91c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Brawler's Belt of Shurikens]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Empowered Totem]] (All Mages)
  - [[Forged Buckler]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Shortbow]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Thug's Knifebelt]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Vanguard's Shortbow]] (Warriors, Crusaders)
