This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. A message appears from Drinal: "*The Harrowing Horde must be destroyed. Thus is the command of the Silver Reaper. Succeed, and you shall be rewarded. Fail, and I will welcome you from beyond your grave.*"
1. Do you accept Drinal's request? … Select: **I do**
1. How devoted are you, convert? … Select: **Somewhat**, and accept quest
1. Kill 15 members of the Harrowing Horde in Obol Plains.
1. Return to the Altar to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
