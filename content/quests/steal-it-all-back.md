## Note

This repeatable quest and the quest [[Still Not for Breakfast]] are used to gain the +5,000 [[House Vahla (Faction)]] required for the next quests.

## Steps

Recover the stolen arcanum from these thieves that can be found on the plateaus in [[Pakiat Bluffs]]:

- 5 [[Alux bluffwalker|alux bluffwalkers]] {{waypoint -420.00, 97.56, -760.87}} or [[Alux cemicaller|alux cemicallers]] {{waypoint -882.87, 133.38, -740.64}}
- 4 [[Pakiat siamang|pakiat siamangs]] {{waypoint -669.52, 73.23, -431.27}}.

## Rewards

- At least 90g 72s
- +5000 faction with **House Vahla**
- -5000 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -5000 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
