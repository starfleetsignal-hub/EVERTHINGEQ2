This quest is repeatable up to 4 times.

## Steps

- Kill 7 [[A stoneslough coiler|stoneslough coilers]] in [[The Petrified Badlands]].
- Fly to the Eye of Dartain and head towards {{waypoint 1471, -1, 3962}}

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +100 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Seekers of the Dark Truth**
