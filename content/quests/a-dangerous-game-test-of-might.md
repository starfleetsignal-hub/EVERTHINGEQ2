## Prerequisites

- You must have at least +24,000 faction with Clan Stormshield to obtain this quest. This amount of faction is reached by completing all the other quests offered by [[Kror Stormshield]], including the repeatable ones.

## Steps

1. Hunt for [[Pharynx]]. {{waypoint -6, 21, 680}}
1. Return to [[Kror Stormshield]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 5g
- +800 faction with **Clan Stormshield**
- [[Warslick Wrap of Defense]]
- [[Warslick Wrap of Stealth]] (scouts)
