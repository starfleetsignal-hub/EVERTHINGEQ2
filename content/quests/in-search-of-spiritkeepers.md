*This quest is also part of the [[Wailing Caves Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Wailing Caves]].
1. Slay 5 [[A Ree spiritkeeper|Ree spiritkeepers]]. These monsters are located in the deeper parts of the dungeon, around {{waypoint -165.47, -55.56, -27.79}}.
1. Return the scalps to [[Lieutenant Darrius]].

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
- +95 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
- [[Corrupted Bracelet of the Spiritkeeper]]
