## Steps

1. Return to [[High Oracle Akshara]] in [[Svarni Expanse]] {{waypoint 67, 12, 513}}
1. Speak with either [[Queen Cristanos]] in Neriak {{waypoint -577, 32, -192}} or [[Antonia Bayle]] in Qeynos Castle.
   - When going to speak to [[Antonia Bayle]], make sure the correct instance of her throne room is chosen ("Vetrovia Report").
