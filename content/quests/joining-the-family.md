## Steps

Sharina sends you to intercept an important Thexian who is getting too close to discovering the ancient weapon of Wu.

1. Head to the Thexian camp near the [[Greater Faydark]] entrance. There is a climbing wall down to the Thexian camp at {{waypoint -558, -37, 384}} or a path leading down to the camp East of [[The Sharpthorn Thicket]].
1. Find and kill [[A Thexian strategist]] {{waypoint -599, -61, 474}}. This is a 55-57^^^ heroic mob that spawns when you walk into the heroic camp.
1. Take [[A Thexian Log Book]] and return it to Sharina.

- +250 faction with **Nybright Family**
