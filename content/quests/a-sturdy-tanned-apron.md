## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Tanned Apron for my apprentice.
   - Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Tanned Apron]]
   - Craft [[A Sturdy Tanned Apron]] on a [[Loom]] using the following materials and fuel:
     - 2 [[Tanned leather pelt|tanned leather pelts]]
     - 4 [[Glowing Filament]]
1. I need to take this apron back to my apprentice.
