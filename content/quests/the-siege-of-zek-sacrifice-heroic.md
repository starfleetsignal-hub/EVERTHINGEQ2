After being returned to Spirit Lake, Bailin Grove the Elder is eager to do what ever can be done to save the spirits of the ancient lake.

## Steps

1. Plant 8 potameid kelp by clicking the **toxin blotches** at the Spirit Lake around {{waypoint -69.44, -3.64, -93.35}}
1. Speak to [[Bailin Grove the Elder (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Bailin Grove the Elder]]
1. Obtain the following items
   - Obtain Treherth's Acorn  {{waypoint 735, -15, -292}} once you gained access to cave with the Quest [[Honor the Dead]]
     - *Named after Salmekia Treherth, a guildmaster amongst the ancient Treefolke of Surefall Glade, this acorn was a spiritual relic used in many druidic ceremonies amongst my Green Hood brethren. It had been kept at the village near Three-Toes Watering Hole*
   - Obtain the branch of the last Glademaster, drops of Knotwood {{waypoint 58.81, -29.10, 175.72}} during the quest [[Knotbarking Up the Wrong Tree]]
     - *A branch should have grown upon one of the treants of this region after Valwood, the last Glademaster, passed beyond the veil.*
1. Obtain Signet of Gannar
   - Enter Scourge Keep by clicking the sewer entrance beneath the [[Bridge of Zek (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Bridge of Zek]] {{waypoint -210, -25, -538}}.
   - The entrance is underwater at {{waypoint -192.61, -36.12, -579.35}}
   - The [[A needler|needler]] fish do not see invis.
   1. You must click the altar (not the helmet) in the shrine of Tallos in Scourge Keep at {{waypoint 13.77, 1, -21.11}} before sigils will drop for you. You will see the message that it "has twenty one empty slots". ***NOTE : if you have done the solo version of the quest beforehand, you will be automatically granted the Signet of Gannar when you click the altar,*** without the need to collect 21 sigils over again.
   1. Collect 21 [[Hound of War Sigil|Hound of War Sigils]] from orcs either overland zone or castle (castle has higher drop chance) and use the altar in the [[Scourge Keep (Advanced Solo)]] at {{waypoint 13.77, 1, -21.11}}
   - *Note: Probably the best way to do the keep in 1 run and avoid farming the Sigils is by killing all the crocs in the lake followed by the named that spawns, than use the old sewer entrance to get inside the keep area. Once you are inside kill as few mobs as possible and get to the top area .. use the closest tower to the bridge to the center top. there go down keep right careful with pulling too many mobs and klick the altar. Once you do that start clearing the zone =)*
   - *Note: Each player needs 21 [[Hound of War Sigil|Hound of War Sigils]], when you hand them in by clicking on the altar. Is not a group update.*
   - *A dangerous item from the Plane of War. The spirits whisper of it. 'Boon of the oracle to share the sight. Twenty-one under the crown to unlock its might.'*
1. Speak with [[Bailin Grove the Elder (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Bailin Grove the Elder]]
