## Steps

1. Scribe [[Spinning Wheel Basics]].
1. Craft three [[Simple Spinning Wheel|Simple Spinning Wheels]]. (You can find a woodworking table at {{waypoint -314, 177, -48}} )
   - Materials Required to craft
     - 3 [[Raw Maple]]
     - 3 [[Tuber Strands]]
     - 3 [[Tanned Leather]]
     - 6 [[Glowing Sandpaper]]
1. Replace the first broken spinning wheel at {{waypoint 66, 153, -131}}.
   - *Note: The three Spinning Wheels are on the floor in the same building where [[Envoy Tami Swifthammer]] is located*
1. Replace the second broken spinning wheel at {{waypoint 58, 153, -124}}.
1. Replace the third broken spinning wheel at {{waypoint 46, 153, -125}}.
1. Return to [[Envoy Tami Swifthammer|Tami]].
