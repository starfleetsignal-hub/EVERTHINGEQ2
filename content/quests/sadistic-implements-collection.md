This collection is a meta-collection of all of the completed Rage of Cthurath [Solo] dungeon and Weekly [Solo] Mission collections. 2 collections from each of the 8 Cthurath [Solo] dungeons, for a total of 16 meta-collection members.

## Rewards

- [[Petamorph Wand: Umbracore Echo]]
- [[Fragment of Collected Void]]
- [[Sadistic Implements (Item)|Sadistic Implements]] this item is needed for the collection: [[Entropy and Madness (Collection)|Entropy and Madness]]
- one of the following:
  - [[Sadistic Chain Boots]]
  - [[Sadistic Sandals of the Warpwolf]]
  - [[Sadistic Leather Boots]]
  - [[Sadistic Plated Boots]]
