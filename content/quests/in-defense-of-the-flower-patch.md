## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Kill 6 [[A thicket deer|thicket deer]]
   - Kill 4 beetles
   - Kill 2 turtles
1. Return to [[Sir Tatters]]

## Rewards

- At least 44s 67c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Band of the Sludge Creep]] (Mages)
  - [[Crest of the Sludge Creep]] (Fighters)
  - [[Loop of the Sludge Creep]] (Priests)
  - [[Ringlet of the Sludge Creep]]  (Scouts)
