## Steps

1. Speak to [[Oolchimedes]] in a nearby hut.
   1. Complete [[Roots in Corruption]].
   1. Complete [[Good for Firewood]].
   1. Speak to [[Oolchimedes]].
1. Obtain the following
   - ![a wild aeraquis](images/A_wild_aeraquis.jpg)Obtain 10 **aeraquis ticks** from wild aeraquis ( bluish/purplish horses in the sky ) around Rootland Rise {{waypoint 14, 126, 456}} and Burrower's Timber {{waypoint 642.00, 105.00, 500.00}}
     - *Note: Quest update not guaranteed with each kill*
   - ![Noxious Swarm Larvae](images/Noxious_Swarm_Larvae.jpg)Click on 8 **Noxious Swarm Larvae** ( green blobs in the water ) in Tellurav Marshlands ponds. (group-wide update) Some possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 707.27, 55.02, 178.31}}, {{waypoint 724.17, 54.64, 203.02}}
     - {{waypoint 711.28, 54.65, 218.94}}, {{waypoint 754.23, 59.31, 246.13}}
     - {{waypoint 744.76, 59.30, 261.91}}, {{waypoint 745.59, 59.22, 270.50}}
     - {{waypoint 751.28, 58.92, 270.00}}, {{waypoint 812.24, 61.33, 251.26}}
1. Return to Portal Scholar [[Miranna Ameema]] on [[Felfeather Isle]] at {{waypoint 1065.16, 179.10, 751.58}}
1. ![a Felfeather Shield Totem](images/A_Felfeather_Shield_Totem.jpg)Destroy 7 **Felfeather Shield Totems** in the [[The Roost Warrens]] {{waypoint 322, 238, -117}} using the [[Potion of Vigorous Unmaking]]. (group-wide update). The Totems are mostly along the perimeter of the Village and are trackable by scouts. Some possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint -21.00, 256.89, 3.37}}, {{waypoint 70.35, 260.68, 98.03}}
   - {{waypoint 15.75, 257.83, 150.60}}, {{waypoint 32.16, 254.17, 46.03}}
   - {{waypoint 33.35, 240.26, -189.91}}, {{waypoint 84.06, 268.25, -172.33}}
   - {{waypoint 83.95, 240.06, -265.64}}
1. Meet [[Miranna Ameema]] at the Wizard Spires {{waypoint -333.35, 118.04, 616.56}} She will not be there.
1. Search for [[Miranna Ameema]] on [[Preening Island]].
   - Take the cloud at {{waypoint 151.47, 280.74, 865.46}} to [[Preening Island]].
1. Save Miranna from the a metal-tainted nilborien attacker at {{waypoint -486.79, 227.86, 1071.68}}

1. Speak to [[Miranna Ameema]].
1. Take the cloud at {{waypoint -302.95, 192.04, 1103.59}} to return to the main island then the banks of the [[Wishbone Beck]].
1. ![Coursequill's Hoot Totem](images/Coursequill's_Hoot_Totem.jpg)Destroy Coursequill's 6 placed hoot totems using [[Potion of Vigorous Unmaking]]. (group-wide update) Some possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint -179.22, 40.94, 606.78}}
   - {{waypoint -152.70, 42.04, 524.95}}
   - {{waypoint -122.97, 41.56, 458.87}}
   - {{waypoint -92.33, 39.58, 406.14}}
   - {{waypoint -53.78, 40.30, 335.96}}
   - {{waypoint 1.64, 40.53, 346.28}}
1. Meet [[Miranna Ameema]] at the Wizard Spires {{waypoint -333.35, 118.04, 616.56}}
