## Prerequisites

This quest scales with level. Prior to level 80, the reward is the title and the faction with the Seafury Buccaneers, no chest piece.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Nellin Jessith]] above the [[Scale Yard]] gate {{waypoint -84, -18, 274}}
1. Speak to [[Lieutenant Dalling]] on the second floor of [[The Freeport Militia House]] {{waypoint 156, -7, 117}}
1. Speak to [[Nellin Jessith]]
1. Enter [[Scale Yard: The Uprising]] {{waypoint -65, -25, 284}}:
   - Kill 6 [[A rioting prisoner|rioting prisoner]]s (even con)
   - Kill the 3 riot leaders (even level, double down):
     - [[Skilly Blocksocket]] {{waypoint 73, -4, -33}}
     - [[Tuluug]] {{waypoint 79, 0, -7}}
     - [[Velino T'Leik]] {{waypoint 81, -3, 25}}
1. Speak to [[Nellin Jessith]] in Freeport
1. Speak to [[Captain Siren]]
1. Speak to [[Harn Greyrick]] at the door to [[Champion's Coliseum]] {{waypoint 238, -3, -50}}
1. Use the portal in [[Overlord's Plaza]] {{waypoint 8, -19, 11}}
1. Speak with [[Captain Siren]] {{waypoint -13, 0, -36}}
1. Watch the meeting
1. Return to [[Captain Siren]] in Freeport

## Rewards

- Chestpiece
  - [[Guileful Chestguard of D'Lere]] (Lvl 80)
  - [[Lucanic Predatory Chestguard]] (Lvl 90)
  - [[Lucanic Chestguard of the Beast]] (Lvl 90)
- [[Title: "Honorary Red Hood"]]
- +1000 faction with **The Seafury Buccaneers**
