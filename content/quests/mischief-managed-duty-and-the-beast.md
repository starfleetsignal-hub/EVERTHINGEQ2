## Notes

- Bringing along portable work stations (or having the AA ability to summon them) is helpful.
- [[Helfa Lightleaf]] in the [[Salt Breeze]] camp {{waypoint 3644.16, -1017.57, 1668.50}} sells fuel if you need some.
- Click the **mining cart** down the southwest tunnel at {{waypoint 252.18, 0.01, 33.46}} to obtain the recipe for [[Tinkered Explosives]]. Explosives clear rock and dirt faster, but are dangerous both to make and use (gnomish things that go BOOM!).*

## Steps

1. Enter and investigate An Unfinished Mine [Solo Tradeskill] Zone in at {{waypoint 3834.34, -1020.90, 1885.50}}.
   1. Craft some [[Sturdy Shovel|Sturdy]] [[Sturdy Shovel|Shovels]] and [[Sturdy Pick|Sturdy Picks]] on the anvil at {{waypoint 179, 9, -164}} just inside the mine.
      - *These tools degrade as you use them, they can't be repaired.*
      - *There is a lot more rock than dirt. Craft four picks for quest; optional one shovel.*
      - ***ALL** crafted implements will disappear from inventory once the beast is captured and the instance exited.*
   1. Equip a tool and start clearing your way into the mine caves.
      - *Wearing your harvesting gear speeds up the clearing process.*
      - *Not every rock and dirt pile needs to be removed, just enough to clear your way down the southeast tunnel.*
   1. at {{waypoint 102.77, -3.58, -29.90}} you hear a *"SCCCRREEEEECHHHH!!!"* from **a monstrous beast**
   1. Locate **a monstrous beast** at {{waypoint 12.44, -14.77, -58.72}}
   1. Step into the beast's area and the [[Recipe Scroll: Live Capture Net]] will pop-up.
   1. scribe the [[Recipe Scroll: Live Capture Net]] and create a [[Live Capture Net]] on a [[Work Bench]].
      - *Can be crafted on rhe anvil at the entrance at {{waypoint 179, 9, -164}}*
   1. Target the beast and use the net.
   1. Put the beast in the cage outside the mine {{waypoint 3848.11, -1019.02, 1851.88}}.
      - *The mine exit is at {{waypoint 167.01, 7.83, -159.48}}.*
   1. Speak again to [[Whilhelm Nam'terin]] at {{waypoint 3821.94, -1020.05, 1863.29}}.
1. Feed the beast you have captured.
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe Scroll: Live Capture Trap]] and create 10 [[Live Capture Trap|Live Capture Traps]] on a [[Work Bench]]. ![Live Capture Trap location](images/Live_Capture_Trap_location.jpg)
      - ***WARNING** Create one at a time as Mass Production only updates one for your quest
   1. Set the 10 traps at marked locations within [[Cobalt Scar]]
      - *Blue dots on the map indicate trap locations*
      - *Placing the traps is a group-wide update.*
      - Trap locations:
        - {{waypoint 3746, -1009, 1805}}  {{waypoint 3807, -1012, 1757}}  {{waypoint 3750, -1016, 1665}}  {{waypoint 3820, -1010, 1649}}
        - {{waypoint 3862, -1020, 1585}}  {{waypoint 3813, -1034, 1507}}  {{waypoint 3887, -1046, 1488}}  {{waypoint 3994, -1051, 1484}}
        - {{waypoint 3693, -1019, 1448}}  {{waypoint 3644, -1012, 1559}}
   1. Wait [[Time|one Norrathian day]] for the traps to work (72 Earth minutes).
      - ***WARNING** Your character that is doing the quest must stay online. If you log your char out or swap to an alt, the timer pauses.
   1. Check the traps for food
      - *Toggle the quest in your journal if the blue dots for trap locations are not visible.*
      - *Note: only a trap with a crab will update the quest. You can either just click each trap as you go, or you can look at each trap to see if there's a crab within and only click the one in which you see a crab.*![Empty Trap](images/EMPTY_Trap.jpg)![Trap with Crab](images/Trap_w-_CRAB.jpg)
   1. Feed the beast in the cage {{waypoint 3848.11, -1019.02, 1851.88}}.
1. Report back to [[Whilhelm Nam'terin|Whilhelm]].
