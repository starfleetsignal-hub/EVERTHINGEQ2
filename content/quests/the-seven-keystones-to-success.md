## Steps

1. Click on the 7 keystones. They look like short square grey pillars. ![Magic Key Stone](images/Magic_Key_Stone.jpg)
   - [[Zivier's Reach]] {{waypoint -49, 105, 770}}  Floating island
   - Above the [[Amphitheater of Song]] {{waypoint 116, 70, 769}}
   - Near the [[Amphitheater of Song]] {{waypoint 509, -152, 910}}
   - [[Mage's Pebble]] {{waypoint 732, 15, 940}}
   - On the floating Island closest to [[The Stratafold]] transport pad {{waypoint 970, 108, 429}}
   - Southern edge of the [[Aetherscar]] {{waypoint 670, 12, 46}}
   - Archipelago north of the [[Aetherscar]] {{waypoint 661, 11, -665}}
1. Return to [[Ta-sche-ra-he]].
1. Speak to [[The Scribe]] at {{waypoint -489, 129, -675}}.
1. Wait for the parchments to be ready (about 3 min).
1. Click on the parchments at {{waypoint -480, 129, -679}}. ![Translated parchments](images/Translated_parchments_%28Pickup%29.jpg)
1. Return to [[Ta-sche-ra-he]].

You will need to do the quests: [[Operation Crustacean Station Continuation]] and [[Green Fruit For Rut Part Deux]] repeatedly until you achieve at least 30,100 [[Pride Pakiat (Faction)]] before proceeding to [[Khat-Ra Pakiat]] at {{waypoint -789, 171, -724}}

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Celestial Ascension (15)]]
- -1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Pride Pakiat Battle Gloves]] (Chain)
  - [[Pride Pakiat Battle Mitts]] (Cloth)
  - [[Pride Pakiat Battle Gage]] (Leather)
  - [[Pride Pakiat Battle Gauntlets]] (Plate)
