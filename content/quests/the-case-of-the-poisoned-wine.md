## Steps

1. Harvest [[Ore Nodes#T3|penumbra ore]] until you obtain 20 pliant loam updates. (Stonecrest ore in Thundering Steppes also gives the updates)
1. Return to Chel Morgan.
1. Harvest [[Bush Nodes#T3|polluted natural garden nodes]] until you obtain 5 Fay Berries updates (Iook along river near N'Mars Tower).
1. Turn in your harvested items to Chel Morgan.
1. Speak to [[Harold Winemaster]] at the docks of [[Port Naythex]]. {{waypoint 807, 2, -169}}
   - You can pick up the quest, [[Defending Harold Winemaster]], and may chose to complete it to learn more about the case, however it's completion isn't require to complete this quest.
1. Return to [[Field Investigator Chel Morgan]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 11s 41c
- [[Field Investigators Evidence kit]] (a 10-slot bag).
