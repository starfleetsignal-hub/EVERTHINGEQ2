- [[Broadcloth Cuffs (Level 30) (Crafted)|Broadcloth Cuffs]]
- [[Broadcloth Shawl (Level 30) (Crafted)|Broadcloth Shawl]]
- [[Etched Leather Bracers]]
- [[Etched Leather Shoulder Pads]]
- [[Broadcloth Mitts (Level 30) (attuneable)|Broadcloth Mitts]]
- [[Broadcloth Pantaloons (Crafted)|Broadcloth Pantaloons]]
- [[Etched Leather Gloves (Level 30) (Crafted)|Etched Leather Gloves]]
- [[Etched Leather Pants (Level 30) (Crafted)|Etched Leather Pants]]
- [[Broadcloth Robe]]
- [[Etched Leather Vest]]
- [[Etched Leather Bandolier (Level 30) (Crafted)|Etched Leather Bandolier]]
- [[Etched Leather Satchel (Level 30) (Crafted)|Etched Leather Satchel]]
- [[Etched Leather Sheath (Level 30) (Crafted)|Etched Leather Sheath]]
- [[Etched Leather Pouch (Level 30) (Crafted)|Etched Leather Pouch]]
- [[Pristine etched leather backpack|Etched Leather Backpack]]
- [[Pristine etched leather quiver|Etched Leather Quiver]]

  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
