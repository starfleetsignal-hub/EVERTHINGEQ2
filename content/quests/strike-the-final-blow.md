## Steps

1. Approach [[Shaman Snowfell]] at the [[World Tree Root]] for a quest update. {{waypoint -650, -15, -305}}
1. Speak with Shaman Snowfell and he will offer you two options:
   - Trust the troglodytes; this option will finish the quest and offer the quest [[Judge Not, Lest Ye Be Judged]]
   - Not trust the troglodytes; this option will finish the quest, turn Shaman Snowfell [[Shaman Snowfell (Named)|aggressive]], and offer the quest [[Lying Trogs!]]

## Rewards

- At least 2g 20s 65c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Frost-Touched Caretaker's Bracelet]] (All Priests)
  - [[Frost-Touched Defender's Bracelet]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Frost-Touched Evoker's Bracelet]] (All Mages)
  - [[Frost-Touched Trapper's Bracelet]] (All Scouts)
