## Steps

![The bookcase that holds the quest starter](images/The_Orc_Runner_Trinkets_%28Starter%29.jpg)

1. Find 4 valuables that were taken from [[Tusk]]: [[The Censer of the Oracles]], [[The Stein of D'Vinn]], [[The Taskmaster Phalange Chimes]], and [[The Urn of Darish]]. These items spawn in any of a dozen random locations.
   - Shrine of Rallos Zek {{waypoint 16, 1, -23}}
   - The Mess Hall {{waypoint -13, 2, 2.5}} or {{waypoint -16, 2, -13}}
   - Artifact and Relic Room {{waypoint 21, 16, 33}}
   - Fyst's skybox at the arena. The doors to the skybox will be locked once you fight in the arena, but you can still get the stein by standing on the gong in the arena, zooming the camera out, and moving it so you can see the stein.
   - Fyst's throne on the rooftop {{waypoint 3, 16, 12}}
   - In the food stores room off of the courtyard {{waypoint 23, 0, 22}}
   - In the Grand Alchemist Study room {{waypoint -21.5, 8, 28.5}}
   - Around the Spectator's Roost {{waypoint 0, -25, -40}}, note that stein was retrieved here and then the chimes appeared at {{waypoint 0, -25, -38.26}}
   - Around the Spectator's Roost {{waypoint -2, -25, -31}}
   - Prisoner/Bathing room {{waypoint 5, 1, 19}}
   - In the Bloodbath Arena {{waypoint -10, -32, 0}}
   - In Prince Gluntok's bedroom (beside the bed) {{waypoint 9.70, 8.78, 29.51}}
   - Inside the main gate, behind the stocks {{waypoint 4.61, 0, 36.64}}
1. Return the four items to [[Tusk]] at the [[Orc Outpost]] {{waypoint -208, -27, -81}}.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 69s 57c
- [[Taskmaster's Earring]]
