## Steps

1. Gather **X** [[Sharpening Stone|sharpening stones]] - *found in "a smooth stone", looks like small brown sparkly rocks adjacent to the larger boulders in the area*  Not trackable. ![a smooth stone](images/A_smooth_stone.jpg)
   - **X**: The amount is different each time it seems to be between 1 and 6
   - *Example locations: {{waypoint -282, 32, -1788}}, {{waypoint -257, 36, -1811}}*
1. Speak with [[Irontooth]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +4,000 faction with **Broken Toe Clan**
- +4,000 faction with **Goblins of Nurga**
