## Steps

1. Travel to {{waypoint -1092, -10, 530}} (underwater, near [[Esoterra Gardens]])
1. After the quest updates upon reaching the location in the prior step, kill 6 [[A lobster monster|lobster monsters]] (no updates for kills before the location update)
1. Click on the den at {{waypoint -1205, -20, 367}} or {{waypoint 803, -9, -1106}} ![Lobster Monster Den](images/Lobster_Monster_Den.jpg)
1. Kill [[The Mother Hen]] that spawns.
1. Return to [[Izny Rut]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Aluxob Leanto]]
- -1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
