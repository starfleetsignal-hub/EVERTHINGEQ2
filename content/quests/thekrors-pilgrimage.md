This quest becomes available when Scars of Destruction signature questline is  completed.

## Steps

1. Inform [[Thekror]] when you are prepared to escort him
1. Escort Thekror to the first draconic obelisk in the Flooded Scar {{waypoint 553, 8, 158}}
1. Speak with Thekror
1. Speak with Thekror again when I am prepared to continue the journey
1. Escort Thekror to the draconic obelisk in the ice at the eastern end of [[Flooded Scar]] {{waypoint -195, 7, -100}}
1. Speak with Thekror
1. Defeat [[A mesmerized molkar]]
1. Speak with Thekror

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
