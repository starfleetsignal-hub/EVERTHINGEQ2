This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Gather the following
   - Gather either 6 **healthy madder roots** from under **madder plants** at [[Vertra Mesa]] {{waypoint 118, 37, -215}}, like from previous quest or 6 **purple bonnet fungi** from under foliage on [[Versa Mesa]] {{waypoint 147, 44, -254}}.
   - Gather either *random from quest giver*
     - 5 **myrobalan** fruit at Bukatan Savanna {{waypoint -414, 49, -329}}, like from previous quest<br>or
     - 5 **Indigofera Leaves** from Needlehead Isle {{waypoint 14, 38, -800}}.
     - ** note: you need to be quite close for the fruit to appear*
1. Return to [[Shabari]]
