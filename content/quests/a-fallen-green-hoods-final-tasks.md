## Steps

1. Locate 4 places in the Citadel (randomly chosen from the following list):
   - [[Bathing Room]] {{waypoint 9, 1, 23}}
   - [[The Armory (Deathfist Citadel)|The Armory]] {{waypoint -9, 1, 23}}
   - [[The Torture Chamber]] {{waypoint -18, -10, -13}}
   - [[The Shrine to Rallos Zek]] {{waypoint 15, 3, -21}}
   - [[The Grand Alchemist Study]] {{waypoint -19, 8, 33}}
   - [[Blood Bath Arena]] {{waypoint 0, -32, -9}}
1. Find 3 books:
   - Orcish Shattered Lands Book: [[Artifact and Relic Storage]] {{waypoint 10, 16, 41}}
   - Tome of Blood Iron Smelting: [[The Blood Iron Forge (Deathfist Citadel)|The Blood Iron Forge]] {{waypoint -21, 0, 23}}
   - Runes of Jaggedpine: [[Deathfist Savings and Loan]] {{waypoint 8, -25, 18}}
1. Speak to [[The Glademaster]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] {{waypoint 332, -20, -464}}

## Rewards

- At least 2g (at level 41)
- One of the following:
  - [[Pine Dagger]] x2
  - [[Pine Maul]]
  - [[Pine Timber]]
  - [[Pine Voulge]]
