## Steps

1. Kill the following in any order:
   - 5 leeches
     - Any leeches in the zone will work. [[A dragon drool leech|Dragon drool leeches]] are the closest. {{waypoint 273, -118, 772}}
   - 5 vepsids
     - [[A vicious Vespid hornet|vicious Vespid hornets]] and [[A vicious Vespid cosm-caller|vicious Vespid cosm-callers]] {{waypoint 7, 129, 632}}
   - 3 swampflies:
     - [[A kunzar swampfly|kunzar swampflies]] {{waypoint 7, 129, 632}}
   - 3 sporconids:
     - [[A sporconid eyesore|sporconid eyesores]] and [[A sporconid redcap|sporconid redcaps]] {{waypoint 206, -121, 721}}
1. Return to Foreman Vink.

## Rewards

- At least 9g 46s 1c
- [[Majestic Bangle]]
- +10,000 faction with **Dalnir's Wheel Taskmasters**
