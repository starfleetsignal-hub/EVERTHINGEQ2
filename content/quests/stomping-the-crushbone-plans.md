## Description

"Jenthis Viridar would like me to travel east of Sapling Spur Outpost--past Memorial Hill--and seek out the Crushbone pawns hiding with the Growzzat bixies. He wants this orc problem taken care of before the orcs take root in the area." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay 8 [[A Crushbone pawn|Crushbone pawns]] around {{waypoint -286.80, 20.64, -150.03}}
1. Return to Jenthis Viridar

## Rewards

- At least 9s 22c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Jenthis Viridar: "Welcome to Sapling Spur Outpost. I am Jenthis Viridar, the current rank around here. I, well... I'd like to offer our assistance, but we're in a bit of a predicament right now."
- Player Character: "I am [Player Character]. what is the problem?"
- Jenthis Viridar: "Orcs. They've been spilling out of Crushbone and into the Faydark at an alarming rate. That they have managed to get so close to Tunare's Sapling is worrisome. I've asked for more soldiers, but my requests are being denied... I... I'm sorry, I shouldn't be complaining to you. Our problem, [Player Character], is that to the west lies "Orc Hill," a staging area for most of the orc attacks west and south of this position. On top of that, to the east..."
- Player Character: "Yes?"
- Jenthis Viridar: "To the east is an area that was once peaceful. The bixies--friendly unless provoked--reside there, but lately orcs have been moving in. We've already waited too long and they're getting complacent. I need you to scatter these would-be orc settlers and ensure they find no ground -- save the ground they die upon. There are only Crushbone pawns there for now, but pawns are nothing if not forerunners."
- Player Character: "All right."

I need to slay eight Crushbone pawns.

I have slain the pawns. I need to return to Jenthis at Sapling Spur Outpost.

- Jenthis Viridar: "Have you taken care of the Crushbone pawns?"
- Player Character: "I have slain several orc pawns. However..."
- Jenthis Viridar: "Yes? Is something wrong?"
- Player Character: "There are many, many more."

I have spoken with Jenthis. I have slain the Crushbone pawns.

I have returned to Jenthis after slaying the orcs.
