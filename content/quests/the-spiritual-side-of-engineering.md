## Steps

1. Collect 7 samples of the dust that [[A Smokehorn berserker|Smokehorn berserkers]] sometimes carry. They can be found around {{waypoint -927, 76, 1463}} and {{waypoint -1073, 71, 1352}}.
   - Each kill gives -100 faction with [[Clan Smokehorn (Faction)|Clan Smokehorn]] - this can be avoided if you have your mercenary attack and do not assist it in any way.
1. Return to Eegar Friggderp for your reward.

## Reward

- At least 2g 69s 57c
