## Steps

1. Loot 5 [[Harpy Feather Quill|Harpy Feather Quills]] from the corpses of harpies.
   - Good locations for harpies include [[The Pillars of Flame]], [[Scornfeather Roost]] or [[Steamfont Mountains]].

## Rewards

- The ability to understand and speak [[Screechsong]], the common language of all Harpies.
