## Steps

1. Head to the [[Drakelands]] and do the following
   - Destroy 6 awakened materiel
     - Look on the high ledges for awakened crates and awakened vases.  They can be tracked. There are also a few crates on some land down at sea level.
   - Spy on Awakened Legion Dracurions - head to {{waypoint 5182, -934, 1389}} for an auto-update
1. Return to Field Marshal Vishra

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
