## Steps

1. Enter [[Evernight Abbey]] and rescue Kendel Druthers
   - Find where Kendel Druthers has been taken.  You can find him in the basement of Evernight Abbey at {{waypoint -1.5, -25, -190}} in the jail area.
1. Find Kendel Druther's gear at {{waypoint 147.03, -5.22, -108.39}}
   - You need to get a Heavy Brass Key to unlock the chest. The key dropped off a mob in a room if you take the hallway to the left as you enter the zone. Around {{waypoint 146.43, -6.16, -110}} The key everyone gets.
   - If your inventory is full, this step will not update.
   - If you are not on this step, you will not update.  Go to the basement before seeking the key and chest.
1. I need to give Kendel his gear.
1. I should speak with Evric Druthers back in Somborn.
