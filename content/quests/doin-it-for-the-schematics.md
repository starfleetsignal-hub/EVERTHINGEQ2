Prerequisites: If you don't see the quest bubble, do more quests earlier in the timeline to earn more faction.

Down in the Aquifer Pumping Station, within the Waterworks section of Maldura, I met a gnemlin tinkerer, by the name of Bhoughbh Bhildur, on the verge of quitting his craft. After convincing him to give it one more chance he asked me to fetch his book of schematics from the Alcove of Reason.

## Steps

1. Search for [[Bhoughbh Bhildur|Bhoughbh's]] schematics in the [[Alcove of Reason]]. It should be on the shelf marked **Zinc Artificer Technotes A-M** {{waypoint -86, 13, 198}}.
1. Speak with [[Moffyn Janglebaum]] {{waypoint -2.46, 26.39, 116.73}}.
1. Obtain the items [[Moffyn Janglebaum|Moffyn]] has demanded for the book:
   - 12 **reinforced arcannium plates** in the [[The Waterworks: Treatment and Reclamation|Waterworks: Treatment and Reclamation]].
     - {{waypoint 36, 34, 168}}
     - {{waypoint 51, 34, 186}}
     - {{waypoint 52, 31, 179}}
     - {{waypoint 58, 31, 163}}
     - {{waypoint 80, 31, 137}}
     - {{waypoint 65, 31, 123}}
     - {{waypoint 50, 29, 133}}
     - {{waypoint 21, 29, 105}}
     - {{waypoint 39, 29, 131}}
     - {{waypoint 37, 32, 151}}
   - 8 **compact hydraulic pumps** in the [[Stone Mercantile District]].
     - You will need to break open **crates of hydraulic parts** to find the pumps.
     - Not all crates contain pumps.
     - There don't seem to be 8 crates in the area, but they respawn quickly.
       - {{waypoint -149, -7, 125}}
       - {{waypoint -157, -7, 135}}
       - {{waypoint -162, -7, 138}}
       - {{waypoint -143, -4, 135}}
       - {{waypoint -141, -4, 127}}
       - {{waypoint -169, -7, 127}}
       - {{waypoint -167, -7, 91}}
       - {{waypoint -170, -4, 79}}
       - {{waypoint -190, -3, 112}}
       - {{waypoint -175, -1, 133}}
       - {{waypoint -142, -7, 71}}
       - {{waypoint -167, -7, 52}}
1. Return to [[Moffyn Janglebaum|Moffyn]].
1. Return to [[Bhoughbh Bhildur]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13200 status
