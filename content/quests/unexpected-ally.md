## Steps

1. I must enter the [[Temple of the Faceless: Imprisonment (Advanced Solo)]] or Temple of the Faceless (Heroic) at {{waypoint -2163, 222, 768}}
   - *(You can work on this quest at the same time as [[Fortunes Turned]] so that you won't have to wait for zone reset. )*
1. Defeat D'iar Ew Ritualists in the Temple of the Faceless (8)
   - Kill everything leading up to the two temples, the lizardmen within the temples and their idols, and kill all gorillas.
   - Avoid [[Sarinich (Advanced Solo)]] 96^ (*NOTE: Sarinich can not be killed until all the above is done first*).
1. Kill [[Sarinich (Advanced Solo)]]
1. Collect the two staffs mentioned by [[Alzilius]].
   - Collect the **Staff of Fright** near [[Phobis (Advanced Solo)]], on the floor {{waypoint -418, 56, -34}}
   - Collect the **Staff of the Wastes** from the ground at the bottom of the Steps of Fear {{waypoint -361, -13, -307}} - to do this, you have to swim down through the waterfall behind where Phobis spawned, then cross the open grass area to find the stair. ![](images/Staff_of_Wastes.jpg)
1. Return to [[Alzilius]] {{waypoint -2044, 218, 816}}
