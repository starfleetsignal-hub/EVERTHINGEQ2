## Steps

1. Slay the following monsters for quest updates:
   - 6 fungus men of any type: [[A sporeal seedling]], [[A sporeal guard]]
   - 4 [[A ravenous bog slug]] around {{waypoint 603, -32, 563}}
1. Return to [[Lieutenant Dawson]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 92c
- +125 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
