## Steps

1. I must slay the Harrowing Horde that have broken through the barriers at the southeast wall in Cardin Ward.
   - Slay 8 [[A Skirth Skulker|Skirth Skulker]] or [[An Orafik Shrillmawat|Orafik Shrillmawat]] {{waypoint -613, 7, 138}}
1. I should return to Cardin Wood and tell Firiona Vie, Chosen of Growth, of my success.
   - Return to [[Firiona Vie]] {{waypoint -365, 14, -26}}
