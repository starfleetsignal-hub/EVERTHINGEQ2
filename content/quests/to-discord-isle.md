## Steps

1. Travel to the [[The Isle of Discord (POI)|The Isle of Discord]], use your [[Hardened Pipe Reed]] at {{waypoint -213, -82, -260}} and speak to [[Demeoth Sharptalon]].
1. Kill the following (in any zone):
   - 10 Vultaks
     - Can be found near Demeoth on the Isle of Discord.
   - 10 Aviaks.
     - Can be found on the same island, beyond the Vultaks.  Also on , , and others.
1. Speak to Demeoth.
1. Return to Harun on Whisperwind Isle to receive your reward

## Rewards

- At least 9g 85s 50c
- +100 faction with **The Windgazer**
