## Steps

1. Travel to [[Nektulos Forest]] and kill [[X'tort V'ndal]] who will spawn near Thexian camp in the [[Valley of the Bears]] (very close to the [[Shard of Hate]] and [[Darklight Wood]] entrances) {{waypoint -1291, 43, -1442}}.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +3000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
