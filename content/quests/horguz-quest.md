### Notes

Oculus wizards are relatively rare spawns, and so this quest is often considered too time-consuming to be worth it.  For those intent on doing it, the oculus wizards can be found in the following locations.

- Wandering a central hallway at {{waypoint 39, -17, -108}} - at this location they're on a shared spawn with 3 other types of 'oculus' evil eyes.  Four eyes will be up at any one time.
- One spawns in [[The Thing of Shattered Iron]]'s room linked with 4 Runnyeye torturers.  {{waypoint 231, -16, 36}}
