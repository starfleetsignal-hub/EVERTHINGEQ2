## Prerequisites

- You have to complete [[Brittle Chips Ahoy]] at least one time.
- The Celestial Watch bone chip collection orb on your server has to be filled at 100%.
- You need to speak [[Stout]]. You can get the [[Stout Language Primer]] off the [[Language Sellers]] in your home town.

## Introduction

A devastating crime was committed within [[North Qeynos]] - [[Seeress Ealaynya Ithis]], Harbinger of Life and Prophet of the Prime Healer was murdered! There are mysterious and dark motivations behind it and unforeseeable consequences due to it. We cannot be distracted from this crime any longer. Justice must be done but more importantly, the evil machinations at play must be stopped! We must find who committed this heinous act and to what end.

## Steps

1. Perhaps one of the Pilgrims at the temple of Life saw something suspicious?  Speak to [[A dwarf pilgrim]] {{waypoint 508, -31, -233}} outside the [[Temple of Life]] in [[North Qeynos]].  She says she does not have the voice to talk, and asks for some tea to calm her nerves.
1. Buy some [[Calming Chamomile Tea]] from [[Baker Voleen]] at [[Voleen's Bakery]] in [[North Qeynos]] for 38 copper. {{waypoint 280, -16, 30}}
1. Bring the [[Calming Chamomile Tea]] tea to [[A dwarf pilgrim]] outside the [[Temple of Life]] in [[North Qeynos]].  A dwarf pilgrim saw Acolyte Squallrider nervously leave the temple the same day of the Seeress' murder.
1. I should question the merchants and citizens of Qeynos about the Acolyte.  Talk to [[Baker Voleen]] at [[Voleen's Bakery]] in [[North Qeynos]] again.  Baker Voleen sold Acolyte Squallrider a great amount of food.
1. I should question more merchants and citizens of Qeynos about the Acolyte.  Talk to [[Stable Hand Stonebear]] in [[South Qeynos]] {{waypoint 395, -20, 210}}.  Stable Hand Stonebear sold Acolyte Squallrider a horse.
1. I should question more merchants and citizens of Qeynos about the Acolyte.  Talk to [[Bog Valorheart]] inside [[Ironforge Exchange]] {{waypoint 337, -20, -98}} in [[North Qeynos]]. You'll get a new quest [[Gettin' Some Hot Ink]].
1. Complete the subquest [[Gettin' Some Hot Ink]] before you proceed.
1. Travel to [[Enchanted Lands]].
1. Leave the docks by the stairs (i.e., don't just jump off the pier). You will get an update telling you that "Fresh hoof prints lead off to the west, along the coastline".
1. Follow the coastline to [[Sir Tatters]] camp, at [[The Great Guard]] you'll find Esdro's camp site. {{waypoint 295, 0, -140}}
1. Head north, [[The Great Guard]] to your left until you reach [[The Lazy Drain]]. {{waypoint 220, -5, -370}}
1. Follow [[The Lazy Drain]] to the east until you reach the second bridge near [[Rivervale]]. Hint: If you are low level you can also go back to the Docks and use the path to [[Rivervale]] to reach the bridge safely. You'll find the dead body of [[Esdros Squallrider]]. {{waypoint -168, -4, -260}}
1. Right click the corpse and inspect it and you'll receive a [[Unsealed Note from the Seeress]].
1. Examine the [[Unsealed Note from the Seeress]]. Note: For the next step you'll need to speak [[Stout]].
1. Head up to the [[Tagglefoot Farms]] following [[The Lazy Drain]] to the north. Watch out for the aggro fishes. You should be safe at the banks.
1. Talk to [[Jubbs Tagglefoot]] at the left house. You'll get a new quest [[Carrots for the Trotters]].
1. Complete the subquest [[Carrots for the Trotters]].
1. Return to [[Shepherd Bainyn Fairwind]] outside the [[Temple of Life]] in [[North Qeynos]].
