## Description

"Zephyre Violethair, a wood elf in Kelethin, is creating an antivenin against the poison of the underwood crawlers beneath the city. By bringing her their poisonous fangs, I can help her produce enough antivenin to help protect all the new visitors to Kelethin." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay 8 [[An underwood crawler|underwood crawlers]].  They can be found under Kelethin, east and a bit south from [[Joleena's Restaurant Acorn Lift]], {{waypoint 456, 7, 379}}
1. Return to [[Zephyre Violethair]]

## Rewards

- At least 76c
- [[Chausses of the Feir'Dal (Version 2)|Chausses of the Feir'Dal]]
- [[Zephyre's Augmented Mail Shirt (Version 2)|Zephyre's Augmented Mail Shirt]]
