## Steps

1. Gather 6 [[Luclinite shard|luclinite shards]] from the [[Phantom Sea]]. Area around {{waypoint -968.10, 0.00, -812.64}}. For level 96, Great Divide around Ry'Gorr Ruins {{waypoint -685, -354, 294}} is the location.
1. Return the [[Teren's Grasp]] and fuse some luclinite shards to
   - the north-east Lucline Propylon at {{waypoint 1839, 480, -964}}
   - the south-east Lucline Propylon at {{waypoint 1917, 480, -761}}
   - the south-west Lucline Propylon at {{waypoint 2129, 480, -765}}
   - the north-west Lucline Propylon at {{waypoint 2057, 520, -958}}
1. Return to [[Ikram El'Suhaib]]

## Rewards

- 3 [[Deepwater Copper]].
