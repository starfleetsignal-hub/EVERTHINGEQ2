## Notes

As I crept through the Nexus Core, I came across a fallen purifier.  I wasn't certain what made it fall to the ground, as there are no marks and no sign of apparent damage, though when I approached it briefly sparked to life and attempted to move toward me, before shutting back down again.  If these purifiers see me as something to be 'cleaned', then I'll need to destroy them if I'm going to have any hope of passing through the Core safely.
