*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Slay 20 Rallosian skeletons. These are found at the [[Tower of Vul]]. {{waypoint -945, 3, 465}}
1. Return to [[Grimble Blumble]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 69s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Earring of Inspired Fear]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Facet of Inspired Fear]] (All Priests)
  - [[Spike of Inspired Fear]] (All Mages)
  - [[Stud of Inspired Fear]] (All Fighters)
