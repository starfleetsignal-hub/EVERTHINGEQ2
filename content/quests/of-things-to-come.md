## Steps

1. Speak to the Historian, [[Filnn Shatterhand]], in front of Glacierbane's Vault inside the city of New Halas. {{waypoint -114.50, 147.22, -69.08}}
1. Search for the tome in Glacierbane's Vault in New Halas. {{waypoint -111.65, 148.72, -87.54}}
1. Find the parts for the summoning horn.
   - **ulthork tusk** - a small pile of bones, found at the bottom of the path with the rock rollers out of New Halas. {{waypoint -41.31, 25.98, -323.03}}.
   - **Brass Forged Ring** - on a Ry'Gorr Orc - This is an uncommon auto-update from any Ry'Gorr orc in Frostfang Sea.
   - **Pure Powdered Snow** (6 total) - These look like purple shinies on the snow in Frostfang Sea. Many can be found between [[Gwenevyn's Cove]] and [[The Great Shelf]].  They remain in the same place even after you collect them which means they'll continue to tempt you until you find the others.  Know locations: {{waypoint 332, 30, -402}}  {{waypoint 91, 30, -366}}  {{waypoint 262, 27, -312}}  {{waypoint 364, 36, -470}}  {{waypoint 529, 45, -398}}  {{waypoint 479, 40, -365}}
1. Return to Paineel and speak with [[The Duality]].  You will be given [[A summoning horn]].
1. Go to the docks in [[The Sinking Sands]] {{waypoint -1507, -214, -402}} and blow the summoning horn to call for the great Lodizal.
1. Speak with [[Stissa]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -1470, -225, -429}}
1. Return to The Duality.
