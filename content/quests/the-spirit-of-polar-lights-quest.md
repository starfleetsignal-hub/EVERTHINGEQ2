## Steps

1. Kill [[An icegill fisher]] {{waypoint 366, -37, -1451}} to obtain the key to the fish traps.
1. Locate and open the 4 underwater fish traps (note: the traps are hanging from chains from the bottom of the ice above) with the key:
   - First {{waypoint 44, -55, -1426}}
   - Second {{waypoint 238, -71, -1641}}
   - Third {{waypoint 477, -70, -1571}}
   - Fourth {{waypoint 430, -57, -1283}}
1. Return to the [[The Spirit of Polar Lights (NPC)|spirit]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- The blessing of the [[The Spirit of Polar Lights (NPC)|spirit]] for a step in the quest [[In Search of a Menagerie]].
