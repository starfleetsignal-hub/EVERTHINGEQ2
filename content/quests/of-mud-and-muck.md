## Notes

[[Researcher Deros]] has asked that I obtain some samples of the [[A marsh elemental]]s within the Tears of Tunare and compare them to the marsh fiends and see if there is some sort of connection between the creatures.

## Steps

1. I should slay [[A marsh elemental]] for studying within the Tears of Tunare.
   1. Slay [[A marsh elemental]]
   1. Continue to gather samples of [[A marsh elemental]] within the Tears of Tunare
      - Obtain a marsh elemental's strengthened will. (Slay marsh elementals until update received.)
      - Obtain a marsh elemental's residue. (Slay marsh elementals until update received.)
      - Obtain a marsh elemental's slimy skin. (Slay marsh elementals until update received.)
      - Obtain a marsh elemental's contained rage. (Slay marsh elementals until update received.)
   1. Slay [[A marsh fiend]]
      - Note: They spawn when you move over a spot in the marsh where bubbles are rising. Possible location of bubbles at: (108, -745, -411)
1. Return to [[Researcher Deros]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
