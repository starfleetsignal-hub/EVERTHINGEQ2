## Steps

1. Inspect the container on [[The Whisperwind Isle (POI)|The Whisperwind Isle]]. {{waypoint -718, -64, -418}}
1. Read the note received from the container. You will succumb to the poison on your skin and are temporarily paralyzed. You are transported to the [[Isle of Awakening]] at {{waypoint 147, 180, 345}} in front of [[Clarissa Cloudpuff]].
   - If you read this in any other zone you are put flat on your back, the screen goes black, and you come to again frozen in place for 15 seconds.
1. Speak to Clarissa Cloudpuff.

- At least 6g 88s 19c
