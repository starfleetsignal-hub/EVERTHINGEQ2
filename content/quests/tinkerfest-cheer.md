This quest is repeatable.

## Prerequisites

- Must speak [[Gnomish]]

## Steps

1. Visit ***any three zones*** (of your choice) celebrating [[Tinkerfest]]:
   - [[North Qeynos]] at {{waypoint 267, -15, 55}}
   - [[East Freeport]] at {{waypoint -104.91, -22.5, 107.97}}
   - near the [[Kelethin First Regional Bank]] in the city of [[Kelethin]] in [[Greater Faydark]] at{{waypoint 300.23, 107.68, 152.06}}
   - on the platform by the elevator {{waypoint 2574, 8, 1227}} in the city of [[Gorowyn]]
   - near [[Indigo Hollow]] in the city of [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]]
   - Inside [[Solusek's Eye]] at {{waypoint 33, 14, -41}}
   - at the [[Drednever Crash Site]] {{waypoint 42.65, 44.68, 523.64}}  in [[The Bonemire]]
   - {{waypoint 1771.58, 453.78, 837.45}} in the [[Moors of Ykesha]]
   - on the platforms leading from the [[Erollis Dock]] to [[New Halas]] {{waypoint -428.14, 52.79, 26.73}}
   - on the docks near the entrance to [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]] in [[Great Divide]].
1. Find the jug of gnomish spirits in the center of the celebrants and click it to refill it.
1. Return to Tomker

## Rewards

- [[Gnomish Spirits!]]
- 2 [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog]]

- Upon completing the quest 5 times:
  - [[Title: the Gnomish Spirited]]
- Upon completing the quest 9 times:
  - [[Title: the Deliverator]]
