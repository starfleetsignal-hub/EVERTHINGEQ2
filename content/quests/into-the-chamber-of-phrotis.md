## Steps

1. clicking on the table near [[Vence Laden]]  to enter the [[Elemental Chamber]].
   - Entering the zone will give you an update to the quest
1. inspect the giant ice brazier behind the [[Will of Phrotis]].  No crystals are found
1. Return to [[Vence Laden]] by clicking on the platform in front of [[Will of Phrotis]].

## Rewards

- Experience Only
