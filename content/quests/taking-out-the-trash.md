## Prerequisites

- You must have started the quest [[When Darkness Comes]] or [[Eliminate Geraufl]] in order to complete this quest.

## Steps

1. Click a box behind any hut to receive [[A sack of froglok trash]]
1. Stand on the platform behind Pohpiks {{waypoint -52, 14, 199}} and examine the sack in your inventory to place it
1. Repeat the search and placement a total of 5 times. (You can only have one bag of garbage in inventory at once.)
1. Speak with Pohpiks for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
