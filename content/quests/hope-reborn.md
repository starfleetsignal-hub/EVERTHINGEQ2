## Steps

1. Craft [[Necromantic Detector Mk II]] at the campfire {{waypoint 4267, -1092, 2034}}. Requires 1 [[Rhenium ore]], 1 [[Effulgent coal]] and the [[Necromantic Detector|old detector]]. ([[Recipe: Necromantic Detector Mk II|recipe]] auto-scribes)
1. Find the source of the scourge:
   - Use the detector.
   - Use the [[Othmir Scouting Potion]], which makes you non-aggro ***only*** to the mobs in the cave. It lasts 6 minutes and can be reapplied NOTE: if you are missing your Othmir Scouting Potion just speak to Yeppa and he will give you another. : I had following error message in my logs when I failed to receive potion - Yeppa gave me missing potion when I hailed him again: : ***An error has prevented you from receiving an item required for this quest.***
   - The undead Othmir do not see through invisibility.
   - Enter [[Cave of Anguish]] {{waypoint 4128, -1081, 2219}} and travel to a dragon skull {{waypoint 3910, -1153, 2240}}
   - It is possible to position one's self behind the skull and complete the combines without drawing attention.
1. Using the skull as a work table, craft [[Hope (Hope Reborn)|Hope]]. Requires  1 [[Amethyst]], 1 [[Effulgent incense]] and [[Hope (Crafted)|Hope]] from the previous quest.
1. Return to Yeppa.

## Rewards

- 5 [[A Mysterious Blue Coin|A Mysterious Blue Coins]]
