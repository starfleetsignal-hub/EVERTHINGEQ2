## Description

Bergna Veliumsmiter, a Thurgadin Battlepriestess and Priest of Brell, has asked me to recover samples of stolen ales, gathered by the Rime, from around Norrath. She is hopeful that offering this collection to the Duke of Below will help to earn his favor in upcoming fights against the Rime.

## Steps

1. Recover 5 samples of ale by clicking on barrels.

     - {{waypoint -607.95, 36.98, -29.28}}
