## Steps

1. Slay [[A Crushbone herb gatherer|Crushbone herb gatherers]] near [[Camp Var'Cha]] around {{waypoint -864, 97, -1}} until you find Crushbone medicine bag (update).
1. Slay [[Corruptor Thurizil]] near [[Camp Var'Cha]] who spawns on the cliffs above the camp. Walk up the stone ramp at {{waypoint -959, 97, -27}} to the top of the cliff and he will spawn in front of you.  This path avoids the cluster of Oracles you would have to get past if you went up the tree ramp to the southwest instead.
1. Return to [[Tuathil Laeds]].

- At least 2s 40c
- [[Lucent Gem of Fortitude]]
- [[Kelethin Courser Whistle]]
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Symbol of Strength]] (Fighters)
  - [[Symbol of Intelligence]] (Mages)
  - [[Symbol of Wisdom]] (Priests)
  - [[Mark of Agility]] (Scouts)
