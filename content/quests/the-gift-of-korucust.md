## Steps

1. Enter [[Chardok]], slay [[The Bridge Keeper]], and loot an [[Ethernere Crystal]]. {{waypoint -419, -66, 6}}
1. Kill the following mobs near the entrance to the Court of Korucust: {{waypoint -638, -67, 204}}
   - 6 [[A Nak'azar prison guard|Nak'azar prison guards]]
   - 2 [[A Nak'azar enforcer|Nak'azar enforcers]]
1. Enter [[Court of Korucust]] and defeat [[Korucust]].
   - To spawn Korucust, you must complete the quest [[The Bones of Korucust]] from within the instance.
1. Return to [[Warlord Gadar Gwalnax]] in [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]].
