## Steps

1. Steal documents from the Order of Rime in [[Ru'Kaus]]:
   - *maps*: {{waypoint 152, 111, 222}}, {{waypoint 144, 111, 224}}
   - *letters*: {{waypoint 162, 111, 214}}, {{waypoint 144, 111, 222}}
   - *notes*: {{waypoint 154, 111, 232}}, {{waypoint 155, 110, 209}}
   - After the last document has been stolen, [[An angry Rime officer|an angry Rime officer]] will spawn and attack. It is not necessary to kill him.
1. Return to [[Mosi Panthira]].

## Rewards

- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- At least 36g 79s 48c
