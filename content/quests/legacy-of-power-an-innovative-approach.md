## Notes

- You need to be at least a level 105 [[Adventurer]] to enter [[Plane of Innovation: Masks of the Marvelous (Solo)|Plane of Innovation: Masks of the Marvelous [Solo]]]
- The buff [[Fount of Power]] should be received upon entering. If you don't have ~125k Potency and ~20+ million hit points it hasn't been applied properly. Cancel and recast from button on hotbar.
- You may want to pick up the Solo Mission quests for each zone first.
- If you haven't already you can start the Legend and Lore for Clockwork here.

## Steps

### Plane of Innovation: Masks of the Marvelous

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -94, 3, 163}} to enter [[Plane of Innovation: Masks of the Marvelous (Solo)|Plane of Innovation: Masks of the Marvelous [Solo]]].
   - Avoid the furnaces with visible flames. They'll root you for a short time and throw fire at you.
1. Defeat the [[Ancient Clockwork Prototype]]. Kill the adds first! Wanders near {{waypoint -52, 3, 6}}.
   - Loot [[Ancient Clockwork Hand]] from the chest.
1. Quest will say Confront Meldrath's spirit.
   - Defeat the [[Clockwork Scrounger XVII]] near {{waypoint 78, 3, -42}}.
   - Defeat [[The Glitched Guardian 10101]] near {{waypoint 154, 3, -151}}. This turns the Ancient Clockwork Hand into [[Electro-Charged Clockwork Hand]].
     - Joust out when "The Glitched Guardian 10101 begins to overheat, a deadly explosion is imminent!" roughly every 25%
   - Equip [[Electro-Charged Clockwork Hand]] and open the door at {{waypoint 134, 3, -43}}.
     - ***Remember to re-equip your normal weapon after opening the door!***
1. Defeat [[An erratic clockwork]], then inspect and speak to it.
1. Follow it.
   - Wait for the [[Security Sweeper]] to pass from right to left, then quickly move down the tunnel and turn right into the corridor and away from the Sweeper. If it spots you, you will get ported back to the start of the zone.
   - Take the first right up another tunnel.
1. Kill the [[Glitched Cell Keeper]] at {{waypoint 127, 10, -218}}.
   - This mob pops a red octagon that makes the "Glitched Cell Keeper" impervious (Stone Skin), and it gathers power from his stores to repair himself.
     - Watch for the red octagon to pop, and get out of it as soon as you see it. Also, note that the octagon's area gets bigger each time it pops, so try to kill it fast before you run our of room to maneuver.
     - If you stand inside the red octagon, he will one-shot kill you with a massive explosion.
     - While the red octagon is active/charging Glitched Cell Keeper doesn't take damage.
     - So, kite/joust your way around and keep moving, and keep you eyes peeled for the octagon popping.
1. Speak to [[Meldrath the Marvelous (Spirit)]] in the next room at {{waypoint 82, 10, -210}}. ![Meldrath the Marvelous](images/Meldrath_the_Marvelous.jpg)
1. Kill the [[Gearclaw the Collector]] at {{waypoint -140, 4, -283}} to receive [[Muon's Planarwave Actuator]].
   - Roughly every 25% the mob slumps forward and becomes immune to any damage. Right-click and select Turn Key.
1. Click on the grate at {{waypoint -169, 5, -279}} to return to the start of the zone, and then click on the door to return to the [[Coliseum of Valor]].

### Plane of Innovation: Gears in the Machine

**Note**: There appears to have been a bug with this part of the quest where any attempt to "speak" with "Meldrath" (presumably  [[Meldrath the Marvelous (Clockwork)]], who will be standing near the zone-in on entry) before collecting the three parts will result in you having to re-do the zone from scratch. *This MAY no longer be an issue - I spoke to Meldrath before collecting anything in this zone without it causing any problem, although at this point he doesn't say anything other than he's trying to mask my presence from his brother (November 2021).*

**Warning:** (February 2024) *While **most** of this quest will update in the Heroic version of this zone, [[The Great Gear]] (see step 12 below) does not spawn, so you will have to run the Solo to complete it.*

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -94, 3, 163}} to enter [[Plane of Innovation: Gears in the Machine (Solo)|Plane of Innovation: Gears in the Machine [Solo]]].
1. Kill [[Repair Bot 5000 (Solo)]] at {{waypoint 36, 4, -31}}.
   - The mob has a stiff power drain. Bring power potions and cure arcane pots if you don't have power regen or cures.
1. Kill the [[Powered Mechanization (Solo)]] at {{waypoint -1, 4, -116}}.
1. Use the door opposite the [[Powered Mechanization (Solo)]]. {{waypoint -43, 4, -110}}
1. Collect the [[Magnetic Ether Compensator]] at {{waypoint -128, 4, -35}}. ![Magnetic Ether Compensator](images/Magneti_Ether_Compensator.jpg)
1. Kill [[Toa the Shiny (Solo)]] near {{waypoint -214, 4, -186}}.
   - Accompanying Toa are [[Keewi the Shiner (Solo)]] and [[Brasoh the Polisher (Solo)]]
   - (not Solo): Periodically during the fight, Toa the Shiny will emote about an electric shock. Stopping all dps for ?? time or take massive damage.
   - (not Solo): Bring [[Manastone]], [[Overclocked Manastone]], and potions as all group members experience massive energy drain during battle.
   - (Note: As a Tank with 158-million HP, I was able to solo all three of these. Once I was out of Power, I kited/jousted to stay out of reach, and kept firing arrows at Tiny & Co. It's worth knowing that Keewi and Brasoh will get down to almost zero health and yet somehow stay alive until after Toa in below half health. I must have shot a good 50-or-so arrows to kill Toa, but I was 2/3 health when he went down, because I was keeping out of his damage range most of the time.)
1. Go to {{waypoint -48, 4, -186}} to spawn [[The Junk Beast (Solo)]] and kill it.
1. Collect [[Maelin's Talismanic Whirlgurt]] at {{waypoint -19, 12, -170}}.  ![Maelin's Talismanic Whirlgurt](images/Maelin's_Talismanic_Whirlgurt.jpg)
   - If it is locked, that means you forgot to pick up the "Magnetic Ether Compensator" in step 5.
1. Speak to [[Meldrath the Marvelous (Clockwork)]] at {{waypoint -16, 4, -122}}.
   - At this point you should have 3 of the 4 parts required, namely:
     - **[[Muon's Planarwave Actuator]]**
     - **[[Magnetic Ether Compensator]]**
     - **[[Maelin's Talismanic Whirlgurt]]**
   - **Possibly obsolete warning:** *Do not speak to Meldrath until you have all three parts in your possession!*
1. Approach {{waypoint 26, 4, -185}} to spawn [[Meldrath the Malignant]] and listen to his rant. ![Meldrath the Malignant](images/Meldrath_the_Malignant.jpg)
1. Kill [[The Manaetic Behemoth (Solo)]] when it becomes active.
   - Periodically it will put down red circles and send out missiles to the center of the circles. The missiles do huge damage if they hit and will explode a short time after landing. The safest place to be is underneath the Behemoth!
1. Walk to {{waypoint 24, 21, -277}} and speak to [[The Great Gear]] (spawns as you approach) ![The Great Gear.](images/The_Great_Gear.jpg)
1. 4 of the [[A glitching clockwork|glitching clockworks]] will become active. Defeat them.
1. Gather the [[Gyro-stabilized Sorcerous Generator]] at {{waypoint 43, 3, -192}}. ![Gyro-stablized Sorcerous Generator](images/Gyro-stablized_Sorcerous_Generator.jpg)
1. Return to [[Meldrath the Marvelous]] at {{waypoint -16, 4, -122}} to receive [[Enigmatic Portal Opening Device]].
1. Return to [[Druzzil Ro]] in the [[Coliseum of Valor]] at {{waypoint -4, 6, 4}}.  You will receive adventure experience when you talk to her.
