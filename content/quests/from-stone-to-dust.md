## Steps

I touched the urn, and silence. Time stopped for one moment, and I felt my cage. They want to keep me in here, forever. They say that it is more than I deserve, an example for the rest who would come. I must overcome my captors and make good my escape! The gargoyles are my jailers, and so they must be the first to fall.

1. Destroy the gargoyles, the [[An animated defeater]] from the Sleeper's Tomb.
