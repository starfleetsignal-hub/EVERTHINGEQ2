## Steps

Nothing is free and building, maintaining, and supporting the herald's camp is expensive. That's why the herald of Karana has requested that we assist in offsetting the camp's cost by retrieving a valuable relic guarded by Ashnu. I need to retrieve the relic.

1. Retrieve the relic guarded by Ashnu in [[Vaashkaani: Golden Rule (Solo)]]
   - The relic is at {{waypoint 610, 71, -59}}
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Stormlord]] {{waypoint -371, -1, -1091}} in the [[Enchanted Lands]]
