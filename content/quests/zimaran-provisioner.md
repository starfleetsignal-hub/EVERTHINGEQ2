## Note

Upon accepting this quest, you will receive [[Zimaran Provisioner Writ]] which will automatically scribe the [[Zimaran Intangible Drink]] and [[Zimaran Intangible Food]] recipes to your recipe book

Once you click the clipboard, you will be granted the fuel necessary to complete the writ:

- [[Tradeskill Writ Fuel]] (30)

## Steps

I need to complete the work order as requested.

- I must create:
  - [[Zimaran Intangible Drink]] (30)
  - [[Zimaran Intangible Food]] (30)

Quest auto-completes after last combine.

## Rewards

- At Least NeedInfo status
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
