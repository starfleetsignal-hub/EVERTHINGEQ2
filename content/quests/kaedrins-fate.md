## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 80+

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Captain Rybolth]] at the Claymore Plaza in [[North Qeynos]] in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 282, -20, -27}}
1. Enter the [[Office of Baron Ironforge]], up the stairs at the [[Ironforge Exchange]] with Captain Rybolth to arrest [[Baron Kaedrin Ironforge|Kaedrin]]. {{waypoint 352, -18, -98}}
   - Fight to capture [[Baron Kaedrin Ironforge]].
   - Captain Rybolth will fight for you like a pet or mercenary.
   - Kaedrin will store all damage when he says, "Whatever pain you inflict on me, I will unleash against you a hundred fold", then reflect it all at you when he says, "Now... die by your own hand!" When he says that, joust out. Then back in.
1. Return to [[Queen Antonia Bayle (Queen Antonia's Chambers)|Queen Antonia]] at the [[Queen Antonia's Chambers|Palace]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]].
