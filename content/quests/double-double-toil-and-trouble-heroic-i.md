I spoke to someone called Dr. Arcana in Loping Plains near a mysterious portal, opened in the Village of Somborn. In their very cryptic way, Dr. Arcana asked that I take advantage of the portal and enter Vasty Deep, in pursuit of powerful magical relics. I was warned to be very careful within the "temple of amoral exploration of magic, science, mutation and intelligent design", noting the inhabitants that may already be inside this "temple", may be just as dangerous as the invaders I will be trailing behind. Overall, I am expected to beat these plunderers to some very powerful arcana.

## Steps

1. I must delve deeper into the laboratories of Vasty Deep if I am to locate any magical artifacts.
   - Gain access to the bowels of Vasty Deep
     - Defeat [[Palovina Vodlak]]
1. I am to claim items of knowledge and power.
   - I am looking for important magical items within Vasty Deep

1. I should take these items to Dr. Arcana.
