The task Dr. Arcana has given me is not a small one. Though they mock my being a "hero", it is understood I am the only one for this job. The directions are simple. Dispose of the witches and warlocks I meet, destroy their minions and twisted bodyguards, and claim what items of power or knowledge they are seeking.
