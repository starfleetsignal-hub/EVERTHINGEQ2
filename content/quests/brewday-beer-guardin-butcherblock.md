## Notes

This NPC is actually on the beach, north of the docks, NOT up in the mountainous area up the ramp.

## Steps

Before you can begin this race you  must [[Mount|dismount]]. The carpet needed for the race will not spawn until you cancel your mount.

1. To join the Brewday competition, first head to the starter pad.
   - Step on the Brewday competition starter pad to begin {{waypoint 253, 28, 283}}
1. The Brewday competition will start soon. You will not be able to join if you are on another mount when it starts.
   - Get ready to race
1. I must knock down as many goblins as possible in the time allotted. (2 minutes 30 seconds)
   - Knock down as many goblins as possible!
1. I need to return to Otengrad, the dwarven brewmaster who tasked me with saving the Beer Garden.
   - Speak with Otengrad

## Rewards

Your reward selection is based off the number of goblins knocked down during the time allotted. The more goblins knocked over, the greater your prize.

- This is an unlimited, repeatable quest, so you can try again and chose new items each time. Make sure to run over the beer floating in the area, as it grants a speed boost!
- This is a great way to collect extra [[Beer Token]] (event currency) in a short time, to spend with [[Balin Copperfoot]].
- To see house items before you pick them as a prize while in-game: hold down CTRL, place your mouse over the item icon, and L click.

### Small Prize

Knock down (1-50)
One of the Following:

- 2 [[Bangers & Mush]] (consumable food item, not to be confused with the house item, [[Bangers and Mush]])
- 2 [[Brezels (Brell's Pretzels)]] (consumable food item)
