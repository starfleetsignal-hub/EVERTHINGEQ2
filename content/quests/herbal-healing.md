## Description

The Kerra Isle outfitter has requested assistance in refilling the medicinal supply stores.

## Steps

1. Retrieve 15 "medicinal supplies" for Harriet.
   - These can be any of the following:
     - The buds from nepeta plants scattered around the village on [[Kerra Isle]].
     - The bark from salix bushes throughout [[The Highland Salts]].
     - The spores from chrysogena fungi growing within [[Toxxulia Forest]] near the [[Toxxulia's Reach|Reach]].
   - *Note: if you search well enough, you can receive all 15 updates on Kerra Isle, in and around the village from 15 nepeta plants. See the Discussion Page for the locations.*
1. Return to [[Harriet]] and let her know you have finished.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,000 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +2,500 faction with **Kerra Isle Outfitters**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Kerra Isle Daily Supply Tasks
