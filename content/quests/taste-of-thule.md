*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Find a note (auto-update) on one of the lizardmen in the area of {{waypoint 151, 7, 261}}.
1. Return to [[Gretchen Spiritstorm]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 55s 91c
- [[Alternate Advancement]] Experience
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Chakram of Mauling]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Erudite's Silver Staff]] (All Mages)
  - [[Longblade of the Cold Hearted]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Randomly Discovered Hammer of Orc Slaughtering]] (All Priests)
  - [[Rod of the Inexhaustible Master]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Sea-Worn Claymore]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Sludge-Coated Aegis]] (All Priests)
  - [[Sludge-Coated Barrier]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Sludge-Coated Effigy]] (All Priests)
  - [[Sludge-Coated Guard]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sludge-Coated Symbol]] (All Mages)
