## Starting the Quest

![a wandering chest near Kurns Tower](images/A_wandering_chest_%28Kurns_Tower%29.png)

1. Click on a wandering chest in [[Fens of Nathsar]] in the Field of Bone by Kurns Tower. It roams behind the west side of Kurn's tower, past the PQ, around the head of the dragon bones, up the east side, and possibly all the way north to near the coastline *(near the Rime area)*, then followed the coast west until he was just north of Kurn's Tower. Then it turned and started going east again.
   - *Note: it moves slow, not trackable .*
   - Note: You cannot see it if you do not have the pre-requisites done.
   - Note: Recommend making a macro with the command line "/target a wandering".
   - *Example: I found the chest at {{waypoint -1505.16, -368.46, -1424.90}}, shortly before it turned northward.*
1. It tells you to get three items:
   - Note: (The lock on this magical chest says, "Those who employ the sorcerer's stone, the enchanter's scroll, and the summoner's blade know the power they hold. Together, they are the key to unimaginable power.")
   - [[Enchanter's Scroll]] - [[Cobalt Scar]] at {{waypoint 4420.46, -819.82, 357.75}} - in the tent on a wooden box
   - [[Summoner's Blade]] - [[Loping Plains]] at {{waypoint -376.48, -6.77, -242.84}} on the box under tent. (Fast travel to the Wizard Spire in the north).
   - [[Sorcerer's Stone]] - [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]] at {{waypoint -1075, 745, -477}} inside the Eye of El'Arad on a table in the back. (teleport to the north druid ring)
1. Return to Fens to a location near {{waypoint -60.73, -63.40, -304.49}} which is closest to Omen's Call.
1. Combine the 3 pieces to the [[Magician's Summoned Key]] -  When you do this, a different chest nearby should spawn and become visible to you. it wanders from Omen's Call to the tower at {{waypoint -555.28, -36.32, -337.68}}, where it is killed by guards and respawns in 10 minutes again at {{waypoint -60.73, -63.40, -304.49}}.
1. By clicking this second chest, you get a [[Crumbled Evocation Stone]].
1. Inspect it to receive the quest "Curse of the Magic Thief"

## Steps

1. I should search and see if I can find information on this crumbled stone.
   1. Click on an **evocation stone** ![Evocation Stone](images/Evocation_stone.jpg) the mage trainers within [[Gorowyn]] in [[Timorous Deep]] (spawns randomly in [[The Arcane Erudition]])
      - Potential locations: {{waypoint 2552.63, 119.86, 1278.64}} and {{waypoint 2526.80, 119.86, 1283.57}}
   1. Speak to the three apprentices nearby, ([[Apprentice Tholak]], [[Apprentice Chalak]], and [[Apprentice Tawen]]).
   1. Speak to [[Vulkun Niab'li]] (the Wizard Trainer) and receive [[Scratched Crow Coin]].
1. This coin is my clue.
   1. Enter [[Crow's Resting Place, Thieves Guild]] in [[The Down Below]].
      - *If you get two identical options, use the second one. The other will not update - none of the below mentioned will have dialog about the thief.*
      - Hail [[Gyle]]
      - Hail [[Colonel "Mutt" Fiddletwang]]
      - Hail [[Rollus the Sly]]
      - After some dialogue, [[Tumpy]] will trade a **Blackscale rune cube** for the information you need.
1. I need to seek a **Blackscale rune cube** - *The cube can be found in the either of the following two zones. It's a large white cube with runes on all sides.* ![Blackscale rune cube](images/Blackscale_rune_cube.png)
   - In the [[Skarize Dracoratory]] in [[The Halls of Fate]] (*not* the Fabled version) at {{waypoint 100.61, -27.54, 0.07}}, {{waypoint 138.33, -27.54, -0.67}}, {{waypoint 123.34, -27.54, 21.01}}, {{waypoint 142.67, -27.07, 16.14}} or {{waypoint 96.59, -27.54, 20.64}}
   - In the [[Archives of the Wyrm]] in [[Fabled Deathtoll]] (you may need a group), at {{waypoint -590.24, 119.82, -244.82}} near [[Fitzpitzle (Fabled)|Fitzpitzle]].
1. Return to Tumpy in [[Crow's Resting Place, Thieves Guild]]
   - *Note: If there is more than one option, choose the bottom one.*
1. Seek the ratonga thief known as [[Vasty One Eye (Kralet Penumbra Contested)|Vasty One Eye]].
   - Evil aligned: Speak with [[Beckah Whistlethorn]] in [[Smuggler's Grotto]] near the Fence (Black Market Broker) [[Fuddlesprock]] in [[Qeynos Province District]]
     - The way in is in the water at {{waypoint 900.83, -31.34, 43.60}}
   - Good aligned: Speak with [[First-Mate Gimblefuzzle]] {{waypoint -405.64, -56.95, -16.21}} at the [[Conqueror's Flame]] near the Fence (Black Market Broker) [[G'Thal]] in [[The City of Freeport]]
1. Seek the ratonga thief known as Vasty One Eye, far underground
   1. Speak with [[Vasty One Eye (Kralet Penumbra Contested)|Vasty One Eye]] (in this zone it's [[Vasith One Eye]]) in [[Kralet Penumbra (Contested)]] at {{waypoint -1.01, 0.08, 8.94}} ![Vasith One Eye is sometimes called "Vasty One Eye" especially by other thieves.](images/Vasith_One_Eye_in_Thallumbra_behind_Tinkered_Portal_Gate.jpg)
   1. Get ready to camp these bosses for hours to obtain the [[Sigil of Krakatus]] from either:
      - [[Kralet Penumbra: Temple of the Ill-Seen (Heroic)]] in a chest from  [[Xoth, the Ever Rending]]
        - *Loot from his chest [[Sigil of Krakatus]] (Not guaranteed drop, 1 per kill max, not group update.).*
      - [[Kralet Penumbra (Contested)]] from a body drop of [[Mo'Ithraf]] at {{waypoint 74.77, -3.07, 232}}
        - *Loot from his body [[Aberrant Cipher]] (Not guaranteed drop, 1 per kill max, not group update.).*
        - In same room there is a clicky on top of the brazier after you loot the cipher. **Clicking this will give you the Sigil, not the boss.**
1. Return to [[Vasty One Eye (Kralet Penumbra Contested)|Vasty One Eye]], and she will seem very forgetful. You need find a healer to help Vasith.
   - Speak with [[Osh (Great Divide)|Osh]] (Othmir) in the [[Great Divide]] at {{waypoint -1630, -535, -2427}}.
     - *Just to the right of zone in from [[The Sinking Sands]], or take the griffon from [[Thurgadin Harbor]] to [[Icy Fingers]].*
     - *Note: at this point you will need to have the [[Cobalt Scar Timeline]] requirement done or Osh will not update, [[Guise of Pursuit]] will not help you here.*
     - *Note: [[Osh (Great Divide)|Osh]] will not update this quest if you have started [[The Essence of a Prophet]] (need to do all othmir quests in great divide and cobalt scar for update 7/4/18)*
1. Gather the following items;
   1. ***Immensely durable band*** - Purchase a [[Band of Illumine Umbrite]] from [[Tillwanee]] in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]] for 500p, {{waypoint -424, 258, 389}}.
      - Requires 50,000 [[Zou'Lidelas (Faction)|Zou'Lidelas Faction]]. You can raise this faction via the following repeatable quests:
        - [[Culling the Time Away]] (+1,000 faction with **Zou'Lidelas**)
        - [[Leech Liberator]] (+250 faction with **Zou'Lidelas**)
        - [[Memory Bloom]] (+250 faction with **Zou'Lidelas**)
        - [[Pox Raiding]] (+250 faction with **Zou'Lidelas**)
   1. ***Potion of huge wave of peace*** - Purchase [[Dram of The Tranquil]] from [[Al'Diun Stirhinis]] for 500p at {{waypoint 1807, -299, 3511}} in [[Paineel]].
      - Requires 50,000 [[Bastion of the Tranquil (Faction)|Bastion of the Tranquil Faction]]. You can raise this faction via the repeatable quest [[The Never Ending Mending of a Broken Land]] (+2,000 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**).
   1. ***Potion of Mental Acuity Bolster*** - Pick up the [[Phial of Mental Fortification]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]] from the bench beside the banker {{waypoint -530, 175, 990}}.
1. Once you've collected all 3 items, return to [[Osh (Great Divide)|Osh]] (Othmir) in the [[Great Divide]]. He will combine them into [[Osh's Curse Disjunction Band]] you need for [[Vasty One Eye (Thalumbra)|Vasty One Eye]].
1. Talk to [[Vasty One Eye (Thalumbra)|Vasty One Eye]], who can be found behind the tinkered portal in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]] (near Maldura's outer wall) {{waypoint 449, 95, 62}} and get her to put the ring on
1. Talk to Vasty again to complete the quest.
