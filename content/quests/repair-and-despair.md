**Easter Egg** In Birchbark Oakleaf's dialog, look for a reference to another *famous* treant. ;)

I was able to convince Birchbark Oakleaf, an ancient treant rooted deep within the soil of Deadwood Refuge, that repairing the damaged Ulteran Spires is in the best interest of the entire island, including his copse.

## Steps

![Ulteran Spire Debris](images/Ulteran_Spire_Debris.png)

1. Gather 8 scattered pieces of the broken Ulteran spires. (General area: {{waypoint -100.52, -26.19, 125.72}})
   - Click the scattered bluish-grey rocks with sparkling runes.
   - Slay the [[A corrupted wisp of Growth|corrupted wisp of Growth]] that appears.
   - Click the rock again to pick up the debris.
1. Return to [[Birchbark Oakleaf (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)‎|Birchbark Oakleaf]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 10,119 status
