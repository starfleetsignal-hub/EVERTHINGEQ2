## Materials

- 3 [[Aerated mineral water]]
- 15 [[Bonded loam]]
- 3 [[Diamondine cluster]]
- 12 [[Luminous Coal]]

## Steps

![Steamfont wetlands mud](images/Steamfontwetlandsmud.png)

1. Gather 3 [[Steamfont Wetlands Mud]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]] {{waypoint -740.70, 77.75, 1330.39}}
1. Create 3 [[Pottery Bowl|Pottery Bowls]] at a Workbench (one in [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] {{waypoint -519, 175, 1037}}*Note: If you fail one or more of the crafts, you have to delete the quest and re-start by talking to [[Jadrek Woolfe]].*
1. Speak to [[Jadrek Woolfe]] back in [[Everfrost]] {{waypoint 222.13, -19.23, 49.59}}.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 15s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- [[Eustace]] -- *tradeskill familiar*
- *Unlocks the ability to purchase Level 40-49 recipe books from Jadrek.*
