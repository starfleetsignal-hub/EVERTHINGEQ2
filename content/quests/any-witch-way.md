## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Found near any of the Dreadfell camps around {{waypoint -221.00, 105.73, 29.22}} or {{waypoint 60, 32, -295}}:
   - Slay 7 [[A Dreadfell gravestalker|Dreadfell gravestalkers]].
   - Slay 5 [[A Dreadfell necrocrafter|Dreadfell necrocrafters]].
   - Slay 6 [[A Dreadfell mortifier|Dreadfell mortifiers]].
1. Slay a [[A Dreadfell deadweaver]].
1. Find and kill the disguised witch:
   - [[A seahag-disguised witch]]. Possible locations:
     - {{waypoint 171, 91, 480}}
     - {{waypoint 147.08, 109.70, 360.89}}
     - {{waypoint 284.79, 120.18, 525.39}}
     - {{waypoint 127.94, 111.77, 442.30}}
   - [[A lycan-infected witch]]. Possible locations:
     - {{waypoint 501.34, 102.23, 0.36}}
     - {{waypoint 418.87, 102.16, 128.87}}
     - {{waypoint 460, 110, -62}}
     - {{waypoint 348, 107, 47}}
1. Return to [[Dabhiti]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- Choice of:
  - [[Grisly Dreadfell Totem]]
  - [[Vile Dreadfell Totem]]
- The following two items only appear the FIRST time you complete the quest, even if you do not choose one of them, so be sure to pick it over the house items if you want one of them.
  - [[Kamapor Blade of Stratagem]]
  - [[Kamapor Great Blade of Stratagem]]
