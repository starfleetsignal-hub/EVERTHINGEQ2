## Notes

- You may want to do this access quest at the same time you are doing [[The Riddle of Razorwing]], part of the [[Claymore Timeline]], as the steps for the first two floors are identical.
- The quest itself has only one stated step: "I need to gather the symbols that Ritnoc told me about and take them to him." You will not receive quest journal updates for the four symbols you must loot.
- The [[Ornate Slashed Eye]] and [[Ornate Winged Eye]] will drop from gray con aviaks on the first and second floor.
- The [[Ornate Ice Eye]] and the [[Ornate Fire Eye]] **will not** drop from grey con aviaks on the higher floors.
- If you are a high level player soloing this instance, mentor down to 80/85.
- Killing all of the bosses in this zone awards you the [[Ascending the Sky]] achievement.

## Steps

1. First Floor - Slashing Talon
   1. Kill aviaks until an [[Ornate Slashed Eye]] drops (uncommon chest drop).
   1. Use the Ornate Slashed Eye on the [[Effigy of the Slashing Talon]] to spawn [[Prophet of The Slashing Talon]].
      - Globules of 'accumulating energy' will spawn and begin to revolve around the statue at the center of the room. After a couple minutes the big globule will die and the prophet swill spawn where the NPC was.
   1. Kill Prophet of The Slashing Talon and loot [[Symbol of the Slashing Talon]] from his corpse.
1. Second Floor - Flapping Wing
   1. Kill aviaks until an [[Ornate Winged Eye]] drops (uncommon body drop).
   1. Use the Ornate Winged Eye on the [[Effigy of The Flapping Wing]] to spawn [[Ancient of the Flapping Wing]].
   1. Kill Ancient of the Flapping Wing and loot [[Symbol of the Flapping Wing]] from his corpse.
1. Third Floor - Icy Breath
   1. Kill aviaks until an [[Ornate Ice Eye]] drops (uncommon chest drop).
   1. Climb the wall at {{waypoint 189, 57, 0}}.
   1. Go to the elevator and ride it up to the [[Great Hall of Ice]].
   1. Use the Ornate Ice Eye on the [[Effigy of The Icy Breath]] to spawn [[Ireth the Cold]] on the pedestal above the elevator.
   1. Kill Ireth the Cold and loot [[Symbol of The Icy Breath]] from her corpse.
      - Pull Ireth off the pedestal and engage her on the floor, otherwise you may be unable to loot the symbol.
      - There is a wing buffet attack, the tank should position against a wall to mitigate the knockback.
1. Fourth Floor - Fire Breath
   1. Go to back to the first climbable wall on the third floor and climb the other wall at {{waypoint -17, 56, 0}}.
   1. Kill aviaks until an [[Ornate Fire Eye]] drops (uncommon chest drop).
   1. Climb the wall at {{waypoint -169, 111, 13}}.
   1. Go to the elevator and ride it up to the [[Great Hall of Fire]].
   1. Use the Ornate Fire Eye on the [[Effigy of The Fire Breath]] to spawn [[Sharti of The Flame]]  on the pedestal above the elevator.
   1. Kill Sharti of the Flame and loot [[Symbol of The Fire Breath]] from her corpse.
      - Pull Sharti off the pedestal and engage her on the floor, otherwise you may be unable to loot the symbol.
      - There is a wing buffet attack, the tank should stay against the north wall to mitigate the knockback.
      - As soon as you use the Effigy, immediately move to the north wall. Avoid using melee pets during this fight to avoid the spawn of heroic adds. You are encouraged to remove melee pets, set mage pets to maximum distance, have the tank melee, and all others should stay ranged, if possible.
1. Return to [[Ritnoc Flyfeather]] outside the instance with all four symbols in your inventory. {{waypoint -181.9, -165.59, -274.89}}

## Rewards

- Access to [[Ascent of the Awakened: Audience with the Guardian]]
