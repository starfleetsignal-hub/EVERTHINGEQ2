## Steps

1. I must create 5 [[Kobold Bane Spell Scroll|Kobold Bane Spell Scrolls]].
   - Scribe the [[Kobold Bane For All Occasions]] recipe
   - You will need:
     - 5 [[Belladonna root|Belladonna Root]]
     - 5 [[Severed ash|Severed Ash]]
     - 5 [[Rough agate (Version 2)|Rough Agate]]
     - 15 [[Sparkling incense]] (level 20 - 29)
1. Craft 5 of the [[Kobold Bane Spell Scroll|Kobold Bane Spell Scrolls]] at the Engraved Desk {{waypoint 755.93, 26.00, 605.20}}
1. I must return the spells to Cordelia.

- At least 1g 4s
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
