## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- Although this quest uses [[Harvestables (Tier 1)|Tier 1 harvestables]] and low level recipes, since the quest scales to your level, it is an excellent way to level a [[Provisioner]]. Furthermore, because any tradeskill class can complete it, this quest can also be a great way to level *any* tradeskill class.
- This [[City Festival]] quest rewards the greatest number of [[City Token|City Tokens]] and players who bring a portable stove and keg can craft right by the NPC, making it a popular choice for those who want to accrue several tokens in a very short time.
- Though you can return to a city or guild hall to use a [[Stove & Keg]], you can save a great deal of time by bringing one of the 2 portable crafting stations (tables) made by a [[Tinkerer]]. All tradeskill classes can use these charms.
  - For level 30-59 crafters, grab a [[Portable Cooking Set]]. One charge of this charm lasts for 5 mins.
  - For level 60 and above crafters, grab a [[Portable Cooking Set v2.0]]. One charge of this charm lasts for 15 mins.

## Steps

1. Craft the following provisions:
   - 4 drinks, either [[White Tea]] or [[Black Coffee]]:
     - White Tea:
       - 1 [[Aerated mineral water]]
       - 1 [[Raw white tea leaf]]
       - 6 [[Basic Kindling]]
     - Black Coffee:
       - 1 [[Aerated mineral water]]
       - 1 [[Black coffee bean]]
       - 1 [[Basic Kindling]]
   - 4 food items, either [[Sunfish Roll]] or [[Deer Steak]]
     - Sunfish Roll:
       - 1 [[Dough (Level 1)|Dough]]
       - 1 [[Sunfish]]
       - 1 [[Basic Kindling]]
     - Deer Steak:
       - 1 [[Deer meat]]
       - 1 [[Root]]
       - 1 [[Basic Kindling]]
1. Return to [[Kella Swampfoot]].

## Rewards

- 4 [[City Token|City Tokens]]
- Tradeskill Experience (for Provisioners)
