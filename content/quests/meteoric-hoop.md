*NOTE*: The Meteor Chunk is also a rare drop off mobs in the small instance [[The Buccaneer's Rest]].

## Steps

1. Kill 20 octopi in Antonica to gather their ink.
   - Any type of octopus will update: [[A Coldwind octopus]], [[An oar grappler]], etc...
1. Harvest some Sandwashed Rock to make a hoop
1. Kill [[A Cavemaw tunneler|Cavemaw tunnelers]] for a small stone hammer. *(rare update)*
