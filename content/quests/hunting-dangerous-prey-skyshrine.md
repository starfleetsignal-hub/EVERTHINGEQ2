## Steps

**Kill the following enemies**

- **Annihilate the Necranaut in Lyceum of the Recondite:** Kill [[Necranaut Dolk]] in [[Lyceum of the Recondite]]
- **Bring death to the Deathmonger in the Underdepths:** Kill [[Sevalor Gorescale]] in [[Skyshrine: The Underdepths]]
- **Banish the Commander and his spirit vessels in the Covenant District:** Kill [[Commander Mirrakar]] in [[Covenant District]]

OR

- **Rout the Captain in the Covenant District:** Kill [[Captain Veezaran]] in [[Covenant District]]
- **Execute the Arch-Necranaut in Lyceum of the Recondite:** Kill [[Arch-Necranaut Yolth]] in [[Lyceum of the Recondite]]
- **Conquer the conqueror in Dracur Prime:** Kill [[Tigloth the Conqueror]] in [[Dracur Prime]]

OR

- **Crush the Commander in Lyceum of the Recondite:** Kill Kill [[Ovilas]] in [[Lyceum of the Recondite]]
- **Conquer the General and his Cohorts in Dracur Prime:** Kill Kill [[Mardonn Myr'Valak]], [[Ariorb Vel'Kera]], and [[Xerxis Mal'Fyrm]] in [[Dracur Prime]]
- **Destroy the Ancient Drakota in the Underdepths:** Kill Kill [[Malteor Flamecaller]] in [[Skyshrine: The Underdepths]]
