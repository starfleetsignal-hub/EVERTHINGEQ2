## Steps

1. Deliver the stones to [[Inayat Wijdani]] in Paineel's [[Hall of Necromancy]] {{waypoint 1652, -298, 3162}}.
1. Once enchanted, return to Fazia in the [[Stonebrunt Encampment]].  Use the teleporter located slightly NE to get to the city and back quickly.

- At least 46g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
