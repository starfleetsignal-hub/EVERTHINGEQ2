## Prerequisties

- You must speak [[Druzaic]].

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Malkonis D'Morte (Living Tombs)|Malkonis D'Morte]] who spawns when you inspect the gargoyle.
1. Read [[A stone tablet]] in your inventory
1. Examine the following urns strewn around the [[Statue Court]] for their unique runes:
   - Rune of Tumek/The Sand Ash Urn: {{waypoint -190, -7, 336}}
   - Rune of Tek/The Cinder Ash Urn: {{waypoint -165, 1, 475}}
   - Rune of Tol/![The Soot Ash Urn](images/Image:The_Soot_Ash_Urn.jpg): {{waypoint -143, -5, 453}}
   - Rune of Hal/The Ember Ash Urn: {{waypoint -79, -3, 440}}
   - Rune of Ovu/The Dust Ash Urn: {{waypoint -54, 2, 355}}
1. Inspect ![The Tumek'Un Monolith](images/Image:The_Tumek_Un_Monolith.jpg). {{waypoint -104, 11, 282}}
1. Speak to [[Tumek'Toht]].
1. Complete the following tasks, in any order:
   - Kill 10 [[A Sulite tjat (Living Tombs)|Sulite tjats]] for common updates. {{waypoint -101, -3, 360}}; found in groups around the Statue Court
   - Kill 8 [[A statue warden|statue wardens]] for common updates. {{waypoint -101, -3, 360}}; roaming around the Statue Court
1. Fill the censers: (right click to fill)
   - ![Censer of Vohk](images/Image:Censer_of_Vohk.jpg): {{waypoint -74, -3, 372}}
   - ![Censer of Multan](images/Image:Censer_of_Multan.jpg): {{waypoint -200, 0, 371}}
1. Reinspect the Tumek'Un Monolith, resummon Tumek'Toht, and speak to him again

## Rewards

- [[An Elddar pinecone]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Artificer's Bracelet]]
  - [[Mantle of the Silent Watcher]]
  - [[Deathtoll Battle Hammer]]
  - [[Mace of Swift Death]]
