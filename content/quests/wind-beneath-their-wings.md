## Notes

The quest [[Sticky Sweet Vengeance]] and the 2 followed by it will be needed later for [[Ballads of Zimara: Song of the Maedjinn]]

## Steps

*Ecihoolo's plan to free her fellow flock members from the Zakir Rish depends on me directly applying the corrosive oil of vitriol she made.*

1. Use the [[Oil of Vitriol]] received from [[Ecihoolo]] to free the owls on [[Auric Palisades]], taking the cloud at {{waypoint -683, 145, -5}}
   - Note: Ensure there are **no** npcs nearby, or pointer will not turn into a Hand to free Owl.
   - Note: NPCs above will be killable nonaggro mobs
     - Kreaw at {{waypoint -656.37, 132.93, -391.38}}
     - Qrashra at {{waypoint -495.88, 94.51, -485.30}}
     - Hooatio at {{waypoint -483.41, 94.45, -492.81}}
     - Arrei at {{waypoint -366.98, 122.73, -616.56}}
     - Caiw at {{waypoint -349.08, 126.54, -676.44}}
1. Return to [[Ecihoolo]] on [[Isles of Wisdom]]
   - Note: Take the cloud {{waypoint -707.38, 145.28, -364.25}} to [[Isles of Wisdom]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +2,000 faction with **Isles of Wisdom**
