## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Succulent Root Basket for my apprentice.
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Succulent Root Basket]]
   1. Craft [[A Sturdy Succulent Root Basket]] on a [[Woodworking Table]] using the following materials and fuel:
      - 2 [[Succulent root]]
      - 4 [[Lambent sandpaper]]
1. I need to take this basket back to my apprentice.
