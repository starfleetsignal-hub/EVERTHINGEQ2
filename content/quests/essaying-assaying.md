## You will need:

- 6 [[Glimmering candle|glimmering candles]]

## Steps

1. Scribe [[Cinderton's Ore Assay Techniques, vol. 1]] you received from [[Karalyn Cinderton]].
1. Craft [[Cinderton Acid]] at the [[Chemistry Table]]  using the following ingredients provided by [[Karalyn Cinderton]]:
   - [[Clockwork Battery Acid]]
   - [[Pure Salt Crystal]]
1. Complete the [[Ore Quality Assay]] by mixing the [[Crushed Mortartoe Mine Ore|crushed ore sample]] and [[Copper Metal]] in the [[Cinderton Acid]] on the same Chemistry table.
1. Turn in the results to [[Karalyn Cinderton]].

## Rewards

- At least 4g 6s
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
