## Steps

- After hailing Shi'Jan, you will be zoned into the [[Trial of Hua Mei]].
- Hail [[The Hua Mein Champion]] (level 91^) to start the battle. He will immediately attack. Kill him.
- Speak to Shi'jan in the arena to get zoned out.
- Speak to [[Shi'jan Sensou|Shi'jan]] again in the [[Hua Mein Village]] to get your reward.

## Reward

- 30g 10s 55c
- [[Arena Master's Token]] (Quest update for [[The Never Ending Mending of a Broken Land]])
