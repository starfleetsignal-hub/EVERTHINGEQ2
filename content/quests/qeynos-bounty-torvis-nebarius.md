## Steps

1. Travel to [[North Freeport]] via [[The Thieves' Way]] and kill [[Torvis Nebarius]] who will spawn in the outer hallway of the building just outside of the sewer at {{waypoint 7, -19, -246}}
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- +3000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
