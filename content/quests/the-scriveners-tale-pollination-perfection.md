## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 8 [[Basilisk meat]]
- 12 [[Flitterfin tang]]
- 6 [[Reef cluster]]
- 18 [[Strangler root]]
- 50 [[Celestial Candle]]

## Steps

1. In [[Vegarlson, the Earthen Badlands]] click on a collapsed elder arborian at {{waypoint -337, 35, -28}} to receive [[Recipe Scroll: Earthen Balm]].
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Earthen Balm]], craft [[An Earthen Balm|Earthen Balm]] on a [[Chemistry Table]], and use the [[An Earthen Balm|balm]] on a collapsed elder arborian at {{waypoint -338, 35, -29}}.
   - There is a [[Chemistry Table]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]] through the teleporter to Crafters' Gallery at {{waypoint 332, 16, 174}}
   - There is also a [[Chemistry Table]] right inside the zone in [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]] (more zoning, less running, if you are so inclined)
1. Speak with [[Temry Loneivy]] at {{waypoint -335, 35, -35}}.

## Rewards

- [[Tincture of Bark Recipe]]
- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
- [[Vegarlson, the Earthen Badlands: Catalogue Creature]] ability (auto scribed in Abilities in Knowledge book, this will be needed for next quest)
