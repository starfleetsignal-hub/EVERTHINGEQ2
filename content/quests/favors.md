## Steps

1. Speak to [[Poko Zing]]. {{waypoint 887, -12, -562}}
1. Harvest nodes in the Forest Ruins to get the following quest updates:
   - 1 bark sample from [[A fallen branch]] - found south of the river and outside the ruins {{waypoint 1075, -20, -605}}
   - 1 bark sample from [[A fallen branch]] - found north of the river and outside the ruins {{waypoint 1166, -22, -750}}
   - 1 bark sample from [[A fallen branch]] - found south of the river and inside the ruins {{waypoint 1047, -10, -695}}
   - 1 bark sample from [[A fallen branch]] - found north of the river and inside the ruins {{waypoint 1083, -10, -741}}
   - 4 rocks from [[Fallen rubble]] inside or outside of the ruins
1. Speak again to [[Poko Zing]].
1. Return to [[Acting Lieutenant Germain]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 92c
- +125 faction with **The Concordium**
