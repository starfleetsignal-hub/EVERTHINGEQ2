## Steps

1. Kill Ry'Gorr orcs at [[Jagged Daggers Isle]] around {{waypoint 600.94, 44.4, 587.63}} and destroy the weapon supplies around
   - Kill 4 [[A Ry'Gorr grenadier|Ry'Gorr grenadier]]s
   - Kill 4 [[A Ry'Gorr infiltrator|Ry'Gorr infiltrator]]s
   - Destroy 4 weapon racks
     - {{waypoint 696.27, 58.34, 645.86}}
     - {{waypoint 683.72, 58.92, 665.78}}
     - {{waypoint 698.56, 55.40, 607.67}}
     - {{waypoint 677.51, 52.61, 594.66}}
1. Kill [[A Ry'Gorr war shaman]] and loot a [[Ry'Gorr Scrying Eye]] from the body
1. Return to Thirin
