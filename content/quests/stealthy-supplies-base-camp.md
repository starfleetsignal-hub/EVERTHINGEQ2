## Notes

Materials needed:

- 8 Chalcedony
- 20 Crowberry
- 12 Fox Pelt
- 38 Ilmenite Ore
- 1 Manganese Ore (rare ore)
- 12 Ptarmigan Meat
- 8 Red Lichen
- 8 Sedge Root
- 16 Speckled Grayling

Fuels needed:

- 20 Zimaran Candle
- 30 Zimaran Coal
- 20 Zimaran Filament
- 40 Zimaran Kindling

## Steps

After Captain Raffik's ship has crashed many supplies were lost or damaged, so Raffik (now Raffik <Shipless Captain>)  needs you to replenish some things. You receive a [[Recipe Scroll: Base Camp Items]] with 7 recipes which auto scribe when you accept quest

1. Craft items
   1. Create anvil
      - this is called [[Makeshift Anvil]] in recipe book
        - *uses 1 [[Manganese Ore]] (rare ore), 10 [[Ilmenite Ore]], 2 [[Fox Pelt]] and 5 [[Zimaran Coal]]
   1. Create [[Forging Tools]]
      - *uses 4 [[Ilmenite Ore]], 2 [[Fox Pelt]] and 5 [[Zimaran Coal]].
      - *You receive a bonus quest reward [[Crafter's Makeshift Smithing Hammer]] appearance weapon*
1. Collect 4 [[Glowing Lichen]]
   - *clickable nodes on a rock at {{waypoint 92, 9, 776}}, Harvest Tracking works for these*
   - *fish in water around there are non aggressive*
1. Craft 4 x [[Dungeon Light Source]]
   - *each uses 1 [[Glowing Lichen]], 3 [[Ilmenite Ore]], 2 [[Chalcedony]] and 5 [[Zimaran Coal]]*
1. Gather 4 [[Gorewing Leather]]
   - *these are sparkling patches of leather up on  hills above camp around {{waypoint 121, 63, 442}}, Harvest Tracking works for these*
   - *use  your [[W.H.A.T. V2]] if you can't kill surrounding mobs.*
1. Create 4 x [[Sturdy Blanket]]
   - each uses a [[Gorewing Leather]], 3 [[Ilmenite Ore]], 2 [[Fox Pelt]] and 5 [[Zimaran Filament]]
1. Craft items
   1. Create 4 x [[Raffik's Rations]] - each uses 1 Crowberry, 2 Ptarmigan Meat, 2 Sedge Root and 5  Zimaran Kindling
   1. Create 4 x [[Mathom's Mouthwatering Mock-Malt]] - each uses 1  Speckled Grayling, 2 Red Lichen, 2 Crowberry and 5 Zimaran Kindling.
1. Create 4 x [[Waterproofing Solution]] - each uses 1 Ptarmigan Meat, 3 Speckled Grayling, 2 Crowberry and 5 Zimaran Candle
1. Speak with [[Raffik (Western Wastes)|Raffik]] at {{waypoint 76, 7, 661}} in the [[Western Wastes]]
   - *he completes the quest and advises you to go speak to gnome Mesmer Gearloose.*

## Rewards

- [[Crafter's Makeshift Clamp]] appearance weapon
- [[Status Coin (75,000)|Status Coin [75,000]]]
- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
