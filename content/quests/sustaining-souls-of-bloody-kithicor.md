## Steps

1. Gather 12 souls from [[The Bloody Battlegrounds]]
1. Just kill all the [[A wandering skeleton|wandering  skeletons]] at the locations listed below, then go to [[The Bloody Battlegrounds]] where you will witness the ghostly battle.
   - {{waypoint 426, 48, 1160}}
   - {{waypoint 746, 29, 1145}}
   - {{waypoint 978, 94, 1142}}
   - {{waypoint 253, 50, 1042}}
   - {{waypoint 351, 53, 977}}
   - {{waypoint 465, 42, 942}}
   - {{waypoint 484, 27, 791}}
   - {{waypoint 344, 30, 743}}
   - {{waypoint 399, 79, 658}}
1. At the [[The Bloody Battlegrounds]], engage the non-aggro human spirits that now populate the area. Kill them one at a time, or in multiples if you can survive the fight. Each one that you kill will leave a spirit behind. You then use the phylactery on each spirit, until you have collected all 12.
1. Return to [[Destora J'Narus]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g 80s
- [[A carrion skitterer]]

## NOTE

This quest requires a triggered event:  ![Spawning point for wandering skeletons](images/Sustaining_Souls_of_Bloody_Kithicor_quest_-_pile_of_bones.jpg)
Kill [[A wandering skeleton]] in the forest near [[The Bloody Battlegrounds]] to start this event. Then kill the remaining 8 skeletons for the event to start. They spawn on "a Pile of Bones" and the placeholders are "a Fallen warrior".
