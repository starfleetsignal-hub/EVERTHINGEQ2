## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Morell-Thule has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items

1. Purchase the [[Moon-Touched Sleep Powder|moon-touched sleep powder]] from Nulvatok for 5000 status
   - In Thundering Steppes he wanders along the road from the [[Bridge Keep]] {{waypoint 170, -1, -158}} to south of the [[Southeast Station|South East Griffon Station]] {{waypoint -178, -1, 568}}
1. Purchase the [[Lulling Sands|lulling sand]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the [[Zimaran Dreamcatcher|Zimaran dreamcatcher]] from Kuutanya for 5000 status
   - Wanders the path on [[Isles of Wisdom]] in [[Zimara Breadth]]
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Lord of Dreams]] at {{waypoint 1097, -1, 646}} in [[The Thundering Steppes]]
