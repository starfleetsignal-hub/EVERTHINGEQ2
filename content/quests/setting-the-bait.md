## Steps

1. Saihah asks you to place the beacon at the predetermined spot near the cave entrance. {{waypoint 1431, 3, 3948.5}}
   - Look for a patch of earth with a green sparkle effect.
   - **Clear out quickly** after you place the beacon; *ALL* the nearby [[A stoneslough coiler|stoneslough coiler]]s will swarm to the spot!
1. Tell Saihah what the beacon did.
1. Deliver the report to [[Ishaq Al'Nair]] in Paineel at {{waypoint 1858, -294, 3473}}

- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
