1. Gather 6 Shadevine Pumpkins in [[Soulstare Forest]] {{waypoint -418.91, 141.66, -708.26}}.

     - {{waypoint -369, 158, -812}}
     - {{waypoint -269.88, 152.32, -665.72}}
     - {{waypoint -315.98, 141.69, -602.45}}
     - {{waypoint -375.10, 140.77, -578.08}}
     - {{waypoint -373.01, 141.79, -546.04}}
1. Return to [[Kapasha Talmil]].
