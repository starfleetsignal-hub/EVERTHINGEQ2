## Steps

1. Visit the following locations, in any order:
   - [[The Serpent of Ah'Rahn]]: {{waypoint 73, 30, 469}}
   - [[The Pool of Purity]]: {{waypoint 147, 21, 376}}
   - [[The Bath of Bloodshed]]: {{waypoint 139, 1, 361}}
   - 'The Mouth of Arms': {{waypoint 70, 8, 361}}
   - 'The Mouth of Rile': {{waypoint 220, 8, 360}}
   - [[The Gate of Sun]]: {{waypoint 143, 30, 167}}
1. Click the scroll on the ground. {{waypoint 143, 30, 167}}

## Rewards

- At least 6g 19s 1c
- Ability to start the quest [[An Offering and Peace]] by examining [[A medallion of urns]] received at the end of the [[The Seal of the Sea]] quest
