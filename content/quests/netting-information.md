## Description

Perhaps not everything in the Lower Corridors has been affected by the Curse of Ykesha, and in fact there may be some creatures that have escaped the grasp of the curse. I have been tasked with retrieving small creatures such as spiders, rats, and bats, and returning them to Firmroot for study. Perhaps by learning about those who have not been affected, a way to counter the curse may be found.

## Steps

Enter [[The Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors]]

1. Use the received *[[A thin woven net|thin woven net]] to capture 6 creatures such as spiders, rats, and bats
1. Kill a dar exterminator {{waypoint -168.89, -15.31, -51.44}}
1. Hand in the mission.
