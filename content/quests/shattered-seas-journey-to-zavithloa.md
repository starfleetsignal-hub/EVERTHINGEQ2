## Steps

1. Zone into [[Zavith'loa: The Hidden Caldera (Solo)]] or [[Zavith'loa: The Hidden Caldera (Heroic)]] at {{waypoint -1263, 79, -726}}.  Fight your way all the way through the zone until you kill the [[Fiery Effigy of Cloatl'thoa (Solo)]].  See the Zone's page for its strategy.
- Note: Heroic instance will also give quest updates (for everyone in group)
1. Return to [[Nuwat Pickletrade]] at [[Merinep]].  The quest updates at {{waypoint -1025, 98, -171}}
1. Talk to [[Seep]] close by
1. Talk to Nuwat at {{waypoint -565, 7, -582}}
1. Talk to [[Researcher Aillena Belzia]] at Sapswill {{waypoint 1228, 17, 1253}}
1. Go to [[The Crow's Nest]] on Isle of Refuge and defeat the enemy agent ([[An Agent of Freeport]] for good aligned characters, [[An Agent of Qeynos]] for evil aligned)
1. Return to Aillena who is now joined by Murrar Sharr (if good-aligned) or Harbinger Gloska (evil-aligned). Follow the conversation between them and Aillena.
