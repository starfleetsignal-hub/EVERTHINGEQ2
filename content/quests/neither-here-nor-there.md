## Requirements

You can only get this quest line if you chose to redeem yourself with the Tizmak Clan.

## Steps

1. Slay 8 [[A lingering detriment|a lingering detriments]] at [[Pit of the Awakened]] -65, 20000, 2885
   - *Note:They look like 3 flying skulls*
1. Speak with [[Misha]]

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
