This is the first in a series of four [[Nights of the Dead]] quests.

## Returning players

Characters who have previously completed this quest line may not see a quest feather over [[Ponchy Shorttoe]]'s head... simply hail him anyway if you have not done this quest this season on the current character.

## Steps

1. Head to [[McQuibble's Farmhouse]] {{waypoint -1857, -25, -210}}, you will get an automatic update upon entering.
1. Run over the three farm fields to the southwest of the farmhouse looking for patchs of soil. Find and click on a small pile of soil to advance the quest. (looks like a small anthill with red sparkles)
   - some are reported around:
     - {{waypoint -1742, -2, -169}}
     - {{waypoint -1763, -2, -87}}
     - {{waypoint -1703, -2, -81}}
     - {{waypoint -1695, -2, -68}}.
1. Run over the two farm fields to the northeast of the farmhouse, again looking for soil patches.
   - some reported around:
     - {{waypoint -1895, -19, -139}}
     - {{waypoint -2001, -19, -287}}
1. Return to [[Windstalker Village]] and speak with:
   - [[Angelina McQuibble]] {{waypoint -1820, -37, -529}}
   - [[Bobby McQuibble]] {{waypoint -1935, -36, -614}}
   - [[Old Man McQuibble]] {{waypoint -1897, -36, -608}}
   - [[Merle Trevine]] {{waypoint -1933, -36, -601}}
1. Go back and speak with Ponchy Shorttoe. Tell him that you will, “go talk to [[Professor Heribert]].”
1. Speak to [[Professor Heribert]] {{waypoint -1923, -36, -610}}

## Rewards

- at least 30s 99c
- [[Slice of pumpkin pie]]
