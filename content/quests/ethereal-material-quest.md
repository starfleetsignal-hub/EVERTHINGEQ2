## Steps

1. The churning obol around the Tourbillion can be used to make strong, arcane weapon and armor.
   - Hunt [[A churning obol|churning obol]] (8)
1. I am to deliver the chunks of arcane obol to Warren Redhammer, the Cardin Ward smithy.
   - Deliver the chunks of arcane obol to [[Warren Redhammer]] {{waypoint -374, 14, 17}}
1. I should return to General Furtog Ogrebane, in the Wayward town of [[Cardin Ward]].
   - Return to [[Furtog Ogrebane|General Furtog Ogrebane]] {{waypoint -455, 10, 14}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
