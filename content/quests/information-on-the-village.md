## Notes

- This quest begins by hailing Ulgofar Wisme, a ratonga merchant inside a building in the Outsider's Landing section of the Village of Shin.
- You must be at least level 55 to start this quest.
- You are required to visit a number of locations around the zone. While the quest text says "examine", just moving into the general area will give you the update.

## Steps

The entire quest involves wandering round the village to the waypoints listed below.  You do not need to click on the items to get an update, you just need to stand near them.

- Examine an urn beside the Nurwin home on the Courtyard of Hope. {{waypoint -1, 16, -90}}
- Examine the small urn outside the storage house near Dragon Turtle's Footstep. {{waypoint -19, 11, -74}}
- Examine the container near the pigsty. {{waypoint -47, 2, -30}}
- Examine one of the urns near the fisher's hut by Mara Beach. {{waypoint -2, 0, 1}}
- Examine the pallet of sacks near the House of Augren. {{waypoint 41, 8, -46}}
- Examine the urn inside the home of Arellia Augren. {{waypoint 75, 8, -71}}
- Examine a covered barrel in the courtyard of the House of Ventur. {{waypoint 7, 16, -78}}
- Examine the stacked wooden container inside the Nurwin house on the Courtyard of Hope. {{waypoint -12, 14, -102}}
- Examine a small sack in the the supply hut beside the Middle Fields. {{waypoint -46, 12, -112}}
- Examine canvas sacks in the House of Nurwin. {{waypoint -170, 7, -173}}
- Return to [[Ulgofar Wisme]] to claim your reward.
