## Materials

- 4 [[Luminous Kindling]]
- 2 [[Milk]]
- 5 [[White peach]]

## Steps

![Rivervale Jumberry](images/RivervaleJumberry.png)

1. Go to [[Rivervale]] to obtain materials for a dessert.
   - Harvest 5 [[White peach|white peaches]].
     - *This will auto-update if you have the peaches in your bags.*
   - Gather 5 [[Rivervale Jumberries]] near the [[The Fools Gold|Fools Gold Tavern]] {{waypoint -53.52, -18.01, -30.04}}
1. Create [[Peach-Berry Sour Cream Pie]].
   - Use the [[Portable Cooking Set (no-trade)|portable cooking set]] from the first Quest.
   - **BE CAREFUL CRAFTING!** Although there are 3 Jumberry bushes available for harvesting, once you have plucked the five berries you need, you CANNOT HARVEST again (even though they still 'sparkle'.)  So, if you fail to make the pie, you will need to travel back to Jadrek, delete your quest, re-take it and then pick more berries. Make sure you still have milk and peaches and kindling :-)
1. Speak to [[Jadrek Woolfe]] back in [[Everfrost]] {{waypoint 222.13, -19.23, 49.59}}.

## Rewards

At least 5g 12s

- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
