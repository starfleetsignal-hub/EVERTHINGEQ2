## Steps

1. Slay [[Vitaela]] at {{waypoint -27, 3, 297}} in Charnel Scar after weakening him with [[Porlos' Fury]]
   - *the potion you get from The Dragon Sage. The potion stifles you for 15seconds (and **the Stifle rune doesn't block**) but auto attack still works.*
1. Return to [[The Dragon Sage]].
