Charles Q Tinkersmith is ready for me to assemble the prototype of the powered viewfinder. He seems sure that this will establish the reputation of his new research facility.

## Steps

1. Assemble the [[Prototype Viewfinder]].
   - *There is a work bench right beside [[Charles Q Tinkersmith|Tinkersmith]].*
   - *Note: you received the [[Powered Viewfinder Prototype Blueprint]] and the various ingredients you've assembled from [[Charles Q Tinkersmith|Tinkersmith]] when you started the quest*
1. Return to [[Charles Q Tinkersmith|Tinkersmith]].
1. Deliver the viewfinder to [[Tobon Blingden]], the Grand Artificer {{waypoint -52.09, 5.35, 248.98}}.
   - When you're looking for the Grand Artificer, you will be ambushed by **a gnemlin thug**.
1. Return to [[Charles Q Tinkersmith|Tinkersmith]] and let him know what happened.

## Needed Materials

- [[Thaumic Coal]] (5)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13200 status
- +1,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +5,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- [[Deepforge Delver]]
