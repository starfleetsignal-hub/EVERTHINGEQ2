*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Head into the sewers all the way down to [[Edgewater Drains]].
1. Pick up the scroll on the north stairs near the bottom level {{waypoint 82, 11, -135}}
   - The scroll is next to a bedroll and wine bottle in a corner.
   - Will be offered to examine the scroll and start next quest, [[A Delivery for Hucklethin Gorsefoot]].
