## Steps

1. Enter the [[Vex Thal: Throne of Oscuris]] in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] at {{waypoint 8.33, 168.63, 557.07}}.
   - This appears to be a solo (2 player) instance, our four person group was instanced into the same instance, but 2 were kicked out.
1. Defeat [[Gatekeeper Skalha Raa]] in the entry area
1. Enter the now active portal at {{waypoint -0.42, 203.10, 0.03}} (glowing ball in the air).
1. Enter the next portal at {{waypoint -670.01, 174.21, -731.27}} (no need to kill trash on way to portal)
1. Defeat [[Eom Va Keta]] at {{waypoint 746.61, 221.29, -805.58}}  (you must kill all trash to spawn named before portal)
1. Enter the now activated portal at {{waypoint 731.75, 221.90, -785.99}}
1. Defeat [[Emperor Ssraeshza]]
   1. Equip the [[Shadowbane Phantastic Gaffi]] from your inventory (given by Nabihan at start of quest)
   1. Fight [[Emperor Ssraeshza]] up to 40% and then wait for all his dialog to conclude
   1. Speak to [[Head Scholar Nabihan]] and receive [[Nabihan's Soul Gem]]
   1. Use [[Nabihan's Soul Gem]] on [[Emperor Ssraeshza]] which makes it unattackable.
   1. right-click [[Emperor Ssraeshza]] and remove the Staff.
1. Inspect [[Staff of the Sacrum Va]] and select "peer into the orb at the head of the staff".
1. [[Luclin (Goddess)|Luclin]], Mistress of Secrets appears and speaks to [[Head Scholar Nabihan]]  which transforms into [[Opal Darkbriar]].
1. Speak to [[Luclin (Goddess)|Luclin]]
1. Return [[City of Shar Vahl]]. You can use the portal at {{waypoint -0.75, 334.10, -36.41}} to return to [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]].
   1. **be sure to re-summon your mount**
1. Speak to [[Al'Kabor]] at {{waypoint -160.19, 31.29, -270.86}}
