## Steps

1. Kill 10 guardians
   - ([[A clay guardian]], [[An incomplete guardian]], [[A hulking clay guardian]]) - large amounts can be found in the Clay Guardian Room at {{waypoint 163, 8, 6}}
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 10 guardians.
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 10 guardians.
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 10 loam or clay goos.
   - [[A living clay]], [[A loam cyst]], [[A loam blister]], [[A loam boil]] all update, they're found throughout the cavern half of the zone.
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 10 loam or clay goos.
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 10 loam or clay goos.
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 10 skeletal Varsoon inhabitants.
   - [[A Varsoon guard]], [[A servant of Varsoon]], etc... large groups of skeletons can be found in the two jails at {{waypoint -248, 8, -159}} and {{waypoint -235, 0, 60}}
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 10 skeletal Varsoon inhabitants.
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 10 skeletal Varsoon inhabitants.
1. Examine the book.
