## Notes

- This is one of the daily (18 hour timer) repeatable missions offered after finishing [[Rage of Cthurath Crafting Timeline]].
- Bring 30 x Formless Sandpaper.

## Steps

1. Harvest in The Unknown:
   - 30 x Kauri Wood
   - 18 x Detritus Root
   - 12 x Oblivion Ore
1. Craft 6 x [[Void Voice Vacuum]] - each one needs 5 x Kauri Wood, 3 x Detritus Root, 2 x Oblivion Ore and 5 x Formless Sandpaper.
   - use Woodworking Table or Varig's Forge multi station next to Taslana
1. Quest auto completes after last crafting combine
