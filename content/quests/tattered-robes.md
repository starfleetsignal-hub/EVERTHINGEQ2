## Steps

1. Kill [[Soul parasite|soul parasites]] for 5 soul beads. Found throughout the instance. One location is {{waypoint 15, -23, 32}}
   - [[An Obdurate Parasite]] can also update this
1. Kill [[Larval feeder|larval feeders]] for 3 lichen-infused filaments. These can be found on the first level anywhere there is water.
1. Kill mist grinnin in The Tombs of Night for 12 patches of woven moss.
