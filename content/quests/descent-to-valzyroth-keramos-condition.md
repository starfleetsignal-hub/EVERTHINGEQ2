## Steps

1. Enter Keramos Caverns: Dunamis Fens
   - Portal to Keramos Caverns from Val'Zyroth Ness is at {{waypoint -925.76, -55.91, 353.12}}. Zone inside to find Dunamis Fens at {{waypoint 12.61, 183.04, -26.94}}
1. Dispatch 4 Thexians guarding the entrance
1. Explore Dunamis Fens to reveal Thexian intentions
1. Examine the gnemlin-tinkered equipment and destroy it {{waypoint -371.37, 162.52, 165.80}}
   - Dispose of 9 Thexian occultists
   - Destroy 12 dunamis collectors. Some possible locations:
   - {{waypoint -298.15, 161.92, 65.26}}
   - {{waypoint -231.09, 162.01, 56.92}}
   - {{waypoint -488.93, 162.41, -86.91}}
   - {{waypoint -412.91, 162.51, 190.81}}
   - {{waypoint -407.33, 162.32, 227.91}}
   - {{waypoint -193.99, 201.06, 283.77}}
   - {{waypoint -192.61, 191.61, 205.76}}
   - {{waypoint -230.38, 195.21, 112.59}}
   - {{waypoint -320.01, 208.27, 158.22}}
   - {{waypoint -393.38, 213.35, 1.09}}
   - {{waypoint -379.23, 162.75, -103.99}}
   - {{waypoint -382.63, 162.33, -123.92}}
1. Dispose of one more Thexian occultist
   - a Thexian master occultist {{waypoint -433.72, 267.54, 38.21}} Clear the zone to drop his bubble.
1. Return to [[Diban Quirkeye]]
1. Enter [[Keramos Caverns: Artisan's Rivel]] to - Portal to [[Keramos Caverns]] {{waypoint -925.76, -55.91, 353.12}}
   - Portal inside to [[Keramos Caverns: Artisan's Rivel]] {{waypoint 167.52, 191.37, -26.31}}
1. See what the Thexians are doing {{waypoint 491.37, 228.31, 319.47}}
   - Destroy 7 Thexian irontollers
1. What were the Thexian irontollers harvesting the clay for? {{waypoint 574.47, 236.40, 225.28}}
   - Kill 4 Thexian occultists
   - Destroy 3 Teir'Dal Cosgrove clay golems
1. Slay the Thexian who escaped Dunamis Fens {{waypoint 341.40, 167.01, 4.67}}
1. Return to [[Diban Quirkeye]].
