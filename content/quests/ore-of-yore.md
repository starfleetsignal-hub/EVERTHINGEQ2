## Steps

1. Finish the conversation with [[Dror Ironstein]]
1. Collect an ore sample from within the [[Underfoot Cathedral]] in [[Kaladim]].
   - Entrance to Kaladim is at {{waypoint -168, 165, -565}}
   - ![Great Hammer of the Underfoot (ore sample)](images/Great_Hammer_of_the_Underfoot%28ore_sample%29.jpg)Note: You will need a flying mount to reach the hammer at {{waypoint 25.09, 50.09, -545.45}}. It can be clicked while flying.
1. Return to Dror.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13200 status
