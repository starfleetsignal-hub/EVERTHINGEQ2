## Steps

1. Collect two souvenirs:
   1. ![Jungle souvenir](images/Jungle_souvenir.png)Travel to the [[The Eidolon Jungle]] around the Fearcreep (using a [[Drinalian Whisper Column]], or the portal in the [[The Feerrott]] at {{waypoint -86, 14, 177}}) and find a book souvenir at about {{waypoint -1308, 218, 653}} or {{waypoint -1356, 218, 636}}
   1. Travel to the [[Obol Plains]] and find a bush at about {{waypoint 768, 85, 81}} , {{waypoint 781, 79, 56}} or {{waypoint 779, 81, -35}} or on the other side of the stairs.
      - ![Obol souvenir](images/Obol_souvenir.png)*The bush has a sparkling effect.*
1. Return to [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]].
