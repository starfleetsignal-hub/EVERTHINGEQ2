## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[A snow leopard|snow leopards]] in [[Hollowfrost Grove]]. {{waypoint -436, 10, -333}}
1. Click the bucket in front of [[Trapper Stu Wart]] at [[Snowsworn's Hunter's Outpost]] to poison the leopard hides. {{waypoint -272, 4, -248}}
1. Right-click the 5 [[Snow Leopard Carcass]] that are in your inventory. You must move a bit out of the camp to do so.
1. Return to Trapper Stu Wart.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 20s 65c
