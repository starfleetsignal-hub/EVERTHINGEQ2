## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Tanned Toolbelt for my apprentice.
   - Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Tanned Toolbelt]]
   - Craft [[A Sturdy Tanned Toolbelt]] on a [[Loom]] using the following materials and fuel:
     - 2 [[Tanned leather pelt|tanned leather pelts]]
     - 4 [[Glowing Filament]]
1. I need to take this toolbelt back to my apprentice.
