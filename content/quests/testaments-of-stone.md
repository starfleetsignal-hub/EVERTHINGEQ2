## Steps

1. Find four items [[Brell Serilis]] needs to help inspire the dwarven race to reclaim [[Kaladim]]:
   - Go to Kaladim:
     - *Tooth of Brell*: Slay [[Crackbak the Scavenger]] in [[Heroes Hold]]. {{waypoint 140, 20, -100}}
     - *Tankard of Kazan*: Behind the throne in [[Stormhammer Keep]] {{waypoint -215, 45, -85}}. Then fill the tankard in the river at {{waypoint -141, 44, -236}}.
     - *Tome of Stone*: Swim underwater beneath the waterfall then through an underwater entrance at {{waypoint -155, 40, -200}}. The tome is lying on a rock in [[The Hidden Nest]]. {{waypoint -210, 55, -190}}
   - Go to [[Mistmoore Catacombs]]:
     - *Candlelight Cleaver*: Climb wall at {{waypoint -13, -160, -32}}, then climb wall at {{waypoint -128, -124, -54}} and examine a paladin helm at {{waypoint -70, -120, -80}} to spawn and kill [[A vampiric dwarf paladin]].
1. Return to Bronlor Stormhammer.

## Rewards

- +7500 faction with **Brell Serilis**
- [[Bronlor's Altar of Brell Serilis]]
