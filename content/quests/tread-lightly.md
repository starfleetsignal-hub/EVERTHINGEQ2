## Steps

[[Zafirah Aamira]] has asked that you go to the council chambers and research any dealings with the strange vermin. Make sure Zafirah is not duplicating research that has already been conducted.
![research notes on the table](images/Tread_Lightly_quest_-_research_notes.jpg)

1. Read the research notes about the vermin, located on the table next to [[Ishaq Al'Nair]] {{waypoint 1858, -293, 3471}}.
1. Return to [[Zafirah Aamira]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
