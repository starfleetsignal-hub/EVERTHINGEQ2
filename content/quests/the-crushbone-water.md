## Steps

1. Get [[Empty Jug]] from Tuathil.
1. Right click to use the jug to get a sample from the pond in [[Crushbone Territory]] at {{waypoint -808, 83, -82}}. You will receive a [[Filled Jug]] to replace the empty one.
1. Return to [[Tuathil Laeds]] with the water.

## Rewards

- At least 28s 54c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Archon's Pauldrons (Version 2)]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Shoulderpads]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Shoulderpads]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Elder's Shoulderpads (Version 2)]] (All Shamans)
  - [[Enlightened Shoulderpads]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Pauldrons (Version 2)]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Shade's Shoulderpads (Version 2)]] (All Scouts)
