## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Bertoxxulous has asked me to assist in gathering plaguebound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with container to assist with collecting the plaguebound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Plaguebound Energy Reserves|plaguebound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect energy by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to [[Herald of the Plaguelord]] {{waypoint -1157.19, -33.96, 525.38}} in [[Antonica]]
