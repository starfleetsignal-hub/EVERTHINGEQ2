## Steps

1. Gather 8 planks of wood from the crates lying around the Thurgadin Docks
   - {{waypoint 1259, -540, 443}}
   - {{waypoint 1293, -540, 486}}
   - {{waypoint 1235, -540, 345}}
   - {{waypoint 1255, -540, 316}}
   - {{waypoint 1206, -540, 403}}
   - Attack the crates to break them, then harvest the planks.  Each crate drops 2 planks. Respawn is relatively fast and you can harvest them repeatedly.
1. Return to Captain Bin
1. Obtain a Potion of Icy Strength from the Rime casters in Storm Gorge
   - Kill any of the Rime nova-vicar or scholarly flurryburst types. You may need to kill several before getting the update.
1. Return to Captain Bin
1. Obtain a "glue substance" from the wholesaler.
   - Buy a [[Glue Substance]] from [[Des Glacierwell]] {{waypoint 1300, -480, 568}} up the elevator on the right side; will cost you 48s.
1. Return to Captain Bin

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
