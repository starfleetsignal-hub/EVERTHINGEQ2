The Quellithulians believe that other-planar creatures are disrupting the teleportation network, but are doing it outside of the bounds of normal reality. I must find these spaces within spaces, and destroy the planar intruders from within them.

## Starting the Quest

This quest remains the same regardless of the zone you start in. The only difference is the person you speak with to start the quest:

- Talk to [[Ilania At'emin]] in [[Antonica]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (Ant)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -2077, 13, -635}}
- Talk to [[Tal'il Amhenti]] in [[The Commonlands]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (CL)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -16, -48, -398}}
- Talk to [[Elawri Nazzuit]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (TS)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint 1184, -26, 151}}
- Talk to [[Alzir Y'maurii]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (Nek)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -352, 2, -636}}
- Talk to [[Valiin Sihami]] in [[Enchanted Lands]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (EL)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -402, 2, 62}}
- Talk to [[Ultann Zawrii]] in [[The Feerrott]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (Feer)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -54, 13, 178}}

## Steps

1. Clear out the teleportation pockets of planar invaders
   1. Locate and zone into [[An Ethereal Rift]]
   1. Kill 3 groups of 4 mobs inside
1. Speak to any Portal Warden to claim your reward.

## Rewards

- 5 [[Quellithulian Token of Appreciation]]

## Notes

You can do these solo.  However, if you bring a group, you can spawn various nameds when you kill all the groups and they give MASSIVE AA as well as a treasure chest with 5 [[Quellithulian Token of Appreciation]] as well.

- Various Named Spawn when done as a group:
  - [[Guardian of Hate]]
  - [[Guardian of Flame]]
  - [[Guardian of Justice]]
  - [[Guardian of Storms]]
