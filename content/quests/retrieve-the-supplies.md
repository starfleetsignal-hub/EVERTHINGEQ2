## Steps

1. Recover 5 supplies (common update) by hunting [[A mist grinnin|mist grinnins]] {{waypoint -101.52, -0.28, -899.05}}
   - Just a little north of [[D'Abth Bridge]] on the western shore, mist grinnin savages and mist grinnin guardians don't count. The map hint is inaccurate.
1. Receive [[A bag of recovered provisions]] once all the supplies are recovered.
1. Return to [[Sergeant Cornelius]].

## Rewards

- 2 [[Old spotted apples]]
- At least 1s 59c
- +150 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
