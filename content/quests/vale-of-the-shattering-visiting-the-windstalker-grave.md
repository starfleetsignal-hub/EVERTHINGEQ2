*This is part 3 of a 3-part series that gives access to the [[Shattered Vale]]*

## Steps

- Travel to Holly Windstalker's grave in Antonica near [[Windstalker Village]]  {{waypoint -1825, -3, -624}}
- Kill the Bloodsabers that attack you - four [[A Bloodsaber brigand|Bloodsaber brigands]] and [[A Bloodsaber cult leader]]
- Return to Rakk in The Elddar Grove, Qeynos Province District

## Reward

- At least 27s 29c
- [[Bracelet of howling winds]]
