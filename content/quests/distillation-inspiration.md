## Steps

1. Speak to [[Dinu]] {{waypoint 1996, -298, 3345}} in [[Paineel]].
1. Take the [[Theoretical Construction Notes For A High Efficiency Distillation Device]] recipe from a book on the table and scribe it.
1. Go to [[Steamfont Mountains]], and talk to [[Gnort Fizzlebottom]] in Gnomeland Security {{waypoint -585, 174, 966}} to buy a [[Resonance distillationificator]] for 10g 80s.
1. Craft the [[High efficiency distillation device]]. This is a level 80 recipe which requires a Work Bench and the following materials:
   - 1 [[Resonance distillationificator]]
   - 2 [[Titanium ore]]
   - 2 [[Sumac lumber]]
   - 1 [[Ethereal coal]]
1. Return to [[Alchemist Janicia]] in Rivervale.

## Notes

- You must be a level 85+ [[Alchemist]] to begin this quest.
- Purchase a [[Mission Stone of Steamfont]] from [[Koros Splinterlimb]] in [[Mara]] for fast travel to Steamfont.
