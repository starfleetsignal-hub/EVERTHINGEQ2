1. Steal *frostruned weapons* from chests in [[Ru'Kaus]], at {{waypoint 169, 80, 286}} and {{waypoint 149, 74, 249}}.
1. Return to [[Beth Littlefort]].
   - You will have to wait around one minute for Beth Littlefort to offer you the following quest.
