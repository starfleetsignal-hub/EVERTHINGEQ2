## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- The quest can be deleted immediately and re-obtained if you wish to have to kill fewer to complete.

## Steps

1. Kill 10 - 15 [[A Scaleborn warrior|Scaleborn warriors]] {{waypoint -86, 137, -269}} and {{waypoint 84, 164, -275}} in the [[Tenebrous Tangle]].
1. Return to the [[Hidden Refuge]] and speak to [[Armorer Gunbog]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Fist Etched Wand]]
- Coin
- good faction with **The Lost Children of Marr**
