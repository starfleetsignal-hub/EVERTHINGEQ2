## Steps

1. Speak with [[Frau Choraline Lent]] {{waypoint -1128.28, -10.85, -464.36}}
1. Speak with [[Elder Wadaco]] {{waypoint -215.04, 87.08, -1026.97}}
1. Speak with [[Kloiq]] {{waypoint -221.00, 87.57, -1029.75}}
1. I must procure a tontu idol.
   - [[Kloiq]] is a researcher now and will have a quest to reduce the time on his research. Idol of Friendship ~3 days.
   - To reduce his time, complete subquest:
   - [[Val'Zyroth Ness: Requisition 371]]
   - [[Val'Zyroth Ness: Requisition 374]]
1. Return to [[Kloiq]] when his research timer has completed.
1. Pick up the idol from the table next to Kloiq.
1. Tell [[Elder Wadaco]] of the idol.
