## Steps

1. Scribe [[Building A Better Rat Trap]].
1. Craft a [[Rat Trap]]. (You can find a woodworking table at {{waypoint -314, 177, -48}} )
   - Materials needed to craft
     - 1 Maple Lumber
     - 1 Iron Cluster
     - 1 Tanned leather
     - 2 Glowing Sandpaper
1. Place the trap on the green glowy at {{waypoint -154, 149, 30}} in the Hopson abode.
1. Return to [[Envoy Tami Swifthammer|Tami]].
