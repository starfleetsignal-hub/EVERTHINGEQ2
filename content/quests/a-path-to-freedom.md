## Steps

1. Lead to Stairway of Zek area and clean most mobs on the stairs as they come aggro when you activate the teleporter.

1. Once you speak to [[Duncan Peadar]] at {{waypoint 1083, -34, -176}} you can activate the teleporter and see Thrael'Gorr prisoners rushing it to escape.

## Rewards

- Title: [[Emancipator of Thrael'Gorr]]
- At least 1p 50g
- +3.000 faction with **Clan Thrael'Gorr**
- [[Thrael'Gorr Hero's Chausses]]

## Notes

Once the teleporter has been activated, you will be rushed by a group of giants.
