## Steps

1. I should place traps around the pilgrim camp.
   - I need to place 5 spike traps in  the places indicated by the golden glows.
1. I should bring forth a manifestation of the Avatar of Cazic-Thule.
   - Speak to [[Neffarus Rissner]].
   - I should pour the [[Cazic-Thule Illusion Potion]] near the Sathirian Camp {{waypoint 27, -1, 137}}.
   - Speak to [[Irkksom Svetann]].
   - [Note: Return to [[Admiral Marc Grenich]] in [[Qeynos Harbor]] or [[Livia Montelius]] in [[East Freeport]] to finish the quest-line.]
