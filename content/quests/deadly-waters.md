## Steps

1. Obtain the following items from sea creatures in the waters near [[Danak Shipyards]]: {{waypoint -214, -74, 1582}} or {{waypoint 120, -45, 1391}}
   - 5 shark skins from [[A terrorspine shark|terrorspine sharks]]
   - 5 swordfish skins from [[A runewake impaler|runewake impalers]]
1. Return to [[Supply Clerk Linxx]].

## Rewards

- +150 faction with **Legion of Danak**
- At least 11g 5s 35c
- [[Daredevil Leggings]]
