## Steps

1. Gather water and a Sea life
   - Test the water around Lodizal *(Anywhere near the giant turtle, around {{waypoint -1649, -544, -2395}})*
     - *While in the water around Lodizal use the [[Empty Frosty Vial]] Laput gave you.*
   - Collect samples of sea life from Iceclad Ocean
     - *Kill any of the icy barracudas or ice urchins - the specific type doesn't matter*
1. Return to Laput

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
