## Steps

1. Talk to [[Elder Kionn]] {{waypoint -12, -632, -727}}.
1. Right click on the stone in the middle of the circle and join ritual.
1. Wait for the dialogue to finish to get the next update (or right click stone and leave ritual).
1. Right click on it again to leave the ritual and return to the zone.
1. Go to [[Silnas]] {{waypoint -52, -626, -617}} for your reward and to continue the questline.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
