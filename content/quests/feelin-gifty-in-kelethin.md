## Steps

1. Complete the following steps in no particular order:
   - Speak with [[Linst Tincobble]] in [[Kelethin]], outside the [[Silver Leaf Inn]] {{waypoint 397, 90, 321}}
   - Speak with [[Talis Eventide]] in Kelethin, on the second floor of the [[House of Falling Stars]] {{waypoint 101, 95, 491}}
   - Speak with [[Paavali Dewshine]] in [[Greater Faydark]], at the [[Sapling Spur Outpost]] {{waypoint -117, 12, -123}}
   - Speak with [[Calda Terrokbane]] in Greater Faydark, at the [[Spire Shadow Outpost]] {{waypoint -373, 14, 175}}
   - Speak with [[Etholiya Highvalor]] in Kelethin, on the second floor of [[Jysolin's Pub]] {{waypoint 197, 114, 335}}
   - Speak with [[Namarie Aurorabloom]] in Kelethin, on the same platform as [[Whittier]], underneath the upper platform near the trunk {{waypoint 419, 88, 209}}
1. Return to G'shugaplum Gigglegibber. Make sure to have six open slots in your inventory!
1. Hand out the gifts:
   - **WARNING: You must give the correct gift to the correct person, or you will have to start the entire quest over!**
   - Deliver the **[[Beard trimming kit]]** to Linst Tincobble {{waypoint 397, 90, 321}}
   - Deliver the **[[Tunare cameo]]** to Talis Eventide {{waypoint 101, 95, 491}}
   - Deliver the **[[Feir'Dal tattoo kit]]** to Paavali Dewshine {{waypoint -117, 12, -123}}
   - Deliver the **[[Faydrake egg]]** to Calda Terrokbane {{waypoint -373, 14, 175}}
   - Deliver the **[[Bowl of Gigglegibber coleslaw]]** to Etholiya Highvalor {{waypoint 197, 114, 335}}
   - Deliver the **[[Amygdalan mask]]** to Namarie Aurorabloom {{waypoint 419, 88, 209}}
1. Return to G'Shugahplum Gigglegibber.

## Rewards

- The ability to set different kinds of lights on your [[Frostfell Tree]].
