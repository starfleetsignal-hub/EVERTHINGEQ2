## Steps

1. Return to the Ritual Room in [[Zimara Breadth]].  Felfeather Ritual Room: Chromatic Chamber at {{waypoint -692, 219, -612}}
1. Speak to Erki and Irko Felfeather inside the Ritual Room.
1. Defeat [Aurous the Gilded](https://u.eq2wire.com/actloot/zone_npc/1331/57935/false/0/0). This is a Heroic I instance. Do this with a full group, or he will beat your face.
1. Wait around and listen to Penkri Felfeather speak to his two sons. This completes the quest.

## Rewards

- unlocks the [[Of Duty and Fate: The Imprint of Balance (Achievement)]]
