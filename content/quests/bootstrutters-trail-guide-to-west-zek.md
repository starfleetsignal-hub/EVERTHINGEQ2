## Steps

Complete the following steps in no particular order:

- Visit [[Warship Dock]] {{waypoint 475, -41, 281}}
- Visit [[Pinewhisper Lookout]], a tower in the [[Orc Encampment]] just east of [[Warship Dock]] {{waypoint 302, -37, 284}}
- Visit [[Splintered Plain|The Splintered Plain]], the area between the [[Orc Encampment]] and [[Three Toes Valley]] {{waypoint 637, -16, 18}}
- Visit [[Shadowed Shrine|The Shadowed Shrine]], inside the cave in the western area of [[Three Toes Valley]] {{waypoint 723, -17, -298}}
- Visit [[Marthank Lookout]], a tower in [[Three Toes Valley]], just west of [[Three Toe's Watering Hole]] {{waypoint 448, -20, -407}}
- Visit [[Three Toe's Watering Hole|Three Toes Lake]] in the eastern area of [[Three Toes Valley]] {{waypoint 273, -21, -350}}
