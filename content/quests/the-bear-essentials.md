## Steps

1. Craft 4 [[Carved Bear Charm|Carved Bear Charms]] at a Woodworking Table.
   - Scribe the [[Book of Basic Bear Charms]] recipe.
   - In addition to the Claws and Fangs supplied you will need:
     - 4 [[Severed ash]]
     - 12 [[Sparkling Sandpaper]] (level 20 - 29)
1. I should give the bear charms to Cordelia.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 7s
- +2500 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Gray Mustang Pack Horse (Mount)]]
