## Description

I have been tasked with clearing out The Misty Dead area of Vasty Deep: The Conservatory. I must enter The Conservatory and kill the bulb horrors and degenerated guardians found within The Misty Dead.

## Steps

1. Clear out [[The Misty Dead]] area in [[Vasty Deep: The Conservatory]]
   - Kill three [[A bulb horror|bulb horrors]]
   - Kill three [[A degenerated guardian|degenerated guardians]]
1. Return to [[Alia]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 80g
- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
