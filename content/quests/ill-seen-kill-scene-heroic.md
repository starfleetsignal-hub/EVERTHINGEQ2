## Description

*The denizen's of the Temple of the Ill-Seen must be destroyed to protect the city of Maldura and her citizens.*

## Zone

Defeat targets within [[Kralet Penumbra: Temple of the Ill-Seen (Heroic)]]

## Steps

- Annihilate the annihilator
  - [[Tlat-a-tor (Heroic)|Tlat-a-tor]]
- Banish the lurid beast
  - [[Grelikor (Heroic)|Grelikor]]
- Defeat the holder of wisdom
  - [[Klandoth (Heroic)|Klandoth]]
- Kill the keeper
  - [[Yagg-kythor (Heroic)|Yagg-kythor]]
- End the ever rending
  - [[Xoth, the Ever Rending (Heroic)|Xoth, the Ever Rending]]
