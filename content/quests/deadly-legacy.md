## Steps

1. In [[Temple Street: Vault of Necromechanica]], collect 5 slates (sparkling tombstones): (Note: you may have to zone out then back in to make the slates re-appear to collect them all)
   - {{waypoint 7, 3, 77}}
   - {{waypoint -3, 3, 83}}
   - {{waypoint -7, 3, 107}}
   - {{waypoint -15, 3, 86}}
   - {{waypoint -15, 3, 70}}
   - {{waypoint 44, 3, 66}}
1. Speak to Sneel in North Freeport
1. Speak with frightened ratonga in Freeport:
   - [[Cles]] in the Academy {{waypoint 29, -7, -120}}
   - [[Feepsin]] on the top floor of the Coalition of Tradesfolke in West Freeport {{waypoint 43, -12, 91}}
   - [[Salla]] on the South Freeport dock  {{waypoint -260, -58, 143}}
1. Speak to Sneel
1. Kill 3 groups of false roekillik. Approach the spot to spawn a group of 3 (double down, grouped):
   - Around the right side behind [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] {{waypoint 28.78, -7.94, -145.18}}
   - Around the right side behind [[Temple of War]] {{waypoint -119, -8, -141}}
   - Next to 4 Integrity Road {{waypoint -88.48, -33.51, -316.35}}
   - behind [[The Freeport Militia House]] {{waypoint 146, -16, 167}}
   - behind [[Champion's Coliseum]] {{waypoint 290, -3, -17}}
1. Speak to Sneel
