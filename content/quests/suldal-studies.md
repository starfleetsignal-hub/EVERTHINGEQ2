## Steps

1. Do the following in no particular order, all steps take place around the [[The Bay of Anuk|Bay of Anuk]] {{waypoint -650, -208, -1200}}:
   - Kill 4 [[A Sul'Dal bladefury|Sul'Dal bladefuries]]
   - Kill 4 [[A Sul'Dal foci|Sul'Dal foci]]
   - Visit the [[The Silent City (POI)|entrance to the Silent City]]. {{waypoint -945, -229, -1048}}
     - You can take a [[Magic Carpet|magic carpet]] to this location.
1. Return to [[Aliyyah the Embalmer]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Spectral Assassin]] - house pet and arena champion.
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Amazing Gray Wand]] (All Mages)
  - [[Bow of the Blades Archer]] (All Scouts except Beastlord)
  - [[Crimson Desert Pouch]] ([[Beastlord]], [[Bruiser]], [[Monk]])
  - [[Dunewalker's Staff]] (???)
  - [[Unfalling Longbow]] (Plate Fighters)
