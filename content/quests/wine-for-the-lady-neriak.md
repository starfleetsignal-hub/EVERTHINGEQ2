## Journal Entry

I have been hired to get a helm for Blat Berisen from Frethe Zizlop. Frethe wants a deck of cards from Zoe Hertzflip. Zoe seeks Barb Islenet's pink goggles. Barb is looking to get a bottle of wine back from Abner Sidlet. This adventure is getting longer and longer!

## Steps

After going to Zoe Hertzflip head to speak to Barb Islenet at {{waypoint 81.70, 23.78, 138.45}}

She will tell you to find Abner Siblet.

Go to Abner Siblet {{waypoint -155.97, 3.00, 243.17}} to complete and receive the quest [[Fribles's Fate]].
