## Steps

1. Go to [[The Fools Gold]]. {{waypoint -70, -17, 2}}
1. Hail [[Darly Brookwaters]] in the [[The Fools Gold]]. {{waypoint -78.5, -14, -10}}
1. Hail [[Professor Dandle Farfeather]] in the [[The Fools Gold]]. {{waypoint -72.5, -14.5, -6}}
1. Take the gate to the [[Enchanted Lands]] for the next quest update.
1. Hail [[Deputy Stoutgut]] in [[Enchanted Lands]] at [[The Runnyeye Ward]] {{waypoint 169.77, -1.38, -483.51}}
1. Slay 10 [[A lamia carver|lamia carvers]] in [[Enchanted Lands]] at {{waypoint -338, 23, -915}}. There are also plenty of lamia carvers along the Northern coast. {{waypoint -282, -2, -1196}}
1. Slay 7 [[A warped nymph|warped nymphs]] in [[Enchanted Lands]] {{waypoint -471.18, 15.38, -741.62}}
1. Read the book to obtain your reward.
