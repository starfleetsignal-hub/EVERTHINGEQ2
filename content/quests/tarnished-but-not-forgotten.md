## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
- Quest starter and updates needed for it have a chance to drop from any mob in [[Gerion, Realm of Conquest (Solo)]]. It may require to resetting and running the zone again if you don't get all updates.

## Steps

*After finding the armored torso and arms, broken off a tarnished statue of a male figure, I am on the lookout for other pieces...*

1. Search for a tarnished statue legs.
1. Search for a tarnished statue head.
1. Search for a tarnished statue sword
1. Restore the statue to its pedestal in Gerion, Hold of D'Lere - zone out to Gerion, Hold of D'Lere and click on pedestal at {{waypoint 91, 0, -52}}

## Rewards

- [[Tarnished Statue of the Overlord]]
