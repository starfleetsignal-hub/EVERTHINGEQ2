## Steps

1. Find a descendent of Orisin Deepwell. Talk to [[Olmyr Deepwell]] in the Hall of Conviction of Teren's Grasp in [[Kylong Plains]] at {{waypoint 1987, 487, -695}}.
1. Find Orisin's Tomb in [[Kaladim]]. You will find the tomb in the Underfoot Cathedral at {{waypoint 0.79, 28, -410}}.
   - You are not the first to arrive! Neragul and his minions are already there. He leaves quickly, but a group of 3 heroic aerakyns level 103 will spawn and attack you. Kill them.
1. Search the Tomb of Orisin Deepwell. Click the tomb where Neragul was previously standing.
1. Track down Neragul, and retrieve the key!
   - Kill [[Neragul]] in his normal location in [[Temple of Veeshan: Halls of the Betrayer (Heroic)|Halls of the Betrayer (Heroic)]], you will receive a letter. Examine the letter. Killing Neragul in the Advanced Solo instance WILL NOT give the update/letter.
1. Find Neragul's Cache: in the middle room where [[Grendish the Betrayer]] is located. Kill room as normal and have Grendish fly off.  Near Grendish's balcony, there is a square wall decoration on the south wall ABOVE the platform. The square has a gold frame, blue/green glowing effects in the middle at {{waypoint 1444.05, 384.20, 387.79}}, you will need a flying mount to fly up to it, click on the glowing square for the cache.
1. Return to [[Loremaster Dorondal]] at {{waypoint 472.41, -208.32, 275.22}} in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain|Thurgadin]]
