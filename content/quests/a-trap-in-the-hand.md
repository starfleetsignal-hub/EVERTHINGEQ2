## Steps

1. Place traps around the village on top of the fragments of green glowing stones. Just go around the natural rock walls surrounding the village and you will find them all.
   - Trap 1 is located near {{waypoint 1314, 45, 2048}}. You may need to look behind yourself and the rock to see this one. ![Place your trap here ...](images/A_Trap_in_the_Hand_quest_-_trap_position.jpg)
   - Trap 2 is located near {{waypoint 1383, 44, 2056}}
   - Trap 3 is located near {{waypoint 1401, 46, 2177}}
   - Trap 4 is located near {{waypoint 1245, 45, 2149}}
   - Trap 5 is located near {{waypoint 1276, 44, 2207}}
1. Return to [[Shaman Yityen]].

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +2,000 faction with **Kerra Isle**
