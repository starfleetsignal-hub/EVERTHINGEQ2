## Steps

1. You must travel to [[Bathezid's Watch]] in [[Fens of Nathsar]] and speak with [[Danelak Hosfoak]] for the reward. *This is the only step in the quest.*

  - [[Cloak of the Master Armorer]]
  - [[Cloak of the Master Carpenter]]
  - [[Cloak of the Master Jeweler]]
  - [[Cloak of the Master Provisioner]]
  - [[Cloak of the Master Sage]]
  - [[Cloak of the Master Tailor]]
  - [[Cloak of the Master Weaponsmith]]
  - [[Cloak of the Master Woodworker]]
