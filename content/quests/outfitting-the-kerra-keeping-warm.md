## Steps

1. Obtain 25 sumac lumber to restock the firewood supplies of the kerra.
   - If you have them in inventory the quest will update.
   - If need be, simply buy or harvest 25 [[Sumac lumber]] and hand it to each person needing the update in your group.
1. Return to Chinook

## Rewards

- At least 20g 5s 35c
- +1,250 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +500 faction with **Kerra Isle Outfitters**
