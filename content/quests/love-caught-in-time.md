## Starting the Quest

You should be actively working on the quest [[Repairing the Eternal Prism]].

## Steps

1. Collect the following items in [[Miragul's Phylactery: Scion of Ice]].
   - a fragile wedding veil - on the bed at {{waypoint -213, 14, 121}}
   - [[Steel Claymore (Love Caught in Time)]] = an ancient sword (Erasmus) on the floor at {{waypoint -29, 1, -1}}
   - [[Leather Wrapped Bow]] - a leather wrapped bow on the floor at {{waypoint -149, -10, 5}}
1. Examine the items in your inventory.
1. Return to [[Mundug Stonefist]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
