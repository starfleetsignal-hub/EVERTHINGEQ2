## Steps

1. Do the following (in no particular order):
   - Slay 4 [[A Crushbone trainee|Crushbone trainees]] in [[Camp Rutok]] around {{waypoint -919, 93, -169}}
   - Slay 6 [[A Crushbone sparrer|Crushbone sparrers]] in [[Camp Rutok]] around {{waypoint -919, 93, -169}}
   - Slay [[Trainer Morgok]] in [[Camp Rutok]] at {{waypoint -877, 85, -182}}
1. Return to Daruk

- At least 31s 49c
- [[Golden fae tavern stool]]
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Archon's Greaves]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Pants]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Leggings]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Elder's Leggings]] (Shamans)
  - [[Enlightened Leggings]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Greaves]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Shade's Leggings]] (All Scouts)
