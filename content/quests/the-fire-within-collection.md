## Beginning the Quest and Finding Pages

- The pages drop from named monsters in [[Solusek's Eye: The Calling (Heroic)]].
- Examining the first page opens the collection.
- Some names drop only a limited number of pages, therefore you may have to run the zone several times to get all the pages needed.

## Rewards

- [[The Fire Within (Book)|The Fire Within]] -- *Reading the book opens the quest [[The Fire Within: Winding Descent]]*
