## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[A Gehein soulsapper|Gehein soulsapper]]s or [[A Gehein soulblighter|soulblighter]]s around the spires.
1. Gather Amhenti's notations. These are fluttering pages on the ground around the spires.
1. Return to [[Tal'il Amhenti]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Cardinian Table]]
