## Steps

1. Gather and hunt the following
   - Gather gauth wort x 10 - in area around {{waypoint 272, 67, -288}}![gauth wort](images/Useful_or_Not_-_gauth_wort.png)
     - *very hard to see purple flowers, look around rocky edges of the area and by other plants*
     - *go into your settings and hide flora to help see them more easily*
   - Hunt  thorn flutters for stingers x 8 - around {{waypoint 502, 60, -335}}![thorn flutter](images/A_thorn_flutter.png)
   - Hunt adult leafmane grazers for gastroliths x  6 - around {{waypoint 502, 60, -335}}![a leafmane grazer](images/A_leafmane_grazer.png)
1. Return to [[Jarkun]]
