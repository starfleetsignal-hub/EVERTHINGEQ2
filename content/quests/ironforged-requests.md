## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 60+

After you complete the quest, be sure to speak with Gavin again to continue the next step of the quest timeline. He will not immediately offer you the next quest.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Gavin Ironforge]] at the [[Call to Arms]] in [[North Qeynos]]  {{waypoint 409, -20, -195}}.
1. Perform a few tasks for Gavin.
   - Hunt 6 [[Elddar kestrel|Elddar kestrels]] in [[Elddar Grove]] to obtain feathers for armor padding (right-click to attack).
   - Gather 6 pieces of Oceangreen Lead (glowing stones) in [[Elddar Grove]] for the armor plating.
   - Go to the Erudite merchant [[Shinolio]] {{waypoint 713, -20, 129}} inside the [[Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 714, -20, 129}} and purchase a [[Qeynosian Armorsmithing Enchantment Stone]] (24s).
1. Return to [[Gavin Ironforge]] Inside the [[Call to Arms (POI)|Call to Arms]].

## Rewards

- [[Fountain of Storms]]
- At least 25g 77s 21c (at level 61)
- At least 29g 85s 79c (at level 92)
