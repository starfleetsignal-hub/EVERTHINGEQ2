This quest is repeatable.

## Description

Pallasa Rargon, a member of the Order of Flame, a remnant of the ancient order that once inhabited the temple of Solusek Ro, welcomed my help in their ancient crusade against the invading Void army. She is certain that the Void have been weakened by our ongoing attacks upon them. She feels it is now time to attack the beasts directly!

## Steps

1. Kill 6 of the void beasts in Najena's Cove.
1. Return to [[Pallasa Rargon]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g
- +500 faction with **Order of Flame**
