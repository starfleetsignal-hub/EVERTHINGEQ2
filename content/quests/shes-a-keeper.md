## Steps

1. Find the area in Dracur Prime where the Prime Draconic Conduit Gem should reside, at {{waypoint -120, 1, 259}}.
1. Use [[Jorlak’s Chrono Periapt]] to zone to the past.
1. Talk to Lendiniara at {{waypoint -104, 1, 252.54}}
1. Gather the following clickies (they spawn all along every wall and have yellow sparkles around them. NOT trackable. ):
   - 4 Caches of Ancient Tears (short brown jars)
   - 4 Repositories of Archaic Symbols (vase with spout on top)
   - 4 Coffers of Draconic History (crate)
1. Use [[Jorlak’s Chrono Periapt]] to zone back and talk with Jorlak.

## Reward

- 48g 35s 24cc
- +3,000 faction with **Claws of Veeshan**
- [[The Loss in Western Wastes]] (house item)
