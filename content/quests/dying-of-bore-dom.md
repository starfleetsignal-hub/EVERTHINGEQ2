## Notes

- You must also have completed [[The Gardens Are In Bloom]] before you can start this stage of the timeline if you did not do the adventure timeline first.
- You cannot do this at the same time as [[Arid Roots]] as they share the same node.
- You cannot be grouped with someone else that has completed this quest or the worm looking things will not appear for you to collect.

## Steps

![Damaged Typharush. Click it to find larva.](images/Damaged-typarush.jpg)

1. Check Typharush plant of signs of borer beetles in the [[Gardens of Nye'Caelona]] at {{waypoint -479, -99, -431}}, primarily around the trunks of the trees.
   - Click on the Damaged Typharush - this is a non-trackable white cluster of branches. The bush will vanish and a beetle larva appears on the ground. May need to check several [[Damaged Typharush]] before getting the update.
   - Pick up the larva by clicking on it.
1. Return to [[Elder Lyraquel]]

## Rewards

- +2,000 faction with **Nye'Caelona**
- At least 1p 53g 9s
