## Steps

1. Kill some of the undead around the Tower of Frozen Shadows in the Iceclad Ocean area of the Great Divide:
   - Kill any 8 undead in Iceclad Ocean
   - Kill [[Delhagin the Frightful]] {{waypoint -1094, -491, -1583}}
1. Return to Erus in Fina's Retreat.
1. Enter the Tower of Frozen Shadow {{waypoint -1116, -526, -1719}} and attempt to find any information relating to Tserrina's intentions.  This can be done with a cleared instance even if the player with the cleared instance already did this step.
   - Find a piece of Tserrina's notes, at {{waypoint 15.85, 217.47, -20.26}} in [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Shadowed Corridors|Shadowed Corridors]] on the Third Floor: The Shadowed Barracks.
   - Find a second set of Tserrina's notes, at {{waypoint 76.92, 290.28, -13.46}} in Darkness Rising in [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Umbral Halls|Umbral Halls]]. Accessible by falling down the hole and running up the spiral ramp.
   - Find the last part of Tserrina's notes, at {{waypoint 13.64, 560.97, 37.54}} in VhalSera's Dominion in [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Umbral Halls|Umbral Halls]].
   - Search for a tome relating to the Age's End prophecy, at {{waypoint -94, 75, -72}} in [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Shadowed Corridors|Shadowed Corridors]] on the Second Floor: The Frozen Library.
1. Return to Erus in Fina's Retreat.
1. Speak with the Duality in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]]  (within the Temple of Brell)  {{waypoint 475, -211.64, 245}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 50g
