## Tips

Equip your mount with a [[Handcrafted Zimaran Hackamore of Status]] to increase your status reward.

As this is a [[Guide Events|Guide Event]], the quest-giver is a player volunteer and is not always there. Monitor your server's thread on the [EQ2 Guide Forums](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/guide/index.php?forums/everquest-ii-guide-events.330/) to see when the quest will be run. It may be completed at any time after it's obtained from the Guide.

## Starting the quest

Speak to a Guide in the form of a hooluk, usually with a bird-themed name, typically located in [[The Barren Sky]] near {{waypoint -469.45, -70.44, -455.00}}

*A mysterious explorer has enticed me from the Undercloud back to the Overrealm, where a newly discovered section called Stratos has been uncovered. This explorer was keen that I would not ruffle any feathers while I'm there, and gave me a copy of their guide book. Enchanted by the allure of the unknown, I find myself taking to the skies once more.*
