## Description

"Gomphum says the grobins are coming from the northwestern passage. he says the grobins most responsible are Pengo and Turkrik. I should take these grobins out to help the Shroomba." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill [[Pengo]] and [[Turkrik]]
   - Go to the cave path to the northwest.
   - [[Pengo]] is found in the left cavern at {{waypoint 119, -46, -290}}
   - [[Turkrik]] is found in the right cavern with [[A Crushbone emissary (Greater Faydark) (8)|a Crushbone emissary]] at {{waypoint 15, -43, -291}}
1. Return to Gomphum

- Gomphum: "Have you done it?"
- Player Character: "I have."
- Gomphum: "Ah, this pleases Gomphum. Perhaps this will stop the constant grobin activity. Perhaps they will go back into their cave paths and not return. They will return to Pengo and Turkrik north of here and leave the Shroomba alone."
- Player Character: "Pengo and Turkrik?"
- Gomphum: "Meanest of the grobins. They are like Gomphum, other grobins listen to them. They are why grobins come to Dyer Mycoria. In fact, if we attack them too, they might all leave forever."
- Player Character: "I can take them out. I will return."

I need to kill Pengo and Turkrik. I need to kill the first goblin assault planner. I need to kill the second goblin assault planner.

I have slain Turkrik. I have slain Pengo. I have slain Pengo and Turkrik. I need to return to Gomphum and tell him the news. I need to speak with Gomphum.

- Gomphum: "Are the grobins still here?"
- Player Character: "Yes, but they will be less of a problem with Pengo and Turkrik gone. Strangely, one of them was conversing with an orc."
- Gomphum: "The beast of blue?"
- Player Character: "Uh, yes."

I have spoken with Gomphum.

I have taken out the two grobin assault planners. Gomphum and his Shroombas should be able to handle the remaining grobins.
