The Bloody Tooth goblins like to keep a full stock of healing brews, but it seems they're running low at the moment. I offered to help refill their supplies.

## Note

Upon accepting this quest, you will receive [[Healing Goo Brew (Recipe Book)]] which will automatically scribe the [[Healing goo brew|Healing Goo Brew]] recipe to your recipe book.

The recipe for this quest can **only** be crafted at a [[Goblin Firepit]] in the area. One is found at {{waypoint 132, -164, -768}}, near [[Glibnox]].

## Supply List

Because these must be crafted in [[Twark]], and the required materials cannot be harvested in [[Obulus Frontier]], bring the following (minimum) supplies:

- 4 Slug Slime (gathered for the quest from the Tame Slugs in the region)
- 12 [[Abyssal angler]]
- 12 [[Umbral trout]]
- 20 [[Thaumic Coal]]

## Steps

1. Tame slugs can be found around the Twark encampment
   - Gather slime from the tame slugs (4)
     - {{waypoint 49, -166, 708}}
     - {{waypoint 105, -184, -620}}
     - {{waypoint 194, -198, -609}}
     - {{waypoint 192, -191, -670}}
     - {{waypoint 215, -184, -727}}
     - {{waypoint 328, -176, -753}}
     - {{waypoint 318, -182, -684}}
1. I can brew this up in a gobline fire
   - Craft [[Healing goo brew|healing brew]] (4)
     - Craft 4 [[Healing goo brew|Healing Goo Brew]] at a [[Goblin Firepit]] (these are single combine, so 4 is 4 items total!)
1. Oogthor is in the main goblin encampment of Twark
   - Return the potions to [[Oogthor]]
