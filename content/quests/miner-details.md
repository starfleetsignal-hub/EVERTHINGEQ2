## Steps

1. Destroy 8 glowing glass bottles that have been unearthed.
   - Near west end of terrace {{waypoint 197.02, 0.00, 363.08}}
   - On View of the Dead under snake statue {{waypoint 157.73, 30.51, 488.87}}
   - In corner of west aqueduct {{waypoint 349.36, 29.36, 420.74}}
   - On southern central building {{waypoint 126.41, 28.05, 401.11}}
   - {{waypoint 408.50, 30.65, 496.63}}
   - On ledge of Waning Baths {{waypoint 435.39, 8.66, 482.15}}
   - On ledge {{waypoint 494.42, 6.51, 424.31}}
1. Kill 6 miners that unearthed the bottles.
   - Each full group of [[Sulite digger|Sulite diggers]] will give a single update.

- [[Primal Velium Shard]] (at level 90)
