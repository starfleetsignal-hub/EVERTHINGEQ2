## Starting the Quest

You must be actively working on the quest [[Repairing the Eternal Prism]] to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Kill the [[Atrocious Amalgam]].
   - When you use your [[Fragment of the Eternal Prism]], you will find that it is brighter than it was previously.

- At least 34g 80s 84c
