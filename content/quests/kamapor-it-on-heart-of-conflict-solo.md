## Steps

Kill all bosses in [[Karuupa Jungle: Heart of Conflict (Solo)]] as you need the last 2 for this quest:

1. Ambush the ape.
1. Slaughter the shaman.

## Rewards

- At least 3p 59g 25s 12c 107,799 status
- 3 [[Vetrovian Dres'Aag]]
- [[Vetrovia Crate (Version 3)|Vetrovia Crate]]
