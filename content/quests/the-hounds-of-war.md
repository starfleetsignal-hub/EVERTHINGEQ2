## Description

I must board The Vigilant: Infiltration and kill the vicious pets of the Tallonites, the war hounds and maulers.

## Steps

   - Kill 3 [[A war hound of Zek|war hound of Zek]]
   - Kill the [[A Tallonite battle mauler|Tallonite battle mauler]] and the [[A Tallonite mage mauler|Tallonite mage mauler]]
1. Return to Ismat

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
