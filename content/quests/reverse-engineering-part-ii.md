## Steps

Go to the Thexian forward camp at {{waypoint -616, -61, 486}}.

1. Gather 3 Greaves from [[A Thexian apothecary|Thexian apothecaries]] (uncommon update).
1. Gather 3 Greaves from [[A Thexian lieutenant|Thexian lieutenants]] (uncommon update).

NOTE: Lieutenants and Apothecaries seem to share spawns.

1. Return to [[Trun'Gul Nybright]] {{waypoint 112.51, -22.23, 337.56}}.

## Rewards

- At least 15g 15s 54c
- +100 faction with **Nybright Family**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Rujarkian Ambusher's Manacle]]
  - [[Rujarkian Soothsayer's Manacle]]
  - [[Rujarkian Shaman's Manacle]]
  - [[Rujarkian Gladiator's Manacle]]
