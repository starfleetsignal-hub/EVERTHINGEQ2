## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Zone into [[Firemyst Gully: A Foul Wind]]. {{waypoint -1197, -12, 708}}
   - The note tells you to hurry to [[Firemyst Gully]] before it is too late.  Alas, no matter how fast you hurry, you are always too late.  When you enter this instance you will find devastation and a large number of undead monsters that require killing; many of these will give AA. The quest completes once you zone in.

## Rewards

- Access to [[Firemyst Gully: A Foul Wind]]
