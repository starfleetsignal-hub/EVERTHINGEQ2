## Steps

1. Kill one of the following
   - 8 [[An icegaze basilisk|icegaze basilisks]] around {{waypoint -871, -319, 2483}}.
   - 8 [[A fanged crawler|fanged crawlers]] near {{waypoint -174, -359, 3111}}
1. Speak with [[Blor Greatbasher]]
1. Speak with [[Swog Bloodytusk]] at {{waypoint -723, -343, 2800}} and do his quests BEFORE doing Ergra's.
1. Speak with [[Ergra Bonecracker]] at {{waypoint -759, -343, 2783}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +200 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
