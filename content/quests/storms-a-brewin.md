## Note

The [[Vaashkaani: Golden Rule (Solo)|Vaashkaani: Golden Rule [Solo]]] half of [[Ballads of Zimara: Vaashkaani Crescendo]] sigline quest can **not** be done concurrently with this quest.

This does require a clean/fresh instance.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Vaashkaani: Golden Rule (Solo)|Vaashkaani: Golden Rule [Solo]]]
   - Click on the Vaashkaani, Alcazar of Zimara portal and select [[Vaashkaani: Golden Rule (Solo)|Vaashkaani: Golden Rule [Solo]]].
   - [[An unwell wisher|Unwell wishers]] at {{waypoint 15.14, 3.86, 49.06}} and {{waypoint 14.37, 3.86, -47.29}}. Save or Attack.
   1. Find the source of Karana's growing presence
   1. Clear the mobs including the two magic carpets on the ground floor at {{waypoint 8.98, 2.96, 41.36}}, {{waypoint 7.04, 2.96, -40.05}}.
   1. Go up a ramp to the platform and clear the mobs plus the third magic carpet at {{waypoint 7.82, 19.92, -0.25}}.
   1. Go down the hallway at the back of the platform.
   1. Kill [[Nezri En'Sallef]] - use HO's to dispel her gold shield if it gets to 25 you die. Her golden shield stifles you, plan accordingly with adornments. {{waypoint 149.43, 20.32, -0.15}}
   1. Remove the orbs from both rooms
      - [[Orb of Cyclones]] {{waypoint 227.24, 32.43, 43.95}}
      - [[Orb of Gales]] {{waypoint 199.5, 32.7, 44.25}}
      - [[Orb of Rains]] {{waypoint 226.84, 32.42, -44.32}}
      - [[Orb of Hail]] {{waypoint 199.90, 32.42, -44.88}}
   1. Continue down the hallway and kill [[Isos]]. He uses stifles, plan accordingly with adornments. Must kill adds to kill Isos; use HOs to knock them out. {{waypoint 262.24, 31.18, 58.37}}
      - Remove the [[Orb of Thunder]] {{waypoint 261.51, 32.42, 66.95}}
   1. Defeat [[The Storm Mistress]] {{waypoint 262, 31, -25}}
      - *She uses stuns. Use your HO on Lightning Balls*
      - *Kill her before the three indestructible globes complete their circuit.*
   1. Remove the [[Orb of Lightning]] {{waypoint 262.69, 32.45, -67.23}}
1. Return to [[Priest Oo'Rah]]
