## Steps

1. Gather the ingredients for making the blooming good tea.
   - Gather 4 *fay blossoms* found on the ground.
   - Harvest 4 *honey from hives* found attached to the trees.
   - ***Note:** Must be at least Artisan level 2 to scrbe recipe.
1. Brew the revitalizing pixie tea.
   - Use the yellow flower next to Odeyana.
1. Return to the Odeyana.
