## Steps

1. Gather 5 Diamond Dust mined by [[A Ry'Gorr grunt|Ry'Gorr Grunts]] at the Ry'Gorr Ruins
   - The diamond dust nodes are found in a crevasse near the ruins.  If you have stealth or invisibility, sneaking into the cave at {{waypoint -715, -387, 242}} and working your way out seems to be a decent tactic.
1. Note: There are two patrols - kill them first or you'll have a really bad day.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
