## Notes

After completing this quest, if you'd like more trees, you can buy more tickets from questgiver for 1p each. Click the option to register a complaint when talking to him.

## Steps

1. Help decorate the areas of [[South Freeport (POI)|South Freeport]]:
   - Click the first green sparkly at top of central stairs' first landing {{waypoint -162, -35, 154}}
   - Click the second green sparkly next to Theives Way manhole {{waypoint -217, -56, 236}}
   - Click the third green sparkly in front of door to 2 Justice Road residence {{waypoint -133, -18, 292}}
1. Click the final green sparkly in [[West Freeport (POI)|West Freeport]] at the south edge of [[Dreadnaught's Plaza]] {{waypoint 247, -4, 176}}
   - Defeat [[Mr. McScroogle]].
1. Return to [[Gevil Gigglegibber]].

## Rewards

- [[Special Frostfell Tree Ticket]]
  - To use the ticket double click the decorated tree to the right of the tree stand.
