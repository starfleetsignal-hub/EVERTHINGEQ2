## Steps

1. Scribe [[Escape Artist Essentials, volume 1]] and craft 3 [[Sturdy chain cutters]]. The ingredients are:
   - 9 [[Ferrite cluster]]
   - 6 [[Redwood lumber]]
   - 15 [[Smoldering coal]]
1. Return to [[Jones]].

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- At least 34g 36s 80c
- 400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
