## Steps

1. Talk to [[Sir Thothur Dorarr]]
1. Kill 10-12 of any skeleton.
1. Talk to [[Sir Thothur Dorarr]]
1. Destroy the nine sigils found in the zone (just by clicking on them). There are skeletons stuck on the top of poles, surrounded by groups of level 17-18 Heroic mobs. Note that the last one inside the hut requires your group to have purchased the shovel from [[Brother Morrim]] at the camp. Dig up the (20) graves one by one. A triple heroic or two double ups paired will spawn after digging each one up. Make sure you get all of them because when you attack the named inside the hut, ALL graves will spawn and help. That is like 20+ undead heroics rushing you, all heroic singles or heroic groups, so you may have 30 undead on you.
1. Return to Dorarr for your reward

## Notes

The torches purchased from [[Brother Morrim]] are used to burn the black carcasses seen with the groups of undead around the side walls to the back, but they disappear if you kill the groups standing around it, so this may have changed since the zone's inception. You can't use the torch while invis so it is pointless. Burning the carcass while fighting the groups seems to do nothing as well.
