This quest is part 6 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Thundering Steppes]].
1. Inspect a sack of food in a large building on [[Dead River Docks]]. {{waypoint -565.52, -14.60, -488.92}}
1. Return to Torq for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
- 3 x Poison ([[Bite of the Berry]])
