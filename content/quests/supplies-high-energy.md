## Notes

Daily crafting mission. Reset timer 18 hours. Quest **expires in 2 days.**

Journal entry: In order to support the continuing population of explorers in the area,  I must help create supplies needed by the adventurers. The adventurers in the area are in need of high-energy winter rations to combat the cold.

When accepting quest you receive [[Recipe Scroll: Supply Mission Materials]], which auto scribes 4 recipes needed for crafting dailies.

Materials needed:

- 30 [[Crowberry]]
- 18 [[Ptarmigan Meat]]
- 12 [[Red Lichen]]
- 30 [[Zimaran Kindling]]

## Steps

- Harvest 30 x [[Crowberry]]
- Harvest 18 [[Ptarmigan Meat]]
- Harvest 12 x [[Red Lichen]]
- Craft 12 x  [[High Energy Winter Rations]] - each uses 5 Crowberry, 3 Ptarmigan meat, 2 Red Lichen 5 Zimaran Kindling.  Makes 2 items.

Quest auto completes upon crafting the last item.
