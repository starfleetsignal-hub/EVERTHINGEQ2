## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Erollisi Marr has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items.

1. Purchase the [[Lost Lover's Kiss|lost lover's kiss]] from [[Demia]] for 5000 status
   - In the [[Frostfang Sea]] she wanders along the path from [[Cragged Spine]] camp at {{waypoint 246.38, 37.17, 176.37}} to the [[White Lodge]] at {{waypoint -56.72, 136.01, 242.44}} just outside [[New Halas]].
1. Purchase the [[Petrified Heartwood Seed|petrified heartwood seed]] from [[Oliska]] for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the [[Gold Imbued Mycelium|gold imbued mycelium]] from [[Nushoon]] for 5000 status
   - In [[Aether Wroughtlands]] this NPC wanders along a path from the Bivouac Camp {{waypoint 753.73, -45.26, -445.81}} to Minstrel's Cradle {{waypoint 343.61, -58.90, -182.69}}
