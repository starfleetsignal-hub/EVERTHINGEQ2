## Steps

1. I must slay the invading brutes that attack me within the halls of Najena's sanctum. I may have to wander the halls to bring them out of hiding.
   - Kill 4 invading brutes.
1. Head to the entrance to the [[Ward of Elements]] and click on the crystal pillar at {{waypoint -44.32, -293.86, 172.11}} to use the [[Al'Sedhati Vidyut potion]].
1. Return to [[Nida Wa'Fiyyah]] within the halls that lead to Najena's sanctum.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g
- +2250 faction with **Order of Flame**
