## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 20+

## Steps

1. I should speak with a few of the citizens around Qeynos, and see if I can get a feel for how things have been in the city.
   - Speak with [[Talit]] in the [[Call to Arms]] shop in [[North Qeynos (POI)|North Qeynos]]. {{waypoint 409, -21, -184}}
   - Speak with [[Musheff af'Neqi]] near the [[Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos (POI)|South Qeynos]]. {{waypoint 731, -20, 181}}
   - Speak with [[Olvene Kettershot]] near [[The Clock of Ak'Anon]] in Qeynos Harbor. {{waypoint 732, -21, -70}}
1. Return to [[Murrar Shar (Qeynos Province District)|Murrar Shar]]
1. Speak to [[Harkam Nubbytoe]] in Elddar Grove near the Baubbleshire. {{waypoint 793, -22, -543}}
