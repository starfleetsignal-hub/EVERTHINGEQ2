## Notes

It appears you need to be at least level 81 to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Collect 10 fallen coconuts from the coconut trees around the island (right click and shake the tree).
1. Return to [[Ritualist Joyaren]]. ![A fallen coconut on the sand](images/Going_Coconuts_quest_-_coconut.jpg)

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +2000 faction with **Kerra Isle**
