## Steps

Complete the following steps in no particular order:

- Visit the [[Grove of Stones]] {{waypoint 207, 14, 108}}
  - This is the druid ring in the [[Valley of Sacrifice]]
- Visit Dwindling Thicket {{waypoint 0, -15, 142}}
  - This is the [[Refuge]], south of [[Spirit Valley]]
- Visit [[The Hidden Thicket]] {{waypoint 109, 12, -181}}
  - This is the small wooded area, northeast of [[Blood Ore Valley]]
- Visit the [[Rusty Pond]] {{waypoint 215, 10, -180}}
  - This is the pond in [[Blood Ore Valley]]
- Visit the The Pit {{waypoint 341, -113, -113}}
  - This is [[The Deathfist Quarry]] in [[Blood Ore Valley]], you must travel down the elevator to get update
- Visit Spiral Lake {{waypoint -31, -4, -72}}
  - This is [[Spirit Lake]], in northeast [[Spirit Valley]]
- Visit Siren's Isle {{waypoint 183, -20, 379}}
  - This is [[Siren's Cove]], near the beach, south of the [[Refuge]]

## Rewards

- At least 53s 57c
