## Notes

- [[Assistant Volwon Zhi'dok]] will only offer one of his four quests daily.

## Steps

1. Gather 10 *fresh herbs* from around [[Riliss (POI)|Riliss]]. <s>{{waypoint -1215, 34, -119}}</s> {{waypoint -969.64, 46.01, -263.89}}
   - They look like small stalks of wild grass, and grow inside Riliss itself. They are located next to rocks, stone structures and the stone wall/hill surrounding the city. When you mouse over one, you will see a tooltip that says "fresh herb".
1. Return to Assistant Volwon Zhi'dok in Bathezid's Watch.

## Rewards

- At least 25g 39s 7c
- +2,000 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
