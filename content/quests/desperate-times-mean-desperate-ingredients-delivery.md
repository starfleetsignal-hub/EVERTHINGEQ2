## Starting the Quest

Once you have completed each of the 10 quests offered by the [[Portable Pantry]] 4 times, you will be offered this quest.

## Steps

1. Return to [[Jailaya]] in [[Moonfield Hamlet (POI)|Moonfield Hamlet]] with all the ingredients you've collected.
