## Notes

To receive this quest, you must have +30,000 faction with the Defenders of the Seal.

## Steps

1. Kill [[Blacksmith Lava-eye]]
1. Kill [[A Molten Juggernaut]]

Blacksmith stands in front of the far forge.
To spawn the Molten Juggernaut (in the same forge where Lava-eye stands), all "An Enslaved Forgelord"s (89 ^^^ mobs in the forges) must be killed first.
The Forgelords are not faction hits so having non-KOS faction with the Defenders of the Seal will make pulling the Forgelords much easier.

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]]
- At least 55g
- +2500 faction with **Defenders of the Seal**
