## Steps

![bulbs on the underside of the tree arches](images/Green_Fruit_For_Rut_-_Fruit.png)

1. Travel to the [[Esoterra Gardens]] near {{waypoint -634, 31, 263}}
1. Click on 10 bulbs. They can be found on the underside of the tree arches. Can be done while flying. Not a group update.
1. Kill the [[A green fruit bat|green fruit bat]] that pops when you collect the 10th bulb.
1. Return to [[Izny Rut]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
