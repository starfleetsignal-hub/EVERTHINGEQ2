## Steps

![](images/Lending_Your_Skills_Archery_Target.png)
**Aiming Point**

1. Speak to scout trainee [[Gerleth Bucklebrow]] by the archery practice area behind the [[In Range]] shop. First, teach your new trainee ranged combat skills. {{waypoint 566, -18, -409}}
1. Take your places on the firing line behind the wooden barricade by clicking the barricade. {{waypoint 559, -17, -407}}
1. Take a shot to show your trainee how it's done.
   - Click on the archery target to take aim: you can see your cursor change to the target pointer with the red crossed swords when you are in the right spot.
   - The active area for targeting is small and somewhat off-center.
   - Turn on ranged autoattack.
1. Take [[Gerleth Bucklebrow|Gerleth]] to [[The Caves]] for stealth training. Talk to him about stealthing once you arrive. (There are two close ways to access [[The Caves]], either pop down the sewer grate and find the iron-bound door, or take the bell by the dojo.)
   - **Stealth yourself** and take your trainee on a tour of [[The Caves]].
     - Below [[Sleg's Lookout]] near the [[An alabaster golem|alabaster golems]]. {{waypoint 44, 23, -113}}
     - Just inside the snake pit with the [[A pale adder|pale adders]]. {{waypoint -29, 34, -50}}
     - Just inside the door to [[Atramentor|Atramentor's]] room. {{waypoint -48, 17, -116}}
1. Take [[Gerleth Bucklebrow|Gerleth]] to [[South Qeynos]] then enter the [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum|Starcrest Commune]] gate for some combat training. {{waypoint 695, -21, 266}}
   - Slay 4 undead with the assistance of your trainee ([[A ghastly specter (Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum)|ghastly specters]], [[A ghastly knight (Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum|ghastly knights]], [[A portal guardian|portal guardians]], etc.)
   - Slay the [[Portal Taskmaster]]. During the fight, your new trainee will panic and evac himself to the gates.
   - Return to the Starcrest gate and discuss the unwisdom of abandoning one's comrades in arms with [[Gerleth Bucklebrow|Gerleth]].
1. Return to [[Master Rysian Gladewalker]].

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Tunarian Alliance**
