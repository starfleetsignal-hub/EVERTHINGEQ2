## Notes

Once you reach +22,000 faction with [[Pride Pakiat]], you'll have to do this quest 8 more times, or 4 times if you pair it with 4 more of [[Green Fruit For Rut Part Deux]], to get you to +30,000 so you can move-on to [[Khat-ra Pakiat]]'s quests which require +30k faction to offer them.

## Steps

1. Travel to {{waypoint -1207, -22.46, 368.29}} in the Southern waters, or {{waypoint 876, -16, -1062}} in the Northern waters.
   - Note: The quest will NOT update unless one swims underwater to lake bottom and the quest updates (bring water breathing abilities)
1. Kill 6 [[A lobster monster|lobster monsters]]
1. Click on the den at either {{waypoint -1205, -20, 367}} in the Southern waters, or {{waypoint 803, -13, -1115}} in the Northern waters.
1. Kill [[The Mother Hen]] that spawns.
1. Return to [[Izny Rut]] at {{waypoint -834, 131, -777}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -1,250 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1,250 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +1,250 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
