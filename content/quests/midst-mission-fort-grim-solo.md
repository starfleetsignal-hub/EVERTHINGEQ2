1. Dissect the sisters - Kill Ulosi and Ulcine Nacisroc ( -28, 43, -273 )

1. Hunt the prowler - Kill [[Felnir the Shadow Prowler]] and The Dire Pyre ( 70, 31, -285 )

Walk through [[Savage Weald: Fort Grim (Solo)]]
