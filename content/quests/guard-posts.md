## Steps

1. Speak with the following NPCs at the various guard posts throughout [[Fens of Nathsar]]:
   - [[Trooper Silviss]] at [[Kylong Post]]: {{waypoint 509, 14, 1501}}
   - [[Trooper Vilzix]] at [[Sathir's Span Post]]: {{waypoint -132, -39, 1234}}
   - [[Trooper Rellsin]] at [[West Riliss Post]]: {{waypoint -197, -9, -256}}
   - [[Trooper Daxxin]] at [[East Riliss Post]]: {{waypoint -1350, 2, -431}}
   - [[Trooper Zathiid]] at [[North Riliss Post]]: {{waypoint -582, -36, -323}}
   - [[Trooper Lannis]] at [[Cabilis Post]]: {{waypoint -222, 31, -1126}}
1. Return to [[Trooper Sakred]].

## Rewards

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Invoker's Wand of Evil Energy]]
  - [[Recurve of Despair]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Rilissian Dragon Bow]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Rilissian Throwing Knife Holder]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Staff of the Soul Channeler]]
- +5000 faction with **Riliss**
