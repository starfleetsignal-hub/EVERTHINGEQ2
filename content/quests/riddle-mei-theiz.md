## Notes

**This quest is available to get and complete only on April 1st.**

## Steps

![Cabbage](images/Riddle04_Cabbage.png)

1. Speak with [[Mei-theiz]] and answer a preliminary riddle. She will then give you a series of riddles to challenge you again.
   - Q: Bofim Axeshadow was given a kitten for his birthday. It is black with white paws, a white belly and a white strip down his nose. What side of Bofim's cat has the most black hair.
   - A: The outside.
   - Q: An ogre has seven daughters and each daughter has a brother. How many children does the ogre have altogether?
   - A: Eight.

![Anchor](images/Riddle04_Anchor.png)

1. Travel to [[New Halas]] and click on the cabbage on the table at {{waypoint -23, 149, 15}}.
1. Return to [[Mei-theiz]] for the next riddle.
1. Travel to the [[The Barren Sky]] Wizard Spire. Click the anchor at {{waypoint -562, -69, -312}}.
1. Return to [[Mei-theiz]] in the Enchanted Lands to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g
- One of the following:
  - [[Rhei Sphinx Plushie]] - a plushie that looks like the quest giver, for your [[Housing|house]]
  - [[Four-Sided Stone of a Different Color]] - a house pet with the appearance of a 4-sided die that changes color each time you zone into your house.
  - [[Twelve-Sided Stone of a Different Color]] - a house pet with the appearance of a 12-sided die that changes color each time you zone into your house.
  - [[Twenty-Sided Stone of a Different Color]] - a house pet with the appearance of a 20-sided die that changes color each time you zone into your house.
  - 10 [[Fool's Gold Coin|Fool's Gold Coins]]
