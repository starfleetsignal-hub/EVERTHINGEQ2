- You lose faction with Order of Rime from doing this quest.

1. Destroy Rime summoning casters (any flurryburst will do).
1. Return to [[Spotter Delna|Delna]] {{waypoint 772, -393, 739}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
