After finishing this quest, you should be a level 9 crafter and ready to visit a [[Crafting Trainer]] to choose your crafting class and pick up some more recipe books. In some cases, you can even reach level 9 during the quest and visit the trainer (to achieve Artisan level 10) before finishing it, to get extra tradeskill experience.

## Description

"The tradeskill tutor has asked me to try my hand at tailoring and armorsmithing." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Craft a [[Tin Chainmail Coat]] using your [[Armorer]] skills
   - buy a [[Basic Coal]] to fuel the [[Forge]]
   - you will need 2 [[Tin cluster|tin clusters]], 1 [[Rawhide leather pelt (no-value) (Version 1)|rawhide leather pelt]], and 1 [[Root (harvest)|root]].
1. Craft a [[Pristine rawhide leather backpack|Rawhide Leather Backpack]] using your [[Tailor]] skills
   - buy a [[Basic Filament]] to fuel the [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]]
   - you will need 2 [[Rawhide leather pelt (no-value) (Version 1)|rawhide leather pelts]] and 1 [[Root (harvest)|root]].
1. Return to the instructor

## Rewards

- At least 19c
- [[Artisan's Tunic]]
- [[Meager harvesting bag]] (if you have completed [[Tutorial: Learning to Harvest]] you will not receive meager harvesting bag)

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Tradeskill Tutorial NPC: "Now let's look at tailors and armorers. Armorers focus on heavy plate and chain armor, while tailors specialize in the creation of lighter cloth and leather armor. Tailors also make backpacks and other items from cloth or leather. Show me that you can create a tin chainmail coat, and a rawhide backpack."
- Player Character: "Coat and a backpack coming up."

I must follow the tutor's instructions to review the crafting process. I need to create a tin chainmail coat using the forge. I must create a rawhide backpack using the sewing table.

I have created a tin chainmail coat. I have created a rawhide backpack. I have reviewed the process of crafting an item. I should return to the tutor with my work. I should return to the tutor with the equipment I created.

- Tradeskill Tutorial NPC: "Excellent work! I hope you now have an idea of what each tradeskill class does, and how to do it. At level 9, you'll need to decide which crafting path you will follow, by speaking to the Crafting Trainer near your local tradeskill area. The Crafting Trainer also sells your recipe books, so be sure to pick those up! come back to me at any time and I'll be happy to give you refreshers on this information. Should you choose to continue a career as a crafter, I wish you the best of fortune!
- Player Character: "Thanks."

I returned to the tutor with the equipment I made. The tutor was pleased with my progress.

I created a backpack and chainmail coat as instructed by the tutor.
