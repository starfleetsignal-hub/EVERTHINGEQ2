## Description

Powerful forces are stirring with the Vasty Deep. I should venture within and explore the entire complex, bringing a halt to their deadly machinations.

## Steps

1. I should seek out [[Delahnus the Dauntless|Delahnus]] in [[Vasty Deep: The Conservatory]].
1. I should kill [[Perah'Celsis (Heroic)|Perah'Celsis]] in [[Vasty Deep: The Abandoned Labs]].
1. I should hunt down [[Queen Gwarthlea]] in [[Vasty Deep: The Vestigial Cella]].
