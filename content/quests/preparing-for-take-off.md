## Steps

1. Go to [[The Crossbridge Tower]] in [[Chardok]] and destroy four air ward totems (clickable rock sculptures):
   - You may want to get the upper two totems first, then drop down and work your way back up, since the named inside the tower is needed for the next step.
   - *Totem of I'ar Rofu*: {{waypoint -419, -66, -17}}
   - *Totem of I'ar Efiv*: {{waypoint -420, -66, 27}}
   - *Totem of I'ar Venes*: {{waypoint -477, -119, 10}}
   - *Totem of I'ar Xis*: {{waypoint -430, -126, -48}}
1. Kill [[Warlord Hir'roul]] inside the tower and loot the [[Key of Nak'azar]]. {{waypoint -394, -64, 1}}
1. Open the nearby chest at {{waypoint -392, -64, -7}} and receive three [[Blasting Stone|Blasting Stones]].
1. Place the bombs at the base of the large chains on the outer edge of the Crossbridge Tower island. You have to find the interactable space and click (can be difficult to find):
   - {{waypoint -453, -66, 38}}
   - {{waypoint -378, -66, 53}}
   - {{waypoint -371, -66, -22}}
1. Return to [[Skyrider Tosuj]] in [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]].

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Gwalnax Brigade**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Belt of the Lava Soldier]]
  - [[Cummerbund of the Flame Assassin]]
  - [[Girdle of the Magma Priest]]
  - [[Sash of the Lava Magi]]

## Notes

- Yet again, SOE and backwards spelling combine: "I'ar" being "Air," the four totems in this quest are Air Four, Five, Six, and Seven.
