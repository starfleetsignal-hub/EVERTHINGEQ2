## Notes

- You will need 30 Basic Coal for crafting the item.  You can buy from a fuel merchant near quest giver at {{waypoint -1143.77, -532.94, 3390.00}}

## Steps

1. Scribe the recipe [[Colorful Oceansfull Shell Necklace]] you received from Kisu
1. Collect 6 shells of each color from around the area.  (Aqua, Orange, and Pink).
1. Craft the Colorful Oceansfull Shell Necklace using the Oceansfull Othmir Work Bench {{waypoint -1138.14, -532.53, 3391.01}}  (*Note: Do not try to use Mass Production to make multiple necklaces in one combine.  It will use the resources but provide only 1 update.*)
1. Return to Kisu for an update.
1. Give out the crafted necklaces to 6 Oathmir's in the area.
1. Return to Kisu at {{waypoint -1216, -539, 3384}}

## Rewards

- [[Oceansfull: Bag of Shells]] which contains the following:
  - 2x [[Aqua Sand Urchin Shell]]
  - 2x [[Orange Sand Urchin Shell]]
  - 2x [[Pink Sand Urchin Shell]]
