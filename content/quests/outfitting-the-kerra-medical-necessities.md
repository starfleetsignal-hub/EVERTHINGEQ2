## Steps

1. Gather 5 of each
   - nepeta buds from small ground plants scattered around the village on Kerra Isle![nepeta plant](images/Nepeta_plant.jpg)
   - Locations include
     - {{waypoint 1332.09, 44.73, 2196.17}}
     - {{waypoint 1292.34, 44.59, 2161.38}}
     - {{waypoint 1235.71, 44.46, 2111.95}}
     - {{waypoint 1184.27, 34.14, 2065.80}}
     - {{waypoint 1173.09, 33.36, 2070.90}}
     - {{waypoint 1371.13, 35.50, 2276.00}}
     - {{waypoint 1358.77, 35.99, 2284.11}}
1. Bark from Salix Bushes around the Highland Salts.  ![salix bush](images/Salix_bush.jpg)
   - Found in the Sundered Frontier around the Stonebrunt Encampment Station.
     - Around  {{waypoint 940, 110, 3739}}
1. [[Chrysogena fungus|Chrysogena Fungi]] from borders of [[Toxxulia Forest]] {{waypoint 433.72, 136.45, 2594.37}}.  ![chrysogena fungus](images/Chrysogena_fungus.jpg)
1. Return to Chinook

- At least 20g 5s 35c
- +1,250 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +500 faction with **Kerra Isle Outfitters**
