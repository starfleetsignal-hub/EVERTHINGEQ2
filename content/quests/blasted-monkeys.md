## Steps

Jimbo McDaniel is fed up with the local area monkeys taking his supplies and tools.  He has enlisted your help in keeping the monkeys away so he can conduct his work.

- Shoo away 12 monkeys.
  - Right click on a [[A curious monkey]] that is standing still anywhere around the encampment, then choose "Shoo!"

## Rewards

- At least 1g 1s 30c
