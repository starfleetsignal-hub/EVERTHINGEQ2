1. Gather 8 [[A festive vine flower|festive vine flowers]] from vines around the grotto
1. Give the flowers to 8 different [[A brownie dancer|brownie dancers]] (right click or double click) at {{waypoint -259, -38, -19}}
1. Return to the Brownie Elder
