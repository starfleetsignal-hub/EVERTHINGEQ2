## Steps

1. Elon Gallwin will randomly offer you one of the following tasks:
   - Kill 20 [[Dervish cutthroat]]s near [[The Undercity Arena]] in  [[The Sinking Sands]].
   - Gather 20 Sul'Dal relics in [[The Eye of Anuk]] in the Sinking Sands. {{waypoint -684, -231, -1184}}
   - Gather 20 Anaz'Mal ceremonial vases scattered in the sands of the northern [[The Twin Tears|Twin Tears]] in the Sinking Sands. (These vases can easily be found scattered along the shore of the oasis right outside The Living Tombs entrance.)
   - Kill 20 [[A Sandscrawler magus|Sandscrawler magus]] in the Sinking Sands.
1. Return to Elon Gallwin

## Rewards

- Approximately 9849 status
- +150 faction with **The Academy of Arcane Science**
