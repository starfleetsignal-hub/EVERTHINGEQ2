## Description

"Seren Leafsway, a wood elf stationed at Spire Shadow Outpost is becoming worried by the lack of messages from her scouts, Elar Roughroot and Flitt Morningglory. I volunteered to go get them for her." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Flitt Morningglory]] on a cliff overlooking [[Steamfont Canyon]] {{waypoint -547, 155, 499}}
1. Speak to [[Elar Roughroot]] in a tree overlooking the path to Steamfont Canyon {{waypoint -534, 142, 379}}
1. Return to Seren

## Rewards

- At least 5s 81c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Seren Leafsway: "Watch yourself around here, traveler. There are ravenous beasts about!"
- Player Character: "So I've noticed."
- Seren Leafsway: "Experienced them already, have you? I am sorry that my warning was not prudent."
- Player Character: "It's okay."
- Seren Leafsway: "You must be a brave traveler then to have already faced some of the beasts. I could use someone of your talent."
- Player Character: "What kind of help do you need?"
- Seren Leafsway: "I have not heard from Elar Roughroot or Flitt Morningglory, the scouts stationed within the trees south of The Combine Spires. They are keeping an eye on an unsettling situation here, close to Steamfont. I need their reports."
- Player Character: "I'll get their reports for you."

I am to find Flitt Morningglory and Elar Roughroot overlooking the destroyed camp site, south of The Combine Spires, and obtain their reports. Obtain Flit Morningglory's scout report. Obtain Elar Roughroot's scout report.

- Flitt Morningglory: "I must not be distracted, adventurer. I am keeping a vigilant eye upon the kobold pack, over yonder."
- Player Character: "Seren sent me for your report."
- Flitt Morningglory: "Oh! Yes, take this scroll to her. Thank you."
- Player Character: "I'm glad to help."

I have obtained Flitt Morningglory's scout report.

- Elar Roughroot: "Hail, adventurer! I should warn you to be careful. If a drop from up here doesn't kill you, the kobolds over there just might."
- Player Character: "Good to know."
- Elar Roughroot: "What are you doing up here, anyway?"
- Player Character: "Seren sent me for your report."
- Elar Roughroot: "Right! My report. Here you are."
- Player Character: "Thank you."

I have obtained Elar Roughroot's scout report. I have obtained Elar Roughroot's and Flitt Morningglory's scout reports. I should return to Seren Leafsway, at Spire Shadow Outpost. Speak with Seren Leafsway.

- Seren Leafsway: "The scout reports, perfect! Your services are greatly appreciated, [Player Character].
- Player Character: "I'm glad to hear it."

I have spoken with Seren Leafsway.

Seren Leafsway was grateful for the scout reports I brought to her from Elar Roughroot and Flitt Morningglory.
