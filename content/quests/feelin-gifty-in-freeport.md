## Steps

1. Obtain the names of the gift recipients:
   - In [[West Freeport (POI)|West Freeport]]:
     - [[Kamaria Cheefar]] "Meow-Meow" on the first floor of the [[Coalition Crafter's Association (POI)|Coalition Crafter's Association]] {{waypoint 27, -12, 85}}
     - [[Gowan Macgillivray]] "Blue Faced, biggie, angry guy," in the basement of [[The Freeport Militia House]] {{waypoint 165, -26, 145}}
     - [[Thabit Kalila]] in [[Mulka's Pet Guuds]] {{waypoint 129.96, -4, -100.05}}
   - In [[South Freeport (POI)|South Freeport]]:
     - [[Gemellus Sperasius]] at the north end of *Freedom Road* {{waypoint -127, -35, 141}}
     - [[Linst Tincobble]] on the boat {{waypoint -304, -55, 148}}
   - In [[East Freeport (POI)|East Freeport]]:
     - [[Klaessa L'tek]] on the bottom floor of [[Seafarer's Roost]] {{waypoint -237, -55, -15}}
1. Return to G'shugaplum Gigglegibber. Make sure to have six open slots in your inventory!
1. Hand out the gifts:
   - **WARNING: You must give the correct gift to the correct person, or you will have to start the entire quest over!**
   - In West Freeport:
     - Gowan Macgillivray {{waypoint 165, -26, 145}} -- [[Tray of Gigglegibber cookies]]
     - Thabit Kalila {{waypoint 107, -10, -57}} -- [[Jumjum pie]]
     - Kamaria Cheefar {{waypoint 27, -12, 85}} -- [[Book of Erud's teachings]]
   - In South Freeport:
     - Gemellus Sperasius {{waypoint -127, -35, 141}} -- [[Turlif whizgig]]
     - Linst Tincobble {{waypoint -304, -55, 148}} -- [[Beard trimming kit]]
   - In East Freeport:
     - Klaessa L'tek {{waypoint -237, -55, -15}} -- [[Halas crafted dagger]]
1. Return to G'Shugahplum Gigglegibber.

## Rewards

- The ability to set different kinds of lights on your [[Frostfell Tree]].
