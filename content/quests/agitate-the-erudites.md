## Steps

1. Enter [[Stonestair Byway: The Animus Corridors]] {{waypoint -6, -17, -328}}
1. Kill any 6 Erudites
1. Place 6 weapon crates by clicking green sparkles:
   - {{waypoint -11, 0, -24}}
   - {{waypoint 5, -5, 9}}
   - {{waypoint -28, -3, -12}}
   - {{waypoint -46, -4, -37}}
   - {{waypoint 12, 0, -24}}
   - {{waypoint -56, 0, -1}}
   - {{waypoint -42, 0, -62}} (ALT LOC)
1. Speak to Corporal Sergius Mordanticus {{waypoint 131, -19, 84}} in West Freeport
