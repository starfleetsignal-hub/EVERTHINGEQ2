## Materials Required

At least:

- 62 [[Luclizite Cluster]]
- 37 [[Humming Azurite]]
- 25 [[Radiant Algae]]
- 12 [[Slow Creeping Root]]
- 50 [[Spectral Coruscating Coal]] provided in the instance

## Steps

Perform the following:

1. Convince [[Purihni "The Edge" Fordel III]] to resolve his difference with [[Gundeg "The Anvil" Midst]].
1. Convince [[Gundeg "The Anvil" Midst]] to resolve his difference with [[Purihni "The Edge" Fordel III]].
1. Take a copy of the recipes from the tables.
   - Midst recipe at {{waypoint -70.30, -0.98, 9.33}}
   - Fordel recipe at {{waypoint -96.46, -1.06, -0.87}}
1. Craft for each smith
   - Craft [[Pristine Luclinite Barbute (Crafted)|Pristine Luclinite Barbute]]
   - Craft [[Pristine Luclinite Scimitar (Crafted)|Pristine Luclinite Scimitar]]
     - The [[Spectral Coruscating Coal]] is beside the forge.
1. Speak to both smiths
   - speak with [[Purihni "The Edge" Fordel III]]
   - speak with [[Gundeg "The Anvil" Midst]]
