## Steps

1. Using the [[Seaspray Wand]] given to you, collect patinaed copper (dropped on the ground) from the following:
   - 6 from metalloid golems in the [[Coppercurl Maze]] area {{waypoint -645.30, 55.13, -392.81}}
     - These look like piles of rock, when you approach they 'wake up' and you use the wand on them, then collect the rock they leave behind once they are defeated.
   - 6 from copperbind soldiers in the [[Coppercurl Maze]] area {{waypoint -705.09, 58.27, -436.00}}
1. return to [[Oolfonso]] at {{waypoint 715, -43, -462}}

## Rewards

- 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1000 faction with **Isles of Wisdom**
