## Steps

1. Go to the [[Myrist Main Stacks]] at {{waypoint 131, -5, -13}}
1. Speak to [[The Maladjusted Codex]] (wanders just outside center ring of room around  {{waypoint 290, -5, 58}})
1. Gather 8 **Uncatalogued Items** in the [[South Wing Stacks]] at {{waypoint 215, -21, 222}} or [[Grand Librarian's Office]] at {{waypoint 425, 15, 244}} (take the elevator at the west side of the Main Stacks to the Mezzanine Level)
1. Return to [[The Maladjusted Codex]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Ancient Tanaanite Stud]] - Earring
- [[Tanaanite Ruthless Helm]] - Mercenary Helm
- One of the following:
  - [[Pyrelink Gloves]] - Chain
  - [[Emberweave Gloves]] - Cloth
  - [[Infernal Leather Handguards]] - Leather
  - [[Blazeplate Gauntlets]] - Plate
