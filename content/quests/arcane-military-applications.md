## Steps

1. Collect the following items from the [[Enlightenment Aviary]], the floor above where the quest starter is.
   - Inanimate rubble (ground spawn).
   - Invoker scroll, auto-update from killing [[A Bloodscale invoker]] {{waypoint -165, 132, 37}} - part of the ring event to summon the green dragon.
   - Animating powder (ground spawn).
1. Use [[A Defensive Summoning Scroll]] from your inventory. This summons [[A weak animated barrier]], level 10vvv.
1. Bring the information to [[Gaer Torrent]] in [[The Barren Sky]] on [[The Whisperwind Isle]], near the teleporter to Norrath. {{waypoint -578.37, -46.41, -386.06}}

## Rewards

- At least 13g 79s 50c
- +50 faction with **The Windgazer**
