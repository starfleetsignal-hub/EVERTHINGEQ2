## Steps

1. Take the elevator up and zone into [[Thurgadin]] at {{waypoint 1111, -470, 442}} and head to the Chapel. The green shiny prompting you to place the spirits, is at {{waypoint 480, -214, 255}}
1. Place the spirits to complete the quest.

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +2,000 faction with **Iceclad Pirates**
