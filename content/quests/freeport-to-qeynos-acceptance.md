## Steps

1. Talk to your new class trainer in which ever inner city zone of Qeynos they are.
   - If you are a neutral class (dirge/troubadour, berserker/guardian, fury/warden, warlock/wizard), then you can choose whether to stay your current class or switch to the other associated class.
   - If you are an alignment restricted class (e.g. paladin/shadowknight, conjurer/necromancer, etc.), then you must talk to the trainer for opposite alignment to your class.
   - All Mage trainers are found in the [[Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos]].
   - All Priest trainers are found in the [[Temple of Life]] in [[North Qeynos]].
   - All Scout trainers are found in [[Arbos]] the Elddar Tree in [[The Elddar Grove]].
   - All Fighter trainers are found on the steps to Qeynos Castle in [[North Qeynos]] except for Brawlers (Bruiser becoming Monk), who report to [[Khai Lightfall]] {{waypoint 770, -20, -375}} in the Dojo in [[The Elddar Grove]].
1. You will be asked several times if you are sure you want to be your class to confirm your choice.

## Reward

- [[Call of Qeynos]] general ability - replaces your [[Call of Haven]] (It's now changed to call to home regardless)
- -100,000 faction with **Exile**
- Finally rid of that blasted **the Exiled** suffix title
- Possible change of class
- IF your class changes, all of your spells/combat arts reset to apprentice 1
- Loss of all class "fun" spells
- Free Respec of all of your character traits, racial traditions, enemy masteries, specialized training (master 2)
