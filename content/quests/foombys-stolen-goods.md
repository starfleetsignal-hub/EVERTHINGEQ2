## Prerequisites

- You must be at least level 32 to start this quest.

## Steps

1. Kill Darkflight Fairies for a *cracked gold monocle*. {{waypoint -370, -1, -550}}
1. Return to [[Foomby Slopdigger]].
1. Kill [[Chomper]] in [[Chomper's Pond|his pond]]. {{waypoint -245, 5, -526}}
1. Return to Foomby Slopdigger.
1. Speak to [[Bellendis Tempestcall]] inside the building at [[The Granary Hill]]. {{waypoint 200, 15, -260}}
1. Kill 15 lamias within 20 minutes (timer starts after first kill). If successful, [[Baz'Tarog the Corrupter]] will spawn at {{waypoint -481, 6, -903}}.
   - Lamias can be found throughout the zone. The largest concentration is around [[Misty Grove]] {{waypoint -434, 5, -940}}
   - Baz'Tarog {{waypoint -475, 10, -911}}must be killed within 5 minutes of killing the last lamia.
1. Return to Bellendis Tempestcall.
1. Return to Foomby Slopdigger to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At step 8 [[Bellendis Tempestcall]] gives you 8g 29s 88c.
- [[Bag of Sewn Evil Eye (HQ Reward)|Bag of Sewn Evil Eye]] - *This item is NOT mountable*
- 44800 status
