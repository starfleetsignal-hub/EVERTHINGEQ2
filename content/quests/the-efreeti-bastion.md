## Notes

- This is the former access quest for [[The Bastion of Flames]], however it's completion is no longer necessary to enter the instance.

## Steps

1. Kill [[A Bastion keeper|Bastion keepers]] or [[A Bastion steward|Bastion stewards]] until you obtain the two auto-updates. Both are found around {{waypoint -165, -369, -218}}.
1. Kill each type of fire-giant until you receive an auto-updating shard from each one. You may not receive an update immediately and may have to kill each type repeatedly until you get the update. The required mobs are:
   - [[A defender of Thyr (Solusek's Eye)|A defender of Thyr]]
   - [[A diviner of Thyr]]
   - [[A pyreduke of Thyr]]
   - [[A guardian of Thyr]]
   - [[A magus of Thyr]]
   - [[A flamelord of Thyr]]
   - [[A protector of Thyr]]
   - [[A warlock of Thyr]]
   - [[A firelord of Thyr]]
1. Forge the efreeti bottle by clicking the first forge in the [[The Forge of Thyr|Forge of Thyr]]. {{waypoint -70, -625, -37}}
1. Kill [[The Castigator]]. {{waypoint -276, -371, -95}}
1. Kill 4 [[An efreeti soulslaver|efreeti soulslavers]]. {{waypoint -275, -185, -120}}

## Rewards

- [[Tiny Bottle of Efreeti Essence]]
