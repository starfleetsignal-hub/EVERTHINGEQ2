## Steps

1. Go to [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]]
   - When you get to the location there is a small purple orb to click - you may need to zoom in a bit to see it
   - Scout out strategic positions {{waypoint -441, 205, -844}}:Looking down at the zone-in from [[City of Shar Vahl]] ![A Strategic Location](images/A_Strategic_Location.png)
   - Scout out strategic positions {{waypoint -457, 206, -471}}: on a high outcrop.
   - Scout out strategic positions {{waypoint -533, 154, -376}}: overlooking [[Whisperling Pond]].
   - Scout out strategic positions {{waypoint -661, 201, 58}}: above the entrance to the Ant Caves
   - Scout out strategic positions {{waypoint -179, 160, 146}}: at [[Spiritual Point]].
   - Scout out strategic positions {{waypoint -55, 192, 511}}: overlooking the entrance to [[Vex Thal]].
1. Seek out the last strategic position {{waypoint 496, 133, 551}}: at [[Hunter's Mote]].
   - The quest will end here, However you will need to return to the NPC to answer the questions to advance the parent quest.
1. Return to [[Scout Amani Abasi]].
   - Tell me what you saw...:  A good spot to see
   - What of Soulstare...:  The owlbears
   - What of Whispering Pond?:  It would be a rest stop...
   - What of Vex...:  You can hear the hum...

And what of Poacher Reef?:  The Pirates are easy..
