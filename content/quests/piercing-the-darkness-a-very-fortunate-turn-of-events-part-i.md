## Steps

1. Travel to {{waypoint 720.96, 57.29, 706.02}} and enter [[Sanctus Seru (City)|Sanctus Seru [City]]].
   - Note: Use the tamed Skik'Nar drone to travel to [[Recuso Tor]] and then to Seru's Ascent.
1. Enter [[Sanctus Seru: Fabrication Hall]] at {{waypoint -181.40, 180.67, 95.55}}.
   - Use the portal at {{waypoint -200.22, 86.85, 281.39}} to get to the top level of the city.
1. Speak to [[Hulda Flintbraid]] at {{waypoint 147.78, -510.72, 4.45}}
   - Part way through the conversation you have to wait for an NPC to come from the back of the zone to progress the quest
1. Speak to [[Hulda Flintbraid]] again.
1. Click the chair at {{waypoint 148.91, -509.94, -3.13}} to sit in it.
   - Read the rules and choose *Begin Test*, when you're ready to start the timer.
   - Do not stand before the test is finished and graded, else you will need to exit the zone and re-enter, and sit again for the test.
   - Spoilers and some information regarding possible issues with this step can be found on the Talk page.
1. Finish the test and speak to [[Hulda Flintbraid]] to request your grade.

## Rewards

- At least 14p 21g 53s 73c 27,931 status
- At least 14p 21g 53s 73c 26,666 status at Level 120
