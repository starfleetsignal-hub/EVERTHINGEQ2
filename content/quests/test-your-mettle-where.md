## Steps

1. Harvest:
   - 40 [[Aether Ore]]
   - 40 [[Trystine Gem|Trystine Gems]]
   - 40 [[Bittersweet Vineroot]]
   - 20 [[Direbeak Hide|Direbeak Hides]]
   - 20 [[Charger Meat]]
   - 20 [[Breadfruit]]
   - 20 [[Pepitas Seeds]]
   - 40 [[Corymbia Wood]]
   - *Note: All can be harvested without the [[W.H.O.O. Basic]] but low-adventure-level crafters are well-advised to use it.*
   - *Note: Though it's not in the journal, the narrative mentions [[Goldbeak]] having gone to [[Smeltfield Vale]], so might as well head that way while harvesting*
   - *Note: Preening Island is a great place to harvest as a low level since there's no aggro there.  Take the cloud at {{waypoint 148, 280, 866}} in Sanctuary to fly there.*
1. Find and harvest [[Goldbeak's Ore]].
   - This is a single node in [[Smeltfield Vale]] (entrance to Smeltfield is {{waypoint -548, 78, 382}}) around {{waypoint -821.79, 82.61, 382.37}}.
     - *Track Harvestables works, if you are not using the [[W.H.O.O. Basic]].* ![](images/Test_Your_Mettle_Where_quest_-_Goldbeak's_Ore.jpg)
   - [[Goldbeak]] will spawn, but is "confused" (so you don't get to speak to her yet)
1. Return to [[Hydona]].
1. Speak with [[Goldbeak]].

## Rewards

- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c 55407 status
- 7,317,049,865 TS exp @129
- +4,000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
