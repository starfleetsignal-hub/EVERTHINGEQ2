## Steps

1. Find a haft for the axe.
   - Kill undead monsters for this rare update. The following all update:
     - [[A diseased corpse|diseased]] and [[A restless corpse|restless corpses]]
     - [[An ire minion|ire minions]] and [[An ire warlock|warlocks]]
     - [[A tortured essence (Crypt of Betrayal)|tortured]] and [[An agonized essence|agonized essences]]
1. Acquire a head piece for the axe.
   - Kill [[A grotesque mass|grotesque masses]] around {{waypoint 59, 7, -59}}
1. Get the counterweight cinch.
   - Kill Bloodsabers. The following all update:
     - [[A Bloodsaber assassin|assassins]], [[A Bloodsaber highguard|highguards]], [[A Bloodsaber inquisitor|inquisitors]], [[A Bloodsaber medic (Crypt of Betrayal)|medics]], [[A Bloodsaber ritualist|ritualists]], [[A Bloodsaber ruffian|ruffians]], [[A Bloodsaber sentinel|sentinels]],
     - [[A crypt tempest|crypt tempests]] also update.
     - any undead listed above update as well.
