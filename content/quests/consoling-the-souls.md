## Steps

1. Travel to {{waypoint -321, 8, 408}} and fill the vial to receive [[Filled Ampoule of Absolution]].
   - You can fill the vial whilst hovering.
1. Find 5 souls, talk to them, and release them. Receive [[Essence of Souls]] after final soul.
   - {{waypoint -39, 13, 1013}} (Zivier's Reach) ![an entrapped spirit](images/An_entrapped_spirit.jpg)
   - {{waypoint 791, 63, 977}} [[Mage's Pebble]]
   - {{waypoint 1521, 128, -8}} Large rocky outcrop on the very edge of map (west cardinal point)
   - {{waypoint 756, 18, -772}} On a big rock on a rocky stretch of coast, just north of the [[Aetherscar]]
   - {{waypoint 45, 35, -1375}} To the right of large rocky outcrop on edge of map (North cardinal point)
1. Examine the [[Essence of Souls]], 'Hold the Element tightly and concentrate' to receive [[Attuned Essence of Souls]].
1. Return to [[Khat-Ra Pakiat]].

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Seeds of Deviation]]
