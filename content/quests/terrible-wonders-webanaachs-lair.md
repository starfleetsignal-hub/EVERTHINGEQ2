## Steps

1. Speak with [[Frau Choraline Lent]] {{waypoint -1128.28, -10.85, -464.36}}
   - Gather 5 drachnid webbing from Scourge Spire Nest - clickie around webbing; trackable
   - Gather 5 arachnid energy from Scourge Spire Nest - purple shiny; trackable
   - Gather 5 drachnid venom from Scourge Spire Nest - green shiny; trackable
1. Craft the *Essence of Webanaach*. Workbench. Requires 1 Formless Kindling
1. Find and enter the lair in Scourge Spire Nest. {{waypoint 303.83, 79.41, 74.13}}
1. Don the illusion Essence of Webanaach.
1. Speak to Quibble {{waypoint 524.22, 9.94, -26.34}}
1. Receive Recipe Scroll: Gooey Web Dissolver Solvent
   - Gather 5 dark nettle; trackable
   - Gather 5 aliphatic minerals; trackable
   - Gather 5 arachnid keytones; trackable
   - Webanaach is an epic who wanders the zone. He can see through the illusion.
1. Craft the Gooey Web Dissolver Solvent at the Tumultuous Mixing Hibiscus {{waypoint 469.52, 7.24, -6.95}} Requires 1 Formless Kindling.
1. Release [[Quibble]] and speak with him.
1. Speak with [[Frau Choraline Lent]] in [[Val'Zyroth Ness]].
