## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[A highwayman (Antonica)|highwaymen]] in [[Antonica]].
1. Return to the bookcase in [[Windstalker Village]] and inspect it. {{waypoint -1822, -38, -542}}
1. Search for *Motte's Lost Tomb* and inspect it {{waypoint -2177, 52, 940}}.
   - The tomb is located in [[Hidden Vale (Antonica)|Hidden Vale]]. When you get to the vale entrance {{waypoint -2050, -21, 695}}, follow the left wall around until you see a non-aggresive [[A den mother|den mother]] with her cubs.

## Rewards

- [[The Guard of Motte]]
- At least 11s 52c
