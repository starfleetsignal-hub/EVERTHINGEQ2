1. You need to be lvl 85 to be able to accept this quest.

## Steps

1. Light the [[Unlit Torch]] by clicking on the fire at {{waypoint -892, 483, 376}}.  You now have 1 min 30 sec to complete the next step.
1. Light the torches.
   - torch one is located at {{waypoint -804, 474, 477}}
   - Torch two is located at {{waypoint -725, 468, 444}}
   - Torch three is located at {{waypoint -726, 465, 382}}
   - Torch four is located at {{waypoint -669, 465, 357}}
   - Torch five is located at {{waypoint -631, 468, 322}}
   - Torch 6 is located at {{waypoint -598, 469, 343}}
1. Return to [[Felidael]].

## Rewards

- At least 35g 70s 61c
