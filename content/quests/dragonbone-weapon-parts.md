## Steps

![A "dragonbone stone", typically found against the inside of the dragon's ribcage](images/A_dragonbone_stone.jpg)

1. Complete the following tasks:
   - Slay 6 [[A dragonbone larvae|dragonbone larvae]] surrounding [[Jaled Dar's Remains]] for their shells. {{waypoint -1574, -367, -1541}}
   - Use the [[Cursecleaner claw]] on 5 *dragonbone stones* {{waypoint -1524, -371, -1512}}
     - These are small white stones found along the ribs of Jaled Dar's Remains. They are not [[Track Harvestables|trackabale]].
1. Return to Pudge the Brave.

## Rewards

- At least 36g 79s 48c
- +2,500 faction with **The Bellywhumpers**
- Access to [[Kurn's Tower]]
