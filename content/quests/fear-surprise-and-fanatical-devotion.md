## Steps

1. Enter [[Longshadow Alley: Daeneth Cloister]] and speak to [[A Nerian gate sentry]] {{waypoint 6, 3, 41}}
1. Speak to [[A Nerian questioner]] wandering near the gate {{waypoint 4, 2, 29}}
1. Kill a Nerian questioner (even level and con)
1. Kill two [[An enraged Nerian|enraged Nerians]] (from two levels less to even, one down to even)
1. Escape back to Freeport to escape the unending assault of enraged Nerians
1. Speak to Thesla

Note: The alley is too small to finish steps because nearby mobs are going to jam fighting. Quickly lure the questioner to the space between the gate and the fountain; it is wide enough not to disturb other mobs. The enraged Nerians will also follow you there.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g @ level 80
- [[An Ornate Freeport Chair]]
