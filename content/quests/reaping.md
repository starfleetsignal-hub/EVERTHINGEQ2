## Steps

1. Collect 5 spirits from the **pure spirits** using the [[Spirit Reaper]] {{waypoint -296, -43, 46}}.
1. Return to [[Laexyra Y'Barriath]]

## Rewards

- At least 92c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Brawler's Bandoleer]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Chipped Totem]] (All Mages)
  - [[Hunter's Shortbow]] (All Scouts, Channelers)
  - [[Protector's Shortbow]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Thug's Bandolier]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Worn Holy Symbol]] (Clerics, Druids, Shamans)
