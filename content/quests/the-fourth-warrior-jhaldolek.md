## Notes

- If at any point you lose or waste the [[Naptheric Paste (no-trade lore)|Naptheric Paste]], you can purchase another one from [[Juna Crankfizz]].

## Steps

1. Go to [[Steamfont Wetlands]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]]. {{waypoint -670, 75, 1475}}
1. Harvest [[Flamesalt]] from the mine nodes in the area (only one can be carried at a time).
1. Use Flamesalt while standing on one of the steam vents in this area. Equip the cloak and stand on top of one of the vents (it’s not geysers in the water, try all the cracks in the ground before you go into the water ( -680, 79, 1480 ) Either [[A fire elemental (Steamfont Mountains)|a fire elemental]] or [[Jhal'Dolek]] will spawn. If it is a fire elemental, kill it, mine another Firesalt, and try again. If it is Jhal'Dolek, use the [[Naptheric Paste (no-trade lore)|Naptheric Paste]], a fire animation will appear around you, and kill Jhal'Dolek.
   - If you kill Jha'Dolek without using the paste first, you will need to mine more Firesalt and try to spawn him again. He has no respawn timer.
   - Note: Be careful not to kill the named mob until the Cloak is consumed with flames. Killing it (by Harm Touch or similar 1-shot) before this message will not update.
1. Loot [[Heart of Jhal'Dolek]] from the chest that drops and use it.
   - It can be traded to other people in your group
1. The named mob drops a Heart. It seems that this needs to be applied to the cloak before leaving the zone. It may disappear from your bags if you do not apply it in Steamfont (?)
1. Return to [[Juna Crankfizz]].

## Rewards

Experience and AA experience only
