- *This quest is also part of the [[Ruins of Varsoon Timeline]].*
- *Adventurers formerly had to complete this questline in order to gain access to the instance, [[Cove of Decay]]. This is no longer required, however adventurers may still obtain and complete the quest series.*

## Steps

1. Obtain the key to *Cell Five* and *Cell Six*, dropped from [[Varsoon guard]]s that spawn in the same room.
   - To save time, try to accept the quest without clearing the room of the guards.
   - Guards in the north jail can also drop the keys. {{waypoint -259, 8, -145}}
1. Go back to [[Thana Rumblehoof]] to release them from their prison cell.
1. They will lead you back to the entrance. Follow them, or they wont move further.
   - **Note**: Be prepared for respawns on the way back to the entrance.
   - **Possible Bug**: Make sure you run behind them or the quest may bug near the entrance.
1. When you reach the entrance talk to [[Thana Rumblehoof]] again. She will inform you to seek out [[Chieftain Kraughl]] in the [[The Thundering Steppes]] {{waypoint 88, 0, 1384}}. Speaking to him will initiate the second quest in this series.

## Reward

- Experience only
