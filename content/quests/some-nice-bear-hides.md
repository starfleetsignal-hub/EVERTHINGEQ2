Danielle has a problem getting quality bear pelts. She would like you to bring her four preserved bear hides.

## Steps

1. Talk with [[Danielle Clothspinner]] {{waypoint 348.85, -22.50, -203.09}}.
1. Obtain four bear pelts from [[Antonica]].
1. Return to [[Danielle Clothspinner]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 4s 91c
- One of the following:
  - [[Danielle's Bearhide Shoulderpads]]
  - [[Danielle's Bearhide Mantle]]
  - [[Danielle's Bearhide Shawl]]
