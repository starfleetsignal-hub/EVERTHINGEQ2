## Steps

1. Go to [[Crushbone Keep]], proceed up the second floor and enter [[The Emperor's Chambers]]. Click on the chest at {{waypoint -23, 23, 10}} to retrieve the sword
1. Take the blade to [[The Witch of the Wood]] within [[Greater Faydark]]. {{waypoint -757, 85, 381}}
   - Important: You must speak [[The Patchwork Tapestry|Fayefolk]] in order to talk to her. This is not to be confused with Faerlie, which can be learned in Kelethin.
