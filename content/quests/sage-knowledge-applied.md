Velatha Zofriel recruited me to use my sage skills to help G.L.E.E.S.H. provide support to the Luclinites who are trying to repair and relocate to the city of Fordel Midst. Their need for parchment and resin is high.

## Steps

- Check the sages' crate near Velatha Zofriel to see what to make for the next shipment to Luclin. You can find the [[Solidified ethereal filament|solidified ethereal filament]] needed around Teren's Grasp.
  - Craft 6 rolls of parchment
  - Craft 6 bottles of resin
- Return to Velatha Zofriel to turn in.

## Rewards

- At least 11s 08c
- 2 [[G.L.E.E.S.H. Mark]]
