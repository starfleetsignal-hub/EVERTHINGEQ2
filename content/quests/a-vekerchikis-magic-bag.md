## Notes

Take the teleportor in the North Wing Stacks at {{waypoint 259, -20, -284}} to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]] and enter [[Vegarlson, the Earthen Badlands]] through the portal at {{waypoint 775, 412, -335}}.

## Steps

1. Obtain 10 potion bags from vekerchiki in [[Vegarlson, the Earthen Badlands]] near {{waypoint -87, 40, 117}}.
1. Speak to [[Assistant Tussle (Veglarson)|Assistant Tussle]] at {{waypoint -352, 42, -419}} in [[Vegarlson, the Earthen Badlands]].
