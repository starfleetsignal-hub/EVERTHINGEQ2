## Note

You should have reached +15,000 of the +40,000 faction you need, provided you have not worked for the other 2 factions while raising your faction with [[House Yrzu (Faction)|House Yrzu]]. Once you finish [[Furthering Education]] you should be at 21,000.  Please note that you can't fall below +20000 faction once you crossed that threshold.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Annuk’ka]] at {{waypoint -502, 51, 781}}. Defeat him when he attacks.
1. Speak to [[Annuk’ka]] again.
1. Speak to [[Elishuru]] at {{waypoint 176, 590, -576}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- +1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
