## Steps

1. Click on 3 crates and a barrel nearby {{waypoint 638, 352, 178}}. The third item examined will autoscribe [[Recipe for Improvised Armament]]; all four examinations will give you [[Supplies inventory list]]
1. Craft Recipe for Improvised Armament on the [[Engraved Desk]] {{waypoint 628, 352, 183}}
   - Requires 4 supplies inventory lists, 1 [[Leonid root]], 1 [[Cadmium ore]], and 1 [[Effulgent Incense]].
1. Speak to Quartermaster Robbins

## Rewards

- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]]
- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
