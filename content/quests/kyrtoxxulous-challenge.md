## Steps

1. *Seek out the mad slave to begin your test in order to unlock the real power of Death's Grip.*
   - Find [[A babbling reet slave]] in Fens of Nathsar standing on top of a big rock about halfway between Western and Eastern Pens, {{waypoint -984, -80, 588}}.
   - Hail him and listen to his cryptic babbling spoken to you in [[Froak]].
1. *To unlock the full potential of Death's Grip I will need to find the meaning of:*
   - "Sennao'kees anvilteah! Sennao'kees!" -- probably [anagram](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anagram) for "As none seek Leviathan! As none seek!"
     - [[The Leviathan]] in Chamber of Destiny
   - "Mayong Alga Dyed Nil! -- perhaps "Mayong Leading Lady!"
     - [[Tairiza the Widow Mistress]] in Tomb of Thuuga
   - "Teach Haven Wings!" -- probably anagram for "Watching Heavens!"
     - [[Travenro the Skygazer]] in Veeshan's Peak
   - Kill these mobs in no particular order for auto-updates.  Once all 3 are dead, you will automatically receive your mythical weapon as a quest reward.  The fabled warlock weapon from the previous quest will also be automatically destroyed at this point.
