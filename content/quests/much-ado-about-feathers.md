## Notes

- If you haven't unlocked flying in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]] yet, you can take the lift up to [[Raj'Durabad]], equip a safefall cloak (such as [[Cloak of the Harvester]] or a LON cloak), or use the Jann Magi illusion, etc., then jump over the edge to land on the mesas in [[Najami Oasis]] below. There are several locations for feathers atop the mesas.

## Steps

1. Find 6 peafowl feathers in [[Najami Oasis]] (*Not a guaranteed update!*). Some possible locations include: ![Molted Feathers](images/Molted_Feathers.png)
   - {{waypoint -177.47, 51.63, -16.83}} -- around this area are plenty of them
   - {{waypoint 32.37, 6.98, 30.05}}
   - {{waypoint 52.54, 13.16, -61.83}}
   - {{waypoint 51.50, 4.35, -141.31}}
   - {{waypoint 109.85, 9.87, -206.67}}
   - {{waypoint -54.14, 9.73, -176.95}}
   - {{waypoint -102.54, 19.30, -112.90}}
   - {{waypoint -99.34, 32.47, -21.03}}
   - {{waypoint -172.10, 43.35, 107.99}}
   - {{waypoint -172.22, 52.25, 14.70}}
   - {{waypoint -30.62, 48.83, -65.29}}
   - {{waypoint -18.47, 44.80, -95.16}}
1. Search for **a peafowl**
   - *Not trackable!* it is static, does not move, is tab target-able. ![a peafowl](images/A_peafowl.png)
   1. first loc: {{waypoint -180, 51, -19}}![Catch peafowl](images/Catch_peafowl.jpg)
   1. Continue searching for the peafowl when it runs towards the Hanging Gardens {{waypoint -104.74, 24.26, -220.34}}
   1. Continue searching for the peafowl when it runs off again deep into the crags of Najami Oasis {{waypoint -249.41, 61.40, -84.88}}
1. Collect 3 **Peafowl Nests**. Some possible locations include: ![Peafowl Nest](images/Peafowl_Nest.png)
   - {{waypoint -249.41, 61.40, -84.88}}
   - {{waypoint -117.07, 27.74, -114.50}}
   - {{waypoint -96.82, 11.27, -235.32}}
   - {{waypoint -184.76, 51.93, -0.53}}
   - {{waypoint -108.66, 29.19, -31.63}}
1. Return to [[Saida]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- +1,000 faction with **Hizite Nomads of Ro**
