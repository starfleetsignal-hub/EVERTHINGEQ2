## Steps

1. Recover the pieces of the marker from the [[Ring War Battlefield]].
   - These will be clickable snow chunks strewn across the battlefield.
1. Return to [[Jelgith Heartwood|Jelgith]] {{waypoint 109.79, -345.48, -621.87}}.
1. Search the Ry'Gorr ruins to the south east
   1. Investigate the [[Ry'Gorr Ruins]]  ![Click on the scroll](images/Point_of_Attacks_quest_-_scroll.jpg)
   1. Click on the scroll at {{waypoint -707, -358, 239}}.
   - Very close to spawn point for [[Poxbreath of the Yellowfang]]
1. Return to [[Jelgith Heartwood|Jelgith]] {{waypoint 109.79, -345.48, -621.87}}.
1. Travel to the [[Goahmari Village]].
1. Speak with [[Tellpa]] {{waypoint 362.07, -15.02, 541.27}}
1. Seek out 3 [[A Ry'Gorr Mountaineer|Ry'Gorr Mountaineers]] located at the following locations (you have to kill the whole group once to update the quest ):Note-> you can kill the group move away a little and come right back to kill again for all 3 updates.
   - {{waypoint 166, -17, 446}}
   - {{waypoint 186, -7, 347}}
   - {{waypoint -103, -141, 648}}
   - If you are on the right step in the quest, they will spawn as you approach, usually as a pair of 88v orcs so you dont need to run at all 3 points just be on one
1. Speak with Tellpa again.
1. Speak with Jelgith.{{waypoint 109.79, -345.48, -621.87}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
