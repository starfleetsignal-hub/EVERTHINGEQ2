## Steps

1. Go to the [[Temple of Solusek Ro]] in [[Lavastorm]] {{waypoint -329, 1, -487}} and speak with [[Marathan Allim]]. {{waypoint -430, -61, -662}}
1. Defeat four waves of [[A construct of Ro|constructs of Ro]].
   - If you back up to the wall in the library, you can control when you start fighting; the constructs' aggro radius is not very large.
1. Speak to Marathan again. He will despawn and reappear as a non-attackable NPC that follows you.
1. Lead Marathan to the [[Torch of Ro]]. {{waypoint -110, 20, 75}}
   - You can use your mount to get there quickly. Don't worry if Marathan runs away or despawns. He will reappear once you've remained still on the ground for several seconds.
1. Return to Civean Il'Pernod.

## Rewards

- 5000 faction with **Solusek Ro**
- Deity Pet: [[Minion of Ro]]
