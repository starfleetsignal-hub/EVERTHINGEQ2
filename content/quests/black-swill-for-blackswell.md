## Steps

1. Tellar Blackswell needs Goroywn Pepper Swill from Eurkon Al'ouz, a bartender who can be found in the city of Gorowyn, in Timorous Deep.
   - Buy a Gorowyn Pepper Swill for 1s 20c from [[Eurkon Al'ouz]] {{waypoint 2600, 87, 1326}} in [[Timorous Deep]]
1. Return to Tellar Blackswell who will give you [[The Book of Bound Mithril]].
1. The book Tellar Blackswell gave me is quite peculiar.
   - Examine [[The Book of Bound Mithril]]
1. Maybe Brell's Prophet in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] knowns someone who can read the archaic dwarvish language.
   - Speak with [[Steinsister Daglara]] {{waypoint 336, 189, 502}} in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
