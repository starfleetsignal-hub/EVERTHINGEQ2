## Steps

1. Talk to Kwa. Find out there is trouble afoot. Agree to get bog water spirits to vanquish his thirst.  He hands you a coin to pay for the drinks.
1. Head east to the [[Hole in the Wall Tavern]].  Be sure to discover [[The Fountain of Valor]] {{waypoint 771, -17, -114}} on your way.  Talk to [[Waitress Brooak]] {{waypoint 740, -21, -111}}.  She takes the coin and gives you bog water spirits.
1. Return to Kwa.  He takes the spirits and rewards you.

## Rewards

- 1 silver and 3 copper
