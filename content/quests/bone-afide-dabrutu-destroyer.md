## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill and destroy the following
   - Kill 8 [[A vicious dabrutu|vicious dabrutus]] at [[Mordant Spires]] {{waypoint 483, 29, -226}}, then *right-click and 'destroy** the **Dabrutu Skull**.![Dabrutu Skull](images/Dabrutu_Skull.png)
   - Destroy 6 **animal bones** at [[Mordant Spires]] around {{waypoint 505.31, 29.59, -354.65}} ![Animal Bones](images/Animal_Bones.png)
   - Kill 6 [[A dabrutu enchanter|dabrutu enchanters]] at [[Mordant Spires]] around {{waypoint 269.65, 4.56, -31.85}}
1. Return to [[Dulamah]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
  - +1,000 faction with **Hizite Nomads of Ro**
- One of the following:
  - [[Dabrutu Ram-horned Skull]]
  - [[Dabrutu Oxen-Horned Skull]]
