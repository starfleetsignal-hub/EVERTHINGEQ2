## Steps

1. Craft 10 [[Improved Barricades]]: 10 cadmium ore; 10 leonid root ; 20 larix lumber ; 50 effulgent coal. <br>NOTE: These items cannot be multi-crafted.  They must be crafted individually.
1. Deliver barricades to [[Arms Master Ryan]] at {{waypoint 689, 351, 67}} (Click on the green dot)
1. Return to Quartermaster Robbins at {{waypoint 625, 351, 178}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +2000 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- 15 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
