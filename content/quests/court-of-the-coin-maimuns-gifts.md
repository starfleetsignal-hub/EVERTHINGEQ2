## Steps

1. Enter [[A Young Nobleman's Residence]], located up a climbable wall in [[The Gilded Twilight Terrace]] at {{waypoint 104, 151, 134}}. Once inside interact with the music box in the back room to replace it.
1. Return to [[Maimun Kazuhla]].
1. Complete the following tasks, in any order:
   - Harvest [[A Tunarian flower]] in either [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint 125, -4, -63}} or [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 403, -20, -229}}.
     - West Freeport on Origins Server
   - Kill crocodiles in [[The Sinking Sands]] for an update.
   - Kill [[Lady Samiel]] in the Sinking Sands; see monster article for direction on how to spawn her.
1. Return to Maimun Kazuhla.
1. Speak to [[Ayala (Named)|Ayala]] in [[Maj'Dul]], who will spawn at {{waypoint -203, 150, -10}}.
1. Wait a minute or two and speak to her again.
1. Return to Maimun Kazuhla.
