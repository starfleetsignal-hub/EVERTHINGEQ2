## Steps

1. Gather warden bulbs from [[Druid Grove]], located in [[Shadow Haven]] section of the [[City of Fordel Midst]].
   - *Look in the grassy area around {{waypoint -669.77, 0.65, -48.00}}*
1. Return and speak to Dasina Wester in the Nexus at {{waypoint 200, -36, 816}}
1. Plant the warden bulbs at the base of each of the Combine Spires in the [[Luclin Nexus]] *(click the sparkling green areas):*
   - {{waypoint 215.55, -36.15, 821.65}}
   - {{waypoint 306.30, -36.15, 825.98}}
   - {{waypoint 310.59, -36.15, 735.31}}
   - {{waypoint 219.76, -36.15, 729.30}}
1. Return and speak to Dasina Wester.

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- [[House of Midst Pearl]]
