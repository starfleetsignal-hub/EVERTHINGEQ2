## Steps

1. Seek out [[The projection of Tunare]] in the [[Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint -208, 42, 417}}.
1. Speak to [[The projection of Cazic-Thule]] in [[Shard of Fear]] {{waypoint 88, 5, 376}}. Note that the mobs within the zone will be gray at level 90, allowing you to proceed to Cazic undetected.
1. Return to [[The Duality]] in Thurgadin. {{waypoint 475.38, -211.64, 245.68}}
1. Speak with the Children of War. Each is in a special instance, accessed through their normal door in Drunder. (For Vallon's Retreat, you must NOT be in a group.  You may be grouped for easy access for Tallon's and Sullon's.
   - [[Sullon Zek (NPC)]], in [[Sullon's Sanctum]] Access using Spire of Rage door
   - [[Tallon Zek (NPC)]], in [[Tallon's Cloister]] Access using Strategist's Stronghold door
   - [[Vallon Zek (NPC)]], in [[Vallon's Retreat]] Access using Tower of Tactics door
1. Speak to [[Erus Dal'viv]] in [[The Fortress of Drunder]] {{waypoint 9.71, 0.57, 4.14}}
1. Gather the materials needed to create the temper from within [[Elements of War]]:
   - 6 Endless Battlefield Soil, a ground-spawn.
   - 6 Essence of War, an auto-update from trash within the zone.
   - The blood of [[Decorin Bonewing]], an auto-update upon his death.
1. Take the ingredients to [[Grelik Velgorg]] on the lower level in [[The Fortress of Drunder]].
1. Return to [[Erus Dal'viv]] on the upper level.
