## Notes

- This quest will enable you to understand the language of the Aviaks: [[Volant]].
- Upon completion of this quest, more quests will be available to you throughout [[The Barren Sky]].

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Polly]] in [[Windgazer's Hideout]]. {{waypoint -655, 8, -518}}
   - If you don't have a mount to reach her, climb up the wall at {{waypoint -584, -48, -450}}.
1. Toss the note off the cliff by going to the edge of the cliff and receive an auto-update. {{waypoint -491, -75, -407}}
1. Speak to [[Turk Whooyip]].
1. Harvest 5 **cactusarii zephryi** fruit, from near {{waypoint -788, -70.42, -512.35}}.
   - The node looks like a regional bush node, is only harvestable during this quest, and is trackable.
1. Speak to Polly.
   - Correct answer: "... whoo ooh?"
1. Speak to Turk Whooyip. Correct answers:
   - "Vultak"
   - "... who ah uh uh huh."
