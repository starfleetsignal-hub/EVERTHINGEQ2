## Steps

- Talk to [[Researcher Kendril]] in the [[Academy of Arcane Science]], North Freeport.
- He will then teleport you to the citadel.
- Hail Tallen Yevix and follow her around as she dispells the illusion and collects Soulfire.
  - Alternately just stand near the entrance.

- [[Replica: Soulfire]]
