This quest is part 14 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Slay [[Scout Captain Underwood]] (level 27). {{waypoint -270, 0, -282}}
   - Found at the same Qeynosian camp at Behemoth Pond that you visited previously for Jaeta.
   - Spawns as soon as you approach the camp, and attacks you.
   - The Qeynosians do not assist him.
1. Return to [[Jaeta H'arn]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 30s 78c
- One of the following, depending on Class:
  - [[Darkened Treant Wood Circlet]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Engraved Treant Wood Circlet]] (All Mages)
  - [[Polished Treant Wood Circlet]] (All Priests)
  - [[Stricken Treant Wood Circlet]] (All Fighters)
