## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 60+

## Steps

1. Locate [[Lachlyn Cauldthorn (The Down Below)|Lachlyn Cauldthorn]] in [[The Down Below]]. {{waypoint 6, 0, -67}}
   - Slay 3 ambushing [[A Bloodsaber enforcer|Bloodsaber enforcers]].
   - Speak to [[Lachlyn Cauldthorn (The Down Below)|Lachlyn]] to learn what he has discovered.
1. Follow up on the Bloodsabers' plot to poison the aquaducts in [[Antonica]]. At each stop, you must perform two actions: a. slay the attacking [[A Bloodsaber poxreaper|Bloodsaber poxreapers]] and b. investigate the water supply.
   - Check the aquaduct section nearest the city. {{waypoint -239, 7, -118}}
   - Check the midsection of the aquaduct. {{waypoint -743, 43, -178}}
   - Check the the aquaduct near the [[Glade of the Coven]]. {{waypoint -1333, 20, -230}}
1. Report back to [[Lachlyn Cauldthorn (Qeynos Province District)|Lachlyn Cauldthorn]] in [[The Elddar Grove (POI)|the Elddar Grove]] at the base of [[Arbos, the Elddar Tree]]. {{waypoint 655, -10, -384}}
1. Speak with the druid alchemist [[Kelia Spiritward]] by the bridge. {{waypoint 720, -19, -419}}
   - Travel to [[Oakmyst Forest]] and gather 8 Palemoon Lillies. You will need to use the Mariner's Bell {{waypoint 730, -19, -370}} to get there. ![A palemoon lily flower in the Oakmyst Forest](images/Palemoon_Lily.jpg)
   - Return to Kelia Spiritward in the Elddar Grove who will turn the Palemoon Lilies into [[Palemoon Antidote|Palemoon Antidotes]].
   - Cure 4 [[A sickened citizen|sickened citizens]] (scattered around Elddar Grove) with the [[Palemoon Antidote|Palemoon Antidotes]].
1. Return to [[Lachlyn Cauldthorn (Qeynos Province District)|Lachlyn Cauldthorn]] in [[The Elddar Grove (POI)|the Elddar Grove]].
1. Return to [[Queen Antonia Bayle (Queen Antonia's Chambers)|Queen Antonia]].
