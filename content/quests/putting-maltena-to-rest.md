## Steps

1. Talk to [[Valten Darkcleanse]] outside [[Nektropos Castle]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] at {{waypoint -1372, 122, -1853}}.
1. Visit 4 graves in various parts of [[Norrath]]:
   - [[Enchanted Lands]]: {{waypoint -411, 26, -610}}
   - [[Lavastorm]]: {{waypoint -736, -111, 445}}
   - [[Everfrost]]: {{waypoint 92, 23, -661}} - *well hidden between two rocks and on ground level*
   - [[Feerrott]]: {{waypoint -1048, 4, 862}}
1. Return to [[Valten Darkcleanse]] outside Nektropos Castle to finish the quest.

## Rewards

- Access to [[Nektropos Castle: The Return]]
