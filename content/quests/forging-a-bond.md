## Steps

1. Gather three staves from behind Askr. {{waypoint 2335, 38, 1055}}
   - 5-minute respawn for the staves if someone else has harvested them.
1. Go the [[The Thundering Steppes]] and examine the *Altar of Storms* to place the *Stave of Storms*. {{waypoint -464, -23, -157}}best place to fast travel is by the docks
1. Defeat the [[A lightning elemental|lightning elemental]] that spawns.
1. Pick up the charged *Stave of Storms* next to the altar.
1. Go to [[Greater Faydark]] and examine the *Altar of Rain* to place the *Rod of Rains*. {{waypoint -837, 93, -60}}
1. Defeat the [[A water elemental (Greater Faydark)|water elemental]] that spawns.
1. Pick up the charged *Rod of Rains* next to the altar.
1. Go to [[Maj'Dul]] and examine the *Altar of Cloud* to place the *Cane of Clouds*. {{waypoint -188, 177, -87}}
1. Defeat the [[A cloud elemental|cloud elemental]] that spawns.
1. Pick up the charged *Cane of Clouds* next to the altar.
1. Return to [[Askr]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s
- +1250 faction with **Karana**
- [[Humble Altar of Karana]]
