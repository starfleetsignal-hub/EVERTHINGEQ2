## Notes

- You need to know [[Volant]] and be a level 64 or higher adventurer in order to get this quest.

## Steps

![](images/Hiding_bush.jpg)![](images/Hiding_stone.jpg)

1. Find three hiding Hoo'luks by inspecting bush and rock harvest nodes.
   - Right click these unnamed nodes and select "investigate." The spawns seem to be random, like harvestables, and are found primarily on the north side of the Island.
1. Return to [[Duf'hee Stormtalon|Duf'hee]].

## Rewards

- At least 9g 85s 50c
