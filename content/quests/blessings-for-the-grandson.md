## Steps

1. Go south at the intersection {{waypoint 677, -249, 133}} to go up and wind around to Brell's temple. Click the green sparkle at {{waypoint 490, -214, 258}}. This is upstairs. The same loc, except for altitude, takes you someplace else.
   - NOTE! Don't hurry out of the Temple just yet. Speak to Alfrig along the south side of the temple for the next quest in this series.

## Rewards

- [[Coldain Cooking Utensils]]
- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
