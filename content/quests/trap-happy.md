## Steps

1. Craft a [[Shellfish Trap|Modified Shellfish Trap for Arctic Climates]]
   - You will need 1 [[Titanium ore]], 2 [[Quicksilver cluster]], 2 [[Sumac lumber]], and 1 [[Ethereal sandpaper]]
   - You can use the [[Woodworking Table]] located behind [[Matthew]] {{waypoint -1642, -533, -2380}} and he will also sell you fuel.
1. Place the trap at {{waypoint -1251, -539, -2196}}.  There is a green sparkly at the location but it is very hard to see.  You can complete the quest even if you can't see it because if you are standing at the right location (next to the tracks, on the opposite site of the rock), when you move your mouse around, there will be a place where you get a brown popup box which says "Convenient Site.  This would be a good location to place the monster trap."  I tried a variety of display options (Balanced/High Performance/Very High Performance/Extreme Performance), and I could only see it on Extreme Performance. Disabling Bloom Effects under "Water and Atmospheric Effects" can also help in some cases.
1. Return to [[Misa]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
