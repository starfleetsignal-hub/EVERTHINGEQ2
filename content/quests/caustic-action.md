## Steps

1. Obtain 8 poison samples from [[Mazkeen witch doctor]]s found in both the Mazkeen camps {{waypoint -730, 140, 860}} and {{waypoint -700, 130, 600}}
   - [[Mazkeen savage]]s are placeholders
1. Return to [[Hodgemottin]]

## Reward

- At least 53s 57c
- [[Hodgemottin's Oxidizer]]
- A bracelet, based on class:
  - [[Cog Constable's Circlet]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Cog Combatant's Circlet]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Cog Conjuror's Circlet]]
  - [[Cog Caller's Circlet]]
