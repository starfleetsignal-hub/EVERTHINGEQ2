## Steps

NOTE:  You cannot receive this quest unless you speak to questgiver next to flightmaster in Great Divide / Thurg Harbor and are on the quest [[The Lands Above]]

- You receive [[Healing salve of Nife]]
1. Find 6 injured soldiers and apply the salve.  The soldiers show up on the map as quest updates and are easy to find.
1. Return to Field Marshall Vishra

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
