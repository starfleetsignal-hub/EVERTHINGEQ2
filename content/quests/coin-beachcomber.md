*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Kill [[A dervish beachcomber|dervish beachcombers]] until you have 5 updates for crates.
1. Gather 10 crates on the beach. They can be found on the beaches north and south of the quest giver. They are usually found near the water's edge.
1. Return to [[Thara Bashirah]].

## Rewards

- At least 2g 99s 47c
- -2500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -2500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- 2500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
