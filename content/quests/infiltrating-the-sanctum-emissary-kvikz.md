## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks, in no particular order:
   - Kill [[Portal Guard Graliz]] in the [[Mouth of the Sanctum]]. {{waypoint 11, 156, -71}}
   - Place first crystal in the [[Ready Room]] - behind flower pot north of the east entrance to the room {{waypoint 150, 158, -94}}
   - Place second crystal in the Ready Room - west side of the north entrance {{waypoint 176, 157, -103}}
   - Place third crystal in the Ready Room - southwest corner of the room, left side of pillar {{waypoint 198, 158, -87}}
   - Place fourth crystal in the Ready Room - next to the second water bowl in from the east side {{waypoint 188, 157, -54}}
   - Find written orders on one of the Scaleborn warriors in the Ready Room. KIll a [[A Scaleborn Warrior]] for auto-update.
1. Return to Kvikz at (2, 135, -231) inside the sanctum at the exit to the Tangle.
1. Complete the following tasks, in no particular order:
   - Kill [[A Scaleborn animator]] or [[Sslan'yiz the Animator]] {{waypoint -140, 135, 29}}
   - Kill [[A sentinel builder]] {{waypoint -140, 135, 29}}
   - Recover and replace the first crystal in the [[Oratorium]] {{waypoint 22, 136, 58}}
   - Recover and replace the fourth crystal in the Oratorium {{waypoint -13, 136, -13}}
   - Recover and replace the third crystal in the Oratorium {{waypoint 19, 141, 44}}
   - Recover and replace the second crystal in the Oratorium {{waypoint 19, 136, 3}}
   - Recover and replace the first crystal in the [[Scaleborn Barracks]] {{waypoint 129, 136, 48}}
   - Recover and replace the second crystal in the Scaleborn Barracks {{waypoint 178, 136, 27}}
   - Recover and replace the third crystal in the Scaleborn Barracks {{waypoint 188, 136, 78}}
   - Recover and replace the fourth crystal in the Scaleborn Barracks {{waypoint 158, 136, 71}}
1. Return to Kvikz.

## Rewards

- [[Deactivated Listening Crystal]] (house item)
- At least 13g 59s 50c
