## Steps

1. Travel to [[Loping Plains]]
   - Gather 4 **Somborn Grave Dust** in [[Somborn Cemetery]] around {{waypoint -431.82, 14.64, 51.44}}.![Somborn Grave Dust](images/Somborn_Grave_Dust.webp)
   - Gather 4 **Timorous Ghostgrass** near [[The Timorous Moor]] (Mount) Station at {{waypoint 59.51, 61.84, 258.56}}. ![Timorous Ghostgrass](images/Timorous_Ghostgrass.webp)
   - Gather 4 **Mistmoore Gargoyle Gravel** near the exit to Lesser Faydark around {{waypoint 789.92, 71.66, -122.77}}.![Mistmoore Gargoyle Gravel](images/Mistmoore_Gargoyle_Gravel.webp)
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in Sundered Frontier
