## Steps

1. Kill 12 [[A Scourge warmage|Scourge warmages]] and [[A Scourge summoner|Scourge summoners]]. Good area: [[Vallon Field]] at {{waypoint 601, 16, 0}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 41g 75s 13,200 status
- +250 faction with **The Green Hoods**
