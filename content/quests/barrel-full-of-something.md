## Description

Various "cures" have been introduced over the years in an attempt to help save the cursed frogloks lost in the lost city of Guk. Generally, these are applied to the single stragglers that somehow manage to find their way out of the ruins, and all of the attempts have met with failure. The alchemists and sages would like to try on a broader base of subjects, and are asking adventurers to pour the vials of possible cures into the mix of food supplies for the frogloks. The Outer Stronghold of Ykesha is likely the best place to attempt such a thing, as it seems to be more structured than the rest of the ruins. I should seek out the barrels of froglok food, and add the cure to them.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Ruins of Guk: Ykesha's Outer Stronghold]]
1. Find 6 barrels of froglok food, and use the received *[[Vial of bubbling liquid]]* on them.
1. Kill [[Lunchmeat]] and [[Ferhustr Frupdor]] for update
1. Return to Vegupa for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
