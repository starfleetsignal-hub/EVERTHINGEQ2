## Steps

1. Obtain 4 [[Intricate Strap Buckles]] from  (flying clockworks).![Intricate Strap Buckles](images/Questitem_IntricateStrapBuckles.jpg)
1. Obtain 4 [[Highly Magical Straps]] from [[Fire Giants]], which are located beyond [[Pyreduke Surtaug (Heroic)]].![Highly Magical Straps](images/Questitem_HighlyMagicalStraps.jpg)
1. Obtain 2 [[Flame Galvanized Hooks]] from [[Scald (Heroic)|Scald]].![Flame Galvanized Hook](images/Questitem_FlameGalvanizedHook.jpg)
   - **Note:** Unfortunately, [[Scald (Heroic)|Scald]] drops only one hook per defeat, leaving the zone clear. As a result, you have to do a complete zone run two times to get your hooks.
   - **Note:** Fortunately the hook is a group-wide update, so everyone who is on that quest step will receive one update...
1. Go back to the **Redwinger's Metallurgic Table of Wonder** at {{waypoint -279, -516, 142}} (room of [[Ayquini the Firemind (Heroic)]]) and double-click it for your quest update.
