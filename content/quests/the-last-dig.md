## Steps

1. Kill any Sabertooth gnolls in The Thundering Steppes for an auto-update.
   - Sabertooth gnolls can be found in the craters in the northern part of the zone and [[The Sabertooth Den]] at {{waypoint 1330, 1, -329}}.
   - Can take over 20 kills to update.

## Rewards

- Experience only
