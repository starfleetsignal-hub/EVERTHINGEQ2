## Steps

1. Travel to [[Grubbin's Fishing Retreat]]. {{waypoint -678, -1, -298}}
1. Kill 8 [[A peculiar chest|peculiar chest]]s in [[Roundbelly's General Store]]. {{waypoint -202, -8, 100}}
   - They alternate spawning with [[A corrupted merchant|corrupted merchant]]s and [[A corrupted brewer|corrupted brewer]]s
   - There are only two to four up at a time and this is the only location in Rivervale for them to spawn. Their respawn time is about 10 minutes.
1. Kill a nightblood in Rivervale.

## Rewards

- Experience only
