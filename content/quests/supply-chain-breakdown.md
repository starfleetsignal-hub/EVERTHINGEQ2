## Steps

*Ranger Alphin is concerned that the gnolls are far more organized than they have been in the past. They could prove to be more of a threat than anyone is giving them credit for. So, he asked me to sabotage the camps by destroying gnoll weapons and supplies while I was assisting the refugees in the Darkpaw Warrens. He hopes this will help weaken the gnoll's overall strength and diminish their organization.*

1. Destroy 15 gnoll weapon caches and supplies in Darkpaw Warrens
   - The weapon caches and food stores are individual updates only
   - Both weapon caches and food stores respawn in a locked instance (confirmed after 2 hours, although it may be less)
1. Speak with Ranger Alphin inside Darkpaw Warrens {{waypoint -28, -43, -253}}
