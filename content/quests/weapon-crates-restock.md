## Description

The Castle Highhold defenders are running low on spare weapons. Quartermaster Robbins would like me to craft some more at the forge, then refill the weapons racks.

## Steps

1. Craft 10 spare [[Defenders' Swords]]
1. Place the spare weapons in the empty crates at {{waypoint 680, 352, 78}}
1. Return to Quartermaster Robbins at {{waypoint 625, 351, 178}}

## Required Resources

1. Ioducus Cluster x10
1. Cadmium Ore x10
1. Larix Lumber x20
1. Effulgent Coal x50

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
- 15 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- +2000 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
