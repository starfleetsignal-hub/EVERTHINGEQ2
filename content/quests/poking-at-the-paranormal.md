## Steps

1. Collect 5 [[War-torn ghost essence|War-torn ghost essences]] from [[A war-torn apparition|war-torn apparitions]] inside [[The Shadowed Grove]] near {{waypoint 371, -29, 450}}.
1. Return to Annelo

## Rewards

- At least 22g 76s 65c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Conjuration of Storms]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Cowardly Sphere of Reluctance]]
  - [[Orb of Metal Barriers]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sphere of Titan Summoning]]
