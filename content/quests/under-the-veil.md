## Steps

![Daar geode and Daar shrub](images/Daar_Geode_and_Shrub.png‎)

1. Speak to [[Charkal]] on Daarspire {{waypoint -191, 270, -16}}
1. Speak with [[Yelinak the Ancient (Vesspyr Isles)|Yelinak the Ancient]]   {{waypoint 81, 219, 115}}
1. Speak with [[Vizzeren]] {{waypoint 15, 195, 2}}
1. Gather potion ingredients for Vizzeren, around {{waypoint 150, 157, -39}}
   - 10 pollinating Daar shrubs
   - 8 glowing Daar geodes
1. Return to Vizzeren - he gives you [[Vizzeren's Arcanum Vision Elixir]]
1. Meet Vishra on the highest plateau of [[Veiled Thessk]] {{waypoint -749, 40, -178}} (quest auto-updates when you get near him)
1. Drink [[Vizzeren's Arcanum Vision Elixir]]
1. Confront [[Darathar (NPC)|Darathar]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Scepter of the Vesspyrian Spellweaver]] (All Mages, All Priests)
  - [[Vesspyrian Defender]] (All Priests)
  - [[Vesspyrian Grand Malleus]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Vesspyrian Tower Defender]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Vesspyrian Stiletto]] (All Scouts)
