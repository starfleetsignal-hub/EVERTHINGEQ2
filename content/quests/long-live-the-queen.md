## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 40+

## Steps

1. Speak with the high elf [[Val'si Summergold]], in front of the [[Lion's Mane Inn]] in [[South Qeynos]]. {{waypoint 542, -10, 143}}
1. Speak with ale-loving [[Darcnir]] inside the [[Lion's Mane Inn]]. {{waypoint 527, -9, 129}}
   - Purchase 1 of Darcnir's favorite, [[Kazon's Rum Barrel Red Ale]] from Bartender [[Kayben Eatny]].  {{waypoint 584, -8, 130}}
   - Return to Darcnir and feed him ale until he talks.
1. Find Darcnir's contacts.
   - Go to the docks behind the [[Lion's Mane Inn]].
   - Slay the two [[An aggressive plotter|aggressive plotters]] that will ambush you.
   - Pick up a note. {{waypoint 543, -27, 106}}
1. Return to [[Field Marshal Vishra]].

## Rewards

- At least 25g 70s 61c (at level 92)
