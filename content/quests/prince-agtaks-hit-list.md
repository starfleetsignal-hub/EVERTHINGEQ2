## Steps

![A Message From Prince Agtak's Father.](images/Prince_Agtak's_Chest.jpg)

1. Kill [[Chief Torturer Goortz]] to open the iron maiden-secret-door. {{waypoint -16, -10, -15}}
1. Speak to [[Agtak]] {{waypoint -7, -10, -45}} behind the iron maiden.
1. Kill the following mobs:
   - [[Prince Gluntok]] {{waypoint 19, 8, 32}}
   - [[Prince Valtoris]] {{waypoint -11, 3, 0}}
   - [[Overseer Sultok]] {{waypoint -6, 1, 21}}
   - [[Emperor Fyst]] {{waypoint 1, 16, 9}}
1. Return to [[The Deathfist Jail]] and speak to Agtak.
1. When the conversation is over, kill the [[A Nublood henchman (Deathfist Citadel)|Nublood henchmen]] that spawn. Talk to Agtak again.
1. Click the chest at {{waypoint 9, 9, 28}} in [[Prince Gluntok's Chambers]] to receive your reward and a message from Prince Agtak's father.
