## Steps

1. Travel to [[Kunzar Jungle]] and use the [[Catalogue Creature]] ability to catalog:
   - Catalog [[A trakaraptor]] in [[Trakaraptor Alley]] around {{waypoint -638, -65, 443}}
   - Catalog [[A Sebilite guardian]] in [[Outer Sebilis]] near the gate to [[Sebilis]] around {{waypoint -479, -121, 956}}
   - Catalog [[A sporconid redcap]] at either [[Doom's Morass]] near {{waypoint 70, -119, 1004}} or outside of [[Outer Sebilis]] near {{waypoint 226, -123, 814}}
   - Catalog [[A sporconid cultivator]] at either [[Doom's Morass]] near {{waypoint 70, -119, 1004}} or outside of [[Outer Sebilis]] near {{waypoint 226, -123, 814}}
   - Catalog [[A dragon drool leech]] outside [[Outer Sebilis]] at {{waypoint 67.5, -126.91, 756.27}}
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in Sundered Frontier

## Hints

The creatures are trackable.  If you don't have tracking, many ranger mercs can track, for instance [[Villax Sned]] from the VoV expansion can track.

## Rewards

- Unlock the Days of Summer 2022: Week 7 Holiday event rewards that you can purchase from [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2293, -199, 2506}}
