## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The avatar summoning rituals require the manipulation of immense power and are inherently dangerous. To reduce the chance of failure and provide an anchor for that power, the herald of Solusek Ro has asked me to collect an item that may have once belonged to that god or one of their most loyal followers

- Collect [[Reliquary of Solusek Ro|Reliquaries of Solusek Ro]] from a powerful creature in Vaashkaani: Argent Sanctuary heroic dungeon.

**This does not update in [Solo]. It needs to be Vaashkaani: Argent Sanctuary [Heroic I]**

- Return to the [[Herald of the Burning Prince]] at {{waypoint -772, -129, -368}} in [[Lavastorm]]
