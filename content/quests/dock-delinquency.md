## Steps

1. Help the dockmaster by kill the following indigenous creatures, all found immediately north of the docks:
   - 4 [[A rustfiend|rustfiends]]. {{waypoint 420, 28, 514}}
   - 6 [[A coastal basilisk|coastal basilisks]]. {{waypoint 512, 25, 564}}
   - 6 [[Aqua goblin runt|aqua goblin runts]]. {{waypoint 473, 35, -271}}
1. Return to [[Dockmaster Waulon]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 87s 4c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Butcherblock Parrying Blade]] (All Mages)
  - [[Chakram of the Swift]] (Beastlord, Bruiser, Monk}
  - [[Mystic Lotuswand]] (All Mages)
  - [[Plain Jambiya]] (non-beastlord Scouts)
  - [[Pronged Mace of the Eagle]] (All Priests)
  - [[Runic Blade of the Rain]] (Fury, Warden)
  - [[Squire's Blade (Level 20)]] (Plate Fighters)
