## Steps

1. Take readings from around Malvonicus' Tower for Shortspanner - left-click the white sparkles at the following /locations![Reality Reading location](images/Reality_Reading_location.jpg)
   - NOTE: This is **NOT** group-wide update.
   - {{waypoint 913, 23, 1284}}
   - {{waypoint 906, 7, 1385}}
   - {{waypoint 912, 85, 1370}} (on top of the exploded tower, use a flying or jumping mount to reach it)
   - {{waypoint 818, 23, 1379}}
1. Take readings from 8 twisted creatures in Malvonicus' Folly - use your [[Reality Decompiler]] on any 'twisted' monster ([[A twisted hominid|twisted hominids]], [[A twisted Far Seas crewmember|twisted Far Seas crewmembers]], [[A twisted sentience|twisted sentiences]], etc...).  The quest updates as soon as you do, but the monster you targeted will immediately attack.  (NOTE - Make sure you use forced melee otherwise you will not get updates.)
1. Return to Assistant Shortspanner
