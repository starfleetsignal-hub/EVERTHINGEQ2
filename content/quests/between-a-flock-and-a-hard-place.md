## Steps

1. Speak with [[Becaaw]] in [[Aves Clearing]] at {{waypoint 1446, 275, -20}}.
   - Fly directly or use the [[Crosswind Isle]] portals at {{waypoint 350, 180, -250}} to travel to [[The Aerie]].
1. Obtain the following:
   - 8 grain baskets around [[Skywing Vale]] {{waypoint 1042, 283, 237}} and other parts of [[The Aerie]].![Grain Basket](images/Grain_Basket.png)
   - Meat from 10 prismatic recluse spiders ([[A prismatic recluse]], [[A prismatic widow]]) found all over [[Aves Clearing]] and [[The Aerie]], an uncommon drop.
1. Return to [[Becaaw]] in [[The Aerie]] at {{waypoint 1446, 275, -20}}.
1. Return to [[Nidinoff]] at {{waypoint 249, -222, -1187}}.
