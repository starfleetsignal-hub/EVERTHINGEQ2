## Steps

1. Return to where you last saw Ordin Sleetfoot and make sure he is okay. Keep going up the tunnel past where you last found Ordin.  You'll see his body at the top of the tunnel at {{waypoint -253, -17, -34}}.
1. Return to Thirin.
