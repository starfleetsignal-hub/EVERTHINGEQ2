## Steps

1. Destroy the [[Training wall]] in [[The Tabernacle of Pain]]. {{waypoint -119, -60, 129}}
1. Speak with the [[Swifttail Trialmaster]]. {{waypoint -73, -43, 160}}
1. Enter the trial ring and defeat [[Maz Serix]]. {{waypoint -71, -43, 156}}
1. Return to the senior Rock shackle monk.

## Rewards

- +7,500 faction with **Tabernacle of Pain**
- Suffix Title: [[Of the Rock Shackle]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Stalwart Shackle of Rock]]
  - [[Arcane Shackle of Rock]]
  - [[Divine Shackle of Rock]]
  - [[Knave's Shackle of Rock]]
