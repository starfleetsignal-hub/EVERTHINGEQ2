*This quest is repeatable up to 2 times..*

## Steps

1. Slay 4 Kromise giants of any kind in Everfrost to receive the spirit's blessing.
1. Return to the [[The Spirit of Arctic Doom (NPC)|spirit]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- The blessing of the [[The Spirit of Arctic Doom (NPC)|spirit]] for a step in the quest [[In Search of a Menagerie]].
