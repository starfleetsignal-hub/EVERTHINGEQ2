## Steps

1. Slay 4 [[A dire warg|dire warg]]s {{waypoint 135, 83, 36}}
1. Slay 2 [[An Ahroun blackstalker|Ahroun blackstalker]]s {{waypoint 187, 87, 81}}
1. Slay 2 [[An Ahroun blackclaw|Ahroun blackclaw]]s
   - All of these mobs can be found at the [[Copse of the Ahroun]] at {{waypoint 202, 89, 47}}.
   - Beware the level 70 ^^^ [[A blackrunner warrior|blackrunner warrior]] running around the area.  However, he never runs to the north side of the rock.
1. Return to [[Philip Glaslow]] for your reward.
