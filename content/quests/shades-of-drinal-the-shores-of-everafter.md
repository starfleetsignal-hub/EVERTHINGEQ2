## Notes

It takes approximately 10 minutes for the previous quest to be finished and the in game mail to arrive from The Duality   (09/19/'13 - the mail arrives immediately after you zone)

NOTE: If you are a Heroic character (i.e. you paid to be immediately jumped to 90 or 95) then after Unexpected Consequences you need to do the quest called Shades of Drinal: TRIAL of Everafter before you do the main quest on this page for Shores of Everafter.

To start Shades of Drinal: Trial of Everafter do the following:

1. Go to the [[Feerrott]] spires  {{waypoint -92, 13, 182}}.
1. Use the portal to the [[The Eidolon Jungle]] and speak with Lady Liae Croae (if Good) or Lord Valkiss Ssi'sh (if Evil) around {{waypoint -46, 15, 199}}.
1. They will send you to kill assassins in the area.
   1. Skrillix Vog (lvl 92 ^^ heroic) {{waypoint -109, 102, 52}}
      - (NOTE: They do not have feathers. I killed scorpions to get them to pop,)
   1. Tonogon the Strong (lvl 92 ^^ heroic) {{waypoint 56, -9, 125}}
   1. Orofonk (lvl 92 ^^ heroic) {{waypoint -93, -3, 354}}
1. When they are killed return to your respective NPC for the next step which will lead you to the main quest on this page called Trials of Drinal: Shores of Everafter which is described below. Heroic characters skip steps 1 and  2 and proceed directly to step 3.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Ravinus]] near the [[Feerrott]] spires  {{waypoint -92, 13, 182}}.
1. Use the portal to the [[The Eidolon Jungle]].
1. Speak with [[Drinal's Steward]] at  {{waypoint -84, 14, 172}}
1. Speak with the following. Veris and Riishu quests can be done at the same time.
   1. Speak with [[Veris Windcall]] at {{waypoint 26, 2, -51}} and complete the following quests:
      1. [[Storm on the Horizon]] (92)
      1. [[Shore Defense]] (92)
      1. [[Tidal Waves]] (92)
      1. [[Pushing Forward]] (92)
   1. Speak with [[Riishu Sulrel]] at {{waypoint -147, 14, 184}} and complete the following quests:
      1. [[Dearly Departed]] (92)
      1. [[Proper Direction]] (92)
      1. [[Stronger Than Death]] (92)
   1. Speak with [[Veris Windcall]] at {{waypoint 18, -5, 500}} and complete the following quests:
      1. [[Emergency Exit]] (92)
      1. [[Only Way Out]] (92)
1. Return to [[Drinal's Steward]] at   {{waypoint -92, 13, 182}}.

- At least 1p 30g
