Gelik Thwaxby, a gnemlin within Maldura's Alcove of Reason, told me that the arcane defense pillars within Stoneheart Yards have stopped functioning. He suspects the Aberrants are draining them in hopes of entrapping anyone who goes to recharge them, but the trick will be on them!

## Steps

1. Recharge a pillar at the [[Stoneheart Yards]] in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]] using the **polished ember umbrite** crystal found on the ground next to it. Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint 656.65, 70.92, -99.33}}
   - {{waypoint 566, 81, -54}}
   - {{waypoint 646, 81, 30}}
   - {{waypoint 616, 68, -139}}
   - {{waypoint 539, 67, -133}}
1. You will be attacked and ported away.
1. Speak to [[Toadwart]] close by at {{waypoint 718, 107, 123}}
1. Recharge pillar again.
1. Return to [[Gelik Thwaxby|Gelik]] in [[Maldura]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 41g 75s 13200 status
- +1000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
- One of the following:
  - [[Verdant Umbrite Post]]
  - [[Pyre Umbrite Post]]
  - [[Cerulean Umbrite Post]]
