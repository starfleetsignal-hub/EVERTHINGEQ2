## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[A restless corpse|restless corpse]]s - halls and rooms from [[Cryptkeeper Maglus]] {{waypoint 170.60, 6.89, -188.22}} to [[Vegalys Keldrane]] {{waypoint 126, 7, -102}} and onwards towards [[Vargon]] {{waypoint 148, 7, -51}}.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 7 [[A tortured essence (Crypt of Betrayal)|tortured essences]] - rooms between [[Vargon]] and [[Adun]] {{waypoint 152, 7, -6}}.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 tortured essences.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 7 [[A crypt tempest|crypt tempest]]s - near "hard" entrance {{waypoint 76, 0, -23}}; shares spawn points with beetles.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 crypt tempests.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 8 tortured essences.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 7 crypt tempests.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 [[An agonized essence|agonized essence]]s - lower crypt {{waypoint -10, 0, -95}}.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 7 agonized essences.
1. Read the book.
