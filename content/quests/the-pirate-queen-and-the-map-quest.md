## Steps

1. Kill 10 Tae Ew lizardmen.
   - Tae Ew [[A Tae Ew defender|defenders]], [[A Tae Ew gatherer|gatherers]], [[A Tae Ew herald|heralds]], [[A Tae Ew hunter|hunters]], [[A Tae Ew judicator|judicators]], [[A Tae Ew justicar|justicars]], [[A Tae Ew protector|protectors]] and [[A Tae Ew warder|warders]] all update. They're very common through all the above-ground parts of the zone.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 Tae Ew lizardman.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 Tae Ew lizardman.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 goo masses.
   - [[A putrid mass|Putrid masses]], [[A noxious mass|noxious masses]], and the mobs in [[The Acidic Mass]]' ring event ([[A swirling ooze|swirling]], [[A bubbling ooze|bubbling]] and [[A boiling ooze|boiling oozes]]) all update.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 goo masses.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 goo masses.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 golems.
   - Golems are obnoxiously scarce in this zone.  You can find them in the following locations.
     - 4 [[A small clay golem|small clay golems]] can be found in the rooms at {{waypoint -33, 0, 48}} and {{waypoint 28, 0, 48}}.
     - 2 [[A clay golem|clay golems]] can be found patrolling at the top of the main stairway near {{waypoint 2, 24, 60}}
     - 2 clay golems can be found patrolling where the pathway circles around a ruined wall at {{waypoint 1, 24, -23}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 golems.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 golems.
1. Read the book.
