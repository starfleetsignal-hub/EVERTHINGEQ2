I came upon a small, very old, graveyard. The stones speak of a group of travelers that were set on by a swarm of shadefiends. Not one was left alive. I shall avenge these unfortunate wayfarers.

## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A deadly shadefiend|deadly shadefiends]] at the edge of [[Shadeweaver Forest]] along the chasm {{waypoint 97, 46, -521}}.
