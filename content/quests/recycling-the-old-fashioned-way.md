*This quest is repeatable up to 5 times., with each turn-in giving a larger coin reward.*

## Steps

1. Slay 5 of the following Thundering Steppes monsters (chosen randomly):
   - [[Entrancing siren|Entrancing sirens]] {{waypoint -210, 0, -707}}
   - Highland lionesses: ([[Highland huntress|highland huntress]] or [[Highland matriarch|highland matriarch]]) {{waypoint 994, -2, 79}}
   - [[A young antelope|Young antelopes]].
1. Return to Grenn Stiles for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 92c
