## Steps

1. Release chickens from their coops. Chicken crates are spread around at the [[Crossroads]], dregs village and the nomad camps.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -1000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -1000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- **Note**: This quest does not grant any experience or achievement experience.
