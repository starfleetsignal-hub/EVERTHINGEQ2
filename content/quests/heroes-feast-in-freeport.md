## Steps

1. Scribe the recipe [[Heroes' Feast Necessities]] you receive after talking to [[Reana Astia]]. {{waypoint 37, -18, 97}}
1. Go into the [[Coalition of Tradesfolke]] and gather the ingredients.
   - [[Heroes' Feast Honey]] and [[Heroes' Feast Sweet Groceries]] can be found by the [[Stove]].
   - [[Heroes' Feast Meat and Veggies]] and [[Heroes' Festival Gold Ingot|Heroes' Festival Gold Ingots]] can be found by the work order NPC's. The ingots are behind the counter.
   - [[Heroes' Festival Fabric]] can be found on the bench beside the [[Forge]].
1. Craft 6 of each of the following items:
   - [[Heroes' Feast Ice Cream]] in the [[Stove & Keg]]
   - [[Heroes' Feast Mead]] in the [[Stove & Keg]]
   - [[Heroes' Feast Roast]] in the [[Stove & Keg]]
   - [[Heroes' Feast Streamers]] in the [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]]
   - [[Heroes' Feast Tableware]] in the [[Forge]]
1. Talk to [[Reana Astia]].
1. Travel to [[Amphitheater of Kelkarn]] and click on the green shinies to set the table.
1. Once all the Shinies are gone return to [[Reana Astia]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- 5 x [[Token of Heroism]]
