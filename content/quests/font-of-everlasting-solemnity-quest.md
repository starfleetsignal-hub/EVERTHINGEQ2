## Steps

1. Kill 20 Bloodscale expilators in the Covenant District of [[Skyshrine: The City of Dracur]]
1. Click the Font of Everlasting Solemnity in the contested Covenant District

## Rewards

- At least 2 = 50p 3 = 50g
- At least **26,400 XP**
- +250 faction with **Claws of Veeshan**
- [[Awakened Petamorph Wand: Osseous Colossus]] (charm)
