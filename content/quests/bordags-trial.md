This is a subquest for [[The Word of the Harbingers - Qeynos]] and [[The Word of the Harbingers - Freeport]] and can only be attempted while you are on that quest.

## Steps

1. Enter the instance [[Bordag's Trial]].  The entrances are in [[Antonica]] or [[Commonlands]].
   - Antonica:  Click on a boulder up against the mountains near the twin guard towers.  {{waypoint -391, -10, -38}}
   - Commonlands:  Click on a boulder up against the mountains near the entrance to Darklight Wood.  {{waypoint 375, -35, -904}}
1. Confront and defeat [[The Scar Bear]].  The Scar Bear scales to your level, is ^ and non-heroic.
1. Return to Bordag

## Rewards

- [[Medallion of Elnakii]]
