This is part 6 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Qeynos]] and [[Kelethin]] who wear Chain and Plate Armor (with the exception of [[Shaman|shaman]] types).

- If you are from [[Freeport]], then your version of the quest is called [[Custom Fitted Armor - Part VI]].

## Steps

From [[The Thundering Steppes]]:

1. Collect 12 bear hides from [[A Sabertooth harvester|Sabertooth harvesters]] near {{waypoint 1160.97, 4.56, -247}}
1. Collect 10 musical instruments from [[An earth rumbler|earth rumblers]] near {{waypoint 886.47, -20.37, 1185.63}}
1. Recover murky steel cutlass. The Giant [[Ott Stompgut]] runs out of the passage leading to the Firerock Giant's area (plus his bear){{waypoint 939, 0, 582}}
