## Description

[[Tobel Patadash]] claims that he is the true prophet of [[Bristlebane]], The Grand Prankster. He seems pretty miffed that [[Garby Gingham Gigglegibber]] is also claiming to be the true "profit" of Bristlebane. In true prankster form, I've agreed to help Tobel prank Garby by giving him a *Diabolic Spiced Pumpkin Pie*. Tobel advised me to speak with pie maker [[Roselia Goldencrust]] on how the pie is made. Roselia has set up shop on the [[Bobick Village Docks]].

## Steps

![ginger root](images/Ginger_root.png)
![an Antonica pumpkin](images/An_antonica_pumpkin.png)

1. Speak with [[Roselia Goldencrust]], near the mailbox on the [[Bobick Village Docks]]. {{waypoint 39, 1, 35}}
1. Collect the ingredients for the *Diabolic Spiced Pumpkin Pie*:
   - Gather 5 [[Ginger root|ginger roots]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]:
     - [[Druid Ring (BBM)|Druid Ring]] {{waypoint 41, 182, 561}}
     - [[Highland Outposts]] {{waypoint 307, 186, 492}}
     - Non-trackable.
     - If you cannot see the roots, adjust graphics to higher resolution or zoom your camera in to first-person. You can also use the TAB key to target the nodes, or "/target ginger".
   - Buy 1 [[Spicy faypepper]] from [[Preston Pepperpicker]] at [[Joleena's Restaurant]] in [[Kelethin]]. {{waypoint 530, 71, 295}}
   - Harvest 6 [[An antonican pumpkin|Antonican pumpkins]] at [[The McQuibble Farm]] in [[Antonica]]. {{waypoint -1890.78, -19.21, -182.68}}.
     - You can use "/target pumpkin" to find these when nearby.
1. Return to Roselia Goldencrust and give her all the ingredients.
1. Speak with Roselia Goldencrust again to receive [[A Diabolical Pumpkin Pie]].
1. Deliver the 'spiced' pie to [[Garby Gingham Gigglegibber]] at {{waypoint 74, -0, 21}}.
1. Tell Tobel Patadash that the prank went off perfectly!

## Rewards

- +1250 faction with **Bristlebane**
