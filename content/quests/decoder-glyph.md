## Steps

1. Retrieve a decoder glyph by killing [[A Haoaeran turf-hunter|Haoaeran turf-hunters]] just south of Trythec Loft {{waypoint 40, 55, 240}}
1. Return to Arozik.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 5c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Bodyguard's Leggings]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Evoker's Leggings]] (All Mages)
  - [[Marshall's Greaves]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Pathfinder's Leggings]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Spiritualist's Leggings]] (Shamans)
  - [[Vicar's Greaves]] (Clerics)
  - [[Windcaller's Leggings]] (Druids)
