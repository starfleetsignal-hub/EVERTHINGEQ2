*This quest is also part of the [[Lavastorm Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Scribe [[Chimney construction schematics]], received when you accepted the quest.
1. Craft [[Chimney repair]] on the Sootfoot Forge next to Smakametta:
   - [[Broken chimney pieces]], also received when you accepted the quest
   - 3 [[Diamondine cluster]]
   - 3 [[Fulginate cluster]]
   - 2 [[Strengthened leather pelt]]
   - 6 [[Luminous coal]]
1. Return to [[Smakametta]].

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- One of the following:
  - [[Ebon cluster]]
  - [[Fused loam]]
  - [[Rhodium cluster]]
  - [[Rough ruby]]
