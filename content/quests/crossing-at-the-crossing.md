## Requirement

- The recipe for [[Starfire Aggregate (Spell Scroll)|Starfire Aggregate]] is a level 20 recipe

## Steps

1. You will receive the [[Starfire Aggregate (Recipe Book)|Starfire Aggregate]] recipe book from Khat-Ra Pakiat at the start of this quest. Scribe it. By this time, you will already have most of the other necessary components from the quests done up to this point.

If you are missing components, you can check the **Component Chest**{{waypoint -809, 171, -709}} just SE of Khat-Ra Pakiat to get them back.![Component Chest](images/Component_Chest.jpg)

1. Use the Spell Cube at {{waypoint -799, 171, -710}} to craft the Starfire Aggregate.  If you have your graphics set low, you may not see the cube if you are zoomed out in 3rd person.  ![Spell Cube](images/Spell_Cube.jpg)
   - In addition to the components gathered previously, you will need 5 [[Celestial Coal]].
1. Speak to [[Khat-Ra Pakiat]] to receive the [[Pakiat Divining Device (Crossing At The Crossing)|Pakiat Divining Device]].
1. Travel to {{waypoint -1110, 36, 960}} on the outside south eastern edge of the map, and use the Pakiat Divining Device.
1. Click on the Starfire Realm well at {{waypoint -1122, 36, 954}} to enter The Starfire Realm {solo-only zone}. ![Starfire Realm well](images/Starfire_Realm_well.jpg)
1. Cast Starfire Aggregate
   - Check your knowledge book under abilities if it did not show up on your hotbars.
   - The zone appears to be a reskinned Crystal Caverns (from DoV) map.
1. Progress through the zone to {{waypoint 42, -25, -68}} and talk to [[Kalisonu]].![Kalisonu Cursed Elemental](images/Kalisonu_Cursed_Elemental.jpg)
1. Wait while she (eventually) touches the Sunstone, then speak to her again.
1. Click on the crystal wall at {{waypoint 148, 15, -49}} to exit.
1. Return to [[Khat-Ra Pakiat]].
