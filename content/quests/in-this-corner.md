## Steps

1. Jump down the pit and click on the wooden post. You will be attacked by a group of tigers.
   - *Note: the tigers already present in the pit should not be aggro to you, so unless you AE attack, they should pose you no threat.*

1. Return to [[Pitmaster Shar'rik]]

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +2000 faction with **Kerra Isle**
