## Steps

1. Go to the island with the giant golden hands at {{waypoint -696.98, 4.23, -694.01}} and then down to the [[Firegard Shrine]] near {{waypoint -833, -62, -786}} collect 4 Firegard Shrine Oil by clicking **Firegard Shrine Oil Pots**.![](images/Firegard_Shrine_Oil.jpg)
1. Attempt to light [[Maiden's Torch]] at {{waypoint -678.03, 79.49, 131.13}}
1. Speak with [[Ph'utha]] at {{waypoint -681.42, 79.49, 133.68}}
1. Speak with [[Al'Kabor]] at {{waypoint -675.91, 79.21, 103.23}}.
