### Steps

Craft 6 items from the random list below:

- [[Carbonite Rapier (Level 20) (Crafted)|Carbonite Rapier]]
- [[Carbonite Battle Hammer (Level 20) (Crafted)|Carbonite Battle Hammer]]
- [[Carbonite Crescent Axe]]
- [[Carbonite Javelin]]
- [[Carbonite Morning Star (Level 20) (Crafted)|Carbonite Morning Star]]
- [[Carbonite Hand Axe]]
- [[Carbonite Parrying Blade (Level 20) (Crafted)|Carbonite Parrying Blade]]
- [[Carbonite Flail (Level 20) (Crafted)|Carbonite Flail]]
- [[Carbonite Shamshir]]
- [[Carbonite Tribal Spear]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

### Rewards

- At Least 12s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
