## Steps

![Splitiron stone](images/Splitiron_Stone.png)

1. In [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]]:
   - Harvest 8 **Splitiron stone** from the [[Splitiron Mine]] {{waypoint 213, 138, 249}}.
   - Kill 10 [[A topaz shrieker|topaz shriekers]] at [[Tranquil Halls]] {{waypoint 528, 146, 380}}.
1. Return to [[Gilzin]] in [[Maldura]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +1,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1,000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
