## Notes

- Consider doing [[Keep Your Nose to the Grindhoof]] at the same time as this quest since this quest also requires killing [[An ambushing grindhoof|ambushing grindhooves]].

## Steps

1. Acquire 10 *grindhoof stringy meat* and 10 *grindhoof horns* from [[An ambushing grindhoof|ambushing grindhooves]]. {{waypoint 543, -41, 712}}
1. Return to [[Herd Master Rudek]].

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **Clan Skleross**
- At least 19g 93s 2c
