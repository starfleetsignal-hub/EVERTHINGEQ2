## Steps

1. Gather healing herbs
   - Harvest 3 [[Spiceleaf Herb]] from near [[Dain's Last Stand]] {{waypoint 638, -195, -2}}
   - Harvest 3 [[Goahmari Herb]] from in [[Goahmari Village]].
1. Return to [[Capru]].

Note: There is a passage between Dain's Last Stand(through the waterfall) and Thurgadin(North West corner). The [[Spiceleaf Herb]]'s will not count if harvested before this quest.

Note: If continuing on earn your flying mount, gather a couple extra Goahmari Herbs for the quest A Keener Sheen.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
