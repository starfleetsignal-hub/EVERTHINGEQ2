## Steps

1. Bandits have stolen Grigissa's egg. Locate and retrieve it![Grigissa's Missing Egg](images/Grigissa's_Missing_Egg.jpg)
   - The bandits are trackable. They are by two tents on the cliff top. You can go directly to the Egg at {{waypoint 540, -175, -585}}.  You don't need to kill all the waves of bandits. Just grab the egg from inside the tent and kill the first wave then run. Watch for knockback
1. Return the egg to Grigissa
1. She wants to leave Vrassath in secret with Tissik <Traveling Merchant> {{waypoint 512, -182, -197}}
1. Speak again to Grigissa. Her egg is missing again. Find it. It is much closer this time {{waypoint 539, -55, -58}} ![Grigissa's (broken) Egg](images/Grigissa's_%28broken%29_Egg.jpg)
1. Bring the remains to Grigissa for update
