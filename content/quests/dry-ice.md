1. Go to the mist goblin area outside [[The City of Mist]] and defeat 6 [[An ice battler|ice battlers]]. To spawn the ice battlers, examine the glowing blue objects strewn about the area. {{waypoint -85, 107, -577}}
1. Return to Khassp Xivvix.
