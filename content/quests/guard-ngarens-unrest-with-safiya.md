## Steps

1. Talk to [[Fisherman Safiya]] nearby.
1. Talk to Safiya again between 7:30 and 8:00 (game time) and tell her you will follow her. At 8:20 she will walk north up the road.
1. Stay close to Safiya and witness her run screaming from the statues.
1. Catch up to Safiya and speak with her again.
1. Walk around the shrine until you get a quest update.
1. Talk to [[Guard N'Garen]] and tell him Safiya is crazy.
1. Choose one of the following actions:
   1. Kill Fisherman Safiya (level 10).  Talk to Guard N'Garen for a reward.
   1. Don't kill Fisherman Safiya.  Talk to her for a reward.

## Rewards

- If you kill Safiya, you can choose one of the following:
  - [[Bounty hunters staff|Bounty Hunter's Staff]]
  - [[Bounty hunters maul|Bounty Hunter's Maul]]
- If you spare Safiya, you can choose one of the following:
  - [[Safiya's flail of mercy|Safiya's Flail of Mercy]]
  - [[Safiya's whip of mercy|Safiya's Whip of Mercy]]
  - [[Safiya's cestus of mercy|Safiya's Cestus of Mercy]]
