Several chapters of Volume II of The Planar Chronicles detail what to expect from some of the elemental planes. Reading through these chapters has raised my spirits, and my will, to survive the coming journey.

## Steps

- Complete this chapter on [Solo] missions.:
  - Kill Kamara Zar in [[Eryslai: Trials of Air (Solo)]]
  - Slay the earthen siblings in [[Vegarlson: Ruins of Rathe (Solo)]]
  - Slaughter Firemonger Rosnarga in [[Doomfire: Elements of Rage (Solo)]]
  - Vanquish the Veiled Avenger in [[Awuidor: The Veiled Precipice (Solo)]]

## Rewards

- At least 5p 19g 75s 122,400 status
- 35 [[Archivist's Tremissis]]
