## Steps

1. Obtain the following:
   - A Clockwork Modulatatron dropped by [[A clockwork explorer]].
   - 4 Chatter Boneboxes dropped by [[A Smokehorn shaman|Smokehorn shamans]], [[A Smokehorn harbinger|harbingers]] or [[A Smokehorn seer|seers]]
     - Quest journal states only shamans, but harbingers and seers update too.
1. Return to Emery.

## Reward

- At least 2g 99s 47c
