## Description

Seeress Miritha of the Lyrech has asked you to obtain 20 Ahroun Collars from the Ahroun clan werewolves. The collars are being studied by Miritha in regards to devising a method that would allow the Lyrech to control their lycanthropic forms at night.

## Steps

1. Obtain 20 collars from Ahroun Werewolves. {{waypoint 187, 87, 81}} (uncommon update)
1. Return to [[Seeress Miritha (human)|Seeress Miritha]] during the day. If you attempt to speak with her at night she will be in her [[Seeress Miritha (werewolf)|werewolf form]] and attack.

## Rewards

- +1,000 faction with **The Lyrech**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Stitched Cape of the Heavenly Mage]] (All Mages)
  - [[Stitched Cape of the Sky Warrior]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Stitched Cape of the Skystalker]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Stitched Cape of the Winds]] (All Priests)
