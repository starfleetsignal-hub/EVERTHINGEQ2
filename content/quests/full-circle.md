## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 20+

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Field Marshal Vishra]] at [[The Sovereign House of Qeynos]] in [[South Qeynos]]. {{waypoint 586, -15, 179}}
1. Obtain reports from the two final members of the Circle of Ten.
   - Speak with [[General Golias Sydwen]] at the top of the steps of [[Qeynos Castle]] in [[North Qeynos]]. {{waypoint 477, 4, -28}}
   - Speak with [[Daylia the Vigil (Qeynos Province District)|Daylia the Vigil]] atop the platform on [[Arbos, the Elddar Tree]] in the [[Elddar Grove]]. {{waypoint 662, 12, -373}}
1. Return to [[Field Marshal Vishra]].
