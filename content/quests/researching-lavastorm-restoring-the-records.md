## Steps

1. Find the missing pages in the library of the [[Temple of Solusek Ro]].
   1. The first page is on the left side of the desk in the middle of the room at {{waypoint -432, -61, -663}}
   1. The second page is on the floor in front of the bookcase at {{waypoint -449, -61, -655}}
   1. The fourth page is on the lowest shelf of the bookcase at {{waypoint -420, -63, -666}}
   1. The sixth page is on the floor in front of the table with the Tome of Tranix on it {{waypoint -431, -63, -678}}
   1. The last page is on the floor to the right of the desk in the middle of the room at {{waypoint -430, -61, -671}}
1. Return to Shrine of Thunder and speak to [[Researcher Caudil]].
