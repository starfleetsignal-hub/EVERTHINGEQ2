## Steps

*Hiwaaya Al'Naderi, one of Bellengere the Three's remaining devoted amongst the Cult of Vul, refuses to let the current opportunity for exploration of void energies be wasted. She has tasked me with procuring samples from the unbound energies and creatures have been amassing in the large void fissures below the Floating Steps in the Spiral of Vul.*

- Gather void energy threads (0/8).
- Gather void-entangled Ulteran magic (0/8).
- Hunt kaborite terrors for void-imbued living kaborite (0/8).
- Hunt regrua voidseekers for void-distorted ceca (0/8).
- Return to [[Hiwaaya Al'Naderi (Spiral of Vul)|Hiwaaya Al'Naderi]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
