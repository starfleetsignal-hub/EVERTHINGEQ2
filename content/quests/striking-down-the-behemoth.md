## Notes

To receive this quest, you must have +40,000 faction (Ally status) with the Defenders of the Seal.

## Steps

1. Find [[Haldor]]
1. Use the provided potion: You will get about 5 times of health
1. Kill [[Haldor]]
1. Return to [[Maizah]]

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]]
- At least 90g
- +2500 faction with **Defenders of the Seal**
