## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Mithaniel Marr has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items.

1. Purchase the [[Tear-Stained Blindfold|tear-stained blindfold]] from Voldris Zul'vrek for 5000 status
   - Wanders west of [[Chrykori Village]], from {{waypoint -287, 0, -49}} to {{waypoint -71, 29, 115}}
1. Purchase the [[Balanced Scale|balanced scale]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the [[Blue-Flecked Bloodstone|blue-flecked bloodstone]] from Laparnuu for 5000 status
   - in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]], near [[Petalcrown Perch]] and [[The Roost Warrens]].  Laparnuu wanders the path from {{waypoint 338, 238, 7}}  all the way to {{waypoint 31, 236, -13}}.
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Seventh Hammer]] at {{waypoint 274, 180, -357}} in [[Timorous Deep]]
