Uko has been tasked with killing the fae drakes and Fayllians. They are aggressive and sometimes tread close to the path, harassing travelers. He would like me to help him with this task. They can be found along the path that leads to Uko.

## Steps

1. Kill 5 Fayllians {{waypoint -120, -50, -220}}
1. Kill 5 Fae Drakes {{waypoint -200, -52, -182}}
1. Return to Uko

Both the Fayllians and the Drakes can be found north of Uko.

## Rewards

- Experience
- At least 9g 13s 1c
