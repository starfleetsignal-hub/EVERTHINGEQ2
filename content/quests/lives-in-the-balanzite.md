## Steps

1. ![Animated Balanzite](images/Animated_Balanzite.jpg)Venture into the [[Shattered Chasm]] to obtain materials for [[Calerel Windsong|Calerel Windsong's]] studies.
   1. Gather 8 shards of **animated balanzite**.
      - *Killing [[A balanzite golem|balanzite golems]] can result in a clickable blue shard.*
      - *Shards left by prior adventurers may already be present on the ground.*
   1. Hunt 8 [[A balanzite horror|balanzite horrors]] {{waypoint -268.02, 50.42, -319.10}} for crystal-baited tails (autoupdate).
      - *You may have to be almost right on top of the purple crystal to cause the horror to erupt from the ground.*
1. Return to [[Calerel Windsong]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- One of the following:
  - [[Garnet Bananzite Ring]]
  - [[Garnet Balanzite Dagger]]
  - [[Garnet Balanzite Spear]]
