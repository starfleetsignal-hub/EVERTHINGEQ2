## Notes

- Jump down first into [[Hidden Refuge]] (*island below waterfall from island one starts on*) for Refuge's Whiskey then can use inter-island cloud transports to [[Vicious Breeding Grounds]]; after that, find other dragon platforms to get to the other islands; [[Bixie Isle]] is last stop on "leap-frog" cloud transports.
- None of the harvestable items are trackable.
- All of these clicks are **zone-wide group** updates. If you are grouped anywhere in the zone, your group members will receive updates.

## Steps

1. Gather the following ingredients from [[Tenebrous Tangle]]:
   1. Gather 2 Refuge's Whiskey around the [[Hidden Refuge]]
      - {{waypoint 179, -233, 165}}
   1. Gather 5 Bzzt Honey around [[Bixie Isle]]![Bzzt Honey](images/Bzzt_Honey.jpg)
      - {{waypoint -656, -63, -888}}
      - {{waypoint -718.65, -64.39, -831.02}}
      - {{waypoint -797.74, -73.69, -752.77}}
      - {{waypoint -793.19, -75.61, -775.38}}
      - {{waypoint -803.15, -72.71, -737.48}}
      - {{waypoint -823.24, -72.76, -771.12}}
   1. Gather 5 Breeding Grounds Coffee around [[Vicious Breeding Grounds]]![Breeding Ground Coffee](images/Breeding_Grounds_Coffee.jpg)
      - {{waypoint 604, -164, 226}}
      - {{waypoint 550.76, -152.44, 285.93}}

![Refuge's Whiskey](images/Refuge's_Whiskey.jpg)

1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]].
