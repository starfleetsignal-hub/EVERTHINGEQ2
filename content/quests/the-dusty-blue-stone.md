## Prerequisite

You no longer need to have at least 90 skill in Mining to harvest in [[The Thundering Steppes]], following the release of the Tears of Veeshan expansion.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Gruffin Goldtooth]] in [[Qeynos Province District]] (Elddar Grove) at {{waypoint 753, -15, -291}} For Origins, Gruffin Goldtooth is in the Graystone Yard at (883.10, -23.45, -175.65)
1. Gather 40 rock samples from windswept stones in [[The Thundering Steppes]]
   - This requires 40 harvests not pieces. 1 update per successful harvest (except bountiful harvests give two updates)
   - *Note: If you haven't completed [[A Gathering Obsession, Part III]], or the first step of [[Journeyman Service]], now is a great time to multitask. You may also want to pick up [[Tiffin for Duggin]] and select to harvest around this area (Thundering Steppes).*
1. Return to [[Gruffin Goldtooth]]
1. Speak with [[Aristide Calais]] in the [[Concordium Tower]] (red portal) in [[Qeynos Capitol District]] (South Qeynos) at {{waypoint 711, 41, 107}}
1. Speak with [[Olden Highguard]] in [[Village of Thundermist]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]] at {{waypoint 613, -1, -56}}
1. Return to [[Aristide Calais]] (red portal)
1. Speak with [[Toranim Skyblade]] in front of the [[Temple of Life]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 535, -32, -174}}
1. Go to [[Stormhold]] and kill between 25 and 30 (random number) powerful undead.
   - Any undead in the zone satisfy the quest.
   - *Note:In the last step when going to Stormhold if you mentor down to level 20, you can pull mobs and AoE them quicker.*
1. Return to [[Toranim Skyblade]]

## Rewards

[[Toranim Skyblade]] offers you the quest [[Restoring Ghoulbane]].
