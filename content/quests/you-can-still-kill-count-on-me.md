## Notes

- There is no cooldown on this quest.

- Faction will stop being given as a reward once the player reaches +10,000 faction with [[Nye'Caelona (Faction)|Nye'Caelona]].

- At or above -9,700 faction, the next quest Arid Roots is available.

## Steps

1. Slay 8 iksar of given 2 types which are of the following:
   - iksar exiles
   - iksar marauders  around Slavers Point ({{waypoint -451.63, 159.66, -873.26}} )
   - iksar abductors  around Slavers Point ({{waypoint -451.63, 159.66, -873.26}} )
   - iksar pariahs
     - * Automatic update upon completion*

## Rewards

- At least 1p 41g 75s 15108 status
- +1000 faction with **Nye'Caelona** (stops giving faction at 11,000 positive)
