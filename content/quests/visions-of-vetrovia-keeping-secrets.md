## Steps

1. Light incense on Needlehead Isle at any of these locations:![Kinnahma Memorial Incense](images/Kinnahma_Memorial_Incense.webp)
   - around {{waypoint 16, 39, -810}}
   - around {{waypoint 15, 39, -822}}
   - around {{waypoint 30, 44, -836}}
   - around {{waypoint 29, 40, -824}}
1. Slay the mysterious vampire that spawns
1. Return to [[Oracle Priti]]
   - Speak with [[Dahsuvak]] {{waypoint 69, 12, 373}}
   - Speak with [[Kavuri]] {{waypoint 151, 6, 413}}
1. Return to [[Oracle Priti]]
1. Make sure sub-quests below are finished then acquire the [[Holy Icon of Srlaka]] from [[Vikram]] at [[Camp Naradasa]] {{waypoint -644, 88, -515}}.
   - Must Complete [[Small Plunder]] from  [[Captain Douglan Wakerunner (Svarni Expanse)|Captain Douglan Wakerunner]] in [[Kamapor]]
   - Then [[Mystery of Camp Naradasa]] from [[Captain Douglan Wakerunner (Svarni Expanse)|Captain Douglan Wakerunner]]
   - Then Complete [[I Believe Icon Purify]] before Vikram will give you the Icon
1. Return to [[Oracle Priti]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 56722 status
- [[Holy Icon of Srlaka Reliquary]]
