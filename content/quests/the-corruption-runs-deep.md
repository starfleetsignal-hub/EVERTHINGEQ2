## Steps

1. Eliminate the corrupted satyr camps.  To eliminate a camp you must click on a stone with plates and dishes on it.  The satyrs surrounding the camps do not necessarily have to be killed.  Camps can be found at the following locations.  Note that each clickable stone has a "respawn time" and if you wait long enough you can reuse one you've already clicked.
   - {{waypoint -2495, -718, 100}}
   - {{waypoint -2480, -717, 121}}
   - {{waypoint -2286, -689, 31}}
   - {{waypoint -2271, -688, 48}}
1. Return to Leif
