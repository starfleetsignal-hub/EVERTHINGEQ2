## Steps

1. Enter the violent rift Lady Najena is holding open.
   - Click rift next to her and choose [[Gerion, Hold of D'Lere (Signature)|Gerion, Hold of D'Lere [Signature]]] instance.
1. Explore Gerion
   - go to [[Gaoler's Overlook]] at {{waypoint 207, 0, 273}} and kill [[A Lucanic Gaoler]]
   - go to [[Deathdrinker's Ledge]] at {{waypoint 339, 0, 58}} and Kill [[A Gerion deathdrinker]].
1. After Lucan's monologue use the [[Thyr Summoning Stone]] to summon Lady Najena.
1. Wait for an update that you can escape and use the portal that appears behind Lady Najena to leave Gerion
