## Prerequisites

- You must be at least adventure level 76 to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Enter *Nuknok Caverns* at {{waypoint 1347, 457, 419}} and use [[Nesbit's Nuknok Leash]] on [[A Nuknok piglet]]. {{waypoint 1373, 455, -259}}
1. Within 10 minutes, return the piglet to [[Nesbit]]. {{waypoint 1396, 406, 768}}

- At least 22g 11s 70c
