## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Cazic-Thule has asked me to assist in gathering fearbound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the fearbound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Fearbound Energy Reserves|fearbound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect [[Fearbound Energy Reserves|fearbound energy]] by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Faceless]] {{waypoint -1109, -1, 919}} in [[The Feerrott]]
