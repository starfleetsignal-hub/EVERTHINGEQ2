## Steps

1. Convince [[Foto Stratobelt]] {{waypoint 1205, -520, 367}} to commission Bin's new vessel by choosing the correct dialogue lines
   1. "Whoa, hold on there... I have come to collect on this contract."
   1. "Hmm, what if I were the same person named in the contract?"
   1. "No, I may not be the person but I work for them..."
   1. "Technically I do..."
   1. "I work for Captain Bin Nalot. Captain Nalot's great great granddaughter. The contract doesn't specify which Captain Nalot!"
1. Return to Bin

- At least 29g 85s 79c
