## Steps

![Cedar Nuts](images/ForagingintheGroveQuestObject.JPG)

1. Gather five Cedar nuts at the Treant Grove {{waypoint 558, 3, -108}}
1. Return to [[Amber Walker]]

## Rewards

- At least 2s 78c

## Notes

The cedar nuts are neither click- nor tab-targetable, and can be found anywhere in Treant Grove - such as directly beneath or behind [[Coerced sapling|coerced saplings]].
