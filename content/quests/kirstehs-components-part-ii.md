This is the second Freeport armor quest for cloth armor wearers.

## Steps

1. Go to the [[Yapping Maze]] in the [[The Commonlands]] and:
   - Kill 8 [[A Thexian dragoon (Commonlands)|Thexian dragoons]], and 10 [[A Thexian excavator (Commonlands)|Thexian excavators]] {{waypoint 1380, -36, -198}}.
   - Collect 2 Medallions from [[A dervish cutthroat|devish cutthroats]] {{waypoint 1384, -29, 290}}.
1. Return to Kirsteh for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following class specific items:
  - [[Wristwraps of Allurement]] - Coercer
  - [[Shadeweave Wristwraps]] - Necromancer
  - [[Defiled Sanguine Wristwraps]] - Warlock
  - [[Spellbound Cerise Wristwraps]] - Wizard
