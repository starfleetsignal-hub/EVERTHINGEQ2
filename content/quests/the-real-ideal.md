## Notes

*Veasna says to you, "That which shall make the ideal, real. **Make sure that the bracers I gave you have been attuned, or are otherwise 'No-Trade.**' Once you've made sure of that, simply take this yellow gem, right click on it, select 'Use,' and then click on the bracers. You won't be able to do this while you're wearing them, so be sure to put them someplace safe in your inventory. A few seconds later, and the process will be complete! Show me what the result is."

## Steps

1. Use the [[Your First Yellow Gemstone]] *(found in your inventory)* on any Vesspyr Defender's Bracers
1. Speak to [[Veasna]]
1. Use the [[Your Second Yellow Gemstone]] *(found in your inventory)* on the upgraded Vesspyr Defender's Bracers
1. Speak to [[Veasna]]
1. Use the [[Your Third Yellow Gemstone]] *(found in your inventory)* on the upgraded Vesspyr Defender's Bracers
1. Speak to [[Veasna]]

## Rewards

- [[Vesspyr Defender's Cloth Bracers]] (All Mages)
- [[Vesspyr Defender's Leather Bracers]] (Bruiser, Fury, Monk, Warden)
- [[Vesspyr Defender's Chain Bracers]] (All Scouts, Defiler, Mystic)
- [[Vesspyr Defender's Plate Bracers]] (Berserker, Guardian, Inquisitor, Paladin, Shadowknight, Templar)
- *The reward is actually the 3 times upgraded bracer*
