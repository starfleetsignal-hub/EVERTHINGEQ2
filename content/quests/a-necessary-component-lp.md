## Steps

1. Use Philip's Seed near [[Corrupted Druid Ring]] {{waypoint -415, 27, 432}}.
1. Harvest the plant that grows. You need to get 5 updates. You have to harvest them quick or they will die and disappear. You can do this entire quest sitting inside the [[Corrupted Druid Ring]] and just move around a bit.  If the plant does not grow, pick up the seed and try planting it again. You have a better chance of the plant sprouting if you look for the "A Raven screeches at you from above" message before dropping the seed.
1. Return to [[Philip Glaslow]] for your reward.
