## Background

- This is not really a quest, but is required to access the archaeologian's quests in the [[Tenebrous Tangle Timeline]].
- Do the following whenever you need [[Archaeologian Tydis]] if he is absent from the Hidden Shelter.

## Steps

- Kill all Scaleborn sentries south of the building at {{waypoint -180, 0, 60}} on Temple Grounds island. You must kill them all before they respawn (6 minutes).
- When no Scaleborns are alive, [[Captive froglok archaeologian|a captive froglok archaeologian]] will run up the path to the waterfall.
- You can now find [[Archaeologian Tydis]] at his spot in the Hidden Shelter.

## Rewards

Access to the following quests:

- [[Expedition for Draconic Bones]]
- [[Expedition for Draconic Eggs]]
- [[Expedition for Draconic Idols]]
