## Steps

You have agreed to to find three dead Thulian dreadknights and their daggers. They are found on top of the floor grates with blue ooze beneath them. When you reach the following locations, an NPC burning skeleton will appear then fall dead to the floor. If you can't interact with the skeletons try zooming in your camera view to First Person and/or crouching. Some of the larger races have camera positions too far above the floor for the skeletons to be interacted with. Click on the corpse to spawn 3 droags. Do not charm them or the quest may bug out. Kill them to update the quest:

1. On the way to [[The Gorgs' Pit]]: {{waypoint -32, 57, 243}}
1. On the way to the [[Pool of Spirits]]: {{waypoint 59, 45, 103}}
1. On the right corridor leaving the [[Hall of Rites]]: {{waypoint -66, 65, -57}}
1. Upon killing the third set of droags, you will get a quest update giving [[Three Ceremonial Daggers of the Scaleborn]]. Examine the daggers to update the quest.
1. Go to the [[Hall of Rites]] and click the rear of the dragon pedestal at {{waypoint 9, 65, 4}}.
1. Report back to [[Xhorroz]] for your reward and the next quest.
