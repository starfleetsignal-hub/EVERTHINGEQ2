## Steps

1. Gather all the parts needed to create a blessed fishing pole:
   - a sturdy piece of bamboo sapling lumber. (click on a stack of bamboo at {{waypoint 531, 334, 478}})
   - a razor-sharp fang from [[A menacing overseer]] {{waypoint 47, 361, 437}}, an uncommon update.
   - an unscathed horn from [[A Gruengach striker]], an uncommon update.
1. Return to [[Fisherman Uri]].
1. Equip [[Uri's Blessed Fishing Pole]] and fish in the pond to catch a [[Red snapper]] from *a clutch of red snapper*.
1. Speak with [[Kun Ling]].

## Rewards

- At least 35g 70s 61c
- +2,000 faction with **Disciples of the Circling Destiny**
