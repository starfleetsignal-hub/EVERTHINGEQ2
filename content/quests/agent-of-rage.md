## Steps

1. Enter the back room of the Temple {{waypoint -80, -7, -187}}
1. Question the Lucanic Knight in the middle of the room
1. Kill the knight (level +2, one up arrow)
1. Kill the Knight again
1. When the Dismal Rage priests appear, wait for them to give you instructions, then leave the zone - you will not be prompted to zone out, just leave and zone out to update.  Or kill them as well, then zone out.
1. Talk to Maldreya, in front of you as you zone out, then Xolantan, then Maldreya again
1. Speak to [[Lucanic Knight Xolantan]] in [[Overlord's Plaza]] {{waypoint 8, -19, 34}}

## Rewards

- At least 11g 5s 35c
- [[A Lucanic Vase of Malice]]
