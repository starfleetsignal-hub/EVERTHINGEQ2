## Prerequisites

- Mages must be a minimum of level 80 to be offered this quest.

## Steps

1. Enter the personal quarters of [[Bellengere the Three (Qeynos Capitol District)|Bellengere the Three]] in the [[The Concordium Tower|Concordium Tower]] (use the yellow teleporter). {{waypoint 701, 87, 130}}
1. Speak to [[Festyaa Naestra]] and [[Bellengere the Three (Qeynos Capitol District)|Bellengere the Three]].
1. Travel to the gate leading to the [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum|Starcrest Commune]] in [[South Qeynos]] and zone in. {{waypoint 695, -21, 366}}
1. Destroy 4 portals.
   - Find a portal with ghosts streaming out of it and click it.
   - Your vision will go purple and somewhere near by will be a crystalline rock.
   - Click the rock to destroy the portal, then slay any beings from the other side that passed through the veil.
   - Note that there is a bug with the portal next to the tree near the entrance - no rock appears
1. Remove a final anchor and kill the [[Unspeakable Horror (mob)|Unspeakable Horror]]- This is a ^ version scaled to your level and is a hard fight - if it keeps being too hard, you can go mentor yourself down 5 levels, go back in, and unmentor... everything will then be 5 levels lower and easier to kill, including the name. Do not kill the horror's adds as these heal the main mob on their death. Once you have killed the named, back up and let the adds explode. They *will* kill you if you stay to close. After the last add has exploded, you then have to click the portal and the stone one more time to update.
1. Return to [[Crexisz Lepidus]].
1. Speak to  [[Felion Shadoweater]] in the [[The Concordium Tower|Concordium Tower]] (use the blue teleporter). {{waypoint 713, 35, 138}}
   - Click the glowing area at Felion's feet to place the anchors for destruction.
1. Speak to [[Bellengere the Three (Qeynos Capitol District)|Bellengere the Three]] in his personal quarters in the [[The Concordium Tower|Concordium Tower]] (use the yellow teleporter). {{waypoint 701, 87, 130}}
1. Bellengre will port you out of the [[The Concordium Tower|Concordium Tower]] and into the canal, where you should speak to [[Crexisz Lepidus]].
