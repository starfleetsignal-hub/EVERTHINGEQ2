## Steps

1. recover and kill the following
   - Recover 6 **Ahroun pelts** around {{waypoint -216, 21, 173}} or {{waypoint -115.55, 17.64, 288.53}}. ![Ahroun Pelt](images/Ahroun_pelt.png)
   - Kill 12 Royal Myrmidons around {{waypoint -310, 13, 278}} or {{waypoint -72, 47, 254}}.
1. Return to [[Kurtis Scarson]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- Choice of:
  - [[Forlorn Cincture of Foresight]]
  - [[Forlorn Belt of Stratagem]]
