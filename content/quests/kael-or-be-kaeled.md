## Notes

One must be level 75 to enter the zone into Outer Kael, **Level 85 to enter Kael Drakkel**.

## Steps

1. Search out the area where the Kromzek nobles live. (Nobles District) {{waypoint -982, 78, 254}}
1. Search out where the Kromzek soldiers dwell. (Iceshard Keep) {{waypoint -564, 119, 160}}
1. Search out where the Kromzek commoners dwell. (Old Kael) {{waypoint -1206, 40, -1560}}
1. Search out the great Kromzek Arena. (Grand Rallosian Arena) {{waypoint -790, 61, -247}}
1. Search out the main Kromzek city (New Kael) {{waypoint -367, 32, -95}}
1. Search out where the king of the Kromzek empire dwells. (The throne of King Tormax the Ancient) {{waypoint -94, -33, 1492}}
