## Steps

1. Attend a clandestine meeting between Ayr'Dal activisists - enter a small instance called [[Spacious House]] {{waypoint -111, -16, 293}}
1. Speak with [[Lady Greensea]]
1. Defeat the 4 [[A mysterious attacker|mysterious attackers]] - two attack immediately, one is in the entrance room and one is upstairs in the courtyard.
1. Return to Thavian Destrus
1. Search [[The Freeport Registrar]] office in East Freeport for clues {{waypoint -122, -37, 44}}
   - One of the books on the the table in the lefthand room is the clue.  It has neither a sparkly effect or a glow on roll-over - just pay attention to the mouse cursor and click when it turns into a hand.
1. Return to Thavian Destrus
1. Confront Lady Greensea outside her house in South Freeport she wanders around near {{waypoint -113, -18, 262}}
1. Kill 6 [[A masked Ayr'Dal|masked Ayr'Dal]] around [[Champion's Coliseum]] in West Freeport
1. Return to Thavian.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g @ lvl 95
