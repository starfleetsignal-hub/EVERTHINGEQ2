## Note

- Completion of this quest is no longer needed to access [[The Tomb of Valor]], but it is still required to start the [[The Bone Bladed Claymore]].
- This is a repeatable quest

## Steps

1. Kill [[A defiled squire (Stormhold)|defiled squires]] on the level 1, near [[The Chessboard (Stormhold)|The Chessboard]] at {{waypoint 42, 5, -84}} until you get:
   - 6th page of Squire's Journal
   - 7th page of Squire's Journal
   - 8th page of Squire's Journal
1. Return to the [[A crushed librarian|librarian]].
1. Kill [[A defiled knight|defiled knights]] until you get:
   - 21st Page of the Knight's Journal
   - 28th Page of the Knight's Journal
1. Return to the librarian.
1. Kill the following:
   - [[Guard Captain Hess]], in the fireplace of the western barracks at {{waypoint -72, 5, -160}} on level 1. Placeholder is [[A defiled knight]].
   - [[Lord Androus]], near The Chessboard at {{waypoint 78, 5, -85}} on level 1.
   - [[A zombie handmaiden|zombie handmaidens]] on level 2 at {{waypoint -101, -25, -73}} until you find *Lady Chesgard's Journal*.
1. Return to the librarian.
1. Find the key to [[The Tomb of Valor]] on level 1 at the foot of the first statue on your right as you walk down from the zone-in; right next to [[Sir Valinayle]] at  {{waypoint -31.72, 14.34, 22.22}}
1. Enter the The Tomb of Valor from a metal grate on the floor at {{waypoint -14.64, -24.88, -44.19}} on level 2 and kill [[Lord Chesgard]]. See [[The Tomb of Valor]] for details on how to spawn him.
1. Return to the librarian.

## Rewards

- At least 36s 93c
- [[Cuff of the Stormscholar]]
