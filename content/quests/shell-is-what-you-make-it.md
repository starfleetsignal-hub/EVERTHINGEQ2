## Steps

1. One of (both are close by, around {{waypoint 542.53, 53.15, -196.39}}):
   1. Collect 10 lightcrawler carapaces from the lightcrawlers around [[Aurelian Coast]]. *Note: Not a guaranteed update.*

1. Return to [[Gaedra Rugon]] at {{waypoint 626.32, 94.57, -419.61}}
