## Prerequisites

- You need at least +30,000 natural faction with The Order of Rime to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Emperor's Athenaeum]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]]. {{waypoint 868, -128, 164}}
1. Obtain the following items from within the instance:
   - 5 scrolls bearing the Solus mark. These are gathered from the ground or in alcoves. Known locations are:
     - {{waypoint 1, 0, -5}}
     - {{waypoint 23, 1, -5}}
     - {{waypoint 62, 4, -80}}
     - {{waypoint 83, 5, -103}}
     - {{waypoint 45, 6, 87}}
     - {{waypoint 46, 6, 100}}
     - {{waypoint 47, 4, 98}}
     - {{waypoint 108, 7, 17}}
     - {{waypoint 220, 11, 16}}
     - {{waypoint 200, 9, 29}}
   - 2 runes bearing the Solus mark. These are obtained from killing any two named monsters within the instance.
1. Return to Delreri Feldrek.

- At least 41g 63s 17c
- +3500 faction with **The Order of Rime**

  - [[Legionnaire's Sabatons]]
  - [[Master's Shoes]]
  - [[Prophet's Chain Boots]]
