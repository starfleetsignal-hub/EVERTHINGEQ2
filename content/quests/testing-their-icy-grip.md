## Steps

1. Speak to [[Wuuf]].  He gives you [[A gnoll totem]]
1. Place the totem at {{waypoint -983, -542, -1820}} (click on the green sparkles)
1. Return to Wuuf.  He gives you another totem.
1. Place the totem at the same location mentioned above.
1. Slay the [[An enraged shadowbeast|enraged shadowbeast]] (89^ berserker) that spawns.
1. Return to [[Wuuf]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- +1,100 faction with **Snowfang Gnolls**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Snowfang Warrior Engraved Ring]]
  - [[Snowfang Arcane Engraved Ring]]
  - [[Snowfang Shamanistic Engraved Ring]]
  - [[Snowfang Hunter Engraved Ring]]
