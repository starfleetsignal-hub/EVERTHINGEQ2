## Steps

1. Perform the following tasks:
   - Use [[Uelmondi's Ornament]] to capture a wisp [[Blazing ignus]] in [[Aetherscar]] to receive [[Uelmondi's Empowered Ornament]].  Look around the NW corner of Aetherscar {{waypoint 632, 28, -247}}.
   - Use [[Uelmondi's Empowered Ornament]] on **a shard of aether crystal** {{waypoint 701, -31, -174}}.  Crystals are common along edges of valley floor and can be tab targeted. ![](images/A_shard_of_aether_crystal.jpg)
   - Kill 8 cnidcara encounters.
1. Return to [[Uelmondi]] at {{waypoint 418, 36, 39}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Taboo Occult Coin]]
- -1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- +1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
