## Steps

1. Enter [[Castle Mistmoore]] and explore the following first-floor areas:
   - [[Castle Mistmoore's Foyer]] {{waypoint 97, 46, -20}}
   - [[The Main Hall]] {{waypoint 0, 42, 3}}
   - [[Mistmoore's Altar]] {{waypoint -67, 43, -4}}
   - [[The Descent into Darkness]] {{waypoint -25, 21, 11}}
   - [[Entertainer's Quarters]] {{waypoint -16, 38, -112}}
   - [[The Caitiff's Quarters]]  {{waypoint -16, 38, 103}}
1. Retrieve the papers from the [[The Devoted Caitiff|Caitiff]]'s chest. {{waypoint -9, 38, 129}}
1. Return to [[Brand]].
