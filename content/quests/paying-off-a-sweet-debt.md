## Steps

1. You will need to collect 10 honey samples from the trees in the shire. Randomly beehives will spawn among the trees and harvesting them will give you a honey sample. There is a random chance of bees attacking you as you collect, but they are very low level and easy to kill.
1. Once you have all the honey, return to Rondo for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Halfling Trickster's Ring]]
