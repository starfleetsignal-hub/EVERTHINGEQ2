## Steps

1. Kill the following in [[The Crypt of Betrayal]]:
   - 20 [[A deathly scarab (Crypt of Betrayal)|deathly scarabs]] -- all over the zone, very easy to find
   - 15 [[A forsaken shriller|forsaken shrillers]]
   - 10 [[A restless corpse|restless corpse]]s -- go to the mausoleum room at {{waypoint 126, 7, -102}}.  Restless corpses are on a shared spawn with [[A diseased corpse|diseased corpse]]es, so clear those as well.

- [[Mantle of the Dreadnaught]] - Bruiser
- [[Shoulderpads of Nature's Fatebinder]] - Fury
- [[Mantle of the Silent Fist Initiate]] - Monk
- [[Mantle of the Spiritweaver]] - Mystic
- [[Blessed Ivy Engraved Shoulderpads]] - Warden
- [[Shoulderpads of Spirit Shaping]] - [[Channeler]]
