## Notes

- *Timed quest: Shattered Seas solo daily, 2 minutes and 30 seconds to make the climb.*

## Steps

1. Race to the tip of [[Karrabukk's Peak (POI)|Karrabukk's Peak]] {{waypoint 251, 357, -551}}
   - You will have a debuff that removes flight and foot speed buffs
   - You will be attacked along the way by [[A hungry swordbeak pterodon|hungry swordbeak pterodons]]

## Rewards

- 3 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- At least 1p 67g 40s 5500 status
- Hidden reward: the achievement [[The Mountaineer]] (30800 status)
