## Steps

1. Locate Halkon Tormax (move forward in the left aisle until you get an update. Do NOT move further at that point or the quest cannot be completed in that instance)
1. Clear out the brew house without being discovered. Do this by knocking over the statue in the trophy room.
1. Kill [[Halkon Tormax]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Chronomancer's Fetish]] (All Mages)
  - [[Dormant Scrying Orb]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Orb of Coursing Velium]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Othmir Skull Goblet]] (All Priests)
