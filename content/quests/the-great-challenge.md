## Starting the Quest

This quest is being held in conjunction with SOE's [[Living Legacy]] advertising campaign during the summer of 2008. The quest is automatically given when players create a new character.[(1)](http://eq2players.station.sony.com/en/news_archive_content.vm?id=1765&section=News&locale=en_US)

## Steps

Achieve level 20 within 14 days from the time you first log in your new character.

Available to anyone during the Living Legacy summer event.

## Rewards

- At least 10g
- One of the following titles as appropriate for your alignment (good/evil):
  - [[Knight of D'Lere]]
  - [[Knight of Bayle]]
  - [[Knight of the Exiled]]
