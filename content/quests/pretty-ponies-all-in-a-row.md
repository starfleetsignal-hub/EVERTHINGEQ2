*This quest is repeatable up to 3 times..*

## Steps

1. Kill 6 centaurs in The Thundering Steppes.
1. Return to Waylon March.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 91c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Balanced Tulwar]] (Berserker, Guardian, Paladin, Shadowknight)
  - [[Cestus of the Veracious Predator]] (Beastlord, Bruiser, Monk)
  - [[Flail of the Steppes]] (All Priests)
  - [[Nightfall Katana]] (Assasin, Bard, Ranger, Rogue, Swashbuckler)
  - [[Stave of Tyranny]] (Bruiser, Monk)
  - [[Thunderous Buckler]] (All Priests)
  - [[Thunderous Effigy]] (All Priests)
  - [[Thunderous Orb]] (All Mages)
  - [[Thunderous Round Shield]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Thunderous Tower Shield]] (Berserker, Guardian, Paladin, Shadowknight)
  - [[Wandering Magi's Walking Stick]] (All Mages)
