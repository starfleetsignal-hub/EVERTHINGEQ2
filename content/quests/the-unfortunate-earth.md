## Steps

1. Lith needs the following:
   - Harvest 4 **bog mud**
   - Kill 4 [[A bog slug|bog slugs]]
   - Kill 4 [[Bog sludge]]s
1. Return to Botanist Lith
1. Speak to [[Lieutenant Dawson]] near the Nettleville gate {{waypoint 567, -27, 418}}
   - Once this quest is complete and if you are level 5, she will offer you the starter of the Peat Bog quest line, [[Reclaiming the Bog]].

## Rewards

- At least 92c
