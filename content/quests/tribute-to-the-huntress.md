## Starting the Quest

This quest is started by speaking to "a priest of Marr" (for good cities) or "a celebrating barbarian" (evil cities), one of whom can be found in each city:

- [[North Qeynos]], inside [[Irontoe's East]] at {{waypoint 336, -21, 140}}
- [[West Freeport]], inside the [[Blood Haze Inn]] at {{waypoint 289, -3, 84}}
- [[Kelethin]], inside [[Jysolin's Pub]] at {{waypoint 202, 108, 343}}
- [[Neriak]], inside the [[Dockside Markets]] {{waypoint -165, -5, 254}}
- [[Gorowyn]], on the docks in [[Timorous Deep]] at {{waypoint 2388, 19, 1383}}

## Steps

![Such a marvelous pomegranate!](images/Tribute_to_the_Huntress_-_pomegranate.png)

1. You will be asked to do one of two tasks:
   - Harvest 3 pomegranates from the tree outside Marrsfist Keep in [[The Commonlands]] {{waypoint 625, -45, 700}}
   - ***Or***
   - Travel to [[The Graveyard]], adjacent to [[Freeport]].  Kill Undead Crusaders, Knights, Lancers and Priests until they drop the artifacts.  The undead are located near [[The Destroyed Tower of Marr]] at {{waypoint 49, -3, 122}}.
1. Travel to the [[Cairn of the Huntress]] in [[New Halas]] in the [[Frostfang Sea]].  Leave a tribute at the spot marked with a green glow {{waypoint 40, 149, -45}}

## Rewards

- [[Valorian Bloom]] (first completion and from 11th completion on)
- [[Pink Lilies in an Elegant Vase]] (fifth completion)
- [[Title: Disciple of Erollisi]] (tenth completion)
