## Steps

1. Deliver one [[Dew Collector Kit]] to [[Camp D'Sas]] {{waypoint 14.50, -90.66, -909.74}} in the Sinking Sands.
1. Deliver one [[Dew Collector Kit]] to the Ortellian Encampment at the [[Swiftrider's Caravan]] {{waypoint 65.53, -86.44, -768.42}} in [[The Pillars of Flame]].
1. Deliver one [[Dew Collector Kit]] to the [[Croc Hunter Camp]] {{waypoint -1349.54, -226.83, -579.11}} in the Sinking Sands.
1. Speak to [[Ewart Nefird]] in [[Sinking Sands]] {{waypoint -522.77, -86.31, -1053.10}}.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 7s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- [[Fanciful Faucets]] -- *recipe book*
- *Unlocks the ability to purchase level 50-59 recipe books from Ewart*.
