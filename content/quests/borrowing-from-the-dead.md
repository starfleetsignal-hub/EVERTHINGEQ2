## Notes

The [[Ideas on repaired armor-bits]] recipe auto-scribes. You do *not* need to locate it in inventory and scribe it manually.

## Steps

1. Find 6 pices of [[Rusty armor]] (from a dead attacker, starting at {{waypoint -410, 14, -1705}}). You can harvest one armor piece from each, but they respawn quickly.![a dead attacker](images/A_dead_attacker.jpg)
1. Repair 3 armor
   - Craft 3 [[Repaired armor]] bits at the goblin forge near [[Irontooth]].  Each combine requires 1 Thaumic coal.
1. Speak with [[Irontooth]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +2,000 faction with **Broken Toe Clan**
- +2,000 faction with **Goblins of Nurga**
