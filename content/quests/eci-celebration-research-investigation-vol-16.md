## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Collect 3 [[Ice Shard|ice shards]]
   - These are rare body drops from trash mobs in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] that will show quest indicator by their name. Good chances from a metallic tainted auduborian,  a floral devourer, a beetle crab.
   - These are group updates
   - You will spend on average 15 minutes getting to and collecting shards
1. Afterwards you take those shards to a shrine in [[Everfrost]], shrine spawns in random spot in that zone.
   1. This is NOT a group update.
   1. One person clicking the altar will cause the altar to disappear and reappear in another location
   1. It is not clear if there is only ever one altar in the zone that is moved by other groups
   1. It may be possible to click simultaneously to get a group update
   1. You may spend as much as 30 minutes tracking down an altar to click
   1. Possible Locations include:
      - {{waypoint 580, -35, -1502}} [[Icegill Lagoon]]
      - {{waypoint 128.12, -3.52, -1153.37}} [[The Lady's View]]
      - {{waypoint -405, -38, -1099}}
      - {{waypoint -46, 21, -384}}
      - {{waypoint 475, -1, -198}}
      - {{waypoint -177, 43, -678}}
      - {{waypoint 54.56, 23.04, -691.77}}
      - {{waypoint 586, -35, 493}}
1. Return to [[Herald of the Wintry Guardian]] {{waypoint 75, -35, -1687}} in [[Everfrost]]
