This is the fifth and final of the Kerran racial quests in Freeport.

## Steps

1. Batarra sends you to talk to [[Custodian Zaddar Sullissia]] in [[The Graveyard]].  She understands that it's repulsive to work so closely with your enemies, but the knowledge gained will outweigh the unpleasantness.

Continue with the quest [[The Door Knocker]] of the [[Freeport Districts Timeline]] in [[The Graveyard]].
