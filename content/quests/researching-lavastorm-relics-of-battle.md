## Steps

1. Go to the lava pit with a drake skeleton in [[Hydra Pass]]. {{waypoint -11, -12.5, -121}}
1. Collect relics of the battle by brushing the dirt from mounds on the ground, waiting a few moments, and then picking up the uncovered items.![dirt mound](images/Dirt_mound.jpg)
1. Return to Shrine of Thunder and speak to [[Researcher Caudil]]. {{waypoint -154, -117, 641}}

- At least 61s 33c
- [[Flame Researcher's Robe]]
