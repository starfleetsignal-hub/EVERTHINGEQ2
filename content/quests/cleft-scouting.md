## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- You must have +20,000 faction with the Court of the Blades to unlock this quest. Once +50,000 faction has been achieved, however, this quest will no longer be available.
- None of the scout NPCs will be visible until their location is approached.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Clefts of Rujark]] and talk to [[Scout Fariq]] near [[The Sinking Sands (The Clefts of Rujark POI)|the Sinking Sands]] gate. {{waypoint 137, -7, -96}}
1. Find two of the following scouts (chosen randomly):
   - [[Scout Afif]] {{waypoint -69, -21, -285}} near the red crystal pool
   - [[Scout Karida]] {{waypoint -135, -2, -101}} in the [[Cleft of Might]]
   - [[Scout Mina]] {{waypoint 33, 0, 111}} in [[The Commissary]]
   - [[Scout Talib]] {{waypoint -16, -1, 27}} near the elevator
   - [[Scout Vasha]] {{waypoint -167, -16, -235}} in [[The Charnel]]
1. Return to Scout Fariq.
1. Return to Dirar Muti.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 23s 29c
- +500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
