## Steps

1. Examine ![the knife](images/Image:Peacock_Club_Knife_in_Table.jpg) on the table. {{waypoint 6, 0, 4}}
1. Speak to [[Camel Dealer Ha'kal]] outside the [[The Court of the Coin (POI)|Court of the Coin]] at {{waypoint 175, 139, 2}}.
   - Camel Deal Ha'kal randomly spawns as a Blades, Coin, or Truth faction NPC. So he may or may not be aggro to you. If aggro, either kill and hope for non-aggro repop, or spam 'H' and talk while he is killing you.
1. Kill camels of the lesser tribes found in the [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]]:
   - [[A Baldoras camel|Baldoras]]: {{waypoint -220, -105, -400}}
   - [[A Hafasal camel|Hafasal]]: {{waypoint -74, -111, -140}}
   - [[An Amal'an camel|Amal'an]]: {{waypoint -881, -159, -1205}} & {{waypoint -331, -146, -1271}}  (Amal'an camels roams from suggested location around the [[The Twin Tears|Twin Tears]] and back in circles, counter-clockwise).
1. Return to Camel Dealer Ha'kal.
1. Find and clear out the Baldoras supply camp in the Sinking Sands. {{waypoint -220, -105, -400}}
1. Examine ![the scroll](images/Image:A_Baldoras_family_scroll.jpg) on top of the two boxes at the Baldoras supply camp.
1. Complete the following within 4 minutes:
   - Visit the ![secret burial site](images/Image:The_Burial_Site.jpg) at {{waypoint -1009, -120, -1249}}
   - Inspect ![the burial site](images/Image:The_Burial_Site.jpg) at {{waypoint -1002, -129, -1278}}
1. Return to [[Peacock Lord Wad'dah Haz'Iz]].
1. Leave [[The Peacock Club]].
