## Steps

1. Visit [[Cardin Ward]] and speak to someone about the Horde key
   - Find [[A treasure hunter]] {{waypoint -474.78, 11.71, -21.63}}
1. Find this key-selling Wayward {{waypoint -409.36, 11.03, -19.30}}
1. Locate [[A Spiritual Swashbuckler]] {{waypoint -407.3, 11.23, -18.82}}
1. Buy a [[Cardin Ward Stout]] from [[Margo McNerian]] {{waypoint -315.18, 12.65, -39.98}}
1. Return to [[A Spiritual Swashbuckler]] {{waypoint -407.3, 11.23, -18.82}}
1. Kill Horde that can hold things
   - Kill 5 Horde that can hold things.  This is an uncommon update.
     - [[A Tirun dreadsplitter]]
     - [[A Skirth dreadskulk]]
1. Go to the treasure's location in [[Cardin Ward]] at {{waypoint -473.86, 11.68, -18.69}}, which is very close to the [[A treasure hunter|treasure hunter]].
   - Click on the treasure chest to open and get your reward.

![treasure chest](images/Treasure_chest.jpg)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Soldier's Ethereal Manacles]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Brilliant Ethereal Manacle]] (All Mages)
  - [[Ethereal Manacles of Meditation]] (All Priests)
  - [[Shadowed Ethereal Manacles]] (All Scouts)
