## Steps

1. Enter [[Hold of Rime: The Ascent]]
1. Hail Berlon's ghost by the zone-in
1. Find Berlon's corpse and resurrect him
   1. Locate Berlon's corpse {{waypoint -570, 86, -354}} near [[Witchdoctor Vuh'Duh]]. (walk around, point of update is not at the corpse)
   1. Resurrect Berlon
      - *Note: Only a priest can do that*
1. Speak to Berlon
