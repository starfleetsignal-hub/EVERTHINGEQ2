## Steps

1. ![Bag of Evidence](images/Bag_of_Evidence.jpg)Gather 6 Bags of Evidence between Ahkaa and the summoned cloud. (group-wide update) Some possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint -666.61, 218.42, -719.60}}
   - {{waypoint -658.65, 219.24, -727.16}}
   - {{waypoint -645.36, 218.32, -718.40}}
   - {{waypoint -622.26, 182.48, -610.27}}
   - {{waypoint -639.68, 182.35, -579.49}}
1. Find the Take to the Skies cloud at the southern end of the [[Auric Palisades]] at {{waypoint -713.30, 144.97, -364.36}} and ride the cloud to the [[Isles of Wisdom]]
1. Speak with [[Ruhk]] on the [[Isles of Wisdom]] at {{waypoint -915.55, 189.53, 332.40}}.
1. Find a way to offer hope to the people of the Isles of Wisdom **by completing three quests for **[[Ecihoolo]] on [[Isles of Wisdom]] at {{waypoint -776.50, 191.59, 223.73}}
   1. [[Components to Freedom]]
   1. [[Wind Beneath Their Wings]]
   1. [[The Hands of Fate]]
1. Learn of an aviak item of significance taken by the Aurai by completing the [[Sacred to the Aurai (Collection)]], then reading the [[Sacred to the Aurai (House Item)|book]].
   - Pages 1-11 spawn on and around the Yazdani platforms in [[Zenith Helionae]]:
     - {{waypoint 59.31, 146.34, -32.32}} Lakeside area (pages 2, 5, 8, 11)
     - {{waypoint 167.81, 162.47, 47.34}} South-West area (pages 2, 3, 6, 9)
     - {{waypoint 16.67, 159.94, 111.29}} South-East area (pages 2, 5, 6, 8, 11)
     - {{waypoint 70.81, 150.42, -343.84}} Northern area (pages 4, 7, 10)
   - Page 12 drops from [[A Page Turner]]  (looks like tornado) - possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 179.32, 160.03, 78.73}}
     - {{waypoint 25.04, 138.34, -99.27}}
     - {{waypoint -48.52, 138.85, -161.74}}
     - {{waypoint 101.89, 150.88, -234.89}}
   - Turn in the collection to receive the book [[Sacred to the Aurai (House Item)|Sacred to the Aurai]].
     - There's a collector ([[Seengo]]) in Sanctuary at {{waypoint 210, 286, 947}}. To get back there use the cloud at {{waypoint 313.82, 167.79, -52.04}}  to get to [[Auric Palisades]], then the Travel portal {{waypoint -768.89, 83.86, 591.98}} to [[Molted Island]].
   - Read the book.
     - *Note: Those who completed the collection but deleted the book "Sacred to the Aurai" can pick up a replacement near Ecihoolo {{waypoint -774, 191, 222}} in [[Zimara Breadth]].*
1. Return to [[Ruhk]] on the [[Isles of Wisdom]]
   - *Note: Take the Travel portal {{waypoint -1092.66, 181.87, 737.26}} on [[Molted Island]] to [[Zimara Breadth]]*
   - *Note: Take to the Skies cloud on [[Auric Palisades]] {{waypoint -713.30, 144.97, -364.36}} to [[Isles of Wisdom]]*
   - Speak to [[Ruhk]] {{waypoint -915.55, 189.53, 332.40}} (You must read the book to be able to turn the quest in)
1. ![Windmaid's Lance](images/Windmaid's_Lance.jpg)Search for the Windmaid's Lance:
   - *Note: Take to the Skies cloud {{waypoint -685.09, 144.01, -4.62}} to [[Zenith Helionae]], then use Platform landed upon to go to [[Columnar Highgarden]]*
   1. Find Windmaid's Lance at {{waypoint 766.15, 227.66, -791.48}}
   1. Click the Lance
   1. kill the adds
   1. collect the Windmaid's Lance
1. ![Swirling Sphere of Color](images/Swirling_Sphere_of_Color.jpg)Search out the Swirling Sphere of Color at {{waypoint 344.77, 402.00, 598.72}}
   - *Note: Take to the Skies cloud {{waypoint 614.89, 206.37, -506.63}} to [[Claved Fate Islets]] (if you cannot fly) -or-*
   - *NOTE: It's shorter to take the Skies cloud back to [[Zenith Helionae]],* *(if you can fly)*, then to go *Strungstone Peaks. The orb is on the peak behind you.*
1. Place the Windmaid's Lance at a central location behind enemy lines in [[Auric Palisades]]
   1. Take to the Skies cloud {{waypoint 848.15, 182.39, 309.78}} to [[Isles of Wisdom]] or to [[Auric Palisades]] (or just evac).
   1. ![Windmaid's Lance Location](images/Windmaid's_Lance_Location.jpg)Place the Windmaid's Lance at {{waypoint -483.21, 95.40, -548.25}}
      - *Look for sparkles in a small cul-de-sac*
1. Return to [[Ruhk]] on the [[Isles of Wisdom]] at {{waypoint -915.55, 189.53, 332.40}}
   - *Note: Take to the Skies cloud {{waypoint -713.30, 144.97, -364.36}} to [[Isles of Wisdom]]*
