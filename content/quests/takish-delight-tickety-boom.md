## Notes

Materials required includes:

- 12x [[Apatite Gem]]
- 12x [[Brittlebush Taproot]]
- 30x [[Astral Candle]]

## Steps

1. Take the lift at {{waypoint 131.71, 31.39, 21.67}} then find 5 scrolls upstairs. ![Elddar Scroll](images/Elddar_Scroll.png)
   - *Note: The lift is in the alcove behind Gnobert, and the switch is a gem to the left of the opening when entering the alcove.*
1. Return to [[Gnorbert]], take the portal back to the Branching Gateway and then proceed to the Elddar Exchange.
1. Find 5 Elddar Exchange scrolls.
1. Return to [[Gnorbert]].
1. Collect 6 Elddar blooms ![Elddar blooms](images/Elddar_Blossoms.png)
1. Return to [[Gnorbert]]
1. Craft 3 Bloom Experiments
1. Collect materials
   - Collect 3 treant samara ![treant samara](images/Elddar_Treant_Samara_Fruit.png)
   - Collect 3 fairy drake dust ![fairy drake dust](images/Elddar_Fairy_Drake_Dust.png)
1. Return to [[Gnorbert]]
1. Craft 3 Creature Experiments
1. Return to [[Gnorbert]]

## Rewards

- At least 9p 30g 45s 97c 67,200 status  - *(2024-04-15 These coins and Status were not awarded)*
- 2  [[Blue Takish Blossom]]
- 4  [[D.I.R.T.Y. D.O.G.G.]]
