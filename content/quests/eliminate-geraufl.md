## Steps

1. Roam the jail area and then head back toward the stairs to get the message "It seems that the froglock has been moved." {{waypoint 166.38, -23.42, 135.82}}
1. Enter [[A Musty Dungeon]] {{waypoint 132, -26, 141}} in the jail area of [[The Freeport Militia House]] in [[West Freeport (POI)|West Freeport]]. It is also called "Lower Dungeons".
1. Kill the guards and then the jailers.
   - There is a [[Ures Bocmeni, the Jailor]] that grants AA exp, you might want to mentor down to get it, he is level 32.
1. Ignite the barrel to blow up the jail door.
1. Kill [[Geraufl]].
   - Geraufl will stun you as soon as the first hit is in. He then escapes thus completing the quest for you.
   - If you one-shot Geraufl the quest will not update and you must zone out and back in again.
