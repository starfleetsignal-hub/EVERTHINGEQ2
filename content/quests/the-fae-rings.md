## Steps

Note: You must be Fae to perform this quest

1. Speak with [[Restauranteur Brizeyl Myrika]] in [[Joleena's Restaurant]] at {{waypoint 536.34, 71.21, 322.41}}
1. Examine to bookshelf in the [[Kelethin Research Library]] at {{waypoint 142.14, 108.85, 178.99}}
   - You will receive the book, [[The History of the Fae]], which you can read to obtain the quest, [[History of the Fae]]
1. Return to [[Lady Aileeta Reila]]

## Rewards

- At least 2s 13c
- Experience
- 3 [[Joleena's Cinnamon Cookie]]
- 3 [[Maarita's rosehip tea]]
