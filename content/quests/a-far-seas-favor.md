## Prerequisite Condition(s)

*The Sundered Frontier Tradeskill timeline is for tradeskillers level 80 and above; you can not start before you have reached level 80 in your profession*

## Steps

1. Take the platform to Paineel.
1. Investigate the buildings there:
   - Investigate the large building to the west: [[Hall of Wizardry]] {{waypoint 1972, -300, 3348}}
   - Investigate the large building to the southwest: [[Hall of the Fell Blade]] {{waypoint 1872, -300, 3415}}
   - Investigate the rooms acessed deep within the Hall of the Fell Blade: [[Paineel Council Chamber]] {{waypoint 1873, -295, 3470}}
   - Investigate the area to the south at the bottom of the ramp: [[Paineel Commons]] {{waypoint 1795, -299, 3457}}
   - Investigate the area to the south at the top of the ramp: [[The Mercantile Court]] {{waypoint 1726, -282, 3424}}
   - Investigate the corridor to the east: [[The Hall of Necromancy]] {{waypoint 1651, -268, 3228}}
1. Return to Felice for your reward (right-click on platform  {{waypoint 1919.64, -301.52, 3317.61}} (and choose Spire Island option).

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
