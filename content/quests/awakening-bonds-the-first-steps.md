## Prerequirements

- Must be a level 100 [[Priest]]
- Must be level 5 of one [[Ascension]] class
- <s>Must have completed the City Timeline</s> *Got removed with LU105*
  - <s>[[The City of Qeynos Timeline]] (Good) including Archtype line up to [[Kaedrin's Fate]]</s>
  - <s>[[The City of Freeport Timeline]] (Evil) including Archtype line up to [[Your Eternal Reward]]</s>
- <s>Must have your [[Epic Weapons|Fabled Class Epic Weapon]] 1.0 Quest completed - *Mythical not needed and the [[Epic Repercussions]] doesn't matter*</s> *Got removed with LU105*
- <s>Must have completed the [[Kunark Ascending Timeline]]</s> *Got removed with LU105*

## Requirements

- Must have 50,000 faction with [[Disciples of the Circling Destiny (Faction)]] in Stonebrunt Highlands.  This is part of the Ning Yun Retreat portion of [[The Stonebrunt Highlands Timeline]] (You must actually complete the entire Ning Yun Retreat portion of the timeline, just getting faction is not enough) ((While that is technically true, the Ning Yun quests are the fastest way to gain faction, since the last quests give 10k each)).

If you have already completed the Epic 2.0 timeline on a character on the same server, you can purchase a "Guise of Pursuit" on that character and use it on the current one to fulfill the 50K faction requirement (once the Stonebrunt quests are done) to buy the "Bamboo Shoot Soup".

## Starting The Quest

1. Go to a hidden tunnel that leads into the [[Cave of Shrines]] on a hill between [[The Hidden Vale]] and the Brokenskull Valley in [[The Commonlands]] {{waypoint -134.19, -10.74, 791.27}} and click all 11 clickable objects. These 11 items and the characters' corresponding thoughts upon inspection in clockwise order are:
   - A golden bowl on a table {{waypoint -12.93, 9.08, 8.85}} : "It's covered with dust. You write your name in the basin."
   - A stone urn next to a stone altar {{waypoint -11.33, 7.58, 15.75}}: "This stone urn has some odd runic markings, but doesn't seem otherwise remarkable."
   - An altar of Innoruuk {{waypoint -9.90, 7.57, 17.23}}: "Scattered on the altar among the candles and bloodstains are what look like small offerings to Innoruuk."
   - A looted chest {{waypoint -4.26, 7.44, 22.17}}: "Whatever was in this chest was looted a long time ago."
   - A sealed chest {{waypoint 23.75, 3.22, 30.63}}: "This sealed chest bears the likeness of what appears to be Rallos Zek, although it looks like an older depiction of him."
   - A broken jar next to an open, broken chest {{waypoint 55.35, 0.02, 21.06}}: "It's completely shattered."
   - A votive offering {{waypoint 63.82, -2.14, -4.95}}: "Candles and wax covers this table, although the purpose of the arrangement is unclear."
   - The lid of an open, elongated chest {{waypoint 45.84, 1.51, 9.21}}: "Was this a chest.. or a coffin?"
   - A bloodstained idol of the Faceless {{waypoint 37.90, 2.91, 15.89}}: "This bloodstained idol closely resembles the Faceless. In spite of its apparent age, it is in pristine condition."
   - A votive brazier {{waypoint 7.86, 5.74, 9.51}}: "This brazier has a small collection of charred feathers and bones in it, and a crusty residue of putrescence lines the inside."
   - A small incense chest on the ground next to a sealed chest {{waypoint 2.87, 7.15, 3.80}}: "This small chest seems to contain a collection of old incense and spices."
1. Go to a hidden tunnel that leads into the [[Hidden Chapel]] in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint 123.38, -25.98, -392.42}} at the end of a mite burrow {{waypoint 98.00, -21.17, -418.59}} and click all 10 clickable objects. These 10 items and the characters' corresponding thoughts upon inspection in clockwise order are:
   - A huge weapon chest on the left {{waypoint -7.54, 0.00, 4.38}}: "The chest is huge, and likely carried weapons from training. It bears the symbols of Mithaniel Marr along its sides."
   - A large round slab on the floor {{waypoint -0.04, 0.21, -0.05}}: "The colors used to decorate this seem vibrant, even given its apparent age."
   - A disc monument leaning on the wall {{waypoint 3.10, 0.00, 9.23}}: "What is this, and how did someone even get it in here?"
   - A statue next to the table {{waypoint 8.27, 0.00, 10.08}}: "The markings on this stone statue are too faded to figure out what this was meant to be."
   - An artsy dreamcatcher {{waypoint 8.09, 2.50, 11.45}}: "This appears to sway in the breeze, although the air in here seems to be perfectly still."
   - A dirty table {{waypoint 11.07, 0.00, 2.60}}: "This table is covered in old cups and plates. Appearantly no one cleaned up."
   - A forecfully opened chest {{waypoint 11.56, 0.00, -4.59}}: "The locks have been shattered, and whatever was inside is gone now."
   - An apothecary mortar {{waypoint 5.80, 1.02, -7.80}}: "There is a sweet odor coming off this bowl. The old herbs and oily residue appear to have been used for healing and the care of the sick."
   - A planter of Tunare on the floor {{waypoint 1.73, 0.00, -3.99}}: "There are large shriveled up roots at the bottom of this pot. The sides are lined with imagery depicting Tunare."
   - A small keepsake box on the table {{waypoint -6.11, 1.41, -7.54}}: "This small keepsake box contains drief up rose petals, a pair of heart-shaped arrowheads, and a battered locket."
1. Go to a hidden tunnel that leads into the [[Sanctuary of Divine Unity]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] by the [[East Bear Cave]] {{waypoint -1310.82, 13.59, -754.86}} and click all 7 clickable objects. These 7 items and the characters' corresponding thoughts upon inspection along the cave walls are:
   - A pile of broken jars and some boxes (right hand side of the cave) {{waypoint -15.11, -7.37, -20.27}}: "This pile of broken jars and empty wooden boxes may have been left here when this place was evacuated."
   - A statue of Innoruuk (right hand side of the cave, next to the previous update) {{waypoint -12.55, -8.26, -26.48}}: "This statue looks slightly different than the others. The face seems to resemble that of the Innoruuk."
   - A small statue of Brell Serilis (left hand side of the cave) {{waypoint 13.36, -13.15, -31.80}}: "This small statue appears to be Brell Serilis, with small statues of Cazic-Thule, Bristlebane, Tunare, and Prexus around it."
   - A sealed ornate chest (right hand side of the cave) {{waypoint 9.28, -13.46, -44.92}}: "This ornate chest is sealed shut, so the contents can't be ascertained. However, the outside bears symbolism representing several deities, including Bristlebane, Erollisi Marr, and Bertoxxulous."
   - A rack of used weapons next to a statue (left hand side of the cave) {{waypoint 24.83, -14.20, -39.96}}: "These weapons look battered from use. They bear the sigils of both Rallos Zek and Mithaniel Marr."
   - A pile of empty book crates (update comes from the large bottom one) next to the weapons rack {{waypoint 28.95, -14.08, -41.13}}: "These crates have shreds of parchment littering the bottoms. These may have held books at one time."
   - An unfinished disc monument (right hand side of the cave) {{waypoint 24.93, -14.82, -61.96}}: "It looks like someone was in the middle of constructing this monument when they left - or were driven out."
1. Hail and carefully follow the dialog with [[Haylis Losaren|Haylis Losaren <Priest of Unity>]] {{waypoint 43.24, -14.69, -47.57}} to start the quest.
   - He will spawn once you have inspected all of the items in all 3 tunnels.
  - ***Notes**: If you cannot see the hidden tunnel in Nektulos Forest, go back to the other two and re-click. The clickies in the previous tunnels are a pre-requirement to seeing the entrance to the Sanctuary of Divine Unity.*

## Steps

1. Find the three disciples (In any order)
   - Find the First Disciple for the [[Censer of Elixia]]
     1. Speak to [[Evoker Nideela]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]] {{waypoint -121.18, -55.27, -242.63}}
     1. Speak to [[Marta Terrilon]] just off the dock in [[Everfrost]] {{waypoint -55, -3, 128}} (If she doesn't advance quest, do [[Marta Terrilon's Fur Hunt|her repeatable]] until she does, (took 3 times)
     1. Complete the Ning Yun Retreat Questline in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]]. Starting with [[Repelling the Gruengach]] through [[The Three Tidings]] at a minimum.
        - *Note: If you do not complete the [[The Three Tidings]] to learn about the 4 truths, speaking to Olnita for step six will not offer the correct responses to further the quest.*
     1. Purchase [[Bamboo Shoot Soup]] for 500p from [[Dao Tom]] in the [[Ning Yun Retreat]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]] (Requires 50k [[Disciples of the Circling Destiny (Faction)]]) {{waypoint 513, 333, 474}} OR using the Guise of Pursuit faction booster obtained by doing the Epic 2.0 on another character on the same account and server.
     1. Speak with [[Ma Yu]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]] {{waypoint 590, 334, 453}} Follow the dialog as usual.
     1. Speak with [[Olnita]] in [[Hua Mein Village]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]] {{waypoint -303, 250, 3128}}
        - *Note1: You can use CoV or CoH in order to teleport to a group member close to her, do the Hua Mein access quest line to gain access to the village, or just fly over the wall. If you do not complete the The Three Tidings to learn about the 4 truths, speaking to Olnita for step six will not offer the correct responses to further the quest.*
        - Truth 1: Everyone suffers in their life
        - Truth 2: Suffering is caused by being selfish
        - Truth 3: Suffering ends when craving ends
        - Truth 4: Follow your path to achieve enlightenment. Following the dialogue, you receive the Censer of Elixia from Olnita.
   - Find the Second Disciple for the [[Sanctus of the Dream Weaver]]
     1. Speak to a fallen pilgrim in [[The Feerrott]] on a hill south of the Spire Portal {{waypoint -168.23, 44.21, 331.11}}.
     1. Speak to a fallen pilgrim in [[The Feerrott]] next ot the entrance of [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]] {{waypoint -1687.58, -8.16, 826.27}}.
     1. Collect the small bell on the floor to receive the [[Sanctus of the Dream Weaver]] in [[The Fabled Temple of Cazic-Thule]] (NE corner of the inner temple, up to level 107^^^ mobs) {{waypoint -62.02, 0, -35.01}}
   - Find the Third Disciple for the [[Lemnos' Forged Omen Stones]]
     1. Pick up a **smudged note** in the Maiden's Gulch area in the southern part of [[Solusek Valley]] in [[Lavastorm]] at {{waypoint -134.23, 14.89, -320.48}}.
        - *Several level 106-107^ [[An embercrux|embercruxes]] roam the area.*
     1. Pick up a **blessed hammer** in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain|Thurgadin]] in [[The Temple of Brell]] on top of the bookshelf {{waypoint 472.83, -201.61, 275.96}}.
     1. Talk to [[Historian Culgreth Wintergrip]] {{waypoint 481.14, -212.08, 280.89}}.
     1. Head into [[Stygian Threshold (Heroic)]] in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]]. Run to the [[Ancient Exodus]] near the end of the zone, and inspect the fallen gnome in the crystal room at {{waypoint 66.45, 88.13, 212.59}}.
        - *Don't clear the zone, or you wont be able to do the next step.*
     1. Make your way back into [[The Howling Burrows]] to kill [[Grozgar|Grozgar the Barkgut War Harbringer]] {{waypoint 287.87, 14.09, -42.02}} to receive [[Lemnos' Forged Omen Stones]].
1. Return to [[Haylis Losaren]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] {{waypoint -1310, -15, -755}}.
1. Search for a shard of **my unwavering duty** *(This should be near the last NPC you spoke to about your epic 1.0)*
   - [[Channeler]]: [[Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint 413, 128, 389}}
   - [[Defiler]]: [[Kunzar Jungle]] {{waypoint 180, 87, -417}}
   - [[Fury]]: [[Darklight Wood]] {{waypoint 441, -23, -265}}
   - [[Inquisitor]]: [[Jarsath Wastes]] {{waypoint -331, -61, -503}}
   - [[Mystic]]: [[Elddar Grove]] {{waypoint 700, -17, -335}}
   - [[Templar]]: [[Kunzar Jungle]] {{waypoint -121, 3, -127}}
   - [[Warden]]: [[Darklight Wood]] {{waypoint 450, -23, -264}}
1. Return to Haylis with my dogma shard
1. Place the items collected on the shrine at {{waypoint 43.24, -13.48, -51.8}} (right-click the small shrine on the right-hand side of the alcove in which Haylis is standing to 'perform the ritual').
1. Completing the ritual has induced a dream state!
   - You will be zoned into [[A Spiritual Dreamscape]]
   - Upon killing everything (tracking helps) you get the message "When the last of the wavering doubts fades, a small gem-like object coalesces among the mists"
   - Collect the glowing sphere at {{waypoint 3, 0, 1}}
1. Return to [[Haylis Losaren]] and show him what you found
