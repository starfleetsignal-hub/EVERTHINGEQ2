## Notes

This quest is an optional starter for the [[Crafting Tutorial Timeline]]. It can be done by both good and evil characters of any citizenship by going to [[Timorous Deep]].

Consider talking to a Crafting Trainer before doing this quest, as it will automatically give you Artisan level 2 for no effort. You can then complete this quest for extra tradeskill experience.

## Description

"I should visit Rozokal Niba'mok in Gorowyn to learn how to harvest and craft objects." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Rozokal Niba`mok]], the Tradeskill Tutorial in [[The Wards]] in [[Gorowyn]] at {{waypoint 2745, 124, 1127}}.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Thrawk For'Ork: "If you have any interest in crafting, perhaps you should visit Rozokal Niba'mok within Gorowyn."
- Player Character: "That sounds like a good idea."

I need to speak with Rozokal Niba'mok.

- Rozokal Niba'mok: "Hello, friend! I'm here to teach interested people a little bit about how to harvest crafting materials, and how to craft tradeskilled items. Are you interested?"

I have spoken with Rozokal Niba'mok.
