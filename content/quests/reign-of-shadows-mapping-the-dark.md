## Steps

1. Speak with [[Captain Meryn Gleamheart]] in the [[Luclin Nexus]] to inquire about Zofia's whereabouts {{waypoint 270, -36, 848}}.
   - He gives you the quest [[Never Let You Echo]]. Complete it and the following quest, [[Save the Last Blast For Me]].
1. Speak with [[Zofia Cobbleshod]] in the [[Luclin Nexus]] {{waypoint 362, -36, 829}}
1. Return to [[Dolgan Inkeye]] in the [[The Bazaar]] {{waypoint -57, -1, 60}}

## Rewards

- [[Fungus Fiend Plushie]]
- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 56,000 status
