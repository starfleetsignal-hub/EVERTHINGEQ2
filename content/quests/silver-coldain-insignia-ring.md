## Starting the Quest

- Examine the *Coldain Insignia Ring* in any zone.  You must be level 90 to begin this quest.
- NOTE: Do not destroy or sell the ring that is given as an award for each quest. You will need those rings on you as you complete each quest. The quest giver will not give an update if you don't have it on you.
- Replacements may be bought for 0 Copper from the [[Shady Swashbuckler XVIII|Shady Swashbuckler]] on the Nektulos Dock.
- BEFORE completing the quest, **remove any adornments** from the copper ring or they will be lost during the turn in.

## Steps

1. Talk to [[Elder Historian Melna]] at {{waypoint 475, -211, 233}} in the Temple of Brell inside [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]].
1. Retrieve the [[Tome of Coldain Kinship]] in Nektropos Castle's library on a shelf in the middle of the library at {{waypoint -25, 13.50, 48}} (for library access see [[Nektropos Castle]]).
1. Return to [[Elder Historian Melna]].
1. Find [[Malidain Glacierbane]], who is at the end of the river on the Daggerflow Glacier in the [[Great Divide]] {{waypoint -807, -333, -23}}
