## Steps

1. Click the rowboat in East Freeport {{waypoint -316, -59, 111}}
1. Kill 9 groups of slavers on your way up the dock (even level, single, double- and triple downs, with one named down-arrow near the top); they are social and have mismatched aggro ranges
   - Items on fire will damage you
1. Click the door {{waypoint 0, 0, -13}}
1. Fight your way downstairs, killing 3 more groups (heroic single, double- and triple-downs)
1. In the cage room kill your 13th group
1. Speak to 13 slaves in cells to "free" them: {{waypoint -23, -31, -26}} and 0 --31 -49
1. Go back up and speak to Kal'ril {{waypoint -3, 0, -11}}
1. Exit the zone by clicking the ladder on the sailing boat {{waypoint 2, -5, -104}}
1. Speak to [[Micha Longtail]] in North Freeport's Academy of Arcane Science {{waypoint 22, -7, -119}}

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- [[An Ornate Freeport Sandstone Bench]]
- One of the following based on level and class:
  - [[Scintillating Brawny Brace]] (for 63 Monk)
  - [[Pulsing Adornment of Agility]] (for 86 Scout)
  - [[Pulsing Adornment of Strength]] (for 80 Fighter)
  - [[Pulsing Adornment of Intelligence]] (for 90 Mage)

(Sorry priests - nothing for you other than the bench)
