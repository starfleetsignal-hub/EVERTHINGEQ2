## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Mottled Hide Toolbelt for my apprentice.
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Mottled Hide Toolbelt]]

      - 2 [[Mottled pelt]]
      - 4 [[Ethereal filament]]
1. I need to take this toolbelt back to my apprentice.
