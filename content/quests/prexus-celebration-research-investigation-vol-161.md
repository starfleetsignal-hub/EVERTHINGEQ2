## Steps

Nothing is free and building, maintaining, and supporting the herald's camp is expensive. That's why the herald of Prexus has requested that we assist in offsetting the camp's cost by retrieving a valuable relic guarded by Ashnu. I need to retrieve the relic.

1. Retrieve the relic guarded by Ashnu in [[Vaashkaani: Golden Rule (Solo)]]
   - The relic is at {{waypoint 588, 71, -50}}
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Oceanlord]] {{waypoint -1765, -402, -2728}} in [[Fens of Nathsar]]
