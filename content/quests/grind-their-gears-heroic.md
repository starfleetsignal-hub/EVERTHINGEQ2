The Dark Gears hide in shadows, waiting for the right opportunity to strike. I must draw them out and defeat them before their plans come to fruition!

## Notes

- You have 8 minutes to complete this quest.
- It's best to clear everything up through Gavitzle before starting this quest.

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A Dark Gears recon|Dark Gears recon scouts]].
   - If a dying Dark Gears scout calls for a medic, wait a bit and [[A Dark Gears medic|Dark Gears medic]] will run up, whom you can then add to your kills.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 26g 80s 11,300 status
- 3 [[Coin of the Eternal Queen]]
