This quest is repeatable., as long as the guide is there to give and complete it.

## Starting the Quest

For this quest, the guide will be panicking about the [[Void Tempest]]s spawning in [[The Ironforge Estate]].  After a bit of roleplaying you will be offered the quest with the following Description text:

*A frantic Concordium Pupli Adept, by the name of Adonia, was rounding up able bodied adventurers to help battle the Void invaders that showed up in North Qeynos.*

## Notes

![Event as it appears in the quest journal](images/Guide_event_-_Void_Invasion_of_Qeynos.jpg)

- The Guide's name does not have to be Adonia.
- The quest scales only to tier 7.  Players level 76 and above will be fighting gray mobs unless mentoring.
- All of the mobs are solo encounters, but you may group and even mentor.  The Guide triggers the spawn based on the **UN**mentored level of the person targeted, however, sometimes making it impossible to kill the encounter while mentoring.  It is advised that you inform the Guide that the lower level member of the group be the trigger target.

## Steps

1. One at a time, kill 4 [[A dark seepage|dark seepages]] or [[A dark horror|dark horrors]].
1. Kill a fifth spawn - dark seepage, dark horror or [[Shadow brute]].  This fifth spawn has an AOE knockback on death.
1. Kill the [[Void Tempest]].  It has a large knockback, but will not chase you far (presumably to protect the uninvolved).
1. Return to the guide.

## Rewards

- 2 [[Concordium Note of Appreciation|Concordium Notes of Appreciation]]
- Achievement experience
  - Repeats of the quest grant only the AA
