## Steps

1. Find the [[Nettleville: Strange Tidings|Nettleville: Strange Tidings gate]] in [[South Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 678, -21, 272}} and zone in.
1. Collect 8 [[Freeblood Salvia Sample|saliva samples]] from infected Freebloods.
1. Slay 6 [[A crazed Freeblood|crazed Freebloods]].
1. Return to [[Akina]].

## Notes

Throughout the neighborhood, [[A council reservist|council reservists]] are inclined to attack first and ask questions later, and have a huge aggro range.

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Celestial Watch**
