## Notes

- You must be at least level 81 to begin this questline on [[Kerra Isle]].
- *You are ineligible to receive this quest if you belong to the Kerran race.*
  - Instead, speaking to Hitus gives you the quest "[[Lol Kerra]]".

## Steps

1. Gather 16 berries from Kerra berry bushes around Kerra Isle (which usually spawn around trees).
1. Return the berries to [[Hitus Sharpclaw]] for your reward. ![](images/Nom_Noms_quest_-_berry_bush.jpg)

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +100 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Dagger of the Sneak]]
  - [[Longsword of the Strongarm]]
  - [[Mace of Clerical Precision]]
  - [[Scimitar of the Reach]]
  - [[Staff of Elemental Command (80)|Staff of Elemental Command]]
