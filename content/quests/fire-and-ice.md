## Notes

- This quest should not be confused with "[[Of Fire and Ice]]", part of the [[Wizard Epic Weapon Timeline]].
- You must speak [[Draconic]] in order to speak with [[Lord Nagafen (Nagafen's Lair)|Lord Nagafen]].
- Only a single person in a group can loot the essence shard from the named drakota.
- Minimum lock out duration on all three lair instances is 2 days 20 hours.

## Steps

1. Speak to the [[Sage of Ages]] in [[Antonica]].
1. Loot a unique essence shard from the corpses of the following drakota:
   - [[Mjolni, the Bringer of Storms]] within [[Mjolni's Lair]] {{waypoint -105, -12, 1013}} in [[The Thundering Steppes]] for the [[Essence Shard of Soul]].
   - [[Dythra, the Gloombringer]] within [[Dythra's Lair]] {{waypoint -900, -9, 337}} in [[The Feerrott]] for the [[Essence Shard of Mind]].
   - [[Ar'ticae, the Frost Maiden]] within [[Ar'ticae's Lair]] {{waypoint -105, -23, -283}} in [[Everfrost]] for the [[Essence Shard of Heart]].
1. Go to [[The Crypt of Vox]] {{waypoint 0, 13, -377}} within [[Permafrost]] {{waypoint 107, -4, -1143}} in [[Everfrost]].
1. While in the instance, examine the essence shards in exactly the following order:
   1. [[Essence Shard of Soul]]
   1. [[Essence Shard of Heart]]
   1. [[Essence Shard of Mind]]

1. Interact with the totem in the center of the room.
   - **Important Note:** Interacting with the totem will kill the person that does it, and also set the lockout timer. If soloing, you must find a way to resurrect yourself as there is no respawn inside of the instance. If you release, you will respawn outside of the instance and lose all of your essence shards. The easiest way is to use [[Daybreak Cash]] to resurrect where you died.
   - **Important Note:** If the totem is clicked prior to using the essence shards, or if the essence shards are used in the incorrect order, [[Spirit of Vox]] will spawn and attack. This will prevent continuation of the quest until the instance can be reset.
1. If the previous two steps were done correctly, [[Lady Vox]] will spawn. Speak with her and advance her dialogue.
1. Return to [[Permafrost]] and go to the unique instance [[Drayek's Chamber: Throne of the Kromise King]]. {{waypoint 0, 60, -389}}
1. Slay [[King Drayek (Throne of Kromise King)|King Drayek]].
   - Do not kill Drayek too fast, as this will break the encounter. If soloing, mentor down to 50, or 55.
1. The quest will auto-complete. There will be some brief story interaction with [[Darathar|The Sage of Ages]].
1. Although not prompted to, return to Lord Nagafen.
