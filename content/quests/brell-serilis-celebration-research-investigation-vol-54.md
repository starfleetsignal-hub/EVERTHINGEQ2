## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Brell Serilis has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items.

1. Purchase the [[Living Stone Blood|living stone blood]] from Golgrin for 5000 status
   - Wanders along the shore at The Sharp Shallows from near the docks of Butcherblock {{waypoint 611, 24, 604}} to {{waypoint 301, 24, 311}}
1. Purchase the [[Deep Well Water|deep well water]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the [[Gloom Shroom Cap|gloom shroom cap]] from Laparnuu for 5000 status
   - in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]], near Petalcrown Perch and the Roost Warrens.  Laparnuu wanders the path from {{waypoint 338, 238, 7}}  all the way to {{waypoint 31, 236, -13}}
1. Return to the [[Herald to the Duke of Below]] {{waypoint -241, 212, -56}} in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
