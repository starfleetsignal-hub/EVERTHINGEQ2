## Steps

1. Elder Jaago asks you to recover his finger and toe bones, which the senior Sebilisans wear as trinkets.  Kill any level 80+ Iksar in Sebilis for a semi-rare body drop update.  20 are needed.
1. Elder Jaago wants his bones buried in a spot under a tree near the Dojo garden in Elddar Grove {{waypoint 726, -19, -405}}.
1. Return to Elder Jaago's cell in Sebilis - the Blade of Tranquility is behind a loose brick near his shackles.
