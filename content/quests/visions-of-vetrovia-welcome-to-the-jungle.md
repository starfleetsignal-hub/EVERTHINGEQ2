## Note

- *[[Karuupa Jungle]] entrance is on the hill above and behind the big waterwheel.  Hail "a plumedrake keeper"* {{waypoint -34, 53, 553}} *to travel to Karuupa Jungle.*

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Chief Zubbluggib]] in [[Karuupa Jungle]] {{waypoint 502, 145, -605}}
1. Place four wards around the Karuupa camp:![Ward Placement Location](images/Ward.png)
   - eastern-most watch tower {{waypoint 451, 89, -432}}
   - south-east watch tower {{waypoint 504, 89, -493}}
   - southern-most watch tower {{waypoint 693, 105, -294}}
   - south-west watch tower {{waypoint 640, 95, -359}}
1. Return to [[Chief Zubbluggib]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 56716 status
- [[Kinnahma Protection Ward (House Item)|Kinnahma Protection Ward]]
- Flight unlocked in [[Svarni Expanse]]
