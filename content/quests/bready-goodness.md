## Description

The Craftkeepers Task Coordinator reports researchers at the Eye of Dartain have requisitioned more essential supplies.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Oolioomon]] in the Paineel Commons {{waypoint 1788, 299, 3461.88}}.

Note; No Mass Production.

1. Craft 10 of either [[Delectable boiled bread roll]] (5 [[Caynar nut]], 10 [[Bamboo shoot]], 10 [[Kejekan palmfruit]], and 5 [[Ethereal kindling]]) or [[Extra-strength erudin espresso]] (15 [[Lapsang tea leaf]], 10 [[Caynar nut]], and 5 [[Ethereal kindling]]). These are Artisan recipes received from [[Replenishing the Observers|an earlier quest]].
1. Return to [[Oolioomon]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +2500 faction with **Craftkeepers**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Craftkeepers Daily Supply Tasks
