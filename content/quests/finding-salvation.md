## Description

The tormented spirits that haunt the remains of Marrsfist Keep must be freed from the curse of Gynok Moltor. To this end I've been tasked to use a special holy symbol, known as the Salvation of Marr to release the spirits from their cursed state.

## Steps

1. Enter the [[Befallen: Halls of the Forsaken]] and use the received [[Symbol of Marr's Salvation]] to free 8 [[A tormented spirit|tormented spirits]], found at these locations:
   - {{waypoint 10, -15, 113}}
   - {{waypoint 39, -17, 141}}
   - {{waypoint 75, -17, 88}}
   - {{waypoint 149, -23, 117}}
   - {{waypoint 144, -23, 75}}
   - {{waypoint 217, -17, 139}}, in the *Temporal Chamber*
   - {{waypoint 214, -21, 56}}, in the *Chapel of Penance*
   - {{waypoint 218, -21, 194}}
