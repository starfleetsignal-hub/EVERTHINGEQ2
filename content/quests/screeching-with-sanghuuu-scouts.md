## Steps

1. Pick (6) Fresh Sunset Daisies in Burrower's Timber. The Daisy's colors are very muted so go slow and you'll have better luck
   - ![Sunset Daisies](images/Sunset_Daisies.jpg)Possible locations are:
   - {{waypoint 433.35, 75.11, 301.63}}, {{waypoint 416.16, 85.68, 329.84}}
   - {{waypoint 416.95, 88.37, 349.64}}, {{waypoint 410.93, 87.99, 357.00}}
   - {{waypoint 420.58, 88.88, 374.69}}, {{waypoint 442.20, 100.97, 357.83}}
   - {{waypoint 572, 95, 452}}, {{waypoint 629, 90, 364}}
   - {{waypoint 502, 85, 377}}
1. ![Eternal Plumage Memorial](images/Eternal_Plumage_Memorial.jpg)Deposit flowers at Eternal Plumage Memorial in Sanctuary {{waypoint 199.46, 288.85, 1036.31}}
1. Return to Jabber
   1. ![Dropped Note](images/Dropped_Note.jpg)Jabber is missing! Pick up the note where he should be.
   1. Read the [[Jabber's Note|note]].
   1. Find Jabber just North of Splendor Wellspring at {{waypoint -565.65, 98.93, 190}}
   1. Save Jabber from his attacker
1. Make sure that Jabber returned to the Sanctuary by speaking to [[Viamme]] {{waypoint 208.26, 274.96, 818.12}}
