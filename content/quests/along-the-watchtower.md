## Steps

1. Search the destroyed Kejekan watchtower for [[Salatev the Banished|Salatev]] and banish him.
   - You can reach Salatev via the climbable wall at {{waypoint -909.33, 520.34, 579.96}}
   - Note - after you kill Salatev, inspect the sacks behind him to pickup Kejekan Supplies - the quest starter for [[Could Be of Use]].
1. Return to [[Marozi]]

## Rewards

- At least 35g 70s 61c
