## Steps

1. Talk to [[Trooper Hallisk]] in [[Riliss (POI)|Riliss]]. {{waypoint -625, 63, -84}}
1. After agreeing to the battle, defeat the 3 [[A froglok battler|froglok battlers]] that spawn behind him.
1. Return to [[Trooper Kresix]].

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Disturbing Earring of the Dark Priest]] (All Priests)
  - [[Earring of Perpetual Aggravation]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Earring of the Lost Tower]] (All Mages)
  - [[Intense Hoop of Power]] (All Fighters)
- +5000 faction with **Riliss**
