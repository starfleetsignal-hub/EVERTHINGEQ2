You need +40k faction with the Guardians of the Underfoot

## Steps

1. Use the provided spell right at the entrance of [[Dartain's Fortress]].  **Note:** Do not put your [[Gruengach Illusion]] on or the summoned elemental will attack you.
1. Return to [[Lyov]]

## Reward

1. +2500 faction with **Guardians of the Underfoot**
1. At least 55g 70s 61c
1. [[Mark of Manaar]]
