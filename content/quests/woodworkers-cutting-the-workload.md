Edrin Tarbeck recruited me to use my woodworking skills to help G.L.E.E.S.H. provide support to the Luclinites who are trying to repair and relocate to the city of Fordel Midst. Their need for wooden dowels and planks is high.

## Steps

- Check the woodworkers' crate near Edrin Tarbeck to see what to make for the next shipment to Luclin.  You can find the [[Solidified ethereal filament|solidified ethereal filament]] needed around Teren's Grasp.
  - Craft 6 **boxes of wooden dowels**
  - Craft 6 **stacks of wood planks**
- Return to Edrin Tarbeck to turn in.

## Rewards

- At least 11s 08c
- 2 [[G.L.E.E.S.H. Mark]]
