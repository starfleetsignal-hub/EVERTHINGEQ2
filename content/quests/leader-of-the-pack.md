## Steps

*utlander Deston, a member of the Qeynos Guard alarmed by the assembly of gnoll packs, informed me the charismatic High Shaman, Rakada Darkpaw, did not originate with the Sabertooth pack, though they were some of the first ones they brought their message of unification to. They are a follower of Chirannis and have swayed packs with the awe-inspiring help of spirits of infamous gnolls from their past.*

1. Find where terramite crystals are mined.
   - Go to Terramite Crystalline Grotto - past Splitpaw Burrow and up the rope ladder {{waypoint 71, -1, 201}}
1. Find the cave of the High Shaman
   - Drop down the hole in the side room of the Grotto near {{waypoint -35, 0, 295}}, and then go to the Convocation of Chirannis room at {{waypoint -9, -23, 268}}
   - Entering the Convocation of Chirannis room will severely lower your hp unless certain conditions are met, so be prepared!
   - Note: Do NOT drop down the hole with smoke rising from it at {{waypoint 8, 1, 252}} or you may die!
1. Defeat High Shaman Rakada Darkpaw
1. Speak with Outlander Deston in Darkpaw Dugout {{waypoint 10, -78, -236}}
   - Note: Outlander Deston is no longer in Darkpaw Warrens at this step.  You must speak with him in Darkpaw Dugout.
