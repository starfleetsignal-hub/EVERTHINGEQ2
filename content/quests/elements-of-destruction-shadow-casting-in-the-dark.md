## Steps

1. Take the elevator to the top floor (Mezzanine) of [[Myrist, the Great Library]] and use the portal at {{waypoint 440, 15, 230}} (Grand Librarian's Office) to reach [[Plane of Justice: Eternal Prisons]].
   - *Note: Clicking a cell door will tell you what kind of key it requires.*
1. Speak to Druzzil Ro at {{waypoint 45, -67, 13}}
   - *either tab to her and press h, or kill an enforcer (in one of the cells) for the Justicarium Key to her cell*
1. Obtain a [[Mithril cell key]] from a green shiny at {{waypoint 27.95, -67.02, 8.59}}, then unlock the cell door at {{waypoint 20.53, -67.51, -5.46}}
1. Kill [[Balgros]] at {{waypoint 22.02, -67.60, -26.17}} to obtain a [[Feyiron cell key]].
   - If you hug the east wall, you can get back to the corner and fight Balgros there without aggroing the [[An enforcer|enforcer]].
1. Open the door to [[Tayil N'Velex|Tayil N'Velex's]] cell at {{waypoint 11, -67, -7}} and speak to her.
1. Travel to the [[Enchanted Lands]] and use **a half-buried statue** at {{waypoint -1012, -1, -385}} to enter [[Isle of Vul: Bellengere's Dark Spire]].
1. Kill the seven [[A Vul adherent|Vul adherents]] and [[Fathad Zastim (Chaos Descending)|Fathad Zastim]] on the beach to obtain the [[Shadow Gem]] (you have to kill them all to break the barrier regardless of when you get the Shadow Gem).
1. Go up the ramp at {{waypoint 29.79, 2.41, -31.59}}. Click the big purple barrier at the top of the ramp to open the way forward.
1. Destroy the barrier blocking the tower door at {{waypoint 30, 40, -88}}.
   - Kill the 4 [[A savage grathok|savage grathok]]. Move the piles of goo they drop to the tower to destroy the stone barrier.
1. Kill the [[A shadowed man|shadowmen]] and gather the [[Void Shard Key]] at {{waypoint 78, 46, -116}} or {{waypoint 68, 45, -110}}. (![](images/Black_Shiney.jpg)A black shiney on the floor)
1. Click the barrier blocking the stairwell at {{waypoint 75, 67, -98}} to open the way.
1. Speak to [[Bellengere the Three]] at {{waypoint 81, 69, -101}}.
1. Return to [[Maelin Starpyre]] at {{waypoint 244, -7, -24}} in [[Myrist, the Great Library]].
