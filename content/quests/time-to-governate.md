## Steps

1. Kill the [[Haoaeran engineer]] near the throm cave. {{waypoint 1334, 159, 89}}
1. Return to [[Tertiary Gurowyn Riz'Din]].
1. Examine the steamer next to Tertiary Gurowyn Riz'Din.

## Rewards

- At least 11s 40c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Brawler's Belt of Shurikens]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Empowered Totem]] (All Mages)
  - [[Forged Buckler]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Shortbow]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Thug's Knifebelt]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Vanguard's Shortbow]] (Warriors, Crusaders)
