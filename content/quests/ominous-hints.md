## Steps

1. Kill the following mobs at the Ry'Gorr Ruins {{waypoint -716, 200, 218}} or around it:
   - Defeat 8 [[A Ry'Gorr grunt|Ry'Gorr grunts]]
   - Defeat 8 [[A Ry'Gorr soldier|Ry'Gorr soldiers]] or [[A Ry'Gorr thaumaturgist|Ry'Gorr thaumaturgists]]
   - Search Ry'Gorr supply crates, which can be found inside the Ruins {{waypoint -695, -365, 186}}
1. Speak with Jelgith at {{waypoint 110, -345, -622}}
1. Find a coldain scout
   - Speak with [[Scout Drevill]] at {{waypoint -749, 317, -0.18}}
1. Kill [[A Ry'Gorr winter scout|Ry'Gorr winter scouts]] for clues of their plans.   Note: you might need to kill many Winter Scouts to get your update, and you do not get a message saying "update not found" when you kill them, so keep killing until you get your ding.
   - Can be found near and within the ruins
1. Speak with Scout Drevill {{waypoint -748.04, -317.33, 0.89}}
1. Speak with [[Captain Icecleaver]] in Thurgadin at {{waypoint 838.45, -294.13, 336.39}}
1. Speak with Jelgith at {{waypoint 110, -345, -622}}in Great Divide

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- [[Deepwater Circle Vestments]]
