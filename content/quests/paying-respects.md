## Steps

1. Elder Su'Linh has asked that you place flowers for fallen loved ones of village members within the Kejaan's Rill.
   - Place the first flower. {{waypoint 681, 185, 4024}}  ![One of the placed flowers](images/Paying_Respects_quest_-_flower.jpg)
   - Place the second flower. {{waypoint 825, 169, 3935}}
   - Place the third flower. {{waypoint 878, 145, 4138}}
   - Place the fourth flower. {{waypoint 748, 181, 4138}}
   - Place the fifth flower. {{waypoint 735, 194, 4070}}
1. Return to [[Elder Su'Linh|Su'Linh]].

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +2000 faction with **Hua Mein**
