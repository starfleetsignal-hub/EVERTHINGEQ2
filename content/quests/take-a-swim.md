This quest is part 8 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Find three sets of bones in the water around [[Port Naythex]]:
   - Ribcage: {{waypoint 1100, -20, -232}}
   - Skull: {{waypoint 846, -21, -25}}
   - Hand: {{waypoint 670, -20, 30}}
1. Return to Torq for your reward.

## Rewards

At least 11s 40c

One of the following:

- [[Dragoon Stud of the Dark Forest]] (All Fighters)
- [[Skull Faced Signet]] (All Priests)
- [[Spike of Hardened Bone]] (All Mages)
- [[Earring of Hardened Bone Shards]] (All Scouts)
