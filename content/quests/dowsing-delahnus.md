## Description

There are reports of a powerful dragon within the Vasty Deep Conservatory. I must play the part of exterminator and flush this threat from its halls.

## Steps

1. Kill [[Delahnus the Dauntless]] in [[Vasty Deep: The Conservatory]]
   - Delahnus is in the [[Well of Gold]] area at the back of the zone.

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x2
- At least 1p 80g
