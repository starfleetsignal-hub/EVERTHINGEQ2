## Notes

- Completion of this quest is necessary for obtaining the quest [[Help in the Highlands]], released in [[Update:55|LU55]].

## Steps

1. Examine the stolen tome on [[Glubbsunk Cove]]. {{waypoint 750, 51, 357}} (Short respawn when picked up)
1. Return to [[Dockmaster Waulon]].
1. Collect 5 missing pages by killing [[An aqua goblin wizard|aqua goblin wizards]] in [[Glubbsunk Cove]].
1. Return to [[Dockmaster Waulon]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s 91c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Dwarven Steel Buckler]] (All Priests)
  - [[Dwarven Steel Orb]] (All Mages)
  - [[Dwarven Steel Rampart]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Dwarven Steel Round Shield]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Dwarven Steel Statue]] (All Priests)
  - [[General's Wicked Axe]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Knuckles of Vigor]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Malignant Rod of the Brawler]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Maul of the Irontoe]] (All Priests)
  - [[Ornate Bo of the Conjuror]] (All Mages)
  - [[Weighted Katana]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
