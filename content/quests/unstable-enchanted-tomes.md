**NOTE**: Once taken, this quest gives you forty-eight hours to complete it.

## Description

The unstable enchanted tomes are preventing the Scions of the Deepwater Knights from collecting important information related to the reclamation of Erudin. They must be destroyed!

## Steps

1. Enter the [[Library of Erudin]] and destroy a great number of unstable enchanted tomes.
   - Kill 12 [[An unstable enchanted tome|unstable enchanted tomes]]
1. Return to [[Rafiqa]]

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
