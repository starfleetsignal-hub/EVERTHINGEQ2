## Starting the Quest

Quest starters are Trades Coordinators:

- Gorowyn  - [[Grandmaster Jayak Lat'wasr]] in [[Gorowyn]], [[The Wards]]
- Freeport - [[Grandmaster Kawayzer]] in [[West Freeport]]
- Kelethin - [[Grandmaster Feldmanus]] in [[Tunare's Pages]] in the area with the broker
- Qeynos   - [[Grandmaster Kimbialis]] at [[The Ironforge Exchange]] in [[North Qeynos]]
- Neriak   - [[Grandmaster Zyrythius]] in the [[Dockside Markets]]
- New Halas - [[Grandmaster Cheri Glodsdottr]] in [[Ravens' Roost]] crafting area

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Tenebrous Tangle]].
1. Find the harvesting bag through the arch in Tenebrous Tangle {{waypoint 11, 29, 197}}.
1. Find the bottle near the river by Long Drop Falls. It is on the bank, about half-way between two reeds. You will have to enter first-person mode to see it. (Edit: First-person no longer required to see it, but it is a very small rusty looking bottle on the bank)  {{waypoint 55, 19.3, 176.4}}.
1. Jump down the waterfall and talk to one of the harvesters at the following locations:
   - {{waypoint 103, -215, 194}}
   - {{waypoint 115, -215, 184}}
   - {{waypoint 108, -215, 162}}
1. Talk to a vigilant froglok alchemist in the Hidden Refuge {{waypoint 176, -235, 147}}.
1. Obtain the following (see note below):
   - 5 [[Ravasect meat]]
   - 5 [[Aviak meat]]
   - 5 [[Rough lumbered rosewood]] (used as fuel)
   - 5 [[Squash]]
1. Talk to a vigilant froglok alchemist in the Hidden Refuge again {{waypoint 176, -235, 147}}.
   - This step will require you to still have all 20 items you just gathered.
1. Scribe the [[Scribbled froglok recipe notes]] received.
1. Make the [[Froglok healing drink|Froglok Healing Drink]] received from scribing the notes.
   - Use the "Mixing bucket" that is right next to the froglok alchemist.
1. Give the drink to the four members of the harvesting team (around the bottom of the waterfall.)
1. Obtain 25 [[Hanging root]].
   - The roots simply need to be in your inventory for the final turn in, you do not have to harvest them yourself.
1. Return to the grandmaster, i.e. the Trades Coordinator who gave you the quest.

## Notes

- It is no longer required to have a minimum of 250 harvesting skills to complete this quest. Your skill level only determines if you can get rare components from harvesting nodes.
- Having the required materials in your inventory will update the quest. If for some reason the quest doesn't update having the materials in your bag, trade them to a friend and have the friend trade them back. Alternatively, buy the raw materials on the broker.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill Experience
- [[Artisan's formal ensemble (male)]] or [[Artisan's formal ensemble (female)]] depending on your character's gender
- Your choice of one advanced book level 60-69 for your tradeskill class
  - Provisioners are only given the Formal Ensemble. Your advanced recipes are purchased from the crafting trainer.
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +1250 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +1250 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +1250 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +1250 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +1250 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +1250 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
