## Steps

1. Kill 30 Aerakyn in the [[Temple of Veeshan: Guardian's Edifice (Raid)]]. Only aerakyn mobs not part of a named ring event or encounter count for this step.
1. Destroy the altar being used to scry on the Heart, located behind Klandicar at {{waypoint -1921, 302, 805}} (raid-wide update, may be only 1 click per zone).
1. Kill 13 named dragons. You will have to come back to the zone 3 times to get 13, because there are only five in the zone, Klandicar being the last.
1. Kill Zlandicar.

## Rewards

- One of the Following:
  - [[Zlandicar's Bloodsoaked Tortured Heart]] (Plate Fighter)
  - [[Zlandicar's Bloodsoaked Riven Heart]] (Brawler)
  - [[Zlandicar's Bloodsoaked Pierced Heart]] (Scout)
  - [[Zlandicar's Bloodsoaked Punctured Heart]] (Scout)
  - [[Zlandicar's Bloodsoaked Heart]] (Mage/Priest)
  - [[Zlandicar's Bloodsoaked Scorched Heart]] (Mage/Priest)
