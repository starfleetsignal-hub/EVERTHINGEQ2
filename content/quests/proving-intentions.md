## Steps

1. Complete the following:
   - Obtain the remains gathered by the necromancy researchers. They should be somewhere in the necromancy hall.
     - The remains are *Unknown Remains for Study* {{waypoint 1652, -265, 3216}} There is a one minute respawn. Standing on the table helps gather it. ![The unknown remains waiting to be studied](images/Proving_Intentions_quest_-_remains_for_study.jpg)
   - Convince [[High Commander Fawzi Zaim|Commander Fawzi]] that nothing unusual is going on in the dry riverbed. {{waypoint 1903, -298, 3445}}
     - Speak with Fawzi in the Hall of the Fell Blade.
   - Destroy the plant sample that [[Ishaq Al'Nair|Ishaq]] has at [[The Eye of Dartain]].
     - Speak with [[Ishaq Al'Nair|Ishaq]] at {{waypoint 1832, 6, 4365}}
   - Tell [[Captain Ghalib Elbaz|Ghalib]] that you found nothing in the Kejaan's Rill riverbed.
     - This is also the last step in the quest [[Investigating Strange Bones]].
1. Return to Jin'tu.

## Rewards

- At least 46g
