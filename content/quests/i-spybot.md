## Steps

1. Use the [[Bag of Tinkered Spybots]] at the following locations:   (must be done in order listed)
   1. The launch pad at the [[The Eye of Dartain]] {{waypoint 1925, -22, 4292}}.
   1. The launch pad at the [[Stonebrunt Encampment]] {{waypoint 951, 121, 3688}}.
   1. The launch pad at the [[Kerra Isle]] {{waypoint 1412, -5, 2419}}.
   1. The launch pad at [[Toxxulia Forest]] {{waypoint 370, -136, 2530}}.
      - *Note: You will receive the message **This looks like a good location for a tinkered spybot** when you're at the right spot.*
1. Return to [[Felice Adae]] on [[Spire Island]].

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
