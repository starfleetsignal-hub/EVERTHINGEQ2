## Notes

- This quest takes place in an instance of the [[D'Morte Burial Chambers (Zone)|D'Morte Burial Chambers]] called [[Acquisition]], and in [[The Crypt of T'haen: Endless Twilight]] instance.  The Epic x4 raid zone called [[The Crypt of T'Haen: Vengeance]] does not give an update for this quest.
- You must have completed enough of the main quest series of the [[Bloodline Chronicles Timeline]] to gain access to [[The Crypt of T'haen]]. Completing the quest [[Shattering the Crystals]] (Good-alignment) or [[Exploring the Crypt]] (Evil-alignment) grants access to [[The Crypt of T'haen]].
- If you need another [[Crest of D'Morte]] go into the [[D'Morte Burial Chambers]] again and touch [[T'Haen's Coffin]] at {{waypoint -52.06, -11.12, 44.55}}

## Steps

1. Visit [[Soul Eater Falls]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] and talk to either [[Trelech Stonehome]] to receive quest: [[Missing Scouts]] or [[Thraixe Il'ferrai]] to receive quest: [[A Small Sampling]].
1. Return to the [[D'Morte Burial Chambers (Zone)|D'Morte Burial Chambers]] and enter the [[Acquisition]] instance.
1. Kill [[Izinar Malkevantrii]] {{waypoint -5, -4, 74}} and his two assistant conjurers. You will receive a new [[Crest of D'Morte]], and your old one will be destroyed.
   - Note: He will attack even if he's a grey con to you.
1. Return to Soul Eater Falls.
1. If you have not already done so, begin one of the Bloodline Chronicles series of quests [[Missing Scouts]] (good) or [[A Small Sampling]] (evil) and continue with the quest series until you receive access to [[The Crypt of T'haen]] via [[Shattering the Crystals]] or [[Exploring the Crypt]].
1. Enter [[The Crypt of T'haen]]. (Select The Crypt of T'haen:  Endless Twilight)
1. Find and destroy *a magnificent coffin* at {{waypoint 94, 56, -66}} from which it is said that [[T'Rath the Usurper]] receives his power.
   - **This step is optionial**, however if you don't destroy the coffin he starts healing at 35%.
   - To reach the coffin go to the narrow bridge at {{waypoint 0, 57, -59}} and cross the chasm. Take a left then follow the path to the room with dozens of coffins.
1. Destroy [[T'Rath the Usurper]] atop the central pillar at {{waypoint -7, 61, -26}}. You will receive a new [[Crest of D'Morte]], and your old one will be destroyed.
   - *Note:* This last step, is identical to the quest [[A Vicious Twist]] (Hand of Marr series) or [[Crushing Betrayal]] (Brethren of Night series). Consider doing at the same time.
