## Starting

To start this quest, you must be on the quest [[Shattered Seas: Chasing Greymast]].  You will be given this quest at a certain step of that quest.

## Steps

1. Escort Kitkalla home - follow the path north to [[Gorir]] {{waypoint 1026, 24, -939}}
1. Speak to Elder [[Karrabukk]], listen, THEN listen to Kitkalla.
1. Go back and forth between the two NPCs listening to one then the other.
1. Finally speak to Kikalla to get the update.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 29g 60s
- [[Gorir's Rage]]

## Notes

- **IMPORTANT!!  After the escort, hail Karrabukk and listen to the conversation with Kitkalla, until you get the update.**
- If you delete this quest after starting it, you can get it again by speaking with Kitkalla at {{waypoint 1029, 11, -716}}.
