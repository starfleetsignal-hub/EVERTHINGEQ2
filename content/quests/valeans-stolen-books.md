## Steps

1. Search [[The Down Below]] for Valean's missing books.
   - The exact location is {{waypoint -12, 0, -7}}.entering The Down Below in Starcrest Commune puts you right at the books.
   - You do not need to examine any books, but you do need to stand *very* close to the stack of books in the corner.
   - Examining the books gives another quest: [[An Intriguing Eye]]
1. Return to Valean for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 50c
- One of the following
  - [[Old meat tenderizer]]
  - [[Sharp carving knife]]
  - [[Waning wand]]
  - [[Well used pitchfork]]
  - [[Well used fighting baton]]
