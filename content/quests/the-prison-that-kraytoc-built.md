## Steps

1. Defeat [[Witch Doctor Vuh'Duh]]
1. Place **Veeshan's Claw Tip**. (Some short races need to look UP to see it.)
1. Slay [[Goredeth Maulhammer]].
   - Run back through the dungeon in reverse, killing a few groups, until you reach [[Goredeth Maulhammer]].
   - Use the **Shamanistic Trident of Undoing** on him to reduce him from Epic to Heroic, then defeat him.
1. Return to [[Viglundr Doomforge]] {{waypoint 525, -357, 823}}

## Additional

After killing [[Goredeth Maulhammer]] a pillar apears at {{waypoint -32, -6, -32}} - a bit more the way down to exit. After touching it, immediately [[Dill Fireshine]] comes up and is directly attacking.

## Rewards

- At least 20g 31s 56c
- All of the following:
  - [[Frozen Adornment of Raw Power]]
  - [[Frosted Adornment of Critical Chance]]
  - [[Frozen Adornment of Increased Criticals]]
  - [[Icy Adornment of Critical Chance]]
  - [[Icy Adornment of Mending]]
  - [[Shrine of Prexus]]
- From [[Dill Fireshine]]: an [[Ancestral Blade Fragment]]
