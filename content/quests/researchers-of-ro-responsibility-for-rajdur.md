*The Far Seas Supply Division has asked me to take responsibility for setting up trade relations with the residents of the newly-discovered regions in the Desert of Ro. Additionally, I am to set up research stations while I am in the area. My first stop will be Hopewell Harbor in the Raj'Dur Plateaus.*

## Starting the Quest

Head to the [[Village of Shin]] on the [[Isle of Mara]] and speak to [[Jaranna Thistlebloom]] at {{waypoint -81, -2, 105}} inside the pub.

## Materials Required

- Either harvest or otherwise acquire:
  - 1 [[Yucca Wood]] (rare)
  - 25 [[Apatite Gem]]
  - 30 [[Brittlebush Taproot]] (Twisted Roots)
  - 20 [[Desert Willow Wood]]
  - 25 [[Dustmaw Hide]]
  - 35 [[Pallasite Ore]]
- Bring along this fuel (or use a summon fuel merchant item):
  - 10 [[Astral Coal]]
  - 5 [[Astral Sandpaper]]
- Bring along these portable workstations (or use the summon workstation AAs):
  - [[Portable Work Bench v2.0]]

## Steps

1. Speak with local merchant [[Bairam Najafi]] at {{waypoint 149, -60, 583}} in the [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]].
1. Harvest the following in Raj'Dur Plateaus:
   - 30 [[Brittlebush Taproot]]
   - 20 [[Desert Willow Wood]]
   - 35 [[Pallasite Ore]]
   - 35 [[Apatite Gem]]
   - 15 [[Dustmaw Hide]]
1. Return to Bairam, who gives you [[Recipe Scroll: Hopewell Harbor Equipment]] (auto-scribes).
1. Summon a work bench and craft a [[Hopewell Display Table]]. You will need:
   - 1 Yucca Wood (rare)
   - 20 Desert Willow Wood
   - 15 Dustmaw Hide
   - 5 Astral Sandpaper
1. Next, craft a [[Desert Work Bench]] on your summoned work bench. You will need:
   - 20 Pallasite Ore
   - 15 Apatite Gem
   - 5 Astral Coal
1. Next, craft a [[Desert Chemistry Table]] on your summoned work bench. You will need:
   - 5 Apatite Gem
   - 15 Brittlebush Taproot
   - 15 Pallasite Ore
   - 5 Astral Coal
1. Click the chest with purple sparkles next to Bairam at {{waypoint 152, -60, 582}} to place the equipment.
1. Speak to Bairam to complete the quest.
