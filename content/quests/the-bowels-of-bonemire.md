## Steps

1. Go to [[Hive Vornerus]] in [[The Bonemire]] for an auto-update. {{waypoint 19, 97, 806}}
1. Enter [[The Den of the Devourer]] {{waypoint 82.24, 93.46, 894.73}} and destroy ten tomes:
   - These are approximate locations, so if the tome is not at the specific point, look around. Also, some of these will be visible, and other will be invisible - chosen at random. If a tome is not appearing at a location, use a visibility spell/ability or an AOE ability.
   - **Important:** Do not kill the Devourer before you have destoryed all of these tomes since you will need to kill it for the next step!
   - Volume I: {{waypoint -106.06, -19.78, 32.63}}
   - Volume II: {{waypoint -191.81, -19.84, 39.66}}
   - Volume III: {{waypoint -165.20, -19.48, 3.69}} or {{waypoint -194, -20, 34}}
   - Volume IV: {{waypoint -273.89, -29.15, -36.75}} or {{waypoint -238, -31, -12}}
   - Volume V: {{waypoint -321, -44, -61}}
   - Volume VI: {{waypoint -303, -40, 6}} or {{waypoint -319.85, -36.09, 44.33}}
   - Volume VII - {{waypoint -185.02, -19.84, 83.55}}
   - Volume VIII: {{waypoint -162.82, -0.28, 188.19}} or {{waypoint -162, 0, 231}}
   - Volume IX - {{waypoint -302.35, -13.58, 233.14}} or {{waypoint -331, -14, 198}}
   - Volume X - {{waypoint -347, 3, 191}}
1. Kill [[The Devourer]], who spawns after all other named mobs have been killed.
   - **You will need to kill the bosses in the following order:**
   - [[Svacklan]] (69^^) {{waypoint -180, -19, 24}}  in [[The Hall of Guardians (Den of the Devourer)|The Hall of Guardians]] and spawned by starting a ring event.
     - The first wave is four Vornerous High Guards (68^), second wave is four Vornerous Honorguards (68^^) and the third wave is Svacklan with two Vornerous Honorguards (69^^)
   - [[Deepguardian Kvaklin]] (69^^) {{waypoint -304, -36, 48}} is spawned by starting a ring event.
     - From the Hall of Guardians, follow the north passage to [[The Hatching Chamber]]. There are four Vornerous hive guardians in each corner of the room. Clear the room four times, and then Kvaklin and two more guardians will spawn.
   - [[Spirit of Water]] (67^^) {{waypoint -159, -4, 212}}  with four water elementals (67) spawns after killing Deepguardian Kvaklin.
     - Take the south passage from the Hall of Guardians to [[The Waterfall Room]]. Note that if you range pull the Spirit of Water, you will be attacked by the ravasect which patrol around the pool.
   - [[The Devourer]] (69^^^) {{waypoint -315, 2, 211}} is found in [[The Queen's Hive]] east of the Waterfall Room.
     - There are two Queen's Guard (69^^) linked to her, and there are ravasect hatchlings which spawn from eggs in the center of the room. You can have someone destroy the eggs right before engaging her if the additional hatchlings make the fight difficult for the group.

1. Go to [[The Bonemire]] and kill the following mobs:
   - [[The Acrid Mother]], or her placeholder, [[An acrid maiden]], on the [[Isle of the Ravasect (POI)|Isle of the Ravasect]]. She roams around {{waypoint 930, -120, 280}}.
   - [[The Humator Harridan]] on the Isle of Ravasect, down the tunnel at {{waypoint 980, -100, 170}}.
   - [[Haraghur the Deathless]] on [[Cacotoxic Stain]] at {{waypoint 240, 230, -710}}.
   - [[The Carnovingian]], or its placeholder, [[A carnoligian]], on [[Carrion Briar]] around {{waypoint -140, -70, -520}}.
   - [[The Carrion Mandragora]], or its placeholder, [[A carrion mandrake]], on Carrion Briar, down the underwater tunnel at {{waypoint -240, -80, -450}}. Note that the placeholder typically spawns nearby at {{waypoint -200, -146, -390}}. If you have a flying mount you can skip the tunnel and fly directly to the spawn platform.
1. Examine [[The Crystal of Drukaunis]] that you received earlier from killing [[The Devourer]].
1. Go to [[Hidden Refuge (Tenebrous Tangle)|Hidden Refuge]] in [[Tenebrous Tangle]] and speak with [[Murrar Shar (Tenebrous Tangle)|Murrar Shar]] for players of Good-alignment, or [[Tayil N'Velex (Tenebrous Tangle)|Tayil N'Velex]] for players of Evil-alignment.

For citizens of [[Qeynos]], your choice of:

- [[Murrar's Beautiful Breeches]]
- [[Murrar's Glorious Greaves]]
- [[Murrar's Lustrous Leggings]]
- [[Murrar's Penultimate Pantaloons]]

- [[Tayil's Beneficent Breeches]]
- [[Tayil's Grim Greaves]]
- [[Tayil's Triumphant Trousers]]
- [[Tayil's Pragmatic Pants]]
