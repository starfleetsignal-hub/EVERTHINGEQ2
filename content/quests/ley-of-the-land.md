## Steps

1. Defeat the Enraged Shik'Nar Imperiatrix within the Blinding: Twisted Vista [Raid]
1. Defeat Eom Va Liako Vess within the Aurelian Coast: The Emergent Eclipse [Raid].
1. Defeat Corrupted Lord Commander Seru within Sanctus Seru: The Fading Arches [Raid].
1. Defeat the Echo of Ancient Knowledge within Fordel Midst: Remembrance [Raid].
1. Defeat Berenz, Blade of Legend with the Wracklands: Crimson Barrens [Raid].
1. Defeat Vyzh'dra the Undying within Ssraeshza's Hallowed Halls [Raid].
