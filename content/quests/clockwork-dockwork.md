## Steps

![](images/Broken_widget_%28pickup%29.png)

1. Collect 5 "[[Broken widget|broken widgets]]" from the dock area.
1. Scribe the [[Blueprint: Widget Repair (no-value no-trade lore)|Blueprint: Widget Repair]] recipe.
1. Craft 5 [[Repaired widget|repaired widgets]] at the workbench located at {{waypoint 1291, -540, 472}}.  You will need:
   1. 5 [[Titanium ore]]
   1. 5 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
   1. 5 [[Ethereal Coal]]
1. Return the widgets to Fenwick.

Note: Fenwick has more quests for you if you are a  skill level 400 tinkerer.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,000 faction with **Iceclad Pirates**
