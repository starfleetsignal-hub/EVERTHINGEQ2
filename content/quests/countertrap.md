## Steps

1. Receive [[Recipe: Alchemical Trap]] to Craft 9 [[Alchemical Trap|Alchemical Traps]] at the chemistry table {{waypoint 4314, -821, 443}}. Requires 9 [[Amethyst]], 18 [[Matoppie roots]], 9 [[Rhenium ore]] and 9 [[Effulgent candle|effulgent candles]] (the recipe auto-scribes)
1. Double-click the 9 ballistae:
   - 5 ballistae northwest of fort {{waypoint 4345, -827, 243}}
   - 4 ballistae north of fort {{waypoint 4519, -823, 374}}
1. Return to Denstarin

- At least 1p 30g
