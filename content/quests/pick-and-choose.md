## Notes

- This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.
- If you start the quest [[Hand of War - Eye of Fear]] you will be unable to complete this quest until you finish both Hand of War - Eye of Fear quest and it's following quest, [[Broken Fist of War]].

## Steps

1. Harvest the following in [[The Feerrott]]:
   - 6 [[Rough bloodstone|rough bloodstones]] from hermetic stone
   - 6 [[Ashen root|ashen roots]] from musty roots
   - 6 [[Severed teak]] from putrid arbor
   - 6 [[Fulginate cluster|fulginate clusters]] from alluvium ore
   - 2 [[Bonded loam]] from alluvium ore
   - 2 [[Browncap mushroom|browncap mushrooms]] from overgrown bed of greenslade
1. Return to [[Vashazdar Manoisti]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 53s 57c
