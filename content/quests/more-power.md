This quest is part 1 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Gnome Series.

## Steps

1. Harvest 10 volatile powders from powder kegs which you will find scattered all over Temple Street.
1. Speak with Neezer Grund. You will receive a blast capsule in your inventory.
1. Click on the device next to Neezer to insert the blast capsule. It explodes (of course).
1. Speak with [[Neezer Grund (Temple Street)|Neezer Grund]] again for your reward.

## Rewards

- Coin
- 20 Volatile Chaw (food)
