## Notes

The 5 Quests [[Still Thin Around the Gills]], [[Still A Fun Guy]], [[More Briny Shell Smell]], [[More Spore Sport]] and [[Even Oozier Food]] can be repeated 10 times each till you reach 40 faction with the [[Ironblood Sprouts (Faction)]] and receive the quest [[Let My People Go]].

## Conversation

A mysterious ironblood myconid by the name of Verna Amanita has asked for my aid in securing his home back from invading forces!

## Steps

1. Collect 7 [[A blackanvil ooze|blackanvil oozes]].
1. Return to [[Verna Amanita|Verna]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +2000 faction with **Ironblood Sprouts**
