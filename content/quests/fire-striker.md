Tinkersmith's latest invention idea is a hammer that throws explosive darts. Sounds a little crazy to me, but I agreed to help him put it together.

## Steps

![Bracket Fungus](images/Bracket_fungus.png)

1. [[Charles Q Tinkersmith]] gives you the recipe [[Explosive Hammer Prototype (Recipe)|Explosive Hammer Prototype]].
1. Find the various ingredients the recipe requires in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]].
   - *Note: Track Harvests is helpful with these items.*
   - Harvest 2 [[Flammable amadou]] from **bracket fungus** at the [[Abandoned Fungus Farm]] (nearly due west of the city). Possible locations:
     - {{waypoint 804.74, 40.34, 291.03}}
     - {{waypoint 801.63, 45.80, 276.91}}
     - {{waypoint 797.62, 47.93, 279.52}}
     - etc.
   - Harvest 2 [[Chert nodule|chert nodules]] at the entrance to the [[Glittervein Depths]] {{waypoint 542.62, 124.8, 449.28}} or the [[Splitiron Mine]] {{waypoint 285.38, 127.68, 219.39}} outside of Maldura .
   - Mine 10 fresh [[Splitiron ore]] (from dark ore nodes).
1. Craft an [[Explosive Hammer Prototype]].
   - There is a work bench right beside [[Charles Q Tinkersmith|Tinkersmith]].
1. Return to [[Charles Q Tinkersmith]]

## Needed Materials

- [[Splitiron ore]] 10
- [[Thalumbral root]] 3
- [[Umbrite]] 3
- [[Thaumic Coal]] 5

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13200 status
