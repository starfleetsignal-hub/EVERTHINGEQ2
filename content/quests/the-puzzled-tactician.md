## Prerequisites

- You must have started the quest [[When Darkness Comes]] or [[Eliminate Geraufl]] in order to complete this quest.

## Steps

1. Help Timrek solve his puzzle by moving the 5 troll statues onto the platforms in the correct order. From left to right, the order is as follows:
   - Brunk
   - Barzan
   - Vrack
   - Kraznar
   - Krunk
1. Speak with Timrek for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
