## Steps

1. Travel to [[T'Narev]]. {{waypoint 109.29, -90.62, -1356.46}}
1. Speak to [[Cong Zi]]. {{waypoint 338, -101, -1324}}
1. Travel to [[The Living Tombs]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -369, -141, -1041}}.  Gather five stoneware fragments, which look like broken pots:
   - {{waypoint -605, 9, 317}} near entrance, hang a left before you drop down
   - {{waypoint -560, 8, 313}} near entrance, further along the same duct as the first
   - {{waypoint -500, 8, 317}} near entrance, further along the same duct as the second
   - {{waypoint -294, 15, 291}} in the water duct leading to the [[Priestess' Court]]
   - {{waypoint -418, 14, 288}} east of the fourth, heading back to the entrance
1. Return to [[Cong Zi]].
1. Return to [[Disciple Shula]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Exp only.
