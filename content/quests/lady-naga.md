## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A djinn pillar sentinel|djinn pillar sentinel]]s at the far southwest corner of [[The Pillars of Flame]]. {{waypoint 1570.25, -97.46, -76.37}}
1. Return to Qadira.
1. Kill [[Samirah, Lady Naga]] in a cave on the same island. {{waypoint 1101, -173, -1693}} (see talk page for strategy)
1. Return to Qadira.

## Rewards

- [[Qadira's Bangle]]
- [[Samirah's mirror]] - Teleports you into the Shimmering Citadel
- [[Tidal Lord]] - Arena Pet
