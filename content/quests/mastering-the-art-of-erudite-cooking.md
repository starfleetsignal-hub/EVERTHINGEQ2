## Steps

![Odus sweet potato](images/Odus_sweet_potato_%28Visible%29.jpg)

1. Gather 8 [[Odus sweet potato|Odus sweet potatoes]]. These appear as small flowers just outside [[Quel'ule]] {{waypoint 34, 370, 728}}
1. Return to [[Chef Bahiyya]] and hail her.
1. Taste the stew by clicking the cauldron beside [[Chef Bahiyya|Bahiyya]].
1. Speak again to [[Chef Bahiyya]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- After this quest is completed, you may also drink from the cauldron next to the NPC to gain a temporary buff called "Chef Bahiyya's Savory Stew" which will increase your Max Health by 400 hitpoints, and your Crit Chance by 5%. This is only active while in The Sundered Frontier or The Stonebrunt Highlands, however.
