## Notes

- This is the former access quest for [[The Overlord's Throne Room]] and [[The Chamber of Rulgax]].
- You will need to know the language [[Goblish]] to read The Book of Baylzuthak.

## Steps

1. Examine a puddle of gunk in the room filled with [[A Gorehorn pitmaster|Gorehorn pitmasters]].  {{waypoint -84, 1, -117}}.
1. Kill [[Hammerhead]] at {{waypoint 3, -6, -195}} and receive a *hornlet charm*.
1. Give the charm to Dyrilisia.
1. Speak to the [[A junk heap|junk heap]] called Rubbish. He is a scarecrow in the [[The Runnyeye Chef|Runnyeye Chef's]] room at {{waypoint 85, -16, -239}}.
1. Inspect a pile of books in Runnyeye at {{waypoint 205, -41, -138}}. [[The Book of Baylzuthak]] will spawn assisted by two [[A journal of witchcraft|journals of witchcraft]] and [[A journal of remedies]]. Kill them all and then read the [[The Book of Baylzuthak (item)|book]] you receive.
1. Speak to the [[Duke Zoryll]] roaming around {{waypoint 170, -41, -150}}.
1. Kill the [[Thing of Shattered Iron]]. {{waypoint 215, -15, 38}}
1. Speak to Duke Zoryll again.
1. Approach the door leading to [[The Overlord's Throne Room]] {{waypoint 174, -16, -221}} and the quest will complete.

## Rewards

- Access to [[The Overlord's Throne Room]] -- *No Longer Needed To Access*
- [[The Book of Baylzuthak (house item)]]
