## Steps

1. Travel to [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]] and kill [[Graderal Shanksplitter]] who will spawn near the large wood mill in [[Three Toe's Watering Hole]] at {{waypoint 581, -22, -264}}
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- +3000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
