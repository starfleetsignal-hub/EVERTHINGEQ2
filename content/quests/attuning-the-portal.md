## Steps

1. Craft the items needed to attune the portal using the [[Portal Components for Fun and Profit]] recipe provided.
   1. Craft the [[Resonance Crystal]].
   1. Craft the [[Portal Attunement Device]].
      - *Note: The nearest workbench is down a few steps from Brumley at {{waypoint -70.08, 11.50, 173.42}}*
1. Return to [[Brumley Langboom|Brumley]].

## Needed Materials

- [[Bornite nodule]] (6)
- [[Thalumbral root]] (1)
- [[Umbrite]] (6)
- [[Thaumic Coal]] (2)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- [[Coin of the Great Artificer]]
- +1,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +5,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
