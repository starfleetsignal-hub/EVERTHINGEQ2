## Notes

- You **have** to speak the language [[Volant]] to receive this quest.
- He may not have a feather over his head, but he will give you the quest if you qualify.
- You can pick up [[Might of the Aviaks]] from [[Gaer Torrent]] at the same time as this quest.

## Steps

1. Enter the [[Palace of the Awakened]] on the [[Isle of Awakening]] in [[The Barren Sky]].  The quest completes immediately.
