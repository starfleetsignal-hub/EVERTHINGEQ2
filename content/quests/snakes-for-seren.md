## Description

"Seren Leafsway, a woodelf stationed at Spire Shadow Outpost is alarmed by the pack of kobolds that have emerged from Steamfont, nearby. She would like to know that her scouts will remain safe, and has asked me to catch baby river racers for them. It seems that they give off a pungent odor that repels the beasts." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill [[A river racer|river racers]] in the river next to the camp and collect 13 spawned babies.
   - [[A darkened racer]] will also spawn babies.
1. Return to Seren

## Rewards

- At least 27s 29c
