## Prerequirements

- Must be level 7 of one [[Ascension]] class to start the quest

## Requirements

- [[High Keep: The Bloodless Incursion Timeline]]

## Steps

1. Unsure where we are going, we need to fight our way onward.
   1. Time to look around and not die in the process. Use [[Poison Protection]]. Proceed to {{waypoint 0, -2, -15}}.
   1. I need to figure out how to wake the Skeleton King. Place the [[Reassembled map of the dead]] on the block at {{waypoint 1, -1, 14}}.
1. I should engage the Skeleton King and hope I do not die. Speak to the Skeleton King.
1. Arthiden Vowbreaker requires a dagger, fueled by living blood.
   - *Note: Make sure you are at least 110 so the king is green or you will not get a chest and you have to wait 3 days for the instance to reset*
   1. I need to make a trade with the Skeleton King. Trade the Bloody Shank, and kill the Skeleton King.
      - *Note: Requires completion of [[High Keep: The Bloodless Incursion Timeline]].*
   1. I need to loot [[Rider's Bane]] from the Skeleton King's corpse.
   1. I should not forget the [[Vessel of Agglomerative Flesh]] at {{waypoint -4, -1, 13}}.
   1. I should speak to Dolas (hail him in the cave).
1. I need to deliver the [[Vessel of Agglomerative Flesh]], like I promised. Speak with [[Kolanas Kirr]] in [[The Ruins of Cabilis: The Necromancer's Den]].
1. Head back to Dolas' camp in the Sinking Sands. You arrive just in time to see Dolas being abducted by the Riders of Ik.
1. It is time to defeat these Riders of Ik and their Horse Queen. Enter [[Kurn's Tower: Saving Dolas]] at {{waypoint -2006, -353, -1801}} in Fens.
   1. I need to use Rider's Bane to defeat the Riders of Ik. Kill all Riders of Ik.
   1. I should rescue Dolas at {{waypoint 62, -2, 20}}.
   1. Time to take out The Horse Queen. Kill The Horse Queen.
      - *Note: Make sure you let Dolas out of the cage BEFORE attacking the Queen or you'll have to wait 90 mins and reset the instance.*
   1. I should discuss our triumph with Dolas.
1. Dolas still cannot return safely to his home.
   1. I need to speak to Archmage Garla. Speak to [[Archmage Garla Cheris]] at {{waypoint -188, 57, -223}} in inner [[Nye'Caelona]].
1. I must track down and obtain ancient iksar relics of power.
   - *Note: These are click updates only, no item will be received. Must Clear all names to get. 5/25/21- Solo or Heroic can be used for Venerated, Reaver's, and *Possibly* Cabilis (Glox did not spawn in the solo, used Heroic for update, cleared timers, tried solo again, Glox spawned on 2nd try (5th & final named))...Baron's, Wizard's, and Shrouded *definitely* require the Heroic version.*
   - Collect a ceremonial incense bowl at {{waypoint -157, 27, -471}} in [[Crypt of Dalnir: Baron's Workshop (Heroic)]].
   - Collect a ceremonial wand at {{waypoint 639, -58, 133}} in [[Crypt of Dalnir: Wizard's Den (Event Heroic)]].
   - Collect a ceremonial emblem at {{waypoint 303, -1, 388}} in [[The Ruins of Cabilis (Heroic)]].
   - Collect a ceremonial shroud at {{waypoint -480, 114, -349}} in [[Kaesora: Tomb of the Venerated (Event Heroic)]].
   - Collect a ceremonial dagger at {{waypoint 80, -4, -1063}} in [[Lost City of Torsis: Reaver's Remnants (Heroic)]].
   - Collect a ceremonial drum at {{waypoint -151, 20, -315}} in [[Lost City of Torsis: The Shrouded Temple (Event Heroic)]].
1. I need to take all this stuff back to Archmage Garla.
   - Speak to [[Archmage Garla Cheris]] at {{waypoint -188, 57, -223}} in inner [[Nye'Caelona]]. Receive a [[Sathirian Protection Bauble]].
1. It is time to end this. Drusella must be defeated.
   1. Enter [[Sacred Temple: Drusella's Time]].
   1. Kill [[Drusella Sathir]]. You may need to use [[Sathirian Protection Bauble]] during the fight. There is nothing to loot here, so if she is gray, it is OK.
1. I need to return to Dolas and tell him of the victory against Drusella Sathir.
   - Speak to Dolas at {{waypoint -721, -62, -195}} in [[The Sinking Sands]].
1. I am to meet Dolas back at his home city, [[Nye'Caelona]].
   1. Speak to Chancellor Thanial Vylardin at -219.50 57.34 -224.35 138.88 0.00 0.00 in inner [[Nye'Caelona]].
   1. Speak to Dolas Vylardin at {{waypoint -218, 57, -222}} in inner [[Nye'Caelona]].
