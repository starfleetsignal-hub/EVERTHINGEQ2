## Steps

1. Search for evidence of Order of Rime activity:
   - Kill [[A hidden elite sleetsaber]] south of  [[Ganak's Battlement]]. {{waypoint -412, 18, 185}}
   - Check the entrance to [[Chelsith]] {{waypoint 564, -147, 1341}}
   - Check the entrance to [[Charasis: Vault of Eternal Sleep]] {{waypoint 607, -126, 197}}
   - Check the entrance to [[Charasis: Maiden's Chamber]] {{waypoint 816, -128, 375}}
   - Check the entrance to Charasis: The [[Emperor's Athenaeum]] {{waypoint 867, -128, 162}}
1. Return to [[Zixka Slizzis]] at the [[Upper Command Camp]].

- [[Small Charasis pillar]]
- At least 41g 63s 17c
- +1000 faction with **Legion of Danak**
