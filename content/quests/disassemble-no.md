## Steps

1. "Find something to help Burnish"    Click on sparkly pot at {{waypoint 712, -43, -475}} You receive a crate called [[Adjusted W.H.O.O. Components]].
1. Unpack it to receive the following:
   - [[Defunct W.H.O.O. Advanced]]
   - [[Processed Debris]]
   - [[Recipe Scroll: Adjusted W.H.O.O.]] which auto-scribes.
1. Craft [[Adjusted W.H.O.O.]] at crafting work bench(requires 5 Zimaran Coal)
1. Return to [[Burnish (Aspiring Artisan)|Burnish]]

## Rewards

- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c 53684 status
