## Steps

1. Speak to [[Taldar D'Aryth|Taldar]] {{waypoint -301, 13, -41}}
1. Speak to [[Baelfang]] {{waypoint -433, 14, 38}}
1. Speak to [[Catha Firebolt|Catha]] {{waypoint -424, 16, 79}}
1. Return to Vanesta {{waypoint -430, 14, 35}}

## Rewards

- At least 5g 13s 8c
- unlocks the daily tradeskill quest from [[Vanesta Leaftraveler]]
- Scribes 3 recipes for future daily tradeskill quests
  - Clean Bedroll
  - Keg of Healing Tonic
  - Polished Focus Crystal
