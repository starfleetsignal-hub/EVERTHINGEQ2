## Steps

1. Collect *Amulets of Binding* (always update) from several undead. All are found around [[Citadel of Gul'thex]] {{waypoint 691.87, -0.00, -719.66}}.
   - 4 [[A Gul'Thex high guard|Gul'Thex high guards]] -- these spawn ONLY with the ring event mobs inside the citadel used for spawning [[Captain T'Sanne]]. There should be at least one in each wave, however, there are only two waves and then T'Sanne spawns alone. After that is a 30 minute respawn timer for the ring event to restart.
   - 2 [[Dragoon lieutenant|dragoon lieutenants]] -- these can be found around and inside the citadel.
   - [[Lord J'Narus]] -- spawns behind the citadel near the north corner. {{waypoint 724, 4, -761}}
1. Return to Pulsarian.

- At least 17s 43c
