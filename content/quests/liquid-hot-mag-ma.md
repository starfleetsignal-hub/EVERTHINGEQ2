## Description

The Dragoons of Thex have indicated that they are interested in sampling some of the magmatic rock located within the Deep Forge. They wish to study the rocks for potential properties that may advance their motives. I should destroy the Overseer so that I may search the area without interruption.

## Steps

1. Destroy the Overseer. This will automatically stop the lava fall allowing you to enter the last room with the Firelord.
1. Obtain eight samples of Magma Rock within [[The Deep Forge]]
   - Sample from the first magma rock
   - Sample from the second magma rock
   - Sample from the third magma rock
   - Sample from the fourth magma rock
   - Sample from the fifth magma rock
   - Sample from the sixth magma rock
   - Sample from the seventh magma rock
   - Sample from the last magma rock
   - These are all big clicky iron-beam "shod" rocks on the outside edge of the lava pool in the last room.  The last one is the largest and is on a sort of pedestal behind the named.
1. Return to [[Isxot D'Zyath]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
