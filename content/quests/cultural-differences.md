## Steps

1. Use [[Boarfiend Costume]] to apply the illusion.
1. Speak to [[Bugluk]] within the [[Boarfiend Caves]]. {{waypoint 1577, 457, -2}}
   - If you happen to die on the way to the boarfiend cave, the Bugluk will not speak to you. You will need to find a safe spot and cancel the illusion (looks like a wolf head) and recast the illusion.
1. Return to Nesbit.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
