## Steps

1. Receive a [[Voice Modulator]] from [[Vernon Tinworthy]] which allows you to eavesdrop on others.
   - Targets spawn and are up from 8PM to 5AM game time.
   - Approach the target until you get a message that says the location is good for modulator use. Move closer to trigger conversation between the NPCs. Move back to the modulator use loc and right-click or use modulator on your quest helper. You may have to do some moving around and back and forth until the dialogue box to "put modulator to your ear" comes up.
   1. Listen in on [[Augurer Phineas]] in [[South Qeynos]] across from the mage tower bridge at {{waypoint 652, -19, 142}}. I could use the device here; {{waypoint 639, -18, 119}}.
   1. Listen in on [[Augurer Arnon]] in [[South Qeynos]] near the Antonica gates at {{waypoint 421.29, -20.17, 242}}.
   1. Listen in on [[Augurer Vharlan]] in [[South Qeynos]] inside the Lion's Mane inn (after 8pm game time).{{waypoint 534, -9, 131}}.
   1. Listen in on [[Novitiate Emily Shorechaser]] in [[South Qeynos]] near the Qeynos Harbor gates at {{waypoint 757, -19, 194}}.
   1. Listen in on [[Augurer Caranthir]] in [[South Qeynos]] next to the Down Below at {{waypoint 383, -20, 167}}.
1. Finish by speaking to [[Towser Flizgibbert]] {{waypoint 692, -16, -456}} in [[The Elddar Grove]].
