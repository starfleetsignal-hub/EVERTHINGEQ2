Requires 10,000 Faction with [[Defenders of the Seal]]

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Cantara]] in [[Dartain's Fortress]] at {{waypoint 535, 132, -245}}.
1. Cantara will give you [[Official Defenders of the Seal Scouting Uniform]], which turns you into a big rock once activated.
1. Go to {{waypoint -131, 81, -803}} (near [[Petar]] on the rocky bridge leading into [[The Tower of Serilis]] to activate the illusion.  It will give you 50k faction with the Underoot, however it can only be used in this loc and it roots you.
1. After a short period of time, two NPC's will come close to you, have a short dialog then disperse. The illusion cannot be canceled, but will then wear off.
1. Return to [[Cantara]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 55g 70s 61c
- +2500 faction with **Defenders of the Seal**
- 1 [[Mark of Manaar]]
