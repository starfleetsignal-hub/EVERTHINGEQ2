## Steps

1. Enter [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Cursed Vaults]] within [[Great Divide]] Take bird to Icy Fingers.
   - *Note: The zone is **NOT** an Advanced Solo one*
1. Find a sample of Mayong's magic: go through the zone, click on the mirror, enter the next floor and speak to Librarian Emiida
1. Defeat 12 the hate-filled tomes in the Libary which spawn rapidly all over that floor.
   - *Note:other group members can only see them when they are on the same step, but they'll spawn for each group member too*
1. Speak to the librarian again
1. Enter the Arcane Archives through the door near {{waypoint -134, 51, -94}}
1. Search out an example of Mayong's magic in the Arcane Archives - go left or right around Zorglim
1. Click the globe at {{waypoint 15, 555, -46}}
1. Kill the spirit of Cara Omica
   - *Note:She spawns as soon as you click the globe: a level 101 ^ Heroic.*
1. Click the globe again
1. Kill Zorglim the Departed
1. Speak with the Duality again in either Freeport or Qeynos
1. A portal to the Research Hall of Mayong Mistmoore opens behind the Duality - zone in
1. Kill Vampires and loot the 6 Runes (Ayr, Hex, Myr, Teir, Wen, & Yal) needed to unlock the door at {{waypoint -53, 0, 30}}
   - *Note: keep your eyes open for Green Shinies, as these are also needed Runes.*
1. Click on the stone tablet at {{waypoint -71, -1, 46}}
1. Speak with Mayong Mistmoore - after several questions you are returned to the Duality
1. Speak with the Duality to finish the Quest
