## Notes

- Leave at least **12** Maedjinn trash alive while gathering the wish-burnished gold and wish-polished silver

## Steps

1. Enter [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion (Solo)|Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion [Solo]]] {{waypoint -797.32, 174.52, 194.32}}
1. Speak with Tekeshi in the vale {{waypoint -801.60, 76.20, 286.95}}
1. Gather 16 wish-burnished gold and wish-polished silver
   - These are small clumps of sparkling gold and silver chunks in the ground,  usually clustered around aggro range for Zakir Rish mobs. Possibly around:
   - {{waypoint -654, 62, 553}}, {{waypoint -591, 71, 594}}, {{waypoint -602, 73, 571}}, {{waypoint -579, 77, 556}}
   - {{waypoint -540, 89, 615}}, {{waypoint -528, 81, 689}}, {{waypoint -650, 76, 669}}, {{waypoint -660, 63, 629}}
   - {{waypoint -668, 61, 625}}, {{waypoint -683, 68, 612}}, {{waypoint -753, 82, 659}}, {{waypoint -732, 72, 549}}
   - {{waypoint -833, 66, 523}}, {{waypoint -864, 81, 510}}, {{waypoint -876, 86, 493}}, {{waypoint -859, 81, 471}}
   - Note: This is not a complete list of locations.  ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Wish-polished_Silver.png)  ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Wish-polished_Gold.png)
   - If you're able to avoid being interrupted while gathering, you can collect the chunks and then run off the aggro'd djinn until they leash, leaving them alive to be killed in the next step without resetting the instance.
1. Return to Tekeshi in the vale {{waypoint -801.60, 76.20, 286.95}}
1. (Inside the instance)
1. Kill 12 Zakir Rish warriors
1. Return to Tekeshi in Splendor Sky Aerie

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Aviak Ring of Malevolence]]
  - [[Vultak Signet of Pride]]
