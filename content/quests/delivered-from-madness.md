## Steps

1. Search Warslik's Woods for 8 Redscale Pitcher Leaves.![](images/Redscale_Pitcher_Leave.jpg)
   - *Note: This is a small dark bluish-black plant that is not trackable.  They tend to blend in with the ground.*
   - *Some possible locations include: {{waypoint -259, -172, -556}}, {{waypoint -307, -191, -669}}, {{waypoint -338.50, -193.20, -697.00}}, {{waypoint -114.00, -171.00, -650.00}}, {{waypoint -138.58, -172.42, -649.90}}*
1. Return to Savix'Host and make him take the medicine.
1. Take him to Crusader's Cave {{waypoint 714, 221, 861}}
   - *Note: You can fly directly to Crusader Tarrac at the inner gate of the cave to escort him there, but walk up the stairs to him to update the quest correctly*
1. Speak to [[Crusader Tarrac]] {{waypoint 648, 247, 1252}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 15108 status
