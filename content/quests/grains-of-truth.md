## Steps

1. Click the **loaves of bread** in the [[Great Artisan Hall]], located in the [[The Bazaar Corridor]] {{waypoint -88.54, -0.65, 18.08}}![Fresh Baked Bread](images/Fresh_Baked_Bread.jpg).
1. Speak to [[Nicleath]] {{waypoint -88.88, -1.38, 13.28}}.
1. Investigate the [[Fordel Farm Land]] in [[Shadow Haven]] {{waypoint -739, 1, -301}}.
1. Remove the reishi infestation by killing [[A reishi sprout|reishi sprouts]] and [[A reishi sporeling|reishi sporelings]].
1. Return to [[Gavin Grimson]] in [[Grimthor's Distillery]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- One of the following:
  - [[Grimthor's Yttrium Circlet]]
  - [[Grimthor's Brawl Hammer]]
  - [[Grimthor's Bouncer]]

## Notes

- While you are in [[Grimthor's Distillery]], you may wish to purchase a stack or so of your favorite alcoholic beverage, you will need it elsewhere.
