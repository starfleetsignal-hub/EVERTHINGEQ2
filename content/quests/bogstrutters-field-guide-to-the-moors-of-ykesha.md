## Notes

- It is recommended you visit the locations in the order listed.
- The waypoints listed are approximate and can represent large areas that are points of interest.
- Having a flying or gliding mount makes it easier to complete this quest.

## Steps

1. Visit the following locations:
   - Upper Moors:
     - *The curious aberration in the mountains*: [[Obelisk of Ahkzul (POI)|Obelisk of Ahkzul]] {{waypoint 957, 513, 1844}}
     - *See what has become of Guk*: [[Entrance to Guk]] {{waypoint 787, 141, -786}}
     - *The place where trolls and frogloks have come together*: [[Firmroot Moot]] {{waypoint 147, -84, -418}}
     - *The home of the Grobb trolls*: [[Grobb]] {{waypoint -195, -82, -903}}
     - *Another curious aberration in the swamp area*: [[Anchor of Bazzul (POI)|Anchor of Bazzul]] {{waypoint -214, -51, 148}}
     - *The home of the frogloks*: [[Tupta]] {{waypoint -66, -54, 637}}
     - *The Brokenskull outpost in Rathe Mountains*: [[Brokenskull Loft]] {{waypoint 391, 71, 874}}
     - *The large ruins to an old troll civilization*: [[Ykesha's Spine]] {{waypoint 223, -97, -172}}
   - Lower Moors:
     - *The Brokenskull Captain's sanctuary*: [[Graw's Crown]] {{waypoint -1334, -653, 353}}
       - The easiest way to access this area without a flying mount is to complete [[A Rat's a Rat's a Rat]] (starter quest is [[Frying Small Fish]]).
       - You can also get to this area by jumping off [[The Overlook]] with glide, featherfall, or any other slow falling ability, and aiming for the large rock spire in the lower moors. A carpet or cloud mount works best if you are falling short. You can acquire a glider mount with the quest [[Endangered Species]]. [[Gnomish Parachute Pack]] will **not** last long enough to get you there.
       - For these who couldn't reach the top, there is another way. Jump from the tree in Tupta to the rock shelf on the lower left from the tree. Then walk along the shelf till getting in front of the spike, then jump down. Longer but an easier way and a parachute can work from here. You will still fall and die, but as long as you land on the spire (dead or alive), you will get the update. Aim for the water!
       - If you have a flying mount simply take a balloon to the Shadowed Cleft station and fly up from there.
     - *An abandoned Brokenskull post now occupied by others*: [[The Shadowed Cleft]] {{waypoint -1257, -985, -33}}
     - *The home of the mushroom resembling men*: [[Tillage Patch]] {{waypoint -770, -918, -364}}
     - *The central home of the Brokenskull clan*: [[Gunthak's Shanty]] {{waypoint -1270, -953, 635}}
     - *The harbor*: [[Brokenskull Harbor]] {{waypoint -1736, -1101, 1203}}
     - *The island shaped like a skull*: [[Brokenskull Rock]] {{waypoint -1884, -1094, 2700}}
     - The Brokenskull Captain's sanctuary: Graw's Crown, the large spire found near the Shadowed Cleft balloon station. {{waypoint -1334, -653, 353}} Copy *(lower Moors)*
1. Return to Amelia Bogstrutter.
