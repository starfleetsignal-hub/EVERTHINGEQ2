## Steps

1. [[Patricia Benson|Patricia's]] son [[Bartholomew Benson|Bartholomew]] is missing and probably tried to follow you to Nettleville. Go back to [[Rendyl|Guard Rendyl]] at the [[Nettleville: Strange Tidings|Nettleville]] gate and ask him about the boy.
1. Enter [[Nettleville: Strange Tidings|Nettleville]] to search for [[Bartholomew Benson|Bartholomew]].{{waypoint 681, -21, 271}}
   - In the old smithy, you find [[Investigator Rashlas]] attempting to make a Freeblood attack [[Bartholomew Benson|Bartholomew]]. Slay the Investigator!
   - Speak to [[Bartholomew Benson|Bartholomew]].
1. Return to [[Patricia Benson]].
