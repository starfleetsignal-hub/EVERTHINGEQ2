## Steps

1. Travel to [[Loping Plains]] and speak with [[Zha Gu'Lau (Loping Plains)|Zha Gu'Lau]] in [[Village of Somborn]]. {{waypoint -355, 15, 40}}
1. Pillage *Ysa's grave*, northeast of Somborn Village, around {{waypoint -494, -13, -141}}. There are several burial sites across these hills. When you harvest them there is a chance you either get the update, or spawn [[A disturbed spirit]].
1. Return to Zha Gu'Lau.
1. Find and read the book "Tragedy of Ysa". {{waypoint -390, 10, -90}}
1. Return to Zha Gu'Lau.
1. Go to the bridge over [[Wayunder Lake]], use the scroll Zha gave you while standing on the bridge, and click on the glowing spirit that appears above you.{{waypoint -560, 10, -75}}
1. The spirit of [[John Dondrael]] will spawn from the lake and walk towards you. Speak to him and he will drop *Ysa's Belongings* next to him. {{waypoint -560, -15, -60}}
1. Return to Zha Gu'Lau.
1. Place the *Ysa's Belongings* at the following locations in the village:
   - Drop the jewelery box near [[Vinatori]]: {{waypoint -389, 9, -84}}
   - Drop the scarf near a house: {{waypoint -335.5, 14.5, -6.6}}
   - Drop the hairbrush near the church: {{waypoint -387, 12, -61}}
1. Return to Zha Gu'Lau.

## Rewards

- +7500 faction with **Cazic-Thule**
- Unlocks [[Spines of Thule]] and [[Terror Link]].
- [[Danak's Altar of Cazic Thule]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Thulian Band]]
  - [[Thulian Ring]]
