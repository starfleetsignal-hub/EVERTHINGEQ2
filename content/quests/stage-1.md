## Steps

1. Poison 6 Haoaeran Syrup Barrels in [[Haoaera]] at the roots of the large trees, around {{waypoint 1711, 131, -434}}.
   - These are the same barrels that you need for the quest [[Haoaeran Syrup]].
   - The barrels are guarded by [[A Haoaeran tapper|Haoaeran tappers]] that you will need for the quest [[Dismantling Haoaera: Tappers]].
1. Return to Ancho Mok'Nok.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 67c
