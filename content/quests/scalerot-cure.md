## Steps

![Shatterweed](images/Shatterweed.png)

1. Obtain the following:
   - 8 *solvent fish oil* by killing Yha-lei. {{waypoint 461, -114, 670}}
   - 6 *shatterweed* by harvesting nodes of shatterweed. These will look similar to the normal Nathsarian shrubbery nodes, but will not have a title when moused over from afar:
     - {{waypoint 437, -15, 1259}}
     - {{waypoint 462, -21, 1249}}
     - {{waypoint 443, -35, 1092}}
     - {{waypoint 470, -55, 1024}}
     - {{waypoint 447, -57, 1025}}
     - {{waypoint 325, -41, 1123}}
     - {{waypoint 231, -35, 1111}}
     - {{waypoint 198, -39, 1083}}
     - {{waypoint 173, -56, 1012}}
1. Give the cure to [[Outrider Aerkun]] at {{waypoint 422, -21, 1193}}.
1. Give the cure to [[Outrider Bewin]] at {{waypoint 164, -63, 994}}.
1. Give the cure to [[Outrider Wyrlyk]] at {{waypoint 644, 9, 1289}}.
1. Give the cure to [[Outrider Clazkaer]] at {{waypoint 301, -48, 1058}}.
1. Return to [[Oracle Vorzalka]] in [[Bathezid's Watch]].

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- +5000 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
