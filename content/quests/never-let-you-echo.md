## Steps

1. Enter [[Echo Caverns: Quarry Quandary (Solo)]] in search of the Fordel Defenders and the Demolitionist.
1. Clear thought horror mobs ([[A deepshade lurker|deepshade lurkers]], [[A deepshade deathwarden|deepshade deathwardens]], [[A deepshade tormentor|deepshade tormentors]], etc.)  until [[Undertaker Zemilox]] spawns. {{waypoint 133.18, 110.00, 9.12}}. Kill him.
1. Proceed past the quarry into the [[Limestone Forest]] {{waypoint 459.89, 137.95, 57.90}}. Kill all the groups with [[A needlite bloodroyal|needlite bloodroyals]] to spawn [[The Needlite Queen]] {{waypoint 694, 180, 283}}.
1. Find two slain Defenders of the Haven at {{waypoint 390, 139, 429}}.
1. Move into [[The Chlorochamber]] and kill all the tegi boglings, including the [[A bogling of Broglyn|bogling of Broglyn]] that pops after each group of three dies to spawn [[Chief Broglyn]] {{waypoint -41.97, 173.86, 378.68}}. Kill him.
   - Summons a [[Corrupted bogling]] four times, so 80%, 60%, 40%, and 20%, which curses you with an incurable massive Crit Bonus and Crit Chance debuff.  You must kill the [[Corrupted bogling]] to remove it, or you will die (which also removes it).  Duo this if you are low DPS, or bring a merc that will rez you.  If you get rezzed, you can kill the add and continue the fight.
   - If you are having trouble with Broglyn, go back and find the boglings that you failed to kill earlier and kill them.  He heals more for every [[A bogling of Broglyn|bogling of Broglyn]] that you didn't kill.
1. Go to [[Tuatara Rim]] {{waypoint -170.95, 217.75, 133.01}} and kill [[A tuatara cliffcoasterS|tuatara cliffcoasters]] and [[A tuatara preyculler|tuatara preycullers]], then slay [[Killmodo]]. This is a DPS check as adds spawn quite frequently. Just concentrate on Killmodo and burn him down.
1. Find the rest of the Defenders of the Haven at {{waypoint -388, 217, -35}} and speak to [[Hrewa Medinal]].
1. Return to [[Captain Meryn Gleamheart]] in the [[Luclin Nexus]].

## Notes

To finish the zone, go to [[The Colossal Cascades]] {{waypoint -393.40, 176.07, 325.71}} and kill [[Scyphodon]].

- Collect **colossal cane** and use it to be able to see [[Scyphodon]].
- When Scyphodon emotes, "Those with a bit of sugar in their veins may soon be devoured!" use **/cancel_maintained Magic Sugar**.  You can also drag the **colossal cane** onto your hotbar, and use and cancel it by clicking.
- Cure elemental dot only while on land. Curing it in the water electrocutes you.
