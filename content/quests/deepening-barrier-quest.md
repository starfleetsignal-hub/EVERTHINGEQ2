## Notes

- [[Wooden plank]] can drop from [[A rock writher]], [[A rubble writher]], [[A crag writher]], and possibly the beetles found in The Thundering Steppes.

## Steps

1. Collect 10 gnoll sweat glands from any gnolls in [[The Thundering Steppes]]. Auto update, uncommon.
1. Collect 1 mystic paint from a [[Steelhoof mystic]]. Can be found in [[Shattered Gully]] {{waypoint 566, 13, 441}} or in the [[Crested Lowlands]] {{waypoint 90, 0, 91}}. Auto update.
1. Travel to the [[Valley of the Ancients]] {{waypoint 714, -29, 616}} to bless the armor. This completes the quest.
