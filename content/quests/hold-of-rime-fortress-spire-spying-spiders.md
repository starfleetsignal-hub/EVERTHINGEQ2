## Description

Bergna Veliumsmiter has told me that Thurgadin's gnomish allies have made clockwork spiders that are nearly impossible to tell from the real thing. These ingenious devices wander the Rime Fortress Spire and record conversations that the Thurgadin army can later use to make battle plans. Unfortunately the recall mechanism doesn't seem to work correctly and I have been asked to enter the fortress and retrieve the errant clockworks.

## Steps

- ***Find the first clockwork spider***
  - This spider roams somewhere in [[Rime Mess Hall]] {{waypoint -141, 0, -23}}. Recommend killing [[Agna Icespear]] first in order to search the spiders unimpeded when the epic mobs change to non-hostile
- ***Find the second clockwork spider***
  - This spider roams somewhere on [[Rime Docks]] {{waypoint -399, -35, 204}}. Recommend draining the lake and killing [[Dockmaster Jit'coel]] before searching.
- ***Find the third clockwork spider***
  - This spider roams somewhere around [[Rime Barracks]] {{waypoint -523, 50, 59}}. Spiders can be both around the upper rim or down in the sparring pit.
- ***Find the fourth clockwork spider***
  - This spider roams somewhere on the outer walls of [[Fortress Control Room]] {{waypoint -300, 140, 204}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 80g
- 1 [[Primal Velium Shard]] (2 if member)
