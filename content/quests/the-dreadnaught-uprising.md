## Steps

1. Use a city bell to enter [[The Sprawl]]
1. Kill 15 rioting Dreadnaughts
   - You can speak to Lerenth {{waypoint -45, -4, -45}} to turn him into a level plus one mob that follows you around and sometimes attacks your opponents
   - Starting at {{waypoint -22, -2, -51}}, NPC [[A rioting basher|rioting bashers]], [[A rioting brute|rioting brutes]], and [[A rioting thug|rioting thugs]] will aggro as you move down the road; they can be one down arrow, even con, or one up arrow
1. Kill [[Modoc the Fist]] (level plus one, even con) {{waypoint 57, -2, -21}}. Kill the two [[A trained hound|trained hounds]] (level plus one, one down) first, as they shield Modoc.
1. Speak to [[Lerenth (The Sprawl)|Lerenth]] {{waypoint -45, -4, -45}}
