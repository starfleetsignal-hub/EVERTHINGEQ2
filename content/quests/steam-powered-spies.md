## Steps

![Clockwork Gnome Observatron](images/Clockwork_Gnome_Observatron.jpg)

1. Speak with [[Gimble Tinkerton]] in the building east of the [[Steamfont Windmill]] {{waypoint -1093, 144, 1102}}
1. Kill [[A clockwork collector|Clockwork collectors]] OR [[Clockwork disassembler|Clockwork disassemblers]] outside of Gimble's building until you have 3 Voltaic Batteries.
1. Charge the batteries at the charging station inside the [[Steamfont Windmill]] {{waypoint -1021, 143, 1110}}
1. Install the charged batteries in the clockwork [[Gnome Observatron|gnome observatrons]] (see the picture) in the building east of the [[Steamfont Windmill]] at {{waypoint -1093, 144, 1102}}. To do this, simply right-click on them. It will install the battery and add the [[Gnome Observatron]] to your inventory.
1. Return to [[Waynil Garwater]]
