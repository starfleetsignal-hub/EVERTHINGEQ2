This quest is part 4 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Gnome Series.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Frondo Wignin]] in the south-west corner of Temple Street {{waypoint 111, -4, 106}}.
1. Walk the stairs connecting the south-west corner of Temple Street with the main part of the zone {{waypoint 69, 2, 70}}. The pig Jimbly will attack you (level 5 with 2 down arrows). Kill Jimbly.
1. Return to [[Neezer Grund (Temple Street)|Neezer Grund]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 16c
- One of the following:
  - [[Jimbly Tooth Necklace (WIS/INT version)]]
  - [[Jimbly Tooth Necklace (STA version)]]
