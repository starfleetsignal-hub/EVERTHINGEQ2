## Notes

![Shown here: the baubles for this race float in the air. The cubes to launch players line the ground.](images/Bristlebane-bounce-example.jpg)
The aim of this game is to race the clock and collect as many baubles as you can by bouncing on the cubes around the [[Croc Hunter Camp]]. The higher up in the air the baubles are, the greater their point value.

- 1 Point: floating fish and green baubles
- 2 Points: red baubles
- 5 points: gold baubles

Tip: aim for the floating fish - they launch you even higher and cause you to accelerate, to making it easier reach the gold baubles, quickly.

## Steps

Before you can start the game, you must [[Mount|dismount]].

1. Hail Princess Evelynn Fadia
1. Step onto the Bristlebane Bounce Pad
1. After the timer counts down and the bells rings/the on-screen counter tell you to go, run around the crock camp
1. Run over the bounce-cubes along the ground and aim for the baubles up in the air
1. When the time runs out, return to the Princess to claim your prize

## Rewards

**When you complete the race you can choose [[Fool's Gold Coin]] (event currency) or one of the prizes listed below, based on how many snacks you collect before the timer runs out.**

- The amount of the Fool's Gold depends on how successful you are, but [[Free-To-Play|All Access]] members get double event currency. Therefore, an All Access member who chooses 5 Fool's Gold Coins (enormous prize) will actually receive 10.
- To see a preview of [[Appearance|appearance-only]] items or [[Housing|house items]] before you choose a prize (while in-game), place your mouse over the item icon, hold down CTRL, and L click.
