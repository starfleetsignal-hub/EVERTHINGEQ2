## Steps

1. Head to the eastern lower Tizmak caves, which can be accessed at {{waypoint -409, -367, -160}}
1. Apply poison to the braziers by clicking on them at:
   - {{waypoint -385, -388, -129}}
   - {{waypoint -351, -392, -14}}
   - {{waypoint -445, -392, -21}}Note: 3 man team all clicked the same Brazier and got all 3 updates..( 6-13-2011 )
1. Return to Zalyn D'Kilneld to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 29g
