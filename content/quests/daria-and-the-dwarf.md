This quest is part 5 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Bugs

- [[Daria]] sometimes does not have a quest feather, however, hailing her allows one to proceed through her dialog and receive the five scrying stones.

## Steps

- Speak to [[Daria]]. She is at the right side of [[N'Marr's Ascent]], in a small camp at the base of the cliff. {{waypoint -408, 2, -1261}}
- Daria will tell you about [[A scrying stone|scrying stones]], and will give you 5 of them to start you off. You don't have to do anything with them for this quest.
- Return to Torq {{waypoint 673.44, 0.46, -273.81}} as he has a feather to continue the quest.
- Return to [[Daria]].
- Return to Torq {{waypoint 673.44, 0.46, -273.81}} for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 5s 56c
