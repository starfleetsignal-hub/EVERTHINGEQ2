## Note

You will need to have completed the [[A Mark of Awakening|Mark of Awakening]] Signature Questline before Soliddar will offer this quest.

## Steps

1. Hunt 10 [[A rage-touched soullight|rage-touched soullights]] around [[Karak Peak]] for their fevered cores.
   - Not every kill will yield a core.
1. Return to Soliddar

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Ethernere Crystal Pedestal]]
