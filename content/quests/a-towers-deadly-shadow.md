## Notes

- **This quest is now repeatable without an 18 hour restriction**

## Steps

1. Kill 13 monsters of a certain kind. They may be any of the following:
   - [[A frostbite shadowbeast]] {{waypoint -1124, -538, -2084}}
   - [[A frostdeath dervish]] {{waypoint -1073, -505, -1607}}
1. Return to [[Bitter]]

- +500 faction with **Snowfang Gnolls**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Fishy Remnants]]
  - [[Mystic Snowfang Drum]]
  - [[Snowfang Simple Chair]]
  - [[Spiritual Snowfang Drum]]
