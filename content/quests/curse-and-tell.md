## Steps

1. Gather 6 **Calabashes of Raffia Wine** in and around [[Raj'Durabad]].  (they are very small and inside the tents)
   - Possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -197, 106, -220}}
     - {{waypoint -287, 104, -248}}
     - {{waypoint -282, 104, -267}}
     - {{waypoint -256, 98, -301}}

![Calabash of Raffia Wine](images/Calabash_of_Raffia_Wine.png)

1. Return to [[Sezgin]]
1. Kill Raj'Dur gamblers ([[A Raj'Dur marauder|Raj'Dul marauders]], [[A Raj'Dur blackguard|Raj'Dul blackguards]], [[A Raj'Dur cutthroat|Raj'Dul cutthroats]]) {{waypoint -189.29, 111.60, -262.80}} to recover the Fizzlethorp amulet.
1. Return to [[Sezgin]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- 3 [[Calabash of Raffia Wine]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Dune Strider Sash]]
  - [[Dune Strider Bracelet]]
