## Required Materials

- 25 [[Celestial Coal]]

## Steps

1. Enter [[Solusek Ro's Tower: From the Ashes (Tradeskill)|Solusek Ro's Tower: From the Ashes [Tradeskill]]] at {{waypoint 95, 3, -165}}.
1. Collect the following:
   - 12 condensed magma harvested from lava pools.
     - There are only three pools.  They respawn in roughly the time it takes to round up the sulfur.
   - 6 sulfur shed by scavenging mephits
     - Go through the doors to the east at {{waypoint -100, -6, -102}}.
     - The hostile Mephics periodically spray a cone of fire, leaving a small heap of burning sulfur on the floor that you can harvest.
     - Harvesting the sulfur damages you.
   - 15 obsidian shards from fallen drakes.
     - Click the dead drakes to mine them for their shards.
     - They can be found in the side rooms. Click on the brazier at {{waypoint -136, -8, -102}} teleport to find more.
1. Craft the incubation vessel at the Wall of Elemental Fire at {{waypoint 0, 103, -35}}.
   - Requires:
     - 15 obsidian shards
     - 12 condensed magma
     - 6 sulphur ash
     - 25 [[Celestial Coal]]
   - Use the door at {{waypoint -100, -6, -102}}, then the braizer at {{waypoint -136, -8, -102}}, then the braizer at {{waypoint 196, 103, -94}} to get the room with the Wall.
1. Fire the incubation vessel in the spouts of flame in Arlyxir's room 4 times.
   - Stand on one of the grates in the middle as it lights up, you will be damaged and tossed up into the air. Do this 4 times.
1. Collect Arlyxir's ashes within the newly created vessel at {{waypoint 10, 105, -81}}.
   - It is a veeeeeery long harvest.
1. Place the vessel at {{waypoint 133, 110, -81}}.
   - Use the brazier at {{waypoint 0, 103, -123}} and go up the flame ramp in the center of the room.
1. Chase flame sparks into the cauldron to superheat it.
1. Exit the zone.
