## Steps

1. Gather items from [[Najami Oasis]] around {{waypoint -43.69, 5.27, -120.49}}  ![Mirage Crystal](images/Mirage_crystal.png)
   - Gather 10 **Mirage Crystals** - along the shores of [[Kalickia River]]
   - Collect 6 **coconuts** from palm trees. Click the trees to "shake" them and possibly drop a coconut on the ground. Not a guaranteed drop for every tree. ![Coconut](images/Najami_Oasis_Coconut.png)
1. Return to [[Rahele]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- +3,500 faction with **Hizite Nomads of Ro**
