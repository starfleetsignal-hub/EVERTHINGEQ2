## Steps

1. Speak to [[Jorlak]] to be sent to the past (entire group).
1. Speak to the adventurers at {{waypoint -936, -11, 39.92}} and answer "Sure".
1. [[Fabled Allizewsaur|A Fabled Allizewsaur]] (94^^^) will attack. Kill him.
1. Speak to [[Jorlak]], he tells you you were sent to the wrong place/time and you will have to use a special item to be sent to the right one (next quest).

## Rewards

- +3,000 faction with **Claws of Veeshan**
- At Least 51g 35s 24c (at level 92)
