## Steps

1. Speak with [[Shae'Ahla]] near the entrance to Qeynos Harbor.
1. Return and cleanse the plant that attacked you (by the scribe's shop).
1. You will be attacked again!
1. Return to [[Vindain Greenfaith]].

## Rewards

1 silver, 27 copper
