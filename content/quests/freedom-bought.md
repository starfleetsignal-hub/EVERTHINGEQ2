## Steps

1. Free the captive Ayr'Dal
   - Zone into [[Beggar's Court: Sweat-Stained Cobbles]]. Hail one of the [[A captive Ayr'Dal|captive Ayr'Dal]] to spawn [[Kaseo Volcatius]] and speak to him.
1. Return to Thavian
1. Speak with [[Desmera Risentide]] in the basement of [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] {{waypoint -9, -34, -135}} - she gives you [[Mesmerizing Flute]]
1. Lead 4 unsuspecting Freeport citizens to Kaseo Volacatius - click any of the generic Freeport residents/denizens, use the flute, then speak to Kaseo
1. Free captured half-elves in Beggar's Court - zone into Beggar's Court: Sweat-Stained Cobbles and talk to any of the [[A captive Ayr'Dal|captive Ayr'Dal]]
1. Return to Thavian

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g @ lvl 95
