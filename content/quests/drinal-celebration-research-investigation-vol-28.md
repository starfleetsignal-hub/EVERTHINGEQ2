## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Drinal has asked me to assist in gathering spiritbound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Spiritbound Energy Reserves|spiritbound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect [[Spiritbound Energy Reserves|spiritbound energy]] by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to [[Herald of the Silver Reaper]] {{waypoint 85, -3, 291}} in [[Loping Plains]]
