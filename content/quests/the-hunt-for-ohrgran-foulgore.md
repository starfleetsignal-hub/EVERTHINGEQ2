- This quest is also part of [[The Commonlands Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Slay [[Ohrgran Foulgore]] found near the river in the southeastern corner of The Commonlands at {{waypoint -1217, -47, 609}}.
1. Return to Lieutenant Darrius.

- At least 2s 67c
- +75 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
- [[Ohgran's threadbare sash]]
