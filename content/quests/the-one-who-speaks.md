## Description

*The citizens of Maldura tell of a creature whose voice can be heard without uttering a word. Those who experienced it first hand are now insane, or better yet, dead. Such a powerful, evil being must never reach the gates of Maldura.*

## Steps

- Defeat targets within [[Kralet Penumbra: Rise to Power (Heroic)]]
1. Kill the listener.
   - [[Xexon, the Listener]]
1. Silence the one who speaks.
   - [[Xacx-Kahda]]
