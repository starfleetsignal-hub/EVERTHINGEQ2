## Steps

1. **Within 30 minutes**:
   - Find a container of lye inside buildings in [[Qeynos Capitol District]].
   - Use the lye to destroy 5 stacks of flour bags in [[Qeynos Province District]].
1. Return to [[Gol M'Tun]].

## Rewards

- Faction Only:
  - +3000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
