## Steps

1. Gather special sand from various undead:
   - Kill 5 [[A sun-baked skeleton|sun-baked skeleton]]s {{waypoint -300, -162, -1355}}
   - Kill 5 [[An Anaz Mal carcass (Sinking Sands)|Anaz Mal carcasses]] {{waypoint -164, -96, -585}}
   - Kill 5 [[An unliving trooper|unliving trooper]]s {{waypoint -617, -147, -1264}}
1. Return to [[Rassad Ali]].
