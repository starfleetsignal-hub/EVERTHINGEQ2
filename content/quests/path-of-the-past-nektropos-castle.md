## Steps

1. Defeat [[Maltus Everling]] in [[Nektropos Castle]]
1. Return to the Chronomagi Tasker

**Reaching Lord Everling**

*This walkthrough is for the quickest way to reach Lord Everling, shy of having someone who already fully unlocked the castle let you through all the doors. It does not include any side quests in Nektropos aside from three that you must do to get Library and balcony access. There are many other quests in **Nektropos Castle** that you can do if you are willing to dedicate several hours to this zone.*

1. Accept the quest Visions of Hatred when you enter the courtyard.
1. Go through the east courtyard door at **-11, -0.20, 0.11  **. Go south down the winding hallways until it veers west and then north. At the end of the hall **{{waypoint -14, -0.18, 41}}  **will be a boar's head, click on it to receive the quest The Boar's Head.
1. On the way back up the hallway, stop by the chapel at **-25, -1, 54  **. Kill the priestesses and the Inquisitor of Ulkorruuk, hail Alexa and listen to everything she says.
1. Go to the north end of the hallway and kill an Everling manservant in one of the northern chambers **{{waypoint -29, 0, -53}}  **. Examine the guard key to finish the Visions of Hatred quest. Now you'll have balcony door access.
1. Return to the courtyard and go through the western door at **11, -0.16, -0.34  **. Go south until you reach the parlor (called the "game room" on eq2map) at **32, 0, 61  **.
1. You're looking for a hastily scrawled note in here. It is either in the dart board **{{waypoint 32, -0.33, 48}}  **, chess table **{{waypoint 29, -0.07, 54}}  **, or one of the pool cue racks on the walls ( **21, 0.10, 60  **or **21, 0.02, 48  **). Only one person in the group will be able to pick this note up, but that's all that's needed. You now have access to the secret passages.
1. Leave and go north down the hallway to the Thexian Dining Room **{{waypoint 38, 0, -17}}  **. Complete The Boar's Head quest from step #2 by putting the eye in the socket at **30, -0.02, -34  **. You now have barracks access.
1. Head south down the hallway again to the Barracks **{{waypoint 41, 0, 21}}  **. Click on the chest inside the room at **17, 0, 33  **to get the quest The Red Marble.
1. Return to the boar head from step #2 **{{waypoint -14, -0.18, 41}}  **and complete The Red Marble quest by clicking the boar head and choosing to place the red marble in its socket. You now have library access.
1. Now you should go through the secret passage. Cross the courtyard back to the west door and take the hallway south past the parlor. Click the wall sconce at **23, 0, 68  **to open the door.
1. Head up the stairs into Elise's Bedroom. Take a moment to read the diary on the dresser here. There is another staircase up here, take it.
1. Go north or east **{{waypoint 7, 30, 82}}  **from the room and listen to Alexa. Accept the quest The Everling Lockets. **NOTE**: Once you kill one of the sisters you will receive an 18 hour lockout timer to the zone.
   - Elise Everling - She spawns at **0.78, -30, -26  **, on the bridge between the upper level. Read the diary on the dresser in her bedroom to get her to spawn.
   - Crysta Everling - Examine the bed in her room in the northwest corner at **58, 13, -84  **(the first time dolls of nyth will spawn, so do this twice). Choose to read the book and find out she's in Melanie's room. Go back up and head east.
   - Melanie Everling - Her and Crysta spawn together in her bedroom after reading Crysta's diary (see Crysta, above). Her room is in the northeast corner at **-49, 14, -77  **.
   - Sheila Everling - Click the dresser and try to break it open in the western bedroom at **70, 14, 8  **. She will then spawn across the room from it.
   - Jenni Everling- In the eastern bedroom at **-64, 13, 1  **. Spawns her teacher partway through the fight.
   - Deirdre Everling - Below the southeastern bedroom. **WARNING**: When you enter her bedroom, there is a very high chance of a level 40 epic x2 mob (Archfiend Izzoroth) spawning! He is leashed at his place on the bottom of the stairs; hurry to the stairwell and head down below the bedroom. Deirdre is down here at **-45, 0, 85  **.
1. Once all six sisters are defeated, Alexa will spawn and inform you that she unlocked all the doors in the castle area.
1. Head into the main castle area and enter the library at **-27, 13, 65  **. Cross through the western door to the balcony at **-12, 14, 55  **.
1. Follow the balcony to the northern door and pass through, turn east and go down the narrow hallway to the Caretaker's room. **{{waypoint -18, 14, -56}}  **After slaying the caretakers you will then have access to the Guard Captain's room.
1. Leave and go west down the hallway. Take another narrow passage to the Guard Captain's room **{{waypoint 18, 13, -57}}  **. Read the book at **14, 14, -42  **and Guard Captain Ga'vin will spawn; kill him to get an update.
1. Click the stand at **30, 14, -43  **to place the sword in the stand. This now acts as a "door knob" for the bookcase doors, which gives you basement access.
1. Head down into the basement and find Alexa at the end of a long hallway to the southeast **{{waypoint -42, -14, 42}}  **. Listen to her talk.
1. Go back up the hallway and to the west; click on a torch on the wall at **-4, -14, -23  **. Enter the research lab.
1. Examine the scroll on the desk at **-12, -14, -12  **. Now you can get to Ollix. (click the candles at **-14, -14, -13  **to leave the lab)
1. Click the hidden slab door at **-22, -14, -22  **. Go through and head to **-19, -14, 8  **to see Alexa again. Kill her and The Everling Lockets will complete.
1. Ollix Everling will spawn where Alexa did. Wait for him to talk then kill him as well. Now you're ready to face Lord Everling.
1. Head west and click the door at **-19, -14, -2  **. Click the slab ahead of that at **-2, -14, -3  **.
1. Click the candles at **11, -14, -11  **. Pass through the door that opens on the west wall. Walk through the wall across to the west at **27, -14, -5  **.
1. Go down the long hallway (watch out for mob pops), pass through the door at the end, slay The Juggernaught and enter the final chamber to face Maltus Everling.

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Chronomancers**
- Ability to purchase [[Replica: Maltus Everling]] from [[Timeless Trader]] for 1p 8g
