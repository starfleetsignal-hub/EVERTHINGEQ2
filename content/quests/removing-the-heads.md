## Description

Once a center of wild magical experimentation and untold power, Kor'sha has been hidden from the eyes of Norrath for ages. Creatures that once remained under the control of the mighty Sebilissian army of old now walk the halls, the strongest of which could prove deadly to any who dare to explore the halls and discover the secrets of Kor'sha. These mighty creatures must be disposed of, not only to clear the way for other explorers, but also to ensure that none of them ever escape from within the confines of the ancient laboratory.
