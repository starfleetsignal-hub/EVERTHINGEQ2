## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of Karana has asked for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Gather the requested supplies
   - Collect 3 [[Enchanted Eye|enchanted eyes]] found in [[Zimara Breadth]]
     - *Note: drops in [[Chests|small chest]] from a Yazdani enchantress*
     - *1-2 drops per group member*
   - Gather or purchase [[Pepitas Seeds|pepitas seeds]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas
     - *Common harvest from BoZ root*
     - *Can buy off broker*
     - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Stormlord]] {{waypoint -371, -1, -1091}} in the [[Enchanted Lands]]
