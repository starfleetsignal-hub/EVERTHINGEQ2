## Steps

1. Examine the idol at {{waypoint -668, 134, -156}}.
1. Examine the following idols around the island, each one has two spawn points:
   - Avit {{waypoint -637, 135, -182}} or {{waypoint -686, 146, -182}}
   - Aeristok {{waypoint -655, 136, -174}} or {{waypoint -657, 134, -142}}
   - Ampoel {{waypoint -733, 144, -145}} or {{waypoint -760, 133, -156}}
   - Soersin {{waypoint -732, 133, -187}} or {{waypoint -769, 132, -138}}
   - Romiak {{waypoint -775, 133, -45}} (on top of pillar) or {{waypoint -718, 176, -70}}
   - Luetef {{waypoint -722, 137, -180}} or {{waypoint -710, 147, -177}}
   - Nebues {{waypoint -689, 134, -203}} or {{waypoint -705, 155, -171}}
   - Foeg {{waypoint -646, 132, -123}} or {{waypoint -668, 146, -42}}
   - Tratos {{waypoint -777, 133, -45}} or {{waypoint -701, 168, -45}}
1. Return to the temple where the 'vault' is. Place an idol in between every gap in the columns.
   - Move your cursor around on the walls between the pillars to find 'loose stones'. Right click these to place the idols.
1. Click on the vault to complete the quest.
