## Steps

1. Kill [[Urtt]] (60^ solo), who wanders from the Mount Station Butcherblock to [[Camp Nybright]].
1. Return [[Urtt's head]] to [[Grund]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 4g 90s 14c
- +100 faction with **Nybright Family**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Growth Touched Cartaker's Armlet]] (All Priests)
  - [[Growth Touched Defender's Armlet]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Growth Touched Evoker's Armlet]] (All Mages)
  - [[Growth Touched Wayfarer's Armlet]] (All Scouts)
