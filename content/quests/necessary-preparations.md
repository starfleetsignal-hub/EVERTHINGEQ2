## Steps

1. Purchase [[Far Seas Trading Company Recipe: Insulating Cushion for Cool Climates]]  from [[Matthew]] for  4g 60s 80c.
1. Scribe the recipe and make [[Insulating Cushion]].
   - You will need 2 [[Bamboo shoot]], 1 [[Mottled pelt]], and 1 [[Ethereal filament]]
   - You can use the [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]] located near [[Matthew]] {{waypoint -1635, -533, -2388}}
1. Place cushion on [[Arjon]]'s bed near [[Misa]] {{waypoint -1380, -533, -2143}}.
1. Talk to [[Misa]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
