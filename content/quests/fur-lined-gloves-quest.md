## Steps

1. Kill gnolls in Antonica to collect 10 large patch of gnoll fur.
   - [[A Darkpaw rune caster|Darkpaw rune casters]] in [[Bramble Woods]] are plentiful, and located near the griffin tower you'll need to visit during Step 2
1. Look around the base of the Griffon Stations for bales of hay and examine them to receive *griffon down*.
1. Gather two Caltorsis tendon' from [[A Caltorsis knight|Caltorsis knights]] around [[The Ruins of Caltorsis]] at {{waypoint -1396, -16, -746}}.
