## Requirements

- [[Spiritual Claws of Khati Sha]] from [[A Chance For Redemption]]

## Steps

- Kill [[Statue of Rallos Zek (Challenge Mode Epic)|Statue of Rallos Zek]] in [[Temple of Rallos Zek: Foundations of Stone (Challenge)]].
- Draw the power from the fallen Statue of Rallos Zek.
- Use the power of the Claws of Khati Sha once again and summon Ralissk, the Keeper of Wisdom.
