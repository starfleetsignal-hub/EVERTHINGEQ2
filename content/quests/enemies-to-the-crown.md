## Steps

1. Hail 5 NPCs to turn them aggro, and kill them (in any order):
   - [[Klorn Twofingers]] near the docks {{waypoint 32, 3, 325}}
   - [[Zylock D'Ryil]] inside [[House D'Morte]] {{waypoint 175, 29, 199}}
   - [[Jareth D'Veers]] at [[Cuisine Excelsior]] {{waypoint -506, 24, 272}}
   - [[J'Xon T'Zandar]] at [[Nightsong Opera House]] {{waypoint -658, 29, -17}}
   - [[Akore K'Despara]] in [[Darklight Palace]] {{waypoint -600, 28, -151}}
1. Speak to Carvox M'Laeren

- Access to the next quest
