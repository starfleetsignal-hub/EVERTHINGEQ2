## Steps

You must have completed [[Below The Frozen Waves]] from [[Thirin Veliumdelver]] to begin this quest.

1. Travel to [[Demon's Delve]], {{waypoint 42.77, -100.80, 331.57}} and use the green ice on 6 of the burning braziers
1. Return to [[Hillfred Kinloch]]

## Rewards

- At least 2s 40c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Archon's Greaves]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Pants]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Leggings]] (Druids)
  - [[Elder's Leggings]] (Shamans)
  - [[Enlightened Leggings]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Greaves]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Shade's Leggings]] (All Scouts)
