## Steps

1. Speak with [[Researcher Aillena Belzia (Phantom Sea)|Researcher Aillena Belzia]] at {{waypoint 1350, 24, 1192}}
1. Fly north and follow the "path of lights" (basically use the speed-up tornadoes) till you reach the small island at {{waypoint 1158, 10, -1186}} and enter [[Ssrez'Ras: Ruins of Stasis]] by clicking on the ruins.
   - Explore for clues.
     - Click the first clue, a snake skin at {{waypoint -14, 64, 19}}.
     - Kill the [[A Ssrez'Ras broodkeeper|Ssrez'Ras broodkeeper]] in this room and the [[A Ssrez'Ras draft eel|Ssrez'Ras draft eel]] at {{waypoint 80, 64, 32}} to activate the elevator platform at {{waypoint 54, 65, 58}}
     - Click the portal activator by the platform to go down.
   - Explore for more clues.
     - Click the second clue, a broken statue at {{waypoint 13, -25, 7}}.
     - Kill the [[Empowered debris]] to activate the next portal.
     - Click the portal activator at {{waypoint -81, -40, 127}}. You must kill an arcane spren for the elevator in next step to work.
     - Go to the elevator platform {{waypoint -20, -150, 28}} and click its activator to go down to the last room.
   - Continue exploring Ssrez'Ras for clues.
     - Kill [[An arcanum funnel|an arcanum funnel]] in the last room to update this step.
     - You may leave using the **Exit to Phantom Sea** activator at {{waypoint 5, -224, -22}}.
   - Return to researcher [[Researcher Aillena Belzia (Phantom Sea)|Researcher Aillena Belzia]] in [[Phantom Sea]].
