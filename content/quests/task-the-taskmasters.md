## Steps

1. Complete the following, in no particular order:
   - Kill 10 [[A Dalnir's Wheel watchman|Dalnir's Wheel watchmen]]. {{waypoint 14, 123, 742}}
     - Each one killed is worth -100 with [[Dalnir's Wheel Taskmasters (Faction)]] for a total of -1000 faction.
   - Threaten [[Taskmaster Tsrazz]] {{waypoint 5, -125, 746}}
   - Threaten [[Taskmaster Rik]] {{waypoint 20, -122, 748}}
   - Threaten [[Taskmaster Scally]] {{waypoint -212, -99, 881}}
1. Return to Duz Heronimous.

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- +1,000 faction with **Synod Reet**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Crest of Targeted Pain]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Emblem of Targeted Pain]] (All Priests)
  - [[Ring of Targeted Pain]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Signet of Targeted Pain]] (All Mages)
