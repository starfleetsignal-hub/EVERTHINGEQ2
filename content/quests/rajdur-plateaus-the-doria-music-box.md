## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key by killing 15 [[A desert coyote|desert coyotes]] around {{waypoint 136.02, 3.69, 125.09}} at [[Sunscar Vale]] in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]].
1. Find the Doria music box key clue (chest drop) in from Mhyt-moos in [[Takish Badlands]] at [[Mhyt-moo Tidal Burrows]] around {{waypoint 57, 0, -89}}
1. Examine the clue to receive the quest [[The Doria Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the Doria Music Box in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]].
