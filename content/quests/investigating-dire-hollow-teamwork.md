## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- Grants access to the instance [[Dire Hollow: Infernal Echoes]]. You must get this quest each time you want to enter the group (Teamwork) version of the instance.
- Your alignment does not matter. You can receive both quests and even have both versions in your journal at the same time.

## Steps

- There are no steps involved. Entering the instance closes the quest.
- The entrance is in [[Nektulos Forest]], located downstream from [[Soul Eater Falls]] on the west side of the river. {{waypoint 500, 2, -1150}}

## Rewards

- The reward is the loot in the instance. See [[Dire Hollow: Grim Tidings]].
