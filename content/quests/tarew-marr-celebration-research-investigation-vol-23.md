## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Tarew Marr has asked me to assist in gathering waterbound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with collecting the waterbound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Waterbound Energy Reserves|waterbound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect [[Waterbound Energy Reserves|waterbound energy]] by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to [[Herald of the Fathom Lord]] {{waypoint -275.14, -0.13, 51.21}} in [[The Mystic Lake]]
