## Steps

1. Find [[Deagle Wendaloo]] in one of the holding cells in [[The Zoo]] within [[Klak'Anon]]. {{waypoint -320, 1, -56}}
   - The path to the cells is up the ramp towards Bloodscrew the Surgeon, and immediately on your right or left.
1. Retrieve Deagle's backpack holding his spare parts from one of the other holding cells. Which cell the backpack spawns in is random, and there can be other backpacks that spawn that do not update the quest.![Deagle's backpack, which spawns randomly in one of the cells.](images/Deaglesbackpack.jpg)
1. Return to Deagle and hear the rest of his harrowing tale.
1. Eliminate [[Bloodscrew the Surgeon]] in the nearby research lab {{waypoint -444, 3, -31}}.

## Notes on The Zoo

When you arrive, you'll find yourself in a large room that's over the top of the Zoo itself.  Go up the ramp toward the Research Lab and make a sharp right turn to travel down the passageway to the holding cells.  Be aware that certain spells, combat arts and positioning of your group can trigger aggro from the monsters above and below; it's possible to use an area-effect spell on a single monster and suddenly find yourself battling three heroic zookeepers and a handful of centaurs that teleported through the floor to fight you.  Be careful!
