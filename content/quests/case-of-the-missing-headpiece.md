**Note**: The pathing for Thaalia is messed-up. The Waypoint-filament will direct you to lower [[Nye'Caelona]], then when you get down there it will point straight upward again, back where you came from. Ignore it. [[Thaalia Faerel]] is against the wall in lower [[Nye'Caelona]], next to the [[Work Bench]] - roughly in the north-east wall of lower [[Nye'Caelona]].

## Steps

1. Hunt Obulus remnants for clues.
   - *Found around the [[Crypt of Dalnir: Baron's Workshop]] entrance {{waypoint 790, -129, -584}} only after 8PM Norrath time. Need to kill between 12 and 17 to get to the next step* .
1. Hunt Bloody Tooth goblins for clues.
   - *Need to kill about 15 or 16 to get to the next step. They are near the Crypt and on Twark*.
1. Receive an Aerdaerys case.
1. Return to [[Thaalia Faerel]].
1. Gather 8 Khalee'Sri crystal geodes from the Garden of Nye'Caelona {{waypoint -506, -105, -435}}.![Khalee'Sri Crystal Geode](images/KhaleeSri_Crystal_Geode.jpg)
   - *Kill 'a garden geode' to drop a very small crystal to pick up. The crystal has a red aura around it that makes it more visible. Does not drop a crystal off every geode. (approximately 50%)*![A Garden Geode](images/A_Garden_Geode.jpg)
1. Return to [[Thaalia Faerel]].
1. Listen for the name the Aerdaerys Case says.
1. Speak with [[Chancellor Thanial Vylardin]]  {{waypoint -112, 95, -326}}.

## Rewards

- [[Khalee'Sri Memories Bracelet]]
- Top Shard of an Obulus Medallion - needed for [[Kunark Ascending: Resurrection Machination]]
- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 15108 status
- 4000 faction with **Nye'Caelona**
