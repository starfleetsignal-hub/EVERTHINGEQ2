## Description

"The Holy Shrine of Karana, in the main hall of Stormhold (under the balcony), has tasked me with finding and collecting the tainted souls of those who have died here. I should look around and see if I can find them."  *--Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill 50 [[A life leech|life leeches]] or [[A small life leech|small life leeches]]. {{waypoint 45, 5, -100}}
1. Return to the shrine and place the souls on the altar.

## Rewards

One of the following:

- [[Necklace of Tainted Souls]]
- [[Ring of Tainted Souls]]
- [[Orb of Tainted Souls]]
