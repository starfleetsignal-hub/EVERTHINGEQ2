## Prerequisite

You must be level 80+ to receive this quest

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Grand Duke Lazarus Hazran]] on the second level of the Milita House {{waypoint 140.96, -7.05, 124.76}}
1. Find your way to the basement two levels down. Upon entering you will be attacked by 2-3 a Freeport Militia Guard (even con)
1. Speak with [[Savant Keza]], {{waypoint 172.42, -23.42, 136.47}}
1. Zone into [[A Bleak Dungeon]] at {{waypoint 132, -26, 141}}
1. Fight your way down and gather 5 arcane items (Scroll, vase, crystal)
1. Return to [[Savant Keza]] and ask her to follow you to [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] in North Freeport
1. Return to [[Foci Tamara Paust]] in [[The City of Freeport]]
