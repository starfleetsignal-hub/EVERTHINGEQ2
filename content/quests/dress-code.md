## Steps

1. Investigate the ***eldritch concentration*** {{waypoint -367.66, 96.30, -155.87}} in the [[Kandra Uplands]].![an eldritch concentration](images/An_eldritch_concentration.png)
1. Defeat [[Veliksi]] who spawns and attacks upon investigating the anomaly.
1. Speak with the defeated [[Veliksi]] {{waypoint -372.77, 94.74, -156.75}}.
1. Click on the *eldritch concentration* again to absorb its essence into Salindis' crystal.
1. Speak with [[Veliksi]] again.
