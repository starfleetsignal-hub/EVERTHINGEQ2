## Steps

1. Kill the following in no particular order: {{waypoint 416, -105, -1029}}
   - Kill 10 [[A Sandscrawler raider|Sandscrawler raiders]]
   - Kill 10 [[A Sandscrawler sorcerer|Sandscrawler sorcerers]].
1. Return to [[Disciple Shula]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Curate's Wristguard of the Battlepriest]] ([[Inquisitor]], [[Templar]])
  - [[Dragoon's Bracers of the Weaponmaster]] (Plate Fighters)
  - [[Naturewalker's Bands of the Forestspeaker]] ([[Fury]], [[Warden]])
  - [[Sensei's Wrist Wraps of the Fightmaster]] ([[Bruiser]], [[Monk]])
  - [[Spiritualist's Chain Cuffs of the Spiritcaller]] ([[Defiler]], [[Mystic]])
  - [[Thaumaturge's Cuffs of the Archcaster]] (All Mages)
  - [[Vagabond's Vambraces of the Bloodsinger]] (All Scouts)
