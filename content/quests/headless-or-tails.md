## Notes

One person present must have [[A lost necklace]] in order to make [[The Headless Horseman]] attackable.  The necklace will be consumed.  The necklace is available by completing the [[The Hedge Hollow]] quest [[A Nightmarish Return]]. The necklace is tradable and three should drop per run as of 2022.

## Steps

1. Slay [[The Headless Horseman]]
   - One person with [[A lost necklace]] needs to walk up to [[The Headless Horseman]] and click on them, which will make them attackable.  The necklace will be consumed.
   - While [[The Headless Horseman]] is a high level epic mob, any level character may participate.  Inside The Hollow Tree is a clickable skull which offers a buff for lower level characters, [[Power of The Hallow]].
   - More details and full strategy is available on [[The Headless Horseman]] page.
