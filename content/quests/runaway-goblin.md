## Steps

1. Interrogate [[Grix]], found running around a tree at {{waypoint -105, 1, -542}}.
1. Go to [[Runnyeye]] and kill [[Blikritz Bauble-eye]] near the dining room at {{waypoint 153, -16, -117}}.
1. Kill [[Runnyeye diviner|Runnyeye diviners]] for *evil eye blood*.
1. Boil the blood in the kitchen's furnace and smear it on the staff.
   - One of the furnaces right outside the *Dining Room* is now clickable and can be used {{waypoint 96, -16, -110}}.
1. Find a clickable piece of junk in the room at the north end of the hallway very near the scarecrow, at {{waypoint 85, -16, -232}}.

## Notes

Although the quest was changed with the [[Update:61|LU61]] reitemization to reward an armor piece rather than a weapon, the flavor text of the quest was not updated and still talks about finding a weapon.  The quest was later updated to read the weapon as a reward *in addition to* the armor piece.

Quest giver NPC summons up at 8:00pm ingame time.
