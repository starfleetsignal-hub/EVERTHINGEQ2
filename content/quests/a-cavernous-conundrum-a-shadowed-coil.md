I am unsure what this coil comes from, it is shadowed and yet almost seems as if it is still living. This broken off piece feels as though it could reform itself into another full creature if given time. I feel oddly compelled to find out more about it, while on the dark side of this moon.

## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A chasm shadow eater|chasm shadow eaters]] at [[Chasm Field Cliffs]] {{waypoint -275, 250, -200}} in [[Echo Caverns]].
