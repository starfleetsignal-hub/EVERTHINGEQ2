## Notes

- Quest has to be done within 5days

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A featherwing guardian]] or [[A featherwing strider]] in [[Western Wastes]] found around {{waypoint -246, 45, -705}}
   - *Encounter of 1 or 2+ mobs count as 1*
   - *[[A featherwing guardian]] that is in an encounter with [[A featherwing whelp]] don't count for the quest and they don't have the quest update feather*

- 25 [[Scarred Dinar]]
- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
