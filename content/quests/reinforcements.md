## Starting the Quest

This quest is received on completing any one of the quest series from [[The Forest Ruins]], [[Oakmyst Forest]], or [[The Peat Bog]].  Depending on which series you did, either [[Lieutenant Dawson]], [[Lieutenant Charlin]], or [[Acting Lieutenant Germain]] will offer you this quest.  You can only get this quest once - if you finish the Oakmyst Forest series and get the quest from Lieutenant Charlin, even if you later finish the Peat Bog series Lieutenant Dawson won't offer it to you.

## Steps

- Speak to [[Consul Bree]] in [[The Caves]]. {{waypoint 0, 0, -30}}

## Rewards

- +450 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
- +300 faction with **The Concordium**
- +150 faction with **The Tunarian Alliance**
