## Notes

When accepting quest you receive a [[Gloomglass Vial]]

## Steps

1. Harvest and hunt the following
   - Harvest 6 x obliviquill stain clouds via [[Gloomglass Vial]] - obliviquills floating above the water around {{waypoint -935, -53, 1056}}
     - *Kill an obliviquill to make 1-2 stain clouds spawn. Use the vial on each stain cloud that spawns.*
   - Hunt whipgauths for 12 gills {{waypoint -1011, -55, 1089}} and {{waypoint -1027, -54, 911}} or waters around this area.
1. Return to [[Kazoo]]

## Rewards

- [[Purple Inkubus]] house pet
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- One of the following:
  - [[Star Blood Helm of Folklore]]
  - [[Star Blood Barbute of Fortitude]]
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
