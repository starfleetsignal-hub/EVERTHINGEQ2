## Prerequisite

Requires Lv 38 Adventuring or Tradeskilling (Harvesting skill of 190 minimum no longer required, but recommended).

## Notes

- **Feerrott**
- Green Blood River Station is best place to farm; may have to clear everything to keep random node spawning beneficial.

## Steps

1. Go harvest (buying does not work)
   - In [[The Feerrott]], 20 of each
     - [[Severed teak]] from a Putrid Arbor
     - [[Pu-erh tea leaf]] from an Overgrown Bed of Greenslade
     - [[Strengthened leather pelt]] from an Animal Den
     - [[Rough bloodstone]] from a Hermetic Stone
     - [[Nerius trout]] from a Hatch of Fish
     - [[Browncap mushroom]] from an Overgrown Bed of Greenslade
     - [[Diamondine cluster]] from a Hermetic Stone
     - [[Wyrm meat]] from an Animal Den
     - [[Owlbear meat]] from an Animal Den
     - [[Cauldron blowfish]] from a Hatch of Fish
   - And the same in [[Rivervale]], 20 of each
     - [[Severed teak]] from a Corrupted Arbor
     - [[Pu-erh tea leaf]] from a Tainted Natural Garden
     - [[Strengthened leather pelt]] from an Animal Den
     - [[Rough bloodstone]] from a Lithic Stone
     - [[Nerius trout]] from a Bedlam of Fish
     - [[Browncap mushroom]] from a Tainted Natural Garden
     - [[Diamondine cluster]] from a Lithic Stone
     - [[Wyrm meat]] from an Animal Den
     - [[Owlbear meat]] from an Animal Den
     - [[Cauldron blowfish]] from a Bedlam of Fish
1. Return to Qho Augren.

## Rewards

- At least 24g 85s 29c
- Tradeskill experience
