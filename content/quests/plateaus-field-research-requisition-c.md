1. Harvest materials:
   - Harvest 15 [[Pallasite Ore]]
   - Harvest 18 [[Desert Willow Wood]]
   - Harvest 21 [[Dustmaw Hide]]
   - Fuel 15 [[Astral Coal]]
1. Craft 3 [[Amazing Amphorae]] on a [[Work Bench]].
1. Speak to [[Bairam]].
