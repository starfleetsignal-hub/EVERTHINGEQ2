## Steps

![A teleporter inhibitor.](images/Teleporter_inhibitor.png)

1. Find the devices that are blocking Shafi's ability to use his teleporter and shut them down.
   - Deactivate the first teleporter inhibitor. {{waypoint -277, 532, -387}}
   - Deactivate the second teleporter inhibitor. {{waypoint -207, 532, -126}}
1. Return to [[Shafi Af'Mon]].
   - *Note: Now you can use the teleport next to Shafi.*
1. Return to [[Laila]] with [[Shafi Af'Mon's Status Report]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- +2,000 faction with **Scions of the Deepwater Knights**
- One of the following:
  - [[Cloak of the Erudian Pathfinder]] (scout choice)
  - [[Cloak of the Erudian Vanguard]] (scout choice)
  - [[Cloak of the Erudian Cleric]] (caster choice)
  - [[Cloak of the Erudian Magician]] (caster choice)
  - [[Cloak of the Erudian High Guard]]
- After completing this quest you gain the ability to use the teleporters at [[Laila]] and [[Shafi Af'Mon]] to teleport between [[The Deepwater Pavilion]] and the [[Erudin Gardens]].
