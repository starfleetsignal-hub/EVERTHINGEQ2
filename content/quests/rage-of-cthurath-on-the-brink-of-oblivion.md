## Steps

1. Examine the "hijacked portal" you came through {{waypoint 564, 94, -698}}
1. Speak with various Darou in the village.
   - Speak with [[Tiadra]] at {{waypoint 559, 92, -668}}
   - Speak with [[Jarkun]] at {{waypoint 548, 94, -696}}
   - Speak with [[Hannan]] at {{waypoint 538, 94, -688}}
1. Examine the Cimmerott Runic Ward at {{waypoint 405, 80, -598}}
1. Kill the attacking ravaging warpwolf
1. Kill 8 more ravaging warpwolves around [[The Gnarls]] {{waypoint 311, 73, -580}}, just outside the village.
1. Return to [[Tiadra]]
