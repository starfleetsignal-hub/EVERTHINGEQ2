## Steps

1. Kill 25 Rilissians for their belts.
   - Auto-updated by killing Rilissian Advancers {{waypoint 20, -34, 1384}}, however bandits do not appear to update.
   - Exiled Rilissians {{waypoint -71, -64, 208}} will provide updates with no Rilissian faction penalty.
1. Return to Barkrich Oakleaf.
