This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Enter [[A Maj'Dul Residence]].
1. Search one of the blue baskets to the left of entrance for a poisonous animal (cobra, scorpion or spider).
1. Place the animal on the bed next to [[A Maj'Dul resident]] so that it will attack and kill the resident.
1. Kill whatever spawns.
1. Return to any Tears grifter for your reward.

## Reward

- At least 3g 23s 29c
- One of the following:
  - 4 [[Blades insignia token|Blades insignia tokens]]
  - 4 [[Coin insignia token|Coin insignia tokens]]
  - 4 [[Truth insignia token|Truth insignia tokens]]
