## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of Prexus has asked for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Gather the requested supplies
   - Collect 3 [[Charger Horns|charger horns]] from creatures found in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]]
     - *Note: drops in [[Chests|small chest]] from a horned charger, near Splendor Wellspring*
     - *1-2 drops per group member*
   - Gather or purchase [[Bittersweet Vineroot|bittersweet vineroot]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas
     - *Common harvest from BoZ dens*
     - *Can buy off broker*
     - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Oceanlord]] {{waypoint -1765, -402, -2728}} in [[Fens of Nathsar]]
