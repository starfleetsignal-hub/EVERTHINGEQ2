## Steps

1. Fly into the fount at {{waypoint 34, 312, -291}} to receive [[Fount of Power]]. The ability will be added to your knowledge book on the Spells tab.
1. Travel to {{waypoint -785, 345, 1116}} and use the "The Coliseum of Valor" to zone into [[Coliseum of Valor: Hero's Devotion]] (Solo, Molo, Duo).
   - Slay the Vaedenmoor invaders.
     - *Note: kill all the mobs in there, once all are dead the quest will update*
1. Speak to [[Mithaniel Marr]] in the center.
1. Click on the sword that spawns when [[Mithaniel Marr]] leaves to enter the [[Coliseum of Valor]].
1. Speak to [[Druzzil Ro]] at {{waypoint -4, 6, 4}}.

## Rewards

- [[Valor Ascended]]
- At least 1p 41g 75s 37259 status
- [[Marr's Permission]] - *Gives every char on the account access to [[Coliseum of Valor]]*
