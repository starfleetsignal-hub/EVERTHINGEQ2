## Steps

1. Slay 15 [[A hungry gorg|hungry gorgs]] in [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]]. This is not the writ to do unless you have quests in SoS as well. There are 8 gorgs and the repops you need to get 15 take a (very) long time.

- +500 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
- 8657 status
- 1 [[City Token]]
