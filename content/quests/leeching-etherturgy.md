## Steps

![Graymantle](images/Graymantle.jpg)

1. Collect 6 leeched graymantle from [[Nuthshae]] and [[Blightscale]].
   - The graymantle is moved like a house item.
   - On [[Blightscale]], place the graymantle in a crack in the ground near {{waypoint 700, 368, 101}}.
   - Click the graymantle again after placing for the update. The respawn timer is well over 30 minutes. If you do as a group, try all clicking at once.
   - Note: multiple graymantles available around island.
   - Caution: if you misplace the graymantle, they disappear. You must be ![Leeched Graymantle placed inside crack, smoking, ready for pickup.](images/Leeched_Greymantle.jpg) close to the center of the blue wave that comes out of the crack.
   - Note:  can be done entirely Stealthed.
1. Return to Soliddar

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Blessed Wurm Scale Belt]]
