*This quest is the former access quest for [[The Firemyst Gully]], however its completion is no longer required to enter the instnace.

## Steps

1. Speak with three knights:
   - [[Knight Grel Stoneshearer]] behind [[Northern Qeynos Tower]] at {{waypoint -403, 15, -304}}
   - [[Knight Grelda Stoneshearer]] outside [[The Keep of the Gnollslayers]] at {{waypoint -2184, -46, 446}}
   - [[Knight Morte Winghammer]] by the southern covered bridge at {{waypoint -1736, -14, 351}}
1. Return to Ignar Steadirt.
1. Kill [[A Sabertooth rune caster]] {{waypoint -1462, -6, 451}}
1. Return to Ignar Steadirt.
1. Kill [[A Darkpaw rune caster]].{{waypoint -1560, -1, 564}}
1. Return to Ignar Steadirt.
1. Speak to [[Centurion Grimbold Steadirt]], the dwarf outside the door of [[The Firemyst Gully]].

## Rewards

- At least 4s 21c
