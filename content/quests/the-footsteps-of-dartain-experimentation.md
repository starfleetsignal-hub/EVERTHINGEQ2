## Steps

![Spirit to spawn mob for Step 5](images/Experimentation_Slobberjaw_Spirit.jpg)

1. Go to Moonfield Hamlet in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]] and see Feraa Yaldeh {{waypoint 158, 411, 1241}}
1. Collect 10 ritual stones from Slobberjaw kobolds, these are uncommon updates.
1. Return to [[Feraa Yaldeh]].
1. See a kobold named [[Eglak]] near the Intercontinental Temporal Station {{waypoint -431, 374, 1346}}
1. Use the [[Enchanted Slobberjaw Eye]] given to you by Eglak to see spirits, which are little glowing balls of light on the ground.
   - Click the spirits and a slobberjaw entity will spawn. Kill it.  You will need to do this six times.
1. Return to Eglak.
1. Fly back to [[Moonfield Hamlet (POI)|Moonfield Hamlet]] and talk to Feraa Yaldeh.
1. Activate your Slobberjaw Eye and go to {{waypoint 250, 368, 1032}} and talk to a displaced erudite spirit.
1. Return to Feraa Yaldeh.
