## Steps

![](images/To_Oogothl_Sprawl-_Ver'bohten_Atoll.png)

1. Enter [[Oogothl Sprawl: Ver'Bohten Atoll (Solo)|Oogothl Sprawl: Ver'Bohten Atoll [Solo]]] by using the spindle teleporter at {{waypoint -320, 13, 472}}.
   - *Note: Do not use the [[Curious Mechanimagica]].*
   - For the walkthrough of the instance see [[Oogothl Sprawl: Ver'Bohten Atoll (Solo)|Oogothl Sprawl: Ver'Bohten Atoll [Solo]]]
1. Defeat and Destroy the following
   - Defeat 12x mechanicaneyez gazers
   - Destroy 9x mechanimagica defenses
     - *Note: these are trackable - Terminus Tech Electro-defender.
     - Some of them are up top so you have to kill [[Glorfab Flit]] before you can use the teleporter going up. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -809, 156, 293}}, {{waypoint -680, 165, 224}}, {{waypoint -724, 180, 151}}
     - {{waypoint -882, 200, 276}}, {{waypoint -721, 196, 214}}, {{waypoint -889, 196, 214}}
     - {{waypoint -776, 270, 102}}, {{waypoint -852, 270, 108}}, {{waypoint -824, 269, 135}}
1. Continue to ascend Ver'Bohten Atoll
   1. Use the "Teleport to [[Chancellor's Court]]" at {{waypoint -728.52, 179.84, 173.67}}.
   1. Use the "Teleport to [[Ver'Bohten Rise]]" at {{waypoint -869.07, 197.16, 110.18}}.
   1. Jump down from the platform at {{waypoint -785.95, 270.01, 193.17}}.
   - Parachute or safe fall down to lower levels to track down missed items, then retrace steps back up.
1. Continue to investigate the situation on Ver'Bohten Atoll
   - Use the "Teleport to [[Baron's Pinnacle]]" at {{waypoint -821.30, 251.31, 265.10}}.
1. Kill [[Ziggin]] at {{waypoint -829.92, 293.61, 259.47}}
   - As you approach the last named [[Ziggin]] will trap you, and monologue interminably. Once he finishs speaking and kill him.
1. Return to [[Xhol'Rog]] in [[Oogothl Sprawl]]

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following:
  - [[Rageful Sword of the Umbrith]]
  - [[Rageful Great Blade of the Umbrith]]
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
