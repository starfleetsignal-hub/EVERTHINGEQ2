## Steps

1. Collect the Neriak cell report in [[Neriak]] at {{waypoint -109, 22, -12}} in [[Indigo Hollow]] behind [[Ulsurus Outfitters]]. Kill the [[Ebon Mask attacker]] that spawns.
   - **Note:** Good aligned might want to use a Guild Rally banner here, or have an evil call them, as [[Neriak]] guards see invis below level 90.
   - The stationary guards will not see invisibility.
   - at L125, and using Stealth, the L130 Epic x4 ^^^ guards can not see through Stealth.
1. Collect the Gorowyn cell report in [[Gorowyn]] at {{waypoint 2594, 87, 1333}} within the [[Timorous Deep]]. Kill the [[Ebon Mask attacker]] that spawns.
1. Collect the Qeynos cell report in [[North Qeynos]] at {{waypoint 480, -21, -378}} behind [[The Ironforge Estate]] in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. Kill the [[Ebon Mask attacker]] that spawns.
1. Collect the Freeport cell report in [[South Freeport]] at {{waypoint -186, -30, 279}} behind the 10 Freedom Road house.  Kill the [[Ebon Mask attacker]] that spawns.
1. Return to the [[Halls of the Unseen]] and bring these reports to Nyran at {{waypoint 25, 3, 49}}

## Rewards

- at least 1p
- +10,000 faction with **The Circle of the Unseen Hand**
