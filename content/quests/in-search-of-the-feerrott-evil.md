## Notes

- This quest is no longer required for access to [[The Feerrott]], but remains in game as a lore quest.

## Steps

1. Go to [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] and kill the [[An orc cartographer|orc cartographer]] {{waypoint 210.46, 12, -75.12}} and his bodyguards pathing around outside near the [[Sullon Mines]].
1. Hail the Orc Cartographer who is now an NPC.
1. Kill three groups of [[A Tallon catapult operator|Tallon catapult operators]] outside [[Warship Dock]] in Zek. {{waypoint 434, -25, 139}}
1. Find and kill [[General Lutzgorg]] in Zek. {{waypoint -397.19, -28, -294.18}}
1. Speak to [[Slaughtergrott the Vigilant (Thundering Steppes)|Slaughtergrott the Vigilant]] on the docks in [[The Thundering Steppes]]. {{waypoint -572, -21, -398}}
   - You may have to zone out and back in again in order for the NPC to spawn. You can zone out and back again easily by using one of the bells on the dock.
1. Use the [[Globe of Swift Travel]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]] or [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] to access the instance [[The LMS Intruder]] and progress to the next quest.
