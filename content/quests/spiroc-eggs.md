## Steps

1. Use the [[Throm Egg Snatcher]] to collect 8 [[Haoaeran Egg|Haoaeran Eggs]] from [[Haoaera]]. The eggs can be found in the branches of the larger trees in Haoaera. {{waypoint 1767, 56, 380}}
1. Return to [[Groag Froam]].

- At least 21s 86c
- +5000 faction with **Mountain Throms of Mok Island**
- 5 [[Hearty Mountain Thromelette (Food)|Hearty Mountain Thromelettes]]
- [[Hearty Mountain Thromelette (Recipe)]]
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Elder's Shoulderpads]] (Shamans)
  - [[Shade's Shoulderpads]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Channeler's Shoulderpads]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Shoulderpads]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Enlightened Shoulderpads]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Archon's Pauldrons]] (Clerics)
  - [[Knight's Pauldrons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
