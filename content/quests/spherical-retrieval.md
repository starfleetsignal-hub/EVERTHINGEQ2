## Steps

1. Zone into Crystalline Breaks.
1. Find the subjugation ceremony location at {{waypoint 163, -15, 49}}
1. Gather 6 Subjugation Spheres. (Blue balls surrounded by white whirling ribbons)
1. Deposit the Spheres in the Chest next to the entrance at {{waypoint 20, -34, 93}}
1. Speak with [[Rongot Wyrmrippa]] via [[Rongot's Whisper Stone]]  You must be within [[Crystalline Breaks]]  ![Deposit the spheres in this chest](images/Spherical_Retrieval_quest_-_containment_chest.jpg)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +4,550 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
- [[Ry'gorr Bench]]
