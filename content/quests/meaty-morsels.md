## Steps

1. You should fill this sack with chunks of yeti meat (20) from the snow beasts.
1. You need to fill the sack with (9) wolf meat from protective pack hunters (semi common update)
1. You should return the meat to the Frostfoots for distribution - hail Baelin Frostfoot at {{waypoint -89, 1, -81}}

## Rewards

- At least 8p
- 19562 status @ level 90
- One of the Following:
  - [[Flowing Ice Cloak]]
  - [[Earhoop of Ice Shards]]
  - [[Sash of Destruction]]
  - [[Bangle of the Frozen Flow]]
