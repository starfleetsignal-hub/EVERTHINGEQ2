Required: [[Druid]] level 25

## Steps

1. Return the leaf to the [[Teleportation and Portals Trainers|portals trainer]] near the priest guild in your home town or by the Darklight Wood rings.
1. Take the ***Blessing of Growth*** the trainer gives you back to the druid ring in [[Antonica]]
   - Note:  You can either take a portal that the trainer opens up for you or you can travel back to the ring yourself.
1. Use the *Blessing of Growth* once inside the ring
1. Return to the [[Teleportation and Portals Trainers|portals trainer]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Circle of Antonica]]
