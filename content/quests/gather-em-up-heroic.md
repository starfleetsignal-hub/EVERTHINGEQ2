Goblins are gathering within Runnyeye, led by some of the most unruly of their kin. I must rid the lands of this filth.

## Steps

*I must complete my mission in [[Fabled Runnyeye: The Gathering (Heroic)]].*

- Oust the Outraged
- Waylay the Wind King
- Bring down the Boar Master
- Defeat Da Gas Passer
- Wreck the Rowdy
