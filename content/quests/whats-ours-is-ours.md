To obtain this quest, you must be an Evil-aligned Kerran of at least level 20.

- I got it at level 10 on TLE server 2/16
- My Kerra got the Quest on TLE server with level 5 6/17

## Steps

![a stolen totem](images/What's_Ours_is_Ours_stolen_totem.png)

1. Enter [[Stonestair Byway: The Animus Corridors]] and speak to [[Hisska Rakepaw]] {{waypoint -5.94, -17.88, -326.73}}
1. Collect 6 stolen totems.  They resemble staves with crystals on top.  They can be found around the battle and the erudite-controlled section of Stonestair Byway, but their specific /loc seems to be randomized.  Don't forget to check inside buildings for them.  I found the last 2 inside erudite "houses".
1. Speak with [[Lieutenant Vesder]] in North Freeport just outside Stonestair Byway {{waypoint -12, -18, -324}} and show him the stolen totems.
1. Return to Caltuur

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g @ lvl 95
