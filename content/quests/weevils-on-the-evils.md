## Description

"Brodo Gillywump, a halfling at Green Gnoll, has provided me with a bag of termites, known as wood weevils. I am to release them upon the Crushbone siege weapons under the city of Kelethin. They will be eaten by the hungry bugs in no time!" -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Double-click on catapults under the city to destroy them. Two of them  Around {{waypoint 281.94, 65.17, 269.04}} and 1 at {{waypoint 603.29, -0.88, 285.06}}![](images/Orccapapult.jpg)
1. Return to [[Brodo Gillywump]]

## Rewards

- At least 76c
- [[Small fae crate]]
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Fighter's Bauble (with Turquoise)|Fighter's Bauble]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Bauble (with Turquoise)|Priest's Bauble]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Bauble (with Turquoise)|Scout's Bauble]]  (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Bauble (with Turquoise)|Sorcerer's Bauble]] (All Mages)

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Brodo Gillywump: "Did you destroy the diseased pebbleberry bushes, [Player Character]?"
- Player Character: "Yes, as well as the Crushbone corruptors that were tainting the shrubs."
- Brodo Gillywump: "Great work, [Player Character]! Great, great work! I knew you wouldn't disappoint. Wait... Did you say the orcs are behind this sickness?!"
- Player Character: "Yes. They attacked me while I was destroying the diseased bushes."
- Brodo Gillywump: "I knew that they were causing the city of Kelethin no short amount of trouble, as of late, but I didn't think that they would stoop so low as to spread disease throughout the Faydark! Well, that does it."
- Player Character: "Does what?"
- Brodo Gillywump: "Gets my blood pumping is what! Oh and I've got an idea. If they are so willing to corrupt the fauna of the Faydark, then it seems only fitting that the fauna be allowed a chance to fight back."
- Player Character: "How are we going to do that?"
- Brodo Gillywump: "I've got a bag of termites, known as wood weevils. Let them loose upon the Crushbone siege weapons that are below Kelethin. They will feed on the weapons, turning them to dust in no time!"
- Player Character: "This sounds fun!"

I should release the wood weevils upon the Crushbone siege weapons under the city of Kelethin to destroy them. Destroy the Crushbone siege weapons.

I have destroyed the Crushbone siege weaponry threatening Kelethin. I should return to Brodo Gillywump, back at Green Knoll. Speak with Brodo.

- Brodo Gillywump: "The wood weevils worked like a charm, didn't they, [Player Character]?"
- Player Character: "Yes, they did!"
- Brodo Gillywump: "I am glad to hear it! Oh, how I would love to see their faces when you let loose those weevils upon their equipment. It tickles me somethin' good to think of it!"
- Player Character: "It was rather fun."
- Brodo Gillywump: "I cannot thank you enough for all of the help you've provided in defending Kelethin and the Faydark from the orcs. We are all in your debt, [Player Character]. Please take this as a small token of our gratitude."
- Player Character: "Thank you, Brodo."

I spoke with Brodo Gillywump. I spoke with Brodo Gillywump and told him of my success.

The wood weevils made short work of the Crushbone siege weapons under Kelethin. Brodo Gillywump was delighted to hear of my success and praised me for all of the help that I have provided in defending Kelethin and the Faydark from the orcs.
