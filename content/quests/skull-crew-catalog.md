## Notes

Upon accepting this quest, you receive the ability [[Catalogue Creature]] if you did not have it already.<br>
For the purpose of this quest killing the mobs, if you can do it, also gives an update.

## Steps

1. Catalog specific skeletons wandering Norrath and terrorizing the living:
   - and orc skeleton - [[The Commonlands]] above dock
   - a Teir'Dal skeleton - [[Darklight Wood]], [[Nektulos Forest]] near D'Morte Burial Chamber around {{waypoint 437, 0, -715}}
   - a human skeleton - calcified skeletons spawning for event in [[Antonica]], [[The Commonlands]], [[Nektulos Forest]]
   - a gnoll skeleton - [[Phantom Sea]] around {{waypoint 572, 351, -120}}, [[Loping Plains]] within Arkelek's Gash at {{waypoint -66, 38, 223}}, Upper Splitpaw
   - an iksar skeleton - [[Timorous Deep]], [[Kylong Plains]] around ruins near the dock, [[Fens of Nathsar]] around [[Ruins of Cabilis]] {{waypoint -173, 35, -1014}}
   - a droag skeleton - [[The Bonemire]] around {{waypoint 385, 199, -524}}
   - a Desert of Ro skeleton - [[The Sinking Sands]] around {{waypoint -1034, -101, -834}}
   - a dwarf skeleton - calcified skeletons spawning for event in [[Antonica]], [[The Commonlands]], [[Nektulos Forest]]
   - a gnome skeleton - calcified skeletons spawning for event in [[Antonica]], [[The Commonlands]], [[Nektulos Forest]]
1. Return to [[Evie Cartahand]] in [[The Sinking Sands]]

## Rewards

One of the following:

- [[Skeletal Chimera Plushie]]
