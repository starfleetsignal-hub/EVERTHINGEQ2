## Steps

Nothing is free and building, maintaining, and supporting the herald's camp is expensive. That's why the herald of Tunare has requested that we assist in offsetting the camp's cost by retrieving a valuable relic guarded by Etosh the Electrifying. I need to retrieve the relic.

1. Retrieve the relic guarded by Etosh the Electrifying in [[Aether Wroughtlands: Chaotic Currents (Solo)]]
   - The relic is at {{waypoint -771, 279, 680}} behind Etosh, on the platform where he spawns
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Mother of All]] {{waypoint 154, -23, 3}} in [[The Lesser Faydark|Lesser Faydark]]
