## Notes

- It will take several runs through the instances to finish the quest.

## Steps

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 727.46, 77.35, 696.05}} to enter [[The Ruins of Ssraeshza (Solo)|The Ruins of Ssraeshza [Solo]]] or [[The Vault of Ssraeshza (Solo)|The Vault of Ssraeshza [Solo]]].
1. Defeat shissar for 8 shissar holo remnants.  *Note: Not a guaranteed update.*
1. Examine the holoprojector and 'Place eight shissar holo remnants into the projector'.
1. Watch the hologram.
1. Defeat shissar for 8 shissar holo remnants.  *Note: Not a guaranteed update.*
1. Examine the holoprojector and 'Place eight shissar holo remnants into the projector'.
1. Watch the hologram.
1. Defeat shissar for 8 shissar holo remnants.  *Note: Not a guaranteed update.*
1. Examine the holoprojector and 'Place eight shissar holo remnants into the projector'.
1. Watch the hologram.
1. Defeat shissar for 8 shissar holo remnants for a fourth and final time.
1. Examine the holoprojector and 'Place eight shissar holo remnants into the projector'.
1. Watch the hologram.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Blade of Coiled Death]]
- [[Shissar Skeleton Plushie]]
