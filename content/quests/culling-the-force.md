## Steps

1. Talk to [[Sonjaz Manx|Sonjaz Manx (NPC)]]. {{waypoint 593, 66, -452}}
1. Kill her when she turns into [[Sonjaz Manx (Mob)|Sonjaz Manx (Named)]].
1. Return to Kriinz Nax.

- At least 1p
- +3750 faction with **The City of Jinisk**
- [[Glowing Gem of Searing Prowess]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Forestpriest's Girdle]] (All Priests)
  - [[Gloomstalker's Belt]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Kunzar Warrior's Strap]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Swampmage's Sash]] (All Mages)
