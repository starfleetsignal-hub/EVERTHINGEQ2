## Notes

- This quest scales to the adventure level tier of the character, i.e. level 21-30 characters will receive the level 25 version of the quest.
- This quest is repeatable up to 10 times. per adventure level tier.
- You may need to kill more than the listed number of monsters in order to retrieve the required drops for this quest.
- Players adventure level 121+ will need to mentor down to a lower level range in order to get these quests offered.

## Steps

1. Slay the following monsters (for your adventure level tier) until you retrieve 5 of the indicated quest item:
   - **Tier 1 (Level 1-10)**
     - Zombies in [[The Graveyard]]
     - [[A rust monster|Rust monster]] in [[Sunken City]]
   - **Tier 2 (Level 11-20)**
     - [[A rock lioness|Rock lionesses]] in [[The Commonlands]]
     - [[A moon wisp|Moon wisps]] in [[The Commonlands]]
   - **Tier 3 (Level 21-30)**
     - [[A mist grinnin|Mist grinnins]] in [[Nektulos Forest]]
     - [[A dusk leaper|Dusk leapers]] in [[Nektulos Forest]]
   - **Tier 4 (Level 31-40)**
     - [[A Vallon miner|Vallon miners]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]]
     - [[A Blackhook raider|Blackhook raiders]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]]
   - **Tier 5 (Level 41-50)**
     - [[A living magma|Living magmas]] in [[Lavastorm]]
     - [[A Sootfoot harvester|Sootfoot harvesters]] in [[Lavastorm]]
   - **Tier 6 (Level 51-60)**
     - [[The Lesser Faydark]] (solo)
       - [[A grovespirit acolyte|Grovespirit acolytes]], [[A grovespirit caller|callers]] and [[A grovespirit defender|defenders]]
       - [[A honeybrood worker|honeybrood workers]]
     - [[The Sinking Sands]] (heroic)
       - [[An Anaz Mal veilguard|Anaz Mal veilguards]] in [[The Living Tombs]]
       - Mummies, such as [[A Sulite tjat (Silent City)|Sulite tjat]] in [[The Silent City]]
   - **Tier 7 (Level 61-70)**
     - [[A living chest|Living chests]] in [[Kaladim]]
     - [[An obsidian construct|Obsidian constructs]] in [[Mistmoore Catacombs]]
   - **Tier 8 (Level 71-80)**
     - Option 1:
       - [[A trained war sokokar|trained war sokokars]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
       - [[A kunzar tiger|Kunzar tigers]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
     - Option 2:
       - Leeches in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
       - Sporconids in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
   - **Tier 9 (Level 81-90)**
     - [[A malignant seedling|Malignant seedlings]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]
     - [[A highland fearstalker|Highland fearstalkers]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]]
   - **Tier 10 (Level 91-100)**
     - [[An insidious fearlings|Insidious fearlings]] in [[Eidolon Jungle]]
     - [[A churning obol|Churning obols]] in [[Obol Plains]]
   - **Tier 11 (Level 100-110)**
     - [[Savalge plumwits|Savage plumwits]] in [[Plane of Magic]]
     - [[A stoneripper|Stonerippers]] in [[Plane of Magic]]
   - **Tier 12 (Level 111-120)**
     - TBD
     - TBD
   - **Tier 13 (Level 121-130)**
     - [[Shoal manglers|Shoal Manglers]] in [[Svarni Expanse]]
     - [[Bullhorn rhinos|Bullhorn Rhinos]] in [[Svarni Expanse]]
1. Return to [[Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber (South Freeport)|Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber]] in [[The City of Freeport]].
