## Steps

1. Investigate the othmir storage cave at {{waypoint -1194, -538, 3065}} and kill the 3 [[A Yah-lei infiltrator|Yah-lei infiltrators]]
1. Speak to [[Kapu]]
1. Recover the stolen supplies (8). Kill Yah-leis in the water or on the beach
1. Return to [[Kapu]]
1. Place 5 barricades at given locations.  These are green glowies on the beach
   - {{waypoint -1223, -542, 3073}}
   - {{waypoint -1262, -541, 3161}}
   - {{waypoint -1269, -541, 3258}}
   - {{waypoint -1252, -540, 3291}}
   - {{waypoint -1234, -542, 3377}}
1. Return to [[Kapu]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +300 faction with **Othmir of Velious**
- [[Othmir Prophet's Chair]]
