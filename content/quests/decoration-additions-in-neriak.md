## Notes

After completing this quest, if you'd like more trees, you can buy more tickets from questgiver for 1p each. Click the option to register a complaint when talking to him.

## Steps

1. Decorate areas of Neriak by clicking on green shinies in certain areas:
   - Inside the [[Spires of Innoruuk]] {{waypoint -292, 29, 200}}
   - At [[K'Nex Falls]] {{waypoint -443, 27, 202}}
   - At the gate of [[Death Grotto]] {{waypoint -577, 28, 14}}
1. In front of the inn in the [[New Foreign Quarter]] {{waypoint 110, 22, 149}}
   - Defeat [[Mr. McScroogle]].
1. Return to [[Gevil Gigglegibber]].

## Rewards

- [[Special Frostfell Tree Ticket]]
  - To use the ticket double click the tree to the left with the Shade on top.
- If you talk to Gutten Gigglegibber again he will tell you that you can decorate the tree you just received by talking to [[Mr. McScroogle]] (see [[A Frostfell Favor]]). If you already did that quest, he will know that and tell you so.
