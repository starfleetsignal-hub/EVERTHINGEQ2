## Steps

1. Kill 20 zombies. These can be found in the former marketplace area.
1. Kill [[A construct of malice|constructs of malice]] until you get an update. These are a little ways down the tunnel past [[The Feasting Cave]] at {{waypoint -174, -17, -250}}.
1. Find [[The Bull's Pit]] at {{waypoint -30.99, -24.68, -215.98}}. When you cross the threshold into the room the quest will auto update.
