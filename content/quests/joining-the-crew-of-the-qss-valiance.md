## Steps

1. Speak with [[Admiral Marc Grenich]] on the Qeynos Harbor docks and tell him you're willing to take up the cause to defend Qeynos.
1. Ring the mariner's bell on the docks that would normally take you outside Qeynos and choose to enter the [[Kunark Expedition]]. You will arrive aboard the QSS [[Valiance]].![The iksar ship draws within boarding range.](images/Iksar_Ship_Approaching.png)
1. Speak with [[Captain Pegg Bristol]] to trigger the attacking ship. Iksar sailors will bring their ship along side the Valiance and board the ship. You cannot board the attacking ship. The attacking sailors will board the Valiance and will begin killing ship crew members but remain unattackable to you. Once the sailors become attackable, they will attempt to kill you.
1. Kill the wave of [[Iksar sailor]]s.
1. Kill the wave of [[Iksar slave]]s.
1. Kill the [[Iksar captain]], [[Iksar cabin boy]], and [[Iksar slave]].
1. Loot the [[Torn mariner's chart]] from the captain's corpse.
1. Return to [[Qeynos Harbor]] by clicking on the ship's wheel and speak with [[Admiral Marc Grenich]].
1. Speak with [[Cartographer Blanche]] in the small wooded area just outside the [[Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos]] and show her the mariner's chart.
1. Return to [[Admiral Marc Grenich]] for your reward.
