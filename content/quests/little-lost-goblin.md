## Steps

1. Talk to a Gigglegibber goblin, located in the basement of the workshop, which is accessed by jumping down the hole near Meg.
1. Click on the giant *Enchanted Snowglobe* in the basement to enter [[Sleeper's Tomb: A Frostfell Visit]].
1. Find and speak with [[Greshdek (Sleeper's Tomb: A Frostfell Visit)|Greshdek]]. {{waypoint 449.01, 21.09, 198.69}}
   - This instance is full of epic mobs that might one shot you, although at L130 they did almost no damage. Pick up at least 100 [[Frosty Snowball|Frosty Snowballs]] from the buckets near the entrance and throw them at the mobs to freeze and kill them.
   - When you get to the big chamber, take the ramp on the left and continue along the path. Just before you get to the corridor with the statues a message will appear on your screen, "You feel as you are being watched". This is just a heads-up that some of the statues are actually mobs. Take the first right in the statue corridor and enter room with Christmas tree. Greshdek is in the back left corner as you walk in.
1. Collect 10 *lost presents* (trackable).
1. Return to Greshdek.
1. Use the [[Frostfell Return Token (Little Lost Goblin)|Frostfell Return Token]] to return to [[Frostfell Workshop]].
1. Speak with Meg Gingersnap.
