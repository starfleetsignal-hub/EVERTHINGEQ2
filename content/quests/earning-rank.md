## Steps

![The Entrance to the Court of Truth Debate Hall.](images/Court_of_Truth_DebateHall_Entrance.jpg)

1. Kill 15 [[A Blades guard|Blades guards]].
1. Kill 15 [[A Coin guard|Coin guards]].
1. Enter the instanced zone [[The Court of Truth: The Debate]] from the manhole cover on the side of the Court of Truth building at Loc {{waypoint -149, 150, 19}}.
1. Win the debate. Take the time to read the text and responses -- to win, three of the five judges must agree.  It is not possible to give a series to this as it varies with each entrance to the zone.
   - To start the debate hail [[Consul Suhail]].
   - Full dialogs and screenshots can be found on Discussion Page.
   - It is possible to win the majority by selecting random options until it works. If you lose the majority, simply leave and come back to try again. There is no punishment for failure so you can do this as many times as necessary.
1. Return to [[Faizal Khajanee]] for the reward.
