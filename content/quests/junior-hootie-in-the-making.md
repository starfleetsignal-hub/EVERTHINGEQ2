## Note

You need to complete [[Whoella|Whoellas]]' quest [[In Need of Reeds]] in order for [[Jabber Longwind]] to offer this quest.

## Steps

1. Gather and Hunt the following
   - Gather 8 buried nuts![(buried nuts) Dirt Mound](images/%28buried_nuts%29_Dirt_Mound.jpg)
     - *Tend to be around clusters of boulders. Only some mounds yield nuts, some possible locations include:*
       - {{waypoint 223.88, 277.21, 750.01}}, {{waypoint 244.19, 218.62, 648.25}}, {{waypoint 262.29, 222.56, 670.35}}
       - {{waypoint 240.93, 221.06, 676.35}}, {{waypoint 311.45, 207.84, 602.57}}, {{waypoint 339.18, 199.79, 607.64}}
       - {{waypoint 295.89, 209.98, 607.58}}, {{waypoint 289.54, 211.51, 571.74}}
       - {{waypoint 59.25, 202.51, 680.38}}, {{waypoint 53.34, 203.03, 693.07}}
       - {{waypoint 40.19, 203.17, 703.93}}, {{waypoint 65.51, 203.64, 701.85}}
       - lots of mounds found near {{waypoint 31, 203, 723}}
   - Hunt 6 wild aeraquis for feathers![a wild aeraquis](images/A_wild_aeraquis.jpg)
     - *these are purple horse-like mobs floating at various points on the hillsides*
1. ![Jabber's Wish Catcher](images/Jabber's_Wish_Catcher.jpg)Adorn 2 of Jabber's wish catchers at {{waypoint 264.13, 286.42, 925.36}}
1. Return to [[Jabber Longwind]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +3,500 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
- [[Sang'Huuu Scout Wish Catcher]] - *House item*
- One of the following:
  - [[Vultak Wand of Willpower]]
  - [[Aviak Crossbow of Balance]]
  - [[Vultak Katar of Malleability]]
  - [[Hooluk Satchel of Malleability]]
