## Requisite

Must be a gnome: part 3 of the 3-part racial questline (good version)

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Borach Anvilstrike]] near the [[Graystone Yard]] gate in [[Qeynos Harbor]] {{waypoint 877, -25, -53}}
1. Enter [[Graystone Yard: Herald of Rage]] {{waypoint 864, -25, -89}}
1. Locate Borach's toolbox, fighting off [[An enraged barbarian|enraged barbarian]]s and [[An enraged dwarf|enraged dwarf]]s, including three that will ambush you when you reach the toolbox {{waypoint 912, -20, -136}}
1. Return the toolbox to Borach Anvilstrike. Wait for him to craft the bot armor and speak to him again.
1. Return to [[Trina Tinkerton]]
1. From [[The Elddar Grove (POI)|the Elddar Grove]], zone into [[The Baubbleshire: Vale's Shadow]] {{waypoint 816, -21, -530}} and slay 10 [[An invading nightblood|invading nightblood]]s, [[An invading lamia|invading lamia]]s, and/or [[An invading lamia castigator|invading lamia castigator]]s
1. Return to Trina Tinkerton

## Rewards

- 8s 10c @ lvl 9
- [[Ring of the Gnomish Tinkerer (To Fail is to Learn)|Ring of the Gnomish Tinkerer]]
