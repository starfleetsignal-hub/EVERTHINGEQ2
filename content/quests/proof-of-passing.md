## Description

I must collect pairs of roekillik ears as proof that I have slain roekillik within Demetrik's Bastion.

## Steps

1. Kill 15 collect 15 roekillik ears from Demitrik's Bastion
   - *Note: The Ears are body drops from any roekillik in the zone*
1. Return to Hafiz

## Rewards

- At least 1p 80g
- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
