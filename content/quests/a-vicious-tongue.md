## Steps

1. Search Twark for 3 goblin pit fighters. Random updates.
   - *Have found updates at these locations, while flying without landing: {{waypoint 279, -175, -643}}, {{waypoint 326, -191, -513}}, {{waypoint 161, -191, -626}} or {{waypoint 267, 179, -646}}*
1. Hunt Bloody Tooth Goblins for a toxic tongue. - kill [[A Bloody Tooth biletongue]] found in [[Twark]] at random locations
   - *for example at {{waypoint 184, -180, -717}}. (There are other biletongues in the area as well.)*
1. Return to 'a nameless spirit' in Warslik's Woods {{waypoint 90, -208, -462}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 14987 status
