*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Naji Uday]]. {{waypoint -1341, -227, -583}}
1. Find the stolen shipment, found at location {{waypoint -1665, -227, 274}}. You will be attacked by three "Raj'Dur".
   - The stolen shipment is on a ten minute timer.
1. Return the shipment to Naji Uday.
1. Return to [[Thara Bashirah]].

## Reward

- At least: 2g 99s 47c
- -2500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -2500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- 2500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
