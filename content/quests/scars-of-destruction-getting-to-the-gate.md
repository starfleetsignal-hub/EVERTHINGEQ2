## Notes/Starting the Quest

1. You require 50,000 Port of Woe faction for Adris to tell you to go see Jenif Creagach
1. Talk to [[Adris Spiritshine (Port Woe)|Adris Spiritshine]] at {{waypoint -69.17, 6.69, 391.92}} in the [[Sodden Archipelago]]
1. Talk to [[Jenlif Creagach]] at {{waypoint -21, 7, 406}} to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Reach the [[Sodden Uplands]] - travel  Southwest through the swamp till you reach a path leading upwards, updates around {{waypoint 471, 19, 532}} and where you first see [[A voracious darkpaw|voracious darkpaws]].
   - *If you already earned flight via tradeskill line, you still have to pass through this point to get the update.*
1. Hunt the following:
   - Hunt 8 [[A voracious darkpaw|voracious darkpaws]] for their pelts {{waypoint 460, 27, 492}}  ![](images/Scars_of_Destruction_Getting_to_the_Gate_quest_-_devourer_seed.jpg)
   - Hunt 14 [[A botanic devourer|botanic devourers]] for devourer seeds
     - *Click the seeds they drop on the ground after killing. 2 seeds per kill.*
1. With the seeds and pelts, meet [[Toman Keltor]] at Blackhook Gate and speak with him at {{waypoint 112.48, 136.07, 95.08}}
