## Steps

1. Scribe [[Blueprint: Gnomish Pirate Spirits]]
1. Craft a [[Gnomish Pirate Spirits]] at a pirate distillery {{waypoint 1292, -540, 479}}. You will need:
   - 1 [[Liquid]]
   - 1 [[Titanium ore]]
   - 1 [[Frostbitten parsnip]] (can be purchased on broker, or harvested on [[Snowfang Isle]] (take Griffin to [[Icy Fingers]]) where parsnips are located near the Snowfang Gnolls {{waypoint -993, -521, -2219}}.)
   - 1 [[Ethereal coal]]
1. Return to Fenwick.

- This item is in fact the same item required for the quest [[What Dreams May Come]], so make 2 if you're on that quest.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +2,000 faction with **Iceclad Pirates**
