## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
  - While this quest can technically be completed an infinite amount of times, it will stop being offered once your faction is greater than -10,000 (indifferent) with Clan Skleross. Should your faction ever dip lower than that in the future, this quest will become available again.

## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A Durus decapitator|Durus Decapitators]] at the [[Howling Stones]]. {{waypoint 583, -71, -19}}
1. Return to [[Lord Sheckt]].

## Rewards

- +15,750 faction with **Clan Skleross**
- At least 19g 93s 2c
