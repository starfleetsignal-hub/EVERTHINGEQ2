## Steps

1. Swim across the river to tell [[Amoora]] that [[Ferink]] loves her.  She can be found standing just outside the [[Kerran Village]], at {{waypoint -196, -48, 164}}.
1. Amoora says that her father has forbidden her to see Ferink.  This message has to be delivered to Ferink, back across the river.  This completes the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 67c
