- Note: Highly recommended to first complete the quest [[The Means to an End...]] and have on one's person [[Barrel to Butcherblock]] as it drops you very near to [[Juna Crankfizz]] for all the turn-ins upcoming.

**The Fourth Warrior** isn't a quest in its own right, but a placeholder for three quests that you receive when speaking with Juna about the book to which you found the pages. In speaking with her, you can receive all of the following quests:

- [[The Fourth Warrior - Silken Drape]]
- [[The Fourth Warrior - The Leather Cloak]]
- [[The Fourth Warrior - Woven Metal]]

You must have the book [[Snuffing the Fireknight]] in your inventory to be able to get any of the quests.  If the book has been placed in your house, the quests will not be offered.  If you do not have the book, Juna is a merchant who sells it if you have already completed the collection for it.  It's also likely that you have to have read the book before being offered the quest.
