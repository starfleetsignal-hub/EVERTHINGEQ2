## Notes

- For characters of Good-alignment is is recommend to start the series of quests offered by [[Rask Helstot]] in [[The Elddar Grove (POI)|The Elddar Grove]] at the same time.
- For the solo instance [[Invasion of the Vale]] there is a minimum 1hr 30min reset timer.

## Steps

1. Enter the solo instance [[Invasion of the Vale]], located in [[Antonica]] at *The Shattered Vale* {{waypoint -2340, 0, -60}}.
   - *Note*: Though not zone-wide zone-in, group members on same step of this quest can zone in together by manually selecting [[Invasion of the Vale]].
1. Complete the ring event at the druid ring by doing the following:
   1. Slay the [[A Black Magi visionary|Black Magi visionaries]] (level 17-18) surrounding the druid ring
   1. Slay the [[A Black Magi elder|Black Magi elders]] (level 19) standing within the druid ring
   1. Slay the [[A Portal Abhorrent|Portal Abhorrent]] (level 20^) that spawns in the center of the druid ring.
   1. Slay the [[A Portal Atrocity|Portal Atrocity]] (level 20^) that spawns in the center of the druid ring.
   1. Slay the [[A Portal Abomination|Portal Abomination]] (level 20^) that spawns in the center of the druid ring.
1. A minute or two after you complete the ring event, [[A Windstalker grizzly]] will spawn towards the front of the instance and begin roaming slowly around the zone. Slay him to advance the quest.
1. Return to [[Hartok Woolyman|Hartok]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- at least 1s 51c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Adept's Woven Leggings]] (All Mages)
  - [[Footman's Worked Legplates]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Initiate's Threaded Leg Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Naturalist's Sewn Leather Pants]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Plunderer's Small Link Chain Leggings]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Seer's Rustic Chausses]] (Shamans)
  - [[Theist's Shaped Plate Greaves]] (Clerics)
