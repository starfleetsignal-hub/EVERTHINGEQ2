## Steps

1. Zone into [[Dracur Prime]].
1. Acquire the [[Draconic Gate Caller Talisman]]. This requires completing two sub-quests from [[Gozzinak]]: [[Fragile Rock]] and [[Gate Uncalling]]
1. Return to Jorlak
