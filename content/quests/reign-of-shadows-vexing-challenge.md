## Note

- Must complete [[Ka Vethan Regrets]] and [[As Ishinae Intended]] to be able to speak with [[Xawon]]

## Steps

1. Search around [[Vex Thal]] in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] {{waypoint -203, 61, 103}} for a clue as to how to gain entrance.
1. Start Subquests to spawn [[Xawon]]
   1. [[Ka Vethan Regrets]]: Speak to [[Ne'xath]] in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] {{waypoint -202, 61, 106}}
   1. [[As Ishinae Intended]]: Speak to [[Ne'xath]] in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] {{waypoint -202, 61, 106}}
1. Speak with [[Xawon]] at {{waypoint -211, 62, 114}}.
1. Hunt 6 [[A deadly shadefiend|deadly shadefiends]] in the crevasse between [[Shadeweaver Thicket]] and [[Needlespine Beach]] around {{waypoint 199, 14, -200}}.
1. Speak with [[Xawon]] again.
1. Obtain (autoupdate) a full set of va vex runes from the minions of Vex Thal you can find in front of the Vex Thal Portal {{waypoint 26, 151, 492}}:
   - Dat rune from [[A Vex Thal stalker|Vex Thal stalkers]], [[A Vex Thal pursuer|pursuers]], and [[A Vex Thal deathstalker|deathstalkers]] around {{waypoint 26, 151, 492}}.
   - Dax rune from [[A Vex Thal stalker|Vex Thal stalkers]], [[A Vex Thal pursuer|pursuers]], and [[A Vex Thal deathstalker|deathstalkers]].
   - Lor rune from [[A Vex Thal stalker|Vex Thal stalkers]], [[A Vex Thal pursuer|pursuers]], and [[A Vex Thal deathstalker|deathstalkers]].
   - Kel rune from [[An akheva enthraller|Akhevan enthrallers]].
   - Kelera rune from [[An akheva enthraller|Akhevan enthrallers]].
   - Raf rune from [[An enslaved grimling|enslaved grimlings]] around {{waypoint 26, 151, 492}}.
   - Set rune from [[An enslaved grimling|enslaved grimlings]].
   - Tak rune from [[An enslaved grimling|enslaved grimlings]].
   - Ved rune from [[A shadowy assassin|shadowy assassins]] around {{waypoint 26, 151, 492}}. This one came out of the ground at this location {{waypoint 9, 149, 473}} following a black smoke, like shadowed harvestables.
   - Vin rune from [[A shadowy assassin|shadowy assassins]] around {{waypoint 26, 151, 492}}.
     - *note: Ved and Vin both dropped from the same assassin after 25+ kills*
     - *note*: Raf, Set and Tak dropped from the same grimling after 25+ kills, I got the Ved and Vin after just a few kills
1. Speak with [[Xawon]] at {{waypoint -211, 62, 114}}.
