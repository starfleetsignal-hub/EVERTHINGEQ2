## Steps

1. You will be randomly assigned one of the following tasks:
   - Collect 10 **Rin Dal ritual daggers** in [[The Living Tombs]].
   - Slay 20 [[An Anaz Mal gravehowler|Anaz Mal gravehowlers]] in [[The Living Tombs]].
   - Collect 10 **Rujarkian weapon crates** in [[The Clefts of Rujark]].
   - Slay 20 [[A Steelslave enforcer|Steelslave enforcer]]s in [[The Clefts of Rujark]]. One known location is on level 2 in the Cavern of Training {{waypoint 94, 17, -157}}. About 14 are up per spawn cycle.
1. Return to [[Captain Woug]] in North Qeynos.

## Rewards

- 9352 status
- +500 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
- 1 [[City Token]]
