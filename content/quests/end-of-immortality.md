## Steps

1. Enter Throne of Storms (first blue portal on the right side of the stairs)
1. Obtain [[An Enormous Metal Key]] by killing the final name in the zone: [[Tribunus-Prime Hagandr]]. Note: the key comes from the chest, so you must be level-appropriate to receive the loot.
1. Talk to the captive orc, [[Wolfsnack]] {{waypoint 265.63, 22.33, 1257.98}}.
1. Speak with Wolfsnack again to have him show you how to enter Tormax's Hidden Treasure Chamber.
1. Help Wolfsnack unlock the Hidden Treasure Chamber.
1. Investigate the opening below the throne. Solve the throne room sequence to gain access to the treasure chamber.
1. Dispel the thaumaturgic magic surrounding the Horn of Hsagra.
1. Report back to Graeme Gordon.
