Note: 1)Updates from the bitterleaf are group-wide meaning only one person needs to click to get the update. 2)You must have completed [[Fightin' Ice with Ice]].

## Steps

1. Head to the east-northeast to the Crystalline Crevasses around {{waypoint -133, -358, 129}} It's fastest to take the griffon to the Dragon Ring.
1. Gather 6 healing herbs from the icy fern plants in the crevasses (same crevasses that have the crystalline spiders mentioned below)
1. Kill 8 [[A crystalline watcher|crystalline watchers]] or [[A crystalline hunter|crystalline hunters]] for their silk at {{waypoint -130.2, -356.4, 137}}.
   - The watchers have a very nasty poison that can do over 1.1k damage per tick, so you may want to have elemental curing potions handy!
1. Talk to Norbu. He will tell you to heal the griffon.
1. Target the griffon across from Norbu and use the Salve Soaked Bandages on it.
1. Return to Norbu to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
