## Notes

This is a Public Quest that is automatically inserted into your quest journal upon entering the designated area.

The quest begins every 30 minutes, at the top of the hour and half past the hour.

Throughout the waves, and immediately afterward, purple harvestable shinys will spawn in the vicinity of the Dragon Ring.  Each shiny will contain one [[Token of Greater Teleportation]].

## Steps

1. Protect the construction crew
   - Waves of Order of Rime soldiers will spawn around the Dragon Ring area, attacking from three fronts:
   - The first wave attacks from three fronts:
     - {{waypoint -68, -350, 156}}
     - {{waypoint -106, -350, 54}}
     - {{waypoint 87, -304, 78}}
   - The second wave attacks from two fronts:
     - {{waypoint -68, -350, 156}}
     - {{waypoint -106, -350, 54}}
   - The third wave attacks from a single front, and includes a named mob that does grant AA
     - {{waypoint -68, -350, 156}}

## Rewards

- 25 [[Token of Greater Teleportation]]
