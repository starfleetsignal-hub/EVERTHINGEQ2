## Notes

- None of the harvestable items is trackable.
- All of these clicks are **zone-wide group** updates.  If you are grouped anywhere in the zone, your group members will receive updates.

## Steps

1. Gather the following ingredients from [[The Sinking Sands]]:
   1. Gather 2 Dervish Curry Spices around the [[Croc Hunter Camp]] near {{waypoint -1335.45, -226.16, -596.08}}![Dervish Curry Spices](images/Dervish_Curry_Spices.jpg)
   1. Gather 3 Oasis Squash around [[The Oasis of Marr]] near {{waypoint -1203.39, -215.70, -248.16}}![Oasis Squash](images/Oasis_Squash.jpg)
   1. Gather 5 Twin Tear Coconuts around [[Twin Tears]] near {{waypoint -584.34, -131.03, -886.41}}![Twin Tear Coconut](images/Twin_Tear_Coconut.jpg)Coconuts are located at the base of the palm trees.
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]].

## Rewards

- [[Days of Summer: 2020]] useable at [[Pas Yu]], the merchant beside [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]] on the [[Spire Island]].
