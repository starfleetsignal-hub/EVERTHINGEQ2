1. Kill 10-15 droags.  The specific mob required is selected from the below list each time the quest is accepted
   - [[A Scaleborn sentry (Tenebrous Tangle)|Scaleborn sentries]] in [[Tenebrous Tangle]]
   - [[A Scaleborn warrior (Tenebrous Tangle)|Scaleborn warriors]] in Tenebrous Tangle
   - [[A Scaleborn stonesetter|Scaleborn stonesetters]] in Tenebrous Tangle
   - [[A Scaleborn reaver|Scaleborn reavers]] in [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]]
   - [[A Scaleborn warrior (Sanctum of the Scaleborn)|Scaleborn warriors]] in Sanctum of the Scaleborn
   - [[A Scaleborn sentry (Sanctum of the Scaleborn)|Scaleborn sentries]] in Sanctum of the Scaleborn
