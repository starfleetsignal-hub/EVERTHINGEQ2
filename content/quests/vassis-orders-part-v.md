This is part 5 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Freeport]] who wear Leather and Chain Armor ([[Shaman|shaman]]).

## Steps

1. You must collect items from creatures in [[Nektulos Forest]].
   - Kill 4 [[A rotting follower of Grelkor|rotting followers of Grelkor]] for their decaying flesh {{waypoint -549.76, 60.37, -1289.93}}.
   - Kill 8 [[Deadly rumbleroot]] for their toxic sap {{waypoint -859.49, 65.97, 17.78}}.
   - Kill 10 [[Blood shriller]]s for their talons {{waypoint -12.00, 0.05, -442.53}}.
   - Kill 1 [[A Thexian Vin chosen|Thexian Vin chosen]] for his Teir'Dal Greaves {{waypoint -1454.99, 42.98, -1413.65}}.
1. Return to [[Vassi Darkscale]].
