## Steps

1. Interact with the dragon skull on the second floor. {{waypoint 25, 0, -110}}
1. Slay [[High Shaman Zen'Durath]] who spawns.
1. Return to Consul Bree to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- [[A Note from the Qeynos Quartermaster - Adventures in Antonica]]. *Reading this note directs you to start the [[Antonica Timeline]].*
- +200 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
- One of the following depending on race or city-alignment (undetermined which affects):
  - +50 faction with **The Royal Antonican Guard**
  - -50 faction with **The Royal Antonican Guard**
- One of the following:
  - [[Armor of Bliss]]
  - [[Faithful Chestguard]]
  - [[Intrepid Chestguard]]
  - [[Overcoat of Inspiration]]
  - [[Robe of the Cavern Spider]]
  - [[The Royal Guard]]
  - [[Tunic of the Wild]]
