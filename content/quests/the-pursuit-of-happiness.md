## Steps

1. Kill 14 [[Nerius vandal|Nerius vandals]] in [[Nektulos Forest]].  They can be found at the tent camps on the beach near the Nektulos dock. You will need multiple camp clearing to get all 14 (rare spawn).

## Rewards

- 1792 status @L21
- +500 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
