## Steps

1. Right-click the [[A lazy Ry'Gorr orc]] {{waypoint 76, 24, 945}} and "Use the [[Ry'Gorr Scrying Eye]]" on him (a short video plays). You can also pick up the eye from your inventory and click on the orc while the icon is on your mouse arrow. Note that you will not get the [[Ry'Gorr Scrying Eye]] if you get the quest through sharing and will be unable to complete the quest.
1. Return to Thirin

## Rewards

- At least 2s 40c
