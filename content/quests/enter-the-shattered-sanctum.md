You must be a Good-aligned Erudite of level 20 or higher to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum]] and
   - Kill 8 invading undead
   - Destroy 6 soul spikes - they look like daggers with a sickly green sparkly effect lying on the ground
1. Return to Maareona Ludimintium

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g @ lvl 95
