## Steps

1. Click on the Priority Orders desk at {{waypoint -94, 4, 105}} to receive the quest. The quest is only available once every 7 days.
1. Click on recipe book on same desk.
1. Craft the following items (level 69 recipes):
   - a anti-sea-sickness potion at a Chemistry Table (makes the 10 required)
   - a protective metal plating at a Forge
   - a utility pocket knife at a Forge
   - a replacement hammock at a Woodworking Table
   - a spare boom at a Woodworking Table
   - a lucky charm talisman at a Work Bench
   - a potent rum at a Stove & Keg
   - a spell of protective warding an Engraved Desk
   - a spare ship's sail at a Loom
1. Return completed items to Supply Cupboard
