At least:![The Fordel Midst Workbench with a coal bin to the right](images/Fordel_Midst_Workbench.jpg)

- 24 [[Humming Azurite]]
- 24 [[Luclizite Cluster]]
- 12 [[Radiant Algae]]
- 12 [[Slow Creeping Root]]
- 30 [[Coruscating Coal]]

## Steps

- While in [[Aurelian Coast]], locate the Fordel Midst Tunnel {{waypoint 139.71, 86.93, -495.93}}, and follow it until you reach the door (portal) at {{waypoint 171.93, 61.89, -676.22}} to enter ***Fordel Midst: Investigating the Shadow Haven***. (Fast Travel to City of Fordel Midst, then exit to Aurelian Coast achieves the update.)
- Make your way to the library at {{waypoint 741.37, -8.55, -704.99}}
  - *NOTE: Avoid the glowing orange urns.*![A glowing urn](images/A_glowing_urn.jpg)
- Pick up the book near the base of the toppled bookcase {{waypoint 772, -8, -739}}.
  - *NOTE: The [[Recipe Scroll: Spectral Recipes]] auto-scribes.*
  - NOTE:  You might not see the [[Spectral Essence of Midst]] and [[Spectral Essence of Fordel]] at this time.  The work around is to leave the zone and reset the zone, zone back and then you will be able to see them.  Sometimes /camp <self> works too.

- Gather at least 7 [[Spectral Essence of Midst]] from small black-ball lightning nodes
- Gather at least 7 [[Spectral Essence of Fordel]] from blue blubbling nodes.  *Note: Nodes are trackable. Two pulls per node.*
- Click the coal scuttle by the workbench to get the [[Spectral Coruscating Coal]]. ![Recipe book](images/Recipe_book.jpg)

- Craft [[True-Sight Spectracles]] on the Fordel Midst Workstation at {{waypoint 792.73, -8.55, -761.71}}
- Find an alternate exit
  1. Craft [[Spectral Phasing Spray]] on the Fordel Midst Workstation. (You need to use this on the Blacksmiths.)
     - Optionally craft [[Spectral Tethering Chains]] as well.
  1. Make your way back to the entrance and use the door at {{waypoint 484.97, -23.44, -531.64}}
     - Use the [[True-Sight Spectracles]] to get through the spectral doors.
     - Use [[Spectral Phasing Spray]] and [[Spectral Tethering Chains]] to neutralize the various hostile creatures in the zone.
       - *NOTE: The spray and chains both take much longer than usual to neutralize the threat. Stay as far away as possible.*
       - *A VoV-equipped 120+ adventurer should be able to easily kill the hostile spectral apparitions, eliminating the time sink for using the spray and/or chains. The spray is still needed for the next step, so don't forget to craft it.*
  1. Proceed to {{waypoint -122.89, -1.46, 4.84}}
     - Continuing using the spectral devices.
- Use [[Spectral Phasing Spray]] on the [[Purihni "The Edge" Fordel III]] and [[Gundeg "The Anvil" Midst]] around -114.84 -1.46 17.76 216.38.
  - (Note: If you are having trouble finding them, make sure you have your "Spectracles" activated in order to see them.)
  - **WARNING: Do not KILL** (inadvertently, or otherwise) Purihni or Gundeg as there is NO RESPAWN and you will have to exit the instance and then wait for the lockout timer to expire.  Deleting the quest and retaking it changes nothing.  Only time heals all wounds.
- Speak to Purihni  to obtain the next quest.
