## Steps

1. Look for the missing figurine.
   1. Skethusk mentioned an old collapsed goblin tunnel, which is found near Grunt's Pass at {{waypoint -318, 48, 604}}. Zone into the [[Abandoned Mine Tunnel]] you may have to stand on top of the boulder in the middle for it to become clickable.![Collapsed Tunnel Entrance](images/Collapsed_Tunnel_Entrance.jpg)
   1. Check the first room first and, if not there,  follow the rails and **search the "loose rubble" until you find the figurine**.![loose rubble](images/Loose_rubble.jpg)
      - *Note:* the location *is* definitely random.
      - *Note: If you have trouble finding back to the exit {{waypoint 101.25, 47.85, -2144.42}}, Artisan's Escape works in the zone.*
1. Return to Skethusk in Obulus Frontier {{waypoint 479, 245, 1505}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +5,000 faction with **Crusaders of Greenmist**
- [[Collection of Goblin Items]] - *examine this to start the next quest*

## Notes

You will also find 3 red shinies in this zone, which are needed for the [[Collection Quests|collection]] quest [[Gardener's Tools]]. These are: ***Goblin Supply Pack***, ***Gardener's Hand Rake***, and ***Gardener's Fertilizer Sack.*** All are trackable and respawn relatively quickly.
