## Steps

1. Gather 9 strangleknot roots found around wall at the beach. Large collection in the alcove around {{waypoint -1120, -529, 3744}}
1. Kill Thrael'Gorr war fishers and poison their food around {{waypoint -569, -539, 4174}}
   - Kill 8 [[A Thrael'Gorr war fisher|Thrael'Gorr war fishers]] around {{waypoint -569, -539, 4174}}
   - Poison 5 food, they are called *a Thrael'Gorr fish stand*
1. Return to [[Terwoo]]
