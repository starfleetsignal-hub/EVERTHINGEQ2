## Notes

- Must be an [[Outfitter]] ([[Tailor]], [[Armorer]] or [[Weaponsmith]]) to get this quest. Two [[Recipe Scroll: Nazeera's Outfitter Recipes|recipes]] needed by this quest are auto scribed when accepting quest.
- Materials needed:
  - 1 [[Ulthork Hide]]
  - 15 [[Sedge Root|Sedge Roots]]
  - 15 [[Zimaran Filament|Zimaran Filaments]]

## Steps

1. Create the following items
   - Create a [[Colorful Shawl]]
   - Create 2 [[Wispy Scarf]]
1. Speak to [[Nazeera]] at {{waypoint 145, 9, -422}} in the [[Western Wastes]]
   - *Note: If you cannot see her when you return, try issuing the command **/target Nazeera** and then "H" hail her to get completion of this quest.*

## Rewards

- 3 [[Mesmeric Shell Powder]]
- [[Status Coin (75,000)|Status Coin [75,000]]]
- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
