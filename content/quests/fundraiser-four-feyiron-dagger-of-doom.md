According to Deputy Hopple, the judges in this contest are specifically looking for a finely honed edge on the submitted feyiron daggers. If I'm not up to the task I can always buy my way out of this one, but I'm betting the prize will be something useful only to a skilled weaponsmith. Either way, I'm sure to take first prize in this contest.

## Steps

1. Craft (or buy) a [[Feyiron Dagger (Level 30) (Crafted)|feyiron dagger]]
1. Return to [[Deputy Hopple]]

## Rewards

- [[Leatherfoot Knuckles Design]]  - recipe for [[Leatherfoot Knuckles]]
