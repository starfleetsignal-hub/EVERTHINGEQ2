- This quest can be started and completed at 55.

## Steps

1. Speak with Shalih Mar at Tenebrous Landing {{waypoint -29, 20, 246}}.
1. Talk to the [[Shade of the Great Wolf]] {{waypoint -21, 23, 161}}.
1. Collect 3 Idols (they have a reuse timer) :  (All 3 can be touched without stealth or getting any aggro if you are creative and sneaky)
   - At the top of the pyramid in [[Temple Grounds]], east of the lake at {{waypoint -122, 73, -105}}'
   - North of the isle, at the back of the wall at {{waypoint -11, 115, -326}}
   - In the treehouse at {{waypoint 38, 42, 103}}
1. Speak the Shade again on [[Fear Tainted Isle]] {{waypoint 163, 229, 805}}.
1. Kill 8 [[A summoned frightling|Summoned frightlings]].
1. Speak the Shade again on [[Gazer Isle]] {{waypoint 778, 463, -95}}.
1. Defeat 6 [[A wicked gazer|Gazers]] on the isle.
1. Report back to Shalih Mar at Tenebrous Landing.

## Rewards

- At least 6g 88s 10c
