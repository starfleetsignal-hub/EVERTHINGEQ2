## Notes

- This is a sub-quest for [[Casualties of the War of the Fay]].

## Steps

1. Enter [[Klak'Anon]] and zone into the [[Court of Innovation]] {{waypoint 11, 11, 86}}.
1. Clear the instance and defeat [[Master Clockwork Protocol (original)|Master Clockwork Protocol]]. You will receive the MCP power cell as an auto-update.
   - See [[Court of Innovation]] for a walkthrough of the instance.
1. Return to [[Kelethin]] and insert (right-click) the power cell into [[Sir Gearheart]]. {{waypoint 398, 145, 168}}
1. Talk to [[Princess Saphronia]] to receive your reward.

- [[Bag of Gold Fairy Dust]]
