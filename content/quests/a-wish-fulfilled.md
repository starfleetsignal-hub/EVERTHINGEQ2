## Steps

1. Gather the following
   - Gather the **Jeweled Flask of Destiny** from  [[Scorched Shores]]  {{waypoint 465.02, -57.04, 261.89}} (near [[Dralira of Ro]] merc )![Wish-Wrought Shackles, a vicious dabratu, and Jeweled Flask of Destiny](images/Wish_Wroght_Shackles_a_vicious_dabratu_Jeweled_Flask_of_Destiny.png)
   - Gather **Wish-Wrought Shackles** from [[Scales of Ro]].
     - *If you have not yet unlocked flight, try going up the lift to [[Raj'Durabad]] and equipping a safefall cloak (such as [[Cloak of the Harvester]] or one of the LON cloaks, or use the Jinn Magi illusion, etc.,  You can also equip [[Overclocked Gnomish Crosstrainers]] to help you jump farther.  Then run and jump from {{waypoint -289, 0.00, 45.57}} the edge there towards the lower mesas.* Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -295, 75, 127}}
     - {{waypoint -300, 71, 163}}
     - {{waypoint -284, 75, 118}}
     - {{waypoint -342, 42, 186}}
   - Gain 8 cursed bones at Mordant Spires from [[A vicious dabrutu|vicious dabrutus]] around {{waypoint 502, 31, -189}}
1. Return to [[Xamira]]
1. finish her tale *(the conversation with her)*
