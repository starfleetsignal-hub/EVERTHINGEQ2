## Prerequisites

- +10,000 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**

## Steps

1. Find and steal *one of the following two items*:
   - The [[Orb of Dartain]] from the Defenders of the Seal (appears as a harvestable globe near {{waypoint 535.67, 132.15, -246.83}}}
   - **or**
   - The [[Elementals' Apparatus]] from the Guardians of the Underfoot (appears as a harvestable globe near {{waypoint -163, 132, -985}})
1. Return to [[Belessunu]].

## Rewards

- At least 55g 70s 61c
- +2500 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**
- 1 [[Mark of Manaar]]
