## Steps

1. Slay Order of Rime groups with some Goahmari assistance.
   - Hail [[A battle ready Goahmari]] {{waypoint 330, -15, 542}}, who is standing near Billie in the tent.  The battle ready Goahmari will become a controllable pet.
   - Slay 3 full groups of Rime with the battle ready Goahmari. These are the same groups from the last quest, and you'll need to kill all three in each group before the quest updates.
   - If you happen to die, return to Billie's tent to get another battle ready Goahmari.
1. Return to Billie to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
