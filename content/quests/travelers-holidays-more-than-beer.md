## Steps

1. Travel to [[Butcherblock Mountains]] and collect the following:
   - Collect 4 dockworkers' ale from around the merchant-space at [[Butcherblock Docks]] {{waypoint 687.76, 24.00, 597.82}}![Dockworker's Ale](images/Dockworker's_Ale.webp)
   - Collect 4 Yarpsnarl Totems in The Broken Fields, along wall due north  {{waypoint -571, 104, 472}}  of Dagnor's Pass also known as the entrance to The Estate of Unrest.
     - *(Note: During the day, the totems are standing in front of the rocks and have very similar color, so they can appear to be hidden.  They are easiest to see at night since they glow.)*![Yarpsnarl Totem](images/Yarpsnarl_Totem.webp)
   - Collect 4 Kaladim Mine Picks slightly east of [[Darkfall Gorge]]  {{waypoint -178.38, 151.53, -419.61}}![Kaladim Mine Pick](images/Kaladim_Mine_Pick.webp)
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]]
