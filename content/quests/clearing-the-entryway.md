## Steps

1. Enter [[Chardok]] and kill the following mobs. They share spawns, and can be found near the entrance, before [[The Wind Chamber (Chardok)|The Wind Chamber]]:
   - 4 [[A Bathezid battler|Bathezid battlers]]
   - 2 [[A Bathezid veteran|Bathezid veterans]]
1. Examine the note given to you upon the last kill.
1. Return to [[Warlord Gadar Gwalnax]].

## Rewards

- At least 19g 93s 2c
- +1000 faction with **The Gwalnax Brigade**
