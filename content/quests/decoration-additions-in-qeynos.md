## Notes

After completing this quest, if you'd like more trees, you can buy more tickets from questgiver for 1p each.

## Steps

1. Decorate [[South Qeynos (POI)|South Qeynos]]. The locations have been marked by Gigglegibber goblins with green sparkles:
   - Travel to {{waypoint 585, -17, 259}}, just west of The Tin Soldier.
   - Travel to {{waypoint 553, -9, 144}}, left of the front door of the Lion's Mane Inn.
   - Travel to {{waypoint 610, -11, 165}}, on the grassy area in front of 5 Erollisi Lane, around the corner from the pet shop.
1. Decorate [[North Qeynos (POI)|North Qeynos]], marked by a green sparkle:
   - Travel to {{waypoint 426, -20, -325}}, by the big tree just east of the statue of Queen Antonia Bayle.
   - Defeat [[Mr. McScroogle]] (scales).
1. Return to [[Gutten Gigglegibber (New Halas)|Gutten Gigglegibber]].

## Rewards

- [[Frosty Frostfell Tree Ticket]]
  - To use the ticket double click the tree to the left with the blue star on top.
- If you talk to Gutten Gigglegibber again he will tell you that you can decorate the tree you just received by talking to [[Mr. McScroogle]] (see [[A Frostfell Favor]]). If you already did that quest, he will know that and tell you so.
