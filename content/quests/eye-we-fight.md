## Steps

![a hollowstare ward](images/A_hollowstare_ward.png)
![A lashling mound for the Oogothl Sprawl quest "Eye We Fight"](images/Eye_We_Fight_-_A_lashling_mound.png)
![a lashling](images/Eye_We_Fight_-_a_lashling.png)

1. Grusla needs you to hunt hollowstare wards and gather living lashlings. Both can be found in Tentacle Fields and Perse Lagoon.
   - Hunt 8x [[A hollowstare ward|hollowstare ward]]s. {{waypoint 389, 28, -115}}
   - Gather 12 lashlings by digging in lashling mounds.
     - These mounds appear in the Tentacle Fields area, near the hollowstare wards. They tend to blend in, so they may be easy to miss. Not all mounds spawn lashlings. When they do, pick them up to update the quest.
1. Return to [[Grusla]]

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- 2 x [[Lashling Mound]]
- [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
