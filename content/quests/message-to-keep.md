## Steps

1. Spy on the [[Ry'Gorr Keep]] in [[Eastern Wastes]]. Zone over to Eastern Wastes and fly south west past the wizard spires around {{waypoint -679, -335, 2810}} to get the update for Spying at Ry'Gorr Keep.
1. Use [[Cutlass the Falcon]] to send the report. The quest will complete after doing this.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- [[Sabre the Falcon]]
