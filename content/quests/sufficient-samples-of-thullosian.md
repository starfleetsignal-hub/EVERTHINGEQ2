## Notes

- Proficiency in the [[Thullosian]] language is not considered a [[Languages|language skill]], so upon completion of this quest it will not appear in the player's language skill tab.
- It is necessary to complete this quest in order to progress in the quest, [[Unlock Their Secrets]].

## Steps

![Samples of Thullosian](images/Thullosian.png)

1. Gather samples of [[Thullosian]] from around the [[Moors of Ykesha]]:
   - Loot 12 *Book of Thullosian Writing* or *Scroll of Thullosian Graffiti* from the corpses of Thullosians around [[Ykesha's Spine]]. These are uncommon drops. {{waypoint 393.64, -62.16, -187.53}}
1. Return the samples to [[Linguobot]].

## Rewards

- Experience only
