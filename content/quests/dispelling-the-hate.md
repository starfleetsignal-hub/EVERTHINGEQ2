## Starting the Quest

Every good-aligned city and many villages in game have a "worried" resident who starts this quest:

- in [[South Qeynos]], speak to [[A worried halfling]] {{waypoint 741, -20, 177}}
- in [[North Qeynos]], speak to [[A worried froglok]] {{waypoint 313, -20, 12}}
- in [[Kelethin]], Speak to [[A worried froglok]] {{waypoint 435, 92, 165}}

## Steps

1. Offer your services to [[Priest Aaronolis Swornlove (Kelethin)|Priest Aaronolis Swornlove]] near [[Jysolin's Pub]] in the city of [[Kelethin]], in [[Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint 238, 110, 348}}, use Granite Hills acorn lift.![](images/ASuspiciousGem.png)![](images/MaleficQuartz.png)
1. Aaronolis asks you to attempt to calm [[Isabell D'Ravius]], who has been starting fights in [[Joleena's Restaurant]] in [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 536, 71, 318}}.
   - When you enter [[Isabell D'Ravius]] will spawn and attack you.  You must kill her.
     - Note: If you hear fighting inside the restaurant (ie. another person/group already fighting her), wait outside until the combatants exit, otherwise it seems to bug the encounter and she will not spawn for you.
1. Return to Aaronolis and report your findings. Isabell's suspicious comments point towards the involvement of [[Innoruuk]], so you must find where his followers are hiding in Greater Faydark.
1. Travel to the [[The Combine Spires]] and find the altar of Innoruuk. (The altar is located near the lower leg of the spire, where all the spiders are.)  Nearby is a suspicious gem {{waypoint -505, 45, 272}}. Collect the gem and return to Aaronolis.
1. The priest sends you to seek out [[Farra Marthen]], a Sister of Peace just past Silver Leaf Inn {{waypoint 324, 92, 335}}, to see if a follower of [[Quellious]] the Tranquil can quench the hate in the stone.
1. Farra gives you a [[Blessed Crate of Tranquility]]. You must collect 12 malefic quartz and place them in this crate to neutralize its emanations of hatred. These appear as small heaps of dirt randomly located on the forest floor. The talk page lists confirmed locations with waypoints, but in general, they appear:
   - around the [[Green Knoll]] {{waypoint 429, 52, 74}}
   - in and around the [[Druid Ring (GFay)|Druid Ring]] {{waypoint 217, 13, 2}}
   - around the [[Sapling Spur Outpost]] {{waypoint -133, 10, -140}}
   - in and around [[The Combine Spires|the Combine Spires]] {{waypoint -448, 45, 268}}
   - near the NPCs around [[Spire Shadow Outpost]] {{waypoint -435, 123, 484}}
   - down [[Echo Echo Canyon]]
   - near the paths in [[The Nursery]] area
1. Return to Aaronolis.
