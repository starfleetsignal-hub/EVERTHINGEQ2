## Steps

1. Gather and kill the following at [[Splendor Wellspring]] around {{waypoint -404.27, 60.34, 315.65}}
   - Gather 6 **kerratail reeds**
   - slay 8 [[A horned charger|horned chargers]]
1. Return to [[Whoella]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- +3,500 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
- One of the following:
  - [[Vultak Chain Bracers of Pride]] -- *chain forearms*
  - [[Aviak Guards of Balance]] -- *cloth forearms*
  - [[Hooluk Bracers of Malleability]] -- *leather forearms*
  - [[Hooluk Vambraces of Ammolite]] -- *plate forearms*
