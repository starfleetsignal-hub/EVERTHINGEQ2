## Required Materials

- 50 [[Etherium]]
- 50 [[Golden ember]]
- 50 [[Gnarled entwood]]
- 25 [[Celestial Coal]]

## Steps

There are two different zones you can use to complete this quest.

Zone 1:

1. Enter [[The Molten Throne: Hate's Essences (Tradeskill)|The Molten Throne: Hate's Essences [Tradeskill]]] at {{waypoint 94, 3, -149}}.
   - *Note: There are two ways to go from here, the jumpy way, or non-jumpy way.*
   - JUMPY WAY
     - *NOTE: Turn OFF and unequip all glide and float abilities and gear for this step. Equip and activate as much bountiful harvest gear as you can (the Essences can bountiful harvest).*
     - Collect an Essence of Hate at {{waypoint 1808, 709, 960}}. After you harvest it a flaming ball heads away towards the next at {{waypoint 1877, 734, 892}}.
     - Walk onto the short flaming path and proceed slowly as it extends before you.
     - Periodically a blue swirly block will appear in the path. Edge carefully toward it until you see the path beyond it. Jump OVER the blue onto the red path beyond.
     - Collect Essences of Hate as you come across them.
   - NON-JUMPY WAY
     - Harvest a Molten Throne debris 3 times. This auto-scribes [[Fragment of Ro Diagram]]
     - Collect 50 [[Molten shard|molten shards]]
     - Craft a [[Fragment of Ro]] on an extinquished construct at {{waypoint 1811, 695, 795}}.
     - Requires:
       - 50 [[Molten shard]]
       - 50 [[Etherium]]
       - 50 [[Golden ember]]
       - 50 [[Gnarled entwood]]
       - 25 [[Celestial Coal]]
     - Collect an [[Essence of Hate]] at {{waypoint 1877, 734, 892}}. After you harvest it a flaming ball heads away towards the next.
     - Walk onto the short flaming path and proceed slowly as it extends before you.
     - Periodically a black swirly black will appear in the path. Use the [[Fragment of Ro]] to 'flame over' the black block and continue on.
     - Collect [[Essences of Hate]] as you come across them.
1. Once you have 9 [[Essences of Hate]], use the [[Inert Deific Vessel]] from your inventory to fill it.

Zone 2:

1. Enter [[The Molten Throne: Portals to Nowhere (Tradeskill)|The Molten Throne: Portals to Nowhere [Tradeskill]]] at {{waypoint 94, 3, -149}}.
1. Harvest a Molten Throne debris 3 times. This auto-scribes [[Fragment of Ro Diagram]]
1. Collect 50 [[Molten shard|molten shards]]
1. Craft a [[Fragment of Ro]] on an extinquished construct at {{waypoint 1811, 695, 795}}.
   - Requires:
     - 50 [[Molten shard]]
     - 50 [[Etherium]]
     - 50 [[Golden ember]]
     - 50 [[Gnarled entwood]]
     - 25 [[Celestial Coal]]
1. Collect an [[Essence of Hate]] at {{waypoint 1877, 734, 892}}.
1. Follow the green ball to {{waypoint 1877, 726, 910}} and use [[Fragment of Ro]].
   - Two white balls (portals) appear.
1. Use one of the portals.
   - You will be telported to another square of flame.
1. Collect an [[Essence of Hate]] if there is one.
1. Use [[Fragment of Ro]].
   - Two to four portals will appear.
1. Use one of the portals.
   - Continue until you have 9 [[Essences of Hate]].
1. Once you have 9 [[Essences of Hate]], use the [[Inert Deific Vessel]] from your inventory to fill it.
