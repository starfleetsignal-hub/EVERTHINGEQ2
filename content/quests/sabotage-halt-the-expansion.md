***This quest is only available to players under level 30***

## Steps

1. **Within 10 minutes** kill 5 [[An overland woodcutter|overland woodcutters]] in Antonica. Overland woodcutters spawn randomly throughout [[Antonica]] but always near trees. Overland Woodcutters have been found at the following locations:
   - Loc {{waypoint -82, -25, 483}}
   - Loc {{waypoint -380, -21, 395}}
   - Loc {{waypoint -460, -21, 395}}
   - Loc {{waypoint -550, -12, 109}}
   - Loc {{waypoint -491, -15, 129}}
   - Loc {{waypoint 97, -14, 170}}
1. Return to [[Gol M'Tun]].

## Rewards

- Faction Only:
  - +1000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
  - -1000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
  - -1000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**

## Notes

You can use the [[Mentoring]] system to perform this quest if you are too high level. You are not required to stay mentored once you have the quest active.
