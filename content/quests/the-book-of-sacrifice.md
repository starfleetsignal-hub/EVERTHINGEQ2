## Notes

- This is a mandatory sub-quest of [[A Mark of Awakening]].

## Steps

1. Harvest an unhatched dragon egg from the [[Reservoir of the Wraith]] at {{waypoint 82, 39, -166}}. No gathering skill required. One minute respawn timer.
1. Return to the Book of Sacrifice and place the egg on top of it. {{waypoint -101, 66, -34}}
