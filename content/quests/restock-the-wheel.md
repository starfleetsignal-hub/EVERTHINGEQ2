## Steps

1. Complete the following steps in any order:
   - Gather 3 lengths of wood from unique *fallen log* nodes. {{waypoint -122, -120, 650}}
   - Mine 3 caches of stone from nearby unique *stone boulder* nodes. {{waypoint 91, -117, 597}}
   - Kill 4 [[A kunzar scaleshredder|kunzar scaleshredder]] in [[Dragon Drool Lake]]. {{waypoint 91, -135, 704}}
1. Return to Taskmaster Gax.

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- +5,000 faction with **Dalnir's Wheel Taskmasters**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Fleet Commander's Cutlass]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Foil of the Stalker]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Golden Wand of the Sunmage]] (All Mages)
  - [[Kunzar Magician's Foil]] (All Mages)
  - [[Outer Sebilis Flail]] (All Priests)
  - [[Rain Summoner's Forged Blade]] (Channelers, Druids)
  - [[Ulak of Unfeigned Madness]] (Beastlords, Brawlers)
