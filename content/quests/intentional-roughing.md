## Steps

1. Complete the following objectives, in no particular order:
   - Kill [[An exiled Rilissian bandit|exiled Rilissian bandits]] for a uncommon update. {{waypoint -20, -95, -516}}
   - Pick up an [[Elvish fashioned sword]]. {{waypoint 24.16, -96.22, -498.28}}
   - Gather a [[Book of elvish tactics]] from one of the following locations (can be tracked):
     - {{waypoint 18.79, -95.61, -476.01}}
     - {{waypoint -11.61, -94.54, -555.49}}
     - {{waypoint -212.51, -34.12, -367.57}}
1. Return to [[Auleren Faithheart]] in [[Omen's Call]].
