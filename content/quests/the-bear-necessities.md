## Description

The Hua Mein Craftsman has requested the assistance of a fellow Craftsman in crafting some supplies for the Hua Mein to use. He has warned me that non-craftsmen will not be able to complete this assignment.

## Steps

1. I have been asked to craft some items to replenish the supplies of the Hua Mein village.
   - **Carpenter:** Craft some [[Regal spuncloth rug|regal spuncloth rugs]] (0/5). Items needed for 5 combines:
     - 10 [[Bamboo shoot]]
     - 15 [[Sumac lumber]]
     - 10 [[Mottled pelt]]
     - 25 [[Ethereal sandpaper]]
     - [[Woodworking Table]]
   - **Woodworker:** Craft some [[Sumac knotted club|sumac knotted clubs]] (0/5). Items needed for 5 combines:
     - 20 [[Sumac lumber]]
     - 10 [[Mottled pelt]]
     - 5 [[Bamboo shoot]]
     - 25 [[Ethereal sandpaper]]
     - [[Woodworking Table]]
   - **Provisioner:** Craft some [[Palmfruit Cider|palmfruit cider]] (0/10). Items needed for 5 combines:
     - 5 [[Kejekan palmfruit]]
     - 10 [[Bamboo shoot]]
     - 5 [[Liquid]]
     - 15 [[Ethereal kindling]]
     - [[Stove and Keg]]
1. Return to [[Xi Lan]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,000 faction with **Hua Mein**
- +2,500 faction with **Hua Mein Craftsmen**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Hua Mein Daily Supply Tasks
