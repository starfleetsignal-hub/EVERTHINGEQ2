## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- You must have +10,000 faction with the Court of Truth to unlock this quest. Once +20,000 faction has been achieved, this quest will no longer be available.

## Steps

![Shiny Debris harvest points in The Sinking Sands.](images/Shiny_Debris_Sinking_Sands.jpg)

1. Collect ancient baubles that are harvested from 'shiny debris' (ground spawn) around various ruins in [[The Stilled Sea]]. {{waypoint -980, -98, -890}}.
   - If you don't collect an 'ancient bauble' when picking up shiny debris, a non-stackable item called [[Worthless junk]] will be placed into your inventory.
1. Kill undead in the Stilled Seas for 5 uncommon updates.
1. Climb down into [[The Eye of Anuk]] {{waypoint -649, -141, -1189}} in the Sinking Sands and search for 2 runes to be transcribed.
   - It's easier to enter the cave entrance for [[The Silent City (POI)|The Silent City]] from [[The Bay of Anuk]].
   - Inspect *an ancient inscribed pillar" {{waypoint -973, -233, -1203}} and "an ancient tablet" {{waypoint -923, -223, -1216}}.
1. Return to Taj As'ad.

## Rewards

- At least 8g 57s
- -7500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- +7500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -7500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
