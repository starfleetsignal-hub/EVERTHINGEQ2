## Notes

- This is the access quest for [[The Forbidden Sepulcher]].
- [[An unresponsive barbarian]] can be in severall places in [[Jagged Plains]]; one is near [[Bitterwind Trench]] {{waypoint 200, 0, -550}}, the other is near [[The Forbidden Sepulcher]] {{waypoint 100, 0, -700}}, and the other is at {{waypoint 118, 21, -614}}

## Steps

- Speak to [[Haijakt]] on the beach near [[Everstorm Bay]]. {{waypoint 215, 0, 50}}
- Enter [[The Forbidden Sepulcher]] using the door at the northwest corner of [[Jagged Plains]]. {{waypoint 137.62, 21.29, -658.96}}
- Slay [[Countess Satrinah]]. She is spawned by killing the [[Disciple of Satrinah]] and nearby prophetesses in the last chamber of the instance.
- Return to [[Haijakt]] to complete the quest and choose your reward.
