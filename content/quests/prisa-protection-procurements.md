## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
  - Quest becomes repeatable after initial turn in. No Scarred Dinar on repeated quests.

## Steps

1. Examine her component baskets to get your list of components.
1. Hunt and gather the followingThey are along the south shore and could include:
   - Hunt either
     - **ulthork tusks**  x 12   {{waypoint -139, 14, 768}} or
     - **rotbone tail clubs** x 12  kill a rotbone stalkers around {{waypoint -236, 51, 639}} for updates
   - Gather either
     - **rime vines**  x 10  {{waypoint -168, 15, 760}} blue vines on the ground
     - **icy clam shells** x 10  {{waypoint -226.13, 12.84, 768.35}}
1. Return to Prisa and place the items in the basket next to her {{waypoint 78.20, 8.83, 610.83}}

## Reward

- [[Ruddy Othmir Jar]] house item
- 40 x [[Scarred Dinar]] *Only the first time*
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
