This quest is repeatable.

## Notes

Used for players wanting to raise their standing with The Dreadnaughts.

This is NOT an unlimited repeatable quest. You can complete it around 5 times and then it disappears. I waited more than 1 day to see if there was a quest timer but there isn´t. (28/11/2024).

## Steps

- Kill any of the following:
  - 6 [[A Black Magi anarchist|Black Magi anarchists]] {{waypoint -6, -2, 76}}
  - 6 [[A Black Magi dissident|Black Magi dissidents]] {{waypoint -6, -2, 76}}
  - 6 [[A Black Magi fanatic|Black Magi fanatics]] {{waypoint -6, -2, 76}}
  - 6 [[A Black Magi insurgent|Black Magi insurgents]] {{waypoint -6, -2, 76}}
  - 6 [[A Dervish crook|Dervish crooks]] {{waypoint -101, -4, 112}}
  - 6 [[A Dervish crook|Dervish hoodlums]] {{waypoint -81, -4, 53}}
- Return to [[Trainer Durbok]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 6s 28c
- +150 faction with **The Dreadnaughts**
