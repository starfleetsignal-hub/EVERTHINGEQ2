## Notes

- Upon accepting the quest, you will be automatically ported to [[The Tribunal: Sacred Temple of the Hammer]].

## Steps

1. Speak with [[The Seventh Hammer]].
1. Speak with [[The Defendant]], [[The Defender]], [[The Prosecutor]], all of the [[A witness|witnesses]], and review the evidence on the table in the middle of the room. Try and deduce if the defendant is innocent or guilty.
1. Once you have formulated your verdict, deliver it to the Seventh Hammer.
   - Click [[#Correct Verdict|here]] to reveal the answer.
1. Return to [[Justinian Theo]]. Clicking the door near where you zoned-in will teleport you back to [[Chrykori Village]] in [[Timorous Deep]].

## Correct Verdict

| Warning: Spoiler! |
|---|
| The defendant is innocent. Select one of the answers that proves his innocence. |

- 1p 30g
