## Notes

- You must understand the [[Orcish]] language

## Steps

1. Investigate the area around [[Thrael'Gorr Camp]] and kill the level 92 [[A Duhjalm spy|Duhjalm spies]] that spawn for updates:
   - First Location: {{waypoint 182, -131, 2481}}
   - Second Location: {{waypoint -133, -120, 2381}}
   - Third Location: {{waypoint -415, -195, 2289}}
1. Return to [[Chief Thrael'Gorr]] to complete the quest.

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,500 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Bo Staff]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Greataxe]]  (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Greatstaff]] (All Mages)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Oracle's Blessed Maul]] (All Priests)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Basher]] (All Fighters, all Scouts)
