## Steps

1. Travel to the [[Thrael'Gorr Camp]] {{waypoint 335.06, -64.76, 2625.72}} and speak with Chief Thrael'Gorr {{waypoint 365.25, -61.65, 2640.44}}.
   - Kill 10 sabertooths for pelts (any snowmane counts).
   - Gather 5 bones from mammoths (kill any mammoth for update except baby ones).
1. Return to Chief Thrael'Gorr.
