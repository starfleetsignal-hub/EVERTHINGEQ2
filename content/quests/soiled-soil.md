## Steps

1. Speak to [[Naisha Vallinasis]] at {{waypoint -67.12, 44.65, -221.90}} Copy
1. Gather 5 soil samples from around the teleportation spires. These are greenish mounds with a blue sparkle effect.
1. Click on the pot to put the soil in, and speak with [[Naisha Vallinasis|Naisha]].
1. Obtain 3 obol dreadleaf seeds. [[An obol dreadleaf|Obol dreadleaves]] are shamblers found southwest of the camp. This is an uncommon auto-update.
1. Click on the pot again to plant the seeds.
1. Speak to [[Naisha Vallinasis|Naisha]].
1. Obtain 3 obol shambler seeds. [[An obol shambler|Obol shamblers]] are shamblers found south of the camp. This is an uncommon auto-update.
1. Click on the pot again to plant the seeds.
1. Speak to [[Naisha Vallinasis|Naisha]]. Wait for her to cast on the pot, then speak to her again.
1. Kill the shambler.
1. Speak one last time to [[Naisha Vallinasis|Naisha]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
