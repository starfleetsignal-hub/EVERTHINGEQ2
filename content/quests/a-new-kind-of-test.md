## Starting the Quest

The quest givers are located outside the entry portals to the Proving Grounds:

- [[Freeport]]: speak to [[Attendant Ulrein]] in North Freeport across from the T2 guildhall {{waypoint -53.66, -20.05, -266.28}}.
- [[Frostfang Sea]]: speak to [[Attendant Ra'Leian]] on the docks near [[New Halas]] {{waypoint -337.42, 30.68, 224.07}}.
- [[Gorowyn]]: speak to [[Steward Velanell]] on the [[Timorous Deep]] docks {{waypoint 2409.08, 18.68, 1399.91}}.
- [[Greater Faydark]]: speak to [[Attendant Larinia]] in the city of [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 311.41, 109.04, 138.88}}
- [[Neriak]]: speak to [[Attendant Oln]] near the gate to [[Timorous Deep]] {{waypoint 44.52, 17.93, 19.91}}.
- [[Qeynos]]: speak to [[Attendant Olhen]] in [[Qeynos Harbor]] near the [[The Clock of Ak'Anon|Clock of Ak'Anon]] {{waypoint 742.66, -20.69, -71.36}}.

## Steps

1. Complete a [[Proving Ground]] match (group or solo).
