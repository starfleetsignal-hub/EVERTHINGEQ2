## Steps

1. Speak to [[Alexandria Tal`Azroth]] in [[The City of Freeport|South Freeport]]. {{waypoint -123, -18, 294}}
1. Speak to [[Qalantir]] on the first floor of the [[Academy of Arcane Science]] in [[The City of Freeport|North Freeport]].
1. Enter [[The Thieves' Way]] and slay five [[A diseased ratonga|diseased ratonga]]s. {{waypoint -161, 1, 178}}
1. Return to Qalantir.

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
