## Prerequisites

- You  need access to Scars of Destruction expansion to do this quest
- You need to be either level 130 adventurer or level 130 crafter to enter [[Sodden Archipelago]], certain progress in either signature line is required to open access to [[Western Wastes]].

## Starting the Quest

- The Guides in EQ2 are volunteers, they are not always online and not always offering this particular quest
- The Guide will announce themselves in General chat channel and specify which zone to find them at to get this quest, this can vary by server (Great Divide, Everfrost so far)
- Their announcement hints about travelling to new zones and to see the Guide about a small task before going there.

## Steps

1. Go to Sodden Archipelago and visit the following locations in any order:
   - Dankmar Swamp  {{waypoint 405, 15, 735}}
   - Blackhook Gate   {{waypoint 137, 137, 72}}
   - Blackhook Bastion  {{waypoint 272, 272, -680}} (you can carefully skirt around the two gates to get to this location)
   - Khaghar  {{waypoint -630, 139, -285}} (note you cannot COV to this location as of 1/8/2025)
   - Den Mother's Altar  {{waypoint -704, 159, -289}}
   - Deepwater Watch  {{waypoint -537, 18, 621}}
   - Strongswell Lighthouse   {{waypoint -596, 44, 778}}
1. **Then find and kill any overland named or its placeholder in this zone**
1. Go to Western Wastes and visit the following locations in any order:
   - Charnel Scar {{waypoint -170, -28, 374}}
   - Dragon Necropolis  {{waypoint -736, 67, 296}}
   - Frozen Nestlands   {{waypoint 483, 64, 406}}
   - Chilblain Field   {{waypoint 83, 52, 76}}
   - Icespine   {{waypoint 463.79, 131.24, -409.48}}
   - Warming Warfields  {{waypoint -213, -14, -491}}
   - Wreck of the Swordrattler   {{waypoint 712.98, 5.98, -685.70}}
1. **Then find and kill any overland named or its placeholder in this zone**
