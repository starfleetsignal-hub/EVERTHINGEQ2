## Notes

- This quest is repeatable up to 10 times.. However, upon completion of the master quest, [[Never Double Cross a Barbarian]], Cornail will offer this quest again as an unlimited repeatable.
- This quest has three stages: level 32, 36 and 40. Each stage must be completed 10 times before the next will be made available. The quests of the final stage, level 40, have a cool-down of one hour before the next quest can be offered.
- If you desire a different target monster, delete and reacquire the quest.

## Level 32

- Slay a number of Orcs in Zek, randomly assigned from this list (there is no cool-down timer on this stage):
  - 10 [[A Tallon catapult operator|Tallon catapult operators]]
  - 12 [[A Tallon grunt|Tallon grunts]]
  - 6 [[A Tallon raider|Tallon raiders]]
  - 6 [[A Tallon spotter|Tallon spotters]]
- Reward: At least 69s 67c

## Level 36

- Slay a number of Orcs in Zek, randomly assigned from this list (there is no cool-down timer on this stage):
  - 4 [[Vallon centurion|Vallon centurions]] - *found in Blood Ore Valley* {{waypoint 352, 12, -170}}
  - 8 [[A Vallon quarry elite|Vallon quarry elites]]
  - 10 [[A Vallon quarry sentry|Vallon quarry sentries]]
  - 10 [[A Vallon miner|Vallon miners]]
  - 6 [[A Vallon undersmith|Vallon undersmiths]] {{waypoint 360, -124, -46}}
- Reward: At least 53s 57c

## Level 40

- Slay a number of Orcs in Zek, randomly assigned from this list (there is a cool-down timer of 60 minutes from quest completion to when Cornail will offer the quest again):
  - 8 [[A Deathfist toiler|Deathfist toilers]] {{waypoint -191, -29, -512}}
  - 8 [[A Sullon elite|Sullon elites]] {{waypoint -316, -28, -472}}
  - 10 [[A Sullon lumberjack|Sullon lumberjacks]] {{waypoint -382, -28, -300}}
- Reward: At least 2g 99s 88c
