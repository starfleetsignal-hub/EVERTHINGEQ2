## Steps

1. I must find the newly established treasure hunter entrance into the subterranean sections of Kaladim in Butcherblock Mountains.
   - Enter the vaults under the city of Kaladim
     - Click the piles of rubble and shovels {{waypoint -380, 158, -523}} in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] to enter the [[Hammerhall Barrel Chambers]]
1. I am to search for anything of great importance to the dwarven people within the Hammerhall Barrel Chambers.
   - Explore Hammerhall Barrel Chambers
     - Throughout the zone, there are roaming mobs that scale to your level, and attackable wooden barrels and wooden crates, which may spawn additional mobs, or offer coin.
     - At some point, it will be announced that 'You hear the sound of massive doors opening, scraping stone against stone!'  That will open the previously sealed door at {{waypoint 197, 17, -89}}.
     - Upon entering the room, [[Brewmaster Thifgur]] will become attackable.
1. Defeat Brewmaster Thifgur
1. Look for anything of great dwarven importance
   - Click on the Hammerstein on the table at {{waypoint 195, 18, -99}}
1. I must return to [[Steinsister Daglara]] {{waypoint 336, 189, 502}}, a cleric of Serilis, in Butcherblock Mountains.

  - [[Hammerstein]]
