## Prerequisites

- You must be at least adventure level 75 to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Kill 12 [[A Dracurion hoplite|Dracurion hoplites]]. {{waypoint -575, -35, -90}}
1. Give the [[Grisly sack of Droag heads]] to [[Lance Corporal Sonoriss]] at the [[Upper Command Camp]]. {{waypoint -140, -16, 180}}

## Rewards

- 1p 10g
- +31,500 faction with **Legion of Danak**
