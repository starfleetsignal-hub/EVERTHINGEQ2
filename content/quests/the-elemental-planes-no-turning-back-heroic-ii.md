The bulk of the text of Volume II of The Planar Chronicle details some of the more dangerous regions of the Elemental Planes. I better study this thoroughly...

## Steps

*See the (Solo) versions for the zone walkthroughs:*

- Complete this chapter on [Heroic II] missions:
  - Defeat the Swift Guardian in [[Eryslai: The Midnight Aerie (Expert)]]
  - Destroy the Earthen Leaders in [[Vegarlson: Council's Stronghold (Expert)]]
  - Extinguish the Fire Lord in [[Doomfire: Elements of Rage (Expert)]]
  - Slay the All-Mother in [[Awuidor: Marr's Ascent (Expert)]]
