## Steps

1. Travel to the [[Qeynos Province District]] and kill [[Marvan Honeyjum]] who will spawn near the bank in [[Qeynos Harbor (POI)|Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 700, -20, -50}}.
1. Return to [[Gol M'Tun]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- AA Experience
  - +3000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
