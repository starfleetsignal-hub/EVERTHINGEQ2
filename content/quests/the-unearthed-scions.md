## Steps

1. Seek out and destroy the four Scions. Although the quest still refers to requiring the pulling of bell ringers to summon the various scions, they're now 100% spawns with a roughly 15 minute respawn time and the bell ringers are no longer needed.
   - [[Archiovianix the Scion of Darkness]]: Spawns at [[The Chessboard (Stormhold)|The Chessboard]] on the first floor. {{waypoint 73, 5, -96}}
   - [[Hephinntari the Scion of Destruction]]: Spawns in [[The Atrium]] on the first sub-level. {{waypoint -73, -24, -98}}
   - [[Qelinatias the Scion of Pain]]: Spawns in the room with the entrance to the [[The Library (Stormhold)|Stormhold Library]] on the first sub-level. {{waypoint -7, -25, -121}}
   - [[Tumarinan the Scion of Suffering]]: Spawns at the bottom of the well in [[The Well Room]]. {{waypoint 29, -48, 13}}
1. Return to the *Ruined Altar of Karana*, located at {{waypoint -17.7, 14.53, -126.06}} for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 96s 67c
- [[Circlet of Storms]]
