## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of Bertoxxulous has asked for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Collect [[Infectious Wraps|infectious wraps]] from creatures found in [[Zimara Breadth]] 0/3.
   - These drop in small chests from : an endless sufferer, an agonizing incarnation, on The Lamentations island in Zimara Breadth {{waypoint 257, 254, -649}}
1. Gather or purchase [[Corymbia Wood]] found in fallen branch harvest nodes in Ballads of Zimara areas 0/10
   - *Common harvest from animal dens*
   - *Can buy off broker*
   - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to [[Herald of the Plaguelord]] {{waypoint -1157.19, -33.96, 525.38}} in [[Antonica]]
