I've met a ratonga in [[West Freeport]] by the name of Varski. He claims to be a fan of mine and through much persuasion, he's offered me a suggestion on something I can find in [[Fallen Gate]]. If I bring these items back to him, he claims he can make them into something else.

## Steps

1. I need to kill the undead raiders until I find a stoppered clay pot.
   - Kill [[A tortured raider|tortured raiders]] until the quest updates.
1. Now I need to find some kind of brass lever on the raiders. I have no idea why these zombies would have something like that on them, so I hope Varski knows what he's talking about.
   - Kill some more [[A tortured raider|tortured raiders]] until quest updates.
1. I need to return to [[Varski]] in [[West Freeport]] and see what he makes from the clay pot and the lever.
   - Speak to [[Varski]] in [[West Freeport]].
