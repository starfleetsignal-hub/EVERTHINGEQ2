## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Enter the Mines of Meldrath: Gears of Intrigue at {{waypoint 255, 24, 312}}  ![](images/Clearing_the_Gears_quest_-_Meldraths'_documents.jpg)
1. Battle through 12 Bloodhorns, Darkblades and [[Clockwork|clockworks]]
1. Collect 12 of Meldrath's equations and studies (located throughout the zone, and especially around the pool)
1. Defeat [[Terrorhoof]]

## Rewards

- +1,750 faction with **Open Hand**
