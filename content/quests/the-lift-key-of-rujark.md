## Steps

1. Find and kill [[The Overseer (The Clefts of Rujark)|the Overseer]]. {{waypoint 7, -5, -74}}
   - You need to actually have the quest to obtain the key from the Overseer - it's an auto-update and not a physical drop.
1. Return to the button and examine it. {{waypoint -5, -2, 46}}

## Rewards

- Experience
- Access to upper (heroic) levels and instances
