![Path to run](images/TowerRun.jpg)
It is said that Emperor Fyst would promote his scouts if they could run the citadel's towers in under 2 minutes, and kill any who failed. I'm sure I can run it faster. Hopefully I won't die if I fall.

## Notes

- ***Note:** This is a 2 minute timed quest.*
- You will be running to the towers on the battlement walls.
- You can easily complete the course in about a minute as long as you take a couple of practice runs first and are familiar with the route
- Going to 1st person view seems to help
- Don't forget to click on the throne after the last tower!

## Steps

1. Run to the Southwest tower:
   - 3rd floor
   - 2nd floor
   - 1st floor
1. Run to the Southeast tower
   - 1st floor
   - 2nd floor
   - 3rd floor
1. Run to the East tower
   - 3rd floor
   - 2nd floor
   - 1st floor
1. Run to the Northeast tower
   - 1st floor
   - 2nd floor
   - 3rd floor
1. Run to the Northwest tower
   - 3rd floor
   - 2nd floor
   - 1st floor
1. Run to the West tower
   - 1st floor
   - 2nd floor
   - 3rd floor
1. Run back and click on the throne to complete

## Rewards

- At least 1p 51g 20s 12,500 status
- [[Dirt encrusted key]]
