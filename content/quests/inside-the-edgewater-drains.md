## Steps

1. Collect 5 items laying around in the sewers (all look like small tables with mechanical parts on them):
   - nesting cogwheels - top level east of Bendik {{waypoint 15, 32, -98}}
   - rusted iron bracket - middle level down a branch hallway on the east side {{waypoint 30, 16, 65}}
   - length of corroded chain - middle level down a branch hallway on the west side {{waypoint 163, 16, 56}}
   - ancient sieve - middle level in room next to south stairs {{waypoint 92.39, 16.66, 97.76}}
   - some copper wire - underwater on bottom level in room next to south stairs {{waypoint 85, 0, 105}}
1. Return to Bendik to talk.
1. Kill [[A Murkwater henchman|Murkwater henchmen]] to find a key (uncommon update).
1. Enter [[The Murkwater Nook]] - top level down hallway from room next to south stairs {{waypoint 29, 32, 73}}.
1. Kill [[Madame Kisa]].
