## Steps

1. Collect 3 stashed components, barrels are marked 'a Brotherhood cache', from around the city:
   - {{waypoint 15, 2, 22}}
   - {{waypoint 11, 4, 4}}
   - {{waypoint -1, 4, -18}}
1. Return to [[Lieutenant Cervaen]].  He will give you 'a small explosive device'.
1. Go to the eastern end of the northern half of Beggar's Court to blow up the supplies.
   - Three barrels are located at {{waypoint -19, 3, -24}}
   - Right-click and place the explosives for each barrel.
1. Return to [[Delcairn Sebastian]].

- 1s 12c

  - [[Mark of Perfection]]
