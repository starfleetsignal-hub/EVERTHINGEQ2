## Steps

1. Drinal's Steward has sent me to the town of Cardin Ward within [[Obol Plains]], to assist them in their battles against the [[Lore and Legend: Harrowing Horde|Harrowing Horde]].
   1. Explore [[Cardin Ward]]
      - Receive the update when approaching [[Firiona Vie]] {{waypoint -365, 14, -26}}
   1. Help [[Firiona Vie]] in her struggle to aid the Cardin Ward townsfolk
      - Complete the quest from [[Firiona Vie]], [[A Harrowing Experience]]
1. **Complete this two quests together as they involve killing the same mobs.** I should assist the town of Cardin Ward in any way I can, as they struggle against the Harrowing Horde.
   - Provide aid to [[General Furtog Ogrebane]] {{waypoint -455, 10, 14}}
     - Complete the questline, starting with [[Ethereal Material (Quest)]]
   - Help [[Galen Cadabrak]] {{waypoint -460, 15, 84}}
     - Complete the questline, starting with [[Constructing Cardin Wardens]]
1. I must catch up with [[Firiona Vie]] at the Incantatory Apex encampment, in [[Obol Plains]].
   - At this point, you need to complete the subquest [[A Trusted Witness]] from [[Firiona Vie]] {{waypoint -365, 14, -26}}.  Once you have completed the subquest, this quest will update.
   - Report back to [[Firiona Vie]] {{waypoint -92, 44, -220}}
1. I must report back to Drinal's Steward now that I have identified that the Harrowing Horde have taken control of the teleportation spires being constructed in Ethernere.
   - Speak with [[Drinal's Steward]]
     - Use the "Drinalian Whisper Column" {{waypoint -73, 44, -235}} in the same encampment where you turned in the subquest
1. I must venture to the teleportation spires at the Incantatory Apex within Obol Plains.
   - Return to the teleportation spires at the Incantatory Apex spires back at {{waypoint 82, 38, -342}}
     - The spires will be activated when you arrive.
1. I must report back to Drinal's Steward now that I have seen the Gehein of the Harrowing Horde open up a tourbillion at the teleportation spires at the Incantatory Apex!
   - Speak with [[Drinal's Steward]]
     - Use the column in the Incantatory Apex camp.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
