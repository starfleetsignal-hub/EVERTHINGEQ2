## Steps

1. Retrieve 8 ***slug mucus*** by killing [[Auroric slug|Auroric slugs]] {{waypoint 595.32, 24.22, -781.34}}.![House Vahla Components](images/House_Vahla_Components.jpg)
1. Set the trap on the ***House Vahla Components*** {{waypoint -248.35, 76.62, -45.31}} in the northwestern [[Kandra Uplands]].
1. Return to [[Shirada]] {{waypoint -151.42, 573.32, -551.04}} in [[Khali'Vahla]].
1. Return to the *decoy components* {{waypoint -248.35, 76.62, -45.31}} and kill 8 of the nearby [[Captured alux|captured]] and [[Frenzied alux|frenzied]] alux, and [[Frenzied siamang|siamang]] thieves.
1. Return once again to [[Shirada]] {{waypoint -151.42, 573.32, -551.04}}.
