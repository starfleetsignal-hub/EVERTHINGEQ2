## Notes

This quest is offered to you only if you belong to the Kerran race. For other races, speaking to Hitus gives you the quest [[Nom Noms]].

## Steps

1. Gather 6 catnip from catnip bushes around Kerra Isle.
1. Return the catnips to [[Hitus Sharpclaw]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 20g 5s 35c
- +100 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- One of the following based on class:
  - [[Wristlet of Clerical Precision]]
  - [[Wristlet of the Sneak]]
  - [[Bangle of the Enduring Challenge]]
  - [[Wristlet of Blocking Fists]]
  - [[Wristlet of the Strongarm]]
  - [[Wristlet of the Onset]]
  - [[Wristlet of Elemental Command]]
  - [[Wristlet of the Reach]]
