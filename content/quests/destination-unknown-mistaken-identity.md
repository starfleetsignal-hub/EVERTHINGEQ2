## Notes

- When you accept quest you receive [[Recipe Scroll: Gazer Gala Goodies]] and various recipes that are autoscribed.
- This quest requires:
  - 5x [[Formless Coal]], everything else is provided.
- There is a [[Varig's Forge]] all-in-one crafting station at {{waypoint 169, 77, -160}} in [[Gardens of Industry]] camp.

## Steps

1. Click one of Banquet Supplies containers around [[Oogothl Sprawl Obelisk]]. These are the sparkling crates at {{waypoint 144, 65, -512}}, {{waypoint 125, 65, -494}}, {{waypoint 128, 65, -495}} to receive [[Gazer Banquet Bundle]], it contains all the required ingredients and fuel.
   - Unpack a [[Gazer Banquet Bundle]] in your inventory. This will require you to have 11 vacant inventory slots plus at least 1 more to start crafting.
   - Using ingredients and fuel in the Gazer Banquet Bundle craft various items. If you fail to craft something and need more ingredients you can click another crate and unpack another bundle.
1. Craft:
   - 8x (4 combines) [[Gooey Netherbian Surprise]] at a summoned [[Stove & Keg]] or the Forge, etc.
   - 8x (4 combines) [[Sludgewater Fizz]] at a summoned [[Stove & Keg]] or the Forge, etc.
   - 1x [[Embroidered Tablecloth]] at a summoned [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]] or the Forge, etc.
   - 4x [[Elaborate Candelabra]] at a summoned [[Work Bench]] or the Forge, etc.
   - 4X [[Silver Platter]] at a summoned [[Work Bench]] or the Forge, etc.
   - 4X [[Embellished Drinking Bowl]] at a summoned [[Work bench]] or the Forge, etc.
   - 4x [[Fancy Silk Pillow]] at a summoned [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]] or the Forge, etc.
1. Place the completed items in crate labeled "Banquet Items" in front of [[Graz'lam]] at {{waypoint 132, 64, -485}}. Accept the [[Recipe Scroll: W.H.E.R.E. V2]] which autoscribes in your recipe book.
1. Craft [[W.H.E.R.E. V2]] using your [[W.H.E.R.E. V1]], an [[Odd Rock]] you should still have from unpacking [[Gazer Banquet Bundle]] and 5 [[Formless Coal]].
   - NOTE: If you have lost your W.H.E.R.E. V1 return to the banquet supplies crate to retrieve another.
1. Speak to [[Flamebearer Denat]] who materializes at Varig's Forge at {{waypoint 169, 76, -164}} in the [[Oogothl Sprawl]]
   - **NOTE**: [[Flamebearer Denat]] in [[Yon Gorroth]] will not progress/complete this quest.
