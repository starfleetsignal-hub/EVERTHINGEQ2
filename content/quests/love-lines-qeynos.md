## Notes

- This quest is repeatable once per year.
- When you speak to the target NPCs during the quest, if you flirt correctly, a large heart appears over their head, swelling and growing. If your game is weak, the heart that appears above their head snaps in two and disappears.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Brandi Bender]], inside [[The Qeynos Hold]]. {{waypoint 281, -18, -121}}
1. Speak with [[Rex Ironforge]], inside [[The Jewel Box]]. {{waypoint 389, -19, -231}}
1. Speak with [[Priestess Alena the Chaste]] at the [[Temple of Life]]. {{waypoint 506, -32, -199}}
1. Speak with [[Salty Joe]] on the docks in the moat near the Qeynos Castle. {{waypoint 427, -27, -48}}
1. Return to Dalron Pinot.
1. Speak with [[Christina Halfsocks]]. {{waypoint 324, -21, -29}}
1. Return to Dalron Pinot with Christina's message.
