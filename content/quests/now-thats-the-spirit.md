## Steps

1. Use the Shamanic Spirit Fetish item that Finze gives you on the rampaging deinodons.
1. Attack rampaging deinodons. Get them to about below 40% health and use the Shamanic Spirit Fetish.  Do this 5 times.
- note: UNequip any more advanced weapons that proc, make use of a lower level (e.g. 80) weapon as to not kill the dinos too quickly.
1. Return to [[Finze]]
