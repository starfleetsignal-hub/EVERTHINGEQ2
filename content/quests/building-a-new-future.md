## Steps

1. Collect raw material to build permanent structures
   - 10 masonry from around Malvonicus' Tower (looks like flat green rocks)![Usable Masonry](images/Usable_Masonry.jpg)
   - 10 lumber from Razortooth Bay (looks like large wooden planks - they seem to be most common around the sunken shrine)![Usable Lumber](images/Usable_Lumber.jpg)
   - 10 scavenged parts from around Malvonicus' Tower (looks like tiny broken pillars)![Usable Parts](images/Usable_Parts.jpg)
1. Deliver the material to Aillena Belzia

Note! If you get attacked while harvesting, you can pop into the cellar in the tower to break the encounter without losing progress. For the lumber, it's in the water. If you get attacked, climb out of the water onto land to break the encounter.
