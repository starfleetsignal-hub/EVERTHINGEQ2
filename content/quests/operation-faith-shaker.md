## Notes

When accepting quest you are given [[Dismal Rage Disguise]] a troll illusion item that only works in zones related to this quest.<br>
Witnessing the speech **roots you in place and dismounts you**, remember to mount up after leaving the instance.

## Steps

1. Equip the [[Dismal Rage Disguise]] - find this item in your inventory, right click and use to get illusion.
1. Infiltrate the Grozmok's congregation at the Temple of War while wearing the disguise - go to North Freeport and click on door at {{waypoint -117, -8, -158}} to enter The Dismal Den.
   - the disguise **DOES NOT** allow you to travel safely through Freeport if you are good aligned and under level 60, use [[The Thieves' Way]] (Freeport sewers) entrance in South Freeport at {{waypoint -217, -55, 240}} to go to North Freeport and mind the posted and wandering guards. Anyone over level 60 can safely run to North Freeport using the spiral ramp at {{waypoint -158, -54, -37}}
1. Place curse upon the Grozmok stone - click the floating stone at {{waypoint -80, -7, -166}}
1. Join the Grozmok's congregation - go down the steps on the floor among other NPCs, closer to the balcony (you will be rooted in place for this part)
1. Witness the Grozmok's address
1. Return to [[Jackal]] in [[Block and Tackle Storerooms]] when the speech is finished and you can move again.

## Rewards

- 3 x [[Block Market Token|Block Market Tokens]]
- this quest completes achievement [[Paths of Sedition (Achievement)|Paths of Sedition]]
