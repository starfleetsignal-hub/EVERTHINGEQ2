## Notes

When accepting quest you are given [[Dismal Rage Disguise]] a troll illusion item that only works in zones related to this quest.

## Steps

1. Equip the [[Dismal Rage Disguise]] - find this item in your inventory, right click and use to get illusion.
1. Infiltrate the Grozmok's congregation at the Temple of War while wearing the disguise - go to North Freeport and click on door at {{waypoint -117, -8, -158}} to enter The Dismal Den (Dismiss mercenary to be able to click on door)
   - the disguise allows you to travel safely through Freeport even if you are good aligned.
1. Place curse upon the Grozmok stone - floating stone at {{waypoint -80, -7, -166}}
1. Join the Grozmok's congregation - go down the steps on the floor among other NPCs (you will be dismounted and rooted in place for this part, remember to mount up again later)
1. Witness the Grozmok's address
1. Return to [[Jackal]] in [[Block and Tackle Storerooms]] when the speech is finished.

## Rewards

- 3 x [[Block Market Token|Block Market Tokens]]
- this quest completes achievement [[Paths of Sedition (Achievement)|Paths of Sedition]]
