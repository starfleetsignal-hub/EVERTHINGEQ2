## Steps

1. Create the warm komodo meals (6)
   - Scribe the [[Recipe: Warm Komodo Meal]]
   - Craft 6 [[Warm Komodo Meal]] at the cookpot {{waypoint 736, -160, 233}} with the following materials and fuel:
     - 6 [[Flying fish]]
     - 6 [[Hanging root]]
     - 12 [[Scintillating kindling]]
   - Note: if you're already a 125 level crafter with Blueprint ability - after you make the first one, you can use the Blueprint feature to insta-complete the remaining five.
1. Return to Shio

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- At least 2g 16s 26c
