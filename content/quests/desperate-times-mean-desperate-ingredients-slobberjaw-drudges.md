## Steps

1. Slay 8 [[A slobberjaw drudge|slobberjaw drudges]] near the [[Erudin Gardens]]. {{waypoint -390, 480, -290}}

## Rewards

- At least 46g

## Notes

This quest can be repeated and completed a total of four times.

There are several 89 heroic ^^ [[An Erudian slaver]] that roam the area and one named 89 heroic ^^^ called [[Xxuilz the Punisher]] {{waypoint -428.93, 472.57, -362.04}}, the easiest way is to get up on the outer wall and kill from there, no adds.

Because Xxuilz has a short respawn timer (~5-7 min) and the fact that he gives an ornate chest (sometimes exquisite) when he is dropped, he is regularly camped by plat farmers.
