## Steps

1. Head to the [[The Village of Shin|Isle of Mara]] and find [[Khrolan Tiu]]. To get this update, speak to Khrolan's brother, [[Aldolan Tiu]], on the fourth floor of the [[Tower of the Four Winds]].
   - As of 4/2/14, [[Aldon Tiu]] will no longer talk to you if you do not have access to the fourth floor.
     - *The access questline starts here: [[Improving Relations with the Locals]]*
1. Speak with the following monks:
   - Freeport Monk - [[Chaoxing]] in [[The City of Freeport]] (West Freeport) at {{waypoint 234, -3, 132}}
   - Qeynos Monk - [[Bai Hui]] in [[Qeynos Province District]] (The Elddar Grove) at {{waypoint 774, -19, -387}}
   - Cobalt Scar Monk - [[Shaoking]] in [[Cobalt Scar]] (West of Scarstone) at {{waypoint 4537, -823, 459}}
   - Withered Lands Monk - [[Fan Liang]] within [[Village of Alivan]] in [[The Withered Lands]] at {{waypoint -385, -699, 457}}
   - Thurgadin Monk - [[An Bo]] in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]] (The Icebridge Crossway) at {{waypoint 677, -249, 133}}
   - New Halas Monk - [[Meili]] in [[Frostfang Sea]] (New Halas Fighter's Guild) at {{waypoint 130, 157, -154}}
   - Siren's Grotto Monk - [[Li Nuo]] at right behind queen in [[Siren's Grotto: Tavalan Abyss (Challenge Heroic)]] Zone. {{waypoint -94, 7, -909}}
     - *Note: You can use a cleared instance to get this update.*
1. Return to Aldolan in the [[The Village of Shin|Village of Shin]].
1. Find Khrolan Tiu
   - Travel to [[Temple of Veeshan: Laboratory of Mutation (Heroic)]] and speak to Khrolan Tiu at {{waypoint -1868, 315, 745}}.
     - *Note: You can use a cleared instance to get this update.*
   - Kill the Aerakyn who spawn.
1. Go to [[Eidolon Jungle]] (Lightshadow Undergrowth from the portal is closest) and speak to Khrolan Tiu at {{waypoint 12.59, -5.35, 510}}
   - Kill Horde members in [[Eidolon Jungle]] until you receive 30 memories.
   - Kill Horde members [[Obol Plains]] until you receive 30 memories.
   - Kill Horde members [[Harrow's End: Baleful Dominion (Heroic)]] until you receive 20 memories.
1. Return to Khrolan Tiu in Eidolon Jungle.
1. Return to Aldolan on the [[The Village of Shin|Isle of Mara]].
1. Go to the [[Forsaken City]]
   - *Note: You will get attacked as soon as you zone in so be sure to zone after your tank. Dont forget that the revive point is just near the door **Use invis before zoning in. You can use invis all the way to the name!!***
   - Fight your way through the Aerakyn to {{waypoint 52.59, -58.06, 97.69}}
   - After you kill Tendrix a chest will spawn, click it to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- [[Zlandicar's Heart (House Item)]] - A house item version of one of the equipable rewards that can be converted between the different appearances.
  - When you examine it can change form into
    - shield - [[Zlandicar's Tortured Heart (House Item)]]
    - dagger - [[Zlandicar's Pierced Heart (House Item)]]
    - claw - [[Zlandicar's Riven Heart (House Item)]]
    - fundamental - [[Zlandicar's Heart (House Item)]]
- One of the following, by class:
  - [[Zlandicar's Tortured Heart]] (Plate Fighter)
  - [[Zlandicar's Riven Heart]] (Brawler)
  - [[Zlandicar's Pierced Heart]] (Scout)
  - [[Zlandicar's Punctured Heart]] (Scout)
  - [[Zlandicar's Heart (Item)|Zlandicar's Heart]] (Mage/Priest)
  - [[Zlandicar's Scorched Heart]] (Mage/Priest)
