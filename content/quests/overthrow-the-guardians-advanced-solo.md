## Steps

*The guardians of the Nexus Core do not allow outsiders to explore through its halls. Find the strongest of the Nexus Core guardians, and destroy them.*

- Enter the zone [[The Nexus Core (Advanced Solo)]]:
  - Defeat [[Luminox Prime (Solo)|Luminox]]
  - Destroy [[Amalgam of Energy (Solo)|Amalgam]]
  - Destroy the [[Energy Delivery Module (Solo)|Blaster Module]]
  - Destroy the [[Maligned Gyro (Solo)|Maligned Gyro]]

- At least 1p 25g
- [[Draconic Etyma]]
