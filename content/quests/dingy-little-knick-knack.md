## Note

- This quest is a subquest of [[They Have Searched an Age for a Dark Champion...]]
- If [[Xilania Nevagon (Neriak)|Xilania Nevagon-Seraph of Hate]] will not speak to you it may be a conflict with the World Event called [[The Power of Hate]].  Delete or advance that quest and then speak to her.

## Steps

1. You will be tasked with finding several armor pieces.
   - *Note: The armor pieces are sell able to merchants, recommend ticking off "not for sale"*
   - 5 pieces located in [[Shard of Fear]]
     - Shard of Fear can be done solo for levels 86+.
     - The Blood Ember Shoulders {{waypoint 289, 17, 317}}  (inside Skeletal Master tower)
     - The Blood Ember Gauntlets {{waypoint 306, 78, 97}}   (top of the shiverback pyramid)
     - The Blood Ember Bracer {{waypoint -83, 44, 33}}   ([[Dracoliche]] area)
     - The Darkforge Greaves {{waypoint 243, 7, -249}}  (behind Skeletal Lord tower)
     - The Darkforge Breastplate {{waypoint -7, 52, -476}}  (one on top of Terror's pyramid)
       - Very Important, to get the Darkforge Breastplate you need to go inside of the Firewall and for that you need to do the [[Shard of Fear Timeline]], pay attention that the parts are all no-zone, so once you start you need to finish, soloing at level 90 took around 1 hour.
   - 1 piece obtained from [[Charasis: Maiden's Chamber]]
     - The Blood Lord's Boots {{waypoint 7, 5, 172}}  ([[Drusella Sathir]]'s room) *(Can be looted from an instance as long as one of the members has not yet left the instance, otherwise the doors close to the room you need.)*
   - 1 piece obtained from [[Charasis: Vault of Eternal Sleep]]
     - The Umbral Helm {{waypoint 41, -3, -125}} (Very last room)
1. Head back to [[Neriak, City of Hate]] and speak with [[Xilania Nevagon (Neriak)|Xilania Nevagon-Seraph of Hate]].
   - She will spawn an [[Umbral Destroyer]]. He is a 85^^^, but a very straightforward fight, no knockbacks or special abilities that I noted.
1. Once you have killed it, speak with [[Xilania Nevagon (Neriak)|Xilania Nevagon-Seraph of Hate]] again.

## Rewards

- At least 36g 79s 48c
- [[The Prophet's Emblem]]
