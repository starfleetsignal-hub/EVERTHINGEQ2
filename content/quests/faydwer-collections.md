Expert Recognition collections, first introduced in the [[Echoes of Faydwer]] expansion, are comprised of the rarest collectibles from most of the other collection quests introduced in the expansion. The reward is an item that, when worn, will allow a character to see otherwise invisible collectable harvesting nodes (the fabled 'blue shinies'), opening an entirely new set of collection quests.<br>
The [[Kunark Expert Adventurer Goggles]] can be used to see both the Kunark and Faydwer blue harvesting nodes. The [[Gnomish Collection Goggles]] only see the spawns in Faydwer.<br>
*For a list of collections and blue shiny spawn locations, see [[Expert Collection Item Known Locations#Echoes of Faydwer Blue Shinies|Here]]*.
