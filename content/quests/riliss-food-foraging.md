## Prerequisites

- You must be at least a level 70 Artisan to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest 25 food items from in [[Fens of Nathsar]].  Cranberries, torsis tea leaves, cabilis cocoa beans, and succulent petals all count towards this.
1. Return to [[Assistant Zhary]].

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- + 2000 faction with **Riliss**
