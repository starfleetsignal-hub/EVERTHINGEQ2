## Notes

- This quest can be spelled "**Y**a!" or "**y**a!" in your journal.
- This is a sub-quest of [[The Adventure of the Two Docks]].

## Steps

1. Find the 6 reports:![](images/Broker_Nearly_Killed_Ya_intelligence_report.jpg)
   - Three scrolls are at [[Mok Rent]].  Look on counters and tables, and on the floor next to those.
   - Three scrolls are at [[Thulwun Station East]].  The scrolls are on the tables near the NPCs and Merchants, on the griffon platform, and on the ground near the manned weapons.
   - See the talk page for more precise locations.
1. Return to Garochi Mirt'dok.

## Rewards

- Experience only
