## Steps

1. Kill 15 Krukiel Bugbears in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] for weapons. But you can kill [[Krulkiel grunt|Krulkiel Grunts]]{{waypoint 150.00, 222.50, -182.50}} instead
1. Return to [[Ninoin D'syl]]

## Rewards

- At least 2s 91c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Holy Acolyte's Plate Pauldrons]]
  - [[Brutal Expert's Arm Wraps]]
  - [[Peerless Sentry's Pauldrons]]
  - [[Keen Bandit's Chain Spaulders]] (Rangers)
  - [[Pure Preserver's Shoulder Pads]]
  - [[Infused Beguiler's Epaulets]]
  - [[Wracked Soothsayer's Spaulders]]

Note that the quest [[The Yarpsnarls]] provides the same armor reward as this quest. Since the armor is level 27 and this quest is level 27, but [[The Yarpsnarls]] is level 23, it's probably a bug with [[The Yarpsnarls]].
