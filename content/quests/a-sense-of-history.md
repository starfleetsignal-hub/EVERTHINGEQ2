## Steps

1. Find [[Janitor Gorreth]] inside [[Crushbone Keep]] in [[The Storeroom]] {{waypoint -38, 0, -24}}
1. He sends you to find some missing pages in:
   - [[Crushbone Armory]] - Along west wall, chest {{waypoint -7, 0, -27}}
   - [[Crushbone Kitchen]] - Along west wall, sacks {{waypoint 45, 0, -61}}
   - [[Temple of the Prophet]] - Along west wall, bookcase {{waypoint 1, 0, -81}}
   - [[Southwest Guard Tower]] - Along west wall, weapon rack {{waypoint 54, 0, 64}}
   - [[Southeast Guard Tower]] - Along east wall, bench {{waypoint -29, 0, 65}}
1. Take them back to Gorreth for your reward.

He asks you to perform another small task to earn his trust, and gives you the next quest, [[Revisionist History]].

## Rewards

- Exp only.
