## Steps

1. Talk again to [[Frizella Emberblossom]] {{waypoint 476, -211, 271}} in [[The Temple of Brell]] in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain|Thurgadin]].
1. Talk to [[Neren Gladefire]] {{waypoint 572, -294, 410}} in [[Remembrance Park]].
1. Talk to [[Arthex D'Nim]] {{waypoint 48, -263, 363}} in [[Stonefrost Sanctuary]] at the back of [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain|Thurgadin]]. This will complete the quest and offer the next one in the series.

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- [[Canister of Dispel Magic]] (item has no use after quest completion and since 2022 is removed at completion)
