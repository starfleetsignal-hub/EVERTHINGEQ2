## Description

I need to bring down Black Bolt's shield around The Waterworks to reactivate the district's defenses. According to a Malduran artificer named Hodges, the shield can be unraveled with a few gathered supplies.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Artificer Hodges]]. Hodges may be found hiding behind a barrel {{waypoint -174.17, -1.53, 143.53}} after killing [[Old Doclin]] in the [[Stone Mercantile District (Algorithm For Destruction Advanced Solo)|Stone Mercantile District]].
- *Note: As of 2023, if you are running an agnostic dungeon version of this, you might see a feather but no Artificer Hodges. You do not have to speak with him. You can skip to the next step and complete the quest as described.*
1. Collect 5 [[An Empty flow-through phylactery|empty flow-through phylacteries]] in the [[Stone Mercantile District (Algorithm For Destruction Advanced Solo)|Stone Mercantile District]] near Artificer Hodges. *(They look like yellow power cells.)*![a flow-through phylactery](images/A_flow-through_phylactery.jpg)
1. Acquire [[Large Malduran crystal shard|large Malduran crystal shards]] in the [[Alcove of Reason (Algorithm For Destruction Advanced Solo)|Alcove of Reason]].
   1. Click on the **shard container** {{waypoint -87.22, 13.27, 177.95}} to activate the [[A spidermech defender|spidermech defenders]].![shard container](images/Shard_container.jpg)
   1. Kill all of the [[A spidermech defender|spidermech defenders]] to spawn [[X-O Arachmech]].  Loot the bodies if doing the Saga quest!
   1. Kill [[X-O Arachmech]] and loot an [[An X-Electro-charged battery|X-Electro-charged battery]]. *(It's possible that X-O will fall below the terrain on it's death, especially if it's killed in the water, preventing the battery from being looted. If this happens, exit the instance and then re-enter to receive a new battery)*
   1. Insert the [[An X-Electro-charged battery|X-Electro-charged battery]] into the **shard container** to receive the [[Large Malduran crystal shard|large Malduran crystal shards]].
1. Kill [[Stonerend]].
   1. Go downstairs to the [[Waterworks: Aquifer Pumping Station (Algorithm For Destruction Advanced Solo)|Waterworks: Aquifer Pumping Station]] {{waypoint 51.92, -6.84, 164.59}}.
   1. Immobilize [[Stonerend]] by killing any three of the four [[A Dark Gears summoner|Dark Gears summoners]]. These necromancers start out non-aggro. Do not cast anything on the named, singular focus is your friend.
   1. Stay clear of the named, wait for a white circle to appear, then run to it and click to place a [[Large Malduran crystal shard|crystal shard]] there. Run out, and allow [[Stonerend]] to crush the crystals. He will take 20% damage each time, the 5th time you do that the remaining Dark Gear Summoner will attack you, kill him and [[Stonerend]] will fall apart.
1. Fill the phylacteries.
   - Collect the shattered crystals by clicking on the **crushed crystal shards**.![crushed crystal shards](images/Crushed_crystal_shards.jpg)
   - Collect the blood from **a dead Dark Gears summoner** by clicking on it.![a dead Dark Gears summoner](images/A_dead_Dark_Gears_summoner.jpg)
1. Go back upstairs and destroy [[Black Bolt|Black Bolt's]] force shield.
   1. Place the 5 phylacteries at marked places along the the bottom of the shield.![Place the filled phylactery](images/Place_the_filled_phylactery.jpg)
   1. Once all 5 are placed, the quest is finished and you can kill the 3 undead mobs:
      - [[Undying Technician (Advanced Solo)|Undying Technician]]
      - [[Undying Mechanic (Advanced Solo)|Undying Mechanic]]
      - [[Undying Apprentice (Advanced Solo)|Undying Apprentice]]
