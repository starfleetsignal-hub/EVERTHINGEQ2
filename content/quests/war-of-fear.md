*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Kill tacticians on both sides of the conflict (Tae Ew and Rallosian). The tacticians are approximately level 40.
   - Slay [[Tae Ew conscript]]s found around {{waypoint -1308, -11, 365}}.
     - Slay three groups of two conscripts to make the tactician spawn.
     - Slay the [[Tae Ew Tactician]] who spawns. {{waypoint -1303, 9, 365}}
   - Slay [[Rallosian footman]] found around {{waypoint -1101, -4, 558}}.
     - Slay three groups of two footmen to make the tactician spawn.
     - Slay the [[Rallosian Tactician]] who spawns. {{waypoint -1066, -4, 555}}
1. Return to [[Gretchen Spiritstorm]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 69s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Bow of Tyranny]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Mindbender Staff]] (All Mages)
  - [[Pouch of the Feerrott]] ([[Bruiser]], [[Monk]])
  - [[Soultaker's Rusted Longbow]] (Plate Fighters)
  - [[Wand of Divine Fear]] (All Priests)
