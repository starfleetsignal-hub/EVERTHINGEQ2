## Steps

1. Become a level 67 adventurer.
1. Return to [[Ole' Man Silvers]] at the same place you found him in [[Tenebrous Tangle]].
1. He sends you to [[Kylong Plains]] to meet him next. He's on the docks at {{waypoint 851, 2, 372}}.

## Rewards

Note: There are no rewards until the very last step of this questline, it is merely a quest to guide new players through the different zones.
