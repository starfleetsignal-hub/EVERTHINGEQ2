1. Collect 10 jibsnapper megalops from around Azure Cradle {{waypoint 609, -47, 844}}.

- They're all around the edge of the water in the crater, non-aggro, tab-targettable.
- Be careful for 83^^^ ghost on road outside of the shipyards.
