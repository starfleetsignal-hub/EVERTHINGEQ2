   - {{waypoint 1334, 14, 2284}}
   - {{waypoint 1231, 14, 2318}}

   - {{waypoint 1093, 15, 2035}}

   - {{waypoint 1181, 16, 1981}}
   - {{waypoint 1274, 23, 2003}}
   - {{waypoint 1380, 18, 1985}}
   - {{waypoint 1424, 24, 2117}}
1. Return the plants to [[Cio'lii]].
