## Steps

1. Kill [[Gurok the Old]] in the same cave just east of [[Urg Drom]]. {{waypoint 1530, 169, -156}}
1. Return to Urg Drom.

## Rewards

- At least 10s 18c
- +30000 faction with **Mountain Throms of Mok Island**
- One of the following depending on class:
  - [[Mark of Strength]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Mark of Intelligence]] (All Mages)
  - [[Mark of Wisdom]] (All Priests)
  - [[Symbol of Agility]] (All Scouts)
