## Steps

1. Clear out some of the natives that live on the shores of the Feerrott. {{waypoint 131, -7, -78}}
   - Kill 5 [[Huuptic fetcher|a Huuptic fetcher]]
   - Kill 5 [[Huuptic idler|a Huuptic idler]]
   - Kill 5 [[Huuptic hunter|a Huuptic hunter]]
   - Kill 1 [[Huuptic elder|a Huuptic Elder]]
1. Return to [[Ambassador Zozor]] at {{waypoint 77, -8, 160}}

## Rewards

- At least 1g 59s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Hidden Loop of the Feerrott]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Hidden Ringlet of the Feerrott]] (All Mages)
  - [[Hidden Ring of the Feerrott]] (All Priests)
  - [[Hidden Signet of the Feerrott]] (All Scouts)
