## Steps

1. Click on the barrel of Green Fruits at {{waypoint -847, 130, -766}}. ![Green Fruits barrel](images/Green_Fruits_barrel.jpg)
1. Click on the barrel of Sting-y Meat at {{waypoint -842, 131, -764}}. ![Sting-y Meats barrel](images/Sting-y_Meats_barrel.jpg)
1. Click repeatedly and quickly on the pot at {{waypoint -844, 131, -769}}.
1. Return to [[Izny Rut]].
