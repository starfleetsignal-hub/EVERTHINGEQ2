## Notes

Quest only mentions it and it is important to take 4 free basic immunity runes that go on Black adornment slot on your waist gear. These can be swapped directly onto the waist as long as your gear does not require repairs.

- Tishan's Coldforged Rune: Blinding Gleam [Tier 0] - gives immunity to Fear effects
- Tishan's Coldforged Rune: Adamant Resolve [Tier 0] - gives immunity to Stifle effects
- Tishan's Coldforged Rune: Adamant Defiance [Tier 0] - gives immunity to Stun effects
- Tishan's Coldforged Rune: Astral Dominion [Tier 0] - gives immunity to Mesmerize effects<br>

## Steps

1. Speak to Quartermaster Pyko just off the dock in Sodden Archipelago {{waypoint -39, 4, 382}}
1. You have option to say "Okay! Give me a few minutes", which triggers the whole tutorial. Or "I already know how to gear myself properly" which completes quest and you will have to take and equip gear from Tishan's box on your own.
1. If you chose "Okay! Give me a few minutes"  you get Tishan's crate in your bags and are instructed to  open it and equip all gear. Quest updates when you  unpack all the gear, you can equip it later.
1. Accept [[Tishan's Gooey Confection]] mount, you are told to open your mount window, equip it and summon it.
1. Accept [[Tishan's Frostmane]] familiar, you are told to open familiar window equip that familiar and summon it.  The familiar goes into your inventory at first, you have to right click it in bags and add to collection, then you can do as Pyko instructed.
1. Next you are told to look  in your spellbook (it will be in abilities) and cast Tolan's Darkwood Mount Infusion, if you have a better version like Vah Shir mount infusion you can cast that to update quest. If you are a new character you can get free  Tolan's Darkwood Mount Infusion from Tishan's  Lockbox next to Pyko
1. Next you need to find in your spellbook ability Jubilant Familiar Infusion, cast it on yourself. If you do not have it get it  from Tishan's box, scribe and cast it.
1. Next you are told to go hire [[Hangar Rotstomper]] mercenary. He sits on crates nearby at {{waypoint 0.33, 6.62, 343}} Speak to him, hire and go back to Quartermaster Pyko
1. Next  you are told to look into your Prestige Tab of Alternate Advancement window for some powerful abilities in the end-lines, this can be done later at your own pace.
1. Lastly Pyko tells you there are other items you can take from Tishan's Lockbox next to him. He mentions specifically to get and carry with you 4 Immunity runes to equip on your belt.

Pyko does not mention but you should take and scribe Monolithic Mercenary Infusion from Tishan's Lockbox. There are also various adornments, ammunition, accolades for your mercenary

## Rewards

- [[Tishan's Crate]] with armor and weapons
- [[Tishan's Gooey Confection]] flying mount. You still have to train this mount to unlock all slots
- [[Tishan's Frostmane]] max level familiar
- [[Hangar Rotstomper]] - fully trained and unlocked to hire anywhere mercenary (Templar)
