## Notes

- This is a timed quest. See the talk page tips and strategies.

## Steps

1. Call for the custodians by playing the ![Serpent Harp of Cul](images/Image:The_Serpent_Harp_of_Cul.jpg) at {{waypoint -470, 23, 371}}.
1. Kill the following custodians within 5-minutes. Below are either the exact locations or general locations of the Custodians as some may choose to roam around the area:
   - [[The Custodian of Cul'Osis]]: {{waypoint -296, -7, 490}}; southwest end of the road
   - [[The Custodian of Cul'Ahn]]: {{waypoint -500, -20, 473}}; southeast across the snake pit from harp
   - [[The Custodian of Cul'Vakh]]: {{waypoint -422, -13, 439}}; at the building south-west of the harp (near tree)
   - [[The Custodian of Cul'Dan]]: {{waypoint -418, -11, 374}}; directly below the harp
   - [[The Custodian of Cul'Kahn]]: {{waypoint -285, -1, 355}}; northwest end of the road (by green pool)
   - The timer ends when the fifth custodian dies
1. Examine the ![Ruins of the Great Directory](images/Image:Ruins_of_the_Great_Directory.jpg) again.

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Leggings of the Dark Mother]]
  - [[Ahketian Tome of Summoning]]
