***This quest is only available to players under level 30***

## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[A traitorous farmer|traitorous farmers]] in Antonica. Farmers spawn randomly around [[Antonica]] but always in tilled farmland plots. There is a good one right next to Gol M'Tun.
1. Return to [[Gol M'Tun]].

## Rewards

- Faction Only:
  - +1000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
  - -1000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
  - -1000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**

## Notes

You can use the [[Mentoring]] system to perform this quest if you are too high level. You are not required to stay mentored once you have the quest active.
