## Notes

- [[More Metal Than Your Mother's Kettle]] from [[Finbert Lintzig]] takes you to the same area.
- Clockwork collectors and disassemblers can be found just east of the Minotaurs from the previous quest. They will be around large gears.

## Steps

![](images/Geobot_IX_%28NPC%29.jpg)
![](images/Geobot_IX_%28Item%29.jpg)

1. Collect parts from 6 Clockwork collectors. Good spot at {{waypoint -1153, 96, 1041}}
1. Collect parts from 6 Clockwork disassemblers
1. Release [[Geobot IX]], the clockwork minion, near the [[Torn Ear Springs]] {{waypoint -1295, 94, 972}} or [[Goldsteam Geyser]] {{waypoint -1019, 94, 828}}.
1. Return to [[Watchman Fleep (NPC)|Watchman Fleep]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Pulsing Gem of Striking]]
- At least 2g 69s 57c
