## Starting the quest

1. Complete the collection [[Akheva Runes]] and turn it in to a collector.
   - This collection does not give a direct reward.
1. Speak to [[Ki'onnaz]] at the [[Maiden's Torch]] {{waypoint -649.01, 79.49, 165.35}}

## Steps

1. [[Ki'onnaz]] offers the quest, and it completes as soon as you accept it.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 2g 91s 4c
- The quest shows **a Fleshless Tongue primer** as a reward, but it never lands in your inventory, instead you are simply granted knowledge of the language.  ![](images/Fleshless_Tongue_Untied_quest_-_a_Fleshless_Tongue_primer.png)

## Notes

- This quest is required as a prerequisite for the [[Reign of Shadows Timeline|Reign of Shadows]] quest [[Fortune Fails the Bold]], part of the RoS timeline.
