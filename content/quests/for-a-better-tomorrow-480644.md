## Steps

1. Speak to [[Preceptor Nyjuss]].  He will give the quest 'For a Better Tomorrow'
1. Kill [[Opal Darkbriar (Darklight Wood)|Opal Darkbriar]] (85x2) in [[Darklight Wood]]. She travels from the [[Nektulos Forest]] zoneline to the [[Neriak, City of Hate]] zoneline. She is non-agro, and will only be attackable after an [[Assassin]] who needs the update hails her.
   - Return to [[Preceptor Nyjuss]].
1. Kill [[Tairiza the Widow Mistress]] in [[The Tomb of Thuuga]]. <s>You need to wear the [[Robe of the Freeport Emissary]] and the ring [[Mark of the Emissary]] when killing it</s>
   - Return to [[Preceptor Nyjuss]].
1. Kill [[The Leviathan]] in the [[The Chamber of Destiny]]. <s>You need to wear the [[Robe of the Freeport Emissary]] and the ring [[Mark of the Emissary]] when killing it.</s>
   - Return to [[Preceptor Nyjuss]].
1. Kill [[Druushk]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]].
   - *Note: **Very important!** Equip the [[Blackened Leviathin Heart]] and your [[Fang of Ichor]] for this kill, the quest actually tells it too.*
   - *Note: Bring backup weapon because right after the kill you will lose your fabled Fang of Ichor*
1. Return to [[Preceptor Nyjuss]] and receive your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Fang of Ichor (Mythical)]]
