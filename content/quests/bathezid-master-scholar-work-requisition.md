## Steps

1. Receive quest from [[Supply Sergeant Anuhadux]]
1. Check clipboard behind him for your task
   - Scribe recipe book [[Bathezid master scholar recipes]] (This is erased after each turn in).
1. Create one of the following from the recipe book as randomly requested by the work requisition:
   - 6 [[Fyreflyte's Daunting Decision (Journeyman)]]
   - 6 [[Groden's Tiny Hut (Journeyman)]]
   - 6 [[Kyri's Hateborn Toxin (Crafted)|Kyri's Hateborn Toxin]]
   - 6 [[Amulet of water breathing]]
   - 6 [[Potion of swiftness]]
   - 6 [[Terror inducing battle chains]]
1. Return to the Requsition Agent ([[Supply Sergeant Anuhadux]]).

## Rewards

- At least 43g 70s 26c
- +750 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
