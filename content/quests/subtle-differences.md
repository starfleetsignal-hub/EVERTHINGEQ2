## Notes

- requires at least 1,000 [[House Vahla (Faction)]]

## Steps

1. Obtain 12 inner eye gems by killing 12 [[Ceffyl grazer|ceffyl grazers]] or [[Ceffyl stallion|ceffyl stallions]] in [[Kandra Uplands]] around {{waypoint -432, 86, -36}}.
1. Return to [[Tesakias]] at {{waypoint -179.83, 573.57, -541.05}} and receive [[Unrelenting Repellent Rune Gem]].
1. Place the 4 gems around platform.
   - southern point - {{waypoint -191, 573, -510}}
   - eastern point - {{waypoint -304, 579, -703}} The location is back around behind the wall.
   - northern point- {{waypoint -144, 578, -765}}
   - western point - {{waypoint -91, 575, -592}}
1. Return to [[Tesakias]] at {{waypoint -165, 575, -593}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Effect Tome]] x 2
- +1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
