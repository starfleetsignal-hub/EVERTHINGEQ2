## Steps

1. Search the area for a [[Stray Hooluk Feather|hooluk feather]]
   - Click nearby pile of feathers. {{waypoint 723, -45, -442}} the pile of feathers is right behind you a few steps when talking to Burnish.  ![Pile of stray feathers](images/Disassemble_Tricking_the_Eyes_quest_-_Stray_Feathers.jpg)
1. Craft [[Hooluk Illusion Potion]]
1. Speak with [[Burnish (Aspiring Artisan)|Burnish]]
