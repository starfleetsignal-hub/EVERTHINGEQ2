The Savage Weald's forest is a danger to all, with its thick fog and canopied treetops that block even a modicum of light. It is a breeding ground for predators - from bandits to grimlings and worse - and so Fordel Midst's city council have requested my assistance in making the forest less savage.

## Steps

- Slay the tribal leader
- Kill the queen
- Hunt the hag
- Pummel the patroller
- Defeat the greater menace
- Eliminate the final threat

## Rewards

- At least 4p 49g 6s 40c 138,599 status
- 65 [[Bazaar Crown]]
- [[Weekly Crown Receptacle (Tier 10)]]
