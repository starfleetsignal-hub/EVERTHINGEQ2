## Steps

1. Speak With [[Gallie Shimmercloud]] {{waypoint 326, 189, 496}}
1. Collect the undeciphered notes from the table near [[Gallie Shimmercloud|Gallie]] {{waypoint 328, 190, 495}}
1. Research the notes
   1. Scribe the book [[Recipe: Gallie's Researched Notes]] to learn how to make [[Completed Notes]]
   1. Craft 5 x [[Completed Notes]] on an [[Engraved Desk]] (there is one located at the docks) {{waypoint 756, 26, 603}} with the following materials and fuel:
      - 10 [[Rough opaline]]
      - 5 [[Severed briarwood]]
      - 5 [[Glimmering incense]]
1. Return the notes to [[Gallie Shimmercloud|Gallie]]
1. Speak with [[Ellerith Groundspark|Ellerith]]

## Rewards

- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
