## Description

Oracle Priti went on a dreamwalk, focusing on the vampire threat, while I was seeking the Holy Icon of Srlaka. In her vision, she saw a force of beasts, unlike she has ever seen surrounding a portal, somewhere in the Rolling Hills of Karda.

## Steps

1. Search [[Rolling Hills of Karda]] for signs of Primordial Malice  ![Malice Conjured Portal](images/Maliceportal.png)
   - Portal {{waypoint -575, 161, 249}}
1. Enter Malice Conjured Portal and defeat Tserrina's goons.
   - [[Castle Vacrul: Confrontation]]
   - Defeat all the mobs to open the vampiric barrier on the left. Follow along the left wall, killing mobs along the way. Witness conversation between [[Mayong Mistmoore]] and [[Tserrina Syl'Tor]] triggered at {{waypoint -125, -44, 422}}
1. Return to [[Oracle Priti]]
1. Speak to [[High Oracle Akshara]] {{waypoint 67, 12, 513}}
