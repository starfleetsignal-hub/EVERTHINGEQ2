## Description

*Maldura is being invaded from within, while the horrors of Kralet Penumbra threaten the city from without. I have to defend Maldura from both threats!*

## Steps

- Destroy enemy targets within Maldura and Kralet Penumbra.
1. Defeat the invasion's leader
   - [[Black Bolt (Heroic)|Black Bolt]] in [[Maldura: Algorithm For Destruction (Heroic)]]
1. Defeat the horror
   - [[Xoth, the Ever Rending (Heroic)|Xoth]] in [[Kralet Penumbra: Temple of the Ill-Seen (Heroic)]]
