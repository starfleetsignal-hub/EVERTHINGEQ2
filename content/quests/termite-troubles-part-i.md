## Notes

- Scales to your crafting level
- If Varick is not visible you may need to target him by typing /target Varick then hail him. Alternately you can camp to character select and then log back in to trigger him.

## Steps

1. Speak with a [[Wyneta Splinterfeet]] in [[Teren's Grasp]], [[Kylong Plains]] {{waypoint 2241, 487, -694}}
   - You do not need to have any prior reputation with Teren's Grasp; however, if you haven't worked with them before, you'll need to manually hail Wyneta, either via keybind or by right-clicking her to select 'hail'.
1. Speak with [[James Reed]] just outside the barricades in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] {{waypoint 466, -36, 171}}
1. Speak with [[Neela Filamaar]] upstairs inside [[Tunare's Pages]] in [[Kelethin]], [[Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint 461, 97, 353}}
1. Return to Varick Stoutheart

## Rewards

- +5000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
