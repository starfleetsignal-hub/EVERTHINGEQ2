## Prerequisites

- [[The Sphere of Containment]]

## Steps

1. Use your abilities to query and dominate [[A docile air elemental]] in [[Kylong Plains]] near [[Teren's Grasp]] at the same location you previously did.
   - A level 80^ [[Air elemental]] will spawn in its place and attack you.
   - You are looking for an uncommon body drop called [[Lesser Element: Air]]. You need to collect 10 of these.
     - When you have all 10, right-click on the stack in your inventory and select 'Use' to receive the update.

## Rewards

- Experience Only
