## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. gather and kill the following
   - Reclaim either 8 "crates" or "barrels" in [[Dankmar Swamp]].
     - *Small dark crates , non trackable, mousing over them shows "Port Woe Goods". Possible locations include {{waypoint 194.40, 4.49, 457.23}} {{waypoint 215.22, 4.49, 476.28}} {{waypoint 174.73, 4.73, 511.55}} {{waypoint 240.73, 4.24, 614.90}} {{waypoint 505, 5, 731}}  {{waypoint 467, 4, 684}} and many more*
     - they spawn among Kappas, along paths, waterways, by rocks, often outside their huts (tree trunks with glowing yellow "windows" and "doors" in Dankmar Swamp, which is the whole area South and SouthWest from Port Woe.
   - Hunt either
     - 12 "kappa fencrushers and mirestalkers" or
     - 12 "kappa murkweavers and mirestalkers" in search of Port Woe items.
1. Return to [[Gragir Sharpe]] (must be completed twice to be able to do the quest [[A Few Peaceful Knights]])

## Rewards

- [[Large Archipelago Crate]] house item
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +2,000 faction with **Port Woe**
- 25 [[Scarred Dinar]] only the first time
