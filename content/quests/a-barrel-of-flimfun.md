**Note Mercs are not useable**

## Steps

1. Pick up the Barreled Flimflam
1. Place barrel inside the entrance of [[Temple Street: Vault of Necromechanica]] {{waypoint -176.91, -36.67, 324.45}}, near {{waypoint 12, 3, 28}}
1. Hail the Barreled Flimflam and he instructs you to find his Tinkered Brain Box
1. Go inside room near repair area at {{waypoint -16, 3, 64}}
   - Go into first person and view the spring board.
   - Click it when the hand appears and it will port you up to the shelf where you can grab the Tinkered Brain Box
1. Return to the Barreled Flimflam
1. Kill 6 mutated pigs
   - There should be 6 mutated pigs alive, if not zone out and zone back in
1. Return to the Barreled Flimflam
1. Meet [[Professor Flimflam]] at the Inventor's Overlook

## Rewards

- At least 19g 93s 2c
