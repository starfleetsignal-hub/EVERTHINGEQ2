## Steps

1. On [[Zenith Helionae]] enter [[Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent (Solo)|Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent [Solo]]] at {{waypoint 288.76, 164.76, -126.28}}![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Arcane_Alloy.png)
1. Gather 14 bars of Yazdani [[Arcane Alloy]]
   - For a zone walkthrough see [[Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent (Solo)|Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent [Solo]]]
   - The [[Arcane Alloy]] can appear at random locations. There can be more than 14 distributed across the zone. The number spawned in any area can vary.
1. Return to [[Sqrauwk]] in [[Zimara Breadth]]

## Rewards

- 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1000 faction with **Isles of Wisdom**
- [[Helionae Marble Fallen Pillar Ruin]] - *House item*
