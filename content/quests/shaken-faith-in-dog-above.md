## Steps

*It seems Outlander Deston's report to Consul Bree went further than just the Qeynos Guard. Steinsister Taswynn, a Priest of Serilis has arrived in Darkpaw Dugout, intrigued by the High Shaman's claim to be a prophet of Chirannis.*

1. Destroy items pertaining to the gnollish worship of Chirannis, the Pack Lord:
   - Destroy 5 Chirannis Ritual Drums in Sabertooth Burrow
     - Possible locations:
       - {{waypoint -75, -46, -227}}
       - {{waypoint -174, -30, -119}} requires a [[Darkpaw ladder]]
       - {{waypoint -98, -40, -140}}
       - {{waypoint -38, -42, -125}}
   - Destroy 5 Chirannis Ritual Drums in Dustpaw Burrow
     - Possible locations:
       - {{waypoint -123, -6, -133}} may require a [[Darkpaw ladder]], but it can be reached by jumping
       - {{waypoint 1, -3, -189}}
       - {{waypoint -23, -12, -138}}
       - {{waypoint -132, -15, -80}}
       - {{waypoint 47, -4.2, -185}}
       - {{waypoint 63, -7.4, -219}}
   - Destroy 5 Pack Lord Totems in Mudpaw Burrow
     - Possible locations:
       - {{waypoint -62, -65, -234}}
       - {{waypoint -71, -67, -257}}
       - {{waypoint -9, -64, -189}}
       - {{waypoint -89, -73, -168}}
   - Destroy 5 Pack Lord Totems in Rockpaw Burrow
     - Possible locations:
       - {{waypoint 50, 3, 203}}
       - {{waypoint 92, 5, 169}}
       - {{waypoint 129, 3, 187}}
       - {{waypoint 10, 3, 219}}
       - {{waypoint 2, 6, 189}}
   - Hunt devotees, zealots, and adherents for 12 Pack Lord prayer beads
     - Automatic group update upon killing the specific type of gnoll, including:
       - A Mudpaw Zealot in Mudpaw Burrow
       - A Dustpaw adherent in Dustpaw Burrow
       - A Nisch Mal Devotee in Splitpaw Burrow
1. Search for the Tome of Chirannis
   - Drop down the hole at {{waypoint -32, 0, 299}} to land at Ancestral Gathering (or use the terraporter if you've unlocked it)
   - Click on 'Ceremonial Chest' {{waypoint -31.30, -23.30, 283.20}} in Ancestral Gathering and in Convocation of Chirannis until you get the update
   - *NOTE: unlike previous steps, this is NOT a group update!*
1. Return to Steinsister Taswynn in Darkpaw Dugout
