## Note

This repeatable quest and the quest [[Lighter Studies]] are used to gain the +1,000 [[House Yrzu (Faction)]] required for the next quests.

## Steps

Use [[Jesinnal's Tablet]] that you received from [[Jesinnal]] on the following before you kill them (you can do more than one at once):

- 3 [[Shadowed stelecat|stelecats]] around {{waypoint 184.57, -2.54, 369.08}} or [[Fishing stonestalker|stonestalkers]] around {{waypoint -111.48, 26.02, 225.59}}.
- 3 [[Plumewit ridgestalker|plumewits]] around {{waypoint 140.64, 17.63, 295.08}}, {{waypoint 534.72, 78.18, 767.19}} or {{waypoint -839.11, 192.22, -347.35}}.
- 3 [[Cnidcara feeder|cnidcaras]] around {{waypoint 445.33, 33.60, -19.45}}, {{waypoint -878.71, -52.05, 28.45}} or {{waypoint -934.42, 5.28, 474.96}} or {{waypoint 518.89, 16.78, -54.33}} .

## Rewards

- At least 62g 37s
- -5000 faction with **House Vahla**
- +5000 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -5000 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
