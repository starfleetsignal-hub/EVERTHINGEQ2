## Steps

1. You will be randomly assigned one of the following tasks:
   - Destroy 20 **Rujarkian war banners** in [[The Sinking Sands]].
   - These can be found all around the "Orc Highway" to the south.
   - Slay 20 [[Sandscrawler grunt]]s in [[The Sinking Sands]].
   - They can be found around {{waypoint -181, -153, -1279}}
   - Destroy 20 **Dervish weapon racks** in [[The Sinking Sands]].
   - These can be found all around {{waypoint -166, -105, -318}}
   - Slay 20 [[Sul'Dal bladefury|Sul'Dal Bladefuries]] in [[The Sinking Sands]].
1. Return to [[Captain Woug]] in North Qeynos.

## Rewards

- 9352 status
- +500 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
- 1 [[City Token]]
