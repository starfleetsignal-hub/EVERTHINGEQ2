## Description

I must help the undead brethren of the frogloks find piece. While adventuring in the Halls of the Fallen, I need to use the Vial of Cleansed Prayers to Mithaniel Marr to free the cursed souls of the fetid shin knights.

## Steps

1. Use the received *[[Vial of Cleansed Prayers to Mithaniel Marr]]* and free 5 souls of the fetid shin within [[The Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen|Upper Guk]]
1. Return to Vegupa Marrsheart

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
