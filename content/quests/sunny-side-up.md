## Prerequisites

- You must be able to speak [[Goblish]] to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Veksar: The Sunken Theater]], kill [[Cribblekeep]] and loot the [[Goblish Eggstractor]] from his chest. {{waypoint -23, -2, -153}}
1. Return to [[The Lost Chancel]] and smash the leviathan egg container. {{waypoint 78, 1, -55}}
1. Return to Grulek.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- One of the following:
  - [[Fishsticker]]
  - [[Gillcrusher]]
  - [[Gutcarver]]
  - [[Scalerot Mace]]
