## Steps

1. Click on the ballot box until you find a metal object.
   - To save time, rather than clicking the option to "[Set Aside]" the ballot, just keep spam-clicking on the ballot box (or hover your mouse over it and press 'F'). When you finally get to the last window, the quest will update and you will the option to take the metal object.
1. Speak to [[A Gigglegibber ballot counter]] and choose either "the other candidate won" or "the first candidate won". Either option will update and complete the quest.

## Rewards

- The key needed to unlock one of the vaults to complete [[Unlocking the Elfin Lord]]
