## Notes

- None of the harvestable items are trackable.
- All of these clicks are **zone-wide group** updates.  If you are grouped anywhere in the zone, your group members will receive updates.

## Steps

1. Gather the following ingredients from [[Antonica]]:
   1. Gather 4 giant Coldwind clams around the Coldwind Coastline beach near {{waypoint -568, -36, 812}}
   1. Gather 4 wild Coven onions around the Glade of the Coven near {{waypoint -1517, -12, 64}}
   1. Gather 4 Old Oak potatoes around The Central Farmlands near {{waypoint -108, -18, 11}}
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]].

## Rewards

- [[Days of Summer: 2020]] useable at [[Pas Yu]], the merchant beside [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]] on the [[Spire Island]].
