## Steps

1. Show the egg to [[Yrsa Gravelcut]] in [[Great Divide]]. Yrsa can be found in a cave at {{waypoint 529, -357, 830}}
1. Take the egg to the Gohmari village
   - At {{waypoint 338, -42, 629}}, you will be attacked by three [[A fanatical shardhammer (Great Divide)|fanatical shardhammers]] (90v, non-heroic).
   - Kill all three of them to update
1. Take the egg to [[Windso]] in the Gohmari village.

## Rewards

- At least 42g 63s 17c
