## Notes

- This quest is also part of the [[Maj'Dul Timeline]].

## Steps

![The clickable books that tell the tale of Zeke Two Tone's adventures.](images/The_Name_of_My_Forefathers_book.jpg)

1. Go to [[The Pillars of Flame]] and click the stack of books on [[The Island of the Naga]] at {{waypoint 898, -180, -1524}}.
1. Examine the book in your inventory.
1. Return to [[Zeke Two Tone]].
1. Click a harp underwater in the Pillars of Flame northeast of the naga island. {{waypoint 810, -240, -1706}}
1. Return to Zeke Two Tone.
