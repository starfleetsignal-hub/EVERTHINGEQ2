## Notes

- There are three different versions of this quest, all bearing the same name. Each version is a limited repeatable quest which can be completed 10 times. Upon completion of the final iteration of a version, the following version becomes available. Only upon the completion of the final iteration of the third version will the following quest be the master quest: [[Surface Assault]].
- If you do not like your target mob you may delete the quest and immediately be offered a new one with (hopefully) a better target mob.
- After [[Surface Assault]] has been completed, variations of previous quests become permanent repeatables.

## Level 28

- Kill a number of monsters in [[The Ruins of Varsoon]], randomly assigned from this list:
  - 10 [[A Varsoon guard|Varsoon guard]]s
  - 10 [[A clay crawler|clay crawler]]s
  - 10 [[A living clay|living clay]]s
- Reward: At least 30s 88c

## Level 32

- Kill a number of monsters in [[The Ruins of Varsoon]], randomly assigned from this list:
  - 10 [[A clay guardian|clay guardian]]s
  - 10 [[A servant of Varsoon|servants of Varsoon]]
  - 6 [[An enchanted clay|enchanted clay]]s
  - 4 [[A Varsoon disciple|Varsoon disciple]]s
- Reward: At least 27s 67c

## Level 34

- Kill a number of monsters in [[The Ruins of Varsoon]], randomly assigned from this list:
  - 6 [[A forge spark|forge spark]]s - 3-4 up at a time, 6 minute respawn
  - 5 [[A forge stoker|forge stoker]]s - 5 up at a time, 10 minute respawn
  - 6 [[A thought engineer|thought engineer]]s - 4 up at a time, 10 minute respawn
  - 6 [[A claychanter|claychanter]]s - 4 up at a time, 30+ minute respawn
- Reward: At least 1g 21s
