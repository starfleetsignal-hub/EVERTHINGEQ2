## Steps

1. Plant kerra totems![](images/Kerra_totem_%28visible%29.png)
   - Plant kerra totem where the swamp meets the ocean at {{waypoint -36.67, 4.71, 539}}
   - Plant kerra totem along the border waters, west from the ocean at {{waypoint 43.37, 5.75, 502.03}}
   - Plant kerra totem where the border waters fork at {{waypoint 75.58, 4.91, 499.36}}
   - Plant kerra totem north of the border fork at {{waypoint 95.87, 4.96, 461.85}}
   - Plant kerra totem at water's edge, directly across from the Port Woe fallen log entry at {{waypoint 115.41, 4.33, 417.53}}
   - Plant kerra totem at the northern most end of the border waters at {{waypoint 142.69, 4.48, 388.46}}
1. Return to [[Kober Botley]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +5,000 faction with **Port Woe**
- [[Forsaken Kerra Totem]]
