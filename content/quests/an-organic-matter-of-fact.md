## Steps

![glomalin deposit](images/Glomalin.jpg)

1. Collect 6 samples of glomalin outside of Moonfield Hamlet.
   - You must equip the [[Glomalin Divining Rod]] in order to see and collect the samples. A good spot to start searching is by the launch pad. {{waypoint 50, 376, 1201}}
   - **NOTE:** The charm item causes you to move in random directions, "pulling" you toward deposits.  So unless you're on a flying mount, stay away from the edges of land!
1. Return to Ebodold Tongofur.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
