This quest is part 5 of a 6-quest series in the [[Isle of Refuge Timeline]].

## Description

"Murrar Shar has sent me to seek out Cleric Mara'Vaen at the head of Dead Canyon. The Qeynosian scouts are trying to push their way forward into the Abandoned Village to seek out the source of evil, but they have been unsuccessful so far. Mara'Vaen should be just inside Dead Canyon to the west past Sunset Meadow." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Report to [[Cleric Mara'Vaen]] {{waypoint 231, -3, 153}} in [[Dead Canyon]], under the tree that's just through the archway at the end of [[Sunset Meadow]].
1. Pull 3 [[A lost scout|lost scouts]] back to the cleric. To pull them, attack them so they follow you, but don't kill them. You can also charm them, but to get credit for Cleric Mara'Vaen resurrecting them, you must cancel the charm before her spell cast completes and the scout is resurrected. She will start casting as soon as the lost scout is nearby, even if it is charmed.
1. Kill 4 [[A Morak devourer|Morak devourers]].
1. Return to Cleric Mara'Vaen to report your success and receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 15c
- One of the following, depending on your class:
  - [[Qeynosian Cloth Shawl]]
  - [[Qeynosian Leather Shoulderpads]]
  - [[Qeynosian Studded Mantle]]
  - [[Qeynosian Plated Pauldrons]]
