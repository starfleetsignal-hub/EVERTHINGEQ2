## Description

"Seren Leafsway thought that Tuathil Laeds could use my help. If I follow the road west from Shadow Spire Outpost, cross the Lower Emerald River, then take the first split to the right, I will pass through the Valley of the Great Swarm. From there, I take the fork to the right, I can find Tuathil where the road bends north into Crushbone territory." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Tuathil Laeds]] in the [[Valley of the Great Swarm]] at {{waypoint -574, 66, -71}}

## Rewards

- At least 3s 11c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Seren Leafsway: "I know of someone else that could certainly use your services, and your skills, if you are interested."
- Player Character: "I am interested."
- Seren Leafsway: "His name is Tuathil Laeds. He's in the Valley of the Great Swarm. You can get there by following the road out of here across the river, taking each fork to the right. Once the path reaches Crushbone territory it will bend north, at that point Tuathil will be to the south of the road."
- Player Character: "I'll go right away."

Speak with Tuathil.

- Tuathil: "[Player Character]?"
- Player Character: "Yes."
- Tuathil: "Good. I've been expecting you. I am Tuathil Laeds. Welcome to Crushbone territory. Seren recommended you to me. I hope you don't mind us pulling you to the front lines out here, but we need fighters of your caliber, before things get too out of control."
- Player Character: "I am glad to help."

I have found Tuathil.

I have found Tuathil Laeds.
