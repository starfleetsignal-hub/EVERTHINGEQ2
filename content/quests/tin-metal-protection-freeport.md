**If your character is good aligned, see the [[Blat Berisen]] page for locations you can access.**

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Frethe Zizlop (City of Freeport)|Frethe Zizlop]] near the armorsmith at {{waypoint 284.49, -3.52, -103.26}}
   - This will start the subquest [[Card Deals (Freeport)|Card Deals]].
1. Speak with [[Zoe Hertzflip (West Freeport)|Zoe Hertzflip]] near the General Goods Merchant in [[West Freeport]] {{waypoint 222, -3, 77}}
   - This will start the subquest [[Ogled Goggles (Freeport)|Ogled Goggles]].
1. Speak with [[Barb Islenet (North Freeport)|Barb Islenet]] in near the [[Guild Halls|T2 guild hall]] at [[North Freeport]] {{waypoint -28, -20, -271}}
   - This will start the subquest [[Wine for the Lady (Freeport)|Wine for the Lady]].
1. Speak with [[Abner Siblet (North Freeport)|Abner Siblet]] inside the Jade Tiger Inn in [[North Freeport]] {{waypoint -105, -21, -79}}
   - This will start the subquest [[Fribles's Fate (Freeport)|Fribles's Fate]].
1. Go to {{waypoint -327, -47, -300}}, northwest of the Crossroads in [[The Commonlands]] . [[Belazzor Fribles, the Inept (Commonlands)|Belazzor Fribles, the Inept]] will spawn and attack you; his level scales to yours.
   - Kill [[Belazzor Fribles, the Inept (Commonlands)|Fribles]].
1. Go back to [[Abner Siblet (North Freeport)|Abner Siblet]] in [[North Freeport]].
1. Return to the remaining NPCs in reverse order to complete each of the subquests. You will get Exp/AA exp for all 4 of them.
1. Return finally to [[Blat Berisen (Commonlands)|Blat Berisen]] in The Commonlands for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Zoe's Wild Deck of Playing Cards]]
- [[Tin Metal Helm of Protection]]
