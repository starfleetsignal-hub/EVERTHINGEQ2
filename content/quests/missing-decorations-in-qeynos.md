## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill the gnolls near the entrance to [[The Down Below]] {{waypoint 432.63, -15.88, 84.08}}.
1. Click on the boxes next to the entrance to retrieve the decorations, and kill the gnolls that spawn again.
1. Place the six [[Pack of magical paper lanterns (Qeynos)|magical paper lanterns]]. These are random locations in the specified zones, and are green sparklies on the ground that you must click. See the discussion page for known locations.
   - Three inside of the gate in [[Qeynos Capitol District]], formerly known as North Qeynos.
   - Three inside of the gate in [[Qeynos Capitol District]], formerly known as South Qeynos.
1. Return to [[Lillita Softpaws|Lillita]]
