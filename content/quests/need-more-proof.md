## Steps

1. Find proof that the erudites are being corrupted by the influences of the Stonebrunt Highlands.
   - Head out to [[Kejaan's Rill]] (east of the [[Stonebrunt Encampment]]).
   - As you approach {{waypoint 415, 246, 3741}} [[A void-touched Kejek]] spawns. Kill it.
1. Return to [[Kejek Chief Nuryen]] and inform him of your findings.
