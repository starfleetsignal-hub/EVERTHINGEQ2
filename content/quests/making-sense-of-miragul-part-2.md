## Steps

1. Click on the NPC zombie bodies laying on the ground to excavate some pieces from the creatures Miragul has conducted necromantic experiments on. The experiments are discarded zombies found on the floor in The Crypt of Disdain {{waypoint 40, 2, -70}} and in The Dismal Hollow {{waypoint 150, 20, -100}}
1. Retrieve the totem from the sorcery room at {{waypoint -265, -2, 183}}, on the altar in the room with the multicolored puzzle
1. Return to [[Lucretia Nasin]]

- At least 31g 73s 78c
