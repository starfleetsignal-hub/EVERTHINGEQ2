*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. You have 5 minutes to push the following four buttons, in any order:
   - {{waypoint -99, 13, 73}}; on the right side of the building west of the pool
   - {{waypoint -306, 9, 174}}; half way up the climbable wall next to the first pool; on the right. Hop off the wall to stand on top of the switch to reach it.
   - {{waypoint -336, 1, 155}}; left side of the building with the dark elf
   - {{waypoint -271, 17, 94}}; up the ramp across from the first pool on the left pillar
1. Inspect the hidden compartment again.
