## Staring the quest

- Ghawg only spawns after 8 PM game time.
- He will only give you the quest if there are no ghosts nearby, otherwise he'll tell you he's hiding. If the quest icon is not above his head, clear the ghosts until it appears.

## Steps

1. Gather 8 Warslik Bile 'shrooms.![Warslik bile shroom](images/Warslik_bile_shroom.jpg)
   - White mushrooms near trees, some possible locations:
     - {{waypoint 535, -187, -475}}   {{waypoint 596, -154, -391}}   {{waypoint 531, -184, -402}}   {{waypoint 464, -198, -306}}
     - {{waypoint 556, -168, -543}}  {{waypoint 388, -211, -385}}    {{waypoint 472, -194, -502}}   {{waypoint 383, -228, -300}}
1. Return to Ghawg.

## Rewards

- [[Chokidai Tooth Earring]]
- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 15108 status

## Notes

- You need to able to speak [[Goblish]] to speak with him
- Ghawg only appears after 8pm game time
- The quest "[[A Vicious Tongue]]" needs to be complete before Ghawg will spawn.
- Kill the "an Obulus remnant" close by to make him offer the quest, you may have to kill more than one.  Killed 2, 3rd didn't spawn until logged out and right back again in same spot.
- *[[Sluggin' It Out]] is the second option of the dialog, when you hail him the first time*
