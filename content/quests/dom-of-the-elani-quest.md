## Steps

1. Talk to the ghost of Tazsu Nelivaza by clicking on the gravestone at {{waypoint -435, 15, 68}}.
1. Kill 10 [[A vengeful spirit (Loping Plains)|vengeful spirits]] in [[The Breathless Hollow]] and hail the NPC spirits that spawn where they are killed. {{waypoint -130, 41, 106}}
1. Return to Tazsu Nelivaza.
1. Click on a mound of dirt in the Breathless Hollow to receive [[Dom of the Elani (Item)|The Dom of Elani]]. {{waypoint -277, -7, -75}}
1. Return to Havras.
