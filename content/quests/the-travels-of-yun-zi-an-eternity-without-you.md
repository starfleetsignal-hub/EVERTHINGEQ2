## Steps

1. Visit the following locations in [[Ethernere]]:
   1. [[The Ethermist Shores]]  {{waypoint 125, -47, -168}} in [[The Eidolon Jungle]]: To get there, travel by spire to [[The Feerrott]] and use the portal to [[The Eidolon Jungle]] near the Wizard Spire. Then fly to the northwest to the beach {{waypoint 125, -47, -168}}.
   1. [[The Greyfall]] {{waypoint -14, 3, 736}} in [[The Eidolon Jungle]].
   1. [[The Fearcreep]] {{waypoint -1476, 228, 892}} in [[The Eidolon Jungle]].
   1. in [[Obol Plains]] {{waypoint -723, -3, 20}}: Go to [[The Tourbillion (Obol Plains)|The Tourbillion]] {{waypoint -2407, 214, 672}} fly into it to zone through to the [[Obol Plains]].  You get the update when you land.
   1. [[Cardin Ward]] {{waypoint -438, 13, -37}} in [[Obol Plains]].
   1. [[Harrow's End]] {{waypoint 411, 24, 548}} in [[Obol Plains]].
1. Return to Yun Zi. There is a wizard spire at {{waypoint 82, 38, -335}} to help get you back, if you have completed the correct timelines for the zone
