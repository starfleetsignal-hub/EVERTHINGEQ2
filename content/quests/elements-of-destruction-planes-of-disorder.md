## Notes

- Bring potions: power, health, cure trauma and arcane. Your'e going to need them.

## Steps

1. Obtain a pure sample of Water within the Plane of Water.
   1. Go to the [[North Wing Stacks]] on the ground floor of the Library, then take the teleporter at {{waypoint 260, -20, -285}} to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]]. Use the portal at {{waypoint 789, 412, -380}} to enter [[Awuidor: The Nebulous Deep (Solo)|Awuidor: The Nebulous Deep [Solo]]].
   1. Progress through the zone. (See [[Awuidor: The Nebulous Deep (Solo)|Awuidor: The Nebulous Deep [Solo]]] for strategies).
   1. Kill the final named, [[Aegaeon, the Violent]] in [[Awuidor: The Nebulous Deep (Solo)|Awuidor: The Nebulous Deep [Solo]]] at {{waypoint 1262, 630, 22}}, to obtain the [[Onzolan crystal]].
1. Speak with [[Maelin Starpyre]] at {{waypoint 246, -7, -26}} in [[Myrist, the Great Library]].
1. Obtain a pure sample of Earth within the Plane of Earth.
   1. Go to the [[North Wing Stacks]] on the ground floor of the Library, then take the teleporter at {{waypoint 260, -20, -285}} to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]]. Use the portal at {{waypoint 774, 412, -336}} to enter [[Vegarlson: Ruins of Rathe (Solo)|Vegarlson: Ruins of Rathe [Solo]]].
   1. Progress through the zone. (See [[Vegarlson: Ruins of Rathe (Solo)|Vegarlson: Ruins of Rathe [Solo]]] for strategies).
   1. Kill the final nameds, [[Koni Ferus]] and [[Pete Bogg]] in [[Vegarlson: Ruins of Rathe (Solo)|Vegarlson: Ruins of Rathe [Solo]]] at {{waypoint -67, 80, 429}}, to obtain a [[Tectonic fragment]]. You will be teleported to {{waypoint 665, 42, -341}}.
   1. Move toward [[Lady Najena (Chaos Descending)|Lady Najena]] and listen to her monologue, then attack her.
      - Pull Najena toward the water around {{waypoint 587, 13, -310}}. She will occasionally summon adds. She cannot be killed.
      - Eventually, Najena will start throwing giant snow mounds at you. Place them near the water to form a bridge. You must place the first snow mound in the ground on the peninsula jutting out into the water.  Each mound must be placed at the end of the growing bridge.  If you did not place your mound in the correct place, you will receive a message telling you the snow mound is not effective there.
      - Najena may not throw snow mounds at you if you are standing on the ice bridge, requiring you to run back to solid land to get your next mound.
      - You CANNOT jump to the portal. The bridge must go ALL the way across, which requires about 5 snow mounds.
      - The final snow mound freezes part of its path slightly underwater. You have enough frozen pathway created when there's ice under the portal.
   1. Once the bridge is complete, click on the portal at {{waypoint 471, 14, -297}} to port back to the start of the zone.
1. Speak with [[Maelin Starpyre]] at {{waypoint 246, -7, -26}} in [[Myrist, the Great Library]].
