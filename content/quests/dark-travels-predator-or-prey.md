There are a great many threats on this side of the moon that relish the dark, and prey on those weaker than them. But now that I've arrived, it's time the predators have become the prey!

## Starting the Quest

Kill overland named monsters and/or x4 epic PQ nameds in [[Echo Caverns]], [[Savage Weald]] or [[Shadeweaver Thicket]]

## Steps

- Defeat heroic and/or x4 epic PQ named encounters across the great and dark outdoors of Luclin

## Rewards

- At least 11p 22g 66s 138,599 status
- 15 [[Coin of the Cabal]]
- 65 [[Bazaar Crown]]
- [[Vex Thal Crate (Version 1)]]
