## Steps

1. Acquire 10 *kunzite clusters*. These can be mined from the [[Gemstone Nodes]] in [[The Rubble Rabble Burrow]]. {{waypoint -77, -29, -96}}
1. Kill [[A Torsis spectre|Torsis spectre]]s in [[The City of Mist]] until you are automatically rewarded a [[Toris spectre essence]] and [[False Phylactery Recipe]]. {{waypoint -150, 148, -913}}
1. Craft [[False Phylactery]]:
   - **Warning**: Do not commission the phylactery! Doing so will not update the quest but consume the specter essence! You will have to abandon the quest and reacquire it.
   - You must be a level 9 Artisan.
   - Tradeskill Station: [[Work Bench]]
   - Recipe: [[False Phylactery Recipe]] (received automatically)
   - Materials:
     - 1 [[Toris spectre essence]]  (received automatically)
     - 10 [[Rough kunzite]]
     - 2 [[Deklium Bangle (Crafted)|Deklium Bangles]]
       - Crafted by a level 78 Jeweler.
       - Materials:
         - [[Deklium cluster]] x3
         - [[Ferrite cluster]] x1
         - [[Rough kunzite]] x2
         - [[Smoldering Coal]] x1
     - 5 [[Basic Coal]]
1. Speak to [[Skulker Slythe]] at [[Hidden Plunderer's Camp]]. {{waypoint -450, 148, -877}}
