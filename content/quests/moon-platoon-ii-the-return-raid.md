It's time once again to gather friends and family, comrades and allies, and venture into the furthest reaches of the dark side of Luclin and defeat her greatest threats.

## Starting the Quest

Kill any raid boss in any RoS raid zone.

## Steps

- Defeat epic beings across the dark side of Luclin
- Return to [[Nevid Piecrust]]

## Rewards

- At least 7p 48g 44s 138,599 status
- 15 [[Coin of the Cabal]]
- 65 [[Mark of Illumination]]
- [[Cask of Illumination (Tier 10) (Fabled)|Cask of Illumination]]
- [[Status Bounty (50,000)]]
