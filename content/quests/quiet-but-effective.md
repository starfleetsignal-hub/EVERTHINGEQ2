## Prerequisites

- You must complete [[Shimmering Citadel : Poets Palace : The First Mirror.]] and [[Shimmering Citadel : Poets Palace : The Second Mirror.]] before you can access the third floor of [[The Poets Palace]].

## Steps

1. Go to the third floor of [[The Poets Palace]].
1. Make your way to the room with [[Nhjela the Mad Poet]].
1. Harvest 'a blue scarab' on the floor.
1. Return to [[The Mute Bard]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Satchel of Song]]
- [[The Mute Bard's Tale]]
