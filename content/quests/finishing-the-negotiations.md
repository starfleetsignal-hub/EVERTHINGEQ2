## Steps

1. Go Speak to [[Captain Vertas]] at the Crossroads (Note:  He told me "Move along slime" and quest updated...
1. Go back to [[Brandus Levine]] who asks you to...
1. Slay 3 [[An orc runner|orc runners]] (level 12)near {{waypoint -655, -45, 394}}. They run along the southern edge of the Commonlands throughout the orc country.
1. Slay several [[Bloodskull warrior|Bloodskull warriors]] (level 12-15) until the quest journal indicates that you have killed enough. (roughly 10-15 warriors)
1. Examine '[[An encoded message]]' and decode it.
1. Return to Brandus for your reward.
