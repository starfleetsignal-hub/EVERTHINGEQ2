## Prerequisites

- Neriak citizenship

## Steps

![Your goal sparkles in the perpetual gloom](images/The_buried_item.jpg)

1. Hunt treants near the [[Shadow Oak]] around {{waypoint 469, -32, -267}} for a useful stave
1. Find the buried item under a tree on the east side of the [[Tower of Bone]].
   - It will have a glowing indicator like the ?s but with extra sparklies dancing in the air over it that are visible at a distance. {{waypoint 651, -19, 232}}
1. Examine the [[An emblem of The Dead|emblem of The Dead]]
1. Speak with [[Goeyl Z'Vuear]], a Dread Guard in the side courtyard of [[Darklight Palace]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
1. Return to Akorra

## Rewards

- At least 2s 91c
- [[The Spurned Circlet]]
