## Steps

1. You have to bake 3 of the cookies Taldar recommends - [[Chocolate Chip Cookie of Seething Malice]]. To do so you must:
   - Buy at-least 3 [[Dough]] components from the provisioner.
   - Collect the [[Kelryne's Cookie Kit]] on the desk just to Taldar's left *(See talk page)*
   - Collect the recipe on the desk beside the cookie kit *(See talk page).* The recipe for [[Chocolate Chip Cookie of Seething Malice]] will just appear in your recipe book, no need to scribe it.
   - Us the the Stove & Keg to make the cookies.
1. Chocolate Chip Cookies of Seething Malice require (per cookie):
     - Level 2 recipe requirements:
       - 1 qty Kelryne's Cookie Kit
       - 1 qty extract of dark hatred
       - 2 qty dough
       - 5 qty Basic Kindling
     - Level 90 recipe requirements:
       - 1 qty Kelryne's Cookie Kit
       - 1 qty dough
       - 1 qty Effulgent Kindling
1. Speak to Taldar again
   - Find a lost spirit and offer cookies {{waypoint -652, -5, -81}}
   - Find another lost spirit and offer cookies {{waypoint -804, 1, 14}}
   - Find a third lost spirit and offer cookies {{waypoint -726, 5, 129}}
1. Return to Taldar
