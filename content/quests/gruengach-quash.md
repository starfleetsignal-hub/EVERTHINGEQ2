## Steps

1. Kill the following mobs near {{waypoint 297, 285, 208}}:
   - 6 [[A Gruengach haruspex|Gruengach haruspexes]].
   - 6 [[A Gruengach annihilator|Gruengach annihilators]].
   - 6 [[A Gruengach legionnaire|Gruengach legionnaires]].
1. Return to [[Ithurial Ra'Nar]].
