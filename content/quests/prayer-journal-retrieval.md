## Steps

1. You must kill the [[Priest of Byzola]] within the ring of stones in [[The Hold of Malice]] in [[Fallen Gate]]. {{waypoint -147, -16, -250}}
   - Priest of Byzola has a placeholder called [[Priest of Dymetreax]] who is a more common spawn.
1. Return to [[Jhos T'sein]] in [[Nektulos Forest]].
1. Take the journal back to [[Tundis N'oxyle]] in [[The Commonlands]] outside Fallen Gate. {{waypoint 1488, -38, -391}}

## Rewards

- At least 17s 43c
