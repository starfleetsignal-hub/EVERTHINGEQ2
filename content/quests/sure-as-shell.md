## Steps

1. Gather and kill the following:
   - Gather 6 volaserpent eggs from tops of trees in the  [[Esoterra Gardens]].
     - *You will need to search in the base of the foliage at the top. They are smallish and white. {{waypoint -729.21, 71.44, 615.12}}* ![Volaserpent Egg](images/Volaserpent_Egg.jpg)
     - *Gathering an egg causes a temporary uncurable fear, unless you have fear immunity.*
       - Since eggs can be harvested while flying, remain so, limits distance/effect of fear.
   - Kill 10 [[Stalking stoneripper]] in the [[Esoterra Gardens]].
1. Return to [[Tesakias]] at {{waypoint -165, 575, -593}}.
