## Prerequisites

- You must have at least +3800 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Collect 10 [[A kunzar scaleshredder|kunzar scaleshredders]]. {{waypoint -165.81, -105.97, 447.74}}
1. Place the kunzar scaleshredders in the barrel of fish. {{waypoint 686.49, -90.77, 569.90}}
1. Speak to [[Thress Kivatei]]. {{waypoint 683.44, -90.77, 571.09}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- +1,600 faction with **The Order of Rime**
