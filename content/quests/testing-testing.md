## Steps

1. [[Lady Aileeta Reila]] will ask you four questions about the history of the Fae
   - The answers to these questions can be found by reading [[The History of the Fae]]
   - The book will not be complete until you complete the quest [[History of the Fae]]
   - If you are lazy, these are the correct answers:
   1. The first fea queen was named Joleena
   1. The important event was the war of the fay
   1. The brownies of lesser Faydark saved the day
   1. The peaceful ring
