## Steps

1. Kill Murkwater thieves to find a whip handle (rare update).
1. Kill 6 piranhas for their teeth - [[A massive needletooth|massive needleteeth]] {{waypoint 100, 1, -122}}and [[A ravenous piranha|ravenous piranhas]] {{waypoint 117, 2, 105}}
1. Kill [[A foul grappler|foul grapplers]] {{waypoint 160, 2, -28}}or [[A fetid grappler|fetid grapplers]] {{waypoint 81, 0, 115}}for four fishing weights (rare update).

## Rewards

- [[Whip of the Furies]]
