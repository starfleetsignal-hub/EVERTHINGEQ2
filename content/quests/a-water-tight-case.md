## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- This is a subquest of [[A Crusade to Faydwer]].
- An *ewer* is a vase that pours water. An *urn* normally refers to a decorative vase or container. The quest items are called urns in-game except in one piece of quest text, where it refers to them as 'ewers'.

## Steps

1. Quench the *Thirst of Devotion*:
   1. Pick up a [[Living flow urn, empty|living flow urn, empty]] and inspect it from your inventory. {{waypoint -224, 37, -707}} or {{waypoint -224, 37, -1000}}
   1. Complete the subquest, [[The Capture of the Living Flow]], four times.
   1. Go to [[Urn of Devotion]] and fill it with the four [[Living flow urn, filled|living flow urn, filled]]. {{waypoint -126.11, 41.24, -780.49}}
1. Quench the *Thirst of Purity*:
   1. Pick up a 'living flow urn, empty' and inspect it from your inventory. {{waypoint -224, 37, -707}} or {{waypoint -224, 37, -1000}}
   1. Complete the subquest, The Capture of the Living Flow, four times.
   1. Go to [[Urn of Purity]] and fill it with the four 'living flow urn, filled'. {{waypoint -130, 40, -955}}
1. Return to the underwater chest.

## Reward

- *Detective's Manual (Until the first two quests are completed you don't get an item)*
