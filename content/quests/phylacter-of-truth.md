## Notes

- All gathering points are ![yellow shinies](images/Image:Yellow_shiny.gif) with their respective names.

## Steps

1. Gather 20 intricate shells from [[The Bay of Anuk]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] {{waypoint -720, -237, -1323}}
   - Underwater; 10-13 spawn spots, 2 -3 minute respawn, looks like shiny
1. Gather 10 fossilized rocks from from the pond in the [[Remnants of the Lifire]] in [[The Pillars of Flame]] {{waypoint 248, -107, -669}}
1. Gather 15 blue crystal fragments from the outdoor areas in the first level of [[The Clefts of Rujark]].
1. Return to Muyassar.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 23s 29c
- -500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- +500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
