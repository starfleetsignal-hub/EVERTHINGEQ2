## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 50 [[Celestial Sandpaper]]

## Steps

1. Collect the recipe scroll in the Smiths' Gallery in [[Myrist, the Great Library]].
1. Collect the High Qaulity Firing Sheet in the Smiths' Gallery in [[Myrist, the Great Library]].
   - Note: Spawned right nearby after the recipe was collected.
1. Buy [[Prepared Malachite Shards]] for 12p from [[Oleene Wanderweaver]] who wanders around the Smiths' Gallery in [[Myrist, the Great Library]].
1. Take the Teleporter to South Wing Catalog and then the Teleporter to Crafters' Gallery at {{waypoint 332, 16, 174}}. Collect a Planar Binding Kit in the Crafters' Gallery.
1. Collect latch bindings in the Crafters' Gallery.
1. Speak with [[Ghinda Borrell]]  at {{waypoint 660, 412, 59}}.
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Myrist Trunk]] that you collected earlier, craft a Myrist Trunk on a woodworking table and place it at {{waypoint 654, 411, 55}}
   - There is a woodworking table nearby.
1. Use the [[Embolden Spellbook]] [[Ghinda Borrell]] gave you on the newly placed non-operational trunk.
1. Speak with [[Ghinda Borrell]]  at {{waypoint 660, 412, 59}}.

## Rewards

- At least 12p 21g 53s 73c 48000 status
