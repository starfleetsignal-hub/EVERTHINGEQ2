## Steps

1. *Find Information on the Lighteater in Kaesora*
   - Enter [[Kaesora: Xalgozian Stronghold (Solo)|Kaesora: Xalgozian Stronghold [Solo]]] (In Fens) at {{waypoint 344, 20, -1306}} and find a book at {{waypoint -483.02, 11.31, -14.83}} (Heroic instance also has update)
1. Talk to Lissys in Crusaders Cave in [[Obulus Frontier]] at {{waypoint 574, 250, 1260}}
1. Return to Mallic the Venerable {{waypoint 168, -204, -521}}
1. Kill goblins around Twark until you find 8 pieces of the Lighteater Map
1. Enter [[The Destroyer's Refuge]] at {{waypoint 733, -59, -410}}
   - kill [[Grachnist the Destroyer]]
     - *joust 19m+ when he says kill kill kill*
     - *Tough monster, lvl 108, that needs a full group* 340 Resolve
1. Return to Mallic the Venerable {{waypoint 170, -204, -522}}

EQ1 Lore about the goblins of Warslik's Woods:  http://everquest.allakhazam.com/db/npc.html?id=4469
