## Notes

- This is the former access quest for [[Nektropos Castle]], however its completion is no longer required to enter the dungeon. A quest feather is not likely to be visible.
- This quest is also part of the [[Nektropos Castle Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Theeral the nomad]] at the nearby camp, at {{waypoint -305, 0, 176}}. She will give you the [[Flesh Bound Tome]].
1. Examine the tome in your inventory to read it. This tome can be placed in your home.
1. Visit [[Soul Eater Falls]]. The quest update is a specific spot located at {{waypoint 503, 26, -1512}}.
1. Visit the top of [[N'Mar's Ascent]]. {{waypoint -397, 60, -1298}}
1. Visit [[Bone Lake]]. The quest update is on the shore on the north side of the lake, at {{waypoint -866, 141, -2148}}.
1. Speak again with [[Theeral the nomad]] at {{waypoint -305, 0, 176}}.
1. Kill [[A Dragoon lieutenant]] found wandering the ramparts of the [[Citadel of Gul'thex]] {{waypoint 695, 20, -705}} (placeholder is [[A Dragoon sentinel]]) or at ( -1032, 34, -1026).
1. Examine the ward monuments surrounding [[Nektropos Castle (POI)|Nektropos Castle]].
   - The fist ward is at {{waypoint -1483, 112, -1988}}
   - The second ward is at {{waypoint -1586, 112, -1885}}
   - The third ward is at {{waypoint -1386, 112, -1689}}
1. Examine the shrine at {{waypoint -1571, 112, -1991}}
1. Speak to the [[Herald of Nektropos]] at {{waypoint -1402, 121, -1856}} at the front gate of the castle.

## Rewards
