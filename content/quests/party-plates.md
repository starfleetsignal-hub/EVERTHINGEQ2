## Steps

1. Scribe [[Plate Recipes For All Occasions]].
1. Craft a [[Stack of Coldain Plates]]. (You can find a work bench at {{waypoint -336, 177, -26}} )
   - Materials required to craft
     - 1 Electrum Cluster
     - 1 Iron Cluster
     - 1 Rough Turquoise
     - 2 glowing coal
1. Return to [[Envoy Tami Swifthammer|Tami]].

- One of the following, as applicable to your home city:

  - +250 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +250 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +250 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
  - +250 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +250 faction with **Ravens of the North**
