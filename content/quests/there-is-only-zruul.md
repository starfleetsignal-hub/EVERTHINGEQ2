## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
- Quest starter and updates needed for it have a chance to drop from any mob in [[Oogothl Sprawl: Zruul's Dominion (Solo)]]. It may require to resetting and running the zone again if you don't get all updates.

## Steps

*I found a weathered, stone base that once held a statue dedicated to Dominar Zruul while fighting the horrors of Zruul Dominion. The other pieces of this statue must also be here.  If I recover them all, maybe I can reassemble the icon.*

1. Search for a Zruul icon piece.
1. Search for a Zruul icon eye.
1. Search for Zruul icon spikes.
1. Click on Icon at {{waypoint -324, 47, 388}} in Oogothl Sprawl.

## Rewards

- [[Icon of Dominar Zruul]] house item
