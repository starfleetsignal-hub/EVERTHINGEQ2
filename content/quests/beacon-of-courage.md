## Steps

1. Enter [[Crushbone Keep]] in [[Greater Faydark]]. {{waypoint -1275.95, 116.58, 49.48}}
   - Kill 8 [[A Crushbone legionnaire|Crushbone legionnaires]], found primarily in the basement or upper level of the keep.
     - 2 at  {{waypoint 47.16, -39.71, -56.73}}, {{waypoint 63.63, -39, -4.94}}, {{waypoint 24.91, -39, -6}}, {{waypoint 43.57, -39, 15.06}}, {{waypoint 71.18, -39, 42.12}},
   - Kill 4 [[A D'Vinnian dragoon (Crushbone Keep)|D'Vinnian dragoons]], found in the upper level of the keep. Only the dragoons in Crushbone keep will update, not the ones in D'Vinnian Throne.
     - {{waypoint -21.91, 25.08, -15.95}}, {{waypoint -36.95, 25.10, -23.87}}, {{waypoint -50.02, 25.08, -22.91}}, and one wanders near here.
   - Kill 4 [[A Dreadguard knight|Dreadguard knights]], found mostly in the upper level.
     - In the Hall of Tactics {{waypoint 13.80, 25.15, -63.52}}, the Emperor's Chambers {{waypoint -9368, 22.66, 35.14}}, and one at {{waypoint -46.77, 26.10, -34.92}},
   - Free [[A Fae prisoner]]. Pick up the keys in the basement at {{waypoint 57, -39, -63}}, open their cell at {{waypoint 68, -40, -48}} and hail one of them.
   - Free and escort [[A captive wood elf]] back to the entrance of Crushbone Keep. Open the cell at {{waypoint 68, -40, -9}} and hail one to make them follow you.
1. Enter [[The D'Vinnian Throne]] {{waypoint -60, 27, -44}} and kill [[Vindicator Crush]].
   - See the [[Crushbone Keep|dungeon page]] for a guide on how to arrive at Vindicator Crush.
1. Return to Sir Bayden Cauldthorn.

## Rewards

- One of the following items:
  - [[Emblem of Erollisi's Kiss]]
  - [[Mark of Mithaniel's Might]]
- Completing this quest unlocks the [[Glorious Steed]] and [[Sacrifice of Marr]] abilities
- +2500 faction with **Mithaniel Marr**
