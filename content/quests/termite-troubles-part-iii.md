## Steps

1. Concoct a batch of [[Termite sterilization potion]] to prevent the termites from breeding.
   - Scribe the recipe you receive, [[Varick's experimental notes]], an artisan level 50 and craft 5 termite sterilization potion
   - Level 60 artisan combine, chemistry table {{waypoint 10, -54, 833}}, thaumaturgy skill.
   - Components required are the harvested items from the previous quest (1 [[Bracket fungus mycelium|bracket fungus mycelium]], 1 [[Karana's Promise seedpod]], 1 [[Acrid frostberry|acrid frostberry]] in addition to 1 [[Smoldering Candle]] for each combine.
1. Return to [[Varick Stoutheart|Varick]].

## Rewards

- +5000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
