## Description

"Captain Steelforge asked me to take his status report concerning the spirit bloom area to Jenthis Viridar at the Sapling Spur Outpost. It looks like I'm ready to move out to the wild world beyond the Nursery!" -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Jenthis Viridar]] at the [[Sapling Spur Outpost]] at {{waypoint -126.95, 12.24, -117.14}}
