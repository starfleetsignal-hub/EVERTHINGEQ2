## Steps

1. Speak with [[Pinchy Presentpeeler]] to be teleported to [[Elfin Wonderland]].
   - Equip the [[Frostfell Elf Hat]] to avoid the constant slow debuff.
   - Rather than fighting the mobs in the instance, you can use the [[Elfin Snowball Satchel]] to one-shot them.
1. Gather elfin magic:
   - Silver elfin magic, down a tunnel (behind the tent): {{waypoint -258.51, -9.53, -162.05}}
   - Purple Elfin magic, guarded by [[An ice wyrm]]: {{waypoint -206.99, 0.75, -319.68}}
   - Yellow Elfin magic, guarded by [[An ice terror]]: {{waypoint -207.77, 12.84, -350.43}}
   - Red Elfin Magic, guarded by [[An ice shard]]: {{waypoint -219.04, 1.45, -394.25}}
   - Green Elfin Magic, guarded by [[An ice golem]]: {{waypoint -252.61, 0, -415.26}}
1. Confront [[Grimagus Gigglegibber (Elfin Wonderland)|Grimagus Gigglegibber]]. {{waypoint -652, -20, -312}}![Lord Bowsprite's idea of a bridge.](images/Rainbow_bridge_%28Elfin_Wonderland%29.jpg)
   - Go to the ice wall in the east of the zone and speak with [[Lord Bowsprite]], who will spawn at {{waypoint -471, -1, -358}}. Walk over the rainbow bridge that he creates.
1. Speak with Lord Bowsprite, who is now standing beside Grimagus.
1. Speak with Grimagus again.
1. Take one of the presents next to Grimagus, this completes this quest and [[The Tale of the Gigglegibber Grump]]. Speak with Lord Bowsprite to return to a city of your choosing.

## Rewards

- [[Frostfell Elf Cap of Superior Warmth]]
- You also receive credit towards the "Festive Spirit" Live Event Achievement.
