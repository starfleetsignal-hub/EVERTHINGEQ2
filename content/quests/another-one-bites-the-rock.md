## Steps

1. Gather 5 [[A toxic glow toad|toxic glow toads]] {{waypoint -283.64, 191.45, 264.85}}.
   - You'll need to sneak up on the aggro toads (get within 20 meters), then simply right-click and select **Gather Toxic Toad**.
   - Note: This quest and the one after it do not group update.
1. Use the toad on [[A crag gorger|crag gorgers]] along the [[Abyssal Overlook]] {{waypoint -212.25, 71.71, 110.98}}.
   - Wait by the toad until the gorger becomes non-aggro, then right-click on the gorger to collect it.
1. Release the [[A crag gorger|crag gorgers]] on the clickable metamorphic rock at {{waypoint -322.24, 232.20, 396.96}}.
1. **Retrieve the staff** and then return to [[Thaneni]].
