## Notes

This quest was put in late in the Beta to guide players back into the rat caves until they found the quest starter for [[Scribblings on the Wall]]. It becomes available after completing [[Joining Their Ranks]] and is repeatable until you finally accept [[Scribblings on the Wall]].

## Steps

[[Alwari Yuk'sel]] is tired of the vermin sneaking into [[The City of Paineel]] nightly and stealing supplies. Alwari has asked me to go into the vermin cave and dispatch any that I come across. This should send a message that this behavior will no longer be tolerated by the erudites.

1. I should dispatch several vermin in the cave for [[Alwari Yuk'sel]] to deliver the message that their thieving behavior will no longer be tolerated..
   - I should kill several (12) vermin in the nearby cave.
1. Return to [[Alwari Yuk'sel]]

## Rewards

- At least 46g
