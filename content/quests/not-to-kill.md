## Steps

1. Collect an iron golem:
   - Use the [[Iron golem shrinking ray]] on [[An iron construct]] in [[The City of Mist]]. {{waypoint -166.27, 134.40, -869.81}}
   - Use the [[Shrunken iron golem leash]] on the resulting [[A shrunken iron golem|shrunken iron golem]].
   - Return to Ebenezer Sprockbok.
1. Collect a jade golem:
   - Use the [[Jade golem shrinking ray]] on [[A jade construct]]  in the City of Mist.
   - Use the [[Shrunken jade golem leash]] on the resulting [[A shrunken jade golem|shrunken jade golem]].
   - Return to Ebenezer Sprockbok.
1. Speak to Sprockbok again.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
