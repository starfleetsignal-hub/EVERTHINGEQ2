## Prerequisites

- Your faction with [[The City of Kelethin (Faction)|City of Kelethin]] must be Indifferent or better to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Take the package to Tuilla Laarivera at the Court of the Fae {{waypoint -268.62, -56.05, 404.30}}.
1. Get the enchanted prayer tablet from Tuilla.
1. Travel to [[The Shadowed Grove]] and kill [[Bristlecone]] (60^^^ Heroic) <Approach Obelisk entrance to spawn him, spawns at {{waypoint 818.90, -40.20, 430.51}} (NW of the tower)>
1. Return to the Duchess with 'a spirit infused tablet'

## Rewards

- At least 22g 55s 00c
- +250 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Polished Scorpion Carapace Bracelet]]
  - [[Polished Scorpion Claw Bracelet]]
  - [[Polished Scorpion Shell Bracelet]]
  - [[Polished Scorpion Tail Bracelet]]
