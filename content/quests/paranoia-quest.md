## Steps

1. [[Captain Boshinko]] has asked that I kill a 'rat' in the larder. He seems to be insinuating that I take care of something else, but I will check it out anyway.
   - Kill [[Gnawbone]] {{waypoint -116.82, -132.19, -30.36}}
     - *Note: To kill this mob you must pay attention to the red script message that pops periodically through the fight. The message basically says the hell-hound is about to regurgitate on you and in order to save yourself you must get behind some crates or counter and crouch (have to actually use the crouch command (default "z" on the keyboard), not sit). If you fail to do so in time, you are one-shot dead.*
1. Return to [[Captain Boshinko]]
1. [[Captain Boshinko]] now requests you kill a "guard in the shadows".  Kill [[Horrot the Horrific]] {{waypoint -50.00, -56.56, -10.82}} then return to [[Captain Boshinko]].
1. He tells you that you were too late and he is now under investigation by the Committee; of course, you now need to kill them.  Kill [[Ryvast]], Kill [[Trysk]], and Kill [[Nymeer]]. {{waypoint -62, -77, 90}}
1. Destroy any evidence they may have collected against [[Captain Boshinko]] - 12 clicky pages on the floor of the Sanctum of the Shadowmancers (Globe room), in the stairs leading to the Bloodpack Hunting Grounds one level lower, and all around that room as well.
1. Return to [[Captain Boshinko]]
1. Place evidence in Higginsbot's old jail cell
   - You need to kill an "undead warden" who wanders in the lower regions and loot "a skeleton key" which is a body drop. It is LORE TEMPORARY which means it despawns when you use it, zone out or when you camp. Track would be useful here. (Found him twice at {{waypoint -72, -110, 27}} look up) and another loc was {{waypoint -21, -36, 61}}
   - The jail door is at the bottom of the stairs on the right when you go down into the tunnels from the Casino area. {{waypoint -103, -145, -183}}
   - This is the same cell that you end up in when [[Bigs the Big]] catches you in the original [[High Keep: The Pickclaw Depths]] instance.  Near {{waypoint -185, -141, -240}}
1. Return to [[Captain Boshinko]] to complete this quest and pick up the next one.

## Reward

- at least 1p 25g
