## Steps

1. Speak to [[Lord Titurius Ocellus]] in West Freeport {{waypoint 74, -20, -5}}
1. In [[The Graveyard]], speak to [[Volusa Tuditania]] at [[The Commonlands]] gate {{waypoint 81, -1, 23}}
1. Patrol the Graveyard:
   - [[The Gallows]] {{waypoint 36, -3, 2}}
   - [[The Destroyed Tower of Marr]] {{waypoint 56, -2, 102}}
   - [[Longstone Courtyard]] (the bell) {{waypoint -59, -10, 98}}
   - [[Traitors' Terrace]] {{waypoint -45, -11, 62}}
   - [[Gate to Sunken City]] {{waypoint -81, -14, -93}}
1. Patrol [[Scourge Tombs]] {{waypoint 0, -2, -93}}
1. Kill 3 [[A Qeynosian spy|Qeynosian spies]] {{waypoint 25, -2, -79}} (heroic, even con and 2 one downs, ungrouped)
1. Speak to [[Guard Kludd]] at the Commonlands gate {{waypoint 87, -1, 24}}
1. Speak to Lord Ocellus in West Freeport

## Rewards

- [[Lucanic Knight's Breastplate]]
- Title: [[Lucanic Knight Adherent]]
- +1000 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
