## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
- To get to [[Nightscar Borne Lands]] area use the [[Longhowl]] at {{waypoint -282, 25, -5}}. To get back to [[Nightscar Steppes]] use [[Longhowl]] at {{waypoint -806, 12, -715}}.

![malformed voidspawn](images/A_malformed_voidspawn.png)
![Nightblood cocoon](images/Nightblood_cocoon.png)

## Steps

1. The nightbloods of the Legion of the Oblivion are most defenseless when they are still in cocoons, lying on the edge of pools in [[Nightscar Borne Lands]].
   - hunt 12 x  malformed voidspawn
   - destroy 8 cocooned nightbloods around {{waypoint -963, 28, -1134}}
1. return to [[Vasynea]] at {{waypoint -298, 25, -19}}

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
