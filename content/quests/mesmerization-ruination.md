## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
- This quest can be done as a group and leads to all the other prelude quests including the tradeskill quests.

## Steps

1. Ride "a geomanced transport" disc at {{waypoint 303, 188, 452}} in Butcherblock Mountains (Highlands Outpost)
1. Locate the purple/bluish portal at {{waypoint 131, 213, -179}}  ![](images/Mesmerization_Ruination_quest_-_Spellbind_Stave.jpg)
1. Enter the portal selecting "Kamp Krulkiel: Retribution"
1. Destroy 8 **Spellbind Staves** and kill 18 Krulkiel
1. Defeat the Krulkiel Warlord, [[Grikrut]]
1. Leave the instance via the portal at {{waypoint 111, 249, -326}}

## Rewards

- +1750 faction with **Open Hand**
