## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Boiled Toolbelt for my apprentice.
   - Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Boiled Toolbelt]]
   - Craft [[A Sturdy Boiled Toolbelt]] on a [[Loom]] using the following materials and fuel:
     - 2 [[Boiled leather pelt|boiled leather pelts]]
     - 4 [[Sparkling filament]]
1. I need to take this toolbelt back to my apprentice.
