## Steps

- Obtain 12 Tera'Noth bark from Lumberheads, bristly devourers, briarroots or greenwoods.
- Obtain 12 Tera'Noth pollen from florphilim, mal blooms, or resonance of growth.

Return to the encampment and fill the Reinforced Strongbox

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- [[Imperial Hero's Mint]] x2
