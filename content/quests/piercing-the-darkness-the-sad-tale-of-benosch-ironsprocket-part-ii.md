## Materials Required

At least:

- 30 [[Humming Azurite]]
- 12 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
- 5 [[Luclizite Cluster]]
- 12 [[Coruscating Coal]]
- A WELL-ARMED escort if planning to harvest directly in front of Hive as there are many Shik'Nar who will one-shot Tradeskillers

### Suggested

[[Portable Work Bench]] or Summon Crafting Stations AA<br>
Make sure you have the necessary fuel, or a fuel merchant summon item, as there is no Fuel Merchant in [[Recuso Tor]]

## Steps

1. Investigate the [[Shik'Nar Hive]] near {{waypoint 180.38, 45.87, 190.48}}.  *Note: It's at the base of the meteor.*
1. Harvest 100 materials to receive [[Recipe Scroll: Insectoid Communication Jammer]]. *Note: The recipe auto-scribes.*
   - Any harvesting location between The Hive entrance and Teeming Caldera will update quests.
   - There are many non-aggro mobs around the pools at {{waypoint -59.89, 8.64, -3.00}}
1. Craft the [[Insectoid Communication Jammer]] on a [[Work Bench]].
   - The nearest [[Work Bench]] can be found in [[Recuso Tor]] at {{waypoint -761.91, 52.08, 333.24}}.
1. Use the [[Insectoid Communication Jammer]] and click on the wall near {{waypoint 297.67, 40.32, 196.12}} to enter [[Shik'Nar Hive: The Sad Tale of Benosch Ironsprocket]].

NOTE: If you've used a summoned work bench, wait 2 minutes or so before using the jammer, you'll get to the next part of the quest and have to wait to summon a work bench otherwise, and use up the jammer timer.

## Rewards

- At least 14p 21g 53s 73c 28,596 status
- At least 14p 21g 53s 73c 27,288 status at Level 118
