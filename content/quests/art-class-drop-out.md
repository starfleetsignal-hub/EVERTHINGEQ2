## Steps

1. Collect the following stones for [[Alishinu]]:
   - A ***resonant zeolite***, a small grey rock found in the [[Metetherial Plains]] at any of the following locations: ![resonant zeolite](images/Resonant_zeolite.jpg)
     - {{waypoint -4.97, 62.11, 187.92}}
     - {{waypoint 1.07, 31.20, 358.39}}
     - {{waypoint 130.96, 51.45, 164.60}}
     - {{waypoint -55.59, 37.30, 245.00}}
   - 4 ***ignis crystals*** from killing wisps like:
     - [[Blazing ignus]] {{waypoint 618.76, 51.24, -122.99}} in the [[Aetherscar]] (up in the air).
     - [[Chiming ignus]] {{waypoint 358.09, -147.90, 897.23}} in the [[Amphitheater of Song]].
     - [[Flickering ignus]] {{waypoint -720.80, 359.87, 1064.34}} on the platform of [[Valor's Roost]].
     - [[Ignus dancer|ignus dancers]] {{waypoint 1189.24, 771.80, -1402.79}} by the [[Scintillating Spires]].
     - [[Ignus remnant|ignus remnants]] {{waypoint 766.19, 38.38, -298.87}} in the [[Aetherscar]].
1. Return the rocks to [[Alishinu]] {{waypoint -30.78, 68.43, 124.76}} at the western bank of the northern [[Myhst River]].
1. Speak to her again after she shaped the stones.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- +1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
