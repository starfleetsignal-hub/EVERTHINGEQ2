   - Catalogue [[An obsidian rocklord]] in the [[Elemental Mesa]] around {{waypoint 243.44, 84.46, 333.60}}
   - Catalogue [[A greater inferno fiend]] in the [[Elemental Mesa]] around {{waypoint 243.44, 84.46, 333.60}}
   - Catalogue [[A jopal lavahurler]] on [[Fennin's Rise]] around {{waypoint -195.61, 26.97, 70.42}}
   - Catalogue [[A diviner of fire]] at the [[Darkened Fissures]] around {{waypoint -210.38, 45.87, 304.57}}
   - Catalogue [[A priest of fire]] at the [[Doomfire Sanctuary]] around {{waypoint 358, 8, -246}}
   - Catalogue [[An obsidian lava spider]] at the [[Darkened Fissures]] around {{waypoint -210.38, 45.87, 304.57}}
   - Catalogue [[A Doomfire phoenix]] on the [[Blazeplate Rise]] around {{waypoint -347, 61, -339}}
   - Catalogue [[A Doomfire ragefiend]] at the [[Corilac Tower]] around {{waypoint -196.74, 17.62, -403.94}} or on the [[Molten Span]] around {{waypoint 39.20, -9.43, -285.20}}
   - Catalogue [[A Doomfire pyrefiend]] at the [[Corilac Tower]] around {{waypoint -196.74, 17.62, -403.94}} or on the [[Molten Span]] around {{waypoint 39.20, -9.43, -285.20}}
   - Catalogue [[A Doomfire Guardian]] at the [[Corilac Tower]] around {{waypoint -196.74, 17.62, -403.94}} or on the [[Molten Span]] around {{waypoint 39.20, -9.43, -285.20}}. May be be a rarer spawn, so keep killing Doomfires until one spawns.
   - Catalogue [[A giant lava mulcher]] at the [[Molten Crossroads]] around {{waypoint 201.83, -9.45, -304.35}}
   - Catalogue [[A defender of fire]] at the [[Darkened Fissures]] around {{waypoint -210.38, 45.87, 304.57}}
   - Catalogue [[A watcher of fire]] at the [[Darkened Fissures]] around {{waypoint -210.38, 45.87, 304.57}}
1. Catalogue then defeat a [[Wilder of Ash]] at {{waypoint 380.62, 53.05, 41.88}}
