## Description

*A travesty has befallen Maldura. One of its own has initiated an invasion from within! I need to help the Shields of Maldura and save the district.*

## Zone

- Destroy enemies within [[Maldura: Algorithm For Destruction (Advanced Solo)]]

## Steps

- Defeat the ashen one
  - [[Old Doclin (Advanced Solo))|Old Doclin]]
- Defeat the prime defender
  - [[X-O Arachmech (Advanced Solo))|X-O Arachmech]]
- Destroy the summoned golem
  - [[Stonerend (Advanced Solo))|Stonerend]]
- Defeat the pump operators
  - [[Undying Apprentice (Advanced Solo))|Undying Apprentice]], [[Undying Mechanic (Advanced Solo))|Undying Mechanic]] & [[Undying Technician (Advanced Solo))|Undying Technician]]
- Kill the leader of the attack
  - [[Black Bolt (Advanced Solo))|Black Bolt]]
