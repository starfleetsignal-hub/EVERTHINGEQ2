The Shields of Maldura are hogging all the fun! Time to get in on this action!

## Notes

- You have 8 minutes to complete this quest.
- It's best to clear everything up through Gavitzle before starting this quest.

## Steps

1. Kill 20 [[An immolated invader|immolated invaders]] that are in combat with Shields of Maldura guards.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 26g 80s 40000 status
- 3 [[Coin of the Eternal Queen]]
