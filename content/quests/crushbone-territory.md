## Description

"Tuathil Laeds, a fae stationed near the Crushbone mount station, would like me to obtain an up-to-date map of Crushbone territory to help him plan out his operations. He would like me to kill the Crushbone conscripts to the north until I find one with a map." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill [[A Crushbone conscript (Greater Faydark)|Crushbone conscripts]] at the [[Road to Crushbone]] around {{waypoint -590, 66, -87}} until you find a map
1. Return to [[Tuathil Laeds]]

## Rewards

- At least 2s 40c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Archon's Barbute (Version 3)|Archon's Barbute]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Hat (Version 2)|Channeler's Hat]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Helm (Version 2)|Earthkeeper's Helm]] (Druids)
  - [[Elder's Helm (Version 3)|Elder's Helm]] (Shamans)
  - [[Enlightened Kasa (Version 2)|Enlightened Kasa]] (Brawlers)

  - [[Shade's Cowl (Version 3)|Shade's Cowl]] (All Scouts)

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Tuathil Laeds: "[Player Character]?"
- Player Character: "Yes."
- Tuathil Laeds: "Good. I've been expecting you. I am Tuathil Laeds. Welcome to Crushbone territory. Seren recommended you to me. I hope you don't mind us pulling you to the front lines out here, but we need fighters of your caliber, before things get too out of control."
- Player Character: "I am glad to help."
- Tuathil Laeds: "Good to hear. I've been working out a plan for our initial operations. I'd like you to take part in them. Before I can do more work, however, I need a more accurate description of what lays deeper within orc territory. That's where you come in. The path to the north is overflowing with Crushbone conscripts. Occasionally, they will hold an updated map of troop deployment for the Crushbone region."
- Player Character: "And you'd like me to get one of these maps?"
- Tuathil Laeds: "Precisely."
- Player Character: "All right."

I need a map of Crushbone territory from a Crushbone conscript to the north.

I have obtained a map of Crushbone territory. I need to bring this map to Tuathil.

- Tuathil Laeds: "Have you acquired the map?"
- Player Character: "Yes."

I have given Tuathil the map. I have given Tuathil the Crushbone territory map.

I have brought Tuathil an updated Crushbone territory map.
