## Steps

1. [[Akina]] has some very private information to convey and wishes to speak to you outside of Qeynos.
   - Travel to [[Antonica]] and activate [[Akina's Spirit Token]] to allow her to converse with you via astral presence.
1. Gather powerful components for use in a ceremony to disrupt the use of necromancy within Qeynos.
   - Gather 8 [[A chunk of luclinite|chunks of luclinite]], body drops from [[A mutated luclinite beetle|mutated luclinite beetles]] in and around [[Crater Pond]] in [[Antonica]]. {{waypoint 134, -15, 442}}
   - Gather [[Essence of Death]] from the [[The Tomb of Varsoon|Tomb of Varsoon]] in Antonica. There are black bubbling harvestable clouds around the rotunda of the tomb. {{waypoint 360, -20, -662}}
   - Gather [[Essence of Life]] where the [[Temple of Life]] meets the pond below. Click on the copper spikes that touch the water to receive the update.
1. Activate [[Akina's Spirit Token]] to receive further instructions from Akina (this can be done within the city).
   - Akina explains the creation of the Animus Portal.
   - Once you agree, she will transport you to another plane of existence, where you listen to dialog from [[Rodcet Nife]], [[Quellious]], [[Tunare]], and [[Mithaniel Marr]].
   - The gods give you a spell, Celestial Avatar, and return you to the [[Temple of Life]].
1. Return to Akina in [[The Temple of Life (Zone)|The Temple of Life]].
   - Place the luclinite chunks at the shiny spots around the center of the Temple.
   - Use the Celestial Avatar spell to defeat the [[Unstable Avatar]] that will appear near {{waypoint 570, -75, -200}} .
     - Celestial Avatar lasts only 20 seconds, but has a 38 second cooldown. Use it wisely! Death prevents may be in order.
     - If the Unstable Avatar kills you, you can restart the ceremony by clicking one of the chunks of luclinite.
1. Speak to Akina.
