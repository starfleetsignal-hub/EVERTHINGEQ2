## Notes

This is part 1 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Antonica]] who wear Leather ([[Brawler]]/[[Druid]]/Channeler) and Chain Armor ([[Shaman]]).

## Steps

1. Kill the following
   - 12 [[A Caltorsis cleric|Caltorsis clerics]] (around [[The Ruins of Caltorsis]] and [[Fangbreaker Keep]] in [[Antonica]])
   - 4 [[A Caltorsis knight|Caltorsis knights]] for fetishes (around [[The Ruins of Caltorsis]])
1. Return to Selwyn

(If you have quests near [[Windstalker Village]], it is the easiest place to find all of the Caltorsis clerics.)
