## Steps

1. Kill:
   - Kill 7 [[A Skirth soulstalker|skirth soulstalkers]].
   - Kill 7 [[An Orafic soulscreecher|Orafic soulscreechers]].
   - Kill 4 [[A Tirun soulcrusher|Tirun soulcrushers]].
1. confront [[Draazak the Ancient (NPC)|Draazak]] at {{waypoint 611, 362, 140}}.
1. Return to Leizoth.

- At least 1p 30g
