This quest is repeatable up to 4 times.

## Steps

1. Kill 7 [[A snaptail devourer|snaptail devourers]] in [[The Highland Salts]].
   - This quest can be repeated up to 4 times.
- Take the transport to the Stoneburnt Encampment Station snaptails are just down the ramp. {{waypoint 922, 111, 3747}}

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +100 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Seekers of the Dark Truth**
