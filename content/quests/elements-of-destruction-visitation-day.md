## Steps

1. Use the teleporter behind [[Quellious]] at {{waypoint 443, 15, 229}} to enter the [[Plane of Justice: Eternal Prisons]].
1. Slay the [[A Sentry of Justice|Sentry of Justice]] patrolling the corridor.
1. Speak with [[Druzzil Ro]] at {{waypoint 46, -68, -14}} through the upper bars of her cell for an update.
   - Alternatively, pick up a [[Mithril cell key]] by harvesting one of the random green shineys in the area
   - Use the key to open cell door to gain access to [[An enforcer]] at {{waypoint 29, -68, -15}} and kill him.
   - Loot his [[Justicarium cell key]] to enter Druzzil Ro's cell and speak to her for an update.
1. Return to the Great Library and speak to [[Maelin Starpyre]] at {{waypoint 244, -7, -26}}

- [[Tanaanite Spellwoven Leggings]] - Mercenary Legs
