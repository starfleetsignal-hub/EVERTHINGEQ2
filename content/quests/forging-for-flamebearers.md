## Notes

This quest is infinitely repeatable while this prelude event is active.

When accepting quest you will have 2 recipes scribed into your book - [[Pyre Plate Armor Piece]] and [[Blaze-forged Weapon]], you will be asked to craft 6 of either one chosen randomly.

- You can buy [[Glowing Coal]] from [[A Flamebearer Supply Master]] nearby.
- Each combine requires 1 x [[Chaotic Elemental Filament]] + 2 of any common harvested leather pelts + 3 of any common harvested roots + 2 of any common harvested metal + 3 Glowing Coals. Each combine makes 1 - 2 items.
- You can use Forge next to Zedock or any other Forge to craft.

## Steps

1. Check smiths' crate by Zedock to find out what exactly needs to be crafted this time.
1. Craft the required 6 items.
1. Speak with Zedock Runegust.

## Rewards

- tradeskill experience
- AA experience (only on first quest completion )
- at least 3=5p 4=54g
- 2 x Brass Lambent
