*This quest is part of the [[Freeport Outlying Areas Timeline]].*

## Steps

- Zaddar gives you a [[Relic of Unholy Power]].
1. Go to [[The Destroyed Tower of Marr]] {{waypoint 60, 0, 100}}. You will find a ring of flames (Mystical Aura of Marr) in front of the tower.
1. Right-click the Mystical Aura of Marr and select "Place the relic". You will receive minor damage.
1. Right-click the relic and select "Pick up the relic".
1. Return to [[Custodian Zaddar Sullissia]]. {{waypoint -73, -13, 107}}

## Rewards

- [[The Unholy Relic of Qua'ddathul]]
- +150 faction with **The Academy of Arcane Science**
