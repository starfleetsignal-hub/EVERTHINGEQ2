## Steps

1. Kill 8 Corrupted Squatters or Corrupted Tenders to receive [[Corrupted Soil]]
   - found near {{waypoint -2495, -718, 100}} (and other nearby camps).
1. Dump the corrupted soil at the spot Silnas Spoke about {{waypoint -2375, -666.55, -214}}.
1. kill 12 insects (beetles and/or flying bugs) to receive [[Insect Carcasses]].
   - found near {{waypoint -2542, -548, -1148}}.
1. Add the insect carcasses to the soil at {{waypoint -2375, -666.55, -214}}.
1. Return to Silnas, who will give you [[A withering sapling leash]] and tell you to lead a sapling to the new soil.
1. Find a total of 3 [[A withering sapling|withering saplings]] in the area surrounding the [[Sanctuary of Waning]]. Use the [[A withering sapling leash|withering sapling leash]] on each of them (1 at a time), and lead them back to the soil at {{waypoint -2375, -666.55, -214}}.
1. Return to Silnas for your reward.
