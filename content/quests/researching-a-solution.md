Brytthel is concerned that the stone in the Queen's crown - her responsibility to safeguard - may be what's attracting all the recent attacks. I asked if there is any way to hide its power, but she's unsure. She says it and another similar stone originally came from the glaufaye who live in the forest outside, so I'm going to ask them if they know of any way to hide these powerful items.

## Steps

1. Exit the city to [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]] and speak to [[Elanluelle]] {{waypoint -486.30, 239.76, 349.24}} in [[Zou'Lidelas]].
1. Return to [[Maldura]] and find the following three books in the [[Alcove of Reason]]:
   - [[Binding of Power With Metal]] at {{waypoint -85, 12, 167}}
   - [[Setting and Mounting With Magic]] at {{waypoint -88, 13, 179}}
   - [[Dwarven Relics: Myths of Creation]] at {{waypoint -87, 14, 198}}
     - *These are small, open books that can appear in a variety of locations on and around the shelves within the [[Alcove of Reason]].*
     - *Often found along the floor close to the base of the book cases near [[Brumley Langboom|Brumley]].*
     - Need to examine them close up - mousing at a distance doesn't help.
1. Use the ancient notes to plan out a recipe for a method of containing magical gemstones.
   - Use an engraved Desk to craft [[Recipe for a Censer of Containment]].
   - There is an Engraved Desk next to [[Brytthel|Brumley]].
1. Return to [[Brytthel]] at {{waypoint 370.65, 82.35, 28.72}}.
