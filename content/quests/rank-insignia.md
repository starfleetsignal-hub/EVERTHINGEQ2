## Background

I should keep my eye out for these rank insignia to properly identify each soldier or officer.  Once I have this information I can give it to [[Gaer Torrent]].

## Notes

- You will be randomly offered the quest after killing a [[A Blacktalon initiate|Blacktalon initiate]]. Keep killing them on the first floor and the quest will eventually pop.

## Steps

1. Kill various mobs to acquire each of the ranks. The mobs will drop (auto-update) the rank insignia on the first kill:
   - Rank 1  - [[A Blacktalon initiate]]
   - Rank 2  - [[A Blacktalon palace guard]]
   - Rank 3  - [[A Blacktalon disciple]]
   - Rank 4  - [[A Blacktalon coadjutant]]
   - Rank 5  - [[A Blacktalon palace knight]]
   - Rank 6  - [[A Blacktalon partisan]]
   - Rank 7  - [[A Blacktalon vanquisher]]
   - Rank 8  - [[A Bloodscale enforcer]]
   - Rank 9  - [[A Bloodscale high priest]]
   - Rank 10 - [[A Bloodscale shadowslayer]]
   - Rank 11 - [[A Bloodscale dreadknight]]
1. Bring the information to [[Gaer Torrent]] on [[The Whisperwind Isle]] in [[The Barren Sky]]. {{waypoint -578, -46, -387}}

## Rewards

- At least 18g 85s 15c
- +75 faction with **The Windgazer**
