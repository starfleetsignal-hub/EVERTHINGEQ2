## Steps

1. Kill Terrok mobs for a [[Terrok ringlet]].
   1. Examine the [[Terrok Strongbox]] {{waypoint 194.5, 17, -66}} inside the [[Stormguard Hall]].
   1. Kill [[Great Grenadier Gonga]] {{waypoint 112, 28, -103}}
      - Placeholder is two [[A Terrok watcher|Terrok watchers]] and [[A Terrok enforcer]] (64^^ and 64^^^ Heroic respectively).
   1. Kill [[Commander Ursus]] {{waypoint 195, 17.5, -93}} inside the [[Stormguard Hall]].
      - Placeholder is three [[A Terrok battlelord|Terrok battlelords]] and [[A Terrok chantlord]] (64^ - 64^^ Heroic).
   1. Kill [[Rampart]] (64^^^ Heroic) with three adds, all are  {{waypoint 183.5, 28, -179}}
      - Placeholder is [[A Rampart trainer]] (63^^^ - 64^^^ Heroic) with three . The placeholder will also give update.
   1. Kill [[A Terrok armsmaster]] {{waypoint 190, 17, -196}}
   1. Kill [[A Terrok cartographer]] {{waypoint 163.5, 17, -90}} 64^^ with 4^ adds
1. Return and examine [[Terrok Strongbox]].
