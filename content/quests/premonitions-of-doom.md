## Notes

- Do not kill the following named monsters as you move through the instance: [[Doom Guard Vimock]], [[Jin'tiz the Cataloguer]], or [[Katalkana]]. In order to complete this quest they all need to be alive when you defeat [[Doom Lord Septimus]].
- In order to open the locked door at {{waypoint 119, 1, -125}}, you will need the [[Doomwing Key]], which can be found at {{waypoint 137, 10, -133}}. (I found the key at {{waypoint 134, 11, -132}}.

## Steps

1. Usurp the identity of [[Doom Lord Septimus]]. You must pass by [[Doom Guard Vimock]], [[Jin'tiz the Cataloguer]], and [[Katalkana]] without killing them.
   1. Loot the [[Refined Mutagenic Potion]] on the table in front of [[Jin'tiz the Cataloguer]]. {{waypoint 120, -27, 20}}
      - Only one member of the group can pick this up.
      - To distract Jin'tiz, pick up the vial of [[Volatile Fluid]] at {{waypoint 98, -27, 5}} and use it on the large egg at {{waypoint 126, -27, -13}}. Jin'tiz will emote and they come flying over to the egg, allowing you to pick up the mutagenic potion without aggroing him.
      - Alternatively, you can cast [[Soothe]] on him. However this may fail and cause him to aggro. If he does aggro, make sure someone grabs the potion and let the group wipe.
   1. Examine the Refined Mutagenic Potion to be offered the subquest [[Transmogrification]]. This sub-quest requires you to obtain a scale from the body of Doom Lord Septimus to refine the Refined Mutagenic Potion.
   1. Kill [[Sothis]] and pick up the [[Torpor Draught]] on a table in his room at {{waypoint -9, -69, 291}}.
   1. Place the draught in the 'Bubbling Cauldron' that [[Katalkana, Steward of the Legion]] walks near to put him to sleep. {{waypoint -23, -68, 449}}
   1. Kill Doom Lord Septimus. {{waypoint -123, -68, 454}}
   1. Once Septimus is dead the Transmogrification subquest will update, and the person who has the quest must drink the [[Transmogrification Elixir]] that has been placed in their inventory.
   1. Hail [[Watcher Ningiza]], who will spawn nearby after a short delay. {{waypoint -108.36, -71.77, 443.96}}
1. Speak with the three officers who you spared. One after the other, Doom Guard Vimock, Jin'tiz the Cataloguer, and Katalkana will come into the Rookery, lay a chest down, and be available for dialogue.
1. Return to [[Nepther'tum]] at the [[Drednever Crash Site (POI)|Drednever Crash Site]] in [[The Bonemire]]. {{waypoint -7, 44, 522}}
