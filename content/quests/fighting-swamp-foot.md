## Note

*You need to be tradeskill level 50 to begin the quest*.  It is part of the "ship out" Trade skill series.

## Steps

1. Scribe the received recipe book [[Mug's Secret Family Pattern for Ykeshan Leather Boots]] then craft 40 [[Ykeshan leather boots]] (you can replace the recipe book and get more Ykeshan leather from the chest behind him).
   - Level 70 tradeskill combine, geomancy skill, sewing table & mannequin.
   - Sewing tables are available at Grobb, Tupta, or the Dropship Landing Zone.
   - Each combine requires:
     - 1 [[Supple Ykeshan leather]] (received from [[Mug]])
     - 2 [[Bristled pelt|bristled pelts]]
     - 2 [[Lichenclover root|lichenclover roots]]
     - 5 [[Smoldering filament|smoldering filaments]]
   - Gives 2-8 [[Ykeshan leather boots|boots]] per combine (based on completion tier, so 8 for pristine). At pristine you will need 5 combines worth.
1. Return to [[Mug (NPC)|Mug]]

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- At least 80g 17s 37c
- +3000 faction with **Survival Accord**
- +3000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
- +3000 faction with **Clan Grobb**
- +750 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- Opens up the quest [[Firmroot Moot Meet]] when [[Ship Out]] is completed
