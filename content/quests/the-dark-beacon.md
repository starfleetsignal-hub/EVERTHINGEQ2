## Steps

1. Speak with [[Tanath L'Vec (Nektulos)|Tanath L'Vec]] underneath [[N'Mar's Ascent]] in [[Nektulos Forest]]. {{waypoint -380, 1, -1270}}
1. Speak with each of the different aspects of Tanath ("Tanath's Shadows") around the room in the following order:
   - NW, SW, SE, NE, NW, SW, SE
1. Speak with Tanath L'Vec again. This time select the new (middle) dialogue option. After the dialogue is complete, click the beacon next to Tanath to collect it.
1. Return to [[Xilania Nevagon (Greater Faydark)|Xilania Nevagon]] to complete the quest and claim your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 27g 50s
- +10000 faction with **Innoruuk**
- One of the following:
  - [[Cloak of Hatred]]
  - [[Drape of Hatred]]
