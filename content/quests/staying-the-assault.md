## Description

"Gomphum needs help defending Dyer Mycoria from the grobin assault. Apparently the grobins tunneled into the Shroomba's city. Gomphum would like me to kill the grobin diggers who have invaded the western germinarium." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay 5 [[A grobin digger|grobin diggers]] around {{waypoint 102, -46, -285}}
1. Return to [[Gomphum]].

## Rewards

- At least 6s 18c
