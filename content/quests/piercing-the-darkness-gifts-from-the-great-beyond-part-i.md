## Materials Required / Notes

- 22 [[Splitfin Dartfish]]
- 32 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
- 24 [[Humming Azurite]]
- 20 [[Coruscating Coal]]
- Suggested: [[Portable Work Bench]] or summon crafting stations AA.

*Note: If you do not get updated examine the journal again, or try to use the [[Hooked Gardening Tool]] in your appearance slot.  Examine the Journal while in the Aurelian Coast.*

## Steps

1. Gather 3 [[Mysterious object (no-trade no-value no-destroy)|mysterious object]] (trackable) in the [[Boneyard Bog]] near {{waypoint -242.54, 2.60, 71.69}}.
   - *Note: Location {{waypoint -230.50, 3.40, -134.85}}, {{waypoint -215.31, 2.35, -180.70}}, {{waypoint -171, 2, 15}}, {{waypoint -330, 2, -165}}, {{waypoint -283, 3, -24}}*
   - *Note: They look like large shells. Not guaranteed updates (you will often receive "a broken object", not the "mysterious object" you are looking for)*
   - *Note: Use tab and shift-tab targeting to more easily find the item. They can be tracked with [[Track Harvestables]].*
   - *Note: The skeletons in the area are hostile, but they have very short aggro ranges, so with patience and care, even lower level characters should be able to gather the items. (many of them don't see invis)*
1. Craft a [[Advanced Lightning Tracker Mach I (Gifts from the Great Beyond Part I)|Advanced Lightning Tracker Mach I]] on a [[Work Bench]] and use it in [[Aurelian Coast]].
   - *Note: You'll have to craft another one for the first questupdate for the next quest. Don't cancel the workbench when you just summoned one.*
1. Examine [[Ironsprocket's M.A.G.I.C. Device]] and 'Look more closely at the contraption.'
   - *Note: If you have no [[Ironsprocket's M.A.G.I.C. Device]] but the quest gives you a hint of using it, travel to [[Gabin Baerea]] {{waypoint 2195, 483, -730}} in [[Teren's Grasp]]. You can buy there the [[Components for M.A.G.I.C.]] (It is really a component package named this way) and then craft another one.
