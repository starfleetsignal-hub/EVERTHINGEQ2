## Steps

1. Kill 7 [[Myst river eel]] by the [[Myhst River]] {{waypoint -180, -8, 215}}.
1. Return to [[Izle Tot]].
1. Feed the piranha in the [[Drukyna Marsh]] near {{waypoint -834, -56, 121}}.
   - Can be done while flying.
   - If one clicks fast enough, can obtain three updates on one disturbance.
1. Return to [[Izle Tot]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Scattered Sphinx Feathers]] × 3
- -5000 faction with **House Vahla**
- -5000 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +5000 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
