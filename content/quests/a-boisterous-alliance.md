## Notes

- If you're working on [[The Keys to Trust]] in the [[Claymore Timeline]], Gribbly might not respond.
- If you can't open the cell just do a /tar grib and it should target him to hail him.

## Steps

1. Kill a prestigios Orate, a spiritual leader, and a prestigious member of the Oratorium:
   - [[Orate Sivark]] or his [[A herald of Dar|placeholder]] in [[The Oratorium]] at {{waypoint 11, 142, 33}}.
   - The flying [[Herald of Dar]] in the Oratorium at {{waypoint 11, 142, 33}}.
   - [[An Awakened spiritualist]] in the [[Ready Room]] at {{waypoint 165, 155, -69}}.
1. Return to Gribbly the Gallant to complete the quest.
