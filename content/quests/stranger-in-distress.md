I arrived in what appears to be an old mine tunnel, and discovered a very unusual looking stranger who appears to be very badly injured.

## Steps

![fibrous roots](images/Fibrous_roots_Harvest.png)
![cave fungus](images/Cave_fungus.jpg)

1. Obtain [[Bandage Recipe]] and a [[Tinkered Portable Loom]] from a nearby bag {{waypoint 546.00, 126.15, 456.93}}
   - *Note: The [[Bandage Recipe]] is automatically scribed to your Recipe Book. It is not in your inventory.*
1. Craft bandages to help Brytthel.
   1. Harvest 2 **fibrous roots** near the great drill, near {{waypoint 429.02, 85.66, 400.88}}.
      - *Track Harvests works for these items.*
      - *Beware! The Maldura Guards are hostile until you earn faction!*
   1. Harvest **healing fungi** from **cave fungus** found in the [[Splitiron Mine]] {{waypoint 307.85, 127.87, 238.51}}.
1. use the [[Tinkered Portable Loom]] and craft the "Healing salve infused bandages"
1. Speak to Brytthel to use the bandages, then continue the conversation to end the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- [[Brytthel's Signet Ring]]
- +5,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
