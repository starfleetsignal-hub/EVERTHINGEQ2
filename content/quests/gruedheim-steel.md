## Steps

1. "A True Gruedheim weapon must be bathed in the blood of thy enemies."  Kill the following:
   - "An enemy that has just begun it's campaign of evil"
     - [[A Crushbone conscript (Crushbone Keep)|A Crushbone conscript]]. Can be found all over the zone, including by the entrance.
   - "An enemy that patrols these halls"
     - [[A Crushbone centurion (Crushbone Keep)|A Crushbone centurion]]. They're the most common monster in the zone and can be found in all the hallways.
   - "The blood of one who lurks in the shadows."
     - [[A Crushbone emissary (Crushbone Keep)|A Crushbone emissary]]. Can be found all over the zone, including by the entrance.
   - "The blood of one who inspires the others."
     - [[A Crushbone oracle (Crushbone Keep)|A Crushbone oracle]]. Two specific locations. 1) southwest corner of the top floor {{waypoint 49, 25, 73}}, shared spawn with a Crushbone thaumaturgist. 2) The room near the center of the basement {{waypoint 8, -40, -34}} (higher level and harder to reach, but a guaranteed spawn).
   - "The blood of one who delves into dark magic."
     - [[A Crushbone thaumaturgist]]. Found throughout the basement.
   - "The blood of one who instructs the enemy."
     - [[A Crushbone trainer (Crushbone Keep)|A Crushbone trainer]]. Found in the [[Upper Guardpost]], the room at the top of the stairs to the second level {{waypoint 46, 25, -19}}.
   - "The blood of a high ranking officer."
     - [[A Crushbone legionnaire (Crushbone Keep)|A Crushbone legionnaire]]. Found on the second floor in and near [[Crush's Chambers]] {{waypoint 17, 25, 44}}
   - "An enemy that delights in pain."
     - [[A Crushbone torturer]]. Found in the basement in the [[Hall of Truths]], {{waypoint 55, -40, 40}}
1. Gather some "ancient earth from the depths of Crushbone Keep".  It's in [[The Hold]] at the far south of the basement, a clickable pile of refuse to the right of the entrance {{waypoint 37, -40, 58}}
1. Head back to [[Greater Faydark]] and talk to [[Turgo Bargedown]] in the caves connecting Crushbone with the rest of Gfay {{waypoint -912, 118, 219}}.
1. Head to the circular building in [[Clan Crushbone]] and speak to [[Burgef Steelwright]] {{waypoint -1164.35, 124.63, 58.12}}.
1. Kill [[Guardmaster Grush'ul]] in one of the tents nearby. {{waypoint -1065, 123, 138}}
1. Return to Burgef Steelwright.
