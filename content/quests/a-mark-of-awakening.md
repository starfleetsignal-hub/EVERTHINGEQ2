## Notes

- You must be at least adventure level 62 to begin this quest.
- Consider doing the other Kingdom of Sky Heritage Quests and Signature Quests ([[A Thorn of Old]], [[Blood of the Brood]] and [[Draco Mortuus vos Liberatio]]) concurrently with this quest since many of the steps take you to the same dungeons.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]] and read the 'Book of Sacrifice'. {{waypoint -102, 67, -34}}
1. Complete the sub-quest [[The Book of Sacrifice]].
1. Travel to [[Palace of the Awakened]] and read the 'Book of Power'. {{waypoint -43, 57, -7}}
1. Complete the sub-quest [[The Book of Power]].
1. Travel to [[The Halls of Fate]] and read the 'Book of Trials'. {{waypoint -20, 2, -97}}
1. Complete the sub-quest [[The Book of Trials]].
   - NOTE: If you have completed any of the Book quests above, but have restarted this quest, simply return to the book and read to the end again to receive your update.
1. Speak to the [[The Keeper]] in [[The Bonemire]] at the entrance to [[The Halls of Fate]], at {{waypoint -3, -383, 25}}. Click on the orb that spawns where the Keeper was to be teleported to the [[Trials of the Awakened]]. Whenever you're ready to enter one the trials, you will have to return to the Keeper and click on the orb.
   1. **[[Trials of the Awakened|Trial of Alacrity]]** (timed 30 minutes)
      1. Make your way to the last room either by fighting to it or traveling invisibly.
      1. Clear the room. This initiates a ring event.
      1. At the end of the ring event, [[The Construct of Alacrity]] will spawn.
      1. Kill it to complete the trial.
      1. Return to the Keeper outside the Halls of Fate. {{waypoint -3, -383, 25}}
   1. **[[Trials of the Awakened|Trial of Endurance]]**
      1. Make your way down until you see a blue column.
      1. Repeatedly clear spawns of level 4x 68 ^^ mobs and eventually three named mobs will then spawn up above. Their names are [[Ratio]], [[Regum]] and [[Ultima]]
      1. The first three are 68^^ mobs. Kill these and [[The Timeless Guardian]] (70^^^) will spawn on the top level at the back of the room. The portal to leave the trial will appear upon his death.
      1. Return to the Keeper outside the Halls of Fate.
   1. **[[Trials of the Awakened|Trial of Spirit]]**
      1. Click the statue to be turned into a dragon. Only one person can be turned into a dragon.
      1. Clear the mobs around the room to initiate a ring event. After you kill multiple groups of 70v Spiritkeepers, 2 spirit guardians and a spirit-bound observer will appear, and then [[The Guardian of Spirit]] (72^^) will spawn up on the ledge above.
      1. Kill him to complete the trial. It will drop [[A guardian spirit]] house item.
      1. Return to the Keeper outside the Halls of Fate.
   1. **[[Trials of the Awakened: Trial of Leadership|Trial of Leadership]]** (Raid x4)
      1. This is an epic x4 raid instance, however the level of difficulty does not require a full raid.
      1. The layout of the instance is similar to the lower part of the [[The Halls of Fate|Halls of Fate]] where [[Sothis]] is found.
      1. You will need to kill three bosses in the following order:
         1. [[Gur'gul the Warden]]
         1. [[The Final Warden]]
         1. [[The Guardian of Leadership]]
      1. Return to the Keeper outside the Halls of Fate.
