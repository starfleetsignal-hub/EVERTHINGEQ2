## Steps

1. Take [[A tamed Shik'nar drone]] at {{waypoint -584.00, 33.52, 358.36}} towards [[Grieg's Spire]] and jump off part way, around {{waypoint 114, 293, -184}}. Proceed towards {{waypoint -283.24, 220.65, -834.07}} to enter [[Wracklands]].
1. Perform the following:
   - Gather 12 **Yttrium Ore**  ![Yttrium Ore](images/Yttrium_Ore.jpg) (Note: cannot be tracked)
     - Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 593.08, 75.51, 774.57}}
     - {{waypoint 548.83, 77.12, 765.71}}
     - {{waypoint 544.86, 75.18, 703.11}}
     - {{waypoint 504.52, 73.95, 709.88}}
     - {{waypoint 530.63, 78.90, 585.37}}
     - {{waypoint 461.65, 76.35, 618.90}}
     - {{waypoint 466.50, 79.50, 663.14}}
     - {{waypoint 623.19, 76.22, 615.11}}
   - Kill golems for 8 golem arcanist gems.  *Note: Not a guaranteed update.*
1. Speak to [[Szeth Hibis'kis]] at {{waypoint 541.13, 34.55, 150.72}}.
1. Perform the following:
   - Defeat 8 cheetahs for their blood.
   - Gather 10 **Ripflow bur blooms**. ![Ripflow bur bloom](images/Ripflow_Bur.jpg)
     - Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 416.67, -10.49, 59.87}}
     - {{waypoint 417.72, 6.65, 101.72}}
     - {{waypoint 438.44, -10.05, 44.63}}
     - {{waypoint 439.32, 7.30, 122.02}}
     - {{waypoint 468.21, -2.51, 140.73}}
     - {{waypoint 468.89, -7.74, 24.70}}
     - {{waypoint 473.17, -5.96, -0.96}}
     - {{waypoint 503.78, -7.13, 50.15}}
1. Return to [[Szeth Hibis'kis]] at {{waypoint 541.13, 34.55, 150.72}}.
