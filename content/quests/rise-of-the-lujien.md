## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[An obol soulwing|obol soulwings]] for meat.
1. Speak to Blackhowl. Click the flames next to him.  *(You'll receive a 15 minute buff, Drinal's Blessing. You can receive it again by returning to the flames in the future.)*
1. Speak again to [[Blackhowl]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
