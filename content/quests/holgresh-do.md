## Steps

1. Locate the caravan: {{waypoint -852, -667, -558}}
1. Kill the following to make totems to intimidate the holgresh
   - 4 [[A holgresh warpriest|holgresh warpriests]]
   - 4 Treants for wood for pikes ([[A withering treant]], or [[A withering sapling]] around {{waypoint -2085, -630, -503}} area)
1. Place pikes around caravan (click the spots with green sparkles)
1. speak to Scout Master Kilkarn
