## Notes

- You will receive -100 Sathirian faction with every one of the Gra'al Shul that you kill. [[Sathirian|Sathirians]] occupy the the newly rebuilt city of [[Sebilis]]. Faction associated with the Sathirians includes:
  - The Rilissians, who occupy the [[Fens of Nathsar]].

## Steps

1. Kill 12 Gra'al Shul Iksar north of [[Kunzar's Edge (POI)|Kunzar's Edge]]. {{waypoint -1494, 53, 444}}
1. Return to [[Hroar Kalacious]]. {{waypoint -1374, 36, 515}}

## Rewards

- At least 6g 88s 19c
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- [[Buckler of the Frontier]]
