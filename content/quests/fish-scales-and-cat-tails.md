1. Complete the following tasks:
   - Obtain 6 scales from [[A Kunzar muckshredder|Kunzar muckshredders]] around [[The City of Mist]]. {{waypoint -103, 126, -849}}
   - Harvest 6 *kerra tail plants* that grow along the banks of the [[The Venom Flow]]:
     - {{waypoint 193.20, 60, -571.75}}
     - {{waypoint 148.57, 85.69, -552.97}}
     - {{waypoint 59.33, 87.90, -548.81}}
     - {{waypoint 23.70, 87.18, -508.56}}
     - {{waypoint -30.35, 93.03, -504.97}}
     - {{waypoint -55.13, 101.59, -536.44}}
1. Return to Slixin Drusar.
