## Description

The Outer Stronghold of Ykesha is well guarded by hostile cursed soldiers loyal to the Warlord. Should Warlord Ykesha choose to rally his troops for any kind of attack, those soldiers certainly would prove to be quite deadly in combat. The Survival Accord would like me to venture into the Outer Stronghold, and thin out the number of guards found within its halls. I should return once I have completed my task.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Ruins of Guk: Ykesha's Outer Stronghold]] and seek out 12 cursed soldiers loyal to Warlord Ykesha and destroy them
1. Return to [[Vegupa Marrsheart]] at Firmroot Moot {{waypoint 87, -87, -434}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
