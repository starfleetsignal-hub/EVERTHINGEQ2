## Steps

1. Go to {{waypoint 1369, -104, 2947}} in [[The Lowland Basin|the Lowland Basin]]. ![small sparkling area where the Kejekan spy can be enticed with fish](images/Catching_the_Spy_quest_-_small_sparkling_spot_on_ground.jpg)
   - You will see a small sparkling spot on the ground.  Click it to summon [[A kejekan spy]], who will vanish shortly after, leaving his notes behind.
   - Click on **a dropped piece of parchment** to receive the [[Dropped message]]. ![Notes dropped by the spy when getting away](images/Catching_the_Spy_quest_-_dropped_piece_of_parchment.jpg)
1. Return to Fazia.

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**
