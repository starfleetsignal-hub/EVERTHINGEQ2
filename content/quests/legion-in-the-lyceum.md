## Steps

The target mobs are found in the open, contested Lyceum area of Skyshrine, The City of Dracur.

1. Kill 8 groups of Doomscale Maligonian droags.
1. Kill 8 Direclaw wurms
1. Return to [[Jorlak]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

+3,000 faction with **Claws of Veeshan**
