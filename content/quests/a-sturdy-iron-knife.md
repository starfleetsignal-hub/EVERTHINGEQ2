1. I need to craft a Sturdy Iron Knife for my apprentice.
   - Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Iron Knife]]

     - 2 [[Iron cluster|iron clusters]]
     - 4 [[Glowing Coal]]
1. I need to take this knife back to my apprentice.
