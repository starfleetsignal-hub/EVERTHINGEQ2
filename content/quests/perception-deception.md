## Prereq

You need +30k with the Guardians of the Underfoot

## Steps

1. Capture a corrupted elemental or a corrupted conjuror in Underfoot area: Use the provided censer on any of these mobs and
1. return to Kirill within 10 minutes

## Reward

1. +2500 faction with **Guardians of the Underfoot**
1. At least 55g 70s 61c
1. [[Mark of Manaar]]
