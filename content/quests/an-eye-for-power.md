## Steps

*The following locations are not absolute, they just give an idea of where they may be located. The spawns seem to be at random locations around and about the points below. You can use the [[Track Harvestables]] ability or a [[Gnomish Divining Rod]] to track the shards.*

|  |  |
|---|---|
| ![Red Shard](images/Red_Shard.jpg) | ![Blue Shard](images/Blue_Shard.jpg) |
| ![Green Shard](images/Green_Shard.jpg) | ![White Shard](images/White_Shard.jpg) |

1. Harvest four shards, which spawn at semi-random locations:
   - ***Note:** The shards can be tracked using the [[Track Harvestables|Track Harvestables Ability]] or using a [[Gnomish Divining Rod]].*
   - **Red Shard:** [[Jagged Plains]]
     - {{waypoint 66, 27, -615}}, {{waypoint 70, 25, -657}}, {{waypoint 84, 28, -562}}, {{waypoint 104, 23, -588}}, {{waypoint 108, 25, -526}}, {{waypoint 110, 25, -534}}, {{waypoint 121, 21, -664}}, {{waypoint 131, 23, -569}}, {{waypoint 133, 20, -642}}, {{waypoint 138, 28, -535}}, {{waypoint 141, 20, -636}}, {{waypoint 142, 21, -602}}, {{waypoint 146, 22, -521}}, {{waypoint 154, 22, -547}}, {{waypoint 163, 26, -466}}, {{waypoint 205, 23, -507}}
   - **Blue Shard:** eastern [[Hollowfrost Grove]] near the [[World Tree Root]]
     - {{waypoint -597, -21, -314}},  {{waypoint -604, -10, -278}},  {{waypoint -617, -16, -360}},  {{waypoint -617, -18, -299}}, {{waypoint -629, -19, -338}}, {{waypoint -639, -12, -248}},  {{waypoint -654, -10, -364}}, {{waypoint -666, -18, -330}}, {{waypoint -689, -15, -357}},  {{waypoint -703, -13, -272}}
   - **White Shard:** western [[Hollowfrost Grove]] near the [[Snowsworn's Hunter's Outpost]]
     - {{waypoint -236, 7, -261}}, {{waypoint -241, 2, -278}}, {{waypoint -269, -4, -352}}, {{waypoint -274, -5, -306}}, {{waypoint -286, -5, -299}} {{waypoint -299, 0, -270}}, {{waypoint -302, -8, -317}}, {{waypoint -337, -5, -414}}, {{waypoint -342, -5, -369}}, {{waypoint -358, 1, -426}}, {{waypoint -359, -9, -293}}, {{waypoint -370, -3, -305}}, {{waypoint -258, 5, -260}}
   - **Green Shard:** [[Guardian's Gulch]]
     - {{waypoint -584, -38, -729}}, {{waypoint -590, -38, -682}}, {{waypoint -599, -38, -772}}, {{waypoint -620, -38, -738}}, {{waypoint -626, -38, -711}}, {{waypoint -636, -38, -753}}, {{waypoint -638, -38, -804}}, (-641, -38, -747), {{waypoint -654, -38, -687}}, {{waypoint -683, -38, -678}}, {{waypoint -688, -38, -753}}, {{waypoint -721, -38, -683}}, {{waypoint -735, -38, -785}}, {{waypoint -741, -38, -689}}
1. Return to [[Master T'Lys]].
1. Find the Talisman pieces. Each of the targets is a small ring event, with a re-spawn of the event in about 15 minutes. If the named mob doesn't spawn during a particular ring event, wait 15 minutes and try again.
   - **Frosthorn:** Kill [[Bloodrage]] at the westernmost fire pit in the [[Jagged Plains]] at {{waypoint 111, 25, -541}}. Kill the minotaur groups surrounding the fire pit to start the ring event.
     - [[Bron Frosthorn]] will not update the quest, and if you get him you are at the wrong location.
   - **Numbfoot:** Kill [[Chieftain Iceburn]] at the [[World Tree Root]] in [[Hollowfrost Grove]] at {{waypoint -639, -18, -300}}. Kill the Numbfoot groups surrounding the Tree to start the ring event.
   - **Icegill:** Kill [[Master Webclaw]] in [[Icegill Lagoon]] at {{waypoint 487, -35, -1622}}. Kill the four goblin groups to start the ring event.
     - You may wish to do [[Rainscour]]'s {{waypoint 157, -31, -1210}} questline at the same time, as his final quest also requires killing Webclaw.
     - **ORIGINS SERVER**: The groups you need to kill are the four standing in front of the dock/stage looking thing. Clear these and eventually a 5th group appears on the dock and may or may not contain Web-claw.  - *<3 Toofs*
1. Enter the instance [[Tobrin's Former Domain]] at the same door you use to access [[Miragul's Menagerie]], {{waypoint -105, -23, -283}}
   - Don't exit the instance or you can't get back in for 1.5 hours to do it all over again. Note, if you die, you will return in Everfrost, which means this will apply, unless you can revive instantly or have someone else bring you back.
     - Lockout is 18 hours on Origins.
   - Kill the linked mobs in each room to unlock the doors.
   - Once you reach the fourth room, get close to the [[Shade of Tobrin]]. Once he is attackable, kill him and his four linked adds.
   - Examine the "eyepatch chest" that appears
     - Each player must examine the chest to progress. ("Tested" on Origins...)
1. When you try to leave the room, you will be attacked by Master T'Lys and his 6 linked level 45 ^ Minions.
   - As soon as the minions are dead, T'Lys says "Curse you! Our paths will cross again!" and you are awarded [[Tobrin's Mystical Eyepatch (HQ Reward)|Tobrin's Mystical Eyepatch]].
