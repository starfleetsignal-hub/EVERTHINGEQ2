## Notes

- Scribe [[Frostfell Lair Decoration]], received when the quest is accepted, if you haven't already learned the recipes.

## Steps

1. Craft 6 [[Frostfell Stocking|Frostfell Stockings]]:
   - 12 [[Frostfell Fabric]], from one of the boxes near the entrance {{waypoint 1, 0, -569}}
   - 12 [[Elven Magic]], from [[Ginneldarf Gigglegibber]] {{waypoint 36, 0, -573}}
   - [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]] {{waypoint 22, 0, -577}}
1. Return to Nute Frostfoot.
