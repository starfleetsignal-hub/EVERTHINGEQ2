## Prerequisites

- You must be at least a level 70 Artisan to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Collect the supplies on [[Sathir's Span]]. {{waypoint -209.36, -7.76, 944.77}}
1. Leave the supplies in a depot (small wooden crate) at the [[Western Pens of Riliss]]. {{waypoint -676, -137, 696}}
1. Return to [[Assistant Zhary]].

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- + 2000 faction with **Riliss**
