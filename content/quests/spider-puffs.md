## Steps

1. Collect 2 **a dame's rocket** at {{waypoint -154, 148, 14}} (Just outside the questgiver's house)
1. Kill 4 [[A woolly silkspinner]] at {{waypoint -234, 135, 15}}
1. Return to [[Ribbit Hopson]]
1. Try some of Ribbit's Spider Puffs (on the table beside him)

## Rewards

- 4 Spider Puffs
- At least 12s 2c
