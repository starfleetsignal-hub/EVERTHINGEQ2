## Notes

Completing this last quest in signature line unlocks flight in [[Sodden Archipelago]] and [[Western Wastes]], it also opens up a Daily Crafting Mission NPC [[Ledat]], ability to use [[Researcher Aushta]] <Tradeskill Researcher> and makes visible red shinies for tradeskill collections in Sodden Archipelago and Western Wastes.

## Steps

1. Search for Kromzek signs
   - travel up and east to click on "Dead Kromzek" at {{waypoint -535, 66, 592}}
   - *The W.H.A.T. V2 can be used to get there and to collect the Dead Kromzek.* ![Dead Kromzek](images/Dead_Kromzek.png)
1. Find the following *(**NOTE:** the Rune Focusing Powder can be collected **AFTER** the Elemental Draconic Runes.  This will save you travel time, especially if you are using the  W.H.A.T. V2)*
      - Find [[Rune Focusing Powder]]
        - *Small sparkly bottle closer to water in area around {{waypoint 546, 8, 149}} or {{waypoint 545, 8, 123}}, Harvest Tracking works for these.* If you don't see it right off, try harvesting some of the [[Elemental Draconic Rune]] in this immediate area. It will pop.
        - **Note:** *this is the area on the map that shows up as blue and is below where the "Dead Kromzek" was located. The waypoint will lead you astray until you drop down to the water and ice area underneath.*
        - *Use W.H.A.T V2 if needed, lots of aggro mobs around there.*
      - Find 17 x [[Elemental Draconic Rune]]
        - *they can be anywhere on the island, glowing orange shinies visible from afar, Harvest Tracking works for these*
        - *Use your W.H.A.T V2 if needed to avoid aggro*
        - *Possible locations include:*
          - * {{waypoint -433.64, 64.01, 585.55}}, {{waypoint -267.52, 51.52, 569.95}}, {{waypoint -213.05, 51.60, 569.85}}*
          - * {{waypoint -34.32, 67.86, 763.93}}, {{waypoint -212.99, 51.60, 570.32}}, {{waypoint -63.38, 73.05, 514.20}}*
          - * {{waypoint 52.61, 58.94, 560.14}}, {{waypoint 132.92, 58.56, 454.42}}, {{waypoint 116.52, 64.25, 442.37}}*
          - * {{waypoint -123.61, 72.91, 521.42}}, {{waypoint 41.83, 66.73, 409.39}}, {{waypoint 206.50, 66.20, 393.48}}*
          - * {{waypoint 240.98, 51.46, 356.22}}, {{waypoint 268.16, 35.22, 458.79}}*
            - *Walking from the location of the Kromzek overland via the ramp down to charnel scar gave me all 17 shinies needed. Note that they will only be visible if you're close enough to see the particle effect, so flying isn't that useful. I'd recommend either using the W.H.A.T V2 or -if you're high enough level and able to kill the mobs, the track harvesting ability found in your Tradeskill Prestige.*

1. Speak with [[Raffik]] at {{waypoint 76, 7, 660}} in [[Western Wastes]]
