## Starting the Quest

Talk to [[Serith Ludd]] at [[Camp Nybright]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]] {{waypoint 129, -22, 309}} and examine the envelope he gives you. You must be level 57 or higher to receive this quest. He will not have a feather over his head.

## Steps

1. You must kill [[Paariio Huttoo]] (level 60 ^). He is one of the fairies roaming [[The Aphotic Intersection]], near the sign post; you can take the horse to Butcherblock station and jump off at the Intersection.
1. Return to Serith Ludd for your reward and the next quest.

## Rewards

- At Least 10g 83s 90c
- 250 faction with **Nybright Family**
