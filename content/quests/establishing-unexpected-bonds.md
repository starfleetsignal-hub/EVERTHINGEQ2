## Steps

1. Talk to [[Skulker Slythe]] at the [[Hidden Plunderer's Camp]]. {{waypoint -451, 148, -877}}
1. Return to Field Chief Ry'zilk, who gives you [[Unsigned renegade treaty]].
1. Speak to Skulker Slythe again.

## Rewards

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- +6000 faction with **Ry'zilk's Renegades**
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Earstud of Midnight]] (All Priests)
  - [[Spike of Vigor]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Stud of Dark Dealings]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Vigorous Stud of Swift Casting]] (All Mages)
