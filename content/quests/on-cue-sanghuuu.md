## Steps

Their enemy far outnumber the Sang'Huuu, and so they've hired me to even the odds. If all goes well, the odds won't be even, but in favor of the hooluk of Splendid Sky Aerie.

- Slay the Terror of the Four Winds within Strungstone Ascent. Within Zimara Breadth
- Defeat the Sovereign within Golden Rule, within Vaashkaani: Golden Rule, Zamara Breadth
- Down the Hawk within Tarnished Oasis , within Aether Wroughtlands

## Rewards

- At least 10p 77g 75s 36c 138,599 status
- 30 [[Zimaran Ballad]]

- At least 4p 31g 10s 14c 67,200 status
