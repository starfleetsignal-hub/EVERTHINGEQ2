## Prerequisites

Required to obtain the quest:

- You must have started the quest [[Improving Relations with the Locals]].
- Lvl 50+ Carpenter.

## Steps

1. Talk to [[Zhen-Hao Nurwin]] inside the Nurwin House.
1. Talk to [[Choo Nurwin]] He runs around outside the Nurwin house.
1. Talk to [[Mei-mei Ventur]] {{waypoint 4, 14, -75}}
1. Gather 5 oysters near {{waypoint -14, -4, 23}} (You can use the same node five times)
1. Return to [[Zhen-Hao Nurwin]] and speak to him again. He then sends you out foraging again.
1. Gather 5 willow branches. Click at the base of the tree where it meets the ground. {{waypoint -156, 0, -103}}
1. Gather 5 rosewoods, which are found in a little bush beside the mayor's house at {{waypoint 30, 14, -120}}
1. Return to [[Zhen-Hao Nurwin]] for your reward

## Rewards

- [[Wantia Artisan's Satchel]]
- [[Nurwin's Sculpting Stylus]]
