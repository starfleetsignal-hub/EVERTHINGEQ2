## Steps

1. Defeat [[Boss Gobblerock]] in the [[Gobblerock's Hideout]]
   - *Purchase a key from Karmen the Swindler in the Commonlands at the The Lonely Tower Camp {{waypoint -1320, -50, 580}} and examine it then you will be able to join this zone.*
   - *Zone in using the drain cover in The Commonlands, located in a tower northwest of the Sprawl zone line at {{waypoint -1415, -70, 234}}*
1. Return to the Chronomagi Tasker

## Notes

- The Recommended level to mentor to is level 35 if you want maximum AA or Loot.
- The maximum multiple of 5 you can mentor to and still complete this quest is level 40.

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Chronomancers**
- The option to purchase [[Replica: Boss Gobblerock]] for 1p 8g from the [[Timeless Trader]]
