## Notes

- Do this quest alongside [[Audience with the Guardian]], since they require similar quest targets and you formerly needed to complete it to access [[Deathtoll]].

## Steps

1. Kill [[Prophet of The Slashing Talon]]:
   1. Kill aviaks on the first floor until an [[Ornate Slashed Eye]] drops (uncommon chest drop).
   1. Use the Ornate Slashed Eye on the [[Effigy of the Slashing Talon]] to spawn Prophet of The Slashing Talon.
      - Globules of 'accumulating energy' will spawn and begin to revolve around the statue at the center of the room. After a couple minutes the big globule will die and the prophet swill spawn where the NPC was.
1. Kill [[Ancient of the Flapping Wing]]:
   1. Kill aviaks on the second floor until an [[Ornate Winged Eye]] drops (uncommon body drop).
   1. Use the Ornate Winged Eye on the [[Effigy of The Flapping Wing]] to spawn Ancient of the Flapping Wing.
1. Harvest the following items:
   - First Floor: 8 'gem of the flapping wing'
   - Second Floor: 8 'claw of the slashing talon'
1. return to the statue on the first floor and inspect the base. {{waypoint -3, -1, 4}}

## Reward

- [[The Totem of Aaryonar]] (Required by the quest [[Feeding the Flames]])
- One of the following:
  - [[Steeldown's Alloy Chestguard]]
  - [[Ancient Plate of Karana]]
  - [[Small Carving of Aaryonar]]
  - [[Razorwing's Bandolier]]
