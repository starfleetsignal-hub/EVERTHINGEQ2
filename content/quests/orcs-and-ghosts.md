## Steps

1. Slay 10 [[A ghostly orc|ghostly orcs]] in [[The Siege Lands]], north of the West Freeport gate near the entrance to [[The Graveyard]] {{waypoint -1380.42, -85.00, -333.97}}.
1. Return to [[Gerun Pontian]].

- At least 6s 31c
- +55 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
