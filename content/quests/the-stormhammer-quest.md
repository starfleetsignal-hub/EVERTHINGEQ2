## Notes

- You must be level 35 to complete this quest.
- All members in your group must have this quest in order to access [[The Haunted Vaults]]. If they don't have the quest, they won't receive an update for acquiring the access key.
- You must be able to understand [[Serilian]] to read the note on the door leading to the Haunted Vaults.
- For an alternate way of entering the vault see the talk page for more information.

## Steps

1. Enter the [[Stormguard Hall]] in [[Kaladim]]. {{waypoint 183, 17, -97}}
1. Find the entrance to [[The Haunted Vaults]]. {{waypoint 196, 17, -94}}
1. Complete the access quest, [[Burning the Black Sun Grizzly Rug]]|{{waypoint 197.08, 17.75, -93.54}}, which is started by clicking on the bear rug.
   - If you have done this quest before, you will have to do it again or you can pan your camera down and right-click the hatch underneath the rug.
1. Kill any [[A Terrok warlord|Terrok warlord]] to acquire the key to the vaults. {{waypoint 160.5, 17, -64.5}}
1. Enter [[The Haunted Vaults]].
1. Click the 'Adamant Chest of Thex' (red chest). {{waypoint 179, 18, -60}}
1. Kill the three skeletons in the throne room {{waypoint 197, 17, -95}}, in order to spawn three NPC dwarf ghosts. Complete the subquests that they offer:
   - [[Remnants of a Traitor]]
   - [[Shards of the Eye of Stormhammer]]
   - [[Refurbished Goods]]
1. Open the Adamant Chest of Thex. {{waypoint 179, 18, -60}}
1. Return to [[Commander O.C. Irontoe]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]. {{waypoint -200, 221, -117}}
1. Enter [[Kaladim]] and slay the undead troll [[Xicotl (Kaladim)|Xicotl]]! {{waypoint -36.5, 24.5, -98.5}}
1. Return to Commander O.C. Irontoe.

- 35200 status at Level 35
- 29333 status at Level 40
- 9747 status at Level 91
- +10,000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
