## Steps

1. Talk to [[Osh]] the Speaker of Lodizal at {{waypoint -1634.92, -535.33, -2428.07}} Take Griffin on docks to Icy Fingers at ( 1333.73, -540.25, 409 ) in [[Great Divide]]
1. Talk to [[Effigy of Prexus]] at {{waypoint -1627.18, -533.20, -2360.51}} in [[Great Divide]]
1. Talk to [[The Duality]] at {{waypoint 475, -212, 246}} in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]]
1. Talk to [[Yelinak The Ancient]] at {{waypoint 17, -242, 471}} in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]]
1. Talk to [[The Duality]] at {{waypoint 475, -212, 246}} in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]]
1. Talk to [[Erus Dal'viv]] at {{waypoint 1033, -423, 754}} in [[Great Divide]]
1. Participate in Public Quest: [[The Battle of Storm Gorge]]
1. Return to [[Erus Dal'viv]]
1. Go to [[Hold of Rime: The Fortress Spire]] to find the Coalescence of Life.  **BEFORE YOU ENTER**: you will need 3 players to progress through this zone!  *(I managed to do it alone by two-boxing two characters.)* After killing the final named, [[Grand Restorer Lingwal (NPC)|Grand Restorer Lingwal]], the Coalescence of Life will spawn in that same room.  Climb up the ladder at  {{waypoint -350, 268.32, 51.44}} .  At the top, look up and you should see a glowing, golden orb.  If more than 1 player needs it, there may be a short delay before the next player may click it.
   - The update may also be obtained by entering a cleared instance, and clicking the golden orb even if they were not part of the original killing of [[Grand Restorer Lingwal]].
1. Return to [[The Duality]] at {{waypoint 475, -212, 246}} in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]]

## Rewards

At least 1p 50g at level 95
