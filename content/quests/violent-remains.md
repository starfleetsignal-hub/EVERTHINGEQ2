## Prerequisites

- You must speak [[Uruvanian]] to progress through this quest.

## Steps

1. Read [[A Multiplicitome]].
1. In [[The Lyceum of Naar'Yora]], click on a book on the fourth shelf of the left-most bookcase. {{waypoint 4, 57, -45}}
   - If the forcefield is up, kill [[A temperamental tome]] to get it to despawn. The forcefield may not despawn immediately.
1. Within 60-minutes, kill:
   - 'a multitome lurker' {{waypoint -48, 57, 2}} 3rd floor near the 'The Book of Power'
   - 'a multitome evoker' {{waypoint 2, 57, 48}} 3rd floor
   - 'a multitome howler' {{waypoint 83, 57, 0}} 3rd floor
   - 'a multitome pummeler' {{waypoint 165, 82, -3}} 4th floor of west tower, center room
   - 'a multitome annihilator' {{waypoint 147, 82, 28}} 4th floor of west tower, southeast corner room
   - 'a multitome riser' {{waypoint 145, 82, -25}} 4th floor of west tower, northeast corner room
1. Return to the 'Astral Binder' and reinspect it. {{waypoint 4, 57, -45}}
1. Within 60-minutes, talk to 'The Multiplicitome', located in [[Erudition Aviary]], 5th floor of west tower {{waypoint 133, 103, -15}}
1. Click a pile of huge books in [[Enlightenment Aviary]], 6th floor of east tower {{waypoint -138, 131, -24}}

## Rewards

- [[Kruekil's Quill]]
