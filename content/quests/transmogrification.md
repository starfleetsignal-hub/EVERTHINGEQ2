## Notes

- This is a sub-quest of [[Premonitions of Doom]]. If you killed [[Doom Guard Vimock]], [[Jin'tiz the Cataloguer]], or [[Katalkana]] on your way down, you will not be able to complete Premonitions of Doom.
- The potion itself has a 'No-Zone' tag, as such, will be lost if the player attempts to leave the zone for any reason before it is consumed.
- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Obtain a scale from the body of [[Doom Lord Septimus]] to refine the [[Refined Mutagenic Potion]]:
   1. Kill [[Sothis]] and pick up the [[Torpor Draught]] on a table in his room. {{waypoint -9, -69, 291}}
   1. Place this potion in the 'Bubbling Cauldron' that [[Katalkana]] walks near to put him to sleep. {{waypoint -23, -68, 449}}
   1. Kill Doom Lord Septimus. {{waypoint -123, -68, 454}}
1. Drink the [[Transmogrification Elixir]] that has been placed in your inventory.
