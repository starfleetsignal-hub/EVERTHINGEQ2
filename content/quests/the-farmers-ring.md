[[Lavic N'Gam]] in [[South Freeport]] wants a ring which last known to be in the possession of a [[The Commonlands]] farmer named Gyberg Thizweald.

## Steps

1. Five areas need to be searched to see if any trace of Gyberg can be found (they may be visited in any order):
   - the [[Southern Crossroads Savanna]] {{waypoint -1398, -78, 417}}
   - [[Horse Fly Trail]] {{waypoint -544, -46, -387}}
   - [[Dead Man's Valley]] {{waypoint 156, -48, -688}}
   - [[Shiv Lane]] {{waypoint -279, -46, -404}}
   - The [[Crossroads]] {{waypoint -528, -43, -276}}
1. Finding nothing with this search, the next logical step is to kill [[Farmer]]s in [[The Commonlands]] until some clue to Gyberg's whereabouts can be found.  This is an uncommon auto-update.
   - The [[Farmer]]s can be found in the fields around {{waypoint -208, -46, -160}}
1. The clue found points to the mausoleum near the nomad camps in [[Dead Man's Valley]].
   - This is northwest of the Crossroads, west of Dog Trapper Lake at {{waypoint 161, -48, -740}}.  Examine the skeleton there on the ground before the mausoleum to find that it is the remains of poor Gyberg.
1. Return to Lavic N'Gam {{waypoint -79, -25, 171}} to report these findings.  This ends the quest, and opens the next in the series, [[Like a Ring in a Haystack]].
