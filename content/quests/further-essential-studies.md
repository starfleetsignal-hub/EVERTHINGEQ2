## Description

The Craftkeepers Task Coordinator has requested assistance on behalf of the Dark Seekers to capture some more essences of the undead in Paineel Ruins.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Oolioomon]] in the Paineel Commons {{waypoint 1788, -299, 3461.88}}.
1. Use the [[Essence entanglement device]] to capture the essences of three ghosts in Old Paineel Ruins.
1. Return to [[Oolioomon]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +2500 faction with **Craftkeepers**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Craftkeepers Daily Supply Tasks
