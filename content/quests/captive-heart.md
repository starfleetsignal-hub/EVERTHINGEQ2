This quest is part of the World Event: [[A Goddess Risen]].

## Steps

1. Go to [[Everfrost]]. After zoning in, go straight through the crack in the rock to the opposite end of the dock. There you will find a portal {{waypoint -57, -9, 59}} that will take you to the zone "[[Shard of Love: A Healing Touch]]".
1. Obtain the items needed to craft the Empyrean Hammer
   1. ![Crumbled Stone of Adoration](images/Crumbled_Stone_of_Adoration.png) Gather "physical bits of Love's planal power".  Each of these appear as a black boulder called *Crumbled Stone of Adoration*. Known locations include:
      1. {{waypoint 89, 14, -213}}
      1. {{waypoint 122, 14, -207}}
      1. {{waypoint 168, 7, -69}}
      1. {{waypoint 111, 0, -46}}
      1. {{waypoint 23, 1, -37}}
      1. {{waypoint -58, 1, -56}}
      1. {{waypoint -84, -1, -61}}
      1. {{waypoint -153, 2, -113}}
      1. {{waypoint -145, 4, -137}}
      1. {{waypoint -65, 12, -197}}
   1. Obtain "a hammer of opposing power"
      1. Return through the [[The Gates of Bliss]] and defeat the 4 attacking [[A spite golem|spite golems]].  One of them will drop the [[Nightgrowth Hammer]]
1. Return to Annalisa Swornlove
1. Craft the Empyrean Hammer
   1. Scribe the [[Empyrean Hammer Crafting Instructions]]
   1. Make sure you have 1 [[Rough malachite]], 1 [[Lead cluster]], and 2 [[Basic Coal|basic coal]].  The first two can be harvested from T1 zones, or bought on the broker, and the coal can be bought from a wholesaler.
   1. Craft the [[Empyrean Hammer]] at a [[Work bench]].
1. Return to Annalisa Swornlove
1. Crack the [[Cairn of the Huntress|Cairn of the Huntress's]] magical encasement ( 28, 148, -48 )
   1. Head into the cairn and click on the statue of [[Erollisi Marr]]
1. Speak to "[[Ullkorruuk|Annalisa Swornlove]]"
1. Speak to [[Aaronolis Swornlove]]

## Rewards

At least 12g 82s 30c
