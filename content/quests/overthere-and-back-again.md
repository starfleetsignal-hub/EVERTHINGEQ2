## Prerequisites

- You must be at least adventure level 75 to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Visit the following locations in [[Jarsath Wastes]]:
   - [[Skleross Encampment]]: {{waypoint -790, -25, -1200}}
   - [[Venril's Crown]]: {{waypoint 195, -60, -484}}
   - [[Iksar Hero]]: {{waypoint 377, 3, -342}}
   - [[Skyfire Mountains]]: {{waypoint -1018, 247, 419}}
1. Return to [[Emissary Dih'ri]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- +10,200 faction with **The Sel'Nok Brigade**
