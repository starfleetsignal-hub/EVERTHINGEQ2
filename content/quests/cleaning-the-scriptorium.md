## Steps

1. Complete the following, in no particular order: (Note: I had to zoom in to see these.)
   - Move A large pile of books {{waypoint 200, 185, -68}} close to [[Scriptorium Keeper Samir]].
   - A Pile of Papers from {{waypoint 207, 185, -73}} close to [[Scriptorium Keeper Samir]].
   - Move A large pile of books {{waypoint 228, 185, -70}} close to [[Scriptorium Keeper Samir]].
1. Talk to [[Scriptorium Keeper Samir]].

## Notes

- If you move a pile of books but do not place them near the Scriptorium Keeper, they may disappear. To make the books visable again, leave the zone and return, or logout and log back in.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 48s 56c
- [[Djinn Scriptorium Papers]]
