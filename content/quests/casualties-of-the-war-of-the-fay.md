## Notes

- As you travel the zones of [[Faydwer (Continent)|Faydwer]] while on this quest you will be periodically attacked by [[A werewolf pack leader]] and three [[A vicious dire wolf|vicious dire wolves]]. They are not roped to any specific point and will chase you across the zone if you attempt to flee. Some known spawn location are:
  - [[The Lesser Faydark]]:
    - Near the [[Butcherblock Mountains]] zone in.
    - Near the Greater Faydark zone in.
    - In [[The Aphotic Intersection]], near [[The Shadowed Grove]].
  - [[Greater Faydark]]:
    - Near the [[Steamfont Mountains]] zone in.
    - Near the [[New Tunaria]] zone in.
- Although this quest is categorized as Epic, only the very last step actually involves an Epic fight (Epic x2). Steps leading to it contain Heroic content.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Ranger Oast (NPC)|Ranger Oast]] at the head of [[The Long Draw]] in [[Loping Plains]]. {{waypoint -50, 12, -435}}
   - If Ranger Oast is not up, [[A wounded werewolf]] will spawn nearby as you approach instead. Kill it to spawn Oast.
1. Speak to [[Captain Trueshot (NPC)|Captain Trueshot]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]], south of the [[Camp of the Legendary Wu]]. {{waypoint 941, 81, -180}}
1. Collect the materials to create a [[Rain Caller]] bow:
   - **Spool of Dwarven Wire**: Buy [[Old Fashioned Dwarven Wire]] from [[Horon Bronzethumb]] in [[West Fort Irontoe]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]. {{waypoint -220, 211, -137}}
     - Requires at least +10,000 faction with The Irontoe Brigade.
       - Some useful ways to build faction are completing the Heritage Quests [[In Honor and Service]] and [[A Tale of Two Towers]], or by killing various level 28-31 mobs in Butcherblock Mountains that yield +100 faction with each kill (Krulkiel bugbears, Yarpsnarl kobolds, etc.)
     - Costs 12g-24g depending on adventure level.
     - This item cannot be purchased on behalf of other players.
   - **Bough of Moonlight Wood**: Click on the [[Moonlight Tree]], located on a cliff near the [[New Tunaria]] entrance in [[Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint -1008.89, 115.24, 426.87}}, and only appears between 10pm and 4am [[Time|Norrath Time]].
   - **Gold Fairy Dust**: Speak to [[Princess Saphronia]] in [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 389, 145, 164}}. The princess will give you a [[Bag of Gold Fairy Dust]] for free if you are Fae, or if you have +50,000 faction with The City of Kelethin (*needs confirmation*). If you do not satisfy one of the above requirements then the princess will offer you the sub-quest, [[Saphronia's Request]], which will reward you the bag of gold fairy dust.
     - Evil characters: Enter the house from the edge of the platform side and stay left against the wall as you go in. There is a level 125 epicx4 guard pathing around outside and a stationary level 95 heroic inside. Both CAN be avoided with care. The epic guard has been known to get stuck right outside the door. In this case he will be impossible to get past and will need to be reset (or find a high-level friend to dispose of him).
   - **2 perfect micro servos**: Kill high-level (52+) clockworks in [[Klak'Anon]].
     - **NOTE**: Remember to pick up the sub-quest from Princess Saphronia before going to Klak'Anon, otherwise you will have to back track.
1. Return to Kelethin and speak with [[Master Bowyer Mossberge]]. {{waypoint 458, 98, 361}}
1. Speak with [[Fethinal the Enchanted]] inside his hollowed-out stump in Greater Faydark {{waypoint -838, 71, 542}}, who will enchant the bow and give you the [[Newly Created Rain Caller Bow]].
1. Return to Captain Trueshot in the Lesser Faydark. {{waypoint 940, 80, -179}}
1. Kill the werewolf dragoons:
   - [[Dragoon Lt. V'Riv]] (58^^^) in Butcherblock Mountains. {{waypoint -294, 183, 704}}
   - [[Dragoon Lt. K'Geth]] (58^^^) in the Lesser Faydark, on top of the gigantic tree root [[The Fugutr Tribe]]. {{waypoint 200, 14, -279}}
1. Kill [[Dragoon Cpt. K'Venx]] (58^^^) in [[The Shadowed Grove]] in the Lesser Faydark. {{waypoint 322, 0, 561}}
   - Once in the Shadowed Grove head to your left and climb the long root up to the ledge, K'Venx will spawn when you arrive.
   - K'Venx uses knockback, so keep yourself up against a wall. If you get knocked back and fail to get the quest update when K'Venx dies, run back out to [[The Aphotic Intersection]] to re-trigger the ambush, then go back to the ledge and kill him again.
1. Return to Captain Trueshot in the Lesser Faydark. {{waypoint 940, 80, -179}}
1. Kill [[Captain Trueshot (Named)|Captain Trueshot]], along with [[Ranger Oast (Named)|Ranger Oast]] & [[Ranger Aldarys]].
   - **To avoid any possible update issues, slay Captain Trueshot first.**
   - He has a debuff which lowers the effective level of his target by 5 levels (making it as if you were mentoring down).
1. Once you kill Trueshot, [[Huntmaster Viswin]] will spawn. He will steal the bow and summon a level 60^^^ [[A werewolf pack leader|werewolf pack leader]] and several level 60^ [[A dire wolf|dire wolves]] to attack you. Kill them to advance the quest.
   - Viswin speaks in [[Languages|Thexian]], but you do not need to understand it to continue.
   - Do not worry if you wipe during this skirmish and the Huntmaster takes the bow. Captain Trueshot will respawn once the werewolves have despawned and will reply to you as if you still had the bow.
1. Head to the Loping Plains and dispatch of Huntmaster Viswin, who will spawn the base of [[Mount Mistmoore]] {{waypoint 750, 78, -50}}.
   - He is a level 60^^^ Epic x2 and will spawn when you get close, even if he has just been killed by others. He spawns with six level 60^^^ [[A vicious dire wolf|vicious dire wolves]].
