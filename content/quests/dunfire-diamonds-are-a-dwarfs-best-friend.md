## Background

[[Captain E.L. Irontoe]] has informed me that the Grungetalon goblins have begun to decipher the worth of some hidden gems embedded into the walls of [[Brell's March]]. My mission is to recover as many of the gems as I can carry in a [[Gempack]] and return to [[West Fort Irontoe]].

## Steps

1. Inspect the chest in [[West Fort Irontoe]] to get a [[Gempack]]. {{waypoint -274, 216, -160}}
1. Enter [[Kaladim]] and go to [[Brell's March]]. {{waypoint -18, 26, -179}} to recover various gems that conceal arcane purposes and to find and destroy the goblin's source of gem analyzing.
   - Harvest 10 Dunfire diamonds from around the area.
   - Kill [[A Grungetalon gemmaster|Grungetalon gemmasters]] to collect some Brell baubles (common update).
   - Kill [[A living chest|living chests]] to collect some Underfoot stars (common update).
1. Seek and destroy the thing that is revealing the magic in the gems:
   1. Find the *Gem Analyzer* in Brell's March. There are two spawn:  {{waypoint 72.70, 23, -159.36}} or {{waypoint -96.86, 45.29, -188.40}}.![Gem Analyzer](images/Gemanalyzer.jpg)
   1. Examine it and complete the subquest, [[Gem Analyzer]].
   1. Once the subquest is complete, return and interact with the gem analyzer.
1. Return to [[Captain E.L. Irontoe]].

  - [[Jagged Blade of Vipers]]
  - [[Misty Ornate Sai]] (Mages)
  - [[Nature's Defender]]
  - [[Shaman's Orc Hammer]] (Shamans)
  - [[Shattering Stiletto]] (Predators)
