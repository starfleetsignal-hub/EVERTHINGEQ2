## Steps

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 727.46, 77.35, 696.05}} to enter [[The Vault of Ssraeshza (Solo)|The Vault of Ssraeshza [Solo]]].
1. Collect 12 full canopic jars. *Note: They are golden cylindrical jars and you have to get pretty close to see them. The jars can be collected in an already cleared zone, even if you get this quest after finishing the dungeon.*
   - They can be found the edges of most rooms excluding the first. Only the full jars update the quest.
   - For notes on how to proceed through the zone, refer to [[The Vault of Ssraeshza (Solo)|The Vault of Ssraeshza [Solo]]].
1. Return to to [[Rhoquan Zexus]] in [[Wracklands]] at {{waypoint 545.48, 34.54, 131.83}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Ensorcelled Soriz Shackle]]
