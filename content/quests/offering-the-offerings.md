This is a DAILY Repeatable quest.

## Steps

![a fallen offering](images/A_fallen_offering.jpg)

1. Collect 6 fallen offerings and replace them on the platform on Sothshae.
   - Offerings may be found along the outer edges of Sothshae.
   - They can also be found on the tops of rock formations.
   - A few possible locations:
     - [[Sothshae]]
       - {{waypoint 650, 227, 698}}
       - {{waypoint 590, 247, 551}}
       - {{waypoint 657, 311, 498}}
       - {{waypoint 697, 226, 737}}
       - {{waypoint 758, 236, 701}}
       - {{waypoint 802, 271, 685}}
       - {{waypoint 819, 263, 598}}
       - {{waypoint 822, 255, 584}}
       - {{waypoint 834, 262, 545}}
       - {{waypoint 852, 249, 588}}
       - {{waypoint 860, 256, 530}}
1. Replace them around {{waypoint 866, 301, 506}} in the shown locations.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 55g
- One of the following:
  - [[Small Russet Blueleaf Seedling]]
  - [[Young Russet Darkleaf Sapling]]
