## Notes

- Pay attention to what Nibbles has to say.
- Avoid all of the Highkeep guards. Sometimes this means waiting for one or more to path away from you.
- If you are spotted by a guard, you'll be detained and taken to [[The Storeroom Office]] {{waypoint 148, -124, 122}}, and have to work your way back after killing the guard.
- Gnomish Stilts and a Feather Fall Cloak may be useful.

## Steps

1. Find Kildiun.
   1. Travel to the **Lift to Highhold** {{waypoint -109, -160, -65}} and go to the [[The Stocks Ledge|Stocks Ledge]] at the top.
   1. Stay on the rocks at the edge so the guards won't see you. When you near the doorway, wait for both guards to fall asleep.
      - It's also possible to kill the pair of guards, so long as you aren't spotted by the roving Captain.
   1. Go through the [[The Store Room|Store Room]] and [[Keg Storage]] to the southwest. Each has wandering guards that you must watch until they're well past, then dash to the next corridor.
   1. Speak to Kildiun in the [[The Chow Hall|Chow Hall]] {{waypoint -186, -71, 110}}.
1. Kildiun will give you the key you need if you'll fetch him a bottle of **Jaestern's Prestige Reserve**.
   1. Head back towards the lift
   1. Just outside the doorway (where hopefully the guards are asleep!) there are four explosive barrels of gunpowder {{waypoint -165, -101, -29}}.
   1. Move a barrel to the top of the ramp near {{waypoint -113, -100, -85}}.
   1. The guards patrolling the ramp will trigger the explosion. If one barrel does not kill both guards, go get another barrel and repeat.
   1. Grab the bottle of **Jaestern's Prestige Reserve** {{waypoint -41, -122, -81}}.
   1. Return to Kildiun to finish the quest.
