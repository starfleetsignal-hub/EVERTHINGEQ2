## Description

"Amber Walker, a provisioner at Green Knoll, is exhausted and would like me to gather leaves and sticks. Once I have collected them, she would like me to use these objects to build a fire. That should give her enough time to rest before she has to cook up more food for Kelethin's defenders." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

![leaves and twigs](images/BuildingAFireQuestObjects.JPG)

1. Collect 4 piles of leaves in [[Treant Grove]] (around [[Butcherblock Canyon]] ( 779, 12, -78 ))
1. Collect 4 twigs
1. Start the fire next to Amber by clicking it
1. Speak to Amber

## Rewards

- At least 2s 57c

## Note

Both leaves and twigs are scattered around the path from Butcherblock Canyon to Orc Hill. The leaves are light green and the twigs look like the T2 dens you can find in the Commonlands or Antonica. They are both harvestable. As of March 2nd, previous respawn time issues have been resolved; the leaves are animated and bright enough to spot from Kelethin's platforms, and the twigs give off a green particle effect.
