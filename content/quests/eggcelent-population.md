Aleesia, a Growth centered glaufaye, wants to see a resurgence of umbral leafhoppers in Luminous Peaks, Luminous Steppe, and Zou'Lidelas. Their numbers have dwindled ever since the cave locust invaded.

## Steps

![Umbral Leafhopper Eggs](images/Umbral_leafhopper_eggs.png)
![Everdark Reeds](images/Everdark_Reeds.png)

1. Harvest 5 **umbral leafhopper eggs**.
   - These are small masses of yellowish eggs, found on the river banks in the [[Luminous Steppe]] around {{waypoint -463.23, 112.92, -13.17}}.
1. Place the eggs in the **Everdark Reeds** in the following areas:
   - [[Luminous Peaks]] {{waypoint -483.75, 156.45, 68.18}}
   - [[Stygian Threshold and Shadowdrip Tarn|Shadowdrip Tarn]] {{waypoint -645.57, 172.88, 188.95}}
   - [[Corpse Cauldron]] {{waypoint -506.76, 163.4, 420.30}}
   - [[Zou'Lidelas]] {{waypoint -301.15, 195.11, 274.31}}
   - [[Umbral River]] {{waypoint -445.18, 163.01, 213.98}}
     - *Note:The locations are only for the area! there are multiple possible locations in each area to get the update. look out for a small flower patch near the lakes/rivers*
1. Return to [[Aleesia]]
