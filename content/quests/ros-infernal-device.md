## Steps

1. Gather the supplies in [[Steamfont Mountains]] that [[Civean Il'Pernod]] has asked for: ![cooled magma](images/Cooled_magma.jpg)
   - Go to [[Grikbar Crater]] {{waypoint -284, 84, 1159}} or [[Mazkeen Crater]] {{waypoint -743, 130, 669}} and mine 15 *magma cores* from *cooled magma* nodes. These are big black rocks that respawn quickly and are found around the perimeter of the craters.
   - Buy 10 *magma crystals* from *Finbert's Salesbot* in [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]]. {{waypoint -583, 174, 965}}
     - Each crystal costs 16s 98c for a total cost of 1g 69s 8c.
1. Return to Civean Il'Pernod.
1. Return to Steamfont Mountains, go to Mazkeen Crater, and kill [[Grak Shattersnout]]. He will spawn on the northern edge of the crater as you approach. {{waypoint -813, 154, 661}}
1. Return to Civean Il'Pernod.

## Rewards

- +2500 faction with **Solusek Ro**
- Select one of the following:
  - [[Charm of Ro]]
  - [[Solusek's Fire]]
