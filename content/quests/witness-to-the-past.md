## Requirements

- Must have completed [[Kunark Ascending: A Nightmare Realized]] quest to view the Vaedenmoor portal {{waypoint 630.03, 242.22, 1191.43}}.

## Steps

1. Zone into [[Vaedenmoor, Witness to the Past]].
1. Speak with [[Projection of Tranquility]].
1. *I should watch the events unfold.*
   1. Listen to the conversation.
   1. Click on the orb in front of [[Lanys T'Vyl]] "quickly".
   1. Listen to the conversation.
1. Speak with [[Projection of Tranquility]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13200 status
- Achievement: [[Celestial Disorder]] which grants [[Rune of the Celestial]]
