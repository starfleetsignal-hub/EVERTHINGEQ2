## Steps

1. Head towards the [[Halls of Fate]]
1. Kill 5 [[A Doomwing agent|Doomwing agents]]
1. Kill 5 [[A Doomwing marauder|Doomwing marauders]]
1. Kill 5 [[A Doomwing warrior|Doomwing warriors]]
1. Return to [[Deegli Bubbowus]]

Comment: The name of the quest in your journal is "Striking the Legion's Army" and it will not find this page directly from game.

## Rewards

- +800 faction with **Drednever Expedition**
- At least 14g 98s 66c
- 5,580 at level 68.
