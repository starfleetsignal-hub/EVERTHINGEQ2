## Steps

1. Enter [[Gerion: Dominion of Pain (Solo)|Gerion: Dominion of Pain [Solo]]]
1. Gather 6 crystalline energy accumulators filled with errant Void energy
   - The energy accumulators are found throughout Dominion of Pain. Some possible locs include![A full energy accumulator](images/A_full_energy_accumulator.png)
     - {{waypoint 281, 1, 227}}, {{waypoint 336, 1, 128}}, {{waypoint 451, 1, 96}}
     - {{waypoint 506, 1, 126}}, {{waypoint 312, 1, 173}}, {{waypoint 332, 3, 271}}
     - {{waypoint 400, 4, 153}}
   - Make sure you loot ALL chests dropped by mobs you kill to obtain the Void Energies or you will have to do the zone again!![An empty energy accumulator](images/An_empty_energy_accumulator.png)
   - Any mob in the instance can drop [[Errant Void Energy]], including named.
   - Some energy accumulators are full, but others require 1-4 [[Errant Void Energy]] (drops from a small chest from mobs in Dominion of Pain.)
   - When you fill up an energy accumulator click it again to pick it up for update.(Not an actual inventory item)
   - NOTE: [[Errant Void Energy]] is NO-ZONE, so it will disappear from your inventory if you leave the instance!
1. Return to [[Hiwaaya Al'Naderi]] in [[Gerion, Hold of D'Lere]]
