## Prerequisites

- You must be able to speak [[Druzaic]], learned from the quest [[Words of Pure Magic]].
- You must be able to speak [[Goblish]], learned from the quest [[Claiming the Goblish Tongue]].
- You must be able to speak [[Krombral]], learned from the quest [[Words of a Giant]].

## Notes

- This quest is also part of the [[Lavastorm Timeline]].
- You may wish to wait until you are high enough level to do the other Lavastorm heritage quest, [[Saving Soles]], since they share many of the same questing areas.
- When you complete the quest, King Tranix is incorrectly identified as a kobold: "The crown was once the property of a formidable kobold king that once led his army in the name of Lord Nagafen, the red dragon." Tranix was actually a fire giant found in Nagafen's Lair in the original EverQuest.

## Steps

1. Obtain 20 shards of the broken goblin table by killing goblins within [[Lavastorm]]. {{waypoint -415, -118, 345}}
1. Return to [[Fendaris K'Lorn]]. {{waypoint -126, -115, 628}}
1. Collect 8 goblin banners written in a secret goblin code:
   - **The Sootfoot Banner of Najena:** {{waypoint -158, -130, 312}}
   - **The Sootfoot Banner of Might:** {{waypoint -225, -114, 147}}
   - **The Sootfoot Banner of Ancient Mines:** {{waypoint -320, -130, 453}}
   - **The Sootfoot Banner of Krombral:** {{waypoint -345, -130, 340}}
   - **The Sootfoot Banner of Nagafen:** {{waypoint -422, -114, 305}}
   - **The Sootfoot Banner of War:** {{waypoint -556, -118, 470}}
   - **The Sootfoot Banner of Flame:** {{waypoint -727, -113, 337}}
   - **The Sootfoot Banner of Marnek:** {{waypoint -737, -111, 443}}
1. Return to [[Fendaris K'Lorn]]. {{waypoint -126, -115, 628}}
   1. To continue from this point, you must be able to read the translated tablet. To read the table you must complete the quest: [[Words of Pure Magic]].
1. Visit the 5 locations mentioned in the goblin poem that Fendaris decoded for you:
   - **First Stanza**: {{waypoint -672.22, -80, 82.64}}
   - **Second Stanza**: {{waypoint -678, -67, -159}}
   - **Third Stanza**: {{waypoint -635, -111, 302}}
   - **Fourth Stanza**: {{waypoint -210, -114, 149}}
   - **Fifth Stanza**: {{waypoint -193, -114, 252}}
1. Find the name of the Unknown Legend. Speak with [[Bunglegreeder]]  inside the hut. {{waypoint -404, -98, 194}}
   1. If you can't understand Bunglegreeder, you must complete the quest: [[Claiming the Goblish Tongue]].
1. Find 8 rare rocks from Lavastorm:
   - **Metamorphite:** mined from "metamorphic stone"
   - **Flame Licked Diamond:** mined from "flame licked ore"
   - **Molten Fragmite:** dropped by [[A molten fragment|molten fragments]] around {{waypoint -262, -129, 390}}
   - **Noxious Pearl:** dropped by [[A noxious emission|noxious emissions]] around {{waypoint -672.20, 0.00, -14.91}}
   - **Basalt Turquizite:** dropped by [[A basalt rumbler|basalt rumblers]] around {{waypoint -532, -67, -165}}
   - **Globule Rose:** dropped by [[A flaming globule|flaming globules]] around {{waypoint -459, -98, 152}}
   - **Dormizite:** dropped by [[A dormant basalt rumbler|dormant basalt rumblers]] around {{waypoint -602, -44, -543}}
   - **Bouldering Heart:** dropped by [[A bouldering|boulderings]] around {{waypoint -606, -45, -568}}
     - On Origins Server, the loc is -437.05 -22.00 -548.21
1. Return to Bunglegreeder who instructs you to search for information about King Tranix. {{waypoint -404, -98, 194}}
1. Enter the [[Temple of Solusek Ro]] at {{waypoint -329, 4, -504}}   and speak with [[Tomekeeper Sunto]] in the library  {{waypoint -437, -60, -683}}
   - Upon entry to the Temple, follow the right wall to get to the library. Most mobs in the Temple do not see invis, so that can also be a help.
1. Seek out the bones of the ten Knights of Flame that were sent out across the [[Shattered Lands]]:
   - *See the talk page for more detailed information.*
   - **Narisin:** [[Antonica]]. Head to [[Windstalker Village]]. In the pond, enter the underwater cave at {{waypoint -1994, 0, -610}}. The bones are at {{waypoint -2051, -37, -564}}.
   - **Desdarn:** [[The Commonlands]] {{waypoint 252, -47, 252}}
   - **Weldin:** [[The Thundering Steppes]].  Enter the roekillik caves at {{waypoint 752, -18, 1182}}. The bones are at {{waypoint 853.91, -32.53, 1064.45}}
   - **Hogosh:** [[Nektulos Forest]]. Go to the bear cave at {{waypoint -1365.26, 29.48, -763.86}} . Bones are in the back at {{waypoint -1374, 28, -698}} .
   - **Karysin:** [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] {{waypoint -192, -25, -554}} On the bridge, hard to see.
   - **Jartellin:** [[Enchanted Lands]]. Go the Lamia cave at {{waypoint -335, 2, -630}}. Bones are in the cave at {{waypoint -398, 0, -624}}.
   - **Edgarin:** [[The Feerrott]] {{waypoint -1932, -23, 557}} On the ground, hard to see.
   - **Mortanis:** [[Everfrost]] {{waypoint 25, 28, -478}}
   - **Liantis:** [[Lavastorm]].  Go to {{waypoint -616.37, -79.86, 28.73}} and jump down into the crevasse. Bones are at {{waypoint -551, -92, 16}}
   - **Usalin:** [[Lavastorm]] {{waypoint -282, -6, -435}} on the ground.
1. Enter the [[Temple of Solusek Ro]] at {{waypoint -329, 4, -504}}   and take the dead knights' bones to the [[Censer of Souls]] at  {{waypoint -322, -45, -708}}. Note: This is not a person, it is the pot sitting on the table.
1. Speak once again with [[Tomekeeper Sunto]] in the library of the Temple of Solusek Ro. {{waypoint -438.60, -60.60, -686.50}}
1. Hail the Tome of Tranix lying on the table a few steps away from Tomekeeper Sunto.
1. Enter [[Solusek's Eye]] and examine the chest in the [[Chamber of the Speaker of Fates]] at {{waypoint 100, -357, 189}}.
   - *Note: The quickest way is to take the teleport to the [[Chamber of the Speaker of Fates]] which is near the zone entrance at {{waypoint 16, 14, -42}}. However if doesn't work unless you've previously visited the [[Chamber of the Speaker of Fates]] and stood on the receiving teleport at {{waypoint 85, -359, 196}}.*
   - **Warning:** If the [[The Speaker of Fates|Speaker of Fates]] or his [[A fatespeaker of Thyr|placeholder]] is up, DO NOT KILL HIM, as he is needed for the following step!
   - Navigating Solusek's Eye can be overwhelming. Refer to the [[Solusek's Eye#Geographic Walkthrough|walkthrough]] for directions.
1. Gather four circlets by killing:
   - [[The Speaker of Fates]] or his "placeholder" [[A fatespeaker of Thyr]]: {{waypoint 44, -360, 173}}. *He is in the same room as the chest.*
   - [[The Castigator]] or his "placeholder" [[A punisher of Thyr]]: {{waypoint -258, -371, -72}}. *He is on the same level as the chest.*
   - [[Lord Crana]] or his "placeholder" [[A glass worker]]: {{waypoint -287, -516, 123}}. *Bit of a run. Follow the path down to the 7th floor lift. Right click the wall button to call it down to the 7th. Edge yourself onto the elevator while still in view of the button and right click to send it back up to the 6th. Note: You have to be really quick to do this. You have to actually be on the elevator as you click the button, or it won't move. It took me a lot of tries to get it to move.*
   - [[Onyxlam]] or his "placeholder" [[A solumite colossus]]: {{waypoint -31, -34, -135}}. *Best to do this last as it's relatively near the zone in. Evac or potion of escape gets you nearby.*
1. Return to the chest in the Chamber of the Speaker of Fates at {{waypoint 100, -357, 189}}.
   - *Note: Quickest way is to return to near entrance and take the teleport to the [[Chamber of the Speaker of Fates]] at {{waypoint 16, 14, -42}}.*
   - After this step you can return to [[Fendaris K'Lorn]] and inform him of your success for a unique dialog.
