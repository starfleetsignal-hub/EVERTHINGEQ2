## Notes

- This is one of the daily (18 hour timer) repeatable missions offered after finishing [[Rage of Cthurath Crafting Timeline]].
- Bring 30 x Formless Coal.

## Steps

1. Harvest in [[Yon Gorroth]], [[Oogothl Sprawl]] or [[The Unknown]]:
   - 30 x Oblivion Ore
   - 18 x Nightscar Prowler Meat
   - 12 x Darou Daikon
1. Craft 6 x [[Mk I Terramalform Mines]] - each one needs 5 x Oblivion Ore, 3 x Nightscar Prowler Meat, 2 x Darou Daikon and 5 x Formless Coal.
   - use Engraved Desk or Varig's Forge multi station next to Taslana
1. Quest auto completes after last crafting combine
