## Steps

1. Go to [[The Glittering Mine]] and speak to [[Miner Gurgin]] at {{waypoint -110, -8, -1120}}.
1. Return to [[Seher Beanbrewer]].

## Rewards

- At least 1g 5s 62c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Defender's Girdle of the Mist]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Healer's Girdle of the Mist]] (All Priests)
  - [[Medium's Girdle of the Mist]] (All Mages)
  - [[Stalker's Girdle of the Mist]] (All Scouts)
