An ancient water spirit explained to me that a component of the broken druid rings is unbalancing the sprites' magical defenses, thus stopping them from being able to successfully fight off the Scourge.

## Notes

- You must speak [[Words of Pure Magic|Druzaic]] to communicate with the elemental.

## Steps

![annealed silvril shard](images/Animated_silvril_shard.png)

1. Gather 12 **annealed silvril shards** in the [[Grove of Stones]] {{waypoint 210, 14, 103}}.
1. Kill 4 Scourge orcs at the [[Strategy Gate]] {{waypoint 76, 3, -83}} in the northwest of [[Spirit Valley]].
1. Kill 4 Scourge orcs at the west end of the [[Timberlands]] {{waypoint -107, -24.78, 119.47}}.
1. Return to the [[A cleansed rivulet|cleansed rivulet]] {{waypoint -63.92, -3.65, -91.86}}.
1. Take the shards to the Ulteran Spires, south of [[Spirit Valley]] in [[Spire Basin]] {{waypoint -45.89, -31.88, 361.66}}.
1. Click the spire debris.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- One of the following:
  - [[Annealed Silvril Hoop]]
  - [[Annealed Silvril Stud]]
  - [[Annealed Silvril Link]]
