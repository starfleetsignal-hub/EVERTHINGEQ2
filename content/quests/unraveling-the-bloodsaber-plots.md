*This quest was part of the former access questline to enter [[The Crypt of Betrayal]], however its completion is no longer required.

## Notes

- The letter you delivered to [[Captain Eitoa]] reveals that the Bloodsabers have been gaining strength and preparing something big in [[Windstalker Village]]. It also reveals that [[Riason Hanagom]] is one of their leaders!

## Steps

1. Kill [[Riason Hanagom]] in [[Windstalker Village]] {{waypoint -1903, -30, -615}}, upstairs at the millhouse tavern.
1. Return to [[Captain Eitoa]] and she will instruct you to find a mage who can counteract the spell on the note so it can be read.
1. Go to the [[The Concordium Tower]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District|South Qeynos]], through the red teleporter, and speak with [[Rune Shimmerstar]]. {{waypoint 713, 41, 104}}
1. Return to [[Captain Eitoa]].
1. Find and kill [[A Bloodsaber courier]] around {{waypoint -907, -32, 228}} who will spawn as you run on the [[Qeynos Highway]].
1. Return to [[Captain Eitoa]] for your reward.
1. Speak to [[Wesaelan Brookshadow]] in [[Qeynos Province District|The Eldar Grove]], to see if his research has uncovered anything further about the Bloodsabers.

  - [[Plunderer's Small Link Chain Spaulders]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Initiate's Threaded Arm Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Theist's Shaped Plate Pauldrons]] (Clerics)
  - [[Footman's Worked Pauldrons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
