## Steps

1. Send the warwolf pup to attack 5 Rime.
   - Use the warwolf pup whistle to summon a temporary pet.   Any Rime will give an update (even those outside the area highlighted on the map) as soon as the pet attacks (i.e. no need to kill the mob).

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
