NOTE: You may need to mentor for this quest as it seems to kill the minecrawler immediately

It appears a tinkered minecrawler got lost on its way back to Splitiron Mines and powered down to conserve energy. I believe I can get it back to the mine's power station after I take its controls before it's completely drained. There are buttons near the top of its hull:

1. Display Battery Charge -- Returns the current battery power.
1. Mechnomagnetic Pulse -- Detects sources of mechnomechanic power surrounding the minecrawler. Usage drains 1% of charge.
1. Draw Power -- Inserts energy prongs into valid sources of power to charge batteries.

## Steps

1. Drive the minecrawler back to the battery charging station in the [[Splitiron Mine]] {{waypoint 182.79, 136.79, 240.02}}.
   - You start at 20% energy, so you'll need to take the most effective route and avoid combat.
   - Although simply using the shortest possible path will allow you to complete the quest, there are also invisible batteries in some spots that can be revealed using **Mechnomagnetic Pulse** and tapped using **Draw Power**. Some locations include:
     - {{waypoint 426.45, 85.51, 316.10}}
     - {{waypoint 440.18, 85.37, 397.85}}
     - {{waypoint 393.49, 87.41, 394.40}}
     - {{waypoint 407.63, 125.94, 290.43}}
     - {{waypoint 349.34, 126.93, 252.66}}
     - {{waypoint 323.61, 127.73, 229.51}}
     - {{waypoint 300.01, 127.85, 235.15}}
     - {{waypoint 282.04, 127.94, 229.43}}
     - {{waypoint 178.16, 136.79, 238.82}}
