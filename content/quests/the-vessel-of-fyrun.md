## Notes

- To reach the book,climb up the wall at {{waypoint -648, 8, 109}} or {{waypoint -621, 8, 109}}, then jump off the ledge into the [[Pool of Immortality]]. From there you can climb up to the cobra statue.
- You must Examine the book to be given the quest. Oddly the book comes with a Use option which opens it and allows you to read it but does not grant the quest. As soon as you have been granted the quest, the ancient urn spawns instantly at the expected location (assuming nobody has just read their book at the same time).

## Steps

1. Harvest ![an ancient urn](images/Image:An_ancient_urn.jpg) in the [[Priestess' Court]]. {{waypoint -332.42, 22.48, 111.83}}
   - Note: Only one person can harvest the urn at a time. Respawn is ~2 minutes, and you can no longer see the urn once you have harvested it.
1. Read the [[Tome of the Vessel]].
1. Fill the vessel by using the urn near the pools at these locations:
   - [[Priestess' Court]]: {{waypoint -287, -11, 148}}
   - [[Priestess' Court]]: {{waypoint -162, -4, 105}}
   - [[Residence Court]]: {{waypoint -296, -11, 397}}
   - [[Trade Court]]: {{waypoint -634, -19, 287}}
1. Read the Tome of the Vessel.
1. Find the pieces of the Writ of Akh Na'ba:
   - First piece: Kill [[A Sul' Dal scribe|Sul' Dal scribes]] for an uncommon update in [[Trade Court]]. {{waypoint -684, -19, 139}}
   - Second piece:  Kill [[A Rin' Dal sage|Rin' Dal sages]] in [[Priestess' Court]]. {{waypoint -226, -5, 148}}
   - Third piece: Kill [[Tirik' Rah]] in [[Priestess' Court]]. {{waypoint -171, -3, 165}}
     - [[Tirik' Rah]] is spawned by killing both groups of [[Protector of Tirik|protectors of Tirik]] (5 minute respawn time). If he does not show up and only the [[A court stalker|hounds]] do, kill the hounds and then kill the protectors again until he spawns with the hounds.
1. Read the Tome of the Vessel.
1. Go to the [[Priestess' Court]] and stand at {{waypoint -116.99, 33.00, 108.71}}. Read the [[Writ of Akh Na'ba]], received after completing the previous step, at exactly this location. A sparkly platform will materialize and lift you up.
   - If you're standing in the right place, you'll be lifted up on an elevator. If not, it says that "you must try another location", so you'll have to re-position and read the writ again. You will know that you are close to the correct spot if you try and camp. It will tell you that "you may not camp on a transporter". This can be frustrating as it is very particular as to the exact spot in which you need to stand. The lift is a small rectangle and you should appear to be standing on thin air just above the ground when on top of it.
   - If you are having difficulty with this step, try summoning [[Rin Priestess of the Fyr'Un]], stand at the given location {{waypoint -115, 13, 109}}, kill the Priestess, THEN examine the [[Writ of Akh Na'ba]] - in that order.
   - Another suggestion: go to {{waypoint -108, 13, 109}} first and then inspect the item you will be told that the lift is ascending. Something does get activated in that area. Then go to {{waypoint -115, 13, 109}} and inspect the item again for the final effect.
1. The quest will complete when the elevator lifts you up high enough.
