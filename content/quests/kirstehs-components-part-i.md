This is the Freeport armor quest starter for cloth armor wearers.(If your alignment is good (coercer) use the Illusionist armor timeline.)(It starts in the Elddar Grove, Gildas Cedartree  739, -21, -399)

## Steps

1. In [[The Commonlands]] near [[Ruins of Valmarr]]:{{waypoint 464, -53.81, 469}}
   - Kill 18 [[A fallen guardian|fallen guardians]]
   - Kill [[Lady Anyanka Polevshi]]
1. Return to Kirsteh

## Rewards

- One of the following class specific items:
  - [[Gloves of Allurement]] - Coercer
  - [[Shadeweave Gloves]] - Necromancer
  - [[Defiled Sanguine Gloves]] - Warlock
  - [[Spellbound Cerise Gloves]] - Wizard
