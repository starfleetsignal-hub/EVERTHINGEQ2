## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key.
   - Kill 15 [[A vicious snapjaw|vicious snapjaws]] around {{waypoint -11.61, 4.33, -151.83}} at [[Najami Oasis]].
1. Find the Frygia Music Box key clue from [[A sand devourer|sand devourers]] in [[Takish Badlands]] at [[Takish Dunes]] and [[Dunetooth Bluff]] around {{waypoint -680.19, 197.20, 158.40}}
1. Examine the clue to receive the quest [[The Frygia Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the **Frygia Music Box** in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]].

- At least 2p 52g 52s 84c
