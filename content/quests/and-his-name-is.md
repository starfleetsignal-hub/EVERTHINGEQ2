## Notes

- At the beginning of this quest, Gulthga will grant you a blessing of Cazic Thule. This adds a proc against lizardmen to the Ethernere Affinity buff. After this quest is completed the proc will be removed.
- you can do this quest at the same time as [[Lost Keys]] to save you time)
- If doing the Warrior Epic 2.0 series, speak to Yulsluu and do his quest series next which is part of [[Shades of Drinal: Skies of Red]]

## Steps

1. Enter [[Throne of Fear: Ascension (Advanced Solo)]]* or [[Throne of Fear (Heroic)]] at {{waypoint -2297, 202, 491}} in Fearcreep.
   - *Note: Quest updates even if beasties are grey.*
1. Defeat [[Pharinich]] near {{waypoint -229, 0, 58}}.
1. Defeat [[A Planar Manifestation]] near {{waypoint -157, -24, 41}}.
1. Defeat [[The Enraged Imp]] near {{waypoint -100, 0, -104}}.  Kill all nearby Imps to spawn, etc.
1. Defeat [[The Thoughtbleeder]] near {{waypoint -19, 0, -140}}.
1. Defeat [[Baelon of Thule]] near {{waypoint 155, 72, 100}} and loot [[An ancient stone tablet]] from the chest
1. Return to [[Gulthga]] to complete the quest.
