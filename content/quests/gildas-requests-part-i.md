This is part 1 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Qeynos]] who wear Cloth Armor.

## Steps

1. You must collect items from creatures in [[Antonica]]. These kill tasks MAY be randomly chosen from a pool of tasks, or may differ depending on the actual class of the player.
   - Kill 15 [[A rotstuffed scarecrow|rotstuffed scarecrows]]
   - Kill 14 [[A Necrosis conscript (Antonica)|Necrosis conscripts]]
   - or
   - Kill 14 [[A Caltorsis knight|Caltorsis knights]]
   - Kill 15 [[A Caltorsis cleric|Caltorsis clerics]]
1. Return to Gildas.
