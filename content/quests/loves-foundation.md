## Steps

1. Prove your faith by obtaining the eight Virtues of Marr from restless [[A Knight of Truth|Knights of Truth]] and the [[A Sentry of Passion|Sentries of Passion]] in Freeport's [[Graveyard]].<br>*These are not guaranteed updates, you may need to slay a few skeletons before obtaining an update.*
   - Obtain the Virtue of Charity
   - Obtain the Virtue of Compassion
   - Obtain the Virtue of Devotion
   - Obtain the Virtue of Honor
   - Obtain the Virtue of Love
   - Obtain the Virtue of Sacrifice
   - Obtain the Virtue of Truth
   - Obtain the Virtue of Valor
1. Return to Sister Leela Prendyn
1. Obtain icons of Love from several shrines to Erollisi around Norrath.
   - Gather an icon of Love within [[Qeynos Capitol District]] {{waypoint 518, -12, 163}}
   - Gather an icon of Love within [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 353, 131, 369}}
   - Gather an icon of Love within [[Antonica]] {{waypoint -730, -16, 241}}
   - Gather an icon of Love within the [[The Down Below|Down Below]] {{waypoint -95, -0.09, -118}}
1. Seek knowledge concerning Ullkorruuk's Followers.
   - Speak to **a restless spirit** in the Down Below {{waypoint -75, -0.11, -109}}
1. Recover the stolen Erollisi incense bowl from Bloodsabers within the [[The Crypt of Betrayal|Crypt of Betrayal]] {{waypoint 0.25, 6.86, -19.97}}. (drops from small chest even off grey mobs).
   1. To get to the Crypt, enter [[Vermin's Snye]] right next to the **restless spirit** and follow the waypoint above until you get to the second round room with ramps spiraling up. If you reached the right room, the door at the top will be closed. In this room there is a gutter in the water down below where you can enter the [[The Crypt of Betrayal]].
1. Return to Sister Leela to receive your rewards.

## Rewards

- +1,250 faction with **Erollisi Marr**
- [[Humble Altar of Erollisi Marr]]
