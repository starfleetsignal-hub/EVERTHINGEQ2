## Steps

1. Gather 6 [[Smelly goo]] in the Warsilk River {{waypoint 128.80, -237.73, -500.70}} . ![river goo (as seen under water)](images/River_goo.jpg)
   - Look for river goo - It's all over in the river right below Growf's location and easy to see without tracking, by hitting your Tab key. It is trackable by standard (not tradeskill) tracking ability
1. Speak with [[Growf]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +4000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
