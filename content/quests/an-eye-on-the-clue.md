## Steps

1. Slay gazers on Gazer Island{{waypoint 887, 458, 12}} until you get an update.
1. Speak to [[Gnibliks Shortstacks]] (who will be near where you killed the gazer), or return to [[Bilgeron Cogsworth IV]].
   - Any gazers in the area will continue to attack, and it's possible for Gnibliks to be killed or de-spawn. If that happens, return to where Bilgeron is located on the [[Temple Grounds]] and Gnibliks will be there.
