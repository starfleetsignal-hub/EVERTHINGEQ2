## Steps

1. Speak with [[Calacea Mossrunner]] in [[Loping Plains]]. {{waypoint -348, 9, -67}}
1. Speak with [[Mirwen Beluran]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]]. {{waypoint -551, 171, 1023}}
1. Go to [[Greater Faydark]] and enter [[The Silent Cavern]] via the door at {{waypoint -456, 55, -271}}.
1. Kill [[Kilzak the Twisted]]. {{waypoint -269, -38.5, -7.5}}
1. Return to [[Rooleean Felodaan]] in [[Greater Faydark]]. {{waypoint -393, 15, 184}}
