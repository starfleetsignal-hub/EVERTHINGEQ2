## Steps

1. Go to [[The Rotting Ground]] in [[The Mystic Lake]] {{waypoint -186, -3, 134}} and defeat [[Zeera the Thorned]] {{waypoint -207, -4, 128}}.
   - Zeera will cast Rain of Thistles shortly after she is engaged. This is a high-damage magic AoE with a 15 meter range which can kill non-fighter classes.
   - Once Zeera is defeated, you receive [[The Seeds of Corruption]], a furniture item.
1. Return to [[Brother Pang]].
1. Speak to [[Kuabu the Songkeeper]] who wanders in the [[Courtyard of Hope]]. {{waypoint 23, 14, -80}}
1. Speak to [[Hunra the Carver]] in The Mystic Lake. {{waypoint 220, -46, 338}}
1. Kill two pirates in the [[Gunthak Pirate Camp]] {{waypoint 68, -39, 303}} (both level 67^^^ Heroic):
   - Kill [[Pirate Keelkrush]] (wanders the camp)
   - Kill [[Pirate Greenwhisker]] {{waypoint 57, -36, 307}}
1. Click on bushes in [[Direvine Wood]] {{waypoint -62, 9, 248}}. [[Pirate Prince Prawn]] will be revealed. After a short conversation, defeat him in combat.
   - The journal directs you to "follow the map", however you never actually receive a map; you may open your waypoints (Alt w) and select "X marks the pirate camp" to be guided to the bushes.
1. Return to Kuabu in [[The Village of Shin]]. {{waypoint 23, 14, -80}}
1. Go to [[The Forsaken City]] and click on a bookcase near the [[God of Knowledge]] {{waypoint -86, -46, -6}}. Kill [[A Delirious Lyricist]] that spawns. (level 68^^^ ghost Troubador)
1. Return to Kuabu in The Village of Shin. {{waypoint 23, 14, -80}}
1. Enter the [[Sepulcher of Zan Fi]] in the Mystic Lake. {{waypoint 13, -25, -9}}
1. Defeat 6 waves of 3 to 4 level 67-70 heroic golems (no rest between waves, pace yourself).
1. Defeat [[Kudzua the Tormentor]]. (level 72^^^ dryad)
1. Click on the tomb in the middle of the room to receive a flute, then use the flute to free the spirit.
1. Return to Brother Pang.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 55s
- +200 faction with **The Whistling Fists Clan**
- One of the following:
  - One of the following, based on Class:
    - [[Curate's Plate Pauldrons of the Zealous]] (Clerics)
    - [[Dragoon's Pauldrons of Power]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
    - [[Naturewalker's Shoulder Pads of the Sapient]] (Druids, Channelers)
    - [[Sensei's Arm Wraps of Perfection]] (Brawlers)
    - [[Spiritualist's Spaulders of the Enlightened]] (Shamans)
    - [[Thaumaturge's Epaulets of Genius]] (All Mages)
    - [[Vagabond's Chain Spaulders of Deftness]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Broken Idol of Zan Fi]]
  - [[Cracked Idol of Zan Fi]]
  - [[Damaged Idol of Zan Fi]]
  - [[Fractured Idol of Zan Fi]]
- Completion of this quest, along with [[Brother Lycabe's Trial: Knowledge of a Lost City]], provides access to the third floor of the tower.
