## Notes

![](images/Investigate_The_Skull.png)

- After each skeletal guardian is killed, examine the skull that spawns on the floor of their bone tower. You will receive their respective *heart of fear* and *corrupted mind*. They are needed for the following quest.
  - If you leave the instance for whatever reason, these items will be removed from your inventory. However, the skulls will reappear in the towers and can be examined again.
- This quest is repeatable.. However, the first time you complete this quest, it will not indicate that it is repeatable. To obtain the repeatable version of this quest, re-harvest [[Ernie's Incorporeal-Visual Activators]], at {{waypoint 255, 18, 349}}.

## Steps

1. Defeat the skeletal guardians of the bone towers. To spawn the skeletal guardians, clear all the [[A fetid fiend|fetid fiends]] from around their corresponding tower:
   - [[The Skeletal Destructor]]: {{waypoint -219, 32, 419}}
     - Examine the skull to receive:
       - [[A skeletal destructor's heart of fear]]
       - [[A skeletal destructor's corrupted mind]]
   - [[The Skeletal Master]] {{waypoint 290, 17, 323}}
     - Examine the skull to receive:
       - [[A skeletal masters heart of fear]]
       - [[A skeletal masters corrupted mind]]
   - [[The Skeletal Lord]] {{waypoint 210, 11, -273}}
     - Examine the skull to receive:
       - [[A skeletal lords heart of fear]]
       - [[A skeletal lords corrupted mind]]
1. Pick up [[A torn journal page]] from the ground. It has two confirmed spawn locations:
   - {{waypoint 208, 60, 23}}
   - {{waypoint -247.39, 15.41, 211.98}}

## Rewards

- At least 6g 56s 28c
