## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key.
   - Kill 15 [[A sand snapper|sand snappers]] around {{waypoint 557.73, -61.05, 301.54}} or {{waypoint 574.89, -17.60, 30.74}}
1. Find the Ardoria music box key clue from Dunetooth goblins in [[Takish Badlands]] at [[Dunetooth Bluff]] around {{waypoint -756.36, 196.10, 97.10}}
   - [[A sullen Dunetooth fiend|sullen Dunetooth fiends]], [[A sullen Dunetooth savage|sullen Dunetooth savages]], or [[A vexed Dunetooth fiend|vexed Dunetooth fiends]], [[A vexed Dunetooth savage|vexed Dunetooth savages]].
1. Examine the clue to receive the quest [[The Ardoria Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the **Ardoria Music Box** in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]].
