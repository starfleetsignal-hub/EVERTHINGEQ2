## Steps

1. Enter [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Crucible]] in [[Everfrost]].
1. ![a hired mercenary](images/A_hired_mercenary.jpg)Find and eliminate 8 rogue members inside The Crucible.
   - These are named "a hired mercenary", which are two groups of 4 mobs each, located at the top of the stairs just before the final named "Codexicon".
   - You must have a new instance, this part of the quest can not be completed in a zone someone has already cleared.
1. Return to [[Evana L'Alval]]

## Rewards

- [[Title: "the Dark Talon"]]
- at least 1p 12g 7s
  - Level 92: 2p 8g 14s
- +10,000 faction with **The Circle of the Unseen Hand**
