## Steps

1. Find and kill a strong raptor in the Highland Salts near the [[Stonebrunt Encampment]].
   - [[Sharptalon]] spawns as you approach {{waypoint 647, 131, 3710}}.
1. Return to [[Jaen Tiuni|Jaen]] in the Hua Mein caves.
1. Take the basket to [[Cio'lii]] at {{waypoint -485, 294, 3151}}
