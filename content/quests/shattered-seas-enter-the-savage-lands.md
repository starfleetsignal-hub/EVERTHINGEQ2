## Steps

1. Fly to [[South Dshinn]] island at {{waypoint -617, 6, 251}}
1. Search for signs of [[Captain Greymast]]: click on row boat at southern small dock {{waypoint -634, 7, 336}}

1. Talk to pygmy [[Guldri]] nearby
1. Help Guldri in exchange for his help - complete the quest he offers you, [[A Deinodon is Angry]]
1. Continue helping the pygmies in exchange for their help in finding Captain Greymast
   - Help the pygmies of South Dshinn - complete solo quests through [[A Tale of Two Trails]]
   - Help more pygmies of South Dshinn - complete solo quests through [[Starque Raving Mad]].
1. Help [[Nuwat Pickletrade]] battle the Allu'thoa on [[South Dshinn]] and complete the final quest: [[Raw Materials]].
