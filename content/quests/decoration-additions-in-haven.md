## Steps

1. Decorate [[Haven]]. The locations have been marked by the Gigglegibber goblins with green sparkles:
   - First location is {{waypoint -44, 151, -83}}
   - Second location is {{waypoint 75, 151, -118}}
   - Third location is {{waypoint -49, 138, 272}}
   - Final location is {{waypoint -95, -73, -174}}
   - After placing the final decoration, [[Mr. McScroogle]] will spawn equal to your level and attack you. Kill him for the final update.
1. Return to Gutten, the Gigglegibber tree merchant, to claim your [[Frosty Frostfell Tree Ticket]] reward.

## Note

- If you talk to Gutten Gigglegibber again he will tell you that you can decorate the tree you just received by talking to McScroogle; see [[A Frostfell Favor]]. If you already did that quest, he will know that and tell you so.
- To use the ticket double click the tree to the left with the blue star on top.
