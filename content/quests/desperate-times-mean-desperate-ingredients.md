## Steps

After explaining the needs she and Chef Bahiyya have, Jailaya will give you the "portable pantry" (look in inventory, looks like a cube) and the quest will complete.

## Rewards

- [[Portable Pantry]] - which offers quests similar to the [[Necromantic Icebox]]

- Click on each entry listed in the Pantry to get the quest for that creature.  If no one else is on the same quest in the area, you can click and do the same quest repeatably.  4 times will clear that creature from the list.
