## Steps

1. Use your abilities to query and dominate [[A docile water elemental]] in the [[Fens of Nathsar]] at the same location you previously did.
   - A level 80^ [[Water elemental]] will spawn in its place and attack you.
   - You are looking for an uncommon body drop called [[Lesser Element: Water]]. You need to collect 10 of these.
     - When you have all 10, right-click on the stack in your inventory and select 'Use' to receive the update.
