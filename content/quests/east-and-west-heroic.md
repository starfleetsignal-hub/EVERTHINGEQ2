## Steps

*The Temple of Veeshan is home to the strongest of the Awakened, the dragons that follow Kerafyrm in his conquest of Norrath. Defeating the strongest members of his armies could throw his forces into disarray and give those allied against him an advantage. Find the leaders in the Temple of Veeshan, and destroy them.*

- Defeat Fherin the Ancient in the Halls of the Betrayer, and Gangel in the Laboratory of Mutation.
  - Destroy [[Fherin the Ancient (Heroic)|Fherin the Ancient]] in [[Temple of Veeshan: Halls of the Betrayer (Heroic)]]
  - Destroy [[Gangel the Resurrected]] in [[Temple of Veeshan: Laboratory of Mutation (Heroic)]]
