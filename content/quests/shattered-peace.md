## Note

- this quest is offered to Ogres only

## Steps

1. Return to the body of Jory Sevitus. (The body will still be where you defeated him even if you return to do the quest after logging out.)
1. Right click on the body and choose Search from the options. This will put a examinable note in your inventory.
1. Read the note by right clicking on it in your inventory.
1. Right click on the corpse of Jory and select Sever Hand from the options. Jory's hand will be in your inventory.
1. Enter the water to access a Curious Entrance at {{waypoint -200, -61, -104}}
1. Slay 8 follower's of peace (non-aggro mob) scattered around the room.
1. Click on the bench in front of the Statue of Quellious. This will spawn 5 monks of peace.
1. Slay the monks of peace.
1. Click the bench in front of the statue again and then the statue to destroy it.
1. Return to Kroota Gukbutcher at ( 220, -3, 100 )  in West Freeport for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 3s 44c
- [[Ring of the Ogre]]
