## Steps

1. Speak with [[Rooleean Felodaan]] at [[Spire Shadow Outpost]] in [[Greater Faydark]] at {{waypoint -393, 15, 184}}.  At -443.24 124.28 495.86 108.02 on Anashti Sul.
1. Speak with [[Th'las Wildwood]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]] at {{waypoint -106, -12, -42}} Upper Grove just to the right of the stable master.
1. Kill [[Ironwood the Cruel]] (60^^^ Heroic) in the north part Lesser Faydark to obtain [[A void crystal fragment]]. {{waypoint -62, -16, -278}}
1. Bring the fragment to [[Rooleean Felodaan]] at the [[Spire Shadow Outpost]] in [[Greater Faydark]]. {{waypoint -393, 15, 184}}
