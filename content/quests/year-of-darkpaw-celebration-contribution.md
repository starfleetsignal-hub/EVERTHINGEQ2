## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
- 1 mission every 18 hours possible
- Quest is available in every zone where a Celebration Merchant is located.
  - See [[Year of Darkpaw Timeline]] for a list of Avatar Camps to find the merchants and glowing book to start the quest.

## Steps

During the Masquerades of Divinity event, I found a book that listed the accomplishments of heroes from across Norrath. Whether it was the air of jubilation about with the Year of Darkpaw celebration or a drive to prove myself I feel that I must add my name to the list. So, I'll need to gather my weapons, spells, and tools to contribute to the festivities through a display of combat, crafting, or gathering.

1. You are asked to complete any 1 of the 4 things listed:
   - either Defeat a named encounter
   - Defeat 20 encounters
   - Complete any tradeskill writ
   - Successfully harvest 20 resources
     - *Note: this is harvest any nodes in any zone 20 times, NOT just 20 harvests*
