## Description

"Grimply Sniffwhistle created a mechanical helper to gather fresh cut treant wood for him. It had a malfunction and Grimply got rid of it. But now he is in need of someone to gather some fresh cut treant wood for  him. He asked me to assist him with that need. Treants can be found in the Treant Grove." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay treants until you get 10 fresh cut treant woods:
   - Seems any treant will do ( [[Coerced sapling|a coerced sapling]], [[A sapling]], etc...)
1. Return to [[Grimply Sniffwistle]].

## Rewards

- At least 92c
