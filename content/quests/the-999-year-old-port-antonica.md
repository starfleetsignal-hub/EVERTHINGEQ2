## Notes

- This is the opening quest for the first expansion of Everquest II, Desert of Flames.
- This quest is part 1 of 3, which grants access to the instances [[Aboard the Smarmy Sprocket]] and [[Prison of Admiral Grimgadget]].
- Accepting the quest you receive the following two items:
  - [[Multidirectional Aerospanning Locationater]] (MAL) looks like a red cube.
  - [[Manual Powered Mechanical Digging Device]] (MPMDD) looks like a pick axe.

## Steps

1. Use the MAL to find the treasure. Keep in mind the following:
   - The treasure is found in a random location (anywhere in the zone). If you're in a group, each person will be heading to different treasure locations.
   - The MAL gives you two basic readings: Directional Color and Distance Strength
     - Directional Color:
       - Red = directly behind you
       - Orange = somewhere behind you
       - Yellow = somewhere in front of you
       - Green = straight in front of you
     - Distance Strength (in order from far to near):
       - Faint, Dim, Dull, Bright, Blinding
   - When you're standing right on top of the treasure the MAL will glow with a **blinding greenish hue**. At this point use the MPMDD to dig up the treasure.
   - Sometimes even a single step in a different direction can change how the MAL glows. If you think you're really close to the treasure and then suddenly seem to be far away, try stepping backwards a number of steps and use the MAL again. It takes a little bit of tweaking to find the treasure.
1. Return to [[Sparrow Cogcarrier]] for your reward.
