## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Pass out 10 fliers to passing heroes.
   - This involves targeting another player character and using the stack of fliers on them. The fliers can be dragged to a hotkey button to be used, which makes it easier. The players can be in any zone.
1. Return to [[Slippery Toughshield|Slippery]]
