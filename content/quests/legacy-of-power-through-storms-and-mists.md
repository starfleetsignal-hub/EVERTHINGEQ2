## Notes

You may want to pick up the Solo Mission quests first.

## Steps

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -95, 3, -165}} to enter [[Torden, Bastion of Thunder: Tower Breach (Solo)|Torden, Bastion of Thunder: Tower Breach [Solo]]].
1. Speak to [[Klodsag]] at {{waypoint -62, -16, -16}}.  *Note:  If you forget to talk to him, the quest doesn't break.  If you ran all the way around and didn't find a chest, you probably forgot to talk to him first. Talk to him and then go back for the chest. It looks and behaves like "void shard' chests, with an onscreen message. If you do not have any dialogue responses when you hail Klodsag, check to make sure you do not have an Ascension Form active, or any illusions active - you will not receive the quest items from the chests if you do not advance the quest with the dialogue.*
1. Head north over the bridge and kill the Primordial Malice to spawn [[Gatekeeper Karatil]].
1. Take the left corridor to the west.
   - The ground gets icy/slippery past {{waypoint 227, 11, -639}}. Only flat ground is slippery. The ramps are not.
1. Travel up around the tower (the ramp up starts near {{waypoint 735, -17, -721}}).
   - *Note: You can train/drop the trash on the ramps.*
1. Kill [[Inquisitor Barol]] around {{waypoint 578, 37, -709}}.
1. Use the teleporter at {{waypoint 726, 198, -719}}.
1. Kill [[Auliffe Chaoswind]] at the top of the tower.
   - This a joust fight, where you need to move inside the white circle, and outside the red circle.
   - When the boss says "I am not locked in here with you, you are locked in here with ME!", move **inside** the white circle (a swirling wall of snow) or die. Note that the wall is always inside the room, but not necessarily near the boss.
   - When the boss says "Back off! Let's see how well you scramble!", move **outside** of the red circle before it disappears or die.
   - The key is to slow burn, about 10-15%, otherwise there can be both white/red joust rings.  If you are at a higher level, use auto attack only.
1. Loot the key from his body and look for his treasure chest at the base of the tower.
   - The [[Frostbite crystal]] is in Auliffe Chaoswind's Treasure chest.
   - There is a portal that takes you to the "Icy Hall", a balcony you can jump down from.
   - The chest spawns at a random location at the bottom of the tower.
   - Dismounting and using a floaty cloak (or racial float) can be faster than running all the way back down the tower.
1. Head back and go to the other branch of the north wing, The Sand Mines.
   - Kill [[Keeper of Past Lore]] near {{waypoint -1441, 289, -1303}}
1. [[Gaukr Sandstorm]] is at the top of the tower at {{waypoint -663, 309, -634}}
   - He has a black screen curse and big power drain.
1. Loot the key from his body and find the chest at the base of the tower.
   - The [[Bead of polished krendicite]] is in Gaukr Sandstorm's treasure chest.
   - The chest spawns at a random location at the bottom of the tower.
1. Return to the [[Coliseum of Valor]].
1. Enter [[Torden, Bastion of Thunder: Winds of Change (Solo)|Torden, Bastion of Thunder: Winds of Change [Solo]]] at {{waypoint -88, 3, -157}}.
1. Go south and kill [[Elif Whitewind]] near {{waypoint -2.68, 10, 451}}.
   - Constant DoT damage plus significant power drain
1. Head left/east at the fork
   - Be careful crossing the bridge as the wind can blow you off if you don't stop moving. Holding the spacebar down while running in the middle of the bridge will keep you from getting thrown off.  Turning off your mount also helps.
     - "You feel unsteady on your feet as heavy winds gust by you!"
1. Kill [[The Hurricane]] that paths clock-wise around the base of the tower.
   - Has a trama det power drain
1. Go back across the bridge to {{waypoint -274, 9, 684}} and hail [[A Wind Spirit]].
   - Have it escort you through the storm.
1. Each time you land on a platform, kill the [[Bastion Windcaller]] and hail the next wind spirit.
   - There are 4 such platforms.
1. Kill [[Yveti Stormbrood]] that flies around on top and loot his body for the key.
   - Yveti is only in range for pulling from the top platforms, where you can be blown off if you move after the gust warning (blue body shimmer). You can pull with Mercenary
   - It's easiest to target Yveti while you are flying towards the top platform.
1. Take the teleport pad at {{waypoint -662, 310, 636}} back to the entrance.
1. Return to the bottom of the tower (east) and find the chest for the [[Funnel iron shavings]].
1. Return to the path going west and kill [[Laef Windfall]] near {{waypoint 554, -16, 633}}.
   - He casts a frontal stun after the message "Laef Windfall prepares to unleash a barrage of powerful wind in front of him! You might want to try to avoid this!". He is immune to damage during his frontal.
   - The stun is not curable (there is no detriment) and will persist for a while even after he dies.Consider rooting him and keeping away.
1. Go up the ramp to the top of the tower, killing trash on platforms to the teleport at {{waypoint 720, 198, 718}}.  NOTE:  The only really dangerous spot as you go up is the area with the moving lightning storms (after using the teleporter).  If one hits you, it does 23-30 million damage, likely killing damage. Avoid them, there's only one platform with killer lightning bolts.
1. [[Torstien Stoneskin]] and [[Hreidar Lynhillig]] protect each other.
   - One or the other periodically gets more damage reduction then the other. If you notice one taking less damage, you can swap, or you can just burn one and then swap to the other. Both have to be at 1% before either will die.
1. Loot the key off their corpse.
1. Take the portal that just spawned to Thunder Coliseum
1. Loot [[Shard of Glowing Arcglass]] from the chest at the base of the tower.
   - The chest location is random.
1. Return all 4 components to [[Klodsag]] in the center {{waypoint -61, -17, -12}} and he will make [[Staff of the Rainkeeper]]
1. Use the new portal at {{waypoint -158, -19, -3}} to [[Tower of the Rainkeeper]] (New portal in this instance, NOT in the Coliseum of Valor.)
1. Kill all the Primordial Malice.
   - You can body pull them if you don't want to kil the giants.
1. Go through the passage at {{waypoint -28, 994, 21}}.
1. Listen in on conversation between [[Agnarr]], [[Lanys T'Vyl]], and [[Karana]].  Then hail Karana and give him the repaired staff.  He will teleport you back to the [[Coliseum of Valor]]
1. Speak with [[Karana]] in the [[Coliseum of Valor]] at {{waypoint 1, 6, 8}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 41g 75s 48,000 status
- [[Horoscopic Quill]]
- [[The Tempest Key]] - Allows access to the "Torden, Bastion of Thunder" zones for the entire account.
