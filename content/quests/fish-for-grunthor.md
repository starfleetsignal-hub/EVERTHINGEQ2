## Notes

- The first turn-in yields the [[A tiny barracuda plushie|tiny barracuda plushie]] and unlocks the unlimited repeatable version of the quest. Both the stand-alone and the unlimited repeatable count as separate entries in the [[Quest Journal]].

## Steps

1. Harvest 10 fish in Everfrost.
   - It has to be 10 separate harvest events - multi-harvests do not count.
1. Return to [[Burm Grunthor]].

## Rewards

- [[A tiny barracuda plushie]] *- only for the first completion*
- At least 1g 82s 97c
