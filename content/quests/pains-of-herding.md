## Steps

1. Travel to the [[Moors of Ykesha]] and search dock area for the missing herd of [[A Skystrider|skystriders]] {{waypoint 1873, 453, 902}}.
1. The quest should update sending you to look for Weylin Slipfoot {{waypoint 1401, -1090, 918}}.![Weylin Slipfoot and his skystrider](images/EQ2_Weylin.jpg)![Tide Skimmers](images/Tide_skimmers.jpg)
   - Weylin is located ALL the way down at the bottom of the gorge.  Those without glide/flight capability will need to take the hot air balloon to the bottom and back up again. Or, those crazy/daring enough can try jumping into the ocean. (It IS possible to jump with a low-level toon ... as long as you hit the ocean!)
1. Catch 12 **Ykesha Barracuda** for Weylin's very tired skystrider.![Ykeshan Barracuda {as seen from underwater}](images/Ykeshan_Barracuda.jpg)
   - Click on the Ykeshan Barracudas, which are unlabeled, green fish.
   - Kill the Tide Skimmers that aggro when you click on Ykeshan Barracuda.
   - Note: individual update, each group member will need to do their own "fishing."
1. Return to [[Weylin Slipfoot]].
1. Return to [[Turry Goldsworth]]{{waypoint 11, -1, 13}} in [[Enchanted Lands]] for reward

## Rewards

One of the following:

- [[Ruby Skimmer]]
- [[Topaz Skimmer]]
- 10 [[Fool's Gold Coin|Fool's Gold Coins]]
