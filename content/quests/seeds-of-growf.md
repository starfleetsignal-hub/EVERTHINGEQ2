## Notes

While in the area  to collect the seeds, pick up [[The Gardens Are In Bloom]] quest from [[Taelonar]] too.

## Steps

![Click a small bag inside the cart. (Click to enlarge)](images/Seeds-of-growf-cart.jpg)

1. Collect seeds from the plants of the gardens of the Cae'dal elves. The seeds are in a gardener's cart at {{waypoint -516, -85, -673}}, in a small bag.
1. Speak with [[Growf]]

## Rewards

- [[Sovereign of Rile]] (currency)
- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +4,000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
