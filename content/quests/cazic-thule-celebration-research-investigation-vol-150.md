## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Cazic-Thule has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items.

1. Purchase the following items
   - Purchase the [[Faceless Mask|faceless mask]] from Erga Tuur for 5000 status
     - *in [[The Feerrott]] wanders the road from Alliz Evol Station {{waypoint -122, -17, 551}} to [[Golgoth Bridge]] {{waypoint -342, -6, 507}}*
   - Purchase the [[Gloom Light Lantern|gloom light lantern]] from Oliska for 5000 status
     - *in [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}*
     - *in [[Qeynos Province District]] at the [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}*
   - Purchase the [[Shadowtouched Scales|shadowtouched scales]] from Laparnuu for 5000 status
     - *in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]], near [[Petalcrown Perch]] and [[The Roost Warrens]].  Laparnuu wanders the path from {{waypoint 338, 238, 7}}  all the way to {{waypoint 31, 236, -13}}.*
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Faceless]] {{waypoint -1109, -1, 919}} in [[The Feerrott]]
