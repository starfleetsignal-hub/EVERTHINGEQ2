## Notes

- Synchronize steps in this quest with ones in [[Court of Truth: Sphinx Hunt]], so as not to waste time.
- If you have issues spawning [[The Terrorgore (The Clefts of Rujark)|The Terrorgore]] in [[The Clefts of Rujark]], zone out then back in.

## Steps

1. Go to the Saracen's Hideout in [[Maj'Dul]]; roof entrance at {{waypoint -170, 150, 118}}.
1. Examine a note on the floor.
1. Go to [[The Sinking Sands]] and speak with [[The Terrorgore (The Sinking Sands)|"T"]] at the south entrance of the [[The Twin Tears|southern tear]]. {{waypoint -468, -126, -690}}.
1. Kill 30 Alliz Raef Ew lizardmen in the area within 15 minutes.
1. Return to the Terrorgore.
1. Kill 30 Anaz Mal gnolls in the area within 15 minutes.
1. Return to the Terrorgore.
1. Meet [[The Terrorgore (The Clefts of Rujark)|The Terrorgore]] in [[The Clefts of Rujark]] at {{waypoint -99, -5, -40}}.
1. Kill 30 Steelslave Orcs in the area within 20 minutes.
1. Return to the Terrorgore.
1. Speak with [[The Terrorgore (The Living Tombs)|The Terrorgore]] in [[The Living Tombs]] at {{waypoint -567, -9, 298}}.
1. Kill 40 mummies in the surrounding area within 20 minutes.
1. Return to The Terrorgore.
1. Stop the [[An alerted Faro' Nuk|ambush]] within 5 minutes.
1. Speak with The Terrorgore.
   - If the Terrorgore dies, he will respawn in a few minutes if he dies. Moving back and forth near his spawn seems to encourage respawn.
