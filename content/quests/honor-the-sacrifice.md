Sister Leela Prendyn has offered to help me honor Messira Goodbolt, the Swornloves' childhood friend, and the sacrifice she made to save Aaronolis. Sacrifice is the last of the Virtues of Marr more often associated with Erollisi, but often held the most important of all of them.

## Steps

1. Gather eight **ember wax** within the Ember Hive area in Kylong Plains {{waypoint 1586, 211, -1891}}.
   - The Sokokar Post Highton gets you very close.
1. Return to Sister Leela.
1. Enter the [[Abysm of Treachery]], behind the **Moveable Rock** {{waypoint -86, 37, -280}}
   - Place the aromatic candles to honor Messira {{waypoint 46, -1, -581}}
   - Speak to [[Priest Aaronolis Swornlove]] in the Abysm of Treachery {{waypoint 41, -1, -599}}
1. Return to Sister Leela to receive your rewards.

## Rewards

- +10,000 faction with **Erollisi Marr**
- [[Cloak of Love]]
