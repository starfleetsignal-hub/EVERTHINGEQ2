## Steps

After accepting this quest, Rask will ask you whether you'd prefer to kill gnolls or Bloodsabers.  Which series of subquests you have to complete depends on how you answer.

*If you choose to kill gnolls*

1. [[Vale of the Shattering: Proof of Worth]]
1. [[Vale of the Shattering: Investigating the Trail]]
1. [[Vale of the Shattering: Dispersing the Unkempt]]

*If you choose to kill Bloodsabers*

1. [[Vale of the Shattering: Proof of Ability]]
1. [[Vale of the Shattering: Investigating the Trail]]
1. [[Vale of the Shattering: Visiting the Windstalker Grave]]

## Rewards

- At least 11s 13c
