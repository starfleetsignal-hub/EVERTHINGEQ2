The Grand Master at the Wu Encampment confirmed that the samples you brought back to him were laced with poison.  You are to counteract and neutralize the poison.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Grand Master Lu Sun]] at the [[Camp of the Legendary Wu]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]] at {{waypoint 781, 111, -281}}.  You will be given a poison antidote.
1. Go back to that same provisioner tent from the last quest (with the 3 barrels {{waypoint -587, -61, 484}}) and right click each one and select pour antidote to update the quest.
1. Return to [[Grand Master Lu Sun]] for your reward.

## Faction

- +250 faction with **Disciples of Wu**

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Thrice Woven Band]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Thrice Woven Crest]] (All Priests)
  - [[Thrice Woven Emblem]] (All Mages)
  - [[Thrice Woven Signet]] (All Fighters)
