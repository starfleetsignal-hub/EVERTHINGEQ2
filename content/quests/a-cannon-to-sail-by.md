## Prerequisites

- You must be either a level 72 adventurer or a level 50 tradeskiller in order to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Dudley Sprocklit]], who is located across the chasm at [[U.J.L. 1.0]]. {{waypoint 1405, 406, 704}}
   - Use the [[Upper Dropzone Gnomecannon]] to travel across the chasm. You must be dismounted to use it. {{waypoint 1567, 453, 817}}
1. Gather 8 ore from the wagons near [[Excavation bot|excavation bots]], around {{waypoint 1346, 412, 653}} and {{waypoint 1323, 407, 823}}.
1. Gather a fiber from one of the [[Sweeper bot|sweeper bots]] by right-clicking on it and selecting "Retrieve fiber". {{waypoint 1365, 406, 750}}
1. Return the items to Dudley Sprocklit.
1. Use the newly-built [[Lower Dropzone Gnomecannon]] and return to [[Ember Tizzlespring]].
   - You must be dismounted to use the cannon.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- Use of the [[Lower Dropzone Gnomecannon]].
