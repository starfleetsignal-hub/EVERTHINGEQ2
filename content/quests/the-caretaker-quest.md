## Steps

1. Deliver the book to [[The Librarian]]. {{waypoint 226, 146, -69}}
   - Upon handing in the book, she will attack you. Each player must start the dialog before anyone completes it or you will need to wait for the Librarian to respawn. The update is given upon completion of the dialogue, killing the Librarian is not required.
1. Return to [[The Caretaker (NPC)|The Caretaker]].

## Rewards

- At least 6g
- [[Shimmering Carpet of the Citadel]]
