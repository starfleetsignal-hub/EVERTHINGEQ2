## Steps

1. Kill the following, whom are all found on the expansive third level:
   - 16 [[Category:Efreeti|efreeti]] {{waypoint -180, -368, 310}}
   - [[The Speaker of Fates]] or [[A fatespeaker of Thyr]] {{waypoint 56, -359, 175}}
1. Return to Ognit Eznertob.

- One of the following:
  - [[Ak'Anon Divining Rod]]
  - [[Eznertob's Edge]]
  - [[Flametongue]]
  - [[Jagged Gear Fragment]]
