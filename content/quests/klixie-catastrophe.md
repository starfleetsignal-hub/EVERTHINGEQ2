## Description

"Thekela is worried that the Klixie bixies may have been attacked by the orc pawns. She wants me to go check on the Klixies. The Klixies can be found to the west of Sapling Spur Outpost." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. "I need to check on the Klixie bixies" - found at {{waypoint 76.09, 11.75, -116.90}}
1. "I need to examine the bodies of the Klixie bixies (0/2)" - Click on any of the bodies in the area.
1. "I need to recover [[Broken Klixie hive|Broken Klixie hives]] (0/3)" - Search around the trees for the hives.
1. "I need to deliver the grim news to Theleka" - Return to Theleka at {{waypoint -160, 7, -116}}

## Rewards

- At least 90c
