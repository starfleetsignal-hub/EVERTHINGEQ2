## Steps

1. Use [[Gift Befitting Chief Ry'Gorr]] at {{waypoint -759, -340, 2738}}
1. Speak with [[Chief Ry'Gorr]] at {{waypoint -762, -340, 2736}}
1. Listen to the report of [[Hurg Gutstomp]] who comes after you hail the chief
1. Return to [[Rogla Dainkiller]]
1. Kill [[A Kromzek emissary|Kromzek emissaries]] found just to the west outside the keep, until you get the update
1. Return to [[Rogla Dainkiller]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- +4,550 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
- [[Arctic Cloak of Clan Ry'Gorr]] - All Mages & All Priests
- [[Arctic Wrap of Ry'Gorr Rage]] - All Mages & All Priests
- [[Arctic Ry'Gorr Wrap of Flesh]] - All Fighter & All Scouts
- [[Arctic Vicious Ry'Gorr Cloak]] - All Fighters & All Scouts
