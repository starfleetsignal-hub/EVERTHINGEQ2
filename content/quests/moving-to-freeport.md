## Steps

- Travel to East Freeport and speak to [[Ambassador Brutus]] {{waypoint -125.32, -37.37, 41.92}}.
  - [[Ambassador Brutus]] may show a feather over his head (instead of a book)... just talk to him anyway and you will finish this quest.  then talk to him again to get the next quest (which is required to complete your citizenship transfer).

- Experience Only
