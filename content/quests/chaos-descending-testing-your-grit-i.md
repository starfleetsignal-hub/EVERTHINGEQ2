## Notes

*A new tradeskill mission is available every 18 hours.*

## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 15 [[Celestial Sandpaper]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest the following within [[Vegarlson, the Earthen Badlands]]:
   - 25 [[Dewpalm lumber]] from [[Fallen dewpalm branch]]
   - 10 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Coalesced reef]]
   - 10 [[Flitterfin tang]] from [[School of tangs]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Mallard Decoy]].
1. Craft the [[Mallard Decoy]] on a [[Woodworking Table]].
   - There is a [[Woodworking Table]] in the [[Smiths' Gallery]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]] at {{waypoint 644.81, 411.68, 53.39}}
1. Speak to [[Derillus]] at {{waypoint 302.61, -5.28, -100.69}} in the [[Myrist Main Stacks]] on the first floor.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 66g 32s 48000 status
- [[Elemental Mission Reward Crate]]
