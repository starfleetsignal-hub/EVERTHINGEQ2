## Notes

- After you finish this quest you can go talk to the Glademaster who tells you they don't need the key anymore. You can also go see [[Captain Vulis]] and do [[Give the key to Captain Vulis]].

## Steps

1. Speak with [[A Green Hood Trap Master]]. {{waypoint 209.28, -38.83, 200.42}}
1. Obtain all parts of the orc foundry map by killing orcs in the zone.
1. Return to [[Shadow]] at {{waypoint 554, 0.00, 310}}.
1. Deliver the foundry map to [[The Glademaster]] at {{waypoint 330, -19, -463}}.
1. Kill the following monsters, all found in [[Three Toes Valley]]:
   - 6 [[A Vallon grunt|Vallon grunts]]
   - 2 [[A Vallon loader|Vallon loaders]]
   - 4 [[A Vallon builder|Vallon builders]]
   - [[Gurk Strongarm]] {{waypoint 525, -20, -280}}.
1. Return to [[The Glademaster]] at {{waypoint 335.33, -19.86, -464.21}}.
1. Complete the sub-quest [[Into the Vallon Foundry]]:
   1. Taint the pots:
      - Taint the Smelting Pot of Ikuris in the northwestern tunnel: {{waypoint 396, -119, -159}}
      - Taint the Smelting Pot of Gurkis in the southern tunnel: {{waypoint 361, -125, -43}}
      - Taint the Smelting Pot of Horkul in the eastern tunnel: {{waypoint 245, -122, -120}}
   1. Acquire 4 mining axes from [[A Vallon miner|Vallon miners]]. **NOTE: Must be done before slaying Nixam who has about a 1 hour respawn**
   1. Slay named gnome [[Nixam Dugdiggle]] in [[The Deathfist Quarry]] at {{waypoint 345.93, -22.20, -188.04}}
1. Speak to [[The Glademaster]].
1. Speak to [[Tusk]]. {{waypoint -210, -27, -78}}
1. Inspect the small chest at atop [[Titan's Tower]]. {{waypoint -24, 75, -366}}
1. Find the Deathfist Citadel sewer drains in the moat behind [[The Deathfist Citadel]]:
   - The first is at {{waypoint 86, -44, -753}}
   - The second is at {{waypoint 44, -43, -786}}
