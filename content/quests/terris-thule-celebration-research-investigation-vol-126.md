## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Terris-Thule has asked me to assist in gathering nightmarebound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the nightmarebound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Nightmarebound Energy Reserves|nightmarebound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect [[Nightmarebound Energy Reserves|nightmarebound energy]] by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Dream Scorcher]] {{waypoint -33, -37, 265}} in [[Darklight Wood]]
