## Steps

1. Speak with [[Falleh Missar]] at {{waypoint 1307, -45, 3038}}.
1. Find the mysterious kerran, [[Taliah the Diviner]] at {{waypoint 952, -48, 3069}}.
1. Speak with [[Finglias]] at {{waypoint 1018, -51, 3479}}.
1. Kill [[Hytkysis]], the [[Forlorn Drake Spirit]] near {{waypoint 432, 148, 2983}} or {{waypoint 252, 173, 3051}}.
1. Return to Finglias.
1. Return to Taliah.
