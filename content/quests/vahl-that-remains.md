## Steps

1. Click the [[Purrik's Tale - an Echo Stone]] in your bags to listen to the story entry.
   - *Note: You must be in third person to see dialog updates*
1. Continue listening to Purrik's Tale in **each of the following locations** (target yourself when using the stone):
   1. Leave the city and (Zone into [[Savage Weald]] {{waypoint -190, 41, -415}} ) then move away from the entrance heading NW to about {{waypoint -805, 52, 111}}.
   1. use the [[Purrik's Tale - an Echo Stone]] and listen to it
   1. Find [[Savarah's Paw]] to the south of the fork in the river {{waypoint -681.88, 38.81, -102.51}}.
   1. use the [[Purrik's Tale - an Echo Stone]] and listen to it
   1. Travel northeast to find a grimling camp {{waypoint -853, 54, -305}}.
   1. use the [[Purrik's Tale - an Echo Stone]] and listen to it
   1. Find [[Cadaverist Caves]] filled with grimlings {{waypoint -936, 71, -426}}.
   1. use the [[Purrik's Tale - an Echo Stone]] and listen to it
   1. Travel to the shore near the foot of [[Dreamspinner Falls]] {{waypoint -407, 57, -669}}.
   1. use the [[Purrik's Tale - an Echo Stone]] and listen to it
1. Search for the burial ground {{waypoint -77, 89, -827}}.
1. Near the [[Ruins of Shar Makhdi]], gather the boar tusk carving from Purrik's burial site {{waypoint -78, 89, -827}}![Boar Tusk Carving](images/Boar_Tusk_Carving.jpg).
   - *The tusk is on the big green rock, next to the bowl, you might have to zoom in to see it.*
1. Return to [[Dahnji Hujara|Dahnji]] at [[Celebration House]] in [[City of Shar Vahl]] {{waypoint -274.20, 35.33, 129.85}}.
1. Speak with [[Hymnist Bahaj]] at [[The Jharin Assemblage]] {{waypoint -86, 29, -392}}

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- [[Purrik's Tale]]
