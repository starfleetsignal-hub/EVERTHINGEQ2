## Steps

1. Plant skeleton skitterers at the Blessed Knoll {{waypoint 426, 34, 743}}
1. Find the evidence. Search on the ground for several piles.
1. Return to Destora J'Narus.

  - [[Fathom Plate Spaulders]] (Plate Armor reward)
