## Steps

1. Enter [[The Wailing Caves]]; find and speak to [[Ariana Neovra]] in the jail cells at {{waypoint -66, -26, -9}}
1. Return to Orwen in The Commonlands

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Chilling Kris]] (All Mages)
  - [[Heatwave Shortsword]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Light Knuckles]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Sceptre of the Crustacean]] (All Priests)
  - [[Sharpened Brute's Sword (Level 10)|Sharpened Brute's Sword]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Summoner's Runic Sceptre]] (All Mages)
  - [[Unbalanced Kris]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
