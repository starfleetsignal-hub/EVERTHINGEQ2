## Background

Now that you have cleared the difficult [[Nizara, City of the Nayad]] instance, you get to go play with one of the hardest raid mobs in the game, [[Chel'Drak the Ancient Lord]] in the [[Antechamber of Fate]].

## Steps

1. Go to the entrance of the [[Sepulcher of Zan Fi (POI)|Sepulcher of Zan Fi]] in [[The Mystic Lake]]. {{waypoint -14, -25, -6}}
1. Use the [[Tanaan Portal Device]] from your inventory and enter the portal that spawns. You will enter the [[Antechamber of Fate]].
   - **BEWARE**: You will be attacked the moment you step over the fallen/broken column in the tunnel after you zone in.
1. Kill [[Chel'Drak the Ancient Lord]].
1. Return to [[Zhen Mei]].
   - Don't forget to hail [[Dhae Lao, the Relic Keeper]] on the first floor to receive your full reward!

## Rewards

- Experience
- From [[Dhae Lao, the Relic Keeper|Dhae Lao]]:
  - [[Ring of the Four Winds]]
  - +1500 faction with **The Whistling Fists Clan**
