## Steps

1. Complete the following in no particular order:
   - Obtain the [[Order Book: Blackshield Destroyer Saltspew|Blackshield Orders]] {{waypoint 559.47, 16.00, 481.31}} on a crate in the camp on the west side of the beach south of Chelduk.
   - Kill [[Captain Gorth]] {{waypoint 559, 15, 484}} in the same camp as the Blackshield Orders.
1. Return to [[Chelduk Hir'Vin|Chelduk]]

- At least 3s 62c
