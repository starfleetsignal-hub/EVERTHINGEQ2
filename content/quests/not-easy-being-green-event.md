## Notes

- The zone [[Innovation: Tinkerer's Trial (Solo)|Innovation: Tinkerer's Trial [Solo]]] is in [[Moors of Ykesha]] near the Spire portal at (1,740, 447, 721).
- This quest is identical to [[Blood, Sweat, and Gears]] from the point you zone into the instance.
- Zone into Tinkerer's Trial (Solo) or (Heroic), run the [[Blood, Sweat, and Gears]] script from the first "Talk to The Boegie" instruction.
- Don't expect to see quest feathers, you will receive general instructions of what to do and where to go in your chat window.
- While you can reset and rerun the quest instance for loot many times, you will only be offered this quest every 18 hours.
- All mobs in heroic are Tank & Spank except perhaps last named in heroic that pops an explosion on the furthest group member from him.  Look for Purple target then joust it.

## Rewards

- 5 x [[Mithril Jubilation Medal]] (in 2026)
- At least 3p 59g 25s 12c 107799 status
