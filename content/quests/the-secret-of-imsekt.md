*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. You have 5 minutes to push the following four buttons, in any order:
   - {{waypoint -678, 9, 111}}; on the upper northeast corner platform, on a rampart
   - {{waypoint -563, 4, 147}}; on the western upper platform, rampart at the northern end
   - {{waypoint -710, 4, 257}}; on the eastern upper platform, rampart at the southern end
   - {{waypoint -652, 1, 215}}; on the eastern upper balcony of the central building. Drop down onto this area from the southeast end of the roof. The button is directly behind the market enforcer on the northern end of this small balcony.
1. Inspect the hidden compartment again.

This map shows a path to complete the run in time. Follow these paths in order: White, Green, Blue, Red (switches 2, 3, 1, then 4)<br>
