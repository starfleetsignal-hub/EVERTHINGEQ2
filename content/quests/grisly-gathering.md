## Steps

1. Kill the following in no particular order:
   - 5 [[A ghoul (Sinking Sands)|ghouls]] {{waypoint -1181, -103, -832}}
   - 5 [[A mummy|mummies]] {{waypoint -1204, -125, -684}}; placeholders are [[A lesser mummy|lesser mummies]]
1. Return to [[Aliyyah the Embalmer]].

## Rewards

- At least 3g 10c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Cloak of the Desert Sword]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Cloak of the Dunestalker]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Cloak of the Sandmage]] (All Mages)
  - [[Cloak of the Sun Priest]] (All Priests)
