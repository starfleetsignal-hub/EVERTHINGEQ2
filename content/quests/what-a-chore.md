*This quest is repeatable up to 5 times..*

## Steps

1. Harvest the following in Everfrost:
   - 5 pelts from animal dens
   - 5 roots from wintry roots
   - 5 ore from frigid ore
   - 5 wood from the corroded arbor
1. Return to Inon.

## Rewards

- At least 79s 87c
