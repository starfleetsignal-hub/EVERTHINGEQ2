This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. You will be sent to 1 of the following fae drake nest locations around Greater Faydark, read quest for location.
   - Is the nest's proximity to the sea causing problems? - This is in the Fae starting area (nursery) up a tree root. {{waypoint 15, -15, -501}}
   - Could it be the orcs? - This is in the Crushbone orc area above the cave. {{waypoint -954, 120, 5}}
   - Could it be interference from the ancient spires? - Near Spire Shadow Outpost here {{waypoint -382, 158, 487}} or here {{waypoint -543, 164, 524}} .

When you approach each, you will be attacked by an egg snatcher (a satyr - same level as you, 2 down arrow solo).

1. Return to Gibrien. {{waypoint 594.89, 45.19, 78.08}}

## Rewards

- -3000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- +3000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
- -3000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- Experience
