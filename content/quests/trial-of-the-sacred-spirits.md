## Steps

1. Speak to Koffi in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] near the entrance to the Ant Mound {{waypoint -601.56, 103.26, 43.97}}.
1. Find the glowing green **Rare Mushroom** {{waypoint -357.51, 117.41, -318.03}} and click it to obtain a [[Strange mushroom]].
1. Examine the [[Strange mushroom]] and consume it.
1. Return to Koffi and speak with her.
1. Click the Ant Cave entrance to zone in to the Trial of Sacred Spirits.
1. Speak to Koffi again {{waypoint -441.00, -332.31, -194.47}}. She will become your temporary quest mercenary for the Trials, and can track the Spirits:
   1. Battle the [[Sacred Badger Spirit]] {{waypoint -377.68, -331.64, -179.06}}.
   1. Battle the [[Sacred Stag Spirit]] {{waypoint -480.32, -334.29, -319.62}}.
   1. Battle the [[Sacred Bat Spirit]] {{waypoint -583.11, -320.85, -316.76}}.
   1. Battle the [[Sacred Hawk Spirit]] {{waypoint -205.39, -395.60, -379.61}}.
   1. Battle the [[Sacred Wolf Spirit]] {{waypoint -231.11, -394.35, -551.13}}.
   1. Battle the [[Sacred Crocodile Spirit]] {{waypoint 30.05, -356.82, -558.74}}.
   1. Battle the [[Sacred Tiger Spirit]] {{waypoint 66.59, -353.61, -322.50}}.
1. Speak to Koffi in [[City of Shar Vahl]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 90g 2s 69,440 status
