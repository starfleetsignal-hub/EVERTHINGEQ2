## Steps

![Hypostatic Oil](images/Hypostatic_Oil_pickup.png)![Runes of Obseisance](images/Runes_of_Obseisance_pickup.png)

1. Gather the following
   - A [[Vial of Hypostatic Oil]] in the [[Smiths' Gallery]] (teleporter is at {{waypoint 387, 15, 170}} on the top floor of the main library) or the [[Crafters' Gallery]]. *They are usually just sitting around on the ground.  Not specifically next to any crafting table, just anywhere.*
   - Gather 7 [[Rune of Obeisance|Runes of Obeisance]] in the South Wing Catalog (Top Floor {{waypoint 380, 15, 205}} ) or in the Grand Librarian's Office {{waypoint 430, 15, 241}} *These are randomly distributed around the room, but unlike the one depicted in the screenshot here, they were all black.*
1. Speak with [[Maelin Starpyre]] near the Mural of the Planes, on the ground floor in the large central area at {{waypoint 247, -7, -24}}
1. Place the 7 runes on the mural symbols in the circles in the centre of the Myrist Main Stacks room where you are currently located.
1. Speak with [[Maelin Starpyre]]
