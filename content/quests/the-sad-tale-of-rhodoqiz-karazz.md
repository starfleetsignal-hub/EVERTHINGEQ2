## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks:
   - Gather 3 *lichenclover root*, from [[Root Nodes]] (aerating root cord).
   - Kill 4 [[A kunzar scaleripper|kunzar scalerippers]]. {{waypoint 637, 61, -531}}
   - Obtain 3 venom sacs from [[A venom flow leech|venom flow leeches]]. {{waypoint 637, 61, -531}}
   - Dump the ash pot by swimming in the [[Lake of Dismay]]. {{waypoint 584, 62, -495}}
1. Return to Rhodoqiz Karazz.
