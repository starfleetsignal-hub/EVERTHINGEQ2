- requires 32,500 [[House Vahla (Faction)]]

## Steps

1. Obatin the following![wakingdew walker](images/Wakingdew_walker.jpg)![Gnarlroot Seedling](images/Gnarlroot_Seedlings.jpg)
   - Obtain 12 wakingdew walker stems by killing [[Wakingdew walker|wakingdew walkers]] in Esoterra Gardens {{waypoint -643.68, 29.23, 468.20}}.
   - Gather 8 gnarlroot seedlings from [[Esoterra Gardens]]
     - Purple fern-like plants near the pools under trees.
1. Return to [[Himaduri]] at {{waypoint -108, 576, -716}}.
