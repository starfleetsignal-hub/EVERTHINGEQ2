## Steps

1. Kill 14-16 [[A grotesque mass|grotesque masses]] in [[The Crypt of Betrayal]].  They can be found near {{waypoint 59, 0, -57}}.  They are soloable.  Nine groups can be found in this area.  Respawn time seems to be long, around 15 minutes plus.

## Rewards

- 2133 status @L25
- +150 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
