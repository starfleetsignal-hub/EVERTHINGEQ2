## Steps

1. Harvest 10 aerating roots from cypress trees (branches with white sparkles)
1. Gather 5 ethereal cores from etherwisps.
1. Return to [[Wegadas]], who will provide you with an [[Ethereal Jaggedpine Branch]].
1. Use the cauldron nearby.  You will need to place the roots in 5 times.
1. Return to [[Wegadas]] to complete the quest.

- At least 1p 30g
- one of the following, depending on class

  - [[Viridian Gloves of Autumn]] leather healer

  - [[Emerald Flame Gloves]] (Scouts)
