## Description

![Oarwille standing beside the Whirling-Arm Protoype 16](images/Whirling-Arm_Prototype_16.jpg)
*A really strange gnome named [[Oarwille Wight]], that I met at the Mariner fort in the [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Orcish wastes]], seems to be the co-inventor of some sort of magic-free flying device named the [[Whirling-Arm Prototype 16]]. Oarwille has asked me to try to find his brother [[Wulbir Wight|Wulbir]] that went missing shortly after his maiden flight just a few days ago. Oarwille thinks that his brother was captured by orcs and is probably being held within [[Deathfist Citadel]]. I should gather a few more adventurers and create a party before heading into the menacing orc fort.*

## Steps

1. Find [[Wulbir Wight]] in [[Deathfist Citadel]]. He is in [[The Deathfist Jail]] at {{waypoint -23, -10, 16}}.
1. Kill [[Jailor Qulkor]] or [[Chief Torturer Goortz]] for the keys, located nearby.
   - If you've already killed the Jailor and/or Chief and forgot to talk to Wulbir, you can still get all the updates needed so you don't have to return.
1. Speak with [[Wulbir Wight]] again and acquire the [[A gnomish schematic|gnomish schematic]] plans.
1. Return to Oarwille in Zek.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 97s 30c
- Free use of the [[Whirling-Arm Prototype 16]] to fly from [[Warship Docks]] to just south of the main entrance to Deathfist Citadel {{waypoint 41, -27, -544}}
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Defender's Cape of Enchanted Energy]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Healer's Cape of Enchanted Energy]] (All Priests)
  - [[Medium's Cape of Enchanted Energy]] (All Mages)
  - [[Stalker's Cape of Enchanted Energy]] (All Scouts)
