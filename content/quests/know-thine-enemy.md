## Important Note

Do not kill Greth before Vashyn, even though you will come across him first. You can walk past him if you keep to left hand side of the cave entrance. If you're using a mercenary, it's recommended you tell them to back off (passive mode) until you're further in the cave. If you did kill him first, you must wait at least 90 minutes before you can reset the instance.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Crypt of T'haen]].
1. Kill [[Vashyn T’Reyn]] {{waypoint 58, 17, -77}} to obtain the *Sanguine Writ*.
1. Kill [[Greth Q’Daena]] {{waypoint 50, 0, 23}} to obtain the *Treatise of Night*.
1. Return to [[Aleeshi Monala]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 59s 57c
