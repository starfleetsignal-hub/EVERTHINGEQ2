## Steps

1. Obtain 5 'superior' brute shag from [[A crystal whisperer|crystal whisperers]] or [[A crystal tender|tenders]]. {{waypoint -1062, -22, 137}}
1. Return to [[Merchant Paxi]].

## Rewards

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- [[Glowing Gem of Striking]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Clear Orb of the Courageous Victor]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Cursed Effigy of Necromancy]] (All Mages)
  - [[Orb of Goblin Culling]] (All Priests)
  - [[Orb of Undead Control]] (All Scouts)
- +5000 faction with **Riliss**
