## Steps

1. Kill a number of mobs inside the temple. Possible targets include:
   - 10 [[A Thulian bloodcaller|Thulian bloodcallers]]
   - 5 [[A Thulian frightfiend|Thulian frightfiends]]
   - 10 [[A Thulian lifestealer|Thulian lifestealers]]
   - 10 [[A Thulian torturer|Thulian torturers]]
1. Return to Zealot Fahid.
