## Steps

1. Travel to [[Kylong Plains]] and use the [[Catalogue Creature]] ability to catalog the following:
   - Catalog [[A tundra beast]] around {{waypoint 1298.80, 422.38, -543.75}}
   - Catalog [[A tatterback]] around {{waypoint 1280, 166, -1891}}
   - Catalog [[A flesh feeder]] around {{waypoint 1212, 101, -1696}}
   - Catalog [[A flesh fiend]] around {{waypoint 1156.91, -16.54, -2056.31}}
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]

## Hints

The creatures are trackable. If you don't have tracking, many ranger mercs can track, for instance the [[Villax Sned]] merc from the VoV expansion.

## Rewards

- Unlock the Days of Summer 2022: Week 2 Holiday event rewards that you can purchase from [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2293, -199, 2506}}
