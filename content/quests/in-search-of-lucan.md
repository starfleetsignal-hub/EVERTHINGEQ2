## Steps

1. Speak to [[Dalar R'taan]] in [[The Commonlands]] near the Ulteran Spires {{waypoint -28, -48, -408}}.
1. Speak to [[Al'leed Velgho]] in [[The City of Paineel|Paineel]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]] {{waypoint 1981, -298, 3375}}.
1. Gather the following items from [[The Hole]] (Public Instance version) {{waypoint 1888, -375, 3367}} necessary to locate Lucan:
   - A harmonic crystal shard, from [[Nomh the Nomadic Guardian]] {{waypoint -189, 65, -811}} in the water at [[The Tower of Serilis]] ([[Guardians of the Underfoot (Faction)|Guardians of the Underfoot]] side). Has 60 minute respawn time.
   - A seal of scrying, from [[Caertax the Deceiver]] in [[Old Paineel]] ([[Caertaxian Legion (Faction)|Caertaxian Legion]] side).{{waypoint -621, -2, -55}}
   - A binding rune, from the [[Vengeful Seal-Watcher]] in [[Dartain's Fortress]] ([[Defenders of the Seal (Faction)|Defenders of the Seal]] side), around {{waypoint 661, 126, -221}}. Clear some fallen warlocks to spawn it (8-10 up usually. If you kill them all and it doesn't spawn, wait a while for respawn (or go kill another named) and kill some more, and it will pop). Mind you can easily miss this guy as he is running circles around the graveyard place on the tombs' tops, above your head, out of sight depending where you are standing.
1. Return to [[Al'leed Velgo]] in [[The City of Paineel|Paineel]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]] {{waypoint 1981, -298, 3375}}.
1. Speak to [[Fyr'remd Lorak]]
1. Speak to Al`leed again

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g 16213 status
