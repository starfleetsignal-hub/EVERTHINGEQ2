## Steps

1. Talk to [[Mav Boilfist]] near the dock in[[Graystone Yard]]
1. Collect 3 items. Note that if you will get a msg saying you need to search other barrels, theym will be next to the inspected one.
   - **Frozen Cherries**, found in A Shipping Barrel behind the the scribe's shop, 813.84, -19.73, -90.86
   - **Yeast**, found in A Shipping Barrel near the most north eastern building, 831,04, -23.33, -148,62.
   - **Honey**, found in A shipping Barrel at the eastern corner of the bank platform against the wall, 930.81, -20.82, -101.62
1. Return to [[Mav Boilfist]]

## Rewards

- At least 1s 24c
