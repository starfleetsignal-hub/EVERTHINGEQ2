This is part 1 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Qeynos]] and [[Kelethin]] who wear Chain and Plate Armor (with the exception of [[Shaman|shaman]] types).

- If you are from [[Freeport]], then your version of the quest is called [[Custom Fitted Armor - Part I]].

## Steps

1. You must collect the following items:
   - 4 Cards from [[A giant kodiak|giant kodiaks]] in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint -2101, -20, -824}} or {{waypoint -1759, -9, -614}}. (You might need to kill a few wolves in order to get more Kodiaks appear)
     - Found from [[Windstalker Highlands]] to [[Keep of the Gnollslayers]].
   - 20 Sheets of Paper from [[A Caltorsis knight|Caltorsis knights]] in [[Antonica]].
     - Found near [[The Ruins of Caltorsis]] and [[Fangbreaker Keep]].
1. Return to [[Ian Cathlan]] to receive your reward.
