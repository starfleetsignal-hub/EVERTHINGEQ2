## Steps

1. Obtain 5 crococile hides. Any crocodile will provide an update, even hatchlings. You can find crocodiles along the beach, in the caves off of the beach, and also on the sea floor.
1. Return to Ghassan.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Band of Tunnel's Light]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Loop of Tunnel's Light]]
  - [[Ring of Tunnel's Light]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Ringlet of Tunnel's Light]]
