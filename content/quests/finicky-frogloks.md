## Steps

1. Go to [[Omen's Call]] and pick up a peanut butter and jelly sandwich, an apple, a peanut butter cookie, and a blueberry pie. They are located inside the tavern:
   - {{waypoint 223, -60, -285}}
   - {{waypoint 197, -59, -284}}
   - {{waypoint 205, -59, -266}}
   - {{waypoint 218, -59, -282}}
1. Take the food items back to [[Jerome]].

## Rewards

- Proficiency in the [[Froak]] language.
- [[Shorty's Goblin Doll]] - House Item
- At least 22g 55s
- After you turn the quest in, [[Shorty (Fens of Nathsar)|Shorty]] will follow you around for a while and say various things.
