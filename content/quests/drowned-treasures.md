## Steps

1. Examine the underwater tomb at {{waypoint 1818, -239, -707}}.
1. Kill [[A desert spirit]] (59 solo) that spawns. More than one may spawn, but you only need to kill one for the update.
1. Return to [[Siraj al Din]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Weapon Belt of the Burning Arrow]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Weapon Belt of the Fireball]] ([[Wizard]])
  - [[Weapon Belt of the Flaming Blade]] (???)
  - [[Weapon Belt of the Firesoul]] ([[Inquisitor]])
