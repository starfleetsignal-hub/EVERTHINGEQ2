## Steps

1. Collect tearweed sprouts in the swamp
   - White sparkly effect on the ground.  Set quality to at least balanced to spot them more easily.
   - {{waypoint -211, -745, 69}}
   - {{waypoint -263, -745, 161}}
   - {{waypoint -155, -745, -261}}
   - {{waypoint -166, -745, 34}}
   - {{waypoint -283, -745, 103}}
   - {{waypoint -386, -745, -268}}
   - {{waypoint -165, -745, 165}}
1. Return to Researcher Wedinn; he will give tearweed spray (inventory item)
1. Use the spray on 6 bugs (teralid hive guardians) in [[Hive of the Rogue]] area in the north-west (marked on map). You can stand in place within range of one teralid and click it repeatedly with the vial and finish the quest quickly.
1. Return to Wedinn

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
