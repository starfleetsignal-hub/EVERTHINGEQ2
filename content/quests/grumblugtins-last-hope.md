## Steps

1. Enter the instance, [[Svarni Expanse: Carrion Crag (Solo)|Svarni Expanse: Carrion Crag [Solo]]] {{waypoint -529, 91, -528}} (the bone cross at the beginning of the path) and search for the captive pygmy.
1. Progress through the zone, past all but the last named mob, to {{waypoint -362, 183, -616}}. You will hear **a Grumblugtin pygmy** say "Help! Help needed!"
1. As you approach {{waypoint -360, 182, -647}}, [[Clawra]] (127^^^), a harpy will spawn.  Kill **Clawra** to update the quest.
1. Return to [[Dahmodar]]
