## Steps

1. Travel to [[Kunzar Jungle]] and use the [[Catalogue Creature]] ability to catalog:
   - Catalog [[A Vespid hornet]] near the [[City of Jinisk]] around {{waypoint 880, 56, -98}}. This is at evac point.
   - Catalog [[A jade construct]] around the [[The City of Mist|City of Mist]] {{waypoint -116, 152, -918}}
   - Catalog [[An iron construct]] around the [[The City of Mist|City of Mist]] {{waypoint -159, 148, -981}}
   - Catalog [[A Rubble-rabble detector]] around {{waypoint -55, -27, -67}}
   - Catalog [[A Rubble-rabble burrower]] around {{waypoint 34, -37, -126}}
     - Recommended order : both Rubble-rabble are right under the Druid Ring (Fast Travel destination), start with them then evac (or use the Druid Ring sokokar to Jinisk) for the hornet at zone entrance, then take the sokokar in City of Jinisk to Hidden Plunderers (City of Mist).
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]

## Hints

The creatures are trackable.  If you don't have tracking, many ranger mercs can track, for instance [[Villax Sned]] from the VoV expansion can track.
