## Notes

- *This quest is also part of the [[Book Quests Timeline]].*
- Some of the required mobs share spawns with each other in addition to being rare, specifically, [[A lancer wasp|lancer wasps]] and [[A thicket deer|thicket deer]]. See their respective monster pages for more info.
- Make sure when you read the book while in the [[Enchanted Lands]] or the quest will not update.
