## Steps

1. Kill the first three named encounters in [[The Palace of Ferzhul]]. {{waypoint -1773, -967, 2667}} This will spawn the NPCs in the next step.  A partially cleared instance will also spawn the NPCs.
1. Return to Transcension Hall, and complete the dialogue with the NPC Bayle to get a quest update.
1. Kill [[Valdoartus Varsoon]] (see here for an explanation of the fight).
1. Return to Transcension Hall once more for an auto update as you approach the center of the room.
1. Thereafter you have to talk once again to the Professor in Firmroot.
1. The professor will send you to [[Antonia Bayle]] or [[Lucan D'Lere]] to receive a FABLED Reward as listed below as well as a choice of one of two cloaks
