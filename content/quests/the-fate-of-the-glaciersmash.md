## Steps

- You receive a [[Gnomish Pirate Rebreather]]
1. Locate the sunken wreckage at {{waypoint 1292.50, -627.96, 222.27}}
1. Find the supply chest at {{waypoint 1293, -627, 224}}
1. Defeat [[A Monstrous Beasty]] within 5 minutes
1. Plunder the supply chest
1. Return to Captain Bin

## Notes

If you use a waypoint to the supply chest, the correct location will be marked on the map but the gold "wisp" will not indicate the correct location. The supply chest is found on the ocean floor under the wreckage of the Glaciersmash's deck. Watch out for the [[A lurking shell-cracker|lurking-shell crackers]] which swim in the area.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
