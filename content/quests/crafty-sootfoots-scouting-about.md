## Notes

- This quest is also part of the [[Lavastorm Timeline]].
- Completion of the previous quest, [[Beaks Away!]], is not necessary to pick up this quest. However it is its natural predecessor in terms of level.
- To complete the Sootfoot Crafting Timeline, you will need the following:
  - A tradeskill level 40 or higher
  - 3 [[Ashen root]]
  - 3 [[Diamondine cluster]]
  - 5 [[Fulginate cluster]]
  - 5 [[Strengthened leather pelt]]
  - 6 [[Luminous filament]]
  - 6 [[Luminous coal]]
  - Bring a portable sewing station or go to Mara to create the Item when needed.

## Steps

1. [[Catalogue Creature|Catalogue]] the following monsters: {{waypoint -231, -130, 313}}
   - [[A Sootfoot gatherer]]
   - [[A Sootfoot harvester]]
   - [[A Sootfoot rockpicker]]
   - [[A Sootfoot whelp]]
   - [[A Sootfoot warrior]]
   - [[A Sootfoot peon]]
   - [[A Sootfoot laborer]]
   - [[Smakametta]] {{waypoint -305, -130, 421}}
1. Return to [[Purrla]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 6g 17s 96c
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
