## Steps

1. Obtain notes and messages from [[Sirran's Memorial]] on [[Strungstone Peaks]]
   - *Note: Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint -682.82, 144.92, -5.32}} to [[Strungstone Peaks]] and find [[Sirran's Memorial]] at {{waypoint 107.36, 379.14, 663.42}}.![Strungstone Peaks memorial](images/Strungstone_Peaks_memorial.jpg)
   - *Note: Mouseover the notes and messages, right-click and Examine the three notes to update.*
   1. examine Sirran's burial mound for a clue {{waypoint 108.12, 379.47, 663.85}}
   1. examine Sirran's burial mound for another clue {{waypoint 105.33, 378.78, 663.34}}
   1. examine Sirran's burial mound for a third clue {{waypoint 105.90, 378.69, 663.50}}
1. Obtain the following
   - Acquire a bird whistle
     - Purchase a [[Bird Whistle]] from [[Rannaahk]] for 12c a Merchant on [[Isles of Wisdom]] at {{waypoint -838.20, 188.75, 337.89}}.
   - Gather 10 miniature Swords at [[Isles of Wisdom]].
     - *Note: Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint 215.08, 379.31, 652.09}} to [[Isles of Wisdom]]*
     - ![Miniature Sword](images/Miniature_Swords.jpg)Gather 10 Miniature Swords, many are near the area of cloud portal. (group-wide update) Possible locations are:
       - {{waypoint -690.73, 143.89, 19.65}}, {{waypoint -718.44, 141.29, 53.27}}
       - {{waypoint -692.07, 138.05, 55.06}}, {{waypoint -671.71, 139.69, 65.83}}
       - {{waypoint -783.49, 143.42, 0.59}}, {{waypoint -797.98, 144.62, -0.76}}
       - {{waypoint -828.46, 143.40, 41.16}}, {{waypoint -842.36, 143.54, 9.40}}
       - {{waypoint -858.32, 142.60, 2.90}}, {{waypoint -865.46, 142.53, -4.04}}
   - Obtain iridescent scales from [[Yazdani Aurai]] on [[Zenith Helionae]].
     - *Note: Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint 215.08, 379.31, 652.09}} to [[Zenith Helionae]].*
     - Kill [[Yazdani Aurai]] for iridescent scales. Possibly in the area of {{waypoint 169.88, 158.42, 23.92}}.
1. Leave (place) the "collection of items" on [[Sirran's Memorial]] on [[Strungstone Peaks]] at {{waypoint 107.36, 379.14, 663.42}}
   - *Note:Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint -682.82, 144.92, -5.32}} to [[Strungstone Peaks]]*
1. Speak with [[Sirran the Lunatic|Sirran the Lunatic's]] ghost
1. obtain the following
   - Obtain a damask cloak for [[Sirran the Lunatic|Sirran the Lunatic's]] ghost from the [[Auric Palisades]]
     - *Note: Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint 215.08, 379.31, 652.09}} to [[Isles of Wisdom]] then to [[Auric Palisades]]*
     - ![Zakir Rish Chest](images/Zakir_Rish_Chest.jpg)From Zakir Rish Chests take a Damask Coat. (group-wide update) Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -671.18, 130.71, -417.49}}, {{waypoint -659.85, 133.30, -384.91}}
     - {{waypoint -481, 96, -551}}, {{waypoint -527, 165, -583}}
   - Obtain little cakes for [[Sirran the Lunatic|Sirran the Lunatic's]] ghost
     - *Note: Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint -705.63, 145.20, -364.45}} to [[Isles of Wisdom]]*
     1. Purchase a [[Candied Fairy-Leaf Cake (Recipe Book)|Candied Fairy-Leaf Cake]] recipe from [[Rannaahk]] a Merchant on [[Isles of Wisdom]] at {{waypoint -834, 188, 339}} and scribe it. (You need to be at least lvl 10 artisan to scribe this recipe)
        1. Ingredients required:
           - ![Fairy Leaf](images/Fairy_Leaf.jpg)Gather 12 [[Fairy-Leaf]] *(Track Harvestables works)*. Possible locations are:
             - {{waypoint -515.90, 150.57, 307.98}}, {{waypoint -512.49, 145.37, 266.21}}, {{waypoint -495.96, 145.82, 254.75}}, {{waypoint -586.58, 140.15, 255.14}}
           - 12 some common harvested roots e.g. Bittersweet Vineroot for crafting levels 130 - 139
           - 8 some common harvested crops e.g. Breadfruit for crafting level 130 - 139.
           - 200 [[Celestial Coal]] *Fuel merchant in [[Sanctuary (POI)|Sanctuary]], purchase from Broker, etc.*
     1. ![an avian oven](images/An_avian_oven.jpg)Craft 4 [[Candied Fairy-Leaf Cake (Quest Item)|Candied Fairy-Leaf Cakes]] at the Oven located in [[Ecihoolo]]'s hut at {{waypoint -765, 192, 227}}.
   - CAUTION: Mass crafting the cakes will only provide 1 update per combine.
1. Return to [[Sirran the Lunatic|Sirran the Lunatic's]] ghost on [[Strungstone Peaks]] at {{waypoint 107.33, 377.93, 665.94}}
   - *Note: Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint -682.82, 144.92, -5.32}} to [[Strungstone Peaks]]*
