## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Tarew Marr has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items.

1. I need to collect the reagents from merchants.
   - Purchase the [[Aqua Essence Extract|aqua essence extract]] from Tordo 500p 5000 status
     - in [[The Mystic Lake]] right click a guard to find NPC, Tordo wanders the pirate area by zone in.
   - Purchase the [[Arcane Infused Conch|arcane infused conch]] from Oliska 500p 5000 status
     - if you are good-aligned, go to [[Qeynos]] Province District and right click a guard to find NPC, she's at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
     - if you are evil-aligned, go to [[Freeport]] and right click a guard to find NPC, she's at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - Purchase the [[Vial of Fresh Kraken Blood|vial of fresh kraken blood]] from Laparnuu 500p 5000 status
     - in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] right click a guard to find NPC, Laparnuu wanders the path through [[The Roost Warrens]] from {{waypoint 32.37, 236.31, -131.20}} to the [[Petalcrown Perch]] at {{waypoint 338, 238, 7}}.
1. return to [[Herald of the Fathom Lord]] {{waypoint -275.14, -0.13, 51.21}} in [[The Mystic Lake]]
