## Description

Lieutenant Kresic has commissioned me to Slay the Elite of the Drogans. I am to kill the Drogan Warlord, Mason, Construction Chief, and the Spiritualist.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Nu'roga]] and eliminate the Drogan Elite
   - Slay the Drogan warlord
   - Slay the Drogan mason
   - Slay the Drogan construction chief
   - Slay the Drogan spiritualist

- At least 1p 60g
