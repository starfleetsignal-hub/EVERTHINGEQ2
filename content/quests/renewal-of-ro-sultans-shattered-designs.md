## Steps

1. Return to [[Raj'Durabad]] in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]] (RoR starter zone) {{waypoint 70.33, 86.75, -365.06}}
1. Enter [[Sultan's Mahallah: Shattered Designs]] {{waypoint -304.06, 117.48, -135.12}} (entrance is the giant tent in overland)
1. Confront [[Aldys, Sultan of the Daggers]]
   - Defeat [[Zizzez]].
   - Subdue [[Silrena]].
1. Destroy the **Sultan's Mirror** {{waypoint -305.07, 117.54, -110.19}}
1. Reclaim **the crystal arca** {{waypoint -320.94, 117.54, -124.99}}
1. Speak with [[Nairion Eldi]] at [[Hopewell Harbor]] {{waypoint 176.75, -60.15, 459.95}}

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 57791 status
- [[Ascension Boost: 25]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Hizite Gem of Nurturing]]
  - [[Hizite Charm of Blooming]]
  - [[Hizite Icon of Blooming]]
- unlocks the achievement [[Renewal of Ro (Achievement)]]
