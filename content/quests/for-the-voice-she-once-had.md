## Steps

1. Find 3 lost objects in [[The Stratafold]]:
   - {{waypoint 585, 45, 494}}
   - {{waypoint 586, 41, 511}}
   - {{waypoint 515, 33, 493}}
1. Return to [[Nejevani]]
1. Find [[Elhena]] in the [[Myhst Valley]] at {{waypoint -352, 12, 427}} and kill the agitated rivereels attacking her. Talk to her.
1. Speak to [[Daysinte]] at {{waypoint -673, -48, 62}}
1. Find the focus in the water {{waypoint -742, -55, 144}}
1. Return to Daysinte
1. Go to [[Deina]] at {{waypoint -956, 47, 111}}
1. Speak with [[Ohlnitan]] at  {{waypoint -958, 45, 104}}
1. Find a small cactus near {{waypoint -651, 83, -76}}
1. Speak to Ohlnitan
1. Speak to Deina
1. Return to Nejevani "on a cliff south-east of the Stratafold" i.e. at her original location.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 93g 34s 70c
- [[Blessed Gnomophone]] (house item)
