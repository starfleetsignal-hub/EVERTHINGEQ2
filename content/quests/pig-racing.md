## Steps

1. Purchase a wager slip for 72s from [[Racemaster Swinson]] near [[The Lower Fields]].
   - There are 8 pigs that race and you are able to buy multiple wager slips for different pigs.
   - The races start every 2 or 3 minutes automatically.
   - If you are holding a wager slip for a pig that wins the race you automatically receive your reward.

## Rewards

- 3g 50s for picking the winning pig.
