## Steps

1. Mine a rare stone from [[Gemstone Nodes#T3|wind swept stones]] nodes in [[The Thundering Steppes]]. This is random and may take a few minutes or over an hour.
1. Kill 15 [[A corpse feeder|corpse feeder]]s (level 22). These are found sparsely all along the [[Dead River Basin]] from [[Cove of Decay Entrance]] to the docks.
1. Kill 15 undead of any type. The weakest are zombies and skeletons in the riverbed near the dock (level 21).
1. Kill 20 [[A weald wolf|weald wolves]] (level 22-26).  Find them roaming the wooded roadways from [[Qeynos Tower 3]] to [[Qeynos Tower 5]], and near the western shore, by the Watcher in the Fields and the North Bandit Camp.
1. Visit [[The Valley of the Ancients]]. {{waypoint 700, 0, 620}}
1. Slay 20 gnolls of any type. The weakest are found in the craters of the [[Fallow Farmlands]] (level 20).
1. Return to the Watcher of the Fields for your reward.

## Rewards

One of the following, based on class:

- [[Steppes Defender's Chain]] - (All Fighters)
- [[Steppes Medium's Chain]] - (All Mages)
- [[Steppes Healer's Chain]] - (All Priests)
- [[Steppes Stalker's Chain]]
