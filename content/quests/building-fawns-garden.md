## Steps

1. To help Fawn start her garden, collect:
   - diatomaceous sand by killing five [[Sand covered crab]]s in [[Antonica]]. The sand crabs are on the southern border of antonica on a beach {{waypoint -561, -37, 820}}.
   - fish eggs from five [[Coldwind barracuda]] in the south western corner of the map. There is a sea near the harbor around {{waypoint 216, -42, 788}}. Kill the Barracudas in that area.
   - seeds from the fur of five mammals in Antonica, these include bears, deer, wolves, etc . A good spot is around {{waypoint 247, -14, 284}}, directly west of the Southern Qeynos Tower.
1. Return to [[Fawn Starstone]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 4s 21c
- +65 faction with **The Tunarian Alliance**
- One of the following:
  - [[Coldwind Coast forged hammer]]
  - [[Coldwind Coast Katar]]
  - [[Coldwind tentacle whip]]
