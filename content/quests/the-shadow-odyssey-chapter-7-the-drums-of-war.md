## Steps

**THIS IS A TIMED QUEST**

1. Note: As of 01/27/2014, time limit did not matter. Still got updates and was able to turn in to complete quest.
1. Within 30 minutes you have to kill:
   - If you are good aligned kill 12 groups of [[A Brokenskull defender|Brokenskull defenders]]
   - If you are evil aligned kill 10 groups of [[A Brokenskull defender|Brokenskull defenders]]
     - Each group of defenders spawns after about 4 minutes. (On 3/13/2021, I experienced respawn times of over 20 mins)
     - These are located at {{waypoint 48, -79, 235}} , {{waypoint -136, -63, 127}} , {{waypoint -211, -86, -125}} and {{waypoint -112, -87, -51}} near the [[Anchor of Bazzul]]
     - There are also numerous groups in the Bottom Part of Moors of Ykesha, in the fields around [[Gunthak's Shanty]].
     - When you approach the groups, they will respawn and be in combat.  You MUST wait until they finish until you can pull them and kill them.
1. Return to [[Professor Fondfate]] in [[Firmroot Moot]].
1. (Evil Characters Only) Return to the Overlord, [[The Overlord's Office]]. Zone in at {{waypoint -5, -20, 24}}
