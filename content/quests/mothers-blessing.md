## Steps

You'll need to use a flying mount and travel to [[Veeshenna Tol]], the large island directly south of Daarspire.

1. Speak to [[Harla Dar (NPC)|Harla Dar]]  {{waypoint -101, 51, 730}}
1. Speak to [[Devotee of Veeshan]] {{waypoint -81, 49, 771}}
1. Investigate the Wurm Mother's altar {{waypoint -37, 53, 790}} (left click it)
1. Gather 5 misia blooms - they're purple flowered shrubs with gold sparkles, and seem to grow up against the cliffs around {{waypoint -173, 106, 679}}![Misia Bloom](images/Misia_Bloom.jpg)
1. Place the misia blooms on the altar as an offering
1. Speak with Harla Dar
1. Return to Lepethida on Daarspire
