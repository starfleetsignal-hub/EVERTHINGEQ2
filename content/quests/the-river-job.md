## Notes

- requires at least 39,000 [[Pride Pakiat (Faction)]]

## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[Fishing stonestalker]] by the [[Myhst River]] near {{waypoint -232, 16, 219}}.
1. Kill 8 [[Myhst river eel]].
1. Put the items in the Stuffs Storage at {{waypoint -932, 80, -35}}.![Stuffs Storage barrel](images/Stuffs_Storage_barrel.jpg)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -850 faction with **House Vahla**
- -850 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +850 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
- (+1250 faction with **Pride Pakiat**, during repeat cycle to 50k )
