## Steps

1. Travel to [[Murkmold Swale]] at {{waypoint 530.05, 26.62, 433.76}} and find the dwarven skeleton at {{waypoint 432.38, 34.17, 480.62}}.
1. Inspect the dwarven skeleton at {{waypoint 432.38, 34.17, 480.62}}. ![a Dwarven Skeleton](images/A_Dwarven_Skeleton.jpg)
1. Return to [[Gaedra Rugon]] at {{waypoint 626.32, 94.57, -419.61}}.
1. Speak to [[Kanthek Ogrebane]] at {{waypoint 629.29, 94.59, -418.04}}.
1. Defeat 10 [[Lightcrawlers]] for the carapaces near  {{waypoint 564, 48, -268}}.
1. Return to [[Gaedra Rugon]] at {{waypoint 626.32, 94.57, -419.61}}.

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Forged Lightcrawler Circlet]]
