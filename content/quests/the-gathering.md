## Prerequisites

- You must be at least adventure level 65.

## Steps

1. Speak with the students of Quellious from the previous quests:
   - [[The Great Galabo]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]. {{waypoint -237, 161, 383}}
   - [[Sister Sam]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]]. {{waypoint -688, 130, 940}}
   - [[Ootani]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]]. {{waypoint 797, 110, -330}}
1. Purchase the following items from vendors in [[Qeynos]]:
1. *a peaceful pie* from [[Kaldin Deeppockets]] in [[Qeynos Province District]]. {{waypoint 805, -20, -524}}
   - *floor mats* from [[Khai Lightfall]] in [[Qeynos Province District]]. {{waypoint 769, -19, -375}}
   - *a calming candle* from [[Sneed Galliway]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 289, -18, -75}}
1. Enter the instance, [[The Gathering (Instance)|The Gathering]], in [[The Lesser Faydark]]. {{waypoint 970, 142, -472}}
1. Wait for the students NPCs to enter the cave and walk to the meditation mats one by one. After all three have entered and sat down, a spider will spawn. **WAIT** until Galabo reacts to the spider, then right click and "shoo" it. If you were patient and did not shoo the spider too soon, you will receive a notification 'You hear someone entering the cave' and [[Pacificator Merrek]] will soon join the group. If you were impatient and did not wait for Galabo to react, Merrek will not enter and you will have to zone out and start over.
1. Listen to the dialogue between Pacificator Merrek and the students.
1. Witness the presence of [[Avatar of Tranquility (The Gathering)|Quellious]].

## Rewards

- Item Reward: [[Cloak of Tranquility]]
- +10,000 faction with **Quellious**
