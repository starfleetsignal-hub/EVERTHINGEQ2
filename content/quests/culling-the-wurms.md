## Note

You must be level 87 to get this quest.

## Steps

1. Kill Wurms in the [[Great Divide]] near {{waypoint -203, -280, -86}}
1. Return to [[Almil Brokenaxe]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Pulsing Adornment of Strength]] (Fighter)
  - [[Pulsing Adornment of Intelligence]] (Mage)
  - [[Pulsing Adornment of Wisdom]] (Priest)
  - [[Pulsing Adornment of Agility]] (Scout)
