## Steps

1. Kill [[Ithari K'Xyrae]]
   - Ithari can be found wandering around in the mines on the east side of the town {{waypoint -509, -53, 370}}. He is a very fast respawn.
1. Take her head to Mayor [[Sselnyl Do'Zyth]]

## Rewards

- At least 65c
- One of the following:
  - [[Blooddrop Loop]] (Dirge)
  - [[Heavy Plated Shield]]
  - [[Faded Wraps]] - (Bruiser, Monk)
  - [[Morning Dew Trinket]] (Dirge)
