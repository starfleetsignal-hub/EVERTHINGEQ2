## Steps

1. Search for ayriegales:
   - 2 in [[The Stratafold]] {{waypoint 571, 66, 455}}
   - 3 in [[Esoterra Gardens]] {{waypoint -523, 22, 535}}
   - 3 in [[Pakiat Bluffs]] {{waypoint -933, 55, 55}} on the green tree branches
1. Speak to [[Khiturfu]], a sphinx, at {{waypoint -602, 131, -830}}
1. Search Khiturfu's Reliquary {{waypoint -581, 131, -820}}
1. Use the harp string on 3 [[Chiming ignus]] in the Amphitheater of Song {{waypoint 436, -153, 976}}
1. Fly up, just above Jansell, and click an aryriegale among the floating tree
1. Return to Jansell
