## Steps

1. Slay 4 [[A withertalon alpha|withertalon alphas 92^]].  Although the game refference the caves north of the Sanctuary of Waning (cave entrance at {{waypoint -2233, -593, -595}}), there is a ledge at {{waypoint -2282, -592, -393}} that is easier to reach and will also have withertalon alphas. when taking this route you will see a cave entrance, enter cave and follow the path to a "Y", take the right pathway which leads you to ALPHAs.
1. Return to [[Vadicus Entiri]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- [[Ancient Withering Symbol]] (All mages)
