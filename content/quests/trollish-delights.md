## Steps

1. Slay four [[A Big Bend rat|Big Bend rats]] near the [[The Sprawl]] gate.
1. Cook the rats by right-clicking on [[The Torch of Oggok]] located in the center of Big Bend {{waypoint -6, 0, 7}}.
1. Deliver the cooked rats to Braz.

## Rewards

- At least 1s
- One of the following:
  - [[Braz's chain wristguard]]
  - [[Braz's cloth cuff]]
  - [[Braz's leather bracer]]
  - [[Braz's plate bracer]]
