## Steps

1. Gather 15 **purple thyme**, located in [[Tunare's Grove]] around {{waypoint -312, -58, -250}}.
1. Return to [[Tuck Longtoes]].

  - [[Glyphed Crusader's Blade]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Razor-Tipped Axe Blade]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Spiked Faydark hammer]] (All Priests)
  - [[Thexian Steel Aegis]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Thexian Steel Motif]] (All Mages)
  - [[Thexian Steel Guard]] (All Priests)
  - [[Thexian Steel Symbol]] (All Priests)
