## Steps

Kill the following enemies

- Kill Kodan : Kill [[Kodan]] in [[The Icy Dig]]
- Eliminate Efreeti Lord Djarn : Kill [[Efreeti Lord Djarn]] in [[The Bastion of Flames]]
- Defeat Banetooth Splitclaw : Kill [[Banetooth Splitclaw]] in [[The Drowned Caverns: Arena of Heroes]]

or

- Depose Queen Zynixia : Kill [[Queen Zynixia]] in [[The Tower of the Drafling]]
- Dispatch Noble Dojorn : Kill [[Noble Dojorn]] in [[The Sanctum of Fire]]
- Slaughter Remia Starshifter : Kill [[Remia Starshifter]] in [[The Crypt of T'haen]]

or

- Execute Lord Everling : Kill [[Lord Everling]] in [[Nektropos Castle: The Return]]
- Banish Lava Born : Kill [[Lava Born]] in [[Maiden's Gulch]]
- Slay Rhayn T'Dax : Kill [[Rhayn T'Dax]] in [[The Tombs of Night]] or [[The Tombs of Night: Retribution]]
