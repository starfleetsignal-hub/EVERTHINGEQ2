## Steps

- Craft 2 each of any random 6 of following items
  - [[Exceptional Essence of Clarity]]
  - [[Exceptional Freedom of Action]]
  - [[Exceptional Essence of Health]]
  - [[Exceptional Essence of Power]]
  - [[Exceptional Essence of Regeneration]]
  - [[Exceptional Freedom of Mind]]
  - [[Exceptional Elixir of Constitution]]
  - [[Exceptional Elixir of Thorns]]
  - [[Exceptional Elixir of Transcendence]]

- Craft 2 each of any random 6 of the following items
  - [[Destructive Rage IV|Destructive Rage IV (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Gut Kick V|Gut Kick V (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Roundhouse V|Roundhouse V (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Roundhouse Kick V|Roundhouse Kick V (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Soulrend V|Soulrend V (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Knee Break V|Knee Break V (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Unholy Strength IV|Unholy Strength IV (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Crusade IV|Crusade IV (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Penitent Kick V|Penitent Kick V (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Lay on Hands IV|Lay on Hands IV (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Harm Touch IV|Harm Touch IV (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Sentinel IV|Sentinel IV (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Assault V|Assault V (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Smoldering Fists V|Smoldering Fists V (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Assault V|Assault V (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Bloodbath V|Bloodbath V (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Crescent Strike V|Crescent Strike V (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Death Cloud V|Death Cloud V (Journeyman)]]
  - [[Holy Circle V|Holy Circle V (Journeyman)]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Reward

- At least 2g 46s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
