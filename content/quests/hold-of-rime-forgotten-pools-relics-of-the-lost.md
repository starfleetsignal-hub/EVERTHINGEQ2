## Description

I've been asked by Bergna Veliumsmiter to make my way into the depths of the Forgotten Pools and recover what relics I can that may have once belonged to the Coldain.

## Steps

- Find 5 Coldain Relics in [[Forgotten Pools]]
  - Locations where Coldain Relics have been found:
    - {{waypoint 4.5, -8, -32.5}}
    - {{waypoint -45, 2, -176}}
    - {{waypoint 100, 22, -375}}
    - {{waypoint -39, 42, -487}}
    - {{waypoint -141.16, 0, -391.79}}
    - {{waypoint -51.65, 40.81, -424.84}}
    - {{waypoint -5.13, 40.28, -572.98}}
- Defeat 2 Petrified Ancients in [[Forgotten Pools]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 80g
- [[Primal Velium Shard]] x1
- +350 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
