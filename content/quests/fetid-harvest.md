## Steps

1. Gather 10 fetidhorn spores, found on/around the trees & vines on the [[Drednever Crash Site]] island. These can be found anywhere on the island but seem to be most common around {{waypoint -121, 74, 940}}
1. Return to [[Captain Dabner Drednever]]

## Rewards

- At least 18g 85s 15c
- 600 faction with **Drednever Expedition**
