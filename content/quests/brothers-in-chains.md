## Steps

1. Free the enslaved frogloks from the clutches of the Ykeshan trolls
   - Search for 6 keys on the troll guards
     - Use the keys on 'a shackle keyhole' next to 'a captured Guk froglok' for the update. You do not get the update for simply looting 6 keys.
   - Seek out 6 shackles being used to restrain the cursed frogloks
     - {{waypoint 97, 13, -202}}
     - {{waypoint 32, 13, -202}}
     - {{waypoint 2, -18, -79}}
     - {{waypoint -4, -17, -103}}
     - {{waypoint 133, -17, -102}}
     - {{waypoint 102, -18, -71}}
   - Return to Firmroot and report your success to Freluup.

## Rewards

- At least 31g 73s 78c
