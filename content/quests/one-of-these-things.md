## Steps

1. Speak to [[Greklin Shalegrock]] at the entrance camp {{waypoint -126, 14, 191}}
1. Use Greklin's papers on 4 more stones around the Tourbillion. Click to target the stones then use the paper. *(Found stones around {{waypoint -8.78, -1.74, 738.58}}*
1. Return to Greklin to complete the quest.
