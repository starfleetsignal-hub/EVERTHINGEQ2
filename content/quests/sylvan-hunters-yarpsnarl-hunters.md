## Steps

1. Kill 12 [[A Yarpsnarl hunter|Yarpsnarl hunters]] in the [[Butcherblock Mountains]].  Yarpsnarl hunters can be found wandering north of the druid ring.

## Notes

[[Yarpsnarl hunter|Yarpsnarl hunters]] can be difficult to find, as they appear to be uncommon spawns sharing a rotation with more common creatures in the Butcherblock Mountains. One possible approach is to kill [[Highlands viper|Highlands Vipers]] and [[Wandering essence|Wandering Essences]] around {{waypoint 175, 212, -64}} to spawn Hunters ; [[Highlands viper|Highland vipers]] in other parts of the zone also seem to work.

At least one of the creature types in the [[Canyon shriller|Canyon Shrillers]] area also appears to be a placeholder. Thus, coupling this quest with the city writs for [[Emerald drake|Emerald Drakes]] and [[Canyon shriller|Canyon Shrillers]] plus [[Trapper Coalbear]]'s positivity-filled, courageous anti-ursine crusade, all near [[Argro Durthor]], might work.
