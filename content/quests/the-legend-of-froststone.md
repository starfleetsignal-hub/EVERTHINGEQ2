## Prerequisites

- You must be able to speak Orcish

## Steps

1. Collect items in [[Eastern Wastes]]:
   - 6 rocks around [[Ry'Gorr Keep]]
   - 6 wood planks on the beach south of [[Ravaged Land]]
     - *see talk page for possible locations)*
1. Speak to [[Docent Sally]] in [[Nipik's Haven]] -1,302 -539 3,436
1. Return to Grand Historian Elred Wintershade outside Thurgadin in Great Divide
1. Speak to Docent Selly inside [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain|Thurgadin]] in [[The Stand of Honor]] {{waypoint 871, -293, 330}}
1. Find crates on the snowy shore near (below) [[Blisterrock Ridge]] in Great Divide {{waypoint -111, -526, -805}}  This is a location update only, no crates are visible.
1. Collect 4 spears. Follow the coast east or west to find them laying in the snow
   - *Note:see talk page for possible locations*
1. Return to the Grand Historian
1. Kill Ry'Gorr orcs near the [[Ry'Gorr Ruins]] {{waypoint -706, 200, 218}} for update
1. Return to the Grand Historian
1. Speak with [[Kosh Brokenwound]] southeast of Ry'Gorr Keep in Eastern Waste {{waypoint -914, -370, 2923}}
1. Kill [[Grand Shaman Ollurn]] in Ry'Gorr Keep {{waypoint -774, -343, 2808}}; update is in chest
1. Return to Kosh Brokenwound
1. Enter [[Crystal Caverns: Collapse]] (heroic) via a cave close to Ry'Gorr Keep {{waypoint -786, -323, 2668}}. Once inside, go to the pink attuner at {{waypoint 76, -14, 146}} to spawn [[Glight Snowchipper]] {{waypoint 76, -14, 151}} and speak with him.
1. Kill 8 [[A Ry'Gorr miner|Ry'Gorr miner]]s
1. Return to Glight Snowchipper
1. Go to the second pink attuner at {{waypoint -7, -5, -55}}. (This summons [[Slagd Frozentoe]]) Kill him. {{waypoint -7, -5, -55}}
1. Return to Glight Snowchipper
