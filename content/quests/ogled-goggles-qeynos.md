## Steps

1. Speak to [[Barb Islenet (Qeynos Capitol District)|Barb Islenet]] - she's on the second floor balcony of the alchemist's shop in South Qeynos {{waypoint 641, -12, 235}}.
   - Complete Barb's subquest [[Wine for the Lady]]
     - To complete Barb's subquest you'll have to complete a subquest for [[Abner Siblet (Qeynos Province District)|Abner Siblet]] and obtain his [[Thundermist Merlot]]
1. Return to Barb and trade the Thundermist Merlot for her goggles
