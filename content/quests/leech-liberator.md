Note: You may need to mentor for this quest as the Leech seems to die immediately when you try to feed it if you are too high a level

Within the Drowned Delta live predators that feast upon the poor, defenseless leeches. Well, they're not defenseless anymore, as I've arrived to save them!

## Notes

- You have 7 minutes to complete this quest.
- You can accidentally kill your healed grimleeches (a failure condition for the quest), so avoid AOE and probably move the cephoplexes away from the leech to fight them.

## Steps

1. Save the injured grimleeches around the [[Drowned Delta]] before they all die!
1. Click a nearby muckpod to receive **muckpod pollen** {{waypoint -426.57, 30.61, -497.88}}.
1. Right click 5 injured leeches to feed them pollen and save them. You'll find the leeches near:
   - {{waypoint -403, 31, -494}}
   - {{waypoint -461, 33, -415}}
   - {{waypoint -302, 33, -335}}
   - {{waypoint -327, 32, -274}}
   - {{waypoint -279, 34, -528}}
1. Fight off swarms of [[A hungry cepholex|hungry cepholex]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +250 faction with **Zou'Lidelas**
- [[Dirt encrusted key]]
