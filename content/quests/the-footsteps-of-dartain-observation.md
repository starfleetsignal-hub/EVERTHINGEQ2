## Steps

1. Speak with [[Falizar D'Narin]] at [[The Eye of Dartain]].
1. Collect 10 pieces of the geodes, an uncommon update from [[A boulder dasher|boulder dashers]] or [[A summoned bastion|summoned bastions]].
   - *Note: Users have reported a greater frequency of updates from the bastions. If you only kill the ones with the quest feather, you should get 10 for 10!*
1. Return to Falizar.
1. Collect the resonating heart from [[Kluhb, the Seal Guard-Champion]] in [[The Hole]] {{waypoint -525, -300, -589}}. Clear the mobs in front of the golden door to start the ring event.
   - *Note: If the ring event is interrupted for any reason, there is a 45 minute wait.*
1. Return to Falizar.
1. Click the platform at {{waypoint 1798, 0, 4308}} and speak with [[A projected erudite]].
1. Return to Falizar.
