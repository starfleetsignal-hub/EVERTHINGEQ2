## Prerequirements

- In order to reach [[Sister Ghoulinda]] on top of the chantry, you need to be able to fly in [[Mahngavi Wastes]], which is unlocked after the quest [[Visions of Vetrovia: Handle with Scare]] for the whole account, however [[Sister Ghoulinda]] will not offer you the quest until you have completed the entire Visions of Vetrovia signature timeline (ending with [[Visions of Vetrovia: News Far and Wide]]).

## Steps

1. Obtain a [[Crone-Speak Rune]] and empower it.
   1. Kill [[A drowned one|drowned ones]] all over [[Accursed Chantry Island]] until you obtain the rune.
   1. Then kill 15 more [[A drowned one|drowned ones]] to empower the rune.
1. Speak to [[Sister Ghoulinda]]
1. Search for a ghostly word tome.
   1. Click on [[Book of Words - Grybmel]] {{waypoint 294.75, 215.33, 795.73}} (At the top of the waterfall)
1. Kill 15 [[A necrocraft abhorrent|necrocraft abhorrents]] on [[Shattered Monolith Cay]] around {{waypoint -196.21, 23.08, -834.64}}
1. Speak to [[Sister Ghoulinda]]

## Rewards

- 3p 63g 74s 16c
