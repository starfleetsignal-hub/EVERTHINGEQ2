## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Caves]] or [[The Forest Ruins]].
   - If you went to The Caves: slay five [[A white spider|white spiders]] {{waypoint -22, 0, -66}} or [[A white spiderling|white spiderlings]] {{waypoint 48, 0, -59}}
   - If you went to The Forest Ruins: slay five [[A bristly tarantula|bristly tarantulas]] {{waypoint 1088, -20, -627}}
1. Return to Feodra.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 10c
