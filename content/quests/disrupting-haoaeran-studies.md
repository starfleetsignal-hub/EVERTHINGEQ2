## Steps

1. Complete the following in no particular order:
   - Kill 4 [[A Haoaeran excavator|Haoaeran excavators]] and [[A Haoaeran tasker|Haoaeran taskers]] around the [[Excavation Site]], a small cave northwest of Chrykori Village {{waypoint 92, 4, -241}}
   - Gather a sample of what the excavators are digging up, by harvesting [[Inscribed pottery]] on the ground of the Excavation Site.
1. Return to Primary Conzuk Zum'Ha.

## Rewards

- At least 55c
