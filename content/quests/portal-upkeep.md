## Prerequisites

- Neriak citizenship

## Description

A stressed matron by the name of [[Drise T'Zorzin]] has offered me compensation for helping maintain [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak's]] mystical [[Sea Gate]].

## Steps

1. Harvest 4 **rancor quartz gems** from around [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
1. Kill the following in [[Nektulos Forest]]:
   - 3 [[A rotheart treant|rotheart treants]] {{waypoint -195.68, 60.45, -1442.36}}
   - 3 [[An ashland wisp|ashland wisps]] {{waypoint 30, 0, -1107}}
   - 3 [[A river stone|river stones]]  {{waypoint 172, 0, -992}}
1. Return to Drise
1. Take the [[Sea Gate alimentation potion]] to the base of the [[Sea Gate]] and spread it in the waters there.
1. Return to Drise for your reward

## Rewards

- At least 17s 67c
