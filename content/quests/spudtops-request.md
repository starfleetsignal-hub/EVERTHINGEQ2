## Steps

1. Go to [[Karatun Hold]] and click the cup at {{waypoint 933, 77, -2303}}.
   - You can drop down to the building and enter through a crack in the wall to click the cup without aggroing anything. The crack is on the wall to the front left of the door into the building.
1. Return to [[Germa Prash]] in [[Highton]]. {{waypoint 1275, 159, -2074}}

## Rewards

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- +800 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- [[Tenacious Leggings]]
