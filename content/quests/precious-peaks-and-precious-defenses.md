## Steps

1. Use the conveyance disc in [[Fiddtan Crest]] ( looted item ) to gain access to the Tincture butte above
   1. ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Tincture_Conveyance_Sigil.png)Kill Zakir Rish silvermage and/or other mobs near them  {{waypoint -484, 161, 336}} until you loot a [[Tincture Conveyance Sigil]] (rare **small chest** drop)
      - The mobs will not have any feathers.
      - *Note: In case you do it in a group, each char needs one to use the golden disk in [[Fiddtan Crest]]*
   1. Use golden conveyance disc {{waypoint -401, 253, 603}} in Fiddtan Crest to access the Tincture butte above. This location is called [[Tincture Orb Gardens]].
      - ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Fiddtan_Crest_Teleport_.png)To get to this disc on foot, locate the green and gold teleporter at {{waypoint -510.88, 143.95, 415.03}}. This will take you to Fiddtan Crest.
      - Note: When you get to Fiddtan Crest there doesn't appear to be a way to get "up" to the disc. Hint, walk up the crystals. There is one to the left of the path up at {{waypoint -499.79, 220.80, 649.40}}
      - (NOTE:  If you are able to fly, simply fly to the golden disc at {{waypoint -401, 253, 603}} and safely access the [[Tincture Orb Gardens]].
1. search the golden regions for arcane defenses
   - ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Tincture_Boundary_ward.png)Click any boundary ward statue along the edge.
   - Possible location at {{waypoint 576.04, 249.98, 726.89}}
   - Possible location at {{waypoint 470, 260, 555}}
1. ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Platinum_Tortile_Sigil.png)Use teleporter {{waypoint 726.80, 275.12, 205.55}} to access [[Albalatin Terrace]] {{waypoint -57.23, 651.65, -91.52}}and kill Zakir Rish Platinum mobs until small chest drops [[Platinum Tortile Sigil]] *for each char in the group*
     - **Be careful and target and inspect each mob the 3 up Heroics are very tough.**
1. Disable the boundary wards
   - ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Albalatin_Boundary_Ward.png)Click to destroy **6** of the Albalatin boundary wards around the area. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -108.49, 649.65, -62.83}}, {{waypoint -98.42, 648.73, -114.25}}
     - {{waypoint -83.29, 649.08, -144.96}}, {{waypoint 4.72, 650.30, -116.88}}
     - {{waypoint 79.86, 650.28, -104.42}}, {{waypoint 73.79, 650.25, -70.65}}
     - {{waypoint -77.72, 650.39, -25.12}}, {{waypoint -24.48, 648.69, -152.68}}
     - {{waypoint 11.00, 662.12, -150.64}}, {{waypoint 80.80, 662.36, -140.11}}
       - Take the teleporter back to [[Glistervein Grove]] at {{waypoint -53.98, 651.61, -89.68}}
   - Click to destroy **6** Tincture wards around the area. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 414.10, 253.13, 415.72}}, {{waypoint 469.02, 260.10, 555.21}}
     - {{waypoint 537.51, 252.64, 699.70}}, {{waypoint 752.57, 259.20, 590.91}}
     - {{waypoint 774.42, 277.47, 515.20}}, {{waypoint 428.08, 250.04, 356.51}}
     - {{waypoint 650.47, 249.98, 748.23}},  {{waypoint 727.03, 255.32, 653.10}}
1. Return to [[Riccaww]] at [[Turquoise Oasis Camp]] {{waypoint -33.75, 87.18, 483.86}}. Camp is Hidden under the rock.

- 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1000 faction with **Isles of Wisdom**
- [[Aviak Defender Statue]]
