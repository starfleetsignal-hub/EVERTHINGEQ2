## Steps

1. Kill the nearby Myr'Dal until you receive [[A dull key]], [[A tarnished key]], or [[A scratched key]] and use it on one of the chests found at {{waypoint 132, -124, 176}}. You only need one of the three keys to complete the quest.
1. Return to [[An Ardent child]].

- Experience Only
