## Steps

*Note: if you choose to **squash** a spiderling instead, [[Capru]] will not talk to you for 24 hours. Also, if you use an alt to gather the fish removing it from the shared bank will not update the quest as of 9/9/11. You can remove it from the shared bank and then drop it in a sales crate and remove it again to get around this issue.*

1. Go fishing for an Iceclad Cutlassfish (found around the Tower of Frozen Shadow) or buy from broker.
   - This step will also update if you have one in your inventory already, saving you a lot of time.
1. Return to [[Capru]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g

## Shopping List

To complete Capru's questline, you'll need the following items:

- 1 [[Iceclad Cutlassfish]] (used for *this* quest)
- 2 [[Sumac lumber]]
- 1 [[Titanium ore]]
- 3 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
- 6 [[Bamboo shoot]]
- 5 [[Mottled pelt]]
- 2 [[Amber]]
- 1 [[Brontotherium Meat]]
- 2 [[Goahmari Herb]]
- 1 [[Aerated mineral water]]

**Fuel**

- 1 [[Ethereal coal]]
- 7 [[Ethereal filament]]
- 1 [[Ethereal kindling]]

And, optionally (if you choose not to fly back to Thurgadin):

- 1 [[Portable Chemistry Set]]
- 3 [[Portable Sewing Table]]
- 1 [[Portable Workbench]]
