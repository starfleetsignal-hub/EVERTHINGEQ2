## Steps

1. Hunt the following
   - Hunt 10 wetland lacerators in [[Dankmar Swamp]] {{waypoint 98.57, 7.17, 391.55}}
   - Hunt 8 sanguine drainers in [[Dankmar Swamp]] are near the lacerators (flying bugs)
1. Return to [[Kober Botley]]
