## Notes

The quest [[The Hands of Fate]] takes you to [[Zenith Helionae]] where you can pick up this quest on the way ...

## Steps

1. Start searching in [[Zenith Helionae]] for an answer to [[Sassarish|Sassarish's]] riddle.
   - when you reach the [[Septentrio Breeze]] at {{waypoint 78.16, 149.74, -321.50}} the quest updates
   1. Gather the [[Septentrio Breeze]] {{waypoint 78.16, 149.74, -321.50}}.
      1. inspect the Septentrio Breeze
      1. defeat the Septentrio Breeze defender
      1. gather a sample of the Septentrio Breeze
   1. Gather the [[Zephirus Breeze]] {{waypoint 144.32, 161.85, 44.10}}.
      1. inspect the Zephirus Breeze
      1. defeat the Zephirus Breeze defender
      1. gather a sample of the Zephirus Breeze
   1. Gather the [[Austeronotus Breeze]] {{waypoint -82.19, 160.23, 6.01}}.
      1. inspect the Austeronotus Breeze
      1. defeat the Austeronotus Breeze defender
      1. gather a sample of the Austeronotus Breeze
   1. Gather the [[Subsolanus Breeze]] in the [[Helionae Razelands]] at  {{waypoint -144.91, 106.76, -459.82}}.
      1. inspect the Subsolanus Breeze
      1. defeat the Subsolanus Breeze defender
      1. gather a sample of the Subsolanus Breeze
1. Return to [[Sassarish]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
