## Steps

1. Go to [[Kunzar Jungle]] (the druid ring in there is close to the area you need.) {{waypoint -605, -40, 276}} use Trakaraptor mask and speak with [[A keen trakaraptor]] at {{waypoint -608, -40, 269}}.
1. Kill [[An anxious Iksar villager]] at {{waypoint -524, -42, 437}}. There may be several.
1. Then kill 5 [[Iksar villager|Iksar villagers]]
1. Then find barrels of meat in and around the gazebo (on 5-10 minute respawn timers).
1. Talk to [[A keen trakaraptor]] again
1. Kill [[Inner Stalker]] (83^^ Heroic) raptor right near him, he will pop up within 15 meters non agro until you get close to him.
   - (NOTE: This one is the worst one for soloing, as he procs the dot AND has a habit of dispelling your snare/root. I'd advise you get a friend unless you are very well geared).
1. Harvest the rock (Respawns every 5-10 mins)
