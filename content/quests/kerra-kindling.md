## Description

The Kerra Isle outfitter has requested assistance in refilling the firewood supplies for the isle.

## Steps

1. I have been asked to help replenish the firewood stores for Kerra Isle.
   - Harvest 25 sumac lumber (must be harvested fresh).
1. Return to [[Harriet]] on Kerra Isle.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,000 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +2,500 faction with **Kerra Isle Outfitters**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Kerra Isle Daily Supply Tasks
