## Steps

![aetherite crystal](images/Aetherite_crystal.png)
![aetherite](images/Aetherite.png)
![blazing ignus](images/Blazing_ignus.png)

1. Gather and collect the following
   - Gather 8 aetherite by destroying aetherscar crystals in the  [[Aetherscar]].
     - White crystal formations found near {{waypoint 885, 12, -206}} {{waypoint 660, 0, 8}} {{waypoint 702, 10, 50}} {{waypoint 774, 30, -35}} {{waypoint 807, 33, -35}} {{waypoint 763, 15, -30}}  (Group update) Good area near the ocean.
   - Collect 18 blazing ignus filiments by killing [[Blazing ignus]] in the [[Aetherscar]].
     - Found near the tips of the formations.
1. Return to [[Himaduri]] at {{waypoint -107.75, 574.43, -715.37}}.
