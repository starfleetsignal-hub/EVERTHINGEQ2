1. Kill 10 skeletal Varsoon inhabitants.
   - [[A Varsoon guard]], [[A servant of Varsoon]], etc... large groups of skeletons can be found in the two jails at {{waypoint -248, 8, -159}} and {{waypoint -235, 0, 60}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 skeletal Varsoon inhabitants.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 skeletal Varsoon inhabitants.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 bats.
   - [[A deafening shrieker|deafening shrieker]]s, [[A dust shriller|dust shriller]]s and [[A mud shriller|mud shriller]]s, found throughout the cavern half of zone
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 bats.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 bats.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 animated tomes.
   - [[An enchanted tome|enchanted tome]]s and [[A spirited tome|spirited tome]]s, found at {{waypoint 237, -8, 55}} and {{waypoint 12, 7, 71}}.  The [[Tome of Death]] and [[Tome of Life]] also update.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 animated tomes.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 animated tomes.
1. Read the book.
