## Prerequisites

As of ToV the minimum mining skill required is no longer in place.

## Steps

1. Gather 6 loose ore samples from the Mortartoe Mine behind the fort.![loose Mortartoe ore sample](images/Loose_Mortartoe_ore_sample.jpg)
1. I must speak to a [[Mortartoe Mine foreman]] {{waypoint -321, 189, -133}} to obtain a copy of a recent quality report.
1. I must return these ore samples and the ore quality report to [[Bartleby Blatherback|Bartleby]].

## Rewards

- At least 1g 5s
- +2500 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- One of the following:
  - [[Dandelion fiber]]
  - [[Malleable loam]]
  - [[Cuirboilli leather pelt]]
  - [[Palladium cluster]]
  - [[Rough jasper]]
  - [[Severed fir]]
