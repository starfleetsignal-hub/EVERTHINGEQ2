## Notes

You can only complete one training session every 18 hours and must complete all five to finish [[A Frozen Egg]]

## Steps

1. Hunt 8 [[Crystalline Hunters]] or watchers at {{waypoint -130.42, -356.84, 137.62}}
1. Feed to your griffawn

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c

If this is your last training day, after you hail your griffawn for the last time, and tell it you like it, too.
One of the following:

- [[Snow Gryphon]]
- [[Tundra Gryphon]]
- [[Highland Gryphon]]
