## Steps

1. Speak to [[Olinnisk]] {{waypoint -64.88, 10.69, 71.08}} on the western bank of the northern [[Myhst River]].
1. Investigate the ***dark bubbling water*** at {{waypoint -66.45, 8.21, 61.20}} and kill the group of 3 [[Watery form|watery forms]] that attacks.![dark bubbling water](images/Dark_bubbling_water.jpg)
1. Return to [[Olinnisk]].
1. Search the [[Myhst River|river's]] bank to the north to gather ***a strangely glowing stone*** {{waypoint -47.45, 17.80, 15.33}}.![a strangely glowing stone](images/A_strangely_glowing_stone.png)
   - *Note: It looks like a little black gem with black smoke coming out of it.*
1. Return to [[Olinnisk]].

## Rewards

- [[Plumewit Eggshell Fragment]] and possibly found as a shiny collectible
- At least 1p 53g 9s
