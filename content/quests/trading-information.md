## Steps

1. Examine a book called 'The Chant of the Exarchs' in [[Exarch's Seclusion]]. {{waypoint -16, 58, -211}}
   - Don't kill Exarch Trulnariz or a herald of Dar (his placeholder) before reading the book! They are needed for the next step, and unfortunately spawn right in front of the book.
1. You must then kill [[Exarch Trulnariz]] or his [[A herald of Dar|placeholder]]. {{waypoint -11, 57, -210}}
   - If you killed the Exarch or his placeholder before reading the book there is another herald of Dar that will update the quest. He is located in the [[Hall of Rites]] at {{waypoint 9, 65, -63}}.
1. Find four keys from [[A Scaleborn harbinger|Scaleborn harbingers]] that spawn in Exarch's Seclusion. This is an uncommon quest update.
   - Another harbinger spawn is [[Banisher Preparatory]]. {{waypoint 170, 60, -50}}
1. Go back to unlock 'The Chant of the Exarchs'.
1. You must now collect 8 pieces of paper and 8 pieces of chalk:
   - Slay 8 droags of any type to get 8 wings (to be used as paper).
   - Harvest 8 Charcoal Clumps in [[Banisher Preparatory]] {{waypoint 150.95, 65.76, -26.46}} or [[Ready Room]] {{waypoint 196.01, 157, -80}}. The nodes have a respawn of ~5 minutes.
1. Go back to the Exarch's Seclusion and click the book to transcribe it. You will obtain [[A transcribed coded map]].
1. Find a droag who can help you translate the map. Take the map to [[A Scaleborn blasphemer]] in the [[Scaleborn Cell]] {{waypoint 77.91, 126.14, 170.87}}.
1. Take a sacrificial blade from the Ceremony Weapon Rack in the [[Room of Recovery]] at {{waypoint -173, 43, -180}}.
1. Slay 8 [[A Scaleborn ritualist|Scaleborn ritualists]], found in the [[Hall of Rites]] {{waypoint 8, 65, 14}}, the [[Pool of Spirits]] {{waypoint 132, 45, 145}}, and other areas.
1. Return to the Ceremony Weapon Rack and place the sacrificial blade in it.
1. Go back to [[A Scaleborn blasphemer]] in the [[Scaleborn Cell]] {{waypoint 77.91, 126.14, 170.87}}.
1. Get an empty archwraith crystal from the bowls around the central fount in the [[Reservoir of the Wraith]]. {{waypoint 99, 39, -140}}
1. Destroy [[A tormented wraith|tormented wraiths]] until the crystal absorbs one of their souls as a quest update (could take over twelve kills).
1. Examine the coded map in your inventory to decode it with the archwraith crystal.
1. Hide the coded map in the 'Tome of Scaleborn Tactics' within the [[Talonflight Armory]]. {{waypoint -49, 128, 199}}
1. Return to Xhorroz to complete the quest. {{waypoint -128, 78, 204}}
