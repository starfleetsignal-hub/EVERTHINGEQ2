## Steps

1. Talk to [[Gisuka Voll]] outside the Temple of War in [[Freeport]].
1. Talk to Vzell, Skrig, Hargula & Maak.  All are turquoise dots on the Freeport map.
1. Enter [[Big Bend]].  Touch The Torch of Oggok in the middle of the zone.  {{waypoint -4, 1, 1}}  Kill the spirits that attack you after you touch it.
1. Talk to [[Gisuka Voll]] in [[Freeport]].

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
- [[A Grand Temple of War Weaponrack]] (house item)
