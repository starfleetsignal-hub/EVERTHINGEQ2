## Steps

1. [[Jesenda]] (kneeling next to [[Queen Amree]]){{waypoint 983, -26, -894}} suggests setting up a false grove in order to lure the assassin out into the open.
1. Click the boat to travel to [[Elddar Grove]]. Meet [[Queen Amree|Queen Amree's]] guards there. {{waypoint 709, -17, -279}}
1. Place the 5 false flower buds and sapling in the grove encircled by Faydark vines.
1. Hide in a nearby fern and wait for the assassin. {{waypoint 672, -17, -242}}
1. When you surprise the assassin, he casts an illusion that spawns around six additional assassins. Slay them!
1. The true assassin is knocked to the ground at the end of the fight. Question him to discover what traitor employed him.
   - Talk to the assassin immediately. If you wait too long he will port himself away, and you will have to repeat the fern-hiding and assassin-slaying again.
1. Return to [[Queen Amree]].

## Rewards

- At least 25g 70s 61c
