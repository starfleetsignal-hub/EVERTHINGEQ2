## Steps

   - Kill 12 [[A drowned trooper|drowned troopers]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]] to recover bone fragments. {{waypoint 96, -14, -189}}. All solo mobs lvl 22-24.
   - Destroy 12 [[A rock writher|rock writhers]] (same location as above).
   - Collect enchanted feathers from the hawks in Thundering Steppes, {{waypoint 261, 4, -432}} and also in different locations.
1. Return to [[Gildas Cedartree]] for your reward.
