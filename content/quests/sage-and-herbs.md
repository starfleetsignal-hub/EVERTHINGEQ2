## Steps

1. Gather and Hunt the following
   - Gather Dragonbane Herbs
     - *They are all underwater along the Southern shore from about {{waypoint 150, -8, 792}} to about {{waypoint -321, 1, 804}} small blue flowers, can be hard to see, but are mostly on the slope and at the base of the slope underwater*
   - Hunt a skeletal icebanes near {{waypoint 434, 32, 420}}
1. return to [[The Dragon Sage]] at {{waypoint -12, 72, 527}} in the [[Western Wastes]]
