## Steps

1. Collect 10 feathers by killing Kragploom aviaks.  Any will work but the [[A Kragploom chick|Kragploom chicks]] are the lowest level and easiest to reach - they're found around {{waypoint 158, 188, 270}}
1. Return to [[Gherrana Cobbleblork]] who is at {{waypoint 342.89, 190.39, 495.11}} up by the griffin station ABOVE Butcherblock docks.

## Rewards

- At least 25s 70c
