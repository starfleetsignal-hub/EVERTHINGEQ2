Zeen Corblecog, Knight of Gearheart, who accompanied me from Kelethin into the underdepths, is trying to maintain the tinkered portal-gate that allows us instant transport to Maldura. She has asked for fresh leech slime to use as a gelatinous arcane conductor.

## Steps

1. Hunt 16 [[A scumleech slug|scumleech slugs]] in the streams of the [[Luminous Steppe]]. One place to find them is {{waypoint -299, 111, 82}}
1. Return to [[Zeen Corblecog (Thalumbra, the Ever Deep)|Zeen Corblecog]].

## Rewards

- At least 62g 37s

## Notes

This quest is only available after you have completed parts of the Signature timeline, then it is repeatable.
