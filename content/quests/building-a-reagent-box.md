## Notes

When accepting this quest you receive a [[Velium Reagent Box (Recipe Book)|Velium Reagent Box]] recipe. This does NOT auto scribe, find in your inventory and scribe it. This is a level 10 Artisan recipe.<br>
Fuels needed:

- 50 Zimaran Coal

Talking to Dipper during this quest opens  his quest [[Eggscellent Adventure in the Necropolis]]

## Steps

1. Gather the following
   - Obtain 4 living crystal lattices - small thin white crystals that you have to "kick" to spawn  a crystalline predator, living crystal lattice is a body drop. Not a guaranteed spawn.
     - ![](images/Living_Crystal.png.png)Some possible locations  {{waypoint 14, 7, 121}},  {{waypoint -118, 10, 151}},  {{waypoint 12, 7, 121}},  {{waypoint 82, 16, 113}} {{waypoint -241, 9, -139}} and more around [[Chillblain Fields]], on and below the ice shelf.
   - Mine velium deposits for 6 luminous velium (small blue crystal like rocks)
     - Some possible locations  {{waypoint 208, 8, 53}},  {{waypoint -182, 8, 177}}, {{waypoint -149, 9, 158}},  {{waypoint -114, 10, 145}},  {{waypoint -23, 8, 128}} and more around [[Chillblain Fields]], on and below the ice shelf.
   - Hunt tidal shardwaves for 3 frigid sparks, Body drops from mobs around {{waypoint 406, 68, 494}}
1. Craft the [[Velium Reagent Box]] specifically on a [[Varig's Velium Forge]] in Remnant Shores camp {{waypoint 61, 10, 585}}
   - *scribe the [[Velium Reagent Box (Recipe Book)|Velium Reagent Box]] from your inventory*
1. Speak with Dipper {{waypoint 81, 8, 600}} to get the Box blessed are recieve the [[Blessed Velium Reagent Box]]
1. Return to the [[The Dragon Sage]]
