## Description

The Far Seas encampment is always in need of healing as the area is still quite dangerous. The dogwood shrubs that grow around the encampment and on the hills above have strong healing properties and make an excellent healing salve.

## Ingredients Needed

- 5 leonid root
- 10 handama leaf
- 25 Effulgent Candle (fuel merchant near forge)

## Steps

1. Harvest 5 [[Dogwood Shrub|Dogwood Shrubs]]. The shrub is an heirloom [[Decorating Houses|house item]] that can be collected only when you have this quest (or a previous quest).
1. Craft 5 [[Healing Salve]] at a [[Chemistry Table]]
1. Return to [[Skrit]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
