## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Rhenium Knife for my apprentice.
   - I need to craft a Sturdy Rhenium Knife for my apprentice.
     - Acquire the following ingredients and fuel:
       - 2 [[Rhenium ore]]
       - 4 [[Effulgent coal]]
     - Using a [[Forge]], craft 1 [[A Sturdy Rhenium Knife (Item)|A Sturdy Rhenium Knife]]
1. I need to take this knife back to my apprentice.
   - I need to take this knife back to my apprentice.
