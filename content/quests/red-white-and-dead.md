## Notes

- This quest can be obtained without doing the previous quests in the [[Jarsath Wastes Timeline|Clan Skleross Timeline]]. However, the final quest offered by [[Shaman Medicus]], [[A Path for Medicus]], will not be offered until the other Clan Skleross quests have been completed.

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Temple of the Red Lord]]. {{waypoint -2392, 290, 421}}
1. Go to [[The Temple of the White Lady]]. {{waypoint -2455, 228, 1362}}
1. Inspect the corpse at {{waypoint -2455, 228, 1362}}.
1. Return to [[Shaman Medicus]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 5g
