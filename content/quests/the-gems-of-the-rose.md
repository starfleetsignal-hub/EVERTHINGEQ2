## Notes

- Dun McDowell was the leader of the Rogues of the White Rose, the Barbarian Rogues Guild in [[EverQuest]].

## Steps

1. Collect 5 Gems of the Rose from the bottom of [[Everstorm Bay]]. Known locations are:
   - {{waypoint 113, -54, -5}}
   - {{waypoint 108, -45, -121}}
   - {{waypoint 75, -55, 17}}
   - {{waypoint 48, -45, -46}}
   - {{waypoint 42, -53, 128}}
   - {{waypoint -3, -40, 59}}
1. Bury the gems at the gravestone of Dun McDowell.

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Blade of the Lost Oasis]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Bloody Ulak]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Chilled Steel Aegis]] (All Priests)
  - [[Chilled Steel Guard]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Chilled Steel Orb]] (All Priests)
  - [[Chilled Steel Symbol]] (All Mages)
  - [[Chilled Steel Tower Shield]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Frozen Flail]] (All Priests)
  - [[Glinting Rake's Blade]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Rusty Staff of the Necromancer]] (All Mages)
  - [[Symphonic Whistling Stave]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
