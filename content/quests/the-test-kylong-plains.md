- Completion of this quest also unlocks [[Showdown at Gra'al Shul Temple]], if you have progressed far enough in [[Hilbraega Stoutmun]]'s questline.

1. Use the *Coersion Antidote* on the mudfin warrior walking around next to [[Hroar Kalacious]].
1. Speak to Hroar Kalacious.

- [[Radiating Gem of Striking]]
