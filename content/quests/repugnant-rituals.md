## Steps

1. Enter [[Hold of Rime: The Ascent]] and kill frost spiders, charging up your Frost Spike
1. Use the [[Charged Frost Spike]] to kill five Hold of Rime soldiers - they must be under 50% health before you can use the item
1. Return to [[Nollarr Goodforger]]

## Rewards

- At least 41g 63s 17c
