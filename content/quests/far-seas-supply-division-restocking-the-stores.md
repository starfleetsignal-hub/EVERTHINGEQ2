## Steps

Fulfill the Far Seas Trading Company Supply Division stocking requirements by gathering:

- 10 fresh clams from the beaches of [[Luk Ki Island]] in [[The Village of Shin]] (**clam** nodes){{waypoint 155.53, -2.21, 32.58}} This is the island NW of the docks.
- 10 rice from the fields near [[The Village of Shin]]. [[The Village of Shin]] (rice stalks) {{waypoint -68, 14, -138}}
- 10 kindling from the [[Mystic Woods]] in [[The Mystic Lake]] (on ground near tree trunks, **fallen kindling** nodes) {{waypoint 146, -47, 121}}
- 10 barnacles from the water near the [[Tomb of the Sensei]] in [[The Mystic Lake]] (Don't climb the rightmost waterfall, use climbing wall at {{waypoint 33, -37, 58}} & along the water line you find **fresh-water barnacle** nodes).
- 10 tufts of beaver pelt from the [[Tranquil Woods]] in [[The Mystic Lake]] {{waypoint 124, -38, 68}} (they look like "shinies", **tuft of beaver fur** nodes).

## Notes

- You can get this quest with a tradeskill level of 50 or higher.
- This is one of three possible quests given by the desk. You can only do one of these orders per week.
- If you have a low adventure level, stay near the western river to avoid the level 56+ Gunthak pirates.

## Rewards

- AA Experience
- At least 6g 5s 52c
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
