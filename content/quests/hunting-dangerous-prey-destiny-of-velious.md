## Steps

Kill the following enemies

- **Conquer the Captain:** Kill [[Praefectus Kriegr]], the Captain in [[Iceshard Keep]]
- **Dethrone the Queen:** Kill [[Queen Drachnia]] in [[Crystal Caverns: Collapse]]
- **Slay Lord Something or other:** Kill [[Lord Doljonijiarnimorinar|Lord Bob]] at [[Forgotten Pools]]

OR

- **Kill Cara Omica:** [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Umbral Halls|Tower of Frozen Shadow, Umbral Halls]]
- **Ixnay the Idol:** Kill the [[Idol of Rallos Zek (Named)|Idol of Rallos Zek]] in [[The Temple of Rallos Zek|Temple of Rallos Zek]]
- **Whack the Witch Doctor:** Kill [[Witch Doctor Vuh'Duh]] in [[Hold of Rime: The Ascent]]

OR

- **End the Grand Restorer:** Kill [[Grand Restorer Lingwal (NPC)|Grand Restorer Lingwal]] at [[Hold of Rime: The Fortress Spire]] (Great Divide)
- **Ground the Thunderbolt:** Kill [[Tribunus-Prime Hagandr]] in [[Throne of Storms]]
- **Pound the Hammer of Zek:** Kill [[Prime Imperium Hammarmund]] in  [[The Temple of Rallos Zek|Temple of Rallos Zek]]
