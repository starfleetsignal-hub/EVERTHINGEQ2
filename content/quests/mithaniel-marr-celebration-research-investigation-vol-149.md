## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The avatar summoning rituals require the manipulation of immense power and are inherently dangerous. To reduce the chance of failure and provide an anchor for that power, the herald of Mithaniel Marr has asked me to collect an item that may have once belonged to that god or one of their most loyal followers

- Collect [[Reliquary of Mithaniel Marr|Reliquaries of Mithaniel Marr]] from a powerful creature in [[Aether Wroughtlands: Chaotic Currents (Event Heroic I)|Aether Wroughtlands: Chaotic Currents]] heroic dungeon.

**This does not update in [Solo]. It needs to be Aether Wroughtlands: Chaotic Currents [Event Heroic I].**

- Return to the [[Herald of the Truthbringer]] {{waypoint -840, -48, 199}} in [[The Commonlands]]
