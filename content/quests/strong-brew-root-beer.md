## Prerequisite

You need to be at least level 2 Artisan to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Gather the ingredients for making the root beer.
   - Gather 10 *sweetvine roots* found on the ground.
   - Harvest 10 *spicebracket fungus* found attached to the tree roots.
   - ***Note:** Harvesting the fungus has a slight chance of yielding a [[Spicebracket fungus|house item version]] of the fungus instead of the component.
   - ***Note:** Must be at least Artisan level 2 to scrbe recipe.
1. Hail the [[A faun brewer|faun brewer]] next to the Faun Elder to receive the [[Strong Brew Root Beer Recipe]]
   - ***Note:** If you happen to have the recipe in your inventory already (from last time doing the quest) this NPC might not respond until you remove that recipe from your inventory (scribe or in worst case destroy it).
1. Brew the root beer
   - Scribe the received recipe
   - Use the Brewing Stump behind the Faun Elder and craft the beer
1. Return to the Faun Elder
