## Notes

This quest takes place within [[Dragon Necropolis: Chetari Warrens (Solo)|Dragon Necropolis: Chetari Warrens [Solo]]] and can be done in the same instance as [[Scars of Destruction: Treasures of the Dragon Necropolis]]

**Travel to Portal:**  The best way to get to the portal is to take 'a geomanced transport' at {{waypoint -504.06, -4.57, 434.45}} . Go past the portal to Darkened Passages. Travel further into the cave to {{waypoint -838, 129, 345}}  .

*(NOTE: I spent over half an hour looking for this place. The geomanced transport waypoint led to a dead end at a rock wall.  I finally flew to {{waypoint -561.81, 125.78, 430.70}} a white ledge at the mouth of the cave. from there I followed the right wall to {{waypoint -838.88, 130.36, 341.56}} where I found the zone entrance.)*

## Steps

1. I followed the right-hand wall around the cave, then went through the tunnel. By doing this I did not encounter or have to kill any named. Once I got to the final room. I followed the wall around and clicked on the eggs. The locations are:
   1. {{waypoint 100.84, 99.05, -67.04}}
   1. {{waypoint 176.46, 98.92, 56.79}}
   1. {{waypoint 177.16, 98.28, 22.99}}
   1. {{waypoint -53.16, 99.44, 203.93}}
   1. {{waypoint -99.09, 99.05, 201.51}}
   1. {{waypoint -136.06, 98.56, 224.45}}
   1. {{waypoint 160.84, 98.56, 204.74}}
   1. {{waypoint -136.44, 99.51, 109.11}}
   1. {{waypoint -212.96, 98.89, 32.48}}
   1. {{waypoint -136.96, 99.05, -23.38}}
   1. {{waypoint -163.61, 98.82, -112.85}}
   1. {{waypoint -97.61, 99.05, -110.97}}
1. Lay the 12 dead wyrm eggs to rest within Heart of the Necropolis area of the zone
   - Sparkly yellow egg shape. They are all in the final room.  You can just move around the outside wall.
   - Once all the eggs were laid, I used the map to port back to the Western Wastes.
1. Return to [[Dipper]] at {{waypoint 73, 9, 600}} in the [[Western Wastes]]
