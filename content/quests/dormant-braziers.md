One of the temple's braziers is light with violet flames, but there are two others that remain dormant. I need to find a way to light them to bring this temple out of the darkness. One is linked to a beast of Kralet Penumbra, the other to a violet orb located somewhere within.

## Notes

- You have 6 minutes to complete this quest.
- It's best to clear the temple before starting this quest.

## Steps

1. Light the west brazier by clicking on it {{waypoint -48.60, 22.34, 158.48}}.
   - You must kill Grelikor before this step can happen.
1. Find **a violet orb** in the waters at the lowest level of the temple.
   - You have to look under the water, you will not be able to spot it from above.
   - The location for the orb varies.
1. Light the east brazier {{waypoint -122.17, 22.34, 158.37}} (above the quest-starter brazier).

## Rewards

- At least 1p 51g 20s 11,300 status
- 1 [[Coin of the Eternal Queen]]
