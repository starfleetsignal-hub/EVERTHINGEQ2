## Steps

1. Collect 5 each of three of the following:
    - [[Blacktimber bee|Blacktimber bees]]
    - [[Blood shriller|blood shrillers]]
    - [[Immense tree snake|immense tree snakes]]
    - [[Nek wasp|Nek wasps]]
    - [[Quetzanek|quetzaneks]]
    - [[A sap urchin|sap urchins]]
1. Deliver everything to vermin keeper [[Zesnitra K'Jartan]] {{waypoint -704, 141, -2089}} in [[Nektulos Forest]]
1. Return to [[Drizas N'Ryt]]

## Rewards

- +5,000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- -5,000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -5,000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
