*This quest is unavailable for player of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Kill ten [[A bog sludge|bog sludges]]. {{waypoint 661, -35, 449}}
1. Return to [[Knight-Captain Santis]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 5c
- +65 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
- One of the following:
  - [[Sludge smeared kite shield]]
  - [[Sludge smeared round shield]]
  - [[Sludge smeared shield]]
  - [[Sludge smeared tower shield]]
