## Steps

1. Head to the [[Bridge Keep]] {{waypoint 201, 1, -226}}| in [[The Thundering Steppes]] to see if the Sage of Ages is there.
1. Perhaps some the creatures contains a clue to the Sage's location, all uncommon updates.
   - I need to kill the stronger undead in the for clues ([[A skeletal protector]] {{waypoint 13, -20, -245}}, [[A skeletal lieutenant]] {{waypoint -338, 0, 468}}, [[A skeletal trooper]] {{waypoint -600, -7, 435}})
   - I need to kill the stronger hawks in the for clues ([[A puerile hawk]] {{waypoint 706, -1, -698}}|).
   - I need to kill the stronger beetles in the for clues ([[A steppe beetle]] {{waypoint -1318, -1, 243}}|). ([[A giant beetle]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] also worked).
   - I need to kill the stronger deer in the for clues ([[A mature antelope|mature antelope]] {{waypoint 184, 1, 1330}}|). ([[A fledgling antelope]] also worked)
   - I need to kill the stronger snakes in the for clues ([[A plains serpent]] or a [[A plains lurker]] {{waypoint 03, -1, 194}}|).
   - I need to kill the stronger crabs in the for clues [ [[A shore crab]] {{waypoint 1416, -3, 226}} or [[A corpse feeder]] around {{waypoint 187, 2, -831}} or {{waypoint 348, -22, 144}} ].
   - I need to kill the stronger sirens in the for clues ([[An alluring siren]] {{waypoint 1476, -8, 295}}| , [[An entrancing siren]], [[An irresistible siren]]).
1. Examine the barrels on the docks near [[Elowys Laceleaf]] {{waypoint -561, -15, -484}}.
1. Return to [[Scholar Demini]] for your reward.

## Notes

- The list of alternative sources of quest updates above would imply that any level 22+ creature of the correct type, in any zone, can be killed to advance the quest.

## Rewards

- [[Demini's Class Ring]]
