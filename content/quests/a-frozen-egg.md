## Starting the Quest

This quest does not have a single starting zone. It begins by examining [[A Frosty Egg]], which is obtained by [[Claim|claiming]] the "Destiny of Velious Digital CE: Stormfeather Brood Guardian."

## Prerequisites

Although you can claim and examine the egg at level 1, you must be level 86 to complete the quest series that starts with [[Border Defense]] in [[Goahmari Village]]. That quest series gains you enough faction to build the trust of the Goahmari gryphon expert [[Kezu]], who will teach you more about your egg and its care.

## Steps

1. *I should consult a griffon trainer in the Village of Thundermist*
   - You are looking for <senior gryphon trainer> [[Elize Flannagan]] at {{waypoint 623, 4, -234}} in [[The Thundering Steppes]]. This is the Thundermist Village griffon tower station, just outside the village.
1. *I should consult a goahmari gryphon expert*
   - Speak with <Gryphon Expert> [[Kezu]] at {{waypoint 366, -14, 477}} in [[Goahmari Village]] in the [[Great Divide]]. He will take the egg away from your for safe keeping.
1. *I should return to gryphon expert once I have proived I am a trustworthy guardian for a young gryphon.
   - At this point, you must complete either the adventurer or tradeskilling quest series to learn how to raise a gryphon and earn the trust of Kezu. (L86+ is needed to start either)
     - The adventure series starts with [[Border Defense]] with [[Billie]], leading to [[Norbu]]
     - The tradeskilling series starts with [[Gnomish Gnegotiations]] with [[Aggi Stonefist]], leading to [[Capru]]

After completing atleast one of the lines above, and receiving your gryphon, return to [[Kezu]].

## Rewards

- [[Stormfeather Brood Guardian]]
