The despicable Dark Elves have been raising the fallen elf dead of past wars to make war on their own people! This must be stopped!

## Steps

1. Go to the [[Vault of the Dead]] in the basement of [[Crushbone Keep]] {{waypoint -31, -34, -81}}
   - Slay 4 [[A Crushbone thaumaturgist|Crushbone thaumaturgists]]
   - Slay 4 [[A conscripted Koada'Dal|conscripted Koada'Dals]]
   - Slay 4 [[A conscripted Feir'Dal|conscripted Feir'Dals]]
1. Return to [[Morodag Greenbow]]

## Rewards

- Experience
- Choose one of the following:
  - [[Drape of the Great War]]
  - [[Fay Arcanist's Cape]]
  - [[Wrap of Fay Preservation]]
