Ishaq Al'Nair has requested a few more samples of each type of creature previously gathered. Obtain the extra samples.

## Steps

1. Collect more samples by killing five Arcane, Elemental and Unnatural creatures.
   - Kill 5 of each in any order:
     - Kill [[A spark of energy]] near {{waypoint 2074, -112, 3436}}
     - Kill [[An Underfoot hardstomper]] near {{waypoint 1933, -106, 3610}}.
     - for Unnatural creatures:  Kill anything "Lingering" (e.g. [[A lingering slitherspine|lingering slitherspines]] or [[A lingering Paineel spirit|lingering Paineel spirits]]) at the Ruins of Old Paineel near {{waypoint 1856, -110, 3529}}
1. Return to [[Ishaq Al'Nair]].

- At least 1p 25g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Paineel Embalming Blade]] (All Mages)
  - [[Ulteran Embalming Blade]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Summer Falchion]] (Druids)
  - [[Ulteran Mace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Knuckledusters of the Conscript]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Eagle Sceptre]] (All Mages)
  - [[Ornate Captain's Sword]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
