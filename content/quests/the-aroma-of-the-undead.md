## Steps

1. Find the ingredients for the recipe:
   1. [[A fermented elixir]]: {{waypoint 6, -14, -42}}; You may have to visit the place more than once to get the update.
   1. Two [[A swine|swine]] from the stables: {{waypoint -24, 0, -24}}
      - [[Swine Lord]] placeholds for the swine.
1. After obtaining these items you need to go to the [[Nektropos Castle Kitchen|kitchen]] on the main floor and use the stove. {{waypoint 12, 0, -30}}

## Rewards

- Experience only

## Notes

- Completing the quest spawns [[A ravenous undead dragoon]] and several [[A ravenous undead dreadguard|ravenous undead dreadguards]] near the entrance to the castle.
