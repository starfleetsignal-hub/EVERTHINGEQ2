## Steps

1. Obtain ingredients so Mathra can speak to the forest.
   - 5 heartwood from [[Blacktimber treant|blacktimber treants]] {{waypoint -895, 134, -1809}}
     - If having trouble finding treants, kill [[Blacktimber bee|blacktimber bees]] and [[An ash wraith|ash wraiths]] as well since they share spawn points.
   - 5 fungal spores from [[A murkbloom watcher|murkbloom watchers]] {{waypoint -954, 138, -1832}}
1. Return to Mathra.

## Rewards

- At least 4s 32c
- +150 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Acolyte's Wrought Plate Pauldrons]] (Clerics)
  - [[Bandit's Light Iron Chain Spaulders]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Beguiler's Embroidered Epaulets]] (All Mages)
  - [[Expert's Fine Cloth Arm Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Preserver's Cured Shoulder Pads]] (Druids)
  - [[Sentry's Hammered Pauldrons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Soothsayer's Bound Spaulders]] (Shamans)
