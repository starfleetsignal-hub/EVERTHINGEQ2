- You must be at least adventure level 76 to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Find and speak with [[Shaman Zaz'i'zaz]], who is located at the lizardman camp outside [[U.J.L. 1.0]]. {{waypoint 1150, 392, 865}}
1. Return to Wayth Bitsnbobs.

- At least 8g 61s 8c
