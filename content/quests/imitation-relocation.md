## Steps

1. Enter [[Sebilis]] and harvest 'a Sathirian Blood Vase' in [[Venril Sathir's Hall]]. {{waypoint -75, -125, 520}}
1. Use [[Vase Transmogrification]] from your inventory.
1. Place the [[Imitation Phylactery]] on the table in the [[Audience Waiting Room]] where you picked up the original phylactery. You must examine it from your inventory in order to place it. {{waypoint 181, -8, 369}}
1. Return to [[Enchanter Glowwp]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g
- +1,000 faction with **Synod Reet**
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Cuffs of the Polliwog Protector]]
  - [[Reet Sash of Championing]]
  - [[Translucent Wart of the Valiant]]
  - [[Reet Crafted Scale Biter]]
