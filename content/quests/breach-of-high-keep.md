## Steps

In [[High Keep: The Bloodless Incursion (Contested)]]

*"There is another threat in High Keep, one that cannot be allowed to extend beyond its walls...*

Destroy a large number of undead roaming the upper levels of the keep

- (0/100)

Destroy a large number of liches within the keep's mines

- (0/100)

Vanquish elite undead from the keep

- (0/8)

### Note

- This quest is noted as 100lv but you can hail to [[Veehzz the Bloodwing]] when you are 92lv. Those who under 95lv can't be shared this quest
