## Prerequisites

- You must have at least 15,000 faction with [[Residents of Teren's Grasp (Faction)|Residents of Teren's Grasp]] to be offered this quest.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Karnor's Castle]] and kill [[Xalgoz (Karnor's Castle)‎|Xalgoz]]. {{waypoint -140, -90, -4}}
1. Return to [[Teren Rysis]] for your reward. {{waypoint 2219, 529, -1009}}

## Rewards

- At least 31g 73s 78c
- +5000 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- One of the following:
  - [[Earring of Legend]]
  - [[Earring of Displaced Time]]
  - [[Displaced Earring of Dreams]]
  - [[Earring of Lost Memories]]
