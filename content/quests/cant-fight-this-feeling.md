## Steps

1. Loot the following items from chests that drop from any mob in [[Shard of Love: A Moment of Valor]], [[Nektropos Castle: Love's Errand]], [[Shard of Love: Seeding Corruption]] or [[Shard of Love: Weeding Corruption]].<br> ***NOTE:*** It is possible NOT to get all three quest drop updates in a single run through the instance. Be prepared to go to different instances and farm.
   1. [[White Feather Fletching]]
   1. [[Iron Heart Arrowheads]]
   1. [[Exquisite Leather Quiver]]
1. Speak with one of the following NPCs randomly assigned to you, to turn in the quest:
   - [[Priestess Thumde]] in [[Frostfang Sea|New Halas]] {{waypoint 57, 148, -44}}
   - [[Priest Minka]] in [[The Commonlands]] {{waypoint 592.32, -48.09, 717.33}}
   - [[Priest Elajeon]] in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint -728, -15, 242}}

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Huntress' Mounted Quiver]]
  - 5 [[Erollisi coin]]
