## Steps

Defeat all encounters in [[Mahngavi Wastes: Phantasmal Shades (Solo)]]

1. Lay out the Leader, [[Bartus Morrand]] {{waypoint 423, 28, -354}}
1. Destroy the devourer, [[Soul Eater Muhg]] {{waypoint 245, 26, -246}}
1. Slay the spirit, [[Dremaera Forsaken]] {{waypoint 56, 32, -296}}
1. Conquer the corrupted, [[Rune Protector Gorok]] {{waypoint -270, 35, -778}}
1. Unsummon the summoner, [[Esmerelda Everghast]] {{waypoint -617, 16, -366}}
