## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill the following
   - Kill 6 [[A glade strider|glade striders]] or [[A fiery drakefly|fiery drakeflies]].
     - *Many in the [[Scaleclaw Killing Fields]] around {{waypoint 596, 60, 73}} and [[Deino Shores]] around {{waypoint -59, 6, 547}}.*
   - Kill 8 [[A deepwood ravager|deepwood ravagers]] in the [[Deepwood Wilds]] around {{waypoint -368.57, 17.48, -10.37}}.
1. Return to [[Jungle Quintessence]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- Choice of:
  - [[Vine-braided Necklace of Insight]]
  - [[Jungle-blessed Necklace of Stratagem]]
