## Steps

1. Talk to [[Mushien Lou]] and recite a poem (perhaps in the style of Haiku).
   - Click the first options to advance for completion.
   - Other options work out too -as long as they fit together... try it out!

Waving to the wind...
...fans of green turn gold with time...
...they fall with the sun.
