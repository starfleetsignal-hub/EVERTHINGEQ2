## Steps

1. Speak with [[Berry Mistletoe]]. {{waypoint -4.45, -5.31, -69.45}}
1. Gather 5 *self-delivering gifts* outside the workshop, on the floating ice sheets.
1. Return to Berry Mistletoe.
1. Find the lost gifts at three of the following locations (chosen randomly):
   - [[Antonica]] {{waypoint -2198, -24, -692}}
   - [[Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint 212.47, 12.60, 1.35}}
   - [[Moors of Ykesha]] {{waypoint 1782, 452, 950}}
   - [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]] {{waypoint -325, 175, -521}}
   - [[Great Divide]] {{waypoint -106, -349, 52}}
   - [[Kylong Plains]] {{waypoint 1620, 460, -804}}
1. Return to Berry Mistletoe.

- One of the Following:
  - [[Candy Cane Crown of Joy]]
  - [[Peppermint Candy Crown of Gloom]]
  - [[A Gift-full Frostfell Cube]]
