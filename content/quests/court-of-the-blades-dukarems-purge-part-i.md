## Steps

1. Speak to the [[Oracle of Embre'sha]] in [[The Pillars of Flame]] at {{waypoint 435, -56, -589}}. You have to climb a wall at {{waypoint 436, -76, -587}} to get to her.
1. Kill the following mobs for the Oracle of Embre'sha:
   - 30 [[A desert prophet|desert prophets]] or [[A desert lunatic (Pillars of Flame)|desert lunatics]] {{waypoint 607, -114, -1113}}
   - [[Raja the Sunspeaker]]
     - To spawn Raja, kill 4 groups of [[A parched desert prophet|parched desert prophets]] and [[A parched desert lunatic|parched desert lunatics]] at {{waypoint 776, -118, -880}}. These also count towards the 30 kills.
1. Return to the Oracle.
1. Return to [[Dukarem, Caliph of the Blades|Dukarem]].
1. Kill the following Blades captains throughout the [[Desert of Ro]]:
   - [[Captain Yuleed]]: [[Maj'Dul]] {{waypoint -30, 177, -88}}
   - [[Captain Fayiz]]: [[Maj'Dul]] {{waypoint -230, 161, -153}}
   - [[Captain Faaman]]: [[The Sinking Sands]] {{waypoint -858, -125, -278}}
   - [[Captain Amruq]]: [[The Sinking Sands]] {{waypoint -1448, -225, -368}}
   - [[Captain Ihmin]]: [[The Pillars of Flame]] {{waypoint 320, -86, -763}}.
1. Return to Dukarem.
