## Steps

1. Visit [[The Hole (POI EL)|The Hole]], north of [[Deputy Stoutgut]] {{waypoint 147, 2, -527}}
   - [[A Runnyeye warmonger]] or [[A Runnyeye scout]] will spawn and attack you, but you are not required to kill him to continue.
1. Return to [[Deputy Stoutgut]]
1. Collect 10 pieces of torn parchment from the halflings in the [[Seamist Plains Village]] {{waypoint 413, 0, -508}}
   - [[Threatening commoner|a threatening commoner]], [[Defiled villager|a defiled villager]], [[Withering villager|a withering villager]], etc...
1. Return to [[Deputy Stoutgut]]

Fun Fact: [[Deputy Crookfellow (Enchanted Lands)]] {{waypoint 235, 10, -461}}will count as a quest monster.

## Rewards

At least 27s 67c

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Crystal of Haunted Visions]] (All Mages)
  - [[Eagle's Talon Barb]]  (All Scouts)
  - [[Runed Sapphire Pendant]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Token of Worldly Gods]] (All Priests)
