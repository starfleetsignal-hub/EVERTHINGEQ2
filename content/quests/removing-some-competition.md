- Requires at least 19,000 [[House Vahla (Faction)]] to get this quest to begin with as a repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill 18 [[Cnidcara]] in the [[Aetherscar]]. found around {{waypoint 732.37, -12.70, -62.09}}.
1. Return to [[Jihyon]].

- At least 90g 72s
- +1250 faction with **House Vahla**
- -850 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -850 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
