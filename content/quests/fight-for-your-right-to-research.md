## Steps

1. Defeat 4 [[A void assaulter|void assaulters]].
   - Go to {{waypoint -436, 396, 510}} and wait for army to spawn. There will be about 10-12 [[A void assaulter|void assaulters]], grab your own and kill them. Respawn time of the army is roughly 4 minutes.
1. Return to [[Defender Ruhi]].
