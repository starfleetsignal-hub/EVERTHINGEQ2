After completing Wind in the Willow Wood, Rupert Valorbound will still be following you.  He doesn't have a quest marker over his head, but talk to him to start the quest anyway. If Rupert Valorbound is no longer following you, simply return to him in Qeynos Province District, near the entrance to Castleview Hamlet.

## Steps

1. Locate [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn]] - it's in Qeynos Harbor near the door to South Qeynos.
1. Bring a drink to [[Tom Sterling]]
   - Click the ale stein in front of [[Birney Abbot]], then the bar counter in front of Tom, then speak to him.
1. Bring a drink to [[Leila Fleetfoot]] (same steps as above, only click on the table in front of her)
1. Bring a drink to [[Belle Madson]] (same steps as above)
1. Subdue [[Thomas Dedry]]
1. Speak to [[Halc Emberstone]]
1. Walk over to [[Logan Camerus]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g @ lvl 95
