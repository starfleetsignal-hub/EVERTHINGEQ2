## Steps

Kill the following enemies

- Massacre the Master : Kill [[Axxyk'Tuur]] in [[The Obelisk of Blight]] (Lesser Faydark)
- Destroy the Destructor : Kill [[The Skeletal Destructor]] in [[Shard of Fear]] (Feerrott)
- Kill the Count : Kill [[Count Valdoon Kel'Novar]] in [[The Crypt of Valdoon]]  (Loping Plains)

Or

- Eradicate the arachnid : Kill [[Spinechill Venomfang]] in [[Shard of Fear]] (Feerrott)
- End the Emperor :  Kill [[Emperor D'Vinn]] in [[The Acadechism]] (Greater Faydark)
- Bleed the Bloodbourne :  Kill [[Gretalik the Bloodbourne]] in [[The Obelisk of Blight]] (Lesser Faydark)

Or

- Off the Originator: Kill [[Irodal the Originator]] in [[Crypt of Valdoon]] (Loping Plains)
- Kill the croc : Kill [[Terrock]] in [[Shard of Fear]] -- *Lead the pony over.* (Feerrott)
- Still the Animator : Kill [[Kelzaral the Animator]] in [[The Acadechism]] (Greater Faydark)

## Notes

The Hunter's missions can be shared with others in the group. There is no confirmation message when sharing the mission, all eligible members of the group will immediately receive the quest.

Mission updates for killing a required target are zone-wide in instanced zones. Individual group members do not need to be near the target when it is killed; the only requirement is that they must be in the same instance as other members of the group.
