[[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep#Item Triggered Quests|Item Triggered Timed Quests]] in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]] reward a [[Dirt encrusted key]]. Unpacking that key yields a status loot item, and one of the following:

- [[An ancient gold key]] unlocks a Gold Chest, which rewards advanced solo quality gear.
- [[An ancient platinum key]] unlocks a Platinum Chest, which rewards heroic quality gear.
- [[An ancient umbrite key]] unlocks an Umbrite Chest, which rewards raid quality gear.
  - *[[Ancient silver engravings]] don't open chests, they can only be sold for status.*<br><br>

These chests spawn randomly in various locations throughout [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]].

- The [[Tracking]] ability can be used to locate these chests.
- *If your class/race can't track, consider hiring a mercenary with tracking and have him track for you.*
- Chests show up even (white) at level 100. You can also just filter for "chest".<br><br>
