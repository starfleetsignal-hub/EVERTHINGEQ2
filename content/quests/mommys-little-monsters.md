## Steps

1. Kill the following mobs in [[Doom's Morass]]: {{waypoint 216, -120, 1007}}
   - [[Sporconid Den Mother]]
   - 6 [[A sporconid sporeling|sporconid sporelings]]
     - To spawn the sporelings, walk over the small non-targetable mushrooms around this location.
1. Return to Fisher Ozrid.

## Rewards

- At least 9g 46s 1c
- +2000 faction with **Kunzar Jungle Villagers**
- [[Starblast Mageblade]]
