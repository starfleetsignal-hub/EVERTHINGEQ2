## Description

Enemies are all around, from the great gateway within Stygian Threshold, to the immolated invaders within Maldura. It is time to put these enemies to rest, once and for all!

## Steps

1. Slay the spirit of the gate
   - [[Bagarash]] in [[Stygian Threshold: The Howling Gateway (Advanced Solo)]]
1. Thwart the invasion
   - [[Black Bolt]] in [[Maldura: Algorithm For Destruction (Advanced Solo)]]
