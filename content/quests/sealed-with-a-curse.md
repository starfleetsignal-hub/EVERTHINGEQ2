## Steps

1. Set the Assembled sigil stone in the Sigil plate/
   - Click on Sigil Plate at {{waypoint -550, 11, 430}}
1. Enter [[Buried Takish'Hiz: Foundation of Knowledge (Solo)]] at [[Goldami Reservoir Camp]] at {{waypoint -553.85, 11.72, 427.98}} to gather and kill the following:
   - *See the walkthrough on the instance's page for detailed walkthrough steps for the dungeon.*
   - Gather 8 **apotropaic figures**. Some possible locations include:![Apotropaic Figure](images/Apotropaic_Figure.png)
     - {{waypoint 23.26, 32.04, -39.65}} On the walk-way at the entrance of the zone
     - ( 52, 31, -21 )
     - {{waypoint 17.16, 28.69, 53.03}}  [[Antechamber of Antiquity]]
     - {{waypoint -41.21, 28.69, 60.47}} [[Antechamber of Antiquity]]
     - {{waypoint -270, 39, 191}} [[Trevaluen, the Branching Gateway]], South Chamber
     - {{waypoint -320, 40, 136}} [[Trevaluen, the Branching Gateway]], East Chamber
     - {{waypoint -295.36, 40.12, 104.15}} [[Trevaluen, the Branching Gateway]], North-East Chamber
     - {{waypoint -157.90, 33.15, 181.44}} [[Sanctum Arcanum]], Entrance Hall
     - {{waypoint -83.72, 33.17, 201.95}} [[Sanctum Arcanum]], Entrance Hall
     - {{waypoint -68.09, 31.32, 215.77}} [[Sanctum Arcanum]], [[Alchemy Wing South]]
     - {{waypoint -122.97, 31.32, 135.41}} [[Sanctum Arcanum]], [[Arcane Wing North]]
   - Hunt 14 [[A cursed Elddar|cursed Elddar]] for protective amulets.
   - Obtain the [[Theosophist's Scroll of Aversion]]. It can be on the floor or on a shelf in the final room [[Istueth, the Takish Archives]]. Some possible locations include: ![Theosophist's Scroll of Aversion](images/Theosophist's_Scroll_of_Aversion.png)
     - {{waypoint 155.21, 55.30, 213.39}}
     - {{waypoint -16.12, 55.81, 204.74}} Second shelf.
1. Return to Shawah.
