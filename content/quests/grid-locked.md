## Prerequisites

You need -10000 faction with Defenders of the Seal

## Steps

Malik, a Defender of the Seal, has asked me to assist the Defenders by setting up sensor beacons within Dartains Fortress

1. I must place the four sensor beacons in Dartain's Fortress, Defenders of the Seal portion of The Hole
   1. I must place the 1st sensor beacon {{waypoint 433, 121, -263}} in the pool.
   1. I must place the 2nd sensor beacon {{waypoint 536, 121, -314}} in the room with the exhausted battlemages.
   1. I must place the 3rd sensor beacon  in the garden {{waypoint 665, 126, -227}}
   1. I must place the 4th sensor beacon {{waypoint 565, 121, -72}} in the Observatory where [[Skygazer Mon'Keed]] is.

## Rewards

1. A [[Mark of Manaar]]
1. At least 55g 70s 61c
1. +2500 faction with **Defenders of the Seal**
