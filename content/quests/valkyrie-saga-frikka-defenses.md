## Notes

- While the in-game information specifically lists a Season 2 Overseer reward chest as the reward, the player will always receive a Treasured [[Overseer Reward Crate|reward chest]] *(plus a Legendary [[Overseer Bonus Reward Crate|bonus chest]] on a crit)* from whatever the highest Overseer season has been unlocked on that player's account, instead.
