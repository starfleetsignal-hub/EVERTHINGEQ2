## Prerequisites

- Must be at least level 20 to be offered the quest.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Thundering Steppes]].
1. Venture to the southern exit of [[Shattered Gully]] that leads to [[Dead River Basin]]. There you will see the shattered trunk of a great tree with a sapling growing out of the top. Quest will auto-update when you get close. {{waypoint 616, 20, 411}}
1. Return to Oracle Ulinara.

## Rewards

- At least 11s 40c
- [[Gift of the Golden Acorn]]
