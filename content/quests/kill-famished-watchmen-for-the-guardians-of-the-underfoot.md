## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A famished watchman|Famished Watchmen]] in the [[Old Paineel]] ([[Caertaxian Legion (Faction)|Caertaxian Legion]]) section of [[The Hole]]. They can be found in the outdoor kitchen, immediately to the right after descending the first climbable wall near the entrance.
1. Speak to Gleb in [[The Tower of Serilis]] section of [[The Hole]]

## Rewards

1. At least 20g 70s 61c
1. +500 faction with **Guardians of the Underfoot**
