## Description

The sages of Firmroot are interested in the methodology employed by the Ykeshan guards to keep the many froglok captives in line. I am to search throughout the Outer Stronghold of Ykesha for any odd tools that the Ykeshans could be employing to maintain their control over the cursed frogloks.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Ruins of Guk: Ykesha's Outer Stronghold]] then seek out the instruments of subjugation used by the trolls
1. You need to find 3 items, but its different for everyone
   - An item used to punish the frogloks and break their will
     - Collect a Branding Iron at {{waypoint 71, -18, -105}} or
     - Collect a whip at {{waypoint 90, 12, -212}} or
     - Collect a pair of sharpened tongs {{waypoint 54, -8, -261}}
   - Something that might pacify or weaken the frogloks
     - Collect an incense at {{waypoint 29, -16, -86}} or
     - Collect a pile of poison food at {{waypoint 3, -7, -211}} or
     - Collect a globe at {{waypoint 127, -18, -73}}
   - See if the trolls are also using mental tactics
     - Collect Idol of Marr {{waypoint 66, -17, -105}} or
     - Collect a bloodied piece of cloth at {{waypoint 54, -8, -264}} or
     - Collect a flayed froglok skin at {{waypoint 122, -8, -242}}
   - Eliminate some of the guards that use the instruments of subjugation
1. Return to Vegupa Marrsheart.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
