***This quest is only available to players under level 30***

## Steps

![A Settler's tent and the mouseover message you see to burn it.](images/Sabotage_-_Lost_Without_A_Light.jpg)

1. Burn 5 settler tents. These tents randomly spawn along the west coast of Antonica in groups of 1 to 3 tents. Tents have Been Found at the following locations:
   - {{waypoint -314, -12, -357}}
   - {{waypoint -246, -11, -412}}
   - {{waypoint 46.00, -14.00, 176.00}}
   - {{waypoint 350.35, -22.59, 618.19}}
   - {{waypoint -295, -18, 681}}
1. Return to [[Gol M'Tun]].

## Rewards

- Faction Only:
  - +1000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
  - -1000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
  - -1000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**

## Notes

You can use the [[Mentoring]] system to perform this quest if you are too high level. You are not required to stay mentored once you have the quest active.
