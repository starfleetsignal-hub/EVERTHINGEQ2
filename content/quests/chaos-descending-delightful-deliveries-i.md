## Notes

*A new tradeskill mission is available every 18 hours.*

## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 15 [[Celestial Kindling]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest the following within [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]]:
   - 25 [[Basilisk meat]] from [[Basilisk lair]]
   - 10 [[Deadly terrorfruit]] from [[Celestial nursery]]
   - 10 [[Flitterfin tang]] from [[School of tangs]]
1. Craft a [[Tangy Cheese Roll]] at a [[Stove and Keg]].
   - There is a [[Stove and Keg]] on the first floor of the Library in the [[Myrist Commissary]] at {{waypoint 25, -20, 74}}.
1. Speak with [[Cookie]] at {{waypoint 25, -20, 74}} in the [[Myrist Commissary]]

## Rewards

- At least 48000 status
- [[Elemental Mission Reward Crate]]
