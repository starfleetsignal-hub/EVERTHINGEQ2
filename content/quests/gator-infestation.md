## Steps

1. Go to the entrance to Grobb {{waypoint -197, -82, -902}} and await the alligator infestation.
   - Almost immediately you will be attacked by an affronted swamp feeder and a vengeful swamp feeder. Followed by an irate swamp feeder and a wrathful swamp feeder. Followed by an angry swamp feeder, a furious swamp feeder and an enraged swamp feeder.  All three encounters are level 79 mobs.
1. Return to Groluk.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2,000 faction with **Clan Grobb**
