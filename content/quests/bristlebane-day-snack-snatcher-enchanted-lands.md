## Steps

You can not be on a [[Mount|mount]] when you start this race, so dismount before you step on the pad.

1. Step on the Bristlebane Snack Snatcher starter pad to begin {{waypoint 63.26, -0.86, 27.72}}
1. Collect as many Bristlebane Day Snacks as possible in the time allotted. (3 minutes)
1. Return to Charlotte O'Piesmasher, the Snack Master, officiating over this competition, to discuss the results.

You will be automatically teleported back to the starting point, near Charlotte O'Piesmasher when the timer runs out.
**Strategy tips:**

- Red snacks give you points, but do not increase your speed.
- The gold snacks increase you speed.
- The purple snacks slow you down.

Avoid the purple snacks and try to get gold snacks as often as you can, so you gather more snacks overall before the timer runs out.

## Rewards

**When you complete the race you can choose [[Fool's Gold Coin]] (event currency) or one of the prizes listed below, based on how many snacks you collect before the timer runs out.**

- The amount of the Fool's Gold depends on how successful you are, but [[Free-To-Play|All Access]] members get double event currency. Therefore, an All Access member who chooses 5 Fool's Gold Coins (enormous prize) will actually receive 10.

- [[Fool's Gold Coin]] (at least 1)
- [[Merriment Julep (Alcoholic)]]
- [[Bristlebane's Party Peppers]]

### Big Prize

46-64 Snacks:

- [[Fool's Gold Coin]] (at least 2)

- [[Bristlebane's Party Peppers]]

65-84 Snacks:

- [[Fireworks: Bead Boomer]] - "fluff" charm with an effect

- [[Prankster's Confetti Boomer]] - "fluff" charm with an effect
- [[Merriment Julep (Alcoholic)]] - drink
- [[Bristlebane's Party Peppers]] -food
- [[Festive Bristlebane Fireworks Launcher]] - a firework effect for your house or guild hall
- [[Ornate Halfling Jug]] - [[Housing|house item]]
- [[Bovoch Matriarch]] - [[Housing|house item]] (pet)
