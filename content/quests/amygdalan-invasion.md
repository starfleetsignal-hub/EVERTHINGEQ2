## Notes

- Do not kill [[Vilucidae the Priest of Thule]] until you have explored all the areas and have read all the books.
- For a walkthrough of [[The Lyceum of Abhorrence]] and explanation of the bosses therein, click [[The Lyceum of Abhorrence|here]].

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Lyceum of Abhorrence]] {{waypoint 122, 236, 826}}. Explore the following areas:
   - First - [[The Corruption Chamber]]: south of the Chamber of Chants {{waypoint -53, 135, -419}}
   - Second - [[The Chamber of Eggs]]: room east of Lair of Fear {{waypoint -77, 157, -554}}
   - Third - [[Gnorbl's Chamber]]: south of Chamber of Feasting {{waypoint 79, 135, -416}}
   - Fourth - [[The Chamber of Feasting]]: room south of Chamber of War {{waypoint 110, 156, -524}}
   - Fifth - [[The Lair of Fear]]: first room after entrance {{waypoint 11, 157, -614}}
   - Sixth - [[The Chamber of Chants]] (aka the Mystic Chamber): south of the Chamber of Eggs {{waypoint -85, 157, -528}}
   - Seventh - [[The Sanctum of Fear (POI)|The Sanctum of Fear]]: level 2 {{waypoint 11, 100, -547}}
   - Eighth - [[The Chamber of War]]: room west of the Lair of Fear {{waypoint 105, 157, -554}}
1. Read the books - Each room on the east and western side of the upper part of the zone has a book in it.
   - First: Chamber/War {{waypoint 125, 157, -573}}
   - Second: Chamber/Feasting {{waypoint 117, 158, -490}}
   - Third: Chamber/Eggs {{waypoint -75, 157, -573}}
   - Fourth: Chamber/Chants {{waypoint -104, 157, -503}}
1. Kill [[Vilucidae the Priest of Thule]].
1. Return to to [[Zhivaz Ne'pasdar]] in [[Tenebrous Tangle]] to claim your reward.

## Rewards

  - [[Chain Tunic of Harla's Word]]
  - [[Light Breeches of Harla's Word]]
  - [[Leggings of Harla's Word]]
  - [[Plate Armor of Harla's Word]]
