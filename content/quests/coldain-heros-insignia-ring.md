This quest starts in the x2 version of Tower of Frozen Shadow.

## Steps

1. Gain entry into Tower of Frozen Shadow (x2)
1. Once inside, speak with [[Marpa Coldbrow]], the Coldain Bookkeeper on the 1st Floor.
1. Defeat the Librarian to open the doors to the outside rooms.
1. All 4 updates can be found in same room which is down the hall to the right closest to the start of Floor 2 (library). {{waypoint -104.64, 50.18, 58.70}}
   - Portrait (large wooden plank against wall) {{waypoint -119, 51, 75}}
   - Hairbrush (on table) {{waypoint -120, 52, 71}}
   - blue and gold Jewelry (on table) {{waypoint -121, 52, 69}}
   - sonnet (small paper on other table) {{waypoint -106, 51, 69}}
1. More than likely you'll have to clear the way down the hallway, i.e. the trash mobs that spawn if you touch them.
1. Report back to [[Marpa Coldbrow]] on the first floor.
1. She sends you to talk to the Dain inside the Thurgadin Throne Room {{waypoint 86, -277, 277}}
   - *Note : This can be done in a clear zone.*
