## Prerequisites

- You have to complete [[Diseased Chips Ahoy]] at least one time.
- The  Diseased bone chip collection orb on your server has to be filled at 100%.

## Note

- For more specific information on any sub-quest, visit their article.

## Steps

1. Find [[Garochi Mirt'dok]] {{waypoint 2189, 68, 1063}}, in the tower on the east side of the [[Gorowyn Beach Outpost]], and receive the sub-quest, [[Broker Nearly Killed Ya!]].
   - Pick up 3 reports in [[Mok Rent]] and 3 in [[Thulwun Station East]] on [[Chrykori Isle]]; return to Garochi Mirt'dok.  The reports are small scrolls found around the immediate area.
1. Head to [[Banba MacBryne]] {{waypoint 2341, 20, 1339}} on Gorowyn Docks and receive the sub-quest, [[Heart of Gold]].
   - Find 2 random crates on the dock and one in the water at {{waypoint 2359, -11, 1450}}; return to Banba
1. Head over to the [[Butcherblock Docks]] where you will find two more sub quests
   1. [[Bitsy Frizzleclang]] {{waypoint 633, 26, 613}} gives the sub-quest, [[A Bitsy Task to Complete]]:
      - Collect 10 Butcherblock metal scraps and 6 skeleton bones; return to Bitsy
   1. [[Arrerada Tarknenhi]] {{waypoint 744, 26, 594}} gives the sub-quest, [[Arrerada's Errands]]:
      - Collect 6 Carbonite clusters and 3 basilisk scales; return to Arreada.
1. Return to Evdokia

## Rewards

- At least 9g 46s 1c (tier 8 players)
- [[Evdokia's Cheese Cake]] (level/stats rewarded based on character level)
