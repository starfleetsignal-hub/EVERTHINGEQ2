## Notes

- **This quest is repeatable**.
- Elder Yelnar is standing on a rock in the water at {{waypoint -1325, -1088, -233}}  (behind the platform where you fight Dozekar).
- Originally it was essential to receive the quest before killing Dozekar and looting Yelnar's Binding Stone -- **this is no longer the case**. If you already have a binding stone just speak with Elder Yelnar to receive the quest, which will then automatically update.
- You need the following components to complete the recipe. An optional portable forge (tinkered or via TS AA) will allow you to craft the combine without leaving the zone:
  - [[Yelnar's Binding Stone]]
  - 5 [[Banyan root]]
  - 5 [[Bubinga lumber]]
  - 5 [[Metallic reptile hide]]
  - 5 [[Osmium cluster]]
  - 20 effulgent coal
- Only click on and *use* Yelnar's Research Prism (the reward from this quest) if you want to keep and use the [[Tradeskill Apprentices|tradeskill apprentice]], [[Elder Yelnar (Dracurion Mastercraftsman)]]. The research prism itself is fully tradable, but the stone it turns into *after* you use it is *no-trade*.

## Steps

1. Speak to Elder Yelnar and receive the quest -- this may be done either before or after you kill Dozekar.
1. Obtain [[Yelnar's Binding Stone]] by killing [[Dozekar the Cursed (Epic)|Dozekar the Cursed]]
1. Speak to [[Elder Yelnar]] again, and tell him you have retrieved his binding stone. When you get the option, say "I am ready", and he will give you the recipe [[Binding Stone Modification]]
1. Scribe the recipe [[Binding Stone Modification]] and use a Forge to craft [[Yelnar's Binding Stone of Shattering]] (this consumes the [[Yelnar's Binding Stone]] and 20 rare materials)
1. Return and speak to to [[Elder Yelnar]] and use [[Yelnar's Binding Stone of Shattering]] on him. Speak to him again to finish the quest, and get your Research Prism.

## Rewards

- [[Yelnar's Research Prism]]
- **Warning:** only click on and use Yelnar's Research Prism if you want to keep and use the [[Tradeskill Apprentices|tradeskill apprentice]], [[Elder Yelnar (Dracurion Mastercraftsman)]]. The research prism can be traded or sold on the [[Broker|broker]], but the stone it turns into *after* you use it is *no-trade*.
