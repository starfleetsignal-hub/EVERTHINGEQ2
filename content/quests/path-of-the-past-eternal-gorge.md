## Note

In order to be able to spawn [[Zarvonn's creation]] you have to self-mentor to level 25.

## Steps

1. Defeat [[Zarvonn's creation]] in the [[The Eternal Gorge|Eternal Gorge]]
1. Return to the Chronomagi Tasker

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Chronomancers**
- The option to purchase [[Replica: Zarvonn's Creation]] for 1p 8g from the [[Timeless Trader]]
