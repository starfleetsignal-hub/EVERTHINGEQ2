## Steps

1. You should pull one of the switches. You need the Warm Pair of Gloves from [[Trappin' and Tradin']] to operate the lever.
1. Return to [[Zobby]].
1. You should pull all of the switches. (Anybody in the raid with the gloves can pull them for this update.)
1. Return to [[Zobby]].
1. Find a way to open the ice encrusted doors by having raid members click all 6 levers simultaneously. You need the Warm Pair of Gloves from [[Trappin' and Tradin']] to operate the lever.
   - [[The Captain's Quarters]] {{waypoint 374, -16, -418}}
   - [[The Lieutenants Quarters]] {{waypoint 73, -16, -418}}
   - [[The Colosseum of Rivalry]] {{waypoint 163, -29, -687}}
   - [[Throne of the Two Brothers]] {{waypoint -234, 3, -478}}
   - [[The Ice Palace]] {{waypoint 54, 3, -325}}
   - [[The Giant's Dwell]] {{waypoint -84, -15, -555}}

Note: This will also update [[Locate the Ice-olated Dragon]]

1. Return to [[Zobby]].
