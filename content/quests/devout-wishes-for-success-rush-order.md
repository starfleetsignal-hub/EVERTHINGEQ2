## Steps

Create 2 each of any 3 from the random list below:

- [[Carbonite Brigandine Gloves|Forged Carbonite Brigandine Gloves]]
- [[Carbonite Plate Gauntlets|Forged Carbonite Plate Gauntlets]]
- [[Carbonite Vanguard Gauntlets|Forged Carbonite Vanguard Gauntlets]]
- [[Carbonite Brigandine Coat|Forged Carbonite Brigandine Coat]]
- [[Carbonite Plate Cuirass|Forged Carbonite Plate Cuirass]]
- [[Carbonite Vanguard Cuirass|Forged Carbonite Vanguard Cuirass]]
- [[Carbonite Brigandine Leggings (Crafted) (Level 20)|Forged Carbonite Brigandine Leggings]]
- [[Carbonite Plate Greaves|Forged Carbonite Plate Greaves]]
- [[Carbonite Vanguard Greaves|Forged Carbonite Vanguard Greaves]]
- [[Carbonite Reverent Leggings|Forged Carbonite Reverent Leggings]]
- [[Carbonite Devout Cuirass|Forged Carbonite Devout Cuirass]]
- [[Carbonite Devout Greaves|Forged Carbonite Devout Greaves]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 12s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
