This quest is part of a 6-quest series; the first five can be done in any order.

## Steps

1. Harvest [[Root Nodes#T3|rancid roots]] until you obtain 10 updates.
1. Kill [[An immense tree snake|immense tree snakes]] until you obtain six vials of venom. These are found near D'Morte Burial Tomb, off the beach path. {{waypoint 651, -3.73, -553}}.
1. Harvest [[Bush Nodes#T3|polluted natural gardens]] until you obtain 20 quest updates.
1. Return to [[Kleron Asana]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Earstud of Condensed Mist]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Facet of Condensed Mist]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Spike of Condensed Mist]] (All Priests)
  - [[Stud of Condensed Mist]] (All Mages)
