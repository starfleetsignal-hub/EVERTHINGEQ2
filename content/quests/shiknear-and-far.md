## Steps

1. Travel to the Shik'Nar Caves near {{waypoint 265.32, 39.66, 196.31}} and place survey geodetic marks.
   - Place the first geodetic survey mark at {{waypoint 636.11, 30.59, 133.33}}.
   - Place the second geodetic survey mark at {{waypoint 934.53, 4.19, 144.12}}.
   - Place the third geodetic survey mark at {{waypoint 1175.37, 52.20, 368.27}}.
   - *Note: There are puddles on the ground that can be clicked to obtain [[Shik'Nar Glop]] which makes the Shik'Nar non-hostile for about 3 minutes or until attacked. (Cannot click on the puddles until your current 'Glop' buff expires) Easily doable with bard Run-Speed or 'Totem of the Deadly Sabertooth' run speed buffs (unless you get distracted by shiny collecting)* You can also return to the quest giver, speak to her, and it will refresh your quest icon for the Glop. You can travel back to get glop from the NPC if Needed
1. Return to [[Shana Burely]] at {{waypoint -764.32, 52.08, 332.02}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Shik'Nar Chitin Cape]]
- [[Geodetic Survey Mark]]
