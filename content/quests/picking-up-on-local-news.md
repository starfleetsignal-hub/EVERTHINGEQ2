## Steps

1. Deliver Cookies to [[Mistress Thora]] at {{waypoint 90, 150, -92}} in the Storage and Containment building.
1. Deliver Cookies to [[Berguff Fullbeard]] at {{waypoint -118, 150, -90}} in Glacierbane's Vault.
1. Deliver Cookies to [[Britta Brightsky]] at {{waypoint -21, 150, 6}} in the Ice Breaker banquet hall.
1. Deliver Cookies to [[Grokka Hopson]] at {{waypoint -157, 150, 22}} in the Hopson abode.
1. Retun to [[Envoy Tami Swifthammer|Tami]].
