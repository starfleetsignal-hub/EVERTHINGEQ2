## Steps

- Destroy 10 Renda'Dal camp provisions in the Elddar Exchange.
- Destroy 8 Wards of Dawn beehives![Ward of Dawn beehive](images/Ward_of_Dawn_beehive.png)
  - These spawn all around the Den of Dawn on the walls.
- Shatter 6 metamorphic watchers and demolishers in the Elddar Exchange.
- Kill 12 members of the Wards of Dawn in the Den of Dawn

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c

All of these items:

- 2 [[Imperial Adventurer's Mint]]
