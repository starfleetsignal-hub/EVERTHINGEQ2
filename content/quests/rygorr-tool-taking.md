## Steps

You must have completed [[Below The Frozen Waves]] from [[Thirin Veliumdelver]] to begin this quest.

1. Travel down to [[Demon's Delve]] and take 6 Ry'Gorr pick axes( around {{waypoint 244, 53, 48}})
1. Return to [[Odon Scourgeson]]

## Rewards

- At least 5s 81c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Knight's Pauldrons]] (Crusader, Warrior)
  - [[Channeler's Shoulderpads]] (Mage)
  - [[Shade's Shoulderpads]] (All Scouts)
