## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[An ire minion|ire minion]]s - in any alcove throughout the zone, usually near the start of the dungeon.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 8 [[A deathly scarab (Crypt of Betrayal)|deathly scarabs]] - in any tunnel in the zone.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 [[A diseased corpse|diseased corpse]]s - halls and rooms from [[Cryptkeeper Maglus]] to [[Vegalys Keldrane]] {{waypoint 126, 7, -102}} and onwards towards [[Vargon]] {{waypoint 148, 7, -51}}.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 8 [[A restless corpse|restless corpse]]s - same as diseased corpses.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 7 diseased corpses.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 restless corpses.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 8 diseased corpses.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 [[A tortured essence (Crypt of Betrayal)|tortured essences]] - upper level, between [[Vargon]] {{waypoint 148, 7, -41}} and [[Adun]] {{waypoint 127, 7, 11}}.
1. Read the book
1. Kill 7 tortured essences.
1. Read the book.

## Notes

- You can find all of the restless corpses and diseased corpses you need in the chamber at {{waypoint 117, 7, -96}}
- The beetles in the room share a spawn with the zombies so make sure to clear them out while you are hunting for zombies.
