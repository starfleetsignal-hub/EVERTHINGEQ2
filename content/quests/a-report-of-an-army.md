## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Visit the captured grazers in [[Kamp Krulkiel]] {{waypoint 273, 272, -275}}
   - Observe the Krulkiels undergoing strict military training in Kamp Krulkiel {{waypoint 299, 273, -442}}
   - Visit the pen of [[A war wolf|war wolves]] in Kamp Krulkiel {{waypoint 41, 271, -615}}
   - Visit the platoon of [[A Muckflick mercenary|Muckflick mercenaries]] in [[Kamp Krulkiel]] {{waypoint 41, 271, -515}}
   - Find out the equipment status on the Krulkiel in Kamp Krulkiel {{waypoint 323, 275, -508}}
1. Return to Gloln Kilnkor

## Rewards

- At least 22s 51c
- +1000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
