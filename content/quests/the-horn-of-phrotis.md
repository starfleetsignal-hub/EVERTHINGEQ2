## Steps

1. Listen to what General Fandrak has to say.
1. Enter [[Permafrost]] in [[Everfrost]] at {{waypoint 107, -4, -1143}}
1. Weaken the defense of Permafrost Keep by killing 10 Kromise Giants.
1. Find the [[Horn of Phrotis]] at {{waypoint 155.62, 6.8, -426.97}}.
1. Return to General Fandrak in the Ferrott for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Cavalier's Bloodstained Bracers]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Mentor's Dark Silk Wrist Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Primalist's Ghostly Chain Cuffs]] (Shamans)
  - [[Scholar's Spellwoven Cuffs]] (All Mages)
  - [[Thief's Blood-Dyed Vambraces]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Vicar's Sanctified Wristguard]] (Clerics)
  - [[Wanderer's Rare Hide Bands]] (Druids)
