## Prerequisites

- The character must have completed [[A Calling in the Forest]], or at least be on an certain step step of the quest. What step this is is unknown, so it it best to complete A Calling in the Forest beforehand.
- Many of the locations require access by completing steps of several other quests in the [[Nektropos Castle Timeline]].

## Steps

Consecutively visit the following locations:

1. Blacksmith Forge: {{waypoint -7, 0, -25}}
1. [[The Chapel (Nektropos Castle)|The Chapel]]: {{waypoint -34, -1, 53}}
1. [[The Servants' Quarters (Nektropos Castle)|The Servants' Quarters]]: {{waypoint -41, 0, -49}}
1. [[Nektropos Castle Game Room|The Game Room]]: {{waypoint 28, 1, 54}}
1. [[Everling Dining Hall]]: {{waypoint 16, 13, 51}}   Note:  This is upstairs.
1. [[The Library (Nektropos Castle)|The Library]]: {{waypoint -27, 13, 55}}
1. *Prince's Bedroom*: {{waypoint -29, 14, -29}}
1. [[Quarters of Lord Everling]]: {{waypoint 18, 14, -45}}
1. *Hidden Study*: {{waypoint -8, -14, -20}} (click the sconce on the west wall - opens a door on the south wall - candelabra to the right of that door on the inside gets you back out)
1. [[Lord Everling's Secret Laboratory]]: {{waypoint 173, -13, 11}}

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Ring of the House of Everling]]
  - [[Fleshbound Bracers]]
  - 2x [[Scalpel of Rikantus]]
