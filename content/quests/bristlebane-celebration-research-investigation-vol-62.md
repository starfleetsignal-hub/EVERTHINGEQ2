## Steps

Nothing is free and building, maintaining, and supporting the herald's camp is expensive. That's why the herald of Bristlebane has requested that we assist in offsetting the camp's cost by retrieving a valuable relic guarded by General Ra`Zaal.

1. Retrieve the relic guarded by General Ra`Zaal
   - Travel to [[Zimara Breadth]] and zone in to [[Vaashkaani: Argent Sanctuary (Solo)|Vaashkaani: Argent Sanctuary]] and clear all nameds in the zone.
     - The chest with the relic is at {{waypoint 107, 11, -340}}, behind the portal that appears after clearing the zone.
1. Return to the [[Herald to the Grand Prankster]] {{waypoint -832, 7, -203}} in [[Rivervale]]
