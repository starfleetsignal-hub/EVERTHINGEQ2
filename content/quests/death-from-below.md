## Notes

- If possible, also get the quest, [[Killing in the Name]], from [[Teren Rysis]] in [[Teren's Grasp]]. It requires that you have at least 15,000 faction with [[Residents of Teren's Grasp (Faction)|Residents of Teren's Grasp]]. The objective of 'Killing in the Name' is to kill [[Xalgoz (Karnor's Castle)|Xalgoz]], who is right next to the target of this quest.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Karnor's Castle]] and kill [[A Mistmoore emissary]] in [[Xalgoz's Study]]. {{waypoint -147, -90, -10}}
1. Return to [[Ebet Corscrag]] outside Karnor's Castle.

- [[Turnkey Cloak]]
