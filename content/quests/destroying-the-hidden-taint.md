## Steps

1. You are asked to complete one of the following tasks:
   - Destroy 10 **Rujarkian braziers** in [[The Clefts of Rujark]].
   - Slay 20 [[A Spiritbound apothecary|Spiritbound apothecaries]] in [[The Clefts of Rujark]].
   - Destroy 10 **Rin'Dal basins** in [[The Living Tombs]].
   - Slay 20 [[An Anuk' Sul chancellor (Living Tombs)|Anuk'Sul chancellors]] in [[The Living Tombs]].
1. Return to [[Ethri Vasou]] in [[North Qeynos (POI)|North Qeynos]].

## Rewards

- 8902 status
- +500 faction with **The Celestial Watch**
