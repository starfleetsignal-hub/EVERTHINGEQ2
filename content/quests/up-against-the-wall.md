## Steps

1. Do the following
   - Kill 10 [[A twisted hominid|twisted hominids]] - find them around and west of Malvonicus' Tower
   - Gather 15 **Useful Raw Materials** for a barricade - they're plentiful around {{waypoint 989, 12, 1342}}![Raw Materials](images/Raw_Materials.jpg)![Raw Lumber](images/Raw_Lumber.jpg)
1. Return to Overseer Clackwhinge
1. Place 3 barriers at the entrance to the cove of refuge - left click on the red translucent barriers under the arch. ![Barrier placement](images/Barrier_placement.jpg)
1. Fight off the [[A twisted aggressor|twisted aggressor]]s.
  - You might need to move away from camp toward tower and back into camp to convince aggressors to show.
1. Return to Clackwhinge

## Rewards

- At least 1p 29g 60s
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Shipswain Chain Greaves (QuestReward)|Shipswain Chain Greaves]] (Chain Armor)
  - [[Shipswain Greaves (QuestReward)|Shipswain Greaves]] (Cloth Armor)
  - [[Shipswain Leather Pants]] (Leather Armor)
  - [[Shipswain Plate Chausses (QuestReward)|Shipswain Plate Chausses]] (Plate Armor)
- Ability to fly near [[Malvonicus' Folly]]
