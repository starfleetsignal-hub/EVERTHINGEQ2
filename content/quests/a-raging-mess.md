## Steps

1. Kill [[The Beguiler]] {{waypoint 201, -15, 118}} and the [[Master of the Guard (Runnyeye)|Master of the Guard]] {{waypoint 193, -40, -148}}
1. Return to Zolbuk

The pre-requisites to this quest seem to be (1) Runnyeye Filth x 10; (2) Even More Filth x 10; (3) Runnyeye Filth a random number of times with minimum 8 and maximum 11x before the Janitor will offer A Raging Mess. There may be a little more "random" in the 10x quests as well. It's easy to lose count.
