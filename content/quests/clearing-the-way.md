## Description

"Zephyre Violethair, a wood elf in Kelethin, is bitter about the havoc wreaked upon Kelethin by the orcs. She views their presence so close to the city as a threat and wants them slain so that she can honor her ancestors' memory." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay 6 orcs on [[Orc Hill]]
1. Return to [[Zephyre Violethair]]

## Rewards

- At least 1s 5c
- [[Bezel-set Citrine Ring (Version 2)|Bezel-set Citrine Ring]]
- [[Zephyre's Leather Cuirass (Version 2)|Zephyre's Leather Cuirass]]

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Zephyre Violethair: "Hi! Do you have a moment to help me out?"
- Player Character: "I might."
- Zephyre Violethair: "My family has always lived in Kelethin and I'd like to honor their memories. The problem I'm having is those darn orcs! They send out their scouts and disrupt me at my prayers. I'd like to have them cleared out so that I can finish the ritual to honor my ancestors in peace."
- Player Character: "I'd be glad to help."
- Zephyre Violethair: "Could you? That's great! Though the Feir'Dal are not as prominent here as we once were, we still keep to the old ways as much as we can. You have no idea what your help means to me."
- Player Character: "I'll do my best."

By slaying some of these orcs beneath Kelethin, I will give Zephyre some peace of mind as she conducts her remembrances of her ancestors below the city. There are several orcs beneath Kelethin on Orc Hill. They need to be removed.

I've slain the orcs near Kelethin. I've removed some of the orcs beneath Kelethin. I must bring word of my success to Zephyre Violethair in Kelethin. Zephyre Violethair is in the building near the Old Kelethin Acorn Lift.

- Zephyre Violethair: "Have you dealt with those awful orcs?"
- Player Character: "Yes, I have."
- Zephyre Violethair: "May Tunare bless you! I know these coins aren't much, but they're all I have."
- Player Character: "No problem. I'm glad to have helped."

I've spoken with Zephyre Violethair. Zephyre Violethair was pleased with my work.

I got rid of several orcs beneath Kelethin so that Zephyre Violethair can visit the hills her ancestors once roamed.
