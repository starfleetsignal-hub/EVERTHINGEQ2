## Steps

![](images/A_grey_geode.jpg)

1. Kill eight non-agro [[A grey geode]] (for example at {{waypoint -836, 11, -1153}} or {{waypoint -892, 10, -1213}}) On death, each drops a crystal core. Right-click and "Gather crystal core" for the update.
1. Return to [[Researcher Aillena Belzia]]
1. Throw the [[Vial of Grim Dealings]] at 8 grimlings
   - *Note: combine that step with the Quest [[Deep in the Lost Shadows]], but remember to hit the Grimlings at least once before using the vial so that you get credit for both quests*
1. Return to [[Researcher Aillena Belzia]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g 80s
- [[Grim Platinum Trove]]
