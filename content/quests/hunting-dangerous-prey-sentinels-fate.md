## Steps

Kill the following enemies

- Vanquish the Corrupted Guardian : Kill [[Corrupted Guardian of the Seal]] in [[The Outer Vault]]
- Decimate the Dauntless : Kill [[Delahnus the Dauntless]] in [[Vasty Deep: The Conservatory]]
- Execute Taranis Gazerra : Kill [[Taranis Gazerra]] in [[Erudin Research Halls]]

Or

- Ice Gwarthlea, the Matriarch : Kill [[Queen Gwarthlea]] in [[Vasty Deep: The Vestigial Cella]]
- Vacate El'Arad : Kill [[El'Arad, Lord Primarch of Erudin]] in [[Royal Palace of Erudin]]
- Dispatch the Dark Walker : Kill [[Zonn Kogir]] in [[The Vigilant: Incursion]]

Or

- Dispatch Demitrik : Kill [[Demitrik]] in [[The Hole: Demitrik's Bastion]]
- Lay Low the Legion Lord : Kill [[Marus Xand]] in [[The Vigilant: Rescue]]
- End to the vision of the Visionary : Kill [[Perah'Celsis (Heroic)]] in [[Vasty Deep: The Abandoned Labs]]
