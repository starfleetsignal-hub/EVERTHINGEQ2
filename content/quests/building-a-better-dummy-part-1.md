## You will need:

- 1 [[Tussah root]]
- 1 [[Severed briarwood]]
- 1 [[Feyiron cluster]]
- 3 [[Glimmering sandpaper]]
  - Sold locally by [[Finbert's Salesbot]] at {{waypoint -584, 174, 963}}.

## Steps

1. Go to [[Adam Cogbuster]] who needs assistance. {{waypoint -651, 175, 1075}}
1. Return to [[Karalyn Cinderton]] who can sell you the recipes for target dummies. {{waypoint -525, 177, 1056}}
   - Buy [[Dummies For Dummies]] from [[Karalyn Cinderton]] and scribe it.
1. Make [[Sturdy Target Dummy|A Sturdy Target Dummy]] at the [[Woodworking Table]] located at {{waypoint -581, 175, 963}}.
   - Note: Do NOT craft the other dummies until the next quests in the line actually ask for them; having them already made in your inventory will not update the quests that require them and you will have to remake them.
1. Take [[Sturdy Target Dummy|A Sturdy Target Dummy]] to [[Jamie Sprocketbreaker]] who's next to [[Adam Cogbuster]] at {{waypoint -651, 175, 1075}}. (See following quest for completion of this sequence.)

## Rewards

- At least 3g 11s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
