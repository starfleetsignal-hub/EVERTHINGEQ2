## Description

*Many of the Shields of Maldura have perished defending its city from the ashen invaders, and many more have perished at the hands of the creatures of Kralet Penumbra. I shall take the fight to these enemies, and avenge the Shields that fell!*

## Steps

- Destroy enemy targets within Maldura and Kralet Penumbra
1. Slay the invaders of ash
1. Silence the one who speaks
