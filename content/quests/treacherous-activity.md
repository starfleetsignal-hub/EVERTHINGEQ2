Sister Leela Prendyn is willing to entertain Priest Brother Aaronolis' opinion, that love cannot be rushed, and the elevation of a prophet will come in time. But I would like to know what insight someone who's already been chosen as a prophet might have on the topic.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Sir Bayden Cauldthorn]] in [[Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint 169, 112, 95}}
   - Sir Bayden is actually in [[Kelethin]].
1. Speak with [[Squire Glurrp]] in the [[Commonlands]] {{waypoint 642, -55, 745}}
1. Investigate the suspicious activities within the ruins of Befallen.  A mob of 3 heroics (no ^ pips though) attacks you. {{waypoint 586, -61, 855}}
1. Enter the instance  [[Befallen:The Marr Devotary]] within Befallen, at the doors of [[Halls of the Forsaken]], {{waypoint 517, -71, 870}}
1. Cleanse The Marr Devotary of all Ullkorruuk followers.
   - Find a way to cross the chasm.
     - Collect the **longbow** and **quiver of arrows** {{waypoint 12, 1, 22}}
     - Collect **a coiled rope** {{waypoint -54, 1, 20}}
     - Collect **a ladder** {{waypoint -30, 0, -2}} and place it at {{waypoint -33, 0, 20}}.
     - Climb the ladder and attach the rope at the top.
     - Look across the chasm for a suitable place to shoot your arrow.  You can see the location from the ladder, but you need to get as close as possible to it so you don't get a "too far away" message.
     - Zipline across, click glowing area of the rope.
   - Slay the followers of Ullkorruuk.
     - Collect **sulfuric remains** from the slain.
   - Destroy the Archtraitor statue {{waypoint -74, -15, 145}}
     - Fill the four canopic jars at the corners of the statue with the sulfur.
     - Click one of the filled containers to blow up the statue.
1. Return to Sister Leela to receive your rewards.

## Rewards

- +5,000 faction with **Erollisi Marr**
- [[Summon Hunter's Companion]]
