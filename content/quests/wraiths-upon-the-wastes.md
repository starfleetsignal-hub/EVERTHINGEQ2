I can't help but reach for the portal. Why is it here? Who could have left it? This magic looks too sophisticated for orcs, although the Scourge are a much more intelligent breed. Could they have done this? Only one way to find out...

## Notes

- *This is a 7 minute timed quest.*
- Found in all versions of Zek, the Scourge Wastes:
  - [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Solo)]]
  - [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Heroic)]]

## Steps

1. Click on the **erratic portal** again.
1. Banish 5 [[A watchwraith|watchwraiths]] at the **destroyed towers**:
   - [[Green Hood Embers]] {{waypoint 455.83, -21.16, -415.66}} -- *You might want to evac after this one.*
   - Underwater, just off-shore at [[Green Hood Landing]] {{waypoint 7.01, -54.84, 485.50}}.
   - Underwater in [[Siren's Cove]] {{waypoint 126.93, -58.32, 498.46}}.
   - [[Breakneck Acre]] {{waypoint 305.69, -20.19, 280.35}}.
   - [[Timberlands]] {{waypoint -336.74, -14.34, 16.98}}.
   - At each tower, click the tower to spawn a [[A watchwraith|watchwraith]], then kill it.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Encrusted iron key]]
