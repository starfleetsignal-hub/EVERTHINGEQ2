## Steps

1. Venture to [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] to kill the the two tomes:
   - [[Tome of Life]] {{waypoint 14, 4, 70}}
   - [[Tome of Death]] {{waypoint 228, 0, 55}}
1. Return to [[Rune Shimmerstar]] for your choice of rewards.

## Notes

- In the past, players had to have [[Keep of Immortality Key]] equipped or in their inventory to receive this quest. However, this is no longer the case.
- [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]] citizens or characters of Evil-alignment can receive this quest.

## Rewards

Choice of:

- [[Robe of Life and Death]]
- [[Maul of Judgement]]
