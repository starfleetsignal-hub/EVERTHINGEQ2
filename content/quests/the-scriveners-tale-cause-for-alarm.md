## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 28 [[Dewpalm lumber]]
- 9 [[Reef cluster]]
- 18 [[Nimbic hide]]
- 50 [[Celestial Sandpaper]]

## Steps

1. Click on 5 **ill custodians** around the librarian quarters at {{waypoint 323.31, -33.63, 163.26}}.
   - Group-wide updates.
1. Click on **a diseased custodian's corpse** at {{waypoint 293, -35, 209}}.![a diseased custodian's corpse](images/A_diseased_custodian's_corpse.jpg)
   - **NOT** group wide, each member needs to click.
1. Speak to [[Dylindra Morganna]] at {{waypoint 306, -35, 165}}.
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Containment Box]] and craft [[Empty Containment Box]] on a Woodworking Table.
   - There is a Woodworking table in [[Myrist, the Great Library]]. Go to the [[South Wing Catalog]] and go through the teleporter to the [[Smiths' Gallery]] at {{waypoint 388, 16, 170}}
1. Right-click on the [[Empty Containment Box]] in your inventory, choose Examine, then **Place the runestone into the box and seal it.**
1. Speak with [[Dylindra Morganna]] in the [[Myrist Librarian Quarters]] at {{waypoint 306, -35, 165}} and give her the [[Sealed Containment Box]].

## Rewards

- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
