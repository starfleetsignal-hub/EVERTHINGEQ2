## Steps

1. I need to collect soullight essences, which can be used to re-power the shattered core of the Tower of Umber, Zeeil said that the essences of any non-fear tainted soullights would do.
   - Collect essences from the [[A soullight|soullights]] hovering throughout the jungle canopy (6)
1. I need to collect the shattered pieces of the Tower of Umber's {{waypoint -965, 12, 505}} core. I must stay wary of the Fear creatures that intend it to stay broken...
   - Collect shattered pieces of the Tower of Umber's core (5)
     - Pick up 5 pieces of rubble around the tower, each one you collect will spawn 1-5 mobs to attack you.  You must defeat the mobs and then pick up the pieces again.
1. Now that I have both the essence and pieces needed, I should return to the [[Tower of Umber]] and reassemble its shattered core.
   - Repair the core using the shattered pieces and soullight essences
     - When you have enough pieces, you need to click on the powercell at the tower, which will create several waves of attackers that you need to fend off.
1. The Tower of Umber's core is powering up, but it's reactivation has incited the creatures of Fear. The core must be defended!
   - At this point, you have a two minute timer to defend the core from waves of mobs.  If the core's health reaches zero, the quest will require you to start again at the previous step.
1. Zeeil has traveled to meet me at the Tower of Umber. I should speak to him there.
   - Speak to Zeeil at the newly restored Tower of Umber
