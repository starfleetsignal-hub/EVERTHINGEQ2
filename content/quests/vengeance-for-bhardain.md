Bhardain, a dhalgar fighter who had left Maldura with his wolf, Shuck, was slain by the very foes from whom he was looking to defend his city. I cannot let Bhardain's death go unavenged!

## Steps

1. Slay 25 Aberrants near the [[Penumbra Expansion]]. {{waypoint 478, 30, -701}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- +1,000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
- +1,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
