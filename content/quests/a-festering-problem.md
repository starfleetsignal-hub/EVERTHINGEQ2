## Steps

1. Destroy the entire camp of [[Qeynos|Qeynosians]] that helped [[Kraofla]] survive your attack. They are located in the northern part of [[The Commonlands]] and are roughly level 30. {{waypoint 427, -25, -893}}
   - There are 3 [[A Qeynosian scout|Qeynosian scouts]] and 2 [[A Qeynosian protector|Qeynosian protectors]] that must be killed.
1. [[Kraofla]] will appear in the camp, destroy her.
1. Return to [[Intelligence Officer Gezlowe]] for your reward.
