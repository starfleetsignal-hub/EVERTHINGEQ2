Required: [[Druid]] level 25

## Steps

1. Return the blessed leaf to the [[Teleportation and Portals Trainers|portals trainer]] near the priest guild in your home town.
1. Hail the [[Teleportation and Portals Trainers|portals trainer]] again and use the portal that he will open for you to the druid ring in [[Moors of Ykesha]], or just run to the druid ring in Moors of Ykesha
1. Use the *Blessing of Growth: Moors of Ykesha* once inside the ring to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Circle of the Moors of Ykesha]]
