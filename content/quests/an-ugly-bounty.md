## Steps

1. Speak with [[Captain Sivisk]] at the top of [[Kylong Post]] tower. {{waypoint 508, 44, 1504}}
1. Go to [[Bathezid's Watch]] and speak with [[Flaxen]]. {{waypoint 1006, 207, 1234}}
1. Kill [[Murgurgl]] in the [[The Lake Ruins]] and automatically receive [[Supper shackle key]]. {{waypoint 473, -115, 567}}
   - Murgurg has a bad knockback, so don't fight too close to the edge of the water.
   - He is on a 72+ minute respawn timer.
1. Kill the four [[A hypnoglob|hypnoglobs]] around [[Threeko]]. {{waypoint 448, -108, 727}}
   - The hypnoglobs are on a 15 minute respawn timer.
1. Speak with Threeko.
   - If in a group, only one person can get this update at a time. Unfortunately, the next group member will have to wait 15 minutes for him to respawn.
1. Return to Flaxen.
1. Return to Captain Sivisk.
1. Obtain the lost coins:
   - **Important Note**: If your inventory is full you cannot harvest these coins and you will receive no message warning you of this. Your quest will not update. You will then not be able to click on the boxes and will require GM assistance to fix this.
   - [[Torsis Ton]]: Kill [[Scales]] in the [[Swamp of No Hope]]: {{waypoint -1252, -135, 1371}}, {{waypoint -1346, -143, 1401}}, {{waypoint -1515, -114, 1632}}, {{waypoint -1442, -117, 1571}}, or {{waypoint -1178, -143, 1464}}
   - [[Royal Crown of Dalnir]]: Examine a box in the northern [[Sarnshak Encampment]]: {{waypoint -992, -203, -967}}
   - [[Ik Gran]]: Examine a box inside the head of [[Jaled Dar's Remains]]: {{waypoint -1436, -371, -1488}}
   - [[Danak Gran]]: Examine a box next to [[Gregor Thunderfist]] in [[Camp Kromdek]]: {{waypoint -876, -292, -1904}}
   - [[Terraz Ton]]: Examine a box next to [[Protectis]] in [[Ruins of Cabilis]]: {{waypoint 364, 18, -1506}}
   - [[Veksar Gran]]: Kill [[Bisbing the Fuzzy]] in the [[Mines of Nurga]]: {{waypoint 99, 48, -2151}}
1. Return to Captain Sivisk.
1. Return to Flaxen.
1. Hail and defeat [[A shambling skeleton]] at the base of the ramp of [[Kurn's Tower]]. {{waypoint -1939, -371, -1919}}
1. Within 15-minutes, travel up the ramp and defeat four waves of [[A praetorian tracker|praetorian trackers]]. Each successive wave spawns a bit further up the ramp. At the top of the tower, defeat [[The Shadow Rider]].
1. Return to Flaxen.
1. Speak with [[Grand Acumen Ralosk]] in Bathezid's Watch. {{waypoint 920, 207, 1264}}

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Band of Thuuga]]
  - [[Bracelet of Thuuga]]
  - [[Earring of Thuuga]]
  - [[Signet of Thuuga]]
  - [[Talisman of Thuuga]]
  - [[Bangle of Thuuga]]
- As of 14th February, 2008, [[Grand Acumen Ralosk]] is back and is giving you a one time offer to exchange your old item for the  [[Bangle of Thuuga]] which gives now +Spell Damage instead of +Heal "if you completed the quest before this date". In order to perform this exchange you have to sell your previous reward to a merchant of your choice and then talk to Grand Acumen Ralosk to be offered a chance to buy the Bangle of Thuuga.
