## Description

The tragic events of Gynok Moltor's murderous betrayal continue to haunt the Halls of the Forsaken to this day. I've agreed to travel there in hopes that my allies and I can put an end to this residual haunting. To this end I must slay the Eidolon of Depravity!

## Steps

1. Defeat the [[The Eidolon of Depravity]] within [[Befallen: Halls of the Forsaken]]
1. Return to Ragheejb Yasir in the Moors of Ykesha.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 50g
- [[Void Shard]] x2
