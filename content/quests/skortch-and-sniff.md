## Steps

1. Hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 6 Blackhook sludgeweavers around the fort areas for goblin fetishes Around  *{{waypoint -50.27, 204.66, -555.57}}*
   - Gather 12 putrid shanks  from Blackhook Territory. They look like green Ham. (I found several in the huts.)
     - *Potential locations include  {{waypoint -17.95, 187.95, -551.13}} {{waypoint -74.72, 193.51, -580.50}} {{waypoint -66.54, 193.44, -573.71}} {{waypoint -47.56, 201.56, -473.89}}*
   - Hunt 6 Blackhook blotguards around the around fort areas for goblin armor pieces
1. Return to [[Skortch]] at {{waypoint 288.98, 283.15, -736.87}}

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
