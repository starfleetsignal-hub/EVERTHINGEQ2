## How to Start

![Portal while the PQ is NOT available](images/Ro_Portal_-_No_PQ.png)![Portal while the PQ is available](images/Ro_Portal_-_PQ.png)

- There is no announcement in the Chronoweve that the PQ is starting. The only indication that it has begun is the portal changing color as shown in the pictures.
- The PQ spawns every 3 hours and is available for 1 hour. In this hour it can be run many times as long as portal is active and there is enough players to zone in. Once PQ starts it will be allowed to finish even if 1 hour time is up.
- Minimum level to enter is level 10 adventurer.
- Enter the [[Public Quests|Public Quest]] by clicking portal to the [[Portal to the Past: Southern Desert of Ro (Public Quest)|Southern Desert of Ro]] in [[The Chronoweave]] while PQ is available and selecting [[Portal to the Past: Southern Desert of Ro (Public Quest)]] from the options. The PQ option is available when you see the additional glow around the portal.
- It's open for one hour but only characters who haven't done it in the past hour can join. Once your character completes PQ it is precluded from joining another instance as it receives a 60 minute lockout timer, you can however log in an alt and participate in another PQ.
- There must be a minimum of 1 player inside the zone to kick off countdown to the start of the Public Quest. If there are more than 18 players in zone it will start early, disregarding the countdown.

## Notes

- Zone map is based on [[Cazel's Mesa]] or [[Scornfeather Roost]].
- The quest [[Hunt the Past]] can also be advanced in here by killing the [[A desert asp|desert asps]], which are not part of the PQ itself.
- Upon start there is a 60 minute time limit for completion, should you fail to kill the [[Ancient Cyclops]] in that time frame you will be booted back the The Chronoweave and receive no reward. Therefore, if you need more than 15 minutes to finish off all the [[A desert madman|Desert Madmen]], you should abandon the attempt.

## Steps

1. To spawn the [[Ancient Cyclops]], follow the suggestions of the camper NPCs.
   1. The first camper talks about desert madmen.  Kill all the ^^^ [[A desert madman|desert madmen]].
      - Killing the respawning ^^ [[A desert madman|desert madmen]] that are fought by the NPCs and the [[A dune spider|dune spiders]] does not seem to be necessary.
   1. The next camper talks about mummies.  Run around to all the spots with tombstones and kill the [[Mummy|mummies]] that spawn from the ground.
      - *Note: The players and not the NPCs have to defeat mummies from all 4 gravesites.*
   - This causes the [[Ancient Cyclops]] and [[Terrorantula]] to spawn.
1. Kill [[Terrorantula]] first to be able to damage and kill the [[Ancient Cyclops]].
   - **Note: [[Terrorantula]] is not killable until NPC "a ranger" gets killed (a warrior shouts "Ranger down!!"). If you attack the spider (or hit with AE) before that happens, it may not kill the ranger in a timely manner which prolongs the whole PQ. If you don't know when, it would be safer to NOT touch the spider at all.**
1. Finally you get to kill the [[Ancient Cyclops]] for the PQ reward.

## Rewards

- 14000 status (at level 125, full participation)
- [[Elemental Quintessence]] x2 ("tag-along" participation), x5 (full participation)
- At level 125: [[Renewal Quintessence]] x4 (full participation)
- [[Ancient Platinum Coin]] x10 (full participation)
- Equipment reward (varies by level)
- Choose from the following paintings:
  - [[Gates of Discord]] (added in 2022)
  - [[Perilous Scars of Velious]] (added in 2022)
  - [[Shadows of Luclin]] (added in 2022)
  - [[Lost Dungeons of Norrath]] (added in 2023)
  - [[Omens of War]] (added in 2024)
  - [[Dragons of Norrath]] (added in 2025)
  - [[Depths of Darkhollow]] (added in 2026)
