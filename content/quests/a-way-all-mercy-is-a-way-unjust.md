## Prerequisites

You need to have +45k with [[Clan Stormshield (Faction)]]

## Steps

1. Track down the Razorfang
   1. Find a dracurian corpse {{waypoint -2207, 193, 1417}}
   1. Find a drake corpse at {{waypoint -2083, 253, 381}}
   1. Find a Sel'nok corpse at {{waypoint -922, -115, -741}}
   1. Find a cockatrice corpse at {{waypoint -635, -122, -669}}
   1. Find a danak regimental body at {{waypoint 296, -59, -487}}
   1. Find a decapitated durus flayer at {{waypoint 913, -131, 46}}
   1. Find a rhino body at {{waypoint 630, -46, 687}}
   1. Find a a dead volcanic leatherback at {{waypoint -650, -40, 1350}}
1. Find Razorfang at   {{waypoint -570, -20, -1216}} (After you kill him, you'll be attacked by a L92^^ Selnok)
1. Return to [[Grim Stormshield]]

## Rewards

- At least 5p
- +5000 faction with **Clan Stormshield**
