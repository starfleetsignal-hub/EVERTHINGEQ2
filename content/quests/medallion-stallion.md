## Notes

- 12 minutes total time but you got to be quick because there's a lot of running around/killing things. You will generally not have enough time to do this one at all. There are only a few watchful ceffyls available and they cannot be tracked.

## Starting the Quest

![](images/Medallion_Stallion_%28Queststart%29.png)

1. To start the quest click on the [[A purple medallion|purple medallion]] which may be found at one of the following 3 locations: {{waypoint -178, 39, 459}} {{waypoint -10, 56, -11}} or {{waypoint 358, -72, 652}}. The medallion may not always be up.
1. There will be watchful ceffyls on the plains that you have to click on to "look through their eyes" to find a heights stallion (purple and that isn't visible normally). You may need to use several watchful ceffyls until you find the purple one. Run to the spot where it was and use the medallion.

## Steps

1. Kill 20 apex stonestalkers (bigger than normal mobs). You can usually find clusters of 3.
1. Go to the area of [[The Stratafold]] that is marked blue in your map (unsure if this changes, mine was around {{waypoint 591, 44, 654}}).

## Rewards

- At least 1p 68g 39s
- 5 [[Encrusted planar key]]
