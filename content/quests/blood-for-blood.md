## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 80+

## Steps

1. Search the old guard barracks and training hall, which make up the back half of the [[North Qeynos]] guildhall building in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. Enter the [[Qeynos Guard Barracks]] through one of the doors on either long side of the building. {{waypoint 360, -21, 45}} or {{waypoint 375, -21, 27}}
1. You discover that the resurrection ceremony has already started! Kill all the [[A ceremony watchguard|ceremony watchguards]] and [[A ceremony observer|ceremony observers]] to interrupt the ceremony.
1. [[Kyle Antihilus Bayle]] will root you while he discusses his wicked plans to become Antonius Bayle VIII.
1. The evil oration is interrupted by the arrival of [[Murrar Shar]]. He tells you how the attack against the Queen was stopped. {{waypoint 11, 0, -1}}
1. Return to [[Field Marshal Vishra]] in Elddar Grove.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c (at level 92)
- At least 1p 25g (at level 98)
