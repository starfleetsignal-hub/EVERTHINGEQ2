## Steps

1. Speak to [[Uelmondi]] at {{waypoint 418, 36, 39}} when you're ready to go.
   - Get teleported into [[Plane of Magic: The Aetherscar]].
   - This is a strictly solo instance: no mercs or other group members.
1. Speak to [[Uelmondi]] at {{waypoint 612, -39, -175}}.
1. Place the 3 crystal shards by clicking on the yellow position markers:![crystal shard (before & after placement)](images/Crystal_shards_%28Terra_Forma%29.jpg)
   - {{waypoint 651, -41, -173}}
   - {{waypoint 682, -36, -173}}
   - {{waypoint 674, -39, -141}}
1. Wait for [[Uelmondi]] to walk into the triangle formed by the crystals then kill the [[Cnidcara feeder|cnidcara feeders]] that spawn and attack the crystal shards.
1. Speak to [[Uelmondi]] at {{waypoint 612, -39, -175}}.
1. Move toward {{waypoint 593, -31, -137}} to return to the [[Plane of Magic]].
1. Speak to [[Majestrix Sangeeta]] at {{waypoint 280, 592, -614}}.
