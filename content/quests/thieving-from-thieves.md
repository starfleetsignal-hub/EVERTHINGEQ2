This quest is repeatable.

## Notes

- If the [[A Maj'Dul resident|Maj'Dul residents]] in the back room are gray, the chest with the key will not drop. You must mentor down to the appropriate level.

## Steps

1. Enter [[A Maj'Dul Residence]].
1. Kill mobs in the back room. The final mob will drop a chest with [[An intricate gold key]].
1. Open the chest in the side room. {{waypoint -6, 0, 23}}
1. Return to any Tears grifter for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least3g 23s 29c
- One of the following:
  - 7 [[Blades insignia token|Blades insignia tokens]]
  - 7 [[Coin insignia token|Coin insignia tokens]]
  - 7 [[Truth insignia token|Truth insignia tokens]]
