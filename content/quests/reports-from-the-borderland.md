## Steps

1. Retrieve 9 reports from around Zek. They are nailed to the trunks of trees at head height, and not very obvious at first glance.
   - In [[Blood Ore Valley]]:
     - Bug's report {{waypoint 266, 12, -213}}
     - Spike's report {{waypoint 128, 14, -198}}
   - In [[Valley of Sacrifice]]:
     - Lute's report {{waypoint 184, 12, 6}}
     - Tusk's report {{waypoint 270, 12, 106}}
   - In [[Spirit Valley]]:
     - Boulder's report {{waypoint -15, -5, 83}}
     - Feather's report {{waypoint -85, -4, -35}}
     - Shackle's report {{waypoint 0, 0, -95}}
   - In [[Refuge]]:
     - Wings' report {{waypoint 72, -38, 250}}
     - Orca's report {{waypoint 15, -21, 155}}
1. Return to [[Quint Cerlius M.E.]]

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Zek Forged Soldier's Shackle]] (Fighters)
  - [[Zek Forged Spy's Shackle]] (Scouts)
  - [[Zek Engraved Magi's Shackle]] (Mages)
  - [[Zek Blessed Shaman's Shackle]] (Priests)
