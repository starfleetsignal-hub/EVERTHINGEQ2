*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Find and learn more about the Memorial of Vengeance. Read the inscription on the NW monolith {{waypoint 5, 0, 11}} at [[The First Gate]].
1. Kill zombies in Fallen Gate to collect their blood. (Uncommon update)
1. Drop the offering into the urn at the foot of the Memorial of Vengeance.

## Reward

- [[Spectral Dragoon Medallion]]
