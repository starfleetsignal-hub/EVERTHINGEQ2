## Description

Iceshard Keep is known to house a great number of enlisted Kromzek who are destined to march on Skyshrine before long. Brother Cormag has asked me to cull a number of these Kromzek soldiers to reduce the number of troops withing King Tormax's gathering army.

## Steps

1. Nullify Kromzek neocoruses
   - Kill 6 [[A Kromzek neocorus|Kromzek neocoruses]] within [[Iceshard Keep]].

## Rewards

- [[Primal Velium Shard]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
- +350 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
