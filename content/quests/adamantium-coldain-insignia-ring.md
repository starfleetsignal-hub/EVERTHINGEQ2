## Steps

1. Speak to Scout Luta and kill the heroic group encounter which spawns. If in a group, everyone in the group should hail the quest giver at the same time.
1. Loot the [[Rime shackle key]] from the small chest.
1. Talk to Scout Luta to complete quest
