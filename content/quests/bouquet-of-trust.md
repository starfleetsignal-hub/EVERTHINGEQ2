## Notes

- This quest in not repeatable.
- When harvesting an *Erollisi Rose* for the first time, you will be offered the quest [[My Romantic Reputation]].

## Steps

![](images/Fresh-cut_rose_ground.jpg)

1. Give 8 [[Fresh-cut rose|fresh-cut roses]] to other players.
   - Fresh-cut roses are harvested from *Erollisi Rose* nodes in either [[Enchanted Lands]] or [[Frostfang Sea]]. Each harvest yields three roses.
   - To give another player a rose, simply target them and use the rose from your inventory.
   - You do not actually have to give roses to eight different people; the same person can be given eight roses, despite the language of the quest text in-game.
1. Return to Priest Aaronolis Swornlove.

## Rewards

- 2 [[Erollisi coin|Erollisi coins]]
