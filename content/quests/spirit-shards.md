## Steps

1. Talk to Savant Ka'im in the Academy of Arcane Science in North Freeport {{waypoint 4, 7, -125}} to receive this City Task.  Kill 1 of the following:
   - Kill 10-15 [[A Blackscale darkflame|Blackscale Darkflames]] in [[The Blackscale Sepulcher]].
   - Kill 10-15 [[A Blackscale darkrain|Blackscale Darkrains]] in [[The Blackscale Sepulcher]].
1. Return to [[Savant Ka'im]].

## Rewards

- 9221 status
- +500 faction with **The Academy of Arcane Science**
