## Steps

Nothing is free and building, maintaining, and supporting the herald's camp is expensive. That's why the herald of Anashti Sul has requested that we assist in offsetting the camp's cost by retrieving a valuable relic guarded by Abin'Ebi the Awkward. I need to retrieve the relic.

1. Retrieve the relic guarded by Abin'Ebi the Awkward in [[Vaashkaani: Every Which Way (Solo)|Vaashkaani: Every Which Way [Solo]]]
   - The relic is at {{waypoint 474.20, -10.62, 291.64}}
1. Return to the [[Herald of Undeath]] {{waypoint -2063, -130, -35}} in [[The Sinking Sands]]
