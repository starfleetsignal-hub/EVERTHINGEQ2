The members of the collection can be found in the following bars across Norrath:

- Claymore Ale, Ashfall Crag Lager and Sapling's Raspberry Lambic are found throughout the various starter cities.
  - At [[Irontoe's East]] in North Qeynos
  - At the [[Lion's Mane Inn and Tavern]] in South Qeynos
  - At the [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn]] in Qeynos Harbor
  - At [[Joleena's Restaurant]] in Kelethin
  - At [[Jysolin's Pub]] in Kelethin
  - At the D'Morte Inn of the [[New Foreign Quarter]] in Neriak, City of Hate {{waypoint 169, 30, 212}}
  - At the [[Seafarer's Roost]] in East Freeport
  - At [[The Jade Tiger]]'s Den in North Freeport
  - South side of [[Champion's Coliseum]] in West Freeport {{waypoint 210, -3, 77}}
  - At [[The Dragon's Anchor]] in Gorowyn
  - At the [[Muckwater Tavern]] in the [[Hate's Envy]] settlement in [[Darklight Wood]]
- Miner's Milk, Somborn Sanguine Ale and Bog Water Brew are found in a select variety of places.
  - In Kaladim:
    - [[Brell's Brew]]
    - [[Stormhammer Keep]]
    - [[Keg Hold]]
  - Inside the [[Bar of Brell]]
  - Inside the Dark Wind Lodge tavern within the [[Village of Somborn]] of the Loping Plains
  - In Maj'Dul:
    - Empty tent just inside the gate {{waypoint -218, 161, -91}}
    - Around the weapon and shields merchant in the [[Midday Market]] {{waypoint 75, 139, -17}}
  - At [[The Lolly Cog]] in Moors of Ykesha
- Bixie Honey Lager, Windstalker's Hard Cider and Crossroads Ale are found in a select variety of places.
  - On the [[Butcherblock Docks|docks]] in Butcherblock Mountains
  - At the tavern of [[Windstalker Village]] in Antonica
  - At the tavern of the [[Lost Village of Bobick]] in Enchanted Lands
  - At [[The Fools Gold]] in Rivervale
  - At the Brewery in [[Blackburrow]]
  - Inside the [[Outsiders' Landing]] bar in The Village of Shin
  - Around the merchant stalls at [[The Crossroads]] in [[The Commonlands]]
  - In the [[Thurgadin Harbor]] bar {{waypoint 1217, -475, 660}}
