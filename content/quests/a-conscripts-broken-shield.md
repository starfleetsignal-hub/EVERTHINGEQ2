## Steps

1. Find straps for the shield:
   - Kill [[A toxic water skater|toxic water skaters]] around {{waypoint -368, -5, 514}}
1. Gather repair materials for the shield:
   - Kill [[A Tae Ew raider|Tae Ew raiders]], [[A Tae Ew pillager|Tae Ew pillagers]] and [[A Tae Ew infiltrator|Tae Ew infiltrators]] found south of the [[Destroyed Druid Ring]] {{waypoint -537, 1, 902}} until you get 10 updates. There are three groups, each with [[A Tae Ew raider]], [[A Tae Ew pillager]] and [[A Tae Ew infiltrator]], and they have a 15 minute respawn time.
   - Kill [[A thule sattar|thule sattars]] until you get an update. {{waypoint -375, -2, 249}}
