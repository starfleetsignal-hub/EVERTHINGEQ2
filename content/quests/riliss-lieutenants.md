## Steps

1. Kill 4 Riliss Lieutenants:
   - [[Lieutenant Galrizz]] {{waypoint -172, -56, 598}}
   - [[Lieutenant Velssris]] {{waypoint -216, -33, 62}}
   - [[Lieutenant Xelsix]] {{waypoint -345, 7, -259}}
   - [[Lieutenant Sarzass]] {{waypoint -1121, 41, -127}}
1. Return to [[Legionnaire Captain Valkan]].
