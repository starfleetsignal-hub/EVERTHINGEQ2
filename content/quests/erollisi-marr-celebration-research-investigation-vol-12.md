## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Erollisi Marr has informed me that summoning an Avatar requires the land to be attuned to their element. To achieve this, altars have been set up around the region, but they need to be supplied with power. I have been asked to assist with this by collecting objects infused with the essence of slain foes and offer them to an altar.

1. Collect 3 [[Infused Arrowhead|infused arrowheads]]
   - These are random drop from trash mobs in [[Aether Wroughtlands]] that will show quest indicator by their name.
1. Afterwards you take those arrowheads to a shrine in [[Frostfang Sea]], shrine spawns in random spots in that zone. Possible Locations include:
   - {{waypoint -450.79, 23.85, 79.87}} (Dock area)
   - {{waypoint 136.37, 160.01, -125.83}} ([[New Halas]])
   - {{waypoint 181.08, 40.44, 88.91}} (At sea level below camp)
   - {{waypoint 494.53, 27.38, -238.73}} ([[The Great Shelf]])
   - {{waypoint 579.15, 34.26, -311.48}} ([[The Great Shelf]])
   - {{waypoint 110.42, 21.78, -598.83}} (North end of [[Gwenevyn's Cove]])
   - {{waypoint -124, 27, -793}} (Western side of [[Pilgrims' Landing]])
   - {{waypoint -190, 21, -651}} ([[Pilgrims' Landing]])
   - {{waypoint -289, 38, -730}} (Central [[Pilgrims' Landing]])
   - {{waypoint -80, 140, 300}} (behind White Lodge)
1. Return to [[Herald of the Huntress]] {{waypoint 20, 275, 125}} in [[Frostfang Sea]]
