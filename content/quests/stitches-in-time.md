## Steps

1. In [[Rivervale]]:
   - kill [[Vale nymph seedling|vale nymph seedlings]] around {{waypoint -282, -3, 64}} until you get hair to repair the threads
   - harvest lithic stones for 10 common updates
   - harvest tainted natural gardens for 10 common updates
1. Go to [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]] and use the loom in the  Tailor's room {{waypoint 24, 0, 95}}

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Cavalier's Bloodstained Breastplate]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Mentor's Dark Silk Gi]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Primalist's Ghostly Hauberk]] (Shamans)
  - [[Scholar's Spellwoven Robe]] (All Mages)
  - [[Thief's Blood-Dyed Chain Tunic]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Vicar's Sanctified Plate Cuirass]] (Clerics)
  - [[Wanderer's Rare Hide Tunic]] (Druids, Channelers)
