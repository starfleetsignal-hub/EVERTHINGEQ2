## Steps

![strange mineral deposit](images/Strange_mineral.png)

1. [[Researcher Tahar]] in what remains of [[Quel'ule]] has asked me to mine samples of the local minerals that appear to have been affected by the changes.
   - All around [[Quel'ule]] are *strange mineral* deposits, which look a lot like rock nodes.  Click them for your updates.
1. Return to [[Researcher Tahar]].
