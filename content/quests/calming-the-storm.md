Note: Updates from the rocks are group-wide meaning only one person needs to click to get the update.

## Steps

1. Gather 6 black stones.
   - Leave the village and take the path up to the northwest. Look for clumps of snow, found at various locations on the mountainside north of Goahmari Village.  Click to break these and click again to pick up the stones.  Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 438, 52, 414}}
     - {{waypoint 363, 29, 345}}
     - {{waypoint 290, -23, 293}}
     - {{waypoint 201, 14, 475}}
     - {{waypoint 366, 0, 172}}
     - {{waypoint 445, -46, 180}}
     - Concetration of black stones found at {{waypoint 409.13, -21.99, 24.65}}
1. Return to Gyatso.
1. Start the meditation ceremony.
   - Click on the large fire in the center of the village at {{waypoint 335, -15, 475}}. Wait about 30 seconds or so for the ceremony to be started.
1. Return to Gyatso to complete the quest.

NOTE: Watch out for hidden terrain stalker lions. If you have see stealth ability/totem you can see them. They hit hard.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- [[Goahmari Meditation Pit]]
