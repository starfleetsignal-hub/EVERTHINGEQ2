*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Slay the following creatures in Feerrott:
   - 5 [[An Evol Ew disciple|Evol Ew disciples]] {{waypoint 154, -9, 184.54}}
   - 5 [[An Evol Ew warder|Evol Ew warders]] {{waypoint -329, -4, 567}}
   - 5 [[An Evol Ew mender|Evol Ew menders]] {{waypoint -317, -7, 823}}
1. Return to [[Grimble Blumble]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 97s 70c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Apostle's Plate Barbute of the Divinehand]] ([[Inquisitor]], [[Templar]])
  - [[Channeler's Cowl of the Runemaster]] (All Mages)
  - [[Hanshi's Skullcap of the Whirlingfist]] ([[Bruiser]], [[Monk]])
  - [[Knight's Helmet of the Warleader]] (Plate Fighters)
  - [[Oracle's Coif of the Fatebringer]] ([[Defiler]], [[Mystic]])
  - [[Protector's Cap of the Tempestwalker]] ([[Fury]], [[Warden]])
  - [[Raider's Chain Helmet of the Heartstriker]] (All Scouts)
