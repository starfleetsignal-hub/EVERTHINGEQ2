[Into the Tainted Forest (Queen's Colony) Video Walkthrough](https://youtu.be/Hrrb26pW0dk)

This quest is part 4 of a 6-quest series in the [[Isle of Refuge Timeline]] for Qeynos-Aligned characters.

## Description

"Murrar Shar is sure that something evil is behind the goblin attacks. He sent a skilled hunter, Predator Sha'dur, into the Tainted Forest to collect samples from the wildlife that had become tainted during the recent events. I am to seek out Predator Sha'dur and see if he needs help collecting the samples. The Tainted Forest is west and then north up the hill from the colony." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Report to [[Predator Sha'dur]] {{waypoint 186, 5, 110}} just inside the archway leading to the [[The Tainted Forest]].
1. Kill [[Tainted Sapswill tapper|tainted Sapswill tappers]] until you find Sha'dur's missing bow.
1. Return Sha'dur's bow and report that you found no arrows.
1. Harvest 3 [[Pile of sticks]] (near the southeast corner of the Tainted Forest, {{waypoint 107.23, 1.59, 94.57}}) to find enough wood for new arrows.
1. Take the hunting samples Sha'dur found before he scuffled with the goblins back to Murrar Shar and receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 15c

One of the following, depending on your class:

- [[Qeynosian Cloth Hood]]
- [[Qeynosian Leather Cap]]
- [[Qeynosian Studded Helm]]
- [[Qeynosian Plated Coif]]

## Notes

This is a good time to begin the quest line starting with [[Deianeira]], who stands near Sha'dur.
