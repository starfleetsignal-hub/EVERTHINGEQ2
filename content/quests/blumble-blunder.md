## Steps

1. Locate the solo instance called [[Blumble's Halfling Hole]]. The entry is a mound that shows an 'X' when highlighted, located on the beach to the east of the [[Fallow Farmlands]]. There are three options, make sure you select [[Blumble's Halfling Hole]].
1. The exact location on the beach varies: {{waypoint -100, 0, -900}}, {{waypoint -50, 0, -1000}}, {{waypoint -87.46, -24.12, -847.55}} or {{waypoint -85.02, -23.90, -990.36}}.
1. Make your way to the back of the instance and speak to [[Grandpa Blumble]] at {{waypoint 836, -19, 1067}}.
   - **Note**: He will give you the next quest:  [[Search for Grandma Blumble]].
1. Exit at {{waypoint 831, -13, 1028}}.  Return to Blerton Blumble.

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Blerton's Letter Opener]]
  - [[Blerton's Cane]]
  - [[Blerton's Crate Opener]]
- Mail for Jacques (finishes [[Watch Your Step in the Thundering Steppes, Part I]])
