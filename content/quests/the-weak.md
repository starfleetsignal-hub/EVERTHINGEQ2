This quest is part 16 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

This is the first of several quests that take place in the [[Sanctorium of the Trials]]. This instanced zone is accessed via the doorway beside Szin {{waypoint -363, 3, -1271}}.

1. Pray to the Sanctorium of the Trials torch (examine it) to activate the door. If you have a mercenary they need to be dismissed before praying.
1. Click the door to enter the *Rite of Passage* version of [[Sanctorium of the Trials]].
1. Slay three waves of skeletons inside:
   - 3 [[A fallen challenger|fallen challengers]]
   - 2 [[A failed contestant|failed contestants]]
   - 1 [[A cursed warrior|cursed warrior]]
1. Exit the instance and talk to [[Szin Te'val]].

## Rewards

- At least 36s 13c
