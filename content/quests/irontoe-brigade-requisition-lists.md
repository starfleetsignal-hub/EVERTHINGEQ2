## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Speak with the first [[Irontoe Forewoman]] in the [[Mortartoe Mines]] at {{waypoint -646, 116, -290}}
   - Speak with the second [[Irontoe Foreman]] in the [[Mortartoe Mines]] at {{waypoint -748, 111, -204}}
   - Speak with the third [[Irontoe Foreman]] in the [[Mortartoe Mines]] at {{waypoint -800, 110, -232}}
1. Return to [[Argro Durthor]]

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
- At least 25s 70c
- One of the following:
  - [[Charm of the Immortal Contest]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Dwarven Stein of Wisdom]] (All Priests)
  - [[Effigy of an Awakened Spirit]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Preserved Spirok Eye]] (All Mages)
