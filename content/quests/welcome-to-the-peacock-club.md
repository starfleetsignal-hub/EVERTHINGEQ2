## Steps

1. Click on knocker to enter the club.
1. Speak to [[Peacock Lord Wad'dah Haz'Iz]] in the back room. He will test you by telling you to take Ceremonial Cutthroat Banners from each [[The Courts of Maj'Dul|Court]]. You get these by entering special instanced versions of each of the Courts. You will need to examine keys that drop from [[A palace sweeper|palace sweepers]] to obtain the subquests required to enter these special instances.
   - Palace sweepers spawn at {{waypoint -228, 160, -128}}. The spawn time is approximately 5 minutes. After spawning the palace sweeper will wander around the nearby market, [[Skygazer Plateau]], outside [[The Sultan's Palace]], past [[The Court of Truth]] and finally down into [[Outcasts' Ledge]] where they despawn around {{waypoint -114, 156, 47}}. Only one palace sweeper will spawn at a time and it is possible to get the same NPC spawning several times in a row. NPC now seems to sometimes drop 2 keys to different houses.
   - The color of the palace sweeper's robe corresponds to the key that they drop: Red = Truth, Yellow = Coin, and Blue = Blades.
   - If in a group, each member will need their own set of the three keys to meet the entrance requirement at each Court.
   - Examine the keys to receive the three subquests:
     - [[A Thief in the House of Blades]]
     - [[A Thief in the House of Coin]]
     - [[A Thief in the House of Truth]]
1. Return to [[The Peacock Club]] with the banners and hand them to [[Peacock Lord Wad'dah Haz'Iz]].
1. Leave the Peacock Club.
