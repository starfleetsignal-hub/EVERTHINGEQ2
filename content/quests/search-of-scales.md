1. Kill [[A Wayward exile|Wayward exiles]] and [[A Wayward outcast|outcasts]] around {{waypoint -182, 40, 185}} until you get the auto-update for the Tribunal scale, but any Wayward will do.
1. Return to [[Venox Tarkog]] to complete the quest.
