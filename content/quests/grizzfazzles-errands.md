## Notes

This can be done with [[Insectsurrection]] at the same time.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Grizzfazzle Coggrease]] at Drednever Crash Site {{waypoint -46, 43, 507}}.
1. Collect 20 Ravasect Thoraxes. These can be from the ravasects near the crash site, or on the Isle of the Ravasect. They must, however, be in the Bonemire.
1. Return to [[Grizzfazzle Coggrease]]
1. Kill [[The Devourer]] in [[The Den of the Devourer]]
1. Return to [[Grizzfazzle Coggrease]]

## Rewards

- At least 27g 50s
- One of the following, based on Class
  - [[Rosary of the Heavenly Mage]] (All Mages)
  - [[Rosary of the Skystalker (Level 60)|Rosary of the Skystalker]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Rosary of the Sky Warrior (Level 60)|Rosary of the Sky Warrior]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Rosary of the Winds]] (All Priests)
