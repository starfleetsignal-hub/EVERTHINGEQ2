## Description

*Count Jooliin has asked that I take the plant that grew from the seed of growth to Lilia Posey, a sage in Kelethin that will be able to extract the needed information from the plant that Jooliin needs. Count Jooliin has asked that Lilia test the plant for its purity and corruption. Her results should tell Jooliin how deep the void corruption runs. I'm sure he'll have to get this information to the Queen after he has reviwed it. Once I've obtained the information that Jooliin needs, I'm to return to him with what arcance wisdom Lilia Posey has compiled.*

## Prerequisites

- Your faction with [[The City of Kelethin (Faction)|City of Kelethin]] must be Indifferent or better to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Deliver the plant, [[Tainted sprout|a tainted sprout]], to [[Lilia Posey|Sage Lilia Posey]] {{waypoint 142, 110, 180}} in the tree city of [[Kelethin]] inside the Research Libary.
1. Give [[Lilia Posey]] about 1 minute. Animation and she speaks to herself. Talk to her again when she is finished, to receive [[A scroll of arcane text]].
1. Take [[A scroll of arcane text]] back to [[Count Jooliin]]
