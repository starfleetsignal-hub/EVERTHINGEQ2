## Description

Several tomes concerning the study of void energy are said to be found within the stacks of Erudin library. I've been asked to retrieve several of these tomes so the loathsome presence of the void can be better understood before it is too late to save Odus.

## Steps

1. Enter the Library of Erudin and retrieve several tomes containing knowledge of void energy.
   - Collect eight tomes
   - *Note: Go to Marred Plateau transport station, head up the ramp, and click on the column on the left to be transported to the library.*

## Rewards

- At least 1p 80g
- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
