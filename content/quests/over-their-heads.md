## Steps

1. Create strong tents (5)
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe: Strong and Sturdy Tent]]
   1. Craft 5 [[Strong Tent|Strong Tents]] on a [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]] (at the docks {{waypoint 668.64, 24.00, 606.70}}) with the following materials and fuel:
      - 5 [[Tussah root]]
      - 5 [[Severed briarwood]]
      - 10 [[Etched leather pelt]]
      - 15 [[Glimmering filament]]
1. Return to [[Ellerith Groundspark|Ellerith]]
1. Speak with [[Gallie Shimmercloud|Gallie]] {{waypoint 326, 189, 496}}
1. Meet with the saliraptor {{waypoint 323, 188, 498}}

NOTE:  You can get BOTH saliraptors if you talk to Saldas right next to Ellerith and do that quest also ( [[Leaping to Conclusions]] ). They look different. If your adventure level is also 30+, you will qualify for that one too.

- [[Horned Mountain Saliraptor]]
