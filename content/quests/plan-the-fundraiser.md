## Notes

- The subsequent fundraiser quests (i.e. all but [[I Hate Fundraisers]]) are tradeskill related quests and can be completed independently of each other. Their completion is not necessary to continue the quest line.

## Steps

1. Slay 7 [[A spearfish|spearfish]] in the water near the [[Lost Village of Bobick]]. Kill [[An ocean glider|ocean gliders]] to spawn more spearfish.{{waypoint -610, -5, 107}} Can be found from west of docks all the way to druid rings.
1. Return to [[Deputy Nettlebrine]]
1. Speak with [[Deputy Hopple]] near [[The Runnyeye Ward]] {{waypoint 169, 0, -473}}

## Rewards

- At least 58s 40c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Apostle's Ordained Plate Pauldrons]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Marked Epaulets]] (All Mages)
  - [[Hanshi's Balanced Arm Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Polished Pauldrons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Oracle's Sacred Spaulders]] (Shamans)
  - [[Protector's Moon Etched Shoulder Pads]] (Druids)
  - [[Raider's Thickened Chain Spaulders]] (All Scouts)
