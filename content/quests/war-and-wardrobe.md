## Notes

- Only one character may loot [[Ancient Koada'Dal Brooch]] at a time, and the quest is not shareable. So if you are in a group, the [[Aqua Goblin Oracle]] will need to be killed multiple times (~10 minute respawn).

## Prerequisites

- You must have completed the quest, [[The Everling Lockets]], to have access to the basement in [[Nektropos Castle]].
- You must speak [[Languages|Orcish]] to advance during the quest.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Glavarius Marud]] in [[Kelethin]]. {{waypoint 293, 109, 94}}
1. Get the first clue:
   1. Travel to [[The Down Below]] under [[Qeynos]] and enter [[Crow's Resting Place, Thieves Guild]] by clicking on the statue. {{waypoint -144, 0, -74}}
   1. Click one of the tables in the front room to get [[A king's court ace]]. {{waypoint -9, 0, 0}}
   1. Speak to [[Fippy Darkpaw, the 4th]] and give him the ace plus 5g. {{waypoint 1.41, 1.17, 7.35}}
1. Get the second clue:
   1. Travel to [[Nektropos Castle]] in [[Nektulos Forest]].
   1. Go to Lord Everling's bedroom at {{waypoint 18, 14, -45}}, click on the bookcase, and go down the stairs to the basement.
   1. Inspect [[Empty Crate Note]], located on some crates. {{waypoint -41, -13, -57}}
   1. Examine the note from your inventory.
1. Get the third clue:
   1. Travel to [[Fallen Gate]] in [[The Commonlands]].
   1. Kill [[Zannas of the Arm]] (33^^^) in [[Little Neriak]]. {{waypoint 167, 18, -83}}
      - Entering his house will cause three [[An intruder countermeasure|intruder countermeasure]]s to spawn. It's safest to distance-pull Zannas out of the house.
   1. Loot *Zannas K'Val's Chest* to receive three notes: [[Tessa's Appreciation Note]], [[Tessa's Note on the War]], and [[Tessa's Contingency Plan]].
   1. Examine Tessa's Contingency Plan from your inventory. (There is a bug that may place these notes in your bank, rather than your inventory)
1. Get the fourth clue:
   1. Travel to [[The Chessboard]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]].
   1. Harvest *a loose stone* to receive [[Tessa's Hidden Notes for Zannas]]. {{waypoint -788, 101, -16}}
      - The stone is trackable. Either use your track harvestables ability, or use a [[Gnomish Divining Rod]].
      - The stone is one of the squares of the Chessboard, but only clickable right in the center of the stone.
      - Harvesting the stone will spawn four linked 34^ [[A triggered guardian|triggered guardian]]s. If you looted the key from Zannas of the Arm (previous step) the trap will be deactivated and the guardians won't spawn. You will receive the hidden note regardless if you kill the triggered guardians or not.
   1. Examine the note from your inventory.
1. Find the gem:
   1. Travel to [[Butcherblock Bay]] in Butcherblock Mountains and kill aqua goblins until you receive [[An old Teir'Dal gemcron]]. {{waypoint 707, 23, 186}}
      - The [[An aqua goblin runt|runts]] do not give updates - only the adult aqua goblins.
   1. Examine the gem from your inventory.
1. Travel to [[Clan Crushbone]] in [[Greater Faydark]], enter the rightmost mine at {{waypoint -1470, 118, 199}}, and speak to [[A punished magi apprentice]]. {{waypoint -1620, 48, -30}}
   - You must be able to speak [[Orcish]] to communicate with the apprentice.
1. Enter [[Crushbone Keep]] and slay four [[A cleric of Hate|clerics of Hate]] to obtain their helms. They spawn at the following locations:
   - [[Hall of the Magi]] {{waypoint 70, 25, -36}}
   - [[The Emperor's Chambers]] {{waypoint -13, 22, 31}}
   - [[Royal Guardpost]] {{waypoint -60, 25, -28}}
   - Clerics are part of a large shared spawn group with [[An oracle of Hate (Crushbone Keep)|oracles of Hate]] (which do not update the quest) and the four kinds of D'Vinnian mages. It may take some time to spawn enough clerics of Hate.
1. Return to the punished magi apprentice.
1. Kill [[Oracle N'Mare]]:
   - Enter [[The D'Vinnian Throne]] within Crushbone Keep. {{waypoint -60.41, 25.87, -40.40}}.
   - Speak to [[Lady Elizara D'Kush]] and then slay her guards to unlock the doors in the entry room.
   - Slay the [[A watchful sentry|watchful sentry]] at {{waypoint 58.57, -6.43, -15.18}} to unlock the door at {{waypoint -42.60, -9.81, -67.35}}.
   - Slay the watchful sentry at {{waypoint -67.91, -9.83, -61.10}} to unlock the door at {{waypoint -64.71, -9.83, -81.94}}.
   - Go through the secret door at {{waypoint -65, -10, -165}}.
   - Kill Oracle N'Mare. {{waypoint -82, -9, -178}}

## Rewards

- [[Robe of the Oracle]] (can be examined and turned into a house item display - [[The Legendary Robe of the Oracle]], and can be converted back)
- 35200 status
