## Steps

1. Obtain 4 updates by completing the following subquests. All are 5-minute timed quests that require you to activate switches within a specific court in the Living Tombs:
   - [[The Secret of Im'Sekt]]
   - [[The Secret of Du'Mutef]]
   - [[The Secret of Qey'Nuf]]
   - [[The Secret of Hak'Pyz]]
1. Inspect the censer again.
1. Speak to [[The Ancille of D'Morte]].

## Rewards

One of the following:

- [[Belt of Woven Souls]]
- [[Band of the Ancille]]
- [[Ancient Tablet of the Desert Oracle]]
