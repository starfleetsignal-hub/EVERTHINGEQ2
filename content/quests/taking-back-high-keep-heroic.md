*The Pickclaw goblins have flooded out of the mines and are running wild through High Keep. Find their strongest members, and destroy them.*

- Enter the zone [[High Keep (Heroic)]]
  - Defeat [[Lucky (Heroic)|Lucky]]
  - Defeat [[Purtog (Heroic)|Purtog]]
  - Defeat [[Raknag (Heroic)|Raknag]]
  - Defeat [[Hammy (Heroic)|Hammy]] from High Keep
