Required Druid level 25

## Steps

1. Return the leaf to the [[Teleportation and Portals Trainers|portals trainer]] near the priest guild in your home town.
1. Take the ***Blessing of Growth*** the trainer gives you back to the druid ring in [[Greater Faydark]]
   - Note:  You can either take a portal that the trainer opens up for you or you can travel back to the ring yourself.  Recommended to accept all the quests at once and not take the portal.
1. Use the *Blessing of Growth* once inside the ring
1. Return to the [[Teleportation and Portals Trainers|portals trainer]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Circle of Greater Faydark]]
