This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

- Enter the pit:
  - Target a hard desert soil patch
  - **Use** [[Aillena's Delicate Artifact Extractor]], either by right-clicking it in your inventory or placing and using it on a hotbar.
  - Gather the [[Ancient relic]] that is uncovered (sometimes nothing is uncovered)
  - Repeat until you have 8 relics
- Return to Researcher Aillena

## Rewards

- 1 [[D.I.R.T.Y. Money]]
