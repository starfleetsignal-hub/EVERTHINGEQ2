## Steps

1. To better camouflage the village for [[Li Sing]], climb the ramp on the southeast side up and out of the Hua Mein village.  Once outside of the village, find the patch of loose dirt (surrounded by green particles) to plant the sapling in. {{waypoint -182.9, 242, 3328}} ![Plant the sapling here ...](images/Hua_Mein_Craft_Supplementary_Camouflage_quest_-_loose_dirt.jpg)
1. Return to Li Sing.
1. Go back to Paineel and talk to [[Adeela]] if he has not already sent you to talk to Researcher Tahar in Quel'ule to continue the Quest line.

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +1,500 faction with **Hua Mein**
- +1,000 faction with **Hua Mein Craftsmen**
- Achievement [[Panda People Person]]

## Notes

- Completing this quest unlocks the Hua Mein daily supply tasks from [[Xi Lan]] at {{waypoint -220, 245, 3099}}.
