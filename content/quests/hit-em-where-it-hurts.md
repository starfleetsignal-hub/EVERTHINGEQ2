## Steps

1. I should create 2 more kegs of explosive powder at a [[Chemistry Table]] at {{waypoint -355, 177, -30}}.
1. I should place the explosives behind the [[Cairn of the Huntress]] at {{waypoint 47, 150, -45}}.
1. Things appear to have gone terribly wrong!  I need to speak to [[Commissioner Varla Z'Velran|Varla]].
1. Once you place the explosives, guards run up and take you to a Temporary Holding Cell (zone) where Varla is waiting to speak to you. The quest ends here after you talk to her, you can zone back out of the cell.

## Rewards

- One of the following, as applicable to your home city:
  - +250 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +250 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +250 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +250 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
  - +250 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +250 faction with **Ravens of the North**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Alkaline loam]]
  - [[Cured leather pelt]]
  - [[Blackened iron cluster]]
  - [[Rough coral]]
  - [[Severed bone]]
  - [[Silver cluster]]
  - [[Sisal root]]
- Access to Tier 2 Advanced recipes from [[Commissioner Varla Z'Velran|Varla]] while in jail at the end of the quest, or find her later in Neriak at {{waypoint -159, -5, 253}}.
- Access to the recipe book [[Ironforge Exchange Basic Recipes]]. It is a clickable found in New Halas at the corner of a table at {{waypoint 61, 154, -131}}.
