1. Enter [[Veksar: The Invasion]] or [[Veksar: The Sunken Theater]]. {{waypoint 631, -179, 290}}
1. Complete the following:
   - Collect 5 Yha-lei weapons by killing Yha-lei.
   - Collect 5 Kwagan weapons by killing Kwagan Goblins.
1. Return to Defender Tuakin.
