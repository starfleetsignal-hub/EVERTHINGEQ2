## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Collect 10 bear pelts by killing bears {{waypoint -203.35, -1.02, -474.21}}
   - Collect 3 batches of goblin blood by killing goblins {{waypoint 0, -1, -605}}
   - Collect river water by stepping into [[The Lazy Drain]].
1. Return to [[Deputy Huckfar]]
1. Speak with [[Deputy Nettlebrine]] in the [[Lost Village of Bobick]] {{waypoint 26, -1, 19}}

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Bone Spike of Fertile Lands]] (Fighters)
  - [[Earhoop of Fertile Lands]]  (Scouts)
  - [[Earring of Fertile Lands]] (Priests)
  - [[Facet of Fertile Lands]] (Mages)
