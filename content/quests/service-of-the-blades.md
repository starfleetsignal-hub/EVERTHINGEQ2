## Notes

*This quest is repeatable..*

- You must have +10,000 faction with the Court of the Blades to unlock this quest. Once +40,000 faction has been achieved, this quest will no longer be available.

## Steps

1. Acquire 5 magic scrolls from Anaz Mal casters, (i.e. [[An Anaz Mal coercer]], [[An Anaz Mal diviner]], [[An Anaz Mal suppressor]])
1. Acquire 5 weapons from [[A Sandscrawler grunt]].
1. Acquire 5 amulets from [[An Alliz Raef Ew scout]].
1. Return to [[Majah L'Asi]].

## Rewards

- At least 12g 57s 51c
- +3000 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -3000 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -3000 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
- After the fifth time: [[Court of the Blades Tapestry]]
- After the eight time: [[Court of Tears Tapestry]]
