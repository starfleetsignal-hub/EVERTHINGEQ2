## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of Morell-Thule has asked for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Gather the requested supplies
   - Collect 3 [[Gilded Wing|gilded wings]] from creatures found in [[Aether Wroughtlands]]
     - *Note: drops in [[Chests|small chest]] from a golden viper in Glistervein Grove*
     - *1-2 drops per group member*
   - Gather or purchase [[Chromatic Carp|chromatic carp]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas
     - *Common harvest from BoZ fish*
     - *Can buy off broker*
     - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Lord of Dreams]] at {{waypoint 1097, -1, 646}} in [[The Thundering Steppes]]
