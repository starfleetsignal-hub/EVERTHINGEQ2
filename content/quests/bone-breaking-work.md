## Steps

1. Destroy 6 [[A vicious dabrutu|vicious dabrutus]] attacking [[Kulaidi Reservoir Camp]] {{waypoint 296, 9, 162}} or {{waypoint 238, 12, 91}}, then pick up the **Dabrutu Skull**.![Dabrutu Skull.png](images/Dabrutu_Skull.png)
1. Slay 4 [[A dabrutu enchanter|dabrutu enchanters]] near [[Mordant Spires]] at {{waypoint 269.65, 4.56, -31.85}}
1. Return to [[Dulamah]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
  - +1,000 faction with **Hizite Nomads of Ro**
- One of the following:
  - [[Resilient Dendromail Spaulders]]
  - [[Resilient Viridsilk Sleeves]]
  - [[Resilient Florahide Sleeves]]
  - [[Resilient Chloroplate Vambraces]]
