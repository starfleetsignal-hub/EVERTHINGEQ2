## Steps

- Report to [[Alim Dabir]] of the [[Bastion of the Tranquil (Faction)|Bastion of the Tranquil]]. {{waypoint 1981, -298, 3338}}
- Report to [[High Commander Fawzi Zaim]] of the [[Fell Blade (Faction)|Fell Blade]]. {{waypoint 1902, -298, 3447}}
- Report to [[Nudhir Il'qatai]], researcher of the necromantic arts. {{waypoint 1644, -267, 3175}}

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +2,000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- You'll be offered one or more of the following, depending on your class.
  - You must choose one:
    - [[Blade of Blanketed Treachery]] (fighter choice)
    - [[Falchion of Steadfast Posture]] (fighter choice)
    - [[Spike of Superior Reticence]]
    - [[Staff of Summoning Earth]] (fighter, shaman choice)
    - [[Star of Persevering Faith]] (fighter, shaman choice)
    - [[Sword of the Abhorrent Sentinel]] (fighter choice)
    - [[Wand of Foreboding Contention]] (shaman choice)
    - *Probably others that haven't been entered yet.*
