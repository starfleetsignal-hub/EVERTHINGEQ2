## Steps

1. Find the missing fishing party east past the nets
   - The fishing skiff is underwater at {{waypoint -1679, -549, -2115}}(close to the surface)
1. Click on the two Othmir fishermen
1. Kill the three [[A frenzied sleeper shark|frenzied sleeper sharks]] that attack
1. Return to Nia.
1. Nia's son [[Fiver]]  is still missing!  Go find him.
   - Fiver is asleep just under the edge of the sunken fishing skiff, unconscious near {{waypoint -1685, -549, -2120}}. Waypoint will send you to the bottom of the ocean but you'll see Fiver by swimming under the underwater platform and looking up inside it.
     - Click to wake him
     - Then click to pick him up.
1. Return [[Fiver]] to Nia.
