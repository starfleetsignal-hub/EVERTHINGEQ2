## Steps

1. Bartie asks you to locate the sneakies goblins.  {{waypoint 95.24, -33.57, -191.70}}
1. Once you have discovered the goblins are crispy speak to the nearby goblins to discover where the missing sneakies are. {{waypoint 117, -35, -205}}
1. Find a way past the frozen walls (by completing [[Gerbi Frostfoot (Icy Keep Retribution)|Gerbi Frostfoot]]'s quest [[Icy Hammer Time!]]).
1. Checkmate the Dark Frozen King on the chessboard in three moves.
   1. Move the Light Frozen Rook by the Light Frozen King two spaces to the left ("left two") to check the Dark Frozen King.
   1. Right click the Light Frozen Knight and select "forward one, left two" to check the Dark Frozen King.
   1. Right click the other Light Frozen Rook in the top left corner and select two space to the right ("right two") to checkmate the Dark Frozen King.
1. Kill the [[The Frozen Corrival (Icy Keep: Retribution)|Frozen Corrival]]
1. Investigate the goblin at {{waypoint 233, -28, -692}}
1. Return to Bartie.
