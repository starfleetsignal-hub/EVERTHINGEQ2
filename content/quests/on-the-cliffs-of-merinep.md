## Steps

1. Extract poison from rampaging Deinodons (5) - any deinodon with 'rampaging' in its' name updates
1. Gather deino bones - travel to the tar pits and click the bones
1. Craft poison traps in the bonecrafter's hut - click the table at {{waypoint -707, 9, 288}}
1. Do the following
   - Remove [[An Allu'thoa bowmaster|Allu'thoa]] from the cliffs above Merinep Cove {{waypoint -1117, 62, -382}}
   - Place the poison traps along the cliffs
1. Return to [[Kowli]]
