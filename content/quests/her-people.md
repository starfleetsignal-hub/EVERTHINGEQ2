This is the third quest in the series to change your [[Citizenship#Kelethin to Qeynos|citizenship from Kelethin to Qeynos]].

## Steps

1. You must speak with a series of people around Qeynos and its outlying zones.
   - [[South Qeynos]]: [[Gracen Docks]] at {{waypoint 722.48, -19.47, 174.69}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]]: [[Randal Gromoe]] at {{waypoint 974, -25, 93}}
   - [[The Elddar Grove]]: [[Palla Jhal]] at {{waypoint 671, -21, -298}}
   - [[North Qeynos]]: [[Lady Rickson]] at {{waypoint 315, -21, -2}}
1. Return to [[Duryo Valstath]] in South Qeynos.

## Rewards

- This quest only gives AA experience, not regular experience.
- +40000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- +5000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**

If a citizen of Kelethin prior to changing to Qeynos, you will lose faction with Kelethin.
