## Steps

1. Perform the following tasks in the [[The City of Mist]]:
   - Gather 5 artifacts from the lower ruins of the city.
   - Kill 3 [[A rival plunderer|rival plunderers]]:
     - {{waypoint -243, 135, -874}} (3 inside building)
     - {{waypoint -187, 134, -867}}
     - {{waypoint -149, 136, -867}} (2 inside building)
     - {{waypoint -224, 136, -870}} (3 inside building)
1. Return to Skulker Slythe.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 5g
- +10,000 faction with **Hidden Plunderers' Camp**
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Exarch's Solemn Ceremony Plate Cuirass]] (Clerics)
  - [[Executioner's Cobalt Breastplate]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Illuminate's Fallen Leaves Gi]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Magus's Ensorcelled Satin Robe]] (All Mages)
  - [[Outrider's Blue Iron Chain Tunic]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Stormcaller's Winter Weave Tunic]] (Channelers, Druids)
  - [[Witchdoctor's Ill Omen Hauberk]] (Shamans)
