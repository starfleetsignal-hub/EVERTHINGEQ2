## Steps

1. Go to [[Stonehoof Badlands]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]] and hail the [[Skeleton of Yasva V'Alear (NPC)|Skeleton of Yasva V'Alear]] {{waypoint -331, -61, -503}} *(by Kylong Plains post, on the last ruins near the Fort)*
1. Kill the mobs in the following order:
   1. Kill [[Tairiza the Widow Mistress]] in [[The Tomb of Thuuga]].
   1. Kill [[The Leviathan]] in [[The Chamber of Destiny]].
   1. Kill [[Nexona]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]].
1. Return to the [[Skeleton of Yasva V'Alear (NPC)|Skeleton of Yasva V'Alear]] {{waypoint -331, -61, -503}} in [[Stonehoof Badlands]] (Jarsath Wastes) to claim your Mythic weapon.
