## Steps

1. Enter the zone [[Siren's Grotto: Alluring Embrace (Advanced Solo)]] or [[Siren's Grotto: Alluring Embrace (Heroic)]] {{waypoint 5539, -1086, 186}}.  You will need to kill all the bosses in the zone - please see the zone pages for the walkthrough and strategies.
1. After defeating [[Queen Dulseris (Siren's Grotto: Alluring Embrace)|Queen Dulseris]], kill the three Tavalans that spawn.
1. Speak to [[Ladlenn Lowoak]] {{waypoint -539, 6, -354}} at the entrance to the queen's room.
1. Return to [[Head Scholar Nabihan (Cobalt Scar)|Head Scholar Nabihan]] in Scarstone, Cobalt Scar.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- [[Glorious Grotto]]
