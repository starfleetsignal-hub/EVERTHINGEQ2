## Steps

1. Speak to [[Raffik]] in [[New Halas]] in the [[Frostfang Sea]] at the [[Ravens' Roost]] tavern {{waypoint -389, 174, -145}}
1. Speak to [[Grog Farbrewer]] at the tavern {{waypoint -392, 174, -158}} to be be given the [[Coldain Cure-All (Quest Recipe)|Coldain Cure-All]] recipe book.
1. Craft [[Coldain Cure-All (Quest Item)|Coldain Cure-All]] from the recipe given.
   - 1x [[Squash]]
   - 5x [[Aviak meat]]
   - 5x [[Ravasect meat]]
   - 1x [[Scintillating candle]]
     - Note: Chemistry Table at {{waypoint -366, 177, -50}}
1. Return to Raffik

## Rewards

- At least 5p 15g
- Tradeskill experience
- [[Coldain Cure-All (QuestReward)|Coldain Cure-All]]
