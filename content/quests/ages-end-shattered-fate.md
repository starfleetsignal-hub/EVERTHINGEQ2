## Notes

- *While the rest of this signature questline was solo - **this quest is a raid event**.  Either make sure you're a much higher geared character, or (as originally intended) bring friends...*

- If you sold your [[Skyshrine Tower Rune Stone (Quest item)‎|Skyshrine Tower Rune Stone]], you can buy a new one from the [[Shady Swashbuckler XVIII]] on the [[Nektulos Forest]] docks.

## Steps

1. Use the [[Skyshrine Tower Rune Stone (Teleport)‎|Skyshrine Tower Rune Stone]] to summon the portal to [[Skyshrine Tower: Age's End]] and enter the portal
   - *Note:raidwide zone in if you are in the same zone*
1. Speak with Jorlak with in [[Skyshrine Tower: Age's End]]
1. Speak with the Overlord and Queen Antonia
   - *Note: They will come closer to Jorlak*
   - *Note: it will be a raid wide update if you hailed them before, if not go hail em*
1. Speak with Jorlak
   - *Note: As soon as you hail him the ring event will start*
1. Destroy the 3 shield generating crystals in the east, north, and west pavilions
   - *Note: Kerafyrm will periodically fly to each of the pavilions which still has a crystal and use a breath attack which will kill you. Keep track of where he is during the fight and avoid being near the pavilion he's attacking.*
1. Return to Jorlak
   - *Note: Do not hail Jorlak again, quest will auto update and encounter will progress*
1. Kill [[Roehn Theer (Age's End)|Roehn Theer]]
   1. Pick up Friona Vie's Lifegiver Staff, this will open a new hotbar for the player that picks it up
      - *Note: The staff can be used by any class; however, it's recommended you use a Priest or Dirge*
   1. When [[Roehn Theer (Age's End)|Roehn Theer]] reaches ~10% this battle is won (if you hit him too hard and go past 10%, the trigger seems to not go off, so be careful here)
      - *Note: If everyone dies during this fight, revive and approach Roehn Theer to re-start the encounter.*
1. Pick up the [[Swords of Destiny]]
   - *Note: You can't hold the Lifegiver Staff and Swords of Destiny at the same time. To pick up the swords, cancel the buff from the Lifegiver Staff first.
1. Kill [[General Velryyn]]
1. Speak to Firiona Vie
1. Speak to Roehn Theer
1. After using the tear of Veeshan to empower his foci, Roehn Theer attacks Kerafyrm.
1. Speak to Jorlak
1. Pickup the two swords of destiny by the tear of Veeshan
1. Return the [[Swords of Destiny]] to Antonia and the Overlord
   - Queen Antonia gets back the [[Qeynos Claymore]]
   - Overlord Lucan gets back the [[Soulfire]]
1. Speak with Firiona Vie
