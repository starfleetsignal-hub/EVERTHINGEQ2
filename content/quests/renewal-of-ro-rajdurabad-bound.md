## Steps

1. Speak with [[Riddan]] in [[Hopewell Harbor]] {{waypoint 175.86, -60.15, 459.60}}
   - Hunt Raj'Dur ambushers at [[Nerive Reservoir Camp]] {{waypoint -318.97, -16.63, 139.17}} for 3 garment pieces. Not guaranteed drops. Very low drop rate ~ 1 in 20.
   - Gather 6 **taaffeite crystals** in [[Sunscar Vale]] (small nodes, not trackable). See Talk Page for locations. ![Taaffeite_Crystals](images/Taaffeite_Crystals.png)
   - Hunt 8 [[A winged hunter|winged hunters]] for talons. Around [[Najami Oasis]] {{waypoint -161.28, 51.79, -17.53}}, look up.
1. Return to [[Riddan]]
1. Search for the hidden entrance to the Raj'Dur village in [[Najami Oasis]] {{waypoint 84, 12, -270}}.
   1. Activate the [[Raj'Dur Ruffian Disguise]] illusion.
   1. Go to lift at {{waypoint 85.00, 12.32, -274.94}} and speak to [[A Raj'Durabad lift guard]]
1. Speak to [[Riddan]] near the lift {{waypoint 24.80, 20.26, -283.21}}
1. Obtain something as tribute to the Sultan of the Daggers from [[Rahele]] at {{waypoint 186.11, -60.15, 547.07}} in [[Hopewell Harbor]].
   - This step requires completion of [[Desert Products]] and [[Crystals and Coconuts]].
1. Return to [[Riddan]] near the lift {{waypoint 24.30, 20.32, -282.16}}
1. Speak to [[A Raj'Durabad lift guard]] again. Take the lift up.
   1. **Note:**  Lift operating **switch** is at (80.26 20.27 -295.19 62.89 0.00 0.00)  It is slightly camouflaged against the wood of the lift.)
1. Speak to [[Akhun]], the Chief Treasurer {{waypoint -284.77, 104.80, -249.49}}
1. Sabotage the Hizite camp a bit.
   - Gather 8 **Hizite Stolen Goods**. ![Stolen Hizite Goods](images/Stolen_Hizite_Goods.PNG)
   - Kill 12 [[A Raj'Dur thief|Raj'Dur thieves]].
   - Destroy 6 **Raj'Durabad Defense Glyphs**. ![Raj'Durabad Defense Glyph](images/Raj'Durabad_Defense_Glyph.PNG)
1. Return to [[Riddan]] near the lift {{waypoint 24.30, 20.32, -282.16}}

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 57,791 status
- One of the following:
  - [[Star of the Great Mother]]
  - [[Etcher, Claws of the Buried]]
  - [[Memory of the Lost]]
  - [[Grace, the Forest's Protector]]
