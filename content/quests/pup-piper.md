The Bloody Tooth goblins use chokidai hide to make the armor they wear. The hides of young pups work best, as the leather is not yet too thick and stiff to work with. I offered to help them catch some.

## Note

Upon accepting this quest, you will receive [[Chokidai Whistle (Recipe Book)]] which will automatically scribe the [[Chokidai Whistle]] recipe to your recipe book.

The recipe for this quest can **only** be crafted at a [[Goblin Workbench]] in the area. One is found at {{waypoint 72, -164, -722}}, near Oogthor.

## Supply List

Because these must be crafted in [[Twark]], and the required materials cannot be harvested in [[Obulus Frontier]], bring the following (minimum) supplies:

- 1 [[Malduran lumber]]
- 3 [[Umbrite]]
- 3 [[Bornite nodule]]
- 5 [[Thaumic Coal]]

## Steps

1. I can use a goblin workbench to make the whistle
   - Craft a [[Chokidai Whistle]]
1. Chokidai pups can be found across the river to the southwest, near the small iksar bandit camp
   - Capture a chokidai pup and bring it back to Oogthor
     - Travel to the area around Warslik Wood at {{waypoint 408, -203, -176}} and capture a Chokidai Pup (click on it, then use the whistle)
     - Return to [[Oogthor]] with the pup within
     - *NOTE: You might have to FLY it back up to him, and not use the totem to port back.*
1. Oogthor is in the main goblin encampment of Twark
   - Speak to [[Oogthor]]

## Rewards

- [[Sovereign of Rile]] currency
- [[Ancient Shadowstone Relic]] status token
- +2000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
- At least 2p 26g 80s
