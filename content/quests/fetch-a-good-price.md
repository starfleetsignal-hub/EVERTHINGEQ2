## Steps

1. Hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 10 x [[A briargrim ravager]] for barktalons - flying dragon like creatures around and to the West of [[The Venerable Absence]] around {{waypoint 227, 26, -868}}
   - Gather 8x featherwing egg clutches - bright pink clutches of eggs by grasses and flowers, visible from far away, between Hoarfang Ridge and Venerable Absence
     - Possible locations:
       - {{waypoint 36, -21, -679}}
       - {{waypoint -203, 53, -722}}
       - {{waypoint -135, 24, -614}}
       - {{waypoint -158.11, 29.94, -594.62}}
       - {{waypoint -137.03, 24.38, -613.32}}
       - {{waypoint -221.98, 35.40, -619.59}}
       - {{waypoint -280.58, 39.68, -639.24}}
       - {{waypoint -305.07, 38.90, -580.09}}
     - *Both of these updates in the same area, the green areas surrounding [[The Venerable Absence]] (the flooded ruins of Temple), North side of the zone*
1. Return to [[Nythruth]] at {{waypoint -480, 123, -325}} [[Hoarfang Ridge]]

## Reward

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- 50 x [[Scarred Dinar]]
- 5 x [[Velium Drachma]]
