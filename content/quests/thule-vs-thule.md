## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Fear Cave]] {{waypoint -2400, 210, 520}}
1. Speak to a lizardman: you will have to kill one
1. Kill 3 [[A D'iar Ew fearcaller]]s at the back of the Fear Cave {{waypoint -2308, 202, 460}}
1. Right-click [[The Fear Gate]] to investigate it - Get up-close to the portal. If you you right-click and select "Investigate the Fear Portal" from outside the floor-mural at its base, it will let you 'investigate' but it does NOT update the quest.
1. Return to Gulthga

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
