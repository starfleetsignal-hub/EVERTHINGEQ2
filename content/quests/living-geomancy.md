## Steps

You are required to visit a number of locations around the zone. Moving into the general area will give the update.

- Visit Village of Shin. {{waypoint 13, 14, -117}}
- Visit one of the fields east of Shin. {{waypoint -62, 17, -149}}
- Visit the [[Tower of the Four Winds]]. {{waypoint 11, 33, -153}}
- Visit [[Luk Ki Island]]. {{waypoint 161, -2, 18}}
- Visit Tears of Jade Spring. {{waypoint -28, 16, -164}}
- Visit Willow view Bridge. {{waypoint -151, 5, -126}}
- Visit House on the Courtyard. {{waypoint 37, 14, -109}}

## Reward

- Experience & AA only
