## Steps

1. Craft 5 [[Wooden ritual dagger|wooden ritual daggers]] on a [[Woodworking Table]] using 5 [[Effulgent sandpaper]] and the following ingredients:
   - 5 [[Tainted shrub (A Timeless Instrument)|tainted shrubs]]
   - 5 [[Blessed water (A Timeless Instrument)|blessed waters]]
   - The recipe, [[The Finer Points of Rituals]] is auto-scribed.
   - There is no fuel merchant or woodworking table nearby, so be sure to bring your own.
1. Speak to Phaerus
