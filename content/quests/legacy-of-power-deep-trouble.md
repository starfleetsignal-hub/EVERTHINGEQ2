## Steps

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 187, 3, 0}} to enter [[Brackish Vaults (Solo)|Brackish Vaults [Solo]]].
1. Kill all [[Coral guardian]].
1. Right click the portal and choose "vapor" to teleport to the next section.
1. Kill the vapor creatures.
1. Right click the portal at {{waypoint 15, 84, -17}} and choose "ice" to teleport to the next section.
1. Kill all the ice creatures.
1. Right click the portal at {{waypoint -18, 143, 27}} and choose "water" to teleport to the next section.
1. Kill all water creatures.
1. Kill [[A brine wyvern]].
1. "Release the Sphere" and gather [[Sphere of Coalesed Water]] at {{waypoint 5, 224, -27}}
1. Return to [[Druzzil Ro]] in the [[Coliseum of Valor]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 41g 75s 49,449 status
- [[Key to the Undertow]] - Allows access to the "Brackish Vaults" zones for entire account.
- [[Celestial Ascension]]
