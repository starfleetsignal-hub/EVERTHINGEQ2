## Notes

In game mail from Lord Bennet informs you about Trouble Beginning in Antonica. It directs you to seek out Abdur Hamdaan who starts the quest. This quest is required to open up more adventure and tradeskill quests.

Areas where you can find updates for this quest are highlighted on maps in blue.

If you have a hard time handling mobs at your level mentoring/chrono mentoring to lower level might make it more manageable.

## Steps

1. Obtain:![Violet Velium Crystals](images/Violet_Velium_Crystals_%28Frostfang_Sea%29.png)
   - 6 x  [[Empyreal Scintilla]] in the [[Village of Shin]] (on [[Isle of Mara]]) around Tower of the Four Winds {{waypoint 13, 14, -106}} - guaranteed from blue nodes on the ground, also occasionally drop from [[A Four Wind wisp]]  who attack you (scale to your level) in the same area.
   - 5 x "Violet Velium Crystals" in [[Frostfang Sea]] - small crystals with purple sparkles up on snowy rocks around dock area, along path  and tunnel from dock up to New Halas, in [[Ravens' Roost]] (housing and crafting area of New Halas), in imp caves in Frostfang Sea {{waypoint 230, 77, -116}}

![Empyreal Scintilla](images/Empyreal_Scintilla.png)

1. Return to [[Abdur Hamdaan]] in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint -2086, 5, 133}}  He is dead.
1. Speak to his [[Apprentice Tavari]] hiding behind a rock nearby.
