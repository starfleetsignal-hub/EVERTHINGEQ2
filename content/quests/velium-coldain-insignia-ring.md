## Steps

1. Speak to Malidain Glacierbane in [[The Throne of the Dains]] in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain|Thurgadin]] {{waypoint 81, -277, 254}}
1. Kill 20 cavalry and 1 cavalry leader in [[The Battle of Storm Gorge]] public quest
   - **NOTE**: This may have to be a heroic or raid difficulty public quest to get these mobs to spawn. (A full single group can spawn the cavalry leader, 5 epic x2 mobs).
1. Return to Malidain in Thurgadin
   - **NOTE**: Must have the [[Adamantium Coldain Insignia Ring]] with you!
