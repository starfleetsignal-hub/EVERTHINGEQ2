## Notes

- **Warning**: Avoid killing the three Behemoth bosses, [[Leviathor'Consuma]], [[Leviathor'Glamar]], and [[Leviathor'Tentar]]. They are needed for the subsequent quest. If you do kill them, you will have to wait to reset the instance.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Chelsith]] and go to near {{waypoint -1, 21, 473}} for an update.
1. Kill the following mobs:
   - 20 [[A Yha-lei sentinel|Yha-lei sentinels]]
   - 20 [[A Yha-lei defender|Yha-lei defenders]]
1. Return to [[Yothe K'lohgk]].
