## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of Mithaniel Marr has asked for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Gather the requested supplies
   - Collect 3 [[Copperbound Bracer|copperbound bracers]] found in [[Aether Wroughtlands]]
     - *Note: drops in [[Chests|small chest]] from a copperbind soldier*
     - *1-2 drops per group member*
   - Gather or purchase [[Charger Meat|charger meat]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas
     - *Common harvest from BoZ shrub*
     - *Can buy off broker*
     - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Truthbringer]] {{waypoint -840, -48, 199}} in [[The Commonlands]]
