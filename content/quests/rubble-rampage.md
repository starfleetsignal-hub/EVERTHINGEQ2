The mining pick speaks to me, urges me to wrap my hands around it and pull it from its hold and slay the inanimate pieces of rubble strewn about the ruins! Because destruction!

## Notes

- You have 5 minutes to complete this quest.
- No group updates on any of the kills here. Solo or Solo (and if needed pet and maybe merc)
- Verified not group friendly....April-2016

## Steps

![](images/Rubble.png)

1. Equip the pickaxe.
   - This gives you the semblance of an undead miner and makes everything in the area non-aggro.
1. Destroy 10 rubble on [[Bluefist Battlefield]].
   - The rubble are small, round, dark grey boulders that occur near the ruins. They can be tracked.
   - Every so often upon breaking the rubble, you may be attacked by an [[An angry rock eater|angry rock eater]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +250 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
- [[Dirt encrusted key]]
