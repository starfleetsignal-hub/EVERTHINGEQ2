Bhoughbh Bhildur, a gnemlin tinkerer in Maldura's Aquifer Pumping Station, has nearly everything to assemble his mysterious machtraption! He just needs to forge some very durable metal. In order to do so, he requires slickstone and Abyssal ore.

## Steps

1. Gather ores
   - Mine 12 **slickstone ore** around the central drill in [[Outer Maldura]], around {{waypoint 433.96, 85.40, 409.89}}.
   - Mine 12 **Abyssal ore** in [[Abyssal Chasm]] or [[Abyssal Overlook]].
     1. Obtain 6 [[Gnemlish mini-boom|gnemlish mini-booms]] (each explosion yields 2 harvestables). Some possible locations:
        - From crates inside the [[Splitiron Mine]]  ![](images/Gnemlish_mini-booms.png)
          - one location is {{waypoint 185, 138, 241}}).
        - At the base of the great drill in [[Outer Maldura]]
          - {{waypoint 432.98, 85.37, 398.94}}
          - {{waypoint 387.07, 86.43, 332.81}}
          - {{waypoint 389, 86, 392}}
     1. Look for **Abyssal stone** along the [[Abyssal Overlook]]. Some possible locations:
        - {{waypoint 145.19, 69.37, -124.74}}  ![](images/Mighty_Magimorphic_Ore_quest_-_Abyssal_stone.jpg)
        - {{waypoint 161.38, 73.46, -132.09}}
        - {{waypoint 134.05, 93.42, 29.11}}
        - {{waypoint -209, 78, -5}}
        - {{waypoint -169.21, 61.74, -214.27}}
     1. Click the stone to use the boom stick, then collect the **Abyssal ore**.
1. Return to Speak to [[Bhoughbh Bhildur|Bhoughbh]]
