## Description

"Lady Astaar Evinien, a fae I met at Green Knoll, asked me to participate in the Ritual of Calling that helps welcome returning fae spirits to Kelethin and recognizes those lost during the Siege of Kelethin. She provided me with instructions as to how to perform the ritual." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Travel to the top of the [[Kelethin Central Stump]] {{waypoint 321, 92, 337}} in Kelethin and double-click the memorial stone; place your [[Simple offering]] on the stone
1. Return to Lady Astaar

## Rewards

- [[Memorial Fae Statue]]

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Lady Astaar Evinien: "Hello there, [Player Character]! Are you here to participate in the Fae Ritual of Calling?"
- Player Character: "What's that?"
- Lady Astaar Evinien: "It's a ritual that allows the fae, as well as those who honor our ways, to return to Kelethin whenever they wish."
- Player Character: "I'd love to do that."
- Lady Astaar Evinien: "Excellent! I have already heard of your prowess from others, so this will be easy for you. The fae use these simple rituals to help us retain our sense of community, especially with so many changes coming our way lately."
- Player Character: "What must I do?"
- Lady Astaar Evinien: "You've heard of the fae spirit buds and may have assumed that we are some type of plant! It's merely a figure of speech. Our spirit buds surround the city, observing and guiding us. When a spirit bud is ready for renewal, it chooses when and where to return. The ritual you'll perform welcomes a spirit back to us, helping it focus on our beloved city."
- Player Character: "I see."
- Lady Astaar Evinien: "Take this simple offering with you to the memorial inside Kelethin to those who fell during the Siege. The best way to welcome a new spirit is by honoring the spirits no longer with us."
- Player Character: "I will do this."

As my part in the Fae Ritual of Calling, I must place the offering Lady Astaar gave me at the memorial stone located on top of the Central Stump in the middle of Kelethin. Place the offering at the memorial stone.<blockquote>The weathered memorial stone is still legible. Its corners are worn smooth, as though it has been caressed by many hands over the years.

You  place your offering on the memorial and consider the effects of wars distant in time and place that affect those around you to this day.</blockquote>I placed the offering and learned about the Siege of Kelethin. I completed the ritual at the memorial in Kelethin. I should speak with Lady Astaar, back at Green Knoll near Kelethin. Return to Lady Astaar.

- Lady Astaar Evinien: "Have you placed the offering at the memorial?"
- Player Character: "Yes, I have."
- Lady Astaar Evinien: "Do you understand? Or do you have questions?"
  - Player Character: "It was a memorial to those who fell during the Siege of Kelethin."
  - Player Character: "I don't understand the meaning of the memorial."

- Lady Astaar Evinien: "The memorial was placed on the remains of a tree burnt during the Siege. Many, not just fae, lost their lives in that time...so we honor the returning fae spirits that much more. We are glad that they can return, you see?"
- Player Character: "I see."
- Lady Astaar Evinien: "May Tunare guide you, [Player Character]! You've shown the fae respect by honoring our ritual. I'm pleased to present you with your very own Memorial Fae Statue."
- Player Character: "Thank you."

I spoke with Lady Astaar. Lady Astaar Evinien was happy to know that I've completed my part in the ritual.

I paid my respects at the memorial to those lost in the Siege of Kelethin and received a memorial statue of my own in return.
