## Notes

- This quest has been updated in 2023 to scale to all levels. Players level 1 - 119 will automatically enter scalable instance [[Innovation: Tinkerer's Trial]]. Players level 120+ have a choice of [[Innovation: Tinkerer's Trial (Solo)]] or [[Innovation: Tinkerer's Trial (Heroic)]] instance.
- This quest is repeatable once per year, however once this quest has been completed, you can reset the zone and run it again for loot or to do daily mission:
  - Level 120+ players will be offered an additional mission: [[Not Easy Being Green (Event)]] will be automatically scribed in quest journal every 18 hours when zoning into [[Innovation: Tinkerer's Trial (Solo)]] or [[Innovation: Tinkerer's Trial (Heroic)]]. You need to complete this mission six times to get the [[Tinkerer's Trial Terminated]] achievement.

## Steps

1. Go to [[Moors of Ykesha]] and talk to [[Tanston]] at {{waypoint 1747, 448, 731}}
1. Gather **planar magnetite**. Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint 1730.42, 452.56, 768.78}}  ![](images/Blood,_Sweat,_and_Gears_quest_-_planar_magnetite.jpg)
   - {{waypoint 1789.23, 452.56, 810.428}}
   - {{waypoint 1814.60, 452.56, 834.02}}
   - {{waypoint 1878.54, 449.30, 833.43}}
   - {{waypoint 1826.88, 452.56, 840.62}}
   - {{waypoint 1814.09, 452.56, 847.90}}
   - {{waypoint 1797.78, 452.56, 877.98}}
   - {{waypoint 1826.88, 452.56, 840.62}}
   - {{waypoint -202.00, 4.26, -154.90}}
1. Return to [[Tanston]]
1. Enter [[Innovation: Tinkerer’s Trial (Solo)|Innovation: Tinkerer’s Trial [Solo]]] for VoV+ players or [[Innovation: Tinkerer's Trial (Heroic)|Innovation: Tinkerer's Trial [Heroic]]] for the scaled version of the zone. (behind Tanston.) ![Innovation: Tinkerer's Trial Entrance](images/Innovation_Tinkerer's_Trial.png)
1. Talk to [[Boegie]] at {{waypoint -71, -10, 109}} He says, "Here we go." Follow him through the door, then he disappears.
   - You will pass through several doors to get to [[Boegie]]. You can dodge many of the aggro mechanisms.
1. Talk to [[Boegie]] at {{waypoint -115, 5, -158}}
1. Talk to [[Gniles Gnimbletok]] at {{waypoint -64.30, 5.29, -208.40}}.
1. Talk to Boegie at {{waypoint -115.19, 5.54, -159.00}}
1. Run or evac to the front of the zone and kill [[Springcrawler]] on top of one of the junk heaps around {{waypoint 131.99, -0.81, 93.67}} for the **friend/foe MCE processor**.
   - possible locations {{waypoint 130, -2, 133}} or {{waypoint 94, 0, 82}} or {{waypoint 106, 1, 160}} or {{waypoint 48, 0, 102}}
1. Go through [[The Waste Duct]] at {{waypoint 174.70, 3.64, -66.60}}; don’t get shredded by [[A clockwork shredder|the clockwork shredder]].
   1. Stand back from the end of the inlet pipe as the Shredder will kill a character standing at the end of the inlet pipe.
   1. Wait for the Shredder to move from right to left (when viewed from the inlet pipe).
   1. Turn right and move quickly down the transfer pipe until you reach the exit pipe.
   1. Turn right and exit the pipes.
1. Pass through [[Bilge Commons]] to press the **Red Button: Clockwork Shredder Self-Destruct** {{waypoint 74.93, 10.04, -212.63}} to kill the clockwork shredders.
1. Kill the roamer [[Bzzzzt Bzzap]] for the hardened flight casing around {{waypoint -54.14, 15.73, -279.24}}
1. Return to [[Boegie]] at {{waypoint -115.19, 5.54, -159.00}}.
   - Can start using Track Harvestables after this point for the **Coil of Reinforced Shielded Wiring** as it may be found on the path to the two Named MOBs.
1. Kill [[Enerjaws]] for the **balancing control gimble** at {{waypoint 24.85, 12.72, -242.31}}
1. Kill [[The Clockwork Collector]] for the **advanced targeting processing unit** at {{waypoint 59.31, 3.44, -37.21}}
1. Loot one coil of **reinforced shielded wiring** (can be located using Track Harvestables). A message will appear once you have completed this step. Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint 8, 3, -51}}
   - {{waypoint 70, 3, -4}}
   - {{waypoint 11, 3, 2}}
   - {{waypoint 56, 4, 127}}
   - {{waypoint -136, 3, -17}}
   - {{waypoint -175, 4, -64}}
   - {{waypoint -188, 4, -111}}
   - {{waypoint -190, 4, -244}}
   - {{waypoint -233, 4, -221}}
1. Return to Boegie at {{waypoint -115, 6, -159}}
1. Activate the [[Sprocket Gearsniffer]] from inventory. In any order go to the following locations (though this list is in the best order assuming you evac to the front door to start), then look for a floating smoke ring in the vicinity:
   - [[Springcrawler|Springcrawler's]] former location{{waypoint 106, -10, 125}}
   - [[The Clockwork Collector|The Clockwork Collector's]] former location {{waypoint 43, 4, -25}}
   - [[Enerjaws|Enerjaws']] former location {{waypoint 26, 13, -243}} (from this location jump down to the next location)
   - [[Bzzzzt Bzzap|Bzzzzt Bzzap's]] former location {{waypoint 26, 3, -274}}
   - Just before door on way to Rhino at 59 4 -127.
1. Kill [[Cogsteady Rhinocticus]] at {{waypoint 120, 3, -139}} (When it becomes invulnerable and starts to charge you, lead it into the electrified gears.)
1. Talk to Boegie at {{waypoint 84, 3, -126}}
