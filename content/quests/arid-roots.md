## Notes

Requires -9,700 faction with Nye'Caelona.

## Steps

![root-rotted typharush](images/Root-rotted_typharush.jpg)

1. Remove 8 root-rotted typharush found throughout the [[Gardens of Nye'Caelona]].
1. Then remove another one for good measure

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 15108 status
- +4000 faction with **Nye'Caelona**
- One of the following:
  - [[Wrap of Magma Scale]]
  - [[Cloak of Mesa Stone]]
