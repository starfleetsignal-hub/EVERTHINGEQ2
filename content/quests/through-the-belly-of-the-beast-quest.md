Note: the rares will be for making the pacification items (5 uses per rare if 4 full bars) which 10shots is *bare minimum* to get through beasties **if** you have been through the run on another character before.  Recommendation: Bring more of your specific rare to make more pacification items. [there are other Tradeskill stations along the way]

*(At adv level 130 I didn't use the pacification item at all. I got attacked twice but just killed the mobs. The quest will update nonetheless when you reach the two light spots at the ends of the tunnel. However, you need to craft one pacification item for the quest update beforehand.)*

## Steps

1. Enter [[Echo Caverns]] {{waypoint 393, -36, 748}} zone.
1. Make your way to {{waypoint 138.92, 108.32, -10.83}} and enter [[Echo Caverns: Recuso Digsite (Tradeskill)]].
   - *Look for the boarded-up door at the bottom of the quarry near where you zoned in.*
1. Contact [[Excavator Gibbons]] {{waypoint 480.98, 203.67, -112.27}}. Your M.A.G.I.C. device will (technically) give you [[Recipe Scroll: Anti-Aggression Techniques Vol IV]], but in practice it just scribes two "calming" recipes (ie [[Calming Eucalyptus Spray]] and [[Calming Epilogue of Sleep]], [[Denmum's Calming Vulrich Drumstick]], [[Wracked Calming Hourglass]], etc) into your recipe book.
1. Create one of the calming items to help with the wildlife's aggression.
   - Use Varig's Forge {{waypoint 511.94, 201.51, -168.75}}.
   - *The exact recipes you get will vary by tradeskill profession. Search your recipe book for "calm" or "calming".*
   - *You may wish to make both items, so you can use one while the other is down.*
1. Use the newly created item(s) to calm the nearby aggressive creatures.
   - *Note: The creatures will not be completely non-aggro, as they'll just have their aggro range lowered for 30 seconds, so steer clear of those after calming them.
   1. Go east through the cave, and as you go, note that there is a branch to the right.
   1. Calm the wildlife to both parts of the tunnel.
      - *It's not necessary to calm everything. Look for places you have room to walk by without aggro.*
      - *You might want to do the south-west branch first -- there's more hard-to-get-by aggro this way.*
      - With carefully chosen movement, it is possible to do this and only have to calm three NPC's (even as low as two, none on the left, two on the right), so don't over-think it. As soon as you reach the light at the end of each tunnel, all creatures in that tunnel turns into NPC.
   1. Step into the beam of light at each end of the tunnel {{waypoint 346, 190, -148}} and {{waypoint 556, 221, -20}} to get the updates.
1. Report back to [[Excavator Gibbons]] (everything in the tunnel will now be non-aggro).
1. Speak with [[Kessi McGillicuddy]] in the [[Luclin Nexus]] {{waypoint 353.28, -36.15, 716.81}}.
1. From [[City of Fordel Midst]] at {{waypoint 393, -36, 748}} enter [[Echo Caverns: Expedition Composition (Tradeskill)]] by clicking the door and selecting the appropriate zone.
   - *Accessed via the Echo Caverns door in the Nexus*.
   - You need to be lvl 120 in your tradeskill for the zone to show up on the list.
   - Note if you log out you will need to re-enter the zone.
1. Speak with [[Pops McGillicuddy]] {{waypoint 23.17, 115.83, -211.11}}.
1. Follow [[Pops McGillicuddy]] up the nearby ramp and speak with him again at {{waypoint -35.21, 142.05, -258.05}}. He takes a ridiculous amount of time to get there.
1. After [[Pops McGillicuddy]] flies off, use the dirigible to follow him.
   - *The dirigible takes a moment to arrive. A very long moment.*
1. Speak to [[Pops McGillicuddy]] when you land to get [[Recipe Scroll: Tuning Device]].
1. Craft 2 [[Tuning Device|Tuning Devices]] *(you will receive 8 total)*.
   - 10 [[Pops' All-Purpose Coal]] *Pick up two sets of coal from the box beside the crafting table {{waypoint 351.22, 190.14, -157.89}}*
   - 20 [[Humming Azurite]]
   - 20 [[Luclizite Cluster]]
   - 20 [[Lunar Mushroom]]
   - 20 [[Radiant Algae]]
1. Use the [[Tuning Device]] on the broken sensors.
   - *The sensors look like yellow tinkered cylinders*.
1. Take the transportation to the next area {{waypoint 556.06, 217.84, -12.67}}
   - *Look for the **Jet Pack**.![Jetpack](images/Through_the_Belly_of_the_Beast_-_Jetpack.jpg) You will need to dismount from your equipped mount before you can use the jet pack.*
1. Speak to Pops where he's hanging off the platform at {{waypoint 477.76, 178.43, 589.38}}
1. Speak to Pops again after he gets up on the platform to receive [[Recipe Scroll: Tinkered Autoscope Mach XIII]].
1. Go to the [[Varig's Forge]] {{waypoint 455.80, 191.43, 635.83}} and craft a [[Tinkered Autoscope Mach XIII]]![Crafting Table with box of Coal](images/Through_the_Belly_of_the_Beast_CraftingTable_at_the_next_Plattform.jpg)
   - 12 [[Humming Azurite]]
   - 12 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
   - 12 [[Luclizite Cluster]]
   - 12 [[Radiant Algae]]
   - 5 [[Pops' All-Purpose Coal]] *Fuel is available in the box next to [[Varig's Forge]] {{waypoint 455.80, 191.43, 635.83}}.*
1. Place and test the [[Tinkered Autoscope Mach XIII|Autoscope]] on the corner of the platform {{waypoint 514.07, 186.42, 634.04}}.
   - *Click to place it, then click again to test it.*
1. Follow Pops by using the provided dirigible. Speak to Pops after landing {{waypoint -431.52, 250.71, 76.11}}.
1. Find a better location for the landing area with a "good view of the falls" {{waypoint -306.32, 252.24, 147.60}}.
1. Return to Pops {{waypoint -431.15, 250.51, 78.26}} to tell him about the new spot.
1. Use the dirigible to return to the entrance.
1. Speak to [[Kessi McGillicuddy]] {{waypoint -35.11, 100.09, -215.25}}.
1. Return to [[Hulda Flintbraid (Nexus)|Hulda]] in the [[Luclin Nexus]].
