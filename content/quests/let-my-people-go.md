## Notes

- You must have +40,000 faction with the [[Ironblood Sprouts (Faction)|Ironblood Sprouts]].
- The 5 Quests [[Still Thin Around the Gills]], [[Still A Fun Guy]], [[More Briny Shell Smell]], [[More Spore Sport]] and [[Even Oozier Food]] can be repeated 10 times each, which will give you 40,000 faction with the [[Ironblood Sprouts (Faction)|Ironblood Sprouts]].

## Conversation

A mysterious ironblood myconid by the name of Verna Amanita has asked for my aid in securing his home back from invading forces! Now that you have secured the area he has asked that you help bring his people over to the new home so they can spread their clan to the new farm and ensure their survival!

## Steps

1. Guide 6 sporelings to the new farm from the [[Ironblood Fungus Farm]].
   - Use the [[An ironblood sprout leader|ironblood sprout leader]] given to you by [[Verna Amanita|Verna]] on one **ironblood sprout** at a time, then bring them to the [[Abandoned Fungus Farm]].
1. Return to [[Verna Amanita|Verna]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +10,000 faction with **Ironblood Sprouts**
- [[Befriended Fungi Necklace]]

## Notes

This quest may be repeated up to ten times.
