## Steps

Nothing is free and building, maintaining, and supporting the herald's camp is expensive. That's why the herald of Drinal has requested that we assist in offsetting the camp's cost by retrieving a valuable relic guarded by Shanrazad the Spared. I need to retrieve the relic.

1. Retrieve the relic guarded by Shanrazad the Spared in [[Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields (Solo)]]
   - The relic is at {{waypoint 74, 14, -557}} on the eastern edge of the platform.
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Silver Reaper]] {{waypoint 85, -3, 291}} in [[Loping Plains]]
