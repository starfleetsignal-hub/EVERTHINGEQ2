1. Collect two ancient roekillik skulls. (They appear as red and shiny.)
   - Possible locations are as follows:
     - Spiral Staircase - {{waypoint -165, 132, -970}}
     - Down elevator and down the SW path - {{waypoint -17, -97, -1032}}
     - By Doomni's Cage - {{waypoint -16.89, -97.73, -1033.56}}
     - {{waypoint 42.86, -105.05, -914.91}}
     - {{waypoint -230.21, -94.53, -987.55}}
