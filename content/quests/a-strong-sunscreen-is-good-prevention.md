## Prerequisites

- Neriak citizenship

## Steps

1. In [[Nektulos Forest]] (take the road east from the [[Ulteran Spires (Nek)|Ulteran Spires]]):
   - Obtain 4 viscous pectin from [[Sap urchin]]s.
     - Many of these can be found on both sides of the road.
   - Find 8 relentless cartilage from [[Lost dragoon]] skeletons.![](images/Lost_dragoons.jpg)
1. Take the pectin and cartilage to [[Tibulus D'Zaren]] at the [[Forge of the Blue Flame]] {{waypoint -14, 23, -19}}.
1. Take the first [[Canister of ameliorate balm]] to [[Kirak N'Tan]] at the [[Seloxia Stronghold]] {{waypoint -89, 15, 371}}.
1. Take the second [[Canister of ameliorate balm]] to [[Imtel K'Myth]] in [[Cristanos Plaza]] {{waypoint -527, 22, 240}} for your reward.
