## Steps

1. Perform the following:
   - Collect pollen from 5 flowers in the [[Forest Barrens]] near {{waypoint -43, 64, 250}}. Possible locations:![](images/Pollen_flowers.png)
     - {{waypoint -47, 63, 270}}
     - {{waypoint -114, 72, 365}}
     - {{waypoint -72, 75, 465}}
     - {{waypoint -30, 63, 324}}
     - {{waypoint -91, 51, 513}}
     - {{waypoint -88, 28, 568}}
     - Note: The flowers do not despawn. If one has been harvested too recently you will get the message *This bud has no more pollen to collect. Look for another flower.* Alternatively, wait a few minutes and the flower will pling again and again and again...
   - Use the [[Pollen Sprayer]] from your inventory 5 times to coat your gear. Apparently, you MUST do this while in Vegarlson; doing it anywhere else will NOT work and will waste a pollen!
1. Walk up to an arborian in the [[Collapsed Mesa]] {{waypoint 58.02, 159.71, -96.19}}
1. Return to Temry Loneivy at {{waypoint -356, 41, -386}}. He's not there.
1. Find Temry Loneivy at {{waypoint -335, 35, -35}} and speak with him.

## Rewards

- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
