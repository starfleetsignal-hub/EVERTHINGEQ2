## Steps

1. Search for 6 odd artifacts.  These are clods of dirt in the snow near the public quest area for Echoes of the Ring War, just southwest of the camp.
   - {{waypoint 136, -336, -460}}
   - {{waypoint 226, -339, -475}}
   - {{waypoint 256, -333, -433}}
   - {{waypoint 303, -336, -445}}
   - {{waypoint 275, -328, -397}}
   - {{waypoint 208, -334, -446}}
1. Return to Adebi
1. Speak to [[Student Genn]]he is at the mender.
1. Gather 8 peices of steam essence from the [[A steam walker|steam walkers]] located around {{waypoint -4, -378, -721}} and {{waypoint -496, -402, -730}}
1. Return to Genn
1. Wait a bit for Genn to clean the artifacts (about 10 minutes real time), then speak to him again
1. Return to Adebi
1. Speak to [[Assistant Beldry]]
1. Search near the Tizmak caves {{waypoint -338, -354, -285}} for 6 missing pages (they look like collectible pages)
   - {{waypoint -250, -394, -248}}
   - {{waypoint -218, -404, -119}}
   - {{waypoint -138, -403, -203}}
   - {{waypoint -200, -410, -52}}
   - {{waypoint -541, -384, -118}}
   - {{waypoint -528, -384, -166}}
   - They can be found in the "Y" entry halls to the cave and more can be found in the  lower section of the caves.
1. Return to Adebi, where quest starts.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
