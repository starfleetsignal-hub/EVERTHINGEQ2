## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Go to the Thieves' Way grate in West Freeport {{waypoint 291, -4, -20}} and you will be attacked by a group of 3 vv level-scaled party crashing orcs.
1. After defeating them click on the two crates nearby. Additional orcs may spawn after clicking on the crates!
1. Place the six [[Pack of magical paper lanterns (Freeport)|magical paper lanterns]] around West and South Freeport. The locations are marked by green sparklies. Locations are random, here are possible ones:
   1. First lantern in West Freeport may be found at:
      - {{waypoint 187, -3, -46}} east of The Arena
      - {{waypoint 208, -4, -82}} in front of the bank
      - {{waypoint 133, -11, -34}} on the path down between The Commonlands gate and Execution Plaza
   1. Second lantern in West Freeport may be found at:
      - {{waypoint 106, -16, 121}} outside the Freeport Militia House
      - {{waypoint 293, -4, 58}} north of the Blood Haze Inn (behind the Arena)
      - {{waypoint 266, -5, 154}} behind the statue of the Overlord and next to the Blood Haze Inn
   1. Third lantern in West Freeport may be found at:
      - {{waypoint 240, -3, 94}} near the Blood Haze Inn
      - {{waypoint 98, -20, 12}} near Execution Plaza
      - {{waypoint 119, -20, 15}} near Execution Plaza
      - {{waypoint 107, -16, 121}} in front of Militia House
   1. Fourth lantern in South Freeport may be found at:
      - {{waypoint 51, -12, 209}} between the gates to Big Bend and West Freeport
      - {{waypoint -97, -25, 159}} by the East Freeport gate
      - {{waypoint -50, -8, 196}} outside the T1 guild hall
   1. Fifth lantern in South Freeport may be found at:
      - {{waypoint -109, -18, 264}} near near the Skyblade Skiff and Mistmoore Crags Estate
      - {{waypoint -162, -30, 260}} at the stairs below 5 Freedom Road
      - {{waypoint -184, -38, 314}} near the Temple Street gate
   1. Sixth lantern in South Freeport may be found at:
      - {{waypoint -145, -35, 170}} near 1 Freedom Road
      - {{waypoint -168, -47, 204}} on the stone stairwell near the dock area
      - {{waypoint -278, -58, 136}} next to the Scout guild boat
      - {{waypoint -265, -56, 203}} on the southernmost dock
1. Return to [[Slinks Hardtoes]] in [[West Freeport]] when you're done.
