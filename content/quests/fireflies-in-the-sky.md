## Note

This quest is only offered once you have accepted [[Of Duty and Fate: My Brother's Keeper]].

## Steps

1. I am to obtain a 'Pouch of "Magic" Sand' and bring it back to Arkemitus for a receipt.
   - *Around the Pakiat Bluffs in [[Plane of Magic]] -- possible spawn  {{waypoint -715.98, 17.89, -815.16}}*  (you will not get a quest update from the step but it will be in your inventory, if you would like to check.) Don't just click the golden glowing, there is a gold nugget to be clicked on.
   - Return to Arkemitus in Splendor Sky Aerie at {{waypoint 1016, 169, 691}}
   - **The quest updates when you BUY a receipt for your "Pouch of Magic Sand" for a Spiritwell Receipt.**
1. Obtain a receipt from delivering **3** requested items from the Glass Blower's needs listing
   - *Exchange 5 of  **ANY** Ballads of Zimara rare harvest  for a Spiritwell Receipt -- (you need 3 sets of 5 Total)*
     - 5 [[Reinhopper Meat]] or
     - 5 [[Nilborien Hide]] or
     - 5 [[Dichroic Titanium]] or
     - 5 [[Ammolite]] or
     - 5 [[Rainbow Corymbia Wood]] or
     - 5 [[Circinata Root]] or
     - 5 [[Prismatic Pike]] or
     - 5 [[Persimmon]] or
     - 5 [[Dasheen]]
1. Obtain a receipt from delivering a second requested item *(as per step 2)*
1. Obtain a receipt from delivering a third and final requested item *(as per step 2)*.
1. Speak to [[Arkemitus Spiritwell]] in Splendor Sky Aerie at {{waypoint 1016, 169, 691}}

## Rewards

- [[Empty Containment Spiritwell Vessel]] - *Required by [[Of Duty and Fate: My Brother's Keeper]]*
