## Notes

- Mesmer needs your help to stop The Overlord's forces from using his Magika Machina Magnifica within Dragon necropolis.
- You receive [[Recipe Scroll: Mesmer's Machinery]] with 3 auto scribing recipes in it.
- Materials needed:
  - 33 Ilmenite Ore
  - 9 Chalcedony
  - 45 Zimaran Coal
- Portable crafting stations (optional, but handy, can be an item or an ability)

**RARES**

- [[Porphyry Stone]] (Rare): Required by [[Scholar]] - [[Glittering Tiara]]
- [[Mountain Blueberry]] (Rare): Required by [[Craftsman]] - [[Colorful Basket]]
- [[Ulthork Hide]] (Rare): Required by [[Outfitter]] - [[Colorful Shawl]]

## Steps

1. Collect 3 [[Kelp Bubble|Kelp Bubbles]]
   - *plants in water outside of camp {{waypoint 60, 7, 712}}, Harvest Tracking works for these*
1. Create 3 x [[Phantasmagnetic Core|Phantasmagnetic Cores]]
   - *each uses 1 [[Kelp Bubble]], 5 [[Ilmenite Ore]] and 5 [[Zimaran Coal]]*
1. Collect 3 [[Sharp Bones]]
   - *among aggressive ulthorks outside of camp around {{waypoint 134, 8, 594}}, use your [[W.H.A.T. V2]] if needed*
1. Create 3 x [[Embellished Magitronic Gears]]
   - *each uses 1 [[Sharp Bones|Sharp Bone]], 5 [[Ilmenite Ore]] and 5 [[Zimaran Coal]]*
1. Obtain [[Mesmeric Shell Powder]]
   - travel to the NorthWest and speak with a siren called [[Nazeera]] at {{waypoint 145, 9, -422}} and do her side quest.
   - *To speak to her you cannot be in your W.H.A.T. form so getting to her might require a bit of mob dodging, travel towards Frozen Nestland around {{waypoint 355, 39, 435}} and take a sharp North towards ramp down to sea around {{waypoint 379, 17, 135}}. Swim over the water to [[Nazeera]] (may few aggressive fish to watch out for).*
   - Nazeera offers a different quest for each of the three major crafting professions. All of them reward 3 Mesmeric Shell Powders, required to advance your main quest.  You will need to return to her after doing the subquest, so portable craft stations are handy if you have the necessary materials on hand. Otherwise can craft in a published house or guild hall.
     - [[Equitable Exchange: Craftsman]] - *([[Provisioner]], [[Carpenter]] or [[Woodworker]])*
     - [[Equitable Exchange: Outfitter]] - *([[Tailor]], [[Armorer]] or [[Weaponsmith]])*
     - [[Equitable Exchange: Scholar]] - *([[Sage]], [[Alchemist]] or [[Jeweler]])*
1. Head back to base camp and craft 3 [[Incorporeal Confusilation Emanators]]
   - each uses 1 [[Mesmeric Shell Powder]], 3 [[Chalcedony]], 1 [[Ilmenite Ore]] and 5 [[Zimaran Coal]].
1. Return to [[Mesmer Gearloose]] at {{waypoint 69, 7, 642}} in the [[Western Wastes]]
   - *Note: He may not have the quest reward icon above his head*

- [[Status Coin (75,000)|Status Coin [75,000]]]
