## Steps

1. Go to [[Everfrost]] and speak with [[Kaira L'or]] in [[Icespire Caverns]]. {{waypoint -167.09, 45.62, -679.63}}
1. Enter the [[Vestibule of the Sacred Temple]] by clicking on 'an icy boulder' behind where Kaira L'or was. {{waypoint -164, 47, -681}}
   - Mobs inside are [[A Vestibule defender|Vestibule defenders]], [[A frosty pest|frosty pests]] and [[A frosted bubble|frosted bubbles]], ranging from level 60 to 62. Defenders will be non-aggro if you've never gained their hate by killing any of the winged maidens outside.
1. Speak with [[The Guardian]]. {{waypoint 11, 3, -636}}
   - The correct answer to her riddle is '11'.
   - If you answer the riddle incorrectly, she will aggro and attack. Killing her causes faction loss with The Tribunal.
1. Use the first *Sacred Vessel*. {{waypoint 8, 4, -641}}
1. Use the second *Sacred Vessel*. {{waypoint 62, -1, -641}}
1. Use the third *Sacred Vessel*. {{waypoint 26, -1, -567}}
   - The rune key is now [[Gavel of Entry]].
1. Return to Justinian Theo.

## Rewards

- +7500 faction with **The Tribunal**
- [[Justinian's Altar of the Tribunal]]
