## Steps

1. Harvest the following:
   - 18 [[Dewpalm lumber]] from [[Fallen dewpalm branch]]
   - 15 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Coalesced reef]]
   - 15 [[Silvered fleck]] from [[Flecked mass]]
   - 10 [[Strangler root]] from [[Strangler root break]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe: Weapon Rack]]
   - Note: recipe retained in Recipe Book(N) and not issued on subsequent missions.
1. Craft **a** [[Weapon Rack]] on a [[Work Bench]].
   - Note: quest does NOT detail *which* weapon rack the "a" refers to, suggesting *any* weapon rack would meet request.
   - Since trial and error resulted in a Work Bench to make a miniature, desktop-sized "weapon rack" not a REAL weapon rack as one would make on a Woodworking Table.
1. Speak to [[Bondrin Steelfist]] at {{waypoint 75.14, -8.46, -75.25}}.
