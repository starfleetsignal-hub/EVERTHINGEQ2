## Notes

- This quest is repeatable up to 5 times.

## Steps

1. Kill 10 of one of the following creatures (randomly chosen):
   - [[A refuse toad]] *(found throughout hallways)*
   - [[A sift dasher]] *(found throughout hallways)*
   - [[An immature sludgewater]] *(found throughout hallways)*
   - [[A drudge globule]] {{waypoint -117, 0, -123}}
1. Return to Marcus.

  - At least 3s 2c - 2nd completion
  - At least 6s 64c - 3rd completion
  - At least 10s 26c - 4th completion
