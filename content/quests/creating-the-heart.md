## Notes

To receive this quest, you must have +40,000 faction (Ally status) with the Defenders of the Seal.

## Steps

![Fae vs Heart of Darkness vase](images/EQ2_heartsofdarkness.jpg)

1. [[Thaqib]] ask that you collect rare materials to concoct the Heart of Odus Potion.
   - 8 Widow's Tears, flowers can be found in the graveyard garden around Thaqib
   - 5 Kaborite flakes, obvious shimmering square blocks on ground past second tunnel in Underfoot area. {{waypoint -91, 72, -787.87}}
   - 6 Hearts of Darkness {{waypoint -292.41, -9.74, -22.50}}  from within Old Paineel, across gorge, found in the fairly large turqoise vases along the top of the elevated path (battlements)
1. Return to Thaqib

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]]
- At least 55g
- +2500 faction with **Defenders of the Seal**
