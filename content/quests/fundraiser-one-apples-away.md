One of the contests that Deputy Hopple is using as a fundraiser for the planned museum means I'll have to get three wild apple muffins. If I'm not up to the task I can always buy my way out of this one, but I'm betting the prize will be something useful only to a skilled provisioner. Either way, I'm guessing that the muffin on display will be sold to raise funds, while Deputy Hopple plans to gobble up the two he requested *for tasting.* It's for a good cause, anyway.

## Steps

1. Make (or obtain) 3 [[Wild Apple Muffin|Wild Apple Muffins]].
   - Make them yourself, have someone else make them, or buy them from the broker. See the talk page for further information.
1. Return to [[Deputy Hopple]]
