It looks like my apprentice could use some new equipment. I should craft them a Sturdy Boiled Apron to speed up their progress.

## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Boiled Apron for my apprentice.
   - I need to craft a Sturdy Boiled Apron for my apprentice.
     - Acquire the following ingredients and fuel:
       - 1 "Boiled hide" --> any T3 hide you can trap, e.g. [[Boiled leather pelt]]
       - 4 [[Sparkling filament]]
     - Using a [[Loom]], craft 1 [[A Sturdy Boiled Apron]]
1. I need to take this apron back to my apprentice.
   - I need to take this apron back to my apprentice.

- [[Pouch of Unused Materials]]
