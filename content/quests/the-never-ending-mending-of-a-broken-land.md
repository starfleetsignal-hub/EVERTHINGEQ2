## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

If you're doing this to grind for your Epic 2.0, you're going to get tired very easily. Here's some help to make it go quicker...

1. Once you pick up the quest, immediately use the Jin'tu's Gift to get to the Hua Mein village.
1. Do the arena then fly to Toxxulia and kill the 10 creatures.
1. (Fly over to Kerra isle and pick up the quest)
   - If you're doing this on repeat, see step 6 substep on how to avoid this extra trip
1. Fast travel out and then back into Sundered Frontier, to the Druid Rings
1. Find the spirit
1. Fly back up to Kerra isle
   - If repeating, you should take the quest again BEFORE accepting the quest reward. This will allow you to avoid step 3 next time.
   - If you're a fast zoner, you may find it quicker to quick travel out and back into the spire and fly from there, especially if the previous step sent you quite far South West
1. Evac. If you don't have evac, Fast travel out and back into the spire, take the portal to Paineel and hand in.

## Steps

1. Complete the diplomatic tasks for Paineel, Kerra Isle, and the Hua Mein once more.
   - Kill any combination of 10 [[A Toxxulia guardian|Toxxulia guardians]], [[A Toxxulia winged sentinel|Toxxulia winged sentinels]] or [[A Toxxulia winged avenger|Toxxulia winged avengers]] around {{waypoint 275, 125, 2748}}
   - Head to Kerra Isle and speak with [[Keyta Wicker]] at {{waypoint 1374, 49, 2196}}. Complete her quest, [[A Message with Spirit]], to obtain the [[Kerra Shaman's Token]].
   - Head to the Hua Mein village and speak with [[Shi'jan Sensou]] {{waypoint -413, 278, 3212}}. Complete his quest, [[Into the Arena]], to obtain the [[Arena Master's Token]].
1. Return to [[Elder Shahib Zaim]] to receive your reward.
