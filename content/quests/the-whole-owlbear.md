## Steps

1. Hunt 8 [[An owlbear soulstarer|owlbear soulstarers]] in [[Soulstare Forest]]
1. Return to [[Kapasha Talmil]].
1. Click the [[Stone of Sahteb Mahlni]] {{waypoint -324, 120, -126}} to cast the offering into the [[Whisperling Falls]].
1. Return to [[Kapasha Talmil]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- [[Ancient Khati Sha Headdress]]
