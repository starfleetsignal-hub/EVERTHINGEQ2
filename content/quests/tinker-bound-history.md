Rosealyn has lost pages from her book "The Minds of Meldrath" and you volunteer to find them for her.

## Steps

![](images/Missing_page_%28Tinker-Bound_History%29.png)

1. Find pages at Tinkerfest locations. They look like the usual 'fluttering paper' harvestable, but with gold sparkles rising from them.
   - In either [[The City of Freeport]] or [[Qeynos Capitol District]] (2)  (tends to be right around the "Inventor's Threshold"/Tinkering platform area.)
   - At the [[Dropship Landing Zone]] in [[Moors of Ykesha]] (2)
   - In [[Gorowyn]] in [[Timorous Deep]] (2)
   - In [[New Halas]] in [[Frostfang Sea]] (2)
1. Return to Rosealyn in Steamfont Mountains
1. Speak to [[Kripkie Clatterpyle]] about getting the book bound.  He's in the basement of [[Frugrin Observatory]] {{waypoint -962, 156, 723}}
1. Defeat the [[A papernado|papernado]] (scales ^ solo)
1. Speak to Kripkie Clatterpyle again
1. Return to Rosealyn

## Rewards

- [[The Minds of Meldrath]]
- 5 [[Silver Jubilation Medal]]
- 5 [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog|Shiny Tinkerfest Cogs]]
