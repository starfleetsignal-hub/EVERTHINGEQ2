## Description

In the depths of the dungeon known as Befallen, a new threat has emerged. A dark offshoot of Solusek Ro followers have formed a necromantic cult known as the Burning Dead and are attempting to harness the dark energies generated there from the curse of Gynok Moltor. I've been asked to collect samples from both the crystalline structures and the necrotic oozes found in the Necrotic Assylum to determine jsut how pervasive the taint of Gynok's curse really is.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Befallen: Necrotic Asylum]] and
   - Collect 4 samples (blue crystals) from the crystalline structures {{waypoint -87, -26, 51}}
   - Collect 6 samples from of necrotic oozes {{waypoint -49, -14, 68}}
1. Return to Squire Hadley in the Commonlands

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
