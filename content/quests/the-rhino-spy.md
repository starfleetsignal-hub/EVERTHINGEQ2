## Steps

1. Go to [[The Rhinoceros Pen]] in [[Chardok]] and examine the 'trampled iksar' (aka Grukus). {{waypoint -390, -48, -413}}
1. Kill [[A sokokar ravager]] to receive [[Icy Powders]]. {{waypoint -247, -39, -357}}
1. Kill [[The Reservoir Defender]] in [[The Water Chamber]]. {{waypoint -515.80, -87.31, -339.62}}
1. Examine *The Ewer of Koldokus* at the back of the Water Chamber and defeat the four [[An aqua sentinel|aqua sentinels]] that spawn. {{waypoint -515, -88, -356}}
1. Return to [[Warlord Gadar Gwalnax]] in [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]].
