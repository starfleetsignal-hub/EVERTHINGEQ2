## Steps

1. Click on the recipe book next to Reedip and scribe the *Tupta Traditional Artisanry* which gets placed in your inventory.
1. Make 5 special mixtures of [[Froglok plaster]] (the wasp threading the frogloks use to build their homes) at a stove and keg.  There is one at {{waypoint -5, -54.27, 842.5}}
1. Return to Reedip.

You can find the components as follows:

- Threading (1 required for each combination) in wasp nests on the sides of the tuptan huts or on the roots/branches near the huts
- Shelf fungus grows on the side of the trees (2 needed for each combination).
- Fresh clay is on ground near the waterfall near {{waypoint -109.78, -91.79, 593.81}}) (3 needed for each combination)

## Rewards

- At least 47g 11s 70c
- +2000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
- -500 faction with **Clan Grobb**
