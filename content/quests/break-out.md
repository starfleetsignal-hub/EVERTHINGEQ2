## Steps

1. I have to find and free all 6 prisoners.
   - They are locked in cages throughout [[The Outer Vault]]. Killing named mobs gives you the keys.
     - I have to free Gnimliks {{waypoint 22, 83, -524}}
     - I have to free Cronos {{waypoint -122, 81, -789}}
     - I have to free Kahnder {{waypoint 68, -107, -832}}
     - I have to free Doomi {{waypoint -153, -74, -931}}
     - I have to free Fyreflinger {{waypoint -281, -296, -618}}
     - I have to free Rotha {{waypoint -121, -143, -836}}
1. Return and speak to Irfan.

## Rewards

- At least 90g 70s 61cc

## Note

Each prisoner freed gives you a temporary pet you can summon. The names are obvious puns on some Dev Names:

- Gnimlix = Gninja (Warden)
- Kahnder = Kander
- Doomi = Domino (Halfling Dirge)
- Fyreflinger = Fireflyte
- Rotha = Rothgar (Dwarf Templar)
- Cronos = Cronyn.
