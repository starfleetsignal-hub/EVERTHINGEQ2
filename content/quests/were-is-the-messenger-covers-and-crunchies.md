I need to deliver this message, and continue my search for Mairen. First, however, I need to figure out a safe way to travel through the jungle.

## Materials Required

- 54 [[Amsonia Roots]]
- 10 [[Greytusk Hide]]
- 10 [[Mahngavi Mango]]
- 20 [[Wild Rice]]
- 25 [[Astral Filament]]
- 10 [[Astral Kindling]]

You need to take these supplies with you **BEFORE** you go on the ride

- 40 [[Amsonia Roots]]
- 10 [[Greytusk Hide]]
- 20 [[Mahngavi Mango]]
- 25 [[Wild Rhino Meat]]
- 50 [[Wild Rice]]
- 1 [[Baptista Root]] --*rare*
- 1 [[Pteradon Meat]] --*rare*
- 5 [[Astral Candle]]
- 5 [[Astral Filament]]
- 5 [[Astral Kindling]]

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Piccolo]] {{waypoint 524, 145, -597}}.
1. Speak with [[Mibb Deinotrainer]] in [[Karuupa Jungle]] at {{waypoint 521, 145, -586}} to receive [[Recipe Scroll: Deino Saddle Blanket]]
1. Craft 5 [[Deino Saddle Blanket|Deino Saddle Blankets]] Making 5 at once only gives 1 credit for this.
1. Speak to [[Mibb Deinotrainer]] to receive [[Recipe Scroll: Deino Treats]]
1. Craft 2 [[Deino Treats]]
1. Feed the Deino Treats to the **placid deino** at {{waypoint 520, 145, -583}}
   - **NOTE:** You won't be able to get this ride again so make sure you have what you need for the next quest before feeding the Deino.
1. Wait for the ride to end
1. Speak to [[Fivalt]] at the end of the ride. {{waypoint -803, 175, -710}}

## Rewards

- +5,000 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Folded Blanket]]
- At least 5p 21g 72s 4c
- 54178 status at tradeskill level 123
