## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable

## Steps

![Stonehoof Badlands rock](images/Rock_in_Jarsath_Wastes.png)
![Howling Stones rock](images/Jarsath_Wastes_rock.png)

1. Travel to [[Jarsath Wastes]], collect dark brown rocks with faint golden sparkles
1. Collect  3 samples from Stonehoof Badlands - around Tylix's Fist ruins at {{waypoint -776, -120, -770}}
1. Collect 3 samples from Howling Stones - several spots along left wall walking down to Howling Stones canyon, possible locations: {{waypoint 562, -73, 9}}, {{waypoint 577, -81, 28}}, {{waypoint 543, -87, 46}}
1. Collect 3 samples from Ragefire's Talon - several spots there: {{waypoint -1465, 201, 996}}, {{waypoint -1506, 208, 951}}, {{waypoint -1572, 201, 956}}, {{waypoint -1673, 184, 1060}}, {{waypoint -1651, 183, 1074}}
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]] in Sundered Frontier.
