- This collection quest is a Forum Lore Quest so obtaining it is non-traditional.
- The pages will be invisible and the book unclickable until you trigger the quest by doing the following:
  1. Obtain 1 [[Cauldron blowfish]] ([[Harvestables (Tier 5)|Tier 5]]) and 1 [[Thicket crayfish]] ([[Harvestables (Tier 3)|Tier 3]]).  You may buy them from a broker, receive them in trade, or harvest your own.
  1. Enter [[The Eternal Gorge]] via the rock teleporter at the top of the inside of [[The Tower of Zarvonn]] in [[The Commonlands]]. {{waypoint 896, -45, -518}}
  1. Find the non-aggro skelton named [[Jeroel the famished]] who wanders in a roughly circular pattern, counter-clockwise around the zone.
  1. Hail him and choose the response "I seek the pages of the book of Behemoths." He will respond with the statement "Seek the binder within the tower." Jeroel will take the blowfish and crayfish from you.
  1. The book in the tower (in The Commonlands) will now be clickable. Once the book is obtained and added to the collection the pages in the tower and Gorge will become visible.
- Pages in [[The Eternal Gorge]] or the [[Valley of the Rogue Magi]] have multiple random spawn points that are not always up.
- Harvesting a page triggers a 90 minute instance reuse timer. The pages do not respawn.
- **Warning:** Jeroel the famished acts as a collector NPC. If you turn in the completed tome "The Lore of Fauna: The Behemoth" he will also finish all of your other ready collections.
