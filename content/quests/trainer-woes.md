## Notes

- This quest can be acquired regardless of completing the listed prerequisite quest. However it is recommended that all the previous quests be completed in order for story purposes.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Deputy Guzzlesea]], who is at the easternmost opening of [[The Great Guard]] at {{waypoint -188, 0, -405}}.
1. Visit the [[Midwater Bridge]] near [[The Runnyeye Ward]] at {{waypoint 158, 0, -358}} and kill the [[Runnyeye scout]] that spawns at the northern end {{waypoint 156, -1, -374}}.
1. Speak with [[Deputy Jumbelly]] where [[The Lazy Drain]] and [[The Great Guard]] meet, at {{waypoint 255, -1, -364}}.
1. Return to [[Deputy Huckfar]].

## Rewards

- At least 17s 67c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Cursed Band of Waking Demon]] (Mages)
  - [[Cursed Crest of Waking Demon]] (Fighters)
  - [[Cursed Loop of Waking Demon]] (Priests)
  - [[Cursed Ringlet of Waking Demon]] (Scouts)
