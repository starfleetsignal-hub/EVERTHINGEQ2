## Materials Required

Very important to have everything or you *will* need to start all over again. At least:

- 300 [[Hardened Zelniak Pelt]]
- 150 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
- 10 [[Humming Azurite]]
- 100 [[Luclizite Cluster]]

## Steps

1. "Look for a pure form of flowing magic"
   1. Take the scroll at {{waypoint -264.44, -161.08, 791.82}} to receive the recipe for [[Wonderous Mechanical Lockpicks]].
   1. Collect 250 **Envenomed Coal** from the coal bin at {{waypoint -290.64, -160.82, 772.83}}.![recipe scroll](images/Message_in_a_Shadowed_Bottle_-_Recipe_scroll.jpg)
      - *Note: 25 per click*
   1. Craft 10 [[Wonderous Mechanical Lockpicks]] on **a temple workstation** at {{waypoint -289.93, -161.08, 775.83}} next to the coal bin.![a temple workstation with coal bin to the left](images/A_temple_workstation.jpg)
      - *Note: Since crafting them is not an actual quest step, mass production works fine.*
      - ***Warning*** : if you zone out before picking *all* the locks, the locks you already picked will reset and wont be unlockable anymore. You'll have to restart the whole instance after a 2-hour lockout. You must be able to produce 10 lockpicks and use them all.
   1. Use [[Wonderous Mechanical Lockpicks]] on the **Sliding Door Controls** at {{waypoint -262.92, -161.70, 658.81}}.
   1. Use [[Wonderous Mechanical Lockpicks]] on the two  **Sliding Door Controls** near {{waypoint -263.01, -161.96, 583.39}}.
   1. Use [[Wonderous Mechanical Lockpicks]] on the three  **Sliding Door Controls** near {{waypoint -262.62, -161.94, 509.48}}.
   1. Use [[Wonderous Mechanical Lockpicks]] on the four  **Sliding Door Controls** near {{waypoint -262.94, -161.96, 433.71}}.
      - *WARNING: Click SLOWLY on the four or the system may bug and not unlock the last door.*
   1. Proceed to {{waypoint -255.74, -160.66, 322.03}}. *(updates the quest)*
1. Click the pillar in the center of the room to **Place your arm into the Flowing Magic** {{waypoint -272.60, -161.45, 284.76}} to "Attempt to cure my wound"
   - (Tilt camera up and click the dark blue glowy aura part of the pillar)

1. Click the pillar in the center of the room to take a sample of the magic.
1. Examine [[Benosch Ironsprocket's Enchanted Field Guide to Luclin]].
1. Examine [[Vial of Contained Shadows]] - may be in your personal harvesting bag.
   - *To exit to [[Wracklands]] use the portal at {{waypoint -328.51, -161.96, 386.85}}.*
