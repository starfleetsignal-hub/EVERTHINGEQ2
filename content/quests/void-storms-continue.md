This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Receive the holy [[Aab'I Hayat]] and the [[Acumen Occultation Instrument]] from the Seeress.
1. Use this potion on 9 tempest affected creatures found under the Void-Storms across Norath.
   - For a comprehensive list of the locations check the [[Tempest affected Creatures List]].
   - You must be wearing the [[Acumen Occultation Instrument]] to see the tempest affected creatures.
   - Once at a spawn point you don't have to move on to other locations; everything is on a short respawn timer.
1. Return to [[Seeress Ealaynya Ithis]] to get a [[Concordium Note of Appreciation]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s 40c
- 1 [[Concordium Note of Appreciation]]
