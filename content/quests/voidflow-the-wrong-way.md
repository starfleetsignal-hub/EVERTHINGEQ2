## Notes

- To teleport to [[Nightscar Borne Lands]] area use the [[Longhowl]] at {{waypoint -282, 25, -5}}. You  need to complete [[Portal Seeker]] to see him.
- To get back to [[Nightscar Steppes]] camp use [[Longhowl]] at {{waypoint -806, 12, -715}}.

## Steps

1. Find the Void anchor receiver in [[Obliviwrought Basin]] area of [[Nightscar Borne Lands]] and reverse the voidflow of the stones.
   1. reverse voidflow of stone Arr {{waypoint -1284, 5, -1165}}
   1. kill attacking ebonlithe anchorweaver
   1. click the stone again to reverse flow.
   1. reverse voidflow of stone Bek {{waypoint -1324, 52, -1195}}
   1. reverse voidflow of stone Chl {{waypoint -1315, 50, -1159}}
   1. kill attacking ebonlithe anchorwitch
   1. click stone again to reverse flow.
1. witness the destruction of the Void anchor receiver.
1. return to [[Vasynea]] at {{waypoint -298, 25, -19}}
