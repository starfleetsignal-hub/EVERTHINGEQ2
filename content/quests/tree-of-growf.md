## Relinquish Your Gathering Goblin?

**Warning:** Accepting this quest makes you lose your Gathering Goblin spell
To start this, you are given the option to relinquish the ability [[Summon Artisan's Gathering Goblin]] and replace it with

- a house item that allows daily harvesting of a rare item (like harvesting a [[Shadeweave bud]] at home)
- A skill that reduces base harvesting time [[Summon Artisan's Gathering Goblin (Harvesting Buff)]]
- the ability to hire a Gardening Goblin brigand mercenary

## Steps

1. Decide if you are willing to free your gathering goblin so it can be happy.
   - ***Warning:** Accepting this quest makes you lose your Gathering Goblin!  The Summon Gathering Goblin spell will buff your harvesting time instead*
1. Type in your character's first name.
1. Growf asks for help making plant pots and provides the [[Notes on fancy plant pots|recipe]].
   1. Gather 6 [[Warsliks clay|fresh clay]] from both branches of the Warsilks River  {{waypoint 128.80, -237.73, -500.70}} ([[Track Harvestables|trackable]] as clay deposit).![clay deposit (as seen under water)](images/Clay_deposit.jpg)
   1. Make 3 [[Fancy Plant Pot|Fancy Plant Pots]] at a [[Goblin Firepit]] {{waypoint 134.03, -164.00, -770.04}}.
   1. Bring the pots back to [[Growf]] and speak with him
   1. Accept the potted plant house item [[An Obulus Frontier Seedling]].
   1. Place the Obulus Frontier Seedling in your home.
   1. Check back on the plant later. (approx. 24 hours... real world time)
   1. Your gathering goblin has returned as [[A gardening Goblin]], and asks you to help make the plant grow.

## Rewards

- [[An Obulus Frontier Seedling]]
- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +4,000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
- By 2020-03: Also the spell/skill [[Summon Artisan's Gathering Goblin (Harvesting Buff)]], that reduces base harvesting time, was already available.
