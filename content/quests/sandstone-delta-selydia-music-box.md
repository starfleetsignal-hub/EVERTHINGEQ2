## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key by killing 15 [[A sandstone walker|sandstone walkers]] in [[Sandstone Delta]] at [[Saltscorch Steppes]] around {{waypoint 393, 116, -519}}
1. ![Arachnid Sandstalker](images/Arachnid_Sandstalker.jpg)I need to find the Selydia music box key in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]] from [[An arachnid sandstalker|arachnid sandstalkers]] at [[Desert of Woe]] around {{waypoint 91, 45, 255}}
1. Examine the clue for [[The Selydia Music Box Key]]
1. Finish the subquest and return to the Selydia Music Box in [[Sandstone Delta]]
