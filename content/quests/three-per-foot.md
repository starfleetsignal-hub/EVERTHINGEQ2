## Steps

1. Obtain 12 toes from [[A dreaded stonegazer|dreaded stonegazers]] in the [[Stonehoof Badlands]]. {{waypoint -899, -116, -719}}
1. Return to Diviner De'Voch.

  - [[Magus's Spellbound Weave Leggings]] (All Mages)
  - [[Witchdoctor's Dark Awakening Chausses]] (Shamans)
  - [[Stormcaller's Summer Weave Leather Pants]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Exarch's Final Communion Plate Greaves]] (Clerics)
  - [[Outrider's Elven Chain Leggings]] (All Scouts)
- +1000 faction with **The Sel'Nok Brigade**
