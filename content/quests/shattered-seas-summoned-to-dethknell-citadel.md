## Steps

1. Speak to [[Overlord Lucan D'Lere]] - use the portal in the [[Overlord's Plaza]] {{waypoint 10, 19, 10}} to access [[The Overlord's Office]]: **The Shattered Seas** (make sure you choose the right quest instance) then talk to [[Lucan D'Lere]]

## Rewards

- At least 59g 40s
- One of the following:
  - [[Electrified Adornment of Fading]]
  - [[Electrified Adornment of Aggressiveness]]

## Notes

- Good aligned players receive [[Shattered Seas: Called to Qeynos Castle]] instead of this quest.
