- Before I will truly be accepted as a citizen of Kelethin I must find and speak with my trainer.

## Notes

You get this quest after going to the queen for, Kelethin: Citzenship, the Queen will then give you this quest to go talk to your class trainer, I believe this only happens if you're a class that is forced to change when you betray (Conjuror/Necromaner, Paladin/Shadowknight, Mystic/Defiler, and Swashbukler/Brigand)

- For Necromancers go to Jiaana Kaalikka
- For Shadowknights go to Ihieer Scytheleaf
- For Defilers go to Illasko Silktree
- For Brigands go to Mulus Viinikka

## Steps

- I must find and speak with [[Anaaj Greenblade]] if I wish to be a Berserker or [[Zemke the Wolf]] if I wish to be a Guardian, both of whom can be found in Kelethin.
- I must speak with [[Allikaatja Piimarik]] if I wish to become a Monk or [[Mobin Grosch]] of I wish to become a Bruiser, both of whom may be found in Kelethin.
- I must speak with [[Ihieer Scytheleaf]] in Kelethin.
- I must speak with [[Meeha Pekko-Maarit]] if I wish to become a Templar or [[Gilles Chillborn]] if I wish to become an Inquisitor, both of whom may be found in Kelethin.
- I must speak with either [[Amma Ilaari]] if I wish to be a Warden or [[Pickle Velvetleaf]] if I wish to be a Fury, both of which can be found in Kelethin.
- I must speak with [[Illasko Silktree]] in Kelethin.
- I must speak with [[Buzz Vitikka]] if I wish to be a Wizard or [[Zeego Miemo]] if I wish to be a Warlock, both of which can be found in Kelethin.
- I must speak with [[Bresiya Tuuri]] if I wish to become an Illusionist or [[Elonjhnir Louthe]] if I wish to become a Coercer, both of whom can be found in Kelethin.
- I must speak with [[Jiaana Kaalikka]] in Kelethin.
- I must speak with [[Mulus Viinikka]] in Kelethin.
- I must speak with [[Kalja Songstress]] if I wish to be a Dirge or [[Dirki Gerika]] if I wish to be a Troubador, both of which can be found in Kelethin.
- I must speak with [[Eagereye Mayweed]] if I wish to become a Ranger or [[Lonegon Bogh]] if I wish to become an Assassin, both of whom can be found in Kelethin.
- I must speak with [[Lythe Dur'gun]] in Kelethin.
- I must speak with [[Latinna Spiritgear]] in Kelethin.

- I must return to [[Queen Amree (Greater Faydark)|Queen Amree]] of the Fae to complete my citizenship ceremony.
  - Queen Amree is in the Fae Royal Hall. I cannot wait to complete my citizenship ceremony with her.

## Rewards

- Citizen of Kelethin
