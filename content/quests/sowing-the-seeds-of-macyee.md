## Notes

- Murtog MacYee was an alchemy merchant in [[EverQuest]].

## Steps

![A Northland Lilac](images/A_Northland_Lilac.jpg)
![A Vox Daisy](images/A_Vox_Daisy.jpg)
![Frigid Poppies](images/Frigid_Poppies.jpg)

1. Gather the seeds from the following locations:
   - **Northland Lilac**: near the southernmost minotaur camp in the [[Jagged Plains]]. {{waypoint -25, 20, -414}}
   - **Vox Daisy**: under a big rock in the [[Jagged Plains]] and not easy to spot. {{waypoint 89, 23, -652}}
   - **Frigid Poppies**: easternmost island in [[Everstorm Bay]]. {{waypoint -274, -19, 154}}
1. Kill any [[A bighorn sheep|bighorn sheep]]s or [[A bighorn dasher|bighorn dasher]]s in the [[Jagged Plains]] for a common update.
1. Bury the seeds at the gravestone of MacYee the Magnificent. You may have to switch to first-person view to see the gravestone.
