## Steps

1. [[Tigon]] claims his ancestors have told him to purge the evil from his homeland.
   - You are asked to hunt 10 [[A void crusher|void crushers]] in the [[Erudin Gardens]] (crushers in [[The Steppes of Erud]] will also update the quest).
1. Return to Tigon once complete.
