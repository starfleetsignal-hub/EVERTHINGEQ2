## Steps

1. Talk to [[Watcher Haytham]] at the top of Prophet's Peak  {{waypoint 622, -52, -915}}. There is a narrow passage at {{waypoint 655, -84, -900}}, which leads to the top.
1. Return to [[Disciple Shula]].
