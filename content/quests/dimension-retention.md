## Steps

1. Obtain the measurements of the Glaciersmash (click on the clouds of blue sparkles).  You won't be able to see these if your Display Option is set to Extreme Performance.  Change it to Very High Performance instead. See discussion page for alternative locations.
   - Obtain the keel's measurements {{waypoint 1363, -647, 206}}
   - Obtain the hull's measurements {{waypoint 1337.19, -641.06, 247.97}}
   - Obtain the deck's measurements {{waypoint 1291.46, -623.78, 225}}
   - Obtain the stern's measurements {{waypoint 1366.48, -625.62, 202.49}}
1. Return to Captain Bin Nalot
