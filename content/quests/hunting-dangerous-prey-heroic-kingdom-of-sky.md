## Steps

Kill the following enemies

- Bury the Vault Keeper : Kill [[Korgo the Vault Keeper]] in [[The Vaults of El'Arad]] (Barren Sky, Isle of Awakening)
- Down the Deepguardian: Kill [[Deepguardian Kvaklin]] in [[Den of the Devourer]] (Bonemire, Drednever)
- Sever the Steward : Kill [[Katalkana]] in [[The Halls of Fate]] (Bonemire, HoF Island)

or

- Quell the Quellithulian messenger : Kill [[The Messenger of El'Arad]] in [[The Vaults of El'Arad]] (Barren Sky, Isle of Awakening)
- Crush the Chamberlain  : Kill [[Chamberlain X'Haviz]] in [[The Nest of the Great Egg]] (Barren Sky, Whisperwind)
- Dissect the Devourer : Kill [[The Devourer]] in [[The Den of the Devourer]] (Bonemire, Drednever Crash Site, inside Hive Vornerus)

or

- Gut Grash : [[Grash]] in [[The Blackscale Sepulcher]] (Bonemire, Cacotoxic Stain)
- Bring about the Doom Lord's downfall : Kill [[Doom Lord Septimus]] in [[The Halls of Fate]] (Bonemire, HoF Island)
- Pluck the Kettleleader : Kill [[Kettleleader Ghrizhaw]] in [[The Nest of the Great Egg]] on (Barren Sky, Whisperwind Isle)

## Notes

The Hunter's missions can be shared with others in the group. There is no confirmation message when sharing the mission, all eligible members of the group will immediately receive the quest.

Mission updates for killing a required target are zone-wide in instanced zones. Individual group members do not need to be near the target when it is killed; the only requirement is that they must be in the same instance as other members of the group.
