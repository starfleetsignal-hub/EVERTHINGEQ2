## Steps

1. Enter the [[Ruins of Takish'Hiz]], east of [[Madman's Rest]] {{waypoint 301.26, 96.84, -534.36}}
1. Catalogue creatures:
   - [[A grove glider]]
   - [[A grove stalker]]
1. Ride the giant green leaf ramps to [[Firiona's Motte]] {{waypoint 567.04, 43.96, -289.84}} (It is below you, the waypoint pathing is buggy)
1. Catalogue creatures:
   - [[A vicious leafcutter]]
   - [[A flora maw]]
1. Return to [[Zaari]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
