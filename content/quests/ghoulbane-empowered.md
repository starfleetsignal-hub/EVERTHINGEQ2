## Notes

- This is a subquest of [[The Symbol in the Flesh]].

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Toranim Skyblade]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 535, -32, -174}}
1. Perform the following actions:
   - Obtain 1 [[Enchanted Adamantine Ore]] (Adorning, 325 skill)
     - If you collected the ore but the quest doesn't update, place the item on your broker then remove it. It can also be bought off of the broker or traded from another character. The quest will update if it is sitting in your bank or in your bags on your character when you speak to Toranim.
   - Obtain 6 'phosphorescent pearls', an uncommon update from [[A deep spawn consort|deep spawn consort]]s in the [[Loping Plains]]. {{waypoint 93, 3, 380}} (on the beach)
   - Kill [[Kassar the Uncontrollable]], spawned in the [[Mistmoore Catacombs]] by clicking on the hanging bones at {{waypoint -56, -28, -98}}.
     - *NOTE: If you have the other subquest [[Gogas Afadin (Quest)|Gogas Afadin]] for the HQ Symbol in the Flesh, the bones are NOT clickable, unless both quests are at the same stage to kill Kassar. In which case the bones are clickable and 1 kill updates both quests.*<br>To get to this location:
     - Enter from [[Castle Mistmoore]] {{waypoint -218, -14, -14}}
     - Enter from Loping Plains:
       - Climb the wall at {{waypoint -12, -159, -30}} next to [[Alain Toad]].
       - Climb the wall at {{waypoint -33, -127, 120}}.
       - Climb the wall at {{waypoint -59, -100, 63}} (has gargoyles near a chimney with a puddle of water to climb up into the castle part)
1. Return to Toranim Skyblade in North Qeynos.
