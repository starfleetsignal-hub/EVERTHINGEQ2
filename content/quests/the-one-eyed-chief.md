*The following quest, [[Karath the Forgotten]], is only available upon completion of all other quests offered by the Watcher of the Fields.*

## Steps

1. Harvest 20 pieces of [[Severed ash]] from [[Wood Nodes#T3|felled high plains arbor]]. You will need a foresting skill of 90.
   - You need 20 *harvests* not 20 pieces.
1. Kill [[A griffawn|griffawn]]s until you get 20 common quest updates.
1. Kill [[A Sabertooth looter|Sabertooth looter]]s until you get a quest update. They can be found near the western Antonica zoneline {{waypoint 1300, 0, -350}} and may be found at the bottom of the crater at {{waypoint 1000, 0, -180}}.
1. Return to the [[Watcher of the Fields]]. Click the banner corresponding to the Chief to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- A ranged slot icon based on class:
  - [[Soulforged Dragon Bow]] - Shadowknight
  - [[Imbued Bone Focus]] - Necromancer
  - [[Griffon Hide Satchel]] - Beastlord
