## Notes

To complete the Bridled Wind Komodo Timeline , you will need the following:

- A tradeskill level 60 or higher
- Materials:
  - 6 [[Flying fish]]
  - 14 [[Hanging root]]
  - 11 [[Horned leather pelt]]
  - 11 [[Rough lumbered rosewood]]
- Fuel:
  - 5 [[Scintillating Filament|Scintillating filament]]
  - 17 [[Scintillating Kindling]]
  - 5 [[Scintillating Sandpaper]]

The fuel can be bought at the crafting station

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Darga Fleetpaw]] on [[Vicious Breeding Grounds]] {{waypoint 511, -151, 240}}
1. Speak to [[Captain Velgre]] {{waypoint 717, -163, 251}}
1. Collect Grubs (5)  ![Porous Rock](images/Porous_Rock.png)
   - Gather grubs from "porous rocks" nearby trees in the area.
   - They appear with [[Track Harvestables]].
1. Return to Captain Velgre

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- At least 2g 16s 26c
