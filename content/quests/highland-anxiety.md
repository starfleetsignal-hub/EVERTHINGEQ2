## Steps

1. Follow the steep paths from [[Reek Rot Bog]] {{waypoint -253, 18, -130}} up to [[Stonemarrow Mountains]] then on to [[Doomview Highlands]] at {{waypoint 54, 173, -107}}
1. Gather and Hunt the following
   - Gather 4 soil samples from [[Mud Nest|Mud Nests]]. Potential locations include:![Mud nest](images/Mud_Nest.png)
     - {{waypoint 108, 177, -50}}, {{waypoint 70, 177, -58}}, {{waypoint 146, 177, -83}}
     - {{waypoint 125, 174, -54}}, {{waypoint 129, 176, -87}}
1. Gather [[Crystal samples]] from [[Distinct Crystal|Distinct Crystals]]. Potential locations include:![](images/Distinct_crystal.png)
     - {{waypoint 90, 177, -47}}, {{waypoint 69, 177, -60}}, {{waypoint 114, 178, -111}}
     - {{waypoint 133, 179, -107}}, {{waypoint 111, 176, -52}}
   - Hunt 6 sick blightwing fiends for samples.
1. Return to [[Tomian Keeneye (Yon Gorroth)|Tomian Keeneye]].
   - **Note**: after completing this quest keep talking to him to update [[Rage of Cthurath: Distant Wastes and Dark Places]]
