## Steps

1. collect 6 shadelink chains from spectres
   1. Kill [[Shadowed Spectres]] for 5 shadelink chains at {{waypoint 398, 299, -570}} *Note: Not a guaranteed update.*
   1. Kill [[Shadowed Spectres]] for 1 more shadelink chain. *Note: Not a guaranteed update.*
1. Return to [[Dworel Norkhitter]] at {{waypoint 616, 428, -624}}
   - *Note: you'll receive a [[Convocation insignia]], when you speak to him*
1. Speak with a Catalysm Sage in [[Recuso Tor]]
   - Use the [[A tamed Shik'Nar drone]] at {{waypoint 593, 429, -582}} to travel and speak with [[Jolanta Farlight]] at {{waypoint -775, 52, 341}}
1. Gather 12 impactite in the [[Teeming Caldera]] between {{waypoint 59, 7.5, 111}} and {{waypoint -637, 90, -297}}.![Impactite](images/Impactite.jpg)
   - A good location for these are {{waypoint -419, 21, -122}} (and headed northeast up the ridge).
   - *Note: Small flat-ish grey rocks.*
   - Note: updates for group. Going NE up the ridge they are mostly left then right.
1. Return to [[Jolanta Farlight]] at {{waypoint -775, 52, 341}}.
1. Speak to [[Doomsayer Adarius]] at {{waypoint -499, 66, 240}}.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 10g 49s 87c 46479 status
- [[Annealed Shadelink Hoop]]
- [[Querent of Ruin Cloak]]
