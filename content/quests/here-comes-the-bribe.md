## Notes

- requires 1,000 [[House Vahla (Faction)]].
- If you choose the option "I'm not falling for this." at the end of your conversation [[Irdd]] will become aggro and attack you. No worries he will respawn again after you defeated him.
- There are actually 3 different versions of this quest that offer a different reward depending on which option you choose during your conversation:
  - Choosing "I'm never weighed down by an abundance of coin." will reward with a [[Calabash of Wealth]].
  - Choosing "I'm looking for love." will reward with a [[Calabash of Love]].
  - Choosing "Knowledge is power. Give me more of that!" will reward with a [[Calabash of Knowledge]].

## Steps

1. Travel to an eastern platform in the [[Pakiat Bluffs]] {{waypoint -865.24, 82.16, -445.89}} and kill the group of 3 [[Alux bluffwalker|alux bluffwalkers]] that are waiting to ambush.![](images/Calabash_of_Wealth.jpg)![](images/Calabash_of_Knowledge.jpg)![](images/Calabash_of_Love.jpg)
1. Claim your reward by clicking one of the following vases on the eastern carpet depending on your initial conversation:
   - The Calabash of Wealth on the left {{waypoint -878.67, 82.24, -453.64}}.
   - The Calabash of Knowledge in the middle {{waypoint -881.69, 82.23, -449.06}}.
   - The Calabash of Love on the right {{waypoint -881.71, 82.19, -445.46}}.
1. Speak to [[Meskhenar]] {{waypoint -870.17, 82.16, -440.01}} when he appears and imprisons you.
   - *Note: Recast your mount after being imprisoned by him.*![](images/Sand-polished_gold_bead.png)
1. Gather the ***sand-polished gold bead*** that can be left behind after defeating a [[Molded sandbrute]] {{waypoint -642.22, 63.86, -653.93}} in the valleys in the [[Pakiat Bluffs]].
   - *Note: They are not marked as quest mobs with no feather and no bag symbol.*
   - *Also Note: It can take several tries, so keep killing the molded sandbrutes until the item drops on the ground.*
1. Return to [[Meskhenar]] {{waypoint -870.17, 82.16, -440.01}}.
