## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Thundering Steppes]] and kill [[Clint Gilcrush]] who is an NPC on the docks at {{waypoint -442, -18, -449}}.  Hail him and he will become attackable.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- +3000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -3000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
