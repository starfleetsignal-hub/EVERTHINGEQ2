## Steps

1. Buy [[Reactive Kesmith]] for 7c from Alchemist [[Garion Dunam]] {{waypoint 653, 20, 282}}.
1. Buy a [[Litsuth Bag (Quest Item)|Litsuth Bag]] for 12c  from Tailor [[Ehuraa Rotherham]] {{waypoint 677, -20, 319}}.
1. Collect 4 [[Selkie Mushroom (Quest Item)|Selkie Mushrooms]] near the Mariner's Bell {{waypoint 686, -36, 350}}.
1. Return to Spiritist Laharn Rahai.
1. Speak to [[Lakosha Maera]] above the Mariner's Bell {{waypoint 673, -20, 335}}.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 24c
- [[Litsuth Bag (Container)|Litsuth Bag]] - container
