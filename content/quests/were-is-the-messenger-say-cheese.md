## Materials Required

- 40 [[Amsonia Roots]]
- 10 [[Greytusk Hide]]
- 20 [[Mahngavi Mango]]
- 25 [[Wild Rhino Meat]]
- 1 [[Baptista Root]] --*rare*
- 1 [[Pteradon Meat]] --*rare*
- 5 [[Astral Candle]]
- 5 [[Astral Filament]]
- 5 [[Astral Kindling]]

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Fivalt]] {{waypoint -803, 175, -709}} to receive [[Recipe Scroll: Cheese Mischief]]  ![](images/Were_is_the_Messenger_Say_Cheese_quest_-_Fivalt's_Quick_Exit.jpg)
1. Gather the following ingredients from the boxes next to [[Fivalt]]:
   1. [[Rennet]]
   1. [[Fresh Milk]]
   1. [[Mystery Ingredients]]
1. Craft the [[Cheesecloth]]
   - There is a [[Far Seas Crafting Station]] next to [[Fivalt]]
1. Craft the [[Manhngavi Muenster|Mahngavi Muenster]]
1. Speak to [[Fivalt]].
1. Craft the [[Potion of Perpetual Purging]]
1. Speak to [[Fivalt]].
1. Enter [[Mahngavi Wastes]] by clicking on **Fivalt's Quick Exit** (large stone totem) at {{waypoint -812, 175, -709}}
1. Search the **debris** {{waypoint 308, 151, 651}}  ![](images/Were_is_the_Messenger_Say_Cheese_quest_-_odd_debris.jpg)

## Rewards

- +5,000 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Cheesemaker's Hat]]
- At least 5p 21g 72s 4c
- 54178 status at tradeskill level 123
- Ability to fly in Karuupa Jungle
