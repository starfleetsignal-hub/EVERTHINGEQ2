## Steps

1. Go to the [[Ryjesium Lake]] and collect 3 *iksar artifacts*. {{waypoint 678, -19, -11}}
1. Return to [[Zeliss Vrang]].

- At least 14g 81s 38c
- [[Radiating Gem of Striking]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Tattered Cloak of Chardok]] (All Priests)
  - [[Tattered Cloak of Karnor's Castle]] (All Mages)
  - [[Tattered Cloak of Teren's Grasp]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Tattered Cloak of the Reach]] (All Scouts)
