## Notes

- This is the access quest for [[Trakanon's Lair]].

## Steps

1. Defeat the following bosses of [[Veeshan's Peak]]:
   - First wing:
     - [[Kluzen the Protector]]
     - [[Elder Ekron]]
     - [[Nexona]]
     - [[Druushk]]
   - Second wing:
     - [[Taskmaster Nichok]]
     - [[Milyex Vioren]]
     - [[Xygoz]]
     - [[Qunard Ashenclaw]]
     - [[Hoshkar]]
   - Third wing:
     - [[Travenro the Skygazer]]
     - [[Silverwing]]
     - [[Phara Dar]]
1. Return to Snyr'dok.

## Rewards

- At least 48g 35s 24c
- Access to [[Trakanon's Lair]].
