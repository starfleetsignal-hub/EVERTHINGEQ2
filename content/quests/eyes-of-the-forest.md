## Steps

![thistlebloom root](images/Eyes_of_the_Forest_%28thistlebloom_root%29.png)

1. Collect components for a flora memory spell
   - Cobalt brownhide claws (6) - kill [[A cobalt brownhide|cobalt brownhides]] or [[A brownhide guardian|brownhide guardians]]
   - Thistlebloom roots (8) - clickable green weeds with silver sparkly effect. These are found near the trees and look like yellow-flower shrubs. They are difficult to differentiate from ground flora if you have it turned on, but they do have a sparkle effect. Known locations:
  - {{waypoint 3463, -990, 1158}}
  - {{waypoint 3541, -989, 1162}}
  - {{waypoint 3626, -1003, 1228}}
  - {{waypoint 3475, -985, 1102}}
  - {{waypoint 3456, -989, 1131}}
  - {{waypoint 3448, -980, 1058}}
  - {{waypoint 3531, -980, 1091}}
  - {{waypoint 3509, -986, 1120}}
1. Return to [[Outrider Metinga]]
1. Travel to the [[Eerie Encampment]] and cast the [[Visible Flora Echoes]] that [[Outrider Metinga|Metinga]] gives you
1. Observe the revealed echoes
1. Return to [[Outrider Metinga]]
