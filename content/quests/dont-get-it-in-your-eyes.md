## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[A crusty rustbeast|crusty rustbeasts]] north of the [[Timeworn Pools]] {{waypoint 119, 1, -175}}.
1. Return to Tertiary Mak'Ki.

- At least 50c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Bodyguard's Bracers]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Evoker's Bracers]] (All Mages)
  - [[Marshall's Bracers]] (Warriors, Crusaders)
  - [[Pathfinder's Bracers]] (All Scouts}
  - [[Spiritualist's Bracer]] (Shamans)
  - [[Vicar's Bracers]] (Clerics)
  - [[Windcaller's Bracers]] (Druids)
