## Notes

- To get to [[Nightscar Borne Lands]] area use the [[Longhowl]] next to [[Lynwyn Singlespring]] at {{waypoint -282, 25, -5}}.
- To get back to [[Nightscar Steppes]] use [[Longhowl]] at {{waypoint -806, 12, -715}}.

## Steps

![Icon of The Consumer](images/Icon_of_The_Consumer.png)
![Altar to the Consumer](images/Altar_to_the_Consumer.png)

1. Slay Legion members and destroy unholy icons and altars
   - kill 6 ebonlithe lamias of the Nightscar Borne Lands
   - kill 10 obliviwrought nightbloods of the Nightscar Borne Lands
   - destroy 6 icons of the Consumer - they respawn, possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -645, 14, -849}}, {{waypoint -588, 26, -862}}, {{waypoint -602, 23, -831}}, {{waypoint -590, 13, -923}}, {{waypoint -822, 31, -1269}}, {{waypoint -824, 31, -1286}} and many more throughout the island and on platforms above.
   - destroy 6 altars to the Consumer - they respawn, possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -871, 24, -852}}, {{waypoint -660, 11, -942}}, {{waypoint -593, 23, -831}}. {{waypoint -583, 13, -924}}, {{waypoint -857, 29, -1272}}, {{waypoint -1308, 51, -1133}} and many more throughout the island and on platforms above.
1. Return to [[Lynwyn Singlespring]] at {{waypoint -293, 25, -11}}
