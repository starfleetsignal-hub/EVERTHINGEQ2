## Steps

1. Find a handle for the mace; an uncommon auto-update from skeletons in The Thundering Steppes.
1. Find something to make a ring for the chain; interact with a *rock for starfall* in a crater southeast from [[Qeynos Tower 3]] at {{waypoint -475, -12, 252}}. It can be found near [[Miner Mallus]]
1. Get chain-links from killing 25 giants in The Thundering Steppes. Firerock Giants are numerous in the southwestern part of the zone, especially around [[Crater Mine]] at {{waypoint 525, -17, 1000}}. The skeleton giants in [[The Valley of the Ancients]] update as well.
