## Description

Nightmares are real, at least according to many of the Malduran citizen's I've encountered. They speak of the nightmarish beasts within the Submerged Lair and the spirits of the dead within Stygian Threshold. I should discover these for myself, and banish them from Thalumbra so that the citizens of Maldura can sleep easier.

## Steps

1. Kill the submerged beast
   - [[Abyssral]] in [[Kralet Penumbra: Submerged Lair (Event Heroic)]]
1. Banish the spirit of the gateway
   - [[Bagarash (Event Heroic)|Bagarash]] in [[Stygian Threshold: The Howling Gateway (Event Heroic)]]
