## Steps

1. Enter [[The Tombs of Night: Retribution]] in order to find four Teir'Dal artifacts.
1. Spawn [[Dvith N`Zur]] {{waypoint 56.5, -41.38, 114.25}}  by killing the five groups of vampires around the base of the waterfall (big cave level 3).  When he spawns, he will not be attackable.
   - Kill the [[A nightmare of Dvith|Nightmares of Dvith]] to make [[Dvith N`Zur]] respawn as an attackable foe. He may respawn underwater at the bottom of the waterfall.
   - Kill [[Dvith N`Zur]] for the first artifact.
1. Examine the rubble next to [[Nilth T'Feyd]] {{waypoint -99, -23, -12}} to find the second artifact.
1. Kill [[Rhayn T'Dax]] {{waypoint 54, -23, -101}} for the third artifact (in the tunnel between the first and second levels).
1. Examine a coffin slightly north of [[Nilth T'Feyd]] {{waypoint -102, -24, -27}} for the fourth artifact.
1. Exit the instance at {{waypoint 117, -1, 126}}
1. Return to [[Dirin Thilae]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 21s
- [[Thexian Language Primer]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Potion of the Night]]
  - [[Salve of the Night]]
- Ability to purchase armor and crafting books released with the Bloodlines adventure pack.
