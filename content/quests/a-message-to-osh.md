## Steps

1. Use the spiritgate to get to [[Fina's Retreat]] in [[Great Divide]]
1. Speak with [[Osh]]
1. return to [[High Shaman Lien]] at {{waypoint -1137, -531, 3302}} in [[Eastern Wastes]]
   - *Note: you can use the Spiritgate to get to Eastern Wastes whenever you like now*
1. Speak with [[Chief Dampion]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- +725 faction with **Othmir of Velious**
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Arctic Ethereal Pendant]]
  - [[Arctic Ghostly Choker]]
  - [[Arctic Phantasmal Gorget]]
