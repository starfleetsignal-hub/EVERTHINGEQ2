[[Missionary G'Zule]] at the [[Temple of War]] wants help dispatching some graverobbers in the [[The Commonlands]].

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Turmoil Cemetery]] in the [[The Commonlands]].  There, two [[A lost soul (Commonlands)|lost souls]] spawn; these are the placeholders for the [[Graverobber]], the mob that must be killed to update this quest.  He spawns at {{waypoint -1316.26, -69.39, 347.69}}.
1. Return to [[Missionary G'Zule]] in [[North Freeport]].

## Note

A discussion whether the [[Graverobber]] only spawns at night can be found on its talk page.

## Rewards

- At least 16s
- Experience
- [[Marrow-Coated Flail]]
