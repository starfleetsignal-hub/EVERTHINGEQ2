## Description

If the League of Freethinkers' suspicions are right, the Libant are looking to penetrate their way into what seems like every major institution established in Norrath. Several lists detailing the personal information of high-ranking officials have already been recovered. I am to enter Evernight Abbey and see if I can recover more list of potential victims so that the Freethinkers can act on the information before it's too late.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Evernight Abbey]] and recover 6 lists of potential Libant victims. This is a group updateable quest and the pages do not respawn. If someone harvests a page while a group member is far enough away, they will not be able to complete the quest. (Locations may differ a bit)*
   - {{waypoint 0, 0, 2}} at zone-in.
   - {{waypoint 127, -7, -117}}
   - {{waypoint -108, -6, -191}}
   - {{waypoint 68, -5, -242}}
   - {{waypoint 13, -25, -173}}, (in a jail cell or at stairs leading out of the room)
   - {{waypoint 26, -16, -310}}
   - {{waypoint -122, -4, -125}}, (on table)
1. Return to [[Tanya Alvernon]]
