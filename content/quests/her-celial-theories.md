## Steps

1. Venture into the [[Fungus Grove]] to obtain materials for [[Calerel Windsong|Calerel Windsong's]] studies.
   1. Hunt 12 mycanoids for fresh corpses.
   1. Gather 8 grove drake hatchlings.
      - *Nest areas around {{waypoint 508.74, 37.30, -410.75}} and {{waypoint 222, 63.36, -383.64}}. These are trackable.*
1. Return to [[Calerel Windsong]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- One of the following:
  - [[Garnet Balanzite Shield]]
  - [[Garnet Balanzite Great Shield]]
  - [[Garnet Balanzite Talisman]]
