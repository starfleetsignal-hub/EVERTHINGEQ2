## Notes

Faction can be tracked under the 'Deserts of Ro' as 'Nomads of Ro'. Initial value is -10,000.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Sinking Sands]] dock and in the [[Croc Hunter Camp]] speak to [[Kania]] {{waypoint -1343.24, -226.83, -604.24}}.
1. At the [[The Oasis of Marr]] speak to [[Mamat]] {{waypoint -1247.67, -215.01, -291.76}} then [[Baniti]] {{waypoint -1231.66, -214.66, -299.34}}.
1. Travel to [[The Barren Sky]] and on [[The Whisperwind Isle]] at [[The Stonefall Breach]] collect dust from 12 [[A djinn dust wisp|Djinn Dust Wisps]] {{waypoint -528.73, -71.99, -353.30}}. These will become agressive/attackable when you approach within 5 metres.
   - There are also [[A djinn dust wisp|Djinn Dust Wisps]] on [[Isle of Awakening]].
1. Return to [[The Sinking Sands]] and hail [[Baniti]] {{waypoint -1231.66, -214.66, -299.34}} to give the pile of dust. At this point [[Baniti]] will turn your screen a monochrome green.
1. On the [[Orc Highway]] smash the glowing pile of rocks {{waypoint -501.26, -102.67, -138.60}} and enter [[The Fractured Cache]].
1. Kill [[Cruor Mother]] {{waypoint -113.84, -71.38, -73.56}} and receive the text "The sudden sound of a stone cracking echoes through the Fractured Cache. Passage into the adjacent caves may be possible!".
1. Attack the cracked wall {{waypoint -68.99, -68.39, -94.91}} and enter the small cave.
1. Kill seven (7) [[A rujarkian grunt|Rujarkian Grunts]] and kill [[Overseer Ovlogg]] {{waypoint -89.13, -70.85, -196.71}}. This will trigger the exposure of the next cracked wall {{waypoint -45.22, -73.18, -176.24}}.  ![](images/EQ2-AncientCrystalArca.png)
1. Kill [[The Ephemeral Guardian]] {{waypoint 2.92, -64.31, -185.60}} and collect the **Ancient Crystal Arca** {{waypoint 7.09, -64.56, -190.94}}
1. Fast travel to the appropriate Quest giver of [[Faezoth]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]] {{waypoint 696.41, -19.42, 223.11}} or [[Silrena]] in [[Freeport]] {{waypoint -116.51, -24.97, 206.94}}. Or exit the cache {{waypoint 24.17, -39.89, -126.23}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- +3,500 faction with **Hizite Nomads of Ro**
- [[Djinn Dust Wisp]]
