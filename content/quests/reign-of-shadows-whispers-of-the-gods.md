## Notes

- Head Scholar Nabihan appears in the Nexus and offers the quest as soon as you have completed [[Something to Bank On|Something to bank on]], but you need to complete [[Lend a Helping Hand]] or [[Crime Pays Someone]] to obtain the Drinalian quicksilver.

## Steps

1. Obtain flawless luclizite ore and blessed with Drinalian quicksilver.
   1. Obtain 6 flawless luclizite ore.
      - *These come from mining piles of rocks at {{waypoint -19.23, -0.62, -24.38}}, {{waypoint -298.26, -1.76, -111.67}}) or {{waypoint -248.55, -1.76, 154}} and lying around the zones, and are not a guaranteed drop. Check different sections of the city like The Bazaar and Shadow Haven as well as the Luclin nexus.*
      - Note: Nontrackable. ![](images/Flawless_Lucizite_Ore_Rocks.jpg)<br>
   1. Speak to [[Yedrik Stoneeye]] in the [[Mithril Merchant Stalls]] of [[The Bazaar]] {{waypoint -159, -0.64, -95}} to have the flawless luclizite ore stone carved into a whisper column.
   1. Speak to one of the following in the [[House of Midst]] area to obtain the Drinalian quicksilver.
      - Speak to [[Lyya Alumere]] {{waypoint 611, -23, -613}} when you finished the [[Lend a Helping Hand]] quest
      - Speak to [[Evuar Vrottoth]] {{waypoint 720, -8, -504}} when you finished the [[Crime Pays Someone]] quest
1. Speak with [[Head Scholar Nabihan]] in the [[Shadow Haven]] at the [[Haven of Worship]] {{waypoint -848, 0.17, -145}}.
1. Place Drinalian Whisper Column at {{waypoint -846, -0.14, -150}}.
1. Sanctify the new Drinalian Whisper Column.
1. Speak with Calum, Drinal's Steward, by interacting with the sanctified Whisper Column.
1. Speak with [[Head Scholar Nabihan]].
