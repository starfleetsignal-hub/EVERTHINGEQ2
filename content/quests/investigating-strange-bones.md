## Prerequisites

- You must complete the quests on Kerra Isle up to [[Feathers for Peace of Heart]] and the quests in Stonebrunt Encampment up to [[An Interesting Find]] to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Ghalib has sent you to look for more strange bones like the one you found in the [[Kejaan's Rill]] riverbed. You need to kill carnivores for 10 [[A shard of strange bone|shards of strange bone]]. These are common loot drops in small chests. The following monsters can drop the bone shards:
   - [[A bristlevine horror]] {{waypoint 463, 150, 2723}} or [[A briarroot horror]] {{waypoint 757, -1, 3358}}
   - [[A crageye stalker]]
   - [[A feral bruiser]] {{waypoint 713, 127, 2982}}
   - [[A petrified roper]] {{waypoint 1399.55, 6.99, 3893.72}}
   - [[A stonecold carrion]] {{waypoint 925.73, 64.59, 3543.39}}
   - [[A Tox fly]] {{waypoint 742, 66, 3112}}
   - [[A toxic kobold]] (located near the Toxxulia guardians and Toxxulia winged sentinels) {{waypoint 282, 142, 2840}}
   - [[A Toxxulia guardian]] or [[A Toxxulia winged sentinel]] {{waypoint 282, 142, 2840}}
   - [[A Toxxulia prowler]] {{waypoint 1282, -42, 3157}}
1. Return to Ghalib.
1. Look around [[Kejaan's Rill]] for any sign of what animal the bones could have come from. The location will be shown on your map with a quest update icon.
   - Find [[Jin'tu]] at {{waypoint 565, 196, 4000}}.
1. Tell Ghalib you found nothing in the Kejaan's Rill river.
