## Prerequisites

- While [[Beardback's Hit List]] and [[Chilled to the Bones]] are unlimited repeatable quests, they must be done at least once to unlock this quest (along with [[Papers Please]]).

## Notes

- When you hail [[Mosi Panthira]] to start this quest, you will receive a [[Group of rolled rugs]] as a gratuity for all your hard work you've done for him and the other quest givers.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Captain Sivisk]] in [[Fens of Nathsar]]. {{waypoint 508, 44, 1504}}

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- [[A flesh feeder (House Item)|a flesh feeder (House Item)]] house item gives 300 status reduction
