## Steps

*Deep within Kaesora, a tomb holds two venerated officers of an ancient iksar empire. They have since risen, and slaughter any who venture within their domain, iksar or not. I have been tasked with venturing into the ruined city and banishing them for good.*

- Defeat targets within [[Kaesora: Tomb of the Venerated (Advanced Solo)]]
  - Unravel the mummified Chatizad
  - Untangle the mummified Zannaska
  - Banish the spectral incarnations of Chatizad and Zannaska
