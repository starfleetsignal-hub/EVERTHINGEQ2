## Notes

- Go to the [[North Wing Stacks]] on the first floor of the Library, then take the teleporter at {{waypoint -259, -20, -284}} to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]] and enter [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]] through the portal at {{waypoint 728, 412, -336}}.

## Steps

1. Obtain 12 infernal spell stones (uncommon update) by killing Doomfire efreeti mages and priests on the [[Molten Span]] near {{waypoint 42, -9, -266}}
1. Return to [[Myrist, the Great Library]] and speak to [[The Maladjusted Codex]] at {{waypoint 290, -5, 58}}.
