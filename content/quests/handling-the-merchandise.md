## Description

"Traeger Verdegreen is going into business for himself. He's apparently made arrangements with some of the merchants in Kelethin to supply him with various goods. My job for him is simply to ask the merchants whether they've gotten the inventory Traeger's ordered." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

In no particular order.

1. Talk to [[Merchant Fallulah]] {{waypoint 295, 109, 136}} near the Kelethin First Regional Bank.
1. Talk to [[Merchant Pinehurst Deadleaf III]] {{waypoint 314, 124, 242}} on the platform between the Kelethin Bank and Gearheart's Forge.
1. Talk to [[Lilia Posey]] {{waypoint 142, 108, 178}} within the Kelethin Research Library.

After completing those steps, return to [[Traeger Verdegreen]].

## Rewards

- At least 1s 16c
- [[Verdegreen's Earring (Version 2)|Verdegreen's Earring]]

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Traeger Verdegreen: "Good job! This is enough for me to get started. Mainly what I need to do is make enough rope to tie together the boxes that I'll be sending out."
- Player Character: "Where are you sending things?"
- Traeger Verdegreen: "Quite a distance away, actually. There are new markets opening up overseas now that trade routes are re-established. I've been stocking up on local handicrafts and hope that they'll earn quite a profit."
- Player Character: "Can I see some of the items?"
- Traeger Verdegreen: "Funny you should ask that! I was wondering if you'd be interested in helping me out one more time. I have some items that I need to pick up here and there around Kelethin. While I'm making my ropes, would  you mind going out to get them together?"
- Player Character: "Not at all."
- Traeger Verdegreen: "That's great! You've been very helpful. I'm sure that these items are ready, it's just a matter of telling the merchants supplying me that you're working for me."
- Player Character: "I'll be back!"

In order to help Traeger in his business, I must speak with the merchants who are to provide the inventory Traeger's ordered. Merchant Fallulah is near the Kelethin First Regional Bank. Merchant Pinehurst Deadleaf III is on the platform between Kelethin First Regional Bank and Gearheart's Forge. Lilia Posey is a sage within the Kelethin Research Library.

- Merchant Fallulah: "Hello."
- Player Character: "Traeger Verdegreen asked me to confirm if your items will be ready for him."
- Merchant Fallulah: "Yes, I am quite ready. He can't start his business soon enough!"
- Player Character: "Thanks for the information!"

I've spoken with Merchant Fallulah.

- Merchant Pinehurst Deadleaf III: "Yes?"
- Player Character: "Traeger Verdegreen sent me to ask about the items you're providing him with."
- Merchant Pinehurst Deadleaf III: "What about them?"
- Player Character: "Are they ready?"
- Merchant Pinehurst Deadleaf III: "Maybe they are. Maybe they aren't. How do I know that Traeger really sent you? What if you're working for the competition?"
- Player Character: "Why don't you trust me?"
- Merchant Pinehurst Deadleaf III: "I don't trust anyone. But I suppose it makes no difference who really sent you. I have everything Traeger requested."
- Player Character: "Thanks."

I've spoken with Merchant Pinehurst Deadleaf III.

- Lilia Posey: "Are you looking for something?"
- Player Character: "Yes. Traeger Verdegreen sent me to ask about the items you're providing him for his merchant business."
- Lilia Posey: "Traeger...Traeger...ah! Yes, I've been holding several local books for him. He's welcome to come retrieve them at any time."
- Player Character: "I'll let him know."

I've spoken with Lilia Posey. I've spoken with the merchants who are to provide the inventory Traeger's ordered. I need to return to Traeger Verdegreen with the good news. Treager Verdegreen is in the building near the Old Kelethin Acorn Lift.

- Traeger Verdegreen: "Have you spoken with the merchants yet?"
- Player Character: "Yes, I have."
- Traeger Verdegreen: "That's a real time saver for me! There's just one last thing you could do for me, if you don't mind."

I've spoken with Traeger Verdegreen.

The merchants are ready to provide Traeger's business with goods. It looks like his business is off to a promising start!
