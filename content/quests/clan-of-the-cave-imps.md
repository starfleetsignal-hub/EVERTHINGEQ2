## Steps

1. Reclaim the provisions the imps have stolen
   - Get 4 supply crates within the imp cave {{waypoint 234, 76, -107}}
     - Each time you harvest a crate, [[An angered glacial scoundrel]] attacks.
1. Slay 6 imps
   - [[A glacial scoundrel|glacial scoundrels]], [[A glacial miscreant|glacial miscreants]], [[An angered glacial scoundrel|angered glacial scoundrels]] and [[Twiggle the Defiled]] all work for this update.
1. Return to Knut

## Rewards

- At least 2s 40c
