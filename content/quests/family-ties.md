## Prerequisite Quest

Must also have completed [[Under the Veil]].

## Steps

![Slumber Spell Urn](images/Slumber_spell_urn.jpg)

1. Speak with [[Vizzeren]] on [[Daarspire]] (receive [[Vizzeren's Arcanum Vision Elixir]]).
1. Use the elixir in [[Fraka Dar|Fraka Dar's]] roost.
1. Destroy 6 slumber spell urns while under the effect of the elixir (recast if necessary). They're found in the area around Fraka Dar, for instance at {{waypoint 423, 183, -821}}.
1. Talk to Fraka Dar.
1. Lead Fraka Dar back to Harla Dar. {{waypoint -101, 51, 730}}
