## Steps

1. Hunt 6 sleetsaber scouts and assassins
   - Kill [[A lurking sleetsaber]] or [[A sleetsaber assassin]] for this update
     - [[A lurking sleetsaber]] can be found in and around the Oubliette of Rime {{waypoint -164, -339.63, 362.69}} as well as in the Storm Gorge area {{waypoint 453, -379, 1263}}
     - You can find [[A sleetsaber assassin]] near the entrance to [[Hold of Rime: The Ascent]] {{waypoint 318, -319, 1192}}
1. Return to Borotar Pickbeard

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
