## Requisite

Must be a gnome: part 2 of the 3-part racial questline (good version)

## Steps

1. Right click the Seek and Destroy Bot next to [[Trina Tinkerton]] and tell it to follow you
1. From [[The Elddar Grove (POI)|the Elddar Grove]], zone into [[The Baubbleshire: Vale's Shadow]] {{waypoint 816, -21, -530}}
1. Slay 3 [[An invading nightblood|invading nightblood]]s, [[An invading lamia|invading lamia]]s, and/or [[An invading lamia castigator|invading lamia castigator]]s. The Seek and Destroy Bot will help on the first two fights, then implode on the third
1. Return to Trina Tinkerton
1. Speak to [[Rohn Tinkerton]] outside the [[Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos (POI)|South Qeynos]] {{waypoint 658, -19, 150}}
1. Speak to [[Johrsen]] near the Nettleville gate in South Qeynos {{waypoint 677, -19, 251}}
1. Return to Trina Tinkerton

## Rewards

- At least 10s 35c @ lvl 9
- At least 1p 20g @ lvl 100
