## Hint:

You will need 30 [[Sparkling Coal]] (Level 20 - 29) for upcoming quest **[[Smith-on-the-Spot]]**

Hint: can be bought in [[The Highlands]] from [[Trader Whitlock]] {{waypoint 332, 189, 492}}

## Steps

1. I should speak to [[Bartleby Blatherback]] in [[West Fort Irontoe]] at {{waypoint -223, 210, -139}}.
1. Collect 5 abandoned weapons (randomly-spawning, non-targetable harvest nodes) from the field just outside [[West Fort Irontoe]] (to the southwest). Prowl around the boundary wall looking for small glints on the ground.![abandoned weapon](images/Abandoned_weapon.jpg)
1. I must return these old weapons to [[Bartleby Blatherback|Bartleby]].
