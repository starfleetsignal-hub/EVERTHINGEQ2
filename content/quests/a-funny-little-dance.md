## Description

"Figgy Wigglestomp is a funny little halfling who loves to dance. However, he was reckless in the grove and a decaying guardian picked them up. Now he wants you to slay the guardians until his special dancing shoes are found." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill [[A decaying guardian|decaying guardians]] near the [[Sapphire Pond]] around {{waypoint -891, 68, 482}} until you find Figgy's Lost Dancing Shoes.
1. Return to [[Figgy Wigglestomp]]

## Rewards

- At least 5s 81c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Figgy Wigglestomp: "Ouch! My feet. I've lost my special dancing shoes."
- Player Character: "But you are a halfling. You are always barefoot."
- Figgy Wigglestomp: "Not me! I am a tenderfoot, but I simply love to dance. That is why I had some special dancing shoes made. But I lost them."
- Player Character: "How did you lose them?"
- Figgy Wigglestomp: "I was prancing around the grove of Sapphire Pond when I was attacked by stone guardians. I would have been crushed if I had not slipped out of my shoes. I bet that the guardian still has them. Could you get them back for me?"
- Player Character: "I love to dance myself. I will find those shoes."
- Figgy Wigglestomp: "Oh, thank you. Keep killing them until they drop in the grove of Sapphire Pond."
- Player Character: "Good advice."

Slay decaying guardians until you find Figgy's missing dancing shoes. Find Figgy's shoes.
