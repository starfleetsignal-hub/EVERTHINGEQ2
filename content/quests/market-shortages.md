## Steps

1. I should use [[Commissioner Varla Z'Velran|Varla's]] recipe: [[Working With Acids]] to craft a [[Vial of acid]] at a [[Chemistry Table]]. The recipe requires  1 Iron Cluster, 1 Tanned Leather Pelt, 1 Electrum Cluster and 2 Glowing Candles.
   - I should damage a roll of fabric at {{waypoint 65, 155, -124}}.
   - I should damage a roll of fabric at {{waypoint 60, 153, -125}}.
   - I should damage a roll of fabric at {{waypoint 55, 153, -122}}.
   - I should damage a roll of fabric at {{waypoint 55, 153, -131}}.
   - I should damage a roll of fabric at {{waypoint 53, 153, -131}} x4.
   - I should return to [[Commissioner Varla Z'Velran|Varla]].
