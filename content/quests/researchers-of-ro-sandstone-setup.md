*I should travel to the Sandstone Delta using the potion that I was given and speak to Shahin at Feldar Farm. If I feel like the area nearby is unsafe, then I should also purchase an extra teleport potion as soon as I arrive.*

### Materials Required

- Either harvest or otherwise acquire:
  - 25 [[Apatite Gem]]
  - 12 [[Bonytail Chub]]
  - 27 [[Brittlebush Taproot]]
  - 12 [[Cactus Fruit]]
  - 15 [[Desert Willow Wood]]
  - 30 [[Dustmaw Hide]]
  - 50 [[Pallasite Ore]]
- Bring along this fuel (or use a summon fuel merchant item):
  - 15 [[Astral Coal]]
  - 15 [[Astral Incense]]
  - 15 [[Astral Kindling]]
- Bring along these portable workstations (or use the summon workstation AAs):
  - [[Portable Work Bench v2.0]]

## Steps

1. Zone into the [[Sandstone Delta]] by using [[Call of the Delta]] potion that [[Hamdi]] gives you at the end of the previous quest (will place you right next to Shahin).
1. Speak to [[Shahin]] at [[Feldar Farm]] {{waypoint 757, 114, -445}}
   - If you have to return to the farm afoot from the carpet at [[Sandstone Landing]]:
     - Take the ramp up around {{waypoint 583, 25, 154}}.
     - There's a fork at {{waypoint 648, 68, 99}} -- go straight, and if you're low level, be prepared to dodge hostile mobs, or use invis/stealth to get around, whether it's a profession spell or something like [[Totem of the Chameleon]].
     - Cross the river and continue following the path to the farm {{waypoint 681, 114, -389}}.
     - Once you reach [[Shahin]], you can purchase additional teleport potions [[Call of the Delta]] for 2p each from [[Tarkin]] at {{waypoint 751, 116, -454}}.
   - Recipes for this quest were already autoscribed at the end of the previous quest.
1. Make the [[Desert Forge]] on a summoned/portable work bench. You will need:
   - 20 [[Pallasite Ore]]
   - 15 [[Dustmaw Hide]]
   - 5 [[Astral Coal]]
1. Make the [[Desert Stove and Keg]] on a summoned/portable work bench. You will need:
   - 5 [[Apatite Gem]]
   - 15 [[Desert Willow Wood]]
   - 15 [[Pallasite Ore]]
   - 5 [[Astral Coal]]
1. Make the [[Desert Engraved Desk]] on a summoned/portable work bench. You will need:
   - 5 [[Apatite Gem]]
   - 15 [[Brittlebush Taproot]]
   - 15 [[Pallasite Ore]]
   - 5 [[Astral Coal]]
1. Place the equipment by clicking the green sparkles immediately behind [[Shahin]].
1. Speak to [[Shahin]].
1. Make 3 [[Pest Repellent Scroll]] on the engraved desk inside the small hut next to [[Shahin]]. For each combine you will need:
   - 5 [[Apatite Gem]] (15 total)
   - 2 [[Brittlebush Taproot]] (6 total)
   - 5 [[Dustmaw Hide]] (15 total)
   - 5 [[Astral Incense]] (15 total)
1. Speak to [[Shahin]].
1. Make 3 [[Water Purification Tablet]] on the stove inside the small hut by [[Shahin]]. For each combine you will need:
   - 2 [[Brittlebush Taproot]] (6 total)
   - 4 [[Bonytail Chub]] (12 total)
   - 4 [[Cactus Fruit]] (12 total)
   - 5 [[Astral Kindling]] (15 total)
1. Speak to [[Shahin]].
   - ***Note:** Don't forget to purchase [[Call of the Delta]] from [[Tarkin]] {{waypoint 752.36, 115.89, -454.74}} before you leave!*
1. Return to [[Jaranna Thistlebloom]] at {{waypoint -81, -2, 106}} inside the pub in the [[Village of Shin]] to complete the quest.

## Unlocking Flight

After completing the main tradeskill signature quests, you unlock public researcher missions in Raj'Dur Plateaus, Takish Badlands, and Sandstone Delta. You will be unable to fly in any of these overland zones until you complete at least 15 of these research missions (combined -- so for instance, you can do 5 missions for each researcher to unlock flight), or by unlocking flight via the adventurer signature timeline.

The research missions are on a 3-hour timer that is universal for your server. To see how much time until the mission resets, go into your quest journal, click the "Mission Timers" tab at the top, filter for Renewal of Ro, and type in the word "research" in the filter by name bar at the bottom.
