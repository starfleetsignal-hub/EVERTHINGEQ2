## Steps

1. Collect 6 weeping tooth worts (little white flowers, found along the edge of the banks in Firmroot). Some known locations:
   - {{waypoint 102.81, -87.51, -552.40}}
   - {{waypoint 100.81, -87.73, -598.32}}
   - {{waypoint 60.24, -87.74, -519.02}}
   - {{waypoint 28.14, -87.33, -424.43}}
   - {{waypoint 131.03, -87.46, -339.34}}
   - {{waypoint 123.44, -94.59, -217.55}}
   - {{waypoint 62.70, -94.59, -202.46}}
   - {{waypoint 105.95, -87.83, -387.89}}
1. Return to Wik {{waypoint -158, -82, -531}}
1. Place the flowers on Floppik's grave {{waypoint 208, -86, -774}} by using the [[Flowers for Beloved]] item from your inventory while you are standing near the grave.
1. Return to Wik

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2,000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
