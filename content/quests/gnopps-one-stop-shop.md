## Background

[[Gnip Gnopp]], a fine constructor of gnomish toys and gizmos in [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters|Gnomeland Security]], would be glad to provide [[Gardy Ex-Giftgiver|Gardy]] with wonderful new mechanized gifts, but he is unable to meet the demand. His shop is closed up and is in a state of disarray. If I help him clean the place up and get things in working order then he would be able to help.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Gnip Gnopp's Gizmo Shop]] and help get it back into working order. The Gizmo Shop is accessed by clicking the grounded gear near Gnip Gnopp. {{waypoint -550, 171, 1051}}
1. At the bottom of the ramp, destroy the boards on the west wall to access the main work area.
1. Complete the following tasks:
   - Remove 5 piles of *rusty rubble*. These are pretty large piles, easily seen.
   - Harvest 8 *useful gizmo parts*. These are small gears and such, scattered all over the floor and tables. They will respawn.
   - Defeat 9 [[A mechanized toy maker|mechanized toy makers]].
1. Return to Gnip Gnopp in Steamfont Mountains.
