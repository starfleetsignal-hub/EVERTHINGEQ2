## Steps

1. Obtain some new picks and some draft beer for the construction crew. There must be someone in the Thurgadin crafters area that can provide such goods.
   - Obtain a stack of new mining picks
     - [[Bundle of Coldain Pick Axes]] can be purchased from [[Belliv Picknose]] {{waypoint 917, -274, 123}} for 1c
   - Obtain some coldain draft beer for the crew
     - [[Coldain Draft Brew]] can be purchased from [[Haligrin Brewbarrel]] {{waypoint 868, -274, 132}} for 1c
1. Return the goods to the construction foreman at the Dragon Ring re-construction site.

## Rewards

- 3 [[Token of Greater Teleportation]]
