## Steps

1. Go to [[The Blackburrow Brewery]] {{waypoint 21, -40, 61}} and find some leather straps. You only need to enter the brewery to get the credit for this step.
1. Kill any gnoll in the zone to get chain links for the morningstar.
1. Kill [[A Sabertooth miner|Sabertooth miners]] or [[A Sabertooth excavator|excavators]] to get a sturdy mining pick. {{waypoint 32, 2, -28}}
1. Kill 16 [[A Sabertooth guard|Sabertooth guards]] to get spikes for the ball. {{waypoint -21, -12, -6}}
1. Kill [[Crugybar the Rock]] to break the spike tips off the mace. He can be found in the mine shaft with the miners and excavators. {{waypoint 3, -7, -64}}

## Rewards

- [[The Chomper (Weapon)]]

## Note

- The broken morningstar dropped on 3/23/2025 from a Sabertooth miner.  Spent many, many days killing in the Blackburrow mine.
