## Steps

1. Defeat raiding parties for clues. These will be linked groups of 2 [[A fanatic ritualist|fanatic ritualists]] and 1 [[A fanatic lightseeker|fanatic lightseeker]] (105vv).  Kill 3 linked encounters.Possible locations:
   - {{waypoint 221, -223, -380}}
   - {{waypoint 268, -227, -356}}
   - {{waypoint 314, -214, -398}}
   - {{waypoint 348, -176, -151}}
   - {{waypoint 389, -229, -266}}
1. Find the camp near the moon lake mentioned in the note: click Turklig's mummy {{waypoint 332, -202, -200}}.
1. Find information in the Crusaders Cave by talking to [[Lissys]] {{waypoint 574, 250, 1260}}.
1. Search [[Crypt of Dalnir: Baron's Workshop (Solo)|Dalnir: Barons Workshop]] for clues
   - Zone in at {{waypoint 790, -129, -584}}. (Also updates in the [[Crypt of Dalnir: Baron's Workshop (Heroic)|heroic version]]).
   - Click the mummy at {{waypoint 28, 1, -198}}.
1. Return to [[Mallic the Venerable]] {{waypoint 170, -204, -522}}.
1. Find the village of Gwerp {{waypoint 557, -106, -299}}.
1. Click on the **Gwerp Monument** {{waypoint 560, -106, -298}}.
1. Return to [[Mallic the Venerable]] {{waypoint 170, -204, -522}}.

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 166400 status
