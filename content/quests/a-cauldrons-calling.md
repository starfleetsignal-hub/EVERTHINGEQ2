## How to Start

1. Examine the cauldron in the secret room within [[The Library (Nektropos Castle)|the Library]], at {{waypoint -24, 14, 33}}.
1. Obtain [[Guestroom key 01]] by completing the [[Visions of Hatred]] quest.
1. Examine a ***cabinet*** on the second floor, in the Guest Room at {{waypoint 21, 13, -21}}.

## Steps

1. Examine the cauldron again. Place ladle in the cauldron.
1. Kill four [[A doll of Nyth|dolls of Nyth]]:
   - [[Crysta's Bedroom]] {{waypoint 45, 14, -73}}
   - Bedroom {{waypoint -27, 14, -28}}
   - Basement storage rooms {{waypoint -50, -13, -46}} and {{waypoint -21, -13, -38}}
1. Examine cauldron again.( /waypoint -24, 14, 33 )
- At least 55s
- One of the following:
  - [[Flail of threshing]]
  - [[Giantfall axe]]
  - [[Malignant mace]]
  - [[Peacemaker]]

## Notes

This quest is a prerequisite for completing the [[From Below to Castle]] tome collection in [[Nektropos Castle: Tribulation]] (a zone designed for Level 70 characters).
