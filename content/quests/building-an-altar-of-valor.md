## Steps

1. Gather the components that will be a part of your small altar to [[Mithaniel Marr]] by digging them up by using [[An old military shovel]] at the following locations:
   - [[The Forgotten (POI)|The Forgotten]] in [[The Commonlands]]. {{waypoint -590, -48, -628}}
   - [[The Mourned]] in the Commonlands.  {{waypoint 173, -45, 257}}
   - [[The Destroyed Tower of Marr]] in [[The Graveyard]]. {{waypoint 53, -2, 114}}
     - NOTE: Access [[The Graveyard]] at {{waypoint -1393.80, -86.23, -335.72}} ) from the Commonlands.
   - [[In Memorial of the Faydark's Champions|Memorial of the Faydark's Champions]] underneath [[Kelethin]] in [[Greater Faydark]]. {{waypoint 262, 57, 128}}
1. Return to Sir Bayden Cauldthorn.

## Rewards

- [[Humble Altar of Mithaniel Marr]]
- Completing this quest unlocks the [[Valor of Marr]] and [[Truthbringer]] abilities
- +1250 faction with **Mithaniel Marr**
