## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of Bristlebane has asked for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Collect 3 [[Tools of the Trade (Item)|tools of the trade]] from creatures found in [[Zimara Breadth]]
   - Random drop from a mystic grappler in small chest, (the hand looking mob)
1. Gather or purchase 10 [[Trystine Gem|trystine gems]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas
   - *Common harvest from animal dens*
   - *Can buy off broker*
   - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to the [[Herald to the Grand Prankster]] at {{waypoint -832, 7, -203}} in [[Rivervale]]
