## Steps

1. Find a bucket. {{waypoint -1120, -58, -442}}
1. Douse 5 crates of torches in various locations in the Commonlands (e.g., the Crossroads at {{waypoint -529.69, -44.87, -316.21}}, nomad camps, Kerran camp at at {{waypoint -186.62, -47.81, 113.13}}. You can find all 5 crates at one of the locations. They will re-pop pretty quickly.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

Coin

- +1000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -1000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -1000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
