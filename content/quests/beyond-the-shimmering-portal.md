### Cliff Notes:  Quick and dirty.

1. Go West to Frostfoot Foothold. Kill the named Fridget.
1. Go East to Holgresh Haunt. Kill the two named, Hakapow and Takaboom. (Take the **Golden Flask)**
1. Back in the main room, kill Lord Frost. (You must pull him off the platform to kill him.)
1. Take the stairs to the next area.
1. Go West to Planar Perspective. Kill trash to spawn Eldrig. You must attack her to make the idols clickable.
   - Move each idol to a pedestal. The idol must emote that it is satisfied with this position. Keep moving the idol until its sits on the pedestal it is happy with.
   - Once all idols are placed, you can now finish killing the named.
   - Take the shard (The blue glowing triangle) and place it on a pedestal back in the main room. {{waypoint -47, 15, 141}} or  {{waypoint 8, 15, 140}}
1. Go to the East room. Kill the trash and take the **Feather** from the hawk sitting on the crate.
   - Go through one of the doors in this room to the arena. Kill the trash to spawn The Master's Reflection. Talk to him. Then kill him, paying attention to the mobs that spawn on the different colored disk in the room.
   - Once he is dead, talk to Camylle in the same room {{waypoint -108, 15, 136}} and she will give you **a lock of hair**.
   - Go through one of the doors and move the shard (Blue triangle thingy) to a pedestal in the main room. {{waypoint -47, 15, 141}} or  {{waypoint 8, 15, 140}}
   - (A giant block of ice begins to crack).
   - Kill Spikeberg.  After he is dead, a spiral staircase made of ice will rise from the room.
1. Go up the stars and at the top, kill Brubunk for the last item, the Ice Spike. (Seems to be a **chip of ivory** on the body, not an Ice Spike)
1. Right click the Golden Flask in your inventory to make **Ehayae's Song**.
1. Place Ehayea's Song on your hot bar.
1. Enter the room to kill/save Solvage the Voracious.  When he emotes about Bloodlust, use the song on him. (He uses Stun). NOTE: If you enter the room before you create the Ehayea's song you will not be able to kill/save the named. You will have to leave and try again.
1. Return to quest giver for your reward.

## Steps

1. Enter the Choronportal next to Sorsaadh to Miragul's Madness.<br>***Note: Content in the SOLO zone is level 127 ^^^ heroic and extremely difficult, if not impossible, at level 120 wearing Visions of Vetrovia box gear. It cannot be accomplished wearing pre-VoV gear.***
1. Explore Miragul's Madness by going into the first room.
1. Explore more of Miragul's Madness by going right into the next room.
1. Search for the unlucky Frostfoot goblin.
   1. Pick the feather from an artic hawk at {{waypoint -70.18, 0.00, 0.92}}    Hawk becomes aggro.   You receive [[Arctic hawk feather]] (no zone).
   1. Had to kill all the mobs in the ice cavern to get Fridget to spawn
      1. Fridget "Gains power from the nearby snow drift!"
      1. He will emote, "Cold as ice!", indicating a buff that reduced damage to him by 90% and increases his potency by 10K%. The buff can be dispelled.
      1. He will emote, "Bye bye ice" when the buff clears.
      1. This fight took 27 minutes for 125 Warden in VoV gear with level 15 geared Claz'zakla and Tunare's Chosen Pet. Resolve 5804, fervor 942.8%
1. Keep searching Miragul's Madness for answers.
   1. Kill all in "Holgresh Haunt" (left door from entrance)
   1. Takaboom and Hakapow will spawn. Straight fight.
   1. Click on [[A golden flask]] at {{waypoint -100.40, -0.99, 26.69}}.
   1. Lord Frost spawns in the middle room.
      1. Has a buff, Seat of Deep Ice - reduces damage 99% until you pull him off the dais.  I pulled him into the cavern just inside the door and was able to damage him then.
      1. Killing Lord Frost removes the ice barrier from the stairs heading up.
   1. The stairs lead to a circular room with rooms to the left (east) and right (west).
   1. Choose the left room.  There is a dead necromancer on the floor.
1. What is behind the chaos of Miragul's Madness?
   1. There is a door at the far end of the room, leading out to the Ampitheater of Arcane
   1. Kill all in the room and the Master's Reflection will spawn.  Talk to him and he will turn aggro.
      1. He will emote that he is receiving power from two of the summoned remnants.  Three remnants will spawn on the summoning pads.  Kill the two he's gaining power from to make the Master's Reflection damageable again.  If you kill the third one (the one he's not gaining power from), it will tell you the remnant is not the correct remnant enhancing the Master's Reflection's powers.
      1. Pay attention to the color of the emotes and the aura that appears around the Master's Reflection. Kill the remnants on the two color pads that create that color: red and yellow pads when orange, yellow and blue pads when green, and red and blue pads when purple/violet.

7. Speak with Camylle (near the entrance of the Ampitheater).  {{waypoint -107, 15, 137}}  She gives you a [[Lock of hair]].

8. In hopes of saving Solvag, I must assemble [[Ehayae's Song]].

1. In the room where the dead necromancer was, there is a glowy vase near the door to the center room called "Tears of the Master".  This is now clickable.  You can right click the top portion and "move" the twin frost shard to the frost shard pedestal at {{waypoint -49.18, 15.71, 138.47}}.  The Twin Frost Shard locks into place.
1. Proceed to the room to the west (right as you come into the center room).  The other twin shard is just inside the door to the right, however it does not become clickable until you kill the boss in the room.
   1. Kill all the things in the room.  Edrigen spawns in the middle.
   1. She has a buff called Godly guise.  There are three idols on the table in the back of the room.  Depending on her correct guise, you have to choose the correct idol and on the correct pedestal.
      1. When you put the correct idol on the correct pedestal, it will message that an idol <of whatever> is pleased with it's location.
      1. Once the idols are all in the correct place, Edrigen can be damaged again.  She will change form about every 8 to 10%.
      1. Some forms you will have to place only 1 idol, some you'll have to place all 4 to debuff.
      1. Once you kill Edrigen, you'll get a message:  Something's slipping away with treasure!  (If you dont find "Slippedeedoodah", you'll get a message that it got away.)
   1. Move the second tear of frost to the pedestal at {{waypoint 9.60, 15.69, 139.11}}
   1. Spikeberg becomes aggro in the middle of the room.
      1. His buff is Sub Zero Core.  Kill the golem ice cores to remove the buff.  Buff stacks multiple times, in accordance with the number of golem ice cores present.  He spawned three and I couldn't put out enough DPS to kill one before it spawned again so there were always three golems and my pets and I just stayed on Spikeberg the whole time.  He also has an AOE ice spike spell that you get notifies for.
   1. Once you kill Spikeberg, a stairway rises in the middle of the floor.  Up top you'll find mobs and Burunk the Boundless, Seal of Disproval.  :D
      1. Burunk's buff is a 10% invulnerability for each savage molkor he spawns.  You have to stay ahead of the spawns.
      1. He provides the [[Chip of ivory]] on death (loot).
   1. Right click [[A golden flask|the golden flask]] from your inventory with all the ingredients and create [[Ehayae's Song]].  Place it on your hotbar.

9, Save Solvag.

1. Watch chat.  Every time Solvag emotes about bloodlust (about every 10%) use the Song on your hotbar.
1. Solvag also has a cone and AOE stun.

10.  Return to Sorsaadh in the Chronoweave.
