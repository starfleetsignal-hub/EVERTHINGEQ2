Within the Midst Library, I discovered a tome entitled, Shadeweaver's Origins II, with pages of notes and diagrams of the thicket's environs, but little in the way of its inhabitants. This particular volume focuses on the western regions and cave systems of Shadeweaver's Thicket, for which I feel is my duty to record its fauna, dead or alive. (Preferably alive, but probably dead.)
