## Steps

1. Collect unclean water from the still waters found within the [[The Commonlands]]:
   - {{waypoint -244, -48, 440}}
   - {{waypoint 540, -48, -560}}
   - {{waypoint 655, -48, 194}}
   - {{waypoint 623, -49, -458}}
   - {{waypoint 468, -48, -325}}
   - {{waypoint -18, -48, -349}}
   - {{waypoint -37, -48, -377}}
   - {{waypoint 378, -47, -415}}

1. Pick some poisonous fungus that grows on the banks of the river closest to the Enlightenment Cemetary around {{waypoint 31, -46, -281}} just south of the zone to [[Neriak, City of Hate]] within the [[The Commonlands]].
1. Poison some Freeport Ale Barrels in the [[The Commonlands]] at the [[Crossroads]]:
   - {{waypoint -520, -44, -303}}
   - {{waypoint -490, -44, -267}}) Be careful a guard wanders here every so often.
1. Poison some fresh water from the Thexian workers in the [[Yapping Maze]], just south of [[Fallen Gate]]:
   - {{waypoint 1398, -34, -217}}
   - {{waypoint 1340, -42, -174}}
1. Return to [[Drizas N'Ryt]].

## Rewards

- +5,000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- -5,000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -5,000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
