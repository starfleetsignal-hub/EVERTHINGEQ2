## Steps

1. Locate [[Elguka the Oracle]] in Freeport - she's outside [[The Temple of War]] in North Freeport {{waypoint -112, -8, -130}}.  She gives you [[Elguka's Talisman of Awakening]]
1. Locate the following people.  Target them, use Elguka's Talisman near them, and then speak to them.
   - [[Zeyx]] near the Freeport Militia House {{waypoint 108, -16, 123}}
   - [[Akkin]] near The Temple of War {{waypoint -50, -8, -152}}
   - [[Velnak]] on the docks {{waypoint -229, -56, 130}}
1. Return to Elguka
1. Defeat 6 Kerrans in [[Stonestair Byway: The Animus Corridors]] - any kerra in zone updates
1. Return to Nelik Ulnya

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g @ lvl 95
