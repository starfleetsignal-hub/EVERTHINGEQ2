## Steps

1. Find the [[Elixir of Spring Prime Recipe]] (gatherable at {{waypoint -52, 207, 6}}, looks like a sparkling piece of paper). Scribe this for later use.
1. Kill all five of the non-aggro roaming training soldiers. This causes Sergeant Schriber to appear briefly, and he unlocks the doors to the other areas. [This is not a required step in the quest, but is needed so as to be able to continue with the quest steps.]
1. Gather the following ingredients:
   - Three [[A Sulfur Rock|sulfur rocks]]. These are harvestable in the barracks area, around the edges of the room centered at around {{waypoint -60, 210, -30}}. They look like grey stones against a grey floor, so they can be difficult to see.
   - One [[A maiden's tears|vial of maiden's tears]]. This drops off [[A zombie maid]] at around {{waypoint -50, 210, -80}}.
   - Four [[A scoop of velium dust|scoops of velium dust]]. Killing each [[A cold velium statue|cold velium statue]] drops two of these. These come to life when on this quest when in the dining room at around {{waypoint 65, 210, 10}}.
1. Once the above ingredients have been acquired, craft the [[Elixir of Spring Prime]] at the Bubbling Cauldron in the outer kitchen area at around {{waypoint 65, 210, 52}}. This is very easy, no tradeskill levels are required.
1. Return the elixir to Vethilot the Young to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g
