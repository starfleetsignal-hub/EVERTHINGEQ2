## Steps

1. Collect 100 bone fragments from undead around in the Graveyard.
   - Each kill gives an average of 12-13 updates, so keep killing until you have a total of 100.
1. Return to Durkix for your rewards.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 5c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Torn backpack]]
  - [[A wooden chest]]
