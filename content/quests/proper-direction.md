## Steps

1. Look for lost souls
   - Look for lost soul near first Tsol Ew camp.  Talk to [[Clurrg]] {{waypoint 137, -85, 114}} ![Clurrg](images/Clurrg.jpg) and select the following questions and statements to his questions:
     - What was the last thing you were doing? (2)
     - Tell me what happened next. (3)
     - You fought bravely, and you will be remembered on Norrath. (2)
   - Look for lost soul near second Tsol Ew camp.  Talk to [[Fellia Groundroot]] {{waypoint 164, -6, 262}} ![Fellia Groundroot](images/Fellia_Groundroot.jpg) and select the following questions and statements to her questions:
     - In a manner of speaking.  Tell me about yours. (1)
     - Only you can answer that. (2)
     - Did it attack you? (3)
   - Look for lost soul near third Tsol Ew camp.  Talk to [[Jelik]] {{waypoint -73, 7, -85}} ![Jelik](images/Jelik.jpg) and select the following questions and statements to his questions:
     - Why?  Why can't you stand? (1)
     - And how do you feel now? (3)
1. Return to [[Riishu Sulrel]]

- At least 1p 30g
