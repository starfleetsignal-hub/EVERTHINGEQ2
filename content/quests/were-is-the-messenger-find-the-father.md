## Materials Required

- 35 [[Amsonia Roots]]
- 25 [[Forlorn Ore]]
- 30 [[Greytusk Hide]]
- 15 [[Star Diopside]]
- 2 [[Baptista Root]] -- *rare*
- 1 [[Deino Hide]] -- *rare*
- 1 [[Tsavorite]] -- *rare*
- 5 [[Astral Coal]]
- 15 [[Astral Filament]]

## Steps

1. Hail [[Rithsar]]
1. Travel to [[Karuupa Jungle]] using a plumedrake keeper at  {{waypoint -26, 52, 547}} in [[Svarni Expanse]] and find Alghoza's father, [[Piccolo]] {{waypoint 524, 145, -597}}
1. Hail again to convince him to go to [[Alghoza]] and receive [[Recipe Scroll: Child's Bedding]]
1. Harvest the following common materials (need to harvest):
   - 50 [[Amsonia Roots]]
   - 30 [[Greytusk Hide]]
   - 15 [[Star Diopside]]
   - 25 [[Forlorn Ore]]
1. Obtain the following rare materials (owned materials validate):
   - 2 [[Baptista Root|Baptista Roots]]
   - 1 [[Deino Hide]]
   - 1 [[Tsavorite]]
   - *Buying on broker works for this rare materials.*
1. I must craft the bedding items.
   - Craft a [[Child's Bedroll]]
   - Craft [[Comfy Child's Blankets]]
   - Craft a [[Child's Pillow]] for Alghoza
   - Craft a [[Glowing Wisp Nightlight]]
   - Note: There is a [[Far Seas Crafting Station]] (universal crafting station) {{waypoint 530, 145, -591}} nearby in [[Karuupa Village]], next to Piccolo's tent.![](images/Piccolotent.png)
1. Place the items in the tent at {{waypoint 525.75, 145.18, -589.75}} by clicking the tent.
