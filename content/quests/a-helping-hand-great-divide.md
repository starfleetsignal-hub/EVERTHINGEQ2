## Steps

1. Gather arctic sea flowers for medicine and kill sleeper sharks for meat
   - Gather 5 arctic sea flowers.  They are underwater on the underside of the ice shelf at {{waypoint -1024, -565, -1992}}
   - Kill 4 [[An Iceclad sleeper shark|Iceclad sleeper sharks]] {{waypoint -975, -563, -2019}} ![Arctic sea flowers growing on the underside of the ice shelf](images/A_Helping_Hand_quest_-_sea_flower.jpg)
1. Return to [[Mirna Greatfrost]]
1. Help 6 injured gnolls, around {{waypoint -935, -516, -2254}}
   - Simply talk to any "a freed Snowfang gnoll" to update
1. Return to [[Mirna Greatfrost]]
