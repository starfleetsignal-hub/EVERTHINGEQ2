***This quest is only available to players under level 30***

## Steps

1. Find a crate of poison near the Hunter's Cabin {{waypoint -741, -16, 232}}.
1. With the poison, contaminate 5 crates of apples. Locations to look include:
   - [[The Keep of the Gnollslayers]] {{waypoint -2112, -46, 446}}
   - [[Sayer's Outfitters]] near where you found the poison {{waypoint -741, -16, 232}}
   - Mariner’s Island {{waypoint 352, -13, 786}}
   - Merchant’s tents {{waypoint 145, -16, 211}}
1. Return to [[Gol M'Tun]].

## Rewards

- Faction Only:
  - +1000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
  - -1000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
  - -1000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**

## Notes

You can use the [[Mentoring]] system to perform this quest if you are too high level. You are not required to stay mentored once you have the quest active.
