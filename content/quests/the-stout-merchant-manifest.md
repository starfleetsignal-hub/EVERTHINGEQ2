## Steps

1. Listen to Fippy Darkpaw's instructions.
1. Read [[Stout Merchant Manifest]] you receive from Fippy Darkpaw.
1. Talk to [[Gornit Penwiggle]] "G.P." at the *Deepmug Tavern* in [[The Baubbleshire]].
1. Talk to [[Proprietor Blagard]] "P.B." at *The Vasty Deep* Tavern in [[Starcrest Commune]].
1. Talk to [[Karg Icemoore]] "K.I." at *The Frozen Tundra* Tavern in [[Graystone Yard]].
1. Talk to [[Borden Mannus]] "B.M." at *The Mermaid's Lure* Tavern in [[Qeynos Harbor]].
1. Talk to [[P.T. Irontoe (Qeynos Capitol District)|P.T. Irontoe]] "P.I." at [[Irontoe's East]] Tavern in [[North Qeynos]].
1. Return to [[Fippy Darkpaw, the 4th]].
