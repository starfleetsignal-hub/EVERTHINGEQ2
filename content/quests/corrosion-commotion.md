## Steps

1. Using the [[Seaspray Wand]] given to you, collect silver deposits (dropped on the ground) from the following:
   - 6 from silverloid sentinels in the [[Turquoise Oasis]] area around {{waypoint -396, 127.62, 385.38}}
   - 6 from silverband soldiers in the [[Turquoise Oasis]] area around {{waypoint -522, 144, 370}}
1. Return speak to [[Oolfonso]] at [[Minstrel's Cradle]] {{waypoint 715.79, -43.56, -459.27}}

## Rewards

- 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1000 faction with **Isles of Wisdom**
- 3 [[Large Violet Fan Grass]]
