## Steps

1. Enter [[Takish'Hiz Arena: Renewal of Ro (Solo)|Takish'Hiz Arena: Renewal of Ro [Solo]]] in [[Goldami Reservoir Camp]] at {{waypoint -547.09, 10.77, 429.24}}.
1. Speak to [[Firiona Vie]] at {{waypoint -79.61, 139.85, -838.73}}.
1. Witness the Renewal of Ro.
1. Defeat the army of [[A cursed Elddar remnant|cursed Elddar remnants]].
1. Wait for the script to update (leaving the zone without doing so, means you have to delete the quest and do it again).
1. Return to the adventure party in [[Goldami Reservoir Camp]].
1. Speak with [[Nairion Eldi]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 57791 status
- [[RoR Flag: Buried Takish'Hiz Unlock]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Elddar Hope Bracelet]]
  - [[Elddar Strength Bracelet]]
