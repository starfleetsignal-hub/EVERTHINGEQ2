## Steps

1. Within 20 minutes, destroy 5 boxes of beakers (long wooden boxes with blue sparkles) around the alchemists of Freeport. East Freeport alchemy shop at {{waypoint -63.48, -37.78, 32.02}} and South Freeport alchemist in the banker/broker area {{waypoint -210.14, -56.47, 150.64}}.  (no other locations were found during a thorough search of the entire city, had to wait for boxes to respawn at both locations to get all 5)
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +2000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- **Note**: This quest does not grant any experience or achievement experience.
