## Notes

- This quest can not be done if [[Not So Safe Deposits]] is completed. But you must have done that quest up to the last step to get this quest offered. It cannot be picked up after either. It is a one-or-the-other choice of quests.
- When you started [[Crime Pays Someone]] but want to do [[Lend a Helping Hand]] instead, then you can delete the [[Crime Pays Someone]] quest and pickup either of the quest. You still can't do both but change your selection that way.

## Steps

1. Travel to the zone [[Echo Caverns]] into the area [[Shattered Chasm]] {{waypoint -282.10, 59.73, -282.55}}.
   - Gather 8 "non-trackable" chasm thorn roots. Some approximate locations:![Chasm Thorn Root](images/Chasm_Thorn_Root.jpg)
     - {{waypoint -60, 100, -418}}
     - {{waypoint -138.96, 118.30, -405.59}}
     - {{waypoint -180.59, 113.87, -335.08}}
     - {{waypoint -163.72, 117.96, -380.30}}
     - {{waypoint -172.45, 115.48, -307.95}}
     - {{waypoint -79.94, 108.42, -267.09}}
   - Hunt 6 [[A deepshade horror|deepshade horrors]] {{waypoint -28.65, 198.35, -298.85}} for ichor.
   - Hunt 5 [[A chasm shadow eater|chasm shadow eaters]] for ethereal cilla {{waypoint 19.72, 131.17, -414.03}} pull with mercenary heal.
1. Speak to [[Evuar Vrottoth]] in the [[House of Midst]] {{waypoint 721, -9, -504}} which is of course found from inside the Shadow Haven, 2nd door on the left side {{waypoint -522, 3, -260}}.
1. Return to [[Freesia Lastchain]].
