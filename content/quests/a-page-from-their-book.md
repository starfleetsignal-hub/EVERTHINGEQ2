## Steps

1. Find the pages of the book.
   - Collect four [[A torn page of strange words]].  They spawn randomly on the docks and are trackable.
1. Find the rest of the book.
   - At {{waypoint 1319, -533, 537}}, [[A thieving gnomish pirate]] spawns and attacks.  Defeat him to update.
1. Return to Dort Lumrick

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
