## Steps

1. Travel to [[Jarsath Wastes]] and use the [[Catalogue Creature]] ability to catalog:
   - Catalog [[A deadly stoneglint]] around {{waypoint 686, -20, 543}}
     - Northwest of Danak Shipyard
   - Catalog [[A Durus decapitator]] in the [[Howling Stones]] around {{waypoint 693, -129, 144}}
   - Catalog [[A Durus scar guardian]] in the [[Howling Stones]] around {{waypoint 709, -119, 58}}
   - Catalog [[A prickly spined succulent]] around {{waypoint -728, -102, -404}}
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]

## Hints

The creatures are trackable.  If you don't have tracking, many ranger mercs can track, for instance [[Villax Sned]] from the VoV expansion can track.
