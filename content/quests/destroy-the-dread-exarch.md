## Description

Deep in the bowels of the cursed keep the foulest of monstrosities holds a very dark court. Dread Exarch Mordek, leader of the Burning Dead, has established an asylum of necromancers who have the potential to spread their dreadful contagion beyond the confines of the keep. I have agreed to destroy Mordek before he and his cult can threaten the Commonlands.

## Steps

1. Destroy [[Dread Exarch Mordek]] in [[Befallen: Necrotic Asylum]]
1. Return to [[Ragheejb Yasir]] in the [[Moors of Ykesha]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 50g
- [[Void Shard]] x2
