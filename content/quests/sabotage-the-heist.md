## Steps

Gil McMartin instructed me as to what is next mandated to shower honor upon Qeynos: I must pilfer the crates of jewels consigned to the vaults of the Overlord.

1. Within 20 minutes, steal 5 crates of jewels found in shops around Freeport.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +2000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- **Note**: This quest does not grant any experience or achievement experience.
