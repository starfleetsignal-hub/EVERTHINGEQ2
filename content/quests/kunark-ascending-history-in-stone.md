## Requirements

- You need to be able to speak [[To Speak as a Dragon|draconic]] to speak with the Scrollkeeper Sataleeti
- You need to be able to speak [[A Mysterious Black Tome|Sathirian]]
- You need to be able to speak [[Goblish]] for this quest.

## Steps

1. (While flying over Nye'Caelona) Fly all the way up to the top of the spire {{waypoint -202, 228, -239}}. There you find [[Runekeeper Talisyn]], [[Scrollkeeper Sataleeti]], and [[High Arcanist Faxalim]].
   1. Hail Scrollkeeper Sataleeti to "eavesdrop" on their conversation.
   1. Follow the conversation of the 3 mages. Convince them of your usefulness.
1. Kill the following:
   - 8 [[A deadly stoneleer|stoneleers]] in the Giant's Walk area {{waypoint -426.61, 88.59, 266.72}}
   - 6 [[A toothed gnasher|gnashers]] in the Grunt's Pass area {{waypoint -219.69, -59.53, 196.37}}
1. Return to Runekeeper Talisyn {{waypoint -200, 228, -238}}
1. Apply the Conductor stone that needs a recharge - just fly straight to the one on the north-west coast at {{waypoint 708, -155, -667}}.  It's broken. ![arcane obelisk](images/Arcane_obelisk.jpg)
1. Attempt to recharge it. You will be attacked by a pair of Bloody Tooth goblins (one 103^, one 103 even), but you don't have to kill them.
1. Search [[Twark]] for the Obfuscation Pylon.
   - *Note: You must click ALL 4 (in order listed) of the rune-inscribed stones (locations listed below) to update the quest before the last pylon will appear randomly in one of the six locations.
   - *Note: You need to be able to speak [[A Mysterious Black Tome|Sathirian]] and [[Claiming the Goblish Tongue|Goblish]] to advance beyond this step!*
   - Inspect (click) the rune-inscribed stone in Twark {{waypoint 137, -240, -861}}. It is a small squarish stone to the right (south) of [[Vhesh]]. *It's important to click it, not just accepting the quest update when you are close to it* ![a rune-inscribed stone](images/A_rune-inscribed_stone.jpg)
   - Inspect another rune-inscribed stone in Twark. {{waypoint 103, -173, -831}}. On the ledge just above the first one. Not on top.
   - Inspect another rune-inscribed stone in Twark. {{waypoint 65, -165, -738}}. At the foot of the tree.
   - Inspect another rune-inscribed stone in Twark. {{waypoint 244, -179, -740}}.
1. The rune-inscribed stones in Twark aren't from the pylon. I need to search for the pylon! The pylon looks like a 6 foot tall grey obelisk with a red outline so it should be easy to spot from some distance. Possible locations: ![Twark Pylon, in random location](images/Pylon.jpg)
   - {{waypoint 209, -186, -681}}
   - {{waypoint 265, -173, -750}}
   - {{waypoint 103, -169, -712}}
   - {{waypoint 103.24, -184.22, -641.12}}
   - {{waypoint 156.02, -165.25, -770.29}}
   - {{waypoint 75.61, -160.17, -781.78}} *Note: Be VERY careful if the pylon is at this location.[[Glarsh]] spawns there as well. He hits very hard!
1. Repair the damaged Obfuscation Pylon {{waypoint 707, -155, -669}}.
1. Apply the conductor stone you got earlier to the now reconstructed obfuscation pylon.
1. Return to Runekeeper Talisyn {{waypoint -200, 228, -239}}.
