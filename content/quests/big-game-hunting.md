## Steps

1. Get the following quest updates by slaying creatures, all around level 14-15:
   - Skull from [[A fierce carrion hound]] (level 17-19) - found only near druid ring {{waypoint 544, -46, 65}}
   - Tusk from [[A plains elephant]] (level 16-18) - found south of Pride Lake {{waypoint 434, -47, -315}} or Dog Pond {{waypoint -1000, 0, -200}}
   - Horn from [[A rhinoceros]] (level 15-17) - found near east side of Pride Lake {{waypoint 382, -45, -516}}
   - Stinger from [[A digger wasp]] (level 11-12) - found on hill a short distance north east of the Crossroads {{waypoint -693, -36, -267}}
   - Mining Pick from [[A Bloodskull mason]] (level 14-15) - found at orc towers {{waypoint -451, -48, 413}}
1. Return to J.P. Feterman.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 91c
- One of the following:
  - [[Windstalkers Trinket of Solitude]]
  - [[Rotting Gnoll Tail]]
  - [[Orb of Seeing]]
  - [[Totem of Strength]]
