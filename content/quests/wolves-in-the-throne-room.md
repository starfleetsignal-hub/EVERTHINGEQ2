## Steps

1. Obtain 30 claws from Lyrech werewolves, found in [[The Wolf's Maw]] during the night. {{waypoint 27, 58, 144}}
1. Return to [[Tavish Dracinov]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Orcish Greathammer of Peaceful Rest]]
  - [[Symbol of the Twisted Plains]]
  - [[Aegis of the Twisted Plains]]
  - [[Windcaller's Crescent Blade]]
  - [[Runic Staff of the Dark]]
  - [[Effigy of the Twisted Plains]]
