## Notes

- [[Dog Day Reconnaissance]] from [[Waynil Garwater]] takes you to the same area.

## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Obtain 4 fresh coyote flanks, by hunting coyotes
     - [[Feral coyote]]s, [[Mountain coyote]]s, etc...
   - Obtain 4 fresh mountain lion meats, by hunting [[Mountain lion]]s
   - Obtain 4 wild radishes, by harvesting the steamfont wild radishes under various trees in the area around the crossroads with [[Watchman Plarg]] {{waypoint -600, 100, 750}}.  They are slightly smaller than the 'natural nettle' shrubs.
1. Return to [[Lola Punwicket]]
