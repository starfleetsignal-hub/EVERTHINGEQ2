## Prerequisites

You must speak Thulian to talk to the Keeper of the Blade.

## Steps

1. Examine the [[Ankh necklace]] you received and talk to [[Sir Trodonis]] again.
1. Go to the [[Village of Somborn]] in [[Loping Plains]] and examine the tombstone. {{waypoint -400, 18, 85}}
1. Speak with [[Horatio Mercuria]] in the Village of Somborn. {{waypoint -328, 14, -18}}
1. Speak with [[Ardan Zimel]] behind [[Ravenscar Mausoleum]]. {{waypoint 800, 84, 75}}
1. Speak with [[Basil Askerville]] {{waypoint 210, 65, -167}} behind where the [[Pumpkin Headed Horseman]] roams, but not inside [[The Wolf's Maw]].
   - Basil Askerville only spawns during in-game night. If it is nighttime, and he is not there when you arrive, kill the werewolves at the camp and he should spawn with a second wave of werewolves. (*He spawned for me during the day after I killed a wave of werewolves.*)
1. Go to [[Arkelek's Gash]] and find the three blades. These appear as swords on the ground. After you examine them, kill the [[Blade of Aeristrom]] that spawns. Defeat them in the following order:
   1. {{waypoint -173, 4, 335}}
   1. {{waypoint -301, -4, 339}}
   1. {{waypoint -333, 1, 463}}
1. Return to Basil Askerville.
1. Speak with Ardan Zimel on the steps of [[Castle Mistmoore]]. He will spawn as you near the location. {{waypoint 457, 133, -10}}.
   - After you finish his dialogue, you will be attacked by several [[A Somborn villager|villagers of Somborn]]. You do not need to kill them.
1. Go to [[Mistmoore Catacombs]], climb the [[The Ascent]] to the second floor (vampire floor), and click on the chest at {{waypoint -74, -31, 28}}.
1. Go to [[Kaladim]], and examine a pile of books on the second floor of a small building. {{waypoint -12, 33, -279}}
1. Obtain the following items to make the duplicate keys:
   - 12 *vibrellium ores*: Kill [[A Grungetalon miner|Grungetalon miners]] {{waypoint 109, 12, -346}}
   - 3 *enigma crystals*: Kill [[A Grungetalon director|Grungetalon directors]]. {{waypoint -11, 26, -248}}
     - Just kill the Director and leave the others in his group alive, run them off, spawn time will be much faster.
     - Clear the rock pile groups to spawn if not up; long spawn time, 15-20 minutes.
   - *key mold*: Defeat [[Iron Lord Kraxxaxis]] and his [[A Grungetalon foreman|Grungetalon foremans]] on the roof of the building where you inspected the book. {{waypoint -16, 43, -282}}
   - *forge hammer of Brell*: Kill the [[A Ratillik cleric|Ratillik clerics]] and [[A Ratillik high priest|Ratillik high priest]] {{waypoint 58, 30, -398}}
   - *forge hammer of Bolgin*: Kill the Ratillik clerics and Ratillik high priest {{waypoint 58, 30, -466}}
   - *forge hammer of Syrill*: Kill the Ratillik clerics and Ratillik high priest {{waypoint 45, 30, -516}}
1. Construct the duplicate keys at the anvil on the first floor of the building where you inspected the book. {{waypoint -16, 27, -279}}
1. Polish the keys at the grinding stone on the second floor. {{waypoint -11, 34, -284}}
1. Return to the chest on the top floor of the Mistmoore Catacombs. {{waypoint -74, -31, 28}}
1. Speak with the [[Keeper of the Blade]] and answer the riddles within 30-minutes. If you fail to answer a riddle correctly, an [[Armor of Mistmyr]] will aggro and attack. Defeating it will be treated as if you had answered the riddle correctly, you will still get a quest update and can move to the next riddle. The riddles are given in [[Thulian]]. If you or your party members do not speak Thulian, then your only option is to fight the Armors of Mistmyr. (The option to fight the armors if you do not speak Thulian is no longer present.)   The correct answers are:
   1. Another Riddle
   1. Ariska Zimel
   1. The Duchy of the Dead
   1. Sir Lucan D'Lere
1. Examine [[A sword hilt|a sword hilt]] from your inventory.
1. Return to [[Sir Trodonis]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] and wait for him to conduct his research (~5 minutes). {{waypoint 866, 44, -280}}
