## Steps

1. Nab [[A human child]] from the [[New Foreign Quarter]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]] at  {{waypoint 151, 21, 88}} by double-clicking one. Note: Only the child named Riley Aprilia seems to work for this quest.
1. Return to [[Exporter Makarios]].
1. He will give you the next quest in line, [[Citizenship Registrar]].
