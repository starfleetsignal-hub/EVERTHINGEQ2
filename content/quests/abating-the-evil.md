## Description

Following the betrayal of Gynok Moltor a horrible curse beset to former keep of the Knights of Marr's Fist. This curse has not only caused the murdered knights to rise from the dead but also attracted several other malevolent creatures. The evil that permeates the Cavern of the Afflicted must be abated! To accomplish this task I've been asked to eliminate one of the most dangerous threats that lurk in the Caverns. I had best bring a group of my most accomplished allies to ensure victory!

## Steps

1. Smite [[The Bonegrinder|the Bonegrinder]] in [[Befallen: Cavern of the Afflicted]]. This is the endmob.
