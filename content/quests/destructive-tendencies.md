## Steps

(Must be 40+ to obtain this quest.)

1. See if anyone will speak about the experiments
   1. Go to [[Temple Street: Vault of Necromechanica]] {{waypoint -176, -37, 325}}
   1. use the [[Temple Street Gnome Illusion]]
   1. speak with [[Glimzig Clangbottom]] at {{waypoint 22, 3, 58}}
1. use [[Glimzig's Zapstick]] on 6 clockworks and gather the deactivated necro clockwork (rightclick to gather)
1. speak with [[Glimzig Clangbottom]] at {{waypoint 22, 3, 58}}
1. Speak with [[Analyst Dimir]]
1. click on the machine outside at {{waypoint 52, 3, 98}}
1. click on the book on the table where [[Analyst Dimir]] was
1. Speak with [[Foci Tamara Paust]]

## Rewards

- [[A Standard of the Overlord]]
- 1000 faction with **The Academy of Arcane Science**
