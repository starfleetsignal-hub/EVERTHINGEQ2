## Steps

1. Go to [[Gobblerock's Hideout]].
1. Reclaim 15 pieces of stolen supplies from Gobblerock clan goblins (any goblin will do).
1. Collect 4 skins of brambleberry ale from [[A Gobblerock hooligan|Gobblerock hooligans]].
1. Collect the stolen supplies' chests:
   - Collect the first stolen chest of supplies {{waypoint -14, 1, 47.5}}.
   - Collect the second stolen chest of supplies {{waypoint -65, 2, 10}}.
   - Collect the third stolen chest of supplies {{waypoint -51, 2, -54}}.
1. Return all the stolen supplies to Master Jaedra.

## Rewards

- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Nektropos Diviner's Mantle]] (Priest)
  - [[Nektropos Magician's Mantle]] (Mage)
  - [[Nektropos Stalker's Mantle]] (Scout)
  - [[Nektropos Warrior's Mantle]] (Fighter)
