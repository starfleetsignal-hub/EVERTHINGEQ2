## Steps

1. Heal 6 x [[A wounded local]] with [[Spiritshine's Healing Dram]] (which you receive when accepting quest). There's several [[A wounded local|a wounded locals]] on dock and around [[Port Woe]]. Target them and use [[Spiritshine's Healing Dram]] from your inventory or quest helper window.
1. Work with Port Woe locals to earn their trust.
   - complete quest [[Fighting the Biters Back]] from [[Kober Botley]] at {{waypoint -91, 9, 340}} and speak with with him again you finished the quest [[Fighting the Biters Back]].
1. Return to [[Adris Spiritshine (Port Woe)|Adris Spiritshine]].
1. Keep working with the Port Woe locals
   - Work with more Port Woe locals
     - Complete [[Leave the Thievin' to Professionals]] from [[Gragir Sharpe]] at the pavilion.
   - Work with another Port Woe local
     - Complete the [[Shelled Sustenance]] and [[Case of the Pinched Pickling]] from [[Tristyl Ashwood]] in the trades building.
   - Work more with Kober Botley, the dock keeper
     - Complete the first quests [[Leave the Thievin' to Professionals]] and [[Case of the Pinched Pickling]] to start [[Takin' From the Forsaken]] questline from [[Kober Botley]]
1. Return to [[Adris Spiritshine (Port Woe)|Adris Spiritshine]]
1. Gather 6 of Tristyl's new disguised pickle jars around the Elder hut, the pavilion, trades building, and the dock.  ![A typical Pickle Jar](images/Scars_of_Destruction_Friend_or_Woe_quest_-_Pickle_Jar.jpg)
1. Deposit the disguised pickle jars amongst the kappa huts and Hodan Basho. {{waypoint 189, 4.53, 456}}
   - Similar locations to the first pickle jars and stolen goods.
   - Possible locations include
     - {{waypoint 221.65, 4.49, 471.66}}, {{waypoint 244.53, 4.90, 598.73}}, {{waypoint 513, 5, 691}}
     - {{waypoint 246.33, 4.63, 624.49}}, {{waypoint 244.30, 4.83, 643.76}}, {{waypoint 457, 5, 718}}
     - {{waypoint 237, 6, 683}}, {{waypoint 193, 4, 454}}, {{waypoint 501, 5, 674}}
1. Return to Adris Spiritshine.
