## Steps

1. Talk to Snaps and see if the eels you collected are edible.  Turns out they aren't.
1. Speak to the othmir fishing lead, [[Banik]], at {{waypoint -1407, -542, -2077}}
1. Return to [[Snaps]]

- At least 1p 15g
