## Steps

![](images/Wisp_Spirit.jpg)

1. Collect a deer carcass. {{waypoint 317.80, -27.19, -69.57}}
1. Kill a wolf. {{waypoint 293, -22, -25}}
1. Release the wisp item in inventory or click the icon on your quest helper, near {{waypoint 283, -57, -204}}
   - The wisp is in your inventory.
   - *Note*: You need to be in the area near [[Direpaw The Mangler]] or you will get this  message: "You must be near Dire Wolf Dens east of the Shadow Oak to use this". If you are at the above coords, and get this message, move around and keep trying.
1. Return to [[Nemain]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s 67c
