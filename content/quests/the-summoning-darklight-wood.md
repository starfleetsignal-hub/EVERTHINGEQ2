## Steps

1. Summon the tainted Sableflame avatar on the roof at the [[Sableflame Crater]], using the [[Staff of the Flamecallers]]. You can stick around to watch the [[Avatar of Sableflame]] lay waste to the Sableflame cultists.
1. Return to Belarshalee.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
- [[Lucent Gem of Prowess]]
- [[Sableflame Emblem]]
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Archon's Bracers]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Bracers]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Bracers]] (Druids)
  - [[Elder's Bracers]] (Shamans)
  - [[Enlightened Bracers]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Nebulous Bracers]] (not to be confused with [[Knight's Bracers]]) (Crusaders & Warriors)
  - [[Shade's Bracers]] (Assassin, Bard, Beastlord, Rogue)
  - [[Shade's Nebulous Bracers]] (Rangers, Swashbucklers)
