## Steps

1. Examine giant bones and "rescue" othmir:
   - a corrupted bone {{waypoint 4297, -1079, 1941}}
   - a blackened bone {{waypoint 4272, -1086, 1896}}
   - an oozing bone {{waypoint 4211, -1078, 1994}}
   - a cracked bone {{waypoint 4308, -1087, 1999}}
   - Kill 5 [[An othmir survivor|othmir survivor]]s, found all around [[Forsaken Othmir Village]] (they will become aggro and attack when approached)
1. Speak to [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)|Osh]]

- At least 1p 30g
- One of the following:
  - [[Aquatic Staff of Energy]]
  - [[Augur's Lance]]
  - [[Combine Soldier's Claymore]]
  - [[Snapper Gnawed Anchor]]
