## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Sumac Mallet for my apprentice.
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Sumac Mallet]]
   1. Craft [[A Sturdy Sumac Mallet]] on a [[Woodworking Table]] using the following materials and fuel:
      - 2 [[Sumac lumber]]
      - 4 [[Ethereal sandpaper]]
1. I need to take this basket back to my apprentice.
