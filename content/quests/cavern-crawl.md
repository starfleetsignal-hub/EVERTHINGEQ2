## Description

Predators of all shapes and sizes live throughout the Stygian Threshold. The citizens of Maldura would like me to venture into the cavern and turn these predators into prey!

## Zone

[[Stygian Threshold (Heroic)]]

## Steps

- Defeat the warden of the forest
  - [[Metamorphous]]
- Kill the harbinger of war
  - [[Grozgar]]
- Kill the river terror
  - [[The Ink Walker]]
- Slay the brood tender
  - [[Heracyne]]
- Dispatch the regents of dawn and dusk
  - [[Diurna]] & [[Nocturna]]
- Slay the forest ancient
  - [[Timbergrod]]
- Kill the spider queen
  - [[Zaraxia]]
- Slay the exile
  - [[Mawz Harak]]

- At least 2p 26g 80s 41,600 status
