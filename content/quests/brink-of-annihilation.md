## Steps

1. Find and speak with [[Geflak]] within the hills of [[Haoaera]]. {{waypoint 1886, 168, -477}}
1. Kill [[Varkrys Doomfeather]] and the [[A Haoaeran fanatic|Haoaeran fanatics]] that spawn. Loot [[The Archtome of Haoaera]] that drops in the chest.
1. Return to [[Makrug Tel'Kor]].
