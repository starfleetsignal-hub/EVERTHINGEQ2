## Steps

1. Venture once more to the cave east of [[The Eye of Dartain]] and climb the ladders within. {{waypoint 1348.68, 1.00, 3967.66}}
1. Once you reach the top, [[A Rumbling Broodmother]] will spawn and attack you.
1. Return to Saihah after defeating the Broodmother.
