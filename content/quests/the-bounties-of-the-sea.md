## Steps

1. Kill 3 [[A giant spearfish|giant spearfish]]. {{waypoint 564, -5, -844}} {{waypoint 712, -5, -246}}
1. Kill evil eyes, such as the [[A void watcher|void watchers]], until you receive six tentacles. {{waypoint -13, 32, -1017}}
1. Kill nightbloods until you receive a suitable horn (semi-common; any nightblood will update regardless of zone). The closest ones are the ones in the Enchanted Lands' [[Misty Grove]], around {{waypoint -661, 2, -893}}.
1. Go to [[The Fae Isle]] in [[Enchanted Lands]] {{waypoint -656, -1, 48}} and place the trident in water.
