## Notes

Flying is necessary to reach most of the nest locations.

## Steps

1. Gather and hunt the following
   - Gather 8 **corpsebeak eggs** around [[Scorched Shores]]. Some possible locations include: ![](images/Manta_Steak_and_Eggs_quest_-_Corpsebeak_Eggs.png)
     - {{waypoint 529.43, -10.85, 205.58}}
     - {{waypoint 575.98, -17.35, 422.65}}
     - {{waypoint 575.19, -17.40, 412.08}}
     - {{waypoint 554.52, -11.92, 437.42}}
     - {{waypoint 545.77, -11.74, 428.86}}
     - {{waypoint 592.65, -29.97, 407.36}}
     - {{waypoint 526.84, -28.07, 325.56}}
     - {{waypoint 560.30, -11.09, 227.09}}
     - {{waypoint 528.03, -11.11, 205.79}}
     - {{waypoint 554.97, -11.76, 168.20}}
     - {{waypoint 564.55, -23.76, 148.89}}
     - {{waypoint 585.67, -44.78, 166.66}}
   - Hunt 12 [[A bluefin manta|bluefin mantas]] {{waypoint 643.14, -62.87, 533.97}} for meat.
1. Return to [[Karim]].
