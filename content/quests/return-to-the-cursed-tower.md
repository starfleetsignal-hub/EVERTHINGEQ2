## Prerequisites

- You need at least +40,000 natural faction with The Order of Rime to obtain this quest.
- You must have gained access to [[Kurn's Tower (Zone)|Kurn's Tower]] by completing the quest, [[Dragonbone Weapon Parts]], part of the [[Fens of Nathsar Timeline#Kurn's Tower|Kurn's Tower Access Questline]].

## Steps

1. Enter [[Kurn's Tower (Zone)|Kurn's Tower (Raid)]] in the [[Fens of Nathsar]]. {{waypoint -1824, -384, -1833}}
1. Defeat [[Ione the Lifebringer (Named)|Ione the Lifebringer]] to retrieve the golden orb.
1. Give the orb to [[Kraytoc Killingfrost (Fens of Nathsar)|Kraytoc Killingfrost]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p
- +5000 faction with **The Order of Rime**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Lingwal's Mystical Ring]]
  - [[Tert's Powerful Ring]]
