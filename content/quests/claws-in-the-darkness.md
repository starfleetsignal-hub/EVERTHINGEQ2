## Steps

1. Slay the following monsters in the [[Mistmoore Catacombs]]:
   - 4 [[A Myr'Dal ravager|Myr'Dal Ravagers]]
   - 4 [[A Myr'Dal savager|Myr'Dal Savagers]]
   - 4 [[A Lykulak shredder|Lykulak Shredders]]
   - 4 [[A Lykulak gnasher|Lykulak Gnashers]]
1. Return to [[Moldosk the Bat]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Bow of Prevailing]]
  - [[Dream Stealing Bow of the Heavens]]
  - [[Focus of the Full Moon]] (All Mages)
  - [[Heat-Imbued Focus Wand]] (All Priests)
  - [[Remnant of the Awakened]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
