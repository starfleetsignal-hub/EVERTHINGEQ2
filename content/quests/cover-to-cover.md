## Steps

1. Obtain the following
   - Obtain 8 beetle crab chitin from Tellurav Marshlands {{waypoint 815, 58, 151}}
   - Obtain 10 **beetle crab larvae**  ![](images/Cover_to_Cover_quest_-_a_beetle_crab_larva.jpg)
1. Return to [[Skirha]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
- One of the following:
  - [[Hooluk Ring of Spirit]]
  - [[Hooluk Buckler of Pride]]
  - [[Hooluk Tome of Ammolite]]
  - [[Hooluk Tower Shield of Pride]]
  - [[Aviak Wand of Pride]]
