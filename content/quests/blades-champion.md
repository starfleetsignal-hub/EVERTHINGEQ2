## Prerequisites

- You must have 50,000 faction with the Court of the Blades to unlock this quest.

## Steps

1. Kill fifteen [[A Coin guard|Coin guard]]s.
1. Kill fifteen [[A Truth guard|Truth guard]]s.
1. Enter [[Blades' Arena]] at {{waypoint -109, 177, -37}} and speak to [[Fight Master Shaarka]].
1. Ten rounds of arena combat follow. The mobs will come in waves ranging from a single mob to four in a group, but typically three at a time. After enough have died the quest will update.
1. Return to Alim Nimr.

## Notes

There is approximately 2 minutes between each round of combat. If you have not killed all of the combatants within that time period, the next round will continue to spawn.

Although the many of the combatants are heroic, they do not pose a significant challenge. If you play on a standard ruleset server, you can bring a mercenary with you if necessary. If you play on a Time-Locked Expansion server, you can complete the quest with a group.
