## Steps

1. Examine the four 'A Maiden of Theer' statues on the second floor, in [[The Circle of the Maidens]] at {{waypoint -6, 28, -29}}.
1. Inspect each of the four statues a second time, receiving a subquest from each one. These quests can be all done at the same time and require killing various amounts of aviaks on the first and second floors:
   - [[An Appetite for Palace Knights]]
   - [[An Appetite for Blacktalon Coadjutants]]
   - [[An Appetite for Blacktalon Partisans]]
   - [[An Appetite for Blacktalon Initiates]]
1. Stand in the middle of the circle below the wall leading up to the third floor. {{waypoint -21, 27, 0}}
1. Within 6 minutes, kill the four [[An armor of theer|armors of theer]] (66^ Heroic) that have spawned in the room.

## Reward

- [[Dissassembled Star Key]] (examine for next step of the quest)
- Your choice of:
  - [[Dragonscale Earrings]]
  - [[Dragonscale Ring]]
