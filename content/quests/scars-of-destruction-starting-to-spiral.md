## Notes

- [[Shadow of the Kobold]] can be done in the same Version of [[Blackhook Spiral: Stonebrunt Defile (Solo)|Blackhook Spiral: Stonebrunt Defile [Solo]]] while on this quest
- [[The Kaborite Stuff]] can be done in the same Version of [[Blackhook Spiral: Fort Sunder (Solo)|Blackhook Spiral: Fort Sunder [Solo]]] while on this quest

## Steps

1. Enter [[Blackhook Spiral: Stonebrunt Defile (Solo)|Blackhook Spiral: Stonebrunt Defile [Solo]]] {{waypoint 215, 286, -794}}
1. Progress through the zone.
1. Kill [[Shraks the Axe]] at {{waypoint 320, 238, 151}}
1. Proceed to the Northern Gates of Wrath at {{waypoint 444, 239, 149}}
1. Open the gate by clicking the lever to the right at {{waypoint 442.85, 240.26, 143.61}}
1. Proceed to kill [[Grawl Bakka]] (Kolbok Chieftan)  at {{waypoint 468.75, 246.07, 347.48}}, has knockback. ( Survive the threats of Sodden Sanctuary)
1. Proceed to kill [[Frenek the Far Lancer]] by crossing the bridge and entering a swirling mist that moves around.  One location is {{waypoint 545, 246, 215}}
   1. NOTE: The *swirling mist* is Frenek's targeting circle.  He is a lookout and lives in the tall guard tower.  When you enter his targeting circle, he will see you, tell you so, and teleport you to him.  This tower's climbing rungs are clickable (*"climb.")*  Clicking the rungs will instantly get you back to the top where Frenek is.  Frenek will throw you off the tower repeatedly and you will need to climb back up.
   1. NOTE: Kill the mobs at the base of the tower before you climb up because they will swarm you when Frenek throws you off the tower.
   1. He casts stifle.  He will throw you off the tower (port you away) several times.
1. Proceed to kill [[Akamizu , Demon of the Lake]] at  {{waypoint 534.64, 209.07, 36.66}} (Still buggy)
   - Go downhill and harvest a local veggies bush to obtain cucumbers from the plants nearby.  This will get her to pop.
   - Engage her behind the waterfall where she is standing on dry land.
   - She will soon run to the water.  Follow her out and go stand on the shore beside some local veggies plants.
   - Each time she moves to the water, harvest a local veggies for a cucumber then hold your cursor over her DON'T FOLLOW HER INTO THE WATER.  It changes to a hand and you can click on her which will cause her to leave the water so she can be damaged.
1. Proceed to kill [[Orok the Overbearing]] at {{waypoint 324, 189, -226}}
   1. NOTE: Kill the Orcs guarding the surrounding goblins. When you kill the orcs guarding the three goblin groups, they will assist you in the fight against Orok by warding you from Stun, Fear, and Stifle respectively.  After the fight, you can stay around for a Goblin Celebration if you like.
   1. Pay attention which goblin group around him helps with stun, fear and stifle, run to them to help make you immune when Orok uses one of those attacks. (after you kill bouncers around them they become: energized goblins = stun , boisterous goblins = stifle, brave goblins = fear)
   - *Note: While you are in this quest! Do not use the portal that spawns right there after killing Orok. Proceed to next step and use portal further in the zone.*
1. Venture deeper to The Gates of Fort Sunder {{waypoint 352, 177, -387}} Inspect the gate.
1. Zone out and return to [[Adris Spiritshine]]  in Sodden Archipelago at  {{waypoint 290, 283, -735}}.
1. Acquire the [[Kaborite Key]] needed to enter Fort Sunder
   - Complete the [[Skortch and Sniff]] questline from Skortch
1. Speak to [[Adris Spiritshine]] and with [[Skortch]] to obtain the [[Kaborite Key]].
1. Enter [[Blackhook Spiral: Fort Sunder (Solo)|Blackhook Spiral: Fort Sunder [Solo]]]
1. Clear trash mobs until a purple message appears and gate switches can be clicked at ( 310, 178, -448 ).
1. Continue to clear mobs until the next gate can be opened.
1. Click the gate switch at  {{waypoint 312, 177, -390}}
1. Kill [[Argh the Anvil]] at {{waypoint 303, 179, -345}} Don't forget to collect your shiney.
1. Proceed to the Great lift and throw the lever at {{waypoint 272, 179, -437}} to lower the lift, get on and then raise the lift.
1. Kill [[Igarn the Insurmountable]] and [[Winx the Unmountable]] who patrol the path around {{waypoint 133, 225, -365}}
1. Kill [[Zeraxyl]] at {{waypoint 53, 211, -489}} and collect your shiney.
1. Ride the mount at {{waypoint 52, 211, -489}} to the Isle of the Forgotten.
1. Kill [[Tajara the Vengeful]]
1. Ride the mount at {{waypoint -369, 320, -652}} to Harmok HQ.
1. Kill [[Harmok the Hammer]]  {{waypoint -21, 182, -421}}
   - Has a very powerful knock back,  and adds. Hiding in the forge helps with knockback, but adds additional damage. There's a small nook under one of the containers full of purple items.
1. Return to Adris Spiritshine
