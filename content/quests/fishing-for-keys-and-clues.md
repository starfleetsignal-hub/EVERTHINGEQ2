## Notes

- The name of this quest is not official. It will not appear in your quest journal.
- You can repeat this multiple times and get multiple house items.

## Steps

![The box below Sir Filian Weagleross's crypt](images/Fishing_for_Keys_and_Clues_location.jpg)

1. Examine a [[Koalindl]] fish in your inventory while in [[The Pool of Jahnda]].
1. Release the fish back into the pool. [[A koalindl scale key]] appears in your inventory.
1. Enter [[The Down Below]] and search for a locked box {{waypoint -205, 0, -25}}
1. Examine the box to receive your rewards.

## Rewards

- [[Seeress Ealaynya Ithis's Prophecies: Book Three]]
- [[Temple of Life (House Item)|Temple of Life statue]].
