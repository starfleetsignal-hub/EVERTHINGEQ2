## aaDescription

"A fairy by the name of Lexxiuna came to the Spire Shadow Outpost seeking the bravest and most skilled to answer a summons by Princess Zhyleen. There must be some trouble brewing in Tunare's Glade. there is no path leading to the glade, but its entrance is directly west of the Combine Spires. That is where I will find Princess Zhyleen." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Princess Zhyleen]] in [[Tunare's Glade]] at {{waypoint -128, 81, 566}}

## Rewards

- At least 4s 82c
- [[Lucent Gem of Fortitude]]
