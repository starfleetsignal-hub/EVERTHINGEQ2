## Steps

1. Cave entrances are located at {{waypoint 1445, -234, -722}} and {{waypoint 1382, -230, -834}}.
1. Click on the tomb {{waypoint 1272, -229, -768}} to start the ring event.
   - Kill [[An undying Sword of Ro]] and 2x [[An undying Abbot of Ro|undying Abbots of Ro]].
   - Kill [[An undying Sword of Ro]] and 2x [[An undying Abbot of Ro|undying Abbots of Ro]] again.
   - Kill the [[Eternal Knight of Ro]].
   - Kill the [[Champion of Solusek]].
     - The Champion of Solusek is assisted by 2x [[An undying Knight of Ro|undying Knights of Ro]] and 2x [[Champion of Solusek's grave sacrament|Champion of Solusek's grave sacraments]].
     - You do not have to kill the adds in the final wave to complete the quest, just the Champion of Solusek.
1. **Optional**: Hail [[Siraj al Din]] again after completion of this quest to receive experience and +4000 faction with the Char'Gin. If you turned in the previous quest to [[Afaf Rabi'ah]], then speak to him after completing this quest and he will be the NPC that rewards you with the extra faction.
