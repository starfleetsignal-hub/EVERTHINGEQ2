## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- You must have +20,000 faction with the Court of the Coin to unlock this quest. Once +50,000 faction has been achieved, however, this quest will no longer be available.
- The crystals have a short reuse timer (~5 min) before they can be clicked again.

## Steps

- Assay (click) 50 blue crystals from the lower regions of the zone.
- Assay (click) 5 red crystals from the lower regions of the zone. These are found deeper in the first level tunnels. {{waypoint -198, -10, 40}} {{waypoint -142, -13, -204}} {{waypoint -94, -21, -282}}

## Rewards

- Coin:  3g 23s 29c
- -500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- +500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
