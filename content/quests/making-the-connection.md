## Steps

1. Obtain the following from [[Veeshenna Tol]] (near {{waypoint -101, 51, 730}} )
   - 8 serene soulwave gossamers by killing [[A serene soulwave|serene soulwaves]] (ground)
   - 8 purest rhythmical soulflare scintillas by killing [[A rhythmical soulflare|rhythmical soulflares]] (flying)
1. Meet Tavoken at the Dragon Ring {{waypoint -105, 213, 368}}
1. Tell Tavoken when I am prepared for him to begin spellweaving
1. Defeat all the monsters that emerge from the portal
   - After some trash monsters ([[Dark seepage (Vesspyr Isles)|dark seepage]] and [[A dark horror]]), [[A shadow brute]] spawns.  Once the shadow brute dies, the quest updates.
   - *This may be slightly bugged, requiring you to speak to Tavokan again and kill another [[A shadow brute|shadow brute]] before the quest updates.*
1. Speak to Tavoken

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Vesspyr Isles Whisper Column]]
- The Dragon Ring on Daarspire now functions
