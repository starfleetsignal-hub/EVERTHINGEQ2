## Steps

1. Speak to General Rost Fellhammer in Thurgadin. {{waypoint 952, -303, 313}}
1. Speak to Archeologist Ferni in Great Divide. {{waypoint 104.33, -345.66, -599.40}}
1. Help the coldain ghosts in the Great Divide battlefield. (Participate in a Ring War Public Quest)
1. Return to Archeologist Ferni in Great divide. {{waypoint 104.33, -345.66, -599.40}}
1. Search the southern archeological camp in the Great divide. Speak to Rallin {{waypoint -790.23, -370.71, -231.28}}
1. Escort him back to the Great War Battlefield camp.
1. Speak to Rallin.
1. Speak to General Rost Fellhammer in the Great Divide. {{waypoint 482.90, 200.91, 63.64}}
1. Defeat the Kromzek Attack. 3 Giants will spawn, at level 92.
1. Return to General Rost Fellhammer {{waypoint 484, -200, -63}} in the Great Divide.
1. Speak with the Duality in Thurgadin (within the Temple of Brell) to finish the quest. {{waypoint 478.11, -212.12, 248.05}}

- At least 53g 36s 24c
