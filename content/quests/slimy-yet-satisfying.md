## Steps

1. Gather the following
   - Find 6 black skycrawlers in [[Burrower's Timber]] around {{waypoint 428, 71, 279}} (click on fallen timber to spawn black sky crawlers)
     - *Not a guaranteed update.*  ![](images/Slimy_Yet_Satisfying_quest_-_Fallen_Timber.jpg)
     - Possible locations {{waypoint 623, 108, 433}}, {{waypoint 627, 107, 466}},  {{waypoint 618, 102, 490}},  {{waypoint 614, 97, 531}}, {{waypoint 547, 85, 511}}
   - Gather 8 **lemon slugs** for meat (found near the Auduborians) around {{waypoint 552, 842, 243}}

![](images/Slimy_Yet_Satisfying_quest_-_a_lemon_slug.jpg)

1. Return to [[Kudurri]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Vultak Hauberk of Malevolence]]
  - [[Vultak Robe of Dichroic]]
  - [[Vultak Breastplate of Trickery]]
  - [[Hooluk Cuirass of Trickery]]
