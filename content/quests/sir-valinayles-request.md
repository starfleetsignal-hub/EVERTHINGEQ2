## Steps

1. Find Sir Valinayle's body. This is on the third level of Stormhold in the corner of a room at {{waypoint 69, -46, -147}}. Clicking his body spawns four level 27^ zombies.
1. Kill 50 undead in Stormhold.
1. Kill [[Lord Gydak (Named)|Lord Gydak]] in the Level IV caves at {{waypoint 97, -58, -65}}.
1. Return to Sir Valinayle, in the Level I entrance corridor, for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 96s 67c
- [[Stormfire]]
