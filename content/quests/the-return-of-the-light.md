## Description

You have discovered a peculiar *lightstone*, which are commonly found on the corpses of wisps. This one is emanating with a strange light, however. Could it be somehow related to the *Knights of Truth* that used to inhabit this region?

## Prerequisites

- This quest can be started and even completed at level 15.
- Lightbringer Wisp spawns north-west of the druid rings around the few trees there. {{waypoint 806, -60, -238}}
  - The spawn timer is 30 minutes on Origins server

## Steps

*I found a strange burned out lightstone on the remains of a wisp that I defeated out in the Commonlands. The stone is very different from the other lightstones I've seen in these parts. Maybe I can find someone who thinks it is valuable and get some coin for it.*

1. Speak to [[Cannix Silverflame]], found immediately west of [[The Crossroads]] in The Commonlands. {{waypoint -313, -47, -360}}
1. Find and touch the lightstone to:
   - [[The Forgotten (POI)|The Forgotten]] {{waypoint 172, -45, 260}}
   - [[The Mourned]] {{waypoint -590, -48, -629}}
1. Speak to Cannix for your first reward, [[Faintly Lit Lightstone]].
1. Kill [[Rama'nai]], a static spawn grouped with 2 savanna lionesses at {{waypoint 877, -59, 7}} in [[The Commonlands]].
1. Speak to Cannix for your second reward, the [[Dimly Lit Lightstone]].
1. Kill two orc captains:
   - [[Captain Gaer]] at {{waypoint -855, -48, 668}} Has Placeholder, Reag. Respawn 7 minutes.
   - [[Captain Ogof]] at {{waypoint -645, -48, 461}} or {{waypoint -477.39, -47.51, 673.25}} Has placeholder, Fogo. Respawn 7 minutes.
   - Note:  This can take **multiple** respawns,
1. Speak to Cannix for your third reward, the [[Softly Glowing Lightstone]].
1. Kill the [[High Priest of Val'Marr]] at {{waypoint 381.7, -47.4, 229.5}}. Diplomats at his tower are PHs. Respawn ~7-8 minutes. Rare chance of spawning named.
1. Speak to Cannix for your fourth reward, the [[Glimmering Lightstone]].
1. Kill [[General Drull]] He can be found in the heroic instance, [[Bloodskull Valley: Maulic's Stronghold]].
   - The entrance is at {{waypoint -4, -48, 729}}
   - In order to enter [[Bloodskull Valley: Maulic's Stronghold]], all group or raid members must talk to [[Amulius Ramio]] at {{waypoint 24, -46, 725}}. He is crouching behind a big rock just outside the [[Bloodskull Valley]] entrance. Hail him until he goes through his entire dialog. This may entail talking to him two times or more, depending on the conversational choices you make.
   - Drull is in the fourth wave of a horde of level 22 and 22v, they come in large groups of 5-10 it seems. More will aggro after a specific time. If you die, it does not take you out of the instance, keep hacking away until the General shows up. His death is the only step needed - completing the entire instance is not required.
   - **Important note**: This zone is now persistent with a minimum duration of 2 days 20 hours, and a maximum duration of 7 days. The status of the zone can be checked by pressing Alt+Z. If you leave before killing General Drull you will need to speak to Amulius Rami to regain entrance.
1. Speak to Cannix. A group of one [[Lucanic Knight Initiate]] and two [[A Militia guard]] will spawn, kill Cannix, and then (if they're not gray to you) attack you. You must kill them to complete the quest even if they're gray.
   - *Note*: You must wait for the soldiers to kill Cannix first. Cannix is no longer attackable and the Lucanic Knight and his guards are non-aggro. Just step back and let them kill Cannix, then kill them to complete the quest. If you kill the knight and guards first, just talk to Cannix to get them to re-spawn.
