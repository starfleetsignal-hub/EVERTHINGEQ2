## Steps

1. Speak to [[Kora N'Vaxin]] at {{waypoint 12, 3, 51}}.
1. Find [[Zarual X'Tazinder]] at {{waypoint 17, 1, 12}}.  After you defeat Zarual, he will drop a small chest with [[Completed Potion of Hate's Identic]]
1. Use the [[Completed Potion of Hate's Identic|potion of Hate's Identic]].
1. Plant scrying devices around the plaza where the Thexian is speaking.  The locations are all on stacks of crates and are marked with green shinies.
   1. {{waypoint -8, 1, -20}}
   1. {{waypoint -19, 0, -10}}
   1. {{waypoint -23, 2, 7}}
   1. {{waypoint -10, 2, 22}}
1. Plant the last scrying stone near the speaker at {{waypoint 5, 2, 0}}.
1. At this point, the [[Thexian Guard|Thexian Guards]] (at level vv Heroic)  become aggro.  Defeat them.
1. Return to [[Ambassador Anttel V'Xeath|Ambassador Anttel]] at {{waypoint -123, -38, 23}}

## Rewards

- At least 28g 76s 61c
