## Steps

1. Destroy and slay the following
   - Destroy 8 **snapper traps** in the waters below [[Lokh Barnacoz]] {{waypoint 11, 1, 254}}![Snapper Trap](images/Snapper_Trap.png)
     - *Note: Traps are all along the river, underwater.  The river is shallow, so you may have to zoom the camera view to see well.  Traps are light brown boxes with netting.   See the Talk page for locations.*
   - Slay 12 [[A Stormfury fishmonger|Stormfury fishmongers]] {{waypoint -21, 79, 216}}
1. Return to [[Ulgaf]].

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- One of the following:

  - [[Spellwoven Viridsilk Gloves]]
  - [[Spellwoven Florahide Gloves]]
