## Prerequisites

- You must have at least +3200 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Collect 8 *poison sacks* from  [[A drachnid widow guard|drachnid widow guards]] or [[A drachnid widow maiden|drachnid widow maidens]] located in the [[Drachnid Cave]]. {{waypoint -2360, 260, 951}}
1. Deposit the items in the barrel. {{waypoint 1020, -39, -14}}
1. Speak with [[Pylemea Stoerki]].

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- +2,400 faction with **The Order of Rime**
