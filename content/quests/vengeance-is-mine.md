## Requirements

Requires +10,000 faction with [[Nye'Caelona (Faction)|Nye'Caelona]]

## Steps

- You have 20 minutes to:
1. kill 30 [[A Vrassath bandits]]
1. kill 14 [[A snarling Vrassath chokidai]]
1. kill 1 skystrider captain, at {{waypoint 529, -107, -290}}
1. knock 20 skystriders out of the air (click on them)
1. kill 30 [[An iksar slaver]]

## Rewards

- 5 [[Encrusted metal key]]
- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +2,000 faction with **Nye'Caelona**
