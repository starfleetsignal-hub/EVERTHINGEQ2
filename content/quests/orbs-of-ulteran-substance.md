## Steps

![Orb of Ulteran Substance](images/Orb_of_Ulteran_Substance.png)

1. Enter the [[Library of Erudin]] and retrieve six orbs of Ulteran substance. (This is a clicky update)
  - Using the picture to the right to identify them, they are fairly large and quite noticeable. They are almost always found on the floor near bookshelves.
1. Return to [[Rafiqa]]

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]]
- At least 20g
