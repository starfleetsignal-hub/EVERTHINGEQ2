## Steps

1. Place the target dummy where [[Jamie Sprocketbreaker]] indicated.
   - Place the dummy at {{waypoint -644, 175, 1064}}.
1. Stand near [[Jamie Sprocketbreaker|Jamie]] and use the [[Troll Snot Flinger (testing prototype)]] on the target dummy.
1. Stand near [[Jamie Sprocketbreaker|Jamie]] and use the [[Gnarmy! (testing prototype)]] on the target dummy.
1. Stand near [[Jamie Sprocketbreaker|Jamie]] and use the [[Tinkered Turkey Launcher (testing prototype)]] on the target dummy.
1. Speak to [[Adam Cogbuster|Adam]] about the results.

Note: Make sure your pet or Mercenary are not going to assist you, as if they destroy the target dummy you will not receive quest credit; or will have to craft another dummy for the remaining weapons.

## Rewards

- At least 5g 9s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Ductile loam]]
  - [[Feysteel cluster]]
  - [[Engraved leather pelt]]
  - [[Oak root]]
  - [[Rough opal]]
  - [[Ruthenium cluster]]
  - [[Severed oak]]
- You should be able to buy T4 (30-39) advanced recipe scrolls from [[Karalyn Cinderton]] at this point.  To earn the ability to buy T5 (40-49) advanced scrolls, make your way to Everfrost and do the quest line from [[Jadrek Woolfe]].  For those with evac, [[Jadrek Woolfe]] is literally right at the point where you land when you evac in Everfrost.
