## Description

I must board The Vigilant: Incursion and kill the Tallonite horde that has overtaken the ship.

## Steps

1. You must kill a number of Tallonites on-board [[The Vigilant: Incursion|The Vigilant]].
   - Kill three [[A Tallonite amahn|Tallonite amahns]]
   - Kill three [[A Tallonite lozonn|Tallonite lozonns]]
   - Kill three [[A Tallonite zonn|Tallonite zonns]]
1. Return to [[Ismat]] in [[Paineel]].

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
