## Steps

1. Collect 5 aloe leaves from a *Ykeshan aloe bush*, which are found around a little pond near [[U.J.L. 1.0]]. {{waypoint 1308, 407, 719}}
1. Return to Wayth Bitsnbobs.
1. Use [[Ykeshan Aloe Salve]] on 5 [[A Thalz'Iz'Zaz mystic|Thalz'Iz'Zaz mystics]] or [[A Thalz'Iz'Zaz warden|wardens]]. {{waypoint 1257, 411, 907}}
   - **Warning**: If you wear any items that proc damage on hostile spells, you might accidentally aggro these mobs.
1. Return to Wayth Bitsnbobs.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
