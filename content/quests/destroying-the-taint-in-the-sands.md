## Steps

1. Ethri Vasou will randomly offer you one of the following tasks, all in [[The Sinking Sands]]:
   - Destroy 20 **Anaz Mal incense burners** around [[The Twin Tears|Twin Tear]]. (around the gnoll oasis and inside the water too)
   - Slay 20 [[An Anaz Mal diviner|Anaz Mal diviners]] at the northern [[The Twin Tears|Twin Tear]].
   - Destroy 20 **Sul'Dal mystical orbs** in [[The Eye of Anuk]].
   - Slay 20 [[A Rujarkian spiritist|Rujarkian spiritists]].
1. Return to Ethri Vasou in North Qeynos.

## Rewards

- 8902 status
- +500 faction with **The Celestial Watch**
