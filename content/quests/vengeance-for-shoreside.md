## Steps

1. Slay four types of Lonetusk orcs, five of each, chosen randomly from:
- [[A Lonetusk crusher|crushers]]
- [[A Lonetusk pounder|pounders]]
- [[A Lonetusk prophet|prophets]]
- [[A Lonetusk sentry|sentries]]
- [[A Lonetusk shaman|shamans]]
- [[A Lonetusk warrior|warriors]]
1. Return to Lieutenant Imperidus {{waypoint -20, 0, 100}}

## Rewards

- At least 55s 77c
- +200 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
