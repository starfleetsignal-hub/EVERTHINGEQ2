Since using a deino to travel across Karuupa Jungle worked so well, I have decided to try feeding and riding another one.

## Materials Required

- 15 [[Amsonia Roots]]
- 5 [[Wild Rhino Meat]]
- 15 [[Wild Rice]]
- 5 [[Astral Kindling]]

## Note

The [[Recipe Scroll: More Deino Treats]] is auto-scribed

## Steps

1. Try to feed *a restless deino* a treat.
1. Craft a [[Meaty Deino Treat]] using *A Mended Cookpot* {{waypoint 311, 151, 656}}
1. Feed the treat to the deino. Hang on for the ride.
1. Speak to [[Assistant Virdell]] {{waypoint 388, 102, 459}}

## Rewards

- +5,000 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- At least 5p 21g 72s 4c 53013 status
