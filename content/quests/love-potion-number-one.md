## Notes

This is a live event quest given out by a Guide or GM for the [[Erollisi Day]] celebration.  The quest is repeatable.

On servers without the guide program this quest can be acquired from Brosha the Brokenhearted (all year, not just during the Erollisi Day celebration) in the Commonlands at {{waypoint 48, -42, -839}}.

## Steps

1. Pick a flower of hope at the beach near [[The Ruins of Caltorsis]] in [[Antonica]] at {{waypoint -1310, -40, -878}}.
1. Collect a rose of heartbreak from a grave in [[The Graveyard]] at {{waypoint -25, -9, 63}}.
1. Collect magical fairy dust by killing any fairy, such as an [[A sullian deceiver]] in the [[Nerian Highlands]] in [[Darklight Wood]] at {{waypoint 61, -34, -218}}.
1. Use the cauldron to brew the love potion in the [[Wanderlust Fair]] in [[Darklight Wood]] at {{waypoint 240, -19, -1}}.
1. Deliver the troll's love potion to [[Luc Alechaser]] in the [[Lost Village of Bobick]], the bar at the docks of the [[Enchanted Lands]] at {{waypoint 45, 2, 43}}.

## Rewards

- [[Erollisi Mistletoe]]
