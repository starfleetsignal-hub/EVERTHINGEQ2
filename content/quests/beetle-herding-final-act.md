## Steps

1. Frighten [[A skittish scarab|skittish scarabs]] into the tarantulas to obtain 25 pristine carapaces.
1. Return to [[Xilla Beetlebinder]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 9g 34s 18c
- Ability to purchase the following from [[Xilla Beetlebinder]]:
  - [[Aegis of the Beetle]]
  - [[Knuckles of Silencing]]
  - [[Orb of the Beetle Herder]]
  - [[Round Shield of the Beetle Herder]]
  - [[Symbol of the Beetle]]
  - [[Virtuoso's Blade of Whispers]]
  - [[Wall of the Beetle Herder]]
