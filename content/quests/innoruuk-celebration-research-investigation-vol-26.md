## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Innoruuk has asked me to assist in gathering hatebound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Hatebound Energy Reserves|hatebound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect energy by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to [[Herald to the Prince of Hate]] {{waypoint -1644, 62, -1477}} in [[Nektulos Forest]]
