## Notes

1. Simply picking up this quest (by clicking on the glowing bottle) can update the quest [[The Tower of Frozen Shadow]] for the step "Find a way to weaken Vethilot". For this to work, you must not already have the quest "A Ruinous Bath" in your journal, this quest must not already have been completed by anyone in this zone instance, and of course you must be on this step of the [[The Tower of Frozen Shadow]] quest. Note that the update is not obtained if you accept the quest being shared to you.
1. This quest is repeatable, but will only give XP (and AA XP) once.

## Steps

1. Collect three potions from around the Shadow Barracks:
   - dinner table {{waypoint 70, 210, 4}}
   - kitchen {{waypoint 17, 214, 83}}
   - armory {{waypoint 14, 210, -58}}
   - Once all three have been acquired, you are given [[The Collected Potions]].
1. Head back to the blood pool, to around {{waypoint 50, 210, -40}}. Use the potions by pouring them into the pool.
   - This will spawn [[Vethilot the Young]], who is not attackable. He will walk into the pool, speak a few words, and then become [[Vethilot the Corpsemonger]].
1. Defeat Vethilot the Corpsemonger.
