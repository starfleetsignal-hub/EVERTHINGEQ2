- You must first complete the [[Brews Across Norrath]] collection to see this set of shinies.
- Unlike the previous [[Brew Day]] collection, these collectibles are found in newer, far away zones, well outside of the typical starter cities.
- "Alivan Pale Ale" & "New Combine Lager", are found in Cobalt Scar in front of the New Combine tent at the fort.
- "Gnemlin Cog Grog" & "Grey Stone Levitation Ale", are found in Maldura in the Mushroom Bar and Grill.
- "Black Pit Porter" & "Far Seas Silver Rum", are found in Tranquil Seas at the food tent in Sapswill's Rest; also in the Phantom Sea at the entrance to the Far Seas Distillery.
- "Beyond the Veil Ale" & Spirit's Saison", are found in Vesspyr Isles on Daarspire in the main area where the banker and merchants are; also in Obol Plains at Cardin Ward in and around the provisioner hall.

There is also an assortment of consumable alcoholic drinks that spawn when harvesting the purple shinies.
**For descriptions of where the other shinies can be located see the end notes  of the [article about Brew Day 2016 on EQ2 Traders](http://eq2.eqtraders.com/articles/article_page.php?article=q187&menustr=080000000000)**.
