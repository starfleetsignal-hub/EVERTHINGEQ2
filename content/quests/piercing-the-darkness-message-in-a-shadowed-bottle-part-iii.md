## Materials Required

At least:

- 50 [[Coruscating Coal]]

## Steps

1. Examine [[Benosch Ironsprocket's Enchanted Field Guide to Luclin]] in [[Wracklands]]
1. You have to "buy" the three parts for the staff at following merchants:
   - **[[Luclinite Staff Head]]** in [[Recuso Tor]] in [[The Blinding]] from [[Krmi Hilflipper]] at {{waypoint -584.88, 38.39, 466}}. You pay with your crafted [[Tegi Ritual Totem]].
   - **[[Luclinite Staff Shaft]]** in [[Aurelian Coast]] from [[Brindian Fordel]] at {{waypoint 122, 89, -443}} He is standing on a hill on the right just before you enter the cave from the Aurelian Coast. You pay with your crafted [[Symbol of the Shissar Empire]].
     - *Go to the City of Fordel Midst and take the portal to the Aurelian Coast. Go through the cave and when you exit, he is on the small hill on the left.*
   - **[[Luclinite Staff Pommel]]** in [[Sanctus Seru (City)]], second level, from [[Beluba]] at {{waypoint 4, 181, -166}}. You pay with your crafted [[Effigy of a Knight of Shadow Haven]].
1. Craft [[Inert Staff of Luclin]] on a [[Forge]]
   - *Note: This 2-3 minute combine, ~90 seconds if twisting. a [[Potion of Innovation]] may be helpful.*
   - *Note: No potion but with all progress AA maxed, this took <60 seconds*
1. Use [[A Vigilant Sanctum Key|your Vigilant Sanctum Key]] or travel to [[The Hole]] entrance in [[The Sundered Frontier]] and return to [[The Duality's Vigilant Sanctum]] to speak to [[Head Scholar Nabihan (Sanctum)|Head Scholar Nabihan]].
1. Examine the [[Blessed Scroll of Light]] and apply it to the staff.
1. Equip then use the staff to cure your wound, which will fail at first.
1. Speak again with [[Head Scholar Nabihan (Sanctum)|Head Scholar Nabihan]].
1. Right click the platform at {{waypoint 587, 133, -38}} and choose "stand on the platform".
1. Speak again with [[Head Scholar Nabihan (Sanctum)|Head Scholar Nabihan]].
1. Return to [[Alyse Ni'Hij]] in [[Teren's Grasp]] in [[Kylong Plains]] at {{waypoint 2209, 483, -728}}.

## Rewards

- At least 14p 21g 53s 73c 26666 status
- Ability to fly in Blood of Luclin overland zones
- An alternate appearance for your pack pony (a "pack bug"), plus the ability for it to bring back Luclin rares.
- [[Shadowed Staff of Luclin]]
- [[Veilwalker's Mounting Prospects]]
- Achievement: [[Piercing the Darkness]] providing the following additional rewards:
  - At least 240000 status plus a choice of one of the following:
    - [[Sanctum Barding Recipes]]
    - [[Sanctum Breeching Recipes]]
    - [[Sanctum Shoe Recipes]]
    - [[Sanctum Stirrup Recipes]]
