NOTE: Even though the quest is listed under [[The Serpent Sewer]] in your journal, it involves hunting in [[Edgewater Drains]] entirely.

## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[A drudge mucus|drudge mucus]] (1st level)
1. Read the book
1. Kill 7 drudge mucus
1. Read the book
1. Kill 7 [[A Murkwater vigilant|Murkwater vigilant]]s (1st and 2nd levels, in the rooms nearest the stairs)
1. Read the book
1. Kill 6 Murkwater vigilants
1. Read the book
1. Kill 7 [[A rotting globule|rotting globule]]s (1st level)
1. Read the book
1. Kill 8 [[A Murkwater henchman|Murkwater henchmen]] (same as vigilants)
1. Read the book
1. Kill 5 Murkwater henchmen
1. Read the book
1. Kill 8 [[A Murkwater onlooker|Murkwater onlooker]]s (in the stairwells between the 1st and 2nd levels)
1. Read the book
1. Kill 8 Murkwater vigilants
1. Read the book a final time
