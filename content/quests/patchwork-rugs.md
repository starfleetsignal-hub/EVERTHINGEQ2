## Steps

1. Kill 10 bears to collect their pelts.
   - [[A giant kodiak|Giant kodiaks]] are around {{waypoint -2250, -14, -900}}
1. Return to Hartok Woolyman for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Antonican Cleric's Flail]] (All Priests)
  - [[Apprentice's Staff]] (All Mages)
  - [[Basalt Edged Axe]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Buckler of the Hunt]] (All Priests)
  - [[Cutlass of Tears]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Forgotten Orb of the Oracle]] (All Mages)
  - [[Round Shield of the Plainsmaster]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Symbol of the Prairie]] (All Priests)
  - [[Trainee's Great Staff of Brawling]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Ulak of the Raging Bull]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Wall of the Plainsmaster]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
