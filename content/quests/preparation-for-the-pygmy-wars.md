## Steps

![](images/Vines.jpg)

1. Collect 15 vines - these are aerial harvests around [[The Tangled Cliffs of Bawgava]].  Updates automatically as you get close enough.
   - There are many vines outside the highlighted area on the in-game map.  Some are as far north as {{waypoint -943, 107, -33}}. There is a sparkle effect on the "fresh" ones, but it's hard to see.
   - Hanging down from trees, just fly through them.
1. Gather deino bone shards from the [[Tar Pits of Inevitability]] - left-click any of the giant bones.
1. Make weapons - click the table at {{waypoint -707, 10, 287}}
1. Speak to Zedj

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
