## Steps

![Image of Iceclad lichen](images/Iceclad_lichen.png)

1. Speak to [[Nipik]] {{waypoint -1633, -534, -2429}}
1. [[Lodizal]] has fallen sick.  Gather the following items to heal him.  **All** of the next 3 items can be found on an island at {{waypoint -1352, -531, -2682}}
   - 5 Iceclad Lichen
     - Harvestable, from the white and orange mottled rocks  {{waypoint -1349.49, 0.00, -2684.06}}
   - 5 wisps for magical energy
     - [[An oceanspray wisp]] and [[A windy wisp]] both work.  They are also plentiful on the ice sheets around {{waypoint -1238, -538, -2081}}
   - 5 [[An armorclad ice urchin|ice urchins]] for medicine
     - Ice urchins can also be found on an underwater ice ledge near the Tower of Frozen Shadow {{waypoint -1054, -565, -1849}}
1. Kill 8 [[A winged krait|winged kraits]]
   - Actually any krait ([[A krait patriarch]], [[A juvenile krait]]) will update {{waypoint -1415, -541, -2376}}
1. Feed Lodizal
   - Click on Lodizal's head {{waypoint -1633, -535, -2428}}
1. Speak to [[Nipik]]
