## Description

There are predators in the wild that continue to hinder travel for the Far Seas. I am to head to their lairs and turn them into prey.

## Starting the Quest

- [[Kraya Oararm]] at {{waypoint 1224.58, 8.21, 1189.29}} within [[Sapswill's Rest]] in [[Tranquil Sea]]
- [[Kraygon Oararm]] at {{waypoint 1223.58, 19.32, 1188.51}} within [[Wanderer's Dock]] in [[Phantom Sea]]

## Notes

- you need to be at least level 98
- *Timed quest: Shattered Seas Weekly.*

## Steps

- kill [[Mo'iana the Ravenous (Heroic)|Mo'iana the Ravenous]] in [[Zavith'loa: The Lost Caverns (Heroic)]]
- kill [[Thresinet]] in [[Castle Highhold: Thresinet's Den (Heroic)]]
