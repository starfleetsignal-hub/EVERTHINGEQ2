## Description

"Lieutenant Fa'Rak's plan book revealed the orc plan as an attempt to turn Greater Faydark against itself. Though their plan is not yet complete, it is already harming the forest and giving the orcs strength. West of Orc Hill, I will find the Crushbone corruptors executing this plan. I need to take them out as well as the Crushbone High Corruptor. once I have completed this mission, Jenthis would like me to report to Whittier at Green Knoll." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay 4 [[A Crushbone corruptor|Crushbone corruptors]] in [[Treant Grove]] {{waypoint 485, 6, -226}}
1. Slay the [[High Corruptor Gar'Lek]] in [[Treant Grove]] at {{waypoint 488.57, 5.91, -233.90}}
1. Speak to [[Whittier]] on the [[Green Knoll]] at {{waypoint 570.51, 45.65, 76.95}}

## Rewards

- At least 2s 57c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Tempered Dagger (Version 2)|Tempered Dagger]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Tempered Knuckles (Version 2)|Tempered Knuckles]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Tempered Mace (Version 2)|Tempered Mace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Tempered Sword (Version 2)|Tempered Sword]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Tempered Stave (Version 2)|Tempered Stave]] (All Mages)

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Jenthis Viridar: "Great work, [Player Character]. Let me digest what I can."
- Player Character: [give him time to read]
- Jenthis Viridar: "This is truly frightening. There's no time to waste. Are you ready?"
- Player Character: "I am ready."
- Jenthis Viridar: "West of Orc Hill are a number of Crushbone corruptors. The orcs aim to turn the forest against itself. We must put a stop to this, but we must also deliver the rest of these orc plans to Kelethin. Travel west of Orc Hill; kill the Crushbone corruptors; kill the Crushbone High Corruptor, and then deliver these orc plans to Whittier at Green Knoll. Can you do this?"
- Player Character: "Yes."

I need to take care of the orcs corrupting the wildlife west of Orc Hill. I need to kill the Crushbone corruptors west of Orc Hill. I need to kill the Crushbone High Corruptor, Gar'Lek.

I have slain the corruptors. I need to bring news of my mission to Whittier at Green Knoll. It is on a hilltop on the outskirts of Kelethin, south of Treant Grove. I need to find Whitter at Green Knoll.

- Whittier: "Greetings, young one."
- Player Character: "Jenthis wished for me to bring you this."
- Whittier: "What's this? Yes, it says that the orcs plan to burn Kelethin to the ground. You were right to come here, [Player Character], and you've arrived at the right time."

I have spoken with Whittier.

I have slain the Crushbone orcs who were perpetrating vile crimes against the Greater Faydark. Whittier has rewarded me for my service to Faydark.
