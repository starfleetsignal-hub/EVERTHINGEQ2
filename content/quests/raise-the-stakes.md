*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Kill a level 40 tactician on both sides of the conflict (Tae Ew and Rallosian):
   - Spawn [[Tae Ew Tactician]] by killing three groups of [[Tae Ew conscript|Tae Ew conscripts]] around {{waypoint -1290, 8, 370}}
     - Note: Does not always spawn
   - Spawn [[Rallosian Tactician]] by killing three groups of [[Rallosian Footman|Rallosian Footmen]] around {{waypoint -1075, 0, 550}}
1. Return to Malkin.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 69s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Bow of Tyranny]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Mindbender Staff]] (All Mages)
  - [[Pouch of the Feerrott]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Soultaker's Rusted Longbow]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Wand of Divine Fear]] (All Priests)
