This quest is part 3 of a 6-quest series in the [[Isle of Refuge Timeline]].

## Description

"The Queen's Colony is no longer a safe place for Qeynosians. A once peaceful clan of goblins has, for some reason, begun attacking the colony's inhabitants. I am to report to Sergeant Haggus outside the west archway and assist in his investigation into the cause of this uprising." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Report to [[Sergeant Haggus]] {{waypoint 64, -4, 167}} outside the west archway.
1. Kill 3 [[A tainted Sapswill invader|tainted Sapswill invaders]].
1. Return to [[Sergeant Haggus]] to report your success.
1. Search past the barricades in [[Sunset Meadow]] for signs of missing soldiers. These are soldier's bodies lying on the ground a small distance away (e.g., {{waypoint 117, -4, 183}}). When you approach one, [[A defiled soldier (zombie)|a defiled soldier]] spawns.  You don't have to kill it.
1. Return to [[Sergeant Haggus]] with your report.
1. Return to [[Murrar Shar]] to report information about the barricades and receive your reward.

## Rewards

All classes receive:

- At least 1s 15c

One of the following, depending on your class:

- [[Qeynosian Cloth Cuffs]]
- [[Qeynosian Leather Sleeves]]
- [[Qeynosian Studded Wristguards]]
- [[Qeynosian Plated Bracers]]
