## Notes

### How to obtain

- Purchased from [[Arabella F'arquharson]] in [[The City of Freeport]] or [[Qeynos Province District]]. Overseer level 10 is required on the account and character purchasing it must be level 120 adventurer.   It will not be visible on the vendor if you have already scribed a copy of it on your account.
  - As an Overseer quest, it is account-wide and (currently) unlimited charges.
  - Since June of 2026, when you complete this quest, a new quest starter is placed in your [[Overseer Stash]] (if you have it) or in regular inventory bags. This [[Heritage Hunt (Daily) (Grants overseer quest)|Heritage Hunt (Daily)]] needs to be right-clicked and "Used" to add to Overseer window, then you can repeat the mission.

*This mission's reward changes over time. As newer and newer Heritage Crates are released on the [[EQ2 Marketplace]], the most-recent 'previous' tier of Heritage crates are then awarded by this daily, instead.*
