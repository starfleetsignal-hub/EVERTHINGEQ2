## Steps

1. kill Shadel monsters and free the enslaved grimlings
   - Cull 12 [[A Shadel crook|Shadel crooks]], [[A Shadel maurauder|Shadel maurauders]], [[A Shadel thug|Shadel thugs]], or [[A Shadel trapper|Shadel trappers]] from the [[Ruins of Shar Mahkdi]] around {{waypoint -292, 57, -566}}.
   - Free 8 recently enslaved grimlings. Some possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -264, 56, -637}}
     - {{waypoint -175, 64, -635}}
     - {{waypoint -288, 56, -637}}
     - {{waypoint -171, 64, -636}}
     - {{waypoint -179, 64, -609}}
     - {{waypoint -134, 64, -631}}
     - {{waypoint -174, 64, -610}}
     - {{waypoint -136, 64, -605}}
     - {{waypoint -226, 66, -662}} two grimlings
1. Return to [[Rosg]].
