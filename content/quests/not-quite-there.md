## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[Marsh elemental|marsh elementals]].
1. Kill 5 [[Marsh muck|marsh mucks]].
1. Collect 5 lost provisions.
   - Spawn locations are all over the swamp. They may not always be at listed locations.
   - {{waypoint 70, -745, -128}}
   - {{waypoint -192, -743, -243}}
   - {{waypoint -167, -744, -295}}
   - {{waypoint -138, -745, -382}}
   - {{waypoint 160, -745, -113}}
   - they respawn and can be re-collected
1. return to [[Lieutenant Silthiria]].
