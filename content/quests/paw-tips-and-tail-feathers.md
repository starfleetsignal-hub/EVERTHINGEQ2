## Description

The Kerra Isle outfitter has requested assistance in obtaining more feathers and claw sheaths for their traditional crafting.

## Steps

![](images/Fallen_feather.png) ![](images/Discarded_claw_sheath.png)

1. Gather 10 vagabond feathers. These are *[[Fallen feather]]* in the feathered vagabond area, and have green sparklies.
1. Gather 10 pouncer claw sheaths. These are *[[Claw sheath|discarded claw sheath]]* in the darkpaw pouncer area, and have green sparklies.
1. Return to [[Harriet]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,000 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +2,500 faction with **Kerra Isle Outfitters**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Kerra Isle Daily Supply Tasks
