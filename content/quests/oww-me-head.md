## Notes

This quest does not appear in your journal like most quests in game and you receive no experience for completing it. It begins by examining a book in the [[Hall of Conviction]] in [[Teren's Grasp]]. The only reward is the book. The name of this quest is taken from the name of the thread posting in the [SOE forums](http://forums.station.sony.com/eq2/posts/list.m?start=150&topic_id=456926) where the quest hints first appeared.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Teren's Grasp]] and enter the [[Hall of Conviction]] (second building on the left going clockwise from the entrance ramp).
1. Examine the jewelry box on the table near [[Hollen the Runesage]].
1. In no particular order, travel to the [[Ulteran Spires]] in [[Antonica]], [[Commonlands]], [[Enchanted Lands]], and [[Greater Faydark]] and harvest the red shiny that appears nearby in each location to "retrieve the key piece".
1. Travel to [[Timorous Deep]] to the island next to [[Gorowyn]] and enter the area with the raptors. {{waypoint 2096.15, 32, 687.67}}
1. Right click the crystal to place the keystone pieces you've already retrieved. A void appears.
1. Right click the void and "reach into the tear".
