## Notes

- If Fethinal at any time is not visible at the given location, first try **<code>/target Fethinal</code>** then "h" to hail him. You may need to try relogging.

## Steps

1. Get the base shield in [[New Tunaria]] at {{waypoint -315, 53, -858}}. It looks like a regular tin buckler on the wall in the east side of the Hall of Truth, facing the huge tree that houses the Temple of Growth.
1. Return to Fethinal.
1. Kill the following elements to retrieve their 'god-given Symbols':
   - [[Element of fire]], located in [[Lavastorm]] within the lava in the center of the Goblet of Ro. {{waypoint -709, -11, -275}}
   - [[Element of water]], located off the far northwest shore of [[Enchanted Lands]]. {{waypoint 440, -11, -1087}}
   - [[Element of air]], located in the far south of [[Loping Plains]] near the the [[Crypt of Valdoon]] climbing wall. {{waypoint 192, 71, 315}}
   - Slay the [[Element of earth]] in [[Kaladim]] within [[Butcherblock Mountains]] past the large doors at the top of the entrance room in the corridor slightly to the left. {{waypoint 15, 26, -183}}
1. Return to Fethinal.
1. Go to [[Antonica]] and inspect the gravestone at {{waypoint -2036, 7, 218}}. Speak with [[Kryfru the Enchanted]] and then search in the grave dirt in front of him to recover the journal.
   - If a group member is nearby, disband group before attempting this step.
1. Take the journal back to Fethinal.
1. Kill following the 4 spirits:
   - [[Spirit of life]], located in [[Greater Faydark]], behind the waterfall at the Lower Emerald River. {{waypoint -552, 5, 154}}
   - [[Spirit of death]], located in [[Loping Plains]], at the [[Somborn Cemetery]]. {{waypoint -382, 16, 55}}
   - [[Spirit of magic]], located in [[The Lesser Faydark]] by the Fugtyr tribe. Climb to the very top of their area then jump in the river and swim downstream. The spirit will be above the first water fall. {{waypoint 128, -1, -375}}
   - [[Spirit of divine]], located in [[New Tunaria]], on the cliff above the water overlooking the lake southeast of the [[Thex Monument]]. {{waypoint -317, 72, -75}}
     - In general, Tunarians do not see invis, but invisibility won't get you past the vampires.
1. Return to Fethinal for your reward.
