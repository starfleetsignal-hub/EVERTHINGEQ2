## Notes

- This is a mandatory subquest of [[The Maiden's Reception]], part of the [[Claymore Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A Blacktalon initiate|Blacktalon initiates]]. These are found in the main room and in the east and west wings on the first floor.
1. Return to the maiden statue.

- [[A part of a Star key]]
