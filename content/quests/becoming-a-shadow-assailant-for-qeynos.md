## Notes

The evil version of this quest is entitled [[Becoming a Shadow Assailant for Freeport]].

## Steps

1. Go to a zone of appropriate level for you and find a Void Tempest
   - You can not mentor for this quest.
   - A list of storm locations can be found under [[Tempest affected Creatures List]]
1. Kill 4 [[Dark seepage]] and/or [[Dark horror]], no dings for updates, just kill.
   - Spawn one at a time.
1. Kill the [[Void Tempest]] and collect the [[Void Storm Shred]].
1. Return to [[Librarian Dungil]].(Note for repeats) You can kill any void storm in any zone for updates, and the pupil adepts will also exchange the Shreds for Appreciation Notes. Each chest contains as many Shreds as the number of people who are grouped to kill it.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 40c
- 1 [[Concordium Note of Appreciation]]
- Title: Shadow Assailant
