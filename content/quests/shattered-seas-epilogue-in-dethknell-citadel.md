## Steps

1. Speak with the [[Lucan D'Lere|Overlord]] in [[The City of Freeport]].
   - *Note: use the portal in the [[Overlord's Plaza]] at {{waypoint 10, 19, 10}} to access [[The Overlord's Office]] then talk to [[Lucan D'Lere]]*

## Rewards

- 2 [[Electrified Adornment of Critical Chance (QuestReward)|Electrified Adornment of Critical Chance]]
- [[Indomitable Pteranodon]]
- At least 5p 50g 38724 status

You will also get the achievement [[Shattered Seas]] which rewards you with:

- Suffix Title: [[Sentinel of the Shattered Seas]]
- [[Adornment Reclamation (Version 2)|Adornment Reclamation]]
  - *Note: you can now buy the [[Adornment Reclamation]] for other chars on your account from the merchant [[Khadra]] in the tower at Wanderer's dock in Phantom Sea as well for 1c. The version from the merchant is heirloom and can be traded to alts over the shared bank, That way you can dislodge old adorns while leveling or doing the questline again*

Note: Also, See your class trainer for a version sold of this spell scroll which does not require completing this timeline on any characters.
