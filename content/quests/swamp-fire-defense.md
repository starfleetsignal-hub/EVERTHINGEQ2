## Steps

1. Place the swamp phlogiston wards at the following locations (indicated by a green shiny on the ground):
   - [[Trakaraptor Alley]]: {{waypoint -596, -46, 358}}, {{waypoint -513, -72, 484}}, and {{waypoint -429, -82, 593}}
   - North [[Outer Sebilis]]: {{waypoint -316, -99, 728}}
   - Main gate of Outer Sebilis: {{waypoint -84, -120, 952}}
   - Main gate of Outer Sebilis: {{waypoint -71, -118, 1012}}
1. Return to Slixin Drusar.
