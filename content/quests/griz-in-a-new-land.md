- NOTE**

To be able to get this quest, you must have completed Knut's previous questline.

Knut Orcbane, an acclaimed barbarian clan hero of yesteryear, stationed at Cragged Spine in Frostfang Sea, has lent me Griz, his sentient grizzly pelt companion. Griz is hungry to help me battle the Plane of War orcs that have taken over Zek, the Scourge Wastes!

## Steps

1. Kill 10 Scourge orcs for Griz.
1. Find the area of the Scourge Wastes calling Griz.
   - Travel to [[Hounds' Gorge]], around {{waypoint -289.91, -29.34, -97.41}}, until Griz speaks to you again.
   - Kill 8 [[A Scourge clearcutter|Scourge clearcutters]].
1. Continue exploring Hounds' Gorge.
   - Search along the northern wall of the gorge.
1. Enter the old Zek grawler cave {{waypoint -460.72, -27.14, -208.00}}.
   - Investigate the bodies of dead bear cubs {{waypoint -440.54, -25.23, -243.46}}.
   - Search for the killer within the grawler cave.
1. Slay a pair of [[A Deathfist orc|Deathfist orcs]] that have evaded the Scourge {{waypoint -414.32, -29.19, -244.63}}
1. Return Griz to [[Knut Orcbane]] in [[Frostfang Sea]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- [[Visage of the Scourge Watchman]]
