## Steps

1. Read the mail from Raffik, 'Pirats needs sords.'
1. Speak with [[Raffik]] on the docks at [[Butcherblock Bay]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] at {{waypoint 669, 24, 601}}
1. Transmute the level 1 [[Rusty Dagger (Level 1)|daggers]] Raffik gives you, to create 2 [[Lapis Lazuli Fragment|Lapis Lazuli Fragments]], or have 2 fragments already in your inventory.
1. Speak with [[Tyla Maki]] at {{waypoint 763, 27, 600}}.
1. Craft an [[Adornment of Pirate Energy]] from the [[Piratical Weapon Adornments For All Ages|recipe]] provided. There is a workbench at the [[Highland Outposts]] at {{waypoint 337, 189, 514}}. You will need 2 [[Lapis Lazuli Fragment|Lapis Lazuli Fragments]] and 1 [[Basic Coal]].
   - The crafting requires Adorning counters. If you do not have the Adorning skill you may get it from [[Sala Maki]] at {{waypoint 759, 26, 599}}.
1. Speak with Raffik.

## Rewards

- At least 85g
- Tradeskill experience
- [[Mutagenic Essence (Recipe Book)|Mutagenic Essence Recipe Book]]
