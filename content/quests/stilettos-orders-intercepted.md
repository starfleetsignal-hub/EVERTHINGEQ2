## Notes and Prerequisites

- [[Stiletto's Orders]] drops from [[Stiletto]] which starts this quest.
- Stiletto's respawn timer is approximately 5 minutes.
- Your Adventure level must be 25 or higher to start this quest.
- Your Crafting level must be Artisan level 9 or higher to complete this quest or have someone commission craft it for you.
- You must enter the [[Cove of Decay]] to complete this quest (access quest no longer required).
  - Although a series of access quests ([[The Cove of Decay - Prison Break|Cove of Decay - Prison Break]]) for this zone still exist, they are no longer required to enter certain instances in the zone. Cove of Decay is a heroic zone (changed from Epic x2).

- Where to find [[Stiletto]]:
  - Somewhere near [[Crater Mine]] and may swim in the lake at {{waypoint 547, -22, 932}}.
  - The area between [[Deadview Pass]] {{waypoint 826.60, -3, 865}} and [[Gnoll Cave]]{{waypoint 240.60, -0.23, 1168.64}}.
  - Near [[Coldwind Cove]] at {{waypoint 339, -17, 762}}.
  - Often found near the Centaur Camp {{waypoint 63, 1, 1389}}.
  - The roaming pattern for [[Stiletto]] is a semi-contained area within the following points:
    - {{waypoint 467, -8, 1150}}
    - {{waypoint 463, -2, 1140}}
    - {{waypoint 450, -23, 1110}}
    - {{waypoint 483, -24, 1125}}

## Steps

1. Investigating Kedge's Lament.
   1. Enter Cove of Decay {{waypoint -91, -3, 1009}} and kill [[The Octagorgon]].
      - *Note: Step 9 of [[An Axe from the Past]] requires this instance as well.*
      - After doing this you can swim to the ocean floor and you will find some interactive chests on the ground {{waypoint -264, -50, 1322}}. Inside one of them, you will find a [[Partially Digested Hand]]. Only one person has to do this. You can use a digested hand from a prior instance. However, you will have to still kill Octagorgon before you can do step 2.
   1. Once you have the Partially Digested Hand, go back to the beach and you will find a firepit. The person who has the hand can right-click on the remains in the Firepit and choose "Place the hand in the fire".
   1. This will spawn a level 32 ^^^ Heroic mob, [[Captain Ulssissaris]]. Kill him and the quest will update.
1. Find information about the torn silk bag ...
   1. Head to [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] and examine an interactive book on a table to the right, just inside the door in the Workshop of the Enchanter {{waypoint 261, 0, 118}}. This room is just South of the entrance to the Chamber of Immortality.
   1. Kill [[Weavemaster Esh'Rax]] in [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] at {{waypoint 18, 4, 98}}.
      - He spawns in the tailor room.
      - He will respawn every 30 minutes. (respawn on Origin is 10 min)
      - He will drop a [[Shroud of the Manastone Recipe]] and the [[Enchanted Linen of the Weavemaster]].
      - Drops are quest reward window so can group.
1. Craft one [[Shroud of the Manastone]] using the enchanted linen, 1 [[Boiled leather pelt]], 1 [[Glimmering Material]] and a [[Basic Filament]].
   - *If you want to craft this yourself, you must be Artisan level 9+ to scribe the recipe.*
   - *You can bring a [[Portable Sewing Table]] (requires a minimum crafting level of 30 to use) and supplies for your group and complete the crafting in RoV, then continue directly to kill Varsoon.*
1. Kill [[Varsoon the Undying]] in the [[Chamber of Immortality]] {{waypoint 235.15, -3.92, 98.24}}.
   - *Step 8 of [[A Strange Black Rock]] also requires [[Varsoon the Undying]].*
   - Loot the stone from a chest in a small room off of the main room, after Varsoon dies to complete the quest.
