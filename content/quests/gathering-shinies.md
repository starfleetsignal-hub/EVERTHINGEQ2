## Notes

The recipe to polish these items auto-scribes. You do *not* need to locate it in inventory and scribe it manually.

## Steps

1. Find 5 shiny things.
   - These are small white [[Scuffed sand shilling|sand shillings]] (they look like real-world Sand Dollars) scattered all along the beach. ![Sand Shilling](images/Sand_Shilling.jpg)
   - *Note: gather 5 tough vines as well, since they are needed for the craft* ![tough vine](images/Tough_vine_visible.jpg)
1. Polish the 5 [[Shiny Sand Shilling|Sand Shillings]] until they shine.
   - Use the [[Goblin Workbench]] {{waypoint 18, -241, -875}}. ![Goblin Workbench](images/Goblin_Workbench.jpg)
   - Note: Craft them 1 at a time, as mass production only updates the quest once.
   - Requires 100 Artisan, 5 [[Scuffed sand shilling|sand shillings]], 5 [[Tough vine|tough vines]], and 5 [[Thaumic Coal]].
1. Bring the shiny things to [[Vhesh]].

## Rewards

- +2000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
- At least 1p 53g 9s
