## Steps

1. Find the treasures that [[Linguobot]] wants:
   - A *perfect piece of scrap metal*, automatically obtained by killing [[Scrounger Lumph]] inside the [[Boarfiend Caves]]. {{waypoint 1631, 456, 46}}
     - The entrance to the Boarfiend Caves is at {{waypoint 1347.26, 455.71, 422.70}}.![Froglok tweaking tools](images/Tweaking_tools.jpg)
   - *Special froglok tweaking tools*, from beside a puddle inside a crafting hut in [[Tupta]]. {{waypoint -71.32, -52, 813.44}}
   - A *curious ore that the Brokenskull seem to mine*, automatically obtained by killing  [[Shipwright Ythkaz]] at [[Gunthak's Shanty]] in the lower Moors. {{waypoint -1291, -939, 545}}
   - A *piece of coral*, found underwater in the lower Moors. {{waypoint -1030, -1113, 1245}}
1. Return to Linguobot.

## Reward

- Ability to speak and understand [[Tik-Tok]].
