## Steps

1. Use [[Track Harvestables]] to find [[Empty Unscorchable Bag|a strange bag]]  located randomly inside one of the [[Cinder Nests]].
   - Potential locations include:
     - {{waypoint 201.45, 67.22, 44.10}}
     - {{waypoint 192, 50, 7}}
     - {{waypoint 283, 102, 11}}
     - {{waypoint 271, 93, 36}}
     - {{waypoint 132.71, 50.75, 377.19}}  ![](images/The_Scrivener's_Tale_Difficulties_Delivering_quest_-_a_strange_bag.jpg)
   - Alternatively, you can purchase an [[Empty Unscorchable Bag]] for 120p 5c from [[Krevat]] at {{waypoint 413, 92, 316}}.
1. Click on the pile of red shards at {{waypoint 166, 44, 75}}.
1. Speak to [[Mourndax V'Zeaar (DF-NPC)|Mourndar V'Zeaar]] at {{waypoint 16, 7, 100}} and give him the [[Unscorchable Bag of Red Shards]].
1. Return to [[Myrist, the Great Library]]
1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 388, 16, 170}} in the [[South Wing Catalog]], second floor of [[Myrist, the Great Library]] to speak with [[Ghida Borrell]] in the [[Smiths' Gallery]] at {{waypoint 660, 412, 59}}.

## Rewards

- [[Elysian Tradeskill Tack]]
- [[Recipe Scroll: Divine Weapons of Ro]]
- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
