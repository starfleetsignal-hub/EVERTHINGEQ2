## Notes

- You must understand the [[Orcish]] language

## Steps

1. Speak with members of Clan Thrael'Gorr.
   - Speak to [[Chief Thrael'Gorr]]
   - Speak to the [[Thrael'Gorr Oracle]]
   - Speak to [[Farg Bloodtusk]]
   - Speak to [[A Thrael'Gorr trooper]] (patrolling the outskirts of the camp)
1. Return to [[Korgak Rotstomper]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- +5,000 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
- Prefix title of "[[The Thrael'Gorr Champion|Thrael'Gorr Champion]]" at conclusion of this quest.
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Breastplate]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Chestwrap]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Hauberk]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Robe]] (All Mages)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Oracle's Breastplate]] (Clerics)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Oracle's Hauberk]] (Shamans)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Oracle's Tunic]] (Druids)
