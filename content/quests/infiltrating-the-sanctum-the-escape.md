## Steps

1. Go to the [[Talonflight Armory]] and inspect a weapons stand at {{waypoint -59, 126, 180}}.
1. Return to [[Thorn]].
1. Escort three 'escaping frogloks' to the entrance of the dungeon.
   - Stand behind them and they automatically path back to the entrance. Their path goes through the [[Chamber of Animation]]. If you try to go the other way, they will just stand around in the Oratorium.
   - They are NPCs so they will not draw aggro.
   - You can fight your way to the entrance with them in tow. If you take too long to move they state that they will find their own way and the quest will update anyway.
   - Alternatively, If you can take a few hits as you go, put on a movement buff and just run straight through all the mobs to the zone exit. The only mobs you will have to fight will be the sentinels on the stairs near the entrance because you will outrun all the others. You can then go AFK by the zone entrance, because even though you will leave the frogloks behind, they will eventually make their way out.
   - (If it can hold, they can also be led through to the exit while you're invisible.)
   - If you somehow lose the frogloks, return to Thorn and tell her that you've lose them. She will give you brand new ones.
1. Return to Thorn to finish the quest.
