## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
  - This Quest is repeatable. No Scarred Dinar reward beyond the first completion.

## Steps

1. Search and hunt for the following
   - Search sulfide deposits for 8 zinc dust on the northern beaches beginning at 1=361 2=18 3=136 or   1=556 2=8 3=238
   - Hunt either
     - 8 Frosthide Maulers for blubber oil at {{waypoint 35, 13, -48}} or
     - get unremitting embers from icebone sentinels at {{waypoint 523, 44.49, 261.2}}
1. Return to [[Gwinres Boulderkind]] at {{waypoint 60.53, 8.93, 599.78}}

## Reward

- [[Watertight Crate]] house item
- 40 x [[Scarred Dinar]] *-- only the first time*
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
