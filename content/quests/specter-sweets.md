## Prerequisites

- +20,000 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**

## Steps

1. Enter [[Dartain's Fortress]] and attract a fallen warlock, using Shiptu's Delicious Ghost Candy (check your inventory).
1. Return to [[Confectioner Shiptu]] within 10 minutes (timed).

## Rewards

- At least 55g 70s 61c
- +2500 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**
