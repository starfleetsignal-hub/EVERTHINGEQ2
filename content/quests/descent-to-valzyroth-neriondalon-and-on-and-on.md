## Steps

1. Speak with [[Nillirn Strikepipe]] at the Gutters {{waypoint 857.82, 158.97, -1075.09}}
   - Receive recipe to scribe for Hate Panacea. Requires 50 Formless Candles.
   - Gather 5 Umbra lily pad rhizomes in Umbrahollow Lake
   - Kill 10 haunting ire for tangible ire in Hate's End (body drops)
   - Obtain 8 corpse crawler chitin in Hate's End (body drops) under Thexian Grave Soil mounds. Not all have corpse crawlers.
   - Procure widow's milk on the ground in front of the counter [[Merchant Lythriss]] in Market View {{waypoint 564.80, 162.84, -909.44}}
1. Use Potions and Poisons Brazier at Market View to craft the Hate panacea {{waypoint 557.97, 162.44, -904.99}}
1. Use the Hate panacea on [[Grubtongue]] {{waypoint 657.25, 160.02, -907.68}}
1. Speak with [[Grubtongue]].
1. Return to Jackal at the Gutters.
1. Return to Merchant Lythriss.
1. Gather knowledge about the Thexian and drachnid alignment
   - Book at {{waypoint 396.10, 220.75, -1077.27}}
1. Return to Jackal at the Gutters.
1. Gather the clockwork head in House Punox {{waypoint 385.91, 220.57, -1081.03}}
1. Find someone in Neriondalon who can help with ancient clockworks
1. [[Phileas Wobbleblok]] {{waypoint 841.62, 172.42, -1013.49}}
   - Obtain 2 functional iris mechanisms - 'a Thexian ironcolossus*a Thexian ironguard'
   - Gather 2 polished lens crystals - 'a Thexian ironcolossus'
   - Secure 6 dolomite encased wiring - 'a Thexian ironcolossus' 'a Thexian ironguard'
1. Return to Phileas Wobbleblock
   - Get 7 mechanimagica signal tethers - 'a Thexian ironguard'
   - Get 6 gnemonic lattic boards - clickable scrap wood on the ground
