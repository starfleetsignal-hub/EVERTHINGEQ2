## Steps

Nothing is free and building, maintaining, and supporting the herald's camp is expensive. That's why the herald of Cazic-Thule has requested that we assist in offsetting the camp's cost by retrieving a valuable relic guarded by Kusala'Din. I need to retrieve the relic.

1. Retrieve the relic guarded by Kusala'Din in [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion (Solo)]]
   - The relic is at {{waypoint -1092, 182, 717}} near the portal.
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Faceless]] {{waypoint -1109, -1, 919}} in [[The Feerrott]]
