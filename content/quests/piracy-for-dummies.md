## Steps

1. Recover the lost dictionary
   - Underwater at {{waypoint 1349, -656, 211}}, click the icy crate
1. Return to Dort Lumrick
   - [[A thieving gnomish pirate]] attacks, steals the book, and immediately despawns!
1. Tell Dort that the book was stolen.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
