*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Kill 4 [[A Bloodskull tower guard|Bloodskull tower guards]] near {{waypoint -560, -48, 457}}.  ![](images/Bloodskull_Intentions_quest_-_Bloodskull_orders.jpg)
1. Collect the Bloodskull standing orders on the ground near one of the guards. Keep killing guards until it drops. The drop isn't from a chest or from a corpse, rather an object that might spawn at the feet of the tower guards upon their death.
1. Return to [[Rainus Canton]].

## Rewards

- At least 6s 66c
- +60 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
