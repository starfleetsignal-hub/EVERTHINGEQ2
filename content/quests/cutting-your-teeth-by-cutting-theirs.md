## Prerequisites

- You must be at least adventure level 65 to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Collect 24 teeth from drolvargs (each kill rewards 2 teeth) around [[The Dreadlands]]. {{waypoint 697, 11, -254}}
   - The following mobs reward teeth:
     - [[A drolvarg scavenger|drolvarg scavengers]]
     - [[A drolvarg snarler|drolvarg snarlers]]
     - [[A drolvarg rager|drolvarg ragers]]
     - [[A drolvarg gnasher|drolvarg gnashers]]
     - [[A drolvarg ravisher|drolvarg ravishers]]
1. Return to [[Borbin Happens]] at the docks.

## Rewards

- At least 18g 9s 57c
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
