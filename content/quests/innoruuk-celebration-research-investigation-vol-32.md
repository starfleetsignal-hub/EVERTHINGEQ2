## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of Innoruuk has asked for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Gather the requested supplies
   - Collect 3 [[Distorted Essence|distorted essence]] found in [[Zimara Breadth]]
     - *Note: drops in [[Chests|small chest]] from a maedjin aggressor (mostly found in the Auric Palisades area)*
     - *1 drop per group member*
   - Gather or purchase [[Aether Ore|aether ore]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas
     - *Common harvest from BoZ ore cluster*
     - *Can buy off broker*
     - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to [[Herald to the Prince of Hate]] {{waypoint -1644, 62, -1477}} in [[Nektulos Forest]]
