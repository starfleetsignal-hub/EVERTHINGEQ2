## Prerequisites

You must have completed the quest [[Sufficient Samples of Thullosian]] once to speak with the [[Linguobot]] during this quest.

## Steps

1. Talk to [[Grezelda Razmadash]] {{waypoint 82, -89, -440}} at [[Firmroot Moot]].
1. Speak with [[Linguobot]] {{waypoint 1382, 406, 749}} in [[U.J.L. 1.0]]
1. Return the translated tome to [[Shaman Skullcleave]].

## Rewards

- [[Thule Iz Good]]
- At least 11g 5s 35c
- +1,200 faction with **Survival Accord**
