## Description

Massive predators are being controlled by an Allu'thoa beast master within Zavith'loa. But that is not all that troubles the Far Seas. Many of their own company are thought to be controlled by the Akheva within Castle Highhold. I and my allies are to venture into both locales and defeat these enemies.
