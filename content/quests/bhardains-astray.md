I noticed that Bhardain, and his wolf, Shuck, are no longer working within Outer Maldura. Where would they have traveled to?

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Thaldhor]] in [[Outer Maldura]] {{waypoint 465.73, 123.11, 275.03}}.
1. Find Bhardain and Shuck near the [[Penumbra Expansion]] {{waypoint 470.58, 30.00, -596.78}}.
1. Click [[Bhardain]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- [[A dhalgar hound drum]]
- +1,000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
- +1,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
