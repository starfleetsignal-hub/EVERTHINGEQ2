## Steps

Valdaan has sent me back out into the ruins in search of more House Arbos treasures and relics.

- Gather 10 House of Arbos scrolls
- Gather 10 House Arbos relic cases
- Hunt 12 glaufaye or thiflings for pillaged Amulets of Sul
- Slay 12 forgotten researchers for House Arbos runestones

Return to the encampment and fill the Reinforced Strongbox

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- [[Imperial Hero's Mint]] x2
