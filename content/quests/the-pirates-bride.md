## Notes

- This is a sub-quest of [[The Stolen Tome]]. However, it is not needed to complete that quest.

## Steps

1. Examine [[A Crumpled Note]] given to you by [[Jai-la Ventur]] at the end of the previous quest.
   - You must examine this while in [[The Village of Shin]] and not [[The Mystic Lake]].
1. Go to [[Gunthak Pirate Camp]] in [[The Mystic Lake]] and search for [[Alyra the Assassin]] {{waypoint 56.08, -39.35, 257.31}}. Kill her when she spawns.
1. Loot [[An Assassination Contract]] from Alyra's chest that she drops and read it.
   - You must examine this while in The Mystic Lake and not The Village of Shin.
1. Return to Jai-La with the unfortunate news to complete the quest and claim your reward.
   - Jai-La wanders around the Village of Shin. You can ask the guards and they will tell you where she is, no matter how much she wanders. Sometimes she is indoors.

## Rewards

- At least 9g 13s 1c
- [[Lamp of Devotion]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Curate's Plate Gloves of the Zealous]] (Clerics)
  - [[Dragoon's Gauntlets of Power]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Naturewalker's Leather Gloves of the Sapient]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Sensei's Hand Wraps of Perfection]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Spiritualist's Handguards of the Enlightened]] (Shamans)
  - [[Thaumaturge's Gloves of Genius]] (All Mages)
  - [[Vagabond's Chain Gloves of Deftness]] (All Scouts)
