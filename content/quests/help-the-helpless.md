## Steps

1. Kill 14-16 [[A crypt tempest|crypt tempests]] in [[The Crypt of Betrayal]].  They can be found around the stairs at {{waypoint 83, 2, -43}}.  If you don't see any around, try killing their placeholders, the [[A deathly scarab|deathly scarabs]].

- +500 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
