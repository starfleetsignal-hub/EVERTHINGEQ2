## Steps

1. Enter [[Raj'Dur Plateaus: Blood and Sand (Signature)|Raj'Dur Plateaus: Blood and Sand [Signature]]] Portal at {{waypoint 162.51, -59.73, 476.51}}
1. Defend [[Hopewell Harbor]] by defeating [[Baka]] {{waypoint 177.10, -57.72, 440.09}} **NOTE:** Soloing my *mage with best possible merc could not kill Baka*. Two-boxing the zone with *NO mercs and a mage/crusader (Pally) was no problem*.  PS. Kiting for the win against all monsters.
1. Help save the Hizite Reservoir Camps by
   - Defend [[Goldami Reservoir Camp]] by defeating [[Quartermaster Hamid]] {{waypoint -530.95, 3.03, 479.34}}
   - Defend [[Nerive Reservoir Camp]] by defeating [[Farid]] {{waypoint -436.56, 8.54, 156.95}}
   - Defend [[Kulaidi Reservoir Camp]] by defeating [[Khadra Chatt]] {{waypoint 326.44, 9.76, 176.73}}
1. *Optional*: [[Mustapha Droughtbringer]] {{waypoint -7.58, -2.20, 151.39}} will complete the solo zone, but isn't necessary to the quest.
1. Return to [[Nouzar]]
