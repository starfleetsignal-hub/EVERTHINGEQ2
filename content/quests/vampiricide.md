## Description

The recently discovered Evernight Abbey seems to serve as a sort of domicile of the insidious Libant vampires. I've been asked to enter their lair and slay a good number of their kind in hopes of quelling a recent rash of violence.

## Steps

1. Slay 20 Libant vampires within [[Evernight Abbey]].
1. I need to return to [[Tanya Alvernon]] in Somborn to collect my reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
