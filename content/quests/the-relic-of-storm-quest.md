## Steps

1. Touch the [[Grove of Stones]]. {{waypoint 207, 14, 110}}
1. Kill [[Grumzod the Exile]] near [[The Deathfist Citadel]]. {{waypoint 54, -36, -849}}
   - He spawns with 4 others: [[Orcish Heretic]], [[Orcish Proselyte]], [[Exiled Captain]] and [[Exiled Lieutenant]]
   - He is on a 3-5 (earth) day spawn timer.
1. Walk to the center of the [[Grove of Stones]].

- [[Faceted Earring of Exemption]]
