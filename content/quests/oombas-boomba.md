## Steps

1. Create a Boomba
   - Scribe [[Blueprint: Boomba Prototype]] to make the [[Boomba (Item)|Boomba]].
   - Use the Work Bench by Fenwick {{waypoint 1291.32, -540.25, 472.13}}
   - Materials required:
     - 1 [[Titanium ore]], 1 [[Quicksilver cluster]], 1 [[Amber]], 1 [[Ethereal coal]]
1. Once made, release the Boomba near the barracades in Storm Gorge {{waypoint 712.00, -375.26, 867.57}}. It will fly off towards the gates and explode (you do not need to follow it to witness the event)
1. Return to [[Oomba]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,000 faction with **Iceclad Pirates**
