## Description

Ash has covered much of Maldura, brought on by an invading force from deep below the mountain stronghold. And in the vast darkness of Kralet Penumbra's watery depths awaits a creature that is anathema to life. My mission is to stop both of these threads, and I will not rest until I do.

## Steps

1. Slay the ash walkers
   - [[Cugdava (Heroic)|Cugdava]] in [[Maldura: District of Ash (Heroic)]]
1. Kill the un-transformed
   - [[Mrwar, the Destroyer]] in [[Kralet Penumbra: Tepid Depths (Heroic)]]
