## Notes

- For this case, you are actually interviewing four or five of the "Three Stooges": Shemp Howard (Zhemp Whardo), Moh Tzorwith (Moe Howard, born Moses Horwitz), Lahree Neif (Larry Fine), and Carlee Besser (who seems to be a combination of Curly and Joe Besser, who replaced Shemp after Shemp's second departure from the Stooges)

## Steps

1. Go to [[Enchanted Lands]] and speak with [[Zherran Tzizzink]] in the [[Lost Village of Bobick]]. {{waypoint -27, 0, 47}}
1. Speak with the following NPCs:
   1. [[Zhemp Whardo]] {{waypoint -205, 0, -348}}
   1. [[Moh Tzorwith]] {{waypoint 48, 0, -403}}
   1. [[Carlee Besser]] {{waypoint 286, 5, -241}}
   1. [[Lahree Neif]] {{waypoint 221, 14, -475}}
1. Examine the [[Case notes]] from your inventory and click on any of the text options to receive an update.
1. Return to Zherran Tzizzink and accuse Carlee.
1. Return to Justinian Theo.

## Rewards

- +2500 faction with **The Tribunal**
