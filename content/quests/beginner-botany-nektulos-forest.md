## Steps

1. Travel to [[Nektulos Forest]] and gather the following in order:
   1. 3 J'Rais mushrooms -- {{waypoint -211, 0.01, 232}}, near the J'Rais Bridge
   1. 3 Druid's ghostweed -- around {{waypoint -32.05, 90.76, -2010.54}}, near the Druid Ring
   1. 3 Darklight Station weeds -- around {{waypoint -1365.77, 20.55, -1401.70}}, near the zone-in from Darklight Wood
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].

## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable

## Rewards

- Unlock the [[Days of Summer: 2023|Days of Summer 2023: Week 8]] Holiday event rewards that you can purchase from [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2293, -199, 2506}}
