## Description

"Randu K'van, a Tier'Dal who resides in Greater Faydark, asked me to help him by finding information about the Crushbone orcs. I brought him some information, but he said it was of no use. This time I am supposed to interrogate a Crushbone expedition member." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay **ONE** of the following in the first Crushbone orc valley along the north side:
   - [[Crushbone Expedition Weaponsmith]]
   - [[Crushbone Expedition Chef]]
   - [[Crushbone Expedition Leader]]
   - [[Crushbone Expedition Planner]]
1. Return to [[Randu K'Van]]

- At least 11s 13c
- One of the following, based on class:

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Randu K'Van: "See if you can find a Crushbone expedition member. You would think that a planner would keep better records. They must have information that we can use."
- Player Character: "I will do what I can."

This time I am to interrogate a Crushbone expedition member. I am looking for a Crushbone expedition member. One that is more important than the rest. Hopefully, it will have the information Randu wants.

I have interrogated one of the orcs that is part of the Crushbone expedition and found a detailed letter on its body. I have gathered information from an orc that was part of the Crushbone expedition. I found some information on a member of the Crushbone expedition. I should return this information to Randu K'Van in The Greater Faydark. I should take this information to Randu K'Van, near the Crushbone mount station.

- Randu K'van: "Again you return. No, I don't even have to ask. You smell like the filth of rotting orc carcasses! Show me what you found... and stay back a few feet."
- Player Character: "Here you go. I am always amazed that the orcs even know how to write."

I have given Randu K'Van the information I found. I have given the information to Randu K'Van.

I found a Crushbone expedition member and took any information I could from its belongings. This seemed to make Randu quite pleased.
