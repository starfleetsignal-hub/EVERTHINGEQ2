## Steps

1. Make your way to [[Edgewater Drains]] beneath [[Freeport]] to find the source of the infection. Kill the lvl 37^ [[An agent of Bertoxxulous|Agent of Bertoxxulous]] at {{waypoint 96.80, 32.50, 41.33}}
   - *See the notes below for easiest way(s) to get to Edgewater Drains.*
1. Return to Prophet Bainyn Fairwind.
1. Go to [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] and buy a Distilled Oaken Root vial from [[Viddle Sprocketbender]] {{waypoint 545, -41, 282}} on the docks
1. "Collect" a [[Superb Noxious Remedy]] potion, a level 30 Alchemist tradeskilled item. You can buy this from a broker, if you don't have any people willing to make you one. If you already have it in your bags the quest will not update.
   - NOTE: You MUST receive the Superb Noxious Remedy when on the correct step of the quest; i.e., after purchasing the Oaken Root. If you don't, the quest will not update. You can make it yourself, if you are an alchemist, or purchase it off the broker, if available.
   - You can also have this mailed to you, perhaps from an alt, but it needs to retrieved from the mailbox only when on the step to collect the remedy (after purchasing the Oaken Root).
   - You can take a [[Superb Noxious Remedy]] potion out of your shared bank, sell it to a vendor, and use the buyback tab to buy it back. This will update the quest.
1. Return to Prophet Bainyn Fairwind with the requested components needed to make your cure

### Notes on getting to Edgewater Drains

1. From the the [[Commonlands]], enter the sewer to [[The Thieves' Way]] at {{waypoint -1251, 83.82, 119.69}}, then to [[The Serpent Sewer]] at {{waypoint 23, 0, 60}}, then to the [[Edgewater Drains]] at {{waypoint -243, 2, 13}} .
1. Alternative route: via the [[Commonlands]], [[The Sprawl]], [[The Serpent Sewer]], then to [[Edgewater Drains]].
   - You can use the bell on the docks to get to the Sprawl.
