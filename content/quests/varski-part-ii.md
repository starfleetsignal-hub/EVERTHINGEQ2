## Steps

1. Go to [[Fallen Gate]] and kill [[A tormented dragoon|tormented dragoons]] at [[Little Neriak]] around {{waypoint 179, 18, -103}} till you get *an etched circlet*
1. Kill [[A blighted denizen|blighted denizens]] around {{waypoint 179, 18, -103}} or {{waypoint 103, 6, -80}} till you get *a quartz rod*
1. Kill [[A tormented battlemage|tormented battlemages]] around {{waypoint 175, 19, -114}} till you get *a spinning glass orb*
1. Speak with [[Varski]] in [[West Freeport]]

## Rewards

- At least 54s
