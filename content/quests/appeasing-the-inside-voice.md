## Notes

- You have to wait a few minutes after you finished the [[Thekror's Pilgrimage]] before you can start this quest

## Steps

1. Kill 'Siren Icemaiden' and 'Icebound Minions' around the corner from quest giver - need to kill 13. Location {{waypoint -151, 7.25, -109}}
1. Return to posessed [[Thekror]] at {{waypoint -195, 7, -100}}
1. Speak to him again, he completes quest
   - *says you should be able to fly now, gives you next quest and asks you to meet him up in Hoarfang Ridge when you're done.*

## Rewards

- [[Draconic Tri-Rune Stone]] house item
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- Abiltiy to Fly in [[Western Wastes]]
