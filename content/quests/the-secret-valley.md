## Steps

1. Speak to [[Marcus]]
   - He is found in a little valley immediately southwest of [[The Ruins of Val`marr]]. {{waypoint 670, 0, 430}}
1. Harvest the following from shrubbery in [[The Commonlands]]:
   - 5 Murdunk Orange
   - 5 Antonican Coffee Bean
   - 5 Black Tea Leaf
   - You must keep the tangible items in your inventory until turn in, they are not 'Quest Update' virtual items.
1. Return to Marcus to receive a [[Potion of Earthwalking]].

## Rewards

- [[Potion of Earthwalking]] (Grants 1-time access to [[Valley of the Rogue Magi]] when used)
- The ability to purchase additional [[Potion of Earthwalking|Potions]] from Marcus
