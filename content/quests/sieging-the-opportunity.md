## Steps

1. Examine [[A satchel of siege components]] in your inventory and pull out some [[Ballista Mechanism|mechanisms]], then craft 9 [[Enhanced Ballista Mechanism|Enhanced Ballista Mechanisms]] at the forge {{waypoint 3620, -1016, 1673}}. Requires 9 [[Rhenium ore]], 9 [[Effulgent coal]] and the mechanisms (the [[Recipe: Enhanced Ballista Mechanism|recipe]] auto-scribes)
1. Speak to Grant
