## Steps

1. Enter [[Forlorn Gist]] from [[Mahngavi Wastes]] at (quest updates when you click the gate) {{waypoint 604, 143, 543}}.
1. Find a villager, [[Marjeta Vasilescu]] {{waypoint -309, 14, 97}}, and speak to her.
1. Return to [[Mahngavi Wastes]] to collect items:
   - 13 **Trusik Artifacts** (clay pots) from [[Ghastly Fate Point]] and Spires {{waypoint -202, 102, 65}} around the [[A forgotten watcher|forgotten watchers]]. Some possible locations:![Trusik Artifact Pot](images/GHASTLYFATEARTIFACT.png)
     - {{waypoint -306.13, 123.19, -55.57}}, {{waypoint -317.51, 123.61, -49.16}}, {{waypoint -353.88, 123.01, -37.87}}, {{waypoint -317.34, 118.90, -68.33}}, {{waypoint -335.94, 111.36, 46.77}}
     - {{waypoint -304.30, 108.62, 44.63}}, {{waypoint -320.71, 117.96, 90.25}}, {{waypoint -245.44, 105.42, 64.21}}, {{waypoint -240.83, 103.42, 98.15}}, {{waypoint -224.29, 96.04, 114.86}}
     - {{waypoint -242.00, 102.19, 78.37}}
   - Gather 13 **Trusik Artifacts** (vases) from [[Shattered Monolith Cay]] {{waypoint -315.11, 44.85, -729.26}} or [[Accursed Chantry Island]] {{waypoint -653.39, 26.11, -465.54}}. Some possible locations:![Trusik Artifact Vase](images/Trusik_Artifact_Bowl.png)
     - {{waypoint -622.11, 26.25, -254.27}}, {{waypoint -658.65, 19.00, -315.98}}, {{waypoint -732.77, 26.18, -348.86}}, {{waypoint -687.29, 21.80, -321.32}}, {{waypoint -626.99, 18.12, -403.63}}
     - {{waypoint -653.39, 26.11, -465.54}}, {{waypoint -291.62, 37.59, -752.48}}, {{waypoint -300.99, 37.60, -755.64}}, {{waypoint -320.07, 39.21, -750.32}}, {{waypoint -249.02, 31.27, -817.05}}
     - {{waypoint -258.98, 32.89, -841.03}}, {{waypoint -245.41, 33.36, -870.84}}
1. Return to [[Marjeta Vasilescu]] at {{waypoint -309, 15, 97}}.
1. Seek out a member of the Ahroun pack of werewolves, [[Kurtis Scarson]] {{waypoint -493, 2, -179}}
   - You **must have completed** his quests, [[Altar Access Quest]] and [[V Fur Vendetta]] for this step.
1. Speak to [[Kurtis Scarson]] at {{waypoint -493, 2, -177}} about entering the castle and he gives you [[Ahroun Pack Pelt]]
1. Seek out one of Mayong's elite Libant vampiric guards, [[Tavian Faust]], at {{waypoint -138, 19, 365}}.

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 58508 status
