This quest is part 5 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Dark Elf Series.

## Steps

- Speak to [[Custodian Zaddar Sullissia]] in [[The Graveyard]]. The entrance to the Graveyard is at {{waypoint 53, 0, -20}} in Longshadow Alley, near the Thieves' Way entrance.

Zaddar is the starting point of the [[Freeport Districts Timeline]], beginning with [[The Door Knocker]].
