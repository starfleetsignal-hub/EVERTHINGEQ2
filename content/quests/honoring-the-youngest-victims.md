## Notes

- can be done while on [[Scars of Destruction: Charnel Scarring]]
- *Quest does not appear until after signature quest [[Scars of Destruction: Running Up That Hill]] has been completed.*

## Steps

1. zone into  [[Western Wastes: Charnel Scar (Solo)|Western Wastes: Charnel Scar [Solo]]] zone in at {{waypoint 217, 65, 402}} and collect the following
   - Collect 12 wyrm egg remains located on the sides of the path leading to the Necropolis
     - ![Wyrm Eggs requiring two clicks to get update.](images/WyrmEggs.png.png)Click on the stone-encased wyrm egg to remove its stone shell then harvest the Wyrm egg
     - {{waypoint 87, 03, 366}}  39.39 -1 355 87 {{waypoint -172, -30, 344}} {{waypoint -188, -30, 434}} {{waypoint -303, -31, 346}} {{waypoint -375, -29, 407}} {{waypoint -395, -25, 430}} {{waypoint -447, -30, 454}} {{waypoint -267, -31, 501}} {{waypoint -532, -24, 518}} {{waypoint -515, -19, 484}} {{waypoint -495, -19, 526}}
   - Collect 4 [[Wyrmling Bones]] and throw them into a vent like the one  located at {{waypoint -44, 8, 347}}
     - *Tne [[Wyrmling Bones]] are on the ground and can be gathered*
1. Return to [[Dipper]] at {{waypoint 80.73, 8.85, 599.26}} in the [[Western Wastes]]

## Rewards

- [[Bronze Wyrm Egg]] house item
- 50 x [[Scarred Dinar]]
- 5 x [[Velium Drachma]]
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
