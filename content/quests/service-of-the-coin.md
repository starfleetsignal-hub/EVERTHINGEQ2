## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- You must have +10,000 faction with the Court of the Coin to unlock this quest. Once +40,000 faction has been achieved, this quest will no longer be available.

## Steps

1. Acquire 5 weapons from Anaz Mal fighters, (i.e. [[An Anaz Mal mauler]], [[An Anaz Mal scrapper]], etc.)
1. Acquire 5 rings from [[An Alliz Raef Ew lookout|Alliz Raef Ew lookouts]].
1. Acquire 5 sashes from [[A dervish cutthroat (Sinking Sands)|dervish cutthroats]].
1. Return to [[Majah L'Asi]].

## Rewards

- At least 12g 57s 51c
- -3000 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -3000 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- +3000 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
- After the fifth time: [[Court of the Coin Tapestry]]
- After the eight time: [[Court of Tears Tapestry]]
