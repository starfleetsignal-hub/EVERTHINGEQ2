## Description

*Deep within Kralet Penumbra are dangerous creatures that have been locked away for centuries. The citizens of Maldura are sure their prisons have eroded with time, however, and will soon break free, bringing destruction and chaos to Thalumbra just as they did centuries before. I must venture into their lair and make sure they can never escape.*

## Zone

- Defeat targets within [[Kralet Penumbra: Submerged Lair (Event Heroic)]]

## Steps

- Destroy the lair's kin
  - [[Cephax Kurcan]] & [[Korphax Kurcan]]
- Slay the creatures of the elements
  - [[Icethidae]] & [[Pyromorphida]]
- Kill the submerged nightmare
  - [[Abyssral]]
