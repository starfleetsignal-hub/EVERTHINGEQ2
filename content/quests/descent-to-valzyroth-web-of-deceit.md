## Steps

1. Ride the trained pilbippi mount {{waypoint -506.30, -4.00, -209.07}} from Pellucid Mere to the Thexian Outpost.
1. Deposit the poison components {{waypoint -7.16, 8.42, -295.75}}
   - Examine the dead sentinel.
1. Find the voice nearby. [[Jackal]] {{waypoint 16.88, 19.05, -358.35}}
   - Hunt 7 rock gnawers Around {{waypoint 34.90, 6.37, -63.18}} Pearls drop on the ground. Not all have pearls.
   - Hunt 12 stone larva encased in skittering stones.
1. Return to [[Jackal]]. Climb spiral path to top of Spire of V'dal {{waypoint -481.77, 64.43, -477.56}}
1. Return to [[Jackal]]
   - Examine 3 contents of crates and chests under guise of Thexian.
   - Examine altar to Innoruuk under guise of Thexian.
   - Stave next to Jackal is illusion.
   - Crates and altar around {{waypoint 93.26, 23.22, -310.50}}
1. Return to Jackal. Speak with Jackal again. You don't have to travel thru the nest go up the pathway 219, 41,-61 . Go up around the mountain
1. Progress through Scourge Spire Nest. {{waypoint 452.88, 85.51, -122.17}}  This is on top of the Mountain nest.
   - Defeat the puppeted horrors.
1. Speak with Tzarveth {{waypoint 436.66, 156.37, -177.94}}
1. Venture through Scourge Spire Nest to reach Umbrahollow Wilds {{waypoint 466.16, 109.31, -380.26}}
1. Survive Umbrahollow Wilds and Umbrahollow tulle to reach the escapee camp {{waypoint 48.15, 200.64, -563.57}} Path at 130,162,-500.
1. Speak to Baeddan.
1. Speak with Maygan. {{waypoint 45.86, 200.16, -576.35}}
1. Speak with Bilke. {{waypoint 41.27, 200.11, -576.29}}
