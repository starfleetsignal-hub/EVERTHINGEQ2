## Steps

1. Enter [[Sebilis]] and go to the [[Jail and Torture Chamber]] and kill [[A Sathirian legionnaire|Sathirian legionnaires]] and [[A chokidai mongrel|chokidai mongrels]] until you obtain [[Sebilis Cell Keys]] (auto-update quest reward). {{waypoint 29.28, -131.75, 262.49}}
1. Once you have the key, unlock the cell with the 2 [[A Reet prisoner|Reet prisoners]]. Using the [[Reet Marching Orders]] to make them follow you, escort them one at a time to the water spout at {{waypoint -29.10, -131.86, 144.39}}.
1. Return to [[Special Agent Froak]].

## Rewards

- At least 11g 5s 35c
- +1000 faction with **Synod Reet**
