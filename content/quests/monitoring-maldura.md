Brumley has sent a request via Gwynvyn to let you know he could use your assistance on a weekly basis. Speak to him directly for the details.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Brumley Langboom]] in the [[Alcove of Reason]] {{waypoint -81.41, 13.09, 195.58}}}.
1. Craft 4 [[Mana Battery]].
   - The nearest workbench is down a few steps from Brumley at {{waypoint -70.08, 11.50, 173.42}}.
1. Replace the mana batteries in the monitoring devices you placed during the quest [[Monitoring the Situation]].
   - Replace the mana battery near the [[Alcove of Reason]] {{waypoint -64.68, 9.69, 118.72}}.
   - Replace the mana battery in the north of the [[Sublevel Access Antechamber]] {{waypoint -114.65, 1.36, -42.15}}.
   - Replace the mana battery in the south of the [[Sublevel Access Antechamber]] {{waypoint -115.82, 1.36, 41.51}}.
   - Replace the mana battery near the [[Mushroom Bar and Grill]] {{waypoint -43.52, -4.22, 40.10}}.
1. Report back to [[Brumley Langboom|Brumley]].

## Needed Materials

- 4 [[Splitiron ore]]
- 12 [[Umbrite]]
- 12 [[Bornite nodule]]
- 20 [[Thaumic Coal]]
