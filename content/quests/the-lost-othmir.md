## Steps

1. Speak to [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)|Osh]] {{waypoint 3662, -1014, 1718}}
1. Speak to [[Wret Jarbarrel]] {{waypoint 3640, -1018, 1662}}
1. Throw the hops into Osh's incense bowl by clicking on Osh's incense bowl at {{waypoint 3662, -1015, 1716}}
1. Speak to [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)|Osh]]
1. Approach {{waypoint 4075, -1077, 1391}}, and kill the 3 [[An ulthork violator|ulthork violators]] that attack.
1. Loot [[A torn ulthork flipper]] from one of the Ulthork that attacked you.
1. Speak to [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)|Osh]] {{waypoint 3662, -1014, 1718}}
