## Steps

1. requires completing the Quest "Malicious Obsession" or requires completing the Quest "Putting Down the Walking Dead" or requires completing the Quest "Touch of the Undead"
1. Maldyn wants you to kill 11 [[Undead ranger]] in the area to find Maldyn's bow.
1. Return to [[Maldyn Greenburn|Maldyn]] to receive your reward.
