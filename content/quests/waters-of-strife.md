## Steps

You receive [[A Blessed Amphora]] from Lepethida

1. Pour the blessed waters into the etherflow pool
   - Travel to the etherflow pool {{waypoint -115, 198, 80}}, right click and use the Blessed Amphora from your inventory.  When you do, [[Rippling Etherrage]] attacks - kill it to update the quest
1. Return to Lepethida

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Vesspyrian Researcher's Table]]
