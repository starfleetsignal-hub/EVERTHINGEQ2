## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[A Cavemaw grub hunter|Cavemaw grub hunters]] outside the cave to the south ({{waypoint -760, -7, -228}}
1. Collect 5 Cavemaw grubs from the grub-rich soil in the same place.
1. Return to [[Magnon Baelvurn]].

- At least 5s 67c
- +90 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
