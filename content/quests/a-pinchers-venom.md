## Prerequisites

- You must have at least -4600 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
  - While this quest can technically be completed an infinite amount of times, it will stop being offered once your faction passes +17800 with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]]. Should your faction ever dip lower than that in the future, this quest will become available again.

## Steps

1. Obtain 8 venom sacs from [[A boneclaw pincher|boneclaw pinchers]] southeast of [[Jaled Dar's Remains]] in [[Field of Bone]]. {{waypoint -1623, -361, -1329}}
1. Deposit the items in the box back on [[The Sheet]]. {{waypoint -2666, -412, -2694}}
1. Speak with [[Bragek Joll]]. {{waypoint -2656, -412, -2702}}

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- +1,600 faction with **The Order of Rime**
