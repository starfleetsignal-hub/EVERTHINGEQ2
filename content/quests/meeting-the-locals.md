## Steps

1. Deliver the goods to the following NPCs
   - Deliver the goods to [[Caan]] {{waypoint -381, 277, 2975}} at the entrance to the caves.
   - Deliver the goods to [[Ma'lo]] {{waypoint -203, 247, 2868}} near the bottom of the ramp up to the caves.
   - Deliver the goods to [[Culi]] {{waypoint -414, 278, 3162}} in the caves.
   - Deliver the goods to [[Hui'ya]] {{waypoint -553, 295, 3107}} in the caves.
1. Return to [[Jaen Tiuni|Jaen]].

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +2000 faction with **Hua Mein**
