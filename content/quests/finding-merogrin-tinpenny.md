## Notes

This quest is given by guides and does not appear in your quest journal.

## Steps

Find Merogrin Tinpenny in [[The Mermaid's Lure]] and convince him to return to his wife on the docks so he can do chores.

## Rewards

- [[Merriment Julep]]
- No experience or AA experience
