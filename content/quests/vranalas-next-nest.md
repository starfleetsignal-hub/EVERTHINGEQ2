## Steps

1. Hunt 8 [[A Thessk shadowwatcher|Thessk shadowwatchers]].  They are found on [[Veiled Thessk]], the island directly southeast of Karak Peak.
1. Return to Vranala

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Wand of the Vesspyrian Spellweaver]] -- *Mage and Priest reward*
  - [[Vesspyrian Ammo Pouch]]
  - [[Vesspyrian Longbow]] -- *Scout reward*
