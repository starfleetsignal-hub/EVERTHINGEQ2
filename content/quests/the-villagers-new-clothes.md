## Starting the Quest

### ***Prerequisite***

- *<nowiki/>*
- Lvl 50+ Tailor

## Steps

- Speak to [[Ping Ventur]]
- Harvest 5 Beaver Hides from the beaver den {{waypoint -271.14, -4.18, -107.23}}
- Harvest 5 Snake Skins from the water snake den {{waypoint -73.15, 4.08, -90.81}}
- Collect 5 flax plants by right clicking tall flowers {{waypoint -144.03, 2.15, -149.33}}
- Harvest 5 Cotton Bolls from the cotton plant {{waypoint -73.70, -1.96, 129.45}}
- Collect a small bolt of fabric by right clicking a chest {{waypoint -70.05, -2.52, 121.48}}
- Return to [[Ping Ventur]] to complete the quest.
