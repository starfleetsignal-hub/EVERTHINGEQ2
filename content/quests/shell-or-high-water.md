## Notes

- The water is deep! Mind your breath or bring a water-breathing bauble like [[Totem of the Otter]]!
- When they spawn to attack, the octopi will be close to your level so have [[Pet|pets]] and [[Mercenary Guide|mercenaries]] ready if your character is a "squishy".

## Steps

1. Find [[Miaw]] underwater {{waypoint -988, -200, -902}}
1. Speak with Miaw
1. Gather eight gold conch shells underwater near Miaw (you may be attacked by [[An inkling octoyogg]] (scales))
1. Return to Miaw at the beach {{waypoint -1030, -145, 640}}

## Rewards

- 3x [[Gold Conch Shell]]
