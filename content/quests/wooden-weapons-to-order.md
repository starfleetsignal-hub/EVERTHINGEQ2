## Steps

Craft 12 items from the random list below:

- [[Sandalwood Wand (Level 50) (Crafted)|Sandalwood Wand]]
- [[Sandalwood Longbow|Sandalwood Long Bow]]
- [[Sandalwood Fighting Baton (Level 50) (Crafted)|Sandalwood Fighting Baton]]
- [[Spirit Totem of the Cobra (Crafted)|Spirit Totem of the Cobra]]
- [[Sandalwood Bo Staff]]
- [[Indium throwing axe (Crafted)|Indium Throwing Axe]]
- [[Indium throwing dagger (Crafted)|Indium Throwing Dagger]]
- [[Indium throwing hammer (Crafted)|Indium Throwing Hammer]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

## Rewards

- At Least 1g 82s 25c
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
