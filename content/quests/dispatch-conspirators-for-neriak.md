## Notes

- **This is the version of quest specifically for citizens of [[Neriak, City of Hate]]**.
- This quest is repeatable Daily (18 hours reset)
- Must complete [[Spider Off Dead]] to get this quest
- The instance contains series of rooms with even con mobs that scale to your level, a few single up ^ mini-bosses and 2 optional single up ^ bosses at the end when quest requirements are already fulfilled.

## Steps

1. After getting quest from Tranessa speak with her again when you are ready for her to teleport you to next zone ( [[Den of Seditious Daenethians]] )
1. Once inside:
   - Slay 15 x Daenethian Teir'Dal and Arasai
   - Collect 18 weapon caches
1. Return to [[Tranessa Vexis]]

- [[Petamorph Wand: Rust Scuttler]]
