## Steps

1. Gather the following, in any order:
   - 4 pieces of decrepit ghoul flesh from [[A decrepit ghoul|decrepit ghouls]]. {{waypoint -1300, -120, -750}}
   - 8 glyphskull skeleton skulls from [[A glyphskull skeleton|glyphskull skeletons]]. {{waypoint -1223, -143, -434}}
1. Return to [[Ghassan the Trader]].

## Rewards

- At least 3g 10s
- [[Shimmering Gem of Striking]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Weapon Belt of the Desert Sword]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Weapon Belt of the Dunestalker]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Weapon Belt of the Sandmage]] (All Mages)
  - [[Weapon Belt of the Sun Priest]] (All Priests)
