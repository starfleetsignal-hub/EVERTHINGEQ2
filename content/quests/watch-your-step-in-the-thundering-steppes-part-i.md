## Steps

1. Deliver a message to [[Blerton Blumble]] at [[Dead River Docks]]. {{waypoint -474, -17, -416}}
1. Blerton gives you the sub-quest [[Blumble Blunder]]. You must complete that quest to continue this one.
1. Return to Jacques.

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
- A Forearm slot equipment based on class.
