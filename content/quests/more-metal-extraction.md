## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill and gather the following
   - In [[Smeltfield Vale]] extinguish 12 [[A scorched shambler|scorched shamblers]] **OR** remove 16 [[An alloy residue|alloy residues]]
   - Shatter 8 silver or gold metal deposits.  ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Silver_Metal_Deposit.png)
     - *Note: it's random either silver or gold, but you'll only see the ones you need for the quest*
1. Return to [[Hootensia]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- 5 [[Zimaran Ballad]]
- +1000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
- One of the following:
  - 2 [[Molted Island Gold Deposit]] - *House Item*
  - 2 [[Molted Island Silver Deposit]] - *House Item*
