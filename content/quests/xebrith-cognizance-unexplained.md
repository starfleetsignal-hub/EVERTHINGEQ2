## Note

- Players are likely to encounter [[Devouring Tendril of Living Tendon (Item)|Devouring Tendril of Living Tendon]] which gives an additional quest.

## Steps

1. kill 15 [[A nullblink scribe-eye]], [[A nullblink snoopeye]] or [[A netherpawn gravel grunt]] encounters in upper levels of [[Gardens of Industry]] around {{waypoint 151, 123, -292}}, {{waypoint 292, 98, 200}}
   - *As with many 'Seek & Destroy' missions in Rage of Cthurath expansion, this step only counts the number of 'encounters' defeated, not individual numbers of mobs themselves.*
