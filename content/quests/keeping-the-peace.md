## Steps

1. Help the Qeynos Guard track down a few mysteries happening in the city of Qeynos by delivering weapons to the Kerra and Erudites
1. Enter [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum|Starcrest Commune]] via the gate in [[South Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 695, -21, 267}}
1. Locate the three locked weapons racks. {{waypoint 762, -25, 322}}
   - Unlock each rack.
   - Collect weapons from the three racks.
1. Deliver the weapons to the unarmed groups of Kerra and Erudites.
   - {{waypoint 742, -20, 305}}
   - {{waypoint 743, -20, 264}}
   - {{waypoint 819, -26, 309}}
1. Return to [[Recruitment Officer Brandit]].

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
