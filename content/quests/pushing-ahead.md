## Notes

- Requires doing [[Free Your Mind]] from [[Assistant Shortspanner]] before attempting to pick up this quest.

## Steps

1. Place wards along the path to the dig site
   - {{waypoint 1252, 13, 1042}}
   - {{waypoint 1242, 12, 1000}}
   - {{waypoint 1236, 11, 958}}
   - {{waypoint 1227, 8, 905}}
   - {{waypoint 1200, 8, 884}}
   - {{waypoint 1158, 10, 867}}
   - {{waypoint 1116, 9, 879}}
   - {{waypoint 1071, 9, 894}}
   - {{waypoint 994, 11, 903}}
   - {{waypoint 950, 20, 943}}
1. Find [[Digmaster Hidswinn (Tranquil Sea)|Digmaster Hidswinn]] at [[The D.I.R.T.Y. Dig]] {{waypoint 1105, 40, 1058}} - left click him
1. Go to the top of the hill {{waypoint 1147, 74, 937}} and set off Hidswinn's flare (left click the black sparkles)
1. Locate [[Researcher Aillena Belzia|Aillena]] - she's at the bottom of The D.I.R.T.Y. Dig {{waypoint 1080, 7, 1106}}
1. Speak to Aillena

## Rewards

- At least 5p 50g
- 2 [[Turquoise Mushroom Cluster]]
