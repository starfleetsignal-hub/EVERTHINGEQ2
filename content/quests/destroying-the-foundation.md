## Steps

1. Interact with the two digging machines, both found on the third floor:
   - DDBD-2830-A {{waypoint -10, 38, -80}}
   - DDBD-2830-B {{waypoint 50, 38, -80}}
1. Slay [[Fulkoir Haggleton (Caves)|Fulkoir Haggleton]] found near the Rockpaw camp. {{waypoint 43, 38, -109}}
1. Return to Emma Torque for your reward.
