## Steps

1. Harvest a [[Griffin eggs|griffin egg]]. They are ground spawn and [[Tabable|tabable]]. Search the area to the North of the road from [[N'Marr's Ascent Station]] {{waypoint -448, 75, -1295}} to the [[Headstone Walkway]], or the shore around [[Bone Lake]].
   - **NOTE:** There are more places where the eggs spawn, but these places have guaranteed spawn. Another one of the *more* reliable spawns is on the west side of Bone Lake under the non-aggro Treants!! One confirmed spawn point is {{waypoint 600, 59, -1375}}.
1. Take the egg to [[A griffin tamer]] while it is still fresh. You have 5 minutes to complete this step or the egg will spoil.
   - **NOTE:** This step is made easy if you opted for searching Bone Lake, as there is a Griffin Tamer on the shore!!
1. Hail the [[A griffin tamer|griffin tamer]] to complete the quest and receive your reward

## Rewards

- This quest is now optional after travel updates in 2010 and now only rewards experience. The old reward (below) is retained here for historical purposes.
- Permission to use any of the [[Griffin Tower|Griffin Towers]] in [[Nektulos Forest]]
