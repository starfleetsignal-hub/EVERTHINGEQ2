## Steps

1. Slay 10 [[A kelp horror|kelp horror]]s. {{waypoint 498, -52, 366}}
   - These roam on the ocean floor just offshore, often within range of dry land. Unlike other underwater creatures these will chase you onto the shore without breaking.
1. Return to Mess Sergeant Akseltz

## Rewards

- One of the following rings, based on Class:
  - [[Zekian Emblem of Distrust]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Zekian Crest of Distrust]] (All Mages)
  - [[Zekian Band of Distrust]] (All Priests)
  - [[Zekian Loop of Distrust]] (All Scouts)
