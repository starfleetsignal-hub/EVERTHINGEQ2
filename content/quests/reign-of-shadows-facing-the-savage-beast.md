## Steps

1. Hunt grimlings to gather 8 [[Savior Tashakhi icon|Savior Tashakhi icons]]. ![Khati Sha's Acrylia Phylactery in the Ruins of Shar Makhdi](images/Khati_Sha's_Acrylia_Phylactery.jpg)
   - Click the **aeromanced transport** disk east of [[Tiesh]] at {{waypoint -585, 271, -809}} to descend to [[Grimling Forest]]. ![Khati Sha's Acrylia Phylactery at Skullcracker Isle](images/Khati_Sha's_Acrylia_Phylactery_at_Skullcracker_Isle.jpg).
   - Kill [[A grimling guard|grimling guards]] and [[A grimling bloodsword|grimling bloodswords]] in the grimling camps in [[Grimling Forest]] {{waypoint -425, 60, -631}} for eight semi-rare small chest drops containing a [[Savior Tashakhi icon]].
1. Click the **The Altar of Tashakhi** in the [[Dartspitter Camp]] {{waypoint -630, 37, -525}}.  ![Khati Sha's Acrylia Phylactery at Smitetooth Camp](images/Khati_Sha's_Acrylia_Phylactery_at_Smitetooth_Camp.jpg).
1. Defeat the attacking [[A Tashakhi priest|Tashakhi priests]].
1. Return to [[Tiesh]] {{waypoint -385, 287, -890}}.  You can use the **aeromanced transport** disk {{waypoint -738, 40, -647}} or Evac to the beginning of the zone to quickly return to Tiesh.
1. Speak with [[Head Scholar Nabihan]], who has appeared near you {{waypoint -378, 287, -888}}.
1. Enter the [[Cadaverist Caves]] at {{waypoint -911, 71.5, -417.3}}.
1. Search for [[Khati Sha the Twisted]] {{waypoint -1130, 70, -351}}
1. Use [[Nabihan's Homing Rune Crystal]] to summon [[Head Scholar Nabihan]] and start the fight.
   - Click on [[Head Scholar Nabihan]] before the fight to reduce the large amount damage dealt to you.
   - Defeat [[Khati Sha the Twisted]] and click on his remains. Note: You must be right up on him to spawn the fight.
1. Collect Khati Sha's Phylacteries...
   - Steal Khati Sha's first phylactery from the [[Ruins of Shar Makhdi]] {{waypoint -271, 56, -616}}
   - Steal Khati Sha's second phylactery from [[Skullcracker Isle]] {{waypoint 38, 31, -116}}
     - *The grimlings in this village are Heroic. Swim around and approach from the sea side, you can reach the phylactery without fighting.*
   - To be able to pick up the last Steal Khati Sha's phylactery you have to do following side-quests in order:
     1. [[Bats All, Folks!]]: Speak to [[Maufoul]], roaming near {{waypoint -774, 33, -642}} or {{waypoint -775, 32, -653}}
     1. [[Lost Memories]]: Speak to [[Grelas the Old]], {{waypoint -836, 43, -800}} in a Hut right next to [[Maufoul]].
     1. [[Memories Are Made of This]]: Speak to [[A grimling memory]] at {{waypoint -136, 85, -691}}.
     1. Now you can pickup Steal Khati Sha's third phylactery at the Smitetooth Clan Camp {{waypoint -836, 43, -797}}.
1. Travel to [[Head Scholar Nabihan|Head Scholar Nabihan's]] rendezvous point {{waypoint -726, 35, -95}}.
1. Speak with Scout Kiira
   - *Your Mount will have been dismissed. Resummon it. You have now unlocked flying in Savage Wield*

  - [[Tenacious Polypore Fiber Handguards]]
  - [[Tenacious Polypore Leather Gloves]]
  - [[Tenacious Polypore Plate Gauntlets]]
