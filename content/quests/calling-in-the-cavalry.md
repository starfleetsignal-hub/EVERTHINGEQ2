## Steps

1. Blow the horn that you received near {{waypoint -25, -60, 130}}.
1. Kill 15 [[An exiled bandit aggressor|exiled bandit aggressors]], also nearby.
1. Speak with [[Thalin Trueguard]] in [[Omen's Call]]. {{waypoint 221, -60, -269}}
