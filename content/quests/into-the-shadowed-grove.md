## Steps

1. Enter the [[The Obelisk of Blight]], and read the following scrolls:
   - Security alert scroll on the table in the room you first zone into. {{waypoint 139, -14, 26}}
   - Infiltration memo scroll on the table in the second room with all the shadowed men. {{waypoint 89, -12, -38}}
   - Hierarchal Information scroll in the room with the first named eyeball on the floor next to the platform in the middle of the room. {{waypoint 24, -5, 48}}
   - Target Prime scroll rests on the table in the middle. {{waypoint -54, -5, 44}}
   - Draconic Implantation Device scroll his hidden in the back of the room, behind a chest to the left of the key. {{waypoint -78, -6, 39}}
   - Draconic Implementation Device scroll in the back right side of the room with [[Axxyk'Tuur]] {{waypoint -51, -5, -79}} on the ledge.
- Return to [[Aleta Rannikko]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]] for reward.
