## Steps

1. Kill 10 soulless zombies. [[A soulless assistant|Soulless assistants]] and [[A soulless resurrector|resurrectors]] will work.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 soulless zombies.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 soulless zombies.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 shadowed creatures. Any shadowed man will work: [[A shadowed acolyte]], [[A shadowed deconstructor]], [[A shadowed evocator]], and so on.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 shadowed creatures.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 shadowed creatures.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 [[A disciple of shadow|disciples of shadows]].
   - "Disciples of Shadows" are lamia located at the very bottom of the Obelisk in [[The Aethenaeum of Shadows]] Room {{waypoint -480, 392, 233}}.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 disciples of shadows.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 disciples of shadows.
1. Read the book.
