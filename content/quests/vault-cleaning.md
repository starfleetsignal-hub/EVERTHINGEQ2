## Description

I must enter the Outer Vault and slay the malefic extrusions and a giant elemental named Crag. Doing this will make it safer for others to enter the Outer Vault to study the Vault of the Underfoot.

## Steps

   - Kill 5 [[A malefic extrusion|malefic extrusion]]s
   - Kill [[Crag]]
1. Return to Hafiz

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
