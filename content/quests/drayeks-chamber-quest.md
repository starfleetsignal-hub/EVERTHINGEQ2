## Notes

- This is the former access quest for an instance within [[Permafrost]], [[Drayek's Chamber]]. It's completion is no longer necessary to enter the instance, however it does offer some back-story to the dungeon.
- You must be able to speak [[Krombral]] to start the quest.
- Although marked 'Epic' in the [[Quest Journal]], there are no epics involved.

## Steps

1. Slay five named giants in [[Permafrost]]:
   - [[Commander Keg]] on top of the western tower {{waypoint 85, 25, -70}} or in the courtyard {{waypoint -99, 0, -130}}
     - Has huge knockback; fight inside or with back to wall
   - [[Terook Skullsplitter]] atop the eastern tower {{waypoint -86, 26, -96}}
   - [[Kerhn Chillwind]] on the middle balcony {{waypoint 0, 41, -236}}
   - [[Ador Frigidbeard]] on the top floor of the keep {{waypoint 0, 60, -271}}
   - [[Gyrok Deepfreeze (Named)|Gyrok Deepfreeze]] also on the top floor {{waypoint 0, 60, -385}}
     - Has huge knockback; fight with back to the door
1. Return to [[Krindal]] in Everfrost.

## Rewards

- Experience only
