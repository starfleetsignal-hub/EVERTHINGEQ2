## Steps

![a note](images/Quest_Court_of_Truth_Confrontation_a_note.jpg)![Truth Dagger](images/Truth_dagger.jpg)

1. Enter [[Asadel Yasurbah's Residence]] {{waypoint 125, 144, 102}} and speak to [[Asadel Yasurbah]].
1. Examine the note on the ground near [[The Arena (Instance)|The Arena]]. {{waypoint -75, 178, 27}}
1. Read [[Hastily scribbled orders]] from your inventory.
1. Speak to [[Baruuq]] in [[Maj'Dul]]. {{waypoint -265, 13, 16}}
1. Go to [[The Pillars of Flame]] and pick up a dagger from the ground around {{waypoint 256, -109, -397}}. (very small, sticking out of the ground. zoom in)
   - Other possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 180, -108, -415}}
     - {{waypoint 220.76, -108.88, -402.13}}
     - {{waypoint 169, -107, -418}}
     - {{waypoint 182, -106, -435}}
1. Return to Baruuq.
1. Speak to [[Fasha the Alchemist]] in [[The Sinking Sands]]. {{waypoint -236, -103, -500}}
1. Kill [[A Spiritbound apothecary|Spiritbound apothecaries]] in [[The Clefts of Rujark]] until you get a quest update. {{waypoint -61, 8, -273}}
1. Return to Baruuq.
1. Find the four pieces of the written agreement. They can spawn in several areas around certain locations:
   - Lower Right: [[Outcasts' Ledge]]: {{waypoint -157.46, 138.35, 106.31}}, {{waypoint -149, 149, 143}}, {{waypoint -185, 138, 186}}, {{waypoint -109, 148, 136}}, {{waypoint -117, 138, 146}}
   - Upper Left: Between [[The Golden Scepter]] and the nearby [[Guard Tower]]: {{waypoint 151.63, 139.82, -110.26}}, {{waypoint 20, 139, -119}}, {{waypoint 45.46, 139.08, -35.80}}, {{waypoint 128, 139, -48}}, {{waypoint 64.96, 139.38, -18.27}}, {{waypoint 30.05, 138.92, -57.53}}
   - Upper Right: Around [[Library of Light]] and [[The Tower of the Moon]]: {{waypoint -236.87, 160.66, -155.42}}, {{waypoint -187, 177, -130}}, {{waypoint -181, 160, -142}}, {{waypoint -199.5, 160.9, -104}}, {{waypoint -152.57, 177.25, -131.63}}
   - Lower Left: [[Champions' Court]]: {{waypoint 50.45, 136.65, 215.26}}, {{waypoint -50.33, 149.62, 178.90}}, {{waypoint 29, 149, 107}}, {{waypoint 64.71, 137.49, 180.69}}, {{waypoint 16, 137, 153}}, {{waypoint -43.30, 137, 216}}, {{waypoint -87, 149, 152}}, {{waypoint 44, 136, 160}}, {{waypoint -73.20, 149.59, 101.8}}
1. Read the completed agreement, [[Baruuq's Voucher]].
1. Enter [[Asadel Yasurbah's Residence]] {{waypoint 125, 144, 102}} and speak to Asadel Yasurbah. Upon completing his dialogue, a ring event will start that ends with you having to kill Asadel, and the quest will complete.

  - +4000 faction with **The Court of Truth**
