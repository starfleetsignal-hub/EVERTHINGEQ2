## Steps

1. Gather [[Ancient brick]]s in [[Ryjesium Lake]] and next to [[Lakeshore Ruins]]. {{waypoint 569, -23, -51}}
1. Go to [[Teren's Grasp]] and try to sell the bricks to the following NPCs:
   - [[Grave]] {{waypoint 2214, 480, -839}}
   - [[Drazden]] {{waypoint 2054, 520, -981}}
1. Locate and burn the following communiques, which appear as clipboards attached the side of trees or walls:
   - Crush and Swill Communique: {{waypoint 692, 286, -1133}}
   - Drolvarg Communique: {{waypoint 878, 23, -375}}
   - Drachnid Communique: {{waypoint 197, 62, 117}}
   - Wyvern Communique: {{waypoint -124, 36, 109}}
   - Krassik Communique: {{waypoint -193, 81, -561}}
   - Seeping Ruins Communique: {{waypoint -1171, 13, -538}}
1. Locate and destroy [[A listening crystal|listening crystals]]:
   - [[Gra'al Shul Temple]], behind a brazier in the back right corner of the Temple: {{waypoint -2041, 93, -171}}
   - [[Varis]], in the room with [[Commander Vask]]: {{waypoint -522, 173, 40}}
   - [[Ru'Kaus]], on the top floor ledge, outside the middle window: {{waypoint 157, 111, 236}}
   - [[Den of the Widow Mistress]], southwest corner of the first chamber to the right: {{waypoint 478, 64, 176}}
1. Return to Grave and Drazden.
1. Go to the following locations, speak with the leader of each camp, and dispose of them all:
   - [[Ambassador Raflin]], Freedom Ambassadors camp: {{waypoint 130, 8, -965}}
     - This will cause faction loss with the Freeport Militia.
   - [[Toad]], Jaded Shrouds camp: {{waypoint 397, 115, 341}}
     - This will cause faction loss with the Green Hoods.
1. Return to Grave and Drazden.
   - Both of them warn you that they have heard that an assassin is looking for you. Grave tells you the assassin may be found skulking around [[Dreg's Landing]]. Drazden tells you he doesn't know where he is, but does know his name is Leevan.
   - Speak with [[Anda]] for the following quest. {{waypoint 1031, 164, -470}}
