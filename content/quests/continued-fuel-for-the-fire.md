I need to gather some Cobalt Chiori to help feed the New Combine troops at the Salt Breeze encampment.

## Steps

1. I need to gather 10 Cobalt Chiori to include in the Iron Rations being made for the soldiers.![cobalt chiori](images/Cobalt_chiori.png)
1. I need to return the Cobalt Chiori to [[Amli]] at the Salt Breeze New Combine encampment.

## Rewards

- At least 75g
- 2 [[A Mysterious Blue Coin]]
