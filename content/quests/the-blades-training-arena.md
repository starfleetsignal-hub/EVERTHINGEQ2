*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Win ten rounds of combat in the arena. Speak with [[Fight Master Shaarka]] after every round.
   - Although some of the mobs carry the names of Blades or Truth, there are no faction hits (or tokens)
   - All mobs are solo. The final opponent will be level+2^
   - The quest ends with the death of the final opponent

## Rewards

- Update to [[Training Arena]]
