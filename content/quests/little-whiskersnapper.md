## Steps

Requires Serilian Language to talk to Snippy

1. talk to the lazy giant at {{waypoint -703.12, 138.68, 578.98}}
1. get 20 [[Stoneleer wing meat]] from the stoneleer
1. return to the lazy giant
1. talk to whippers at {{waypoint -637.25, 130.96, 612.49}}
1. gather 30 frontier berries along Giant's Walk - "frontier berries bush" can be found close to trees
1. return to whippers at {{waypoint -637.25, 130.96, 612.49}} but he's gone
1. speak to the lazy giant
1. find whippers at {{waypoint -5.67, 102.38, 747.78}} in Wurmscale Barrens
1. gather a magma slug near the lava river above
1. return to whippers at {{waypoint -5.67, 102.38, 747.78}} in Wurmscale Barrens
1. return to [[Snippy Whiskersnapper]] at {{waypoint -860.54, 224.92, 482.90}}

## Rewards

- 5 [[Encrusted metal key]]
- At least 1p 53g 9s
