## Description

"Bobble Whirlwidget is charged with building a secret ship for the overlord. Word has it that the Wobblecog family of Qeynos is on the verge of perfecting a ship that travels underwater. The Overlord mustn't let Qeynos gain the upper hand. Bobble is researching a creature called the nautilus to try and recreate the methods it uses to become bouyant in the sea. I need to search Sandstalker Bay for these nautili." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Collect 5 nautilus shells from [[Sandstalker Bay]].
   - These shells are in the water, not on the beach.
   - You don't have to dive to see these.
1. Return to Bobble Whirlwidget.

## Rewards

- At least 3s 35c
- [[Nautilus Shell Earring]]
