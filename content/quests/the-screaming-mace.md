## Notes

- This quest is also part of the [[Temple of Cazic-Thule Timeline]].
- The respawn rate of Agony is approximately 17 minutes if you kill all three named thought bleeders. However, if you just kill Agony and leave at least one of the other two named thought bleeders alive, the respawn time is faster (around 5 minutes). Therefore, if you need the journal for more than one character, kill Agony and not the rest of the encounter.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]] in [[The Feerrott]]. {{waypoint -1701, -8, 820}}
1. Collect the blood of the four:
   - [[Hukulan the Despoiler]] {{waypoint 47.5, 24, -25}}
   - [[Kaxor the Cenobite]] {{waypoint -55, 41, -107}}
   - [[Xilarga the Tae Ew Champion]] {{waypoint 56, 40, -108}}
   - [[Tzugaax the Torturer]] {{waypoint -38, -1, 2}}
   - Kill 40 lizardmen in the Temple of Cazic-Thule.
1. Find the [[Fountain of Fear]]. {{waypoint 0, 27, -231}}
   - Enter the bottom section of the fountain for the update.  This starts a 1-hour timer.
   - Move to the upper section of the fountain, halfway up the temple, and attack the oozes to start the ring event that will spawn [[The Acidic Mass]].
1. Within the time limit, find and examine the [[Basin of Bara'Kur]]. {{waypoint 62, 2, -7}}
   - You will receive a [[Congealed Blood Sphere]].
1. Kill [[A Thulian terrorfiend|Thulian terrorfiends]] for a key (auto-update).
   - These share spawns with the other Thuilian amygdalans. Some possible spawns are {{waypoint 22.10, 0.88, 22.15}}, {{waypoint -71, -20, -100}}, {{waypoint -0.68, -5.50, -81.26}}, or {{waypoint 66, -19, -109}}
1. Use the key to enter a special instance of [[The Sanctum of Fear]] called [[The Chamber of the Keeper]]. {{waypoint -18, -10, -173}}
1. Kill [[Gorfuzod the Keeper]]. See [[The Chamber of the Keeper#Walkthrough|Chamber of the Keeper Walk-through]] for a detailed explanation on how to summon him.
1. Once you have killed the Keeper, the [[Screaming Mace]] spawns above the table at the back of the room. Claim it to complete the quest.
