## Steps

You will need 25 [[Smoldering kindling]] for fuel, available for purchase in Grobb from [[Potabak]] or [[Thomryp]]

1. You will receive 5 [[Swamp spice|Swamp spices]], 5 [[Fens sifter spore|Fens sifter spores]], 15 [[Swamp rat|swamp rats]], and the recipe "[[Zubzub's favorite snack]]."
   - Scribe the recipe *[[Zubzub's favorite snack]]*.
1. Craft 10 [[Spiced swamp rat]] with the ***plate of spicy rats*** recipe at a Stove and Keg (if you make a mistake and are short on ingredients, just ask Zubbub for more).
1. Turn in the plates of spicy rats to [[Zubzub]].

## Rewards

- [[Zubzub's Spiced Swamp Rats]]
- At least 12g 50s
- +5000 faction with **Clan Grobb**
