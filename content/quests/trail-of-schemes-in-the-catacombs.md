## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 80+

## Steps

1. Investigate the catacombs beneath the Qeynos sewers.
   - The nearest entrance is the sewer grate in [[Elddar Grove]]. {{waypoint 518, -17, -397}}
   - Enter [[Vermin's Snye]] via the sewer grate in the [[Down Below]]. {{waypoint -71, 0, -110}}
1. Head towards the [[Crypt of Betrayal]] in the northeast corner of the tunnels at {{waypoint 34, -1, -194}}. You'll be ambushed by two [[A Bloodsaber assailant|Bloodsaber assailants]] and [[A Bloodsaber assassin]]. At lower levels, this may be a harder fight than usual for the questline.
1. When you've killed the attackers, you find a [[Blood for Blood Ceremony Scroll]].
1. Return to [[Daylia the Vigil (Qeynos Province District)|Daylia the Vigil]].

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c (at level 92)
- At least 1p 25g (at level 98)
- [[Statue: Bayle Nobility]]
