## Steps

1. Talk to Kwa {{waypoint 783, -21, -94}}.  He has received word of Ogres in [[The Down Below]] from [[Rrak Boldfist]].
1. Talk to [[Rrak Boldfist]] {{waypoint 706, -1, -141}}.
   - Head northeast to [[The Repository]].
   - Inside, Rrak tells you that he saw what must have been an Ogre hunting in [[The Down Below]].  It was not too far from the [[Castleview Hamlet]] entrance.
1. Return to Kwa and tell him what you have learned.  Kwa asks you to investigate.
1. Enter [[The Down Below]], just West from Kwa.  The entrance grate {{waypoint 792, -21, -97}} is right next to [[Vindain Greenfaith]], the High Elf mentor.  Swim over to the stairs.  After climbing the stairs, turn left towards the entrance of the Down Below.  This is also a good time to pick up the quest [[Goods Down Below]] from [[Falin Shaloon]] {{waypoint -196, 0, -200}}.  Head to the portal at the top of the stairs to [[The Baubbleshire]].  Just before, there is a ***pack*** {{waypoint -158, 0, -174}} on the floor outside the first alcove.  Click on it to find the ***Ogre satchel***.
1. Return to Kwa.  The grate back to [[Castleview Hamlet]] is directly under the entrance to the catacombs in the water.  Click on the grate.  Speak with Kwa. He examines it and sees the mark of a Qeynosian citizen that betrayed Freeport.  He will return the items.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 10c
- [[Guktan carryall]] - 5 slots
