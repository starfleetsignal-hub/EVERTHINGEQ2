## Important

Unlike the other tradeskill missions, the zone used by this quest never had its timer changed to 90 minutes. Once you've completed this quest, you will be locked to that instance for 18 hours. If you have already done this quest today, and you are given the quest again, you can decline it (or delete it if you've already accepted it), wait a while and return. You should be offered a new mission for a different zone.

## Steps

1. Speak to the shipbuilding foreman about getting the job back on schedule, and provide assistance as instructed.

*Travel to [[The Shipyard Cove]] (In [[The Commonlands]], close to [[Bloodskull Valley]]) {{waypoint 62, -41, 717}}. For speedy travel, buy charms from the NPC, [[Koros Splinterlimb]], in the Isle of Mara. He sells single-use, swift travel charms called [[Mission Stone of Shipyard Cove]] and [[Return to Mara]] for 6g each. One will take you to the door of the tradeskill instance and the other will take you back to the docks of Isle of Mara.
*Once inside, hail [[Nyla Shipwright]] and finish her subquest [[Shipyard Building Work]]. You have the option to complete this a total of four times, however there is no longer any advantage to doing so because you are not awarded additional experience for completing the subsquests.
*The recipe book is on the table beside her; click it and scribe it before you begin crafting.
*Click on the paper next to her and keep Nyla's list of items that appears on your screen open, until everything is complete; doing so makes it easier to search for the recipes at each table. The list will show that you need to make 3 of each item, but more can be made to speed up the entire process. (See time saving tips below.)
* All materials needed to complete this subquest are located within the zone. Gather them by clicking objects (barrels, boxes, etc.) near the ship and by the cluster of crafting stations near the fire pit. Gather more than you need to complete one round of the subquest.
* To complete the subquest you will be asked to make three items on Nyla's list. If you hail Nyla and take the subquest four times, you will end up making 12 of each item on her list. Her list asks for nine different items. While this task can be carried out in this manner, there are ways to speed up the process considerably.
*You only need to get the subquest from Nyla once. Create at least 12 of each item in the list and then hail her to complete the quest. She will accept all of the completed items and construction will begin. It will take some time, because it still goes through each phase of the construction process.
***Time saving tips for solo and group missions**:
*It is recommended you complete all the items at once. Completing the subquest does not grant additional tradeskill experience. You are only awarded experience for creating all of the items and completing the quest. There is no advantage to completing the subquest four times.
*Because the difficulty of making the items is much greater at lower levels, the ideal group for this special zone is best suited to have at least one artisan from the following tradeskill subclasses: [[Outfitter]], [[Craftsman]], and [[Scholar]].
*Within these crafting instances Mass Production, a [[Tradeskill Prestige]] ability, or the item [[The Hand of the Maker]] can be used to make multiple items at one time. With mass production, these can be made in multiples of 15 at once. To complete the main quest and the subquest, 12 of each item on Nyla's list is required, but making more than needed is considerably faster compared to making each item one at a time and picking up and turning in the subquest in several rounds that asks for multiples of three.
*When you have all of the items made, then hail her and turn in the quest. You'll notice that if you click the list of needed items next to her again, you will see negative numbers for each item, meaning that you have completed all of the items. Sit back and wait; more NPCs will appear to work on the ship and when the are done and the entire quest (including the main quest from the Isle of Mara) will auto-complete. After the auto-completion, a chest will appear in the instance.
