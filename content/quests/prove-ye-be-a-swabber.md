## Notes

Make sure you use the [[Brokenskull Disguise]] you received from [[Fip Marrsquire]], otherwise you will not be able to hail [[Grak Liversplat]].

## Steps

Grak is deep in a cave. The entrance is {{waypoint 385, 70, 875}}.

This quest can be completed in two ways, depending on how you respond to [[Grak Liversplat|Grak]].

**Method One - Kill The Kittens**

1. Attack and kill 8 [[A kitten|kittens]].
1. Return to [[Grak Liversplat]].

**Method Two - Find Another Way**

1. Take the [[Simple brown sack]] from the pile inside the arena  {{waypoint 1054, 124, 708}}.
1. Use it to capture 8 [[A kitten|kittens]].
1. Return to [[Grak Liversplat]].

## Rewards

- At least 23g 11s 77c
- [[Swabber|Title: "Swabber"]]
