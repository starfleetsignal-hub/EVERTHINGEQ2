## Steps

1. Gather 8 of Zaveta's blades from the village - (non-targetable harvests that look like sparkling daggers mostly found inside the bone canopies and surrounding areas) ![](images/Blade.PNG)
1. Return to the [[Hidden Beach]] and talk to Zaveta there {{waypoint -1407, 9, 931}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- One of the Following:
  - 2 [[Electrified Adornment of Extra Attacks]]
  - 2 [[Electrified Adornment of Damaging]]
  - 2 [[Electrified Adornment of Haste]]
  - 2 [[Electrified Adornment of Heightened Power]]
