## Notes

- While you can obtain the other quests offered by [[Shaman Medicus]], this quest cannot be obtained without first completing [[Is There a Shaman in the House?]] and the rest of the [[Jarsath Wastes Timeline|Clan Skleross Timeline]].
- Before you leash the spirit bear, make sure no one else nearby is trying to leash it, as well. If you leash the bear and someone else starts the quest or tries to leash the bear, you will lose yours, even if they are not grouped with you. If you fail, hail [[Shaman Medicus]] again to respawn the projection.

## Steps

1. Use [[Medicus' spiritual leash]] on [[Shaman Medicus' spiritual projection]] and lead it to [[The Herculean Rampart]]. {{waypoint -893, 188, 344}}
   - You do not actually have to physically escort the projection. You can fly or mount over to the location and the quest will auto-update.
1. Speak with [[Lord Sheckt]] at the [[Skleross Encampment]]. {{waypoint -796, -23, -1188}}
