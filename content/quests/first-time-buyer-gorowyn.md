*Note: This quest scales with your player level **up to** level 10.*

## Steps

In order to get this quest, hail Caretaker Velshin. If your character is tall, it can be difficult to see the quest feather icon above his head.

1. Buy a room at [[Lower Tenements]] (the door near the Innkeeper) and enter it.
