## Prerequisites

Completion of the following [[Enchanted Lands]] quests:

- [[Bootstrutter's Trail Guide to Far Shire]]
- [[Bootstrutter's Trail Guide to Fay Shire]]
- [[Bootstrutter's Trail Guide to Gobland]]
- [[Bootstrutter's Trail Guide to South Mist]]

## Steps

Visit the following locations in [[Rivervale]]. Some of the names listed are the former names of the buildings, which the quest text still uses:

- [[The Jum Jum Shack]] {{waypoint -16, -18, -102}}
- [[Brushfoot's General Store|Kevlin's Gear]] {{waypoint -46, -10, -169}}
- [[Food on Foot (Rivervale)|Food on Foot]] {{waypoint -102, -25, -85}}
- [[Middlehill's General Store|Rantho's Weaponry]] {{waypoint -205, -11, -124}}
- [[The Loom|Digg's Tall n'Stout]] {{waypoint -192, -19, -13}}
- [[Cask's General Store|Vale Stables and Livestock]] {{waypoint -74, -18, 108}}
- [[The Pot of Gold]] {{waypoint -54, -24, -53}}
- [[Rivergate]] {{waypoint -193, -5, 213}}
- [[Shakey's Farm]] {{waypoint -382, 0, -273}}
- [[The Laughing Trout|Hall of the Laughing Trout]] {{waypoint -487, -1, -141}}

- At least 1g 76s 16c

  - [[Defender's Beads of the Geyser]] -- (All Fighters)
  - [[Healer's Beads of the Geyser]] -- (All Priests)
  - [[Medium's Beads of the Geyser]] -- (All Mages)
  - [[Stalker's Beads of the Geyser]] -- (All Scouts)
