## Steps

1. Go to [[Everfrost]] and harvest six pieces of the [[The Gwenavyne]]. These are found underwater. {{waypoint 237, -101, -1570}}
1. Investigate the grave of *Corsan Dushire* in the [[Village of Somborn]] in the [[Loping Plains]]. {{waypoint -452, 16, 73}}
1. Follow the [[Ghost of Corsan Dushire]] to a bandit camp at {{waypoint -451, -18, -145}} and kill [[A grave robbing bandit]].
   - You do not have to actually follow the (very) slow ghost as he walks towards the bandit camp. Once he starts walking, you can simply go straight to the camp on your mount.
1. Return to [[Sir Bayden Cauldthorn]].
1. Go to [[Smokehorn Basin]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]] and slay [[The Smokehorn Champion]]. {{waypoint -880, 60, 1531}}
1. Return to Sir Bayden Cauldthorn.

## Rewards

- [[Bayden's Altar of Mithaniel Marr]]
- Completing this quest will unlock the [[Marr's Perseverance]] and [[Valorous Service]] abilities
- +7500 faction with **Mithaniel Marr**
