## Description

Upon defeating Melanie Everling's bound soul withing Harrow's End, she returned momentarily, to thank me for the temporary respite from her eternal imprisonment within Drinal's home. She was happy to hear that her sister, Jenni, is doing well, and asked that I tell Jenni that she is fine as well. I'm not sure what to tell Jenni.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Jenni Everling]] in Obol Plains

One of the following, depending on your class:

- [[Melanie Everling's Exquisite Locket]]
- [[Melanie Everling's Striking Locket]]
- [[Melanie Everling's Flawless Locket]]
- [[Melanie Everling's Impeccable Locket]]

- You can use an open instance to talk to Melanie. However, make sure to get your butt in there ASAP because she can despawn anytime between 10 seconds and 30 minutes).
