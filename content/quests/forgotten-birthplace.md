## Steps

1. Enter [[Emperor Atrebe's Laboratory: The Fabled City of Kor-sha]] and slay 10 skeletons. {{waypoint -36, 3, -74}}
1. Upon killing the final named, [[Ka'El Ka'Vrish]], [[Dominus Rile Sathir]] will enter and speak with [[Atrebe Sathir]]. After the dialogue completes, kill [[Hightrooper Jassnik]] and [[Hightrooper Zalgix]] when they aggro. The quest will auto-complete. {{waypoint -238, -72, -171}}

## Rewards

- At least 15g 83s 87c
