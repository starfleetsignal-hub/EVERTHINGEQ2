## Background

- This is not really a quest, but is required to access the armorer's quests in the [[Tenebrous Tangle Timeline]].

## Steps

- Do the following whenever you need [[Armorer Gunbog]] if he is absent from the Hidden Shelter.
  - Kill all Scaleborns in the treehouse at {{waypoint 40, 0, 100}} on Temple Grounds island. You must kill them all before they respawn (6 minutes).
  - When no Scaleborns are alive, [[Captive froglok armorer|a captive froglok armorer]] will run out of the treehouse and down the river to the waterfall.
  - You can now find [[Armorer Gunbog]] at his spot in the [[Hidden Refuge]].

## Rewards

Access to the following quests:

- [[Restocking the Supplies: Pounders]]
- [[Restocking the Supplies: Cutters]]
- [[Restocking the Supplies: Blockers]]
