## Notes

- The start of the access questline for [[Kurn's Tower]].
- This quest becomes [[Clean Out The Caves (Repeatable)|repeatable]] on completing the next quest: [[Everyone Loves a Snitch]]

## Steps

1. Kill the following monsters in [[The Rubble Rabble Burrow]]: {{waypoint -76, -29, -94}}
   - 8 [[A Rubble-rabble burrower|Rubble-rabble burrowers]]
   - 8 [[A Rubble-rabble detector|Rubble-rabble detectors]]
1. Return to Merrik Hanz.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- +2,000 faction with **Kunzar Jungle Villagers**
