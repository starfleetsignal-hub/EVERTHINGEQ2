## Notes

This quest requires minimum level 10 crafting.<br>
**Bring with you 3 Glowing Candles and 1 [[Savanna resin]]**
![](images/Entombed_Relic_Box.png)

## Steps

1. Enter The Graveyard at {{waypoint -1394, -86, -334}} in The Commonlands.
1. Search for evidence of suspicious activity - move forward, pause by the first crypt on the right {{waypoint 22, -5, 16}} and observe two Teir'Dals walking down into that crypt.

![](images/Urn_of_the_Valorous.png)
![](images/Urn_of_the_Devoted.png)
![](images/Simple_Clay_Jug.png)
![](images/Mystic_Ash.png)
![](images/A_rust_scuttler_corpse.png)

1. Enter "Crypt of Devotion (Tradeskill)"
1. Search:
   - hidden compartments (clickable "loose bricks" and columbarium plates on walls) for 6 x artifacts
     - brick possible at {{waypoint -50, 0, -45}} and {{waypoint -77, 0, -37}}, 2 plates at {{waypoint -74, 1, -53}}, 1 compartment at {{waypoint -84, -1, -37}}.
   - grave goods (click urns, clay jugs) for 11 x artifacts
1. You will need to open door to cell at {{waypoint -77, 0, -34}} to find your last artifacts. To open door you need to:
   - pick up recipe for "corrosive muck" from jar with dusty scrolls
   - gather mystic ash from couple plates laying around (possible locations {{waypoint -68, 0, -44}} and {{waypoint -85, 1, -37}}).
   - gather "rust scuttler saliva" from couple dead bugs laying around, possible loc: {{waypoint -68, 0, -51}}, {{waypoint -80, 2, -54}}
   - you also need 1 savanna resin from Crossroads in The Commonlands and 3 Glowing Candles from any fuel merchant
   - Craft 1 corrosive muck on Alchemists' Bowl at {{waypoint -89, 0, -42}}
   - Click door at {{waypoint -77, 0, -34}} to apply muck, go inside cell and pick up any missing artifact. Loose brick at {{waypoint -70, 0, -36}}.
1. Reach upper room to search it - only after you finish all searches you can gather crypt skeleton key from wall at {{waypoint -62, 0, -30}}, then open door at the top of stairs {{waypoint -93, 8, -56}} and go inside.
   - You will get rooted in place and observe a conversation between 3 conspirators.
1. Return to Salira in The Commonlands when quest updates and you are able to move.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- [[Raised Knight Plushie]] house item
