## Steps

1. Kill 10 "small" rats.
1. Return to the [[Gigglegibber Hunt Master]]
1. Kill 10 "medium-sized" rats.
1. Return to the [[Gigglegibber Hunt Master]]
1. Kill 10 "large-sized" rats.
1. Return to the [[Gigglegibber Hunt Master]]
1. Kill the Rat "King".
1. Return to the [[Gigglegibber Hunt Master]]

## Notes

- Any rat will update any of the kill steps. The size descriptor is a joke. They can be found all over [[Norrath]].
- See the talk page for locations with high rat concentrations.
