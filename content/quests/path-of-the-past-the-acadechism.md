## Notes

[[The Acadechism]] is a level 60-68 heroic instanced zone with a success lockout of 18 hours.

The entrance is found on top of Crushbone Keep, in Greater Faydark.

To get to [[The Acadechism]], climb the wall west of Crushbone Keep at {{waypoint -1112, 124, 6}} and follow the cliff path, climbing as needed. You will find the entrance door on the roof of Crushbone Keep at {{waypoint -127, 168, -15}}.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Acadechism]] and Defeat [[Emperor D'Vinn]].
1. Return to the Chronomagi Tasker

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Chronomancers**
- Ability to purchase [[Replica: Emperor D'Vinn]] from [[Timeless Trader]] for 1p 8g
