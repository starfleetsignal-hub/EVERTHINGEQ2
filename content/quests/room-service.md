## Steps

1. Enter a [[Maj'Dul Residence]]
1. There will be 2 pets and an NPC. Kill the pets.
   - Right-click on the NPC and select a type of beating to administer.
   - ***NOTE:* It appears that if you rapidly select types of beatings over and over the chance of him kneeling increases. This is NOT confirmed!*
     - Most of the time they will say they will not tell you anything, or they die.
     - With luck, they will kneel and cough up the information you need
   - If he died or becomes unresponsive, zone out and back in and repeat the above steps until you get the info
     - The door you zone out of will be on the roof. You have to go back to the ground-level door to zone back in. There is no lockout timer.
1. Return to [[A Tears grifter]] for your reward.

## Reward

- At least3g 23s 29c
- One of the following:
  - 4-6 [[Blades insignia token|Blades insignia tokens]]
  - 4-6 [[Coin insignia token|Coin insignia tokens]]
  - 4-6 [[Truth insignia token|Truth insignia tokens]]
