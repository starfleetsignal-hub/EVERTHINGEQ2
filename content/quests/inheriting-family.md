## Notes

***To finish the entire quest line you need:***

- 15 bonded loam
- 3 diamondine cluster
- 5 white peaches
- 3 aerated mineral water
- 2 milk
- 12 luminous coal
- 12 luminous kindling

## Ingredients

Tradeskill ingredients needed for the quest:

- 4 [[Luminous Kindling]]

## Steps

1. Harvest 5 [[Nerius trout]].
1. Speak to [[Jadrek Woolfe]].
1. Gather some items to make the dish more impressive.
   - Harvest some [[Icy Fennel]] by clicking some **Icy Fennel** plants around [[Icegill Lagoon]] {{waypoint 160, -36, -1222}}  ![An Icy Fennel plant](images/Inheriting_Family_quest_-_Icy_Fennel.jpg)
   - Harvest some [[Pine Nuts]] by clicking some green pine trees in [[Hollowfrost Grove]] {{waypoint -331, 1, -212}}  ![Pine tree](images/Inheriting_Family_quest_-_Pine-Nuts.jpg)
     - When you complete harvesting you will receive [[Portable Cooking Set (no-trade)|Portable Cooking Set]] x 3
1. Craft [[Trout with Pine-Nut Dressing & Fennel Puree]].
   - *The  recipe book [[Food for Fussy Guests]] will be in your bags.*
1. Speak to [[Jadrek Woolfe]].

## Rewards

- At least 5g 10s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
