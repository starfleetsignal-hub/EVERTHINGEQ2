## Steps

1. Obtain the following items in any order:
   - 5 pieces of maple from [[Wood Nodes#T2|wind felled trees]] (requires a foresting skill of at least 20).
   - 3 tuber strands from [[Root Nodes#T2|desert roots]] (requires a gathering skill of at least 20).
   - 2 compound cams from Dervish bows from [[A Dervish cutthroat|Dervish cutthroats]].
     - Note that while the quest clue says to kill Dervish cutthroats, these updates are possible from any Dervish mob at the western Commonlands Dervish camps.
     - You can find some around these coordinates {{waypoint 1042.87, -44.35, 420.29}}
1. Take these components back to J.P. Feterman.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 67c
