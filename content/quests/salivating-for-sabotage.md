## Steps

1. Scribe the [[Othmir Notes on Wurm Saliva]] recipe you receive, and craft 3 weakened axes on a [[Forge]] {{waypoint -1147, -532, 3390}}, using the following materials:
   - 3 [[Deforester's Axe]]
   - 3 [[Wyrm Saliva]]
   - 3 [[Ethereal coal]]
1. Exchange 3 axes in the [[Flamespout Fields]] for those you crafted
   - ![exchange axe](images/Exchange_axe.jpg)Simply click on an existing axe embedded in a stump to make the exchange
1. Return to [[Livi]]

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +150 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
