## Steps

1. Visit [[The Deathfist Quarry]] - {{waypoint 342, -114, -114}} ride the elevator to the bottom and run down the ramp
1. Read the book
1. Kill 8 [[A Vallon miner|Vallon miner]]s - lowest level of the quarry
1. Read the book
1. Kill 6 [[A Vallon undersmith|Vallon undersmith]]s - lowest level of the quarry, at the ends of the side passages
1. Read the book
1. Kill 5 [[A Vallon sentry|Vallon sentries]] - {{waypoint 680, -18, -326}} guarding the [[Shadowed Shrine]] or in the road nearby {{waypoint 637, -22, -234}}
1. Read the book
1. Kill 3 [[A Vallon seer|Vallon seer]]s - inside the Shadowed Shrine
1. Read the book
1. Kill 6 [[A Vallon grunt|Vallon grunt]]s - three spots around the mill in [[Three Toes Valley]]
1. Read the book
1. Kill 6 [[A dust adder|dust adder]]s - {{waypoint 72, 11, 68}} in [[Spirit Valley]], or in [[Defiled Forest]]
1. Read the book
1. Kill 4 [[A Vallon centurion|Vallon centurion]]s - {{waypoint 355, 11, -169}} outside the mine
1. Read the book
1. Kill 8 dust adders
1. Read the book a final time

## Rewards

- [[The Far Side of Tears, Part Two (House Item)]]
