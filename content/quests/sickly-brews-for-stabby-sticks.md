## Steps

1. Gather 6 [[Bitter herb (Level 100)|bitter herbs]] (trackable, multi-harvest node) - found down the road (around {{waypoint -418, 18, -1767}}).![bitter herb](images/Bitter_herb.jpg)
1. Craft 2 poisons - which is the [[Sickly-Brew]] at the Goblin Firepit.  Each combine requires 5 [[Thaumic Coal]].
1. Speak with [[Brappa]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +4,000 faction with **Broken Toe Clan**
- +4,000 faction with **Goblins of Nurga**
