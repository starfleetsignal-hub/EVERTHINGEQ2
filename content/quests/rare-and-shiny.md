## Notes

- While you can loot more than one of the shiny Iceclad crab shells, you can only get the quest once per each time you have the [[Crustacean Critters]] quest. Additional shells can be traded.

## Steps

1. Return to [[Narp]].

  - [[Othmir Chair]]
  - [[Othmir Shiny Trinkets]]
  - [[Othmir Woven Chair]]
