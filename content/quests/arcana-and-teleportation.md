## Steps

1. Go to the teleporter to your right at {{waypoint 1919, -301, 3321}}.
1. Simply left-click the teleporter frame and the device will automatically be installed.
   - *Note: You must click on the portal FRAME, not the swirling portal.*
1. Once that is done, right-click and select "To The Hole". This will finish the quest.
1. Return to [[Alim Dabir]] and receive the next quest.

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**
