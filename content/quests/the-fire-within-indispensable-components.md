## Steps

1. Collect blackened lava chunk for fuel on the bridge at {{waypoint -106, -370, -23}}, {{waypoint -114, -373, 16}}, {{waypoint -105, -372, 66}}, {{waypoint -112, -377, 96}}, {{waypoint -16, -382, 110}}, and {{waypoint -36, -381, 52}}.
1. Collect from trash mobs you can find on the way to [[Lord Kargurak (Heroic)]]
   - 8 [[Aspect of Flame]] from [[An underworld guardian|underworld guardians]]![Aspect of Flame](images/Questitem_AspectOfFlame.jpg)*Note:  Be careful to pull them where their corpses can be looted; killing them on bridges may result in some corpses falling into the lava and thus non-lootable*
   - 8 [[Fire Purified Mercury]] from [[Guardian of the way|guardians of the way]]
   - 12 [[Fine Fire Dust Particles]] from An Elite Efreeti Defender
1. Obtain [[Redwinger's Tempered Tongs]] from [[Ayquini the Firemind (Heroic)]].![Redwinger's Tempered Tongs](images/Questitem_Redwinger's_Tempered_Tongs.jpg)
1. Obtain [[Redwinger's Tempered Flask]] from [[Lord Kargurak (Heroic)]]![Redwingers's Tempered Flask](images/Questitem_Redwinger's_Tempered_Flask.jpg)
1. Go back to **Redwinger's Metallurgic Table of Wonder** at {{waypoint -279, -516, 142}} (room of [[Ayquini the Firemind (Heroic)]]) and double-click it for your quest update.![Redwinger's Metallurgic Table of Wonder](images/Redwinger's_Metallurgic_Table_of_Wonder.jpg)
1. Obtain a breastplate from [[Qaaron the Usurper (Heroic)]]
1. Return to **Redwinger's Metallurgic Table of Wonder** and double-click it for your quest update.
1. Inspect the breastplate to get the next quest

## Rewards

- At least 47p 71g 30s 50c
