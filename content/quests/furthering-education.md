## Steps

1. Collect a gardencap mushroom near {{waypoint -460, 9, 447}}![a gardencap](images/Gardencap.jpg)
1. Use the gardencap on an ignus cultivator nearby. ![ignus cultivator](images/Ignus_cultivator.jpg)
1. Kill a [[Stalking stoneripper]] and loot a [[Silver Heart]] from its corpse.
1. Use the [[An empowered gardencap|empowered gardencap]] in your inventory to receive a [[Preserved Silver Heart]].
1. Return to [[Salindis]] in [[Khali'Yrzu]] at {{waypoint 185, 588, -567}}.
1. Click on a softly resonating crystal next to [[Salindis]].![a softly resonating crystal](images/A_softly_resonating_crystal.jpg)
1. Speak to [[Salindis]].
