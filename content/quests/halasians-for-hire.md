## Steps

1. Find [[Raffik]] in [[New Halas]] in [[Frostfang Sea]] at {{waypoint 92, 155, -176}}
1. Craft 5 [[Henchman Adornment Pack (Quest Item)|Henchman Adornment Packs]] from the [[Henchman Adornment Pack (Quest Recipe Book)|recipe]] provided.
   - 5x [[Soluble loam]]
   - 5x [[Beryllium cluster]]
   - 5x [[Succulent root]]
   - 20x [[Lambent Coal]]
     - Note: A work bench is at {{waypoint -335, 177, -25}}
1. Speak with Raffik.

## Rewards

- At least 9p 13g
- Tradeskill experience
- [[Henchman Adornment Pack (QuestReward)|Henchman Adornment Pack recipe book]]
