## Notes

A repeatable quest for Faction. Repeatable up to 3 times and only if your Port Woe faction is less than 20,000. This quest requires:

- 9 [[Larch Wood]]
- 3 [[Whittling Knife|Whittling Knives]] (Purchased from the Fuel Merchant)
- 15 [[Zimaran Sandpaper]]
- [[Recipe Scroll: Clamming Fork|Recipe]] for [[Clamming Fork|Clamming Forks]] auto scribed when accepting quest.

## Steps

1. Craft 3 [[Clamming Fork|Clamming Forks]]
1. Dig up 6 [[Cherrystone Clam|Cherrystone Clams]] on the beach at {{waypoint 1.50, 5.04, 665.39}}
1. Speak with [[Dunstad]] at {{waypoint -75.45, 6.45, 334.40}}

## Rewards

- +1,000 faction with **Port Woe**
- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
