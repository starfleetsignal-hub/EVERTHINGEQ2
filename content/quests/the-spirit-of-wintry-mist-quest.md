*This quest is repeatable up to 2 times..*

## Steps

1. Slay 4 [[A tundra terror|Tundra terrors]] in [[Guardian's Gulch]] to receive the [[The Spirit of Wintry Mist (NPC)|spirit's]] blessing.
1. Return to the spirit for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- The blessing of the [[The Spirit of Wintry Mist (NPC)|spirit]] for a step in the quest [[In Search of a Menagerie]].
