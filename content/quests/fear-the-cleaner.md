## Note

This can be done in a cleared instance (solo or heroic).

## Steps

1. Enter [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma (Solo)|Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma [Solo]]] {{waypoint 603.06, 85.41, 327.38}}
   1. Place the Southern arcane nullifier {{waypoint 751.41, 62.18, 343.49}}
   1. Place the Western arcane nullifier {{waypoint 807.92, 60.03, 294.54}}
   1. Place the Northwestern arcane nullifier {{waypoint 891.83, 55.24, 192.72}}
   1. Place the Northern arcane nullifier {{waypoint 821.52, 44.15, 133.82}}
   1. Place the Eastern arcane nullifier {{waypoint 703.65, 57.20, 268.96}}
1. Return to [[Kraskre'sh]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1,000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
- One of the following
  - [[Aviak Charm of Shaping]]
  - [[Hooluk Charm of Skyward]]
  - [[Aviak Charm of Ammolite]]
