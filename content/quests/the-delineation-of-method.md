## Notes

There is a minimum level of Tradeskill required e.g. 112 is too low.

## Materials Required

- The materials are given to you by the quest.
- 30 [[Luclizite Cluster]]
- 12 [[Humming Azurite]]
- 25 [[Radiant Algae]]
- 25 [[Coruscating Coal]]

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Hulda Flintbraid (Nexus)|Hulda Flintbraid]] {{waypoint 355.77, -36.15, 718.67}}
1. Find the ghostly blacksmiths in the [[Great Artisan Hall]] {{waypoint -66.69, -1.64, 5.14}}, of [[The Bazaar]] area, and speak to each in turn to receive the recipe: [[Recipe Scroll: Practice Balanzite Barbute]].
   - Pick up the components for the recipe in the crate behind either of the two blacksmiths.
1. Craft the [[Practice Balanzite Barbute]] on a nearby forge.
1. Speak to one of the blacksmiths and give him the helm.
1. Return to [[Hulda Flintbraid (Nexus)|Hulda]] in the [[Luclin Nexus]].

## Rewards

- At least 4p 46g 96s 59c
- +10,000 faction with **Fordel Midst Trade Commission**
- [[Fordel Cart]] (house item)
