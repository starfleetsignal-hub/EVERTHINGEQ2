Teach the Snowfang Gnolls to preserve veggies

## Steps

![Frozen Parsnip Plant](images/Frozen_Parsnip_Plant.jpg)

1. Gather 10 root vegetables from *Frozen Parsnip Plants* around Snowfang Isle and purchase [[Far Seas Trading Company Recipe: Basic Root Preserves]] from [[Matthew]] for 4g 60s 80c
1. Return to [[Ruffin]]
1. Scribe the recipe and craft [[Preserved Parsnips]] at the cook fire next to [[Ruffin]].
   - You'll need [[Frostbitten parsnip|frostbitten parsnip]], 1 [[Liquid]] and 1 [[Ethereal coal]].
1. Talk to [[Ruffin]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
