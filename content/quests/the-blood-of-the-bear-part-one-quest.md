## Steps

1. Go to [[The Ruins of Varsoon]].
1. Kill 8 [[Clay guardian|clay guardians]]. {{waypoint 26, 0, 40}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 7 clay guardians.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 4 [[Enchanted clay|enchanted clays]]. {{waypoint 117, 0, 97}}, respawn time about 5 minutes
   - **Note**: Do NOT clear the entire room (see next step).
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 4 [[Claychanter|claychanters]]. {{waypoint 117, 0, 97}}, respawn time about 20 minutes
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 clay guardians.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 4 [[Spirited tome|spirited tomes]]. {{waypoint 13, 4, 73}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 clay guardians.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 4 [[Forge ember|forge embers]]. {{waypoint 263, -12, 15}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 8 clay guardians.
1. Read the book.
