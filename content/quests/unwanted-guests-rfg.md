## Steps

1. Use the Mariner's Bell ("Travel outside of city" bell) to journey to the [[The Feerrott]].
1. Speak to the Iksar Pilgrim.
1. I must find the encampment and help them resist the Sathirians advances.
   - Hail and kill a [[Sathirian Scout]] on your way to the pilgrim Encampment near {{waypoint 78, 3, 22}}.
   - Hail and kill a [[Sathirian Scout Leader]] on your way to the pilgrim Encampment, near {{waypoint 117, -1, 102}}.
   - Speak to [[Irkksom Svetann]] to receive the quest [[Disrupt the Sathirian Efforts]].
1. Return to [[Livia Montelius]] in [[East Freeport]] to finish the quest-line.

## Rewards

none

## Notes

1. This quest can be done as a group which is handy since the respawn time on the Scouts and Leaders can be slow.
1. This quest can be done at low levels (we did it at 13), but there are level 35+ agro mobs in the area.
