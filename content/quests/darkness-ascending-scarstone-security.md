## Steps

1. Speak to [[Buildmaster Angor]] {{waypoint 4428, -822, 438}}behind the command tent in Scarstone
1. Kill six [[A vexed forest watcher|vexed forest watchers]] in [[Hawkeye Trails]] (northeast of Scarstone). This is an uncommon auto-update.
1. Return to Scarstone and click on the six seclusion totems encircling the perimeter of the command tent.
1. The quest will auto-complete. Speak to Vishra in front of the command tent entrance for the next quest

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
