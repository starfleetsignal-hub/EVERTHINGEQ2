## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Harvest 10-19 **toxxilac**
   - *The harvestable item is called "a toxxilac flower", they are on the ground right after you pass through the arch right after the druid portal and along the river*
     - *These are unique harvesting nodes found commonly around [[The Temple Grounds]]. They are purple flowers with red stems. You will easily find them along the riverbank as well as uphill among the gorgs.*
1. Return [[Surgeon Kweezil]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- 3 [[Marrsprin Tablet]]
- First turn-in: good faction with **The Lost Children of Marr**
