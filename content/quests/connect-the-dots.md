## Steps

1. Find and eliminate the [[A sleetsaber scout|Rime sleetsaber scouts]] by following their light signals:
   1. North of [[Dreg's Landing]]: {{waypoint 906, 46, -119}}
   1. Past [[Karnor's Castle (POI)|Karnor's Castle]] on a large rock outcrop: {{waypoint 657, 59, -590}}
   1. Above the entrance to [[Icebound Pass]]: {{waypoint 454.75, 213.48, -943}}
   1. On a rock spying on the Hillock Crushers: {{waypoint 704, 304, -1041}}
   1. Near [[Charnax]]: {{waypoint 1202, 418, -961}}
   1. Northern slope out of the [[Icebound Pass]]: {{waypoint 1373, 437.03, -1210}}
   1. Outside [[Karatun Hold]]: {{waypoint 980, 172, -2200}}
   1. Out in the open past Karatun Hold: {{waypoint 1116.79, 149.21, -2779.40}}
1. Report back to [[Kilur Beardback]]
