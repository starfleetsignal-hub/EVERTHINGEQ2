This is a very bumpy greenish flesh, that seems familiar in some way. I feel oddly compelled to find out more about it, while on the dark side of this moon.

## Notes

chest for Greenish Flesh dropped today from a Deadly shadefiend for me

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill 15 bogling [[A bogling cultist|cultists]], [[A bogling scorcher|scorchers]], [[A bogling scout|scouts]], or [[A bogling warrior|warriors]] in [[Limestone Forest]] around {{waypoint 717, 186, 302}} in [[Echo Caverns]].
