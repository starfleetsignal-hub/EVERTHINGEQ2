## Notes

The first 2 kill tasks MAY be randomly chosen from a pool of tasks, or may differ depending on the actual class of the player. The 3rd step is always the same.

## Steps

1. Kill the following mobs *(see Notes about it)*
   - Kill 12 [[A granitescale basilisk|granitescale basilisks]] in the [[Tower Lands]] in [[Antonica]]
   - Kill 12 [[A marine slug|marine slugs]] {{waypoint -367, -8, -332}} in [[The Thundering Steppes]].
1. Kill the following mobs *(see Notes about it)*
   - Kill 6 [[A Varsoon apprentice (Thundering Steppes)|Varsoon apprentices]] near [[The Ruins of Varsoon]]
   - Kill 6 undead near the [[Dead River Docks]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]].
1. Kill [[Advisor Pandrus]] at [[The Ruins of Caltorsis]] in [[Antonica]].

  - [[Spellbound Cerise Wristwraps]] - ([[Wizard]])
  - [[Wristwraps of Allurement]] - ([[Coercer]])
