## Description

*The Palace of Unity is under attack, while Maldurans traveling beyond the gates continue to go missing. I must help save the palace, and venture into Kralet Penumbra to stop whoever is kidnapping the citizens of Maldura.*

## Steps

- Destroy enemy targets within Maldura and Kralet Penumbra.
1. Kill the dragoon warlord
   - [[Dragoon Z'Koz]] in [[Maldura: Palace Foray (Event Heroic)]]
1. Slay the mind fiend
   - [[Urzzra-Uzulu]] in [[Kralet Penumbra: Uzulu Deep (Event Heroic)]]
