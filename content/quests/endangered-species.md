## Notes

- You must be level 60 to start this quest.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Raakil Shiningheart]]. {{waypoint 727, -143, 255}}
1. Kill 12 of of the nearby drakes ([[An azure drake]], [[An azure drakeling]], [[A clutch guardian drake]], [[A squalburst drake]], [[A thundermaw drake]] and [[The Broodmatron]] all appear to update). You find a strange collar on one of the drakes.
1. Return to Raakil.
1. Kill nearby Scaleborn droags to search for evidence.  Eventually one will drop a small chest which contains a [[Strangely Written Note]], which updates the quest.
1. Return to Raakil.
1. Go to the top level of [[The Temple Grounds]] and click the big rock tablet at {{waypoint -51, 116, -279}}.
1. Find the following pieces of evidence to help you decipher the tablet:
   - first piece {{waypoint 43, 49, -85}}
   - second piece {{waypoint -118, 62, -97}} (Note: it is on the right side of the temple on the first platform, tucked up against the wall.)
   - third piece {{waypoint -26, 115, -327}}
1. Return to the big rock tablet on the Temple Grounds at {{waypoint -51, 117, -279}}. Using the three notes you acquired in the previous step, you must decipher the message from a [[Strangely Written Note]] and select the correct answer on the tablet. Selecting the wrong answer prevents you from attempting again for about two minutes real-time.
   - All you really have to do is decipher the first letter, since the four options on the tablet all begin with a different letter.
   - The correct answer is ***For Our Rites***.
1. Click on 6 wind komodo eggs in the area around the tablet. The eggs are ground spawns, bright blue and easy to see.
1. Return the eggs to their nests on the [[Vicious Breeding Grounds]]. The first time you click on a nest three linked [[A Scaleborn ambusher|Scaleborn ambushers]] will spawn. You must defeat them and then click on the nest again to get the quest update.
   - {{waypoint 648, -161, 180}}
   - {{waypoint 693, -162, 225}}
   - {{waypoint 680, 164, 261}}
1. Return to Raakil.
1. Pet the wind komodo. {{waypoint 702, -164, 270}}

## Rewards

- [[Tamed Wind Komodo]]
- At least 5g 84s 70c
