## Steps

1. Do the following two steps in any order:
   - Visit [[Blackhook Landing]] {{waypoint -25, -43, 450}}
   - Slay [[A Blackhook raider|Blackhook raider]]s, found nearby, for 8 updates.
1. Return to Quint Cerlius M.E.
