## Description

*The beasts of Stygian Threshold and Kralet Penumbra are harbingers of death to anyone who trespasses into their lairs, but I am the harbinger of their destruction!*

## Steps

- Destroy enemy targets within Stygian Threshold and Kralet Penumbra.
1. Kill the exiled one
   - [[Mawz Harak]] in [[Stygian Threshold (Heroic)]]
1. Destroy the destroyer
   - [[Mrwar, the Destroyer]] in [[Kralet Penumbra: Tepid Depths (Heroic)]]
