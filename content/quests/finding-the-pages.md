*This quest is part of the [[Freeport Outlying Areas Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Find seven ritual pages for Varacus:
   - 5 pages are obtained automatically by killing any undead in the zone.
   - One page is a ground spawn behind the [[The Destroyed Tower of Marr]]. {{waypoint 49, -3, 122}}
   - One page is a ground spawn under an elevated tomb. {{waypoint -57, -12, 65}}
1. Speak with [[Keeper Varacus T'Kur]]. {{waypoint 91, -2, 96}}
   - You will receive the [[Ethernere Ledger]].
1. Speak with [[Custodian Zaddar Sullissia]]. {{waypoint -73, -13, 107}}

## Rewards

- At least 1s 5c
- +150 faction with **The Academy of Arcane Science**
