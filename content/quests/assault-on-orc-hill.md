## Description

"Jenthis wants me to head west to Orc Hill. Once there, he would like me to kill several of the Crushbone invaders. He believes that we need to take them out as a preemptive strike, before they attack Sapling Spur Outpost!" -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay 8 [[A Crushbone invader|Crushbone invaders]] on [[Orc Hill]] {{waypoint 276.49, 62.04, -99.28}} and {{waypoint 288.15, 64.81, 24.70}}
1. Return to [[Jenthis Viridar]]

## Rewards

- At least 13s 23c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Jenthis Viridar: "Do you have Fhera's report?"
- Player Character: "Yes, the orcs have attacked."
- Jenthis Viridar: "There is orc activity in the south? Tell me what has happened."
- Player Character: "The orcs attempted to burn down Tunare's Sapling, but I was able to stop them."
- Jenthis Viridar: "Thank heavens you were there to stop them, [Player Character]. We have much to concern ourselves with, and the next stop is Orc Hill."
- Player Character: "Do you have a plan?"
- Jenthis Viridar: "I've been working out the details of what the orcs are doing. They aren't exactly the best writers. But after poring over the orders you recovered, I have surmised that they wish to surround us. Hence, the creation of the new outpost on our eastern flank."
- Player Character: "Should I return to their outpost in the east?"
- Jenthis Viridar: "Their outpost in the east is less important than their base of operations in Orc Hill. We need to strike, just as we did with the orc pawns to the east. I would like you to travel to the west of here to Orc Hill. Take out the Crushbone invaders before they can strike."
- Player Character: "Very well."

Kill several Crushbone invaders.

I have slain the Crushbone invaders. I need to return to Jenthis.

- Jenthis Viridar: "Have you slain the invaders?"
- Player Character: "I have."

I have spoken with Jenthis. I have taken out the Crushbone invaders.

I have completed the task Jenthis gave me.
