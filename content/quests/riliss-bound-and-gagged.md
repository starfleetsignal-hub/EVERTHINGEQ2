## Steps

1. Use [[Grezrik's sleeping elixir]] on [[A froglok captive]] in the [[Western Pens of Riliss]] {{waypoint -694, -137, 688}}. The froglok will attack, but do not kill it. After a short while, it will succumb to the sleep potion. At this point simply click it and [[A sedated froglok]] will be added to your inventory.
   - If you happen to kill the froglok, simply delete and reacquire the quest.
1. Take the sedated froglok to [[Trooper Kresix]] in [[Riliss (POI)|Riliss]]. {{waypoint -791, 49, -246}}

## Rewards

- At least 6g 27s 71c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Nathsarian Shroud of Defense]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Nathsarian Shroud of Energy]] (All Mages)
  - [[Nathsarian Shroud of Faith]] (All Priests)
  - [[Nathsarian Shroud of Stealth]] (All Scouts)
