## Notes

- the quest [[Mighty Mighty Metals]] can be taken after this quest is finished also requires  [[Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent (Solo)|Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent [Solo]]] and works in a cleared version of it as well
- the quest [[Here Ghost Nothing]] can be taken after this quest is finished and requires the same zone as the quest [[Ballads of Zimara: Vaashkaani Crescendo]]

## Steps

1. Enter [[Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent (Solo)|Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent [Solo]]] in *[[Zenith Helionae]].*
   1. *Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint 215.08, 379.31, 652.09}} to [[Zenith Helionae]].*
   1. *The portal to [[Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent (Solo)|Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent [Solo]]] is at {{waypoint 288.76, 164.76, -126.28}}.*
1. ![Odd Stone](images/Odd_Stone.jpg)![Keepsake Globe](images/Keepsake_Globe.jpg)Gather 12 Sirran's Illusioned **Keepsake Globes** by destroying **Odd Stone**s
   - they are found all over the map and many past nameds, (group-wide update) [Collect the spheres under the rocks for update. They will disappear, there are only 12 zone wide. If you miss one you will have to repeat the zone]
   - For a zone walkthrough see [[Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent (Solo)|Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent [Solo]]]
   - Odd Stone Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 776.42, 234.59, -434.31}}, {{waypoint 658.22, 206.66, -702.22}}, {{waypoint 576.11, 222.07, -841.83}}, {{waypoint 571.74, 194.79, -663.65}} on [[Columnar Highgarden]]
     - {{waypoint 563.59, 275.82, 573.08}} on [[Claved Fate Islets]]
     - {{waypoint 165.80, 361.97, 695.30}}, {{waypoint 103.27, 378.60, 663.28}}, {{waypoint 86.06, 386.70, 739.29}}, {{waypoint 123.04, 387.37, 747.04}} on [[Strungstone Peaks]]
1. Return to [[Sirran the Lunatic|Sirran the Lunatic's]] ghost on [[Strungstone Peaks]] in [[Zimara Breadth]].
