## Required

- Tradeskill level 50+
- Grab the required amount of each T6 fuel and liquid (total cost 5g 15s 77c)
  - 1 [[Aerated mineral water]]
  - 4 [[Lambent candle]]
  - 6 [[Lambent coal]]
  - 6 [[Lambent filament]]
  - 6 [[Lambent incense]]
  - 1 [[Lambent kindling]]
- Materials List:
  - 4 [[Beryllium cluster]]
  - 1 [[Conger eel]]
  - 7 [[Indium cluster]]
  - 2 [[Maj'dul coffee bean]]
  - 5 [[Rough Nacre]]
  - 4 [[Severed sandalwood]]
  - 2 [[Soluble loam]]
  - 3 [[Stonehide leather pelt]]
  - 4 [[Succulent root]]
    - Materials may be harvested, or they can be purchased from other players on the broker.
- *Note: To make this quest easier, consider buying portable craft stations that allow you to do all of the combines in [[Sinking Sands]]:
  - [[Portable Sewing Table]]
  - [[Portable Desk]]
  - [[Portable Forge]]
  - [[Portable Cooking Set]]
  - [[Portable Chemistry Set]]

## Starting the Quest

Quest starters are Trades Coordinators:

- [[Gorowyn]]  - [[Grandmaster Jayak Lat'wasr]] in [[Gorowyn]], [[The Wards]]
- [[Freeport]] - [[Grandmaster Kawayzer]] in [[West Freeport]], [[Coalition Crafter's Association (POI)]]
- [[Kelethin]] - [[Grandmaster Feldmanus]] in [[Tunare's Pages]] in the area with the broker
- [[Qeynos]]   - [[Grandmaster Kimbialis]] at the [[The Ironforge Exchange]] in [[North Qeynos]]
- [[Neriak]]   - [[Grandmaster Zyrythius]] in the [[Dockside Markets]]
- [[New Halas]] - [[Grandmaster Cheri Glodsdottr]] in the [[Ravens' Roost]], near the player housing.

## Steps

1. I need to locate the trade liaison, [[Vanesta Leaftraveller]] that the Grandmaster mentioned. She may be found at the lower entrance to the Sandslide Passage {{waypoint -1158, -212, -276}} in [[The Sinking Sands]]. After hailing the liaison, you will receive [[Emergency Shelter for Dummies]] recipe book.
1. Scribe the recipe book to receive level 50 recipe for [[Rough Tent]].
1. Craft the tent at a [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]]. To craft it, you will need:
   - 3 [[Succulent root]] (root harvest on [[Harvestables (Tier 6)|T6 zones]] like Sinking Sands)
   - 2 [[Severed sandalwood]] (tree harvest on T6 zones)
   - 2 [[Indium cluster]] (ore harvest on T6 zones)
   - 6 [[Lambent filament]] (fuel from merchants)
1. Return with the tent to [[Vanesta Leaftraveller|Vanesta]].
1. [[Vanesta Leaftraveller|Vanesta]] is not sounding very well. Go talk to [[A shady dervish merchant]] at the Croc Hunters' camp {{waypoint -1348, -227, -591}} in the Sinking Sands. Buy [[Bert's Big Book of Health]] recipe book to get an update.  (The book costs 4g 60s 80c)
1. [[Vanesta Leaftraveller|Vanesta]] mentioned a mushroom infestation she'd seen in Darklight Wood. Maybe a fungus potion will help. Scribe the Book you just bought to get the recipe.
1. Craft the Fungus Potion at a [[Chemistry Table]] and return to [[Vanesta Leaftraveller|Vanesta]]. (***You have to speak with [[Vanesta Leaftraveller|Vanesta]] after every item you have crafted!***) To craft it, you will need:
   - 1 [[Conger eel]] (fish harvest on T6 zones)
   - 1 [[Rough Nacre]] (stone harvest on T6 zones)
   - 2 [[Soluble loam]] (ore harvest on T6 zones)
   - 4 [[Lambent candle]] (fuel from merchants)
1. Craft an Energy Drink at a [[Stove & Keg]] and return to [[Vanesta Leaftraveller|Vanesta]].  To craft it, you will need:
   - 2 [[Maj'dul coffee bean]] (bush harvest on T6 zones)
   - 1 [[Liquid]]
   - 1 [[Lambent kindling]] (fuel from merchants)
1. Craft a Plague Remedy at an [[Engraved Desk]] and return to [[Vanesta Leaftraveller|Vanesta]]. You'll need:
   - 1 [[Succulent root]] (root harvest on [[Harvestables (Tier 6)|T6 zones]] like Sinking Sands)
   - 4 [[Rough Nacre]] (stone harvest on T6 zones)
   - 4 [[Beryllium cluster]] (stone harvest on T6 zones)
   - 6 [[Lambent incense]] (fuel from merchants)
1. Craft an Acupuncture Kit at a [[Forge]] and return to [[Vanesta Leaftraveller|Vanesta]]. You'll need:
   - 5 [[Indium cluster]] (ore harvest on T6 zones)
   - 3 [[Stonehide leather pelt]] (animal harvest on T6 zones)
   - 2 [[Severed sandalwood]] (tree harvest on T6 zones)
   - 6 [[Lambent coal]] (fuel from merchants)
1. Return to the Grandmaster for your reward.
