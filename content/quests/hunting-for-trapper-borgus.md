*This quest is repeatable up to 5 times..*

## Steps

1. Kill a number (between 8 and 12) of antelope, beetles, crabs or snakes (chosen randomly) in [[The Thundering Steppes]]:
   - All the mobs are found in the northern areas of the zone, specifically around the [[Antonican Road|Antonican]], [[Seaside Road|Seaside]] and [[Thundermist Road|Thundermist Roads]]. They all share spawns, so if the type you need isn't up keep killing the other types until the desired type spawns.
1. Return to Trapper Borgus.

## Rewards

- Coin
- Experience
