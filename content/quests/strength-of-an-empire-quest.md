Quest is repeatable once every 18 hours.

## Description

Upon inspecting the statue , I heard the voice of Rallos Zek and felt strongly compelled to carry out its will! In war there are casualties! A great warrior must test the Kromzek Empire's strength. I will kill Kromzek soldiers within New Kael.

## Steps

1. Kill Kromzek in New Kael.

## Rewards

- At least 10p
- +50 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
- +50 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
- [[Primal Velium Shard]] x1
