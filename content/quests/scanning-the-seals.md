Brumley says he had believed the immolated, which are apparently some kind of wight-like creature, were sealed up in areas of the city. Now he's alarmed they've found a way to escape, and he's asked me to help check the city is still secure.

## Steps

1. Scan the shiny spot in front of the sealed door ([[Maldura: Bar Brawl|Bar Brawl]]) near the [[Alcove of Reason]] {{waypoint -68.54, 9.52, 118.03}}.
1. Scan the sealed door ([[Maldura: District of Ash|District of Ash]]) north of the [[Sublevel Access Antechamber]] {{waypoint -107.90, 1.36, -44.89}}.
1. Scan the sealed door ([[Maldura: Algorithm For Destruction|Algorithm For Destruction]]) south of the [[Sublevel Access Antechamber]] {{waypoint -106.87, 1.36, 44.72}}.
1. Scan the sealed door ([[Maldura: Forge of Ashes|Forge of Ashes]]) near the [[Mushroom Bar and Grill]] {{waypoint -44.06, -4.23, 43.91}}.
1. Report back to [[Brumley Langboom|Brumley]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- [[Coin of the Great Artificer]]
- +1,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +5,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
