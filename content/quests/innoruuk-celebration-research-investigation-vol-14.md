## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Collect 3 [[Seething Razor Shard|seething razor shards]]
   1. Shards can be found in the Aether Wroughtlands. Near the Adja-Beryl Berms {{waypoint -436.39, 103.63, 272.31}}
      1. A Silverband Soldier {{waypoint -214.89, 155.95, 489.89}}
      1. Silverloid Sentinels {{waypoint -421.73, 157.32, 371.37}}
      1. Blightmetal Stalkers {{waypoint -190, -0.69, -412}}
      1. Named MOBs throughout the zone also drop these shards.
1. Afterwards you take those shards to a shrine in [[Nektulos Forest]] - the shrine spawns in random spot in that zone.
   1. This is NOT a group update.
   1. One person clicking the altar will cause the altar to disappear and reappear in another location
   1. It is not clear if there is only ever one altar in the zone that is moved by other groups
   1. It may be possible to click simultaneously to get a group update
   1. You may spend as much as 30 minutes tracking down an altar to click
   1. Possible Locations include:
      1. {{waypoint -1402.82, 35.60, -1312.88}}
      1. {{waypoint -382.62, 60.42, -1317.64}}
      1. {{waypoint -392.54, 0.44, -265.36}} just east of the Behemoth Pond island, on the road.
      1. {{waypoint -250, 0, 96}} next to the Commonlands Griffon Station.
      1. {{waypoint 373, 0, -580}} On the path to/from the beach.
      1. {{waypoint -429.09, 84.31, -1877.79}}  Along path.
      1. {{waypoint 606, -0.25, -717}}
      1. {{waypoint -1314, 0, -938}}
      1. {{waypoint -833, 57, -1340}}
      1. {{waypoint -969.32, 12.68, -729.48}} Directly north of Torch 10 on the map
      1. {{waypoint -1335, 113, -1863}} in front of NEK castle
      1. {{waypoint -302, 0, -662}} near the wizard spires
      1. {{waypoint -969, 13, -729}}
1. Return to Herald to the Prince of Hate {{waypoint -1644, 62, -1477}} in Nektulos Forest
