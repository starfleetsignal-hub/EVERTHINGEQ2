## Steps

1. Kill [[A Nurgan minedigger|Nurgan minediggers]] in the [[Mines of Nurga]] until one of them drops a [[Nurgan chest key]] (chest drop). {{waypoint -300, 52, -1956}}
1. Search the mines for 12 chests and open them to loot the gems.![a typical chest](images/Gobbling_Gems_chest.jpg)
   - [[A Nurgan overseer|Nurgan overseers]] roam the mines and can see invis. Opening the chests breaks invis, as well.
1. Return to Collector Gebblie.
