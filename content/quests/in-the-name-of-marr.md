## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- You must have *indifferent* or higher faction with [[The Lost Children of Marr (Faction)|The Lost Children of Marr]] to start this quest.
  - You can raise this faction by killing the dragonkind in [[Tenebrous Tangle]], and by doing quests for [[Grumglub]].

## Steps

1. You will get ONE of the following kill tasks (all mobs are level 56-58):
   - Kill a random number of [[Scaleborn stonesetter|Scaleborn stonesetters]] and [[A stonesetter overseer]]. Both are found at {{waypoint -190, -4, 31}}.
   - Kill a random number of [[Scaleborn stonesetter|Scaleborn stonesetters]] and [[A sentinel bladehand]].
   - Kill a random number of [[Twilight gorg|twilight gorgs]] and [[A sentinel bladehand]].
     - The Bladehand spawns at the top of the treehouses {{waypoint -70, 78, -42}} or {{waypoint 36, 30, 92}}.
   - Kill a random number of [[A Scaleborn sentry|Scaleborn sentrys]] and [[A devout reaver]].
     - The Reaver spawns in the building beside the pool in front of [[The Temple of Scale]].
   - Kill a random number of [[Twilight gorg|twilight gorgs]] and [[A devout reaver]].
   - Kill a random number of [[Scaleborn stonesetter|Scaleborn stonesetters]] and [[A devout reaver]].
   - Kill a random number of [[Scaleborn watchdog|Scaleborn watchdogs]] ([[A twilight gorg]], [[A territorial gorg]] {{waypoint 117.72, 57.54, -0.92}}) and [[A stonesetter overseer]].
   - Kill a random number of [[Scaleborn watchdog|Scaleborn watchdogs]] ([[A twilight gorg]], [[A territorial gorg]] {{waypoint 117.72, 57.54, -0.92}}) and [[A devout reaver]].
1. Speak to [[Lady Erillis]] again when finished to get your reward.

## Rewards

- Coin
- +2000 faction with **The Lost Children of Marr**
