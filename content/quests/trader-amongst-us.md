## Steps

1. Speak with [[Klok Scekki]] in Crusader's Cave at {{waypoint 664, 248, 1288}}.
1. Speak with [[Trader Gorakris]] outside the village of [[Vrassath]] at {{waypoint 523, -179, -224}}.
1. Return to Klok Scekki in Crusader's Cave at {{waypoint 664, 248, 1288}}.
1. Klok Scekki will ask you to return later, but fails to say how long you must wait. I checked back after about an hour, but he was still not ready. Will check back tomorrow.
