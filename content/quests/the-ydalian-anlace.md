## Description

I've been asked to travel to Evernight Abbey and find a powerful artifact known as the Ydalian Anlace. It's been described as a sort of short double-edged sword used in sacrificial ceremonies. No doubt used in a myriad of deplorable acts, I should find the Anlace and bring it to Ragheejb Yasir.

## Steps

1. Inside [[Evernight Abbey]], pick up the Ydalian Anlace at the side of [[Lord Marcus Thex]], the boss of the instance. You will need to kill Lord Marcus Thex in order for the item to appear. It is located on the coffin on which he stands after you defeat him.
1. Return to [[Ragheejb Yasir]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- 2 [[Void Shard]]
