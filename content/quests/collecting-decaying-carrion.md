## Description

Compared to the denizens of the Halls of the Fallen who have fairly recently become cursed, the creatures that stalk the Lower Corridors have been writhing in the grip of the Curse of Ykesha for lifetimes. It may be prudent to study the curse and possibly learn more about the long term effects it has on those who suffer under its influence. To this end, I should collect pieces of flesh from the creatures within the Lower Corridors, and return them to Firmroot.

## Steps

1. Seek out 15 of those under the curse within [[The Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors]] and collect pieces of their flesh for study.
1. Return to [[Vegupa Marrsheart]] at Firmroot Moot {{waypoint 87, -87, -434}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
