## Steps

1. Investigate the areas indicated T'Vel and see if they might be suitable for a landing area.  The areas that trigger the updates will be marked by a small patch of silver sparkles.
   - Investigate the spot above the New Combine Foothold {{waypoint -3514, -766, -197}}
   - Investigate a clearing out in the forest {{waypoint -3287, -756, -282}}
   - Investigate a spot near some ruins {{waypoint -3200, -748, -417}}
1. Return to Flight Officer T'Vel

- At least 1p 25g
