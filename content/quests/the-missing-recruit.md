## Steps

![Soltrim's corpse](images/Soltrim's_corpse.jpg)

1. Search for Soltrin's corpse, and retrieve [[Soltrin's field kit]].
   - Examine the corpse at the west end of [[Innoruuk's Scar]], due north of [[T'Vatar Post]] {{waypoint -376, -50, -101}}
1. Return the field kit to Ginwyss.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 10c
- One of the following (dependent on class):
  - [[Dark Bodyguard's Tunic]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Dark Evoker's Robe]] (All Mages)
  - [[Dark Marshall's Breastplate]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Dark Pathfinder's Tunic]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Dark Spiritualist's Tunic]] (Shamans)
  - [[Dark Vicar's Breastplate]] (Clerics)
  - [[Dark Windcaller's Tunic]] (Druids, Channelers)
