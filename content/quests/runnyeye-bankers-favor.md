## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks:
   - Collect 20 ***Optical Nerves*** from Evil Eyes
   - Collect 20 ***Pristine Minotaur Horns*** from Gorehorn Minotaurs
   - Kill [[The Runnyeye Chef]] {{waypoint 109.77, -16.00, -234.10}}
1. Return to [[The Goblin Banker]].
