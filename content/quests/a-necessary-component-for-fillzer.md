## Steps

1. Gather 5 *Mudcorpse Mushrooms* along the shore of the nearest mudfin village, from {{waypoint -1322, 1, -247}} to {{waypoint -1191, 1, -255}}. They spawn in the water right next to the shoreline.
1. Return to [[Fillzer Faramant]] in [[Kunzar's Edge (POI)|Kunzar's Edge]]. {{waypoint -1347, 11, 531}}

## Rewards

- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
