## Steps

![Balarus' Armor, labeled as Therius' Armor](images/Therius_armor.jpg)
![Balarus' Blade, labeled as Therius' Blade](images/Therius_blade.jpg)

1. Go to [[New Tunaria]] and loot the armor and sword on pedestals at {{waypoint -358, 96, -497}} and {{waypoint -368, 96, -481}}.
   - There are several mobs that can see through invisibility that are green and aggro to a level 70 in the area of these pieces. Fighting for the pieces is probable.
1. Go to [[The Combine Spires]] in [[Greater Faydark]], at {{waypoint -436, 75, 322}}, and click on the shimmering ghostly presence in the center.
1. Kill [[Balarus Verimisilian]].
1. Return to [[Juna Crankfizz]].

- Experience Only
