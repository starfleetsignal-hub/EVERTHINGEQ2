## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of E'Ci has asked me for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Collect 3 [[Hardened Scales (Item)|hardened scales]] from Shockspines found in [[Aether Wroughtlands]]
   - Hardened scales are a random drop in a [[Small Chest|small chest]] from shockspines in [[Aether Wroughtlands]] in the [[Fiddtan Crest]] area {{waypoint -473.46, 217.96, 568.05}}
1. Gather or purchase 10 [[Direbeak Hide|direbeak hides]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas.
   - *Common harvest from animal dens*
   - *Can buy off broker*
   - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to [[Herald of the Wintry Guardian]] {{waypoint 75, -35, -1687}} in [[Everfrost]]
