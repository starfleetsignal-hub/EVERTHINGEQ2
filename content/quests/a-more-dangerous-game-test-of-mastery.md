## Prerequisites

- You must have at least +45,000 faction with Clan Stormshield to obtain this quest. This amount of faction is reached by completing all the other quests offered by [[Grim Stormshield (Jarsath Wastes)|Grim Stormshield]], including the repeatable ones.

## Steps

1. Kill [[Grimebelly]]. {{waypoint -2514, 285, 535}}
1. Return to [[Grim Stormshield]].
