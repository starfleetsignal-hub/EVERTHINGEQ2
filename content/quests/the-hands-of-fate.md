## Notes

- This quest takes you to [[Zenith Helionae]] where you can pick up the quest [[The Flutters and Mutters Riddle]] on the way
- After the quest you can get the [[Stoking the Forge (Quest)|Stoking the Forge]] quest which will need the same zone as [[Ballads of Zimara: Ascending Collection]]

## Steps

*Ecihoolo is a clever hooluk. She realized the mystic grapplers are conjured and maintained using jann wish gems found on Auric Palisades, and that they are incompatible with the metallic crystalline that has begun to form in the Helionae Razelands. She's proposed we use this against them.*

1. Gather metallic Crystalline and embed them in jann wish gems
   1. ![Metallic Crystalline](images/Metallic_Crystalline.jpg)Gather 6 [[Metallic Crystalline]] from [[Helionae Razelands]]
      - Note: Take to the Skies cloud {{waypoint -684.00, 144.00, -4.74}} to [[Zenith Helionae]]
      - Note: these will appear in your bag, they will not count down on the quest helper. Not a guaranteed update
      - Possible locations include:
      - {{waypoint -167.42, 107.98, -286.05}}
      - {{waypoint -122, 112, -398}}
      - {{waypoint -129, 110, -407}}
      - {{waypoint -169, 109, -448}}
      - {{waypoint -193, 105, -373}}
      - {{waypoint -235.18, 102.00, -210.20}}
      - {{waypoint -243, 103, -392}}
      - {{waypoint -270.25, 101.67, -229.39}}
      - {{waypoint -311.33, 103.80, -206.75}}
      - {{waypoint -374.73, 106.46, -226.52}}
      - {{waypoint -385.88, 106.84, -233.93}}
   1. ![Jann Wish Gem](images/Jann_Wish_Gem.jpg)Embed the [[Metallic Crystalline]] into 6 **Jann Wish Gems** on [[Auric Palisades]] to update the quest (group-wide update). Possible locations include:
      - Note: Take to the Skies cloud {{waypoint 313.04, 167.65, -52.44}} to [[Auric Palisades]]
      - {{waypoint -571.80, 206.21, -679.80}}
      - {{waypoint -606.81, 206.66, -695.59}}
      - {{waypoint -608.42, 183.07, -603.92}}
      - {{waypoint -623.85, 182.48, -610.58}}
      - {{waypoint -714.59, 223.97, -694.56}}
      - {{waypoint -718.49, 219.05, -670.81}}
1. Destroy 8 weakened mystic grapplers. Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint -372.38, 123.74, -629.69}}
   - {{waypoint -412, 125, -642}}
   - {{waypoint -608.42, 183.07, -603.92}}
   - {{waypoint -618.75, 125.18, -416.93}}
1. Take to the Skies cloud {{waypoint -705.14, 145.16, -363.90}} to [[Isles of Wisdom]]
1. Return to [[Ecihoolo]]
