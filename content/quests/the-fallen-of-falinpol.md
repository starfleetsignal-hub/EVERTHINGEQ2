## Steps

1. Find and speak to [[Karenak]]. {{waypoint 394, 305, -567}}
1. Rain fire upon 15 of the Claws of Veeshan
   - Timer: 20 min
   - Eligible targets will be found near [[Falinpol]] on [[Daarspire]] island. These include:
     - [[A Daar sentinel|Daar sentinels]]
     - [[A Wayward etherwing|Wayward etherwings]]
     - droags ([[A Falinpol attendant|Falinpol attendants]] and [[A Wayward realmwatcher|Wayward realmwatchers]] -- watch the latter, they are ^^^!)
1. Return to Soliddar.

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Precious Rhenium Band]]
