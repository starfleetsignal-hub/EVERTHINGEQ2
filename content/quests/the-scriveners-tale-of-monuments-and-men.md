## Steps

1. Harvest the following from [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]]
   - 5 **cinder blooms** in the [[Cinder Fields]].
     - The blooms are found on cliffs near lava, for example, near {{waypoint 287, 46, 420}} and {{waypoint 93, 20, 186}} and  {{waypoint 272, 51, 397}}.
     - The cinder blooms are untrackable.![Cinder Bloom](images/Cinder_bloom.png)
   - 8 [[Strangler root]] from [[Celestial nursery]] or [[Strangler root break]]
   - 8 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Coalesced reef]]
1. Speak with [[Zuvet]] at {{waypoint 167, 43, 74}}.

## Rewards

- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
