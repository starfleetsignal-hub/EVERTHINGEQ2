## Steps

1. Each of the **Paineel outland patrol guards** will be shown on the map with a quest completion icon (a flashing book), so they're easy to find. The patrols can be found in these general locations:
   - Highland Salts: {{waypoint 965, 54, 3612}}
   - Lowland Basin: {{waypoint 2048, -99, 3705}}
   - Toxxulia Forest: {{waypoint 945.94, -51.74, 3465}}
     - *Note: If you have a mount, there's no need to use the Temporal guides to travel to stations. Generally it will be faster for you to simply ride (or fly) to the locations of the guards as shown on your map.*
1. Return to [[Masih Najjar]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
