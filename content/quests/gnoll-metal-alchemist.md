## Steps

*Fenlys Nimbledigit, the down-on-her-luck gnome turned merchant has roped me into helping her once again. This time she has her sights set on howling iron ore and goshenite spell-stones. I have a good idea where I can find both within Darkpaw Warrens.*

1. Gather 12 howling iron ore from Dustpaw Burrow
   - {{waypoint 12.95, -2.71, -188.13}} ![](images/Howling_Iron_Ore.png)
   - {{waypoint -10.90, -4.36, -176.66}}
   - {{waypoint -4.38, -0.86, -167.45}}
   - {{waypoint -49.03, -11.80, -122.93}}
   - {{waypoint -107.84, -11.43, -143.63}}
   - {{waypoint -112.67, -12.04, -137.43}}
   - {{waypoint -117.14, -11.50, -122.69}}
   - {{waypoint -108.57, -12.11, -123.21}}
   - {{waypoint -93.11, -12.05, -121.44}}
   - {{waypoint -95.04, -12.07, -111.70}}
   - {{waypoint -110.36, -17.93, -84.24}}
   - {{waypoint -94.08, -17.45, -47.84}}
1. Hunt Underpaw gnolls for 8 goshenite spell-stones
   - Group update from killing Underpaw gnolls in Underpaw Stronghold {{waypoint 79, -25, 76}} or near Terramite Crystalline Grotto {{waypoint 72, 2, 210}}
1. Speak with [[Fenlys Nimbledigit]] in [[Darkpaw Dugout]] {{waypoint -20, -80, -264}}
