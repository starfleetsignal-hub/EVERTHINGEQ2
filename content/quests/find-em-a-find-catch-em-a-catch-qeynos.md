## Notes

- This quest is repeatable once per year.
- If you already received an [[Erollisi Day Cloak]] from previous years it will not be offered as a reward again since it is a Lore item.

## Steps

1. Find the following people in [[Qeynos Capitol District]] and learn a little about them:
   - [[Amund MacRae (Qeynos)|Amund MacRae]] {{waypoint 288, -20, 13}}
   - [[Marjorie Falconius (Qeynos)|Marjorie Falconius]] {{waypoint 284, -17, 32}}
   - [[Vixxi Starguide (Qeynos)|Vixxi Starguide]] {{waypoint 357, -21, 146}}
   - [[Zak Silvanus (Qeynos)|Zak Silvanus]] {{waypoint 425, -27, -57}}
   - [[Lysndra (Qeynos)|Lysndra]] {{waypoint 411.61, -20.72, -192.50}}
   - [[Colt Longpier (Qeynos)|Colt Longpier]] {{waypoint 432, -20, -333}}
   - [[Tyluna Whittlebit (Qeynos)|Tyluna Whittlebit]] {{waypoint 394, -19, -237}}
   - [[Beldrin Findlebolt (Qeynos)|Beldrin Findlebolt]] {{waypoint 286, -18, -82}}
1. Return to David Torchsong and choose the appropriate matches:
   - **Warning**: If you do not choose the correct matches, you will have to do the previous step again.
   - Zak and **Lysndra** both appreciate industry and hard work. **Colt** is also an acceptable answer.
   - Tyluna and **Colt** share a love for animals. **Lysndra** is also an acceptable answer
   - Amund and **Vixxi** each admire fine physical specimens.
   - Marjorie will please **Beldrin** with her cooking.
1. Speak with David Torchsong again.
