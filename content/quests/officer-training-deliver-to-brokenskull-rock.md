## Note

*make sure that you have your brokenskull Illusion up, otherwise you can't get the update from Fip Marrsquire.*

## Steps

1. I should deliver this packet of weapons to [[Quartermaster Thumpstomp]] on [[Brokenskull Rock|Brokenskull Island]] at {{waypoint -1811, -1092, 2698}}.
   - The full balloon route is:
     - Take the balloon to [[The Overlook]].
     - Take the balloon from [[The Overlook]] to [[Shadowed Cleft]].
     - Take the balloon from [[Shadowed Cleft]] to [[Brokenskull Rock]].
   - Or as an alternative:
     - Take the south route from the crossroads where Scabby Gabby is to the Loft, and float or glide down to lower Moors as running from your landing spot to the closest balloon post or to the island can be faster than the balloon route.
1. I need to return to [[Petty Officer Kimtiz]] and let her know I've made the delivery.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
