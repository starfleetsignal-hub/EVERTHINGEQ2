## Steps

1. Kill Letalis geodes in the area around {{waypoint 484, 283, -544}} and pick up the dazzle crystals they leave behind. 12 crystals are required.![a Letalis geode](images/Letalis_geode.jpg)![Dazzle Crystal](images/Dazzle_Crystal.jpg)
1. Return to [[Jhavin Caelael]] at {{waypoint 607, 428, -558}}
1. Place a [[Refulgent Rune Crystal]] at each of the following points:
   - Stacez'ekloh spire of Grieg's Spire at {{waypoint 623, 429, -529}}
   - Winrul'ssat spire of Grieg's Spire at {{waypoint 743, 429, -529}}
   - Nneluta'wenor spire of Grieg's Spire at {{waypoint 743, 429, -634}}
   - Stacez'ekloh spire of Grieg's Spire at {{waypoint 624, 429, -634}}
   - The southern side of cave door below Grieg's Spire at {{waypoint 584, 303, -549}}
   - The northern side of cave door below Grieg's Spire at {{waypoint 587, 302, -573}}
1. Return to [[Jhavin Caelael]] at {{waypoint 606, 428, -556}}

## Rewards

- [[Refulgent Crystal Stud]]
- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
