## Steps

1. Recover 10 intact research papers within Malvonicus' Folly
   - The papers are random ground spawns - look for white sparkling loose pages and books and left click to harvest.  They're found inside and just outside the exploded tower, **including** on the floating exploded parts (prepare for alot of jumping, falling, etc.).![Floating Papers](images/Floating_Papers.jpg)![Research Papers](images/Research_Papers.jpg)
1. Recover research from the basement
   - Click on the wooden trapdoor to zone into Clackwhinge's Cellar, then left-click the bookcase -22.50 -9.00 152.96 58.59
1. Return to Clackwhinge

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
