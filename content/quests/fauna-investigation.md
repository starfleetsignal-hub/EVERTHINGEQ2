## Description

"Brodo Gillywump, a halfling at Green Knoll, is alarmed by a sickness that he fears is spreading through the Faydark. He wants me to go out and get some samples of what the deer within Treant Grove have been eating. To discover what they eat, I should approach a deer or stag in the wild." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

![nibbled root](images/Fauna_investigate.png)

1. Gather [[Nibbled root|nibbled roots]] by walking up to the sapling fawn or sapling stags near {{waypoint 532.79, -0.88, -87.98}}
1. Return to [[Brodo Gillywump]]

## Rewards

- At least 2s 57c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Brodo Gillywump: "This is not good. No, no. Not good at all!"
- Player Character: "What is wrong, Brodo?"
- Brodo Gillywump: "Something is wrong with the flora and fauna near here. I don't know what's making them sick, but I am afraid that it'll spread."
- Player Character: "What do you mean?"
- Brodo Gillywump: "I have seen deer that are obviously sufferin' from an ailment. And the Faydark is more than just a collection of woods. The Faydark is more like a livin' organism! When one part of it is sick, then it may spread."
  - Player Character: "We don't want that!"
    - Brodo Gillywump: "No! Absolutely not. But unfortunately, I don't know what's causin' the sickness. Will you help me find out?"
  - Player Character: "What's causing it?"
    - Brodo Gillywump: "Not a clue! That's part of the problem. I do not know what it is that is causin' the sickness. Will you help me find out?"
- Player Character: "What can I do?"
- Brodo Gillywump: "Nearby, you'll find deer and stags. I'd like you to go find some and see if you can get samples of whatever it is they've been munchin' on. that might be worth studying. They'll get spooked when you approach 'em, but usually when that happens, they'll drop what they're eatin' anyway."
- Player Character: "I'll be back with the samples for you, Brodo."

Collect samples of whatever the deer and stags are eating.

I have collected the samples. Bring the samples back to Brodo at the Green Knoll.

- Brodo Gillywump: "Did you get the samples, [Player Character]?"
- Player Character: "Yes I did."
- Brodo Gillywump: "Excellent! Yes, these roots are tainted. Err, wait... it's not excellent that they are tainted. That's not what I mean. I am just glad to have identified the source. The pebbleberry bushes, that the deer love to eat, have been tainted."

I have given Brodo the samples. I have collected the samples and given them to Brodo.

I have brought Brodo the samples he requested.
