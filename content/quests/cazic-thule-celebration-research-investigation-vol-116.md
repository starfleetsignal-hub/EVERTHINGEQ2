## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The Herald of Cazic-Thule has informed me that summoning an Avatar requires the land to be attuned to their element. To achieve this, altars have been set up around the region, but they need to be supplied with power. I have been asked to assist with this by collecting objects infused with the essence of slain foes and offer them to an altar.

1. Collect 3 [[Fear Infused Ichor|fear infused ichor]] (random body drop from most mobs in [[Aether Wroughtlands]])
1. Use the [[Fear Infused Ichor|fear infused ichor]] and empower an altar.  Random spawn in The Feerrott, its yellow sparkles visible far away, possible locations of altar
   - {{waypoint -353, -4, 465}} by [[Golgoth Bridge]]
   - {{waypoint 90, -10, 181}} near the road through [[The Jungle of Alliz Evol]]
   - {{waypoint -593, 7, 334}} near the [[Ogre Guard Tower Ruins]]
   - {{waypoint -713, -2, 798}} near [[The Ruins of Thule]]
   - {{waypoint -542, -13.83, 800}} near [[Restored Druid Ring]]
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Faceless]] {{waypoint -1109, -1, 919}} in [[The Feerrott]]
