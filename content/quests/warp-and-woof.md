## Notes

- Requires having completed [[Whips and Stains]] to see the [[A spore-addled warpwolf]].
- Quest is automatically offered when you approach.
- Journal text: "I stumbled across a warpwolf, dazed and under attack by a sporefiend, in Carved Caps. Maybe it was my interactions with Kith in Yon Goroth or her confused cries, but I felt compelled to come to her rescue."

## Steps

![warpwolf puppy](images/Warpwolf_puppy.png)

1. hail and kill the "a sporefiend hunter" next to warpwolf
1. convince warpwolf to follow you and lead it back to [[The Unknown Obelisk]] {{waypoint -892, -52, 985}} where he changes into [[A recovering warpwolf]]
1. find and save 7 missing warpwolf puppies (trackable a warpwolf puppy) around [[Carved Caps]] area {{waypoint -1172, 21, 965}}
1. return to [[A recovering warpwolf]] at [[The Unknown Obelisk]]

## Rewards

- [[Warpwolf Puppy]] house pet
- [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into  15 x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
