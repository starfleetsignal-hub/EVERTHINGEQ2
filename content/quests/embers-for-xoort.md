## Steps

1. Slay [[A fiery forgotten guardian|fiery forgotten guardians]] in the [[The Forest Ruins]] until you collect two embers.
1. Return to [[Sage Xoort]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
- +65 faction with **The Concordium**
- One of the following:
  - [[Ember scarred axe]]
  - [[Ember scarred cestus]]
  - [[Ember scarred dagger]]
  - [[Ember scarred dirk]]
  - [[Ember scarred greatflail]]
  - [[Ember scarred hammer]]
