This quest is part 1 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Dark Elf Series.

## Steps

1. Visit [[The Destroyed Knowledge Portal]] in the center of Longshadow Alley {{waypoint 0, 0, 0}}.
1. Visit the North Freeport gate {{waypoint 16, 0, 75}}.
1. Visit the entrance to Thieves' Way {{waypoint 44, 0, -23}}.
1. Visit the docks {{waypoint -48, 0, -78}}.
1. Return to Aldera V'Exxa for your reward

- At least 1s 6c
