## Notes

- While this quest is no longer required to enter [[The Condemned Catacomb]], it is still required for permanent access.
- All group members must either have, or have previously completed, this quest for the group to zone in

## Steps

1. Find the [[The Condemned Catacomb]]
   - The entrance is a sewer grate located on the *Necrosis Goblin Isle* directly below the cliff-top camp at {{waypoint -1139, -24, -1046}}.

## Rewards

- Experience
- Access to the [[The Condemned Catacomb]]
