## Materials Required

- 30 [[Amsonia Roots]]
- 10 [[Forlorn Ore]]
- 10 [[Greytusk Hide]]
- 15 [[Mahngavi Mango]]
- 10 [[Narra Wood]]
- 10 [[Star Diopside]]
- 1 [[Kamapor Tea]] --*rare*
- 1 [[Tsavorite]] --*rare*
- 15 [[Astral Coal]]
- 45 [[Astral Incense]]
- 5 [[Astral Candle]]

Materials can be bought for recipes below.

## Notes

Upon accepting the quest, [[Recipe Scroll: Alghoza's Gifts]] will auto-scribe, granting you the necessary recipes.

## Steps

1. I should create a journal that Alghoza can write in
   1. Craft 5 [[Sheaf of Paper]] (multicraft will not update)
   1. Craft the [[Journal Binding]]
   1. Craft the [[Vial of Purple Ink]]
   1. Craft [[Alghoza's Journal]]
1. I should create an Ensorcelled Broadcasting Amulet that Alghoza can use in an emergency
   1. Craft the [[Small Metallic Links]]
   1. Craft the [[Amulet Enchantment Scroll]]
   1. Craft the [[Ensorcelled Broadcasting Amulet]]
1. Give the gifts to Alghoza in Karuupa Jungle at {{waypoint 525, 145, -593}}
