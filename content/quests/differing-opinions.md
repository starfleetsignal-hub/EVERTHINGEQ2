## Steps

1. Speak to [[General Thalgus Icemaul]]
1. Speak to the four Thurgardin citizens whose name were written in your Journal
   - [[Lieutenant Dralgin]] in Icewell Keep {{waypoint 314, -292, 218}}
   - [[Marrin Fallenfrost]] in the Thurgadin Exchange {{waypoint 881, -274, 138}}
   - [[Feldia Hammerwind]] in Remembrance Park {{waypoint 576, -294, 412}}
   - [[Drolk Heavyblaze]] in the Velium Keg {{waypoint 707, -313, 354}}
1. Return to [[General Thalgus Icemaul]] in [[Storm Gorge]] {{waypoint 1042, -425, 762}}

- At least 11g 72s 32c
