## NOTE:

- The quest will not update in [[Castle Vacrul: Throne of the Ydal (Solo)]] . You must be in the quest version of this zone, not the mission version.
- Also, another player **CAN** join you in this "solo" zone as long as they are on the same step of the Signature quest. If grouped with someone who has already completed the Signature, the quest version of zone will not be available until they leave group.
- ***IF*** you ***DUO*** the quest-version of zone, ***BOTH PLAYERS*** will need to drop mercs. The zone only admits '1 or 2 players' and each merc counts as a player.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Castle Vacrul: Throne of the Ydal]] and fight your way through the dungeon.
1. Enter Mayong's [[Ydal Throne Room]] {{waypoint 448, 110, 257}}.
1. Find out if Mistmoore has the Ewer of Sul'Dae.
   - Defeat Mayong as per step 5 of the walkthrough in [[Castle Vacrul: Throne of the Ydal]]
1. Return to [[Tavian Faust]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 58508 status
- [[Libant's Ring of Insight]]
