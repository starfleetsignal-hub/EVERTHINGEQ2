- You must be at least a level 70 Artisan to obtain this quest.

1. Release the [[Trained Riliss Falcon]] from within [[Bathezid's Watch]].
1. Return to [[Assistant Zhary]].

- Tradeskill Experience
- At least 17g 22s 36c
- + 2,000 faction with **Riliss**
