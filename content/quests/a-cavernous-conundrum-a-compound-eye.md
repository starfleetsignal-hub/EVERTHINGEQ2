I can only describe this as a large eye that appears to have many different facets within it, like a finely cut jewel. The eye has been preserved and was kept as some form of trophy. I feel oddly compelled to find out more about it, while on the dark side of this moon.

## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A chloro-fly|chloro-flies]] at [[The Chlorochamber]] {{waypoint 240.50, 157.79, 442.12}} in [[Echo Caverns]].

## Rewards

- [[Crown Receptacle (Tier 5) (Version 1)|Crown Receptacle (Tier 5)]]
- [[Compound Eye]] :  this item is needed for the collection [[Creatures of the Dark Side]]
