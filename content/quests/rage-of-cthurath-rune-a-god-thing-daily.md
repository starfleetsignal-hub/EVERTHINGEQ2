## Notes

- This quest was originally given by Lady Najena herself. Since [[LU131]] [[Gerion's Reckoning]] it is given by [[Flamebearer Aobrey]]
- Requires adventure level 135+
- This [Solo] mission is a 'replay' of [[Rage of Cthurath: Rune a God Thing]] Signature finale but with a few extra boss mechanics thrown in... for funsies.
- Players may re-run this mission every 18 hrs (*See the 'Mission Timer' tab in the quest journal for exact reset times*)

## Steps

1. Use the [[Teleport]] portal at {{waypoint -119, 1, -68}} to enter [[Gerion: Rage of Cthurath (Solo)|Gerion: Rage of Cthurath [Solo]]]
   - Clear the first room. As you eliminate soldiers, additional [[Avarice consumed souls]] will spawn in the center, these will also need to be eliminated.
   - Once cleared you will see message that teleport spindle has been activated, then use the [[Teleport to Throne Room]] portal at {{waypoint -119, 1, -68}}
1. Lady Najena at {{waypoint 85.38, 955.84, 11.72}} will instruct you to place the final cimmerott runic ward.
   - This will update the quest.
1. Place the final commerott runic ward at {{waypoint -71, 969, 0}} ![](images/Manifestation_of_The_Consumer_%28Level_143%29.png)
1. *Note: Newly geared 135s may wish clear the room first; as the boss fight pulls the room when you attack him. Otherwise, feel to ignore the 'trash' before the fight and just kill them during main boss fight when they're pulled en-masse.
1. Defeat the [[Manifestation of The Consumer]] (143 ^ ^ ^ Heroic)
   - A new static add also spawns from the central pit, [[Xebrith Tendrils]] (143 ^ ^ ^ Heroic) can be killed by players near the ledge. ![](images/Xebrith_Tendrils_143_Heroic.png). It spawns at the start of the fight, and once more after the first 'Drain/Adds' phase.  *It is highly recommended to defeat the tendrils both times during the fight, especially if newly geared/leveled*
     - While the Xebrith Tendrils are active, a large Left or Right 'Arm of the Consumer' will spawn from the walls behind the throne. In order to dodge these large untargetable hazards, the player is instructed to physically crouch or sit.  *Removing the above Xebrith Tendrils will despawn the untargettable hazards, negating this mechanic until the next round the tendrils spawn again.*
   - At 75% and 50% the boss will perform a scripted 'health/power drain' (*"The Consumer is feeding off of you!"*) When Lady Najena says "Get behind the stone I summoned!" get behind the large stone that appears in front of the throne on the lower platform behind you and stay there until the stone is destroyed.
     - If the Left or Right 'Arm of the Consumer' is still up, it WILL destroy the stone prematurely on the next swing, exposing you to the remainder of the boss's 'drain' effect.  *Again, defeating the Xebrith Tendrils behind you will remove the Arms of the Consumer from the fight until the tendrils spawn again.*
   - After each 'drain' phase, the boss goes invulnerable, requiring the defeating of summoned adds to continue. A shadowy portal will open on the ground in front of the boss and multiple waves of adds enter from it; eliminate the waves to re-engage the boss.
- Reward is given immediately upon death of the boss.
