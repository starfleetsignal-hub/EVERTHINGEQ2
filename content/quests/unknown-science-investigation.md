## Steps

1. Enter [[Temple Street: Vault of Necromechanica]] {{waypoint -183, -37, 314}} (SE corner of The City of Freeport)
1. Kill a linked [[A necromechanist]] (level, one down) and [[A necrotik]] (level, two down) to obtain necrotik clockwork cores (6) (Seem to spawn anywhere in the zone including inside rooms)
1. Click on green shiny highlighted pages to obtain necromechanical research notes (6)
1. Return to Motius

- +500 faction with **The Academy of Arcane Science**
