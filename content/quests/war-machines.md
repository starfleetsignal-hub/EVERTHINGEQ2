## Steps

1. I need to head north and infiltrate the ogre city of Oggok. I have to cripple their ability to wage war against Gehein. This means eliminating their leaders as well as damaging their weapon supplies and ability to create new weapons.
   - Slay Oggok warbringers (5) inside stone huts, fast repop, easier to just wait in one hut.
   - Destroy ogre weapon caches (6) inside and near doorway to stone huts, slow repop.
   - Destroy ogre forges (3), near stone huts, fast repop, easier to just wait next to one of them.
1. I should return to Zeeil at Ashen Tower and inform him of the ogres' destruction.
   - Speak to [[Zeeil]] {{waypoint -331, -7, 839}} at Ashen Tower
