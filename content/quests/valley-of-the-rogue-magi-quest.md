## Description

Now that I consumed this potion I will be able to pass through the rockpile, allowing me to enter the Valley of the Rouge Magi.

## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- Upon completing the quest [[The Secret Valley]], you receive the item [[Potion of Earthwalking]]. This is not an item you can equip, but when you examine it, it gives this quest, and the ability to pass through the rocks to enter the [[Valley of the Rogue Magi]].
- Subsequent purchases of the potion from [[Marcus]], who stands at the entrance, gives direct access (examination is not needed again) to the instace for up to  3 characters.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Valley of the Rogue Magi]] at {{waypoint 679, -52, 448}}. The quest completes upon entry.

## Rewards

- Access to [[Valley of the Rogue Magi]].
