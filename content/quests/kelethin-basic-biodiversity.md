This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Gibrien gives you a random task from the following:
   - Take a [[Biodiversity Kit]] to [[Gabriella Mosswood]] {{waypoint -820, 86, 245}} at [[Emerald Lake]]
   - Take salt cubes to a rock under the city at {{waypoint 25, -3, 184}}
   - Kill 3 orcs
1. Return to Gibrien.

## Notes

- If you do not like the random task assigned, you can delete it and try for a different one.

## Rewards

- -3000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- +3000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
- -3000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- Experience
