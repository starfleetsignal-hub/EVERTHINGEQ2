## Steps

1. Go to the *Tomb of Te'Anara, Guildmaster of the Jaggedpine Treefolk*, underwater near [[Coldwind Cove]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]]. {{waypoint 394, -79, 1437}} **Note:** Swim on the surface, or fly, to the waypoint and then **DIVE to the sea bottom or the tomb will not spawn.**
1. Gather 3 sacks from around the tomb.
1. Approach the camp of Unkempt Druids in the eastern part of the [[Ruins of Karana]]. {{waypoint -692, 12, 552}}
1. Speak with [[Eilwyn Loamskin]].
1. Retreive the key from one of the nearby chests.
   - The surrounding [[A fanatical ranger|fanatical rangers]] will attack once the chest is opened. Killing them is not necessary.
1. Return to the tomb and open it with the [[Gold key to the Tomb of Te'Anara]] and the [[Silver key to the Tomb of Te'Anara]].
1. Examine the tomb again to obtain the [[Shard of the Rainkeeper]].
1. Return to Askr.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 6s
- +2500 faction with **Karana**
- [[Karana's Amulet of the Gale]]
