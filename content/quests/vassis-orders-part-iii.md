This is part 3 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Freeport]] who wear Leather and Chain Armor ([[Shaman|shaman]]).

## Steps

1. You must collect items from creatures in [[Nektulos Forest]].
   - Kill 12 [[Ash shriller|ash shrillers]].
   - Kill  2 [[Ash viper|ash vipers]].
   - Kill  4 [[Haze stalker|haze stalkers]] or [[Dusk leaper|dusk leapers]] (may depend on class)
1. Return to Vassi.

- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Sandals of the Dreadnaught]] - ([[Bruiser]])
  - [[Runic Ceremonial Boots]] - ([[Defiler]])
  - [[Boots of Nature's Fatebinder]] - ([[Fury]])
  - [[Sandals of the Silent Fist Initiate]] - ([[Monk]])
  - [[Blessed Ivy Engraved Boots]] - ([[Warden]])
