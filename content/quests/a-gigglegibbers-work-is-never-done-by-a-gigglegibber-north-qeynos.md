***This quest scales to the level tier of the character (i.e. level 21-30 characters will receive the level 25 version of the quest).*** You may need to kill more than the listed number of monsters in order to retrieve the required drops for this quest.

## Steps

1. Complete the appropriate kill task based on character's level tier:
   - **Tier 1 (Level 1-10)**
     - Kill 5 [[Muck toad|muck toads]] in [[The Peat Bog]]
     - Kill 5 [[Mystail matriarch|mystail matriarch]] in [[Oakmyst Forest]]
   - **Tier 2 (Level 11-20)**
     - Kill 5 [[Mutated rat|mutated rats]] in [[Antonica]]
     - Kill 5 [[Beaded lizard|beaded lizards]] in [[Antonica]]{{waypoint -437, -21, -453}}
   - **Tier 3 (Level 21-30)**
     - Kill 5 [[Skeletal footsoldier|skeletal footsoldiers]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]]
     - Kill 5 [[Undead caretaker|undead caretakers]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]]
   - **Tier 4 (Level 31-40)**
     - Kill 5 [[Runnyeye mercenary|Runnyeye mercenaries]] in [[Enchanted Lands]]
     - Kill 5 [[Threatening commoner|threatening commoners]] in [[Enchanted Lands]]
   - **Tier 5 (Level 41-50)**
     - Kill 5 [[A primal frostfin|primal frostfins]] in [[Everfrost]]
     - Kill 5 [[Wooly silk slinger|wooly silk slingers]] in [[Everfrost]]
   - **Tier 6 (Level 51-60)** - choice of Faydwer or Sinking Sands
     - *Faydwer*
       - Kill 5 [[Grovespirit|grovespirits]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]]
       - Kill 5 [[Honeybrood worker|honeybrood workers]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]]
     - *Sinking Sands*
       - Kill 5 [[An Anaz Mal veilguard]] in [[The Living Tombs]]
       - Kill 5 mummies such as [[A Sulite tjat (Silent City)|Sulite tjat]] in [[The Silent City]]
   - **Tier 7 (Level 61-70)** - choice of Faydwer or the Overrealm.
     - *Overrealm* (solo version)
       - Gather 5 enchanted lumps of coal from the ridgestone golems in [[The Barren Sky]]. Any ridgestone golem on Whisperwind Isle will work, for example [[A ridgestone defender]].
       - Gather 5 twinkles of an evil eye from any dryeye vehemences in [[The Barren Sky]]. Any dryeye mobs on the Isle of Aversion will work, for example [[A dryeye vehemence]] or [[A dryeye entangler]].
     - *Faydwer* (heroic version)
       - Remove 5 gnawed and slobbered boots from the Kragbak kobolds in [[Kaladim]]. These are heroic mobs in the low 30's.
       - Retrieve 5 hunks of ensorcelled obsidian from the obsidian constructs in [[Mistmoore Catacombs]]. These are heroic mobs and are not soloable.
   - **Tier 8 (Level 71-82)** - [[Kunzar Jungle]] near the [[Druid Ring]]
     - Kill 5 [[A kunzar scaleripper|scalerippers]] and/or [[Scaleshredder|scaleshredders]] in the rivers of [[Kunzar Jungle]] to get 5 [[Shimmery scale|shimmery scales]].
     - Kill 5 [[Rubble-rabble burynai]] in the [[Rubble-rabble Caves]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]] to get 5 [[Grievance pole|grievance poles]].
   - **Tier 9 (level 83-90)** - Sundered Frontier and Stonebrunt Highlands
     - Kill 5 [[A Toxxulia guardian|Toxxulia Guardians]] (near the Toxxulia Forest Station launchpad) in [[The Sundered Frontier]]
     - Kill 5 [[A slitherstrike mamba|Slitherstrike Mamba]] (near [[Moonfield Hamlet]]) in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]]
   - **Tier 10 (Level 91-95)**
     - Kill 5 [[Insidious fearlings|insidious fearlings]] in [[Eidolon Jungle]]
     - Kill 5 [[Churning obol|churning obol]] in [[Obol Plains]]
1. Return to [[Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber (North Qeynos)|Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber]]

This quest is repeatable up to 10 times per tier per year.

- [[Frostfell Tree]] (Reward for completing the quest the first time.)

  - [[Festive Gigglegibber Tapestry]] (*NEW* for 2010)
