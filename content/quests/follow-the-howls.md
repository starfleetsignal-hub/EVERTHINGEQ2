## Description

Howling can be heard from the Stygian Threshold. I have been asked to enter the cavern and discover the source of the howling, and end it should it prove a danger to Maldura.

## Steps

1. Kill the spider queen.
   - [[Zaraxia]] in [[Stygian Threshold (Heroic)]]
1. Slay the howling exile.
   - [[Mawz Harak]] in [[Stygian Threshold (Heroic)]]

- At least 2p 26g 80s 41,600 status
