## Notes

- You MUST finish the [[Ballads of Zimara Timeline]] signature quest before the quest starter will drop for you.
- it can be done in any order, fastest travel route by discovery name going clockwise
- You can save a lot of time by mousing over the area to start the associated 5 sub-quests.
  1. Step #4 [[Old Workshop]] {{waypoint -349, -25, -909}}
  1. Step #2 [[Ancient Battle Site]] {{waypoint -324, 105, -245}}
  1. Step #3 [[Timeworn Ruins]] {{waypoint -735, 298, 605}}
  1. Step #5 [[Remote Vista]] {{waypoint 925, -43, 516}}
  1. Step #1 [[Lost Supply Wagon]] {{waypoint 565, -58, 64}}
- unlocks the [[Experimental Reconstruction]] achievement once this quest and the 5 subquests are done

## Steps

- ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Golem_Upper_Chassis.png)Travel to {{waypoint 563, -58, 59}} to discover [[Lost Supply Wagon]]
  - inspect **an Golem Upper Chassis** (looks like a plate chest piece) on the crate to start the quest [[Aether Wroughtlands: Experiment BU-T13-R's Upper Chassis]]
- ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Golem_Lower_Chassis.png)Travel to {{waypoint -326, 107, -259}} to discover [[Ancient Battle Site]]
  - inspect a **Golem Lower Chassis** (looks like plate pants) in the wreckage to start the quest [[Aether Wroughtlands: Experiment BU-T13-R's Lower Chassis]]
- ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Golden_Harmonic_Controller.png)Travel to {{waypoint -734, 297, 604}} to discover [[Timeworn Ruins]]
  - inspect a **Golem Harmonic Controller** in the dirt pile (very tiny - look closely) to start the quest [[Aether Wroughtlands: Experiment BU-T13-R's Harmonic Controller]]
- ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Golem_Sensory_Focus.png)Travel to {{waypoint -340.49, -23, -907}} to discover [[Old Workshop]]
  - inspect a **Golem Sensory Focus** (a small black orb on the wooden tables) to start the quest [[Aether Wroughtlands: Experiment BU-T13-R's Sensory Focus]]
- ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Golem_Arcane_Generator.png)Travel to {{waypoint 930, -43, 517}} to discover [[Remote Vista]]
  - inspect **an Golem Arcane Generator** (a dark crystal formation in the basket) to start the quest [[Aether Wroughtlands: Experiment BU-T13-R's Arcane Generator]]

## Rewards

- Experience only
