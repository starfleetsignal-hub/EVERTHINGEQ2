## Steps

1. You must find some water.
   - There is a tree next to the aqueduct north of the [[Glade of the Coven]] {{waypoint -1465, -13.5, -360}}. Click on the tree to climb up onto the aquaduct where you must inspect a strange globe.
   - You may need to right-click in the area around the tree to get a menu option for 'Climb the Tree.'
1. Inspect the vessel again.
1. You must inspect a larger portion of the country.
   - Go to the [[Oracle Tower Station]] and fly to the [[Steppes Station]]. Update is somewhere in the air.
1. Inspect the vessel again.
1. You must find some fire.
   - Go to [[Sir Jeager's Camp]] {{waypoint -800, -14, 891}} and inspect the campfire.
   - Note:  You can take the griffon from Steppes to Qeynos Gate and jump off right at Jaeger's.
1. Inspect the vessel again.
1. You must find some earth.
   - Go to the fork in the [[Qeynos Highway]] just West of [[Sayer's Outfitters]] {{waypoint -707, -16, 185}}.
1. Inspect the vessel again to finish the quest.

## Rewards

- Ability: [[Set Recall Point]], used to set the bind point for the [[Call of Qeynos]] ability
