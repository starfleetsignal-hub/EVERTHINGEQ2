## Steps

1. Gather some eyes for Oglethorpe
   - Gather two coyote eyes - any wolf type monster in the zone updates
     - [[A mountain coyote]], [[A feral coyote]], [[A Grikbar hound]], [[A mountain howler]], [[A mountain stalker]]
   - Gather two minotaur eyes
     - Any [[Clan Smokehorn (Faction)|Smokehorn]] or Bloodhorn minotaurs update.
   - Gather two drake eyes
     - [[An ebon drake|Ebon drakes]] update
   - Gather two spider eyes
     - [[A yellow recluse spider]], [[A yellow recluse spiderling]], [[A pale spitting spider]], [[A Mazkeen recluse]], [[A Mazkeen war spider]], [[A large yellow recluse]], [[A large pale spitting spider]]
1. Return to Oglethorpe.

Note: The drop rate for these eyes is uncommon at best. It will take a bit of hunting to get them all.

## Reward

- At least 2g 99s 47c
