## Notes

- This quest can be shared with someone who has completed [[By Land? By Air? By Sea?]], however if the required amount of time has not elapsed, the player will be told how much longer they need to wait before the quest can be accepted.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Halls of Fate]] and kill [[Sothis]].
1. Inspect the book at {{waypoint -1.88, -68.96, 310}}.
1. Return to [[Arbiter Selek]].

## Rewards

- At least 13g
- [[Arbiter's Token]]
