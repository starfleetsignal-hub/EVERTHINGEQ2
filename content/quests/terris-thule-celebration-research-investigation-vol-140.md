## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The avatar summoning rituals require the manipulation of immense power and are inherently dangerous. To reduce the chance of failure and provide an anchor for that power, the herald of Terris-Thule has asked me to collect an item that may have once belonged to that god or one of their most loyal followers

- Collect Reliquaries of Terris-Thule from a powerful creature in [[Zimara Breadth: Wisdom's Plight (Event Heroic I)|Zimara Breadth: Wisdom's Plight]] heroic dungeon.

**This does not update in [Solo]. It needs to be Zimara Breadth: Wisdom's Plight [Event Heroic I].**

- Return to the [[Herald of the Dream Scorcher]] {{waypoint -33, -37, 265}} in [[Darklight Wood]]
