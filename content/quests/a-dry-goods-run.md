## Steps

1. Receive the recipe: [[High Explosives For All Occasions]] from [[Commissioner Varla Z'Velran|Varla]]
1. I should make the [[Keg of Explosive Powder|keg of explosive powder]] at a [[Chemistry Table]] at {{waypoint -355, 177, -30}}. The recipe requires 1 Severed Maple, 1 Iron Cluster, 1 Electrum Cluster and 2 Glowing Candles.
1. I should place the keg of explosives in the storehouse at {{waypoint 97, 150, -85}}.
1. I should detonate the keg and stand well back!
1. I should return to [[Commissioner Varla Z'Velran|Varla]].
