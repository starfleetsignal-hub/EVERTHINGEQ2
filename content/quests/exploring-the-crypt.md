## Notes

- This quest unlocks [[The Crypt of T'haen: Endless Twilight]], a sub-instance of [[The Crypt of T'haen]].
- Do not kill T'rath the Usurper as you will need to kill him for the next quest in this line. If you do kill him you'll need to wait and reset the zone to try again.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Crypt of T'haen: Endless Twilight]] instance. The entrance is within [[The Tombs of Night]] at {{waypoint -107, -32, 102}}.
1. Scout various locations around the the crypt:
   - Lower Falls {{waypoint 37, 1, 0}}
   - Burial Chamber {{waypoint -11, 0, -35}}
     - You might need to swim under water for this update in the cave, the loc did not give an update.
   - Middle Caves {{waypoint -5, 24, -56}}
   - Resting Chamber {{waypoint 90, 57, -88}}
1. Return to [[Nayli R'Ger]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 9s 10c
- One of the following:
  - 5x [[Potion of the Night (5.6 Power)|Potion of the Night]]
  - 5x [[Salve of the Night (Version 2)|Salve of the Night]]
- Access to the Bloodline Chronicles Raid zone
