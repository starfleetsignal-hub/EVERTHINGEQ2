## Steps

1. **Note that this quest cannot be taken until you have completed [[Caught by the Tail]] for [[Outrider Storian]].**
1. Follow Metinga's path to [[Scarstone]]
   - Use [[Metinga's Glade Rune]] that she gave you and her path will appear as swirls of leaves and green sparkles, but as long as you go to Scarstone the game doesn't care how you get there.
1. Speak to [[Norg Gorefist]] in a tent in front of Scarstone at {{waypoint 4304, -821, 401}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- [[New Combine Trinket Box]]
