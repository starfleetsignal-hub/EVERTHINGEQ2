## Steps

1. Furtog Ogrebane and I are going to defend Wayward mages from the Horde at the southeast barrier, known as the Cardin Gate.
   1. Speak with Furtog Ogrebane when ready to depart
   1. Slay the ambushing Harrowing Horde (4)
      - Approach the Cardin Gate near {{waypoint -746, 10, 247}}, and 1-2 horde mobs will spawn at a time and attack you.  When you have killed 4, the quest will update.
1. I should return to General Furtog Ogrebane, where he was fighting, near the Cardin Gate.
   - Return to [[Furtog Ogrebane|General Furtog Ogrebane]] {{waypoint -708, 11, 240}} near the Cardin Gate
1. I should return to the Wayward town of Cardin Ward, and speak with General Furtog Ogrebane. He says a reward awaits me!
   - Return to [[Furtog Ogrebane|General Furtog Ogrebane]] {{waypoint -455, 10, 14}} in Cardin Ward

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Dagger of the Emerald Flame]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Aetherblade]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Mourning's Edge]] (Druids)
  - [[Cudgel of Mourning]] (All Priests)
  - [[Fist of the Harrowing Sirocco]] (All Fighters, All Mages, All Priests, Beastlord)
  - [[Switch of Sorrow's End]] (Mage reward)
