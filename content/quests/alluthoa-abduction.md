You receive [[Corrith's Barbed Grasp]] at the start of the quest.

## Steps

1. Capture a subdued Allu'thoa high shaman
   - [[An Allu'thoa high shaman]] is in the Allu'thoa village [[Gazanti]] at possible locations {{waypoint -1076, 124, 1070}}{{waypoint -1049.21, 92.28, 943.50}}{{waypoint -1149.73, 101.43, 950.41}}. The named spawns at the same places but does not give the update.
   - Use the Corrith's Barbed Grasp on it; it de-aggros and returns to its spawn spot.  Left click to "capture" it.
1. Return to the beach camp - quest updates when you get near Corrith
1. Speak to Corrith, then interrogate the captured Allu'thoa.
1. Found the high shaman at (-1074, 124, 1059).

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g 80s
- [[Dshinn Twisted Palm]]
