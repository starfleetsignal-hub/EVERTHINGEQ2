## Steps

1. Kill 12-14 [[A Sabertooth lookout (Thundering Steppes)|Sabertooth lookouts]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]].  They can be found around the beach near the [[Antonica]] zone line {{waypoint -65, -23, -996}} and the cliffs above the beach. There is also some near the Antonica secondary zone tunnel. Some can also be found grouped with ^^ gnolls in and around craters.

## Rewards

- 1680 status @L21
- +500 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
