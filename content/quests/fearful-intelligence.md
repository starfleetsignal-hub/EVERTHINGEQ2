## Steps

1. I should search for more pieces of the medallion.
   - Search for the first medallion piece
     - Kill [[An eastern guardian]] {{waypoint -2364, 206, 548}}
   - Search for the second medallion piece
     - Kill [[A western guardian]] {{waypoint -1507, 222, 356}}
   - Search for the third medallion piece
     - Kill [[A southern guardian]] {{waypoint -2101, 221, 1156}}
1. I have assembled the medallion, and it seems to be a small map of the nearby area. Perhaps if I head there, I can find out more about the meaning of this object.
   1. Go to the area marked on the medallion map {{waypoint -2336, 209, 858}}
   1. Defeat the massive shiverback
      - [[Tsarr Kurr]], level 94^
   1. Return proof of your victory to the amygalians
      - Speak with [[Urlthoo]] {{waypoint -1335, 218, 597}}
