## Steps

1. Speak with [[Firiona Vie]] at the [[Cloister of Growth]] in [[Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint 680.56, 51.58, 136.08}}
1. Travel to [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]], proceed to the adventure party at [[Goldami Reservoir Camp]] at {{waypoint -543.00, 10.96, 428.84}}, and [[Firiona Vie]] will pop up when you approach the entrance to [[Buried Takish'Hiz]].
1. Speak to [[Firiona Vie]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 57791 status
- The ability to fly within [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]]
