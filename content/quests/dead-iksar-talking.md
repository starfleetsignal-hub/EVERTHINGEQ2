## Steps

1. Enter [[Sebilis]] and speak with [[A traitorous spirit]] in the [[Ancient Sebilisian Crypt (Sebilis)|Ancient Sebilisian Crypt]]. {{waypoint -25.07, -7.43, 182.91}}
   - Follow the dialog closely; you must take the answer path that leads him to tell you about Venril Sathir's Phylactery.
1. Report your findings to [[Foreman Guppka]] at the Reet camp in Kunzar Jungle.

## Rewards

- At least 32g 50s
- +1,000 faction with **Synod Reet**
- [[Nightwatch Chain Sleeves]]
