## Steps

![a dirty doll](images/A_dirty_doll_%28Lesser_Faydark%29.jpg)

1. Harvest a [[Dirty doll]] near the [[Tower of Zet]] in [[The Shadowed Grove]]. {{waypoint 848, -40, 456}}
   - Spawn locations are random. Known locations have been adeed to the talk page.
1. Return the doll to Pinjya.

## Rewards

- at least 13g 70s 47c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Earring of the Frenzied Sands]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Facet of the Sandstorm]]
  - [[Spike of the Sandstorm]]
  - [[Stud of the Sandstorm]] (All Fighters)
