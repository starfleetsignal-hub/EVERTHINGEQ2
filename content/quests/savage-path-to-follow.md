## Steps

1. Search for other savage creatures around {{waypoint -29, 39, -101}}
1. kill the following
   - Kill 4 [[A savage greytusk|savage greytusks]] around {{waypoint -29, 39, -101}}
   - Kill 6 [[A savage hyena|savage hyenas]] around {{waypoint -61, 41, -26}} or {{waypoint -185, 44, -63}}
   - Kill 6 [[A savage rhino|savage rhinos]] near {{waypoint -382, 39, -285}} or {{waypoint -325, 41, -119}}
1. Search for the infection source: **a dune walker** at {{waypoint -410, 40, -179}}  ![](images/Savage_Path_to_Follow_quest_-_a_dune_walker.jpg)
1. Return to [[Defender Bhandat]].
