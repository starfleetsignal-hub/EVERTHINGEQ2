A dhalgar historian by the name of Dhalkuhm has been recording the events leading up to and surrounding the Aberrant siege of the city. He has devised a magical way of capturing an image onto canvas, and has asked for my help.

## Steps

1. Kill abyssal [[A fathomlurker|fathomlurkers]] between Abyssal Overlook and  the [[Splitiron Mine]] in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]] {{waypoint -28.87, -65.71, -178.37}} to obtain 6 [[Fathomlurker Mistsac|Fathomlurker Mistsacs]].
1. Use a [[Scroll of Arcane Canvas]] to record the requested images:
   1. [[Outer Maldura]] {{waypoint 503.16, 135.24, 223.41}}
   1. [[Stoneheart Yards]] {{waypoint 469.3, 115.2, 181.90}}
   1. [[Blackanvil Bay]] {{waypoint 740.43, 59.8, -72.23}}
   1. [[Ironblood Fungus Farm]] {{waypoint 785.82, 53.00, -264.08}}
   1. [[Blightstone Barrens]] {{waypoint 781.86, 108.46, -493.64}}
   1. [[Penumbra Expansion]] {{waypoint 507.25, 62.40, -626.72}}
1. Return to [[Dalkuhm]].
