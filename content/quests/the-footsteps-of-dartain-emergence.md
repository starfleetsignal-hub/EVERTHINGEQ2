## Requirements

- <s>You must be at least level 86</s> . I received this at level 84. The quest does soon send you to The Hole; a level 90 area.

## Steps

1. Find evidence of the presence of Dartain in the [[Paineel Council Chamber|Council Chamber]] in [[The City of Paineel]].
   - This is a clickable stack of books at {{waypoint 1852, -294, 3462}}.
1. Return to [[Delinah Khahiz]]
1. Speak with a Historiographer in Paineel, [[Pleni Mas'arc]], at {{waypoint 1850, -298, 3446}}.
1. I must search within Dartain's Fortress within [[The Hole: Spirit's Resonance]] {{waypoint 1852, -372, 3452}} for any artifacts that might have belonged to Dartain.
   1. One crystal is in the observatory in the pit beneath the telescope {{waypoint 557, 121, -49}}
   1. One crystal is in the garden area on the ground {{waypoint 728, 124, -204}} Though I don't find it there...
   1. One crystal is in the fire area in SW corner {{waypoint 608, 87, -118}} (or it can be a book to the right of the room in last name room)
   1. One crystal can be found in the pool room by the door. {{waypoint 594, 128, -339}}
   1. A book can be found at either {{waypoint 536, 132, -235}}
   1. or in [[Nortlav the Scalekeeper]]'s room, {{waypoint 510, 147, -170}}
1. Return to Pleni Mas'arc for your reward.
