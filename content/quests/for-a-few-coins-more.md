## Notes

- You must be able to understand the [[Di'Zokian]] language to complete this quest.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Colonel Leevan]], on a cliff next to [[Varis]], until a 10-minute countdown timer begins. {{waypoint -430, 119, -361}}
1. Keep trying to convince him that you are not the murderer until he stops and aggros. Defeat him and the speak to him again.
1. Speak with [[Indigo]] near [[Challenge Rock (POI)|Challenge Rock]]. {{waypoint -1665, 85, -597}}
1. Obtain the first tablet:
   - Go to [[Charnax]] and click on the chest on the top floor. {{waypoint 1242, 452, -1162}}
   - Inspect the notice on the wall. {{waypoint 1242, 427, -1170}}
   - Pick up the [[Key of Charnax]] from inside the yeti cave in [[Icebound Pass]]. {{waypoint 1327, 385, -780}}
   - Return to the chest and use the key to obtain the [[Tablet of the Youth]].
1. Obtain the second tablet:
   - Go to a tower in [[Westin Rouk]] and click on the chest on the top floor. {{waypoint 825, 412, -1592}}
   - Go to the base of the tower and talk to [[Seepay]]. {{waypoint 848, 383, -1543}}
   - Collect 4 *spider gems* from [[A Ryjesium ore foreman|Ryjesium ore foremans]] in the mines of Westin Rouk (uncommon update). {{waypoint 514, 351, -1581}}
   - Steal the *coin bladder* from [[Karatun Hold]]. It looks like a bag of flour and can be at three alternate locations:
     - [[Arbor King Zamont]]'s feet. {{waypoint 784, 90, -2249}}
     - On the table in the same structure as Arbor King Zamont. {{waypoint 822, 89, -2234}}
     - On a table in a shed. {{waypoint 923, 77, -2305}}
   - Return to Seepay.
   - Follow Seepay and open the chest once he unlocks it to obtain the [[Tablet of the Lost]].
1. Obtain the third tablet:
   - Go to the [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]] outside [[Chardok]] and open the chest on the top floor. {{waypoint 1478, 165, -2904}}
   - Speak with [[Historian Holdak]] just outside the door. {{waypoint 1491, 165, -2921}}
   - Gather 4 *sarnak petroglyphs* from the floor in the back of the [[Den of the Broodmother]]. {{waypoint 1718, 233, -2049}}
   - Return to Historian Holdak and he will give you the [[Tablet of the Wizard]].
1. Obtain the fourth tablet:
   - Go to the northernmost tower of [[The Sel'Nok Towers]] and click on the chest on the top floor. {{waypoint 2274, 197, -2622}}
   - Read the note behind the [[The Vault of Sel'Nok]] in the southernmost Sel'Nok Tower. {{waypoint 2082, 188, -2549}}
     - You must be able to understand the [[Di'Zokian]] language.
   - Defeat the Vault of Sel'Nok to receive the [[Key of Sel'Nok]].
   - Obtain 6 gems from the nearby [[A Sel'Nok infiltrator (Kylong Plains)|Sel'Nok infiltrators]]. {{waypoint 2092, 135, -2441}}
   - Unlock the Sel'Nok chest and receive the [[Tablet of the Ruler]].
1. Return to Indigo. {{waypoint -1665, 85, -597}}
1. Speak with one of Indigo's minions. Then, within 20-minutes, defeat two waves of [[A drolvarg hooligan|drolvarg hooligans]] and [[A drolvarg henchman|henchman]] before finally killing Indigo. You will receive [[Music Puzzle Box]].
1. Return to [[Colonel Leevan]]. {{waypoint -430, 119, -361}}
   - Upon completion, Colonel Leevan mentions a "*bounty posted at the tower just beyond the pass into the Fens, which might interest a bounty hunter such as yourself.*" referring you to [[An Ugly Bounty]] that starts your Journey into the Fens of Nathsar.
