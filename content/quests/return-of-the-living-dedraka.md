## Steps

1. Explore old Dedraka village {{waypoint -123, 87, -535}} or {{waypoint -171.21, 104.75, -642.90}}
1. Slay undead pygmies {{waypoint -123, 87, -534}}
1. report to Bimse at the new Dedraka pygmy village {{waypoint -476, 37, 81}}
1. Slay Dedraka pygmies {{waypoint -575, 44, 200}}
1. Speak with Bimse, outside the new Dedraka Village {{waypoint -472, 36, 80}}
