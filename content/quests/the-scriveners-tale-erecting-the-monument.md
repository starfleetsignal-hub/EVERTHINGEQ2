## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 100 [[Celestial Coal]]

## Steps

1. Collect the components from the supply crate at {{waypoint 162, 43, 73}}.
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Monument Repair Kit]] and craft the [[Monument Repair Kit]] on the Worn Monument at {{waypoint 165, 43, 79}}.
1. Right-click on the Worn Monument at {{waypoint 165, 43, 79}} and choose 'Replace Worn Monument'.
1. Speak with [[Zuvet]] at {{waypoint 167, 43, 74}}.
1. Collect the components from the supply crate at {{waypoint 162, 43, 73}} and craft another [[Monument Repair Kit]] on the Monument at {{waypoint 165, 43, 79}}. Then Right-click on the Monument at {{waypoint 165, 43, 79}} and choose Polish Monument'.
1. Speak with [[Zuvet]] at {{waypoint 167, 43, 74}}.

## Rewards

- [[Pile of Red Shards]]
- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
