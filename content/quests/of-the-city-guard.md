## Steps

1. Help the Qeynos Guard track down a few mysteries happening in the city of Qeynos by investigating the brawls in [[Graystone Yard: Herald of Rage|Graystone Yard]].
   - The gate is located [[Qeynos Harbor]] in the [[Qeynos Province District]]. {{waypoint 864, -25, -89}}
1. Speak to 3 [[Enraged Qeynos Guard]] to urge them to return to their duty.
1. Locate and destroy 6 potential sources of the spreading rage.

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
