## Steps

1. Obtain the following items:
   - An Ancient Wastes Binding Spell from [[Foulgore the Rotten]] or [[Jund'Erin (Exploration Determination)|Frostking Jund'Erin]].
   - Petrified Draconic Remnants from [[Vul Lord Tarinax]] or [[Grand Shaman Shaekk]].
   - 50 Ancient Draconic Fables from T2 and T3 bosses
     - [[Orgain the Frenzied]]
     - [[Taskmaster Peggs]]
     - [[Overseer Decarun]]
     - [[The Stalking Doombringer]]
     - [[Hexxin the Nethertitan]]
     - [[Xychintus the Voidcaller]]
     - [[Guard Imobiun]]
     - [[Court Sorcerer Viranor]]
     - [[Enforcer Rimefield]]
     - [[Digger Grendok]]
     - [[Marrowseeker Hazzir]]
     - [[Lacetor the Fiendish]]

## Notes

All of the above quest items also have a chance at dropping from T2 and T3 trash mobs.
