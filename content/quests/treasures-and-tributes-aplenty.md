## Notes

- Repeatable Quest - must be completed twice

## Steps

1. hunt and obtain the following
   - Hunt 14 umbraclaw ravagers
   - Obtain 9 kaborite geodes or 9 Blackhook plunder crates
1. Return to Sputter

## Rewards

- Blackhook Rhodium Foot appearance item first time only
