## Notes

- Repeatable Quest - must be completed twice

## Steps

*Sputter, one of the goblins I saved in the Spiral of Vul, has grown less confident that the trinkets I gathered so far is enough to guarantee his and his kin's welcome return to the goblin clan by Chief Mucktongue.*

1. hunt and obtain the following:
   - Hunt ebonbane snapjaw for leather
   - Hunt 14 umbraclaw ravagers for fur
   - Obtain 9 kaborite geodes or 9 Blackhook plunder crates
1. Return to Sputter

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
