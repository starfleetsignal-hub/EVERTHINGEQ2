## Steps

1. Gather 12 Luclinite Ore from the [[Grikbar Crater]] on the west side of the zone. {{waypoint -306, 90, 1168}}
1. Return to Brom Knotwood

## Rewards

- At least 53s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Apostle's Plate Barbute of Commitment]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Cowl of Brilliance]] (All Mages)
  - [[Hanshi's Skullcap of Prowess]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Helmet of Force]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Oracle's Coif of the Perceptive]] (Shamans)
  - [[Protector's Cap of the Reflective]] (Druids)
  - [[Raider's Chain Helmet of Alacrity]] (All Scouts)
