*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Visit the following locations in the Feerrott. At each location, you will receive the message that your beacon glows. Inspect the beacon to get the quest update.
   - [[Murdunk's Fall]] {{waypoint -247, 8, 75}}
   - [[Restored Druid Ring]] {{waypoint -567, -14, 843}}
   - [[Tower of Vul]] {{waypoint -1006, -3, 450}}
   - [[The Sentinel Stones]] {{waypoint -713, 2, 545}}
   - [[Golgoth Bridge]] {{waypoint -371, -5, 465}}
   - [[Ogre Guard Tower]] {{waypoint -599, 5.6, 308}}
1. Return to Mashara to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 53s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Apostle's Plate Gloves of the Divinehand]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Gloves of the Runemaster]] (All Mages)
  - [[Hanshi's Hand Wraps of the Whirlingfist]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Gauntlets of the Warleader]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Oracle's Handguards of the Fatebringer]] (Shamans)
  - [[Protector's Leather Gloves of the Tempestwalker]] (Druids)
  - [[Raider's Chain Gloves of the Heartstriker]] (All Scouts)
