## Steps

1. Enter any of the main areas of the city of Qeynos (North Qeynos, South Qeynos, Qeynos Harbor, Elddar Grove).
1. **Within 30 minutes**, steal a vial of acid. Acid is typically located inside buildings on a table or counter.  They appear as a row of vials.
1. Use the vial of acid to destroy a weapons rack. You may need to enter all areas of Qeynos to find the weapons rack.
1. Return to [[Gol M'Tun]].

## Acid Beaker Locations

- Broker in [[North Qeynos]] across from [[The Ironforge Exchange]].
- The Fletching shop [[In Range]] in [[The Elddar Grove (POI)]] at Loc {{waypoint 572, -18, -412}}.

## Weapon Rack Locations

- Outside [[Irontoes East]] bar in [[North Qeynos]].
- Outside [[Call to Arms]] in [[North Qeynos]].
- Outside [[Antonia's House of Pottery]] in [[The Elddar Grove (POI)]].
- Inside the [[Silent Fist Dojo]] in [[The Elddar Grove (POI)]].
