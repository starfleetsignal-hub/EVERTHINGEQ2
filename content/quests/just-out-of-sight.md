## Description

"Lieutenant Fyladii told me that the grobin lookouts on the overhand to the south of Captain Steelforge's camp are hampering their efforts to enter the cave. She has asked that I find a way onto the overhand and confront the grobins." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Go to the tree trunk path {{waypoint 16, -40, -523}}
1. Climb up and go west over bark bridge; kill 4 [[A grobin lookout|grobin lookout]]s {{waypoint 129.06, -0.40, -502.96}}
1. Return to Lieutenant Fyladii

## Rewards

- At least 90c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Lieutenant Fyladii: "I'm glad you've come. The sentries told us how you saved the Nursery, and I am hoping you might be able to save us. You see, we're under observation and sporadic attack from the grobin lookouts on the cliff above us, and we're effectively pinned down here."
- Player Character: "Goblins are as weak as they are cowardly. They're on the cliffs above, you say?"
- Lieutenant Fyladii: "Yes, on that cliff to the south. You'll need to find a way up there, but I imagine they'll be less of a threat once they're face to face with a real adventurer."
- Player Character: "I'll see what I can do."

Find a way up to the grobins.

Defeat grobin lookouts.

Report back to Lieutenant Fyladii.

- Lieutenant Fyladii: "I thought I saw you headed back this way. How did things go with the lookouts?"
- Player Character: "I was able to take several of them down, so they can no longer watch over your activities here."
- Lieutenant Fyladii: "Ah, I can't tell you how much we appreciate your help. With the lookouts gone, it will be far easier to infiltrate and even storm the Drippy Cave if Captain Steelforge wishes it. Those grobins will be out of here in no time!"
- Player Character: "Glad to be of service. Farewell."

I was able to defeat the grobin lookouts.

I was able to defeat several of the grobin lookouts overlooking the Drippy Cave. Captain Steelforge's troops should be safer now.
