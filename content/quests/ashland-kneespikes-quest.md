## Steps

1. Kill treants in Nektulos Forest until you get a spike. Treants can be found all throughout the north and north eastern parts of the zone, south of and around Bone Lake..
1. Kill [[Guardian mist grinnin]]s until you get another spike. {{waypoint 350, 5, -1270}}
1. Harvest [[Ore Nodes#T3|penumbra ore]] until you get rivets.
