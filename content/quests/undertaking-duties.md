## Description

The frogloks request that I collect the bones of their skeletal ancestors, the corrupted ton shamans, in Upper Guk so they may be laid to rest.

## Steps

1. Collect 8 bones from corrupted ton shamans in [[The Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen]]
1. Return to Vegupa Marrsheart to report your success.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
