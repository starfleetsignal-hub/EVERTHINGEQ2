## Notes

- The quest name is an [[Easter Eggs|easter egg]] as it is a reference to a song on *The Simpsons*, ["See My Vest"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TyWVaZsUQjc), which was itself a parody of "Be Our Guest" from Disney's *Beauty and the Beast*.

## Steps

1. Kill 7 [[A stonechest gorilla|stonechest gorillas]] across the bridge south of [[Mok Rent]]. {{waypoint 1737.60, 92.52, -9.86}}
1. Return to [[Vircho Ku'Guur]].

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
