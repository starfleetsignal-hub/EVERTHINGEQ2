## Description

"Thekela would like me to bring her some Emerald Trout. I can catch the fish in the Emerald River, south of Sapling Spur Outpost." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Harvest 5 dead Emerald trout around {{waypoint -73, -11, 97}}
1. Return to [[Thekela Gillywump]]

## Rewards

- At least 1s 16c
- [[Pot of Thekela's honey]]

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Thekela Gillywump: "I'm sorry about my outburst earlier, [Player Character]. Do you have time for one more task?"
- Player Character: "Sure, what do you need?"
- Thekela Gillywump: "Fish! Modo and I both love the Emerald Trout found in the Emerald River to the south. If you could catch us a couple, they would make for some nice comfort food... assuming the orcs haven't ruined that too."
- Player Character: "Let's hope not. I'll go catch some."

Thekela Gillywump would like me to catch some Emerald Trout.

Something is wrong... the Emerald River is full of black goo! I should collect several of the dead Emerald Trout.

I have collected several dead Emerald Trout. I need to bring these trout back to Thekela.

- Thekela Gillywump: "Do you have the trout?"
- Player Character: "Yes, I do. But they're not edible."
- Thekela Gillywump: "What do you mean? Is something wrong with them?"
- Player Character: "There's something foul in the water."
- Thekela Gillywump: "The Emerald Trout were poisoned? Oh no. It must have been the orcs."
- Player Character: "Yes, it seems so. There's black sludge in the water."

I have given Thekela the trout. I have delivered the dead trout to Thekela.

I have returned to Thekela with the Emerald Trout. Unfortunately the lake was poisoned by the orcs, making the fish inedible.
