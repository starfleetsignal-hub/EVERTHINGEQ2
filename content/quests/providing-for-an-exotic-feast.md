## Starting the Quest

- ( In journal only)*
- ***Prerequisite:*** T6 Gathering Skill (240).
- ***Prerequisite:*** Lvl 50+ Provisioner

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Tai Nurwin]] {{waypoint -9, 15, -104}}.
1. Gather the following items:
   - Salamander eggs - It is at the base of 2 boulders in the water (a gazebo and bridge over the pool are nearby) {{waypoint -141, -1, -111}}.
   - Dye {{waypoint 23, 14, -58}}.
   - Pickled Pomegranate  -  Under a tent near the docks {{waypoint -39, -2, 146}}.
   - Dried fruit {{waypoint 49, 8, -48}}.
   - Ant Legs - Auto update in dining room next to Tai Nurwin {{waypoint -8, 15, -94}}.
   - Squid ink - Across the river from where you found the pickle barrel, next to a house and near the racks of drying fish {{waypoint -17, 0, -2}}.
1. Return to [[Tai Nurwin]].
