## Steps

![green sparkly for living leaves](images/Pine_Souls_%28living_leaves%29.png)

1. Collect the following
   - Kill 6 [[A forest retaliator|forest retaliators]] for [[Piece of animated bark|animated bark]]
   - Obtain 5 [[Living leaf|living leaves]] from the treetops near Scarstone - fly through the green sparklies (on the trees directly outside the fort) with your flying mount.

**Do not combine to create the Voice of the Leaves Draught until you are at the location and ready to progress in the quest, otherwise you will have to delete and restart the quest as when you log back in it will no longer have registered that you created the draught!**

1. Create [[Voice of the Leaves Draught]] potion
   - Right-click either the stack of bark or stack of leaves in your inventory, then select the combine option from the examine box
1. Return to where you were attacked and speak to [[A forest spirit]] {{waypoint 3500, -868, 402}}
1. Return to [[Tindi Wildbranch]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
