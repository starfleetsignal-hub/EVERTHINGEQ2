## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
- The quest [[Visions of Vetrovia: Handle with Scare]] will need the same zone

## Steps

![Natron](images/Natron.png)![Mahngavi Sage](images/Mahngavi_sage.png)![Undying Tormentor](images/Undyingtormentor.png)

1. Gather and hunt the following
   - Gather 8 **Natron** along [[Embolina's Beach]] {{waypoint 248, 8, -557}}
   - Gather 6 **Mahngavi Sage** around [[Kohgoda Falls]] {{waypoint 467.56, 25.47, -264.72}}
   - Hunt 12 [[An undying tormentor|undying tormentors]] for **sulphurous ichor** in [[Thornbone Wood]] {{waypoint 244.30, 25.71, -269.05}}
1. Return to [[Sukanya]] to receive [[Exorcist Powder]]
1. Use the [[Exorcist Powder]] on 10 [[A spectral bandit|spectral bandits]] before killing them, while inside of [[Mahngavi Wastes: Phantasmal Shades (Solo)|Mahngavi Wastes: Phantasmal Shades [Solo]]] {{waypoint 639, 48, -449}}
   - *Note: the quest [[Visions of Vetrovia: Handle with Scare]] will need the same zone*
1. Return to [[Sukanya]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- Choice of:
  - [[Kamapor Bracelet of Foresight]]
  - [[Kamapor Bracelet of Stratagem]]
