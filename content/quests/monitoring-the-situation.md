Brumley suggested placing a monitoring device near each of the areas where the immolated are known to be, to alert him to any unusual activity. Unfortunately he's run out of supplies... so he asked if I could make the gadgets.

## Steps

1. Create 5 [[Tinkered monitoring device|tinkered monitoring devices]] at a workbench using the [[Tinkered Monitoring Devices]] recipe provided.
   - Mass production doesn't update the quest.
   - The nearest workbench is down a few steps from Brumley at {{waypoint -70.08, 11.50, 173.42}}.
1. Place monitoring gadgets in the areas scanned previously.
   - Monitor the sealed door ([[Maldura: Bar Brawl|Bar Brawl]]) near the [[Alcove of Reason]] {{waypoint -64.68, 9.69, 118.72}}.
   - Monitor the sealed door ([[Maldura: District of Ash|District of Ash]]) in the north of the [[Sublevel Access Antechamber]] {{waypoint -114.65, 1.36, -42.15}}.
   - Monitor the sealed door ([[Maldura: Algorithm For Destruction|Algorithm For Destruction]]) in the south of the [[Sublevel Access Antechamber]] {{waypoint -115.82, 1.36, 41.51}}.
   - Monitor the sealed door ([[Maldura: Forge of Ashes|Forge of Ashes]]) near the [[Mushroom Bar and Grill]] {{waypoint -43.52, -4.22, 40.10}}.
   - Back in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]]: Monitor the entrance to the [[Glittervein Depths]] where [[Brytthel]] was injured {{waypoint 523, 131, 432}}.
1. Return to [[Brumley Langboom]] in [[Maldura]]
