## Notes

- Completion of this quest is unnecessary to progress [[The Crossing of the Fanged Sea]].
- The book you must read to start the quest is in the **un-**instanced version of [[The Tower of the Drafling]], [[The Drafling Tower]] in [[Rivervale]].

## Steps

1. You must find the two parts of Tinmizer's Astounding Frost Disruptor:
   - Part A: Inside a tent in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] {{waypoint -195, -27, -95}}
   - Part B: [[Tagglefoot Farms]] in the [[Enchanted Lands]] {{waypoint -778, -1, -653}}
   - The quest will automatically complete once the last part is picked up.
