## Steps

1. Gather 10 **Vacrul canosteon jars** from [[Castle Vacrul: Rosy Reverie (Solo)|Castle Vacrul: Rosy Reverie [Solo]]], {{waypoint 450, 110, 259}}. ![Canosteon Jar in Rosy Reverie](images/Full_Vacrul_Canoseteon_Jar.png)
   - Jars start as empty, and can be filled with [[Brittle Bones of the Undead]] from small chest drops.
   - Some possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -7.28, -0.75, 14.34}} (Full Jar)
     - {{waypoint 77.81, -0.75, -20.40}} (Full Jar)
     - {{waypoint 63.26, -0.75, 20.16}} (Full Jar)
     - {{waypoint 50.84, -0.79, 26.18}} (Full Jar)
     - {{waypoint -2.37, -0.79, 31.23}} (Full Jar)
     - {{waypoint 75.53, -15.41, 76.06}} (Empty Jar)
     - {{waypoint 120, -15, 124}}         (Empty Jar)
     - {{waypoint 187.87, -15.47, 97.10}} (Empty Jar)
     - {{waypoint 228.69, -15.49, 74.87}} (Empty Jar)
     - {{waypoint 226.73, -15.49, 56.16}} (Empty Jar)
     - {{waypoint 327.22, -15.49, 168.13}} (Empty Jar)
1. Return to [[Arqa Elharroun]] in [[Forlorn Gist]].
