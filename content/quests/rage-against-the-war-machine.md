## Steps

1. Speak with [[Mairi Hendry]] in Iceshard Keep.  She's on the ice ledge surrounding the building at {{waypoint -703, 120, 222}}.
1. Eavesdrop on the conversation between Praefectus Krieger and Vindicator Soren - stand in the hallway close enough that you can 'hear' the conversation, but far enough away that you don't draw aggro.  This step auto-updates when the conversation is completed
1. Plant anti-Kromzek propaganda around iceshard keep.  The places to place it are large sparkling blue sections of the wall.
   - Plant propaganda in the Storeroom
   - Plant propaganda in the Armory
   - Plant propaganda in the Brew House
   - Plant propaganda in the Barracks
1. Return to Greer Mcrae

## Rewards

- One of the Following based on Class:
  - [[Cestus of Flowing Blood]] (Beastlord, Brawlers)
  - [[Executioner's Dogma]] (Warriors, Crusaders)
  - [[Monomaniac's Hammer]] (All Priests)
  - [[Visionary's Dirk of Dismemberment]] (All Scouts except Beastlord)
  - [[Wand of the Roiling Tempest]] (All Mages)
