## Steps

1. Enter [[Kaladim]] and go to [[Everhot Mine]]. {{waypoint 113, 13, -357}}
1. Slay 4 [[A Grungetalon miner|Grungetalon miners]].
1. Loot or mine 6 [[Everhot ore]] from around the area.
1. Return to [[Tlekk Anvilstrike]].

## Rewards

- At least 1g 59s 57c

+5,000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
