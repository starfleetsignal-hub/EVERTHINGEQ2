## Steps

1. Talk to Occultist L'Takk in the Temple of War in North Freeport {{waypoint -86, -10, -165}} to receive this City Task.
   - Kill 13 [[A fetidthorn horror|fetidthorn horrors]] near the [[Drednever Crash Site]] in the [[The Bonemire]].
1. Return to [[Occultist L'Takk]]

## Rewards

- 8314 status at level 70
- +150 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
