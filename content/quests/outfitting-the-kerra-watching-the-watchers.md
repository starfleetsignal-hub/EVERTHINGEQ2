## Steps

1. Deliver the healing salve to [[Soren]] {{waypoint 246, 412, 1211}}, the kejek agent in [[Moonfield Hamlet]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]].
   - Travel from [[Kerra Isle]] to the [[Toxxulia Forest]] pad, then use the same launchpad to go to to [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]]. Once in the Highlands, use the launchpad there to travel to the [[Moonfield Hamlet]].
1. The quest ends here, but return to [[Chinook]] to get quest to speak to [[Natasha]]

## Rewards

- At least 20g 5s 35c
- +1,250 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +500 faction with **Kerra Isle Outfitters**
