## Prerequisites

- Neriak citizenship

## Description

Rilda D'Vzhiarn has explained to me the suicidal shunning of Queen Cristanos at the hands of the Disciples of Innoruuk.

## Steps

Investigate the conflict between these 2 groups by speaking with Disciples at the [[Spires of Innoruuk]].

1. Speak with [[Lararra V'Zaar]]
1. Go back and speak to [[Rilda D'Vzhiarn]] on the second floor of the [[Darklight Palace]] and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- [[Glory to Queen Cristanos]]<br>
- [[The Sacred Feminine]]
