## Notes

- requires 31,000 [[House Vahla (Faction)]] and completing the [[Show Not Tell]]

## Steps

1. Kill the following:
   - 12 [[Tidal glider]] near {{waypoint 852, 0, 535}}
   - 8 [[Planar water elemental]] near {{waypoint 701, 8, 313}}.
   - Use the Stratafold Teleportation Platform for fastest travel.
1. Return to [[Mindfold Matriarch Taneesha]] at {{waypoint -232, 575, -732}}.
1. Speak to her again.
