## Quest Description

Cordelia Galeston, a Far Seas Trading Company representative, has offered me work and coin payment in exchange for my assistance to the Company in the butcherblock Mountains.  She has asked me to help by repairing a traveler's damaged tome that was recently damaged by aquagoblins.

## Steps

1. Pick up the [[Damaged Tome]] and 2 [[Torn Tome Page|Torn Tome Pages]] from the crate next to Cordelia.
1. Scribe the recipe [[Do-It-Yourself Tome Repair For Beginners]].
   - You will need 3 [[Sparkling Incense]] (level 20 - 29)
   - You can buy some sparkling incense from the Adorning Merchant nearby. {{waypoint 762, 26, 598}}
   - Craft [[Repaired Tome]] at an Engraving Desk (near the Adorning Merchant). {{waypoint 755.93, 26.00, 605.20}}
1. I should return the repaired tome to [[Cordelia Galeston]]

## Rewards

- At least 1g 6s
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
