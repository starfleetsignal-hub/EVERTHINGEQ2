## Steps

1. Grab the Spheres from the chest right next to [[Rongot Wyrmrippa]].
1. Throw (use) the spheres in the flame spouts at {{waypoint -1098, -366, 2887}}
1. Return to [[Rongot Wyrmrippa]] at {{waypoint -751, -343, 2786}}
