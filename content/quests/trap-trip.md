## Steps

1. [[Mira]] says more shells and shellfish are needed to craft the othmir special armor. She suggested I take this opportunity to test the new trap design the Far Seas Trading Company has offered to trade them.
   - Craft 2 [[Shellfish Trap|Shellfish Traps]] using the recipe [[Far Seas Trading Company Recipe: Modified Shellfish Trap for Arctic Climates (no-value no-trade lore)|Far Seas Trading Company Recipe: Modified Shellfish Trap for Arctic Climates]].
   - You'll need 2 [[Titanium ore]], 4 [[Quicksilver cluster]], 4 [[Sumac lumber]], and 2 [[Ethereal sandpaper]].
   - Craft at [[Woodworking Table]]  - Located behind [[Matthew]] {{waypoint -1642, -533, -2380}}  Matthew will sell you the fuel.
1. ![Convenient Site for Shellfish Trap](images/Convenient_Site.jpg)Place the traps below the waterline of the ice flow south of Fina's Retreat.  There will be green sparkles marking the spots for the traps.  {{waypoint -1316, -544, -2067}} and about 10m away {{waypoint -1311, -544, -2059}} click on the greenies.
1. Return to [[Mira]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
