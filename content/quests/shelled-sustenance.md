## Steps

1. Hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 10 crimsonback terrapins on the beach around {{waypoint 36.89, 4.81, 703.46}}
   - Gather 8 **stork eggs**  ![](images/Shelled_Sustenance_quest_-_Stork_Eggs.jpg)
     - *Possible locations include: {{waypoint -26.75, 12.54, 553.39}}<br>{{waypoint -24.03, 14.29, 858.18}} {{waypoint -37.41, 4.53, 598.92}} {{waypoint -26.78, 7.50, 611.63}} {{waypoint -26.56, 7.54, 611.49}} {{waypoint -4.78, 6.05, 626.83}} {{waypoint -3.60, 14.87, 613.14}} {{waypoint 25.56, 4.80, 604.16}} {{waypoint 45.99, 6.05, 696.21}}*
1. Return to [[Tristyl Ashwood]]
