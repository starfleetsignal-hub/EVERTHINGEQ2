## Description

Mayor Mendeputt, the brownie leader in Lesser Faydark, wishes to give a nice set of obsidian blades to a certain someone, as a token of his affection. He sent one of his brownie citizens, Trelawny, into Butcherblock Mountains to acquire the obsidian needed, but she has found the task to be a bit too big for her!

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable once per year.

## Steps

1. Kill 12 [[Animated obsidian|animated obsidian]] elementals.
   - These may be found along the cliff edge northeast of the Highland Outpost griffon station {{waypoint 232.03, 189.92, 358.62}} or along the cliffs northeast of the Druid Circle {{waypoint 29.87, 185.93, 428.39}}.
   - These elementals are non-aggro until you come quite close to them, when they respawn into an aggro mob at your level.
   - Although they respawn quickly, you'll need to move along to another elemental; where you just slew the previous one, the elemental will not become aggro to you.
1. Return to Trelawny.

## Rewards

- [[Lover's Purse]] (a [[Decorating Houses|house item]])
