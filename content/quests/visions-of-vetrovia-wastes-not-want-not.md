## Steps

1. Speak with [[Vaziri]] {{waypoint 729, 167, -401}} before going to [[Mahngavi Wastes]] (entrance to Mahngavi Wastes from Karuupa Jungle at {{waypoint -883, 234, -792}}, take the pathway at {{waypoint -828, 220, -765}} to reach it.
1. Speak to [[Dhusala]] {{waypoint 18, 99, 569}} and complete her sub-quests:
   1. [[Wards To Live By]]
   1. [[My Scroll To Take]]
   1. [[Sweet Spell of Success]]
1. Speak to [[Dhusala]] {{waypoint 18, 99, 569}} after completing her sub-quests
1. Gather items for a vampire trap. ![Vampire Trap](images/Vampiresnare.png)
   - Obtain 8 **muted sunstones** (not guaranteed drops) from [[A graveland bandit|graveland bandits]] in [[Warpwood Cairnlands]] around {{waypoint 52, 109, 371}} or {{waypoint 18, 100, 197}}.
   - Obtain 12 **necrofazed blood** from [[A necrofazed brute|necrofazed brutes]] or [[A necrofazed wildling|wildlings]] around {{waypoint 247, 105, 156}} or {{waypoint 139, 112, 341}}.
1. Return to [[Dhusala]].
1. Set the vampire snare at the southeast corner of [[Forlorn Cemetery]] {{waypoint -182, 104, 689}} and run in circles around the trap until it triggers.
1. Interrogate the trapped vampire, [[Tavian Faust]], once he is trapped.
1. Return to [[Dhusala]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 57433 status
- [[Oracle's Ring of Foresight]]
