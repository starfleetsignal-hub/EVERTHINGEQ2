## Steps

1. Obtain the names of the following people in [[Gorowyn]]:
   - Snow haired man at the smelly swimmin' hole in Gorowyn is [[Bhaltair Tanicus]] {{waypoint 2679, 4, 1237}}
   - Short, bald, gear guy in the cave where hammers bang in Gorowyn is [[Gnottle Flangebang]] {{waypoint 2756, 134, 1225}}
   - Scaley-horn girl at fightin' place in Gorowyn is [[Tardukli Ku'li]] {{waypoint 2639, 18, 1167}}
   - Tall, Grey-skin smarty in Tim's Maw is [[Dima Fahim]] {{waypoint 2438, 23, 1261}}
   - Blue-faced, biggie, angry guy with boxes on Gorowyn Docks is [[Dughall Macleod]] {{waypoint 2342, 14, 1354}}
   - Pokey and pointy girl watching sun swims outside Gorowyn is [[Tyeena Baybreeze]] {{waypoint 2398, 62, 984}}
1. Return to G'shugaplum Gigglegibber. Make sure to have six open slots in your inventory!
1. Hand out the gifts:
   - **WARNING: You must give the correct gift to the correct person, or you will have to start the entire quest over!**
   - Bhaltair Tanicus gets [[Turlif whizgig]]
   - Gnottle Flangebang gets [[Beard trimming kit]]
   - Tardukli Ku'li gets [[Halas crafted axe]]
   - Dima Fahim gets [[Victories of Gorowyn]]
   - Dughall Macleod gets [[Box of studs and rings]]
   - Tyeena Baybreeze gets [[Tray of Gigglegibber cookies]]
1. Return to G'shugahplum Gigglegibber.

## Rewards

- Extra lighting effects for your [[Frostfell Tree]].
