## Steps

1. Defeat the hostile boss creatures within Ssraeshza's Hallowed Halls [Raid]
   1. Locate and defeat Remnant Ferahhal
   1. Locate and defeat the Timeless Golem
   1. Locate and defeat R'thessil and Zeltheen
   1. Locate and defeat Ssyre, the Fading Fire
   1. Locate and defeat Kua, Watcher of Wanes
1. Locate and defeat the Deactivated Blood of Ssraeshza within Ssraeshza's Hallowed Halls [Raid]
1. Locate and defeat Vyzh'dra the Unleashed within Ssraeshza's Hallowed Halls [Raid]
1. Look for someone interested in what just transpired...
