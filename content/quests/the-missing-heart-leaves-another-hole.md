## Steps

1. Kill 7 [[Myst river eel]] by the [[Myhst River]] {{waypoint -180, -8, 215}}.
1. Feed the piranha in the [[Drukyna Marsh]] near {{waypoint -834, -56, 121}} - places to click are in the water - will be white and round blinking spots. Hand icon will show when you mouse over one of them.
1. Return to [[Izle]].

- You will have to do this quest and Can't Step in the Same River Twice, Twice 3 or 4 times to get your faction to -2000 or higher at which point she will stop offering the two quests

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -3000 faction with **House Vahla**
- -3000 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +3000 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
