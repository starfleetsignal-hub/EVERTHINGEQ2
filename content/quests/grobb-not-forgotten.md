## Steps

1. Find [[A froglok citizen|froglok citizens]] and defeat them - just wander around the North Freeport docks and they'll periodically spawn and attack.
1. Return to Nelik Ulnya
1. Seek the [[An elder troll spirit|elder spirits]] in Big Bend {{waypoint 22, 0, -8}}
1. Enter the spiritual pocket
   - This will take you to a small instance called [[Throne Room of Warlord Ykesha]].  If you have a merc, it's allowed to enter the zone with you.
1. Defeat 6 froglok spirits
1. Speak with Warlord Ykesha
1. Return to Nelik Ulnya in Freeport

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g @ lvl 95
- [[Ring of the Troll]]
