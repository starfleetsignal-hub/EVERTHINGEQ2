## Steps

1. Slay 5 [[A lesser mummy|lesser mummies]]. They are found on the northeast corner of Sinking Sands, mostly on the downward slope toward the ocean, between {{waypoint -1100, 0, -1300}} and {{waypoint -1100, 0, -830}}. Placeholders are the regular [[A mummy|mummies]].
1. Return to [[Aliyyah the Embalmer]].

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Sand Scrubbed Bone Armband]]
  - [[Sand Scrubbed Clawed Armband]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sand Scrubbed Feathered Armband]]
  - [[Sand Scrubbed Talon Armband]] (All Fighters)
