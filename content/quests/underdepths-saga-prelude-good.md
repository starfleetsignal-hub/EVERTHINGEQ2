The dwarven spirit Edrur has greeted me when I entered Thalumbra via the Tinkered Portal-Gate. Since I have not received his descendants request for help, he has indicated that I must go to Kelethin and find those who have intercepted the call for help.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Kelethin]] near [[Gearheart's Forge]] and approach [[Dangle Springbolt]] {{waypoint 323, 109, 155}}. Note: The quest completes just by approaching Dangle Springbolt, with a significant amount of experience.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
