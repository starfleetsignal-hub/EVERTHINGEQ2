## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Recover the four rib bones of Korucust, in any order:
   - [[First missing rib bone]]: Aggro, but do not one-shot, [[A cenobite warden]], who wanders between the two back rooms of the instance. You will automatically be teleported to a cage high up in the instance. Pick up the bone right in front of you. To get back down, click on the lock on the side of the cage that faces the open area. You will be teleported just outside the cage. Now, since you probably have played [Dark Souls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Souls), you should have no trouble figuring out how to get down to the ground without dying.
   - [[Second missing rib bone]]: Kill [[A Nak'azar puritan]] {{waypoint -936, -69, 326}}
   - [[Third missing rib bone]]: {{waypoint -960, -69, 95}}
   - [[Fourth missing rib bone]]: {{waypoint -956, -69, 287}}
1. Return to the iron maiden. {{waypoint -791.52, -68.85, 173.82}}

## Rewards
