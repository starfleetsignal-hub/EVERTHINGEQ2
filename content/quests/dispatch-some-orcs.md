## Steps

1. Slay "many" *(between 12 and 14)* [[A Vallon prospect|Vallon prospect]]s. {{waypoint 261, 13, -88}}
1. Slay [[An orc dispatch]]. He will spawn behind you at {{waypoint -160, -36, 100}} when you enter the [[Mythical Forest]] {{waypoint -180, -36, 130}}.
1. Return to Birchbark Oakleaf

## Rewards

- At least 1g 59s 57c
- 6 [[Acorn bread]] *(Food, level 20)*
- 6 [[Barkdraught]] *(Drink, level 20)*
