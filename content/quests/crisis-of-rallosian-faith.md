## Note

- this quest is offered to Ogres only

## Steps

1. Enter Big Bend at {{waypoint 64, -12, 267}}
1. Talk to Somdoq at {{waypoint -39, 1, 11}}
1. Talk to Lochught at {{waypoint 57, -1, -13}}
1. Return to Freeport and talk to Gruurz on the upper level of the Seafarer's Roost at {{waypoint -231, -49, 8}}
1. An NPC named Jory Sevitus will run near you outside of the Seafarer's Roost after you talk to Gruurz. Talk to him until he fights with you.
1. Return to Kroota Gukbutcher

## Rewards

- At least 6s 90c
