## Steps

1. Speak to [[Riccaww]]

1. Follow the Aviaks until they disappear, then catch up with them at Coppercurl Maze {{waypoint -421.18, 54.34, -509.75}}
1. Defeat the raging rock formation near {{waypoint -602.66, 42.21, -622.95}}
1. Speak with Riccaww at Coppercut Maze {{waypoint -421.18, 54.34, -509.75}}
1. Defeat 8 volatile rock formations {{waypoint -530.66, 49.53, -603.58}}
1. Catch up with the aviaks at Adja-Beryl Berms {{waypoint -810.91, 102.45, 154.83}}
1. kill [[An activated silverloid sentinel]]
1. Speak with [[Riccaww]] at the [[Adja-Beryl Berms]] {{waypoint -810.91, 102.45, 154.83}}
1. Defeat 3 [[A silverloid sentinel|silverloid sentinels]] {{waypoint -760.69, 117.69, 225.59}}
1. Speak with [[Riccaww]] at [[Turquoise Oasis]] {{waypoint -29.88, 87.18, 480.68}}
   - [[Riccaww]] will say in words to the effect of:- 'there may be more work later'. This will continue with [[Precious Peaks and Precious Defenses]]
