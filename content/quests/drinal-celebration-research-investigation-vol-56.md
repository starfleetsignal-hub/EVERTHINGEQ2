## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Drinal has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items

1. Purchase the [[Silver Death Shroud|silver death shroud]] from Andrenol for 5000 status
   - In Loping Plains, wandering along the roads north of [[POI|Wayunder Lake]], from {{waypoint -574, -15, -87}} to {{waypoint -739, -5, -39}}
1. Purchase the [[Blood of the Unforgiven|blood of the unforgiven]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the [[Moonlit Dust|moonlit dust]] from Kuutanya for 5000 status
   - Wanders the path on [[POI|Isle of Wisdom]] in [[Zimara Breadth]]
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Silver Reaper]] in [[Loping Plains]] at {{waypoint 85, -3, 291}}
