## Prerequisite

You must be adventure level 23 or higher to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Visit the [[Shrine of Decay]] in the [[The Thundering Steppes]] {{waypoint 345, 5, 1185}}. To get this update you must step on the center large stone platform.
1. Return to [[Riason Hanagom|Riason]].

## Rewards

- At least 36s 13c
