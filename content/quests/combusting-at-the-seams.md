## Notes:

- This quest now scales as of 2023. Players level 1 - 119 will need to enter Doomfire: Ro's Sweatshop. This instance allows you to be chrono mentored to a lower level and will scale down to that mentored level. Players level 120+ must enter either Doomfire: Ro's Sweatshop [Solo] or Doomfire: Ro's Sweatshop [Heroic] to complete this quest.
- The quest will also grant Platinum Jubilation Medals, as will the instances "daily" (18-hour) mission
- You will need 5 x [[Glowing Filament|glowing filament]] to complete the quest, so plan accordingly if you don't have an item to summon a fuel vendor.
- You will need to complete [[Doomfire: Ro's Sweatshop (Solo)|Doomfire: Ro's Sweatshop [Solo]]] in order to access the loom on the last island.
- You will need to collect 40+ [[Firesilk Thread|Firesilk Threads]] by killing the slugs around {{waypoint 376.26, 62.48, 202.60}} and {{waypoint 415.56, 61.90, 281.31}}, {{waypoint 360.51, 52.60, 120.35}}, {{waypoint 361.60, 53.17, 71.01}} . *(Note: since these are a harvest, they appear in your harvest bag.)* Only slugs that carry bags on top of them drop Threads, slugs without bags will not drop them. Let those go and they will path to lava and come back carrying bags. Threads also randomly drop on bodies of other mobs but at an uncommon rate. 40 threads are needed to craft the doll at the end, harvest more threads to examine for buff  that allows you to damage last 3 named.
- Do not kill the named frog [[Magmus Hoptimus]] until you have turned in the whole quest done. The [[Asbestos Warts]] he drops go away when you zone to turn in quest, and you will have to do zone again to get it if you wish to make optional [[Hot Hoppers]] (bring 5 x [[Coruscating Filament]] as fuel to craft them).  So leave the frog, turn in quest, go back and kill him to get the Asbestos Warts, then go to loom at end to craft them. Hot Hoppers will allow you to jump farther and do a double jump, useful to pick up collectibles in the zone that are intentionally placed out of normal reach.
- You need to kill first 2 named mobs, before the flame horse will let you ride it. To ride the horse you need to have in inventory a [[Magma Morsel]] (track harvests helps) these spawn in random nests/pods in the first area, usually accessed via vines or platforms.

## Bosses

1. Elemy Elmonnier, wanders through the [[Loom and Doom]] area {{waypoint 275, 68, 155}}. He will spawn after enough of the weavers have been killed.
   - Elemy's steed is about to trample the ground around him! Get back!
   - Elemy Elmonnier says, "I shall brand you with the flaming hooves of my steed!"
1. Burnard Backbreaker, wanders through [[Cinder Heights]] area {{waypoint 350, 50, 72}} He will spawn after enough of the trash mobs, namely the goblins, imps, and slugs in the area have been killed. Sizzlespit silk harvesters and silk couriers seem to be required before the boss will spawn.
   - The effulgent express crawls out from the molten below. Don't let them reach Burnard!
1. Magmus Hoptimus, spawns after killing the surrounding frogs.  {{waypoint 154, 57, 302}}
   - Optional boss, not required to advance through the zone. Drops Asbestos Warts required to craft Hot Hoppers.
1. Fireye, sitting and drinking at the base of the tower on [[Lonely Isle]] at {{waypoint -428, 59, 293}}
   - Use a Firesilk Thread before attacking or he will be immune to damage.
1. Inquez the Ignited, perched on the southern cliff edge just outside the tower into which one teleports on [[Phoenix Isle]] {{waypoint -197, 100, 436}}
   - Trauma cure, [[Firesilk Thread]] both required for this encounter. Firesilk Thread is needed to remove its invulnerability.
   - Inquez erupts, spreading ignited feathers all around!
   - **THE TRAUMA DETRIMENT MUST BE CURED OR Inquez WILL NOT DIE!**
   - Crackling eggs erupt from the volatile surface! Destroy them before whatever's contained hatches! (click on eggs to destroy)
1. Savretta Seyzon, standing before the Luminous Loom at {{waypoint -417, 58, -376}}
   - Hail to spawn. Reappears as Savretta Seyzon, at {{waypoint -363, 61, -341}}
   - Note: She will use an ability called Searing Seam. You will need to cure this or you will quickly die.

## Steps

1. Acquire **Flaming Runes of Ro** from fire elementals (0/8) by killing the event spawns found nearby or at any event location.
1. Go to the [[Dropship Landing Zone]] in the [[Moors of Ykesha]] near the spire and click on the post at {{waypoint 1750, 448, 727}}
1. Enter the instance [[Doomfire: Ro's Sweatshop (Solo)|Doomfire: Ro's Sweatshop [Solo]]].
1. Talk to [[Girunai]] {{waypoint 331, 109, 441}} and he will ask you to help
1. Collect 12 **Corilac iron heddles** from weaver's supply crates around the looms. Not all crates have heddles. The crates will respawn after some time, you can exit and zone in again to find more of them. Possible crate locations include:
   - {{waypoint 405.40, 113.57, 339.50}}
   - {{waypoint 381.28, 99.09, 294.25}}
   - {{waypoint 385.48, 99.09, 307.64}}
   - {{waypoint 374.32, 97.15, 284.86}}
   - {{waypoint 367.65, 96.71, 276.51}}
   - {{waypoint 372.23, 96.71, 308.47}}
   - {{waypoint 304.44, 94.19, 351.23}}
   - {{waypoint 354.11, 89.10, 265.01}}
   - {{waypoint 304.62, 94.16, 352.35}}
   - {{waypoint 281.92, 90.73, 264.79}}
   - {{waypoint 237.72, 93.63, 285.15}}
1. Return to [[Girunai]] {{waypoint 331, 109, 441}} and he will give you the [[Mended Scorched Doll (Recipe Book)]]. Scribe it.
1. Collect the four doll parts. You must kill the  named in the area to spawn each part. The parts will be deposited  by searing scavengers along with a message. The first will either be by the loom or on the platform. It is a decent sized object with sparkle effects, although they can be difficult to spot as the whole zone contains sparkle effects, especially around the many looms.
   - Doll leg is by the loom at {{waypoint 317.54, 76.92, 124.94}} after you kill [[Burnard Backbreaker]]
   - Doll arm is on a platform at {{waypoint 221.97, 79.51, 34.79}} after you kill [[Elemy Elmonnier]]
     - after picking up the 2 doll parts here find a Magma Morsel and click on flame-branded stallion at {{waypoint 207, 67, 65}} to ride across lava to Lonely Isle
   - Doll body is behind the tower at {{waypoint -463, 57, 315}} after you kill [[Fireye]].
     1. Fireye, sitting and drinking at the base of the tower on [[Lonely Isle]] at ( -428, 59, 293 ) Copy.
        - Use a Firesilk Thread before attacking or he will be immune to damage.
     - enter portal inside tower at {{waypoint -427, 57, 293}} **CLICK PORTAL QUICKLY!** The entire inside of tower will burn you and kill you in 2 seconds. Rush in spam clicking portal.
   - Doll head is in the nest at {{waypoint -164.90, 78.29, 381.33}} after you kill [[Inquez the Ignited]] (her detrimental effect Phoenix Quills she puts on you MUST be cured or she will not die). When picking up doll part don't walk onto the nest or you will die quickly after receiving a warning in caps "IT REALLY BURNS". Stand on the North edge branches until you can click doll head.
1. Ride Inquez the Reignited {{waypoint -204, 87, 389}} to fly to Fabrician's Flat area.
1. **DO NOT CLICK THE LOOM** until you kill Savretta. Speak to Savretta Seyzon. Go through the dialogue to spawn her on the platform. Kill her, only then you are free to use her loom.
1. Repair the doll at the loom. {{waypoint -418.34, 58.54, -377.45}}
1. Talk to [[Jacinder]] {{waypoint -407.32, 59.81, -372.55}}
1. Recover **Ayonae's Cithara** {{waypoint -365.95, 58.74, -285.29}}
1. Return to [[Chandrima]] in [[Bucherblock Mountains]] {{waypoint 264.28, 188.45, 435.95}}
