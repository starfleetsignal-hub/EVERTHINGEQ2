## Steps

1. Remove the alloy residue and metal deposits from Smeltfield Vale
   - Kill 16 [[An alloy residue|alloy residue]] {{waypoint -764, 62, 553}} ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Silver_Metal_Deposit.png)
   - Pick up silver or gold metal deposits (located in the general area around {{waypoint -764, 62, 553}})
1. Return to [[Hootensia]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
- One of the following:
  - [[Molted Island Gold Deposit]] - *House Item*
  - [[Molted Island Silver Deposit]] - *House Item*
