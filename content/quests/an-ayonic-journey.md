## Requirements

- You must be a level 80 [[Troubador]].
- Ability to speak and understand the following languages:
  - [[Uruvanian]] from [[Words of Air]]
  - [[Death's Whisper]] from [[The Whispers of Death]]
  - [[Druzaic]] from [[Words of Pure Magic]]
- Access to [[The Poets Palace]]
- Access to [[Court of Korucust]]

## Starting the Quest

1. In [[The Thundering Steppes]], Speak with [[Maestra Orlita]] in the back of the Centaur camp{{waypoint 52, 1, 1393}}
1. In [[The City of Freeport|The City of Freeport (East)]], speak with [[Lyricist Te'naj]] in the Amphitheatre at the top of the hill {{waypoint -22, -23, 112}}
1. In [[The City of Freeport|The City of Freeport (East)]], at the [[Conqueror's Flame]], the island in the middle of the water where the Fences are, and speak with [[Lyricist Sha'son]] {{waypoint -393.63, -56.95, 0.74}}.
1. In [[Qeynos Province District|Qeynos Province District (Harbor)]], speak with [[Lyricist Kuehja]]. She stands on the balcony of the third floor of the building directly North of [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn]] {{waypoint 817, -14, 36}}
1. In [[Qeynos Province District|Qeynos Province District (Harbor)]], speak with [[Lyricist Trucci]]. She stands north of [[Lyricist Kuehja]], across the water behind the round building {{waypoint 830, -25, -20}}.
1. In [[Qeynos Capitol District|Qeynos Capitol District (South)]], enter the red portal of the [[South Qeynos Mage Tower|mage tower]].  Click on [[A Chaotic History]] book {{waypoint 711, 42, 109}} and read it to be offered the quest.

Ask the NPC's what the song is about.

## Steps

![The Hagralaphone](images/The_Hagralaphone.jpg)

1. Travel to [[Rivervale]] and enter [[Laughing Trout]] *(small door amongst the roots of a large tree, {{waypoint -487, 0, -141}})*.  Click ![the hagralaphone](images/Image:The_Hagralaphone.jpg) {{waypoint -444, -6, -155}}
1. Travel to [[Enchanted Lands]] and talk to [[Camfred]] {{waypoint 300, 0, -293}}
1. Travel to [[Antonica]] and go to [[The Tower of Vhalen]] {{waypoint -1302, 38, 48}}.  Click the bell to summon [[A vision of Vhalen]] and speak with him.
1. Travel to [[The Thundering Steppes]] and go to the [[Shrine of Decay]] {{waypoint 359, 5, 1196}}.  Speak with The Ghost of Vhalen to receive the [[North Wind of Karana]].
1. Travel to [[The Poets Palace]] and speak to [[The Djinn Master]].
   - You will need to have completed [[Be Careful What You Wish For]] for access to Poets Palace, [[Words of Air]] to speak [[Uruvanian]], and the the 3 ring events ( [[Shimmering Citadel : Poets Palace : The First Mirror.|The First Mirror]], [[Shimmering Citadel : Poets Palace : The Second Mirror.|The Second Mirror]], [[Shimmering Citadel : Poets Palace : The Third Mirror.|The Third Mirror]] ) to open 4th Mirror to reach Master Djinn.
1. Still in Poets Palace, rub the lamp [[The Vessel of Music]] {{waypoint -110, 295, -57}}.  The [[Ghost of Vhalen]] will appear.  Speak to him in order to receive the [[West Wind of Karana]].
1. Travel to the [[Palace of the Awakened]] and make your way to the sixth floor where [[Skygazer]] resides.  Speak to The Ghost of Vhalen (near the harmony bookcase) {{waypoint 58.97, 140.97, 24.15}} to receive the [[South Wind of Karana]].
1. Travel to [[Castle Mistmoore]] and make your way to the thaumaturge room at {{waypoint -253, 6, -15}}.  Click on the Ornate Case 89 to receive the piano key. *NOTE: when you click on the case, you will receive "a small block" which is used to activate the piano in the next step.
1. Go to the [[Castle Mistmoore]] main entrance. Use the key on the piano in the main room {{waypoint 4.53, 41, -2.98}} (mouse over the keys until you see a ?, then right-click to place piano key 89) to summon the [[Ghost of Vhalen]] and receive the [[East Wind of Karana]].
1. Travel to [[Rivervale]] again and re-enter [[Laughing Trout]].  Click the hagralaphone again {{waypoint -444, -6, -155}}, at which point a 15 min timer begins counting down.
1. Travel to the ramp outside of [[The Tower of the Drafling]] {{waypoint -495, 21, 41}} and speak to [[The Hagralazoo]]. When you finish the conversation, she will turn into an 81^^^ mob which you must kill (relatively easy kill).  You will then receive an icy gem.
   - You must speak [[Death's Whisper]] to  be able to talk to The Hagralazoo.
1. Travel to [[Kylong Plains]] and head toward [[Teren's Grasp]] Click the frozen totem at {{waypoint 1759, 461, -656}}.
1. Speak with [[Zyn'Phro]] {{waypoint 1757, 463, -647}} nearby to update the quest.
   - You must speak [[Druzaic]] to be able to talk to [[Zyn'Phro]].
   - **IMPORTANT:** Be sure to select the second option "I seek the Ward of Kelkarn". If you do not, you will be attacked and will have to speak with [[Zyn'Phro]] again to continue.  You will have to hail him in order to get him to speak again.
1. Travel to the [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]] in [[Kylong Plains]] and talk to [[Historian Holdak]] {{waypoint 1496, 165, -2924}}.
1. Travel to [[Chardok]] and collect the artifacts below.
   - [[The Flame of Shazzuk]] at the center of the wind chamber {{waypoint 32, -23, -458}}
   - [[The Statue of Thi'zok]] in Bathezid green cristal  gatekeeper room {{waypoint -352, -18, -541}})
   - [[The Eye of Gorowyn]] on 2nd floor of the island near the second sokokar post. Take the Spiral stairs down to {{waypoint -391, -103, -2}}
   - [[Blood of the Betrayer]] inside [[Court of Korucust]], a bowl near the pit in the room to the right from the main hall {{waypoint -933, -69, 298}}
     - This can be soloed by running into the room and clicking the bowl quickly before the social finishes and the mobs become aggro.
   - [[The Box of Tortured Delights]] inside [[Court of Korucust]], the red cube inside the cage {{waypoint -982, 29, 77}}
     - You need to attack [[A cenobite warden]] to get teleported to Jail.
     - **IMPORTANT:** If you are L90 and oneshot the cenobite warden on the pull then you will have to wait for the zone to reset before trying again.  I suggest pulling with a debuff to circumvent this issue.
   - [[Hand of the Metal God]] in the corner of the room with [[Gaodon]] {{waypoint -34, -54, 40}} back outside in Chardok.
   - [[The Runes of Gor]] inside the barracks, down by the edge of the water, next to [[A Caroeth gatekeeper]] {{waypoint 205, -75, 95}}.  *If sneaking you can stand at {{waypoint 212, -75, 103}} in the water and click on the [[The Runes of Gor]] then evac or CoH out of the zone.*
1. Return to [[Historian Holdak]] with the artifacts.  You will receive [[The Wards of Kelkarn]].
1. Play the [[The Wards of Kelkarn]] to protect yourself from the Ayonic curse.
1. Travel to [[City of Mist]] and click on the mandolin (it's in a box in the corner) at {{waypoint -227, 148, -950}}.  Speak to the Ghost of Vhalen.
1. Travel to [[Jarsath Wastes]] and click The Yore Flame {{waypoint -2430, 92, 1690}}.  Either jump, take the ramp that takes you beneath the platform at the [[Temple of the White Lady]] or fly down if you have a flying mount.  This will begin the side quest [[Feeding the Flame of Yore]].
   1. Harvest 10 [[Xegonite cluster]]s.  As of GU56, you no longer have to harvest the clusters; you can acquire them through trades or the broker if you wish.
   1. Return to The Yore Flame {{waypoint -2430, 92, 1690}}, and click it to summon [[Shade of Modeus]]. Your 10 Xegonite clusters will be consumed during this step.
1. Speak with [[Shade of Modeus]].
1. Kill the following:
   - [[Sebilis]]: [[High Ambassador Guralz]] (or his placeholder) in the Audience Chamber {{waypoint 99, 2, 412}}.
   - [[Sebilis]]: [[Praetorian Captain Nagol]] (or his placeholder) near [[Venril Sathir's Lair]] {{waypoint -8, -129, 476}}.
   - [[The Crypt of Agony]]: [[Fallen Emperor Vekin]]
1. Kill [[Drusella Sathir]] in [[Charasis: Maiden's Chamber]].
1. Click the 4 musical instruments in the corners of Drusella's Chamber.
   - When you click on the first one, [[A meliva mistress]], will spawn and run around saying "You shall not have the harp!"  She will become agro (82^^^ Heroic) and attack when you click on the 3rd or 4th instrument. (She may or not become aggro before you are awarded your epic)
   - FYI: I did not have 4 instruments appear.  After clicking on 2 I was rewarded and the quest was finished.  Melvia never became aggro'd.
   - The instruments will respawn after a few minutes if you have trouble finding all four of them.
   - You will be rewarded the [[Ayonic Axe]] and [[The Wards of Kelkarn]], a house item. You need to place The Wards of Kelkarn in your character's home, and play them to begin the mythical version of the epic.

STOP!
If your goal is to have the Item for the [[Epic Conversion Timeline]], you do NOT need to do the RAID version. You can begin that timeline now.
