## Notes

- There are two ways to complete this quest.

## Primary Method

1. Loot 7 [[Goblin Territory Marker|Goblin Territory Markers]] from the corpses of goblins.

## Alternate Method

Those working on the [[Kunark Ascending Crafting Timeline|Kunark Ascending Tradeskill Timeline]] who find themselves in [[Twark]] without Goblish can complete the quest by locating ground-spawn territory markers found in only in Twark (details below).

For the sake of clarity in this article, the phrase "*body-drop version*" refers to Goblin Territory Markers obtained through combat, which are No-Trade inventory items. The terms "*visible markers*" or "*statues*" refers to the tiny statues that can be collected while working on the [[Kunark Ascending Crafting Timeline|Kunark Ascending Tradeskill Timeline]].

### Notes for the Kunark Ascending Crafting timeline

Goblish is required to be able to speak to [[Glibnox]] for the Kunark Ascending tradeskill timeline. However, if you haven't already completed "Claiming the Goblish Tongue", but it *is* in your journal, you can *complete* it in Twark without further combat.

![Above: The territory markers appear in the Tracking window (left). On the right and rightlighted in yellow: Unlike the looted version, these are harvestable statues. (click to enlarge).](images/Goblin-territoy-marker-tracked.jpg)
Assuming you have the quest, simply use [[Track Harvestables]] in [[Twark]] to find the tiny "goblin territory marker" statues that update it (low-level toons will want to have progressed the timeline to the point where they are no longer kill-on-sight before attempting this!)

- The statues appear near the [[A Bloody Tooth Watcher|Bloody Tooth Watchers]] in the [[Twark]] region of the [[Obulus Frontier]].
- They are tiny and very hard to see. Use [[Track Harvestables]] to find them effectively.
  - *If you don't have this in your AA spec, press **L** to open the AA tree and use the drop-down arrow menu on the far right of the AA tree. Create a harvesting-specific AA spec that includes 5 points in [[Track Harvestables]]. You can switch back later if you also have specs for [[Mass Production]], transmuting, and so on.*
- They can only be harvested while "Claiming the Goblish Tongue" is in your journal.

Low-level toons who foolishly arrive in Twark with neither Goblish, nor the quest to learn it, have only two options:

1. Leave the zone, go get the quest, and re-enter via Crusaders Cave. This is doable on a flying mount: stay as high as possible, and avoid the lava pool in the middle (there's an aggro mob that floats over it)
1. Stay in Twark, and get a high-level friend to come kill goblins until they drop the starter. Weirdly, killing Twark goblins doesn't seem to damage your faction with them *(To be confirmed - also do Twark goblins actually drop the starter?)*
