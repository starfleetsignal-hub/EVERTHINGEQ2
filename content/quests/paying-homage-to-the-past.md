## Steps

![Skull of a fallen Iksar](images/Skull_of_a_fallen_Iksar.jpg)

1. Smash the ancient Shissar vase at {{waypoint -13.87, 0.27, 61.31}} (Inside Issik's Quarters).
1. Kill the [[A Shissarian asp]] that was hiding in the vase.
1. Collect five skulls of fallen [[Iksar]].  These are in random spots around the zone, out in the open.
1. Place the skulls in the brazier {{waypoint -11.98, 1.23, 62.16}} (Also in Issik's Quarters, next to where the vase was.) and set them ablaze.
1. (Take the shackles (On the wall next to the brazier.) {{waypoint -10.51, 0.27, 59.64}} off the wall.
1. Toss the shackles into the sea -  do so at the docks {{waypoint 1.47, -4.66, 103.68}}.
1. Speak with [[Brood Matron Vrim Malthyk]].
