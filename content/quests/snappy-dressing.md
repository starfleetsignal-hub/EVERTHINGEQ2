## Steps

1. Receive quest items:  [[Iceclad crab sinew]], [[Pretty shell (quest item)|pretty shell]], [[Snow cougar pelt]], and [[Othmir Shell Armor (Recipe Book)|Othmir Shell Armor]] recipe.
1. Scribe the recipe, and make the [[Othmir Shell Armor (Item)|Othmir Shell Armor]].  You'll need an ethereal filament, and a sewing table.  Head back to [[Matthew]] for both.
1. Show the Othmir Shell Armor to [[Matthew]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
- [[Othmir Shell Armor (Armor)|Othmir Shell Armor]]
