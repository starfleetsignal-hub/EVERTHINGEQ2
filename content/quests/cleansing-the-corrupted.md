## Steps

Complete one of the following randomly chosen tasks:

- Kill 4 [[A Stone Hive guardian|Stone Hive guardians]] {{waypoint -503, 1, -32}}
- Kill 6 [[A Darkflight deathwisher|Darkflight deathwishers]] {{waypoint -670, -4, 170}}
- Kill 6 [[A Darkflight painweaver|Darkflight painweavers]] {{waypoint -586, -2, 27}}
- Kill 6 [[A nightblood tormentor|Nightblood tormentors]] {{waypoint -623, -1, -352}}
