## Steps

1. Fulfil the frogloks' work order as requested
   - Check for damage on the houses of the frogloks, repair 6 holes, and return with a full report
1. Drop your report in the basket near Reedip to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
- -500 faction with **Clan Grobb**
