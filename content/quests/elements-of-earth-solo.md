The research into the Elemental Planes continues for the Great Library of Myrist. This time, I've been tasked with studying the elements of earth, so I am off to Vegarlson: Ruins of Rathe.

## Steps

- Defeat targets within [[Vegarlson: Ruins of Rathe (Solo)]]
1. Destroy Glimmerstone
1. Slaughter Slurpgaloop
1. Kill Heaper
1. Defeat The Monstrous Mudwalker
1. Chop up the siblings of earth
