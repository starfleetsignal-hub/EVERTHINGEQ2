There is no longer a minimum level of skill needed to gather, mine, etc. from any node in the game.  However, if you are significantly below 240 skill in gathering, mining, etc. you may experience a number of "You failed to [gather, forest, etc.] anything from ..."

- This quest seems to cause some conflict with the quest [[Desserts from the Desert]], as both are based on harvesting tier 6 nodes and both are based on your crafting level. Having both open; the game will give updates first for Desserts from the Desert.

## Notes

- Do Not Leave until you are 300+ in all skill before the next Part
- Harvest every node, leverage the random node spawn to your benefit

- **Sinking Sands**
- Bounce between the two Croc Camps, {{waypoint -1326.93, -226.83, -583.11}} and {{waypoint -1210, -216, -242}}, for non-Mining and non-Fishing
- Bounce between the two Croc Caves, {{waypoint -1223, -211, -332}} and {{waypoint -1334.2, -226.17, -637.58}}, for Mining and Fishing

## Steps

1. Go harvest (buying does not work) in any tier 6 zone ( [[The Sinking Sands]], [[The Pillars of Flame]], [[The Lesser Faydark]] ), 25 of each
   - [[Severed sandalwood]] from Arid Timber
   - [[Artichoke]] from Parched Shrubbery
   - [[Maj'dul coffee bean]] from Parched Shrubbery
   - [[Caiman meat]] from Beast Den
   - [[Sabertooth meat]]*  harvests best in Pillars of Flame
   - [[Prickly pear]] from Parched Shrubbery
   - [[Darjeeling tea leaf]] from Parched Shrubbery
   - [[Conger eel]] from Clutch of Fish
   - [[Tiger shrimp]] from Clutch of Fish
   - [[Stonehide leather pelt]] from Beast Den
     - *Note: the name of the harvest nodes are different in each zone, see the talk page for examples*
1. Return to Qho Augren.

## Rewards

- At least 34g 74s 4c
- Tradeskill experience
