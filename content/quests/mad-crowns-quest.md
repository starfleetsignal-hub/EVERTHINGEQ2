## Steps

Must have completed Eidolon Jungle questline before he offers quest.

1. Maybe some of the other Wayward in Cardin Ward know something about Anton's lost love?
   - Speak with [[Aaryll Elerloth]] {{waypoint -342, 15, -3}}
   - Speak with [[Jibby Klangwaddle]] {{waypoint -428, 9, -46}}
   - Speak with [[Meryl Hightower]] {{waypoint -421, 15, 61}}
1. One of the Cardin Ward townsfolk has seen Anton thumbing through a book that he keeps hidden in the town. Maybe it contains a clue of his lover?

![Anton's book](images/Anton's_book.jpg)

   - Search for Anton's book around the south-western most building in Cardin Ward
     - Gather Book on the ground {{waypoint -317, 15, -1}}
1. I should return to Anton the Mad within the town of Cardin Ward.
   - Return to [[Anton the Mad]] {{waypoint -456, 11, -32}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
