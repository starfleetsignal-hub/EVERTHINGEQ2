## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[Fishing stonestalker]] by the [[Myhst River]] near {{waypoint -232, 16, 219}}.
   - Receive [[A bunch of messy stelecat kidney stones]] after the last kill.
1. Clean [[A bunch of messy stelecat kidney stones]] in the [[Drukyna Marsh]] near {{waypoint -854, -55, 32}}.
1. Return to [[Izle Tot]].
- You will have to do this quest and The Missing Heart Leaves Another Hole 3 or 4 times to get your faction to -2000 or higher at which point she will stop offering the two quests

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -5000 faction with **House Vahla**
- -5000 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +5000 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
