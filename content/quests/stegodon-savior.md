## Notes

- *Timed quest: Shattered Seas solo daily, 4 minutes.*

## Steps

1. Kill 10 pterodons - 4 minutes to kill
1. Speak to the pygmy deinocaretaker next to the sick stegodon - 1 minute to deliver (turn back to quest giver)

## Rewards

- At least 5p 2g 20s 5500 status
- 3 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- Achievement [[Deino Doctor]] upon first completion
