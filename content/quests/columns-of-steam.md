## Steps

1. Kill 24 [[A Galebreaker pilgrim|Galebreaker pilgrims]] at {{waypoint -412, -37, -651}} in [[Everfrost]].

### Notes

At launch, there was no way to gain positive faction with [[The Order of the Blade (Faction)|The Order of the Blade]] (the Everfrost valkyries) - players started with just barely Threatening faction, and would become KOS while completing [[The Crossing of the Fanged Sea]].  As of [[LU48]], there are a few one time quests for positive faction with the valkyries that will get the player up to Dubious, but there is still no way to grind for positive faction, and the quests become unavailable if the player reaches KOS faction with the valkyries.  There is no particular benefit from having non-KOS faction with the valkyries after the quests are completed, however.

## Rewards

- 5549 status @ L45
- +500 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
- 1 [[City Token]]
