## Steps

Find [[Firmroot Moot]] within [[Moors of Ykesha]]<br>
<OL>
<LI>Hail [[Captain Cogglespot]] at [[Firmroot Moot]] {{waypoint 81, -88, -438}}.[[Captain Cogglespot]] fears that something might have happened to Fondfate</li><LI>
Locate [[Professor Fondfate]] in the Thullosian ruins {{waypoint 365, 20, 148}}.You will be ambushed by 2 linked level 80 ^^Heroic mobs. If they are already up, make sure you get the location update before progressing to the next step.(If you approach carefully you will be able to spawn only one mob and by killing it to get the update)
<LI>Slay Fondfate's captors
<LI>Return to [[Firmroot Moot]] and speak with [[Professor Fondfate]] {{waypoint 74, -86, -434}}.
<LI>Speak with [[The Great Morsley]], beside [[Professor Fondfate]]. This will end the quest and offer the next one.
</OL>
