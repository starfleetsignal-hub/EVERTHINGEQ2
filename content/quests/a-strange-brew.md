## Steps

1. Sneak into the [[Thexian Camp|Thexian Camps]] in the South East part of the zone and poison their drink supplies.  In the tent next to the high dragoon there are three wooden kegs {{waypoint -589.56, -61.47, 484.41}}.  Examine each for the update.
1. Return to Uric M'Ots to complete quest.
