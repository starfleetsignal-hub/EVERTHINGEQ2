This quest is repeatable. It is also part of a group of repeatable quests. See [[Additional Zek Quests#Alchemist's Components|Alchemist's Components]].

## Steps

1. Kill the following:
   - Kill 3 bears in Zek to get auto-updates
   - Kill 3 deer in Zek to get auto-updates
   - Kill 3 fish in Zek to get auto-updates
1. Give the components to [[Leelee Brewbubble]] at {{waypoint 555, -42, 357}}, behind a tent on the [[Warship Dock]] by the ship's bell

## Rewards

- Coin
- Experience
