## Notes

- For Frostfell token hunters, this repeatable yearly version of the quest is the fastest way to gain heirloom tokens if you have higher level alts. Members earn 36 tokens per run. Chronomentor down to level 15 before starting the quest although it can be completed at any level. You will still need to have done the prior quests on your alts.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Icy Keep: The Return (Heroic)]] in [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]]. {{waypoint 104, -4, -1150}}
1. Enter the room with [[Ice Maiden D'Ina]]. {{waypoint 0, 3, -322}}
1. Slay [[The Frozen Corrival]].
   - At half health, the Frozen Corrival will knock you back and split himself into an icy whirlwind and four miniature versions of himself. There will be one [[A Dark Hearted Corrival|Dark Hearted Corrival]] and three [[A Light Hearted Corrival|Light Hearted Corrivals]]. Use single-target abilities to focus on the Dark Hearted Corrival without killing the others. If you kill a Light Hearted Corrival, the Frozen Corrival will reform at full health and receive a buff for each Light Hearted Corrival you killed. After killing a Dark Hearted Corrival, he will reform at half health and you can kill him.
1. Return to [[Aildiun Mawdrun]].
