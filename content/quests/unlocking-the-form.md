## Notes

- Although the quest text doesn't show this, the sarcophagi need to be examined in the order they are listed. Examining the sarcophagus of Tanus Q'Banye or Craet T'Kizt before that of Athaks T'Laz will not offer the option to defile the sarcophagus.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Crypt of T'haen]]. The entrance is within [[The Tombs of Night]] at {{waypoint -107, -32, 102}}.
1. Find the sarcophagus of [[Athaks T'Laz]] {{waypoint -114.86, 1.87, 18.99}} who will spawn when you right click the sarcophagus and use the crystal to defile it. Kill him.
1. Find the sarcophagus of [[Tanus Q'Banye]] {{waypoint -36.62, 2.20, -108.78}} who will spawn when you right click the sarcophagus and use the crystal to defile it. Kill him.
1. Find the sarcophagus of [[Craet T'Kizt]] {{waypoint 9.45, 21.22, -116.56}} who will spawn when you right click the sarcophagus and use the crystal to defile it. Kill him.
1. Return to the dark altar at {{waypoint -107, 26, -6}} and use the crystal on it.
1. Defeat the [[Guardian of the Altar]] and his companions that spawn.
1. Return to [[K'shar Haelbreng]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 59s 57c
- [[Pulsating Dark Crystal (Version 2)|Pulsating Dark Crystal]] (a vampire illusion charm that consumes any vampiric incisors as a fuel).
- 4 [[A vampiric incisor (Level 70)|vampiric incisors]]
