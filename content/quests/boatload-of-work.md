## Steps

1. Gather 3 planks of wood {{waypoint -133, 45, -837}}
   - Gathered from harvestable nodes that look like planks of wood, very large and easy to spot.
1. Return to Yasha Redblade
1. Complete the [[Pilgrim's Landing]] barricade
   1. Receive [[Makeshift barricade]]
   1. Go to the break in the barricade line {{waypoint -318, 34, -713}}
   1. Use the makeshift barricade
1. Return to Yasha Redblade

## Rewards

- At least 54c
