## Description

"I need to return to the great tree stump near the center of Kelethin. The magical totems used by the Grender to enhance its power have been destroyed, so I should be able to remove the Grender and keep it from continuing to harm the creatures of Greater Faydark." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Jalmari Keletana]] near the [[Old Kelethin Acorn Lift]] around {{waypoint 64.81, 92.99, 49.24}}
1. Speak with the following NPCs (in no particular order):
   - [[Hamnal Teolofin]] near the [[Joleena's Restaurant Acorn Lift]] around {{waypoint 584.98, 70.41, 289.53}}
   - [[Eleoona Faleisinae]] near the [[Granite Hills Acorn Lift]] around {{waypoint 96.87, 93.90, 423.30}}
1. Speak with [[Lady Milika Vikaarinen]] in the [[Fae Royal Hall]] at {{waypoint 421.67, 136.57, 258.71}}
1. Speak with [[Queen Amree (Greater Faydark)|Queen Amree]] in the [[Fae Royal Hall]] at {{waypoint 422.20, 136.63, 261.64}}
1. Slay [[The Grender]]
   - The entrance to its lair is in the secret room in the [[Kelethin Central Stump]]
   - The ramp to the secret room is at {{waypoint 344.92, 9.33, 388.59}} and the entrance to [[Grender's Lair|The Grender's Lair]] is at {{waypoint 303.12, 35.26, 321.85}}
   - [[The Grender]] is in the back of the instance at {{waypoint -146, -29.13, -17.98}}
1. Return to [[Queen Amree (Greater Faydark)|Queen Amree]]
1. Return to [[Lady Katri Eloranta]]

## Rewards

- At least 2s 13c
- +10,000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
- One of the following:
  - [[Cloak of the Magi (Version 2)|Cloak of the Magi]]
  - [[Cloak of the Pious (Version 2)|Cloak of the Pious]]
  - [[Cloak of the Stout-hearted (Version 2)|Cloak of the Stout-hearted]]
