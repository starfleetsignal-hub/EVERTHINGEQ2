## Steps

1. Gather samples of Thullosian flesh and refuse for [[Shaman Skullcleave]] to study.
1. Return with samples of Thullosian flesh for Skullcleave. (These can be found NE of Balloon Conductor [[Boarfiend Caves]] in [[Firmroot Moot]]. {{waypoint 380.04, -75.61, -211.64}})
1. Gather refuse from the Thullosian camps for study. Locations include:
   - {{waypoint 398, 19, 107}}
   - {{waypoint 438, -38, -151}}
   - {{waypoint 623, 17, -205}}
   - {{waypoint 521, 16, -44}}
   - {{waypoint 479, 16, -13}}
   - {{waypoint 542, 16, 108}}
1. Return the samples to [[Shaman Skullcleave]]

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +1200 faction with **Survival Accord**
