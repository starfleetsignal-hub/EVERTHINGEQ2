## Notes

- This quest must be done solo. If you are grouped with other people on this quest, they will not get updates.
- This quest is not available on progression (TLE) servers until Destiny of Velious is unlocked.
- The reward from this quest is used to create [[Daleen's Harmonic Bow]] by combining it with [[Daleen's Bow]]. For more information, see the [[Channeler Epic Weapon Timeline]].

## Lore

This quest is pre-cursor event to the introduction of the Channeler class at the launch of [[Tears of Veeshan]].  You assist and witness the anointing of [[Daleen Blackwood|Daleen]] as the first post-shattering Channeler by the denizens of the Spirit Realm, and Daleen's pledge to teach and recruit others into the sacred task of slowing the inevitable cycle of defeat/rebirth of Ral-Gatha and other errant True Spirits.

## Steps

1. Find [[Yeomal Fallenecho]] {{waypoint 514, -31, -255}} at [[The Temple of Life]] in [[North Qeynos]] if you are Good or [[Izius J'lok]] at [[The Temple of War]] in [[North Freeport]] if you are Evil.
1. Enter [[Sellok's Hovel|the Cult's hovel]] {{waypoint -396, 13, 13}} in the [[Village of Somborn]] in [[Loping Plains]] (it's a small solo instance)  ![Entrance to Cult's Hovel](images/The_Truespirits'_Lament_quest_-_Cult's_Hovel_Entrance.jpg)
   1. Investigate the shape at the other end of the hovel
   1. Talk to and defeat the new Truespirit: [[Ral-Gatha]]
   1. Talk to [[Daleen Blackwood]]
   1. Leave Sellok's Hovel by clicking the Grate in the ceiling.
1. "Confront [[Ral-Gatha]]" : speak to him just outside the hovel in Loping Plains.
1. Talk to [[Daleen Blackwood]]
1. Return to [[Yeomal Fallenecho]] or [[Izius J'lok]]
1. Find Sellok's Broken Hideout in [[Nektulos Forest]] {{waypoint 1052, -0, -338}}. When choosing your mode of travel, bear in mind that it's in [[Port Naythex]], just north along the coast from the docks.
   - *Note: Do **not** enter as a group. There is only one set of shinys for the Axiom collection step.*
   1. Search for the Axioms (you won't find them, just move around the small zone)
   1. Talk to Daleen to be able to see the Axioms (green shinies)
   1. Collect the Axioms to complete the [[Axiom of Ral-Gatha]] Collection.
      - *For this step, Daleen is a [[Collector]] and will cash-in ALL completed collection rewards you have ready along with the Axioms.*
      - *As you pick up the Axioms, there is no requirement to add them to your collection, you can keep the set in your bags and still progress this quest.*
   1. Talk to Daleen
   1. Find and enter the [[Spiritual Wound (Nektulos Forest)|Spiritual Wound]] (it's a small solo instance) - it spawned as a purple portal at the other end of the hideout from Daleen, in the center of the circle of candles.
      1. Confront and defeat Ral-Gatha  ![Spirit's realm wound](images/The_Truespirits'_Lament_quest_-_A_spirit's_realm_wound.jpg)
      1. Talk to Daleen
   1. Leave the spiritual wound then talk to Daleen again
1. Find the [[A broken arrow|broken arrow]] in the underwater Sirens' Cave in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]. For travel, go to the docks. The cave entrance is on the triple-mass island, just north of [[Sigan's Peril]] in the <nowiki/>harbor - it's down a hole in the ground at {{waypoint 736.17, 12.39, -257.84}}.
   - *Note: Although the mobs that you fight will scale with your level, please note that the mobs in Butcherblock will not, like the level 25 sirens in the underwater cave.  Look out for a big concrete-like block in the middle of the small cave, which is click-able {{waypoint 731, -7, -313}}.*
1. Return to [[Yeomal Fallenecho]] or [[Izius J'lok]], to have the arrow repaired. The exit from the underwater cave is ( 732, 12, -255 )
1. Return to Daleen in [[Sellok's Broken Hideout|Sellok's Hideout]] {{waypoint 1052, -0, -338}}. in the Nektulos Forest, at [[Port Naythex]], just north along the coast from the docks.  ![Pick up the arrow ...](images/The_Truespirits'_Lament_quest_-_Arrow.jpg)
1. Enter the spirit realm wound
   1. Approach [[Ral-Gatha]] and he will turn agro.
   1. Fight [[Ral-Gatha]], but don't kill him - wait until Daleen shoots and kills it with the arrow.
   1. Pick up the arrow
   1. Leave the spirit realm wound
1. Talk to Daleen
