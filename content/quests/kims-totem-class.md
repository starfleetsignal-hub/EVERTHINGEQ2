## Prerequisites

- You need to have at least started [[Improving Relations with the Locals]], and spoken to [[Zhen-hao Nurwin]].  After that you will be offered this quest by Kim Nurwin on the other side of the same building.
- Level 50+ Woodworker

## Steps

1. Harvest wood planks to the northwest of the house at {{waypoint -159, 7.5, -187.5}} This can now be done at any skill level.
1. Go into [[The Mystic River]] to the South.
1. Go to Mara Beach.  Anywhere along the beach will do.
1. Harvest quartz to the east at {{waypoint -239.5, -1.5, -65.5}}
1. Purchase 5 [[Basic Sandpaper]] (T1) from any resource you prefer.
   - Can purchase from [[Ulgofar Wisme]] at {{waypoint -67, -2, 134}}
1. Go to the East part of the [[Courtyard of Hope]] and examine the urn at {{waypoint -12, 15, -103}}
1. Return to Kim Nurwin. {{waypoint -174, 7, -176}}
