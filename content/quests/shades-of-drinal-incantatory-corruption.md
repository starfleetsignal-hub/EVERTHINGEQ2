## Steps

1. As a prerequisite to this quest you must first speak with the [[Drinalian Whisper Column]], go to the spires at the Incantatory Apex and observe the action, and then return to speak with the [[Drinalian Whisper Column]] again.
1. Talk to [[Firiona Vie]] at the Incantatory Apex encampment.
1. Speak to [[Tal'il Amhenti]] at {{waypoint -84.54, 43.25, -230.27}} and help him:
   - [[Saving Apprentice Phophar]] (94) - opens up [[K'rin Valdim|K'rin]]'s first quest, below
   - [[Gehein Some, Lose Some]] (94)
   - [[Soldiers in the Ether]] (94)
   - [[Tourbillion Interruption]] (94)
1. Speak to [[K'rin Valdim]] at {{waypoint -67.37, 44.74, -214.63}} and help him:
   - [[Valdim's Grand Plan]] (94)
   - [[Desired Siphon Components]] (94)
   - [[Well Worth the Troubles]] (94)
1. Speak to [[Firiona Vie]].
1. Go to the spire {{waypoint 81, 38, -332}} and click the portal in the center (click at the bottom). You will zone into [[A Nether Space]].
   - *NOTE: The exit from [[A Nether Space]] may "disappear" at some point during the action. If you use <code>/loc clipboard</code> to record your zone-in point then return to this point after destroying the interstice and wave your "hand" around you should be able to find it. Zone-in in my instance was at {{waypoint -32, 0, -3.3}}*.
   - *Evac also works to get out of the instance, sending you outside right on top of the spire next to Firiona*.
1. Kill [[Lore and Legend: Harrowing Horde|Harrowing Horde]] until [[Quozz]] spawns. Kill him.
1. Click on and attack the interstice.
1. Leave the zone* and speak to [[Firiona Vie]] near the spire at {{waypoint 53, 36, -313}}
1. Return to the Incantatory Apex camp and speak to [[Drinal's Steward]] via the Whisper Column to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
