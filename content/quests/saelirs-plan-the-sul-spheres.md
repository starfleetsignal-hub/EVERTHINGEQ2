This is the 3rd quest of the [[Kelethin Betrayal Timeline|Kelethin Betrayal]]. **You will be warned repeatedly that you will now lose ALL faction with Kelethin and your combat arts/spells will change to Apprentice I level upon completion of the Betrayal quest.**

## Steps

1. Speak with Saelir Varryn {{waypoint 224, 112, 400}}
1. He asks you to steal the following Sul Spheres in Kelethin:
   - Hiraen Aravirt's Sul Sphere {{waypoint 271.84, 106.51, 151.37}} is in Venla Vaara's chest near the bank. Search the chest to get the Sul Sphere.
   - The Greenlight's Sul Sphere {{waypoint 342, 129, 287}} is on the 2nd floor of the 1st room at the Protectors of Growth near Gearheart's Forge.
   - Maayiri Ellia's Sul Sphere {{waypoint 104, 96, 495}} is on the 2nd floor of the 1st room at the House of the Falling Stars, SE platform of Kelethin.
   - Jyoril Maarson's Sul Sphere {{waypoint 537, 71, 297}} is in the 2nd room of Joleena's Restaurant. On the chair near Bettina Meriaan is the backpack you need to search to receive the update.
1. Return to [[Saelir Varryn]] to complete the quest.
