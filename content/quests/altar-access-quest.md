## Steps

1. Destroy 6 **wolfsbane satchels** near the **Altar to Drinal** {{waypoint -261, 15, 278}}  (If you have finished the TS quest line to the point that you can buy a [[Illusion: Werewolf (Version 2)|werewolf illusion]] in [[Renfry's Basement|Renfrey's Basement]], you can use one here.)
1. I noticed a number of altars in Forlorn Gist's District of Night. I should examine them to learn more about the citizens who call this village home.
   - Examine another altar {{waypoint -252, 13, 263}}.
   - Examine another altar {{waypoint -274, 13, 267}}.
   - Examine another altar {{waypoint -284, 13, 269}}.
   - Examine another altar {{waypoint -238, 13, 264}}.
   - Examine Drinal's altar {{waypoint -262, 16, 283}}
1. Return to [[Kurtis Scarson]].
