## Description

"Matstyr Alesmith, a hard working Dwarf that bases his operation at Spire Shadow Outpost, has offered me some part time work. My assignment is to hunt Crushbone corruptors and gather their bags for Matstyr. They can be found near the Treant Grove." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay [[Crushbone corruptor|Crushbone corruptors]] in [[Treant Grove]] {{waypoint 341.63, 39.10, -132.90}} until you get 6 bags
1. Return to [[Matstyr Alesmith]]

## Rewards

- At least 2s 86c
- Experience
