## Steps

1. Kill 12 [[A glade deer|glade deer]] at {{waypoint -277, -14, 260}}.
1. Kill 12 [[A young brown bear|young brown bear]]s at {{waypoint -1212, -18, 277}}.
1. Kill 12 [[A timber wolf|timber wolves]] at {{waypoint -1212, -18, 277}}.
1. Kill [[A forest warden|forest wardens]] at {{waypoint -767, -9, 716}} until you receive an update that will tell you the page is under a nearby rock. Note: forest wardens only spawn during daylight hours.
1. Travel to the rock {{waypoint -845, -6, 704}} and retrieve the page.
1. Return to [[Scholar Demini]] for your reward

## Notes

- This quest serves as an introduction to the [[The Sage of Ages|Sage of Ages]], a central character in the original story for the Shattered Lands. Scholar Demini originally believes the Sage is a myth, but after you complete this quest for the journal, he is convinced that the Sage is real and asks you to find him.
- You will eventually find and rescue the Sage with the [[Maiden's Gulch: Rescue of the Sage]] quest which is available at level 45 and is a prerequisite to learning the [[To Speak as a Dragon|Draconic language]].
