## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.. However [[Trial of Jiang Anlan: Heroic (Instance)]] has a minimum persistence of 2 days 20 hours, so no new terracotta warriors will spawn should you zone into the instance again after completing this quest.
- This quest scales with player level up to 70.
- You can receive additional [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]] as a rare body drop from the terracotta warriors. These can be turned into [[Brother Huang Mantian]] for titles.
- Mercenaries can be summoned to assist you in the solo version of this quest.

## Steps

- Kill 100 terracotta warriors in 30 minutes.

## Steps

1. Collect 50 terracotta shards in 30 minutes.  Shards can be obtained by:
   - Killing mobs - Shards drop about half the time, if you're lucky.
   - Destroying vases - Each vase gives between 2 - 10 shards. Up to Five vases have been found in a single instance.
   - Collecting ?s on the ground - Each ? gives 1 shard.
1. After collecting 50 shards go to the tri-way split and in the middle there is an urn which you click. {{waypoint 0, -44, 20}}

## Rewards

- 40 [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]]
