## Prerequisites

- You must be at least level 40 before Yoru will offer the quest.
- You must be able to craft a level 9 artisan recipe.

## Steps

1. Harvest 10 pieces of wood in [[Antonica]], from [[Wood Nodes#T2|wind felled trees]].
1. Return to [[Yoru the Old]].
1. Craft a [[Sunfish Casserole]], (a level 9 [[Artisan]] recipe that can't be commissioned, and must be crafted during this step of the quest. Can't be done beforehand).
1. Return to Yoru the Old.
1. Fill a flask at the keg in [[Frontier Farm]] at {{waypoint -725, -16, 231}}.
   - As soon as you start this step you have 15 minutes to fill the flask and 15 more to return it to Yoru.
1. Return to Yoru the Old.
1. Meditate with Yoru the Old until a group of four level 41 heroic highwaymen spawn. Kill them.
   - The highwaymen will take about a minute to spawn.
1. Speak with Yoru the Old.
1. Speak with [[Cythan]] in [[The Feerrott]] at {{waypoint -1, 26, 963}}. The closest transport is a transport horse at the Greenblood River Station.
1. Kill the increasingly harder monks that spawn (approximately 10), beginning with [[Spirit monk|Spirit monks]] and ending with the [[A Spirit Master|Spirit Master]].
   - This step has a 5-minute timer.
1. Speak with Cythan another time.
1. Spawn and kill the following spirits:
   - The spirits have a big knock back, and once hailed stay up for only 10 to 15 minutes before despawning.
   - [[Rivervale]]: Speak to [[Burdop]] at {{waypoint -27, 7, -212}} to summon [[Borbin the Prevailer]].
   - [[Enchanted Lands]]: Speak to [[Lo'volonus]] at {{waypoint -99, 31, -1127}} to summon [[Oodan the Tranquil]].
   - [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]]: Speak to [[Zan Filis]] at the top of [[Deathfist Lookout]] {{waypoint -23, 75, -369}} to summon [[Brother Raster]].
   - [[The Feerrott]]: Speak to [[Tarnis]] at {{waypoint -1874, -23, 749}} to summon [[Sartar the Unrivaled]], the hardest of the four spirits.
1. Return and speak to [[Cythan]] who becomes [[Cythan the Last]] and attacks you.
1. Speak to Cythan who reappears when Cythan the Last is killed.
1. Click the rock behind Cythan to complete the quest. {{waypoint -1, 26, 965}}

## Rewards

- [[Flowing Black Silk Sash]]
- 54400 status
