## Steps

1. Enter [[Kaladim]] and kill the following mobs:
   - [[A Shrool porter]] at {{waypoint -70, 29, -343}} to receive a greybloom mushroom as a chest-drop.
   - [[A Quaketail brewer]] at {{waypoint -115, 45, -97}} to receive a gnoll egg as a chest-drop.
   - groups of [[Grungetalon chopper]] or  [[Grungetalon chipper]] around {{waypoint 72, 27, -238}} to receive troglodyte saliva as a chest-drop.
     - Mobs may not be in area when you arrive, so wait a few minutes and they will appear or roam in.*
1. Return to [[Delma Oreseeker]].

## Rewards

- At least 1g 59s 57c
- +5000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
