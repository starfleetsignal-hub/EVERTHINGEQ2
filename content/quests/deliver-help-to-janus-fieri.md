## Steps

1. Deliver note to [[Janus Fieri]], at the [[Crossroads]] in the Commonlands.
   - A local farm hand has been slain. Janus is investigating. He asks you for help.
1. Seek out documents in Nomad camps. Click on stack of books in NW Nomad Camp {{waypoint -154.65, -47.79, -828.49}}, and receive a tattered journal.
1. Return the journal to Janus.
   - It turns out that the murdered farm hand was Janus' only brother. He asks you to continue your help.
1. Kill ten Bloodskull lumberjacks {{waypoint -134, -48, 540}} to avenge Janus' brother's death.
1. Return to Janus at the crossroads.
