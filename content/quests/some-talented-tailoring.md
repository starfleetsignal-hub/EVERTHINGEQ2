- [[Stonehide Leather Bracers|Tailored Stonehide Leather Bracers]]
- [[Stonehide Leather Shoulder Pads|Tailored Stonehide Leather Shoulder Pads]]

- [[Sandcloth Mitts (Level 50) (Crafted)|Tailored Sandcloth Mitts]]
- [[Sandcloth Pantaloons (Level 50) (Crafted)|Tailored Sandcloth Pantaloons]]
- [[Stonehide Leather Gloves|Tailored Stonehide Leather Gloves]]
- [[Stonehide Leather Pants|Tailored Stonehide Leather Pants]]

- [[Stonehide Leather Tunic|Tailored Stonehide Leather Tunic]]

## Rewards

- At Least 1g 21s 50c
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
