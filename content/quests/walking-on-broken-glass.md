## Steps

1. Reach Deepwater Watch beach at {{waypoint -507.75, 4.62, 494.54}}
1. Destroy 6 dismal wisps on the shores of Deepwater Watch
1. Survive the journey to the Deepwater Lighthouse {{waypoint -581.47, 44.36, 775.43}}
1. Dig for buried shattered crystal pieces around the lighthouse
1. Return to Rizilla Maderer at {{waypoint 24, 6, 357}} only she's nowhere to be found, quest completes as you walk into the traders building.
