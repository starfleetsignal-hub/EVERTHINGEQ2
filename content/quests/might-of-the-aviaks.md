## Notes

- You have to speak [[Volant]] to receive this quest.
- You can pick up [[Culture of the Aviaks]] from [[Arejo Cloudbreach]] at the same time as this quest.

## Steps

1. Enter the [[Palace of the Awakened]] on the [[Isle of Awakening]] in [[The Barren Sky]].  The quest completes immediately.
