## Steps

1. Kill 10 mechnamagica interceptors before time runs out (you have 3 minutes).

Once you have pulled the lever to lift the gateway to move into the second part of the zone, there are exactly ten of these interceptors flying around in the area surrounding -54,24,170. Note that time starts ticking away immediately.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 67g 40s 6250 status
- 3 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]

## Notes

If you do not have high DPS, it is suggested that before killing any interceptors, you can pet-pull the named out of the room and kill it. Then start to pull the flying interceptors but don't kill them yet, get them all down ~20% or so then start killing them. The interceptors have some form of AOE stifle that can be avoided at 9m range to them. A good spot to fight them is slightly up the ramp from the gateway.
