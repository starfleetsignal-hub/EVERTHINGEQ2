Within the Midst Library, I discovered a tome entitled, Savage Origins II, with pages of notes and diagrams of the weald's grimling and Shadel campgrounds, caves and dwellings, but little in the way of their inhabitants themselves. As a scholar of some renown, be it with quill or crossbow, I feel the need to fill in the blanks and complete this tome.
