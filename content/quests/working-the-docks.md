*This quest is repeatable up to 5 times..*

## Prerequisites

- You must speak [[Halasian]] to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Kill 8-12 of one of the following monsters (amount and mob chosen randomly) in The Thundering Steppes. All monsters can be found in the northern areas of the zone:
   - [[A grassland beetle|grassland beetles]]
   - [[A plains snake|plains snakes]]
   - [[A shore crab|shore crabs]]
   - [[A young antelope|young antelopes]]
   - [[An entrancing siren|entrancing sirens]] {{waypoint -423, -81, -928}}
   - [[An alluring siren|alluring sirens]] {{waypoint 1476, -8, 295}}
1. Return to Brant Omannus.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 91c
