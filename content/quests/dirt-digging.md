- ***To finish the entire Leaper tradeskill quest line you need:***

  - 10 [[Etched leather pelt]]
  - 10 [[Rough opaline]]
  - 10 [[Severed briarwood]]
  - 5 [[Supple loam]]

1. Collect rough fossils (5) (located around the quest giver and griffin station)
1. Return to [[Ellerith Groundspark|Ellerith]]
