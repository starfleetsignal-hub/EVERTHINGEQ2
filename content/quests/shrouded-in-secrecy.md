## Steps

1. Go to [[Castle Mistmoore]] and enter [[Mayong's Arcane Library]]. {{waypoint -64, 8, -63}}
1. Read several books strewn about the library.
1. Talk to [[Monika Davissi]] in [[The Forgotten Waterway]].
1. Return [[Chardasus Divok]].
1. Go to the [[The Timorous Moor]], at {{waypoint 100, -5, 260}}.
1. Kill Monika when she spawns. (75^^^, gives AA, 10-min respawn)
1. Return to Chardasus for your reward

## Rewards

- Choice of:
  - [[Bracelet of the League Arcanists]]
  - [[Bracelet of the League Guardians]]
- +3500 faction with **The League of Freethinkers**
