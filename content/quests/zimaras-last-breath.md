Zimara Breadth is teeming with danger, and my goal is to put a stop to it. Or at the very least slow it down!

1. Defeat 3 of the greater threats within [[Zimara Breadth]].

   - [[Glimmerclaw]] in [[Zenith Helionae]] at {{waypoint 148.38, 136.12, -114.25}}
   - [[Ironrot]] in [[Helionae Razelands]] at {{waypoint -140.36, 105.15, -431.71}}
