## Prerequisites

- +40,000 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**

## Steps

1. Find and talk to Pu-balum at the Paineel Overlook.  He will auto-follow you.
1. Head towards the Hole entrance, where Erudian assassins kill him.
1. Return to Belanum for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 90g 70s 61c
- +2500 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**
- [[Mark of Manaar]]
