## Steps

1. Read the note at the top of [[The Bells of Vhalen]] in [[Antonica]], at {{waypoint -1303, 47, 43}}. Pay special attention to it's final words, for they reveal the password to unlock the Book of Sunder.
   - You must have [[The Idol of Everling]] in your inventory to see the note.
1. Speak with [[The Book of Sunder]] in [[Nektropos Castle: Tribulation]] within the hidden library room. {{waypoint -28, 13, 32}}
   - Full dialogues for The Book of Sunder and [[Rikantus Everling]] can be found on the discussion page.

## Rewards

- The password to The Book of Sunder
- [[Ethernere]] and [[Rikantus Everling]] Lore
