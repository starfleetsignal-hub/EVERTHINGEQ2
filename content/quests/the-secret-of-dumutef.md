*This quest is repeatable..*

## Prerequistes

- Completion of the quest [[A Slippery Situation]]

## Steps

1. You have 5 minutes to push the following six buttons, in any order:
   - {{waypoint -302, 17, 491}}; on the left side of the roof of the building across the street from the oily wall
   - {{waypoint -308, -10, 493}}; on a pillar at the street right under the previous switch
   - {{waypoint -358, 12, 500}}; 3/4 up the oily wall at the end of the ledge on the right side
   - {{waypoint -381, -10, 487}}; behind the tree
   - {{waypoint -533, -18, 456}}; left side of the building by the snake pit
   - {{waypoint -481, 3, 372}}; on first climbable wall from entering the Residence Court from the Trade Court. You may need to jump off the wall and stand on top of the switch to be able to activate it.
1. Reexamine the hidden compartment.
