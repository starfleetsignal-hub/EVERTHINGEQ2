## Steps

1. Travel to the Ry'Gorr caves, called [[Demon's Delve]]. The cave entrance is located deep under water {{waypoint 59, -99, 391}}
1. Examine the suspicious cave-in to the north {{waypoint -11, -67, 157}}
1. Return to Thirin Veliumdelver.
1. The quest completes, but Thirin asks you to meet him back down at Demon's Delve where he gives you the next quest

## Rewards

- At least 2s 40c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Fighter's Necklace]] (Fighter)
  - [[Scout's Necklace]] (All Scouts)
