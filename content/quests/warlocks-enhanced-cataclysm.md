- For a complete overview of the quest steps for all classes, see [[Renewal of Ro Epic Spells]]

## Steps

1. Find a way to upgrade my scroll to Journeyman
   - Complete [[Raj'Dur Plateaus: The Sultan's Dagger (Signature)]]
1. Find a way to upgrade my scroll to Adept.
   1. Obtain [[Sorcerer's Scrying Mirror]], from either [[Buried Takish'Hiz: The Sacred Gift (Signature)]] or [[Sandstone Delta: Eye of the Storm (Solo)]].
   1. Take that item to [[The Wanderer]] in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]] {{waypoint -419.85, -59.44, 848.07}}.
1. Find a way to upgrade my scroll to Expert.
   - Complete [[Sandstone Delta: Eye of the Storm (Heroic I)|Sandstone Delta: Eye of the Storm [Heroic I]]].
1. Find a way to upgrade my scroll to Master.
   1. Obtain [[Sorcerer's Bottled Spirit]] from either [[Buried Takish'Hiz: The Sacred Gift (Event Heroic II)|Buried Takish'Hiz: The Sacred Gift [Event Heroic II]]] or [[Sandstone Delta: Grave Grange (Heroic II)|Sandstone Delta: Grave Grange [Heroic II]]]
   1. Take that item to [[The Wanderer]] in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]] {{waypoint -419.85, -59.44, 848.07}}.
