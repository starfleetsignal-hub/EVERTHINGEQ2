## Steps

Create (2) each of any 6 random assigned spells below:

- [[Blazing Avatar|Blazing Avatar (Journeyman)]]
- [[Chromatic Shower|Chromatic Shower (Journeyman)]]
- [[Simple Minds IV|Simple Minds IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Pandemic V|Pandemic V (Journeyman)]]
- [[Fanaticism|Fanaticism (Journeyman)]]
- [[Ice Storm V|Ice Storm V (Journeyman)]]
- [[Focused Intervention|Focused Intervention (Journeyman)]]
- [[Hierophantic Genesis|Hierophantic Genesis (Journeyman)]]
- [[Lich|Lich (Journeyman)]]
- [[Maelstrom|Maelstrom (Journeyman)]]
- [[Oberon|Oberon (Journeyman)]]
- [[Porcupine|Porcupine (Journeyman)]]
- [[Shock Wave|Shock Wave (Journeyman)]]
- [[Chromatic Storm IV|Chromatic Storm IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Dark Nebula IV|Dark Nebula IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Ice Comet|Ice Comet (Journeyman)]]
- [[Apocalypse|Apocalypse (Journeyman)]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 81s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
