1. Harvest:
   - 60 Deeppod Bean from shrubs
   - 60 Tontu Tofu from shrubs
   - 60 Clayroot Vine from roots
   - 60 Stonebark Lumber from fallen branches
1. I should look around the giant mushrooms - go to Mycelial Vale around {{waypoint -532.61, -10.17, -24.67}}
1. I should look for 5 signs of what lurks in Mycelial Vale - examine strange mushrooms
   - {{waypoint -450.46, -22.54, -18.81}}
   - {{waypoint -285.61, -3.24, -106.27}}
   - {{waypoint -290.68, -22.35, -271.93}}
   - {{waypoint -253.51, 25.46, 124.21}}
   - {{waypoint -215.51, -2.17, 33.92}}
