Tusk, an orc member of the Green Hoods, was able to tell me some of what happened to the Deathfist Empire after Emperor Fyst's death. I now understand the Heralds of Zek who rose to power under Blacktusk's reign are now the Scourge orc Sanctifiers of War. Tusk has warned me that if the Plane of War is allowed to have a physical connection to Norrath the march of War could be unstoppable!

## Steps

1. Explore [[Siege Valley]] within the heart of Zek.
1. Go to [[Well of War]] to search for possible anchors.
1. Inspect the four manifestations of Drunder by clicking the four pedestals inside the [[Well of War]].
   - {{waypoint 332.38, 7.90, -123.24}}
   - {{waypoint 316.38, 7.90, -120.91}}
   - {{waypoint 318.44, 7.90, -104.99}}
   - {{waypoint 333.96, 7.90, -106.86}}
1. Return to [[Tusk (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Tusk]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- +500 faction with **The Green Hoods**
