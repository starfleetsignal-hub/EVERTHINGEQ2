1. Kill 8 [[A Haoaeran tapper|Haoaeran tappers]] in [[Haoaera]]. {{waypoint 1773, 129, -409}}

- At least 1s 59c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Fighter's Belt]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Belt]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Belt]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Belt]] (All Mages)
