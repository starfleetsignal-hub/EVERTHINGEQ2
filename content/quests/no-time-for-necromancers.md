## Prerequisites

- Mages must be at least level 60 to be offered this quest.
- Must have also completed the racial quests (For Erudites only).  If not, Bellengere will direct you to the appropriate Mentor's location.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Crexisz Lepidus]] on the ground floor of the [[The Concordium Tower|Concordium Tower]]. {{waypoint 709, -20, 119}}
1. Travel to the gate leading to the [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum|Starcrest Commune]] in [[South Qeynos]] and zone in. {{waypoint 695, -21, 366}}
1. Seek out the heretic.
   - Investigate the house just west of the entrance for signs of the heretic.
   - Search the waterway for signs of the heretic. Speak to [[A heretic disciple]] then defeat her. {{waypoint 718, -34, 319}}
   - Confront then defeat heretic [[Rennan'Uquisitis]] and his [[A heretic disciple|heretic disciples]]. {{waypoint 804, -20, 292}}
1. Return to [[Bellengere the Three (Qeynos Capitol District)|Bellengere the Three]].
1. Collect ingredients for a portal spell.
   - Collect a handful of fiery mane by clicking one of the steeds by [[Irontoes East]]. {{waypoint 335, -21, 118}}
   - Collect 6 shards of the Luclinite meteor in [[Antonica]], which may be found on the large meteor stones, or on the bottom of the pond near the meteor.
     - Gathering the ore will spawn [[A mutated pond glider|mutated pond gliders]] that must be slain. {{waypoint 133, -15, 442}}
     - Don't forget to click the rock again to get your update.
   - Collect a vial of purest ocean spray near [[The Tower of the Oracles]]. {{waypoint -701, -39, -504}}.
     - It's a spray of blue/white at the bottom of the ramp, at the water's edge. NOTE: You may need to turn graphics settings UP to see it.
1. Speak to [[Bellengere the Three (Qeynos Capitol District)|Bellengere the Three]] behind the [[The Concordium Tower|Concordium Tower]] and click the green misty area to place your ingredients for the spell. {{waypoint 738, -21, 94}}
1. Speak again to [[Crexisz Lepidus]].

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Concordium**
