## Description

I have been given a set of totems to place within The Crucible. It's possible that the magic in these totems might be able to help weaken the boundaries placed by Miragul that anchor his phylactery within the Eternal Prism. I should look for small concentrations of magical energies within The Crucible, and place a totem within it.

## Steps

1. Seek out places of magical concentration within [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Crucible]] and place (use) the received ***[[Purification totem|purification totems]]*** by clicking on white lights in various rooms
   - {{waypoint -34, 0, -51}}, at the zone in
   - {{waypoint 88, 2, 39}}, a Reflection of Exile
   - {{waypoint -112, 0, 32}}, a Reflection of Silence
   - {{waypoint -18, 3, -11}}, behind chair
   - {{waypoint -21, 16, 154}}, in the Halls of Ascension
   - {{waypoint 48, 16, 112}}, a Reflection of Exploration
     - *Note: Your graphics must be on High performance or higher quality to see the white lights.*
