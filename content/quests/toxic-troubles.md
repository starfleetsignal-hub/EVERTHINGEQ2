## General

[[Lurgz]] has been giving some thought to the toxic stegodon bite. He knows that their bites are not normally toxic. He has sent me to confirm his suspicions within the Allu'thoa village on North Dshinn.

## Steps

1. Search the huts for clues.
   - Examine any Fungus Egg in the shaded area. ![Zavith'loa fungus egg](images/Toxic_Trouble-Fungus_Egg.png)
1. Destroy 8 of the Zavith'loa fungus eggs.
   - Possible Locations:
   - {{waypoint -1099.54, 91.93, 914}} {{waypoint -1069.47, 91.80, 912.55}} {{waypoint -1055.93, 91.86, 950.16}} {{waypoint -1037.16, 80.08, 876.36}}
   - {{waypoint -1039.78, 80.08, 871.91}} {{waypoint -1187.83, 70.26, 836.21}} {{waypoint -1062.80, 91.78, 960.28}} {{waypoint -1058.45, 91.89, 963.65}}
1. Return to [[Lurgz]]
