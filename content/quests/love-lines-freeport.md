## Notes

- This quest is repeatable once per year.
- When you speak to the target NPCs during the quest, if you flirt correctly, a large heart appears over their head, swelling and growing. If your game is weak, the heart that appears above their head snaps in two and disappears.

## Notes

1. Speak with [[Brandi Bender]] near the [[Inventor's Outlook]]. {{waypoint -105, -23, 103}}
1. Speak with [[Rex Tinforge]] near the Western Plaza. {{waypoint -133, -38, 67}}
1. Speak with [[Priestess Alena the Chaste]] near the [[Temple of War]]. {{waypoint -64, -8, -119}}
1. Speak with [[Salty Joe]] at the southern end of the docks outside the Port Authority. {{waypoint -305, -56, 73}}
1. Return to Dalron Pinot.
1. Speak with [[Christina Halfsocks]] on the second floor of [[Seafarer's Roost]]. {{waypoint -230, -48, -15}}
1. Return to Dalron Pinot with Christina's message.
