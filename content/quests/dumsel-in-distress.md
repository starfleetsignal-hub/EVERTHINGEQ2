## Notes

- On [[PvE]] servers, this is a guide or GM-related event. [[PvP]] and Exchange servers may have this quest as a constant fixture.

## Steps

1. Mudeye will let you know that his sister has been kidnapped, and ask you to find her.
   - The precise level range that will be offered the quest is unknown, but it's lower than 75. A group reported that they were offered the quest when they mentored to a level 39 character.
1. Mudeye's sister is [[Dumsel]] and will be hiding somewhere in the Feerrott.
   - Known possible locations include the [[Fear Camp]] near {{waypoint -1011, -6, 711}} and the [[Alliz Tae Outpost]].
1. Now it's just a matter of fighting your way back to the docks. Do so, and the [[Guides of the Shattered Land|Guide/Guides]] will talk about how much they missed each other, and give you a reward.

## Rewards

- [[Dumsel's Cage]] (House-mountable Trophy)
