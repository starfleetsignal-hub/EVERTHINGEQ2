## Steps

1. Find a Flint Streaked Summoning Orb within the Deep Forge. This drops off of the final named mob in the zone, [[Firelord Kaern]]
1. Take the stone to someone who would be interested in buying it. This is Taxaynir L'Nvir in Lavastorm, directly outside the cave entrance to Najena.
1. Give her the stone. She will summon a greater fire elemental, and will not be hailable for about 1 minute. Eventually, the elemental will die, and you can hail her to receive the reward.

## Rewards

- At least 25g 70s 61c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Hoop of Elemental Spellweaving]]
  - [[Sash of the Arts]]
  - [[Fire of the Spurned]]
