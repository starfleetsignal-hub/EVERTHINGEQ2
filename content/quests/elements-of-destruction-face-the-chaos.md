The zone for this quest, **Ward of Chaos Elements**, is a solo zone. If you are grouped, you will each get your own instance.

## Steps

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 298, -5, -116}} in [[Myrist, the Great Library]] to enter [[Ward of Chaos Elements (Solo)|Ward of Chaos Elements]].
1. Travel to the [[Ward of Wind and Frost]] at {{waypoint -95, -377, -1}} and kill [[An icecrete rumbler]] and collect an [[Icecrete Shield (Face the Chaos)|Icecrete Shield]] ![](images/Icecrete_Shield.png)
1. Travel to the Abandoned Garrison at {{waypoint -198, -376, 179}} and kill [[A chillsoot dervish|chillsoot dervishes]] and to collect 4 [[Chillsoot ember|chillsoot embers]] that they drop on the ground.
1. Return to the zone entrance at {{waypoint -112, -354, 95}} and right click to throw [[Chillsoot ember]] at Glimmerghast to make him aggro. You may need to throw more than one.
1. Kill [[Glimmerghast, the fog archon]] to open the barrier at {{waypoint -211, -354, 94}}.
   - Use the [[Icecrete Shield (Face the Chaos)|Icecrete Shield]] before and periodically during the fight to receive less damage, recast time is 12.5 seconds.
1. Travel to the [[Ward of Floods and Storms]] at {{waypoint -191, -329, 155}}
1. Kill [[A shock tempest]] and collect a [[Fulgurite Strike (Face the Chaos)|Fulgurite Strike]]. ![](images/Fulgurite_Strike.png)
1. Kill 4 [[Ballast stone rumblers]] and place the bodies in the south part of the pool at {{waypoint -92, -329, 207}}, the water at the far end of the wooden walkway. ![stone rumbler corpse placement](images/Stone_rumbler_corpse_placement.jpg)
1. Kill [[Cursemuck, the mud golem]].
   - Use the [[Fulgurite Strike (Face the Chaos)|Fulgurite Strike]] to strip Cursemuck's damage reduction buff called Slippery Defense. ![Slippery Defense](images/Slippery_Defense.png)
   - Reapply the [[Fulgurite Strike (Face the Chaos)|Fulgurite Strike]] when Cursemuck emotes that he *shakes off the fulgurite*.
1. Travel to the [[Ward of Earthen Might]] at {{waypoint -248, -355, 10}}.
1. Kill [[A stormstone twister]] to collect a [[Wellstone Spout (Face the Chaos)|Wellstone Spout]].
   - *Note: The twisters have a big knockback, so you may want to clear them to avoid being kicked into the hole or the lava.*
   - *Note: There are half a dozen of them and they respawn. It may take 4, 5, or more kills to get the [[Wellstone Spout (Face the Chaos)|Wellstone Spout]].*
1. Kill all of the [[A flamestone fury|flamestone furies]] in order to raise the stone bridges to Blazeanger's island.
1. Kill [[Blazeanger, the lavawurm golem]] at {{waypoint -273, -440, -77}} to remove the barrier at {{waypoint -339, -375, 125}}.
   - Fight him on the small island he stands on.
   - The bridges to the center island fall when he is engaged and he leashes if pulled to the outer circle.
   - Use the [[Wellstone Spout (Face the Chaos)|Wellstone Spout]] to damage Blazeanger.
   - Reapply the [[Wellstone Spout (Face the Chaos)|Wellstone Spout]] when Blazeanger emotes that his *ill temper is back*.
   - Use a chunk of Blazeanger on the ground to exit the island. ![a chunk of Blazeanger](images/A_chunk_of_Blazeanger.jpg)
1. Travel to [[Ward of Fire and Flame]] at {{waypoint -309, -381, 184}} , collect 5 [[Cinder gem|cinder gems]] (the red crystals on floor), also collect 4 [[Tattered paper]], and kill all [[A wildfire|wildfires]] in the room. ![Tattered Paper](images/Tattered_Paper.jpg)![Cinder Gem](images/Cinder_Gem.jpg)
1. Travel to the [[Totality of Knowledge]] at {{waypoint -401, -402, 90}} and gather the [[Filtered Breath (Face the Chaos)|Filtered Breath]] scroll in the room. ![Filtered Breath Scroll](images/Filtered_Breath_Scroll.jpg)
1. Kill all [[A wildfire|wildfires]].
1. Click the **Fires of Knowledge** at {{waypoint -401, -402, 64}} to throw in the papers and gems, spawning Cinderstench.  ![Fires of Knowledge](images/Fires_of_Knowledge.jpg)
1. Kill [[Cinderstench, the smoke archon]] to remove the barrier at {{waypoint -417, -391, 126}}.
   - Use [[Filtered Breath (Face the Chaos)|Filtered Breath]] when you see the emote, *A Toxic Mist overwhelms your senses*. "fills the air with toxin."
1. Travel to the [[Atrium of Chaos]] at {{waypoint -467, -407, 126}} and kill all of the [[A chaos storm|chaos storms]] to activate the lift at {{waypoint -490, -408, 125}}. Once active, click the lift.
1. Defeat Lady Najena
   1. Approach Lady Najena at {{waypoint -498, -179, 114}} and listen to her before she becomes aggro.
      - *Note: use [[Icecrete Shield (Face the Chaos)|Icecrete Shield]] scroll through the entire fight, to reduce the damage taken by 75% and to cast while running*
      - *Note: use [[Filtered Breath (Face the Chaos)|Filtered Breath]] scroll through the entire fight, to heal and restore your power*
   1. At various percentages of her health, Najena becomes immune to damage and summons one of her elementals. Kill the elemental, then return DPS to Najena.
      - At 85% health, she summons Glimmerghast. Use the [[Icecrete Shield (Face the Chaos)|Icecrete Shield]] to defeat him.
      - At 65% health, she summons Cursemuck. Use the [[Fulgurite Strike (Face the Chaos)|Fulgurite Strike]] to defeat him.
      - At 45% health, she summons Blazeanger. Use the [[Wellstone Spout (Face the Chaos)|Wellstone Spout]] to defeat him.
      - At 25% health, she summons Cinderstench. Use [[Filtered Breath (Face the Chaos)|Filtered Breath]] to defeat him.
   1. At 5% health, Najena becomes immune and says **"Who will Norrath turn to when the inevitable shissar invasion begins now? You will regret your actions this day, pet. This I promise!"*, then disappears. ![Najena -  Ward of Chaos Elements](images/Najena_-_Ward_of_Chaos_Elements.jpg)
1. Speak to [[Maelin Starpyre]] at {{waypoint 244, -7, -24}} in [[Myrist, the Great Library]].

## Rewards

- Doomfire, the Burning Lands Raid Key
- Breastplate Based on Class
  - [[Blazeplate Chestguard]] -- *plate*
  - [[Pyrelink Cuirass]] -- *chain*
  - [[Infernal Leather Tunic]] -- *leather*
  - [[Emberweave Robe]] -- *cloth*
- Choice of:
  - [[Fist of the Chillsoot Quencher]]
  - [[Inferno, the Pyreiron Poker]]
  - [[Abyss Reaver, Avenger of Tides]]
  - [[Sky's End, the Gale Blocker]]
  - [[Glimmerwall, the Bluster Buster]]
  - [[Eye of the Vegerog]]
- At least 1p 41g 75s 48000 status
- Will award Epic 2.0 spells if any of your other characters on your account completed the [[Epic Weapon 2.0 Timeline|Epic 2.0 Timeline]].

## Merchant Unlocked

[[Alhuurn Khalen]] will sell apprentice Epic 2.0 spells for characters that betray and have completed the quest.
