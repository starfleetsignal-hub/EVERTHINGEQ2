## Steps

1. Go to the shore of the [[Lake of Ill Omen]] in [[Fens of Nathsar]] and use the [[A portal seed|portal seed]] when standing near the *Altar of Karana*. {{waypoint 146, -119, 146}}
1. Speak with [[Askr]].
1. Defeat the first wave of elementals that spawn.
1. Defeat the 2 [[A putrescent anathema|putrescent anathemae]] that spawn. The [[Avatar of Plagues]] will kill Askr.
1. Allow the [[Avatar of Storms]] to finish off the Avatar of Plagues.
1. Speak to the Avatar of Storms and he will resurrect Askr. You will have to right-click and hail the Avatar to speak with it.
1. Speak with Askr.

## Rewards

- At least 27g 53s 23c
- +10000 faction with **Karana**
- Cloak: [[Cloak of Storms]], increases spell and combat art damage
