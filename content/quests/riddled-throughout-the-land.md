## Notes

- **This quest is available to get and complete only on April 1st.**
- To travel to and from the Enchanted Lands and Wanderlust Fair, use the [[Travel in Norrath|druid rings in each zone]]. In the Enchanted Lands, the ring is found east of the docks.
- The same method can be used to travel to Greater Faydark, using the spire to the east in the Enchanted Lands.

## Steps

![deck of cards](images/Deck_of_cards.jpg)

1. Speak with [[Imenand]] and answer a few preliminary riddles. He will then give you a series of three riddles to test your scholarly prowess...
   - Q: A gigglegibber found some chain and wishes to use it for a belt. It was three times his height, so he cut it in half. He is one snake tall. How long is his new chain belt?
   - A: Two times the length from its middle to one end.
   - Q: A wizard from Gorowyn claims to have an amazing object. He says that this object gets wetter as it dries. What is the item?
   - A: A towel.

![egg](images/Egg.jpg)

1. Travel to the [[Wanderlust Fair]] in [[Darklight Wood]] and retrieve the deck of cards from behind [[Quild K'Aleate]] (the General Goods vendor) {{waypoint 271, -20, -17}} They are on a crate behind him.
1. Return to [[Imenand]].
1. Travel to the Coldwind Coast Southern Beach in [[Antonica]] and retrieve the egg {{waypoint -722.50, -37.53, 862.23}}.

![map](images/Map.jpg)

1. Return to [[Imenand]].
1. Travel to the [[Spire Shadow Outpost]], the camp below the spires, in [[Greater Faydark]] and retrieve the map {{waypoint -389.18, 14.60, 177.97}}.
1. Return to [[Imenand]].
