## Steps

1. Speak with [[Billiam Fitzherbert]], the Fordel Midst Trades Commissioner (in the [[Shadow Haven]] area) {{waypoint -680.60, 9.63, -369.52}}.
1. Search [[Aurelian Coast]] for the missing caravan {{waypoint 357, 50, -175}}.
1. Look around the campfires and in the Elder's Cave for 8 Stolen Bank Lock boxes. They can not be tracked, can be inside the caves where there can be multiple Stolen Bank Lockboxes per location. ![Stolen Bank Lockbox](images/Quest_Stolen_Bank_Lockbox.jpg). Possible cave locations are:
   - {{waypoint -6.19, 80.76, -474.24}}, {{waypoint 4.91, 80.23, -470.58}}, {{waypoint 10.86, 80.49, -486.75}}
   - {{waypoint 3.28, 91.05, -628.04}}, {{waypoint 8.66, 88.83, -648.18}}, {{waypoint -8.11, 88.33, -656.08}}, {{waypoint -61.00, 90.16, -623.58}}
   - {{waypoint 42.41, 79.56, -562.05}}, {{waypoint 64.23, 79.71, -582.30}}, {{waypoint 54.65, 79.47, -598.04}}
1. Other Locations:
   - {{waypoint 205.44, 63.28, -315.65}}
   - {{waypoint 188.66, 79.22, -215.50}}
   - {{waypoint -48.69, 93.73, -530.75}}
   - {{waypoint 90.31, 76.78, -259.64}}
   - {{waypoint 338.31, 73.16, -276.40}}, {{waypoint 346.74, 74.64, -287.52}}
   - {{waypoint 306.08, 65.76, -211.95}}
1. Return to [[Billiam Fitzherbert]].
1. Return to [[Principa Haleene Sirinae]].
