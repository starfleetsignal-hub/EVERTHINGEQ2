## Steps

1. Attempt to recover Herga's helm at {{waypoint 753, 55, -509}}
1. Kill the [[A frenzied Ry'Gorr|frenzied Ry'Gorr]] that pops.
1. Recover the helm.
1. Return to [[Battlepriest Herga]] {{waypoint 518, 27, -243}}

- At least 76c
- Suffix title: [[Friend of Herga]]
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Fighter's Bracelet (Version 1)|Fighter's Bracelet]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Bracelet (Version 1)|Priest's Bracelet]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Bracelet (Version 1)|Scout's Bracelet]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Bracelet (Version 1)|Sorcerer's Bracelet]] (All Mages)
