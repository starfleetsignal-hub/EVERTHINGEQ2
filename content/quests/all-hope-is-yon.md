## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
- Gathered items are not trackable. When you complete the quest and deposit items in the satchel it will take a couple minutes before scroll respawns to give you new quest.

## Steps

Upon signing the scroll from Narezi you will receive the quest. Narezi needs you to gather more items for her and recover more antiques.

1. You will be assigned random 2 tasks from list below (1 gathering and 1 killing)
   - *All the items she needs will be found in [[Writher Ruins]] and [[Writher Crevasse]] areas of [[Yon Gorroth]].*
   - 12 antique Norrathian forest vases - at the bottom of [[Writher Crevasse]]
   - 12 antique Norrathian etched vases - at the bottom of [[Writher Crevasse]]
   - 8 parasitic samples from husked voidmaws
   - 8 undead venom sacs from blightcoil revenants
1. Deposit items in the satchel next to [[Kith]] and scroll by [[Yon Gorroth Obelisk]] {{waypoint -630, -1, 442}}

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following:
  - [[Antique Norrathian Forest Vase]]
  - [[Antique Norrathian Etched Vase]]
