## Steps

1. Speak to the Shade of the Great Crocodile at {{waypoint -1436, -229, -468}}
1. Kill some [[A dervish croc hunter|dervish croc hunters]] along the beach
1. Speak to the Shade of the Great Crocodile at {{waypoint -891, -127, -340}}
1. Find 5 crocodile eggs.
   - *Known locations are {{waypoint -1018, -136, -410}}, {{waypoint -1052, -136, -409}}, {{waypoint -1196, -124, -581}}, {{waypoint -1049, -109, -672}}.*
   - *Note: Look near bigger stones and pillars in the dunes*
1. Speak to the Shade of the Great Crocodile at {{waypoint -859, -145, -1105}}
1. Kill 6 unliving soldiers near the Eye of Anuk. {{waypoint -697, -156, -1274}} Includes:
   - [[An unliving augur]]
   - [[An unliving lookout]]
   - [[An unliving theurgist]] with its [[An unliving trooper]]
   - [[An unliving trooper]]
   - *Note: ( [[An unliving healer]], [[An unliving soldier]], [[An unliving tracker]], [[An unliving vision seeker]]  )*
1. Return to [[Shalih Mar]] to complete this quest.
