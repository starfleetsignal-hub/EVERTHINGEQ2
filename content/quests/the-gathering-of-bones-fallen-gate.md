## Steps

Kill 11 [[A blighted patron|blighted patrons]] (ghosts) in [[Fallen Gate]]. These can be found inside the buildings found at {{waypoint 104.73, 6.88, -69.00}}.

- 1280 status at Level 21
- 482 status at Level 91
- +500 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
