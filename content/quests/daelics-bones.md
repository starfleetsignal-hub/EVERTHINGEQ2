## Steps

1. Gather 15 [[Soggy bones]] from the bottom of the ocean floor in [[Everstorm Bay]]. The bones, which are added to your inventory, are gathered underwater from Sunken Bones nodes located west of the Everfrost Docks, around where where the two small icebergs are. They are random spawns, however see the talk page for locations confirmed by other players.
   - They can be tab-targeted and [[Gnomish Divining Rod|Gnomish Divining Rods]] work on them, as well.
1. Return to [[Daelic Coldbrow]].
1. Bury the bones at the [[World Tree Root]] {{waypoint -650, -15, -305}} to receive your reward

## Rewards

- At least 3g 7s
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Ever Frozen Defender's Waistband]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Ever Frozen Healer's Waistband]] (All Priests)
  - [[Ever Frozen Medium's Waistband]] (All Mages)
  - [[Ever Frozen Stalker's Waistband]] (All Scouts)
