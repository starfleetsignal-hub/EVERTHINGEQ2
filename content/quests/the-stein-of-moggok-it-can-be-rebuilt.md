## Prerequisites

- In order to start the quest you must be level 35 or higher.

## Notes

- This quest is also part of the [[Feerrott Timeline]].
- This quest must be completed to progress along the [[Illusionist Epic Weapon Timeline|Illusionist Epic]].

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Feerrott]] and slay four ogre ghosts to collect the four broken pieces of the stein:
   - **Left-Half**: [[Bouncer Flerb (Feerrott)|Bouncer Flerb]] {{waypoint -27, -4, 392}}; found along the path south from the docks to near the domesticated sattars and [[Rallosian Mage Shrine]]
   - **Right-Half**: [[Bouncer Fug (Feerrott)|Bouncer Fug]] {{waypoint -446, 2, 146}}; found to the east of [[Murdunk's Fall]] amongst the [[A thule sattar|thule sattars]].
   - **Handle**: [[Bouncer Hurd (Feerrott)|Bouncer Hurd]] {{waypoint 12, 23, 845}}; wanders around the southwest of the zone near [[Cythan]].
   - **Lid**: [[Bouncer Prud (Feerrott)|Bouncer Prud]] {{waypoint -292, -5, 493}}; spawns near [[Golgoth Bridge]] and runs along the road northeast towards [[Alliz Tae Outpost]] at {{waypoint -1296, 2, 650}}.
     - *Alternatively:, you can kill all four bouncers at one time in [[The Eidolon Jungle]] after praying to the Shrine of Rallos Zek, located at {{waypoint -448.86, 4.05, 123.79}}.*
     - *Note: the bouncers all spawn at one time, and were level 100 epic; however, I was still able to solo kill all four as a level 100 assassin.*
1. Return to [[Rumdum]] and he will tell you that you need the Blood Ruby to rebuild the stein.
1. Go back to [[The Feerrott]] and slay [[Clurg the Bartender]]. To spawn Clurg, swim to the bottom of the pond that is found at {{waypoint 30, -9, 698}}. Killing him will give you the update.
1. Go back to [[Rumdum]].  He will sell you the recipe to craft the stein for 4 silver (enough to buy him another ale). You will get [[The Blood Ruby]], [[The Four Parts of the Stein of Moggok]] and [[Clurg's Family Stein Recipe]]. You must be at least a level 9 artisan to scribe the recipe [[Clurg’s Family Stein Recipe]].
1. Craft the Stein to complete the quest and get your reward. The recipe is a level 9 artificing recipe and requires the two components given to you in the previous step, a [[Glimmering material]] (semi-rare harvestable) and a [[Basic Coal]] for fuel. This is done at a [[Work Bench]]. You can have someone craft this as a consignment for you if they have already done the quest and have the recipe.
