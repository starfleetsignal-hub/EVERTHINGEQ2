## Notes

- Must be at least tradeskill level 110 to obtain.
- MUST HAVE A MEMBERSHIP TO ZONE IN !!! This zone is identical to [[The Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen]] and the requirements to progress through the zone are the same as the original. All of the undead frogloks are level 120 Epic x4^^^ and *will* one-shot the player. They have a smaller aggro radius than normal, but you cannot get within 15 meters of them. Chalice have a range of 18.5m.
- To move through the zone, you must use a [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] which will banish (despawn) any aggressive monsters. If there is a linked encounter, one chalice will banish everything in the encounter, so be sure to use that to your advantage.
- Always assume there are monsters just past any doorway or intersection, right around the corner where you can't see. Approaching while hugging one wall so you can pan your camera out to check is helpful.
- Avoid intersections and standing in the middle of hallways unless you've banished the knights which roam the area. They move quickly and can teleport. Clear an area and move to the corners of the room where possible.
- It is recommended you craft at least 100 [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] (two combines) to ensure you have enough chalices to banish all of the frogloks and other monsters in the zone needed to move around safely. [drag item to hotbar key for ease of use, button updates total Chalices  remaining]
  - if one avoids killing the floating yellow blobs(uncertain agro range), one will have ample Chalice left for last boss. e.g. Crafted 150, killed EVERYTHING, had 48 Chalice left.
- The [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] and [[Remnant of valor (no-zone)|Remnants of Valor]] are NO-ZONE. If you leave the zone before completing the quest, you will need to craft new chalices.

## Resources Needed

- 50 [[Celestial Coal]] *(recommend 100)*
- 600 "any of the common Planes of Prophecy [[Harvestables (Tier 12)]]" *(recommend 1200)*
- NOTE:  I tried to do this with the 50 Restored Chalice of Valor that you get using the 600 harvests, and I ran out of them (used all 50) and was not able to complete the zone.  I highly, highly, recommend you harvest 1,200 and make 100 chalices.

## Steps

![remnant of valor](images/Remnant_of_valor_%28pickup%29.png)

1. Enter [[The Fabled Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen (Tradeskill Mission)|The Fabled Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen [Tradeskill Mission]]] in the [[Plane of Magic]] near Valor's Roost at {{waypoint -814.03, 343.13, 1079.34}}
1. Harvest two [[Remnant of valor (no-zone)|Remnants of Valor]] and craft a [[Restored Chalice of Valor]]
   1. Harvest two Remnant of Valor at {{waypoint -16.14, -0.15, 1.12}}. This will eventually respawn, but other remnants spawn after a monster is banished.
   1. Click on **an ancient scroll stand** at {{waypoint -18.09, 0.77, -6.64}} to receive the recipe [[Restored Chalice of Valor]].
   1. Craft the [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] (one full combine makes a stack of 50) at the forge next to the ancient scroll stand.
      - One combine requires 600 of any common [[Planes of Prophecy]] harvest.
      - It is recommended you craft another stack of 50 after the quest updates.
      - You can obtain more remnants by banishing the frogloks near the entrance.
1. Banish [[An Ancient Fungoid]] {{waypoint 106.96, -37.17, -193.53}}.
   1. Move down the ramp and use the [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] on the mobs to get past them.
      - *[[A fetid tuk knight]] moves to one of the 2 positions and teleports back after a few seconds*.
   1. Use the [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] on [[An Ancient Fungoid]].  Harvest the fungus near him (see step 4.1).  (Note: The fungus was hard to see, it's in the glowing area, I had to zoom in to see it.)
1. Banish [[Rideepa the Prideful]] {{waypoint 173.20, -60.87, -235.13}}.
   1. Harvest a [[Pure fungus (Version 1)|pure fungus]] at {{waypoint 94.73, -34.97, -208.17}}.
   1. Use the fungus at the statue at the bottom of the pool at {{waypoint 68.64, -40.14, -112.38}}. Be sure to banish everything above the pool (look up and Use the [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] on the Glimmers) before entering. (Note: Again, the statue was hard to see, I had to zoom in.)
   1. Move through the gate at {{waypoint 111.08, -33.53, -119.88}}. (Note: The gate opens after you use the fungus on the statue in Step 2 above.)
   1. Hug the right side in the first small hallway, then go around the left and hug the corners to get past the roamers to reach the safe spot at {{waypoint 156.80, -46.32, -165.68}}.
   1. [[A decayed gaz knight]] teleports all over the rooms.
   1. Use the [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] on [[Rideepa the Prideful]].
   1. Grab the totem that was behind [[Rideepa the Prideful]].
1. Banish [[Froppit the Everliving]] {{waypoint -75.50, -47.59, -246.44}}.
   1. Move the totem from behind [[Rideepa the Prideful]] {{waypoint 172.00, -62.17, -246.03}} back to the "pool" room and place it on the on the pedestal at {{waypoint 8, -25, -100}}.
   1. Move to the safe corner at {{waypoint 51.76, -39.34, -179.13}}, then to the next safe corner at {{waypoint -34.14, -39.45, -199.25}} and proceed down the hallway.
   1. The [[A reanimated unstable ghoul|reanimated unstable ghouls]] roam in circles and despawn at the end of the big slope. You will need to dodge between them and remove the static mobs.
   1. Don't take the stairs down; there's a constant parade of [[A reanimated unstable ghoul|reanimated unstable ghouls]] up the stair. Go around the curved hall the long way.
   1. Use the [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] on [[Froppit the Everliving]].
1. Banish [[Molinap the Destructor]] {{waypoint -78.56, -31.14, -388.50}}.
   1. Move up to the puzzle at {{waypoint -54.88, -42.99, -258.12}}.  You must click the three blocks in order: lower (mouth symbol), upper right (an eye with a slitted pupil symbol), upper left (spiral symbol) to open the door behind you. *If you die past this point, this is where you'll revive.*
      - The puzzle order can apparently change.  When you click a block and it stays in, you did that one correctly. Confirmed that the order may instead be mouth, spiral, eye.
      - The puzzle opens the door at {{waypoint -80, -43, -263}}. Go through the door.
   1. Move down, use the [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] on the [[A deadly cadavodile|cadavodiles]](4)  in the water so as to avoid any unpleasant surprises if one missteps ramp back into water, use the [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] on the overhead glimmers, then head up the path (it circles the room a couple of times as it rises) and turn right at the intersection at the top {{waypoint -10.04, -32.21, -373.19}}.
   1. Use the [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] on [[Molinap the Destructor]].
1. Banish [[A Ghoul Usurper]] {{waypoint 147.87, -64.30, -255.58}}.
   1. Move through the gate at {{waypoint 8.88, -33.29, -323.41}} and jump down the big black hole. *There's a [[A deadly cadavodile|deadly cadavodile]] on the lip of the pool at the bottom, be sure to zap it with the chalice.*
   1. Clear the assorted ghoul frogloks by using the [[Restored Chalice of Valor]]. *Watch out for the roaming [[A fetid shin knight|fetid shin knights]], they appear to walk a little then teleport, and they move fast. Unfortunately, it's possible to have one teleport right on top of you.*
   1. There are some dead ghoul bodies in the halls you must move to the following locations:
      - {{waypoint -24.31, -58.59, -254.90}}
      - {{waypoint 107.68, -63.20, -250.61}}
      - {{waypoint 151.99, -63.45, -258.65}}
      - *The named spawns right by the final pedestal as soon as you place the body upon it. This also is the next revive point.*
   1. Use the [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] on [[A Ghoul Usurper]].
1. Banish [[Lord Kurpep]] {{waypoint 123.59, -68.26, -389.54}}.
   1. Move to {{waypoint 122.03, -64.05, -291.31}} then further down.
   1. Use the [[Restored Chalice of Valor]] on [[Lord Kurpep]].
