## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Innoruuk has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items

1. Purchase the [[Vial of Red Centipede Venom|vial of red centipede venom]] from Nihala for 5000 status
   - Wanders the road in the Valley of the Dead in [[Nektulos Forest]] near {{waypoint -415, 60, -1584}}
1. Purchase the [[Black Dahlia Petal|black dahlia petal]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the [[Fractured Crystal Mirror|fractured crystal mirror]] from Kuutanya for 5000 status
   - Wanders the path on Isle of Wisdom in [[Zimara Breadth]])
1. Return to the [[Herald to the Prince of Hate]] at {{waypoint -1644, 62, -1477}} in [[Nektulos Forest]]
