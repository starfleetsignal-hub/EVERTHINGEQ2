## Old Quest

The following series was *retired,* but is being retained for historical purposes.

1. Find eight (8) Nights of the Dead objects in various zones:
   1. A **basket of apples** is located in each major city.
      - *Note:  You only need 1 basket from any of the following cities:*
      - [[North Qeynos]] at {{waypoint 365, -19, 116}} near [[Irontoe's East]]
      - [[West Freeport]] at {{waypoint 25, -18, 83}} first floor of the [[The Coalition of Tradesfolke (POI)]] near the crafting trainer
      - [[Gorowyn]] at {{waypoint 2615, 119, 1152}} near the [[Research Assistants|Research Assistant]]
      - [[Kelethin]] at {{waypoint 460, 93, 326}} near [[Tunare's Pages]]
      - [[New Halas]] at {{waypoint -332, 176, -43}} near the tradeskill area
      - [[Neriak]] at {{waypoint 1, 7, 353}} in the [[Dockside Markets]]
   1. An **odd arrangement of bones** in the basement of the [[The Haunting (Quest)|(City) Haunted House]] at {{waypoint -11.45, -4.75, 10.09}}.
   1. A **plate of rats** in the basement of the [[Haunted Mansion]] in [[Loping Plains]] at {{waypoint -379.02, 0.00, -0.85}}.
      - Complete the quest [[This Won't End Well...]] to gain access to the Haunted Mansion.
   1. A **Di'Zok skull** in [[The Hedge Hollow]] at {{waypoint 995, 8, -603}}.
   1. A **Plate of Nights of the Dead Frosted Cookies** in [[Maj'Dul]] at {{waypoint 78, 148, -136}} , on a table inside the The Golden Scepter.
   1. **Sticky spider eggs** in [[Antonica]] at {{waypoint -1700, -19, -53}}, on the southwest field of the McQuibble farm.
   1. A **broken canopic jar** in Charnel Hollow (located in the same place as [[The Cauldron Hollow]] and the [[The Hedge Hollow]] at {{waypoint 973, 8, -506}}. You must be on [[Survive the Night]] quest to get the Broken Canopic Jar.  If you do not get it during [[Survive the Night]] the jar will be next to [[Setri Lur'eth]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] at {{waypoint 1088.12, -0.40, -328.54}} after you finish the quest.
   1. A **Mouldering Jack-o-Lantern**  Found near the Ghost Hunter Contest Antonica: {{waypoint 55, -19, -22}} or Commonlands: {{waypoint -1103, -60, 15}}
1. Talk to [[Terrah Sinew]] on the docks in Nektulos Forest {{waypoint 803, 6, -120}}.
