## Steps

1. Destroy 8 Syllokk Idols in the [[Wurmscale Barrens]]  {{waypoint -211, 122, 580}}
   - *Note: They spawn when you are around 10m close to them. Look near the edge of the road or near the walls in large areas until the river ends*![Syllokk Idol](images/Syllokk_Idols.png)
   - *Some can be found in these locations:   {{waypoint 652, 213, 790}}  {{waypoint 220, 137, 641}}  {{waypoint 533, 148, 685}}  {{waypoint 556, 180, 666}}  {{waypoint 125, 136, 708}}  {{waypoint 156, 136, 688}}  {{waypoint 494, 201, 794}}  {{waypoint 74, 134, 866}}   {{waypoint 129, 137, 710}}  {{waypoint 461, 134, 747}}  {{waypoint 104, 135, 860}}  {{waypoint 417, 201, 808}}
     - *Not trackable.*

*<nowiki/>*

1. Kill "a Syllokk zealot" that spawns.*<nowiki/>*
1. Defeat 5 Remaining Zealot Patrols. There are groups of them around [[Grunt's Pass]] {{waypoint -251.98, 35.35, 441.27}}.
1. Return to Zhazsze.
