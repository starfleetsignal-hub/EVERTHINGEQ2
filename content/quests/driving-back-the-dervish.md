## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[A Dervish crook|Dervish crooks]] {{waypoint -101, -4, 112}}
1. Return to Liege Aquila {{waypoint -125, -5, 88}}

## Rewards

- At least 50c (1st time)
- At least 4s 82c (2nd time)
- At least 4s 82c and +150 faction with **The Freeport Militia** (3rd time)
