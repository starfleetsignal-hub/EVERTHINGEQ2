## Prerequisites

- You must have at least -6000 faction with the Legion of Danak to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks:
   - Kill 10 skeletons ([[An ancient denizen]], [[An ancient inhabitant]], [[An ancient occupant]]) in either of the ruins found in [[Stonehoof Badlands]]. {{waypoint -936, -77, -382}} or {{waypoint -326, -61, -530}}
   - Gather the "old map", at {{waypoint -326, -61, -530}}.
1. Return to [[Field Master Selrahc]].

## Rewards

- +150 faction with **Legion of Danak**
- At least 19g 96s 3c
