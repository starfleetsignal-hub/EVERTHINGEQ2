This quest is part 4 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Dark Elf Series.

## Steps

1. Upon accepting this quest you receive Poisoned Loaf of Bread (LORE, NO-TRADE, NO-VALUE) in your inventory.
1. Go to the vendor booth of provisioner Lynsia T'Kanix on the central square of Longshadow Alley {{waypoint -1, 0, -19}}.
1. Examine the Poisoned Loaf of Bread in your inventory and select "Put the bread on the ledge".
1. Wait a few seconds. Teah J'Nal will walk up to the booth, take the bread and eat it. The bread proves to be skillfully poisoned, and Teah drops dead.
1. Return to [[Aldera V'Exxa]] for your reward.
