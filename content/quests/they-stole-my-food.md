*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Slay three types of lizardmen to get semi-common quest updates. All are found around the druid ring {{waypoint -550, 0, 890}}.
   - [[A Tae Ew infiltrator|Tae Ew infiltrators]] (drop the barrel)
   - [[A Tae Ew pillager|Tae Ew pillagers]] (drop the box)
   - [[A Tae Ew raider|Tae Ew raiders]] (drop the chest)
1. Return to Krozk to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 67s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Apostle's Plate Cuirass of the Divinehand]]
  - [[Channeler's Robe of the Runemaster]]
  - [[Hanshi's Gi of the Whirlingfist]]
  - [[Knight's Breastplate of the Warleader]]
  - [[Oracle's Hauberk of the Fatebringer]]
  - [[Protector's Tunic of the Tempestwalker]]
  - [[Raider's Chain Tunic of the Heartstriker]]
