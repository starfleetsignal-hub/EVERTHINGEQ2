## Steps

1. Left-click crates at the following locations to destroy them. At each crate, a group consisting of [[A gnoll machinist]] (10vv Berserker), [[A gnoll guard]] (10vv Assassin) and [[A machinist assistant]] (10vv Coercer) will spawn guarding them.  You do not technically have to kill them to complete the quest, but they'll interfere with your attempts to destroy the crates if you don't.
   - {{waypoint 690, -33, 600}}
   - {{waypoint 750, -33, 600}}
   - {{waypoint 800, -33, 580}}
1. Return to Lieutenant Dawson for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
- +175 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
