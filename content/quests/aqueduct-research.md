## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- You must have +20,000 faction with the Court of Truth to unlock this quest. Once +50,000 faction has been achieved, however, this quest will no longer be available.

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Living Tombs]] in [[The Sinking Sands]]. {{waypoint -368, -142, -1037}}
1. Hop onto the aqueduct on the left from the zone-in (before the jump down) and go to {{waypoint -501, 11, 311}}.
   - You may need to jump toward the north wall get a quest update.
     - If this doesn't work, go to {{waypoint -629, -18, 313}}.
1. Go to beneath the central drainage arm of the aqueduct in the [[Residence Court]]. {{waypoint -360, 14, 408}}
1. Gather an 'ancient aqueduct fragment' (looks like a ground shiny) from the aqueduct on your way back to the entrance. Locations include the following:
     - {{waypoint -354, 14, 287}}
     - {{waypoint -408, 14, 288}}
     - {{waypoint -435, 14, 288}}
     - {{waypoint -449, 14, 285}}
     - {{waypoint -344, 14, 289}}
     - {{waypoint -309, 14, 292}}
1. Return to Muyassar Zuhur.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 23s 29c
- -500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- +500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
