## Steps

1. Capture 8 wild young sokokars by targeting them and using [[Sulrig's Net]].
   - They are found to the
     - south and southwest of [[Western Pens of Riliss]]. {{waypoint -690, -140, 708}}
     - northwest of [[Western Pens of Riliss]]. {{waypoint -618, -91, 498}} and {{waypoint -874, -111, 327}}
1. Return to [[Trainer Sulrig]].

## Rewards

- At least 8g 11s 19c
- +2,000 faction with **Riliss**
- [[Glowing Gem of Casting Haste]]
