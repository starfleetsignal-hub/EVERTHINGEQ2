## Notes

- You receive [[The Fall of Erudin: Part 1]] right after accepting this quest.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Sulayman]] the Dramaturge in the [[Deepwater Pavilion]] located {{waypoint -149, 181, -446}}.
1. Watch the performance *The Fall of Erudin* at the amphitheater behind [[Sulayman]]. Get right up on the stage at {{waypoint -162, 174, -701}}.
   - *The story will take some time to tell, and expands upon a great bit of Erudin and Odus -related lore.*
   - *The play begins immediately after speaking with Sulayman, but allow 15 minutes for the automated quest dialogue to finish. For those who enjoy lore, this is very interesting!*
   - *If you missed the update at the conclusion of the play, return to Sulayman to start it again.*
