## Steps

1. Click on the Stolen Box at {{waypoint -102, 99, -85}} and kill the Aluxob muggers that spawn. ![Stolen Box](images/Stolen_Box.jpg)
1. Click on the box again to receive [[Mysterious Box]].
1. Return to [[Karo-amat]].
1. Hide the [[Mysterious Box]] at {{waypoint -909, 78, -554}}.

![One of the Aluxob muggers](images/Aluxob_mugger.jpg)
![Place the Box for Safe Keeping](images/Place_the_Box_for_Safe_Keeping.jpg)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Pride Pakiat Battle Shrouds]] (Cloth)
  - [[Pride Pakiat Battle Shoulders]] (Chain)
  - [[Pride Pakiat Battle Shoulderguards]] (Leather)
  - [[Pride Pakiat Battle Pauldrons]] (Plate)
