This is the starter quest for the tradeskill version of the player griffon mount.
The only pre-requisite is that you have a level 85 tradeskill. None of the trade quests need to be completed in [[The Great Divide]] tradeskill line to start this quest line.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Fenwick]] on the dock in the [[Great Divide]] at Thurgadin Harbor {{waypoint 1292.74, -540.25, 473.59}}
1. Return to Aggi Stonefist

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
