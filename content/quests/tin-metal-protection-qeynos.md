**If your character is evil aligned, see the [[Blat Berisen]] page for location to start and complete this quest.**

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Frethe Zizlop (Qeynos Province District)|Frethe Zizlop]] in [[The Hall of Steel]] in [[Qeynos Harbor (POI)|Qeynos Harbor]] ([[Qeynos Province District]]) {{waypoint 677, -20, 22}}.
   - This will start the subquest [[Card Deals (Qeynos)|Card Deals]].
1. Speak with [[Zoe Hertzflip (Qeynos Province District)|Zoe Hertzflip]] on the second floor of [[The Mermaid's Lure]] on the docks in Qeynos Harbor {{waypoint 932, -19, 1}}.
   - This will start the subquest [[Ogled Goggles (Qeynos)|Ogled Goggles]].
1. Speak with [[Barb Islenet (Qeynos Capitol District)|Barb Islenet]] on the second floor balcony of [[The Herb Jar]] alchemist's shop in [[South Qeynos (POI)|South Qeynos]] ([[Qeynos Capitol District]]) {{waypoint 641, -12, 235}}. (You can stand on the streat below her and talk with her)
   - This will start the subquest [[Wine for the Lady (Qeynos)|Wine for the Lady]].
1. Speak with [[Abner Siblet (Qeynos Capitol District)|Abner Siblet]] in the [[Lion's Mane Inn and Tavern]] in South Qeynos {{waypoint 559, -8, 129}}.
   - This will start the subquest [[Fribles's Fate (Qeynos)|Fribles's Fate]].
1. Go to {{waypoint 359, -21, 606}}, south of the Qeynos griffon station in [[Antonica]]. [[Belazzor Fribles, the Inept (Antonica)|Belazzor Fribles, the Inept]] will spawn and attack you; his level scales to yours.
   - Kill [[Belazzor Fribles, the Inept (Antonica)|Fribles]].
1. Go back to [[Abner Siblet (Qeynos Capitol District)|Abner Siblet]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]].
1. Return to the remaining NPC's in reverse order to complete each of the subquests. You will get Exp/AA exp for all 4 of them.
1. Return to [[Blat Berisen (Antonica)|Blat Berisen]] in Antonica for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Zoe's Wild Deck of Playing Cards]]
- [[Tin Metal Helm of Protection]]
