## Steps

1. In [[Butcherblock Mountains]], harvest six traps from the chests inside a building in [[West Fort Irontoe]]. {{waypoint -267, 215, -159}}
   - Each chest has three traps inside of it and there are 3 chests. If you are in a group you'll have to wait for a respawns. Respawn time on chests is 1-2 minutes.
1. Go to [[Kaladim]] and do the following:
   - Obtain 10 pelts by killing Frothbark Kobolds (most found by the entrance, uncommon update).
   - Kill 2 [[A Frothbark whipcracker|Frothbark whipcrackers]]. {{waypoint 25, 26, -116}}
   - Place six traps by harvesting the markers near the entrance.
1. Return to [[Captain T.B. Irontoe]].

- [[A whipcracker ringlet]]

  - [[Medium's Chain of Enchanted Energy]] (All Mages)
  - [[Healer's Chain of Enchanted Energy]] (All Priests)
  - [[Defender's Chain of Enchanted Energy]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Stalker's Chain of Enchanted Energy]] (All Scouts)
