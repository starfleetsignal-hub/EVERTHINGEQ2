## Steps

1. Enter the [[A Temple chamber|Temple chamber]] in the back room of the [[Temple of War]] in [[North Freeport (POI)|North Freeport]] {{waypoint -87, -7, -190}}
1. Speak to [[Lerenth (a Temple chamber)|Lerenth]].
1. Speak to [[The Forgotten (a Temple chamber)|The Forgotten]].
1. Speak to [[Lerenth (a Temple chamber)|Lerenth]] again. "You're in trouble..." / "You've come this far..." will get him to accept his "reward"
1. Quest auto-completes after [[Lerenth (a Temple chamber)|Lerenth]] is "rewarded"

## Rewards

- At least 6g 27s 71c @ level 79
- [[An oggok bone chime]]
