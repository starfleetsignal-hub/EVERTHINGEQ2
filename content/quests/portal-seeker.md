## Steps

1. Find the Legion of the Oblivion's portal to [[Nightscar Borne Lands]] somewhere in the [[Oblivion's Bask]] plateau {{waypoint -49, 136, -234}}
1. Attempt to enter the portal - you will be knocked back
1. Hunt constellate and nebullate wisps x 6, which are in the same area.
1. Return to [[Vadreem (The Unknown)|Vadreem]] on [[Nightscar Steppes]].

## Rewards

- [[Void Nebullate]] - vanity pet
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- One of the following:
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]]
  - [[Star Blood Longbow of Rifts]]
  - [[Parasitic Crossbow of Parables]]
