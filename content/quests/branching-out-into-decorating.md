- Scribe [[Frostfell Lair Decoration]], received when the quest is accepted, if you haven't already learned the recipes.

## Steps

1. Craft 6 [[Frostfell Tree (Crafted)|Frostfell Trees]]:

   - 36 [[Elven Magic]], from [[Ginneldarf Gigglegibber]] {{waypoint 36, 0, -573}}
   - [[Work Bench]] {{waypoint 22, 0, -577}}
1. Return to Nudd Frostfoot.
