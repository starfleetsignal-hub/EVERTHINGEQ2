## Steps

1. Hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 12 [[Malicious dustmaw|malicious dustmaws]] for skin, for example around {{waypoint -273.95, -53.39, 652.02}}. ![A malicious dustmaw](images/A_malicious_dustmaw.png)
   - Hunt 8 [[Arachnid sandstalker|arachnid sandstalkers]] for shells, for example around {{waypoint -273.95, -53.39, 652.02}}. ![An arachnid sandstalker](images/An_arachnid_sandstalker.png)
   - Gather 8 **Ros's Eye Agates from quest-specific nodes**.  ![Ro's Eye Agate](images/Ro's_Eye_Agate.png)
     - These are small sand-colored clickable rocks scattered around [[Sunscar Vale]] and  [[Desert of Woe]]
     - They are above most of the big stone formations around the area, with some possible locations:
       - {{waypoint -150, -10, 360}}
       - {{waypoint -140, -5, 436}}
       - {{waypoint -53, -1, 237}}
1. Return to [[Rahele]].

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- +3,500 faction with **Hizite Nomads of Ro**
- One of the following:
  - [[Spellwoven Dendromail Bracers]]
  - [[Spellwoven Viridsilk Armguards]]
  - [[Spellwoven Florahide Armguards]]
  - [[Spellwoven Chloroplate Gussets]]
