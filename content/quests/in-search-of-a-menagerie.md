## Notes

- This quest is no longer required to access [[Miragul's Menagerie]].

## Steps

1. Examine the Yeoman's Remains near [[Undertow]] in the [[Icewail Caverns]]. {{waypoint -374, -28, 54}}
1. Use the key you find on the remains to open the chest on the ocean floor in the same room. {{waypoint -402, -35, 41}}
1. Examine the [[Scroll case]] you find.
1. Complete four sub-quests for the four spirits in Everfrost to receive their blessings:
   - [[The Spirit of the Frigid Pine]], obtained from [[The Spirit of Frigid Pine]] at {{waypoint -723, 1, -300}}
   - [[The Spirit of Arctic Doom (Quest)|The Spirit of Arctic Doom]], obtained from [[The Spirit of Arctic Doom (NPC)|The Spirit of Arctic Doom]] at {{waypoint -41, -1, -1184}}
   - [[The Spirit of Polar Lights (Quest)|The Spirit of Polar Lights]], obtained from [[The Spirit of Polar Lights (NPC)|The Spirit of Polar Lights]] at {{waypoint 369, -38, -1398}}
   - [[The Spirit of Wintry Mist (Quest)|The Spirit of Wintry Mist]], obtianed from [[The Spirit of Wintry Mist (NPC)|The Spirit of Wintry Mist]] {{waypoint -676, -38, -828}}
1. After completing all four tasks for the spirits, go to the doors of [[Miragul's Menagerie]] in [[Icewail Caverns]]. Click the velium plate beside the door to complete the quest. {{waypoint -92, -23, -285}}
