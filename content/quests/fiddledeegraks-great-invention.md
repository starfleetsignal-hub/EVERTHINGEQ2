## Steps

1. Speak to [[Bertram Fiddledeegrak]].
1. Travel to [[Steamfont Mountains]] and enter the [[Abandoned Mines of Ak'Anon]] {{waypoint 61.02, 92.59, 1242.45}} near the Druid Ring.
1. ![coaxial cable](images/Coaxial_cable.jpg)![covalent fusecell](images/Covalent_fusecell.jpg)Gather five **coaxial cables** and six **covalent fusecells** from the zone.
   - Formerly, clockworks were set with latest expansion stats, and if lacking said expansion, and/or the latest, greatest, and best of the best in terms of gear, players expected to die numerous times while attempting to complete this part of quest (or spend the rest of the day killing beasties one at a time). Now, the quest scales with player level, so this is easily doable solo or with a second player or mercenary.
1. Return to [[Bertram Fiddledeegrak]] in [[Moors of Ykesha]].
1. Gather seven **flooblecrank boxes** around the [[Dropship Landing Zone]] in [[Moors of Ykesha]]. These spawn next to crates and barrels: some locations include:
   - {{waypoint 1778, 452, 764}}
   - {{waypoint 1694, 452, 796}}
   - {{waypoint 1685, 452, 801}}
   - {{waypoint 1801, 453, 977}}
   - {{waypoint 1873, 453, 940}}
1. Return to Bertram.
1. Enter [[Fiddledeegrak's Dropdown Workshop]] near Bertram at {{waypoint 1832, 452, 880}}
1. Speak with Bertram within his workshop.
1. Find Bertram's assistants within the workshop.
1. Defeat [[Clampazzo X]], the haywire clockwork at {{waypoint 122.67, -3.23, -435.67}}. (^, awards AA.)
1. Speak with one of Betram's assistants, [[Ramazi]] at {{waypoint 121.91, -3.23, -439.72}}.
1. Speak with Bertram within his workshop.
1. Witness Bertram test his invention within his workshop.
1. Speak with Bertram within his workshop.

## Rewards

- AA experience
- One of the Following:
  - Five [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog]]
  - [[Inert Fiddledeegrak Port'em Sheenery]]
