## Notes

![](images/Chaotic_elemental_filament.png)
When accepting this quest you get recipe for Sublimated Cruxstone scribed in your recipe book. This recipe requires:

- [[Chaotic Elemental Filament]] - picked up from the ground, several of them are wandering in the immediate area among the elementals. If you already have some [[Chaotic Elemental Filament]] in shared tradeskill bag (from Perks) **or** shared bank, you will not see these in Antonica and need to either use the shared ones or get them from other players.

- each combine also needs 2 of any common harvested leather pelts, 2 of any common harvested metal, 3 of any common harvested gemstone and 3 Glowing Candles.
- each combine makes 1 - 2 Sublimated Cruxstones.
- [[Chemistry Table]] to craft on is available in camp nearby at {{waypoint -2045, 2, 170}} just **bring [[Glowing Candle|Glowing Candles]]**
- Merchant selling fuels will be available in camp only after you finish this quest.

## Steps

1. Craft 4 x Sublimated Cruxstones - recipe was scribed in your book upon accepting quest, you are warned it will require filament shed by elementals.
1. Return to [[Apprentice Tavari]]. He gives you a tool [[Hyperspatial Exscindator]] to use it on a nearby rift in attempt to close it.
1. You are  knocked back and wake up next to a [[Andreas Harroway]], speak with him. He tells you about many more rifts  that are opening throughout Norrath and Luclin where your help is needed and offers you next (adventure) quest [[Rend Control Now]]. Based on your adventure level he will hint on which challenging zones to visit to find rifts.
