***After talking to [[Whittier]] you receive a book, [[Identifying Beetles]], to place in your home***

## Description

"Whittier explained to me that the Fae are adverse to killing unless it's in self-defense. They place a high value on life, but realize that sometimes compromises must be made in the name of safety. Clearing out some wood-boring beetles beneath the city will help protect the trees from disease and rot." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Examine the log located in the Treant Grove {{waypoint 447.17, 2.86, -28.94}} or near Sapling Spur Outpost at {{waypoint -161, 7, 23}}
   - Slay the 2 [[Crystalline beetle|crystalline beetles]] that spawn and attack
1. Examine the giant root located in the Treant Grove {{waypoint 398, 13, -146}}
   - Slay the 2 [[A bark beetle|bark beetles]] that spawn and attack
1. Examine the giant mushroom located in the Treant Grove {{waypoint 431, 2, -63}}
   - Slay the 2 [[Blue tunneler|blue tunnelers]] that spawn and attack
1. Return to [[Whittier]]

## Rewards

- At least 1s
- [[Whittier's Splinted Breastplate (Version 2)|Whittier's Splinted Breastplate]]
