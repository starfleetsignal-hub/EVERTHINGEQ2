## Steps

1. Kill 25 water elementals. [[A frigid turbulence|Frigid turbulences]], [[A frigid current|frigid currents]] and the [[A Tidal Flux|Tidal Flux]] in [[Icegill Lagoon]] all update. They are all found underwater beneath the location of the Icegill Goblins listed below. There are also a bunch of them at {{waypoint 523, -93, -1622}}
1. Kill 40 [[Category:Icegill Goblins (Group)|Icegill Goblins]]. {{waypoint 145, -10, -1278}}
1. Kill 20 [[A glacial construct|glacial constructs]]. {{waypoint -351, -22, 69}}

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Band of the Last Dance]]
  - [[Crest of the Last Dance]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Loop of the Last Dance]]
  - [[Ringlet of the Last Dance]] (All Scouts)
