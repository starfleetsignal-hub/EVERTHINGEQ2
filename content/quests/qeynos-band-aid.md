## Steps

1. Find the missing Heroes' Festival band member, [[Gimit Delerg]].
   - Gimit is at the bar in the [[Lion's Mane Inn and Tavern]] at {{waypoint 580, -8, 130}}.
1. Gimit challenges you to a fight outside the bar. This can be in one of several places in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]] and should be marked with a blue dot on your map. Talk to him again and he will tell you where to meet him. Known locations:
   - The far side of the T2 guild hall at {{waypoint 468, -11, 76}}
   - Behind the stables near some barrels at {{waypoint 347, -21, 199}}
   - Behind the mage tower at {{waypoint 757, -20, 96}}
   - In a corner behind a tree near the Starcrest zone at {{waypoint 703, -19, 232}}
   - Behind the last house on Karana Ct. {{waypoint 659.87, -16.96, 169.97}} - the last house is *not* player housing, rather it's the building with the pet merchant
   - Behind the pet store at {{waypoint 642, -17, 165}}
1. Fight the dwarf, who scales to your level.
1. After knocking some sense into him, return to [[Slippery Toughshield|Slippery]] next to the Claymore monument in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]].
