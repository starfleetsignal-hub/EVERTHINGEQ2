## Notes

- *Timed quest: Shattered Seas solo daily, 1 minute.*

## Steps

1. Kill 50 seagulls with the bow.
   - A stream of seagulls with appear coming inland towards you.
   - Place your cursor over the near birds and spam the "F" key to kill birds.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 67g 40s 5800 status
- 3 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- Achievement upon first completion
