## Notes

- The note to start the quest is hanging on the pillar at eye level on the first floor

## Steps

1. Find [[The Hatchery (POI)|The Hatchery]]  (4th floor {{waypoint -153, 86, 13}}). To reach the right 4th floor go back to the climbing wall at {{waypoint 40.26, 1.12, -0.49}}  and up, then  to {{waypoint -27.14, 26.85, 0.41}}   and go up 2 more levels.
   1. If you are unable to pick up the note, you have to complete [[Might of the Aviaks]] or [[Culture of the Aviaks]].
   1. You have to speak with Arejo Cloudbreach (in Barren Sky at {{waypoint -575, -45, -386}} first.
1. Collect and inspect the following
   - Collect egg shell fragments on the ground.
   - Inspect an incubator.
1. Speak with [[Arejo Cloudbreach]] on [[The Whisperwind Isle]] in [[The Barren Sky]]. {{waypoint -575, -45, -386}}

## Rewards

- At least 13g 59s 50c
- +50 faction with **The Windgazer**
