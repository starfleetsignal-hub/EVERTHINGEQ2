## Prerequisites

- You must be able to speak [[Orcish]] in order to complete this quest.

## Steps

1. Go to [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] and divide the totem by using a large axe in the barn within the [[Defiled Forest]]. {{waypoint -409, -28, -291}}
1. Speak with [[A Tallon high shaman]] in the [[Orc Encampment]]. {{waypoint 550, -15, 18}}
1. Complete the sub-quest [[Tallon Orc Faceoff]].
   - Slay [[A Tallon Champion]], who spawns to the right of the shaman.
   - Return to the Tallon high shaman.
1. Speak with [[A Vallon high shaman]] in the [[Blood Ore Valley]]. {{waypoint 325, 12, -160}}
1. Complete the sub-quest [[Green Hood Beheading]].
   - Slay 7 Green Hoods. {{waypoint 430, -22, -350}}
   - Return to the Vallon high shaman.
1. Speak with [[A Sullon high shaman]]. {{waypoint -210, -30, -90}}
1. Complete the sub-quest [[Sullon Orc Tongues]].
   - Slay 4 [[A Sullon centurion|Sullon centurions]]. {{waypoint -441, -33, -99}}, {{waypoint -351, -28, -400}}
   - Return to the Sullon high shaman.
1. Return to Tychus Zeksworn.

## Rewards

- +2500 faction with **Rallos Zek**
- Unlocks the Blessing and Miracle Pair: [[Battle Chaos]] and [[Tactical Retreat]]
