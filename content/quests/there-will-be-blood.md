## Steps

1. Find someone in need of help in [[Loping Plains]].
   - You will find [[Marcellis]] and [[Alessia]] on the bridge to the northeast of [[Village of Somborn]] around {{waypoint -555.29, 15, -79}}. [[Marcellis]] attacks after a few seconds. He's a Heroic ^^^ but I managed to kite him each time. Bring a group for best results*. Around 20% health he will despawn.
   - Once they despawn you will be timed on each of the following steps.
1. Chase [[Marcellis]] and [[Alessia]]. (Note: You can ask the guards for locations)
   - The first location is near the end of the river at {{waypoint -73, 10, -404}}. He will attack again, despawning at around 20% health.
1. Keep chasing Marcellis and Alessia.
   - The next spawn point is at the bottom of the stairs on the first platform headed towards [[Mistmoore Castle]]. {{waypoint 701, 20, -24}}
1. Escort [[Alessia]] back to [[Village of Somborn|Somborn]] and [[Aaron]]. (Note: You can fly or evac back to [[Village of Somborn|Somborn]])
   - [[Aaron]] is standing near a tree at {{waypoint -425, 10, -27}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
