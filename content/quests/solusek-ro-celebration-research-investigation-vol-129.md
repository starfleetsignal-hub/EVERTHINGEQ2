## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Solusek Ro has asked me to assist in gathering flamebound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the flamebound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Flamebound Energy Reserves|flamebound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect [[Flamebound Energy Reserves|flamebound energy]] by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Burning Prince]] at {{waypoint -772, -129, -368}} in [[Lavastorm]]
