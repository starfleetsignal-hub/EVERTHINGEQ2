## Notes

- Kaari Eesah won't respond to you if you're on [[Kaari's Field Report]]

## Steps

They are all around Shadowed Grove, at ( 340.42, -36.42, 312.26)

1. Kill 3 void beasts.
1. Kill 3 shadowed men.
1. Kill 3 corrupted Growth essences
1. Return to Kaari Eesah

- 18g 65s 46c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Archaic Spiritualist's Chain Boots]] (Shamans)
  - [[Boundless Naturewalker's Leather Boots]] (Druids)
  - [[Defiant Vagabond's Chain Shoes]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Flowing Sensei's Foot Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Gallant Dragoon's Sabatons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Resplendent Thaumaturge's Shoes]] (All Mages)
  - [[Reverent Curate's Plate Boots]] (Clerics)
