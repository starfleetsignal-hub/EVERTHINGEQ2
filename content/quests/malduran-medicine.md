Once a week, Gwynvyn checks with the healers of Maldura to see if they need assistance in restocking their healing potions. They are often running short of something, and this week is no exception.

## Steps

1. Craft five items at a chemistry table. You will be asked to make one of the following types of potion:
   - [[Maldura Antivenin Potion]]
   - [[Maldura Healing Potion]]
   - [[Maldura Regeneration Potion]]
1. Deliver the potions to healer [[Dalryl]] in the [[Metal Mercantile District]] {{waypoint -127.92, -3.19, -78.18}}.

***Note:** There's an alchemy table in the healer's area near Dalryl {{waypoint -124, -3, -91}}.*

## Rewards

- [[Coin of the Grand Artificer]]
- [[Ancient Arcannium Coin]]
- At least 2p 26g 80s
- +2,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +1,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
