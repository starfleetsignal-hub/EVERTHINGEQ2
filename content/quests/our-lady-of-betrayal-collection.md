- Most of the pages are found on the main floor of the castle.  Since you enter the zone through the basement and work your way through the zone backwards from the way you progress through the regular version of Nektropos Castle, the main floor will be the final floor before you can zone out the front door.
- The pages are randomly distributed among the available locations and they do respawn. The book belongs also to the collection. All pages are LORE but are tradeable. The book for the collection is NO-TRADE
- It seems that the pages only start to spawn after you collected the book itself. So get it first. It is very close to the point where you zone in (see above) If you cannot find the last page check coordinates ( -28, 0, 10)
