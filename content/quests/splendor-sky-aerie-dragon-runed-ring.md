## Notes

- You MUST finish the [[Ballads of Zimara Timeline]] signature quest before the quest starter will drop for you.
- it can be done in any order, fastest travel route by discovery name going buttom right to top left
  1. Step #1 {{waypoint -662.44, 116.96, 1085.05}} to discover [[Abandoned Camp]]
  1. Step #3 {{waypoint 400.73, 420.94, 888.39}} to discover [[Alchemist's Lab]]
  1. Step #2 {{waypoint 8.27, 134.02, 239.54}} to discover [[Overgrown Nest]] (on the ground)
  1. Step #5 {{waypoint 285.64, 253.49, 177.49}} to discover [[Esoteric Study]]
  1. Step #4 {{waypoint 743.64, 222.81, -177.50}} to discover [[Quiet Exavation Site]]
- unlocks the [[Band of the Five Maws (Achievement)|Band of the Five Maws]] achievement once this quest and the 5 subquests are done

## Steps

- ![Runescribed Ruby](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Runescribed_Ruby.png)Travel to {{waypoint -662.44, 116.96, 1085.05}} to discover [[Abandoned Camp]]
  - inspect a Runescribed Ruby (tiny - on the ground and a grey/blue color) next to a small crate to start [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Runescribed Ruby]]
  - *Note:This is on the small floating island.*
- ![Runescribed Emerald](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Runescribed_Emerald.png)Travel to {{waypoint 8.27, 134.02, 239.54}} to discover [[Overgrown Nest]]
  - inspect a Runescribed Emerald (EXTREMELY tiny and dark grey) inside the nest on the ground to start [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Runescribed Emerald]]
- ![Runescribed Sapphire](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Runescribed_Sapphire.png)Travel to {{waypoint 400.73, 420.94, 888.39}} to discover [[Alchemist's Lab]]
  - inspect a Runescribed Sapphire (TINY - look close!) on top of a crate to start [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Runescribed Sapphire]]
  - This is located in one of the spheres hanging from the tree, blindly following a wisp to this location is a waste of time.
- ![Runescribed Onyx](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Runescribed_Onyx.png)Travel to {{waypoint 743.64, 222.81, -177.50}} to discover [[Quiet Exavation Site]]
  - inspect a Runescribed Onyx on top of the stool to start [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Runescribed Onyx]]
- ![Runescribed Opal](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Runescribed_Opal.png)Travel to {{waypoint 285.64, 253.49, 177.49}} to discover [[Esoteric Study]]
  - inspect a Runescribed Opal on the bottom shelf of a dark bookcase (VERY hard to see) to start [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Runescribed Opal]]
  - *Note:This is located in one of the spheres hanging from the tree, blindly following a wisp to this location is a waste of time.*

## Rewards

- Experience only
