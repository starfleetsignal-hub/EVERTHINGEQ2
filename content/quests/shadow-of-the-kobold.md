## Notes

- Complete while doing the Sig line quest [[Scars of Destruction: Starting to Spiral]] while in [[Blackhook Spiral: Stonebrunt Defile (Solo)|Blackhook Spiral: Stonebrunt Defile [Solo]]]

## Steps

1. Hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 9 Blackhook painblockers
   - Gather 5 Clan Kolbok Totems
     - Possible locations include {{waypoint 455, 244, 332}}, {{waypoint 492, 247, 325}}, {{waypoint 571, 247, 223}}, {{waypoint 626, 255, 96}}, {{waypoint 542, 243, 125}}, {{waypoint 500.21, 245.45, 132.88}}
     - *Note a Kolbok Phantom may spawn when you click the totem*

## Reward

- [[Clan Kolbok Totem]] *house item*
- 50 [[Scarred Dinar]]
- 5 [[Velium Drachma]]
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
