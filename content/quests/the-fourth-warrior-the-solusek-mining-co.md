## Steps

1. Go to [[Solusek's Eye]] in [[Lavastorm]].
1. Go down to the elevator at {{waypoint -312, -40, -162}} and then down to {{waypoint -166, -225, 48}}.
1. Use the [[Focused MCE Ray]] that Juna gave you on [[A magmatic golem|magmatic golems]], [[A magmatic titan|titans]] or [[A magmatic sentry|sentries]]. This will spawn [[A superdense magmatic golem]].
   - The Focused MCE Ray can only be used on a golem if it is not in-combat. Fortunately, there are many golems in this area so if you aggro two, you can safely kill the non-converted one and still have plenty golems left to convert.
1. Upon killing a superdense magmatic golem, a harvestable [[Dozurite]] node will appear. Mine the dozurite from the node until it disappears.
1. ![A large refining machine](images/EQ2_000061.jpg)Once you have **30 dozurite** find a *large refining machine* to convert the dozurite into [[Refined dozurite]]. It takes three dozurite to make one refined dozurite. There are several machines throughout the golem area that can be used:
   - {{waypoint -5, -250, 33}}
   - {{waypoint -259, -239, -40}}
   - {{waypoint -32, -240, -6}}
1. ![Lamps to insert refined dozurite](images/EQ2_000063.jpg)Once you have the 10 refined dozurite, go to the tall lamps scattered around the dungeon and insert the dozurite into them. The lamps look like old-fashioned street lamps with a glowing yellow light. Not all lamps are clickable. Even though the lamps can be clicked in any order, to lead you smoothly into the following step you can follow the order here:
   - {{waypoint 15, -285, 90}} (sublevel 4)
   - {{waypoint -5, -250, 30}} (sublevel 4)
   - {{waypoint -25, -245, 30}} (sublevel 4)
   - {{waypoint -15, -240, 0}} (sublevel 4)
   - {{waypoint -50, -240, -150}} (sublevel 4)
   - {{waypoint -145, -230, -85}} (sublevel 4)
   - {{waypoint -225, -240, -25}} (sublevel 4)
   - {{waypoint -225, -210, -30}} (sublevel 3)
   - {{waypoint -300, -220, 20}} (sublevel 3)
   - {{waypoint -270, -40, -190}} (sublevel 1)
   - {{waypoint -215, -20, -60}} (sublevel 1)
1. ![Power lever](images/EQ2_000065.jpg)Once sufficient power has been generated, you will receive a message in your chat box indicating as much. Go to the large hole near the zone entrance and click on the levers flanking the hole: {{waypoint -104, -11, -18}} and {{waypoint -104, -11, 68}}.
1. ![Machine to repower cloak](images/EQ2_000066.jpg)Interact with the device at the western end of the circular platform.{{waypoint -69, -12, 25}}
1. Return to [[Juna Crankfizz]].
