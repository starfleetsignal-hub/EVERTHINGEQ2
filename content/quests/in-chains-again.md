## Requisite

Must be Aerakyn: part 1 of the 3-part Aerakyn racial questline (evil version)

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Myll'dreth]] across from the guild hall in [[South Freeport (POI)|South Freeport]] {{waypoint -48, -7, 231}}
1. Speak to [[Kel'drax]] on the south pier in South Freeport {{waypoint -253, -56, 201}}
1. Speak to [[Illevius]] in the plaza in [[East Freeport (POI)|East Freeport]] {{waypoint -92, -37, 13}}
1. Follow the leads (listed in suggested order but can be done in any):
   - Speak to [[Corina Farsight]] in the back room of the [[Temple of War]] {{waypoint -84, -7, -190}}. Agree to her price; she will die in the attempted scrying
   - Retrieve the book [[Age's End - A First Hand History]] from the top floor of [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] {{waypoint 4, 7, -116}} (large brown book with star on cover)
   - Enter [[Temple Street: Vault of Necromechanica]] from South Freeport {{waypoint -176, -37, 322}} and visit four locations for auto-updates:
     - Empty house {{waypoint -16, 3, 65}}
     - Street junction {{waypoint 2, 3, 113}}
     - Behind [[The Defiled Temple]] {{waypoint 43, 3, 107}}
     - Southwest plaza {{waypoint 93, -4, 115}}
1. Return to Psellic in West Freeport. He is not there but the quest auto-updates at his spot

## Rewards

- At least 10g 97c @ lvl 55
