## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable
- Zone in to Lesser Faydark from Greater Faydark...it will lessen your travel time for step 1 below.

## Steps

![Sharpthorn root bark](images/Sharpthorn_root_bark.png)![Shadow shroom](images/Shadow_shroom.png)![Faydark squash](images/Faydark_squash.png)

1. Travel to [[Lesser Faydark]] and gather
   1. Sharpthorn root bark around {{waypoint -605, -45, 149}} (hanging on one of the huge vines) near the [[Greater Faydark]] road
   1. Shadow shrooms around {{waypoint 703, -13, 489}} in [[The Shadowed Grove]]
   1. Faydark squash around {{waypoint 85, -22, -56}},  {{waypoint -16, -10, 4}}, {{waypoint -66, -11, 23}} in [[Glade of the Unicorns]]
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].
