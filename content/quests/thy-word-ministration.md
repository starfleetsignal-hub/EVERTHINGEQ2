## Steps

1. Enter Castleview Hamlet via the [[Castleview Hamlet: Cloister of Purity]] from [[Qeynos Harbor]], [[Qeynos Province District]]. {{waypoint 723, -21, -118}}
1. Speak to [[A Renda'Dal activist|Renda'Dal activists]] and [[A Renda'Dal soldier|soldiers]].
   - Some of the Renda'Dal to whom you speak will convert. Continue until you have converted 6.
   - Other Renda'Dal are zealots who will attack when you speak to them. Slay 6 (in self-defense only).
1. Return to [[Akina]].

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Celestial Watch**
