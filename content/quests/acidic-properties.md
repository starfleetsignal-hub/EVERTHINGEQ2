## Steps

1. I need to collect the acid to help free the trapped spirit. I'll first need to find a vial...
   - Collect an acid-proof container from the dreadooze
     - Loot [[A resin-covered glass vial]] from the dreadoozes (body drop).
   - Collect acid sacs from the dreadooze (4)
     - Loot 4 [[Acid-filled sac]] from the [[Dreadoozes]] (body drop).
1. I've managed to fill the vial with acid - I should return it to the statue to see if I can free the trapped spirit.
   1. Examine the vial to fill it with acid
   1. Return to the totem
      - Hail [[Deleea]] {{waypoint -1894, 240, 433}}
   1. Free the spiritual essence from the totem
      - Right-click her totem to "Free Gem".
   1. Speak with the freed spirit
      - Hail [[Deleea]] {{waypoint -1894, 240, 433}} to complete the quest
