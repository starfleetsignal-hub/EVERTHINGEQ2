## Notes

- For each step in this quest, you must complete a subquest.

## Steps

1. Complete the following subquests in any order:
   - [[A Strange Creature]] {{waypoint 1629, 277, -1913}} Examine a creature (bug) on the wall inside the hive at this location. Then return to Traigia Hothkin for your first reward.
   - [[Another Strange Creature]] {{waypoint 1537, 250, -1852}} Examine the second creature (bug) on the wall across from the hive entrance, then return to Traigia Hothkin for your next reward.
   - [[Yet Another Strange Creature]] {{waypoint 1510, 278, -1917}}
1. Return to [[Traigia Hothkin]] in [[Highton]]. {{waypoint 1277, 163, -2097}} Proceed as before, for a total of 6k faction with Teren's Grasp.
