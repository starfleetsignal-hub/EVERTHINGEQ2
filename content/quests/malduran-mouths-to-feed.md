Once a week, Gwynvyn coordinates with local crafters to arrange for a special meal for all the hard-working Maldura guards, who often have to make do with cold rations during their long shifts.

## Prerequisites

You'll need (in total):

- 5 [[Mycelial tendrils]]
- 15 [[Fruticose lichen]]
- 15 [[Abyssal angler]]
- 25 [[Thaumic Kindling]]

## Steps

1. Cook the requested food at a stove and keg. This will be one of the following:
   - [[Mushroom Tempura]]
   - [[Mycelium Moussaka]]
   - [[Trout and Tempeh Bibimbap]]
   - [[Truffle Macaroni and Mycelium]]
     - *Note: a Maldura Stove & Keg is at {{waypoint -5.82, -10.42, -10.42}}*
1. Deliver the food to [[Quartermaster Lysslyn]] {{waypoint -188.64, -3.43, -33.87}}.

## Rewards

- [[Coin of the Grand Artificer]]
- At least 2p 26g 80s
- +2,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +1,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
