## Notes

In-game reports appear to indicate that this quest is given by game guides.

## Steps

1. Talk to Halden, a Dwarf standing on barrels at The Thundering Steppes docks.
1. Gather a list of resources given to you by Halden.
1. Find Halden to turn in gathered resources.

## Rewards

- 10 [[Cheese and Ale Stew]]
