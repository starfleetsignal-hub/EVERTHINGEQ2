## Steps

![](images/A_greenfire_brazier.jpg)

1. Kill [[A larval feeder|larval feeder]]s in the water on the top level near {{waypoint 103, 1, 88}} for 3 updates
1. Slay [[A bloated muckfly|bloated muckflies]] for 3 updates
1. Find an "unusual flame": the greenfire brazier, near the mist grinnin goblins around {{waypoint 72, 6, 5}}
   - You may have to find the cooking fire, down the bottom of the waterfall from level one, to get it to spawn or refresh the instance a few times.
1. Kill [[A hellhound|hellhound]]s for 3 "wiry and flexible" heated sinews
1. For the stitching material, you need to hunt five [[A mist grinnin fisher|mist grinnin fisher]]s.
