## Notes

This quest can be done while doing [[Scars of Destruction: Enemies and Allies]] in [[Dragon Necropolis: Vaults of Veneration (Solo)|Dragon Necropolis: Vaults of Veneration [Solo]]] {{waypoint -747, 67, 283}}

## Steps

1. Hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 5 [[Exalted dragon remains]] for arcane gemstones
   - Gather 8 **Vrakorr focal crystals**![Vrakorr focal crystal](images/Vrakorr_focal_crystal.png)
     - *starting at {{waypoint 228, 60, 210}}.*
     - *Possible locations: {{waypoint 205, 49, 235}}, {{waypoint 399, 55, 234}}, {{waypoint 216, 55, 360}}, {{waypoint 365, 59, 458}},  {{waypoint 395, 59, 447}}*
     - *More crystals are located in the perimeter of Coldtalon Crypt around the final boss - Lord Ulvaxazoviak, {{waypoint 438, 125, -102}}, {{waypoint 498, 125, 75}}, {{waypoint 484, 125, -189}}, {{waypoint 739, 125, -111}}, {{waypoint 596, 125, -124}},{{waypoint 535, 125, -174}}, {{waypoint 634, 125, -28}} beside Ulvaxazoviak´s Pedestal*
1. Return to [[Dipper]] at {{waypoint 73, 9, 600}} in the [[Western Wastes]]

- [[Elaborate Drakiz Treasure Case]] house item
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- One of the following:
  - [[Coastal Pearls of Fortitude]]
  - [[Sacred Scar Chain Boots of Force]]
  - [[Sacred Scar Sandals of Force]]
  - [[Sacred Scar Boots of Force]]
  - [[Sacred Scar Sabatons of Force]]
