Brumley has sent a request via Gwynvyn to let you know he could use your assistance on a weekly basis. Speak to him directly for the details.

## Steps

1. Receive the recipe [[Progress Report Binding]] from [[Gwynvyn]].
1. Speak to [[Brumley Langboom]] in the [[Alcove of Reason]]  {{waypoint -81.41, 13.09, 195.58}}}.
1. Receive a [[Tinkered scanning gadget]] from Brumley.
1. Go to the [[Maldura: District of Ash (Heroic)|District of Ash]] {{waypoint -108.05, 1.64, -47.42}}.
   - This step takes place in the [[Maldura: District of Ash (Heroic)]]. All you need is caution, you won't need to fight.
   - The door is in the north side of the [[Sublevel Access Antechamber]] in [[Maldura]].
1. Use the [[Tinkered scanning gadget|scanning gadget]] to collect readings from two [[An immolated invader|immolated invaders]].
   - Look for [[An immolated invader|immolated invaders]] to the southwest {{waypoint 33, 31, -121}}, near the [[Forge of Brell]].
1. Return to Maldura and craft the readings into a [[Scanner Readings Progress Report]].
   - There is an engraved desk next to [[Brumley Langboom]]
   - You will need:
     - 3 Thalumbral Root
     - 3 Umbrite
     - 5 Thaumic Incense
1. Speak to [[Brumley Langboom|Brumley]].

## Rewards

- [[Coin of the Grand Artificer]]
- [[Ancient Arcannium Coin]]
- At least 2p 26g 80s
- +2,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +1,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
