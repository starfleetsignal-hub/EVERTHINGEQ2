## Prerequisites

- You must have at least -6,900 faction with Synod Reet.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Sebilis]] and steal *crates of iksar steel*. They all can be found in rooms near the entrance:
   - {{waypoint -14, 0, 10}}
   - {{waypoint 5, -2, 30}}
   - {{waypoint 27, -2, 38}}
   - {{waypoint 87, -5, 82}}
   - {{waypoint -19, -2, 50}}
   - {{waypoint -35, -2, 46}}
1. Return to [[Mender Kupput]].

## Rewards

- At least 9g 46s 1c
- +1000 faction with **Synod Reet**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Swamplord Wristwraps]]
  - [[Devoured-hide Armguards]]
  - [[Leggings of Thievery]]
  - [[Reet Warrior's Wraps]]
