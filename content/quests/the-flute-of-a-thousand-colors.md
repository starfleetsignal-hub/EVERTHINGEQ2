## Steps

1. Equip [[The Flute of a Thousand Colors (Item)|The Flute of a Thousand Colors]] and use it from your charm slot on the southern beach {{waypoint 1723, 4, 648}} of the [[Isle of Mok]] in [[Timorous Deep]] to make the Windhowler Reed appear nearby.
1. Harvest the Windhowler Reed to obtain a [[Silver Threaded Windhowler Reed]].
1. Use the flute from your charm slot to place the reed in the flute.
1. Use the flute from your charm slot to receive the [[Second Magic Lyric]].
1. Use the flute from your charm slot just north of [[Ganak's Battlement]] {{waypoint -378, -58, -357}} in the [[Stonehoof Badlands]] area of [[Jarsath Wastes]] to spawn [[Zaz the Wailer]] 80 Heroic ^^ just north of you. Kill him to receive the [[Bone reed]]. (Single Ornate Chest Drop, Also easily solo kited)
1. Use the flute from your charm slot to place the reed in the flute.
1. Use the flute from your charm slot to receive the [[Third Magic Lyric]].
1. Use the flute from your charm slot in the drachnid cave {{waypoint -2251, 232, 927}} between [[The Temple of the Red Lord]] and [[Temple of the White Lady]] in the [[Skyfire Mountains]] area of [[Jarsath Wastes]] to make the Resonating Crystals appear nearby.
1. Harvest the Resonating Crystals to obtain a [[Crystal flute plate]].
1. Use the flute from your charm slot to attach the plate.
1. Use the flute from your charm slot to receive the [[Fourth Magic Lyric]].
1. Kill [[Armsmaster Keelo]] (or his placeholder [[A drolvarg trainer]]) {{waypoint 20, -56, 183}} just to the south of the Rhinos in [[Karnor's Castle]] to receive the [[Deklium plating]]. (Uncommon Auto-Update) These mobs are 73^^^, so don't be a hero. Solo-able at lvl 86 kited. Pull him to first room. At 50% he will call his friends, run to bridge and friends will lose agro.
1. Use the flute from your charm slot to attach the plate.
1. Use the flute from your charm slot to discover that the restoration is complete.
1. Speak to [[Freesia Ticklewing]] {{waypoint 161, 108, 171}} in [[Kelethin]].
1. Speak to [[Smendrik]] {{waypoint 2604, 86, 1296}} in the city of [[Gorowyn]] in [[Timorous Deep]].
