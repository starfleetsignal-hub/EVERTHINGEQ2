## Steps

1. Create 5 sheafs of the [[Customized Arrows]].
   - Scribe the [[Ninoin's Custom Arrow Specifications]] recipe
   - In addition to the Feathers and Arrowheads supplied you will need:
     - 5 [[Severed ash]]
     - 15 [[Sparkling sandpaper]] (level 20 - 29)
       - You can buy fuel from [[Trader Whitlock]] on the highland camp. {{waypoint 335, 189, 491}}
   - *There is a woodworking table at Highland Outposts.  {{waypoint 328.05, 188.61, 508.61}}*
1. I must give the arrows to Cordelia, who will meet me at the [[Highland Outposts]] {{waypoint 346.14, 189.71, 493.75}}.
