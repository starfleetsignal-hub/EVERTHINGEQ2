## Steps

1. Once you are ready, inform [[Field Marshal Vishra|Vishra]] as such and he will zone you into [[Scarstone Commander's Tent: Small Council]].
1. Speak with either [[Overlord Lucan D'Lere|Lucan]] (evil aligned players) or [[Queen Antonia Bayle (Scarstone Commander's Tent: Small Council)|Antonia]] (good aligned players).
1. Follow the dialogue between Antonia, Lucan, and Vishra.
1. Exit the command tent (click on the tent flap at {{waypoint 4365, -821, 402}} ).
1. Speak to [[Jorlak]] at {{waypoint 4297, -821, 397}} just outside of [[Scarstone]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
