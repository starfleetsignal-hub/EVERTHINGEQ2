## Steps

1. Speak with Bilke. {{waypoint 41.27, 200.11, -576.29}}
   - Hunt 9 shardback gnawers for saliva.
   - Apply saliva to 13 mining equipment and steamwork golems. Around Val'Zyroth Cave {{waypoint 138.37, 109.46, -774.82}}
1. Return to Bilke.
   - Collect 2 Teir'Dal glyph stone Xil - body drop {{waypoint 586.99, 133.52, -671.23}}
   - Collect 2 Teir'Dal glyph stone Vexx - body drop {{waypoint 586.99, 133.52, -671.23}}
   - Collect 2 Teir'Dal glyph stone Ryn - body drop {{waypoint 415.93, 102.35, -327.25}}
   - Collect 2 Teir'Dal glyph stone Xos - green shiny {{waypoint 404.20, 102.34, -332.27}}
   - Collect 2 Teir'Dal glyph stone Thox - green shiny {{waypoint 476.69, 103.26, -316.87}}
   - Collect 2 Teir'Dal glyph stone Ghen - green shiny {{waypoint 590.30, 136.17, -706.26}}
   - Collect 2 Teir'Dal glyph stone Lek - green shiny {{waypoint 535.64, 160.20, -811.35}}
1. Place Teir'Dal glyph stones in the palm of Hate's Hand {{waypoint 609.29, 136.18, -707.83}}
1. Return to [[Jackal]] {{waypoint 40.40, 200.14, -575.50}}
1. Enter Neriondalon via secret waterfall tunnel
   - go up tree branch {{waypoint 211.03, 126.02, -745.31}}
   - door at {{waypoint 327.81, 159.53, -783.10}}
1. Find [[Nillirn Strikepipe]] at the Gutters {{waypoint 857.82, 158.97, -1075.09}}
