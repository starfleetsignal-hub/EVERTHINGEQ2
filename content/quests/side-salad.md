## Materials

- 4 [[Luminous Kindling]]

## Steps

![Feerrott Greens near the Greenblood River](images/Feerott_Greens.png)

1. Harvest 5 [[Browncap mushroom|browncap mushrooms]] in [[The Feerrott]] from overgrown bed of greenslade (from any normal harvestable bush).
1. Purchase [[Vinegar]] from [[Merchant Murgina]] at the camp at the Wizard Spire {{waypoint -142, 14, 184}}
1. Gather 4 [[Feerrott Greens]] near the [[The Greenblood River|Greenblood River]] {{waypoint -215.86, 7.51, 270.86}}
1. Create [[Feerrott Greens Salad]] using the [[Portable Cooking Set]]
1. Speak to [[Jadrek Woolfe]] back in [[Everfrost]] {{waypoint 222.13, -19.23, 49.59}}.

## Rewards

At least 5g 11s

- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
