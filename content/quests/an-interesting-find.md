## Steps

1. Find 3 scholars in [[The City of Paineel]]:
   - [[Necromantic Scholar Kirarn]] at {{waypoint 1656, -267, 3197}} in the [[Hall of Necromancy]].
   - [[Strategic Scholar Jup'kib]] at {{waypoint 1920, -298, 3412}} in the [[Hall of the Fell Blade]].
   - [[Archanic Scholar Lii'dial]] at {{waypoint 1987, -298, 3379}} in the [[Hall of Wizardry]].
1. Return to [[Inayat Wijdani]].
1. Speak to [[Captain Ghalib Elbaz]] to complete the quest.
1. If you are out of quests here, then you have 2 options:  a) Go back to the encampment and head north.  There's an area with 4 more quest, one of which is to Kerra Isle.  b) Complete the repeatable "kill X monsters" quests on the east side of Paineel.(You must be lv. 80 to speak to Borailus)

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +1000 faction with **Fell Blade**
- +1000 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**
