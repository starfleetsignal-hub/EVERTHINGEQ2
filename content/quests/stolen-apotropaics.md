## Description

The League of Freethinkers has come to learn about the theft of a number of beneficial wards often used to protect the weak from malignant creatures such as the Libant. Furthermore, the Freethinkers are concerned that the Libant may be looking for a way to pervert the protective magic of these items and make them harmful. I've been asked to enter Mistmyr Manor and retrieve a number of these items, known as Apotropaic Cubes.
