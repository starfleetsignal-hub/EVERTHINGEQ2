## Notes

- This quest in not repeatable.

## Steps

1. Go to the [[Thundering Steppes]] and listen to an argument between a rowdy dwarf and a rowdy human {{waypoint -581, -15, -485}}. Offer to let one of them hit you. Once the dialogue completes, the quest will update.
   - You may have to leave the docks and return to the pair if the conversation options do not appear.
1. Return to [[Priest Aaronolis Swornlove]]

## Rewards

- [[Romance in a Jar]]
- 4 [[Erollisi coin]]s
