## Steps

![thawthorne bush](images/Daring_Rescue_%28thawthorn%29.png)

1. Gather 8 thawthorn plants.  They grow on the cliffs surrounding Howlers Cove - you'll need your flying mount to look for them. They look like glowing golden bushes and are spotted very easy at night.  For example, there's one at {{waypoint 4642, -848, 538}}.
1. Return to Healer Salinca Cheldr
1. Teleport 8 defenders to safety - they look like bodies laying on the rocks, and are untrackable.  They are on the cliffs south of Scarstone {{waypoint 4642, -848, 538}}
1. Return to Healer Salinca Cheldr
