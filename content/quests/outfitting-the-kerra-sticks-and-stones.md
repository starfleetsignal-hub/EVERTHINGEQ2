## Steps

1. Collect the following (all will be engulfed in yellow or green particle effects):
   1. 5 [[Fallen feather|fallen feathers]] from the [[A feathered vagabond|feathered vagabonds]]. **NOTE:** collect the feathers from the ground - DON'T bother killing the vagabonds!![fallen feather](images/Fallen_feather.jpg)
      - Found near the entrance to the village around the dead cockatrices. {{waypoint 1129, 13, 2322}} (If you spam harvest you can get 2 updates from 1 note)
   1. 5 discarded pouncer [[Claw sheath|claw sheaths]] from around the [[A darkpaw pouncer|darkpaw pouncers]].![discarded claw sheath](images/Discarded_claw_sheath.jpg)
      - Also found near the entrance to the village around the dead cats. {{waypoint 1083, 11, 2204}} (If you spam harvest you can get 2 updates from 1 note)
   1. 5 [[Sturdy reed|sturdy reeds]] from around the waterfall in the center of [[Kerra Isle]].![sturdy reed (with green sparkles)](images/Sturdy_reed.jpg)
      - Check the waterfall in [[Martah's Loft]] where the Kerran Queen resides. {{waypoint 1212, 40, 2247}} (If you spam harvest you can get 2 updates from 1 note)
1. Return to [[Chinook]]

## Rewards

- At least 20g 5s 35c
- +1,250 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +500 faction with **Kerra Isle Outfitters**
