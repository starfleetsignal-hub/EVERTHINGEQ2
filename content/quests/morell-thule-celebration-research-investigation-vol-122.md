## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Morell-Thule has asked me to assist in gathering dreambound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the dreambound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Dreambound Energy Reserves|dreambound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect [[Dreambound Energy Reserves|dreambound energy]] by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Lord of Dreams]] at {{waypoint 1097, -1, 646}} in [[The Thundering Steppes]]
