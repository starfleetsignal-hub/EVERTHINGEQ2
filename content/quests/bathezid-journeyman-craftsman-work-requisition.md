## Steps

1. Collect the requisition list from the Work Requisitions clipboard (next to Supply Sergeant Anuhadux)
1. Scribe the [[Bathezid journeyman craftsman recipes]] received upon accepting the quest.
1. Create 6 of one of the following (randomly determined after clicking the clipboard):
   - [[Celebratory victory wine]] (Stove and Keg - smoldering kindling)
     - 4 Raw Cranberry
     - 2 Torsis Tea Leaf
     - 10 Smoldering Kindling
   - [[Collapsible tent]] (Loom - smoldering filament)
     - 4 Lichenclover Root
     - 2 Bristled Pelt
     - 5 Smoldering Filament
   - [[Deadly redwood ballista]] (Woodworking Table - smoldering sandpaper)
     - 4 Redwood Lumber
     - 3 Bristled Pelt
     - 5 Smoldering Sandpaper
   - [[Salted dried chokidai strips]] (Stove and Keg - smoldering kindling)
     - 4 Barracuda
     - 2 Mineral Salt Loam
     - 10 Smoldering Kindling
   - [[Standard issue bunk bed]] (Woodworking Table - smoldering sandpaper)
     - 4 Redwood Lumber
     - 2 Bristled Pelt
     - 5 Smoldering Sandpaper
   - [[Sturdy redwood staff]] (Woodworking Table - smoldering sandpaper)
     - 4 Redwood Lumber
     - 2 Raw Kunzite
     - 5 Smoldering Sandpaper
   - [[Wand of smiting]] (Forge - smoldering coal)
     - 4 Ferrite Cluster
     - 2 Raw Kunzite
     - 5 Smoldering Coal
1. Return to Anuhadux.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill Experience
- At least 38g 11s 18c
- +750 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
