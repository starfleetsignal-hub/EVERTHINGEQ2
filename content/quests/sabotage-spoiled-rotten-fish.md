## Steps

1. Within 20 minutes, find a bucket of pig dung to contaminate buckets of fish.  Can be found most quickly in the stables.
1. Within the next 20 minutes, spoil 5 buckets of fish found along the docks and near merchants in South and East Freeport.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +2000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- **Note**: This quest does not grant any experience or achievement experience.
