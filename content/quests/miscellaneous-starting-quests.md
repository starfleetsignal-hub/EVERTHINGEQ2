Three quests are available at any level and, as they can be started in multiple areas, do not fit nicely into other Timelines.

- [[The Great Challenge!]] - automatically given to new characters; the challenge is to reach level 20 in 14 days
- [[First Time Buyer]] - available from the innkeepers in the Qeynos (and formerly Freeport) villages
- [[The Mysterious Egg]] - /claim the Mysterious Egg, given to everyone who has the Kingdom of Sky expansion
