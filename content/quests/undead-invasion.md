### Note

- This quest is noted as 103lv but you can hail to [[Onerind Fe'Dhar]] when you are 92lv. Those who under 95lv can't be shared this quest

## Steps

1. Enter [[High Keep: The Bloodless Incursion (Contested)|High Keep: The Bloodless Incursion]].
1. Kill 50 undead from the upper floors
1. Kill 50 liches within the mines
1. Kill an elite undead (named) from the upper floor
1. Kill an elite undead (named) from the lower floor

## Rewards

At least 1p 55g

- 7 [[Draconic Etyma]]
- [[High Keep Mission Crate]]
- [[Sparkling Crate]]
