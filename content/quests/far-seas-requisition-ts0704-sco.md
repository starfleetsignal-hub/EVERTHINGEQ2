## Steps

- Find the location of [[Crater Mine]]. {{waypoint 515, -8, 1030}}
- Grab a stein of ale from the Centaurs in the [[Northern Bandit Camp]]. {{waypoint 1226, 0, 594}}
- Collect 8 spears from the Steelhoof Centaurs (common update from lvl 26+ centaurs around [[Centaur Encampment]] near [[Coldwind Cove]])
- Speak with [[Taskmaster Deldrin]] on the docks in [[The Thundering Steppes|Thundering Steppes]].
