## Steps

1. Speak with [[Jama Kharhir]] of The Jharin {{waypoint -104, 31, -378}}
1. Place healing water totems:
   - Place totem at the water's edge near [[Ward Singer Span]] {{waypoint 90, 12, 183}}.
   - Place totem near the southwesternmost spot in the lagoon {{waypoint 230.2, 15.63, 283.40}}.
   - Place totem at the water's edge near the [[Sahteb Mahlni Spire]] {{waypoint 331, 13, 85}}.
   - Place totem north of [[Fisher's Tail Isle]] {{waypoint 204.31, 14.20, -256.60}}.
   - Place totem on the rocks of [[Grawleh Beach]] {{waypoint 31, 9, -210}}.
1. Return to [[Khazla Aradir]].

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
