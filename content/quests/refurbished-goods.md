- This is a subquest of [[The Stormhammer (Quest)|The Stormhammer]].

## Steps

1. Kill the following mobs in [[Kaladim]] for common updates:
   - 20 Clan Grungetalon Goblins.
   - 20 Clan Kragbak Kobolds.
1. Return to [[The Haunted Vaults]] and speak with [[General Canloe Nusbak]].

- Progress in [[The Stormhammer (Quest)|The Stormhammer]]
