## Steps

1. Harvest critter dens to find clues of the Sage's Fate. These are a Tier 2 Harvesting Node and can be found throughout [[Antonica]].
1. Kill these animals in hopes of finding a page *- all found between {{waypoint -1505, -8, 490}} and {{waypoint -1704, -5, 208}}*:
   - Kill 12 [[A feral timber wolf|feral timber wolves]] {{waypoint -1739, -17, 460}} for a page.
   - Kill 12 [[A young kodiak (Antonica)|young kodiaks]] {{waypoint -1739, -17, 460}} for a page.
   - Kill 12 [[A Sabertooth battler|Sabertooth battlers]] {{waypoint -1390, -10, -334}} or {{waypoint -1444, -13, 359}}
1. Kill a [[A defiled squire (Antonica)|defiled squire]] {{waypoint -2248, -44, 304}} for a page. These are found near the entrance to [[Stormhold]] and it is a rare update. You may need to kill all of the defiled squires in the area several times. The respawn is approximately 5-10 minutes.
1. Return to Scholar Demini for your reward.
