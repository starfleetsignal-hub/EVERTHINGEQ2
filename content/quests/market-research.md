## Steps

1. Return to the city of Paineel.
1. Talk to Aaliya in the Hall of Wizards. {{waypoint 1993, -300, 3357}}
1. Talk to Safa in the Hall of Fell Blades. {{waypoint 1886, -300, 3425}}
1. Talk to Adeela in Craftkeepers in Mercantile Court up the ramp. {{waypoint 1727, -283, 3469}}
1. Talk to Sumit in the Hall of Necromancy. {{waypoint 1647, -267, 3216}}
1. Return to Felice for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
