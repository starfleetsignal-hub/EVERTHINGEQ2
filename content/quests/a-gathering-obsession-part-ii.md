## Reminder

- If you are running this quest as a level 20+ tradeskiller, don't forget to pick up a [[Return to Mara]] from [[Koros Splinterlimb]] in the tavern opposite the broker to instantly transport back to the Isle of Mara.

## Steps

1. Go harvest 10 of each (buying does not work) in any tier 2 zone (Antonica, The Commonlands, Greater Faydark *(Tunare's Glade, Combine Spires, Crushbone area, Upper Emerald River area)*, the western half of Darklight Wood and Frostfang Sea).
   - [[Severed maple]]
   - [[Murdunk orange]]
   - [[Antonican coffee bean]]
   - [[Tanned leather pelt]]
   - [[Tuber strand]]
   - [[Raw carrot]]
   - [[Raw black tea leaf]]
   - [[Raw crab meat]]
   - [[Freewater grouper]]
1. Return to Qho Augren.

## Rewards

- At least 33g 11s 22c
- Tradeskill experience
