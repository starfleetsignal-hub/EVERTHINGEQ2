## Steps

1. Kill 2 [[A coldain tracker|coldain tracker]]s to obtain their [[A Mystical Tether|Mystical Tether]]s. These are uncommon updates that update for the entire group
1. Click on 2 wolf posts at {{waypoint 727, -375, 868}}
1. Return to Adrad

- At least 1p 25g
- +800 faction with **The Order of Rime**
