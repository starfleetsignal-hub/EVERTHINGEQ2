## Note

If you are in a group, you cannot share this quest and get [[Rongot's Whisper Stone]]; each person must individually hail him to get this item.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Crystalline Breaks]] at {{waypoint -758, -327, 2619}} by click/select on Crystal Cavern door. ![Read the note found on the corpse](images/Crystal_Caver_quest_-_note.jpg)
1. Find a band of Ry'Gorr.  Updates at {{waypoint 58, -23, 86}}
1. Inspect a Ry'Gorr Corpse at {{waypoint 51, -14, 158}} or {{waypoint 70, -15, 147}} and read the note he carries.
1. Use [[Rongot's Whisper Stone]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +4,550 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
