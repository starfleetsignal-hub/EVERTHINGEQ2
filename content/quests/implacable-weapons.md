## Steps

1. Complete the following in any order:
   - Defeat [[Nirag the Boundless]] in [[Takish Badlands: The Boundless Gulf (Raid)]] for a guaranteed drop.
   - Defeat [[Sanum Ordast]] in [[Buried Takish'Hiz: Emergence from Stone (Raid)]] for a guaranteed drop.
   - Purchase the [[Tunarian Blessed Basin]] from Khepri for 425 [[Mira Miracula]].
   - Purchase the [[Vial of Cleansing Life]] from Khepri for 500 [[Shard of Rejuvenation]]. These shards are found on H3 Instanced Bosses, and each boss can drop between 1-10 shards.
1. Right click and use the [[Tunarian Blessed Basin]] on the [[Vial of Cleansing Life]], and transform it into the [[Cleansed Tunarian Blessed Basin]].
1. Use the Boundless Ember drop from Nirag  to purify the Shackle from Sanum to produce the Fury of Regrowth.
1. At this point your quest should update to say With the Fury of the Regrowth in my possession I must defeat Amatheon.
1. Kill [[Amatheon]] for 'Amatheon's Purpose' (MUST have done the other steps FIRST, or it will not drop).  Also be very close to Amatheon when he dies or you will not get credit for the kill.  I was the person jousting the curse and did one last joust before he died and well no update.
1. Combine Ametheons Purpose with the Fury of Growth and receive the Echo of Apotheosis
1. Use the 'Echo of Apotheosis' on your Enraptured Weapon.
