## Steps

1. Now that [[Rungupp]] is slain, find out if any reward is offered.
1. Return to the [[Paineel Commons]] and as you approach the commons, look to the right. You will see a wanted poster that looks like a crafting clipboard. {{waypoint 1804.58, -298.99, 3445.45}} ![Wanted poster on the wall to the right of the entrance to Paineel Commons](images/An_Old_Troll's_Head_quest_-_wanted_poster.jpg)
   - Read the text of the wanted poster which will complete the quest.
