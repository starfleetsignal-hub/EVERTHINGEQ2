## Steps

1. Plant 6 Growth-blessed saplings in the following locations (marked with blue dots on the in-game map)
   - {{waypoint 4039, -849, 359}}
   - {{waypoint 3866, -862, 346}}
   - {{waypoint 3729, -862, 405}}
   - {{waypoint 3506, -867, 374}}
   - {{waypoint 4028, -836, 100}}
   - {{waypoint 4123, -842, -89}} - this one may be guarded by [[Mangla the Repeller]]
     - **Note**: Plant locations "respawn" after about 30 minutes real time. If Mangla won't let you near the last planting spot, just wait for one of the others to respawn.
1. Defeat 6 [[A forest retaliator|forest retaliators]], then tell the forest spirits to inhabit the saplings.
1. Return to [[Tindi Wildbranch]]
