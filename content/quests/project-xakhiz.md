## Steps

1. Go to the beach south of the [[Waning Forest]] {{waypoint -1585, 26, -30}} and complete the following:
   - Kill 4 [[A Gra'al Shul coercer|Gra'al coercers]].
   - Use the net to capture [[A coerced mudfin]].
1. Return to [[Hroar Kalacious]] in [[Kunzar's Edge (POI)|Kunzar's Edge]]. {{waypoint -1374, 36, 515}}

- At least 14g 81s 38c
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
