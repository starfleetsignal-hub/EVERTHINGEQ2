## Notes

- Be aware that *every* member of your group needs to have this mirror quest done in order to be able to progress further inside this instance.
  - If someone in your group is unsure; have them check their quest journal, or right click the mirror and see what option shows up.  If they can select "Enter the Mirror" they can enter to the next floor.  If they can select "Gaze into the Mirror" they need to get and/or finish the mirror's quest.
- Once you have entered the mirror you cannot return to previous floor. Clicking on the mirror from the next floor will zone you out to [[The Shimmering Citadel]].
- **Kael Drakkel:** Make sure you take the quest before killing [[Aysor the Mighty]], or you will not be able to update the quest and gain access to the third floor until you can reset the instance.
- **Live:** On Live you can do the statue event before getting the quest. If you already killed Aysor after clicking the mirror, run back and [[Aysor's eye]] will be waiting.

## Steps

  - [Ninth statue for first cyclops event may be bugged and disappear when placed on the floor](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/shimmering-citadel-poets-palace-the-second-mirror.614664/)**

In the pedestal room, there are three types of pedestals lining the perimeter of the room (ignore the ones in the middle): brown, turquoise, and blue. Each type has a number and is placed either in clockwise or counter-clockwise fashion in respect to the other pedestals of the same type. There are nine brown pedestals, five turquoise pedestals, and four blue pedestals.

1. First cyclops statue event:
   1. Take all nine statues off the table in the first room {{waypoint 36, 184, -49}} and move them into the pedestal room. {{waypoint -36, 186, -4}}
   1. Inspect a statue and it will have a number on it. Place that statue on a brown pedestal of the same number. Repeat this step for all nine statues.
      - You know that a statue was placed on the correct pedestal if the statue does a /cheer emote and you get a message saying "the statue clicks into place".
      - 1 rune goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -2, 186, -92}}
      - 2 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -2, 186, -55}}
      - 3 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -4, 186, -25}}
      - 4 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -30, 186, -14}}
      - 5 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -57, 186, -12}}
      - 6 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -89, 186, -16}}
      - 7 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -100, 186, -49}}
      - 8 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -83, 186, -83}}
      - 9 Runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -57, 186, -107}}
   1. When the last statue is placed correctly, nine linked [[A cyclops statue|cyclops statues]] will spawn on the pedestals in the center of the room. Kill them all and new statues will respawn in the first room.
1. Second cyclops statue event:
   1. Take all five statues off the table in the first room and move them into the pedestal room.
   1. Repeat the same logic from the previous step, however instead of brown pedestals, use the turquoise pedestals.
      - 1 rune goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -12, 186, -107}}
      - 2 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -3, 186, -41}}
      - 3 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -43, 186, -15}}
      - 4 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -100, 186, -29}}
      - 5 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -76, 186, -98}}
   1. When the last statue is placed correctly, eight linked [[A cyclops statue|cyclops statues]] will spawn on the pedestals in the center of the room. Kill them all and new statues will respawn in the first room.
1. Third cyclops statue event:
   1. Take all four statues off the table in the first room and move them into the pedestal room.
   1. Repeat the same logic from the previous steps, however use the blue pedestals.
      - 1 rune goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -3, 186, -75}}
      - 2 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -16, 186, -15}}
      - 3 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -73, 186, -15}}
      - 4 runes goes on the pedestal at {{waypoint -100, 186, -68}}
   1. When the last statue is placed correctly, [[Aysor the Mighty]] will spawn. Defeat him to spawn [[Aysor's eye]].
1. Kill [[Aysor's eye]] and the quest will auto-complete.

## Rewards

- Access through the second mirror.
