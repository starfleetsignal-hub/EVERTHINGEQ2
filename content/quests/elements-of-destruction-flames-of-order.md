## Notes

- ***You may not receive all of the [[Thermite briquette|thermite briquettes]] needed for this quest on one run through the zone.***
- The quest is listed as heroic but can be soloed
- You receive an [[Inert Doomfire Purifying Rune]] when you click on the corrupted  fire to start the quest.

## Steps

![](images/Thermite_briquette.png)

1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 260, -20, -285}} to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]]. Use the fire portal at {{waypoint 728, 412, -336}} to enter [[Doomfire: Elements of Rage (Solo)|Doomfire: Elements of Rage [Solo]]].
1. Progress through the zone. (See [[Doomfire: Elements of Rage (Solo)|Doomfire: Elements of Rage [Solo]]] for strategies).
1. Kill wilders, fire wasps, nilborien, or embernauts to obtain 19 [[Thermite briquette|thermite briquettes]].
   - These are common drops. One is dropped for each party member.
1. Use the [[Inert Doomfire Purifying Rune]] from the quest helper to recieve the [[Imbued Doomfire Purifying Rune]] and open the **Portal to Corilac Tower** at {{waypoint 371, 112, 463}}
1. After killing [[Firemonger Rosnarga]], use the **Portal to Corilac Tower** at {{waypoint 371, 112, 463}}.
1. Click on **The Corilac Embergem**![The Corilac Embergem](images/TheCorilacEmbergem.jpg) at {{waypoint -170, 20, -418}}.

## Rewards

- [[Corilac Champion Accolade]]
- [[Seal of the Fire Vicar]]
- At least 1p 41g 75s 48000 status
