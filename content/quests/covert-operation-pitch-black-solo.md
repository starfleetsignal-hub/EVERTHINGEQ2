This mission is a delicate, and must be handled with great care and swiftness if I am to succeed. For the safety of those who inhabit the dark side of Luclin, I must not fail!

## Steps

- Slice through the mushroom in [[Echo Caverns: Fungal Foray (Solo)]].
- Hunt the hunter in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket: Loda Kai Isle (Solo)]].
- Take back the throne in [[Shar Vahl: Siege Break (Solo)]].
