## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill 4 [[A clockwork shredder|clockwork shredder]]s (found in [[The Scrapyard]])
1. Kill 4 [[A mechnamagica battle drone|mechnamagica battle drone]]s (found in the [[The Ore Extraction Site]])
1. Kill 4 [[A mechnamagica assault drone|mechnamagica assault drone]] (found in [[The Clockwork Hydroelectric Dam]])
1. Kill 4 [[A klakdrone watcher|klakdrone watcher]]s (found in [[Klak'Anon Central]])
1. Return to [[Agent Sidleworth]].

## Rewards

- At least 15g 15s 34c
- Complete 5 times to receive [[Deactivated Clockwork Arachnid]]
