## Note

- Ratonga automatically learn the language CONFIRMED (Select the option that tells the NPC that the language sounds similar to Ratonga) after speaking to Roosey, but can still receive the quest.
- This language is required to complete the Signature Quest [[The Stormhammer (Quest)|The Stormhammer]].
- This language is required to complete the Heritage Quest [[The Staff of the Observers]].
- This language is needed later for quests in [[Fens of Nathsar Timeline#Bellywhumper Faction|Fens of  Nathsar]].
- This language is needed later for the Heritage Quest [[Ritsy's Lucky String (quest)|Ritsy's Lucky String]].
- You may NOT get the quest until you are level 28.
- Even though the /waypoint will tell you the actual point is off the map, be assured that your quest giver is at the Gnomeland Security Headquarters where most of the other buildings are - this one is the one in the very back of the area.
- You will need to examine the items to update the quest.

## Steps

1. Find and examine 7 [[Deepearth runes]]. These are fairly rare no-trade corpse drops from all Kobolds and Bugbears. **NOTE:** These no longer drop unless you have the quest.
   - Kobolds can be found in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] and [[Steamfont Mountains]]. (kobolds are also in Kaladim)
   - Bugbears can be found in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] and [[Kaladim]].
   - They drop fairly quickly from the Cliff Defenders ring event in [[Kaladim]].
1. Return to [[Lingualogian Roosey]] to learn the [[Serilian]] language.
