## pre req

This quest is not available until:

- Completion of [[Lujien, not Lycan]].
- Must have attained Level 95

![Destroy the shrine](images/Shrine.jpg)

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Bloodmoon (NPC)|Bloodmoon]] at {{waypoint -96, 50, 96}}.
1. Defeat him and you'll receive [[The Horn of Ancient Howls]].
1. Return and speak to [[Duskrender]].
1. Speak to [[Hildan]] at {{waypoint 230, 86, 51}}.
1. Get a leaf from an obol dreadleaf (uncommon auto-update).
1. Return to [[Hildan]].
1. Click the flames on the table by Hildan to receive [[A Vial of Ethereal Fire]].
1. Go to the monument west of the camp at {{waypoint 806, 82, 50}}.
1. Throw the vial onto the shrine by clicking the statue. Click it again to destroy the horn.
1. Kill the worshippers that attack.
1. Return to [[Duskrender]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Essence Drop of Cunning]] - scouts
  - [[Essence Drop of Judgement]] - priest
  - [[Essence Drop of Sapience]] - mage
  - [[Essence Drop of Brawn]] - tank
