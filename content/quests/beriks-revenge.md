## Description

"A sword I found seems to have some level of intelligence. It claims to be Berik, a former Knight of Thunder who devoted himself to the eradication of undead. It certainly seems unhappy with its current plight."  *--Everquest II Journal*

## Notes

- **Important note**: Upon examination of the sword a quest window will open; **DO NOT ACCEPT THE QUEST UNTIL YOU ARE FINISHED WITH THE DIALOGUE**. If you do accept the quest first, then delete the quest from your log and examine the Sword of Thunder again. At the end of the dialog a new item will be put in your inventory called "[[Berik, Sword of Thunder (Quest Starter)|Berik, Sword of Thunder]]", Examine this Sword and accept quest. This is the sword you will need to inspect after each further step.
- At the completion of both this quest and [[The Bone Bladed Claymore]], the player can join the two rewarded blades to form [[Electrified Bone Bladed Claymore]].

## Steps

1. Berik, Sword of Thunder will tell you to kill 100 undead knights in Stormhold. Any undead count: All skeletons and zombies work (Knight, sentry, squire, defender, burnt, etc.) Once this step is done talk to the sword again.
1. Next it will ask you to kill 3 named bosses in [[Stormhold]]:
   - [[Defiled Lord Byron]], he who gave the order to forge him. {{waypoint -45, 9, -60}}
   - [[Blacksmith Brigg]], he who forged him. {{waypoint -138, -1, -46}}
   - [[Battlepriest Alexandria]], she who blessed him. {{waypoint -49, -24, -74}}
1. Inspect the sword one last time and you are done!
   - Both [[Sword of Thunder]], and [[Berik, Sword of Thunder (Quest Starter)|Berik, Sword of Thunder]] will be taken from your inventory.

## Rewards

- [[Berik, Sword of Thunder (Level 20) (no-trade)|Berik, Sword of Thunder]]
  - This has a *runesword* look to it, and is legendary, so it can be reforged to decorate it. It is no-trade, however.
