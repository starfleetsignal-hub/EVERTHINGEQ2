## Prerequisites

- Your faction with [[The City of Kelethin (Faction)|City of Kelethin]] must be Indifferent or better to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Village of Panderpana]] {{waypoint -502, -56, -246}}
1. Kill [[A brownie preserver|brownie preservers]] until an update is given for [[A brownie prayer tablet]].
1. Return to [[Duchess Maareanna]]

## Rewards

- At least 9g 41s 26c
- +100 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
