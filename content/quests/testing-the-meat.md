## Steps

You receive [[Ruby Emperor Meat]] at the beginning of this quest.

1. Feed the [[Ruby Emperor Meat]] to [[An animated coral]] - target the coral and use the meat
1. Feed the [[Ruby Emperor Meat]] to [[A saber-toothed mountain dog]]
1. Feed the [[Ruby Emperor Meat]] to [[An emerald weaver]]; the weaver will attack
1. Speak to [[Researcher Aillena Belzia]] back on The Isle of Refuge in [[Sapswill's Rest]] {{waypoint 1256, 9, 1191}}
1. Gather 5 herbs on the graveyard/treehouse island.  They have red flowers on them and are fairly easy to spot ![Quest Testing the Meat, the herbs Aillena wants](images/TestingTheMeat_HerbsForAillena.png)
1. Return to Aillena
1. Talk to [[Overseer Clackwhinge]] {{waypoint 1247, 8, 1127}}
1. Kill 10 grimlings
1. Return to Clackwhinge
1. Return to Ruowwrya

## Rewards

- At least 1p 29g 60s
- One of the following:
  - [[Oar of the Lost]]
  - [[The Great Scimitar of Karrabukk]]
  - [[The Sextant of Fates]]
  - [[Threadbreaker]]
