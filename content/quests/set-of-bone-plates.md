## Prerequisites

- You must have at least -4600 faction (-10,000 with illusion works) with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
  - While this quest can technically be completed an infinite amount of times, it will stop being offered once your faction passes +3800 with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]]. Should your faction ever dip lower than that in the future, this quest will become available again.

## Steps

1. Collect 10 *Chokidai Bone Plates* from [[A wild chokidai predator|wild chokidai predators]] or [[A feral chokidai scavenger|feral chokidai scavengers]]. {{waypoint 275, 67, -558}}
1. Place the items in the box of bones and leather. {{waypoint 685.24, -90.77, 567.81}}
1. Speak to [[Thress Kivatei]]. {{waypoint 683.44, -90.77, 571.09}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- +1,600 faction with **The Order of Rime**
