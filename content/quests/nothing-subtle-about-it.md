## Steps

![Examinations of Affect Arts Vol II Conjuring and Control](images/Examinations_of_Affect_Arts_Vol_II_Conjuring_and_Control.jpg)
![Myhst river eel](images/Myhst_river_eel.png)
![awe-laced river stone](images/Awe-laced_river_stones.png)

1. Gather an Examinations of Affect Arts Vol II: Conjuring and Control of Brutal magic at {{waypoint 211, 595, -500}} or {{waypoint 233, 594, -484}}.
1. Speak with [[Himaduri]] at {{waypoint -918, 34, 205}} on [[Manacross Island]].
1. Gather and kill the following
   - Gather 6 awe-laced river stones. They are trackable by the regular tracking skill, not harvest tracking skill. The stones have a slight shimmer to them, and they can be tab-targetted. A good spot is the lake. Some possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -233, 8, 349}}
     - {{waypoint 3.98, 0.04, -245.54}}
     - {{waypoint -1, -24, -822}}
     - {{waypoint -373, -24, 687}}
     - {{waypoint -237.41, 0.72, 461.62}}
     - {{waypoint -275.67, 0.24, 469.16}}
     - {{waypoint -233.39, -1.15, 338.18}}
     - {{waypoint -179.97, 5.19, 227.95}}
     - {{waypoint -167.86, 0.27, 141.96}}
     - {{waypoint -295.91, 6.91, 479.12}}
   - Kill 12 [[Myhst river eel]] near {{waypoint -233, 8, 349}}.
1. Speak to [[Himaduri]] at {{waypoint -918, 34, 206}} on  [[Manacross Island]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
