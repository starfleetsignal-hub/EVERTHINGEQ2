## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 20+

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Priest Ian Turner]] at the [[Temple of Life]] in [[North Qeynos]] in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 577, -75, -221}}
1. Gather items used to make the Superior Salve of Nife.
   - Purchase [[Crystal Vials]] from [[Khalil'mun]] at [[The Herb Jar]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 665, -19, 245}}
   - Gather 4 grove lily pads from the streams in [[Elddar Grove]].
     - Note that they are often next to large boulders in the water. Known locations:
       - {{waypoint 751, -23, -379}} ![A grove lily pad](images/Seeking_Superior_Salve_quest_-_grove_lily_pad.jpg)
       - {{waypoint 752, -23, -399}}
       - {{waypoint 531, -22, -431}}
       - {{waypoint 650, -23, -423}}
       - {{waypoint 693, -24, -345}}
       - {{waypoint 680, -24, -325}}
       - {{waypoint 657, -23, -306}}
       - {{waypoint 631, -23, -299}}
       - {{waypoint 693, -24, -345}}
       - {{waypoint 607, -23, -294}}
   - Defeat 4 [[Harbor pincher|harbor pinchers]] in the zone [[Qeynos Province District]] under the piers in near the Qeynos Harbor. You may need to right click and select "Attack the Pincher" to start the fight. {{waypoint 944, -29, 46}}
1. A medic in Eldarr Grove will combine the ingredients and give you the salve {{waypoint 522.16, -18, -414}}
1. Return to [[Harkam Nubbytoe]].
1. Use the salve on [[A wounded Baubbleshire defender]].
   - {{waypoint 793, -21, -546}}
   - {{waypoint 792, -21, -544}}
1. Speak to [[Harkam Nubbytoe]] again.
