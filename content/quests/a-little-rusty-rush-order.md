- [[Feyiron Chainmail Coif (Level 30) (Crafted)|Forged Feyiron Chainmail Coif]]
- [[Feyiron Plate Sabatons|Forged Feyiron Plate Sabatons]]
- [[Feyiron Vanguard Sabatons|Forged Feyiron Vanguard Sabatons]]
- [[Feyiron Brigandine Coif|Forged Feyiron Brigandine Coif]]
- [[Feyiron Chainmail Bracers (Level 30) (Crafted)|Forged Feyiron Chainmail Bracers]]

- [[Feyiron Chainmail Coat (Level 30) (Crafted)|Forged Feyiron Chainmail Coat]]
- [[Feyiron Chainmail Gloves (Level 30) (Crafted)|Forged Feyiron Chainmail Gloves]]
- [[Feyiron Plate Spaulders|Forged Feyiron Plate Spaulders]]

- [[Feyiron Plate Gussets|Forged Feyiron Plate Gussets]]
- [[Feyiron Vanguard Gussets|Forged Feyiron Vanguard Gussets]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
