## Steps

1. Harvest the following:
   - 15 [[Dewpalm lumber]] from [[Fallen dewpalm branch]]
   - 15 [[Strangler root]] from [[Strangler root break]]
   - 15 [[Silvered fleck]] from [[Flecked mass]]
   - 10 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Coalesced reef]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Jewelry Case]].
1. Craft the [[Jewelry Case]] on a [[Work Bench]].
1. Speak to [[Eifae Goldthorn]] at {{waypoint 46.68, -13.58, -5.58}}.
