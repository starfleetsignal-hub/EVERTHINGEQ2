## Steps

1. Perform the following tasks
   - Kill 8 [[A kunzar swampfly|kunzar swampflies]]. {{waypoint -34, -113, 507}}
   - Gather 3 *lichenclover roots* from [[Root Nodes]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]].
   - Gather 3 *Torsis tea leaves* from [[Bush Nodes]] in Kunzar Jungle.
1. Return to Cookie Yevek.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- +5,000 faction with **Dalnir's Wheel Taskmasters**
