## Steps

1. Speak to [[Taliseda]] at {{waypoint -369, 95, -157}} and defeat her when she attacks.
1. Speak to [[Taliseda]] again.
1. Destroy the eldritch concentration at {{waypoint -368, 94, -157}}![eldritch concentration](images/An_eldritch_concentration.jpg)
1. Speak to [[Salindis]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- +1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
- Choose one of the Following:
  - [[Greatblade of the Majestrix]]
  - [[Yrzu Aetherwand]]
  - [[Bow of the Ether Garden]]
  - [[Favor of the Yrzu]]
