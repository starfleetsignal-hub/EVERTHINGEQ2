## Steps

Find the missing pages that were torn apart by wildlife.

- Kill 5 [[A Ykeshan snapper|Ykeshan snapper]]s.
- Kill 5 [[A Ykeshan saltshell|Ykeshan saltshell]]s.
- Kill 5 [[An Innothule swamp feeder|Innothule swamp feeder]]s.
- Kill 5 [[A Gunthak razortooth|Gunthak razortooth]]s.
- Kill 5 [[A void-touched snapper|void-touched snapper]]s (around Brokenskull Rock).
- Kill 5 [[A void-touched saltshell|void-touched saltshell]]s (around Brokenskull Rock).
- Kill 5 [[A Ykeshan crocodilian|Ykeshan crocodilian]]s.
- Kill 5 [[A Ykeshan rustyback|rustybacks]].

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
