## Steps

1. Slay the following, all found around [[Pirate's Perch]] {{waypoint -2036, -158, 92}} and [[Buccaneer Beach]]:
   - 5 [[A Samiel buccaneer|Samiel buccaneers]]
   - 6 [[A Samiel plunderer|Samiel plunderers]]
1. Examine the two crates stacked together on [[Pirate's Perch]]. {{waypoint -1987, -229, 20}}
1. Return to [[Numair]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Desertmage's Golden Wand]] (Coercer, Conjuror, Illusionist, Necromancer)
  - [[Wizard Dagger of the Sands]] (Warlock, Wizard)
  - [[Scourge of Clerical Intuition]] (Defiler, Inquisitor, Mystic, Templar)
  - [[Mystic Blade of the Wilderness]] (Fury, Warden)
  - [[Insane Desert Walker's Sacrifice Blade]] (Bards, Predators, Rangers, Rogues)
  - [[Rune Knuckles]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Sword of Dark Rites (Level 40)|Sword of Dark Rites]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
