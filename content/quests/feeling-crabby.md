## Notes

The Crab Trap recipe auto-scribes. You do *not* need to locate it in inventory and scribe it manually.

## Steps

1. Gather the following
   - Find some wood - *from [[Driftwood]] on the beach shore. Trackable.*![driftwood](images/Driftwood_visible.jpg)
   - Gather vines - *from [[Tough vine]] on cliffs. Trackable.*![tough vine](images/Tough_vine_visible.jpg)
1. Improvise some [[Crab Trap|crab traps]].
   - Use the [[Goblin Workbench]] {{waypoint 18, -241, -875}}. ![Goblin Workbench](images/Goblin_Workbench.jpg)
   - Requires 100 Artisan, 3 [[Driftwood]], 3 [[Tough vine]], 3 [[Thaumic Coal]]
1. Place the [[Crab Trap|crab traps]] along the waterline {{waypoint 56, -243, -909}}. ![Place Trap](images/Place_Trap.jpg)
1. Return to [[Kreeg (Goblin)|Kreeg]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +2000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
- 5 [[Thaumic Coal]]
