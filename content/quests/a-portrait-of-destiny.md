## Prerequisites

- Your character or someone in the group must have unlocked all three levels of the dungeon.

## Starting the Quest

1. Enter [[Nektropos Castle]] and find the room of [[Pelleas]] {{waypoint -36, 0, -57}}.
1. Examine the family portrait on the table against the wall near the bed. If the portrait isn't on the table, kill Pelleas and wait for a [[Personal attendant|personal attendant]] to enter the room and place it there. If the wandering personal attendant was previously killed then the portrait will not be delivered. A possible workaround is to zone out and then zone back into Nektropos Castle. The quest will NOT be offered at this point...but you MUST do this before the next step. **NOTE: This quest is buggy.** *The portrait may be visible and unclickable, wait for the attendant; the portrait may be visible and clickable, but the painting in Crysta's Bedroom may not give the quest; return to Pelleas' room and wait for the attendant; In a group, the portrait may be visible and clickable, but time the responses. Sometimes only one group member will get the 'click' and subsequent quest; return to Pelleas' room and wait for the attendant.*
1. Head to [[Crysta's Bedroom]] at {{waypoint 57, 13, -78}} (same room as the paintbrush below). Click on the painting above the dresser. You will get the quest after you complete the dialog.

## Steps

1. Search for clues about the six daughters in the painting:
   - Paintbrush -- Examine the blank canvas on the easel in [[Crysta's Bedroom]] (other side of the bed). {{waypoint 52, 13, -87}}
   - Heart -- Examine the large mirror on the wall in [[Sheila's Bedroom]]. {{waypoint 70, 13, 8}}
   - Wrench -- Examine the broken container next to the dresser in [[Melanie's Bedroom]]. {{waypoint -45, 13, -86}}
   - Leash -- Examine the leash on the wall in [[Jenni's Bedroom]]. {{waypoint -74, 13, 6}}
   - Flower -- Examine the shovel leaning against the wall in the basement of [[Deirdre's Bedroom]]. {{waypoint -44, 0, 85}}
   - Sword -- Return to the courtyard and examine the doll on the floor near [[Master Eldin Necrosis]] to update [[Elise Everling|Elise's]] clue. {{waypoint 11, 0, 31}}
1. Find the other five dolls of the Everling sisters in [[Nektulos Forest]]; doll respawn is 20 seconds.  **NOTE:** The dolls may be picked up in any order, *but if you can Fast Travel, do not zone from the castle. Instead* FT *to Nek Druid ring and collect the dolls in this order:*
   1. Melanie's doll near the entrance to [[The Obelisk of Lost Souls]] {{waypoint -86, 92, -2226}}
   1. Crysta's rag doll in a trailer in [[Camp Leeot]] to the west of [[N'Mar's Ascent]]  {{waypoint 135, 65, -1472}}.
   1. Shiela's rag doll on top of a tower in [[Citadel of Gul'thex]] {{waypoint 700, 23, -708}}.
   1. Deidre's rag doll is at [[Behemoth Pond]] in the behemoth skeleton {{waypoint -212, 6, -282}}.
   1. Jenni's rag doll is in the western bear cave {{waypoint -1381, 29, -701}}.
1. "There must be someplace that hints of the use of these rag dolls." Go to the basement of [[Nektropos Castle]] and enter the secret research laboratory by examining the sconce on the wall at {{waypoint -8, -14, -18}}.
1. Examine the six circles on the floor to place the six dolls of the Everling sisters.
1. Right click the circle and place [[The Idol of Everling]] onto the center of the circles. They appear automatically as you go. Any order seems OK. After all idols are in place a statue appears in the centre. Click this to complete.
