## Steps

![Mana-enriched soil for planting seeds.](images/Soilfilledbarrel.jpg)

1. Collect Green Wisp Seeds from the bushes near the Fountain of Valor, in the center of Castleview Hamlet {{waypoint 769, -18, -111}}.
1. Collect Blessed Water from the fountain, by examining the water flask near the fountain.
1. Gather soil from the barrel at the NE corner of the armor shop. (The barrel is actually outside the shop) ({{waypoint 737, -13, -110}}
1. Return to [[Vindain Greenfaith]].

## Rewards

Gardener's Pouch (5 slot, lore, no trade, no value bag)
1 silver, 17 copper
