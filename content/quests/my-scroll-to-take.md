## Steps

1. Kill and loot the following
   - Slay 8 clutches of Dreadfall Coven witches (Dreadfall [[A Dreadfall gravestalker|gravestalkers]], [[A Dreadfall necrocrafter|necrocrafters]], [[A Dreadfall deadweaver|deadweavers]], and [[A Dreadfall mortifier|mortifiers]]) around {{waypoint -230, 105, 26}}.
   - Loot 6 [[Dreadfell Scroll|Dreadfell Scrolls]] and [[Dreadfell Tome|Dreadfell Tomes]] from small chests.
1. Return to [[Dhusala]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
