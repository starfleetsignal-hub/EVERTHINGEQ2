## Description

I must collect cargo crates from aboard The Vigilant: Infiltration so that the erudites may reclaim some of their much needed supplies.

## Steps

1. Find the cargo crates aboard [[The Vigilant: Infiltration]]
   - Collect 5 cargo crates
1. Return to Ismat

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
