This quest is part 3 of a 6-quest series in the [[Isle of Refuge Timeline]] for Freeport-Aligned characters.

## Description

"Tayil N'Velex has informed me that the outpost was created for training purposes. Those who seek citizenship with Freeport can come here and hone their skills before entering the city. They had originally thought the island was uninhabited, but have since found out that they were wrong. A sect of the Tunarian Alliance, a Qeynosian faction, inhabits the island. If the training outpost's to be successful, the elves and humans need to be removed. I have been ordered to test out my combat skills and help out Chef Gorga outside the west gate." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Chef Gorga]] {{waypoint 65, -4, 194}} outside the west archway.
1. Kill three bees.  ([[A worker bee|Worker Bees]] will be flying around near the barricades.  [[A soldier bee|Soldier bees]], or [[A Queen Bee]] will spawn after smacking one of the bee hives hanging from the trees).
1. Speak to Chef Gorga.
1. Kill three elk ([[An elk calf|elk calves]], [[A cow elk]], or [[A bull elk]]) roaming near the barricades.
1. Speak to Chef Gorga.
1. Return to to Tayil N'Velex for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 15c

- One of the following, depending on your class:
  - [[Freeportian Cloth Cuffs]]
  - [[Freeportian Leather Sleeves]]
  - [[Freeportian Plated Bracers]]
  - [[Freeportian Studded Wristguards]]
