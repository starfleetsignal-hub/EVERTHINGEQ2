## Steps

1. Kill [[A hellhound (The Tombs of Night)|hellhounds]] in [[The Tombs of Night]] until you find six unmarred hellhound fangs. {{waypoint -68, 0, 94}}, {{waypoint 28, -24, 12}}
1. Kill vampires until you find two auto-update vampiric canines.
   - These are not the same as the lore and legend item: [[A vampiric incisor]].
1. Kill [[The Leucous Maw]] {{waypoint 86, -21, -100}}

## Rewards

- [[Ceremonial Fanged Necklace]]
