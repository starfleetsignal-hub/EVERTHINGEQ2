## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 25 [[Coruscating Kindling]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest the following:
   - 10 [[Splitfin Dartfish]]
   - 16 [[Slow Creeping Root]]
   - 50 [[Luclizite Cluster]]
1. Craft a [[Dartfish Divining Rod]] on a [[Work Bench]]
1. Return to [[Daini the Wright]] at {{waypoint -585.92, 38.39, 451.75}}

## Rewards

- At least 5866 status
- 4 [[Recuso Specie]]
- [[Empyral Stocking Crate]]
