## Steps

You must visit (by foot) each of the three tower locations before unlocking their use.

1. Greater Faydark Station {{waypoint -983, 92, -536}}
1. Lesser Faydark Station {{waypoint -632, 101, 340}}
1. Docks Station {{waypoint 334, 188, 480}}

## Rewards

The ability to travel through Butcherblock Mountains via Griffin.

## Note

You can start this quest at any of the 3 towers. Make sure you get the update for the tower you started at before you leave. You may have to run around the base some to trigger it.
