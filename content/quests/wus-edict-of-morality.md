## Steps

1. Gather 2 samples of the [[Twisted nightshade]] plant from [[The Shadowed Grove]] around {{waypoint 594, -38, 578}}
1. Return to [[Master Kyau]]

## Rewards

- At least 13g 79s 50c
- +100 faction with **Disciples of Wu**
- [[Nightshade Healing Salve]]
- [[Twisted Nightshade Poison]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Blood Baptising Bow]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Dismal Rage's Staff]] (All Priests)
  - [[Faydark Defender's Bow]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Faydark Defender's Satchel]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Metal Focus of Mystery]] (All Mages)
