## Steps

1. Click on the chest in Dukarem's room. You will now be on a 2-minute timer.
1. Go to the door of the Court of Tears in [[Maj'Dul]]. {{waypoint -173, 138, 173}}
1. Enter [[The Court of Tears]] and kill [[Jafi Nsir (The Court of Tears)|Jafi Nsir]].
1. Read [[Note to Jafi]] in your inventory.
1. Speak to [[Danaria, Princess of the Blades]] in [[The Court of the Blades]].
1. Speak to [[A Blades soldier]] on the top level of [[The Clefts of Rujark]]. {{waypoint 116, 52, -76}}
1. Enter [[Lockjaw's Lair]] in [[The Sinking Sands]]. {{waypoint -1105, -219, -678}}
1. Kill [[Jafi Nsir (Lockjaw's Lair)|Jafi Nsir]] and his [[A Tears assassin (Lockjaw's Lair)|assassins]] who spawn a little ways down from the entrance.
1. Exit the lair and speak to [[A Blades warrior]] across the pool. {{waypoint -1168, -215, -667}}
1. Go to the [[Port of Tears]] in the Sinking Sands. {{waypoint -1515, -214, -399}}
1. Speak to [[Honest Ali]], located nearby. {{waypoint -1450, -225, -381}}
1. Approach the front door of the Court of the Blades in Maj'Dul. {{waypoint -16, 152, 118}}
1. Speak to [[Captain Aziz]], located nearby. {{waypoint -53, 149, 140}}

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Belt of the Blades]]
  - [[Cinch of the Blades]]
  - [[Girdle of the Blades]]
- 15g 15s 54c
- +5000 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -5000 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -5000 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
- Access [[The Court of the Blades: Dukarem's Assault]]: The Ability to zone into Dukarem's Assault by clicking on the door of the Court of the Blades.  Instance has an 18 hour success lockout.
