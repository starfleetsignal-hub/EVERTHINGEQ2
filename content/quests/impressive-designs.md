## Steps

Create 12 items from the random list below:

- [[A pristine oil painting of a castle|oil painting of a castle]]
- [[A pristine teak chest|teak chest]]
- [[A pristine standard teak table|standard teak table]]
- [[Teak strong box]]
- [[A pristine oil painting of a stone castle|oil painting of a stone castle]]
- [[A pristine rough linen portrait|rough linen portrait]]
- [[A pristine rough linen scenery painting|rough linen scenery painting]]
- [[A pristine elaborate teak shelf|elaborate teak shelf]]
- [[A pristine fancy teak shelf|fancy teak shelf]]
- [[A pristine teak military stool|teak military stool]]
- [[A pristine teak oval table|teak oval table]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

## Rewards

- At Least 54s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
