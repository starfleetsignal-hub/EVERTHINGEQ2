## Steps

1. Hunt and apply the following
   - Hunt 12 shrewdeye ritekeepers for Totems of Xktkez (up in the ruins around {{waypoint -71, 107, -462}}![annointing oils](images/Anointing_Oils.png)
     - *Note: the shrewdeye ritekeepers share spawns with other gazers in the Vital Terrace area. Kill all gazers in the area to increase the chances for shrewdeye ritekeepers to spawn.*
   - Apply lashling paralysis venom to 10 jars of anointing oils - in same area on the ground and on platforms above {{waypoint -78, 106, -520}}
1. Return to [[Grusla]]

## Rewards

- 2 X [[Jar of All Seeing Anointing Oils]]
- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following:
  - [[Coral Hauberk of Rifts]]
  - [[Coral Robe of Delusion]]
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
