## Steps

1. Inspect the odd obelisk outside the mouth of Crusader's Cave {{waypoint 738, 277, 805}}.
1. Return to Hierophant Prime Jarakkas.
1. Search for another arcane obelisk in Giant's Walk. {{waypoint -813.92, 186.65, 614.44}}
   1. Kill the attacking Giant
1. Find the place known as Nye'Caelona  {{waypoint -315.32, 136.52, -154.56}}
1. Explore the odd city of Nye'Caelona.
   1. Land and you will be attacked by a guard which will automatically teleport you to [[Kylong Plains]]
      - *Note: this happens regardless on your faction with them (scripted teleport)*
      - *Note: the quest will not update when you die from the guard attack => You need to be able to withstand one hit from a guard, then you will be ported. It can happen that they melee attack once as well after the casted the teleport, therefor run into melee range and back off as soon as they move/cast*
      - *Note: guards almost allways 1 shot you, find a geomancer, they will encase you and port you out.
      - *Note: Until you gain faction with Nye'Caelona, when you are caught by a guard, you will be teleported to Kylong Plains every time. Unless you attack the guard or have a merc or pet who attacks the guard. In that case you will die and have to come back all over again.*
   1. run through the tunnel at {{waypoint 2327, 161, -2925}} in [[Kylong Plains]] to return to [[Obulus Frontier]]
      - *Note: Don't forget to resummon your mount!*
1. Return to Hierophant Prime Jarrakas {{waypoint 627, 253, 1338}}.
