## Steps

1. Collect debris sample in [[Zimara Breadth]] at {{waypoint -699.39, 232.41, -771.10}} (Sample is right by the zone-in)
   1. Notes: Travel to [[Zimara Breadth]].
      - *The portal from [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] to [[Zimara Breadth]] is on [[Molted Island]] at {{waypoint -1091, 181, 736}}* ![Use this crystal on the left side of the upper ledge of the Smeltfield Vale to teleport up to the Molted Vale](images/Teleport_to_Molted_Island.jpg)
      - *There is no update to flight status in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] when you receive this quest, so if you haven't done the adventure sigline to the [[Zimara Breadth]] stage (see [[Ballads of Zimara: Facing the Heat]]) then you will have to hoof it to the teleport crystal at {{waypoint -783.77, 84.94, 607.32}}. (You can use Totem of Escape to shorten the distance to run, feather fall helps here as you can float down to where you need to be)*
      - *Use this to teleport up to [[Molted Island]].*
      - *The Return to Smeltfield Vale portal is in Splendor Sky Aerie at {{waypoint -1077.43, 178.08, 669.76}}.*
      - *The teleport crystals are visible only to those who have completed, or are on, this quest.*
1. Craft [[Processed Zimaran Sample]].
   - *You'll need a portable workbench, 1 aerated mineral water and 5 Zimaran coal (plus 5 more for the next step).*
1. Create the [[W.H.O.O. Advanced]].
1. Find additional debris sample on the Isle of Wisdom.
   - Take to the Skies cloud is at {{waypoint -709, 145, -357}} (inside [[Zimara Breadth]]) to [[Isles of Wisdom]]. You can use your [[W.H.O.O. Advanced]] form to get to cloud and fly over to [[Isles of Wisdom]].
   - debris is at {{waypoint -871, 151, 51}}
1. Return to [[Goldbeak]] in [[Sanctuary (POI)|Sanctuary]].
   - Take to the Skies cloud {{waypoint -673, 142, -20}} to [[Auric Palisades]]

## Rewards

- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c 54824 status
- +4,000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
- [[W.H.O.O. Advanced]] - *Magic harvester for use in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] and [[Zimara Breadth]]*
- Flight enabled in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] and [[Zimara Breadth]]
