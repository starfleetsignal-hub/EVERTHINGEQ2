## Steps

1. Return to [[The Clefts of Rujark]] and kill the following:
   - [[Advisor Nazgoth]] {{waypoint 153, 69, 178}} - top Level
   - [[Rujark the Broken Lord]] - spawns immediately after Advisor Nazgoth dies
1. Return to [[Zahr Shabao]].

## Rewards

- At least 18g
- +6000 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- One of the following:
  - [[Belt of the Blades Champion]]
  - [[Sceptre of the Blades Champion]]
