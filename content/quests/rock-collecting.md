## Notes

- All of the rocks in this quest are gathered from special nodes called 'a gray rock' along the shore of the [[Magma Grotto]], and just past the tunnel in [[Najena's Valley]]. Even if the step seemingly calls for a different node, they are all gathered from 'a gray rock'. They can be tracked and have many spawn points. Some are listed here:
  - {{waypoint -223, -129, 386}}
  - {{waypoint -347, -130, 388}}
  - {{waypoint -241, -129, 274}}
  - {{waypoint -279, -130, 321}}
- Although the rocks you need to find have different names, they are ALL gray rocks.
- All of the required rocks can be found on the shore without any agro mobs around.

## Steps

1. Find two gray rocks.
1. Find an ashen rock.
1. Find a sooty rock.
1. Find 2 light black rocks.
1. Find a light gray rock.
1. Find a dark gray rock.
1. Return to [[Blisterzonk]].
