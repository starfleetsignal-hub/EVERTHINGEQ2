## Prerequisites

- You must have +6000 faction with [[The Gwalnax Brigade (Faction)|The Gwalnax Brigade]] to unlock this quest.

## Steps

1. Go to the [[The Hall of Clay]] in [[Chardok]] and obtain the following, in any order: {{waypoint -95, -43, 42}}
   - *Tome of Aardman* {{waypoint -106, -54, 73}} or {{waypoint -101, -54, 53}}
   - 4 [[Grom clay]], looted from the chests of [[An imperial golem|imperial golems]]
   - 6 [[Hearts of Gaodon]], which are all given by [[Gaodon]] after giving him his brother's heart:
     1. Kill [[Silzok Foi'zarth]] and loot [[Heart of Silzok]].
     1. Kill named Gaodon {{waypoint -47, -54, 50}} to unshackle NPC Gaodon.
     1. Hail NPC Gaodon {{waypoint -52, -43, 55}} and progress through his dialogue to receive all six hearts.
1. Return to [[Battle Master Dolak]] in [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]].

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Gwalnax Brigade**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Lesser Emblem of Invading Forces]]
  - [[Loop of Greater Molten Flow]]
  - [[Loop of the Void Army]]
  - [[Voidman Ring of the Culled]]
