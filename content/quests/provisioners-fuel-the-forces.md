Gerry Stumbletoe recruited me to use my provisioning skills to help G.L.E.E.S.H. provide support to the Luclinites who are trying to repair and relocate to the city of Fordel Midst. Their need for salted meat and beer is high.

## Steps

- Check the provisioners' crate near Gerry Stumbletoe to see what to make for the next shipment to Luclin. You can find the [[Solidified ethereal filament|solidified ethereal filament]] needed around Teren's Grasp.
  - Craft 6 barrels of salted meat
  - Craft 6 kegs of beer
- Return to Gerry Stumbletoe to turn in.
