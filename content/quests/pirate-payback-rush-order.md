## Steps

Create 2 each of 3 of the randomly selected spells:

- [[Bodyguard III|Bodyguard III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Forward Charge III|Forward Charge III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Armored III|Armored III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Siphon Strength|Siphon Strength (Journeyman)]]
- [[Uppercut|Uppercut (Journeyman)]]
- [[Instill Panic|Instill Panic (Journeyman)]]
- [[Prayer of Healing III|Prayer of Healing III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Unflinching Will III|Unflinching Will III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Knight's Stance III|Knight's Stance III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Crouching Tiger III|Crouching Tiger III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Weapon Counter|Weapon Counter (Journeyman)]]
- [[Arctic Talon|Arctic Talon (Journeyman)]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 24s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
