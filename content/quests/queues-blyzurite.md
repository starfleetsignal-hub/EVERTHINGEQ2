## Steps

1. Mine 7 *blyzurite speckled ore* in the [[Wurm Caverns]] at {{waypoint 1972, 203, -2009}}, or [[Den of the Broodmother]] at {{waypoint 1724, 233, -2066}}.
1. Return to [[Queue]] and give him the crystals. {{waypoint 1493, 161, -2059}}
1. Return to [[Germa Prash]] in [[Highton]]. {{waypoint 1275, 159, -2074}}

## Rewards

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- [[Steelbark Belt]]
