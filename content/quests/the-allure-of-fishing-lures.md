## Steps

1. Use [[Grandpa Krill's Special Shark Bait]] in the ocean near the rock arch {{waypoint -1525, -1100, -543}}. You MUST be in the water when using bait -- the shark spawns at the exact location you are standing and if you are on the ground it will reset again and again.
1. Kill [[Carcha]] the shark that spawns, loot [[Grandpa Krill's Favorite Lure]] from corpse
   - everyone in group has to spawn it but all can loot from 1 corpse
   - The quest is marked heroic in the quest book. The shark is a lvl 80^ though.
1. Return to [[Grandpa Krill]]

## Rewards

- At least 11g 5s 35c
- +1200 faction with **Survival Accord**
- One of the following, based on class
  - [[Executioner's Adamantine Pauldrons of Dominance]] (plate)
