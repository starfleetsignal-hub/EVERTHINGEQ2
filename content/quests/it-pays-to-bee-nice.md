## Description

*[[Soneej Viivi]] at [[The Fae Court|the Fae Court]] has asked me to gather some honeybrood pollen and honey for her to take back to her Mother in [[Kelethin]]. She said that she needs six pinches of pollen and one flask of honey, which shouldn't be too hard to get, depending on how much honey these bees have on them.*

There are 3 quests that require you to kill Honeybrood bees. It would be a good idea to get all 3 at the same time: [[Learning about Neurotoxins]] from [[Uric M'Ots]] in [[Camp Nybright]], and [[Refilling the Stores]] from [[Lady Koivuu]] and [[It Pays To Bee Nice]] from [[Soneej Viivi]], both in [[The Fae Court]].

## Step

- Gather 6 **[[A pinch of pollen|pinches of pollen]]** from Honeybrood Bees.
- Gather 3 dabs of honey from Honeybrood Bees to obtain **[[A flask of honey]]**.
