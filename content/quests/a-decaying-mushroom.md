## Description

"While in Treant Grove I found a mushroom that seemed to be decaying naturally. The Crushbone's corruption might be spreading, just as Brodo Gillywump feared!" -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Take the [[Decaying Mushroom Scraping]] to [[Brodo Gillywump]] on [[Green Knoll]] at {{waypoint 592, 45, -3}}

## Rewards

- At least 2s 10c
