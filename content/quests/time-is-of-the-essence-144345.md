## Steps

1. Head to [[Fens of Nathsar]]  and complete the collection [[Axiom of the Great Chokidai (Collection)|Axiom of the Great Chokidai]].
   - *Note: You **MUST wear the [[Talisman of Vah Kerrath]]** neck item you received in the previous quest to see the green shinies and the items must be collected in the order given. These collection quest shinies are a serious PITA to find. Be patient and continue to push forward.*
   - *Note: If you don't have a sokokor it will save some time if you go do that.*
1. Zone in to the Spirit Realm, zone in at bone dragon {{waypoint -1580.72, -366.92, -1537.36}} in [[Fens of Nathsar]]
1. Hail Ralissk, the Keeper of Wisdom
1. Zone back into the [[Fens of Nathsar]] and speak with [[Foz]] at {{waypoint -1916.17, -38.83, 945.08}}
   - *Note:must speak Froak*
1. Kill [[Grojf]] {{waypoint -2650.78, -49.00, 1272.18}} (L95^^^ Froglok spawns when near)
   - *Note:(tank and spank) Do not even think about trying to solo this mob without ToV Gear (suggested:spiritual stance, grandmaster level defensive warder and with a healer MERC) [Also advisable to have a death prevent, as "Grojf's Severed Windpipe" can hit for 200-500k dmg].*
1. Return to Foz
1. Return to Ralissk in the Spirit Realm
1. Kill [[Dromo the Agonizer]] (L97^^^) in the Fens of Nathsar at {{waypoint -2192.68, -265.48, -1524.77}}.
   - *Note: He casts a group fear with attached DoT effect. He also casts a group stun/stifle, and group DoT, if in range.*
   - *Note: One strat is to range the him down, and keep tank up; as fear is an AOE originating at boss (very large range), Templar/Sanctuary and Warden/Fury with Tortoise Shell help.*
   - *Note: we found he casts a few of the same type of spell every 3 SECONDS, then takes a short break between 6-15 seconds, then moves on to another type of spell he then casts every 3 Seconds*
   - *Note: easy kill if you duo him with a healer in ToV gear*
1. Return to Ralissk in the Spirit Realm. He ports you in the special Kerra island instance
1. Listen to the council meeting at the bonfire. Eventually you get ported back to Ralissk.
1. Speak to Ralissk to finish the quest

## Rewards

- [[Sash of Vah Kerrath]]
