**NOTICE TO ALL ADVENTURERS** - *The [[The Far Seas Trading Company (Faction)|Far Seas Trading Company]] (FSTC) is seeking adventurers capable of fulfilling orders in [[Nektulos Forest]]! Fun, Fame and easy money await you! Flexible hours, on the job training, and a generous compensation package! Apply in person to FSTC Representative [[Taskmaster D'Zran]] at the [[Port Naythex]] docks in [[Nektulos Forest]]!* - Text of a handbill found on the floor of [[Seafarers Roost|the Seafarers Roost]] in [[East Freeport]].

## Starting the Quests

![](images/Taskmaster_D'Zran_and_the_Orders_Desk.jpg)

1. Click on the orders desk next to [[Taskmaster D'Zran]] at {{waypoint 806, 6, -115}}.
1. Take one or all six. See the details below. Fulfill the terms of the contracts and return the completed orders to D'Zran.
1. Once you have completed all of the SCOs you will have access to the Special Contracts.

## Standard Contract Orders

1. **Far Seas Requisition #NF0771-SCO** [25]
   - Collect 24 cleaned wings from fairies in Nektulos (Sullian or Asilian fairies) {{waypoint -997, 141, -2131}}
   - Collect 12 vials of ash-dust from [[A Gul'Thex mystic|Gul'Thex mystics]] {{waypoint 499, 4, -708}}
   - Gauge the ash-residue within the [[Forgotten Woods]] {{waypoint -576, 0, -1374}}
   - Reward: Experience only
1. **Far Seas Requisition #NF0808-SCO** [25]
   - Forest 32 planks of various indigenous pieces of wood in the [[Nektulos Forest]]. You can get these from [[Duskwood arbor]].
   - Collect 12 planks of [[Petrified ashwood]] from [[Duskwood arbor]] in Nektulos Forest
   - Collect 6 lengthy branches from rumbleroots in Nektulos Forest {{waypoint -746, 0, -158}}
   - Reward: Experience only
1. **Far Seas Requisition #NF0119-SCO** [27]
   - Find the [[Valley of the Bears]]. {{waypoint -1280, 18, -1153}}
   - Find the [[Valley of the Lost Souls]]. {{waypoint -250, 0, -1425}}
   - Find the [[Valley of the Dead]]. {{waypoint -411, 0, -1711}}
   - Collect 12 toe bones from [[A Dragoon sentry|Dragoon sentries]] at [[The Funeral Pyre]] {{waypoint -966, 14, -740}}
   - Collect 16 clumps of flesh from the local [[Golem|golems]] (patchworks in [[Gargoyle Gorge]] and those surrounding [[Nektropos Castle]]) {{waypoint -1055, 97, -1645}}
     - You can also get updates from the golems in the [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] or the rock heaps in [[The Thundering Steppes]] for this step.
   - Reward: Experience only
1. **Far Seas Requisition #NF0386-SCO** [27]
   - Collect 12 individual scrapings of bone mold from [[A Dragoon sentry|Dragoon sentries]] in Nektulos {{waypoint -966, 14, -740}} or skeletons in The Thundering Steppes
   - Collect 3 infected patches of skin from [[A patchwork man|patchwork men]] in Gargoyle Gorge in Nektulos. {{waypoint -1055, 97, -1645}}
   - Harvest 32 specimens of [[Bush Nodes#T3|polluted natural gardens]] in Nektulos.
   - Reward: Experience only
1. **Far Seas Requisition #NF0551-SCO** [27]
   - Collect 12 armor vestments from [[A Sullian fairy|Sullian fairies]]; found around [[Bone Lake]] in Nektulos. {{waypoint -755, 0, -2185}}
   - Collect 8 epees from Sullian fairies.
   - Visit the road to [[Nektropos Castle (POI)|Nektropos Castle]] {{waypoint -1327, 0, -1848}}
   - Reward: Experience Only
1. **Far Seas Requisition #NF0291-SCO** [28]
   - Inspect [[Gargoyle Gorge]] in Nektulos. {{waypoint -926, 79, -1605}}
   - Collect 24 skulls from skeletons in Nektulos.
   - Collect 12 high quality root specimens from [[A deadly rumbleroot|deadly rumbleroots]] in Nektulos. {{waypoint -758.5, 72.5, 107.5}})
   - Reward: Experience Only

## Special Contracts

*Once the SCOs are complete, these contracts become available.*

1. **Far Seas Requisition Special Contract #NF002** [28]
   - Collect 32 Fillets from [[A shallows fish|shallows fish]] in The Thundering Steppes.
   - Mine 12 *shiny stones* from [[Ore Nodes#T3|penumbra ore]] in Nektulos.
   - Collect 6 medallions from [[A Gul'Thex mystic|Gul'Thex mystics]] in Nektulos {{waypoint 663, 8, -783}}
   - Reward: Experience only
1. **Far Seas Requisition Special Contract #NF331** [30]
   - Collect 18 cores from [[A deathbloom treant|deathbloom treants]] in Nektulos. {{waypoint -319, 90, -2000}}
   - Find a lost shipment under a wheelbarrow at [[Camp Leeot]] in Nektulos. {{waypoint 123, 65, -1414}}.
   - Kill 12 [[A Steelhoof marauder|Steelhoof marauder]] in The Thundering Steppes. {{waypoint 132, 9, 1307}}
   - Reward: Experience only
1. **Far Seas Requisition Special Contract #NF409** [28]
   - Collect 12 pristine stitches from [[A patchwork man|patchwork men]] in Nektulos. {{waypoint -1055, 97, -1645}}
   - Make a rubbing of the fallen marker near [[Bone Lake]] in Nektulos {{waypoint -689, 147, -2127}}
   - Collect 18 globs of ectoplasm from the [[A warlock orb|warlock orbs]] in The Thundering Steppes. {{waypoint -553, 0, 909}}
   - Reward: Experience only
1. **Far Seas Requisition Special Contract #NF112-A** [30]
   - Collect 4 gauntlets from [[A Rallosian ghost|Rallosian ghosts]] in The Thundering Steppes; underwater at {{waypoint 355, -50, 1390}}.
   - Collect 12 exceptionally pungent roots from the [[Root Nodes#T3|rancid roots]] in Nektulos.
   - [[Soul Eater Falls]] {{waypoint 471, 25, -1537}}
   - [[Death's Maw]] {{waypoint -396, 60, -1314}}
   - [[Black Water Lake]] {{waypoint -524, -2, -942}}
   - [[Headstone Walkway]] {{waypoint -856, 60, -1340}}
   - Reward: Experience only
