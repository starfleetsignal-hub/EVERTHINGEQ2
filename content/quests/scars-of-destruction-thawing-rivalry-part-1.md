## Steps

1. Enter [[Sodden Archipelago: Thawed Marshes (Raid)|Sodden Archipelago: Thawed Marshes [Raid]]]
1. Go to {{waypoint 558.85, 18.11, 510.38}}
1. Defeat [[Barlsbit the Scavenger]] in the [[Dankmar Swamp]] at {{waypoint 523.36, 4.80, 606.44}}.
1. Find the scavenger corpse at {{waypoint 620.37, 18.77, 559.60}}.
1. Defeat [[Viterol the Voracious]] in the [[Dankmar Swamp]] at {{waypoint 645.08, 30.96, 580.74}}.
1. Find another contraption near southeast coast at {{waypoint 499.70, 5.32, 733.13}}. Auto updates when close enough.
1. Defeat [[Monuseru, Titan of the Depths]] in the [[Dankmar Swamp]] at {{waypoint 443.18, 4.52, 776.23}}.
1. Defeat [[Grand Shaman Grungizt]] in the [[Dankmar Swamp]] at {{waypoint 328.25, 21.34, 487.98}} and retrieve the contraption for Benton.
1. Return the contraptions to Benton.
