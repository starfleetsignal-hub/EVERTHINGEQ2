## Steps

1. Speak to [[Priest Kafri]], a Hooluk priest at {{waypoint -822.47, 191.40, 224.07}}
1. Gather and kill the following
   - ![Stratos Marjoram](images/Stratos_Marjoram.jpg)Gather 10 **Stratos Marjoram** plants for potpourri. (group-wide update) Some possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -770.72, 191.54, 248.65}}, {{waypoint -838.64, 187.49, 376.73}}, {{waypoint -887.95, 193.65, 383.81}}
     - {{waypoint -891.21, 188.78, 305.93}}, {{waypoint -883.50, 165.96, 246.77}}, {{waypoint -856.49, 165.72, 255.32}}
     - {{waypoint -836.68, 165.77, 290.04}}, {{waypoint -803.95, 164.69, 287.78}}, {{waypoint -767.34, 166.33, 297.48}}
     - {{waypoint -769.58, 166.04, 340.35}}
   - Defeat 6 [[A skylight spren]] for flickering flames {{waypoint -858.39, 150.00, 60.36}}.
1. Return to [[Priest Kafri]] at {{waypoint -822.47, 191.40, 224.07}}
1. Return to [[Miranna Ameema (Isles of Wisdom)|Miranna Ameema]] at {{waypoint -911.19, 189.61, 331.11}}
1. Hunt the following
   - hunt winged vipers for 8 rotten fangs near {{waypoint 24.25, 150.94, -5.14}}.
     - *Note:Take Skies cloud at {{waypoint -682.82, 144.92, -5.32}} to [[Zenith Helionae]]*
   - hunt [[A columnar crawler|columnar crawlers]] near {{waypoint 669.82, 209.12, -517.33}} for 10 venom glands.
     - *Note:Take Skies cloud at {{waypoint 313.23, 167.74, -51.97}} to [[Columnar Highgarden]]*
1. Return and talk to [[Miranna Ameema (Isles of Wisdom)|Miranna Ameema]] on [[Isles of Wisdom]] at {{waypoint -911.19, 189.61, 331.11}} and receive the [[Ophthalmic Dilation Wand]]
1. ![a metalbound behemoth](images/A_metalbound_behemoth.jpg)Use the [[Ophthalmic Dilation Wand]] on [[A metalbound behemoth]] in [[Auric Palisades]] around {{waypoint -566, 205, -669}}
1. Return and talk to [[Miranna Ameema (Isles of Wisdom)|Miranna Ameema]] on [[Isles of Wisdom]] at {{waypoint -911.19, 189.61, 331.11}}. You'll be told to wait/find some other tasks (*You must complete all 3 subquests from Hooatio. If you do them after the previous step she will be ready to speak to upon completion of the last quest, you won't have to wait*)
1. Perform the following side quests for [[Hooatio]] - two doors to the right of Miranna at {{waypoint -859.88, 189.67, 372.18}}
   - [[Sticky Sweet Vengeance]]
   - [[Destruction of Amassed Weapons]]
   - [[Zakir Rish Removal]]
     - If you have completed these quests prior to this step in the quest, just wait about 5-10 minutes and Miranna will have the update.
1. obtain the following
   - Obtain Life-Sparked Clay on [[Claved Fate Islets]]
     - *Note: Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint -682.82, 144.92, -5.32}} to [[Claved Fate Islets]]*
     - ![Life-Sparked Clay](images/Life-Sparked_Clay.jpg)Pick up 4 Life-Sparked Clay after killing [[A construct of attrition|Constructs]]. (group-wide update) not guaranteed drop.
       - The harrying subduers (floating planar elementals around the same location) work for this, too.
     - * This appears as sparkling mounds of dirt on the ground*
   - Obtain Arcana Spirit Jewels and Arcana Flesh Plates on [[Columnar Highgarden]]
     - *Note: Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint 848.61, 182.53, 310.60}} to [[Columnar Highgarden]]*
   - Hunt for 8 Arcana Spirit Jewels from  [[An arcaneframe razorfang|arcaneframe razorfangs]], white tigers around  {{waypoint 584, 217, -814}} and {{waypoint 621, 206, -573}} and [[An arcaneframe hammerhoof|arcaneframe hammerhooves]] for 10 Arcana Flesh Plates on [[Columnar Highgarden]] {{waypoint 737, 227, -446}}.
1. ![](images/An_arcaneframe_hammerhoof.png)Return to [[Miranna Ameema (Isles of Wisdom)|Miranna Ameema]] on [[Isles of Wisdom]] at {{waypoint -911.19, 189.61, 331.11}}
1. Speak with [[Vahravi]], the Allyrian at {{waypoint -906.42, 188.34, 323.33}} to receive [[Aviak Flageolet (Ballads of Zimara: Song of the Maedjinn)|Aviak Flageolet]]
1. Charm local fauna into dancing (group-wide update)
   - Play [[Aviak Flageolet (Ballads of Zimara: Song of the Maedjinn)|Aviak Flageolet]] on [[A Zimara cottontail]] {{waypoint -916.37, 174.52, 231.02}}
   - Play [[Aviak Flageolet (Ballads of Zimara: Song of the Maedjinn)|Aviak Flageolet]] on [[A skylight spren]] {{waypoint -871.45, 164.98, 225.14}}
   - Play [[Aviak Flageolet (Ballads of Zimara: Song of the Maedjinn)|Aviak Flageolet]] on [[A stratos fawn]] {{waypoint -832.62, 143.40, -2.46}}
   - Play [[Aviak Flageolet (Ballads of Zimara: Song of the Maedjinn)|Aviak Flageolet]] on [[A russet hootie]] {{waypoint -826.71, 149.29, -25.38}}
1. Return to [[Vahravi]], the Allyrian at {{waypoint -906.42, 188.34, 323.33}}
