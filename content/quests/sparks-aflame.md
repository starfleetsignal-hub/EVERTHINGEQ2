## Starting the Quest

- ***Prerequisite:*** Lvl 50+ [[Alchemist]]

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Spark Augren]] about his early aptitude for the family business of alchemy.
1. Help him gather ingredients for his experiment:
   - Harvest 5 **salt** at the west and north-east facing beaches.
   - Harvest 5 **aluminum** among the rocks near the waterfalls by the Tower of the Four Winds. {{waypoint -15, 0, -167}}
   - Harvest 5 **sulfur** in the rocks near [[Terai Augren]], not far from the Mystic Waterfalls. {{waypoint -245, 0, -72}}
1. Purchase 10 **basic coal** from a merchant. The nearest choice would be [[Ulgofar Wisme]] inside the banker building at Outsiders' Landing. {{waypoint -68, -2, 134}}
1. Return to [[Spark Augren]] for your reward. (Make sure you still have all of the items in your inventory, including the Basic Coal.)

## Rewards
