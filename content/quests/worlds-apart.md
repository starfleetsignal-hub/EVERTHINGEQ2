## Steps

1. Find the Shade of the Great Tiger near the rim of the crater of the Hole. {{waypoint 1963, -123, 3450}}
   - *Note: She can be found at the top of the ramp leading down to the western lift.*
1. Destroy 6 lingering undead near the crater rim. {{waypoint 1857, -107, 3458}}
   - *Note: [[A lingering Paineel guard|lingering Paineel guard]]s and [[A lingering Paineel spirit|lingering Paineel spirit]]s provide updates.*
1. Meet the Shade of the Great Tiger near the Petrified Badlands.{{waypoint 1876, -14, 3962}}.
1. Destroy 6 Elementals I find in the Petrified Badlands.{{waypoint 1707, -8, 4053}}
   - *Note: [[A wayward stonevine|wayward stonevine]]s , [[A boulder dasher|boulder dasher]]s and [[A summoned bastion|summoned bastion]]s provide updates.*
1. Seek out the Shade of the Great Tiger near the Toxxulia Forest. {{waypoint 849, 33, 2982}}.
1. Destroy 6  [[A malignant seedling|malignant seedling]]s near Toxxulia Mound. {{waypoint 592, 113, 2963}}
   - *Note: they can be found just up the trail to the east..*
1. Return to [[Shalih Mar]] near the portal on the great spire Island. {{waypoint 2047, -237, 2684}}
