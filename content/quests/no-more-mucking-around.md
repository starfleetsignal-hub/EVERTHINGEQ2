## Notes

- This quest is repeatable., however the quest journal does not indicate this.

## Steps

1. Complete the following steps at [[The Pool of Devotion]]: {{waypoint 67, -6, -99}}
   - Clear out (click) on 4 *neglectful mucks* that are hopping around the pool.
   - Defeat [[A wandering eye]], which spawns in the middle of the pool after clearing out at least two *neglectful mucks*.
     - The muck despawns when a person clicks on it, so you will have to wait for enough mucks to repop if doing this in a group.
