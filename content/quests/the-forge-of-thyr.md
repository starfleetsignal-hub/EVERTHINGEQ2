## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[A blacksmith of Thyr|blacksmiths of Thyr]] in the forge area of Solusek's Eye.
   - Only two spawn at a time, and the respawn rate is pretty long (~20 min).
   - Placeholders for the blacksmiths are [[Weaponsmith Thaydor]] and [[Armorsmith Gravik]].

## Rewards

- At least 6g 19s 1c
