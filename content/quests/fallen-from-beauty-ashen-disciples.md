*This quest in unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Jin Li]] in the other end of [[The Pillars of Flame]], northwest of [[The Sun Spires]]. {{waypoint 1550, -114, -1200}}
1. Kill the encounter that spawns directly east of Jin Li around {{waypoint 1515, 0, -1205}}. The encounter consists of a solo harpy named [[Erilis Windsister]] and some adds (level 57). Be careful of the heroic mobs that patrol this area.
1. Return to [[Shing Ho]].
