## Steps

1. Talk to Ole' Man Silvers at top of the ramp from the Butcherblock Docks, at the [[Highland Outposts]]. {{waypoint 320, 190, 425}}
1. Get your character to adventuring level 32, then meet Silvers at the road to Greater Faydark.In Butcherblock Mountains {{waypoint -1018, 99, -423}}
1. Meet Ole'Man Silvers in [[Steamfont Mountains]] near the wall leading up to the [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]].
