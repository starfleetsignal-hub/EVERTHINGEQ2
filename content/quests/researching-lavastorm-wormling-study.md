## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[A magma wormling|magma wormlings]]. {{waypoint -148, -129, 477}}
1. Speak to [[Vima Madrigan]] in the [[Shrine of Thunder]]. {{waypoint -143, -117, 632}}
1. Brush away a pile of sand on the beach in [[Magma Grotto]]. {{waypoint -176, -128.7, 455}}![pile of sand](images/Pile_of_sand.jpg)
1. Pick up the baby magma wormling that was under the pile of sand.![baby magma wormling](images/Baby_magma_wormling.jpg)
1. Return to [[Vima Madrigan]] at the Shrine of Thunder.
