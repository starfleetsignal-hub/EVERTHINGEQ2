## Materials Required / Notes

- 10 [[Coruscating Coal]]
- Suggested: [[Portable Work Bench]] or summon crafting stations AA.

## Steps

1. Craft an [[Advanced Lightning Tracker Mach I]] on a [[Work Bench]]. (You must do this first before you get the update to start step #2)
   - Note: you will need a total of four (4) [[Advanced Lightning Tracker Mach I]].  Three (3) more are required for Step 3.
1. Gather the following: (note: must be harvested in Aurelian Coast to get updates. Pulling from broker or bringing them with you doesn't count)
   - 25 [[Splitfin Dartfish]]
   - 32 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
   - 24 [[Humming Azurite]]
1. Craft an [[Advanced Lightning Tracker Mach II]] on a [[Work Bench]]. (NOTE: craft two more while you're at it. You'll need them on follow-up quest)
1. Test the [[Advanced Lightning Tracker Mach II]]. Must be in Aurelian Coast

## Rewards

- At least 14p 21g 53s 73c 26,666 status
- At least 14p 21g 53s 73c 25,772 status at Level 125
