## Steps

1. Travel to [[Mistmoore Catacombs]]. {{waypoint 297, -6, 429}}
1. Kill the following monsters:
   - 10 [[A blightbeast|Blightbeasts]] (lowest level of the catacombs, in the back)
   - 10 [[A bloodbat|Bloodbats]] (near zone-in from Loping Plains. Only four are up at one time.)
1. Return to Moldosk.

## Rewards

- At least 16g 49s 60c
