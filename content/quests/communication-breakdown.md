## Steps

1. Collect the components for the airship fuel from Innothule Swamp
   - Kill 4 [[Dusky gold fauna stalker|dusky gold fauna stalkers]] {{waypoint -43, -86, -480}}, {{waypoint 76, -63, 296}}, {{waypoint 322, 53, 357}}, {{waypoint 307, 67, 403}} or  {{waypoint 99.42, -55.40, 305.90}}(autospawns when you get close and are invisible until aggroed, respawn is roughly 1 minute) and loot the [[Dusky gold fauna stalker dust]].
   - Collect 5 coal from the path to the [[Brokenskull Loft]], large dark colored rocks at the base of gray rock formations<br>
     - {{waypoint 106, -53, 291}}
     - {{waypoint 196, -16, 256}}
     - {{waypoint 251, 20, 316}}
     - {{waypoint 309, 34, 314}}
     - {{waypoint 312, 73, 415}}
1. Return to [[Grunty]]

## Notes

- The [[Dusky gold fauna stalker|dusky gold fauna stalkers]] will continue to spawn even after you have looted all of the dust required for this quest. They will only stop spawning after the quest has been completed.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2,000 faction with **Clan Grobb**
