A gnome explorer suggested that if I wish to proceed, I better make sure nothing behind me remains alive. Except for the gnome, of course.

## Steps

1. Slay [[Svacklan (Fabled)|Svacklan]]
1. Defeat [[Deepguardian Kvaklin (Fabled)|Deepguardian Kvaklin]]
1. Extinguish the [[Spirit of Water (Fabled)|Spirit of Water]]
1. Dissect the [[The Devourer (Fabled)|Devourer]]

## Notes

- This quest is only available in the Fabled version of the instance. You must be at least level 95 to enter.
- Unlike the original version of this instance, the ravasect will not respawn.

## Rewards

- 5 Draconic Etyma
- At least 1p 55g
