## Steps

![void-laced kaborite](images/Void-laced_kaborite.jpg)

1. Find a specimen of void-laced kaborite within [[The Eye of El'Arad]] (the small island on the right off the main one)
   - The *void-laced kaborite* spawns randomly on or around one of the desks, and has a very quick respawn time (about 30 seconds).
     - {{waypoint -1071, 744, -453}}
     - {{waypoint -1094, 745, -463}}
     - {{waypoint -1099, 744, -455}}
1. Return to [[Tawfiq En'Tal]].
