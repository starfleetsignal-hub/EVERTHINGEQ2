## Steps

1. Harvest the following:
   - 18 [[Nimbic hide]] from [[Basilisk lair]]
   - 15 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Coalesced reef]]
   - 15 [[Silvered fleck]] from [[Flecked mass]]
   - 10 [[Strangler root]] from [[Strangler root break]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Newly Tipped Quills]] (The recipe gets automatically put in your recipe book now.)
1. Craft the [[Newly Tipped Quills]] on a [[Work Bench]].
1. Speak to [[Eifae Goldthorn]] at {{waypoint 46.68, -13.58, -5.58}}.
