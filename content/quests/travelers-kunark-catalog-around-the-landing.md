## Steps

1. Travel to [[Kylong Plains]] and use the [[Catalogue Creature]] ability to catalog the following:
   - Catalog [[A drachnid enthraller]] around {{waypoint 251.73, 41.50, 188.63}} (in a cave, entrance at {{waypoint 378.91, 73.43, 104.18}})
   - Catalog [[A drachnid bloodknight]] around {{waypoint 358.57, 45.65, 7.63}}
   - Catalog [[A hulking brute]] around {{waypoint 427.19, 22.06, -706.73}}
   - Catalog [[A drolvarg gnasher]] around {{waypoint 291.88, 54.28, -176.05}}
   - Catalog [[A drolvarg rager]] around {{waypoint 459.39, 65.35, -277.90}}
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]

## Hints

The creatures are trackable. If you don't have tracking, many ranger mercs can track, for instance the [[Villax Sned]] merc from the VoV expansion.

## Rewards

- Unlock the Days of Summer 2022: Week 1 Holiday event rewards that you can purchase from [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2293, -199, 2506}}
