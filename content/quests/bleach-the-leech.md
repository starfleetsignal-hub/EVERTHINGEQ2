## Steps

1. Clean the leeches off 4 alligators in the pen
   - Use [[Grobb's Anti-Leech Treatment]] on 4 [[Confined gator|confined gators]] and kill the [[Alligator leech]] for update. Be careful or you might aggro the confined crocodiles at the same time.
1. Return to [[Groluk]]

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2,000 faction with **Clan Grobb**
