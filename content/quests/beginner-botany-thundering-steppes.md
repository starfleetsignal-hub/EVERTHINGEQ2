## Steps

1. Travel to [[Thundering Steppes]] and gather the following in order:
   1. 3 Dock Seaweed -- around  {{waypoint -518.58, -36.63, -376.13}} (under water south of the dock)
   1. 1 Thundermist thatching -- around {{waypoint 585.06, 1.30, -161.20}}  (Thundermist village)
   1. 3 Ruins of Karana deadwood {{waypoint -508, -8, 441}} (around the dead forest trees)
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].

## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable

## Rewards

- Unlock the [[Days of Summer: 2023|Days of Summer 2023: Week 7]] Holiday event rewards that you can purchase from [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2293, -199, 2506}}
