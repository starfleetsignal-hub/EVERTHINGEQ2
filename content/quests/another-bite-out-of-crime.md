## Notes

- This is a repeatable quest.

## Steps

*I suspect there's more stolen gnomish digging equipment hidden within the Darkpaw Warrens. There's a crate for me to deposit the recovered equipment in, located near Outlander Deston of the Qeynos Guard, in Darkpaw Dugout.*

1. Locate stolen gnomish digging equipment (either 10 automated rockpickers or 10 mechanical components) in Darkpaw Warren
   - Some possible automated rockpicker locations:
     - {{waypoint -10, -4, -170}}
     - {{waypoint -27, -13, -116}}
     - {{waypoint -20, 0, 87}}
     - {{waypoint 25, -17, 192}}
     - {{waypoint 46, -17, 211}}
   - Some possible mechanical component locations:
     - {{waypoint 65, -2, 181}}
     - {{waypoint -25, -12, -140}}
     - {{waypoint 4, -1, 10}}
     - {{waypoint -15, 3, -12}}
     - {{waypoint -6, 1, 148}}
     - {{waypoint 60, -16, 199}}
     - {{waypoint 89, 3, 173}}
     - {{waypoint 14, 3, 206}}
   - Found in Sabertooth Burrow, Mudpaw Burrow, Dustpaw Burrow, and Rockpaw Burrow
   - These items may also respawn in a locked instance after some time (1-2 hours?)
1. Return to Darkpaw Dugout, and deposit the equipment in one of the crates near Outlander Deston {{waypoint 6, -77, -235}}

## Reward

- At least 2p 15g 55s 7c
- One of the following:
  - [[Gnollish Chair]]
  - [[Gnollish Chest]]
