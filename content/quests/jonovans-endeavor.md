## Steps

Jonovan sends you out to kill 10 spiders, bees, beavers, bats, faeries, dusk darts, wisps, treants, zombies, gargoyles or bears in the Nektulos Forest.

The reward is minor coin and experience. This is a repeatable kill task. After doing this kill task a massive number of times (reported at 27) the [[A Wicked Entity in Nektulos]] quest will open up.

- Minor coin
