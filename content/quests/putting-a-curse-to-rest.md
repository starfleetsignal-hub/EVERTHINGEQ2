## Steps

1. Enter [[Befallen: Cavern of the Afflicted]] and put an end to 2 Maledictive Terrors
   - {{waypoint 15, 2, -59}}
   - {{waypoint 38, -64, -142}}
1. Dismiss the frightful darkenings by clicking on the symbol in the water at {{waypoint 109, -60, -83}}.
1. Return to Friar Arlam Va'orr.

## Rewards

- At least 31g 73s 78c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Buckler of Fallen Valor]]
  - [[Relic of Memorial]]
