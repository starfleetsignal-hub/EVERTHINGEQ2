## Notes

Must complete the repeatable quest [[Relentless Defender]] from any [[A Flamebearer]] at least once to get this quest from Andreas

## Steps

1. Upon accepting the quest Andreas dies. Click his body to try to revive him, only instead of reviving him you get pulled into Arcanum Visualization zone where he is held in a swirl of light.
1. Search the Arcanum Visualization for what it has to reveal - kill 3 dragons' echoes.
1. Inspect Vrakorr Rune Claw in the middle {{waypoint 34, 0, 0}}
1. Defeat [[The Wielder]], who spawns.
1. Try to revive Andreas again (this zones you out of Arcanum Visualization)
1. Speak to Andreas back in Antonica

## Rewards

- At least 1p 94g 90s 62c
- 10 x [[Brass Lambent]]

One of the following:

- [[Defensive Dragon's Echo Plushie]]
- [[Protective Dragon's Echo Plushie]]
