## Steps

1. Follow the waypoint to the forge in Temple Street {{waypoint 10, 4, 60}}.
1. Right-click and inspect the forge.
1. Throw the spider in the forge.
1. Borrow a pair of tongs located near the back wall for the forge.
1. Right-click to remove the spider from the forge.
1. Return to [[Fedul]].

## Rewards

- At least 1s 40c
