## Notes

- This quest requires:
  - 1x [[Whittling Knife]]
  - 10x [[Zimaran Coal]]
- when you start the quest, you'll receive an auto-scribed [[Recipe Scroll: Knitting Needles|recipe]] for [[Knitting Needles]] (uses [[Straight Sticks]], [[Whittling Knife]] and 5 [[Zimaran Coal]])

### How to achieve +20,000 Faction standing with Port Woe

- **Crafting way**
  - *7 crafting quests open up to reach that. Each quest for +1000 faction can be repeated only 3 times and **only if your faction is under 20,000.**  These quests will be enough to reach 20,000 faction.*
  - +1,000 [[Meet and Greet: Baskets and Plates]]
  - +1,000 [[Meet and Greet: Clamming Up]]
  - +1,000 [[Meet and Greet: Dockside Repairs]]
  - +1,000 [[Meet and Greet: Flavorful Food]]
  - +1,000 [[Meet and Greet: Hand in Glove]]
  - +1,000 [[Meet and Greet: Knitting? Netting?]]
  - +2,000 [[Meet and Greet: Lost and Found]]  (not repeatable)
- **Adventure way**
  - *To reach the required 20,000 faction with Port Woe you can also do many quests from the Adventure Timeline, for example:*
  - +3,000 [[Fighting the Biters Back]]
  - +3,000 [[Shelled Sustenance]]
  - +5,000 [[Case of the Pinched Pickling]]
  - +3,000 [[Walking on Broken Glass]]
  - +5,000 [[Leave the Thievin' to Professionals]]
  - +2,000 [[Getting the Goods Back]]

## Steps

1. Craft Knitting needles
   - obtain the following items to craft the [[Knitting Needles]]
     - Pickup [[Straight Sticks]] around {{waypoint 14, 6, 381}}  from sparkling pile of sticks (respawn about 10 seconds)
     - Buy the [[Whittling Knife]] from [[Kassa]], the fuel merchant in the same hut as [[Ameela Ziad]]
     - Craft the [[Knitting Needles]].
   - *You receive a bonus Quest reward at this point, an appearance weapon [[Crafter's Makeshift Knitting Needle]]*
1. Talk to seven locals around the village *They all have the quest update flag over their heads*
   - Speak with [[Grimar]] at {{waypoint -30.93, 4.94, 369.68}}
   - Speak with [[Ismara]] at {{waypoint 87.82, 5.35, 307.63}}
   - Speak with [[Garron]] at {{waypoint -31.41, 5.16, 357.99}}
   - Speak with [[Polonia]] at {{waypoint 20.45, 5.71, 372.30}}
   - Speak with [[Barsten]] at {{waypoint -77, 7, 363}}
   - Speak with [[Dunstad]] at {{waypoint -75.42, 6.45, 334.59}}
   - Speak with [[Zolea]] at {{waypoint -59.46, 9.33, 373.71}}
1. Speak to [[Ameela Ziad]] {{waypoint 31, 6, 354}}
   - You receive auto scribed [[Recipe Scroll: W.H.A.T. V1|recipe]] for [[W.H.A.T. V1]]
     - Harvest Magic-Infused Plant {{waypoint 81, 8, 268}} for [[Magic-Riddled Plant]] *(respawn approximately 15 seconds)*
     - Gather Magic-Infused Waters {{waypoint 44, 4, 302}} for [[Magic-Infused Waters]] *(respawn approximately 25 seconds)*
1. Craft a [[W.H.A.T. V1]]
   - needs your [[W.H.A.T. Prototype]], [[Magic-Riddled Plant]] and [[Magic-Infused Waters]], and 5 [[Zimaran Coal]].*
   - *Note: Once you crafted this do not destroy it, you will need it later to craft a better version.*
1. Speak to [[Ameela Ziad]] {{waypoint 31, 6, 354}}
1. Gain [[Wilfric]]'s approval.
   - When you have at least +20,000 faction with Port Woe speak with [[Wilfric]] {{waypoint -92, 9, 346}}
     - He completes this quest and suggests you should report back to Ameela

## Rewards

- Receive [[Crafter's Makeshift Scissors]] (Appearance item)
- [[Status Coin (75,000)|Status Coin [75,000]]]
- At least  6p 26g 6s 44c
