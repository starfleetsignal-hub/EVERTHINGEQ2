***This is a Sub-Quest of [[Coin for the Captain]]***

## Steps

Be sure to read the commands of the captain carefully as they are worded exactly.

1. Speak with [[Bimini Bowchaser]] at the [[Butcherblock Dock]] {{waypoint 668.23, 22.00, 566.50}}.
   - Tell him that the captain wants her to "flemish down the boat falls an' be sure all the lines are coiled correctly."
1. Speak with [[Mizzen Staysail]] at the [[Butcherblock Dock]] {{waypoint 716.01, 24.00, 613.82}}.
   - Tell him to "knock off the bright work an' clamp down the deck."
1. Speak with [[Tally Topgallant]] at the [[Butcherblock Dock]] {{waypoint 604.36, 59.44, 607.54}}.
   - Tell her to "lash up an' stow 'afore you batten down the hatches."
1. Return to [[Captain Stonnacky]]

## Rewards

- At least 17s 43c
