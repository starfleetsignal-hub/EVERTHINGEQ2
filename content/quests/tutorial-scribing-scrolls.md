## Description

"The tradeskill tutor has asked me to try my hand at sagecraft." --*Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Craft a [[Shackle|Shackle (Journeyman)]] scroll using your sage skills
   - buy [[Basic Incense]] to fuel the [[Engraved Desk]]
   - You will need 1 [[Rough malachite (Crate Reward)|rough malachite]], 2 [[Root (harvest)|root]], and 2 [[Severed elm]].
1. Return to the instructor

## Rewards

- At least 15c
- [[Artisan essentials volume 6 (Uncommon)|artisan essentials volume 6]]

## Dialogue

- Tradeskill Tutorial NPC: "Sages possess an unsurpassed knowledge of the arcane. They apply this knowledge by scribing spells onto scrolls that increase the might of healers and mages. Create a Scroll of Shackle and bring it to me."
- Player Character: "I'll start now."

I must follow the tutor's instructions to review the crafting process. I need to create a spell scroll of Shackle (Journeyman) using the engraved desk.

I have created a spell scroll of Shackle (Journeyman) I have reviewed the process of crafting an item. I should return to the tutor with my work. I should return to the tutor with the scroll of Shackle.

- Tradeskill Tutorial NPC: "Excellent work. Now let's look at alchemists; they create deadly poisons and magic potions from almost any material, benign or otherwise. These concoctions provide benefits in battle, such as healing, resistance, or extra damage against a foe. Alchemists also make combat art upgrades for fighter types to improve their abilities in battle. Make me an Essence of Intercept to practice these skills."

I returned to the tutor with the scroll I made. The tutor was pleased with my progress.

I created the scroll as instructed by the tutor.
