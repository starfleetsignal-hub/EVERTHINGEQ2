## Steps

1. Craft a [[Dynamist condenser]] on a [[Work Bench]] (one can be found at {{waypoint 2226, 488, -684}}).
   - Requires:
     - 3 [[Mana infused alloy]] gathered from the tables in the buildings in [[Teren's Grasp]]. The nodes look like glowing rocks.
     - 1 [[Iron cluster]]
     - 1 [[Tanned leather pelt]]
     - 1 [[Electrum Cluster]]
     - 3 [[Glowing Coal]]
1. Gather 6 [[Solid dynamist particulates]] by using the [[Dynamist condenser]] on [[A dynamist spren]].
   - *Note: Has about a 1 in 3 to 1 in 6 return rate. Be sure to click on the 'a solid dynamist' if you get one.*
1. Return to [[Grommit Fizzlewitz]].

## Rewards

- 1 [[Deepwater Copper]].
