## Steps

1. Receive an [[Empty Bottle]] from Mushien Lou
1. Fill the bottle with sacred water flowing with the energy of the sacred tiger from the waterfall {{waypoint -298, 241, 2981}} (right-click), then return to Mushien with the [[Filled Bottle]].

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +2,000 faction with **Hua Mein**
