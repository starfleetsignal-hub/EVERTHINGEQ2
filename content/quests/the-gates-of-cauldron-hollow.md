## Notes

- This was the former access quest for [[The Cauldron Hollow]].

## Steps

1. Find a book about Cauldron Hollow as mentioned by [[Arqis]]. The book can be found in the following locations:
   - at the Academy in North Freeport {{waypoint 11, -7, -128}}
   - in the Three Towers in South Qeynos, opposite from Sage Indis Surion {{waypoint 708, 82, 127}}(yellow portal)
   - at the Library of K'Lorn in Neriak {{waypoint -686, 18, 248}}
   - at Research Library in Kelethin {{waypoint 137, 108, 175}}
     - Right click the book and inspect it to get a list of books in this stack.
   1. Read the [[Vanishing of Cauldron Hollow]] book.
1. Return to [[Arqis]] in Nektulos Forest {{waypoint -283, 0, 172}}.
1. Speak with a fairy named [[Dalninia]] on the island in the Torrent River in [[Nektulos Forest]] {{waypoint -198, 0, -343}}.
1. Get [[Dalninia]] to help you by proving yourself to [[Queen Thayilia]], queen of the Asilian fairies in the [[Nektulos Forest]] {{waypoint -499, 2, -292}}.
1. [[Queen Thayilia]] ( -481, 4, -303) will ask you to slay [[Queen Dyleena]] (Level 29^), ruler of the rival Sullian faeries, found near Bone Lake {{waypoint -742, 145, -2199}}.
   - ***Note:** Attacking [[Queen Dyleena]]  will summon multiple [[Sullian Royal Defenders]] (Level 28) to her aid.  The defenders are not linked to Queen Dyleena, and do not have to be killed for this quest.*
1. Return to [[Queen Thayilia]] to let her know that [[Queen Dyleena]] has been dealt with.
1. Now, with [[Queen Thayilia]]'s alliance return to [[Dalninia]].
1. Enter the Funeral Pyre {{waypoint -960, 12, -715}} and get a quest update.  The Knights of the Beacon will spawn and attack when you enter the Pyre.
   - If you are doing this in a group, then each group member must enter the Funeral Pyre to receive an update before killing the Knights, or they will not receive credit.
1. Kill the Knights of the Beacon (level 27) that spawned when you entered the Pyre. There are 3 of them and they are between vv and v.
1. Return to [[Arqis]].
1. Visit the Treant named [[Rulinthus]], found in the far north end of Nektulos {{waypoint 2, 89, -1977}}, near [[The Obelisk of Lost Souls]] portal and the druid ring.
1. Kill [[Tilberbous]] (Level 29^), found just south of the Funeral Pyre {{waypoint -1051, -2, -581}}.
1. Return to [[Rulinthus]].

## Rewards

- [[Twig of Rulinthus]], examinable quest starter for [[Cauldron Hollow: Lighting the Torches of Forewarning]].
