## Description

After a run in with a crumbling tomb guard down here in the Vermin's Snye, I found pieces to a chair I believe can be fixed with some replacement parts.

## Steps

1. Locate parts with which to rebuild the chair. All parts drop from [[A crumbling tomb guard|crumbling tomb guards]]. The drop rate is ~33% and it's possible to get multiple parts from the same mob.
   - Look for chair legs and a back
   - Look for more chair legs and a seat
   - Look for a chair cushion

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable., therefore you can get as many of the chairs as you would like!

## Rewards

- [[A tomb guard chair]] - a house item
- At least 4s 13c
