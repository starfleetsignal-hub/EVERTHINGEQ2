## Notes

- This is a repeatable quest.

## Steps

*Nisch Mal Nolphia asked me to return to the Darkpaw Warrens and free more captives from the Underpaw gnolls.*

1. Collect Underpaw shackle keys and free 5 captive Splitpaw gnolls
   - Keys drop from a small chest from any group containing living Underpaw gnolls.  1 drop per group member.
   - Use the keys to free captive Splitpaw gnolls in Underpaw Stronghold {{waypoint 79, -25, 76}} - this is a group update
1. Speak with Nisch Mas Nolphia in Darkpaw Dugout {{waypoint 4, -76, -275}}

## Rewards

- At least 3p 36g 79s 77c
