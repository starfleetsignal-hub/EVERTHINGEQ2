## Notes

- requires at least 30,100 [[Pride Pakiat (Faction)]]

## Steps

1. Travel to {{waypoint 608, 108, 826}} and use [[Starfire Meditation Beads]] from your inventory.
1. Travel to {{waypoint 423, -153, 940}} and use [[Starfire Meditation Beads]].
1. Travel to {{waypoint 623, 187, 297}} and use [[Starfire Meditation Beads]].
   - Note: This location is on one of the floating islands
1. Travel to {{waypoint 62, 138, -80}} and use [[Starfire Meditation Beads]].
   - Receive [[Essence of Mind]] after final mediation
1. Examine [[Essence of Mind]] in inventory and choose 'Hold the Element tightly and concentrate.' to receive [[Attuned Essence of Mind]].
1. Return to [[Khat-Ra Pakiat]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Starfire Crystal]] × 2
- -1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
