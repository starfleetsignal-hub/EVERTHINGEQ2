## Steps

1. Enter the [[Tombs of Night]]
1. Obtain a dark crystal by killing [[Ruen T'Dax]] at {{waypoint -8, -24, 63}}  Note:  If he is not there and you see a blank wall, you will have to reset the instance.
1. Obtain a vial of noble blood by killing [[Vieya N'Larn]] at {{waypoint -100, -24, -17}}
1. Enter [[The Crypt of T'haen]] {{waypoint -107, -32, 102}} (not [[The Crypt of T'Haen: Vengeance]]).
1. Find and examine a dark altar. The altar looks like a lamp post. {{waypoint -107, 26, -6}}
1. Return to [[Laren Jadefire]].
1. Speak to [[K'shar Haelbreng]] for your reward.

## Rewards

At least 1g 59s 57c
