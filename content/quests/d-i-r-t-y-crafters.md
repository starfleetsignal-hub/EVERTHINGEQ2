## Notes

- You must be a level 20 Crafter (of any type) for the quest to be offered.
- No physical items are crafted.  Continuing to craft beyond the required 10 is a waste of resources
- *You tuck away the final product for safe keeping* is the default message when the item is completed, whether it updates the quest or not.
- Turning in the quest erases the recipe from your book.  You will have to scribe the recipe again if you repeat the quest.

## Steps

1. Zastik Togginscog gives you a recipe based on your tradeskill class.  Scribe it.
1. Craft 10 of the item (resources and fuel needed change based on your crafting level):
   - Alchemist: [[Odorous Foul Fragrance]]s
   - Armorsmith: [[Excavation Hat]]s
   - Carpenter: [[Wheelbarrow]]s or mast riggings
   - Jeweler: [[Book Clasps]]
   - Provisioner: [[Meaty Rice Entree (M.R.E.)|Meaty Rice Entrees (M.R.E.s)]]
   - Sage: [[Ream of Paper|Reams of paper]]
   - Tailor: [[Fresh Cloths]]
   - Weaponsmith: [[Shovel Tip]]s
   - Woodworker: [[Shovel Handle]]s
1. Return to Zastik Togginscog.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- At least 5g
- 2 [[D.I.R.T.Y. Money]]
