## Steps

1. Find 4 pieces of discarded armor around the Order of Rime camps: {{waypoint -385.77, -102.08, 1153.12}}, {{waypoint -358, -89, 1382}}, and {{waypoint -217.70, -399.32, 870.27}}.
1. Kill 2 [[An invading shardhammer (Fens of Nathsar)|invading shardhammers]], found around the camps.
1. Speak with [[Sliza Xarezia]] in [[Riliss (POI)|Riliss]]. {{waypoint -861, 58, -179}}

- At least 36g 79s 48c
- [[An opened crate]]

  - [[Nathsarian Spiked Strap of Energy]] (All Mages)
  - [[Nathsarian Spiked Strap of Faith]] (All Priests)
  - [[Nathsarian Spiked Strap of Stealth]] (All Scouts)
