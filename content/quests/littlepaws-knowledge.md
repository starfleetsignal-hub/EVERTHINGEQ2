## Steps

1. Speak to [[Littlepaw]] at {{waypoint -53, 53, 177}}.
1. Place the [[Littlepaw's Harrowing Horde Harrow|harrows]] at:
   - {{waypoint 26, 61, 163}}
   - {{waypoint -39, 48, 240}}
   - {{waypoint -129, 43, 116}}
1. Return to [[Littlepaw]].
1. Go to [[Venox Tarkog]] near the Sanctuary and speak to him to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Censer of the Lost Soul]] (priest)
  - [[Aetherplate Shield]] (plate tank)
  - [[Celestial Balance]] (plate tank & scouts)
  - [[Claws of the Harrowing Sirocco]] (brawler)
  - [[Symbol of Sorrow's End]] (mage)
