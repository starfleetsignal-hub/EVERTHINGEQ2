- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Right click on the door next to the quest giver and select the option to go 'through the door'. You will enter the instance [[The Shattered Vale: A Bold Confrontation]].
1. Kill all [[A Sabertooth curate|Sabertooth curates]].
1. Kill [[Enraged Elemental Being]].
1. Kill [[High Shaman Vascha]].
