## Steps

![](images/Bottle_of_Fresh_Ink.jpg)
![Myrist Access Ledger](images/Myrist_Access_Ledger.jpg)

1. Click the Myrist Acess Ledger at {{waypoint 62, -19, -29}}
1. Speak to [[Virlana Levora]] at {{waypoint 64, -20, -29}}
1. Go to the [[Myrist Conservatory]] near {{waypoint 46, -20, -75}}
1. Click on a [[Newly Pressed Paper]] in the room.
   - Kill the [[Wild gardening]] that spawns
1. Collect 7 more [[Newly Pressed Paper]].
1. Speak to [[Virlana Levora]] at {{waypoint 64, -20, -29}}
1. Go to [[Myrist Commisary]] near {{waypoint 48, -21, 48}} and collect 3 bottles of fresh ink. (mostly along back wall)
1. Speak to [[Virlana Levora]] at {{waypoint 64, -20, -29}}
1. Click the Myrist Access Ledger at {{waypoint 62, -19, -29}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +1000 faction with **Scholars of Myrist**
- [[Tanaanite Spellwoven Armguards]] - Mercenary Forearms
- [[Essence of Dust]] -- *collection item*
