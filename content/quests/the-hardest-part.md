## Steps

1. Hraen Vutha tells you to go about your business until Ishka contacts you. After a few minutes, read the letter you will receive in your in-game mail and accept the item.
   - There's a mailbox in [[Kunzar's Edge (POI)|Kunzar's Edge]].
1. Return to Hraen.
