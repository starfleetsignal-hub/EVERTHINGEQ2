## Description

The Far Seas Trading Company has hired a mercenary by the name of Stromur to slaughter a large number of Brokenskull lookouts, but he's got other business to take care of, most likely in a pub, so I'm taking on his take myself.

## Steps

Slaughter Brokenskull pirate lookouts

- Kill 30 [[A Brokenskull pirate lookout|Brokenskull pirate lookouts]].
