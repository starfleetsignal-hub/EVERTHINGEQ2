## Notes

- This quest was the original access quest for the [[Fallen Dynasty Timeline]]. It's completion is no longer necessary to access the timeline or the [[Isle of Mara]].

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Feerrott]].
1. Approach the rowboat to the left of the [[The Drowning Causeway|Drowning Causeway]] to recieve an update. {{waypoint 72, -7, -111}}
1. Examine the rowboat to zone into [[The Far Pelican (Instance)|The Far Pelican]].
   - You cannot zone into the instance if you are grouped with a character (including a mercenary) that is not on the same quest.
1. Kill the Pirates of Gunthak aboard the ship.
1. Kill [[Deputy Surestout]] and [[Bizzy]] that spawn once all the pirates are dead.
1. Speak with [[Captain Bobick]].

## Rewards

- Formerly, safe passage to the [[Isle of Mara]].
- [[The Far Pelican (Item)]]
