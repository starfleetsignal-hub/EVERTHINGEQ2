## Steps

![stirge egg](images/Stirge_egg.jpg)
![Click the rock to spawn the highland crawlers](images/Highland_crawler_rock.jpg)

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Collect 10 stirge eggs in the fields West of the dwarven outpost.
     - The eggs are [[Ground spawn]] at the base of the trees in the wooded area just outside [[West Fort Irontoe]].
   - Collect 10 pieces of meat by killing [[Highland crawler|highland crawlers]] West of the dwarven outpost.
     - To spawn the crawlers, you must click the small rocks that they are hiding under near the larger boulders that surround the wooded area, just outside [[West Fort Irontoe]].
     - The crawlers range from lvl 23 to 25
1. Return to [[Verung Kae'Rush]]

## Rewards

- At least 30s 78c
- +1000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
