## Steps

1. Collect scout reports. The first two scouts will be shown on the map with a quest completion icon (a flashing book), so they're easy to find. They wander a bit, but approximate locations are:
   - Petrified Badlands: {{waypoint 1510, -40, 3710}}
   - Toxxulia's Forest: {{waypoint 944.85, -51.65, 3466.59}}
   - Kejaan's Rill: {{waypoint 320, 224, 3710}}
   - The third scout is actually missing and will not appear on your map. You *must* find the first two patrols *before* the third will trigger an update in Kejaan's Rill.
   - There is a climb point at {{waypoint 701.40, 7.90, 3391.30}} in Toxxulia's Forest that can help you get to Kejaan's Rill faster if you don't have a flying mount.
   - If you're on a flying mount and don't get an update, dismount and walk around the area.
1. Return to [[Masih Najjar]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
