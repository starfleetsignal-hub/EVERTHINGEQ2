## Steps

1. Enter the city of [[Maldura]] {{waypoint 260.54, 150.94, 483.56}} in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]].
1. Speak to 3 citizens in Maldura to learn about the city and its enemies:
   - [[Gulmar]] in the [[Metal Mercantile District]] {{waypoint -127.01, -2.74, -79.62}} by the healers.
   - [[Brulsael]] in the [[The Deep Quarry|Deep Quarry]] {{waypoint -33.57, 16.77, -195.26}}.
   - [[Frith Clatterstein]] in the [[Stone Mercantile District]] {{waypoint -176.26, -1.80, 137.47}} near the banker.
1. Speak to [[Dirvy Boomfield]] in the [[Alcove of Reason]] {{waypoint -54.58, 10.07, 130.5}}.
1. Speak with [[Graldek the Ironshadow]] in the [[Mushroom Bar and Grill]] {{waypoint -53.96, -8.48, -1.21}}.
1. Leave the city. Kill 12 undead [[A disturbed dhalgar|disturbed dhalgars]], [[An awoken gnemlin|awoken gnemlins]], or [[A wandering skeleton|wandering skeletons]] at [[Tranquil Halls]] in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]] {{waypoint 428.90, 167.60, 536.21}}. These are located just outside the city to the Southwest.
1. Return to [[Graldek the Ironshadow|Graldek]] in [[Maldura]].
1. Speak to [[Eccles Zaphardt]] near the [[Entrance to Maldura]] {{waypoint -199.56, -3.50, -27.62}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g 33394 status
- +1000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
