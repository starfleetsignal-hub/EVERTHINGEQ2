## Steps

1. Kill [[Silverpaw]], spawned by clicking a pile of rubble at {{waypoint 1365, 384, -792}} in [[Kylong Plains]] (This Loc is in the Yeti Caves just inside the first tunnel to the left if you come in by way of the south east entrance).  He will drop [[A calcified old ring]]
   - The named is 80 ^^^ Heroic, but can be soloed.  Stand as far to the left of the spawn point as possible, and click it.  Root the named as quickly as possible and burn him down, being sure to keep him rooted.
1. Examine the ring to clean it.  You will receive [[A faintly glowing sorcerer's band]].
1. Speak with [[Lianashanai Nadeu]] at {{waypoint 449, -34, -1455}} in [[Everfrost]]. - Just North of the Wizard Spires.
1. Visit the following statues:
   - Tarew Marr - Under the water in the sea near [[Coldwind Cove]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]] at {{waypoint 432, -88, 1438}}.
   - [["E'ci - the Wintry Guardian"]] - Near [[Ryjesium Peaks]] in [[Kylong Plains]] at {{waypoint 1517, -18, -17}}.
     - 3-4 [[Yha-lei desecrater|Yha-lei desecraters]] (77 heroic) will spawn and attack you. They appear exactly around the statue when it is clicked. Since the quest already updated, just evac to safety. Since the statue is clickable so far enough way, you can just click on it at max range and not even get attacked.
   - [["Povar - the Veiled One"]] - In the [[Loping Plains]], off the southwest coast of the [[The Timorous Moor]] at {{waypoint 181, -31, 559}}.
1. Return to [[Lianashanai Nadeu]] in [[Everfrost]].  You will receive the [[Cor Per'Edhel ed'Hegel]].
1. Return to [[Jaestin Chaldean]] in [[Maj'Dul]] - [[The Tower of the Moon]] to complete this quest.
