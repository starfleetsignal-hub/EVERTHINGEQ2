## Notes

- Requires decent Pride Pakiat faction (20k worked for me)
- If you fail this quest, there is a "cool down" period of about 5 to 10 minutes before you can try again.

## Steps

You have to fly around to each of the locations and find the books.  Listed below are the book names, their locations, and the clue the quest will give you.  Clicking the wrong book will spawn a mob.

- Into the breach - Half a drawing of a gate or portal
- Celestial Travels - Stars with naked eye
- Stormwaker an Autobiography - Burns Scar the Page
- Food Fit for the Planes - A page with a partial list of ingredients
- The Generals of War - orc quotes
- Studies on the Anatomy of Planar Beings - A cross section on the heart
- The Rights of Mortals - Legalese I can barely wrap my head around
- Theories on the convergence of realities - Folding existance an abstract
- Unnatural Disasters - Fall of nelodolan
- All things alchemical - Strange ingrediants for an even stranger tonic

Keep in mind that everytime you pick this quest books spawn randomly in these locations:
