## Steps

1. Remove 5 icegaze basilisk carcasses from [[Howling Pass]]
1. Place down 4 griffin repelling incense (along ridge above howling pass and on top of wizard tower and on top of bridge pole beside griffin pad)) by clicking on the red sparkles in [[Howling Pass]]
1. return to [[Carpus]]
