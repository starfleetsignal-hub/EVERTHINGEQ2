**NOTICE TO ALL ADVENTURERS** - *The [[The Far Seas Trading Company (Faction)|Far Seas Trading Company]] (FSTC) is seeking adventurers capable of fulfilling orders in [[The Thundering Steppes]]! Fun, Fame and easy money await you! Flexible hours, on the job training, and a generous compensation package! Apply in person to FSTC Representative [[Taskmaster Deldrin]] at the [[Dead River Docks]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]]!*

## Starting the Quests

1. Click on the orders desk near to [[Taskmaster Deldrin]] at {{waypoint -479, -21, -407}}.
1. Take one or all 6. See the details below. Fulfill the terms of the contracts and return the completed orders to [[Taskmaster Deldrin|Deldrin]].
1. Once you have completed all of the SCO's at least once you will have access to the Special Contracts.
