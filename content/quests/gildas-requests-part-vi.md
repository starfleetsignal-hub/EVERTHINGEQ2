## Steps

1. Collect a few specimens for Gildas in [[The Thundering Steppes]]:
   - Kill any 6 skeletons in the Thundering Steppes (for example [[A skeletal protector]] or [[A drowned trooper]] in [[Dead River Basin]] east of [[Bridge Keep]])
   - Kill 12 [[A Sabertooth harvester|Sabertooth harvesters]] (Northern part of the Griffon Field {{waypoint 1148, -10, -249}} L25 Heroic ^,^^^
   - Kill 10 rumblers near or in the caves at {{waypoint 965.84, -22.99, 1192.07}} and {{waypoint 749, -17.98, 1176.01}} on the SW shores. The [[An earth rumbler (Thundering Steppes)|earth rumblers]] outside the caves are L24 heroic ^^^. Once in the cave, there are L25 ^^ earth rumblers and L26 heroic ^^ [[A granite rumbler|granite rumblers]] that are easier. The [[An under rumbler|under rumblers]] in the [[The Rumbler Caves]] near the [[Village of Thundermist]] don't count.
   - Kill [[Celeg Ixuri]] near Varsoon entrance (triggered when you run near the entrance to [[The Ruins of Varsoon]]) Heroic 25^^^ with 2 friends
1. Return to Gildas for your reward.

- One of the following (depending on class):

  - [[Robe of the Befuddler]] - ([[Illusionist]])
  - [[Defiled Sanguine Robe]] - ([[Warlock]])
  - [[Spellbound Cerise Robe]] - ([[Wizard]])
  - [[Robe of Allurement]] - ([[Coercer]])
