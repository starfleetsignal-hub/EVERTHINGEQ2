## Steps

1. Enter [[The Crypt of T'haen: Endless Twilight]].
1. Find and destroy *a magnificent coffin* at {{waypoint 94, 56, -66}} from which it is said that [[T'Rath the Usurper]] receives his power.
   - **This step is optionial**, however if you don't destroy the coffin he starts healing at 35%.
   - To reach the coffin go to the narrow bridge at {{waypoint 0, 57, -59}} and cross the chasm. Take a left then follow the path to the room with dozens of coffins.
1. Destroy [[T'Rath the Usurper]] atop the central pillar at {{waypoint -7, 61, -26}}.
1. Return to [[Arthial the Protector]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Access to The Crypt of T'haen, and option to enter the raid instance, [[The Crypt of T'Haen: Vengeance|The Crypt of T'haen: Vengeance]].
- One of the following:
  - [[Honorary Chestplate of Marr]] (plate armor)
  - [[Honorary Hauberk of Marr]] (chain armor)
  - [[Honorary Tunic of Marr]] (leather armor)
  - [[Honorary Robe of Marr]] (cloth armor)
