## Steps

1. Gather 30 pieces of garbage in [[Antonica]]
   - Piles are found in Antonica just outside the city gates along the paths
1. You are then asked to donate to the Queen's favorite charity, the restoration of Nettleville.  You MUST have 1g to do this!  Go to [[Friar Montego]] in [[North Qeynos (POI)|North Qeynos]] {{waypoint 448, -20, -244}}, just past the mender and buy a receipt for your donation.
1. Return to [[Darion]] in South Qeynos.

## Reward

- +3000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -10000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
