## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable up to 10 times..*
- This quest can only be picked up again one hour in real time after completing the previous one; the one hour timer only starts when the previous quest is completed, not when it was acquired.

## Steps

1. Kill a number of the following mobs:
   - 5 [[An oculus illusionist|oculus illusionists]]
   - 5 [[An oculus charmer|oculus charmers]]
   - 10 [[A Runnyeye brewer|Runnyeye brewers]]
   - 10 [[A Runnyeye smith|Runnyeye smiths]]
   - 10 [[A clay remnant|clay remnants]]
   - 10 [[A savage warboar|savage warboars]]
   - 10 [[A Gorehorn pitmaster|Gorehorn pitmasters]]

## Rewards

- At least 1g 26s 57c
