## Steps

1. Speak to [[Mazni Nah'fal]]
1. Take Mazni's Effigy into combat with you
   1. Equip Mazni's Summoning Stone and summon Mazni's effigy.
   1. Test the combat skills of any 12 Rime mobs. Any Rime will give an update near the big wall by the Public Quest, I tried some mobs up north and did not get updates off of them. Stick to solo mobs - groups only give one (1) update, and aren't worth the effort.
1. Talk to Mazni for update
1. Speak to [[Mazni Nah'fal]] at [[Nipik's Haven]] (Eastern Wastes {{waypoint -1233, -538, 3158}})
1. Fight the Kromzek in Eastern Wastes
   - make sure you've equiped the Mazni's summoning stone, used it. Mazni's effigy must be up to make the kills count
   - If you have lost, sold or misplaced the effigy then you will need to delete the quest and you will need to speak to [[Mazni Nah'fal]] and re-do the quest.  (Information here: http://forums.station.sony.com/eq2/posts/list.m?topic_id=496881)
1. Speak to [[Mazni Nah'fal]] at [[Nipik's Haven]] (Eastern Wastes {{waypoint -1233, -538, 3158}})
