## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 8 [[A sand scourge|sand scourges]] at [[Saltscorch Steppes]] around {{waypoint 63.03, 67.11, -596.23}} and along the path heading west to the river. ![Ortallian Brimsalt & Blightswarm Eggs](images/Ortallian_Brimsalt_&_Blightswarm_Eggs.png)
   - Gather items for Ozraa. This changes, possible tasks include:
     - 12 **blightswarm eggs** at [[Tsmang Blightswarm Hive]] around {{waypoint 194.42, 76.26, -699.01}}
     - 12 **Ortallian brimsalt** at [[Rath'Mana]] around {{waypoint 384.83, 137.63, -403.25}}
1. Return to [[Ozraa]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
