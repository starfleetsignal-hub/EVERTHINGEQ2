See if you can obtain some extra-long fishing rods to help the Snowfang Gnolls fish from their high cliffs.

## Steps

1. Purchase [[Far Seas Trading Company Recipe: Extended Fishing Rod]] from [[Matthew]] for 4g 60s 80c.
1. Scribe the recipe and craft 5 [[Extended Fishing Rod|Extended Fishing Rods]] at the [[Forge]] located behind [[Matthew]] {{waypoint -1642, -533, -2380}}
   - You'll need 10 [[Titanium ore]], 5 [[Sumac lumber]], and 5 [[Ethereal coal]]
1. Return to [[Ruffin]] (NB Mass Production still only gives one update per craft.)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
