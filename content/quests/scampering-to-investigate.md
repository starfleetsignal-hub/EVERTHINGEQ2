## Steps

1. ![Note](images/Gathered_Note.jpg)Click on 10 sparkly [[Damaged note|damaged notes]] (not trackable) around [[Malvonicus' Folly]] {{waypoint 919, 7, 1389}} or [[The D.I.R.T.Y. Dig]] {{waypoint 1076, 10, 1111}}.
  - If your adventure level is low for the zone, the notes around the Dig site are safer to gather and will refresh so you can gather all in that area.
  - You can only gather 2 notes at a time and must use them to restore 1 note.
  - Requires 1 effulgent incense, 1 leonid root and 2 notes per combine.
  - Engraving desk is next to Skrit {{waypoint 1123, 17, 1266}} and fuel merchant is at {{waypoint 1192, 9, 1218}}.
1. Speak to [[Skrit]]

## Rewards

- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]]
