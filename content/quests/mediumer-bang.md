## Steps

1. Collect 4 *gearboxes* using [[Glazid's Flashpod]] on [[A Bonedigger harvester|Bonedigger harvesters]] or [[A Bonedigger footpeddler|Bonedigger footpeddlers]] in the [[Field of Bone]]. {{waypoint -1509, -368, -1906}}
   1. Target the Bonedigger and use Glazid's Flashpod. It will freeze in place.
   1. Right click on the Bonedigger and select "Swipe gearbox".
1. Return to [[Tinkerist Glazid]].

## Rewards

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- +5000 faction with **Exiles of Droga**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Exarch's Blessed Sacrament Plate Barbute]] (Clerics)
  - [[Executioner's Truesteel Helmet]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Illuminate's Still Water Skullcap]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Magus's Glowing Gossamer Cowl]] (All Mages)
  - [[Outrider's Blacksteel Chain Helmet]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Stormcaller's Spring Weave Cap]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Witchdoctor's Primal Wrath Coif]] (Shamans)
