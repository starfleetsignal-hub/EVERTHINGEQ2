## Notes

- requires at least 39,000 [[Pride Pakiat (Faction)]]

## Steps

1. Travel to the [[Esoterra Gardens]] near {{waypoint -634, 31, 263}}
1. Click on 8 bulbs. They can be found on the underside of the tree arches.
1. Travel to {{waypoint 876, -16, -1062}} or {{waypoint -1160, -18, 435}} and kill 8 [[A lobster monster]].
1. Click on the barrel of Green Fruits at {{waypoint -847, 130, -766}}.
1. Click on the barrel of Sting-y Meat at {{waypoint -842, 131, -764}}.
1. Return to [[Izny Rut]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -850 faction with **House Vahla**
- -850 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +850 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
