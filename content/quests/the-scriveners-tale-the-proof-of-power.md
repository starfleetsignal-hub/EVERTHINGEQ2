## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 24 [[Reef cluster]]
- 35 [[Flitterfin tang]]
- 75 [[Nimbic hide]]
- 75 [[Strangler root]]
- 50 [[Celestial Incense]]
- Engraving Desk (Optional)

## Steps

1. Type **/cry** to receive [[Recipe Scroll: Celestial Nilborien Mount Illusion]] (you must be in [[Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wind]] when you do this) *"Xegony has heard your plea and has blessed you by giving you a recipe to create a nilborien illusion... Just like in the story!"*
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Celestial Nilborien Mount Illusion]]
1. Craft a [[Celestial Nilborien Mount Illusion]] on an [[Engraved Desk]].
   - You may want to use a Potion of Progress here if you are impatient or don't like longer craft times. This one takes about 2-3 mins and is nowhere near as easy and fast as the entire earlier portion of the quest line.
   - Save yourself some time and use a summoned desk rather than the suggestion below.
   - There is an [[Engraved Desk]] on the second floor of the Library. Go to the [[South Wing Catalog]], then through the teleporter  at {{waypoint 332, 15, 173}}  to the [[Crafters' Gallery]].
1. Use the [[Celestial Nilborien Mount Illusion]] from your mount inventory while in [[Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wind]].
   - You must replace your current equipped mount with the illusion, then Summon Mount. (If it does not want to go into your mount window, you could have maxed your number of mounts.  Trying removing a mount and putting this in its place and then equip it.) It will not update the quest if used in the mount appearance slot
1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 143, -179, -820}}  to [[Crosswind Isle]]. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 384, 181, -250}}  to [[The Aerie]] and travel to the Great Nest at  {{waypoint 1228, 279, -43}}
1. Take the teleporter at  {{waypoint 819.69, 356.69, 79.80}}    <s>{{waypoint -516, 357, -429}}</s> to retrace your path back to [[Recipes for Adventure]] at {{waypoint 454, -202, -1432}}  in [[The Windglades]], or evac.
   - after talking with [[Recipes for Adventure]] you will receive in game mail from Ghida next time you zone somewhere. You can change mounts from [[Celestial Nilborien Mount Illusion|Celestial Nilborien Illusion]] to your regular mount.

## Rewards

- At least 14p 21g 53s 73c 48000 status
