## Steps

1. Kill [[A Scaleborn stonesetter|Scaleborn stonesetters]] for 4 common updates.
1. Travel to the center of the temple on [[Gazer Isle (POI)|Gazer Isle]] for a quest update. Cloud station at {{waypoint 295, 10, -84}}.
1. Speak with [[Glutton]], an NPC gazer, pathing around the temple {{waypoint 922.64, 455.65, -12.82}}. His in-game name is actually 'a ravenous gazer', however after speaking with him he reveals that his name is Glutton.
1. Speak with [[Grumglub]], back near the zone-in. {{waypoint 60, 22, 208}}
1. Grumglub sends you back to Gazer Isle to find his precious recipes carved onto the stone benches. The mouse-over points for some of the benches, like the pillars in the previous quest, are difficult to find.
   - {{waypoint 760.56, 459.01, -118.33}} (Eye Goulash) -- top of small, flat bench.
   - {{waypoint 856.37, 454.30, 91.18}} (Dragonfly Soup) -- [[Waterfalls|Waterfalls Tome]] is collected from this same bench.
   - {{waypoint 937.45, 456.55, -25.98}} (Crispy Wing Stuffed Tentacles)
   - {{waypoint 913.78, 456.26, -32.92}} (Retinal Radish Soup) --  corner group, one pointing "out"
   - {{waypoint 1014.61, 467.76, -121.56}} (Gloompall Gaseous Stew) -- bench pointing towards tree
   - {{waypoint 982.73, 468.28, -128.74}} (Ale Cheese Soup in a Bread Bowl) -- top of bench closest to mountain.
   - {{waypoint 838.56, 463.16, -81.73}} (Flatbread Fairy Fondue) -- top of small flat bench, underneath longer pillar.
1. The quest then updates automatically and you have a number of mobs to kill on Gazer Isle for the recipe ingredients:
   - 10 Dragon Fly Fairies
   - 6 Evil eyes
   - 5 Gazers (NOTE: Gazers update both the Evil Eyes and Gazer recipe ingredients for this quest)
   - WARNING to provisioners: Default faction with the Gloompall Fairies is high threatening - killing ten of these will take you to scowling (KoS / kill on sight), and gathering from around them will no longer be peaceful. There is no way to increase reputation with this faction.
1. Return to Glutton.
1. Speak with your "Empire Agent", [[Spearhead]] for Qeynosians, [[A Freeport emissary (TT)|a Freeport emissary]] for Freeportians.

## Rewards

- Your choice of:
  - [[Flatbread Fairy Fondue]] x20
  - [[Ale Cheese Soup in a Bread Bowl]] x20
