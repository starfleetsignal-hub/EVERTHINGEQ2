## Steps

1. Collect 5 [[Throm-spiced clam]]s from baskets inside the cave east of [[Mok Rent]]. {{waypoint 1623.51, 0.91, -142.95}}
1. Return to [[Vircho Ku'Guur]].

## Rewards

- At least 1s 59c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Fighter's Ring]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Ring]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Ring]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Ring]] (All Mages)
