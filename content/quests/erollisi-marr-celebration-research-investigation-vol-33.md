## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of Erollisi Marr has asked for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Gather the requested supplies
   - Collect 3 [[Scorched Fangs|scorched fangs]] found in [[Zimara Breadth]]
     - *Note: Drops in [[Treasure Chest]] of Scorched Direfang (Helionae Razelands - the segmented snakes in the burnt area.)*
   - Gather or purchase [[Chromatic Carp]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas
     - *Common harvest from BoZ fish cluster*
     - *Can buy off broker*
     - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to [[Herald of the Huntress]] {{waypoint 20, 275, 125}} in [[Frostfang Sea]]
