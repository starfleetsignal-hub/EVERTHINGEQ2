You must be at the proper step of [[Underdepths Saga: Into Darkness]] to receive this quest

## Steps

1. Perform the following tasks at [[Ironblood Fungus Farm]].
   - Kill 12 ironblood myconids (any of the various kinds around the farm update).![a damaged defense rod](images/A_damaged_defense_rod.jpg)
   - Repair 6 **damaged defense rods** (click the leaning rods to fix them) {{waypoint 906, 39, -254}}, {{waypoint 793, 51, -272}}, {{waypoint 797, 50, -342}}, {{waypoint 807, 44, -431}}, {{waypoint 860, 42, -221}}, {{waypoint 905, 41, -245}}
1. Return to Grygrim

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 12687 status
- +5000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +5000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
