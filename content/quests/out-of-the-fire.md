## Steps

1. Galen needs seed pods from a burned twilight fern, which grow near Soul's Crossing {{waypoint -43, 56, 117}}, and the regeneration negation draught from Damion Trebatius, a wizard at Incantatory Apex, an arcane project area northwest of Cardin Ward {{waypoint -59, 44, -219}}.
   - Gather twilight fern seed pods (6)
     - Right-click small green bushes and choose to Set Fire to the Twilight Fern
       - *Note, if you do this near an obol shambler, it will anger them and they will attack you*
     - Pick up the seed pod that drops from the fern
   - Obtain regeneration negation draught from Damion Trebatius {{waypoint -62, 44, -218}}
1. I must return to Galen Cadabrak in Cardin Ward and tell him the Harrowing Horde have taken over the spires at Incantatory Apex.
   - Return to [[Galen Cadabrak]] {{waypoint -460, 15, 84}}
