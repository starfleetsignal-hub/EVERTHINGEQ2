## Steps

1. Retrieve the [[Thalz'lz'Zaz artifact]] from [[Quartermaster Stumps]] 78^^^ Heroic located at {{waypoint 883, 510, 1822}} near the [[Obelisk of Ahkzul]].
1. Return the looted artifact to [[Shaman Zaz'i'zaz]] at {{waypoint 1149, 392, 864}}.
