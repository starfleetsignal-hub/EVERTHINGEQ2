## Starting the quest

Harvest Bountiful Brewday gardens in any zone to find the quest starter. Bushes appear while wearing [[Improved Ale Goggles]] in Antonica, Commonlands, Greater Faydark, Timorous Deeps, New Halas and Darklight Woods

## Steps

1. Continue harvesting bountiful Brewday gardens to locate Serilian Teardrop Diamond, Emerald and Ruby (autoupdates)
1. Give the bowl to a Brewmaster (which one will be random, you'll need to try them):
   - [[A Krulkiel brewmaster]] in Butcherblock Mountains, below [[Kamp Krulkiel]]  {{waypoint 11, 183, -276}}
   - [[A Mazkeen brewmaster]] in Steamfont Mountains at the Greater Faydark entrance {{waypoint -544, 127, 712}}
   - [[A Skindancer brewmaster]] in The Thundering Steppes, near the [[Shrine of Decay]] at [[Giant's Forge]] {{waypoint 270, 0, 1193}}

## Reward

- Choice of:
  - [[Grand Brewday Wreath]]
  - 8 [[Beer Token|Beer Tokens]]
