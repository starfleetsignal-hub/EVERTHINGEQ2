## Steps

1. Find the **Waning Decanter**.
   - Appears as a clay jug.
   - Possible location: Ledge at the [[Waning Baths]] {{waypoint 437.74, 9.00, 484.51}}.
1. Use the decanter:
   - Fill 5 vessels. These look exactly like the Waning Decanter.
     - Possible locations {{waypoint 454.50, 7.46, 463.05}}, {{waypoint 440.03, 7.43, 439.54}}, {{waypoint 533.27, -1.92, 506.70}}, {{waypoint 546.14, 8.46, 348.91}}, {{waypoint 571, -1.92, 498.77}}

1. Release those who guard the receptacles.
   - Slay [[Faro'Nuk enforcer|Faro'Nuk enforcers]].

## Rewards

- [[Primal Velium Shard]] (at level 90)
