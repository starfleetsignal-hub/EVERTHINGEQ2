1. I need to find the secret entrance to [[Neriondalon]] {{waypoint -112.82, 92.06, -1102.13}}
1. I should enter the Duchy of the Dead {{waypoint 880.10, 189.83, -1192.00}}
1. I should use the amulet *a hauntingly strange amulet*
1. I should speak to the [[Maligned Spirit]] {{waypoint -33.02, 222.15, 356.84}}
   - I need water from the fountain {{waypoint -12.37, 222.44, 347.25}}
   - I need soil from a grave {{waypoint 6.45, 222.75, 367.17}}
   - I need belonging once owned by a vampire {{waypoint 1.10, 222.82, 302.83}}

   - Craft the disguise on the spectral workbench. {{waypoint -30.35, 222.21, 362.97}} Requires 1 Formless Kindling
1. Speak to the [[Maligned Spirit]].
1. Enter the crypt area - {{waypoint -39.39, 222.10, 329.98}}
1. Don the disguise *Vampire Disguise*
1. Use the amulet to summon the spirit again
1. Find and speak to the spirit {{waypoint -137.43, 222.78, 334.15}}

   - I need a gravediggers shovel handle {{waypoint -64.13, 224.44, 217.23}}

1. Craft the Spectral Shovel on workbench {{waypoint -132.95, 223.94, 342.25}} Requires 1 Formless Kindling
1. Show the shovel to the [[Maligned Spirit]]
1. Place it on the ground {{waypoint -137.00, 222.63, 330.85}}

1. Pick up the Spectral Shovel.
