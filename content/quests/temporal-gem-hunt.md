## Steps

1. Talk to [[Jorlak]] who will send you to the recent past in the [[Lyceum of the Recondite]].
1. Use the Chronoportal to the [[Lyceum of the Recondite]] {{waypoint -0.19, 1.67, 16.52}} and speak to [[Jorlak]] in the instance {{waypoint 135.38, 0.22, 68.58}} to receive the subquests [[Hello Again, Friend of a Friend]] and [[Broken Bones]]
1. Upon completing the subquests you will receive the [[Temporal Draconic Conduit Gem]].
1. Return to [[Jorlak]] in Skyshrine.
