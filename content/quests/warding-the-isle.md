## Steps

1. To place an offering, click on what looks like a totem topped by a big kerra hand pole in the following locations  (they circle the outer edge of Kerra Isle). If you have a flying mount you can obtain the update while hovering over the ward to avoid having to fight anything. If you have a leaper, you can land in the palm of the hand.
   - Place offering at the first ward {At Tiger Pit Island} {{waypoint 1301, -28, 2516}} ![One of the wards](images/Warding_the_Isle_quest_-_ward.jpg)
   - Place offering at the second ward {{waypoint 1304, 13, 2338}}
   - Place offering at the third ward {{waypoint 1432, 14, 2366}}
   - Place offering at the fourth ward {{waypoint 1480, 23, 2214}}
   - Place offering at the fifth ward {{waypoint 1464, 24, 1997}}
   - Place offering at the sixth ward {{waypoint 1136, 12, 1929}}
   - Place offering at the seventh ward {{waypoint 1052, 18, 2088}}
   - Place offering at the eighth ward {{waypoint 1077, 6, 2276}}
   - Place offering at the final ward {{waypoint 1181, 13, 2377}}
1. Return to [[Shaman Yityen]].

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +2000 faction with **Kerra Isle**
