Create 6 items from the random list below:

- [[Tranquil Sandcloth Robe]]
- [[Tranquil Sandcloth Cap]]
- [[Tranquil Sandcloth Cuffs]]
- [[Tranquil Sandcloth Shawl]]
- [[Tranquil Sandcloth Slippers]]
- [[Tranquil Sandcloth Mitts]]
- [[Tranquil Sandcloth Pantaloons]]
- [[Woven Stonehide Boots]]
- [[Woven Stonehide Bracers]]
- [[Woven Stonehide Gloves]]
- [[Woven Stonehide Pants]]
- [[Woven Stonehide Shoulder Pads]]
- [[Woven Stonehide Skullcap]]
- [[Woven Stonehide Tunic]]
- [[Dexterous Stonehide Boots]]
- [[Dexterous Stonehide Bracers]]
- [[Dexterous Stonehide Gloves]]
- [[Dexterous Stonehide Pants]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 2g 43s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
