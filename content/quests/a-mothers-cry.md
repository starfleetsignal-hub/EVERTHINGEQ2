## Prerequisites

- If you have not completed [[Vranala's Next Nest]] you must complete this before she will offer you the update qst.
- Must have completed [[Family Ties]] to hail Harla Dar.

## Steps

1. Find a mother who cannot accept her own death.
   - Go to [[Vesspyr Isles]] and speak to [[Vranala]] {{waypoint -520, 216, -625}}.
1. Attempt to locate remnants of Vranala's nest in [[Jarsath Wastes]].
   - Go to [[Jarsath Wastes]] and collect 20 a broken egg (very hard to see) or a broken nest (the nests sparkle blue):
     - Various locations:
       - {{waypoint -757, 235, 487}} egg
       - {{waypoint -808, 236, 435}} egg
       - {{waypoint -875, 237, 377}} egg
       - {{waypoint -941, 254, 718}} egg
       - {{waypoint -955, 236, 459}} egg
       - {{waypoint -991, 234, 912}} egg
       - {{waypoint -1050, 242, 1123}} nest
       - {{waypoint -1087, 227, 798}} nest
       - {{waypoint -1176, 224, 391}} egg
       - {{waypoint -1186, 229, 595}} egg
       - {{waypoint -1204, 230, 935}} nest
       - {{waypoint -1274, 225, 1046}} nest
       - {{waypoint -1276, 229, 1094}} egg
       - {{waypoint -1344, 228, 578}} egg
       - {{waypoint -1355, 206, 531}} egg
       - {{waypoint -1544, 204, 350}} egg

1. Attempt to locate remnants of Vranala's nest in [[Veeshan's Peak]]
   - Go to VP and gather 20 more eggs and nest found in each wing (middle wing had fewer spawn points)
     - *Note: if you are in a group, time it so you all click the nest / eggs at the same time to get multiple updates from one nest / egg*
1. Talk to [[Harla Dar]] {{waypoint -101, 51, 730}} in [[Vesspyr Isles]] to bless the remnants.
1. Speak to [[Vranala]] {{waypoint -520, 216, -625}}
1. Examine the [[Disused manacles]] and speak with the [[Eidolon of Heroism]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
