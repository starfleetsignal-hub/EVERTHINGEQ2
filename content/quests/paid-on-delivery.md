## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 20+

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Farmer Helver]] in [[Antonica]] at Sayer's Outfitters. {{waypoint -723, -16, 223}}
1. Speak with [[Clerk Feld]] in [[The Qeynos Hold]] bank in [[North Qeynos]]. {{waypoint 276, -18, -116}}
1. Return to [[Rinna]].
1. Speak with [[Courier Laventy]] in [[South Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]] to get a new authorization. {{waypoint 569, -15, 201}}
1. Take the replacement authorization to [[Clerk Feld]] at the bank.
1. Deliver the payment to the farmers in [[Antonica]] near the aquaduct. Kill the three [[A shifty farmer|shifty farmers]] that ambush you.{{waypoint -133, -15, -74}}
1. Take the payment to [[Farmer Helver]] instead.
1. Return to [[Rinna]].
