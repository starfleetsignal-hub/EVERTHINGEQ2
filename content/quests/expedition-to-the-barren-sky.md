## Notes

- To progress through this quest you will need to learn [[Volant]], the language of the Aviaks, learned from the quest [[Words of a Feather]].

## Steps

1. Use the [[Ulteran Spires|Ulteran Spire]] on [[Vultak Scavenging Site (POI)|Vultak Scavenging Site]] to travel to [[The Barren Sky]]. {{waypoint -712, 145, -148}}
   - Although all the Ulteran Spires interconnect, this specific spire must still be used to receive the quest update.
   - Take cloud mount at {{waypoint -238, -6, -6}} to Vultak Scavenging Site.
1. Speak with [[Jabber Longwind (Barren Sky)|Jabber Longwind]], who is standing next to spire.
1. Go to [[The Whisperwind Isle (POI)|The Whisperwind Isle]] and speak with [[Graywind the Elder]]. (require to know Volant language) {{waypoint -677, 8.4, -509}}
1. Go to [[The Cloudmist Isle]] and strike down [[The Ghastly Overcast]] {{waypoint -597, 248, -145}}. Upon defeat, speak with [[The Gentle Breeze]] who has spawned at the same location.
1. Complete the following tasks in any order:
   - Kill [[A Bloodscale breeder|Bloodscale breeders]] for 10 updates on  around {{waypoint 389.38, 196.25, 653.51}}.
   - Harvest 'blast-filled bedrock' nodes for 20 quest updates, also on . These are found around [[The Drake Breeding Ground]] and [[Pool of the Awakened]]. They look like sandwashed rock (T2 gemstone) harvesting nodes.
     - If you have the "Track Harvestables" ability, it can be used to find the bedrock nodes.
   - Kill [[A crystalline burrower|crystalline burrowers]] for 5 quest updates on . {{waypoint -491, 125, 439}}
1. Return to the Cloudmist Isle and talk to the Gentle Breeze again. If Gentle Breeze is not there, spawn him in the same manner as before.
1. Speak with both [[Graywind the Elder]] and [[Creakbeak the Elder]] to finish the quest.

## Reward

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Darkened Ravasect carapace Bracelet]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Engraved Ravasect Carapace Bracelet]] (All Mages)
  - [[Etched Ravasect Carapace Bracelet]] (All Priests)
  - [[Polished Ravasect Carapace Bracelet]] (All Fighters)
