## Steps

1. Get the 3 parts of the Mark:
   1. **The Totem of the Itinerant:**
      1. Proceed to the [[Pyramid of Fear]], at {{waypoint -321, -1.3, 834}} and complete the ring event to spawn [[An Evol Ritualist]] and update the quest.
      1. Return to Kai.
   1. **A Vial of Shadow:**
      1. Slay the [[Shadow of Therik Mu'sah]]at the [[Tower of Vul]]. {{waypoint -953, 12, 495}}
         - If [[Shadow of Therik Mu'sah]] is not there, go back to [[Kai Vhri'Jah]] and speak to him again. Then rush back and kill [[Shadow of Therik Mu'sah]].
      1. Return to Kai.
   1. **The Writ of the Void:**
      1. Slay [[A Tae Ew Shifter]]. {{waypoint -1288, 4, 674}}
         - If you cannot find a Shifter in any of it's normal spots, go back to Kai, hail him again and return to shifter spots, one should now be there.
      1. Return to Kai.
1. [[Kai Vhri]] will spawn upon completing dialogue with Kai Vhri'Jah.
