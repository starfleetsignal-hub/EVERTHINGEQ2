## Steps

1. Speak with [[Finbert Lintzig]] got quest then talk to him again to get some **Black Boxes**(x3) at Gnomeland Security Headquarters {{waypoint -584, 175, 959}}
1. Install the 3 [[Little Black Box|Little Black Boxes]] given to you by [[Finbert Lintzig]] into the [[Inoperative Clockwork]] {{waypoint -1119, 79, 944}}
   - The [[Inoperative Clockwork]] turns into [[Opticron]] (non-aggro) which is serviced by several clockworks that it destroys and then becomes the [[Haywire Opticron]] (42^).
1. Slay the [[Haywire Opticron]] (respawn time is about 5-6 minutes)
1. Return [[Finbert Lintzig]]

## Rewards

- At least 26g 44s 59c
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Manaforge Greaves]] (Berserker, Guardian, Paladin, Shadowknight)
  - [[Manafuse Leggings]] (Bruiser, Monk)
  - [[Manalink Leggings]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Manarnadine Leggings]] (Defiler, Mystic)
  - [[Manasworn Pants]] (All Mages)
  - [[Manatroth Legguards]] (Inquisitor, Templar)
  - [[Manovate Leather Leggings]] (Fury, Warden)

## Fun Facts

When booting up, before turning into an attackable mob, the Opticron says 2 things in [[Tik-Tok]]. When this quest was first put in the game, the language was not available to players, but nearly all clockwork mobs and NPCs spoke in it. The language was later made available to players via a [[The Mysteries of Tik-Tok|quest]] in [[Moors of Ykesha]] when The Shadow Odyssey launched.

For those of you who don't want to wait to get the language, then come back for this quest, here is what the NPC says:

\aNPC 12655222 Opticron:Opticron\/a says in Tik-Tok, "Opticron Status: 99%."
\aNPC 12655222 Opticron:Opticron\/a says in Tik-Tok, "Aberrant Battle Module Configuration: Online. Initiate Annihilation Mode."

The NPC then spins around, "killing" the clockworks that came to its aid, then turns into the Named Monster **Opticron**
