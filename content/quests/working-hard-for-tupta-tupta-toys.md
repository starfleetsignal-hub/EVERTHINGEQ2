## Steps

1. Click on the recipe book next to Reedip and scribe the *Tupta Traditional Artisanry* which gets placed in your inventory.
1. Make 5 *wooden froglok toy* (recipe makes 1 wooden froglok toy per combine)
   - Level 80 tradeskill combine, geomancy skill, woodworking table {{waypoint -76, -53, 815}}
   - 1 [[Tuptan hardwood]], primary component, found just outside Tupta. {{waypoint -32, -62, 630}}
   - 3 [[Ykeshan shellfish]], found in the river at the top of waterfall near Tupta. {{waypoint -140, -95, 569}}
   - 2 wasp threading, gathered from wasp hives in tupta. {{waypoint -65.07, -53.03, 809.51}}
   - 1 smoldering sandpaper
1. Return to Reedip.

## Rewards

- At least 47g 11s 70c
- +2000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
- -500 faction with **Clan Grobb**
