## Steps

1. Collect the first Starmap
1. Collect the second Starmap
1. Collect the third Starmap
   - All three maps are on the long table in the room with the last named.  Note, all three maps can be collected with out agroing the named if necessary by going up and behind the named.  Stay close to a wall though. ( as of 09/11/10 you can no longer harvest them without getting within the named's aggro range)
