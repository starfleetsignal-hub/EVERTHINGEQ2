## Prerequisites

- You must have started the quest [[When Darkness Comes]] or [[Eliminate Geraufl]] in order to complete this quest.

![](images/Cold-blooded_flower.jpg)

## Steps

1. Gather 3 flowers. When looking for them, keep in mind the following:
   - They spawn randomly throughout the zone.
   - They may be located in between tree roots and tucked in corners.
   - Turning off flora may make your search easier.
   - Possible spawn loc: {{waypoint -56, 13, 131}}
1. Give the flowers to [[Oemor (Lost VoK)|Oemor]] {{waypoint -132, 14, 196}}, near Teiluj.
1. Speak with Teiluj for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience

## Notes

The names of the froglok lovers are "Romeo" and "Juliet" spelled backwards; however in this case "Romeo" is female and "Juliet" is male.
