## Prerequisites

You must be at least level 85 in your Tradeskill Profession.

## Notes

The shopping list for the 12 quests in the quest line beginning with this quest is:

- 1 mottled pelt
- 4 quicksilver cluster
- 9 sumac lumber
- 14 titanium ore
- 3 aerated mineral water
- 8 ethereal coal
- 1 ethereal filament
- 1 ethereal kindling
- 2 ethereal sandpaper

## Steps

1. Find an othmir who will talk about crafting and trade.  [[NPC|Mira]] {{waypoint -1357, -533, -2160}} in the othmir fishing camp SW of Fina's Retreat will do nicely.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1000 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
