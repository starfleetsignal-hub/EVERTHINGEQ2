## Steps

1. Collect 6 pieces of *discarded wood* north of [[T'Vatar Post]], around {{waypoint -379, -54, -50}}.
1. Return to [[Ilmtar D'Viervs]].

## Rewards

- At least 1s 5c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Bodyguard's Leggings]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Evoker's Leggings]] (All Mages)
  - [[Dark Marshall's Greaves]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Pathfinder's Leggings]] (Bard, Rogue, Beastlord, Assassin)
  - [[Dark Pathfinder's Leggings]] (Rangers)
  - [[Spiritualist's Leggings]] (Shamans)
  - [[Vicar's Greaves]] (Clerics)
  - [[Windcaller's Leggings]] (Druids)
