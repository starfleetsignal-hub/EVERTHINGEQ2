## Description

It seems that researchers, scholars, and treasure hunters will pay quite well for iksar artifacts from the ancient facility of Kor'sha. Personal effects are especially sought after, as they provide some clues as to how those working within its walls lived from day to day. Any remnants of those that once lived there will likely have encrusted over long ago, but I have been given a vial of acid to help cleanse them and search them for any interesting items.

## Steps

1. Use the received *[[A bottle of corrosive|bottle of corrosive]]* to cleanse and search 5 iksar skeletons within [[Emperor Atrebe's Laboratory: The Fabled City of Kor-sha]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
