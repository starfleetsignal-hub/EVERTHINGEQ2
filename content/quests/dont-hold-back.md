## Steps

![Loose Debris](images/Loose_Debris.jpg)
![Zone Entrance Exit](images/ZoneEntranceExit.jpg)

1. Travel to [[Wracklands]] to the Diako Orcs area around [[Sunset Pillars]] {{waypoint 443, 15, -72}}.
1. Defeat 25 Diako Orcs
   - **Hint**: If you can already use your flying mount, **also start** the daily quest [[Wracklands: Rallos Around the Diaku]] at Marker of Rallos Zek at {{waypoint 356, 129, -370}}
1. Enter the solo instance [[Shining Mesa Stronghold (Zone)|Shining Mesa Stronghold]] by landing next to the Loose Debris {{waypoint 212, 120, -289}} northern of the [[Shining Mesa Stronghold (POI)|Shining Mesa Stronghold]] in [[Wracklands]] and click the loose debris.
   - Note: No mercenary or other player is allowed in this zone. So you are realy solo....luckily the "heroic"-mobs can still be defeated.
1. Before attacking the first name, clear the trashmobs:
   - [[A Diaku defender]], has a kick, tries to kick you in the direction to [[A Diaku warboar]]
   - [[A Diaku warboar]]
   - [[A Diaku Boar-Tender]]
   - [[A Diaku oracle]]
   - [[A scion of flame]], rooming and respawning, but respawntimer must be > 2h.
   - and non-aggro [[A war-hardened spirit]] at the fire mold.
1. Attack [[A Diviner of Flame]] at {{waypoint 176, 121, -267}}
   - Beside the uncurable elemtal curse **Glorious Rage**, you are nearly permanently cursed by the curable element AE-DOT **Incinerating Flames**, doing 03 - 0.4m heat damage per 3 seconds.
1. [[An Invoker of Flame]] respawns at {{waypoint 264, 122, -154}}
   - Casts curse **Torrent of Fire**, doing 2.7m -3.4m DD, DOT 1.4m - 1.7m heat damage per 2 seconds and AE-DOT 1.4m - 1.7m every tick.
1. [[A Daiko Inquisitor]] {{waypoint 309, 120, -255}} spwans.
   - *Note*: If you didn't clear the trashmobs at his spawn-location, then they standing beside the [[A Daiko Inquisitor]]. But you can mezz [[A Daiko Inquisitor]] and then pull the trashmobs.
   - Has a kick, so take a position next to a wall
   - When he kicks you, you can douple-click (or right-click) him to "kick" him, too.
   - If you permanently loose your target, then you have to douple-click him to "kick" him. Then targeting should work again.
1. At the fire mold [[A Daughter of the Songod]]  {{waypoint 249, 345, 134}} spawns.
   - On start of fight the message "A sun spark forms the molten pit, granting the Daughter of the Songod greater defence and power" is shown.
   - Periodically an add, [[A sun spark]], spawns.
     - As long as the Add [[A sun spark]] is alive, [[A Daughter of the Songod]] has the Mob-Buff **Solshield**: "50% less damage"; 5.0m - 6.1m reactive direct damage with interrupt, can trigger every second.
   - Mob-Buff **Mesmerize Resistance**: Immune to mesmerize effects
   - DoT on player **Glorious Rage**: each 287,44 - 351,320 of magic, poison, slashing, cold damage every 3 seconds.
1. [[Khap]] spawns at the gate.
   - at every x5% health, starting with 95%, an add, [[A Diaku Warlord]], is called, interrupting not possible.
   - As long as adds are supporting [[Khap]], she has 2 Mob-buffs:
     - **Brutal Shield**: For each [[A Diaku Warlord]] which is in combat, [[Khap]] has increased defence.
     - **Brutal Sacrifice**: For each [[A Diaku Warlord]] which is defeated, [[Khap]] has increased damage-output.
   - *Note: Focus spawned [[A Diaku Warlord]] and burned it before coming back to [[Khap]].*
1. Get Quest-Reward and leave zone by...
   - the straigt forward zone-exit is at {{waypoint 137, 0, 0}}. On the way you can some more trashmobs...or your train them.
   - Alternative Exit: Zone-Entrance-Exit at {{waypoint 232, 121, 252}}
