## Steps

1. Head to [[The Ethermist Shores]]
   - Activate 4 reality anchors.  Do this by clicking the ghostly dervish looking thing in the middle of the anchor. Locations are:
     - {{waypoint 264, -45, -169}}
     - {{waypoint 308, -41, -207}}
     - {{waypoint 93, -50, -243}}
     - {{waypoint 69, -48, -231}}
   - Collect 30 power crystals from the [[A Skirth siphoner|Skirth siphoners]] {{waypoint 132, -49, -193}}.  They are drops called [[A crystallized shard of reality]], each Skirth siphoner will drop 2-3 of them (very occasionally 4).
1. Return to Veris.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- [[Nebulous Reality Anchor]]
