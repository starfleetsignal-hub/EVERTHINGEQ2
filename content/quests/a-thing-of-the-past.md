## Steps

1. Enter either [[Veksar: The Invasion]] or [[Veksar: The Sunken Theater]] {{waypoint 631, -179, 290}}, and gather a page in [[The Runearium]], at {{waypoint 40, 6, -195}}.
1. Gather 5 more pages in the zone:
   - The Runearium: {{waypoint 45, 6, -199}}
   - [[The Golem Factatory]]: {{waypoint 87, 6, -140}}
   - [[Cribblekeep's Treasureliquary]]: {{waypoint -15, -1, -198}}
   - [[The Grand Sancloister]]: {{waypoint -50, -3, -149}} and {{waypoint -11, -3, -156}}
1. Return to the Runearium and examine [[Phinea's Notebook]] from your inventory.
1. Return to Scholar Phinea.
1. Speak with Scholar Phinea again after a brief moment.
