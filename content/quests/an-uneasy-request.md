## Steps

1. [[Apprentice Aulerun]] has asked that you meet his master [[Nudhir Il'qatai|Nudhir]] {{waypoint 1946, -112, 3405}} in [[The Ruins of Old Paineel]] for further instruction. He can be found above the south city entrance, west from the Paineel portal.
1. Speak with Nudhir...
1. Speak with Nudhir again when you are ready for combat!
1. Fight Nudhir and kill him. ![Nudhir's corpse waiting to be revived](images/An_Uneasy_Request_quest_-_Nudhir's_corpse.jpg)
1. Attempt to revive Nudhir.
   - Right click on Nudhir's body and select 'Attempt to revive'.

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**
