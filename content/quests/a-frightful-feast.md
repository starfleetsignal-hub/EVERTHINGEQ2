## Spawning Orus'Dahk

Similar to summoning [[Phar'Ahkt]] during the quest, [[I Seek Knowledge]], in order to summon [[Orus'Dakh]] his name must be spelled out using runes. Alternate methods of spawning Orus'Dakh are to hail him after another player hails him, or to share this step of the quest in a group. The runes formerly used to summon Orus'Dakh are not consumed when used. They were:

- [[The rune of orus|orus]] (note: This rune is particular seems to have a VERY low drop rate.)
- [[The rune of duak|duak]]
- [[The rune of ahk|ahk]]
- [[The rune of kan|kan]]
- [[The rune of hek|hek]]

## Steps

1. Pick up the following artifacts within the [[Residence Court]]. These items spawn and despawn randomly every few minutes:
   - ![a bucket of spoiled meat](images/Image:A_bucket_of_spoiled_meat.jpg) at {{waypoint -278, -5, 350}} or {{waypoint -280, -5, 408}}
   - ![a bottle of spoiled wine](images/Image:A_bottle_of_spoiled_wine.jpg) at {{waypoint -300.63, 17.18, 416.54}} or {{waypoint -380, 4, 425}} (on a narrow ledge 2/3rds of the way up the building, you will need to complete the Oily Wall quest to reach this {Or just drop down from the aqueduct above -Toofs})
   - ![spoiled peacock eggs](images/Image:Spoiled_peacock_eggs.jpg) at {{waypoint -390, -11, 477}} or {{waypoint -437, -31, 475}}
   - ![a pot of spoiled coffee](images/Image:A_pot_of_spoiled_coffee.jpg) at {{waypoint -547, -8, 458}} or {{waypoint -486, 22, 374}} (on the narrow protrusion out from the roof, easy to miss)
   - ![a pot of spoiled soup](images/Image:A_pot_of_spoiled_soup.jpg) at {{waypoint -354.56, -8.89, 369.70}} or {{waypoint -322, 8, 350}}
   - ![a crock of spoiled cheese](images/Image:A_crock_of_spoiled_cheese1.jpg) at {{waypoint -354, 12, 500}} (on the ledge near the oiled wall) or {{waypoint -300, -7, 507}} (behind the pillar)
1. Speak again with [[Orus'Dakh]] to complete the quest.
   - **Warning**: Choosing the dialogue option that includes 'goodbye' will instantly despawn Orus'Dakh.

## Rewards
