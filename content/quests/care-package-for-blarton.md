## Notes

- This quest is level 10 but you must avoid large numbers of level 17-20 creatures on the road to the village.

## Steps

1. Deliver a package to [[Blarton Blumble]] in [[Windstalker Village]] at {{waypoint -1830.68, -37.85, -541.38}}.
1. Return to [[Matsy Rollingpin|Matsy]] for your reward.
- ***Optional:*** While you are in [[Windstalker Village]], speak again to [[Blarton Blumble|Blarton]] to get his first quest.  Also speak to [[Licha]] and [[Hartok Woolyman|Hartok]] to get their quests.  You will need to be higher level to finish these quests.  See [[Antonica Timeline]] for details.

## Rewards

- Three [[Traditional halfling cookies]] (level 20 superior food)
- At least 92c
