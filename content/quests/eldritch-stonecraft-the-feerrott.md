## Steps

1. Scribe the recipe scroll [[Druid Ring Rebuilding: Special Edition]].
1. Craft six of one of the following items, as required by the quest, at the tables provided within the druid ring.
   - [[Eldritch Etchings]]
   - [[Gabion Mesh]]
   - [[Growth-Imbued Sarsen Stone]]
   - *The fuel and special crafting components are sold by [[A Tunarian quartermaster]] at the druid rings.*
   - *The recipes use Tier 2 non-food resources*

## Rewards

- At least 55s 40c
- 6 [[A Floret of Growth|Florets of Growth]]
