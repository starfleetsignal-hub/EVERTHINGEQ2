## Steps

1. Collect 5 [[An earthcrawler|earthcrawler]] leg joints around {{waypoint -76, -20, 581}}.
1. Rinse legs in [[Crater Pond]] {{waypoint 78, -24, 436}}.
1. Return to [[Landwyn Velamir]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 92c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Cracked Bow of the Huntress]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Gnoll-hide Pouch]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Runic Bow of the Guardian]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Sunsleeper's Staff]] (All Mages)
  - [[Warden's Wand of the Forest]] (All Priests)
