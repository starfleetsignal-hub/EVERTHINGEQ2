## Steps

1. You are given *[[A satchel of upgraded siege components]]*. Click the 9 ballistae in the specified locations, the other ballistae are inert:
   - 5 ballistae northwest of fort {{waypoint 4345, -827, 243}}
   - 4 ballistae north of fort {{waypoint 4519, -823, 374}}
1. Then you "test the siege weaponry" by double-clicking any ballista
1. Speak to Denstarin

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
