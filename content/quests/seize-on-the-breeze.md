## Steps

![Fragile stalactite gems underneath the island](images/Fragile_stalactite_gems_.png‎)

1. Gather 8 fragile stalactite gems
   - Note: The gems are under the small island to the west of Veeshenna Tol {{waypoint 194, 126, 802}} and you just need to fly into them for the update.
1. Defend Harla Dar from 5 attacking Droag.
