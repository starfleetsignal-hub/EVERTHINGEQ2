## Notes

- The mobs needed for the first step, [[A tormented Serillian spirit]], only spawn during in-game night (8pm - 8am), and potentially have a very long respawn timer (~1 hour). They also run around where they spawn, so they won't necessarily be at their exact location listed, but definitely around that general area.
- It is recommended that you make a targeting macro for  the tormented Serillian spirits:<br>**/target a torment**

## Steps

1. Capture [[A tormented Serilian spirit|tormented Serilian spirits]] using [[Serilian Spirit Gems]] during game in-game night. At 0% health the spirit will fall down giving you about 2 seconds to select the target and use the gem. The gem has a 20 second reuse timer and if the spirit is left alone at low health it will heal and begin to run around again:
   - [[Steamfont Mountains]]: Outside the entrance to [[Klak'Anon]].
   - [[Loping Plains]]: Very end of [[Arkelek's Gash]]. {{waypoint -372, 6, 488}}
   - [[Mistmoore Catacombs]]: Ground floor in the water straight back from the zone-in near the climbable wall. {{waypoint 23.80, -161, 104.72}}
   - [[Kaladim]]: [[Underfoot Cathedral]]. Can spawn day or night. {{waypoint 22, 28, -482}}
1. Release the spirits back into their tombs in Kaladim's Underfoot Cathedral. Clicking a tomb will spawn a group of heroics, [[A ratillik Cathedral defiler]] (35^) and 2 [[A Terrok berserker|Terrok bersekers]] (35^^):
   - {{waypoint 10, 28, -505}}
   - {{waypoint 10, 28, -516}}
   - {{waypoint 10, 28, -527}}
   - {{waypoint 48, 30, -516}} (under a stack of stone blocks)
1. Return to Bronlor Stormhammer.

## Rewards

- [[Cloak of the Underfoot]]
- +10000 faction with **Brell Serilis**
