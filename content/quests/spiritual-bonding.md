- Note: I was able to obtain this quest at 23 on Stormhold (completed at 24), since the launch of Fallengate 2017, but being as it involves killing groups of 30 Heroic^^^s it may take a couple levels to complete without help. {Go Qlear!, Stormhold discovery 6/28/2017}
- Being as this is a once in the lifetime of a Beastlord opportunity, many may choose to wait to Grand-master one of their Epic Warders later on, to save hundreds of hours leveling them.
- Be very careful once u get this pot, I was trying to link it to chat and accidentally applied it to my reptile warder, and it's incredibly valuable!

## Steps

1. Confront the Yarpsnarl Kobolds
   - Kill 4 encounters of Yarpsnarl Kobolds
   - Find 5 animal corpses, and release their spirits (clicky)
1. Return to [[Shalih Mar]].

## Rewards

- [[Draught of Affinity]]
