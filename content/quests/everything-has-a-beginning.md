## Steps

1. Ghalib has asked you to find out where the vermin are coming from.
   - Take the eastern lift up to [[The Ruins of Old Paineel]] and head to the caves at {{waypoint 1496, -117, 3060}}.
1. Seek out and slay [[A commanding vermin]] (who has two linked roekillik with him).
1. Return to Ghalib.

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
