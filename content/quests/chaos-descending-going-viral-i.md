## Notes

*A new tradeskill mission is available every 18 hours.*

## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 15 [[Celestial Coal]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest the following within [[Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands]]:
   - 25 [[Silvered fleck]] from [[Flecked mass]]
   - 10 [[Strangler root]] from [[Strangler root break]]
   - 10 [[Flitterfin tang]] from [[School of tangs]] *(fish nodes can be found in the marshes in the south)*
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Timeless Hourglass]].
1. Craft the [[Timeless Hourglass]] on a [[Work Bench]].
   - There is a [[Work Bench]] in Detroxxulous at {{waypoint 523.24, 85.28, 89.54}}
1. Speak to [[Esludan]] at {{waypoint 24.77, -20.10, 3.05}} in the [[Myrist Foyer]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 66g 32s 48000 status
- [[Elemental Mission Reward Crate]]
