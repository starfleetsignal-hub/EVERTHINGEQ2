*This is a limited repeatable quest which can be initially only be repeated 10 times. At the conclusion of the long questline, this quest becomes an unlimited repeatable quest.*

## Steps

Slay 10 of one of the following mobs in [[The Thundering Steppes]]:

- Lionesses, in [[Thundermist Valley]] around {{waypoint 940.50, -1.12, 165.79}} or in the [[Crested Lowlands]] near [[Qeynos Tower 3]] {{waypoint -206.00, 14.00, 181.70}}.
- Hawks, on [[Seaside Road]] {{waypoint -186.02, -0.84, -471.10}}.
- Beetles, on [[Seaside Road]] {{waypoint -186.02, -0.84, -471.10}}.
- Zombies, just south of the docks around {{waypoint -342.16, -22.93, -239.87}} or in the eastern [[Fallow Farmlands]] {{waypoint -19.44, -0.53, -770.78}}.
- Gnolls, in the [[Fallow Farmlands]] {{waypoint 191.28, -24.14, -650.86}}.
- Centaurs, at the [[Northern Bandit Camp]] {{waypoint 1195.25, -0.61, 590.02}} or by [[Captain Ganwail]] in the [[Crested Lowlands]] {{waypoint 125, 0, 60}}.

## Rewards

- At least 17s 13c
