## Steps

1. Kill [[A holgresh fireflinger|holgresh fireflingers]], [[A holgresh incanter|incanters]] or [[A holgresh warpriest|warpriests]] until you get 4 spell books.
   - the new questmob icon works but monsters may not always have a spellbook
1. Return to Elder Kionn within the Sanctuary of Tears

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Corrupted Chainlink Legguards]] (Shamans)
  - [[Corrupting Cloth Pantaloons]] (Mages)
  - [[Withering Combine Armor Greaves]] (Clerics)
  - [[Withering Treant Greaves]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - Brawler legs
  - [[Mist Panther Skin Leggings]] (Druids)
  - [[Corrupted Soul Chainlink Legguards]] (Scouts)
