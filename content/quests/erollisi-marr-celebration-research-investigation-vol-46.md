## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The avatar summoning rituals require the manipulation of immense power and are inherently dangerous. To reduce the chance of failure and provide an anchor for that power, the herald of Erollisi Marr has asked me to collect an item that may have once belonged to that god or one of their most loyal followers

- Collect [[Reliquary of Erollisi Marr|Reliquaries of Erollisi Marr]] from a powerful creature in Vaashkaani: Argent Sanctuary heroic dungeon.

**This does not update in [Solo]. It needs to be Vaashkaani: Argent Sanctuary [Heroic I]**

- Return to the [[Herald of the Huntress]] {{waypoint 20, 275, 125}} in [[Frostfang Sea]]
