## Steps

1. Kill 15 wedding guests. There are 8 available, but they have a short respawn time.
1. Speak to the angry relative again.
1. Kill decaying servers until you get 5 updates. The updates are semi-rare and the number of decaying servers is limited, but they have a short respawn time.
1. Speak to the angry relative again.
1. Kill the Head Usher.
1. Speak to the angry relative to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- [[Sealed Moldy Scroll]] *(required by the quest [[Beauty's Only Skin Deep]])*
