## [[A Broken Barrier]]

1. Find Arch Druid [[Veryn P'Neru]] INSIDE the [[Shadow Oak]] at {{waypoint 442, -15, -265}} in [[Darklight Wood]].

## Reports from the Wounded

- Speak to Veryn and get the following four quests. They can be done simultaneously and in any order:

### [[Broken Barrier: Wounded of the Fire]]

- Return to Veryn to complete all 4 Wounded quests and to receive the next quest.

## [[Broken Barrier: Elemental Bindings]]

   - She will give you your next quest, and it's a toughie...

## [[Broken Barrier: Tilzax V'Reryn]]

   - Easy, eh? Tilzax will give you your next quest.

## [[Broken Barrier: The Bite of the Wolf]]

   - Tilzax gives you your next "quest".

## [[Broken Barrier: Veryn's Call]]

   - After giving you a nice chunk of AA for walking down some stairs, Veryn will send you off on the final quest of the Fabled epic.

## [[Broken Barrier: Lessons of the Fallen]]

   - Congratulations! With enough Heal Crit you'll soon be able to forget what "power" is while healing.

## Making it Mythical: [[Broken Barrier: Sealing of the Blade|Sealing of the Blade]]

****Important Note:** The above steps must be done **in order** and the items must be used before you leave each zone or the item will vanish.*

## Rewards

- You receive the [[Bite of the Wolf (Fabled)|fabled Bite of the Wolf]] at the end of [[Broken Barrier: Lessons of the Fallen]]. This is all you need in order to start and complete the [[Epic Conversion Timeline]].
- [[Broken Barrier: Sealing of the Blade|Sealing the blade]] with the three epic items will grant you the [[Bite of the Wolf (Mythical)|Mythical Bite of the Wolf]] which now includes a clickable 3-hit stoneskin, an increase to the ticks of your heal over time spells and a slightly more powerful power regen.
