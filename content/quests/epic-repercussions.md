## Requirements

- You must have completed [[Fiery Jewel of the Underfoot]].
- You must be able to speak [[Draconic]].
  - *You must speak [[Krombral]] to finish the quest [[To Speak as a Dragon]].*
- It is no longer required for you to have your class [[Epic Weapons|epic weapon]].
  - If you don't have your epic weapon, [[Kinloch Skry'mir]] will give you a [[Shattered Weapon]].
- You need to be at least level 85 to zone into [[Vasty Deep: The Vestigial Cella]].
- This quest is not available to [[Beastlord|Beastlords]] or [[Channeler|Channelers]].
- *Do not betray during this quest*. You cannot change your class until it is completed.

## Steps

![Aglthin pondweed](images/Aglthin_pondweed.png)

1. Speak with [[Lama Mukhlisah]] at {{waypoint -439, 380, 654}} in [[Quel'ule]], which is located in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]].
1. Lama Mukhlisah needs many items in order to hold the conversation with the ancient scholars.
   - Hunt [[A slitherstrike mamba|slitherstrike mambas]] to obtain an unscathed venom sac, a rare update. The mambas can be found around {{waypoint 171, 369, 1010}}.
   - Hunt [[A Stonebrunt scintilla|Stonebrunt scintillas]] for six glittering particles. These are common updates from scintillas which float around {{waypoint 44, 364, 1006}}.
   - Harvest four Aglthin Pondweed at the bottom of [[Aglthin Pond]]. {{waypoint -60, 369, 929}} (clicking breaks invis)
1. Return to [[Lama Mukhlisah]] who awaits you behind the [[Mausoleum of Scholars]] at {{waypoint 551, 233, -97}}. She will then summon a risen scholar who speaks of a tome penned by Reba Garus. After the scholar has spoken, hail Lama once more.
1. Seek out a book about spell transference within Erudin's great library.
   - Head into the [[Library of Erudin]] {{waypoint -554, 530, -137}}. The update can be found on the First floor of the [[Library of Erudin]], above where [[Archivist Fahim]] is located, as an open book lying on any of the tables; you must agro Fahim in the center of the basement to make the stairway appear to the First Floor of the Library. You can have your pet or merc attack while you raise to the second floor. He will not follow as he has a rather short leash.
   - Grab the book on one of the crescent black marble tables on the first floor.  Here are some possible locations for the update:
     - {{waypoint -261, -1, 13}}.
     - {{waypoint -151.26, -1.08, -95.04}}.
     - {{waypoint -143.01, -1.08, 8.61}}.
     - {{waypoint -249.40, -1.25, -12.62}}.
1. Return to [[Lama Mukhlisah]] at {{waypoint -439, 380, 654}} in Quel'ule.
1. Collect the items required to perform the spell transference.
   - The 8 ensorcelled mercury can be found as uncommon updates from killing [[Flowing mercury]] in [[The Hole]] around {{waypoint -71, -81, -1035}}.
   - The Mindflow Crystal is in [[Demitrik's Bastion]] (entrance in Paineel at {{waypoint 1850.64, -371.68, 3450.42}}).
     - You will need to kill the first set of nameds ([[Shirinu]], [[Tirini]] and [[Protector Ghondu]]) to activate the ladder and take the West Side teleporter at {{waypoint 46, 36, 0}} to get to the area where the update is located (SW of the the East Side teleporter).
     - The update is a pile of rubble  at {{waypoint -214.41, -37.84, 134.99}} directly below  {{waypoint -225, -2, 138}} ; you will need to jump down two ledges. The wayfinder does not work for this.
     - A cleared zone will work fine for this update.
   - The Erud Binding Powder is obtained by killing [[V'lad Olkeen]] at {{waypoint 60.54, -16.00, -306.51}} in the [[Erudin Research Halls]].
   - Convince a dragon to part with their blood of their own choice.
     1. Defeat [[Queen Gwarthlea]] in [[Vasty Deep: The Vestigial Cella]]. If you have already killed Queen Gwarthlea in the past, you can skip this step and use a cleared instance instead.
     1. After [[Queen Gwarthlea]] is dead, hail [[Delahnus the Dauntless]] for the dragon blood; he will be standing where the Queen was when the fight started.
1. Speak to [[Lama Mukhlisah]] in [[Quel'ule]] at {{waypoint -439, 380, 654}}. She will tell you that she can no longer help you. (if she is not showing at the location type /target Lama then when you have her targeted hit H for hail.)
1. Seek out [[Sa'ib Waseem]] in [[Paineel]] at {{waypoint 1996, -299, 3341}}.
1. Sa'ib agrees to meet you at his hut in [[Toxxulia's Reach]]. Speak to him there to complete the quest. {{waypoint 619, 113, 2971}}
