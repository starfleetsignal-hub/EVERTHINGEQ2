## Steps

1. Turn 3 [[A dissenting citizen|dissenters]]. They jump you near Beggar's Court {{waypoint -149, -53, -352}}
   - make sure to _personally_ dispatch 3 of the 4 guys, if your pet or the guard kills them they wont count, and you need to delete the quest and get it again.
1. Right-click the corpses and choose "Turn".
1. Return to [[Glaria]].
1. Seek out 5 crazed Freebloods in [[Longshadow Alley: Daeneth Cloister]] {{waypoint -91, -35, -362}}.
  - Look for [[A Freeblood savage]] in the houses around the zone.
1. Return to [[Glaria]].

## Rewards

- At least 25g 70s 61c
