## Notes

- This quest is only available for players of evil-alignment. For players of good-alignment, the counterpart is [[A Wizard's Request]].

## Steps

1. Obtain the following in any order:
   - 8 vials of drake blood from the [[An ebon drake|ebon drakes]]. (uncommon update) {{waypoint -915, 100, 1365}}
   - 5 shambler spore pods from the [[A shambling shaper|shambling shapers]]. (common update)
1. Return to [[Eurynomis]].

## Reward

- 2g 99s 47c
