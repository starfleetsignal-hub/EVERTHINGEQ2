## Steps

1. Gather 6 crystals within the west part of the Tizmak Cave
   - *Note: Some of the crystals are traps and pop [[A crystalline predator|crystalline predators]].*
   - They can be gathered under invisibility.
1. Speak with [[Bethra Lartius]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- [[Tizmak Hanging Sconce]]
