1. Talk to Savant Ka'im in the Academy of Arcane Science in North Freeport {{waypoint 4, 7, -125}} to receive this City Task.
   - Slay the 15 [[Blacktalon summoner|Blacktalon Partisans]] in the [[Palace of the Awakened]]. on level 2 {{waypoint 12, 26, -10}} and level 3 {{waypoint -0.78, 57, -3}}
1. Return to [[Savant Ka'im]].
