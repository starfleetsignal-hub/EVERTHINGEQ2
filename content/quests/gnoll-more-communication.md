## Steps

*I found a crudely written plan on the corpse of one of the gnolls. The plans are incomplete, but if I find more of these plans someone back at the Darkpaw Dugout will more than likely be able to piece them together and figure out what the gnolls are trying to do.*

1. Collect 10 more crude gnoll plans
   - Crude gnoll plans drop from a small chest from any group in Darkpaw Warrens containing at least 1 gnoll.
   - 1 drop per encounter, and this is a solo update
1. Speak with Ranger Alphin in Darkpaw Warrens {{waypoint -28, -43, -253}}
