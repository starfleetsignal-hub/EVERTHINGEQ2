I'm pretty sure Tinkersmith was planning to test the explosive hammer prototype on me, but when I raised the question he suggested constructing an armored clockwork test target. This seems like something I can assist with.

## Steps

1. Construct the [[Target Clockwork]].
   - *There is a work bench right beside [[Charles Q Tinkersmith|Tinkersmith]]*
   - *You receive the [[Explosive Hammer Prototype (Charm)|Explosive Hammer Prototype]] and the recipe for [[Test Target Clockworks]] from [[Charles Q Tinkersmith|Tinkersmith]] when you started the quest*
1. Place the clockwork target in the sparkling area near the column.
1. Hit the clockwork with the explosive hammer.
1. Return to [[Charles Q Tinkersmith]]

## Needed Materials

- [[Splitiron ore]] (1)
- [[Thalumbral root]] (3)
- [[Umbrite]] (3)
- [[Thaumic Coal]] (5)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13200 status
- Coin of the Grand Artificer
- +1,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +5,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
