## Steps

1. Speak with [[Hyggin]] {{waypoint -707, 9, 289}}
1. Speak with Skrit to receive the recipe [[Skrit's Pickles (recipe)|Skrit's Pickles]] which autoscribes to your spell book, and which you may not be able to see until you click on the cooking pot.
1. Craft 5 [[Skrit's Pickles (Skrit's Pickles)|Skrit's Pickles]] at the cooking pot next to Skrit.
   - Resources needed: 5 [[Hylocereus fruit]]s and 5 [[Sulfur fruit]]s (bush nodes) from Pygmy Brambles as well as 5 [[Effulgent kindling]].
   - Note that mass produce will not work.  You need to do the recipe 5 times.
1. Speak to Hyggin

## Rewards

- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]]
- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
