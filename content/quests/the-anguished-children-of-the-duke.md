## Steps

1. Recover three stolen totems from the [[Mines of Meldrath (POI)]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]]. Entrances are located at {{waypoint -1230, 95, 1085}} and {{waypoint -1300, 95, 1055}}.
   - {{waypoint -1330, 75, 1280}}
   - {{waypoint -1450, 80, 1150}}
   - {{waypoint -1460, 95, 965}}
1. Return to Bronlor Stormhammer.
1. Speak to [[Deviser Obin Prayerbolt]] in [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]]. {{waypoint -635, 175, 1095}}
1. Go to [[Klak'Anon]] and kill [[A malfunctioning Bibliotecha 1000]]. {{waypoint -230, -10, -60}}
1. Return to Obin Prayerbolt.

## Rewards

- +5,000 faction with **Brell Serilis**
- Summon: [[Underfoot Attendant]]
