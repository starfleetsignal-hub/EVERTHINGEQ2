## Notes

- **This quest is available to get and complete only on April 1st.**
- For the first riddle, taking the world globe to Moors is the fastest route.
- For the second riddle, use the Ulteran spire (south of the docks) in Enchanted Lands to go to The Sundered Frontier.

## Steps

![candle](images/Candle.jpg)

1. Speak with [[Imenand]] and answer a preliminary riddle. He will then give you a series of riddles to challenge you again.
   - Q: A goblin found an interesting thing while wandering through the forest. It has eyes but can't see, tongue but can't talk, a soul but can never find love.
   - A: A shoe.
   - Q: In Everfrost there is something that can seen, taken and even held, but never touched. What is it?
   - A: Your breath.

![fire](images/Fire.jpg)

1. Travel to [[Moors of Ykesha]] and click on the candle on the counter at 1,680 453 806.
1. Return to Imenand for the next riddle.
1. Travel to the [[The Sundered Frontier]], then travel to [[Paineel]]. Hail the Temporal Guide in Paineel and travel to [[Kerra Isle]]. Click the fire at 1,310 14 2,330 (in the hunter camp, not far from the landing pad).
1. Return to Imenand in the Enchanted Lands to complete the quest.
