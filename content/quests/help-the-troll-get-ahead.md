- *This quest is also part of the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Locate and kill [[A Nightfall giant]] in Nektulos Forest around {{waypoint -733.91, 55.48, -174.05}}.
1. Return to [[Kregnok Legbreaker]] {{waypoint 1015, -42, -687}}. He will turn hostile (Level 30 non-heroic) and attack you.

## Notes

- If you kill [[Kregnok Legbreaker]] at the end of the quest, he may drop a [[Chests|chest]] containing [[Kregnoks Axe]].

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Everling's Family Crest]]
  - [[Nightfall Splinter]]
  - [[Partially Decaying Giant Tooth]]
  - [[Totem of the Fray]]
