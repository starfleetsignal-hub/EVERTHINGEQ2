## Steps

1. Take the empty jug that [[Wyrwynne Shadhe]] gave you and fill it with water from the pond at the [[Druid Ring (BBM)|Druid Ring]] at {{waypoint 0.82, 179.15, 569.53}}.
1. Collect 10 spiritual floras by sprinkling water on the tree stumps scattered throughout [[Butcherblock Mountains]] and killing the [[Tormented growth|tormented growths]] that spawn afterwards.   You can find stumps near Kaladim, the Quarry, and East Fort Irontoe.    Exact locations include:
   - {{waypoint -316, 150, 153}}
   - {{waypoint -316, 153, 90}}
   - {{waypoint -244, 150, 119}}
   - {{waypoint -354, 152, 83}}
   - {{waypoint -367, 151, 43}}
   - {{waypoint -352, 152, 11}}
   - {{waypoint -455, 151, -67}}
   - {{waypoint -487, 153, 13}}
   - {{waypoint -103, 208, -105}}
   - {{waypoint -37, 211, -78}}
   - {{waypoint 1, 176, -307}}
   - {{waypoint -137, 151, -406}}
   - Added: Without having to head to the above stumps loc by loc, you can go just outside East Fort Irontoe and there is more than 10 just there (where the [[A Shadesire defender|Shadesire defenders]] are), or alternatively, find 4 stumps easily northwest of the Druid Ring and then head to the Quarry where there are at least 8 more.
1. Return to [[Wyrwynne Shadhe]].

## Rewards

- Experience
- At least 69s 67c
- +2000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
