## Steps

1. Obtain the following ingredients:
   - 6 [[Barracuda]] fished from any fishing node in [[Kunark]]
   - Enter [[Chelsith]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]] and collect:
     - 4 *perfectly marinated steaks* from any Yha-lei
     - 4 *tender lurker tentacles* from [[An abyss lurker|abyss lurkers]]
     - 4 *jars of secernment* from [[A flammed secernment|flammed secernments]]
1. Return the ingredients to [[Goddin Ratsachak]] in [[Kylong Plains]], who makes you [[A Krunchy Kunzar Roll]].
1. Deliver the Krunchy Kunzar Roll to [[Tszuij Adawhi]] at [[Dreg's Landing]]. {{waypoint 912, 12, 215}}
1. Return to Goddin Ratsachak.

## Rewards

- At least 18g 39s 23c
- [[A Krunchy Kunzar Roll]]
- *One of the following:*
  - [[Blessed Underfoot Stones]] - Fighter
  - [[Lucky Kobold's Foot]] - Scout
  - [[Bauble of Spiritual Effectiveness]] - Necromancer
  - [[Soothing Amulet of the Void]] - Fury/Templar
