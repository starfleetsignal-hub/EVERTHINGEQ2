## Steps

1. Return to Deepwater Watch - updates as soon as you step on the beach, around {{waypoint -505, 4, 486}}
1. Search the isle for clues as to where Rizalla went - a few steps up the path {{waypoint -531, 4, 519}} you hear a voice which updates quest
1. Continue to search Deepwater Watch for Rizalla -  few more steps up the path, voice updates quest around {{waypoint -543, 14, 600}}
1. Search the heart of Deepwater  Watch for Rizalla - keep going South up a path towards tower, updates around {{waypoint -458, 37, 737}}
1. Reach the Deepwater Watch lighthouse - keep going West towards lighthouse, when you get to the doorway a Kyanite Knight standing guard will attack you
1. Defeat 2 x a Kyanite Knight
1. Enter Strongswell Lighthouse - update around middle of the room, clickable pillar appears by back wall (teleport)
1. Use the pillar to ascend lighthouse - click to go up to second floor, be ready to fight, several watchers and sentries  up there, plus one non aggro hooded NPC
1. Approach the figure at the top of the lighthouse - approach a hooded lich who attacks you.
1. Defeat Yevrah Ti'Rizeth - when he is almost dead he will take from you ruby pendant that Dougas gave you and port away.
1. Search the room for signs of Rizalla - approach small table at {{waypoint -596, 178, 750}} with Rizalla's skull on it  ![](images/Mystery_of_Deepwater_Watch_quest_-_Rizalla's_skull.jpg)
1. pick up Rizalla's skull.
1. Return to Dougas Troter in [[Port Woe]]
