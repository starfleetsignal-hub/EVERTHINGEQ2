## Steps

1. Speak to [[Hichapcit]] {{waypoint -931.91, 81.94, -376.15}} on an eastern platform in [[Pakiat Bluffs]].
1. Master the trials she tasks you with:
   - Kill 5 [[Molded sandbrute|molded sandbrutes]] {{waypoint -642.22, 63.86, -653.93}} in the valleys in the [[Pakiat Bluffs]].
   - Retrieve the ***heart of the storm***.![a pile of rocky sand](images/A_pile_of_rocky_sand.jpg)
     1. Collect [[Heavy Sand|heavy sand]] from ***a pile of rocky sand*** {{waypoint -411.81, 49.57, -660.83}} in a northern valley in [[Pakiat Bluffs]].![turbulent sandstorm](images/Turbulent_sandstorm.jpg)
     1. Click on a ***turbulent sandstorm***, that paths from the valley to the east of the sand pile {{waypoint -490.79, 54.28, -717.67}} all the way to the shore and back, to retrieve the *heart of the storm*.
1. Return to [[Hichapcit]] at {{waypoint -931.91, 81.94, -376.15}}.
1. Speak again to [[Elishuru]] {{waypoint 174.03, 589.48, -577.04}} who has returned to [[Khali'Yrzu]].
   - *Note: There is a nearby teleportation platform {{waypoint -839.96, 83.28, -162.99}} in [[Pakiat Bluffs]] to take you to [[Khali'Yrzu]].*
