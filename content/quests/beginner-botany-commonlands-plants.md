## Steps

1. Travel to [[Commonlands]] and gather the following in order:
   1. 3 Savannah leaves around the druid rings around {{waypoint 746.36, -59.8, -131.94}}
   1. 3 Bits of Bark around the [[Forgotten Valley]] around {{waypoint 557.71, -52.65, 502.56}}
   1. ![Grave Moss](images/Grave_Moss.png)3 Turmoil Grave Moss at [[Turmoil Cemetery]] around {{waypoint -1323.57, -69.27, 365.5}}
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].

## Hints

- Gathered items are not trackable
- Turning off Flora in the display options goes a long way to helping you see the sparkles emitted by the moss. The moss itself is extremely tiny and nearly the exact same color as the grass textures  of the ground in the cemetery.

## Rewards

- Unlock the [[Days of Summer: 2023|Days of Summer 2023: Week 2]] Holiday event rewards that you can purchase from [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2293, -199, 2506}}
