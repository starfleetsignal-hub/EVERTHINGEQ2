*After discovering the newfound ability, I am urged to seek out ways to hone it and increase its power. The road ahead is uncharted, the journey awaits!

This quest grants the paladin's epic spell, [[Holy Stampede]]. The apprentice version of the spell is granted at the start of the quest, and the spell upgrades throughout the quest line.

## Starting the Quest

1. Complete the Renewal of Ro [[Renewal of Ro Timeline#Renewal of Ro Signature Quest|signature]] quests.
1. Complete the Renewal of Ro [[Renewal of Ro Timeline#Music Box Quests|music box]] quests.
1. Turn in sets of fertilizer to [[Merchant Fiza Tazi]] in the [[Hopewell Harbor]] region of [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]]. Each "set" requires one [[Chest of scorpion exoskeletons]], one [[Desert charger horns]], and one [[Red desert sand]]. Each set has a 20% chance of returning a [[Manaspark Lotus Flower]] (the remaining chance is for a random collectible from RoR).
1. Examine the [[Manaspark Lotus Flower]] from your inventory.

Simply starting the quest rewards you with the apprentice version of [[Holy Stampede]].

## Steps

1. I must find some way to upgrade my scroll to a higher spell tier.
   - Find a way to upgrade my scroll to Journeyman
   - Defeat [[Leafjaw]] within [[Takish Badlands: Overgrowth (Solo)]].
   - Completing this step rewards the journeyman version of [[Holy Stampede]].
1. I must find some way to upgrade my scroll to a higher spell tier.
   - Find a way to upgrade my scroll to Adept
