## Notes

- This is one of five "training sessions" you must complete in order to complete the quest [[Dream Weaving]] to receive a [[Gryphon Call]].
- You can only complete one of these five training sessions every 18 hours.

## Steps

1. Do one of the following:
   - Harvest 5 [[Brontotherium Meat]] from dens in [[The Great Divide]].
   - Buy these 5 meat slabs from a broker.
1. Quest auto-updates and auto-finishes .

Note: If you already have them on you before you start the quest you can post five of them on the market and removing them to auto-finish the quest. Removing them from a harvest box (either the Harvesting Supply Depot guild hall amenity or the Tinkered Personal Harvest Depot) will also work to update your quest.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- Choice of [[Frost Gryphon]] or [[Boreal Gryphon]] on completion of the final of the 5 quests.
