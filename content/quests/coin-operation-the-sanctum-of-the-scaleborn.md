## Notes

- You must be able to speak [[Druzaic]] to complete this quest.

## Steps

![How an idol looks on the wall.](images/An_idol.jpg)

1. Enter the [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]] and interact with 'The Yore Flame' within the [[Mouth of the Sanctum]] at {{waypoint 12, 157, -40}}.
1. Speak with the [[Shade of Bylze]]. He will task you with finding 20 hidden scrolls within the room you are in (Mouth of the Sanctum). The scrolls are hidden inside of small idols on top of ledges lining the walls. Target them with a ranged weapon/spell to break them, which causes the scrolls to fall to the ground directly beneath.
   - Once you spot the idols, you can just move around the room attacking each one. If you are missing a specific one, use the table [[#Scroll Locations|below]] to find it:
   - Each person on the quest must collect their own set of scrolls. Respawn time on the idols is about 6 minutes.
   - Scroll #14 will fall into a bowl of water.
1. Return to [[Shade of Bylze|The Shade of Bylze]].
1. Slay [[A silent sentinel|silent sentinels]] for common updates until you have received all 7 pieces of armor.
1. Speak to the Shade of Bylze for your reward.
