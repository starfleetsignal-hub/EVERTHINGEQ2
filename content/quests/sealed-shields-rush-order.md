## Steps

Create 2 each of any 3 from the random list below:

- [[Fulginate Melodic Coat|Forged Fulginate Melodic Coat]]
- [[Fulginate Melodic Coif|Forged Fulginate Melodic Coif]]
- [[Fulginate Melodic Leggings|Forged Fulginate Melodic Leggings]]
- [[Fulginate Kite Shield (Level 40) (Crafted)|Forged Fulginate Kite Shield]]
- [[Fulginate Tower Shield (Level 40) (Crafted)|Forged Fulginate Tower Shield]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

## Rewards

- At Least 72s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
