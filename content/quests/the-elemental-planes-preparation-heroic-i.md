The first few chapters of Volume II of The Planar Chronicler detail some of what one might encounter within the Elemental Planes. I feel as though I'm prepared, but one can only really depend on experience in the face of danger.

## Steps

- Complete this chapter on [Heroic I] missions.:
  - Bring down the Bixel Queen in [[Eryslai: The Bixel Hive (Heroic)]]
  - Slay the Siblings of the Earth in [[Vegarlson: Ruins of Rathe (Heroic)]]
  - Destroy the Master of Ro in [[Doomfire: The Enkindled Towers (Heroic)]]
  - Defeat the Violent Herald in [[Awuidor: The Nebulous Deep (Heroic)]]

## Rewards

- At least 7p 79g 62s 50c 122,400 status
- 3 [[Planar Stones (Chaos Descending)|Planar Stones]]
- 65 [[Archivist's Tremissis]]
- [[Weekly Elemental Mission Reward Crate (Version 6)|Weekly Elemental Mission Reward Crate]]

  - 15 [[Celestial Alexandrite]]
  - 15 [[Celestial Citrine]]
  - 15 [[Celestial Garnet]]
  - 15 [[Celestial Morganite]]
  - 15 [[Celestial Onyx]]
  - 15 [[Celestial Spinel]]
  - 15 [[Celestial Zircon]]

## Note

This quest, along with others, is required to complete the [[Celestial Doctrine]] quest given by [[Vorai]], located in [[Myrist, the Great Library|Myrist, The Great Library]] in the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]].
