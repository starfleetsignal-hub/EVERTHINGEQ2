## Steps

1. Go to [[The Sinking Sands]] and kill the following mobs to receive uncommon quest updates:
   - [[Dune spiderling|dune spiderlings]] for 5 exquisite strands of dune spiderling silk.
   - [[Whale shark|whale sharks]] for 7 silver-lined shark gills.
   - [[Coastal caller|coastal callers]] for 9 golden siren locks of hair.
   - any type of crocodile or caimen throughout the zone for 21 pristine caimen teeth
- Return to Isdala, Princess of Truth.
- Speak to [[Ayala]] in the Court of Truth. {{waypoint 6, 0, 6}}
- Speak to Ayala again.
- Speak to [[Zabah]] in [[Maj'Dul]] at {{waypoint 7, 150, 118}}.
- Return to Isdala, Princess of Truth.

## Rewards

- At least 6g 19s 1c
- Select one of the following:
  - [[Amorisa]]
  - [[Love's Bite]]
