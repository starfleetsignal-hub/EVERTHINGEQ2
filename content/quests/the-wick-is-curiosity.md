## Notes

- requires at least -2,000 [[Pride Pakiat (Faction)]]

## Steps

![A typical crystal](images/Crystal.png)
Click on the crystals in-order - the 'next' one does not spawn until the current one has been picked-up. Clicking on the crystals is NOT a group update. Each member must click on the crystals.

1. Pick up crystal at {{waypoint -855, 31, 247}}.
1. Pick up crystal at {{waypoint -896, 29, 320}}.
1. Pick up crystal at {{waypoint -855, 31, 397}}.
1. Pick up crystal at {{waypoint -828, 29, 398}}.
1. Kill the [[An earth elemental|earth elemental]] that spawns and loot the chest to receive [[A satchel of stolen crystals]].
1. Return to [[Ta-hetu]] at {{waypoint -462, 162, -777}}.
1. Click on the Starfire Crystal Storage![Starfire Crystal Storage](images/Starfire_Crystal_Storage.jpg) at {{waypoint -448, 165, -804}}.
1. Return to [[Ta-hetu]] at {{waypoint -462, 162, -777}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
