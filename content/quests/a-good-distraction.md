## Steps

1. Speak to [[Ordin Sleetfoot]], right next to Thirin in the cave.
1. "I should travel into the Velinoid Catacombs and act as a distraction so none of the Velinoids discover Ordin.  He says I will eventually find him as I travel through the cavern."  The Catacombs are, of course, the area you opened up in the last quest.
1. You'll come on Ordin at the first fork.  2 Velinoids will attack - kill them and speak to Ordin.
1. Continue on in the catacombs.  Go either left or right, it's a circle, and you find Ordin again at the other side.  2 more Velinoids attack, and again you kill them and hail Ordin.
1. Return to Thirin.
