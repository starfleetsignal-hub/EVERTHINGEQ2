## Starting the Quest

The quest will immediately be offered to you upon examining [[Mayong Mistmoore's Amulet|the amulet]] once it is in your possession.

## Steps

1. Seek out an elf near the entrance to [[New Tunaria]] in the [[Greater Faydark]].
   - This is [[Commander Marcus Traeis]] at {{waypoint -1006, 85, 660}}
1. Show Marcus the amulet.

## Rewards

- One of the Following:
  - [[Koada'Vie Earring]]
  - [[Koada'Vie Necklace]]
  - [[Koada'Vie Signet Ring]]
  - [[Marcus' Lucky Coin]]
