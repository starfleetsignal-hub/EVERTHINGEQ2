## Steps

1. Push 4 Barrels along the [[Walk of Glory]] starting at {{waypoint -329, -393, 4098}}
   - **NOTE**: You can kick the barrels while invisible, without coming out of invis.  Easy quest, with no aggro!
1. Speak with [[Klipklap]]

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +500 faction with **Othmir of Velious**
