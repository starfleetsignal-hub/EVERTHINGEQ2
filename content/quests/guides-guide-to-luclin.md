## Prerequisites

- You need access to the Blood of Luclin expansion to do this quest.
- You need to have unlocked the access to the overland zones by completing the adventure or crafter Prelude Quests [[Mythic Passage Timeline]]
- You need to be at least level 110 adventurer or crafter to enter [[The Blinding]]

## Starting the Quest

- The starting zone seems to be the Teren's Grasp Spire.
- The guide is not always there.
- The quest is offered to the whole group, but it can't be shared.
- You only need the guide to start the quest, there is no time limit to finish the quest.
- *Note: on Thurgadin Server last seen on 5th March 2020 at Moors of Ykesha Spire, so it seems he spawns around spires in any zone. The guide announces himself in general chat!*
- *Note: 23 Dec 2021 - You will get a key in the mail from the Duality called [[A Vigilant Sanctum Key]].  Use the key and you will be ported to the Mythic passage.*
