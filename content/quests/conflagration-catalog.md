## Notes

- You have a choice of using Catalogue Creature ability (one is auto scribed in your book if you don't have it already), or killing the creatures. Both methods will update this quest.

## Steps

1. *I must catalog specific flame-veined beings and fire elementals **in Doomfire: Ro's Sweatshop***
   - Catalog a blazewing devourer
   - Catalog a construct of fire
   - Catalog a searing scourge
   - Catalog a fire hopper
   - Catalog a silk courier
1. Return to Boilz Sizzlespit.
1. *I must catalog flame-veined beings and fire elementals that appear **across Norrath** during the Scorched Sky Celebration. Note: the creatures below are NOT in the instance, instead they are the fiery ones spawning in many zones, that activate when you approach them.
   - Catalog [[A Scorch Storm Elemental]]
   - Catalog [[A Pyre Elemental]]
   - Catalog [[A Blaze Elemental]]
   - Catalog [[An Ember Elemental]]
   - Catalog [[A Fiendish Incinerator]].
1. Return to Boilz Sizzlespit inside Doomfire: Ro's Sweatshop instance. (Portal in Moors of Ykesha)

## Rewards

- 5 x [[Mithril Jubilation Medal]]

One of the following:

- [[Essence of Conflagration]] weapon aura
- 4 x [[Coin of Ember]]
