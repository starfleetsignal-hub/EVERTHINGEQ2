## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Solusek Ro has informed me that summoning an Avatar requires the land to be attuned to their element. To achieve this, altars have been set up around the region, but they need to be supplied with power. I have been asked to assist with this by collecting objects infused with the essence of slain foes and offer them to an altar.

1. Collect 3 [[Infused Flame Shard|infused flame shards]] (random body drop from a mystic grappler in Zimara Breadth)
   - NOTE: corpses left by these mobs disappear quickly. In order to give yourself more time to loot the corpse, make sure the mob isn't moving before you kill it
   - also drops from A metal-infused daggerclaw and A scorched direfang
1. Use the [[Infused Flame Shard|infused flame shards]] and empower an altar.  Random spawn in Lavastorm, its yellow sparkles visible far away, possible locations of altar
   - {{waypoint -140, -130, 347}} near the original entrance to [[Najena's Dungeon]]
   - {{waypoint -427, -114, 305}} just west of [[Dagger Spires Pass]]
   - {{waypoint -522, -117, 446}} near the Sootfoot Camp across from [[Fennin's Pass]]
   - {{waypoint -654, -67, -182}} a little southwest of the Burning Prince
   - {{waypoint -46, 8, -207}} in [[Hydra Pass]]
   - {{waypoint -129, 19, 54}} near the entrance to [[Dragon's Breath Tunnel]]
   - {{waypoint -185, -10, -560}} in a gully in [[Solusek Valley]]
   - {{waypoint -285, 85, -215}} near Solusek Eye entrance
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Burning Prince]] at {{waypoint -772, -129, -368}} in [[Lavastorm]]
