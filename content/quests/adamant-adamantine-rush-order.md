## Steps

Craft 6 items from the random list below:

- [[Rosewood Wand (Level 60) (Crafted)|Rosewood Wand]]
- [[Rosewood Longbow (Level 60) (Crafted)|Rosewood Longbow]]
- [[Rosewood Fighting Baton (Level 60) (Crafted)|Rosewood Fighting Baton]]
- [[Spirit Totem of the Beholder (Crafted)|Spirit Totem of the Beholder]]
- [[Rosewood Sorcerer's Staff (Level 60) (Crafted)|Rosewood Sorcerer's Staff]]
- [[Adamantine throwing axe (Crafted)|Adamantine Throwing Axe]]
- [[Adamantine throwing dagger (Crafted)|Adamantine Throwing Dagger]]
- [[Adamantine throwing hammer (Crafted)|Adamantine Throwing Hammer]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

## Rewards

- At Least 6g 64s 30c
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
