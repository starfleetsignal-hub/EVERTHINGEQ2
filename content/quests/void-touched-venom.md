## Steps

1. Kill 15 void-touched animals around [[Anchor of Bazzul (POI)|Anchor of Bazzul]] {{waypoint -216, -52, 148}}
   - [[A void-touched fauna stalker|Void-touched fauna stalkers]], [[A void-touched alligator|void-touched alligators]], and [[A void-touched snake|void-touched snakes]] all work; [[A void-touched Brokenskull|Void-touched Brokenskulls]] do not.
1. Return to [[Lucius Vandermarr]] in [[Firmroot Moot]].

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +1200 faction with **Survival Accord**
