## Steps

![The remains of an explorer](images/Remains_of_an_explorer.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Turtle]] who roams between {{waypoint 31, 8, -54}} to [[The Hidden Camp]] at {{waypoint -120, 14, 160}}.
1. Speak to [[Rada Stoutheart]] aka Red. {{waypoint -125, 15, 182}}
1. Kill 3 [[An Evol Ew Thulian neophyte|Evol Ew Thulian neophyte]] near {{waypoint 162, -9, 174}}, {{waypoint 27, -12, 396}}, or {{waypoint -415, -2, 649}}.
1. Examine the remains of an explorer inside a tent. {{waypoint 158, -9, 164}}
1. Examine a book which will spawn inside one of the tents in one of the four Alliz Evol Ew camps at {{waypoint -318, -4, 578}}, {{waypoint 27, -12, 393}}, {{waypoint 23, -5, 508}} or {{waypoint -420, -6, 680}}
1. Speak to [[The Eye of Thule]] on top of [[The Temple of Alliz]] {{waypoint -323, 10, 852}}. You may have to kill two placeholders there, 2 [[Thulian ravage]]s.
1. Hunt down and kill 4 [[Modinite fugitive|Modinite fugitives]]. They will spawn at fixed locations, but only a couple will be up at the same time. Known locations are:
   - {{waypoint -261, -13, 924}}, southwest of the Temple of Alliz (southwest of where you just talked to the Eye of Thule)
   - {{waypoint 58, 26, 940}}, southwest of where Bouncer Hurd or its placeholder roams
   - {{waypoint -219, -1, 679}}, northwest of the Temple of Alliz next to the [[Toppled Hand of Thule]]
   - {{waypoint -429, -6, 667}}, northeast of the Temple of Alliz next to the lizardman camp
   - {{waypoint -314, -1, 592}}, southwest of [[Golgoth Bridge]] next to the lizardman camp
   - {{waypoint -447, 0, 300}}, west of [[Ogre Guard Tower Ruins]]
   - {{waypoint -247, 8, 75}}, west of [[Murdunk's Fall]]
   - {{waypoint -740, 7, 200}}, next to the [[Altar of the Four Canons]]
   - {{waypoint -1236, -1, 589}}, south of [[Alliz Ew Basin]] lake
   - {{waypoint -1231, 3, 657}}, northwest of [[Alliz Tae Outpost]]
   - {{waypoint -1121, 0, 765}}, west of the little pond on [[The Anaconda River]] leading through the tunnel towards [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]]
1. Speak to the final Modinite, [[An inquisitor prisoner]], inside a tent at a lizardman camp. {{waypoint -704, 0, 879}}
1. Return to the Eye of Thule. {{waypoint -325, 10, 854}}
1. Slay 6 [[A fetid Rallosian allegiant|fetid Rallosian allegiant]]s. {{waypoint -885, 0, 531}}
1. Return to the Eye of Thule.
1. Examine [[Runes of the Great Eye]] in your inventory.
1. Find the 4 broken hands of Thule (in any order):
   - {{waypoint -118, 3, 263}}
   - {{waypoint -245, -2, 687}}
   - {{waypoint -1014, -2, 840}}
   - {{waypoint -1286, -2, 463}}
1. Find the hidden tunnel that ends at a gateway. {{waypoint -900, -8, 334}}
