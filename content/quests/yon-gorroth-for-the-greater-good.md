## Steps

1. Talk to the [[Quartermaster Ryna]] at {{waypoint 564, 94, -687}} in [[Yon Gorroth]] and receive a [[Tishan's Crate (Item) (Version 3)|Tishan's Crate]]
1. Unpack the [[Tishan's Crate (Item) (Version 3)|crate]] and it will give you armor, accessories and weapons
   - Quest updates when you open the [[Tishan's Crate (Item) (Version 3)|Tishan's Crate]]
   - *Alternatively access [[Tishan's Lockbox (Yon Gorroth)|Tishan's Lockbox]] at {{waypoint 567.26, 94.55, -689.39}}.*
     - *You will get gear with 600 Resolve for your class from the crate, this will require 26 free slots in your bags.*
     - *Note: one handed primary weapons come without adornments, and the Waist has no orange Adornment (immunity runes).*
1. Talk to the quartermaster again and receive [[Tishan's Putrescent Nullfang]] mount, Equip this mountand summon it
1. talk to Quartermaster Ryna and you'll receive [[Tishan's Assisting Appendage]] familiar.
1. Talk to Quartermaster Ryna.
1. *Summon your new familiar [[Tishan's Assisting Appendage]]*
   1. Find this familiar in your bags, right click it and "Add to collection"
   1. find it in your familiar tab on character window, drag it to Equipped slot
   1. summon your new familiar
   1. talk to Quartermaster Ryna
1. Quartermaster Ryna suggests to cast [[D'Morte Vampire Mount Infusion]] from your abilities book.
   - *If you don't find it in your Abilities, get it from [[Tishan's Lockbox (Yon Gorroth)|Tishan's Lockbox]]*
1. Talk to Quartermaster Ryna and she suggests casting [[Zimaran Swag It Out Familiar Infusion]] from your abilities book.
   - *This step is skipped, when you have it allready casted*
1. Speak to Quartermaster Ryna and she tells you to hire [[Hangar Voidstomper]], a mercenary who's sitting on his mount to the left of her.
1. Speak to [[Hangar Voidstomper]] at {{waypoint 567, 94, -685}}
   - *You can hire him, recast Merc buff [[Spirit of Arcanna'se Mercenary Infusion]] and get him some armour out of [[Tishan's Lockbox (Yon Gorroth)|Tishan's Lockbox]].*
   - *If you don't find the merc buff in your Abilities, get it from [[Tishan's Lockbox (Yon Gorroth)|Tishan's Lockbox]]*
1. Speak to Quartermaster Ryna, she tells you about Prestige AA points and immunity runes in Tishan's box, then finishes the quest.
   - *Note: not prompted, but it is probably a good idea to clear the trash out of your bags.*

## Rewards

- No extra rewards for finishing the quest
- You will get the following during the quest:
  - Tisha's Crate given by Ryna
  - [[Tishan's Putrescent Nullfang]] (Mount) ![](images/Tishan's_Putrescent_Nulfang.jpg)
