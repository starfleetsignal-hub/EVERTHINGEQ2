## Steps

1. Visit the [[The Bells of Vhalen]]. {{waypoint -1300, 39, 47}}
1. Kill [[A grizzled badger|grizzled badgers]] or [[A glade deer|glade deer]] until you find a clue to the Sage's whereabouts.
1. Visit this list of locations in [[Antonica]] to find any sign of the Sage.  All are south and easterly from the [[The Bells of Vhalen]].
   - [[Bramble Woods]]
   - [[Qeynos Highway]] (The road running through the middle of the zone)
   - [[Northern Farmlands]] (AKA the McQuibble farm area at {{waypoint -1685, -25, -41}})
   - [[Gnollslayer Highlands]] (On big plateau west of Stormhold)
   - [[Scarecrow Fields]] (Southwest of the [[Keep of the Gnollslayers]])
   - [[Keep of the Gnollslayers]] (By [[The Thundering Steppes]] Griffin Tower, little island moat just north of tower.)
1. Return to [[Scholar Demini]] to for your reward.
