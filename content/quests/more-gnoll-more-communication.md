## Notes

- This is a repeatable quest.

## Steps

*I found a notice posted by the rangers asking for more of the gnoll plans found in the Darkpaw Warrens. While I am down there I should keep on the lookout for any gnolls carrying plans.*

1. Collect 10 more crude gnoll plans
   - Crude gnoll plans drop from a small chest from any group in Darkpaw Warrens containing at least 1 gnoll.
   - 1 drop per encounter, and this is a solo update
1. Speak with Ranger Alphin in Darkpaw Dugout {{waypoint -13, -77, -251}}
