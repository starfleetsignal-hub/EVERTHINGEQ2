*This quest is repeatable up to 5 times., with each turn-in giving a larger coin reward.*

## Prerequisites

- You must speak [[Ayr'Dal]] to receive this quest

## Steps

1. Kill 8 - 12 monsters (randomly chosen) within [[The Thundering Steppes]]. (You can abandon and restart the quest if you want to try to roll a different mob or kill count for the quest.)
   - Sirens
   - Centaurs
   - Gnolls
   - Skeletons
   - Firerock Giants

## Rewards

- At least 17s 13c
