## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 20+

## Steps

1. Collect four Relief Supplies crates near [[Rinna]].
1. Tend to the [[An injured Baubbleshire citizen|injured Baubbleshire citizens]]
1. Hand out food by placing it on plates near [[An injured Baubbleshire citizen|injured Baubbleshire citizens]] and [[A hungry Baubbleshire citizen|hungry Baubbleshire citizens]].
1. Return to [[Rinna]].
1. Speak to [[Accountant Melet]] in the [[Ironforge Exchange]] in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 341, -16, -95}}
1. Search outside the [[Ironforge Exchange]] for a clue: a scrap of cloth with the Bloodsaber insignia (looks like a pink magical shiny). {{waypoint 347, -21, -81}}
1. Search the [[Down Below]] in all corridors for glowing parchment to recover 4 **Royal Requisition Lists** stolen by the Bloodsabers.
   - ![Royal Requisition List](images/Royal_Requisition_List.jpg)
     - The lists will respawn rapidly. Check on desks in alcoves. Possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -11, -4, -134}}
     - {{waypoint -27, -4, -142}}
     - {{waypoint -35, 0, -131}}
     - {{waypoint -35, 0, -123}}
     - {{waypoint -65, 0, -151}}
     - {{waypoint -22, 0, -102}}
     - {{waypoint 126, 0, -121}}
     - {{waypoint 145, 0, -146}}
     - {{waypoint -145, 0, -74}}
1. Return to [[Accountant Melet]].
1. Return to [[Rinna]].

## Rewards

- XP only
