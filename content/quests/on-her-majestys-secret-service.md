## Notes

- This quest is also part of the [[Tenebrous Tangle Timeline]].
- If you complete this quest and then [[Betrayal Timeline|betray]] to [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]] before completing [[Learning from the Crystal]] (the eleventh quest in the Claymore Timeline, the final one in [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]]), you will be UNABLE to finish this quest as [[Murrar Shar (Tenebrous Tangle)|Murrar Shar]] will refuse to talk to you. Completing this quest will flag you to talk to Murrar regardless of alignment.
  - Petitioning will NOT work as of January 2023 on Varsoon Server. The GM will tell you to betray back to finish the quest, thus potentially forcing you to buy another betrayal token or go through the betrayal process again in order to continue the Claymore Timeline.
- With the release of the Sentinel's Fate expansion, both this quest and [[On The Overlord's Secret Service]] were moved from their home cities to Tenebrous Tangle.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Jabber Longwind (Tenebrous Tangle)|Jabber Longwind]]. {{waypoint -24, 21, 244}}
1. Speak to [[Murrar Shar (Tenebrous Tangle)|Murrar Shar]] in [[Hidden Refuge (POI)|Hidden Refuge]]. {{waypoint 191, -236, 184}}
   - If you do not have a mount that will absorb the fall for you, jump off the waterfall and you will land in the pool at the bottom.
