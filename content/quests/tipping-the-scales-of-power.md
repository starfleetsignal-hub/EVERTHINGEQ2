## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[Docile slither]]s or [[Agitated slither]]s along the bank of the [[Lower Tendril River]]. {{waypoint -207, -63, 126}}
1. Return to [[Ilmtar D'Viervs]].

## Rewards

- At least 4s 82c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Tempered Dagger]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Tempered Knuckles]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Tempered Mace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Tempered Sword]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Tempered Wand]] (All Mages, All Priests)
