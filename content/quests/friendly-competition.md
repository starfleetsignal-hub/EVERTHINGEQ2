## Prerequisites

- Must speak [[Gnomish]]

## Steps

1. [[Jon Jeffas]] suggested that I try my hand at building my own battleclock for the amateur category.
   - I should gather some spare springs from around the valley that leads to the Greater Faydark {{waypoint -480.59, 125.79, 610.62}}.
   - I should gather a sharp whirlyblade from near the entrance to klak'Anon {{waypoint -244.61, 88.34, 1392.58}}.
   - I should gather some armored plates from the scraps of destroyed clockworks around the Mazkeen Crater {{waypoint -755.46, 132.07, 774.70}}.
   - I should gather some used gnomish grease from around Gnomeland Security {{waypoint -559.79, 174.56, 921.26}}.
1. Return to [[Jon Jeffas]] with the components
1. I will attempt to use these blueprints ([[Battleclock Blueprints 1.1]]) to assemble a battleclock ([[Quixilver II]]).
   - *This recipe requires Artisan level 2, and is trivial at a Tinkering skill of 5. In addition to the supplies you collected, you'll need 5 [[Sparkling Coal]] per attempt [[Fizza Cogsworth]] sells it, right next to the Work Bench.*
   - *If you are not a tinkerer and attempt to craft this recipe at the work bench you will succeed due to "Tinkerfest Inspiration."*
1. I will place my battleclock into the ring and see how long it lasts...
   - *You must be in the ring - right click [[Quixilver II]] and select "use".
   - *If you have the expendables slot, this gets added there automatically so might not be in your inventory.*
1. I should see what [[Jon Jeffas]] thinks of the results.
