## Description

Deep within Demitrik's Bastion dwell roekillik geomancers and their creatures, the mocked elementals and the underfoot floaters. I must enter Demitrik's Bastion and kill the geomancers and their pets.

## Steps

1. I must enter [[Demitrik's Bastion]] and smash the underfoot floaters and mocked elemental golems, and kill the roekillik geomancers.
   - Kill 5 [[An underfoot floater|underfoot floater]]s
   - Kill 4 [[A mocked elemental golem|mocked elemental golem]]s
   - Kill 10 [[A roekillik geomancer|roekillik geomancer]]s
1. Return to Hafiz

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
