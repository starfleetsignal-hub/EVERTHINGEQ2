## Notes

- The dialog when you start the quest is about the [[Ewer of Sul'Dae (Lore)]].
  - *During the conversation you can tell that you've killed [[Godking Anuk]], but the quest will be the same.*

## Steps

1. Gather 12 **Discordant Suffused Rubble** in [[Mahngavi Wastes]] on [[Shattered Monolith Cay]] {{waypoint -119, 6, -687}}.![Discordant Suffused Rubble](images/Discordant_suffused_rubble.png)
1. Return to [[Tavian Faust]] in [[Forlorn Gist]].
1. Purchase a [[Crone-cast Focal Gem]] from [[Corpseolynne]] at {{waypoint -339, 14, 126}}
   - Requires to do 2 quests from [[Corpseolynne]] - [[Components for Corpseolynne's Concoction]] and [[Price of Information]] to make her a merchant.
1. Return to [[Tavian Faust]] in [[Forlorn Gist]] to receive [[Discordant Pummeler Animating Scroll]]. ![Vacrul Barrier Ward Totem](images/Vacrul_barrier_ward_totem.png)
1. Destroy 3 **Vacrul Barrier Ward Totems** in [[Acrid Loft]] and just across the bridge in [[Somber Acre]]:
   - {{waypoint -97, 0, 162}}
   - {{waypoint -183, 49, 150}}
   - {{waypoint -157, 51, 198}}
1. Return to [[Tavian Faust]].
