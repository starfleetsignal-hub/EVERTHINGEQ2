The research into the Elemental Planes continues for the Great Library of Myrist. This time, I've been tasked with studying the elements of water, so I am off to Awuidor: The Nebulous Deep.

## Steps

- Defeat targets within [[Awuidor: The Nebulous Deep (Solo)]]
1. Gut Glaucos
1. Defeat Nehalenea Frostbringer
1. Pummel Pontis Aqueous
1. Wash away Oceanus the Titan
1. Assassinate Aegaeon
