High up in the Sacred Borough I found the remains of a small being in a hollow log. The bones are covered in the same webbing I saw in the forest below. This poor little one perished trying to get away from a Shadeweaver. There is a tiny doll beside the bones. The anger is welling within my chest.

## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A darkened shadeweaver|darkened shadeweavers]] within [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] around {{waypoint -116, 57, -157}}.
