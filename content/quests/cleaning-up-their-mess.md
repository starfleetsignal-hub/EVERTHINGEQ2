## Steps

1. Kill 10 skeletons in [[Kunzar Jungle]].
   - If you get your updates by killing [[A risen brittlebone|risen brittlebone]] near [[The Crypt Grounds]] {{waypoint 394, -115, 930}}, you will lose -100 faction with Synod Reet per skeleton. The [[A spurbone skeleton|spurbone skeletons]] in [[The City of Mist]] will not induce this faction hit.
1. Return to Taskmaster Scally.

## Rewards

- At least 13g 64s 58c
- +5000 faction with **Dalnir's Wheel Taskmasters**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Exarch's Blessed Sacrament Plate Greaves of Oration]] (Clerics)
  - [[Executioner's Truesteel Legplates of Dominance]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Illuminate's Still Water Leg Wraps of Overbearing]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Magus's Glowing Gossamer Leggings of Blasting]] (All Mages)
  - [[Outrider's Blacksteel Chain Leggings of Stealth]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Stormcaller's Spring Weave Leather Pants of Restoration]] (Channelers, Druids)
  - [[Witchdoctor's Primal Wrath Chausses of Commanding]] (Shamans)
