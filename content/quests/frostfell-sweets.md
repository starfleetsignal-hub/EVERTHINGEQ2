On Live servers the pieces are a random reward from picking up presents named To: You, From Santa Glug dropped by Santa Glug every few hours during Frostfell event.

For Time-Locked servers, there are only three ways to complete this collection as the shinies for it do not spawn in any city or in Frostfell Wonderland because it is a level 90 quest. You can get these shinies on Time-Locked servers by getting them as very rare rewards from [[Frostfell Gifts]] or from [[Overseer Feature]] rewards or as rewards from turning into [[A Burynai Digger]]. They can be bought/sold on the broker but their rarity typically makes this a cost prohibitive method.
