*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Notes

- This is the first in a line of quests that have you kill five Named Monsters that will drop quest starters *(trinkets)*, which will give you a quest to collect them all: [[A Gnollish Trinket]]. Each quest starter can initiate the quest and once started the other quest starters will not initiate the quest.

## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[A rotstuffed scarecrow|rotstuffed scarecrows]]. Up to three are called by [[Animator Z'Apha]] if you are slow killing him; otherwise, they can be found in the [[Scarecrow Fields]] {{waypoint -1961, -20, 637}}
1. Kill [[Animator Z'Apha]] at {{waypoint -2013, -6, 528}}.
1. Return to [[Bulvar Rakgam]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s 67c
- +110 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
