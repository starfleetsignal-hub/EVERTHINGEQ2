## Description

The Kerra Isle outfitter has requested assistance in weaving some supplies of fine cloth for the kerra to sell and trade.

## Steps

1. Purchase the [[Traditional Kerra Cloth Weaving]] recipe scroll (10g 36s 80c) from [[Purrak]], who stands near [[Harriet]]. Requires -10,000 faction with Kerra Isle.
1. Using a loom, make the fine cloth. To complete the quest, you will need:
   - 12 [[Bamboo shoot]]
   - 8 [[Sumac lumber]]
   - 4 [[Ethereal filament]]
1. Return to [[Harriet]] on Kerra Isle.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,000 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +2,500 faction with **Kerra Isle Outfitters**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Kerra Isle Daily Supply Tasks
