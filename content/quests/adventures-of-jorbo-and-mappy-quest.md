## Notes

- *This quest is also part of the [[Book Quests Timeline]].*
- Keep the quest starter book with you as you'll need to read it after killing each group of 10 monsters.  ![Click on thie pile of books](images/Adventures_of_Jorbo_and_Mappy_Quest_-_Pile_of_books.jpg)
- The first six steps of the quest [[Even More Adventures of Jorbo and Mappy (Quest)|Even More Adventures of Jorbo and Mappy]] require you to kill the same mobs, so it is recommended that these two quests be completed simultaneously.

## Steps

*I have found a book by the name of "Adventures of Jorbo and Mappy." Many of the pages are missing, but I think it may be possible to locate them.*

1. Kill any 10 [[Corrupted halflings]]
1. Kill any 10 corrupted halflings
1. Kill any 10 corrupted halflings
1. Kill 10 [[A shadowed halfling|shadowed halflings]]
1. Kill 10 shadowed halflings
1. Kill 10 shadowed halflings
1. Kill any 10 corrupted halflings
1. Kill any 10 corrupted halflings
1. Kill any 10 corrupted halflings
