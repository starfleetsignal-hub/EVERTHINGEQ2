## Steps

1. Travel to the [[Qeynos Province District]] and kill [[Nor`sona A`tyel]] who will spawn near the entrance to [[The Down Below]] in [[The Elddar Grove (POI)|The Elddar Grove]] at {{waypoint 540, -18, -420}}.
1. Return to [[Gol M'Tun]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- AA Experience
  - +3000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
