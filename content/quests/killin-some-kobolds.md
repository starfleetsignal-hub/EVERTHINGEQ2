## Description

"Seren Leafsway is alarmed by the pack of kobolds that have emerged from Steamfont, seeking to make Greater Faydark their territory, and attacking the unwary. She has asked that I face the issue straight on and lower their numbers." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill 6 kobolds in front of [[Steamfont Alley]] {{waypoint -444, 122, 470}}
1. Return to Seren

## Rewards

- At least 27s 29c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

Although you have a choice of dialogue here, it does not change the conversation.

- Seren Leafsway: "I wonder, are you willing to take a more direct strike at the kobold threat, [Player Character]?"
  - Player Character: "I am!"
  - Player Character: "Maybe."
- Seren Leafsway: "Splendid! Then get to it! Go up the path, south of The Combine Sires, into the remains of the encampment there, and slay the ravenous kobolds."
- Player Character: "With pleasure!"

I am to slay the kobolds that have taken up residence south of The Combine Spires, near Steamfont. Cull the kobold pack.

I have culled the kobold pack. I should return to Seren Leafsway, at Spire Shadow Outpost. Speak with Seren Leafsway.

- Seren Leafsway: "It's so good having such a skilled adventurer to help. Thank you!"
- Player Character: "You're welcome."

I have spoken with Seren Leafsway.

Seren Leafsway was pleased by my work. She is confident that the kobolds can now be properly handled, and contained due to my culling of their pack.
