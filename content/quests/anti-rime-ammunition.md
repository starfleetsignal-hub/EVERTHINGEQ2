This is a [[Mikk Eishammer#Related Quests|Forgemasters of Thurgadin Supply Task]]

## Steps

1. Craft 5 lightweight portable cannons
   - The Lightweight Gnomish Cannon recipe can be found within the [[Thurgadin Supply Manifesto]] recipe book, which can be obtained by clicking on the Forgemasters Recipe Notes on the counter in front of [[Mikk Eishammer]]
   - Required materials:
     - [[Forge]]
     - 5 [[Titanium ore]]
     - 5 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
     - 5 [[Amber]]
     - 5 [[Ethereal coal]]
1. Speak to [[Mikk Eishammer]]

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +1,500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
