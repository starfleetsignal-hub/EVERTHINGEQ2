A band of gnomes were relieved to find me within Kelethin. They assume the Subtunarian clockworks and elementals, that had been seeking aid, are from a gnomish civilization - possibly displaced citizens from Ak'Anon! I've agreed to answer their call and see what can be done to assist the threatened people.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Zeen Corblecog (Greater Faydark)|Zeen Corblecog]] {{waypoint 294, 127, 309}} in [[Kelethin]] near [[Gearheart's Forge]].
1. Travel to [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]] by clicking the tinkered portal-gate {{waypoint 299, 128, 311}}.
1. Investigate the nearby city.
   - Speak to [[Gwynnura]] in [[Stoneheart Yards]] {{waypoint 616, 87, 75}}.
1. Help with the harvesting efforts at the [[Ironblood Fungus Farm]].
   - Speak to [[Grygrim the Tender]] {{waypoint 777, 56, -213}} and complete his quests, [[Not So Tender Touch]] or [[Ironblood and Guts]].
1. Return to [[Zeen Corblecog (Thalumbra, the Ever Deep)|Zeen Corblecog]] near the portal {{waypoint 461.87, 95.73, 42.88}} and speak to her.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g 34279 status
