*This quest if only obtainable by players of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Kill blackshields in the Sunken City to prove to Manius that you have what it takes.
   - Kill 2 [[A Blackshield raider|Blackshield raiders]] {{waypoint 64, 1, 5}}
   - Kill 2 [[A Blackshield plunderer|Blackshield plunderers]] {{waypoint 64, 1, 5}}
   - Kill 2 [[A Blackshield pillager|Blackshield pillagers]] {{waypoint 64, 1, 5}}
   - Kill 2 [[A Blackshield ravager|Blackshield ravagers]] {{waypoint 64, 1, 5}}
   - Kill 2 [[A Blackshield pirate (Sunken City)|Blackshield pirates]] {{waypoint 64, 1, 5}}
1. Return to Manius. {{waypoint 89, 1, 57}}

## Rewards

- At least 92c
  - [[Blackshield raider earring]]
