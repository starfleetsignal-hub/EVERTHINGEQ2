## Notes

- In order to be able to speak to [[Ghrix Si'lith]] you need to be wearing the [[Vrassath Guard Suit]], obtained by clicking the chest at {{waypoint 618, 254, 1317}}after completing the quest [[Suit Up]].
- [[Vrassath Guard Suit]] is also needed for the [[The Warriors Call: Volume III]]

==Steps

1. Collect ingredients for Sathuss'Host's meal
   - 8 centipede chunks from [[A frontier centipede|frontier centipedes]] - they're most common in the northeast near [[Slaver's Point]].
   - 8 slug meat from [[A forest slug|forest slugs]] - scattered throughout Warslik's Wood in groups of 3
1. Present them to Sathuss'Host at {{waypoint 594, -141, -98}}
   - *Note: make sure you used [[Vrassath Guard Suit]]*
1. Take them to Chef Xarlyx {{waypoint 546, -169, -185}}

- [[Carved Luclinite Band]]
