## Steps

- Kill 7 [[A turbulent knot|turbulent knots]] in [[Toxxulia Forest]].

found near {{waypoint 936, 0, 3283}}

- At least 46g
- +100 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Seekers of the Dark Truth**
