The Duality believes he has enough information now to open a portal that will lead to the origin location of the mysterious ore... wherever that may be. It sounds as if it may be from Maldura, but there's only one way to find out for sure!

## Steps

1. Use the recipe **Ore Reagent for Portal Spell** to craft the [[Prepared ore reagent]].
   - There is a chemistry table in The Duality's Sanctum at {{waypoint 529.46, 130.09, -49.37}}
   - You will need one [[Metal mechanism]] from the elemental remains on the dock in [[The Village of Shin]] for this recipe.
1. Give the reagent to The Duality and then enter the portal he opens.
   - *NOTE: You need to be on step 6 of [[What Lies Beneath]] in order to see the portal.*

## Needed Materials

- [[Metal mechanism]]
- [[Incandescent material]] (1)
- [[Effulgent candle]] (1)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13200 status
- 11599 status (at tradeskill level 110)
