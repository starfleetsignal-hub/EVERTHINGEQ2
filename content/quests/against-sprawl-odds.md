## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill and gather from random 2 tasks:![harvest dropped by netherbians](images/Harvest_sack.png)
   - Recover 8 items from altarbound sacrificers and altarclaw subjugators in [[Detritus Pantheon]] area {{waypoint 380, 48, 374}}
   - Search for 8 quality tools from netherbian corpses in [[Coral Forest]] {{waypoint -125.23, 0.77, 411.61}}
   - Cull 12 lashers in [[Tentacle Fields]]
   - Pick up 8 harvests dropped by netherbians running through [[Tentacle Fields]] {{waypoint 404.73, 32.13, -129.50}}
1. return to [[Nanru]]

## Rewards

- 4p 4g 15s 72c
