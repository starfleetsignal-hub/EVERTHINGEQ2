## Steps

1. Purchase [[A fancy imported dress]] for 10s 53c from [[A dress merchant]] in a building on the docks in [[The Thundering Steppes]] {{waypoint -460, -18, -430}}
1. Return to [[Licha]].

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Adept's Woven Robe]] (All Mages)
  - [[Footman's Worked Breastplate]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Initiate's Threaded Gi]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Naturalist's Sewn Tunic]] (Druids)
  - [[Plunderer's Small Link Chain Tunic]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Seer's Rustic Hauberk]] (Shamans)
  - [[Theist's Shaped Plate Cuirass]] (Clerics)
