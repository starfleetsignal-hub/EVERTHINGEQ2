## Description

Pallasa Rargon, a member of the Order of Flame, a remnant of the ancient order that once inhabited the temple of Solusek Ro, welcomed my help in their ancient crusade against the invading Void army. She asked that I hunt dark seepage from the waters surrounding the Void anchor in Najena's Cove for the order to study and experiment with.

## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[Dark seepage (LS)|dark seepages]] (grouped mobs of 3) near the rocks and island off the coast of Najena's Cove {{waypoint 64.05, -129.77, 507.05}}.
1. Return to [[Pallasa Rargon]].
