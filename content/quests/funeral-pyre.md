## Notes

- This quest is repeatable., although you will have to zone out, reset, and zone back in to do it more than six times as there are only six sarcophagi to burn in [[The Burial Mound]].

## Steps

1. Enter The Burial Mound, underneath the central pillar, at {{waypoint 10, -1, 2}}.
1. Click any of the sarcophagi at {{waypoint -13, 0, -7}} to ignite its shroud and spawn [[A disturbed shroudsoul|a disturbed shroudsoul]], [[A whispering tormentor|a whispering tormentor]] or [[A Shroudsoul Noble]].
