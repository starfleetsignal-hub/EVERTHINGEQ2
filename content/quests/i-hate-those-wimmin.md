## Steps

1. Visit the [[Seamist Plains Village]] {{waypoint 412, 1, -523}}.
1. Slay the [[A lamia temptress|lamia temptress]] in [[Seamist Plains Village]] {{waypoint 409, 0, -506}}.
1. Return to [[Deputy Stoutgut]].
1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Slay 15 [[A lamia charmer|lamia charmers]] on the beach near [[The Misty Mine]] {{waypoint 332, 0, -1050}}.  Charmers around {{waypoint 253, 26, -596}} also work.
   - Slay 6 [[A lamia temptress|lamia temptresses]] on the beach near [[The Misty Mine]] {{waypoint 332, 0, -1050}}.
1. Return to [[Deputy Stoutgut]].
