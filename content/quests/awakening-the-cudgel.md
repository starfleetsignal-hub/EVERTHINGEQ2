## Steps

1. Before attending any raid listed below DON'T FORGET to return to [[Takk]] in [[The Elddar Grove (POI)|The Elddar Grove]], [[Qeynos Province District]] {{waypoint 702, -17, -336}} to pick up the next portion of the quest (for the mythical version of the weapon), which requires you to retrieve three fetishes to fully unlock the powers of the cudgel. These all drop from raid targets and can be done in any order.
   - The first is the Fetish of Spirituality, which drops from [[Pawbuster]] in The Execution Throne Room.
   - The Second is the Fetish of Preservation, which drops from [[Venril Sathir]] in his lair.
   - The Third is the Fetish of Enlightenment, which drops from [[Hoshkar]] in Veeshan's Peak.
1. After you have obtained all three fetishes, return to Takk to receive your mythical.
