## Prerequisites

- Requires +40,000 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to start

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Bargbae Skullhelm]] wandering the Rime Camp {{waypoint 488, -378, 1213}}
   - Bargbae will not speak to you unless you are wearing your class-specific Order of Rime illusion.  To get it you must complete [[The Order of Rime Timeline]] through [[Infiltration System]].  If you have lost your Rime illusion, you may speak to [[Sliza Xarezia]] in [[Riliss (POI)|Riliss]] ([[Fens of Nathsar]]) to receive a new one.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- +800 faction with **The Order of Rime**
