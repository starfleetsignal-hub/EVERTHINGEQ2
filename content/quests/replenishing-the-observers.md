## Steps

You May Mass Produce These Items

1. Craft the following with the [[Observatory Supply List|recipe]] you are given (You may wish to bring a [[Portable Cooking Set]] and a [[Portable Sewing Table]])
   - 2 [[Snug sleeved blanket|***snug sleeved blankets***]]
     - 6 [[Bamboo shoot|bamboo shoots]]
     - 4 [[Sumac lumber]]
     - 2 [[Ethereal filament]]
   - 4 [[Delectable boiled bread roll|***delectable boiled bread rolls***]] (two per crafting)
     - 4 [[Caynar nut|caynar nuts]]
     - 4 [[Bamboo shoot|bamboo shoots]]
     - 4 [[Kejekan palmfruit|kejekan palmfruits]]
     - 2 [[Ethereal kindling]]
   - 4 [[Extra-strength erudian espresso|***extra-strength erudian espresso***]] (two per crafting)
     - 6 [[Lapsang tea leaf|lapsang tea leaves]]
     - 4 [[Caynar nut|caynar nuts]]
     - 2 [[Ethereal kindling]]
1. Return to Aysha {{waypoint 1820, 6, 4371}}

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- +1,000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +1,500 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**
- +1,000 faction with **Craftkeepers**
