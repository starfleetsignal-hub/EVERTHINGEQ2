## Steps

Zone into [[Castle Highhold: Thresinet's Den (Heroic)]]:

- Crush the crusher of skulls
- Wreck the Shralok brothers
- Defeat the Mucktail spirit leader
- Defeat the queen's guard
- Usurp the necrotic queen

## Rewards

- At least 2p 16g 20,000 status
- 15 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- One of the following:
  - Crate of Armor (Chain, Cloth, Leather or Plate) depending on class
