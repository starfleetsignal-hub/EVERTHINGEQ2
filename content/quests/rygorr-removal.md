## Notes

- This is a repeatable quest.

## Steps

1. kill serveral Ry'Gorr
   - Kill 5 Ry'Gorr Winter Scouts or 6 Ry'Gorr Soldiers
   - Kill 5 Ry'Gorr thaumaturgists or 6 Ry'Gorr grunts
1. Speak with [[Naruk Bloodfur]] at {{waypoint -489, -382, -130}}

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +5,000 faction with **Tizmak Clan**
