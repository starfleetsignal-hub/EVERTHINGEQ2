## Notes

The combines for this timeline are long. Without potions they take 2-3 minutes each. Plan accordingly.
There is a lot of running on the quest. Bring as many evac items as you can manage.
A floaty cloak is also useful to save time getting down from the towers.

## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 50 [[Etherium]]
- 50 [[Plumewit hide]]
- 50 [[Gnarled entwood]]
- 100 [[Celestial Coal]]

## Steps

1. Enter [[Bastion of Thunder: Lightning Strikes (Tradeskill)|Bastion of Thunder: Lightning Strikes [Tradeskill]]] at {{waypoint -95, 3, -164}}.
1. Use the teleporter pad at {{waypoint -212, -19, 44}} to travel to the Tower of the Rainkeeper then speak to [[Karana]] at {{waypoint -29, 994, 191}}.
1. Use the teleporter pad at {{waypoint -38, 995, -234}} to return to the Torden Atrium.
1. Speak to [[Voonark]] at {{waypoint 558, -17, 634}} at the base of the Tower of Thunder in the southwest.
1. Collect the following in any order (look for a bucket before you harvest Pure Water): *Note: Track Harvesting works on the nodes and the bucket used for gathering the water. Look for the bucket at each tower while harvesting the other mats.* ![a bucket](images/A_bucket_visible.jpg)
   - 10 [[Aluminum Orb|Aluminum Orbs]] ![an aluminum orb](images/An_aluminum_orb.jpg)
     - Found around the base of [[The Tower of Thunder]] (SW) {{waypoint 660, 309, 635}}.
   - 10 [[Zinc Orb|Zinc Orbs]] ![a zinc orb](images/A_zinc_orb.jpg)
     - Found around the base of [[Acolyte Tower: Arcanium]] (SE) {{waypoint -660, -15, 634}}.
   - 10 [[Copper Orb|Copper Orbs]] ![a copper orb](images/A_copper_orb.jpg)
     - Found around the base of [[The Glacial Tower]] (NW) {{waypoint 660, 309, -635}}. *Note: The floor is slippery. Jumping repeatedly helps! Walk mode Helps as well.*
   - 10 [[Torden Clay]] ![Torden Clay](images/Torden_Clay_visible.jpg)
     - Found around the base of [[The Sandstorm Floor Tower]] (NE) {{waypoint -660, -15, -635}}.
   - 5 Pure Water from the pool in front of Karana.
     - You need [[A bucket]].
     - The bucket is found around the bottom, or at the top, of one of the towers. It is trackable; however, tracking won't "see" it from the base of the tower if it has spawned at the very top, so you may have to go up a few levels before detecting it.
     - Possible locations of [[A bucket]]:
       - Sand Tower {{waypoint -752, 198, -709}}
       - Lightning Tower {{waypoint 727, 198, 719}}
       - Ice Tower {{waypoint 774, 198, -716}}
       - Wind Tower {{waypoint -631, -16, 552}}
     - Use the teleporter pad at {{waypoint -212, -19, 44}} to go back to the Tower of the Rainkeeper
     - Fish for [[Pure Water (from the Hall of the Rainkeeper)|pure water]] in the pool at {{waypoint -32, 992, 139}}
1. Upon harvesting the last item on the list you receive the recipe [[Untempered Lightning Rods]] which is auto scribed in your recipe book.
1. Return to zone-in and craft a [[Stack of Untempered Lightning Rods]] at the 'Forge of Storms' {{waypoint 22, -2, 5}} next to to the zone-in.
   - Requires:
     - 10 [[Aluminum Orb|Aluminum Orbs]]
     - 10 [[Copper Orb|Copper Orbs]]
     - 10 [[Zinc Orb|Zinc Orbs]]
     - 50 [[Etherium]]
     - 50 [[Celestial Coal]]
     - you get auto scribed recipe for [[Heated Clay Temper (Item)|Heated Clay Temper]] at the end of crafting combine.
1. Craft the [[Heated Clay Temper (Item)|Heated Clay Temper]] using the [[Heated Clay Temper (Recipe Book)|recipe]]
   - Requires:
     - 10 [[Torden Clay]]
     - 5 [[Pure Water]]
     - 50 [[Plumewit hide]]
     - 50 [[Gnarled entwood]]
     - 50 [[Celestial Coal]]
1. Examine the [[Heated Clay Temper (Item)|Heated Clay Temper]] in your inventory and select in a pop up window the option  to apply it to the lightning rods.
   - *Gives: [[A Stack of Clay-Covered Lightning Rods]]*
1. Speak with [[Edlias]] the wyvern at {{waypoint -616, -16, -550}} in the NE tower. ![Edlias](images/Edlias.jpg)
   - This step gives tradeskill exp. {at TS level 115, ~444,983,241; not counting bonuses.} (Maybe be percentage based, at 108 only got 7,779,358 exp not counting bonuses)(8,146,781@102)
1. Pick up a block of ice at the top of the NW Tower {{waypoint 748, 198, -721}}.![Block of Ice](images/Block_of_Ice.jpg)
   - Note: This will start a 5 min timer. If you do die the melting iceblock returns to the top of the tower. When the timer expires a fresh block of ice will appear.
   - It is possible to featherfall all the way back to the hall while holding your icecube, and a lot easier than skidding back on foot. A [[Gnomish Parachute Pack]] alone will not save a character carrying a block of ice.
   - To assist in lessening travel time, making use of speed-boosting items will be helpful! e.g. Totem of the Deadly Sabertooth(gives 75% boost!), Earring of the Solstice(45% boost), etc.
1. Return to Edlias and place the block at {{waypoint -606, -14, -556}}.
   - Known issue: The sparkly locator will not be there if you had to set the block down before reaching Edlias; you may have to try a few placements before it "clicks".
1. Speak to Edlias. Then place the rods on the sand patch in front of him. Note the hint about "standing aside" -- move to his side or stand *behind* him if you don't want to get knocked off the tower and die.
1. When Edlias has finished pick up the rods, then click the ice block to quench them. Once quenched, you can retrieve the now tempered [[Stack of Tempered Lightning Rods|Stack of Lightning Rods]].
1. Place the four tempered lightning rods around the Tower of Thunder (SW), stand back to avoid being knocked back to your death by the strike of lightning and then collect the [[Solidified Lightning]] from each of the four placed lightning rods: ![lightning rod placement](images/Lightning_rod_placement.jpg)
   - {{waypoint 585, -15, 717}}
   - {{waypoint 769, -17, 680}}
   - {{waypoint 765, -17, 590}}
   - {{waypoint 605, -17, 538}}
1. Return to [[Varig Ro]] in the [[Coliseum of Valor]]
   - This step gives tradeskill exp. (8082571 -bonuses@103)
