## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable.
- The rocks have no names above them, show "Interesting rock" when mousing over them.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Butcherblock Mountains]]<gallery>

File: Rock_from_Glubbsunk_Cove.png|rock from Glubbsunk Cove
File: Rock_from_Kragploom_Roost.png|rock from Kragploom Roost
File: Rock_from_Mortartoe_Mines.png|rock from Mortartoe Mines
</gallery>

1. Obtain 3 rock samples from [[Glubbsunk Cove]] - from docks go to {{waypoint 711, 21, 192}} and mine small brown sparkling rocks at water edge.
1. Obtain 3 rock samples from [[Kragploom Roost]] - go to {{waypoint 602, 37, -309}} and mine sparkling gray rocks around camp. Several spawns close to the top of the climbing wall.
   - if you can't fly, you can use path along left side of rock wall that begins at {{waypoint 131, 217, -188}}, then use climbing wall at {{waypoint 495, 326, -129}} to Kragploom Mesa and another climbing wall at {{waypoint 603, 351, -297}} to Roost.
1. Obtain 3 rock samples from the [[Mortartoe Mines]] - go to {{waypoint -595, 153, -208}} enter the tunnel and and mine sparkling black rocks along corridors' walls.
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]

## Rewards:

- Unlock the [[Days of Summer 2025: Week 3]]
