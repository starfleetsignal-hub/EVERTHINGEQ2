1. Harvest materials:
   - Harvest 15 [[Goat Meat]]
   - Harvest 18 [[Apatite Gem|Apatite Gems]]
   - Harvest 21 [[Brittlebush Taproot]]
   - Fuel 15 [[Astral Candle]]
1. Craft 3 [[Improved Desert Curse Protection Potion]] on a [[Chemistry Table]].
1. Speak to [[Bairam]].
