## Steps

1. Kill 12 skeletons in [[Kunzar Jungle]].
   - Killing [[A risen brittlebone|risen brittlebones]] at [[The Crypt Grounds]] {{waypoint 394, -115, 930}}, will cause you to lose 1200 faction with [[Synod Reet (Faction)|Synod Reet]]. The [[A spurbone skeleton|spurbone skeleton]]s in [[The City of Mist]] are heroic, but don't have any faction hits.
1. Return to Fisher Sivv.

## Rewards

- At least 13g 14s 41c
- +2,000 faction with **Kunzar Jungle Villagers**
