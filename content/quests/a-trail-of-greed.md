## Description

During the Age of Turmoil, the Ruins of Guk saw many of Norrath's adventurers delving into its halls, some looking for fortune and glory, other seeking to help protect the living frogloks from the curse. Unfortunately, many of those brave souls were lost to the dangers within the Lower Corridors, and there they remain, forgotten by time itself. I am to collect mementos from the lost adventurers, so that the citizens of Firmroot can honor their bravery properly.
