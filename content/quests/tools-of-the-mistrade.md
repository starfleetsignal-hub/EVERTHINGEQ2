## Steps

1. Slay 8 [[A Deathfist runesmith|Deathfist runesmith]]s in the [[Valley of Sacrifice]]. {{waypoint 230, 0, 90}}
   - They are on a shared spawn with the [[A runesmith escort|runesmith escort]]s. To spawn more Deathfist runesmiths, kill runesmith escorts.
   - The fairies in the area will aggro and kill the orcs, so it is advised to kill the fairies to maximize orc spawns. The fairies are not part of any faction.
1. Return to Quint Cerlius M.E.

## Rewards

- Experience only
