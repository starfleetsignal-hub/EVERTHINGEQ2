## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key by killing 15 [[A hippolisk|hippolisks]] {{waypoint 81, 4, -271}} in [[Sandstone Delta]] at [[The Salt Mire]].
1. I need to find the Mezolydia music box key in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]] from [[A prickly armapine|prickly armapines]], which may be found on the cliffs above [[Nerive Reservoir Camp]] {{waypoint -281, 40, 90}} and on the ground around at [[Najami Oasis]] {{waypoint 57, 12, -58}}![Prickly Armapine](images/Prickly_Armapines.jpg)
1. Examine the clue for [[The Mezolydia Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the Mezolydia Music Box in [[Sandstone Delta]].
