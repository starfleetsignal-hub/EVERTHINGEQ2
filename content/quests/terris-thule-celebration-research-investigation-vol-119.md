## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Terris-Thule has informed me that summoning an Avatar requires the land to be attuned to their element. To achieve this, altars have been set up around the region, but they need to be supplied with power. I have been asked to assist with this by collecting objects infused with the essence of slain foes and offer them to an altar.

1. Collect 3 [[Infused Nightmare Shard|infused nightmare shards]] (random body drop from most mobs in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]])
1. Use the [[Infused Nightmare Shard|infused nightmare shards]] and empower an altar.  Random spawn in Darklight Wood, its yellow sparkles visible far away, possible locations of altar
   - {{waypoint -360, -54, 84}} south of [[T'Vatar Post]]
   - {{waypoint -499, -54, 126}} west of the [[Wellspring of Nightmares]]
   - {{waypoint 364, -30, 27}} near [[Nightshadow Rise]]
   - -51 -30-57 -8.31 near [[The Searing Tendril]] next to the bridge
   - {{waypoint 538.55, -33.00, 197.44}} east of [[The Tower of Bone]]
   - {{waypoint -447, -49, -26}} next to [[Innoruuk's Scar]] bridge
   - {{waypoint 66, -36, -196}} near sullian fairy ring
   - (533, -40, -167)
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Dream Scorcher]] {{waypoint -33, -37, 265}} in [[Darklight Wood]]
