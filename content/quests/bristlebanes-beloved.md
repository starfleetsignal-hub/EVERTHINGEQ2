## Description

Hoppert Barbinsteel feels that both Garby and Tobel have lost sight of what it truly means to be a follower of Bristlebane. He has asked me if I would like to help him remind both of them that having fun is the most important thing to a believer in Fizzlethorpe Bristlebane, not seeking revenge on those who have pranked you. To accomplish this feat, Hoppert has charged me with discovering a creature said to be Bristlebane's most beloved pet, the mysterious bovoch.

## Steps

![Bristlebane's Beloved (gnomentific name: Bovinasauros Rex or B.Rex, common name: Bovoch)](images/Bristlebanes_beloved_creature.jpg)

1. Go to [[Antonica]] and enter [[Bristlebane's Basin]], a special instance of [[Shattered Vale|The Shattered Vale]]. {{waypoint -2335, 8, -69}}
1. Speak with [[Iffatia Honeymeadow]].
1. Kill the 4 [[An exotic game hunter|exotic game hunter]]s.
   - If you killed the exotic game hunters before speaking with Iffatia Honeymeadow, abandon the quest, reacquire it, and re-enter the instance. The mobs will have respawned.
1. Return to Iffatia Honeymeadow and receive [[Bristlebane grain]].
1. Feed the grain to [[Bristlebane's Beloved (NPC)|Bristlebane's Beloved]] and receive a [[Beloved egg]].
1. Speak wtih [[Hoppert Barbinsteel]] in [[Enchanted Lands]] and give him the Beloved egg. You will be transformed into "The Beast of Bristlebane".
1. Run to [[Garby Gingham Gigglegibber]] and [[Tobel Patadash]] and give them a good fright!
1. Return to Hoppert Barbinsteel.
   - To return to your body, open up your [[Knowledge]] (spell book) and use the ability, [[Return to My Body]].
