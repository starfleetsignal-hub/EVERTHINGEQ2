## Steps

1. Confront [[Mesmer Gearloose (Western Wastes)|Mesmer Gearloose]] at {{waypoint 64.37, 7.93, 639.97}} in [[Western Wastes]]
1. Gather and hunt the following
   - Gather 12 dragonsteel slag ![dragonsteel slag](images/Dragonsteel_slag.png)
     - Possible locations include {{waypoint 287.39, 47.93, 403.09}} {{waypoint 274.56, 45.36, 400.50}} {{waypoint 289.12, 25.70, 474.19}} {{waypoint 306.68, 32.34, 464.92}} {{waypoint 304.33, 54.27, 395.72}} {{waypoint 317.46, 50.29, 376.50}} {{waypoint 353.66, 26.62, 482.02}} {{waypoint 330.91, 53.15, 362.89}} {{waypoint 357.24, 44.93, 333.43}}
     - Will not show using Track Harvestable, tends to be located on the ice, against the rocks and down towards or at sealevel.
   - Hunt 8 [[A tidalfrost stalker|tidalfrost stalkers]] or [[A skeletal icebane|skeletal icebanes]] and loot their corpses for [[Draconic Focus Stones]]. {{waypoint 527, 9, 142}}
1. Acquire a [[Shamanic Rune Cage]].
   - speak with [[Prisa]] after you completed the [[Return of the Cobalt Curse]] questline
1. Examine the stack of [[Draconic Focus Stones]] and merge them in the [[Cabalic Rune Cage]].
   - *[[Draconic Focus Stones]] are in your inventory*
   - *Note: If the stones will not give a merge option return to Prisa to get the update.*
1. Return to [[Mesmer Gearloose (Western Wastes)|Mesmer Gearloose]] at {{waypoint 64.37, 7.93, 639.97}}.
1. Acquire an antifreeze compound of some type.
   - speak with [[Gwinres Boulderkind]] after you completed the [[Compound Problems]] and [[Monumental Task]] quests.
   - she will give you [[Freezing Rebuff Buffing Rub]]
1. Return to [[Mesmer Gearloose (Western Wastes)|Mesmer Gearloose]] who gives you a [[Cabalic Energy Feedback Apparatus]]
1. use the [[Cabalic Energy Feedback Apparatus]] to destroy the tinkered barricade generator at {{waypoint -543.61, -3.89, 446.77}}
1. Return to [[Mesmer Gearloose (Western Wastes)|Mesmer Gearloose]]

- At least 4p 36g 48s 99c
