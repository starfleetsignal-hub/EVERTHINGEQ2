## Steps

1. Craft a Pocket-Sized Dimensional Trap
   1. Scribe the [[Schematic: Pocket-Sized Dimensional Trap]] recipe [[Frizella Emberblossom|Frizella]] gave you.
   1. Use a forge to make a [[Pocket-Sized Dimensional Trap]]. (Frizella has provided [[Extra Thick Glass Vial|all]] [[Bottled Lightning|the]] [[Ultra Dense Magnetic Coil|materials]])
1. Talk to [[Brontis]] {{waypoint -120, 0, -25}} in [[The Fortress of Drunder]]. This will complete the quest and offer the next one in the series.

- At least 30g 10s 55c
