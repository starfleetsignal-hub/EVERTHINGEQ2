## Steps

1. You are randomly asked to complete one of these tasks in [[The Sinking Sands]]:
   - Collect 20 **decaying cobra fangs** (look like shinies around the skeletal cobra dens) One location is {{waypoint -362, -81, -417}}
   - Kill 20 [[A Sandscrawler thief|Sandscrawler thieves]]
   - Collect 20 **Anaz'Dal relics**
   - Kill 20 [[A Sul'Dal infiltrator|Sul'Dal infiltrators]]
1. Return to [[Outrider Bernd]] in [[The Elddar Grove]].

## Rewards

- 8106 status @ L60
- +500 faction with **The Tunarian Alliance**
