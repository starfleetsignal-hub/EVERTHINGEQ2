- *This quest is repeatable up to 5 times.*
- *This quest is also part of the [[Antonica Timeline]].*
- *This quest is marked 'Solo' but it might require you to kill heroics.*

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Blackburrow]] and complete one of the following tasks: *(chosen randomly)*
   - Gather 5 ore samples from the [[A Sabertooth miner|Sabertooth miners]] or [[A Sabertooth excavator|Sabertooth excavators]]. {{waypoint 32, 2, -28}} *(common update)*
   - Recover plans that the [[A Sabertooth tactician|Sabertooth tacticians]] are going to use. {{waypoint -22, 16, 85}} *(uncommon update)*
   - Gather 3 rune stones from the [[A Sabertooth diviner (Blackburrow)|Sabertooth diviners]]. {{waypoint 6, -12, 2}} *(common update)*
1. Return to Sergeant Tillin.

## Rewards

- 1st completion: At least 2s
- 2nd completion: At least 4s
- 3rd completion: At least 8s 82c
- 4th completion: At least 13s 64c
- 5th completion: At least 19s 87c
