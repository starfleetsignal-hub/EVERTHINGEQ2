This is the second quest in the series to change your [[Citizenship#Kelethin to Qeynos|citizenship from Kelethin to Qeynos]].

## Steps

1. Visit the [[Southern Qeynos Tower]] and the [[Northern Qeynos Tower]] in [[Antonica]].
   1. At the southern tower find [[Jenkins]] at {{waypoint -389, 4, 268}}
   1. At the northern tower you want to see [[Rathford]] at {{waypoint -392, 20, -289}}
1. Return to Duryo Valstath.

## Rewards

- This quest only gives AA experience, not regular adventuring experience.
