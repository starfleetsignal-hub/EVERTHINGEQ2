## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A swarm shadow|swarm shadows]].
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 swarm shadows.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 swarm shadows.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 shadowed creatures.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 shadowed creatures.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 shadowed creatures.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 soulless zombies. [[A soulless assistant|Soulless assistants]] or [[A soulless resurrector|resurrectors]] will work.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 soulless zombies.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 soulless zombies.
1. Read the book.
