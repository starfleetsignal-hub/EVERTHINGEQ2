## Steps

Zone into [[Ossuary: Sanguine Fountains (Heroic)]]

1. Clear the first two parts of the zone to spawn the statue.  After killing the statue the other names will spawn.
1. Kill [[High Inquisitor V'Kaudin]]
1. Destroy [[The Embodiment of Gore]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 16g 20,000 status
- 15 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- One of the following:
  - Crate of Armor (Chain, Cloth, Leather or Plate)
