## Notes

*A new tradeskill mission is available every 18 hours.*

## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 15 [[Celestial Coal]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest the following within [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]]:
   - 25 [[Silvered fleck]] from [[Flecked mass]]
   - 10 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Coalesced reef]]
   - 10 [[Dewpalm lumber]] from [[Fallen dewpalm branch]]
1. Craft an [[Ornate Bookmark]] at a [[Work Bench]]
   - There is a [[Work Bench]] in the [[Smiths' Gallery]] of the Library at {{waypoint 675.71, 411.68, 31.05}}.
1. Speak with [[Ramyn Matrick]] at {{waypoint 660.51, 411.33, 17.14}} in the [[Smiths' Gallery]] in the Great Library.

## Rewards

- At least 48000 status
- [[Elemental Mission Reward Crate]]
