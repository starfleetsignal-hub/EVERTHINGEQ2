## Description

"I have volunteered to help Mentha Haora in her attempts to cleanse the undead and collect some data for her superiors. I need to take the Scroll of Arcanic Disruption and use it on the scorched skeletons near the Combine Spires. In doing this, the data that Mentha's superiors want will be stored in the scroll, which I can return to her." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Cleanse 8 [[Scorched skeletal apprentice|scorched skeleton apprentices]] or [[Scorched skeletal wizard|scorched skeleton wizards]] at [[The Combine Spires]] by using the [[Scroll of Arcanic Disruption]] on them.
1. Return to [[Mentha Haora]]

## Rewards

- At least 18s 7c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Mentha Haora: "The scroll has been completed."
- Player Character: "What scroll?"
- Mentha Haora: "The scroll of Arcanic Disruption. More tests from my superiors. They want to see if their spell can truly "cleanse" the skeletons. Based on what it did to these bone fragments, however, I don't think there will be much left once the skeleton has been cleansed."
  - Player Character: "We won't know until we try."
  - Player Character: "And here's where I come in."
- Mentha Haora: "Exactly! We still have to test it on the actual skeletons. Use the spell imbued within this scroll on the skeletons. The scroll itself will keep track of whatever data my superiors wish to collect."
- Player Character: "I'll do it."

Use the scroll upon the scorched skeletons.

I have cleansed the skeletons. Return to Mentha Haora.

- Mentha Haora: "What did you find, [Player Character]? Were the skeletons cleansed?"
- Player Character: "I am not sure. They began to glow and then they crumbled."
- Mentha Haora: "Hmm. If they were cleansed they would return to the soil. Perhaps that is what happened. The Scroll of Arcanic Disruption will certainly hold the answer. Thank you for the help!"
  - Player Character: "You're welcome."
  - Player Character: "It was nothing."

I have spoken with Mentha. I have cleansed the skeletons.

Mentha Haora, a member of Kelethin's Order of the Arcane, was hopeful that the scroll had successfully cleansed the undead I used it on, but even if I did not permanently resolve the issue the information stored within the scroll will help with her investigation.
