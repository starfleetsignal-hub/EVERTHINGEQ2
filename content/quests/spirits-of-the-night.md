*This quest is part of the [[Freeport Outlying Areas Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Slay four undead found in the zone. Any of the following will update the quest:
   - [[A cursed Darkfathom]]
   - [[A decaying Darkfathom]]
   - [[A drowned squatter]]
   - [[A forgotten resident (Sunken City)|a forgotten resident]]
   - [[A perished citizen]]
   - [[A restless resident]]
1. Return to [[Inquisitor Thorson]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 5c
- +150 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
