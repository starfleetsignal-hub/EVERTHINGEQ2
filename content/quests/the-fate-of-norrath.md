## Prerequisites

- You must be adventure level 80 to begin this quest.

## Notes

- This quest has you obtain the items needed to progress through more difficult raids. Originally you needed to complete these raids to gain access to [[Veeshan's Peak]], but that is no longer a requirement.
  - [[Mistmyrian Soulcube]] was used against [[Venril Sathir]].
  - [[Isydryl's Wand of Intervention]] was used against [[Selrach Di'Zok]].
  - [[The Greenmist Orb]] was used against [[The Leviathan]].
- The unique instance for this quest, [[The Moppet Shoppe]], can only be accessed if on certain quest-steps.

## Steps

1. To begin, examine "the remains of a battle" at the loc shown. Using a first-person view helps the adventurer find a clickable location in each case.
1. Examine a green shiny from the ground around the battle site. If you can't see it, use first-person at loc {{waypoint -209, 149, -1108}}
1. Examine the bodies. You will see white or green sparkles on clickable corpses. If you can't see them at a distance, use first-person.
1. Examine the area around the bodies.
1. Gather the [[Lucan Moppet]] (tiny doll with a red glow) at loc {{waypoint -232, 149, -1122}}
1. Enter [[The Moppet Shoppe]] under the bridge in [[The City of Mist]] {{waypoint -231, 125, -1008}} and speak with [[Fiddy Bobick]] once inside.
   1. If there are multiple options for The Moppet Shoppe, its was the second one for me and has [[Fiddy Bobick]] in it
1. Recover the three ancient relics by defeating the following raid bosses:
   - [[Mistmyrian Soulcube]] from [[Tairiza the Widow Mistress]] in [[The Tomb of Thuuga]].
   - [[Isydryl's Wand of Intervention]] from [[Pawbuster]] in [[The Execution Throne Room]].
   - [[The Greenmist Orb]] from [[Imzok's Revenge]] in [[The Protector's Realm]].
1. Return to Fiddy Bobick in the Moppet Shoppe.
