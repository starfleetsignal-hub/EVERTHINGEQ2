### Steps

Craft 12 items from the random list below:

- [[Adamantine Rapier (Crafted)|Adamantine Rapier]]
- [[Adamantine Battle Hammer (Crafted)|Adamantine Battle Hammer]]
- [[Adamantine Crescent Axe (Crafted)|Adamantine Crescent Axe]]
- [[Adamantine Javelin (Crafted)|Adamantine Javelin]]
- [[Adamantine Morning Star (Crafted)|Adamantine Morning Star]]
- [[Adamantine Hand Axe (Crafted)|Adamantine Hand Axe]]
- [[Adamantine Parrying Blade (Crafted)|Adamantine Parrying Blade]]
- [[Adamantine Flail (Crafted)|Adamantine Flail]]
- [[Adamantine Shamshir]]
- [[Adamantine Tribal Spear (Crafted)|Adamantine Tribal Spear]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

### Rewards

- At Least 2g 46s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
