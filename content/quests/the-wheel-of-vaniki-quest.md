## Steps

1. Speak to [[Surveyor Menak]] on a mountain ledge at {{waypoint 42, -24, -884}}.
1. Obtain [[The Wheel of Vaniki (Quest Item)|The Wheel of Vaniki]] from a Commonlands rock that is right next to the dock at the [[Ratongan Village]] at {{waypoint -233, -48, 14}}.
1. Return to [[Surveyor Menak]].
1. Speak to [[Engineer Klaaska]], just east of Surveyor Menak at {{waypoint -19, -40, -889}}.
1. Find some items to restore the wheel:
   1. *Sacred Bottle of Contentment* at {{waypoint -288, -49, 136}}
   1. Examine the [[The Wheel of Vaniki (Quest Item)|wheel]].
   1. *Vial of Interaction* at {{waypoint -242, -48, -6}}
   1. Examine the [[The Wheel of Vaniki (Quest Item)|wheel]].
   1. *Ancient Alkaline Barrel* at {{waypoint -152.87, -47.76, -831.10}}
   1. Examine the [[The Wheel of Vaniki (Quest Item)|wheel]].
1. Return to [[Engineer Klaaska]] and get your reward.
