## Description

"Charles Arker is here from Freeport to make a name for himself before his marriage. It seems part of his name-making included falling in love with one of the Tunarian spies -- a most unsuitable and distasteful situation. Charles cannot bring himself to kill the Tunarian and asked me to find her and take care of things for him. Her name is Laena; she could be anywhere on the island so I will need to hunt carefully." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Enter the Wilderwood {{waypoint 181, 5, 109}} to search it by going up the stairs near Charles and into the trees a short way.
1. Approach the Rocky Dig {{waypoint 114, 1, 104}} south-east of where you entered the woods.
1. Enter Cliffdiver Canyon {{waypoint 217, -3, 141}} to search it.
1. Continue through Cliffdiver Canyon to Wilderbear Vale where the ghost of [[Laena]] {{waypoint 267, 1, -52}} will spawn and attack you.
1. Kill Laena to receive a Parchment Scrap
1. Return to [[Charles Arker]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 3s 95c
- [[Laena's ring]]
- [[Parchment Scrap (The Tunarian Plot)]], examine to start the quest [[The Tunarian Plot]]
