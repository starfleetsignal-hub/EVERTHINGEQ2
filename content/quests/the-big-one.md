## Steps

1. Retrieve Dirzdia's lure from a shark named [[Smolderfin]] in [[Smoldering Lake]].
   1. Fish some [[Smoldering trout (Quest Item)|smoldering trout]] from [[Smoldering Lake]] {{waypoint -43.25, -61.88, -93.55}}.
   1. Use the [[Smoldering trout (Quest Item)|smoldering trout]] (2 to 4 of them) to chum the waters, spawning [[Smolderfin]].
   1. Kill [[Smolderfin]].
      - **Important Note**: Make sure to loot the ***corpse*** of Smolderfin for [[Rich Flesh]], the quest starter for the follow-up quest!
1. Return to [[Dirzdia T'Zauvirr]].

## Rewards

- At least 3s 62c
- One of the following:
  - [[Fighter's Earring]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Earring]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Earring]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Earring]] (All Mages)
