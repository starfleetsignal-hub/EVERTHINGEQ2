## Steps

**Note:** Mass Production will not update your quest.

1. Scribe the [[Observatory Supply Manifest, page 3|recipe]] in your inventory and craft the following items:
   - 4 [[Lens polishing cloth|lens polishing cloths]] (sewing table)
   - 4 [[Log book|log books]] (engraved table)
   - 2 [[Magnifying lens|magnifying lenses]] (work bench)
   - *components Needed: 6 [[Amber]], 4 [[Quicksilver cluster]], 20 [[Bamboo shoot]], 16 [[Sumac lumber]], 4 [[Mottled pelt]], 2 [[Ethereal coal]], 4 [[Ethereal filament]], 4 [[Ethereal incense]]*
   - Crafting stations are in the [[Paineel Commons]] at {{waypoint 1800, -299, 3493}} on both sides
1. Return to [[Adeela]] {{waypoint 1727, -282, 3471}}

## Rewards

- At least 20g 5s 35c
- +1,000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +2,500 faction with **Craftkeepers**
