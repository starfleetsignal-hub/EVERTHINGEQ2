## Steps

As you're going through the quest dialogue with Maareona, you'll see three Kerras - [[Ajarn]], [[Laharn Rahai]], and [[Amazu Kharliko]] - run past and into Starcest Commune.  Players who've done the Kerran racial quests will recognize them and know what they're up to, but Maareona doesn't.

1. Enter [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum]] and investigate the kerra's presence (auto-update once you zone in)
1. Investigate a meeting between Kerra and Erudites taking place at The Perfunctory Philosopher - it's the building at the west edge of town.  Read (or scroll through) some dialogue between Amazu Kharliko, [[Bahati Linnxar]], [[Philosopher Orrinalanya]], and [[Scholar Obidudyn]]
1. Enter the waterway south of the Qeynos gate {{waypoint 703, -24, 328}}
1. Slay all the undead within the waterway
1. Destroy the last portal - just left-click it
1. Return to Maareona Ludimintium
