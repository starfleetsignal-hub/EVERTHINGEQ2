## Steps

1. Talk to [[Huntmaster Dipmeylap]] {{waypoint 595, 151, -736}}.
1. Return to [[Bimse]].
1. Kill 3 [[An invading glade strider]]. (they spawn as you approach Bimse)
1. Talk to [[Bimse]] again.
1. Investigate [[Deepwood Wilds]] {{waypoint -444, 26, 123}}.
1. Slay 6 Dedraka pygmies.
1. Return to [[Bimse]].

## Rewards

- 2p 52g 59s 84c
