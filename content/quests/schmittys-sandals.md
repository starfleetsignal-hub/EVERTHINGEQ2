## Steps

Help Schmitty gather the materials he needs to make a new pair of sandals:

1. Collect 4 sea slug antennas by killing [[A sea slug|sea slugs]] on the bottom of [[Butcherblock Bay]].
   - The sea slugs can be found just off the docks, right between the 2 arms of the dock or near the right arm {{waypoint 620, -12, 518}}
   - sea cucumber will not work (possibly changed, worked on 2/1/11)
1. Collect 3 superb pieces of terratrodder hide by killing [[A terratrodder|terratrodders]] {{waypoint 354, 29, 11.32}} on the beach of the bay.
1. Collect  4 anemone needles by killing [[An anemone|anemones]] on the bottom of the bay.
   - They can be found at the bottom of the bay between goblin islands near {{waypoint 578, 0, -39}}
1. Return to [[Schmitty McEricson]] to give him the components for sandals. He'll tell you he found his lost ones and will make you a pair of your own with the parts you found.
1. Wait 5 minutes before returning to him for your reward.
