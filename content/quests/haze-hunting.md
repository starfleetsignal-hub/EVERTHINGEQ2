## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[Haze stalker|haze stalkers]]. These are found all throughout the southcentral part of the zone.
   - Level 19-21 stalkers can be found on both sides of the river near [[The Commonlands]] gate.
   - Level 21-23 stalkers can be found on the island around [[Behemoth Pond]].
   - Level 23-25 stalkers can be found in the forest near the [[Hidden Beach Path]]. {{waypoint 483, 7, -623}}
1. Return to Lieutenant Kaneth.
