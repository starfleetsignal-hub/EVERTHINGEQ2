To get this quest you must also have completed [[Echoes in the Woods]]

## Steps

1. Enter the instance [[Takish Badlands: Kigathor's Glade (Solo)|Takish Badlands: Kigathor's Glade [Solo]]], just outside the [[Blinded Eye Camp]] at {{waypoint -394.33, 245.01, 213.48}}
   1. Go to the [[Petrous Woods]] {{waypoint -551, 75, -298}} within [[Takish Badlands: Kigathor's Glade (Solo)|Takish Badlands: Kigathor's Glade [Solo]]]
   1. Defeat 16 [[A shadowed recollection|shadowed recollections]]
   1. Defeat [[Kigathor Stonebark]] {{waypoint -700, 151, -555}} to return him to the circle of life. (You have to kll the other named in the zone to spawn him).
1. Return to [[Zaari]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- One of the following:
  - [[Resilient Dendromail Greaves]]
  - [[Resilient Viridsilk Trousers]]
  - [[Resilient Florahide Greaves]]
  - [[Resilient Chloroplate Greaves]]
