## Notes

- [[Xebrith Cognizance: Madness]] and [[Xebrith Cognizance: Consequence]] can be completed around the same area surrounding [[Rot Reek Bog]] {{waypoint -294, 20, -322}}
- bog gnashers can drop [[Withered Fragment of Yawning Abyss (Item)|Withered Fragment of Yawning Abyss]] quest starter.

## Steps

1. Kill 15x [[A bog gnasher]] at [[Rot Reek Bog]] {{waypoint -294, 20, -322}} or at [[Stonemarrow Mountains]] {{waypoint -151, 67, -170}}
   - *As with many 'Seek & Destroy' missions in Rage of Cthurath expansion, this step only counts the number of 'encounters' defeated, not individual numbers of mobs themselves.*

## Rewards

- x100 [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
- [[Status Coin (75,000)]]
