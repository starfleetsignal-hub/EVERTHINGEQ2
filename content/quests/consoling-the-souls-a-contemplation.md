- requires at least 39,000 [[Pride Pakiat (Faction)]]

## Steps

1. Travel to {{waypoint -321, 8, 408}} and fill the vial.
1. Find 5 entrapped spirits, talk to them, and release them. ![an entrapped spirit](images/An_entrapped_spirit.jpg)![an entrapped spirit](images/Other_entrapped_spirit.jpg)
   - {{waypoint -39, 13, 1013}} *Edge of [[Zivier's Reach]] overlooking the [[Amphitheater of Song]]*
   - {{waypoint 791, 63, 977}} *[[Mage's Pebble]]*
   - {{waypoint 1521, 128, -8}} *Furthest rim of [[Plane of Magic]] at the west cardinal point*
   - {{waypoint 756, 18, -772}} *Archipelago north of [[Aetherscar]] with the [[Auroric slug|Auroric slugs]]*
     - Warning: if you start from the last one first, this one will not appear till the rest are done.
   - {{waypoint 45, 35, -1375}} *Furthest rim of [[Plane of Magic]] at the north cardinal point*
