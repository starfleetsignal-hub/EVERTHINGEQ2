This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Gather more materials for Saedie
   - Collect 10 void essences, a common drop from void creatures in [[Najena's Cove]].
   - Collect 2 void lattices, an uncommon drop from the same void creatures.
1. Return to [[Saedie Kalterra]]
1. Kill a corrupted [[Elemental Lord]]. In any of the three caves at {{waypoint 48.96, -104.91, 244.12}}, right click and turn three of the orbs to the same attunement (the alcoves will all match), then activate orbs by clicking orb to the left of each cave door.

## Notes

- The repeatable quest [[Dispatching the Destroyers]] can easily be completed at the same time as Cleansing the Elements Again.
- The quest [[Elemental Mystery]] requires an Elemental Lord of Posion that can only be spawned on the appropriate step of one of the three daily Cleaning the Elements quests. If you are attempting to complete this timeline in short order, do not summon the Elemental Lord a 2nd time, until you are also on the quest Elemental Mastery. Doing otherwise, will incur a 24hr lockout before completing that part of the timeline. Likewise, do the next Cleansing, after Elemental Mastery, for the 2nd needed Elemental Lord

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- +500 faction with **Order of Flame**
- [[Unbound Elemental Essence]]
