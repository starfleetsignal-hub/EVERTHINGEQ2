## Steps

1. Find [[Raffik]] at [[Teren's Grasp]] in [[Kylong Plains]]  at {{waypoint 2117, 520, -935}}
   1. Note for lower level adventurers: Take Wizard Spires or Druid Ring to Kylong Plains, which takes you directly to  Teren's Grasp without having to complete the Sokokar flight questlline.
1. Speak to [[Xhary]] standing near Raffik at {{waypoint 2122.48, 520.00, -936.16}}
1. Craft [[Charm of Expertise (Quest Item)|Charm of Expertise]] from the [[Charm of Expertise (Quest Recipe)|Charm of Expertise]] recipe given.
   - 1 [[Raw cranberry]]
   - 1 [[Cabilis cocoa bean]]
   - 1 [[Lichenclover root]]
   - 1 [[Smoldering Candle]]
     - Note: [[Chemistry Table]] at {{waypoint 1732, 487, -1031}}
1. Return to [[Raffik]]

## Rewards

- At least 10p 14g
- [[Charm of Expertise (QuestReward)|Charm of Expertise]] recipe book
