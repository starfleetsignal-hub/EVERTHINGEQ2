This quest is part 3 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Barbarian Series.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Issik Dethyr]], near the [[The Sprawl]] gate {{waypoint 87, -4, 12}}.
1. Return to Malachi.
1. Smash the furniture in Issik's quarters to teach him a lesson.  His quarters are up the stairs, east of the dock {{waypoint -20, 0, 50}}.
   - Smash the desk.
   - Smash the chair.
   - Smash the weapon rack.
1. Return to Malachi.
