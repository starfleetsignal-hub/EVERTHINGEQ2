## Steps

![a snowy cliff beak](images/A_snowy_cliff_beak.jpg)

1. Kill 8 [[A snowy cliff beak|a snowy cliff beaks]]. These are birds found flying in air above the Goahmari Village and the hills surrounding it.
1. Click on the egg across from Norbu at {{waypoint 585, -37, 379}}. It can then be moved as if it is a house item. place it right outside the fire around  {{waypoint 576, -37, 361}}.
1. The egg will disappear after warming and the quest will auto-complete.

You're well on your way to having your first flying mount! Now it's time to train it. Use the [[Stormfeather's Pride Griffawn Feather]] that you received for completing this quest and hail your baby griffawn to begin its training.

## Notes

You can only complete one training session every 18 hours. After completing the 5th, you will obtain your mount where ever you are at.

If you have started [[A Frozen Egg]] from the DoV Collectors Edition /claim item, you can now obtain retrieve your egg from [[Kezu]].
