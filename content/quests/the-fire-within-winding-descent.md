## Starting the Quest

Read [[The Fire Within (Book)|The Fire Within]] book in any zone.

## Steps

1. I need to find all nine pieces of the experimentation formulas within [[Solusek's Eye: The Calling (Heroic)]]:
   - Pages located at:
     - {{waypoint 49, -32, -133}}
     - {{waypoint -15, -28, -184}}
     - {{waypoint -108, -33, -205}}
     - {{waypoint -66, -33, -85}}
     - {{waypoint -53, -23, -154}}
     - {{waypoint -205, -27, -165}}
     - {{waypoint -256, -31, -189}}
     - {{waypoint -319, -40, -169}}
     - {{waypoint -301, -40, -150}}
1. I should put all the scraps back together into a single document
   1. Right click and Use [[A stack of vellum scraps]] to assemble the scraps in your inventory
   1. Right click and Read [[Reassembled Experimentation Formulas]]
1. The experimentation formulas in the Redwinger's book are encoded. I must find his code book in order to decipher them. Defeat Onakoome (Heroic)  at {{waypoint -175, -369, -297}} to get the book.
1. Gather the key in the room of [[Galadoon]] at {{waypoint -289.69, -369.62, -93.44}}.
1. Right click and Examine [[Redwinger's Code Book]], unlock it with the key.  Examine it again and read it.
1. Right click and Examine [[Encoded Experimentation Formulas]] and decipher the formula using the code book.

## Rewards

- [[Decoded Experimentation Formulas]]
- At least 47p 71g 30s
