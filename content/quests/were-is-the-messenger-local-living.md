The Far Seas Supply Division has contacted me requesting aid in exploring marketing and trade opportunities in the lands of Vetrovia.

## Starting the Quest

Read the letter in your mail, **"Short-Term Job Offer"** from Captain Ethan Darani:

*What an exciting time this is, with the discovery of a lost land practically in our own back yard!<br><br>We are pleased that our sponsorship of the Swimming Oak has borne such fruit.<br><br>Now that we are creating a foothold in that lush land, it is time to look at marketing and trade opportunities, and we feel that someone with your creative talents would be well-suited to the task.<br><br>Would you consider accepting a short-term commission with the Far Seas Supply Division to explore the possibilities of this new land? If you agree, please speak with [[Representative Mairen]] at our Svarni Gateway camp in Vetrovia.<br><br>Sincerely,<br>Captain Ethan Darani<br>Far Seas Supply Division*

This grants the quest.

***Note: If mail does not arrive, or you delete mail or the quest, you can pick up the quest from a [[Jerol]] in [[The Village of Shin]] at {{waypoint -89, -2, 80}}***

## Materials Required

- Note: The quest requires harvesting of the below materials:
  - 20 [[Amsonia Roots]]
  - 20 [[Forlorn Ore]]
  - 10 [[Greytusk Hide]]
  - 20 [[Mahngavi Mango]]
  - 15 [[Narra Wood]]
  - 15 [[Star Diopside]]
- Fuel ([[Vikuski]], a fuel merchant, can be found at {{waypoint 101, 13, 496}}:
  - 10 [[Astral Filament]]
  - 5 [[Astral Candle]]
  - 5 [[Astral Coal]]

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Representative Mairen]] at {{waypoint 639, 7, 530}} in [[Svarni Expanse]].
   - Take the raft on the dock at {{waypoint 571, 2, 490}} to [[Kamapor]].
1. Speak to [[Alghoza]] in [[Kamapor]] {{waypoint 111, 5, 387}}
1. Speak to [[Arpita Goyal]] {{waypoint 114, 5, 388}} to receive [[Recipe Scroll: Batik Weaving]].
1. Harvest the following:
   - 15 [[Narra Wood]]
   - 20 [[Amsonia Roots]]
   - 20 [[Mahngavi Mango]]
   - 20 [[Forlorn Ore]]
   - 15 [[Star Diopside]]
   - 10 [[Greytusk Hide]]
1. Craft the items needed to create my batik (Note: There is a fuel merchant and [[Far Seas Crafting Station]] (universal crafting table) at {{waypoint 99.37, 12.79, 493.2}}):
   - [[Tightly Woven Fabric]] *(or Sewing Table)*
   - [[Bright Fabric Dye]] *(or Chemistry Table)*
   - [[Batik Canting]] *(or Workbench)*
1. I must combine the items that I made to create a batik weaving:
   - [[Batik Weaving]] *(or Sewing Table)*
1. Return to [[Representative Mairen]] {{waypoint 639, 7, 530}} in [[Svarni Expanse]]
   - [[Representative Mairen]] is gone. Speak to [[Rithsar]] {{waypoint 641, 7, 530}} to update.

## Rewards

- +5,000 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Teal Batik Tapestry]]
- At least 5p 21g 72s 4c 55382 status
