- All collection item, except the incomplete manuscript, are harvestable lore pages (see talk for more locations).
- Start this collection by examining a chest in [[Stormhammer Keep]] in [[Kaladim]] at {{waypoint -101, 46, -81}}. This will then spawn the harvestable incomplete manuscript book in [[The Hidden Nest]] area beneath the waterfall, at {{waypoint -216, 53, -213}}. You will need to to collect the book and add it to your collection before you are able to see the pages. If the book isn't there even though you have inspected the chest, kill some of the book minions and it should spawn somewhere in the room. It may also be partially clipped by a wall.
- This quest is also the starter quest for the [[The Stormhammer Timeline|Stormhammer Timeline]].
