## Prerequisites

You need to have +10k faction with Guardians of Underfoot

## Steps

1. collect 6 construct hearts (body loot from any mobs in Dartain's Fortress, e.g. [[A vigilant watcher|vigilant watchers]])
1. return to [[Slav]]

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]]
- At least 55g 70s 61c
- +2500 faction with **Guardians of the Underfoot**
