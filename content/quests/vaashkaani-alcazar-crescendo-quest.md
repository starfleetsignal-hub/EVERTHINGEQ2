Oonnora, a Felfeather mage from Splendor Sky Aerie, has cast a spell enabling me to step back into my memory of facing Zakir-Sar-Ussur and freeing the virtuosa, Lyrissa Nostrolo, in "Vasshkaani: Nostrolo's Encore"! The fateful events of that day will reverberate through the planes for ages!

## Steps

I must help free Lyrissa Nostrolo, the virtuosa being forced to create an army of maedjinn for the Djinn Sovereign, from within his royal alcazar, Vaashkaani.

The Djinn Sovereign must be stopped.

1. In [[Zimara Breadth]] on [[Auric Palisades]] enter the [[Vaashkaani: Alcazar Crescendo|Vaashkaani: Alcazar Crescendo (Solo)]] portal at {{waypoint -488.06, 162.15, -604.66}}
   - You receive the [[Glorious Maestra's Cithara]] when you enter the dungeon.
1. Kill [[Khazinehdar Zuhraasa]] at {{waypoint 621, 71, -1}}
   - From time to time, a Djinn Reflection will try to break out of the mirror. Clicking it during the process will push it back into the mirror.
   - Any Djinn Reflection that manages to escape the mirror, will reduce damage done to Khazinehdar by 25% if the reflection isn't charmed, mesmerized, feared, stunned or dead.
   - If 5 Djinn Reflection manage to break through, you will die.
1. Use the [[Sovereign's Hold]] portal at {{waypoint 622, 70, -0}}
1. Kill the trash.
1. Take the magic carpet at {{waypoint 888, 70, -0}} to the centre platform
1. Step forward to spawn [[Zakir-Sar-Ussur]] the Djinn Sovereign
   - A tactic for low DPS characters. Once [[Zakir-Sar-Ussur]] starts to spawn use the magic carpet to return to the outer ring then retreat back to the corridor so that you will not be dropped to your death.
1. Kill [[Zakir-Sar-Ussur]] the Djinn Sovereign
1. Wait until he says "It will magnify Nostrolo's song! A legion of maedjinn will soon spill forth from their wroughtlands, and all the planes will have you to thank!" then use the [[Glorious Maestra's Cithara]] to make him vulnerable.
   - He will aided by [[A fateful left]] and [[A fateful right]] hand spawns. If a Left with a Right Hand are active he can be invulnerable.
   - Use the cithara on the fateful hands to take away >80% of their HP.
   - If booted off the central platform and you do not land on a safe location then you can fall below the platform and die. You can fight in the corridor leading into the room but some spawns may initially be out of sight (in walls).
1. Use the portal on the central platform to enter [[Vaashkaani Bastille]]
1. Speak to [[Lyrissa Nostrolo]] {{waypoint 375, 23, -356}}.
1. On the platform behind [[Lyrissa Nostrolo]] set down the Arcana Crystal {{waypoint 383, 23, -355}}.
1. Speak to [[Lyrissa Nostrolo]] again.
1. This will trigger a ring event. Defend her from attacking Djinn.
1. After defeating the attacking Djinn then [[Kapuji-bashi Haakhaz]] will spawn, defeat him as well
1. Speak to [[Lyrissa Nostrolo]] again.
1. [[Helalli]], [[Chandrima]] and [[Vahravi]] will spawn. Speak to [[Vahravi]].
1. After a discussion the Allyrians and [[Lyrissa Nostrolo]] leave.
1. [[General Ra'Zaal]] arrives.
   - Ensure a Stifle or Stun rune is in the Waist (I experienced both though Stifle more prevalent).
1. Kill [[General Ra'Zaal]]
   - Casts AOE Stun and Stifle (I experienced both though Stifle more prevalent).
   - Summons adds that provide stacks. If 10 ghuls are present, you instantely die.
   - Completing a HO gives you an effect "Outmaneuver" which makes you immun to stun and allows you kill the ghuls.
   - Quest completes on defeat of [[General Ra'Zaal]]
1. Take the portal at {{waypoint 367, 20, -335}} to leave the zone
