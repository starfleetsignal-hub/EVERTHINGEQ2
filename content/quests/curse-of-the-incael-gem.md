## Steps

1. Find a way to defeat [[Incael]] to save [[Misha]]
   1. Speak with [[Incael]] at {{waypoint -241, -268, 2944}}
   1. Kill [[A lingering priest]] till one of them respawns as ghost npc, speak with the ghost and receive [[Blessed Icon of The Prime Healer]]
1. Use the [[Blessed Icon of The Prime Healer]] on 8 a lingering soul
   - A lingering soul spawns for a few seconds after you killed [[A lingering rogue]],[[A lingering mage]],[[A lingering priest]] or [[A lingering knight]]
   - *Note: group update when you use the [[Blessed Icon of The Prime Healer]]*
1. Return to [[Misha]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
