## Steps

1. Acquire 8 ***Coldwind Four-Leaf Clover*** {{waypoint -840.21, -15.00, 788.67}} on the southern part of the [[Coldwind Coastline]] in [[Antonica]].![Coldwind Four-Leaf Clover](images/Coldwind_Four-Leaf_Clover.jpg)
  - Note: **NOT** Group-wide updates, each will have to click their own.
1. Return to [[Zruk]] {{waypoint 56.97, -1.49, 0.21}} in [[Enchanted Lands]].
1. Acquire 8 ***wishbonez*** from [[Ryjesium vulrich|Ryjesium vulrichs]] that can be found between [[Dreg's Landing]] and the [[Lakeshore Ruins]] in [[Kylong Plains]].
1. Return to [[Zruk]] {{waypoint 56.97, -1.49, 0.21}} in [[Enchanted Lands]].
1. Acquire 6 ***white heather*** that can be found in [[Felderham's Folly]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]].
1. Return for a final time to [[Zruk]] {{waypoint 56.97, -1.49, 0.21}} in [[Enchanted Lands]].

## Rewards

One of the Following:

- [[Unlucky Vulrich]]
- 8 [[Fool's Gold]]
