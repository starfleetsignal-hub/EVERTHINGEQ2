*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Find the dock on [[Qeynos Shepherd's Isle]] to the south. {{waypoint -49, -37, 1056}}
1. Kill [[A dankfur dock warden]] (or just right click on him if he is sleeping!)
1. Collect the shipping manifest.
1. Return to [[Tyllia Navar]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s 67c
- +85 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
