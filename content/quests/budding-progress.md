## Starting the Quest

- Starts in your own house where you placed [[An Obulus Frontier Seedling]]

## Steps

![Pure Water in Gardens of Nye'Caelona](images/Screenshot_1.png) ![Soil in Gardens of Nye'Caelona](images/Screenshot_3.png)
The seed has developed into a bud and the Gardening Goblin has returned to check on it.

1. Converse with the Gardening Goblin about how to make the plant grow larger.
1. Collect [[Nye'Caelona water|water sample]] and a [[Nye'Caelona soil|soil sample]] from the Nye'Caelona garden (trackable)
   1. Water is from the pool in the back of Nye'Caelona garden. {{waypoint -620, -88, -348}} in Obulus Frontier (Kunark Ascending)
   1. Soil is near the roots of the trees in Nye'Caelona garden. It appears on Track Harvestables.
      - *example locations {{waypoint -622.91, -71.79, -502.00}}, {{waypoint -448, -100, -434}}*
1. Bring the soil and water back to the Gardening Goblin in your house.
1. Finish conversation with goblin.
1. Goblin asks you to check back in a day or so.

## Rewards

- [[Gardener's Soil Samples]]
- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +1,500 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
