Quill drops from the following mobs in a "small chest":

- Researchers or students (can be grey) in [[Sebilis]] Laboratory and Study (also dropped from a Sathirian gravelord 2010-02-07)
- [[Hierophant Prime Kulgarn]] in [[Sebilis]]
- [[A Gorowyn hierophant]] in the Chardok Library
- Sebilis Patron near the Bank in [[Sebilis]] (these will drop as a grey mob 2010-7-28)
- Dropped from Sathirian Archon (small chest drop 2012-01-07)

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Ashe Boderra]] {{waypoint 29, 1, 29}} on the docks in [[Enchanted Lands]], to receive a book titled [[The Tragic Tale of Slezaf]].
1. Speak to [[Edmund Slezaf]] {{waypoint -477, -20, -381}} on the docks in [[The Thundering Steppes]] and give him 5 Platinum in exchange for [[Slezaf's Broken Lute]].
1. Examine the Lute.
1. Speak to [[Freesia Ticklewing]] {{waypoint 161, 108, 171}} in [[Kelethin]], to receive [[The Flute of a Thousand Colors (Item)|The Flute of a Thousand Colors]].
1. Equip the flute and use it from your charm slot to complete the quest and receive the [[First Magic Lyric]], Then inspect the Lyric to receive next step.
