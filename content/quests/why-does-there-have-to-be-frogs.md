## Description

Artifact collectors have been looking for noteworthy pieces dating back to pre-Shattering Norrath for study. The discovery of Guk has generated a great deal of excitement within the archaeological community, and they are asking for objects from within the Halls of the Fallen. This is likely also an opportunity to study more of the effects of the curse on some of the stronger creatures found within the Halls, so I might want to look for personal items or objects within their proximity.

## Steps

1. Search for evidence of the practices of a spiritual leader from [[The Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen]]
   - Pick up the idol at {{waypoint 171, -63, -254}} (group update)
1. Return to [[Ragheejb Yasir]]

- At least 2p 50g
