## Notes

This quest takes place in [[Dragon Necropolis: Tombs of Enlightenment (Solo)|Dragon Necropolis: Tombs of Enlightenment [Solo]]]. You should do this quest at the same time you are doing the second half of [[Scars of Destruction: Treasures of the Dragon Necropolis]]

## Steps

1. Enter [[Dragon Necropolis: Tombs of Enlightenment (Solo)|Dragon Necropolis: Tombs of Enlightenment [Solo]]] at {{waypoint -750, 67, 280}} in [[Western Wastes]]
   - Gather 8 Vrakorr Sigils around the outside edge of the hall around {{waypoint -497, 132, -465}}![Vrakorr Sigil](images/Vrakorr_Sigil.png) **Before you go through the tunnel**
   - After you go through the tunnel at {{waypoint -386, 121, -333}} ...
   - Locate and click on 8 Vrakorr Lode Staves on all the ramps leading up to [[Rakirith the Reanimated]] starting around {{waypoint -685, 143, 221}}
   - Hunt 8 Guardians, Patrollers, and Attendants as you make your way through the zone
1. Return to [[Dipper]] at {{waypoint 73, 9, 600}} in the [[Western Wastes]]

## Rewards

- [[Vrakorr Lode Stave]] house item
- 50 x [[Scarred Dinar]]
- 5 x [[Velium Drachma]]
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
