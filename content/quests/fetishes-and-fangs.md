## Steps

1. Perform the following:  *Note: Kill ALL shissar mobs as they seem to share the same spawn points.*
   - Defeat 10 [[Shissar stalkers|skeletal shissar]] for cursed fangs
   - Defeat 8 [[Shissar revenants]] for ancient shissar fetishes. *Note: Updates are not guaranteed.*
1. Return to [[Rhoquan Zexus]] at {{waypoint 545.48, 34.54, 131.83}}.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- [[Disciple of Rhag Fetish]]
