## How to start quest

- **This is the adventure way of starting prelude event.** Right at the first conversation you have to choose if you want to go the crafting way, or the adventure way.
- Speak with [[Salira Freecreek]] in The Commonlands at Crossroads {{waypoint -526, -44, -314}}
- when given choice of answers choose the first option: "I'll take up the investigation to the southeast."

## Notes

If it makes it easier for you to kill even con mobs this quest can be done while mentoring someone, or using chronomages to lower your level.

## Steps

1. Find where the troll could have come from - go to the very southeast of [[The Commonlands]] where the manhole cover is {{waypoint -1277, -47, 629}} to get first update. (Note: this manhole cover is usually a markable object, but you can't mark it while this quest is active)
1. Click the manhole cover to enter Derelict Drain.
1. Search for Elddar artifacts
   - Hunt for 5 ancient Elddar artifacts - kill 4 x [[A sewer wretch]] plus [[Kulgg]] the Sewer Brute, a boss who spawns in the last room, **loot bodies for updates**.
   - Search the sewer for 12 more artifacts - click sparkly bags, barrels and crates.
1. Return to Salira in Crossroads.

- [[Sewage Slop]] house pet
