## Notes

- May be repeated hourly.

## Steps

1. Catalogue a number of inhabitants of Eryslai:
   - Catalogue [[A greater servitor of air]] all over [[The Windglades]] and the [[Upper Glade]]
   - Catalogue [[A lesser servitor of air]] all over [[The Windglades]] and the [[Upper Glade]]
   - Catalogue [[A niborien skyhunter]] all over [[The Windglades]] and the [[Upper Glade]]
   - Catalogue [[A greater air torrent]] at the [[Torrential Pond]] around 159.02 59.97 -1238.43 287.76
   - Catalogue [[A lesser air torrent]] at the [[Misty Falls]] around {{waypoint 146.17, -226.64, -1163.86}}
   - Catalogue [[A topiary mistform]] in the treetops in the [[Upper Glade]] around {{waypoint 337.78, 155.21, -1126.34}}
   - Catalogue [[A bixel hive lookout]] around the inside edges of [[The Bixel Hive]]
   - Catalogue [[A bixel hivemage]] in the [[The Bixel Hive]] around the entrance and throughout the hive
   - Catalogue [[A hive queensguard]] in the top [[The Bixel Hive]] at [[The Queen's Cell]] around {{waypoint -520.05, 641.74, -177.15}}
   - Catalogue [[An efreeti windlord]] at the [[Precipice of Wind]] around {{waypoint -314.66, -490.71, 777.99}}
   - Catalogue [[A crackling stormrider]] at the [[Precipice of Wind]] around the tops of the arches in the center
   - Catalogue [[A phoenix stratocruiser]] at the [[Precipice of Wind]] around the edges of the island
   - Catalogue [[A prismatic recluse]] in the [[The Aerie]] in the shrubbery all over the island
   - Catalogue [[A zephyren aeromancer]] in the [[The Aerie]] all over the island
1. Defeat a [[Wilder of Air]] (You do NOT have to catalogue it) - Possible locations:
   - {{waypoint -725.00, 513.05, -266.89}} at the edge of [[The Bixel Hive]]
   - {{waypoint -324.41, -325.06, 1015.45}} up the trunk of a huge tree at the [[Precipice of Wind]]
   - {{waypoint -94.32, -112.77, -1203.50}} or {{waypoint 258, 72, -1444}} in [[The Windglades]]
