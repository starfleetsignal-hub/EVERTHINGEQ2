This quest is repeatable.

## Description

The preparations that Marathan and I have made over the last few days have created a trap of sorts for these beings of another realm! The temple should draw them in and contain them! I need to travel into the Temple of Solusek Ro and use the crystal Marathan prepared for me! I must enter the second sight and defeat the enemies within the temple! I shall become the reaper and destroy them!

## Steps

1. Defeat enemies trapped within the [[Temple of Solusek Ro]] (use the teleport at the top of the stairs if you can)
   - Kill 12 [[A void-touched infiltrator|void-touched infiltrators]] that wander the halls of the Temple. Look up, they tend to float near the ceiling.
     - Use the [[Purplish Crystal]] to become a [[Void Stalker]] in order to see and kill the infiltrators. Effect lasts for 25 minutes. Canceling the effect (or dying) will return you to your body.
     - You are given the following damage spell abilities - Void Plasma, Blaze of Will, Solusek's Flame to blast the infiltrators with and a heal spell for yourself - Draw Plasma. You are also given a weapon's damage spell - Marathan's Angst.
     - Don't rush, I completed it with 11 minutes left and that is with waiting for power and hp to recharge, which they do rather fast.
1. Return to [[Marathan Allim]]

## Note

If you are in a group, only 1 member has to transform and kill the mobs. All the group members will get up dates as long as they stay with your body.--76.180.22.138 16:55, January 6, 2013 (UTC)
