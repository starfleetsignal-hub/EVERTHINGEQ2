## Steps

1. Examine the [[Geonid Focus Crystal]], Gritstone gave you, to speak with the Phenocryst (you may need to zoom out to 3rd person to see the dialog clickies)
1. Wander throught the Caverns of Living Stone and the Wakening Lands to find Phenocryst
   1. Phenocryst's Mindstone is found at {{waypoint 750, -456, 3746}}
   1. Speak with the mind stone to gather it
1. Return to [[Gritstone]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- [[Geopet Plushie]]
