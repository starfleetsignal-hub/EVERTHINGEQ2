## Notes

- You must have +20,000 faction with one of the [[The Courts of Maj'Dul|Courts of Maj'Dul]] to be able to enter their headquarters.
- This quest rewards the ability to teleport back to [[Maj'Dul]], [[Call of Ro]].
- This quest is also part of the [[Citizenship Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Dharr]] at his [[Dharr's Residence|home]] in southern [[Maj'Dul]]. {{waypoint -115.21, 137.94, 117.87}}.
1. Kill [[An exiled Sha'ir]] in [[The Sinking Sands]]. {{waypoint -291, -103, -238}}
1. Return to Dharr's Residence to find that he has [[Dharr's Corpse|been murdered]]. Kill the [[A Sha'ir investigator|Sha'ir investigator]] who spawns shortly after entering.
1. Speak to the [[A Sha'ir Messenger|Sha'ir Messenger]] who wanders about Maj'Dul. Look for her book icon on the map.
   - See the Talk page for theories as to how the messenger spawns and paths.
1. Attune the orb to four key places. These places are different for each character, chosen from a pool of locations listed below:
   - [[Maj'Dul]]
     - Immerse the Orb in the Fountain in [[The Golden Scepter]] courtyard {{waypoint 93, 149, -144}}
     - Bring the orb into a tunnel {{waypoint -135, 140, 80}}
   - [[The Sinking Sands]]
     - Immerse the orb in the spring water of [[The Oasis of Marr]] {{waypoint -1106, -204, 55}}
     - Place the orb in a campfire beneath the Maj'Dul Plateau {{waypoint -140, -109, -41}}
     - Go to the top of a rock spire off the coast {{waypoint -1328, -177, -1140}}
     - Cavern beneath [[The Eye of Anuk]] {{waypoint -657, -222, -1199}}
     - Must place the orb in a campfire near the Oasis of Marr: In the [[Croc Hunter Camp]] near the Croc Caves {{waypoint -1242, -214, -296}}
   - [[The Pillars of Flame]]
     - Climb a tall spire off the coast at the Djinn island {{waypoint 1574, -98, -64}}
     - Harpy's Nest up a climbable wall {{waypoint 706, -96, -1229}}.
     - Touch the orb to the earth at the foot of [[The Great Bonsai]] {{waypoint 152, -190, -1670}}
     - Immerse the orb in a small spring near the Sunstriders {{waypoint 249, -108, -676}}
     - Place the orb in a Blacksmith's Forge at the Swiftrider camp {{waypoint 63, -86, -742}}
       - Dismount and actually climb onto Mender Grunthik's Forge from the front for the update. You may need to move around a bit.
1. After visiting all four locations, you receive your reward.

## Rewards

- The ability, [[Call of Ro]]
