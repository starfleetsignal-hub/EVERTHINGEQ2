## Notes

- This is the first [[Aerakyn Mentors in Norrath|Aerakyn mentor]] quest for [[Qeynos]].

## Steps

1. Speak to Lonce, who accuses you of kidnapping his sister, [[Sloish Jellysmots]] {{waypoint 339, -22, -180}}.
1. Go to [[Irontoes East]], and speak to [[Volryyk]] in a side room {{waypoint 339, -20, 151}}.
1. Go to T[[The Sovereign House of Qeynos|he Sovereign House of Qeynos]], and speak to [[Sessren]] upstairs {{waypoint 570, -8, 193}}.
1. Go to [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn]] in [[Qeynos Province District]], and speak to [[Averniss]] {{waypoint 810, -23, 53}}.
1. Speak to [[Kyth]] (right next to Averniss).![The Door for the House of Endings](images/The_House_of_Endings_Door.png)
1. Return to the Capitol District, and enter the door marked, "[[The House of Endings]]" {{waypoint 645, -18, 205}}.
1. Speak to Sloish upstairs {{waypoint 5, 5, 5}}.
1. Return to Lonce {{waypoint 339, -22, -180}}.

## Rewards

At least 16g 56s 21c at level 60.
