## Steps

1. Zone into [[Big Bend: Sanctum of Spirits]] at {{waypoint 60, -12, 271}}.
1. Kill 6 [[A possessing spirit]] - they have feathers on their heads.
1. Use [[Dismal Censer]] to free 6 [[Of a possessed ogre]] or [[A possessed troll]]. You have to be very close to them to use the Censer and you can only use the censer when the possessed ogres and trolls are below 30% health.  You may need to kill the Possessing Spirit running the troll or ogre first.

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
