## Steps

1. Gather 12 **Stolen Magic Tablets** found in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus: The Sultan's Dagger (Signature)]] from inside the tents.  Note, tablets do not show up until the first named [[Bashida the Blade]] is killed.
   - *See the Talk Page for locations.*![Stolen Ancient Tablets](images/Stolen_Ancient_Tablets.png)
1. Return to [[Abitha Dora]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- 2 x [[Ancient Sulite Tablet]] - House item
- One of the following:
  - [[Resilient Dendromail Shoes]]
  - [[Resilient Viridsilk Boots]]
  - [[Resilient Florahide Shoes]]
  - [[Resilient Chloroplate Boots]]
