## Starting the Quest

[[Brehita the Weaponbreaker]] may be found in either the [[Coliseum of Valor]] at {{waypoint 103, 0, 30}} or in the [[Services Gallery]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]]

NOTE: You must be level 110

## Steps

1. Use the Flame symbol next to [[Brehita the Weaponbreaker]] and zone into the [[Soulbound Chamber]].
1. Speak to Brehita inside to receive a [[Soulbound Slicer]].
1. Click on the coal bin, then click on the [[Soulbound Slicer]] in your inventory to receive a [[Planar Weapon Essence]].
1. Speak to Brehita again.
1. Click on brazier in the center of the chamber.
   1. Imbue a single essence.
   1. Select the weapon (Must be a weapon from the [[Plane of Magic]], [[Chaos Descending]], or [[Blood of Luclin]] expansions, or your Epic 2.0!)
1. Speak to Brehita again.

## Rewards

![](images/Extract_Planar_Essence_%28Spell_Scroll%29.jpg)

- At least 1p 68g 39s 90c
- "Access" to the [[Soulbound Chamber]] to break down more items/weapons to imbue/level up your weapons
- [[Extract Planar Essence (Spell Scroll)|Extract Planar Essence]] *(A permanent ability to extract Planar Essences from any weapon that has energies that are "Planar".)*

## Notes on Using the Extract Planar Essence Ability

- For the purposes of this article, 'weapon' refers to equippable items that slot in Primary, Secondary, and Ranged.  This includes shields, symbols, wands, etc.
- Planar weapons that can be leveled are destructible, Legendary or better, and level 100-120. It also must not have the "NO-VALUE" tag. The weapon does not have to be repaired. Weapons from GU 115: Reignite the Flames or later do not have planar weapon essence to extract nor can be imbued with any essence.
- The only way to obtain a planar weapon essence is by extracting it from a planar weapon.
  - [[Planes of Prophecy]] weapons give a [[Planar Weapon Essence]] when converted.
  - [[Chaos Descending]] weapons give a [[Greater Planar Weapon Essence]] when converted.
  - [[Blood of Luclin]] weapons give an [[Empyral Weapon Essence]] when converted.
- Breaking down a leveled planar weapon returns only one [[Planar Weapon Essence]] (or [[Greater Planar Weapon Essence]], or [[Empyral Weapon Essence]]).  You won't get extra, even if the weapon was leveled.
- Each time the planar weapon levels up, it receives a 5% buff to base Ability Modifier, Potency, and Primary attributes (this is in the description in the weapon's Examine window).
- Weapon Planar Level essence requirements (varies by item resolve):
  - Planar Level 1 requires 10 [[Planar Weapon Essence|Planar Weapon Essences]]
  - Planar Level 2 requires 10 [[Planar Weapon Essence|Planar Weapon Essences]]
  - Planar Level 3 requires 25 [[Planar Weapon Essence|Planar Weapon Essences]]
  - Planar Level 4 requires 35 [[Planar Weapon Essence|Planar Weapon Essences]]
  - Planar Level 5 requires 45 [[Planar Weapon Essence|Planar Weapon Essences]]
  - Planar Level 6 requires 55 [[Planar Weapon Essence|Planar Weapon Essences]]
  - Planar Level 7 requires 65 [[Planar Weapon Essence|Planar Weapon Essences]]
  - Planar Level 8 requires 75 [[Planar Weapon Essence|Planar Weapon Essences]]
  - Planar Level 9 requires 85 [[Planar Weapon Essence|Planar Weapon Essences]]
  - Planar Level 10 requires 95 [[Planar Weapon Essence|Planar Weapon Essences]]
  - Total = 500 [[Planar Weapon Essence|Planar Weapon Essences]].
- You can't see the weapon's Planar Level until it is level 1 or higher, at which point the Examine window will display "Planar Level 1".  This means the base Planar Level of a qualifying weapon is level 0.
- There is currently no way to see how much "experience" your Planar Weapon has except immediately after adding a [[Planar Weapon Essence]] essence, when the amount of experience currently and needed for the next level is displayed in your chat window.
- You always have to revisit the [[Soulbound Chamber]] in order to add essences to your weapon.
