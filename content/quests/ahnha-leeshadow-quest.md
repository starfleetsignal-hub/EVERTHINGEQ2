## Steps

1. Fly to [[The Isle of Aversion]] and summon [[Ahnha Leeshadow]] {{waypoint -294, 244, -564}} with your pipe reed. Speak to her to receive a report.
1. Return to Harun on [[The Whisperwind Isle (POI)|The Whisperwind Isle]]. Summon him with your pipe reed if necessary. Speak to him to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 9g 95s 82c
- +75 faction with **The Windgazer**
