## Steps

1. Draw some sap from the largest tree in Everfrost, the [[World Tree Root]]. {{waypoint -631, 0, -301}}
1. Collect 2 vials of blood from [[A glacial construct|glacial constructs]]. {{waypoint -405, -6, -33}} (random, uncommon update)
1. Gather 10 pipe-like pieces from [[Category:Icegill Goblins (Group)|Icegills goblins]]. {{waypoint 54, -35, -1427}} (random, uncommon update)

- One of the following, based on Class:

  - [[Mentor's Dark Silk Hand Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Primalist's Ghostly Handguards]] (Shamans)
  - [[Scholar's Spellwoven Gloves]] (All Mages)
  - [[Thief's Blood-Dyed Chain Gloves]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Vicar's Sanctified Plate Gloves]] (Clerics)
  - [[Wanderer's Rare Hide Leather Gloves]] (Druids)
