## Steps

![An example of one of the crystals](images/Crystal_Clear_%28Zek%29.jpg)

1. Gather six types of crystals. (Red, Orange, Blue, Black, White, Green)
   - They are found on the ground in the [[Defiled Forest]], between {{waypoint -440, -29, -70}} and {{waypoint -510, -29, -230}}.
   - Each section of this valley spawns a different type of crystal and they all look the same unless highlighted. To avoid confusion, just pick them all.
1. Return to Birchbark Oakleaf

## Rewards

At least 1g 59s 57c
