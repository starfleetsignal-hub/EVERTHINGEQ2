## Steps

1. Find the hiding Thexians along the crumbling wall and guard towers within [[The Commonlands]], near Turmoil Cemetary at {{waypoint -1308.5, -69.3, 341.5}}
1. Slay the Thexian escapees (10)
1. Gather Thexian Priest Robe from body of a Thexian Priest {{waypoint -1260.09, -61.71, 227.74}}  and 5 Thexian tomes of arcanum (in and outside the towers)
1. Return to [[Dyymona K'Vexx]]
1. Deliver missive to L'Rak Ta'Wound at the Nerian Embassy in Freeport.{{waypoint -120.44, -37.8, 30.3}}
1. Return to [[Dyymona K'Vexx]]
