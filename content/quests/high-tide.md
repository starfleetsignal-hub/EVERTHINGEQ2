## Steps

1. Speak to [[Merrik]] at [[Yeppa's Last Stand]] {{waypoint 4265, -1096, 2059}}
1. Return to [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)|Osh]] and ask him to open the portal to [[Fina's Retreat]] in [[Great Divide]], then take it
1. Speak to Shaman [[Diu]] At [[Fina's Retreat]], out on the ice {{waypoint -1490, -541, -2426}}
1. Collect 5 [[An offering from the Shrine of Prexus|offerings from the Shrine of Prexus]] {{waypoint -1629, -533, -2362}}
1. Head to [[Eastern Wastes]] using the portal at Fina's Retreat {{waypoint -1471, -514, -2253}} and speak to [[High Shaman Lien]] {{waypoint -1138, -531, 3304}}. Once you have done that, [[High Shaman Lien|Lien]] will offer to send you directly back to Cobalt Scar, **but before you take it** if you are on [[A Solemn Request]] quest then speak to [[Odder]] at {{waypoint -1235, -540, 3439}}.
1. Speak to [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)|Osh]] at [[Yeppa's Last Stand]] {{waypoint 4259, -1094, 2049}}
1. Receive a blessing from [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)|Osh]] (speak to him and click through the dialogue)
1. Destroy the first bone barrier - kill [[A risen shaman apprentice]] at {{waypoint 4065, -1099, 2173}} to dispel the barrier and then hit the bones with any attack to break them
1. Destroy the second bone barrier - kill two [[A risen shaman acolyte|risen shaman acolyte]]s at {{waypoint 4100, -1120, 2280}} and {{waypoint 4063, -1120, 2295}} then destroy the barrier at {{waypoint 4035, -1120, 2372}} with one attack
1. Destroy the third bone barrier - kill the [[A risen shaman|risen shaman]]s at {{waypoint 3835, -1127, 2422}} then smash the barrier at {{waypoint 3906, -1141, 2354}}
1. Destroy [[Salton|Salton, Corrupted Shaman of the High Tide]]. Consult the strategy for killing him before engaging!
   - **If you die, be sure to speak to [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)|Osh]] again to receive another blessing!**
1. Return to [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)|Osh]] and follow the conversation
