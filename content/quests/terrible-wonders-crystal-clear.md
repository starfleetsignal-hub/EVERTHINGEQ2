## Steps

1. Check on [[Quibble]] {{waypoint -629.25, -17.68, -252.90}}
1. Investigate the red dust {{waypoint -413.42, -15.98, -246.66}}
1. Speak with [[Frau Choraline Lent]]{{waypoint -1128.28, -10.85, -464.36}}.
1. Speak to [[Brun]] {{waypoint -1134.01, -11.70, -463.46}}
   - Harvest 20 Glowstone Crystal
   - Harvest 20 Cavernhide Leather
   - Harvest 20 Deepvein Ore
1. Return to Brun.
   - Receive Recipe Scroll: Brun's Test. Create the potion. Stove and Keg -use campfire. Requires 5 Formless Kindling.
1. Speak to Brun. Speak to Brun again.
