## Steps

1. Lumzig wants to you craft [[A Massive Heatsink|a massive heatsink]], from the [[Schematic: Massive Heatsink]], and [[Slender Slab of Metal|the]] [[Heat Spreader Fins|provided]] [[Thick Thermal Adhesive|materials]], using a [[Forge]].
   - *Note: Use the Forge in zone located in Gnomeland Security at {{waypoint -608, 175, 1000}}, or use a Portable Forge.*
1. Return to [[Lumzig Schepwicket]] {{waypoint -957, 156, 732}}. Speaking with him will complete the quest and offer the next one in the series.
