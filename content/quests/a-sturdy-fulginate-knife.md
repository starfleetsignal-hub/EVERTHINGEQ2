## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Fulginate Knife for my apprentice.
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Fulginate Knife]]
   1. Craft [[A Sturdy Fulginate Knife]] on a [[Forge]] using the following materials and fuel:
      - 2 [[Fulginate cluster]]
      - 4 [[Luminous coal]]
1. I need to take this knife back to my apprentice.
