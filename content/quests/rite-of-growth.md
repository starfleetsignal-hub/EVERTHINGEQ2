Dalidra, a member of the ancient race of glaufaye, resides in the canopy above Corpse Cauldron, a lake in Luminous Peaks of Thalumbra. She explained to me that tradition dictates I can be granted an audience with Tylwyth, the highest glaufaye in all creation, by passing a rite of life.<br>

This quest becomes available when you are on the [[Underdepths Saga: Luminous Quest]], as soon as you have spoken to Lamari and Atalya.

## Steps

1. Encourage growth in the [[Luminous Peaks]] by slaying invasive species.
   - Kill 12 [[An ooyog|ooyogs]] {{waypoint -523, 172, 220}}.
   - Kill 10 [[A fetid poxfiend|fetid poxfiends]] {{waypoint -501, 240, -20}}.
1. Return to [[Dalidra]].
