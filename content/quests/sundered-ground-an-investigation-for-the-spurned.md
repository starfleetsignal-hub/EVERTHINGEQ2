*The good version of this quest is [[Sundered Ground, an Investigation for the Order of Arcane]]*

## Steps

1. Kill colored [[An underground golem|underground golems]] until [[An undertok]] spawns (see talk page for spawn locations)
1. Kill the undertok and click the body to retrieve *a clockwork mechanism*
1. Return to Lanxena in [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
1. Speak to [[Viti Bipklang]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]] at {{waypoint -182, -3, 258}}
1. Retrieve 8 clockwork mechanisms from undertoks; spawn them by killing undergolems
1. Return to [[Viti Bipklang]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]] at {{waypoint -182, -3, 258}}
1. Speak to [[Queen Cristanos]] in [[Darklight Palace]] {{waypoint -578, 33, -193}}

## Rewards

- [[Building on Sundered Ground]] - recipe book
- 33440 status @ lvl 100
- Suffix title, [[The Undertok Dismantler]]
