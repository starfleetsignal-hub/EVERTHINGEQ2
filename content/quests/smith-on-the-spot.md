## Steps

1. Craft 10 [[Irontoe Battleaxe|Irontoe Battleaxes]]
   - Scribe [[Irontoe Favorite Battleaxe Recipe]]
   - You will need:
     - 10 [[Boiled leather pelt (Version 10)|boiled leather pelt]]
     - 10 [[Carbonite cluster]]
     - 10 [[Severed ash]]
     - 30 [[Sparkling Coal]] (Level 20 - 29)<br>Hint: can be bought in [[The Highlands]] from [[Trader Whitlock]] {{waypoint 332, 189, 492}}
     - A forge, can be found next to Bartleby {{waypoint -216, 211, -141}} or at the docks {{waypoint 667, 24, 601}}
1. Return to [[Bartleby Blatherback|Bartleby]]. {{waypoint -223, 210, -139}}

## Rewards

- At least 1g 5s
- +4000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Dandelion fiber]]
  - [[Malleable loam]]
  - [[Cuirboilli leather pelt]]
  - [[Palladium cluster]]
  - [[Rough jasper]]
  - [[Severed fir]]
  - [[Steel cluster]]
