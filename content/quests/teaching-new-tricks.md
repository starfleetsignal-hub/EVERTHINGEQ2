## Notes

You can only complete one training session every 18 hours and must complete all five to finish [[A Frozen Egg]]

## Steps

1. Teach the griffawn to stay
   - Use /stay multiple times until you get 4 updates
     - *Note:This and the other 3 commands can be found on the social window too*
1. Teach the griffawn to play dead
   - Use /cutthroat multiple times until you get 4 updates
1. Teach the griffawn to taunt
   - Use /taunt multiple times until you get 4 updates
1. Teach the griffawn to dance
   - Use /dance multiple times until you get 4 updates
     - need to dismount to use /dance command.

## Rewards

- At least 70g

One of the following if this is your last training day after you hail your griffawn for the last time and tell it you like it, too:

- [[Snow Gryphon]]
- [[Tundra Gryphon]]
- [[Highland Gryphon]]
