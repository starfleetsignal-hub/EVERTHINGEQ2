This quest is part 15 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Slay the following creatures to prove yourself:
   - 2 [[Sullian fairy|Sullian fairies]] around [[Bone Lake]] {{waypoint -732, 141, -2087}}
   - 3 [[A Dragoon sentry|dragoon sentries]] found at [[The Funeral Pyre]] and north of [[N'Mar's Ascent]].
   - 1 [[Deathbloom treant]] in the [[Valley of the Dead]] which is north of N'Mar's Ascent {{waypoint -319, 90, -2000}} or at {{waypoint -428, 90, -2031}}.
     - The blue hunting area the quest helper puts on your map is completely off. Do not follow it.
1. Return to Szin.

## Rewards

- At least 36s 13c
