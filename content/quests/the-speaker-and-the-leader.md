## Description

*One calls itself the speaker, the other the leader. Both are a threat to the citizens of Maldura, and so both must be brought down.*

## Steps

- Destroy enemy targets within Kralet Penumbra and Maldura.
1. Defeat the speaker
   - [[Xacx-Kahda]] in [[Kralet Penumbra: Rise to Power (Heroic)]]
1. Kill the leader
   - [[Black Bolt (Heroic)|Black Bolt]] in [[Maldura: Algorithm For Destruction (Heroic)]]
