## Steps

1. Given the strange events reported by the scouts, I need to investigate the Tourbillion more directly.
   - Investigate the Tourbillion {{waypoint 51, 0.7, 701}}
1. **NOTE: Descending into the depths of the Tourbillion will kill you!**
1. The Harrowing Horde surrounding the Tourbillion appear to be draining its energy! I need to stop them before it is completely destroyed.
   - Defeat Harrowing Horde that are draining the Tourbillion
     - At this point, you will need to kill a few of the [[A Skirth siphoner|Skirth siphoners]] around the area of the Tourbillion.  After killing between 3 and 6 mobs, you will receive a red text message that you have angered the Horde.
1. A mini ring event then follows, where 3 waves of Harrowing Horde mobs spawn, followed by a [[Tirun Portal Master]].  Kill it to get your update.
1. I should report my findings back to Veris.
   - Speak to [[Veris Windcall]] {{waypoint 17, -5, 500}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
