## Steps

[[Researcher Tahar]] in what remains of [[Quel'ule]] has asked me to assist him by catching one of the creatures in the area of [[Quel'ule]] to learn more about the effects of the magic fields on them.  This time he needs an adult creature.

1. Scribe the [[Blueprint for the Construction of a Superlative Entrapment Device]] you were just given. Use the Work Bench.
1. Craft "a Superlative Entrapment Device" (requires 1 [[Titanium ore]], 2 [[Quicksilver cluster]], 2 [[Sumac lumber]], 1 [[Ethereal coal]]) at the Workbench just west of researcher Tahar {{waypoint -131, 366, 613}}.
1. I should use the trap on an adult fearstalker near [[Quel'ule]].
1. Near where you captured a cub, use the device on an adult fearstalker.
1. Return to [[Researcher Tahar]].

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- +3000 faction with **Researchers of Quel'ule**
