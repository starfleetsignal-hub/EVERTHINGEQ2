## Note

Even if you answered to [[Kalib Al'Sayegh]] that there are kejek in the area when completing [[Ridge Reconnaissance]], you can change your answer to "It was a false report. Theres nothing there." after the fact without any lasting consequences. Until you answer this way, you cannot progress with the quests offered by [[Lyre Shiro]] and [[Imba Shiro]] until you answer "correctly" and complete this quest.
