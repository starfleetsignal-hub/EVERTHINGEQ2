Findink Topgallant, an eccentric gnome I found in the middle of Savage Slough, is the last of his shipmates to survive an attack by cannibalistic pygmies! He's been fighting the pygmies ever since, but recently the cannibals moved their village and destroyed his camp. He ran for his life, with few provisions.

## Steps

1. Recover lost items from Dedraka tribe pygmies ![Salted Deino Meat, Barrels of Boom Powder, and Mechano Harpooners](images/FINDINK'S_EFFECTS.png)
   - 5 **barrels of boom powder**. Some possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -545, 90, 45}}, {{waypoint -510, 95, 48}}, {{waypoint -594, 94, 41}}, {{waypoint -673, 119, 129}}, {{waypoint -543, 53, 115}}
   - 4 **mechano harpooners**. Some possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -593, 44, 228}}, {{waypoint -547, 90, 49}}, {{waypoint -697, 119, 110}}, {{waypoint -688, 119, 136}}
   - 6 **bags of salted deino meat**. Some possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -572, 42, 169}}, {{waypoint -535, 52, 113}}, {{waypoint -552, 90, 23}}, {{waypoint -511, 94, 63}}, {{waypoint -493, 95, 49}}, {{waypoint -684, 119, 118}}
1. Return to [[Findink Topgallant]]
