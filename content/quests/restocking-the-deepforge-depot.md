Gwynvyn checked the Deepforge Founders' supplies of harvestables and let me know what they need most this week.

## Steps

1. Go to [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]] to gather fresh harvests.
   - Gather 50 level 100-109 raws. Gwynvyn will specify what type is needed.
1. Return to [[Gwynvyn]]

## Rewards

- 3 [[Coin of the Grand Artificer]]
- At least 2p 26g 80s 61,600 status
- +2,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +1,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
