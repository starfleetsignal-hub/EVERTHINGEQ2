## Entrance

The zone entrance is [[Crypt of Forlon]] in [[Forlorn Gist]] at {{waypoint 463, 111, 257}}, up the hills.

## Steps

Kill all the named in [[Forlorn Gist: Nightmares of Old (Solo)]] as you need the last 2 for this quest:

1. Massacre the mistress. [[Perisha, the Mistress of Flies]] {{waypoint 149.27, 48.25, 364.33}}
1. Annihilate the nightmare. [[Aga-Yagaba]] {{waypoint 444.42, 108.74, 202.93}}

- At least 3p 59g 25s 12c 107,799 status
- 3 [[Vetrovian Dres'Aag]]
