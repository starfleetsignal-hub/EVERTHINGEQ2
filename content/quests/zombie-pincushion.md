## Description

I found a throwing dagger in one of the zombies I had just dispatched. It was covered in the festering rot of the zombie for far too long for it to be of any use, but if I look long enough I'm sure I could find some usable ones.

## Steps

1. Kill [[A festering zombie|festering zombies]], found in the alcoves of Vermin's Snye.

## Rewards

- At least 4s 13c
- 10 [[Tin throwing dagger|tin throwing daggers]]
