## Steps

1. Enter the gate to the [[Baubbleshire: Vale of Shadow|Baubbleshire]]: Vale's Shadow from [[Elddar Grove]]. {{waypoint 816, -23, -530}}
1. Slay 5 [[An invading nightblood|invading nightbloods]].
1. You hear a cry for help! Find [[Martha Picklenose]] and help her escape. {{waypoint 819, -11, -500}}
1. Return to [[Elddar Grove]] and speak to [[Martha Picklenose|Martha]] there. {{waypoint 809, -21, -528}}
