## Prerequirements

- Must be level 6 of one [[Ascension]] class to start the quest

## Steps

Stop! Have you spoken with Dolas first?

1. I must find the remaining pieces of the map.
   - *Note:You will need to mentor down from 120 to 110 in order to get the chests to drop in each instance*
   1. [[Upper left-hand corner]] - Chest drop from [[Krelgrek Crag-Chair]] in [[Wurmbone Crag (Heroic)]]
   1. [[Upper middle section]] - Chest drop from [[Mrwar, the Destroyer]] in [[Kralet Penumbra: Tepid Depths (Heroic)]]
   1. [[Left middle section]] - Chest drop from [[Black Bolt]] in [[Maldura: Algorithm For Destruction (Heroic)]]
   1. [[Right middle section]] - Chest drop from [[Fiery Effigy of Clotl'thoa]] in [[Zavith'loa: The Hidden Caldera (Heroic)]]
   1. [[Bottom left corner]] - Chest drop from [[Captain Berlon Bilgewater]] in [[Brokenskull Bay: Bilgewater Falls (Heroic)]]
   1. [[Lower middle section]] - Chest drop from [[Drinal]] in [[Harrow's End: Baleful Dominion (Heroic)]] (Requires someone in your group with [[Shades of Drinal Timeline]] to zone you in?)
1. I must speak with Dolas.
   - Speak with Dolas at {{waypoint -750, -57, -192}} in [[The Sinking Sands]].
1. I need to reassemble this map.
   1. Make inquiries about this map. Speak with [[Kolanas Kirr]] in [[The Ruins of Cabilis: The Necromancer's Den]].
   1. Search for headstone shards and graveyard soil. Complete the following [[Collection Quests]]:
      - [[Debilitated Headstone Shards]]
      - [[Graveyard Soil Samples]]
      - [[Reanimation Incantation (Collection)|Reanimation Incantation]]
   1. I need to show the necromancer this incantation. Speak with [[Kolanas Kirr]] in [[The Ruins of Cabilis: The Necromancer's Den]]. He produces a [[Reassembled map of the dead]], and gives you [[A strange flask filled with herbs]].
1. I should tell Dolas about Naya'Patel.
   - Speak with Dolas at {{waypoint -750, -57, -192}} in [[The Sinking Sands]].
1. I need to find Dolas at the [[Dropship Landing Zone]].
   - Meet Dolas at {{waypoint 1851, 453, 972}} in [[Moors of Ykesha]]. Hire him as your Mercenary.
   - *Note: If you call, zone, summon ally, or otherwise escape; you will have to return and reacquire Dolas.*
1. I need to find the five markers that unlock the realm of the dead.
   1. Inspect the first marker of the dead at {{waypoint 312, -84, -435}}
   1. Inspect the second marker of the dead at {{waypoint 427, -52, -186}}
   1. Inspect the third marker of the dead at {{waypoint 640, 16, -214}}
   1. Inspect the fourth marker of the dead at {{waypoint 573, 107, -477}}
   1. Inspect the final marker of the dead at {{waypoint 753, 169, -667}}
1. When I clicked the fifth marker, I heard a sound, something like a lock disengaging.
   1. Find the entrance at {{waypoint 639, 150, -858}}. Four linked [[Protector of the Dead]] will attack.
   1. I need to harvest blood from the nayads. Harvest [[Poisonus blood]] by inspecting the corpse.
   1. I need to add this blood to the jar of ingredients. Examine [[A strange flask filled with herbs]] in your inventory. Receive [[Poison Protection]].
1. It is time to enter the realm of the dead and see what I can find.
   - Enter [[Naya'Patel: Realm of the Dead]]. Climb Ladder at {{waypoint 642, 151, -833}}.  Quest completes upon zone-in.
