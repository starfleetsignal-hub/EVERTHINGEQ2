This quest is part 9 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Collect 5 owlbear feathers. You will get a feather from every kill of any type of owlbear. You can find level 24 owlbears in [[Death's Maw]] at {{waypoint -528.84, 14.84, 52.23}}
1. Return to Torq for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Emerald of Conflict]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Nightfall Giant's Toothpick]] (All Priests)
  - [[Petrified Cat's Eye]] (All Mages)
  - [[Token of Greater Vibrations]] (All Scouts)
