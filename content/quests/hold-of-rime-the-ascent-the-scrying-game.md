## Description

Bergna Veliumsmiter has asked me to place scrying devices at marked locations throughout the ascent. Conversations that occur near these devices will be relayed to the Thurgadin forces to, hopefully, some advance warning on Rime troop movements or upcoming attacks.

## Steps

- Place the 4 scrying devices at the marked locations in [[Hold of Rime: The Ascent]]
  - *Note:The four locations I found were, one on a pillar on the slopes at the beginning, one in the room with the large number of Rime, one in the middle of a pile of junk before the ice bridge, and the last in the room with the witch doctor.*
  - The waypoints are
    - {{waypoint -130, 32, 6}}
    - {{waypoint -26, 67, -235}}
    - {{waypoint -205, 78, -323}}
    - {{waypoint -514, 86, -303}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 80g
- [[Primal Velium Shard]] x1
- +350 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
