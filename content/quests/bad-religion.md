## Steps

1. Go to [[Beggars Court: Sweat-Stained Cobbles]] instance in North Freeport at {{waypoint -149, -51, -375}}
1. Defeat 8 Heathen Barbarians in the northern area. {{waypoint 10, 3, 0}}
   - These appear to spawn randomly as you walk around the area and inside doors in the area.
1. Remove 6 pieces of propaganda placed by the heathens.
   - These look like sparkling clipboards hanging on the walls, merchant stands and poles. They appear to be random spawns throughout the entire zone.
   - Locations include:
     - {{waypoint 53, 3, 9}}
     - {{waypoint 37, 5, -22}}
     - {{waypoint -33, 2, -25}}
     - {{waypoint -11, 4, -39}}
     - {{waypoint 3, 3, -14}}
     - {{waypoint 45, -7, 64}}
     - {{waypoint 3, -7, 74}}
1. Return to Administrator Frugi in North Freeport.

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
