## Requirements

- You are no longer required to complete your Epic 1.0 quest. If necessary, you will be given a [[Shattered Weapon]] by [[Kinloch Skry'mir]] at {{waypoint -35, 24, 214}} which can be used instead of your class weapon.
- This quest is not available to the [[Beastlord]] and [[Channeler]] classes.

## Starting the Quest

1. Speak with [[Kinloch Skry'mir]] at {{waypoint -35, 24, 214}} in the [[Tenebrous Tangle]].
1. Now talk to [[Researcher Sel'Quar]] at {{waypoint -150, -117, 634}} in the [[Shrine of Thunder]] by the docks in [[Lavastorm]] in order to receive the quest.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Sa'ib Waseem]] in the [[Hall of Wizardry]] in [[The City of Paineel]] in The Sundered Frontier at {{waypoint 1996, -298, 3340}}.
1. Travel to [[The Eye of Dartain]] and collect the book [[The Art of Spellshifting]]. The book is on a desk at {{waypoint 1805, 0, 4304}}. (It has a star on the cover.)
1. Return to [[Sa'ib Waseem]].
1. You are instructed to check on Apprentice Tam, who has not yet returned.
   - Head into the [[Vermin Caves]] at {{waypoint 1458, -120, 3064}} and "Inspect" Apprentice Tam's body at {{waypoint 1255, -166, 3295}}. (Inspecting Tam's body will not break any Invis. spell)
1. Return to [[Sa'ib Waseem]] with [[Tam's empty satchel]].
1. Since Tam's satchel was empty, you must return to the Vermin Caves and take back the Fiery Jewel.
   - Defeat [[A scavenging vermin|scavenging vermin]] and [[A wandering vermin|wandering vermin]] for the update. It's a common update, so it shouldn't take long to obtain the [[Gnawed Jewel of the Underfoot]].
1. Return to [[Sa'ib Waseem]]
1. You are instructed to collect components to create a new jewel.
   - Kill 12 [[An underfoot levitatestone|underfoot levitatestones]] for their calcified lightstones (these are auto-updates). They're around  {{waypoint 1588, -6, 4345}} [[The Eye of Dartain]].
   - Kill 8 [[A stonecold carrion|stonecold carrion]] or [[A stonegraft scoundrel|stonegraft scoundrels]] for their desiccated eye lids (these are auto-updates). These are around {{waypoint 1417, -29, 3724}} between Paineel and [[Stonebrunt Encampment]].
   - ![A twilight bloom](images/Twilight_bloom.jpg)
   - Collect 6 Twilight Blooms from [[Toxxulia Forest]]. These are around the area where the feral bruisers, Toxxulia savages and the bristlevine abhorrences roam.  Some locs include:
     - {{waypoint 573, 144, 3052}}
     - {{waypoint 523, 146, 3179}}
     - {{waypoint 450, 144, 3170}}
     - {{waypoint 483, 147, 3193}}
     - {{waypoint 533, 124, 3341}}
     - {{waypoint 667, 151, 3009}}
     - Although the twilight blooms look similar to Toxxulia flower buds, they are not the same thing. The twilight blooms have a sparkle effect and cannot be tracked.
1. Return to [[Sa'ib Waseem]] with the components.
1. Return to the Vermin Caves and find a "luminescent location" with "arcane infused waters" and saturate your new [[Jewel of the Underfoot]].
   - The update is at {{waypoint 1293, -249, 3241}} at the bottom of the waterfall. Click on the luminescent item on the ground near the bottom of the pool. Note this will break invisibility or stealth.
   - [[Malefactor Reekiteeth]] (85^) will spawn and attack you.
   - Pick up the [[Fiery Jewel of the Underfoot (Quest Item)|Fiery Jewel of the Underfoot]].
1. Return to [[Sa'ib Waseem]] to complete the quest.
