*This quest is repeatable up to 5 times..*

## Steps

1. Kill a random number of one of the various monster types within the zone. Below are a few of the possibilities:
   - [[A bramble beast]]
   - [[A Darkflight witch|darkflight witches]], [[A Darkflight painweaver|painweavers]], or [[A Darkflight reaper|reapers]]
   - [[A tainted maiden]]
1. Return to [[Sheriff Anrey Scarfoot]].

## Rewards

- At least 27s 67c
- 5x [[Cool ale]]
- 5x [[Sharp cheese]]
