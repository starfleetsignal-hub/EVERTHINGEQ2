## Notes

- This is a **repeatable** crafting quest.
- The recipes are auto scribed in your recipe book the first time you pick up this quest.
- Each recipe makes 1 - 2 items.
- Bring any common roots, leather pelt, metal and glowing coals. Crafting stations are available in the zone next to quest giver.
- The primary ingredient [[Savanna resin]] can be harvested 3 at a time from small red bottles placed on the floor, shelves, crates in this zone or at Crossroads area of [[The Commonlands]]. You must have the quest active to see and pick up the resin.

## Steps

1. Check the smiths' crate - click on one of crates in next nook behind Iffa {{waypoint -31, -20, 12}}, you will be asked to make either Freeport Militia Recruit Shield or Freeport Militia Recruit Sword.
1. Craft 6 of your assigned item.
1. Return to Iffa Dingmetal.

## Rewards

- At least 5s 54c
- [[Ally's Reward Bag]]

  - has a chance to give one of the collection members for [[Ancient Teir'Dal Artifacts]]
