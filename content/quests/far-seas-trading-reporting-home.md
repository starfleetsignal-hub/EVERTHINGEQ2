## Description

Ethan needs to send an update report back to headquarters and has asked me to get the latest status updates from his crew in Ethernere.

## Steps

1. Obtain status updates from the Far Seas Traders in the tower on [[Daarspire]].
   - Check on [[Jeremy|Jeremy's]] status in the lower level of the tower. {{waypoint 143, 157, -168}}
   - Check on [[Claudia (Vesspyr Isles)|Claudia's]] status in the lower level of the tower. {{waypoint 154, 157, -162}}
   - Check on [[Roy|Roy's]] status in the upper level of the tower. {{waypoint 170, 188, -149}}
   - Check on [[Kate|Kate's]] status in the upper level of the tower. {{waypoint 173, 188, -143}}
1. Return to Captain Ethan.
1. At an Engraved Desk, craft a [[Far Seas Trading Company Expedition Report]] Prepare for this to take about 10 minutes.
1. Deliver to [[Captain Ingrid]], walking on the docks on the [[Isle of Mara]]. {{waypoint -24, -2, 122}}
1. Return to Captain Ethan

## Materials Needed

- 2 [[Matoppie roots]]
- 5 [[Effulgent incense]]
