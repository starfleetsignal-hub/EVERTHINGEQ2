## Steps

![Living Waters of Opulent Mere](images/Living_Waters.png)

1. Find the pool known as [[Opulent Mere]] at {{waypoint -49, 147, -128}}
   - If you aren't able to fly, the path up the ramp at [[Arch Terrace]] {{waypoint -295, 65, 199}} will lead to the pool
1. Gather living waters of [[Opulent Mere]]
1. you get teleported and [[A Priest of Xktkez]] says something
1. Kill the attacking [[A newly-formed oculus]]
1. Confront [[Xhindar]] at {{waypoint -37, 68, -457}}
   - [[Xhindar]] will absorb the first 5 attacks like most solo named
1. Speak with [[Xhindar]] to complete quest.
   - *Note: [[Xhindar]] will fall into a deep trance after this quest. He will awaken after 10 minutes of real time, and offer the next quest [[A Growing Threat]].*

- [[Grand Idol of Xktkez]]
- [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
