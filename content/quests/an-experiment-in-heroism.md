## Steps

1. Destroy evidence of the Awakened's experiments.
   - Kill [[The Experiment]] in [[Temple of Veeshan: Laboratory of Mutation (Heroic)]]
   - Destroy 5 evidence of experiments in [[Temple of Veeshan: Laboratory of Mutation (Heroic)]]
   - Destroy 5 evidence of experiments in [[Temple of Veeshan: Halls of the Betrayer (Heroic)]]
     - *Note:The evidence are pages on the floor throughout the zones and must be picked up before zone out.*
1. Examine the [[Disused manacles|disused manacles]] and speak with the [[Eidolon of Heroism]]
