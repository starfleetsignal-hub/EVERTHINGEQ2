## Steps

1. Defeat the following:
   - [[First key guardian]] {{waypoint -160, 151, -1020}}
   - [[Second key guardian]] {{waypoint -93, 151, -980}}
   - [[Third key guardian]] {{waypoint -318, 151, -1048}}
   - [[Fourth key guardian]] {{waypoint -354, 151, -1003}}
1. Defeat [[Lord Rak'Ashiir]] and his two [[A noble advisor|noble advisor]]s in the main building. {{waypoint -232, 154, -1052}}
   - Be sure everyone in your group enters the room, or they will be locked out when the door closes.
1. Click on the urn behind Lord Rak'Ashiir.
1. Examine [[Rile's Phylactery]] in your inventory to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Bow of the Transcended]]
  - [[Exquisite Wand of Nature]]
  - [[Nathsarian Hunting Bow of No Hope]]
  - [[Satchel of No Hope]]
  - [[Wand of Lost Souls]]
