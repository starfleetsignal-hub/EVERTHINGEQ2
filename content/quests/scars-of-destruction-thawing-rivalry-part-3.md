## Steps

1. Speak with [[Thraine Frostbeard]] at {{waypoint 776.48, 5.49, 97.28}}.
1. Meet up with the pilots in [[Western Wastes: Exploration Determination (Raid)]] to access the caves. The door to the raid zone is in [[Western Wastes]] at {{waypoint -441.85, 124.05, -257.37}}.
1. Defeat [[Guard Imobiun]].
1. Defeat [[Court Sorcerer Viranor]].
1. Defeat [[Enforcer Rimefield]].
1. Defeat [[Jund'Erin (Exploration Determination)|Frostking Jund'Erin]].
1. Speak to [[Thraine Frostbeard]] at {{waypoint -694.90, 207.55, -432.56}} within the raid zone.
