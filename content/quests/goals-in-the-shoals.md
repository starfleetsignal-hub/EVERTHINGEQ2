Toadwart, the scrawny, albino goblin in Maldura's Mushroom Bar and Grill, is not yet ready to return to his master. Hopefully, after I help him gather some soul bleeder bones and chitin terror eggs, he will be.

## Steps

1. Gather the following in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]].
   - Gather the bones of 12 [[A soul bleeder|soul bleeders]] in [[Dark Shoals]]. Some possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -21, 42, -491}}
     - {{waypoint -96, 50, -490}}
   - Collect 6 **chitin terror eggs** in [[Savage Spires]]. Some possible locations:
     - These are small and look like rocks, but have a black sparkle particle effect. Suggest you put your particle effects to High (in Options).
       - {{waypoint -96, 50, -490}}
       - {{waypoint -174, 36.26, -497.23}}
       - {{waypoint -114, 32.86, -472.41}}
       - {{waypoint -67.31, 31.95, -480.28}}
       - {{waypoint -68.79, 42.78, -453.28}}
       - {{waypoint -90.42, 62.70, -442.15}}
       - {{waypoint -191.29, 0.00, -560.77}}
       - (-22, 38, -507) Two different spots right next to each other.
       - (-27, 38, 503)

1. Return to [[Toadwart]] in [[Maldura]].

- At least 1p 41g 75s 13200 status
- +1000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**

  - [[Illumine Umbrite Wand]]
