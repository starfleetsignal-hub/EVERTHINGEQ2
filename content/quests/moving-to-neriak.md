## Steps

1. For citizens of evil alignment, this quest is started by talking to an ambassador:
   - Citizens of Freeport must talk to [[Ambassador Brutus]] in [[The City of Freeport|East Freeport]] {{waypoint -125, -37, 4}}
   - Citizens of Gorowyn must talk to [[Ambassador Zelzekla]] in [[Gorowyn]] {{waypoint 2664, 66, 1171}}
1. Talk to [[Ambassador V'Nox]] in [[Cristanos Hall]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]] {{waypoint -495, 22, 202}}

- Access to next quest
