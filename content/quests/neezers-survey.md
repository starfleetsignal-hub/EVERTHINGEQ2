## Steps

1. Read *Neezer's Questions*, which appears in your inventory.
1. Speak with [[Drundo Parn]] in the tavern. {{waypoint 881, -18, -443}}
1. Speak with [[Remo Zigg]]. {{waypoint 800, -1, -455}}
1. Speak with [[Fillzer Fuzzle]]. {{waypoint 819, -15, -451}}
1. Return to Neezer, answer his questions, and receive your reward.

- Coin
- [[Nogginspark's Wondrous Tickerdoodle|Nogginspark's Wonderous Tickerdoodle]]
