## How to start

Completing [[Rage of Cthurath: Wrest of the Unknown]] (main signature adventure questline for this expansion) flags the whole account to start getting these dropped quest starters.

Quest starter is found in treasure chest dropped by:

- [[A husked voidmaw]] in [[Writher Ruins]] area, Southeast part of Yon Gorroth {{waypoint -475, 35, 234}}

## Notes

- This is a POI quest to find other secluded quest giving locations.
  - *(Some locations require flight)*
- Players are tasked with finding the five locations within [[Yon Gorroth]]. At each location there is a hidden 'Xebrith Cognizance' which will reveal itself once the player is close enough. Clicking on them will offer 'Kill X number of Y mob' type quests that can be completed separately from this quest.
- Players do not have to interact with these Cognizances, merely to discover them, to get credit for *this* quest. (But they will still need to interact with them if they want the individual quests given by them)
- All 5 of the locations are also part of the larger list of locations needed for Yon Gorroth exploration achievement [[Exploration: Yon Gorroth]].
- The Incomprehensible, Madness, and Consequence can be completed later around the same location surrounding Rot Reek Bog {{waypoint -294, 20, -322}}

## Steps

![Xebrith Cognizance location indicator](images/Xebrith_Cognizance_location_indicator.png)

1. Explore/Discover these 5 locations within [[Yon Gorroth]]  (in any order)
   - Reach [[Rot Reek Stub]] at {{waypoint -219, 172, -289}} to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Humanity]]
   - Reach [[Revenant's Disc]] at {{waypoint -413, 90, 485}} to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: The Incomprehensible]]
   - Reach [[Skur'rax Stain]] at {{waypoint 272, 153, 361}} to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Forbidden]]
   - Reach [[Spiral Slag]] at {{waypoint 576, 116, 678}} to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Consequence]]
   - Reach [[Doomview's Secret]] at {{waypoint 132, 223, -54}} to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Madness]]
