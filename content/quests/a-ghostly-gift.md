## Notes

- You must be able to speak [[Death's Whisper]] to complete this quest.

## Steps

![Example of 'a hidden image](images/A_hidden_image.jpg)

1. Examine 4 hidden images:
   - {{waypoint -17, 159, -135}} on wall left of entrance
   - {{waypoint 11, 156, -105}} on pillar in front of entrance
   - {{waypoint 13, 157, -65}} on pillar
   - {{waypoint 56, 160, -60}} just inside sliding doors at SW corner of entrance hall
1. Kill six [[A Scaleborn warrior (Sanctum of the Scaleborn)|Scaleborn warriors]] for their hearts.
1. Go to the [[Scaleborn Barracks]] {{waypoint 144, 135, 71.64}} and place the hearts into six bowls around the room.
   - Upon completion of this step, a 5-minute timer will start, so you may want to clear the path to the [[Ready Room]] beforehand.
1. Speak to [[The shade of a Fallen Orate]] {{waypoint 150, 157, -80}} in the Ready Room within 5 minutes!
1. Kill ten Scaleborn warriors for their horns.
1. Return to the Scaleborn Barracks and interact with the ritual bowls again.
1. Return to The shade of a Fallen Orate to complete the quest.

## Rewards

Receive one of the following:

- [[Great Sword of the Departed]]
- [[Wand of the Departed]]
