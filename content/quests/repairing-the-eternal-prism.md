## Steps

1. Repair the Eternal Prism (note that you will not be offered the pop-up quests in the instances if you don't have this quest active)
   1. Complete the Quests in [[Miragul's Phylactery: Scion of Ice]]
      - [[Disrupt the Wizardry]]
      - [[End the Enchanting]]
      - [[Love Caught in Time]] from [[Mundug Stonefist]]
   1. Complete the Quests in [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Anathema]]
      - [[Necromantic Neverwhere]] automatically offered when you enter [[The Crypt of Disdain]]
      - [[A Sorcerous Tune]] automatically offered when you enter [[The Fundament of Sorcery]]
   1. Complete the Quest in [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Crucible]]
      - [[Stifle the Sentience]] automatically offered when you enter the zone
   1. Use the box at {{waypoint -251, -6, -382}} next to [[Lucretia Nasin]] in [[Hollowfrost Grove]] and receive the [[Fragment of the Eternal Prism]]
   1. Use the [[Fragment of the Eternal Prism]] *(can be in any of the Miragul's Phylactery zones)*
1. Return to [[Connor Farquharson]]

***Steps 4 and 5 can be done at any point during the quest progression, he will have a book over his head when you get out of the Crucible in this case.***

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
