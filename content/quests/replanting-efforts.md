## Steps

1. Craft a [[Gardener's Shovel]] (from the [[Othmir Gardening Tools]] recipe received from the previous quest, [[Seed Sampling]]) on a Forge {{waypoint -1147, -532, 3390}}, using the following materials:
   - 3 [[Sumac lumber]]
   - 1 [[Titanium ore]]
   - 1 [[Ethereal coal]]
   - Note: You must craft the shovel while on this step of the quest.  Crafting one ahead of time, won't let you proceed to the next step.
1. Plant 3 nuts near tree stumps ![A typical planting site](images/Planting_Site.jpg)
   - Click on the green glowies
1. Return to [[Livi]]

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +150 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
