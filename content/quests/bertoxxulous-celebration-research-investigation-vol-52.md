## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Bertoxxulous has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items.

1. Purchase the [[Disgusting Slime Sample]] from Dolvin for 5000 status
   - In [[Antonica]] he wanders along the [[Qeynos Highway]] from the river bank camp between the North and South Qeynos bridges {{waypoint 140.22, -16.65, 214.82}} to a point between the two forts {{waypoint -431.53, -16.52, 177.92}}
1. Purchase the [[Cracked Jacinth]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the [[Blood Mites]] from Laparnuu for 5000 status
   - in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]], near Petalcrown Perch and the Roost Warrens.  Laparnuu wanders the path from {{waypoint 338, 238, 7}}  all the way to {{waypoint 31, 236, -13}}
1. Return to [[Herald of the Plaguelord]] {{waypoint -1157.19, -33.96, 525.38}} in [[Antonica]]
