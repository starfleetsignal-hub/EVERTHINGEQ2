## Steps

Zone into [[Zavith'loa: The Lost Caverns (Advanced Solo)]].

- Slaughter the beastmaster - [[Beastmaster Kau'mua (Advanced Solo)]] and [[Ceracuspis]]
- Search for the missing explorers -  kill [[Mo'iana the Ravenous (Advanced Solo)]].  Ring event.  Kill the [[Kindred of Mo'iana]] to spawn the named [[Mo'iana the Ravenous (Advanced Solo)|Mo'iana]].

- At least 1p 67g 40s 6,250 status
- 3 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
