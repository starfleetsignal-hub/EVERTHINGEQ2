## Steps

1. Kill the following mobs for samples:
   - [[An icegaze basilik]] in [[Howling Pass]]
   - [[A brownbeak]] outside of the Ry'Gorr Fortress and in Dragon's Head Drifts
   - [[A frostpaw direbear]] or [[A direpaw cub]] near Dragon's Head Drifts
   - [[A ravaging snowmane]] or [[A snowmane sabertooth]] in Dragon's Head Drifts
   - [[A woolly mammoth bull]] in Dragon's Head Drifts
1. Return to [[Carpus]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Blade]](Bruiser, Monk, Beastlord)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Arcane Orb]] (All Mages)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Round Shield]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Tower Shield]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Oracle's Buckler]] (All Priests)
