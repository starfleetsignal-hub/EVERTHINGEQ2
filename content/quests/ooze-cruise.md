Alaric Clangzback, a gnemlin merchant in the Stone Mercantile District of Maldura, has some tok oil, but it is in short supply due to the siege upon the city. Thankfully, he is willing to sell me some, in exchange for some Blackanvil ooze.

## Steps

1. Kill 12 [[A Blackanvil ooze|Blackanvil oozes]] at [[Blackanvil Bay]] in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]] {{waypoint 811.00, 30.36, -49.91}}.
1. Return to [[Alaric Clangzback]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 41g 75s 12854 status
- +1000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Astral Umbrite Ring]]
  - [[Pyre Umbrite Ring]]
