## Prerequisites

- You must have +10,000 faction with Riliss to unlock this quest.
- You must have completed the quests, [[Guard Posts]], [[Sarnak Interlopers]], and [[Sokokarn't]] to unlock this quest.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Hammin]] in the [[Field of Bone]]. {{waypoint -1436, -281, -1155}}
1. Puchase [[A sealed stone case]] from [[Blugie]]. You must have +30,000 faction with The Bellywhumpers to be able to purchase this item. {{waypoint -1438, -281, -1157}}
   - If you complete all the Bellywhumper quests in [[Fens of Nathsar]] you will not have enough faction to purchase the item. There exist two paths to increasing faction at this point:
     - Complete the repeatable quest [[The Bellywhumper Harvest]] while farming Bonedigger Burynai in the [[Field of Bone]]; or
     - Complete the quests, [[Clean Out the Caves]] and [[Everyone Loves a Snitch]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]], which unlocks more Bellywhumper quests starting with [[Hail to the Chief]].
1. Return to Klok Tulren.

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c
