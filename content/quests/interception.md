## Steps

1. Head to [[New Tunaria]] and kill the [[A shambling overgrowth|shambling overgrowths]] that auto-spawn near the front gates, at {{waypoint -16, 75, -945}}. Loot the plant food from the chest that one of them drops.
1. Examine the plant food.
1. Hail [[Elia Redleaf]] in [[Kelethin]]. {{waypoint 148, 111, 117}}
