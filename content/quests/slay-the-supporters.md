## Description

I must go aboard The Vigilant: Infiltration and slay the supporters of Vigoth Rodnan to weaken his hold on the ship. His supporters are Znag the Obrut, King Cruszhem, Arch-Amahn Wretchedcorpse and Kage-Zonn Radas.

## Steps

   - Kill [[Znag the Obrut]]
   - Kill [[King Cruszhem]]
   - Kill [[Arch-Amahn Wretchedcorpse]]
   - Kill [[Kage-Zonn Radas]]
1. Return to Ismat

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
