## Notes

1. This quest requires:
   - 5x Formless kindling

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Anefa]] at {{waypoint 543.80, 94.78, -678.61}} and receive [[Recipe Scroll: Treats for Very Good Boys]].
   - Autoscribes [[Kith's Meaty Treats]]
1. Harvest:
   - 20x [[Nightscar Prowler Meat]]
   - 10x [[Grasslands Leather]]
   - 15x [[Darou Daikon]]
1. Craft 5x [[Kith's Meaty Treats]]
   - Requires 5x [[Formless Kindling]]
1. Feed [[Kith (in Yon Gorroth)|Kith]] at {{waypoint 462, 81, -573}} the treats.
1. Pat the head of [[Kith (in Yon Gorroth)|Kith]] and have him take you to the Void Obelisk.
1. Use the [[Void Obelisk]] teleporter to enter [[Oogothl Sprawl]].

## Rewards

- [[Status Coin (75,000)]]
- At least 16p 54g 73s 48c
