## Steps

1. Place one ward on each corner of Dreadkeep. These are on the outside corners and highlighted with sparkles. They are located at {{waypoint 724, 74, 1370}}, {{waypoint 796, 75, 1370}}, {{waypoint 796, 75, 1270}}, {{waypoint 737, 75, 1270}} ![Ward in position at one of the corners](images/Placed_ward.jpg)![Final centre ward in position](images/Centre_ward.jpg)
1. Place ward in center and activate.
1. Bless the ward - left click it ***ONCE***
   - **WARNING:** each time you click the ward it summons an extra wave of monsters for the next step.
1. Defeat the [[A Dreadkeep defender|Dreadkeep defenders]] (four lvl 101vH)
1. Return to Joreye.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g 80s
