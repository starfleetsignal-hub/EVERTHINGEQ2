## Steps

!["Mine" for uncut gem under/near boulders.](images/Uncut_gem.jpg)

1. Mine 6 uncut gems from small rock-like nodes around the southern edge of [[U.J.L. 1.0]]. {{waypoint 1093, 395, 1050}}
   - The spawn locations are random. See the talk page for some of the locations where other players have found them. Or just look around the bases of the large rocks in tha area for small round grey lumps.
1. Bring the gems to [[Belfie Bentsprocket]] at [[Dropship Landing Zone]], who will craft them into a [[Gnomish Keepsake]]. {{waypoint 1798, 453, 959}}
1. Give the Gnomish Keepsake to [[Shaman Zaz'i'zaz]] in the [[Thalz'Iz'Zaz Area]]. {{waypoint 1149, 393, 866}}
1. Return to Wayth Bitsnbobs.

  - [[Loop of the Gukish Lords]] (Scouts)
