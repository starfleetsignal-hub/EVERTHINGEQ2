## Steps

1. Click the boat by [[Queen Amree]] to return to the [[Qeynos Province District]].
1. Speak to [[Jesenda]] near the dojo in the [[Elddar Grove]] {{waypoint 748, -20, -354}} then lead/protect her to the gate from [[Elddar Grove]] that leads to [[The Willow Wood: Besieged Hamlet]] and zone in. {{waypoint 810, -21, -564}}
1. Take [[Jesenda]] to the site of the gnoll ambush behind the target up the hill and don't lose sight of her. She will examine the ground for a few moments, then take off at a jog without saying anything to you. Your job is to protect her as she wanders through the zone. You will be ambushed by [[An assassin]] along the way. {{waypoint 811, -21, -649}}
1. [[Jesenda]] continues tracking, often through aggro groups of gnolls, until she reaches the gate to the [[The Forest Ruins: A Clandestine Meeting|Forest Ruins]]. There the assassin who attacked you earlier will give an evil villain soliloquy before having a trio of [[A gnoll|gnolls]] attack you.
1. Follow the assassin's trail into the [[The Forest Ruins: A Clandestine Meeting|Forest Ruins]] (click the door).
1. Collect 8 piles of loam (each looks like a small pile of dirt). They are very spread out- it's safer to veer to the right and collect/click them.
1. Return to [[Queen Amree]].
