If you are looking for the quest of the same name:

## Starting the Quest

This quest remains the same regardless of the zone you start in. The only difference is the person you speak with to start the quest:

- Talk to [[Vel Adreni]] in [[Lavastorm]] by the [[Ulteran Spire (LS)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -416, -31, -572}}
- Talk to [[Eruvim Gul'aviz]] in [[Everfrost]] by the [[Ulteran Spire (EF)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint 463, -35, -1334}}
- Talk to [[Adalla Ful'adin]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] by the [[Ulteran Spire (Zek)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -60, -33, 350}}
- Talk to [[Taloc D'arzin]] in [[Loping Plains]] by the [[Ulteran Spire (LP)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint 62, 36, -334}}

## Prerequisites

Some materials, including fuel, may be purchased from [[A Quellithulian supply chandler]]. You will need T2 common crafting ingredients as well.

## Steps

1. Create six of one of the following items as assigned to help repair the damage to the spire.
   - [[Woven Ether Bindings]]
   - [[Infused Kaborite Cell]]
   - [[Rune Inscribed Sheet]]
1. Turn in the completed items to the NPC who gave you the quest to begin with.

## Rewards

- 6 [[Quellithulian Token of Appreciation|Quellithulian Tokens of Appreciation]]
