## Steps

1. Collect 2 [[Twark slug|tame forest slugs]] from [[Twark]] (around {{waypoint 271, -180, -658}}
1. Release the 2 slugs in the gardens of Nye'Caelona by using Twark slug
   - The icon for the slugs in your inventory will be darkened until you reach the right spot; a good spot is near the tree at {{waypoint -543, -92, -438}}.
1. Speak to [[Elder Lyraquel]]

## Rewards

- [[Sovereign of Rile]] currency
- +2000 faction with **Nye'Caelona**
- At least 1p 53g 9s
