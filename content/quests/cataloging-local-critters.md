## Steps

1. Use the [[Catalogue Creature]] skill on the following creatures, in no particular order.

   - [[A rust monster]] {{waypoint -30, 0, -39}}
   - [[A small rust monster]] {{waypoint -30, 0, -39}}
   - [[A small scorpion]] {{waypoint 53, 0, 59}}
1. Return to [[Inquisitor Thorson]].

- +150 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
