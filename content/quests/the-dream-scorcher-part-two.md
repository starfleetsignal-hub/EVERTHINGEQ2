## Steps

1. Kill the following 3 nameds IN ORDER to receive [[Nightmarion, Focal of Mental Energy]]:
   1. Kill [[Tairiza the Widow Mistress]] in [[The Tomb of Thuuga]] for the Prism of Mentality.
   1. Kill [[Selrach Di'Zok]] in [[Temple of Kor-Sha]] off of [[Chardok]] for the blood. Or alternatively kill [[Venril Sathir]] in [[Venril Sathir's Lair]] off of [[Sebilis]] for the blood.
   1. Kill [[Hoshkar]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]] for the power.
1. Kill [[Kullaba Dreadpool]] in [[Kullaba Dreadpool's Lair]].
   - This is a solo step. Enter the door to [[Kullaba Dreadpool's Lair]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]] behind [[City of Mist]] at {{waypoint 23, 145, -963}}. When you enter the zone, use the [[Nightmarion, Focal of Mental Energy|Nightmarion]], then proceed to clear the zone.

- [[Dream Scorcher (Mythical)]]
