## Starting the Quest

You must complete [[A Nightmarish Illness]] first, then re-enter The Hedge Hollow and "save" the gnome by killing the Trained Entity seen chasing him in the "train" animation that auto-starts when you get close to {{waypoint 1006, 819, -564}}.  The "Trained Entity" and the gnome spawn at the back side of the house you just passed, kill the "Trained Entity" and get the quest from the gnome.

This quest is repeatable once per day.

## Steps

1. Find 4 items:
   - a journal {{waypoint 916, 8, -638}}
   - a dolly {{waypoint 863, 8, -585}}
   - a skull {{waypoint 918, 8, -525}}
   - a dagger {{waypoint 874, 8, -505}}
1. Return to the terrified gnome
