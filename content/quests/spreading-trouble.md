## Steps

1. Kill 6 void-touched creatures. These include: ([[A void-touched juvenile lava crawler|void-touched juvenile lava crawlers]], [[A void-touched magma wormling|void-touched magma wormlings]], or [[A void-touched Sootfoot whelp|void-touched Sootfoot whelps]].
   - Void-touched lava crawlers spawn at {{waypoint -222, -112, 227}} and have a very fast respawn.
   - Void-touched magma wormlings spawn on the beach at {{waypoint -90, -129, 452}}
1. Kill [[A Void attacker]] at the first turn in the path when you enter [[Najena's Dungeon]]. {{waypoint -76, -129, 344}}
1. Return to [[Blisterzonk]].

## Rewards

- At least 11g 82s 49c
- [[Shimmering Gem of Prowess]]
- -2500 faction with **Sootfoot Talent Society**
