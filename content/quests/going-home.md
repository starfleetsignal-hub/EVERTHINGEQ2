## Steps

1. Go to Osh at the shore {{waypoint 4396, -1096, 1411}}
1. Dismount any mounts, sit (default: x) and speak to Osh
1. Speak to Nika, pacing the camp {{waypoint 4294, -1081, 1468}}
1. Speak to Osh, now back in camp
1. Osh will follow you. Go from waypoint to waypoint as he makes them available. Hail him, especially when you get into the othmir camp.  Do not fly (Dis-mount is safest), or Osh will return to camp and you will have to start over
1. Speak to Yeppa at [[Yeppa's Last Stand]] {{waypoint 4265, -1094, 2044}}

- At least 1p 20g

  - [[Fishbone Greathammer]]
