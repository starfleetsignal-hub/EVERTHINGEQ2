## Steps

1. Slay 10 wisps. They can be [[A grove wisp|grove wisps]] {{waypoint -176.84, -48.02, -436.96}} or [[A moon wisp|moon wisps]] {{waypoint -550, 0, -550}}. You may not get a quest update with every one you kill, however.
   - *Note*: to hit wisps with melee attacks, you will need a magical weapon.
1. Return to Mooshga.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 67c
- 10 [[Wisp tofu]]
