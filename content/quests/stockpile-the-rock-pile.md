## Description

The citizens of Firmroot Moot have requested that I collect samples of rock from inside the remains of Upper Guk. I should look for strange rocks so that I can easily chip off some pieces to carry in my pack.

## Steps

1. Find 4 more strange rock fragments within [[The Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen]].  The black rocks are located around the zone in various locations:
   - {{waypoint 7, -39, -142}}
   - {{waypoint -60, -46, -292}}
   - {{waypoint -11, -53, -332}}
   - {{waypoint 131, -69, -370}}
   - {{waypoint 115, -69, -368}}
1. Return to Vegupa Marrsheart for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
