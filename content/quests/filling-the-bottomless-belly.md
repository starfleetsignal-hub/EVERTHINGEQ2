## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- [[Gathering]] skill 140+ AND/OR [[Trapping]] skill 140+.

## Steps

1. Harvest 100 foodstuffs from shrubs and dens in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]].
1. Return to [[Duggin Brandywine|Duggin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill Experience
- 3s 7c
- One of the following:
  - [[An ornate dining plate]]
  - [[Ornate fork]]
