## Steps

Gil McMartin impressed upon me what must next be accomplished to bring glory to Qeynos: I must destroy tomes of foul incantation, used to rain terror upon the Shattered Lands and currently in the possession of neophytes in the City of Freeport.

1. Within 20 minutes, destroy 5 crates of books within Freeport.  Can be found in merchant shops in East and South Freeport.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +2000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- **Note**: This quest does not grant any experience or achievement experience.
