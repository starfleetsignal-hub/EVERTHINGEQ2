## Steps

![Lynchpin](images/Lynchpin.jpg)

1. Find [[Lynchpin]] in one of the hollow trees surrounding [[Karnor's Castle]]. Known locations are:
   - {{waypoint 400, 23, -167}}
   - {{waypoint 637, 22, -352}}
1. Defeat the [[A drolvarg snarler|drolvarg snarlers]] surrounding Lynchpin.
1. Ride Lynchpin back to [[Dreg's Landing]].
1. Return to [[Borbin Happens]] at the docks.

## Rewards

- At least 14g 81s 38c
- [[Radiating Gem of Prowess]]
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
