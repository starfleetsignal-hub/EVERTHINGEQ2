## Steps

![](images/Gaseous_scouts.jpg)

1. Find a [[Nameless druid (Kylong Plains)|nameless druid]] in the Ryjesium Peaks of [[Kylong Plains]] {{waypoint 1435, 417, -541}}
1. Fish 10 *gaseous scouts* (look like small clouds) off the cliffs, ranging from about {{waypoint 1527, 384, -319}} around to {{waypoint 1355, 385, -252}}. There are approximately 8 of them, with a relatively short respawn of up to 3-4 minutes, but they are also found around {{waypoint 1209, 401, -571}} so you dont have to wait for repop.
1. Return to the [[Nameless druid (Kylong Plains)|nameless druid]].
1. Return to [[Veryn P'Neru]].

## Notes

This quest can be done at the same time as:

- [[Broken Barrier: Wounded of the Water]] in Fens of Nathsar.
- [[Broken Barrier: Wounded of the Earth]] in Kunzar Jungle.
- [[Broken Barrier: Wounded of the Fire]] in Jarsath Wastes.
