## Steps

1. Speak with [[Rogla Dainkiller]] at {{waypoint -644, -343, 2722}}
1. Kill the following, both around {{waypoint -409, -373, 3034}}
   - [[A snowmane sabertooth|snowmane sabertooths]] till you have 8 pristine tusks from them.
   - [[A ravaging snowmane|ravaging snowmanes]] for a plush snowmane pelt. (uncommon update)
1. Return to [[Rogla Dainkiller]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +4,550 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
