## Steps

1. Gather the components for the Coldain Chipper:  (May be done in any order)
   - **Get a [[Pristine rhino horn]]**
     - *Note:the [[A brontotherium patriarch|brontotherium patriarch]] in Great Divide (ring war area) will update this step (heroic encounter)  Only one drops, No Trade, BUT... it has the new 'tradeable for 48 hrs...' flag on it, and everyone in the group can trade it around to get the update.  DON'T DO THIS.  Everyone needs the item to craft the Chipper.  The Patriarch respawns almost immediately anywhere between the questgiver and the Ring War area.*
   - **Get 3 [[Petrified treant twigs]]**
     - *Note:Dropped by Petrified Ancients in [[Forgotten Pools]] This IS now a group update.*
   - **Get 5 [[Sharpening rock|sharpening rocks]]**
     - *Note:obtained by harvesting little targettable 'piles of dense stone' in the [[Caverns of Living Stone]]* *in [[Eastern Wastes]]. *{{waypoint -23, -400, 3559}} These harvests are NOT group update.  Everyone must get their own.![](images/A_pile_of_dense_stone.jpg)
1. Craft the chipper on a woodworking table (requires 1 glowing sandpaper) using [[The Coldain Chipper recipe]].
   - *Note: You CAN lose your components if you fail to make a pristine on the Coldain Chipper recipe (tradeskill level 15), so beware if you are doing this on a level 1 or 2 crafter.*
1. Return to [[Malidain Glacierbane]]
