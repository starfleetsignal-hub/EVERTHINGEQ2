## Prerequisites

- You must complete all the other quests at [[Sathir's Span]] before this quest becomes available.

## Steps

1. Pick up the crate that appears next to [[Merchant Hesskin]].
1. Deliver the crate to [[Cavil Restault]] in [[Omen's Call]] within 20 minutes. {{waypoint 205, -61, -272}}

## Rewards

- At least 6g 27s 71c
