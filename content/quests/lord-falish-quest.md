## Steps

1. Go to Falish's grave {{waypoint -457, 15, 70}} in the graveyard in town.
1. Inspect it and [[Lord Falish (Named)|Lord Falish]] will spawn.
1. Kill him and loot the [[Amulet of Lord Falish]].
1. Return to [[Philip Glaslow]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 28g 76s
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Arcane Sage's Vest]] (All Mages)
  - [[Dauntless Gladiator's Breastplate]] (Plate tanks)
  - [[Fearless Pathfinder's Chain Tunic]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Feral Rainkeeper's Tunic]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Otherworldly Ritualist's Hauberk]] (Shamans)
  - [[Resilient Grandmaster's Gi]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Righteous Augur's Plate Cuirass]] (Clerics)
