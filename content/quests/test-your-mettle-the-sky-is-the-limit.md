## Steps

1. Get a [[Steppes Invasion Debris|debris sample]].
   - On the beach, on the south side of the [[Dead River Docks]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]] at {{waypoint -474.47, -24.14, -359.32}}
1. Return and speak to [[Orren]]
1. Search for [[Goldbeak]]
   1. Take the the cloud at {{waypoint -461, -71, -439}} to [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] and speak with [[Hydona]] in [[Sanctuary (POI)|Sanctuary]] {{waypoint 334.30, 286.45, 966.37}}
      - *Use this same route if you have also accepted the Adventure Timeline Quest: [[Ballads of Zimara: The Aerie Overture]].*
      - *The [[Ulteran Spires|Ulteran Spire]] is unlocked to [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] at this step but the Adventurer Timeline Quest will not advance until you take the Cloud to [[Splendor Sky Aerie]], or complete the quest [[For the Greater Good!]].*
   1. speak again with [[Hydona]] in [[Sanctuary (POI)|Sanctuary]] {{waypoint 334.30, 286.45, 966.37}}

## Notes

1. The list of materials required for this Tradeskill Crafting Timeline is:
   - 3 [[Aerated mineral water]]
   - 12 [[Aether Ore]]
   - 9 [[Bittersweet Vineroot]]
   - 3 [[Breadfruit]]
   - 6 [[Charger Meat]]
   - 9 [[Corymbia Wood]]
   - 9 [[Direbeak Hide]]
   - 15 [[Pepitas Seeds]]
   - 9 [[Trystine Gem|Trystine Gems]]
1. There is a Fuel Merchant in Sanctuary at {{waypoint 312.66, 276.23, 1068.17}} if needed. The list of fuels required for this Tradeskill Crafting Timeline is:
   - 15 [[Zimaran Candle]]
   - 35 [[Zimaran Coal]]
   - 15 [[Zimaran Kindling]]
   - 30 [[Zimaran Sandpaper]]

## Rewards

- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c 56000 status
- 4,573,156,166 TS exp no bonuses but use this for powerleveling Tradeskill
- +4,000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
