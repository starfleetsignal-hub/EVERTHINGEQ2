## Description

"Integrating into Kelethin's fae society requires me to remove diseased creatures before they can infect the rest of Greater Faydark. Lady Katri Eloranta told me that the fae have managed to keep these creatures contained on Green Knoll, but have been unable to heal them completely." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay 2 of each of the following creatures (in no particular order):
   - [[A diseased spider]] on [[Green Knoll]] around {{waypoint 418.66, 48.71, 23.85}}
   - [[A diseased beetle]] on [[Green Knoll]] around {{waypoint 575.65, 44.29, 144.40}}
   - [[A diseased slither]] on [[Green Knoll]] around {{waypoint 516.43, 58.64, 124.36}}
1. Return to [[Lady Katri Eloranta]]

## Rewards

- At least 1s 70c
