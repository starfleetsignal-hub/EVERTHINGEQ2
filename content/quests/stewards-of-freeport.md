## Steps

1. Kill 3 [[A dissident recruiter|dissident recruiters]] in [[Scale Yard: Traitor's End]].  These are auto-pops, so be careful running around with low health!
1. Return to Derald Walldons
1. Speak to a captured dissident in the jail below the Freeport Militia House
1. Head to {{waypoint -160, -59, -198}} in [[North Freeport (POI)|North Freeport]] and enter [[Den of the Deserters]]. The entrance is a door on the back side of a house.
   - **NOTE:** The game not only makes it sound as if the hideout is in Beggar's Court, but allows you to zone into Beggar's Court when it normally wouldn't. Don't bother - there's nothing in there.
1. Defeat [[Aldran Millhouden]] (scales v coercer) and his two [[A dissident bodyguard|dissident bodyguards]] (scales vv guardian).
1. Return to Derald Walldons.

## Rewards

- At least 10g 97c @ lvl 56
- [[Ring of the Freeportian]]
