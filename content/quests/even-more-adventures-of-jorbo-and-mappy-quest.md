## Notes

- *This quest is also part of the [[Book Quests Timeline]].*

Although the quest journal does not label this quest as heroic, nearly every mob it requires is heroic. Keep the quest starter book with you as you'll need to read it after killing each group of 10 monsters. If you also have the quest [[Adventures of Jorbo and Mappy (Quest)|Adventures of Jorbo and Mappy]], you can work these quests through the first 60 kills at the same time.

## Steps

1. Kill any 10 corrupted halflings. This includes [[A corrupted bandit|corrupted bandits]], [[A corrupted brigand|brigands]], and [[A corrupted thief|thieves]]. These are found throughout [[West Rivervale]]. {{waypoint -201, -9, -76}}
1. Kill any 10 corrupted halflings.
1. Kill any 10 corrupted halflings.
1. Kill 10 [[A shadowed halfling|shadowed halflings]]. These are found throughout [[East Rivervale]]. {{waypoint -494, 2, -211}}
1. Kill 10 shadowed halflings.
1. Kill 10 shadowed halflings.
1. Kill any 10 blightrats. This includes [[A blightrat|regular blightrats]], [[A young blightrat|young blightrats]], [[A blightrat avenger|blightrat avengers]], [[A snapjaw blightrat|snapjaw blightrats]], and [[A fetid blightrat|fetid blightrats]]. These are found in northern West Rivevale. {{waypoint -152, 2, -208}}
1. Kill any 10 blightrats.
1. Kill any 10 blightrats.
