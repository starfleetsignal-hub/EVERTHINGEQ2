## Steps

1. Gather and hunt the following
   - Gather 8 **fallen palm wood** in [[The Salt Mire]] area around {{waypoint 99, 5, -265}}![Fallen palm wood](images/Fallen_palm_wood.png)
   - Hunt 6 [[A sandscorned prowler|sandscorned prowlers]] in the area around [[Ulgaf]].
1. Return to [[Ulgaf]].
1. Finish the construction of the ballista on the [[The Scale Flats]] {{waypoint 209.59, 114.01, -36.88}}![Ballista](images/Ballista.png)
1. Return to [[Ulgaf]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
