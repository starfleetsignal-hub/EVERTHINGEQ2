## Steps

1. Eldriaks will fly to Daarspire; speak to him when he arrives there {{waypoint -13, 204, -106}}
1. Hunt 12 [[A shadowveil soulblight|shadowveil soulblights]] at [[Veiled Thessk]] {{waypoint -755, 39, -178}}
1. Kill [[Soulscourge]]!  It is a named soulblight that will immediately spawn once the 12th soulblight is killed
1. Return to Eldraks on Daarspire {{waypoint -13, 204, -106}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Cloak of the Eternal Broodlands]]
