## Steps

![Click the glowing flower centers](images/Release_spores_of_decay.png)

1. Release 8 spores of decay from the giant flowers by clicking the glowing flower centers.
1. Return to Vuulan.
1. Kill the [[A Defender of Growth|Defender of Growth]].
1. Talk to Vuulan.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Bronze Icon of the Scale Temple]] (Charm Slot)
