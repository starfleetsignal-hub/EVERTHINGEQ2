Upon inspecting the abandoned goblin blade you notice how dulled and rusty it has become. Perhaps you should find some chunks of rock so that the Frostfoot may be able to make a whetstone. With their blades sharpened the Frostfoot should increase their survivability.

## Steps

1. You should gather 6 chunks of rock. These are known to drop from [[Frozen Statue of the Ice Maiden]] and [[Frozen Statue of the Snowbeast]].
1. You should find someone who could make use of the rocks.
1. Speak to Nolram Frostfoot {{waypoint -93.56, 1.25, -81.86}}

## Rewards

- At least 8p
- 10 [[Epic Token of E'ci]]

Select one of:

- [[Goblin Bladed Mace]]
- [[Goblin Bladed Chakram]]
