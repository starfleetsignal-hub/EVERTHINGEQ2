## Notes

This quest and [[Good for Firewood]] are needed for [[Ballads of Zimara: Riddlestone Remediation Services]]

## Steps

1. Collect the following
   - In Burrower's Timber use the Sacrificed Corruption bauble to collect 6 Metallic Tainted Auduborian samples. Note: must not be in combat to collect samples.(group-wide update)
     1. Head to {{waypoint 518.32, 84.61, 265.03}} near Burrower's Timber.
     1. Target 'a metallic tainted auduborian'
     1. Get just close enough to use the Sacrificed Corruption. Once used, you can defeat the mob. **(Note: if you get too close the mob will not update.)**
   - ![Healthy Auduborian Bark](images/Healthy_Auduborian_Bark.jpg)Gather 8 Healthy Auduborian Bark. (group-wide update) Some possible locations in Burrower's Timber include: **NOTE:**  These will appear as green sparklies on the ground.
     - {{waypoint 497.99, 93.89, 217.74}}
     - {{waypoint 503.64, 91.50, 242.78}}
     - {{waypoint 515.53, 94.94, 220.94}}
     - {{waypoint 523.59, 90.84, 237.31}}
     - {{waypoint 556.33, 83.20, 323.82}}
     - {{waypoint 585.41, 107.11, 411.00}}
     - {{waypoint 585.84, 107.54, 403.93}}
     - {{waypoint 599.56, 106.54, 433.60}}
     - {{waypoint 618.68, 107.81, 432.20}}
     - {{waypoint 625.08, 107.22, 461.95}}
1. Return to [[Oolchimedes]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +3,500 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
