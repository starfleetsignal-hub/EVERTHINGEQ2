1. Visit the following locations:
   - [[Camp Char'Nik]]: {{waypoint 295, -99, -1032}}
   - [[Grueling Pits]]: {{waypoint 265, -90, -998}}
   - [[Camp Gar'Nik]]: {{waypoint 180.99, -104.05, -1122.83}}
   - [[Scrawling Cliffs]]: {{waypoint 437, -80, -881}}
1. Hunt the following Sandscrawler Goblins for their scalps: {{waypoint 181, -102, -988}}
   - [[Sandscrawler bandit]]s for 5 updates
   - [[Sandscrawler trooper]]s for 5 updates
   - [[Sandscrawler shamanite]]s for 5 updates
   - [[Sandscrawler diviner]]s for 5 updates (appear to be an uncommon spawn)
1. Return to [[Sergeant Tuskin]]
