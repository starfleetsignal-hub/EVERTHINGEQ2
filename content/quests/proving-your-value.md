## Prequisites

Need to have -10k faction with the Guardians of the Underfoot

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Matve]] {{waypoint -71, 77, -769}}
   1. He is located on a rock bridge down by the Tower of Serilis
1. Matve has asked that I collect chapters from the Old Paineel classic "The Wartime Use of Arcana". These are located in the Caertaxian section.
   1. I should search for a chapter near the Old Paineel Overlook. Chapter on Sorcery. {{waypoint 24, 103, -83}}
   1. I should search for a chapter near the Blood-stained Mess Hall. Chapter on Enchanting. {{waypoint 58, 37, -35}}
   1. I should search for a chapter near the Shattered Pillar. Chapter on Summoning.  {{waypoint -69.13, 2.86, 51.01}}
   1. I should search for a chapter on the other side of Old Paineel. Chapter on Necromancy. {{waypoint -220, -40, 52}}

## Rewards

1. [[Mark of Manaar]]
1. At least 55g 70s 61c
1. +2500 faction with **Guardians of the Underfoot**
