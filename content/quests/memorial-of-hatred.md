*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Find and learn more about the Memorial of Hatred. Read the inscription on the SE monolith {{waypoint -13.83, 0, 16.96}} in [[The First Gate]].
1. Kill zombies in Fallen Gate to collect 8 ghastly hearts. (Uncommon update)
1. Drop the offering into the urn at the foot of the Memorial of Hatred.

- [[Spectral Dragoon Earcuff]]
