## Steps

1. Repeatable quest - must be done twice
1. Destroy 9 eldritch spellstone staffs or gahter 9 squat spiketus for water
1. Obtain 14 Stonebrunt centipede meat  - click on rocks for a chance at centipede - note it is a group update but if grouped the centipedes do not seem to spawn
1. Return to Blortch

## Rewards

- One of
  - Stonebrunt Rock (House item)
  - Squat Spiketus (House item)
