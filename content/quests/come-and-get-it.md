## Steps

1. Create the bait (5)
   - Scribe the [[Recipe: Grub Bait]]
   - Craft 5 [[Grub Bait]] at the brewing pot located to his right at the base of the tree {{waypoint 737, -161, 235}} with the following material and fuel:
     - Large Grub harvested from Porous Rock
     - 5 [[Hanging root]] (not every type of node is on every island, you will have to search other islands for them sometimes)
     - 5 [[Scintillating kindling]] (There is a fuel merchant on the platform in the tree).
     - Note: if you're already a 125 level crafter with Blueprint ability - after you make the first one, you can use the Blueprint feature to insta-complete the remaining four.
1. Place the bait in the traps (5)
   - {{waypoint 603, -153, 247}}
   - {{waypoint 588, -155, 215}}
   - {{waypoint 671, -164, 275}}
   - {{waypoint 667, -164, 260}}
   - {{waypoint 624, -159, 203}}
1. Return to Captain Velgre
