## Description

The Far Seas Trading Company would like me to travel the islands and waters of the Tranquil Sea and dispatch any major threats to their operations in the area.

## Notes

- You must be at least level 98 to receive this quest.
- *Timed quest: Shattered Seas Weekly.*
