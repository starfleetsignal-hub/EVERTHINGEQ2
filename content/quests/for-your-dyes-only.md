     - {{waypoint 114, 39, -232}}
     - {{waypoint 118, 39, -262}}
     - {{waypoint 118, 40, -278}}
     - {{waypoint 157, 54, -334}}
     - {{waypoint 190, 57, -350}}
     - {{waypoint 226, 55, -329}}
     - {{waypoint 145, 43, -231}}
     - {{waypoint 119, 38, -211}}
     - {{waypoint 86, 34, -196}}
     - {{waypoint 54, 29, -216}}
   - Gather 5 **myrobalan fruit** (small round fruit on ground) from [[Bukatan Savanna]]. Some possible locations:![Myrobalan Fruit](images/Updatedmyrobalanfruit.png)
     - {{waypoint -370, 44, -298}}
     - {{waypoint -416, 49, -330}}
     - {{waypoint -422, 49, -334}}
     - {{waypoint -385, 49, -363}}
     - {{waypoint -312, 56, -441}}
1. Return to [[Shabari]]
