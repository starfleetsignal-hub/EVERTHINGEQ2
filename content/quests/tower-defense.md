## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 60+

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Lachlyn Cauldthorn (Qeynos Capitol District)|Lachlyn Cauldthorn]] outside of the [[Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 662, -21, 141}}
1. Speak with [[Horys Dn'yer]] at the doors of the Tower to begin your investigation of [[The Explosion]]. {{waypoint 706, -20, 131}}
1. Volunteer to enter the Tower and help get things under control.
   - Defeat 8 [[A ghastly knight (The Explosion)|ghastly knights]] and [[A ghastly specter (The Explosion)|ghastly specters]] inside the Tower.
   - Rescue 3 [[A frightened Concordium student|frightened Concordium Students]].
     - Blue Portal {{waypoint 715, 33, 147}}
     - Red Portal {{waypoint 708, 48, 90}}
     - Red Portal {{waypoint 712, 41, 102}}
   - Speak with [[Bellengere the Three (Explosion)|Bellengere the Three]] (yellow portal). {{waypoint 701, 87, 130}}
1. Report back to [[Lachlyn Cauldthorn (Qeynos Capitol District)|Lachlyn Cauldthorn]] outside the tower
1. Return to [[Queen Antonia Bayle (Queen Antonia's Chambers)|Queen Antonia]] at the Palace.

## Rewards

- At least 25g 92s 66c (at level 61)
- At least 1p 20g (at level 89 upwards)
