*Must be a Barbarian to receive this quest.*

## Steps

1. Find [[Fluwkowir Haggleton]]:
   - Go to the docks and Fluwkowir will spawn about midway down the ramp. Loc (1,027, 0, -376) in Oakmyst Forest.
   - Hail him. He attacks you. You know what to do.
1. You receive ***Fluwkowir's Satchel*** with a note written in [[Lucanic]] and the money Jorn was looking for.
1. Talk to Jorn.
1. Talk to [[Lieutenant Charlin]].

- 98c
