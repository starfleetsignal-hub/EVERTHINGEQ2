## Steps

![Tin Of Bait](images/Tin_of_Bait.jpg)

1. Harvest a ***tin of bait*** at {{waypoint -2041, 1, 175}}.
   - Be warned that the tin is on a box in the gnoll camp (very small and hard to spot at first glance).
1. Return to [[Blarton Blumble|Blarton]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 59c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Darkpriest's Totem of Revivification]] (All Priests)
  - [[Plentiful Totem of the Ambush]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Skeleton Arm Wand]] (All Mages)
  - [[Viper's Poisonous Pouch]] (All Fighters)
