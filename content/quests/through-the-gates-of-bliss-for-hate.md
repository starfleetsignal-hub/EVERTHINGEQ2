## Starting the Quest

This quest has multiple possible starting locations for any alignment.

- [[Landax Z'Torzin]] at {{waypoint -1243, -86, 30}} in [[The Commonlands]] near the gate to [[West Freeport]]
- [[Sabstin V'Despth]] at {{waypoint -328.55, 28.08, 169.60}} in [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
- [[A delighted human]] on the docks {{waypoint 2365.41, 6.79, 1391.67}} in [[Gorowyn]]

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Priestess Annalisa Swornlove (Ant)|Priestess Annalisa Swornlove]] in next to the [[Druid Ring (Ant)|Druid Ring]] in [[Antonica]] at -2,235 -23 -705![](images/Valorian_bloom.jpg)
1. Harvest a virtuous component. Look in [[The Commonlands]], south of the Freeport Station griffin tower, where the Avatar of Valor has been seen.
   - Harvest a valorian bloom, found around {{waypoint -591, -48, -629}}
1. Return to [[Priestess Annalisa Swornlove (Ant)|Priestess Annalisa Swornlove]] and give her the valorian bloom.
1. Speak to [[Priestess Annalisa Swornlove (Ant)|Priestess Annalisa Swornlove]] again now that the arrow has been blessed.
1. Find a place in Greater Faydark that had meaning for Lady Shea.
   - Around the base of Tunare's Sapling at {{waypoint -95, 0, 4}}, find an ethereal arrow the size of a spear and click on it.![](images/Etheral_arrow.jpg)
   - [[A Disciple of Love|a Disciple of Love]] will spawn and attack you.
1. Find the weakest point between the Shard of Love and Norrath
   - In [[Everfrost]] on the north end of the dock at {{waypoint -54, -9, 63}} there is a scatter of rubble. Touch any of the rubble.
1. Return to [[Priestess Annalisa Swornlove (Ant)|Priestess Annalisa Swornlove]] in [[Antonica]] and show her the blessed arrow.
1. Return to [[Everfrost]] and meet [[Priestess Annalisa Swornlove (EF)|Priestess Annalisa Swornlove]] on the north end of the dock at {{waypoint -62, -8, 67}}.
   - They complete the ritual, and open the portal. You may now return at any time and the portal will be here.
   - Note: You must be level 50 to enter the [[Shard of Love: A Moment of Valor|instance]].
1. Speak to any of the three Disciples of Hate.
