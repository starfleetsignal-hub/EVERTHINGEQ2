## Steps

1. Speak to [[Cookie (Ratonga)|Cookie]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]] at {{waypoint 25, -20, 74}} a Ratonga in the room south of the main entry room.
1. Wait about a minute (~15 sec real time) and speak to [[Cookie]] again.
   - If you wait too long, Cookie will throw your order out and you have to repeat steps 1 and 2.
1. Speak with [[Recipes for Adventuring]] at {{waypoint 280, 15, 29}} which is a floating book on the 2nd floor.

## Rewards

- [[Recipe Scroll: Divine Weapons of Rathe]]
- At least 14p 21g 53s 73c 48000 status
