## Steps

1. Repair headstones and put to rest any spirits that rise from them in the Kejek Village.
   - Repair 8 headstones at the top of the hill before the ramp to the village. {{waypoint -1135.39, 513.50, 445.30}}. One is hidden at the base of the tree at {{waypoint -1103.32, 513.63, 427.19}}. Watch for level 87-89 [[A grave nightmare|grave nightmares]] to spawn and attack. Spawns may be rapid so take care going through the area.
1. Return to [[Lyre Shiro|Lyre]].

- At least 45g 70s 61c
