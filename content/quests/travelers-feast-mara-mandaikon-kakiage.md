## Notes

- None of the harvestable items are trackable.
- All of these clicks are **zone-wide group** updates. If you are grouped anywhere in the zone, your group members will receive updates.

## Steps

1. Travel to then zone [[Isle of Mara]]
   - Gather 4 Mandaikon at the rice fields of [[The Village of Shin]]![Mandaikon](images/Mandaikon.jpg)
     - {{waypoint -89.44, -1.55, -60.70}}
     - {{waypoint -41.34, 17.39, -149.27}}
     - {{waypoint -65.86, 17.39, -163.92}}
     - {{waypoint -82.39, 17.39, -174.06}}
     - {{waypoint -95.57, 17.39, -190.32}}
   - Gather 2 Luk Ki Shrimp from traps on the island [[Luk Ki Island]]![Luk Ki Shrimp Trap](images/Luk_Ki_Shrimp_Trap.jpg)
     - {{waypoint 159.31, -2.86, 19.85}}
     - {{waypoint 176.41, -2.49, -16.75}}
     - {{waypoint 196.00, -2.76, -14.31}}
   - Gather 5 Burdock Root![Burdock Root](images/Burdock_Root.jpg)
     - {{waypoint -221.41, -1.98, -82.42}}
     - {{waypoint -156, 0, -7}}
     - {{waypoint -162.38, -0.80, -45.25}}
     - {{waypoint -192.94, 2.07, -41.97}}
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]].
