## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A Danak regimental|Danak regimentals]] southwest of [[Ganak's Battlement]]. {{waypoint 127, -26, -51}}
   - Each kill reduces your faction with the Legion of Danak by 100 points.
1. Return to Warlord Sel'Nok.

- At least 32g 50s
- +1000 faction with **The Sel'Nok Brigade**
