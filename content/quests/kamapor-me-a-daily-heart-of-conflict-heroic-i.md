## Steps

Defeat all encounters in  [[Karuupa Jungle: Heart of Conflict (Heroic I)]] :

1. Chop up some Vines.
1. Defeat the Deino Duo.
1. Ixnay the Invader
1. Ambush the ape.
1. Slaughter the shaman.
