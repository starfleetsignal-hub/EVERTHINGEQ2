I discovered a large pile of bones that appear to be those lost to the shadow eaters. Their teeth leave a very distinctive mark which every bone appears to be covered in. These bones look as if they could have been from an entire family. I will avenge them in these shadowed depths.

## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*

1. Kill 15 [[A chasm shadow eater|chasm shadow eaters]] in [[Chasm Fiend Cliffs]].
