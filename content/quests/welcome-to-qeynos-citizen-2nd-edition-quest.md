## Steps

1. As soon as you accept the quest it will auto-complete.

### Former Steps

When this quest was still the reward for the [[First Time Buyer (Qeynos)]], the following steps were necessary:

1. Talk to [[Mervos Stadrin]] in [[North Qeynos]]. {{waypoint 368, -16, -63}}
1. Visit the [[The Claymore Plaza]] in North Qeynos. {{waypoint 311, -19, -20}}
1. Talk to Sage [[Indis Surion]] in the [[South Qeynos]] Tower. {{waypoint 702, 82, 129}}
1. Speak with [[Taskmaster Lynette]] in the lighthouse at [[Coldwind Point]] in [[Antonica]] (south-west tower near docks). {{waypoint 346, 2, 818}}
1. Speak with [[Hwal Rucksif]] in the  [[The Keep of the Ardent Needle (POI)|The Keep of the Ardent Needle]] in Antonica. {{waypoint -560, -13, -316}}
1. Speak with [[Knight-Lieutenant Alesso]] in Antonica, near the North Qeynos gates. {{waypoint 181, -21, -14}}
1. Finally, visit the statue of the Queen in the courtyard at [[The Ironforge Estate]]. {{waypoint 450, -20, -309}}

## Rewards

- Increased knowledge of the beautiful and eternal city of [[Qeynos]].
