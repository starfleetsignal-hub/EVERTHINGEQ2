## Steps

1. Speak to [[Nikora]]. She is found behind a rock at the top of the ramp to the Blackshield dock {{waypoint -380, -51, -860}}.
1. Slay 20 [[A Blackshield assassin (Commonlands)|blackshield assassins]] (level 12) southeast of Nikora. {{waypoint -376, -51, -863}}  There is also a group of them near {{waypoint -489, -48, -756}}.
1. Speak to [[Nethet]].
1. Speak to [[Jereth Blackshield]]. {{waypoint -391, -60, -879}}
1. Slay the three [[A Blackshield thug|blackshield thugs]] that spawn and attack you. (level 12)
1. Return to [[Nethet]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 29s
- One of the following based on class:
  - [[Antonican Defender's Belt]]
  - [[Antonican Healer's Belt]]
  - [[Antonican Medium's Belt]]
  - [[Antonican Stalker's Belt]] (Scouts)
