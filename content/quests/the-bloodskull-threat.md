*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Travel to the [[Hidden Vale]] in [[The Commonlands]]. {{waypoint -290, -24, 758}}
   - Kill 6 [[A Bloodskull shaman|Bloodskull shamans]].
   - Kill [[Vurog Ma'gar]].
   - Kill [[Jyrok the Stone]].
   - Collect the scouting report from [[Arnisu Tobian]].
1. Return to Tecera.

## Notes

- The jewels you loot from the Vurog and Jyrok either start or add to the quest [[An Orcish Trinket]].

## Rewards

- At least 10s 18c
- +100 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
