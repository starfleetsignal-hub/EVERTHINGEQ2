## Steps

1. Read [[Salutations of Naar'Yora]]. {{waypoint -58, 0, 161}}
1. Slay [[Blacktalon palace guard|Blacktalon palace guards]] 5 stolen scythes (common update).
1. Lay down an offering of stolen scythes before the two statues in the [[Spiroc Foyer]]: [[Va're'nok the Swift]] and [[Andan the Stalwart]].
   - You may wish to clear the mobs all the way down the hallway before doing this, to aid in completing the next step.
1. Within 10 minutes, kill the ghosts of the four winds that spawn one after another starting in [[The Walk of Flights]]:
   - [[Hero of the North Wind]]: {{waypoint -87.5, -6.5, 76}}
   - [[Hero of the East Wind]]: {{waypoint -87.5, -6.5, 52}}
   - [[Hero of the South Wind]]: {{waypoint -87.5, -0.5, 1}}
   - [[Hero of the West Wind]]: {{waypoint -60.5, -0.5, 0}}
1. Place each spear in the appropriate weapon rack in the [[The Vestibule of the Four Winds]]:
   - Spear of the East Wind {{waypoint -56.5, 0, -12}}
   - Spear of the West Wind {{waypoint -63.5, 0, -12}}
   - Spear of the North Wind {{waypoint -63.5, 0, 12}}
   - Spear of the South Wind {{waypoint -56, 0, 12}}
1. Examine the ghostly chest, [[Trove of the Four Winds]], back in the Spiroc Foyer. {{waypoint -139.5, 0, 130.5}}

## Reward

- At least: 27g 50s
- Your choice of:
  - [[Idol of the Four Winds]]
  - [[Shield of the Four Winds]]
  - [[Symbol of the Four Winds]]
  - [[Katar of the Four Winds]]
  - [[Sword of the Four Winds]]
