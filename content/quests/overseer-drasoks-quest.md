## Steps

1. Kill [[Overseer Dra'soks (Named)|Overseer Dra'soks]]. He roams the cliffside path to [[Bathezid's Watch]], starting from around {{waypoint 545, 16, 1400}} and going to around {{waypoint 1259, 207, 1220}}.
1. Speak with [[Lieutenant Valssed]] in [[Riliss (POI)|Riliss]]. {{waypoint -884, 45, -258}}

  - [[Crest of Karnor's Chosen]]

  - [[Signet of Karnor's Chosen]]
