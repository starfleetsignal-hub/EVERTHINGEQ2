This is not a normal collection quest. The collection members are not obtained by harvesting shinies.<br>
Instead, the thirteen collection members are themselves all obtained from the completion of other collection quests. This is called a "meta-collection".
Awards: [[Dhalgar's Last Call]]. Upon **Banners of Thalumbra** Quest turn in and accepting the [[Bottomless Dhalgar Stein]] it will grant you the Dhalgar's Last Call ability which is a Recall just like a city or guild recall excepting it takes you to a Bar that you choose to set your recall to. Visit a Bartender and select the 2nd choice "Actually, I'd like to talk to you about returning to this bar' option.  Default location is [[Aidan]] in [[Maldura]], Other known locations are the Bartenders: [[Zleerty]] in [[Karuupa Jungle]] and [[Mohsen]] in [[Takish Badlands]], there are many others as referenced [[Stein of Recollection (Crate Reward)|here.]]
