Several chapters of Volume II of The Planar Chronicles detail what to expect from some of the elemental planes. I must have my spells readied for what's to come.

## Steps

- Complete this chapter on [Solo] missions.:
  - Banish Aurorax from [[Eryslai: The Bixel Hive (Solo)]]
  - Defeat the Council of Four in [[Vegarlson: Council's Stronghold (Solo)]]
  - Slay the Summoned Reprisal in [[Doomfire: Vengeance of Ro (Solo)]]
  - Vanquish Aegaeon, the Violent in [[Awuidor: The Nebulous Deep (Solo)]]

## Rewards

- At least 5p 19g 75s 122,400 status
- 35 [[Archivist's Tremissis]]
