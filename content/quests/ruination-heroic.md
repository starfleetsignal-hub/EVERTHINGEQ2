## Steps

*A once vast, marvelous city, Cabilis now lies in ruins. Within its cracked and broken walls, beings of dark magic wait for those foolish enough to explore its depths. But as I am no fool, I will bring death upon these creatures on behalf of the Crusaders of Greenmist, who wish to explore the city of their people.*

- Defeat targets within [[The Ruins of Cabilis (Heroic)]]
1. Slay the sentinel
1. Slaughter the smithy
1. Unravel the war baron
1. Defeat the historian
1. Kill the Grand Master
