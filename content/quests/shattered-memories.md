## Description

It might be possible to find pieces of Miragul's memories, locked away within The Crucible. If I can find a way to collect those memories and return them for study, they could provide valuable insight into Miragul himself. I might have to locate keys to open the memories and release them, so I should search for those as well.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Crucible]]
1. Kill the npcs inside to get chest drops that contain Thought Keys, these will open the chests (you need 4).
1. Open the chests located at:
   - [[A Reflection of Exile]] (first floor menagerie) {{waypoint 69, 2, 5}}
   - [[A Reflection of Silence]] (first floor library) {{waypoint -105, 0, 22}}
   - [[A Reflection of Arrogance]] (second floor, Master's Room) {{waypoint -108, 16, 137}}
   - [[A Reflection of Mastery]] (third floor, Codexicon's room) {{waypoint -33, 50, 188}}
1. Return to Lakewind in Everfrost

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
