## Steps

1. Stop the advancing forces:
   - Kill 4 Bloodscale soldiers in the Covenant District {{waypoint 0, -1, -88}}
   - Kill 4 Doomscale soldiers in the Lyceum of the Recondite {{waypoint 85, -1, 44}}
   - Kill 4 Dreadscale soldiers in Dracur Prime {{waypoint -58, 0, 34}}
1. Speak to [[Hazrem]].
1. Collect 8 emblems from fallen dracurions in the Covenant District.
   - Known locations:
     - {{waypoint -75, -1, -104}}
     - {{waypoint -164, -1, -170}}
     - {{waypoint -210, -1, -210}}
     - {{waypoint -117, -1, -302}}
     - {{waypoint -83, -1, -231}}
     - {{waypoint -88, -1, -186}}
     - {{waypoint -78, -1, -104}}
     - {{waypoint -165, -1, -172}}
1. Return to [[Hazrem]] to complete the quest.
