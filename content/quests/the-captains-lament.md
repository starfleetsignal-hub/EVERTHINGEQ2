## Notes

- Your tradeskill *must* be at least level 93 (which is when you receive the mail to start this quest).
- Obtain 1 [[Effulgent Coal]] before you head to [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]] or visit the merchant [[Sata]] in the tent to the left of him to purchase your fuel.
- Use the [[Travel in Norrath|Ulteran Spires]] near each location for quick porting between locations for every step that requires travel between zones.
- Though it is not a quest reward, the Mara Spirit Anchor that appears in Cardin Ward in the second to last step will remain in that location, making travel to and from Mara and Obol Plains for crafting, harvesting, and adventure easy.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Keevan Fastmarch]] at  {{waypoint -125, 13, 178}} in the Hidden Camp in Feerrott.
1. Speak to [[Ghostclaw]] at {{waypoint 129, -7, -87}} on the beach in Feerrott.
1. Speak to [[Researcher Tahar]] at  {{waypoint -200, 366, 588}} in [[Quel'ule]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]]. If you are using fast travel, then you will want to choose the location closest to the center of the map.
   - Unpack the [[Bundle of Ulteran Spire Technology]] by examining it, and scribe the [[Experimental spire device plan]] recipe to craft an [[Unattuned spire device]] at a [[Work Bench]] using the remaing materials provided:
     - [[Experimental spire device plan]]
     - [[Teleport Core]]
     - [[Stabilization Field Dampener]]
     - [[Spire Resonator]]
   - There is a [[Work Bench]] at {{waypoint -133, 366, 615}}.
1. Speak to [[Ghostclaw]] in Feerrott again
   - Ghostclaw will give you [[Armlet of Ethernere]], which gives the user a superficial appearance of belonging in the Ethernere. It is not required that you use the illusion to continue the quest, but it also has some good crafting stats, so you'll want to hang onto it.
1. Use the crafted device on a departing spirit in the [[Somborn Cemetery|Somborn Cemetary]] in [[Loping Plains]] around {{waypoint -429, 16, 70}}
   - Make sure you don't have ranged auto selected or wand or you will kill the spirit.
   - Once you have attuned the device, you will be ported to [[Cardin Ward]] in [[Obol Plains]].
1. Tether the spirit anchor at the Ethernere Crystal just outside Cardin Ward, by clicking the glowing green spot on the ground {{waypoint -516, 0, -62}}.
1. Return to [[Captain Ethan]] in the Village of Shin using the Isle of Mara Reality Anchor in the room with the fuel merchant in Obol Plains at {{waypoint -410, 15, 56}}.
