## Steps

1. Enter [[Beggar's Court: Sweat-Stained Cobbles]] - Click on the Gate in Northern Freeport to Beggar's Court {{waypoint -149, -51, -376}}
1. Visit 4 buildings. Enter each one and wait a few seconds for the quest to update
   - western: {{waypoint 59, -6, 63}}
   - southwestern: {{waypoint 31, 0, 69}}
   - southeastern: {{waypoint -32, -6, 68}}
   - eastern: {{waypoint -28, 0, 44}}
1. Speak to any guard on the upper tier {{waypoint 1, 2, 11}}
1. Speak to [[Snars]] {{waypoint 60, 5, -25}} the Taskmaster. He attacks you (level -1, even con); kill him
1. On a **one minute timer**, hide Snars' body in the cubby in the building next to you {{waypoint 80, 5, -39}}. Move the body by double-clicking it (**Place in alcove just to the right inside the door, his body will turn green if a good spot**). If you do not move him in time, you have to re-enter the zone and repeat the kill & hide the body steps. Also note that even if the body turns green, it will generally be the wrong spot. You will have to redo this step several times until rng favors you.
1. Return to Kain in East Freeport
