## Notes

- *This quest in unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*
- This quest only becomes available once you have reached 30,000 faction with the Ashen Disciples.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Jiao Li Rong]]. {{waypoint 879, -113, -835}}
1. Kill the [[A desert spirit|desert spirit]] that spawns.
1. Return to [[Shing Ho]].
