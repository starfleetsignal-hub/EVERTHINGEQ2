## Steps

1. Defeat the hostile boss creatures within the Sanctus Seru: The Fading Arches [Raid]
   1. Locate and defeat Luminary Percontorius Felvin
   1. Locate and defeat Luminary Cohortis Emon
   1. Locate and defeat Luminary Hertu Asundri
1. Locate and defeat Lord Triskian Seru and take his sword as proof! (loot Lord Triskian's Sword of Truth when you defeat him)
