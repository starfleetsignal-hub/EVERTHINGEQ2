## Steps

1. Enter [[Flooded Scar: Abyssal Attack (Signature)|Flooded Scar: Abyssal Attack [Signature]]] {{waypoint 320, 8, 99}}
1. A behemoth of indescribable terror rose from the depths below Western Wastes, unleashing terror within the Flooded Scar!
   - Survive the parasitic creatures and arcane affects of the Abyssal Attack.
     - Kill the [[A Feeler]] at {{waypoint 90, 7, 28}}, {{waypoint 28, 6, 84}}, and {{waypoint -63, 6, 30}}
   - Send the unspeakable behemoth back under the waves.
     - Kill the Crabominable Arms (kill the adds before the tentacles do) {{waypoint -154, 8, 28}}
     - *Note: The above strategy did not work for me. Instead, I concentrated on killing the 3 Crabominable Arms and *ignored* the adds, letting my merc and AOE damage them (the adds) while I fought.*
1. Return to [[Adris Spiritshine]] in [[The Remnant Shores]] at {{waypoint 66, 8, 640}}
   - Zone out of the instance will be at {{waypoint -132, 10, 71}}
