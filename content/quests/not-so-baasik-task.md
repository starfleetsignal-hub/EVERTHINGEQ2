## Steps

1. Mask yourself with pungent carrion flower oil
   - Collect oil around {{waypoint -50.89, 129.32, 135.98}} or {{waypoint -81, 126, 216}} from a flower with black sparkles
1. Hunt 6 Mandoko boneshamans in the same area.
1. Collect items from the Mandoko burial site around {{waypoint -211, 123, 82}}
1. defeat the attacking [[A Mandoko boneshaman]]
   - *Note: You'll recieve [[Soiled Scroll]] after you kill him*
1. Collect items from the Mandoko burial site around {{waypoint -211, 123, 82}}
1. return to [[Baasik Vehn'imiss]] at {{waypoint 43, 132, 101}}

## Reward

- [[Mandoko Talisman]]
- 4p 4g 15s 72c
