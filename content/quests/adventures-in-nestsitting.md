## Steps

1. Return the missing fledglings on [[Preening Island]].
   - *Note These are little fledgling birds roaming the island. Click on them and they will return to the nest. (UPDATE: They do not spawn in order. Try each waypoint until you find the first one, then continue with the the waypoint list until you find the 2nd one. You cannot see them until you have gotten the previous one. I.E., you may locate the first fledgling with the third waypoint. Then just start at the top waypoint and run through them again. Same for the last Fledgling.)*
   - *If you find that you cannot locate all of the fledglings, leave the island and come back and free the last one.*
   - {{waypoint -476, 214, 1137}}  ![](images/Adventures_in_Nestsitting_quest_-_a_wandering_fledgling.jpg)
   - {{waypoint -325, 189, 1060}}
   - {{waypoint -523, 227, 1071}}
   - {{waypoint -399, 209, 1061}}
   - {{waypoint -415, 208, 1209}}
   - {{waypoint -536, 226, 1074}} This final location is where one of the fledglings is caught in a trap that you must attack to Free.
1. Return to [[Kudurri]]
