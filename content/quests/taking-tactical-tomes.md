## Notes

- If you're working on [[The Keys to Trust]] in the [[Claymore Timeline]], Gribbly might not respond.

## Steps

1. Go to the [[Talonflight Armory]] and click on the large tome at {{waypoint -50, 129, 199}}.
1. Retrieve 8 different tomes placed about the dungeon:
   - Volume I - {{waypoint -69, 128, 190}} or {{waypoint -64, 130, 151}} in the Talonflight Armory room or {{waypoint 6, 142, 45}}, {{waypoint 42, 136, 1}} or {{waypoint 15, 142, 24}} in the Oratorium
   - Volume II - {{waypoint -39, 128, 190}}, {{waypoint -44, 129, 151}} or {{waypoint -72, 128, 154}} in the Talonflight Armory room
   - Volume III - {{waypoint -9, 136, -13}} or {{waypoint 1, 139, 16}} in the Oratorium
   - Volume IV - {{waypoint 5, 136, 54}} or {{waypoint 1, 135, 97}} in the Oratorium
   - Volume V - {{waypoint 46, 135, 77}} or {{waypoint 42, 136, 25}} in the Oratorium
   - Volume VI - {{waypoint 139, 136, 81}} or {{waypoint 196, 136, 39}}in the Scaleborn Barracks room
   - Volume VII - {{waypoint 159, 159, -102}} or {{waypoint 198, 158, -76}} in the Ready Room
   - Volume VIII - {{waypoint -147, 133, 60}} or {{waypoint -125, 137, 79}} or smaller table in opposite corner {{waypoint -161, 137, 34}} in the Chamber of Animation room
1. Return to Gribbly for your reward.

## Rewards

- Choice of one of the following rewards:
  - [[Scaled Earmuffs]]
  - [[Frames of Focus]]
  - [[Skewer of the Scaleborn]]
