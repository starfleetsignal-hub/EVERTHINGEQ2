## Starting the Quest

- Various zones will have groups of 'shadowy' monsters. The monsters initially appear as NPCs, but will become aggro and attack when players get close.  They scale to one level below the player's level v,  and attack in a group of three.
- On defeating them, they will drop a treasure chest with [[A strangely marked hide]] inside.  Examine it to start the quest.
- These creatures are not visible on Time Locked Servers until after [[Destiny of Velious]] is activated there.  [https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/update-notes-thursday-august-17-2017.580331/](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/update-notes-thursday-august-17-2017.580331/)

## Steps

1. Find [[Grim Stormshield]] {{waypoint -1413, 234, 97}} northeast of the Druid Ring in Jarsath Wastes.
   - There is a firebranded horse on Jarsath Wastes docks, next to the bell. You can use it to ride directly to them, which is useful if you're lower level.
1. Listen to his conversation with [[Daleen Blackwood]]
