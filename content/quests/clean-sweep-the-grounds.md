## Steps

1. Perform the following tasks:![Yrzu Animating Rune](images/Yrzu_Animating_Rune.jpg)![Yrzu elemental(non-agro)](images/Yrzu_elemental%28non-agro%29.jpg)
   - Destroy 7  Yrzu Animating Runes (they look like bronze house trophies) near {{waypoint -358, 10, 1130}} and {{waypoint -188, 12, 999}}.
   - Kill 7 [[Yrzu elemental]] near {{waypoint -358, 10, 1130}} and {{waypoint -188, 12, 999}}.
1. Return to [[Mindfold Matriarch Taneesha]] {{waypoint -300, 581, -663}}.
