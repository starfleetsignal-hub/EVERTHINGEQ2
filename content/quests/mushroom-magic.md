## Steps

1. Go to [[Outer Sebilis]] and talk to [[Loremaster Hax]]. {{waypoint -364, -111, 1048}}
1. Return to [[Doom's Morass]] and kill the [[A sporconid ritualist|sporconid ritualists]] conducting a ritual on [[Marrax]]. Click on one of the ritualists to make them aggro. {{waypoint 168, -120, 1080}}
1. Click on one of the mushrooms surrounding Marrax. If you are in a group you may need to wait approximately 5 minutes for the clickable mushroom to respawn.
1. Return to Fisher Rissen.
