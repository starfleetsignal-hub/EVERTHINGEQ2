## Notes

Grab the letter from the mailbox, then read it before trying to Speak with [[Captain Ghalib Elbaz]] or the quest will not start.
Since the update of Tuesday March 7, 2017, the Letter sometimes is no longer sent via mail. But Jerol in Village of Shin now offers "a Letter of Summons" to those who have completed "Lack of Documentation."

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Captain Ghalib Elbaz]] in [[The City of Paineel]] near {{waypoint 1944, -299, 3386}}.
1. Speak to [[Saihah Al'Lad]] in [[The Eye of Dartain]] (Observatory) at {{waypoint 1771, 0, 4320}}.

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +1000 faction with **City of Paineel**
