## Steps

1. Visit the following locations in Zek:
   - Tower Ruins: east of fort, behind the orc stockade {{waypoint 312, -39, 278}}
   - East Catapult: northeast of fort {{waypoint 440, -20, 140}}
   - West Catapult: northwest of fort {{waypoint 590, -30, 140}}
   - Wreck of Q.S.S. Ebon Strongbear: in water southeast of dock {{waypoint 470, -70, 500}}
     - Note: To avoid most of the angry kelp plants and bottom feeders it is a good idea to head to {{waypoint 359, -44, 431}} before going down to the shipwreck.
   - Grove of Stones: druid ring up the northeast road {{waypoint 207, 14, 111}}
1. Speak to Hammer
