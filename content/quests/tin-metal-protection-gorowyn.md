## Steps

1. [[Frethe Zizlop (Gorowyn)|Frethe Zizlop]] in the housing area crafting of [[Gorowyn]]. {{waypoint 2823, 121, 1235}}
   - This will start the subquest [[Card Deals (Gorowyn)|Card Deals]].
1. [[Zoe Hertzflip (Gorowyn)|Zoe Hertzflip]] in The Good Athenaeum {{waypoint 2666, 68, 1354}}.
   - This will start the subquest [[Ogled Goggles (Gorowyn)|Ogled Goggles]].
1. [[Barb Islenet (Gorowyn)|Barb Islenet]] in a white tent on a platform in Timerous Maw. {{waypoint 2482, 34, 1348}}
   - This will start the subquest [[Wine for the Lady (Gorowyn)|Wine for the Lady]].
1. [[Abner Siblet (Gorowyn)|Abner Siblet]] in The Dragons Anchor. {{waypoint 2591, 87, 1306}}
   - This will start the subquest [[Fribles's Fate (Gorowyn)|Fribles's Fate]].
1. Go to {{waypoint 2941, 22, 1003}} left of Berisen along the rocky coast line. [[Belazzor Fribles, the Inept (Timorous Deep)|Belazzor Fribles, the Inept]] will spawn and attack you; his level scales to yours.
   - Kill [[Belazzor Fribles, the Inept (Timorous Deep)|Fribles]].
1. Go back to [[Abner Siblet (Gorowyn)|Abner Siblet]] in [[Gorowyn]].
1. Return to the remaining NPCs in reverse order to complete each of the subquests. You will get Exp/AA exp for all 4 of them.
1. Return to [[Blat Berisen (Timorous Deep)|Blat Berisen]] in Gorowyn for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Zoe's Wild Deck of Playing Cards]]
- [[Tin Metal Helm of Protection]]
