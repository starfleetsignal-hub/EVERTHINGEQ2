## Description

*Seek out the being that still leaches the power of the Fyr'Un for its own purposes. Only after it has been slain may the Vessel regain its true power.*

## Steps

1. Enter the [[The Fountain of Life|Fountain of Life]] in the [[The Silent City|Silent City]] at {{waypoint 897, -1, 251}}.
1. Slay [[Godking Anuk]].
1. Click on the central green pool to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- [[Overflowing Vessel of Fyr'Un]]
- 43400 status at level 100
