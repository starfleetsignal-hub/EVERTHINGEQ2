## Steps

1. Gather 12 fesmiarium scraps.  *Note: They are tiny bone-like scraps that blend easily into the sand. They will re-pop in the same spots, and in spots that had none before. They get easier to recognize after a couple.*![Scraps](images/Scraps.jpg)
   - Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint 298.00, -8.35, 260.36}}
   - {{waypoint 265.45, -15.52, 266.05}}
   - {{waypoint 257.05, -11.17, 230.5}}
   - {{waypoint 324.47, -8.42, 220.12}}
   - {{waypoint 333.54, -5.88, 250.12}}
   - {{waypoint 339.00, -4.15, 249.96}}
   - {{waypoint 339.18, -8.36, 259.7}}
   - {{waypoint 255.27, -10.92, 230.69}}
   - {{waypoint 323.83, -8.47, 220.45}}
1. Kill [[A bloodtribe shinespotter]] that attacks.
1. Return to [[Tegin The Curator]] at {{waypoint 546.95, 34.56, 134.20}}
