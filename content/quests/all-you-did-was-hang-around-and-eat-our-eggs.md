## Steps

1. Walk up the ramp {{waypoint 481, 364, -533}} and slay [[Warbird Jarkin]]'s Advisors
   - [[Windcaster Ortan]], {{waypoint 511, 409, -551}}, 32^ (First House upstairs)
   - [[Featherseer Tolmak]], {{waypoint 607, 412, -527}}, 32^ (Third House)
1. The Final step is to slay [[Warbird Jarkin]] himself, (Second House Upstairs). He can be found {{waypoint 571, 423, -546}}. Make sure you kill him last, or the quest may not update, forcing you to wait until he respawns.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 21s
- One of the following:
  - [[Band of the Warbird]]
  - [[Golden Band of the Windcaster]]
  - [[Hammerborn Silver Band]]
  - [[Polished Ring of the Farseer]]
