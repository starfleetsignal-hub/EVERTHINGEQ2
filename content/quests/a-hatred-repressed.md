## Steps

1. Speak with [[Tanath L'Vec (Loping Plains)|Tanath L'Vec]] in [[Loping Plains]]. {{waypoint -392, 9, -88}}
   - To read the complete text of all dialog with Tanath L'Vec during this quest, click [[A Hatred Repressed/Dialog|here]].
1. Collect the lost pages of the journal from [[The Timorous Moor]] in Loping Plains:
   - Page 6 (barrel): {{waypoint 24, -3, 332}}
   - Page 7 (harvestable page): {{waypoint 100, -4, 364}}
   - Page 8 (harvestable page): {{waypoint -146, -3, 477}}
1. Return to Tanath L'Vec.
1. Read [[A Passenger's Journal]] from your inventory.
1. Speak with Tanath L'Vec.
1. Find and loot the [[Essence Culler]] from a chest in a wagon near the [[Reconstructed Ulteran Spire]]. {{waypoint 150, 45, -330}}
1. Step into the center of the Reconstructed Ulteran Spire, then click on the wisp that spawns above the portal.
1. Use the Essence Culler on one of the Sareth'Tal ghosts, around {{waypoint 90, 75, 180}}. You will receive an [[Essence of Sareth'Tal]].
1. Use the Essence of Sareth'Tal from your inventory to release the spirit at {{waypoint 220, 90, 70}}.
1. Pick up the *Dark Beacon* that spawns on the ground where the spirit disappeared.
1. Return to Tanath L'Vec.
1. Return to [[Xilania Nevagon (Greater Faydark)|Xilania Nevagon]].
