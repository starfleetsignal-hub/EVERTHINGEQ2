## Steps

1. Quest giver is in "inner" part of Nye'Caelona, use the teleporter in the center of the tower.![Teleporter to Inner Nye'Caelona](images/Teleporter_to_Inner_Nye'Caelona.jpg)
1. Remove wings from 12 stunned frontier riverflies.
   - Use the [[Lightning Wand]] given to you by [[Merith Iliqirelle]] on the [[A frontier riverfly|frontier riverflies]] located around {{waypoint 318, -224, -88}}.
   - The wand can be used before or during combat. Kill the riverfly for the update.
1. Return to [[Merith Iliqirelle]] {{waypoint -239, 57, -236}}.

- [[Cae'Dal Alchemist Bookcase]]

- 15108 status
