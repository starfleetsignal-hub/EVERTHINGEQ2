## Steps

1. Collect the mark of each of the researchers you have helped within the city.
   - [[Alim Dabir]] {{waypoint 1981, -298, 3337}}
   - [[Captain Ghalib Elbaz|Ghalib Elbaz]]{{waypoint 1945, -299, 3386}}
   - [[High Commander Fawzi Zaim|Fawzi Zaim]] {{waypoint 1903, -299, 3448}}
   - [[Ishaq Al'Nair]] {{waypoint 1858, -294, 3473}}
   - [[Nudhir Il'qatai]] {{waypoint 1643.90, -267.06, 3175.77}}
   - [[Zafirah Aamira]] {{waypoint 1658, -267, 3175}}
1. Return to Saihah at {{waypoint 1872, -293, 3518}}
