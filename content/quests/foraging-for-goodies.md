## Steps

1. Forager Rilloke will ask you to kill/gather one of the following:
   - ![Shambler Stalk](images/Shambler_Stalk.jpg)Fallen **shambler stalks** from [[A vile trampler|vile tramplers]] {{waypoint 415, 145, 2710}}
     - Shambler stalks are a collectable node that spawns when you kill [[A vile trampler|vile tramplers]]. You can often harvest multiples from just one node.
     - [[A bristlevine abhorrence|bristlevine abhorrences]] **no longer** drop these.
   - **Toxic crawler eggs**  {{waypoint 1027, 52.68, 3379.64}}
     - Toxic crawler eggs are a collectable node that spawns when you kill [[A toxic crawler (The Sundered Frontier)|toxic crawlers]] by the Druid Rings {{waypoint 975.84, -52.91, 3418.83}}.  They are small white spheres that can be hard to see.
   - **Tox fly larvae** {{waypoint 597.59, 75.25, 3359}}
     - Kill [[A Tox fly|Tox flies]] that will drop the larvae when they die.
   - **Toxxulia flower buds**
1. Return to [[Forager Rilloke]] at {{waypoint 845, -51, 3386}}

## Reward

- At least 46g
- +50 faction with **Kerra Isle**
