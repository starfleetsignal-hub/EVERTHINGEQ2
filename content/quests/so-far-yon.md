## Notes

Horron is at the location where his party was attacked, you ask him to go find safety by Yon Gorroth Obelisk and agree to investigate the source of horrors within Writher Crevasse.

## Steps

![necromantic gem](images/So_Far_Yon_-_necromantic_gem.png)

1. Search for the source of the horrors lurking in Writher Crevasse - go down the ramp at {{waypoint -376, 32, 90}}, then find a necromantic gem.
1. Kill the following
   - destroy 8 necromantic gems you find - sparkling white long crystals embedded in walls of the crevasse
   - kill 13 rotforged horrors
1. return to the Void Obelisk campsite at {{waypoint -628, -1, 443}} to ensure Horron made it safely.
1. speak to Horron.

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] = 15 [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
