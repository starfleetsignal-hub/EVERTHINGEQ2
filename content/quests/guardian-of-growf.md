## Note

You must be able to speak Goblish and be at or near lvl 100 to get Growf to give you this quest together with the [[Scribbled notes on Go-Away Pest Spray|recipe]]. There are plenty of [[Goblin Territory Marker|goblin territory markers]] to complete this language right here in Twark.

## Steps

1. Gather 4 [[Acrid herb|acrid herbs]], trackable near  {{waypoint 236, -221, -407}}. ![acrid herb](images/Acrid_herb_visible.jpg)
   - Note: Turning off Flora will make these easier to see at a distance.
1. Craft [[Go-Away Pest Spray]] on the Goblin Firepit by [[Glibnox]] {{waypoint 134, -163, -770}}. Requires 5 [[Thaumic Coal]].
1. Return to [[Growf]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +4000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
