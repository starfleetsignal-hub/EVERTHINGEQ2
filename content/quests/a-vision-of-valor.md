## Starting the Quest

This is a lead-in quest to the founding of New Halas, and was first given out by GMs and/or [[Guides of the Shattered Lands|Guides]] on April 29, 2010.

- Starting on May 18th, 2010, NPCs giving this quest have been found in the following locations:
  - [[North Qeynos]], a priest of Marr in the [[Temple of Life]]
  - [[West Freeport]], {{waypoint 281, -4, 93}} [[A harassed barbarian]] outside the front door of [[The Blood Haze Inn]]
  - [[Kelethin]], [[Greater Faydark]], [[A priest of Marr]] {{waypoint 149, 111, 97}} next to [[Captain R.K. Irontoe]] and the Broker [[Almilla Sumac]].
  - [[Neriak]], [[A harassed barbarian]] close to entrance from [[Darklight Wood]] {{waypoint 44, 18, -35}}.
  - [[Timorous Deep]], on the docks, [[A tormented barbarian]].

## Steps

1. Head to the [[Everfrost]] dock and hail [[Sister Leela Prendyn]] next to the [[Shard of Love]] entrance.
1. Head to the [[Commonlands]] to check on the relics that were erected by the Knights of Marr for any signs of change.
   - Head for the statue Valor's End: The Mourned {{waypoint -594, -49, -631}} (at the end of [[Horse Fly Trail]], a proximity update).
   - Head to the ruined "Keep of Marrsfist" {{waypoint 622, -46, 701}} and check for any signs of change.
     - This is near the [[Hidden Canyon Station]]. Harvest **a valorian bloom** {{waypoint 617, -47, 707}} for an update.
1. Head back to the Sisters of Erollisi on the Everfrost dock.
   - Speak with [[Sister Leela Prendyn]] again.
   - You will receive [[Pomegranate seeds]] and a [[Flask of Blessed Water]].
1. Return to the ruined Keep of Marrsfist and plant the pomegranate seeds you received, at the base of the dead tree. {{waypoint 622, -46, 701}}
   - Right-click the pile of dirt at the base of the dead tree and choose to plant the seeds, then examine the Blessed Water to water the soil.
1. You will be transported to "A Vision of Valor" instance.
   - Speak with Mithaniel Marr, then leave the vision.
1. Return to the Sisters of Erollisi in [[Everfrost]] with the news of Mithaniel Marr's plans and speak with Sister Leela Prendyn one last time to finish the quest.
