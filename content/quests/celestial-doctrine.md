I must complete the tasks given to me by Vorai, the jann I met within Myrist, in an effort to expand my knowledge.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Vorai|Vorai (The Guide)]], who is wandering around the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]] to get her quest: Celestial Doctrine.
1. Complete the following Heroic (Weekly) quests from the "[[A Planar Chronicler Vol. 2]]", and simply complete the two Public Quests:
   - [[The Elemental Planes: Preparation (Heroic I)]] weekly mission
   - [[The Elemental Planes: No Turning Back (Heroic II)]] weekly mission
   - [[A Chapter on the Elemental Planes (Event Heroic)]] weekly mission
   - [[Doctor's Orders: Eryslai, Winds of Twilight]] Public Quest mission
   - [[Doctor's Orders: Awuidor, the Dark Fathoms]] Public Quest mission

## Rewards

- A Celestial Spell Scroll Crate for your class or for one of the 4 Ascenion classes
