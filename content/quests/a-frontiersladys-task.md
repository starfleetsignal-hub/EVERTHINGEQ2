## Steps

1. Speak to [[Sighard Sayer]] on the second floor of the [[Frontier Farm]] at {{waypoint -727, -16, 236}}.
1. Return to Dancer.
1. Place the arrow in a training dummy in [[Archer's Wood]] at {{waypoint -731, -3.5, 627}}.
1. Return to Dancer for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Band of Amber Sight]]  (All Scouts)
  - [[Crest of Amber Sight]] (All Priests)
  - [[Emblem of Amber Sight]]
  - [[Signet of Amber Sight]]
- At least 1s 59c
