## Steps

1. I should kill [[Taril V'lenial]], the leader of the Briarstone skeletons, in the hopes he has the satchel.
   - He spawns in front of the entrance to [[The Tower of Bone]] at {{waypoint 596, -24, 255}}.
1. Return to Thadus with the *aged dwarven satchel*.

- At least 2s 91c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Mark of Agility]] (Scouts)
  - [[Symbol of Intelligence]] (Mages)
  - [[Symbol of Strength]] (Fighters)
  - [[Symbol of Wisdom]] (Priests)
