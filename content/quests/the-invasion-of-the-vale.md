## Notes

- While the quest level is 15, the foes are 16 to 20^
- The giantslayers in the zone respawn, beware of this as you attempt to clear the magi

## Walkthrough

1. Enter the solo instance [[Invasion of the Vale]], a version of the Shattered Vale, located in [[Antonica]] at {{waypoint -2327, 7, -76}}
1. Slay the [[A Black Magi visionary|Black Magi visionaries]] (level 17) surrounding the druid ring
1. Slay the [[A Black Magi elder|Black Magi elders]] (level 19) standing within the druid ring
1. Slay the [[A Portal Abhorrent|Portal Abhorrent]] (level 20^) that spawns in the center of the druid ring.
1. Slay the [[A Portal Atrocity|Portal Atrocity]] (level 20^) that spawns in the center of the druid ring.
1. Slay the [[A Portal Abomination|Portal Abomination]] (level 20^) that spawns in the center of the druid ring.
