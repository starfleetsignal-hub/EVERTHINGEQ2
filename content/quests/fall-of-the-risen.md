## Description

The spirits of Castle Valmarr haunt their former outpost in hopes that someone will release them from the curse of Gynok Moltor. To this end, I've agreed to slay a number of the fallen inhabitants in hopes that they cease to attack those wishing to sanctify the forsaken keep of the Marrsfist Knights.

## Steps

1. Enter the [[Befallen: Cavern of the Afflicted]] and slay a number of its inhabitants
   - Slay 10 fallen vicars
   - Slay 10 fallen chevaliers
1. Return to the Squire.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
