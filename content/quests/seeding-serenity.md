## Steps

![Crystals from the underside of Veiled Thessk](images/Seeding_Serenity_Crystal.jpg)
![Place crystals where the ground sparkles](images/Sparkling_Ground.png)

1. Gather 8 crystals from the underside of [[Veiled Thessk]]. Hover near them and click to harvest. They are located on the outer edges of the island. Some possible locations:
   - {{waypoint -965, -87, 67}}
   - {{waypoint -962, -90, 78}}
   - {{waypoint -719, -55, 118}}
   - {{waypoint -711, -56, 116}}
   - {{waypoint -697, -67, 99}}
   - {{waypoint -794, -110, -272}}
   - {{waypoint -797, -98, -266}}
1. Kill 6 [[A shadowveiled soulblight|shadowveiled soulblights]] for their ethereal tendrils.
1. Return to Gortov
1. Place 6 serenity imbued crystals (on sparkling ground around the island). Some possible locations:
   - {{waypoint -370, 137, -469}}
   - {{waypoint -323, 141, -533}}
   - {{waypoint -342, 138, -526}}
   - {{waypoint -263, 147, -591}}
   - {{waypoint -381, 141, -581}}
   - {{waypoint -552, 181, -679}}
   - {{waypoint -472, 177, -634}}
   - {{waypoint -428, 142, -651}}
   - {{waypoint -378, 149, -664}}
   - {{waypoint -651, 141, -680}}
   - {{waypoint -620, 141, -591}}
   - {{waypoint -663, 139, -528}}
   - {{waypoint -538, 140, -436}}
1. Return to Gortov.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
