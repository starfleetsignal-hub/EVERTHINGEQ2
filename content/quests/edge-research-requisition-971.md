## Required Materials

*In addition to the materials harvested for the Requisition, you will need:*

- 1 [[Humming Sapphire Gem]] -- *rare*
- 25 [[Coruscating Incense]]

## Steps

1. Harvest Materials:
   - 18 [[Humming Azurite]]
   - 15 [[Luclizite Cluster]]
   - 45 [[Slow Creeping Root]]
1. Craft [[Protective Research Housing Tubes]].
1. Return to [[Purihni "The Edge" Fordel III (The Bazaar)|Purihni "The Edge" Fordel III]].

## Rewards

- [[Research Mission Reward Crate]]
- 8 [[Recuso Field Voucher]]
- At least 1p 25g 7,700 status
