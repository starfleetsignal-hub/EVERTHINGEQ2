## Steps

![First Key](images/Key_for_Key_Evidence.jpg)

1. Go to [[The Feerrott]] and speak with [[Kaira L'or]]. {{waypoint -452, -6, 785}} If you can Fast Travel, the Druid Rings are closest to Kaira L'or.
1. Find the first key to the north, in an eddy within [[The Anaconda River]]. {{waypoint -528, -6, 204}}
1. Return to Kaira L'or.
1. Find the second key near Kaira, in [[The Greenblood River]]. {{waypoint -433, -16, 892}}
1. Return to Kaira L'or.
1. Return to Justinian Theo.

## Rewards

- +5000 faction with **The Tribunal**
- [[Summon: The Tribunal's Bailiff]]
