## Description

Lady Astaar Evinien at the Sapling Spur Outpost asked me to participate in the Ritual of Calling that helps welcome returning Fae spirits to Kelethin. By doing so, I will be able to return to Kelethin whenever I wish." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Examine a weathered memorial stone in [[Greater Faydark]] in [[Kelethin]] on top of the [[Kelethin Central Stump]]{{waypoint 319.49, 92.21, 337.66}}
1. Return to [[Lady Astaar Evinien]]

## Rewards

- Experience
- [[Call of Kelethin]]

## Journal Steps

Lady Astaar provided me with instructions to perform the ritual in Kelethin. As my part in the Fae Ritual of Calling, I must place this offering at the memorial stone located on top of the Central Stump in the middle of Kelethin.

I placed the offering and learned about the Siege of Kelethin. I completed the ritual at the memorial in Kelethin. Lady Astaar Evinien will be happy to know that I've completed my part in this ritual. I should speak with Lady Astaar at Sapling Spur Outpost near Kelethin.

I received the Call of Kelethin. I've spoken with Lady Astaar.

I paid my respects at the memorial to those lost in the Siege of Kelethin and received the Call of Kelethin in return.
