## Steps

1. Find [[Marweth Wrathborn]] near the docks.
   - On your way to the docks, you'll be approached by an innocent citizen who'll beg for you to help him escape.  Instead, you'll automatically punch him.
1. Speak to [[Marweth Wrathborn|Marweth's]] followers, then defeat them when they attack.
1. Speak to [[Marweth Wrathborn|Marweth]] again.
   - [[Marweth Wrathborn|Marweth]] will refuse to fight you.  Instead you'll be attacked by a group of [[An enraged follower|enraged followers]].
   - The [[An enraged follower|enraged followers]] will get back up roughly 5 seconds after being knocked out.  The only way to prevent being re-attacked is to get out of their aggro range while they're down.
1. Return to [[Gavin Seabreach]].

## Rewards

- At least 25g 76s 63c (at level 92)
