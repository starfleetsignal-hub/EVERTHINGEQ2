## Steps

Create 1 each of any 6 from the random list below:

- [[Chaos III|Chaos III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Battle Cry III|Battle Cry III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Controlled Rage II|Controlled Rage II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Grave Sacrament II|Grave Sacrament II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Devout Sacrament II|Devout Sacrament II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Demonstration of Faith III|Demonstration of Faith III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Dreadful Wrath|Dreadful Wrath (Journeyman)]]
- [[Rock Skin|Rock Skin (Journeyman)]]

Create 2 each of any 3 from the random list below:

- [[Refusal of Atonement|Refusal of Atonement (Journeyman)]]
- [[Retaliate|Retaliate (Journeyman)]]
- [[Mountain Stance|Mountain Stance (Journeyman)]]
- [[Bruising Spirit II|Bruising Spirit II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Precise Strike II|Precise Strike II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Unholy Blessing III|Unholy Blessing III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Demolish|Demolish (Journeyman)]]
- [[Body Like Mountain II|Body Like Mountain II (Journeyman)]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 72s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
