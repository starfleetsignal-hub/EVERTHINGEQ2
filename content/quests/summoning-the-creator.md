## Steps

1. Kill the following named monsters, all found throughout the first level of the Obelisk:
   - [[Paroxroz]] {{waypoint -175, 602, 310}} - placeholders are [[A paroxysmal observer|paroxysmal observers]]; 15 minute respawn timer.
   - [[Avulsor]] {{waypoint -102, 605, 365}} - placeholders are [[A paroxysmal observer|paroxysmal observers]]; 15 minute respawn timer.
   - [[Xychlzys]] {{waypoint -15, 600, 380}}
   - [[The Harbinger]] {{waypoint 29, 591, 310}}
   - [[Troxlep]] {{waypoint -29, 600, 340}} - placeholders are [[A spectral sentry|spectral sentries]].
1. Return to [[The Book of Summoning]]. Keep on talking to it to get the dialog option to spawn the Creator of Shadows.
1. Kill [[The Creator of Shadows]]. Speak to [[The Book of Summoning]] to spawn the Creator.

## Rewards

- At least 2g (at level 41)
- 2g 99s 88c
