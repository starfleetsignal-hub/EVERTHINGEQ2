## Steps

1. Slay 8 invading elementals wandering around nearby [[A violent rift|rift]]. They become aggressive when you get near and scale to your level.
1. Use [[Hyperspatial Exscindator]] you received from Tavari on the a violent rift itself in an attempt to close it. You get knocked back and wake up not far, next to a person wearing magically imbued armor.
1. Speak with [[Andreas Harroway]], Flamebearer Energist.

## Rewards

- [[AA]] experience
- 3 x [[Brass Lambent]] currency
- [[Fog Wraith]] - vanity pet scroll
- opens 3 repeateable tradeskill quests in nearby camp {{waypoint -2041, 1, 180}}

## Notes

- [[A living nimbusludge]]
- [[A planar fog elemental]]
