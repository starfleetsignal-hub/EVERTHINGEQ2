## Steps

1. Collect 6 unearthed coldain remains
   - a wolf called Ghost will follow you when you accept the quest, lead him to the ring of war battle field
   - if you lost the wolf speak with Gareri Greatlasher again
   - The coldain remains look like short piles of snow with a red flicker in the middle.  When you get near one, a white emote will appear on your screen, "Ghost barks excitedly, there must be Coldain remains nearby"
   - Click on the snow pile to spawn an [[Animated slush]].  Kill the animated slush to receive the update.
1. Speak with Gareri Greatslasher
