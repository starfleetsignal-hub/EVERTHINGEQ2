## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of Anashti Sul has asked for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Gather the requested supplies
   - Collect 3 [[Swarm Wings|swarm wings]] from creatures found in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]]
     - *Note: drops in [[Chests|small chest]] from a noxious swarm in Tellurav Marshlands*
     - *1-2 drops per group member*
   - Gather or purchase [[Corymbia Wood|corymbia wood]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas
     - *Common harvest from BoZ lumber*
     - *Can buy off broker*
     - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to the [[Herald of Undeath]] {{waypoint -2063, -130, -35}} in [[The Sinking Sands]]
