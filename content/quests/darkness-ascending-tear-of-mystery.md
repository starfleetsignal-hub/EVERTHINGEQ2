## Notes

This signature quest is only available after completion of the Cobalt Scar series ending with [[Tear in the Grotto]].

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Artimus Klangfangle]] {{waypoint 4426, -823, 493}} in [[Scarstone]]
1. Head Northwest to [[Howlers Cove]] {{waypoint 4864, -1093, 118}}.
   - Use the animated gelate as many times as required on six blighted buccaneers (99^^) until they drop to the ground. They can be found around shipwrecks (they must be below 50% for the gelate to work).
   - Kill the congealed necrosis (101^) that pops out.
1. Return to Artimus, who is now in the Scarstone command tent at {{waypoint 4404, -822, 460}}.
1. Click the candle on the barrel next to the Tear of Veeshan.
1. Speak again to Artimus.
1. Return to Vishra to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
