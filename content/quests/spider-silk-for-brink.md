## Steps

1. Obtain 5 spider silk samples by killing [[Striped silkspinner|silkspinner spiders]]. These are found west of [[The Nursery]] past the growlers at {{waypoint 144, -30, -724}}
1. Return to [[Brink Trudger]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 55c
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Brink's Legplates]]
  - [[Brink's Woven Band]]
  - [[Brink's Green Knot]]
  - [[Brink's Chain Helm]] - Ranger
