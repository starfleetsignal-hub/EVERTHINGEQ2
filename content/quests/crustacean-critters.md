## Notes

- **This quest is repeatable** (no 18 hours timer anymore)
- **You can use [[A shiny Iceclad crab shell]], which gives the quest [[Rare and Shiny]], once per each time you have this quest**

## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[An Iceclad crab|Iceclad crabs]]
1. Return to Narp

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +200 faction with **Othmir of Velious**
