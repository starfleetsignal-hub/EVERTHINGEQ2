## Starting the Quest

You should be actively working on the quest [[Repairing the Eternal Prism]].

## Steps

1. Four columns will be highlightable.  Line up the three common shapes on each column - align with ice pointer.
1. Group dopplegangers will spawn around totem.
1. Kill dopplegangers.

## Rewards

- At least 31g 73s 78c
