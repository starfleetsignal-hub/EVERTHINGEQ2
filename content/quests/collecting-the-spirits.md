## Steps

![](images/Tortured_spirits.jpg)

1. Fish 5 **tortured spirits** from the [[Wellspring of Nightmares]] using the [[Spiritist's Fishing Pole]].
   - You must equip the [[Spiritist's Fishing Pole]] in your primary slot to be able to target the **tortured spirits**. Then, just target a spirit and use your [[Fishing]] skill.  Don't forget to re-equip your primary weapon when you've finished!
1. Return to [[Atalika]] in the [[Shadow Oak]] {{waypoint 441, -23, -268}}

## Rewards

- At least 21s 31c (Lvl 55)
- [[Neriak Destrier whistle]]
- [[A Lost Soul]]
