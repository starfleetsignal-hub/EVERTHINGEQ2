## Steps

1. Travel north to [[Phantom Sea]] zone to dock at Blackwall {{waypoint -1149, 6, 1492}}which is the SE corner of the SE island since they don't have names.

**NOTE:** *For quick zone access Artisans over level 20 can purchase a Return to Highhold Bauble from the Far Seas merchant in Sapswill's Rest.*

1. Talk to [[Guld]] at {{waypoint -1179, 26, 1439}}
1. Continue helping Guld - complete the quest [[Taking the Thunder Pass]] and then speak to [[Corrith Midner]]
1. Help Corrith Midner get to safety - complete the quest(s) [[Field Bandage]]
1. Continue to help on North Dshinn
   - Continue to help Corrith Midner - complete the quests [[Crew Cuts and Bruises]], [[To Cast a Trap]], [[Allu'thoa Abduction]], and [[Ceremonial Vestments and Condiments]]
   - Help [[Zaveta Stickyfingers]] - complete the quests [[Zaveta's Blade Runner]], [[Caustic Collection]], [[Redoubt About It]], and [[Zaveta's Treasure Hunt]]
1. Search for any other Far Seas Co. members on North Dshinn - go to {{waypoint -1041, 26, 217}} and kill the two [[A mysterious attacker (Phantom Sea)|mysterious attackers]] (one 101^H one 101H linked) attacking either [[Tamm Gloryday]] (for good characters) or [[Correh Thorngrip]] (for evil characters). The respawn is about 10 minutes.
1. The following steps are different for good and evil
   - Good aligned:
     1. Speak to Tamm Gloryday
     1. Defeat the [[An attacking Akhevan|attacking Akhevans]]
        - If you are defeated by the attacking Akhevan, just speak with Tamm/Opal again to respawn them.
     1. Uncover Tamm's hidden rowboat at the [[Lost Harbor]] - go under the docks and click the green bush
     1. Take Tamm's rowboat to Kithicor Island
   - Evil aligned:
     1. Defeat Correh Thorngrip
     1. find Correh's hidden rowboat at the [[Lost Harbor]] - go under the docks and click the green bush
     1. Take Correh's rowboat to Kithicor Island
