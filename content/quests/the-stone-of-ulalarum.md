1. ![Ul'Alarum Oracle Stone](images/Ul'Alarum_Oracle_Stone.jpg)Harvest the ***Ul'Alarum Oracle Stone*** from a tent outside of [[Crushbone Keep]].
   - Spawn location is random (see discussion page for reported spawns to date) in one of the tents just outside the exit from the cave leading to [[Crushbone Keep]].
1. Return to [[Blinik Belestro]]
