## Steps

1. Find a [[Plain brown moth]].
1. Return to Elowys Laceleaf.
1. Find a [[Striped brown moth]].
1. Return to Elowys Laceleaf.
1. Find a [[Spotted yellow butterfly]].
1. Return to Elowys Laceleaf.

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
- [[Medium Collector's Pouch]]

## Notes

- If you already have one of the items needed for the quest in your possession when you reach a step, you can get the quest to update by selling an item to a vendor and then buying it back. Conveniently, there is a vendor [[Sage Elrendir]] located just inside the hut next to Elowys.
- You must return to Elowys with the item in inventory to complete each step of the quest. If you do not have the item in inventory (ie you sold it) then she will not update the quest.
