## Steps

1. Kill the following mobs, all found within [[The Shimmering Citadel]]:
   - [[A shimmering guardian (Shimmering Citadel)|A shimmering guardian]]: In the courtyard right outside from the tower where [[The Muse (Named)|The Muse]] is located.
   - [[A silent bones dirge]] or [[A silent bones troubador]]: On the level beneath the zone-in, such as {{waypoint -76, 59, -57}}.
   - [[An Aravu watchmistress]]: Near the zone-in {{waypoint -8.00, 81.00, 89.00}}
1. Return to the Muse for your reward.

## Rewards

One of the following:

- [[Chain Leggings of Prosperity]]
- [[Slippers of Arcane Fervor]]
- [[Tome of Holy Affinity]]
- [[Staff of the Celestial Warden]]
