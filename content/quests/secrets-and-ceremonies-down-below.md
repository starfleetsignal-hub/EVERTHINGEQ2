## Notes

- Scales with player level, requires level 20+
- If grouped, **only 1 person can gather the two jars**, and there is a 36-hr zone reset timer.  Picking up the jars will "bug" if you inadvertently pick up just 1 jar. *Update 25-Dec-2016,  There is now only a 90 Minute lockout timer.*
- The jars appear to be linked, and both can be picked up with a single click.

## Steps

1. Locate and enter the [[Down Below]]. The nearest entrance is behind the [[In Range]] archery shop in [[Elddar Grove]]. {{waypoint 518, -17, -397}}
1. Seek out the place where the Bloodsabers are congregating. {{waypoint 8, 0, -122}}
1. Search the Bloodsaber hideout to locate a [[The Crypt of Betrayal: Vile Ceremony|Curious Entrance]] and venture within. {{waypoint -5, -3, -143}}
1. Slay [[A Bloodsaber assassin|Bloodsaber assassins]], [[A Bloodsaber highguard|Bloodsaber highguards]], [[A Bloodsaber ritualist|Bloodsaber ritualists]] until [[Droglor Plaguerain]] appears. {{waypoint 45, -1, -162}}
1. Kill [[Droglor Plaguerain]].
1. Gather Antique Limestone Jars (glowing green).
1. Return to [[Harkam Nubbytoe]].
