## Steps

1. Go into the fields north of Quel'ule and assist three fallen soldiers to teleport back to Quel'ule.
   - Assist a wounded soldier. {{waypoint -314, 416, 443}}
   - Assist a wounded soldier. {{waypoint -419, 432, 399}}
   - Assist a wounded soldier. {{waypoint -350, 452, 320}} (Also Seen at {{waypoint -311, 416, 443}})
     - The updates are obtained from *an injured Quel'ule soldier*, which are only visible to people on the quest.  To get the update, right click and choose *Use Teleport Stone* or just double click him. Only one person will get credit but the soldiers respawn quickly -- if in a group, you can do the circuit and they will respawn by the time one person finishes.
1. Return to [[Chief Strategist Jasim|Chief Strategist]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g
- [[Quel'ule Teleport Stone]]
