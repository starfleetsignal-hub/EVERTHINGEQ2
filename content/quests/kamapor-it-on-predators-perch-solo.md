## Steps

Kill all the nameds in [[Karuupa Jungle: Predator's Perch (Solo)]] as you need the last 1 to complete this quest:

- Zone in from Kamapor Jungle.  {{waypoint -750, 195, -275}}.  It is a portal on the far East side of the map.
- Extinguish the scorcher. I found the first 2 mobs to be spank / tank.  Hinokawu was a tough fight, ***MUST use Heroic Opportunity for all***

1. Kill Pterrordax at {{waypoint -750, 225, -356}} - Kill the group of Pteradactyls to begin this fight. At the center of this area, Pteradactyls fly overhead along with Pterrordax who will land to begin the fight, he will be attackable as he descends. This fight requires you avoid the White circles, and keep you back to the wall because of the knockback. It EMOTEs "Pterrordax gobbles the pygmy, Healing its wounds!". Unsure how to counter this.Beware the objects being thrown or dropped from above, they will do major damage.
1. Haribon & Agilon - The Plumedrake King and Queen. Same general location. Attacked one until it stopped taking damage then switched to the other, rinse/repeat.
1. Hinokawu (AKA Scorcher of the Skies) - Same location. It Stops taking damage at certain points, It EMOTES "Hinakawu hungers!"  Continue with Heroic Opportunities, and it continues taking damage, Tank/Spank.
