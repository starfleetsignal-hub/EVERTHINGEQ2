## Steps

1. Researcher Ma'jok gives you an [[Elemental summoning staff]]. Use it to summon an elemental against a nearby [[An Underfoot hardstomper|Underfoot hardstomper]].
1. Return to Researcher Ma'jok {{waypoint 2022, -123, 3424}} with the results you witnessed.
