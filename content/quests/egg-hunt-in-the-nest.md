## Notes

- If you do not see Mavaerick in the jail, it means he is already at the [[Windgazer's Hideout]].
- If you finish the [[Egg Hunt in the Nest]] quest and Mavaerick is not in place, you have to free him again from [[Prisoner's Isle]].

## Steps

1. Collect six eggs in [[The Nest of the Great Egg]]: {{waypoint -675, -21, -498}}
   1. Day of Yolk
   1. All Feathers Eve
   1. Season of Preservation
   1. The Hooting
   1. War of the Eggs
   1. Year of the Plague
   - These are ground spawns throughout the zone. Each major area of the zone holds one type of egg. So when you find one, you can move on to the next hall/room.
   - **Make sure everyone in the group has picked up 6 eggs before killing Chamberlain in the last room. The next step is to kill him, and if you don't wait 5 minutes for respawns you'll be waiting 90 minutes to reset the zone.**
   - Three eggs spawn at a time. They have about a 5 minute respawn timer, so you might want to pick up half on the first run through, then send 3 people back to get their updates before killing Chamberlain.
   - In order to get to the last level you have to get a key to the lift. This is called [[A strange crystal egg]]. It drops from either [[Switch]] or [[Nutty]] who patrol with a group in [[The Library of the Seven]]. {{waypoint -20, 56, 1}}
1. Kill [[Chamberlain X'Haviz]] near [[The Great Egg]]. {{waypoint 180, 103, 0}}
1. Return to [[High Guard Mavaerick]] on [[Windgazer's Hideout]] at {{waypoint -661, 8, -498}}.
