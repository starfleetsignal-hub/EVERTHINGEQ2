## Steps

1. Find the following items in [[Maj'Dul]]:
   - Several of the items are received as rewards from subquests, while the rest are found in random locations about the city. Some people have reported not seeing any of the items until after they've left Maj'Dul and returned, so to save some frustration, do the subquests before looking for the pickups.
   - Items found by completing subquests:
     - [[The Idol of Dunefury]]: Reward from the quest [[My Service for an Idol]].
     - [[The Sunspire Necklace]]: Reward from the quest [[Trap Snapping]].
     - [[Bones of Dalgin B'Dynn]]: Reward from the quest [[The Bones in the Box]].
   - Items found randomly throughout the city:
     - [[The Urn of Al'Hadid]]
     - [[The Box of Tupperwuzzle]]
     - [[The Lifire Ewer]]
     - [['King's Tomb Tavern' painting|The 'King's Tomb Tavern' painting]]
     - [[Scent of Aros'Thalinor perfume|The Scent of Aros'Thalinor]]
     - [[Rod of the Rukh|The Rod of the Rukh]]
1. Speak to [[Te'Toa the terrible]]. {{waypoint 67, 140, -112}}

## Rewards

- [[Bones of Dalgin B'Dynn]] - *required by the next quest, [[The Tale of Dalgin B'Dynn]]*
- One of the following:
  - [[Terrorantula Emblazoned Barbute]]
  - [[Jaldaran Golden Bracelet]]
