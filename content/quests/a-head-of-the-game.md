## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
  - While this quest can technically be completed an infinite amount of times, it will stop being offered once your faction is greater than -10,000 (indifferent) with the Legion of Danak. Should your faction ever dip lower than that in the future, this quest will become available again.

## Steps

1. Collect the heads of 12 [[A Dracurion hoplite|Dracurion hoplites]]. {{waypoint -625, -37, -70}}
1. Return to Lance Corporal Sonoriss.

## Rewards

- 8g 11s 18c
- +10,000 faction with **Legion of Danak**
