## Steps

1. Enter [[Thexian Hideaway]] behind the Thexian Camp {{waypoint 576, -47, -75}}; go in far enough to trigger [[Talvus Thex]]'s dialog
   - *Note*: You can have 1 to 3 characters in this instance, mercs count as one.
1. Kill 3 waves of attackers: 2 [[A Thexian guard (Thexian Hideaway)|Thexian guards]] (18v and vv), 3 [[A Thexian guard (Thexian Hideaway)|Thexian guards]] (one 18 v and two vv) and [[Vaetiul K'Vanos]] (level 18)
   - *Note*: If you leave the zone during this event, it should pick right up where you left off. Prince Tavlus does not need to respawn.  When you reenter Thexian Hideaway one or more of the guards will still be attackable. Defeat them. Then defeat the next, and so forth until your quest is progressed with the defeat of Vaetiul K'Vanos.
1. Return to Miriam in Darklight Wood

## Rewards

- At least 14s 13c
