## Steps

1. Enter the landing pad again {{waypoint 429, 183, -577}} under [[Droumlunds]] Isle.
1. Attempt to wake [[Fraka Dar]] {{waypoint 403, 183, -828}}.
1. Fight against attacking Fraka Dar.
1. Talk to Fraka Dar again.
1. Kill 10 [[An aerakyn invoker|aerakyn invokers]] and [[An aerakyn realmstalker|aerakyn realmstalkers]] for their amulets.
1. Return to Fraka Dar.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
