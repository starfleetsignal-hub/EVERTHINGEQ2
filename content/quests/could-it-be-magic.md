## Description

I found Bawli, a pygmy from Tranquil Seas, at the docks in the Thundering Steppes!  She had stowed away on a Far Seas vessel intending to be taken to Zek, the Orcish Wastes.  I assured her that I would acquire the arcane clay that she sought in Zek, and return to her quickly.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable once per year.

## Steps

1. Defeat 12 [[A Crossed Swords clay dervish|Crossed Swords clay dervishes]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]].
   - The dervishes are on the beach east of the docks beyond [[Mess Sergeant Akseltz]] {{waypoint 412, -43, 381}}.
   - These elementals are non-aggro until you come quite close to them, when they respawn into an aggro mob at your level.
1. Return to Bawli in [[The Thundering Steppes|Thundering Steppes]].

## Rewards

- [[Cloak of the Love Bird]]
- 5 [[Erollisi coin]]
