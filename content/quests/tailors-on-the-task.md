Calla Bronzeleaf recruited me to use my tailoring skills to help G.L.E.E.S.H. provide support to the Luclinites who are trying to repair and relocate to the city of Fordel Midst. Their need for tweed and leather cord is high.

## Steps

- Check the tailors' crate near [[Calla Bronzeleaf]] to see what to make for the next shipment to Luclin.  You can find the [[Solidified ethereal filament|solidified ethereal filament]] needed around Teren's Grasp.
  - Craft 6 **Bolts of Tweed**
  - Craft 6 **spools of leather cord**
- Return to Calla Bronzeleaf to turn in.

## Rewards

- At least 11s 08c
- 2 [[G.L.E.E.S.H. Mark]]
