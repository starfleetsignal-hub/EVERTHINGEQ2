NOTE: You must have +10,000 (amiable) or higher standing with the City of Qeynos before Gil will offer you this quest. Once you qualify, he will no longer offer any of his other quests.

## Steps

Talk to Darion at the Sovereign House in [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 582.81, -8.70, 182.34}}to become a citizen of Qeynos. He  presents several options for response, all of which speak of going to the Temple of Life. However, once you finish the conversation line, the Becoming a Citizen of Qeynos quest completes and it sends you along to The Fulfillment of Dreams quest.
