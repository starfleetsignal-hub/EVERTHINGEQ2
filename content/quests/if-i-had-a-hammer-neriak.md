## Description

"The local mender has offered to show me how to use some of the harvests I gathered to craft a small lucky charm using his anvil." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Obtain [[Recipe: Lucky Wolf Paw Charm]] from the paper on top of the crate behind [[Dalvos D'Nar]].
1. Obtain [[Coal]] from the sack next to the anvil.
1. Read the recipe to scribe it and craft a [[Lucky Wolf Paw]] charm. It requires 1 [[Tin cluster]] and 1 [[Lead cluster]].
1. Speak with [[Dalvos D'Nar]].
1. Visit [[Szinjss X'Vemtor]], the tradeskill tutor in [[Neriak, City of Hate]] {{waypoint -174.76, -0.05, 302.83}} to learn more about crafting.

## Rewards

- At least 85c
- [[Handbook of the Dark Bargainers]]
