This is part 5 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Freeport]] who wear Chain and Plate Armor (with the exception of [[Shaman|shaman]] types).

- If you are from [[Qeynos]], [[Kelethin]], or [[New Halas]], then your version of the quest is called [[Ian's Forgetful Family - Part V]].
- Mobs modified slightly as of LU32/33

## Steps

1. Kill a variety of creatures within [[Nektulos Forest]] for materials:
   - Kill 12 [[Deadly rumbleroot|deadly rumbleroots]] for their fibers. {{waypoint -710, 53, 89}}
   - Kill 12 [[A rotting follower of Grelkor|rotting followers of Grelkor]] for their corroded chainmail. {{waypoint -546, 60, -1291}}
   - Kill 10 [[Blood shriller|blood shrillers]] for their claws. {{waypoint 432.87, -0.33, -648.57}}
   - Kill 1 [[A Thexian Vin chosen|Thexian Vin chosen]] for his Teir'Dal Greaves {{waypoint -1454, 42, -1413}}.
1. Return to [[Dyric Pire]].
