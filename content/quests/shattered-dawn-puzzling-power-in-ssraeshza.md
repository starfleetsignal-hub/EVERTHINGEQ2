## Steps

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 727.02, 77.96, 663.56}} to enter [[The Ruins of Ssraeshza (Solo)|The Ruins of Ssraeshza [Solo]]].
1. Speak to [[Zelrene Ryzal]] at {{waypoint 12.75, -30.68, 12.11}}.
1. Proceed through the zone.
   - *Note: See [[The Ruins of Ssraeshza (Solo)|The Ruins of Ssraeshza [Solo]]] for help proceeding through the zone.*
1. Speak to [[Zelrene Ryzal]] in [[Wracklands]] at {{waypoint 740.38, 76.82, 695.62}}
