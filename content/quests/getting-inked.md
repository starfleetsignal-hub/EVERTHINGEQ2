## Steps

1. Collect 5 [[Octopus ink sac]] from [[A Ykeshan cephalopod|cephalopods]]  in the lagoon south of Salty Sail Port in the lower Moors. {{waypoint -1053, -1107, 1287}}
  - *Alternatively you can collect [[Ink sacs]] from [[Void-touched cephalopods]] in [[Lavastorm]], around [[Munzok's Material Bastion]].*
1. Return to [[Grandpa Krill]]

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- 1200 faction with **Survival Accord**
