## Steps

1. Speak with [[Captain Beltho]] at [[The Keep of the Gnollslayers]]. {{waypoint -2103, -44, 437}}
1. Visit the back entrance to [[Blackburrow]], which can be found inside a giant fallen log in a small valley hidden southeast of [[Archer's Wood]]. You can reach the valley by crossing two islands joined by log bridges starting with [[Wooded Isle]] around {{waypoint -765, -39, 988}}. The quest should update when you walk close to {{waypoint -1214, -3, 912}}.
1. Return to Captain Beltho at The Keep of the Gnollslayers.
1. Return to Captain Eitoa for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 4s 32c
