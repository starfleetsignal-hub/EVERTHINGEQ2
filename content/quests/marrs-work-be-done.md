## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 20+

After completing Dire Delivery to Ironforge, speak to Kwipp Stalwart across the street from Ironforge Exchange in Qeynos Capitol District at  {{waypoint 317, -23, -111}}. She's heard good things about you from the Circle of Ten.

## Steps

1. Collect updates from other frogloks within the city.
   1. Speak with [[Rrak Boldfist]] in [[South Qeynos]] near the entrance to [[Starcrest Commune]]. {{waypoint 687, -19, 240}}
   1. Speak with [[Foorog Gallantblade]] in [[Qeynos Harbor]] by the gate to [[Castleview Hamlet]]. {{waypoint 717, -22, -100}}
   1. Speak with [[Kwa Stalwart]] in [[Qeynos Harbor]] by the gate to [[Graystone Yard]]. {{waypoint 872, -25, -69}} After some dialog, he will [[Kwa Stalwart (mob)|attack you]]. Speak to Kwa again after the battle.
1. Return to [[Kwipp Stalwart (Qeynos Capitol District)|Kwipp Stalwart]].
