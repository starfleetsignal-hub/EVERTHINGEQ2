## Steps

1. Use the received [[Tonal Reciprocator]] and record monkey sounds from 12 monkeys.
   - *Note: The monkeys can be found almost everywhere, but are quite easy to find in the Innothule Swamp around Firmroot Moot. see talk page for locations*
1. Return the recording to [[Recorderbot lwf8.6]]

## Rewards

- At least 11g 5s 35c
- +1200 faction with **Survival Accord**
- [[Monkey Caller]]
