## Steps

1. Turn over rocks and gather 6 crawlers - *found further down the road near the [[A frontier bighorn]], {{waypoint -402, -1, -1534}}*. ![crawler rock](images/Crawler_rock.jpg)
1. Click on the medium size rocks and pick up the "squirmy worm" that appears.![squirmy worm](images/Squirmy_worm.jpg)
   - the squirmy worm will always appear *in front* of player, one may not see it if in third-person until you shift your view.
1. Speak with [[Rumbletum]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +4,000 faction with **Broken Toe Clan**
- +4,000 faction with **Goblins of Nurga**
