## Steps

1. Find and examine 'Crafter Messenger Corpse'. {{waypoint -274, -22, -927}}
   - Make sure you have at least two free slots in your inventory before advancing the next step. If not, the items from the corpse will not be placed in your inventory.
1. Click on the pile of wood behind [[Scout Vazhda T`Larr]]. {{waypoint -286, 11, -1246}}
1. Create 2 [[Cabbage Stew (Crafted)|Cabbage Stews]] using the firepit that appeared.
   - You do not need to do anything with the [[Collapsible Cooking Kit]] in your inventory. Just simply begin cooking the recipe.
1. Speak with Scout Vazhada T`Larr.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill Experience
- At least 55g
