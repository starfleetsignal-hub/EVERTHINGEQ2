## Steps

1. Talk to Kain.
1. Talk to Mysterious Figure on the docks in Freeport, a quest book will appear when you approach location -299 -59 200
   - Ported to Salty Lass II (boat).
1. Talk to Kain. He is worried something is wrong, talk to the others until a worry is expressed about Doran the Crow.
1. Talk to Doran (to the south, near the bow/front of the ship)
1. Click on the large artifact.
   - A Wraithguard appears and attacks. He was hidden in the artifact!
   - A fire breaks out, and a Leviathan is summoned, use these to defeat the Lucanic Wraithguard Knight.
1. Drag the Lucanic Wraithguard over the fire near the stern (back) of the ship--then drag him forward to the bow so that the Leviathan can eat him.
   - Be careful with your dps if you're level 100+.  Use singular target.
   - This is a difficult fight for some classes. Be prepared to heal yourself and/or win with low health.
1. Target each tentacle and wound it, they will get down to a small amount of health and stay there.  The Leviathan releases the ship after each tentacle is hurt.
1. Talk to Kain again. Afterward, you will be zoned to shore in the Commonlands by the Blackshield's dock.
