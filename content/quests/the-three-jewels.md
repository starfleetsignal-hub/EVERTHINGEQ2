## Steps

![a red amber deposit](images/A_red_amber_deposit.png)

1. For this quest you need to mine three different types of amber in various locations around the area.
   - *Note: all the nodes look the same - like small crystaline mining nodes. They are all trackable (Track Harvestables)*
   - Obtain a sample of Blue Amber from a node somewhere around [[Maven's Reach]]. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -47.85, 383.43, 589.90}}.
     - {{waypoint 81, 380, 597}}
     - {{waypoint 42, 386, 670}}
     - {{waypoint 21, 382, 700}}
     - {{waypoint -22, 383, 685}}
     - {{waypoint -40.53, 384.61, 586.17}}
     - {{waypoint 76.17, 384.07, 575.37}}
     - {{waypoint 85, 381, 623}}
   - Obtain a sample of Red Amber from a node near the [[Ning Yun Retreat]]. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 182, 337, 485}}
     - {{waypoint 262, 345, 527}}
     - {{waypoint 329, 347, 532}}
     - {{waypoint 224, 341, 498}}
   - Obtain a sample of White Amber from a node in [[Ghanlin's Descent]]. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 277, 283, 256}}
     - {{waypoint 360, 273, 194}}
1. Return to [[Zhen Gao]].
