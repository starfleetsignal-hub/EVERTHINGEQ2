## Notes

- This is a repeatable quest.

## Steps

1. Destroy 5 Chirannis Ritual Drums in Sabertooth Burrow or in Dustpaw Burrow
   - Possible Sabertooth Burrow locations:
     - {{waypoint -75, -46, -227}}
     - {{waypoint -174, -30, -119}} requires a [[Darkpaw Ladder]]
     - {{waypoint -159.64, -29.92, -114.87}} requires a [[Darkpaw Ladder]]
     - {{waypoint -98, -40, -140}}
     - {{waypoint -39.29, -42.90, -125.40}}
     - {{waypoint -119.99, -47.31, -214.57}} requires a [[Darkpaw Ladder|Darkpaw Ladde]]
   - Possible Dustpaw Burrow locations
     - {{waypoint -123, -6, -133}} may require a [[Darkpaw Ladder]], but it can be reached by jumping
     - {{waypoint 1, -3, -189}}
     - {{waypoint -23, -12, -138}}
     - {{waypoint -132, -15, -80}}
1. Destroy 5 Pack Lord Totems in Mudpaw Burrow or in Rockpaw Burrow
   - Possible Mudpaw Burrow locations:
     - {{waypoint -62, -65, -234}}
     - {{waypoint -9, -64, -189}}
     - {{waypoint -89, -73, -168}}
   - Possible Rockpaw Burrow locations:
     - {{waypoint 50, 3, 203}}
     - {{waypoint 92, 5, 169}}
     - {{waypoint 129, 3, 187}}
     - {{waypoint 10, 3, 219}}
     - {{waypoint 2, 6, 189}}
1. Hunt devotees, zealots, and adherents for 12 Pack Lord prayer beads
   - Automatic group update upon killing the specific type of gnoll, including:
     - A Mudpaw Zealot in Mudpaw Burrow
     - A Dustpaw adherent in Dustpaw Burrow
     - A Nisch Mal Devotee in Splitpaw Burrow
1. Return to Steinsister Taswynn in Darkpaw Dugout {{waypoint -22, -77, -269}}

## Rewards

- 2p 15g 55s 7c
- One of the following:
  - [[Chirannis Ritual Drum]]
  - [[Pack Lord Totem]]
