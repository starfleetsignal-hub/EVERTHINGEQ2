## Steps

1. Speak to a reforging specialist in Qeynos or Freeport.
   - [[Rorak Ironbellows]] can be found in [[North Qeynos (POI)|North Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 394, -21, -177}}.
   - [[Jemilar]] can be found in [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -260, -56, 88}}.
   - [[Litheon McElhannon]] can be found in [[Qeynos Harbor (POI)|Qeynos Harbor]], [[Qeynos Province District]] in [[The Hall of Steel]] at {{waypoint 670, -20, 14}}.
1. Kill any "monster who presents a sufficient challenge" (cons higher than gray; green works)
1. Return to the Reforging specialist.
