## Notes

The scamps and steins are spread throughout the zone. Gather them to complete the first two tasks.<br>
In order to get past the crystals in this zone, you must destroy mining equipment boxes until you receive a miner's combusti-ball.<br>

## Steps

1. Travel to Lesser Faydark (from Greater Faydark, Butcherblock or Loping Plains), take the horse from nearest Mount Station to Fae Court but jump off near brownie hideaway location, enter the brownie hideaway within the tunnel at {{waypoint -262, -33, 63}}
1. Complete the following:
   - Capture 6 brownie scamps
   - Reclaim 8 blessed steins
   - Apprehend the leader of the scamps.
     - past the second golden crystal there will be 2 Bags of gold, click them both to spawn Lugharcan. Fight him until you capture him.
1. Return to Acolyte Kosma at {{waypoint 343, 189, 501}} at the Highland Outpost in Butcherblock Mountains
