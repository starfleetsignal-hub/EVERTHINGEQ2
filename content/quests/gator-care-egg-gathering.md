## Steps

1. Gather 10 alligator eggs for the crocodile pens. They look similar to the egg clusters in The Egg Harvest, but are found on land in the swamp area around [[Firmroot Moot]]. See the talk page for locations where players have found the eggs.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- -150 faction with **Guktan Guard**
- +250 faction with **Clan Grobb**
