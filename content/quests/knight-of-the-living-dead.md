## Steps

1. Obtain three of each of various bones, all of which are autoupdates from killing various undead around the island..  must be done at night.
   1. Obtain three blackened bone fragments
   1. Obtain three shards of burnt bone
   1. Obtain three scorched piece of bone. From a lifelike watcher.
   1. Obtain three slivers of singed bone
1. Return to [[Coral Knight]] at {{waypoint -513.62, 23.75, 643.30}}
