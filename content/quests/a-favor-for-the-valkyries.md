## Steps

1. Obtain the following five quest updates:
   - Scepter - obtain by killing [[A Frosthorn stormcaller]]
   - Diadem - obtain by killing [[A Frosthorn vindicator]]
   - Tiara - obtain by killing [[A Frosthorn shadowgore]]
   - Idol - sitting on the outcropping at {{waypoint 118, 26, -529}}
   - Urn - sitting on the ground below the overhang at {{waypoint -25, 21, -430}}
     - Make sure all party members pick up the idol/urn at the same time as they disappear when looted
1. Return to [[Idona]]. {{waypoint -200, 38, -636}}
1. Speak to [[Thyr Stormhammer]] at [[Bitterwind Pioneer's Encampment]]. {{waypoint 459, 0, -210}}

## Rewards

- At least 2g 20s 65c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Ever Frozen Defender's Medallion]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Ever Frozen Healer's Medallion]] (All Priests)
  - [[Ever Frozen Medium's Medallion]] (All Mages)
  - [[Ever Frozen Stalker's Medallion]] (All Scouts)
- +5000 faction with **The Order of the Blade**
