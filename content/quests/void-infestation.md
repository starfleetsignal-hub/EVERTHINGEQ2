## Steps

1. Head towards {{waypoint -68.30, -129.65, 306.56}}.
1. Kill 8 [[An invading brute|invading brutes]] inside [[Najena's Dungeon]].
   - *Note: They will pop and automatically engage you.*
   - *Note: They don't pop till you move. If you need to rest after a kill, just don't move anywhere and you can regen to full.*
   - *Note: They will stop spawning after you have killed 8.*
1. Check the deepest floor of [[Najena's Sanctum]]
   - *You can access the lower levels by taking the elevator at {{waypoint -37.54, 126.80, 206.36}} and going all the way down; right-click the lever at the elevator and click "Lower elevator". It should lead into Najena's Ward of Elements.*
   - *Note: You will need to lower the elevator two times, the first stop leads to the level with [[Najena's Hollow Tower]] .*
1. Return to [[Pyrissa Flamecog]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g
- +2250 faction with **Order of Flame**
