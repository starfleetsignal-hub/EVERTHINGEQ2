## Steps

1. Kill [[A Dracurion hoplite|Dracurion hoplites]] {{waypoint -575, -35, -90}} until you have found the following items (uncommon update):
   - 12 *magically imbued axes*
   - 12 *arcanic shields*
1. Return to [[Sergeant Slithess]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- +1,000 faction with **Legion of Danak**
