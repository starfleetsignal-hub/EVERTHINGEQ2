## Steps

1. Enter the [[Ossuary of Malevolence: Primordial Altar]] {{waypoint 685, 50, 787}}
1. Explore and find items in there
   - *Note: need to kill trash, you can no longer invis to {{waypoint 22, 3, -280}} for first update*
   - *Note: either go left or right behind the pillars if you don't wanna kill every trash mob*
   - Fight to the far end to explore the Location
   - Click on the object behind the altar on the right side
   - Click on the object behind the altar at the center
   - Click on the object behind the altar on the left side
1. Continue exploring - return to the entrance and you'll see [[Villandre V'Zher]] on the stairs.  Fight her, and she'll port out at 60% Health
1. Turn around! [[Drensh]] awaits at the bottom of the stairs.  He hits like a truck but doesn't have too much health.  Kill him.
1. Return to your city's emissary at Wanderer's Dock, Kithicor Island.

- At least 1p 35g
- 2 [[Electrified Adornment of Critical Chance (QuestReward)|Electrified Adornment of Critical Chance]]
