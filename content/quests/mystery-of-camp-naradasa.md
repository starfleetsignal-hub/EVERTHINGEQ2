## Steps

1. Search for the Kinnahma camp at {{waypoint -627.02, 89.10, -521.04}}
1. Speak to [[Shohreh]] at {{waypoint -627.02, 89.10, -521.04}}
1. Speak to [[Vikram]] {{waypoint -644, 88, -515}}.
1. Replace the drained purifying icons in [[Grumblugtin Village]]. ![Purifying Icon](images/Purifyingicon.png)
   1. Attempt to replace the **Drained Purifying Icon Vata** at {{waypoint -424, 119, -593}}.
      - Defeat the [[A cadaverous brawler|cadaverous brawler]].
      - Replace the **Drained Purifying Icon Vata**.
   1. Replace the **Drained Purifying Icon Pitta** at {{waypoint -518, 122, -716}}.
   1. Attempt to replace the **Drained Purifying Icon Kapha** at {{waypoint -383, 139, -756}}
      - Defeat the [[A cadaverous chanter|cadaverous chanter]].
      - Replace the **Drained Purifying Icon Kapha**.
1. Return to [[Vikram]].
