## Steps

1. Talk to Captain Stovka on the ship at the docks in  South Freeport {{waypoint -292, -58, 150}} to receive this City Task.
   - Kill 12 Vornerus Ravasects at  in [[The Bonemire]]. The ravasects you have to kill are random. You either have to kill [[Vornerus defender|Vornerus defenders]], [[Vornerus screamer|Vornerus screamers]] or [[Vornerus stalker|Vornerus stalkers]]. They can all be found at the same location near [[Hive Vornerus]].
1. Return to [[Captain Stovka]].

## Rewards

- 13633 status
- +500 faction with **The Seafury Buccaneers**
