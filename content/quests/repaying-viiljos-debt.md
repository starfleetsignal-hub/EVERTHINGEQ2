## Description

*While attempting to have a scroll translated for Count Jooliin by the resident Fae sage, I was required to take on a task for the sage to prove my trust. Viiljo the sage apparently owes a monk some Faydark Sorghum, a rare and valuable herb that only grows in the Northeastern region of the Lesser Faydark near the Brownie village of Panderpana. Viiljo wants me to collect some Faydark Sorghum and then hand deliver it to Master Kyau at the Wu encampment to the Northwest. I'm told that I can reach the Wu camp by passing through the Aphotic Intersection and then heading North.*

## Prerequisites

- Your faction with [[The City of Kelethin (Faction)|City of Kelethin]] must be Indifferent or better to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest 8 herbs called Faydark Sorghum near [[The Village of Panderpana]] {{waypoint -515, -50, -220}}
1. Bring the herbs to [[Master Kyau]] at the [[Camp of the Legendary Wu]] in the north {{waypoint 845, 138, -468}}
1. Return to Viiljo Aelii

## Rewards

- At Least 9g 27s 1c
- +100 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
