## Steps

![An altar to Xktkez, the All Seeing](images/Glory_of_the_All_Seeing.png)

1. Hunt sludgegloom crawlers in Tentacle Fields for 9 unblemished tendrils  {{waypoint 323, 28, -266}}
1. Return to Xhindar
1. Leave 9 unblemished tendrils as offerings for Xktkez, the All Seeing. Click on altars labeled [[Glory to the All Seeing]] located throughout the [[Vital Terrace]] area. Possible locations are:
   - {{waypoint -70.38, 106.67, -517.23}}, {{waypoint -52.92, 106.71, -515.36}}, {{waypoint -46.20, 105.93, -503.59}}
   - {{waypoint -85.97, 109.62, -414.09}}, {{waypoint 10.28, 102.18, -504.15}}, {{waypoint 18.97, 102.18, -493.82}}
   - {{waypoint 52.70, 104.22, -468.04}}, {{waypoint 40.29, 102.97, -445.05}}, {{waypoint 24.44, 102.79, -450.01}}
   - {{waypoint -61.68, 107.30, -494.75}} and {{waypoint -261.02, 132.71, -461.97}}
1. Return to Xhindar
