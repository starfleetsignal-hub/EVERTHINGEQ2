## Prerequisites

1. Minimum Level: 95
1. You must have completed the [[Tears of Veeshan Signature Timeline]] to see the quest starter.
1. Must be in Heroic group (advanced solo will not complete quest)

## Steps

1. Open the lockbox at {{waypoint 137, 158, -170}} (it doesn't actually "open", just puts the beads in your inventory).  A set of shimmering pearls will be placed in your inventory
1. Examine the shimmering pearls to update quest
1. Find the former holder of the item

note: you will have to do multiple heroic instances UNTIL the named "Zordakalicus Ragefire" spawns

   1. After completing a Heroic instance, a pillar will appear that is needed to advance this quest.  The pillar [[Champions From Beyond]] has a chance to spawn [[Zordakalicus Ragefire (Epic)|Zordakalicus Ragefire]].
      - To have a chance in getting Halikus, Servant of Ragefire, the group must kill [[Zordakalicus Ragefire (Epic)|Zordakalicus Ragefire]], by choosing the "I..." option.
