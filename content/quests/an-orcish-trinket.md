## Notes

- You may want to complete [[The Bloodskull Threat]] at the same time as this quest as they both require killing the same named mobs.

## Steps

1. Inspect any one of the following 3 relic items:
   - [[Argorym's blade]] from a chest next to [[Lieutenant Vrah'Kna]]'s tower {{waypoint -407.38, -48.02, 451.32}}
   - [[Daughter of the Sky]] looted from [[Jyrok the Stone]] in the [[Hidden Vale]] {{waypoint -245.99, -47.77, 870.93}}
   - [[Son of the Sky]] looted from [[Vurog Ma'gar]] in the [[Hidden Vale]]{{waypoint -245.99, -47.77, 870.93}}
1. Speak to [[Rilkrik]] at [[The Crossroads]] {{waypoint -517, -44, -303}}. He will tell you to get the other 2 items you do not have. Go kill the above named to loot those items.
1. Speak to the [[Spirit of Raegnir]], a ghostly orc wandering [[The Siege Lands]] at {{waypoint -1305, -78, -278}}
