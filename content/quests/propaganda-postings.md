## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 40+

## Steps

1. Place the first [[Propaganda Posters|propaganda poster]] on the outside wall of the [[Coldwind Coast Stables]] in [[South Qeynos (POI)|South Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 417, -20, 229}}
1. The next [[Propaganda Posters|poster]] goes up on the outside wall at [[Sneed Galliway's Trading Post]] in [[North Qeynos (POI)|North Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 283, -20, -58}}
   - Speak to [[Drobble Clankworth]] after you place the poster.
1. The third [[Propaganda Posters|poster]] should be posted at the [[In Range]] archery shop in [[The Elddar Grove (POI)|The Elddar Grove]], [[Qeynos Province District]]. {{waypoint 584, -18, -407}}
1. Put up the next [[Propaganda Posters|poster]] at the foot of the stairs inside [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn]] in [[Qeynos Harbor (POI)|Qeynos Harbor]], [[Qeynos Province District]]. {{waypoint 808, -23, 75}}
   - [[Guard Dravin]] will become very annoyed with you and you will have to fight him.
1. The final [[Propaganda Posters|poster]] goes on the outside wall of [[The Herb Jar]] in South Qeynos, Qeynos Capitol District. {{waypoint 668, -18, 236}}
1. Return to [[Marik McPherson III]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g 00s (at level 100)
