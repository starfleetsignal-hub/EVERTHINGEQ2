Grassblade, an accomplished Green Hood burglar, was nearly caught by Scourge orcs at Fort Klag in Hounds' Gorge. He got out alive, but he dropped his satchel of blessed lockpicks. I've promised to recover them for him, in hopes of a favor.

## Steps

![a lock pick satchel](images/A_lock_pick_satchel.png)

1. Find [[Grassblade (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)‎|Grassblade's]] lock pick satchel.
1. The satchel is located in [[Fort Klag]] in one of the tents {{waypoint -205.84, -27.56, -100.77}}. Click the satchel.
1. Find 8 of Grassblade's lock picks by slaying Scourge orcs in the fort.
1. Return to Grassblade.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- +500 faction with **The Green Hoods**
