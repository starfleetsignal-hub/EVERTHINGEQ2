## Steps

![spotted budling beneath large mushrooms](images/Spotted_Budling.jpg)

1. Accomplish the following in the [[Swamp of No Hope]], in no particular order:
   - Kill 5 [[A dragonhawk|dragonhawks]] {{waypoint -1266, -90, 675}}
   - Kill 5 [[A carrion recluse|carrion recluses]]:
     - {{waypoint -689, -139, 805}}
     - {{waypoint -1266, -90, 675}}
     - {{waypoint -849, -123, 623}}
     - {{waypoint -1540.56, -64.39, 838.88}}
     - {{waypoint -1474, -81, 835}}
     - {{waypoint -1819, -78, 1362}}
     - {{waypoint -1662, -75, 1343}}
     - {{waypoint -1589, -82, 1347}}
   - Harvest 10 *spotted budlings*. These spawn below clusters of large mushrooms. See the talk page for locations.
1. Return to [[Pen Keeper Valjik]] on [[Sathir's Span]].

## Rewards

- At least 11g 3s 75c
- +2,000 faction with **Riliss**
