## Steps

1. Go back to [[Izzal Din]], in the [[Jarsath Wastes]] {{waypoint -689, -108, -854}}.
   - Izzal was amazed at the weapon that I obtained at The Tabernacle of Pain.
   - He explained that it is the reformed metal tablets that once held the tenants of marr, but how [[The Truth of Marr (Fabled)|The Truth of Marr]] spent a detrimental amount of time within the demi plane known as The Sphere of Malevolence. As a result of that time, it has become corrupted.
   - He believes that there must be a way to cleanse the sword and restore it to its prior glory.
1. [[Priestess Wenhaver]] in [[North Qeynos]] at the [[Temple of Life]] {{waypoint 10, -4, -11}} knew of an old text that may hold the key to restoring the sword.
   - According to an ancient text, I am to cure the irascibility of the once gallant item with rings of contrition.
   - The priestess also warned me that they *must be well guarded or within the clutches of very powerful beasts*
1. You must acquire the 3 rings of contrition (in any order).
   - The [[Ring of Valor]] is a quest item looted from the body of [[Imzok's Revenge]] in [[The Protector's Realm]]
   - The [[Ring of Mercy]] is a quest item looted from the body of [[Tairiza the Widow Mistress]] in [[The Tomb of Thuuga]]
   - The [[Ring of Generosity]] is a quest item looted from the body of [[Hoshkar]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]].
     - [[The Leviathan]] kill no longer needed to gain access to [[Veeshan's Peak]]
1. Inspect a ring after you get all 3. Head back to [[Izzal Din]] and you're done!

## Notes

- If you've already completed the [[Epic Repercussions]] quest, you'll have the [[The Enervated Truth of Marr|Enervated Truth of Marr]]. The [[The Truth of Marr (Fabled)|Truth of Marr]] will be removed from your inventory and you will not receive the Mythical weapon.
- You will be awarded the [[Mythical Brandisher]] achievement, even if you've already completed the [[Epic Repercussions]] quest.
