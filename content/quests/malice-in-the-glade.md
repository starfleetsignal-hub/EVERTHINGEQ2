## Notes

- This quest is for good aligned characters and will only be available for a short time till Wednesday, September 16, 2015.
- *evil aligned characters see [[Malice in the Woods]]*

## Lore

The House of Fallen Stars have heard some disturbing news from Tunare's Glade. Apparently, there has been some dark characters lurking within it. They cloak themselves in hate and malice, corrupting the strength of Growth within the Faydark.

## Steps

1. Investigate the dark characters that have entered [[Tunare's Glade]] in [[Greater Faydark]].
   - Defeat three members of the Primordial Malice {{waypoint -151.30, 36.12, 449.54}}. These include a [[A Primordial Malice cursespinner|Primordial Malice cursespinner]], a [[A Primordial Malice cultist|Primordial Malice cultist]], and a [[A Primordial Malice shadowknight|Primordial Malice shadowknight]].
1. Return to [[Miriel Tawnyglade]].
1. Disturbances have also been felt at a few locations of Growth within the Shattered Lands.
   1. Defeat three members of the Primordial Malice near the [[The Ruins of Caltorsis|Ruins of Caltorsis]] in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint -1407.73, -22.00, -807.95}}. You will receive a [[Cultist augmentation bone]].
   1. Defeat three members of the Primordial Malice in the [[Enchanted Lands]] {{waypoint -1003.34, 3.71, -297.99}} on the far east coast by the docks. You will receive a [[Cultist augmentation bone]].
   1. In [[Everfrost]], collect **a mysterious bauble** {{waypoint -636.25, -20.92, -313.53}} near the [[World Tree Root]].
1. Return to [[Miriel Tawnyglade]].
1. Take a [[Cultist augmentation bone]] and the **bauble** to [[Mathal Dewpetal]] at the [[Order of Arcane]] in [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 271.69, 105.04, 413.01}}.

## Rewards

- At least 33440 status (at level 100)
- At least 18240 status (at level 85)
- At least 7372 status (at level 49)
- At least 1520 status (at level 10)

## Note

Upon completion of this quest, [[Mathal Dewpetal]] instructs you to hunt down and kill as many Primordial Malice as you find. Turn in any more **arcane Malice baubles** you obtain to merchant [[Aldaelon Rootbed]], just outside at {{waypoint 268.91, 109.59, 373.50}}. The baubles can be exchanged for a number of house and appearance items. See [EQ2 Trader's Corner](http://eq2.eqtraders.com/articles/article_page.php?article=q169&menustr=080000000000) for screenshots of the various items available.
