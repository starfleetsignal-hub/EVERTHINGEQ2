## Description

"I told Gomphum about the orc I saw with Pengo the grobin. Gomphum says he did not know the orcs were working with the grobins, but he believes this information will be useful to an elf named Jenthis Viridar. I can find Jenthis at Sapling Spur Outpost: south through Echo Echo Canyon." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Jenthis Viridar]] {{waypoint -126.95, 12.24, -117.14}}

## Rewards

- At least 6s 12c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Gomphum: "Are the grobins still here?"
- Player Character: "Yes, but they will be less of a problem with pengo and Turkrik gone. Strangely, one of them was conversing with an orc."
- Gomphum: "The beast of blue?"
- Player Character: "Uh, yes."
- Gomphum: "A man spoke to Gomphum of these beasts. He asked that Gomphum tell him if they are spotted, and now they have been. Would you find Jenthis Viridar and tell him as much? He is this man. He says he is south from the canyon our cave paths lead to. Sapling Spur Outpost was what he called it. Safe journey my friend."
- Player character: "Yes, I will tell Jenthis."

I need to speak with Jenthis Viridar

- Jenthis Viridar: "Welcome to Sapling Spur Outpost. I am Jenthis Viridar, the current rank around here. I, well... I'd like to offer our assistance, but we're in a bit of a predicament right now."
- Player Character: "I am [Player Character]. I have news from Gomphum."
- Jenthis Viridar: "What's a Gomphum?"
- Player Character: "He's a Shroomba. He lives in Dyer Mycoria. there was an orc with the grobins in that cave system."
- Jenthis Viridar: "Oooh, yes I recall him... An orc you say? I swear these things are spontaneously generated. How many orcs were there?"
- Player Character: "Just one, I killed it."
- Jenthis Viridar: "Well... that's a relief. Still... the news gets worse and worse every day."

I have spoken with Jenthis Viridar. I have delivered the news to Jenthis Viridar.

I brought news of the orc in Dyer Mycoria to Jenthis Viridar.
