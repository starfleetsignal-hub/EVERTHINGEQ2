## Description

Maldura's gnemlin district is under attack and the Shields of Maldura are struggling to maintain order in the chaos. They could use my help!

## Zone

[[Maldura: Algorithm For Destruction (Advanced Solo)]]

## Steps

- Kill the old ashen one
  - [[Old Doclin]]
- Defeat the prime defender
  - [[X-O Arachmech]]
- Destroy the summoned golem
  - [[Stonerend]]
- Defeat the pump operators
  - [[Undying Apprentice (Advanced Solo)|Undying Apprentice]], [[Undying Mechanic (Advanced Solo)|Undying Mechanic]] & [[Undying Technician (Advanced Solo)|Undying Technician]]
- Kill the leader of the attack
  - [[Black Bolt]]
