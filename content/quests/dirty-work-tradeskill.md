## Steps

1. Clean the fossils (5)
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe: Fossil Cleaning Instructions]]
   1. Craft 5 [[Cleaned Fossil|Cleaned Fossils]] on a [[Work Bench]] near the Highland Outposts' Station {{waypoint 336, 189, 515}} or any Work Bench with the following material and fuel:
      - 5 [[Supple loam]]
      - 5 [[Glimmering sandpaper]]
      - You can buy fuel from [[Trader Whitlock]] on the highland camp.
1. Return to [[Ellerith Groundspark|Ellerith]]

## Rewards

- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- At least 21s 49c
