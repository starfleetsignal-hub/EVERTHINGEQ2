This quest is restricted to [[Beastlord|Beastlords]] only.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Elvann]], at {{waypoint -108, 14, 168}} who is next to Verita.
1. Kill 10 insects within [[The Feerrott]].
1. Find the voice, belonging to Nisivian the Swarmwatcher, at {{waypoint 68, 25, 913}} in the southwestern part of [[The Feerrott]].
1. Return to [[Elvann]].
1. Head to the druid rings, at {{waypoint -563.65, -14.78, 839.15}} in [[The Feerrott]].![Shalih Mar near Druid Rings](images/Shalih_Mar.png)
   - *Note:You can get there quickly by taking the spire to [[Enchanted Lands]] then using the [[Enchanted Lands]] druid ring back to [[The Feerrott]].*
1. Speak to [[Shalih Mar (Steamfont Mountains)|Shalih Mar]] at {{waypoint -572, -15, 829}}.
1. Use the spiritual pocket at {{waypoint -561, -15, 843}} to enter [[The Presence of Kaldrahir]].
   1. Click near the bottom of the portal if you're having trouble interacting with it.
1. Speak with [[Kaldrahir, the Pack Leader]].![Kaldrahir in spiritual pocket](images/Kaldrahir.png)
1. Find the three sigil stones. Each time you enter these zones use the [[Talisman of Kaldrahir]] you were given.
   1. Feerrott: {{waypoint -725, 3, 554}} at The Sentinel Stones
   1. Enchanted Lands: {{waypoint -557, 4, -736}} in Misty Grove (in the nymph area)
   1. Steamfont Mountains: {{waypoint -1113, 79, 943}} in the crater next to the Inoperative Clockwork
1. Return to [[Kaldrahir, the Pack Leader]].
   - *Note:Taking the druid ring from Steamfont to Feerrott is the quickest way back there.*
1. Speak to [[Kaldrahir, the Pack Leader]] after he does the ritual.
1. Take the druid ring to [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]].
1. Click the keystone at {{waypoint 223, 15, 114}}.
1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 226, 15, 118}} to enter the [[Prison of Thir'Slaa]].
1. Speak to [[Thir'Slaa, the Broodwatcher]] at {{waypoint 28, -7, -119}}.
1. Use the [[Will of Thir'Slaa]] on 6 insects or arachnids in [[The Feerrott]].
   1. You need to be in combat with the mobs to be able to use the Will.
   1. Make sure your warder and/or mercenaries are not assisting you as the Will has a long cast time.
1. Return to [[Prison of Thir'Slaa|Thir'Slaa's prison]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]].
1. Use the [[Will of the Hive]] on the insects that the Devourer spawns.
   - *Note:This has a long cast time and you may get overwhelmed, so just kill some if there's too many for you to successfully cast.*
1. Speak to [[Thir'Slaa, the Broodwatcher]] to complete the quest and gain your ability to tame insect warders.
