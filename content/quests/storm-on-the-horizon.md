1. Protect and rescue.
   - Kill 8 [[A Skirth gnasher|Skirth gnashers]] or [[An Orafic shrillglom|Orafic shrillgloms]] along the beach around {{waypoint 36, -49, -319}}.
   - Rescue 6 injured Wayward defenders.
1. Speak with [[Veris Windcall]].
