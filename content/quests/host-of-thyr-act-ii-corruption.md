## Steps

1. Slay the following monsters, whom are all found on the second level (the goblin level):
   - 20 [[A stone spider|stone spiders]]; starting at around {{waypoint -32.38, -123.49, 30.14}}
   - [[Gritok Fireclaw]] or his placeholder, [[A disciple of Fireclaw]]. {{waypoint -233, -131, 22}}
1. Return to Ognit.

## Rewards

- At least 8g 60s 47c
- [[Drednever scrying sphere]]
- +1,500 faction with **Drednever Expedition**
