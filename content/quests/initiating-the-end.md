## Notes

- This is the second [[Aerakyn Mentors in Norrath|Aerakyn mentor]] quest in [[Qeynos]].

## Steps

1. Enter [[The House of Endings]] {{waypoint 645, -18, 205}}. ![The Door for the House of Endings](images/The_House_of_Endings_Door.png)
1. Speak to [[Errkesh]], right inside on the left.
1. Speak to [[Gren Bluesong]] down the stairs {{waypoint 7, -5, -19}}.
1. Enter [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum]] at {{waypoint 692, -20, 267}}. Mobs do **not** see through invisibility/stealth.
1. Gather 10 relics from [[A ghastly knight (Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum)|ghastly knights]] and [[A ghastly specter (Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum)|specters]].
1. ![Example of an Erudite-hidden relic location](images/Erudite_Relic_Location_Initiating_the_End.png)Gather 10 relics hidden by the Erudites. They appear as black/green stars rising from the ground.
   - Some locations:
     - {{waypoint 736, -20, 263}}
     - {{waypoint 743, -2, 307}}
     - {{waypoint 736, -24, 318}}
     - {{waypoint 823, -20, 277}}
     - {{waypoint 805, -20, 298}}
     - {{waypoint 813, -20, 299}}
     - {{waypoint 821, -20, 291}}
   - *Note:* The locations respawn. Some locations are safer for waiting in than others.
1. As you approach the exit back to Qeynos, speak to [[Kyth]] {{waypoint 706, -20, 264}}.
1. Return to the House of Endings, Qeynos Capitol District {{waypoint 645, -18, 205}}.
1. Speak to Gren again {{waypoint 7, -5, -19}}.
1. Step into the ring on the second floor {{waypoint 2, 5, 1}}. Gren will commence his speech.
1. Once the speech is complete, attempt to convince [[Sloish Jellysmots|Sloish]] to leave the Cult.

## Rewards

At least 20g 28s 20c at level 60.
