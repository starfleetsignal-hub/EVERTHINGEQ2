## Steps

1. ![Dogwood Shrub](images/Dogwood-shrub-displayed.jpg)Harvest 3 [[Dogwood Shrub|dogwood shrubs]] on bluffs nearby, near the Sapwill's Rest NPCs, and can be found with [[Tradeskill Alternate Advancement|the tradeskill tracking ability]]. The shrub is also an heirloom [[Decorating Houses|house item]] **ONLY** as long as you have this quest. (so stock up on them.)
1. Get [[Excavator's Gauze Bandages|strips of cloth]] from Gnorbert's supplies
1. Click Gnorbert's backpack for cloth {{waypoint 1117, 30, 1119}}, recipe for [[Healing Salve Infused Bandages]] autoscribes
1. Craft the bandages at a [[Chemistry Table]] (table in [[Sapswill's Rest]] {{waypoint 1229, 18, 1265}})
1. Talk to [[Gnorbert]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
