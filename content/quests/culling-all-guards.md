## Description

The ruins of Upper Guk are overrun with cursed froglok putrid shin warriors. The putrid shin warriors are defiling the once great city under the leadership of Lord Kurpep the Shin Lord. Enter Upper Guk and slay the putrid shin wrriors.

## Steps

1. Kill 5 putrid shin warriors within [[The Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen]]
1. Return to [[Vegupa Marrsheart]] in Firmroot and report my success.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
