## Notes

[[A History of Sultans]] and [[Curse and Tell]] must be completed before [[Ismail]] will speak to you.

## Steps

1. Take the lift at {{waypoint 85.00, 12.32, -274.94}} and re-enter [[Raj'Durabad]].
1. Speak to [[Ismail]] {{waypoint 12.64, 94.75, -429.20}}
   - complete the subquest [[The Threatening Truth]].
1. Speak to [[Ismail]] and finish the conversation where [[Zizzez]] takes part.  ( **Zissez** is a flying imp-like jester who appears beside Ismail when you begin the conversation.  I had to step back because he spawned inside me, and I couldn't find him.  He appears right beside Ismail. )
1. "Find out what's going on with the Sultan of Daggers"
   1. Enter the "Sultan's Mahallah", which is the the large tent at {{waypoint -304.34, 117.54, -117.97}}
   1. Speak to [[Aldys, Sultan of the Daggers]]
   1. Defeat [[Riddan]].
   1. Defeat [[Zizzez]].
   1. Defeat blue lantern (hanging from top of tent near Riddan)
   1. Pick up [[Riddan]].
1. Return to [[Jawdah the Mystic]]{{waypoint 177.44, -60.15, 458.47}}
1. Speak to [[Nouzar]] {{waypoint 178.83, -60.15, 462.84}}
   - Subquest [[Raj'Durabad Feeling About This]].
1. Speak to [[Nouzar]] and [[Jawdah the Mystic]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 57791 status
- One of the following:
  - [[Hizite Hoop of Sanctified Lead]]
  - [[Resilient Viridsilk Cloak]]
