## Steps

1. Fly to [[The Cloudmist Isle]] and summon [[Chesem Sharpeye]] {{waypoint -593, 244, -193}} with your pipe reed. Speak to him when he arrives.
1. Collect three [[Mist Essence|Mist Essences]] from the corpses of [[A cloudmist walker|cloudmist walkers]] on the island.
1. Harvest three 'Trapped Wind' from around {{waypoint -597, 248, -145}}, which will automatically turn into [[A Sentient Wind Essence]] in your inventory.
1. Go to [[The Isle of Eaglewatch]] and run over the indicated locations on the western, eastern, and southern edges of the island for automatic updates. The locations are obvious, indicated by a small cloud on the ground and a faint rainbow above them.
1. Return to Chesem on Cloudmist Isle. Summon him again if necessary.
1. Return to [[Harun Clearsky|Harun Clearskyon]] [[The Whisperwind Isle (POI)|The Whisperwind Isle]]. Summon him with your pipe reed if necessary. Speak to him to complete the quest.
