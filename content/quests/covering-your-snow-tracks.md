## Notes

- This quest can be completed in either the Easy or Hard version of [[The Icy Keep]].
- For a guide on how to move about the zone, see [[It's Time to Sleigh the Ice Dragon!]].
- You don't need your [[Ice Axe of Vivid Hues]] to break the icy walls, this can be done with any weapon.
- If you leave the instance for any reason, all quest progress will be lost.

## Steps

1. You must kill anything that might know about you letting the dragon escape!
   - Find, unfreeze, and then kill 10 [[A partially thawed goblin|partially thawed goblins]].
     - Click on a frozen goblin to thaw them out. There are more than 10.
   - Defeat the egg guardians:
     1. [[Gate Captain H'Bri]]
     1. [[Tamer Nicoli]]
     1. [[Kastus]]
        - Once defeated you can break the wall in the western tower.
     1. [[Odaufe]]
     1. [[Ice Maiden D'Ina (The Icy Keep)|Ice Maiden D'Ina]]
        - After D'Ina is dead, destroy the egg to spawn [[A Hatched Ice Dragon]]. It will stun you and escape. Once you are no longer stunned, click on the egg shards again and [[Snarf Frostfoot (The Icy Keep)|Snarf Frostfoot]] will spawn run towards you. Speak to him to teleport back to [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]].
