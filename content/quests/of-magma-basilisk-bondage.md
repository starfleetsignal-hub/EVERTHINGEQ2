## Description

Pallasa Rargon, a member of the Order of Flame, a remnant of the ancient order that once inhabited the temple of Solusek Ro, welcomed my help in their ancient crusade against the invading Void army. She is alarmed that the Void have found a way to charm the local fauna and use them against us. She asked that I hunt the magma basilisks that have been enslaved by the invading forces.

## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[Enslaved magma basilisk|enslaved magma basilisks]], *grouped with the lacerators* Kill the Lacerators and the Basilisks automatically die giving you the update, killing only the Basilisks is not enough for an update.
   - *Note: which drop the void lattices you need for step 2 of [[Cleansing the Elements]]*
   - *Note: Killing Lacerators should automatically kill magma basilisk.*
1. Return to Pallasa.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g
- +2250 faction with **Order of Flame**
