## Steps

1. Speak to [[A Vah Shir memory]] in the [[Ruins of Shar Makhi]] {{waypoint -136, 85, -691}}.
   - ![a Vah Shir memory](images/A_Vah_Shir_memory.jpg)
1. Hunt 8 [[A moor phantom|moor phantoms]] for Vah Shir tooth trophies in [[Hollowshade Moor]] around {{waypoint -261, 10, -173}}.
   - *Kill all the other mobs in the area and the phantoms will spawn.*
   - ![a moor phantom](images/A_moor_phantom.jpg)
1. Return to the Vah Shir memory {{waypoint -136, 85, -691}}.
1. The [[A Vah Shir memory|Vah Shir memory]] is gone, speak with the [[A grimling memory|grimling memory]].
   - ![A grimling memory](images/A_grimling_memory.jpg)

## Rewards

- [[Grimling Bone Fixture]]
- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
