The Throne of Storms is where King Tormax the Ancient has held court for hundreds of years. To protect him from his many enemies Tormax has filled his court with legions of his most loyal protectors. Should the Ragebourne have any chance to discover the secret to the ancient king's near immortality, his protectors must be dealt with.
