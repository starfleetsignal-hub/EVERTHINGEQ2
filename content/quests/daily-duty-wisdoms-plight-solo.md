## Steps

Today, the Zakir Rish of Zimara Breadth will endure what the Sang'Huuu have endured since the invasion began, and perhaps they will think twice before starting wars with those who have me as an ally.

1. Enter [[Zimara Breadth: Wisdom's Plight (Solo)]] in [[Zenith Helionae]] at {{waypoint 310.64, 166.60, -80.73}}
1. Eliminate [[Psamtic the Sour]] the Efreeti Conjurer at {{waypoint -691.41, 143.72, -8.11}}
1. Execute [[Kosrow Al'Vaz]] the Iron Enforcer moves about {{waypoint -567.56, 139.73, 277.22}}
1. Break [[Glisterfist Prime]] the Metallic Invader at {{waypoint -803.14, 189.97, 245.52}}
   1. Kill the [[Russet Invader]]
   1. Kill the [[Ferrous Invader]]
   1. Kill [[Glisterfist Prime]]
      - Will spawn [[A ferrous invader|Ferrous Invaders]]
