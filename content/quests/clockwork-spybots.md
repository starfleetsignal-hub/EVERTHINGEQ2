## Steps

1. Scribe [[Blueprint: Tinkered Pirate Spybot|Blueprint: Tinkered Pirate Spybot;]]
1. Craft a [[Tinkered Pirate Spybot]] at the work bench. You will need:
   - 1 [[Titanium ore]]
   - 1 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
   - 1 [[Amber]]
   - 1 [[Ethereal coal]]
1. Head up the elevator and out to the barricades and release the Spybot around {{waypoint 641, -380, 965}}; if that location does not work, try {{waypoint 753.59, -376.48, 855.93}}
1. Return to Fenwick. (1295, -540, 471)

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +2,000 faction with **Iceclad Pirates**
