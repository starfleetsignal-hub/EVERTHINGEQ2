## Description

The spirits of Castle Valmarr haunt their former outpost in hopes that someone will release them from the curse of Gynok Moltor. In an attempt to finally put to rest the awful events which occurred so long ago, I've been tasked to retrieve the remains of the fallen pages that were slain the night of Gynok's massacre.

## Steps

1. Enter the [[Befallen: Cavern of the Afflicted]] and retrieve the remains of 10 fallen pages. These are simple clickable skeleton shaped ground spawns [[A slain pages remains]].  ***NOTE:  Skeleton click gives a GROUP UPDATE***
   - {{waypoint 20.43, 0.00, -29.25}} -- top level, behind the lion statue
   - {{waypoint 27.50, -1.43, -15.70}} -- top level, just outside Edgrim's room
   - {{waypoint 88.52, -36.52, -44.81}} -- bottom level, north part of Well Room area
   - {{waypoint 95.84, -41.11, -54.58}} -- bottom level, just NW of previous remains
   - {{waypoint 155.93, -51.25, -20.31}} -- bottom level, The Carrion Nest area (center of room)
   - {{waypoint 91.63, -57, -108}} -- bottom level, ramp down to Bonegrinder/Armory (under a girder)
   - {{waypoint 64.74, -60.08, -110.88}} -- bottom level, ramp down to Bonegrinder/Armory (in depression)
   - {{waypoint 79.20, -64.43, -170.41}} -- bottom level, in hallway to Lair of the Bonegrinder
   - {{waypoint 97.52, -64.21, -168.39}} -- bottom level, just outside the Lair of the Bonegrinder, in shadows of the wall on right
   - {{waypoint 135.57, -72.19, -144.98}} -- bottom level, Lair of the Bonegrinder, south side near chest
1. Return to Squire Hadley.
