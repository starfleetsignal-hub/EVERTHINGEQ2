## Info

The quest description for this quest is bugged. You are not supposed to look for the Bixies north of the spires, but rather near the Opal Pond near the Old Kelethin Acorn.

## Description

"This Sniffwhistle is a smart cookie. He wants me to gather Trebuz Bixie parts so he can create a cognatic bixie bopping machine." -- *Everquest II Journal.*

## Steps

1. Slay Trebuz bixies around {{waypoint 192, 26, 219}} until you find:
   1. a Trebuz bixie head
   1. a Trebuz bixie body
   1. 2 Trebuz bixie legs
   1. 2 Trebuz bixie wings
   - Seems any Trebuz bixie will do ( a Trebuz stinger, a Trebuz soldier, etc...)
1. Return to [[Grimply Sniffwistle]]

## Rewards

- At least 92c
- Experience

## Journal Steps

Grimply needs some parts from the Trebuz bixies to create his machine. Their hives are located in the Valley of the Great Swarm. I need to find a Trebuz bixie head. I need to find a Trebuz bixie body. I need to find some Trebuz bixie legs. I need to find some Trebuz bixie wings.

I found a Trebuz bixie head. I found a Trebuz bixie body. I found some Trebuz bixie legs. I found some Trebuz bixie wings. I gathered the parts for Grimply's machine. Now that I have the Trebuz bixie parts, I should take them to Grimply, at Spire Shadow Outpost. I should take these bixie parts to Grimply.

I gave the Grimply the bixie parts. I have given Grimply all of the bixie parts I gathered.

I helped Grimply with his cognatic bixie bopping machine.
