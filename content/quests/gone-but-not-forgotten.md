## Steps

1. Enter [[Scale Yard: Traitors' End]] {{waypoint -65, -25, 283}}
1. Speak to [[Zillik, the Overseer]] at the zone-in
1. Fight 8 prisoners or rogue informants (dressed all in orange) into submission (even con). They will then follow you to the zone-in {{waypoint 0, -5, 0}}
   - You can have only one beaten prisoner in tow at any time. Attempting to beat another prisoner will kill it
1. Speak to Zillik
1. Speak to Micha in the North Freeport Academy of Arcane Science.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
