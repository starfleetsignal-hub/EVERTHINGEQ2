## Steps

You receive [[Wand of Nature's Binding]] at the beginning of the quest

1. Use the Wand of Nature's Binding on 11 [[A risen Mucktail|risen Mucktails]] and then kill them.
1. Return to Mibton Biddleleaf

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Fairwind Chain Bracers]] (Chain Forearms)
  - [[Fairwind Bracers (QuestReward)|Fairwind Bracers]] (Cloth Forearms)
  - [[Fairwind Leather Bracers]] (Leather Forearms)
  - [[Fairwind Plate Bracers]] (Plate Forearms)
