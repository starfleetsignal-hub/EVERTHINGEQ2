## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 20+

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Clerk Feld]] in [[The Qeynos Hold]] bank in [[North Qeynos]] to find out why he set you up to be killed. {{waypoint 276, -18, -116}}
1. Find and enter the apartment of Feld's Bloodsaber contact at 3 Tranquil Lane (the easternmost apartment) in [[South Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 478, -20, 265}}
1. Slay the Bloodsaber assassins in the apartment, then locate the body of Feld's contact upstairs. {{waypoint -3, 5, -2}}
1. Return to [[Rinna]]. ![Body of Feld's contact](images/Body_of_Feld's_contact.png)
1. Speak with [[Investigator Keklem]] outside the [[Harbor Cache]] in [[Qeynos Harbor]]. {{waypoint 781, -21, -34}}
1. Return to [[Rinna]], but you will find [[Guard Scheln]] waiting for you instead.
1. Talk to Harkam Nubbytoe, the Halfling standing nearby to find out about Rinna.

## Rewards

- XP only
