## Steps

1. Use the [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] entrance in [[Zone|City of Shar Vahl]] at {{waypoint -137.16, 31.74, 243.09}} and select [[Shadeweaver's Thicket: Untamed Lands (Solo)]].![ZoneIn Midst Mission: Untamed Lands](images/ZoneIn_Midst_Mission-_Untamed_Lands.jpg)
1. Free the spirit: [[Spiritist Karina]] on the [[Plateau of Broken Spirits]] {{waypoint 175.86, 57.69, -554.19}}.
1. Dispatch the phantom: [[The Desolator]] on [[The Desolate Grounds (Untamed Lands)]] {{waypoint 376.99, 17.74, -550.60}}.

## Rewards

- 1 [[Bazaar Crown]]
- [[Crown Receptacle (Tier 5)]]
- At least 2p 99g 37s 67,200 status
