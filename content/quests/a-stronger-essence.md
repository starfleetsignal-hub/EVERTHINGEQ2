## Notes

- If you are doing this quest at a higher level, use lower rank spells or combat arts to avoid one-shoting the mobs. If the mobs are grey-conned to you, consider using [[Methodical Strikes]].

## Steps

1. Use [[Meta-Siphon]] on the following mobs in the [[Ruins of Verelisk]] {{waypoint 1918.52, 39.42, 267.43}}. The meta-siphon can only work after the mobs have been reduced to 25% or less health:
   - 5 [[A fleshless guard|fleshless guards]], [[A rotting sathirian guard|rotting sathirian guards]], [[A putrid sathirian sentry|putrid sathirian sentries]] or [[A Shadow Lord attendant|Shadow Lord attendants]].
   - 5 [[A slain historian|slain historians]] {{waypoint 2055.95, 74.83, 160.39}}
   - 5 [[A rotting sathirian priest|rotting sathirian priests]] or [[A slain devout|slain devouts]]
1. Return to [[Meileimeileemtem Jeliimtieoileom]].

## Rewards

- At least 10s 18c
