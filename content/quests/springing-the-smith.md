## Steps

1. Find a way to safely collect a lump of Warsmelt Iron.
   1. Enter the [[Spire of Rage]]
   1. Defeat [[Hamslammer]] for a pair of gloves to safety handle the Warsmelt Iron
   1. Collect the Warsmelt Iron in the forge at {{waypoint -3, 164, -51}}
1. Return to [[Muram Bladebender]] in [[The Fortress of Drunder]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 1g
- [[War boar of Drunder]]
