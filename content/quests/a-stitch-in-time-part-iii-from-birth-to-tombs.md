## Notes

- The combines for this timeline are long. Without potions, some of them can take 2-3 minutes each. Plan accordingly.
- The zone timeout is 18 Hours.

## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 25 [[Celestial Coal]]
- 50 [[Celestial Kindling]]

## Steps

1. Enter [[Solusek Ro's Tower: From the Ashes (Tradeskill)|Solusek Ro's Tower: From the Ashes [Tradeskill]]] at {{waypoint 95, 3, -165}} in [[Coliseum of Valor]].
1. Move to {{waypoint 0, -7, -42}}.
1. Speak with The Immolated Lexicon at {{waypoint 0, -5, -99}}.![The Immolated Lexicon](images/The_Immolated_Lexicon.jpg)
   - A floating tome on the dais.
   - use the Summon Immolated Lexicon ability.
1. Click on and translate the runes on the walls in the room and side rooms.  Continue clicking until you get the recipe.![Translate Runes](images/Translate_Runes.jpg)
   - *Some locations: {{waypoint 16, -7, -108}} {{waypoint 5, -7, -106}} {{waypoint -5, -7, -106}} {{waypoint -15, -7, -108}}*
   - After translating the last rune, you receive [[Recipe: Obsidian Incubation Vessel]] (auto-scribed)
1. Collect the following:
   - 12 [[Condensed magma]] from lava pools.![a pool of condensed magma](images/A_pool_of_condensed_magma.jpg)
     - *They are found in the area near the entrance and at {{waypoint 133, 110, -81}}. There are only three nodes in the entrance area, but they respawn quite quickly. Just go get your mephit poop and come back.*
     - *They can also appear in the lava pool where the phoenix spawns {{waypoint 139, 110, -81}} .*
   - 6 [[Sulphur ash]] shed by scavenging mephits ![Sulphur Shed](images/Sulphur_Shed.jpg)
     - *Go through the doors to the east at {{waypoint -100, -6, -102}}.*
     - *The hostile Mephits periodically spray a cone of fire, leaving a small heap of burning sulfur on the floor that you can harvest.*
     - *Harvesting the Sulphur Shed damages you.*
     - *Don't get too close to the mephits, for they can and will kill you.*
   - 15 [[Obsidian shard|obsidian shards]] from fallen drakes.
     - *Click an emberscale flamewing to mine them for their 3 shards.*![an emberscale flamewing](images/An_emberscale_flamewing.jpg)
     - * They can be found in the side rooms downstairs, but there will only be 9 shards, plus aggro from the mephits.*
     - * Click on the brazier at {{waypoint -136, -8, -102}} and teleport to The Burning Roost. After teleporting, follow the hallways north and east to a big room with dead drakes and giants. Mine all the drake shards needed here.*
1. Next you have to craft the incubation vessel.
   - For this you need the Wall of Elemental Fire in [[The Infernal Chamber]].
   - From The Burning Roost (big room with the cauldron) just click the brazier at {{waypoint 196, 103, -94}} to teleport.
   - Go to the Wall of Elemental Fire on the other side of the room, avoiding the grates!![Wall of Elemental Fire (crafting station)](images/Wall_of_Elemental_Fire.jpg)
   - Now 'just' click on the appropriate section of the Wall of Elemental Fire to do the combines. *(Don't click on the ground or base)*
1. Fire the incubation vessel in the spouts of flame in Arlyxir's room:
   - To do this you need to stand on one of the grates (e.g. {{waypoint 0, 102, -80}}) in the middle as it lights up. Repeat four times.
   - Each firing will throw you into the air, and you will take massive (~80-90%) damage. Heal as needed.
     - *Unless you happen to be naked, in which case you may not take any damage...*
   - Warning: Don't get knocked into the flame walls or lava, you'll die.
1. Collect Arlyxir's Ashes within the newly created vessel. Harvest from pile of glowing ashes in center of Arlyxir's room, at {{waypoint 10, 105, -81}}.![Arlyxir's Ashes](images/Arlyxir's_Ashes.jpg)
   - It is a very **long** (and *amusing*) harvest.
1. Use the brazier at {{waypoint 0, 103, -123}} to come back to the room where the cauldron stands in the middle (The Burning Roost room).
   - Go up the flaming ramp (no damage) on the West side of the cauldron and stand on the rim. Click in the middle to place the collected Arlyxir's Ashes.![Place Incubation Vessel](images/Place_Incubation_Vessel.jpg)
   - If you notice when you get close to the sparks it says they flee from you. What you are supposed to do here is run around outside the cauldron on the floor herding the sparks into the cauldron so that when they flee from you, they collide in it. You do this right you won't be anywhere near the cauldron when a baby Phoenix emerges and will receive no damage at all. So, you just chase them in.
   - Once the baby Phoenix has been born run up to the rim of the cauldron and empower the seal.
   - Run away. (if you stay too close, you'll be eaten by the now hungry phoenix).![Arlyxir -hungry Phoenix w/ 2,974,858,550,912 hitpoints.](images/Arlyxir.jpg)
1. Exit the zone.
1. Back in [[Coliseum of Valor]] enter the [[Plane of Disease: Crypt of Decay (Tradeskill)|Plane of Disease: Crypt of Decay [Tradeskill]]] through the portal at {{waypoint -191, 3, 0}}.
1. Slide the coffin lid at {{waypoint 320, -475, -75}}. ![Coffin Lid](images/Coffin_Lid.jpg)
   - Collect the shovel and [[Embalming kit]] from inside the coffin. ![Shovel & Embalming Kit](images/Embalming_Kit.jpg)
   - *Optional: Take the note to receive the house item [[A scrawled note]].*![a scrawled note - house item](images/A_scrawled_note_%28A_Stitch_in_Time%29.jpg)
   - Suggestion: Set graphics to **Extreme Performance** to mostly eliminate the screen splashes.
1. Collect the following:
   - 6 Plaguebringer protoplasm.
     - Use the embalming kit rapidly as a pillbug approaches, it will stop moving and get larger. Keep embalming until it explodes to a protoplasm. ![pillbug safe spot-no throwback!](images/Pillbug_safespot.jpg)
     - ***NOTE:** There may be a bug where the Plaguebringer Bugs won't grow or explode!*
     - by clearing to a corner of the [[Pillbug]] path will allow one to kill them without being thrown-back/stifled.
   - 15 dormant pathogens.
     - Click on the large cubes to Clean Waste twice, and collect a pathogen (not all cubes validate this step).
   - 6 blighted marrow.
     - Mine an Amalgamation of Decay and an Amalgamation of Pestilence for marrow found at random locations on floor in Pedestal of Pestilence room; {{waypoint -49, -483, -111}},{{waypoint -51, -483, -73}}, etc..![Amalgamation of Decay, et al.](images/Amalgamation_of_Decay.jpg)
1. Craft an Empower the Seal of Bertoxxulous in the Cauldron of Contagion at {{waypoint -11, -473, -92}}.![Cauldron of Contagion](images/Cauldron_of_Contagion.jpg)
   - Requires:
     - 1 [[Inert seal of power (A Stitch in Time)|inert seal of power]]
     - 6 [[Plaguebringer's protoplasm]]
     - 15 [[A dormant pathogen]]
     - 6 [[Blighted marrow]]
     - 50 [[Celestial Kindling]]
1. Return to [[Varig Ro]] in the [[Coliseum of Valor]] to finish the quest and get major tradeskill experience.
