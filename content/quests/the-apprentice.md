## Steps

1. Felderham gives you a [[Geo-Arcanic Tremor Stake]] and instructs you to drive it into the ground before the gates of [[Kaladim]].
   - NOTE: Felderham has the nasty habit of not always giving you the stake at first.  You may need to hail him again before proceeding to this step.
   - As you approach the trees where the Irontoe Brigade guards are near {{waypoint -176, 151, -444}}, the stake will begin to hum. Plant it there by right-clicking the item and selecting 'Use'.
1. Defeat [[Feagon Jornstoff (Mob)|Feagon Jornstoff]] and his employer, a [[Ratonga]] Serilian named [[Xvithith Gik]].
1. Return to Hamish. {{waypoint -288, 150, 196}}
