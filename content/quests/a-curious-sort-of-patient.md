## Prerequisite

- You must be adventure level 78 to be offered this quest.

## Steps

1. Inspect the trees in Innothule Swamp
   - *Note: If you have a flying mount you can avoid fights by landing on the trees.*
   1. Collect bark from the third diseased tree {{waypoint -138, -84, -211}}
   1. Collect bark from the second diseased tree {{waypoint 49, -78, 110}}
   1. Collect bark from the first diseased tree {{waypoint 143, -87, -189}}
   1. Collect bark from the fourth diseased tree {{waypoint 221, -85, -567}}
1. Return to [[Merza Valorarm]]

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2,000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
