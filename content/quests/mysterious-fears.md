## Steps

1. I need to locate the caves deep within [[Dread Basin]] where the beasts reside.
   - Go to the [[Cave of Fear]] at  {{waypoint -1936, -16, 515}}
1. Kill creatures inside the cave until you get [[A gem of terror]]. You will not receive the "you did not find any quest items" message as you kill the mobs; you only get a message once you get the gem.
1. Examine [[A gem of terror|gem of terror]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 99s 87c
