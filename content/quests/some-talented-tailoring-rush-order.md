## Steps

Create 6 items from the random list below:

- [[Sandcloth Cuffs (Level 50) (Crafted)|Sandcloth Cuffs]]
- [[Sandcloth Shawl (Level 50) (Crafted)|Sandcloth Shawl]]
- [[Stonehide Leather Bracers]]
- [[Stonehide Leather Shoulder Pads]]
- [[Stonehide Leather Bandolier (Level 50) (Crafted)|Stonehide Leather Bandolier]]
- [[Sandcloth Mitts (Level 50) (Crafted)|Sandcloth Mitts]]
- [[Sandcloth Pantaloons (Level 50) (Crafted)|Sandcloth Pantaloons]]
- [[Stonehide Leather Gloves]]
- [[Stonehide Leather Pants]]
- [[Stonehide Leather Satchel (Level 50) (Crafted)|Stonehide Leather Satchel]]
- [[Sandcloth Robe]]
- [[Stonehide Leather Tunic]]
- [[Pristine stonehide leather backpack|Stonehide Leather Backpack]]
- [[Stonehide Leather Pouch (Level 50) (Crafted)|Stonehide Leather Pouch]]
- [[Pristine stonehide leather quiver|Stonehide Leather Quiver]]
- [[Pristine stonehide leather sack|Stonehide Leather Sack]]
- [[Stonehide Leather Sheath (Level 50) (Crafted)|Stonehide Leather Sheath]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 2g 43s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
