## Steps

1. Kill 10 yellow faeries near the [[Lost Village of Bobick]].
   - [[A mischievous maiden|Mischievous maidens]] and [[A very mischievous maiden|very mischievous maidens]] do NOT cause faction hits with The Seamist Faeries, but any fairy with Seamist in its name does.
1. Return to [[Gubbo Chaley]]

## Rewards

- At least 27s 67c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Fae Dusted Bracelet of Acumen]] (All Mages)
  - [[Fae Dusted Bracelet of Alacrity]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Fae Dusted Bracelet of Piety]] (All Priests)
  - [[Fae Dusted Bracelet of Resolve]] (All Fighters)

## Notes

- The entire dialog of the quest giver is almost taken verbatim from the Ralph Bakshi animated film "Wizards". In the movie, there is a scene in which an evil soldier finds his 'dead' friend and proceeds on a tirade about fairies before his friend wakes up. He then goes on a tirade about his friend ruining his career and life before accidentally shooting his friend, which he blames on the fairies and which also leads to a repeat of his previous tirade. It closes with the soldier being court martialed.
