## Steps

1. Obtain the names of the gift recipients:
   - In [[North Qeynos (POI)|North Qeynos]]:
     - [[Gemellus Sperasius]] down at the water's edge near the bridge into the castle {{waypoint 420, -27, -47}}
     - [[Nonnie Runtuftle]] inside the furniture store {{waypoint 337, -21, -94}}
   - In [[The Elddar Grove (POI)|The Elddar Grove]]:
     - [[Linst Tincobble]] by the archery targets {{waypoint 550, -17, -419}}
     - [[Paavali Dewshine]] in the zen garden {{waypoint 739, -21, -393}}
     - [[Thabit Kalila]] inside the treehouse of [[Arbos, the Elddar Tree]] {{waypoint 653, 11, -389}}
     - [[Gowan Macgillivray]] inside the [[Antonica's House of Pottery]] {{waypoint 714, -17, -315}}
1. Return to G'shugaplum Gigglegibber. Make sure to have six open slots in your inventory!
1. Hand out the gifts:
   - **WARNING: You must give the correct gift to the correct person, or you will have to start the entire quest over!**
   - In North Qeynos:
     - [[Turlif whizgig]] to Gemellus Sperasius {{waypoint 420, -27, -47}}
     - [[Faydwer jewel beetle]] to Nonnie Runtuftle {{waypoint 337, -21, -94}}
   - In The Elddar Grove:
     - [[Beard trimming kit]] to Linst Tincobble {{waypoint 550, -17, -419}}
     - [[Feir'Dal tattoo kit]] to Paavali Dewshine {{waypoint 739, -21, -393}}
     - [[Jumjum pie]] to Thabit Kalila {{waypoint 653, 11, -389}}
     - [[Tray of Gigglegibber cookies]] to Gowan Macgillivray {{waypoint 714, -17, -315}}
1. Return to G'Shugahplum Gigglegibber.

## Rewards

- The ability to set different kinds of lights on your [[Frostfell Tree]].
