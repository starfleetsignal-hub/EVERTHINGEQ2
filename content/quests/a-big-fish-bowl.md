## Steps

1. Kill swordfish and sharks in [[Jarsath Wastes]] in the water between [[Danak Shipyard]] and [[Chelsith]]
   - Kill 50 swordfish in [[Jarsath Wastes]]
   - Kill 50 sharks in [[Jarsath Wastes]]
1. Return to the [[Fens of Nathsar]] to click on the big bucket again.

## Rewards

- A giant (Granitegrip) will walk down the beach very slowly towards the full bucket and attack you. The giant can be between 80^^^ and 85^^^. *needed for [[The Broken Fist]]*
