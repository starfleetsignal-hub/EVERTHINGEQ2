## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The avatar summoning rituals require the manipulations of immense power and are inherently dangerous. To reduce the chance of failure and provide an anchor for that power, the herald of Bertoxxulous has asked me to collect a long lost item that may have once belonged to that god or one of their most loyal followers.

- Collect [[Reliquary of Bertoxxulous|Reliquaries of Bertoxxulous]] from a powerful creature in the Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion heroic dungeon.

**This updates only in Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion [Event Heroic]**

- Return to the [[Herald of the Plaguelord]] {{waypoint -1157.19, -33.96, 525.38}} in [[Antonica]]
