This quest is part 12 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Slay some [[Qeynos|Qeynosian]] explorers. You will not lose any faction for killing them. They are found at the [[Qeynosian Scout Camp]] beside [[Behemoth Pond]]. {{waypoint -270, 0, -270}}
   - 3 [[A wood elf scout|wood elf scouts]]
   - 2 [[A halfling explorer|halfling explorers]]
   - 3 [[A high elf planetologist|high elf planetologists]]
   - **Note**: These all place-hold for each other. If you don't see the mobs you need, kill the others and hope for a good re-pop!
   - *Note* While at the Qeynosian camp, talk to [[Vanquisher Sharhan]] to get the quest [[Necromantic Blood Runes]].
1. Return to Jaeta for your reward.
1. Read the book given as the reward, [[Why We Fight, Chronicle One (Quest Starter)|Why We Fight, Chronicle One]], to continue the quest series

## Rewards

- At least 25s 70c
- [[Why We Fight, Chronicle One (Quest Starter)|Why We Fight, Chronicle One]]
