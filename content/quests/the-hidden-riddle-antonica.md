*This quest is also part of the [[Antonica Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Click on a bookcase in [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] {{waypoint -60, 4, 8}} and choose 'Conflict and Harmony'
1. Get autoupdates by killing:
   - [[A Varsoon apprentice|Varsoon apprentice]]s
     - These can be found next to the bookcase, or in [[The Thundering Steppes]] around the grate-entrance to *The Ruins of Varsoon* at around {{waypoint -651, -4, 757}}.
   - [[An incomplete guardian|incomplete guardian]]s in [[The Creator]]'s room  at {{waypoint 118, 0, 104}}.
1. Click the bookcase, choose 'Conflict and Harmony' and use the keys.
1. Return to Rikantus.

## Rewards

- At least 36s 13c
