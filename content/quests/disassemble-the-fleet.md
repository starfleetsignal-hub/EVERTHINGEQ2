## Steps

Zone into [[Brokenskull Bay: Hoist the Yellow Jack (Heroic)]]

- Kill the pirate lookout
- Murder the medic
- Annihilate the navigator
- Defeat the torturer
- Kill the Brokenskull captain

## Rewards

- At least 2p 16g 20,000 status
- 15 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- At least one of the following
  - Crate of Armor (Chain/Cloth/Leather/Plate) depending on class
