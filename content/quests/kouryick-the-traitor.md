## Steps

1. Head to East Freeport and meet Kouryick in the Seafarer's Roost. Speak with him in his drunken state and agree to meet somewhere private after he finishes his drink.
1. Head over to Longshadow Alley where Kouryick and Captain Rillian will spawn in the alley leading from the dock.
1. Speak to Captain Rillian first, then to Kouryick, who will realize he has been betrayed and attack you.
1. Fight back until you "knock Kouryick out".
