## Steps

1. Speak to [[Dela]] in [[Overlord's Plaza]].
1. Speak to [[Eaneal]] by [[The Execution Plaza]].
1. Speak to [[Ullon]] by the docks in East Freeport.
1. Return to Derald Wallson
1. Locate traitors in [[West Freeport (POI)|West Freeport]] and turn them into the Freeport Militia
   - While walking around, you will be attacked by [[A conspiring citizen]] (level scales solo guardians).  At under 20% health they surrender.  Arrest them by talking to them and selecting "You're under arrest. I'm taking you to the Freeport Militia!"  Walk them up to [[Captain Clausin Bludwins]]. You don't need to speak to Clausin - the quest updates automatically when you're close enough.
1. Return to Derald Wallson.

## Rewards

- At least 10g 97c @ lvl 56
