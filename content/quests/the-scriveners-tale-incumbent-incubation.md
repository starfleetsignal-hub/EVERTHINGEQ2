## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 250 [[Celestial Sandpaper]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Collect a strange glowing red shard at {{waypoint 182, 33, 131}}.![strange glowing red shard](images/Strange_glowing_red_shard.jpg)
   - Group-update! Auto-followers will... continue to auto-follow to ported spot.
1. Speak with [[Zuvet]].
1. Harvest the following within [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]]:
   - 50 [[Dewpalm lumber]] from [[Fallen dewpalm branch]]
   - 75 [[Nimbic hide]] from [[Basilisk lair]]
   - 60 [[Strangler root]] from [[Celestial nursery]] or [[Strangler root break]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Crafted Cinder Beds]] and craft 5 [[Crafted Cinder Beds]] on a [[Woodworking Table]].
   - There is a [[Woodworking Table]] in at the zone in {{waypoint 15, 6, 108}}
   - There is a [[Woodworking Table]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]]. Go to the [[South Wing Catalog]] and go through the teleporter to the [[Smiths' Gallery]] at {{waypoint 388, 16, 170}} *Note: Mass production does NOT work.
1. Place the beds in [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]]. ![Crafted Cinder Bed placement](images/Crafted_Cinder_Bed_placement.jpg)Some possible locations:
   - {{waypoint 182, 33, 131}}
   - {{waypoint 164.46, 40.92, 49.58}}
   - {{waypoint 114.15, 22.32, 306.71}}
   - {{waypoint 202, 34, 216}}
   - {{waypoint 129, 37, 257}}
   - {{waypoint 113, 18, 180}}
   - {{waypoint 74, 17, 220}}
   - Not Group-wide update, spot also becomes unusable until bed despawns (approx. 1m).
1. Speak with [[Zuvet]] at {{waypoint 167, 43, 74}}.

## Rewards

- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 47000 status
- [[Mourndax V'Zeaar]] - House Item
