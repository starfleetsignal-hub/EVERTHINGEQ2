## Notes

*You must be level 78 to receive this quest.*

## Steps

1. Visit the sites of the ancestors of the [[Moors of Ykesha]]
   - Go to the top of the waterfall outside [[Tupta]] {{waypoint -94, -91, 443}} ([[Tiki]])
   - Go to the entrance of the path leading to [[Grobb]] {{waypoint -158, -82, -531}} ([[Wik]])
   - Go to the base of the path leading to Guk {{waypoint 311, -91, -222}} ([[Zul]])
   - Go to the stone ruins under [[Firmroot Moot]] {{waypoint 136, -88, -500}} ([[Mog]])
1. Return to [[Tiptup Strongheart|Tiptup]] in [[Tupta]]

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2,000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
