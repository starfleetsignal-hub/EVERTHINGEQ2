## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks:
   - Kill 10 T'Rethir vampires in [[Nerian Highlands]] (thinbloods do not count). {{waypoint -253, 4, -305}}
   - Gather *a blood-scrawled note* on a chest underground at Multiple locations {{waypoint -250, 1, -445}} or {{waypoint -254.78, 2.38, -408.89}}. Can also spawn on the wooden desk in the middle of the lair.
   - Note: Each group member needs their own copy of the note. It takes about 5 minutes to spawn.
1. Return to Larxena.

- At least 1s 51c
- One of the following (based on class):

  - [[Scout's Wristlet]] (Scouts)
  - [[Priest's Wristlet]] (Priests)
  - [[Sorcerer's Wristlet]] (Mages)
