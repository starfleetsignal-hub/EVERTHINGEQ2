## Description

"Grimply Sniffwistle, a tinkering gnome at Spire Shadow Outpost, is determined to make a clockwork bixie complete with stinging capabilities. He charged me with obtaining the spider venom that the clockwork bixie will be injecting into its kobold targets." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[A huge spider|huge spiders]] found near {{waypoint -495, 41, 244}}
1. Return to Grimply

## Rewards

- At least 4s 4c
