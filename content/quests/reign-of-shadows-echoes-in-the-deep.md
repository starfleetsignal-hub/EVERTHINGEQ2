## Steps

1. Enter [[Echo Caverns]] by using the zone-in from the [[Luclin Nexus]] in the [[City of Fordel Midst]].
1. Speak with one of the miners, [[Saef Stennes]] {{waypoint 18, 158, -28}}.
1. Explore the [[Echo Caverns]]
   1. Search for [[The Chlorochamber]] {{waypoint 225, 158, 427}}.
   1. Head east to find the sounds of rushing water {{waypoint -297, 179, 339}}.
   1. Explore north of the [[Colossal Cascades]] {{waypoint -174, 218, 120}}.
   1. Find the source of the sounds of battle northeast of [[Tuatara Rim]] {{waypoint -425, 207, -58}}.
   1. Explore [[Shattered Chasm]] until you hear a voice {{waypoint -379, 136, -263}}
1. Find out who warned you about traveling there
   1. Seek out the source of an echoing voice in [[Shattered Chasm]] {{waypoint -88, 125, -189}}.
   1. Investigate the green shiny the mysterious tegi left behind {{waypoint -88, 125, -189}}![green shiny spot](images/Green_shiny_spot.jpg).
   1. Travel to the **Mystical Stone** {{waypoint -208, 115, -339}} and click it. ![Mystical Stone](images/Mystical_Stone.png)
   1. Step onto the **geomanced transport** that the stone unlocked {{waypoint -209, 115, -328}}![a geomanced transport](images/A_geomanced_transport.jpg).
1. Speak with [[Vinzerah Ral'Moor]] at the [[Hermit's Hideaway]] {{waypoint -187, 314, -321}}.
1. Find **The Scepter of Below**
   1. Take the geomanced transport back down and continue west to find the the city of [[Zel Manann]] {{waypoint 697, 17, -467}}. Find **The Scepter of Below** {{waypoint 752, 11, -498}} ![The Scepter of Below](images/The_Scepter_of_Below.png).
   1. click **The Scepter of Below**. *This will spawn [[A zelmie elite enforcer]].*
   1. Defeat [[A zelmie elite enforcer]].  If you die or miss the update, click the scepter again to spawn it again.  The other ones in the area will not count.
   1. Click the Scepter again.
1. Return to [[Vinzerah Ral'Moor]] take the transporter by {{waypoint -187, 313, -322}}.
1. Seek out [[Dolgan Inkeye]] in [[The Bazaar]] to help read the arcane map  {{waypoint -57.68, -1.47, 59.55}}.
