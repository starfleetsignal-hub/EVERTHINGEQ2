1. Enter [[Veksar: The Sunken Theater]] and slay 10 undead ([[A tortured sentry|tortured sentries]], [[A phantom sentry|phantom sentries]], or [[An undead petitioner|undead petitioners]]). {{waypoint -11, -3, -126}}
1. Return to Scholar Phinea in Omen's Call.
1. Speak to Scholar Phinea again after a brief moment.
