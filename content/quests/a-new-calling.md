## Steps

1. Speak with Rox, he will send you to Nipik
1. Search for [[Osh]] in the waters west of Fina's Retreat
   - Osh is in a sunken boat at {{waypoint -1140, -544, -2665}}
1. Kill the Undead ([[A destructive ice shade]], 87v, and 2 [[A destructive shadowbone skeleton]], 87vv)
1. Kill the [[A great bone lich]], 87^
1. Speak with Osh
1. You have 3 minutes to lead Osh back to Nipik at Fina's Shrine {{waypoint -1572, -537, -2380}}.  If you get too far from Osh you may leave him behind.  If you don't get Osh back to Nipik in 3 minutes, you must go back to the sunken boat and speak with him again.  (Flying him there didn't work).
1. Speak with Nipik ![Nipik's dropped cane in the snow](images/A_New_Calling_quest_-_Nipik's_dropped_cane.jpg)
1. Watch the ceremony.
1. Speak with Osh.
1. Find Nipik at {{waypoint -1632, -502, -2275}} and speak with him again.
1. Pick up the weapon Nipik dropped.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- +750 faction with **Othmir of Velious**
- [[Nipik's Cane (Weapon)]]
