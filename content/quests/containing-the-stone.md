According to my research, if a magical stone is set in metal that's empowered with the same type of magic as the stone it holds, it should be possible to attune the container so it hides the power of the stone within. I can do the forge work, but I'll need the help of the glaufaye for the magic.

## Steps

1. Exit the city to [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]] and discuss the recipe with [[Elanluelle]] {{waypoint -486.30, 239.76, 349.24}} in [[Zou'Lidelas]].
1. Return to [[Maldura]] and forge the [[Censer of Containment]] in the great [[Forge of Brell]] {{waypoint 68.02, -7.68, -131.54}}. (Note: Be careful not to miss any counters! They will kill you, put you back at entrance, and you will have to start over (rares not lost.)
1. Exit the city to [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]] and speak to [[Elanluelle]] at {{waypoint -486, 239, 349}} again:
   - *** Note: make sure you finish the conversation before you start to craft** the [[Empowered Censer of Containment]]! If you forge the item before getting instructions to do so, the quest will not update and you have to delete and start over and you have lost your 2 [[Arcannium]]!*
1. Click the Fire in front of Elanluele after the quest updates from speaking to her.
1. Reforge the item. Will need 1 bornite nodule and 1 Thaumic Coal.
1. Once that is completed, speak to her once more to update the quest.
1. Bring the completed censer to [[Brytthel]] in Maldura at {{waypoint 370.65, 82.35, 28.72}}.
   - *Note: Now you can finish [[What Lies Beneath#Steps|What Lies Beneath]]*

## Needed Materials

- [[Arcannium]] (2) -- *rare*
- [[Bornite nodule]] (21)
- [[Umbrite]] (20)
- [[Thaumic Coal]] (2)

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- [[Coin of the Grand Artificer]]

- +1000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +5,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
