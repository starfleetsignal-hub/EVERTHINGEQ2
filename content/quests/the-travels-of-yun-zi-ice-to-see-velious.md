## Steps

1. Visit the following locations in [[Velious]]:
   1. [[Fina's Retreat]] {{waypoint -1581, -540, -2356}} Take the bell to The Sinking Sands, and talk to the Othmir on the Docks. They will teleport you directly there.
   1. [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]] {{waypoint 771.93, -181.12, 46.66}} in [[Great Divide]] behind the waterfall.  Enter Thurgadin, City of the Coldain from the Thurgadin Harbor and travel through the city to exit at Dain's Last Stand {{waypoint 674, -250, 64}}.  The update is back in The Great Divide on the other side of that exit.
   1. [[Ulteran Spires]] {{waypoint -968, -336, 2351}} in [[Eastern Wastes]]
   1. [[New Combine Foothold]] {{waypoint -3695, -779, -214}} in [[The Withered Lands]] (talk to the New Combine Flight Master {{waypoint 1278, -540, 304}} in [[Great Divide]] at the Thurgadin Harbor).
   1. [[Skyshrine: The City of Dracur]].  Entrance located at [[Village of Alivan]] {{waypoint -559, -686, 367}} (talk to the Flight Master to zone into Skyshrine) in [[The Withered Lands]] or use a [[Pennant of Skyshrine Loyalty]] house item.  Use a pony to travel from [[New Combine Foothold]] to [[Village of Alivan]]. For first timers, slowly invis your way to the village because very few mobs see it. The quest updates when you enter [[Skyshrine: The City of Dracur]]. Return to Withered Lands by speaking to the covert shadow-talon drake inside Skyshrine at  {{waypoint -14, 1, -18}}
   1. [[Salt Breeze]] in [[Cobalt Scar]] {{waypoint 3623, -1016, 1692}} Use the same griffin on the Thurgadin Harbor ([[Great Divide]] docks) to travel to Cobalt Scar. Ride west a short distance to Salt Breeze. (Note: Global Globe bell in guild will not have option to travel to Cobalt scar)
1. Return to [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]] on [[Spire Island]] of [[The Sundered Frontier]] at {{waypoint 2297, -199, 2506}}
