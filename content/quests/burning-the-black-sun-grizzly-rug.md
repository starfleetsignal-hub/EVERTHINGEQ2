## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- This is the access quest for the [[Haunted Vaults]].

## Steps

1. Read the note in a room just down the hallway from the rug. {{waypoint 166, 18, -91}}
1. Collect a number of items in [[Kaladim]]:
   - 4-6 *Grungetalon Blasting Sticks* from [[A Grungetalon geoanimator|Grungetalon geoanimators]] {{waypoint 52.84, 26.59, -279.91}}
   - 2-10 *Blasting Juices* from [[A Quaketail brewer|Quaketail brewers]] {{waypoint -98, 45, -97}}
   - 1-6 *Firework Fungus Dust* from [[A Shrool porter]] {{waypoint -70, 26, -365}}
1. Return to the [[Black Sun Grizzly Rug]] and burn it! {{waypoint 195, 17, -92}}

## Rewards

- Access to the [[Haunted Vaults]].
