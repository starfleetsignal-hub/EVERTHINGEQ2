*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Collect the taxes:
   - Collect [[Zaikik]]'s taxes by inspecting the mailbox outside his house. {{waypoint -251, -48, -14}}
   - Collect [[Kadek]]'s taxes by inspecting the mailbox outside his house. {{waypoint -296, -47, -11}}
   - Collect [[Mnemir]]'s taxes.
     - Inspect the mailbox outside Mnemir's house. {{waypoint -256, -48, -30}}
     - Kick and punch Mnemir until he gives you the money (right-click options on him).
   - Collect [[Rootka]]'s taxes.
     - Hail Rootka and tell her that her taxes weren't all in her mailbox. {{waypoint -269, -47, -1}}
     - Continue to speak with her to get the rest of her taxes.
   - Collect [[Maharai]]'s taxes by inspecting the mailbox outside her house. {{waypoint -196, -48, 110}}
   - Collect [[Leldwana]]'s taxes
     - Inspect the mailbox outside her house. {{waypoint -200, -48, 140}}
     - Hail her and tell her the money was not in the box.
   - Collect [[Entui]]'s taxes by inspecting the mailbox outside his house. {{waypoint -216, -48, 130}}
1. Return to Feralis.

## Rewards

- At least 10s 18c
- +85 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
