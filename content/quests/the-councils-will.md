## Notes

- This quest is repeatable up to 10 times.. However, upon completion of the master quest, [[Terror of the Feerrott]], Ambassador Zozor will offer this quest again as an unlimited repeatable.
- This quest has three stages: level 37, 40 and 44. Each stage must be completed 10 times before the next will be made available. The quests of the final stage, level 44, have a cool-down of one hour before the next quest can be offered.
- If you desire a different target monster, delete and reacquire the quest.

## Level 37

- Slay 5 creatures in [[The Feerrott]], randomly assigned from this list (there is no cool-down timer on this stage):
  - [[A marsh damselfly]] {{waypoint -41, -7, 450}}
  - [[An underbrush fiend]] {{waypoint -370, -5, 471}}
  - [[A bitter bloom]] around {{waypoint 77, -8, 160}}
  - [[A brush constrictor (Feerrott)|a brush constrictor]] around {{waypoint 77, -8, 160}}
  - [[A carrion dreg]] {{waypoint -41, -7, 450}}
  - [[A jungle leaper]] {{waypoint -21, -1, 661}}
  - [[A thule sattar]] {{waypoint -556, -2, 152}}
- Reward: At least 27s 67c

## Level 40:

- Slay 10 creatures in [[The Feerrott]], randomly assigned from this list (there is no cool-down timer on this stage):
  - [[An Evol Ew disciple]] {{waypoint 154, -9, 185}}
  - [[A forest creep]] {{waypoint -557, -15, 841}}
  - [[A thulian affliction]] {{waypoint -1442, -7, 1031}}
  - [[A fetid Rallosian allegiant]] {{waypoint -962, 12, 502}}
- Reward: At least 66s 19c

## Level 44:

- Slay a number of creatures in [[The Feerrott]], randomly assigned from this list. All these can be found in the southeast of [[The Feerrott]] on the path to [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]]. There is a cool-down timer of 60 minutes from completion to when Zozor will offer the quest again:
  - 10 [[A Thulian protectorate|Thulian protectorates]]
  - 10 [[A sattar abomination|sattar abominations]]
  - 10 [[A terror wraith|terror wraiths]]
  - 8 [[A construct of thule|constructs of thule]]
- Reward: At least 2g 6s 47c
