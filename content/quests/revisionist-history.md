## Steps

![Edicts of the Emperor in pile of books on top of bookcase](images/Edicts_of_the_Emperor_location.jpg)

1. Speak with [[Korbev the Shamed]] {{waypoint -6, 26, -30}} in an alcove in the [[Hall of Tactics]]
1. He insists you prove you are not a spy. Go fetch a copy of the [[Edicts of the Emperor]] {{waypoint -25.00, 22.00, 12.00}} from [[The Emperor's Chambers]]!
   - The book is on top of a bookshelf in the Northeast corner of the room, which is fill to bursting with all manner of Dark Elves.
1. Return to Korbev. You can keep the book.
1. Take a message to a friend of his, Gelzeg {{waypoint 68.02, -39.29, -16.88}}, rotting in the [[Crushbone Jail]].
   - Gelzeg is [[A Crushbone traitor]] in the third cell.
1. Go to see [[Janitor Gorreth]] {{waypoint -39.08, -0.04, -24.43}} on the ground floor. He asks you to take the journal to [[Vargorn the Historian]] so that it may be copied and distributed. Vargorn is standing outside the keep on the left as you zone out to Greater Faydark.
1. Vargorn gives you a copy of your own, for your troubles.
1. Keep asking him if he's done till the quest updates - about 5 times or so, till he'll be finally done(no need to wait, just to keep asking him if he's done)
