## Starting the Quest

- Examine the [[Spiritbreaker]], it doesn't matter which zone you are in to get the quest.

## Steps

1. Kill the Pirate Kings in [[The Dreadcutter: The Pirate Kings (Raid)]]
1. Loot the essence off of each Pirate King
   - *Note: It is possible to trade the 3 shards amongst the Channelers who where there for the kill, that way more then 1 Channeler can get the update / finish the quest*
1. Examine [[Spiritbreaker]] and finish the Conversation with the spirits

## Rewards

- [[Spiritbreaker, Eianosheoll's Folly]]
- a new suffix title: [[The Breaker of Spirits|, The Breaker of Spirits]]
