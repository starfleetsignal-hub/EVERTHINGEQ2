## Note

- At least finish [[Legacy of Power: Secrets in an Arcane Land]] to unlock this quest.
- At Least 100lv adventure

## Steps

1. Travel to the [[Esoterra Gardens]] near {{waypoint -634, 31, 263}}
1. Collect three Gnarlroot Root Pulp. ![gnarltree root pulp](images/Gnarltree_root_pulp.jpg)
   - Sparkly 'feet' of the arched trees.
1. Place the pulp at {{waypoint -673, 13, -904}} and wait for the lumbering sandbrute to step on it
1. Pick up your flattened pulp and locate a starfire shard to cut the pulp near {{waypoint -543, 134, -638}}
   - (little blue crystals, multiple spawns)
1. Use the shard to cut the pulp. ![a sharp piece of starfire crystal](images/A_sharp_piece_of_starfire_crystal.jpg)![a sharp piece of starfire crystal item](images/A_sharp_piece_of_starfire_crystal_item.jpg)
1. Gather a Sphinx feather.
   - Blue sparkles on bluffs below Sphinx areas ![a Sphinx feather](images/A_Sphinx_feather.jpg)
   - Possible locations {{waypoint -432, 94, -772}} {{waypoint -455, 134, -846}} {{waypoint -480, 133, -851}} {{waypoint -603, 84, -826}}.
1. Kill 5 brackish wakingdews in [[Drukyna Marsh]] {{waypoint -653.23, -53.00, 102.35}} ![brackish wakingdew](images/Brackish_wakingdew.jpg)
1. Prepare the Quill in an aluxob kiln at {{waypoint -680, -38, -12}} ![an aluxob kiln](images/An_aluxob_kiln.jpg)
1. Kill 6 pure waterforms in [[Myhst Valley]] {{waypoint -185, 8, 237}}
1. Collect fine Aetherscar Dust in [[Aetherscar]].
   - Around {{waypoint 636, -40, -177}}. Looks like a pile of orange sand.
   - NOT a group update.  May respawn anywhere in the area. ![](images/Aetherscar_Dust.jpg)
1. Form the ink in an active aetherwell in [[Aetherscar]].  {{waypoint 938, 11, -124}} ![an active aetherwell](images/An_active_aetherwell.jpg)
1. Return to [[Kiteenan]].
