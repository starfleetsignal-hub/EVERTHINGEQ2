## Notes

- the training spearfishermen don't seem to drop the [[An unfinished breastplate]] anymore, but the [[A Sabertooth spearfisher|Sabertooth spearfisher]] do drop it. Confirmed training spearfishermen still drop it (dropped 22/11/2020).
- Just to confirm, the training spearfisher dropped a small chest on 3/24/25 with an unfinished breastplate.

## Steps

1. Kill [[A Sabertooth miner|Sabertooth miner]]s {{waypoint -63, -4, -63}} for four auto-updates.
1. Go to [[The Blackburrow Brewery]]. {{waypoint 26, -39, 63}}
1. Kill [[Nantglas the Cold]]. {{waypoint -83, 0, -11}}
1. Go to the [[Collapsed Everfrost Tunnel]]. {{waypoint -92, 0, -92}}
1. Kill 15 gnolls in the zone.
