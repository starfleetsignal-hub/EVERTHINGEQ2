## Notes

[[Vision Quest]] can be done at the same time, both quest updates can be done on the shores of Bile Stream around {{waypoint -157, 73, -285}} and {{waypoint -240, 60, -67}}

## Steps

1. hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt oculus sentinels for 7 Zon Zobboz dilation runes. ![an oculus sentinel](images/An_oculus_sentinel.png)
   - Gather 13 bilirubin barnacles. Along the shore {{waypoint -373, 60, 136}}![A bilirubin barnacle along Bile Stream.](images/Bilirubin_Barnacle.png)
1. Return to [[Grusla]]

## Rewards

- 3 x [[Bilirubin Barnacles]]
- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following:
  - [[Star Blood Hammer of Descent]]
  - [[Coral Forest Staff of Force]]
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]]
