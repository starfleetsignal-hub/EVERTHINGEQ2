The Bloody Tooth goblins need to remain armed and vigilant to protect their clan from the many threatening creatures in Obulus Frontier. I offered to help make some new "thumpy sticks" for their fighters.

## Note

Upon accepting this quest, you will receive [[Thumpy Sticks (Recipe Book)]] which will automatically scribe the [[Thumpy stick|Thumpy Sticks]] recipe to your recipe book.

The recipe for this quest can **only** be crafted at a [[Goblin Workbench]] in the area. One is found at {{waypoint 72, -164, -722}}, near Oogthor.

## Supply List

Because these must be crafted in [[Twark]], and the required materials cannot be harvested in [[Obulus Frontier]], bring the following (minimum) supplies:

- 8 [[Malduran lumber]]
- 24 [[Abyssal pelt]]
- 24 [[Splitiron ore]]
- 40 [[Thaumic Coal]]

## Steps

1. I can use a goblin workbench to make the weapons
   - Craft some "thumpy sticks" (8)
1. Oogthor is in the main goblin encampment of Twark
   - Return to [[Oogthor]] with the weapons

## Rewards

- [[Sovereign of Rile]] currency
- [[Ancient Shadowstone Relic]] status token
- +2000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
- At least 2p 26g 80s
