In a place called Whisperling Scarp I found an ancient fountain and beside it a stack of scrolled letters that appear to have belonged to a pair of lovers. They describe how their love was torn asunder when they accidentally wandered across the forbidden bridge into the Desolate Grounds. They fought bravely but were killed by a being that wanders there called a detritus. I shall avenge these lovers for no other reason than to honor such beautifully written letters of kindness and affection.

## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A skeletal detritus|skeletal detrituses]] in the [[Desolate Grounds]] around {{waypoint 394, 19, -170}}.
