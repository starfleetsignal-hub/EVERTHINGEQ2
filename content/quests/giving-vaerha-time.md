## Steps

1. Wait for [[Vaerha Farsight]] to finish consulting her notes. This can take up to 10 minutes.
   - You can speed up her examination process by running out of the cave towards the waterfall that leads to the main bridge outside. Quest will instantly update.
1. Return to Vaerha Farsight.

## Rewards

- At least 11s
- Summonable pet: [[Baby Stonechest Gorilla]]
