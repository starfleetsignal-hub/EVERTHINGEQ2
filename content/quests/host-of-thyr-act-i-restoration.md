## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[Solumite titan]]s near {{waypoint -164, -33, -128}}.
1. Gather a resonator band from *discarded scrap metal* found at {{waypoint -323, -40, -183}}.
   - This looks like a gear on the ground, in an adjascent room to the soluminte titans which is full of ghosts and next-to a much larger machine.
1. Return to Ognit Eznertob.

## Rewards

- At least 8g 60s 47c
- [[Solumite Reanimation Stone]]
- +1,000 faction with **Drednever Expedition**
