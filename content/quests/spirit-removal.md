## Steps

1. Enter [[Big Bend: Sanctum of Spirits]] {{waypoint 60, -12, 268}}
1. Kill 6 [[A possessed ogre|possessed ogres]] or [[A possessed troll|trolls]]; "crazed" do not count
1. Destroy 6 Zraxthian items (items with green particle effect); right-click, select destroy, then attack it. Possible locations:
   - statue {{waypoint 2, 0, -15}} near Torch of Oggok
   - tome {{waypoint 11, 0, -11}} on crate near Torch
   - statue {{waypoint 10, 0, 19}} near Torch
   - scroll {{waypoint -15, -4, 61}} room near dock, on floor (not always!)
   - tome {{waypoint -35, -4, 74}} on crate on dock (not always!)
   - scroll {{waypoint 17, 3, -40}} room near exit
   - statue {{waypoint -33, 4, -38}} in a room
   - tome {{waypoint -34, 1, 0}} in a room at central square
   - tome {{waypoint -32, -3, 48}} in a room close to docks
   - tome {{waypoint 29, 0, 9}} in the forge room at the Torch plaza
   - scroll {{waypoint 33, -1, 5}} in a room
   - tome {{waypoint -15, -1, 33}} in a room
1. Speak to Corporal Sergius Mordanticus in West Freeport

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
