## Steps

1. Speak with [[Farmer Walcott]] at {{waypoint -99, 0, 47}}.
1. Kill [[A witherstraw scarecrow|Witherstraw scarecrows]] until you receive an automatic update for a ***Pristine Witherstraw Scarecrow Cap***.
1. Kill [[A Darkpaw mystic|Darkpaw mystics]] at {{waypoint -55, -16, 561}} or {{waypoint -1313, 2.6, 784}} until you receive an automatic update for ***Darkpaw Sinew***.
1. Return to the cave with the [[A Darkpaw animator|Darkpaw animators]] and click the bale of hay to gather a bundle of ***gnoll quiverstraw***.
1. Kill [[A sparrow-hawk|sparrow-hawks]] at {{waypoint 46, -14, 176}} until you receive a rare auto-update for ***a rainbow feather***.
1. Return to [[Farmer Walcott]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Quiverstraw Necklace]]
- At least 11s 13c
