## Materials Required

- 225 [[Dewpalm lumber]]
- 225 [[Nimbic hide]]
- 108 [[Reef cluster]]
- 192 [[Silvered fleck]]
- 225 [[Strangler root]]
- 228 [[Sublime pulverulence]] (gathered throughout the Duality's Vigilant Sanctum, 276 if Mass Production is used, 10 combines instead of 9 or 6)

## Steps

1. Collect the [[Recipe Scroll: Luclinite Propylon Pieces|Luclinite Propylon Pieces Scroll]] lower level at {{waypoint 538.17, 121.94, -70.86}}, {{waypoint 540.69, 121.94, -60.35}} or {{waypoint 589, 121.94, -58}} or upper level on top of the shelf at {{waypoint 559, 133, -30}} or {{waypoint 597, 131, -47}}. Scribe the scroll.
   - Note: if the scroll cannot be found: exit zone, reset Dungeons, delete quest, use key and obtain quest again from Scholar Nabihan.   ![Luclinite Propylon Pieces Scroll](images/Luclinite_Propylon_Pieces_Scroll.jpg)<br>Scribe the scroll in inventory.
1. Collect the following items (*all are trackable*):
   - FYI: These nodes are unaffected by Bountiful Harvest, and you only receive 1 at a time per harvest.
   - ***Note:** If you want to use Mass Production in Step 3 (see below) you will need to collect at least 40 of each material. Extras will be deleted from your inventory on handing in the quest.*
   - 24 (*40*) [[Unblemished quadrolite]] from the [[Obulus Frontier]].![unblemished quadrolite](images/Umblemished_quadrolite.jpg)
     - Found along the Giant's Walk (centered at {{waypoint -470, 88, 192}}). The nodes are rocks with a slight blue sheen.
   - 36 (*40*) [[Raw planeshard]] from the [[Plane of Magic]].![raw planeshard](images/Raw_planeshard.jpg)
     - Found on the Metetherial Plains (use the port pad). The nodes look like sideways, shining white-ish crystals. (Around {{waypoint 71, 32, 256}} and {{waypoint 337, 58, 308}}and then southward).
   - 36 (*40*) [[Annealed ethereal thread]] from [[Vesspyr Isles]].![annealed ethereal thread](images/Annealed_ethereal_thread.jpg)
     - Found around the central hub on the first island (centered around Falinpol at {{waypoint 25, 196, -6}}. The node looks like a purple shiny with big blue swirls coming out of it.  Track "annealed ethereal thread" on harvest tracking.
1. Use [[A Vigilant Sanctum Key]] to return to [[The Duality's Vigilant Sanctum]].
1. Craft 3 [[Luclinite Propylon]] at the planar workstation (downstairs at {{waypoint 584, 122, -54}})
   - This will require you to first craft
     - 9 [[Metamagic Attuning Prism]],
     - 9 [[Astral Transposition Fibril]], and
     - 6 [[Quadrolith Glyph Key]].
     - ![sublime pulverulence(s)](images/Sublime_pulverulences.jpg)You will also need a total of 228 [[Sublime pulverulence]] for fuel (trackable, within the Sanctum, 276 if Mass Production is used).
   - Only 1 or 2 [[Sublime pulverulence|sublime pulverulenc]]<nowiki/>e nodes spawn at a time, but they yield around 10 or so at each pull, and respawn almost immediately.
   - NOTE: While the  [[Luclinite Propylon|Luclinite Pr]]*<nowiki/>*[[Luclinite Propylon|opylon]]<nowiki/>s must be made individually, the components need not be. Accordingly, if you have Mass Production or [[The Hand of the Maker]], you can speed things up by making 10 ea. of the [[Metamagic Attuning Prism]], [[Astral Transposition Fibril]], and [[Quadrolith Glyph Key]]. In this case you will want to collect at least 40 each of the quadrolite, planeshards, and threads in Step 2, and carry extra of the other materials (250 of everything will cover it).
1. Speak to [[Head Scholar Nabihan (Sanctum)|Head Scholar Nabihan]].
