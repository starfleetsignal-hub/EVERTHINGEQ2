## Steps

![a wandering kerran spirit](images/A_wandering_kerran_spirit.jpg)

1. Speak to [[A wandering Kerra spirit]] that wanders around [[The Petrified Badlands]].
   - Wanders anywhere in the area from [[The Eye of Dartain]] {{waypoint 1780, 0, 4288}} to the northwestern edge of The Petrified Badlands at {{waypoint 2024, -15, 3880}} to just north of [[The Highland Salts]] at {{waypoint 1034, 31, 3494}}.
   - You will be attacked by [[A wandering nightmare]] as you move around.
   - *Note: you can see where the spirit on the map is while you are near Keyta. if you waypoint point (click on him on the map), the waypoint will follow him*
1. Return to Keyta.

## Reward

- 32g 12s 60c
- [[Kerra Shaman's Token]] (Quest update for [[The Never Ending Mending of a Broken Land]])
