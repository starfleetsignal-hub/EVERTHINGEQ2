## Steps

1. Travel to [[The City of Qeynos|South Qeynos]] and inspect a potion case in the Alchemist's shop {{waypoint 660, -19, 237}}.
   - Alternatively : on Origin server , the nutrients can be found in [[Nettleville]] in the Alchemist's shop {{waypoint 655, -20, 283}}
1. Return to [[Oakmyst Forest]] and *speak* to a [[Treant Sapling|treant sapling]] at {{waypoint 1000, 12, -265}}
1. Return to [[Scholar Neola]] {{waypoint 1011, 13, -311}}.

## Rewards

- [[Dryad Vine Handwraps]]
- At least 1s 51c
