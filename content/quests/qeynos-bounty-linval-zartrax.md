## Steps

1. Travel to [[Nektulos Forest]] and kill [[Lin'Val Zar'trax]] who will spawn near Thexian camp in the [[Valley of the Bears]] {{waypoint -1435, 42, -1395}}.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- +3000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
