## Prerequisite

- [[Toadwart]] will not show up in the [[Mushroom Bar and Grill]] until you pass him running errands in somewhere in [[Maldura]]. You will know this has happened when you get a pop-up window with a mystery 121 Status Point reward. This location varies.
- Trying to share this quest, I got the message "requires completing the quest, "[[It's Pillar Time]]".  Turned it in and [[Toadwart]] appeared.

## Notes

I finally cornered the scrawny, albino goblin who I kept seeing running around Maldura, in the Mushroom Bar and Grill. His name is Toadwart, and he is fiercely loyal to his mysterious master. I suspect I can convince him to reveal his master if I help him obtain the ferrovanadium he has been sent to fetch.

## Steps

1. Obtain 10 [[A chunk of vanadinite|chunks of vanadinite]] from  [[A tinkered minecrawler|tinkered minecrawlers]] in the [[Glittervein Depths]] {{waypoint 527, 130, 438}} of [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]]
   - Killing [[A tinkered minecrawler|tinkered minecrawlers]] is a group update.  You can click on the [[A damaged minecrawler|damaged minecrawlers]] to get a chance of an update, but it is NOT a group update.
1. Go to the [[Forge of Brell]] in [[Maldura]] {{waypoint 75.32, 35.94, -171.60}} and smelt the ferrovanadium by clicking on the "Forge of Brell coals" in one of the large forges.
   - Mouse over the fire spots until you get the hand cursor showing **Forge of Brell coals**.
   - Click 5 times to get the updates*<nowiki/>*
1. Return to [[Toadwart]].

( 527, 130, 438 )

## Notes

Toadwart is an errand boy for a mysterious master. He can and does run throughout Maldura, and [can be spotted in other locations](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/maldura-quests-feedback-bugs.564363/page-6#post-6281790).  You need to travel Maldura until you get an unexpected status ding from spotting Toadwart, then you will be able to reliably find him in the Mushroom Bar and Grill.
