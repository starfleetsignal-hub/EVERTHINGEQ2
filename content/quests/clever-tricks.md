## Prerequisites

- -10,000 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**

## Steps

1. Enter [[Dartain's Fortress]] and retrieve the telescope from the [[Skygazer Observatory]].
1. Speak to [[Stockmaster Zahra]] who will exchange the item for [[Ten Platinum from the Defenders of the Seal]]
1. Return to [[Ekur]].

## Rewards

- At least 55g 70s 61c
- +2,500 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**
- [[Mark of Manaar]]
