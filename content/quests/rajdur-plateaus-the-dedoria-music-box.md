## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key.
   - Kill 15 [[A dryland corpsebeak|dryland corpsebeaks]] around {{waypoint 306.68, 105.62, 303.96}} in the cliffs around [[Hopewell Harbor]], [[Corpsebeak Canyon]], [[Sunscar Vale]], or [[Scorched Shores]].
1. Find the Dedoria Music Box key clue in [[Takish Badlands]] from [[A residual sparklet]] at {{waypoint -460, 94, -453}}
1. Examine the clue to receive the quest [[The Dedoria Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the **Dedoria Music Box** in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]].
