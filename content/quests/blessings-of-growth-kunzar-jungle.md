Required: [[Druid]] level 25

## Steps

1. Return the blessed leaf to the [[Teleportation and Portals Trainers|portals trainer]] near the priest guild in your home town.
1. Hail the [[Teleportation and Portals Trainers|portals trainer]] again and use the portal that he will open for you to the druid ring in [[Kunzar Jungle]], or just run to the druid ring in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
1. Use the *Blessing of Growth* once inside the ring to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Circle of Kunzar Jungle]]
