## Notes

- Must be a [[Craftsman]] ([[Provisioner]], [[Carpenter]] or [[Woodworker]]) to get this quest. Two recipes needed by this quest are auto scribed when accepting quest.
- Materials needed:
  - 1 Mountain Blueberry (rare from shrubs)
  - 5 Sedge Root
  - 2 Speckled Grayling
  - 4 Crowberry
  - 4 Red Lichen
  - 5 Zimaran Sandpaper
  - 10 Zimaran Kindling

## Steps

1. Create the following items
   - Create a [[Colorful Basket]] - uses 1 [[Mountain Blueberry]] (rare from shrubs), 5 [[Sedge Root]] and 5 [[Zimaran Sandpaper]]
   - Create 2 [[Shiny Grilled Fish]] - each uses 1 [[Speckled Grayling]], 2 [[Crowberry]], 2 [[Red Lichen]] and 5 [[Zimaran Kindling]]
1. Speak to [[Nazeera]] at {{waypoint 145, 9, -422}} in the [[Western Wastes]]
   - *Note: If you cannot see her when you return, try issuing the command **/target Nazeera** and then "H" hail her to get completion of this quest.*

## Reward

- 3 [[Mesmeric Shell Powder]]
- [[Status Coin (75,000)|Status Coin [75,000]]]
- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
