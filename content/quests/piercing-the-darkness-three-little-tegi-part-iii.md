## Steps

1. Travel to {{waypoint 746.88, 84.22, 307.83}} near tree by rotwarp lake and hail [[Centi Dator Froed]]. Eventually it will scratch you.
   - *Note: Sometimes leaving [[Wracklands]] and coming back will spawn [[Centi Dator Froed]] for you if he's not up.*
1. Speak to Hulda  in [[Sanctus Seru: Fabrication Hall]] at {{waypoint 145.50, -510.72, 5.24}}.
1. Return to [[Wracklands]] and travel to the Shissar Temple ruins near {{waypoint 532.28, 86.97, 429.38}}. You get an auto-update.
1. NOTE:  If you haven't travelled to the bartender  {{waypoint 6.96, 180.50, 192.59}}  on the second floor or Sanctus Seru City to purchase travel globes, use the directions below:
   - Exit to The Blinding   {{waypoint -408, 88, 1}} .
   - Take the bug back to Greg's Spire  {{waypoint 570, 48, 588}} .
   - Head down the ramp into the cave  {{waypoint 706.37, 428.42, -600.52}} .
   - Exit the cave and follow the path to the Wracklands  {{waypoint 384.02, 299.25, -592.93}}
   - Enter the Wracklands {{waypoint -290.02, 218.38, -896.48}}
