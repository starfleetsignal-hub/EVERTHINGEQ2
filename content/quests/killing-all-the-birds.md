## Steps

1. Off North Freeport's Compassion Road {{waypoint -172, -58, -175}}, click the sewer cover to enter [[The Shadowhand Crypt]]
1. To pass the heroic, level plus one, triple-up [[A cult enforcer|enforcers]] equip [[A white cowl]]
1. At the far end of the zone, kill the cultists: 3 level minus one even cons; 6 level minus two one down arrow, and [[Speaker R'tanan]], level plus one (none grouped)
   - When you kill the first, the rest will auto-attack when you near them, even with the cowl on; the enforcers will also auto-attack as you head for the zone-out.  If you hail the NPC upon entry and tell him that you were followed the 2 triple ups will zone out.
1. Collect 6 green-glowing scrolls around the meeting spot
1. Return to Adrianna in the North Freeport Temple

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- [[Cultist's White Cowl]]
- One of the following (based on level and class):
  - [[Scintillating Brawny Fastening]] (for 63 Monk)
  - [[Pulsing Adornment of Agility]] (for 86 Scout)
  - [[Pulsing Adornment of Strength]] (for 80 Fighter)
  - [[Pulsing Adornment of Intelligence]] (for 90 Mage)
