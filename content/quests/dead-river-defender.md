*There's no hope of recovery or repair at the Dead River Docks in Thundering Steppes while the undead are such an aggressive threat.*

## Steps

- Defeat ravaging rotflesh (0/12)
- Defeat vicious gorebones (0/12)
- Return to [[Ardoth Prowrider]]

## Rewards

- +1,000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
  - Choice of at least one of these items:
- [[Ravaging Rotflesh Plushie]]
- [[Vicious Gorebones Plushie]]
