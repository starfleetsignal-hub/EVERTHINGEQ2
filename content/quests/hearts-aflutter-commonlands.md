## Steps

1. To join the Hearts a'Flutter competition, I must first head to the starter pad.
   - Step on the Hearts a'Flutter starter pad to begin {{waypoint -1092, -58, 20}}
1. The Hearts a'Flutter competition will start soon. I will not be able to join if I am on another mount when it starts.
   - Get ready to race
1. I must collect as many Essences of Erollisi as possible in the time allotted. (3 minutes)
   - Collect as many Essencese of Erollisi as possible!
1. I need to return to Stinky Sideswiper, the lovesick troll officiating over this competition, to discuss the results.
   - Talk to Stinky Sideswiper (you should be automatically teleported back to the starting point, near Stinky Sideswiper)
