## Steps

Kill all nameds in [[Mahngavi Wastes: Warpwood Cairn (Heroic I)]] as you need the last 2 to complete this quest:

1. Destroy death's champion.
1. Slay the dark necromancer.

## Rewards

- At least 5p 38g 87s 68c 107,799 status
- 5 [[Vetrovian Dres'Aag]]
- [[Vetrovia Crate (Version 8)|Vetrovia Crate]]
