*This quest is repeatable..*

## Quest Text

The shimmering scale glimmers with a hint of Ro's Brilliance. Desert tales have spoken of the possibility of a scale controlling the ability to change the shape of the dragon. Perhaps locating this dragon would net you a reward... or a quick death.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Barakah (NPC)|Barakah]] in [[Maj'Dul]] at {{waypoint 67, 139, 108}}. Prepare for a fight! Once the person on the quest hails her, [[Barakah (Maj'Dul)|Barakah the Dragon]] will spawn.
   - Barakah the NPC is only up at night, spawning at approximately 10PM Norrath time.

## Rewards

- The chance to fight an epic dragon, isn't that enough?
