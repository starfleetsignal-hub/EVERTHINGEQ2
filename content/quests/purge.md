## Steps

1. Purchase a [[Quickening Seed]] from [[Fu Laiang]] at {{waypoint 818, 109, -311}}.  It will cost 1s 44c.
1. Return to Inara.
1. Obtain the [[Unicorn Horn|Horn of a Unicorn]].  These come from unicorns or rarely from Nightblood Invaders.  Unicorns can be found at {{waypoint 300, -25, -66}}. [[Equestrielle the Corrupted]] can also drop it, so feel free to beat down this heroic unicorn.
1. Return and speak to Inara.  She will take the corruption into herself to try to defeat it.
1. Defeat the [[Inara's Channeled Corruption]] (level 60 Solo mob, spawns when you finish speaking with Inara).
1. Speak to Inara again to collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 18g 85s 15c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Archaic Spiritualist's Chausses]] (Shamans)
  - [[Boundless Naturewalker's Leather Pants]] (Druids)
  - [[Defiant Vagabond's Chain Leggings]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Flowing Sensei's Leg Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Gallant Dragoon's Legplates]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Resplendent Thaumaturge's Leggings]] (All Mages)
  - [[Reverent Curate's Plate Greaves]] (Clerics)
