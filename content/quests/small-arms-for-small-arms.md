## Description

The pygmies report being attacked recently by aggressive lizard-like creatures. They've asked to trade for some weapons they can use to defend their village.

## Ingredients Needed

- 5 iodocus cluster
- 5 cadmium ore
- 10 larix lumber
- 25 Effulgent Coal (fuel merchant near forge)

## Steps

1. Craft 5 pygmy daggers
1. Return to [[Skrit]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
