## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 60+

Baron Ironforge does not initiate this quest and may only appear at the Ironforge Exchange after you've spoken with [[Gavin Ironforge]]. If he is not present, or doesn't give you the message, go back and talk to Gavin and then return.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Baron Kaedrin Ironforge]] at [[Ironforge Exchange]] in [[North Qeynos (POI)|North Qeynos]]. {{waypoint 339, -16, -94}}
   - Baron Kaedrin will give you [[Bellengere's Note|a message]] to take to [[Assistant Rystan]].
1. Use the Yellow Portal then speak with Assistant Rystan at [[The Concordium Tower]] by the yellow portal. {{waypoint 715, 87, 124}}
1. Return to [[Baron Kaedrin Ironforge]].

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c (at level 92)
- At least 1p 15g (at level 89)
