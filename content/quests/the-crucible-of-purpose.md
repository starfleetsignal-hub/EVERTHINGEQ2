## Steps

1. Receive the [[Crucible of Purpose]] from [[Khat-Ra Pakiat]].
1. Speak to [[Badalsonu]] at {{waypoint 940, 12, -145}}. west of the Aetherscar
1. Examine the [[Crucible of Purpose]] to receive the [[Unattuned Blessing of Purpose]].
1. Examine the [[Unattuned Blessing of Purpose]] to receive the [[Attuned Blessing of Purpose]].
1. Return to [[Khat-Ra Pakiat]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Transient Opal Pebble]]
- [[Celestial Ascension]]
- -1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
