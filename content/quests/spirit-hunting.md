## Notes

- When [[Runaway Spirits (Advanced Solo)]] or [[Runaway Spirits (Heroic)]] updates this quest will update as well.
- This quest cannot be deleted from the [[Quest Journal]]. To delete it, travel to the [[The Village of Shin|Village of Shin]] in Mara and speak to [[An escaped fabled spirit|an escaped fabled spirit]] {{waypoint -84.41, -2.52, 82.88}}.

## Steps

1. Kill 30 spirits either in [[The Fabled Acadechism (Advanced Solo)]], [[The Fabled Court of Innovation (Advanced Solo)]], [[The Fabled Crypt of Valdoon (Advanced Solo)]], [[The Fabled Acadechism (Heroic)]], [[The Fabled Court of Innovation (Heroic)]], [[The Fabled Crypt of Valdoon (Heroic)]]

## Rewards

- One of the Following:
  - [[Blue Malice Gemstone]]
  - [[Green Malice Gemstone]]
  - [[Red Malice Gemstone (QuestReward)|Red Malice Gemstone]]
- On the first completion the Achievement [[The Fabled Spirit Hunter]] is gained and it rewards with:
  - [[Soulquell, The Enigmatic Transducer]]
  - Suffix Title: the Spirit Hunter
