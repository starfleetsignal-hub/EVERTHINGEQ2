## Steps

1. Find the parts that were stolen from [[Mildred]] by the Brokenskull trolls.
   - Fan - Random update from [[A Brokenskull officer]] {{waypoint 1300, 442, 539}}
   - Whirly gig - Random update from [[A Brokenskull deckhand]] {{waypoint 1300, 442, 539}}
   - Displacement arm - Random update from [[A Brokenskull mate]] {{waypoint 1361, 450, 568}}
   - Extraction pincers - Random update from [[A Brokenskull swabber]] {{waypoint 1250, 431, 518}}
1. Return to [[Mildred]] at U.J.L.

## Rewards

- At least 19g 93s 2c
- Ability to use Mildred as a Mender
