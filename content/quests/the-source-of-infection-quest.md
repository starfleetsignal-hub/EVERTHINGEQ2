## Steps

1. Find and eliminate the Source of Infection!  Fly to the excavation site.  Right next to the pit will spawn [[The Source of Infection]].  Kill it for the update.  Location to spawn the mob is around {{waypoint -745, -78, -740}}.
1. Return to Jimbo and tell him of your success.

## Rewards

- At least 4g 1s 30c
- Access to purchase items from [[Jimbo McDaniel]]
