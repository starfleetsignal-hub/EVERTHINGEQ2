This is part 4 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Qeynos]] and [[Kelethin]] who wear Chain and Plate Armor (with the exception of [[Shaman|shaman]] types).

- If you are from [[Freeport]], then your version of the quest is called [[Custom Fitted Armor - Part IV]].

## Steps

1. You must kill the following monsters in the [[The Crypt of Betrayal]]:
   - 25 [[A deathly scarab (Crypt of Betrayal)|deathly scarabs]]
   - 10 [[A forsaken shriller|forsaken shrillers]]
   - 15 [[Restless corpse|restless corpses]]
   - *Enter the zone from Vermin's Snye at {{waypoint 3, -5, 4}}. Scarabs are everywhere. Shrillers are in the tunnels close to the entrance. Follow the tunnel, turn right at the first T-junction. When you hit the hallway with the urns turn right. Camp in the next room at {{waypoint 121, 9, -94}}. Various undead spawn in this room, the hallway you passed, the room opposite of the quest NPC in the camp room, as well as in the entrance hallway beyond the second room (behind the back of the quest NPC). Kill placeholders until Restless Corpses spawn. Careful when leaving the camp room past the entrance hallway (behind the quest NPCs back). An Archaic Fury (level 23 heroic triple up) roams here.*

1. Return to [[Ian Cathlan]] to receive your reward.
