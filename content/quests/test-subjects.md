*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. [[Catalogue Creature|Catalog]] the following creatures:
   - [[An Evol conscript]] {{waypoint -300, 0, 850}}
   - [[An Evol Ew defender]] {{waypoint -90, -5, 426}}
   - [[An Evol Ew disciple]]  {{waypoint -300, 0, 850}}
   - [[A fetid Rallosian allegiant]] {{waypoint -920, 0, 520}}
   - [[Bouncer Hurd (Feerrott)|Bouncer Hurd]] - He roams between [[Cythan]] and the southern lake {{waypoint -10, -2, 900}}
1. Return to Mashara to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 69s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Defender's Medallion of Fear]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Healer's Medallion of Fear]] (All Priests)
  - [[Medium's Medallion of Fear]] (All Mages)
  - [[Stalker's Medallion of Fear]] (All Scouts)
