## Steps

You are asked to kill 10-15 (random):

- [[A Scaleborn reaver|Scaleborn reavers]] in the [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]];
- [[A Scaleborn warrior (Sanctum of the Scaleborn)|Scaleborn warriors]] in the [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]]; or
- [[A Scaleborn sentry (Sanctum of the Scaleborn)|Scaleborn sentries]] in the [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]]

## Rewards

- 10452 status at level 71
- +500 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
