## Steps

1. Talk to [[Daylia the Vigil (Qeynos Province District)|Daylia the Vigil]], atop the platform on [[Arbos, the Elddar Tree]] in the [[Elddar Grove]]. {{waypoint 662, 12, -373}}
1. Speak to [[Gerleth Bucklebrow]] by the archery practice area behind the [[In Range]] shop. It seems he knows more than you'd think about the highwaymen! {{waypoint 566, -18, -409}}
1. Stealthily eavesdrop on a secret meeting between two factions of highwaymen by entering the wooden door in the southwestern-most part of the [[Down Below]]. {{waypoint -15, 7, 0}}
   - The roaming [[A highwayman meeting guard|highwayman meeting guard]] can see invis, so time your run down the hallway to avoid him.
   - In the meeting room you will find 6 [[A highwayman conspirator|highwayman conspirators]]. Attempt to sneak up close enough to hear their plans.
   - When discovered, slay the conspirators.
   - Kill the [[A highwayman meeting guard|highwayman meeting guard]] and the two [[A highwayman|highwaymen]] in the front room.
1. Return to [[Daylia the Vigil (Qeynos Province District)|Daylia the Vigil]] and warn her of the assassination plans.
1. Find and slay the [[A hired assassin|hired assassin]] in [[North Qeynos]] using tracking and stealth. He is perched **UP** on the southeast beam holding the drawbridge chain leading to the Palace, roughly above Guard [[Burkin]]. {{waypoint 414, 5, -26}}
1. Return to [[Daylia the Vigil (Qeynos Province District)|Daylia the Vigil]].
1. Return to [[Master Rysian Gladewalker]].
