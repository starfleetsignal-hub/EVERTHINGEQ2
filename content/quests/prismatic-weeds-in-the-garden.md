## Steps

![A prismatic fescue](images/Pismatic_fescue.png)

1. Weed out the following:  *Note: All are NPC trackable.*
   - Kill 8 [[Prismatic sparks]] near {{waypoint 307.32, 93.69, -77.31}}
   - Destroy 10 [[Prismatic fescue]]
     - *Note: Pink and green aloe-like plants.*
     - Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 386.29, 55.19, -44.19}} {{waypoint 346.95, 59.49, -22.04}} {{waypoint 430.50, 39.96, 31.26}} {{waypoint 430.10, 48.94, 5.16}}
     - {{waypoint 327.61, 52.21, -142.51}} {{waypoint 300.37, 65.43, -174.70}} {{waypoint 296.10, 89.58, -107.73}} {{waypoint 290.42, 98.45, 6.14}}
     - {{waypoint 281.45, 98.67, 3.97}} {{waypoint 312.16, 97.40, 9.96}} {{waypoint 301.27, 86.05, 94.20}}
1. Return to [[Luras Thornbloom]] at {{waypoint 681.40, 84.09, -395.87}}
