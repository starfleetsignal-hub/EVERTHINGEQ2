## Steps

1. Return to the same locations as in the previous quest, but this time, you must find and inspect a chest at each location.  You might be forced to deal with some orcs in order to get close enough to the chest and inspect it.
   - Wreck of Q.S.S. Stormbear: in water southeast of dock {{waypoint 470, -70, 500}}
   - West Catapult: northwest of fort {{waypoint 590, -30, 90}}
   - East Catapult: northeast of fort {{waypoint 442, -20, 132}}
   - Tower Ruins: east of fort, next to barn, behind fence {{waypoint 221, -40, 208}}
   - Grove of Stones: druid ring up the northeast road {{waypoint 170, 10, 120}}
1. Speak to Hammer
