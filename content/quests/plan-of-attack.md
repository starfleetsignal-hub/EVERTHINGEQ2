## Steps

1. Investigate the K'Val Ruins
   - Investigate the entrance of the K'Val Ruins {{waypoint -3261, -717, -763}}
   - Look for one of the larger areas of the ruins {{waypoint -3336, -714, -1109}}
   - Search for any lookout points in the ruins {{waypoint -3313, -606, -737}} (fly out and up, or use the elevator at {{waypoint -3376, -713, -910}}. Elevator call button is on right hand pillar when walking up steps (South West pillar).
   - Seek out the commander's area in the ruins {{waypoint -3725, -719, -1110}}
1. Return to Lieutenant Rockshatter {{waypoint -3622, -783, -207}}
