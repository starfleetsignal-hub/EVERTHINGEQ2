## Description

The League of Freethinkers has just caught wind that a group of Libant debauchers are set to visit Mistmyr Manor. Not wishing to squander this important news, the Freethinkers have asked me to travel to the manor, find the debauchers and slay them at once!

## Steps

1. Enter [[Mistmyr Manor]], find the Libant debauchers and slay 6 of them
1. Return to [[Tanya Alvernon]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
