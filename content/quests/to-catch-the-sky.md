## Steps

1. Go to the secret meeting place {{waypoint 48.00, 484.00, -316.11}} on the [[Isle of the Watchers]]
1. After collecting the sky by clicking the green shiny, wait for Criggle to show up in the same location (happens almost immediately).
1. After talking to him return to [[Clarissa Cloudpuff]]

## Rewards

- At least 18g 85s 15c
- [[A Concentrated Piece of the Sky]] -- A scroll that grants the fun spell [[Summon Wisp]].
- [[Claws of the Earth Sculpture]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Criggle's Chaotic Characteristics]]
  - [[Clarissa's Content Countenance]]
  - One of the following, based on Class:
    - [[Droag Earring of Station]]
    - [[Droag Stud of the Assault]]
    - [[Hoop of the Floating Isle]]
    - [[Reaver's Earstud]]
