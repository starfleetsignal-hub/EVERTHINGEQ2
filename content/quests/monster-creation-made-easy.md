## Prerequisites

- Neriak citizenship

## Steps

1. Collect the following items:
   - Harvest 3 **rancor quartz** from nodes against cave walls in Neriak
   - Kill 5 undead Shin'Ree orcs in [[The Commonlands]] {{waypoint -267, 48, -817}}
   - Kill 5 [[A black wolf|black wolves]] in the Commonlands {{waypoint -499, -46, -646}}
1. Speak to Durrysn back in Neriak
1. A rumbler called [[Writhing sinew]] (10) will spawn and attack you. Kill it.
1. Destroy [[The Gem of Dominion]] (10v)
1. Speak to Durrysn again

## Rewards

- [[Jewel of Dominion]]
- 2 [[Shard of Dominion]]
