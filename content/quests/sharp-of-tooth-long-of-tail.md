## Steps

1. Find and catch the largest alligator in Innothule Swamp using [[Groluk's Patented Gator Wrangler]] to leash [[Grokkluz]] at {{waypoint -33, -88, -438}}.
   - *Note: It seems necessary to actually step into the water (mounted, or on foot) to spawn Grokkluz. If he does not spawn, have patience and try again in a few minutes. Approach the gator to make him attackable and able to be leashed.*
   - *Defilers should remove Abominus (walking under water) or he may not spawn.
   - *If you choose to fight Grokkluz for AA, note that he has a respawn timer of ~5 minutes and will decrease Clan Grobb faction by 100 points (and, oddly enough, increase Clan Brokenskull faction by 50 points).*
1. Bring Grokkluz back to [[Groluk]] in Grobb.
   - *There is a 10 minute time limit to complete this portion.*
