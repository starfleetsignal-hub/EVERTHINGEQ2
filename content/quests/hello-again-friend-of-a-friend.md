## Steps

1. Talk to [[Jorlak]] {{waypoint 136, 0, 68}} in zone, you will then talk to [[Tavoken]] {{waypoint 101, 0, 303}}
1. Talk to [[Tavoken]], but must assist him before he will aid me.
1. Kill [[Ovilas Ironwing (Heroic)|Ovilas Ironwing]]
1. Talk to [[Tavoken]], then return to [[Jorlak]] and [[Tavoken]] a couple more times for update.
1. Return to [[Jorlak]] in [[Skyshrine: The City of Dracur]].

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +3,000 faction with **Claws of Veeshan**
