1. Kill the [[Ravenscale Protectorate]] in [[Ravenscale Repository]] and loot the [[Crown of Tearis]] from it's corpse.
1. The quest now sends you to [[New Tunaria]] to find [[Oronar Camthalion]] {{waypoint -64, 93, -216}}

- At least 36g 79s 48c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Royal Belt of Battle]]
