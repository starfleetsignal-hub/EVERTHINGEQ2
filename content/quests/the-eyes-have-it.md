## Notes

- Scales with player level, requires level 60+
- You must have completed both [[The Heart of Qeynos]] and [[A Body to Die For]]
- If you deleted, or did not receive Lilly's Letter, speak with Messenger Tibeld {{waypoint 859, -25, 47}} in Qeynos Province District, or Jerol in The Village of Shin.

## Steps

1. "Receive" the letter sent by [[Lilly Ironforge]] from your mailbox. Read it to get the quest.
1. Speak with [[Lilly Ironforge]] in [[North Qeynos (POI)|North Qeynos]]. She is located at [[The Jewel Box]].
1. After the conversation you will be transported to the zone [[Daylia's Office]].
1. Speak with [[Daylia the Vigil (Daylia's Office)|Daylia the Vigil]] then with [[Queen Antonia Bayle (Daylia's Office)|Queen Antonia Bayle]].
1. Speak with [[Murrar Shar (Qeynos Province District)|Murrar Shar]] at the [[Qeynos Harbor (POI)|Qeynos Harbor]] in [[Qeynos Province District]].
1. Speak with [[Queen Antonia Bayle (Queen Antonia's Chambers)|Queen Antonia]] at the Palace.

## Rewards

- At least 20g 25s 3c (at level 61)
- At least 25g 70s 61c (at level 92)
- At least 1p 20g (at level 100)
