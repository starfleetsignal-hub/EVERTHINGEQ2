## Steps

1. Question the Sathirian captive, [[Selethisk]], regarding Project Xakhiz. He is in a cage here within [[Kunzar's Edge]]. {{waypoint -1350, 11, 547}}
1. Return to [[Hroar Kalacious]] in Kunzar's Edge. {{waypoint -1374, 36, 515}}

  - [[Rainkeeper's Cap of Regrowth]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Ritualist's Coif of Summons]] (Shamans)
  - [[Sage's Cowl of Invoking]] (All Mages)
