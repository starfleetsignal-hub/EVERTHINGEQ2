## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Bristlebane has asked me to assist in gathering trickerybound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the trickerybound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Trickerybound Energy Reserves|trickerybound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect [[Trickerybound Energy Reserves|trickerybound energy]] by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to [[Herald to the Grand Prankster]] {{waypoint -832, 7, -203}} in [[Rivervale]]
