- [[A pristine oil painting of a knight|oil painting of a knight]]
- [[A pristine ash chest|ash chest]]
- [[A pristine halfling wooden table|halfling wooden table]]
- [[A pristine kerran chair|kerran chair]]
- [[A pristine open chest of skulls|open chest of skulls]]
- [[A pristine canvas painting|canvas painting]]
- [[A pristine painting of the sun god|painting of the sun god]]
- [[A pristine thexian chair|thexian chair]]
- [[A pristine ornate halasian chest|ornate halasian chest]]
- [[A pristine standard ash shelf|standard ash shelf]]
- [[A pristine standard ash stool|standard ash stool]]
- [[A pristine thexian table|thexian table]]
- [[A pristine thexian wine rack|thexian wine rack]]
- [[A pristine large ash dining table|large ash dining table]]
- [[A pristine large ash ornate table|large ash ornate table]]

  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
