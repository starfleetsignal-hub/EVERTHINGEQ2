## Notes

- See the note about shared spawns on the [[A lancer wasp|lancer wasp]] page.
- This quest auto-completes upon killing the last mob needed; go back to [[Deputy Kegie]] to pick up [[A Deputy's Dog]], the next quest in the chain.

## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order. All of the required mobs are found northwest of the [[Lost Village of Bobick]], around {{waypoint 178, 1, -102}}:
   - Kill 5 [[A lancer wasp|lancer wasps]]
   - Kill 10 [[An unruly impaler|unruly impalers]]
   - Kill 10 [[A weakened wasp|weakened wasps]]

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Defender's Cloak of the Mist]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Healer's Cloak of the Mist]] (All Priests)
  - [[Medium's Cloak of the Mist]] (All Mages)
  - [[Stalker's Cloak of the Mist]] (All Scouts)
