## Steps

1. Kill Gloompall fairies for various parts:
   - The spring, bolt, coil and size 3 gear are all uncommon auto-updates available from nearly any Gloompall fairy.
   - The lens will drop from [[A Gloompall witch]] near {{waypoint 870, 455, 121}}.
     - The Gloompall witches are the first part of a ring event.  After killing all the witches, [[A Gloompall crone|Gloompall crones]] spawn, and after the crones are killed, [[The Gloompall Hag]] spawns.  After the Hag is killed, there seems to be timer of about one hour real time before the witches respawn.  If the witches aren't up, complete the ring event and come back later.
1. Return to [[Bingles Multicrank|Bingles]].

## Rewards

- At least 10g 74s 80c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Beads of the Aeromancer]] (All Mages)
  - [[Beads of the Sky Warrior]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Beads of the Skystalker]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Beads of the Twilight Priest]] (All Priests)
