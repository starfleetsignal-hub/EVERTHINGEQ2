## Description

Skrit would like me to use a tinkered snare to stun one of the grimling runners who are sometimes seen spying on the dig site. She wants to check what they are carrying back and forth, in case there are any clues to their origin or intention.

## Steps

{Daily Tradeskill Mission, Tuesday}

1. Use the [[Tinkered snare]] to stun then right click [[A grimling runner]] {{waypoint 867, 30.69, 948.28}} and pick "search bags"
   - You will get [[A twisted hominid's possessions]]
   - There is also a rare chance that you get [[Rallic pack pattern]]
     - If you are trying to get the rallic pack pattern and don't receive it, delete and reacquire the quest for another chance instead of turning it in.
1. Return the grimling runner's bag to [[Skrit]] {{waypoint 1225, 18, 1264}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
