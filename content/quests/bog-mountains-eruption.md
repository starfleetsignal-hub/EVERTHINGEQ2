**NOTE:** This quest is repeatable every 24 hours.

## Steps

Head to [[Bog Mountain]] (i.e. the general area around the zone-in to Runnyeye)

1. Defeat 12 [[A Grungetalon beastrider|Grungetalon beastriders]], [[A Runnyeye chopper|Runnyeye choppers]], or [[A Runnyeye spellflinger|Runnyeye spellflingers]] (total, not each)
1. Return to Maisy Sweetbread

## Rewards

- One of the Following:
  - 2 [[Goblish Stone Block]]s
  - 10 [[Beer Token]]s
