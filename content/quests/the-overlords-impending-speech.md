This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Security Guard Brazen]] {{waypoint 118.7, -20, 74}}
1. Speak to [[Security Guard Shlizzh]] {{waypoint 78, -20, 44}}
1. Speak to [[Security Foreman Palius]] {{waypoint 117, -20, 16}}
1. Speak to [[An Anxious Reveler (Freeport)|an Anxious Reveler]] {{waypoint 91, -22, 47}} who turns into is a solo easy kill.
1. Report back to Palius and wait for D'Lere to give a speech

## Rewards

Choice of:

- [[Firework: Confetti Shower]]
- [[Firework: Prismatic Burst]]
- [[Firework: Rainbow Ring]]

Completing the quest for the fifth time offers an additional reward:

- [[Royal Scroll of Assistance]]
