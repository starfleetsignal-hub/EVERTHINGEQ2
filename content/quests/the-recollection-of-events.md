## Steps

1. Gather 8 Wither Reed Plants from the swamp. They are found on grassy patches. Mostly near the northern end of the cove, none were found south of the combine wagons. These are track-able with "Track Harvestables" skill.
1. Return to [[Silnas]] at the ritual place {{waypoint -4, -632, -725}}
1. Throw plants into the fire.
1. Listen to the elder spirits.
1. Talk To [[Silnas]].

- At least 29g 85s 79c

  - [[Spiritual Necklace of Sacrifice]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Spiritual Amulet of Sacrifice]] (All Priests)
