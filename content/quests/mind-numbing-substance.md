## Steps

1. Look for anything which could add this substance to the water source
   - At the exit of the cave (upper area after climbing both ladders) there are two tumble weed looking plants flanking the exit; examine either one of these for the update. {{waypoint 1307, 52, 3944}}
1. Return to Ishaq at {{waypoint 1832, 6, 4365}} with your findings

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
