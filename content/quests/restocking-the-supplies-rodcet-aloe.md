## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

![the hand of nife](images/The_hand_of_nife.jpg)

1. Harvest 10-15 **the hand of nife** to get Rodcet Aloe.
   - These are unique harvesting nodes found all around the [[Vultak Scavenging Site (POI)|Vultak Scavenging Site]] island {{waypoint -630, 0, -70}}. It doesn't take long to find them, but you will have to cope with the vultaks on that island (level 60-62). If you are VERY careful you can invis up to them and harvest all you need without ever fighting. You might need to make 2-3 rounds of the island. Occasionally one node may be harvested multiple times, at a max of up to three (3) times.
1. Return [[Surgeon Kweezil]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Kweezil's Queasy Reliever]] - 5 charges
- Coin
- First turn-in: good faction with **The Lost Children of Marr**
