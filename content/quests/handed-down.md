## Steps

1. Obtain the following items from mobs around {{waypoint 406, -25, 1169}}:
   - 4 feathers of [[A Nathsar stonebeak|Nathsar stonebeaks]]
   - 6 skins of [[A Kunarkian tusker|Kunarkian tuskers]]
   - 6 skins of [[A sabretooth scuffler|sabretooth scufflers]]
1. Return to [[Assistant Vihl]] at [[Kylong Post]].

## Rewards

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- +1000 faction with **Riliss**
- [[Devoured-hide Cap]]
