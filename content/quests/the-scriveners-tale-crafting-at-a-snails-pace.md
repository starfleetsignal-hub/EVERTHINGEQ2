## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 4 [[Nimbic hide]]
- 10 [[Strangler root]]
- 12 [[Silvered fleck]]
- 5 [[Reef cluster]]
- 50 [[Celestial Filament]]

## Steps

1. Find [[Yrag the Swift]], a snail that wanders the first (ground) floor of [[Myrist, the Great Library]].  (The snail may be heading either direction around the room.)
1. After you click him, nothing will happen but the quest then tells you to see Ghida.
1. Take the elevator at {{waypoint 421.50, -4.96, -12.86}}, then use the teleporter in the South Wing Catalog {{waypoint 388, 16, 170}} to the Smiths' Gallery and speak with [[Ghida Borrell]]  at {{waypoint 660, 412, 59}}.
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: enchanted running shoes]] and craft [[Enchanted Running Shoes]] on a sewing table.
   - There is a sewing table in the Crafters' Gallery. You can reach it by taking the teleporter to the South Wing Catalog and taking the teleporter across the room to the Crafters' Gallery.
1. Find [[Yrag the Swift]] on the bottom floor of [[Myrist, the Great Library]] and click on him. Nothing will happen BUT the quest will update.
1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 388, 16, 170}} to speak with [[Ghida Borrell]] at {{waypoint 660, 412, 59}}.

## Rewards

- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
