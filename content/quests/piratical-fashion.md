## Steps

1. Read the mail from Raffik, 'Dwarfs dress nice.'
1. Speak with [[Raffik]] near the docks at [[Butcherblock Bay]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] at  {{waypoint 527, 25, 567}}.
1. Speak with [[Dockmaster Waulon]] at {{waypoint 744, 26, 561}}.
1. Speak with a [[Fae]] tailor at {{waypoint 669, 24, 608}}.
1. Scribe the recipe: [[Pirate Outfit]] and craft a [[Pirate Outfit (extra small)]].  Everything you need can be harvested within Butcherblock Mountains.
   - 1 [[Belladonna root]]
   - 1 [[Boiled leather pelt]]
   - 1 [[Gold cluster]]
   - 2 [[Sparkling Filament|Sparkling Filaments]] can be bought from [[Trader Whitlock]] at {{waypoint 334, 189, 493}}.
1. Speak with Raffik.

## Rewards

- At least 5g
- Tradeskill experience
- [[Harvester's Balm (recipe book)|Harvester's Balm recipe book]]
