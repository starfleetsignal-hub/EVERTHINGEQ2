## Notes

The Soothsayer's Scrying Bones recipe auto-scribes. You do *not* need to locate it in inventory and scribe it manually.

## Steps

1. Use the Goblin Firepit next to Glibnox and craft [[Soothsayer's Scrying Bones]].
   - Requires 100 Artisan, 4 chokidai bones, 5 Thaumic Coal
1. The scrying bones should lead the way to the iksar I seek
   1. Use the [[Soothsayer's Scrying Bones]]
      - "A wispy trail leads to the south!" to Warslik's Wood {{waypoint 209, -227, -139}}
      - *Note: the Quest doesn't update at the location the scrying bones lead you to, but you must generally pass a little beyond the marked location instead.*
   1. Use the [[Soothsayer's Scrying Bones]] again
      - "A wispy trail leads towards the hills!" {{waypoint -72, -126, 65}}.
   1. Use the [[Soothsayer's Scrying Bones]] again
      - "A wispy trail leads through the pass!" - through Grunt's Pass up to the mountains {{waypoint -289, 45, 542}}.
   1. Use the [[Soothsayer's Scrying Bones]] again
      - "A wispy trail leads up and to the west!" - leads further through the mountains (Wurmscale Barrens) to the tunnel entrance {{waypoint 697, 221, 825}}
      - When standing at the last spot the trail leads one to {{waypoint 698.11, 221.31, 827.36}}, the quest does **NOT** auto-update to the last prompt, while still prompting to use the bones. Having arrived at the last spot at the top of the trail into the Crusader's Cave entrance, after it says to cast the bones then do that again to receive the emote to move West. Then you just need to move further into the cave mouth before the quest will properly update to the next prompt. Esentially you need to 'encounter' the Crusaders inside the cave mouth so the quest will tell you to convince them to trust you.
      - Pro Tip: enter  [[Obulus Frontier]] from either [[Jarsath Wastes]] or [[Kylong Plains]] and make use of the broken [[Soothsayer's Scrying Bones]] which will lead to same entrance and gain one needed update.
1. "I need to convince these crusaders to trust me."
   - Get the armor from at least 5  "a dead crusader" a bit on the way back on the road to complete the quest.  Keep looting corpses until you get the auto-update to complete the quest that will give you the disguise in order to start the next quest.
   - *Note:known locations include {{waypoint 685, 219, 820}}, {{waypoint 606, 193, 647}},  {{waypoint 638, 207, 729}}*
