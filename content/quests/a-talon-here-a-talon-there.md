Upon slaying a very large ice drake you noticed the talon of the creature to be of superb quality. There may be a use of such talons by the Frostfoot goblins. You should collect a large number of them and present them to the Frostfoots to see if you can get some sort of reward from it.

## Steps

- Obtain twenty ice drake talons
- Find someone to buy the ice drake talons

## Rewards

- At least 9p
- 10 [[Epic Token of E'ci]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Drake Talon Hammer]]
  - [[Drake Talon Wand]]
  - [[Goblin Bladed Chakram]]
  - [[Dagger of the Coldest Nights]]
