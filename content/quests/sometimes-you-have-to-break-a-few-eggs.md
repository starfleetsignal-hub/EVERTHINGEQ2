## Steps

1. Travel up to [[Kragploom Mesa]] {{waypoint 495, 326, -127}}.  To get there, head to {{waypoint 129, 216, -190}} and then take the stone ramp southwest. Climb the wall at the top of the ramp.
1. Gather 8 eggs from the Kragploom nests (left click to harvest)
   - Not all eggs are harvestable - if the egg is harvestable it will glow blue and the cursor will change to a hand shape
   - Be warned - each time you harvest an egg, you'll be attacked by two [[A Kragploom egg watcher|Kragploom egg watchers]] (30v Berserkers)
1. Return to Torin.

## Rewards

- At least 69s 89c
