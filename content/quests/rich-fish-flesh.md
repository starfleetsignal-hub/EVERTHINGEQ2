## Notes

- If you have already completed the preceding quest, [[The Big One]], you are unable to re-summon [[Smolderfin]]. The workaround is to summon the shark again on an alt that hasn't completed the quest, log back in on the character you want to complete the quest on, kill the shark and loot the quest starter.

## Steps

- Speak with [[Calnozz J'Melvirr]]{{waypoint -356, -51, 42}}

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
