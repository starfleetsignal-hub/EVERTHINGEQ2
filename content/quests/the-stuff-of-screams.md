## Steps

1. Kill 12-14 [[An animated scarecrow|animated scarecrows]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]].
   - They can be found in the plowed fields on either side of the road from the [[Antonica]] zone in around {{waypoint -19, 0, -770}}.

## Rewards

- 1437 status
- +500 faction with **The Celestial Watch**
- 1 [[City Token]]

## Notes

- This quest is flagged as Solo, but includes killing Heroic mobs.  The animated scarecrows in Thundering Steppes were heroic at launch in 2004, but demoted to solo some time before 2007, and re-heroiced in 2020.
