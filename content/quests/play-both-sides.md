## Steps

1. Talk to Commandant Vatinius in the Freeport Militia House in West {{waypoint 161, -17, 121}} to receive this City Task.
   - Kill 14 [[A Blacktalon sentry|Blacktalon sentrys]] [sic] in the [[The Barren Sky]] - around {{waypoint 129, 471, -402}} (Isle of the Watchers).
   - Kill 15 [[A Strifewing marauder|Strifewing marauder]]s in the Barren Sky - around {{waypoint 64, -13, -770}}
   - Kill 12 [[A Strifewing brute|Strifewing brute]]s in the Barren Sky - on Strifewing Isle
1. Return to [[Commandant Vatinius]].

## Rewards

- 11320 status
- +500 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
- 1 City Token
