## Steps

1. Wearing the [[Ale Goggles (Equip)|Gilin's Ale Goggles]], talk to [[Gurag Bloodwart]] on [[The Thundering Steppes]] dock {{waypoint -573, -14, -483}}
1. Take off the goggles and talk to her again
1. Wearing the ale goggles, talk to [[Smurg Fleshreaper]] near [[Gorowyn]] {{waypoint 2164, 22, 1165}} (east of docks past Haoaeran)
1. Take off the goggles and talk to Smurg again
1. Wearing the ale goggles, talk to [[Tarcha Niba'dn]] on the [[Faydedar Watch]] near [[Gorowyn]]  {{waypoint 2343, 20, 1340}} (northwest of the Gorowyn docks)
1. Take off the goggles and talk to Tarcha again
1. Wearing the ale goggles, talk to [[Dala Plateheart]] in [[Windstalker Village]] in [[Antonica]] at {{waypoint -1908, -36, -618}}
   - *Note : Sometimes Dala will not be visible*
   - first try **/target Dala** and hit "H" to hail her (she might be under the floor)
   - A *log out will make her visible again.*
1. Take off the goggles and talk to Dala again
1. Talk to [[Gilin Rockbreath]] in the Bar of Brell to complete the quest
