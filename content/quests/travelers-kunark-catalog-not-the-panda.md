## Steps

1. Travel to [[Fens of Nathsar]] and use the [[Catalogue Creature]] ability to catalog:
   - Catalog [[A Nurgan mineguard]] around {{waypoint -342, 32, -1829}}
   - Catalog [[A Nurgan tentwatcher]] around {{waypoint -418.32, 16.61, -1744.16}}
   - Catalog [[A forsaken Cabilisian spirit]] around {{waypoint -157, 27, -1151}}
   - Catalog [[A wretched Cabilisian trooper]] around {{waypoint -157, 27, -1151}}
   - Catalog [[A Bellywhumper cursecleaner]] around {{waypoint -1930, -319, -1354}}
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]

## Hints

The creatures are trackable.  If you don't have tracking, many ranger mercs can track, for instance [[Villax Sned]] from the VoV expansion can track.
