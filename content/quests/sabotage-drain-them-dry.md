## Steps

1. Destroy the water barrels supplying Freeportians. Barrels spawn randomly and can be found at the [[Crossroads]], the nomad camps, and both bandit camps {{waypoint -257.02, -48.04, -0.30}} and {{waypoint 414.09, -47.79, -79.69}}.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -1000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -1000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
