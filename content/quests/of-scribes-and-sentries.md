*This quest in unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Fai Long]] south of [[The Scrawling Cliffs]]. {{waypoint 271, -81, -760}}
1. Inspect a skeleton near [[Fai Long]] at {{waypoint 291, -104, -732}}. [[A desert spirit]] will spawn, yet you do not have to kill it.
- Return to [[Shu Fang Qi]].

## Rewards

- +8500 faction with **The Ashen Order**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Bow of the Pillars (Level 50)|Bow of the Pillars]] (All Scouts except Beastlord)
  - [[Flamepriest's Focus]] (All Priests)
  - [[Pouch of the Pillars]] ([[Beastlord]], [[Bruiser]], [[Monk]])
  - [[Short Bow of the Promised Land]] (Plate Fighters)
  - [[Staff of Sacrifices]] (All Mages)
