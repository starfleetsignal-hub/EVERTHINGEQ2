## Steps

1. At night, head to the smallest field of [[The McQuibble Farm]] {{waypoint -1685, -25, -41}}, [[Professor Heribert]] will spawn here; speak to him.
1. This will trigger an encounter with [[The Scarecrow King]] and his three [[Scarecrow guard]]'s (they scale to your level Epic ×2).
1. [[Ponchy Shorttoe]] will spawn at {{waypoint -1694, 0, -34}}. Talk to him and get your quest reward.

## Notes

- Everyone in the group or raid must speak to [[Professor Heribert]] simultaneously to advance the quest.
- **Warning**: If you die you have to wait until another game day passes to attempt again.
  - 3 real-world minutes = 1 [[Norrath|Norrathian]] hour; thus 72 real-world minutes = 1 Norrathian day.
- Two low levels or even a solo low level character with a [[Mercenaries|merc]] may do this if you are patient.
- High levels can solo this without any issues, so it could happen that you spawn the mobs and someone takes them before you have a chance to engage. Just be patient. In two Norrathian hours the quest giver will be back. Don't talk to him if you see others around. If you did talk to him and someone took the mob, the simplest solution is to delete the quest, go back to the tavern, talk to the professor to restart the quest and then go back to the field. This is the only quest that takes a bit of finesse, but usually drops fabled loot that's useful at low levels (70 or less).
