[[GM Event|GM Events]] can start in any city or zone in the game. On servers without the guide program this quest can be acquired from [[Vicx]] at [[Wanderlust Fair]] in [[Darklight Wood]] {{waypoint 235, -18, -4}}.

- Kill 20 fairies or sprites
