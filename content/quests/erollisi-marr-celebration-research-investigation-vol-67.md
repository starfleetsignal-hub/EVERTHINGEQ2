## Steps

Nothing is free and building, maintaining, and supporting the herald's camp is expensive. That's why the herald of Erollisi Marr has requested that we assist in offsetting the camp's cost by retrieving a valuable relic guarded by Queen Era'selka. I need to retrieve the relic.

1. Retrieve the relic guarded by Queen Era'selka
   - Travel to [[Zimara Breadth]] and zone in to [[Zimara Breadth: Razing the Razelands (Solo)]] {{waypoint -589.69, 148.67, 134.77}} and clear all nameds in the zone.
     - The chest with the relic is at {{waypoint 166, 157, -22}}
       - *Bug report (2024-02-19): If your inventory is full, you open the Relic chest and attempt to take the relic then the attempt to retrieve the relic will fail. The quest will fail to update and it is impossible to complete the quest. Another attempt to complete the quest by retrieving the relic will fail as the game reports you have already retrieved the relic. Logging out and/or leaving the zone will not correct the defect. Clear the dungeon lockout Timer, clear the dungeon again then you can complete the quest.*
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Huntress]] {{waypoint 21.71, 278.00, 188.71}} in [[Frostfang Sea]]
