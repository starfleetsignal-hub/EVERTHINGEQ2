## Steps

1. Zone into [[Oogothl Sprawl: Coral Roost (Solo)|Oogothl Sprawl: Coral Roost [Solo]]] at {{waypoint 231, 80, 319}}. ![Petrified Nautilus Shell](images/Petrified_Nautilus_Shell.png)
   - For the walktrough of the zone see [[Oogothl Sprawl: Coral Roost (Solo)|Oogothl Sprawl: Coral Roost [Solo]]]
1. Gather 8 x young nethernaught rays from petrified nautilus shells in the rocks throughout the zone. Look around every coral rock for embedded shells.
   - You can't just click the shell. You have to pick up the young nethernaught rays, after you kick the shell.
   - May need to rerun the zone again to gather the needed number of rays. Minimum lockout reset time is 1 hour 30 minutes.
   - Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -84.79, 2.48, 447.41}}, {{waypoint -116.59, 7.45, 501.35}}, {{waypoint -134.19, 1.02, 351.59}}
     - {{waypoint -147.66, 8.84, 489.70}}, {{waypoint -170.71, 2.50, 359.43}}, {{waypoint -177.79, 3.31, 400.33}}
     - {{waypoint -233.03, 11.73, 518.99}}, {{waypoint -275.39, 8.37, 451.78}}, {{waypoint -333.88, 16.44, 391.16}}
     - {{waypoint -316.83, 13.10, 526.98}}, {{waypoint -102.83, 24.29, 519.29}}, {{waypoint -410.60, 24.70, 566.15}}
     - {{waypoint -313, 48, 463}}, {{waypoint -363, 12, 529}}, {{waypoint -106, 0, 423}}
   - after using the first teleporter {{waypoint -130, 89, 301}}
   - after using the second teleporter {{waypoint 47, 118, 293}}
   - If you have insufficient [[Young nethernaught ray|young nethernaught rays]] to complete this quest, reset the zone (Minimum Duration 1 hour 30 minutes) then repeat this zone.
1. Return to [[Nanru]]
