## Steps

1. Speak to either the McGillicuddy expedition crew at {{waypoint 696.95, 5.53, 79.87}} or the Coldain expedition crew at {{waypoint 762.16, 5.49, 88.83}}.
1. Enter [[Blackhook Spiral: Spiritual Terror (Raid)]].
1. Defeat [[The Stalking Doombringer]] and retrieve a Void-Soaked Shade in a Bottle.
1. Defeat [[Hexxin the Nethertitan]] and retrieve a Nethertitan Horn.
1. Defeat [[Xychintus the Voidcaller]] and retrieve a Voidcaller's Stolen Voice.
1. Defeat [[Vul Lord Tarinax]] and retrieve a Bottled-Void Breath.
1. Return to [[Engineer Titus Icechipper]] in [[Sodden Archipelago]].
