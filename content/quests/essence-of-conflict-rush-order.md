## Steps

Create 1 each of any 6 from the random list below:

- [[Abuse IV|Abuse IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Challenge IV|Challenge IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Blasphemy IV|Blasphemy IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Slam III|Slam III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Decree|Decree (Journeyman)]]
- [[Devouring Mist|Devouring Mist (Journeyman)]]
- [[Enrage IV|Enrage IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Rising Dragon III|Rising Dragon III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Guardian Sphere|Guardian Sphere (Journeyman)]]
- [[Instill Panic II|Instill Panic II (Journeyman)]]

Create 2 each of any random 3 below:

- [[Holy Aid IV|Holy Aid IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Shout IV|Shout IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Rampage|Rampage (Journeyman)]]
- [[Savage Assault|Savage Assault (Journeyman)]]
- [[Silent Palm|Silent Palm (Journeyman)]]
- [[Shadow Coil III|Shadow Coil III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Righteousness IV|Righteousness IV (Journeyman)]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 1g 62s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
