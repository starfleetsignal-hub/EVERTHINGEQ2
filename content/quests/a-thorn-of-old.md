## Notes

- Consider doing the other Kingdom of Sky Heritage Quests and Signature Quests ([[A Mark of Awakening]], [[Blood of the Brood]] and [[Draco Mortuus vos Liberatio]]) concurrently with this quest since many of the steps take you to the same dungeons.
- To save you the potential frustration of not having crafting mats, before you start the quest make sure to have the following items in your inventory: 10 [[Rough lumbered rosewood]], 15 [[Adamantine cluster]], 20 [[Rough topaz]], 10 [[Scintillating Coal]], 14 [[Scintillating Incense]].
- It is possible to have the crafting steps commissioned, provided the crafter has the recipe for that given step.
- Unless noted, if at any point you sell your unfinished Wurmslayer, you will need to restart the quest in order to reobtain it.

## Steps

1. Kill the following mobs:
   - [[Azdalin]] in the [[Vicious Breeding Grounds]] within [[Tenebrous Tangle]]. {{waypoint 646, -163, 556}}
   - [[Gylton]] in [[The Audience Chamber]] within [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]]. {{waypoint 27, 85, 382}}
   - [[Lord Xyfl]] in the  [[Temple of Solusek Ro]] within [[Lavastorm]]. {{waypoint -335, -48, -639}}
     - Starts as an NPC, just click on him to receive the update. If you spam click him he will aggro and attack.
1. Return to [[Fuzzmin the Lost]] in [[The Barren Sky]] at {{waypoint -581, 51, -424}}. He gives you a [[Rusty Wurmslayer]].
1. Go to the [[Hidden Refuge (Tenebrous Tangle)|Hidden Refuge]] in [[Tenebrous Tangle]] and speak to [[An old man (Weaponsmith Master)|an old man]] behind the waterfall, at {{waypoint 82, -213, 194}}. He will give you a level 50 artisan recipe for Re-shafted Wurmslayer.
   - Be very friendly and cooperative in your conversation. If you say the wrong thing the old man will grow tired of the conversation and you will have to start the dialogue over.
1. Retrieve the oak shaft from a chest in [[The Lair of Scale]] within the Sanctum of the Scaleborn, at {{waypoint 23, 121, 307}} or {{waypoint -3, 122, 334}}.
   - If you use these waypoints from the entrance, it will lead you to a locked door. You'll first need to go to the [[Hall of Rites]] {{waypoint 8, 65, 14}}, take the south hallway to [[The Gorgs' Pit]] {{waypoint 10.91, 57.13, 309.99}}, and then climb up to the Audience Chamber. From here the wisp will direct you to the chest.
1. In the Sanctum of the Scaleborn, go to the unique forge at {{waypoint -116, 65, -34}} and craft [[Re-shafted Wurmslayer]].
   - You must have 10 [[Rough lumbered rosewood]] and 5 [[Scintillating Coal]].
   - While you must be a level 50 artisan to scribe the recipe, it is a level 60 [[Universal tradeskills|Geocraft]] combine. If you are lucky or skilled at crafting you can do this with lower than 300 Geocraft.
   - You must create a *pristine* item or you will fail and your resources will be lost.
1. Return to the old man in Tenebrous Tangle. He will give you a level 55 artisan recipe for Re-bladed Wurmslayer.
1. Go to [[The Workshop of Naar'Yora]] (third floor) in [[Palace of the Awakened]] within the Barren Sky, and loot [[An imbued bar of metal]] from a chest at {{waypoint 177.29, 57.17, 31.5}}.
1. Return to the unique forge in Sanctum of the Scaleborn {{waypoint -116, 65, -34}}, and craft [[Re-bladed Wurmslayer]].
   - You must have 15 [[Adamantine cluster]] and 5 [[Scintillating Coal]].
1. Return to the old man in Tenebrous Tangle. He will give you a level 60 artisan recipe for Formed Wurmslayer.
1. Go to [[The Carnivorous Pillar]] in [[The Halls of Fate]] within [[The Bonemire]] and loot [[A Quellithulian enchantment]] from *a scroll case* at {{waypoint -51, -58, -110}}.
1. Return to the unique forge in Sanctum of the Scaleborn {{waypoint -116, 65, -34}}, and craft [[Formed Wurmslayer]].
   - You will need 20 [[Rough topaz]] and 7 [[Scintillating Incense]].
1. Return to the old man in Tenebrous Tangle.
1. You must now kill three dragons:
   - [[Sothis]], within [[The Halls of Fate]].
   - [[Harla Dar]], within [[The Temple of Scale: Pedestal of the Priestess]], which is accessed from a totem at the bottom of [[The Temple of Scale]] at {{waypoint 9.12, 57.63, -236.45}}.
   - [[Lord Vyemm]], within [[The Laboratory of Lord Vyemm]].
1. Return to the old man in Tenebrous Tangle. He will give you a level 65 artisan recipe for The Wurmslayer and three vials of dragon blood.
1. Return to the unique forge in Sanctum of the Scaleborn {{waypoint -116, 65, -34}}, and craft [[The Wurmslayer]].
   - You will need 7 Scintillating Incense.
   - If you destroy the vials of blood or the Formed Wurmslayer, simply return to the old man and he will reimburse them.
   - The item is a level 70 difficult Geocraft combine, but at level 65 artisan (required to scribe) it should not be that difficult.

## Reward

- [[The Wurmslayer]]
- 63,180 status - Level 80
- 70,200 status
- 74,100 status - Levels 90 through 130
