## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 80+

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Queen Antonia Bayle (Lore)|Queen Antonia Bayle]] near the gate to [[Baubbleshire]] in the [[Elddar Grove]], [[Qeynos Province District]]. {{waypoint 787, -22, -536}}
1. The Queen requests you serve as a witness at the trial of [[Baron Kaedrin Ironforge]] before the Circle of Ten. The trial is to be held within the [[The Chamber of the Claymore (Zone)|Chamber of the Claymore]] in [[North Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. The entrance is a square on the ground in front of the Claymore on the [[Claymore Monument]]. {{waypoint 292, -18, -20}}
1. Stand near [[Queen Antonia Bayle (The Chamber of the Claymore)|the Queen]] and observe the trial.
