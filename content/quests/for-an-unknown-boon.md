## Steps

1. Hunt the following
   - *Both types of  mobs are in [[Flowfall Gully]] around quest hub and on paths around it  {{waypoint -859, -50, 914}}, {{waypoint -1096, -37, 940}}*
   - Hunt 4 mature voityughs for toxin prongs
     - *Kill a voityugh, and 1-2 mature voityughs have a chance to spawn*
   - Hunt 12 fungloids for calloused stonehide
1. Return to [[Kazoo]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- One of the following:
  - [[Industrial Signet of Rifts]]
  - [[Star Blood Ring of Succession]]
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
