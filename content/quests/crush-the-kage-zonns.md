## Description

I must board The Vigilant: Infiltration and kill Tallonite kage-zonns and their leader Kage-Zonn Radas.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Vigilant: Infiltration]].
   - Kill 3 [[A Tallonite kage-zonn|Tallonite kage-zonn]]s
   - Kill [[Kage-Zonn Radas]]
1. Return to Ismat

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
