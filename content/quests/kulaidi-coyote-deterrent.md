## Steps

1. Hunt and use the following
   - Hunt 12 [[A desert coyote|desert coyotes]] around {{waypoint 136.02, 3.69, 125.09}}.
   - Use the [[Cacti Toxin Extractor|Cacti Abhorrence Toxin Extractor]] on 8 [[A cacti abhorrence|cacti abhorrences]] around {{waypoint -46.81, -0.03, 133.95}}.
1. Return to [[Dulamah]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- +1,000 faction with **Hizite Nomads of Ro**
- One of the following:
  - [[Hizite Ring of Strength]]
  - [[Chain of the Desert Dweller]]
