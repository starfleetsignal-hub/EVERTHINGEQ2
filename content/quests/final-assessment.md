## Steps

- Upon accepting the quest you receive a [[Brokenskull Artifact]], [[Duality's Instructions]], and an auto-scribed recipe, [[Artifact Test Sample]]
1. Craft an Artifact Test Sample at the [[Chemistry Table]] {{waypoint 530, 130, -50}}
   - Requires 1 [[Uncut tourmaline]], 1 [[Cadmium ore]], the artifact and the instructions
1. Click on the 3 flasks on the table next to the Duality
1. Speak to the Duality
1. Return to [[Trade Liaison Wells]] in [[The City of Freeport|West Freeport]] or [[Qeynos Capitol District|North Qeynos]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
- Ability to fly near [[Malvonicus' Folly]] in [[Tranquil Sea]]
