## Steps

![An orcish torch](images/Excavation_Site_torch.png)

1. Burn the 6 bloodskull warmachines.
   - You will need gra [[An orcish torch]] from one of several spots along [[The Battered Wall]] to burn the warmachines. You will need one torch for each warmachine, and so have to go back for a new one for each warmachine.
   - The warmachines can be found at the following locations:
     - trebuchet at {{waypoint 69, -44, 825}}
     - siege tower at {{waypoint 113, -46, 885}}
     - trebuchet at {{waypoint 156, -44, 881}}
     - siege tower at {{waypoint 196, -48, 802}}
     - trebuchet at {{waypoint 315, -46, 835}}
     - siege tower at {{waypoint 300, -47, 796}}
1. Kill [[The Troglodyte Champion]] in [[The Bloodskull Arena]]. You must fight a minor ring event.
   - Kill the 2 [[A troglodyte berserker|troglodyte berserkers]] in the arena.
   - Kill the 4 [[A troglodyte chosen|troglodyte chosen]] that appear.
   - Finally kill the [[The Troglodyte Champion]].
1. Return to [[Terithal]].
