## Steps

1. Fly to [[Strifewind Isle]] and collect a sample of water {{waypoint 159, -21, -510}}
1. Kill 3 [[A dustwind drifter|dustwind drifters]] {{waypoint 215, -21, -544}} *common updates*
1. Kill 5 [[A Strifewing marauder|Strifewing marauders]] {{waypoint 115, -16, -637}} *common updates*
1. Kill 10 [[A dryland scavenger|dryland scavengers]] {{waypoint 167, -20, -482}} *fairly common updates*
1. Fly to the [[Isle of Desolation]] and kill [[Muckpaw the Gnawer]] {{waypoint 498, 301.5, -764.5}}
1. Return to [[Clarissa Cloudpuff]] on the [[Isle of Desolation]].

## Rewards

- At least 12g 75s 46c
