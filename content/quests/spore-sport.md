A mysterious ironblood myconid by the name of Verna Amanita has asked for my aid in securing his home back from invading forces!

## Steps

1. Collect 8 **healthy spores**--very small mushrooms--from the [[Ironblood Fungus Farm]] to the north of [[Blackanvil Bay]].
   - **NOTE**: Disabling Flora from the graphics options makes these much easier to spot.
1. Return to [[Verna Amanita|Verna]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +2000 faction with **Ironblood Sprouts**
