## Steps

1. Speak with Vegalys Keldrane's three fallen comrades, all of them in the southwest area of the Crypt of Betrayal.
   - [[Adun]] at {{waypoint 128, 9, 16}}.
   - [[Borthen]] at {{waypoint 157, 9, 8}}.
   - [[Vargon]] at {{waypoint 144, 8, -35}}.
1. Return to [[Vegalys Keldrane]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 17s 43c
