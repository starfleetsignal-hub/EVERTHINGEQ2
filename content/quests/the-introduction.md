## Steps

![Turn up your particles to see the black splash better](images/Black_splash_indicating_a_spawning_scorpion_point.jpg)

1. Wander around, on the ground, along the coast at {{waypoint -899, 18, -59}}. Red text will appear: "A recluse bluffstalker is attacking you!'. Kill [[A recluse bluffstalker]] that spawns. Step slightly away from erupting sand particles and step back to this spawn spot until eruptions disappear. There are 2 you need to find before the black one will spawn.
   - *Note the spawn points have black exploding sand animation*
1. Proceed to {{waypoint -703, 12, -867}}. Kill [[A recluse bluffstalker]] that spawns. Be aware this is close to spawn point for [[Tombstrike]] Both locations are required.
1. Walk or fly low to {{waypoint -955, 12, -450}} and kill the [[A great black bluffstalker]] that spawns.
1. Return to [[Izle Tot]].
1. Kill 5 [[Frenzied cnidcara]] in the [[Aetherscar]] {{waypoint 465, 31.18, -158.81}} and {{waypoint 722.30, -19.83, -65.33}}.
1. Click on the dried cnidcara corpse at {{waypoint 685, -35, -192}}.
   - Not a group update. Everyone must click. ![](images/Dried_cnidcara_corpse.jpg)
1. Return to [[Izle Tot]].
