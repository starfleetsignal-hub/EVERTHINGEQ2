## Steps

1. Enter the [[The Willow Wood: Besieged Hamlet|Willow Wood]] gate from [[Elddar Grove]] in the [[Qeynos Province District]] to try to discover the fate of Trelly's brother Jendan. {{waypoint 810, -21, -564}}
1. Locate [[Jendan Greenthorn]] chained up in the old inn. {{waypoint 860, -20, -627}}
1. Free Jendan by pulling the switch hidden behind the front desk of the inn. {{waypoint 859, -20, -626}}
1. Hail [[Jendan Greenthorn|Jendan]].
1. Return to [[Trelly Greenthorn]].

## Rewards

- At least 84s 37c (at level 36)
