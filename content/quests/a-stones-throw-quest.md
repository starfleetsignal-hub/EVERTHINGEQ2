## Steps

1. Kill [[A Corrupted Sifaye Stonehoarder|corrupted sifaye stonehoarders]] to get ritual stone fragments (uncommon drop).
   - They hang around the rock formations, and outer walls.
1. Return them to the queen and watch her gogo dance
   - She will take a few moments, then talk to her again.
1. Return to [[NPC|Elder Kionn]] {{waypoint -14, -632, -729}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
