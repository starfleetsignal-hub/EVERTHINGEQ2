## Steps

1. Find a Toxxulia prowler and take it to Nudhir's apprentice Aulerun in a camp at the western tip of Toxxulia Forrest.
1. Head near {{waypoint 1313, -46, 3039}}
   - Leash [[A Toxxulia prowler]] using the [[A strong animal leash|strong animal leash]] provided
1. Return to [[Apprentice Aulerun]] (nearby in a hut {{waypoint 1313, -46, 3039}})
