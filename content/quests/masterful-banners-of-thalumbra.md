This is not a normal collection quest. The collection members are not obtained by harvesting shinies.<br>
Instead, the seven collection members are themselves all obtained from the completion of other collection quests. This is called a "meta-collection".

## Rewards

- [[Indicium of Thalumbra]] this item is needed for the collection: [[Thalumbra Indicia]]
- one of the following:
  - [[Aphotic Linked Gloves]] - *Chain Gloves*
  - [[Aphotic Stitched Gloves]] - *Cloth Gloves*
  - [[Aphotic Tanned Gloves]] - *Leather Gloves*
  - [[Aphotic Polished Gauntlets]] - *Plate Gloves*
