## Steps

1. Enter [[Shadeweaver's Thicket: Perilous Nook]] {{waypoint -316.46, 99.92, 162.15}}.
1. Speak with [[Master Ward Singer Juma]] {{waypoint -279.93, 96.71, 197.47}}.
1. Place ward totems on either side of the slab of broken masonry:
   - {{waypoint -351.89, 102.05, 214.77}}
   - {{waypoint -362.71, 102.38, 213.03}}
1. Defeat [[A Xin Zov'Liako]].
1. Defeat [[A shadeling darkguard]].
1. Defeat [[A Vex Thal deathstalker]].
1. Target the new **Spirit Barrier** between the two ward totems {{waypoint -356.69, 102.47, 211.96}} and use the [[Dar Khura Focus]].
1. Defeat [[Dorshasszha the Ward Destroyer]].
1. Speak with [[Senior Ward Singer Oye]] {{waypoint -339.01, 102.26, 178.89}}.
1. Speak to [[Scout Amani Abasi]] beside the gate to [[City of Shar Vahl]] {{waypoint -546.81, 160.50, -729.44}}.

## Rewards

- At least 9p 35g 55s 69,440 status
- [[Vah Shir Pride Banner]]
