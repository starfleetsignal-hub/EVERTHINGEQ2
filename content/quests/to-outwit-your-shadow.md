## Steps

1. Destroy the tunnel opening in the [[Abandoned Kejek Village]] (see [[Ridge Reconnaissance]] quest).
   - Use the *Barrel of Dynamite* (in your inventory) where you get the pop up text near the tunnel opening {{waypoint -1008, 522, 663}}. Do not fall into the hole or you will die.
   - *Note: if you can invis, you can sneak around the "Overseer" to the left , then go behind the hole to activate the Dynamite.*
1. Return to the [[Chief Strategist Jasim|Chief Strategist]]. {{waypoint -368, 375, 600}}

- At least 1p 20g
- One of the following:
  - [[Crest of the Chosen Hero]]
  - [[Signet of the Chosen Hero]]
  - [[Emblem of the Chosen Hero]]
  - [[Band of the Chosen Hero]]
