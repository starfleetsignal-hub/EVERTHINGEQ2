## Notes

The starting zone [[Block and Tackle Storerooms]] is located in [[The City of Freeport]].

- Evil characters can simply use Fast Travel from map to go to Freeport.
- Good characters can go to Commonlands dock and use "Voyage Through Norrath" travel bell (the large bell with spinning globe), select Freeport from the options.
- Both these travel options place you on South Freeport dock, there are no guards in immediate area and the entrance to Block and Tackle Storeroom instance is just to the south on corner of the building.

## Steps

[[Jackal]] needs you to bring 12 spider venom samples from **challenging** (meaning NOT conning gray to you) spiders found around Norrath. Where you are sent depends on your true (unmentored) adventure level. **You are given a choice of 2 or 3 zones** with level appropriate spider type mobs to kill. You do not have to go to all those zones, just one of them and kill enough spiders to get 12 updates.

- Level 1-9  [[The Graveyard]], [[Oakmyst Forest]] or [[The Ruins]]
- Level 10-19 [[The Caves]], [[Greater Faydark]] or [[The Wailing Caves]]
- Level 20-39 Darkpaw Warrens
- Level 50-60 The Sinking Sands west of the docks, near {{waypoint -1295.65, -145.66, -502.06}} and in the Northeast Dune Spiders near {{waypoint -1053.31, -132.58, -1293.80}}
- Level 60-70 [[Loping Plains]]
- Level 70-80 [[Fens of Nathsar]] or [[Moors of Ykesha]]
- Level 100  [[Vesspyr Isles]], [[Phantom Sea]] or [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]]
- Level 110 <Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wing>, <Doomfire, the Burning Lands> or <Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands>
- Level 120-129 [[Echo Caverns]] in Limestone Forest area {{waypoint 465, 139, 39}}, [[Shadeweaver Thicket]] or [[Karuupa Jungle]] near {{waypoint 803, 48, -13.44}}
- Level 130-135 [[Zimara Breadth]] on top of rocks on Columnar Highgarden isle near {{waypoint 855, 255, -665}}, [[Aether Wroughtlands]]  at Coral Edge above zone in, or [[The Unknown]] on Nightscar Borne Lands island near {{waypoint -631.06, 18.61, 33.23}} and {{waypoint -796.92, 34.43, -1240.65}}
