## Preq's

1. MUST be of Freeblood Race
1. Must have Marketplace  'Cloud of Bats' ability
1. Must be at least Level 86

## Steps

*There are herbs that can calm the colony, keeping the bats from breaking from the colony and not returning. I should search for these herbs in the Great Divide.*

- Search for 8 bloodroots in the Great Divide.
  - These are reported to be between the [[Crystalline Crevasses]] and the [[Ry'Gorr Ruins]]. (roots are usually by rocks)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
