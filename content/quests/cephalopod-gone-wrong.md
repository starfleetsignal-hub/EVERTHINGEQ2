## Description

Pallasa Rargon, a member of the Order of Flame, a remnant of the ancient order that once inhabited the temple of Solusek Ro, welcomed my help in their ancient crusade against the invading Void army. She asked that I cull the number of void-touched cephalopods from the waters surrounding the Void anchor.

## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[A void-touched cephalopod]] in the nearby waters.  {{waypoint -6, -130, 585}}
   - *Note: They also update the void essences/lattices required for the quest [[Cleansing the Elements]].*
1. Return to [[Pallasa Rargon]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g
- +2250 faction with **Order of Flame**
