## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Sentry Trillis: "We still need to find out who or what is behind the grobin attack. Fortunately, I know just the dwarf to speak to. Look for Captain Steelforge near the Drippy Cave. You'll find him if you follow the road to the southwest."
- Player Character: "I'll report to Steelforge. Farewell, Trillis."
- Sentry Trillis: "And farewell to you. You've shown that you are truly a savior of the Nursery, and the blooms within. Give Captain Steelforge my regards."
- Player Character: "I will."

Speak with Captain Steelforge.

- Captain Steelforge: "Ah, what are you doing here? A whelp such as yourself shouldn't be this close to Drippy Cave. You never know what could come out of that dark hole and gobble you up!"
- Player Character: "Not to worry, sir. I'm here at the request of Sentry Trillis. I helped him with the investigation at the Amethyst Pond."
- Captain Steelforge: "Oh! So you're the one I've heard about. You helped with the shrumblers and the bixies as well, did you not? Forgive my tongue-lashing. We can't have lookie-loos putting themselves in danger, and I didn't realize you were an accomplished adventurer."

I have found Captain Steelforge.
