*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment*

## Steps

1. [[Catalogue Creature|Catalog]] the following creatures in Feerrott, in any order:
   - [[A bitter bloom]] {{waypoint 13, 4, -31}}
   - [[A brush constrictor (Feerrott)|a brush constrictor]] {{waypoint 17, 27, 879}}
   - [[A brush nightstalker]] {{waypoint -43, -3, 412}}
   - [[A carrion dreg]] {{waypoint 85, -1, 79}}
   - [[A jungle leaper]] {{waypoint -13, 19, 835}} or  (-133, 9 859 )
   - [[A marsh damselfly]] {{waypoint -46, -7, 473}}
   - [[An underbrush fiend]] {{waypoint -189, -16, 545}}
1. Return to [[Tarin Varinblade]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 53s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Badge of Unquestionable Dedication]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Badge of Unquestionable Faith]] (All Priests)
  - [[Sphere of Unknown Hues]] (All Mages)
  - [[Transforming Totem of the Changeling]] (All Scouts)
