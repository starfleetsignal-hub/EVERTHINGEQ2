## Steps

1. Talk to [[Gemna Wilfreson]] in the [[Stone Mercantile District]] and give her a **kitten**. {{waypoint -141, -4, 132}}
1. Talk to [[Thomas Nadwidget]] in the [[Metal Mercantile District]] and give him a **lightstone**. {{waypoint -199, 5, -134}}
1. Talk to [[Redthel]] in [[The Deep Quarry]] and give her **some flowers**. {{waypoint -34, 17, -192}}
1. Talk to [[Cholomondeley]] on the musicians' stage in the [[Mushroom Bar and Grill]] and give him a **mirror**. {{waypoint 13, -6, 25}}
1. Talk to [[Euphemia]], standing right next to Cholomondeley, and give her a **songbook**.
1. Return to Sira.

## Rewards

- [[Holly Leaf Frostfell Cap]]
- 3 [[Frozen Token of E'ci]]
