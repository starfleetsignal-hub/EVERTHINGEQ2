## Notes?

This is NOT a* 'DPS the Boss' *encounter.  You can only defeat the boss and end the event by closing all the surrounding rifts (9 total)

... this will involve both combat AND crafting in the middle of that combat.  The recipes are 'Global' skill, so you do not need to be a seasoned tradeskiller to help accomplish this goal.

Achievements connected to these PQs:

- [[Who Needs a Rift?]]
- [[Get Those Void Rifts Outta Darklight Wood!]]
- [[Get Those Void Rifts Outta Enchanted Lands!]]

## Where to find?

Currently, the Public Quest alternates between [[Darklight Wood]] {{waypoint -139.3, -38.7, 227.8}} and [[Enchanted Lands]] {{waypoint -835.2, 15.0, -837.0}}.

The current respawn timer is a 1 hour 'respawn window' that begins 1 hour after the defeat of the previous PQ. Essentially 1-2hrs from the end of one PQ to the start of the next in the opposite zone.

## Steps?

1. The format is similar in either [[Darklight Wood]]'s [[A Xaephian Zetamorph]] or [[Enchanted Lands]]' [[A Nightblood Daemog]] with only slight variations in Bosses themselves.
1. There are x9 [[Void Rift]] (^^^) in the event area that need to be closed. ![Reduces a Void Rift by 35%](images/Major_Void_Rift_Destabilization.png)![Reduces a Void Rift by 20%](images/Medium_Void_Rift_Destabilization.png)![Reduces a Void Rift by 10%](images/Minor_Void_Rift_Destabilization.png)
   1. Closing a [[Void Rift]] requires reducing the stability percentage from 100% to 0%.  This is accomplished via CRAFTING, clicking on the Rifts themselves begins the process.
      1. When attempting to craft you will be given one of three random recipes to craft, either [[Major Void Rift Destabilization]], [[Medium Void Rift Destabilization]], or [[Minor Void Rift Destabilization]].
         1. All recipes take the same materials: x2 [[Voidrium velum]] and x1 [[Volatile voidspore]].![harvest from rift guardian corpses](images/Voidirium_velum.png)![pick up as they float around the Boss](images/Volatile_voidspore.png)
            1. Major destabilization, scales as a 'more difficult' recipe, but reduces a rift by 35% when successful... this is where skilled crafters can shine!
            1. Medium destabilization, reduces a rift by 20% when successful.
            1. Minor destabilization, an 'easier' recipe, reduces a rift by 10% when successful.
            1. Which recipe you receive when first clicking on a Void Rift appears to be random.  *Though it has been noted by some that if you are initially succesful with the 'Major' version, you are more likely to obtain it as additional Voids are sealed in the battle.*
         1. To obtain the required materials, you will need to pick them up during the battle.
            1. [[Voidrium velum]] comes from the corpses of defeated rift minions.  When defeated, their corpses become 'harvest nodes'. Collect the velum from those corpses like you would any other harvest node.  *Those with advanced harvesting skills and AAs DO benefit from extra harvests and bountiful (x2) harvests.*
            1. [[Volatile voidspore]]s are obtained by 'picking up' the, still active, [[Volatile voidspores]] (magenta swirls that follow the Boss around).  Click on a voidspore to 'pick it up' and obtain x3 of the crafting material.  If you ever need more, simply find the boss during the fight.
   1. The rifts are all guarded by minions that spawn periodically.  If you're the first to approach a given Rift, the minions will scale to your level - making it so that ANY level player can assist in this event, so long as higher levels players aren't recklessly running around and triggering all the rifts for no reason.
      1. If the current minions are too high level for you, wait until one of the higher level players that triggered those minions defeats them.  The corpses are harvestable by all, no matter the level
1. Again, while the Rift boss DOES aggro and engage in deadly combat; it is not defeatable through normal combat, only by closing the 9 rifts will allow it to be defeated via combat and end the event sucessfully.
   1. Damage to the boss is capped at 10% per closed Rift.  0 rifts closed = damage stops at 90% health, 1 rift closed = damage stops at 80% health, etc. All 9 rifts closed allows the boss to be taken down to 0% health.
1. That said, it is still important to keep the boss 'under control' as it can easily take down or otherwise interfere with players while they're attempting to craft the required rift destabilizers.   A tank (or other sufficiently geared character) should keep it occupied while the rest of the crafters do their thing closing the portals.
   1. *Currently, the best option/strat is simply to give the boss a wide berth while closing the rest of the portals, and only engaging it in combat long enough for it to spawn any additional needed voidspores, after which, the player(s) simply run out of range and let the boss reset aggro and return to the middle.*
