## Note

***NOTE**: You must be level 78 to receive this quest.*

## Steps

1. Sketch the airship for Grunty
   - Go back to [[Firmroot Moot]] and to {{waypoint 78, -86, -430}} for bow update
   - Go to {{waypoint 80, -86, -410}} for stern update
1. Return to [[Grunty]]

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2000 faction with **Clan Grobb**
