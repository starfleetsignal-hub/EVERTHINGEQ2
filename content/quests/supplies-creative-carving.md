## Notes

Daily crafting mission. Reset timer 18 hours. Quest **expires in 2 days**.

Journal entry: In order to support the continuing population of explorers in the area, I must help generate a local revenue stream.

When accepting quest you receive [[Recipe Scroll: Supply Mission Materials]], which auto scribes 4 recipes needed for crafting dailies

Materials needed:

- 36 [[Chalcedony]]
- 12 [[Fox Pelt]]
- 30 [[Zimaran Coal]]

## Steps

1. Harvest 6 bones from the shoreline - these show on Harvest Tracking as [[Pristine Piece of Bone]], they are sparkling skeletal bones among aggressive mobs on the beach right outside and  on cliffs above camp. Use your W.H.A.T. if you're squishy.
1. Craft 6 x [[Scrimshaw Doodad|Scrimshaw Doodads]].
1. Quest auto completes upon crafting the last item.
