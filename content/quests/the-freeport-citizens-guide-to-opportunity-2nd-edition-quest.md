## Steps

1. Visit a variety of places in [[Freeport]] and [[The Commonlands]]:
   - Talk with [[Renny Parvat]] in [[East Freeport]]. {{waypoint -280, -56, 102}}
   - Visit [[Execution Plaza]] in [[West Freeport]]. {{waypoint 94, -22, 44}}
   - Talk with the Sage [[Navarius Orvalis]] in [[North Freeport]]. {{waypoint 9, -7, -133}}
   - Talk with [[Taskmaster's Assistant Koz'ma]] at the [[Crossroads]] in [[The Commonlands]]. {{waypoint -491, -44, -260}}
   - Talk with [[Cannix Silverflame]] along the road northwest of the [[Crossroads]]. {{waypoint -312.89, -47.25, -359.58}}
     - *Note: Cannix will not speak with you to update this quest if you are currently working on the Heritage Quest [[The Return of the Light]]*
   - Talk with [[Lieutenant Darrius]] at [[The Freeport Militia House]] in [[West Freeport]]. {{waypoint 136, -16, 114}}
1. Visit the Overlord's statue {{waypoint -153, -23, -57}} at the back of the merchant shop area of [[North Freeport]].
   - As of LU54, the Overlord's statue has been torn down, but visiting the pedestal it once stood on is enough for the update.
