## Steps

1. Tranquilize and tag 5 [[A wild chokidai|wild chokidai]] in The Green Overlook west of Cholzakla Al`xin {{waypoint 2650, 54, 945}} .
   - Tranquilize them by using the [[Long Range Tranquilizer]] on them from a distance.
   - Tag them by using the [[Short Range Tagger]] on them up close while they are laying down.
   - **Note:**  If the creatures are grey you can just tag them; no need to tranquilize them first.
1. Return to Cholzakla.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 86c
- One of the following, depending on class
  - [[Knight's Gauntlets]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Archon's Gauntlets (Level 12) (Ability Modifier)|Archon's Gauntlets]]
