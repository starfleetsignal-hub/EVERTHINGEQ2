## Notes

- **If you can't fly yet, the ramp down to Writher Crevasse starts at {{waypoint -373, 35, 83}}.**
- The vases in Writher Crevasse are partially submerged in water.
- All items can be found all over Southeastern part of Yon Gorroth, on the Wither Shore, Writher Crevasse (blue streams on map) and in Writher Ruins above it.

## Steps

1. Help recover lost Norrathian trinkets and antiques from the ruins and crevasses in southeast regions of Yon Gorroth.
   - recover antique Darou urns x 8
     - gold urns around Writher Shore and Writher Ruins areas {{waypoint -726.81, 12.98, 306.85}}, {{waypoint -633, 0, 267}}, {{waypoint -578, 6, 366}}, {{waypoint -538.97, 44.20, 386.85}}, {{waypoint -439.40, 51.10, 431.65}}, {{waypoint -436, 52, 449}}, {{waypoint -433.86, 51.10, 474.83}}, {{waypoint -406.94, 54.72, 410.61}}
   - gather antique  Norrathian forest vases x 10
     - orange vases at the bottom of Writher Crevasse.  {{waypoint -423, 5, 340}}
   - recover antique Norrathian etched vases x 10
     - blue/silver vases at the bottom of Writher Crevasse. {{waypoint -427.12, 4.98, 371.45}}
   - collect crushed Darou tablets x 8
     - silver tablets around Writher Shore and Writher Ruins areas, same as urns above
     - {{waypoint -499.16, 33.77, 463.23}}, {{waypoint -346.30, 59.95, 510.60}}, {{waypoint -361.25, 57.63, 521.03}}m
1. Return to Narezi
