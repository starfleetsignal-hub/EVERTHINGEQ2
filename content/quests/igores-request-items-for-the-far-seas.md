While most are crafting and marketing popular Vetrovian designs, Igore wants the darker side of things represented as well.

## Note

Upon accepting this quest, you will receive the following:

- [[Illusion: Werewolf]]
- [[Recipe Scroll: Werewolf Masks and More]] which will automatically scribe the [[Werewolf Mask]], [[Potion of Creepy Critter Attraction]], and [[Ornamental Sarcophagus]] recipes to your recipe book

## Steps

1. I must gather items for Igore's task.
   - Gather [[Wooden Scrap]] (75)
   - Gather [[Animated Roots]] (75)
   - Gather [[Sturdy Spiderweb]] (75)
   - Gather [[Twisted Bits of Metal]] (75)
   - Gather [[Shredded Werewolf Fur]] (48)
   - Gather [[Dirt-Encrusted Stones]] (48)
   - Gather [[Dessicated Bones]] (75)
   - Gather [[Ectoplasmic Goo]] (48)
   - Gather [[Cocooned Critter]] (75)
     - *Note: To progress this quest, click on the door {{waypoint 0, 0, 29}} in Renfry's Basement to return to [[Forlorn Gist]], then use the [[Illusion: Werewolf]] you received upon accepting the quest to make the items visible and make all mobs non-hostile.  Should you lose or dismiss the illusion, another can be purchased from inside [[Renfry's Basement]]; [[Ziggy]] sells them for 1p.*
1. I must craft the requested items.
   1. Craft a [[Werewolf Mask]] (2)
   1. Craft a [[Potion of Creepy Critter Attraction]] (2)
   1. Craft an [[Ornamental Sarcophagus]] (2)
      - *Note: There is a [[Far Seas Crafting Station]] inside [[Renfry's Basement]] at {{waypoint -363, 16, 78}}*

## Rewards

- At least 9p 30g 45s 97c
- 56000 status at tradeskill level 125
- 20 [[Vetrovian Dres'Aag]]
- [[Vetrovia Crafting Crate]]
