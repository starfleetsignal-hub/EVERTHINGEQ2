By the looks of it, this old fisherman's bones have been stripped clean by the ocean's predators, hopefully after the poor sap was dead! There's no shortage of dangerous beasts in these waters. I should clear them out as quickly as possible.

![](images/A_fated_humanoid.png)

## Notes

- ***Note:** This is a 5 minute timed quest.*
- Found in all versions of Zek, the Scourge Wastes:
  - [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Solo)]]
  - [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Heroic)]]

## Steps

1. Solo: Slay 20 ocean predators before time runs out.
1. Heroic: Slay 40 ocean predators before time runs out.
   - "Ocean predators" can include [[A daggertooth shark (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|daggertooth sharks]], [[A kelp horror (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|kelp horrors]], [[A mud grappler (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|mud grapplers]], [[A silt grappler (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|silt grapplers]], etc.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Encrusted iron key]]
