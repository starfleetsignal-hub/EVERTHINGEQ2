## Steps

1. [[Apprentice Aulerun]] has asked you to slay the Toxxulia cat you leashed earlier.
   - Use right click to engage, then kill the [[A Toxxulia prowler|Toxxulia prowler]] in the hut with you.
1. Speak to [[Apprentice Aulerun]]

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**
