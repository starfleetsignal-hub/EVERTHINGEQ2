## Steps

1. Click on any of the supply crates and barrels from earlier in the questline. The closest are the boxes next to Angor {{waypoint 4428, -821, 432}}
   - {{waypoint 4417, -821, 448}}
   - {{waypoint 4346, -821, 469}}
   - {{waypoint 4352, -821, 421}}
   - {{waypoint 4378, -821, 388}}
   - {{waypoint 4438, -821, 366}}
1. Examine the [[Force Projector Notes]] in your inventory. This will create 5 recipes
   - Scribe [[Recipe: Force Ignition Coil]], [[Recipe: Main Control Board]], [[Recipe: Power Storage Matrix]], [[Recipe: Force Projector]] and [[Recipe: Force Projector Power Source]]
   - Craft these 5 items at a work bench {{waypoint 4415, -821, 362}}. Requires 5 [[Effulgent coal|effulgent coals]] and the following parts obtained from the crates:
     - [[Flexible Copper Sheet]]
     - [[Copper Filament]]
     - [[Adamant Core]]
     - [[Tuned Manastone]]
     - [[Simple Mana Overlay]]
     - [[Manasink]]
     - [[Frictionless Cogwheel]]
1. Return to Angor

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
