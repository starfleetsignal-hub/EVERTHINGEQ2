## Steps

1. Kill 10 Adherant vampires
1. Kill [[A savage hellhound|savage hellhounds]]  or [[A hellhound ravager|hellhound ravagers]] until you get the update.
1. Click on the lamppost-like altar at {{waypoint -107, 26, -6}}
   - The lamppost is in the upper area, near the Usurper, and has a purple light instead of the usual blue, and is decorated with skulls, spikes and batwings.
   - The altar will not update unless you enter the Crypt of T'Haen in a normal instance, not in the Endless Twilight instance, even though the quest starter can drop in Endless Twilight.
1. Kill 50 vampires in the Crypt of T'Haen to complete the quest.
   - Vampires in [[The Tombs of Night]] also update this step.
