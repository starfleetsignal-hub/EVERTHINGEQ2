## Steps

1. Collect and fill the following
   - Collect 12 filled wyrmstone pieces
     - Red stones with sparkles on them from within the Venerable Absence.
       - Large lake within the Warming Warfields around {{waypoint 66, -32, -590}} (lake also has gold colored ruins at its shores).
       - Stones are at shore of lake and at rock walls around lake and can gather while flying.
   - Fill 8 empty wyrmstone pieces with destructive energy.
     1. Collect red stones *without* sparkles on them from [[The Venerable Absence]] and recieve [[Empty Wyrmstone Piece]]
        - Found in the same places as the filled stones and can gather while flying.
     1. Fill the stones you have collected.
        - Blue and white sparkles of "destructive energy" in the waters and on the surrounding shores of [[The Venerable Absence]].
          - *Can be done while flying.*
        - **Note: harvest Wyrmstones then Destroy them with the destructive energy. Red then Blue Sparkly.
1. Return to [[Thekror]] at the [[Hoarding Ridge]] Camp.
