### Steps

Craft 6 items from the random list below:

- [[Titanium Lance|titanium lance]]
- [[Titanium Voulge|titanium voulge]]
- [[Titanium Ritual Dagger|titanium ritual dagger]]
- [[Titanium Great Sword|titanium great sword]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

### Rewards

- At Least 12g 11s 05c
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
