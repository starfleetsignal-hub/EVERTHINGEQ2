## Steps

![Broken stele fragment](images/Broken_stele_fragment.png)

1. Head to the broken stele in the [[Sanctuary of Mourning]] {{waypoint -1852, -594, -1082}}.  The quest will update as soon as you approach any Remnant of Growth Medium, you don't have to actually talk to it.
1. Collect 6 broken stele fragments from the [[Queen's Hives - South|Queen's Hives]] to the east. They look like oblong grey stones, and can found around the following locations:
   - {{waypoint -2808.71, -537.25, -1304.0}}
   - {{waypoint -2720.57, -541.95, -1296.24}}
   - {{waypoint -2699.26, -544.11, -1239.00}}
   - {{waypoint -2788.71, -545.33, -1215.49}}
   - {{waypoint -2624.54, -544.01, -1268.34}}
1. Return the broken stele fragments to the Remnant of Growth Mediums.  Again, the quest will update as soon as you approach one.
1. Return to Brint

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
