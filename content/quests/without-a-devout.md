## Steps

Create 4 each of any 3 from the random list below:

- [[Feyiron Brigandine Gloves|Forged Feyiron Brigandine Gloves]]
- [[Feyiron Plate Gauntlets|Forged Feyiron Plate Gauntlets]]
- [[Feyiron Vanguard Gauntlets|Forged Feyiron Vanguard Gauntlets]]
- [[Feyiron Brigandine Coat|Forged Feyiron Brigandine Coat]]
- [[Feyiron Plate Cuirass|Forged Feyiron Plate Cuirass]]
- [[Feyiron Vanguard Cuirass|Forged Feyiron Vanguard Cuirass]]
- [[Feyiron Brigandine Leggings (Level 30) (Crafted)|Forged Feyiron Brigandine Leggings]]
- [[Feyiron Plate Greaves|Forged Feyiron Plate Greaves]]
- [[Feyiron Vanguard Greaves|Forged Feyiron Vanguard Greaves]]
- [[Feyiron Reverent Coif|Forged Feyiron Reverent Coif]]
- [[Feyiron Reverent Leggings|Forged Feyiron Reverent Leggings]]
- [[Feyiron Devout Barbute|Forged Feyiron Devout Barbute]]
- [[Feyiron Devout Cuirass|Forged Feyiron Devout Cuirass]]
- [[Feyiron Devout Greaves|Forged Feyiron Devout Greaves]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 24s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
