## Steps

1. listen to [[Chief Dampion]]
1. speak with [[High Shaman Lien]] at {{waypoint -1137, 531, 3304}}
1. Kill the following
   - 6 [[A molten sphere|molten spheres]] {{waypoint -643, -408, 3531}} in [[Flamespout Fields]] found around the small vulcans
   - 6 [[A rubble dervish|rubble dervishes]] in [[Flamespout Fields]] found in the area between {{waypoint -381, -424, 3586}} and the Caverns of Living Stone Griffin Station
   - 6 [[A blizzard dervish|blizzard dervishes]] in Howling Pass found between the Ry'Gorr Keep Station and the Scar Bridge Station
1. Speak with [[High Shaman Lien]] at {{waypoint -1156, -533, 3341}}
   - Speak with him so he opens the portal to [[Fina's Retreat]] in [[Great Divide]]
   - Speak with again to finish the quest
