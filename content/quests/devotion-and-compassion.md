Sister Leela Prendyn has been giving more thought to the conversation I had with Sir Bayden Cauldthorn. She has suggested it is time to speak with a few other chosen prophets; Pacificator Merrek, Quellious' prophet, and Kurista, the prophet of Growth. Hopefully, they will be able to reveal something about the lack of a prophet.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Pacificator Merrek]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] {{waypoint 352, 190, 493}}
1. Speak with [[Kurista]] in [[Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint 47, 93, 81}}
1. Seek out the seed of devotion that needs tending in [[New Tunaria]].
   - Speak to [[Oreyion Gwaeth]] {{waypoint -5, 74, -738}}.
   - Slay eight [[A Tunarian guard|Tunarian guards]], around {{waypoint -254, 53, -858}}
1. Speak with Sister Leela Prendyn in New Halas {{waypoint 35, 148, -33}}
1. Seek a way to help the Hooluks on Whisperwind Isle in [[Barren Sky]].
   - Speak to [[Folun Draftglider]] {{waypoint -621, 8, -592}}.
   - Help the Hooluk scout on the Isle of the Guardians {{waypoint 777, 405, -18}}.
   - Help the Hooluk scout on the Isle of Desolation {{waypoint 421, 292, -578}}.
   - Help the Hooluk scout on Strifewind Isle {{waypoint 70, -17, -679}}.
   - Help the Hooluk scout on the Isle of Aversion {{waypoint -309, 260, -699}}.
   - Help the Hooluk scout on the Isle of Discord {{waypoint -52, -152, -39}}.
   - Help the Hooluk scout on Cloudmist Isle {{waypoint -575, 250, -120}}.
   - Return to Folun Draftglider on Whisperwind Isle.
1. Return to Sister Leela to receive your rewards.

## Rewards

- +7,500 faction with **Erollisi Marr**
1. [[Swornlove's Altar of Erollisi Marr]]
