## Steps

![Imitation Ewer](images/Imitation_Ewers.jpg)![Metetherial Ewer](images/%28exchanged%29Metetherial_Ewer.jpg)

1. Swap the Imitation Ewers in Khali'Vahla for the stable ones in Khali'Yrzu (**NOTE:** Gathering is a group update, but Swapping them out is **NOT**.)
   - Gather 6 Imitation Ewers on the Vhala platform.
   - Swap them with 6 Metetherial Ewers at:
     - {{waypoint 98.83, 579.56, -588.93}}
     - {{waypoint 152, 586, -580}}
     - {{waypoint 162, 593, -525}}
     - {{waypoint 194, 595, -504}}
     - {{waypoint 190, 588, -615}}
     - {{waypoint 162, 584, -728}}.
1. Return to [[Chandira]] at {{waypoint -247, 578, -629}}
