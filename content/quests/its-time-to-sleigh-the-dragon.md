## Prerequisites

- You must have completed [[A Deepice Mystery]] to be able to hail [[Snarf Frostfoot]].

## Notes

- This quest can be completed in either the Easy or Hard version of [[The Icy Keep]].
- You can use [[Spring-Loaded Gnomish Stilts]] to jump the chasms.
- You don't need your [[Ice Axe of Vivid Hues]] to break the icy walls, this can be done with any weapon.
- If you leave the instance for any reason, all quest progress will be lost.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Icy Keep]] in [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]]. {{waypoint 104, -4, -1150}}
1. Defeat the first four egg guardians:
   - [[Gate Captain H'Bri]] {{waypoint 0, 1, -61}}
   - [[Tamer Nicoli]] {{waypoint -100, 1, -139}}
   - [[Kastus]] {{waypoint -100, 26, -87}}
     - To reach Kastus, use the board at {{waypoint 98, 0, -91}} to cross the gap leading to him.
   - [[Odaufe]] {{waypoint 95, 25, -83}}
     - Once Kastus is defeated, you can break the ice wall in the western tower at {{waypoint 82, 15, -69}} and reach Odaufe.
1. Gather the remaining three planks and together with the first one you used to reach Kastus, form a bridge to cross the large chasm with the blue swirly at {{waypoint 119, 19, -139}}. The location of the planks are:
   - {{waypoint -94, 1, -82}}
   - {{waypoint -85, 26, -70}}
   - {{waypoint 87, 25, -68}}
1. Climb down the wall. {{waypoint 62, 20, -215}}
1. Talk to [[A freezing goblin]]. {{waypoint 101.93, 0.63, -179.92}}
1. Kick the snow boulder right next to the goblin.
1. Jump down the chimney and defeat [[Ice Maiden D'Ina]]. {{waypoint -75, -47, -287}}
1. Click on the nearby egg twice to break it and spawn [[A Hatched Ice Dragon]]. It will stun you and escape. Once you are no longer stunned, click on the egg shards again and [[Snarf Frostfoot (The Icy Keep)|Snarf Frostfoot]] will spawn run towards you. Speak to him to finish the quest.
   - Speak to Snarf again to teleport back to [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]].
   - If you kill the dragon before it has a chance to encase you in ice, the quest becomes bugged and you must re-enter the zone and start the quest from the beginning. Make sure your mercenary and pets are on passive and you have no reactive items equipped.
