## Steps

1. . Pick up the traps you previously placed on top of the flat green glowing stones.
   - Trap 1 is located near {{waypoint 1314, 45, 2048}} ![A trap with captured rat](images/Check_the_Traps_quest_-_trap.jpg)
   - Trap 2 is located near {{waypoint 1383, 44, 2056}}
   - Trap 3 is located near {{waypoint 1401, 46, 2177}}
   - Trap 4 is located near {{waypoint 1245, 45, 2149}}
   - Trap 5 is located near {{waypoint 1276, 44, 2207}}
