## Notes

*A new tradeskill mission is available every 18 hours.*

## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 15 [[Celestial Kindling]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest the following within [[Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wind]]:
   - 25 [[Nimbic hide]] from [[Basilisk lair]]
   - 10 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Coalesced reef]]
   - 10 [[Strangler root]] from [[Strangler root break]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Zephyren Stew]] and craft an [[Zephyren Stew]] on a [[Stove & Keg]].
1. Speak to [[Fhreum]] at {{waypoint 49.97, -20.69, 63.94}} in the [[Myrist Commissary]] within the Great Library.

## Rewards

- At least 48000 status
- [[Elemental Mission Reward Crate]]
