## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 40+

## Steps

1. Your mission (if you accept it...) is to close the black market in [[Qeynos Harbor]], [[Qeynos Province District]].
   - In [[Qeynos Harbor]], find the canal that runs beside [[Aliindar|Aliindar's]] brokerage. Dive into the canal underwater, swim into the tunnel, and enter the hole in the wall to find the [[Smuggler's Grotto]]. {{waypoint 921, -29, 1}}
1. Destroy the black market goods.
   - Right click the dark brown crates to destroy them, then right-click them again and choose attack.
   - Three irate smugglers  will attack you and must be slain.
1. Question [[Kajiz]] in the [[Smuggler's Grotto]] to obtain the location of the remaining black market goods. {{waypoint 879, -32, 25}}
1. Enter the secret [[Smuggler's Vault]]. {{waypoint 881, -32, 21}}
   - Kill 4 smugglers
   - Destroy 8 wooden crates of stolen goods by attacking the boxes.
1. Return to [[Kyle Antihilus Bayle]].

### Notes

- There are four base types of smugglers - [[An assassin smugler|assassins]] (Assassins), [[A heretic smuggler|heretics]] (Inquisitors), [[An outlander smuggler|outlanders]] (Furies) and [[A thaumaturge smuggler|thaumaturges]] (Wizards). The particular types you encounter in this quest are determined at random.
