## Steps

Craft 12 items from the random list below:

- [[Ash Wand]]
- [[Ash Longbow|Ash Long Bow]]
- [[Ash Fighting Baton]]
- [[Spirit Totem of the Winged Viper (Crafted)|Spirit Totem of the Winged Viper]]
- [[Ash Sorcerer's Staff]]
- [[Carbonite throwing axe (Crafted)|Carbonite Throwing Axe]]
- [[Carbonite throwing dagger (Crafted)|Carbonite Throwing Dagger]]
- [[Carbonite throwing hammer (Crafted)|Carbonite Throwing Hammer]]
- [[Ash Bo Staff]]
- [[Vision Totem of the Owl (Crafted)|Vision Totem Of The Owl]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

## Rewards

- At Least 12s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
