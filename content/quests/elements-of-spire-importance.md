## Steps

- Travel to the [[Phantom Sea]]. For level 25 it's Thundering Steppes. (This is a scaling quest, repeatable, and only up till expansion release.)

Fly to the [[Grim Shales]] island (the far top right one) and defeat 10 [[An arcane runestone|arcane runestones]].

- For level 96 it is Withered Lands, Tears of Tunare, near loc {{waypoint -134, -712, -588}}

Defeat ten (10) [[A marsh elemental]].

- Return to [[Ziyaad Al'Majid]] in [[Teren's Grasp]].

## Rewards

- 3 [[Deepwater Copper]].
