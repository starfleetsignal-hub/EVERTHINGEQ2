## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[An Innothule threadspinner|Innothule threadspinner]]s.
   - Innothule threadspinners are found in the Innothule Swamp section of [[Moors of Ykesha]], along the path from the main swamp area to [[Tupta]].  {{waypoint -65, -80, 504}}. Fastest way to get there is take the balloon to Tupta and head north.

## Rewards

- At least 16613 status
- +500 faction with **The City of New Halas**
- 1 [[City Token]]
