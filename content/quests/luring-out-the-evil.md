## Steps

1. Return to [[The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers]] and kill [[Category:Mist Grinnin Goblins (Group)|mist grinnin goblins]] until you get the update.
   - This is an automatic update while killing any goblin in the zone, although the quest update feather may not show. It may or may not take a  long time to get the update. Some players have received the update after clearing just the first level and part of the second, while other players had to kill well over a hundred.
1. Return to [[Trelech Stonehome]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 44s 67c
- One of the following:
  - [[Charm of Defense]] (Scout choice)
  - [[Scepter of Healing]] (Priest choice)
  - [[Token of Defense]] (Fighter choice)
  - [[Wand of Ice]] (Mage choice)
