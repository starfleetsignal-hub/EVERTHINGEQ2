## Notes

- This quest can only be obtained via completion of [[Not So Safe Deposits]] and voids the ability to take [[Freesia Lastchain|Freesia Lastchain's]] questline.
- When you started [[Crime Pays Someone]] but want to do [[Lend a Helping Hand]] instead, then you can delete the [[Crime Pays Someone]] quest and pickup either of the quest. You still can't do both but change your selection that way.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Lyya Alumere]] {{waypoint 611, -23, -613}} in the [[House of Midst]] {{waypoint -518.37, 2.75, -261.66}} from [[Shadow Haven]].
   - *It doesn't matter which potion you choose. All the ingredient lists are the same and require hunting in [[Echo Caverns]].*
   1. Gather 8 **Chlorolotus** blooms in [[The Chlorochamber]] in Echo Caverns around {{waypoint 37, 155, 338}}.
      - *Shiny white clicky flowers on the water surface.*
   1. Hunt 5 [[A cavern beetle|cavern beetles]] for their eggs in [[The Chlorochamber]] {{waypoint 144, 159, 300}}.
   1. Hunt 6 [[A rock gnawer|rock gnawers]] for their ichor in [[The Upper Quarry]].
1. Return to [[Lyya Alumere]] in the [[House of Midst]] {{waypoint 611, -23, -613}}..
1. Turn in to [[Principa Haleene Sirinae]] {{waypoint -288, 0, 48}}.

## Rewards

- [[Arcane Component Bottle]]
- At least 2p 52g 59s
