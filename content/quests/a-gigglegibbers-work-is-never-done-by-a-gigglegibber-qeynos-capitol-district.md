## Notes

- This quest scales to the adventure level tier of the character, i.e. level 21-30 characters will receive the level 25 version of the quest.
- This quest is repeatable up to 10 times. per adventure level tier.
- You may need to kill more than the listed number of monsters in order to retrieve the required drops for this quest.
- Players adventure level 121+ will need to mentor down to a lower level range in order to get these quests offered.

## Steps

1. Slay the following monsters (for your adventure level tier) until you retrieve 5 of the indicated quest item:
   - **Tier 1 (Level 1-10)**
     - [[A muck toad|muck toads]] in [[The Peat Bog]]
     - [[A mystail matriarch|mystail matriarchs]] in [[Oakmyst Forest]]
   - **Tier 2 (Level 11-20)**
     - [[A mutated rat|mutated rats]] in [[Antonica]]
     - [[A beaded lizard|beaded lizards]] in [[Antonica]]
   - **Tier 3 (Level 21-30)**
     - [[A skeletal footsoldier|skeletal footsoldiers]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]]
     - [[An undead caretaker|undead caretakers]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]]
   - **Tier 4 (Level 31-40)**
     - [[A Runnyeye mercenary (Enchanted Lands)|Runnyeye mercenaries]] in [[Enchanted Lands]]
     - [[A threatening commoner|threatening commoners]] in [[Enchanted Lands]]
   - **Tier 5 (Level 41-50)**
     - [[A primal frostfin|primal frostfins]] in [[Everfrost]]
     - [[A wooly silk slinger|wooly silk slingers]] in [[Everfrost]]
   - **Tier 6 (Level 51-60)**
     - [[The Lesser Faydark]] (solo)
       - [[A grovespirit acolyte|Grovespirit acolytes]], [[A grovespirit caller|callers]] and [[A grovespirit defender|defenders]]
       - [[A honeybrood worker|honeybrood workers]]
     - [[The Sinking Sands]] (heroic)
       - [[An Anaz Mal veilguard|Anaz Mal veilguards]] in [[The Living Tombs]]
       - Mummies, such as [[A Sulite tjat (Silent City)|Sulite tjat]] in [[The Silent City]]
   - **Tier 7 (Level 61-70)**
     - [[The Barren Sky]] (solo)
       - [[A ridgestone defender|Ridgestone defenders]], [[A ridgestone guardian|guardians]], [[A ridgestone sentinel|sentinels]], and [[A ridgestone trampler|tramplers]]
       - [[A dryeye vehemence|Dryeye vehemences]] or [[A dryeye entangler]]
     - [[Faydwer (Continent)|Faydwer]] (heroic)
       - [[A Kragbak battler|Kragbak kobolds]] in [[Kaladim]]
       - [[An obsidian construct|Obsidian constructs]] in [[Mistmoore Catacombs]].
   - **Tier 8 (Level 71-80)**
     - [[A kunzar scaleripper|Kunzar scalerippers]] and [[A kunzar scaleshredder|scaleshredders]] in the rivers of [[Kunzar Jungle]]
     - [[A Rubble-rabble burrower|Rubble-rabble burrowers]], [[A Rubble-rabble detector|detectors]], and [[A Rubble-rabble rouser|rousers]] within the [[Rubble-rabble Caves]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
   - **Tier 9 (Level 81-90)**
     - [[A Toxxulia guardian|Toxxulia Guardians]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]
     - [[A slitherstrike mamba|Slitherstrike Mamba]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]]
   - **Tier 10 (Level 91-100)**
     - [[An insidious fearling|Insidious fearlings]]  {{waypoint -1736.32, 221.31, 1017.40}} in [[Eidolon Jungle]]
     - [[A churning obol|Churning obol]] in [[Obol Plains]]
   - **Tier 11 (Level 101-110)**
     - [[Auroric slug|Auroric slugs]] in [[Plane of Magic]]
     - [[Diamondwing volaserpent|Diamondwing volaserpents]] in [[Plane of Magic]]
   - **Tier 13 (Level 125)**
     - Lizards in [[Svarni Expanse]]
     - Pricklers in [[Svarni Expanse]]
   - **Tier 15 (Level 135)**
     - Frosthide maulers in [[Western Wastes]]
     - Golden vipers in [[Aether Wroughtlands]]

1. Return to [[Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber (North Qeynos)|Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]].
