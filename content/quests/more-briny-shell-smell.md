A mysterious ironblood myconid by the name of Verna Amanita has asked for my aid in securing his home back from invading forces!

## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[An umbrite terror|umbrite terrors]] in [[Blackanvil Bay]] and along the coastal waters to collect their shells
1. Place the shells on marked spots around the [[Abandoned Fungus Farm]].
1. Return to [[Verna Amanita|Verna]].

## Rewards

- At least 90g 72s
- +600 faction with **Ironblood Sprouts**

## Notes

This quest may be repeated up to ten times.
