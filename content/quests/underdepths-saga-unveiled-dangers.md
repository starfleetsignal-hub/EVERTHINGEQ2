Lamari is counting on me in order to dispel the magic that holds Queen Alwenielle, in hopes that it will reveal who or what is truly behind the death and destruction that threatens Maldura.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Stygian Threshold: The Howling Gateway (Advanced Solo)]] {{waypoint -748.93, 171.73, 167.97}}.
   - Clear the [[A Barkgut hunter|Barkgut hunters]] and [[A Barkgut gatherer|Barkgut gatherers]], then slay [[Gagrash]] and [[Hagrash]].
   - Right-click to dissolve the two barriers {{waypoint 53.42, 80.42, 186.00}}. They must be done quickly, one right after the other.
   - Gather the [[Illusion Trituration Crystal]] at {{waypoint 52.55, 82.33, 180.99}}.
   - **This can be done in the Heroic also**
1. Return to [[Lamari]] at the southernmost point of [[Zou'Lidelas]] {{waypoint -438.99, 239.93, 457.32}}.
1. Travel back to [[Maldura]] to [[Queen Alwenielle]] in the [[Palace of Unity]] {{waypoint 376.47, 85.85, -0.03}}, where you will smash the crystal.
   - Kill 4 members of the Primordial Malice near [[Queen Alwenielle]].
   - Speak to the Queen.
1. Seek out [[Tobon Blingden]], the Great Artificer {{waypoint -52.09, 5.35, 248.98}}. He is gone!
1. Rouse [[Barry Krangswell]] nearby and speak to him {{waypoint -60.18, 5.15, 241.55}}.
1. Return to to [[Queen Alwenielle]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g 32543 status
- +1000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
- [[Umbrite Adornment of Prismatic Resilience (Lesser)]]
