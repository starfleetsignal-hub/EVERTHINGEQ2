## Steps

1. After zoning into Eryslai, The Kingdom of Wind, speak with Rourre at {{waypoint 505.49, -201.77, -1396.77}}. He is just to the right of the zone-in.
1. Travel to the [[Misty Falls]] and speak with [[Nidinoff]] {{waypoint 249, -222, -1187}}.
1. Gather 8 **Air Scintilla**
   - Kill [[A servitor of air]] or [[A greater servitor of air]] for a chance to spawn air scintilla upon death.
   - Killing [[A lesser servitor of air]] will help respawn [[A servitor of air]] and [[A greater servitor of air]].
   - Look up, there's more up there.
   - The air scintillas have a bluish center with a white swirly bubble around them, as seen in the screenshot below.
1. Speak with [[Nidinoff]] at {{waypoint 249, -222, -1187}}.
