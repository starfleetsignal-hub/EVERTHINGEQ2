## Steps

1. Speak to [[Hildan]] at {{waypoint 230, 86, 51}}.
   - Collect 5 flowers from north of the camp.  Updates are the shimmering low-to-the-ground purple flowers around {{waypoint 594.16, 83.92, -148.89}} and {{waypoint 700.83, 80.73, -108.76}}.
   - Collect 5 [[Ethernere mushroom|mushrooms]] from north of the camp. They're small, glow, and are mixed among the flowers.
   - Kill 8 feral luijens
1. Return to [[Hildan]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- 1p 30g
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Deft Astral Ring]]
  - [[Precocious Astral Ring]]
  - [[Sacrosanct Astral Ring]] (Priest)
  - [[Savage Astral Ring]]
