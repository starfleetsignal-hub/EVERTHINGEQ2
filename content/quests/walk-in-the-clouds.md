## Steps

1. Travel (fly) to {{waypoint -280.76, -136.36, 2361.12}} and hail Chief Thrael'Gorr
1. Escort the orcs to {{waypoint 388.80, -55.71, 2636.96}} and kill the Drakonine Watcher (Level 92^ solo)
   - If you can fly, you can bypass the level 92 [[A sinister sentinel|sinister sentinels]] that are on the ground between your starting and ending locations.
1. Return to Tragg Sharptongue

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,500 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Shortbow]]
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Longbow]]
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Champion's Wand]]
  - [[Arctic Frostwind Oracle's Blessed Staff]]
