1. Find and gather 8 crates, found spawned on the sea bottom around [[Everstorm Bay]], mostly west of the dock:
   - {{waypoint -11, -50, 21}}
   - {{waypoint 9, -50, 46}}
   - {{waypoint 46, -56, 42}}
   - {{waypoint 57, -37, -120}}
