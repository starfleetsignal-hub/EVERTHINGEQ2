## Steps

1. Speak to [[Adeela]] in the Mercantile Court {{waypoint 1728, -282, 3470}}.
1. Harvest 3 of each or you can buy them if you dont want to harvest:
   - [[Amber]]
   - [[Bamboo shoot|Bamboo Shoots]]
   - [[Mottled pelt|Mottled Pelts]]
   - [[Quicksilver cluster|Quicksilver]]
   - [[Sumac lumber|Sumac Lumber]]
   - [[Titanium ore|Titanium Ore]]
   - [[Kejekan palmfruit|Kejekan Palmfruit]]
   - Note: The quest will also auto-update if you already have them in your inventory.
1. Return to [[Adeela]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- +1000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +2500 faction with **Craftkeepers**
