## Note

- Appears to max level out at level 90 in quest journal

## Steps

1. Obtain the following items:
   - **Tinkered Gizmo** (purple shiny)
   - **Tinkered Gadget** (purple shiny)
   - **Tinkered Widget** (purple shiny)
   - [[Gnomish Spirits!]] (complete the quest, "[[Tinkerfest Cheer]]")
   - 5 [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog]]
1. This quest auto-completes, so you don't have to return to the [[Guides of the Shattered Lands|Guide]] that gave you the quest... but they might offer refreshments or some other trinket if you do.
