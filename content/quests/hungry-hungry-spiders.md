## Steps

1. Scribe the [[Capru's Insect Trap]] recipe that you receive, and craft an [[Insect Trap]].  Materials required:
   - [[Work Bench]], 1 [[Sumac lumber]], 1 [[Titanium ore]], 1 [[Quicksilver cluster]], 1 [[Ethereal coal]]
1. Head to the Feerrott and trap 3 insects
   - Get close to the insects (*Capru suggests "some big, juicy jungle dragonflies"*) and use your insect trap on them
   - Mobs which seem to work are:
     - [[A wetland damselfly]] and [[A marsh damselfly]], which can be found just through the tunnel from the docks, among other places
1. Return to [[Capru]]

*Please note that if you have your auto attack mode set to ranged you will attack as you trap and not be able to trap. Change it to Melee while performing the trap actions.*

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
