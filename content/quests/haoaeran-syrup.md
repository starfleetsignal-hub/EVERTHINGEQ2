## Steps

1. Collect samples from 5 barrels of syrup .  They can be found at the roots of the large trees in [[Haoaera]].
   - These are the same barrels that you need for the quest [[Stage 1]].
   - The barrels are guarded by [[A Haoaeran tapper|Haoaeran tappers]] that you will need for the quest [[Dismantling Haoaera: Tappers]].
   - Sample locations: {{waypoint 1711, 131, -434}}, {{waypoint 1781, 127, -444}}, {{waypoint 1759, 131, -410}}.
1. Return to Groag.

## Rewards

- At least 17s 30c
- +5000 faction with **Mountain Throms of Mok Island**
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Fighter's Necklace]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Necklace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Necklace]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Necklace]] (All Mages)
