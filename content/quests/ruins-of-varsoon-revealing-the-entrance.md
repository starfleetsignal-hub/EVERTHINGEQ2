## Steps

1. Find the hidden entrance to [[Varsoon the Undying|Varsoon's]] new lair in [[The Thundering Steppes]]. This is located south of the [[Ruins of Karana]] at {{waypoint -483, 4, 1064}}.
1. Return to Zaen.
1. Find the key to Varsoon's lair on [[An Archaic Fury]] in [[The Crypt of Betrayal]] around {{waypoint 7.86, -4.77, -0.05}}.
   - Wanders throughout the zone.
   - Three placeholders: [[A restless corpse]], [[A forsaken shriller]], [[A deathly scarab (Crypt of Betrayal)|a deathly scarab]].
   - Former [[Druid]].
1. Return to Zaen Kalystir.

## Rewards

- At least 22s 51c
- [[Keep of Immortality Key]]
