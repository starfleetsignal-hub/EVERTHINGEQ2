## Description

"It seems that word of my accomplishments has spread like a wildfire. The Innkeeper here would like to set me up with my own home. I can also speak with the Innkeeper about the specifics of housing." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Buy a room at [[Silver Leaf Inn]] (the door near the Innkeeper) and enter it. You can also right click the door and select "Tour" to complete the quest without purchasing an inn room.

## Rewards

- [[Basic Chandelier]]
- [[Basic Mirror]]
- [[Basic Table]]
- [[A market bulletin board]]

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Innkeeper Twiggy: "Hello, [Player Character], I am the Innkeeper for Kelethin. My name is Twiggy! We received word of your arrival and have a room prepared for you. Is there anything you'd like to know about housing before you move in?"
- Player Character: "Please."
- Innkeeper Twiggy: "What would you like to know?"
- Player Character: "How can I place an item in my house?"
- Innkeeper Twiggy: "To place an item in your house, you must first find the item in your inventory. Once you've done so, right-click on the item and select the "place" option. Then you'll see a tinted outline of the item in your house. When the item is highlighted in green you can place it in that location. If it is highlighted in red, you cannot."
- Player Character: "What kind of housing is available to me?"
- Innkeeper Twiggy: "There are a few different types of housing available in Kelethin. This particular tree is very comfortable and cozy, though you might later prefer to reside in one of those modern rental acorns."
- Player Character: "Is any other housing available?"
- Innkeeper Twiggy: "Yes, Kelethin has houses ranging from the basic one bedroom model on up to four bedroom apartments, although the most ostentatious of these are only available to the most prestigious citizens of the city."
- Player Character: "What is the marketboard for?"
- Innkeeper Twiggy: "The marketboard is a house item, similar to books, beds, pets and other such items. What sets the marketboard apart from the rest however is that once your marketboard is mounted on a wall in your home you can use it to sell items to other citizens of Kelethin. These items can be purchased by your fellow citizens at a Broker. Our Broker is based out of the Kelethin First Regional Bank."
- Player Character: "How do I sell items using my marketboard?"
- Innkeeper Twiggy: "Selling through your market board works the same way as directly selling through a broker, it just allows you to do it from the comfort of your own home."
- Player Character: "I can sell things through a broker?"
- Innkeeper Twiggy: "Yes. For a fee, brokers are willing to post the goods you are willing to sell to other adventurers. We have our very own broker here within Kelethin, too!"
- Player Character: "What is my house for?"
- Innkeeper Twiggy: "You will find that your house has many uses, the first of which is the ability to store house items. Each house has a limit to the number of house items you can place in it, so as you gain more possessions you wish to display you will need larger houses to do so."
- Player Character: "Anything else?"
- Innkeeper Twiggy: "Yes, houses have what we call vaults, which are extra storage within your house. The larger your house is, the more storage space is available. By placing items in this house vault and using your marketboard, you can sell these items without being in your room."
- Player Character: "That's enough for now, thanks."

Use the room door to be given your inn room. You can right-click the door and then click on the "Access" button to get your room. Or, double-click to bring up the "Buy" option immediately. Once you have purchased your room, click on "Enter" to enter your room.
