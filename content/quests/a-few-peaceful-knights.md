## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
  - Quest becomes repeatable after initial completion
- Enemies only spawn at night

## Steps

1. Return to [[Deepwater Watch]] island {{waypoint -505, 4, 486}} and kill the following
   - Dispatch 4 x [[A backstabbed sentry]]
   - Remove 4 x [[A deceived watcher]]
   - Defeat 4 x [[A betrayed guardian]]

## Reward

- 25 x [[Scarred Dinar]] *awarded only on first time completion.*
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- [[Port Woe|+2,000]]
