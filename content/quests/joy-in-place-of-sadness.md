## Steps

1. Gather 5 pieces of coral from underneath the glacier (Note: You may want water breathing)
   - Go to the edge of the sheet of ice {{waypoint -1176, -542, -2419}}  ![Coral suspended from the ice under the water](images/Joy_in_Place_of_Sadness_quest_-_coral.jpg)
   - Go into the water and underneath the sheet you will see the coral.
1. Return to Rui
1. Listen to the story of Nipik to receive a house item, [[The Legend of Nipik]]
1. Place the coral decorations in the marked places around town
   - First decoration {{waypoint -1576, -538, -2349}}
   - Second decoration {{waypoint -1617, -535, -2408}}
   - Third decoration {{waypoint -1605, -537, -2369}}
   - Fourth decoration {{waypoint -1562, -536, -2403}}
   - Fifth decoration {{waypoint -1589, -535, -2394}}
1. Return to Rui.
