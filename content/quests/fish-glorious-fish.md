## Steps

1. Fish for 15 Spotted Churnfish from nodes titled *A School of Churnfish* in the water near the docks and the point to the North.
1. Return to [[Jessith Malrion]] on the docks of [[Omen's Call]].

## Rewards

- At least 5g 84s 70c
