## Steps

1. Recover some stolen items in Qeynos Harbor.
   - Find a ship's spar.  This is in a barrel at {{waypoint 817, -23, 31}}.
   - Find a ship's sail.  This is in a barrel at {{waypoint 799, -22, 48}}.
   - Find a ship's map.  This is behind a wheel on the wall {{waypoint 804, -24, 63}} inside Fish's Alehouse and Inn.
1. Speak to [[Dajor Botswein]].
1. Recover the ship's lamp from any pirate in the [[The Thundering Steppes]], in the dry riverbed near [[Obsid Boulderboom]] {{waypoint 752, -20, 937}}.<br>*NOTE: This is an auto-update, not a drop and may take several kills to get the update.*
1. Return to [[Dajor Botswein]] who will then direct you to [[Barmaid Drinna]] in [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 786, -23, 84}}.
1. She will then give you your reward and the next quest in the series: [[From Sea to Sea]]

## Rewards
