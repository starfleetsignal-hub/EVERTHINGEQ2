I finally located Douglan Wakerunner, captain of The Swimming Oak, outside the northern end of Kamapor, the centaur village located in Svarni Expanse. He has no time to talk though, as he is helping to defend the village from some savage crocodiles!

## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[A savage shoal mangler|savage shoal manglers]] (the crocodiles in the ponds above the ferris wheel){{waypoint 79, 24, 329}}.
1. Return to [[Captain Douglan Wakerunner]].
1. Kill 8 [[A savage shoal mangler|savage shoal manglers]] in the Kamapor paddy fields at the following locations: {{waypoint -8.02, 29.95, 480.24}}  or {{waypoint 116, 17, 533}} or {{waypoint -7, 29, 491}}.
1. Return to [[Captain Douglan Wakerunner]] at {{waypoint 34, 12, 429}}.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- [[Crate of Kamapor Tea]]
