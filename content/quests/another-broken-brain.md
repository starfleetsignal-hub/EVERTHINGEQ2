## Notes

1. Go to Strifewind Isle to enter Treasury of Claw. Only 1-3 players may enter the zone.  This is NOT the same [[Treasury of Claw]] as the [[Isle of Desolation]]; there is NOT a wall that you can run and jump on near Nagnag (to make the mobs from the hall drop off you as you can in [[Treasury of Claw]] on the [[Isle of Desolation]] so either kill as you move toward her room or expect a lot of mobs on you once you reach her room.
1. Nagnag is only 64^ but her babies, who she will call to help her, are 64vv heroic encounters. Take some help, or kill them first.
1. [[Granddaddy Strifewing]] is a random spawn in one of the four rooms.
1. If [[Granddaddy Strifewing]] refuses to spawn or the bot happened to spawn at an impossible to reach location, you can reset by exiting and re-entering the zone.

## Steps

1. Enter the [[Strifewind Isle]], [[Treasury of Claw]] {{waypoint -59, -7, -552}}. Head east and kill [[Nagnag Strifewing]] (64^) to open the doorway to the room next door. (Newly opened doorway is back out in the main hallway.) Climb up the wall and kill [[Granddaddy Strifewing]] (64^) in one of the four rooms, for example, {{waypoint -169.61, 110.89, 3.05}} and then pick up the bot.
1. Return to [[Frigui Togginscog|Frigui]] on the [[Isle of Desolation]].
