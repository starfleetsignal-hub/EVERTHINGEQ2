## Steps

1. Perform the following tasks:
   - Gather 10 bone meal *Note: They look like white mounds.*
     - Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -148.47, 5.82, 493.57}} {{waypoint 34.83, 8.68, 471.09}} {{waypoint 0.45, 10.90, 426.59}} {{waypoint -406.25, 5.07, 459.14}}
     - {{waypoint -487.02, 8.92, 531.50}} {{waypoint -619.51, 8.07, 564.02}} {{waypoint -609.24, 4.87, 651.39}} {{waypoint -606.61, 4.78, 661.26}}
     - {{waypoint -484.16, 6.94, 753.05}} {{waypoint -385.24, 5.81, 610.53}} {{waypoint -355.45, 5.36, 635.52}}
   - Kill 8 [[A toxin eel|toxin eels]] or [[Thresh fins]] near {{waypoint -254.00, -0.85, 699.57}} The entanglers and turtles seem to be placeholders.
1. Return to [[Ashter Ryswell]] at {{waypoint -698.02, 12.62, 459.01}}.
1. Gather the following:
   - 8 sunbloom sprouts.
   - ![](images/Sunbloom_Sprout.png)
     - Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -588.61, 4.55, 648.02}} {{waypoint -525.50, 4.05, 709.73}} {{waypoint -438.69, 4.09, 539.67}} {{waypoint -398.91, 3.85, 505.88}}
     - {{waypoint -639.98, 3.95, 624.15}} {{waypoint -666.01, 4.46, 596.84}} {{waypoint -693.78, 4.14, 600.84}} {{waypoint -655.52, 4.20, 570.80}}
     - {{waypoint -709.07, 4.26, 585.41}} {{waypoint -545.01, 3.63, 642.90}}
   - 12 pheromones from [[A toxic sunbloom|toxic sunblooms]].
1. Return to [[Ashter Ryswell]] at {{waypoint -698.02, 12.62, 459.01}}.
