## Steps

1. Travel to [[Lavastorm]] and speak with [[Sumarvaldr]] in [[Solusek Valley]]. {{waypoint -59, 0, -482}}
1. Defeat Sumarvaldr in combat to receive [[Shard of the Storm Reaper]].
   - Sumarvaldr leaves the area once this part is done, and has an estimated 5-minute respawn. The player must get the final blow or the quest will not update, so suspend any merc if you have one active.
1. Travel to [[Everfrost]] and speak with [[Faythwen Summerglade]] at the entrance to [[Bitterwind Trench]]. {{waypoint 227, -19, 54}}
1. Kill [[Arnbjorg]], located on [[The Lady's View]], to receive [[First Half of the Shard of Pines]].  {{waypoint 153, -9, -1254}}
1. Kill [[Gudrun]], inside the nearby [[Kromise Caves]], to receive [[Second Half of the Shard of the Pines]]. {{waypoint -9, -10, -1150}}
1. Examine either shard half from your inventory. They will combine (after cool sound effect) into [[Shard of the Pines]].
1. Return to Askr.
