## Notes

This is the good-aligned version of the world event quest and is repeatable. The evil version of this quest is [[Bring on the Blight]].

## Starting the Quest

In Qeynos, Speak to [[Seeress Ealaynya Ithis]] in the building at the [[Temple of Life]]. Before you can talk to her you have to talk to any blighted creature around the city.

## Steps

1. Talk to the [[Seeress Ealaynya Ithis]] at the [[Temple of Life]].
1. Use the given potion on any blighted citizen within the city (respawn is a few minutes).
1. Return to the Seeress and you'll get the [[Pestilent Serum 92C]] another cure potion.
1. Use it on 12 of the blighted citizens which you can find in all parts of Qeynos. Placeholders are healed citizens. Respawn is a few minutes.
1. Return to the Seeress.
1. Go and find a dark robed one either behind the mane of the lion, its behind the Lions Mane Inn {{waypoint 558, -10, 120}} or above the bells within the shadow of the crown in South Qeynos.
1. Kill [[Putnam Chortlewhirl]], he is invisible and will jump you when you are near on of the 2 spots. He will also run after you if he is up and you port into South Qeynos using the mariners bell.
1. Return to the Seeress to finish the quest.
