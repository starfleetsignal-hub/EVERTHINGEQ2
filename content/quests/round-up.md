## Steps

1. Find and leash three [[A Kunarkian tusker|Kunarkian tuskers]] {{waypoint -87, -77, 330}} and tie them to the post in front of [[Gilriss the Handler]]. The tuskers cannot be leashed while they are in combat.
   - See the talk page for more information.
1. Speak with Gilriss the Handler.

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- +2000 faction with **Riliss**
- [[Dracurion Militiaman's Gauntlets]]
- [[Nathsarian Torque of Energy]] (necromancer)
