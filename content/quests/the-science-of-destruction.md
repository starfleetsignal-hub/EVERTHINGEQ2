## Description

The gnemlin districts of Maldura have been gated shut by the Shields of Maldura to protect the rest of the city from an attack from within. I've been tasked with helping secure the district.

## Steps

- Defeat targets within [[Maldura: Algorithm For Destruction (Heroic)]]
1. Dismantle the pump operators.
   - [[Undying Apprentice (Heroic)|Undying Apprentice]], [[Undying Mechanic (Heroic)|Undying Mechanic]], and [[Undying Technician (Heroic)|Undying Technician]]
1. Kill the bringer of destruction.
   - [[Black Bolt (Heroic)|Black Bolt]]
