## Description

"I ran into an unfortunate (and lost) Qeynosian scout who had been chased up a tree by a pack of Morak devourers. He was shaken up by the incident. He's terrified of canines and had no idea that the Moraks in the area were hounds. I'll take a quick look ahead and clear out the Moraks for him." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill 5 more [[A Morak devourer|Morak devourers]].
1. Return to the scout {{waypoint 271, -5, -11}}.
1. Complete the following in no particular order:
   - Explore [[Sapswill Hill]] {{waypoint 145, 5, -151}}.
   - Explore [[The Abandoned Village (Queen's Colony)|The Abandoned Village]] {{waypoint 200, -1, -184}}.
   - Kill 5 additional Morak devourers
1. Return to the scout to receive [[A Diplomatic Gift]].
1. Deliver the gift to [[A Sapswill soothsayer]] {{waypoint -71, 13, -163}} at the [[The Last Stand]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 3s 37c
- [[Torn Scout Pants]]
