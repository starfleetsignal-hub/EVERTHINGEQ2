## Steps

1. Create recipe for Plex's invisibility cloaks
   1. Scribe the recipe [[Specification: Invisibility Cloak]] received from Plex.
   1. Create the [[Recipe: Invisibility Cloak]] at an [[Engraved Desk]] (one is located in the corner of the main tent in Scarstone, another in a smaller side tent {{waypoint 4424, -821, 362}})
      - Requires 1 [[Effulgent incense]]
      - Requires 1 [[White peony tea leaf]]
      - Requires 1 [[Parchment (Out of Sight Out of Mind)|Parchment]] provided by Plex.
1. Create 7 [[Invisibility Cloak|Invisibility Cloaks]] at a [[Sewing Table & Mannequin|Sewing Table]] (one is located in the corner of the main tent in Scarstone, another in a smaller tent {{waypoint 4450, -821, 377}})
   1. Scribe the Invisibility Cloak recipe
   1. Examine the [[Ingredients for Plex's Cloaks]] received from Plex to receive the following materials required to craft the cloaks:
      - [[Orafik Eye]]
      - [[Fearstalker Pelt]]
      - [[Prismatic Scale]]
      - Requires 7 [[Effulgent filament|effulgent filaments]]
1. Return the Invisibility Cloaks to Plex

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
