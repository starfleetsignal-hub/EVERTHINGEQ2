## Steps

![Ritual Tablet for Step 5](images/Enlightenment_Ritual_Tablet.jpg)

1. Find Sanuit Al'radyr in Quel'ule {{waypoint -435, 389, 831}}
1. Go to [[Erud's Point]] {{waypoint 75, 450, 185}} and use the Eye of Quellious given to you by Sanuit.
1. Return to Sanuit.
1. See Hulgin Khizar {{waypoint -112, 366, 717}}
1. Search for ritual tablets. These are all around the large region around Quel'ule, mainly near rocks or trees. and look like red broken books. The are NOT a group update and disappear when harvested. They do respawn and can be reharvested but not quickly.Known locs include:
   - *For a list of known locations, see the talk page.*
1. Return to Hulgin.
