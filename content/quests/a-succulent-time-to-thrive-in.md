## Notes

- The quest title can have **'s**ucculent' or **'S**ucculent' They are the same quest.
- This is a sub-quest to [[The Adventure of the Undercover Disciple]].

## Introduction

After finding curious seeds on some bloody clothes in the Condemned Catacombs in Antonica, I took them to a master of plant knowledge in Qeynos' Elddar Grove.

## Steps

1. Burn the succulent seedpod (![a cactus-like plant](images/Image:A_succulent_seedpod.jpg)) in [[The Sinking Sands]] {{waypoint -1400, -230, -530}}, in the grassy area at the [[Croc Hunter Camp]] north of the dock.
1. Burn a seedpod in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] {{waypoint 490, -30, 140}}, right past the gates. Watch out for the roaming orcs.
1. Burn a seedpod in the [[The Commonlands]] {{waypoint 740, -35, -670}}, right after the zone-in from [[Nektulos Forest]], along the north zone wall.
   - Low-level characters may find foot travel through Nektulos Forest dangerous.  If you cannot take the griffons:
     - Use the new bell at the Nektulos Forest dock; to avoid the level 70 guards at the Commonlands dock, swim through the water.
     - Take the spires in [[Antonica]] to [[Kingdom of Sky|the Overrealm]] and back to the Commonlands.
1. Return to [[Elgorn Birchgrove]] in [[The Elddar Grove]].

## Rewards

- Experience only
