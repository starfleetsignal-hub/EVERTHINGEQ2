## Steps

1. Speak to Yendilor inside Skyshrine's Covenant District while on the quest Disc of Binding Ruined to begin this quest.
1. Ignite 4 fuses linked to vials of dragonfire and find 8 crates of lesser binding stones. These can be performed/found simultaneously.
   - **Vials** reported locations:
     - {{waypoint 161, 1, -158}}
     - {{waypoint -45, 1, -213}}
     - {{waypoint 18, 1, -173}}
     - {{waypoint 222, 1, -245}}
   - **Crates** reported locations *(Note:The crates respawn)*:
     - {{waypoint -182, 1, -223}}
     - {{waypoint -207, 1, -183}}
     - {{waypoint -28, 1, -218}}
     - {{waypoint -23, 1, -136}}
     - {{waypoint -103, 1, -266}}
     - {{waypoint -166, 1, -390}}
     - {{waypoint -198, 1, -315}}
     - {{waypoint -107, 1, -273}}
1. Speak to Yendilor to complete the quest.
   - *Note: You should now be able to complete the quest [[Disc of Binding Ruined]].*
