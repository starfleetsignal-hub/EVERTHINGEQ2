## Note

Ignore the Map, the blue dot, and the clear indication that Bilgeron is with you in The Bonemire. He isn't. You have to return to the Tenebrous Tangle to speak with Bilgeron. He will give you "Bilgeron's Map and Notes"... find this in your inventory and examine it to receive the next quest: [[One Last Chance at Treasure]]

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Bilgeron Cogsworth IV|Bilgeron]] near the spires on the [[Temple Grounds]] island in the [[Tenebrous Tangle]]
