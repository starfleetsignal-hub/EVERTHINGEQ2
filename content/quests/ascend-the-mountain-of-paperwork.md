## Steps

1. Speak to the following NPCs:
   - [[Taskmaster Scally]] {{waypoint -212, -99, 881}}
   - [[Taskmaster Rik]] {{waypoint 20, -122, 748}}
   - [[Taskmaster Tsrazz]] {{waypoint 5, -125, 746}}
1. Return to Taskmaster Gax.
1. Speak with [[Associate Tuz]] at the gates of [[Outer Sebilis]]. {{waypoint -98, -120, 956}}

- [[Glowing Gem of Striking]]

  - [[Commanding Stud of Presense]]
  - [[Earring of Domination]]
  - [[Hoop of Dominion]]
  - [[Yielding Earhoop of Surrendering]]
