## Steps

1. Go back to [[Big Bend]] (entrance at {{waypoint 58, -12, 270}}) and fight [[Skalmos]].  He will be on the pier.
   - *Note: If you pull him off of the pier and kill him he will re-spawn another attackable version of him but you can still hail the NPC he turns into after death*
   - *Note: for level 100 Chars: when you attack him and his health doesn't drop really .. unequip all your weapons/armor/jewelry everything and just melee him bare hand*
1. Once defeated speak to him and receive message to give to [[Gisuka Voll|Gisuka]] back at [[Temple of War]].
1. Go to [[Dethknell Citadel]] (portal at {{waypoint 8, -19, 13}}) to speak to [[Lucan D'Lere|The Overlord]] in his office.
1. Return To [[Gisuka Voll|Gisuka]] in [[The City of Freeport]].
1. Enter the [[Temple of War]] through the secret entrance behind the temple ([[The Dismal Den]]) {{waypoint -116, -8, -157}}
1. Speak to [[Gisuka Voll|Gisuka]] when you zone in. Go downstairs and wait for the events to happen.
1. After the exchange, zone out and speak to [[Gisuka Voll|Gisuka]] to complete the quest.
