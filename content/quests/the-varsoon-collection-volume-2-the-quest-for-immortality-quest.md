## Steps

Within [[The Ruins of Varsoon]]:

1. Kill 10 loam or clay goos.
   - [[A living clay]], [[A loam cyst]], [[A loam blister]], [[A loam boil]] all update, they're found throughout the cavern half of the zone.
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 10 loam or clay goos.
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 10 loam or clay goos.
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 6 bats.
   - [[A deafening shrieker|deafening shrieker]]s, [[A dust shriller|dust shriller]]s and [[A mud shriller|mud shriller]]s, found throughout the cavern half of zone
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 6 bats.
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 6 bats.
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 10 flying tomes.
   - [[An enchanted tome|enchanted tome]]s and [[A spirited tome|spirited tome]]s, found at {{waypoint 237, -8, 55}} and {{waypoint 12, 7, 71}}.  The [[Tome of Death]] and [[Tome of Life]] also update.
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 10 flying tomes.
1. Examine the book.
1. Kill 10 flying tomes.
1. Examine the book.
