## Notes

This quest requires completion of the collection [[Lexicon of Spaek]], which is the completion of all Red Book POI quest collection turn-ins for all 4 zones, and all dropped quest collection turn-ins for all 4 zones. See the talk page for more information: Heck of a Ghoul Time

You MUST visit all POI's and dropped yourself until you have the achievement [[Triumphs Achievements|Triumphs]] - VOV - [[I Dropped What I Was Doing]] and [[Came to Me in a Vision]]. Can finish the collections with runes from bank but she wont talk to you if you havent done yourself

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Sister Ghoulinda]] in [[Ghoulinda's Lair]]
   - Door is at [[Irewater Pond]] {{waypoint 540.33, 95.51, 165.02}}

## Rewards

- at least 68p 70g 67s 92c
- [[Sister Ghoulinda]] now offers a Solo Daily Mission Quest
