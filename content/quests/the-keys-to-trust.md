## Prerequisites

- This heroic quest is part 6 of the [[Claymore Timeline]]. You must have completed the previous steps to begin this one.

## Steps

1. Kill [[Drovbaaz the Restrainer]] or his placeholder [[An Awakened cellkeeper|an Awakened cellkeeper]] {{waypoint 69, 126, 174}} in the cellkeeper area (15 minute respawn)
1. Speak to [[Lady Erillis]] in the Hidden Refuge in the Tenebrous Tangle {{waypoint 165, -232, 174}}

## Rewards

- Your choice of:
  - [[Chestguard of the Clouds]]
  - [[Wind-worked Hauberk]]
  - [[Tunic of Rituals]]
  - [[Robe of Dark Corridors]]
