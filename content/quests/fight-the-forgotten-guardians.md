## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Forest Ruins]]
1. Slay five [[A forgotten guardian|forgotten guardians]], [[A fiery forgotten guardian|fiery forgotten guardians]], or [[A bound watcher|bound watchers]].
1. Return to Marshal Surefoot.

## Rewards

- At least 55c
- One of the following:
  - [[Leatherfoot cap]]
  - [[Leatherfoot headguard]]
  - [[Reinforced leatherfoot headguard]]
