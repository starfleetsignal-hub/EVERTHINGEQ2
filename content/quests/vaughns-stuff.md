## Steps

1. Slay [[A Blackfurl deckhand|Blackfurl deckhands]] at [[Blackfurl Beach]] until you recover his satchel. {{waypoint 976, -25, -878}}
1. Slay Blackfurl deckhands until you recover his journal.
1. Slay Blackfurl deckhands until you recover his ring.
1. Slay Blackfurl deckhands until you recover his stolen letter.
1. Return to [[Outlander Vaughn]] for your reward.

- At least 1s 20c
