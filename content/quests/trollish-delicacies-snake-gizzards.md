## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[An Innothule glider|Innothule gliders]] for their gizzards (auto-update)
1. Return the gizzards to [[Grackz]] in [[Grobb]]

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- -150 faction with **Guktan Guard**
- +1,000 faction with **Clan Grobb**
