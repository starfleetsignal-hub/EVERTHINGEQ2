## Steps

Gil McMartin furnished me with details of what will next be necessary to stem the tide of bile from the Citadel: I must avenge the looting of slain Qeynosians by foul merchants, through despoiled their ill-gotten wares stored in the City of Freeport.

1. Within 20 minutes, destroy 5 merchant carts (wooden carts of crates with blue sparkles) in the merchant areas of Freeport.  Locations include {{waypoint 211.75, -3.67, 117.17}}, {{waypoint 201.10, -3.49, 68.57}}, {{waypoint -128.79, -37.86, 61.15}}, {{waypoint -224.15, -56.07, 131.55}}, {{waypoint -228.80, -56.08, 72.70}}.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +2000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- **Note**: This quest does not grant any experience or achievement experience.
