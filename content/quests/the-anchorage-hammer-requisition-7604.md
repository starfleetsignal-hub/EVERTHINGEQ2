## Steps

1. Harvest the following:
   - 18 [[Silvered fleck]] from [[Flecked mass]]
   - 15 [[Strangler root]] from [[Strangler root break]]
   - 15 [[Dewpalm lumber]] from [[Fallen dewpalm branch]]
   - 10 [[Basilisk meat]] from [[Basilisk lair]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Gear Polishing Kit]].
1. Craft the [[Gear Polishing Kit]] on a [[Work Bench]].
1. Speak to [[Bondrin Steelfist]] at {{waypoint 75.14, -8.46, -75.25}}.
