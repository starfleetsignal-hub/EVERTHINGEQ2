After I bandaged Brytthel, she promised to tell me more of what's going on, but first asked me to bring news of her injury in the mines to someone called Brumley Langboom in the city. She gave me her signet ring so the guards will know I can be trusted, and told me to meet her in the city later if I want to know more.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Maldura]] at {{waypoint 262, 151, 482}}) and speak to [[Brumley Langboom]] at {{waypoint -81.41, 13.09, 195.58}}.
1. Return to the entrance of the [[Glittervein Depths]] (Outer Maldura/Thalumbra) Exit [[Maldura]] and release the scanning device at {{waypoint 523, 131, 432}} Its a sparkly area on the ground in front of the cave entrance.
1. Return to Brumley.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- [[Coin of the Great Artificer]]
- +1,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +5,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
