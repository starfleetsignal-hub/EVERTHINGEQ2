This is a [[Mikk Eishammer#Related Quests|Forgemasters of Thurgadin Supply Task]]

## Steps

1. Cook 5 portions of [[Hearty Coldain Stew|hearty coldain stew]] for the hungry warriors
   - The Hearty Coldain Stew recipe can be found within the [[Thurgadin Supply Manifesto]] recipe book, which can be obtained by clicking on the Forgemasters Recipe Notes on the counter in front of [[Mikk Eishammer]]
   - The [[Brontotherium Steak]] and [[Invigorating herbs]] required for the recipe can be obtained by clicking on the bucket of **Brontotherium Steak Mix** on the counter in front of [[Mikk Eishammer]]
   - Required materials:
     - [[Stove & Keg]]
     - 5 [[Brontotherium Steak]]
     - 5 [[Liquid]]
     - 10 [[Invigorating herbs]]
     - 5 [[Ethereal kindling]]
1. Speak to [[Mikk Eishammer]]

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +1500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
