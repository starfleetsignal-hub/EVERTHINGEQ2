## Steps

![A pile of what??](images/Butcherblock_pile_of_excavated_objects.jpg)

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Kill 10 [[A Yarpsnarl sapper|Yarpsnarl sappers]] in the chasm in [[The Broken Fields]] {{waypoint -521, 114, 489}}
   - Collect 4 soil samples from the piles of excavated objects in the chasm in [[The Broken Fields]] {{waypoint -522, 113, 489}}
     - Several piles can be found at {{waypoint -370, 109, 440}}. If you are doing this quest with others, everyone doing the quest must harvest 4 samples themself.
1. Return to [[Wyrwynne Shadhe]].
