## Steps

![a loose dragon vertebra](images/A_loose_dragon_vertebra.jpg)

1. Harvest 6 [[Dragon vertebra]] on The [[Shattered Weir]]. These are ground spawns located all around the [[Shattered Weir]]. All spawns are located by larger, easily visible fossils. Confirmed locations are:
   - -607 -450 +323
   - -675 -438 +436
   - -780 -451 +178
   - -780 -470 +100
   - -849 -471 +110
   - -793 -450 +368
   - -832 -450 +257
   - -776 -450 +371
1. Return to [[Drukin Tellfizz]] for your reward.
   - *Note: To get out, move along the ledge on the western side until you get to {{waypoint -770, -460, 199}} where you can find a clickable rock.*
   - *Note: Respawn rate on the nodes is like 30 seconds.  Find one, sit there for 3 minutes.  No combat required.*

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Ancient Bronze Band]]
  - [[Ancient Gemmed Band]]
  - [[Ancient Gold Band]]
  - [[Ancient Silvered Band]]
