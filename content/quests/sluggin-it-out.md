## Notes

- **Ghawg only spawns after 8pm game time**
- This quest is the second option of the dialog, when you hail him the first time.

## Steps

1. Hunt 12 forest slugs ([[A forest slug]]), for meat
   - *Note: 3 at {{waypoint 362, -198, -495}}, 3 at {{waypoint 387, -228, -232}}, 3 at {{waypoint 298, -201, -159}}, 3 at {{waypoint 183, -234, -283}}, 3 at {{waypoint 6, -197, -5}}*
1. Return to Ghawg at {{waypoint 600, -157, -628}}

## Rewards

- [[Grisly Remnant Bone Totem]]
- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 15108 status
