## Steps

1. Enter [[The Tombs of Night]].
1. Kill Neonate Vampires around {{waypoint 57, -22, -113}} until you get five Symbols of Darkness updates.
1. Kill Adherent Vampires around {{waypoint 11, -25, 16}} until you get three Torques of Station updates.
1. Kill [[Khasra D’Han]]. {{waypoint 83, -24, 74}}
   - He will drop [[A hastily written note]]
1. Kill [[Viil N’Rahn]]. {{waypoint 20, -38, 111}} If you drop down from entrance level you will need to go up the ramp to Maw of Mortality slightly until you get the message 'You feel near where the note indicated Vlil might be' otherwise he won't be spawned.
1. Return to [[Tarren Marrsen]].
1. [[Tarren Marrsen]] then sends you to [[Aleeshi Monala]] for your rewards and the next quest in the series.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 59s 57c
