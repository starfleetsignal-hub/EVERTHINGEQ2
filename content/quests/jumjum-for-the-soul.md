## Steps

1. Enter [[The Tower of the Drafling]] at {{waypoint -489, 24, 50}} to acquire jumjum from a bixie. Only the [[A bixie overseer|bixie overseers]], [[A bixie retainer|retainers]], [[A bixie lieutenant|lieutenants]] or [[A stone hive nursemaid|nursemaids]] will provide an update.
   - Bixies in [[Greater Faydark]] will also give this update (as of August, 2011). These bixies are less than level 10 and are significantly easier to kill than the ones in the Tower of the Drafling.
1. Return to [[Nillipuss]].
1. Recover the *jumjum pouch* from [[Shakey]]. {{waypoint -318, -1, -273}}
1. Return to Nillipuss.
1. Find [[Rando Vineweaver]] in [[Brushfoot's General Store]] at {{waypoint -43, -10, -172}}. He will be lying dead on the ground with [[A lamia assassin]] standing over him.
   - **Important Note**: Don't kill the assassin just yet!
1. Return to Nillipuss.
1. Head back to where Rando was found and slay the lamia assassin. If the assassin was killed during the previous step, you must wait several minutes for it to respawn.
1. Return to Nillipuss.  Upon speaking with him he will attack you. Kill him to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 6g 5c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Cavalier's Bloodstained Pauldrons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Mentor's Dark Silk Arm Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Primalist's Ghostly Spaulders]] (Shamans)
  - [[Scholar's Spellwoven Epaulets]] (All Mages)
  - [[Thief's Blood-Dyed Chain Spaulders]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Vicar's Sanctified Plate Pauldrons]] (Clerics)
  - [[Wanderer's Rare Hide Shoulder Pads]] (Druids, Channelers)
