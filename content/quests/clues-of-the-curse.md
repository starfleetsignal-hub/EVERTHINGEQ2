## Steps

1. Speak to Draeth after he examines the bones. He will give you a potion recipie to complete
1. Kill 8 [[A forest avenger|forest avengers]] for their eyes
1. Obtain 6 inanimate bones in [[Howlers Cove]]
1. Examine one of the ingredients to create the potion
1. Find necrotic artifacts on the bottom of the ocean floor.  Possible locs are {{waypoint 4829, -1134, 323}} , {{waypoint 4758, -1123, 234}} , {{waypoint 4681, -1120, 282}} , {{waypoint 4462, -1116, 55}} , {{waypoint 422, -1112, -44}} , {{waypoint 4896, -1120, 281}}
1. Return to Draeth V'Zaar

- At least 1p 35g
