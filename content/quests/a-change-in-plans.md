Lavic has taken on a new contract, and is willing to hire some help to do the "easy part".

## Starting the Quest

- Speak to [[Lavic N'Gam]] in [[South Freeport]] {{waypoint -79, -25, 171}}.

## Steps

1. Acquire "the scholar's tool" by harvesting critter dens in [[The Commonlands]] until an update is received.  This requires a minimum trapping skill of at least 20.
1. Now collect the following items in any order:
   - pustules from [[A scourge rat]]; (around)  {{waypoint -10, -46, -161}}.
   - an insignia ring from [[A Dervish go-between]]; (runs by)  {{waypoint 1226, -25, 442}} and
   - a rusty sword from [[A fallen guardian]] (around)  {{waypoint 487, -48, 296}}.
1. Take these items to the [[Dervish Camps]] in the western Commonlands and place them in a barrel there {{waypoint 1344, -40, 256}}.
1. Return to Lavic N'Gam in South Freeport to finish the quest and open the next in the series, [[No More Witnesses]].
