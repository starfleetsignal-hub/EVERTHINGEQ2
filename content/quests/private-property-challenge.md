The sign near the entrance reads: "*Whosoever trespasses upon these premises will be dealt with swiftly and unmercifully. The rum is ours!*" It looks like the Brokenskulls have claimed this property as their own.

## Note

You do not have to kill the lvl 104 Brokenskull dockers (15 in total)  located around the zone in 5 groups of three, but it may make progress through the zone easier if they are eliminated.

## Steps

1. Kill [[Oofgoof Doof]] to receive [[North lift lever]] and [[South lift lever]] to gain access to upper floor.
1. Insert one of the levers into either of the lift switch mechanisms and operate to lower the lift.  ![Before and after inserting the lift lever](images/Private_Property_quest_-_Base_&_Lever.jpg)
   - Use the lift to gain access to the upper floor.
1. Kill the large rat [[Sir Wharfie]] to reveal the 8 stowaways hiding at the following locations:
   - {{waypoint -205, -49, 132}}
   - {{waypoint -193, -49, 157}}
   - {{waypoint -234, -49, 194}}
   - {{waypoint -259, -49, 187}}
   - {{waypoint -272, -49, 156}}
   - {{waypoint -287, -49, 110}}
   - {{waypoint -261, -49, 89}}
   - {{waypoint -229, -49, 88}}
1. After killing all 8 stowaway lushes, [[Zaxfalump]] spawns at {{waypoint -286, -49, 150}} and following a brief dialogue with his first mate Fwump, makes his way to position himself on a chest at {{waypoint -242, -49, 139}} and becomes aggro.
1. Kill him to incite his first mate [[Fwump]] to attack.
1. Killing Fwump completes the quest and 4 collectibles will appear at random locations within the zone.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 67g 40s 20000 status
- 10 x  [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
