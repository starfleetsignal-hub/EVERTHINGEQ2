1. Kill erudite workers within the [[Erudin Research Halls]]
   - Kill 5 [[A merciless overseer|Erudin overseers]]
   - Kill 5 [[A malicious technomagus|Erudin technomagi]]
   - Kill 5 [[A malevolent magitechnician|Erudin magitechnicians]]
1. Return to Rafiqa
