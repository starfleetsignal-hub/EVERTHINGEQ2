## Notes

- possible that you have the finish the quests from the other NPCs there as well to get this one
- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Collect arcane materials in the Aether Wroughtlands:
   - This quest will send you to collect 2 different things... one from each list below (at random)
     - *List 1*
       - 12 devourer mandibles {{waypoint -269.45, 99.74, 314.03}}
       - 12 Firespine gnawer embers {{waypoint 212.88, -35.98, -430.25}}
       - 12 emerald devourer mandibles {{waypoint -436, 103, 272}}
       - 12 Volatile Rock Turquise deposits {{waypoint -529, 54, -233}} (kill Volatile Rock Formations)
     - *List 2*
       - These are approximate areas not exact locations
       - 8 mercury coral {{waypoint -598.59, 126.04, 389.56}} or in the Water {{waypoint -626, 126, 398}}
       - 8 jadeglow pollen {{waypoint -592, 57, -384}}
       - 8 lavafern spores {{waypoint 389, -9, -296}} or {{waypoint 352, -14, -381}} or {{waypoint 394, -9, -304}}or {{waypoint 25, -39, -342}} or {{waypoint -123, -37, -404}} or {{waypoint -168, -31, -402}} or {{waypoint -84, -27, -529}}![Lavafern spores](images/Lavafern_spores.jpg)
       - *Note the spores are not the plants they are the sparkles*
1. Return to [[Minnahalle]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
- 5 [[Zimaran Ballad]]
