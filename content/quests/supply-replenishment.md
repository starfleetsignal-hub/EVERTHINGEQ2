## Steps

1. Go to [[The Pillars of Flame]] and kill:
   - 7 - 10 [[A searing scorpion|searing scorpions]], [[Sunstrider gazelle|sunstrider gazelles]], [[Sunfeather falcon|sunfeather falcons]] or [[Sunvenom cobra|sunvenom cobras]] (randomly assigned)
   - 5 - 7 [[A desert prophet|desert prophets]] located around [[Prophet's Peak]] {{waypoint 659, -87, -905}}
1. Speak to [[Fadi Miyaz]] to finish the quest and claim your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 23s 29c
- -500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- +500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
