## Steps

1. Slay several [[A Tidesylph ritualist|Tidesylph ritualists]] around BBM coast. There are two ways to get in the cave: jump down from a hole on the island{{waypoint 831.00, 23.00, -268.00}}, or from the underwater entrance{{waypoint 739.01, 12.49, -258.92}} (Shrillers and Foulers are placeholders).
1. Return to [[Griss Brumbaugh]]
1. [[Griss Brumbaugh]] sends you to [[Grimshaw Packwright]] in [[East Fort Irontoe]]

## Notes

Three [[A Tidesylph ritualist|Tidesylph ritualists]] spawn at a time on a platform above the water, but below the surface of the island. There's a hole that drops down to their location. They have about a 10 minute spawn timer.

Furthermore, if you drop down into the water there is an underwater passage leading to a larger chamber with four packs of ritualists mixed with foulers and pets. Underwater breathing is definitely recommended.
