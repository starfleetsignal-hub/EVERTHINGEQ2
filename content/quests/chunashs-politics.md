## Note

This is a GM event. [[Chunash]] is not an NPC and is not found in the Commonlands unless the event is active.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Chunash]] about the Bloodskull Priests.
1. Kill 20 Bloodskull Warriors in the Commonlands.
1. Speak to [[Chunash]] and inform him that you have killed 20 Bloodskull Warriors.
1. [[Chunash]] will inform you that you were followed and a group of orc berserkers will spawn and he will momentarily dissapear.
1. Kill the group of orc berserkers.
1. Speak to [[Chunash]] and receive your reward.
