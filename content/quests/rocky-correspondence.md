## Notes

- This quest is no longer heroic, but Kiw'the Il'zhe will still do tremendous damage at the beginning of the fight as he may cast [[Ice Comet (Spell)|Ice Comet]], which causes quite a bit of damage.

## Steps

1. Deliver a letter to [[Kwi'the Il'zhe]] in [[Maj'Dul]]. {{waypoint 122, 139, -26}}
   - To get to Maj'Dul, take the magic carpet from the docks. When you hail Kwi'the, he will attack. The quest will update after you kill him.
1. Return to [[El'Khazi, Purveyor of Fine Carpets]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 25g 15s 2c
