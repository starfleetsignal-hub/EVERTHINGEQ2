## Steps

1. Go to [[The Shadowed Grove]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]] and speak with *a cursed elf*. {{waypoint 564.89, -38.15, 402.65}}
1. Upon finishing his dialogue, he will turn into [[Taerenun]]. Kill him.
   - If more than one person in the group is working on this step, everyone must finish clicking through the dialogue before he dies, or they will not get the update. He has a 15 minute respawn.
1. Return to Juna.

## Rewards

Experience and AA Experience only
