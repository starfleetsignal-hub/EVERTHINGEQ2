Create 2 items from 6 of random list below:

- [[Broadcloth Cuffs (Level 30) (Crafted)|Tailored Broadcloth Cuffs]]
- [[Broadcloth Shawl (Level 30) (Crafted)|Tailored Broadcloth Shawl]]
- [[Etched Leather Bracers|Tailored Etched Leather Bracers]]
- [[Etched Leather Shoulder Pads|Tailored Etched Leather Shoulder Pads]]
- [[Broadcloth Mitts (Level 30) (attuneable)|Tailored Broadcloth Mitts]]
- [[Broadcloth Pantaloons (Crafted)|Tailored Broadcloth Pantaloons]]
- [[Etched Leather Gloves (Level 30) (Crafted)|Tailored Etched Leather Gloves]]
- [[Etched Leather Pants (Level 30) (Crafted)|Tailored Etched Leather Pants]]
- [[Broadcloth Robe|Tailored Broadcloth Robe]]
- [[Etched Leather Vest|Tailored Etched Leather Vest]]
- [[Etched Leather Bandolier (Level 30) (Crafted)|Etched Leather Bandolier]]
- [[Etched Leather Satchel (Level 30) (Crafted)|Etched Leather Satchel]]
- [[Etched Leather Sheath (Level 30) (Crafted)|Etched Leather Sheath]]
- [[Etched Leather Pouch (Level 30) (Crafted)|Etched Leather Pouch]]
- [[Pristine etched leather backpack|Etched Leather Backpack]]

- At Least 24s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
