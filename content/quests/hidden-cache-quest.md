## Notes

- *Timed quest: Shattered Seas solo daily, 6 minutes.*

## Steps

1. kill 6 [[A Mucktail treasurekeeper]] before they reach the cache
   1. the mobs pop in the water close by and are non attackable at start and walk slowly
   1. to attack them you need to click on them several times till there health falls below 50%
   1. once the health is below 50% they are attackable and level 101 ^^ heroic

## Rewards

- At least 1p 50g 5800 status
- 3 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- achievement [[Keeper of the Cache]]
