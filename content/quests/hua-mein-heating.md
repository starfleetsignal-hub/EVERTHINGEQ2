## Description

The Hua Mein Craftsman has requested assistance in gathering firewood for the Hua Mein stores.

## Steps

1. I have been asked to harvest some firewood to replenish the supplies of the Hua Mein village.
   - Harvest 25 [[Sumac lumber]]. (NOTE:  Having the lumber in your inventory when picking up the quest DOES NOT update the quest. Neither do the guild harvesters, buying lumber from broker or taking it from your bank).
1. Return to [[Xi Lan]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,000 faction with **Hua Mein**
- +2,500 faction with **Hua Mein Craftsmen**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Hua Mein Daily Supply Tasks
