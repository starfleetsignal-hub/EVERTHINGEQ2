## Steps

1. /claim the [[Mysterious Egg]].
1. Examine egg.
1. Find a Pet Merchant in any pet shop.
1. Purchase the [[Egg Warmer]] for 1g
1. Place in house
1. Right click on the [[Egg Warmer]], choose **place egg**
1. When the egg is ready, collect the bird by right-clicking on the [[Egg Warmer]]
   - The egg may take up to 24 real-world hours to hatch, but should hatch after about 15 minutes. (Zoning out of your house and back in usually hatches the egg)

## Rewards

- [[Aviak Hatchling]]
