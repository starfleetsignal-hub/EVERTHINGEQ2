## Notes

![best route to do it quick](images/EQ2PoPTome.png)

- Initial timer 15 minutes. .
- Suggested order for cataloging (if picked up in [[Aetherscar]]). If you picked up the quest elsewhere, start with the area nearest you and loop through the list.
  - catalogue slugs just north of [[Aetherscar]]
  - [[Auroric Tide]] on the west side of map, 3 updates
  - [[The Stratafold]]
  - [[Amphitheater of Song]]
  - [[Zivier's Reach]]
  - [[Esoterra Gardens]]
  - [[Manacross Island]]
  - [[Drukyna Marsh]]
  - [[Pakiat Bluffs]]
  - [[Kandra Uplands]]
  - [[Myhst River]]
  - [[Metetherial Plains]]
  - Back to [[Aetherscar]] (or wherever you started).
- There is a 5-minute extension for the final step. The final creature is vaguely described, but will be a regular named found in the area highlighted in blue on your map. Catalogue the creature, then kill it.
  - Note: Kill changes location depending on pickup location.

<br>

## Steps

1. 11 or 12 minutes to catalogue about 20 ish creatures in 12 different sections of the map.
1. 5 minute extension on success to go find, catalogue and kill a construct:
   - construct of air {{waypoint 523, 65, 284}}
   - construct of earth {{waypoint 785, 65, 968}}
   - construct of fire {{waypoint 676, -13, -13}}
   - construct of water {{waypoint -785, 11, 311}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 68g 39s
- 5 encrusted planar key
