## Steps

1. Slay the following creatures, found on the north side of the [[Orc Highway]] (at the top of the ramp from the beach) {{waypoint -837.72, -126.37, -322.96}}:
   - 6 [[Dune scarab|dune scarabs]]
   - 6 [[Dune spider|dune spiders]]
1. Return to [[Numair]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Shimmering Gem of Prowess]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Trinket of the Desert Sword]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Trinket of the Dunestalker]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Trinket of the Sandmage]] (All Mages)
  - [[Trinket of the Sun Priest]] (All Priests)
