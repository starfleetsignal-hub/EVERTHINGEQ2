## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 40+

## Steps

1. Gather 3 glowing stacks of books from the [[Concordium Tower]]. You will find them by zoning through the blue portal. {{waypoint 705, -20, 128}}
1. Take the red portal and speak to [[Librarian Galdin]]. {{waypoint 705, 48, 90}}
1. Zone into [[The Concordium Vault]]. {{waypoint 718, 48, 102}}
1. Locate a clue by searching the shelves, then defeat the 3 [[A tomecreeper|tomecreepers]] that will attack you. {{waypoint -1, 0, 2}}
1. Return to [[Field Marshal Vishra]].

## Rewards

- At least 26g 71s 68c (at level 92)
