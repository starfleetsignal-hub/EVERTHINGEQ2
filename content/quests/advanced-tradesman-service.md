## Notes

The title of this quest will vary based on **your** crafting profession.  However, the steps are the same for all classes.

## Required

- L40+ Tradeskill Profession

## Starting the Quest

Quest starters are Trades Coordinators:

- Gorowyn  - [[Grandmaster Jayak Lat'wasr]] in [[Gorowyn]], [[The Wards]]
- Freeport - [[Grandmaster Kawayzer]] in [[West Freeport]]
- Kelethin - [[Grandmaster Feldmanus]] in [[Tunare's Pages]] in the area with the broker
- Qeynos   - [[Grandmaster Kimbialis]] at the [[The Ironforge Exchange]] in [[North Qeynos]]
- Neriak   - [[Grandmaster Zyrythius]] in the [[Dockside Markets]]
- New Halas - [[Grandmaster Cheri Glodsdottr]] in the [[Ravens' Roost]] crafting area

## Steps

1. Craft 3 sets of 4 (12 total) level 40 handcrafted items. (Your quest journal will say which items to craft).
1. Find [[Keevan Fastmarch]], Resource Surveyor, in [[The Feerrott]], near the Teleport Spires, in the Hidden Camp at {{waypoint -125, 14, 179}}.
1. Find [[Fiderik Nalot]], Resource Surveyor, in [[Everfrost]], near the pioneer camp due West of the zone-in dock at the little island where the revive tent is {{waypoint 218, -19, 52}}.
1. Find [[Thullekla Dy'xin]], Resource Surveyor, in [[Lavastorm]], checking mineral samples on the beach {{waypoint -222, -129, 505}}.
1. Return to the Grandmaster for your reward.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill Experience
- One advanced book level 40-49 for your tradeskill class or [[Provisioner Mastery volume 3]] for Provisioners.
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +750 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +750 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +750 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +750 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +750 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +750 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
