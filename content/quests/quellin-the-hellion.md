## Steps

1. Speak to [[Master Ruhe]] at the [[Silent Fist Dojo]] in [[Elddar Grove]], [[Qeynos Province District]] to receive a blessing of Quellious. {{waypoint 756, -20, -396}}
1. Return to [[Graystone Yard: Herald of Rage|Graystone Yard]] to destroy the Sullonite Artifact. {{waypoint 920, -154, 287}}
   - You will have to click the Sullonite Artifact 6 times to destroy it.
     - Each time you click it, [[Marweth Wrathborn]] will attack.  [[Marweth Wrathborn|Marweth]] uses only one attack, the massively damaging Circular Rage. The only defense is to joust back out of range.
     - Each time [[Marweth Wrathborn|Marweth]] is reduced to no health, she will become dazed, but quickly begin regenerating.  You'll be able to get one click in on the Sullonite Artifact in between battles with [[Marweth Wrathborn|Marweth]].
1. Speak again with [[Marweth Wrathborn]].
   - [[Marweth Wrathborn|Marweth]] remains unrepentant, forcing you to banish her from Qeynos.
1. Return to [[Gavin Seabreach]].

## Rewards

- [[Ring of Barbarian Tendencies]]
- At least 25g 70s 61c (at level 92)
