## Notes

1. This is one of the daily (18 hour timer) repeatable missions offered after finishing [[Rage of Cthurath Crafting Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Gather and harvest:
   - 30x [[Perse Perch]]
   - 18x [[Detritius Root|Detritus Root]]
   - 12x [[Nightscar Prowler Meat]]
1. Craft 6x [[Abyssal Weavenet|Abyssal Weavenets]]
   - Requires 30x [[Formless Filament]]

## Rewards

At least 11p 16g 55s 16c

- [[Unknown Goodies (Tradeskill Daily Reward Version)|Unknown Goodies]] crate opening into a random item from list of possibilities
- [[Token of the Formless Crafter]] - currency
- [[Status Coin (75,000)]]
