## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key.
   - Kill 15 [[A cursed Elddar remnant|cursed Elddar remnants]] around {{waypoint 95.49, 125.02, -653.16}} in [[Takish Badlands]] in the [[Ruins of Takish'Hiz]].
1. I need to find the Hypolydia Music Box key elsewhere.
1. Go to [[Sandstone Delta]]
1. Find the Hypolydia Music Box key clue from [[A grisly fathomlurker|grisly fathomlurkers]] around {{waypoint -418.42, -9.65, 240.66}} or {{waypoint -474.36, -24.14, -615.12}} in [[Sandstone Delta]] at [[Sea of Sand (Sandstone Delta)|Sea of Sand]].
1. Examine the clue to receive the quest [[The Hypolydia Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the **Hypolydia Music Box** in [[Takish Badlands]].
