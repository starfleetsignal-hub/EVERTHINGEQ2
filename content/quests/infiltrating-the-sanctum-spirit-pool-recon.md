## Steps

1. Scout the [[Pool of Spirits]] {{waypoint 121, 45, 142}}, a room on the lower level of the Sanctum.
1. Return to [[Thorn]].
1. Eliminate 4 [[A Scaleborn benedictor|Scaleborn benedictors]] and destroy 4 prayer relics around the [[Fount of Awakening]]. {{waypoint -99, 86, 178}}
   - The prayer relics are dragon idols on the corners of the fount. To get to them, climb the fount using one of the climbing walls (there is one on each side of the fountain) and click them when close. They have a 5 minute respawn timer.
1. Kill 8 [[A summoned intellect|summoned intellects]] and [[Zorv the Soulsnarer]] or his [[A Scaleborn soulsnarer|placeholder]] in the Pool of Spirits {{waypoint 141.2, 44.3, 145.3}}.
   - There will not be enough summoned intellects in the room, so you will have to wait for respawns.
1. Return to Thorn for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 13g 59s 50c
- Your choice of one of the following:
  - [[Bangflashers]]
  - [[Smoke Vials of Escape]]
  - [[Webby Cannister]]
