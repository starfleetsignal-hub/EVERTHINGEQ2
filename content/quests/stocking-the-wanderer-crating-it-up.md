## Notes

TThis is a faction quest, it's repeatable up to 3 times and available **only when you have between 20,000 and 50,000 [[Port Woe]] faction** and have started [[Captain Raffik]]'s quest [[Stealthy Supplies: The Crafty Wanderer]]. If your faction with Port Woe is already at 50,000 you cannot pick it up nor turn it in anymore. You can go buy Vital Supplies from [[Kassa]] to progress main crafting questline.

Materials needed:

- 15 x [[Larch Wood]]
- 10 x [[Ilmenite Ore]]
- 25 x [[Zimaran Sandpaper]]

## Steps

1. I need to collect supply crates from around [[Ismara]]'s hut - collect 5 crates around {{waypoint 86, 5, 308}}, they respawn fast.
1. Craft 5 x [[Replacement Crates]] - recipe was auto-scribed earlier.
1. Speak with [[Barsten]] at {{waypoint -76, 6, 362}}

## Rewards

- +2,000 faction with **Port Woe**
- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
