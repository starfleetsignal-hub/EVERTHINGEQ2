## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable.
- Easy way to get to Pillars of Flame: go to Sinking Sands, from docks take carpet ride to Camp D'Sas or Undercity Arena, zone to Pillars is near there.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Pillars of Flame]]<gallery>

File:Rock_at_Camp_K'taakh.png|rock at Camp K'taakh
File:Rock_from_The_Giant's_Field.png|rock from The Giant's Field
File:Rock_around_the_Great_Bonsai.png|rock at the Great Bonsai
</gallery>

1. Collect 3 samples of rock from Camp K'taakh - go to {{waypoint 576, -109, -495}} and mine dark black rocks around orc camp.
1. Collect 3 samples of rock from The Giant's Field - go to {{waypoint 1344, -109, -377}} and mine sparkling rocks among giants.
1. Collect 3 samples of rock near the Great Bonsai - go to {{waypoint 176, -189, -1642}} and mine sparkling gray rocks around edges of the island
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]

## Rewards:

- Unlock the [[Days of Summer 2025: Week 2]]
