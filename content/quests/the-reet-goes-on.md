## Prerequisites

- You must have at least -6,900 faction with Synod Reet.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Sebilis]] and speak with [[A Sathirian traitor]] by the main entrance, at {{waypoint -581, -104, 950}}. She will teleport you to the [[Slave Quarters]].
1. Gather the 'stolen cache' to the north of where you appear (5-minute respawn). {{waypoint -236.27, -25.75, 440.05}}
1. While in the Slave Quarters, use the [[Transit Key]] given to you to return to Foreman Guppka to teleport out of the dungeon back to [[The Crypt Grounds]].
1. Speak with [[Foreman Guppka]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- +1,000 faction with **Synod Reet**
