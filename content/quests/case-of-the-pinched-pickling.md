## Steps

1. Follow the trail of  small green glowing droplets to {{waypoint 208, 4, 486}}
1. Gather stolen brine jar - click small  beige vase with red lid next to hut right there.  ![](images/Case_of_the_Pinched_Pickling_quest_-_Brine_Jar.jpg)
1. Gather more stolen brine jars - find 5 more same looking jars/vases among kappas, close to their huts, hidden in tall grasses
   - possible locations include {{waypoint 256.49, 4.47, 616.57}} {{waypoint 247.45, 4.76, 625.83}} {{waypoint 247.75, 4.83, 637.71}} {{waypoint 244, 5, 646}} {{waypoint 243.97, 5.10, 653.01}} {{waypoint 201, 5, 671}}
   - {{waypoint 131, 5, 606}} {{waypoint 131, 5, 623}}, also more on West side of swamp around {{waypoint 461, 5, 719}}, {{waypoint 506, 5, 723}} {{waypoint 520, 5, 676}}
1. Return to [[Tristyl Ashwood]]
