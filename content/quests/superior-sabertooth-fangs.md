1. Obtain 5 fangs from [[A sabretooth scuffler|sabretooth scufflers]] southwest of [[Riliss (POI)|Riliss]] in the [[Rilissian Lowlands]]. {{waypoint -182.05, -73.35, 175.53}}
1. Return to [[Merchant Paxi]].

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Band of Untold Wealth]]
  - [[Crest of Untold Wealth]]
  - [[Emblem of Untold Wealth]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Loop of Untold Wealth]] (All Scouts)
- +5000 faction with **Riliss**
