## Steps

1. In the [[Hall of Rites]] there is a small dragon head statue at the base of a column inside the pit {{waypoint 9, 54, -17}}. Right click and choose the 'Grab the trial stone from the mouth.'
   - You may evac and return to Thorn immediately, or complete the ring event you just triggered: first wave of 8 65^^^, second wave of 4 66^^^, third wave of 2 66^^^, then 67^^^ [[Slasher of Souls]]. Barriers will prevent you from exiting the pit via the stairs until the named is dead.
   - The statue has a respawn time of 25 minutes.
1. Speak to Thorn.
1. Kill the following:
   - 10 [[A Scaleborn banisher|Scaleborn banisher]]s, usually found in [[Hall of Rites]], [[Banisher Preparatory]] {{waypoint 99, 38, -150}}
   - 4 [[A Scaleborn harbinger|Scaleborn harbinger]]s, usually found in [[Exarch's Seclusion]], [[Banisher Preparatory]]
   - [[A Scaleborn theurgist]] found in the Banisher Preparatory at {{waypoint 177, 65, -38}}. [[Orate Kruunz]] is his placeholder and they have ~15 minute respawn time.
1. Kill 4 Scaleborn banishers in the [[Reservoir of the Wraith]] at {{waypoint 72, 39, -158}}. Banishers in other locations will not update the quest.
1. Climb the fount in [[Reservoir of the Wraith]] and click the skull on the northeast corner {{waypoint 78, 45, -163}}. Speak to [[The Severed Shade]].
1. Kill the following:
   - 6 [[A recovering soulclencher|recovering soulclencher]]s found in the [[Room of Recovery]] {{waypoint -137, 42, -177}}
   - 10 [[A Scaleborn acolyte|Scaleborn acolyte]]s found in [[Soulrend (POI)|Soulrend]] {{waypoint -66, 36, -163}}
   - [[A Scaleborn soulrender]] in Soulrend. [[Soulrender Xirloz]] is his placeholder and they have ~15 minute respawn time.
1. Speak to The Severed Shade.
1. Head back towards Soulrend and [[Soulclencher Volz]] will trigger in hallway {{waypoint 2, 37, -158}}. Kill him for update.
1. Go to the ancient draconic forge {{waypoint -116, 65, -34}}, right-click it, and select "Break Open Metal Case" to complete the quest.
