## Notes

When you come back to Vadreem with grotto glops, you will see that Derlak arrived in the camp. Derlak's first quest [[Sacrilege Remediation]] can be done in the same area where part 2 of this quest.

## Steps

1. Vadreem requires more components to dissolve the magical wards:
   - Get 4 corrosive sludge by hunting grotto glops - in [[Yrgoth Grotto]] around {{waypoint -455, 14, -311}}![a grotto glop](images/A_grotto_glop.png)
   - Gather 6 cilia from brain coral pods - at the water edge around {{waypoint -550, 14, -79}} and  {{waypoint -534, 22, 362}}
1. Return to Vadreem at [[Nightscar Steppes]] at {{waypoint -273, 25, -22}}.
   - you receive [[Dissolving Glop]]
1. Remove the wards and kill
   - Remove 6 "Legion of the Oblivion ward" with the [[Dissolving Glop]] {{waypoint -411, 57, -317}}![The Legion of the Oblivion ward](images/The_Legion_of_the_Oblivion_ward.png)
   - Cull 8 patrolling shadowfangs
1. return to [[Vadreem (The Unknown)|Vadreem]] at {{waypoint -272, 25, -22}} in [[The Unknown]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- One of the following:
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]]
  - [[Industrial Blade of Parables]]
  - [[Star Blood Great Blade of Parables]]
