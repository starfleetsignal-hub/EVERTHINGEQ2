## Description

"I discovered an unearthed Tart Faydark Truffle! This is definitely something Thekela Gillywump would be interested in." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Thekela Gillywump]] {{waypoint -160.06, 7.15, -116.65}}

## Rewards

- At least 2s 57c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

I need to bring this truffle to Thekela.

- Thekela Gillywump: "Hello again, [Player Character]!"
- Player Character: "I found this. I thought you might be interested."
- Thekela Gillywump: "A Tart Faydark Truffle! Amazing! Where did you find it?"
- Player Character: "I found it in the Treant Grove."
- Thekela Gillywump: "Oh my, this Faydark Truffle is rotten! It is befouled and corrupted. No doubt, those meddling orcs are to blame. They will be the death of this forest, mark my words."
- Player Character: "It's rotten? I'm sorry to hear that."
- Thekela Gillywump: "Well, I'm honored that you thought of me. It's a shame this Truffle isn't edible."
- Player Character: "It is a shame."

I have brought the truffle to Thekela.

I have given Thekela the Tart Faydark Truffle. Unfortunately, the truffle was inedible.
