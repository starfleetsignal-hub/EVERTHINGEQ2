## Steps

Speak to [[Limora Roamhill (NPC)|Limora Roamhill]]

1. Locate 8 Flame Tuatara Eggs (these are small red eggs at the bases of trees nearby). Some locations include: {{waypoint 88, -42, 273}}, {{waypoint 119, -45, 273}}, {{waypoint 112, -46, 227}}, {{waypoint 73, -44, 202}}, {{waypoint 24, -37, 223}}, {{waypoint -43, -37, 258}}, {{waypoint 102, -48, 0.79}}, and {{waypoint 131, -43, 30}}.
1. Return to [[Limora Roamhill (NPC)|Limora Roamhill]].
1. Catch 6 Flame Tuatara hatchlings. (These are tiny, quick critters that look like a mini version of the critter by Limora, also by the trees where you found eggs).
