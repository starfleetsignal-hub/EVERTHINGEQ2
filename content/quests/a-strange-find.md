While inside Sablevein Tear I found a plant that seemed out of place. I should show the plant to Verex to see what he makes of it.

## Steps

1. Bring the plant to [[Verex N'Za]] inside [[Hate's Envy]] {{waypoint -421, -63, 260}}
1. Verex tells you to show it to [[Sselnyl Do'Zyth]]
1. Hail Mayor [[Sselnyl Do'Zyth]], for your rewards

## Rewards

- At least 65c
- One of the following (varies by class):
  - [[Woolen Footwraps]]
  - [[Wax Hardened Boots]]
  - [[Anodized Ore Sleeves]]
  - [[Hammered Link Sleeves]]
  - [[Ironweave Shoulderguards]] (Dirge)
