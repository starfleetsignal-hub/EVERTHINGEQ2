## Steps

1. Obtain the following reagents:
   - 8 *augury horns* from [[An ashfire drake|ashfire drakes]]. {{waypoint -1859, 208, 999}}
   - 3 *samples of pyroclastic pollen* from magma blooms.
     - Magma blooms look like [[Flame flower|flame flowers]] and grow along the edge of the lava pits in [[Dracur's Breath]]. {{waypoint -1304, 211, 293}} or {{waypoint -1370, 207, 300}}
   - 2 *slabs of cooled komatite* from [[A magmatic destroyer|magmatic destroyers]].
     - Magmatic destroyers appear when you walk around the edge of one of the many lava pits strewn about the [[Skyfire Mountains]]. See the [[A magmatic destroyer|monster page]] for a list of spawn points.
1. Return to Shaman Medicus.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 5g
