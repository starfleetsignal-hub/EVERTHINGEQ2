## Description

I've been asked by Bergna Veliumsmiter to enter the Hold of Rime: The Ascent and test the strength of Rime forces.

## Steps

- Defeat 3 [[Vigilant shardhammer sentinels]]
- Defeat 10 [[Lurking sleetsavers]], [[Fanatical shardhammers]], [[Vigilant nova-vicars]] or [[Scholarly flurrybursts]]
- Defeat 3 [[Stoic patrollers]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 80g
- [[Primal Velium Shard]] x1
- +350 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
