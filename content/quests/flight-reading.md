## Preq's

1. MUST be of Freeblood Race
1. Must have Marketplace  'Cloud of Bats' ability
1. Must be at least Level 86

## Steps

1. Enter Tserrina's Frozen Library, a special instance accessed by clicking on the mirror to Tower of Frozen Shadow {{waypoint -1118, -526, -1722}} in the Great Divide.
   - *Note: There are aggressive level 93 books in this instance, but they do not see through invis*
1. Find Tserrina's leathery tome, which was found on the 2nd floor at {{waypoint 25, 70, -115}}
1. Return to Drammind to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- **[[Drammind's Bat Whistle]]**

Now that you have your bat whistle, go ahead and summon your swarm! You may do the side-quests in any order, but only one may done done every 18 hours, like the gryphon mount quests.

## Mount Side Quests

Hail the colony and select one of the quests.

1. "We must add to the colony" selects **For the Colony** - Find 6 lost bats in the Great Divide. These are clickable objects in the ROI regions on the map and can be very spread out. As well as being well spread out, they can be some distance outside the Region Of Interest (ROI) indicated on the map {{waypoint -520.17, -356.17, 510.82}}, {{waypoint -494.95, -360.39, 580.64}}, {{waypoint -197.40, -305.18, 564.45}}
1. "I will gather food for you" selects **Food For Flight** - Kill 8 millipedes on the Blisterrock Ridge.
1. "You look restless" selects **Calming the Colony** - Search for 8 bloodroots in the Great Divide. These are reported to be between the Crystalline Crevasses and the Ry'Gorr Ruins. (roots are usually by rocks)
1. "Let's see if any of you want to leave" selects **Cave for the Colony** - Enter the Tizmak Caves {{waypoint -278.54, -336.13, -74.55}} . Once you do this, the quest should auto-complete.
1. "Let me show you my strength" selects **Strength of the Colony** - Kill 8 Ry'Gorr orcs in the Great Divide. They are found around the Ry'Gorr Ruins and Scar Bridge.
1. "Soon we will be done" declines a quest at this time.

When done with all 5 quests, Hail your bat swarm and you will receive another "Quest Complete!" box. Your marketplace bat mount will now be flight accessible!

Each side-quest will yield At least 29g 85s 79c
