## Steps

1. Scribe the [[Silken Padding Recipe]] that [[Capru]] gave you, collect your ingredients, and craft five [[Silken Padding|Silken Paddings]] at a Sewing Loom. There is one in Thurgadin Harbor. {{waypoint 1256, -540, 434}}
   - Shopping List:
     - 5 [[Crystalline Spiderling Silk Threads]] (given to you by [[Capru]])
       - **Warning:** if you have the **harvested** mat [[Crystalline Spiderling Silk]] in your inventory. The quest will use yours first, before it uses the quest-provided mat. Denmum as per forums: "*Recipes are set to take the smaller pile of ingredients first if there is more than one pile to choose from.*" Therefore, it is advisable to put the harvest silk in your bank or broker box before you begin crafting, or **you will lose your harvested silk mats**!
     - 5 [[Bamboo shoot]]
     - 5 [[Mottled pelt]]
     - 5 [[Ethereal filament]]
   - If you lost the [[Silken Padding Recipe|recipe]] or [[Crystalline Spiderling Silk Threads|Silk Threads]], talk to [[Capru]] again
1. Return to [[Capru]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- The recipe stays in your book so that it can be used as a house item pillow, provided you have the ingredients.
