## Steps

1. Collect three souvenirs:
   1. ![Falinpol souvenir](images/Vesspyr_souvenir.png)Travel to the [[Vesspyr Isles]] and find a Scroll found in the Falinpol area (where the mission quests are) {{waypoint 85, 198, 9}}, {{waypoint 47.93, 194.01, 2.21}}, {{waypoint 46, 193, 23}}, {{waypoint 80, 195, -20}}, {{waypoint 76.03, 184.03, -125.28}}
   1. ![Droumlunds souvenir](images/Droumlunds_souvenir.png)Travel to the the [[Droumlunds]] island (north island on west side of map) and find a Golden plant near Lord Vyemm {{waypoint 278, 316, -866}}, {{waypoint 217.91, 313, -850.69}}, {{waypoint 302.97, 315.58, -911}}
   1. ![Veeshenna Tol souvenir](images/Veeshenna_Tol_souvenir.png)Travel to [[Veeshenna Tol]] main island (southernmost island on east side of the map) and find a statue near the Fractured Hive entrance {{waypoint -399, -6, 627}}, {{waypoint -389, -17, 580}}, {{waypoint -366.42, -15.91, 608.01}}
1. Return to [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]].
