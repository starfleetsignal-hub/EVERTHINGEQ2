## Steps

1. Speak to Osh {{waypoint 4280, -1076, 1454}}
1. Kill 3 [[An ulthork gigas|ulthork gigas]]es southwest of Merrik {{waypoint 4478, -1090, 1687}}
   - Use the pet window (default Alt+P) to control the hunters Merrik sent with you
     - Note - If you are a Beastlord, you will need to release your warder to have control over the hunters.
     - Once you have sent the hunters in to attack, you can call your warder back to join the fight.
1. Speak to Merrik
