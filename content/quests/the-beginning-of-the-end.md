## Steps

1. Search a library for [[A charred book]], either in Qeynos or Freeport for the meaning of the Sigil.
   - In South Qeynos, enter the [[Concordium Tower]] and take the red teleporter and head down the stairs. The book is open on the ground at {{waypoint 706, 41, 111}} near the corner of the room.
   - In North Freeport, head into the [[Academy of Arcane Science]] and find the open book on a table at {{waypoint 24, -7, -127}}
1. Return to [[The Duality]].
1. Investigate two more sigils. Find a secret entrance to [[Everling's Lost Chamber]] {{waypoint -1382, 122, -1874}} near Nektropos Castle to learn more about them
   - The first rune is found by clicking on a bookcase at {{waypoint 8, -16, 61}}
   - The second rune is found by clicking on a stack of books on the ground next to the bookcase at {{waypoint 41, -10, 103}}
1. Return to The Duality.
