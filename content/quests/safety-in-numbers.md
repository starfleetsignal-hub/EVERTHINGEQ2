## Steps

1. Kill 8 holgresh attackers that spawn along the way.
1. Use the signal at the indicated location {{waypoint -486, -674, -416}}.
   - Move immediately after using the signal flare or you can get stuck when the wagon appears.
1. Speak to Scout Master Kilkran.
1. Speak to Captain Haliran.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- One of the Following:
  - [[Satyr Hide Belt]] (Fighter)
  - [[Mist Panther Skin Belt]] (Scout)
  - [[Withertalon Raptor Hide Belt]] (All Priests)
  - [[Twisted Satyr Hair Belt]] (Mage)

- Adds a horse in the area for easy return.
