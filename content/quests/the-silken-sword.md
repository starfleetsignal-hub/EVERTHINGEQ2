## Prerequisites

- Be a Level 50+ Weaponsmith

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Ping Ventur]] at {{waypoint 36, 15, -81}}.
1. Speak with [[Gai Ge Venur]] in the courtyard near {{waypoint 23, 15, -77}}.  He paths around somewhat, but remains in that area.
1. Speak with [[Kim Nurwin]] at {{waypoint -175, 7, -177}}.
1. You must then collect the components needed to craft the Silken Sword:
   - The turtle shell is in a bucket at {{waypoint -10, -1, 15}}.
   - The silk for the grip can be found in a box at {{waypoint -68, -2, 120}}, near the Importer/Exporter.  The quest requires two silk, but both can be obtained from the same box.
   - The scabbard is in an armoire at {{waypoint -22, 12, -77}}.
   - Iron ore is a harvestable node found at {{waypoint -160, 1, -6}} (note: the node stops being clickable after the 5th ore. if you delete/loose an ore, the quest is unable to be uncompleted, and will need deleted and restarted)
1. Return to [[Mei-mei Ventur]] for your rewards.
