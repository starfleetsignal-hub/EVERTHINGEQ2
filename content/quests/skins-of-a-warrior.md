## Steps

1. Obtain 10 [[Low-quality drolvarg skin|low-quality drolvarg skins]] from drolvargs around {{waypoint 458, 17, -70}}.
1. Return to [[Trader Walworth]].

## Rewards

- At least 14g 81s 38c
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Crystal Wand of Energy Channeling]] (All Mages)
  - [[Curved Blade of Havoc]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Dagger of Ill Omens]] (All Mages, Scouts, Shamans, Warriors, Crusaders)
  - [[Dagger of the Grasp]] (All Mages)
  - [[Flail of Karnor's Castle]] (All Priests)
  - [[Ulak of Perfect Strikes]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Winterbearer]] (Druids)
