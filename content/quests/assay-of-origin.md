## Steps

![elemental remains](images/Elemental_remains.png)

1. Speak to [[Trader Cylianna]] outside the Far Seas bank {{waypoint -37.44, -2.70, 142.04}}.
1. Click the "elemental remains" by Cylianna to find a [[Metal mechanism]] within the elemental.
   - *Note: Harvest **6** of them ! You will need 5 metal mechanisms for the next step, you'll need another one later.*
1. Speak to Cylianna again and receive [[Ore Assay Recipes]], which auto-scribe and require a forge. In any order:
   1. Perform the adamantine ore assay.
   1. Perform the ferrite ore assay.
   1. Perform the titanium ore assay.
   1. Perform the rhenium ore assay.
   1. Perform the cadmium ore assay.
      - *Note: The recipes are searchable under "negative result" or "assay".*
1. Speak to Cylianna.
1. Return to [[Ethan Dariani]].

## Needed Materials

- [[Metal mechanism]] (5) *plus another one for later*
- [[Adamantine cluster]] (1) -- *[[Ore Nodes|T7 ore nodes]]*
- [[Cadmium ore]] (1) -- *[[Ore Nodes|T11 ore nodes]]*
- [[Ferrite cluster]] (1) -- *[[Ore Nodes|T8 ore nodes]]*
- [[Rhenium ore]] (1) -- *[[Ore Nodes|T10 ore nodes]]*
- [[Titanium ore]] (1) -- *[[Ore Nodes|T9 ore nodes]]*
- [[Effulgent coal]] (5)
