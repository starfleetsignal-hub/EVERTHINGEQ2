## Steps

1. Go to [[Antonica]] and speak with [[LuAnne Greyton]] in [[Windstalker Village]]. {{waypoint -1841, -38, -506}}
1. Return to [[Gardy Ex-Giftgiver]] and inform him of the attack on the snowglobe maker's shop.
1. Go to [[Steamfont Mountains]] and speak with [[Gnip Gnopp]] in [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]]. {{waypoint -552, 173, 1059}}
   - There is a portal on the other side of the tree from Gardy, next to [[Queen Bunny]], that will take you to Gnomeland Security Headquarters. {{waypoint 202, -28, -1368}}
1. Complete the subquest [[Gnopp's One Stop Shop]] for Gnip Gnopp so he can start cranking out the toys for Gardy.
1. Return to Gardy and give him [[Gnip Gnopp's delivery guarantee]].
   - There is a portal back to [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]] at {{waypoint -618, 176, 931}}.
1. Speak with [[Tinsel]] to get a new cap for Gardy. {{waypoint 116, -18, -1524}}
1. Return to Gardy and give him his new elf cap.
1. Speak with Tinsel again to get a new cloak for Gardy.
1. Return to Gardy and give him his new cloak.
1. Oh no! Goblins have broken into the crate! Use the [[Gizmonic zapper]] to shock 8 clockwork toys found throughout Frostfell Wonderland Village.
1. Return to Gardy.
1. Speak with one of [[Santa Glug]]'s helpers, [[Glitzen]] or [[Gasher]]. {{waypoint 287, -34, -1345}}
1. Speak with Santa Glug.
1. Tell Gardy the good news!
