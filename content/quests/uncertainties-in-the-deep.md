## Steps

1. Search the ocean floor for the ring, a shiny found at {{waypoint 125, -48, -20}}.
1. When you examine the shiny the quest updates and you are immediately attacked by [[Ebon Tooth]]. Kill it to advance the quest.
1. After Ebon Tooth is dead, re-harvest the shiny to retrieve [[A silver wedding ring]].
   - If for some reason you delete the quest between harvesting the ring and turning it in, you'll have to destroy the ring from your inventory before being able to harvest it again.
1. Return to Li Ning Ventur.
