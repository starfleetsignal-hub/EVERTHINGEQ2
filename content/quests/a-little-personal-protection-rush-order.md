## Steps

Create 2 each of any 6 items from the random list below:

- [[Fulginate Brigandine Gloves|Forged Fulginate Brigandine Gloves]]
- [[Fulginate Plate Gauntlets|Forged Fulginate Plate Gauntlets]]
- [[Fulginate Vanguard Gauntlets|Forged Fulginate Vanguard Gauntlets]]
- [[Fulginate Brigandine Coat|Forged Fulginate Brigandine Coat]]
- [[Fulginate Plate Cuirass|Forged Fulginate Plate Cuirass]]
- [[Fulginate Vanguard Cuirass|Forged Fulginate Vanguard Cuirass]]
- [[Fulginate Brigandine Leggings|Forged Fulginate Brigandine Leggings]]
- [[Fulginate Plate Greaves|Forged Fulginate Plate Greaves]]
- [[Fulginate Vanguard Greaves|Forged Fulginate Vanguard Greaves]]
- [[Fulginate Reverent Coif|Forged Fulginate Reverent Coif]]
- [[Fulginate Reverent Leggings|Forged Fulginate Reverent Leggings]]
- [[Fulginate Devout Barbute|Forged Fulginate Devout Barbute]]
- [[Fulginate Devout Cuirass|Forged Fulginate Devout Cuirass]]
- [[Fulginate Devout Greaves|Forged Fulginate Devout Greaves]]
- [[Fulginate Devout Sabatons|Forged Fulginate Devout Sabatons]]
- [[Fulginate Reverent Boots|Forged Fulginate Reverent Boots]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 1g 8s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
