## Steps

1. Go to [[Blackburrow]] {{waypoint -1588, -9, 792}}
1. Kill ten [[A Sabertooth miner|Sabertooth miner]]s (first cave entrance to your left) {{waypoint 32, 2, -28}}
1. Return to Squire Kivan

## Rewards

- At least 2s 10c
- One of the following, based on Class:
- [[Divine Theist's Wristguard]] (Clerics)
- [[Enchanted Adept's Cuffs]] (All Mages)
- [[Focused Initiate's Wrist Wraps]] (Brawlers)
- [[Mighty Footman's Bracers]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
- [[Radiant Seer's Chain Cuffs]] (Shamans)
- [[Untainted Naturalist's Bands]] (Druids)
- [[Vicious Plunderer's Vambraces]] (All Scouts)
