## Steps

1. ![Blue flowers in an Ulthork corpse](images/Blue_Flowers.png.png)Pluck 8 patches of sparkly [[Blue corpse flowers]] from ulthork corpses on the hills above Remnant Shores
   - Possible locations include   {{waypoint 70, 51, 620}}
   - {{waypoint 90.39, 48.19, 532.64}} {{waypoint 101.66, 54.91, 484.90}} {{waypoint 37.96, 56.46, 529.14}} {{waypoint 21.39, 56.24, 567.19}} {{waypoint 33, 52, 602}} {{waypoint -199, 13, 746}} {{waypoint -187, 13, 763}} {{waypoint -248, 12, 763}} {{waypoint -204, 47, 635}}
1. Return to [[Prisa]], she gives you [[Yeppa's Hope]]
1. Use [[Yeppa's Hope]] on 8 large, corrupted dragon bones around Remnant Shores, bones are trackable, have black sparkles.
   - Possible locations include  {{waypoint -9, 56, 590}}{{waypoint 193.62, 7.62, 592.03}} {{waypoint 251.98, 7.46, 662.39}} {{waypoint 221.81, 16.93, 690.38}} {{waypoint 127.76, 7.58, 603.83}}
1. Return to [[Prisa]]
