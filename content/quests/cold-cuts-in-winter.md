## Notes

- This quest can be done concurrent with [[Rise of Thrael'Gorr]] after you speak with [[Chief Thrael'Gorr]].

## Steps

1. Kill 6 mammoths for their meat.
1. Speak with [[Granuk Meatgrinder]] in the Ry'Gorr Keep.
1. Return to [[Farg Bloodtusk]] at the [[Thrael'Gorr Camp]] with the crate of provisions you received from Granuk.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,500 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
