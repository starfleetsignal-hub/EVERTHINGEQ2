## Description

The Quel'ule scholar Azira has requested assistance from a fellow Scholar in crafting some items to help equip the fighters at the front lines. She has warned me that non-scholars will not be able to complete this assignment.

## Steps

1. I have been asked to craft some supplies to aid the fighters defending Quel'ule.
   - Sage: Craft some scrolls of [[Shield of Faith V|Shield of Faith V (Journeyman)]] (level 81).
     - 5 [[Amber]]
     - 15 [[Sumac lumber]]
     - 10 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
     - 5 [[Titanium ore]]
     - 25 [[Ethereal incense]]
   - Jeweler: Craft some runes of [[Makeshift Arrows VI|Makeshift Arrows VI (Journeyman)]] (level 81). Items needed for 5 combines:
     - 5 [[Amber]]
     - 15 [[Bamboo shoot]]
     - 10 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
     - 5 [[Titanium ore]]
     - 25 [[Ethereal coal]]
   - Alchemist: Craft 50 [[Focused Essence of Health]] (level 81).
     - 5 [[Amber]]
     - 15 [[Bamboo shoot]]
     - 10 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
     - 5 [[Titanium ore]]
     - 10 [[Ethereal candle]]
1. Return to [[Researcher Azira]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,500 faction with **Researchers of Quel'ule**

- This quest is one of the Quel'ule Daily Supply Tasks
