## Steps

![Lion Statue](images/Lion_Statue.jpg)

1. Place *Lion Statues* in the [[Temple of Solusek Ro]] in [[Lavastorm]] at the following locations:
   - The balcony above the central chamber. {{waypoint -334, -38, -644}}
   - The library. {{waypoint -436, -61, -667}}
   - Tazgar's dias. {{waypoint -334, -48, -678}}
   - The forge room. {{waypoint -240, -60, -667}}
1. Return to Sir Bayden Cauldthorn.
1. Go to [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]] in [[The Feerrott]] and foil the plans of the lizardmen. {{waypoint -1700, -8, 818}}
   - Destroy the first foci on top of the eastern small pyramid. {{waypoint -51, 40, -108}}
   - Destroy the second foci on top of the western small pyramid. {{waypoint 51, 40, -108}}
   - Destroy the third foci in the green golem room inside the temple. {{waypoint -60, -19, -108}}
   - Destroy the fourth foci in the red golem room inside the temple. {{waypoint 58.05, -19.5, -108.71}}
1. Investigate the [[The Pool of Dread]]. {{waypoint -0.1, 7, -15.5}}
1. Kill the [[Coalescence of Dread]] that spawns.
1. Return to Sir Bayden Cauldthorn.

## Notes

If you are under level 65, [[Venekor (Younger)|Venekor the Younger]] may be sitting between the pyramids and you will not be able to climb the pyramids without getting his attention. You cannot sneak or stealth past him. If you are solo, one strategy is to clear the mobs around the base of one of the small pyramids, then climb the large pyramid and range pull the named lizardmen on top where the foci is found. To range pull from the large pyramid, you should either have the ability to pull from a long distance (such as the [[Longarm]] bow) or have a groupmate who can pet pull.

With the pyramids clear of lizardmen, you will need get the top as quickly as possible and click the foci (small statues) there. If you have anything which can shield you from damage (for example, Divine Aura for the Paladin) use that, because you can be interrupted when you click on the foci. On standard servers, you can use a mercenary to attack Venekor which should buy you enough time to destroy the foci before he kills you. You will need to repeat this for each pyramid, you won't survive long enough to destroy both foci.

On TLE servers, you will either need a raid force strong enough to simply kill Venekor, or a group of players willing to sacrifice themselves to attack Venekor and give you enough time to destroy the foci. Alternatively, you could simply wait and come back later, checking for when Venekor has despawned or has been killed by other players.
