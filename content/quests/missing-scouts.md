## Notes

- This quest is part 1 of an 11-quest series in the [[Bloodline Chronicles Timeline]], which grants you access to [[The Crypt of T'haen]] along the way.
- Be sure to select [[The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers]], not [[The Tombs of Night]], as the latter instance will not spawn the required named monsters.
- The quest listed as a prerequisite, The D'Morte Family Crest, is not required to start this quest. Although in terms of lore progression it is recommended that it be started beforehand.

## Steps

1. You must collect four scout reports inside [[The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers]]. The reports are found on *wounded scouts* in the zone. These are spawned by killing specific named monsters. Once the named monster is dead, simply click on the scouts body that spawns somewhere nearby to receive the quest update. (steps listed in most efficient order)
   - 3rd scout's report: [[The Mold Reaper]]: Spawns at the end of a ring event involving [[A mist grinnin sporechanter (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|mist grinnin sporechanters]] at {{waypoint 78, 6, 3}}
   - 1st scout's report: [[Gulp]] (you can jump off [[The Forgotten Tears (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|the Forgotten Tears {{waypoint 58, -1, 108}}]]):
     1. Kill the 4 groups of  [[A mist grinnin netslinger (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|mist grinnin netslingers]] on the lowest level {{waypoint 60, -40, 58}}, {{waypoint -4, -41, 62}}, {{waypoint -21, -41, 18}}, and {{waypoint -9, -41, -4}}
     1. Kill the 2 groups of grinnins that spawn at {{waypoint 33, -41, 122}} and {{waypoint 20, -41, 102}}
     1. Kill Gulp, who spawns with [[Lureclaw the Angler]], at the base of the waterfall {{waypoint 54, -41, 107}}
   - 4th scout's report: [[Chief Strongarm (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|Chief Strongarm]]:
     1. Kill all 7 groups of [[A mist grinnin ruffian (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|mist grinnin ruffians]] in [[The Maw of Mortality (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|The Maw of Mortality]] {{waypoint 20, -23, 95}} and the nearby caves {{waypoint -20, -24, -92}} and {{waypoint -67, -24, -69}}
     1. Return to the Maw of Motality {{waypoint 36, -26, 58}} and kill the 3 groups of [[A mist grinnin summoner|mist grinnin summoner]]s with 4 ruffians each that (eventually) run into the POI
     1. Kill the [[A mudraker (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|mudrakers]] that spawn around the NPC version of Chief Strongarm (you might have to hail the NPC to spawn the mudrakers)
     1. Kill Chief Strongarm
   - 2nd scout's report: [[Dvith N'Zur]]: Kill him in [[The Hand of the Crypt (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|The Hand of the Crypt]] {{waypoint -74, -32, 116}}
1. Return to [[Trelech Stonehome]] for your reward.
