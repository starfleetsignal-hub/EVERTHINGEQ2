## Steps

![Gaushak Inkleaf](images/Gaushak_Inkleaf.png)

1. Gather **Gaushak Inkleaf** and kill [[A cht'thk spiker|cht'thk spikers]]
   - Gather 8 **Gaushak Inkleaf**. A large bush mostly found around the base of the big trees. Some possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -576, 59, -628}}
     - {{waypoint -615, 53, -669}}
     - {{waypoint -575, 58, -638}}
     - {{waypoint -709, 48, -669}}
     - {{waypoint -557, 55, -644}}
     - {{waypoint -714, 51, -627}}
     - {{waypoint -546, 53, -654}}
     - {{waypoint -698, 54, -579}}
   - Hunt 12 [[A cht'thk spiker|cht'thk spikers]] for their spikes. {{waypoint -446, 59, -645}}
1. Plant Gaushak inkleaf
   - *Locations are marked by green sparkles.*
   - Plant Gaushak inkleaf as camouflage outside the southern wall {{waypoint -833, 37, -763}}
   - Plant Gaushak inkleaf as camouflage at the fork in the path {{waypoint -856, 37, -766}}
   - Plant Gaushak inkleaf as camouflage outside the eastern wall {{waypoint -873, 44, -776}}
   - Plant Gaushak inkleaf as camouflage along the path {{waypoint -846, 37, -766}}
1. Return to [[Rosg]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- One of the following:
  - [[Scavenged Vah Shir Cloak]]
  - [[Scavanged Vah Shir Pitchatka]]
