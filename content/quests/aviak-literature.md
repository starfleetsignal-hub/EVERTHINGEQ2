## Steps

1. Gather 7 [[Encyclopedia of Aviak Literature]] from 'a stack of books', which are the same node as the stack of books which offer the quest. They are found on level 3 and up.
1. Bring the encyclopedias to [[Arejo Cloudbreach]] on [[The Whisperwind Isle]] in [[The Barren Sky]]. {{waypoint -575, -45, -386}}

## Rewards

- At least 13g 59s 50c
- +50 faction with **The Windgazer**
