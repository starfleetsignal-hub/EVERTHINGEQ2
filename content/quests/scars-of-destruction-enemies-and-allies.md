## Notes

- You receive [[Transient Phantasmagnetic Disturbance Mechanisms]] when you start the quest
- [[Focal Focused in the Vaults]] needs the same zone

## Steps

1. Enter [[Dragon Necropolis: Vaults of Veneration (Solo)|Dragon Necropolis: Vaults of Veneration [Solo]]] {{waypoint -747, 67, 283}}
1. Explore the Vaults of Veneration in search of Gearloose's homing drone.
1. Defeat the [[Lucanic Wraithknight Fontus Vess]] <Wraithguard Commander> at {{waypoint 316, 57, 161}}
1. Continue searching the Vaults of Veneration for Gearloose's homing drone at {{waypoint 376.41, 55.50, 375.75}}
   1. Defeat [[Culdakar]] at {{waypoint 367, 56, 393}}
      - *Fear rune*
   1. find Jazirah Punox's corpse around {{waypoint 383, 55, 373}} *updates the quest*
   1. Gather the rune claw around {{waypoint 383, 55, 373}}
1. Gearloose's homing drone couldn't have gotten much further
   - Enter the tunnel at {{waypoint 401, 55, 376}} and click the tunnel at {{waypoint 417.91, 40.26, 400.29}}
1. Kill [[Peraxum the Preposterous]] at {{waypoint 283, 117, -182}}
   1. (use Stifle rune)
1. Locate the homing drone at {{waypoint 374.45, 123.24, -153.56}}
1. The homing drone indicated Gearloose's Magika Machina Magnifica is here, but all i see is a clockwork dragon!
   - attack the [[A Mechanical Dragon]], it will turn into [[Lady Karkona]] and the mobs around will attack you as well
     - *(may need to use your [[Transient Phantasmagnetic Disturbance Mechanisms]])*
   - Kill [[Lady Karkona]] at {{waypoint 382, 125, -105}} When she emotes that she begins to inhale, use your [[Transient Phantasmagnetic Disturbance Mechanisms]] on her to stop it.
1. The Overlord must still be somewhere within the Vaults of Veneration
   - Go to {{waypoint 664.80, 125.03, -95.84}}
1. Kill the [[A Lucanic Knight]] that attack you
1. watch the conversation with "Overlord Lucan D'Lere"
1. Defeat the Void-empowered, resurrected [[Lord Ulvaxazoviak]]
   1. Fight with Lucan against Lord Ulvaxazoviak until Lucan asks for the *Claw rune.*
1. Give the claw to Lucan to finish off Lord Ulvax (Lucan becomes clickable, hand icon).
   1. Dispose of the threats ( 2 [[A Void Devourer|a Void Devourers]] who show up) while Lucan battles Lord Ulvaxazoviak.
1. Lucan was able to open a portal and left with the claw! I have to follow him.
   - Exit the zone through the portal at {{waypoint 647, 125, -108}} *("Return to the Western Wastes.")*
1. Speak with Adris Spiritshine waiting for you right outside in the Narthex of Necropolis {{waypoint -733, 67, 297}}
