## Description

The Dragoons of Thex have sent me to spy on the Dark Elf researchers who have setup a library within the Deep Forge. I am to copy some of their notes and bring them back to the Thexian Dragoons so they can investigate and slow down the research being conducted. I am to show the power of the Thexian Dragoons by slaying one of the members of the Spurned.

1. Copy some research notes from within [[The Deep Forge]]

   - Copy research notes from the [[Magmamatic Mage Headquarters]] {{waypoint -128, 1, -7}}
   - Copy research notes from the [[Magmamatic Mage Headquarters]] {{waypoint -128, 1, -7}}

1. Kill any of the Spurned as an example of your strength.
   - Kill any of the Spurned in the [[Magmamatic Mage Headquarters]] within [[Deep Forge]].
