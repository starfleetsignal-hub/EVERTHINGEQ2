## Steps

1. Enter [[Takish Badlands]] via unfettered carpet {{waypoint -534.56, 3.57, 472.01}}
1. Speak with [[Zorah]] at [[Madman's Rest]] {{waypoint 631.58, 155.29, -729.54}}
1. Speak with [[Silrena]] {{waypoint 625.59, 155.29, -705.11}}
   - Rescue [[Chahida]] {{waypoint 342.70, 82.99, -497.75}}![Rescue the Maetuh Saqr](images/Rescue_the_Maetuh_Saqr.png)
   - Rescue [[Ayoub]] {{waypoint 337.09, 54.01, -151.61}}
   - Rescue [[Saadet]] {{waypoint 268.14, 73.39, -376.49}}
   - Rescue [[Mimoun]] {{waypoint 411.32, 57.10, -412.77}}
   - Rescue [[Amaya]] {{waypoint 369.30, 30.94, -261.51}} - she is not clickable until you kill the Growth Spren next to her.
   - Rescue [[Jibran]] {{waypoint 104.36, 98.49, -419.96}}
1. Return to [[Zorah]]
1. Speak to [[Nairion Eldi]] {{waypoint 630.62, 155.29, -728.00}}
   - Gather 6 **moissanite** in [[Renewal Pond]] {{waypoint 133.15, 80.17, -368.88}} and [[Last Quench Channel]] {{waypoint 520.44, 73.65, -525.43}}![Moissanite & a tidal mist](images/Moissanite_&_a_tidal_mist.png)
   - Gather 6 **disentanglement salts** from [[A tidal mist|tidal mists]] in [[Mhyt-moo Tidal Burrows]] {{waypoint 170.01, 0.19, -57.99}} (not a guaranteed update).
1. Return to [[Nairion Eldi]]. Receive [[Imprint Dissolution Chisel]].
   - Destroy 6 **Caedor's Obelisks** after removing the mental imprint with the chisel. The Obelisks are trackable.![Caedor's Obelisk](images/Caedor's_Obelisk.png)
     - Some possible locations for the obelisks are listed on the Talk page.
1. Return to [[Nairion Eldi]].
   - *This step requires the 6 sub-quests from Zaari and Kamyar, in notes below, for completion.*
   - ![Arbo Shale](images/Ardo_Shale.jpg)Gather 8 **Arbo Shale** all over the [[Tera'Noth Peninsula]]. These geologic items are NOT trackable, harvest- or npc-wise. Some possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -95.59, 104.74, -335.98}}
     - {{waypoint -102.58, 104.74, -348.99}}
     - {{waypoint -123.15, 101.43, -393.2}}
     - {{waypoint -135.29, 105.56, -422.2}}
     - {{waypoint -151.30, 105.56, -410.23}}
     - {{waypoint -182.28, 132.24, -294.36}}
     - {{waypoint -195.84, 131.42, -283.44}}
     - {{waypoint -193.03, 131.14, -250.24}}
     - {{waypoint -226.61, 133.81, -269.40}}
   - Gather **druid's offering bowl** {{waypoint 658.78, 156.17, -715.00}}![Arbo Shale Druid's Offering Bowl & Takish'Hiz Gold](images/Arbo_Shale_Druid's_Offering_Bowl_&_Takish'Hiz_Gold.png)
     - *NOTE: Requires completion of [[Flustered By Flora]], [[Culling Seedlings]] and [[Garden Spikes for Takish Growth]] in order to see the offering bowl*
   - Gather **Takish'Hiz gold** {{waypoint 550.82, 140.64, -653.97}}
     - *NOTE: Requires completion of [[Market Opportunities]], [[Component Parts]], [[Sea of Opportunity]], and* [[Just a Few More Things...]] *to see the Takish'Hiz Sunset Vase*
1. Return to [[Nairion Eldi]]
