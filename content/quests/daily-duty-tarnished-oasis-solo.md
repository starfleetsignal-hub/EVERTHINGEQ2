## Steps

Further into Aether Wroughtlands I go, this time to the realm of copper, and the Zakir Rish that dwell there. They have allies in dragonkind, but the Sang'Huuu have me as their ally, and I shall not fail them!

- Enter [[Aether Wroughtlands: Tarnished Oasis (Solo)]]:
1. Fell the Forerunner
1. Defeat the Tainted
1. Crush the Copper Colossus
1. Drub the Deceptive
1. Hunt the Hawk
