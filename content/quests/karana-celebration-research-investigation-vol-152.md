## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Karana has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items

1. Purchase the [[Stormcaller's Chime|stormcaller's chime]] from Phalvina for 5000 status
   - Wanders along the road between the docks {{waypoint 18, -1, 21}} and the bridge to Rivervale {{waypoint -158, 0, -249}}
1. Purchase the [[Charged Turquoise|charged turquoise]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the [[Captured Storm Cloud|captured storm cloud]] from Nushoon for 5000 status
   - In [[Aether Wroughtlands]] this NPC wanders along a path from the Bivouac Camp {{waypoint 754, -45, -446}} to Minstrel's Cradle {{waypoint 344, -59, -183}}

1. Return to the [[Herald of the Stormlord]] {{waypoint -371, -1, -1091}} in the [[Enchanted Lands]]
