## Steps

1. Collect 8 Infused Velium Shards in [[Great Divide|The Great Divide]]. Possible locations are:
   - {{waypoint 141, -234, -47}}
   - {{waypoint -32, -244, -40}}
   - {{waypoint -597, -298, -8}}
   - {{waypoint -212, -278, -269}}
   - {{waypoint -137, -281, -294}}
   - {{waypoint -46, -255, -265}}
   - {{waypoint 132, -338, -469}}
   - {{waypoint 311, -329, -408}}
1. Return to [[Cryomancer Eriden]].

## Notes

The shards are very sparse, randomly-spawning, non-trackable harvestable nodes which look similar to gem nodes in Kunark areas. They're found outside of Thurgadin, in the Ring War Battlefield {{waypoint 216, -335, -451}} and Daggerflow Glacier {{waypoint -517.56, -305.28, -51.06}} where the shardwurms roam. Check the Ring War war area first then loop around the Daggerflow cliff sides.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
