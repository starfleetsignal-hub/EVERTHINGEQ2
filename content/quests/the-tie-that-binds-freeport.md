This quest rewards the ability to set the home (bind point) of the [[Call of the Overlord]] ability.

## Starting the Quest

Speak with [[Heilanna]] near the Freeport griffon tower at {{waypoint -565, -45, -830}} in [[The Commonlands]].
You receive [[An engraved vessel]]. Inspect the vessel to start your journey.

## Steps

1. You must find some water. This is at the Pride Lake near the Nektulos station. Update at {{waypoint 420, -50, -370}}. Inspect the vessel again to advance the quest.
1. You must inspect a larger portion of the country. Do this by returning to the Nektulos griffon station and fly back to the freeport griffon station. Update is somewhere in the air. Inspect the vessel again to advance the quest.
1. You must find some fire. This is at the towers on the road towards [[West Freeport]] {{waypoint -932, -49, -41}}. On the eastern  tower there is a ladder. When hovering the mouse over the ladder you can select "Climb up". Once you are up, right-click the stick to make some fire. Inspect the vessel again.
1. You must find some earth. Just head back to the center of the crossroads. Inspect the vessel again to finish the quest.

## Rewards

- Ability: [[Set Recall Point]], used to set the bind point for the [[Call of the Overlord]] ability
