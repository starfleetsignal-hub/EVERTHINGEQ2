## Steps

1. Go to the second floor of [[Sulassk Post]] and use the [[Arcanist's Anchor (no effect)|Arcanist's Anchor]] to spawn [[Thisk Lang]]. Incapacitate him with the [[Nerve Toxin]]. {{waypoint -998, 12, -737}}
   - If you kill Thisk without using the nerve toxin, you earn a named AA bonus, but the quest does not update. You'll need to talk to Hraen Vutha again to get a new beacon.
1. Search his body for the *Varis beacon*.
1. Return to [[Hraen Vutha]] in [[Kunzar's Edge (POI)|Kunzar's Edge]]. {{waypoint -1373.73, 24.43, 516.27}}

## Rewards

- At least 9g 86s 15c
- +600 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
