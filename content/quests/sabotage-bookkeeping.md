## Steps

1. Enter the Province District of Qeynos (North Qeynos Harbor, Elddar Grove).
1. **Within 30 minutes**, find and destroy 5 merchant ledgers (they look like open books) on various merchant counters. You may need to enter multiple areas of Qeynos to find enough ledgers.
1. Return to [[Gol M'Tun]].

## Rewards

- Faction Only:
  - +3000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
