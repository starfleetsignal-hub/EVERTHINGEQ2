## Steps

1. Enter [[Shadeweaver's Thicket: Untamed Lands (Solo)|Shadeweaver's Thicket: Untamed Lands [Solo]]]
   - Zone in to the [[City of Shar Vahl]] using the gate at {{waypoint -539.69, 160.83, -751.71}}. Within the city, the entrance is a sewer grate at {{waypoint -136, 31, 242}}.
   - Steps for progressing through the zone may be found at [[Shadeweaver's Thicket: Untamed Lands (Solo)|Shadeweaver's Thicket: Untamed Lands [Solo]]].
   - Note: You are not required to kill any nameds in order to update this quest.  If you prefer, you can proceed directly to [[The Desolate Grounds (Untamed Lands)|The Desolate Grounds]] {{waypoint 377, 18, -551}}.
1. Fight [[A Ka Vethan xakra|Ka Vethan xakras]] and weaken them to 40% health, then use the [[Va Urssan]] to reform them in [[Death's Fingers]] {{waypoint 546.22, 17.40, -369.74}}.  You will need 5 in total. Some general locations:
   - {{waypoint 587, 15, -685}}
   - {{waypoint 599, 21, -506}}
   - {{waypoint 484, 21, -519}}
   - {{waypoint 518, 15, -385}}
   - *These have a decent amount of HP so don't be afraid of laying into them. Even if you get them to 0 Health, they will regen.*
   - **Tips** : to reduce drastically your fight time, drag them into the static fields, do a few hits them drag them out and voila ! Alternately, dispel them. Same result.
1. Return to [[Ne'xath]] in Shadeweaver's Thicket {{waypoint -202, 61, 106}}.

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
