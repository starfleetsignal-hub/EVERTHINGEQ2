## Steps

1. Harvest a *Jinisk sentry backpack* found next to the [[Jinisk sentry remains|remains of a Jinisk sentry]] to receive [[A Jinisk sentry log]]. The backpack has a respawn timer of five minutes and can be found at {{waypoint 769, 68, -500}}, {{waypoint 723, 67, -520}} or {{waypoint 715, 68, -446}}.
1. Kill 3 members of Ry'zilk's Renegades ([[A renegade snooper|renegade snoopers]], [[A renegade infiltrator|renegade infiltrator]], etc.). {{waypoint 723, 67, -520}}
1. Return to Captain Venk.
