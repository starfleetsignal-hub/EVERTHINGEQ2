## Notes

This quest is infinitely repeatable while this prelude event is active.

When accepting quest you will have 2 recipes scribed into your book - [[Stygianrift Repulsor Rune Plate]] and [[Nether Plane Ward]], you will be asked to craft 6 of either one, chosen randomly.

- You can buy Glowing Coal from [[A Flamebearer Supply Master]] nearby.
- Each combine requires 1 x Chaotic Elemental Filament + 2 of any common harvested metal + 3 of any common harvested roots + 2 of any common harvested  gemstone + 3 Glowing Coal. Each combine makes 1 - 2 items.
- You can use Work bench behind the tents at {{waypoint -2040, 1, 163}} or any other Work Bench, including summoned ones.

## Steps

1. Check Artificers' crates around Jainossa to  find out what exactly needs to be crafted this time.
1. Craft the required 6 items.
1. Speak with [[Jainossa Dawnsong]].

- AA experience
