## NOTE:

- Part of the [[Oceansfull Festival]] event
- While the dungeon itself can be repeated every 90 mins without limit, this mission can only be completed by individual characters *once* per 18hr serverwide 'window'.   Once the 'window' resets, characters may complete the mission again. Repeating for each 18hr window the entire length of the festival.  Players can view the current server-replay timer by searching for "Oceansfull" (*or simply 'oc**) in the bottom search bar of the "Mission Timer" tab of the quest journal.  The 'Mission tracker' window also shows if the currently logged in character has already completed the quest during the current 18hr window.
  - *This also means players can take advantage of this window-to-window mechanic to complete this quest twice in a short amount of time if they complete it right before the end of one 18hr 'window', then complete it right after the new 18hr 'window' opens up. It's still another 18 hours until the next window after that, but the player doesn't have to 'wait' the full 18 hrs in the current window, can be completed at any point in that current 'window'.*<br>
