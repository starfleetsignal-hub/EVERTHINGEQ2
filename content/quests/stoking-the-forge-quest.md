## Steps

1. Kill the following
   - kill [[A heated beetle|heated beetle]] on [[Helionae Razelands]] near {{waypoint -226.38, 106.45, -202.56}} to obtain 8 heated beetle elytra
   - Kill [[A scorched direfang|scorched direfangs]] near {{waypoint -241.92, 99.86, -213.95}} to obtain 10 scorched direfang briquettes
1. Speak to [[Sqrauwk]].

- 4p 4g 15s 72c
