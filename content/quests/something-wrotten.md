## Steps

1. Speak to [[Dabhiti]] at {{waypoint 15, 98, 575}}.
1. Return to [[Wrott Havenmore]].
1. Explore [[Accursed Chantry Island]] {{waypoint -522, 9, -382}}.
1. Enter the accursed chantry at {{waypoint -622.37, 16.37, -363.13}}
1. kill [[A drowned one|drowned ones]] (counted up to 5 kills needed).
1. Speak with [[Wrott Havenmore]] within the Chantry at {{waypoint -630, 17, -356}}.
1. Search for 5 red pearls (red glowy areas around the inside of the walls) hidden in the walls of the Accursed Chantry. ![Red Pearl](images/Redchantrypearl.png)
1. Place the red perals at the pillars
   - Place red pearl in pillar 1 {{waypoint -619, 16, -373}}.
   - Place red pearl in pillar 2 {{waypoint -611, 16, -368}}.
   - Place red pearl in pillar 3 {{waypoint -616, 16, -359}}.
   - Place red pearl in pillar 4 {{waypoint -624, 16, -363}}.
1. Place red pearl in center on floor {{waypoint -618, 16, -365}}.
1. Sacrifice ceremony is triggered!
   - *Just wait a moment for the quest to update*
1. speak with [[Wrott Havenmore]].
1. Kill [[Wrott Havenmore]]
   - *Turn your mount back on after the fight*
1. speak to [[Dabhiti]] {{waypoint 15, 98, 575}}.
