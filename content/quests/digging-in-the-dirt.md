## Prerequisite

- You must be at least level 85 to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Fly to the Quel'ule Station.
1. Search for lost Clan Gragnar kobolds. Find a path which starts at {{waypoint -324, 387, 931}} , and follow it to its end in a cave where the quest updates around {{waypoint -579, 478, 891}}. - *Note: If you're able to jump or fly you can also just enter the cave at {{waypoint -529, 485, 847}}*
1. Find something which proves the existence of clan Gragnar by looting [[An ancient helmet]] from [[A Gragnar hoarder]] at {{waypoint -562, 478, 946}} in the corner of the cave you previously explored, then return to [[Kalaish Al'Rabin|Kalaish]].
   - You can invis to do this just stay close to the edge the shaman in the middle does see thru invis
1. Return the helmet to Kalaish Al'Rabin.
1. Find the lost scout who never returned from patrol in the mountains. [[Ozlem Fatar|Ozlem Fatar's]] body is at  {{waypoint -175, 373, 929}}  examine it to receive his [[Ozlem's Journal|journal]].
1. Next return to Kalaish Al'Rabin in Moonfield Hamlet.
1. Next you must talk to [[Khaliq Keshalian]] at {{waypoint -475, 379, 658}} [[Quel'ule]] near the launch pad.
1. Help him recover [[A smashed voidcraft controller]] from the terrorfangs. The controller comes from [[A terrorfang plunderpaw]] which wanders near {{waypoint -118, 270, 168}}.
1. Return to [[Khaliq Keshalian]].
1. Khaliq asks you to activate 3 beacons for him. You'll probably want to do Primary and Tertiary before Secondary.![beacon](images/Digging_in_the_Dirt_beacon.jpg)
   - Primary beacon - {{waypoint -543, 376, 633}}
   - Secondary beacon - {{waypoint -141, 366, 718}}
   - Tertiary beacon - {{waypoint -439, 388, 828}}
1. Return to Khaliq once more and talk to him after his experiment fails.
   - If Khaliq does not return the item to your inventory, hail him again. If Khaliq will not respond to you, come back 24 hours later (in real life).
1. Use the [[Voidcraft Controlling Device]] at the edge of [[Cleft of Tranquility]]. A blue message across your screen will indicate a suitable location, around {{waypoint 51, 447, 243}}, at the end of the cliff.
1. Return to [[Khaliq Keshalian]].
1. Search for the sources of magical anomaly. Enter the gruengach caves {{waypoint 370, 275, 165}} and kill [[Xosith the Greedy]] {{waypoint 125.28, 238.46, 296.80}}.  Loot a [[Large iron key]] from him. (This is the only Heroic step in the quest.)
1. Open the chest in the other chamber in the cave at {{waypoint 283, 227, 78}} to claim your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Grimblade]]
- At least 74,088 status
