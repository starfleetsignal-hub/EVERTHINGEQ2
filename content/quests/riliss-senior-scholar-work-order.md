*Supplies at the front are running low. I hope I can trust you to ensure this order gets filled promptly and to the expected quality levels.*

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

- I need to create:
  - (6) [[Khalysta's Restoration (Crafted)|Khalysta's Restoration]]
  - (6) [[Torment of Rijacki (Crafted)|Torment of Rijacki]]
  - (6) [[Lucky earring]]
  - (6) [[Ion stones]]
  - (6) [[Zoltaroth's Rescue|Zoltaroth's Rescue (Journeyman)]]
  - (6) [[Touch of Silverfrost|Touch of Silverfrost (Journeyman)]]
- Turn the completed order into [[Preparations Officer Zekilius]]

## Rewards

- At least 38g 86s 34c
- +750 faction with **Riliss**
