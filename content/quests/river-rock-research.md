## Steps

![An example of the rocks you need to collect.](images/River_rock.jpg)

1. Collect 8 river rock samples throughout [[Kejaan's Rill]]. Often found next to the larger scenic rocks.
   - Some locations include: {{waypoint -142, 236, 3359}} {{waypoint 110, 224, 3690}} {{waypoint 197, 225, 3723}} {{waypoint 345, 228, 3693}} and {{waypoint 350, 225, 3736}}.
   - The river rocks are considered harvestables and can be tracked.
   - The rocks eventually do respawn, if you cannot find enough nodes at first.
1. Return to Fazia.

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**
