- The Sang'Huuu are threatened by evil from every direction, and struggle to keep their home safe. They request aid, and so I give it. Across the celestial skies of Zimara shall I travel, and deliver destruction to those who wish the same upon the hooluk of Splendor Sky Aerie.

## Steps

1. Overthrow the Queen within Razing the Razelands
1. Defeat the General with Argent Sanctuary
1. Slay the Spared within Ferric Fields

- At least 10p 77g 75s 36c
- 30 [[Zimaran Ballad]]
