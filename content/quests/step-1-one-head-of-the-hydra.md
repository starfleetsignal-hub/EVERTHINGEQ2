## Notes

- This quest and the others offered by [[Standard Ku'Uul]] can be started without having done the previous quest, [[Taking Action]].

## Steps

1. Kill [[Captain Redspit]] in a cave on a cliff on the west end of [[The Green Overlook]]. {{waypoint 2697, 118, 850}}
1. Open the chest that the Captain Redspit was guarding. {{waypoint 2698, 118, 849}}
1. Return to [[Standard Ku'Uul]].

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
