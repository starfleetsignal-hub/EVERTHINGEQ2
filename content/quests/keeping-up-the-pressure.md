## Steps

1. Kill up to 12 Deathfist orcs in the [[Deathfist Citadel]].
1. Return to [[Emissary Baeth]].

## Rewards

- At least 27s 67c (59s 17c  at level 41)
- A ring based on class:
  - [[Crest of Fire Setting]]  (All Scouts)
  - [[Emblem of Fire Setting]] (All Priests)
  - [[Ring of Fire Setting]]   (All Fighters)
  - [[Signet of Fire Setting]] (All Mages)
