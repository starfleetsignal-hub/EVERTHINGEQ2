## Steps

1. Kill 25 vampires in [[Castle Mistmoore]].
1. Return to [[Tychus Zeksworn]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +10000 faction with **Rallos Zek**
- [[War Mantle of Rallos Zek]]
- Unlocks the blessing and miracle pair: [[Battlerage]] and [[War's Aftermath]]
