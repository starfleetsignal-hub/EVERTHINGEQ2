## Steps

![Serenity Crystal](images/Meditation_and_Annihilation_serenity_crystal.jpg)

1. Enter the water to meditate {{waypoint -406, 138, -492}}.
   - Slay a pair of attacking [[An aerakyn assassin|aerakyn assassins]].
1. Return to Gortov.
   - When you return to his original location, you find that Gorotov has fled! (Note: You must trigger the update from this location before going to the next step)
   - Search for Gortov on the small island to the west. {{waypoint -121, 29, -512}}, he will appear when you approach the location, but be careful on landing that you don't go near the named on the island ([[Voltross the Relic Seeker]])!
1. Confront Gortov.
   - After a taunt or two, [[Gortov (Mob)|Gortov]] attacks. Slay him.
1. Destroy 8 serenity crystals - the ones that you planted on Karak Peak. They respawn quickly.
1. Return to [[Jortreva]].
