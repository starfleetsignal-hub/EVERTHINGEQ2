## Steps

1. [[Researcher Tahar]] in what remains of [[Quel'ule]] has asked me to assist him by catching one of the creatures in the area of [[Quel'ule]] to learn more about the effects of the magic fields on them.
   - Just outside the city on the path leading to the south, there are many small [[Fearstalker cub|fearstalker cubs]] to choose from.  Click one to update the quest. {{waypoint 6, 367, 745}}, {{waypoint -394, 370, 644}}
   - Clicking the cub will not break invis.
1. Return to [[Researcher Tahar]].

## Rewards

- At least 20g 5s 35c
- [[A tame fearstalker|a tame fearstalker]]
- +3000 faction with **Researchers of Quel'ule**
