The dark side of the moon is in the midst of dangerous times, no more so than today. And so I've been tasked with bringing down the enemies of Fordel within Shar Vahl: Siege Break.

## Steps

- See [[Shar Vahl: Siege Break (Solo)]] for zone walkthrough:
1. Kill the phantom invader.
1. Dismantle the amalgam.
1. Defeat the intruder.
1. Hunt the huntress.
1. Break the siege.
