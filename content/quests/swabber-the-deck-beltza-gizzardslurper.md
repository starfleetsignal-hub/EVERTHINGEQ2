## Note

*make sure that you only have your brokenskull disguise up, otherwise you can't get the update from Scabby.*

## Steps

1. Hail [[Beltza Gizzardslurper (NPC)|Beltza Gizzardslurper]] in [[Firmroot Moot]] {{waypoint 58, -72, -524}} and ask for her axe, she will decline and attack you
1. Kill [[Beltza Gizzardslurper (Named)|Beltza Gizzardslurper]].
1. Return to [[Scabby Gabby]] in the caves of [[Brokenskull Loft]].
   - If you have died since picking up the quest, she may bug out and not appear to complete your quest. Simply go to a safespot, cancel your illusion and then re-apply it, and she should be talking to you again.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
