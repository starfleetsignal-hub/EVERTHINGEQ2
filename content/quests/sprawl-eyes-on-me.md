## Notes

You are given [[Shock Stick (Sprawl Eyes on Me)|Shock Stick]] when accepting quest.

## Named

1. [[Russula]]
1. [[Xaos Cleaver]] at entrance to Forbidden Forest area, strong initial knockback
1. [[Adleza Pura]] has AE stun, summons adds
1. [[KrzzyxKrzz]] knockback
1. [[Dominar Zruul]] linked with [[Lyyrk Nert]] and [[Tyzzx Ton]]

## Steps

1. Enter [[Oogothl Sprawl: Zruul's Dominion (Solo)|Oogothl Sprawl: Zruul's Dominion [Solo]]] - teleport at {{waypoint -281, 84, -147}} at [[Eerie Ridges]] in [[Oogothl Sprawl]].
1. Search shocked mechaneyes for items - use your Shock Stick on mechaneyez patrollers, then click their husks to search their back compartment. After 4th patroller you will get a message about finding a Voidflow Crystal (just an update, not an actual item)
1. Search Forbidden Forest for hidden voidflow crystals
   - *these are small pink crystals hiding in tall grasses*
   1. When you get in vicinity of first crystal you will get a message *I found a hidden crystal in Forbidden Forest*
   1. destroy the hidden crystal
   1. then destroy 7 more hidden voidflow crystals, possible locations:
      - {{waypoint -603, 170, -356}}, {{waypoint -504, 159, -407}}, {{waypoint -549, 189, -491}}, {{waypoint -756, 194, -263}}<br>
      - {{waypoint -641, 171, -225}}, {{waypoint -706, 185, -242}}, {{waypoint -792, 193, -367}}, {{waypoint -838, 193, -343}}
   1. destroying the 7th crystal spawns [[Dominar Zruul]] and knocks you back.
1. Speak to [[Dominar Zruul]]
1. Reach Synod Dais, Dominar Zruul's Throne - teleport at {{waypoint -590, 159, -295}} will activate when you kill 4th named Krzzyx Krzz at {{waypoint -616, 187, -470}}.
1. Investigate Dominar Zruul's throne - kill Dominar Zruul and his 2 priests and approach the throne where Zruul stood.
1. Destroy Zruul's psychic projector - click Zruul's Psychic projector at {{waypoint -690, 286, -344}}. *It cannot be destroyed*
1. Click the projector again to gather it.
1. Return to Xhol'Rog in [[Oogothl Sprawl]]
