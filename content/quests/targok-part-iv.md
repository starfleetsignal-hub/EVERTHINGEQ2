This quest is part 4 of a 4-part quest series involving [[Nektulos Forest]].

## Steps

*NOTE: Targok will not offer this quest until you have completed the quest, [[Varski, Part IV]].*

1. Kill 50 Thexians in Nektulos Forest.
   - Thexians of all types can be found in the [[Valley of the Bears]] at their camps and guarding the surrounding area.
1. Return to Targok.

## Rewards

- At least 11s 40c
