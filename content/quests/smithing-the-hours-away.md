Udella Kegmail recruited me to use my smithing skills to help G.L.E.E.S.H. provide support to the Luclinites who are trying to repair and relocate to the city of Fordel Midst. Their need for metal plate and nails is high.

## Steps

- Check the smiths' crate near Udella Kegmail to see what to make for the next shipment to Luclin. You can find the [[Solidified ethereal filament|solidified ethereal filament]] needed around Teren's Grasp.
  - Craft 6 **stacks of metal plate**
  - Craft 6 **boxes of nails**
- Return to Udella Kegmail to turn in.

## Rewards

- At least 11s 08c
- 2 [[G.L.E.E.S.H. Mark]]
