## Steps

*Outlander Deston, a member of the Qeynos Guard alarmed by the assembly of gnoll packs, was rather hopeful that this tenuous collaboration of gnoll packs would crumble quickly once the shaman had been defeated, but considering the shaman was really a freeblood, and they survived the battle, his fears have not been conquered.*

1. Destroy 6 roughhewn stone coffins
   - Found mostly in the Ancient Gnoll Graveyard and Terra Fungi areas, including at:
     - {{waypoint -77, -68, -62}}
     - {{waypoint -126, -69, -102}}
     - {{waypoint 113, -79, -123}}
     - {{waypoint 74, -96, -179}}
     - {{waypoint -5, -68, -319}}
     - {{waypoint -34, -67, -56}} (requires a [[Darkpaw Ladder]], or AOE)
1. Hunt 25 fighter skilled gnolls
   - Only specific gnolls count for this step, including:
     - Tesch Mal sentinel
     - a Rockpaw battler
     - a Garatch Mot warmonger
     - a Dustpaw watcher
     - a Sabertooth enforcer
     - a Mudpaw repeller
     - a Snowfang hunter
1. Speak with Outlander Deston in Darkpaw Dugout

## Rewards

- At least 3p 36g 79s 77c
- [[Serilian Hammered Plate Vambraces]]
- [[Roughhewn Stone Coffin]]
