## Steps

1. Travel to [[Jarsath Wastes]] and use the [[Catalogue Creature]] ability to catalog:
   - Catalog [[A skyshadow drake]] near [[Valley of the Ancients]] around {{waypoint -2043, 170, 1356}}
     - West of Sokokar Post of the White Lady
   - Catalog [[An ancient skyfire wurm]] near [[Valley of the Ancients]] around {{waypoint -1675.87, 172.58, 1618.88}}
   - Catalog [[An elder skyfire wurm]] near [[Ragefire's Talon]] around {{waypoint -1657.91, 191.32, 1006.66}}
   - Catalog [[A gnashing devourer]] near [[Orinox Cave]] Around {{waypoint -1566.56, 204.31, 326.25}}
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]

## Hints

The creatures are trackable.  If you don't have tracking, many ranger mercs can track, for instance [[Villax Sned]] from the VoV expansion can track.

- Unlock the Days of Summer 2022: Week 8 Holiday event rewards that you can purchase from [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2293, -199, 2506}}
