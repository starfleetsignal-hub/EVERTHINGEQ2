## Steps

![The sifting trough you need to use.](images/Stonebrunt_sifter.jpg)

1. Use the sifter to sift through the samples for magical metals or gems.
   - The Sifter is inside the tree just to the south of Fazia {{waypoint 1033, 113, 3809}}.  Click on it to receive your update.
1. Return to Fazia.

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**
