## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Peat Bog]].
1. Collect eight Bog Faerie Wings from [[A bog faerie|bog faeries]]. {{waypoint 626, -34, 518}}
1. Return to Varion Smitelin for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Embossed fairy wing necklace]]
- At least 2s
- +65 faction with **The Concordium**
