## Notes

Once you reach +22,000 faction with [[Pride Pakiat]], you'll have to do this quest 8 more times, or 4 times if you pair it with 4 more of [[Operation Crustacean Station Continuation]], to get you to +30,000 so you can move-on to [[Khat-ra Pakiat]]'s quests which require +30k faction to offer them.

## Steps

![bulbs on the underside of the tree arches](images/Green_Fruit_For_Rut_-_Fruit.png)

1. Travel to the [[Esoterra Gardens]] near {{waypoint -634, 31, 263}}
1. Click on 8 bulbs. They can be found on the underside of the tree arches.
1. Kill the [[A green fruit bat|green fruit bat]] that pops when you collect the 8th bulb.
1. Return to [[Izny Rut]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -1,250 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1,250 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +1,250 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
