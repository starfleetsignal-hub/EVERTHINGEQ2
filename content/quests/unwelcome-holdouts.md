## Description

If Kor'sha is to ever be explored, then the current occupants will need to be dealt with. I must clear out enough of the creatures inhabiting the lost iksar structure, so that exploration teams might be able to uncover the secrets hidden within its walls.

## Steps

1. Destroy creatures found within [[Emperor Atrebe's Laboratory: The Fabled City of Kor-sha|Kor'sha]]
   - Destroy 12 ancient guards
   - Destroy 12 hostile abberations
