## Description

[[Tobel Patadash]] has come up with his next plan to prank the Gigglegibber Goblins and has asked for my help once again. In noticing that the Gigglegibber are obsessed with hoarding riches and loot at an almost fanatical diligence, Tobel has concocted a most mischievous plan to trick the Gigglegibber out of some of their loot. He has asked me to sneak inside the Gigglegibber's hideout and seek out the room containing their numerous bank vaults. After finding a way to access one of the vaults, I am to replace a sack of gold coins with a sack of chocolate coins covered in fake gold wrappers. Once i replace the sack of coins, I need to return to Tobel and deliver the real sack of coins.

## Steps

![Underwater Entrance to the Gigglegibber's hideout](images/Gigglegibber_hideout_manhole.jpg)
![gigglegibber note on a table inside the gigglegibber hideout](images/Gigglegibber_note.jpg)

1. Get [[A Sack of Chocolate Gold Coins]] from [[Tobel Patadash]].
1. Enter [[The Gigglegibber Hideout]] through a manhole cover underwater beneath the [[Bobick Village Docks]] in [[Enchanted Lands]]. {{waypoint 40, -29, 115}}
   - The zone is filled with [[A Gigglegibber goblin|Gigglegibber goblins]]. If you get too close to them they will aggro and teleport you out of the instance.
   - The stationary goblins near the wall do not see invis, but the roaming goblins do.
1. Approach the vaults in the room near the zone-in. {{waypoint 236, -16, -240}}
1. Gather [[A Scrap of Paper]] from the back room of the instance. It has multiple spawn locations within that room, and can be tracked using [[Track Harvestables]]. {{waypoint 167.97, 1.32, -330.06}} or {{waypoint 200.02, 6.16, -341.49}}
1. Read the note from your inventory.
1. Go back to the vault room and unlock the second-from-the-right of the vaults.
1. Take a sack of gold coins from the unlocked vault and replace it with the sack of chocolate gold coins.
1. Return the sack of gold coins to Tobel Patadash.
1. Speak with [[Garby Gingham Gigglegibber]]. {{waypoint 74, -1, 22}}
1. Return to Tobel Patadash.

## Rewards

- At least 12g 57s 51c
- +5000 faction with **Bristlebane**

## Hints and Spoilers

If you get the note first from upstairs, you don't lose it if you get kicked out.  Do this part first, and that will make the other part easier.

When you try to get in the vault, first thing to do is to wait for the roaming goblin in the hallway to start walking away.  Open the vault doors.  Another goblin will come up to the door, wait until he is going off to the left.  Once he is doing this, go off to the right, and hug the wall.  Once you are there you are safe.  When you are near the vaults, wait for the roamer to head to you, than away from the vaults.  Once he is gone, go up to the vaults, get the update, then back up again to the safe spot.  Read the note.  Then wait for the goblin again.  Once he has roamed by again, click the vault second away from you (third from the left), and wait.  Click it again really fast after that, and wait.  Once this is done you will have the coins.  Sneak out the same way you came in, and you won't get kicked out.

If for some reason you are trying to do this with a class that has a pet, you can send your pet in to attack and will not have to worry about being kicked out.

If you evac while inside the hideout you will end up outside the entrance to Runnyeye, not the entrance to the hideout or the docks as you would hope or logically think you would.
