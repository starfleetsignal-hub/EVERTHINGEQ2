## Starting the Quest

- *
- ***Prerequisite:*** Lvl 50+ Sage

## Steps

1. Speak to Xin-xu
1. Speak to [[Ghil Ventur]] in [[Antonica]], near the [[Coldwind Point|Coldwind Point Lighthouse]].
1. Gather 5 pieces of bark from mulberry bushes in [[The Village of Shin]], within the Courtyard of Hope. {{waypoint 29, 14, -60}} You can harvest one bush 5 times.
1. Collect starch from the Nurwin's storage shed. {{waypoint -50, 11, -114}}
1. Speak to [[Spark Augren]]. {{waypoint 13.62, 8.69, -31.84}}
1. Return to [[Xin-xu Augren]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Wantia Artisan's Satchel]]
- [[Augren's Auspicious Inkwell]]
