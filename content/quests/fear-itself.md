## Steps

1. I need to absorb the essence of slain Fear creatures using the blood ruby that Gulthga gave me. Additionally, I will need to collect branches from the surrounding trees.
   - Use the [[Fear-Deficient Blood Ruby]] to link to Fear creatures, then kill them to absorb their essence (9)
   - ![A fear tree branch](images/A_fear_tree_branch.png)Collect [[A fear-stricken branch|Fear tree branches]] (3)
     - Various trees in the area have attached branches that are clickable for this update.  They are "small greyish-brown branches" all observable from ground level and found near -1,500, 220, 647.
       - {{waypoint -1416, 218, 525}} (this one is outside the blue-shaded search area)
       - {{waypoint -1557, 225, 700}}
       - {{waypoint -1545, 227, 797}}
1. Now that I have all the components, I can combine them into a fear-divining rod.
   - Combine the blood ruby and branches into a rod
     - Right-click on the [[Fear-Infused Blood Ruby]] in your inventory, and choose Use to create the [[Fear-Divining Rod]].
1. I need to use the fear-divining rod to analyze various creatures from the Plane of Fear.
   - Analyze several [[A fearstruck soullight|fearstruck soullights]] (3)
   - Analyze several [[A savage shiverback|shiverback gorillas]] (5)
   - Analyze several [[A feargaze creeper|feargaze creepers]] (5)
   - Analyze several [[A dreadooze|dreadoozes]] (5)
   - Analyze several [[An insidious fearling|insidious fearlings]] (5){{waypoint -1742, 219, 964}}
1. I've discovered something interesting about the Fear creatures here, and need to report back to Gulthga to determine what it means.
   - Speak to Gulthga

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- [[Ceremonial Urn of the Faceless]]
