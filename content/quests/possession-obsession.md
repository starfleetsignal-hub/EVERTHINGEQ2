## Steps

Jarkun believes a Void being attacked elder Kernalo and possessed him. You are going to investigate.

1. Search out the location of the attack.
   - Go to {{waypoint 251, 67, -237}}
1. Destroy 3x  dark skitterings at Kernalo's body
   - the mechanic of only 1 of 3 aggroing, then resetting  so you can kill  3 total, is intentional.
1. Examine Kernalo's body
   - Examine corpse of Herd Elder Kernalo at {{waypoint 249.17, 67.36, -235.86}}
1. Return to Jarkun
