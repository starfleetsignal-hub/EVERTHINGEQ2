## Steps

1. Climb the ladder to the upper level and speak to [[Meg Gingersnap]]. {{waypoint -15, 4, -56}}
1. Go outside and harvest a decorated Frostfell tree. {{waypoint -55, 0, -22}}
1. Return to the Workshop's basement and click the *Enchanted Snowglobe*. {{waypoint -16, -5, -58}}
1. Head due west through [[Maldura]] and click the sparkly spot in the [[Palace Foyer]]. {{waypoint 137, 62, -1}}
1. Speak to Jarith near where you arrived. {{waypoint -161, -7, -33}}

## Rewards

- 1 [[Frozen Token of E'ci]]
