I found three strange black rings that seemed to exude an ancient magic. After putting them on, I find myself overcome by an insatiable lust for battle. I believe I must satisfy the thirst of the rings if I am to free myself from their influence.

## Steps

1. Kill 40 creatures in [[Nektulos Forest]].

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Crest of Imaginable Power]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Emblem of Imaginable Power]]
  - [[Ring of Imaginable Power]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Signet of Imaginable Power]]
