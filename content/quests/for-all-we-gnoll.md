## Steps

*Outlander Deston, a member of the Qeynos Guard under Consul Bree, confused me for the additional forces he had requested, when I met him in Darkpaw Dugout. He was rather relieved when I offered to assist the investigation into the assembly of gnoll packs anyway.*

1. Enter Darkpaw Warrens
   1. Click on the door at {{waypoint -24, -76, -255}}
1. Clear the Darkpaw Warrens entrance of gnolls
   1. Defeat the 4 non-aggro Sabertooth gnolls in the entrance room
   1. Move to {{waypoint -24, -42, -261}}, the entrance to the next room.  This will update the quest, and cause Ranger Alphin and the Splitpaw Refugee gnolls to spawn.  Outlander Deston will also appear in the first room.
1. Search goods scattered around Darkpaw Warrens for any sign of outside influence
   1. Search 6 crates for a clue (the larger crates)
   1. Search 7 barrels for a clue
   1. The crates and barrels do not despawn when searched, but each can only be searched one time.
1. Return to Outlander Deston near the entrance inside Darkpaw Warrens {{waypoint -64, -28, -285}}
