## Steps

1. Kill [[A shadowed man (Nektulos Forest)|shadowed men]] until you find the *Stalker's Journal* (common update). Shadowmen can be found close to D'Morte Burial Tomb, not far from the beach path {{waypoint 586.48, 1.38, -535.24}}.
1. Return to Pulsarian.
