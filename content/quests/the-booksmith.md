## Steps

1. Locate 3 unique books:
   - A non-fiction book: *"Mistmoore than You Know"* at {{waypoint 98, 70, -68}}.
   - A book with a unique binding: *"Of Bats & Men"* at {{waypoint 85.30, 45.79, 8.39}}.
   - A rare book: *"The Old Vampire and the Sea"* at {{waypoint 31, 72, 84}}.
1. Defeat [[Spectral Librarian Emiida]].
1. Speak with Gemmi Booksmith in the Great Divide at {{waypoint 1070.11, -456.86, 507.76}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g
- [[Rug of the Cursed]]
- [[Rug of the Spurned]]
