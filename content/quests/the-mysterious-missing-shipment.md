## Steps

1. Speak to [[Nethet]], a ratonga found outside the Freeport city gates. {{waypoint -1270, 0, 60}}
1. You must now complete Nethet's quest [[A Friend in Need]] to continue this one.
1. Return to [[Brandus Levine]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 13s 23c
- One of the Following, Based on Class:
  - [[Focused Initiate's Foot Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Divine Theist's Plate Boots]] (Clerics)
  - [[Radiant Seer's Chain Boots]] (Shamans)
  - [[Mighty Footman's Sabatons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Enchanted Adept's Shoes]] (All Mages)
  - [[Vicious Plunderer's Chain Shoes]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Untainted Naturalist's Leather Boots]] (Druids, Channelers)
