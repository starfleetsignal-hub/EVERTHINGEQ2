## Steps

1. Kill 4 [[A crumbling tomb sentry|crumbling tomb sentries]].
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 5 [[A crumbling tomb guard|crumbling tomb guard]]s.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 4 [[A festering zombie|festering zombie]]s.
1. Read the book.

## Rewards

- Experience only
- Completing all three quests from [[A Collection of Epitaphs (Quest Starter)|A Collection of Epitaphs]], in any order, rewards [[A Collection of Epitaphs (House Item)|A Collection of Epitaphs]], a house item
