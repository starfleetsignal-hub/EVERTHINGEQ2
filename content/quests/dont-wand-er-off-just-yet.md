## Requirements

- Must be Level 80
- Must speak [[Druzaic]]

## Steps

1. Zone into [[Miragul's Phylactery: Scion of Ice]]
1. Defeat [[Shade of Miragul]] for his robe
1. Zone into [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Anathema]]
1. Defeat [[Patriae Vykel]] for the drop of blood
1. Zone into [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Crucible]]
1. Defeat [[The Codexicon]] for the page
1. Return to [[Aubrak]]
