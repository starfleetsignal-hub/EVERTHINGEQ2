## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Sundered Frontier]] to place some [[Geographic Simulacrumator|Geographic Simulacrumators]].
   - *A quick way to get the updates would be to start with the [[Toxxulia's Mound|Mound]] update, followed by the [[Hua Mein Village|Hua Mein]] update, then evac to [[The City of Paineel|Paineel]] if you can, and finish with [[Kerra Isle]] before returning to Lochanya.*
   - Deploy a geographic simulacrumator in [[Paineel]] - {{waypoint 1933, -300, 3375}}
   - Deploy a geographic simulacrumator at [[Toxxulia's Mound]] - {{waypoint 192, 116, 2638}}
   - Deploy a geographic simulacrumator at [[The Village of Kerra]] - {{waypoint 1256, 45, 2128}}
   - Deploy a geographic simulacrumator in [[Hua Mein Village]] - {{waypoint -290, 250, 2894}}
1. Return to [[Lochanya]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]].
