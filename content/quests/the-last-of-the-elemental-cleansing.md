This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Gather materials for Saedie
   - Collect 10 void essences, a common drop from void creatures in [[Najena's Cove]].
   - Collect 2 void lattices, an uncommon drop from the same void creatures.
1. Return to [[Saedie Kalterra]]
1. Kill a corrupted [[Elemental Lord]]. They can be spawned in one of the rooms of Golden Skull Cave by setting all 3 portals to the same element using three of the orbs and then activating the final orb.

## Notes

- The repeatable quest [[Dispatching the Destroyers]] can easily be completed at the same time as The Last of the Elemental Cleansing.
- The quest [[Elemental Mystery]] requires an Elemental Lord of Posion that can only be spawned on the appropriate step of one of the three daily Cleaning the Elements quests.

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- +500 faction with **Order of Flame**
- [[Unbound Elemental Essence]]
