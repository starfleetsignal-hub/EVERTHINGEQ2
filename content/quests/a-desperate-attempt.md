## Steps

![Image of ghostly arctic seaweed](images/A_ghostly_arctic_seaweed.png)

1. Speak with [[Sana]] at {{waypoint -1480.18, -515.23, -2256.21}}
1. Gather medicinal ingredients on the thin ice sheets at {{waypoint -1596, -532, -2535}}
   - Gather 5 ghostly arctic seaweed
     - These are found on the underside of the ice sheets {{waypoint -1536.68, -545.95, -2514.96}} and not on the ocean floor!
   - Kill 5 [[An armorclad ice urchin|armorclad ice urchins]]
1. Speak to Sana who is now with Nia at {{waypoint -1402, -538, -2098}}.  He will cast a ritual spell on Fiver.
1. Speak to Sana again.
