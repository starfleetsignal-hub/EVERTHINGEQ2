## Steps

1. Slay the following gnolls: (all level 11)
   - [[Lieutenant Gnar'Sok]] - by waterfall {{waypoint 980, 0, -240}}
   - [[Lieutenant Chorsun]] - by poachers {{waypoint 840, 0, -280}}
   - [[Lieutenant Scyn]] - above pond {{waypoint 910, 0, -240}}
1. Return to Lieutenant Charlin for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Protective Helm]]
  - [[Verdant Cap]]
  - [[Guardian Staff]]
  - [[Mystoak Bow]]
  - [[Spayrun]]
- +150 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
- +175 faction with **The Tunarian Alliance**
