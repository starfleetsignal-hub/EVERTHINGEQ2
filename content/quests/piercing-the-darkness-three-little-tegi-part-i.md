## Materials Required / Notes

At least:

- 100 [[Humming Azurite]]
- 25 [[Lunar Mushroom]]
- 15 [[Slow Creeping Root]]
- 35 [[Splitfin Dartfish]]
- 25 [[Coruscating Coal]]
- Recommended: [[Portable Work Bench]] or Summon Crafting Stations AA

## Steps

1. Speak to the following:
   - Red tegi, [[A tro jeg carver]] at {{waypoint -24.03, 55.74, 632.18}}
   - Light-blue tegi, [[A vas ren etcher]] at {{waypoint 138.87, 72.10, 679.91}}
   - Grey and brown tegi, [[A grol baku retainer]] at {{waypoint 12.61, 15.79, 587.99}}
1. Speak to one of the high shaman
   - Check the three locations to find one that will speak to you.
1. Craft a [[Ceremonial Amulet of the Tegi]] on a [[Work Bench]]. *Note: The [[Recipe Scroll: Ceremonial Amulet of the Tegi]] auto-scribes.*
1. Speak to the shaman that gave you the recipe again.

## Rewards

- At least 14p 21g 53s 73c 25,199 status
- At least 14p 21g 53s 73c 25,483 status at Level 125
