## Steps

1. Find and destroy the mysterious drakota in Nektulos Forest by calling them with the [[Sky lure]] when you see the message "you hear the flapping of wings."
   - The message usually appears when you are near some magilith stones(the special harvests for the druid ring quests).
   - Kill 9 [[A mysterious drakota|mysterious drakota]]. These are solo 1-down mobs that scale to your level.
     - *Note: how many drakota spawn seems to depend on how many other players you are in a group with.*

## Rewards

- 5 [[A Floret of Growth|Florets of Growth]]
