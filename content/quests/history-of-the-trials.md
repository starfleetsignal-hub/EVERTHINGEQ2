This quest is part 4 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Read the book.
1. Collect the blood of 5 [[A Nerius fiddler|nerius fiddlers]].+570.01 +2.95 -55.42 and {{waypoint 857, -1, -178}}
1. Read the book.
1. Collect the blood of [[A Nerius hermit]]. +584.76, -0.06, -332.40
1. Read the book.
1. Collect the blood of 3 [[A shoreline barracuda|shoreline barracudas]] (found in the water underneath [[Port Naythex]]).
1. Read the book.
1. Collect the blood of 3 [[A dusk dart|dusk darts]]. (found in the water underneath Port Naythex).
1. Read the book to obtain your reward. **You must be in Nek forest for this to work, you can't go to another zone and read the book.**

  - [[Girdle of the Contestant]]
  - [[Cord of the Contestant]]
