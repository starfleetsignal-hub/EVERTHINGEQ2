## Steps

1. Kill [[S'neuchi]]. {{waypoint -809, -116, 344}}
   - He's not social with the mobs around him.  He's a 72^^^ Heroic [[Underconned|undercon]] [[Defiler]], make sure you stifle/stun his spells which are poison based and do 2-2.5k depending on resists. Respawn time is 10 minutes.
   - A good spot to pull him to is {{waypoint -863, -118, 341}}.
1. Return to [[Oracle Vorzalka]] in [[Bathezid's Watch]].
