## Steps

I need to find more creatures that will drop planar ions.

- Kill 10 Verdant Wildfires in the Chamber of Chronicles. (each linked pair provided a quest update)
  - Quest informs you to find more planar ions.
- Kill 10 a fluttering repeller in the Wellspring of Renewal. (Unsure if this is an automatic update per kill but it appeared to be
- Return to the planar blossom in the Takish Archives.
  - Beware this is a H3 mob area min resolve is 9645 for trash.
