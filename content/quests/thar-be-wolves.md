## Steps

![Iofer stalks are very small. My werewolf merc is in the background for comparison.](images/Iofer_stalks_in_everfrost.jpg)

1. Kill the following in [[Bitterwind Trench]] and [[Jagged Plains]]:
   - 10 [[An ice wolf]]
   - 2 [[An alpha ice wolf]]
1. Harvest 5 iofer stalks around the [[Jagged Plains]]. They are small and extremely hard to see.
   - {{waypoint -49, 20, -389}}
   - {{waypoint -131, 23, -515}}
   - {{waypoint 71, 27, -595}}
   - {{waypoint 43, 22, -394}}
   - {{waypoint -37.27, 21.33, -426.99}}
1. Speak to [[Thala Rohquilin]].
1. Use the 5 bundles of iofer around the camp. They can be placed anywhere within the camp perimeter.
1. Return to [[Thala Rohquilin]].

- At least 65s 79c

  - [[Iced Gladiator's Armlet]]
  - [[Iced Outrider's Armlet]]
  - [[Iced Prognosticator's Armlet]] (All Mages)
