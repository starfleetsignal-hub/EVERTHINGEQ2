## Steps

1. Enter the [[Splitpaw Crypts]] via the Gnollish Terraporter near The Mirror in [[The Thundering Steppes]] {{waypoint 710, -2, -414}}
1. Walk forward to {{waypoint -82, -67, -55}}and the quest will update with the message that another treasure seeker is in the area.![Not the Only One in Here,...](images/Not_the_Only_One.jpg)
   - *Note: If there are no aggressive skeletal gnolls in the first room, zone out into [[The Thundering Steppes]] and come back into [[Splitpaw Crypts]].*
1. Search the corpse of Nekrasov the Shadow {{waypoint -1.65, -64.20, -190}}![Nekrasov the Shadow](images/Nekrasov_the_Shadow.jpg)
1. The ghost of Thieving Hardy will appear. Talk to him.
1. Kill everything in the zone to clear the webs and open up each the lower levels
1. Kill [[Rawndoc Gorepelt]] {{waypoint -50.88, -64.86, -223.72}}. ![Rawndoc Gorepelt](images/Rawndoc_Gorepelt.jpg)During the fight a number of non-agro skeletal gnolls will spawn, Rawndoc will remain immune until they are killed.![skeletal gnoll](images/Skeletal_gnoll.jpg)
   - *Note: Stay away from the Totems as they cause AoE damage.*
1. After the fight, return the ruby to the Ancient Splitpaw Totem {{waypoint -59.39, -64.79, -226.45}}![Ancient Splitpaw Totem](images/Ancient_Splitpaw_Totem.jpg)
