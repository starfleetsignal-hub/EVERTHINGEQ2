## Steps

1. Harvest [[Amulet of the Jade-Fist]] from the [[Jade-Fist Amulet Case]] in [[The City of Mist]]. {{waypoint -149, 148, -911}}
1. Speak with the [[Swifttail Trialmaster]]. {{waypoint -73, -43, 160}}
1. Enter the trial ring and defeat [[Rampage Kar]]. {{waypoint -74, -43, 156}}
1. Return to a senior Bronze shackle monk
