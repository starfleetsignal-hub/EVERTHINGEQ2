You must have at least +10,000 faction with City of Kelethin to obtain this quest. If your faction is too low, you may do the repeatable faction quests in the [[Citizenship Timeline#Becoming a Citizen of Kelethin|Citizenship series]] to raise your faction.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Queen Amree]] in the [[Fae Royal Hall]] in [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 422.20, 136.63, 261.64}}

## Rewards

- +20,000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
- +10,000 faction with **Fae of Kelethin**
- [[Welcome to Kelethin!]]
- [[Call of Kelethin]] ability
- [[Set Recall Point]] general ability

## Penalty

- Sets faction with [[City of Qeynos]] to 5,000
