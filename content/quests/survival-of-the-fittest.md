## Steps

1. Enter [[The Willow Wood: Besieged Hamlet]] from [[Elddar Grove]] in the [[Qeynos Province District]]. {{waypoint 810, -21, -563}}
1. Kill 10 gnolls ([[A Rockpaw scavenger|Rockpaw scavengers]] and [[A Rockpaw destroyer|Rockpaw destroyers]]) in Willow Wood.
   - If you're having trouble with the gnolls, getting them down to about 50% health and then leading them to a guard works - the game is fairly generous about giving you credit for NPC-assisted kills in this zone.
1. Return to [[Eireneith Alannia]].

## Rewards

- At least 5s 81c @lvl 20
