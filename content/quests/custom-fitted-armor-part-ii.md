This is part 2 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Freeport]] who wear Chain and Plate Armor (with the exception of [[Shaman|shaman]] types).

- If you are from [[Qeynos]], [[Kelethin]], or [[New Halas]], then your version of the quest is called [[Ian's Forgetful Family - Part II]].
- Note that this quest now takes place in [[The Commonlands]] rather than [[Fallen Gate]].

## Steps

1. You must collect items from creatures in [[The Commonlands]].
   - Kill 6 [[A Thexian dragoon (Commonlands)|Thexian dragoons]] {{waypoint 1389, -36, -185}}... (If you take bells to go to Commonlands, just go to the griffon tower near the dock and go to Nektulos station.. you will be near the dervishes and thexians.)
   - Kill 12 [[A Thexian excavator (Commonlands)|Thexian excavators]].
   - Kill 8 [[A dervish cutthroat (Commonlands)|dervish cutthroats]]. Any Dervish creature will give an update, not just cutthroats.
1. Return to Dyric.
