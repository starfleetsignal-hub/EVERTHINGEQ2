## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Captain Steelforge: "The grobins have been tunneling feverishly, but we are uncertain why they would want to expand the cavern network. We do know that they've locked horns with the Shroombas who live within the Shroomba Subterrane caves in Echo Echo Canyon."
- Player Character: "I saw the Shroomba not too far from here, in the valley. Are they enemies of the grobins?"
- Captain Steelforge: "The Shroomba tend to mind their own affairs, but when angered, they can be a formidable enemy. If the grobins are indeed tunneling throughout the mountains here, it's likely they are damaging the Shroomba's homes. Speak with Morganellus, outside of Echo Echo Cavern, and see if you can join forces with them."
- Player Character: "I will. Farewell, Captain."

Speak with Morganellus.

- Morganellus: "Hello, child of the heavens. I am Morganellus."
- Player Character: "Child of the heavens?"
- Morganellus: "We came from the rich earth. It is clear you did not, so you must have come from the heavens. do not worry, we can still dwell together. what is your name?"
- Player Character: "I am [Player Character]."
- Morganellus: "It is pleasing to know you. Since we share this land, we should better know each other. In Dyer Mycoria, in the caves to the South, we are taught to respect the children of the heavens, like you. You should visit my friend there."

I have spoken with Morganellus.

I have spoken with Morganellus outside the Echo Echo Canyon.
