## Steps

1. Talk to Savant Ka'im in the Academy of Arcane Science in North Freeport {{waypoint 4, 7, -125}} to receive this City Task.
   - Kill 10-15 [[A Blackscale aegis|Blackscale Aegis]] in the [[The Blackscale Sepulcher]].
   - Kill 10-14 [[A Blackscale dreadknight|Blackscale Dreadknights]] in the [[The Blackscale Sepulcher]].
   - Kill 11-15 [[A Blackscale shadowpriest|Blackscale Shadowpriests]] in the [[The Blackscale Sepulcher]].
1. Return to [[Savant Ka'im]].

## Rewards

- 9221 status
- +500 faction with **The Academy of Arcane Science**
