## Notes

- This is a repeatable quest that can be completed once per day. [[The Fractured Cache: The Return]] zone has a 1 hour 30 minutes lockout, with a three (3) day maximum duration.
- With sufficient faction there are house items, plushies and mounts to be purchased in the [[The Sinking Sands]] from the [[Nomads of Ro]] merchant [[Baniti]] {{waypoint -1232.78, -214.69, -299.69}}.
- Faction can be tracked under the 'Desert of Ro' as 'Nomads of Ro'.

## Description

The aerakyn have had some success with removing the aging magical defenses of the Fractured Cache, but they aren't the only ones who are interested in the available artifacts long buried under the sands of the Desert of Ro. They've asked me to return to the cache in search of items and to clear it of any pillaging orcs, too.

## Steps

![](images/EQ2-TheFracturedCache.png)
On the [[Orc Highway]] enter [[The Fractured Cache]] {{waypoint -501.26, -102.67, -138.60}}.
![](images/EQ2-AncientScroll.png)![](images/EQ2-RujarkianGrunt.png)
Moving through the zone

- Collect twelve (12) "Ancient Scrolls".
- Kill twelve (12) [[A rujarkian grunt|Rujarkian Grunts]].

![](images/EQ2-CruorMother.png)![](images/EQ2-OverseerGroono.png)
Kill the following to spawn [[The Ephemeral Guardian]]:

- Kill [[Cruor Mother]] {{waypoint -118.14, -71.43, -76.62}}.
- Kill [[Overseer Groono]] {{waypoint -90.57, -70.26, -201.22}}

![](images/EQ2-TheEphemeralGuardian-02.png)
Kill [[The Ephemeral Guardian]] {{waypoint 2.22, -64.28, -192.74}}
Attempt to gather the "Ancient Crystal Arca" ![Ancient Crystal Arca](images/EQ2-AncientCrystalArca.png)

## Rewards

- +3,500 faction with **Hizite Nomads of Ro**
