## Steps

1. Right-click and read the page on the table near Ishaq. ![Read the component list on this page](images/Not_Everything_has_a_Manual_quest_-_page.jpg)
1. Travel outside Paineel by the western lift.
1. Kill in any order:
   - Kill [[A spark of energy]] near {{waypoint 2074, -112, 3436}}
   - Kill [[An Underfoot hardstomper]] near {{waypoint 1933, -106, 3610}} or [[An Underfoot mudslug]] near {{waypoint 1620, -140, 3441}}
   - Kill [[A lingering Paineel guard]] near {{waypoint 1686, -110, 3422}} or [[A lingering slitherspine]] near {{waypoint 2011, -119, 3464}}
1. Return to Ishaq in the city of Paineel.

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
