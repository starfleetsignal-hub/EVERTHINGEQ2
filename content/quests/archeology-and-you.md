## Steps

1. Collect the first bucket of sand... You can only carry one at a time as they are heavy!
1. Dump the bucket at the dumpsite by clicking on the dirt pile at {{waypoint -883, -101, -766}}.
1. Collect the second bucket of sand... You can only carry one at a time as they are heavy!
1. Dump the bucket at the dumpsite.
1. Collect the third bucket of sand... You can only carry one at a time as they are heavy!
1. Dump the bucket at the dumpsite.
1. Collect the fourth bucket of sand... You can only carry one at a time as they are heavy!
1. Dump the bucket at the dumpsite.
1. Collect the fifth bucket of sand... You can only carry one at a time as they are heavy!
1. Dump the last bucket at the dumpsite.
1. Return the empty buckets to the Digmaster.
