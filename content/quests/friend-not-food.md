## Steps

1. Zone into [[Portal to the Past: Grobb]] through portal next to Zedd and speak to [[Grubthunk]] at {{waypoint -62, 13, 162}}.  He agrees to give you cat in exchange for a meal.
1. Speak to him again to get sub quest [[Liquid Lunch]]
1. Obtain Sir Fluffykin's  freedom - complete [[Liquid Lunch]]  sub quest and speak to [[Grubthunk]].
1. Return to [[Zedd]] in [[The Chronoweave]]

## Rewards

- One of the Following:
- [[Grobb Brew Barrel]]
- 4 x [[Ancient Platinum Coin]]
