## Steps

![](images/Geobot_IX_%28NPC%29.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Watchman Fleep]].
1. Complete the following in no particular order, all can be completed along the road to [[Klak'Anon]]:
   - Kill 7 [[A haywire clockwork spider|haywire clockwork spiders]]
   - Kill 7 [[A deranged clockwork gnome|deranged clockwork gnomes]]
     - Haywire clockwork spiders and deranged clockwork gnomes are on a shared spawn.  Wayward clockwork gnomes are **not** on that shared spawn.
   - Release Geobot at [[Beryl Shrimp Geyser]] next to the [[Klak'Anon]] zone line. {{waypoint -336, 93, 1353}}
1. Return to [[Plinka Fiddle]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Pulsing Gem of Fortitude]]
- At least 2g 69s 57c
