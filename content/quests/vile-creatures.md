## Description

The most vile of the known creatures of Thalumbra call the deepest parts of Kralet Penumbra their home. The citizens of Maldura ask that I brave the dungeons depths and slay its leaders.

## Steps

- Defeat targets within [[Kralet Penumbra: The Master's Chosen (Heroic)]]
1. Defeat the weapon.
   - [[Ogabo, Weapon of Yothshaval]]
1. Dispatch the chosen.
   - [[Yothshaval, Acolyte of Penumbra]]
