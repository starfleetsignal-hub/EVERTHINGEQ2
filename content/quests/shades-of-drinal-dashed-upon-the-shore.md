## Steps

The Augurs of Ulter hold the knowledge of the god's maw

1. Help [[Wegadas]] at the beach {{waypoint -41.08, -3.57, 534.65}}
   - [[Wegadas's Woven Knowledge]] - This also unlocks quests from [[Madyl D'Lareth|Madyl]]
   - [[A Jagged Branch]] (95)
   - [[Ethershade Parley]] (95)
   - [[Augur Aggression]] (95)
   - [[Druidic Cleansing]] (95)
   - [[Rooted in Growth]] (95)
1. Help [[Madyl D'Lareth]]:
   - [[Unkempt Desires]] (95)
   - [[Spiritual Guidance]] (95)
   - [[Dreary Coast Guard]] (95)
1. Return to [[Drinal's Steward]] or a Whisper Column
