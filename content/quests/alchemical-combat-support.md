## Notes

This quest is infinitely repeatable while this prelude event is active.

When accepting quest you will have 2 recipes scribed into your book - [[Elemorphic Solvent]] and [[Planar Burn Balm]], you will be asked to craft 6 of either one chosen randomly.

- You can buy Glowing Candles from [[A Flamebearer Supply Master]] nearby.
- Each combine requires 1 x Chaotic Elemental Filament + 2 of any common harvested leather pelts + 2 of any common harvested metal + 3 of any common harvested gemstones + 3 Glowing Candles. Each combine makes 1 - 2 items.
- You can use Chemistry Table behind Tavari's tent or any other Chemistry Table to craft.

## Steps

1. Check alchemist's chests behind [[Apprentice Tavari]] to  find out what exactly needs to be crafted this time.
1. Craft the required 6 items.
1. Speak with [[Apprentice Tavari]].

## Rewards

- tradeskill experience
- AA experience (only on first completion)
- at least 3=5p 4=54g
- 2 x Brass Lambent
