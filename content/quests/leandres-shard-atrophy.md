## Steps

1. Slay a number of sentient mobs. Nearby iksar across the river works good from where you received the quest. The quests does let you know you are making progress via a private tell from Leandre to yourself, so keep asking her if you are done.
1. Speak to [[Leandre]] by using the [[An incandescent wand]] or by targeting yourself and hail (press H by default)

1. No rewards.
