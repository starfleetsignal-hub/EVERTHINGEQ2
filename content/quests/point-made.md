### Steps

Craft 12 items from the random list below:

- [[Fulginate Gavel]]
- [[Fulginate Great Flail (Level 40) (Crafted)|Fulginate Great Flail]]
- [[Fulginate Double Headed Axe (Level 40) (Crafted)|Fulginate Double Headed Axe]]
- [[Fulginate Lance (Level 40) (Crafted)|Fulginate Lance]]
- [[Fulginate Voulge (Level 40) (Crafted)|Fulginate Voulge]]
- [[Fulginate Dagger (Level 40) (Crafted)|Fulginate Dagger]]
- [[Fulginate Great Sword]]
- [[Fulginate Great Spear (Level 40) (Crafted)|Fulginate Great Spear]]
- [[Fulginate War Maul]]
- [[Fulginate Claws]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

### Rewards

- At Least 81s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
