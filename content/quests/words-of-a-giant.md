## Steps

1. Loot 5 [[Giant Idol|Giant Idols]] from the corpses of giants.
   - Areas with a lot of giants include [[The Thundering Steppes]], [[Everfrost]], [[Permafrost]], and [[Solusek's Eye]].
   - Examine the first idol you get to start the language quest, [[Words of a Giant]]. Find and examine 5 more to complete the quest.  Each idol you find and study will give you a **Studied Giant Idol** in its place while you are on the quest, [[Words of a Giant]]. These studied idols sell the same as the regular ones.

## Rewards

- Experience
- The ability to understand and speak [[Krombral]], the common language of all Giants.
