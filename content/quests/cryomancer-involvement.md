## Steps

1. Head to the Temple of Brell located inside [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]].Get there by going south from Hilgan and clicking on the door at {{waypoint 809, -181, 53}}.
1. Speak with the following
   - [[Cryomancer Addicus]] {{waypoint 563, -214, 267}}
   - [[Cryomancer Dobba]] {{waypoint 528, -214, 246}}
   - [[Cryomancer Garn]] {{waypoint 474, -210, 279}}
1. Speak with [[Millety Snowblind]] at {{waypoint 539, -214, 267}}
   - *Note: Tell her Dobba and then "She said it was wonderful to have outsiders visiting Brell's Temple"*
1. Return to [[Hilgan Gravelheart]]

- At least 1p 25g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
