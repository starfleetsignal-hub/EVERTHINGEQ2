## Steps

1. Click on the wall sconce at {{waypoint -67, 270, 5}}.
1. This teleports you into a featureless circular room called the Fear Cage.
1. Click on the floating fear orb above {{waypoint -191, 266, -5}}.
1. Kill the named spectre, [[De'manti Sheda]].
1. Click on the fear orb again to zone the group out.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g
- 4 [[Flames of Scorn]]
