## Notes

You can only complete one training session every 18 hours and must complete all five to finish [[A Frozen Egg]]

## Steps

1. Hunt 12 [[A brontotherium cow]] located at {{waypoint -406.22, -358.46, -344.42}}.
   - To get there go through the Tizmak caves located at {{waypoint -284.91, -335.12, -81.71}} to Blisterock Ridge. Tizmak are non-agro and wolf mobs in area don't see thru invis. (And unlike the [[Shardwurm]], baby calves do not count as an update).
   - [[A brontotherium bull]] also gives credit. The bulls can be found all along the upper ridge between Dain's Last Stand and Ring War Battlefield.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c

One of the following if this is your last training day after you hail your griffawn for the last time and tell it you like it, too:

- [[Snow Gryphon]]
- [[Tundra Gryphon]]
- [[Highland Gryphon]]
