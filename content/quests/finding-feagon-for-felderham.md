## Steps

1. Find Hamish Felderham's assistant, [[Feagon Jornstoff]].  He's in a side-tunnel of the [[Mortartoe Mines]] at {{waypoint -832, 106, -263}}
1. Defeat the two [[A summoned construct|summoned constructs]]
1. Pick up [[Feagon's Journal]] where he dropped it
1. Read the book from your inventory
1. Return to Hamish and share this new information with him.

## Rewards

- At least 13s 91c
