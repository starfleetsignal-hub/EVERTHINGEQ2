Quest is repeatable once every 18 hours.

## Description

Upon inspecting the bust of Rallos Zek within the temple, I heard the voice of Rallos Zek and felt strongly compelled to carry out its will. A great warrior should make the Kromzek kneel before Rallos Zek.

## Steps

1. I must slay 15 Kromzek priests within the Temple of Rallos Zek (Zone [[Eye of Zek]] within [[Kael Drakkel]]).

## Rewards

- At least 10p
- +50 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
- +50 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
- [[Primal Velium Shard]] x1
