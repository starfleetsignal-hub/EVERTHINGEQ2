## Description

"Traeger Verdegreen is going into the merchant supply business. Right now, he's learning how to make ropes and needs a variety of greenery from Greater Faydark to begin the process. I've agreed to help him out." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Harvest the following (in any order):
   - Harvest some bogberry plants near the ponds beneath Kelethin.
     - Bogberry Plant can be harvested at or near {{waypoint -71.67, -11.08, 72.63}}
   - Harvest some type of flax plants near the large roots of the trees that support [[Kelethin]].
     - Faydark Flax can be found near large roots of the [[Kelethin]] trees {{waypoint 95.18, 64.61, 270.72}} and {{waypoint 204.09, 61.88, 218.47}}
   - Harvest some form of sisal found near the rocky areas in [[Greater Faydark]].
     - Silvani Sisal can be harvested at or near {{waypoint 196.51, 62.97, 279.60}} and {{waypoint 143.05, 23.79, 433.54}} , {{waypoint -21.44, 61.82, 253.07}}in the [[Granite Hills]].
1. Return to [[Traeger Verdegreen]].

## Rewards

- At least 1s 16c
- And one of the following:
  - [[Kelethin Chain Boots (Version 2)|Kelethin Chain Boots]]
  - [[Kelethin Leather Sandals (Version 2)|Kelethin Leather Sandals]]
  - [[Kelethin Linen Sandals (Version 2)|Kelethin Linen Sandals]]
  - [[Kelethin Plate Boots (Version 2)|Kelethin Plate Boots]]

## Note

None of the boots are usable by Bruisers, Shadowknights, Coercers, Necromancers, Inquisitors, Defilers, Assassins, Brigands (i.e. all the classes that cannot start out as Good-Aligned) or Beastlords (probably an oversight).

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Traeger Verdegreen: "Yes? Can I help you?"
- Player Character: "I'm wondering if you're hiring anyone to do odd jobs?"
- Traeger Verdegreen: "As a matter of fact, I am! I'm starting an important business and we're getting some of our supplies together. One thing that I need immediately and I don't have is rope. I know how to make ropes, I just need some raw materials. Are you interested in doing some leg work for me?"
- Player Character: "Sure, why not?"
- Traeger Verdegreen: "That's great! Greater Faydark has an abundance of different plants that I can use to make sturdy, long-lasting ropes. I like to take a blend of three different types of plant fibers and combine them. Are you familiar with the plants around here?"
- Player Character: "No, but I'm willing to learn."
- Traeger Verdegreen: "One of them is a bogberry plant. The young branches are very flexible and strong. It grows near sources of water, like ponds and streams. We pound out the fibers in these branches and then use them to add a bit of stiffness to the final product."
- Player Character: "All right."
- Traeger Verdegreen: "The second type is a relative of flax and is very easy to work with. In Greater Faydark, you can find these plants growing near some of the very large roots from the trees that support Kelethin. The third type is a form of sisal. It prefers a slightly drier climate, so you'll want to concentrate your search near rocky areas such as Granite Hills."
- Player Character: "Got it. I'll be back with the plants."

In order to help Traeger in his business, I need to locate three different types of plants used in making ropes. The bogberry plant grows in or around shallow pools of water. I might find some near the ponds beneath Kelethin. I can find a type of flax plant in Greater Faydark near some of the very large roots of the trees that support Kelethin. I'll find a form of sisal near rocky areas in Greater Faydark, such as Granite Hills.

I've located the bogberry plant. I've located the Faydark flax leaves. I've located the Silvani sisal leaves. I've learned how to recognize some of the plants used in making ropes. I need to return to Traeger Verdegreen with these plants. Traeger Verdegreen, near the Old Kelethin Acorn Lift, will be waiting for me to return with these plants.

- Traeger Verdegreen: "Have you gotten the plant materials?"
- Player Character: "Yes, here they are."
- Traeger Verdegreen: "Good job! This is enough for me to get started. Mainly what I'll need to do is make enough rope to tie together the boxes that I'll be sending out."
- Player Character: "Where are you sending things?"
- Traeger Verdegreen: "Quite a distance away, actually. There are new markets opening up overseas now that trade routes are re-established. I've been stocking up on the local handicrafts and hope that they'll earn quite a profit."

I've spoken with Traeger Verdegreen and given him the plants. I gave the items to Traeger Verdegreen.

I've learned to recognize several types of plants and managed to gather enough of them to give Traeger rope-making materials for years to come.
