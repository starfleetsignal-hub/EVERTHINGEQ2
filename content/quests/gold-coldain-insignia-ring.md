## Steps

1. Collect 2 high quality cougar pelts and 2 high quality kodiak pelts. These are uncommon chest drops from [[A snowmane sabertooth|snowmane sabertooths]] or [[A ravaging snowmane sabertooth|ravaging snowmane sabertooths]] {{waypoint -352, -373, 3005}} and [[A sootpaw direbear]] {{waypoint -434, -374, 3183}} or frostpaw direbears respectively in [[Eastern Wastes]], found around Dragon's Head Drifts. The high quality pelts needed are in chest drops.
1. Scribe the [[Coldain Hunting Blanket recipe]] (artisan lvl 2 required), get 1 [[Glowing filament]] and craft the blanket on a sewing table. The blanket recipe is level 15; if you fail, you lose the pelts and must head back to Eastern Wastes and hunt until you get the pelts again.
1. Lay the blanket on the ice-encased corpse at {{waypoint 271, -335, -447}} (*where you found the original copper ring*) on the ring war battlefield.
1. Return to Malidain back at the rivers end.
