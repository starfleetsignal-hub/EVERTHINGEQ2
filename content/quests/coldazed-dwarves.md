## Steps

- You receive a [[Bonechill Wand (Quest Item)|Bonechill Wand]].
1. Use the Bonechill Wand on 8 coldain
   - Attack any attackable coldain.  Once they drop below 20% health, use the wand.
1. Return to Bargbae
1. Speak with [[Grand Restorer Lingwall]] in the Mess Hall in [[Hold of Rime: The Fortress Spire]] Use the door at {{waypoint -48, -322, 424}} in the Oubliette of the Rime.  (Note:  this is a special instance, there will be two listed on the door, both called Hold of Rime: The Fortress Spire - its the second one, which is an empty instance except for Lingwall).
   - You will need to reapply your Rime illusion after entering

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +800 faction with **The Order of Rime**
