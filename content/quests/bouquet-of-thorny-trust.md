## Steps

![](images/Fresh-cut_rose_ground.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Priest Aaronolis Swornlove]] nearby.
1. Collect 8 [[Fresh-cut rose|fresh-cut roses]] in the [[Enchanted Lands]] and Bestow the roses on any other player in the game.
   - *The roses are a random ground spawn. Each harvest yields two [[Fresh-cut rose|fresh-cut roses]].*
   - *To bestow the roses to other players "use" the roses from your inventory, trading them to the other player doesn't update the quest*
1. Return to [[Priest Aaronolis Swornlove]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- 2 [[Erollisi coin|Erollisi coins]]
