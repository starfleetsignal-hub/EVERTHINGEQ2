## Note

You will only be offered this quest **ONCE**.  Milupet will not speak to you in subsequent years. However, in later years, you **CAN** still speak directly with [[Thumore D'armer]] in [[A Collection of Dreams]] and receive [[Shattered Remains]].

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Drakin Hammerheart]] in North Qeynos {{waypoint 398, -20, -189}}.
1. Speak with [[Dalhya Felshire]] in North Qeynos {{waypoint 412, -20, -189}}.
1. Click the sleeping body next to [[Dalhya Felshire]] then choose to fall asleep to enter the dream state zone, [[A Collection of Dreams]].
1. Speak with [[Thumore D'armer]], who is directly west from where you zone in.

## Rewards

- Experience Only

Be aware that this quest may only start mobs at level 10, so doing it earlier (such as level 8), you will find for example the first mobs (mummies) are level 10.
