## Steps

A talisman was taken from Jonovan and his father by a Roothorror.

1. Kill any rumbleroot in Nektulos until you get the uncommon quest update.  Low-level rumbleroots are found in the hills east of Jonovan in [[Death's Maw]] {{waypoint -611, 37, 71}}.
1. Return to Jonovan.

## Rewards

- At least 4s 4c
