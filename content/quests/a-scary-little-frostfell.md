## Steps

1. Harvest *presents* from around [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]] until you get the following items:
   - [[Bitter Almond Candy Canes]]
   - [[Exhumed Bones]]
   - [[Feerrott Fruitcake]]
1. Deliver to completed gift to one of three randomly selected NPCs:
   - [[Lephore Rhysling]] in [[Loping Plains]]. {{waypoint -401, 17, 39}}
   - [[Aglod]] in [[Frostfang Sea]]. {{waypoint 96, 58, 163}}
   - [[Zokk Gorebite]] in [[The Feerrott]]. {{waypoint -146, 13, 181}}
