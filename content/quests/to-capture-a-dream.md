## Steps

1. Talk to [[Ruffin]] on the [[Snowfang Isle]] {{waypoint -917, -522, -2069}} in the Icy Fingers, [[Great Divide]] and receive [[Snowfang Dreamcatcher Recipe]].
   - *Note: For crafters without a high adventure level [[Ruffin]] can be reached via a non aggro path (watch out for [[An Iceclad sleeper shark|Iceclad sleeper sharks]]) by swimming under the ice, or by swimming around the ice on the right side, and climbing a ladder at {{waypoint -910.54, -543.73, -2191.91}}. Another possibility is to take the gryphon at Icy Fingers to Thurgadin and jump off and plummet to your death and revive at Snowfang isle.*
1. Return to [[Capru]].

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
