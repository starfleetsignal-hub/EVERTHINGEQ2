Since the deino dropped me at an encampment, I am going to ask there regarding Mairen. As is par for the course, I need to help them out before they will help me.

## Materials Required

- 125 [[Amsonia Roots]]
- 5 [[Greytusk Hide]]
- 100 [[Star Diopside]]
- 5 [[Astral Incense]]
- 5 [[Astral Kindling]]

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Assistant Virdell]]
   - Harvest 25 [[Amsonia Roots]]
   - Harvest 5 [[Greytusk Hide]]
   - Harvest 20 [[Star Diopside]]
   - Receive [[Recipe Scroll: Ghoul Goodies]]
1. Craft 5 [[Scroll of Enhanced Undead Control]]
   - *There is a [[Far Seas Crafting Station]] next to  [[Assistant Virdell]]* {{waypoint 395, 105, 460}}*.*
1. Speak with [[Assistant Virdell]]
1. Speak with [[Assistant Virdell]] again.
   1. Gather the **Ghoul Gruel Ingredients** from the bucket.![Bucket of Ghoul Gruel Ingredients](images/Were_is_the_Messenger_Mad_Machinations_quest_-_Ghoul_Gruel_Ingredients.jpg)
   1. Craft 5 [[Ghoul Gruel]]
1. Speak with [[Assistant Virdell]] about the contact in [[Forlorn Gist]]

## Rewards

- +5,000 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Zombie Peasant Plushie]]
- At least 5p 21g 72s 4c
- 53591 status at tradeskill level 124
