## Steps

1. Find a cleric of the Great 'something' - this is Chaplain GraveGuzzle in the first set of towers near Sir Tatters. {{waypoint 285, 6, -244}}
1. Kill void shrillers to fill a bottle.
1. Find someone that collects 'evil' weapons. The artifact Rikantus is in Antonica near the Steppes Station. Click the chest behind Rikantus {{waypoint -1970, 9, 818}}.
