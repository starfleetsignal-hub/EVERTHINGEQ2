## Description

Beasts of war have been loosened within the Palace of Unity. Stop them, and whoever's responsible for this travesty!

## Zone

Defeat targets within [[Maldura: Palace Foray (Event Heroic)]].

## Steps

- Slay the war wolf
  - [[Dreadmaw (Event Heroic)|Dreadmaw]]
- Kill the war boar
  - [[Dreadtusk (Event Heroic)|Dreadtusk]]
- Down the dragoon
  - [[Dragoon Z'Koz]]
