## Starting the Quest

[[GM Event|GM Events]] can start in any city or zone in the game.  Although this quest has occurred in [[Antonica]].

## Steps

***I met a handsome prince who is trying to rescue a princess, who was turned by an evil enchanter into a scarecrow. The prince had learned a riddle that might hold a formula for a potion to break the spell.***

***I must find the ingredients that the prince needs.***

1. Find "Citrus sweet from bush be took"
   - Collect 5 murdunk oranges
1. Find "Several roots from the ground not brook"
   - Collect 5 tuber strands
1. Find "Saliva from a howling crook"
   - Kill gnolls until you get an auto-update for canine saliva
1. Return to the guide with all of the ingredients.

## Rewards

***I helped the prince rescue the princess... though I'm not sure he'll thank me.***

- [[Oggokian beauty cream]]
