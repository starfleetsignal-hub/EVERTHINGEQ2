This quest may be done in conjuction with [[Renewal of Ro: Tailing Dragons]].

## Steps

1. Enter [[Takish Badlands: Overgrowth (Solo)]] in [[Firiona's Motte]] {{waypoint 742.43, 58.08, 42.50}}
   1. Collect 10 **jars of viable seeds**. ![Jar of Seeds](images/Jar_of_Seeds.png)
      - These are not guaranteed updates. See talk page for locations.
      - These may also be picked up in a cleared zone.
   1. Defeat 12 [[An overgrowth entangler|overgrowth entanglers]].
      - possible jar locations: {{waypoint 647, 100, 73}} {{waypoint 551, 87, 110}} )
   1. Use the branch at {{waypoint 541, 87, 71}} ), kill more overgrowth entanglers.
   1. Kill the named: [[Florafang]].  Check for the shiny left behind.
      - possible jar location:  {{waypoint 644, 57, 18}}
   1. Use the branch at {{waypoint 644, 48, -28}} to get to the overgrowth leafcutters.
      - possible jar locations: {{waypoint 689, 48, -178}}  {{waypoint 711, 52, -274}}
   1. Kill the named: [[The Gobbler]] {{waypoint 762, 60, -29}}.  Check for the shiny left behind.
      - possible jar location: {{waypoint 575, 44, -310}}  {{waypoint 582, 46, -347}}
   1. Use the branch at {{waypoint 549, 42, -309}}
      - possible jar locations: {{waypoint 400, 51, -379}}   {{waypoint 366, 44, -321}}  {{waypoint 435, 64, -502}}
   1. Kill the named: [[Cleavespine]] {{waypoint 492, 112, -599}}. Check for the shiny left behind.
      - possible jar locations:  {{waypoint 331, 80, -480}}  {{waypoint 182, 101, -543}}  {{waypoint 116, 105, -557}}  {{waypoint 85, 107, -497}}  {{waypoint 48, 107, -477}}  {{waypoint 217, 103, -363}}
   1. Kill the named:  [[Tawerek]] {{waypoint 190, 89, -452}}  Check for the shiny left behind.
   1. Kill the last named: [[Leafjaw]] {{waypoint 140, 85, -403}}  Check for the shiny left behind.
   1. Use the port at the end of the swamp to leave the area.
   1. Return to [[Zaari]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- [[Takish'Hiz Jar]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Ring of the Silver Oasis]]
  - [[Ring of the Great Forest]]
