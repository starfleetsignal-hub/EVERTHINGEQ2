## Steps

1. Go to [[The Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen]]
1. ![speared remains](images/Speared_remains.jpg)Collect the three symbols by clicking the speared remains. Has to be done in order (this can be done on a previously cleared instance):
   1. The first skeleton is located at {{waypoint 172, -62, -247}}, in the same room as [[Rideepa the Prideful]] (Second Named).
   1. The second one is located at {{waypoint -44, -56, -253}} in a little dead end tunnel by [[Froppit the Everliving]] (Third Named).
   1. The last skeleton is located at {{waypoint 156, -65, -250}} in the Halls of Usurpation next to where [[A Ghoul Usurper]] spawns (Fifth Named).
1. Return to [[Evana L'Alval]]
1. Head down hall and click on door. Talk to Misa Ravensoul and accept quest.

## Rewards

- [[Title: "the Shadow Blade"]]
- At least 1p 6g 7s
- +10,000 faction with **The Circle of the Unseen Hand**
