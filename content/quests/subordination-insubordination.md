## Steps

1. Enter [[The Palace of Roehn Theer]] and kill some mobs.
   - Kill [[Lieutenant Buldoral]].
   - Kill [[Azara the Seer]].
   - Kill [[Kendis Parmare]].
   - Kill [[Penda Parmare]].
   - Kill [[Iilsaad's Barrier]].
   - Kill [[Maalus Shadowfyre]].
   - Kill [[Mandoril]]/[[Medorius]]/[[Mikla]].
1. Kill [[Roehn Theer]].
