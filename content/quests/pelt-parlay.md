## Prerequisites

You will need to speak Gnollish to complete this quest (and others for the Snowfangs). You can learn the Gnollish language by completing the quest [[Bark Like a Gnoll]].

## Steps

1. Scribe the recipe: [[Mira's Succulent Crab Casserole Recipe]] that Mira gave you to create [[Succulent Crab Casserole]]
1. Make the Casserole.
   - You'll need the [[Crab meat (Level 70) (Version 1)|crab meat]] Mira gave you, 1 [[Aerated mineral water]] and 1 [[Ethereal kindling]] (both available at [[Matthew]] {{waypoint -1637, -533, -2378}}.
   - Craft at the cooking fire - Located next to [[Mira]] {{waypoint -1354, -533, -2155}}
1. Find a gnoll to speak to about the pelts.  Speak to [[Ruffin]], the Snowfang Provisioner, at  {{waypoint -915, -522, -2069}}
   - Beware the aggro mobs between the Othmir and the Snowfangs.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
