## Steps

1. ![Allu'thoa appearance illusion](images/Allu'thoa_appearance.PNG)Speak to Corrith.  He gives you a [[Jar of Pickling Brine]] and a buff that gives you an Allu'thoa appearance illusion. (See picture)
   - If the buff wears off, speak to Corrith and ask him to recast it.
   - This buff removes your ability to fly so it's best to use your run speed buffs/gear/totems.
1. Find the Allu'thoa ceremony {{waypoint -1101, 64, 718}}
1. Speak to the [[A Thoa Diviner|Thoa Diviner]] to begin the ceremony
1. Throw the brine to disrupt the ceremony - From your inventory (The screen will go white for a few seconds) Expect MAJOR Damage ! !
1. Slay the Allu'thoa in the ceremony
   - Slay 6 [[A frenzied Clotl'thoa zealot|frenzied Clotl'thoa zealots]]
   - Slay the Thoa Diviner
1. Return to Corrith
