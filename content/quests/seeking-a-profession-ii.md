"I need to cook some fried frog leg.  This can be done at a stove and keg using a frog leg and dough."

## Steps

1. Create fried frog leg
   - Using Alchemy skills at a stove and keg, combine the following ingredients:
     - 2 Raw Frog Leg
     - 1 Dough
     - 1 walnut kindling
