This quest is from Part 2 of the Beastlord Prelude for good aligned characters and will only be available for a short time during Game Update 61.

## Steps

1. Enter the Harbinger portal near the Claymore in Antonica at {{waypoint -418, -6, 633}}
1. Speak with [[Elder Animist Sahdi]]
1. Sahdi requests that you speak to the other Harbingers.  Each one will assign you a subquest, and each subquest will reward you a medallion.
   - [[Bordag]] - [[Bordag's Trial]] (kill the Scar Bear, instance near the two towers in Antonica)
   - [[Gardunk]] - [[Gardunk's Trial]] (find three codex pieces in the immediate area, giant black stones)
   - [[Field Priest Keven]] - [[Keven's Trial]] (enter instance near Archer Woods, kill deer, lead pups to deer)
   - [[Animist Poren]] - [[Poren's Trial]] (enter instance near lighthouse docks via boat, kill aqua goblins)
   - [[Elder Patriarch Vuzx]] - [[Vuzx's Trial]] (zone to EL via dock, collect glittering bushes (not green smoking))
1. Once you completed all 5 subquests and received the quest rewards, talk to Sahdi again.
1. Return to Meli.
