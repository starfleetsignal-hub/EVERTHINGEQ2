## Notes

Takes place inside [[Sodden Archipelago: Blackhook Badlands (Solo)|Sodden Archipelago: Blackhook Badlands [Solo]]] instance and can be done at the same time as [[Scars of Destruction: Into Blackhook Badlands]]

## Steps

1. Enter [[Sodden Archipelago: Blackhook Badlands (Solo)|Sodden Archipelago: Blackhook Badlands [Solo]]] via Portal at {{waypoint 116, 136, 85}}
1. Investigate first location at eastern edge of  Blackhook Territory:
   - Travel through the zone
     - Kill Antalflik to open the gate (The named big red ant)
     - Kill Ultarana (stuns) to pass him to {{waypoint -54, 190, -513}}
       - You will find two barrels of Blockhook Explosives at  {{waypoint -52, 190, -516}}. Click on the explosives to investigate. The quest will update.
       - Click on the explosives again to light them. The quest will update again.
       - Note: these are not the black barrels lining the path. they are brown barrels with bright red markings on them on the right, after the named is killed.
1. Investigate the second location at eastern edge of Blackhook Territory:
   - Travel further in, kill Kahblamo.
   - You will find a *mounted crossbow* "A Blackhook Weapon" at {{waypoint -77, 193, -614}} Click on it to investigate.  The quest will update.
   - Click the weapon again to destroy it. The quest will update again.
1. Find and destroy 2 more explosive stockpiles and 1 more weapon:
   - The second Explosive Stockpile is at {{waypoint 66, 191, -577}}.
   - Find the Third Explosive Stockpile at {{waypoint 356, 251, -605}}
     - *The third Explosive Stockpile is located past Grelzmok through the gate he guards.*
   - The second Blackhook Weapon is at {{waypoint 300, 202, -409}}.
     - Kill [[Grelzmok the Destroyer]]
1. Return to [[Baasik Vehn'imiss]] in [[Sodden Archipelago]]
