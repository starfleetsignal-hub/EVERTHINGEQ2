## Description

*After a confrontation with one of the Rallosian skeletons I recovered the remnants of a Rallosian sword. I believe with a little bit of effort I can find components needed to rebuild the weapon right here in the Feerrott.*

## Steps

1. Kill Rallosian Undead until you find a replacement handle and get an auto-update.
1. Kill Rallosian Undead until you find a replacement pommel and get an auto-update
1. Kill Rallosian Undead until you find two replacement leather straps.
   - This quest auto-completes after finding the second leather strap.
   - On 9/18/2025 these updates can be gotten from the level 20ish zombies near Antonica zone in Thundering Steppes.
