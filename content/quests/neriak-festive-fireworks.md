## Notes

- It's suggested that you turn off Flora in Options -> Display, as it makes it much easier to see the fireworks launchers.
- The [[Festive Fireworks]] quests can be found in [[Gorowyn]], [[Kelethin]], [[Neriak, City of Hate]], [[New Halas]], [[Freeport]] and the [[Qeynos Capitol District]].
- The fireworks you must light look like bottles that you can interact with by clicking on them. They are found all over the city, close to the NPCs for the event. Players with good resolution should find them easy to spot.

## Steps

![An example of a Scorched Sky celebration fireworks launcher](images/Celebration_fireworks.png)
Before you begin this quest you **must** buy a [[Festive Sparkler]] from any [[A Sparkler Merchant]] **or** [[Ember Merchant Assistant]]  (only 10 copper) or the NPC won't offer the quest.

1. The local fireworks coordinator noticed that I have a festive sparkler, and asked if I would like to help set off some of the fireworks in the city.
1. Light celebration fireworks in the area around the celebrants (0/10).

The quest will autocomplete when you light the last one and it is repeatable.

## Rewards

- 3 x [[Platinum Jubilation Medals]]
- A choice of one of the following:
  - [[Neriak Fireworks Launcher]]
  - 1 [[Coin of Ember]]
