## Note

You can save some time by simultaneously doing the [[Weeding the Garden]] and [[Agent of Growth]] quests from [[Wuoshi]] which require the same enemy kills as this quest.

## Steps

- Dispose of 6 [[A root tender|root tenders]].
- Weed 12 [[A tunaric mantrap|tunaric mantraps]].
- Return to Marech

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
