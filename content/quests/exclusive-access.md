## Steps

1. Enter [[Charasis: Vault of Eternal Sleep]] and defeat the guardians of the locks:
   - [[The Keeper of Dreams]] {{waypoint -34, -10, -173}}
   - [[The Keeper of Nightmares]] {{waypoint 147, -7, -68}}
1. Defeat the [[Guardian of Eternity]] {{waypoint 63, -5, -267}}
1. Enter the [[Altar of the Dragonslayer]]. {{waypoint 28, -3, -138}}
   - This location is accessed by placing the blue *Crystal of Dreams* from [[The Twin Pillars]] on the [[Lock of Dreams]] and the purple *Crystal of Nightmares* on the [[Lock of Nightmare]].
1. Return to [[Quiyn]].

## Rewards

- At least 31g 73s 78c
