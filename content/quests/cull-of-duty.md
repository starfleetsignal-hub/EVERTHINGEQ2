## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

![an umbrith](images/An_umbrith.png)
![a gloomstrider](images/A_gloomstrider.png)

## Steps

The flora and fauna I am targeting can be found in Flowfall Gully, Carved Caps and The Overflow of the Unknown.
![uuzgeth grass](images/Uuzgeth_grass.png)

1. You are given 2 tasks to complete from list below (1 culling and 1 gathering):
   - Kill one of the following
     - Cull 8 x umbrith (flying snakes) around {{waypoint -1101, 25, 1008}}
     - Cull 8 x gloomstriders around {{waypoint -1063, 29, 1073}}
   - Harvest or Gather
     - Harvest 10 x uuzgeth grass (red clumps of tall grass) around {{waypoint -1153, 20, 953}}
     - Gather 10 x gloomstrider eggs
