## Steps

1. Collect some Fire Tears from the [[Sableflame Crater]] {{waypoint 76.79, -65.48, 78.40}}.
1. Harvest Fiery Ore around the crater to get Fire Tears.
1. Return to Belarshalee.

## Rewards

- At least 92c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Archon's Nebulous Boots]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Nebulous Slippers]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Nebulous Boots]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Elder's Nebulous Boots]] (Shamans)
  - [[Enlightened Nebulous Sandals]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Nebulous Boots]] (Warriors & Crusaders)
  - [[Shade's Nebulous Boots]] (All Scouts)
