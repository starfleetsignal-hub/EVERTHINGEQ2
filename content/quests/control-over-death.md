## Steps

![Pile of bones ready to be reanimated](images/Control_Over_Death_quest_-_bonepile.jpg)

1. [[Apprentice Aulerun]] has asked that you venture into [[Toxxulia Forest]] and reanimate any bonepiles you find as practice. Make sure to slay anything you summon!
1. Slay what you summon.
   - You must find, reanimate and kill 10 [[A reanimated Toxxulia cat|reanimated Toxxulia cat]]s using Necromatic Resurrection (should be a spell scroll titled "[[Ritual of Necromantic Arts]]" in your bag). Very fast spawn time on bonepiles AND cats (about 60 seconds).
     - Known locations
     - {{waypoint 1250, -48, 3038}}
     - {{waypoint 1204, -48, 3084}}
     - {{waypoint 1171, -49, 3051}}
     - {{waypoint 1129, -52, 3163}}
     - {{waypoint 1143, -52, 3216}}
     - {{waypoint 1203, -50, 3195}}
     - {{waypoint 1286, -44, 3116}}
     - {{waypoint 1360, -36, 3089}}
     - {{waypoint 993, -51, 3082}}
     - {{waypoint 1044, -52, 3162}}
1. Return to [[Apprentice Aulerun]]
