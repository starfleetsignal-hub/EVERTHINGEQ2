## Notes

- It is advised that the last [[A konikor trumpeteer|konikor trumpeteer]] you kill be the one standing over the Horn of Kab {{waypoint 8, 4, -520}}, as the hidden countdown will begin when the last trumpeteer is slain.
- If you are having a hard time scaling the trumpets, run up the innermost of the three ribs flanking the sides of each trumpet and spam jump as you near the top and you should get on the upper platform quickly.
- If you fail the gauntlet, you will have to wait 20-30 minutes for [[The Malefic Formation]] to respawn. When it is spawnable, a message will flash on your screen and chatbox saying, "A strong mystical energy is collecting within the Wind Chamber".
- **The same person must blow all six trumpets**.

## Steps

1. Summon and slay [[Ra'Tsinis]] in [[Chardok]].
   1. Enter [[The Wind Chamber (Chardok)|The Wind Chamber]] {{waypoint 39, -23, -456}}
   1. Kill [[The Malefic Formation]] that spawns after the horns are automatically sounded.
   1. Kill the six [[A konikor trumpeteer|konikor trumpeteers]] that spawn at the mouthpieces of the horns.
   1. Within ~5 minutes, blow the horns (by clicking the mouthpiece) in the following order:
      - Kab: {{waypoint 8, 4, -520}}
      - Vul: {{waypoint 61, 4, -395}}
      - Uzu: {{waypoint 7, 4, -395}}
      - Myr: {{waypoint -30, 4, -481}}
      - Zet: {{waypoint 91, 4, -432}}
      - Roehn: {{waypoint -30, 4, -431}}
   1. Kill [[Ra'Tsinis]] that spawns in the center of the chamber.
1. Return to [[Warlord Gadar Gwalnax]] in [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]].

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Gwalnax Brigade Insignia]]
  - [[Sphere of Venom Shields]]
