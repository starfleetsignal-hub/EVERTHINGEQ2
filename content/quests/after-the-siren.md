## Steps

1. Hunt  crystalline predators for 5 living ice crystals - white crystals  that you have to kick to make [[A crystalline predator]] spawn around [[Chillblain Fields]] {{waypoint 18.05, 54.60, 61.22}} on and below ice shelf. Not a guaranteed update from each kill.
1. Return to [[Zorie]] at Hoarfang Ridge
1. Place wards around camp - on blue sparkly spots around perimeter of newly created campsite
   - Place East molkar repulsion ward {{waypoint -499, 123, -332}}
   - Place South molkar repulsion ward {{waypoint -489, 123, -314}}
   - Place West molkar repulsion ward {{waypoint -452, 125, -314}}
   - Place Northwest molkar repulsion ward {{waypoint -446, 124, -355}}
   - Place North molkar repulsion ward {{waypoint -478, 122, -349}}
1. Return to Zorie

## Reward

- [[Molkar Repulsion Ward]] house item
- 50 x [[Scarred Dinar]]
- 5 x [[Velium Drachma]]
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
