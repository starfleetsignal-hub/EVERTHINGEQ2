## Steps

1. Defeat [[Galintari the Apocryphal]] in [[The Firemyst Gully]]
1. Return to the Chronomagi Tasker

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Chronomancers**
- The option to purchase [[Replica: Galintari the Apocryphal]] for 1p 8g from the [[Timeless Trader]]

## Notes

- The maximum level you can chrono-mentor to and still be able to complete this quest is 25.
