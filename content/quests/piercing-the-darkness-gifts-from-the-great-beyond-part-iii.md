## Materials Required / Notes

- 72 [[Splitfin Dartfish]]
- 96 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
- 72 [[Humming Azurite]]
- 24 [[Lunar Mushroom]]
- 18 [[Rockhopper Meat]]
- 75 [[Coruscating Candle]]
- 70 [[Coruscating Coal]]
- Suggested: [[Portable Work Bench]], [[Portable Chemistry Set]] or summon crafting stations AA.

## Steps

1. Use an [[Advanced Lightning Tracker Mach II]] to track the location of and then gather 3 [[Seeped Shadows]]![](images/Seeping_shadows.jpg)
   - Potential locations for [[Seeped Shadows]] include:
     - {{waypoint 161.32, 72.84, 140.46}}  This ribbon will constantly lead you to "Grumpy Jack" inside the cabin. the seeping shadow is NOT behind this cabin. Turn back and go up the slight hill at {{waypoint 125.89, 57.99, 130.09}}  then follow the ribbon to the seeping shadow behind the tree.
     - {{waypoint 155.47, 65.37, 25.21}}  Follow the river to {{waypoint 144.14, 28.49, 269.02}}  then follow the path to {{waypoint 113.26, 57.70, 109.19}}   Go between the two cabins then look behind the one on the left.
     - {{waypoint 143.13, 61.80, -299.14}}  This is at the base of a tree just off of the side of the river. Follow the river to {{waypoint 146.21, 58.52, -281.15}};   then look north for the tree in front of you.
     - {{waypoint 177.13, 39.13, 330.65}};  This is in a crevice just off of the river. Follow the river to {{waypoint 139.67, 26.28, 288.58}}; and look SW to where the two hills meet.
     - {{waypoint 261.12, 89.42, 0.72}}; If you can't fly - Follow the river to {{waypoint 376.87, 46.87, -196.03}} go up and around to {{waypoint 315.29, 89.23, -109.85}}, cross the bridge at {{waypoint 311.65, 97.22, 19.11}} then go behind the tree (you can't get to it from the rocks in front of the tree) and look down, the seeping shadows should be on the ledge.
     - {{waypoint 279.03, 89.72, -133.13}} If you can't fly - Follow the river to {{waypoint 376.87, 46.87, -196.03}} go up and around to {{waypoint 315.29, 89.23, -109.85}}, cross the bridge at {{waypoint 311.65, 97.22, 19.11}} then go left/North.
     - {{waypoint -218, 10, -452}}; The "X" for this one shows up on top of the purplish/pinkish rocks. To get to it, jump into the Umbral Sea and swim to around {{waypoint -412.34, -0.19, -516.54}}; then head West - southwest.
   - *Note: You will need to craft two more [[Advanced Lightning Tracker Mach II]] to locate each [[Seeped Shadows]], each takes an [[Advanced Lightning Tracker Mach I]]. Other players can harvest your tracked Seeping Shadow so craft extras. See Discussion for tips on obtaining [[Seeped Shadows]].*
   - *NOTE: Cast one Advanced Lightning Tracker Mach II at a time. Once you cast one, a little bug will fly off, and you will be able to see where it went on your map. After collecting one, change your location like the other side of the river or up another hill. No need to zone out and back in again.*
   - *Note: You don't "lose" a tracker, when the tracker doesn't find any nearby.*
1. Craft and use 3 [[Anti-Illusionary Libation|Anti-Illusionary Libations]] using the [[Recipe Scroll: Anti-Illusionary Libation]] received earlier at a Chemistry Table.
   1. Perform the following: Head back to [[Sanctus Seru (City)|Sanctus Seru [City]]]
   1. Enter [[Sanctus Seru: Fabrication Hall]] to purchase 3 [[Empty Libation Container|Empty Libation Containers]] from [[Fen Fen]] at {{waypoint 111.10, -510.72, 25}}
      - (You may also buy Recipe Books 111 through 120. **NOTE: Shadow Prospecting is a No-Trade REWARD for this quest, so avoid the temptation to buy it from Fen Fen**.)
   1. Craft 3 [[Anti-Illusionary Libation|Anti-Illusionary Libations]] at the [[Chemistry Table]] nearby at {{waypoint 130, -511, 31}}.
   1. Return to [[Aurelian Coast]] and use 3 [[Anti-Illusionary Libation]] on all [[Mysterious object (no-trade no-value no-destroy)|mysterious object]] in your inventory. You will receive three items:
      - [[Effigy of a Knight of Shadow Haven]]
      - [[Tegi Ritual Totem]]
      - [[Symbol of the Shissar Empire]]
1. Examine the [[Effigy of a Knight of Shadow Haven]] from you inventory and  "***Look closer ...***"
