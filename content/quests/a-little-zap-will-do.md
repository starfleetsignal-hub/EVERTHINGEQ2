## Steps

1. Speak with [[Tarif I'sak]] aboard [[The Vigilant: Incursion|The Vigilan]] in the cargo hold.
1. Use the [[Magic Rejuvenator]] to collect 5 essence updates.
   - *Note: the Rejuvenator only works on tallonite amans, lozonns and zonns which are under 20% health.*
1. Return to [[Om'ran Quat'ir]] in Paineel.

## Rewards

- At least 90g 70s 61c
- +1000 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**
