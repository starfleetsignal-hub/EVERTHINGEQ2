## Notes

- You can fly now in the [[Echo Caverns]]

## Steps

1. Return to [[Echo Caverns]] (this requires zoning in through the Nexus. Using a bauble will not update the quest)
1. Search for the passage to the surface of the dark side of Luclin along the northern-most edge of [[Chasm Fiend Cliffs]].
   - Immediately inside of [[Echo Caverns]], look up and to the East, where there is a passageway near the ceiling {{waypoint -106.05, 249.39, -43.28}} that will take you directly to [[Chasm Fiend Cliffs]] {{waypoint -240.40, 264.45, -144.91}}. (The exit is south and a little down from [[Hermit's Hideaway]]). (Easiest way I found is fly NE from the Abandoned Quarry) .
   - Proceed to a second passage at {{waypoint -351.28, 194.36, -446.37}}
1. Place Inkeye's survey marker in the [[Savage Weald]] {{waypoint -388, 289, -861}},
1. Speak with the Vah Shir ghost, [[Tiesh]], right next to the marker.

- [[Acrylia Coin of Savage Weald]] - *grants you access to [[Savage Weald]] and its dungeons*
- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 56,000 status
- One of the following:
  - [[Toxic PolyPore Scale Boots]]
  - [[Toxic Polypore Fiber Shoes]]
  - [[Toxic Polypore Leather Boots]]
  - [[Toxic Polypore Plate Sabatons]]
