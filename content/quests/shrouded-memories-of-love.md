## Steps

![Mithaniel Marr, moments after crushing his sister's coffin.](images/Erollisi_Relased.jpg)Access from Everfrost using the portal at the end of the dock.

1. Speak with Gwestanna and Arawen.
   - [[Gwestanna]] can be found at {{waypoint 200, 15, -170}}
   - [[Arawen]] can be found at {{waypoint -140, 14, -186}}
1. Return to [[Lanielle]].
   - At this point you can obtain the quest [[No More Mucking Around]] and [[Rekindle the Love]].
1. Gain access to [[The Chapel of Love]]. {{waypoint -3, 38, -257}}
   - Access to the Chapel of Love is granted once you have killed the following named monsters. They appear when their spawn has been cleared out by the surrounding monsters.
     - [[Thane-Malach]] {{waypoint 68, -6, -99}}
     - [[Valoron the Just]] {{waypoint -10, 37, -213}}
     - [[Willowmeena]] {{waypoint 169, 14, -185}}
     - [[Woefulness]] {{waypoint -113, -5, -107}}
1. Confront [[Mithaniel Marr (Named)|Mithaniel Marr]].
   - Defeat Mithaniel in battle, during which he turns into an unattackable NPC. After he finishes his dialogue and crushes his sister's coffin, the quest will automatically complete.
   - At this point you can obtain the quest, [[Johnny Pomegranate Seed]], from [[Lanielle]].
