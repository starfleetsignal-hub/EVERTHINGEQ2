## Steps

1. Enter "Portal to the Past: Guk" from inside the Chronoweave.
1. Recover the other missing Village of Shin pottery piece. This drops in a chest from any mob in the zone.  2nd chest does not always drop in the same instance.
1. Speak to [[Vusal Zaminli]] in [[The Chronoweave]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- 1 [[Ancient Platinum Coin]]
