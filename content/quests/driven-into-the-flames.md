## Description

I am to return to the Temple of Solusek Ro and find braziers with certain properties described to me by Marathan! When I find these braziers, I am to drive one spike into the center of each of them, turning them into some sort of magical lure! These should draw out the beings that Marathan wishes to capture out from their shadows like a lightning rod of sorts, and contain them within the temple!
