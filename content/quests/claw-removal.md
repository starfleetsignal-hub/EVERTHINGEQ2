## Steps

1. Enter [[The Unknown: The Obliviwrought Conclusion (Solo)|The Unknown: The Obliviwrought Conclusion [Solo]]]
   - *The entrance is at {{waypoint -762, 17, -768}} in [[Nightscar Borne Lands]] (Longhowl the warpwolf near Vadreem can take you to the beach, and the entrance is close.)*
1. Kill the following
   - Slay 13 apocryphal atrocities
     - Found in the [[Brimboil Nursery]], before the first named, in groups of three
   - Slay 7 groups of ebonlithe lamia
     - Found after the first named (The Apocryphal Creation) in groups of three. One update per group/encounter.
   - Destroy 12 runic voidflow crystals![runic voidflow crystal](images/Runic_voidflow_crystal.png)
     - Found throughout the zone. Click to destroy.
   - *Be SURE to **destroy the crystals BEFORE slaying Molotor** or you will have to reset instance.*
1. Slay [[Molotor]], the Viscerite Daemon (4th named in the instance)
   - Infernal Tyrant Dennigrah will arrive after Molotor is defeated, and she will run up the ramp to Nightscar Mettle
1. Dispose of [[Gozaggrul]], Keeper of the Claw
1. Reclaim the Vrakorr Rune Claw
   - The claw is floating in an orb near the exit, at {{waypoint -1170, 56, -721}}. Click on it to update the quest.
1. Return to [[Vadreem]] on [[Nightscar Steppes]]
1. Destroy the Vrakorr Rune Claw in the Consumer's Offering Bath
   - The offering bath is in [[Oblivion's Bask]] at {{waypoint 195, 158, 91}}.
   - *Note: this is near the heroic named Alexandra Veck.*
