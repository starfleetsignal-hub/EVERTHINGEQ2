## Steps

1. Enter [[The Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors]]. Kills some mobs and retrieve an idol.
   - Kill 10 frogloks, auto updates
   - Kill 5 trolls, auto updates
   - "Find something useful to bring back..." - This is an idol in the [[Overseers Chamber]], at the top of the spiral pathway starting in the [[Chamber of Hollow Life]]. {{waypoint -200, -11, -226}}
1. Report to [[Gurlig Valorheart]].

## Rewards

- At least 31g 73s 78c
