## Steps

1. Gather 10 sturdy wood planks along the coast (they often break when you pick them up, ratio approximately 2:1 - I got 10 strong planks and 19 failures).  Fly around the outside of the island rather than down the valleys. (note: I found the best concentration of these planks on the far east and northeastern sections of the island.)
1. Return to [[Captain Greymast]]

## Notes

Although the entire island is a potential location for the wood, I had the best luck finding the "sturdy wood planks" scattered along the water's edge on the island. Here are some additional locations: {{waypoint -1200, 7, -512}}, {{waypoint -1302, 9, -1188}}, {{waypoint -625, 6, -1297}}, and {{waypoint -1012, 8, -1264}}.
