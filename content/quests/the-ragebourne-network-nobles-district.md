## Steps

1. Travel to the Nobles District and find [[Aggie Winthrop]] at {{waypoint -1164, 100, 814}}.
   - Collect the First Sentinel Crow at {{waypoint -907, 157, 356}}.
   - Collect the Second Sentinel Crow at {{waypoint -980, 86, 747}}.
   - Collect the Third Sentinel Crow at {{waypoint -1371, 93, 478}}.
   - Collect the Fourth Sentinel Crow at {{waypoint -1160, 90, 275}}.
1. Return to [[Aggie Winthrop]].
1. Return to [[Caerwyn Thomlin]].

## Rewards

- At least 48g 35s 24c
- Completing this quest grants access to use the Nobles District Teleporter.
