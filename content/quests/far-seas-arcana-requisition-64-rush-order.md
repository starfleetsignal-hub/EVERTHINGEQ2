## Notes

- You have 8 minutes 30 seconds to complete this quest.
- You will need the following fuels:
  - 60 scintillating incense
- You will need the following raws:
  - 30 hanging roots
  - 30 rough lumbered rosewood
  - 6 rough topaz

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Kaisha Swifteasel]] to receive the quest.
1. Click on the Requisition Order Desk next to [[Kaisha Swifteasel]] to receive your requisition.
1. Craft six random low level 60's Journeyman spells.
1. Speak with [[Kaisha Swifteasel]] to finish the quest.

- Known Requisition combinations
  - Requisition A
    - [[Grim Sorcerer IV]] (level 62)
    - [[Fiery Magician IV]] (level 62)
    - [[Protoflame II]] (level 62)
    - [[Absolution IV]] (level 62)
    - [[Fae Fire III]] (level 63)
    - [[Fortify Elements V]] (level 63)

## Rewards

- At least: 4g 92s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
