This is one of a group of quests in the [[Freeport Sabotage Quest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Destroy five small boats at the docks and on the waterways in the Commonlands. In addition to the docks, the boats may be found in:
   - the lake at [[Lucan's Mount]] {{waypoint 15.47, -13.54, -752.08}}.
   - [[Pride Lake]] {{waypoint 465, 0, -549}}.
   - the river shore at the Kerran camp {{waypoint -195, 0, 165}}.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least
- +1000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -1000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -1000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
