## Description

"Tuathil Laeds, a fae stationed near the Crushbone mount station, wants me to go into Crushbone territory, climb to the high-cap just west of Camp Rutok, and release an "ashway ulm." This will help get more reliable and up to date information to Tuathil which will in turn help him plan future operations." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Release the [[Ashway Ulm (Quest Item)|Ashway Ulm]] on top of a mushroom platform just west of [[Camp Rutok]] at {{waypoint -812, 115, -164}} .
1. Return to [[Tuathil Laeds]]

## Rewards

- At least 1s 59c
- [[Grand Kelethin bookcase]]
