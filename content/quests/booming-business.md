- Note: You will need at least 6 [[Basic Coal]] to compete quest.

## Steps

1. Scribe the [[Sootfoot Boom Rock (Recipe)|Sootfoot Boom Rock]] recipe you received.
1. Head to the [[Shrine of Thunder]] and speak to [[Pyrissa Flamecog]] to receive 12 danger sticks. {{waypoint -128, -115, 646.57}}
1. Harvest Lava Rocks from the carts near [[A Sootfoot gatherer|Sootfoot gatherers]] at  {{waypoint -296, -130, 331}}. You can harvest more than once from each cart.
1. Harvest Coarse Ash from the red pile near [[Smakametta]]. {{waypoint -305, -130.1, 420.79}}
1. Craft the [[Sootfoot Boom Rock (Item)|Sootfoot Boom Rocks]] at the Sootfoot forge next to Smakametta. You will need at least 6 [[Basic Coal]].
1. Return to Smapty.
