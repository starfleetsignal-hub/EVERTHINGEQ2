## Description

"Jenthis needs me to travel south of Orc Hill to the druid rings. Once there, I can climb up to Orc Hill without having to fight my way through their sentries and heavy guards. Once I get up to Orc Hill, I need to find my way to Lieutenant Fa'Rak, kill him, and take the plan book from his tent." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay [[Lieutenant Fa'Rak]] on [[Orc Hill]] in the tent at {{waypoint 219, 72, -138}}
1. Obtain [[Fa'Rak's Plan Book]] by inspecting the book on the table in the tent at {{waypoint 216.92, 72.03, -135.47}}
1. Return to [[Jenthis Viridar]]

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Jenthis Viridar: "Have you slain the invaders?"
- Player Character: "I have."
- Jenthis Viridar: "Good. I believe I have all the information I'm going to get from this book. They plan to continue creating outposts in the Faydark around Kelethin. Based on what the scouts report, the orc timetable can be sped up as necessary. Unfortunately, we still don't know of their ultimate purpose. We need to rectify that."
- Player Character: "What must I do?"
- Jenthis Viridar: "West and south of our location--west of Tunare's Sapling--you'll find a set of druid rings. They rest just under Orc Hill. A tree in that area accesses Orc Hill without alerting their forward sentries. Once you're in the orc camp, find Lieutenant Fa'Rak, kill him, and retrieve his plan book. From there we'll have something solid to work on."
- Player Character: "Okay."

I need to sneak into the camp on Orc Hill, kill Lieutenant Fa'Rak, and take his plan book. I need to kill Lieutenant Fa'Rak on Orc Hill. I need Lieutenant Fa'Rak's plan book.

I have slain Lieutenant Fa'Rak. I have the plan book. I need to bring the plan book and news of Fa'Rak's death to Jenthis. I need to bring the order book back to Jenthis.

- Jenthis Viridar: "The lieutenant? The plan book?"
- Player Character: "Here is the plan book. The lieutenant is dead."
- Jenthis Viridar: "Great work, [Player Character]. Let me digest what I can."
