Gwynvyn coordinates the delivery of replacement equipment for the guards of Maldura on a weekly basis.

## Steps

1. Craft five items at a forge. You will be asked to make one of the following types of equipment:
   - [[Malduran Forged Arrow]]
   - [[Malduran Short Sword]]
   - [[Malduran Tower Shield]]
1. Deliver them to [[Quartermaster Lysslyn]] {{waypoint -188.64, -3.43, -33.87}}.

## Rewards

- [[Coin of the Grand Artificer]]
- [[Ancient Arcannium Coin]]
- At least 2p 26g 80s
- +2,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +1,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
