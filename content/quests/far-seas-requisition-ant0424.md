## Steps

1. Kill the following
   - 6 [[A Coldwind manta ray|Coldwind manta rays]] found anywhere along the [[Coldwind Coastline]]
   - 4 [[A lowland gecko|lowland geckos]] found in north-central Antonica.
   - [[A lowland viper|Lowland vipers]] seem to be place holders for lowland geckos. Kill them to make them spawn.
1. Seek out [[Taskmaster Lynette]] on the second floor of the [[Coldwind Point|Coldwind Lighthouse]] for your reward. {{waypoint 345, 0, 819}}

## Rewards

- At least 6s 23c
