## Prerequisites

- You have to complete [[The Mystery of the Secret Cabal]].

## Introduction

Bayle's Amulet of Benediction, the item that the Seeress sent the acolyte to retrieve, is hiding its usefulness or it was only a distraction as we attempted to investigate the Seeress' murder.

## Steps

1. Deliver [[Bayle's Amulet of Benediction (Quest Item)|Bayle's Amulet of Benediction]] to [[Vishra (South Qeynos)|Vishra]] outside the [[The Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos]] to get your final reward.

- [[Bayle's Diminished Amulet of Benediction]] (tier 3)
