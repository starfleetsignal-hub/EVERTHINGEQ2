## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- To access [[The Treasure Chamber]] you, or someone you're grouped with, must have completed the quest [[The Key of Coins]].

## Steps

1. Open [[The Treasure Chamber]] door {{waypoint -4, 10, -195}} to find a chamber at {{waypoint 10, 10, -220}} that has 7 chests. Find the correct one {{waypoint 11.64, 11.52, -236.29}} and open it.
