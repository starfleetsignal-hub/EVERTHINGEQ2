## Notes

- This quest is no longer required for access to [[The Poets Palace]]. However, it is still required if you wish to obtain the access quest for [[The Pedestal of Sky]] from the [[The Djinn Master (NPC)|Djinn Master]] inside Poets Palace.
- The locations for the pillow, mat, eggs, and chalice are random spawns in a specific area. See the **talk page** for a detailed list of possible spawns and photos.
  - Using [[Track Harvestables]] or a [[Gnomish Divining Rod]] may help you.

## Steps

1. Go to [[Maj'Dul]] and search for a red pillow that can spawn under *any* merchant tent.
   - Possible locations: {{waypoint 61, 140, -70}} or {{waypoint -67, 149, -137}} or {{waypoint 53, 139, 46}} or {{waypoint -176, 177, -93}}
   - Trackable as "a red pillow"
1. Go to [[The Great Bonsai]] in [[The Pillars of Flame]] and search for a harvestable mat. {{waypoint 149, -189, -1623}} or {{waypoint 308, -167, -1663}}
1. Go to [[The Seer Stone]] or [[The Table of Eyes]] in the Pillars of Flame and search for harvestable eggs. *({{waypoint 23.83, -45.14, -399.69}} and {{waypoint -122, -58, -452}} respectively)*
   - If using a land mount, take the ramp at {{waypoint -43, -75, -622}} or {{waypoint -20, -93, -253}} in order to climb up to the cyclops area and then cross the path at {{waypoint -80, -46, -396}}.
   - The eggs can even spawn on the ramps outside the immediate Seer Stone area.
   - If no eggs in one area, then check the other.
   - They are trackable.
1. Go to [[The Living Tombs]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] and search for a chalice.
   - Spawns in random locations throughout The Living Tombs. It tends to spawn on top of towers an on ledges. It is trackable as "an ancient chalice"
   - Locations found:
     - {{waypoint -451, 23, 344}}
     - {{waypoint -191, 33, 281}} - on top of a building in the left corner,
     - {{waypoint -438, 0, 356}} - up the oily wall
     - {{waypoint -377.33, 26.46, 496.16}}  - on the roof corner next to the eternal falcons
     - {{waypoint -193, 33.59, 282.70}} - Statue Court
     - {{waypoint -432, 22, 359}} - up the first wall of Residence Court on a ledge
     - {{waypoint -43.24, 22.35, 360.38}} - ledge in Residence Court
   - NOTE: In order to climb the oily wall you must complete the quest:  [[A Slippery Situation]].
1. Return to [[The Caretaker (NPC)|The Caretaker]] with the items you found.
1. Go to[Maj'Dul and enter [[Tower of the Moon:Infiltration]]. {{waypoint -152, 176, -146}}
   - NOTE:  It is recommended to enter solo since only one person can collect the quest items.
   - Gather first djinn vessel on the second floor; spawns on any of the tables around the perimeter of the room. {{waypoint 1, 15, 18}}
   - Gather second djinn vessel on the third floor. {{waypoint 3, 28, -2}} or {{waypoint -8, 27, 4}}.
1. Return to The Caretaker. When you finish her dialogue she will attack. Kill her to end the quest.
