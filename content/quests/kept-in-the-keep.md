Grassblade is perplexed by the containment, let alone usage, of life energies, being utilized within the keep.  He does not know who of the Deathfist Empire would have had the dark magic knowledge necessary to create soul reliquaries.

## Steps

1. Find and destroy Pebbles' reliquary.
   - Destroy the attackable vase at {{waypoint 9, 9, 35}} (Prince Gluntock's Former Chambers second floor of southwest tower).
1. Return to Grassblade in the sewer.
   - Grassblade has not returned. (Quest should update as you get to the well...you do not have to jump down into the well)
1. Search for Grassblade
   - He is chained up in [[Lich's Brew]] at {{waypoint -8, 8, 42}} (second floor of the southeast tower).
   - Speak to him to advance the quest.
1. Destroy the soul reliquaries.
   - Five attackable vases on the floor of the room you are in.

Note: elevators {{waypoint 20.88, 1.14, 41.67}} to get to the Prince Gluntock's Former Chambers or lich's brew, just click on the floor.  There is no button.  You can also get to the former chambers thru the balcony but it's not worth the effort.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13200 status
- +500 faction with **The Green Hoods**
