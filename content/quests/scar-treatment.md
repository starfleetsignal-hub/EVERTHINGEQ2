## Steps

1. Use [[Uelmondi's Sealing Orb]] on 6 creatures in the [[Metetherial Plains]], kill them and gather the concentration of essence that drops.
   - purple bubbles under the corpse![a concentration of essence](images/A_concentration_of_essence_.jpg)
1. Gather a shard of Starfire Crystal at {{waypoint -827, 137, -589}} or {{waypoint -555, 132, -637}}![a shard of Starfire Crystal](images/A_shard_of_Starfire_Crystal.jpg)
1. Click on an upwelling of arcane power at {{waypoint -330, 11, 429}}![an upwelling of arcane power](images/An_upwelling_of_arcane_power.jpg)
1. Return to [[Uelmondi]] at {{waypoint 418, 36, 39}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Khali'Yrzu Crate of Crystals]]

- +1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
