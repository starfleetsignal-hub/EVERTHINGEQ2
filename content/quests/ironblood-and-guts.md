Grygrim, the head tender at the Ironblood Fungus Farm in Thalumbra, doesn't know when the next wave of Aberrant attacks might push them back into Maldura. He asked for my help harvesting the ironblood fungus. I figure I'll help clear out some of the attacking ironblood myconids, while I am at it.

## Steps

1. Continue helping out at the [[Ironblood Fungus Farm]]:
   - Harvest 10 **ironblood fungus**. These are small clumps of two or three mushrooms with thin stems. Possible locations:
     - {{waypoint 802, 48, -289}}, {{waypoint 808, 48, -294}}, {{waypoint 828, 47, -311}}, {{waypoint 851, 41, -429}}, {{waypoint 864, 42, -430}}, {{waypoint 866, 43, -440}}, {{waypoint 845, 41, -432}}, {{waypoint 831, 41, -426}}, {{waypoint 831, 43, -438}}, {{waypoint 825, 44, -388}} ![ironblood fungus](images/Ironblood_fungus.jpg)
   - Kill 8 ironblood myconids (any type will do).
1. Return to [[Grygrim the Tender|Grygrim]] .

- At least 1p 41g 75s 12687 status

- +5000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
