## Description

"Sentry Trillis has asked me to investigate the grobins that Scout Moranne spotted near Amethyst Pond. I am to clear out some of the scouts so that the sentries of the Nursery can approach the pond without confrontation." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[Grobin scout|grobin scouts]] near the Amethyst Pond {{waypoint -19.14, -50.31, -668.54}}
1. Return to Sentry Trillis

- At least 1s 16c
