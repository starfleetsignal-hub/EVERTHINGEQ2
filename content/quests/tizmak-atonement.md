## Steps

1. Free the captured Tizmaks and defeat any guards
   - Free 6 captured Tizmak found near {{waypoint -419, -336.5, -5.85}}
   - Kill 8 [[A D'Kilneld Company guard|D'Kilneld Company guard]] or [[A D'Kilneld Company trainee|D'Kilneld Company trainee]]
     - They spawn as group of 2 or 3 each time you free a tizmak
     - *Note: The Steps resets when you kill a Tizmak*
1. Speak with [[Toridar Mudhoof]] {{waypoint -261, -354, 141}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +15,000 faction with **Tizmak Clan**
