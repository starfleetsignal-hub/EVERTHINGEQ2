## Description

The Hua Mein Craftsman has asked if I would burn a memorial offering for their ancestors.

## Steps

1. Gather 5 of either [[Sumac lumber]] or [[Bamboo shoot|bamboo shoots]] (closest place for harvesting is at the bottom of the ramp from Kejaan's Rill to Toxxulia's Reach). Having the materials in your inventory will update the quest immediately.
1. Click the *Burned Offering* green sparklies at the entrance to the [[Caves of the Great Tiger Spirit]] {{waypoint -405, 280, 3053}} (the ramp up is at {{waypoint -296, 253, 2902}})
1. Return to [[Xi Lan]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,000 faction with **Hua Mein**
- +2,500 faction with **Hua Mein Craftsmen**
