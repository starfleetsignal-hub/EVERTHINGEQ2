## Steps

1. Use your Teleport Pebble from earlier quest to instantly transport to [[Quel'ule]]. Try to find the Theerian Spy.
   1. Talk to an  analyst around {{waypoint -268, 359, 597}}.
   1. He will attack you; [[A Theerian agent|kill him]] for the update.
      - *Note: This mob has Harm Touch that can hit for 9000+. Be prepared.*
1. Return to [[Jaswinda]] {{waypoint -154, 194, -361}}.
