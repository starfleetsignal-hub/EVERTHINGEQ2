## Description

"The local mender has offered to show me how to use some of the harvests I gathered to craft a small lucky charm using his anvil." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Obtain [[Recipe: Lucky Wolf Paw Charm]] from the table.
1. Obtain coal from the sack next to the table.
1. Read the recipe to scribe it and craft a [[Lucky Wolf Paw]] charm. It requires 1 [[Tin cluster]] and 1 [[Lead cluster]].
1. Visit [[Glin Hammerhelm]], the tradeskill tutor at Ravens' Roost in [[Frostfang Sea]] {{waypoint -350, 176, -51}} to learn more about crafting.

## Rewards

- At least 85c
- [[Handbook of the Ravens of the North]]

- Bull the Crafty: "Greetings! I noticed you harvesting out there. Are you interested in trying your hand at making something out of them?"

- Bull the Crafty: "Nay, not hard at all once you wrap your head around it. But I'm not one to keep talking all day. Why don't you just jump in and try it?"

- Player Character: "I'll get those items now."

Bull the mender has instructed me to get what I'll need for crafting. There is a recipe on the table, a sack of coal on the ground, and I'll need some of the tin and lead clusters I harvested earlier. I need to get the recipe from the table.

I picked up the recipe, and should examine it in my inventory to scribe it. I need to pick up some coal from the sack.

I obtained some coal from the sack. I obtained the supplies I will need. I should scribe the recipe book, and ensure I have a lead and tin cluster in my bags. Then click on the anvil in front of the mender to use it. Select the lucky wolf paw recipe and click 'Create', then 'Begin'. I should ensure I have a tin and lead cluster in my bags, then use the mender's anvil to craft.

I created a Lucky Wolf Paw charm. Bull the mender is at the wrecked boat on Pioneers' Landing. I should let him know that I made the lucky wolf paw charm. I should return to Bull once again.

Once I reach Halas, the tradeskill tutor can be found in the crafting area of Ravens' Roost, near the bank and housing. I should find the tradeskill tutor in the city to learn more about crafting.
