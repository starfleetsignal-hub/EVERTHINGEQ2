## Notes

- This is a subquest of [[The Symbol in the Flesh]].

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Gogas Afadin (NPC)|Gogas Afadin]] in [[Nektulos Forest]]. {{waypoint -1392, 25, -1399}}
1. He will perform the empowerment ritual for you, but first he needs you to:
   - Obtain 1 [[Enchanted Azurite Capillary]] (Adorning, 325 skill)
     - If on collecting the capillary, the quest doesn't update, place the item on the broker then remove it. It can also be bought on the broker or traded from another character. The quest will update if it is sitting in your bank or in your bags on your character when you speak to Toranim.
   - Obtain 6 'phosphorescent pearls', an uncommon update from [[A deep spawn consort|deep spawn consort]]s in the [[Loping Plains]]. {{waypoint 93, 3, 380}}
   - Kill [[Kassar the Uncontrollable]], spawned in the upper level of [[Mistmoore Catacombs]] by clicking on the hanging bones at {{waypoint -56, -28, -98}}.  To get to the location:
     - Enter the upper level from [[Castle Mistmoore]] {{waypoint -218, -14, -14}}
     - Enter upper level from the lower level of [[Mistmoore Catacombs]]:
       - Climb the wall at {{waypoint -12, -159, -30}} next to [[Alain Toad]].
       - Climb the wall at {{waypoint -33, -127, 120}}.
       - Climb the wall at {{waypoint -59, -100, 63}} ( it is the wall that has a puddle of water and gargoyles outside)
1. Return to Gogas with the components and 20g so that he will craft the weapon for you.
