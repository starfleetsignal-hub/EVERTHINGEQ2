## Steps

1. Collect some local shellfish
   - Gather 10 swamp crustaceans (in the edges of the ponds around Firmroot Moot.  Look in the water for shells.)
   - Gather 10 ykeshan starfish (on edges of the islands - take the balloon to the Overlook to reach the Tillage Patch, small white dots around the edge where the balloon drops you off, on the green border)
1. Leave the supplies in the basket next to Hophip

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +250 faction with **Survival Accord**
