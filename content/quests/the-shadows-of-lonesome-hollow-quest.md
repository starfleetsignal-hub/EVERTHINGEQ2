## Steps

1. Kill 10 soulless zombies. [[A soulless assistant|Soulless assistants]] or [[A soulless resurrector|resurrectors]] will work.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 soulless zombies.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 soulless zombies.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 shadowed creatures.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 shadowed creatures.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 shadowed creatures.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 evil eyes. [[Mitalla the Voice]], [[A spectral sentry|spectral sentries]], [[A paroxysmal observer|paroxysmal observers]], [[A ghastly sentry|ghastly sentries]] and [[Paroxroz]] will work.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 evil eyes.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 evil eyes.
1. Read the book.
