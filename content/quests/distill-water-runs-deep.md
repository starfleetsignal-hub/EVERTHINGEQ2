## Notes

This quest has one combat encounter with special fight mechanic. Mentoring down might make it easier.

The crafting part of this quest requires you to be level 2 artisan.

It will also require 15 basic kindling, bring them with you. If you forget kindling and have to leave the Glimmermash Depths zone, you can get back to it by clicking on portal in Enchanted Lands at {{waypoint -989, 1, -273}} (you can run and swim there from EL dock East and past the druid ring)

## Steps

1. "I should find Prinna Springkettle's distillery near the Great Guard in the Enchanted Lands" - go to Enchanted Lands dock and run along coast past the great Guard wall to the first hut at {{waypoint 325, -1, -207}} , enter instance Springkettle's Distillery.
1. "Search distillery for enough potcheen to replenish the supply at the Bar of Brell"
   - search the potcheen jugs - click any jug on floor or tables
   - examine potcheen distillery equipment - click Copper Potcheen Still
   - search distillery for clues as to what occured - enter 2nd room to realize the place was ransacked and thief escaped leaving portal in the corner of the room open
1. Enter portal "Follow the thief!" in the back of Springkettle's Distillery. This takes you back to Enchanted Lands on the East side of the zone on a small island with a dock {{waypoint -1029, 2, -342}}
1. "Search for potcheen thief" - approach the goblin [[Naggeneen]] sitting on the crates on dock.
1. "Confront Naggeneen" -  talk to goblin, you will get knocked back and he escapes leaving a portal open behind him. "Follow Naggeneen into the portal.
1. You end up in [[Glimmermash Depths]]. "search for a sign of the thief" - follow the path down and click on sparkling "Curious Rucksack" at {{waypoint -3648, -77, -358}}. You receive [[A Guide to Potcheen Distilling]] book. Look for it in your bags and scribe it for recipe to make [[Potent Potcheen]].
1. "Search for Naggeneen" - proceed through the zone, you can  harvest along the way what you will need later to make 3 x  potcheen . If you choose to pre-harvest ingredients get the following:
   - 3 x  [[Underfoot Sweet Beet|Underfoot Sweet Beets]] - green leafy plants at {{waypoint -3911, -71, -369}}
   - 6 x [[Underfoot Potatoes]] - up on ledge above main path, path to that ledge starts at {{waypoint -4008, -63, -94}}
   - 9 x [[Fresh Spring Water]] - blue waterspouts in pond at {{waypoint -3854, -85, 53}} swim around, more will spawn
   - 6 x [[Malted Barley]] - flat beige sacks around distillery area where you will craft potcheen,  see next step.
1. Go up to the ledge with distillery crafting station at {{waypoint -3841, -27, -171}}, path up to that area starts at {{waypoint -3809, -76, -100}}  "No sign of Naggeneen but I found his distillery"
1. "Distill some potent potcheen to draw Naggeneed out of hiding" - craft 3 x [[Potent Potcheen]] on Cobbled Potcheen Distillery
1. Deposit potcheen nearby - sparkly yellow spots nearby, be prepared to fight.
1. "Defeat Naggeneen" -  he spawns at your level, you cannot do damage to him except with heavy steins he drops on the ground  every so often.  During fight keep yourself healed and look around for heavy stein, click it to receive [[Lager Ranged Missile]], put it on your hot key and click to use with Naggeneen targetted, this will damage him by 20%, repeat until he's dead.
1. Return to Prinna Springkettle in Bar of Brell.
