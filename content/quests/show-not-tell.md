## Steps

1. Kill the following mobs: ![Charm of Greater Acquiesce](images/Charm_of_Greater_Acquiesce.jpg)
   - 8 [[Manafin salamander]] for toxin sacs in [[Auroric Tide]] near {{waypoint -987, 8, 244}}.
   - 8 [[Seabound cnidcara]] in [[Auroric Tide]] near {{waypoint -987, 8, 244}}
   - 4 alux for ration bags in the [[Drukyna Marsh]] {{waypoint -685, -53, 124}}or the [[Pakiat Bluffs]] (uncommon drop).
1. Return to [[Himaduri]] at {{waypoint -155, 576, -757}} and receive a [[Benefactor Reveal Potion]].
1. Travel to {{waypoint 921, 18, 913}} and click on the Charm of Greater Acquiesce (next to the rock in the center of camp) ![Matriarch Taneesha revealed by the potion](images/Matriarch_Taneesha_revealed.jpg)
1. Return to [[Himaduri]] at {{waypoint -155, 576, -757}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Khali'Vahla Arcanum Orb]]

- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
