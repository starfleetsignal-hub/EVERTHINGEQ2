## Steps

1. Create an Elm Roundshield
   - Using Fletching skills at a Woodworking Table, combine the following ingredients:
     - 1 Raw Elm
     - 1 Raw Tin
     - 1 Raw Rawhide Leather
     - 1 Raw Root
     - 1 sandpaper
