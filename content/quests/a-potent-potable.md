## Note

If you have difficulty completing the quest, you may speak to Nika at any time to end it and begin the next quest, [[A Salty Farewell]]. After you complete that one, a repeatable quest will be offered, [[Recurring Ministration]], allowing you to obtain the achievement [[Potent Potioner]]. You cannot come back to *A Potent Potable* if you skip it; it will be gone forever.

## Steps

1. Harvest plants and rescue othmir:  ![A coastal salycinth](images/A_Potent_Potable_quest_-_coastal_salycinth.jpg)
   - Collect 6 coastal salycinths
     - {{waypoint 4379, -1096, 1321}}
     - {{waypoint 4395, -1094, 1310}}
     - {{waypoint 4419, -1094, 1301}}
     - {{waypoint 4435, -1092, 1292}}
     - {{waypoint 4427, -1096, 1251}}
     - {{waypoint 4443, -1097, 1215}}
   - Escort 3 stranded othmir to Nika
     - {{waypoint 4435, -1091, 1263}}
     - {{waypoint 4165, -1074, 1550}}
     - {{waypoint 4104, -1077, 1387}}
1. Speak to Nika
1. Use [[Nika's Salycinth Antidote]] on numerous [[A poisoned othmir|poisoned othmir]] to keep them alive.  You have a two minute timer, and must not let more than 3 othmir die during that timer.  You can receive more of the antidote by clicking on the basket at {{waypoint 4297, -1081, 1480}} Note that this one is not possible to do due to the slow casting of the potion. Just move on to the next one as few have ever done it.
1. Speak to Nika

- Completes the [[Achievements|Achievement]], [[Potent Potioner]], which rewards the title, "[[The Nullifier]]"
