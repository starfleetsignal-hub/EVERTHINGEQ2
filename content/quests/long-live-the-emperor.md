## Notes

The instructions below say that "*This quest is an advanced solo daily, it is only available if you have completed the Kunark Ascending Signature Quest series*" - but that reads a little contradictory if you are trying to complete it as the Public Quest at the end of [[Kunark Ascending: Resurrection Machination]]. So be aware that, for the Resurrection Machination Signature quest, this quest is triggered by placing the Greenmist on the crystal in the middle of the fractured runestone in Twark at {{waypoint 269, -179, -659}}. After that, you can re-do this quest as described below.

## How to pick up the quest

This quest is an advanced solo daily, **it is only available if you have completed the Kunark Ascending Signature Quest series**, otherwise you will be offered other quests. It is not enough to use the [[The Essential Kunark Guide|Essential Kunark Guide]], you must have completed [[Kunark Ascending: A Nightmare Realized]].

1. Speak to [[Strashka]] in [[Obulus Frontier]]
1. Select *Sure, why not* in conversation
1. Select *For the Emperor!*

## Steps

1. Aid in Rile's struggle to overthrow Venril from the Sathirian throne.
   - Click the stone in the middle of the ground at {{waypoint 270, -180, -659}} to start the event.
   - Defeat 30 of Venril's army in the PQ area.
   - Kill [[Xalgoz (Obulus Frontier)|Xalgoz]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 26g 80s
- 3 [[Sovereign of Rile]]
- [[Long Live the Emperor! Reward Crate]] {{waypoint 269, -179, -659}}
