## Steps

1. Go to [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] in [[North Freeport]]
   - Head to your left as you enter and you will see some elevators, one going up, another going down.  Head downstairs, go across left bridge that heads slightly upwards and click that left-hand door leading you to Fulania T'Nal's room {{waypoint 23, -34, -148}} within the Academy's Arcane Suites.
1. Once inside, find and hail Fulania, finish dialog, allowing Leandre to kill Fulania
1. Search the corpse and retrieve Dark Elf Giblets.
1. Eat and enjoy being noxious for about an hour (though no damage ticks off at this time). (no need to wait till noxious is off in order to advance)
1. Talk to yourself again (like any self-respecting person crazy enough to eat Dark Elf Giblets would do) to further the quest.
