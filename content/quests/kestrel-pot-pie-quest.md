## Steps

1. Collect 5 ice shrooms in the path that leads to the docks (Entrance at {{waypoint -217.3, 136.4, 2.55}})
1. Kill 3 [[A downy kestrel|downy kestrels]] located on the path to the Cragged Spine (Birds are at {{waypoint -121.3, 146.7, 106.5}})
1. Return to [[Alice Swamprunner]]
1. Try some of the pies

## Rewards

- At least 12s 2c
- 4 [[Kestrel Pot Pie (Item)|Kestrel Pot Pie]]
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Brawler's Belt of Shurikens]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Empowered Totem]] (All Mages)
  - [[Forged Buckler]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Shortbow]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Thug's Knifebelt]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Vanguard's Shortbow]] (Warriors, Crusaders)
