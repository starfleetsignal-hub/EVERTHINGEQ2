## Steps

1. Go to [[Kylong Plains]] and collect the three stones:
   - Clouds: {{waypoint -1350, 1, 476}}
   - Rains: {{waypoint -1408, 3, -188}}
   - Storm: {{waypoint -911, 8, -331}}
1. Examine the summoning circle to place the stones. {{waypoint -1448, 53, 355}}
1. Lure the [[A large elemental|large elemental]] that spawns nearby towards the summoning circle. Do not kill it.
1. Collect the 3 charged crystals.
1. Return to Askr.

## Rewards

- At least 5g 7s
- +7500 faction with **Karana**
- [[Askr's Altar to Karana]]
