## Steps

1. Eirik requests that you make the stew from the recipe provided, [[Eirik's Traditional Healing Stew Recipe]], using the the [[Brontotherium Steak]] he provides, along with the gathered herbs from the previous quest.  Additional materials required:
   - [[Stove & Keg]] (next to Eirik at {{waypoint 863, -274, 136}})
   - 1 [[Aerated mineral water]] (fuel vendor near Eirik at {{waypoint 888, -274, 138}})
   - 1 [[Ethereal kindling]] (fuel vendor near Eirik at {{waypoint 888, -274, 138}})
1. Return to [[Eirik]]

Note: three steps from now during [[Hunting for History]] you will need 1 ethereal coal.  You may want to consider buying some now from the vendor at {{waypoint 888, -274, 138}}.  You will also need 2 titanium ore and 1 mottled pelt (both are common harvests).

## Rewards

- [[Coldain Saucepan]]
- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
