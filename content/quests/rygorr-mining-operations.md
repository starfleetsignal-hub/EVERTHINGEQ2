## Steps

1. Talk to [[Thirin Veliumdelver]] {{waypoint -8.41, -66.11, 222.27}} just inside the entrance of [[Demon's Delve]]
1. Collect a Ry'Gorr Explosive Mining Barrel {{waypoint 243, -53, 49}} (also can be found at {{waypoint 247, -49, 94}} and possibly other locations) and use it in front of the hastily made wall {{waypoint -7, -67, 161}} A message pops up saying "This seems like a good distance to place the Ry'Gorr Explosive Mining Barrel" when you are in the correct location
1. Step past the destroyed wall into the [[Velinoid Catacombs]]. Quest updates about 5 steps in.
1. Return to [[Thirin Veliumdelver]] at the entrance of [[Demon's Delve]]

## Rewards

- At least 99s 81c
- [[Coldain leather bound book]]
