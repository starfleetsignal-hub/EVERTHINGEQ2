## Steps

1. kill the following
   - 1 a brontoherium bull {{waypoint -133, -353, -454}}
   - Collect 6 Velium pine. These may be obtained as a body drop from [[A Ry'Gorr grunt|Ry'Gorr grunts]] or collected from logs found under the trees near the Ry'Gorrs.
   - Gather 6 Glacier shrub seeds found at the Daggerflow Glacier {{waypoint -692, -331, -79}}
1. Speak with [[Yakalo Spiriteye]] at {{waypoint -160, -407, -131}}
1. Return to [[Tamke Mudhoof]]

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +10,000 faction with **Tizmak Clan**
- [[Tizmak Drum]]
