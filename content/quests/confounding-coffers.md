## Notes

- ***Note:** This is a 5 minute timed quest!*
- It's best to clear the Keep before starting this quest.

## Steps

1. Destroy all five coffers of the same type as the one you clicked.
   - *Note: you need to click the coffers to make it damageable*
1. Destroy all 5 coffers of a second type
1. Destroy all 5 remaining coffers

The five coffer locations are:

- At the bottom of the stairwell leading up to the castle roof {{waypoint -14.42, 1.00, -22.45}}.
- In [[The Scourge Barracks]], distributed through the room {{waypoint 12.72, 2.22, -0.18}}.
- In [[The Glutton's Bliss]], at the end with the dais {{waypoint -5.79, 2.00, -0.79}}.
- In [[The Depleted Armory]] {{waypoint -6.74, 1.00, 19.94}}.
- In the [[Old Deathfist Stockpile]], distributed through the room {{waypoint 14.62, 16.00, 32.01}}, on the top floor of the southwest tower of the Keep.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 51g 20s 12,500 status
- [[Dirt encrusted key]]
