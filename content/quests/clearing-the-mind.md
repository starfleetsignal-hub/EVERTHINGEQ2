## Description

If there is to be any hope of cleansing the Eternal Prism, the terrors that currently lurk within must be dealt with. I must enter the area known as The Crucible, and confront as many of these nightmares as I can find. With luck, my efforts will help to cleanse the Prism.

## Steps

1. Destroy 15 creatures from within [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Crucible]]
1. Return to [[Chief Scholar Lakewind]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
