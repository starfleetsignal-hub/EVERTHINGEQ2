## Steps

![](images/A_collection_of_research_manuals.jpg)

1. Find 6 stolen research manuals.
   - These are found within and along the walls of the Gruengach cave including part of the cave with [[Xosith the Greedy]] 88^^ Heroic.
   - Entrance at {{waypoint 370, 275, 164}}. They respawn quickly and have a short viewing distance (depending on your graphics settings). The name shows from a good distance
   - Track Harvestables works on these ("collection of research manuals").
   - {{waypoint 224, 245, 213}}
   - {{waypoint 199, 239, 237}}
   - {{waypoint 188, 238, 295}}
   - {{waypoint 123, 238, 296}}
   - {{waypoint 233, 248, 135}}
   - {{waypoint 314, 227, 124}}
   - {{waypoint 324, 227, 115}}
   - {{waypoint 343, 227, 99}}
   - {{waypoint 351, 227, 81}}
1. Return to [[Ithurial Ra'Nar]].
