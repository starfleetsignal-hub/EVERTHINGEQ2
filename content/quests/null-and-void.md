## Notes

When you accept quest you receive [[Dazing Spore Bursts]] to use on voidcrust ravagers.

## Steps

![Psychic Energy Gem](images/Psychic_Energy_Gem.png)

1. Defeat 9 spore-dazed voidcrust ravagers found along [[Leech Scale Shore]] around {{waypoint -588.45, 15.43, 651.89}} all the way to {{waypoint -724, 18, 1002}}
   - Use [[Dazing Spore Bursts]] tool on them, then kill them for updates, some linked encounters will count as one update while some will count the full number.
1. Reach the [[Ginnungagap Spiral]] - go to small area with white crystal and 3 seers around it {{waypoint -599, 21, 1117}}
1. Defeat 3 voidmen seers
   - click and smash the floating void arcane stones around each seer to activate them, then kill them.
1. Obtain the Psychic Energy Gem - pick up the white spinning crystal in the middle of that area
1. Return to Oruri.
