- You must speak [[Gnomish]] to complete this quest.

1. Head to the Gnomish camp in [[Solusek's Eye]].
1. Speak to [[An expedition archivist (Solusek)|an expedition archivist]]. {{waypoint 23, 14, -43}}
1. Return to the Shrine of Thunder and speak to one of the researchers.
