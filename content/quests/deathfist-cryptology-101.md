## Steps

1. Find 4 Unkempt Stones (*a cursing stone*):
   - behind [[Titan's Tower]] {{waypoint -61, -3, -393}}
   - in [[The Charred Vale]] {{waypoint -423, -36, -396}}
   - near the [[Orc Outpost]] {{waypoint -312, -27, -133}}
   - [[Three Toes Valley]] {{waypoint 380, -20, -322}}
1. Interact with a desk in one of the tents north of the [[Bridge of Zek]] {{waypoint -217, -26, -615}}
1. Return to each of the unkempt stones **in the order listed above**. At each location, slay the orc cryptology team that spawns and attacks you.  These consist of two [[A Deathfist escort|Deathfist escort]]s and an orc cryptologist (in order, [[A Tallon cryptologist]], [[A Vallon cryptologist]], [[A Sullon cryptologist]], [[A Derris cryptologist]]).
1. Return to Lute
