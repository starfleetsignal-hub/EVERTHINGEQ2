## Steps

1. Speak with [[Emma Torque]] {{waypoint -20, 0, -40}} to receive a *[[Drawing Ray]]* device.
1. Collect the objects for the ritual
   - Kill five [[An albino python|albino pythons]] for scales {{waypoint 11, 37, 20}}
   - Harvest incantation dust ([[Ground spawn]]) from the Rockpaw Camp. {{waypoint 52, 38, -108}}
   - Obtain a river stone from [[A river behemoth]] {{waypoint 30, 37, -130}}.
     - Kill the river behemoth and its remains will pop.  Right click it and use the Drawing Ray, then left click the remains for the update (see discussion page).
1. Return to [[Consul Bree]] for your reward.
  - **Note**: You must hand in your [[Drawing Ray]]. You may want to keep the drawing ray as it is a fun little toy: when targeting something, activate the ray to find out what the target does.

## Rewards

- At least 14s 50c
- +175 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
