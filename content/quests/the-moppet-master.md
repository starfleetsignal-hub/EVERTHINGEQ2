## Notes

- The respawn time for each moppet is ~30 seconds, so if you are doing this quest in a group and the moppet does not reappear after a short period of time, it is in one of the other locations. Also, if you are doing this quest as a group, only one person can click on the moppet at a time, and only that person will get the update.
- If you kill the [[A deputy moppet|deputy moppet]] out of order it takes 15 minutes to re-spawn.

## Steps

1. Kill [[Grumblerutt]] inside the [[The Loom]] in [[Rivervale]]. -190 ,-19 -11
1. Hunt for a tailor moppet:
   - Inside Merchant Rosewood's store {{waypoint -114, -26, 11}}
   - Inside [[Cask's General Store]] {{waypoint -74, -18, 108}}
   - Behind [[Cask's General Store]] on the hill {{waypoint -70, 8, 166}}
1. Hunt for the fisherman moppet:
   - Inside [[The Loom]] in back room {{waypoint -192, -19, -13}}
   - Right outside [[Brushfoot's General Store]] {{waypoint -40, -10, -178}}
   - Northeast of [[Brushfoot's General Store]] behind a tree {{waypoint -144, 5, -234}}
1. Hunt for the farmer moppet:
   - Upstairs inside [[Middlehill's General Store]] {{waypoint -215, -7, -130}}
   - Inside [[Brushfoot's General Store]] behind the counter. [[A lamia assassin]] (40^^^H) is in here. {{waypoint -42, -9, -169}}
   - Inside the [[Jumjum Shack]] to the south of [[Brushfoot's General Store]] {{waypoint -16, -18, -102}} along with a pair of 37 heroic [[A corrupted brigand|corrupted brigands]]
1. Hunt for the mayor moppet:
   - Just west of the river among the tainted maidens {{waypoint -341, 10, 58}}
   - In [[Roundbelly's General Store]] in the little storeroom, guarded by 3 linked 37 ^ heroics. {{waypoint -200, -9, 105}}
   - To the south of [[Roundbelly's General Store]] among the roots of the stump house (Between the tree and the root above southern end of river) {{waypoint -200.30, 0.10, 222.13}}
   - Along the south wall amongst the corrupted halflings {{waypoint -68, 8.5, 165}}
1. Hunt for the [[A deputy moppet|deputy moppet]]:
   - Behind a tree at {{waypoint -224, -4, 111}}
   - Inside Merchant Rosewood's store {{waypoint -111, -27, 16}}
   - Behind [[Brushfoot's General Store]] {{waypoint -35, -11, -182}}
   - Next to a tree east of the waterfall on the northern border of the zone {{waypoint -235, 4, -213}}
1. Hunt for the [[A marshal moppet|marshal moppet]].  He's a 35^^-39^^ heroic with a direwolf friend of the same level.  He can be found running around the roads in Rivervale and frequently passes [[The Fools Gold]] {{waypoint -70, -17, 2}} and [[The Loom|Digg's Tall n'Stout]] (aka [[The Loom]]) on a very rough 10 minute cycle. {{waypoint -192, -19, -12}}
   - Starts his run at {{waypoint -403.65, 0.02, -116.33}}
   - Also spotted at {{waypoint -427, -1, 143}} and {{waypoint -419, 0, 213}}
   - Wait at /waypoint -182, 6, -180 and spotted crossing the bridge by the waterfall. Killed 30Nov2025
1. Return to [[Sir Tatters]] for your reward.
