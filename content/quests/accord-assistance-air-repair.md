## Steps

1. Gather (or buy) 50 redwood lumber for the airship repair efforts.
1. Return to [[Hophip Valorfeet]] and put the redwood lumber in the delivery basket next to him.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2000 faction with **Survival Accord**
