## Steps

1. Speak to [[Einva]] in the Great Library of Thurgadin, outside the city in [[Thurgadin Harbor]] {{waypoint 1058.45, -475.45, 503.13}}.
   1. ![Spiceleaf Bush](images/Spiceleaf_Bush.jpg)Gather 3 Spiceleaf **Bush**, just outside the north door of Thurgadin around the great waterfall at Dain's Last Stand.  {{waypoint 661.48, -190.72, 56.74}}<br>**Note:** *The quickest way to harvest these, is to go back into Thurgadin from the door you left, and go through city to north door and harvest outside. You need to harvest to update. Having them already, or purchasing them, does not update.*
   1. Make a [[Sheaf of spiceleaf paper]] for Einva at an engraved desk. The recipe is automatically scribed to your recipe book.
      - In addition to the Spiceleaf herbs, you'll need two [[Effulgent Incense|Effulgent Incenses]]; one now and one later.
1. Return to Einva in Great Divide. **Check your in-game mail for a letter to keep things progressing.**
1. Search the library for 3 reference books mentioning Maldura. A few possible locations:
   - {{waypoint 1072.47, -475.09, 528.80}}
   - {{waypoint 1097.34, -473.41, 500.08}}
   - {{waypoint 1080.67, -475.26, 499.18}}
   - {{waypoint 1062.36, -475.08, 518.66}}
   - {{waypoint 1085.87, -475.82, 490.84}}
   - {{waypoint 1068.22, -473.25, 522.67}}
   - {{waypoint 1057.29, -456.86, 513.09}} (upper floor of library)
   - {{waypoint 1065.50, -456.48, 522.03}} (upper floor of library)
   - {{waypoint 1088.97, -452.425, 507.357}} (upper floor of library)
     - *Note: (1) Unlike the books for the griffon quests, there are no name labels on the reference books. Mouse over the books until you locate clickable volumes.'
1. Transcribe the references into a [[Transcribed Notes on Maldura|notebook]] at an engraved desk.
   - *Note: Done by using the recipe "Transcribe Notes on Maldura" and an effulgent incense at the engraved desk next to Einva*
1. Return to Einva.

## Needed Materials

- [[Effulgent incense]] (2)
