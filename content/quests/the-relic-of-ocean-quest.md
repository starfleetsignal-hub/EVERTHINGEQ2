## Steps

1. Touch the [[Grove of Stones]]. {{waypoint 207, 14, 110}}
1. Kill 20 [[A cave adder|cave adders]] {{waypoint -491, 0, -73}}
   - ~5-minute respawn - there are about half a dozen at a time.
1. Return to the center of the [[Grove of Stones]].
