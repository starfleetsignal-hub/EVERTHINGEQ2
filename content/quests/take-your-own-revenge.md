## Steps

1. Speak to [[Skulker Ganiz]]. {{waypoint -455, 148, -869}}
1. Speak to [[Skulker Nazin]]. {{waypoint -459, 148, -880}}
1. Kill a [[A mistgoblin scout|mistgoblin]] ([[A mistgoblin warrior|any]] [[A mistgoblin shaman|type]]). {{waypoint -109, 129, -640}}
1. Click the bottle at Ganiz's feet. {{waypoint -460, 147, -887}}
1. Inspect the chest behind Ganiz's body.
