## Steps

1. Talk to [[Graldek Ironshadow]] at the [[Mushroom Bar and Grill]] in [[Maldura]] {{waypoint -53.96, -8.48, -1.21}}.
1. Zone into [[Maldura: District of Ash (Advanced Solo)]] {{waypoint -108.00, 4.90, -48.60}}. (*HEROIC* instance is confirmed as of 7/18/2023 to completely update the quest for all group members, including the pages as well as the [[Folded Parchment]] )
   - Gather 12 **Thalumbra cave maps** *(sheets of paper lying on the ground)*. ![Maldura mining map](images/Maldura_mining_map.jpg)
     - *Note: These will respawn, given enough time, if you aren't finding enough initially.*
   - Investigate the presence of the Dark Gears.
     - Clear the zone of powerful foes:
       - [[The Quarry Behemoth (District of Ash Solo)|The Quarry Behemoth]]
       - [[Tothrak (District of Ash Solo)|Tothrak]]
       - [[The Fire Gazer (District of Ash Solo)|The Fire Gazer]] *(Take the lift down from the Forge of Brell. To Spawn Stand at edge of lava, in the center. This will Spawn a Ring Event. Name Spawns at end of Ring Event)*
       - [[Gavitzle (District of Ash Solo)|Gavitzle]]
       - [[Gavitzle the Charbroiled (District of Ash Solo)|Gavitzle the Charbroiled]] and [[Cugdava the Changer (District of Ash Solo)|Cugdava the Changer]].
         - You receive a [[Folded Parchment]] after killing [[Gavitzle the Charbroiled (District of Ash Solo)|Gavitzle the Charbroiled]].

1. Return to [[Graldek Ironshadow]].
1. Confront [[Queen Alwenielle]] in the [[Palace of Unity]] {{waypoint 376.47, 85.85, -0.03}}.
1. Exit to [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]]
1. Good aligned return to [[Zeen Corblecog (Thalumbra)|Zeen Corblecog]] at {{waypoint 461.87, 95.73, 42.88}}. Evil aligned return to [[Virun D'Xyafin (Thalumbra)|Virun D'Xyafin]] at {{waypoint 470, 95, 66}}
   - (Good) Speak to **Queen Amree** via teleprojector.
   - (Evil) Your contact is missing! Speak with [[Zintra De'Oziana]] instead (activates when you get close).

## Rewards

- *At least 1p 35g 33394 status
- +1000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
- [[Polished Astral Umbrite Necklace]].
