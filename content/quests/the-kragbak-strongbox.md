## Steps

1. After completing A Couple Ringlets Missing, kill Kragbak's until you receive a Kragbak Ringlet.
1. Examine the Kragbak Ringlet
1. Locate the Kragbak Strongbox {{waypoint -164.14, 67.44, -267}}
1. Obtain ringlets to open strongbox
   - Find the first ringlet on [[A Kragbak channel lord|a channel lord]]. Channel lord spawns at the top of the kobold cliffs {{waypoint -165, 67, -265}} during the first wave of the Kobold Cliffs ring event
   - Find the second ringlet on a legendary Kragbak fighter. Kill [[The Klaw]] in the Ogrebane Arena ring event
   - Find the third ringlet on [[A Kragbak battler]] - they're common all over [[Keg Hold]]
   - Find the fourth ringlet on the Master of the Deadwatch. Kill [[Reverend Rutzkill]] during the final wave in the Kobold Cliffs ring event.
