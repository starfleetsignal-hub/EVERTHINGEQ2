## Steps

1. Kill [[An albino cave snake (Tombs of Night)|albino cave snake]] for 4 rare updates.
1. Kill [[A mist grinnin herbalist (Tombs of Night)|mist grinnin herbalists]] for 3 very rare updates.
1. Kill [[An Obdurate Parasite]] around {{waypoint -67, -24, -56}}
   - To spawn, kill [[A soul parasite (Tombs of Night)|soul parasites]] in the area. He moves around a bit after spawning at the location above, and floats pretty high up, so look around. If he does not spawn, zone out and in and try again. You do NOT need to kill every soul parasite in the zone.
