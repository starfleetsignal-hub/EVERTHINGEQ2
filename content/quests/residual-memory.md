## Steps

1. Kill Skirth gnashers  for 8 common updates on The Ethermist Shores {{waypoint 106, -44, -226}}
1. Speak with Nalisa Veldershin {{waypoint 33.54, 2.80, -46.17}}
1. Capture a [[Tirun brute]] {{waypoint 324, -42, -168}} using the [[Pacification Scroll]] given to you by Nalisa Veldershin.
   - Speak with a tirun brute
1. Return to Nalisa

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
