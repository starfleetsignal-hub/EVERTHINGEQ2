## Description

It's amazing what you can find down here in these catacombs. I found a bottle half full of Bloodsaber brew off a Bloodsaber proficient, and it's quite good. I think I'll try and get my hands on some more of this brew.

## Steps

1. Look for five more bottles of Bloodsaber Brew by killing [[A Bloodsaber proficient|Bloodsaber proficients]]. {{waypoint 91, 0, -165}}
