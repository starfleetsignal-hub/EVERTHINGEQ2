## Notes

- This is a mandatory sub-quest of [[A Mark of Awakening]].

## Steps

1. You must then find and harvest a fallen adventurer (2-minute respawn) in [[The Wordless Chamber]] at {{waypoint -3, -53, 270}}.
1. Return to the book. {{waypoint -20, 2, -97}}
1. Read the book on the bench again and choose 'The Keeper'. Follow the prompts until it says 'Skip to end'.
