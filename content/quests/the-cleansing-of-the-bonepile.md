## Notes

- You have 30 minutes to complete this quest. The timer starts as soon as a player steps into the proximity of [[An apprentice of the Dismal Rage]].
- If at any point you die or are unable to kill the mobs in the set amount of time they will respawn with their strength and/or numbers decreased.

## Steps

1. Defeat 10 [[Orc ghost|Orc ghosts]].
1. Defeat 15 [[Orc skeleton|Orc skeletons]].
1. Defeat 20 [[Orc zombie|Orc zombies]].
1. Slay [[Marrow, the Broken]].
1. Loot the treasure chest.
