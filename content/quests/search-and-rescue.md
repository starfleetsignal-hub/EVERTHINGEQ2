## Steps

1. Examine the bag, [[Collection of Goblin Items]], in your inventory.
1. Find someone who is interested in these goblin things
   - Speak with [[Glibnox]] at {{waypoint 132, -164, -768}} in [[Twark]] to receive [[Twark Transport Totem (Quest Item)|Twark Transport Totem]]
1. Go back to the [[Abandoned Mine Tunnel]] (near Grunt's Pass at {{waypoint -318, 48, 604}}) and enter.  Bring 32 thaumic coal with you for the sub-quests.
   - *Watch out for [[Greshdek]] during my travels.* This NPC is not in this zone or the cave, but found at the end of the final sub-quest for Brappa.
1. Enter the tunnel and follow the rails until they end, take the left fork, and click on the rocks at  {{waypoint -272, 57, -2016}}, where you zone into [[Fens of Nathsar]]
   - Complete all of the sub-quests from the 3 goblins ([[Irontooth]], [[Rumbletum]], and [[Brappa]]) at the [[Nurgan Mining Camp]]. A [[Potion of Progress]] can make this fairly painless. Get a quest from all three goblins and gather the required items. The items from the next quests will be in the same places. Use the [[Potion of Progress]] just before starting the first craft, and if you're quick at gathering for the other quests, it should last for all of them.
   - The quest, [[Temple Visitor]], will lead you to the top level of a temple in the zone, [[Nu'roga]].
     - *This is difficult, but not impossible if you can't handle the ~L73 local goblins. After doing 5 quests you should have enough faction to turn the goblins non-agro making things easier.*
1. **After** completing the [[Temple Visitor]] sub-quest for Brappa, use the [[Twark Transport Totem (Quest Item)|Twark Transport Totem]] while near to [[Greshdek]] in Nu'roga at {{waypoint -25, 155, -32}} which is at the top of the zone.
1. After you teleport back, speak to Glibnox

## Rewards

- [[Twark Transport Totem (QuestReward)|Twark Transport Totem]] *Find this in your inventory. It's extremely useful during the rest of the quest series and for daily/weekly quests.*
- [[Sovereign of Rile]] currency
- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +4,000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
