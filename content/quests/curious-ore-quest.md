## Steps

1. Gather 3 samples of [[Curious ore]] by mining nodes of the same name along a cliff across the water to the west of Tykor. {{waypoint -130, 2, 110}}
1. Return to Tykor

## Rewards

- At least 1s
- [[Reinforced Miners Sack]]
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Bodyguard's Sandals]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Evoker's Slippers]] (All Mages)
  - [[Marshall's Boots (red)|Marshall's Boots]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Pathfinder's Boots]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Spiritualist's Boots]] (Shamans)
  - [[Vicar's Boots]] (Clerics)
  - [[Windcaller's Boots]] (Druids, Channelers)
