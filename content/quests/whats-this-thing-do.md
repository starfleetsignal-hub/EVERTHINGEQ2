## Steps

1. Speak with [[Deputy Nettlebrine]] again to receive [[An Unusual Object]]
1. Examine [[An Unusual Object]]
1. Collect 3 pieces of [[An Unusual Object]] by killing [[A briarpaw cub|briarpaw cubs]] around {{waypoint 87, 21, -300}}
1. Examine [[An Unusual Object]]
1. Collect 5 leather straps by killing [[A briarpaw cub|briarpaw cubs]]
1. Examine [[An Unusual Object]]
1. Collect a magic-based bonding adhesive by killing [[A mischievous maiden|mischievous maidens]]
1. Examine [[An Unusual Object]]
1. Return to [[Deputy Nettlebrine]]

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Edged Obsidian Mace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Enchanted Jambiya of the Mist]] (All Mages)
  - [[Enchanted Sai]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Feral Runeblade]] (Druids)
  - [[Knuckles of Eradicating Strikes]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Mist-Rusted Stave]] (All Mages)
  - [[Obsidian Edged Shortblade]] (Crusaders, Warriors)

## Deprecated Rewards

- [[Mist-Rusted Wand]] (All Mages)

## Trivia

- The flavor text for this quest refers to rebuilding a katar - a reference to the original reward for this quest, [[Etheric Katar]].
