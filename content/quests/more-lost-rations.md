## Steps

1. Retrieve rations that were left behind by clicking on the three boxes on the ground at {{waypoint 282, 225, 3690}}.
   - Each member of the group must wait for the boxes to respawn (very short timer).
1. Return to Fazia. ![Some supplies were left behind by the looters](images/More_Lost_Rations_quest_-_ransacked_caravan.jpg)

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- [[Refulgent Gem of Fortitude]]
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**
