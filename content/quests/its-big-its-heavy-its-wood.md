## Steps

1. Gather 12 [[A treespirit log|treespirit logs]] from Treespirit Elementals.
   - The wood is gathered from Treespirit elementals, he will tell you a couple of locations for these but there are plenty near the Greater Faydark zone {{waypoint -537, -40, 363}}. Any type of treespirit will do.
1. Return to [[Tuck Longtoes]].

## Rewards

- At least 9g 27s 1c
- +100 faction with **Nybright Family**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Centurion's Short Blade]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Faydark Magi's Dragonhilted Dagger]] (All Mages)
  - [[Growth Dragon Hunter's Dagger]] (Bards, Predators, Rouges)
  - [[Katar of Laceration]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Maj'Dul Forged Hammer]] (All Priests)
  - [[Pronged Blade]] (Druids)
  - [[Warlock's Runic Sceptre]] (All Mages)
