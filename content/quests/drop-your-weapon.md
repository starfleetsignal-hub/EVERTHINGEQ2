## Steps

1. Gather 6 [[Rusty weapon|rusty weapons]], trackable starting around {{waypoint -0417, 16, -1741}}. ![rusty weapon](images/Rusty_weapon_visible.jpg)
1. Repair 3 weapons by crafting [[Repaired Stabby-Stick]] at the Goblin Forge in front of [[Irontooth]]. Each combine requuires 1 Thaumic coal.
1. Speak with [[Irontooth]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +3,000 faction with **Broken Toe Clan**
- +3,000 faction with **Goblins of Nurga**
