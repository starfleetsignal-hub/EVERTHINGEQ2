## Notes

A repeatable quest for Faction. Repeatable up to 3 times and only if your Port Woe faction is less than 20,000.

## Steps

- Harvest materials. These have to be harvested yourself while on this quest.
  - 30 x [[Ilmenite Ore]]
  - 30 x [[Larch Wood]]
  - 25 x  [[Fox Pelt]]
  - 30 x [[Sedge Root]]
- Obtain a rare [[Golden Sennegrass Root]] - harvest yourself from sturdy root nodes,  or obtain by any other means.
- Speak with [[Barsten]] at {{waypoint -76.55, 6.71, 363.03}}

## Rewards

- +1,000 faction with **Port Woe**
- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
