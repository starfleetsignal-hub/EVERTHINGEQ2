## Steps

![Humming rubble](images/Humming_Rubble.png)
![Humming rubble](images/Humming_rubble2.png)
![Forgotten Ward underneath Humming rubble](images/Forgotten_Ward.png)

1. Gather 4 old wards buried under rubble - these are spawned by removing [[Humming Rubble]] in [[Writher Ruins]]. (harvestable while flying)
   - *Look for small pieces that are more blue than native rocks in the area, they look like chunks that broke off larger structures that ruins are made of. Not all pieces of rubble will have wards underneath. If you see Forgotten Ward pick it up to update quest.*
   - Possible locations of rubble:
   - {{waypoint -374, 62, 469}}, {{waypoint -379, 65, 423}}, {{waypoint -411, 53, 411}}
   - {{waypoint -408, 63, 378}}, {{waypoint -412, 62, 355}}, {{waypoint -376, 57, 504}}
   - {{waypoint -398, 49, 510}}, {{waypoint -407, 39, 546}}, {{waypoint -435, 29, 557}}
   - {{waypoint -563, 11, 358}}, {{waypoint -566, 6, 241}}
1. Go back to Yon Gorroth obelisk and place wards in designated locations.
   - Ward 1 {{waypoint -624, -2, 407}}
   - Ward 2 {{waypoint -706, -2, 421}}
   - Ward 3 {{waypoint -666, -2, 469}}
   - Ward 4 {{waypoint -601, 0, 478}}
1. Return to [[Muurak]].

## Rewards

- 2 x [[Antique Darou Ward]]
- [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]

At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
