## Steps

1. Find and kill the Klixie called [[Yellowjacket]] to get back [[Ami Augren|Ami's]] doll.
1. Return the doll to [[Ami Augren]].

## Rewards

- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Elder's Crown]]
  - [[Redstone Loop]]
  - [[Golden Wraps]]
  - [[Steel Plated Shield]]
  - [[Dewdrop Trinket]]
