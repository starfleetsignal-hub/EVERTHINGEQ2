## Description

"Mentha Haora has been assigned the task of investigating the undead around the Combine Spires. As part of her studies, she would like me to collect some bone fragments from the scorched skeletons that congregate about the spires. I can find the spires just southeast of Spire Shadow Outpost." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Collect 10 scorched bone fragments by killing [[Scorched skeletal apprentice|scorched skeleton apprentices]] or [[Scorched skeletal wizard|scorched skeleton wizards]] at [[The Combine Spires]].
1. Return to [[Mentha Haora]]

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Scout's Hoop (Version 2)|Scout's Hoop]] (Scout)
  - [[Fighter's Hoop (Version 2)|Fighter's Hoop]] (Fighter)
  - [[Sorcerer's Hoop (Version 2)|Sorcerer's Hoop]] (Mage)
  - [[Priest's Hoop (Version 2)|Priest's Hoop]] (Priest)
