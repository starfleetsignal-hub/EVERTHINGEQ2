## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable
- To get to [[Withered Lands]] use Voyage through Norrath bell to go to Great Divide, Thurgadin dock. Then use New Combine Flightmaster on dock at {{waypoint 1278, -540, 304}} to fly to Withered Lands. Before you can use the horse travel system in the zone you need to manually click on horse at each station to unlock it. If you have them already unlocked it will help get closer to destinations used in quest.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Withered Lands]]

![](images/Screenshot_2025-10-22_224254.png)

1. Collect 3 samples from K'Val Ruins - small brown sparkling rocks on both sides of entrance to ruins around {{waypoint -3195, -735, -567}} and around {{waypoint -3320, -735, -596}}
1. Collect 3 samples from Queen's Hives - South - small brown sparkling rocks along rock walls around {{waypoint -2503, -550, -893}}
1. Collect 3 samples from Tears of Tunare - small brown sparkling rocks on edges of marsh, close to Holgresh Overlook station
   - possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -390, -744, -244}}
     - {{waypoint -371, -744, -286}}
     - {{waypoint -397, -744, -299}}
     - {{waypoint -366, -745, -349}}
     - {{waypoint -325, -745, -384}}
     - {{waypoint -408, -745, -396}}
     - {{waypoint -462, -745, -382}}
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]] in Sundered Frontier.
