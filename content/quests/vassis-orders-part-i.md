This is part 1 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Freeport]] who wear Leather and Chain Armor ([[Shaman|shaman]]).
Requires player level 20+.

## Steps

1. You must collect items from creatures in [[The Commonlands]].
   - Kill 18 [[A fallen guardian|fallen guardians]] for their weapons (near [[Ruins of Valmarr]] {{waypoint 441, -48, 294}}).
   - Kill [[Jereth Blackshield]], just northwest of Freeport griffon tower {{waypoint -391, -60, -879}}
1. Return to Vassi.

  - [[Handwraps of the Silent Fist Initiate]] - ([[Monk]])
