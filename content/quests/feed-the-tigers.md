## Notes

This quest is repeatable daily.

## Steps

1. Slay 7 [[A feathered vagabond|feathered vagabonds]] On the southeastern side of [[Kerra Isle]] near {{waypoint 1127.27, 13.21, 2293.72}}.
1. Toss the meat into [[The Tiger Pit]]. You do this by clicking on the floor of the pit. Note - you have to click on the floor of the pit while standing above the pit.
1. Return to Mik'roe.

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +1,000 faction with **Kerra Isle**
