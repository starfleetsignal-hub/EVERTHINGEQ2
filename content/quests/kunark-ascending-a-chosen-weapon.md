## Notes

- You need to have completed the [[Greenmist Timeline|Greenmist HQ Timeline]] to open the Chest with Recipe for "Greenmist Design Pattern"
  - *Note:when you finish the Signature quest line once (including sufficient faction standing with Nye'Caelona) you can buy the [[The Essential Kunark Guide]] for 100p from [[Sarya Val'Sara]] at {{waypoint -231, 91, -241}} and skip the Heritage Quest!* But the Greenmist proc will not function for your alt until the Heritage quest is complete.
- You need to be at least a level 2 [[Artisan]] to craft the weapon

## Steps

1. Enter [[Crypt of Dalnir: Baron's Workshop (Solo)]] {{waypoint 784.81, -128.84, -585.71}}. **(NOTE - if you are already 120, you MUST do this mentored or [[Timeless Chronomage|Chrono'd]] down to 100.  The sword & shield fight requires a chest drop to unlock the door, and will not drop if they're grey) (Note 2: If you can't escape by spell, bring a totem or other means of escape with you.  Hopefully you won't need it.)**
1. Obtain a weapon to prove Rile is Cazic-Thule's Chosen.![an Obulus wizard's mirror](images/An_Obulus_wizard's_mirror.jpg)
   1. Upon Entering the zone, click the right statue on the dais, to open the door on the right side.
   1. In the second hallway between chambers, there are two niches recessed into the wall on the right. They both contain Crypt Stalker spiders mobs, and a casket. (**If you go into a casket, you may get stuck.** *I was jumping around trying to get up and out of the right casket room and landed in the coffin. I had to buy a totem of escape from the broker to get out of it.)* In one of the caskets there is [[An Obulus wizard's mirror]] : You **MUST** obtain the mirror from that coffin after defeating all the Crypt Stalker spiders , else zone is just a [Heroic] instance, Dalnir has no dialog. {{waypoint -144, 31, 29}} In the other crypt, kill all the spiders and at the end of a mini-ring event, the coffin will open and the venomous queen will attack. You also need to break all jars in BOTH rooms, as one of them will drop a key needed to open doors later. To break-in to the crypt behind each niche, climb up to the alcove, target, and attack the wall. The mirror spawned inside the coffin itself for me. Be sure to break all the urns to open those as well.
      1. Need to kill Tootooz the Cryptomaniac for the walls to be clickable / killable
   1. [[Crypt of Dalnir: Baron's Workshop (Solo)#Steps:|Progress through the zone]] to Haggle Baron Dalnir (He is at the end of the dungeon.
   1. Hail Haggle Baron Dalnir to show him the mirror, then hail him again for conversation. He then attacks you; kill him. You are awarded Dalnir's Work chest Key
   1. Note: You can open both doors before killing [[Dalnir]] and pick up all the items *except* the recipe. You need Dalnir's Workchest Key to open the chest with the Greenmist Design Pattern.
   1. Pick up the following, crafting components found in *random* places within each room:
      - Recipe for "Greenmist Design Pattern" {{waypoint -280, 26, -441}} ![Dalnir's work chest (for recipe)](images/Dalnir's_work_chest.jpg)
      - Khukri blade mold (looks like a small rectangular jewelry box) {{waypoint -305, 28, -465}} ![khukri blade mold](images/Khukri_blade_mold.jpg)
      - 3 Imbued tynnonium on tables around {{waypoint -176, 28, -440}} ![imbued tynnonium (bar)](images/Imbued_tynnonium.jpg)
      - 2 Coal from one of the bins around the room ![a bin of infused coal](images/A_bin_of_infused_coal.jpg)
      - Fear soaked leather (looks like a small bag) {{waypoint -161, 26, -442}} ![bundle of fear-soaked leather](images/Bundle_of_fear-soaked_leather.jpg)
      - *Materials should be fresh not from a previous attempt at forging*
   1. Construct the khukri blade from one of the forges in the room Haggle Baron Dalnir was in to update the quest
1. Have the Greenmist Weapon blessed by fear, by talking to [[Xuurk]] in [[Outer Sebilis]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]]. {{waypoint -347, -116, 1009}}
1. Return to [[Hierophant Prime Jarrakas]].

- [[Obulus Wizard's Mirror]]

- 40289 status
- One of the following:
  - [[Dread's Visage of Greenmist]]
  - [[Fear's Visage of Greenmist]]
