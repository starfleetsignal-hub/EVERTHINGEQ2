## Steps

1. "Search for the suspicious group along the shore"  {{waypoint 255, 24, 312}} Enter "Mines of Meldrath: Hollowed Halls"
1. Battle through 8 Bloodhorns and clockworks
1. Defeat "a Darkblade Assassin"![](images/Under_the_Cover_of_Roughspun_Darkness_quest_-_Tinkered_Thalium_Plates.jpg)
1. Defeat 5 more Darkblades
1. "Search for the source of the frustrated mumblings" Speak to [[Mesmer Gearloose (Hollowed Halls)|Mesmer Gearloose]] at {{waypoint -115, -22, 177}}
1. Collect 8 **Tinkered Thalium Plates** (located throughout zone, they sparkle purple)
1. Return to Mesmer
1. Follow the drone to "a cracked wall" and break through the wall
1. Defeat [[Terrorhoof]]
1. Speak to [[Mesmer Gearloose (Hollowed Halls)|Mesmer Gearloose]]
1. Return to [[Physiker Tsaada Hopebringer]]
