## Notes on the Besieged Docks

- Zoning out and back in resets the zone.
- The mobs constantly respawn.
- The instance is based on pre-LU62 [[East Freeport]], but with the old citadel gate retitled to "West Freeport gate".
- Keep your group close together or distant members will not receive updates for clickable tasks.
- Waypoints for the interactive NPCs/objects can be found on the talk page.
- The entrance bell is located in [[Fens of Nathsar]].

## Steps

1. ![to Besieged Freeport Docks](images/Besieged_Freeport_Docks.jpg)Travel to the shores north of the [[Field of Bone]] (*Dragon's Rest sokokar post is closest*) and click the bell to zone into a special instance of pre-LU62 East Freeport {{waypoint -1820, -400, -2433}} called [[Besieged Freeport Docks]]
1. Kill 10 [[A Freeport defender|Freeport defenders]] (level 78^)
1. ![Iksar Healing Tonic](images/Iksar_Healing_Tonic.jpg)Heal 6 wounded crusaders (NPCs) using [[An iksar healing tonic]] acquired from a crate on the dock next to [[Xuurk (Besieged Freeport Docks)|Xuurk]] {{waypoint -302, -56, -76}}
1. Speak to Xuurk
1. Seal off the gates:
   - North Freeport gate: move the four barrels {{waypoint -186, -56, -109}} up against the gate
   - ![Moveable Barrel](images/Moveable_Barrel.jpg)![Refuse Pile](images/Refuse_Pile.jpg)West Freeport gate: move the pile of refuse {{waypoint -51, -21, 90}} to in front of the gate
   - ![Moveable Cart](images/Moveable_Cart.jpg)South Freeport gate: move the cart {{waypoint -183, -57, 105}} to in front of the gate
1. Speak to [[Quartermaster Hedoral (Besieged Freeport Docks)|Quartermaster Hedoral]] on the dock {{waypoint -290, -56, -82}}
1. Spread confusion in the enemies forces:
   - ![a powderkeg ("attack" to ignite)](images/A_powderkeg.jpg)Destroy 5 [[A powderkeg|powderkegs]] around the upper plaza
     - If you zone out while on this step but before blowing up the barrels they will not be up when you return. Speak to the Quartermaster again to respawn them
   - ![destroy supplies](images/Destroy_supplies.jpg)Destroy 5 supply stockpiles on the southern dock (approx loc {{waypoint -203.83, -56.07, 49.65}}
   - Speak to 5 confused iksar citizens
1. Return to Xuurk
1. Kill [[Dreadnaught Blackengrit]], a level 80^^^. Hits hard, is immune to root/mez/stun, but has little HP
   - If he was up when you reached this step, he will despawn and respawn on the hill road, and path back down to the dock
1. Use the bell near the ship to zone back out to the Fens near the Lost Temple
   - ***Note:** "Fast Travel" Will not update the quest, you need to use the bell
1. Return to [[Xuurk]] in the [[Lost Temple of Cabilis]]
