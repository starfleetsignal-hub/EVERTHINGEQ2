## Description

The Craftkeepers Task Coordinator has requested assistance to deliver some essential supplies to the [[Moonfield Hamlet (POI)|Moonfield Hamlet]] outpost in the northern part of the continent.

## Steps

1. The supplies destined for Moonfield Hamlet can be collected from beside Oolioomon in Paineel Commons.
   - Collect the supplies for delivery from the crate titled *Supplies for Moonfield Hamlet* which is directly next to [[Oolioomon]].
   - [[Moonfield Hamlet (POI)|Moonfield Hamlet]]  is located in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]]. You can reach the highlands via transport from the Toxxulia Forest launch pad. (Paineel Launch Pad -> Toxxulia Forest -> Stonebrunt Highlands -> Moonfield Hamlet)
1. Deliver the supplies to Moonfield Hamlet. The depot (*Moonfield Hamlet Supply Depot*) is next to banker [[Mueri]] at {{waypoint 212, 413, 1258}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +2,500 faction with **Craftkeepers**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Craftkeepers Daily Supply Tasks
