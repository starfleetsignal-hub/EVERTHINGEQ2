## Steps

1. Scribe the [[Traditional Othmir Drum Recipe]] given to you by Arjon.
1. Craft a [[Traditional Othmir Drum]].
   - You will need 3 [[Sumac lumber]], 1 [[Titanium ore]], and 1 [[Ethereal sandpaper]]
   - You can use the [[Woodworking Table]] located behind [[Matthew]] {{waypoint -1642, -533, -2380}}
1. Deliver the drum to [[Finnegan]] at {{waypoint -1252.69, -538.60, -2197.02}}.
1. Return to [[Arjon]] {{waypoint -1378.59, -533.50, -2143.70}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
