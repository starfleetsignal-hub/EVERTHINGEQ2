## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Collect 5 goblin ears, from goblins, northwest of [[Deputy Stoutgut]] {{waypoint 163, 12, -555}}
     - [[A Runnyeye scout|Runnyeye scouts]], [[A demented Runnyeye medic|demented Runnyeye medics]], etc...
   - Collect 1 spidersilk thread, from [[An elusive hatecrawler|elusive hatecrawlers]], west of [[Deputy Stoutgut]] {{waypoint 271, 5, -413}}
   - Collect 2 lasher hooks, from [[A suffering lasher|suffering lashers]], southwest of [[Deputy Stoutgut]] {{waypoint 364, -1, -406}}
   - Collect 1 briarpaw claw, from Briarpaws, southeast of [[Deputy Stoutgut]] {{waypoint 90, 23, -325}}

1. Return to [[Deputy Stoutgut]]

## Rewards

- At least 27s 67c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Bow of the Lost Shadowknight]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Enchanted Pouch]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Enchanted Wand of Divinity]] (All Priests)
  - [[Halfling Crafted Short Bow]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Staff of the Swanheart]] (All Mages)
