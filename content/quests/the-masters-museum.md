## Steps

![Cyprus needles are harvested from a small clump of weeds](images/A_small_clump_of_weeds.jpg)

1. Collect one of the following two collections of items, assigned randomly:
   - Collection 1:
     - A 'Flawless pelt' from [[Great sandstalker sabertooth]] in [[The Pillars of Flame]]. {{waypoint 863, -118, -860}}
     - An 'Impeccable Windsister headdress' from any [[Windsisters (Faction)|Windsister harpy mob]] in [[The Pillars of Flame]]. {{waypoint 426, -134, -1357}}
     - A 'Cyprus needle', harvested from *a small clump of weeds*, which spawns randomly in the area around [[The Great Bonsai]] in [[The Pillars of Flame]]. {{waypoint 156, -190, -1678}} or {{waypoint 332, -157, -1651}}
       - Spawn time is approx. 4 minutes, but can switch between each tree.
       - Trackable using the skill [[Track Harvestables]].
       - May be coincidence, but the weeds did not show up until I harvested another node (was a beast den) in the area.
   - Collection 2:
     - The 'Broken Shackle' of [[Tan'Ke Rei, the Pillar Guardian]] in the SW corner of [[The Pillars of Flame]], not far from the carpet in [[The Giant's Field]]. {{waypoint 1583, -77, -75}}
     - A 'Polished Sandscrawler Crystal' from any type of Sandscrawler Goblin in [[The Pillars of Flame]]. {{waypoint 379.36, -47.58, -864.13}} (Sandcrawlers in [[The Sinking Sands]] also work)
     - A 'Perfectly Preserved Kromtorr Magi Turban' from [[Kromtorr magi]] in the [[The Pillars of Flame]]. {{waypoint 1178, -79, -575}}
1. Return to [[Kedar the Curator]].
