## Steps

1. You must find and collect four scrolls on-board [[The Vigilant: Incursion|The Vigilant]].
   - Two scrolls are located in vases at {{waypoint -27, 52, 34}}
   - The other two scrolls are located at {{waypoint 27, 52, 34}}
1. Return the scrolls to [[Amtul'lah Jad|Amtul'lah]].
