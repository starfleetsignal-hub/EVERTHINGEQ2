This looks like a tiny scabbard made for a very small sword. I would almost wonder if it was made for some sort of doll. I feel oddly compelled to find out more about it, while on the dark side of this moon.

## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill 15 zelmie [[A zelmie commoner|commoners]], [[A zelmie enforcer|enforcers]], [[A mounted zelmie enforcer|mounted enforcers]],  [[A zelmie guard|guards]], or [[A zelmie ranger|rangers]] at [[Zel Manann]] around {{waypoint 709.40, 17.93, -448.62}} in [[Echo Caverns]].
