## Steps

1. Speak to Galen Cadabrak at {{waypoint -460, 15, 84}} in Obol Plains to begin this quest.
   1. Investigate the westernmost side of the Tourbillion. {{waypoint -585, -3, 37}}
   1. Investigate the southernmost side of the Tourbillion. {{waypoint -649, -0.39, 94}}
   1. Kill the attacking Horde and you'll receive [[Tourbillion Segments]].
   1. Return to [[Galen Cadabrak]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
