## Notes

This is the Craftsman version of a quest in the [[Artisan Epic Timeline]].

- This instance is **NOT** *puzzled*, one can access all rooms without difficulty.
- The rats inside will scale to be 10 levels above your level. You need to complete a sub-quest [[Building a Better Rat Trap]] to bypass them. Or, if you are level 100 you can just kill them and not bother with the sub-quest. (The sub-quest, itself, awards a decent chunk of Tradeskill XP, ironically, larger than the parent quest does - this makes it a quick and painless source of tradeskill XP for something you'd likely already be doing in the zone and only adds 45-60 extra seconds to the run)
  - The rats do not see through Ranger Stealth or Invis of [[Totem of the Endless Void]]
  - To get past them for the sub-quest, keep close to the walls. You can duck into the hall to the left to wait for the right moment to dodge.
  - Even though they are 10 levels above you, the rats are easy kills once you're geared up at around level 100 or above.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Nektropos Castle]]. Click the door and choose to enter the [[Nektropos Castle: Craftsman Errands]] version of the instance.
   - Complete the sub-quest [[Building a Better Rat Trap]] in order to evade the rats.
   - You can kill only one rat per trap use (after you make the traps) but you have 20, so use them liberally.
1. Collect the following
   - Search the pantries and dining halls for a bottle of demi-sec champagne. ![bottle of demi-sec champagne](images/Bottle_of_demi-sec_champagne.jpg)
     - *The champagne is on the table in the dining hall at {{waypoint 32, 1, -18}}.*
     - *There are three rats in the dining room. Two are near the entrance, and one is inside to the right (be careful when entering).*
   - Investigate the larders and pantries for a bucket of fish.![bucket of fish](images/Bucket_of_fish.jpg)
     - *The fish may be found in the storeroom north of the kitchen at {{waypoint 37, 0, -52}}.*
     - *There are two rats immediately behind the door to the storeroom. Don't stand too close to the door when you open it.*
   - Search the cupboards and storerooms for a picnic basket.![picnic basket](images/Picnic_basket.jpg)
     - *The picnic basket is in the storeroom north of the kitchen at {{waypoint 22, 0, -69}}.*
     - *There are three rats in here, with one very close to the door. Don't stand too close when opening it.*
1. Return to [[Neeta Cabbageleaf]] for your reward.
