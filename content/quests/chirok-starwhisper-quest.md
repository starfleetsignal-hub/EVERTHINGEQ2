## Steps

1. Find and speak to [[Chirok Starwhisper (NPC)|Chirok Starwhisper]] on the opposite side of [[Prisoner's Isle]]. You will have to avoid heroic mobs to get there.
1. Slay [[A Blacktalon guard|Blacktalon guards]] (level 65) until you get a uncommon quest update.
1. Speak to [[Chirok Starwhisper (NPC)|Chirok Starwhisper]] again to set him free.
1. Return to [[Ahnjun Sprywing]] for your reward.

## Reward

- At least 13g 59s 50c
- +125 faction with **The Windgazer**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Woven Veil of the Heavenly Mage]] (All Mages)
  - [[Woven Veil of the Sky Warrior]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Woven Veil of the Skystalker]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Woven Veil of the Winds]] (All Priests)
