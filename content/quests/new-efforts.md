![](images/Partially_chewed_ore.jpg)

- Collect 10 [[An ebon drake|ebon drakes]] flame sacs (common update). {{waypoint -928, 98, 1370}}
- Collect 5 samples of the ore that the drakes eat called 'partially chewed ore'.
- Return to [[Plinka Fiddle]] for your reward.
