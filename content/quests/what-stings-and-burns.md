## Notes

- Go to the [[North Wing Stacks]] on the first floor of the Library, then take the teleporter at {{waypoint -259, -20, -284}} to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]] and enter [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]] through the portal at {{waypoint 728, 412, -336}}.

## Steps

1. obtain the following:
   - 8 fire wasp eggs in the hive dwellings in the [[Cinder Nests]] near {{waypoint 134, 59, 10}}. They are all over the map in the dwellings, not just the mentioned location.
     - They look like small spikey dull yellowish clumps inside the hive house structures. They are NOT the yellow clumps on the rock dishes on the ground.![fire wasp eggs, Click to Enlarge](images/Firewasp_eggs.png)
   - 6 fire wasp larva chitin - These guys seem to randomly spawn when you loot the fire wasp eggs.
1. Examine the [[Arcane Tome Paper]] in your inventory and choose **Concentrate on the paper**.
1. Speak with [[The Unbound Maladjusted Codex]] that spawns when you examine the paper.

- [[Chain Of Molten Beads]] -- *collection item*
