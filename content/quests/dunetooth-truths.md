## Steps

1. Click twice on a log at the southern edge of [[Petrous Woods]] {{waypoint -552.18, 82.94, -204.25}}
   - [[A sullen Dunetooth fiend]] will spawn.
1. Return to [[Dramon Gontalor]].
1. Hunt Dunetooth goblins ([[A sullen Dunetooth fiend|sullen Dunetooth fiends]] and [[A vexed Dunetooth savage|vexed Dunetooth savages]]) around {{waypoint -757.34, 235.92, -105.21}} for trinkets.
1. Return to [[Dramon Gontalor]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Spellwoven Dendromail Helm]] -- *Chain*
  - [[Spellwoven Viridsilk Hood]] -- *Cloth*
  - [[Spellwoven Florahide Sallet]] -- *Leather*
  - [[Spellwoven Chloroplate Greathelm]] -- *Plate*
