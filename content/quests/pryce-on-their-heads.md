## Steps

1. Search the [[City of Fordel Midst]] for vampyres of the Coterie of the Eternal Night.
   - *Vampyres spawn throughout the city. There are several in the Bazaar and around the main city area. Also found in Shadow Haven. Be sure to check all the connecting halls.  Trackable as **a vampyre**.*
   - Possible locations in [[Shadow Haven]] include:-
     - {{waypoint -629.56, 2.76, -337.47}}
     - {{waypoint -768.90, -1.33, -14.61}}
     - {{waypoint -765.57, 1.84, -193.65}}
     - {{waypoint -616.75, 1.84, -174.24}}
     - -551, 1, -189
     - -585, -1, -116
   - Possible locations in [[The Bazaar]] include:-
     - {{waypoint -46.46, -1.76, 120.99}}
     - {{waypoint 326.00, -0.83, 154.49}}
     - {{waypoint 84.75, -1.76, -110.93}}
     - {{waypoint 114.46, -1.52, -52.30}}
     - {{waypoint -180.77, -2.75, -99.73}}

1. Slay 10 vampires.
1. Return to Thaddaeus Pryce.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s
- One of the following:
  - [[Vampyre Spike]]
  - [[Ancient Midst Wand]]
