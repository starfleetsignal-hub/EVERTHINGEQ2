## Steps

1. You must first gather Misty Thicket shadeblooms. They will appear all over the shire as a harvestable flower.
1. After collecting 3 shadeblooms, speak with [[Maela Meadows]].
1. Return to [[Rondo "The Belly" Roundstew|Rondo]].

## Rewards

- [[A Shadebloom Bouquet]]
- [[Gardener's Pouch]]
