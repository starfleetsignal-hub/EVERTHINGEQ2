## Note

This quest is only available to tinkering skill of 400 and over.
If you are returning to this questline, you will need the following materials to complete it:

- 4 [[Ethereal coal]]
- 3 [[Amber]]
- 1 [[Frostbitten parsnip]]
- 1 [[Liquid]]
- 3 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
- 4 [[Titanium ore]]

## Steps

1. Scribe [[Blueprint: Advanced Beasty Warning System]].
1. Craft Advanced Beasty Warning System at the work bench
   - You will need 1 [[Titanium ore]], 1 [[Quicksilver cluster]], 1 [[Amber]] and 1 [[Ethereal coal]].
1. Place the System near [[Captain Bin Nalot]]. You'll find a green shiny at {{waypoint 1294, -540, 411}}
1. Return to Fenwick

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +2,000 faction with **Iceclad Pirates**
