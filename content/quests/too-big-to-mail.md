## Steps

1. I need to take the package of [[Geothermic Equipment]] and deliver it to Jinan Haffah {{waypoint -141, 366, 707}} in Quel'ule northeast of Moonfield Hamlet, past Aglthin Pond.
   - Click the crate of Geothermic Equipment behind Alwar Tissuyr to pick it up.
   - Travel to [[Quel'ule]].  There's a Transport Station not far from Alwar.
   - Speak with [[Jinan Haffah]] {{waypoint -140, 366, 708}} to complete the quest.
