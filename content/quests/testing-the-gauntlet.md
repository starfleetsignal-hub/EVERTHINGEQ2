## Steps

1. Go to [[The Barracks of Caroeth]] in [[Chardok]] and slay the following mobs: {{waypoint 161, -59, 107}}
   - 8 [[A Caroeth vanquisher|Caroeth vanquishers]]
   - 4 [[A Caroeth ridgeguard assassin|Caroeth ridgeguard assassins]]
     - These start as NPCs on the edge of the cliffs overlooking the outside barracks area. Approach the side of the cliff underneath them and they will jump down.
   - 4 [[An imperial golem|imperial golems]]
   - 2 [[An enchanted barricade|enchanted barricades]] (further up past the red crystal gate)
   - 1 [[An imperial rampart]] {{waypoint 308, -30, 203}}
1. Return to [[Battle Master Dolak]] in [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]].
