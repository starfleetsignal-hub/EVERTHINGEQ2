## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Tuber Basket for my apprentice.
   - Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Tuber Basket]]

     - 2 [[Tuber Strand|Tuber Strands]]
     - 4 [[Glowing Sandpaper]]
1. I need to take this basket back to my apprentice.
