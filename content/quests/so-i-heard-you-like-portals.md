## Prerequisites

- Mages must be at least level 40 to be offered this quest.

## Steps

1. In [[The Elddar Grove (POI)]], travel to the gate leading to the [[The Baubbleshire: Vale's Shadow|Baubbleshire]] and zone in. {{waypoint 816, -21, -530}}
   - Slay 5 [[A nightblood warper|nightblood warpers]]. Right-click a portal to summon the [[A nightblood warper|nightblood warpers]] that are opening the portals.
   - Close 5 demonic portals by right-clicking them again after the [[A nightblood warper|nightblood warpers]] have been slain.
1. Travel to the gate leading to the [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum|Starcrest Commune]] in [[South Qeynos]] and zone in. {{waypoint 695, -21, 366}}
1. Close 4 demonic portals by standing in the green aura and activating the [[A necrotic focus|necrotic focus]].
   - {{waypoint 734, -20, 307}}
   - {{waypoint 752, -20, 275}}
   - {{waypoint 779, -20, 280}}
   - {{waypoint 809, -25, 321}}
1. Return to [[Bellengere the Three (Qeynos Capitol District)|Bellengere the Three]].
1. Return to the gate leading to the [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum|Starcrest Commune]] and zone in.
1. Right click the fountain at {{waypoint 769, -20, 281}} to use the necromantic relic. Unfortunately, this doesn't work as intended and you will have to fight off three or four waves of undead.
1. Return to [[Bellengere the Three (Qeynos Capitol District)|Bellengere the Three]].

## Rewards

- [[Banner of Elddar]]
- +1000 faction with **The Concordium**
