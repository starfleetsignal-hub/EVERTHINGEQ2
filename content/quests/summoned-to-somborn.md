In-game mail reads:

**An opportunity has presented itself in Somborn, the village in Loping Plains, where someone has done the remarkable by opening a usable portal between Norrath and a particularly interesting place in Ultera, steeped in magic and experimentation. Now, where they have opened a door, we can follow--but we must be quick! However shrouded their goals may be, their intrusion will be obvious, and it is sure to gain attention. This is a mission of distruption, elimination, and a bit of acquisition. I trust you are up to it?**<br>
**I await your arrival.**<br>
**Dr. Arcana**

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Village of Somborn]], [[Loping Plains]] at {{waypoint -346.02, 13.05, -16.61}} (Quest updates automatically on arrival)
1. Search and find [[Dr. Arcana]] at {{waypoint -313.53, 14.69, -2.14}} and speak to him.
