## Notes

- You receive [[Glorious Maestra's Cithara]] when you start the quest
- Duoed this instance tonight (12/5), both toons progressed, neither being stuck as previously warned. Leaving warning text in place, in case it comes back.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Vaashkaani: Alcazar Crescendo]].
   1. Make my way to the Djinn Sovereign's throne
      1. Kill Khazinehdar Zuhraasa {{waypoint 621, 71, -0.5}} -- use HO's to debuff
      1. Take the portal to Sovereigns Hold {{waypoint 622, 70, -0}}
      1. kill trash
      1. then use the carpet at {{waypoint 888, 70, -0}} to the center platform
   1. The Djinn Sovereign must be stopped to access the Virtuosa's Prison
      - Kill [[Zakir-Sar-Ussur]] the Djinn Sovereign
      - use the [[Glorious Maestra's Cithara]] to make him vulnerable.
      - *(Note: He does not attack you. This is a scripted story event that cannot be failed.)*
      - start attacking him till he says "It will magnify Nostrolo's song! A legion of maedjinn will soon spill forth from their wroughtlands, and all the planes will have you to thank!" then use the [[Glorious Maestra's Cithara]] and DPS him down.
   1. Enter the Virtuosa's Prison
      1. Take the portal at {{waypoint 848, 70, -14}} to [[Vaashkaani Bastille]]
   1. Speak to [[Lyrissa Nostrolo]]  {{waypoint 375, 23, -356}}. group update
   1. Set the arca down {{waypoint 383, 23, -355}}. group update
   1. Speak to [[Lyrissa Nostrolo]] again. group update
   1. Defend her from attacking djinn.
      1. defeat the attacking djinn
      1. [[Kapuji-bashi Haakhaz]] will spawn, defeat him as well
   1. Speak to [[Lyrissa Nostrolo]] again. group update
   1. Speak to the Allyrians
      1. [[Helalli]], [[Chandrima]], and [[Vahravi]] will spawn. Speak to Vahravi. group update
      1. After a discussion, everyone will disappear.
1. Take the portal at {{waypoint 367, 20, -335}} to leave the zone and return to [[Miranna Ameema]] in [[Zimara Breadth]] on the [[Isles of Wisdom]].
