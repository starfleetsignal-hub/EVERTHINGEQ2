## Notes

- The water is deep! Watch your breath or bring a water-breathing bauble like [[Totem of the Otter]] or [[Hadden's Earring]]!

## Steps

1. Continue to loot clam shells until you find 3 separate treasures:
   - [[Vordan Earring]]
   - [[Vordan Necklace]]
   - [[Message in an Ancient Bottle]]
1. Examine the Ancient Bottle to read the message which directs you to one of NPCs listed:
   - [[Landar Longley]] in [[Kylong Plains]]
   - [[Marrabal Zharan]] in [[Timorous Deep]] at {{waypoint 2292, 0, 709}}
   - [[Dissignar Razehl]]  on the beach in the Nursery of Greater Faydark.
1. Talk to them to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- 3 x [[Platinum Jubilation Medal]]
- [[Sunken Treasure Chest]]
