Scales to crafting level

## Steps

1. [[Catalogue Creature|Catalogue]] the list of creatures for [[Mug (Moors of Ykesha)|Mug]]
   - [[A blood gorger (Moors of Ykesha)|A blood gorger]] near [[The Overlook]] or [[Firmroot Moot]]
   - [[An Innothule swamp feeder]] near [[Firmroot Moot]]
   - [[A swamp sokokar]] near [[Firmroot Moot]]
   - A [[Monkey]] near [[Firmroot Moot]]
   - [[A fauna stalker]] near [[Firmroot Moot]]
   - An [[Escaped baby gator]] near [[Firmroot Moot]]
   - [[A Nuknok gorer]] near the [[Boarfiend Caves]] above [[Firmroot Moot]]
   - [[A meadow glider]] near the [[Shadowed Cleft]]
   - A [[Vile strider]] near the [[Shadowed Cleft]]
   - [[Brokenskull striker]] near the [[Shadowed Cleft]]
1. Upon [[Catalogue Creature|Cataloguing]] the last creature, you will receive your reward

## Rewards

- Tradeskill XP
- At least 1p 15g
- +5000 faction with **Survival Accord**
