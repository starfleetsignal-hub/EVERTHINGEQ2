## Notes

**Important: on the step to save 12 Norrathians - make sure you save all 12 of them before you get to Molderbeard. Norrathians do respawn so go back and look for more if needed.**

## Named mobs

Progress through the instance killing along the way:

  - Fungoid Alpha:  {{waypoint -1053, -40, 1075}}
  - Death Spark:   {{waypoint -939, -20, 945}} . Use stifle immunity rune in your belt.
  - Goligok:  {{waypoint -850, 1, 783}} .  Use stun immunity rune in your belt, after you kill him a barrier you passed earlier at {{waypoint -1130, -40, 1016}} will open.
  - Molderbeard:  {{waypoint -1025, 27, 1057}}.  Clear the green mushroom mobs around him, they buff him and he will kill the whole group. Intercept (Tank) required during the fight.  Use stun immunity rune in your belt, save 12 Norrathians BEFORE you kill him. Resets if you take him too far away from spawn point. Has knockback.
  - Derlak:   {{waypoint -881, 14, 1016}}.

![example of a hunted Norrathian you need to "Save"](images/Example_of_a_hunted_Norrathian.png)

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Unknown: Sacrificial Pursuits (Solo)]]  Portal at {{waypoint -385, 3, 101}}
1. Speak to Derlak - he will run off to deal with Molderbeard, leaving you to save the Norrathians.
   - Save 12 Norrathians - there are hiding Norrathians you have to click and Save, non trackable, but look in tall reddish grasses in areas where you see corpse on the ground. Tab key should also pick them up as your target.
     - Possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -994.19, -38.03, 793.54}}
     - {{waypoint -1064.24, -29.01, 894.41}}
     - {{waypoint -1049.26, -39.15, 1051.44}}
     - {{waypoint -1127.36, -36.72, 1058.56}}
     - {{waypoint -1139.04, -38.85, 1010.31}}
     - {{waypoint -1122.17, -35.82, 923.60}}
     - {{waypoint -891.55, -16.73, 852.13}}
   - Defeat Molderbeard at {{waypoint -1023, 26, 1044}}
1. Defeat Death Spark {{waypoint -941.30, -20.84, 932.10}}
1. Defeat the Unknown Betrayer
   - *Note: Make sure you get all 12 Norrathians first or you'll have to repeat the zone*
   - Derlak at {{waypoint -882, 14, 1015}}
     - *Quest complets when you kill Derlak, leave through portal that spawns where he stood.*
     - {{waypoint -855.87, -48.36, 939.52}}
