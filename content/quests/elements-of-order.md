## Steps

1. obtain the following
   - Obtain 10 **sand-polished demantoid** (uncommon drop) from [[A sandstone walker|sandstone walkers]] at [[Saltscorch Steppes]] around {{waypoint -60, 68, -605}}  ![](images/Elements_of_Order_quest_-_Crystallized_Sulfur.jpg)
   - Obtain 16 **Crystallized Sulfur** from [[A flame of devotion|flames of devotion]] at [[Rath'Mana]] {{waypoint 434.51, 115.61, -463.81}}
     - *Each flame killed drops two sulfur crystals, which must then be harvested.*
1. Return to [[Ehasbehas]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- One of the following:
  - [[Rune-Carved Prowler Fang]]
  - [[Night Metal Thurible]]
