I must enter Vasty Deep: The Abandoned Labs and kill the empowered terrors and unfathomable horrors that haunt its halls. I must also slay Homunculis.

## Steps

   - Kill 4 [[An unfathomable horror|unfathomable horror]]s

1. Return to Alia
