## Notes

- See [[The Fabled Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors (Solo)]] for a zone walkthrough.

## Steps

1. Take the ghoul to school.
   - Slay [[Furdip (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|Furdip]], the Frenzied Ghoul.
1. Overthrow the king.
   - Slay [[King Vroak Vriipp (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|King Vroak Vriipp]].
