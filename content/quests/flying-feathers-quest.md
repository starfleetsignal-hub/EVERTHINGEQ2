## Steps

1. Find [[Raffik]] in [[Great Divide]] at [[Goahmari Village]] at {{waypoint 306, -15, 514}}
1. Pick up 5 Gryphon Research books on both floors of the Thurgadin Harbor Library at {{waypoint 1088, -456, 506}} One can use tradeskill `Track Harvestables´ to find the books quickly or just mouse-over the shelves.
1. Scribe the [[Essence of the Zephyr (Crafted)|Essence of the Zephyr]] from the recipe given.  The required ingredients:
   - 1 [[Plump lychee]],
   - 2 [[Matoppie roots]],
   - 1 [[Aduki bean]],
   - 1 [[Ethereal Candle]]
1. Craft on a [[Chemistry Table]]. The nearest [[Chemistry Table]] is on the top level of the library at the far end. {{waypoint 1234, -456, 651}}
1. Return to [[Raffik]] ![Gryphon Research](images/Gryphon_Research.jpg)

## Rewards

- At least 13p 10g
- [[Essence of the Zephyr (QuestReward)|Essence of the Zephyr]] Recipe Book
