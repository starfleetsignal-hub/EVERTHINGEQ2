## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[A cave shimmer|cave shimmer]]s. {{waypoint -61, -18, 21}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 8 [[A Sabretooth guard|Sabretooth guard]]s. {{waypoint -21, -12, -6}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 [[A Sabertooth tactician|Sabertooth tactician]]s. *(multiple locations)*
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 [[A Sabertooth oracle (Blackburrow)|Sabertooth oracles]]. {{waypoint -61, -5, 65}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 [[A stalwart Sabertooth|stalwart Sabertooth]]s. {{waypoint -48, -18, 12}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 [[A blightfang spider|blightfang spider]]s ([[A blightfang broodtender|blightfang broodtenders]] and [[A blightfang hatchling|blightfang hatchlings]] also update), found on level three. {{waypoint -19, -36, -35}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 7 blightfang spiders.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 blightfang spiders.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 blightfang spiders.
1. Read the book.
