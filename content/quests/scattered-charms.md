## Starting the Quest

- [[Qeynos]] or [[The City of Freeport]]

## Steps

1. Harvest purple shinies in Qeynos & Freeport until you pick up an [[Ethernaut Charm Bracelet]]. Examine it and accept the quest.
1. Continue harvesting purple shinies until you get an [[Ironstein Charm]].
1. Continue harvesting purple shinies until you get a [[Zephyrswell Charm]].
1. Continue harvesting purple shinies until you get a [[Bayle Charm]].
1. Travel to Lavastorm and speak to Cliara Quickbellows {{waypoint -131.23, -114.95, 644.70}}
1. Travel to Antonica and speak to Baloll Oldsong at {{waypoint -725, -15, 229}}.
1. Travel to Commonlands and speak to Yrrey Burbridge {{waypoint -497, -43, -264}}.

## Rewards

One of the following:

- [[Staff of Theer Replica]]
- 6 [[Token of Heroism|Tokens of Heroism]]
