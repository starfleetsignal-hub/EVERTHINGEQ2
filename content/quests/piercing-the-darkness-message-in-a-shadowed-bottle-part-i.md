## Materials Required

Although these are technically for the next quest in the series, it takes place in the same zone so it's advised to have them on you before entering the zone.

At least:

1. 300 [[Hardened Zelniak Pelt]]
1. 150 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
1. 10 [[Humming Azurite]]
1. 100 [[Luclizite Cluster]]

## Steps

1. Click the shadowy portal near {{waypoint 535.84, 86.97, 431.51}} in the [[Wracklands]] to enter [[Empty Mines of the Temple Ssraeshza: Piercing the Darkness]].
1. Examine **pool of green blood** at {{waypoint -30.07, -31.70, -0.77}}.![pool of green blood](images/Pool_of_green_blood.jpg)
1. Click on the pulsing blue column at {{waypoint 9.66, -31.75, 33.54}} to 'Attempt to Cure Yourself'.
1. For the next part there are four glowing blue items that need picking up and placing with their matching larger glowing blue statues around the room. You can find these items surrounded by pillars that circle the Centre of the room:
   - {{waypoint -48, -31, 1}}
   - {{waypoint -1, -31, -48}}
   - {{waypoint 48, -31, -1}}
   - {{waypoint -1, -31, 48}}
   - *Note: There are two versions of each of these items, one you can pick up (the smaller version) and one that you can't (the larger version). Also, you need to make sure you are close enough to the statue when you place the item. It's sometimes a little bit picky about placement. Put items on the ground at statue, not into hands. When each item is placed properly, you will get a chat message indicating the item has locked into place.*
   - Move the hammer to the statue at {{waypoint -79.48, -32.00, -78.23}} NE corner.
   - Move the symbol to the statue at {{waypoint -79.67, -32.00, 76.76}} SE corner.
   - Move the staff to the statue at {{waypoint 75.49, -31.96, -78.60}} NW corner.
   - Move the sword to the statue at {{waypoint 70.69, -32.00, 74.47}} SW corner.
1. Descend the stairs that open at {{waypoint 7.73, -31.07, -3.8}}.
1. Click on the pulsing blue column at {{waypoint -25.50, -61.14, -43.78}} to 'Attempt to Cure Yourself'.
1. Click the **Shadowy Focus** at {{waypoint -66.59, -78.97, -98.15}}.  (Each Shadowy Focus is in order as you decend.)![Shadowy Focus](images/Shadowy_Focus.jpg)
1. Click the **Shadowy Focus** at {{waypoint -97.85, -96.75, -65.64}}.
1. Click the **Shadowy Focus** at {{waypoint -65.28, -114.54, -34.05}}.
1. Click the **Shadowy Focus** at {{waypoint -34.31, -132.32, -67.16}}.
1. Turn off the collectors in each of the 4 dark rooms on the ground floor. The collectors are at the rear of each room:
   - {{waypoint -66.58, -150.14, -186.44}}
   - {{waypoint -181.03, -150.14, -66.49}}
   - {{waypoint -66.05, -150.14, 50.58}}
   - {{waypoint 53.53, -150.14, -66.54}}
1. Touch the Sphere at {{waypoint -63.80, -146.07, -67.27}} -- (*the dark column in the center of the room*).
