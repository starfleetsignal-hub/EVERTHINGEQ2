## Steps

1. Gather 20 Clockwork Oculus Discs by destroying various clockwork mobs in [[Steamfont Mountains]].
1. Return to [[Goz Hepplewhite]].
1. Speak to [[Bindo Frugrin]] in the [[Frugrin Observatory]]. {{waypoint -950.13, 156.56, 749.80}}
1. Find 5 'Steam Aether Essences'. Kill [[A steam gargantua|steam gargantuas]], located around {{waypoint -604, 70, 1500}}, level 48-49's.  They are air elementals in the hotsprings.
1. Return to [[Bindo Frugrin]] in the [[Frugrin Observatory]] and wait for him to polish the disks.
1. Return to [[Goz Hepplewhite]] with the freshly polished 'Clockwork Oculus Discs'.
1. Wait for [[Goz Hepplewhite]] to go outside while he tests the suit in front of the ladies gossiping outside his front door (or follow him and watch his clothing disappear).
1. When [[Goz Hepplewhite]] respawns inside the building, he will despawn and respawn again with all his clothes on. Hail him again for your reward.

## Reward

- At least 7g 93s 11c at lvl 43
- One of the following:
  - [[Thinking Stud]]
  - [[Hepplewhite's Stealth Suit]] (This only makes your equipped armor invisible, like it did with Hoz in the quest.)
  - [[Acid Resistant Leggings]]
  - [[Cozy Research Slippers]]
  - [[Hepplewhite's Insulated Chestpiece]]
  - [[Gnomish Mechanized Bow]]
