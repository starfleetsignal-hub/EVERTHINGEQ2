## Description

"Whittier's treant brethren have agreed to help battle the Crushbone orcs that are threatening the Faydark and the city of Kelethin! They will attack the orc encampments once I have signaled them from inside the orc tents using the willow reed flute Whittier gave me." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Enter 3 Crushbone tents around [[Kelethin Outer Stump]] and use [[Willow reed flute]] at each location
   - Tents are at {{waypoint 540, 54, 437}}, {{waypoint 596, 24, 527}}, {{waypoint 465, 34, 563}} and {{waypoint 386, 32, 505}}
1. Return to Whittier

## Rewards

- +1500 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
- At least 2s 57c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Knight's Gauntlets (Level 10) (Version 3)|Knight's Gauntlets]] (Fighters)
  - [[Shade's Gloves (Version 3)|Shade's Gloves]] (Scouts)
  - [[Channeler's Gloves (Version 2)|Channeler's Gloves]] (Mages)
  - [[Archon's Gauntlets (Level 10) (Version 1)|Archon's Gauntlets]] (Priests)

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Whittier: "My treant brethren saw the sun because of you, [Player Character]! They can be stubborn and slow to act, but they are very honorable. They know you saved them by applying the healing elixir to them."
- Player Character: "Are they willing to help?"
- Whittier: "They have agreed to take up root against the Crushbone threat, yes! Mossmane trusts you and asked that you show our brethren the Crushbone camps, so that they know exactly what to prune."
- Player Character: "How should I do that?"
- Whittier: "Take this reed. When you have entered a Crushbone tent, blow the reed and my treant brethren will attack the encampment."
- Player Character: "I'll do exactly that."

I am to blow the willow reed flute when I have entered a Crushbone tent situated on the southern ridge around the Kelethin Outer Stump. Signal the treants from within Crushbone tents.

- Whittier: "I heard the wind's song through the reed, [Player Character]. Tell me my brethren answered it!"
- Player Character: "They did. And the Crushbone camps are no more."
- Whittier: "That is a weight lifted from my branches! The orcs will always be a burl in my trunk, but you saved the city of Kelethin from them today, and for that I honor you, [Player Character]!"
- Player Character: "Thank you, Whittier."

I spoke with Whittier. I spoke with Whittier and told him of my success.

Whittier was relieved to hear of the destruction of the orc encampments from around Kelethin, and honored me for all that I had done to save the tree city from the Crushbone orcs that were threatening it.
