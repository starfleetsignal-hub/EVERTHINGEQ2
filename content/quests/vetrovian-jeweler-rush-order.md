## Note

Upon accepting this quest, you will receive [[Vetrovian Jeweler Writ]] which will automatically scribe the [[Forlorn Intangible Ring]] and [[Forlorn Intangible Scout Ability]] recipes to your recipe book

Once you click the clipboard, you will be granted the fuel necessary to complete the writ:

- [[Tradeskill Writ Fuel]] (10)

## Steps

I need to complete the work order as requested.

- I must create:
  - [[Forlorn Intangible Ring]] (3)
  - [[Forlorn Intangible Scout Ability]] (3)

Quest auto-completes after last combine.

## Rewards

- [[City Token]]
- At Least 51800 status at tradeskill level 125
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
