## Steps

1. Defeat 8 [[An insatiable locust|insatiable locusts]] in the paddies {{waypoint 344, 131, -439}} or {{waypoint 351, 132, -567}}
1. Speak with [[Zuzlub]]
1. Gather 6 **tarhizi fungus** (small bright green mushroom clusters). Nearby floramyst creatures will take exception and attack. Some possible locations:
   - {{waypoint 658, 134, -736}} ![Tarhizi Fungus](images/Tarhizifungus.png)
   - {{waypoint 681, 135, -724}}
   - {{waypoint 746, 139, -689}}
   - {{waypoint 755, 138, -656}}
   - {{waypoint 724, 138, -631}}
   - {{waypoint 773, 136, -617}}
1. Return to [[Zuzlub]]
