## Notes

- If you're looking for the level 80 epic tradeskill quest, it's [[The Proof of the Pudding]]

## Steps

1. Visit the following locations in the Commonlands in any order:
   - the entrance to [[The Graveyard]] {{waypoint -1363, -83, -345}}
   - [[The Wailing Caves]] {{waypoint -209, -43, -915}}
   - the entrance to [[Nektulos Forest]] {{waypoint 1116, -42, -636}}
   - the [[Wasp Mound]] southwest of the Crossroads {{waypoint -0.03, -35, 21}}
   - [[The Lonely Tower]] in the far southeast corner of the Commonlands by the river {{waypoint -1330, -52, 577}}.
1. Report back to to J.P. Feterman.

## Rewards

- At least 15s 81c
