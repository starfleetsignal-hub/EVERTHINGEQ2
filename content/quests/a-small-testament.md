## Steps

1. Speak with [[Grand Master Lu Sun]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]]. {{waypoint 782, 111, -280}}
1. Complete the following:
   - Travel to [[Butcherblock Mountains]]. Enter [[Kaladim]], and Gather 10 gloom mushrooms. These can be found in the back, at {{waypoint -70, 26, -382}}.
   - Gather bark from [[The Great Tree]] in the Lesser Faydark, at {{waypoint -242, -57, -384}}.
   - Speak with [[Arloona Delyssa]], next to the Great Tree in the Lesser Faydark, at {{waypoint -242, -57, -384}}.
1. Return to [[Grand Master Lu Sun]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]]. {{waypoint 782, 111, -280}}
1. Return to [[Rooleean Felodaan]] in [[Greater Faydark]]. {{waypoint -393, 15, 184}}
