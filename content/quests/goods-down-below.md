This quest is repeatable up to 5 times..

## Steps

1. Slay the vermin he specifies (chosen randomly) in any order:
   - [[Tomb rifter pest|tomb rifter pests]] {{waypoint -172, 1, -119}}
   - [[A venom spitter|venom spitters]] {{waypoint -121, 0, -203}}
   - [[Tomb forager|tomb foragers]] {{waypoint -76, -1, -102}}
   - [[A crypt substance|crypt substances]] {{waypoint -66, 0, -154.5}}
   - [[A tomb rifter bleeder|tomb rifter bleeders]] {{waypoint -202, 0, -124}}
   - [[Malefic arachnid|malefic arachnids]] {{waypoint -90, 0, -167}}
   - [[A coiled burrower|coiled burrowers]]
1. Return to [[Falin Shaloon]] for your reward.

## Notes

- This quest is not available at level 20+; you have to mentor down to level 15 or lower to get it.

## Rewards

- Experience
- At least 1s 51c - 1st completion.
- At least 3s 2c - 2nd completion.
- At least 4s 53c - 3rd completion.
- At least 6s 63c - 4th completion.
- At least 10s 25c - 5th completion.
