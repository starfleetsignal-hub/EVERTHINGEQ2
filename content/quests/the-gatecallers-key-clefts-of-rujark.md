## Steps

1. Kill [[Captive Coin Labourer]] at {{waypoint -106, 17, 38}} in the [[Clefts of Rujark]] to get [[A fancy steel key (Version 1)|a fancy steel key]].
   1. Examine that key to get the quest [[The Key of Coins (Clefts of Rujark)|The Key of Coins]] if you have not finished the quest already.
   1. Open the door at {{waypoint -4, 10, -195}} to finish [[The Key of Coins (Clefts of Rujark)|The Key of Coins]]
1. Open the chest at {{waypoint 7, 10, -232}}

## Rewards

- At least 14s 1c
- 1 [[Flawless Pearl]]
- 1 ~ 3 x [[Fragmented Nacre]]
- 1 ~ 3 x [[Flawless Nacre]]
- 1 ~ 3 x [[Rough Nacre]]

## Notes

- To access [[The Treasure Chamber]] you must have completed [[The Key of Coins (Clefts of Rujark)|The Key of Coins]] quest or be accompanied by someone who has.
