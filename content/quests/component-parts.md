## Steps

1. Battle [[A cursed Elddar|cursed Elddar]] in the [[Ruins of Takish'Hiz]] for components. {{waypoint 77.81, 131.39, -635.43}}
   - Obtain 6 cursed Elddar Limbs
   - Obtain 6 Elddar Geochantment Runes
   - Obtain 8 Animated Elddar Rubble
1. Return to [[Kamyar]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- One of the following:
  - [[Spellwoven Cursed-Cloth Cape]]
  - [[Resilient Cursed-Cloth Cloak]]
