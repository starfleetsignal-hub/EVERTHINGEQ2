## Notes

Complete while doing the Sig line [[Scars of Destruction: Starting to Spiral]] while in [[Blackhook Spiral: Fort Sunder (Solo)|Blackhook Spiral: Fort Sunder [Solo]]]

## Steps

1. do the following while in [[Blackhook Spiral: Fort Sunder (Solo)|Blackhook Spiral: Fort Sunder [Solo]]]
   - Throw the unstable Ulteran-suffused kaborite in the Blackhook flames (cast a cure as soon as you throw the kaborite and you won't be stunned)
   - Note: You will be tossed back, so make sure you don't fall off the cliff.
     - Locations near entrance at {{waypoint 326, 177, -402}}, {{waypoint 337, 177, -435}}, {{waypoint 301, 178, -434}}, {{waypoint 296, 178, -404}}, {{waypoint 325, 178, -365}}
     - Locations on Rocky Ridge at  {{waypoint 209, 243, -410}}, {{waypoint 233, 237, -359}}, {{waypoint 115, 212, -453}}, {{waypoint 67, 212, -473}},
   - Click on 6 Scrolls or Books on Forgotten Isle which you can get to by clicking the flying mount
   - NOTE:  You might have to scroll out so you can target the top, inside, of the brazier before you can toss.  Be mindful of the kickback or you will fall off a cliff.
1. Return to [[Shaahida]]
