## Notes

1. Make sure the quest updates, if you clicked on the entrance it will you let you enter, but if you didn't place the sigil stone properly, the quest will NOT update and you will not get credit. Exit and look for the sigil stone in front of the entrance, click on that and the quest should update.
1. Can be done concurrently with  [[Chalice of Growth]] if you were already able to complete [[Sealed With a Curse]].

## Steps

1. Set the assembled sigil stone in the sigil plate {{waypoint -550.38, 11.42, 429.15}} to empower the entrance to Buried Takish'Hiz.
1. Enter [[Buried Takish'Hiz: Terrene Threshold (Signature)|Buried Takish'Hiz: Terrene Threshold [Signature]]]
   - *See the instance page above for a detailed walkthrough for the dungeon.*
1. Explore the dungeon.
   1. Kill any [[A stonefallen drakota|stonefallen drakota]] to start the zone.
   1. Defeat [[Shiroth Avinox]] {{waypoint -771.01, -17.14, -19.26}}. Pick up **A Resident's Journal** off the ground and read it (Scroll is named **[[A Troubling Account]]** while in inventory.![A Resident's Journal](images/A_Resident's_Journal.png)
1. Search for [[Nairion]].
   1. Take the portal at {{waypoint -684.41, -64.60, 97.67}}
   1. Defeat [[The Haunting of Tualanan]]. Follow the pattern of the first tile until [[The Haunting of Tualanan]] spawns {{waypoint -215.75, 44.94, 319.97}}. The entire floor becomes solid after she dies.
   1. Take the portal at {{waypoint -248.11, 47.13, 395.16}}.
   1. Defeat [[Terranus]] {{waypoint -932.65, 33.08, 553.09}} to drop the wall.
   1. Hail [[Malachiel Caedor]], who walks away, leaving [[Anyena Terratha]] and [[Yorven Terratha]] to kill.
   1. Take the left door (behind his throne) and find [[Nairion]] at {{waypoint -906.24, 22.98, 361.31}}.
   1. Defeat [[Malachiel Caedor]] {{waypoint -930.36, 33.85, 327.33}}.
   1. Rescue [[Nairion]] and return to the adventure party at [[Goldami Reservoir Camp]].
1. Zone out and speak with [[Firiona Vie]].
1. Enter [[Buried Takish'Hiz: The Sacred Gift (Signature)|Buried Takish'Hiz: The Sacred Gift [Signature]]] (see zone page for walkthrough)
   1. clear it of befouling entities.
      1. Defeat [[Exorax]].
         - *Spawns refuse piles that must be clicked on to remove. Don't click on the infested ones.*
   1. Continue to clear it of befouling entities.
      1. Defeat [[Ganadan]]
         - *Attackable when sufficient [[A visage of growth]] are killed*
      1. Defeat [[Anirath Andirin]].
         - *Also spawns refuse to click on, same as [[Exorax]].*
   1. Speak to [[Firiona Vie]] {{waypoint -1140, 74, 210}} **Before you leave the instance (same room)**
   1. watch her ceremony
   1. Speak with her again after she finishes her speech.
1. Zone outside to [[Goldami Reservoir Camp]] and speak to [[Firiona Vie]]
