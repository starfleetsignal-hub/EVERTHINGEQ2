## Prerequisite

This is part of the [[Sarnak]] racial quest line

## Steps

1. Enter [[Scale Yard: Traitors' End]] {{waypoint -65, -25, 284}}
1. Speak to [[Tentio Viscus]] {{waypoint 78, 0, -12}}
1. Speak to the [[An accused citizen|accused citizen]] next to Tentio
1. Fight the citizen (your level, one up)
1. Speak to Tentio
1. Outside the Gorowyn Chancery in Freeport, speak to the mysterious sarnak {{waypoint -112, -38, -21}}
1. Speak to the Ambassador

## Rewards

- At least 19g 93s 2c @ level 79
