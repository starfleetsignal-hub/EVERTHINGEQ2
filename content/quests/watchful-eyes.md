## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Locate and hail 15 [[A Bathezid's Watch vanguard|Bathezid's Watch vanguards]]. They spawn randomly near rocks and trees in between [[Kylong Post]] {{waypoint 487, 12, 1496}} and [[Sathir's Span Post]] {{waypoint -153, -39, -1222}}. See the talk page for a list of locations that they can be found.
   - They are trackable.
   - It may be useful to make a target macro to find them quickly.
   - Only people on this quest can see these mobs. They despawn once hailed, but can quickly respawn in the same location.
   - Speaking with these spawns does not appear to break invisibility.
1. Return to [[Dragoon Linzinek]].

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
