## Steps

1. Locate [[Silbian]]. He's hiding from a fearstalker in an alcove in the cliff face northeast of [[Aglthin Pond]] {{waypoint -100, 368, 898}}.
1. Kill the non-aggro [[A highland fearstalker|highland fearstalker]] directly in front of Sibian.
1. Return to [[Lujayn Molefiir]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- One of the following:
  - [[Silbian's Fate]]
  - [[Fearstalker Stud Earring]]
  - [[Fearstalker Fang Earring]]
  - [[Fearstalker Hoop]]
