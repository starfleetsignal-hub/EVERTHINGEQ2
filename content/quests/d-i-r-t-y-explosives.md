## Steps

1. Speak with Gnorbert at [[The D.I.R.T.Y. Dig]] {{waypoint 1120, 31, 1122}}.
   - Note: quest indicator shows up above him when you get close.
1. Talk to Gnorbert
1. Click the large boulders laying on Gnorbert until the quest updates.
1. Talk to Gnorbert again
1. Click on Gnorbert's backpack {{waypoint 1117, 30, 1118}} to receive the following ingredients:
   - [[Excavator's Spark Powder]]
   - [[Excavator's Tinder]]
   - [[Excavator's Fuel]]
   - The [[Excavator's High Efficiency Explosives (recipe)|recipe]] auto scribes
1. Craft [[Excavator's High Efficiency Explosives]] on [[Chemistry Table]] (there is one in [[Sapswill's Rest]] {{waypoint 1229, 18, 1265}})
1. Click on the green shiny to place the barrel of explosives among the rocks.
1. Click the barrel to detonate and move quickly away.
1. Speak to [[Gnorbert]]
