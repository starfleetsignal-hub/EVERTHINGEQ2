## Prerequisites

- You must speak the [[Koada'Dal]] language.

## Steps

1. In [[The Pillars of Flame]] inspect [[The Rock of Eldonis]], underwater at {{waypoint 2000, -266, -537}}.
1. Read the rock.
1. Examine the rod in the rock.
1. Slay [[The Guardian of Eldonis]] within 15 minutes.
1. Click the rod again to receive [[Rod of Rathe Shattering]].
1. Enter [[The Fountain of Life]] in [[The Silent City]]. {{waypoint 895, -1, 245}}
1. Slay [[Godking Anuk]] to make the wall to the upper temple climbable.
1. Break the Ewer of Sul'Dae by using the Rod of Rathe Shattering. You must climb the wall or be summoned to {{waypoint 0, 7, -217}}
1. Return to [[Seeker Abdeef]] in [[Maj'Dul]]. {{waypoint -227, 161, -104}}

## Notes

- As of [[LU48]], it is no longer necessary to perform the access quest [[The Eyes of Anashti Sul]] in order to gain access to [[The Fountain of Life]].
- Although the Guardian of Eldonis is designated as an x2 raid boss, it does not require two groups to defeat. You can easily complete this step with a single group.
- You do not need to be present when Godking Anuk is killed. You can be brought into the zone, summoned to the pool at the top of the temple and use the Rod of Rathe Shattering to complete the quest step. Be aware that the gnolls, the Power of Anuk and Armor of Anuk will respawn after Godking Anuk is defeated.
