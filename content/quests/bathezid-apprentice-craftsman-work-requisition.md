## Steps

1. Collect the requisition list from the Work Requisitions clipboard (next to Supply Sergeant Anuhadux)
1. Scribe the [[Bathezid apprentice craftsman recipes]] received upon accepting the quest.
1. Create 6 of one of the following (randomly determined after clicking the clipboard):
   - [[Carved wooden dice]] (Woodworking Table - smoldering sandpaper)
     - 4 Redwood Lumber
     - 2 Deklium Cluster
     - 5 Smoldering Sandpaper
   - [[Delectable battle rations]] (Stove and Keg - smoldering kindling)
     - 4 Raw Succulent Root
     - 2 Deklium Cluster
     - 10 Smoldering Kindling
   - [[Hot brute meat broth]] (Stove and Keg - smoldering kindling)
     - 1 Raw Succulent Root
     - 3 Raw King Prawn
     - 2 Torsis Tea Leaf
     - 10 Smoldering Kindling
   - [[Longbow of pain]] (Woodworking table - smoldering sandpaper)
     - 4 Redwood Lumber
     - 2 Lichenclover Root
     - 5 Smoldering Sandpaper
   - [[Lucky billy doll]] (Woodworking Table - smoldering sandpaper)
     - 4 Redwood Lumber
     - 2 Lichenclover Root
     - 5 Smoldering Sandpaper
   - [[Ornate weapon rack]] (Woodworking Table - smoldering sandpaper)
     - 4 Redwood Lumber
     - 2 Ferrite Cluster
     - 5 Smoldering Sandpaper
1. Return to Anuhadux.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill Experience
- At least 38g 11s 18c
- +750 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
