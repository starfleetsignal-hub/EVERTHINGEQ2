## Note

- *You can do this quest steps turn and turn about with [[Karath Smoothmane - Breaking of the Pact]].
- *Both quest books can be bought by [[Indis Surion]], [[The Concordium Tower]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. Take the yellow spot.
- *The needed mobs are next to each other (here: the basin where [[Resheza]] spawns {{waypoint 116, -24, -716}}).
- *This quest is also part of the [[Book Quests Timeline]].*

## Steps

All the following monster are found in The Thundering Steppes:

1. Kill 10 [[A Sabertooth looter|Sabertooth looters]]. {{waypoint 169, -29, -686}}
1. Kill 10 Sabertooth looters.
1. Kill 10 Sabertooth looters.
1. Kill 10 [[A Sabertooth diviner (Thundering Steppes)|A Sabertooth diviner]]. {{waypoint 169, -29, -686}}
1. Kill 10 Sabertooth diviners.
1. Kill 10 Sabertooth diviners.
1. Kill 10 [[A Sabertooth tracker|Sabertooth trackers]], [[A Sabertooth scout|Sabertooth scouts]], or [[A Sabertooth hunter|Sabertooth hunters]]. Try the basins around {{waypoint 808, -21, -441}}.
1. Kill 10 Sabertooth trackers, Sabertooth scouts, or Sabertooth hunters.
1. Kill 10 Sabertooth trackers, Sabertooth scouts, or Sabertooth hunters.
