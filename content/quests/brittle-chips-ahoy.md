## Steps

1. Enter Antonica and look for the glowing skeletons on the ground outside the gates of Qeynos.
1. Search the skeletons for bone chips to spawn a [[Brittle Antonica resident]] (level will scale to match yours) and kill them.
1. Repeat the searching and killing 10 times for 10 bone chips.
1. Return to Shepherd Bainyn for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 0c (tier 1 players)
- At least 90c (tier 2 players)
- At least 11s 36c (tier 3 players)
- At least 41s 65c (tier 4 players)
- At least 55s 35c (tier 5 players)
- At least 3g 56s 78c (tier 6 players)
- At least 6g 88s 21c (tier 7 players)
- At least 9g 46s 1c (tier 8 players)
