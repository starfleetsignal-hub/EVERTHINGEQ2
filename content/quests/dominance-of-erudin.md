## Description

The once mighty city of Erudin has been pulled apart by the massive void energies inexorably pulling on Odus. However, the population within its halls have been even more twisted and corrupted. I must cleanse them.

## Steps

- I should seek out a [[Scholar Primarch]]
- I should seek out [[Taranis Gazerra]]
- I should hunt down [[El'Arad, Lord Primarch of Erudin]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 80g
- 12 [[Mark of Manaar|Marks of Manaar]]
