## Steps

1. Travel to [[Greater Faydark]] and gather the following in order:
   1. Emerald Lake shelf Fungus -- on top of the enormous fungus where Gabriella Mosswood stands {{waypoint -820.19, 87.30, 241.25}}
   1. 3 Tunare's Sapling water reeds -- in the pond {{waypoint -79.44, -8.96, 55.85}}
   1. 3 Shores of Growth kelp -- around {{waypoint -314.61, -69.24, -728.68}}
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].

## Hints

- Gathered items are not trackable
