## Starting the Quest

The starter for this quest exists in five separate locations:

- [[North Freeport]], Academy of Arcane Science, main floor {{waypoint 11, -7, -128}}, stack of books on top of the table
- [[South Qeynos]], Mage Tower, yellow teleporter {{waypoint 707, 82, 127}}, examine the stack of books with the big green tome on-top next to [[Telamoninioon Orinsidian]] "Words of Pure Magic" text.
- [[Kelethin]], Kelethin Research Library building, {{waypoint 136, 109, 178}}, half stack of books on table
- [[Neriak, City of Hate]], Library of K'Lorn, {{waypoint -686, 18, 249}}, books on top of the shelf
- [[New Halas]], Glacierbane's Vault, {{waypoint -117, 148, -82}}, open book on the floor beside Dannon Ramsdell

## Notes

1. Each shrine can spawn in two different zones, about once an hour, with a delay that can be anywhere from a few moments to up to 45 minutes.
1. At any given time a tier's shrine can be up in both locations, one location but not the other, or neither, or nowhere.

## Steps

![The Myr Druzaic Shrine in Zek, the Orcish Wastes](images/Myr_Druzaic_Shrine.jpg)
![Kab Druzaic Shrine in Feerrott, near the Gulch of Thule.](images/Kab_Druzaic_Shrine.jpg)

1. After examining the quest starter, you must find four Druzaic Shrines in the Shattered Lands.
   - Vul'Zet Druzaic Shrine (Tier 3)
     - [[The Thundering Steppes]] {{waypoint 972.99, -21.68, 1187.11}}, on the coastline near the [[An earth rumbler|earth rumblers]] and roekillik miners.
     - [[Nektulos Forest]] {{waypoint -1605, 48, -1347}}, East of Darklight Station and [[Dragoon K'naae]].
   - Vul'Myr Druzaic Shrine (Tier 4)
     - [[Enchanted Lands]] {{waypoint -984, -2, -619}}, on the Isle of the Three Pines in the northeast corner of the zone.
     - [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] {{waypoint 14, -39, -866}}), behind [[Deathfist Citadel]]. *(Proceed with caution if [[Grumzod the Exile]], epicx2, is in the area!)*
   - Vul'Kab Druzaic Shrine (Tier 5)
     - [[Rivervale]]  {{waypoint -809, -5, -290}} , near [[The House of Circles]], behind [[Rukir Pineleaf|Rukir Pineleaf's]] house.
     - [[The Feerrott]] {{waypoint -1094, 0, 913}}, In a cave next to the [[Gulch of Thule]].  Head East from the Druid Ring till you get to {{waypoint -1144, -2.49, 874}}. in front of the cave.
   - Vul'Uzu Druzaic Shrine (Tier 5)
     - [[Everfrost]] {{waypoint 503, -108, -1605}}, **underwater,** west of where [[Master Webclaw]] spawns. (Due north of the Spire)
     - [[Lavastorm]] {{waypoint 86, -32, 139}}, Southwest of the lower [[Dragon's Breath Tunnel]]  entrance. It's dark, so you can miss it. (I found it at {{waypoint 84.76, -32.32, 137.19}}.  A bit further west.
