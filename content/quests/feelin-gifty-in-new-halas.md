## Steps

1. Speak with the following NPCs in [[New Halas]]
   - [[Kalib Mal]] at {{waypoint 26, 149, 9}}
   - [[Virginia Strongarms]] in [[Coldwind Hall]] at {{waypoint 85, 154, -161}}
   - [[Eric]] at {{waypoint -54, 154, -114}}
   - [[Glip Mightblade]] in [[Hapson's Humble Abode]] at {{waypoint -153, 149, 28}}
   - [[Kendi Wiggletail]] at the [[White Lodge]] (outside New Halas) at {{waypoint -67, 139, 279}}
   - [[Marcus Angelfire]] at the White Lodge at {{waypoint -65, 139, 271}}
1. Return to G'Shugahplum Gigglegibber. Make sure to have six open slots in your inventory!
1. Hand out the gifts:
   - **WARNING: You must give the correct gift to the correct person, or you will have to start the entire quest over!**
   - [[Studded leather saddle]] to Kalib Mal {{waypoint 26, 149, 9}}
   - [[Rustic picture frame]] to Virginia Strongarms {{waypoint 85, 154, -161}}
   - [[Puppy]] to Eric {{waypoint -54, 154, -114}}
   - [[Complete whetstone set]] to Glip Mightblade {{waypoint -153, 149, 28}}
   - [[Romantic letter kit]] to Kendi Wiggletail {{waypoint -67, 139, 279}}
   - [[Book of classic love stories]] to Marcus Angelfire {{waypoint -65, 139, 271}}
1. Return to G'Shugahplum Gigglegibber.

## Rewards

- The ability to set different kinds of lights on your [[Frostfell Tree]].
