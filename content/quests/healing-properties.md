## Steps

1. Harvest 3 of each of the following (in no particular order):
   - medicinal lichen: found near [[Granite Hills]] against the cliff wall around {{waypoint -24.13, 65.17, 338.50}}
   - medicinal berries: found near ponds like [[Aquamarine Pond]] {{waypoint 494.89, -1.32, 205.99}}
   - medicinal mushrooms: found under patches of large mushrooms near the entrance to lesser faydark {{waypoint 646.06, -0.88, 312.37}}
1. Return to [[Lady Aileeta Reila]]

- At least 1s 51c
- Experience
