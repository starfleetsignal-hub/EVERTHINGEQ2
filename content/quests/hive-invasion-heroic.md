## Steps

*A swarm of bixies have taken over part of Guk, and need to be driven out. Seek out the strongest of the creatures lurking within the Fractured Hive, and destroy them.*

- Enter the zone [[The Fractured Hive (Heroic)]]:
  - Defeat the [[Furious Honey Elemental (Heroic)|Furious Honey Elemental]]
  - Defeat [[Ziza Cerata (Heroic)|Ziza Cerata]]
  - Defeat [[Bazziz Zz (Heroic)|Bazziz Zz]]
  - Defeat the [[Monstrous Roc (Heroic)|Monstrous Roc]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- 5 [[Draconic Etyma]]
- [[Sparkling Crate]]
- A crate of heroic gear
