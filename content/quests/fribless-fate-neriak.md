## Steps

I have been hired to get a helm for Blat Berison from Frethe Zizlop. Frethe wants a deck of cards from Zoe Hertzflip. Zoe seeks Barb Islenets' pink goggles. Barb is looking to get a bottle of wine back from Abner Siblet. Abner will give me the wine once I deal with a "problem" named Fribles. I shouldn't have been surprised by yet another task.

- Slay [[Belazzor Fribles, the Inept (Darklight Wood)|Belazzor Fribles]], a Ratonga Pirate, at the southern end of the scar outside Neriak. He spawns when you are near {{waypoint -561, -51, 23}} and  return to [[Abner Siblet]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]].
