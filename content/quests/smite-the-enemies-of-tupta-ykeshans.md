## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.  Note:  Not any longer.

## Steps

1. Kill 15 Ykeshans in Innothule Swamp (The undead frogloks and trolls "living" in and around The Ruins of Guk).  {{waypoint 720, 146, -837}}.  From Tupta, go to Firmroot Moot, then take the 1-way ballon ride from Firmroot to Guk, Drop Point.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +1000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
- -250 faction with **Clan Grobb**
