## Steps

1. Capture [[Grassgalor (Named)|Grassgalor]] {{waypoint 2775, 67, 983}} in The Green Overlook by using the [[Chokéball]], recieved upon accepting the quest, on it after reducing its health to 30% or below.
   - Once captured, you will receive a [[Chokéball: Grassgalor]] to keep.
1. Return to Eruwin.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 86c
