## Steps

![Flowers to be watered occur in groups of four](images/Wuoshi_plant.jpg)

1. Obtain water from the etherflow pool next to Wuoshi (proximity update){{waypoint -649, 377, 630}}.
   - You will have to make a new visit to the pond after watering each group of four flowers.
1. Water Wuoshi's 12 plants on the small island to the north of Aeterna Gardens
   - {{waypoint -495, 341, 206}}
   - {{waypoint -496, 327, 162}}
   - {{waypoint -536, 260, 171}}
1. Return to Wuoshi

- At least 1p 30g
