## Steps

[[Gladithae Sioniel]] in the [[Lightshadow Contingent]] camp asked me to retrieve pieces of the supply carts and hunt the enemy lizardmen.

1. Slay [[A Tsol Ew mistalker|Tsol Ew miststalkers]] and [[A Tsol Ew mistwatcher|mistwachers]].
   - You'll need to slay 12 of them.  They're in random locations near the Lightshadow Contingent camp.  Also many can be found at {{waypoint 169, -9, 174}}.
1. I should look around the Lightshadow Undergrowth for the pieces of the broken Wayward supply carts. Collect 10 partially concealed wooden planks.  You can wait for respawns and they will progress the quest. Some possible locations are:
   - {{waypoint 164, 1, 110}}
   - {{waypoint 173, -10, 228}}
   - {{waypoint 175, -9, 242}}
   - {{waypoint 111, -8, 246}}
   - {{waypoint 38, -5, 188}}
   - {{waypoint 99, 3, 33}}
   - {{waypoint 123, 0, 68}}
   - {{waypoint 165, 1, 111}}
   - {{waypoint 53, 1, 59}}
   - {{waypoint 18, -8, 241}}
1. Return to Gladithae.
