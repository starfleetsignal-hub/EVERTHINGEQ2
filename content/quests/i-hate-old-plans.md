## Steps

1. Talk to [[Deputy Blumble]] {{waypoint 222, 14, -480}}
1. Examine [[The Broken Wall Plans]]
1. Collect 10 large nails, from Briarpaws, southeast of [[Deputy Stoutgut]] {{waypoint 90, 23, -325}}
1. Collect 6 medium nails, from [[An elusive hatecrawler|elusive hatecrawler]]s, west of the wall {{waypoint 271, 5, -413}}
1. Collect 8 small nails, from [[A suffering lasher|suffering lasher]]s, near the [[Seamist Plains Village]] {{waypoint 364, -1, -406}}
1. Examine [[The Broken Wall Plans]]
1. Collect 7 leather straps from [[A bloodthirsty lasher|bloodthirsty lasher]]s (sharing spawn with suffering lashers), near the [[Seamist Plains Village]] {{waypoint 397, 7, -414}}
1. Examine [[The Broken Wall Plans]]
1. Collect 3 obscure adhesives, from [[A seed of obscurity|seeds of obscurity]], in [[Seamist Plains Village]] {{waypoint 490, 2, -503}}
1. Return to [[Deputy Blumble]] {{waypoint 222, 14, -480}}
1. Return to [[Deputy Stoutgut]] {{waypoint 170, -1, -483}}
