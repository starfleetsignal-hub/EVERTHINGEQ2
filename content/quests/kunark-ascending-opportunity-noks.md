## Steps

1. Speak with [[Trooper Sotek]] {{waypoint 730.56, 249.57, 1336.11}} in the [[Crusader's Cave]] in [[Obulus Frontier]].
   - *Note:  He won't appear without doing the previous quest [[Kunark Ascending: Beyond the Veil]].*
1. Kill 8 of the nearby [[A Sel'Nok infiltrator|Sel'Nok infiltrators]].
1. Return to [[Trooper Sotek]] {{waypoint 730.56, 249.57, 1336.11}}, who got knocked unconcious by the one infiltrator attacking him while you defeated 8 of them, to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g 29,695 status
- 15000 faction with **Crusaders of Greenmist**
