## Steps

1. Place burning Holgresh effigies in the cave. They place automatically as you run by the location and this can be done while invisible:
   - {{waypoint -1183.7, -566, -1120.7}}
   - {{waypoint -1011.45, -587.28, -1216.29}}
   - {{waypoint -870.52, -587.35, -1164.31}}
   - {{waypoint -903.5, -562.19, -940}}
1. Collect Holgresh wings for more effigies from [[A holgresh elementalist|holgresh elementalists]] or [[An elder holgresh|elder holgreshes]].
1. Return to [[Lieutenant Washburn]] at the Combine Caravan.
1. Inform [[Tracker Morin]] {{waypoint -1713, -599, -1227}} that you made it back.

  - [[Withering Soul Chainlink Legguards]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Withered Chainlink Leggings]] (Shamans)
  - [[Withering Cloth Pantaloons]] (All Mages)
