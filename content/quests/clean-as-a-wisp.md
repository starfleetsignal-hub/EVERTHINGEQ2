## Steps

1. Capture wisp essences by using the Wisp Bait from your inventory on the wisps before they are killed. Wisp Bait can be used while in combat.
   - Capture 3 from cleansing wisps
   - Capture 3 from addled wisps
1. Return to Isar

## Rewards

- At least 90g 70s 61c
