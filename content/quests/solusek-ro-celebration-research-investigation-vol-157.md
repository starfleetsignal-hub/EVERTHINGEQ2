## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Solusek Ro has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items

1. Purchase the [[Living Flame Ember|living flame ember]] from Ulfreeti for 5000 status
   - Lavastorm - Wanders along the Smoldering Shore area, from {{waypoint -360, -129, 525}} to {{waypoint -134, -129, 482}}
1. Purchase the [[Cold-flame Flint|cold-flame flint]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the [[Aether Lava Stone|aether lava stone]] from Nushoon for 5000 status
   - In [[Aether Wroughtlands]] this NPC wanders along a path from the Bivouac Camp {{waypoint 754, -45, -446}} to Minstrel's Cradle {{waypoint 344, -59, -183}}
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Burning Prince]] at {{waypoint -772, -129, -368}} in [[Lavastorm]]
