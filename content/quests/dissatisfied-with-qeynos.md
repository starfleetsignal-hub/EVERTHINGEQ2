## Steps

The turn-in for this quest is after speaking to Sprockfuddle. To get the rest of the steps you have to speak to him again and he'll give you the quest "Trade Secrets," which contains steps 2-10 listed below.

1. [[Sprockfuddle]] is located in the Smuggler's Grotto found by going underwater at {{waypoint 882, -26, 31}} and traveling into the sewer entry.
1. Speak to [[Vernon Tinworthy]] {{waypoint 807, -14, 42}}, on the top of the stairs (third floor) of [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn|Fish's Alehouse]]. He doesn't trust you, and sends you out to speak to several other people and to gain their trust.
1. Speak to [[Mary Dillon]] {{waypoint 801, -23, 60}} downstairs. Avoid asking about the shield, and instead console her about Thierry.
1. Head to [[South Qeynos]] and speak to [[Thierry Dillon]] {{waypoint 620, -16, 225}} wandering the streets. Choose the following in conversation:
   1. "I've been talking to Mary Dillon."
   1. "I was under the impression you left her."
   1. "So what happened?"
   1. "Mary's still pining away for you."
   1. "More worthwhile than your own wife?"
1. Speak to [[Ellinnea Umalia]] {{waypoint 647, -19, 144}} across the bridge from the [[Concordium Tower]]. Flatter her and ask about Towser.
1. Head to [[The Elddar Grove]] and speak to [[Towser Flizgibbert]] {{waypoint 692, -16, -456}} near the bridge leading to [[The Baubbleshire]]. Ask about Ellinnea and flatter him.
   1. "I've heard about you from Ellinnea Umailia."
   1. "Sounds like you're a compassionate person. And a smart one!"
   1. "She's not a very friendly sort."
   1. "Ellinnea couldn't help boasting; a transparent effort to put you down, really."
   1. "Your device would be much better, I'm sure."
   1. "You're a genius, Flizgibbert!"
1. Head north and speak to [[Arbuthnot Jumroller]] {{waypoint 787.63, -22.68, -528.55}} In the Halfling area of the Qeynos Province District to the north of Towser. Praise Towser and ask about the truth potion.
   1. "You're friends with Towser Flizbiggert, aren't you?"
   1. "I hear Towser's a great teacher"
   1. "I met him through the design contest"
   1. "Truth Potion?"
1. Head to [[North Qeynos]] and speak to [[Jo Ironshield]] {{waypoint 284, 17, -167}} on a hill just north of the bank. Grill him and get the truth potion.
1. Speak to [[Magus Weskah]] {{waypoint 332, -21, 148}} in one of the small rooms at [[Irontoe's East]].
1. Return to [[Vernon Tinworthy]] at [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn|Fish's Alehouse]] in Qeynos Harbor, {{waypoint 807, -14, 42}}, with the information.
