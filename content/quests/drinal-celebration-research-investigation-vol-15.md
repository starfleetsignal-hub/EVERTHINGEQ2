## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Drinal has informed me that summoning an Avatar requires the land to be attuned to their element. To achieve this, altars have been set up around the region, but they need to be supplied with power. I have been asked to assist with this by collecting objects infused with the essence of slain foes and offer them to an altar.

1. Collect 3 [[Infused Silver Tear|infused silver tear]] (random body drop from a lot of mobs in [[Zimara Breadth]] - confirmed from a metal-infused daggerclaw, a winged viper, a columnar crawler, arcaneframe daggerclaw, basalt formation Near  {{waypoint -313, 95, -382}}
1. Use the [[Infused Silver Tear|infused silver tear]] and empower an altar.  Random spawn in **Loping Plains**, its yellow sparkles visible far away, possible locations of altar:
   - {{waypoint 65, 36, -347}} next to the wizard spires
   - {{waypoint -43, 51, 297}}
   - {{waypoint -157, -3, 467}} at the eastern end of The Timorous Moor beach
   - {{waypoint 631, 87, -101}} Mount Mistmoore, just off the road east of the mount station
   - {{waypoint 179, -1, 364}} Western end of The Timorous Moor beach
   - {{waypoint 204, 69, -120}} Pumpkin Head area
   - {{waypoint -560, -15, -50}} Next to the bridge by Wayunder Lake
   - {{waypoint 743, 78, 30}}  Next to Ravenscar Mausoleum
   - {{waypoint -690, 0, -190}} Near Shinspitter Camp
   - {{waypoint -425, 30, 410}} Near Corrupted Druid Ring
   - {{waypoint 460, 131, -41}} Halfway up to Castle
   - {{waypoint 545, 198, 143}} Outside Castle
1. Return to [[Herald of the Silver Reaper]] {{waypoint 85, -3, 291}} in [[Loping Plains]]
