## Steps

1. Speak to [[Asadel Yasurbah]] inside [[Asadel Yasurbah's Residence|his apartment]] in [[Maj'Dul]] at {{waypoint 124, 143, 102}}.
1. Speak to the following NPCs:
   - [[The Sinking Sands]]:
     - [[Barusha the Tradesman]] {{waypoint -1162.11, -152.04, -307.01}}
     - [[Trader Hazin]] {{waypoint -230, -102, -483}}
       1. At the end of his dialoge, you will be attacked by three [[Raj'Dur raider|Raj'Dur raiders]].
       1. Speak with Trader Hazin again.
   - [[The Pillars of Flame]]:
     - Speak to [[Peddler Otis]] {{waypoint -32, -90, -815}}
     - Speak to [[Merchant Breeze]] {{waypoint 1278.00, -165.59, -1693.00}}(Eastern Stinging isle carpet recommended)
1. Return to Asadel Yasurbah.
1. Speak to [[A Saracen fan]] {{waypoint 21, 149, 106}} or {{waypoint 16, 149, 114}}
   - This mob has a 40-minute spawn timer.
1. Speak to [[Preceptor Jazeel (Maj'Dul)|Preceptor Jazeel]]. {{waypoint 35, 137, 212}}
1. Zone into [[Champion's Court: Jazeel's Challenge]] and speak to [[Preceptor Jazeel (Jazeel's Challenge)|Preceptor Jazeel]].
   - You will not be able to zone in if other group members are in Maj'Dul. *See Discussion*
1. Kill [[Rockbop the Mangler]].
1. Speak to [[Preceptor Jazeel (Jazeel's Challenge)|Preceptor Jazeel]] again.
1. Leave the instance and head over to [[A Maj'Dul Residence]] at {{waypoint -170, 150, 118}}.
   - The rooftop residence can be reached by climbing the lattice and crossing over the catwalks to the door labeled 'An Abandoned Residence'.
1. Grab the scarf from the blue basket.
- Return to Ishara, Caliph of Truth.
