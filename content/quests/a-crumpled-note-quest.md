## Notes

- *This quest cannot be completed by characters of Evil-alignment*
- *The quest starter item will not drop from gray mobs.*
- *It is possible to complete this quest twice and receive two separate "completed quests" in your [[Quest Journal]]. All you need is to have another [[A crumpled scroll]] drop and you can complete the quest at the other quest target (i.e. Amren Talbot if you turned in the first scroll that you got to Sir Aidan Godard, and vice versa).*

## Starting the Quest

When examining the starter, you are offered a choice of who to turn it in to:

- *The mages in the Concordium must see this*: [[Amren Talbot]] on the bottom floor of [[The Concordium Tower]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District|South Qeynos]] {{waypoint 726, -20, 128}}
- *I will show this note to the Steel Warriors*: [[Sir Aidan Godard]] in [[Qeynos Province District]] {{waypoint 690, -20, 2}}

## Rewards

- At least 2s 10c
