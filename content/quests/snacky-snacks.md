The Bloody Tooth goblins' food supplies are running low, so I offered to help refill their favorites.

## Note

Upon accepting this quest, you will receive [[Broken Tooth Snacky Snacks (Recipe Book)]] which will automatically scribe the [[Broken Tooth Snacky Snacks]] and [[Broken Tooth Shroom Juice]] recipes to your recipe book.

If you are in a guild, it is worth planting a rally flag, as it is difficult to get to this area without the use of a [[Twark Transport Totem]] or from adjacent zones, like [[Jarsath Wastes|Jarsath]] or [[Kylong Plains]] other than to fly which may be time-consuming.

The recipes for this quest can **only** be crafted at a [[Goblin Firepit]] in the area. One is found at {{waypoint 132, -164, -768}}, near [[Glibnox]].

Refere to the Discussion Page for the complete list of supply items required for this set of quests.

## Supply List

Because these must be crafted in [[Twark]], and the required materials cannot be harvested in [[Obulus Frontier]], bring the following (minimum) supplies:
For snacks:

- 5 [[Fruticose lichen]]
- 15 [[Umbral trout]]
- 15 [[Abyssal angler]]
- 25 [[Thaumic Coal]]
- For juice:
- 5 [[Fruiting sporangium]]
- 15 [[Fruticose lichen]]
- 15 [[Wild tempeh]]
- 25 [[Thaumic Coal]]

## Steps

1. I can cook these recipes in a gobline fire
   - Cook [[Broken Tooth Snacky Snacks|Broken Tooth Snacky-Snacks]] (10)
     - Each combine makes 2, so 5 total
   - Cook [[Broken Tooth Shroom Juice]] (10)
     - Each combine makes 2, so 5 total
1. Oogthor is in the main goblin encampment of Twark
   - Return to [[Oogthor]]
