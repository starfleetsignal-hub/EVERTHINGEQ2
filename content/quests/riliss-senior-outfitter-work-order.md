*Supplies at the front are running low. I hope I can trust you to ensure this order gets filled promptly and to the expected quality levels.*

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

- I need to create:
  - (6) [[Reinforced ferrite kite shield]]
  - (6) [[Spiked shield of bashing]]
  - (6) [[Chaos imbued pantaloons]]
  - (6) [[Bag of tricks]]
  - (6) [[Sturdy ferrite mace]]
  - (6) [[Sword of smiting]]
- Turn the completed order into [[Preparations Officer Zekilius]]

## Rewards

- At least 42g 44s 60c
- +750 faction with **Riliss**
