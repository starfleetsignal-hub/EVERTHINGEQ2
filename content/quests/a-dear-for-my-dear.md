## Steps

1. Talk to [[Merrik Hanz]] for tips on catching the burynai. {{waypoint 103, 8, -139}}
1. Using the [[Burynai leash]], catch [[A Rubble-rabble burrower]] or [[A Rubble-rabble detector]] from the [[The Rubble Rabble Burrow|Rubble Rabble Burrow]]. {{waypoint -76, -29, -94}}
1. Return the burynai to Braz Terrick.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- +3750 faction with **The City of Jinisk**
- [[Glowing Gem of Critical Magnitude]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Dungeon Delver's Orb]] (All Mages)
  - [[Orb of Queen's Retribution]]
  - [[Sphere of Wind Alteration]] (All Priests)
  - [[Villainous Cloud of Mist]]
