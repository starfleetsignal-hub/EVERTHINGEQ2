## Notes

1. Flight should be enabled in all zones on completion of this quest. However it may not be activated until you zone or camp.

## Steps

1. Speak with the foreman of the bivouac area in the [[Aether Wroughtlands]].
   - *You'll receive a recipe for [[W.H.O.O. Ultimate]] which auto scribes and 2 items are needed to craft it: [[Processed Aether Sample]] and [[W.H.O.O. Advanced]]
1. Craft the [[W.H.O.O. Ultimate]]
   - *The forge at {{waypoint 720.93, -43.60, -472.77}} is actually a multi-purpose craft table that can craft the [[W.H.O.O. Ultimate]]. Or you'll need a portable workbench plus 5 Zimaran coal*
   - *W.H.O.O. Ultimate autoscribes as usable ability in your knowledge book upon completion of the crafting. It also pops on a hot key if you have one open.*
1. Speak with the foreman, who completes the quest and mentions a strange golem nearby that needs help. There is a [[Burnish (Aspiring Artisan)]] at {{waypoint 694, -47, -439}} who has a follow up quest series starting with [[Disassemble: No!]].
