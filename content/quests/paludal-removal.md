## Steps

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 174.26, 61.90, -648.60}} to enter [[Fordel Midst: Wayward Manor (Solo)|Fordel Midst: Wayward Manor [Solo]]].
1. Speak to [[Vusor Umerus]] at {{waypoint 480.50, -23.69, -594.72}}.
1. Defeat the following:
   - 16 [[Reishi]]
   - 10 [[Paludal muck]]
   - 8 [[Muck diggers]]
1. Continue to House of Midst's Library at {{waypoint 741.13, -8.55, -707.60}}.
1. Speak to [[Vusor Umerus]] at {{waypoint 752.69, -8.55, -706.46}}.
1. Gather the following (I found them all on the floor, did not need to search the actual bookcases. One book was on a desk.)
   - 'Creations Of The Shadows'
   - 'Theoretical Sorcerous Engineering'
   - 'Ceremony Of The Stars'
   - 'Philosophy of Indefinite Studies'
   - 'Geomantic Philosophy'
   - 'Ethical Popular Alteration'
   - 'Chronology of Sorcerous Academics'
   - 'Applied Alchemy'
     - The location of each book is random.
   - *Note: The book graphics can be a bit glitchy, you may need to be very close and/or at the right camera angle to see them. Zooming in to first person or almost first person helps.*
   - Note: During the fight he will Summon 4 books, one over each fallen book shelf, destroy them so that you can destroy Famyra.
1. Speak to [[Vusor Umerus]] at {{waypoint 752.71, -8.55, -705.82}}.
