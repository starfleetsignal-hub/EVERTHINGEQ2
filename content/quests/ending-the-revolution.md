## Steps

1. Travel to [[Antonica]] and head north of [[Stormhold]] to {{waypoint -2336, 7, -68}}. Enter a special instance of the Shattered Vale called [[Squire's Vale]].
   - *Note: This instance scales down no lower than level 10.*
   - *Note: The named only scales up to level 70.*
1. There are several squires here, all one level below or same level as character, solo mobs. They can be avoided if you want.
1. Kill Shay Tivati. Be ready for a prolonged fight. She is one level below the character, solo v mob.
   - Just as you kill her the 1st time, she summons an earth elemental to protect her and she is untargettable. The elemental is same level as character, solo v mob.
   - After you kill the elemental, you have about 5 seconds rest before you have to fight Shay Tivati a 2nd time.
   - Just as you kill her the 2nd time, she summons an air elemental to protect her and she is untargettable. The elemental is same level as character, solo v mob.
   - After you kill the elemental, you have about 5 seconds rest before you have to fight Shay Tivati a 3rd time.
   - Just as you kill her the 3rd time, she summons a fire elemental to protect her and she is untargettable. The elemental is same level as character, solo v mob.
   - After you kill the elemental, you have about 5 seconds rest before you have to fight Shay Tivati a 4th time.
   - Just as you kill her the 4th time, she summons a water elemental to protect her and she is untargettable. The elemental is same level as character, solo v mob.
   - After you kill the elemental, you have about 5 seconds rest before you have to fight Shay Tivati a final time.
   - For the final fight, Shay Tivati turns into a wisp, but is otherwise the same as before.
1. Return to [[Matthias Siegemaker]]. You will automatically be offered the final citizenship quest.

- AA Experience
- -10,000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- +5,000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
