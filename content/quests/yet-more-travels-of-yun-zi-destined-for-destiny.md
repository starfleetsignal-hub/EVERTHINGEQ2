## Steps

1. Collect three souvenirs:
   - Travel to [[Icy Fingers]] in the [[Great Divide]] and collect a a Brain Coral souvenir. The quickest way to get to Icy Fingers is to use [[Otter's Rest]] Painting to Fina's Retreat. Alternatively, use [[Fast Travel]] to the [[Port of Tears]] in [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]] and speak with [[Stissa, Emissary of the Speaker|Stissa]] {{waypoint -1504.21, -214.28, -386.14}}. Possible locations for the brain coral are:
     - {{waypoint -1577, -536, -2390}}
     - {{waypoint -1634, -533, -2396}}
     - {{waypoint -1621, -536, -2429}}
     - {{waypoint -1555.84, -534.33, -2427.36}} {{waypoint -1591, -536, -2398}}
   - Travel to the [[Eastern Wastes]] and collect a Crystal souvenir. Possible locations are:
     - {{waypoint -903, -336, 2386}}
     - {{waypoint -948, -332, 2425}}
     - {{waypoint -974, -336, 2368}}
   - Fast Travel to [[Cobalt Scar]], or take the griffin from the Thurgadin docks in the [[Great Divide]], and collect a small black figurine. Possible locations for the figurine are:
     - {{waypoint 3625, -1016, 1666}}
     - {{waypoint 3622, -1015, 1643}}
     - {{waypoint 3653, -1016, 1704}}
     - {{waypoint 3684, -1015, 1690}}
1. Return to [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]].
