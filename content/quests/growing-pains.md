## Notes

- This is one of five "training sessions" you must complete in order to complete the quest [[Dream Weaving]] to receive a [[Gryphon Call]].
- You can only complete one of these five training sessions every 18 hours.

## Steps

1. Receive and scribe a [[Silk Bandage Recipe]].
1. Using a [[Sewing Table and Mannequin]], craft a [[Silk Bandage]] with the following items:
   - 1 [[Crystalline Spiderling Silk]] (You should still have the second silk from [[Dream Weaving]] quest, or get another from [[Ktlk]]. [[Everquest2:Ktlk|Ktlk]] is a spider that you can speak to in Goahmari Village).
   - 1 [[Bamboo shoot]]
   - 1 [[Ethereal filament]]
1. Click on your young gryphon or right-click Hail to finish the quest.

P.S.  Right-clicking and selecting "Bandage griffon forearm" will not complete the quest. >.<

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- Choice of [[Frost Gryphon]] or [[Boreal Gryphon]] on completion of the final of the 5 quests.
