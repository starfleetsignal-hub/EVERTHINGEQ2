## Steps

![Lightthirsty Valerian in Tentacle Fields](images/Lightthirsty_Valerian.png)
![Mechanimagica Vasculum next to Xhol'Rog](images/Mechanimagica_Vasculum.png)

1. Obtain 13 aberrant ebondust by hunting void reapers in [[Oogothl Necropolis]] around {{waypoint 452, 39, -438}}
1. Return to [[Tomian Keeneye (Oogothl Sprawl)|Tomian Keeneye]] at {{waypoint 230.97, 80.29, -213.44}}
1. Speak with [[Nanru]] at {{waypoint 260, 77, -128}}
1. Gather lightthirsty valerian at Tentacle Fields. Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint 421, 36, 59}}, {{waypoint 436, 36, 33}}
   - {{waypoint 398, 33, 23}}, {{waypoint 409, 36, -19}}
   - {{waypoint 399, 38, -19}}, {{waypoint 410, 37, -45}}
   - {{waypoint 506, 42, 20}}, {{waypoint 520, 47, 30}}
   - {{waypoint 500, 42, 69}}, {{waypoint 433, 35, 118}}
   - {{waypoint 410, 33, 121}}, {{waypoint 452, 38, 148}}
1. Return to [[Tomian Keeneye (Oogothl Sprawl)|Tomian Keeneye]].
1. Speak with [[Nanru]].
1. Obtain mechanimagica vasculum  - this is a box next to [[Xhol'Rog]] at {{waypoint -263, 39, 588}}.
   - **Note**: To see the box you must complete the following sub-quests:
   - [[Expendables for Tentacles]] from [[Nanru]]
   - [[Eye for an Eye]] from [[Xhol'Rog]], unlocks after completing [[Expendables for Tentacles]] from [[Nanru]]
     - [[Rage Against the Machines]] from [[Xhol'Rog]]
     - [[Temple of the Ver'Bohten Eye]] from [[Xhol'Rog]]
   - After completing these sub-quests you should see the box next to [[Xhol'Rog]] at {{waypoint -263, 39, 588}} and click on it for update.
1. Return to [[Tentacle Fields]]. {{waypoint 364, 36, 267}}   Gather 12 more lightthirsty valerian (little yellow flowers).
1. Return to [[Tomian Keeneye (Oogothl Sprawl)|Tomian Keeneye]] at the Gardens of Industry.
   - Tomian hints at working with Nanru as source of information. Complete Nanru's quests:
     - [[Following a Sprawling Trail]], [[Tools for Success]] and [[Rays the Roost]] before you can continue signature questline.
