## Notes

- requires 32,500 [[House Vahla (Faction)]]

## Steps

- Take also the quest [[Between a Rock and a Hard Beak]], as that is also in the Pakiat Bluff area
1. Collect and kill the following
   - Collect 10 Arcane Artifacts on the platforms in the [[Pakiat Bluffs]]. ![Arcane Artifact](images/Arcane_Artifact.jpg)
     - They look like various types of vases.
   - Kill 8 [[Molded sandbrute]] in the [[Pakiat Bluffs]] {{waypoint -701, 126, -497}}.
1. Return to [[Pannaxxun]] at {{waypoint -203, 573, -560}}.

## Rewards

- At least 90g 72s
- +1250 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1250 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1250 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
