## Steps

1. Return to [[Sir Tallen Yevix]] in [[West Freeport]] {{waypoint 78.84, -20.30, 16.03}} and right click on the clockwork next to him and select "Use".
1. Report back to [[Researcher Kendril]] in [[North Freeport]].
1. Collect the missing Dimensional Transducer pieces.
   - Go to [[The Commonlands]] to search the spot where the Antenna translocated {{waypoint -6, -48, -427}}, near the [[Ulteran Spires (CL)|Ulteran spires]]. Collect a purple shiny somewhere near the spire.
   - Use the swirling disc inside the spires to travel to [[Lavastorm]]. Collect a purple shiny somewhere near the spire {{waypoint -380.72, -31.24, -601.41}}.
   - Take the spires to [[The Thundering Steppes]].  Collect a purple shiny somewhere near the spire {{waypoint 1140.34, -25.51, 173.62}}.
   - ***Note***: Be sure and physically collect the shinies. This quest gives you an update for them just by proximity, but [[Tinmizer's Magnificent Messenger]] requires you to collect them.
1. Talk to Researcher Kendril in North Freeport.

## Rewards

One of the following:

- [[Elixir of the Wind]] x5
- [[Philter of Peacefulness]] x5
