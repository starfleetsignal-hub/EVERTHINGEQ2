## Steps

1. Travel to [[Frostfang Sea]] and gather the following in order:
   1. 3 Pilgrim's Landing crustose spores: {{waypoint -245.90, 38.44, -794.76}}
   1. 3 Ry'Gorr Isle driftwood: {{waypoint 988.26, 24.68, -325.92}}
   1. 1 to 3 Erollisi bloom - near the [[Cairn of the Huntress]]: {{waypoint 61.14, 148.14, -41.94}}
      - *Note: the amount of Erollisi bloom that is needed, is different for each toon*
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].

## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable

- Unlock the [[Days of Summer: 2023|Days of Summer 2023: Week 5]] Holiday event rewards that you can purchase from [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2293, -199, 2506}}
