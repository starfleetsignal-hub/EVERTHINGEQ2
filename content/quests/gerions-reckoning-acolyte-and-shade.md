## Steps

1. Enter The Acolyte's Arcane Study (the portal near Lady Najena)
   - Note: multiple characters can enter the same instance, but each must click on the portal.
1. Track down the acolyte
1. Remove the barrier protecting the acolyte
   - Click on the Voidskrit Blade on the table at {{waypoint -24, -15, -401}} and then click on the barrier to destroy it
1. Destroy the acolyte
1. Speak with Lady Najena (in Gerion at {{waypoint -105, 0, 31}} )
