## Steps

1. I need to lure [[An etherveil planeshifter|etherveil planeshifters]] into the [[Energy Flux Vacuity|traps]] Olvin provided me. Once the traps have collected their essence, I should collect them and return them to Olvin for analysis.
   - Trap etherveil planeshifter essence within Olvin's traps, then collect them (6)
     - The etherveil planeshifters can be found in the water nearby.  Place the trap on the ground, out of range of one of the mobs.  Move in to aggro/attack the ethereal planeshifter, then move back so that the mob travels over the trap.  It will then emit black smoke.  At that point, you can kill the mob, and collect the trap.  If you run out of traps, return to Olvin and hail him again.
     - Alternatively, fly close and get the agro on the planshifter, then drag it to the coast, when it's over land, run back to it so you're pretty much on top of it and THEN cast the trap.  Kill and pick up the esscence
1. I need to bring these traps back to Olvin for analysis.
   - Return the essences to Olvin
