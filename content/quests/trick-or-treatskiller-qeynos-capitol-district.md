## Notes

- Recipe for fright conjuration powder is given upon accepting quest. It's not automatically scribed so look for it in your bags. However if you've done the quest in previous years you might already have the recipe scribed.
- You will need 30 basic coal

![](images/Ephemeral_Fright.png) ![](images/A_scarecrow.png) ![](images/Recent_burial.png)

- There will be various objects to click across the Qeynos zones to get the required items for this quest.
  - You will need six (6) each of the following:
    - Ephemeral Fright - These are the purple floating skulls in random locations around the city.
    - Scarecrow Bat - Use the Find NPC from the guard to locate a Scarecrow. Click on the scarecrow repeatedly to get the bats. There is a pause between clicks.
    - Fresh Burial Dirt - These are around the tombstones you hit with your shovel for Waking the Dead.

## Steps

- Craft fright conjuration powder (6). The nearest Workbench from Zintris is in The Herb Jar, second floor at {{waypoint 650, -12, 243}}
- Return to Zintris
- Place the Candy Dispensers by clicking on the green and black swirls.
  - Stables {{waypoint 433, -20, 237}}
  - Qeynos Harbor, near Graystone Yard {{waypoint 837, -25, -70}}
  - Elddar Grove {{waypoint 637, -18, -267}}
- Return to Zintris

## Rewards

One of the following:

- [[Ephemeral Frights Plushie]]
- 15 [[Candy Corn|Candy Corns]]
- [[Ephemeral Frights]] (flavor pet)
- unlocks candy dispensers you just placed, they give special candy with various effects when used
