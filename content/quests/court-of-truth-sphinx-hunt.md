## Notes

- Synchronize steps in this quest with ones in [[Court of Truth: The Terrorgore]], so as not to waste time.

## Steps

1. Gather (or puchase) the following minerals by mining [[Gemstone Nodes#Tier 6 (level 50-59)|Tier 6 gemstone nodes]]:
   - 20 [[Beryllium cluster]]
   - 20 [[Rough Nacre]]
   - Go to [[The Croc Caves]] {{waypoint -1330, -226, -636}} in [[The Sinking Sands]] and mine 'sand scoured stone' at the base of the cave walls.
1. Speak to [[Steward of the Vizier]] in [[The Tower of the Moon]] {{waypoint -150, 176, -150}} in [[Maj'Dul]].
1. Go to the Sinking Sands:
   - Kill [[Anaz Mal diviner|Anaz Mal diviners]] for 15 common updates.
   - Kill Alliz Raef Ew lizardmen for 25 uncommon updates.
1. Return to Steward of the Vizier.
1. Go to [[The Clefts of Rujark]] and kill 50 orcs.
1. Go to {{waypoint -1320, -188, -1130}} in the Sinking Sands.
1. Place the staves on five rocks, one of which is on the above ledge. When all are placed, [[Altairenanhk (Sinking Sands)|Altairenanhk]] will come in flying after several moments. Kill him for the quest update.
   - Altairenanhk has a knockback, so either fight in the cave, or pull back to land. If you die in the attempt, you can resummon him without having to redo any previous steps.
1. Return to Ishara, Caliph of Truth.
