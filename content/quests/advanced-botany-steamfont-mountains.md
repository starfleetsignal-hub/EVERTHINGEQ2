## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable

## Steps

![Spider Ivy!(Sagebrush)(images/Sagebrush.png)!(Drakefang Berries)(images/Drakefang_Berries.png)](images/Spider_Ivy.png)

1. Travel to [[Steamfont Mountains|Steamfont mountains]] and gather
   - 3 spider ivy - Found growing around the base of tree trunks (red leaves) in the fields below [[Raven Eye Spire]] {{waypoint -982, 182, 602}}  **Note:** These do NOT have a sparkle effect.
   - a sagebrush - Spawns around the top edges of [[Grikbar Crater]] - possible locations {{waypoint -93, 95, 1028}} ; {{waypoint -91, 108, 1288}} ; {{waypoint 12, 100, 1153}} ; {{waypoint -221, 93, 1269}} ; {{waypoint -295, 96, 1199}}; {{waypoint -2, 104, 1111}}
   - 3 drakefang berries -  Found in [[Dragonscale Hills]] {{waypoint -915, 100, 1365}}
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].

## Rewards:

- Unlock the [[Days of Summer: 2024|Days of Summer 2024: Week 4]] "account flag"
