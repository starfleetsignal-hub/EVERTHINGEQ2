## Steps

1. Enter [[Karnor's Castle]] and use [[Catalogue Creature]] on the following mobs in no particular order:
   - [[A drolvarg guardian]], directly ahead of the entrance gate {{waypoint 220, -57, 129}}
   - [[A drolvarg trainee]], continue past the guardians to the [[Drolvarg Training Grounds]] {{waypoint 38, -63, 146}}
   - [[Drolvarg ballistaleer]], north of the entrance gate, in the corner turret {{waypoint 284, -48, -4}}
1. Return to [[Xvenloo Ghox]] outside Karnor's Castle.

  - [[Witchdoctor's Ill Omen Chain Boots]] (Shamans)
