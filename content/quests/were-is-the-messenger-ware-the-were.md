I have been told to find Renfry in his basement in Forlorn Gist. Not only do I have a message for him from the Far Seas Supply Division, but I am also hoping he may have news of Mairen.

## Materials Required

- 10 [[Amsonia Roots]]
- 30 [[Forlorn Ore]]
- 15 [[Greytusk Hide]]
- 30 [[Narra Wood]]
- 10 [[Star Diopside]]
- 1 [[Tsavorite]] --*rare*
- 1 [[Astral Incense]]
- 3 [[Astral Sandpaper]]

## Steps

1. Enter [[Renfry's Basement]] in [[Forlorn Gist]]
   1. Travel to [[Forlorn Gist]]. You can use the [[Last Light Gate]] at {{waypoint 605, 144, 543}}.
      - *Note: Once you enter [[Forlorn Gist]], you will gain the ability to fly in [[Mahngavi Wastes]].*
   1. Enter [[Renfry's Basement]] in [[Little Town]] {{waypoint -363, 16, 77}}
1. speak with [[Renfry]]
1. speak with [[Renfry]] again to ask about [[Representative Mairen]], and receive [[Recipe Scroll: Forlorn Defenses]]
1. Craft some items to help with defenses.
   - Note: You can use the *Rickety Crafting Table* at {{waypoint -18, 0, 21}} inside [[Renfry's Basement]]
   - Craft 2 [[Forlorn Barricade|Forlorn Barricades]]
   - Craft [[Gist Ballista]]
   - Craft [[Sanctuary from Werewolves]]
1. Give [[Renfry]] the items. Wait a moment while Renfry tests the ballista.
1. Speak with [[Renfry]] again

## Rewards

- +5,000 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- At least 5p 21g 72s 4c 53013 status
