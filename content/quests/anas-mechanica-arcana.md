## Notes

- The duck parts respawn after around five seconds.

## Steps

![](images/Duck_Carcass.jpg)

- Find the lost clockwork duck, Quacker Lin.
  - Quacker Lin was found at {{waypoint -1099, 166, 584}}.  Near the climbable wall to [[Shiculiant]] in [[Recluse Hollow]].
- Find the lost clockwork duck, Quacker Stan.
  - Quacker Stan was found at {{waypoint -1327, 95, 849}}.  Near [[Torn Ear Springs]].
- Find the lost clockwork duck, Quacker Mort.
  - Quacker Mort was found at {{waypoint -1188, 68, 1256}}. Behind [[Kerafyrm's Crater]].
- Find the lost clockwork duck, Quacker Lemmy.
  - Quacker Lemmy was found at {{waypoint -419, 74, 1664}}. This is at the far back of the [[Steamfont Wetlands]] area (on top of a rock, not in the water).
- Return to [[Sergi Voombin]]

## Rewards

- [[Anas Mechanica Case]]
- At least 3g 10c
