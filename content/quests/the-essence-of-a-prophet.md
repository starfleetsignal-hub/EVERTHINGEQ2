## Steps

Note: You must first finish all of the Fina's Retreat quests up through The End of an Era and receive mail to be offered this Heritage Quest.

1. Speak with [[Brother Cato]] {{waypoint -23, 17, -151}} near the Tears of Jade Spring in [[The Village of Shin]].
1. Speak with [[Brother Lasca]] on the bottom floor of [[Edgewater Drains]] (the lowest of the three Freeport sewer zones). {{waypoint 139, 2, 94}}
   - To find Brother Lasca, go to [[The Thieves' Way]] from any Freeport city zone (West Freeport has the closest entrance), or from the sewer entrance in Commonlands for those of good alignment, then to {{waypoint 24, 1, 61}} to enter [[The Serpent Sewer]]. Next go to {{waypoint -243, 1, 11}} to enter [[Edgewater Drains]].
1. Seek out the first turtle spirit in [[Tenebrous Tangle]] at {{waypoint 152, -232, 167}}. This is near the [[Hidden Refuge]]. Click the floating turtle in the circle of stones. This zones you into the [[Realm of the Air Turtle]].
1. Defeat the embodiment of perseverance, the air spirit [[Nimbus]], a level 90^ ghost turtle at {{waypoint 88.46, -217.49, 188.82}}. He swims in pond under the waterfalls.
1. Use [[Lasca's Beacon]] to talk to [[Brother Lasca]]
1. Seek out the second turtle spirit in the [[Feerrott]] at {{waypoint -892, -7, 365}}. This is near the entrance to [[The Trembling Lagoon]]. Click the floating turtle. This zones you into the [[Realm of the Water Turtle]].
1. Defeat the embodiment of resilience, the water spirit [[Delmar]], a level 90^ ghost turtle near {{waypoint -903, -15, 114}}.
1. Use [[Lasca's Beacon]] to talk to [[Brother Lasca]]. The zone exit is on the island.
1. Seek out the third turtle spirit in [[Lesser Faydark]] at {{waypoint 0.74, -65, 211}}. This is behind the water falls near [[The Hidden Grotto]]. Click the floating turtle. This zones you into the [[Realm of the Earth Turtle]].
1. Defeat the embodiment of steadfastness, the earth spirit [[Festus]], a level 90^ ghost turtle near {{waypoint 825, 87, -601}}.
1. Use [[Lasca's Beacon]] to talk to [[Brother Lasca]].
1. Seek out the fourth turtle spirit in the [[Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -505, -133, -785}}. This is near [[The Twin Tears]]. Click the floating turtle. This zones you into the [[Realm of the Fire Turtle]].
1. Defeat the embodiment of willfulness, the fire spirit [[Neci]], a level 90^ ghost turtle near {{waypoint -558, -137, -852}}.
1. Use [[Lasca's Beacon]] to talk to [[Brother Lasca]].
1. Return to [[Osh]] in [[Great Divide]] at [[Iceclad Ocean]] at Fina's Retreat (He is under a tent to the right as you zone in. -1633, -535, -2428
1. Enter:   Tower of Frozen Shadow: Shadowed Corridors.
1. Speak to [[A resentful spirit]] {{waypoint 0, 47, 11}} on the second floor (the frozen library by using the mirror)
   - **NOTE:** You can use a cleared instance for this step, but you will still need to reset and go into the zone again for the next stage.
1. Kill [[Alvik the Cold]] {{waypoint 30, 211, 75}} on the third floor in [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Shadowed Corridors]].
1. Return to [[Osh]] in [[Great Divide]] at [[Iceclad Ocean]]

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c 83200 status
- Both of the Following:
  - [[Lodizal Shell Shield]]
  - [[The Legendary Lodizal Shell Shield]]
