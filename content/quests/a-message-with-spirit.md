## Steps

![a wandering kerran spirit](images/A_wandering_kerran_spirit.jpg)

1. Speak to [[A wandering kerran spirit]] that wanders around [[The Petrified Badlands]].
   - Seems to wander anywhere in the area from [[The Eye of Dartain]] {{waypoint 1780, 0, 4288}} to the northwestern edge of the Badlands at {{waypoint 2024, -15, 3880}} to just north of [[The Highland Salts]] at {{waypoint 1034, 31, 3494}}.  NOTE:  the last several times, he's been around {{waypoint 1647, -10.26, 4044}}.  Pretty much due NE of the Eye of Dartain transport pad.
   - You will be attacked by [[A wandering nightmare]] as you move around.
1. Return to [[Keyta Wicker|Keyta]]

## Reward

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- [[Kerra Shaman's Token]] (Quest update for [[Mending a Broken Land]])
