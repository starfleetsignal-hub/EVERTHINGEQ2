## Steps

1. From the dialogues choose to attack [[Zalyn D'Kilneld|Zalyn]]. This quest will then be offered.
1. [[Zalyn D'Kilneld|Zalyn]] and [[Bethra Lartius|Bethra]] will teleport away. Head southwest to {{waypoint 5, -348, 120}} where [[Zalyn D'Kilneld]], [[Bethra Lartius]] and [[Igrayne (Mob)|Igrayne]] will spawn and attack.
1. Loot the [[D'Kilneld Company Map]] from Zalyn's body. The quest will automatically complete.

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- [[D'Kilneld Company Emblem of Power]]
