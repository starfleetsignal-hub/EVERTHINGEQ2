## Steps

1. Apply [[Hope (Crafted)|Hope]] to 6 large bones surrounding Yeppa's Last Stand.
   - Stand next to the bone and use the potion from either your hotbar or inventory. (Note: the bones do not highlight, and are not clickable,  just stand next to them and use the potion.)
   - If you can't safely access the bones outside of the camp, wait (approximately 5 minutes) and the timer will reset for the bones that are safe to access, allowing you to reapply Hope to them again.
   - Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 4287.62, -1095.82, 2047.56}}
     - {{waypoint 4297.42, -1092.97, 2034.61}}
     - {{waypoint 4301.28, -1086.58, 2012.77}}
     - {{waypoint 4276.93, -1096.89, 2068.77}}
     - {{waypoint 4258.46, -1096.55, 2081.88}}
     - {{waypoint 4403, -1089, 1981}}
     - {{waypoint 4308, -1087, 2007}}
1. Speak to Yeppa.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g.
