## Steps

1. Slay 10 [[A granitescale basilisk|granitescale basilisks]] found northeast of [[The Tower of the Oracles]] at {{waypoint -1225, 0, -795}}.
1. Slay a [[A darkclaw crab]] found on the beach next to [[Coastal Grove]] at {{waypoint -2400, 0, -450}}
1. Return to [[Licha]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Band of Disturbed Visions]]
  - [[Crest of Disturbed Visions]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Loop of Disturbed Visions]] (All Priests)
  - [[Ringlet of Disturbed Visions]] (All Scouts)
