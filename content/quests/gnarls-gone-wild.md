## Steps

![Colichaw bulb](images/Colichaw_bulb.png)

1. Remove 8 invasive colichaw bulbs (Thin pink stalks) in [[The Gnarls]] Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint 72, 46, -500}}, {{waypoint 24, 49, -568}}, {{waypoint 19, 43, -544}}
   - {{waypoint -10, 39, -522}}, {{waypoint 30, 42, -474}}, {{waypoint 22, 42, -459}}
   - {{waypoint -9, 38, -460}}, {{waypoint -16, 35, -479}}
1. Slay a blightwing fiend that spawns when you remove 8th colichaw bulb.
1. Slay 12 more blightwind fiends in the area.
1. Return to [[Tomian Keeneye (Yon Gorroth)|Tomian Keeneye]]

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following:
  - [[Coral Forest Charm of Rifts]]
  - [[Star Blood Charm of Frenzy]]
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
