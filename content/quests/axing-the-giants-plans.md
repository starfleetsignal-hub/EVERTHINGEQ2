## Steps

![sample axe in tree](images/Duhjalm_ettin_axe.png)

1. Steal 10 ettin axes
   - They are clickable axes called a Duhjalm ettin axe on the ground and in trees at the Flamespout Fields at {{waypoint -511, -453, 3759}}
   - Areas for axes: {{waypoint -564, -483, 4032}} Scattered all over
1. Return to [[Klipklap]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- +300 faction with **Othmir of Velious**
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Arctic Ring of Kromzek Incitement]]
  - [[Arctic Ring of Forestation]]
  - [[Arctic Band of Ettin Slaying]]
  - [[Arctic Band of the Weaponer]]
