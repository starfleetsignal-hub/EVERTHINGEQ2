## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- You can trade any extra quest items picked up to [[Gixen]], found on the [[Enchanted Lands]] docks, for [[Frozen Token of E'ci|Frozen Tokens of E'ci]].
- If you are grouped with more than three people, you will be warned that the zone only allows three people. However, with group-wide zone-in, each will be placed in their own separate instance. Using CoV, the other two will be able join first group member.
  - May not be worth it, however, as there is no beasties' deaths recorded for other's quest, nor presents to pick up.

## Steps

![Location of the Gibblegibber Storehouse.](images/Gibblegibber_storehouse_entrance.png)

1. Go to [[Enchanted Lands]] and enter [[Gigglegibber Gifty Storehouse]], underneath the docks of the [[Lost Village of Bobick]]. {{waypoint 41, -30, 118}}
1. Speak with [[Gomet]], [[Santa Glug]]'s helper. {{waypoint -22, 1, 23}}
1. Help clear the storehouse of the invading creatures and collect Santa Glug's "gifties":
   - To find the gifties you must break the attackable crates found throughout the instance. It is possible to clear the instance and not obtain all the needed quest items. If this happens, zone out and in to reset the spawns.
   - Gather 6 [[Red Gifty Box|Red Gifty Boxes]] -OR- 6 [[Green Gifty Box|Green Gifty Boxes]] (requirement is randomly chosen; boxes have wooden appearance).
   - Gather 6 [[Clockwonky Toy Gnome|Clockwonky Toy Gnomes]] -OR- 6 [[Clockwonky Toy Monster|Clockwonky Toy Monsters]] (requirement is randomly chosen; boxes have rusty appearance).
   - Slay 9 of one type of monster found in the instance.
1. Return to [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]] and speak with [[Gardy Ex-Giftgiver]].

## Rewards

- Coin
- [[Thrice-Polarized Frostrotron]] (for completing the quest the first time)
- 3 [[Frozen Token of E'ci|Frozen Tokens of E'ci]] (for completing the quest a second time and every time thereafter)
