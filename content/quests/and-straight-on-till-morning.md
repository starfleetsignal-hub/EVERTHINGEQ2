## Steps

1. Speak with [[Researcher Aillena]] on the [[Cloudmist Isle]] {{waypoint -568, 248, -177}} in [[The Barren Sky]]
1. Free some trapped wind essences
   - **Adventure way**: Collect 5 wind essences by killing [[A cloudmist walker|cloudmist walkers]] or [[A nebulous haze|nebulous hazes]] in the area.
   - **Tradeskill way**: Collect 5 wind essences by freeing them from tanglebrush in the area (no longer requires a high gathering skill).
1. Release the essences on the highest point I can find.
   - Click the green shiny on the Isle of the Watchers (above The Strifewind Isle) at {{waypoint 90, 471.41, -423.28}}
1. Speak with [[Ixode]], Aillena's air elemental.
