- You must have completed the 10 sabotage quests in the Commonlands series and the 8 quests in the Freeport District series before you are offered this quest.

## Steps

Gil McMartin confided in me what is required to weaken the resolve of the citizens of Freeport: I must destroy the nightly guard patrol records, used to confirm the security of the city, and positioned throughout Freeport.

1. Enter Freeport.
1. Within 30 minutes, find and destroy 5 guard ledgers.
   - On Origins server there are located
     1. 271.04, -2.34, 121.99 - West Freeport
     1. 302.62, -1.87, 90.70 - West Freeport
     1. -94.14, -21.43, -83.37 - North Freeport
     1. -103.82, -21.43, -73.44 - North Freeport
     1. -236.82 -48.96 -13.95 - East Freeport
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +3000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- **Note**: This quest does not grant any experience or achievement experience.
