This is part 6 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Freeport]] who wear Leather and Chain Armor ([[Shaman|shaman]]).

## Steps

1. You must collect items from creatures in [[Nektulos Forest]].
   - Kill 2 [[A mist grinnin savage (Nektulos Forest)|mist grinnin savages]] for their enchanted ore.  ({{waypoint -188, 3, -691}} just north of the [[D'Abth Bridge]])
   - Kill 10 [[An elder ash owlbear|elder ash owlbears]] for their talons. {{waypoint -840, 91, 169}}
   - Kill 12 [[A bloated soul leech|bloated soul leeches]] for their ooze. (in the eastern river, and [[Mound Lake]] ({{waypoint -500, 0, -415}}))
   - Kill [[Narek Bonecarver]], (level 25^ heroic w/ 2 friends) he spawns at the pirate camps on beach when you walk across the wooden planks towards the beach.
1. Return to Vassi.

**EDITOR'S NOTE**: There are rumors that walking over the wooden planks that lead to the beach will spawn Narek if you have this quest activated already.
