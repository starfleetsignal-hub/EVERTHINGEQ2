## Steps

1. Eliminate 3 [[A vagabond|vagabonds]] and 3 [[A courtesan|courtesans]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
   - To be able to attack them, you will need to talk to them first.
   - You'll find them scattered throughout the city, but the best place to hunt for them is in the [[New Foreign Quarter]] {{waypoint 93, 22, 74}}.
   - **Note**: If a dreadguard spots you taking one of these creatures out, it will attack.
1. Return to [[Clerk V'Nox]] and she will give you a follow-on quest, [[Fees and Paperwork]]
