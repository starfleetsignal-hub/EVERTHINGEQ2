## Steps

1. Enter [[Throne of Fear: Ascension (Advanced Solo)]] or Throne of Fear (Heroic) at {{waypoint -2297, 202, 491}}  in the Fear Cave.  *(You can save time by doing this quest at the same time as [[And His Name Is...]] so you won't have to wait for zone reset.)*
  - one can use previously cleared version of this zone from earlier quest completion, no need to reset.
1. Look over the zone walkthrough for help with this zone.
1. Collect 4 runestone pieces. They are at:
   - {{waypoint -195, 0, 80}} (Pharinich's room) ![](images/Runestone_piece.jpg)
   - {{waypoint -174, -27, -1}} (Planar Manifestation's room, east side)
   - {{waypoint -10, 0, -203}} (Thoughtbleeder's room)
   - {{waypoint 127, 16, -157}} (antechamber before Baelon's room)
  - Group members experience zone-wide updates.
1. Speak to [[Alzilius]] again in Eidolon Jungle.
1. Kill [[A fearscarred ward]]. This mob is outside of the zone, stands next to Alzilius.
1. Speak to [[Alzilius]] again to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Exquisite Sconce of Fear]]
