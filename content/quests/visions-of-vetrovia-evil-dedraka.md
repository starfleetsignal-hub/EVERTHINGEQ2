## Note

*You **must** complete the **sub** **quest line**  that starts with [[Locust Commotions]] and ends with [[Bona Fide Treasure Seeker]] **before you can** **begin Step 6 on this quest.** Otherwise, you will need to RESET the instance, [[Karuupa Jungle: Dedraka's Descent (Solo)|Karuupa Jungle: Dedraka's Descent [Solo]]] and run it TWICE instead of once. The final sub quest, [[Bona Fide Treasure Seeker]], may be completed together with the Signature Quest in the solo instance.*

## *Steps*

1. Enter [[Karuupa Jungle: Dedraka's Descent (Solo)|Karuupa Jungle: Dedraka's Descent [Solo]]] {{waypoint -231, 109, -692}}.
   1. [[Zaag the Unburied]] {{waypoint -310, 105, -629}}.
      - Teleports to adds. Chase him down, then tank and spank.Tip: clear all the adds leading up to this fight so the adds will not join.
      - note: if on [[Visions of Vetrovia: Evil Dedraka]] quest, click the corruption that spawns behind him after he is dead {{waypoint -293, 105, -614}}.
      - ![Residual Corruption](images/Residual_Corruption_01.jpg)
   1. [[Scarfeather]] Epic X3 {{waypoint -415, 115, -422}}.
      - Has increments. To increase damage to him, pull Scarfeather to the bubbles in front of a grazing ceredon. Note: need to approach the front of the ceredon or they may not knock him down.
   1. [[Krelburn]] {{waypoint -480, 124, -289}}.
      - Do not agro the blazing drake flies, they give him a buff (when you agro the flies they will fly to you and Krelburn). Krelburn will eat any flies near it to gain buffs. You will see a new icon appear on the first left row of buffs it has, and he will turn dark red and firey. There is an area to the right of bridge {{waypoint -472, 125, -313}} where there are fewer flies that will spawn. If Krelburn eats a fly, cast defensive or healing spells/Combat arts and wait for his buff to expire as his buff will reflect damage to you. (You can try to damage Krelburn when it has it's buff up but you need mitigation especially against Heat damage. This advice is for players with only starter gear as your DPS will be very low for this boss.)
      - Dance to stay out of the black spots which will spawn a plant which is a 1-shot. He will emote "**sends out more of its seeking vines underground**"
      - Joust when he emotes **"Krelburn reaches..."**
   1. DO NOT kill [[Cannibrea]] as part of this quest.  You need him 'up' for when you get to the Bona Fide Treasure Seeker side quest - it uses the same zone. If you kill Cannibrea now, you will have to reset the zone and start over.
   1. Once [[Krelburn]] is dead, Click the vines behind him, at {{waypoint -511, 133, -263.84}}, to climb up. Walk to the vines at {{waypoint -588, 175, -54}} and use them to climb down on the other side. Then go to the corruption source. Behind the stone, speak to [[Vaziri]] {{waypoint -644, 117, 115}}.
      1. (If you combined this with Bona Fide Treasure Seeker: You must kill the three epics before you can touch the stone. Then, touch the stone, then Vaziri will appear and you can talk to him for the update. Then, use the portal to zone out. Immediately zone back in to find Findink to update Bona Fine Treasure Seeker.)
      1. Click the **Corruption Source** at {{waypoint -644, 118, 123}}, which is guarded by epic 2x encounter [[Elder Gromekus]] with adds [[Ponshudu]] and [[Olipna|Olipna.]]*See [[Karuupa Jungle: Dedraka's Descent (Solo)|Karuupa Jungle: Dedraka's Descent [Solo]]] for a zone guide to kill the named.*
         - Hail [[Vaziri]] , then...
   1. Leave the dungeon and go to [[Shaman Bawiggi]]'s hut in [[Karuupa Jungle]] {{waypoint 731, 168, -394}}. Talk to [[Vaziri]] {{waypoint 730.19, 168.49, -398.55}} who now stands next to [[Shaman Bawiggi]].
      - NOTE:  If you have the repeatable quest "Undoing Dedraka Damage" in your quest book, Vazari might not talk to you. Complete this quest then DO NOT take the next one.
- Hail [[Vaziri]] to start the ceremony.
- Return to [[Chief Zubbluggib]] {{waypoint 493.49, 1145.24, -610.27}}
