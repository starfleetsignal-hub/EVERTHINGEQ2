## Steps

1. Speak with [[Taskmaster Urz]] and receive a [[Quatcha harness]]. {{waypoint -288, 21, 4}}
1. Use the quatcha harness to leash 3 [[A jungle quatcha|jungle quatchas]] and bring them back to Taskmaster Urz. Only one can be leashed at a time.
1. Return to Taskmaster Gax.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- +5,000 faction with **Dalnir's Wheel Taskmasters**
- [[Quatcha Catcher|Title: "Quatcha Catcher"]]
