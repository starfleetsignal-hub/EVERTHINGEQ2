## Steps

1. kill the following
   - Slay 15 small insects near the hives - [[A teralid mound-guardian|teralid mound-guardians]] and [[A teralid mulcher|mulchers]], and [[A virulid assaulter|virulid assaulters]] and [[A virulid sentry|sentries]] update.
   - Slay 10 medium insects near the hives - [[A teralid patroller|teralid patrollers]] , [[A teralid hive-guardian|hive-guardians]] and [[A teralid cultivator|cultivators]] or [[A teralid terraformer]] update.  *Note: Best place to find these is Queens Hive - North {{waypoint -1972.93, -530.88, -1479.90}}*
   - Slay 5 large insects near the hives - [[A virulid bombast|virulid bombasts]] or conqueror update. *Note: A good area for small and large ones is the area around {{waypoint -2650, -551, -1147}}*
1. Investigate the insect area around the hives. Go to one of the hive tunnel entrances:
   - The northern entrance at {{waypoint -2109.23, -527.13, -1516.69}}
   - The southern entrance at {{waypoint -2746.90, -546.67, -1242.94}}
   - Upon reaching the area outside the southern hive tunnel entrance, you will be contacted telepathically ("a voice in my head") asking you to meet with them.
1. Enter the hive tunnels and find [[Adephala|Queen Adephala]] at {{waypoint -2622, -607, -1654}}.
1. Speak to the Queen to finish the quest.

## Notes

- Although this quest is listed as heroic in your journal, it can be completed solo.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
