## Notes

Seems that Gluppin forgets to actually take the statues, leaving you with 400 wt. of junk

## Steps

1. Find the statues that represent the aspects of the Marr twins
   - An ancient statue of Valor {{waypoint -172, -21, -26}} East wall of the Cathedral of Thoughtlessness area, as you leave the corridor from the entrance, turn right
   - A statue of Honor {{waypoint -28, -61, -270}} West wall of the room with ramp leading to [[A Reanimated Hand]]'s room
   - A statue of Courage {{waypoint -218, -19, -250}} SW wall of Fount of Frenzied Blood (Furdip)
   - A statue of Love {{waypoint -156, -45, -315}} on the East wall in the Throne Room of Guk (King Vroak Vriipp)
1. Return to [[Historian Gluppin]]

## Rewards

- At least 31g 73s 78c
