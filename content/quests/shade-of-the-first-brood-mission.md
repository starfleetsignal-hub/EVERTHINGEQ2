## Steps

1. Defeat [[Jaled Dar's Shade]].
   - This is a straightforward tank and spank, just pour the damage on.
   - The many adds surrounding the dragon need to be rounded up and brought back to the dragon and destroyed.
   - One or more players should grab adds and bring them back to the dragon where they'll be facing a barrage of player damage. They help the dragon not take damage, so they must be eliminated.
   - Adds will respawn at certain points of the fight, so again, someone should gather them up and bring them back to the group.

- [[Shade of the First Brood Reward Crate]]
