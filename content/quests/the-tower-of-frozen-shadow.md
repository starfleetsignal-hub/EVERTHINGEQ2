## Steps

This quest updates by killing mobs and picking up the mirror quests. You must pick up the mirror quests yourself; quests shared to you by friends will not update properly. If you do this quest in tandem with [[Finding the Dain]], [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Shadowed Corridors - Shadowbeast Cooks]], and [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Umbral Halls - Mirrored Gargoyles & Golem Ushers|Tower of Frozen Shadow: Umbral Halls - Mirrored Gargolyes & Golem Ushers]], you will save significant time and effort.

1. [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Shadowed Corridors]] [[First Floor: Submergence]]
   - Obtain a key from [[Xalgoti (Heroic)|Xalgoti]].
1. [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Shadowed Corridors]] [[Third Floor: The Shadow Barracks]]
   - Speak with Vethilot the Young at (/way -55.74, 206.63, 26.68). Give him a minute to spawn there after you approach. Accept his quest, [[The Alliance]].
   - Gather the potion bottle at {{waypoint 58.01, 210.29, -34.11}} to receive the quest [[A Ruinous Bath]].
1. [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Shadowed Corridors]] [[Second Floor: The Frozen Library]]
   - Kill [[Spectral Librarian Emiida]] and obtain the key
   - Gather Tserrina's Journal at {{waypoint 112, 44, 54}} to receive the quest [[Tserrina's Strife]].
1. [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Umbral Halls]] [[Fourth Floor: Darkness Rising]]
   - Kill [[Deception]] and obtain the key
   - When zoned in move to {{waypoint -43.79, 293.19, -4.69}} to receive the quest [[A Light for the Shadow]]
1. [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Umbral Halls]] [[Sixth Floor: Stewardship]]
   - Kill [[Narmek Berreka]] and obtain the key
   - Click Mirror at {{waypoint 132.08, 548.50, -57.49}} to receive the quest [[Mirror, Mirror]].
1. [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Umbral Halls]] [[Fifth Floor: Bride's Scorn]]
   - Kill [[Dizzina the Lulled]] and obtain the key
   - Click gift box {{waypoint 43.51, 358.40, -128.11}} to receive the quest [[Not Him Again]].
1. [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Haunt of Syl'Tor]] [[Seventh Floor: The Palindrome]]
   - Kill [[Trouveur D'Kish]] and obtain the key
   - after Palindrome is warped receive the quest [[Puzzled]].
1. [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Haunt of Syl'Tor]] [[Eighth Floor: The Haunt of Syl'Tor]]
   - Kill [[Tserrina Syl'Tor]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g
- 20454 status (At level 100)
- [[Mirror of the Shadows]] - received when step 7 is completed, this item placed in a guild hall allows characters to zone directly from their guild hall into the TOFS heroic and raid zones that they have gained access to.
- [[Shards of the Scorned]]
