## Steps

1. When you zone after finishing previous quest, you should get a game mail from [[Trade Liaison Wells]] with this quest.
   - Good Characters
     1. Go to the [[Ironforge Exchange]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District|North Qeynos]] and talk to Wells at {{waypoint 349, -17, -89}}
     1. Use the **blue** transporter in [[The Concordium Tower]] {{waypoint 705, -20, 127}} at and click on the sparkly books.![sparkly books](images/Sparkly_books.jpg)
   - Evil Characters
     1. Go in [[The City of Freeport]] to [[West Freeport]] to [[Coalition of Tradesfolke]] and talk to Wells at {{waypoint 33.84, -17.33, 102.94}}
     1. Click on 5 sets of sparkly books on various bookshelves in [[Academy of Arcane Science]] at {{waypoint 18, -7, -119}} in [[North Freeport]] (NOTE: All 5 sets do not appear at once. Each set will sparkle once a set is clicked.)
1. Return to Trade Liaison Wells

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
