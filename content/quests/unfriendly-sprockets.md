## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Eliminate four clockwork engineers or assemblers in [[The Clockwork Assembly Plant]] {{waypoint -131, 21, 41}}
1. Obtain 20 pristine gears and sprockets from any clockwork
1. Return to [[Agent Grisscob]].

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- On the third and sixth completions: [[Unusual Square Gear]]
- Repeating this quest, as well as completing the other repeatable quests ([[Built to Kill]] and [[Law of Viscosity]]) five times each: [[Summon Clockwork Surveillance Unit]]
- On the tenth completion: [[Klak'Anon Mine Cart]]
