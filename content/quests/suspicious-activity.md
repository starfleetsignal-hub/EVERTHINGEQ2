*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

The following monsters are on a shared spawn south and east of the quest giver. A location with a high concentration is {{waypoint 36, -20, 747}}.

1. Kill 5 [[A Sabertooth runt|Sabertooth runts]]
1. Kill 2 [[A Sabertooth neophyte|Sabertooth neophytes]]
1. Inspect the boat on the shore to the south west. {{waypoint 99, -36, 738}}
1. Return to [[Tyllia Navar]]

## Rewards

- At least 92c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Apprentice Embalmer's Blade]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Balanced Katana (Level 13)|Balanced Katana]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Child's Mace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Cracked Warlock's Sceptre]] (All Mages)
  - [[Darkened Blade]] (All Mages)
  - [[Heavy Metal Longsword]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Ulak of the Martyr]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
- +85 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
