## Steps

1. Read ***The Journal of Rocco Barisano***.
1. Kill 6 [[Refuse toad|refuse toads]]. *(found throughout hallways)*
1. Read the book again.
1. Kill 5 [[Measly filthy moccasin|measly filthy moccasins]]. *(found throughout hallways)*
1. Read the book again.
1. Kill 6 [[Immature sludgewater|immature sludgewaters]]. *(found throughout hallways)*
1. Read the book again.
1. Kill 8 [[Sewage vermin]]. *(found throughout hallways)*
1. Read the book again.
1. Kill 7 [[A crazed channel mender|crazed channel menders]]. {{waypoint -25, 1, -75}}
1. Read the book again.
1. Kill 6 crazed channel menders. {{waypoint -25, 1, -75}}
1. Read the book again.
1. Kill 5 [[Sewage nuisance|sewage nuisances]]. *(in barred recesses along the walls of the larger rooms)*
1. Read the book again.
1. Kill 10 refuse toads. *(found throughout hallways)*
1. Read the book again.
1. Kill 4 [[Drudge globule|drudge globules]]. {{waypoint -117, 0, -123}}
1. Read the book a final time.
