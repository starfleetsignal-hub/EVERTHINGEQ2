## Notes

- Requires doing [[Stretched Reality]] from [[Assistant Shortspanner]] before attempting to pick up this quest.

## Steps

1. Place a fallout counter inside the ruins of Malvonicus' Tower (left-click the white sparkles) {{waypoint 907, 7, 1391}}
1. Place a fallout counter a bit southeast of the gate {{waypoint 1080, 8, 1329}} ![A fallout counter in position](images/Fallout_counter.jpg)
1. ...Northwest edge of Sapswill's Rest, overlooking the ocean {{waypoint 1314, 17, 1168}}
1. ...Past the north gate of Sapswill's Rest {{waypoint 1252, 13, 1052}}
1. ...West end of the northern beach {{waypoint 1260, 9, 855}}
1. Return to Clackwhinge

- At least 1p 29g 60s

  - [[The Cloak of Distant Places]]
