## Notes

- This quest scales to the adventure level tier of the character, i.e. level 21-30 characters will receive the level 25 version of the quest.
- This quest is repeatable up to 10 times. per adventure level tier.
- You may need to kill more than the listed number of monsters in order to retrieve the required drops for this quest.
- Players adventure level 121+ will need to mentor down to a lower level range in order to get these quests offered.

## Steps

1. Slay the following monsters (for your adventure level tier) until you retrieve 5 of the indicated quest item:
   - **Tier 1 (Level 1-10)**
     - [[A crusty rustbeast|Crusty rustbeasts]] in [[Timorous Deep]] {{waypoint 205, 5, -2}}
     - [[Haoaeran scavenger|Haoaeran scavengers]] and [[Haoaeran skyhunter|Haoaeran skyhunters]] in [[Timorous Deep]] {{waypoint 81, 2, -48}}
   - **Tier 2 (Level 11-20)**
     - [[A rotting sathirian priest|Rotting sathirian priests]] in [[Timorous Deep]]
     - [[A vicious raptor|Vicious raptors]] in [[Timorous Deep]]
   - **Tier 3 (Level 21-30)**
     - [[Shadesire defender|Shadesire defenders]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
     - [[A frenzied gorger|Frenzied gorgers]] and [[Mountain shrieker|mountain shriekers]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
   - **Tier 4 (Level 31-40)**
     - Wolves and coyotes in [[Steamfont Mountains]]
     - [[A Grikbar scamp|Grikbar scamps]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]]
   - **Tier 5 (Level 41-50)**
     - [[A yellow recluse spider|Yellow recluse spiders]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]]
     - [[A mountain stalker|Mountain stalkers]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]]
   - **Tier 6 (Level 51-60)**
     - [[The Lesser Faydark]] (solo)
       - [[A grovespirit acolyte|Grovespirit acolytes]], [[A grovespirit caller|callers]] and [[A grovespirit defender|defenders]]
       - [[A honeybrood worker|honeybrood workers]]
     - [[The Sinking Sands]] (heroic)
       - [[An Anaz Mal veilguard|Anaz Mal veilguards]] in [[The Living Tombs]]
       - Mummies, such as [[A Sulite tjat (Silent City)|Sulite tjat]] in [[The Silent City]]
   - **Tier 7 (Level 61-70)**
     - [[A living chest|Living chests]] in [[Kaladim]]
     - [[An obsidian construct|Obsidian constructs]] in [[Mistmoore Catacombs]]
   - **Tier 8 (Level 71-80)**
     - Option 1:
       - [[A trained war sokokar|trained war sokokars]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
       - [[A kunzar tiger|Kunzar tigers]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
     - Option 2:
       - Leeches in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
       - Sporconids in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
   - **Tier 9 (Level 81-90)**
     - [[A manglevine pariah|Manglevine pariahs]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]
     - [[A needlebeak bloodgorger|Needlebeak bloodgorgers]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]]
   - **Tier 10 (Level 91-100)**
     - [[Insidious fearlings]] in [[Eidolon Jungle]]
     - [[Churning obol|Churning obols]] in [[Obol Plains]]
   - **Tier 11 (Level 100-110)**
     - [[A diamondwing volaserpents|Diamondwing volaserpents]] in [[Plane of Magic]]
     - [[A cross greatmole|Cross greatmole]] in [[Plane of Magic]]
1. Return to [[Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber (Gorowyn)|Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber]] in [[Timorous Deep]].
