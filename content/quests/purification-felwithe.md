## Steps

1. Pray at an urn inside the [[Temple of Growth]]. {{waypoint -449, 49, -857}}
   - The praying urns will only spawn when the Tunarian Templars in the temple are present. This means that you must either sneak inside and pray quickly or pray while fighting. **Do not kill the Templars!**
1. Return to the altar in the [[Tower of Purity]]. {{waypoint -129, 72, -972}}
1. Go to the library and inspect a book on one of the tables. {{waypoint -395, 48, -949}}
   - This is a random book among the many, called "The recent history of Felwithe".
   - **Do not kill the librarians**, as you will need them for the next step.
1. Kill [[A librarian|librarian]]s, one at a time, until you get:
   1. Page one
   1. Page two
   1. Page three
1. Return to the altar in the Tower of Purity.
1. Kill [[A smith|smith]]s {{waypoint -384, 46, -748}}, one at a time, until you get:
   1. instructions
   1. the hilt
   1. the blade
   1. the blade sheath
1. Return to the altar in the Tower of Purity.
1. Inspect the urn at the center of the [[Pedestal of Purity]] {{waypoint -679, 85, -1158}} to start ring event. The urn takes a bit to start the event.
   - You have to kill 3 waves consisting of several non-linked elemental mobs each:
     1. Fire: 8x level 62^^^ [[Pure fire]]
     1. Water: 8x level 63^^^ [[Pure water]]
     1. Earth: 4x level 65^^^ [[Pure soil]]
1. Return to the altar in the Tower of Purity. You will receive [[Sacred garments (Version 2)|sacred garments]].
1. Go to [[Pool of Purity]] {{waypoint -185, 60, -150}} and enter it while wearing the sacred garments. The quest will auto-complete.
