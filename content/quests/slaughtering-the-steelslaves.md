## Notes

- You must complete [[Blades: Rujarkian Raiders]] for Hadoth Al'Uzza to offer you this quest.

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Clefts of Rujark]] at {{waypoint -725, -111, -91}} and kill the following:
   - 6 [[A Steelslave watcher|Steelslave watchers]]
   - 6 [[A Steelslave mauler|Steelslave maulers]]
1. Return to Hadoth Al'Uzzal.

## Rewards

- At least 5g 40s 7c
- +3000 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
