## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Harvest 8 - 15 'draconic bones'. These are unique harvesting nodes found uncommonly around [[Vicious Breeding Grounds]].
   - There is a direct flight route to there from Hidden Refuge. You will have to cope with the creatures on that island (level 62, some heroic). An alternative place for the bones is on Gazer Island. They are guarded by several gazers in the ruins.
   - It will take some time to find enough draconic bones. It is suggested that you work on all three draconic quests simultaneously.
   - The best spot is Gazer Island. Around the broken temple ruins. They repop fairly quickly. Verified 03/26.
1. Return to [[Archaeologian Tydis]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- [[Spell Etched Stone]]
- First turn-in: good faction with **The Lost Children of Marr**
