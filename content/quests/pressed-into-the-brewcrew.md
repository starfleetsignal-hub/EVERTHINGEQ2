## Note

- This quest is meant to be repeatable once per year, but due to a bug (according to Kaitheel) it is able to be done daily currently, using this is not an exploit and will be fixed for next year.
- The given locations below are **estimates** as they seem to be random within the POI area.
- Since some of the crew members are near the bars, you may want to also do the achievement [[The Great Brewday Pub Crawl]] at the same time.

## Steps

Captain Gimdahr asks you to gather his Crewmates:

1. Wake up (click) the following Brewcrew Buccaneers:
   - [[Stormcoat]], a gnoll, in the center part of the beach in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] {{waypoint 407, 22, 430}}.
   - [[Jyndeth]], a gnome, on the northeastern docks across the river from the bell on the [[Isle of Mara]] {{waypoint -23, -1.9, 16}}.
   - [[Tryh]], a half elf, near a fire in the encampment of [[Kunzar's Edge]] in [[Kylong Plains]] {{waypoint -1370, 10.5, 540}}. If you have the sokokar then you can just fly to Kunzar's Edge from the post at the docks, otherwise you have to take a long swim while potentially avoiding sea creatures.
1. Find [[Krihz]], a ratonga, in [[Windstalker Village]] in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint -1899, -36, -613}}.
1. Speak to Krihz.
1. Kill 12 [[Animated hops]] [Scale to level] on [[The McQuibble Farm]] in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint -1846, -22, -201}}.
1. Return to [[Captain Gimdahr]] next to the [[Dead River Docks]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]] {{waypoint -461, -25, -387}}.
