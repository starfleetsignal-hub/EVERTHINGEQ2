## Steps

1. Click on 3 crates in the area. Locations include
   - {{waypoint -1187, -35, -544}} {{waypoint -1151, -33, -535}} {{waypoint -1201, -35, -518}} {{waypoint -1213, -35, -540}}
1. Return to Jackal.
1. Speak to a gnemlin slave, dhalgar slave, and Thomis Glumgully.
1. Find Famke Tidylocket at {{waypoint -1076, -31, -263}} and click on her.
1. Return to Thomis.
1. Gather the following:
   - 12 Gloriosa Fellenwort. Locations include:
     - {{waypoint -997, -22, -185}} {{waypoint -1059, -34, -167}} {{waypoint -1039, -33, -160}} {{waypoint -1036, -34, -276}} {{waypoint -1033, -34, -282}} {{waypoint -941, -34, -319}} {{waypoint -937, -33, -215}} {{waypoint -963, -33, -221}}
   - 7 Deepnest Sentry Poison Glands. Locations include:
     - {{waypoint -1012, -39, -190}} {{waypoint -1052, -30, -258}} {{waypoint -951, -31, -309}} {{waypoint -965, -34, -338}} {{waypoint -934, -32, -222}}
   - 8 Glowing Kelp Bulbs by hunting Mizzdyrr Fishmen around {{waypoint -1233, -42, -304}}
1. Return to Thomis.
1. Talk to Uldurm Gemsight at {{waypoint -1105, -11, -491}}, then pick up the gem on the ground.
1. Place the wards throughout the wasp area. Locations include:
   - {{waypoint -895, -34, -202}} {{waypoint -866, -32, -197}} {{waypoint -825, -24, -185}} {{waypoint -785, -22, -214}} {{waypoint -757, -19, -221}}
1. Place the last ward at {{waypoint -852, -32, -236}}
1. Kill wasps near and click on the nest at {{waypoint -816, -23, -297}}
1. Return to Thomis.
1. Go to the work camp at {{waypoint -560, -0, -197}}
1. Kill the rest of the Thexians at the camp.
1. Speak to Baeddan at {{waypoint -568, -0, -186}}
