## Notes

- This is the access quest for [[The Vault of the Fallen]]. An alternative method of entry is to loot [[The Seal of Tseralith]], a chest drop from [[Quiora T'Las]].

## Steps

1. Head to the [[Academy of Arcane Science]] in [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -25, -8, -100}}.
1. Read the book titled, 'Thexian Incantations: circa Neriak's Fall', on a table at {{waypoint 12, -5, -130}}.
   - Pay attention to the section/paragraph numbers. These are the combination to a strongbox at the end of the quest.
1. Head back to [[Fallen Gate]].
1. Gather the items mentioned in *Thexian Incantations: circa Neriak's Fall*:
   - Urn of Ashes - examine the green urn in the first tent of [[Little Neriak]], at  {{waypoint 189, 18, -69}}.
   - Scroll of Binding - examine the bookcase on the 2nd floor of the house in [[Little Neriak]], at {{waypoint 193, 23, -113}}.
   - Fallen Dragoon Bangle - examine the strongbox on the top left ledge just inside [[The Bull's Pit]], at {{waypoint -31, -18, -204}}.
     - Input the combination from the Academy book **142635** to receive a no stat quest item called Fallen Dragoon Bangle.
1. Examine the Fallen Dragoon Bangle to to complete the quest and receive the real version: [[Fallen Dragoon Bangle]].

## Rewards

- [[Fallen Dragoon Bangle]]
- Access to [[The Vault of the Fallen]] - boss instanced zone for [[Fallen Gate]]
