## Steps

1. Prove to [[Vhesh]] you aren't dead or a vampire.
   - Gather 4 "stinky shrubs" {{waypoint -8, -207, -791}}
     - *Note: found as "allium shrub" in many locations and trackable* ![allium shrub](images/Allium_shrub.jpg)
1. Bring the "stinky shrubs" to the goblin.

## Rewards

- +2000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
- At least 1p 53g 9s
