## Prerequisite

This is part of the [[Sarnak]] racial quest line

## Steps

1. Speak to [[A fugitive sarnak]] behind [[The Jade Tiger]] {{waypoint -140.94, -24.37, -92.17}}
1. Kill [[A simulacrum]] (even con)
1. Speak to a fugitive sarnak on the South Freeport dock {{waypoint -233, -56, 133}}
1. Kill [[A shadow]] (even con)
1. Speak to a fugitive sarnak behind 5 Freedom Road {{waypoint -171, -30, 287}}
1. Kill [[Thalzuk, the Dishonored]] (even con)
1. Speak to the Ambassador in the Chancery

- At least 9g 46s 1c @ level 79
- [[Ring of the Sarnak]]
- [[The Sarnak Code of Honor]] (House item, readable book)
