## Steps

1. Return to Writher Ruins to cull more threats and recover more items:
   - *You will be asked to do 2 tasks from options below*
   - Recover 8 x antique Darou urns
     - gold urns around [[Writher Shore]] and [[Writher Ruins]] areas
       - {{waypoint -633, 0, 267}}, {{waypoint -578, 6, 366}},  {{waypoint -436, 52, 449}}
       - {{waypoint -443, 48, 305}}, {{waypoint -252, 69, 477}}, {{waypoint -173, 70, 455}},
   - collect crushed Darou tablets x 8
     - tablets around [[Writher Shore]] and [[Writher Ruins]] areas
       - {{waypoint -471, 44, 446}}, {{waypoint -453, 45, 476}}, {{waypoint -480, 50, 357}}
       - {{waypoint -492, 40, 309}}, {{waypoint -507, 41, 307}}, {{waypoint -533, 34, 313}}
       - {{waypoint -520, 28, 282}}, {{waypoint -434, 48, 271}}
   - cull 12 x pestilent harbingers
     - floating bones mobs at [[Writher Ruins]] around {{waypoint -509, 47, 352}}
   - cull 12 rotforged horrors
     - *at the bottom of [[Writher Crevasse]], if you can't fly yet use a ramp down that starts at {{waypoint -380, 27, 101}}*
1. Return to [[Herd Chief Turrak Greatstep]].

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- [[Antique Darou Urn]]
