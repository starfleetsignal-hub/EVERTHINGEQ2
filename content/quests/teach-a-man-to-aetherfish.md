1. Kill 8 [[Aethersalmon]] in [[Vallation Bay]] near {{waypoint 33, 8, -822}}.
1. Speak to [[Aunsellus Tishan]] at {{waypoint 1172, 792, -1584}} on the [[Scintillating Spires]].
1. Return to [[Himaduri]] at {{waypoint -155, 576, -754}}.
