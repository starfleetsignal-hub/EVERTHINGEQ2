## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- The first turn-in yields -2500 reputation with the Sootfoot Talent Society, but subsequent turn-ins yield only coin.

## Steps

1. Perform one of the following tasks:
   - Kill 8 [[A living magma|living magma]], floating above pools of lava in [[Dagger Spires Pass]]. {{waypoint -671, -111, 354}}
   - Kill 8 [[A magma worm|magma worms]] basking in the heat of Dagger Spires Pass. {{waypoint -622, -110, 357}}
1. Return to [[Magnurmal]].

## Rewards

- At least 11g 82s 49c
- -2500 faction with **Sootfoot Talent Society** (first completion only)
