## Steps

1. Kill [[The Cluckatrice]], a 75^ named cockatrice that wanders west and southwest of [[The Tabernacle of Pain]]. Some possible locations are:
   - {{waypoint 400, -62, 218}}
   - {{waypoint 597, -80, 415}}
   - {{waypoint 215, -104, 496}}
1. Speak with the [[Swifttail Trialmaster]]. {{waypoint -73, -43, 160}}
1. Enter the trial ring and defeat [[Tyzik Maul]]. {{waypoint -84, -42, 161}}
1. Return to the senior Stone shackle monk.
