## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key.
   - Kill 15 Raj'Dur bandits [[A Raj'Dur cutthroat|Raj'Dur cutthroats]], [[A Raj'Dur marauder|Raj'Dur marauders]], [[A Raj'Dur blackguard|Raj'Dur blackguards]] at [[Raj'Durabad]] around {{waypoint -228.05, 98.47, -310.35}}
1. Find the Eafrygia Music Box key clue from [[A shadowy grappler|shadowy grapplers]] in [[Takish Badlands]] at the [[Sea of Sand (Takish Badlands)|Sea of Sand]] around {{waypoint 6.53, -61.56, 306.30}}
1. Examine the clue to receive the [[The Eafrygia Music Box Key]]
1. Finish the subquest and return to the [[Eafrygia Music Box]] in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]].
