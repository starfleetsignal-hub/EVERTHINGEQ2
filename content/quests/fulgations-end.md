## Steps

1. Use [[Tera-Cog Phonulator]] on [[Remmy Fulgation]] {{waypoint -780, 92, 1321}} to make him talk. You may have to use multiple times until you get an update.
1. Use [[Sympho-Recalibrator]] on Remmy Fulgation.
1. Use [[Tera-Cog Phonulator]] on Remmy Fulgation. Slambot will kill Remmy Fulgation.
   - If you have multiple people doing the quest together only one can get credit and will have to wait for him to reset.
1. After Remmy Fulgation has been killed by Slambot, click on his body to obtain [[Fulgation's Secrets]].
1. Return to [[Klipp Plaxiphone]] for your reward.

## Reward

- At Least 2g 99s 88c
- [[Pulsing Gem of Fortitude]] (Bruiser, Dirge)
