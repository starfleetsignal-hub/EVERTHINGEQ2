This quest is part 2 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Steal the attack plans of the [[A Nerius Commander|Nerius Commander]] and [[A Nerius Tactician|Tactician]]. These can be found on a table in the tent where the Nerius Tactician spawns: {{waypoint 932, -0, -326}}.
   - **Important Note:** To save time, avoid killing the commander and tactician as you will need to kill them for the following quest! If you do kill them, they will respawn - but it will take a 5-10 minutes.
1. Return to [[Bruhn K'Viir]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 17s 13c

5% of 1 AA point if the quest is greyed out
