## Steps

1. Hail [[Shalih Mar (Steamfont Mountains)|Shalih Mar]] near the path to [[Greater Faydark]] in the [[Steamfont Mountains]] {{waypoint -493, 124, 578}}.
1. Speak to the [[Shade of the Great Bear]] on a hill near [[Mazkeen Crater]] {{waypoint -701, 136, 663}}.
1. Kill 5 [[A Mazkeen spider tamer|Mazkeen spider tamers]] and [[A Mazkeen war spider|their]] [[A Mazkeen recluse|pets]]. (You must kill both the kobold *and* the spider for the update)
1. Speak to the Shade of the Great Bear near {{waypoint -729, 103, 1037}}.
1. Kill 6 Bloodhorn Minotaurs.
1. Speak to the Shade of the Great Bear near {{waypoint -384.15, 98, 1136.77}}.
1. Kill 6 clockwork machines near [[Klak'Anon]].
   - *Note: [[A deranged clockwork gnome]], [[A wayward clockwork gnome]], [[A haywire clockwork spider]] update the quest. The various kinds of copter clockworks don't.*
1. Return to Shalih Mar.
