## Notes

- If you kill any of the creatures in the zone it will activate a 30 minute quest success/fail timer. The quest can still be completed within this time limit.
- Movable boards appear in various locations throughout the zone that will allow you to cross wide chasms. If a location seems unreachable, look for one of these glowing, blue boards. Use the mouse the wheel to spin the board.
  - Using [[Spring-Loaded Gnomish Stilts]] will also let you jump far enough, however it may cause some odd pet or merc pathing issues.

## Steps

1. Zone into [[The Icy Keep]] in [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]]. {{waypoint 103, -4, -1144}}
1. Harvest and skin the dead yeti laying in the snow immediately to your right as you enter the zone.
1. Put on the [[A Yeti Suit|yeti suit]] by examining it from your inventory and follow the path to the right to enter the courtyard through a hole in the wall.![Device location](images/Frostfell_Device.jpg)![Plank placed](images/Plank_placed.jpg)
1. Place listening devices at the following locations. The spot to place a listening device is marked with blue sparkles. Placing a listening device will not drop the disguise, however it will drop stealth or invisibility. Additionally, collecting purple shinies will drop the disguise:
   - North side of chasm at courtyard entry hole: {{waypoint -106, 1, -188}}
   - Between towers by barricade:  {{waypoint -21, 1, -119}}
   - Between towers against south wall: {{waypoint -10, 1, -78}}
   - Inside west tower at bottom: {{waypoint 90, 0, -80}}
   - Inside west tower at top: {{waypoint 82, 15, -71}}
   - Inside east tower at bottom: {{waypoint -76, 1, -109}}
     - To reach this location and the following ones, pick up the plank at {{waypoint 99, 0, -88}} and use it to cross chasm to the east tower.
   - End of upper walk exiting west from tower: {{waypoint 17, 19, -60}}
   - Roof of east tower in alcove: {{waypoint -81, 27, -104}}
   - End of west walk along castle wall: {{waypoint -21, 20, -216}}
   - End of east walk along castle wall: {{waypoint -166, 20, -197}}
1. Return to [[Aildiun Mawdrun]].
