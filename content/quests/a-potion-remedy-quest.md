## Steps

1. Purchase [[A Potion Remedy (Quest Item)|a Potion Remedy]] for 6g from [[Al'Diun Stirhinis]] {{waypoint 1805, -299, 3509}}.
1. Return to [[Nudhir Il'qatai]] {{waypoint 1947.04, -112.75, 3403.75}} in [[The Ruins of Old Paineel]]
1. Simply left-click on the corpse to resurrect him.
1. Click on the Researcher's Assistants Request Log in the Council Chambers {{waypoint 1852, -294.86, 3481.4}} to finish quest.
