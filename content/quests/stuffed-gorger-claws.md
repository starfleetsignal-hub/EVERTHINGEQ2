*Must be at least level 2 to receive this quest.*

## Steps

1. Kill the 4 [[Grit gorger|grit gorgers]] in the shallow pool on the west side of Chrykori Beach.
1. Return to Taroche

## Rewards

- At least 55c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Bodyguard's Gloves]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Evoker's Gloves]] (All Mages)
  - [[Marshall's Gauntlets]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Pathfinder's Gloves]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Spiritualist's Gloves]] (Shamans)
  - [[Vicar's Gauntlets]] (Clerics)
  - [[Windcaller's Gloves]] (Druids)
