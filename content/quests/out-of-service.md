## Description

I must enter Erudin Research Halls and decommission incomplete magitech gazers. I must be careful, as the gazers have the ability to camouflage themselves. It would probably be safest for my group and I to decommission as many incomplete magitech gazers as possible.

## Steps

1. kill 10 incomplete magitech gazers
1. Return to Rafiqa

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
