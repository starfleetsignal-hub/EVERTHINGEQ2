## Steps

1. Investigate 4 Metetherial Ewers.
   - These are the ewers used at the end of the previous quest.
   - **NOTE:**  Unlike the previous quest's ewers, these are **NOT** group updates
1. Speak to [[Chandira]] at {{waypoint -247, 578, -629}}
1. Travel to {{waypoint -200, 16, 888}} in [[Zivier's Reach]].  Non-combat Yrzu knocks you back, then runs away.
   - This is an important step, as you will not see the Yrzu elementals without this update.
1. Kill 6 [[Yrzu elemental]] and collect [[Fused brinidine]] from the bodies. ![Yrzu elemental](images/Yrzu_elemental.jpg)
1. Return to [[Chandira]] at {{waypoint -247, 578, -629}}
