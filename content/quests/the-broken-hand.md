## Prerequisites

- You must be level 80.
- You must be level 10 Artisan.
- You must be able to speak Sathirian. The language quests begin with completing [[A Mysterious Black Tome]]. Then inspect/read the tome.
- You must have completed [[The Trial of Tynnonium]].
- You must have completed the [[Tome: Teachings of Master Wu]] Collection (Make sure you turn the quest in to a collection merchant or you will not be able to start the quest).
- You must have completed [[Wu's Edict of the Material]] quest-line that starts with [[Wu's Edict of Existence]].
- You must have completed [[To Speak as a Dragon]]. Or buy the primer. Language is required not the quest completed.
- You must have [[Kunzar Jungle Villagers (Faction)]] at -6000 or better.

## Steps

### Obtaining the History of the Hand

1. Have the books [[Teachings of Master Wu]] and [[Journal of Wu, Seeker of Enlightenment]] in your inventory.  The Journal is the reward for the quest [[Wu's Edict of the Material]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]].  If you sold or destroyed it, a replacement can be purchased from the Wu encampment in [[The Lesser Faydark]].  The Teachings is from the [[Tome: Teachings of Master Wu]] collection quest in [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]]. If you sold or destroyed [[Teachings of Master Wu]], you can obtain this book once more as a drop in  [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]].
1. Speak with [[Master Makoto Shoda]] in the Fens.  He wanders the beach between the Span and Omen's Call.  He will take your books, but he'll give them back later.
1. Speak with [[Chronicler Lao]], the Chronicler of the Ashen Order inside the Ashen Order Camp. To get there:
   - Go to [[The Pillars of Flame]], off of [[The Sinking Sands]], via the north-most entrance on the waterline in [[The Sinking Sands]].
   - Run up the hill past the [[T'Narev]] carpet and into the camp of the Ashen Order.
1. Speak with [[Chronicler Lao]] at {{waypoint 31, -56, -1184}} inside the camp in the back, under one of the tents.
1. Find Lao's student, [[Tobee]] at {{waypoint 1180, -98, -1178}} out near the [[Sun Spires]] in [[The Pillars of Flame]]. She will be surrounded by some bandits.
1. Speak to the bandits so that they become attackable. There was one heroic ^ Level 83 [[Korroz the Bandit]] and 3 down arrow bandits.
1. Speak with [[Tobee]] who will give you a book, [[History of the Hand]].
1. Return to [[Chronicler Lao]] and speak with him.

### Finding the Blades

After Chronicler go back to [[Fens of Nathsar]] and speak to [[Master Makoto Shoda]]. He will then ask you to find 5 blades scattered about the world (he will return your books as well). Find the blades in any order.

##### Blade of Balance

The language Draconic from the quest [[To Speak as a Dragon]] is required for this step

1. Speak with [[Flink the Impish]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]] (Skyfire) at {{waypoint -907, 244, 766}}.  He will ask you to bring back Bloody Draconic Hearts to balance on the scales against your inner demons.
   - These are semi-rare drops on Wurms, Wyverns and Drakes all over Skyfire. You can also buy them off the broker,
   - You need 57(!). You want EXACTLY 57. More or less and a large group of enemies will spawn.
1. Take the hearts back and place them on the balance. An 81^ doppleganger of yourself will spawn.
1. Kill it and take blade from scale.

##### Blade of Peace

![](images/Kaiaren_of_the_One_Eye_Grave.jpg)
![a fire sprite](images/EverQuest_II-firesprite.jpg)

1. Obtain "Kaiaren's Scroll" which from a grave in Kunzar Jungle {{waypoint 427, 10, -362}}, in a cave behind a waterfall {{waypoint 376, 7, -276}}. You must break through the wall behind the waterfall in order to search the cave system for the grave of Kaiaren of the One Eye. Attack the wall and then try walking through it, sometimes it does not disappear. The grave is at {{waypoint 425.8, 9.32, -366.27}}
1. Talk to a fire sprite in Lavastorm {{waypoint -268, 83, -46}} near Solusek Eye (around rim of lava pit to left from porter) in Lavastorm. Soon after you talk to the fire sprite [[Eejag]] an 85^^^ heroic will spawn Kill him to obtain the blade.
1. Eejag will de-spawn after 5-10 minutes if not engaged; the fire sprite will spawn again after about 10 minutes.

##### Blade of Order

To complete this step you must to speak Sathirian, you must have completed [[The Trial of Tynnonium]] for your Shackle Grandmaster title, and you must have at least -6000 faction with the [[Kunzar Jungle Villagers (Faction)]] (or better).  Find the quests to do it on the [[Kunzar Jungle Timeline]].

1. Go to [[Tabernacle of Pain]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]] and talk to [[Grandmaster Kaybal]] {{waypoint -115, -39, 182}}.
1. Go to {{waypoint -106, -84, 295}} and talk to [[Kindo Youngfist (Npc)|Kindo Youngfist]].
1. Return to the [[Grandmaster Kaybal]]
1. Challenge him.
   - *Note:Go to Tibalmaster, {{waypoint -69, -43, 156}} the guy give you access to platform at earlyer shackle quests*
   - [[Grandmaster Kaybal]] is an 85^^^ heroic, so bring some help.
   - During the fight, he may split into three people (one ^^^, one ^^, and one ^). He may reset, aggro, or both, and he may also kick his target up in the air or teleport it.
   - After fight he'll respawn where you first talked to him.
1. Talk to [[Grandmaster Kaybal]] again to receive the blade.

##### Blade of Harmony

1. Enter [[Shard of Fear]] from the cave at the far back of [[The Feerrott]].
1. Pull the final mob, [[Terror]], to spawn [[Elder Jhen]], a level 83 ^^^ heroic monk, who adds to the encounter when [[Terror]] is at 90%.  WARNING: Do not OneShot [[Terror]] because [[Elder Jhen]] won't spawn and then you will have to wait the 2 days before you can enter the zone to kill him again.  Good idea to Chrono way down and take weapons off for fight.
1. Kill the monk to receive the blade.

##### Blade of Tranquility

1. Enter [[Sebilis]].
1. Obtain the subquest [[The Broken Hand: Bones of the Elder]], initiated in [[Sebilis]] from [[Elder Jaago]] in a jail cell at {{waypoint 71, -131, 102}}.
   - This is the first room after you jump into the water from the main foyer and swim down the waterfall.
   - [[Elder Jaago]] will not be visible until you open the cell and walk inside.
   - The subquest requires 20 bone trinkets which drop randomly off of level 80+ iksar in [[Sebilis]] (Legionaires in basement are a good bet).   They are body drops and only appear to the monk on the quest.   Then bury the bones in [[The Elddar Grove]], near the Dojo's garden {{waypoint 727, -19, -406}}.  Click around on the ground by the tree to get update.   Elder Jaago appears and tells you to return to his cell and find the loose brick.
1. Once the subquest is completed, return to the quest giver's cell.  He asks you to search a loose brick.  The brick is not obvious its on the lower north wall of the cell.

### Reassembling the Hand

1. Return to [[Master Makoto Shoda]] with the five blades. He will send you to find information on how to reassemble the hand.  The information is in scattered pages of the collection The Forbidden Pages, that can be found in [[Charasis: Maiden's Chamber]] and [[Charasis: Vault of Eternal Sleep]].  Some are ground spawns and some drop from named mobs in the zones. The body drops will only update one monk at a time so don't group with other monks expecting updates.  Good idea to Chrono down to 80 while running through this zone to make sure the pieces drop.
   1. [[Charasis: Maiden's Chamber]]:
      1. Page 5 - the Impaler Tzilug (chest) {{waypoint 155, -18, 62}}
      1. Page 3 - the Hallowed Hall (ground) {{waypoint 110, -18, 243}}
      1. Page 4 - bottom of retractable bridge chamber (ground) {{waypoint 49, -42, 153}}
      1. Page 7 - Drusella Sathir (chest) {{waypoint 39, 5, 179}}
   1. [[Charasis: Vault of Eternal Sleep]]:
      1. Page 2 - bottom of the pit outside the [[Praetor of the Phylactery]]'s chamber (ground) {{waypoint 5, -18, 193}}
      1. Page 1 - Golem's mini event room (ground) {{waypoint 37, -5, -290}}
      1. Page 6 - the [[Praetor of the Phylactery]] (body loot) {{waypoint 27, -3, -132}}
1. Turn in the collection then scribe the recipe given, "Diagram: Claw of Instruction". Recipe requires:
   1. 3 tynnonium clusters (T8 rare)
   1. 1 rough fire emerald (T8 rare)
   1. 20 ferrite clusters (T8 common)
   1. 20 deklium clusters (T8 common)
   1. 10 smoldering coal (vendor)
1. Craft the diagram at a Work Bench.** *FYI it is wise to be at least a level 10 crafter for this to go well for you.***
1. Return to [[Master Makoto Shoda]] to show him what you had created and to get the completed Hand of Serenity. Also given the next quest: [[The Mended Hand]].

STOP!
If your goal is to have the Item for the [[Epic Conversion Timeline]], you do NOT need to do the RAID version. You can begin that timeline now.
