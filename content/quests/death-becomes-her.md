## Notes

You must have +20,000 faction with Defenders of the Seal before you can obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Inside [[Dartain's Fortress]], collect the sword at {{waypoint 410, 119, -252}}.  The sword is in the water pool immediately after zoning into the [[Dartain's Fortress]] part of [[The Hole]].
1. Return to Basimah.  She sends you to place the sword at the base of her grave at {{waypoint 726, 125, -275}}.
   - At her grave, right-click and "use" the sword from your inventory.

## Rewards

- At least 20g 70s 61c
- +2500 faction with **Defenders of the Seal**
- [[Mark of Manaar]]
