## Description

It is assumed that many of the treasured heirlooms of the iksar noble families have become the playthings of the creatures that now infest Veksar. I should search among them for these lost items and return them to Omen's Call.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Veksar: The Sunken Theater]] and seek out iksar noble heirlooms among the beasts
   - An iksar noble family's glasses
   - An iksar warrior's greatsword
   - A banner with a coat-of-arms
   - An iksar noble family's signet ring
   - An iksar family's amulet
   - An iksar family holy symbol
   - An iksar bard's instrument
   - A noble iksar chalice
   - A Sasil family artifact
     - Destroy each sarcophagus near -11, -3, -60 to spawn Lord Sasil's Arms, Head, Ribcage, and Legs
     - Defeat Lord Sasil's Arms, Head, Ribcage, or Legs for artifact
1. return to Lieutenant Kresic near Omen's Call

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
