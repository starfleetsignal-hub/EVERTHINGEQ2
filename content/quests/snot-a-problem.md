## Steps

1. Place the target dummy where [[Jamie Sprocketbreaker|Jamie]] indicated, by the target wall around {{waypoint -644, 175, 1064}}. WARNING: Do not attack/defeat the dummy with normal attacks; it will destroy the dummy and you'll need to make another one.
1. Stand near [[Jamie Sprocketbreaker|Jamie]] and use the [[Troll Snot Flinger (testing prototype)]] on the target dummy.
1. Speak to [[Adam Cogbuster|Adam]] about the results.
