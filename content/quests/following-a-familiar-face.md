## Steps

1. Perform the following tasks:
   - Kill 12 [[Ignus dancers]] on [[Scintillating Spires]] near {{waypoint 1159, 771, -1426}}
   - Collect 8 perfect laths by destroying starfire crystals in the [[Pakiat Bluffs]] near {{waypoint -544, 133, -636}} or {{waypoint -786, -298, 16.36}}![Lath of Starfire Crystal](images/Lath_of_Starfire_Crystal.jpg)
   - You have to move around, they are yellow crystals formations found all over on and below the bluffs.![starfire crystal](images/Starfire_crystals.jpg)
1. Return to [[Himaduri]] at {{waypoint -109, 574, -714}} and receive [[Catenated Perceptos Rune]].
1. Use the Rune.
1. Speak to [[Ghab]] at {{waypoint 920, 19, 912}} at the Mage's Pebble
1. Return to [[Himaduri]] at {{waypoint -155, 576, -757}}.
