## Steps

1. Speak to one of the [[A poor beggar|poor beggars]] at [[Outcasts' Ledge]] {{waypoint -266, 137, 34}} or at the top of the stairs -71 +177 +79.
   - You may have to kill the beggars a few times before one of them will become hailable. Even the female ones at the loc's above are not always quest update versions of the beggars. There are also poor beggars sporadically through out the zone which may update your quest as well. There are a couple right next to the zone in, for example.
1. Return to [[Nazheem]]

## Rewards

- At least 2g 79s 7c @ level 55
