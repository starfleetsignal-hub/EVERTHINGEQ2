*This quest is also part of the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].*

## Steps

***The following steps all take place in [[Nektulos Forest]]***

1. Slay 10 [[A Nerius crab|Nerius crabs]] {{waypoint 857, 0, -230}}
1. Examine the book
1. Slay 10 [[A Nerius crab|Nerius crabs]]
1. Examine the book
1. Slay 10 [[A Nerius crab|Nerius crabs]]
1. Examine the book
1. Slay 10 [[Nerius marauder|Nerius marauders]] {{waypoint 605, -1, -215}}
1. Examine the book
1. Slay 10 [[Nerius marauder|Nerius marauders]]
1. Examine the book
1. Slay 10 [[Nerius marauder|Nerius marauders]]
1. Examine the book
1. Slay 10 [[Nerius seer|Nerius seers]] {{waypoint 605, -1, -215}}
1. Examine the book
1. Slay 10 [[Nerius seer|Nerius seers]]
1. Examine the book
1. Slay 10 [[Nerius seer|Nerius seers]]
1. Examine the book

<i>The Nerius spawns are among many other types. A useful tip is to kill all spawns in and around all three camps each time to get more of the type ([[A Nerius marauder]] or [[A Nerius seer]]) that you want to spawn. This will also spawn the uncommon [[A Nerius Pirate Captain]], [[A Nerius Scallywag]] and [[A Nerius scoundrel]] for treasure and master chest drops.</i>
