## Steps

1. Collect all of the missing artifacts from [[The Condemned Catacomb]] by killing  goblins to find the rare artifacts.
1. Collect 4 jeweled eyes from [[A limestone watcher|limestone watchers]].
   - The watchers are not attackable until you disturb each of the four skeletons in in the walls around the alcove. Distubing a skeleton will spawn a level 26 brittle bones, which you must kill.
   - As soon as the fourth skeleton dies, the [[A limestone watcher|limestone watcher]] will become attackable.
1. Collect the three missing statues of the ancients:
   - In an alcove down the first tunnel on the left, at {{waypoint -172, 1, -31}}
   - Behind [[Thaumaturge Zumzum]], at {{waypoint -145, 1, -73}}
   - In the last alcove in the tunnel before [[Vindiami the Ancient]], at {{waypoint -95, 1, -119}}
1. Return to [[Eenekie Blazdine]] for your reward.
