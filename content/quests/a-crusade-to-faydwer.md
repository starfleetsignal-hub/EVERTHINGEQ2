## Notes

- You must have a [[Crow coin|crow coin]], rewarded at the end of the previous quest, in your inventory to be given the option to zone into [[Crow's Resting Place, Thieves Guild]].
- You will need [[Koada'Dal (Language)|Koada'Dal]] language to receive quest [[A Poem to the Past]] in the [[New Tunaria]] step.

## Steps

![A rhympost.](images/Agaggleofrhym.jpg)![Five pieces of evidence.](images/Fivepiecesofevidence.jpg)

1. Go to [[Kelethin]] and speak with [[Lady Alethea Jyleel]] in [[Joleena's Restaurant]]. {{waypoint 534, 77, 321}}
1. Within twelve minutes, harvest 6 *jars of rhym* from *rhymposts* around Kelethin:
   - Aerie rhympost, between levels on the Fae Residential Aerie Platform: {{waypoint 433, 106, 508}}
   - Memorial rhympost, on the rim of the memorial stump: {{waypoint 322, 100, 319}}
   - Court house rhympost, behind the [[Fae Royal Court]]: {{waypoint 377, 140, 174}}
   - Library rhympost, behind the [[Kelethin Research Library]]: {{waypoint 132, 109, 185}}
   - Bough rhympost, tip of the branch that connects the [[Old Kelethin]] platform and the Kelethin Reserach Library platform: {{waypoint 177, 125, 61}}
   - Lift rhympost, walk out behind the building near the [[Old Kelethin Acorn Lift]]: {{waypoint 42, 93, 85}}
1. Return to Lady Alethea Jyleel.
1. Locate the *Gully of the Ursa Rhym* in northwester [[Greater Faydark]] at {{waypoint 621, 10, -234}} and examine the following items (closeups of the items are on the talk page):
   - *The Chessboard*
   - *The Challenge Gong*
   - *The Hive of the Diva*
   - *The Hive of the Composer*
   - *The Hive of the Baritone*
1. Go to [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]] and approach [[Inspector Berlok Beeglesnoop]]. {{waypoint -648, 175, 1079}}
1. Berlok is quite insane, but constant reminders of his youth will get you through his dialogue. Answers the dialogue in the following order:
   1. What did you do in your youth?
   1. I wish I could be a great investigator like you were in your youth.
   1. When you were young you must have been a great writer.
   1. I think reading helps you stay young.
   1. When you were young, did you ever get lost?
   1. In your younger days, you must have taught the Gnomeland Security all you know.
   1. Were you this crazy when you were young or is this an old age thing?
   1. I am the general of Felwithe.
   1. When you were young, you must have seen some splendid shields.
   1. That accounts for two books. Where is the last?
1. Go to [[New Tunaria]] to find Inspector Berlok Beeglesnoop's three books. These are rewarded by completing the following subquests:
   - [[A Poem to the Past]], started from a shield on a wall. {{waypoint -310, 53, -858}}
   - [[The Gumshoe Guide]], a collection quest started from a book. {{waypoint -347, 54, -873}} or {{waypoint -347, 54, -842}}
     - The pages spawn around the nearby courtyard.
     - Just like with a normal collection quest, turn in the completed collection at any collector.
   - [[A Water Tight Case]], started from an underwater chest. {{waypoint -191, -11, -922}}
1. Upon completing the last subquest, you will receive [[A Set of Books. Berlok's Investigative Guidelines]]. Examine it and you will receive a spell scroll titled [[The Ins-And-Outs of Investigation]]. Scribe that scroll to receive the [[Investigating]] ability.![evidence in the gully](images/Evidenceinthegully.jpg)
1. Return to the *Gully of the Ursa Rhym* in Greater Faydark and click on the 'evidence in the gully'. You will automatically use the new Investigation skill. {{waypoint 621, 9, -231}}
1. Read the book you received, [[The Case of the Ursa Rhym]].
1. Collect 6 clues in the quarry of [[The Broken Fields]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] (pictures of both the types of clues are on the talk page). {{waypoint -293, 110, 371}}
   1. Investigate 3 *scorched armor plates*.
   1. Investigate 3 *scorched daggers*.
1. Speak to the rat NPC [[Chypp]], with a silent P. {{waypoint -356, 110, 389}}
   - After you finish the dialog, two [[An elven skeleton|elven skeletons]] and four [[A raider of Ree (Butcherblock Mountains)|Raiders of Ree]] will spawn as you move further down the canyon. You do not need to kill them.
1. Go to [[The Wailing Caves]] in [[The Commonlands]] and investigate 6 pieces of *dark elf evidence* in the store room area. {{waypoint -41, -33, -92}}
1. Find and investigate *expedition evidence*, which can be found at one of the following locations: {{waypoint -111, -50, -52}}, {{waypoint -123, -51, -58}}, {{waypoint -57, -26, -39}} or {{waypoint -47, -26, -11}}.
1. Kill [[Lord Ree]]. {{waypoint -280, -49, -86}}
   - Lord Ree has a ~30 minute respawn timer. The door at {{waypoint -281, -49, -60}} will open if he has spawned, so just keep trying.
1. Examine the book on the table with a black cloud emanating from it. {{waypoint -292, -49, -79}}
1. Hail the [[An Ebon Mask shadow master|Ebon Mask shadow master]] and defeat the encounter. You will receive [[Missing pages|missing pages]].
   - If you fail the encounter, you will have to wait until Lord Ree respawns.
1. Return to Chypp in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] and request his eye necklace.
   - Mention that your dog lost an eye in a bear attack.
1. Examine the missing pages from your inventory using [[The Eye of Ree]].
1. Return to Lady Alethea Jyleel in [[Greater Faydark]].
