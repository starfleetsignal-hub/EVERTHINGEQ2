## Steps

1. Find and then speak to [[Mlheda]] {{waypoint -107.38, 85.35, -37.98}} on the eastern bank of the northern [[Myhst River]].
1. Kill the [[Enraged stelecat]] {{waypoint -103.52, 85.60, -33.20}} that breaks loose of her spell.
1. Speak to the exhausted [[Mlheda]] again.
1. Return to [[Olinnisk]] {{waypoint 115.63, 579.26, -618.40}} who has in the meantime relocated to [[Khali'Yrzu]].
