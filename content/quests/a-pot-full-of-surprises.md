## Notes

- You cannot obtain this quest if you have any active Tabernacle of Pain quests.

## Steps

1. Right-click on 5 [[A hardened clay vase|hardened clay vase]]s around [[The Tabernacle of Pain]] and replace them with poppies. {{waypoint -144, -77, 241}}
1. Return to Havik Thyx.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- [[Glowing Gem of Criticals]]
