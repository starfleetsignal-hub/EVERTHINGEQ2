## Steps

1. After picking up the quest, speak to [[Cerennhir Aelindel|Cerennhir]] again (i.e. twice) - this dialog will give you your first quest ding, and put a [[Runed Codex Coffer]] in your inventory.
1. Obtain the following items, dropped from the same [[Gorowyn]] mobs inside the palace.
   - [[Bragallich Tel'llach - Parma]]
   - [[Bragallich Tel'llach - Aicanaro]]
   - [[Bragallich Tel'llach - Curuvar]]
   - [[Bragallich Tel'llach - Anto]]
   - these drop from [[A Gorowyn Knight Sentinel]], [[A Gorowyn Archimage]] or [[A Gorowyn High Priest]] in [[The Chardok Palace]] inside [[Chardok]], past the blue and red gates.  This item and the other three items drop most frequently from the mobs in the room with [[Stalis Ra'Dal]] at {{waypoint 923, -44, 349}}
1. Use the [[Runed Codex Coffer]] to assemble the pages and then you receive the [[Sealed Codex Coffer]].
1. Return to [[Cerennhir Aelindel]] in [[New Tunaria]].
