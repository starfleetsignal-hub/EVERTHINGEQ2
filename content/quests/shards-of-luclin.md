## Steps

![Tillage moon rocks](images/Tillage_moon_rocks_underwater.jpg)
![Moon rock closeup](images/Tillage_moon_rock_closeup.jpg)

1. Agata needs you to collect 10 Tillage moon rocks from the waters around the Tillage patch water area {{waypoint -726, -944, -185}}
1. Return to [[Agata]] {{waypoint -809, -917, -398}} with the rocks.

- The rocks are "clicky" items and do not require any harvesting skill to collect. They are spread out within the same body of water, so be prepared to dive under the water many times to collect all of them. If you have a [[Totem of the Otter]] or water breathing, use it before you begin diving for rocks.
- None of the creatures in the water are aggressive (no aggro), so the area you need to dive in is safe at any level.
- Each rock will disappear after being collected.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- At least 64g 81s 38c
- +750 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
