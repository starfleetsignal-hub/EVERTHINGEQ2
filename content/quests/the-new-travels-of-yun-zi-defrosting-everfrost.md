   1. [[Jagged Plains]] at {{waypoint 112, 22, -437}}
   1. [[Maidens Walk]] at {{waypoint -175, 59, -870}} (take the cave at entrance {{waypoint -161, 22, -527}})
   1. [[Hollowfrost Grove]] at {{waypoint -231, 1, -409}} (take the cave at entrance {{waypoint -138, 21, -448}})
   1. [[Guardian's Gulch]] at {{waypoint -423, -38, -657}} (take the cave at entrance {{waypoint -329, -1, -417}})
   1. [[Icegill Lagoon]] at {{waypoint 198, -37, -1247}} (take the cave at entrance {{waypoint -178, -38, -1047}})
