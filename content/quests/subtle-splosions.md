## Steps

- You are given a [[G.D.D. (Gnomish Destruction Device)]]
1. Destroy 4 Rime weapon racks
   - The racks are in the Rime section of Storm Gorge past the wall.
   - Target them and use the GDD from your inventory to destroy them
1. Return to Jubie

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
