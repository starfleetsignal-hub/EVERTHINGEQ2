## Notes

The entrance to Buried Takish'Hiz is near Hamdi in [[Takish Badlands]], {{waypoint 620.76, 155.99, -645.70}}.

Materials required includes:-

- 10x [[Apatite Gem]]
- 15x [[Brittlebush Taproot]]
- 18x [[Dustmaw Hide]]
- 15x [[Pallasite Ore]]
- 80x [[Astral Coal]]

## Steps

1. Collect 5 [[Elddar Artifacts]] (Go back to central hub, then take North spoke to Elddar Exchange, then take a left)
1. Return to [[Gnorbert]] {{waypoint 138.01, 31.30, -2.56}}
1. Collect 5 [[Elddar Soil]]![Elddar Soil](images/Elddar_Soil.png)
1. Craft 5 [[Plaster Cast]]
1. Craft 5 [[Artifact Replica]]
1. Speak to [[Gnorbert]] to receive a [[Broken Mining Tools - unpack me]]
1. Unpack the [[Broken Mining Tools - unpack me]] crate, and repair the tools:
   - Repair 6x [[Tool Handle|Tool Handles]]
   - Repair 3x [[Pickaxe Base|Pickaxe Bases]]
   - Repair 3x [[Shovel Base|Shovel Bases]]
1. Speak with [[Gnorbert]]

## Rewards

- [[Group of Artifact Crates]]
- 4 x  [[D.I.R.T.Y. D.O.G.G.]]
