## Steps

1. Gather more weapons from the Highhold smithy's forge: Fly over the walls of castle (still in Phantom Sea, not in dungeon) and go to {{waypoint 518, 351, 52}}
1. Kill 8 attackers within Highhold's courtyard
1. Return to Yonette

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- One of the Following:
  - [[Mucktail Tooth Belt]]
  - [[Belt of the Keep Master]]
