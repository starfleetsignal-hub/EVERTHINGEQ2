## Prerequisite

You need to complete [[Roughing Up the Reinforcements]] to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Investigate what the Ry'Gorr orcs are doing on the small iceberg EAST of [[Jagged Daggers]] isle {{waypoint 92, 26, 960}}
   - Gather 4 pickaxes on the iceberg
   - Kill 3 Ry'Gorr orcs (Bad Re-spawn Timer)
1. Return to Knut

## Rewards

- At least 2s 40c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Archon's Barbute]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Hat]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Helm]] (Druids)
  - [[Elder's Helm]] (Shamans)
  - [[Enlightened Kasa]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Barbute]] (Crusaders & Warriors)
  - [[Shade's Cowl]] (All Scouts)
