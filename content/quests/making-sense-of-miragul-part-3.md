## Steps

1. Lucretia Nasin has once more sent me into Miragul's Phylactery. Since her purpose is now clear, I know I should be looking for items related to Miragul's ability to teleport through time, space, and dimensions.
   - Pick up the tome at {{waypoint -82, 0.37, 33}} in the [[A Reflection of Silence]].
   - Click on glowing spacial disturbances throughout the zone.
   - Kill [[The Codexicon]] and loot the residual powder that proves Miragul has crossed through the very dimensions themselves. (A grey encounter will not drop the required update.)

## Notes

- The quest [[Getting There Before the Coalition]] is exactly the same as this quest.  You will need to click on the book and each of the Spacial Disturbances ***twice*** (The Spacial Disturbances re-set after you collect all six, and become clickable again).  These are not group updates.
- Both powders (the final update) will be awarded at the same time from the loot window.

## Rewards

- At least 31g 73s 78c
- +5,000 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
