## Description

The Craftkeepers Task Coordinator has requested assistance on behalf of the Eye of Dartain researchers to obtain some boulder dasher dust for grinding lenses.

## Steps

1. You've been asked to use the [[Dust suction device]] to obtain three dusts from the boulder dashers (or other earth elementals) near the [[The Eye of Dartain]].
   - If you're a low level adventurer, you can tag those that are fighting with the [[A Paineel knight guardian|guards]] and [[A summoned bastion|elementals]] if you're fast enough.
1. Return to [[Oolioomon]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +2,500 faction with **Craftkeepers**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Craftkeepers Daily Supply Tasks
