## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 20+

## Steps

1. Talk with [[Harkam Nubbytoe]] to get quest update on where to look for Rinna.
1. Search for [[Rinna]] in the [[Down Below]] {{waypoint -14, 0, -35}}.  The nearest entrance is behind the [[In Range]] archery shop in [[Elddar Grove]]. {{waypoint 518, -17, -397}}
1. Some [[A corrupted Qeynos guard|corrupted Qeynos guards]] will attack you as you near [[Rinna]], allowing a mysterious liaison to escape.
1. Speak to Rinna. She is standing inside the nearby alcove.
1. Follow the liaisons' footprints which start just outside the alcove at {{waypoint -15, 0, -44}} until you are attacked at the final footstep.  ![](images/On_the_Run_quest_-_Liason's_Footprints.jpg)
1. Return to [[Rinna]] in the Down Below {{waypoint -14, 0, -29}}
