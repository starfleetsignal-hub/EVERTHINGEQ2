## Description

The Quel'ule scholar Azira has requested assistance from a fellow Scholar in crafting some items to help the fighters at the front lines. She has warned me that non-scholars will not be able to complete this assignment.

## Steps

1. You must be a scholar to do this quest. You can not craft the items with another crafter and hand them over or craft them through commission, it will not update the quest, since *you* must be the one to craft the items.
   - Sage: Craft 5 scrolls of [[Winds of Healing VI|Winds of Healing VI (Journeyman)]] (level 80).
     - 5 [[Amber]]
     - 15 [[Sumac lumber]]
     - 10 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
     - 5 [[Titanium ore]]
     - 25 [[Ethereal incense]]
   - Alchemist: Craft 50 [[Focused Arcane Remedy]] (level 80).
     - 5 [[Amber]]
     - 15 [[Bamboo shoot]]
     - 10 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
     - 5 [[Titanium ore]]
     - 10 [[Ethereal candle]]
   - Jeweler: Craft 5 runes of Stalk VI (Journeyman) (level 81). Items needed for 5 combines:
     - 5 [[Amber]]
     - 15 [[Bamboo shoot]]
     - 10 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
     - 5 [[Titanium ore]]
     - 25 [[Ethereal coal]]
1. Return to [[Researcher Azira]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,500 faction with **Researchers of Quel'ule**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Quel'ule Daily Supply Tasks
