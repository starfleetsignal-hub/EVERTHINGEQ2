*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Find and learn more about the Memorial of Power. Read the inscription on the SW monolith {{waypoint -1, 1, 22}} in [[The First Gate]].
1. Collect 8 ghastly brains from the zombies within the Fallen Gate.
1. Drop the offering into the urn at the foot of the Memorial of Power.

## Rewards

- [[Spectral Dragoon Belt]]
