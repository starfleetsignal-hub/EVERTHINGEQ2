## Steps

1. Travel down to the [[Velinoid Catacombs]] and lower the health of [[Evigis the Ancient]] {{waypoint -238, -22, -82}} below 20% and use [[Gerla's Elemental Lullaby]].
1. Return to [[Torli Blackbow]]

## Rewards

- At least 17s 22c
- [[Woven Rug of Prosperity]]
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Forged Dagger]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Forged Knuckles]] (Brawlers, Beastlord)
  - [[Forged Mace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Forged Staff]] (All Mages)
  - [[Forged Sword]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
