## Steps

   - Slay [[Akharu the Remembrancer]]. {{waypoint 47, -340, -182}} This loc is correct. If you open your map, one of the single blue dots (it is halfway stacked) on the northernmost branch of the Halls of Fate island is where you will find him.
   - Obtain Akharu's Orders. (he will drop a chest)
   - Slay [[Bashir the Dracologist]]. {{waypoint 155, -336, 207}}
   - Obtain Bashir's Orders. (he will drop a chest)
   - Slay 10 [[A Doomwing excavator|Doomwing excavators]].
   - Destroy 4 sets of excavation tools {{waypoint -241, -342, 6}}, {{waypoint 159, -333, 193}}, {{waypoint 49, -339, -186}}, {{waypoint 28, -342, -211}}
1. Return to [[Deegli Bubbowus]]

## Rewards

- +5,000 faction with **Drednever Expedition**
- At least 18g 85s 15c
- [[Ancient Draconic Relic]]
- 5,580 XP at level 68.
