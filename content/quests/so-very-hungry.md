## Steps

1. Gather 6 badger bones {{waypoint -1372, -526, -2544}} Note: These are items on the ground.  Don't bother killing badgers!
1. Trap 4 glacier eels. Click on the glacier eel tunnels southwest of where you were gathering the bones around {{waypoint -1248, -541, -2457}} . The tunnels are small icy lumps on the ice. When you get a successful pull of an eel, click on the stick den that pops up quickly for the updates. Note: If you are on the Othmir daily quest "Slippery as an Eel", you will have to finish it before the stick-dens appear.
1. Return to Snaps
