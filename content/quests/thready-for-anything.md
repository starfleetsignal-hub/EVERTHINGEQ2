## Notes

Follow general steps to progress through [[Doomfire: Ro's Sweatshop (Solo)]] and look for updates as you go.

## Steps

1. Hunt searing scourges for  10 x sizzle garnet shards on the main island
1. Gather 6 x flamewood timber on Lonely Isle (harvestable unnamed brown logs)
1. Obtain 4 x needlefire trampler claws on Phoenix Isle
1. Gather 6 luminous embers on Fabrician's Flat (small fireballs moving around that area)

## Reward

- [[Wild Flame-branded Steed Plushie]]
- 5 x [[Platinum Jubilation Medal]]
- 6 x [[Coin of Ember]]
