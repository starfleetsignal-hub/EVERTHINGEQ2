## Starting the quest

1. There is a rare chance that the [[A captive explorer]] spawns in the zone
1. Approach [[A captive explorer]] {{waypoint -85, 7, 28}} *(same floor as the Nek Forest entry)*. Once you kill a number of mobs near her, she will get a quest feather. Speak to her to start the quest.

## Steps

1. Speak to the captive explorer again to finish the quest

## Rewards

- Experience only
