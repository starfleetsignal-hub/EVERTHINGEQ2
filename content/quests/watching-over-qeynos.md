## Steps

1. In [[South Qeynos]], travel to the gate leading to the [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum]] and zone in. {{waypoint 695, -21, 366}}
1. Slay 5 of the undead.
1. Go to the central fountain and observe events. A Concordium mage comes and performs a necromantic ritual that slays the undead, replacing them with stronger undead.
1. Slay 5 of the [[A former Starcrest citizen|newer, stronger undead]].     NOTE: If you try to evac to avoid being killed, you will end up in Antonica.  Take the death and revive in the instance you're in.
   - Use [[Akina's Spirit Token]] to tell the priestess of what you have witnessed.
   - Akina will give you a new spell, Divine Avenger.
   - Retreat to the entrance gates and cast the spell on one of the [[A Starcrest citizen|Starcrest citizens]] defending the gates.
   - The Divine Avenger-enhanced citizen will slay one of the new undead for you.
1. Return to [[Akina]] in the [[Temple of Life]].

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Celestial Watch**
