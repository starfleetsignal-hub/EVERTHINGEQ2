## Steps

1. Kill 3 groups, 4 mobs each (2 80^^^ and 2 80^^ Heroic), of [[An unwanted house guest|unwanted house guests]] in [[The Main Ballroom]] {{waypoint 175, 11, -58}}. You CANNOT leave the zone and come back to complete this quest. The mobs will not spawn. You will have to delete the quest and talk to Taechun Welby to get the quest again and spawn the mobs needed. However, you CAN otherwise clear the ballroom and then reset the quest. Only the houseguests will be there. One set on a each side balcony and one in the rear balcony of the room.
1. Return to [[Taechun Welby|Taechun]]

## Rewards

- At least: 65g
