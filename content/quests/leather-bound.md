## Prerequisites

- You must have at least +3800 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Collect 10 *Sokokar skins* from [[A trained war sokokar|trained war sokokars]] or [[A maternal sokokar|maternal sokokars]].
1. Place the items in the box of bones and leather. {{waypoint 685.24, -90.77, 567.81}}
1. Speak to [[Thress Kivatei]]. {{waypoint 683.44, -90.77, 571.09}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- +1,600 faction with **The Order of Rime**
