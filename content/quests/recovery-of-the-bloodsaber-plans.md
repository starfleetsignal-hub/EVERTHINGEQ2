*This quest was part of the former access questline to enter [[The Crypt of Betrayal]], however it's completion is no longer required.

## Steps

1. Recover illicit plans from a Bloodsaber fanatic.
   - Kill [[X'Loitl the Bloodsaber Fanatic]] in the [[Vermin's Snye]], a random spawn in the Bloodsaber area around {{waypoint 87, 0, -168}} or {{waypoint 16, 0, -193}}. The Bloodsabers in these areas are placeholders.
1. Return to Wesaelan.
1. Speak to [[Captain Eitoa]] who wanders the road near the gate to North Qeynos in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint 63, -19, -36}}.
   - **Note**: There is no actual step or quest linking Captain Eitoa or Wesaelan together, so it's best to go to Captain Eitoa right away or you may forget!

## Rewards

- At least 17s 43c
