## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[An ulthork aquassaulter|ulthork aquassaulter]]s west of the camp
1. Speak to Nika
1. Speak to [[Merrik]] in the camp
1. Speak to Nika at the shore {{waypoint 4396, -1095, 1418}} and follow her conversation with Osh
1. Push the three rafts out to sea. You will have to wait between each sailing for Nika's prayers
1. Speak to Osh
1. Speak to Merrik back at camp

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Othmir Standing Net]]
