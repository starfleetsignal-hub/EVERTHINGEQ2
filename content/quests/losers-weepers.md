## Notes

The Charm of Lost Things recipe auto-scribes. You do *not* need to locate it in inventory and scribe it manually.

## Steps

*Those with the [[Track Harvestables]] (tradeskill AA ability) can also track the harvestsables needed to complete this quest.*

1. Gather the following
   - Find 2 [[Weeping willow wood]]. This node appears very close to the tree-trunk bottoms in the region above Vhesh. Possible locations they have been found:
     - {{waypoint 301, -201, -806}}
     - {{waypoint 9.35, -202.98, -813.95}}
     - {{waypoint 70.02, -202.18, -842.25}}![weeping willow wood](images/Weeping_willow_wood_visible.jpg)
   - Find a [[Lonesome lily]]. This is a single node on a tiny island off the Twark coast at {{waypoint -235, -235, -903}}.  ![lonesome lily](images/Lonesome_lily_visible.jpg)
1. Return to Vhesh.
1. Assemble the [[Charm of Lost Things (Crafted)|Charm of Lost Things]].
   - Use the goblin firepit next to him {{waypoint 132, -239, -867}} to craft the [[Charm of Lost Things (Crafted)|charm]].
   - Requires 100 Artisan, 1 [[Lonesome lily]], 2 [[Weeping willow wood]], 5 [[Thaumic Coal]].
1. Return to Vhesh.

## Rewards

- +2000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
- At least 1p 53g 9s
