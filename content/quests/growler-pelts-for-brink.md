## Steps

1. Obtain 5 growler hides by killing [[Grey growler|growler wolves]]. These can be found west of [[The Nursery]] at {{waypoint 14, -43, -785}}
1. Return to [[Brink Trudger]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 55c
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Brink's Finely Crafted Earring]] - Ranger
  - [[Brink's Carved Wooden Bow]] - Ranger
  - [[Brink's Finely Stitched Bracers]]
