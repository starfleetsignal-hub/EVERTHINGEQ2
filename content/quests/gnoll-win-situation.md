## Steps

*Outlander Deston, a member of the Qeynos Guard alarmed by the assembly of gnoll packs, has made his way into Darkpaw Warrens with my help. He now suspects that some sort of spellwork is behind the cooperation we are seeing between gnoll packs, and has tasked me with exploring this line of suspicion.*

1. Enter the Culling Grounds {{waypoint -40, -33, -220}}
1. Collect 10 discarded weapons. Some can be found around {{waypoint -41, -45, -77}}.
   - There are 2 types of weapons on the ground. These are group updates.
   - NOTE: killing the undead gnolls in the [[Culling Grounds]] will cause [[Gorlon the Goner]] to spawn.  Either avoid killing all of the ghostly/skeletal gnolls while collecting the discarded weapons, or kill another boss from Darkpaw Warrens Group 1 before doing this quest, if you want to fight a different boss from this group.
1. After collecting the weapons, a gnoll will shout out.
   - Go to {{waypoint 63, -25, -177}} and speak to [[Arruth Whitepatch]] (in one of the Culling Grounds rooms, up the rope ladder)
1. Search for the source of smell in [[Dustpaw Burrow]]
   - Go to {{waypoint 61, -14, -248}} or to {{waypoint -119, 12, -158}} to enter [[Dustpaw Burrow]] rooms
1. Destroy 6 gnoll devotion braziers in [[Dustpaw Burrow]]
   - Click on a brazier to destroy it. This is a group update.
   - The braziers spawn in any room in [[Dustpaw Burrow]], including the room closer to the Darkpaw Warrens entrance with the broken terraporter.
1. Destroy 1 more gnoll devotion brazier
   - Upon destroying the final brazier, a Sabertooth scribe will spawn.  Kill it to receive [[Sabertooth Scribe Scroll (Quest Item)|Sabertooth Scribe Scroll]]
1. The scroll mentions Harclave, the gnollish hero. Find and examine Harclave's weapon in [[Splitpaw Burrow]]
   - Go to {{waypoint -58, 2, 83}} in [[Splitpaw Burrow]] (near the terraporter) and examine the weapon on the wall.
1. Speak with [[Outlander Deston]], now at the Splitpaw Burrow terraporter near Harclave's weapon.
