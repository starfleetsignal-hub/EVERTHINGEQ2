*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Search for [[A runner of Ree]] and kill him. He runs back and forth on the mountain path between the edge of the Orc camp on the West side of Dog Trapper Lake and the large crate at the end of the Blackshield docks.
1. Travel to the Blackshield docks {{waypoint -592, -145, -877}} and hail [[A Blackshield dock officer]].
   - NOTE: This is also a good time to do the side quest [[Smugglers' Secrets]] from [[Tiff Squeelunkle]] just east of the eastern nomad camp.
1. Show dock officer the receipt from the orc runner you killed.
1. Grab the package from the box by inspecting the large crate on the end of the docks. {{waypoint -598, -145, -880}}
1. Return to Ventar.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 59c
- +70 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
