## Steps

1. Kill three mobs are located in the [[Talonflight Armory]] {{waypoint -31, 128, 172}}:
   - Kill [[A Scaleborn trainer]]
   - Kill [[A talonlord bladehand trainer]]
   - Kill [[Talonlord Steelrend]] or his placeholder [[A talonlord bladehand]] at {{waypoint -55, 126, 155}}.
1. Go to the door in the south to get the update {{waypoint 13, 126, 241}}. You will be ambushed by 3 64^ heroic Scaleborn warriors.
1. Go to the jail to get the update. Standing at {{waypoint 62, 127, 180}} for a few seconds seems to work.
   - The update is a bit finicky. You can try jumping into the cell from the stairs, or running around the room.
1. Return to [[Emissary Kvikz]].

## Rewards

- [[Smoke Vials of Escape]]
- At least 14g 59s 50c
