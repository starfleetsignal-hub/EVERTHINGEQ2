## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of the Tribunal has asked for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Gather the requested supplies
   - Collect 3 [[Thunderous Core|thunderous cores]] from creatures found in [[Zimara Breadth]]
     - *Note: drops in [[Chests|small chest]] from a thunderous cumulus, in Columnar Highlands*
     - *1-2 drops per group member*
   - Gather or purchase [[Direbeak Hide|direbeak hide]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas
     - *Common harvest from BoZ dens*
     - *Can buy off broker*
     - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Seventh Hammer]] at {{waypoint 274, 180, -357}} in [[Timorous Deep]]
