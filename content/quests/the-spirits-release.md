## Description

I have found a magic stone that contains the spirit of a former Darkblade rogue. The spirit within has pleaded with me to avenge their death and free their spirit. I've agreed to kill the Netherot Chanters who are responsible for the rogue's imprisonment.

## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[A netherot chanter|netherot chanters]]. If none are up, kill the trash mobs to pop them. {{waypoint 79, 1, 112}}
   - Found netherot chanters at
     - {{waypoint -83.16, 1.31, 114.21}}
     - {{waypoint -93.68, 1, 105.54}}
     - {{waypoint -75.75, 1.31, 114.89}}
1. Examine the sphere.
