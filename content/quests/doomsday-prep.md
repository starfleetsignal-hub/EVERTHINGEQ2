## Steps

![](images/Metetherial_stone.jpg)![halcyiolite flask](images/Halcyiolite_flask.jpg)

1. Mine 10 [[Raw arcanium]] from Metetherial Stone in the [[Kandra Uplands]] and [[Metetherial Plains]]. They look like flattend, slightly shimmering ore nodes found near rocks and cliffs. Possible locations include:  (**NOT** a group update; **NOT** trackable by Harvest or Standard tracking.)
   - {{waypoint -401.77, 81.84, -11.49}}
   - {{waypoint -459.55, 46.70, 214.45}}
   - {{waypoint -426.42, 44.32, 236.87}}
   - {{waypoint -360.34, 56.31, 73.39}}
   - {{waypoint -317.97, 49.75, 199.28}}
   - {{waypoint -425.30, 10.17, -134.35}}
   - {{waypoint -320.12, 48.98, 92.37}}
1. Return to [[Jihyon]] {{waypoint -188, 575, -608}} and receive [[Compiled Arcanium (Recipe Book)]]. Scribe the scroll.
1. Create two compiled arcanium at the Vahla Kiln. ![Vahla Kiln](images/Vahla_Kiln.jpg)
   1. Items required for the combine:
      - Purchase two [[Enigma dust]] from [[Vasturi]] at {{waypoint -100, 574, -704}}
      - Gather up four flasks of [[Halcyiolite]]. They're located around the [[Khali'Vahla]] area, usually on tables and next to chests.
   1. Take the teleport at {{waypoint -147, 576, -742}} to the Artisan Terrace and use the "Vahla Kiln"-device (see picture) {{waypoint -137, 1263, -757}} to craft two Compiled Arcanium.
1. Return to [[Jihyon]] at {{waypoint -188, 575, -608}}.

- Choose one of the Following:
  - [[Magic's Slicing Fan]]
  - [[Magic's Piercing Lace]]
  - [[Magic's Conjured Teeth]]
  - [[Magic's Avenging Caduceus]]
