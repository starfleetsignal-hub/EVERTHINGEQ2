## Steps

1. Find instructions for making a [[Skinning Knife]]. Purchase [[Far Seas Trading Company Recipe: Basic Skinning Knives]]  from [[Matthew]] for 4g 60s 80c
1. Take the recipe back to [[Ruffin]] - hail Ruffin
1. Scribe the recipe and use the fire next to [[Ruffin]] to make a [[Skinning Knife]].
   - You'll need 2 [[Titanium ore]], 1 [[Mottled pelt]], and 1 [[Ethereal coal]].
1. Talk to [[Ruffin]] again.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
