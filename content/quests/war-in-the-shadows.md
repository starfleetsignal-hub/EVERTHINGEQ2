## Steps

1. Find [[Rav'hyl Daln]] near the library of the [[Academy of Arcane Science]].  He's outside the Academy, at {{waypoint -14, -8, -139}}.
1. Enter [[Longshadow Alley: Daeneth Cloister]] to find the liason between the vampires and the Thexians.  He's on the dock at the far end of the Alley {{waypoint -47, 5, -63}}.
1. Return to [[Glaria]].
1. She sends you to find the vampire hideout, and destroy the leaders of their operation. The hideout is near the [[Beggar's Court]] entrance {{waypoint -117, -53, -344}}.  Look for a wooden door in the stone wall to enter [[The Hidden Crypt]].
1. Return to [[Glaria]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 25s 61c
- [[Ring of the Freeblood (War in the Shadows)|Ring of the Freeblood]]
