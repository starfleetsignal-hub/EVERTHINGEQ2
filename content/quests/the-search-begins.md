## Notes

- If [[Rashad]] is not at his location then he has most likely been killed as killing him is the last step of his quest series. His respawn is roughly 7 minutes long.

## Steps

1. Search for a skeleton in the [[The Croc Caves|Croc Caves]]:
   - {{waypoint -1196, -218, -625}}
   - {{waypoint -1132, -217, -547}}
   - {{waypoint -1240, -234, -832}}
1. Return to [[Rashad]].

## Rewards

- At least 2g 99s 47c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Savage Spined Barb]]  (All Fighters)
  - [[Serene Animal Spirit]]
  - [[Spellbook of the Worldly Necromancer]]
  - [[Sphere of Bronze Blasts]] (All Scouts)
