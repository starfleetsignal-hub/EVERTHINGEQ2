## Steps

1. Speak with [[Elrenn Hildreth]] {{waypoint -622, -1, -23}}.
   - *He is behind a tree just outside of [[Druid Grove]] (the center green area).*
1. Search [[Aurelian Coast]] for the small band of Fordel Midst loggers.
   - Talk to [[Rhap]] in [[Aurelian Coast]] {{waypoint 388, 51, -1}}.
1. Enter [[Echo Caverns]].
1. Make your way to the [[Abandoned Quarry]] .  You must be able to fly in Echo Caverns.
   - Enter the hole in the wall at {{waypoint -99, 249, -43}}
   - Take the left fork at {{waypoint -173, 273, -60}}
   - Enter [[The Shattered Chasm]] at {{waypoint -243, 265, -145}}
   - Fly to [[Hermit's Hideaway]] at {{waypoint -188, 312, -318}}
   - Fly to the [[Abandoned Quarry]] {{waypoint -2.00, 107.00, -251.00}} from [[Hermit's Hideaway]].
1. Find [[Daloryn Krey]] in the [[Abandoned Quarry]] at {{waypoint -35.60, 100.01, -210.33}}
1. Defeat the creature attacking Daloryn.
1. Revive Daloryn (click on his body).
1. Return to [[Elrenn Hildreth]] in [[Shadow Haven]] {{waypoint -622, -1, -23}}.
1. Return to [[Hapith Gracearms]] at {{waypoint -801.64, -1.06, -70.40}}.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s
- Agent of The Hearth, prefix title
- One of the following:
  - [[Goldtalon,Gift of The Hunter]]
  - [[Swiftbold,Gift of the traveler]]
