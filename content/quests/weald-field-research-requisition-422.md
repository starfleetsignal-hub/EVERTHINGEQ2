## Required Materials

*In addition to the materials harvested for the Requisition, you will need:*

- 1 [[Lantern Hogfish]] - *rare*
- 25 [[Coruscating Sandpaper]]

## Steps

1. Harvest Materials:
   - 15 [[Splitfin Dartfish]]
   - 18 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
   - 45 [[Rockhopper Meat]]
1. Craft [[Wracked Ceramic-Lined Soup Bowl]].
1. Return to [[Erdani Ruilep]].

## Rewards

- [[Research Mission Reward Crate]]
- 8 [[Recuso Field Voucher]]
- At least 1p 25g 7,700 status
