## Steps

1. Check what supplies Baelfang has to work with (click items on the ground next to Baelfang) to receive [[Notes on Baelfang's supplies]]
1. At the engraver's desk {{waypoint -322, 14, -5}} create [[Recipes for Matoppie Bedding]]. You will need the [[Notes on Baelfang's supplies]] in your inventory, along with:
   - 1 x matoppie root
   - 2 x iridium clusters
   - 1 x effulgent incense
1. The completed recipe book, "[[Recipes for Matoppie Bedding]]", will update the quest with 2 new recipes appearing in your craft window: Matoppie Blanket and Matoppie Pillow allowing you to craft the next 2 items
1. Craft 2 [[Matoppie Blanket|Matoppie Blankets]] on a [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]] at {{waypoint -394, 15, 65}}. Each blanket requires:
   - 3 matoppie roots
   - 2 thick bear pelt
   - 5 Effulgent Filament
1. Craft 2 [[Matoppie Pillow|Matoppie Pillows]] on a [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]]. Each pillow requires:
   - 2 matoppie roots
   - 2 thick bear pelt
   - 5 Effulgent Filament
1. Click the green shiny next to Baelfang to place the crafted items.
1. Speak to Baelfang
