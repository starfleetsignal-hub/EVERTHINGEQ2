## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 50 [[Celestial Incense]]
- 50 [[Celestial Coal]]

*Note: The harvest materials required as part of the quest are not included  here.*

## Steps

1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 260, -20, -285}} from the [[North Wing Stacks]] on the first floor of the Library to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]] to access the overland zones and harvest the following:
   - 25 [[Reef cluster|reef clusterfrom]] coalesced reef.
   - 25 [[Silvered fleck]] from flecked mass
   - 25 [[Dewpalm lumber|dewpalm lumberfrom]] fallen dewpalm branch.
1. Harvest the following: *Yes, these are two separate steps.*
   - 25 [[Reef cluster]] from coalesced reef
   - 25 [[Silvered fleck]] from flecked mass
   - 25 [[Dewpalm lumber]] from Doomfire
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Decay Suspension Scroll]] and create [[Decay Suspension Scroll]] on an [[Engraved Desk]].
   - There is an [[Engraved Desk]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]] through the teleporter in the [[South Wing Catalog]] on the second floor to the [[Crafters' Gallery]] at {{waypoint 332, 16, 174}}
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Protective Coating]] and create [[Protective Coating (Item)|Protective Coating]] on a [[Work Bench]].
   - There is a [[Work Bench]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]] through the teleporter in the [[South Wing Catalog]] on the second floor to the [[Smiths' Gallery]] at {{waypoint 388, 16, 170}}
1. Speak with [[Ramyn Matrick]] in the [[Smiths' Gallery]] at {{waypoint 653, 411, 21}}.

## Rewards

- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
