## Steps

1. Look for Mordain Glacierbane in [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Shadowed Corridors]]
1. Mordain is on the 1st floor (Submergence) near the endmob of that floor. You need to kill [[Xalgoti (Heroic)|Xalgoti]] before you can examine the body to update the quest, you had to have been present for the kill, and you have 1 hour after killing him to examine the body.
1. Return to Malidain.

Warning! You will lose your Gold ring including any and all adornments at the moment of turning in the quest.
