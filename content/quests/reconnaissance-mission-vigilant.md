## Description

The Vigilant is way overdue from its normal arrival times and its crew is no longer responding to communications. Use the command sphere and travel out to the Vigilant and gather information as to its dilemma!

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Vigilant: Incursion]]
1. I should travel to the Navigation Bridge and kill the Tallonite leader.
   - Kill [[Vice Vigoth Sanad]].

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x3
- At least 1p 80g
