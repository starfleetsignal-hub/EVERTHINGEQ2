## Notes

- This quest scales to the adventure level tier of the character, i.e. level 21-30 characters will receive the level 25 version of the quest.
- This quest is repeatable up to 10 times. per adventure level tier.
- You may need to kill more than the listed number of monsters in order to retrieve the required drops for this quest.
- Players adventure level 121+ will need to mentor down to a lower level range in order to get these quests offered.

## Steps

1. Slay the following monsters (for your adventure level tier) until you retrieve 5 of the indicated quest item:
   - **Tier 1 (Level 1-10)**
     - [[A clawsnap|Clawsnap crabs]] in [[Greater Faydark]]
     - Wasps in [[Greater Faydark]]
   - **Tier 2 (Level 11-20)**
     - [[Crushbone sparrer|Crushbone sparrers]] in [[Greater Faydark]]
     - [[Crushbone trainee|Crushbone trainees]] in [[Greater Faydark]]
   - **Tier 3 (Level 21-30)**
     - [[Shadesire defender|Shadesire defenders]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
     - Gorgers in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
   - **Tier 4 (Level 31-40)**
     - [[A Vallon miner|Vallon miners]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]]
     - [[A Blackhook raider|Blackhook raiders]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]]
   - **Tier 5 (Level 41-50)**
     - Spiders in [[Steamfont Mountains]]
     - Wolves or coyotes in [[Steamfont Mountains]]
   - **Tier 6 (Level 51-60)**
     - [[Greater Growth essence|Greater Growth essences]] and [[Lesser Growth essence|lesser Growth essences]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]]
     - [[Honeybrood worker|Honeybrood workers]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]]
   - **Tier 7 (Level 61-70)**
     - [[A living chest|Living chests]] in [[Kaladim]]
     - [[An obsidian construct|Obsidian constructs]] in [[Mistmoore Catacombs]]
   - **Tier 8 (Level 71-80)**
     - Gorillas in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
     - Bloodthorns and mantrps in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
   - **Tier 9 (Level 81-90)**
     - [[An inimical sparklet|Inimical sparklets]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]
     - [[A pond piranha|Pond piranhas]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]]
   - **Tier 10 (Level 91-100)**
     - [[Insidious fearlings]] in [[Eidolon Jungle]]
     - [[Churning obol]] in [[Obol Plains]]
   - **Tier 11 (Level 101-110)**
     - [[Seabound cnidcara]] in [[Plane of Magic]]
     - [[Aranid bluffstalkers]] in [[Plane of Magic]]
1. Return to [[Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber (Kelethin)|Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber]] in [[Kelethin]].

  - [[Frostfell Wreath]]
  - 4 [[Frozen Token of E'ci]]
