## Steps

1. [[Sothshae]] {{waypoint 751, 366, 366}}
   - Gather repeller remnants on [[Sothshae]]. These are the broken golems lying on the ground.
   - Release scale-crafted repellers on [[Sothshae]].  Look for ghostly sparkling places, click the cloud to "place" the rebuilt golem.
1. [[Nuthshae]] {{waypoint 877, 269, -47}}
   - Gather repeller remnants on [[Nuthshae]]. These are the broken golems lying on the ground.
   - Release scale-crafted repellers on [[Nuthshae]].  Look for ghostly sparkling places, click the cloud to "place" the rebuilt golem.
1. Return to [[Savtek]].
