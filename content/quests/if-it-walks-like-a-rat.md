You must be a Ratonga and at least Level 20 to start this quest.

- *This is false as of 5/2020, can be started as low as level 9*

## Steps

1. Tilla is concerned about the stories of ratonga doing bad things that she's been hearing.  Investigate around the city.
   - Find the stolen goods - they look like sparkling treasure chests.
     - Behind the guild hall {{waypoint 391, -20, 53}}
     - Near where the aquaduct and the outer wall meet {{waypoint 287, -21, -192}}
     - Overlooking the moat where the aquaduct crosses it {{waypoint 420, -24, -125}}
   - Clean the poisoned food stores.  These are sacks that spew green smoke.
     - Two food stores can be found in [[Voleen and Copperpot's Bakery]] {{waypoint 282, -16, 40}} and {{waypoint 274, -16, 39}}
     - One in [[Sneed Galliway's Trading Post]] {{waypoint 294, -17, -82}}
   - Investigate the attacks in the corners and alleyways - you'll be attacked by groups of three [[A ratonga blackjack|ratonga blackjacks]] and must kill them for the update.
     - Behind Irontoes East {{waypoint 342, -21, 158}}
     - Behind the North Qeynos Guild Hall {{waypoint 381, -20, 63}}
     - Near where the aquaduct and the outer wall meet {{waypoint 287, -21, -92}}
     - Beside [[The Ironforge Estate]] {{waypoint 447, -20.5, -375}}
     - Overlooking the moat where the aquaduct crosses it {{waypoint 420, -24, -185}}
1. Return to Tilla

## Rewards

- At least 5s 81c @ lvl 20
