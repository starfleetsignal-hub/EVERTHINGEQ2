This is not normal collection quest. The collection members are not obtained by harvesting shinies. <br>
Instead, One piece (per instance) can be harvested from the fallen chandelier after killing [[Gudre Blackhand]] in Castle Highhold (Advanced Solo). Multiple pieces are harvestable each instance, though once a piece is harvested, the others disappear.
