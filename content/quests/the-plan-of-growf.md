## Notes

*If you start Growf's line before journeying to Fens of Nathsar, Growf may not instantly offer this after the last quest. Once you return from [[Search and Rescue]], Growf should then offer this quest. As of Feb 2026, this quest is offered immediately without progress or completion of [[Search and Rescue]].*<br>
*If you don't have a Gathering Goblin, you won't get this quest (update Mar 18, 2022 - this quest will be offered even if you do not have the gathering goblin ability).*

## Steps

1. Growf says gathering goblin may be unhappy and asks you to verify.
1. Summon and talk to your gathering goblin.
1. Tell Growf what your gathering goblin said.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +4,000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
