## Description

"Leslie Afterstar has been sent into the forest to decrease the predator population that is eating all the good game. The population has become so dense that he is requesting your help to thin their numbers for him. Slay several young brown-claw bears and report back to Leslie." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay 5 [[A young brown-claw bear|young brown-claw bears]] at {{waypoint -1, 7, 160}}.
1. Return to Leslie Afterstar

## Rewards

- At least 2s 57c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Archon's Boots (Version 3)|Archon's Boots]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Slippers (Version 2)|Channeler's Slippers]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Boots (Version 2)|Earthkeeper's Boots]] (Druids)
  - [[Elder's Boots (Version 3)|Elder's Boots]] (Shamans)
  - [[Enlightened Sandals (Version 2)|Enlightened Sandals]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Boots (Version 3)|Knight's Boots]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Shade's Boots (Version 3)|Shade's Boots]] (All Scouts)
