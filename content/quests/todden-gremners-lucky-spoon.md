## Steps

1. Lure out the beast that attacked Todden using the meatpies as bait.
   1. Receive three [[Meatpie|meatpies]] from [[Todden Gremner|Todden]].
   1. Go to the spot Todden lost his spoon {{waypoint -43, 369, 1217}}.
   1. Use all three [[Meatpie|meatpies]] and place them as bait.
   1. [[Frightfang]] will spawn. Kill him and loot [[Todden Gremner's Lucky Spoon (Item)|Todden Gremner's Lucky Spoon]] from the corpse (this step updates the quest).
1. Return to [[Todden Gremner]].
