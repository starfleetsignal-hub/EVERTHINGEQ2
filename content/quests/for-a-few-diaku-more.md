## Steps

1. Kill Diakaroo to obtain 12 **Corral Gate Keys**. As of 25/04/2025, this can be done alone (as long as you've got decent gear around ~ 9000 resolve) in the Heroic instance if you've killed all Diaku in the Solo and you don't want to wait to reset the zone.
1. Plant 8 keys in tinkered toolboxes. Toolbox Locations:
   - At the first gate. {{waypoint -756.65, 65.80, -513.72}}
   - At the plaza de pinatas after the platform {{waypoint -685.98, 62.60, -458.37}}
   - At the plaza de pinatas near the second gate {{waypoint -611.42, 62.75, -489.27}}
   - In front of Rodeo de Joro {{waypoint -524.18, 62.13, -606.61}}
   - At the Rodeo de Joro {{waypoint -563.96, 61.05, -674.45}}
   - Behind the third gate on the platform {{waypoint -630.23, 62.37, -685.00}}
   - Behind The Lion's Den {{waypoint -704.08, 61.49, -617.20}}
   - Behind the last gate {{waypoint -665.00, 61.64, -578.85}}
1. Return to [[Bridon Kizzlebash]].
