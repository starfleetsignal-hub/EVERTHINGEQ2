## Notes

- This quest is part 1 of an 11-quest series in the [[Bloodline Chronicles Timeline]], which grants you access to [[The Crypt of T'haen]] along the way.
- Be sure to select [[The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers]], not [[The Tombs of Night]], as the latter instance will not spawn the required named monsters.
- The quest listed as a prerequisite, The D'Morte Family Crest, is not required to start this quest. Although in terms of lore progression it is recommended that it be started beforehand.
- **Important Note**: Hailing the wounded scouts before they die may bug them, causing them not to drop the rock sample

## Steps

1. You must mine four rock samples inside [[The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers]]. The rock samples are dropped from *wounded scouts* in the zone. These are spawned by killing specific named monsters. Once the named monster is dead, simply mine the rock sample that spawns near the scouts body. The scout will always be found near their related named mob.
   - [[The Mold Reaper]]: Spawns at the end of a ring event involving [[A mist grinnin sporechanter (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|mist grinnin sporechanters]] at {{waypoint 78, 6, 3}}
   - [[Lureclaw the Angler]]: Spawns after killing **all** the mist grinnins around his spawn at the base of [[The Forgotten Tears (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|The Forgotten Tears]] around {{waypoint 47, -44, 106}}, as well as the rest of the mist grinnins on this level.
   - [[Chief Strongarm (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|Chief Strongarm]]: Spawns at {{waypoint 28, -25, 59}} after killing **all** mist grinnin packs in [[The Maw of Mortality (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|The Maw of Mortality]], then the [[A mist grinnin summoner|mist grinnin summoners]] that spawn running towards the center of the Maw, and lastly the [[A mudraker (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|mudrakers]] that spawn around the NPC version of Chief Strongarm.
   - [[Dvith N'Zur]]: Kill him to spawn the *wounded scout*, who appears in the central pit of [[The Hand of the Crypt (The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers)|The Hand of the Crypt]]. {{waypoint -74, -32, 116}}
1. Return to [[Thraixe Il'ferrai]] for your reward.
