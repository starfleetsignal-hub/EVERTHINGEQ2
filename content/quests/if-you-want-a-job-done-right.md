## Steps

1. Slay the [[Overseer V'thex]] in new location {{waypoint 552, -44, -29}} or old location {{waypoint 509, -46, -27}}.
1. Loot [[A note from Prince Talvus]] from the corpse of Overseer V'thex and take it back to Miriam.

## Rewards

- At least 42s 88c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Elder's Nebulous Leggings]] (Shamans)
  - [[Shade's Nebulous Leggings]] (Assassin, Bard, Beastlord, Rogue, Ranger)
  - [[Channeler's Nebulous Pants]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Nebulous Leggings]] (Druids)
  - [[Enlightened Nebulous Leggings]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Archon's Nebulous Greaves]] (Clerics)
  - [[Knight's Nebulous Greaves]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
