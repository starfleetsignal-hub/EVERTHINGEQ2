## Steps

1. Speak to [[Chief Ko-ma Nurwin]] in a house off the [[Courtyard of Hope]]. {{waypoint 44, 15, -119}}
1. Speak to [[Guard Captain Urtai]] near [[Sighing Willow Pond]]. {{waypoint -142, 1, -96}}
1. Speak to [[Aras Nurwin]] in the Outpost Tavern. {{waypoint -87, -2, 109}}
1. Speak to [[Terai Augren]] near the Mystic Waterfall. {{waypoint -249, -1, -79}}
1. Speak to [[Jai-la Ventur]] who wanders near the shore. {{waypoint 116, -2, -26}}
   - Note: At around 6:30 AM she walks out to the beach. Be sure to follow her to the beach. You must walk up behind her to trigger the next step. She will toss something into the water and the quest completion flag will appear. When speaking with Jai-La, you will also be offered the quest [[The Pirate's Bride]]. She also appeared there around 1:30 AM and later in the day, probably around 5:00 PM. The times seem to be somewhat random. It seems that if you remain too close while she's on the beach, she won't throw anything into the water. If she comes to the beach, back off a bit and then you should be able to hail her. She may be "up" all the time now.
1. Speak to [[Guard Captain Urtai]] again.
1. Return to [[Chief Ko-ma Nurwin]] to claim your reward.

## Reward

- [[A square Wantia table]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Honorbow]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Loosely Stitched Pouch of Falling Stars]] (All Scouts, Berserker, Bruiser, Guardian, Monk)
  - [[Starfeller]] (All Scouts, Channelers)
  - [[Wand of Blessing]] (All Mages, All Priests)
  - [[Wand of the Amaranth]] (All Mages, All Priests)

- +200 faction with **The Far Seas Trading Company**
