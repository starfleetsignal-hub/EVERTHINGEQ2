## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 25 [[Coruscating Filament]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest the following:
   - 10 [[Hardened Zelniak Pelt]]
   - 18 [[Luclizite Cluster]]
   - 50 [[Radiant Algae]]
1. Craft a [[Pristine Zelniak Harness]] on a [[Work Bench]].
1. Return to [[Curator Ferris]] on the upper floor of [[Sanctus Seru (City)]] at {{waypoint 1.41, 180.79, -167.11}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 70g 5934 status
- 4 [[Inquisitive Mark of Seru]]
- [[Empyral Stocking Crate]]
