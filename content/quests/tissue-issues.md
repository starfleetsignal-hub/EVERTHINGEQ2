## Description

I must enter Vasty Deep: The Abandoned Labs and collect tissue samples from unfathomable horrors and Homunculis the Creation.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Vasty Deep: The Abandoned Labs|the Abandoned Labs]] and collect five tissue samples from [[An unfathomable horror|unfathomable horrors]] and [[Homunculis|Homunculis The Creation]].
1. Return to [[Alia]]
