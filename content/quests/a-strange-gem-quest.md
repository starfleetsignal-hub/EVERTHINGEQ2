- This quest is repeatable.
- Gives access to the instance: [[A small hideout]]

## Notes

- This instance is targeted at groups level 15-20.
- Every group member must have the gem and be on the quest to enter.

## Steps

![A Strange Statue](images/A_Strange_Statue.jpg)

1. Examine the gem.
1. Go to the cliff-edge north of the [[Ulteran Spires (Ant)|Ulteran Spires]] and drop down onto a ledge to the cave entrance {{waypoint -2057, 7, -738}}.
1. Enter the cave, click on the statue at the back and insert the gem.

## Rewards

- Access to [[A small hideout]]
