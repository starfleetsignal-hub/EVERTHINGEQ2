## Notes

- *This quest in unavailable to characters of Evil -alignment.*
- *This quest is repeatable..*
- This quest will become unavailable once you hit 20,000 faction with the Ashen Order.
- If you receive an undesirable list of target monsters, delete the quest and pick it up again; the target monsters are all randomized.

## Steps

- Kill five of one of the following (random):
  - [[A Sandscrawler defender|Sandscrawler defenders]]
  - [[A Sandscrawler raider|Sandscrawler raiders]]
- Kill five of one of the following (random):
  - [[A desert lunatic|desert lunatics]]
  - [[A desert prophet|desert prophets]]
  - [[A Windsister dustfeather|Windsister dustfeathers]]
  - [[A Windsister jaggedfeather|Windsister jaggedfeathers]]
- Kill five of one of the following (random):
  - [[A flame twister|flame twisters]]
  - [[A large blanketfish|large blanketfish]] (be sure to kill wandering fish including the regular blanketfish to get spawns. Rare only if people fail to do this. Same goes with any other spawns like sorcerer below. Take carpet ride to Stinging Isle, climb down to Naga Island, go down ramp, about half wayto next ramp is a teeming area of fish.  Careful with aoe bc there are mobs of heroic marlins.)
  - [[A golden sunfeather falcon|golden sunfeather falcons]] (near Prophet's Peak) {{waypoint 627, -50, -936}}
  - [[A sand pike|sand pikes]] Take carpet to giant's field, recreationally kill some lesser djinn on djinn's pillar if you want, jump in water, tab around until you find sand pikes, then head to the ramp south of Jin Li, avoiding pirhana mobs (keep tabbing as you go).
  - [[A sandstalker sabertooth|sandstalker sabertooths]]
- Kill five of one of the following (random):
  - [[A Sandscrawler miner|Sandscrawler miners]]
  - [[A Sandscrawler sorcerer|Sandscrawler sorcerers]] (very rare, so best to avoid these)I found these at the barriers east of Prophets peak in the valley, clear all the Sandcrawlers there they repop. They are also found in the valley between the miners with mobs of raiders, shamans and defenders. The sorcerers are usu near the tents.
- Return to [[Shu Fang Qi]]

## Rewards

- +1500 faction with **The Ashen Order**
