## Steps

1. Speak with [[Pitmaster Shar'rik]] About the leather scap.
1. Speak with [[Cincia Gelt]] about the leather scrap. 1314 17 2,294 on Kerra Island, Sundered Frontier.
1. Cincia instructs you to throw the scalp into a fire found in the middle of the village. {{waypoint 1322, 45, 2134}}
1. Talk to the ghost of [[Telloc Thaar]].
1. Telloc requires you to take the claws of the largest tiger on [[Kerra Isle]].
   - By clearing all the tigers on around {{waypoint 1453, 25, 1993}}, [[Coltang]] will spawn. He will come chasing you right away so be ready!  Kill him and inspect his corpse for the claws. (Someone kill [[Coltang]] before you could? Not to worry just inspect the corpse to get update.)
1. Return to [[Telloc Thaar|Telloc]].
1. Next, you must kill twelve [[A Toxxulia prowler|Toxxulia prowlers]] (found in many areas throughout [[Toxxulia Forest]]) and take their barbs.
1. Return to Telloc with the barbs in hand.
1. Throw the prowler barbs into the fire, and speak with Telloc again.
1. Now you must find someone who can complete the effigy necessary for Telloc's ritual.
   - Talk to [[Loran Tico'Ma]] {{waypoint -375, 248, 2988}} in the [[Hua Mein Village]].
   - *Note: to access the Village, you must complete enough of [[The Sundered Frontier Timeline]] (or of the [[Sentinel's Fate Crafting Timeline]]) to receive [[Jin'tu's Gift]].*
   - *It is possible to use a call such as [[Call of the Tinkerer]] to port yourself to someone at this location to get this update.*
   - *It is possible to walk there by running at the edge of the mountain next to the exit and then glide down to the water. (If do not have enough faction the guards will be agro)
1. To complete the effigy, Loran needs you to harvest 8 Toxxulia running roots.  Roots are Harvest Trackable.
   - This quest step requires two people to complete. The running roots are found in pairs in and around [[Toxxulia's Reach]]. Each person must click on their root of the pair at the same time. Both people will get credit for harvesting one root.
   - *Note: a player who is not on the quest can still assist with this step.*
   - *Note: Two-Boxing is possible with quick switching from one alt to the other*
1. Return to [[Loran Tico'Ma|Loran]].
1. Speak to [[Telloc Thaar]] with the completed effigy.
   - Telloc summons [[JoJo]], an undercon level 85^^^ Named ghost tiger. Whoever spawns him will be stunned, and after JoJo kills Telloc Thaar the tiger attacks the person who spawned him.
     - He seems to be immune to stuns, although it is unknown at the moment if he has a high stun resistability or completely ignores them.
1. Defeat [[JoJo]] to complete the quest.
   - *If you fail to defeat [[JoJo]], re-summon the tiger by clicking on the fire.*
