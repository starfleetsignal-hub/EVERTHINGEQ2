## Steps

![](images/A_rampaging_cerodon.jpg)

1. Kill an angry' Deino (any dinosaur with 'rampaging' in its name, eg a rampaging cerodon).
1. Kill 6 more 'rampaging' dinosaurs.
1. Return to [[Guldri]] at {{waypoint -624, 6, 350}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 29g 60s
- One of the Following:
  - [[Saliraptor Talon]]
  - [[Kilwren's Secret]]
  - [[The Sea's Memory]]
