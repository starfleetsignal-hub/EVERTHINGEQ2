## Materials Required / Notes

- 25 [[Coruscating Filament]]
- *Note: The harvest materials required as part of the quest are not included  here.*
- Suggested: [[Portable Sewing Table]] or Summon Crafting Stations AA

## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Blinding]] and harvest the following:
   - 1 [[Jumping Creeper]] from crawling root break (**Rare**)
   - 32 [[Slow Creeping Root]] from crawling root break
   - 28 [[Luclizite Cluster]] from coalesced stone cluster
   - 12 [[Radiant Algae]] from lunar berry bush
   - *NOTE: All the above harvests are abundant in the area below Grieg's Spire platform {{waypoint 619.09, 428.17, -582.54}}, where it is safe to harvest them.*
   - *Receive [[Recipe Scroll: Sanctus Seru Formal Garb]] (which auto-scribes) on completion of all required harvests.*
1. Create [[Sanctus Seru Formal Garb (Crafted)|Sanctus Seru Formal Garb]] on a [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]].
   - Nearest [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]] are in [[Recuso Tor]] at {{waypoint -593.12, 38.39, 466.09}}.
   - Or in the Fabrication Hall at  {{waypoint 140.01, -510.72, -11.40}}.
1. Return to [[Sanctus Seru (City)|Sanctus Seru [City]]], take the portal to 2nd floor go to {{waypoint -203, 182, 1.79}} (Arx Seru door), quest should update.
1. Equip [[Sanctus Seru Formal Garb (Crafted)|Sanctus Seru Formal Garb]] and click on the entrance of **Arx Seru** near {{waypoint -196.93, 183.22, -0.67}}.
1. Enter [[Sanctus Seru: Party for One (Piercing the Darkness)|Sanctus Seru: Party for One [Piercing the Darkness]]]  and sit at {{waypoint -107.19, 78.12, -5.20}}.
   - *Note: The game checks for the Formal Garb several feet from the door.  If you wait until at the door to equip it, the zone won't be available.  Back up a bit and try again.  No need to remove and re-equip the gear.*
1. Wait for [[Lord Triskian Seru]] to finish speaking then speak to him.
1. Leave [[Sanctus Seru: Party for One (Piercing the Darkness)|Sanctus Seru: Party for One [Piercing the Darkness]]] and return to [[Sanctus Seru: Fabrication Hall]] at {{waypoint -181.19, 180.67, 95.49}} to speak with [[Hulda Flintbraid]].
1. Examine/read [[Benosch Ironsprocket's Enchanted Field Guide to Luclin]]. (Book in your backpack)
