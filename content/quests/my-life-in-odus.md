## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[A highland fearstalker|highland fearstalkers]], and loot their bodies for their meat.
   - *Note: the quest says "near Aglthin Pond," no other fearstalkers will update this quest.* {{waypoint 238, 364, 914}}
   - *Note: only 1 drop each fearstalker, good to know if you are grouped*
1. Return to [[Chef Bahiyya]] and hail her.
1. Taste the stew by clicking the cauldron beside Bahiyya.
1. Speak again to Chef Bahiyya.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
