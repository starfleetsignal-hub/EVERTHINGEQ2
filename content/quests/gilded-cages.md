## Steps

1. Craft 3 [[Square Cage|Square Cages]] from the recipe provided by Aggi, [[Practical Bird Cages]].  The materials needed to craft all 3 are:
   - [[Woodworking Table]] (one can be found at {{waypoint 878, -274, 119}} in [[The Thurgadin Exchange]])
   - 6 [[Sumac lumber]]
   - 3 [[Titanium ore]]
   - 3 [[Ethereal sandpaper]]
1. Return to [[Aggi Stonefist]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
