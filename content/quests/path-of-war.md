*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Find the high priests of both sides of the conflict (Tae Ew and Rallosian). They are level 40-42.
   - [[High Priest of Thule]] is found at the head of the Anaconda River {{waypoint -1196, -2, 623}}. Talk with him first, then kill him.
   - [[Kromok the Acolyte (NPC)|Kromok the Acolyte]] is found north-west of the Tower of Vul {{waypoint -820, -1, 379}}. Talk with him first, then kill him.
1. Return to [[Gretchen Spiritstorm]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 69s 57c
- [[Alternate Advancement]] Experience
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Ash Coated Acolyte's Armband]] (All Priests)
  - [[Ash Coated Researcher's Armband]] (All Mages)
  - [[Ash Coated Soldier's Armband]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Ash Coated Stalker's Armband]] (All Scouts)
