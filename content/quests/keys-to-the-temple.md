## Steps

1. Go to [[Temple Street: Vault of Necromechanica]]
1. Gather 8 necromechanical schematics (glowing pages found in the streets and rooms)
1. Kill 5 [[A necromechanist|necromechanists]]
1. Kill [[A senior necromechanist]] and accept and examine the [[Necro-Core Forbulator 1122x8733-2b]]   {{waypoint 59, 3, 72}}  or {{waypoint 79, -4, 110}}
1. Speak with [[Foci Tamara Paust]] via [[Foci's Whisper Stone]]
1. Use the [[Temple Street Gnome Illusion]] and speak with [[Glimzig Clangbottom]] {{waypoint 21, 3, 59}}
   1. NOTE: If you have deleted the Temple Street Gnome Illusion (and you aren't a gnome) you can get a replacement item from the jar next to Foci Tamara Paust.
1. Speak with [[Glimzig Clangbottom]] to get [[Necro-Core Forbulator 1122x8733-2b Decoy]]
1. Use it at {{waypoint 44, 3, 83}}
1. Return to [[Foci Tamara Paust]] in [[The City of Freeport]]

## Rewards

- [[Freeport Cloak of the Academy]]
- +1000 faction with **The Academy of Arcane Science**
