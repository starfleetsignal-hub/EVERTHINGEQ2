This is not a normal collection quest. The collection members are not obtained by harvesting shinies.<br>
Instead, the fifteen collection members are themselves all obtained from the completion of other collection quests. This is called a "meta-collection".

## Rewards

- [[Transporting the Troops]]
- [[Small Stack of Far Seas Ferrin]]
- [[Shield of the Phantom Sea]] this item is needed for the collection: [[Triumphs of Shattered Seas]]
