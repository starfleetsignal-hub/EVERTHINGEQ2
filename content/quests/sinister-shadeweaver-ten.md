They're out there, oh yes, ten of the greatest evildoers known to man, woman, child and cub. This Sinister Shadeweaver Ten, as they've been labeled, must be cataloged and studied, and I'm the only one with a quill and ink. It's always me...

- Catalogue the puddle of bones, [[Sludgebile]] {{waypoint 609, 18, -760}}
- Catalogue the vile owlbear, [[Karadine]] {{waypoint -325, 164, -827}}
- Catalogue the fiery fiend, [[Lungfire Fiend]]  {{waypoint 160, -9, -158}}
- Catalogue the Fallen Sergeant, [[Shelsishaazzz]] {{waypoint 330, 137, 533}}
- Catalogue the spirit crocodile, [[Chebura]] {{waypoint -370, 120, -152}}
- Catalogue the fire flinging tegi, [[Grukblug Fireflinger]] {{waypoint 370, 95, 519}}
- Catalogue the Loda Kai warlord, [[Xanora Ghostmoon]] {{waypoint 810, 69, 445}}
- Catalogue the massive webspinner, [[Plaguenon the Webspinner]] {{waypoint -232, 44, -528}} (Epic x3)
- Catalogue the bony tornado, [[Whirlbone]] {{waypoint 781, 19, -582}} (Epic x3)
- Catalogue the phantom of doom, [[Doomshade]] {{waypoint -423, 190, 409}} (Epic x3)
  - Doomshade spawns after the death of the two other epics
