## Steps

1. Examine a Mysterious Symbol then, Talk to [[Gamble Blitherton]] {{waypoint 128, -50, 1392}} southwest of [[Danak Shipyard]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]].
1. Recover 20 ancient Shissar artifacts from the [[Yha-Lei]] around the shores of Danak (guaranteed update per kill). {{waypoint 412, -45, 1262}}
1. Return to [[Gamble Blitherton]].
1. Talk to [[Drarson McCale (Butcherblock Mountains)|Drarson McCale]] {{waypoint 658, 24, 621}} in [[Butcherblock Mountains]].
1. After talking to him, 3 mobs will spawn and ambush you. This will result in you being knocked out and ported to [[Drarson McCale]]'s hideout in [[Halls of the Unseen]].
1. You will awaken on the floor of the [[Halls of the Unseen]]. Talk to [[Drarson McCale (Halls of the Unseen)|Drarson McCale]] in the [[Halls of the Unseen]].
1. Join The Circle of the Unseen Hand
   - If you decide not to join the Circle, you will be knocked out again and then ported back to Butcherblock Mountains. You must talk to Drarson McCale again to continue the quest
   - Click on the barrel along the west wall at {{waypoint 60, 1, -4}} to leave the zone, but get [[Nyran Dudez]]'s {{waypoint 25, 3, 47}} quest first.
