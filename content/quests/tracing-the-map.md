## Steps

1. Examine [[A Brethren map]] in your inventory, received after inspecting the Makeshift Grave (see quest starter above).
1. Visit the following locations:
   - The top of the *Temple of Cul* (next to the harp): {{waypoint -477, 23, 372}}
   - The entrance to the aqueduct: {{waypoint -354, 11, 353}}
   - Further into the aqueduct: {{waypoint -344, 14, 291}}
   - Drop down into [[Priestess' Court]], take a right and approach Varin N'Mar: {{waypoint -347, -12, 188}}
1. Speak to [[Varin N'Mar]].
1. Speak with [[Lect'Zadh]] in Priestess' Court and ask about 'Malkonis D'Morte' and 'the Ancille of D'Morte'.

After speaking to Varin you must inspect the Lect'Un Monolith, then speak to Lect'Zadh to close the quest. He is spawned by placing the following runes in the Lect'Un Monolith at {{waypoint -184.00, -4.00, 165.00}}

1. [[The rune of lect]]
1. [[The rune of zoda]]
1. [[The rune of ahk]]
1. [[The rune of duak]]
1. [[The rune of hek]]

   - If Lect'Zadh happens to despawn while talking to him, wait about 5 minutes, he will respawn with book over his head to finish questioning.
