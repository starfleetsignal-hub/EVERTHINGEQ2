## Prerequisites

- You must be at least level 20 to receive this quest.

## Steps

*I've agreed to help Hwal Rucksif gather raw materials for his smithing. It seems that the sentries in Antonica have enlisted his assistance in re-outfitting themselves, but he just doesn't have the materials available to him to get the job done.*

1. Harvest 15 [[Iron cluster|iron clusters]] and 15 [[Severed maple|severed maple]].
   - Both harvesting nodes can be found in [[Antonica]], the same zone as the quest giver.
     - "Callus Ore" and "Wind Felled Trees" can both be found around {{waypoint -570, -30, 350}}
   - Must be harvested by the quester.
     - If accidentally deleted, the player may replace them at the broker and still get credit.
   - If player is on the quest, [[A Gathering Obsession, Part II]], this step will not update until the player has harvested the required items for *A Gathering Obsession, Part II*.
1. Return to [[Hwal Rucksif]] with the harvested items in your inventory.
1. Venture to the following three locations and collect the required items: *(updates are group updates, not objects)*
   - [[Blackburrow]]: a piece of *tyranium ore* from [[A Sabertooth miner]] {{waypoint 32, 0, -42}} or [[A Sabertooth excavator]] {{waypoint 32, 2, -28}}.
   - [[Stormhold]]: a piece of wood from [[A caveroot horror]] {{waypoint 3, -26, -122}}. *(see monster page for more info)*
   - [[Thundering Steppes]]: the hide of [[Bloodtalon]] at around {{waypoint 1027, 8, 415}}. *(see monster page for more info)*
1. Return to Hwal Rucksif.
1. Return to [[Blackburrow]] and obtain beer from the [[The Blackburrow Brewery]] at {{waypoint 21, -40, 61}} on Level III.
   - While there are three entrances to [[Blackburrow]] from [[Antonica]], the closest entrance to the Brewery is the "secret" entrance at {{waypoint -1111, -33, 1061}}. Getting to this entrance requires a short underwater swim at a small cave {{waypoint -1023, -41, 1177}}. Once inside Blackburrow, follow the tunnel and just as it opens up you'll see some brown kegs stacked three-high. Select the top keg for the update. {{waypoint 19, -38, 59}}.
1. Return to Hwal Rucksif.
1. Travel to [[The Bells of Vhalen]] in [[Antonica]] at {{waypoint -1300, 39, 47}} to release Hwal's grandfather from the family curse. Click the manhole cover on the floor of the tower to enter a one-room instance called [[Garanel's Resting Place]] whose sole occupant is [[The ghost of Garanel Rucksif]].
1. Return to Hwal Rucksif.
1. Obtain two items from the [[The Thundering Steppes]]:
   - Leather from [[A mature antelope]], found by the Antonica Station Griffen Tower around {{waypoint 191, 2, -831}} or around 74 -2 1,245, {{waypoint 184, 1, 1330}} or {{waypoint 406, 12, -509}}.
     - Most of the monsters at these locations place-hold for each other, so if a mature antelope isn't up, then keep clearing creatures in these areas and one will eventually spawn.  Good place is where all the griffons are and the giant scouts.
     - If you find a group of 3-4 mature antelope with young antelope, you'd be best killing the mature and running off the rest. This ensures the mature antelope respawn, and quicker than the standard respawn timer.
   - A steel bar from [[A Firerock scout]], found around {{waypoint 895, -1, 531}}, {{waypoint 300, -1, 679}} or {{waypoint 913, -0.99, 541}}.
1. Take the supplies to [[Minty Frostbeard]] in [[The Village of Thundermist]] in The Thundering Steppes at {{waypoint 597, 2, -145}}.
1. Return to Minty Frostbeard one Norrathian day later (*72 minutes real-time*) to retrieve the boots.
   - Even after a Norrathian day expires, the player may need to rezone or relog to get Minty to update her quest status.
1. Return to [[Hwal Rucksif]], who will take the new boots and give you his old boots as a reward.
