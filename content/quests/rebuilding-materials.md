## Description

The Quel'ule scholar Azira has requested assistance in obtaining more raw materials for their rebuilding efforts.

## Steps

1. You have been asked to obtain more raw materials to aid in the rebuilding efforts.
   - Harvest 25 of any resource ***except*** bushes and fish nodes (food harvests).
1. Return to [[Researcher Azira]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,500 faction with **Researchers of Quel'ule**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Quel'ule Daily Supply Tasks
