## Steps

1. Meet with [[Blint Tizzlespring]] in [[The Baubbleshire: Vale's Shadow]]
1. Collect the 3 Clockwork Study Diagrams.  These look like scrolls with white sparkles rising from them.  You'll have to wander out among aggressive monsters to get all 3.
   - One at {{waypoint 870, -18, -485}} inside a gear
   - One at {{waypoint 896, -17, -486}} inside the inn building
   - One at {{waypoint 900, -17, -480}} also inside the inn building
1. Return to Blint Tizzlespring
1. Repair the broken clockwork (just leftclick on it)
1. Talk to Blint again
1. Return to Tilla in North Qeynos
1. Go to [[The Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos (POI)|South Qeynos]] and speak with [[Apprentice Librarian Cippin]].  Pick the option that zones you into a small intance, [[Syndra's Rampage]]
1. Kill [[Syndra Del'zeem]] (Even con, no AAs, Necromancer spells)
1. Return to Cippin
1. Find the secret entrance to Starcrest Commune
   - Head to {{waypoint 703, -19, 233}}.  *Once you are there*, the quest will update and a sewer grate will appear under your feet
1. Zone into [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum]]
1. Destroy the study materials and research of the necromancers
   - Destroy 4 [[A portal guardian|portal guardians]]
   - Destroy 4 Necromantic Research - they look like piles of sparkling books and can be found at
     - {{waypoint 739, -20, 265}}
     - {{waypoint 752, -24, 320}}
     - {{waypoint 759, -24, 320}}
     - {{waypoint 812, -20, 301}}
     - {{waypoint 823, -20, 283}}
1. Return to Cippin.  You can leave Starcrest either through the main gate or through the sewer grate you entered through, it doesn't matter.
1. [[General Golias Sydwen]] has asked to speak to you! Head to his apartment on Erollisi Lane {{waypoint 532, -10.5, 142}} and click the door to enter [[The Office of General Golias Sydwen]]
1. Watch the "exchange". It's spoiler-intensive - see talk page for details.
1. Return to Tilla
