This quest is part 7 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

You must catalog several creatures in Nektulos:

- [[A Nerius fiddler|a Nerius fiddler]] - all over the beach. {{waypoint 856, 0, -278}}
- [[A forest leaper|a forest leaper]] - north part of beach. {{waypoint 936, 2, -345}}
- [[A dusk dart]] - off shore near docks. {{waypoint 784.60, -3.30, 39.47}}
- [[A shoreline barracuda]] - off shore near docks. {{waypoint 951.91, -3.30, -109.75}}
- [[A shoreline shark]] - off shore in deeper water. {{waypoint 954.15, -3.30, -156.23}} or {{waypoint 764, -17, 4}}
- [[A shadowed man (Nektulos Forest)|a shadowed man]] - usually found near D'Morte Burial Chamber entrance {{waypoint 665, 0, -609}}; also found far north of [[N'Mar's Ascent]]. {{waypoint -140, 89, -2246}}
- [[Immense tree snake|an immense tree snake]] - found near D'Morte Burial Chamber entrance. {{waypoint 636.00, -4.00, -530.00}}
- [[A firelight shriller|a firelight shriller]] - found by the rumbleroots on the hilltops east of the Commonlands entrance. {{waypoint -663.21, -96.30, 163}}
- [[An ash owlbear]] - found by the rumbleroots in the hills east of the Commonlands entrance. {{waypoint -568, 38, 121}}
- [[A Dragoon sentry]] - near [[The Funeral Pyre]]. {{waypoint -961, 13, -759}}

The quest completes automatically when you catalog the last monster.

## Rewards

- At least 13s 91c
