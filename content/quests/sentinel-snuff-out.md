## Description

The League of Freethinkers has had great difficulty reconnoitering Mistmyr Manor due to great number of sentinels that stalk the many rooms and pathways. I've been charged to slay several of these sentinels so the Freethinker scouts may move about the Manor without being detected.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Mistmyr Manor]] and slay 10 animated black knights
1. Return to [[Tanya Alvernon]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
