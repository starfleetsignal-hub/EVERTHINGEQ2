## Required Materials

*In addition to the materials harvested for the Requisition, you will need:*

- 1 [[Lustrous Rockhopper Hide]] -- *rare*
- 25 [[Coruscating Filament]]

## Steps

1. Harvest Materials:
   - 18 [[Hardened Zelniak Pelt]]
   - 15 [[Splitfin Dartfish]]
   - 45 [[Luclizite Cluster]]
1. Craft [[Havenborne Ceremonial Robes]] on a [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]]
1. Return to [[Gundeg "The Anvil" Midst (The Bazaar)|Gundeg "The Anvil" Midst]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g 7,700 status
- [[Research Mission Reward Crate]]
- 8 [[Recuso Field Voucher]]
