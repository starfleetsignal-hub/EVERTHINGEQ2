This quest is part of the World Event: [[A Goddess Risen]].

## Steps

1. Search for Messira in the Commonlands
   - Speak to [[Merchant William]] at {{waypoint -529, -43, -310}}
1. Search for Messira in the Village of Shin
   - Speak to [[Merchant Kaleb Farbring]] at {{waypoint -69, -3, 119}}
1. Search for Messira in Antonica
   - Speak to [[Duffy]] at {{waypoint 147, -17, 228}}
1. Search for Messira in Gorowyn
   - Speak to [[Messira Goodbolt]] at {{waypoint 2439, 18, 1263}}
   - [[A highly competitive merchant]] will spawn and aggro you.  Defeat it.
   - Speak to Messira again.
1. Return to Aaronolis Swornlove
1. Speak with Annalisa Swornlove at {{waypoint 8, 149, -64}}, slightly NW of her brother.
1. Find the dark oracle's cave "in Ryjesium's ruined reflection on Kunark"... Clicking on the Movable Rubble at {{waypoint 916, 49, -184}} in [[Kylong Plains]] and this will zone you into the [[Faceless Oracle's Demesne]].
   - Speak with [[Tiressiaz]] at {{waypoint 12, 0, 15}}, who will ask you to Collect components for his spell(To speed things up run past all monsters without killing, they don't hit hard)
     - Obtain 4 bonerot fungus from the bones (by Killing [[Risen spurbone|risen spurbones]] or [[Boneghoul]]) in the Faceless Oracle's Demesne.
     - Extract 6 thylene fumes from the poisons (by Killing [[Foul fume|foul fumes]] or [[Rancid miasma|rancid miasmas]]) in the Faceless Oracle's Demesne.
     - The mobs will respawn after a few minutes.
   - Return to Tiressiaz (only click once, let her talk for a while)
1. Return to Annalisa Swornlove

## Rewards

At least 12g 84s 31c
