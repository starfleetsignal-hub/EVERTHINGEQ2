[[Porto Bunglefoot]], the Combine's solitary chef, needs me to help him properly treat some mammoth meat.

## Steps

1. I need to smoke and salt the mammoth meat.
   - [[A Barrel of Mammoth Meat]] and [[High Quality Salt]] is placed in your bags together with a [[How To: Cure Mammoth Meat]] recipe which is autoscribed.
   - I should treat the mammoth meat at a stove.
     1. Using a [[Stove & Keg]], craft 5 [[A Barrel of Smoked Mammoth Meat|Barrels of Smoked Mammoth Meat]], using the following fuel:
        - 5 [[Effulgent kindling]]
1. Return this treated meat to [[Porto Bunglefoot]].
