This is a [[Mikk Eishammer#Related Quests|Forgemasters of Thurgadin Supply Task]]

## Steps

1. Harvest 4 [[Brontotherium Meat]] from [[Den Nodes|Frosty dens]]
   - [[Den Nodes|Frosty dens]] are in [[Great Divide]], typically in [[Snowfang Isle]] and [[Fina's Retreat]] (on the Iceclad map - Griffin flight node "Icy Fingers"). They can also be found in [[Eastern Wastes]] near the [[A sootpaw direbear|sootpaw direbear]]s. [[Den Nodes|Frosty dens]] are also found around the Ring War remnants area in lesser numbers.<br>*Note: If you're a member, use fast travel to Sinking Sands, then talk to the Othmir on the docks to get to Fina's Retreat, this is WAY faster than the griffons*
   - Brontotherium Meat purchased from broker or found in inventory/bank does not update this quest!
1. Return to [[Mikk Eishammer]]

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +1,500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
