## Steps

1. Banish the Kly lookouts (30)

2. Stop the Kly abductors from reaching the crypt with their goblin captives (10)

3. Escort the Bloody Tooth goblins back to Twark (10)

4. Kill the Kly Overseer
He spawns anywhere in the blue area even up on top of the crypt.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 5 x [[Encrusted metal key]]
