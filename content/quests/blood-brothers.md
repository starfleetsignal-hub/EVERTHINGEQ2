## Notes

- You must understand the [[Orcish]] language

## Steps

1. Place messages around Ry'Gorr Keep by clicking the objects with blue sparkles in the following locations:
   - Near Rogla Dainkiller at {{waypoint -648, -343, 2723}}
   - Near Chief Ry'Gorr at {{waypoint -765, -341, 2739}}
   - Near Grimlik Spirittalker at {{waypoint -662, -341, 2799}}
   - Near Swog Bloodytusk at {{waypoint -726, -344, 2802}}
1. Return to Chief Thrael'Gorr to complete the quest. Speak to [[Korgak Rotstomper]] for the next quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,500 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
