## Required Materials

*In addition to the materials harvested for the Requisition, you will need:*

- 1 [[Radiant Pome]] -- *rare*
- 25 [[Coruscating Candle]]

## Steps

1. Harvest Materials:
   - 15 [[Radiant Algae]]
   - 18 [[Lunar Mushroom]]
   - 45 [[Humming Azurite]]
1. Craft [[Deep Magmatic Ink]] using a [[Chemistry Table]]
1. Return to [[Puhrihni "The Edge" Fordel III]]

## Rewards

- [[Research Mission Reward Crate]]
- 8 [[Recuso Field Voucher]]
- At least 1p 25g 7,700 status
