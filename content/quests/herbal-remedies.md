## Steps

![A small herb bulb, as found throughout the Badlands.](images/Small_herb_bulb.jpg)

1. Gather 5 dried herbs from within [[The Petrified Badlands]].
   - These are "small herb bulbs" strewn about the area, and there are more spawn locations than those listed here. They are considered harvestables which can be tracked.
   - {{waypoint 1493, -3, 4192}}
   - {{waypoint 1517, -5, 4219}}
   - {{waypoint 1644, -10, 3872}}
   - {{waypoint 1688, -15, 3833}}
   - {{waypoint 1696, -9, 4101}}
   - {{waypoint 1711, -8, 4000}}
   - {{waypoint 1780, -8, 3986}}
   - {{waypoint 1817, -9, 4039}}
   - {{waypoint 1858, -15, 3902}}
1. Return the dried herbs to Ishaq in [[The Eye of Dartain]] at {{waypoint 1832, 6, 4365}}

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
