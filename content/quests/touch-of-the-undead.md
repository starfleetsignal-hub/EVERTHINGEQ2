## Steps

1. Explore Ghorkaal for possible vampiric arcanum - go to [[Charnel Grove]] in northeast Ghorkaal for an update {{waypoint 727, 49, 851}}
1. With your Ghorkaal Teir'Dal Illusion active, speak to [[Ritualist D'Rhivirr]]
1. With your Ghorkaal Teir'Dal Illusion active, speak to [[Tola R'Vorr]] {{waypoint 727, 49, 815}}
1. Look underneath [[Stillwater Bridge]] {{waypoint 778, 16, 1106}}
1. Try to defeat the [[A Teir'Dal attacker|Teir'Dal attackers]] - there are three of them, two 102vv and one 102v.  When you're just about to kill the last one, you'll be teleported back to Wanderer's Dock in "spirit form".
1. Return to underneath Stillwater Bridge and click on your body to retrieve it.
1. Return to [[Waulon Highpebble (Wanderer's Dock)|Waulon Highpebble]] on Wanderer's Dock to complete quest (must cancel Ghorkaal Teir'Dal Illusion before he'll speak to you)
