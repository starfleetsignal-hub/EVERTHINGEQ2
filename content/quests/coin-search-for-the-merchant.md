## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- You must have +10,000 faction with the Court of the Coin to unlock this quest. Once +20,000 faction has been achieved, this quest will no longer be available.
- As of 11 August 2019, this quest was only offered once, at +10,000.

## Steps

1. Talk to [[Scout Irfam]] at the end of the road through [[Sandslide Passage]]. {{waypoint -885, -132, -301}}
1. Travel to [[The Eye of Anuk]] and search for Scout Akuf and clues. Location update at {{waypoint -648, -141, -1182}}
1. Inspect a skeleton at {{waypoint -750, -223, -1253}} and a zombie will spawn lvl 48 solo mob. Kill him to retrieve the documents.
   - This is not a group update, approx. 1-2 min respawn
1. Return to Thara for your reward

## Rewards

- 8g 56s
- -7500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -7500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- 7500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
