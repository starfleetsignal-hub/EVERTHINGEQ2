## Steps

1. Kyau gives you a sack of coins. Take it to near [[Camp Nybright]] {{waypoint 19, -26, 237}} where a greedy bandit spawns. Speak with him to make attackable, then kill him
1. Return to [[Master Kyau]]

## Rewards

- At least 9g 13s 1c
- +100 faction with **Disciples of Wu**
- [[Journal of Wu, Seeker of Enlightenment]]
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Band of Infernal Tidings]]
  - [[Crest of Infernal Tidings]]
  - [[Emblem of Infernal Tidings]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Loop of Infernal Tidings]] (All Scouts)
