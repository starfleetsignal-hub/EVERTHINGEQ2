## Notes

*A new tradeskill mission is available every 18 hours.*

## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 15 [[Celestial Candle]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest the following within [[Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands]]:
   - 25 [[Silvered fleck]] from [[Flecked mass]]
   - 10 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Congealed reef]]
   - 10 [[Dewpalm lumber]] from [[Fallen dewpalm branch]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Contained Disease Vial]]
1. Craft the [[Contained Disease Vial]] on a [[Chemistry Table]].
   - There is a [[Chemistry Table]] in the [[Crafters' Gallery]] in the Great Library at {{waypoint 932.06, 411.68, 31.40}}
1. Speak to [[Essherus]] at {{waypoint 296.00, -34.91, 187.23}} in the [[Myrist Librarian Quarters]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 66g 32s 48000 status
- [[Elemental Mission Reward Crate]]
