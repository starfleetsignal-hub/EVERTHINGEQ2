## Notes

- Must be a [[Scholar]] ([[Sage]], [[Alchemist]] or [[Jeweler]]) to get this quest. Two recipes needed by this quest are auto scribed when accepting quest.<br>
- Materials needed:
  - 1 Porphyry Stone (rare from stones)
  - 13 Ilmenite Ore
  - 2 Chalcedony
  - 15 Zimaran Coal

## Steps

1. Create the following items
   - Create [[Glittering Tiara]]
     - uses 1 [[Porphyry Stone]] (rare from stones), 5 [[Ilmenite Ore]] and 5 [[Zimaran Coal]] (Work Bench)
   - Create 2 x [[Sparkly Bracelet]]
     - each uses 1 [[Chalcedony]], 4 [[Ilmenite Ore]] and 5 [[Zimaran Coal]] (Work Bench)
1. Speak to [[Nazeera]] at {{waypoint 145, 9, -422}} in the [[Western Wastes]]
   - *Note: If you cannot see her when you return, try issuing the command **/target Nazeera** and then "H" hail her to get completion of this quest.*

## Reward

- 3 [[Mesmeric Shell Powder]]
- [[Status Coin (75,000)|Status Coin [75,000]]]
- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
