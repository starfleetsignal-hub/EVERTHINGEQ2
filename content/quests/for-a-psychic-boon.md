## Steps

![Bloomstalker](images/Bloomstalker.png)
![Ripe Puffball](images/Ripe_Puffball.png)

1. Hunt bloomstalkers for 12 nettles
   - Bloomstalkers are the large flowers without name that come alive as you approach them, Carved Caps area above Void Obelisk area. {{waypoint -1060, 29, 1108}}
   - upon  killing the 12th bloomstalker you receive [[Bloomstalker Nettles]] - a clicky item with 12 charges.
1. Gather 8 x nettle-stunned puffballs  {{waypoint -1057, 27, 877}}
   - target Ripe puffball (trackable as a mob) in the same area and use your [[Bloomstalker Nettles]] on them, then pick them up while they are stunned.
1. Return to [[Oruri]]

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following:
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
  - [[Doomview Sword of Incursions]]
  - [[Coral Forest Halberd of Incursions]]
