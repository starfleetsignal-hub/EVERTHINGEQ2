## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- You must have +20,000 faction with the Court of the Blades to unlock this quest. Once +50,000 faction has been achieved, however, this quest will no longer be available.

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Pillars of Flame]] and kill:
   - 5 harpies, found all throughout northern part of zone.
   - 3 harpy nests {{waypoint 1480, -118, -1086}}
   - [[A Windsister nest guardian]] - spawns when a nest is destroyed
1. Return to Dirar Muti.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 23s 29c
- +500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
