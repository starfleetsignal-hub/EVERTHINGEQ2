## Steps

1. Craft 2 [[Basic Doorbot|Basic Doorbots]] at a [[Work Bench]]
   - There is a workbench near [[Dora Wicket]]. Each doorbot requires scintillating coal which can be purchased from Dora or [[Merchant Thryst]] nearby.
   - You will need a total of 66 [[Scintillating Coal|scintillating coal]] to complete all of the quests (each doorbot requires 6 coal).
1. Release the [[Basic Doorbot]] on top of the climbing wall near Dora to scan [[The Nest of the Great Egg]]: use the bot at {{waypoint -591, -19, -472}}
   - This location is at the top of the second climbable wall on Whisperwind Isle, **not** inside Nest of the Great Egg.
1. Personally examine the door to the [[Palace of the Awakened]]: take the cloud to The Isle of Awakening then the transport disk to travel to the door at {{waypoint 128, 150, 120}} for update.
   - Note: You are personally viewing the door, do NOT use the doorbot for this step.
1. Release the [[Basic Doorbot]] near the [[Isle of Awakening]] cloud to scan the Vaults of El'Arad: use the bot around at {{waypoint 58, 169, 308}}
1. Return to [[Dora Wicket|Dora]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 9g 7s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
