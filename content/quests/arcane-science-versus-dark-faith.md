## Prerequisite

You must be level 40+ to receive this quest (you can accept the quest after turning in [[Flirting with Death]] while level 20+)

The quests [[Flirting with Death]] and [[Turning Steel Into Gold]] must be completed to begin this quest.

## Steps

- Speak to [[Adrianna (Freeport)|Adrianna]] in [[Temple of War]] {{waypoint -90, -9, -163}}
- Speak to [[Micha Longtail]] in [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] {{waypoint 22, -7, -119}}
  - The quest auto-completes when you speak to the second NPC

## Rewards

- At least 8g 61s 8c
- [[A Small Ornate Freeport Bookcase]]
