## Notes

This Public Quest starts automatically one hour after previous one was finished.

The goal is to defeat [[Cornhusker]], to prevent it from consuming all of Norrath's corn and plunging the world into famine.

**!!! MERC ALERT !!!** *If you plan on participating in this fight, you will need to DISMISS your Merc, or swap to a Merc that does NOT cure ailments on your or your group.  See section on Harvest Moon for details*

## The Battle

### Phase 1

![undead farmhands have been working tirelessly to raise Cornhusker!](images/An_undead_farmhand.png)
The battle starts with [[An undead farmhand|undead farmhands]], who begin tilling the soil to spawn new corn plants and also summon Cornhusker. Defeat all 35 of the farmhands to start the next phase.  Farmhands create Tilled Soil that eventually spawns Young Cornstalks - a 'grow countdown' will begin in-place of each defeated farmhand - these growth patches will need to be managed, ideally, by all participating players, not just the lowbies.

Farmhand defeated -> Tilled Soil -> Young Cornstalk -> Mature Cornstalk

![Players should frequently disturb this soil to prevent the cornstalks from taking root!](images/Tilled_soil.png)
![cut these fresh stalks down periodically to prevent reaching maturity](images/Young_cornstalk.png)
![Too late!  The boss is stronger now.  Our only hope now is the Harvest Moon!](images/Mature_cornstalk.png)
Tilled Soil and Cornstalks left unattended during Phase 1 will progressively grow (counting down from 30 seconds each stage) into the final mature versions.  Starting in Phase 1, to hinder the stalks from maturing, players will need to 'chop them down' periodically while still in the freshly grown phase (do this by clicking on them during the fight) this will slow down their progress by adding 10 seconds to the maturity countdown.  This countdown continues into Phase 2, and stalks can be prevented from reach maturity through an entire fight if players are persistent enough.

Young Stalks that reach 0 timer become 'Mature' and are now UNKILLABLE (until 'Harvest Moon' debuff in Phase 2 allows it) - these mature stalks will both buff the boss and periodically spawn killable [[A corn popper|corn poppers]] that shoot popcorn and do damage directly to nearby players.... players can 'dodge' these slow moving popcorn kernels, but several mechanics during the fight require (or force) players to stand still for several seconds, making them vulnerable to these attacks.
<br><Sub>In practice, since most players aren't aware of the mechanics of the fight, 100% of stalks will reach maturity during Phase 1, or at the very beginning of Phase 2, but hey, it's nice to dream, right?</sub>

The next phase spawns Cornhusker, who has been summoned, but is still weak.  Corn stalks have begun to grow as a result of the previous work of the undead farmhands.  This is the opportunity to strike.

### Phase 2

![... these nasty buggers spawn when unattended cornstalks reach full maturity, take them out!](images/A_corn_popper.png)
Cornhusker is a typical Tank & Spank boss - with two twists

- an additional 100% damage reduction mechanic prevents its immediate destruction by high level characters.
- Boss will occasionally be 'mezzed' during the encounter, resetting its threat list, causing to attack random players until control is regained by the tank.  You will see the related text in chat when this mechanic happens.
- **ALL PLAYERS - Tanks, Healers, DPS, Support - need to stand IN FRONT OF THE BOSS within his frontal cone for the majority of the fight** until all (or at least a sufficient number) of mature cornstalks have been cut down... this is due to 'Harvest Moon' debuff and how it is acquired by players.
  - The aformentioned scripted 'mezzes' are the other main reason why everyone needs to be in front of the boss... when a mezz happens, then the boss will change direction suddenly to face his new prime target - if everyone is already in front of him, then his frontal cone will be pointed correctly at everyone, regardless of who's being targetted.

*Throughout phase 2, multiple untargetable [[A maize scarecrow|scarecrows]] spawn throughout the field and occasionally cast AOE stuns and fears on nearby participants (either a stun or an actual fear... both can be removed with 'control restoring' effects), these effects also damage nearby players.*

#### Harvest Moon

**~ ! ~ THIS IS THE PRIMARY MECHANIC OF THE FIGHT ~ ! ~**<br>
To reduce (or remove entirely) the boss's 100% damage reduction, players will need to destroy the 'Mature Cornstalks' from Phase 1. If enough players are chopping stalks starting in Phase 1, this can keep the boss from getting too powerful in Phase 2 by preventing stalks from reaching the 'mature' phase. (This also prevents additional corn popper adds, as they can only be spawned by mature stalks)
![You have 20 seconds to chop down as many MATURE cornstalks as possible... get choppin'](images/Harvest_Moon.png)![If your chat is too cluttered, look for this 'arcane' debuff under your status -OR- look for this Harvest Moon icon in your list of detriments!](images/Harvest_Moon_FindMe.png)
Eventually, a 'Harvest Moon' debuff is cast by the boss on participants within his frontal cone - if you have the buff, a message will appear in your chat
"With the Harvest Moon, you are able to chop down the mature cornstalks"
letting you know that you can now fully destroy 'Mature Cornstalks'. Doing so will make the boss more vulnerable to damage as more and more stalks are destroyed (this also prevents the destroyed stalks from spawning fresh corn poppers).

- Some things to note about the Harvest Moon debuff:
  - **Harvest Moon is a DEBUFF that is 'shouted' by the boss.   You can see (and hear) it as a big red frontal cone emanating, periodically from the Boss.  It is critical that at least some players in the event acquire this debuff regularly until enough (or all) Mature Cornstalks are chopped down.**
  - **!!! MERC ALERT !!!**
  - *Be aware that the Harvest Moon 'buff' is technically a debuff (since it originates from the boss) and as such can be accidentally 'cured' by your Mercs (or you) if you leave them up during the fight.  If you plan on getting 'maximum participation' rewards, it might be best to leave the merc suspended, as it can cure you before you have a chance to take out any of the mature cornstalks.*
  - Harvest Moon only stays on a player for a limited number of ticks before they will have to re-acquire it.  This means players will only be able to chop down 4-5 (possibly 6?) stalks before it expires.  To make matters worse, if you get hit with a scarecrow fear during that time, you will likely lose your opportunity window, as the scarecrow fear effect lasts almost as long as Harvest Moon itself... so have your 'restore control' spells/abilites/cooldowns ready if you get hit by a Scarecrow AOE while under the influence of Harvest Moon..
  - <s>Ideally, you would look for cornstalks that have NOT yet matured and chop those ones down FIRST, that way the maturity countdown timers being maintained by nearby players do not have to be worried about anymore, freeing those players up for other tasks (like chopping down more stalks!)</s>  Currently the Harvest Moon debuff only allows Mature cornstalks to be cut down - not stalks in other stages of development.
  - You do NOT need to kill the corn poppers to chop down its related stalk (but killing them is still a nice thing to do if you have the DPS and have a moment to spare as they can be quite a pain to everyone else)

As soon as Cornhusker is defeated, the event ends and everything related despawns... except the 'participation' chest that spawn randomly nearby.

### Participation credit

Participation is calculated PER PLAYER and is a combination of damage, kills, and number of stalks/soil cut down/destroyed during the fight.

![Non-Max level characters CLICK HERE!](images/Ancient_Cornstalk.png)

#### Non-max level players

[Currently lvl 130 at time of writing]

*As with [[Headless or Tails|the Headless Horseman PQ]], there is a 'Noob Buff' that will allow non-max level characters to have boosted damage numbers to match the current max-level characters. To obtain this buff, just click on the 'Ancient Cornstalk' {{waypoint 349.2, -1.10, -683.6}} that spawns at the beginning of the fight near a large rock near the NW edge of the south crater.  While non-max level characters are not technically 'required' to click this to participate, doing so does help those players immensely, as non-max level characters can more easily click/destroy corn stalks in the 2nd phase by having buff as well as doing damage to the boss and adds if necessary... but mostly surviving long enough to click the stalks
<br>**  BE WARNED - The noob buff does NOT persist through death.  If you are defeated during the fight and have to return (or get resurrected), you WILL Need to click on the Ancient Cornstalk again to regain the buff to participate more effectively*

### tl;dr

- Kill all 35 farmhands to spawn the boss
- The boss is 100% immune to damage, to reduce this %, players will need to destroy Mature Cornstalks while under the influence of the bosses' AOE 'Harvest Moon' debuff.
- Once enough Mature Cornstalks are cut down, the boss starts to take damage.  The more stalks that are cut down, the faster the boss dies.
