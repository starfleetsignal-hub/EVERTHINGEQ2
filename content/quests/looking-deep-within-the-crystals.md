Baenek Frostaxe has warned me that the [[Crystal Caverns: Collapse|Crystal Caverns]] are beginning to collapse! He has asked that I take some samples of the crystals growing within the caverns and compare them for myself.

## Steps

1. Find an animated crystal. To receive this, kill [[The Life Leech]] at {{waypoint -12, -9, 19}}
1. Obtain a sample of a cold blue crystal {{waypoint -145, 0, -104}}
1. Obtain a sample of a deep purple crystal {{waypoint 58, -19, -79}}
1. Obtain a sample of a lively green crystal {{waypoint 73, -12, 54}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 80g
- +350 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
- [[Primal Velium Shard]]
