## Required Materials

- 50 [[Golden ember]]
- 50 [[Celestial Coal]]

- 50 dark abhorrent ore from dark abhorrent ore nodes in zone
- 50 twisting worms from soiled mound nodes in zone
- 50 fiend stool from fiend stool nodes in zone
- 50 blackwood from blackwood nodes in zone
- 50 charred bone marrow from a resting pain bringer in zone
- 50 red shard of malice from shards of malice nodes in zone

## Steps

1. Enter [[Shard of Hate: Eye Spy (Tradeskill)|Shard of Hate: Eye Spy [Tradeskill]]] at {{waypoint -762, 347, 1044}} in the [[Plane of Magic]].
   - Reset zone if you just completed the timeline before entering otherwise the [[A fiendish attendant]] wont show up.
1. Create the following on The Fiend Fount at {{waypoint 8, 12, 5}}:
   - Ire-Bound Flame
     - Requires:
       - 50 dark abhorrent ore from dark abhorrent ore nodes in zone.
         - Pick up the golem body at {{waypoint 182, 7, 110}} and place it at {{waypoint -21, 12, -71}}. Run to the far side of that platform. The hungry hate mongrel hound on the teleport pad will run to the body.
         - Take the now cleared teleport pad at {{waypoint 21, 7, -51}} to the upper ledge to harvest the ore.
         - To get down, go toward the ledge, there appears to be an invisible stair. Watch for the floating mob, then jump down toward the teleport pad.
       - 50 twisting worms from soiled mound nodes in zone.
   - Resident Attunement Lens
     - Requires:
       - 50 fiend stool from fiend stool nodes in zone.
         - The stool is dropped by an imp that patrols behind the sleeping dragon near {{waypoint -12, 27, -139}}
         - *Note: Dismount and crouch when walking near the dragon to avoid waking it.*
       - 50 blackwood from blackwood nodes in zone.
   - Sensory Delver
     - Requires:
       - 50 charred bone marrow from a resting pain bringer in zone.
         - Found in the Rectory of Confession near {{waypoint 176, 33, -33}}
       - 50 red shard of malice from shards of malice nodes in zone
         - *Note: Only harvest when they're red. If harvested while blue, 5 already collected red shards will be destroyed.*
1. Craft the Arcane Ocularum on The Malicious Mixer at {{waypoint -17, 14, 10}}.
   - Requires:
     - 1 Sensory Delver
     - 1 Ire-Bound Frame
     - 1 Resident Attunement Lens
     - 50 [[Golden ember]]
     - 50 [[Celestial Coal]]
1. Use Arcane Acularum on [[A fiendish attendant]] near {{waypoint -199, 18, 9}}
   - You will be ported into the fiend's body. Listen to the Mistresses of Hate.
