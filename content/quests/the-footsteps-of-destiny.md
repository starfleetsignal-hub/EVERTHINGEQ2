## Notes

- Speak with [[Stissa, Emissary of the Speaker]] {{waypoint -1504.21, -214.28, -386.14}} at the [[Port of Tears]] in [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]] to travel to the [[Great Divide]].
- Heroic Characters will start with [[Shades of Drinal: Trial of Everafter]] instead.

## Steps

1. Travel to Fina's Retreat.
1. Find evidence of the assassin's whereabouts {{waypoint -1483, -522, -2375}}
1. Inspect the othmir's corpses for clues of the assassin's actions
   1. Defeat the three [[A vengeful othmir spirit|vengeful othmir spirits]]
1. Continue to follow the assassin's trail {{waypoint -1469, -537, -2194}}
1. Find the assassin and eliminate him {{waypoint -1610, -506, -2266}}
   1. Defeat [[Keldrahgo the Stealthed]]
1. Return to Lady Liae Croae (Good) or Lord Valkiss Ssi'sh (Evil)

## Rewards

- 1p 30g
