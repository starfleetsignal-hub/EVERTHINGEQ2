## Pre-requisites

You need to be level 85 and have completed [[A Rodent Problem]] questline from [[The City of Paineel|Paineel]].

## Steps

1. Kill A Roekillik Courier (83^ or 84^). The courier cannot be tracked and can spawn at any of the following locations in the roekillik caves:
   - {{waypoint 883, -286, 3230}}
   - {{waypoint 1286, -222, 3252}}
   - {{waypoint 940, -197, 3380}}
   - {{waypoint 1088, -191, 3444}}
   - {{waypoint 1082, -186, 3270}}
   - {{waypoint 1089, -195.97, 3449}}
1. Return to Ritsy.

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 57c
