## Notes

- fly over the City of Mist (doesn't matter where really) and land somewhere near the entrance, if you don't see [[Kerss Rekssok (Mist)|Kerss Rekssok]] run to the edge of the plateau.
- Sharing error message: *[char name] is not eligible to receive the Quest "Eye in the Mist" (requires a designer to write a custom failure string and requires completing the Quest "[[Threads for the Weave]]")*

## Steps

1. Enter the [[Lost City of Torsis: The Spectral Market (Solo)]]
1. Find clues in the zone
   1. Hail the ghost close to the zone in
   1. Speak [[A handmaiden's spirit]] at {{waypoint -69.73, 6.86, -83.36}}
   1. Pick up the "glinting object" *purple shiny* behind where the spirit was
   1. Find more clues
      1. Seek another object - a "Chardok gem pouch" *small pouch* at {{waypoint 42.46, 6.17, -9.22}}
      1. Seek yet another object -  Kill [[Merchant Caniz]], click his body for a key, and then click the "Torsis merchant chest" at {{waypoint 78.20, 6.62, -52.10}}
1. Head North out to The Fountain of Mists around {{waypoint 15.96, 3.36, -189.04}}, your journal will auto update stating that you have found everything there is to offer.
1. Return to [[Kerss Rekssok (Mist)|Kerss Rekssok]]
