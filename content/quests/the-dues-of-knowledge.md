## Steps

1. Click on three tomes (Tome of Shattered Reveries, Tome of Emphatic Enchantment, Tome of Petulant Prophecies).  These may move around.
   - The first tome (Tome of Shattered Reveries) was found in the library section, near {{waypoint -152, -14, -383}}.
   - The second (Tome of Emphatic Enchantment) is downstairs, at {{waypoint -138, -59, -292}}.
   - The third (Tome of Petulant Prophecies) is also downstairs, near {{waypoint -169, -59, -324}}.
1. Return to [[Haadiya]].

## Rewards

- At least 90g 70s 61c
