## Notes

- You will need 5 [[Carbonite cluster|carbonite clusters]], 5 [[Gold cluster|gold clusters]], and 100 [[Basic Coal]] to complete the subquests. Buy them off a broker or harvest them from [[The Thundering Steppes]] (*stonecrest ore* - carbonite, *wind-swept stones* - gold).

## Steps

1. Find and talk to [[Lord Bowsprite]], who is on the bottom level of the instance. {{waypoint 234, -16, -250}}
1. Return to [[The Gigglegibber King]].
1. Speak with [[Snoseniffer the Schemer]]. {{waypoint 201, 6, -341}}
1. Speak with the following NPCs an complete their subquests:
   - [[Gigglegibber Mayor]] from [[A Gigglegibber ballot counter]] {{waypoint 207, 1, -333}}
   - [[Gigglegibber Melodies]] from [[Imadeus Gigglegibber]] {{waypoint 172, 1, -332}}
   - [[Making Coins]] from [[A Gigglegibber blacksmith]] {{waypoint 229, -16, -230}}
   - [[Subterranean Underwater Vessel]] from [[A Gnomish inventor]] {{waypoint 208, 1, -316}}
1. Unlock the four vaults at the bottom level of the instance {{waypoint 236, -16, -240}}. The answers to the lock combinations are (from left to right):
   - 10
   - 60
   - 0
   - 15
1. Release and speak to [[Lord Bowsprite]].
1. Return to [[The Gigglegibber King]].
1. Return to [[Pinchy Presentpeeler]] in [[The City of Freeport]] or [[Qeynos Province District]].
