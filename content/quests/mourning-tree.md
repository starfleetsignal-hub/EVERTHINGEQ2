## Steps

1. Seek the Tree of Growth in the Wakening Lands. {{waypoint 675, -438, 4161}}
1. Find the source of the mysterious voice  - Speak with Rumbleslate at {{waypoint 428, -433, 3619}}
   - cave entrance is at {{waypoint 697, -455, 3737}}
1. Read the [[Stone of Sorrow (Mourning Tree)|Stone of Sorrow]] you got from [[Rumbleslate]]
1. Find the remains of 6 holgresh. (White sparklies on the ground...must get fairly close) **NOTE:  Not a group update.** (6 per member)
   - Some known locations (more listed on the talk page):
     - {{waypoint 541.47, -472.84, 4091.54}}
     - {{waypoint 487, -474, 4246}}
     - {{waypoint 496.25, -475.91, 4168.85}}
     - {{waypoint 490.69, -484.83, 4052.89}}
     - {{waypoint 559.41, -491.47, 4015.55}}
     - {{waypoint 593, -490, 3907}}
     - {{waypoint 615.36, -470.99, 3847.03}}
     - {{waypoint 744.18, -455.03, 4017.79}}
     - {{waypoint 770.72, -451.91, 4088.43}}
     - {{waypoint 791, -449, 4143}}
1. Read the [[Stone of Sorrow (Mourning Tree)|Stone of Sorrow]] again.
1. Find 6 roots of the Tree of Growth, located around the [[Tree of Growth]] {{waypoint 674, -438, 4161}} **NOTE: Not a group update.** (6 per member)
   - Known locations include (but are not limited to):
     - {{waypoint 626, -464, 4085}}  ![Roots of the Tree of Growth](images/Mourning_Tree_quest_-_roots_of_growth.jpg)
     - {{waypoint 663, -453, 4093}}
     - {{waypoint 661, -442.56, 4127}}
     - {{waypoint 749.28, -454, 4203.52}}
     - {{waypoint 723, -456, 4238}}
     - {{waypoint 700.5, -448, 4216.5}}
     - {{waypoint 553.89, -470.95, 4189.25}}
     - {{waypoint 561.33, -469.16, 4117}}
     - {{waypoint 532, -469.25, 4179.5}}
1. Read the Stone of Sorrow one more time.
1. Return to [[Bilvee Hummingwing]]
