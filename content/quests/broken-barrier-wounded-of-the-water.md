## Steps

1. Find a [[Nameless druid (Fens of Nathsar)|nameless druid]] in the water south of [[Omen's Call]] in [[Fens of Nathsar]] near the Yha-lei {{waypoint 443, -125, 501}}
1. Use the provided hydromancer power siphon on 10 Yha-lei hydromancers when they are at 30% health or lower in order to collect the water essences. You may need to mentor or use lower-level abilities to avoid one-shotting the hydromancers.
1. Return to the [[Nameless druid (Fens of Nathsar)|nameless druid]].
1. Return to [[Veryn P'Neru]].

## Note

This quest can be done at the same time as:

- [[Broken Barrier: Wounded of the Air]] in Kylong Plains.
- [[Broken Barrier: Wounded of the Earth]] in Kunzar Jungle.
- [[Broken Barrier: Wounded of the Fire]] in Jarsath Wastes.
