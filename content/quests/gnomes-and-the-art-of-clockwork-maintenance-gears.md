## Steps

1. Kill 7 [[A drudge mucus|drudge mucus]] - throughout the halls of the first level.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 [[A Murkwater onlooker|Murkwater onlooker]]s - stairways from first to second floor {{waypoint 146, 31, -101}} and {{waypoint 111, 33, 86}}.

1. Kill 8 Murkwater onlookers.
1. Read the book.
