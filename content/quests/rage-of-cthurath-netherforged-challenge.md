## Notes

This quest will require you to be **level 10 crafter**, the recipe for cimmerott runic ward makes 2 - 5 runes and cannot be commisioned. Bring at least 100 x Formless Coal to craft.

## Steps

1. Borrow Tiadra's netherforge crucible in [[Yon Gorroth]] at {{waypoint 497, 82, -645}}
   1. Use [[Oogothl Sprawl Obelisk]] at {{waypoint 134, 65, -514}} to go to [[Yon Gorroth]]
   1. *Use Kith to teleport to the city*
   1. the "netherforge crucible" is in the same tent, next to Garam. Click it to take it with you.
1. Return to [[Tomian Keeneye (Oogothl Sprawl)|Tomian Keeneye]] in the [[Gardens of Industry]] in [[Oogothl Sprawl]]
   1. *You can buy the needed 100 [[Formless Coal]] from [[Splanos]] at {{waypoint 496.25, 82.71, -639.07}}*
   1. *Use Kith to teleport to the Obelisk*
   1. Use [[Yon Gorroth Obelisk]] at {{waypoint -643, -1, 448}} to go back to [[Oogothl Sprawl]]
   1. Return to Tomian at {{waypoint 231, 80, -213}}
      - He won't be there.
1. Search the area for a clue at {{waypoint 207, 79, -188}} and click on the eyeblight to revive it.
1. Follow the eyeblight to {{waypoint -294, 87, -165}}
1. Search for Tomian in the [[Eerie Ridges]] and find him at {{waypoint -331, 98, -219}}
1. Go to {{waypoint -322, 94, -254}} and kill the oculus taskdriver.
1. Rescue Tomian by speaking with him.
1. Speak to him again and he gives you recipe [[Cimmerott Runic Ward (Recipe Book)|Cimmerott Runic Ward]] and [[Cimmerott Runic Ward Components Bundle]]
1. Place the netherforge crucible at {{waypoint -669, 187, -366}}
1. Scribe the recipe and unpack the bundle of materials, then craft 10 x [[Cimmerott Runic Ward (Item)|Cimmerott Runic Ward]]. Each "full" combine will make 5 wards.
1. Catch up to [[Tomian Keeneye (Oogothl Sprawl)|Tomian Keeneye]] at the [[Oogothl Sprawl Obelisk|obelisk]] at {{waypoint 131.0, 65.96, -515.02}}
1. Return to "Lady Najena" in [[Yon Gorroth]]. {{waypoint 561, 94, -682}}
   1. *Note: you can use Kith to teleport to the city*
