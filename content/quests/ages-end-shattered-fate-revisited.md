## Notes

- This quest has the same quest story as [[Age's End: Shattered Fate]].

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Jorlak]] with in [[Skyshrine Tower: Age's End]]
1. Speak with the Overlord and Queen Antonia
   - *Note: They will come closer to Jorlak*
   - *Note: it will be a raid wide update if you hailed them before, if not go hail em*
1. Speak with [[Jorlak]]
   - *Note: as soon as you hail him the ring event will start*
1. Destroy the 3 shield generating crystals in the east, north, and west pavilions
1. Return to [[Jorlak]]
   - *Note: Do not hail Jorlak again, quest will auto update and encounter will progress*
1. Kill [[Roehn Theer (Age's End)|Roehn Theer]]
   1. Pick up Friona Vie's Livegiver Staff, this will open a new hotbar for the player that picks it up
      - *Note:it can be used by any class, keep in mind the class that uses the abilities at the right times. Suggest: Healer or Dirge*
   1. When [[Roehn Theer (Age's End)|Roehn Theer]] reaches ~10% this battle is won (if you hit him too hard and go past 10%, the trigger seems to not go off, so be careful here)
      - *Note: If you wipe during this fight, revive and approach Roehn Theer to re-start the encounter*
1. Pick up the [[Swords of Destiny]]
   - *Note: The char that got the Livegiver Staff can't hold the Swords of Destiny at the same time. If you soloed it, camp to yourself to pick up the swords* (I was able to right click on the staff buff and cancel it to pick up the swords)
1. Kill [[General Velryyn]]
1. Speak to [[Jorlak]]
1. Return the [[Swords of Destiny]] to Antonia and the Overlord
   - Queen Antonia gets back the [[Qeynos Claymore]]
   - Overlord Lucan gets back the [[Soulfire]]
1. Speak with Firiona Vie
