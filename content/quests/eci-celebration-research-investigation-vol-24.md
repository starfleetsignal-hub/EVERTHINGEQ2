## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of E'Ci has asked me to assist in gathering icebound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the icebound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Icebound Energy Reserves|icebound energy]] to fill the container. Collect [[Icebound Energy Reserves|icebound energy]] by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to [[Herald of the Wintry Guardian]] {{waypoint 75, -35, -1687}} in [[Everfrost]]
