## Steps

*Lieutenant Dawson gives you an [[Arcanic beacon|arcanic beacon]].*

1. Inspect the machine in the center of Two Logs Pond. {{waypoint 677, -34, 498}}
1. Slay [[A gnoll machinist]] who spawns and attacks you.
1. Inspect the machine again.
1. Return to Lieutenant Dawson for your reward.

- At least 92c
- +125 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
