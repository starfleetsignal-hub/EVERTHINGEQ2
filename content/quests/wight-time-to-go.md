I gave Oarwille Wight, a gnome tinkerer and friend of the Green Hoods, a clear path out of the Breakneck Acre barn, but I am not sure how long that path will be clear.

## Steps

1. Clear out all the orcs between the barn and the ruined tower in [[Breakneck Acre]].
1. Return to the barn, where [[Oarwille Wight]] is now waiting for you on the ground floor. Speaking with Oarwille gives you the quest.
1. Follow Oarwille to the ruined tower.
1. Catch up to Oarwille along the path to the [[Grove of Stones (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Grove of Stones]] at {{waypoint 354, -17, 68}}.
   - Speak to Oarwille.
   - Kill orcs in the area until he feels safe again.
1. Speak to Oarwille again.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- +500 faction with **The Green Hoods**
- [[Reconstructed Standard Potency Warforged Infuser]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Barrage of War]]
  - [[Bolts of Strife]]
  - [[Massacre, Wand of War]]
