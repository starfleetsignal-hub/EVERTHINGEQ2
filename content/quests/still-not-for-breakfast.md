## Note

This repeatable quest and the quest [[Steal It All Back]] are used to gain the +5,000 [[House Vahla (Faction)]] required for the next quests.

## Steps

Obtain these spell components in [[Drukyna Marsh]]:

- 8 [[Wakingdew root ball|wakingdew root balls]] {{waypoint -634.77, -47.07, -2.20}} by killing the ones that spawn from killing the carnivorous [[Brackish wakingdew|brackish wakingdews]] on the western land part of [[Drukyna Marsh]].![Cnidcara Egg Clutch (on rock)](images/Cnidcara_Egg_Clutch.jpg)![Cnidcara Egg Clutch (in water)](images/Cnidcara_Egg_Clutch%28w%29.jpg)
- Gather 6 ***Cnidcara Egg Clutches*** in the eastern water part of [[Drukyna Marsh]].
  - *Note: The [[Cnidcara nester|cnidcara nesters]] will attack to defend the eggs if they are nearby. The eggs can be found near or on rocks for example in these locations:*
  - {{waypoint -777.42, -55.17, 228.43}}
  - {{waypoint -859.82, -55.17, 99.15}}
  - {{waypoint -843.71, -56.09, 65.48}}
  - {{waypoint -678.09, -55.17, 140.45}}
  - {{waypoint -795.69, -55.17, 229.12}}
  - {{waypoint -827.99, -56.58, 78.55}}
  - {{waypoint -881.18, -55.17, 74.05}}
  - {{waypoint -686.67, -57.68, 165.97}}
  - {{waypoint -641.28, -55.46, 178.09}}
  - {{waypoint -630.54, -55.06, 154.46}}

## Rewards

- At least 90g 72s
- +5000 faction with **House Vahla**
- -5000 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -5000 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
