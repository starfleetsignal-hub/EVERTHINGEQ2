*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Go to the orcish lowlands around {{waypoint -1084, -48, 594}}
1. Do the following:
   - Kill 7 [[A Bloodskull warrior|Bloodskull warriors]].
   - Kill 3 [[A Bloodskull priest|Bloodskull priests]].
   - Collect 5 samples of Bloodskull Frenzy Root, a harvestable item that looks much like other root in the zone, but smaller. They are located in the area with the Bloodskull orcs.
1. Return to [[Rainus Canton|Rainus]].

## Rewards

- At least 5s 11c
- +60 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
