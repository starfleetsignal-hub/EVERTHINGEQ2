## Steps

*Fenlys Nimbledigit, a gnome desperate to help support her family, was very impressed by my accomplishments and exploration of the Darkpaw Warrens. I volunteered to help her by gathering some rare and sought after goods from within the dangerous caves.*

1. Gather 6 runic velium crystals in Snowfang Burrow in Darkpaw Warrens
   - Snowfang Burrow:  {{waypoint 17, -3, -136}}
   - Crystals are a group update
1. Hunt for 10 zygomyd mycelium in Terra Fungi
   - Semi-frequent update from most zygomyds in Terra Fungi {{waypoint -22, -77, -255}}
1. Return to Fenlys Nimbledigit in Darkpaw Dugout {{waypoint -20, -80, -264}}

## Rewards

- At least 3p 36g 79s 77c
- [[Serilian Shaped Scale Helm]]
- [[Snowfang Lightfoot Plushie]]
