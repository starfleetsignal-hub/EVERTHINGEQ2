## Notes

- In [[EverQuest]], Tundra Jack and his pet polar bear Iceberg roamed the tundra section of Everfrost Peaks, both saving and killstealing young barbarian adventurers. [[The Ghost of Tundra Jack]] and [[Iceberg]] still roam [[Guardian's Gulch]].

## Steps

1. Search for the lost twin mining picks. They are in the box near the southernmost Frosthorn minotaur camp in the [[Jagged Plains]]. {{waypoint 88, 26, -473}}
1. Kill any Frosthorn minotaurs for the two keys.
1. Open the Frosthorn treasure chest that you inspected in Step 1.
1. Bury the twin minings picks at the gravestone of Tundra Jack.
