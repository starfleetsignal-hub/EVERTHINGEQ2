## Steps

![Eye of Aeristok](images/Eye_of_Aeristok.png)

1. Click on the [[The Fountain Room of Aeristok|Statue of Aeristok]]. {{waypoint -1, 0, -2}}
1. Find 6 eyes of Aeristok. These are jewels that spawn on the ground and emit a very obvious glow. Each jewel has a random spawn location in a general area, and only one of each eye spawns at a time. Respawn is approximately 5 minutes.
   - **Vibrant**: Hallway leading to the [[Nest of the West Wing]] {{waypoint -0.29, -0.13, -84.45}}
   - **Dynamic**: [[Nest of the West Wing]] {{waypoint 55, -0.13, -111}}
   - **Colorful**: [[Nest of the West Wing]] {{waypoint 53, -0.14, -155}}
   - **Prismatic**: Hallway leading to the [[Nest of the East Wing]] {{waypoint -0.30, -0.13, 80}}
   - **Vivid**: [[Nest of the East Wing]] {{waypoint 90, -0.13, 111}}
   - **Sparkling**: [[Nest of the East Wing]] {{waypoint 54, -0.13, 109}}
1. Find 6 more eyes by slaying [[A flying guillotine|flying guillotines]] or [[A palace sentinel|palace sentinels]] (common update).
1. Return to the Statue of Aeristok and inspect the base. {{waypoint -1, 0, -2}}
1. Withing 15-minutes, slay [[The Visage of Aeristok (Monster)|the Visage of Aeristok]].
