1. slay/gather the following
   - Slay 10 of the Doomwing Legion for [[Vegnit Snogwit]].
   - Obtain the Doomwing Legion Orders (Clickable page at {{waypoint 134, 70, 641}}
   - Investigate (kill) the Quellithulian (Xalazalag) located at one of the Doomwing Legion dig sites.{{waypoint -163.28, 70.79, 753.23}}
1. Return to [[Vegnit Snogwit|Vegnit]] at the [[Drednever Crash Site]].
