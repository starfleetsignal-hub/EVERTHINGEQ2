## Steps

1. Travel to [[Butcherblock Mountains]] and speak with [[Danak Dhorentath]], Prophet of Fear. He is on the path to [[Unrest]] {{waypoint -303, 184, 755}}.
1. ![An Essence of Fear](images/An_Essence_of_Fear.png)In [[Shard of Fear]] {{waypoint -1834.66, -27.85, 455.24}} in [[The Feerrott]]. Speak to [[An Essence of Fear]] at the very back of the dungeon in front of the fire wall around the temple {{waypoint -8, -2, -300}}. If you can not enter you may need to Mentor to Level 70 - 75.  Note: Click on the building/structure to enter, not the glowing part.
1. Kill [[A mass of iksar spirits]] (80^^^) in [[Turmoil Boneyard]] {{waypoint -5, 20, 260}} (grey kill counts)
   - The spawn is triggered by the conversation. If the mass is not up, speak to the Essence of Fear again
1. Speak to the Essence of Fear again
1. Speak to [[Xuurk (Lost Temple of Cabilis)|Xuurk]] in [[Lost Temple of Cabilis]] {{waypoint 51, -10, 223}}
   - ![Xuurk](images/Xuurk.jpg)Go to the Ruins in [[Fens of Nathsar]]; the temple is an underground door at {{waypoint -93, 31, -1047}}, near the Cabilis sokokar post.
