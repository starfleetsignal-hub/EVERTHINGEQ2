## Steps

1. Gather 12 shells along the edge of the nearby river. Sometimes they are clipped partially under the terrain. They appear on the shore and in the river near the shore. Some known locations are:
   - {{waypoint 322.29, -33.89, 1256.73}}
   - {{waypoint 934, -38, -60}}
   - {{waypoint 826, -38, 451}}
   - {{waypoint 438, -44, 1217}}
1. Return to [[Herd Master Rudek]].

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **Clan Skleross**
- At least 9g 46s 1c
- [[Jade-inlaid Gauntlets]]
