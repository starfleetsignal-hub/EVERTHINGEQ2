Once a week, the healers of Maldura need to restock their supplies of healing salve-infused bandages. The supplies can be gathered just outside the city, and the bandages should be returned to the healers' supply containers.

## Steps

![cave fungus](images/Cave_fungus.jpg)

1. Travel to [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]] and gather the following:
   - Harvest 5 [[Fibrous root|fibrous roots]] near the great drill, near {{waypoint 429.02, 85.66, 400.88}}.
   - Harvest 5 **healing fungi** from **cave fungus** found in the [[Splitiron Mine]] {{waypoint 307.85, 127.87, 238.51}}.
     - *Note: These items can be harvested ahead of time and will auto update the quest.*
1. Craft 5 fresh salve-infused bandages.
   - There is a Sewing Table in the healer's area {{waypoint -132.35, -3.59, -89.15}}
1. Deliver the bandages to healer [[Dalryl]] {{waypoint -131.43, -3.19, -81.89}} in the [[Metal Mercantile District]].

## Needed Materials

- Thaumic filament  (25)

- [[Coin of the Grand Artificer]]
- [[Ancient Arcannium Coin]]
