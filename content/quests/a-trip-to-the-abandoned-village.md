## Steps

1. Go to [[The Abandoned Village (Kunzar Jungle)|The Abandoned Village]] and complete the following tasks: {{waypoint -236, 3, -30}}
   - Kill 8 [[A jungle quatcha|jungle quatchas]] or [[An elder jungle quatcha|elder jungle quatchas]]
   - Gather 5 heirlooms (chests), at the following locations:
     - {{waypoint -230, 3, -30}}
     - {{waypoint -207, 3, -59}}
     - {{waypoint -176, 11, -130}}
     - {{waypoint -201, 10, -119}}
     - {{waypoint -209, 11, -130}}
1. Return to Refugee Yurix.

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- +5,000 faction with **Outer Sebilis Residents**
- [[Outer Sebilis Watchman's Belt]]
