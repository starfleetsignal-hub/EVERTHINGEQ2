## Steps

1. Speak with [[Cricks]] in [[North Freeport]] {{waypoint -91, -24, -59}}.
1. Speak with [[Ulmar Mul'veem]] in [[North Freeport]] in the Jade Tiger Inn {{waypoint -72, -21, -77}}.
1. Click the sleeping body next to Ulmar then choose to fall asleep to enter the dream state zone, [[A Collection of Dreams]].
1. Speak with [[Thumore D'armer]], who is directly west from where you zone in.
