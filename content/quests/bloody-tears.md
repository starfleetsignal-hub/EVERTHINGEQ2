## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[A Tunarian thrall|tunarian thralls]] and 5 [[A dwarven thrall|dwarven thralls]]. {{waypoint -89, -32, 73}}
1. Kill 6 [[A Tunarian guest|tunarian guests]] and 6 [[A Crushbone guest|orc guests]]. {{waypoint -137, -32, 84}} {{waypoint -127, -31, 70}}
1. Kill 5 [[A Mistmoore maid|Mistmoore maids]]. {{waypoint -98, -28, -66}}
1. Kill 6 [[A loyal convert|loyal converts]]. {{waypoint -176, -26, -5}}

## Rewards

- [[Chelmonte Thorn Whip]]

## Trivia

- This quest is a massive tribute to Konami's famous vampire-hunter, *Castlevania* series, *Castlevania II: Simon's Quest*. The name of the NPC offering it, is in reference to Simon Belmont. The quest's name "Bloody Tears" is also the title of the song first introduced in *Castlevania II*. Even the quest reward, referencing the Thorn Whip is a direct reference to *Castlevania II*.
