## Steps

1. Find the first symbol in [[The Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen|Upper Guk]] - Head to Font of the Everliving (same room as the named with the constant stream of frogloks coming from the pool).
1. Examine the first symbol (Disfigured Symbol of Valor) at {{waypoint -61, -43, -264}} (Top Row).
1. Examine the second symbol (Disfigured Symbol of Honor) at {{waypoint -72, -52, -244}} (Bottom Row).
1. Examine the third symbol (Disfigured Symbol of Chivalry) at {{waypoint -80, -47, -252}} (Middle Row, Right Side).
1. Examine the fourth symbol (Disfigured Symbol of Justice) at {{waypoint -64, -47, -252}} (Middle Row. Left Side).
1. Return to Muktop Quickpage in Firmroot Moot so that he may analyze the effects of this aura.

## Rewards

- At least 15g 83s 87c
