Enter [[Echo Caverns: Fungal Foray (Solo)]] to find the rest of the missing Defenders of Haven and the demolitionist, [[Zofia Cobbleshod]].

## Note

1. Do NOT kill the fourth Name ( [[Sporefiend Zugu]] ) until you have progressed this quest to the "find the final boom barrel" step.
1. You don't have to complete the zone (ie, kill the final Name) to complete the quest.
1. Can also be done in the Heroic version.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Echo Caverns: Fungal Foray (Solo)|Echo Caverns: Fungal Foray [Solo]]] and kill the first **three **bosses
   - *see the [[Echo Caverns: Fungal Foray (Solo)|Echo Caverns: Fungal Foray [Solo]]] for walk-through of the zone.*
1. Click on the portal (behind and above where the third Name was standing) indicating "destination unknown" to be ported to Fungus Grove.
1. Find the injured defender, Selig Balderdash, at {{waypoint -455, 173, -125}}
1. Head towards [[Fungus Grove]] to catch up with [[Zofia Cobbleshod]] {{waypoint 156, 86, -311}}.
   - *Note: If you have trouble finding the [[Fungus Grove]], you may want to go back to the start of the zone (Evac, Totem of Escape, Gnomish Safety Recaller, etc.) then once you are into [[The Shattered Chasm]], stay to the left until you see mushrooms.*
1. Speak with [[Zofia Cobbleshod]] at {{waypoint 156, 86, -311}} ![displays location of Zofia as located at end of Burynai Lamp path.](images/%28distant%29Zofia_Cobbleshod.jpg)
1. Recover 5 stolen **Boom Barrels** in [[Fungus Grove]]. Some possible locations:
   - {{waypoint 192, 75, -500}}
   - {{waypoint 254, 64, -272}}
   - {{waypoint 274, 65, -243}}
   - {{waypoint 316, 64, -259}}
   - {{waypoint 323, 36, -380}}
   - {{waypoint 373, 36, -293}}
   - {{waypoint 402, 36, -315}}
   - {{waypoint 459, 41, -272}}
   - {{waypoint 494, 87, -271}}
   - {{waypoint 496, 58, -518}}
   - {{waypoint 523, 87, -241}}
   - {{waypoint 558, 86, -348}}
1. The 6th Boom Barrel is a drop from the fourth Name. ***Make sure you have collected all 5 barrels in the previous step or you will have to repeat the instance***:
   1. Travel to [[Sods and Bends]] {{waypoint 470, -0.80, -471}}.
   1. Continue to {{waypoint 301, 13, -472}}, slaying [[A voracious fungus fiend|voracious fungus fiends]] along the way until you get the message, *"A massive fungus fiend awakens from its slumber in search of food!"*
   1. Kill [[Sporefiend Zugu]] for the last barrel and quest update.
1. Return to [[Zofia Cobbleshod]] in the Fungus Grove at {{waypoint 156, 86, -311}}
   - Note - she's still in your Fungal Foray instance (ie, not the version of her that will appear in the City of Fordel Midst later).
1. Return to [[Captain Gleamheart]] in the [[Luclin Nexus]] {{waypoint 272, -36, 847}}.

## Rewards

- [[Bluetiful Lumi Fungi]]
- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
