## Description

The League of Freethinkers has asked me to sanctify the rooms within Evernight Abbey using a specially blessed thurible. It is hoped that using the thurible to cense the abbey will deliver several celestial shockwaves which are meant to dispel the dark enchantments placed on the Libant's domicile.

## Steps

1. Use the received [[Thurible of Sanctification]] to cense several more prominent rooms within [[Evernight Abbey]]
   - Cense the [[Scriptorium (Evernight Abbey)|Scriptorium]] {{waypoint -132, -7, -119}}
   - Cense the [[Stores and Accoutrements Alcove]] {{waypoint 133, -7, -115}}
   - Cense the [[Foyer of the Seer]] {{waypoint 69.5, -4.5, -251.5}}
   - Cense the [[Thaumaturge Foundry]] {{waypoint -131.5, -4, -251.5}}
   - Cense the [[Somnolence Antechamber]] {{waypoint 5.5, -10, -203}}
   - Cense the [[Cloister of Liturgy]] {{waypoint -1, -12.5, -305}}
     - NOTE: Before you enter, make sure you are at 100% health and then click The Libant Blood Stone at {{waypoint -6, -13, -294}}.
1. Return to [[Tanya Alvernon]] to claim your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
