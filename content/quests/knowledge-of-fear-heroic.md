## Steps

*The Arcanna'se Spire is filled with tomes of knowledge pertaining to the arcane, but it is the knowledge of fear, and of Cazic Thule, that most interests the Crusaders of Greenmist, so they have given me this task, to explore the spire and see if it holds anything related to their god, and to slay anything that would try to stop me.*

- Defeat targets within [[Arcanna'se Spire: Repository of Secrets (Heroic)]]
1. Eliminate the alchemist
1. Oust the outrider
1. Banish the botanist
1. Defeat the warden
1. Vanquish the vanguard
