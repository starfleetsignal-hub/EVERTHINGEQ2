## Notes

- This quest in not repeatable.
- This is a subquest of [[Unforgettable]].

## Steps

1. Shoot 10 residents of New Halas with conjured arrows using the [[Bow of the Hart]]. Any NPC in [[New Halas]] will work.
1. Return to [[Priestess Annalisa Swornlove (Frostfang Sea)|Priestess Annalisa Swornlove]]

## Rewards

- [[Erollisi's Arrow]]
- 2 [[Erollisi coin|Erollisi coins]]
