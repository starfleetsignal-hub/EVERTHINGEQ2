## Prerequisites

The following quests must be completed:

- [[Growth in an Arid Land]]
- [[Wings in Danger]]

## Steps

1. Collect the Cae'Dal Sword from rack behind her at {{waypoint 676, 253, 1410}}
1. Kill 12 Kromdul {{waypoint 170, -225, -114}}, or Chalp Giants {{waypoint 190, -130, 150}}, you MUST equip the [[Battered Cae'Dal Soldier Blade]]
   - *Note: individual update only.  Group can get the update all on the same mobs as long as everyone hits it while equipping the blade.*
1. Return to Captain Tazthas

## Rewards

- 11,576 status
- [[Obulus Hireling's Shoulderguards]]
