## Notes

- This quest is only available to citizens of [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]]. Citizens of any other city, including any of Evil-alignment, will only be offered the first three quests by Brandus.
- The quest [[Proving Your Worth (Commonlands)|Proving Your Worth]] requires killing many of the same mobs. It is strongly recommended that you do both quests at the same time. You will have to get both quest lines to the same point first.

## Steps

1. Slay the following dervishes, all are level 17-18.
   - 3 [[A Dervish cutthroat|Dervish cutthroats]] - found widely in the southwest part of Commonlands, including the road
   - 3 [[A Dervish zealot|Dervish zealots]] - found commonly along the road that goes past the druid ring
   - 3 [[A Dervish thug|Dervish thugs]]
   - 2 [[A Dervish assassin|Dervish assassins]]
   - 2 [[A Dervish ruffian|Dervish ruffians]]
   - 2 [[A Dervish heretic|Dervish heretics]]
   - 2 [[A Dervish outlander|Dervish outlanders]]
   - 2 [[A Dervish thaumaturge|Dervish thaumaturges]]
1. Return to Brandus for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 10s 18c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Worn Militia Armguards]]
  - [[Worn Militia Sleeves]]
  - [[Worn Militia Vambraces]]
