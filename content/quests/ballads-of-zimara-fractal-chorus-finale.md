## Steps

1. From the [[Auric Palisades]], use the portal at {{waypoint -711, 229, -755}} to go to the [[Aether Wroughtlands]].
   - **Note:** You must use the portal... using any other means to reach [[Aether Wroughtlands]] will not update the quest.
1. Make your way to the outpost {{waypoint 715, -44, -447}}.
1. Speak to the 3 NPC's to finish the quest.
   - [[Oolfonso]] {{waypoint 715, -43, -462}}
   - [[Tehes]] {{waypoint 701, -43, -459}}
     - Gives the optional quest [[Clearing the Campsite]]
   - [[Riccaww]] {{waypoint -30.37, 87.18, 482.80}}
