*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Wailing Caves]].
1. Collect 2 samples of bile from [[A primordial seep|primordial seeps]] near the entrance at {{waypoint -28, -5, -55}}
1. Kill [[Soulspike (named)|Soulspike]] for a sample of its venom.
   - Kill the spiders around the spawn at {{waypoint 53, -13, -20}} if it isn't up.
1. Return to Vibia Valens.

## Rewards

- At least 4s 32c
- [[Soulspike's Bite]]
