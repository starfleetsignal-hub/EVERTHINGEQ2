## Steps

There are two Level 32 [[A Thexian guard (Nektropos Castle)|Thexian guards]] in the Jail.  Try to avoid killing them until after [[A jailed skeleton]], requests the killing of the Thexian guards.  Otherwise, they do not re-spawn and you will have to wait for the zone to reset.

1. Travel to the [[Nektropos Castle Jail|jail]] at {{waypoint -23, 0, 23}} and speak to [[A Jailed Skeleton]], who you will discover is named Gascot.
1. Slay 4 [[A Thexian guard (Nektropos Castle)|Thexian guards]]. There are 5 [[A Thexian guard (Nektropos Castle)|Thexian guards]] in the Barracks at {{waypoint 28, 0, -25}}.
1. Speak to Gascot again.
1. Examine a breadbox. {{waypoint 37, 0, -54}}
