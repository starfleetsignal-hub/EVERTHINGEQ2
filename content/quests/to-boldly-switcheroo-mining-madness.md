## Notes

This is one of the tradeskill dailies. Requires 25 Zimaran Coal. Quest completes when you finish crafting 5 Glarb's Mining Picks

## Steps

Glerb has requested Mining Picks

1. Collect 10 Blackhook Scrap Metal (Note: these are Harvest Trackable)
1. Craft 5 x Machete Bowl - bring 25 Zimaran Coal, you can use Goblin Workbench next to Glarb

## Rewards

At least 11p 16g 55s 16c

- [[Boldly Boxerroo!]] random reward from this crate
- [[Spiral of Vul Tradeskill Research Material (1 Day)]]
- [[Status Coin (75,000)]]
