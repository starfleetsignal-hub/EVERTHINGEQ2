This writ is available for any Craftsman (i.e. those who plan on specializing in Carpentry, Provisioning, or Woodworking).

## Steps

It requires you to create:

- 2  [[Orange Fizzlepop]]
- 1  [[Large Burlap Rug]]
- 2  [[Maple wand|Maple Wand]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

### Notes

Full shopping list per writ is:<br>
1x [[Murdunk orange]]<br>
3x [[Tuber Strand]]<br>
3x [[Severed maple]]<br>
1x [[Iron cluster]]<br>
1x [[Electrum cluster]]<br>
1x [[Tanned leather pelt]]<br>
1x [[Aerated mineral water]]<br>
2x [[Glowing Kindling]]<br>
2x [[Glowing Filament]]<br>
2x [[Glowing Sandpaper]]<br>

## Rewards

- At Least 3s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
