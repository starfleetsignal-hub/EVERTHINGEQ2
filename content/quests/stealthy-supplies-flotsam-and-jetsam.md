## Notes

1. The base camp in Western Wastes has basic amenities: Voyage Through Norrath mariner bell, Banker, Fuel Merchant, Varig's Velium Forge all in one crafting station,  Daily and Weekly adventure quest NPCs, Mender and other quest givers.
1. This quest requires:
   - 2  Ilmenite Ore (with Capital i)
   - 2 Chalcedony
   - 5 Zimaran Coal

## Steps

1. speak to him again when you are ready for journey.
   - you will get zoned to [[Western Wastes]] twice - *"something went wrong and the Lodizal totem didn't fully protect us from the protective spells!"*
1. You wake up on a rock in Western Wastes feeling something went wrong and have to swim to get to camp on the shore.
1. Head to the base camp and speak to [[Mathom Smeltfury]] at {{waypoint 77, 7, 665}}
1. Warm up at the fire
   - click the fire next to Mathom
1. speak to [[Mathom Smeltfury]] again.
1. Use the [[Arcane Tuning Fork]] on a nearby rock
   - click sparkling rock at {{waypoint 103, 9, 572}} and receive auto scribing recipe for [[W.H.A.T. V2]]
1. Craft [[W.H.A.T. V2]]
   - uses your old [[W.H.A.T. V1]] plus 2 [[Ilmenite Ore]], 2 [[Chalcedony]] and 5 [[Zimaran Coal]]
   - All-in-one "Varig's Velium Forge" is at {{waypoint 61, 10, 586}}
1. Salvage  6 ship debris
   - collect "Ship Salvage" right on beach outside camp around {{waypoint 123, 8, 589}} and on small island just off the shore.  ![](images/Stealthy_Supplies_Flotsam_and_Jetsam_quest_-_Ship_Salvage.jpg)
   - *There are aggressive ulthorks around this area, use and possess your [[W.H.A.T. V2]] if you can't kill them.*
   - *Note: you can cancel your "W.H.A.T." form in the detriments windows, you will return to your char. so no need to walk all the way back*
1. Speak with [[Mathom Smeltfury]]
   - He completes this quest and advises you to speak to Captain Raffik *(who shows up right next to you)*
