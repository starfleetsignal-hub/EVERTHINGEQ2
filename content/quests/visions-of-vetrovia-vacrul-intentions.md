## Notes

- Use the [[Ahroun Pack Disguise]] to get zone options to enter the zones.
  - *It appears that you cannot have Mystic Moppet on when you use the disguise.*
- Make sure you pick up [[Arqa Elharroun]]'s {{waypoint -378.26, 14.72, 54.97}} quests before you enter the solo instances
  1. [[So Jar So Good]] needs [[Castle Vacrul: Rosy Reverie (Solo)|Castle Vacrul: Rosy Reverie [Solo]]]
  1. [[No Guts, No Gory]] needs [[Castle Vacrul: Caverns of the Forsaken (Solo)|Castle Vacrul: Caverns of the Forsaken [Solo]]]

## Steps

1. Enter [[Castle Vacrul: Rosy Reverie (Solo)|Castle Vacrul: Rosy Reverie [Solo]]] {{waypoint 441.73, 110.24, 270.64}}.
   - *Note : you will want to pick up Arqa Elharroun's quest [[So Jar So Good]] beforehand.*
   - *Note: you can use the forlorn stable master to travel to the entrance {{waypoint -325.03, 14.00, 52.20}}.*
1. Explore the secrets inside.
   - A Libant vampire spawns and destroys your disguise at around {{waypoint 64.64, -0.76, -7.34}}.
1. Continue exploring Castle Vacrul.
1. Defeat all encounters in [[Castle Vacrul: Rosy Reverie (Solo)|Castle Vacrul: Rosy Reverie [Solo]]].
1. Talk to [[Tavian Faust]] in [[Forlorn Gist]] at {{waypoint -139, 19, 366}}.
1. Enter [[Castle Vacrul: Caverns of the Forsaken (Solo)|Castle Vacrul: Caverns of the Forsaken [Solo]]] at {{waypoint 441.73, 110.24, 270.64}}.
   - *Note: you will want to pick up Arqa Elharroun's quest [[No Guts, No Gory]] beforehand.*
   1. Gather **an allelopath blightsnail** to lift the *weedwhip strangulation* curse {{waypoint -265, -83, 317}} ![Allelopath Blightsnail](images/Blightsnail.png)
      - *Do not skip this step! If you forget to get the snail, Vishra will not appear and Marjeta will not talk to you after you kill Lysander.*
   1. Continue exploring the depths of [[Castle Vacrul: Caverns of the Forsaken (Solo)|Castle Vacrul: Caverns of the Forsaken [Solo]]]
      - *The quest updates when you find [[Vishra]] in The Craw by the Royal Bloods Halls/Cruor Keep exit {{waypoint 5, -58, 162}}.*
      - *He merely speaks briefly then dissappears, and has been known to depop before you actually see him. Check that your journal deems you to have "seen" him before continuing.*
   1. Continue exploring the mysteries of [[Castle Vacrul: Caverns of the Forsaken (Solo)|Castle Vacrul: Caverns of the Forsaken [Solo]]]
      - Use the **Leave the Craw** door at {{waypoint 4, -58, 145}} and progress to [[Cruor Keep]] {{waypoint -83, -45, 35}}.
   1. Slay [[Lysander Mistmoore]].
   1. Speak to [[Marjeta Vasilescu]] at {{waypoint -106.72, -45.56, -0.21}}. She spawns as soon as Lysander is dead.
1. Kill [[Vorigan Mistmoore]].
1. Return to [[Tavian Faust]] in [[Forlorn Gist]].
