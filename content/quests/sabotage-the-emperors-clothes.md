## Steps

1. Within 20 minutes, destroy 5 spools of cloth around Freeport.  Found in housing areas in North and South Freeport.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +2000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- **Note**: This quest does not grant any experience or achievement experience.
