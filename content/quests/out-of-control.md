## Background

The [[Arachnomechanicon]] has gone haywire and is running amok in Steamfont!  Oglethorpe and Emery have requested your help in dealing with the rampaging mecha-monstrosity.

## Steps

1. Kill [[Arachnomechanicon]], who will be pathing around the base of the hill where [[Oglethorpe]] and his cronies are.
   - If you do not follow the spider quick enough, he will despawn. You must wait for him to respawn, and go through the dialog again. If you kill it before accepting the quest it respawns as a non agro version in under a minute; talk with Oglethorpe again and go through the dialogue to respawn.
1. Return to Olgethorpe for the reward.

- At least 1g 66s 50c

  - [[Dabbledrifter Boots]] - [[Fury]], [[Warden]]
  - [[Jabberdine Boots]] - [[Defiler]], [[Mystic]]
  - [[Boggleputter Sabatons]] - [[Inquisitor]], [[Templar]]
