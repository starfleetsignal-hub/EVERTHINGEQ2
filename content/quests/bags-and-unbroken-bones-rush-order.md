## Steps

Create 1 items from 6 items in random list below:

- [[Boiled Leather Bracers]]
- [[Boiled Leather Shoulder Pads (Crafted) (Level 20)|Boiled Leather Shoulder Pads]]
- [[Canvas Cuffs (Level 20) (Crafted)|Canvas Cuffs]]
- [[Canvas Shawl (Level 20) (Crafted)|Canvas Shawl]]
- [[Boiled Leather Gloves (Crafted) (Level 20)|Boiled Leather Gloves]]
- [[Boiled Leather Pants (Level 20) (attuneable)|Boiled Leather Pants]]
- [[Canvas Mitts (Level 20) (Crafted)|Canvas Mitts]]
- [[Canvas Pantaloons (Level 20) (Crafted)|Canvas Pantaloons]]
- [[Boiled Leather Tunic (Crafted) (Level 20)|Boiled Leather Tunic]]
- [[Canvas Robe]]
- [[Pristine boiled leather backpack|Boiled Leather Backpack]]
- [[Pristine boiled leather quiver|Boiled Leather Quiver]]
- [[Pristine boiled leather sack|Boiled Leather Sack]]

Create 3 each from 2 items in random list below:

- [[Boiled Leather Bandolier (Crafted) (Level 20)|Boiled Leather Bandolier]]
- [[Boiled Leather Pouch (Crafted) (Level 20)|Boiled Leather Pouch]]
- [[Boiled Leather Satchel (Crafted) (Level 20)|Boiled Leather Satchel]]
- [[Boiled Leather Sheath (Crafted) (Level 20)|Boiled Leather Sheath]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 12s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
