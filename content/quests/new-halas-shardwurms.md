## Start quest

- Talk to New Halas Representative, [[Klaidar of Erollis]] {{waypoint 491, -214, 267}}. He is in [[The Temple of Brell]] in [[Thurgadin]].
- The quest ist unlimited repeatable.

## Steps

Kill 8 [[An ancient shardwurm]], [[A shardwurm]] and [[A young shardwurm]] in [[Daggerflow Glacier]] area {{waypoint 517.56, -305.28, -51.06}} in [[The Great Divide]].

## Rewards

- [[City Token]]
- 13168 status
- +500 faction with **The City of New Halas**
