## Steps

1. Riishu asked me to locate his wife, Lania Sulrel and help her return to him:
   - Find Riishu's wife [[Lania Sulrel]] {{waypoint -70, -8, 484}} ![Lania Sulrel](images/Lania_Sulrel.jpg)
     - Note:  After you find her she will run away from you.
   - Find Lania... she couldn't have run too far! {{waypoint -128, -0, 380}}.
     - When you find her she will be attacked by two grouped [[A Tsol Ew gladestalker|Tsol Ew gladestalkers]].  Kill them.
   - Speak to Lania again.
   - Return Lania to Riishu.
1. Speak to Riishu.
