## Steps

Nothing is free and building, maintaining, and supporting the herald's camp is expensive. That's why the herald of Tarew Marr has requested that we assist in offsetting the camp's cost by retrieving a valuable relic guarded by Hasira the Hawk.
I need to retrieve the relic.

1. Retrieve the relic guarded by Hasira the Hawk
   - Go to [[Aether Wroughtlands]] and zone into [[Aether Wroughtlands: Tarnished Oasis (Solo)|Aether Wroughtlands: Tarnished Oasis [Solo]]] {{waypoint -337.44, 43.88, -359.34}} and clean all named in this zone.  The chest with relic is near {{waypoint -777.89, 53.74, -369.66}}**Note: If you loot the chest while full, it will be bugged and say you have already looted it.**
   - *Note: [[Aether Wroughtlands: Tarnished Oasis (Heroic I)|Aether Wroughtlands: Tarnished Oasis [Heroic I]]] works as well*
   - *Note: Must be Level 128 to enter Instance.*
1. return to [[Herald of the Fathom Lord]] {{waypoint -275.14, -0.13, 51.21}} in [[The Mystic Lake]]
