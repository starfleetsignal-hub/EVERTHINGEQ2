## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[A Murkwater henchman|Murkwater henchmen]] - top and middle floors: rooms next to stairwells.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 8 [[A Murkwater vigilant|Murkwater vigilant]]s - same spawn spots as henchmen.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 7 Murkwater vigilants.
1. Read the book.
