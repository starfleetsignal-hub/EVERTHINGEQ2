## Steps

1. Enter [[Evernight Abbey]] and use Elliot's *Experimental Holy Water* on 10 weakened vampires
   - The potion only works when the vampires are below 15% health.
1. Return to [[Elliot Bloch]]

## Rewards

- At least 15g 83s 87c
- +5000 faction with **The League of Freethinkers**
- [[Bloch's Water Sprinkler]]
