## Access Notes

The Tower of Frozen Shadow can be entered by clicking on a mirror at  {{waypoint -1118, -526, -1722}} in the Great Divide. You have the choice between several instances:

- Tower of Frozen Shadow: Shadowed Corridors (Heroic) - This zone encompasses the 1st - 3rd floors of the Tower
- Tower of Frozen Shadow: Umbral Halls (Heroic) - This zone encompasses the 4th - 6th floors of the Tower
- Tower of Frozen Shadow: Haunt of Syl'Tor (Heroic) - This zone covers the 7th and 8th floors of the Tower
- Tower of Frozen Shadow (Epicx2) - It contains all 7 floors as well as Tserrina on the 8th floor. Access is gained by completing this quest. Completion of this zone and the quest gained inside is required to successfully engage x4 Challenge mode content.

## Steps

1. Meet Fidget in [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Shadowed Corridors]] at {{waypoint -19, 0, -6}}.
1. Collect six mirrors:
   - From the instance [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Shadowed Corridors|Shadowed Corridors]]:
     - [[A mirror (The Frozen Library)|a mirror]] - *reward from the quest [[Tserrina's Strife]]*.
     - [[A mirror (The Shadow Barracks)|a mirror]] - *reward from the quest [[A Ruinous Bath]]*.
   - Instance [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Umbral Halls|Umbral Halls]]:
     - [[A mirror (Darkness Rising)|a mirror]] - *reward from the quest [[A Light for the Shadow]]*.
     - [[A mirror (A Bride's Scorn)|a mirror]] - *reward from the quest [[Not Him Again]]*
     - [[A mirror (VhalSera's Dominion)|a mirror]] - *reward from the quest [[Mirror, Mirror]]*
   - Instance [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Haunt of Syl'Tor|Haunt of Syl'tor]]:
     - [[A mirror (The Palindrome)|a mirror]] - *reward from the quest [[Puzzled]]*
1. From the instance [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Haunt of Syl'Tor|Haunt of Syl'tor]], use the mirror and select "Investigate First Floor" to get to a version of the first floor with an additional room.
1. On this first floor, go to the portal room at {{waypoint 6, 5, -80}}.
1. Place the six mirrors in the six slots available.
1. Go through the portal to the eighth floor, the Haunt of Syl'tor, where Tserrina can be found.
1. Defeat [[Tserrina Syl'Tor]] in [[Tower of Frozen Shadow: Haunt of Syl'Tor]]. *Note: while Tserrina presents as an epicx2, this is actually the heroic version of this encounter.*
1. Speak with [[Dain Kalus Frostreaver the VI]].
1. Speak with [[Captain Gunnhilde Bluebeard]] in the [[Great Divide]] next to the Tower of Frozen Shadow zone-in mirror.
1. Speak to [[Dain Ulrinn Frostreaver the VII]] {{waypoint 86, -277, 226}} in [[Thurgadin]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g
- [[Memories of Tserrina]] - reward when Tserrina is defeated.
- Five white adornments, such as:
  - [[Frozen Adornment of Raw Power]]
  - [[Frosted Adornment of Critical Chance]]
  - [[Frozen Adornment of Increased Criticals]]
  - [[Frozen Adornment of Haste]]
  - [[Frozen Adornment of Reuse]]
- Access to Tower of Frozen Shadow (x2) - It contains all 7 floors as well as Tserrina. Completion of this zone and the quest gained inside is required to successfully engage x4 Challenge mode content.
