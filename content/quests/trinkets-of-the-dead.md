## Description

Eldin, a necromancer I met in Nektropos Castle needs my help getting personal items from the ghosts that inhabit the castle. He believes having these personal items will allow him to harness the souls of these ghosts. I am not quite sure if he is just crazy or really can do what he boasts I suppose I will gather the items and find out. Eldin has asked me to retrieve several different kinds of items from the ghosts in Nektropos Castle.

## Steps

1. Obtain the following updates:
   - I need to find the censor. Kill the [[A priestess of Ullkorruuk|priestesses of Ullkorruuk]] around the altar to pop the [[Inquisitor of Ulkorruuk]] {{waypoint -26, -1, 54}}.
   - I need to find the holy water. You get it when you kill the Priestesses above. {{waypoint -24, -1, 57}}.
   - I need to get the shirt from the [[An Everling manservant|Everling manservant]] {{waypoint -21, 0, -61}}.
   - I need to get the keyring from [[An Everling stable boy]] {{waypoint -23, 0, -20}}.
   - I need to take a scabbard from [[A Thexian guard]] {{waypoint 28, 0, -25}}.
   - I should take the coin purse of [[A dark patron]] {{waypoint 27, 0, 13}}. (Need to complete The Boar's Head quest and The Red Marble quest
   - I need to get the hair brush from [[Elise's Handmaiden]] {{waypoint 48, 13, 78}} (need to complete access quest to get sconce to turn.
   - I should retrieve Sheilia Everling's bodice from her [[Sheila's Handmaiden|handmaiden]]
   - I need to get Crysta's bracelet from [[Crysta's Handmaiden]] {{waypoint 47, 13, -78}}.
   - I need to find Jenni's hair bow from her [[Jenni's Handmaiden|handmaiden]] {{waypoint -69, 13, -1}}.
1. Return to [[Master Eldin Necrosis]]
