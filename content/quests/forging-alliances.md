## Prerequisites

You must be a level 40+ Scout to be offered this quest.

## Steps

1. Collect the unsigned pardons. They're on the second floor of [[The Freeport Militia House]] at {{waypoint 138, -7, 132}}.
1. Speak to [[T'ral D'zarn]] at {{waypoint 4, -7, -136}} in the [[Academy of Arcane Science]], North Freeport.
1. Enter [[Longshadow Alley: Daeneth Cloister]] by clicking on the door at {{waypoint -92, -35, -367}} in North Freeport.
1. Get 5 wanted posters. These look like clipboards with a white glow. Known locations:
   - {{waypoint -23, 4, 32}}
   - {{waypoint 37, -0.09, 9}}
   - {{waypoint 36, -1, -15}}
   - {{waypoint 20, 0, 2}}
   - {{waypoint -20, 1, -11}}
1. Return to [[T'ral D'zarn]] in the [[Academy of Arcane Science]].
1. Speak to [[Privateer Twitchwhisker]] in South Freeport ( -234, -56, 155 )

1. Go to the magistrate's office in [[The Freeport Militia House]] and swap the papers by clicking the table around {{waypoint 169, -23, 132}}. Note:it's downstairs, shiny Scroll on a table.
1. Return to [[Privateer Twitchwhisker]] at {{waypoint -235, -56, 155}}.
1. Return to [[Captain Siren]] at {{waypoint -302, -58, 138}} to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- [[A Large Seafury Buccaneer Crate]]
- +1000 faction with **The Seafury Buccaneers**
