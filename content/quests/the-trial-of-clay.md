## Steps

1. Destroy 25 [[A hardened clay vase|hardened clay vase]]s around the lower part of [[The Tabernacle of Pain]].
1. Speak with the [[Swifttail Trialmaster]]. {{waypoint -73, -43, 160}}
1. Enter the trial ring and defeat [[Glazjo Roughscale]]. {{waypoint -84, -42, 161}}
1. Return to the senior Clay shackle monk.
