## Steps

Create 6 items from the random list below:

- [[Tranquil Broadcloth Robe]]
- [[Tranquil Broadcloth Mitts]]
- [[Tranquil Broadcloth Pantaloons]]
- [[Tranquil Broadcloth Shawl]]
- [[Tranquil Broadcloth Slippers]]
- [[Woven Etched Boots (Level 30) (Crafted)|Woven Etched Boots]]
- [[Woven Etched Gloves (Level 30) (Crafted)|Woven Etched Gloves]]
- [[Woven Etched Pants (Level 30) (Crafted)|Woven Etched Pants]]
- [[Woven Etched Shoulder Pads]]
- [[Woven Etched Tunic (Level 30) (Crafted)|Woven Etched Tunic]]
- [[Dexterous Etched Boots]]
- [[Dexterous Etched Gloves]]
- [[Dexterous Etched Pants]]
- [[Dexterous Etched Shoulder Pads]]
- [[Dexterous Etched Vest]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 48s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
