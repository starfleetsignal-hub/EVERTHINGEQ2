## Prerequisites

- You must be at least adventure level 75 to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This is the initial quest of [[The Shadow Odyssey Timeline]] for characters of good-alignment. For characters of evil-alignment, see [[The Shadow Odyssey, Prologue: The Overlord's Command]].
- If attempting this quest at high level, you must accept the quest, [[Shattered Seas: Called to Qeynos Castle]], from [[Rosalina Goodfallow]] before the dialogue option will appear for this quest.

## Steps

1. Go to [[Qeynos Capitol District]] and enter [[Antonia Bayle's Chambers]]. {{waypoint 488, 5, -19}}
   - You must drop group to enter.
1. Speak with [[Queen Antonia Bayle (Antonia Bayle's Chambers)|Queen Antonia Bayle]]. {{waypoint 18, 0, 55}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g 9700 status
