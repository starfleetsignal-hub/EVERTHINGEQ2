## Note

Must be level 92
![Mysterious Raven](images/A_mysterious_raven.jpg)

## Steps

1. Meet with [[Taith McJarun (Antonica)|Taith McJarun]] outside [[Evernight Cemetery]] {{waypoint -1953, -14, 740}}in [[Antonica]]
1. Speak with [[Firiona Vie (Antonica)]] {{waypoint -1953, -14, 740}}
1. Speak with [[Rikantus]] {{waypoint -1972, 9, 821}}
1. Return to Firiona Vie
1. Return to Taith McJarun
1. Return to Erus in [[South Qeynos]] or [[Freeport]]
1. Speak to a **mysterious raven** (hail it to activate) then follow it to [[Ghostclaw]]
1. Speak with Ghostclaw in [[South Qeynos]] or [[North Freeport]] (the location will show-up as a Quest-book on your Map).
1. Meet with Ghostclaw in [[Thundering Steppes]] {{waypoint 746, 2, -704}}
1. Enter [[Ring of Karana]] solo instance next to Ghostclaw
   - You will have to disband if you are grouped with someone not on that step of the quest
   - Zone scales to your level
1. Use [[Ghostclaw's Talisman]] on 6 [[A wracked essence|wracked essence]]s
   - Fight them until they are 50% health or lower to use the talisman. You have to stand really close to them, almost on top of them.
   - Wracked essences respawn. Zone out and back in for immediate respawns.
   - *Note: best to mentor to ~95, equip some low level weapons, & remove any offensive buffs. Or these [[A wracked essence|wracked essence]]s will be killed in one hit.*
   - *Note: Alternative: unequip your weapons, use singular focus and just melee autoattack*
   - *Note: There will be group-wide updates for those in zone.*
1. Defeat [[Tirun Enforcer]] (your level ^); it depops before dying
1. Return to Ghostclaw
1. Follow the raven (should be standing next to Ghostclaw - hail Ghostclaw again if you don't see it) to [[Ravinus]] {{waypoint 612, 12, -709}}. The raven might be underground, if so tab to select it and then hail it to make it move.  Note: I was not able to tab target to hail the raven. I had to camp to myself and hail Ghostclaw again for the raven to respawn for me.)
1. Return to Erus

  - [[Ghostpaw's Enduring Talisman]]
  - [[Ghostpaw's Aeon Talisman]]
  - [[Ghostpaw's Fated Talisman]]
  - [[Ghostpaw's Eternity Talisman]]
