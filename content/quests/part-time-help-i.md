## Description

"Matstyr Alesmith, a hard working Dwarf that bases his operation at Spire Shadow Outpost, has offered me some part time work. My assignment is to hunt wolves and gather their pelts for Matstyr." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay wolves until you get 14 pelts
   - Seems any wolf will do ( [[A Spur stalker]], [[A prowler]], etc...)
1. Return to [[Matstyr Alesmith]]

## Rewards

- At least 2s 86c
- Experience

## Journal Steps

I need to gather some wolf pelts for Matstyr. Wolves, known as growlers or howlers, can be found around Orc Hill. I need to hunt wolves for their pelts.

I have enough pelts. I have the pelts for Matstyr. I need to return these pelts to Matstyr, at Spire Shadow Outpost. I should return these pelts to Matstyr.

I returned the pelts to Matstyr. I have returned the pelts to Matstyr.

I returned to Matstyr Alesmith with the pelts. It seems like his workload is still quite high.
