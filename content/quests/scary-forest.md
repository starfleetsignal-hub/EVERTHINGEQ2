## Steps

1. I need to find an uncorrupted soullight and direct it to follow me, using the branch given to me by Clubroot.
   - Use [[Clubroot's Favorite Branch]] on [[A soullight]]
     - *Note: make sure you don't use "force Ranged/spell weapon attack" when you try to grab a soullight, because when you use the item you will attack the soullight and you can't grab it*
     - Note: Each group member needs to grab a soullight for the update "cure corrupted teants"
1. With a soullight following me, I can now use [[Clubroot's Favorite Branch]] to direct them to sing to corrupted treants. I may need to attack the treants as well, to snap them out of their nightmares.
   - Cure corrupted treants ([[A wandering shaderoot]]) (6)
   - Group-wide update for all members with captured soullights.
1. I should return to Clubroot and see if he needs further help.
   - Return to [[Clubroot]] {{waypoint -528, -3, 547}}
