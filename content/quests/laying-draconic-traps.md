## Steps

1. Gather 4 x dormant draconic elemental rune bands - [[Warming Warfields]] below camp, round red pucks.![elemental rune band](images/Elemental_rune_band.png)
   - *Possible locations: {{waypoint -432, 49, -478}}, {{waypoint -228, 38, -552}}, {{waypoint -156, 26, -614}}, {{waypoint -100, 32, -615}}, {{waypoint -301, 19, -726}}, {{waypoint -271, 8, -798}}, {{waypoint -314, 28, -678}}
1. You receive a [[Draconic Elemental Rune Band]] (with 4 charges), use them from inventory near Kromzek banners in the [[Rime Claw Caves]] above [[Hoarfang Ridge]]
   - Drop elemental rune band at the banner in the southern cave {{waypoint -770.01, 244.94, -321.69}}
   - Drop elemental rune band at the second level of the southern cave {{waypoint -862, 278, -329}}
   - Drop elemental rune band at the banner deep in the caves  {{waypoint -854.94, 233.39, -443.46}}
   - Drop elemental rune band at the banner in the northern cave {{waypoint -795.67, 216.69, -509.33}}
1. Return to [[Mergus Stonesledge]]
