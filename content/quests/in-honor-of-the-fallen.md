## Steps

1. Examine burynai corpses strewn about [[The Expanse of Bone]] to collect the belongings of the fallen, while exacting revenge for the Bellywhumpers Clan:
   - *Shield*: {{waypoint -18, -7, 65}}
   - *Shiny orb thingy*: {{waypoint 76, -1, 34}}
   - *Pudge Family Shiny List*: {{waypoint 83, -2, -28}}
   - *Sparklebullow Ring:* {{waypoint 79, -6, -61}}
   - *Bellywhumper Warrior's Bow*: {{waypoint 23, -6, -89}}
   - *Llump Family Bracelet*: {{waypoint 67, -6, 86}}
   - Slay 2 [[A charbone destroyer|charbone destroyers]]
   - Slay 2 [[An icebone destroyer|icebone destroyers]]
   - Slay 2 [[A scalebone slayer|scalebone slayers]]
   - Defeat [[Jennre Warsinger]]
1. Investigate further, gather items of value, and continue exacting revenge:
   - Go to the [[Atrium of Silence]] (second floor).
   - *Void metal socket*: {{waypoint 173, 289, 0}}
   - *Void metal chalice*:{{waypoint 0, 289, -173}}
   - *Void metal container*: {{waypoint -173, 289, 0}}
   - *Void metal sphere*: {{waypoint 0, 289, 173}}
   - Destroy 4 groups of [[A remnant of Theer|Theer remants]]
     - Killing one remnant of Theer does not update the quest, you must kill the entire pack.
   - Kill 4 Theerians ([[A Theerian warder]] or [[A Theerian stormbringer]])
   - Kill [[Thovalakk]]
   - Kill [[Telvorsinn]]
1. Speak with [[Chief Llump the Wise]] in [[Fens of Nathsar]], near the entrance to Kurn's Tower.

## Rewards

- At least 2p
- +6000 faction with **The Bellywhumpers**
