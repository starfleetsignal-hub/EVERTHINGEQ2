## Starting the Quest

Every evil-aligned city and many of the villages have a "satisfied" or "delighted" NPC that starts this quest:

- In [[North Freeport]], speak with [[A delighted human]] at {{waypoint -31, -20, -300}}
- In [[West Freeport]], speak with [[A satisfied darkelf]] at {{waypoint 260.68, -3.64, -92.58}}
- In [[Neriak, City of Hate]], speak with [[A satisfied darkelf]] at {{waypoint -20, 11, 74}}
- In [[Gorowyn]], speak with [[A delighted human]] at {{waypoint -18, -11, -76}}
- In [[Haven]], speak with [[A satisfied darkelf]]

## Steps

![a rancor quartz for step 4 (with a maximized target arrow to illustrate scale)](images/A_rancor_quartz_2.jpg) ![a dirt pile for step 5 (with a sablevein on the background to illustrate scale)](images/A_dirt_pile.jpg)

1. Speak with [[Xilania Nevagon (Neriak)|Xilania Nevagon, the Seraph of Hate]] at the [[Spires of Innoruuk]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]] {{waypoint -295, 29, 184}}
1. Go to the [[Nightsong Opera House]] and kill [[Sorndinn De'Tretlar]]. The [[Nightsong Opera House]] can be found at {{waypoint -672, 29, -28}}; go into the building and up towards the stage area to trigger him.  When he spawns he will immediately attack you. He is a solo mob who scales to your level.
1. Return to [[Xilania Nevagon (Neriak)|Xilania Nevagon, the Seraph of Hate]] after killing [[Sorndinn De'Tretlar]]. She now sends you to speak with [[Sabstin V'Despth|Sabstin V'Despth, Priestess of Hate]] {{waypoint -249, 28, 170}}.
1. [[Sabstin V'Despth]] needs you to harvest 12 rancor quartz; these are Tab-able small rocks that spawn along the rock walls within [[Neriak, City of Hate]].
1. After harvesting the 12 quartz, return to [[Sabstin V'Despth|Sabstin]]. Next you must go out into [[Darklight Wood]] and bury the 12 rancor quartz near the settlements. To do this, harvest the **non** Tab-able small dirt piles found near the areas with NPCs
   - 5 dirt piles can be found inside and just outside of [[Hate's Envy]] {{waypoint -427, -62, 245}}
     - {{waypoint -362, -62, 251}}
     - {{waypoint -362, -62, 195}}
     - {{waypoint -440, -62, 242}}
     - {{waypoint -410, -62, 192}}
     - {{waypoint -400, -62, 262}}
   - 3 can be found near [[T'Vatar Post]] {{waypoint -370, -48, 37}}
     - {{waypoint -380, -50, 53}}
     - {{waypoint -363, -49, 24}}
     - {{waypoint -340, -50, 33}}
   - 3 can be found near [[Wanderlust Fair]] (261, -20. -11)
     - {{waypoint 239, -19, 13}}
     - {{waypoint 248, -18, -27}}
     - {{waypoint 284, -21, -14}}
   - 2 can be found near [[Warpfont Spire]] {{waypoint -92, -36, 282}}
     - {{waypoint -128, -40, 278}}
     - {{waypoint -161, -41, 278}}
   - One can be found near [[Sableflame Crater]] {{waypoint 124, -45, 32}}
     - {{waypoint 30, -46, 129}}
   - Other individual ones can be found on or near the roads, or by [[Shadow Oak]] {{waypoint 435, -32, -255}}
1. After burying the 12 crystal, return to [[Sabstin V'Despth]] for your reward.
