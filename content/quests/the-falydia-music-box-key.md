## Steps

1. Slay 15 [[A sand scourge|sand scourges]] in [[Sandstone Delta]] around {{waypoint -192.84, 84.92, -534.68}}
1. Zone to [[Raj Dur Plateaus]]
1. Slay [[A cacti abhorrence|cacti abhorrences]] around {{waypoint -46.81, -0.03, 133.95}} until a box drops.
1. Open the box and examine the scroll inside.
1. Slay 15 more [[A cacti abhorrence|cacti abhorrences]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- One of the following:
  - [[Falydia Music Box Key]]
  - [[Renewal Crate]]
  - [[Falydia Key]] - Collectible
