## Steps

1. [[Hunter Rajik|Rajik]] tells you to kill a number of beasts and bring the meat back to him. Each time you receive this quest, the required animals may be different:
   - 8 [[A feral bruiser|feral bruisers]] around {{waypoint 642, 132, 3088}}
   - 15 [[A toxic crawler (Sundered Frontier)|toxic crawlers]] around {{waypoint 1299, -47, 3066}}
   - 15 [[A briarroot horror|briarroot horrors]] around {{waypoint 823, 7, 3248}} BUG! If you are flying near briarroot horrors, and they hit you in the air, you will not be stunned, but you will not be able to land and death is certain, scout's escape will not work, actually nothing works. Either delete this request and get another, or land near them and walk into the area. /bug it if it happens to you.
   - 4 [[A Toxxulia guardian|Toxxulia guardians]]
   - 4 Toxxulia Wurm Meat from Toxxulia's Reach around {{waypoint 339, 128, 2612}}
   - 15 Toxxulia prowler meat {{waypoint 1239, -52, 3093}}
1. Return for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +50 faction with **Kerra Isle**

## Notes

- Note: If you don't want the task you get, you can delete the quest and talk to him again for a new quest (and new random task).
- Eventually, [[Hunter Rajik|Rajik]] will tell you that your help is no longer needed.  Quest right nearby is [[Foraging for Goodies]]
