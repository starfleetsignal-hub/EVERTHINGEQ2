## Steps

1. Enter [[Frostfell Workshop]] behind the magical wardrobe in [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]] at {{waypoint 40, -4, -1181}}, and speak with [[Meg Gingersnap]]. {{waypoint -15, 4, -55}}
1. Pick up the snow globe gift on the table next to her.
1. Jump down into the basement and speak to Jarith. {{waypoint -14, -5, -52}}
1. Click on the *Enchanted Snowglobe* by Jarith to enter the [[Maldura Steamworks Sublevel]].
   - Your mercenary will suspend.
   - Do not click the gate right by you after zoning, it will exit you out of the instance.
1. Speak to [[Sira Cogtree (Maldura Steamworks Sublevel)|Sira Cogtree]] on a ledge at bottom of the waterfall. {{waypoint 78, -8, 158}}
1. Return to the basement of Frostfell Workshop and speak to Jarith.
   - Since you gave up your [[Frostfell Return Token]], you have to find an alternative way to leave the instance. You can port out, or simply click on the gate near where you entered. {{waypoint 104, 57, 52}}

## Rewards

- [[Tinkered Snowglobe]] (a [[Decorating Houses|house item)]]
- 1 [[Frozen Token of E'ci]]
- +1000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
