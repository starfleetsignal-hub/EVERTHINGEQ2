## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Tunare has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items

1. Purchase the [[Life Enriched Soil|life enriched soil]] from Saelle for 5000 status
   - Wanders along the road in the Glade of the Unicorns, in Lesser Faydark, from {{waypoint 77, -20, -204}} to near the Upper Grove Stable at {{waypoint -44, -15, -89}}
1. Purchase the [[Elder Root|elder root]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the [[Kaleidoscopic Water|kaleidoscopic water]] from Nushoon for 5000 status
   - In [[Aether Wroughtlands]] this NPC wanders along a path from the Bivouac Camp {{waypoint 754, -45, -446}} to Minstrel's Cradle {{waypoint 344, -59, -183}}
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Mother of All]] at {{waypoint 154, -23, 3}} in [[The Lesser Faydark|Lesser Faydark]]
