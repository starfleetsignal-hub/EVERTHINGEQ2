## Prerequisites

- Neriak citizenship

## Steps

1. Speak to a House X'Rzheduis slave, he will become aggro (level 10) and attack
1. Slay him
1. Speak to the slave's former master, [[Kacha X'Rzheduis]] outside [[The Burnished Coin]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]] {{waypoint -155, 12, 285}}.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 10c
