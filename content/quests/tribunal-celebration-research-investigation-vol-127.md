## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of the Tribunal has asked me to assist in gathering justicebound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the justicebound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Justicebound Energy Reserves|justicebound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect [[Justicebound Energy Reserves|justicebound energy]] by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Seventh Hammer]] at {{waypoint 274, 180, -357}} in [[Timorous Deep]]
