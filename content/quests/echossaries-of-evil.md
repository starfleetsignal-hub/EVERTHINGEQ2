Evil's afoot and aflight within Echo Caverns, which this manual seems to indicate with its crude drawings of various creatures with a large "Stay away" written underneath them. There are no other notes, which is where I come in. I'll catalogue these so-called Echossaries of Evil!

## Steps

- Catalogue the crumbling grumbler, [[Grumblecrumble]]
- Catalogue the toxic tegi, [[Krullock Poisonhands]]
- Catalogue the surly waterspout, [[Rilesurge]]
- Catalogue the Emperor Chloro-Fly, [[Anisopt]]
- Catalogue the Cap King, [[Irkales]]
- Catalogue the Zelmie Hero, [[Captain Terrasque Kel'Avar]]
- Catalogue the bloodthirsty leech, [[Bloodletter (Monster)|Bloodletter]]
- Catalogue the great reishi, [[The Great Saprophyte]]
- Catalogue the mother of roots, [[The Root Mother]]
