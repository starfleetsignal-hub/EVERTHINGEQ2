## Steps

1. Enter [[The Willow Wood: Besieged Hamlet]] at {{waypoint 810, -21, -564}}
1. Investigate gnoll dig points - head to roughly {{waypoint 875, -20, -619}} for an auto-update
1. Kill 6 [[A Rockpaw digger|Rockpaw diggers]]
   - The first digger will be an NPC that turns into a monster once left-clicked.  After it is killed, Rockpaw diggers will begin spawning from that same spot one at a time - if you're running low on health or power, get out of aggro range!
   - After killing the 6 Rockpaw diggers you automatically receive [[Ensorcelled Gear]]
1. Find a way to seal off the 4 Rockpaw tunnels
   - To do this, click on a rock that will be lying in front of the tunnel.  The four tunnels are at
     - {{waypoint 877, -16, -601}}
     - {{waypoint 878, -17, -615}}
     - {{waypoint 862, -18, -615}}
     - {{waypoint 863, -20, -642}}
1. Return to [[Milo Faren]] in The Elddar Grove {{waypoint 721, -20, -447}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g @ lvl 95
