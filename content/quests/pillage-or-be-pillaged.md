## Steps

1. Complete the following steps in no particular order:
   - Find a way to release the captured animals
     - Examine the hastily contructed wooden fence near the [[A captured grazer|captured grazers]] {{waypoint 251, 271, -264}}
   - Destroy the crate of stolen weapons
     - Examine the crate labeled Ironforge Exchange: Order Number: 8951 - Spears, Short {{waypoint 296, 273, -490}}
   - Destroy the crate of stolen Kaldim supplies
     - Examine the crate labeled Property of the Everhot Foundry{{waypoint 288, 273, -490}}
   - Destroy the barrels of dwarven spirits
     - Examine the barrels labeled Dwarven Spirits {{waypoint 283, 273, -492}}
   - Slay 8 [[A Krulkiel grunt|Krulkiel grunts]]
     - They are found on the two slopes leading up to Kamp Krulkiel
   - Slay [[Wolfmaster Dulkyr]]
     - Found near the wolf pen {{waypoint 22, 270, -592}}
   - Slay 6 [[A Muckflick mercenary|Muckflick mercenaries]]
     - Found near [[Wolfmaster Dulkyr]] {{waypoint 42, 272, -616}}
1. Return to [[Gloln Kilnkor]]
