## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 20 [[Deadly terrorfruit]]
- 60 [[Basilisk meat]]
- 125 [[Reef cluster]]
- 90 [[Nimbic hide]]
- 250 [[Celestial Kindling]]
- Stove and Keg (Optional)

## Steps

1. (If you left Eryslai after getting quest) Take the [[Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wind]] portal in Myrist at {{waypoint 717, 412, -378}}
1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 143, -179, -820}} to [[Crosswind Isle]]. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 319, 181, -250}} to [[The Bixel Hive]]. Travel to {{waypoint -552, 362, -371}} and receive [[Recipe Scroll: Sticky Honey Trap]].
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Sticky Honey Trap]] and craft 5 [[Sticky Honey Trap]] on a Stove.
   - There is a [[Stove & Keg]] at [[The Aerie]]. Take the portal at {{waypoint -515, 356, -421}} to [[Crosswind Isle]] then portal to [[The Aerie]] to the stove at {{waypoint 932, 331, 23}}
   - There is a [[Stove & Keg]] in the [[Myrist Commissary]], just off the foyer of the Library at {{waypoint 25, -20, 74}}.
1. Travel back to [[The Bixel Hive]].
1. Place 5 traps in the hive. Trap locations are shown by yellow glowing areas in the hive.  Some possible locations:
   - {{waypoint -584, 385, -243}}
   - {{waypoint -542, 415, -170}}
   - {{waypoint -572, 441, -91}}
   - {{waypoint -603, 499, -162}}
   - {{waypoint -517, 526, -109}}
   - {{waypoint -663.23, 573.24, -136.58}}
   - {{waypoint -463.89, 637.35, -182.21}}
   - Note: The locations are all accessible without combat, but you may need to fly up and around mobs to avoid aggro. The bixels do not see invisibility, so that's another means of skirting aggro. There also was a bug on beta which could bug you if you and a group mate click one of the trapping locations at the same time.
1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint -516, 357, -429}} to retrace your path back to [[Recipes for Adventure]] at {{waypoint 454, -202, -1432}} on [[The Windglades]] (or evac).

## Rewards

- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
