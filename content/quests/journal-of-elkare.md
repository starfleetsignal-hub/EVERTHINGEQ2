*This quest was part of the former access questline to enter [[The Crypt of Betrayal]], however it's completion is no longer required.

## Steps

1. Find the great oak tree in the [[Central Farmlands]] in [[Antonica]] at {{waypoint -108, -18, 111}}.
1. As you approach the tree [[A brigand leader|a brigand leader]] will attack you. Kill him and you will get the tome.
1. Return to [[Wesaelan Brookshadow]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 6s 23c
- [[Lesser Amulet of Warding]]
