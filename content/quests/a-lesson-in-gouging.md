This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Enter [[A Maj'Dul Residence]].
1. Inspect a basket {{waypoint -7, 0, 2}} and move the creature that spawns near the aristocrat {{waypoint 14, 0, 4}} to kill him.
1. Kill whatever creature spawns.
   - Note: If it happens to be an upset djinn, then you must kill the two flame torrents that also spawn.
1. Return to any Tears grifter for your reward.

## Reward

- At least3g 23s 29c
- One of the following:
  - 5 [[Blades insignia token|Blades insignia tokens]]
  - 5 [[Coin insignia token|Coin insignia tokens]]
  - 5 [[Truth insignia token|Truth insignia tokens]]
