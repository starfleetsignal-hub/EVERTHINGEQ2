## Note

After completing this quest, take the [[Sootfoot Armoring Secrets (Quest Item)|Sootfoot Armoring Secrets]] back to [[Purrla]] {{waypoint -145, -117, 633}} to complete the quest [[Crafty Sootfoots: Corporate Espionage]] before moving on to [[Improving Relations with the Locals]]

## Steps

![dense magma node](images/Dense_magma_node.jpg)

1. Fish 10 [[Magma sample (Sootfoot Item)|magma samples]] from 'dense magma nodes' in nearby lava pools. A few locations are listed below:
   1. {{waypoint -203, -130, 315}}
   1. {{waypoint -261, -112, 229}}
   1. {{waypoint -259, -128, 362}}
1. Return to [[Smakametta]].
