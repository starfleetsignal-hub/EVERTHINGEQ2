## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 1 [[Deadly terrorfruit]]
- 8 [[Wandervine fruit]]
- 10 [[Strangler root]]
- 50 [[Celestial Kindling]]

## Steps

1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Instructedible Cookie]]
1. Use the [[Stove and Keg]] on the first floor of the Library in the [[Myrist Commissary]] at {{waypoint 25, -20, 74}} (room just south of the main entrance) to make the [[Instructedible Cookie]].
1. Speak with the floating book [[Recipes for Adventuring]] at {{waypoint 280, 15, 29}} on the 2nd floor in the library.
1. Travel to [[Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wind]] using the portal at {{waypoint 715, 412, -379}}.
1. Now in Eryslai speak with [[Recipes for Adventure (Eryslai)|Recipes for Adventure]] at {{waypoint 454, -202, -1432}} .

## Rewards

- [[Golden Fleck]]
- [[Instructedible Cookie Recipe]]
- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
