## Description

*An Aberrant being of immense power lurks somewhere within Kralet Penumbra. Its voice is said to be heard only within one's mind, and its words are death. It is time someone silences this speaker and anything allied with it.*

## Zone

- Defeat targets within [[Kralet Penumbra: Rise to Power (Advanced Solo)]]

## Steps

- Kill the listener
- Mute the speaker

- At least 1p 51g 20s 11,300 status
