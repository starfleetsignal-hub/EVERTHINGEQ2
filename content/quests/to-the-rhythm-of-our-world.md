## Steps

![Image of a sacred shell above the waterline](images/A_sacred_shell_%28above_water%29.png)Diu is at {{waypoint -1567, -539, -2343}}

1. Gather 6 sacred shells from the icy shore west of Fina's Retreat {{waypoint -1175, -541, -2271}}
   - *(They can be above or below the water line.)*
1. Diu isn't where you left him!  Speak to [[Miau]].  She tells you he went north.
1. Find Diu at {{waypoint -1492, -541, -2426}} and speak to him.
1. Click the spirit drum next to Diu as fast as you can.
   - Mouse over the drum and press F repeatedly.
1. Once [[A materialized demonic entity]] (level 88^, non-aggro) spawns, slay it.
1. Speak to Diu
