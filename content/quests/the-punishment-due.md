## Steps

1. Kill 4 [[An Earth Destroyer|Earth Destroyers]]
   - Possible locations, mostly on the small floating sky islands, in a more-or-less clockwise loop:
     - {{waypoint -1285, 357, -845}} (floating island east of Khat-Ra)
     - {{waypoint -963.73, 72.98, -78.79}} (Pakiat Bluffs)
     - {{waypoint -585, 168, 65}} (floating island south of Pakiat Bluffs)
     - {{waypoint -838, 342, 1062}} (Valor's Roost)
     - {{waypoint -311.02, 16.39, 941.16}} (Zivier's reach, on the ground)
     - {{waypoint -137, -22, 1182}} (floating island south of Zivier's reach)
     - {{waypoint 874, 12, 842}} (Mages Pebble)
   - The 4th kill awards [[Earth elemental shards]].
1. Return to [[Khat-Ra Pakiat]] and receive [[Essence of Body]].
1. Examine the [[Essence of Body]] and 'Hold the Element tightly and concentrate' to receive [[Attuned Essence of Body]].
1. Speak to [[Khat-Ra Pakiat]].
