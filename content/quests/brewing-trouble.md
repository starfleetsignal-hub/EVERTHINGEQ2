This quest is part 3 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Dark Elf Series.

## Steps

1. Speak with Kalraath L'Vin near the North Freeport gate {{waypoint -6, 0, 63}}.
1. You receive Alchemical Medium (NO-TRADE NO-VALUE) in your inventory. This must be returned to Aldera. Should you destroy it, or lose it for some reason, then you can speak to Kalraath L'Vin again to receive a new one.
1. Return to [[Aldera V'Exxa]] for your reward. Pay attention to the dialogue, she will do a little quiz with you before giving the next quest.

- Coin
