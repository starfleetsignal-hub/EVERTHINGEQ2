## Steps

1. Return to Kerra Isle and enter the camp area in the center of the island.
1. Visit [[Chinook]] {{waypoint 1363.87, 47.56, 2160.13}} on Kerra Isle (If you plan to stay and do the next line of quests before returning to Felice Adae, you will need to bring *a [[Portable Woodworking Kit]] and [[Portable Sewing Table]], along with the following ingredients: 15 [[Titanium ore]], 10 [[Bamboo shoot]], 20 [[Sumac lumber]], 15 [[Mottled pelt]], 15 [[Quicksilver cluster]], 5 [[Ethereal sandpaper]] & 10 [[Ethereal filament]] *for "[[Outfitting the Kerra: The Best Defense]]")
1. Return to [[Felice Adae]] on Spire Island {{waypoint 2081, -234, 2642}}

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
