## Background

[[Captain E.L. Irontoe]] in [[West Fort Irontoe]] has ordered me to retake some stolen goods from the towers at [[Bloodforge Gate]] in [[Kaladim]]. These goods were meant for the good soldiers of the +100 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**. I need to retrieve these goods and investigate their purpose.

## Notes

- +10000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**, which is the exact amount required to progress the heritage quest [[Casualties of the War of the Fae]].

## Steps

1. Enter [[Kaladim]]. {{waypoint -170, 165, -567}}
1. Scavange the required items within the two towers of the [[Bloodforge Gate]], {{waypoint -27, 28, -132}} and {{waypoint 18, 28, -132}}:
   - 6 'unpolished hammers' from killing Clan Boombottom goblins (common update).
   - 6 'bottles of Flaming Clurg' by harvesting transparent bottles usually found on the middle and top floors.
   - 8 'boxes of Rodcet Bandages' by harvesting boxes usually found on bottom and middle floors.
   - 1 barrel of 'Pungla's Pyro Pickes' from the top floor (roof) of either tower.
   - Volume 1 of the Boombottom blasting processes from the west tower, 2nd floor, second shelf on bookshelf. {{waypoint 13, 44, -137}}
   - Volume 2 of the Boombottom blasting processes from the east tower, 2nd floor, bottom left of bookshelf. {{waypoint -23, 44, -127}}
1. Return to [[Captain E.L. Irontoe]] for your reward.
