Before the coming flood envelops you and your allies within the depths of Awuidor, a message is delivered: "Bring back the Dark Fathom of Water and so shall you be rewarded!" Well, now, how can I pass up that offer?!

## Steps

- Obtain a Dark Fathom of Water
- Zone out and hail [[Dr. Arcana (Myrist, the Great Library)|Dr. Arcana]] for your reward.

## Reward

- At least 2p 49g 48s
- 5 [[Archivist's Tremissis]]
- [[Chaos Descending Public Quest Mission Reward (Version 2)|Chaos Descending Public Quest Mission Reward]]
