## Steps

1. When you accept this quest you receive a temporary  called, [[Collect Field Reports]]. When standing within 15 meters from the following mobs, use the ability to obtain their field reports. All can be found running around the area between [[Tylix's Fist]] and [[Ganak's Battlement]]:
   - [[Scout Cluzwen]]: runs from the pond  {{waypoint -656, -128, -584}} up the hill to the west {{waypoint -437, -77, -647}}; sometimes near location {{waypoint -656, -128, -585}}
   - [[Scout Hir'Tak]]: {{waypoint -422, -70, -394}}
   - [[Scout Mirt'Zin]]: {{waypoint -887, -86, -389}}
   - [[Scout Tonkur]]: stops at {{waypoint -431, -59, -227}} or {{waypoint -745, -123, -690}}
   - [[Scout Niba'Nin]]: {{waypoint -877, -89, -448}}; runs between {{waypoint -976, -104, -568}} and {{waypoint -614, -65, -224}}
   - [[Scout Volzekla]]: {{waypoint -830.5, -123.44, -624.98}}  or {{waypoint -1053, 97, -589}}
1. Return to Battlemaster Kythe.

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- +1000 faction with **The Sel'Nok Brigade**
