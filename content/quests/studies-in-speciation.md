## Steps

Kahla has asked you to gather:

1. Four cave spider bodies
1. Four cave serpent bodies
1. Four cave bat bodies
1. Return to Kahla for your reward.

## Notes

- Any spider in the zone will satisfy her request: [[A white spiderling|white spiderling]]s at the entry bells, [[A white spider|white spider]]s on the first level underneath the forge and armorer gnolls, and [[An albino arachnid|albino arachnids]] on the upper level.
- Either type of bat in the zone will satisfy her request: [[A shale bat|shale bat]]s on the first floor near the Dustpaw tent, or [[A terra bat|terra bat]]s on the upper level near the gnoll tents.
- **Note**: When Kahla says 'cave serpents', she really means [[A cave serpent|cave serpent]]s found on the first and second levels. Snakes like the pale adders and albino pythons may be considered serpents by some, but not by Kahla, and thus do not satisfy her request.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 68c
- One of the  Following:
  - [[Torn backpack]]
  - a [[Wooden pet cage]]
