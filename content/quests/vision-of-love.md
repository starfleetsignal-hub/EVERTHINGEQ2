## Notes

- This quest can be completed once per year.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Rehema Sa'din]] in [[Quel'ule]]. {{waypoint -436, 380, 678}}
1. Defeat 12 [[Slobberjaw pup|slobberjaw pups]] outside [[Moonfield Hamlet]]. {{waypoint 43, 370, 1085}}
   - These kobolds are non-aggro until you come quite close to them, when they respawn into an aggro mob at your level.
1. Return to Illixxol.

## Rewards

- [[A Gazing Lover]]
- 5 [[Erollisi coin|Erollisi coins]]
