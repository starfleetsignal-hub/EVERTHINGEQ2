*This quest is also part of [[The Commonlands Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Acquire items in [[The Wailing Caves]]:
   - 5 [[A strongbox deceiver|strongbox deceiver]] meat
   - 5 [[A primordial menace|primordial menace]] goos
   - 5 [[A Shin`Ree spiritist|spiritist]] essences
   - 5 [[A lurid ripper|lurid ripper]] egg sacs
     - Each of these items is an uncommon drop, so you may need to kill a few of each.
1. Return to Gimble Cognoggin for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 10c
- One of the Following (based on class):
  - [[Ivory Crafted Earring]]
  - [[Hoop of Woven Strands]]
  - [[Earhoop of Fashioned Ivory Shards]] (Mage)
  - [[Earstud of Woven Strands]] (Scout)
