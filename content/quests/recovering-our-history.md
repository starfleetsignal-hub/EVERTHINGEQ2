## Steps

1. Speak to [[Historian Velanie]] in [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] {{waypoint 26, -7, -113}}.
1. Head to [[Beggar's Court: Sweat-Stained Cobbles]] and recover the stolen artifacts.  They're boxes with silver sparkles rising from them, and can be found at the following locations.
   - {{waypoint 62, -6, 70}}
   - {{waypoint -19, -7, 70}}
   - {{waypoint 66, 4, 9}}
   - {{waypoint 46, -4, 36}}
   - {{waypoint -47, -5, 31}}
   - {{waypoint 31, 13, -32}}
   - {{waypoint -30, 2, -33}}
   - {{waypoint 67, 5, -35}}
   - {{waypoint -16, -2, 77}}
   - {{waypoint -31, 0, 0}}
1. Return to Historian Velanie
1. Return to Derald Walldons
1. Place 5 wanted posters (click the sparkling tablets on the walls at the following locations.  There's six total locations, you can pick any 5.
   - {{waypoint 133, -16, 117}} (In front of [[The Freeport Militia House]])
   - {{waypoint 47, -18, 94}} (In front of [[The Coalition of Tradesfolke]])
   - {{waypoint -14, -20, 15}} (Near [[Overlord's Plaza]])
   - {{waypoint -117, -38, 68}} (In front of [[The Freeport Registrar]])
   - {{waypoint -113, -21, -65}} (Entrance of [[The Jade Tiger's Den]])
   - {{waypoint -222, -55, 7}} (Entrance of [[Seafarer's Roost]])
1. Return to Derald Walldons

## Rewards

- At least 10g 97c @ lvl 56
