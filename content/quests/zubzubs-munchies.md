## Steps

1. Use the [[Swamp rat trap]] you receive from [[Zubzub]] and trap 50 [[A swamp rat|swamp rats]] from Innothule Swamp surrounding [[Firmroot Moot]] (this includes the Grobb area, a much safer place to find them). If the mobs are grey to you, there's a lot of rats around {{waypoint 330.71, -81.14, -321.59}}.
1. Return to [[Zubzub]]

## Rewards

- +5000 faction with **Survival Accord**
