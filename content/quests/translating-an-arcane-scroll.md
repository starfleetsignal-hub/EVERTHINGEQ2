## Description

*The scroll I obtained from Lilia Posey in Kelethin is written in a script that Count Jooliin cannot read. He's asked me to take it to Viiljo Aelii, a Fae sage local to the Court of the Fae that should be able to read the script and decipher the text that it contains.*

## Prerequisites

- Your faction with [[The City of Kelethin (Faction)|City of Kelethin]] must be Indifferent or better to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Deliver the [[A scroll of arcane text|scroll of arcane text]] to [[Viiljo Aelii]] {{waypoint -300, -57, 445}} near Count Jooliin
1. Complete the sub-quest [[Repaying Viiljo's Debt]]
1. Talk to Viiljo Aelii after doing his quest
   - Wait 15 seconds for an animation, and talk to him again to receive [[A translated scroll of arcane text]]
1. Return to Count Jooliin

## Rewards

- At least 9g 13s 1c
- +250 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
