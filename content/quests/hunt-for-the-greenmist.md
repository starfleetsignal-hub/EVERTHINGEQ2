## Steps

1. Visit the mess tents around [[Deathfist Citadel]]. Each tent has a poster inside that you can inspect. One of the posters will update the quest.
1. Kill mobs around the Citadel:
   - Slay 4 [[A Derris trooper|Derris trooper]]s {{waypoint 92, -36, -561}}
   - Slay [[A Gorynn trooper]] {{waypoint 95, -38, -837}}
   - Slay [[A Drunder trooper]] {{waypoint -69, -36, -737}}
1. Visit Split Rock Pass {{waypoint -250, -37, 130}}. A set of orcs will spawn as you approach. Slay them ([[A Greenmist first mate]] and [[A Greenmist marauder|Greenmist marauder]]s)
1. Inspect the chest at [[Blackhook Landing]] to receive [[A map (A crudely drawn orc map)|a map]].{{waypoint -34, -41, 447}}
1. Examine the [[A map (A crudely drawn orc map)|map]] in your inventory
1. Return to Lute
