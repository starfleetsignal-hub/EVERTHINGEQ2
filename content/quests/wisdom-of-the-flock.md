## Steps

![One of the statues](images/Ampoel_the_Selfless.jpg)

1. Find a copy of the book, [[Wisdom of the Flock (Item)|Wisdom of the Flock]], from [[Skylord Stormclaw]] (71^^^), or one of the [[A Blacktalon vanquisher|Blacktalon vanquishers]] in the [[Oratory of Naar'Yora]]. {{waypoint 1, 111, 1}}
1. Visit the statues of the great aviak ancestors:
   - Va're'nok the Swift - {{waypoint -118, 0, 134}} Main floor, entrance on right
   - Andan the Stalwart - {{waypoint -60, 0, 134}} Main floor, entrance on left
   - Aeristok the Vanquisher - {{waypoint 0, -1, 0}} Main floor in central room (Fountain Room of Aeristok)
   - Ampoel the Selfless - {{waypoint 146, 58, 4}} in front of workshops, up 2 main ladders
   - Kruekil the Soaring - {{waypoint -15, 86, 25}} Reflectory (up 3 main ladders)
   - Vethros the Watchful - {{waypoint -15, 86, -25}} Reflectory (up 3 main ladders)
   - Ma'tal of the Fifth Wind - {{waypoint -30, 143, -58}} 6th floor on back wall (see note below).
   - Chey'ruk the Vigilant - {{waypoint -64, 143, -20}} 6th floor on back wall (see note below).
   - Inkarun the Decisive - {{waypoint -64, 140, 28}} 6th floor on back wall (see note below).
   - Maer'sol the Caring - {{waypoint -30, 143, 64}} 6th floor on back wall (see note below).
   - *Note: 4 are in the tower section where [[Enmity of Naar'Yora]] spawns for the quest [[An Audience with Aaryonar]] and the [[A Naar'Yora defender|Naar'Yora defenders]].  You will be able to get to all 4 statues without killing mobs on that level, but getting there requires killing level 71^^^ mobs (group may be required).*
1. Speak with [[Arejo Cloudbreach]] on [[The Whisperwind Isle]] in [[The Barren Sky]] at {{waypoint -575, -46, -387}}.

## Rewards

- At least 18g 85s
- +75 faction with **The Windgazer**
