## Description

The Hua Mein Craftsman has requested assistance in cooking for an upcoming feast in the Hua Mein village.

## Steps

1. I have been asked to bake some fish for an upcoming feast in the Hua Mein village.
   - Cook up some [[Tasty Baked Fish|tasty baked fish]] for the feast (Recipe called "**baked fish**") (0/5).
   - total (if all combines is successful) of items required:
     - 10 [[Seahorse roe]]
     - 10 [[Marr cherry]]
     - 10 [[Bamboo shoot]]
     - 5 [[Ethereal kindling]]
     - [[Stove and Keg]]
1. Return to [[Xi Lan]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,000 faction with **Hua Mein**
- +2,500 faction with **Hua Mein Craftsmen**
