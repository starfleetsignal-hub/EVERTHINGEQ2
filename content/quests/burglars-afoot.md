## Notes

- This quest is also part of the [[Tenebrous Tangle Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Go to the [[Hidden Refuge (POI)|Hidden Refuge]]. {{waypoint 103, -215, 180}}
   - If you do not have a mount to absorb the fall, jump off the waterfall and land in the pool at the bottom.
1. Speak to [[Tayil N'Velex (Tenebrous Tangle)|Tayil N'Velex]] or [[Murrar Shar (Tenebrous Tangle)|Murrar Shar]].
1. Visit the top of the two treehouses around [[The Temple Grounds]]. The first is near the waterfall that leads down to the Hidden Refuge {{waypoint 53, 22, 95}}, and the second is by the [[Shrine to the Wurmking]] {{waypoint -82, 57, -49}}.
   - You do not need to kill the mobs at the top of the tree, just engage them all to get the quest update. Once all the mobs are engaged the captive froglok will run.
1. Kill [[A herald of Dar (Tenebrous Tangle)|a herald of Dar]]. It spawns at {{waypoint -12, 47, -185}} when a character on this quest approaches the [[The Temple of Scale (POI)|The Temple of Scale]]. Once killed, examine the [[Scaleborn Temple Note]] that the herald dropped (each party member must do this individually).
1. Kill the [[Soundless Guardian]] {{waypoint -112, 74, -112}}, found at the top of the Shrine to the Wurmking, in order to obtain [[A map (map of the Tenebrous Tangle)|a map]]. To spawn him, clear the [[A hewn stone drake|hewn stone drakes]] on the lower tiers of the pyramid. Once defeated, examine the map.
   - Hold onto the map until you have finished this quest. It is the quest starter for [[Whole Lotta' Holes]], but can only be started once Burglars Afoot has been completed.
1. Inspect the clues at the following locations:
   - [[Gazer Isle (POI)|Gazer Isle]] {{waypoint 1004, 468, -122}}
   - [[Fear Tainted Isle|Fear Tainted Isle (POI)]] {{waypoint 48, 244, 770}}
   - [[Vultak Scavenging Site (POI)|Vultak Scavenging Site]] {{waypoint -712.32, 171.82, -82.82}}
1. Upon inspecting the final clue you receive [[Unseen hand card puzzle]]. To avoid the 15 minute timer, go to the location in step 8 and examine the card to be presented with an eight question quiz:
   - The Circle of the Unseen Hand began its days in the backrooms and broom closets of what tavern?
     - Crow's Tavern
   - Crow's Tavern would later be closed down and sold after the Qeynos Guard discovered it as a front for the Unseen Hand thieves guild. What name, if any, has this tavern been renamed to in present day Qeynos?
     - [[Irontoes East]]
   - Irontoes East was purchased by an immigrant to Qeynos. Who is this immigrant that owns Irontoes's East?
     - [[P.T. Irontoe]]
   - Irontoe's East is named for the tavern called Irontoe's located in what ancient city?
     - [[Kaladim]]
   - What legendary barkeep planted the seeds of the Circle of the Unseen Hand?
     - Crow
   - The Circle of the Unseen Hand currently establishes hidden thieves taverns in most major cities. What is the name of the hidden tavern in Qeynos?
     - [[Crow's Resting Place]] (4th option)
   - The Crow's Resting Place is a branch of the global thieves guild called the Circle of the Unseen hand. Each branch has a leader. Who is the leader of Crow's Resting Place?
     - [[Fippy Darkpaw]]
   - The Circle of the Unseen Hand has begun a secret trade association with many of the brewers in the city of Qeynos. Which merchant is not part of this illicit activity?
     - Ping Fuzzlecutter
1. After answering all questions correctly, you have 15 minutes to inspect the note on the edge of a plank on [[Vicious Breeding Grounds]]. {{waypoint 371, -115, 510}}
1. Kill [[Drippy Darkpaw]] who will spawn once the note is clicked. You receive a key to a chest (which does not show in your inventory).
   - Drippy has has a significant knockback and although it doesn't do much damage, it can knock you off the island. It's best to not stay on the plank after reading the note.
1. Unlock the chest at {{waypoint 563, -147, 641}} to complete the quest.
