This quest is the first of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Drop supplies off at three scout camps near the docks.
   - {{waypoint 526, -0, -50}} on beach
   - {{waypoint 754, -0, -355}} on beach
   - {{waypoint 495, 2, -523}} where the beach path enters the forest
     - *Note: When you inspect each camp (click on the barrel), [[A supply vigilante]] will spawn and attack you. These are level 21 solo mobs. They do not have to be killed, but are a good way to get out your frustration if you've been running around for countless hours looking for these barrels.*
1. Return to Bruhn for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 17s 13c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Acolyte's Wrought Plate Gloves]]
  - [[Bandit's Light Iron Chain Gloves]]
  - [[Beguiler's Embroidered Gloves]]
  - [[Expert's Fine Cloth Hand Wraps]]
  - [[Preserver's Cured Leather Gloves]]
  - [[Sentry's Hammered Gauntlets]]
  - [[Soothsayer's Bound Handguards]]
