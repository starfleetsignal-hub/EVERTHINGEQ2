## Notes

*This quest is repeatable.*

## Steps

1. kill the following
   - Slay 6 [[An undead ravager|undead ravagers]]. ![Undead Ravager](images/Undeadravager.png)
   - Slay 8 of the following *random from quest giver*
     - [[A cadaverous defiler|cadaverous defilers]]![Cadaverous Defiler](images/Cadaverousdefiler.png)<br>or
     - [[A cadaverous maulers|cadaverous maulers]]
1. Return to [[Shohreh]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- Choice of:
  - [[Kamapor Stud of Foresight]]
  - [[Kamapor Hoops of Stratagem]]
