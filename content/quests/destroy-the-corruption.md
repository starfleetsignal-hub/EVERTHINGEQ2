## Steps

1. Destroy [[Rotweed]] in the [[Teardrop Cave]] {{waypoint 907, 16, -180}}
1. Return to [[Empress Anassa]] with proof of your deed.

## Notes

- If you [[Mentor|mentored]] to level 10 and the quest isn't offered, mentor to to level 15 or any higher level and the quest should become available. Origins: You must be level 8 to receive this quest.

- At least 2s 10c
- Choose one of the following:
  - [[Rainbow hued cap]]
  - [[Rainbow hued gloves]]
  - [[Rainbow hued shawl]]
