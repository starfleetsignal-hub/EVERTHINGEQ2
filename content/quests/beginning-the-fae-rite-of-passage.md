This quest is for [[Fae]] only. Non-Fae receive [[An Introduction to the Fae]].

## Steps

1. Go to the [[Kelethin Research Library]] at {{waypoint 141.77, 108.75, 170.48}}
1. Kill the [[Shadow attacker]] that spawns and attacks you
1. Speak with [[Bark Chiselchin]] outside the [[Kelethin Research Library]]
1. Return to [[Lady Katri Eloranta]]

## Rewards

- At least 1s 5c
- One of the following:
  - [[Embroidered Fae Linen Sash]]
  - [[Elegant Fae Vambraces]]

## Notes

- EQ2wire claims this quest was deleted from the game, but as of October 2021 it is still available.
