## Steps

1. Scribe the received recipe book [[Recipe for Grobb-Style Polished Buckles]] then craft 100 [[Polished buckle|polished buckles]]
   - Level 70 tradeskill combine, geomancy skill, forge {{waypoint -362, -65, -1032}}
   - 1 [[Chunk of crude lead]] (received from [[Zubzub]] or if you run out from a box at her feet)
   - 2 [[Ferrite cluster|ferrite clusters]]
   - 2 [[Deklium cluster|deklium clusters]]
   - 5 [[Smoldering coal|smoldering coals]]
   - 5-20 [[Polished buckle|buckles]] per combine
1. Return to [[Zubzub]]

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- At least 79g 24s 78c
- +750 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- Opens up the quest [[Zubzub's Munchies, Part I]] when [[Ship Out]] is turned in
