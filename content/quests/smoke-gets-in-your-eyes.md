1. I must light all the incense around [[Ning Yun Retreat]].

   - The third incense {{waypoint 563, 332, 545}} (behind Zhou Yin)
   - The fourth incense {{waypoint 507, 331, 526}} (on ground next to mat)
   - The fifth incense {{waypoint 518, 333, 475}} (on ground next to Dao Tom <Circling Destiny Faction Merchant>)
1. I should return to [[Tai Wei]].
