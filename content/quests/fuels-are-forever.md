## Steps

![The high quality gear is next to the clockwork.](images/Gear_from_a_destroyed_clockwork.png)

1. Enter the [[Maldura: District of Ash (Heroic)|District of Ash]] {{waypoint -108.05, 1.64, -47.42}}.
   - This step takes place in the [[Maldura: District of Ash (Heroic)]]. All you need is caution, you won't need to fight.
   - The door is in the north side of the [[Sublevel Access Antechamber]] in [[Maldura]].
1. Find the items [[Charles Q Tinkersmith|Tinkersmith]] requires.
   - Harvest a [[High quality gear]] beside **a destroyed clockwork** {{waypoint -84.28, 3.88, -114.59}}, east and down the stairs from the zone-in.
   - Harvest [[High grade coal]] from a coal scuttle across the room [NW] from the zone-in {{waypoint -34.51, 15.91, -161.91}}. Try to harvest it from a distance away to avoid aggro.
   - Harvest the [[Industrial strength solvent]] from the **Barrel of Industrial Strength Solvent**, up the stairs and to the right, southwest of the zone-in {{waypoint 14.78, 33.23, -142.49}}.
1. Return to [[Maldura]] and craft [[Liquid Fuel]] in the **High Pressure Steam Tank** east of [[Charles Q Tinkersmith|Tinkersmith]] {{waypoint 20.04, -7.83, -182.22}} with the [[Liquified Fuels]] recipe you were given when picking up the quest.
1. Bring the [[High quality gear]] and the [[Liquid Fuel]] to [[Charles Q Tinkersmith|Tinkersmith]].

## Needed Materials

- [[Thalumbral root]] 3
- [[Malduran lumber]] 3
- [[Thaumic Coal]] 5

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13200 status
