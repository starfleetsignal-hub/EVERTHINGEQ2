## Steps

1. Speak to [[Barter Lord Tum Jal'Daris]] in [[The Golden Scepter]]. {{waypoint 75, 148, -123}}
1. Complete the following tasks, in no particular order:
   - Destroy 4 Jal'Daris shipping crates in [[Maj'Dul]].
     - One location is behind the tents next to the entrance to Maj'Dul, the second location is behind the Coin faction hall, and the third is in the courtyard surrounding the Golden Scepter at {{waypoint 110, 141, -117}}. You only need to destroy the 4 boxes at one location.
   - Destroy 4 Jal'Daris shipping crates at the [[Port of Tears]] in [[The Sinking Sands]]. {{waypoint -1569, -230, -389}}
1. Return to Barter Lord Tum Jal'Daris.
1. Speak to [[Barter Lord Feth Ardala]] in [[The Golden Scepter]]. {{waypoint 89, 156, -121}}
1. Kill the following:
   - 2 groups of [[A Dervin thug|Dervin thugs]], found around Maj'Dul.
   - [[Bindi the Croc Hunter]], found in [[The Sinking Sands]] around {{waypoint -1331, -227, -583}}. See monster article for more information.
1. Return to Barter Lord Feth Ardala.
1. Enter [[Asadel Yasurbah's Residence]] in Maj'Dul at {{waypoint 125, 144, 102}} and speak to [[Asadel Yasurbah]] once inside.
1. Enter [[Champion's Court: Setting the House in Order]] in Maj'Dul at {{waypoint 27, 137, 211}} and kill [[Perceptor Yezmar]] along with his adds.
1. Return to Asadel Yasurbah in his residence.
1. Speak to [[Neriph, Caliph of the Coin]] in [[The Court of the Coin]].
