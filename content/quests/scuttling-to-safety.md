Charles Q Tinkersmith is trying to establish a special research branch for further development of gnemlin research. He is working on some reinforced armor and needs help obtaining beetle scales for the construction.

## Steps

1. Craft the [[Tinkered Beetle Trap|beetle trapping device]].
   - There is a work bench right beside [[Charles Q Tinkersmith|Tinkersmith]].
1. Trap an [[An armored scuttler|armored scuttler]] in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]] found around {{waypoint 161.7, 79.5, -271}}.
1. Bring the beetle back to [[Charles Q Tinkersmith]]

## Needed Materials

- [[Thaumic material]] (1)
- [[Splitiron ore]] (3)
- [[Umbrite]] (3)
- [[Thaumic Coal]] (5)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Coin of the Grand Artificer]]
- +1,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +5,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
