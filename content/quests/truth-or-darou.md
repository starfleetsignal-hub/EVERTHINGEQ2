## Steps

1. Confront Herd Elder Kernalo's impostor - go to {{waypoint 601, 101, -576}} and speak to Kernalo.
1. Fight him until he changes into  Vadreem, who runs away.
1. Find where Vadreem, the amorphilid, ran off to - go outside the village past the Gnarls, up in Stonemarrow Mountains and into Doomview Highlands. Speak to Vadreem at {{waypoint 110, 173, -84}}
1. Gather evidence of  Vadreem's account of events - find the blade he spoke of at the very back of the safe harvesting area at {{waypoint 202, 70, -269}}
1. Confront Jarkun back in village at {{waypoint 547, 94, -695}}. Kill him when he attacks you.
