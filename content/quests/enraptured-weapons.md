## Steps

1. "I must seek remnants from powerful foes in order to gather the most potent energies that can be used to further improve my weapon". Both of these remnants are guaranteed drops, one per person, if you are on this step.
   - Defeat [[Slither Rattlebones]] in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus: The Hunt (Raid)]] - Slither will drop a [[Slitherbone Fang]].
   - Defeat [[Raaijs Viruniq the Rath'Mana High Priest]] in [[Sandstone Delta: The Standing Storm (Raid)]] - Raajis will drop a [[Tome of Rath'Mana]].
1. Right click the [[Slitherbone Fang]] and 'use' it on the [[Tome of Rath'Mana]]. This will create an [[Imminence of the Regrowth]] that you can 'use' on your Enlivened Weapon, which will upgrade your weapon and complete the quest.
