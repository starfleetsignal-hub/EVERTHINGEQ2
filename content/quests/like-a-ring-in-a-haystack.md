*Lavic N'Gam in [[South Freeport]] wants a ring which last known to be in the possession of a [[The Commonlands]] farmer named Gyberg Thizweald.  Thizweald is dead, however, so now the location of the ring is a mystery.*

## Steps

1. A number of creatures must be killed to see if they have Gyberg's ring.  The exact quantity required of each may vary and appears to be 10-12. These are (in no particular order):
   - 10-12 beetles
     - [[Dung beetle]], [[A stone beetle (Commonlands)|stone beetle]], [[A massive emerald beetle|massive emerald beetle]], [[A hulking fire beetle|hulking fire beetles]],  etc.
   - 10-12 lizards
     - [[A monitor lizard|monitor lizard]] and others
1. Having found nothing on any of them, the next step is to kill 6-8 [[A carrion hound|carrion hounds]] {{waypoint -995, -47, -108}} in hopes one of them swallowed the ring.
1. Finding little of the ring in the carrion hounds, next travel to [[The Tower of Zarvonn]] to look around there {{waypoint 908, -909, -515}}. The update will appear once the "Tower of Zarvonn" location message is on the screen.
1. The ring turns out not to be there either, so it's time to return to Lavic {{waypoint -79, -25, 171}} to report the findings.
