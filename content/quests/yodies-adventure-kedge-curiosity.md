1. Speak to Taffil in Antonica {{waypoint -807, -29, -508}}. Easiest way to get there is to take the bird from the Dock area to the Oracle Tower Station.
   - Taffil grants you the Overseer Quest:- [[Yodie's Adventure - Kedge Curiosity]] which auto scribes.
   - Open your Overseer tab and start the Overseer Quest. It takes 30 minutes to complete.
1. Completion grants you the next quest:  [[Yodie's Adventure - Merchant of Trouble]].
