This quest is part 3 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Kill [[A Nerius Tactician]] and [[A Nerius Commander]]. They are found next to the attack plans from the previous quest. {{waypoint 932, 0, -326}}
1. Return to [[Bruhn K'Viir]] for your reward.
1. Read [[The History of the Order of Nektulos]], the book received as a reward, to continue the quest series.

## Rewards

- [[The History of the Order of Nektulos]]
- At least 22s 51c
