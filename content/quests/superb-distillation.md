Create 2 each of any 6 from the random list below:

- [[Superb Arcane Remedy]]
- [[Superb Elixir of Constitution]]
- [[Superb Elixir of Transcendence]]
- [[Superb Noxious Remedy]]
- [[Superb Caustic Poison]]
- [[Superb Hemotoxin]]
- [[Superb Warding Ebb]]
- [[Manhandle II|Manhandle II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Slam II|Slam II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Holy Aid III|Holy Aid III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Rising Dragon II|Rising Dragon II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Challenge III|Challenge III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Abandoned Fury III|Abandoned Fury III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Blasphemy III|Blasphemy III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Righteousness III|Righteousness III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Shout III|Shout III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Enrage III|Enrage III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Shadow Coil II|Shadow Coil II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Unholy Hunger III|Unholy Hunger III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Calm Tranquility III|Calm Tranquility III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Brutality III|Brutality III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Bloodlust III|Bloodlust III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Abuse III|Abuse III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Call of Shielding II|Call of Shielding II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Merciless Stomp III|Merciless Stomp III (Journeyman)]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 24s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
