## Steps

![a brimming fish basket](images/Brimming_Fish_Basket.jpg)
![A goblish weapon rack](images/A_goblish_weapon_rack.jpg)

1. Poison 6 fish baskets of the icegill fishers. Do this by targeting *a brimming fish basket*, found randomly on the islands of [[Icegill Lagoon]], and using the [[A stinking fish poison|stinking fish poison]] that you received from Rainscour. A couple sample locations are:
   - {{waypoint 501, -36, -1455}}
   - {{waypoint 411, -35, -1337}}
1. Destroy 6 weapon caches that they are delivering to the Kromise. Do this by targeting *a goblish weapon rack*, found randomly on the islands of [[Icegill Lagoon]], and using the [[A burn burn stick]] that you received from Rainscour.
