## Description

The Hua Mein Craftsman has requested assistance in gathering shiny shells for the Hua Mein craftsmen to craft with.

## Steps

![shiny shell](images/Shiny_shell.png)

1. I have been asked to gather some shiny shells for the Hua Mein craftsmen to use in their crafting. (Every Friday)
   - Gather 15 [[Shiny shell|shells]] from the bottom of the lake. These look like the bottom half of a clam shell and are non-targetable with tabbing, but you'll get the hand cursor to know what to gather.
1. Return to [[Xi Lan]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,000 faction with **Hua Mein**
- +2,500 faction with **Hua Mein Craftsmen**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Hua Mein Daily Supply Tasks
