## Notes

- You must be level 55 to complete this quest.

## Steps

![](images/A_jumper_%28Visible%29.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Grumglub]]. {{waypoint 60, 22, 208}}
1. Find and grab 4 [[A jumper|jumpers]], small frogs hopping around the banks of the pond at [[The Temple Grounds]]. {{waypoint 12, 45, -64}}
1. Return to Grumglub for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 9g 27s 1c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Carved Wand of Divining]] *(All Fighters, All Mages, All Priests, Beastlord)*
  - [[Glyphed Crusader's Blade]] *(Berserker, Guardian, Paladin, Shadowknight)*
  - [[Jagged Mace from the Tangle]] *(All Priests)*
  - [[Katar of the Nocuous]] *(Beastlord, Bruiser, Monk)*
  - [[Lamentation of the Wild]] *(Channeler, Fury, Warden)*
  - [[Thirstquencher]] *(All Mages, All Scouts, Berserker, Channeler, Defiler, Guardian, Mystic, Paladin, Shadowknight)*
  - [[Twilight Magician's Stiletto]] *( All Mages, All Scouts, Berserker, Channeler, Defiler, Guardian, Mystic, Paladin, Shadowknight)*
