## Steps

1. Scout out the north coast of [[The Lowland Basin|the Lowland Basin]] past the vermin cave
   - Go to {{waypoint 1369, -104, 2943}} in [[The Lowland Basin|the Lowland Basin]].  You will find [[A kejekan spy]], who will vanish as you approach.
     - *Note: This DOES NOT update for the whole group.*
     - *Note: the spy has a repop of 30 seconds you may have to approach him again to make him spawn*
1. Return to Fazia.

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**
