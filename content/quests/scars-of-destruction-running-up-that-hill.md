## Steps

NOTE: Finishing [[Thekror|Thekrors]] Questline up to [[Appeasing the Inside Voice|Appeasing the inside Voice]] will unlock flight in Western Wastes - this makes all following steps a lot easier/faster.

1. Survey the ships from the rocky outcrop above the Remnant Shores camp at {{waypoint 90.89, 59.93, 605.54}}
1. Survey the area from the ridgetop north of the Remnant Shores camp at {{waypoint -38.26, 61.24, 433.07}}
1. Go to {{waypoint -420.50, -31.29, 469.75}} in the [[Charnel Scar]] of the [[Western Wastes]]. Path down starts at  {{waypoint 243, 43, 334}}
1. Destroy the [[A coalesced gladiator]]
1. Kill another 2 [[A coalesced gladiator|coalesced gladiators]]
1. Return to [[Elna Tsernin (Western Wastes)|Elna Tsernin]] at [[The Remnant Shores]] {{waypoint 77.81, 8.23, 648.26}}

## Rewards

- 2 x [[Weathered Dragon Tooth]] house items
- 50 [[Scarred Dinar]]
- 10 [[Velium Drachma]]
- At least 4p 36g 48s 99c
