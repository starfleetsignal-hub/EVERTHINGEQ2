*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment*

## Steps

1. [[Catalogue Creature|Catalog]] the following creatures, which are all found to the east, along [[The Anaconda River]]:
   - [[A forest creep]] {{waypoint -967, -9, 857}}
   - [[A pungent recluse]] {{waypoint -573, 1, 151}}
   - [[A swamp feeder]] {{waypoint -554, -3, 684}}
   - [[A thule sattar]] {{waypoint -375, -2, 249}}
   - [[A blighted water skater]] {{waypoint -433, -5, 191}}
1. Return to Tarin to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 99s 67c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Defender's Sash of Fear]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Healer's Sash of Fear]] (All Priests)
  - [[Medium's Sash of Fear]] (All Mages)
  - [[Stalker's Sash of Fear]] (All Scouts)
