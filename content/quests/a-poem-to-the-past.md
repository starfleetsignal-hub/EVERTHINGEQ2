## Prerequisites

- You must understand the [[Koada'Dal (Language)|Koada'Dal]] language to begin this subquest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- This is a subquest of [[A Crusade to Faydwer]].

## Steps

![A koada rose, glowing near the base of a tree.](images/Koadarose.jpg)

1. Collect the following:
   - 5 coins from [[A Tunarian guard|Tunarian guards]], an uncommon update. I had to mentor down to 60 to get updates.
   - 12 *koada blooms*, which appear as glowing flowers found around any trees in the dungeon. See the talk page for a list of known spawns. These are trackable...
   - 4 *gazes of Koda'Vie* from [[A sentry of righteousness|sentries of righteousness]], who patrol the area around the [[Temple of Growth]]. {{waypoint -379, 53, -858}}
1. Sacrifice the ingredients you collected at the [[Flame of the Star]], one of the two large braziers on the steps outside the [[Hall of Truth]]. {{waypoint -397, 53, -831}}
1. Talk to [[A vision of General Jyleel]].

## Reward

- *Investigator's Handbook*
