## Steps

1. Gather deinodon bone - go to the [[Tar Pits of Inevitability]] {{waypoint -938, 30, 305}} and click on any of the big bones sticking out of the tar pits.
1. Craft the skinning knife - go back to South Dshinn and click the table next to [[Hyggin]] {{waypoint -706, 9, 287}}
1. Speak to [[Kilwren]] {{waypoint -627, 34, 143}} to bless the skinning knife
1. Kill and skin an aged deinodon - find [[An aged apatodon]] (one possible location: {{waypoint -1274, 12, 343}})

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
- [[Pygmy Skull Banner]]
