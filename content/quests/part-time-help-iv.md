## Description

"Matstyr Alesmith, a hard working Dwarf that bases his operation at Spire Shadow Outpost, has offered me some part time work. My assignment is to bring him some large boulders so he can build a more permanent residence." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay 6 [[A rotting guardian|rotting guardians]] near the [[Upper Emerald River]] around {{waypoint -627, 47, 91}} until you find 10 boulders
   - Quest says to kill [[Decaying guardian|decaying guardians]] or [[A rotting guardian|rotting guardians]], however [[A rotting guardian|rotting guardians]] are closer and of a lower level than [[Decaying guardian|decaying guardians]].
1. Return to [[Matstyr Alesmith]]

## Rewards

- Experience
- At least 11s 13c

## Journal Steps

I need to gather guardian boulders for Matstyr. I can find the boulders on decaying guardians and rotting guardians. I need to gather ten guardian boulders.

I have enough boulders. I have the boulders for Matstyr. I need to return these boulders to Matstyr, at Spire Shadow Outpost. I should return these boulders to Matsyr.

I returned the boulders to Matstyr. I have returned the boulders to Matstyr.

I returned to Matstyr Alesmith with the boulders. He was quite pleased.
