## Description

[[Garby Gingham Gigglegibber]] doesn't seem to know when to give up! In response to Tobel's second prank, Garby has asked that I secretly switch all the yummy halfling pies with a sack of "yucky" gold coins. Though I fail to see what makes this an actual "prank", it's amusing nonetheless and I do believe that is precisely what Bristlebane would find so brilliant about this task.

## Steps

![An Idol of Good Fortune](images/Idol_of_good_fortune.jpg)

1. Get [[A Sack of Gold Coins]] from [[Garby Gingham Gigglegibber]].
1. Speak with [[Roselia Goldencrust]] and try to convince her to leave her pie stand. Regardless of the dialogue options you choose, she will not leave her stand. {{waypoint 39, 1.5, 36}}
1. Return to Garby.
1. Go to [[The Timorous Moor]] in [[Loping Plains]] and harvest Garby's [[Idol of Good Fortune]].
   - The item can spawn in several location in the Timorous Moor, and is trackable using [[Track Harvestables]]. Confirmed locations are {{waypoint 95, -3, 384}}, {{waypoint 24, -4, 337}}, or {{waypoint -134, -5, 494}}.
1. Give the Idol of Good Fortune to Roselia Goldencrust and she will leave her pie stand.
1. Grab the 4 pies from her table and replace it with the [[A Sack of Gold Coins|sack of gold coins]].
1. Speak to Roselia Goldencrust again.
1. Return to Garby.
