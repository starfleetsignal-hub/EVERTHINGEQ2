## Steps

1. Frighten [[A skittish scarab|skittish scarabs]] into the tarantulas to obtain 18 pristine carapaces.
1. Return to [[Xilla Beetlebinder]] to receive your reward.

  - [[Sensei's Gi]]
  - [[Spiritualist's Hauberk]]
  - [[Thaumaturge's Robe]]
  - [[Vagabond's Chain Tunic]]
