## Steps

Kill the following enemies

- Bury the Vault Keeper : Kill [[Korgo the Vault Keeper]] in [[The Vaults of El'Arad]] (Barren Sky, Isle of Awakening)
- Crush the Chamberlain  : Kill [[Chamberlain X'Haviz]] in [[The Nest of the Great Egg]] (Barren Sky, Whisperwind)
- Down the Doom Lord: Kill [[Doom Lord Septimus]] in [[The Halls of Fate]] (Bonemire, HoF Island)

## Notes

Mission updates for killing a required target are zone-wide in instanced zones. Individual group members do not need to be near the target when it is killed; the only requirement is that they must be in the same instance as other members of the group.

## Rewards

- Legend Hunting Crate
- At least 15600 status
