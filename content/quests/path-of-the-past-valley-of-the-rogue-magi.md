## Steps

1. Defeat [[Magus Geofry Marus]] in the [[Valley of the Rogue Magi]]
1. Return to the Chronomagi Tasker

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Chronomancers**
- The ability to purchase [[Replica: Naerius D'Lyle]] from [[Timeless Trader]] for 1p 8g

## Note

The access quest for this instance ([[The Secret Valley]]) is started by inspecting a poster in the middle of The Crossroads. This can be problematic for Non-Evil characters because of the level 30 Evil-aligned Guard standing right next to the quest starter.  Players need to either grab the quest starter BEFORE Chrono'ing down to the appropriate level or be prepared to be 'beat on' by the guards while trying to read the quest starter to access the quest.
