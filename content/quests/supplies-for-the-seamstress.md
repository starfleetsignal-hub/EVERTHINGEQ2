## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable up to 2 times..*
- You must be level 60 or below to be offered this quest.

## Steps

1. Obtain items by killing 4-8 of one type of monster (randomly chosen) in [[The Pillars of Flame]]. Some possible targets are:
   - [[A Windsister jaggedfeather|Windsister jaggedfeather]]s
   - [[A blanketfish|blanketfish]]
   - [[A sandstalker sabertooth|sandstalker sabertooth]]s
   - Sandscrawler goblins
1. Return to Darzi Sendoa.

## Rewards

- At least 6g 19s 1c
