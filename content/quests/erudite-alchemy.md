This quest is part 1 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Erudite Series.

## Steps

1. Harvest 5 Fe'lein Herbs which grow scattered around in Stonestair Byway.
1. Grind the herbs in the pestle in the scribe shop {{waypoint -48, 0, -62}}.
1. Return to [[Vess Al'Ishni]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s
- 2 Abbatoir coffee (drink)
- 2 Abbatoir herbal cake (food)
