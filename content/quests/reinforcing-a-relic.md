## Steps

1. Talk to [[Hwal Rucksif]] in [[The Keep of the Ardent Needle (POI)|The Keep of the Ardent Needle]] in [[Antonica]].
1. Bring back the [[Dwarven Work Boots]] (received from the Heritage quest [[These Boots Were Made For...]]) and hail Hwal again.
   - *Note: these must not be the house item, they must be the wearable version.*
   - If you sold your boots, you can buy a new pair from the [[Shady Swashbuckler XVIII]] on the [[Nektulos Forest]] docks for the same price you sold yours for.

## Reward

Your choice of:

- [[Reinforced Heavy Dwarven Work Boots]]
- [[Reinforced Medium Dwarven Work Boots]]
- [[Reinforced Light Dwarven Work Boots]]
- [[Reinforced Very Light Dwarven Work Boots]]

These newly remade boots can no longer be turned into a furniture item; for 7g 20s, however, you may buy another pair of original Dwarven Work Boots from Shady Swashbuckler XVIII and turn them into your house item.
