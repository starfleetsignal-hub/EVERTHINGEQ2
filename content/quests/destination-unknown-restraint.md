## Notes

1. This quest requires:
   - 5x Formless Coal

## Steps

![Tenacious Rothweed to gather as intact weed](images/Tenacious_Rothweed.png)
![Gorroth stone](images/Gorroth_stone.png)

1. Speak with [[Anefa]] {{waypoint 546.03, 94.65, -677.16}}
1. Speak with [[Nankane]] at {{waypoint 487, 58, -312}} outside of village in [[Managauth Fields]]
1. Gather 12 of the intact [[Tenacious Rothweed|Tenacious Rothweeds]]. Some weeds will be too destroyed by insects to be of use and will not update quest.
1. Collect a [[Gorroth Stone]]. Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint 479, 61, -351}}; {{waypoint 339, 69, -318}}
   - {{waypoint 348, 69, -267}}; {{waypoint 365, 68, -247}}
   - {{waypoint 342, 69, -288}}; {{waypoint 342, 68, -358}}
1. Speak with [[Nankane]]. {{waypoint 487, 58, -312}}
1. Speak with [[Flamebearer Denat]] to receive a [[Darou Rune]] and [[Recipe Scroll: W.H.E.R.E. V1]] that is autoscribed.
1. Create [[W.H.E.R.E. V1]].
   - Requires 5x [[Formless Coal]] and a [[Work Bench]], you can use Grandmaster Artisan's Crafting Nook in [[Darou Village]] at {{waypoint 498, 83, -650}}.
   - There is a Fuel Merchant in the vicinity of the crafting table
1. Speak with [[Anefa]] {{waypoint 546.03, 94.65, -677.16}}

## Rewards

- [[Status Coin (75,000)]]
