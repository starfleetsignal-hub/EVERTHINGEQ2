## Steps

1. Speak to [[Neeta Cabbageleaf]] at {{waypoint -62, -17, -9}} inside The Fools Gold in [[Rivervale]] to obtain the quest."
1. After talking to Neeta again, you will be ported to into [[The Tower of the Drafling: Tradeskill]].
   - The zone scales to your adventure level and the bixies will be 10-15 levels higher than you.

![bixie hive sentry (as viewed by L110 Adv)](images/Bixie_hive_sentry.jpg)![stunned-bixie hive sentry](images/Stunned-bixie_hive_sentry.jpg)

1. Use the [[Soporific Dispensorator]] to put the bixies to sleep and gather jumjum from the fountain in the middle of the room at {{waypoint -72, 62, -24}}. ![Jumjum Fountain](images/Jumjum_Fountain.jpg)
1. Use the [[Cabbageleaf Teleportation Crystal]] (the icon looks like a necklace) to return to Neeta.
1. Speak with Neeta to complete the quest.

## Reward

- At least 1p 35g

## Notes

- This is a solo quest. If you are in a group, you'll be removed from it. Mercenaries are allowed.
- It's recommended you move the [[Soporific Dispensorator]] to your hotbar, as you'll use it often in this quest.
  - This item sets off a smoke bomb that puts the bixies to sleep so you can pass by them safely. Note that it doesn't just stun them, it turns them into non-aggressive mobs that cannot be attacked.
  - Its cooldown is minimal, so use it frequently; run back into the smoke if you get too close to a bixie that was hiding around a corner.
- If you are adventure level 100+ you can simply run to the fountain, kill any bixies who've followed you and collect the jumjum without using the Soporific Dispensorator.
- Proceed carefully, but if you do get killed you will revive back in [[Neeta Cabbageleaf]].
- The soporific will be in your bags when you zone into the tower.
