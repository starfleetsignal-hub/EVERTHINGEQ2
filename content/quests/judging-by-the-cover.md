## Steps

1. Gather damaged books on the [[Myrist Mezzanine]] and in the [[South Wing Catalog]]
   - Gather 5 books with broken spines.
   - Gather 4 books with decayed bindings.
   - Gather 5 books with ruined covers.
1. Return to [[Librarian Rashasha]] at {{waypoint 255, 16, 120}}.
