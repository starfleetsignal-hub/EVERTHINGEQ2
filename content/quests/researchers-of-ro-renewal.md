*Firiona Vie had sent a request for some Growth Prayer stones for a major ceremony. I must craft them and bring them to her as soon as possible.*

## Materials Required

- Either harvest or otherwise acquire:
  - 35 [[Apatite Gem]]
  - 25 [[Pallasite Ore]]
  - 15 [[Brittlebush Taproot]]
  - 10 [[Dustmaw Hide]]
  - 1 Armapine Hide (Rare Hide)
- Bring along this fuel (or use a summon fuel merchant item):
  - 25 [[Astral Coal]]
  - 5 [[Astral Incense]]

## Steps

1. Craft 5 [[Growth Prayer Stone|Growth Prayer Stones]] from the [[Recipe Scroll: Renewal Recipes]] (auto-scribed when you accept quest) on the Work Bench behind Bairam at {{waypoint 150, -60, 580}}. For each combine you will need:
   - 7 [[Apatite Gem]] (35 total)
   - 5 [[Pallasite Ore]] (25 total)
   - 5 [[Astral Coal]] (25 total)
1. Speak to [[Firiona Vie]] in [[Goldami Reservoir Camp]] at {{waypoint -552, 10, 435}}[[Raj'Dur Plateaus]].
1. Enter [[Takish'Hiz Arena: Renewal of Ro (Tradeskill)|Takish'Hiz Arena: Renewal of Ro [Tradeskill]]] through the portal next to Firiona at {{waypoint -545, 10, 429}}.
1. Place the five growth stones you crafted by clicking on the glowing outlines at:![Growth Prayer Stone Location](images/Growth_Prayer_Stone.png)
   - {{waypoint -52, 140, -827}}
   - {{waypoint -120, 140, -829}}
   - {{waypoint -140, 140, -894}}
   - {{waypoint -83, 140, -930}}
   - {{waypoint -28, 140, -866}}
1. Speak to Firiona Vie inside the arena at {{waypoint -79, 140, -838}}.
   - **NOTE:  Entire event provides a group-wide update. Only one member needs to hail Firiona Vie and 'deal with' the cursed Elddar remnants, using either of the approved methods below.**
1. Witness the ceremony:
   - Watch the surprise appearance during the ceremony and wait as the unexpected visitor does his monologue.
   - Deal with the [[A cursed Elddar remnant]]'s. There are two ways to do this:
     1. If you're a level 125 adventurer, just kill them. They're level 129^ and 130^, but have less health than usual. (There are 8 groups of 3 to dispose of)
     1. Low-adventure-level crafters: Find and click on a [[Growth Blossom]] to get 50 Growth Spores, then run round the perimeter of the arena collecting Growth Roots. Use the roots to root 2 of each linked encounter then use growth spores on the third. Then you can take out the two rooted-but-aggro ones with more spores. Rinse & repeat. ![Growth Blossom](images/Growth_Blossom.png)
        - *Growth Blossoms spawn in or near the flower arrangements scattered round the zone. There can be up to three of these, but only one is required. Possible locations:  {{waypoint -92, 140, -826}},  {{waypoint -127, 140, -826}},   {{waypoint -135, 140, -871}}*
        - *Hint: the Growth Roots and Blossoms spawn when the plants do, and can be harvested immediately, before any Cursed Elddar appear. This is much safer for lowbie crafters, and helps speed things up a bit as well.*
   - Watch the rest of the events until the quest updates again.
1. Return to [[Bairam Najafi]] at {{waypoint 149.78, -60.17, 583.20}} in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]].
1. Craft [[Events of the Renewal Ceremony]] tome on the Work Bench behind Bairam Najafi at {{waypoint 150, -60, 580}}. You will need:
   - 1 Armapine Hide
   - 15 Brittlebush Taproot
   - 10 Dustmaw Hide
   - 5 Astral Incense
1. Speak to [[Bairam Najafi]] at {{waypoint 149.78, -60.17, 583.20}} in the [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]]. to complete the quest.
