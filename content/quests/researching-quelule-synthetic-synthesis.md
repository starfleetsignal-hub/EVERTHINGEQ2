## Steps

1. [[Researcher Tahar]], in what remains of [[Quel'ule]], has asked me to try to create a synthetic kaborite crystal by channeling power through the amber lens into the altered minerals I collected. An undertaking which may be highly dangerous...
   - Scribe the [[Compendium of Mineral Refining Techniques]] from your inventory and craft a Synthetic Kaborite Ward at a [[Work Bench]] using the synthetic karborite recipe. Doing so requires an [[Altered mineral]] (provided), 2 [[Quicksilver cluster]], the [[Amber lens]] (provided) and 1 [[Ethereal coal]].
   - If you fail to properly counter an event, you will die unless you stop the recipe before the next pulse. If this happens, you can get a new amber lens and material from the boxes on the desk next to [[Researcher Tahar]].
1. Return to [[Researcher Tahar]].

## Rewards

- At least 60g 50s 55c
- +3000 faction with **Researchers of Quel'ule**
- A house item version of the [[Synthetic Kaborite Gem]].

## Notes

- You can continue to use the material and amber lens boxes to craft a house item version of the ward different from the kaborite gem.
