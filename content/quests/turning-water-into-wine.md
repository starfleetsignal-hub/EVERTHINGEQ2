## Description

"Lasarian Nasin's stock of expensive port is nearly depleted because the Militia has confiscated the latest shipment.  To ensure that the Coalition of Tradesfolke can still meet demands, Lasarian watered down what little port he had to artificially increase the volume.  No one closely related to Lasarian can sell it, to avoid tarnishing his reputation as a purveyor of fine goods. He's asking me to deliver the goods and personally accept the blame if anyone realizes they're being cheated."  *--Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Painter Brushwuttle]] on the south side of the Coliseum {{waypoint 197, -3, 72}}
1. Speak to [[Surabaya]] in front of the guild hall in South Freeport {{waypoint -32, -7, 191}}; defeat him (even con)
1. Speak to [[Metilia Arcavius]] in front of [[The Freeport Reserve]] in West Freeport {{waypoint 217, -3, -94}}
1. Speak to [[Speculator Montanlooa]] on the South Freeport docks {{waypoint -249, -56, 205}}; defeat her (even con)
1. Return to Lasarian Nasin.

## Conclusion

"I had to endure a few fights with buyers, but I managed to unload all of Lasarian Nasin's watered-down wine."  *--Everquest II Journal*
