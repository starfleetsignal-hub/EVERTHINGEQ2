## Steps

1. Kill [[A deathly scarab (Crypt of Betrayal)|deathly scarabs]] until you have 15 antennae (common update).
1. Return the antennae to Maglus.
1. Enter [[The Deathly Scarab Hive]], which is right above Maglus.
   - Collect 5 deathly scarab antennae from [[Deathly scarab worker|deathly scarab workers]].
   - Kill the queen, [[Queen Ankhestara]], which will spawn along with 5 deathly scarab broodlings(25v Heroic), after you have killed all the guardians.
1. Exit the hive and return to Maglus.

## Notes

- This quest is not labeled heroic in your quest journal, but it involves fighting heroic monsters. Venturing into the hive with fellow adventurers (or at least a friendly healer) might be a smart idea.
