## Steps

1. Look for Hazrem's squad in the northeast square of the contested Covenant District. This updates when you reach {{waypoint -274, -0, -255}}
1. Slay Yorlig the Hunter at {{waypoint -300, -1, -285}}
1. Return to [[Hazrem]] to complete the quest.
