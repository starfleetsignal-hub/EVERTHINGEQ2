Being a gnemlin has not stopped Bynn Cloingborn from looking up to Gilzin Bluefist, a dwarf of significant importance from Maldura's ancient  past. According to local legend he opened dwarven eyes to the potential in magic and arcane augmentation, allowing for mages to become a respected class within Maldura.

## Steps

1. Receive [[Cloingborn's thaumaturgic actuator]] from [[Bynn Cloingborn|Bynn]].
1. Fill the actuator with 6 [[Gilzin's lingering magic]] at [[Bluefist's Battlefield]] in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]] {{waypoint 297.62, 81.41, -456.99}}.
   - Each time you collect magic, [[A risen miner|risen miners]], [[A risen warsmith|risen warsmiths]], [[A risen guardian|risen guardians]] etc. may attack you.
1. Return to [[Bynn Cloingborn]].
