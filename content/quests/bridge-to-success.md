## Notes

- you need to able to speak [[Goblish]] to speak to Kutgurk
- you **must have started** [[Kunark Ascending: A Chosen Weapon]] for Kutgurk to appear.

## Steps

![](images/Warsilk_rabbits_foot.jpg)
![](images/Luclinite_shard.jpg)

1. Confront [[Gabrowg]] {{waypoint 172, -204, -517}}  As soon as you get close, he'll send you flying - he has some kind of magic shield protecting him.
1. Find a way to defeat [[Gabrowg]]. Talk to [[Kutgurk]] at {{waypoint 145, -218, -434}} (the game doesn't prompt you to do this) and he'll give you two ideas .
   - "fresh hopper's feet" - Find a rabbit.  There are a couple on the slopes north of [[Vrassath]] {{waypoint 579, -171, -412}}.  This is not a one kill, get loot.  I had to kill 3 rabbits to get the rabbit foot.  Right click it, [[An ironbelly rabbit (Obulus Frontier)|select Attack]], and loot [[Warslik rabbit foot]] from its corpse.
   - "spinn'-gem shards" - go to [[Luclinite Chunk]] {{waypoint 340, -245, -274}} (follow the river south from the bridge), kill [[A luclinite cluster]], and loot [[Luclinite shard]] from it.
1. Talk to Gabrowg and refuse to pay him.  He'll attack.  [[Grabrowg (Named)|Kill him]].  He has a knockback, so get underneath his tent.
1. Return to Kutgurk {{waypoint 145, -218, -434}}

## Rewards

- [[Intricate Luclinite Band]]
- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 15108 status
