## Description

"Princess Zhyleen would like me to help cure the saplings. I am to travel just outside of Tunare's Glade. There will be two flowers at the glade's entrance, each containing a curative warden spirit. I should coax a spirit from its flower then use it to cleanse the saplings in the glade." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Examine the flower lamp at entrance to [[Tunare's Glade]] at {{waypoint -282.59, 35.27, 317.29}}
   - You will receive a [[Curative Warden]].
1. Use the Curative Warden on 10 glade shrublings by examining the shrubs. (these are the bushes all around the glade. Right click them and pick release Warden)
1. Return to [[Princess Zhyleen]]
