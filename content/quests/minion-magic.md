## Steps

1. Search [[Hold of Rime: The Fortress Spire]] for instructions on how to summon minions.
   - Right click and search the pockets of "an unconscious flurryburst guard" that is on the floor at the top of the first ramp in the mess hall {{waypoint -184, 15, -34}}
1. Collect 5 Rime talismans.
   - Each Rime soldier from this point on will drop [[A Rime Pack]].  Right-click the pack while it's in your inventory, and choose Unpack.  This will place a [[Cabbage Stew (Crafted)|Cabbage Stew]] and a [[Rime Talisman]] in your inventory.  You need to unpack 5 packs in order to receive the 5 talisman updates.
1. Press the button in the Fortress Control Room
1. Kill [[Frigorific]] 96 ^^^ heroic - easy kill

## Rewards

- At least 41g 63s 17c
