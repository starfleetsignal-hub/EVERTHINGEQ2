## Steps

![Click the Tunare water plants to activate them](images/Tunare_water_plant.jpg)
![Place the roots at sparkling spots in the pond](images/Daarspire_etherflow_root_location.jpg)

1. Gather 6 [[Tunare water root|roots]] from the [[Tunare water plant|Tunare water plants]]. Click the plant to make it active, then slay it and loot the root.
1. Transport the 6 roots to the Daarspire etherflow pool (around  {{waypoint -110, 199, 66}}). Look for sparkling places in the water, and click them to place a root.
1. Return to Wuoshi

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Golden Icon of the Scale Temple]] -- *Charm item*
