## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 40+

## Steps

1. Head to [[Qeynos Harbor]] in the [[Qeynos Province District]] and speak with [[Captain Sellia]] on the docks {{waypoint 966, -25, 11}}.  You will be zoned into [[The Blue Sunrise]]
1. Speak with [[Field Marshal Vishra (The Blue Sunrise)|Field Marshal Vishra]].
1. Defeat 6 [[A Siren's Folly cutthroat|Siren's Folly cutthroat]]s
1. Extinguish 4 fires on the deck - left click on the water buckets and then left click on any fire
1. Defeat 6 more Siren's Folly cutthroats
   - Defeat [[Captain Hookwind (mob)|Captain Hookwind]].
1. Speak to [[Queen Antonia Bayle (The Blue Sunrise)|Queen Antonia Bayle]].
1. Return to [[Field Marshal Vishra]] at [[The Sovereign House of Qeynos]] in [[South Qeynos (POI)|South Qeynos]] ([[Qeynos Capitol District]]).

## Rewards

- [[Pennant of the Celestial Watch]]
- 25g 70s 61c (at level 90)
