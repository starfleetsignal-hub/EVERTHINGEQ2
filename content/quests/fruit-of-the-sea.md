## Steps

1. Kill the following monsters:
   - Kill 4 sharks ([[A Gunthak razortooth|Gunthak razortooths]]).
   - Kill 4 octopi ([[A Ykeshan cephalopod|Ykeshan cephalopods]]).
   - Kill 4 seaturtles ([[A Ykeshan saltshell|Ykeshan saltshells]], around {{waypoint -1073, -1100, 1260}} or {{waypoint -935, -1120, 1900}}) or {{waypoint -1805, -1112, 628}}).
   - Kill 4 crabs ([[A Ykeshan snapper|Ykeshan snappers]], around {{waypoint -1081, -1093, 1196}}).
1. Return to [[Klip Marrsquire]].

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +1200 faction with **Survival_Accord**

## Notes

- Suggestion: Get the quest [[Getting Inked]] at the same time, kill the Octopi first (need 5 for other quest) then return that quest and get [[Grandpa Hates Sharks!]].
