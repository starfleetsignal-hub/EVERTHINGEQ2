## Steps

![An undead rift (targeted)](images/An_undead_rift.png)

1. Enter [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum]]
1. Speak to [[Oomitelmora]]
1. Destroy 5 undead rifts
   - Oomitelmora gives you [[Oomitelmora's Disintegration Vials]].  Despite the plural name it's an unlimited usage charm.
   - The rifts are impossible-to-miss green and black rifts in the ground.  As you approach a rift, it will spawn a [[A ghastly specter (Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum)|ghastly specter]] or [[A ghastly knight (Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum)|ghastly knight]] (scales, vv).  Each rift is also guarded by [[A portal guardian (Starcrest Commune)|a portal guardian]] (scales, even), which technically does not need to be killed but will aggro players trying to get close enough to the portal to close it.
   - Once the ghastly specter/knight has been killed, the rift becomes targetable.  Stand close to it and use the Disintegration Vial.
1. Speak with Oomitelmora
1. Return to Qeynos Capitol District and speak with Lakosha Maera.
