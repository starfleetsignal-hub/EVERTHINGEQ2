## Steps

1. Take western lift and go to {{waypoint 2030, -162, 3361}}(The lift switch is the little black ball in the small triangle.)
   - *Prepare to be attacked by a group of 5 level 76 ratongas, (2 down arrows each), soloable.*
1. Kill 3 attackers
1. Return to Captain Elbaz. (If you can float then you can jump off the cliff edge and float down to Elbaz below in the city).

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
