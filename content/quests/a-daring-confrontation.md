## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Right click on the door next to the quest giver and select the option to go 'through the door'. You will enter the instance [[The Eternal Gorge: A Daring Confrontation]].
1. Kill all the erudite sisters, one located in each building.
1. Kill [[Mother Znedari]].

- 5 [[City Token|City Tokens]]
