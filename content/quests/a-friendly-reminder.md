## Steps

1. Speak to [[Kronryn N'Xytyl]] {{waypoint -377, -46, -75}}
   - His ghost is on the top of the incomplete Griffon Tower that can be seen to the northeast from [[T'Vatar Post]]
   - He will become aggro and attack you. Kill him.
1. Return to [[Laexyra Y'Barriath]]

## Rewards

- At least 92c
