## Steps

1. Speak to [[The Duality]] at {{waypoint 1971, 480, -876}} followed by a quest update.
1. Start the ritual by speaking to [[The Duality]] again and wait for the next quest update.
1. Speak to [[The Duality]] a third time.
1. Use the Spire Portal at {{waypoint 1976, 480, -838}} to travel to [[The Blinding|The Blinding (Luclin)]]. *Note: Top center of map.*
