## Steps

1. Fly to [[Isle of Awakening]]
   1. Kill 5 [[A warscale decimator|warscale decimators]] {{waypoint 541, 186, 303}}
   1. Kill [[A starving warscale drake]] {{waypoint 443, 183, 339}}
   1. Kill [[Wings of War]] (be carefull : knock back)
      - *Note the steps must be done in order or you will have to wait for respawns*
      - The creatures to be killed can be identified by a feather to the right of their name
1. Return to [[Clarissa Cloudpuff]] on the [[Isle of Desolation]].

## Rewards

- At least 14g 81s 38c
- [[Warscale Wing Trophy]]
