## Prerequisites

- Crafters are needed in order for this Public Quest to be successful.

## Starting the Public Quest

- [[A Dracurion Stoneweaver|Dracurion Stoneweavers]] will hover over the large [[The Iksar Hero|Iksar Hero]] statue outside of the [[Danak Shipyards]] {{waypoint 204, 54, 1260}}, signaling that the Public Quest will be starting shortly.
- [[A Danak messenger]] will ride through [[Jarsath Wastes]], stopping at {{waypoint 122, -40, 1137}} when the [[The Iksar Hero|Iksar Hero]] is about to spawn.

## Steps

1. Destroy [[The Iksar Hero]].
   - Once spawned, the [[The Iksar Hero|Iksar Hero]] will follow the path from just outside the [[Danak Shipyards]] {{waypoint 204, 54, 1260}} to [[Ganak's Battlement]] {{waypoint -243, -43, -60}}.
     - The statue moves slowly, giving time to gather reinforcements.
     - [[The Iksar Hero]] can only be damaged when it is shot by a ballista bolt, then kneels.
   - Repair each ballista before the [[The Iksar Hero|Iksar Hero]] reaches it.
     1. Along the statue's path are a number of **disabled ballistas**, guarded by [[A Dracurion Infiltrator|Dracurion Infiltrators]] which need to be cleared to allow crafters to work (*pull them back to the statue*).
     1. Once cleared, crafters will find [[A slain ballista operator]] on the ground, and when clicked, the crafter will have the recipe **Ballista Repair** scribed into their recipe book.
     1. Gather the components needed to repair the ballista, a **pile of lumber**, **ballista rope**, and **a small pouch of nuts and bolts**, from the ground nearby.
     1. Using the disabled ballista as your crafting station, craft **Ballista Repair**.
   - Fire the repaired ballista.
     - Once the ballista has been repaired, it can be fired, which will cause the [[The Iksar Hero|Iksar Hero]] to kneel.
     - Clear the adds quickly. If the [[A Dracurion Stoneweaver|Dracurion Stoneweavers]] adds are still up at the end of the 1 minute kneel, they sacrifice their life, depop, and heal the statue. Killing the adds reduces the ability of the statue to resist taking damage, kneeling or otherwise, so make sure they're being destroyed.
   - Continue to repair and fire ballistas as [[The Iksar Hero]] works his way down the path, until he has been destroyed.
