*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Harvest three glands from four different types of diseased creatures in the Feerrott by right clicking on the following corpses: (Can be tracked as a creature, not as a harvest)
   - 3 diseased dreg corpses - found in the area south of Vashazdar around normal dregs, near [[Bouncer Flerb]]. {{waypoint 10.18, -6.62, 473.61}}
   - 3 diseased spider corpses - found in the southwest corner of the zone, near [[Bouncer Hurd]] and [[Cythan]]
     - {{waypoint -41, 16, 868}}
     - {{waypoint -16, -2, 737}}
     - {{waypoint 84, 24, 868}}
     - {{waypoint 1, 10, 781}}
     - {{waypoint -21, 16, 812}}
   - 3 diseased sattar corpses - found in the area east of falls, near thule sattars.
     - {{waypoint -466, 5, 140}}
     - {{waypoint -490, 1, 112}}
     - {{waypoint -663, 214, 110}}
     - {{waypoint -412, 8, 144}}
     - {{waypoint -631, -3, 162}}
   - 3 diseased dragonfly corpses - found around Allize Tae Outpost, just before the Gulch of Thule.
     - -1,163 -6 724
     - -1,148 -7 739
     - -1,249 ,8 763
1. Return to [[Vashazdar Manoisti]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 69s 57c
