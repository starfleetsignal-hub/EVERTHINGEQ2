## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Mithaniel Marr has informed me that summoning an Avatar requires the land to be attuned to their element. To achieve this, altars have been set up around the region, but they need to be supplied with power. I have been asked to assist with this by collecting objects infused with the essence of slain foes and offer them to an altar.

1. Collect 3 [[Emblem of Valor|infused emblems of valor]] (random body drop from several mobs in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]])
1. Use the [[Emblem of Valor|infused emblems of valor]] and empower an altar.  Random spawn in The Commonlands, its yellow sparkles visible far away, possible locations of altar
   - {{waypoint -1345, -80, -275}}
   - {{waypoint -580, -47, -407}} Northeast of Crossroads
   - {{waypoint -1087, -53, 111}} just outside Freeport entrance guard walls
   - {{waypoint -1345, -70, 310}} next to Turmoil Cemetery
   - {{waypoint -381, -48, 461}} South East
   - {{waypoint 315, -45, 265}} next to Corpse Pitch Bridge
   - {{waypoint 593, -47, 277}} near the Ruins of Val'Marr, along the road
   - {{waypoint 453, 47, -226}} northeast of the druid ring, near the river
   - {{waypoint -21, -46, -451}} Next to Wizard Spires
   - {{waypoint 173, -49, -732}} south of the Darklight Wood entrance
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Truthbringer]] {{waypoint -840, -48, 199}} in [[The Commonlands]]
