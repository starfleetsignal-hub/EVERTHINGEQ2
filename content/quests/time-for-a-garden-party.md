## Prerequistes

- Either you or someone in your group must have completed [[Punja's Progression]] to scale the ice wall in the [[The Halls of Learning|Halls of Learning]] or know the alternate route around the ledge.
- Alternatively, there's a way to get to the top without the wall. Head to the north side, jump up the rocks just east of the central wall, then walk along the lip of that wall, southward, until you get to the break in the wall, Then head west, then north along the lip until you get to the slightly lower wall. Now you're on top.

## Steps

1. Kill [[A channel cleanser|channel cleansers]] {{waypoint 123, 31, 477}} until you receive all the runes. They will drop a small chest that will contain one rune. Quest will update when each rune that is needed is looted:
   - [[The rune of adla]]
   - [[The rune of kom]]
   - [[The rune of herk]]
     - **Origins:** make sure loot options are set correctly (NBG all items or FFA), as otherwise people who don't need a rune will automatically get them, and they aren't tradeable items.
1. Examine [[The Arbo'Un Monolith]] {{waypoint 502, 49, 386}} in [[The Halls of Learning]] to summon [[Arbo'Akh]]. Ask it about Thalbinous.
1. Speak to [[Thalbinous]]. {{waypoint 424, 48, 538}}
   - To spawn Thalbinous you have to kill all three groups of [[A fiendish weedling|fiendish weedlings]] found in the Halls of Learning. One group spawns by Thalbinous, the second on one side the Hall and third on the other. When all are dead run back up the ice wall and speak to Thalbinous.
1. Find pine cones:
   - Kill [[A twisted arbor]] for the initial update
   - Examine the trees at the following locations:
     - {{waypoint 488, 49, 547}}
     - {{waypoint 486, 49, 386}}
     - {{waypoint 548, 42, 305}}
     - {{waypoint 569, -2, 380}}
     - {{waypoint 533, 7, 522}}
     - {{waypoint 475, 7, 526}}
1. Return to Thalbinous.
   - You may have to kill the weedlings again for him to reappear.
