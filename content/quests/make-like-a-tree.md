## Description

"Whittier hadn't expected me to plant my roots at Green Knoll for long. So when I told him I planned to move on he asked me to deliver a blessed acorn to a woodelf friend of his, Seren Leafsway, who was recently sent to Spire Shadow Outpost. The outpost is east of Kelethin and north of the wizard spires, next to Lower Emerald River." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Seren Leafsway]] in the relocated [[Spire Shadow Outpost]] {{waypoint -382, 14, 176}}

## Rewards

- At least 4s 82c
- [[Lucent Gem of Prowess]]
- [[Enormous potted seedling]]
- [[Scepter of Flames (Version 2)|Scepter of Flames]]

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Whittier: "Are you looking to explore more of the Faydark, [Player Character]?"
- Player Character: "I am."
- Whittier: "I thought so. I didn't expect you to plant your roots here for too long. Would you mind taking this blessed acorn to my friend, Seren Leafsway?"
- Player Character: "Not at all."
- Whittier: "She's east of here, at Spire Shadow Outpost, planted next to Lower Emerald River."
- Player Character: "I'll find her."

Speak with Seren Leafsway.

- Seren Leafsway: "Watch yourself around here, traveler. There are ravenous beasts about!"
- Player Character: "That's why Whittier sent me. He wanted you to have this blessed acorn."
- Seren Leafsway: "Ha, ha, ha, ha! Thank you, [Player Character], but I think my treant friend misled you."
- Player Character: "What do you mean?"
- Seren Leafsway: "Oh, do not be cross with him. He wears his heart on his bark, to be certain. He is always trying to help others and keep watch over us."
- Player Character: "Why did he send me though?"
- Seren Leafsway: "You must have helped him a great deal, performed a great service, perhaps?"
- Player Character: "I helped him stop the orcs from burning Kelethin."
- Seren Leafsway: "That is precisely why he sent you. He thought you could help me."

I have spoken with Seren Leafsway.

Seren Leafsway was surprised by the blessed acorn I gave her and explained how Whittier was not being straight forward with me. The acorn was simply an excuse to send me to her. He must have thought I would want to help her once I learned of the situation at Spire Shadow Outpost.
