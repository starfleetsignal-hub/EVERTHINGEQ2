## Starting the quest

Click a bottle of rum on the wall near the entrance:

- [[F.S. Distillery: The Rum Cellar (Solo)]] {{waypoint 8, -158, -151}}
- [[F.S. Distillery: Stowaways (Advanced Solo)]] {{waypoint -234, -62, 99}}

## Steps

1. Collect 11 bottles of rum on the walls within [[F.S. Distillery: The Rum Cellar (Solo)]] (7bottles) and [[F.S. Distillery: Stowaways (Advanced Solo)]] (4 bottles). The bottles are always in the same place. Mission completes when you collect the 11th bottle.
