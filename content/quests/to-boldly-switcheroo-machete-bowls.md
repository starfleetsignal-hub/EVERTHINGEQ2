## Notes

This is one of the tradeskill dailies. Requires 25 Zimaran Coal. Quest completes when you finish crafting 5 Machete Bowls

## Steps

Glerb has requested Machete Bowls

1. Collect 5 [[Small Round Boulder|Small Round Boulders]] (Note: these are labeled but not Harvest Trackable)
1. Craft 5 x [[Machete Bowl|Machete Bowls]] - bring 25 [[Zimaran Coal]], you can use Goblin Workbench next to Glarb

## Rewards

At least 11p 16g 55s 16c

- [[Boldly Boxerroo!]] random reward from this crate
