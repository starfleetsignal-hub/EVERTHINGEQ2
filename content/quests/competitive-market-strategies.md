## Notes

- [[Vikana|Vikana's]] quests may be done in [[The Merchant's Den (Solo)]], [[The Merchant's Den (Heroic I)]], or [[The Merchant's Den (Heroic II)]].

## Steps

1. Enter The Merchant's Den ([[The Merchant's Den (Solo)|Solo]], [[The Merchant's Den (Heroic I)|Heroic I]], or [[The Merchant's Den (Heroic II)|Heroic 2]])  {{waypoint -288.37, 53.76, -347.10}}
   - *For full steps on clearing the zone, see the zone walkthroughs for ([[The Merchant's Den (Solo)|Solo]], [[The Merchant's Den (Heroic I)|Heroic I]], or [[The Merchant's Den (Heroic II)|Heroic 2]])*
1. Clear through the zone, clicking the stolen goods as you go.
   - *NOTE: You CAN pick up the stolen goods in an old instance. These are **Maj'Dul Pottery, Kelethin Crate**, and **Erudin Rugs** clickies around the zone.*
   - *You CAN NOT get the other two updates in an old cleared instance.*
1. Pass through the secret door in wall {{waypoint -162, 0, -61}}
1. Kill [[Elder Furdock]] {{waypoint -240, 0, 16}} and his adds to update the "Furdock and Friends" step of the quest.
1. Free **captive Vikana associates**.
   - *You may be able to get these updates in a cleared instance of the zone.*
   - Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -219.37, 0.16, 17.51}}
     - {{waypoint -253.22, -4.45, 63.77}}
     - {{waypoint -229.43, -4.45, 71.77}}
     - {{waypoint -210.85, -4.45, 50.05}}
     - {{waypoint -181.31, -4.40, 58.60}}
     - {{waypoint -180.90, -4.44, 50.49}}
1. Zone out and return to [[Vikana]] {{waypoint -249.12, 13.58, 127.97}}
