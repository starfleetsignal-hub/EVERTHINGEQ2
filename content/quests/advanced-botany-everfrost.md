   1. Arctic Lupines in Guardian's Gulch -- around {{waypoint -394.31, -38.29, -879.08}}  {{waypoint -606, -38, -836}} {{waypoint -686, -38, -689}} and {{waypoint -504, -38, -821}}

1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].
