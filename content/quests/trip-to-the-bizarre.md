## Steps

1. Enter [[Fordel Midst: Bizarre Bazaar (Solo)|Fordel Midst: Bizarre Bazaar [Solo]]] at {{waypoint 170, 62, -645}}
1. Proceed through the zone.
   - Note: See [[Fordel Midst: Bizarre Bazaar (Solo)|Fordel Midst: Bizarre Bazaar [Solo]]] for help proceeding through the zone.
1. Once [[Bazaar Baron Brixwald]], click on **Ancient Discarded Weapon Piles** until you find the **petrified zipacaite hammer** (it's located under one of the piles, you will need to click it again once you find it to get the update). Note: You do not have to kill Bazaar Baron Brixwald to search the piles to find the hammer.
1. Return to [[Gaedra Rugon]] in [[Aurelian Coast]] at {{waypoint 626.32, 94.57, -419.61}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Amulet of the Haven]]
