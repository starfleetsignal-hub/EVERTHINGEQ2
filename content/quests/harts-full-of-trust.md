## Steps

1. Hunt 6 [[A Hart of the Grove|Harts of the Grove]] in the [[Coastal Grove]] in [[Antonica]]. {{waypoint -2246, -24, -585}}

1. Return to [[Priest Aaronolis Swornlove]]
