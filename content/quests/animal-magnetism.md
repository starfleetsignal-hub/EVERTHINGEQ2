## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[A wurm|wurms]] or [[An ancient wurm|ancient wurms]] found around {{waypoint -931, -481, 3812}} for musk glands
1. Spray musk on 3 supplies Located Around: {{waypoint -645, -435, 3722}} (Giant Crates)
   - Spray musk on supplies {{waypoint -792, -170, 3851}}
   - Spray musk on supplies {{waypoint -741, -483, 3854}}
   - Spray musk on supplies {{waypoint -644, -474, 3904}}
     - *Note:look at the map to find the ones you need in the Flamespout Fields*
1. Speak with [[Klipklap]] {{waypoint -1164, -531, 3448}}

- At least 1p 25g
- +300 faction with **Othmir of Velious**
