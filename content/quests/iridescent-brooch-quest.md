## Steps

1. Kill undead in The Thundering Steppes for an uncommon update.
   - [[An unearthed settler]], [[An unearthed villager]], [[A fallen goliath]], [[A skeletal footsoldier]], [[A revenant scout]] and [[A drowned trooper]] confirmed to update.
1. Interact with a barrel on [[Dead River Docks]] at {{waypoint -447, 0, -395}}.
1. Kill [[A Sabertooth soothsayer|Sabertooth soothsayer]]s around {{waypoint 710, 0, -412}} for an uncommon update.

## Rewards

- At least 36s 13c
