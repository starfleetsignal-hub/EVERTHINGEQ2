## Notes

This quest is infinitely repeatable while this prelude event is active.

When accepting quest you will have 2 recipes scribed into your book - [[Battlers' Bulwark Stew]] and [[Flamebearer Brew]], you will be asked to craft 6 of either one chosen randomly.

- You can buy Glowing Kindling from [[A Flamebearer Supply Master]] nearby.
- Each combine requires 1 x Chaotic Elemental Filament + 2 of any common harvested wood + 3 of any common harvested roots + 2 of any common harvested meat (for stew) or crop (for brew) + 3 Glowing Kindling. Each combine makes 1 - 2 items.
- You can use [[Khana Rodhari|Khana's]] campfire pot or any Stove & Keg to craft.

## Steps

1. Check provisioner's barrels behind Khana to  find out what exactly needs to be crafted this time.
1. Craft the required 6 items.
1. Speak with Khana Rodhari.

## Rewards

- tradeskill experience
- AA experience
- at least 3=5p 4=54g
- 2 x [[Brass Lambent]]
