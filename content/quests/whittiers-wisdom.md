## Description

"Whittier the treant says that there are only two things in life, healing and self-defense. I'm curious to learn more about self-defense around Kelethin." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Visit the following loacations (in no particular order):
   - [[Lesser Faydark Road]] {{waypoint 704.71, 0.30, 357.34}}
   - [[Loping Plains Road]] {{waypoint 138.46, 5.09, 606.53}}
   - [[Sapling Spur Outpost]] {{waypoint -151, 7, -116}}
   - [[Underwood Road]] {{waypoint 96.36, 15.10, -19.30}}
   - [[Orc Hill]] {{waypoint 296.49, 62.04, -99.28}}
1. Return to [[Whittier]]

## Rewards

- At least 1s 5c
- [[Whittier's Linen Surcoat]]
- [[Whittier's belt pouch]]

## Journal Steps

Whittier has given me a list of places he wants me to visit beneath Kelethin. I must locate the Lesser Faydark Road. I must locate the Loping Plains Road. I must locate Sapling Spur. I must locate Underwood Road. I must locate Orc Hill.

I've found the Lesser Faydark Road. I've found the Loping Plains Road. I've located Sapling Spur. I've found Underwood Road. I've located Orc Hill. I've been to the locations Whittier specified. Now that I've seen some of the areas beneath Kelethin, as well as roads that lead to areas I might want to explore later, I should return to Whittier and tell him. Whittier is in Kelethin, roaming one of the platforms near Joleena's Restaurant. He'll be interested to hear how I fared.

I've spoken with Whittier.

After exploring areas beneath Kelethin and learning to stand my ground, Whittier explained to me that there are really only two paths in life -- healing and self-defense. I will be fine as long as I do not regret anything I choose to do.
