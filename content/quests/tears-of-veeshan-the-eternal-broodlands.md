## Steps

1. Find a way to enter the [[Vesspyr Isles]].
   1. Speak to Dartain/Al'Kabor (the quest step will say to speak to Al'Kabor) of The Duality (in a zone accessed by) speaking to [[Tevan Brassgear|Tevan]] or [[Blaira Va'Tyrn]].
   1. Speak to either :
      - If good-aligned : [[Kurista (Qeynos Province District)|Kurista]] in the [[The Elddar Grove]] {{waypoint 676, -16, -253}}.
      - If evil-aligned : [[Elguka the Oracle]] outside [[The Temple of War]] {{waypoint -112, -8, -130}}.
        - Players of any alignment can speak to either Kurista or Elguka, however, sticking to the same alignment makes the NPCs easier to reach.
   1. Use the [[Draconic Sigil]] while at the [[Obol Plains]] druid ring {{waypoint -433, 27, 441}}.
   1. Enter the newly-summoned portal to [[Vesspyr Isles]] in the druid ring.
1. Follow the guidance of [[Yelinak the Ancient (Vesspyr Isles)|Lord Yelinak]]:
   1. Speak with Drinal's Steward in the Vesspyr Isles dragon circle {{waypoint -128, 214, 350}}.
   1. Speak with [[Yelinak the Ancient (Vesspyr Isles)]] in [[Daarspire]] {{waypoint 76, 219, 109}}. N O T E ! ! You must have the following Vesspyr Isles quests completed to be able to continue with this quest. (In other words, he tells you to follow the [[Vesspyr Isles Timeline]], then return to him when you've reached the steps below.)
      - ---Lepethida's quests are the first you encounter in this zone, beginning with [[Sent for Savtek]]
        - [[Droumlund Intruders]], which is the 7th quest from Lepethida at {{waypoint 112, 210, -11}} on Daarspire
      - ---[[Harla Dar]]'s quests unlock after completing Lepthida's 5th quest, Waters of Strife
        - [[Family Ties]], which is the 7th quest from Harla Dar at {{waypoint -104, 51, 738}} on Veeshana Tol
      - ---Darathar's quests follow three quests from Field Marshal Vishra at {{waypoint -797, -46, 38}} on Veiled Thessk
        - [[Deceiver's Fate]], which is the 2nd quest from Darathar on Veiled Thessk
      - ---Vishra's quests are unlocked while working on Lepethida's 6th quest, Veiled Threat
      - ---Wuoshi's quests unlock after completing Harla Dar's 3rd quest, Guardian of the Growth
        - [[The Purity of Growth]], which is the 4th quest from Wuoshi at {{waypoint -599, 379, 623}} on Aeterna Gardens <br>
      - Note:  If you did the previous quests while getting armor upgrades, simply move away from Yelinak enough to get the quest updated, a flag will appear over his head.
   1. After speaking with Yelinak, he will tell you to talk to [[Chymot]] on [[Karak Peak|Karak-ta]] in the Vesspyr Isles {{waypoint -76, 30, -491}}.
   1. Defeat the 3 attacking [[An Awakened skystalker|Awakened skystalkers]].
   1. Speak to Chymot again.
   1. Return to [[Yelinak the Ancient (Vesspyr Isles)|Yelinak the Ancient]] on [[Daarspire]] {{waypoint 76, 219, 109}}.
   - Note: In order to give Lord Yelinak enough time to consider the situation, you must have completed [[Family Ties]], [[Droumlund Intruders]], [[The Purity of Growth]], and [[Deceiver's Fate]].
1. Attempt to establish contact with [[Lendiniara]].
   1. Speak to Chymot at the lower tower entrance on Daarspire. {{waypoint 181, 154, -83}}
   1. Enter the advanced solo or heroic version of [[The Nexus Core (Advanced Solo)|The Nexus Core]] via the portal inside the tower.
   1. Kill [[Luminox Prime]] within the Nexus Core, the final named of the zone.
   1. Activate the core in the central room of the Nexus Core.
   1. Return to [[Yelinak the Ancient (Vesspyr Isles)|Yelinak the Ancient]] on [[Daarspire]] {{waypoint 76, 219, 109}}.
