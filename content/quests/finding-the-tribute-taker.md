## Steps

1. Give the [[Well-groomed panther carass]] to [[King Grugla]] up in the broken aqueduct just south of [[Mok Rent]]. {{waypoint 1871, 95, -154}}
   - To reach King Grugla, using the Mok Rent griffon tower {{waypoint 1833, 61, -322}} as a starting point:
     1. Turn your back to the tower and go to the ramp upwards at {{waypoint 1770, 56, -381}}.
     1. Climb the ramp and the two wooden bridges that connect ramps to the **Upper Entrance** where [[Ancho Mok'Nok]] and [[Secondary Tykenil Riz'Dn]] are at {{waypoint 1862, 112, -371}}
     1. Turn to the right (west) and go through the pathway through the wall. To avoid the aggro [[A jungle khvara|jungle khvara]] at the other end of the pathway, keep to the left of the pathway and turn left immediately at the other end of the pathway {{waypoint 1896, 114, -368}}.
     1. Follow the river to the left (south) from the entrance, to an aqueduct at {{waypoint 1871, 114, -301}}.
     1. Follow the aqueduct to its edge at {{waypoint 1869, 113, -224}}, looking down towards the edge.
     1. At the edge, below you, is a vine. Walk (drop) to the vine and follow it to a broken piece of a bridge on the other side at {{waypoint 1867, 114, -192}}.
     1. Walk this platform to its edge, dropping down again to the platform below it at {{waypoint 1873, 96, -141}}. This is where King Grugla is. From here, you can take another vine to get down safely. (Alternatively, you can try jumping down to the water).
1. Pick up the [[Baby Stonechest Gorilla]] and stone tablet that spawn on the ground near King Grugla.
1. Return to [[Vaerha Farsight]].

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
