## Steps

1. Obtian 4 *rune stones* from  [[An invading flurryburst (Jarsath Wastes)|invading flurrybursts]] at their base camp. {{waypoint 957, -32, 12}}
1. Obtain 6 *elemental ice shards*, again from [[An invading flurryburst (Jarsath Wastes)|invading flurrybursts]].
1. Return to [[Vixna Slizzis]] at the [[Upper Command Camp]].

## Rewards

- At least 41g 63s 17c
- +1,000 faction with **Legion of Danak**
