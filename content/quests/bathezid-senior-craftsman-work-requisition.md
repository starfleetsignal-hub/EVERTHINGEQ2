## Steps

1. Collect the requisition list from the Work Requisitions clipboard (next to Supply Sergeant Anuhadux)
1. Scribe the [[Bathezid senior craftsman recipes]] received upon accepting the quest.
1. Create 6 of one of the following (randomly determined after clicking the clipboard):
   - [[Arrowproof waterskin]] (Loom - smoldering filament)
     - 4 Bristled Pelt
     - 2 Lichenclover Root
     - 5 Smoldering Filament
   - [[Chocolate covered battle snacks]] (Stove and Keg - smoldering kindling)
     - 4 Cabilis Cocoa Beans
     - 2 Raw Succulent Root
     - 10 Smoldering Kindling
   - [[Painting of Venril Sathir]] (Woodworking Table - smoldering sandpaper)
     - 4 Lichenclover Root
     - 2 Redwood Lumber
     - 5 Smoldering Sandpaper
   - [[Reinforced redwood buckler]] (Woodworking Table - smoldering sandpaper)
     - 4 Redwood Lumber
     - 2 Ferrite Cluster
     - 5 Smoldering Sandpaper
   - [[Siege ladder]] (Woodworking Table - smoldering sandpaper)
     - 4 Redwood Lumber
     - 2 Ferrite Cluster
     - 5 Smoldering Sandpaper
   - [[Wooden training staves]] (Woodworking Table - smoldering sandpaper)
     - 4 Redwood Lumber
     - 2 Ferrite Cluster
     - 5 Smoldering Sandpaper
1. Return to Anuhadux.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill Experience
- At least 38g 11s 18c
- +750 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
