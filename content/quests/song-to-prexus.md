## Steps

- Visit [[Lonely Isle]] at {{waypoint 350, 0, -780}}
- Return to [[Landwyn Velamir|Landwyn]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Old memento pouch]] (5 slots)
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Adept's Woven Shoes]] (All Mages)
  - [[Footman's Worked Sabatons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Initiate's Threaded Foot Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Naturalist's Sewn Leather Boots]] (Druids)
  - [[Plunderer's Small Link Chain Shoes]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Seer's Rustic Chain Boots]] (Shamans)
  - [[Theist's Shaped Plate Boots]] (Clerics)
