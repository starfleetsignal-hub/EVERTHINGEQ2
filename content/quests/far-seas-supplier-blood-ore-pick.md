## Notes

- [[Catapult Ammo Suppliers]], Far Seas Supplier - Blood Ore Pick, [[Utilitarian Desires of the Miners]] and [[Disrupting the Mining Operation]] all require you to kill [[A Vallon miner|Vallon miners]] so it is advisable to do these quests together, if possible.

## Steps

1. Visit [[Blood Ore Valley]]. {{waypoint 360, 0, -120}}
1. Kill [[A Vallon miner|Vallon miner]]s (level 33) around [[The Deathfist Quarry]] or on its bottom level. {{waypoint 342, -114, -114}}
1. Inspect an unused pick on the top level of the quarry. {{waypoint 300, -30, -130}}
1. Speak to Gaertarn

## Rewards

- Experience only
