## Notes

- None of the harvestable items are trackable.
- All of these clicks are **zone-wide group** updates. If you are grouped anywhere in the zone, your group members will receive updates.

## Steps

1. Travel to zone [[Kylong Plains]] and collect in this zone the requested ingredients.
   - Gather 2 Dreg's Green Beans around [[Dreg's Landing]] near {{waypoint 862, 15, 214}}![Dreg's Green Beans](images/Questitem_Dreg's_Green_Beans.jpg)
   - Gather 4 Waning Onions around [[Waning Thicket]] near {{waypoint -1365, 25, -462}}![Waning Onion](images/Waning_Onion.jpg)Note: Onions are VERY sparsely populated.
   - Gather 5 Ember Hive Mushrooms around [[Ember Hive]] near {{waypoint 1559, 213, -1881}}<br> *NOTE: The quickest way to these is go to the druid ring, then take the Sokokar to Sokokar Post Highton, and the mushrooms are a bit to the west.*![Ember Hive Mushrooms](images/Ember_Hive_Mushrooms.jpg)
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]].

## Rewards

- [[Days of Summer: 2020]] useable at [[Pas Yu]], the merchant beside [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]] on the [[Spire Island]].
