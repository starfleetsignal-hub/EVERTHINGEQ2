- [[Oolong Kalish Tea]]
- [[Fayberry Pie (Crafted)|Fayberry Pie]]
- [[Fayberry Wine (Crafted)|Fayberry Wine]]
- [[Sweet Onion Wine]]
- [[Lion Casserole]]
- [[Pork Casserole (Crafted)|Pork Casserole]]
- [[Crusted Mackerel]]
- [[Crayfish Casserole (Crafted)|Crayfish Casserole]]
- [[Steppes Mountain Espresso]]
- [[Gin and Tonic]]
- [[Sweet Onion Omelet (Crafted)|Sweet Onion Omelet]]
- [[Vox Breath Beer (Crafted)|Vox Breath Beer]]
- [[Mountain Man]]
- [[Beer Braised Pork (Crafted)|Beer Braised Pork]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

- At Least 12s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
