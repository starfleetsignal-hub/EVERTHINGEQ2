## Steps

1. Slay a [[Muckflick runner]] {{waypoint 127.96, 191.92, 595.28}} to receive a note.
1. Return to [[Brulten Hiltstill]]

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
- At least 2s 91c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Brutal Expert's Wrist Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Holy Acolyte's Wristguard]] (Clerics)
  - [[Infused Beguiler's Cuffs]] (All Mages)
  - [[Keen Bandit's Vambraces]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Peerless Sentry's Bracers]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Pure Preserver's Bands]] (Channelers, Druids)
  - [[Wracked Soothsayer's Chain Cuffs]] (Shamans)
