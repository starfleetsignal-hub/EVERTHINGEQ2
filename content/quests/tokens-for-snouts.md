## Notes

- It is recommended that you gain enough faction with both [[Bathezid's Watch (Faction)|Bathezid's Watch]] and [[Riliss (Faction)|Rillss]] prior to doing this quest. Although you can use stealth or invisibility, there are heroic guards in some of these areas who will see you.

## Steps

1. Collect the following items (all are [[Track Harvestables|trackable]]):
   - [[Sarnak tail clasp]] from [[Bathezid's Watch]]: {{waypoint 982.38, 207.1, 1232.81}}
   - [[Polished pike blade]] from [[Riliss (POI)|Riliss]]: {{waypoint -863, 45, -246}}
   - [[Broken froglok manacles]] from [[Western Pens of Riliss]]: {{waypoint -737, -146, 674}}
   - [[Tusker harness ring]] from [[Sathir's Span]]: {{waypoint -207, -7, 934}}
   - [[Collection of fishhooks]] from [[Omen's Call]]: {{waypoint 182, -118, -175}} or {{waypoint 243, -61, -233}}
   - [[Silvered gear]] from [[Drogan Exile Camp]]: {{waypoint -592, -203, -1337}}
1. Return to [[Snouts]] at the [[Bellywhumper Burrows]].

## Rewards

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- +5,000 faction with **The Bellywhumpers**
