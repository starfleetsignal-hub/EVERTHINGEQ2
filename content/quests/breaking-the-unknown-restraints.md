## Notes

Current issue in Beta - "ebonlithe enchantresses" have an ability that hits for trillions of damage, kill them first and fast before they cast it. (Needs confirmation once Live)

- You will need to complete the full instance in order to complete this quest.
- Named mobs you will need to kill:
  1. [[Sinferzall]] - paths across your path at beginning of zone at {{waypoint -228, 89, 11}}.  Disarms.
  1. [[Do'Guen]] {{waypoint -269, 92, 108}} - put stun immunity rune in your belt, has big knockback, summons adds. (He wanders. I found him at {{waypoint -891.55, -16.73, 852.13}}
  1. [[Scaladia Yen]] {{waypoint 20, 157, 340}} - seems to be straightforward tank and spank.
  1. [[Carnagor]] {{waypoint 28, 158, 52}} - has a knockback, kill adds (especially in heroic)
  1. [[Infernal Tyrant Dennigrah]] {{waypoint 206, 157, 56}} - spawns after Carnagor is killed.

## Steps

![an intriguing crystal](images/An_intriguing_crystal.png)
Vadreem suspects that crystals placed on pillars around [[Oblivion's Bask]], on the Edge of Oblivion, are suppressing outsiders from flight. You are sent to investigate.

1. Enter [[The Unknown: Edge of Oblivion (Solo)]] through portal at {{waypoint -199, 83, -265}} in The Unknown.
1. Investigate one of the crystals in [[Infernal Heights]] area {{waypoint -291, 98, 174}}
   1. click crystal once to investigate it
   1. click crystal again again to break it
   1. click again to destroy voidflow reservoir stone.
1. Destroy the following
   - Destroy total of 7 (6 more) obscured voidflow reservoir stones on pillars throughout the zone. Possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -160, 133, 264}}, {{waypoint -66, 157, 226}}, {{waypoint 31, 157, 375}}, {{waypoint 92, 157, 14}}, {{waypoint 38, 157, 75}}, {{waypoint 47, 157, 37}}, {{waypoint 170, 156, 63}} (166, 157, 42) and more.
   - Destroy the voidflow polestar gem at {{waypoint 199, 158, 90}} - you need to kill all named mobs in the zone in order to destroy gem.
     - *Note there's a huge knockback when you destroy it*
1. Return to [[Vadreem]] on [[Nightscar Steppes]] in [[The Unknown]].
