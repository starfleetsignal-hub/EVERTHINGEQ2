## Notes

- See discussion page for problems regarding this quest!

## Steps

1. Go to the [[Corrupted Druid Ring]]. {{waypoint -415, 27, 430}}
1. Place the [[Night Jar]] on the ground in the center of the ring (closer to the [[A raven|raven]]).
1. Use the [[Ritual Dagger]] on the Night Jar.
1. The raven perched on the stone will start squawking and [[Aosoth the Crone]] will spawn (level 66^^^). Kill her and bring her head back to Ihriel for your reward.
   - If more than one person in your group needs the kill, only one person needs to place the Night Jar and use the Ritual Dagger, for all to get the kill credit.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Blade of the Lost Oasis]] (Plate Fighters)
  - [[Blade of the Sky]] (non-Beastlord Scouts)
  - [[Club of Solid Stone]] (All Priests)
  - [[Forest Falchion]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Heavenly Wand of Frozen Time]] (All Mages)
  - [[Knuckles of Rationality and Sense]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Skymage's Sacrifice Dagger]] (All Mages)
