## Steps

1. Craft 4 [[Armored Doorbot|Armored Doorbots]] at a [[Work Bench]]
1. Scan [[Halls of Fate]] in [[The Bonemire]] {{waypoint -304, -341, -55}}
1. Scan [[The Den of the Devourer]] {{waypoint 41, 39, 488}} (Dreadnever Crash Site)
1. Scan [[Deathtoll]] {{waypoint -27, -99, -587}} (Carrion Briar)
1. Scan [[The Blackscale Sepulcher]] {{waypoint 219, 184, -388}} (Cacotoxic Stain)
1. Return to [[Dora Wicket|Dora]].

## Notes

- The locations for the doorbots are all near the cloud stations and safe for lower-level adventurers.

## Rewards

- At least 6g 14s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
