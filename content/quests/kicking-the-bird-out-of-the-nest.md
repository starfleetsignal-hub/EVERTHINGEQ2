## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- Each turn-in allows the player obtain a different recipe book.

## Steps

1. Kill [[Chamberlain X'Haviz]]. {{waypoint 180.00, 103.00, 0.00}}
   - To reach X'Haviz:
     - Climb down the ladder at {{waypoint -14, 85, 0}} to enter the basement.
     - Find and kill [[Switch]] or [[Nutty]], who roams on the basement, and loot [[A strange crystal egg]]. This will allow you to use the elevator on the next floor.
     - In [[The Main Library]], climb up the ladder at {{waypoint 186, 56, 0}}.
     - Head to the lift at {{waypoint 146, 82, 0}} and use the egg to activate the lift.
1. Return to [[Kit Oo'Loh]]. {{waypoint 36, 86, 16}}

## Rewards

Your choice of one of the following no-trade recipe books.

- [[Recipes for the Counselor's Head]]
- [[Recipes for the Taskmaster's Gullet]]
- [[Recipes for the Chamberlain's Flayed Skin]]
- [[Recipes for the Kettleleader's Preserved Corpse]]
- [[Recipes for the Aggressor's Claws]]
