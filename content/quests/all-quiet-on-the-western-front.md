## Steps

1. Speak with [[Barrot Gulleyton]] in [[West Fort Irontoe]] {{waypoint -189, 210, -100}}
1. Stop the attacking Krulkiel raiders and their leader!
   - Three waves of level 31 Heroic groups of raiders will attack from the west of [[Barrot Gulleyton]] {{waypoint -169, 212, -52}}
     - The first wave will be 3 [[A Krulkiel raider|Krulkiel raider]]s and 1 [[An elite Krulkiel raider|elite Krulkiel raider]]
     - The second wave will be 3 Krulkiel raiders, 1 elite Krulkiel raider, and 1 [[A Krulkiel medic|Krulkiel medic]].
     - The third wave will be 2 Krulkiel raiders, 2 Krulkiel medics, and [[Warlord Enregak]]
1. Return to [[Gloln Kilnkor]]
