## Steps

1. Examine and apply [[Acid (Item)|acid]] to 5 weapons laying around [[Pyrrin Roost]]. The weapons appear as spears. {{waypoint 288, 83, 194}}
1. Return to [[Gerwen Di'Ki]].

## Rewards

- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Bodyguard's Shoulders]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Evoker's Shoulderpads]] (All Mages)
  - [[Marshall's Pauldrons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Pathfinder's Shoulderpads]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Spiritualist's Shoulderpads]] (Shamans)
  - [[Vicar's Pauldrons]] (Clerics)
  - [[Windcaller's Shoulderpads]] (Druids)
