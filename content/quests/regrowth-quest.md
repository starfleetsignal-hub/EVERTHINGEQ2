## Prerequisite

Must be a High Elf to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Cleanse three trees by applying the ***Cleansing Salve*** he gives you.
   - West of the gates to [[Castleview Hamlet]] at {{waypoint 899, 3, -207}}
   - East of [[The Western Falls]] at {{waypoint 940, 5, -197}}
   - East of the top of the ramp to the docks at {{waypoint 922, 3, -357}}
     - spawns [[A gnoll corrupter]]
1. Take the gnoll paw to Adalin.
1. Take the gnoll paw to [[Lieutenant Charlin]].

- 2s
