## Steps

NOTE:  You must be tradeskill level 10 or higher to do this quest.

1. Receive a letter in the mail from 'Office of the Harbormaster.'
1. Examine [[Message in a Bottle]] received with the previous quest in your house. You can examine bottles in other people’s houses to forward the quest as well. Activate the spell.
1. If you read the letter while still with Raffik, it should update immediately.
1. Speak with [[Raffik]], he gives you [[Vulrich Drumsticks (Recipe Book)|Vulrich Drumsticks]] recipe, find it in bags and scribe it.
1. Craft some vulrich drumsticks. Use the cooking pot at {{waypoint 21, 0, 16}};

Weathered Kindling is the stack of wood next to the cooking pot. ![a pile of weathered kindling](images/A_pile_of_weathered_kindling.jpg)

Harvest carnivore dens to get vulrich meat or kill vulriches walking around the isle for dropped meat that also works. ![feral vulrich](images/Feral_vulrich.jpg)

1. Speak to Raffik and feed the little beggar. He gives you [[Globe of Homecoming]] recipe. Find it in bags and scribe it.
1. Craft 2 [[Globe of Homecoming (Quest Item)|Globes of Homecoming]] at the workbench. There are wind swept rock and plains roots nodes in the zone, you will need 2 electrum clusters, 2 tuber strands and 2 rough turquoises.
1. Speak with Raffik.
1. The clickable boat that could be used to exit the isle in previous quest is no longer there.  You can use the Globe of Homecoming (which offers a teleport to any major city) or fast travel or Call to Home spell to exit zone.
