## Description

The Craftkeepers Task Coordinator has requested assistance on behalf of the Bastion of the Tranquil to go to Toxxulia's Reach and gather some of the young plant shoots that the local herbivores eat.

## Steps

![young plant shoot](images/Young_plant_shoot.png)

1. Speak to [[Oolioomon]] in the Paineel Commons {{waypoint 1788, 299, 3461}}.
1. I have been asked to gather young plant shoots from around Toxxulia's Reach. The young shoots needed have a tall dense cluster of green and purple leaves.
   - Gather 5 young plant shoots, which can be found near the Toxxulia Forest launch pad. (NOTE: You DO NOT need to have appropriate level gathering skills to collect these plants, they are a special node.) They are just a little southwest of the [[Toxxulia Forest Station]].
1. Return to [[Oolioomon]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +2500 faction with **Craftkeepers**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Craftkeepers Daily Supply Tasks
- This is technically a mission and will not show up in your quest log as completed.
