1. In [[Splendor Wellspring]] ![Climb Vines](images/Climb_Vines.jpg) the vine up to the ledge {{waypoint -565.55, 204.35, -38.35}} and ![a sickly giant owl](images/A_sickly_giant_owl.jpg) closer to a sickly giant owl {{waypoint -458.04, 238.14, -74.73}}
1. ![Owl Pellet](images/Owl_Pellet.jpg)Examine Owl Pellet in front of a sickly giant owl {{waypoint -465.42, 238.76, -68.52}}
1. Speak with the ghost of [[Hihne]] {{waypoint -472.16, 239.55, -81.42}}
1. Gather and Hunt the following
   - ![Melon Radish](images/Melon_Radish.jpg)Gather 8 Melon Radishes. (group-wide update) These can be difficult to spawn, some possible locations include: **NOTE:** I had to zoom in to see them. Toggle Night Vision on. You'll see them readily but Ultravision will not assist.

     - {{waypoint -306, 98, 206}}; -343. 114. 135
     - {{waypoint -407.54, 66.49, 152.20}}; {{waypoint -430.88, 72.62, 120.51}}
     - {{waypoint -476.38, 78.92, 93.98}}; {{waypoint -478.42, 82.92, 74.71}}
     - {{waypoint -498.15, 96.82, 23.02}}; {{waypoint -568.46, 97.89, 168.51}}
     - {{waypoint -578.69, 101.16, 82.86}}; {{waypoint -625.13, 102.69, 131.68}}
     - {{waypoint -627.20, 96.90, 178.68}}; {{waypoint -631.70, 96.97, 156.65}}

Hunt 5 pond creeper slugs for slime in Splendor Wellspring {{waypoint -470.35, 60.58, 149.82}}

1. Return to a sickly giant owl and apply chelation potion.
1. Return to [[Viamme]] at {{waypoint 210.62, 274.96, 815.29}}
