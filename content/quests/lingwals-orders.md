## Prerequisites

You must have completed all the quest series from Bargbae Skullhelm ([[Coldazed Dwarves]]), Adrad Postumius ([[Remove the Reinforcements]]) and Fedya ([[Poison on the Menu]]) before this quest will be offered to you

## Steps

1. Go to Thurgadin and speak with [[Gardar Spiritcutter]] while wearing the Rime illusion {{waypoint 11, -264, 317}}
1. Kill Gardar (92^)
   - *There is more information about this step on the talk page*
1. Return to Lingwal in the The Fortress Spire
1. Speak to [[Vira Warcarver]] at Thurgadin Harbor {{waypoint 1204, -479, 447}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- +800 faction with **The Order of Rime**
- +4000 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Pauldrons of the Impostor]] (Warriors, Crusaders)
  - [[Spaulders of the Deceiver]] (Clerics)
  - [[Spaulders of the Traitor]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Pauldrons of the Betrayer]] (Shamans)
  - [[Spaulders of the Turncoat]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Pauldrons of the Schlemiel]] (Druids)
  - [[Mantle of the Fallen Rime]] (All Mage)
