## Description

The curse of Gynok Moltor taints every room inside the Halls of the Forsaken. I've been given a Marr-Blessed Censer and instructed to perform a brief ceremony in several of the most prominent room in hopes that the curse may be sanctified.

## Steps

1. Enter the [[Befallen: Halls of the Forsaken]] and use the received *[[Marr-Blessed Censer]]* to cense several of the more prominent rooms
   - Cense the Echoing Cavern
   - Cense the Vestry of the Archbishop {{waypoint -54.80, -14.64, 145.45}}
   - Cense the Great Hall {{waypoint 133.17, -24.91, 119.53}}
   - Cense the Temporal Chamber {{waypoint 217.76, -17.39, 139.04}}
   - Cense the Chapel of Penance {{waypoint 216.23, -21.33, 73.46}}
   - Cense the Broken Stair {{waypoint -18.64, -14.46, 224.27}} (This area requires killing the nameds after arranging the statues)
   - NOTE: Each spot can only be censed once per zone. Be sure all group members are present before censing.
1. Hail [[Squire Hadley]] again
