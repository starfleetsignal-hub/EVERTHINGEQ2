## Steps

![blue beam](images/BlueBeam_ReignofShadows-AgainstThalOdds.jpg)
![teleporter](images/Portal1_ReignofShadows-AgainstThalOdds.jpg)
![door](images/Door1_ReignofShadows-AgainstThalOdds.jpg)

1. Enter [[Vex Thal: Shadow Citadel]] by clicking a portal at {{waypoint 6.54, 168.63, 559.55}}. from inside Shadeweaver's Thicket.
1. Slay [[Rhag'Xiuhsiss]] in the [[Tower of Conscience]] {{waypoint 87.29, -30.63, -215.17}}.
1. Find **blue beam of shadow** and step into it to get the "Clinging Shadows" buff {{waypoint 36.14, -19.04, -50.60}}.
1. Use the **teleporter** at {{waypoint -101.72, -3.27, 29.97}}.
1. Click on **the door** at {{waypoint 0.98, -40.81, -292.30}} and use the **teleporter** at {{waypoint -10.41, -40.81, -316.19}}
1. Slay [[Enforcer Shaaho]] in the [[Chamber of Reincarnation]] at {{waypoint 0.43, -3, 100}}
1. Find another **blue beam of shadow** {{waypoint -67.29, 30.95, -196.05}}
1. Use the **teleporter** at {{waypoint -0.60, 20.05, -290.49}}.
1. Speak with [[Aten Ha Ra]] {{waypoint -85, 88, -400}}.
1. Defeat [[Aten Ha Ra]] to obtain [[Aten Ha Ra's Breath of Kaas]]
1. Use the portal to return to Shadeweaver's Thicket at {{waypoint 0.43, -3, 100}}
1. Speak with [[Al'Kabor's projection]] in [[The Jharin Assemblage]] in the [[City of Shar Vahl]] at {{waypoint -89, 31, -408}}.
