## Steps

1. Right-click the [[The Clock of Ak'Anon|Clock of Ak'Anon]] in [[Qeynos Harbor]], [[Qeynos Province District]] to attach the cog code clanker. {{waypoint 736, -21, -41}}
   - Kill the clockwork [[Clock Guardian]]
   - Click the clock again to activate the the cog code clanker.
1. Go to {{waypoint 780.31, -20.50, 5.48}} to zone into [[Harbor Cache's Cellar]] to attend the secret meeting.
   - Listen to [[Kyle Antihilus Bayle|Kyle Antihilus Bayle's]] impassioned speech.
1. Return to [[Marik McPherson III (Qeynos Capitol District)|Marik Mchpherson III]] at [[The Tin Soldier]].
