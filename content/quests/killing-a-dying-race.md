## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[A risen Snowfang gnoll|risen Snowfang gnolls]] {{waypoint -1147, -537, -2126}}
1. Return to Mirna Greatfrost

- At least 1p 15g
- +1,100 faction with **Snowfang Gnolls**
- One of the following:

  - [[Krait Spine Earring of Grace]]
  - [[Barracuda Fang Earring of Velocity]]
