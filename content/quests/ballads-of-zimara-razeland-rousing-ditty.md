## Notes

- Side-quest [[Forced Reduction in Force]] also requires mobs in [[Zimara Breadth: Razing the Razelands (Solo)|Zimara Breadth: Razing the Razelands [Solo]]], but is not offered until this quest is complete. However, if you can avoid killing 12 undead aviak slaves and 3 controlled downtroddens you should be able to return to the same instance to complete the second quest.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Zimara Breadth: Razing the Razelands (Solo)|Zimara Breadth: Razing the Razelands [Solo]]] on the [[Isles of Wisdom]] at {{waypoint -591.34, 148.75, 136.99}}
1. Click the Windmaid's Lance at {{waypoint -483.76, 94.97, -546.21}}
   - *This is immediately below the zone in point, but there's an invisible wall in the way. It can be reached without killing everything you need for the next step by keeping to the edge.*
1. Kill the following
   - 11 Undead Aviaks
   - 12 Maedjinn Controllers (there are more in the [[Helionae Razelands]] area if you run out)
1. Kill the [[Instruments of battle]]
1. Defeat the unidentified caster behind the instrumental entity.
   1. Kill [[Eaglovok]] {{waypoint -388.05, 123.92, -648.42}} to gain access to the next area
      - *Beware of knockbacks*
   1. Teleport to [[Helionae Razelands]] using the crystals at  {{waypoint -336.40, 124.23, -648.05}}.
      1. The crystals deposits you at ( -228, 106, -462 )![](images/Teleport_to_Helopae_Razelands_crystal.jpg)
   1. Kill [[A risen warbler]] on the path to the next area, {{waypoint -184.31, 113.64, -176.95}} ( this will update the quest and gives you [[Aviak Flageolet (Ballads of Zimara: Razeland Rousing Ditty) (no effect)|Aviak Flageolet]])
      - *There is a name in the [[Helionae Razelands]] area ([[Gilded Back Demolisher]]); you don't have to kill it to progress, but might as well (knockback & stun).*
1. Seek [[Miranna Ameema (Razing the Razelands)|Miranna Ameema]] in [[Zenith Helionae]] at {{waypoint 11.11, 156.30, 76.94}} inside the solo and near the marble gazebo structures. She will give you [[Aviak Flageolet (Ballads of Zimara: Razeland Rousing Ditty) (with effect)|Aviak Flageolet]]
1. Attempt to use the [[Aviak Flageolet (Ballads of Zimara: Razeland Rousing Ditty) (with effect)|Aviak Flageolet]] against 10 maedjinn.
   - *NOTE: This updates in the overland also. Maedjinn can be found on Auric Palisades up from the cloud arrival spot*
1. Return to [[Ruhk]] on the [[Isles of Wisdom]]
   1. Take the portal at {{waypoint -484.31, 162.46, -613.15}} to [[Zimara Breadth]]
   1. Speak to [[Ruhk]] at {{waypoint -915.55, 189.53, 332.40}}
