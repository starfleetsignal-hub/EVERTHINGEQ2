## Steps

1. I must collect 10 element cores for the [[Caertaxian Legion (Faction)|Caertaxian Legion]].
   - The [[Element core|element cores]] can be found as a body drop on mobs in [[The Tower of Serilis]] ([[Guardians of the Underfoot (Faction)|Guardians of the Underfoot]] faction area) part of [[The Hole]].  Head through the North tunnel -51, 109, -329 for starters.
1. Return to [[Ziyatum]] after collecting them.

## Reward

- At least 20g 70s 61c
- +500 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**
