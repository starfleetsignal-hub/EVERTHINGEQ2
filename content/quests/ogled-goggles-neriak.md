## Steps

I have been hired to get a helm for Blat Berison from Frethe Zizlop. Frethe wants a deck of cards from Zoe Hertzflip. Zoe seeks Barb Islenets' pink goggles. This adventure can't go on for too long, can it?

Obtain [[Pretty Pink Goggles]] from [[Barb Islenet]] in the New Foreign quarter {{waypoint 82, 23, 138}} and return them to [[Zoe Hertzflip]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]].
