## Steps

1. Kill mobs in [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]] for bits.
   - The number of bits and types of mobs varies, even during the same day.
   - These may include the following mobs:
     - Fiery Spirits {{waypoint 4, 11, 72}}
     - Shamblers or Rocklords {{waypoint 276, 67, 107}}
     - Lava Spiders {{waypoint -130, 73, 376}}
     - Doomfire Phoenixes {{waypoint -390, 61, -303}}
     - Basilisks {{waypoint 6, -9, -152}}
     - Fiery steeds {{waypoint -182, 23, 97}}
     - Flamelords {{waypoint 232, 8, -412}}
1. Return to [[Dr. Arcana]].
