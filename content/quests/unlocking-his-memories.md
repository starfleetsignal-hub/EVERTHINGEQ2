## Steps

1. Aquire the following relics
   - Dig up (4) war relics.  These are *glowing snow* on ground.
   - Acquire (6) war relics from lingering Kromrif spirits .
   - *Note:Both can be found in N NW area of the Ring of War Area*
1. Speak with Hilgan Gravelheart
