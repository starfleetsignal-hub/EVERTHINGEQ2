## Description

You have been given the mission of defeating Najena's powerful creations. Defeat them and return to the Mission camp for your reward.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Najena's Hollow Tower]].
1. Defeat [[Ra'Zhish the Steam Lord]].
1. Defeat [[Magmadin]].
1. Defeat [[The Reformed Magolemus]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
