- *This quest is also part of [[The Thundering Steppes Timeline]].*

## Prerequisites

- You must speak [[Krombral]] (Giant-language) in order to receive this quest.
- You must have completed [[Little Tarby Lost]] to receive this quest.
- Tradeskill level 9.

## Notes

- You might want to do [[The Dusty Blue Stone]], a prerequisite for [[Restoring Ghoulbane]] while on this quest. It requires you to get 40 rock samples from wind swept stones, which can be done at the same time as one of the steps on this quest.
- You also might want to do [[Journeyman Service]] as it requires you to harvest 50 harvestables in The Thundering Steppes.

## Steps

*I examined the axe Obsid gave to me for saving his pet bear Tarby. The axe really might still have some magic left in it. I think I can repair it and return it to its former glory. I need to start by looking for a few stones to replace the cracked blade.*

1. Examine [[Cracked Stone Hand Axe]] that you received at the end of [[Little Tarby Lost]].
1. Mine 10 granite pieces, an uncommon/rare harvest from [[Gemstone Nodes#T3|wind swept stones]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]]. You can find many of these around here  {{waypoint 1249, 254, 269}}.
   - Upon receiving your 10th piece, you will get a quest update rewarding you [[A pristine piece of granite]] and the [[Granite Tomahawk Recipe]].
1. Scribe the Granite Tomahawk Recipe. At any forge, combine the pristine piece of granite with a piece of [[Severed ash]], a [[Sparkling material]], a [[Glimmering coal]], and the Cracked Stone Hand Axe to craft a Granite Tomahawk that creates a [[Dull tomahawk]].
1. Return to [[Obsid Boulderboom|Obsid]] to show him the dull tomahawk.
1. Sharpen the dull tomahawk by killing 100 skeletons in The Thundering Steppes.
   - Upon defeating your 100th skeleton, you will get a quest update rewarding you a [[Sharpened Granite Tomahawk]].
1. Return to Obsid Boulderboom to show him the Sharpened Granite Tomahawk.
   - **Note**: a timer for the next step starts as soon as you finish speaking to him.
1. Within a one hour time limit, kill 25 Skindancer Gnolls around [[Shrine of Decay]] at {{waypoint 391, 1, 1188}}.
   - [[Skindancer sentry|Skindancer sentries]], [[Skindancer seer|Skindancer seers]], [[Skindancer legionnaire|Skindancer legionnaires]], [[Skindancer knight|Skindancer knights]] and [[Skindancer defiler|Skindancer defilers]] all give updates for this step.
1. Return to Obsid Boulderboom.
1. Enter the [[Cove of Decay]] and slay the [[The Octagorgon]] for its ink. Note that this giant octopus cannot be pulled out of the water, and shortly after engaging it, a group of heroic skeletons will spawn.
   - **Note**: Once [[The Octagorgon]] is dead, consider examining the highlightable crates on the ocean floor underneath the spawn point to receive a [[Partially Digested Hand]], one of the required items in [[Stiletto's Orders Intercepted]].
