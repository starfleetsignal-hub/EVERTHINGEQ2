## Steps

1. Kill the following:
   - 6 [[A Nathsar stonebeak|Nathsar stonebeaks]] {{waypoint 131, -103, 415}}
   - 6 [[A Kunarkian tusker|Kunarkian tuskers]] {{waypoint 45, -93, 23}}
   - *Note: There is a good run of tuskers and stonebeaks from {{waypoint 96, -101, 108}} to {{waypoint 127, -101, 354}}*
1. Return to [[Merchant Vix]].

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- [[Glowing Gem of Fortitude]]
- +5,000 faction with **Riliss**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Military Issued Band]]
  - [[Military Issued Crest]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Military Issued Loop]]
  - [[Military Issued Ringlet]] (All Scouts)
