## Steps

All the following monsters can be found in [[The Thundering Steppes]].

1. Kill 5 [[An animated scarecrow|animated scarecrow]]s or [[An enraged strawwalker|enraged strawwalker]]s at the [[Fallow Farmlands]]  around {{waypoint 413, 2, -732}}.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 sirens (any type; [[An alluring siren|alluring]], [[An entrancing siren|entrancing]], or [[An irresistible siren|irresistible]]).  Some are swimming around {{waypoint -102, -25, -988}}.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill revenants in the [[Dead River Basin]] around {{waypoint 356, -22, 259}}.
   - 6 [[A revenant soldier|revenant soldier]]s.
   - 4 [[A revenant scout|revenant scout]]s.
   - [[A revenant commander]] near {{waypoint 240, -13, 277}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 2 [[A fallen giant|fallen giant]]s in [[The Valley of the Ancients]] around {{waypoint 700, 0, 620}}.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 2 [[An unbound familiar|unbound familiar]]s near [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] entrance around {{waypoint -614, -1, 744}}.
1. Read the book.
1. Visit the [[Circle of Thorns]], near the entrance to [[Sundered Splitpaw: Upper Tunnels]] at {{waypoint 264, -21, 1110}}.
   - **Warning**: [[Squire Wimbley]] spawns on the quest update (lvl 28-29 heroic) but you do not have to kill him.
1. Read the book.
1. Speak to [[Rikantus]] in [[Evernight Cemetary|Evernight Cemetery]] in [[Antonica]] at {{waypoint -1974, 9, 821}}.

## Rewards

- [[The Trials of Sir Morgan (House Item)]]
- At least 1g 21s
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Manacle of Innoruuk's Dragoon]] (Fighters)
  - [[Manacle of Innoruuk's Invoker]] (Mages)
  - [[Manacle of Innoruuk's Seraph]] (Healers)
  - [[Manacle of Innoruuk's Vigilante]] (Scouts)
