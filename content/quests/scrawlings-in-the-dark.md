*This quest is also part of the [[Antonica Timeline]].*

## Steps

All monsters and quest items are found within [[Blackburrow]] {{waypoint -1242.33, -9.26, 944.33}}.

1. Find a tome in a chest that is hidden behind crates at {{waypoint 18.04, -15.55, 49.64}} [**Level 2**](https://maps.eq2interface.com/poi.php?id=20370)
1. Kill the following for an auto-update:
   - 1 [[A Sabertooth diviner (Blackburrow)|Sabertooth diviner]] or [[A burly Sabertooth|burly Sabertooth]] at {{waypoint 6, -12, 2}}
   - 1 [[A Sabertooth chanter (Blackburrow)|Sabertooth chanter]] at {{waypoint -40, -19, -26}}
   - 3 [[A Sabertooth oracle (Blackburrow)|Sabertooth oracles]] at {{waypoint -21, -16, 89}}
1. Return to [[Rikantus]].

## Notes

- This is the very beginning of the Rikantus/[[Nektropos Castle]] storyline, which continues all the way through the expansion, Chains of Eternity, where we see two of the *Sisters from the Castle in Ethermere*. We learn here that Rikantus is a trader of rare trinkets.
- This content is heroic no matter which way you cut it. However, with [[Mercenaries|mercs]] and careful body pulls, you can survive it solo, especially with a pet class. Otherwise, a small group will be needed.
