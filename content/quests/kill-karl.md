## Notes

- Use the potion ONCE to enfeeble him on the pull.  Any other time that it is used, it will heal him to 100%, and you will have to start over.

- Do not let the x4 moniker fool you; once he has been shrunk at the very beginning, he can be killed easily with a fighter/shaman duo, or as a group.  Power may be an issue, tends to be a long fight with a small group.
