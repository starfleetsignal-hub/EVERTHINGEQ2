## Steps

1. Collect bones and shavings:
   - 5 corrupted bones. They look like large white logs lying on the ground.
     - {{waypoint 4265, -1086, 1887}} {{waypoint 4234, -1087, 1932}} {{waypoint 4221, -1086, 1954}} {{waypoint 4243, -1078, 1990}}
     - {{waypoint 4212, -1080, 2018}} {{waypoint 4235, -1093, 2147}} {{waypoint 4305, -1087, 2013}}
   - Shavings from an uncorrupted skeleton on the beach
     - {{waypoint 4384, -1091, 1543}} {{waypoint 4410, -1094, 1494}}
1. Speak to [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)|Osh]]
1. Speak to [[Merrik]] {{waypoint 4265, -1096, 2059}}
1. Speak to [[Nika]] {{waypoint 4250, -1094, 2055}}
1. Speak to [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)|Osh]]
1. Click Osh inside the instance to start the ritual
1. Kill the [[An infected pilgrim|infected pilgrim]] that spawns
1. Click Osh to zone back to Cobalt Scar
1. Speak to [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)|Osh]] and follow the conversation with Nika
