## Steps

1. Speak to [[Amaht Darmhaziz]] at the [[Mausoleum of Scholars]] {{waypoint 454, 244, -38}}.
1. Go to the [[Library of Erudin]] (must be a new instance - *update: can also be a cleared instance*) and collect 8 pages. These pages are found on and around tables and rarely around benches. There are 20+ possible locations. Some locations of the pages include:
   - {{waypoint -248, -2, 17}}
   - {{waypoint 37, 0, -120}}
   - {{waypoint -24, 0, -177}}
   - {{waypoint 34, 38, -1}}
   - {{waypoint -49, 38, 1}}
   - {{waypoint 95, 37, -77}}
   - {{waypoint -68, 37, -42}}
   - {{waypoint -51, 92, -153}}
   - {{waypoint -8, 153, -216}}
   - {{waypoint 41, 19, -202}} *Updated to correct error in coordinates*
1. Return to Amaht.
1. Find a chest at {{waypoint 271, 253, 104}}, near the Mausoleum (Amaht erroneously tells you that the chest is near the Gardens of Erudin). A group of 3 [[A Gruengach silencer|Gruengach silencers]] (all 90H bruisers) will spawn; kill them.
1. Return to Amaht.
1. Go to the [[Royal Palace of Erudin]] and search for Dartain. {{waypoint 175, 224, -49}}
1. Speak with Dartain/Al'Kabor on the 3rd floor center room behind the fake wall.
1. Kill the last named in Royal Palace of Erudin -- [[El'Arad, Lord Primarch of Erudin]].
1. Speak again with Dartain/Al'Kabor
1. Go to Kelethin and speak with Eva Corunno'thes {{waypoint 46, 93, 79}}
