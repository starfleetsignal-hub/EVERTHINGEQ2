## Steps

1. Collect the following:
   - 8 *cockatrice feathers* from [[An emerald stonegazer|emerald stonegazers]]. {{waypoint 267, -64, 219}}
   - 8 *tiger pelts* from [[A kunzar tiger|kunzar tigers]] or [[A mature kunzar tiger|mature kunzar tigers]]. {{waypoint 120, -36, -51}}
   - 5 *poison sacspp from [[A Vespid hornet|Vespid hornets]]. {{waypoint 916, 58, -125}}
1. Return to where Rhodoqiz Karazz was and speak to her [[Spectral Godmother]].

## Rewards

- At least 27g 88s 81c
- +3,750 faction with **The City of Jinisk**
