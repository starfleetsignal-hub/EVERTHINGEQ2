## Steps

1. Go back to the starting room and purchase a [[Dwarven Ale (Alcoholic)]] from [[Mraugl Stonecrusher]] the bartender in the [[Bar of Brell]]. {{waypoint 351.5, -21, 151}}
1. Return to [[Oldar Foambeard|Oldar]]
1. Travel to [[New Halas]] ([[Frostfang Sea]]). In the housing area is a distillery at {{waypoint -391, 174.5, -153.5}}, click it to "Pour a Mug of Brew."
1. [[Tetda Rockstein]] appears and demands you wrestle her to prove you are worthy of the Thurgadin Ice Brew.
   - Kill [[Tetda Rockstein (Named)|Tetda Rockstein]] - even con to your level.
   - Click the distillery to "Pour a Mug of Brew."
1. Return to [[Oldar Foambeard|Oldar]] in the [[Bar of Brell]].
   - [[Oldar Foambeard|Oldar]] has you drink the 'Thurgadin Ice Brew' and you are teleported randomly to one of the zones listed below:
     - [[Skyfire Mountains]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]] -- Level 90s, 80s, 50s
     - [[Steamfont Mountains]] -- Level 90s, 50s, 40s
     - The front area of [[The Mystic Lake]] -- Level 80s, 30s, 20s
     - [[The Feerrott]] -- Level 70s, 50s, 40s, 20s
1. Return to [[Oldar Foambeard|Oldar]] once again.
1. Now he's lost all of his stuff and he wants you to retrieve it.![brown sack of armor](images/Brown_sack_of_armor.jpg)
   - Go to [[Dropship Landing Zone]] in [[Moors of Ykesha]] and click on a brown sack on the top level of a platform at {{waypoint 1748, 472, 903.5}} to harvest the armor, once you collect it a [[Scrap worker]] and a [[Scrap harvester]] (same level as you the worker is vv and the harvester is v). You do not need to kill them to get the update.
     - Note: NO Group updates, each group member will need to click, and subsequently, get their own attack.![Oldar's Shield](images/Oldar's_Shield.jpg)
   - Go to [[The Stilled Sea]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] and click on a shield at {{waypoint -1353.5, -178, -737.5}} - near the Skystrider Launch Dock (airship), once you collect the item two [[Mad monkey|mad monkeys]] (same level as you a v and a vv) appear and attack you. You do not have to kill them to get the update.![a brown sack w- pants](images/A_brown_sack_w-_pants.jpg)
   - Go to [[Qeynos Castaway's Isle]] in [[Antonica]] (island next to [[Coldwind Point]] lighthouse island) and click on a brown sack at {{waypoint 237, -18.5, 875}} to harvest his pants, once you collect them a [[Stinky dankfur gnoll]] (same level as you even con) appears and attacks you.  You do not have to kill him to get the update.
1. Return to [[Oldar Foambeard|Oldar]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g @ Lvl 83

- One of the Following:
  - [[Hammer's Wrath Boarcrest Helm]]
  - [[Small Thurgadin Distillery]] x2 - (Required if you want to craft the items from the the recipe book, [[Brewday High-tech Distilleries]])
  - [[Hammer's Wrath Buckler]]
  - [[Glacierbane Axe]]
  - 8 x [[Beer Token]]
