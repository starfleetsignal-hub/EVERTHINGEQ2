## Steps

1. Kill the following mobs
   - Slay 6 kromrif shocktroopers. Around {{waypoint 315, -353, -526}}
   - Slay 4 kromrif lancers. (found in pairs near shocktroopers)
1. Speak with [[Captain Frozenshard]] (90^) at {{waypoint 488.74, -355.12, -486.31}}.  He's a 90^HEROIC.  Bring a buddy to help. Buddy won't be able to see him until he attacks you after dialog.
1. Kill Captain Frozenshard (90^)  and his dog [[Mordhund]] (90^)
1. Speak with Gareri Greatslasher
1. This opens up quest Axes to Grind. To get it talk to [[Cala Giantender]] {{waypoint 70.19, -331.31, -520.55}}
