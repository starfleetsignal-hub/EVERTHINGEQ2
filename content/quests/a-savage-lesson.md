## Steps

1. Go to [[Kaladim]].
1. Retrieve 3 *pristine bugbear pelts*. These are an uncommon quest update from any of the Terrok guards in the zone. They are very common from the front gate onwards. {{waypoint -12, 25, -130}}
1. Retrieve 3 *kobold pelts*. These are very common near the entrance, and around {{waypoint -175, 55, -225}}.
1. Return to [[Tychus Zeksworn]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Tychus' Altar of Rallos Zek]]
- +7500 faction with **Rallos Zek**
- Unlocks the blessing and miracle pair: [[Tactical Formation]] and [[Rallos' Devastation]]
