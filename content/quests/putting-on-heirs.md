## Steps

1. Speak with [[Lerad Boilfist]] in [[Qeynos Harbor (POI)|Qeynos Harbor]] {{waypoint 943, -25, 108}}
1. Repair 5 structural cracks in the Qeynos walls.  These can be found on the surrounding wall - they look like cracks surrounded by silver sparkles.  Possible locations include...
   - {{waypoint 898, -24, 82}}
   - {{waypoint 903, -24, 74}}
   - {{waypoint 907, -25, 57}}
   - {{waypoint 992, -24, -4}}
   - {{waypoint 1014, -24, -12}}
   - {{waypoint 921, -25, 25}}
   - {{waypoint 887, -25, -68}}
   - {{waypoint 844, -25, -51}}
   - {{waypoint 817, -20, -70}}
1. Return to Mav Boilfist
1. Speak with [[Marv Boilfist]] near the gate to the Baubbleshire {{waypoint 812, -21, -534}}
1. Enter [[The Baubbleshire: Vale's Shadow]] and...
   - Defeat 6 [[An invading nightblood|invading nightbloods]]
   - Rescue 4 trapped Baubbleshire citizens
   - Find [[Brelaine]] {{waypoint 842, -19, -436}}
1. Return to Marv Boilfist
1. Return to Mav Boilfist

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g @ lvl 95
