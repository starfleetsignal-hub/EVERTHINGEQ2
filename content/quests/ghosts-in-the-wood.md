## Steps

1. Go to [[The Crypt Grounds]] and look for interactable wind chimes. You will receive a status update when nearby. {{waypoint 353, -117, 920}}
1. Gather the [[Wind chime]].
1. Examine a machine next to a tree. {{waypoint 353, -117, 920}}
1. Return to Taskmaster Scally.

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- +5,000 faction with **Dalnir's Wheel Taskmasters**
