## Steps

![singing spores](images/Singing_Spores.png)

1. Consume a [[Fung-Eyesight 'Shroom]] to be able to see the spores.
1. Gather 12 singing spores along path around {{waypoint 232.06, 63.92, -261.73}}.
1. Return to Moyid.
