## Steps

1. Collect 5 of the wards buried around Wanderer's Dock
   - Look for sparkling piles of dirt on the beaches north and south of the NPC hub.  Clearing a pile of dirt reveals a ward.  Click again to harvest the ward.  The wards are about 50% likely to not be working anymore, you need 5 working wards.
1. Return to Joreye

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- One of the Following:
  - [[Short Far Seas Oar]]
  - [[Deadly Far Seas Belaying Pin]]
  - [[Gore-Ridden Hook of the Deckhand]]
  - [[Polished Rigger's Axe]]
