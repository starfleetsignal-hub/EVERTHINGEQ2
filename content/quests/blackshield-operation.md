## Steps

1. Obtain the up-to-date copy of the [[Order Book: Blackshield Landing Party|Blackshield orders]] from a chest in a camp on the northwest end of the beach north of Gorowyn Beach Outpost {{waypoint 2552, 12, 743}}
1. Return to [[Primary Sha'Ki]]

## Rewards

- At least 2s 86c
- The quest item: [[Order Book: Blackshield Landing Party]] becomes a house item
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Fighter's Bauble]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Bauble]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Bauble]]  (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Bauble]] (All Mages)
