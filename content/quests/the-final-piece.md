## Steps

1. Speak to [[An Ortallian treasure hunter]]. {{waypoint -606, -233, -1312}}
   - To reach him, jump into the [[The Bay of Anuk|Bay of Anuk]] and swim to his location. You can also take the carpet at the docks to the [[The Twin Tears|Twin Tears]], and jump down the [[The Eye of Anuk|Eye of Anuk]], which is connected to his cave.
1. Return to [[Rashad]], who will attack you. Dispose of him.
1. Return to the Ortallian treasure hunter for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Shimmering Gem of Mental Fortitude]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Scarab Carved Martial Circlet]]  (All Fighters)
  - [[Scarab Engraved Arcane Circlet]] (All Mages)
  - [[Scarab Etched Devout Circlet]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scarab Marked Covert Circlet]] (All Scouts)
