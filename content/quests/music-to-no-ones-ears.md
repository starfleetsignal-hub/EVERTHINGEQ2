## Description

The Far Seas Trading Company has asked if I've been anywhere near Zavith'loa or the Ossuary of late, and have heard the thunderous cacophony coming from within the caverns and the cathedral. I must say that I have, and that I agree with the Far Seas that the noise must be silenced.

## Steps

1. Kill the following bosses
   - [[Mo'iana the Ravenous (Advanced Solo)|Mo'iana the Ravenous]] in [[Zavith'loa: The Lost Caverns (Advanced Solo)]]
   - [[Valdimus V'Derun (Advanced Solo)|Valdimus V'Derun]] in [[Ossuary: Resonance of Malice (Advanced Solo)]]

- At least 5p 40g 35,000 status
