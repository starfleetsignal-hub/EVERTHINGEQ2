## Requirements

- No longer required to have a certain skill level to harvest, however there will be lots of failures until you reach 340 harvesting in each skill.

## Steps

1. Harvest 35 of each of the following from [[Kylong Plains]], [[Fens of Nathsar]], [[Kunzar Jungle]], [[Jarsath Wastes]], or [[Moors of Ykesha]].
   - [[Redwood lumber]] Kylong Plains - Calcified Timber
   - [[Raw cranberry]] Kylong Plains - Kylong Briar
   - [[Cabilis cocoa bean]] Kylong Plains - Klylong Briar
   - [[Rough kunzite]] Kylong Plains - Ornamental Geode
   - [[Deklium cluster]] Kylong Plains - Ornamental Geode
   - [[Raw succulent petal]] Kylong Plains - Kylong Briar
   - [[Torsis tea leaf]] Kylong Plains - Kylong Briar
   - [[Barracuda]] Kylong Plains - Southshore School
   - [[King prawn]] Kylong Plains - Southshore School
   - [[Lichenclover root]] Kylong Plains - Germinating Roots
   - [[Bristled pelt]] Kylong Plains - Varmint Burrow
     - *Note: the name of the harvest nodes are different in each zone, see the talk page for examples*
1. Return to Qho Augren.

## Rewards

- At least 27g 40s 54c
- Tradeskill experience
