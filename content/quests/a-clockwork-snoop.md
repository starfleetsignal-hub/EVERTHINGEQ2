## Notes

- You must be adventure level 35 before you can use the [[Gnome Observatron]].
- Remember to hail the observatron after placing it, otherwise it may despawn and you will have to reacquire the quest.

## Steps

1. Release and activate the first [[Gnome Observatron]] near [[Klak'Anon]]. {{waypoint -408, 108, 1294}}
1. Release and activate the second Gnome Observatron near Klak'Anon. {{waypoint -495, 116, 1179}}
1. Release and activate the third Gnome Observatron near Klak'Anon. {{waypoint -495, 113, 1392}}
1. Return to [[Waynil Garwater]].

## Rewards

- At least 2g 99s 88c
- [[Pulsing Gem of Prowess]]
