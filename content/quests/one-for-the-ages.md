## Steps

1. Obtain a complete log (Catalogue Creature) of the creatures in the area.  You must use the *[[Catalogue Creature]]* ability.
   - I must find [[A sinister sentinel]] (north-east of Thrael'Gorr Camp) {{waypoint 310, -93, 2551}}
   - I must find [[A drakonine guardian]]  (south of Thrael'Gorr Camp) {{waypoint 266, -18, 2714}}
   - I must find [[A lingering detriment]] (around the Pit of the Awakened) {{waypoint 131, -218, 2804}}
   - I must find [[A sootpaw direbear]] (Dragon's Head Drifts area) {{waypoint -357, -371, 3187}}
   - I must find [[A frostpaw direbear]] south of Ry'Gorr Keep {{waypoint -797, -338, 3124}}
   - I must find [[A fanged crawler]] (Dragon's Head Drifts area) {{waypoint -207, -279, 2885}}
   - I must find [[A cinder walker]] (south of Dragon's Head Drifts) {{waypoint -105, -337, 3400}}
   - I must find [[A molten sphere]] (south of Dragon's Head Drifts) {{waypoint -105, -337, 3400}}
