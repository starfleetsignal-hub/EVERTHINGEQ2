*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. You have 5 minutes to push the following four buttons, in any order:
   - {{waypoint -346, -12, 187}}; behind [[Varin N'Mar]]
   - {{waypoint -176, 29, 182}}; left of the pool, up the ramp on the right side of the roof of that building
   - {{waypoint -177, 26, 64}}; on the top of the northern building in the far left corner
   - {{waypoint -259, 1, 92}}; on the left side of the ramp across from the pool
1. Inspect the hidden compartment again.
