## Steps

NOTE:  This quest is optional for completing the Signature Quest.

1. Retrieve 10 artifacts from the grimlings
   - Kill the [[An acquisitive grimling|acquisitive]], [[An avaricious grimling|avaricious]], and [[A covetous grimling|covetous grimlings]].  They will drop [[Treasure Chest#Small Chest|small treasure chests]] with [[A remnant of a shissar artifact]] in them.
1. Defend yourself from 15 grimlings
   - Grimlings will attack you in groups of three 96vvHeroic - one [[An ambitious grimling|ambitious grimling]], one [[An eager grimling|eager grimling]], and one [[An overzealous grimling|overzealous grimling]].  Each dead grimling counts as an update, so you have to beat 5 groups of 3 grimlings. They seem to have less hitpoints than the others. They seem to spawn mostly near the huge rock and road nearby the digging site.
1. Return to Assistant Shortspanner

## Rewards

- At least 1p 29g 60s
- [[Miniaturized Temporal Decompiler]]
