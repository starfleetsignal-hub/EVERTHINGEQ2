## Description

King Vroak Vripp was once a respected leader of the froglok people, in ancient times past. Although he should have passed peacefully and handed down his mantle lifetimes ago, but has been consumed by the curse that has gripped the ancient city of Guk. The descendants of Guk have longed hoped that he would finally rest, and be freed from his eternal torment. I must locate him within the depths of the Lower Corridors of Guk, and see it done.

## Steps

1. Destroy [[King Vroak Vriipp]] in [[The Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors]]
1. Return to [[Ragheejb Yasir]] at Dropship Landing Zone
