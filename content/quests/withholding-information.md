## Description

The ancient researchers from Kor'sha likely left information regarding their experiments within the walls of Kor'sha. This could also be of interest not only to the iksar, but also to the sarnak of Bathezid's Watch. I should collect these fragments of research from within the facility, before it falls into the wrong hands.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Emperor Atrebe's Laboratory: The Fabled City of Kor-sha]] and look for 10 carved pieces of stone, detailing the research of the iksar
   - {{waypoint 25, 3, -109}} NE corner of Sentry Order West room
   - {{waypoint -17, 3, -76}} SW corner of Sentry Order East room, to the right of the firepot
   - {{waypoint -11, -1, -84}} to the right as you enter Sentry Order Chiasm (Sanedrac the Lost's) room
   - {{waypoint 8, -34, -106}} NW wall at the bottom of the elevator shaft
   - {{waypoint 32, -32, -95}} left wall of the corridor as you leave the elevator shaft, to the right of a weapons rack
   - {{waypoint 40, -35, -189}} right (East) side behind the first smashed wall
   - {{waypoint 11, -38, -148}} West side in corridor just after the first smashed wall
   - {{waypoint -13, -42, -238}} to the left of bench behind table in workshop
   - {{waypoint -24, -42, -246}} to the left of cabinet on the North wall of workshop
   - {{waypoint -48, -57, -213}} SW corner of Chamber of Evolution (Selgrak the Monster's) room
1. I should find any research notes that might be held by the researchers of Emperor Atrebe's Laboratory
1. Kill 10 researchers
1. Return to [[Lieutenant Kresic]]
