## Notes

- This is the good-aligned version of the world event quest. The evil-aligned version of this quest is [[Tempests of Mystery]].
- Kelethin residents can visit [[A Concordium Teleportation Scholar]] next to the bank in [[Kelethin]] to get ported to the North Qeynos Gate in [[Antonica]]. You can also get ported back to [[Kelethin]] from this location.

## Starting the Quest

Speak to [[A Concordium Pupil Adept]]. You'll find the Pupil Adepts all over Norrath. Check the [[Concordium Pupil Adept List]] for the locations.

## Steps

*While traveling, I happened upon a [[A Concordium Pupil Adept|Concordium Pupil Adept]] that was investigating the bizarre and certainly unnatural storms that have appeared across Norrath. They are very concerned about their nature and origin.*

1. You'll receive the [[A Concordium Pupil Adept's report]]. Bring it to [[Librarian Dungil]] through the blue portal in the [[Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos]].
1. Find [[The Inimicus Codex]] within the [[Concordium Tower]]. You'll find it next to [[Aristide Calais]], use the red portal. The Book is on the floor next to a table {{waypoint 710, 42, 109}}.
1. Deliver [[The Inimicus Codex]] to [[Librarian Dungil]].
1. You need to speak with a member of the Order of Flame inside the [[Shrine of Thunder]]. Head to [[Lavastorm]] and talk to [[Pallasa Rargon]], a Disciple of Flame.
1. You'll receive the [[Acumen Occultation Instrument]]. Return to [[Librarian Dungil]] at the [[Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos]].
1. [[Librarian Dungil]] sends you to speak to [[Seeress Ealaynya Ithis]] within the [[Temple of Life]] in [[North Qeynos]].
1. The Seeress will give you the holy [[Aab'I Hayat]]. Now you must use this potion on 9 tempest-affected creatures. You'll find these creatures under the Void-Storms across Norrath. For a comprehensive list of the locations check the [[Tempest affected Creatures List]]. You have to wear the [[Acumen Occultation Instrument]] to see the tempest-affected creatures.
   - **Warning!** &mdash; If doing this quest with a group and you mentor significantly below your normal level, do not under any circumstances cleanse a creature for your group, as the void beast which spawns will be your full un-mentored level.  ***HOWEVER***:  if you let the lower level person turn the quest in, everyone in the group gets the update.  So have 5 level 80's go with 1 level 70.  Let the level 70 turn in and 8 out of 10 times, the void beast will be level 70; however, EVERYONE will get the update!!!
   - Update As of 7/23 this quest no longer gives an update for every group member and you will need to kill 9 void beasts for each member...Also the quest EXP is about have the amount is was before the change.
   - You can /yell the void beast off, making it reset while you keep your update.
1. Return to [[Seeress Ealaynya Ithis]].

The Last Step of this Quest is repeatable. See [[Void Storms Continue]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s 40c (scales with level, @ L80 at least 9g 46s 1c)
- Suffix title: [[Tempest Chaser]].
