*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Go into the cave at {{waypoint -772, -11, -200}}:
   - Kill 4 [[A Cavemaw tunneler|Cavemaw tunnelers]].
   - Kill 2 [[A Cavemaw tamer|Cavemaw tamers]].
   - Destroy 3 supply crates.
1. Return to [[Magnon Baelvurn]].

## Rewards

- At least 1s 59c
- +90 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Plunderer's Small Link Chain Helmet]]
  - [[Seer's Rustic Coif]]
  - [[Footman's Worked Helmet]]
  - [[Theist's Shaped Plate Barbute]]
  - [[Adept's Woven Cowl]]
  - [[Naturalist's Sewn Cap]]
  - [[Initiate's Threaded Skullcap]]
