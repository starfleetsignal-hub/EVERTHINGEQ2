   1. Pot on the ground in [[The Bonemire]] at the [[Drednever Crash Site]] around {{waypoint -40, 43, 488}} or {{waypoint -82.39, 43.62, 507.92}} or {{waypoint -17.47, 43.63, 497.30}} or {{waypoint 6.33, 35.47, 489.66}}
   1. Collection of tools on the ground in [[The Bonemire]] near the entrance to [[The Halls of Fate]] around {{waypoint -26, -376, 30}} {{waypoint -2.05, -374.54, 38.83}} {{waypoint -13.86, -374.86, -6.49}}
