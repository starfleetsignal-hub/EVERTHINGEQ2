## Steps

- Kill 4 ghouls ([[A plodding ghoul|plodding]], [[A cannibalistic ghoul|cannibalistic]], [[A ravenous ghoul (Loping Plains)|ravenous]], etc.) {{waypoint 66, 39, -283}}
- Kill 7 [[A gremlin trickster|gremlin tricksters]] {{waypoint -1, 27, -371}} {{waypoint -115, 45, -204}} and {{waypoint 149, 44, -327}}.
- Return to [[Philip Glaslow]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- at least 9g 85s 50c
- [[Philip's Handkerchief]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Chain of the Ghoul Summoner]] (All Mages)
  - [[Chain of the Immortal Castle]] (All Priests)
  - [[Chain of the Lupine Warrior]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Chain of the Plainstalker]] (All Scouts)
