## Steps

1. Slay 15 [[A carrion hound|carrion hounds]] (level 10-12) or [[A carrion hound pup|carrion hound pups]] (lvl 10-11). They are found at [[The Dog Pond]] {{waypoint -1053, -51, -58}}
   - [[A fierce carrion hound|fierce carrion hounds]] (level 16-19) near the [[Commonlands Druid Rings]] {{waypoint 592, -60, -149}} also update.
1. Return to [[Mooshga]] for your reward.

- At least 1s 59c
