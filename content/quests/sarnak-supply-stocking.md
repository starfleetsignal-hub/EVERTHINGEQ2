## Prerequisites

![Danelak Hosfoak](images/Danelak_Hosfoak.jpg)

- ***If you plan to adventure you way to the needed faction,** you must start with the quest [[Favor of the Di'Zok]]
  - It is strongly recommended you mentor down to level 75 in order to be able to see the feathers and take the quests you need for faction.
- **If you plan to tradeskill you way to the needed faction,** you must start with the quest **[[Bathezid's Watch: Medical Necessities]]**.
  - Again, it is strongly recommended you mentor down to level 75 in order to see the feathers and take the quests you need for tradeskill faction.
- To clarify, your faction with Bathezid must be => -20k before he'll talk to you.
- Faction with [[Bathezid's Watch]] in [[Fens of Nathsar]] is required, or the NPCs will claim they do not know you when hailed.
- Quests [[Toothy Maws]], [[Blades of Steel]], [[Cloudy and Future Danger]], [[Last Minute Reprieve]], [[Advancing on the Advancers]], and [[Riliss Lieutenants]] give +5,000 faction each.
- Must be level 80 Crafter to be offered the quest.
- Once you complete [[New Lands, New Profits]], you can begin doing the supply writs to gain Bathezid faction, around level 70. You no longer require level 80 to start grinding faction.
  - Characters of low-adventure level *must* complete New Lands, New Profits or they will be killed on sight (KoS) by the residents of Bathezid.
- You only need to do the work that applies to your class. (Not every class, of course!)

Note:  If you don't zone in from Kylong Planes, go see Outrider Kelgyn for the quest Favor of the DiZork.

### Artisan's Epic Notes

- See the [[Artisan Epic Timeline|artisan epic timeline's main page]] for full details about the prerequisites, which have changed in the years since the series launched.
- When the quest is complete, Danelak will ask you about the Shatterlands beyond. If you chose the the text option about Rivervale, you are directed to go there and look for the next NPC for the next quest in the epic.
- As with most quests of this type, **Mass Production will not give you multiple updates**.  You have to actually craft the recipe 10 times each.

## Steps

1. Craft the requested items (for your class only)
   - *The crafted items do not appear in your inventory when made, but update the quest*
   - Alchemist
     - *uses 80 [[Mineral salt loam]] (ore-nodes plentiful at [[Camp Kromdek]] and [[Field of Bone|The Field of Bone]]), 40 [[Rough kunzite]], 20 [[Deklium cluster]] (gem-nodes plentiful on [[Field of Bone|The Field of Bone]]) 70 [[Smoldering candle]].*
     - Craft 100 [[Dedicated Elixir of Intellect]] (Alchemist Essentials Volume 75)
     - Craft 10 [[Smoldering Fists VI|Smoldering Fists VI (Journeyman)]] (Alchemist Essentials Volume 75)
   - Armorer
     - *uses 80 [[Ferrite cluster]], 40 [[Bristled pelt]], 20 [[Lichenclover root]] and 100 [[Smoldering coal]].*
     - Craft 10 Pristine [[Ferrite Melodic Mantle|Ferrite Melodic Mantles]] (Armorer Essentials Volume 75)
     - Craft 10 [[Ferrite Plate Spaulders]] (Armorer Essentials Volume 75)
   - Carpenter
     - *uses 80 [[Redwood lumber]], 40 [[Bristled pelt]], 20 [[Ferrite cluster]] and 100 [[Smoldering sandpaper]].*
     - Craft 10 [[Redwood strong box|Redwood Strong Boxes]]
     - Craft 10 [[Redwood stool|Redwood Stools]]
   - Jeweler
     - *uses 80 [[Mineral salt loam]], 40 [[Rough kunzite]], 20 [[Deklium cluster]], and 100 [[Smoldering coal]].*
     - Craft 10 runes of [[Torture VI]] Journeyman
     - Craft 10 runes of [[Double-Cross VI]] Journeyman
   - Provisioner
     - *uses 20 [[Raw succulent petal]], 50 [[Lichenclover root]], 10 [[Raw cranberry]], 60 [[King prawn]], 10xLiquid, 10xDough, and 120 [[Smoldering kindling]].*
     - Craft 20 [[Steamed King Prawn Dumplings]]
     - Craft 20 [[Succulent Mead]]
   - Sage
     - *uses 60 [[Redwood lumber]], 40 [[Lichenclover root]], 20 [[Deklium cluster]] and 100 [[Smoldering incense]].*
     - Craft 10 [[Grim Sorcerer V]] Journeyman
     - Craft 10 [[Synergism IV]] Journeyman
   - Tailor
     - *uses 50 [[Lichenclover root]], 80 [[Bristled pelt]], 10 [[Redwood lumber]], and 100 [[Smoldering filament]].*
     - Craft 10 [[Tailored Dexterous Bristled Leather Shoulder Pads]]
     - Craft 10 [[Tranquil Damask Shawl]] (The blue one, not the black one)
   - Weaponsmith
     - *uses 80 [[Ferrite cluster]], 40 [[Bristled pelt]], 20 [[Rough kunzite]] and 100 [[Smoldering coal]].*
     - Craft 10 [[Ferrite Morning Star]]
     - Craft 10 [[Ferrite War Maul]]
   - Woodworker
     - *uses 80 [[Redwood lumber]], 40 [[Deklium cluster]], 20 [[Bristled pelt]] and 100 [[Smoldering sandpaper]].*
     - Craft 10 [[Redwood Sorcerer's Staff|Redwood Sorcerer's Staffs]]
     - Craft 10 [[Redwood wand|Redwood wands]]
1. Return to [[Danelak Hosfoak]] for the reward
