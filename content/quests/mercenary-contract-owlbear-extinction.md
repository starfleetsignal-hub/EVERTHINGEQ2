## Steps

1. Kill 20 [[An ash owlbear|ash owlbears]], located east of [[Commonlands Station]] at {{waypoint -568, 38, 121}}.
1. Return to Pulsarian.

## Rewards

- At least 17s 43c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Dragoon Femur Bone]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Thexian Charm of the Battlepriest]] (All Priests)
  - [[Thexian Leadership Badge]] (All Mages)
  - [[Token of the Thexian Assailant]] (All Scouts)
