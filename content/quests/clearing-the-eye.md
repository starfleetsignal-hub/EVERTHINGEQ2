## Steps

1. Slay four [[An Erudian scholar|Erudian scholars]].
1. Slay four [[An Erudian observer|Erudian observers]].
1. Slay four [[An Erudian analyst|Erudian analysts]].
   - *Note: all 3 kinds of mobs can be found at [[The Eye of El'Arad]]*
1. Return to [[Thesslin]].

## Rewards

- At least 45g 70s 61c
