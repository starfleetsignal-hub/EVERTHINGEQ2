## Steps

1. Enter [[Blackhook Spiral: Frenzied Breach (Raid)]].
1. Defeat [[Orgain the Frenzied]] to progress towards the spiral center.
1. Defeat [[Taskmaster Peggs]] to progress towards the spiral center.
1. Defeat [[Overseer Decarun]] to progress towards the spiral center.
1. Defeat [[Foulgore the Rotten]] to progress toward the Spiral.
1. Talk to [[Thraine Frostbeard]] at {{waypoint -305.86, 116.72, 91.36}} (inside the raid zone).
