## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key.
   - Kill 15 [[An enchanted entrapper|enchanted entrappers]]  (carpets inside tents) between {{waypoint -14, 91, -489}}  and  {{waypoint -289.53, 117.17, -140.86}}
1. I need to find the Lydia Music Box key elsewhere.
1. Go to [[Takish Badlands]].
1. Find the Lydia Music Box key clue from [[A grove stalker|grove stalkers]] in [[Takish Badlands]] at [[Ruins of Takish'Hiz]] around {{waypoint 95.49, 125.02, -653.16}}
1. Examine the clue to receive the quest [[The Lydia Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the **Lydia Music Box** in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]].
