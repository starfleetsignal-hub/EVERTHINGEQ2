## Steps

1. Visit each of these locations in order: ![Green sparkles mark each patrol point](images/Replacing_the_Missing_Patrol_quest_-_Patrol_point.jpg)
   1. {{waypoint -158, 229, 3391}}
   1. {{waypoint 54, 223, 3693}}
   1. {{waypoint 670, 0, 4053}}
   1. {{waypoint 510, 198, 3966}}
   1. {{waypoint 670, 185, 4048}}
   1. {{waypoint 1077, 137, 3962}}
1. Return to [[Masih Najjar]] to complete the quest.

## Notes

- Each location is marked with a green sparkly effect on the ground.
- Using a leaping or flying [[Mounts|mount]] makes this quest much easier and faster to complete.
- If you are on a flying mount, you may need to land at each location to update the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
