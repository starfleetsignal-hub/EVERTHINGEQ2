## Steps

1. Speak to [[Malisha Viranta]] near the jar {{waypoint -453, 15, 77}}
1. Pick up the [[Malisha's Soul Trapper|jar]]
1. Speak to Malisha again
1. Speak to [[Colonel Adolar]] {{waypoint -452, 10, 10}}
1. Go in the area where the Wayward bandits are and steal the souls of 6 of them. This will work on multiples at the same time if you kill a group of them
   1. Kill a bandit
   1. Place the jar near the spirit
   1. click the jar to open the lid
   1. click the jar to pick it up, rinse and repeat
1. Return to [[Colonel Adolar]]
1. Return to Malisha to complete the quest

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Gravedigger's Mark]]
