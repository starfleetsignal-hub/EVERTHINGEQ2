## Prerequisites

- Neriak citizenship.

## Steps

- NOTE: The quest giver, and answers, you choose will determine the reward you get! See Rewards section below for more details.

1. Once you have the quest, speak with the other NPC listed above. (i.e., if you picked up the quest from Lararra, you will need to confront to Rilda).

## Rewards

- If you got the quest from Lararra inside the Spires of Innoruuk and choose, "I have been swayed by your dedication to the Dark Prince." while speaking to Rilda, one of the following:
  - [[Praise the Prince of Hate]]- slightly taller, with 1 humanoid skull on top and 2 demon skulls below
  - [[Dark Icon of Hate]]

- If you got the quest from Rilda inside the Darklight Palace and choose, "I have been swayed by your dedication to Her Highness." while speaking to Lararra, one of the following:
  - [[Glory to Queen Cristanos]]- slightly shorter, with 1 demon skull on top and 2 humanoid skulls below.
  - [[The Sacred Feminine]]
