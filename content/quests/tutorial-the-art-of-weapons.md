## Description

"The tradeskill tutor has asked me to try my hand at making weapons." --*Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Craft an [[Elm Greatstaff]] using your [[Woodworker]] skills.
   - Buy [[Basic Sandpaper]] to fuel the [[Woodworking Table]].
     - Components: 2 [[Severed elm]], 1 [[Lead cluster]], 1 [[Rawhide leather pelt (no-value) (Version 1)|Rawhide leather pelt]].
1. Craft a [[Tin hand axe]] using your [[Weaponsmith]] skills.
   - Buy [[Basic Coal]] to fuel the [[Forge]].
     - Components: 2 [[Tin cluster]], 1 [[Rawhide leather pelt]], 1 [[Rough malachite (Crate Reward)|Rough malachite]].
1. Return to the Instructor

## Rewards

- At least 25c
- [[Artisan essentials volume 5 (Uncommon)|artisan essentials volume 5]]

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Tradeskill Tutorial NPC: "Next we'll look at weapons. These can be made by both weaponsmiths and woodworkers. Weaponsmiths are masters of metallic weaponry including swords, axes, daggers, and hammers. Woodworkers are skilled at working with wood, creating wooden weapons such as staffs, batons, and cudgels, as well as bows and arrows and wooden bucklers and round shields for the defense of their allies. They also craft powerful totems that possess restorative properties. Practice both crafts by making a tin hand axe, and an elm greatstaff."
- Player Character: "I'll do my best."

I have created an elm greatstaff. I have created a tin hand axe. I have reviewed the process of crafting an item. I should return to the tutor with the weapons.
