## Steps

1. Obtain some fruit from the satyrs. Talk to [[NPC|Pruso Tenderhilt]] at {{waypoint -2369.15, -670.78, -191.09}} in [[Sanctuary of Waning]].
1. Attemp to calm a holgresh.
1. Talk to the calmed holgresh. Random, slightly rare update. (Note you may have to try talking to several calmed holgresh before you get a dialogue).
   - *Note a holgresh forager seems to work best*
1. Return to [[NPC|Researcher Eriun]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
