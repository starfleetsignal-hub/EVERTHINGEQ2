## Steps

1. Kill an [[Alux marshsifter]] {{waypoint -711.23, -53.60, 111.10}} along the shore in [[Drukyna Marsh]] and loot [[A crudely sketched map]].
1. Examine the [[A crudely sketched map|crudely sketched map]] in your inventory to get a clue where to search for [[Annuk’ka]].
1. Investigate the nesting area in the northeastern part of [[Drukyna Marsh]] {{waypoint -822.20, -50.29, 29.47}} and kill the 2 [[Nest watcher|nest watchers]] that ambush.
1. Inspect the nearby ***makeshift nest*** {{waypoint -822.20, -50.29, 29.47}} that likely belongs to the sphinx.
