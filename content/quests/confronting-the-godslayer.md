From the Stonebrunt Highlands enter [[The Vasty Deep|Vasty Deep]] {{waypoint -159.91, 174.49, -368.56}}.

Then go to [[The Abandoned Labs]] door {{waypoint 0.08, -37.63, 21.69}}and select *Perah'Celsis' Abominable Laboratory* as your destination.

## Steps

1. Retrieve a binding thread from [[Perah'Celsis (Epic)|Perah'Celsis]] from within [[Perah'Celsis' Abominable Laboratory]]
   - Killing [[Waansu]] also updates this step of the quest
1. Return to [[Fyr'remd Lorak]] near the Quel'ule Station
1. Defeat [[Roehn Theer (Palace of Roehn Theer)|Roehn Theer]] in [[The Palace of Roehn Theer]]
1. Speak to [[Overlord Lucan D'Lere]] also in [[The Palace of Roehn Theer]] in the same room as Iilsaad's Barrier

## Rewards

- At least 41g 63s 17c
- 19760 status @ L90.
- 16890 status @ L101
- One of the following:
  - [[Fiery Fragment of Aeteok]]
  - [[Glimmering Remnant of Enoxus]]
  - [[Resonating Shard of Enoxus]]
  - [[Surging Essence of Aeteok]]
