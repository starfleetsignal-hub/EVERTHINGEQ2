## Steps

I must make my way to the Temporal of the Wurm Mother, defeating all of the Awakened Legion that I encounter along the way.

1. Find your way to the Great Hallway.
   - Hail any of two drakes that sit on both sides of the Ancient Bridge (you can use any of the portals in the zone to get there) and agree to proceed. It will take you to the Great Hallway
   - The update is: *A drake flew me down to the Great Hallway!*
1. Defeat [[Sevalor Gorescale]] in battle.
   - He will guaranteedly drop [[A Vial of Time Distilled Protoplasm]] (you can use it on [[Dagarn]] and [[Dozekar The Cursed]] to turn the fight into a challenge mode) and [[Ancient Skyshrine Control Wand]] (this wand is required in a battle against [[Dozekar The Cursed]] and also - on successful completion of the quest - unlocks any of the ancient mercenaries in [[Skyshrine: The Forbidden City of Dracur]], which is accessed via the portal in [[Skyshrine: The City of Dracur]] at {{waypoint -33, 0, -9}}).
1. Defeat [[Malteor Flamecaller]] in battle.
1. Defeat [[Dozekar The Cursed]] in battle.
1. Return to [[High Commander Vyrin]] to complete the quest.
