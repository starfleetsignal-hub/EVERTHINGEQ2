## Steps

1. Meditate at the [[Ancient Rings]] in [[Greater Faydark]]. The update takes a long time, I nearly reported it as a bug {{waypoint -295, 50, 29}}
   - This is not the [[Druid Ring (GFay)|druid ring]] near [[Kelethin]], but a non-functional ring. It is reachable by a climbable tree at {{waypoint -340, 26, 5}}.
1. Meditate near the tree stump on [[Tidesylph Isle]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]. You are in the right spot when you see the white text: "A feeling of tranquility washes over you". Be prepared to wait for a full minute (or more). {{waypoint 533, 43, -121}}
1. Return to [[Pacificator Merrek]].
1. Speak with [[Sister Sam]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]] and defeat her in combat. {{waypoint -688, 130, 940}}
1. Examine the [[A sparring partner|sparring partner]] item that she gives you. Defeat the clockwork that spawns.
1. Speak with [[Julius]] in the [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] {{waypoint -611, 175, 993}} and have him fix the sparring partner for 10g.
1. Return the clockwork to Sister Sam and meditate with her.
1. Return to Pacificator Merrek and meditate with him.

## Rewards

- Item Reward: [[Symbol of The Tranquil's Might]] or [[Symbol of The Tranquil's Mind]]
- +2500 faction with **Quellious**
