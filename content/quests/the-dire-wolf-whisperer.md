## Prerequisites

- You must be at least adventure level 55 to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Capture a [[A dire wolf (Loping Plains)|dire wolf]] using the [[Dire Wolf Muzzle]]. {{waypoint -444, 10, -252}}
1. Return to Wolfmaster Heinrich.

## Rewards

- At least 13g 99s 72c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Werewolf Loop of the Unkind]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Werewolf Ring of the Unkind]] (All Priests)
  - [[Werewolf Ringlet of the Unkind]] (All Mages)
  - [[Werewolf Signet of the Unkind]] (All Scouts)
