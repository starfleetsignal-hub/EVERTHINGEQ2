## Steps

1. Collect 8 baby snakes near the [[Lower Tendril River]]. {{waypoint -164, -57, 89}}
   - Kill [[A docile slither|docile slithers]] and/or [[An agitated slither|agitated slithers]] and collect the babies that spawn on the ground after their death. They are very tiny any may be hard to see.
1. Return to Calnozz.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
