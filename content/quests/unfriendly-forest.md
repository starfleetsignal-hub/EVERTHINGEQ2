## Steps

1. Talk to [[A Far Seas mariner]] near the dock {{waypoint 1235, 19, 1197}}
1. Speak to [[A forest guardian]] at [[The Blessed Knoll]] {{waypoint 413, 34, 760}}
   - Low level adventurers might wish to keep to the south side of the isle and go to Knoll from SE side to avoid getting knocked down from the sky
1. Harvest 3 [[Tunarian Trumpetleaf]] plants around Blessed Knoll
   - ![Tunarian Trumpetleaf](images/Tunarian_Trumpetleaf_visible.jpg)Plants are trackable, always up, respawn quickly, and are heirloom house items that stack.
1. Speak to the mariner at the dock

## Rewards

- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]]
- [[Healing Poultice]] recipe
- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
