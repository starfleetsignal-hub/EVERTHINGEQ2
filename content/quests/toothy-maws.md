## Steps

1. Obtain 12 uncracked sabertooth fangs dropped by [[A sabretooth scuffler|sabretooth scuffler]] (trackable) found near {{waypoint -27, -63, 780}} and {{waypoint -60, -39, 1373}}.
1. Return to Vitaliz.

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- +5000 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Blackguard's Sabertooth Bone Manacle]]
  - [[Champion's Sabertooth Bone Manacle]]
  - [[Curate's Sabertooth Bone Manacle]]
  - [[Sorcerer's Sabertooth Bone Manacle]]
