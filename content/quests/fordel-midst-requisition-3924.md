## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 25 [[Coruscating Coal]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest the following:
   - 10 [[Humming Azurite]]
   - 18 [[Radiant Algae]]
   - 50 [[Luclizite Clusters]]
1. Craft a [[Azurite Inlaid Throne]] on a [[Work Bench]].
1. Return to [[Xavi Heliul]] in [[Aurelian Coast]] at {{waypoint 161.79, 81.47, -456.49}}.

## Rewards

- At least 5934 status
- 4 [[Spectral Mark of the Haven]]
- [[Empyral Stocking Crate]]
