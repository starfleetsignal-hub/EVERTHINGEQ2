## Description

"The Tuwasp bixies can't be allowed to establish a new hive, because they are a threat to the spirit blossoms in the Nursery. Sentry Talvee asked me to chase off the bixies by uncorking a vial of Tunare's Breath near their hive." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Use [[Vial of Tunare's Breath]] given to you by Sentry Talvee near the Tuwasp Hive {{waypoint -137, -54, -592}}.
1. Return to Sentry Talvee.
