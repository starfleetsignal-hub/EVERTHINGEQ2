## Prerequisites

- Able to speak [[Thulian]].
- At least adventure level 60.

## Notes

- Consider doing the other Kingdom of Sky Heritage Quests and Signature Quests ([[A Thorn of Old]], [[Blood of the Brood]] and [[A Mark of Awakening]]) concurrently with this quest since many of the steps take you to the same dungeons.

## Steps

1. Harvest five dragon bone parts in [[The Bonemire]], which only spawn if the player is on this step of the quest. They are trackable, spawn in the general area of these locations, and have a short (1-3 minute) respawn timer. **NOTE:** Bones do not necessarily respawn in the same location as collected by a group mate.  Tracking is important here. ![](images/A_pile_of_dragon_bones.jpg)
   - **Dragon Bone Wings** - [[Shattered Weir (POI)|Shattered Weir]]: {{waypoint -747, -439, 392}}, {{waypoint -624, -450, 415}}, {{waypoint -738, -450, 349}}, {{waypoint -757, -439, 388}}
   - **Dragon Limbs** - [[Drednever Crash Site (POI)|Southern Island]] (Drednever Crash Site): {{waypoint 192, 62, 799}}, {{waypoint 333, 59, 764}}, {{waypoint 180, 63, 785}}, {{waypoint 334, 54, 706}}.  Also found at {{waypoint 214.57, 67.39, 769.61}}
   - **Dragon Skull** - [[Isle of the Ravasect (POI)|Isle of the Ravasect]]: {{waypoint 947, -106, 171}}, {{waypoint 855, -110, 187}}, {{waypoint 883, -103, 156}}, {{waypoint 918, -118, 284}}, {{waypoint 847, -113, 289}}, {{waypoint 959, -111, 332}}
   - **Dragon Bone Tail** - [[Cacotoxic Stain]]: {{waypoint 411, 198, -618}}, {{waypoint 424, 198, -637}}, {{waypoint 418, 199, -491}}, {{waypoint 359, 200, -552}}
   - **Dragon Ribs** - [[Remnants of Xoch]], part of the central island (Halls of Fate): {{waypoint 239, -355, 11}} , {{waypoint 239, -355, -27}}
1. Return to [[Sinephobis]] in the Tenebrous Tangle at {{waypoint -104, 265, 871}}. He will give you a recipe to make [[An ornate phylactery]] and demands that you gather rare [[Harvestables (Tier 7)|Tier 7 harvests]] ([[Tenebrous Tangle]], [[The Barren Sky]], [[The Bonemire]], [[Loping Plains]]) in order to craft the phylactery:
   - [[Blue-silver sheet]] from ore nodes
   - [[Perfectly cut purple geode]] from ore nodes
   - [[Perfect osseous lumber]] from wood nodes
   - [[Strip of supple leather]] from animal den nodes
   - You can also buy these off the broker or be traded them from another player. If you already have the items, you can place them in a harvest depot or sales display and remove them for the update.
1. Go to the forge in the [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]] and craft [[An ornate phylactery]]. {{waypoint -116, 65, -34}}
   - You must create a *pristine* item or you will fail and your components will be lost.
   - The recipe requires one [[Scintillating incense]], a T7 fuel.
   - You can use the [[Commission System]] for this step. If you use the [[Commission System]] be sure the person you are commissioning uses their instructions otherwise it will take your instructions.
1. Return to Sinephobis in the Tenebrous Tangle. {{waypoint -104, 265, 871}}
1. Steal the soul of [[Do'ellin the Young]] on the Isle of Ravasect in the Bonemire at {{waypoint 686, -166, 261}}. When Do'ellin gets down to about 10% health, he will break the encounter and appear as an NPC, [[Do'ellin]], with '<Subdued>' under his name. At this point, use [[An ornate phylactery]] on him to steal his soul and receive [[Do'ellin's phylactery]].
   - As you approach the location, he may take a moment to spawn. Start the fight from the waterfall side and/or in the pool, or his knockback might throw you off the island.
   - High-levels should take care not to one-shot him since he may get stuck at 1% (mentor down to level 65).
   - Do'ellin <Subdued> will despawn in less than 60 seconds (he'll emote "If this is the way is has to be..." just before depop).
   - If you accidentally aggro the Subdued version, try using the phylactery on him. If it fails to absorb his soul, you will need to kill him to get [[Do'ellin the Young]] to spawn again (~15 minute respawn).
   - If you have a full inventory, you will not receive Do'ellin's phylactery. You will have to craft another ornate phylactery and fight Do'ellin the Young again.
1. Return to Sinephobis in the Tenebrous Tangle. {{waypoint -104, 265, 871}}
1. Within six minutes, place the rods that you received at the following locations:
   - [[Fear Tainted Isle (POI)|Fear Tainted Isle]]: {{waypoint -100, 265, 875}} (next to Sinephobis)
   - [[Gazer Isle (POI)|Gazer Isle]]: {{waypoint 755, 459, -103}} (take the cloud from Fear Tainted Isle)
   - [[Bixie Isle (POI)|Bixie Isle]]: {{waypoint -506, -60, -853}} (take the cloud from Gazer Isle, to Ravasect Incursion Point, and then to Bixie Isle)
   - If an issue arises while placing the rods, check the discussion page.  If you run out of time, return to Sinephobis and restart the step.
1. Return to Sinephobis. He will summon Spirit of Do'ellin, a 70^^^ ghost dragon, nearby. **Allow Do'ellin to kill the priests of fear, or the quest may bug out. If you kill him prematurely you will have to wait 30 minutes for respawn.**
1. Kill [[Spirit of Do'ellin]]. {{waypoint -96, 265, 906}}
   - The ghost dragon is considered a massive [[Underconned|undercon]] with a nasty **AOE** knockback. Get yourself positioned into a corner, where the temple stones and the mountain wall meet together just behind the dragon.
1. Speak to [[Sinephobis]] for your reward.
