- *Adventurers formerly had to complete this questline in order to gain access to the instance, [[Cove of Decay]]. This is no longer required, however adventurers may still obtain and complete the quest series.*

## Steps

1. Complete these three tasks in any order:
   - Kill [[Incaulebis the Maleficent]] (31^^^ Heroic) {{waypoint -467, 0, 851}} near the back entrance to [[The Ruins of Varsoon]].
   - Kill [[The Banished]] (30^^^ Heroic) who roams around {{waypoint 957, -1, 1079}}.
   - Speak to [[Cassondra]] at {{waypoint 1210, 3, 334}} and obtain [[The Adornment of Stormsunder]].
     - *The Adornment of Stormsunder* must be completed to progress this quest.
1. Return to Wiseman Oluran.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 21s
- [[Faceted Leatherfoot Earring]] - fighter
- [[Hoop of Halfling Honor]] - scout
- [[Earring of the Leatherfoot Guard]] - mage
