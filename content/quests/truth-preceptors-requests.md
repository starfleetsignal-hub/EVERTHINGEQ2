*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Gather 4 crocodile eggs. These can be found in [[The Croc Caves]] {{waypoint -1152, -212, -485}} at the crocodile nests. You need to right-click the nests and "search the nest" to get an egg, then the nest disappears.
1. Collect 5 venom glands from [[A dune spiderling|dune spiderlings]]. {{waypoint -1076, -140, -379}}
1. Kill 1 [[An escaped Rujarkian pitfighter|escaped Rujarkian pitfighter]]. They can be found near spiderlings and their placeholders are [[A burdened Rujarkian raider|burdened Rujarkian raiders]].

## Rewards

- At least 2g 99s 47c
- -2500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- +2500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -2500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
