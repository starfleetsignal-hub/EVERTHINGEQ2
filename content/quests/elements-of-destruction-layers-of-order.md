## Notes

![](images/Inert_Vegarlson_Purifying_Rune.png)

- ***You may not receive all of the [[Tectonic fragment|tectonic fragments]] needed for this quest on one run through the zone.** (Note: When I did this quest, I killed the Stones of Thudos even though they were non-aggro, before I killed the mobs near them. I received more than enough tectonic fragments to complete the quest.)*
- The quest is listed as heroic but can be soloed
- You receive an [[Inert Vegarlson Purifying Rune]] when you click on the conflagration of earth to start the quest.

## Steps

![](images/Tectonic_fragment.png)

1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 260, -20, -285}} to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]]. Use the portal at {{waypoint 774, 412, -335}} and enter [[Vegarlson: Council's Stronghold (Solo)|Vegarlson: Council's Stronghold [Solo]]].
1. Progress through the zone. (See [[Vegarlson: Council's Stronghold (Solo)|Vegarlson: Council's Stronghold [Solo]]] for strategies).
1. Kill mudglops and vekerchiki to obtain 19 [[Tectonic fragment|tectonic fragments]].
   - The drops are uncommon. One is dropped for each party member.
1. Use the [[Inert Vegarlson Purifying Rune]] from the quest helper to recieve the [[Imbued Vegarlson Purifying Rune]].
1. Proceed through the rest of the zone and kill the Council of Four at {{waypoint 1340, 784, 39}}
   - <s>Note that you cannot log out during this zone. If you do, the [[Stone of Thudos|Stones of Thudos]] in the zone will respawn, preventing you from opening any door leading higher.</s> (As of July 24, 2021, it is possible to re-enter the zone an hour later, access every level; find the Portal to the Heart of Thudos and complete the quest.)
1. Use the **Portal to Heart of Thudos** that appears at {{waypoint 1501, 784, 42}}.
1. Click on [[The Thudos Corestone]] at {{waypoint -216, 113, 160}} to cleanse the [[The Thudos Corestone]] of the errant elemental aspects.
