## Steps

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -280.48, 180.72, 0.42}} to enter [[Sanctus Seru: Echelon of Divinity (Solo)|Sanctus Seru: Echelon of Divinity [Solo]]].
1. Defeat the following:
   - 12 [[Luminary interceder|Luminary interceders]]
   - 12 [[A Luminary arbiter|Luminary arbiters]]
   - 13 [[An Arx Luminary|Arx Luminaries]]  *<s>Note: You must progress through most of the zone first. Be careful not to kill the luminaries until you've killed the required interceders and arbiters.  There are a limited number, so you may need to reset the zone to complete this step.</s> (01/21/2023: The Arx Luminaries are now part of the same step as the interceders and arbiters. As a result they may all be killed together.)*
   - STOP:  Before you proceed, click on the link below and go to the walk through. You MUST obtain and blow the horn or you will have to start all over. The Arx Luminaries DO NOT appear until you blow the horn and talk to Unhilynd. Follow the steps in the walk through.
   - *See [[Sanctus Seru: Echelon of Divinity (Solo)|Sanctus Seru: Echelon of Divinity [Solo]]] for help proceeding through the zone.*
   - **NOTE**: I confronted and killed Lord Triskian Seru BEFORE I finsihed killing all the Luminary Interceders, Arbiters and Arx luminaries. I DID NOT get the update, even though I went back and finished killing the luminaries. after he was defeated.  You MUST kill all the required luminaries before confronting Lord Triskian Seru.
1. Speak to [[Lord Triskian Seru]] at {{waypoint 124.09, 79.06, 3.85}} and defeat him.  Note: the epic mobs are not really a threat, destroy chronospheres as soon as you can attack them.
1. Return to [[Hulda Flintbraid]] at {{waypoint -72.44, 88.88, 257.57}}.
