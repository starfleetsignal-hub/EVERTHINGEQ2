## Starting the Quest

Read the mail from Captain Ethan Dariani with the subject "Greetings once again"

- Note: Mail is not required. If you did not receive it, or if it was deleted, simply speak with [[Ethan Dariani]] in the Far Seas tavern in [[Isle of Mara]] at {{waypoint -89.17, -2.26, 104.82}}
- Must be tradeskill level 100 to receive mail/quest.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Ethan Dariani]] in the Far Seas tavern in [[Isle of Mara]] at {{waypoint -89.17, -2.26, 104.82}}
1. Speak to [[Trader Cylianna]] on the Isle of Mara at {{waypoint -37.44, -2.70, 142.04}} and complete the quest [[Assay of Origin]].
1. Speak to Ethan to update quest.
1. Speak to [[Dror Ironstein]] at {{waypoint -232, 214, -191}} in Butcherblock at "Irontoe Outpost West". He will ask you to complete the sub-quest [[Ore of Yore]].
1. Speak to [[Forgemaster Gert]] at {{waypoint 915.78, -274.46, 121.67}} in the City of [[Thurgadin]] and complete the quest [[More Ore of Yore]].
1. Consult an expert of the Underfoot.
   - Speak to [[The Duality (The Duality's Vigilant Sanctum)|The Duality]] in [[The Duality's Vigilant Sanctum]] {{waypoint 1849, -371, 3451}} below [[Paineel]] in [[The Sundered Frontier|Sundered Frontier]] (if you have [[A Vigilant Sanctum Key]] you can use that to port directly to [[The Duality's Vigilant Sanctum (Mythic Passage)]] and then zone out (Use the door not the portal) and back into (The What Lies Beneath version) and complete the following quests
     1. [[Underfoot Defender]]
     1. [[Subtunarian Subterfuge]]
     1. [[Into the Unknown]]
1. Explore [[Maldura]] and complete the following quests:
   1. [[Stranger in Distress]]
   1. [[Menace in the Mine]]
   1. [[Scanning the Seals]]
   1. [[Monitoring the Situation]]
   1. [[Attuning the Portal]]
   1. [[Monitor Malfunction]]
   1. [[Researching a Solution]]
   1. [[Containing the Stone]]
      - Update: If you have already done all the subquests for Maldura, just go and speak to Brytthel at the top of the zone in the Palace of Unity.
1. Return to [[Ethan Dariani|Ethan]] in Mara.

## Rewards

- At least 6p 30g 13,200 status
- You get to keep both the Empowered Censer and this one however if both are in your bag and one is used to call the fuel merchant they are both on the same timer.
- One of the following:
  - [[Tranquil Censer of Confinement]] -- *Mages and Priests*
  - [[Embellished Censer of Confinement]] -- *Scouts, Warriors, and Brawlers*
  - [[Dedicated Censer of Confinement]] -- *Priests and Scouts*
  - [[Hardened Censer of Confinement]] -- *Warriors and Crusaders*
