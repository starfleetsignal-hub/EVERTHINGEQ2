## Steps

1. Click 6 stacks of crates and barrels around the camp:
   - {{waypoint 4378, -822, 388}}
   - {{waypoint 4438, -821, 366}}
   - {{waypoint 4428, -822, 432}}
   - {{waypoint 4417, -821, 448}}
   - {{waypoint 4346, -822, 469}}
   - {{waypoint 4352, -821, 421}}
1. Craft 5 [[New Combine Equipment Revamp: Instructions for a Journeyman]] at an [[Engraved Desk]] {{waypoint 4422, -821, 363}}:
   - Examine the [[Material List]] in your inventory, then scribe the resulting [[Recipe: New Combine Equipment Revamp]] (Listed as *Equipment Revamp: Instructions for a Journeyman* in your recipe book). Requires 5 [[White peony tea leaf]], 5 [[Effulgent incense]] and the material lists
1. Speak to 4 craftsmen:
   - [[Horc]] {{waypoint 4236, -820, 438}}
   - [[Ishkar]] {{waypoint 4368, -822, 461}}
   - [[Eshkan]] {{waypoint 4371, -821, 381}}
   - [[Carron]] {{waypoint 4335, -821, 461}}
1. Write the [[Report For High Command]] at an engraved desk:
   1. Right-click and examine the [[Notes on the Journeymen]] in your inventory and scribe the resulting [[Recipe: High Command Report]]. Requires 1 effulgent incense, the remaining Instructions for a Journeyman, the remaining Material List and the 4 Notes on the Journeymen
1. Retun to Quentin

## Rewards

- 7 [[A Mysterious Blue Coin]]
- At least 1p 30g
