## Steps

![epizoa polyp](images/Epizoa_polyp.png)
![Terminus Tech Electro-defender](images/Terminus_Tech_Electro-defender.png)

1. Gather 16x [[Epizoa Polyp Goo]] from epizoa polyps in Coral Forest. You pick up 2 goos at a time. Possible locations of polyps include:
   - {{waypoint -185, 0, 443}}, {{waypoint -145, 7, 475}}, {{waypoint -86, 5, 484}}, {{waypoint -72, 3, 448}}
   - {{waypoint -86, 0, 398}}, {{waypoint -50, -1, 361}}, {{waypoint -35, 3, 338}}, {{waypoint -86, 0, 341}}
1. Teleport to [[Ver'Bohten Atoll]] area of the zone using the large portal marked ***Curious Mechanimagica*** {{waypoint -321, 14, 475}}.
   - **DO NOT** use the spindle zone portal to instance ***To Oogothl Sprawl: Ver'Boten Atoll***.
   - *Note: Right click the portal to zone into the Atoll.*
   - *Note: If there are multiple, ungrouped people attempting to complete this step then your priority should be the destruction of the mechanimagica defenses as they have a slow respawn rate while the mech grazers have a high respawn rate.*
1. Destroy the following
   - Destroy 16x mech gazers weakened by the goo.
     - Use the [[Epizoa Polyp Goo]] on them first, then kill them.
     - *This works on: [[A mechaneyez preserver]], [[A mechaneyez treasurer]], [[A mechaneyez trove keeper]]*
     - *If you run out of goo, you can return to Coral Forest and get more.*
   - Destroy 8x mechanimagica defenses. These are Terminus Tech Electro-defender, can be tracked. They respawn. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -680, 165, 224}}, {{waypoint -724, 180, 151}}, {{waypoint -728, 145, 369}}, {{waypoint -767, 201, 388}}
     - {{waypoint -779, 171, 142}}, {{waypoint -813, 196, 256}}, {{waypoint -852, 148, 303}}, {{waypoint -853, 149, 302}}
     - {{waypoint -863, 160, 219}}, {{waypoint -866, 200, 291}}, {{waypoint -882, 200, 276}}, {{waypoint -886, 169, 128}}
     - there is more of them on upper level at ***Chancellor's Court***, just finish killing mechaneeyez and destroying Electro-defenders before [[Ziggin]] will spawn.
1. Continue to explore Ver'bohten Atoll - use the one-way teleporter ***Teleport to Chancellor's Court*** at {{waypoint -728, 179, 171}} (be prepared to fight) then speak with [[Ziggin]] at {{waypoint -866, 197, 115}}
1. After [[Ziggin]] attacks you fight him until he escapes in a puff of smoke when down to 5% health.
1. Continue to ascend Ver'bohten Atoll in search of clues
   1. use the ***Teleport to Ver'Bhten Rise*** teleporter at {{waypoint -760, 200, 160}}
   1. Move to the ledge at {{waypoint -785, 270, 191}} then jump down to the lower platform.
   1. Use the one-way teleporter ***Teleport to Baron's Pinnacle*** at {{waypoint -825, 251, 264}}.
   1. Move West around the edge then North to observe [[Ziggin]] and [[High Lord Glazereyez]] at {{waypoint -793, 294, 282}} (under the green dome)
   - *Do not engage in combat with [[Derolictor]] (lvl 143 Heroic ^^^).*
1. Return to [[Xhol'Rog]].
   - Using an appropriate spell teleport within the zone back to the Obelisk, or return down to the ***Mechamagica Teleporter*** at {{waypoint -723, 143, 333}} to return close to [[Xhol'Rog]].
   - If you don't have flight from the Tradeskill Timeline then taking precautions (e.g. [[Parachute]]) you can jump down at:
     1. {{waypoint -771, 288, 272}}
     1. {{waypoint -787, 250, 194}}
     1. {{waypoint -716, 195, 214}}
     1. Then use the [[Mechamagica Teleporter]] at {{waypoint -723, 143, 333}}
