## Steps

![harvestable wood with sparkles](images/Wood_for_Sonset_quest.jpg)

1. Gather fish and wood:
   - harvest 10 [[Fresh thresher fin]]s from "a shiver of threshers" fish nodes off the shoreline
   - harvest 5 wood from either the regular *felled hardwood* node or fallen logs with silver sparkles
1. Kill [[Gurakuur (Named)|Gurakuur]] in [[Lost Khorr]] {{waypoint 487, 74, -999}}
1. Double-click (or right-click and "Burn") Gurakuur's body
1. Go to [[The Three Sacred Sisters]] and click the sparkles between two of the lakes {{waypoint 318, 182, -391}}
1. Return to Urra

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
