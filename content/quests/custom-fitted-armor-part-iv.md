This is part 4 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Freeport]] who wear Chain and Plate Armor (with the exception of [[Shaman|shaman]] types).

- If you are from [[Qeynos]], [[Kelethin]], or [[New Halas]], then your version of the quest is called [[Ian's Forgetful Family - Part IV]].
- Mobs modified slightly as of LU32/33

## Steps

1. Kill a variety of denizens within [[Edgewater Drains]] for fun and profit.
   - Kill 10 [[Sewage viper|sewage vipers]] for their forging tools. **level 3** at {{waypoint 107.96, 2.76, -122.34}}
   - Kill 12 [[A drudge mucus|drudge mucus]] slugs for their caustic fluid. **level 1** at {{waypoint 130.12, 33.00, -58.75}}
   - Kill 12 [[A contaminated rat|contaminated rats]] for the hell of it. **level 2** at {{waypoint 116.31, 16.15, -94.15}}
1. Return to [[Dyric Pire]].
