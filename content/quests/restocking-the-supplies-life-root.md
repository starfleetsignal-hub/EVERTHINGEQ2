## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Harvest 10-18 'life root'. These are unique harvesting nodes found among the roots of trees on [[The Temple Grounds]] (rare) and Breeding Grounds (not as rare). The quest can be deleted immediately and re-obtained if you wish to have to harvest fewer to complete.
   - You will have to cope with the monsters on Breeding Grounds if you go there (level 62). Try the 2 trees at main entrance {{waypoint -2, 20, 208}} in between each root of the trees. Then wait and they will spawn again.
1. Return [[Surgeon Kweezil]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- 5 [[Soft Bandages Test]]
- First turn-in: good faction with **The Lost Children of Marr**
