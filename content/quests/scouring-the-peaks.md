## Steps

Note: Bring an iceclad cutlassfish with you for the next step (harvest-able item from [[Icy Fingers]] zone area) for the [[Milly's Meals]] quest.

1. Talk to [[Capru]] in the [[Goahmari Village]] {{waypoint 347, -15, 518}} in the [[Great Divide]]
   - To save yourself a trip and get the next quest, after talking to Capru, right-click on a spider and click 'pet'. This will place a new quest feather over [[Capru]], opening up the next set of quests starting with [[Milly's Meals]].
1. Return to [[Aggi Stonefist]] to finish the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
