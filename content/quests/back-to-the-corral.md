## Steps

1. Break 8 of 12 **Mech Materials Chests**. Possible locations include:
   - At docks {{waypoint -463.31, -14.96, -587.42}}.
   - Just past docks {{waypoint -435.17, -15.09, -506.76}}.
   - At the start of the first bridge {{waypoint -638.37, 28.63, -405.27}}.
   - Just inside the gate in [[Diakaroo Bonanza]] {{waypoint -724.60, 61.81, -488.08}}.
   - In the [[Plaza de Piñatas]] {{waypoint -599.76, 62.29, -456.97}}.
   - In the [[Plaza de Piñatas]] {{waypoint -590.03, 62.26, -459.26}}.
   - In the [[Plaza de Piñatas]] {{waypoint -591.60, 61.45, -490.57}}.
   - In the [[Plaza de Piñatas]] {{waypoint -632.46, 62.69, -479.18}}.
   - Beyond the gate exiting [[Plaza de Piñatas]] {{waypoint -553.82, 61.71, -577.71}}.
   - Beyond the [[Rodeo de Joro]] {{waypoint -584.70, 62.07, -669.34}}.
   - In the area outside [[Sergio]]'s pen {{waypoint -647.05, 61.70, -660.35}}.
   - In the area outside [[Sergio]]'s pen {{waypoint -663.19, 62.63, -618.21}}.
   - In the area outside [[Sergio]]'s pen {{waypoint -712.97, 62.40, -595.75}}.
1. Kill 11 [[Diakaroo boltshooter|Diakaroo boltshooters]], [[Diakaroo bulldozer|bulldozers]], [[Diakaroo sawbones|sawbones]], and [[Diakaroo outrider|outriders]].
1. Return to [[Bridon Kizzlebash]].
