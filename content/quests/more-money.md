This quest is part 3 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Gnome Series.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Malyk]] {{waypoint -24, 3, 92}} in a house just off the docks to the north.
1. Speak with [[Malvir Destrin]] {{waypoint 6, 3, 28}} near the [[South Freeport]] gate.
1. Speak with [[Darsus Tavalin]] {{waypoint -26, 3, 87}} in the eastern corner of Temple Street.
1. Return to [[Neezer Grund (Temple Street)|Neezer Grund]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s
- [[A bacon-scented backpack]]
