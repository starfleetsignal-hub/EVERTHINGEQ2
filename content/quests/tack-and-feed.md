## Steps

1. Enter [[Chardok]] and obtain the following:
   - 4 *kromhair lariats* (rope) from [[The Barracks of Bathezid]]: {{waypoint -330, -18, -520}}
   - 2 *rhino mincemeat stacks* from [[The Rhinoceros Pen]]: {{waypoint -390, -48, -391}}
   - 1 *jug of sokokar water* from [[The Water Chamber]]: {{waypoint -481, -85, -363}}
   - 6 [[Grime crawler roe]], body drops from [[A grime crawler|grime crawlers]]: {{waypoint -470, -67, -103}}
1. Return to [[Skyrider Tosuj]] in [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]].
