## Steps

1. Search one of the beds {{waypoint -471.1, -0.22, -147.16}} in [[The Laughing Trout]]. {{waypoint -467, -2, -115}}
1. Gather 10 hides from [[A vale deer|vale deer]] in Rivervale (uncommon update). They spawn on a ridge area from {{waypoint -270, 10.4, 100}} to {{waypoint -282.92, 9.57, -48.45}}.
1. Gather 5 spiked wings from [[A lamia soulsiren|lamia soulsirens]] (uncommon update). They are found near [[Shakey's Farm]] between {{waypoint -335.07, -1.47, -278.90}} and {{waypoint -351.72, 0.04, -213.98}}.
