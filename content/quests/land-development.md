## Steps

1. Perform the following tasks:
   - Kill 8 creatures on the [[Metetherial Plains]].
   - Collect 6 eggs from plumewit nests in the [[The Stratafold|Stratafold]]. ![a plumewit nest](images/A_plumewit_egg.jpg)Possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -92, 42, 395}}
     - {{waypoint -101, 49, 529}}
     - {{waypoint 217.82, -8.66, 385.08}}
     - {{waypoint 292, 67, 352}}
     - {{waypoint 296, 63, 322}}
     - {{waypoint 393, 72, 356}}
     - {{waypoint 438, 43, 360}}
     - {{waypoint 630, 82, 781}}
     - {{waypoint 631, 64, 716}}
       - *(Egg respawn time is about 2 minutes)*
1. Speak to [[Uelmondi]] at {{waypoint 418, 36, 39}}.
1. Gather soil from a patch of infused soil at {{waypoint 453, 30, -185}} or {{waypoint 724, -11, -401}}.![a patch of infused soil](images/A_patch_of_infused_soil.jpg)
1. Speak to [[Uelmondi]] at {{waypoint 418, 36, 39}}.
