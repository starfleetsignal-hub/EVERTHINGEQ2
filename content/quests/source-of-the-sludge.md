## Description

"Thekela would like me to search for the sludge that the orcs used to poison the Emeral River. She suggested that I search Orc Hill, to the west of Sapling Spur Outpost." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Head to Orc Hill and collect 3 bubbling buckets called 'sludge sample' {{waypoint 247.71, 67.77, -205.66}}
   - Make sure to look all around the orc tents as well.
1. Go back to [[Thekela Gillywump]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s 57c

- Thekela Gillywump: "We need to find out what kind of poison they used, so we can work to counteract it."
- Player Character: "Where would it be?"
- Thekela Gillywump: "The orcs are concentrated to the west of here, atop Orc Hill. that would be the best place to look."
- Player Character: "I'll go have a look."

I have picked up several sludge samples. I should return these sludge samples to Thekela. I need to bring these sludge samples back to Thekela.

- Player Character: "Yes."
- Thekela Gillywump: "Yuck! This is viscous, and foul. It's a wonder it didn't kill off all the fish. What manner of creature would stoop to something so craven?"
- Player Character: "An orc, evidently."
- Thekela Gillywump: "I will get this substance to our alchemists in Kelethin. I have hopes that they will devise a way to clean up this mess."
- Player Character: "Good luck."

I have given Thekela the sludge samples. I have brought Thekela the sludge samples.
