## Steps

1. Defeat 50 creatures within the iron and copper realms. In areas such as:
   - [[Iron Flats]]
   - [[Minstrel's Cradle]]
1. Destroy a solo boss encounter. For example:
   - [[Balimon]] in [[Fiddtan Crest]] at {{waypoint -450.59, 277.41, 616.19}}
   - [[Firethrone]] in [[Iron Flats]] at {{waypoint -323.30, -41.37, -441.81}}
   - [[Zox]] in [[Iron Flats]] moving around {{waypoint 110.15, -44.57, -374.35}}

## Rewards

- At least 4p 31g 10s 14c 67,200 status
- 3 [[Zimaran Ballad]]
- [[Well Wrought Gear Crate III]]
