*This quest in unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Kill [[Guerilla Lorkil]]. He automatically pops as you move around the area southwest of the monks, in a variety of places:
   - {{waypoint 291, -92, -1047}}
   - {{waypoint 355, -112, -1339}}
   - {{waypoint 612, -89, -870}}
1. Return to [[Shing Ho]].
