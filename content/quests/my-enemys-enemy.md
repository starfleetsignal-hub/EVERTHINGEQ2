## Steps

1. Speak with [[Hrodryr]] in the hollow tree north of the [[Lakeshore Ruins]]. {{waypoint 637, 22, -352}}
1. When you finish Hrodryr's dialogue, use the [[Homing Beacon]] from your inventory.
1. Return to [[Borbin Happens]] at the docks.

## Rewards

- At least 14g 81s 38c
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- One of the following based on class:
  - [[Arcane Sage's Shoes]] (All Mages)
  - [[Dauntless Gladiator's Sabatons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Fearless Pathfinder's Chain Shoes]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Feral Rainkeeper's Leather Boots]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Otherworldly Ritualist's Chain Boots]] (Shamans)
  - [[Resilient Grandmaster's Foot Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Righteous Augur's Plate Boots]] (Clerics)
