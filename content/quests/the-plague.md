*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Harvest three glands from four different types of diseased creatures in the Feerrott.
   - 3 Diseased Dregs - found in area south of camp around normal dregs, near Bouncer Flerb. {{waypoint 22, -12, 371}}
   - 3 Diseased Spiders - found in the area of Cythan in the southwest area of zone, near Bouncer Hurd. {{waypoint 11, 18, 826}}
   - 3 Diseased Sattars - found in the area east of falls, near thule sattars. {{waypoint -576, 0, 152}}
   - 3 Diseased Dragonflies - found around Allize Tae Outpost, just before the Gulch of Thule. {{waypoint -1200, 0, 636}}
1. Return to Cvaka to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 69s 57c
