## Notes

- This is a subquest for [[The Word of the Harbingers - Qeynos]] and [[The Word of the Harbingers - Freeport]] and can only be attempted while you are on that quest.
- The Word of the Harbingers - Qeynos and the Word of the Harbingers - Freeport were both removed (from quest pages: *Removed when the Beastlord class became available in LU62) *so the subquest listed here is defunct.

## Steps

1. Head to Enchanted Lands and gather 5 berries.  Gather ripe berries and avoid gathering rotten ones.
   - The berry bushes are in plain sight from the docks.  The good ones sparkle.  The bad ones are surrounded by green smoke.
   - Gathering from the bad bushes doesn't hurt the quest.  White text reading "This berry bush seems withered" appears on the screen.
1. Return to Elder Patriarch Vuzx
