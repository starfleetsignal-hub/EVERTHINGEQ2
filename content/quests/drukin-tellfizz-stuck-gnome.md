## Steps

1. Help [[Drukin Tellfizz]] escape the ledge.
   - Equip the bow and the grappling ammo he gives you and shoot the dragon bone on the ledge across. The dragon bone becomes targetable with a white targeting arrow pointing down to it. If you lose the white targeting arrow before you get a shot off, talk to Drukin again to be re-targeted.
1. Speak with Drukin once you guys have made it to the other side.

## Rewards

- At least 5g
