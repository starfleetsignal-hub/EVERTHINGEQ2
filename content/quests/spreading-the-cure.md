## Steps

1. Prophet Bainyn Fairwind of Rodcet Nife has commissioned you to distribute a cure potion to several individuals across the Shattered Lands.  He has stated some will be reluctant to receive these potions, but you must insist upon it.
   - [[Ry'na Easadana]] in the Dragoons of Thex Camp in [[Lavastorm]] outside Najena's dungeon {{waypoint -86, -129, 340}}
   - [[Abel Haleborn]] outside the cavern to Miragul's Phylactery in [[Everfrost]] {{waypoint -180, 10, -430}}
   - Alodia Lifespring in the Village of Somborn in [[Loping Plains]] {{waypoint -414.14, 9.97, -18.89}}
   - Sadami Ishigoki in the [[Village of Shin]] on the Isle of Mara {{waypoint -35.37, 3.00, -25.95}}
   - [[Giddy Sprysprocket]] at the Drednever Crashsite in [[The Bonemire]] {{waypoint -45.29, 43.9, 562.62}}
   - Jayashri outside the Court of Blades in [[Maj'Dul]] {{waypoint 20.79, 149.58, 130.61}}
   - Celest I'alna in Teren's Grasp within the [[Kylong Plains]] {{waypoint 2161.00, 520.00, -897.14}}
   - [[Fresa Wealgear]] in the Department of Gnomeland Security in [[Steamfont Mountains]] {{waypoint -594.91, 174.93, 1080.59}}
1. Return to Prophet Bainyn Fairwind with the news of your efforts.
