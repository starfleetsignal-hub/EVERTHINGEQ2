## Description

Several creatures have been summoned, and remain entrapped within the area of Miragul's Phylactery known as The Anathema. With no master and a lifetime of entrapment, these beings are likely to be enraged and hostile to all who enter. I've been asked to destroy as many of the summoned and enslaved creatures within The Anathema.

## Steps

1. Kill 12 summoned and enslaved creatures within [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Anathema]]
   - Any non-named creature except for the furious sparks and furious resurgences will count
1. Return to [[Chief Scholar Lakewind]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
