*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Collect poison from the following monsters:
   - Kill 10 [[A dustwhip scorpion|dustwhip scorpion]]s. {{waypoint -340, -110, -950}}
   - Kill 5 [[A skeletal cobra|skeletal cobra]]s. {{waypoint -833.5, -118, -967.5}}
1. Return to [[Assad M'Lyshad]].

## Rewards

- At least 13g 59s 50c
- On the third completion:
  - [[Desert Cobra Plushie]]
- On the ninth completion:
  - [[Skeletal Cobra Plushie]]
- On 15th completion:
  - [[Iron Cobra Plushie]]
