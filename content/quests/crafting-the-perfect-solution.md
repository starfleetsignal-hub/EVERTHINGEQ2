## Steps

1. Scribe [[Recipe: Distilled Icy Essence]] and craft it at a chemistry table {{waypoint 4314, -821, 443}}. Requires 1 [[Matoppie roots]], 1 [[Effulgent candle]] and the 10 [[Enchanted Ice]] from the previous quest
1. Scribe [[Recipe: Wyrmbane Solution]] and craft it at a chemistry table. Requires the [[Distilled Icy Essence]], [[Distilled Essence Treatment]] (received at start of quest) and 1 effulgent candle
1. Return to Angor

- At least 1p 30g
