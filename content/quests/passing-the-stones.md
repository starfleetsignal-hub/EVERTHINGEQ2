## Steps

1. Go back to the 3 caves. In each cave, stand on the raised platform near one of the pillars and examine the okoute stones in your backpack, then choose the option to drop one here. This can be done while invisible.
1. Return to [[Pyrissa Flamecog]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g
- +2250 faction with **Order of Flame**
- [[Earring of Utmost Knowledge]]
