## Notes

- None of the harvestable items are trackable.
- All of these clicks are **zone-wide group** updates.  If you are grouped anywhere in the zone, your group members will receive updates.

## Steps

1. Gather the following ingredients from [[Darklight Wood]], the "level 1-20 newbie zone" next to [[Neriak, City of Hate]]:
   - ![Harrowglade Beetle](images/Harrowglade_Beetle.jpg)Gather 4 Harrowglade Beetles around the Druid Ring at [[The Harrowglade]] {{waypoint 161, -47, 287}}
   - ![Rotbeak Eggs](images/Rotbeak_Eggs.jpg)Gather 4 Rotbeak Eggs around [[Nerian Highlands]] {{waypoint 29, -33, -211}} between a lot of [[A rotbeak|rotbeaks]].
   - ![Nightshadow Cheddar](images/Nightshadow_Cheddar.jpg)Gather 1 Nightshadow Cheddar at [[Wanderlust Fair]] {{waypoint 259, -19, -4}} ![a nightshadow Cheddar](images/Questitem_NightshadowCheddar.jpg)
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]].

## Rewards

- [[Days of Summer: 2020]] useable at [[Pas Yu]], the merchant beside [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]] on the [[Spire Island]].
- Unlocks Wrist, Charm, and Head from Panda Merchant. (Search for "Shadow")
