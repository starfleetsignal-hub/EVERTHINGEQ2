## Steps

1. Return to [[Sapswill's Rest]] on [[Isle of Refuge]] and click on a crate {{waypoint 1219, 18, 1266}} to drop off the samples. ![Drop samples off here](images/Skrits'_crate.jpg)
1. Travel to the [[Wanderer's Dock]] on [[Kithicor Island]] in [[Phantom Sea]] {{waypoint 1362, 24, 1188}}
   - Fly north off Tranquil Sea map to zone into Phantom Sea
1. Speak to Skrit, passed out on the ground {{waypoint 1211, 18, 1282}}.

## Rewards

- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]]
- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
