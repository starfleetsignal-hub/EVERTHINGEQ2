*This is part 3 of a 3-part series that gives access to the [[Shattered Vale]]*

## Steps

1. Locate the **Unkempt Druid Ring** in [[Antonica]], in the [[Glade of the Coven]] to the southwest of [[Windstalker Village]], at {{waypoint -1442.40, -5.65, -233.73}}
   - *There is not actually a visible druid ring, but when you approach this location the mob(s) will appear.*
1. Kill the ring of Unkempt druids - as of 2017 this is a single [[An Unkempt harbinger|Unkempt harbinger]]
1. Return to [[Rask Helstot]] in The Elddar Grove.

## Reward

- At least 10s 31c
- [[Earring of the Shattering]]
