Upon inspecting the statue, I heard the voice of Rallos Zek and felt strongly compelled to carry out its will!  In war there are casualties!  A great warrior must cull the weak!  I will kill giants within Old Kael.

## Steps

1. Carry out the will of the statue and slay those Kromzek who are weak.  I will kill giants within Old Kael. (15)

- [[Primal Velium Shard]]

- +50 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
