## Steps

Zone into [[Zavith'loa: The Lost Caverns (Heroic)]].

1. Kill the Master of Beasts - Kill [[Beastmaster Kau'mua]] and [[Ceracuspis]] at (-3,907, -5, 420).
1. Slay [[Mo'iana the Ravenous (Heroic)]]. Ring even. Kill the Kindred of Mo'iana to spawn the name.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 16g 20000 status
- 15 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- One of the following:
  - Crate of Armor (Chain, Cloth, Leather or Plate)
