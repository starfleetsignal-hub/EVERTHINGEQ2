## Steps

Defeat all encounters in [[Karuupa Jungle: Dedraka's Descent (Heroic II)]]:

1. Bury the unburied.
1. Trample the tyrant.
1. Avenge the drakeflies.
1. Pluck the pteranodon.
1. Conquer the cannibals.
