## Steps

1. Click on the recipe book next to Broosha and scribe *Makin Stuf Da Grobb Way* which gets placed in your inventory. This only needs to be done the first time you do one of the Goodies for Grobb quests, as you will get all of the recipes needed.
1. Collect the material you will need in Grobb, specifically:
   - 15 [[Grobb shellfish]], which can be found next to the ponds in the swamp near Grobb, e.g. {{waypoint -31, -81, -507}} or {{waypoint -81, -87, -426}}.
   - 15 [[Congealed goo]], which can be collected from [[Slimy slug|slimy slugs]] found in a conduit around {{waypoint -444, -72, -964}}.
   - 10 [[Smoldering kindling]], purchased from fuel vendors.
1. Make 10 *swamp goulash* using the recipe you scribed earlier. Each pristine combine makes 2 goulash.
1. Leave the goulash in the delivery bin next to Broosha.

## Rewards

- At least 27g 11s 70c
- -500 faction with **Guktan Guard**
- +2000 faction with **Clan Grobb**
