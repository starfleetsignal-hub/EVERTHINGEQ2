## Description

The Craftkeepers Task Coordinator has requested assistance in restocking Paineel's harvest supplies.

## Steps

1. You are asked to gather 50 non-culinary local harvests.
   - This means no food or fish nodes, but you can harvest anywhere in [[The Sundered Frontier]].
1. Return to [[Oolioomon]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +2,500 faction with **Craftkeepers**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Craftkeepers Daily Supply Tasks
