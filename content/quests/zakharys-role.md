## Steps

*Adrad Postumius has ordered me to deliver a message to Cannoneer Zakhary near the Rime Wall.*

1. Find [[Cannoneer Zakhary]] {{waypoint 530, -372, 1066}}
1. Inspect Zakhary's body.  You receive a [[Rime Ballista Key]]
1. Fire the ballista (left click it)
1. Return to Adrad
