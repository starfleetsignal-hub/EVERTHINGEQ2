## Notes

- requires 23,500 [[House Vahla (Faction)]]

## Steps

![Metetherial Fibre Strand (Balanced)](images/Metetherial_Fibre_Strand_%28Balanced%29.jpg)![Metetherial Fibre Strand (Extreme Performance)](images/Metetherial_Fibre_Strand_%28Ext_Perf%29.jpg)

1. Collect 9 strands of metetherial fiber in the [[Metetherial Plains]] near {{waypoint 88, -4, 517}}.
   - Hint: Watch your graphic details. <s>Need "High Performance" or better to be seen!</s> Strands are visible with both **Balanced** and *Extreme Performance* and with nearly everything on low.
   - They look like blue floaty wisps up close and like a light blue tornado from a distance (see picture). ![light blue tornado from a distance](images/MetetherialFibre_farDistance_veryLowPerformance.jpg)
   - Follow the white areas in the landscape, they seem to spawn at intersections.
1. Deposit the strands in Metetherial Ewers back in Khali'Vahla
   - Possible locations include {{waypoint -132, 576, -571}}, {{waypoint -221, 574, -543}}, {{waypoint -203, 574, -523}}, {{waypoint -82, 574, -696}}, {{waypoint -94, 574, -722}} ![Metetherial Ewer](images/Metetherial_Ewer.jpg)
   - There is a knockback shortly after each deposit. Put your back to a wall, or take to the air to avoid the knockback.
1. Return to to [[Mindfold Matriarch Taneesha]] near the fountain at {{waypoint -232, 576, -732}}.
1. Speak with [[Chandira]] at {{waypoint -247, 578, -629}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
