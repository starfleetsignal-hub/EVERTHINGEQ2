## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Collect 3 [[Infused Turtle Shell|infused turtle shells]] (random body drop from several creatures in Zimara Breadth)
1. Use the [[Infused Turtle Shell|infused turtle shells]] and empower an altar. Random spawn in [[Fens of Nathsar]], it's yellow sparkles are visible from far away. Known locations:
   - South of [[Sokokar Post Ruins of Cabilis]] {{waypoint -237, 35, -1005}}
   - South East of [[Sokokar Post Drogan Exiles]] {{waypoint -679, -201, -1100}}
   - Near the northern zone to [[Kunzar Jungle (POI)|Kunzar Jungle]] {{waypoint -1432, -4, -403}}
   - In [[Camp Kromdek]] {{waypoint -873, -316, -1659}}
   - Near [[Bonedigger Burrows]] {{waypoint -2035, -310, -1430}}
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Oceanlord]] {{waypoint -1765, -402, -2728}} in [[Fens of Nathsar]]
