## Steps

1. Deliver Arjon's dinner invitation to Finnegan.
1. Return to Arjon {{waypoint -1378.59, -533.50, -2143.70}}.
   - Finnegan will move to Arjon and Misa's campfire {{waypoint -1377, -533, -2144}} after this quest is turned in. You can accept the quest [[Letter to Thurgadin]] at either location.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
