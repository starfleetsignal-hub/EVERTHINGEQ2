## Note

- Upon hailing or completing dialogue with each of the five spirits, a random jump scare will flash on the screen. One or two zombies may fill the screen; the screen may turn purple and an Avatar of Fear may appear behind the ghost; the screen may fill with intermittent static and a skull will chomp at the screen.

## Steps

1. Speak to 5 restless spirits in the various graveyards across [[Norrath]] and convince them to return to the Ethernere. This can be done in any order. Note: Some will attack you but not all. It appears to be random, though in-general the NPCs with quest bubbles over their head will not attack, while the ones without will attack after the dialog. The names of the ghosts are interchangeable; see the talk page for a list of names.<br>**Possible locations:**
   - [[Antonica]]:
     - Below the [[Tower of Vhalen]] {{waypoint -1296.45, -15.35, 70.68}}
     - [[Evernight Cemetery]] near the waterfall to [[Thundering Steppes|The Thundering Steppes]] {{waypoint -1984.06, 9.11, 806.87}}
     - Behind a timberclaw gnoll camp between the [[Tower of Vhalen]] and [[North Qeynos]] {{waypoint -959.07, -7.75, 431.08}}
     - Graveyard northwest of [[Windstalker Village]] {{waypoint -1747, -13, -738}} and {{waypoint -1755.13, -13.46, -749.10}}.
   - [[The Commonlands]]: [[Turmoil Cemetery]] {{waypoint -1321.55, -69.33, 329.85}}
   - [[The Thundering Steppes]]:
     - Graveyard south of the [[Fallow Farmlands]] {{waypoint -0.94, 2.31, -678.61}}
     - [[Coldwind Shores Cemetery]] north of the western shore  {{waypoint 1346.80, -2.26, -85.85}}
     - On an island in the [[The Grave Pool|Grave Pool]] {{waypoint 128.04, -22.22, 757.91}}
   - [[Nektulos Forest]]: Cemetery next to [[Nektropos Castle]] {{waypoint -1297.70, 117.71, -1825.74}}
   - [[Enchanted Lands]]: By the [[The Fellowship Tree|Fellowship Tree]] {{waypoint 347.84, 28.13, -935.51}}
   - [[Lavastorm]]: By the DarkCleanse Family Gravesite in the southern part of [[Dagger Spires Pass]] {{waypoint -738.64, -112.00, 439.64}}
   - [[The Sinking Sands]]: In the resting ground in the NW corner of the map {{waypoint -372.23, -178.87, -1466.65}}
   - [[Loping Plains]]: [[Somborn Cemetery]] {{waypoint -431.74, 15.89, 71.26}}
   - [[Cobalt Scar]]: Just outside [[Scarstone]] {{waypoint 4351.46, -821.06, 328.63}}
   - The Mystic Lake: In front of the climbable wall that leads up to Tomb of the Sensei {{waypoint 34.44, 0, 66.88}}
1. Return to [[Setri Lur'eth]] in Nektulos Forest.  Upon talking to her, you will be teleported to your major city hub (Qeynos, Freeport, or Haven).
