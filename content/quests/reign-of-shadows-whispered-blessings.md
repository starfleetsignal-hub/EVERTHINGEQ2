## Steps

1. Speak to [[Head Scholar Nabihan]] in the [[Luclin Nexus]] of the [[City of Fordel Midst]] {{waypoint 375, -36, 776}}.
1. Return to the [[City of Shar Vahl]] and speak to [[Hymnist Wadakha]] at [[The Jharin Assemblage]] {{waypoint -84, 31, -410}}.
1. Enter [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] via the [[Shadeweaver Gate]] {{waypoint -353, 92, 178}} and begin preparing for the offering ceremony to the Feral Spirit.
   - Find a way to prepare my soul.
     - Complete quests [[Feral Offerings]], [[The Whole Owlbear]], and [[Thieves in the Thicket]] to automatically complete this step.
   - Find a way to have my body prepared.
     - Complete quests [[Spirits in the Night]] and [[Spirits Amongst Them!]] to automatically complete this step.
1. Speak with [[Haadirr Darjin]] in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] {{waypoint -480, 158, -796}}.
1. Return to [[Hymnist Wadakha]] in [[City of Shar Vahl]] {{waypoint -84, 31, -410}}.
   - *He will not have the book over his head at this point, you just need to stand near him.*
1. Speak with [[Al'Kabor's projection]]  {{waypoint -89, 31, -408}}.
1. Speak with [[Hymnist Wadakha]] {{waypoint -84, 31, -410}}.
