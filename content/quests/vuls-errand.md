## Steps

*Hiwaaya Al'Naderi has evidence that some of the members of the Cult of Vul are attempting to use parasitical spellshards to leech arcane energies from beasts within the Spiral of Vul, despite the mingling of Ulteran and void energies. She intends to teach them how dangerous such actions are, when performed without careful study and experimentation.*

- Hunt void-born beasts in Sable Savanna (0/6).
- Fill parasitical spellshards with wild Ulteran magic (0/6).
- Deposit the parasitical spellshards in the arcane chests in Fort Vul (0/6).
- Return to [[Hiwaaya Al'Naderi (Spiral of Vul)|Hiwaaya Al'Naderi]]
