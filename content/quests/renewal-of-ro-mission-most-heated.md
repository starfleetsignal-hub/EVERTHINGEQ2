## Steps

1. Use the [[Ortallian Zealot Disguise]] and enter [[Rath'Mana: Mission Most Heated]] {{waypoint 589.24, 112.99, -631.62}} (same portal as the Sandstone Delta: Eye of Night instances).
1. Search for [[Nairion Eldi]].
   1. At {{waypoint 332, 138, -394}} the illusion is broken by [[An Ortallian inquisitor|Ortallian inquisitors]]. Defeat them.
   1. Kill [[Villijs Ghandaq]].
   1. [[Lord Nagafen]] arrives. Witness the scene until he departs.
   1. Recover the crystal arca {{waypoint 348.39, 137.94, -394.06}}. It's been swiped by [[Zizzez]] (again!)
   1. Speak with [[Nairion Eldi]].
      - *You will automatically leave the zone.*
1. Speak with [[Silrena]] at [[Sandstone Landing]] {{waypoint 667.52, 51.26, 179.01}}

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 57791 status
- [[RoR Flag: Sandstone Delta Unlock]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Ortallian Devotee Amulet]]
  - [[Heart of Ro Necklace]]
