## Steps

1. Collect the requisition list from the Work Requisitions clipboard (next to Supply Sergeant Anuhadux)
1. Scribe the [[Bathezid master craftsman recipes]] received upon accepting the quest.
1. Create 6 of one of the following (randomly determined after clicking the clipboard):
   - [[Di'Zok stimulant drink]] (Stove and Keg - smoldering kindling)
   - [[Expandable cloth lined strong box]] (Woodworking Table - smoldering sandpaper)
     - 4 Ferrite Cluster
     - 2 Lichenclover Root
     - 5 Smoldering Sandpaper
   - [[Field latrine]] (Forge - smoldering coal)
   - [[Freezing rain arrows]] (Woodworking Table - smoldering sandpaper)
   - [[Redwood battering ram]] (Woodworking Table - smoldering sandpaper)
   - [[Sarnak battle bread]] (Stove and Keg - smoldering kindling)
     - 4 Raw Cranberry
     - 2 Cabilis Cocoa Bean
     - 10 Smoldering Kindling
1. Return to Anuhadux.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill Experience
- At least 38g 11s 18c
- +750 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
