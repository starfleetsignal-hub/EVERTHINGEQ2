## Steps

1. Take the shield to [[Crater Pond]] to be cleaned. The 'perfect' spot is atop the center meteorite.
1. Collect three [[A lowland badger|lowland badger]] furs. A few can be found in the area between {{waypoint -610, -12, 108}} and {{waypoint -204, -15, 147}} - i.e. between [[Frontier Farm]] and [[Central Farmlands]].
1. Collect five acidic poison glands from [[An earthcrawler|earthcrawlers]]. Generally located east of Crater Pond near {{waypoint -70, -26, 517}}.
