## Steps

1. Slay 12 bixies on [[Bixie Isle]].
1. A level 61^ [[A mega bixie brute|mega bixie brute]] will spawn after you kill the 12th bixie. Kill it.
1. Return to [[Gnibliks Shortstacks|Gniblicks]] near the [[Tenebrous Landing]]. {{waypoint 60, 22, 198}}.
1. Speak to [[Bilgeron Cogsworth IV|Bilgeron]].
