*This quest should not be confused with "[[Fire and Ice]]", part of the [[Prismatic Weapon Timeline]].*

## Requirements

- You must be a level 80 [[Wizard]].
- You must be able to understand the [[Koada'Dal]] language.

## Steps

1. Read the Journal to be offered the quest
1. Speak with [[Cerennhir Aelindel]] in the library in [[New Tunaria]]. {{waypoint -409, 49, -980}}
   - You must have [[A Dilapidated Journal]] in your inventory or [[Cerennhir Aelindel]] will not speak with you.
   - This item is looted from either [[A Yha-lei defender]], [[A Yha-lei disciple (Chelsith)|a Yha-lei disciple]], [[A Yha-lei enforcer]], [[A ghastly excretion]], or [[An abyss lurker]], or Leviathor Tentar. This is a chest drop, and you may need to mentor to 90.

## Rewards

- Experience Only
