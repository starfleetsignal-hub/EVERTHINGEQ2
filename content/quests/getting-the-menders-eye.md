## Steps

1. Obtain [[Eye of a Bronze Gargoyle]] by clicking on a gargoyle statue in the [[Amphitheater of Rancor]]. {{waypoint 33, 7, -49}}
   - The gargoyle can update multiple players if everyone clicks it at the same time (i.e. by using a countdown).
   - The quest automatically completes once the gargoyle is clicked, and the following quest is offered.
