## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Anashti Sul has asked me to assist in gathering deathbound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the deathbound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Deathbound Energy Reserves|deathbound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect [[Deathbound Energy Reserves|deathbound energy]] by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to the [[Herald of Undeath]] {{waypoint -2063, -130, -35}} in [[The Sinking Sands]]
