## Steps

You'll recieve 1 [[A spirit totem crate]].

1. Place spirit totems around the [[Thrael'Gorr Camp]] at the marked locations.
   1. Place the first spirit totem {{waypoint 406.52, -89.36, 2499.55}}.
   1. Place the second spirit totem {{waypoint 316.96, -100.04, 2528.53}}.
   1. Place the third spirit totem {{waypoint 269.11, -72.02, 2622.53}}.
   1. Place the fourth spirit totem {{waypoint 272.61, -82.42, 2705.27}}.
   1. Place the fifth spirit totem {{waypoint 316.27, -78.29, 2743.28}}.
   1. Place the sixth spirit totem {{waypoint 366.39, -73.80, 2764.20}}.
1. Return to [[Thrael'Gorr Oracle]] {{waypoint 359, -64, 2612}}
