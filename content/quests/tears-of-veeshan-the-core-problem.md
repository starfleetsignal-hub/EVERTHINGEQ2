## Steps

1. Speak with [[Zal'Urid]], near the entrance to [[The Nexus Core (Advanced Solo)]]. {{waypoint 284, 157, -195}}
1. Talk to [[Chymot]], outside the lower entrance of the tower. {{waypoint 177, 154, -85}}
1. Talk to Vizzeren {{waypoint 15, 195, 2}} in Falinpol on Daarspire in Vesspyr Isles and he will give you [[Vizzeren's Arcanum Vision Elixir]]
1. Use [[Vizzeren's Arcanum Vision Elixir]] *before* entering instance to update the quest (which then allows you to see and collect the shards).
   - *Note: You must be on or have completed the [[Family Ties]] quest and can get a new elixir from Vizzeren. {{waypoint 15, 195, 3}}*
1. Find and collect the Nexus Core shards
   - Collect the shard piece from [[Izshrash'Khar: Stratum of the Protectors]] (Shissar Temple), either the [[Izshrash'Khar: Stratum of the Protectors (Advanced Solo)|Advanced Solo]] or [[Izshrash'Khar: Stratum of the Protectors (Heroic)|Heroic]], on Sothshae. {{waypoint 711.91, 276.70, 696.15}}
     - *Note: Shard is behind Imperial Construct at {{waypoint -117.59, 1784.94, -103.02}}*
   - Collect the shard from [[Ssrez'Ras: Chamber of Stasis]] (Shissar Temple), either the [[Ssrez'Ras: Chamber of Stasis (Advanced Solo)|Advanced Solo]] or [[Ssrez'Ras: Chamber of Stasis (Heroic)|Heroic]], on Sothshae.
     - *Note: From right at the entrance you can jump down into the red bubbling pool where Ziskaya stands and click the shard at {{waypoint -109, -18, 7}}*
1. Return to Zal'Urid with the shards.
1. Search for [[Chymot]] on [[Veiled Thessk]]. {{waypoint -900, -50, 18}}
1. Retrieve the Nexus Core Heart from within [[The Fractured Hive (Advanced Solo)]] or [[The Fractured Hive (Heroic)]] by defeating [[Queen Meacidaris]], the final named.
1. Return the Nexus Core Heart to Zal'Urid.
1. Give Zal'Urid [[Crusader Jortreva's Healing Scale]]. (Note this is a quest reward from the quest [[Meditation and Annihilation]], which must be completed before proceeding. To begin the quest line which ends with Meditation and Annihilation speak to [[Jortreva]] on [[Karak Peak]] {{waypoint -464, 139, -313}})
1. Defeat the two attacking [[An Awakened etherhunter|Awakened etherhunters]].
1. Speak to Chymot outside the tower.

- At least 1p 35g
