## Steps

1. Pick a spoon off the nearby table and equip it.
1. Play the drum game by matching the indicated drum pattern five times.
   - The pattern is random, and may become hard to follow in the fourth and fifth rounds. You can use a pen and paper to help you remember.
   - If you fail several times, Imadeus will give up on you and give you the key for free. Alternatively, you can just give up on yourself and AFK for ~5 minutes and Imadeus will offer you the key.

## Rewards

- The key needed to unlock one of the vaults to complete [[Unlocking the Elfin Lord]].
