*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Cvaka Zichovani]], who is standing next to Malkin.
1. Find the [[Toppled Hand of Thule]]. {{waypoint -1000, 0, 840}}
1. Slay the [[Left Eye of Thule]] and the two [[Eye of Thule subservient|Eye of Thule subservients]], who spawn when you approach the toppled hand.
1. Return to Cvaka.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 99s 37c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Cavalier's Pauldrons (44)|Cavalier's Pauldrons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Mentor's Arm Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Primalist's Spaulders]] (Shamans)
  - [[Scholar's Epaulets]] (All Mages)
  - [[Thief's Chain Spaulders]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Vicar's Plate Pauldrons]] (Clerics)
  - [[Wanderer's Shoulder Pads]] (Druids)
