Now that I have learned why the Sisters of Erollisi are in [[Antonica]], I should return to [[Landax Z'Torzin]].

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Sabstin V'Despth]] at the [[Spires of Innoruuk]] in [[Neriak]].
1. Kill 6 [[A member of The Dead|members of The Dead]] in [[Darklight Wood]] by the [[Neriak]] entrance. Walk around until they spawn and aggro you.
1. Speak with [[Sabstin V'Despth]] at the [[Spires of Innoruuk]] in [[Neriak]].
1. Harvest 2 [[Hate blossom|hate blossoms]] in [[Nektulos Forest]], found primarily in the [[An ash owlbear|ash owlbears]] area {{waypoint -860, 33, -170}}
   - **NOTE:**  *You must stay far enough away that the [[Hate blossom|hate blossom's]] AOE doesn't hit you or it will disappear.  Also, the [[Hate blossom|blossoms]] share a respawn timer with [[Rose of Innoruuk|roses of Innoruuk]] and [[Spiteweed]].  If you don't see any [[Hate blossom|hate blossoms]], trigger some of the others to respawn these nodes.*
1. Meditate in the [[Spires of Innoruuk]] in [[Neriak]]. Just walk in the main doors and a beam of light will stun you. Just click through the story prompts to complete it.
1. Speak with [[Sabstin V'Despth]] at the [[Spires of Innoruuk]] in [[Neriak]].
1. Return to [[Sister Katey Dalwynn]] in [[Antonica]].
