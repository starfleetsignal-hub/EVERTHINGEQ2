## Steps

1. Kill spiders ([[Ruin crawler|ruin]] or [[Mountain crawler|mountain crawlers]]) to collect 8 [[Venom sac|venom sacs]] from the local spiders, all over e.g. {{waypoint 1067, 416, 741}}.
1. Return to [[Shaman Zaz'i'zaz]] at {{waypoint 1149, 392, 864}}.

- At least 22g 11s 70c
