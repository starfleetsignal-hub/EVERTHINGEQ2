## Requirements

- Must be level 7 of one [[Ascension]] class to start the quest
- Must complete [[Shattered Seas Timeline]] to get the update from Assistant Shortspanner
- Ability to speak [[Uruvanian]]
- [[Vesspyr Isles Timeline]] at least up through [[Family Ties]], which must be completed before Harla Dar will speak with you about the stone. You only need to do a small part of the quests here. Backtrack from [[Family Ties]] and [[Under The Veil]] quest lines to see where to start the two required quest lines.
- [[A Strange Black Rock]] for [[Glowing Black Stone]] *needs to be in your inventory when you zone into get the harp from Shimmering Citadel: Inside the Bottle*
- *You must be a level 100 [[Artisan]]
- 10/23/2018 -- The harp was also collected from [[Tipple's Focus]] at {{waypoint 19.50, 1.24, -8.09}} -- So don't worry if you zone in there -- you will see the Harp on the left at the coordinates above in the very first room. Good luck!

## Steps

1. *Speak with a researcher of arcane artifacts who has dealt with curses*: -- [[Assistant Shortspanner]] in Tranquil Sea at {{waypoint 986, 27, 1088}}
1. Head to [[Phantom Sea]] and approach Aliena Belzia in [[Ssraeshza Temple (Heroic)]] in the northern room before killing the named, or you will need to return. The quest updates when you get close to her.
1. *Find a way to help Aliena Belzia*: After receiving the update, start clearing the zone bosses. The boss room order is South, East, Middle, North. Once you have killed the [[The Vathsis|Vathsis]] in the northern room, talk to Aillena again.
1. Obtain:
   - *a highly explosive item that can be triggered from within a shield* -- [[Resonant umbrite|resonant umbrite]] from the last named in [[Stygian Threshold (Heroic)]]. This is a chest drop and only 1 drops per chest. Only 1 mage can be updated per instance.
   - *a shield spell which can withstand an explosion* -- [[A diacoustic aquatic shield spell scroll]] from [[Black Bolt]] in [[Maldura: Algorithm For Destruction (Heroic)]]. This is a chest drop and only 1 drops per chest. Only 1 mage can be updated per instance.
   - *an item that can cause the fragile umbrite crystals to explode via multi-layered high frequency sound waves* -- Go to [[The Shimmering Citadel|Shimmering Citadel]].
     1. Rub the lamp in [[The Shimmering Citadel|Shimmering Citadel]] {{waypoint -277, 185, -71}} and talk to [[An Uruvan djinn]] *(must speak [[Uruvanian]])*. This update is on the top floor of the Eastern tower.
     1. Enter [[Shimmering Citadel: Inside the Bottle]] *(The Empty Bottle)* from [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]] and click the Inaudible Harp. ![The zone-in to Shimmering Citadel: Inside the Bottle/Tipple's Focus](images/An_ornate_metal_lamp.png)
        - You're looking for **an ornate metal lamp**, NOT the lamp that goes to [[The Palace of Sabaron|Sabaron's Palace]] or the one that goes to [[Azhar's Penitence]]*. Thus far, only 12 random spawn locations of the 15 possible are known. *Once inside, [[An inaudible harp]] can be harvested at {{waypoint 61, 1, -20}} to update the quest.
        - Note: this may be bugged and take you to [[Tipple's Focus]] or 'A Djinn's Treasure Cache' when you click on it. This may be the case if you haven't completed the prerequisites for this quest, or be at level or chrono to level 50. Complete HQ and ToV quest first to avoid this. **Note Update: Remember you should now be able to update this step in Tipple's Focus as of 10/23/2018 -- Please correct this entry if it was a fluke -- check last entry in requirements above.**
1. Return to [[Bellengere the Three (Sundered Frontier)|Bellengere the Three]].
1. Place the [[Resonant umbrite|reonant umbrite]] and [[An inaudible harp|inaudible harp]] around the [[Blackscale rune cube]], cast the [[A diacoustic aquatic shield spell scroll]], and click the harp.
1. Speak with [[Bellengere the Three (Sundered Frontier)|Bellengere the Three]] again.
   - *Note: Before heading to [[Vesspyr Isles]] for the next step, make sure to have the [[Glowing Black Stone]] in your inventory. If you got rid of it after doing the quest, you can repurchase it from [[Shady Swashbuckler XVIII]]*.
1. *Identify the creator of the Blackscale rune cube*: Talk to [[Harla Dar]] on [[Veeshenna Tol]] in [[Vesspyr Isles]] at {{waypoint -102, 51, 740}}.
1. *Find a way to get [[Lady Karkona (Vesspyr Isles)|Karkona's]] attention*:
   - Harvest [[Karkona's scale]] from a purple shiny on [[Veeshenna Tol]] {{waypoint -55, 53, 794}}.
   - Harvest [[A cracked spirital anchor]] from a table in the building on [[Veiled Thessk]] in [[Vesspyr Isles]] {{waypoint -793, -45, 51}}.
   - Place the scale in the anchor at {{waypoint 214, 126, 792}}, then click the anchor to spawn [[Lady Karkona (Vesspyr Isles)|Lady Karkona]].
1. Speak with [[Lady Karkona (Vesspyr Isles)|Lady Karkona]].
   - Note:  It's amusing, but fruitless to mouth-off to dragons!
1. At the end of the dialogue, hail [[Lady Karkona (Vesspyr Isles)|Lady Karkona]] again to present the [[Glowing Black Stone]] (from your inventory) to her and update the quest.
1. Return to [[Bellengere the Three (Sundered Frontier)|Bellengere the Three]] in [[The City of Paineel|Paineel]].
1. Speak with [[Lady Karkona (The Sundered Frontier)|Lady Karkona]] in Paineel near Bellengere.
1. Gather the following items:
   - *Gather arcane object of draconic blood*.
     1. Go to [[Chardok]]
     1. Zone in to [[Lair of the Haunts]] by clicking on the **Moveable Stone Wall** at {{waypoint -36, -58, 100}}
     1. Kill [[Tombwatch L'Drykk]] (105^^^, may need a group, same difficulty as the Scrollkeeper) at the bottom of [[Lair of the Haunts]]. All the quest mobs are invisible to group members, forcing the mage to pull them.  Once they are in combat, everyone can see the mobs.
     1. Harvest the [[Dragon Blood Shard]] on top of the chest at {{waypoint -438, -117, 1}} ![The Dragon Blood Shard](images/Dragon_Blood_Shard.png)
   - *Gather arcane object of draconic breath*.
     - Harvest [[Urn of Dragon Breath]] in the [[Lost City of Torsis: Reaver's Remnants (Solo)]] *(or [[Lost City of Torsis: Reaver's Remnants (Heroic)|Heroic]])* in the hidden room at {{waypoint 8.90, -5.15, -1204.65}}. Just walk through the wall to the left of where the named stood.  It looks like a big vase (a previously cleared instance does not seem to work). The heroic instance is quite hard compared to the solo instance which accepts two people or a merc.
   - *Gather arcane object of draconic destruction.*
     - Harvest the [[Wand of the Wurmslayer]] in the [[Crypt of Dalnir: Baron's Workshop (Heroic)]] in the sarcophagus with the mirror {{waypoint -144, 27, 50}}.  ![Wand of the Wurmslayer](images/Wand_of_the_Wurmslayer.png)
1. Return to [[Lady Karkona (The Sundered Frontier)|Lady Karkona]] in [[The City of Paineel|Paineel]] (gives you recipe).
1. Craft the [[Dragon's Tongue Staff]] (level 100 combine):
   1. Get a [[Shadebark]] for the combine.
   1. Go to [[Kaesora: Xalgozian Stronghold (Heroic)]] *The crafting table you need only appears in the Heroic instance.
   1. Harvest one [[Asmyx]] (greenish rocks on the floor, can only be found in a fresh instance).
      - Example locations: {{waypoint -154.52, 31.79, 432.56}}, {{waypoint -184.91, 36.25, 431.87}}, {{waypoint -169.67, 31.79, 493.88}} {{waypoint -408.94, -1.47, 478.19}}, etc.
   1. Craft the [[Dragon's Tongue Staff]] at the [[Ancient Arcane Workbench]] {{waypoint -563, -17, 410}} - *(only available in the Heroic instance)*.
1. Talk to [[Lady Karkona (The Sundered Frontier)|Lady Karkona]] in [[The City of Paineel]]
   - *Note: The rune cube appears to be attackable as soon as you get to [[Lady Karkona (The Sundered Frontier)|Lady Karkona]]. Make sure you talk to her before you attack it.*
1. Equip the [[Dragon's Tongue Staff]] then destroy the rune cube. *(The rune cube regens HPs fairly quickly, so don't expect to just hit it once and have it die.)*
1. Talk to [[Lady Karkona (The Sundered Frontier)|Lady Karkona]] again.
1. Talk to [[Bellengere the Three (Sundered Frontier)|Bellengere the Three]].
