## Steps

1. Find the Chokidai pack at {{waypoint 1767, 155, -2564}} in [[Kylong Plains]] between the Jarsath Wastes post and the road to [[Chardok]].
1. When you get the update that you have found the pack, use the Chokidai Mask in your inventory and speak to the [[Knowing chokidai]].
1. He will send you out to kill 10 Wurms and 10 Broken Toe Goblins which you can find scattered near the area.
1. Go back and speak with the [[Knowing chokidai]].
1. You then call to the members of the pack and have them follow you to kill the [[Di'Zok Poacher|Di'Zok Poachers]]. Right-click on the chokidai pack members (all 3 of them) and they will follow you.
1. Find the [[Di'Zok Poacher|poachers]] just down the road towards [[Chardok]] at {{waypoint 1604, 132, -2561}}.
1. Go back and speak with the [[Knowing chokidai]]. He will then ask you if you are ready to confront your nature.
   - Might want a couple of friends for this one.  When you are ready, go near the [[Pack Mentality]]. It will  then attack you (LVL 83^^ Heroic).
1. Harvest the rock at spawn point of the [[Pack Mentality]] {{waypoint 1755, 158, -2573}}
