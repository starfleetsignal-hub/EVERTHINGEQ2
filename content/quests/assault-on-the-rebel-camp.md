## Steps

1. Head to [[Ry'zilk's Base Camp]] and kill 3 members of Ry'zilk's Renegades ([[A renegade snooper|renegade snoopers]], [[A renegade infiltrator|renegade infiltrators]], or [[A renegade medic|renegade medics]]). {{waypoint 820, 90, -450}}
1. Kill 2 [[A chief renegade infiltrator|chief renegade infiltrators]]. {{waypoint 808, 87, -455}}
1. Speak with [[Field Chief Ry'zilk]]. {{waypoint 798, 88, -270}}
1. Return to Captain Venk.
