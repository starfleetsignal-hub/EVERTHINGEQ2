## Steps

1. Touch the first marker {{waypoint 1025, 18, 1259}}
1. Touch the second marker {{waypoint 752, 15, 1093}}
1. Seek out the sounds of battle {{waypoint 777, 29, 1045}} then kill the [[A bone rot warrior|bone rot warriors]] attacking the Far Seas guard
1. Find the third marker {{waypoint 569, 19, 940}} - Click on a dead Far Seas soldier (the game does not prompt you to do this) then touch the marker.![A dead soldier](images/Dead_soldier.jpg)
1. Find the fourth marker (click the dead soldier at {{waypoint 474, 23, 845}}) the marker is at {{waypoint 430, 31, 776}}
1. Investigate the Druid Ring (speak to the dying soldier in front of the druid ring)![A dying soldier](images/Dying_soldier.jpg)
1. Return to Joreye

## Rewards

- At least 7p 35g
- One of the Following:
  - [[Double Belt of the Cursed Forest]]
  - [[Worn Belt of the Cursed Forest]]
