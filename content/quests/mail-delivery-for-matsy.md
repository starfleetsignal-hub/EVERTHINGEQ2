## Steps

1. Deliver a letter to [[Jacques]], immediately inside [[Thundering Steppes]] at {{waypoint 235, 33, -1032}}.

## Notes

- This completes the Matsy quest line, and introduce you to the [[Thundering Steppes Timeline]]. However, you should probably be level 20 before you continue with Jacques' quest line.
- In the meantime it is recommended that the player completes the other quest lines that start in [[Frontier Farm]]. See the [[Antonica Timeline]] for details.
