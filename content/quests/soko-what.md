## Notes

- Make sure you have the brokenskull disguise up, otherwise you can't get the update from Scabby. If you die, you will keep the disguise, but you will not be able to get updates. Simply cancel and reapply the disguise.
- *Note: To complete this Quest you have to finish all 5 sub quests*
- *Note: [[Prove Ye Be a Swabber!]] needs to be done to unlock the other 4 Swabber the Deck subquests*

## Steps

1. Talk to Grak Liversplat {{waypoint 1059, 124, 704}} in the [[Brokenskull Loft]] (enter the cave) to receive [[Prove Ye Be a Swabber!]]. Complete that quest to obtain the rank of Swabber.
1. Take Southwest passage from Firmroot Moot to Brokenskull hideouts. Talk to Scabby Gabby {{waypoint 767, 71, 751}} and complete the following four quests (in any order, one at a time):
   - [[Swabber the Deck - Yurip Honorbound]]
   - [[Swabber the Deck - Beltza Gizzardslurper]]
   - [[Swabber the Deck - Delivery to Gunthak's Shanty]]
   - [[Swabber the Deck - Stock the Loft]]
1. Kill [[Head Groomer Marrithex]] {{waypoint 451, 41, 1200}} - 8 minute respawn.
1. Return to [[Fip Marrsquire]] {{waypoint 110, -88, -498}}
