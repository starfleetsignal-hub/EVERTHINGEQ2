## Notes

- **Warning**: Read the notes for the first step before killing [[The Leviathan]]!

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Chamber of Destiny]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]] and obtain the *Chelsith Stone* by defeating [[The Leviathan]].
   - Upon killing the Leviathan, the quest will auto-update stating that you have obtained the Chelsith Stone, and allow you to progress to the next step. However, you do not actually physically receive the item. For the following quest, [[The Secret of the Stone]], you find that indeed you do need a physical Chelsith Stone. The actual [[The Chelsith Stone (Quest Item)|Chelsith Stone]] is obtained in the following way:
     1. Loot a [[Fishman Skin]] from the corpse of any [[A Yha-lei disciple (Epic)|Yha-lei disciple]] from within the Chamber of Destiny.
     1. While engaged in combat with [[The Leviathan]], use the Fishman Skin to apply the disguise.
     1. After about 60-90 seconds, the Leviathan will 'eat' you and you will enter his stomach, filled with [[A gullet devourer|gullet devourers]] and a drachnid corpse. Click on the corpse and you will receive the real [[The Chelsith Stone (Quest Item)|Chelsith Stone]].
1. Return to [[Kunzar Jungle]] and zone into the special instance [[The Moppet Shoppe: Into the Bowels of the Beast]] and speak to [[Fiddy Bobick]].
1. Defeat [[Azdaedus]].
1. Speak to Fiddy Bobick again.
