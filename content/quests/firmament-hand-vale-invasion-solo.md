The invasion has begun! Not for the Zakir Rish, but for me. I will leave no djinn standing! Or floating! In an upright position!

Banish the Grand Conjurer

## Steps

1. Enter [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion (Solo)]] in [[Raptor's Landing]] at {{waypoint -798.93, 174.52, 195.80}}
1. Speak to [[Kusala'Din]] at {{waypoint -744.83, 62.94, 408.61}}
1. Destroy [[Magmalatorr]] the Conjured Destroyer
1. Speak to [[Kusala'Din]] at {{waypoint -525.88, 80.49, 679.44}}
1. Banish [[Tkesh'Tura]] the Conjured Figment
1. Use the crystal shard teleporter at {{waypoint -790.32, 84.75, 618.89}}
1. Dispatch [[Kusala'Din]] the Grand Conjurer at {{waypoint -1045.90, 180.73, 735.97}}

- At least 4p 31g 10s 14c
