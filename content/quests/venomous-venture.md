## Steps

1. Obtain the following in any order:
   - 5 batches of [[A dune spiderling|dune spiderling]] venom. {{waypoint -1020, -130, -425}}
   - 2 vials of [[A toxic cobra|toxic cobra]] venom. {{waypoint -1311, -143, -667}}
   - 4 vials of [[An asp|asp]] venom. {{waypoint -1210, -216, -242}}
     - On Origins server Anashti Sul the asps can be found {{waypoint -1102, -206, -265}} and share the same spawn points as the [[A plated digger|a plated digger]] along the [[Sandslide Passage]].
1. Return to [[Ghassan the Trader]].

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Earring of the Frenzied Djinn]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Facet of the Frenzied Djinn]]
  - [[Spike of the Frenzied Djinn]]
  - [[Stud of the Frenzied Djinn]] (All Fighters)
