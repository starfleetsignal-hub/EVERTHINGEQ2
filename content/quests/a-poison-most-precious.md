## Steps

![An empty othmir bucket (visible)](images/An_empty_othmir_bucket_%28visible%29.png)

1. Gather sea water and ink sacks:
   - Collect 4 [[An empty othmir bucket|empty othmir bucket]]s, in the sand around the camp and west to the shore
   - Swim out into deep water in the ocean and use the empty buckets from your inventory to fill them - receive [[A full bucket of seawater]]
   - Kill [[A coastal drifter|coastal drifter]]s for [[A sac of octopus ink|sacs of octopus ink]].
1. Speak to Nika
1. The recipe *a sludge of ink and salt* (level 1 Artistry) is placed in your recipe book (and removed when you complete the quest). Craft 4 [[A sludge of ink and salt|sludges of ink and salt]] of them using the Pressure Cooking Fire {{waypoint 4286, -1077, 1449}}
1. Speak to Nika
