## Prerequisites

- You need +6,500 faction with [[The Gwalnax Brigade (Faction)|The Gwalnax Brigade]] to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Barracks of Caroeth]] in [[Chardok]] and obtain the following items, in any order:
   - [[Key of Caroeth]] from [[Warlord Kilrok Caroeth]] {{waypoint 206, -73, 73}}
     - If your group is having difficulty clearing the mobs in the room with Warlord Kilrok Caroeth, use a [[Insta-Fizz Fuzzlecutter]] (found randomly throughout the buildings of Chardok; Looks like a glowing wine glass) to spike the drinking keg outside the barracks. This will put all the mobs in the lower room of barracks to sleep and make the [[A barracks servant|barracks servant]] stationary at the bottom of the stairs instead of roaming back and forth.
   - [[Brell pops]] {{waypoint 200, -72, 65}}
   - [[Gelatinous orb]] {{waypoint 211, -74, 120}}
   - [[Caroeth Codex of Combat]]; Use the Key of Caroeth to open the chest at {{waypoint 247, -73, 76}}.
1. Return to [[Dragoon Slitek]] at the [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]].

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Gwalnax Brigade**
- One of the following:
  - [[Emission of Void Particles]]
  - [[Voidbeast Separated Claw]]
  - [[Voidbeast Tooth]]
  - [[Voidman's Armor Shard]]
