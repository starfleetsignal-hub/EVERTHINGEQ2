## Notes

- This is a repeatable quest.
- The steps may vary with each quest received.  See below for the options.

## Steps

*Fenlys Nimbledigit, the unscrupulous merchant I met in Darkpaw Dugout, has agreed to pay me for my continued help and has sent me back into the Darkpaw Warrens in search of more valuable items.*

1. Gather runic velium crystals OR howling iron ore:
   - Gather 6 runic velium crystals in Snowfang Burrow in Darkpaw Warrens
     - Snowfang Burrow:  {{waypoint 17, -3, -136}}
     - Crystals are a group update
   - Gather 8 howling iron ore from Dustpaw Burrow {{waypoint -118, -11, -155}}
1. Hunt for zygomyd mycelium OR goshenite spell-stones:
   - Hunt for 10 zygomyd mycelium in Terra Fungi
     - Semi-frequent update from most zygomyds in Terra Fungi {{waypoint -22, -77, -255}}
   - Hunt Underpaw gnolls for 8 goshenite spell-stones
     - Group update from killing Underpaw gnolls in Underpaw Stronghold {{waypoint 79, -25, 76}} or near Terramite Crystalline Grotto {{waypoint 72, 2, 210}}
1. Return to Fenlys Nimbledigit in Darkpaw Dugout {{waypoint -20, -80, -264}}

## Rewards

- At least 3p 36g 79s 77c
