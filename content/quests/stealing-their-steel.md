## Steps

1. Take 5 weapons from [[A Haoaeran lightfoot|Haoaeran lightfoots]] to the west of Tanzikla {{waypoint 489, 159, 149}}. You can either loot those fallen in battle or kill some yourself.
1. Return to Tanzikla.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Protector's Shortbow]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
