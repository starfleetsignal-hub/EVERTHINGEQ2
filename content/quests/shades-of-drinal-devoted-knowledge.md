## Steps

1. Speak to [[Venox Tarkog]] at {{waypoint 219.36, 79.85, -47.35}} and help him:
   - [[Convenient Conversion]] (95)
   - [[Religious Studies]] (95)
1. Speak to [[Deema Mertshak]] at {{waypoint 261, 88, 34}} and help her:
   - [[Mertshak's Search for a Bite]] (95)
   - [[Lujien, not Lycan]] (95)
   - [[Littlepaw's Knowledge]] - available after assisting [[Duskrender]]
1. Speak to [[Duskrender]] at {{waypoint 260.15, 89.22, 67.32}} and help him:
   - [[Ascension of a God]] (95)
   - [[Ascension Assistance]] (95)
1. Speak once more to [[Venox Tarkog]] at {{waypoint 219.36, 79.85, -47.35}}
   - [[Search of Scales]] (95)
1. Speak to [[Drinal's Steward]] via the Whisper Column at {{waypoint 272, 94, 104}} in the Sanctuary.
1. Enter the Rock of Eternity, a cave at {{waypoint -261.62, 2.19, 381.60}}
   - Kill all the Gehein. Click on the living mud at {{waypoint -183, -35, -3}}. You'll get attacked by two [[A globbin|globbin]]s every time you click it.
   - *NOTE: Each player in the group must collect their own living mud. You may have to kill the NPC for each player.*
   - Leave the cave.
1. Use the [[Living Mud|mud]] at the Blessed Kiln in [[Cardin Ward]] {{waypoint -371, 14, 16}}. It is the forge, that is also used by [[Warren Redhammer]], the mender.
   - You will need the following items:
     - A [[Rough turquoise]] (T2 common gem)
     - A [[Spark of Ether]] purchased from [[Keiya Oakwood]] for ***0c*** {{waypoint -396.02, 15.69, 54.74}}
     - The [[Relic of Fate (Recipe Book)]] gathered from the floor near the Blessed Kiln at {{waypoint -373, 14, 13}} and scribed.
     - Craft the item using the Blessed Kiln
1. Return to [[Drinal's Steward]] (via whisper column or by the Tourbillion) to complete the quest.
