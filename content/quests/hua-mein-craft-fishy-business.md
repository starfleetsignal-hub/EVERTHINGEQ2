## Steps

1. Scribe the [[Hua Mein Special Baked Fish Recipe]] in your inventory and craft some [[Tasty Baked Fish|tasty baked fish]] for the Hua Mein.
1. Return to Li Sing.

*The recipe calls for 2 [[Seahorse roe]], 2 [[Marr cherry]], 2 [[Bamboo shoot]], and 1 [[Ethereal kindling]]. Bring items along with [[Portable Cooking Set]] should you want to make this a faster process.*

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +1,500 faction with **Hua Mein**
- +1,000 faction with **Hua Mein Craftsmen**
