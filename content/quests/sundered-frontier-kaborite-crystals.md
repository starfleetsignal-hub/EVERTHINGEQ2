This is not a normal collection quest. The collection members are not obtained by harvesting shinies.
Instead, the collection members are themselves all obtained from the completion of other collection quests. This is called a "meta-collection".

## Rewards

- [[Rune of Al'Kabor]] this item is needed for the collection: [[Runes of Fate]]
- one of the following:
  - [[Chipped Kaborite Crystal]]
  - [[Darkened Kaborite Crystal]]
  - [[Glyphed Kaborite Crystal]]
  - [[Sundered Kaborite Crystal]]
