## Steps

1. Speak to [[Blackhowl]]
1. Speak to [[Hildan]] {{waypoint 228, 86, 51}}
1. Kill Horde for 10 uncommon updates
1. Click on fissures to trap gas:
   - [[Breathledge]] {{waypoint 156, 35, -102}}
   - [[Hallow Cleft]] {{waypoint -467, -21, 132}}
   - [[Lacerate of Mortality]] {{waypoint -222, 3, 304}}
1. Return to [[Blackhowl]]

## Rewards

- [[Monolith of the Silver Reaper Replica]]
- At least 1p 30g
