## Steps

1. Collect the following items, which are spread around the [[Defiled Forest]] {{waypoint -388, -23, -301}}and the [[Bridge of Zek]] {{waypoint -255, -25, -484}}, mostly near trees and other inanimate objects:
   - 4 [[An airtight box|airtight box]]
   - 5 [[A bottle|bottle]]s
   - 5 [[A jar|jar]]s
1. Return to Orca

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - 5 [[Green Hood Grog]] (*lvl 40 drink* )
  - 5 [[Green Hood Goulash]] (*lvl 40 food*)
