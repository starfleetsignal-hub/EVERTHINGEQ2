*I met Hunter Vannil in the Willow Wood.  He takes an interest in seeing to the safety of those people who are coming and going out of the Forest Ruins nearby.  He asked if I would do a favor to make the area safer for the populace by killing eight rock adders.*

***Note:*** *To talk to Hunter Vannil you will need to understand the [[Ayr'Dal]] language. If you are not a Half Elf, you will need to buy the Ayr'Dal Language Primer in Brooklily Books from [[Salinia Brooklily]] {{waypoint 822, -20, -606}}.*

## Steps

1. Kill eight [[A rock adder|rock adders]] in the [[The Forest Ruins]]. The gate is to the west of Hunter Vannil.  The snakes are in a clump of boulders near {{waypoint 1042, -21, -589}}.
1. Return to [[Hunter Vannil]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 20c
