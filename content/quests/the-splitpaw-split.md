## Steps

*Nisch Mas Nolphia informed me that gnoll scouts reported that the enemy clans have been taking Splitpaw prisoners. She asked me to locate these captives in gnoll camps throughout the Darkpaw Warrens and free them. Her scouts will direct them to safety.*

1. Collect [[Underpaw Shackle Key|Underpaw Shackle Keys]] and free 5 captive Splitpaw gnolls
   - Keys drop from a small chest from any group containing living Underpaw gnolls.  1 drop per group member.
   - Use the keys to free captive Splitpaw gnolls in Underpaw Stronghold {{waypoint 79, -25, 76}} - this is a group update
1. Speak with Nisch Mas Nolphia at the Splitpaw Burrow terraporter {{waypoint -58, 2, 69}} or near the entrance {{waypoint -26, -44, -240}}

## Rewards

- At least 3p 36g 79s 77c
- [[Serilian Shaped Scale Coat]]
