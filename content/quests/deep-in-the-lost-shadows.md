## Steps

1. ![Captain Greymast](images/Captain_Greymast.jpg)Find [[Captain Greymast]] and click on him to revive him
   - Possible locations:  {{waypoint -532, 103, -1032}}, {{waypoint -705, 87, -985}}, {{waypoint -532, 82, -1118}}  {{waypoint -629, 93, -953}}
1. Kill 12 [[A grimling spellsplitter|grimling spellsplitters]], [[A grimling skullsmasher|skullsmashers]] or [[A grimling spiritchanter|spiritchanters]]
1. Kill [[Kragaht]] - spawns after last Grimling killed.
   - in case [[Kragaht]] does not spawn, find another (agro) Grimling and kill it.
1. Return to [[Captain Greymast]] at {{waypoint -157, 7, -910}}

Note : This quest can be combined with the quest [[Grim Reaping]], but to get credit for each kill you need to hit the Grimlings once before using the vial.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- One of the following:
  - [[Gilded Far Seas Sextant]]
  - [[Splintered Far Seas Oar]]
  - [[Blade of the Far Seas Magi]]
