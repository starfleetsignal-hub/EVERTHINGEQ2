## Description

*In Kralet Penumbra, where darkness blankets the creatures of the abyss, the horror lays in wait, while in Maldura, the light of cerulean flames herald the coming of an invasion. I must slay this creature of the abyss, and save Maldura from the flames.*

## Steps

- Destroy enemy targets within Maldura and Kralet Penumbra.
1. Defeat the horror of the darkness
   - [[Xoth, the Ever Rending (Heroic)|Xoth]] in [[Kralet Penumbra: Temple of the Ill-Seen (Heroic)]]
1. Defeat the living ash
   - [[Cugdava (Heroic)|Cugdava]] in [[Maldura: District of Ash (Heroic)]]
