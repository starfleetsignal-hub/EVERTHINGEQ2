## Steps

1. Gather six discarded Arcane Source orbs near {{waypoint 566, 27, -763}} or  {{waypoint 193, 204, -598}}. **Note:** This is the rocky shore area where Auric Slugs abound.
   - Easy to spot the golden orbs. They respawn fast and two may be up at any one time. (Note: As of Nov 25th, 2019 there were 6 of them up at once when I did the quest.)
1. Return to [[Tesakias]] at {{waypoint -165, 575, -593}}.

![Discarded Arcane Source Orb](images/Discarded_Arcane_Orb.jpg)
