## Notes

To complete this quest, you will need the following t10 tradeskill items:

- Fuel
  - 9 [[Effulgent coal]]
  - 9 [[Effulgent filament]]
  - 9 [[Effulgent sandpaper]]
- Common Harvests
  - 15 [[Rhenium ore]]
  - 6 [[Swamp ash lumber]]
  - 15 [[Matoppie roots]]
  - 12 [[Thick bear pelt]]

## Steps

1. The recipe book, [[Camp Supplies, Revision II]], containing all the necessary recipes is autoscribed.
1. I need to make camp supplies from materials found in the forest.
   - Using a [[Forge]], craft 3 [[Rhenium Barricade|Rhenium Barricades]]
   - Using a [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]], craft 3 [[Bear Hide Tent|Bear Hide Tents]]
   - Using a [[Woodworking Table]], craft 3 [[Swamp Ash Storage Crate|Swamp Ash Storage Crates]].
     - The nearby woodworking table at {{waypoint -3780.93, -782.40, -81.34}} has a non-standard appearance. It looks like a pair of sawhorses supporting a plank.
1. I should deliver these new supplies to Porto.
