The dark side of the moon is in the midst of dangerous times, no more so than today. And so I've been tasked with bringing down the enemies of Fordel within Savage Weald: Chaotic Caverns.

## Steps

- See [[Savage Weald: Chaotic Caverns (Solo)]] for zone walkthrough:
1. Spill the bloodbath.
1. Chip apart some rocks.
1. Foul up the foul.
1. Slay the pit beast.
1. Banish the summoned spirit.
