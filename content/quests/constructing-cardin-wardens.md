## Steps

1. Kill six obol mucklings and gather four arcane geodes. The mucklings can be found near the stream north of Cardin Ward. Arcane geodes are found within churning obol that surround the tourbillion. Each slain churning obol will spawn a harvestable rock.
   - Find [[A churning obol|churning obol]] at {{waypoint -622, -1, 98}}
   - Find [[An obol muckling|obol muckling]]s at {{waypoint -208, 7, -193}}

![an arcade geode](images/An_arcane_geode.jpg)

1. I should return to Galen Cadabrak in the Wayward town of Cardin Wood now that I have the items he requires.
   - Return to [[Galen Cadabrak]] {{waypoint -460, 15, 84}}
1. Galen now creates a [[Cardin Guardian Stone]] from the items I collected and issues it to me.
1. Galen has entrusted me with releasing the Cardin guardian stones that are to be stationed around Cardin Ward.
   - Release the first guardian stone at the eastern entrance {{waypoint -493.09, 10.36, 15.13}}
   - Release the second guardian stone at the western entrance {{waypoint -339.82, 8.84, -102.39}}
   - Release the third guardian stone along the southwest wall {{waypoint -370.03, 13.95, 28.79}}
   - Release the fourth guardian stone along the southeast wall {{waypoint -482.28, 14.28, 70.55}}
1. I should return to Galen Cadabrak in the Wayward town of Cardin Ward.
   - Return to [[Galen Cadabrak]] {{waypoint -460, 15, 84}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
