## Notes

- This quest is placed in the Quest Journal under [[Emperor Atrebe's Laboratory: The Fabled City of Kor-sha]], however none of the steps involve entering Kor-sha. The entirety of the quest is done within [[Veksar: The Sunken Theater]].
- **NOTE: it is placed under Veksar: The Sunken Theater nowadays (11/3/2025).**

## Steps

1. Find the lost map to [[Emperor Atrebe's Laboratory: The Fabled City of Kor-sha|Kor-sha]]:
   1. Enter [[Veksar: The Sunken Theater]] and make your way to the final room, [[The Broken Theater]]. {{waypoint -73, -3, -71}}
   1. Once [[Garudon]] is dead, go to the back of the chamber and click on one of the pillars that is barely sticking up from the floor. {{waypoint -73, 0, 5}}
   1. Click on the front pillar with the crystal. The crystal will disappear and the area will become illuminated.
   1. Place the moveable staves from around the instance on the correct triangles. When a staff is in the correct place, a beam of colored light will be emitted. The staff locations and their corresponding triangles (if facing away from the entrance to the Broken Theater) are as follows:
      - Far right triangle: [[The Golem Factatory]] {{waypoint 76, 6, -173}}
      - Middle-left triangle: [[The Grand Sancloister]] {{waypoint -2, -2, -151}}
      - Far left triangle: [[The Tomb of Lord Sasil]] {{waypoint 0, -3, -73}}
      - Middle-right triangle: [[The Broken Theater]] {{waypoint -85, 0, -29}}
   1. Wait for the map to appear and the quest to update.
1. Return to Silssa Kuinn outside Omen's Call.
