## Steps

You have been asked by [[Zafirah Aamira]] to obtain a wide sampling of the vermin that can be found in the caves to the northeast.

1. Slay an assortment of vermin within the caves to the northeast of [[The City of Paineel]] {{waypoint 1446.44, -125.03, 3064.81}}.
   - Slay 5 [[A scavenging vermin|scavenging vermin]]
   - Slay 4 [[A wandering vermin|wandering vermin]]
   - Slay [[A commanding vermin]]
1. Return to [[Zafirah Aamira]]

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
