## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Prexus has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items

1. Purchase the [[Deep-Sea Coral|deep-sea coral]] from Glistra for 5000 status
   - Wanders along the road from Sathir's Span to Riliss, from {{waypoint -156, -38, 805}} to {{waypoint -113, -39, 47}}
1. Purchase the [[Abyssal Water|abyssal water]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the [[Wave Polished Aquamarine|wave polished aquamarine]] from Nushoon for 5000 status
   - In [[Aether Wroughtlands]] this NPC wanders along a path from the Bivouac Camp {{waypoint 754, -45, -446}} to Minstrel's Cradle {{waypoint 344, -59, -183}}
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Oceanlord]] {{waypoint -1765, -402, -2728}} in [[Fens of Nathsar]]
