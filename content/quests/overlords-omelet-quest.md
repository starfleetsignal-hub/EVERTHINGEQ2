1. Kill young vulriches until you get 3 uncommon quest updates.
   - [[A young vulrich|Young vulriches]] (level 12) are found near [[The Dog Pond]] and just to the east of it {{waypoint -1050, 0, -290}}.
1. Return to [[Mooshga]].
