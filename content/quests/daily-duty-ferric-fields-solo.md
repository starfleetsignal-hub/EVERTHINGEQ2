## Steps

Today, the Zakir Rish of Zimara Breadth will endure what the Sang'Huuu have endured since the invasion began, and perhaps they will think twice before starting wars with those who have me as an ally.

1. Enter [[Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields (Solo)|Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields [Solo]]] in [[Aether Wroughtlands]] through the portal at {{waypoint -286.41, 18.51, -574.12}}
1. Execute the Executioner [[Katakir the Cruel]] {{waypoint -83, 36, -632}}
1. Melt the Master Metallurgist [[Theya Shen'Safa]] {{waypoint -19, 50, -786}}
1. Massacre the Mass of Mayhem [[Metalloid]] {{waypoint 276, 36, -688}}
1. Crush the Wrought Iron Watcher [[Syadun]]  {{waypoint 44, 37, -662}}
1. Don't spare the Spared [[Shanrazad the Spared]]
