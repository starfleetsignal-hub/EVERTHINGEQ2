Tana, a follower of Master Wu, wants you to cull Sharpthorns and Treespirits.  Both can be found south of Tana.

## Steps

1. Kill the following in no particular order:
   - 7 Sharpthorns ([[A sharpthorn prophet]], [[A sharpthorn soothsayer]],  [[A sharpthorn stickler]]) {{waypoint -639, -56, 192}}
   - 7 Treespirits ([[A treespirit dirtcaller]], [[A treespirit piercer]], [[A treespirit seer]], [[A treespirit thumper]]) {{waypoint -556, -37, 359}}
1. Return to [[Tana]] for your reward

## Rewards

- At Least 9g 13s 1c

## Notes

- This quest can be done in conjunction with [[Fun with Hemotoxins]] which also requires killing sharpthorns.
