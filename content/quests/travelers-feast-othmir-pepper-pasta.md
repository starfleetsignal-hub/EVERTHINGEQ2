## Notes

- None of the harvestable items are trackable.
- All of these clicks are **zone-wide group** updates. If you are grouped anywhere in the zone, your group members will receive updates.

## Steps

1. Travel to zone [[Eastern Wastes]] and collect in this zone the requested ingredients.
   - Gather 4 *Icegaze Basilisk Eggs* around the spire at {{waypoint -934, -334, 2375}}![Icegaze Basilisk Eggs](images/Icegaze_Basilisk_Eggs.jpg)
   - Gather 5 *Flamespout Peppers* around [[Flamespout Fields]] near {{waypoint -716, -337, 3469}}
     - On plateau *above* [[Flamespout Fields]] , due West of the Nipik's Haven Station. Plateau accessible from Tunnel at -121, -385, 3404, North of Caverns of Living Stone Station. Note: [[A cinder walker|Cinder Walkers]] can see through both invisibility and stealth.![Flamespout Peppers](images/Flamespout_Peppers.jpg)
   - Gather 3 *Net Dried Seaweed* out of the fishing nets that the Othmir have hung up to dry around {{waypoint -1232, -537, 3229}} near [[Nipik's Haven]]![Net Dried Seaweed](images/Net_Dried_Seaweed.jpg)
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]].

## Rewards

- [[Days of Summer: 2020]] useable at [[Pas Yu]], the merchant beside [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]] on the [[Spire Island]].
