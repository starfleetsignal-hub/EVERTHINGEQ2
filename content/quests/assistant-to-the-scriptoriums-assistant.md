## Steps

1. Collect the following items, in no particular order:
   - **A haunch of succulent flesh** from killing [[The Meatbeast]] just outside the library in the grassy area to the left of [[The Caretaker (NPC)|The Caretaker]]: {{waypoint 78, 147, -111}}
   - **A golden pile of coins** from 'a large chest' which randomly spawns on the second or third floor of either the north, south, or east tower. Appears on track harvestable ability.
     - The chest will despawn after you pick it up. You will need to find one for each group member. It also shows up on harvestable tracking so if it isn't on track you can save your trip up the tower.
   - **Nelo's Tome of Finite Wisdom** from killing [[Nelo's Tome of Finite Wisdom]] on the top floor of the West tower (where you pick up the quest): {{waypoint 222, 224, -65}}
1. Speak with [[Scriptorium Keeper Samir]] {{waypoint 214, 185, -41}} and try to convince him to leave.
1. Return to [[Scriptorium Assistant Amro]].
1. Speak with Scriptorium Keeper Samir again and try to confound him with a riddle.
1. Return to Scriptorium Assistant Amro a final time to receive your reward.

- At least 6g 19s

  - [[Chainmail Boots of Prosperity]]

  - [[Totemic Spear (Level 50)|Totemic Spear]]
