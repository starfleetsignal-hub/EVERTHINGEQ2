## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Right click on the door next to the quest giver and select the option to go 'through the door'. You will enter the instance [[The Unkempt Vale: A Lordly Confrontation]]
1. Kill all four bosses in the instance:
   - [[Arch Protector Dolis]]
   - [[Ambassador of the Mist]]
   - [[Ambassador of the Thicket]]
   - [[Ambassador of the Grove]]

## Rewards

- 5 [[City Token|City Tokens]]
