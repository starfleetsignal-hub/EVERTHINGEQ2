## Steps

1. Gather 8 caustic crystals from [[The Boiling Wells]]
   - The crystals are found along the edges of the water.
   - About half the time, the crystal will break when you try to harvest it - the game will emote in red when this happens
   - On a successful crystal harvest, you will be attacked by [[An arcane waterspout]].
1. Return to Zaveta

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g 80s
- [[Gazanti Grave Marker]]
