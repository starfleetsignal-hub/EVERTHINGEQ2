## Steps

1. Kill  [[Dracomancer Vandalix]] {{waypoint 145.8, 205.1, -616.1}}
1. Kill 4 [[A Blackscale dracomancer|Blackscale dracomancer]]s - On the top of the four towers on the [[Cacotoxic Stain]]
1. Kill 12 Blackscales of any sort
1. Return to [[Deebs Beaklut]]

## Rewards

- +3200 faction with **Drednever Expedition**
- At least 18g 85s 15c
- [[Blackscale Weapon Collection]]
- 5,700 XP at level 70.
