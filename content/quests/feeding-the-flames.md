## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- If you have lost [[The Totem of Aaryonar]], another one can be acquired by clicking the statue in [[Ascent of the Awakened]], at {{waypoint 3, -1, -4}}.

## Steps

1. Acquire 20 horns from Doomwing Legion droags in [[The Halls of Fate]].
   - Do not kill [[Doom Guard Tammuz]] or [[Doom Guard Vimock]], as you will need to kill them for one of the follow-up quests.
1. Return to the [[The Atrium of Kildrukaun]] and place the droag horns in the braziers in the following order (left-to-right):
   - The Flame of Yarna
   - The Flame of Na'Ar
   - The Flame of Ona
   - The Flame of An'Oa
1. Examine [[The Totem of Aaryonar]] from your inventory. This will spawn [[Yonar the Dromunculus]] who will give you the next three quests in the questline. They can be completed in any order:
   - [[Reclaiming the Totems]]
   - [[Obscuring Knowledge]]
   - [[Beyond Death's Door]]
