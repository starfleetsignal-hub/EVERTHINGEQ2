## Steps

1. Look for the captain's signet ring in The Seafury Hideout near the boat docked in [[South Freeport]]; the zone-in is at {{waypoint -285, -57, 133}}
   1. Right-click on the shovel at {{waypoint -5, 1, -14}} (in the corner at the bottom of the stairs) and select "Grab Shovel" (this will break invis).
   1. Go up the stairs, go to the far left corner {{waypoint -8, 6, -25}}, right-click on the dirt and select "Use Shovel" (this will break invis).
   1. Take the key downstairs into the barracks, right-click the dresser {{waypoint -3.5, -1.2, -35.4}} and select "Use Key" (breaks invis).
   1. Run back to the zone entrance and exit the zone.
1. Return the ring to [[Gil McMartin]] {{waypoint -923, -47, 13}} in [[The Commonlands]].

## Rewards

- +5000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -5000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -5000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- -300 faction with **The Seafury Buccaneers**
