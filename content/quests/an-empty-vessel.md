## Notes

- You must have progressed to the point in the quest [[A Star to Unlock]], part of the [[Claymore Timeline]], where your craft 'the Star Key' to unlock this quest.

## Steps

![](images/An_empty_glass_jug.png)

1. Look around the first and second floor for an 'an empty glass jug'. Confirmed spawns:
   - [[The Fountain Room of Aeristok]]: {{waypoint -5, -1, 2}}
   - Along the side corridors of the Fountain room:
     - {{waypoint 7.94, -0.13, -85.49}}
     - {{waypoint 8, -0.13, 121}}
   - Second Floor: {{waypoint 39, 28.5, -15}}
1. Kill 6 [[A menacing droplet|menacing droplets]], found on the first floor around the fountain at {{waypoint -9, -1, 3}}. Sinister fluidities, on the second floor, do not count.
1. Return to the peculiar urn at {{waypoint 2, 0, -43}} and pour the water into it.
1. Within 2 minutes, click on [[An exquisite urn]], nearby at {{waypoint -11.5, 0, 27}}

## Rewards

- At least 13g 50s 50c
