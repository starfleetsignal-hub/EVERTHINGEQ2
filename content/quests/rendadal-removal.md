- This quest seems to be available after completed [[Restoration and Reclamation]]

## Steps

- Destroy 8 Renda'Dal camp provisions (barrels near h1 heroic encounters)  ![Renda'Dal camp provisions](images/Renda'Dal_Provisions.png)

    - {{waypoint -371, -63, 231}}
    - {{waypoint -669, -63, 309}}
- Shatter 5 [[A metamorphic watcher]] or [[A metamorphic demolisher]] (h1 large golems along the  outer walls and on some pathways)
