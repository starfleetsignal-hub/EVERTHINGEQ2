## Steps

1. Find a deepore boulder to make a millstone.  These are lighter colored rocks that look like mining nodes. (Several can be found around {{waypoint -353, -388, -711}} and {{waypoint -637, 0, -551}} east of the Ring War battlefield.  Approximate ten minute respawn time).
   - When you inspect one a [[Boiling blistercrag]] will spawn and attack you; kill the boiling blistercrag to update the quest.  This may take several inspections (up to ten have been reported).
1. Deliver the boulder to [[Cala Giantender]].
1. Collect [[Dull Coldain Axe|axes]] from Ring War battlefield, sharpen them on millstone, next to [[Cala Giantender]].
   - The war axes have a green sparkle around them; picking them up doesn't update the quest.  Check your inventory, they will be there.  Make sure you have five before returning to [[Cala Giantender]]
   - The millstone can be found right next to [[Cala Giantender]]; sharping the axes WILL update the quest.
1. Talk to [[Cala Giantender]] for update.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
