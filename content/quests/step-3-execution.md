## Steps

![The tacking post, with Captain Boil's ship below](images/Step_3_visaid.jpg)

1. Use Blacktalon's tacking post {{waypoint 2836, 62, 907}} to fly to Captain Boil's ship.
1. Kill [[Captain Boil]] {{waypoint 3153, 2, 1136}}. Click the tacking post on the ship to fly back to [[Gorowyn Beach]].
1. Speak to [[Primary Sha'Ki]] {{waypoint 2311, 37, 1014}} in [[Gorowyn Beach Outpost]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s 86c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Fighter's Hoop]] (Fighters)
  - [[Sorcerer's Hoop]] (Mages)
