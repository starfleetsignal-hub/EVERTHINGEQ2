**Note**: You can get this quest concurrently with [[Shades of Drinal: Skies of Red]], even though it's listed as a pre-requisite. Do that and then take [[Unexpected Ally]], which you can do at the same time as [[Fortunes Turned]], and cut-out some extra running around.

## Steps

1. Find the unturned Tsol Ew
   - [[Alzilius]] {{waypoint -2044, 218, 816}}
1. Collect [[An impure ethereal essence|impure essence]] from the [[An etherveil planeshifter|planeshifters]] (6)
   - The planeshifters are found at the base of the waterfall below the cliffs of the Fearcreep, in [[The Eidolon Jungle]] {{waypoint -1200, 30, 500}}.  Each one may drop [[An impure ethereal essence]].
1. Cleanse the essence in the druid ring at {{waypoint -563, -14, 852}}
1. Return the cleansed essence to [[Alzilius]] {{waypoint -2044, 218, 816}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Decanter of Baroddas]]
