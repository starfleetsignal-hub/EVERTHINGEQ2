## Notes

- To get the actual quest update for the steps below, you have to stand still at each of the locations for around 1-2 minutes and then move slightly away. You do not get the update while standing, but only after you have moved away from it.

## Steps

1. Visit the following locations:
   - Fountain in [[The Shimmering Citadel]]: {{waypoint 138, 143, -23}}
   - [[The Great Bonsai]] in [[The Pillars of Flame]]: {{waypoint 135, -189, -1672}} (Make sure you face the Ashen Order and not the sea.)
   - [[Pirate's Perch]] in [[The Sinking Sands]]: {{waypoint -2029, -160, 73}} (climb to the top of the mountain and stand in the shade under the palm tree)
1. Return to [[The Muse]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Cowl of Arcane Fervor]]
  - [[Belt of Shadow's Essence]]
  - [[Pants of Karana's Blessing]]
  - [[Vambraces of Divine Inspiration]] ([[Berserker]], [[Guardian]], [[Inquisitor]], [[Paladin]], [[Shadowknight]], [[Templar]])
