## Steps

1. The Eidolon tells you to go back to the beginning.
   - Head back to [[Steamfont]] where you found the portal at {{waypoint -1235, 86, 927}} and kill the minotaur. He will have a body drop manacle for each member of the group.
1. Find the shards of the manacle scattered throughout [[Norrath]].
   - Around these locations: These areas are highlighted on your map and are fairly small.
     - [[Lavastorm]]: {{waypoint -195, 85, -208}} near the entrance to [[Solusek's Eye]].
     - [[Kunzar Jungle]]: {{waypoint -65, -44, 156}} Roughly this location, near the [[Tabernacle of Pain]].
     - [[Enchanted Lands]]: {{waypoint -66, 19, -865}} East of [[Runnyeye]].
     - [[Nektulos Forest]]: {{waypoint -480, -6, -961}} In the lake north of the wizard spire.
     - [[Thundering Steppes]]: {{waypoint 1025, -26, -113}} Just north of the wizard spire in the crater.
     - [[Pillars of Flame]]: {{waypoint 1508, -145, -933}} Roughly around here, on the top of the ramp going down to the water.
     - [[Feerrott]]: {{waypoint -1810, -15, 812}} Just east of [[Cazic Thule]] entrance.
     - [[Moors of Ykesha]]: {{waypoint -2, -81, -396}} Just east of [[Firmroot Moot]].
1. Find a blacksmith capable of repairing the manacle.
   - Return to [[Eshkan]] {{waypoint 4243, -819, 432}} in [[Cobalt Scar]].
   - This step takes a minute or so to complete.
1. [[Eshkan]] has put together the pieces, but they are a manacle no longer. Speak to the Eidolon again, who is now more cryptic than ever.
   - Go to [[The Fabled Sanctum of the Scaleborn]] and kill 15 ^^^ droag named, stealing their power for your bracers.
1. Use the bracer and speak with the [[Eidolon of Heroism]]
1. The Eidolon's last words were "... We are all fated to die. Some can be reborn. So this goes."
   - Head to [[The Fabled Halls of Fate]] and kill [[Sothis]].
1. Use the [[Disused manacles|disused manacles]] and speak with the [[Eidolon of Heroism]]
