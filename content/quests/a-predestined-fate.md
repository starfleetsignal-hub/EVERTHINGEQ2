## Steps

1. Speak again with [[Rooleean Felodaan]]. He will ask you to wait while he destroys the shards.
1. After he has destroyed the shards, hail him again and he will aggro. Kill him to get [[The orb of Tunare]]. If you are grouped its 1 update per person Wuoshi TLE - couple of mins respawn.
1. Return to [[Arloona Delyssa]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]]. {{waypoint -247, -56.5, -384}}
