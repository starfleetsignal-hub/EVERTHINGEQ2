## Steps

1. Kill 7 badgers.
   - [[A young grove badger]] and [[A grove badger]] are found just north/northeast of [[Lost Village of Bobick]] around {{waypoint 75, 19, -287}}.
1. Speak with [[Deputy Kegie]] in a wagon at [[The Riverwatch]] outside [[Rivervale]] {{waypoint -206.44, 0.40, -352.39}}

## Rewards

- At least 46s 69c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Decaying Nose Ring]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Orb of Lesser Deflection]] (All Priests)
  - [[Screaming Serpent's Shell]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Tinkered Gauge of Infamy]] (All Mages)
