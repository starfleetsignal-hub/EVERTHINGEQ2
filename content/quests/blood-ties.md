## Notes

- Scales with player level, requires level 60+
- You can purchase upgraded versions of the quest reward from [[Oodifo]] {{waypoint 284, -18, -65}} at [[Sneed Galliway's Trading Post]] in North Qeynos. Level 70, 80 and 90 versions of the items are available.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Lilly Ironforge]] in [[North Qeynos]] behind the [[The Ironforge Estate|Ironforge Estate]], standing next to the opening of the [[Ironforge Family Catacombs]]. You may have to target her and press **h** to Hail. {{waypoint 511, -21, -355}}
1. Enter the [[Ironforge Family Catacombs]]. The entrance is next to Lily. (Note: The quest text says Ironforge Crypt). Slay [[A ceremony guard|ceremony guards]] to clear your way.
1. Defeat [[Kyle Bayle]].
1. Return to [[Queen Antonia Bayle (Queen Antonia's Chambers)|Queen Antonia]].

## Rewards

- At least 23g 45s 42c (at level 61)
- At least 1p 25g (at level 100)
- Select one of the following based on class and level:
  - Level 60
    - [[Royal Crate of Sharpened Spikes]] (scouts, Berserker, Bruiser, Guardian, Monk)
    - [[Shortbow of Bayle]] (scouts, Berserker, Channeler, Guardian, Paladin, Shadowknight)
    - [[Longbow of Bayle]] (scouts, Berserker, Channeler, Guardian, Paladin, Shadowknight)
    - [[Royal Crate of Sharp Thorns]] (scouts, Berserker, Bruiser, Guardian, Monk)
    - [[Royal Wand of Bright Infusions]] (mage, priest)
    - [[Royal Wand of Illusionary Projection]] (mage, priest)
  - Level 80
    - [[Wisened Bow of Bayle]] (scout/tank)
    - [[Royal Crate of Sharpened Spines]] (scout/warrior/brawler)
    - [[Wand of Blessed Protection]] (mage, priest)
    - [[Wand of Illusionary Protection]] (mage, priest)
  - Level 90
    - [[Intricate Longbow of Bayle]] (scout/tank, level 90)
    - [[Wisened Shortbow of Bayle]] (scout/tank, level 90)
    - [[Royal Crate of Petrified Thorns]] (scout/warrior/brawler, level 90)
    - [[Royal Crate of Sharp Spines]] (scout/warrior/brawler, level 90)
    - [[Royal Wand of Delusionary Protection]] (mage/priest, level 90)
    - [[Royal Wand of Shadowed Infusions]] (mage/priest, level 90)
