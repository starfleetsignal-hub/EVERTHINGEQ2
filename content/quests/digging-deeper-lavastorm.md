## Description

You have been asked to thin the population of guards within Najena's Hollow Tower.

## Steps

1. Quell the number of guards within [[Najena's Hollow Tower]]
   - Defeat 10 Fire Ogre Najenaar guards
     - There are 15 Fire Ogre Guards in the zone, in case you need to switch up group members.
   - Defeat 20 Najenaar Flame guards
     - There are 35 or so Flame Guards in an instance. You have some leeway.
1. Return to Isxot D'Zyath.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
