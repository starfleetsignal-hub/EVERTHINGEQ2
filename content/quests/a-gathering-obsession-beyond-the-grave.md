## Notes

If you have not completed the quest [[Captain Overboard?]], you will be offered that quest before being offered this quest. However completion of Captain Overboard is not required. It is necessary to have completed [[The Captain's Lament]] and [[Fond Memories]] to be able to start this quest, although it is not necessary to do the sub-quests of Fond Memories.

## Steps

1. Find **a Wayward guard**, who reports seeing a child heading to the northwest path {{waypoint -353, 11, -48}}
1. Find the missing child [[Qho Augren (Obol Plains)|Qho Augren]] on top of the rock wall at {{waypoint -34.18, 80.53, 17.59}} ![Qho hiding up on the wall.](images/Qho_on_the_wall.png)
1. Pick some 20 **[[Plump lychee|fresh]] [[Plump lychee|lychees]]** and **[[Ripe mangosteen|mangosteens]]** (Astral Shrub) for Qho (These must be harvested in [[Obol Plains]])
1. Return to Qho with the fruit
1. Talk to [[Steve Echgar]] in town ![The cause of all your troubles.](images/QhoAugren.png)
1. Talk to Qho near the tourbillion {{waypoint -680, 1, 70}} Qho will enter the tourbillion to [[Eidolon Jungle]] (Drop down the round pit)
1. Talk to Qho near the tourbillion {{waypoint -2454, 207, 618}}
1. Talk to Qho near the entrance to the [[Temple of the Faceless (Heroic)|Temple of the Faceless]] {{waypoint -2162, 222, 767}}. He will get an attitude and run off into the woods.  Go find him.![Qho at the entry to the Temple of the Faceless](images/Qho_At_Faceless_entry.png)
1. Talk to Qho near [[Gulthga (NPC)|Gultgha]] {{waypoint -1375, 217, 593}}
1. Gather a small tree to keep Qho quiet (clickable tree at {{waypoint -1332, 218, 570}}
   - ***NOTE:** You can continue gathering additional trees to keep as house items, but bank them before turning the quest in, or you may lose them.*
1. Talk to Qho near [[Gulthga (NPC)|Gultgha]] {{waypoint -1375, 217, 593}}
1. Talk to Qho at  {{waypoint -444, -9, 555}}
1. Talk to Qho near the base camp {{waypoint -67, 13, 160}} or {{waypoint -441, -11, 560}} ![Qho near The Base Camp. You may need your map to locate by flashing book.](images/Qho_Near_Base_campl.png)
1. Click the rift to [[The Feerrott|Feerrott]] {{waypoint -63, 13, 168}}NOTE: Fast travel doesn't update the quest, you have to use the rift.
1. Talk to Qho on the beach {{waypoint 109, -7, -86}}
1. Talk to Qho in his pond in the [[The Village of Shin|Village of Shin]] {{waypoint -33, 10, -112}} or {{waypoint -28, 10, -116}}
1. Return to [[Steve Echgar]] in [[Cardin Ward]]

## Rewards

- At least 5p 7g 7s
- 10 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- [[Artisan's Fully Trained Pack Pony]] NOTE: this is simply an upgrade to your existing [[Artisan's Pack Pony]]. It will be able to harvest rares once you re-summon the pony; it is not a new spell and doesn't rename the pony.
