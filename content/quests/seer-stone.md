## Steps

1. Retrieve the following items by slaying creatures:
   - 5 piles of bone dust - any skeleton, e.g. [[A fallen crusader (Commonlands)|fallen crusader]]  at Ruins of Val'Marr {{waypoint 566, -47, 261}}, or [[A brittle skeleton (Commonlands)|brittle skeletons]] at either graveyard.
   - 5 water glands - any vulrich, eg [[Giant vulrich]] {{waypoint 946, -46, 64}} or [[A young vulrich|young vulrich]] around [[The Dog Pond]]
   - 5 sparkly bits - any wisp, e.g. [[A moon wisp]] {{waypoint -110, -48, -453}}
1. Return to Madam Vi for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 91c
- One of the Following (based on class):
  - [[Antonican Defender's Mantle]] (Fighter)
  - [[Antonican Stalker's Mantle]] (Scout)
  - [[Antonican Medium's Mantle]] (Mage)
  - [[Antonican Healer's Mantle]] (Priest)
