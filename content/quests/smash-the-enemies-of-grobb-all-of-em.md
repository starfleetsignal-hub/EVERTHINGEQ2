## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

- Kill 6 Brokenskull trolls (head towards obelisk of ahkzul; includes void-touched brokenskulls)
- Kill 6 Ykeshan frogloks and trolls (head towards entrance of guk)
- Kill 6 Thullosians

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- -150 faction with **Guktan Guard**
- +1,000 faction with **Clan Grobb**
