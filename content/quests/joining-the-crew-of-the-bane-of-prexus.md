## Steps

1. Ring the mariner's bell on the docks that would normally take you outside Freeport and choose to enter the [[Kunark Expedition]]. You will arrive aboard the [[Bane of Prexus]].![The iksar ship draws within boarding range.](images/Iksar_Ship_Approaching.png)
1. Speak with [[Captain Pegg Bristol]] to trigger the attacking ship. Iksar sailors will bring their ship along side the Bane of Prexus and board the ship. You cannot board the attacking ship. The attacking sailors will board the Bane and will begin killing crew members but remain unattackable to you. Once the sailors become attackable, they will attempt to kill you.
1. Kill two waves of [[Iksar sailor]]s.
1. Kill the wave of [[Iksar slave]]s.
1. Kill the [[Iksar captain]], [[Iksar cabin boy]], and [[Iksar slave]].
1. Loot the [[Torn mariner's chart]] from the Small Chest.
1. Click on the ship's wheel to return to [[East Freeport]]
1. Speak with [[Livia Montelius]].
1. Speak with [[Cartographer Orex]], upstairs in the [[Seafarer's Roost]], and show him the mariner's chart.
1. Return to [[Livia Montelius]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[The Mariner's Lament]] - a book for your house
- [[What We Know of Kunark]] - a book for your house
- [[An iksar ship in a bottle]] - a house item

*I helped to defend the [[Bane of Prexus]] from the Iksar and discovered a chart that gives clues as to how to reach Kunark. The Bane was too battered to continue but the information was brought back to the Buccaneers for study.*
