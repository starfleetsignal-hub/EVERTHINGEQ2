## Steps

1. Enter [[Frostfell Workshop]] from [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]]. {{waypoint 40, -4, -1181}}
1. Speak to [[Meg Gingersnap]]. {{waypoint -15, 4, -55}}
1. Kill the two [[A clockwork toy|clockwork toy]] outside the workshop. {{waypoint 64, -8, -39}}
1. Return the parts to Meg
1. Exit the instance and return to [[Jarith Icewing]].

- 1 [[Frozen Token of E'ci]]
