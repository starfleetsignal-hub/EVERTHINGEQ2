## Steps

1. Find [[Alakzaer T'Aleett]]. He's in the [[Nerian Highlands]] {{waypoint -253, 0, -205}}. You can get there by taking the path that goes up past Smoldering Lake.

## Rewards

- Adventure and Achievement experience

## Notes

The named mob [[Searscale]] 14^ spawns on the path right by Alakzaer. There was a loud screeching just before he appeared, so if you need to move away you have a couple of seconds. Just watch out for respawns on the path behind you.
