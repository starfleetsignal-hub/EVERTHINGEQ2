## Steps

1. Kill 25 [[A Tallon grunt|Tallon grunt]]s. {{waypoint 645, -38, 225}}
1. Gather 5 Black Saps from [[Root Nodes#T4|vestigal roots]] in the zone. Although you may get several items per harvest, you will need to harvest 5 separate times to get the quest updates.
1. Visit the [[Mythical Forest]] {{waypoint -180, 30, 140}}. This is the next valley east of [[Refuge]].
1. Speak to Gaertarn

## Rewards

- Experience only
