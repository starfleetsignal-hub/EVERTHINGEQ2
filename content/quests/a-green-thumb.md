## Description

"Whittier, a treant at Green Knoll, hopes that Dhala Veera, a fae who has often tended to his wounds will know of a way to help his corrupted treant brethren within Treant Grove." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Dhala Veera]] on Green Knoll {{waypoint 591, 45, 0}}
1. Use *[[Dhala's Rejuvenating Bloom]]* on 6 [[A corrupted sapling|corrupted saplings]] in [[Treant Grove]]
- Note: If you have autoattack mode set to "Forced Range/Spell", using the potion will initiate combat with the treant, and you will get the message "your target is engaged in combat" when the potion completes instead of an update. Plus you'll have to kill the treat. Set auto attack to "Automatic".

1. Speak to [[Whittier]] {{waypoint 573, 45.75, 73}}

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Whittier: "there must be a way to cleanse the sickness from the suffering treants within the grove. Perhaps Dhala Veera knows. She often tended to my bark when Sunneva's cats mistook me as a scratching post.
- Player Character: "I will ask her for you."

Whittier suggested I speak with Dhala Veera, also at Green Knoll. speak with Dhala Veera.

- Dhala Veera: "Well met, adventurer."
- Player Character: "Whittier sent me to ask for your help."
- Dhala Veera: "He is not sick, is he?"
- Player Character: "No. Wait, you know of the sickness?"
- Dhala Veera: "Yes, I have been to the Treant Grove and seen their suffering. Unfortunately, it took time to get the components necessary, particularly the Bloom of Growth morning dew, but I just finished creating a cleansing elixir for them."
- Player Character: "That's perfect!"
- Dhala Veera: "Take this elixir and poor it on the roots of the corrupted saplings within the grove, [Player Character]. It should help them."
- Player Character: "Right away!"

Dhala Veera gave me a Rejuvenating Bloom elixir. I should use Dhala's Rejuvenating Bloom elixir upon the corrupted saplings within Treant Grove. Cleans the corrupted saplings.

I have used Dhala's Rejuvenating Bloom upon the corrupted saplings within Treant Grove. I should return to Whittier, the treant back at Green Knoll. Speak with Whittier.

- Whittier: "I have awaited your return, [Player Character]. Dhala told me that she provided you with an elixir. Did it help my corrupted brethren of the grove?"
- Player Character: "It did!"
- Whittier: "Ah, that is a ray of sunshine! Thank you."

I spoke with Whittier. I spoke with Whittier and told him of my success.

Whittier was relieved to know that I had cleansed his treant brethren with Dhala's Rejuvenating Bloom.
