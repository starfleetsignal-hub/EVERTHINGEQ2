## Steps

![A location for a runic monitor.](images/Intelligence_Monitoring.png)

1. Head to the [[Erudin Gardens]] to place the [[Runic Monitor (Jaswinda)|runic monitors]].
   - Click on the Red Sparkling Effect to place the first monitor at {{waypoint -157, 524, -110}}
   - Place the second monitor at {{waypoint -184, 524, -110}}
   - Place the third monitor at {{waypoint -184, 524, -361}}
   - Place the fourth monitor at {{waypoint -157, 524, -361}}
   - *Note: You may need to look up to see the red sparkling effect.*
1. Return to [[Jaswinda]] {{waypoint -154, 194, -361}}.
1. Note: It's in the land ABOVE the [[The Deepwater Pavilion|Deepwater Pavilion]], in a little city full of lvl 90's (many groups).  Take the transporter to the Marred Plateau Station.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
