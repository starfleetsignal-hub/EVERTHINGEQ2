## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[An undead pawn|undead pawns]] at [[The Chessboard]] near {{waypoint -831, 101, -15}} for the bits of enchanted ore.
1. Return to [[Holdyn Mackendrick]].
1. [[Holdyn Mackendrick]] sends you back to [[Raghunatha]] at the docks, near {{waypoint 695, 24, 583}} .

## Rewards

- At least 1g 60s 26c
- A class-dependent item:
  - [[Wand of the Betrayed Inquisitor]] (All Priests)
  - [[Silver Mountain's Magic Stick]] (All Mages)
  - [[Steamfont Pouch of Geysers]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Rust Colored Onyx Bow]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Gnome-crafted Arrow Launching Apparatus]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
