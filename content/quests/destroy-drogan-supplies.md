## Description

Lieutenant Kresic has commissioned me to destroy crates within Nu'roga

## Steps

1. Enter [[Nu'roga]] and destroy 8 Drogan Supply Crates.
   - NOTE: Be sure to have your groupmates close by as once a supply crate is destroyed, those who are not around it will not receive an update and will not be able to complete their quest!
   - Possible locations:{{waypoint -51.57, 0.00, 20.43}}, {{waypoint 36.01, -0.01, -40.38}}, {{waypoint 52.98, 63.22, 20.07}}, {{waypoint -38.80, 63.27, -40.94}}, {{waypoint 40.59, 156.87, 29.08}}, {{waypoint 43.20, 156.55, 23.11}}, plus 2 more on the wooden ramp up to the top level.
1. Return to Lieutenant Kresic

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
