## Objectives

1. ![](images/Lockbox.png)Travel to [[Nektulos Forest]] and find a chest {{waypoint -966.97, 1.36, -573.69}} in the middle of the bridge NW of The Pot. The Ulteran Spire - Teleporter has the closest access.
   - Right-click it and break the lock.  Right click the chest again and loot it.
   - You will be ambushed by 4 81-heroic [[Ebon Mask Ambushers]] when you break the lock. Kill them and the two waves that follow.  Finally a ^^ named, [[Aastra L'Darlan]] will spawn.  Kill her.
1. Return to [[Nyran Dudez]] by returning to [[Butcherblock Mountains]] and clicking the barrel behind the docks {{waypoint 660, 24, 621}} to enter [[Halls of the Unseen]].

## Rewards

- +10,000 faction with **The Circle of the Unseen Hand**
- 2p 8g 15s
