This is not a normal collection quest. The collection members are not obtained by harvesting shinies.<br>
Instead, the twenty collection members are themselves all obtained from the completion of other collection quests. This is called a "meta-collection".

## Rewards

- [[Planar Entropy Essence]] this item is needed for the collection: [[Vestiges of Prophecy]]
- [[Waistguard of the Elements of Augury]]
