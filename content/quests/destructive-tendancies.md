## Steps

1. See if anyone will speak about the experiments
   1. Go to [[Temple Street: Vault of Necromechanica]]
   1. use the [[Temple Street Gnome Illusion]]
   1. speak with [[Glimzig Clangbottom]] at {{waypoint 22, 3, 58}}
1. use [[Glimzig's Zapstick]] on 6 clockworks to gather them
1. speak with [[Glimzig Clangbottom]] at {{waypoint 22, 3, 58}}
1. Speak with [[Analyst Dimir]]
1. click on the machine outside (to the right)
1. click on the book on the table where [[Analyst Dimir]] was
1. Speak with [[Foci Tamara Paust]]
