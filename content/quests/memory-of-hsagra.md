## Notes

- You recieve [[Pendant of Hsagra]] when you start the quest

## Steps

1. Find a good place on [[Icespine]] to bury the pendant of Hsagra
   - Quest updates at a sparkly pile of snow at {{waypoint 401, 114, -428}}
1. Destroy any manifestations of Awakened magic in [[Icespine]]  x 8 - any mobs killed in the same area give updates
1. Bury the pendant of Hsagra at the prepared spot - Same location as above, click on Burial Spot
1. The spirit of Hsagra to appears for a few seconds. *just wait*
1. Return to [[Mergus Stonesledge]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
