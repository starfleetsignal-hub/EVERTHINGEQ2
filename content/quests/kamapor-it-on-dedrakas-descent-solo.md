## Steps

Kill all nameds in the zone [[Karuupa Jungle: Dedraka's Descent (Solo)]] as you need the last 2 for this quest:

1. Bring down the pteranodon.
1. Rout the cannibals.

- At least 3p 59g 25s 12c 107,799 status
- 3 [[Vetrovian Dres'Aag]]
