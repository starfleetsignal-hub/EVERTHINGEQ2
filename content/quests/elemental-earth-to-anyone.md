## Notes

- This is one of the daily (18 hour timer) repeatable missions offered after finishing [[Rage of Cthurath Crafting Timeline]].
- Bring 30 Formless Incense.

## Steps

1. Harvest in The Unknown:
   - 30 x Oogothl Onyx
   - 18 x Grassland Leather
   - 12 x Gorroth Garbanzo
1. Craft 6 x [[Xebrith Ballad of Exploration]] - each one needs 5 x Oogothl Onyx, 3 x Grassland Leather, 2 x Gorroth Garbanzo and 5 x Formless Incense.
   - use Engraved Desk or Varig's Forge multi station next to Taslana
1. Quest auto completes after last crafting combine
