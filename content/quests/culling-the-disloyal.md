## Steps

1. Run along Compassion Road in North Freeport (along the coast, starts at Beggar's Court gate) looking for [[A dissenting citizen|dissenting citizens]]. Hail them
   - Kill 8 citizens: tell em to "Follow me..." starts a fight (your level, one down)
   - Escort 4 citizens to Beggar's Court gate {{waypoint -144, -53, -355}}: tell em to "Don't worry..." turns the citizen docile and it follows you
1. Speak to [[Tullia Domna]] {{waypoint -210, -58, -177}}
   - If you insist she goes with you, you will be instantly killed by [[Hodga Blackblood]], standing next to her. If you go the crazy-but-harmless route your quest still updates
1. Speak to [[Saveth Noolright]] at [[The Amphitheater of Kelkarn]] {{waypoint -68, -21, 130}}. Kill him (even con)
1. Speak to [[Lerenth (The City of Freeport)|Lerenth]] at the Militia House

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c @ level 79
- [[Dethknell Citadel Over the Academy]]
