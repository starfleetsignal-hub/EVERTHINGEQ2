I've convinced Bhoughbh Bhildur, a gnemlin tinkerer in Maldura's Aquifer Pumping Station, that he may have been on to something with this last machtraption. He doesn't have the time to construct new mini-thaumaturgical cylinders, so he's looking to retro-fit some existing ones.

## Steps

1. Exit the city to [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]] and speak with [[Sasha Klanghurst]] in [[Outer Maldura]] at {{waypoint 435.40, 134.81, 297.55}}.
1. Take a [[Shock Stick]] from the low rectangular chest by [[Sasha Klanghurst|Sasha]] {{waypoint 435.17, 134.81, 295.72}}.
1. Use the [[Shock Stick]] on [[A clockwork rodent|clockwork rodents]] and collect the **mini-thaumaturgical cylinders** from them once they're immobilized.
   - *Note: There are many clockwork rodents in the [[Stoneheart Yards]], for example {{waypoint 487, 96, 49}}*
1. Return to [[Bhoughbh Bhildur|Bhoughbh]] in [[Maldura]].
