## Pre-Requisite

- Need to be Level 80 to equip the [[Sir Gnawser Chow Disguise]], reward from [[A Rat's a Rat's a Rat]] in order to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Find 6 plates of cheese and 6 bags of grain around the pirate shanty (clicky items).
1. Return to Rattamus.
