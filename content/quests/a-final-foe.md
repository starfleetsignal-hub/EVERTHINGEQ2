## Steps

1. Slay [[Overseer Dentfang]] (level 11). He is found in the last dead end in Peat Bog when proceeding through the zone clockwise {{waypoint 784, -33, 484}}.  Dentfang is grouped with 3 [[A gnoll conspirator|Gnoll Conspiritors]] (all 11vv)
1. Return to Lieutenant Dawson for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Mossy Topper]]
  - [[Contention's Wall]]
  - [[Orb of the Cloudy Eye]]
  - [[Mindspike]]
  - [[Murky Axe]]
- +175 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
