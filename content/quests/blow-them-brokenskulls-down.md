## Description

Mercenaries have been hired by the Far Seas Trading Company to raid the Brokenskull pirate hideout, but none have returned. I'm sure with the help of some of my closest allies we can complete this mission where those others could not.

## Steps

1. Zone into [[Brokenskull Bay: Spoils of Maraud (Contested)]]
1. Kill 150 mobs in the zone.  (There are many more than that available.)
