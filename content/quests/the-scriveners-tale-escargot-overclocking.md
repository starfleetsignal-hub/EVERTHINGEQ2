## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 50 [[Celestial Incense]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Go to the [[North Wing Stacks]] on the first (ground) floor  of the Library and take the teleporter to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]] {{waypoint 260, -20, -285}} to access the overland zones and harvest the following: (Mats you already have don't count). **All of these mats can be harvested safely in [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]].**
   - 16 [[Deadly terrorfruit]]
   - 12 [[Strangler root]] (e.g. [[Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wind]])
   - 9 [[Dewpalm lumber]] (e.g. [[Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wind]])
   - 2 [[Silvered fleck]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Scroll of Enhanced Spirit of the Wolf]] you received at the beginning of this quest and create [[Recipe Scroll: Enhanced Spirit of the Wolf]] on an [[Engraved Desk]].
   - There is an [[Engraved Desk]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]]. Go to the [[South Wing Catalog]] on the second floor of the Library and take the teleporter to the [[Crafters' Gallery]].
1. Find [[Yrag the Swift]] on the main floor of the Library in the [[Myrist Main Stacks]] and click on him, Then click on the [[Recipe Scroll: Enhanced Spirit of the Wolf|Enhanced Spirit of the Wolf]] spell either in your Quest Helper, or in your inventory. He will grow larger, and then speed off.
1. Take the elevator at {{waypoint 421.50, -4.96, -12.86}}, then use the teleporter in the South Wing Catalog {{waypoint 388, 16, 170}} to the Smiths' Gallery and speak with [[Ghida Borrell]]  at {{waypoint 660, 412, 59}}.
1. Speak with [[Ramyn Matrick]] at {{waypoint 653, 411, 21}}.

## Rewards

- [[Crystal Shard Backpack]] - *88 slot backpack with an appearance*
- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
