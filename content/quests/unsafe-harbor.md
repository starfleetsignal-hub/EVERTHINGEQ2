## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 40+

## Steps

1. Slay 6 [[A traitorous sailor|traitorous sailors]] in [[Qeynos Harbor (POI)|Qeynos Harbor]] in the [[Qeynos Province District]].
   - Sailors seem to spawn in pairs.
   - Look for sailors near supply crates.
     - {{waypoint 807, -23, 34}}
     - {{waypoint 883, -25, -29}}
     - {{waypoint 780, -23.22, 77.3}}
1. Find supply crates on the harbour area. Take 3 crates of supplies to [[Sergeant Delve]] at the Royal Antonican Guard drop-off point. {{waypoint 905, -25, 87}}
   - The crates are moved like a furniture item.
   - Set the supply crates near Sergeant Delve for updates.
1. Speak to to [[Sergeant Delve]].
1. Seek the rogue captain at [[The Mermaid's Lure]]. {{waypoint 954, -26, 11}}
   - Click the door at {{waypoint 952, -20, 10}} to zone into the small instance [[The Plotter's Hovel]]
   - Confront [[Captain Hookwind]]
   - Hookwind will run, leaving you to fight 4 [[A menacing sailor|menacing sailors]] (scales vv, not linked).
1. Return to Sergeant Delve.
1. Return to Field Marshal Vishra.
1. Head to the gates of Qeynos Castle in [[North Qeynos (POI)|North Qeynos]] in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]]
1. Click the gates to enter [[Queen Antonia's Chambers]] to meet with [[Antonia Bayle|Queen Antonia Bayle]]. {{waypoint 492, 5, -20}}
1. Speak to Field Marshal Vishra so he can begin briefing the Queen
1. Listen to the conversation between Vishra and Queen Antonia.
1. Speak again with Field Marshal Vishra at [[The Sovereign House of Qeynos]] where you first met him.

## Rewards

- [[Lion's Mane Counter]]
- At least 25g 70s 61c (at level 92)
