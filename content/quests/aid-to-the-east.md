## Steps

1. I should locate the village of Nipik's Haven on the shore of the Eastern Wastes.
   - make use of house item [[Gift of Nipik's Haven]] for easy access.
1. Mira told me to seek the othmir named [[Livi]] {{waypoint -1147, -533, 3385}}

Note: In order to cross into the Eastern Wastes, go to the Scar Bridge Station {{waypoint -527, -344, 521}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1500 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
