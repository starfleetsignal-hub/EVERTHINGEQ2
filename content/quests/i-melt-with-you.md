## Description

Cippie, an othmir within Fina's Retreat in the Icy Fingers of the Great Divide, is very concerned for one of his kin, Kidder. Kidder should have returned a day ago from an imports run to Sinking Sands. I've promised Cippie that I will investigate Kidder's absence right away!

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable once per year.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Kidder]] in the [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]], located in one of the pavilions at the bottom of the dock. {{waypoint -1443, -225, -426}}
   - The quickest way to travel to the Sinking Sands is to speak to [[Stissa]] on Lodizal's gangway. {{waypoint -1633, -530, -2399}}
1. Collect 8 *bixie wax* from [[A Tuwasp wax worker|Tuwasp wax workers]] found south of the [[The Nursery|Nursery]] within [[Greater Faydark]]. {{waypoint -110, -49, -610}}
   - The Tuwasps are non-aggro until you come quite close to them, when they respawn into an aggro mob at your level.
1. Return to Kidder, now located back at [[Fina's Retreat]] in [[The Great Divide]]. {{waypoint -1606, -535, -2423}}

## Rewards

- [[Othmir Romantic Remembrance]]
- 5 [[Erollisi coin]]
