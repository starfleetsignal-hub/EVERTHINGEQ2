## Notes

- *This quest in unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*
- *This quest is repeatable..*
- This quest will become unavailable once you hit 20,000 faction with the Ashen Disciples.
- If you receive an undesirable list of target monsters, delete the quest and pick it up again; the target monsters are all randomized.

## Steps

- Kill five of one of the following (random):
  - [[A Sandscrawler defender|Sandcrawler defenders]]
  - [[A Sandscrawler raider|Sandcrawler raiders]]
  - [[A Sandscrawler shamanite|Sandscrawler shamanites]]
- Kill five of one of the following (random):
  - [[A desert prophet|desert prophets]]
  - [[A desert lunatic|desert lunatics]]
  - [[A Windsister dustfeather|Windsister dustfeathers]]
- Kill five of one of the following (random):
  - [[A large blanketfish|large blanketfish]]
  - [[A sand pike|sand pikes]] (around {{waypoint 415, -237, -1513}})
  - [[A flame tornado|flame tornadoes]]
  - [[A sandstalker sabertooth|sandstalker sabertooths]]
  - [[A golden sunfeather falcon|golden sunfeather falcons]]
- Kill five of one of the following (random):
  - [[A Sandscrawler sorcerer|Sandscrawler sorcerers]]
  - [[A Sandscrawler miner|Sandscrawler miners]]
- Return to [[Shing Ho]]

## Rewards

- +1500 faction with **The Ashen Disciples**
