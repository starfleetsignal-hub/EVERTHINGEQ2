## Steps

The Zakir Rish are a major threat to the Sang'Huuu, but not the only one. The Yardani, a clan of Aurai, are slithering on the hooluks' doorsteps. Time to drive them back!

1. Enter [[Zimara Breadth: Razing the Razelands (Solo)]] on the [[Isles of Wisdom]] at {{waypoint -589.70, 148.62, 133.37}}
1. Break [[Eaglovok]] the Shackle Breaker at {{waypoint -382.86, 124.36, -649.35}}
   - *Has a knockback*
1. Use the Crystal Shard teleporter at {{waypoint -333.51, 124.60, -647.11}} to Helionae Razelands
1. Banish [[Gilded Back Demolisher]] the Released Calamity at {{waypoint -268.45, 91.83, -271.81}}
   - *Has a knockback*
1. Oust [[Sina A'rak]] the Overseer at {{waypoint 77.62, 149.74, -321.92}}
1. Kill [[Doda K'Bael]] the Beast Master at {{waypoint -34.74, 156.80, 9.24}}
1. Defeat [[Queen Era'selka]] the Matriarch at {{waypoint 145.04, 161.85, 44.09}}

## Rewards

- At least 4p 31g 10s 14c
- 3x[[Zimaran Ballad]]
- [[Status Coin (75,000)]]
