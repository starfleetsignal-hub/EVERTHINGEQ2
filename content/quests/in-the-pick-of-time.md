## Steps

1. Find the mining expedition on the closest island due west of [[Camp Char'Gin]]. {{waypoint 1517, -233, -1581}}
1. Save what's left of the mining expedition by killing [[An Auroral Selkie]] at {{waypoint 1572, -214, -1510}}. She will spawn once all the other selkies on the island have been slain.
1. Return to [[Siraj al Din]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience only
