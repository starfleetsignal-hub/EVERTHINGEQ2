This quest is repeatable. It is also part of a group of repeatable quests. See [[Additional Zek Quests#Alchemist's Components|Alchemist's Components]].

## Steps

1. Kill [[A Sullon lumberjack|Sullon lumberjacks]] in Zek to get 10 auto-updates
   - ***NOTE:** Allakhazam's reports that this quest MAY have been changed to update on cats. A user reports a message says you find *cat eye sap*. This information has NOT been confirmed and may have been mistakenly intended regarding one of the other quests in this group.*
1. Give the components to [[Leelee Brewbubble]] at {{waypoint 555, -42, 357}}, behind a tent on the [[Warship Dock]] by the ship's bell
