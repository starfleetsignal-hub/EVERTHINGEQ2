## Tips

Equip your mount with any status boosting mount gear like [[Handcrafted Zimaran Hackamore of Status]] or [[Handcrafted Formless Hackamore of Status]] to increase your status reward.

As this is a [[Guide Events|Guide Event]], the quest-giver is a player volunteer and is not always there. Monitor your server's thread on the [EQ2 Guide Forums](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/guide/index.php?forums/everquest-ii-guide-events.330/) to see when the quest will be run. It may be completed at any time after it's obtained from the Guide.

## Starting the quest

Speak to a Guide in the form of [[Ayesorr]], typically located in [[Enchanted Lands]] near the Dock.

*Once again, a mysterious adventurer has come forth and offered me the task to enter a reality beyond ours, a boundless realm of entropy and madness, where mortal lives are often treated with excessive cruelty. I have to ask myself, am I brave enough, strong enough and determined enough to except* [sic] *this challenge and venture in to* [sic] *the Xebrith Tendril and face the challenges and the Rage of Cthurath."*
