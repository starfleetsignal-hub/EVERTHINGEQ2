## Steps

1. Double-click any of the ballista here.{{waypoint 4533.83, -822.48, 395.32}}

![](images/Examine_corroded_spring.png)

1. Examine a [[Corroded Torsion Spring]] in your inventory
1. Scribe [[Notes: Corroded Torsion Spring Investigation]] and create [[Investigation Results]] at a chemistry table {{waypoint 4314, -821, 443}}.
   - Requires:
     - 1 [[Amethyst]]
     - 1 [[Effulgent candle]] *(can be bought from [[Feev Dimbright]] at {{waypoint 4369, -821, 392}} )*
     - the torsion spring.
1. Return to Denstarin

- At least 1p 30g
