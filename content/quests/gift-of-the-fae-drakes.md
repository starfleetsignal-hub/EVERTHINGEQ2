Uko's mentor, Inara, is trying to combat the corruption seeping into the Lesser Faydark.  To do this, she needs you to gather powder from the wings of 10 fae drakes.

## Steps

1. Kill 10 fae drakes to collect their [[Fae Drake Wing Powder|wing powder]].  They can be found in and around [[The Fae Drake Breeding Grounds]], with some lower-level fae drakes appearing as far East as the Lower Grove Station. Fae drakes in Greater Faydark also count.
1. Return to Inara.

## Rewards

- At least 13g 59s 50c
