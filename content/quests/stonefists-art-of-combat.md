## Steps

1. Speak with [[Trainer Ragnhild Stonefist]]
1. Defeat a sparring partner
1. Speak with [[Trainer Ragnhild Stonefist]] again
1. Return to [[Yasha Redblade]]

Note:  It doesn't matter which partner you select.  Any of them satisfies the quest requirement.

## Rewards

- At least 13c
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Trusty Knuckles]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Trusty Stave]] (All Mages)
  - [[Trusty Dagger]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Trusty Mace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Trusty Sword]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
