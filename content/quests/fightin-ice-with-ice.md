## Steps

1. Head southeast from the village where you will find a Rime encampment at {{waypoint 198, -59, 949}}
1. Use the Swirling Snow Gust from your inventory while inside the camp
1. Click on the two tents and one crate to destroy the camp
   - Be ready to fight the mobs that spawn when you destroy an item.
1. Return to Billie to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- One of the following (Class-based):
  - [[Griffin Down Dart]] (scout class)
  - [[Griffin Egg Shell Bauble]] (fighter class)
  - [[Beads of the Griffin Watcher]] (mage class)
  - [[Griffin Feather Charm]] (priest class)
