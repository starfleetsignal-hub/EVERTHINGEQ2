Mei Lan has a hard time understanding gnomes, much less their celebration of Tinkerfest. I will go to the Steamfont Mountains in her stead, and try to find her some non-explosive examples of the holiday.

## Steps

1. I must gather some gnomish-themed items from the [[Steamfont Mountains]] to represent Tinkerfest.
   - Collect 4 **broken Gnomeland gizmos** {{waypoint -646, 131, 922}} at the base of the wall to climb up to Gnomeland Security HQ![Broken Gnomeland Gizmo](images/Broken_Gnomeland_Gizmo.webp)
   - Collect 4 **scratched observatory lenses** {{waypoint -980, 172, 720}} at Frugrin Observatory![Scratched Observatory Lens](images/Scratched_Observatory_Lens.webp)
   - Collect 4 **Meldrath's mining lanterns** {{waypoint -1225, 88, 987}} near The Great Gear![Meldraths Mining Lantern](images/Meldraths_Mining_Lantern.webp)
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in Sundered Frontier

## Rewards

- Unlock the Days of Summer 2021: Week 7 Holiday event rewards that you can purchase at [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2293, -199, 2506}}
