*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Harvest the following in the Feerrott. They are unique nodes that are only found in particular areas, always on the riverbanks. You can target them by hitting tab as you run.
   - Marsh Fern - south branch of Greenblood River
     - {{waypoint -376, -8, 667}}
     - {{waypoint -448, -7, 552}}
     - {{waypoint -21.24, -0.80, 661.22}}
     - {{waypoint -430.27, -12.67, 836.76}}
     - {{waypoint -266, -14, 905}}
   - Green Tipped Fern - northeast branch of Anaconda River
     - {{waypoint -304, 0, 319}}
     - {{waypoint -322.80, -4.18, 427.89}}
     - {{waypoint -368.63, -3.97, 380.41}}
     - {{waypoint -447, -5, 188}}
   - Thistled Fern - Anaconda River near falls
     - {{waypoint -607, -7, 241}}
     - {{waypoint -654, -10, 493}}
     - {{waypoint -655.57, -4.83, 279.89}}
     - {{waypoint -694, -6, 291}}
   - Spiny Fern - east Anaconda River
     - {{waypoint -996, -6, 405}}
     - -1,235.97 -5.62 450.44
     - -1,295 -6 495
     - -1,136 -6 707
     - {{waypoint -1206, -4, 558}}
- Return to [[Vashazdar Manoisti]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 53s 57c
