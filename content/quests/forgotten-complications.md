## Steps

1. Continue harvesting [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog]] nodes until the next three updates, all to find "more complications," are fulfilled. You will harvest [[Blunder Differential]], [[Crimped Thinamabob]], and [[Whatchcallzit Tinewheel]], all of which will be auto-consumed to update the quest.
1. Speak to one of the following NPCs to complete the quest (varies):
   - [[Knibbnack Temperbrass]] at {{waypoint -530, 175, 988}} in [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] (banker building) in [[Steamfont Mountains]]
   - [[Whiskas Shortwire]] at {{waypoint -489, -44, -267}} at The Crossroads in [[The Commonlands]]
   - [[Maryll Sparkstitch]] at {{waypoint -722, -16, 209}} outside the Qeynos Highway cabin in [[Antonica]]

## Rewards

- [[Gold Jubilation Medal]] x3
- One of the following:
  - [[Tinkered Contrabulous Backpack]]
  - [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog]] x5
