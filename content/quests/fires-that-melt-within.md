## Description

Marathan Allim wants me to seek out a heating source within the Temple of Solusek Ro. I am to heat the elements and then quickly run down and forge them into rods, as they must be forged while still at a malleable temperature. I will need to locate a forge within the temple as well! This sounds easy enough!

## Steps

1. Melt the material so that it can be forged into rods.
   1. Go to The [[Temple of Solusek Ro]] and go down to the room with the efreeti Tazgar {{waypoint -332.05, -48.61, -659.96}}. Go up the central stairs and make 3 right turns to reach the balcony. The furnace is in the middle of the balcony, at {{waypoint -335, -40, -637}}.
   1. Go to the forge quickly and form the melted bar into 5 rods.
      - Jump off the balcony to the floor of the Efrit room, take the left-hand exit (if you are looking away from the stairs) then turn immediately right and go down to the forge room and click on the forge, at {{waypoint -242, -60, -680}}.
   1. Repeat the above steps to make 5 more rods (a single combine).
1. Return to [[Marathan Allim]]
