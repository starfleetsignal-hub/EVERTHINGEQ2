## Steps

1. Obtain antiviral components located around and just east of [[Minstrel's Cradle]]:
   - Kill [[A vicious lavafang|vicious lavafangs]] to gather 8 gizzard brimstones (not a guaranteed update)
   - Gather 12 **Flame Thistles**, yellow flowers on the ground.  Possible locations are  ![](images/Antiviral_Therapy_quest_-_Flame_Thistle.jpg)
     - {{waypoint 360, -14, -410}}
     - {{waypoint 373, 24, -274}}
     - {{waypoint 393, -7, -422}}
     - {{waypoint 510, 1.98, -413}}
     - {{waypoint 544, 20, -219}}
1. Return to Oolfonso
