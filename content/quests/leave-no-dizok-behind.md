## How to get initial faction

You start with low faction with The Gwalnax Brigade (-30,000 faction, threatening). To raise your initial faction you need to harvest ground spawns called [[Dull kromslayer|dull kromslayers]], which are found around [[Karatun Hold]] in [[Kylong Plains]]. They are marked 'LORE', so only one can be carried at a time. They spawn randomly, yet very quickly, around several set locations. Sometimes it spawns clearly visible, with the entirety of the lance able to be seen, however at other times half or more of the lance is clipped underground. To add insult to injury, the shaft of the lance is the same color as the ground (light brown). Go to the following locations, zoom-in to first-person, and search the ground thoroughly. It may help to mouse slowly all over the location in addition to keeping a sharp eye out. Known locations are:

- {{waypoint 924, 185, -2109}}
- {{waypoint 998, 127, -2268}}
- {{waypoint 959, 170, -2145}}
- {{waypoint 968, 170, -2121}}
- {{waypoint 985, 149, -2216}}
- {{waypoint 1130, 144, -2072}}

Once you have found the item, turn it in to [[Warlord Gadar Gwalnax]] in the [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]], at {{waypoint 1470, 165, -2910}}. Each turn-in will yield +5000 faction with The Gwalnax Brigade. Repeat this process until you have -5000 faction and [[Battle Master Dolak]] will offer you this quest.

## Steps

1. Find the phylacteries of the Battle Master's slain troopers. The phylacteries are small flasks glowing green next to their bodies:
   - {{waypoint 812, 118, -2228}}
   - {{waypoint 810, 221, -2189}}
   - {{waypoint 1092, 122, -2236}}
   - {{waypoint 977, 137, -2344}}
   - {{waypoint 1110, 144, -2031}} which is also the location of [[Infiltrator Zatuk]]
   - {{waypoint 816, 196, -2317}}
1. Return to [[Battle Master Dolak]] in [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]]. {{waypoint 1477, 166, -2918}}

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- +5000 faction with **The Gwalnax Brigade**
- [[Radiating Gem of Striking]]
