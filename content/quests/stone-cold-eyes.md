## Note

This quest is repeatable up to Unlimited times.

## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[A watchful custodian|watchful custodians]] located in the [[Oratorium]] room, for uncomon updates.
1. Return to the chest and examine it again, putting the golem eyes in the sockets.

## Rewards

- 20x [[Swift Slicer]]
