## Steps

1. Retrieve [[Spellbook of Carthen Olaren|Carthen Olaren's spellbook]] at the top floor of the [[Tower of Bone]]. {{waypoint 623, -3, 212}}
   - 2 [[Aged tome guardian]]s spawn when you click on the spellbook.
1. Return to Unlara.

## Rewards

- 5s 67c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Forged Dagger]] (Bards, Predators, Rangers, Rogues)
  - [[Forged Knuckles]] (Brawlers, Beastlord)
  - [[Forged Mace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Forged Staff]] (All Mages)
  - [[Forged Sword]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
