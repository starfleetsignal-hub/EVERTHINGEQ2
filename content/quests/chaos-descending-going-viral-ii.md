## Notes

*A new tradeskill mission is available every 18 hours.*

## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 15 [[Celestial Candle]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest the following within [[Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands]]:
   - 25 [[Nimbic hide]] from [[Basilisk lair]]
   - 10 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Coalesced reef]]
   - 10 [[Strangler root]] from [[Strangler root break]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Auto-Injector]].
1. Craft the [[Auto-Injector]] on a [[Chemistry Table]].
   - There is a [[Chemistry Table]] in the [[Crafters' Gallery]] in the Great Library at {{waypoint 931.19, 411.68, 31.68}}
1. Speak to [[Bunjeru]] at {{waypoint 289.82, -34.89, 199.81}} in the [[Myrist Librarian Quarters]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 66g 32s 48000 status
- [[Elemental Mission Reward Crate]]
