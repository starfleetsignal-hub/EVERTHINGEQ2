## Steps

1. Defeat 12 nesting [[A Windsister jaggedtalon|Windsister jaggedtalons]] at [[Blooming Rindle]]![A Windsister jaggedtalon](images/A_Windsister_jaggedtalon.png) {{waypoint 610, 106, -233}}
1. Release the sands at the following locations
   - Release the **Sands of Clarity** on the banks of [[Blooming Rindle]] {{waypoint 660.70, 96.92, -153.55}}
   - Release the **Sands of Blessing** in the waters of [[Blooming Rindle]] {{waypoint 664.97, 111.31, -83.84}} or at {{waypoint 664, 95, -106}}
1. Return to [[Sathara]]

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Wish-Forged Rani Haar]]
  - [[Spellwoven Dendromail Gloves]]
