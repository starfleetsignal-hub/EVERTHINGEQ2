## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
- All items can be found at [[Fleshborer Beach]], [[The Overflow]], [[Flowfall Gully]] and [[Carved Caps]] in [[The Unknown]].

## Steps

![a siphonfright](images/A_siphonfright.png)
![fermentation gourd](images/Fermentation_gourd.png)
![siphonfright eggs](images/Siphonfright_eggs.png)

1. Oruri needs some local items to help unlock the psychic energy gem's potential. You get 1 kill and 1 gather task randomly asigned:
   - hunt siphonfrights for numbing saliva x 12 - flying worms around Fleshborer Beach {{waypoint -1068, 23, 512}}
   - gather sporefiend fermentation gourds x 8 - up in Carver Caps among sporefiends  {{waypoint -1060, 53, 949}}
   - hunt obliviquill for beaks x 12 - fathomlurkers around The Overflow {{waypoint -1024, -39, 773}}
   - harvest siphonfright eggs x 8 - in water on Fleshborer Beach {{waypoint -793, 14, 341}}
1. Return to Oruri.
