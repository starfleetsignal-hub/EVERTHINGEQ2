## Notes

- Takes place inside [[Sodden Archipelago: Blackhook Badlands (Solo)|Sodden Archipelago: Blackhook Badlands [Solo]]] instance and can be done at the same time as [[Answers for Baasik Questions]].

## Steps

1. Enter [[Sodden Archipelago: Blackhook Badlands (Solo)|Sodden Archipelago: Blackhook Badlands [Solo]]] via Portal at {{waypoint 116, 136, 85}}
1. get closer to [[Toman Keltor]] at around {{waypoint 192.27, 170.60, -66.18}} (When you first zone in, there is a cart in the middle of the road. When you approach it the mobs turn aggro.)
1. Defeat the 11 Mandoko attackers.
1. Speak with [[Toman Keltor]] at {{waypoint 187, 170, -70}} and receive [[Port Woe Laborer Disguise]]
1. To proceed past the bone barrier you must beat [[Antalflik]] named Ant boss at {{waypoint 300, 148, -260}}
1. Use the [[Port Woe Laborer Disguise]] and progress past the bone barrier - quest update at {{waypoint 77.91, 192.39, -326.60}}
1. Beat [[Ultarana]] (equip anti stun rune on your belt) {{waypoint 110.57, 192.11, -404.23}}
1. You are spotted and ambushed around {{waypoint 24, 187, -450}}. "Further traverse Blackhook Territory keeping an eye out for Adris"
1. Work your way back around to defeat [[Kahblamo]] at {{waypoint -81, 193, -597}}  he has strong knockback, scaffolding nearby can be used to hide under. Keep travelling further in to find Adris at {{waypoint 41, 187, -566}}
1. Find a [[Blackhook Shackle Key]] by killing [[Grelzmok the Destroyer]] at {{waypoint 320, 202, -446}} to free Adris.
1. Go back and speak to Adris at {{waypoint 41, 187, -566}} He runs off. "Catch up with Adris beyond the gates to Blackhook Bastion"
1. Kill [[Bleakblood the Corpse Blaster]] at  {{waypoint 180, 282, -767}}
1. get to Adris Spiritshine beside the zone exit portal at {{waypoint 223, 285, -788}}
1. Leave the zone to update quest.
1. Speak to Adris at {{waypoint 290, 284, -733}}
   - *Note: This opens up side quests from [[Skortch]] and [[Shaahida]] next to Adris.*
