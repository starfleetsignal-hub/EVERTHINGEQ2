## Steps

1. Catch 6 Nuknok fish in the stream around {{waypoint 886, 343, 326}} (Note: These fish are very hard to see unless you are in the water and are not targetable. You have to either look underwater or hover your cursor until you get a hand icon.) It helps to get off your mount and use first person view. Note that you need to move from group to group of fish, catching only one in each group.
1. Return the fish to [[Ranger Zim'za'ai]]
