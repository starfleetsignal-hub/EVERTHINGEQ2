1. Fly to [[Blackwind Isle]].
   - Collect 5 Globules of Goo from [[A mudstone ooze|Mudstone Oozes]] around {{waypoint -343, 119, 430}}.
   - Collect 3 crushed crystal shards from [[A crystalline burrower|crystalline burrowers]] aound {{waypoint -448, 123, 370}}.
