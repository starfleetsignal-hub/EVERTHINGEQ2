## Steps

1. Enter [[The East Natatorium]] in the [[Trade Court]] at {{waypoint -683, -16, 223}} to spawn and kill a group of four [[An undead pilgrim|undead pilgrim]]s and [[An undead shepherd]]. These are the small pools of water on both sides of the Trade Court. Approaching them spawns the shepherd and pilgrims.
1. Enter [[The West Natatorium]] at {{waypoint -586, -16, 229}} and kill the mobs that spawn from there.
1. Kill [[A channel prowler|channel prowler]]s for a common update. They are found back near the Steward. {{waypoint -354, 12, 334}}
1. Speak to the Steward of Fyr'Un.

## Rewards

- At least 12g 57s 51c
