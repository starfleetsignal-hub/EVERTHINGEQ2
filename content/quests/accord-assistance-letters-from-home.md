## Steps

1. Check the mailboxes near Tupta, Grobb, and Dropship Landing for letters and packages to deliver
   - Collect letters from Tupta {{waypoint -133, -43, 768}}
   - Collect letters from Grobb {{waypoint -288, -78, -874}}
   - Collect letters from Dropship Landing Zone {{waypoint 1790, 452, 799}}
1. Leave the supplies in the basket next to Hophip

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2000 faction with **Survival Accord**
