## Notes

1. You receive [[Gwinres' Ensorcelled Stone]] when you start the quest

## Steps

1. Reach the draconic obelisk at the Western Mouth of the [[Flooded Scar]] found here — {{waypoint 537.54, 8.07, 142.13}}
1. Use [[Gwinres' Ensorcelled Stone]] near
   - the Irral draconic obelisk at {{waypoint 541.43, 8.07, 151.34}}
   - the Ternock draconic obelisk at {{waypoint 817.03, 6.35, 238.12}}
   - the Tilabil draconic obelisk at {{waypoint 592.26, 6.33, -161.31}}
1. Defeat the [[A draconic defender|woken draconic defender]]
1. Return to [[Gwinres Boulderkind]] at {{waypoint 60.53, 8.93, 599.78}}
