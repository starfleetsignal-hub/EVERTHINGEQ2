This quest is repeatable.
![The Queen giving her speech](images/Queen_Antonia_Bayle_%28Heroes'_Festival%29.jpg)

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Security Guard Bobbins]] {{waypoint 412, -12, -15}}
1. Speak to [[Security Guard Sheshi]] {{waypoint 412, -12, -24}}
1. Speak to [[Security Foreman Kyerah]] {{waypoint 437, -12, -15}}
1. Find the assassin.
   1. Hail [[An Anxious Reveler (Qeynos)|an Anxious Reveler]] that spawns on the drawbridge {{waypoint 419, -12, -18}}.
   1. The reveler will attack you, kill it.
1. Return to Security Foreman Kyerah.
1. Queen Antonia Bayle will spawn and go through a speech, at which point the quest will complete.

## Rewards

One of the following:

- [[Firework: Confetti Shower]]
- [[Firework: Prismatic Burst]]
- [[Firework: Rainbow Ring]]

Completing the quest for the fifth time offers an additional reward:

- [[Royal Scroll of Assistance]]
