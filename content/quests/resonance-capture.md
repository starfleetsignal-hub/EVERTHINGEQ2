## Steps

1. Use the [[Resonance Siphon]] given to you by Geldax on 5 [[A warped wispling|warped wisplings]] {{waypoint -142.09, -41.66, 267.72}}. (Un-equip any items that proc and turn off ranged attacks, wands will put you in combat!)
1. Return to Geldax.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
- [[Lucent Gem of Prowess]]
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Fighter's Ring]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Ring]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Ring]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Ring]] (All Mages)
