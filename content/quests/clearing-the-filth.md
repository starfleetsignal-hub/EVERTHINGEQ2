## Steps

1. Clear out a number of beasts that have profaned [[Veksar: The Sunken Theater]]
   - Kill 8 yah-lei
   - Kill 8 goblins
   - Kill 2 golems
   - Kill 6 undead
   - Return to Lieutenant Kresic near Omen's Call

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
