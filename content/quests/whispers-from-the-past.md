## Steps

1. Speak to [[Fyr'remd Lorak]]{{waypoint -534, 377, 638}} in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]] near the Quel'ule Station.
1. Destroy 12 Void creatures and collect their marks (auto update). For example kill the Voidbeasts around {{waypoint -423, 459, 335}}
1. Return to Fyr'remd Lorak.
1. Venture into the [[Library of Erudin]] and obtain the tome from the guilty Primarch (the final encounter of the zone).
   - Note You must do the murder mystery in order to get the update. You must answer the questions correctly. See [[The Mystery of the Slain Scholar]].
1. Return to Fyr'remd to tell him of your progress.
1. Obtain 8 samples of decrepit blood from undead in [[Befallen: Halls of the Forsaken]].
1. Enter [[The Temple of Anashti Sul's]] at {{waypoint -943.94, -229.02, -1040.76}} From [[Sinking Sands]], and summon her by clicking on the raised bath at {{waypoint 0, -13, -99}}.
   - Note: The cave to the backdoor entrance of [[Silent City]] is at {{waypoint -876, -235, -1261}}. This is also the entrance for [[The Temple of Anashti Sul's]].  Being in a group may prevent this option from showing up, and since only the person who summons her will get an update, everyone on this step should drop group and go in solo to get their updates.
1. Speak with [[Anashti Sul]] to learn more about [[Roehn Theer]].
1. Return to [[Fyr'remd Lorak]] and inform him of what you have discovered.
