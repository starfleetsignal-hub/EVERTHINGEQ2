## Requisite

Must be Aerakyn: part 3 of the 3-part Aerakyn racial questline (evil version)

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Llaros]] inside the [[Freeport Block & Tackle]] in [[South Freeport (POI)|South Freeport]] {{waypoint -217, -56, 146}}. The dialogue choices allow you to treat him as the assassin or innocent:
   - If Llaros is the assassin, he will attack you. Solo, scales to your level.
   - If Llaros is innocent, he will vanish
1. Speak to Plex in West Freeport

## Rewards

- At least 10g 97c @ lvl 55
- [[Ring of the Dragon (The Left Hand)|Ring of the Dragon]]
