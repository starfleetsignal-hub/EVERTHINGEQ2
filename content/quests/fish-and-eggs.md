1. Extract the contents of 8 [[Vile strider egg]] around [[Shadowed Cleft]] using the gnomish Eggstractor.
   - *The nests are spaced around the base of the cliffs.*
   - *Eggs are glowing they have already been extracted and you will have to wait for a reset or move to a different one.*
   - Known Locations
     - {{waypoint -1249, -1009, 148}}
     - {{waypoint -1142, -999, 216}}
     - {{waypoint -1335, -1012, 237}}
     - {{waypoint -1098, -1000, 281}}
     - {{waypoint -1208, -1008, 384}}
     - {{waypoint -1122, -1004, 439}}
     - {{waypoint -1114, -1003, 466}}
     - {{waypoint -1069, -1006, 412}}
1. Return to [[Klip Marrsquire]]
