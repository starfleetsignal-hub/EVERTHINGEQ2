## Steps

1. Travel to [[Dreg's Landing]] in [[Kylong Plains]] and speak with [[Yersenia Sistephinos]] to receive a [[Decaying Mist Receptacle]]. {{waypoint 860, 12, 196}}
1. Place the barrel next to a fallen stump south of [[Karnor's Castle]]. {{waypoint 512, 13, -110}}
1. Return to Yersenia Sistephinos and keep speaking with her until you receive [[A crate of decaying mist receptacles]].
1. Speak with [[Kefitzat Haderech]], standing next to Yersenia Sistephinos, to be teleported outside [[Varis]].
   - Make sure your whole group is standing very close to Kefitzat Haderech, practically on top of him, when you wish to be teleported. The teleportation mechanic has a very short collection range.
1. Speak to [[Kaizik Tyvkl]] once you arrive, at {{waypoint -244, 79, -543}}. Defeat the [[A drolvarg loot hoarder|drolvarg loot hoarder]] and [[A drolvarg ambusher|drolvarg ambushers]] that spawn.
1. Speak to Kaizik Tyvkl again.
1. Return to Hrath Everstill.

## Rewards

- +7500 faction with **Bertoxxulous**
- [[Hrath's Altar of Bertoxxulous]]
