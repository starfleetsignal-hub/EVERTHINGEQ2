## Note

Ilaen Lilac will only speak to wood elves, bridging the [[Qeynos Racial Timeline#Wood Elf series|Wood Elf racial]] and [[Qeynos Districts Timeline|Qeynos Districts]] timelines.

## Steps

1. Inspect dead deer until you find [[A crude arrow]].
1. Return to Ilaen Lilac.
1. Speak with [[Acting Lieutenant Germain]] near the district gates.

## Rewards

- At least 92c
