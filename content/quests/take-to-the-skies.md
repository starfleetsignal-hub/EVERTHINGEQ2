## Notes

- You cannot get this quest until you have completed Step 6 of [[Renewal of Ro: Illusion From the Disillusioned]].
- You need to finish this quest for the "a gem of deific fervor" to finish the quest [[Renewal of Ro: Illusion From the Disillusioned]]

## Steps

1. Hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 5 [[A Tsmang Blightswarm drone|Tsmang Blightswarm drones]] for **Runic-Scarred Chitin** in [[Tsmang Blightswarm Hive]]. The hive entrance is at {{waypoint 176.65, 78.31, -691.51}} ![Shadowshard quartz](images/Shadowshard_quartz.png)
     - * Chitin is not a guaranteed update.*
   - Gather 6 **shadowshard quartz** in the [[Tsmang Blightswarm Hive]].
1. Return to [[Ulgaf]]. Receive [[Ulgaf's Spell Powder Filled Arca]].
1. Destroy **Swordfury Casting Eyes**:![Swordfury Casting Eye](images/Swordfury_Casting_Eye.png)
   - At a high spot in Swordfury village {{waypoint -219.52, 97.35, -508.14}}
   - At [[Eye's Sacrifice]] {{waypoint -321.33, 10.71, -112.81}}
   - In the north of [[Saltscorch Steppes]] {{waypoint 230.23, 95.25, -597.99}}
   - At Godsfall {{waypoint 614, 113, -685}}
1. Return to [[Ulgaf]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- The ability to fly in Sandstone Delta (after checking in with Selaera)
- [[Swordfury Casting Eye]]
