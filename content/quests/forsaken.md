## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Lesser Faydark]] and speak with [[Zha Gu'Lau (Lesser Faydark)|Zha Gu'Lau]] west of the entrance to the [[Greater Faydark]]. {{waypoint -407, -34, 51}}
1. Slay the brownie patrols west of [[Tunare's Grove]]:
   - Western patrol, around {{waypoint -400, -40, -120}}
   - Southern patrol, around {{waypoint -428, -34, -5}}
   - Eastern patrol, around {{waypoint -540, -45, 5}}
1. Return to Zha Gu'Lau.
1. Kill treespirits southeast of Zha Gu'Lau. {{waypoint -535, -40, 340}}
1. Kill [[A brownie sage|brownie sages]] near [[The Village of Panderpana]]. {{waypoint -700, -50, -150}}
1. Return to Zha Gu'Lau.

## Rewards

- +5000 faction with **Cazic-Thule**
- Unlocks [[Servant of Fear]] and [[Amygdalan Assault]]
- Pet: [[Servant of Thule]]
