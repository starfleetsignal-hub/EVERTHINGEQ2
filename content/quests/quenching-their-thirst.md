## Steps

1. Visit three bodies of water to get the quest updates:
   - for the Jug of Sea Water enter the water at the docks {{waypoint -1010, -148, -730}}
   - for the Jug of River Water enter the river west of [[The Crossroads]] {{waypoint 0, -52, -296}}
   - for the Jug of Lake Water enter the water at [[Pride Lake]] {{waypoint 390, 0, -490}}
1. Return to Madam Vi for the quest reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 67c
