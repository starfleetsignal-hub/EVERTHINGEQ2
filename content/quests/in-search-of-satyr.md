## Steps

1. Clear the area around the broken stele in the [[Sanctuary of Mourning]]
   - Clear 3 debris from around the area (click on the pieces of broken masonry)
   - Clear 3 brush from around the area (click on the purple bushes growing on the main stele)
   - Clear 4 [[A stelae corrupter|stelae corrupters]] (91v paladins) from the area.  They appear as NPCs but aggro when you get near.
1. Return to Brint

- At least 1p 25g
- One of the Following, based on Class:

  - [[Withering Treant Gauntlets]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Corrupting Cloth Gloves]] (All Mages)
  - [[Withered Chainlink Gloves]] (Shamans)
  - [[Mist Panther Skin Gloves]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Withering Combine Armor Gauntlets]] (Clerics)
  - [[Corrupted Soul Chainlink Gloves]] (All Scouts)
