## Prerequisites

- You must have at least +17,800 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Obtain 8 venom sacs by killing [[A bonesplint spitter|bonesplint spitter]]s in the [[Field of Bone]]. Be careful not to step on them, which may kill them without giving you an update. {{waypoint -1954, -365, -1691}}
1. Go back to [[The Sheet]] and deposit the sacs in the cask. {{waypoint -2666, -412, -2694}}
1. Speak with [[Bragek Joll]]. {{waypoint -2656, -412, -2702}}

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- +1600 faction with **The Order of Rime**
