## Steps

1. Search 3 of the crates at Hate's Covenant and return to Jackal.
   - Speak with a gnemlin slave. {{waypoint -1195.82, -35.43, -512.97}}
   - Speak with a dhalgar slave. {{waypoint -1095.41, -11.99, -480.42}}
   - Speak with Thomis Glumgully. {{waypoint -1042.67, -12.87, -495.10}}
1. Find Famke Tidylocket, the missing gnemlin. {{waypoint -1076.91, -31.95, -263.57}}. Examine her body.
1. Return to Thomas Glumgully.
   - Gather 12 gloriosa fellenwort in Stingerpuff Flats (Ants/wasps) Small white clicky flowers.
   - Obtain 7 deepnest sentry poison glands (kick ant hills) in Stingerpuff Flats.
   - Get 8 glowing kelp bulbs hunting Mizzdyrr fishmen at the beaches. /way -1196.85 -42.57 -460.02
1. Return to Thomis Glumgully.
1. Speak with Uldurm Gemsight {{waypoint -1105.74, -11.48, -490.63}}
   - Gather Verdant Umbrite next to him. Return to Thomis Glumgully.
1. Erect wasp repellent wards along Stingerpuff Pass
   - {{waypoint -894.86, -35.04, -202.39}}
   - {{waypoint -866.06, -32.14, -198.03}}
   - {{waypoint -826.20, -24.28, -185.54}}
   - {{waypoint -796.85, -17.57, -183.00}}
   - {{waypoint -766.31, -15.90, -175.75}}
1. Place last ward on NE end of Stingerpuff Pass {{waypoint -853.21, -33.04, -236.59}}
1. Kill the frenzied mudnest stinger!
   - Cull 12 great mudnest stinger swarm.
   - Destroy mudnest stinger wasp nest {{waypoint -812.83, -22.80, -296.69}}
1. Return to Thomis Glumgully.
1. Reach the work camp near Pellucid Mere {{waypoint -553.39, -0.48, -205.17}}
1. Kill the Thexian Dread Sentinel at the Pellucid Mere work camp.
   - Kill 5 more Thexians.
1. Speak with Baeddan {{waypoint -568.46, -0.48, -186.11}}
