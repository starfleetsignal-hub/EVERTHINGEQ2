You must be a Good-aligned Froglok of at least level 20 to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Gain Access to The Willow Wood - speak to [[Hargrove Leafsway]]
1. Enter [[The Willow Wood: Besieged Hamlet]] and speak to [[An injured defender]]
1. Slay gnoll invaders ([[A Rockpaw destroyer]] and [[A Rockpaw scavenger]] update)
1. Head to the docks and speak to [[Elise Windswood]]
1. Head to the gate that used to lead to the Forest Ruins and kill [[Marblefist (Willow Wood)|Marblefist]] and [[Granitepaw]]
1. Head to the gate to Qeynos Povince District and speak to [[Cristyn Eregrove]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g @ lvl 95
