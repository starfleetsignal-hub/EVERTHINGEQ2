## Steps

Though surrounded by lightning striking chaotically across the silvery realm of Aether Wroughtlands, I will prevail against the Zakir Rish!

1. Enter [[Aether Wroughtlands: Chaotic Currents (Solo)]] in the [[Adja-Beryl Berms]] at {{waypoint -360.62, 139.04, 438.33}}
1. Stagger the Staggering - [[Satashi the Staggering]]
1. Vanquish the Volatile - [[Vashtu the Volatile]]
1. Execute [[Etosh the Electrifying]]
   - Follow the walkthrough for [[Aether Wroughtlands: Chaotic Currents (Solo)]] for strats.
