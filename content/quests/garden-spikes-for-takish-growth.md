Zaari, a member of the Maetuh Saqr, the protectors of Takish'Hiz, formed some druidic harmony stones to help temper the new flora in Takish Badlands, encouraging it to send its roots deep, allowing it to grow healthy and strong in this new environment.

**Zaari has tasked me with planting the druidic harmony stones in particular spots around Takish Badlands.**

## Steps

1. Plant druidic harmony stones around the [[Takish Badlands]]. ![Druidic harmony stone location](images/Druidic_harmony_stone_location.png)
   - Place druidic harmony stone at [[Ruins of Takish'Hiz]] {{waypoint 450.24, 106.29, -596.20}}
   - Place druidic harmony stone at [[Renewal Pond]] {{waypoint 161.59, 93.26, -401.47}}
   - Place druidic harmony stone at [[Whispering Pillars]] {{waypoint -89.77, 117.01, -429.53}}
   - Place druidic harmony stone at [[Tera'Noth Peninsula]] {{waypoint -92.95, 115.57, -49.61}}
   - Place druidic harmony stone at [[Firiona's Motte]] {{waypoint 633.69, 65.97, -209.20}}
1. Return to [[Zaari]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
