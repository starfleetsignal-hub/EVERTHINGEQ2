## Steps

1. I need to gather glisten frill roots which grow near the Tourbillion, and slay Wayward thaumaturges for the arcane leather.
   - Slay [[A Wayward thaumaturge|Wayward thaumaturges]] {{waypoint -358, -1, -242}} for arcane leather (6)
   - Gather glisten frill roots from the bushes {{waypoint -786, -1, 4}} around the Tourbillion (6)

![Bush that yields glisten frill roots](images/Glisten_frill_bush.jpg)

1. Anton gave me the docile attal that is required by the spell.
   - Examine Anton's [[Docile Attal]] in your inventory.
1. I need to use the Submissive Bridle Elixir on the tuataras around Wayward Plains, and then escort them back to Anton the Mad in Cardin Ward.
   1. Cast [[Submissive Bridle Elixir]] on two tuataras and return to Anton the Mad
   1. Speak with Anton the Mad

## Rewards

- [[Bridled Tuatara Windgazer]]
- At least 1p 30g
