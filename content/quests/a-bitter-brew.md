## Steps

1. Harvest [[Bush Nodes#T3|polluted natural gardens]] until you receive 30 updates.
1. Return to Kitty.
1. Harvest polluted natural garden nodes until you have 3 fayberries, 2 steppes mountain beans, and 2 oolong tea leaves.
1. Return to Kitty.
1. Visit [[Port Naythex]] {{waypoint 673, 1, -274}} to check on the status of [[The Maid for the Mist]] ship repairs.
1. Return to Kitty.
1. Kill [[A Sullian fairy|Sullian fairies]] {{waypoint -997, 141, -2131}} until you obtain 10 fairy dusts (common update). These are found around [[Bone Lake]] and along the river just south of it.
1. Return to [[Kitty Valencia]].

## Rewards

- At least 1g 12s 33c
