## Steps

Craft 12 items from the random list below:

- [[Briarwood Wand (Level 30) (Crafted)|Briarwood Wand]]
- [[Briarwood Longbow|Briarwood Long Bow]]
- [[Briarwood Fighting Baton]]
- [[Spirit Totem of the Bat (Crafted)|Spirit Totem of the Bat]]
- [[Briarwood Sorcerer's Staff (Level 30) (Crafted)|Briarwood Sorcerer's Staff]]
- [[Feyiron throwing axe (Crafted)|Feyiron Throwing Axe]]
- [[Feyiron throwing dagger (Crafted)|Feyiron Throwing Dagger]]
- [[Feyiron throwing hammer (Crafted)|Feyiron Throwing Hammer]]
- [[Briarwood Bo Staff]]
- [[Spirit Totem of the Wolf (Crafted)|Spirit Totem Of The Wolf]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

## Rewards

- At Least 36s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
