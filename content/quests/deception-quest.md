## Prerequisite

- You must speak [[Orcish]] to advance in this quest.

## Steps

1. Find the other four drakota teleportation rings:
   - Go to the [[Vault of the Fallen: Lair of Trenda'Loz]] within [[Fallen Gate]] and slay [[Trenda'loz the Caller]]. {{waypoint 33, -26, -193}}
   - Go to [[The Sanctum of Fear: Niscanith's Lair]] within [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]] and slay [[Niscanith]]. {{waypoint -16.25, -10.00, -173.24}}
   - Go to [[Firemyst Gully: Vanudozalon's Lair]] in [[Antonica]] and slay [[Vanudozalon the Elusive]]. {{waypoint -1200, -12, 709}}
   - Go to [[Bloodskull Valley: Kaljanar's Lair]] in [[The Commonlands]] {{waypoint 0, -48, 726}}. You'll notice that Kaljanar has already been beaten, so speak with the dying Deathfist orc at {{waypoint 89.91, -47.61, 842.18}}. He informs you the orcs have already taken into possession the final ring hand and are keeping it in their Citadel. Head to [[The Deathfist Citadel|Deathfist Citadel]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]] and enter the instance [[Deathfist Citadel: Assault]] through the sewer gate {{waypoint 43, -42, -782}}. Make your way to the top of the citadel and kill [[Regent Grozmag]].
1. Now with all five rings in your possession, you need to enter the instance [[Darathar's Flight]]. Go to [[The Tower of the Oracles]] in [[Antonica]], right-click and select use on the 'Ulteranis', the large orb at the top of the tower. {{waypoint -1033, 14, -629}}|
   - In order to enter this zone, you must have someone in the raid who is on this step of the quest.
1. Make your way through the instance lay waste to the treacherous [[Darathar]].
1. Return to [[Lord Nagafen (Nagafen's Lair)|Lord Nagafen]].
