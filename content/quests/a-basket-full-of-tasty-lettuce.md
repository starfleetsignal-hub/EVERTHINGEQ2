## Notes

- The first turn-in of this quest enters the [[Quest Journal]] as a unique quest. However, the quest starter item can be picked up more than once, and each subsequent turn-in enters the the quest journal as an unlimited repeatable quest bearing the same name as the original.

## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A torpid bloodbloom|torpid bloodbloom]]s.
1. Return to [[Grumglub]].

## Rewards

- At least 9g 15s 55c
- +2000 faction with **The Lost Children of Marr**
- Subsequent deliveries of this quest will reward +500 faction.
