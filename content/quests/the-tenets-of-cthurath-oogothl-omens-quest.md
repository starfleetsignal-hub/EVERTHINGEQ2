## How to start

Completing [[Rage of Cthurath: Wrest of the Unknown]] (main signature adventure questline for this expansion) flags the whole account to start getting these dropped quest starters.

Quest starter is found in treasure chest dropped by [[A nullfang subjugator]] in [[Oogothl Sprawl]] {{waypoint 115, 4, 357}}

## Notes

- This is a POI quest to find other secluded quest giving locations.
  - *(Some locations require flight)*
- Players are tasked with finding the five locations within [[Oogothl Sprawl]]. At each location there is a hidden 'Xebrith Cognizance' which will reveal itself once the player is close enough. Clicking on them will offer 'Kill X number of Y mob' type quests that can be completed separately from this quest.
- Players do not have to interact with these Cognizances, merely to discover them, to get credit for *this* quest. (But they will still need to interact with them if they want the individual quests given by them)
- All 5 of the locations are also part of the larger list of locations needed for Oogothl Sprawl exploration achievement [[Exploration: Oogothl Sprawl]].

## Steps

![Xebrith Cognizance location indicator](images/Xebrith_Cognizance_location_indicator.png)

1. Explore/Discover these 5 locations within [[Oogothl Sprawl]]  (in any order)
   - Reach [[Necrogaze Spire]] {{waypoint 583, 269, -674}} to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Inconsequentiality]]
   - Reach [[Watcheye Watch]] {{waypoint 66, 213, -251}} to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Dread]]
   - Reach [[Nullfang Spear]] {{waypoint -291, 131, 463}} to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Unexplained]]
   - Reach [[Sage's Ire]] {{waypoint -184, 186, -451}} to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Failed Understanding]]
   - Reach [[Scalewrought Blink]] {{waypoint -94, 240, 63}} to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Arcane Knowledge]]
