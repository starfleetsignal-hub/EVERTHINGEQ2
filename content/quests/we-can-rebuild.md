I met [[Tinker Thermald]], a celebrating gnome in [[Steamfont Mountains]] who offered me a drink of his special cask-strength gnomish spirits. In the spirit of Tinkerfest, I accepted!

## Steps

1. Click the barrel of **Cask-Strength Gnomish Spirits** next to [[Tinker Thermald]] in order to zone into [[A Dream of Clockwork]]. He is inside a building that is behind the Tinkerfest merchants, and beside a large clockwork spider and a Mender / Forge.
1. Speak to **a dream figment** {{waypoint -249.75, -16.47, 170.45}}, who asks for your help in repairing a valuable clockwork. Unscrupulous adventurers have split up the pieces, but probably are not too far away. Recover the pieces!
   - An Adventurous Fighter {{waypoint -126.74, -22.55, 88.67}} ([[King's Cog]])
   - An Adventurous Priest {{waypoint -182.49, -36.21, 5.15}} ([[King's Capacitor]])
   - An Adventurous Coercer {{waypoint -9.92, -1.74, 8.52}} ([[King's Speech]])
   - An Adventurous Bard {{waypoint 28.14, -12.05, 114.35}} ([[King's Resistor]])
   - An Adventurous Scout {{waypoint -55.20, -12.96, 205.14}} ([[King's Gear]])
   - An Adventurous Wizard {{waypoint -124.06, -22.20, 167.05}} ([[King's Pin]])
1. Return the parts to the dream figment gnome, then wait while he reassembles the fragments.
1. Speak to the **possessed clockwork** to return to [[Steamfont Mountains]].
1. After you awaken in a bed, speak to the **concerned gnome** to receive your reward {{waypoint -611.40, 175.46, 993.88}}.

## Reward

- [[Cask Strength Gnomish Spirits]]
- 5 [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog]]s
- [[Rusty Mechanized Bow]]
