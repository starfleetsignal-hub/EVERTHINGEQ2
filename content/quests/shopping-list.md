## Steps

1. Click on bucket, stool, table, bowl on table and charm hanging from ceiling in Hyggin's hut
1. Speak to Hyggin. Accept the recipe book he offers, [[Traditional Pygmy Furnishings]]; the five recipes autoscribe.
1. Craft one each of the items at the [[Woodworking Table]] near the dock {{waypoint -608, 6, 257}}.<br>Resources required: 7 [[Larix lumber]], 5 [[Iodocus cluster]]s, 3 [[Leonid root]]s, 5 [[Effulgent sandpaper]]
   - [[Pygmy Bucket]]
   - [[Deinodon Bone Table]]
   - [[Pygmy Charm]]
   - [[Deinodon Bone Stool]]
   - [[Woven Reed Bowl]]
1. Speak to Hyggin

- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]]
