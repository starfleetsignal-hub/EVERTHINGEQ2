## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[Skittering monitor|skittering monitors]] around Trythec Loft.
1. Return to Arozik.

  - [[Evoker's Hat]] (All Mages)
  - [[Marshall's Barbute (red)|Marshall's Barbute]] (Warriors, Crusaders)
  - [[Pathfinder's Cowl]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Spiritualist's Helm]] (Shamans)
  - [[Vicar's Barbute]] (Clerics)
  - [[Windcaller's Helm]] (Druids, Channelers)
