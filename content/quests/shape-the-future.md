## Notes

- requires 0 [[House Yrzu (Faction)]]

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Alishinu]] {{waypoint 202.14, 593.79, -509.99}} slightly north of [[Taliseda]].
1. Inspect the nearby ***unsculpted stone*** {{waypoint 232.96, 593.84, -500.11}}.![an unsculpted stone](images/An_unsculpted_stone.jpg)
1. Inspect the nearby ***sculpted stone*** {{waypoint 252.88, 593.58, -536.71}}.![a sculpted stone](images/A_sculpted_stone.jpg)
1. Speak to [[Hrensia]] {{waypoint 228.43, 593.91, -492.31}} near the *unsculpted stone*.
1. Return to [[Alishinu|Alishinu's]] original location {{waypoint 202.14, 593.79, -509.99}} marked by ***blue sparkles*** to discover that she is gone.
1. Ask [[Salindis]] {{waypoint 184.73, 588.55, -571.45}}, who stands east of the hole in the [[Khali'Yrzu]] platform, if she knows where [[Alishinu]] went.
1. Find [[Alishinu]] {{waypoint -30.78, 68.43, 124.76}} at the western bank of the northern [[Myhst River]], where she is being attacked by a group of 2 [[Hunting stelecat|hunting stelecats]]. Kill both and then speak to her.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- +1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
