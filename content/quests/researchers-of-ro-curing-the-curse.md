*Bairam says that many who explore Buried Takish&#x27;Hiz either do not return, or return mad, and this cuts into our potential customer base. I need to figure out some item that we can sell that will either protect or cure them.*

## Materials Required

- Either harvest or otherwise acquire:
  - 6 [[Goat Meat]]
  - 30 [[Apatite Gem]]
  - 30 [[Brittlebush Taproot]]
- Bring along this fuel (or use a summon fuel merchant item):
  - 30 [[Astral Candle]]

## Steps

1. Go to [[Greater Faydark]] and speak to [[Miralith Flitterwing]] at {{waypoint 587.30, 45.30, 65.81}} (Fast Travel or Mariner's Bell to Kelethin)
1. Speak to desert herbalist [[Rasime Najafi]] at {{waypoint 149, -60, 586}} in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]]
1. Harvest the following from shrubs and schools of fish:
   - 25 [[Takish Fig]]
   - 25 [[Cactus Fruit]]
   - 25 [[Bonytail Chub]]
1. Return to Rasime, who gives you the auto-scribing recipe [[Recipe Scroll: Desert Curse Prevention Potion]].
1. Create 6 [[Desert Curse Prevention Potion|Desert Curse Prevention Potions]] on a Chemistry Table. You can use the chemistry table you created for Bairam earlier at {{waypoint 145, -60, 581}}. For each combine you will need:
   - 1 [[Goat Meat]] (6 total)
   - 5 [[Apatite Gem]] (30 total)
   - 5 [[Brittlebush Taproot]] (30 total)
   - 5 [[Astral Candle]] (30 total)
1. Speak to Bairam.
1. Examine the crate of trade goods right next to Bairam at {{waypoint 152, -60, 583}}.
1. Find the Far Seas Messenger - [[Rela Lesan]] at {{waypoint 209.54, -60.25, 495.53}}.
1. Return and speak to Bairam to complete the quest.
