## Steps

![Seamist Lillies!(Nymph Leaves)(images/Nymph_Leaves.png)!(Bog Reed)(images/Bog_Reed.png)](images/Seamist_Lillies.png)

1. Travel to [[Enchanted Lands]] and gather
   1. 3 seamist lilies  {{waypoint 247.40, 25.29, -574.80}} (North of the Seamist Plains)
   1. 3 nymph leaves {{waypoint -603.21, 21.80, -776.77}} (Misty Grove)
   1. 1 bog reed (looks like a bundle of reeds vs single)
      - Possible Locations: {{waypoint 38.34, 16.84, -812.09}}; {{waypoint 23, 12, -912}} ; {{waypoint -67.58, 15.54, -887.70}} (Bog Mountain around the pond where The Sludge Creep is)
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].
