## Description

"Whittier is tired of being attacked by wasps whenever he ventures into the Faydark below Kelethin. He would like to take a walk without worrying about getting a bee in his bonnet and asked me to clean it up a bit for him." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay 10 wasps below [[Kelethin]] anywhere around granite hills acorn lift, between {{waypoint -6, 70, 266}} and {{waypoint 1, 55, 360}}.
   - Seems any wasp will work ([[Wasp|a wasp]], [[Furious wasp|a furious wasp]], etc).
   - Killing ground mobs seems to help to spawn the wasps.
   - Many of the wasps are flying fairly high -- too high for tab-targeting. Keep looking up and pull with a ranged attack.
1. Return to [[Whittier]]

## Rewards

- At least 1s
- [[Ring of the Wasp Slayer (Version 2)|Ring of the Wasp Slayer]]
