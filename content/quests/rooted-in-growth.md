## Steps

1. Fly to the 5 statues in the ocean area {{waypoint 600, -5, 950}} and bless them, (try it while hovering).
   - marked by a blue dot on map.![View of all 5 statues from the shore, after blessing](images/Rooted_in_growth.jpg)
1. Return to [[Wegadas]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- Unlocks the Ethernere druid ring for your use.
- [[Wegadas's Blessed Wand]] nice appearance-only wand (spelled Wegadas's in game!)
- Class specific choice of:
  - Scout/Tank
    - [[Shards of the Harrowing Sirocco]]
    - [[Aetherbow]] or
    - [[Wind of the Emerald Flame]] (132 STR/AGI bows)
    - [[Heart of the Emerald Flame]]
  - Mage/Priest
    - [[Wand of Mourning (QuestReward) (Level 90)|Wand of Mourning]]
    - [[Wand of Sorrow's End]]
