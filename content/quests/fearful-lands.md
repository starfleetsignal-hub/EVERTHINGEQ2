## Steps

1. I should seek out the Gehein at the Tower of Umber somewhere north and east of Zeeil.
   - Speak to a Gehein at the next Tower of Drinal
     - [[Giirn]] {{waypoint -1071, 3, 492}}
1. Giirn, a Gehein on the edge of madness, has asked for my help in restoring the Tower of Umber to its proper function.
   1. Slay the invaders ([[A dreadeye watcher|dreadeye watchers]] and [[A gibbering fearling|gibbering fearlings]]) within the Tower of Umber (10)
   1. Speak to Giirn near the Tower of Umber
1. I should return to Zeeil at Ashen Tower and let him know about the turning of his brethren.
   - Speak to [[Zeeil]] {{waypoint -331, -7, 839}} at Ashen Tower

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
