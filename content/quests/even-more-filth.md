## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable up to 10 times..*
- This quest can only be picked up again one hour in real time after completing the previous one; the one hour timer only starts when the previous quest is completed, not when it was acquired.

## Steps

1. You will receive one of the following kill tasks:
   - 4 [[An oculus illusionist|oculus illusionist]]s
   - 4 [[An oculus wizard|oculus wizard]]s
   - 6 [[A Runnyeye brewer|Runnyeye brewers]]
   - 10 [[A Gorehorn pitmaster|Gorehorn pitmaster]]s (only the 3 up-arrow pitmasters will update)
   - 10 [[A Runnyeye miner|Runnyeye miner]]s
   - 10 [[A scrap dasher|scrap dasher]]s

## Rewards

- At least 58s 83c
- 94s 73c (at level 36)
