This is part 2 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Freeport]] who wear Leather and Chain Armor ([[Shaman|shaman]]).

## Steps

1. Collect items from creatures in [[The Commonlands]], all near [[Fallen Gate]]
   - Collect 5 shields from the [[A Thexian dragoon (Commonlands)|Thexian dragoons]] at their mining dig
   - Collect 9 mining tools from the [[A Thexian excavator (Commonlands)|Thexian excavators]]
   - Get a Dervish Cutthroat dagger from [[A dervish cutthroat (Commonlands)|a dervish cutthroat]]
1. Return to Vassi.
