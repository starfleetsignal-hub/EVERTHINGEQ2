*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Slay domesticated creatures, found around {{waypoint -100, 0, 400}}:
   - 10 [[A domesticated dreg|domesticated dreg]]s
   - 10 [[A domesticated fiend|domesticated fiend]]s
   - 10 [[A domesticated sattar|domesticated sattar]]s
1. Return to [[Gretchen Spiritstorm]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 53s 57c
- [[Alternate Advancement]] Experience. (First Completion Only)
