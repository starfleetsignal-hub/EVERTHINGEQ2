## Notes

- Disable your merc, or you might not get credit!

## Steps

1. Go to [[Enchanted Lands]] and visit the locations below along the western shoreline. As you approach each location, [[An agitated lamia]] will spawn and transform into [[A dryad]] when brought below 50% (even if you one-shot them they will still transform):
   - {{waypoint 544.01, 2.48, -750.10}}
   - {{waypoint 505.71, 0.78, -813.90}}
   - {{waypoint 517.66, 0.78, -914.92}}
   - {{waypoint 437.10, -0.61, -1034.42}}
1. Return to [[Kurista (Greater Faydark)|Kurista]].

## Rewards

- +2500 faction with **Tunare**
