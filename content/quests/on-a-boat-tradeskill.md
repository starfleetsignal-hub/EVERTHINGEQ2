## Notes

- Must be lv 100+ in order to get mail

## Steps

1. Find [[Raffik]] on [[Isle of Mara]] at {{waypoint -19, -2, 91}}.
1. Harvest 2 [[Wild Flameflower|Wild Flameflowers]] on the island of [[Veiled Thessk]] in [[Vesspyr Isles]] at {{waypoint -607, -38, -75}}.![Wild Flameflower](images/Wild_Flameflower.jpg)
   - *They are trackable using Track Harvestables (Must be  harvested. Having them in your bag beforehand will not work).*
   - *The [[Wild Flameflower|Wild Flameflowers]] are also house items and you can gather additional flowers even when you don't have the quest or have completed it.*
1. Scribe the [[Brew of Readiness (Recipe during Quest)|Brew of Readiness]] recipe.
1. Craft a [[Brew of Readiness (Quest Item)|Brew of Readiness]] from the recipe given.
   - *1 [[Hylocereus fruit]]*
   - *1 [[Sulfur fruit]]*
   - *1 [[Effulgent Candle]]*
     - Note: The nearest chemistry table is on [[Daarspire]] at {{waypoint 170, 188, -130}}
     - Alternatively, use the chemistry table on [[Isle of Mara|Mara]], at {{waypoint -63, -2, 128}}
1. Return to [[Raffik]]

## Rewards

- At least 23p 9g
- [[Brew of Readiness (QuestReward)|Brew of Readiness]] - Recipe book
- Leave the zone then return -(after about **an hour**) Raffik will now be a Fury mercenary you can hire.
