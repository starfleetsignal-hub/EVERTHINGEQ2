***This quest scales to the level tier of the character (i.e. level 21-30 characters will receive the level 25 version of the quest).***

## Steps

1. Complete the appropriate kill task based on character's level tier:
   - **Tier 1 (Level 1-10)**
     - Kill 5 *zombies* in [[The Graveyard]]
     - Kill 5 *rust monsters* in [[Sunken City]]
   - **Tier 2 (Level 11-20)**
     - Kill 5 [[A rock lioness|rock lionesses]] in [[The Commonlands]]
     - Kill 5 [[A moon wisp|moon wisps]] in The Commonlands
   - **Tier 3 (Level 21-30)**
     - Kill 5 [[A mist grinnin|mist grinnins]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] (Guardian Mist Grinnin count)
     - Kill 5 [[A dusk leaper|dusk leapers]] in Nektulos Forest (only the level 25 dusk leapers on top of the rock around {{waypoint -651, 67, 12}} count, others will not)
   - **Tier 4 (31-40)**
     - Kill 5 [[A Vallon miner|Vallon miners]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]]
     - Kill 5 [[A Blackhook raider|Blackhook raiders]] in Zek
   - **Tier 5 (41-50)**
     - Kill 5 [[A living magma|living magma]] in [[Lavastorm]]
     - Kill 5 [[A Sootfoot harvester|Sootfoot harvesters]] in Lavastorm
   - **Tier 6 (51-55)**
     - Kill 5 *Grovespirits* in [[The Lesser Faydark]]
     - Kill 5 [[A honeybrood worker|honeybrood workers]] in The Lesser Faydark
   - OR
     - Kill 5 [[An Anaz Mal veilguard|Anaz Mal veilguarda]] in [[The Living Tombs]]
     - Kill 5 *mummies* in [[The Silent City]]
   - **Tier 7 (Level 61-70)**
     - Kill 5 [[A living chest|living chests]] in [[Kaladim]]
     - Kill 5 [[An obsidian construct|obsidian constructs]] in [[Mistmoore Catacombs]]
   - **Tier 8 (Level 71-80)**
     - Kill 5 [[A trained war sokokar|trained war sokokars]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
     - Kill 5 [[A Kunzar tiger|Kunzar tigers]] in Kunzar Jungle
   - OR
     - kill 5 *Leech* mobs in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
     - kill 5 *sporconids* mobs Kunzar Jungle
   - **Tier 9 (Level 81-90)**
     - collect 5 *sweet scented potpourri* from [[A malignant seedling|malignant seedlings]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]] (south of Toxxulia Forest Station)
     - collect 5 *tentacle springs* from [[A highland fearstalker|highland fearstalkers]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]]
   - **Tier 10 (Level 91-95)**
     - Kill 5 [[An insidious fearlings|insidious fearlings]] in [[Eidolon Jungle]]
     - Kill 5 [[A churning obol|churning obol]] in [[Obol Plains]]
1. Return to [[Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber (South Freeport)|Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber]]
