## Steps

1. Speak to [[Mav Boilfist]]
1. Speak with [[Shipping Coordinator Hando]] found at the dock.
1. Check crates at the end of the docks, it will be in a small barrel on the south side of the dock
1. Speak to [[Shipping Coordinator Hando]] to clear the package
1. Deliver the package to [[Mav Boilfist]]

## Rewards

- [[Courier's pouch]]
- At least s2 c16
