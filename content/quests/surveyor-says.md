## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Visit [[The Fae Isle]] {{waypoint -517, -3, 71}}
   - Visit [[The Riverwatch]] {{waypoint -196, 8, -308}}
   - Visit [[Granary Hill]] {{waypoint 225, 3, -284}}
   - Visit [[The Runnyeye Ward]] {{waypoint 166, 10, -433}}
1. Return to [[Deputy Nettlebrine]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 82s 32c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Jumjum Soaked Scholar's Wristband]] (All Mages)
  - [[Jumjum Soaked Shepard's Armband]] (All Priests)
  - [[Jumjum Soaked Sleuth's Wristband]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Jumjum Soaked Soldier's Wristband]] (All Fighters)
