## Steps

1. Speak to [[Sumit]] in the Hall of Necromancy {{waypoint 1647, -267, 3216}}.
1. Scribe the [[Preliminary Notes on an Experimental Device for the Study of Life Essences, Draft 7|recipe]] in your inventory and craft an [[Essence entanglement device]] at a Workbench.
   - This is done at a work bench and requires 1 [[Amber]], 2 [[Quicksilver cluster]], 2 [[Titanium ore]], 1 [[Ethereal coal]].
   - Crafting stations are in the Paineel Commons at {{waypoint 1800, -299, 3492}} on both sides.
1. Return to [[Sumit]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- +1,000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +1,500 faction with **Seekers of the Dark Truth**
- +1,000 faction with **Craftkeepers**
