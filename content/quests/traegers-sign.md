## Description

"Traeger Verdegreen asked me to provide one last service for him. He's made a drawing out of his business's logo and he would like me to deliver it to Sassirah, a tailor, who will then create a banner for his business." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Talk to [[Sassirah Fallenleaf]] {{waypoint 298, 126, 254}} on the [[Gearheart's Forge]] platform.
1. Return to [[Traeger Verdegreen]].

## Rewards

- At least 1s 5c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Traeger Verdegreen: "I'm devising a symbol for my business. You know, something that will really make it stand out from the rest. If you could take this drawing over to Sassirah the tailor near Gearheart's Forge I'd really appreciate it. She's making a banner for me."
- Player Character: "Sounds simple enough."
- Traeger Verdegreen: "This would be the last thing I ask of you, I promise! After you've dropped of the drawing, stop back by and I'll pay you for the work. Really, you've been a great help to me."
- Player Character: "No problem."

In order to help Traeger in his business, I need to deliver artwork to a tailor who will create a banner for him. Sassirah is a tailor whose shop is near Gearheart's Forge in Kelethin.

- Sassirah Fallenleaf: "Hello!"
- Player Character: "Traeger Verdegreen sent me over with this drawing for his banner."
- Sassirah Fallenleaf: "That's great! I'll get right to work on it. If you wouldn't mind taking him back this sample patch I made earlier. It won't work with his new design, but maybe he can think of something useful to do with it. Thank you!"
- Player Character: "You're welcome."

I've spoken with Sassirah. I delivered the artwork to Sassirah. I need to return to Traeger Verdegreen with the sample patch that Sassirah made. Traeger Verdegreen is in the building near the Old Kelethin Acorn Lift.

- Traeger Verdegreen: "Did Sassirah like the drawing?"
- Player Character: "Yes, she's getting started on it soon."
- Traeger Verdegreen: "If you ever need a work reference around here, just ask."
- Player Character: "Thanks, I might need it!"

I've spoken with Traeger Verdegreen. I gave the sample to Traeger Verdegreen.

I provided the tailor with Traeger's sketch. Though it wasn't much work on my part, Traeger's offered to provide a reference for me if I decide to seek further work around Kelethin. He recommended that I speak with a treant named Whittier to see if he has any work for me.
