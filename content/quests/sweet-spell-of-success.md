## Steps

1. Gather and hunt the following
   - Gather water from 6 **chameleon cacti** in [[Warpwood Cairnlands]] around {{waypoint 292, 108, -90}}, {{waypoint 150, 119, 303}}, {{waypoint 104, 115, 433}} and {{waypoint 383, 99, 107}}.  They are most often found on the higher parts of rock formations. ![Chameleon Cactus](images/Chameleoncacti.png)
   - Hunt 16 **amorphous splotch** (small green goop mobs) at [[Irewater Pond]] around {{waypoint 423, 93, 304}}.
1. Return to [[Dhusala]]. Receive [[Dreadfell Obscuration Potion]] . ![Witch Ceremony](images/Witchbusiness.png)
1. Locate the ceremony near [[Decaying Copse]] {{waypoint -85, 8.41, -392.96}}.
1. Use the [[Dreadfell Obscuration Potion]] to witness the ceremony. Wait for the end of the conversation.
1. Return to [[Dhusala]].
