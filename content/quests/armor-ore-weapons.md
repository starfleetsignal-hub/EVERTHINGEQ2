## Prerequisites

- You need at least -6000 faction with Legion of Danak to start this quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest 20 [[Ferrite cluster|ferrite clusters]] from [[Bedded ore]] nodes in [[Jarsath Wastes]]. The closest nodes are around [[Venril's Crown]]. {{waypoint 169, -91, -377}}
1. Take the ore to [[Somers the Hard (Npc)|Somers the Hard]] in [[Danak Shipyards]]. {{waypoint -105, -40, 1117}}
1. Speak to [[Supply Clerk Linxx]]. {{waypoint -187, -33, 1079}}

## Rewards

- [[Nightwatch Boots]]
- At least 11g 5s 77c
- +150 faction with **Legion of Danak**
