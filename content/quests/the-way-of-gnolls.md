## Steps

*Nisch Mas Nolphia has an idea to help me gain favor with the gnolls and more importantly with Nisch Mal Polphar. I need to prove my strength to the pack and the best way to do that is by collecting trophies from slain gnolls in the Darkpaw Warrens.*

1. Enter the [[Darkpaw Warrens (Solo)]] at {{waypoint -24, -76, -255}}
1. Gather [[Gnoll Trophy|Gnoll trophies]] from felled gnolls
   - Loot 15 'Gnoll Trophy' from a small chest from any group with a gnoll in Sabertooth Burrow.  The chest can include 1 trophy per gnoll in the group.
1. Seek Out and Speak with [[Nisch Mas Nolphia]]
   - Return to {{waypoint -26, -44, -240}} and speak to the gnoll

## Rewards

- At least 3p 36g 79s 77c
- [[Serilian Shaped Scale Chausses]]
