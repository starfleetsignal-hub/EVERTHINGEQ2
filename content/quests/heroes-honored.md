## Notes

*Before killing Lord Kurpep in substep 3 to complete the quest, it might be wise to announce to the group that you are on this quest, and that noone should Evac or Zone the group after kill - especially outside the instance, since the item shard of the Eye of Marr is NO-ZONE*

## Steps

1. Find clues in [[The Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen]] on the nature of the Ykesha curse, and the fate of those who stood against it
   - 5 written clues as to what may have been the thoughts of the last frogloks within the Ruins of Guk (pick up pages)
     - {{waypoint 10, -31, -107}} (closest page to the zone in, east from the first pool)
     - {{waypoint -62, -52, -243}} (after killing [[Froppit the Everliving]])
     - {{waypoint -11, -59, -263}} (to the east as you drop down to the pool after killing [[Molinap the Destructor]])
     - {{waypoint 91, -64, -283}} (near the second to last named, [[A Ghoul Usurper]])
     - {{waypoint 148, -61, -392}} (in the room with the last named, Lord Kurpep)
   - 10 clues as to the fate of the froglok defenders within Guk (kill updates)
   - Any evidence of the followers of the Shin Lord's plan to defend agains the curse (Kill [[Lord Kurpep]] - The Shin Lord, and loot a "shard of the Eye of Marr." Place the shard on top of the large crystal, on the platform at {{waypoint 129, -23, -422}}, which radiates a strange energy causes the Ykeshan's curse. This will update for the whole group.)
1. Report to Gurlig.
