1. Kill 6 [[Carnivorous graniteback|carnivorous granitebacks]] around {{waypoint 3, 210, -73}}.

- +1000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Opulent Cummerbund of the Brigade Defender]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Opulent Cummerbund of the Brigade Healer]] (All Priests)
  - [[Opulent Cummerbund of the Brigade Magician]] (All Mages)
  - [[Opulent Cummerbund of the Brigade Stalker]] (All Scouts)
