## Steps

1. Collect 7 pieces of [[Scrap metal]] from various random locations around The Baubbleshire. They look like giant metal cogs on the ground and in the water.
1. Return to [[Neezer Grund (The Baubbleshire)|Neezer Grund]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Coin
- [[A Hunk of Scrap Metal]]
