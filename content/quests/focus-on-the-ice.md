## Steps

1. Search for the magic foci near the Tower of Frozen Shadow
   - Click on 3 different spires along the walkway to the Tower of Frozen Shadow
     - {{waypoint -1026, -511, -2371}}
     - {{waypoint -1104, -531, -2286}}
     - {{waypoint -1096, -535, -2196}}
     - {{waypoint -1124, -534, -2011}}
     - {{waypoint -1033, -540, -1892}}
   - The clickable towers have a "respawn" of about 30-45 minutes - if you can't find the third tower just wait and the other two will be re-clickable.
