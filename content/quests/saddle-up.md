## Notes

- Harvest additional bovoch items from the [[Jester's garden|Jester's gardens]] to get the next three updates.
- Return to one of these NPCs to complete the quest (check your quest journal, ending NPC can vary):
  - [[Bermond Stonebraid]] at {{waypoint -575, -21, -460}} in [[Antonica]] (*closest to the Ulteran spire Portal, Griffin station oracle tower)*
  - [[Kurzol Bonegnash]] at {{waypoint -628, -50, -665}} in [[The Commonlands]] (*closest to the docks)*
  - [[Flyngin Leftfoot]] at {{waypoint -276.49, 0.04, 103.67}} in [[Enchanted Lands]] *(right next to the Ulteran Spire Portal)*
  - [[Jaromir]] at {{waypoint -179.22, -57.75, 86.47}} in [[Darklight Wood]] *(use the druid ring)*

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Unsaddled Beltie Bovoch Plushie]]
  - 10 [[Fool's Gold Coin|Fool's Gold Coins]]
