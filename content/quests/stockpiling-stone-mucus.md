## Steps

1. Harvest 10 *stone mucus* in [[Fens of Nathsar]]. {{waypoint -2230, -80, 1258}}  *(right on the rocks above the entrance to Kunzar)*
1. Return to [[Kellaen Mednevn]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]].
   - Remember to put your illusion back on after you zone into Kunzar Jungle.

## Rewards

- At least 36g 87s 50c
- +1,800 faction with **The Order of Rime**
