## Notes

- Scribe [[Frostfell Lair Decoration]], received when the quest is accepted, if you haven't already learned the recipes.

## Steps

1. Craft 6 [[Frostfell Dinner]]:
   - 12 [[Frostfell Groceries]], from one of the boxes near the entrance {{waypoint 1, 0, -569}}
   - 12 [[Elven Magic]], from [[Ginneldarf Gigglegibber]] {{waypoint 36, 0, -573}}
   - [[Stove & Keg]] {{waypoint 17, 0, -578}}
1. Return to Gumos Frostfoot.
