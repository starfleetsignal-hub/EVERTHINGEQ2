This is a large, very solid and heavy scale-like material. On its surface, it looks as if it is covered in some form of fungus or moss. It could be some form of natural armor. I feel oddly compelled to find out more about it, while on the dark side of this moon.

## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A fungus fiend|fungus fiends]] at [[Sods and Ends|Sods and Bends]] {{waypoint 498.84, -4.33, -446.61}} in [[Echo Caverns]].

- [[Crown Receptacle (Tier 5) (Version 1)|Crown Receptacle (Tier 5)]]
- [[Fungus Covered Scale]] -- *collectible*
