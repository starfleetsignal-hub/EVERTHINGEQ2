## Steps

Gil McMartin entrusted me with knowledge of what must next be done to win the hearts of the undecided: I must free and scatter the dogs that are being trained to act as vicious enforcers for the Citadel.

1. Within 20 minutes, destroy 5 crates of puppies to set them free. All five were located around the area just east of the stables in West Freeport {{waypoint 84.81, -9.57, -39.26}}.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +2000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -2000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- **Note**: This quest does not grant any experience or achievement experience.
