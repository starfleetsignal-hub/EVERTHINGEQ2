## Notes

- This quest is only available to characters of good alignment. Evil aligned characters should complete [[Kufu's Intentions]] instead.
- You must have at least 30,000 faction with the Ashen Order to obtain this quest. It is not necessary to complete the preceding quests in this timeline such as [[Diplomatic Liaisons (Ashen Order)|Diplomatic Liaisons]].
- To obtain the required faction without doing the quest line, you can kill [[An Ashen Disciple|Ashen Disciple]] monks around [[T'Narev]]. If you are in a group, killing groups of Ashen Disciple monks will raise your faction with the Ashen Order much faster than completing the repeatable quests offered by [[Shu Fang Qi]].
- **If you have completed the quest [[Kufu's Intentions]] as an evil character and then betrayed, this quest will be offered to you but you won´t be able to complete it.** When you talk to Kufu he tells you he is busy and asks you to tell Shen to stop sending such obvious little spies.

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Cave of Knowledge]] {{waypoint 94, -220, -1208}} and talk to [[Kufu, The Keeper of Knowledge]]{{waypoint 234, 2, 288}}. The entrance is found inside a cave at sea level, below [[T'Narev]].
   - When you are inside the Cave of Knowledge, you can find [[Kufu, The Keeper of Knowledge]] by always taking paths to the right when given a choice.
   - You can use stealth or invisibility to avoid the encounters inside the Cave of Knowledge and the camp where Kufu is located is safe.
1. Return to [[Zhao Wu]].
