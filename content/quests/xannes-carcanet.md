## Description

A mysterious Erudite named Ragheejb Yasir is recruiting adventurers to find rare artifacts and is paying well for them. These items, located in some of the most dangerous dungeons all across Norrath, will not be easy to recover without the help of some proven allies. This time I've been asked to find the Xanne's Carcanet in Mistmyr Manor. Rumors claim that Xanne was once Mayong Mistmoore's consort though what remains of her fate is unknown.

## Steps

1. Find Xanne's Carcanet within [[Mistmyr Manor]]
   - Kill [[Primogen Daishirou]] to loot this item.
1. Return to [[Ragheejb Yasir]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- 2 [[Void Shard]]
