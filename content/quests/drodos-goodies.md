## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Collect a deck of King's Court playing cards by killing [[A grove badger|grove badgers]]
   - Collect a halfling foot comb by killing [[A lancer wasp|lancer wasps]]
   - Collect a set of goblin dice by killing [[A klakrok drone|klakrok drones]]
   - Collect a halfling brunch box by killing [[A briarpaw cub|briarpaw cub]]
1. Return to [[Augin Drodo]]

## Rewards

- At least 2g 10s 21c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Apostle's Ordained Plate Gloves]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Marked Gloves]] (All Mages)
  - [[Hanshi's Balanced Hand Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Polished Gauntlets]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Oracle's Sacred Handguards]] (Shamans)
  - [[Protector's Moon Etched Leather Gloves]] (Druids)
  - [[Raider's Thickened Chain Gloves]] (All Scouts)
