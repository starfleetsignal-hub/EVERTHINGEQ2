## Steps

1. Speak with [[Field Marshal Vishra]] within [[Siren's Grotto: Tear in the Grotto]] and accept him as a quest mercenary.
1. Kill the siren queen.
   1. To get to the queen, you have to kill the four [[A Tavalan summoner|Tavalan summoners]] in the middle
   1. Approach the middle waterfall-like door and it will open.
   1. Send Vishra to attack [[Queen Dulseris (Siren's Grotto: Tear in the Grotto)|Queen Dulseris]] and stay near the seashells.
   1. Shortly after you begin the fight, you'll receive a red message, *"Queen Dulseris summons forth protectors, you may be able to put the barriers back up to keep them at bay!"* Click the three seashells quickly to open them and rebuild the barrier.
      - If you are too slow with the shells, x4 [[A Royal Protector|Royal Protectors]] will add. If that happens, just revive, then **/togglemerc** to re-summon Vishra. If that doesn't work then try relogging.
   1. When the barriers are up, kill the queen.
1. Gather the gem at {{waypoint -99, 7, -866}}.
1. Return to [[Field Marshal Vishra]] in [[Cobalt Scar]].
