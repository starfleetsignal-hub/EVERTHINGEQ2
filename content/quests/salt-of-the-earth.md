## Prerequisites

You must be 30 artisan to pick this quest up or she will tell you to come back at 30.

## Steps

1. Gather three [[Pure Salt Crystal|pure salt crystals]] from around the hotspring geysers.![salt crystals](images/Salt_crystals.jpg)
   - Head to any geyser in [[Steamfont Mountains]], except for those in the [[Steamfont Wetlands]]. [[Goldsteam Geyser]] at {{waypoint -1020, 96, 846}} and [[Beryl Shrimp Geyser]] at {{waypoint -350, 93, 1336}} are good spots. If your adventure level is far below the zone, Beryl Shrimp has no aggro directly around the geyser, making it a safer location.
1. Harvest the little white salt crystal nodes.
1. Return the crystals to [[Karalyn Cinderton]].

## Rewards

- At least 2g 8s
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Pure Salt Crystals]]
