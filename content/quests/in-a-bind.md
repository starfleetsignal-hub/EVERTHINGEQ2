## Notes

- Must be a level 50 or higher sage in order to pick up this quest. Other tradeskill classes cannot get this quest.

## Steps

1. Ask advice from a master sage in one of the big cities.  This would be [[Oolieamim]], who can be found in all six big cities.
   - [[Qeynos Capitol District]] - ([[Oolieamim (Qeynos)]] {{waypoint 349, -18, -98}})
   - [[Kelethin]] - ([[Oolieamim (Kelethin)]] {{waypoint 453, 91, 355}})
   - [[New Halas]] - ([[Oolieamim (New Halas)]] {{waypoint -363, 177, -44}})
   - [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]] - ([[Oolieamim (Freeport)]] {{waypoint 29, -12, 84}})
   - [[Neriak]] - [[Dockside Markets]] ([[Oolieamim (Neriak)]] {{waypoint -164, -2, 275}})
   - [[Gorowyn]] - [[The Wards]] ([[Oolieamim (Gorowyn)]] {{waypoint 2738, 123, 1146}})
1. Consult the Apprentice Bookbinder on the 2nd floor of [[The Tower of the Moon]] {{waypoint -150, 176, -149}} in [[Maj'Dul]] about their bookbinding techniques. He will give you the recipe [[Bookbinding for Beginners]].
1. Scribe this recipe book.
1. Make a stonehide leather notebook for [[Meledi Augren|Meledi]] at an engraved desk.
1. Return to [[The Village of Shin]] and give the notebook to [[Meledi Augren|Meledi]].

You will need:

- 2 Beryllium Cluster
- 1 Rough Nacre
- 1 Stonehide Leather Pelt
- 2 Succulent Root
- 8 Lambent Incense

## Rewards

- [[The Bookbinder|Title: The Bookbinder]]
- Ability to craft notebooks for players to write.
