## Steps

Kill the following enemies

- Fell the Fallen : Kill [[Patriae Vykel]] in [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Anathema]]
- Slay the Ghoul Lord : Kill [[Hoptor Thagglor]] in [[The Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors]]
- Exorcise Gynok's Inner Demons : Kill [[The Eidolon of Depravity]] in [[Befallen: Halls of the Forsaken]]

Or

- Torch the Codexicon : Kill [[The Codexicon]] in [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Crucible]]
- Extinguish The Reformed Magolemus : Kill [[The Reformed Magolemus]] in [[Najena's Hollow Tower]]
- Do in Zraxth, Ykesha's High Advisor : Kill [[Zraxth]] in [[The Ruins of Guk: Ykesha's Outer Stronghold]]
- Note: The most difficult of the three.

Or

- Extinguish the Burning Dead : Kill [[Dread Exarch Mordek]] in [[Befallen: Necrotic Asylum]]
- Put out the Firelord : Kill [[Firelord Kaern]] in [[The Deep Forge]]
- Liquidate Lord Marcus Thex : Kill [[Lord Marcus Thex]] in [[Evernight Abbey]]

- [[Hunter's Union Mission Reward Crate]]
