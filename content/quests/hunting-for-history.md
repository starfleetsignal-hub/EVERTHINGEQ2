## Steps

1. Scribe [[Archaeological Grade Ice Pick Recipe]]
1. Craft the ice pick at the nearby forge using
   - 2 [[Titanium ore]], 1 [[Mottled pelt]], 1 [[Ethereal coal]]
1. Click on nearby snow mounds until you get an update. The mounds of snow may be found on the Ring War Battle Field, for example I found 1 mound at {{waypoint 224.11, -333.25, -438.43}}.
1. Speak with [[Tora]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
