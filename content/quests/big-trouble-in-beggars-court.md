## Steps

1. Locate and sell a package to [[Durio Dexus]] {{waypoint -30.34, -5.00, 8.75}}
1. Locate and deliver documents to [[Kaseo Volcatius]] {{waypoint 62.77, -6.12, 101.54}} near the North Freeport gate.
1. Return to Durio.

You may now continue with the quest [[Tricking the Traitor]].
