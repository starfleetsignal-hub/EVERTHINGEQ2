## Prerequisites

- You must be at least tradeskill level 65 to receive this quest.

## Ingredients

- 10 [[Drolvarg fang|drolvarg fangs]]
- 20 [[Ferrite cluster|ferrite clusters]] (32 for complete questline)
- 20 [[Redwood lumber|redwood lumbers]] (28 for complete questline)
- 50 [[Smoldering coal]] (70 for complete questline)

## Steps

1. Scribe [[101 Things To Do With Fangs, volume 1]] and craft 1000 [[Fang tipped arrow|fang tipped arrows]]. You create 100 arrows for each successful attempt.
   - The closest [[Forge]] is at {{waypoint 865, 12, 138}}.
   - The arrows are with Artisan (level 1-9) Geomancy skills, but are a level 70 combine.
1. Return to [[Jones]] in [[Dreg's Landing]].

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- At least 74g 40s 49c (50g of which is reimbursement for the fuel used during quest)
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
