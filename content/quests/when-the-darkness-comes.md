## Steps

1. Travel to [[Antonica]] (near Wiz Spire / Druid Portal), and click on the large stone at {{waypoint -2247, -14, -247}} to enter the one-time instance [[A Buried Hideout]].
   - Similar to the last quest, the quest text still prompts you to summon the [[Spirit of Giidib]] by activating the [[Shard of Marr]] in order to find the location of the instance. You no longer need to do this.
1. Enter the hideout and kill all the monsters. Then double-click [[A captured froglok]] to release him.
   - For a little fun, although not really needed for solo mobs, there is a rock near the entrance you can throw that will bring the single mobs to the top. The rock respawns quickly to throw again.
1. Return to [[Frup Groaak]].
