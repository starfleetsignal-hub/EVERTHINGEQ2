*This quest is also part of the [[Book Quests Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[An ire minion|ire minion]]s - any alcove in the zone.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 ire minions.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 [[A diseased corpse|diseased corpse]]s - halls and rooms from [[Cryptkeeper Maglus]] to [[Vegalys Keldrane]] {{waypoint 126, 7, -102}} and onwards towards [[Vargon]].
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 7 diseased corpses.
1. Read the book
1. Kill 5 [[A restless corpse|restless corpse]]s.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 8 restless corpses.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 diseased corpses.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 [[A tortured essence (Crypt of Betrayal)|tortured essences]] - rooms between [[Vargon]] and [[Adun]] {{waypoint 152, 7, -6}}.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 5 tortured essences.
1. Read the book.

## Notes

- You can find all of the restless corpses and diseased corpses you need in the chamber at {{waypoint 117, 7, -96}}.
- The beetles in the room share a spawn with the zombies so make sure to clear them out while you are hunting for zombies.
