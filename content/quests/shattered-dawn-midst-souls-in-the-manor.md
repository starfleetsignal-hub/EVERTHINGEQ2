## Note

The quest [[Paludal Removal]] from [[Vusor Umerus]] at {{waypoint 163.88, 62.16, -625.09}} sends you to the same instance.

## Steps

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 169, 62, -679}} to enter [[Fordel Midst: Wayward Manor (Solo)|Fordel Midst: Wayward Manor [Solo]]].
1. Talk to 8 frightened spirits to free them. Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint 485.47, -23.44, -539.56}}
   - {{waypoint 688.71, -22.10, -625.89}}
   - {{waypoint 713.95, -23.48, -564.20}}
   - {{waypoint 713.38, -8.65, -507.68}}
   - {{waypoint 559.28, -8.65, -535.51}}
   - {{waypoint 621.32, -8.65, -611.87}}
   - {{waypoint 663.93, -8.65, -621.97}}
   - {{waypoint 634.34, -8.65, -657.32}}
     - *Note: See [[Fordel Midst: Wayward Manor (Solo)|Fordel Midst: Wayward Manor [Solo]]] for help proceeding through the zone.*
     - *Note: You don't have to do the full zone with the crafting part you can just kill the first named, the 2nd on the 2nd floor and the librarian final named*
1. Speak to [[Incantor Lizerian]] at {{waypoint 689.53, -8.65, -673.94}}.
1. Return to [[Recuso Tor]] in [[The Blinding]] to speak to [[Doomsayer Adarius]] at {{waypoint -499, 66, 240}}.
