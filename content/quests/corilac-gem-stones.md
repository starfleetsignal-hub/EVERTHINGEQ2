*Shinies spawn somewhere in the general vicinity of the annual [[Scorched Sky]] celebrations in the various zones.*

- [[Antonica]] near [[Windstalker Village]] {{waypoint -1891, -38, -550}}
- [[Butcherblock Mountains]] {{waypoint 290, 189, 441}}
- [[The Commonlands]] at [[Dog Trapper Lake]], on top of the giant rock at {{waypoint 11, -13, -750}}. A tiny ramp up starts at {{waypoint 14, -44, -733}}
- [[Great Divide]] southwest of [[Scar Bridge]] at {{waypoint -343, -347, 658}}
- [[Lavastorm]] near Dragon's Breath Tunnel Mount Station at {{waypoint -102, 17, 67}}
- [[Sinking Sands]] near the Druid Ring at {{waypoint -1460, 229, -9}}
- [[Tranquil Sea]] on [[South Dshinn]] near [[Lobha Peaks]] at {{waypoint -1413, 121, -539}}

Visit at least 3 locations to collect all 3 sets of gems:

- [[Polished Lapis Lava]] - [[Darklight Wood]], [[Sinking Sands]], [[Tranquil Sea]]
- [[Cut Magmatic Crystal]] - [[Darklight Wood]], [[Sinking Sands]], [[Tranquil Sea]]
- [[Cracked Blazestone]] - [[Darklight Wood]], [[Sinking Sands]], [[Tranquil Sea]]
- [[Cut Lavaflow Marble]] - [[Great Divide]], [[Pillars of Flame]], [[The Commonlands]]
- [[Pyre Pearl]] - [[Great Divide]], [[Pillars of Flame]], [[The Commonlands]]
- [[Chipped Flame Ruby]] - [[Great Divide]], [[Pillars of Flame]], [[The Commonlands]]
- [[Molten-Heart Diamond]] - [[Antonica]], [[Butcherblock Mountains]], [[Lavastorm]]
- [[Cut Cinder Agate]] - [[Antonica]], [[Butcherblock Mountains]], [[Lavastorm]]
- [[Black Scorch Stone]] - [[Antonica]], [[Butcherblock Mountains]], [[Lavastorm]]
