## Prerequisites

- You must be able to fly (or otherwise access flight-only locations).

## Steps

1. Examine the [[Stone of Sorrow]] in your inventory.
1. Investigate the soil around the roots of the [[Tree of Blight]] in [[Eastern Wastes]]. This is located under a group of roots at 642 -453 4,203.
   - [[Gloomvine]] 93^^^ will attack you. He's fairly easy to solo/duo, but you do not have to kill him, the update comes from clicking the soil.
1. Investigate the fate of Wuoshi and the Bloom of Growth: Examine the [[Stone of Sorrow]] again and head to [[Lesser Faydark]] to the Fugutr Tribe Camp at {{waypoint 105, 15, -375}} and jump in the river above the waterfall.  Head North to {{waypoint 162, 1, -643}} and enter the [[The Emerald Halls: Wuoshi’s Fate]].
1. Towards the middle of the zone (up the ramp near Wuoshi and to the left) you will find [[Sabstin V'Despth]] {{waypoint 838, 133, -584}} a non-aggro NPC. Talk to her and she will aggro, 93^^^. Every 25% health she will summon a 93^^^ add that is best killed before you continue with her, or you will end up with all three adds on you.
   - You do not need to kill any of the trash in the zone if you can avoid it, however you may want to kill the [[Withered Guardian]] {{waypoint 666, 128, -524}} (non-aggro 94^^^, across the bridge to the left of the zone in) and [[Sawtooth the Corrupted]] {{waypoint 783, 88, -600}}. If these are killed before you engage V'Despth they will not be summoned during the fight.
1. Examine the Bloom of Growth: Once dead, examine the flower just below where the [[Sabstin V'Despth]] was standing. {{waypoint 816, 106, -603}}
1. Examine the [[Stone of Sorrow]], it will tell you to find [[Firiona Vie]].
   - Her location depends on your alignment:
     - For good-aligned players, she can be found inside the [[Fae Royal Hall]] in [[Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint 423, 136, 261}}
     - For evil-aligned players, she can be found inside the [[Shadow Oak]] in [[Darklight Wood]] {{waypoint 445, -23, -266}}
   - NOTE: For Steps 7, 8 and 9 (below) the gathering must be done individually.  NO GROUP UPDATES
1. You must now gather 3 items (parts of a chalice) from three [[Destiny of Velious]] instances. These may be done in any order and can be gathered from cleared instances:
   - "Search amongst the treasure within [[Iceshard Keep]]." Head to Eastern Wastes and take the griffon to the Kael Drakkel Station.  Zone in to [[Outer Kael]] at {{waypoint -246, -278, 4484.16}}. Then, zone in to [[Iceshard Keep]] at {{waypoint -1676, 20, -529}}. The item is a shiny, inside the treasure room on the right side of the main building, between the chests on the right side {{waypoint -463, 119, 402}} or {{waypoint -438, 118, 426}} or {{waypoint -489, 120, 397}}.
   - "Search under bough within [[Forgotten Pools]]." Head to the [[Great Divide]] and zone in at {{waypoint 374, -337, 1108}}. Inside the red/blue tree room, towards the back under a tree. {{waypoint -62, 36, -509}} or {{waypoint -26, 39, -470}} or {{waypoint -21, 42, -527}}
   - [[Crystal Caverns: Collapse]]: Go to the [[Eastern Wastes]], then zone into the Crystal Caverns at {{waypoint -757, -328, 2621}}. The item is around -82.02 15.21 -76.18. next to the alchemy table with the different colored potions. This can be done solo if sneaky enough.
1. Return to [[Firiona Vie|Firiona Vie.]] Once again, her location depends on your alignment:
   1. For good-aligned players, she can be found inside the [[Fae Royal Hall]] in [[Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint 423, 136, 261}}
   1. For evil-aligned players, she can be found inside the [[Shadow Oak]] in [[Darklight Wood]] {{waypoint 445, -23, -266}}
1. Gather elements of Earth and Air that originated from the [[Plane of Growth]] before The Shattering, but are now found in [[Lesser Faydark]] and [[Tenebrous Tangle]]:
   - In [[Lesser Faydark]], gather earth from the burial site of the [[Heartwood Master]]. To get to the burial site, go to the rocks at {{waypoint -735, -22, -267}} and use them to climb up to the memorial located at {{waypoint -736, 5, -336}} in [[Lesser Faydark]].
   - Gather air from 10 wisps (green shinies) from [[Tenebrous Tangle]]. These are found floating above only the tiny islands surrounding the larger islands in the zone - they are not visible until you get very close. All you need to do is fly through them to 'catch' them.
     - {{waypoint -587, 180, 819}}
     - {{waypoint -925, -195, 941}}
     - {{waypoint -1356, 355, 7}}
     - {{waypoint -1253, -244, -502}}
     - {{waypoint -546, -87, 41}}
     - {{waypoint -441, -167, 96}}
     - {{waypoint -291, -263, 222}}
     - {{waypoint -93, 4, -870}}
     - {{waypoint -102, 61, -766}}
     - {{waypoint 580, 303, -448}}
       - Note: There are multiple wisps on some islands.
1. Return to [[Firiona Vie]].  As before,
   1. For good-aligned players, she can be found inside the [[Fae Royal Hall]] in [[Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint 423, 136, 261}}
   1. For evil-aligned players, she can be found inside the [[Shadow Oak]] in [[Darklight Wood]] {{waypoint 445, -23, -266}}
1. You receive [[Druid's Divining Rod]], which you need to use at the [[Tree of Blight]] 642 -453 4,203 in Eastern Wastes. When used, a 92^^^ [[Wetrot]] will spawn, easily solo'd. It will drop a non-trade item, but it seems to update the group.
1. Speak to the [[Stone of Sorrow]]
1. Return to [[Firiona Vie]] for your reward.
