## Steps

1. Seek out [[Jurie Gearshaper]] outside the [[Frugrin Observatory]] {{waypoint -911, 175, 762}} in the [[Steamfont Mountains]].
1. Hail [[Jurie Gearshaper|Jurie]] and convince her to join the Anchorage.
   - This is the successful dialog:
     - **You:** Hail, Jurie Gearshaper
     - **Jurie:** Hello there, you.
     - **You:** Gears, cogs, trinkets, and servos!
     - **Jurie:** Now, you've got my attention! What can I do for you?
     - **You:** I am looking for someone with just your excitement for all things tinkering.
     - **Jurie:** You really know how to speak to a lady don't ya?
     - **You:** No, I just know talent when I see it.
     - **Jurie:** Oh stop. No, better yet, go on... Ha!
     - **You:** In all seriousness, I am needing some help in the tinkering department. We are looking for new and unique recipes and I think you would be perfect for the job. If you are interested, that is.
     - **Jurie:** You had me at servos!
     - **You:** Great, when you are able, head to Cobalt Scar and seek out a man by the name of Whilhelm Nam'terin. He can point you in the right direction and I can meet you there later.
1. Return to to [[Whilhelm Nam'terin]] in [[Cobalt Scar]] {{waypoint 3821.94, -1020.05, 1863.29}}
