## Steps

Go to the Thexian forward camp at {{waypoint -97, -17, 5}}, {{waypoint -559, -61, 386}}, or {{waypoint -715, -38, 275}}.

1. Gather 2 Breastplates from [[A Thexian dragoon (Lesser Faydark)|Thexian dragoons]]
1. Gather 2 Breastplates from [[A Thexian soldier|Thexian soldiers]]
1. Gather 2 Breastplates from [[A Thexian archon|Thexian archons]]
1. Return to [[Trun'Gul Nybright]] {{waypoint 112, -22, 336}}.
