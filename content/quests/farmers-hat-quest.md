## Steps

1. Kill centaurs in The Thundering Steppes for 10 updates.
1. Harvest 20 [[Belladonna root|belladonna roots]] from [[Root Nodes#T3|mesquite catch weeds]].
   - You need 20 *harvests* not 20 pieces.
1. Find a *farmer's prayer* off of [[An unearthed villager]] found around {{waypoint -295, -20, -248}}.
   - **Note**: Rare update.
