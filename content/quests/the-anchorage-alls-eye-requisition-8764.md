## Steps

1. Harvest the following:
   - 18 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Coalesced reef]]
   - 15 [[Strangler root]] from [[Strangler root break]]
   - 15 [[Silvered fleck]] from [[Flecked mass]]
   - 10 [[Wandervine fruit]] from [[Celestial nursery]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Wood Planer]].
1. Craft the [[Wood Planer]] on a [[Work Bench]].
1. Speak to [[Eifae Goldthorn]] at {{waypoint 46.68, -13.58, -5.58}}.
