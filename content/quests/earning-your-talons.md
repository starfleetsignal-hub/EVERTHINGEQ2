## Steps

1. Search the banks of Wishbone Beck for signs of the terrorizing bear.
   - ![Beaver Corpse](images/Beaver_Corpse.jpg) Clickable beaver corpse at {{waypoint -17, 41, 288}}
1. ![fetid bristlehide's path](images/Fetid_bristlehide's_pathfetid_bristlehide's_path.jpg)Follow the fetid bristlehide's path from the river to {{waypoint -205.88, 123.14, 283.61}}
1. Follow the fetid bristlehide's path again to {{waypoint -140.07, 164.07, 164.01}}
1. Follow the fetid bristlehide's path again to {{waypoint -239.26, 187.64, 27.83}}
1. ![a fetid bristlehide](images/A_fetid_bristlehide.jpg)Slay a fetid bristlehide.
1. Return to [[Jabber Longwind]]

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +3,500 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
- One of the following:
  - [[Aviak Club of Spirit]]
  - [[Aviak Spear of Willpower]]
  - [[Aviak Sword of Trickery]]
  - [[Vultak Staff of Malevolence]]
  - [[Vultak Poignard of Malleability]]
  - [[Hooluk Great Blade of Pride]]
