## Notes

The recipe Fishing Hook recipe will auto-scribe at the start of the quest. You do not need to locate and manually scribe it.

## Steps

1. Find 9 metal scraps.
   - Gather three [[Rusty nail|rusty nails]] from each "broken barrels" all over the beach shore. Trackable.![broken barrel](images/Broken_barrel.jpg)
1. Craft 3 [[Fishing Hook (Crafted)|fishing hooks]].
   - Use the goblin workbench {{waypoint 16, -242, -875}}. Requires 100 Artisan, 3 [[Rusty nail|rusty nails]] and 1 Thaumic coal per hook (3 hooks).
1. Bring the hooks to [[Kreeg (Goblin)|Kreeg]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +2000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
