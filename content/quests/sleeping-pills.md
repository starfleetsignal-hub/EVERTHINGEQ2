## Steps

![Bush for aconite petal](images/Redandbrownbush.jpg)

1. Kill 15 [[A Nathsarian frog-biter|Nathsarian frog-biters]], found throughout the [[Swamp of No Hope]] {{waypoint -466, -118, 1295}}. They can also be found around a pond at {{waypoint -484, -130, 836}}.
1. Collect 8 *swamp aconite petals* from small bushes located around the ponds near the [[Western Pens of Riliss]]. The bushes are small, targetable, colored orange and green, without names over them. They are not [[Tabable]]. They are always up, but can only be interacted with during this quest:
   - {{waypoint -788, -114, 541}}
   - {{waypoint -829, -116, 570}}
   - {{waypoint -865, -115, 498}}
   - {{waypoint -923, -106, 456}}
   - {{waypoint -924, -115, 536}}
   - {{waypoint -1102, -111, 603}}
   - {{waypoint -1215, -109, 567}}
   - {{waypoint -1251, -109, 437}}
   - {{waypoint -1286, -109, 526}}
1. Return to [[Keeper Gresrik]] at the Western Pens of Riliss.

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- +5000 faction with **Riliss**
