1. Become a level 57 adventurer.
1. Meet [[Ole' Man Silvers]] in Sinking Sands. He sends you to meet him near the spires in [[Tenebrous Tangle]].

## Rewards

Note: There are no rewards until the very last step of this questline, it is merely a quest to guide new players through the different zones.
