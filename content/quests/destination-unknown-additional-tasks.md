## Notes

- This quest does not grant bonus XP; non-Veteran XP bonuses do not affect the amount given.
- When you accept quest you receive [[Recipe Scroll: Tasks for the Unknown]] which autoscribes recipes for [[Cimmerott Voidnull Stone]], [[Ritual Preparation Powder]] and [[W.H.E.R.E. V3]]
- This quest requires:
  - 10x [[Formless Coal]]
  - 1x [[Blighted Ore]]
  - 1x [[Flowfall Vines]]
  - 1x [[Tiama Wood]]
  - 1x [[Zobboz Gem]]
- The four items needed for step 1 all spawn somewhere in Detritus Rise or Tentacle Fields. Climb to a high point such as {{waypoint 441, 105, -320}} and look around for sparkling objects.
- More than one of the same item can also spawn in multiple locations.
- They are not trackable.

## Steps

1. Collect (note: can spawn in multiple locations in the indicated areas): ***Note for groups: The items respawn quickly.***
   - [[Terminus Tablet]] -- Detritus Rise {{waypoint 309, 95, -482}}, {{waypoint 400, 74, -401}}, ..
   - [[Necrotic Grave Soil]] -- Detritus Rise {{waypoint 467, 120, -520}}, {{waypoint 494, 98, -416}}, {{waypoint 503, 117, -468}}, {{waypoint 541, 117, -451}}...
   - [[Syndicate Stone]] -- Detritus Rise/Tentacle Fields {{waypoint 481, 113, -472}}, {{waypoint 528, 84, -332}}, ...
   - [[Ximor Reliquary]] -- Tentacle Fields {{waypoint 584, 66, -243}} , {{waypoint 518, 44, -169}} , {{waypoint 533, 52, -196}}, {{waypoint 475, 83, -313}}, ...
1. Craft [[Ritual Preparation Powder]] (There is a crafting station at  {{waypoint 172, 76, -161}} )
   - uses 5x [[Formless Coal]] + 4 rares you can harvest
     - 1x [[Blighted Ore]]
     - 1x [[Flowfall Vines]]
     - 1x [[Tiama Wood]]
     - 1x [[Zobboz Gem]]
1. Craft [[Cimmerott Voidnull Stone]]
   - 5x [[Formless Coal]]
1. Speak with [[Flamebearer Denat]] at {{waypoint 169, 76, -164}} in the [[Oogothl Sprawl]]
1. Use the [[Oogothl Sprawl Obelisk]] at {{waypoint 134, 65, -514}} to travel to [[The Unknown]].
1. Speak to [[Flamebearer Maslama]] in the Unknown at {{waypoint -892, -51, 1005}}, Accept [[Unknown Rune]] she gives you.
1. Craft [[W.H.E.R.E. V3]], there's an all-in-one crafting station [[Varig's Forge]] next to her.
   - Requires 5x [[Formless Coal]]
1. Speak to [[Flamebearer Maslama]] in the Unknown.

## Rewards

- [[Eddie the Eyeblight]] familiar
- one advanced recipe book per tradeskill class
  - Assistant Flamebearer Silverpot will sell the additional two recipe books once the player reaches level 135 in their tradeskill class.
    - *Provisioners only have 1 advanced recipe book*
- 75 x [[Token of the Formless Crafter]]
- [[Status Coin (75,000)]]
- At least 64p 66g 52s 78c
- Flight enabled in [[Yon Gorroth]], [[Oogothl Sprawl]]
  - flight in [[The Unknown]] requires getting to level 135 crafting
- [[It's a Known Unknown]] achievement
