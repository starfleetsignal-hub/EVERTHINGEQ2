## Steps

1. Speak to [[Lasarian Nasin]] in [[The Coalition of Tradesfolke]] in West Freeport {{waypoint 25, -18, 76}}
1. In [[The Commonlands]] collect:
   - 8 pieces of *acariquara lumber* from nodes northeast of the [[Crossroads]] {{waypoint -588, -48, -414}}; harvesting the node spawns [[An ancient wood-boring beetle]] (your level, one up) - kill the beetle or run it off to harvest the log
   - 4 *rich iron* from the cliff faces above the dock; you can fly and harvest these nodes. If on a TLE without flying, approach nodes from above and hop your way down the cliff to each of the four nodes.
1. Speak to [[Sanura Lithestripe]] in [[Armor by Ithkar]] in East Freeport {{waypoint -128, -37, -5}}
1. Return to Lasarin Nasin in West Freeport
1. Speak to Kain Starkson
