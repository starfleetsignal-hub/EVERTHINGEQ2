## Steps

![Cookie's firepit](images/Cookie's_Fire_Pit1.JPG)

1. Wash the vegetables by entering the river near Cookie and using [[Cookie's Vegetables]] from your inventory. {{waypoint -22, -123, 567}}
1. Roast the vegetables by clicking the smaller firepit just outside Cookies tent. {{waypoint -65, -116, 665}}
1. Wait a minute until you can click the fire pit again.
1. Put the vegetables in a bowl on the table right next to Cookie.
1. Speak to Cookie to complete the quest.
