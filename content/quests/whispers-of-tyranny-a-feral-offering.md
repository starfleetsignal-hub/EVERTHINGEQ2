## Steps

1. Speak to [[Hymnist Wadakha]] at {{waypoint -85.43, 31.12, -408.44}}
1. Meet [[Hymnist Wadakha]] in [[Shadeweaver Thicket]] at {{waypoint -325.71, 118.40, -131.80}}
1. Defeat 6 shadowed tourched grimlings like [[Grimling bloodsword]] or [[A shadow-touched grimling]].
1. When you are ready, speak again with him to begin the ceremony
1. Click the spot near him to throw offering into the depths of the falls
1. Speak with Sahteb Mahiini, The Feral Spirit
1. Speak again to [[Hymnist Wadakha]]
1. Return to [[Head Scholar Nabihan (City of Shar Vahl)]] at {{waypoint -161.64, 31.29, -269.77}}

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 56000 status
