*This quest is repeatable..*

## Prerequisites

- You must have +10,000 faction, but less than +40,000 faction, with the Court of Truth to unlock this quest.

## Steps

1. Acquire 5 silk strands from [[A dune tarantula]].
1. Acquire 5 masks from [[A Sandscrawler magus|Sandscrawler magus]].
1. Acquire 5 weapons from [[An Alliz Raef Ew scout]].
1. Return to [[Majah L'Asi]].

## Rewards

- At least 12g 57s 51c
- -3000 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- +3000 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -3000 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
- After the fifth time: [[Court of Truth Tapestry]]
- After the eight time: [[Court of Tears Tapestry]]
