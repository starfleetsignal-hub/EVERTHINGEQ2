## Steps

1. Light the torch next to [[Civean Il'Pernod]].
1. Light the torch at [[Lucan's Mount]], in [[The Commonlands]]. {{waypoint 18, -12, -739}}
1. Light the torch at [[N'Marr's Ascent]], in [[Nektulos Forest]]. {{waypoint -369, 45, -1253}}
1. Light the torch near [[The Ruins of Varsoon]], in [[Thundering Steppes]]. {{waypoint -690, 4, 690}}
1. Light the torch near [[The Firemyst Gully]], in [[Antonica]]. {{waypoint -1271.81, -1.20, 701.25}}
1. Return to Civean Il'Pernod.
1. Find and read the history of [[Solusek Ro]]. You can find the book in multiple places:
   - [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] in [[The City of Freeport]], on the second floor, on a small table. {{waypoint -0, 7, -119}}
   - [[Concordium Tower]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. Take the red portal and go down the first set of stairs. The book, which appears as an open book, is behind [[Wynia Vethe]] on a desk. {{waypoint 706, 42, 91}}
   - [[Oracle Tower]] in Antonica, on a stand, on the top floor. {{waypoint -1031, 27, -625}}
1. Return to Civean Il'Pernod.
