## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
  - I must harvest enough resources from the [[Karuupa Jungle]] to satisfy [[Duffin Brandywine|Duffin]] 0/100
- I must gather resources from around [[Karuupa Jungle]] to aid [[Duffin Brandywine]], who is "stuck" in [[Renfry's Basement]].

## Steps

1. I must gather some provisions for [[Duffin Brandywine]] from [[Karuupa Jungle]].
1. Return to [[Duggin Brandywine|Duffin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill Experience
- At least 1p 48g 19s 8c
- One of the following:
  - [[Forlorn Cleaver]]
  - [[Small Forlorn Plate]]
