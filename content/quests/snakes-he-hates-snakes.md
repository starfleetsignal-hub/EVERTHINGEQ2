*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Slay the following creatures in Feerrott:
   - 10 [[A swamp constrictor|swamp constrictors]] {{waypoint 25, -8, 549}}
   - 5 [[A brush constrictor|brush constrictors]] - mostly found with the jungle leapers, in the general surrounding area around {{waypoint 43, 12, 848}}, also less commonly in area around {{waypoint -42, -3, 415}} with the [[Brush nightstalkers|brush nightstalker]]. Alternatively, you can obtain these from the [[Cleansing of the Cove (Zone)|Cleansing of the Cove]] solo instance instead. 6 of them are right inside the entrance of that instance. (This requires that you finish [[Mashara Tiogran|Mashara]]'s quest line first.)
   - 5 [[A brush nightstalker|brush nightstalker]] {{waypoint -43, -3, 412}}
1. Return to Grimble to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 53s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Defender's Mantle of Fear]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Healer's Mantle of Fear]] (All Priests)
  - [[Medium's Mantle of Fear]] (All Mages)
  - [[Stalker's Mantle of Fear]] (All Scouts)
