## Notes

- This quest is offered to any player, regardless of moral alignment.

## Steps

1. Slay the following monsters:
   - 10 [[A searing scorpion|searing scorpions]] {{waypoint 130, -94, -416}}
   - 10 [[A sandstalker sabertooth|sandstalker sabertooths]] {{waypoint 502, -192, -692}}
1. Return to [[Master Akuno Beslin]].
1. Right click and 'meditate' on the meditation mat at {{waypoint 430, -192, -1584}}
1. Return to Master Akuno Beslin.

## Rewards

- +8500 faction with **The Ashen Order**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Curate's Plate Gloves of the Battlepriest]] ([[Inquisitor]], [[Templar]])
  - [[Dragoon's Gauntlets of the Weaponmaster]] (Plate Fighters)
  - [[Naturewalker's Leather Gloves of the Forestspeaker]] ([[Fury]], [[Warden]])
  - [[Sensei's Hand Wraps of the Fightmaster]] ([[Bruiser]], [[Monk]])
  - [[Spiritualist's Handguards of the Spiritcaller]] ([[Defiler]], [[Mystic]])
  - [[Thaumaturge's Gloves of the Archcaster]] (All Mages)
  - [[Vagabond's Chain Gloves of the Bloodsinger]] (All Scouts)
