## Prerequisites

- You must have started the quest [[When Darkness Comes]] or [[Eliminate Geraufl]] in order to complete this quest.

![](images/Secret_fireflies.jpg)

## Steps

1. Equip the [[A firefly net|firefly net]] Grepp gives you in one of your charm slots.
   - *It's no longer necessary to equip the charm. Pressing the icon shown at the quest helper works fine.*
   - *The firefly net will be removed if you leave the village. You will need to abandon the quest and speak to Grepp again to get another net.*
1. Roam the zone looking for fireflies, watching specifically for the message "You notice the gentle hum the fireflies." (Use ultravision, infravision or tinkering tools to help see them.)
1. When you see hear the fireflies (typically small swarms near trees throughout the zone), activate the net to catch them. You need to catch three swarms of them.
1. Speak with Grepp to complete the quest.
