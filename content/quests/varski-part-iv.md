This quest is part 4 of a 4-part quest series in the [[Fallen Gate Timeline]].

## Steps

*NOTE: Targok will not offer this quest until you have completed the quest, [[Averus, Part IV]].*

1. Kill a [[Tormented armorsmith]] in the [[Hate's Forge]] area of [[Fallen Gate]] to find the "big" money. It may take several kills before the quest will update.
1. Return to Varski.

## Rewards

- At least 8g 67s
