## Steps

1. Fly to the [[Vultak Scavenging Site]] to release the bot.
   1. NOTE: When you land at {{waypoint -625, 137, -71.09}} the bot will be automatically spawned for you. It is not something in your inventory or something for you to interact with.
1. Lead the bot to the portal to the [[Barren Sky]] {{waypoint -716, 145, -148}}. You have 2 minutes to complete this step.
1. Return to [[Bilgeron Cogsworth IV|Bilgeron]].
