## Steps

1. Enter Hold of Rime: [[Forgotten Pools]] and use the [[Sludge Collector]] to collect a sample of the sludge
   - Can be collected from the edge of the water, does not require going into the water
1. Kill Kyburs for their samples
1. Return to [[Tara Coldcarver]]
1. Pour the sludge samples into the bubbling cookpot next to [[Tara Coldcarver]] at {{waypoint 522, -357, 830}}
1. Return to [[Tara Coldcarver]]

## Rewards

- At least 41g 63s 17c
