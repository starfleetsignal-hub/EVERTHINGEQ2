## Steps

1. Gather 4 [[Sarnak bone|bones]] - found in the "chewed sarnak bones" (trackable) (around {{waypoint -418, 18, -1767}}) down the road![chewed sarnak bones (with human skull and rib cage?)](images/Chewed_sarnak_bones.jpg)
1. Craft 2 [[Bone Totem|bone totems]] at the Goblin Workbench.  Each combine requires 5 [[Thaumic Coal]].
1. Speak with [[Brappa]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +3,000 faction with **Broken Toe Clan**
- +3,000 faction with **Goblins of Nurga**
