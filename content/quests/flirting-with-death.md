## Steps

1. Use a city bell to enter [[The Graveyard]]
1. Speak to [[Adrianna (The Graveyard)|Adrianna]] {{waypoint -48, -9, 95}}
1. Kill 10 [[A ravenous zombie|ravenous zombies]] in the east area
1. Kill 10 [[A ravenous skeleton|ravenous skeletons]] in the north area
   - All are NPCs that aggro when you near them; even cons
1. Speak to [[Lerenth (The Graveyard)|Lerenth]], pathing north to south in the east (zombie) area
1. Speak to [[Adrianna (The Graveyard)|Adrianna]].
1. Speak to [[The Forgotten (The City of Freeport)|The Forgotten]] in [[West Freeport (POI)|West Freeport]].
