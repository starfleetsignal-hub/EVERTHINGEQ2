## Steps

1. Speak with [[Frau Choraline Lent]] {{waypoint -1128.28, -10.85, -464.36}}
1. Find the ancient mines of Khor Boldir to the northwest - Portal to Khor Boldir from [[Val'Zyroth Ness]] {{waypoint 108.34, 89.37, -1194.53}}. Zone inside to find [[Khor Boldir: Corrupted Burrows]] at {{waypoint 31.35, 62.21, 171.61}}
1. Brun should know what's going on {{waypoint 102.40, 47.12, 177.62}}
   - Receive Recipe Scroll: Brun's Smoke 'Splosions. Requires 1 Formless Kindling.
   - I need to find a small amount of sulfur {{waypoint 70.99, 43.77, 174.39}}
   - I need to find a glass vessel {{waypoint 90.29, 43.52, 183.96}}
   - I need to find a chunk of flint {{waypoint 67.98, 44.05, 168.72}}
   - Craft the smoke 'splosions with the mortar and pestle {{waypoint 100.80, 43.15, 184.60}}}
1. Speak to Brun. Use a smoke s'plosion in Khor Boldir. They work - find Quibble {{waypoint 120.35, 56.32, 178.80}}
   - The smoke 'splosions have a timer of ~1 min. Move fast and try to stop in areas without mobs to recast when needed. There is orange text that warns they will wear off soon.
1. Find the key to the cage and unlock the cage door. {{waypoint 194.84, 43.60, 139.36}}
1. Speak with [[Quibble]].
1. Return to [[Frau Choraline Lent]] in [[Val'Zyroth Ness]].
