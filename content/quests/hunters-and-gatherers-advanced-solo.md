## Description

*Somewhere within the great caverns of the Stygian Threshold rests the spirits of some of Thalumbra's fiercest hunters and gatherers. They have been known to still prey and feast on the living. The citizens of Maldura request I venture into the cavern and stop these spirits.*

## Zone

- Defeat targets within [[Stygian Threshold: The Howling Gateway (Advanced Solo)]]

## Steps

1. Kill the gatherer
   - [[Gagrash]]
1. Slay the hunter
   - [[Hagrash]]
1. Banish the alpha
   - [[Bagarash]]
