## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- Items must be harvested in either [[The Sinking Sands]], [[The Pillars of Flame]], or [[The Lesser Faydark]]. Traded or purchased items will not update the quest.
- Fish does not count towards the foodstuffs requirement.
- This quest seems to cause some conflict with the quest [[A Gathering Obsession, Part VI]], as both are based on harvesting tier 6 nodes and both are based on your crafting level. If you are currently on both quests, [[Desserts from the Desert]] will update first.

## Steps

1. Harvest 100 food items, including: artichoke, darjeeling tea leaf, Maj'Dul coffee bean, and prickly pear from shrubs; and sabertooth meat and caiman meat from dens.
1. Forest 100 [[Severed sandalwood]] from tree nodes.
1. Return to [[Duggin Brandywine]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 3s 7c
- One of the following:
  - [[A small wooden bowl]]
  - [[Wooden table setting]]
