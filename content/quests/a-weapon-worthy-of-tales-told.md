## Requirements

- Must be level 9 of one [[Ascension]] class to start the quest

## Steps

1. Find a place that is suffused with ancient planar energies
   - zone into *(do not CoV)* [[Arcanna'se Spire: Forgotten Sanctum (Solo)]] or [[Arcanna'se Spire: Forgotten Sanctum (Heroic)|(Heroic)]] - *a cleared zone works*
1. Plunge the empowered weapon into Pyre of Destruction in the fire room and you will be ported to a [[A Planar Fragment]].
1. You will fight your doppelganger.
   - Get aggro on all the adds and keep them off the NPC's, kill your doppelganger
1. Once you defeat yourself, click to exit the planar zone and it returns you to Forgotten Sanctum.
1. Click the Pyre of Destruction again to receive your Fabled Epic 2.0
