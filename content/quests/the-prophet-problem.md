## Steps

1. Travel to [[Prophet's Peak]] {{waypoint 702, -88, -944}} and kill the following:
   - 15 [[A desert lunatic (Pillars of Flame)|desert lunatics]]
   - 15 [[A desert prophet|desert prophets]]
1. Return to [[Sergeant Tuskin]].

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Swiftrider Mercenaries**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Faydark Apprentice's Truncheon]] (All Priests)
  - [[Faydark Flame Dagger]] (All Mages)
  - [[Flame Assassin's Burning Blade]] (All Scouts except Beastlord)
  - [[Pitmaster's Blade]] (Plate Fighters)
  - [[Sceptre of the Kelethin Magus]] (All Mages)
  - [[Spring's Scimitar]] ([[Fury]], [[Warden]])
  - [[Ulak of the Uncontestable Master]] ([[Beastlord]], [[Bruiser]], [[Monk]])
