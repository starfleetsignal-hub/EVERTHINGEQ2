You start this is in the [[Pillars of Flame]], which you access by going to the [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]], then zoning into [[The Pillars of Flame|Pillars of Flame]] from any of the access points. The smart thing is to go to the Docks in the [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]] and take a carpet-ride to the [[Camp D'Sas|Camp D'Sas Station]], then zone into [[Pillars of Flame]] to get you close to the first step..
