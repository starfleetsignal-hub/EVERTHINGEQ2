## Steps

1. Kill the following
   - Destroy 14 luclinite clusters for arcane shards.
     - *Luclinite clusters are over the water all around the Luclinite Chunk {{waypoint 273, -225, -272}}.*
   - Hunt 12 Bloody Tooth remnants for finger bones.
     - *Near the Luclinite Chunk shoreline, around {{waypoint 221, -242, -231}}.*
1. Return to [[Merith Iliqirelle]] {{waypoint -239, 57, -236}}
