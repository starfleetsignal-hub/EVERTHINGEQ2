This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Capture 6 stink scarabs on [[Caiman Beach]] near the dock {{waypoint -1400, -227, -490}} or on the grassy patches around [[Twin Tears]] {{waypoint 400, -120, -676}} and {{waypoint -1138, -211, -237}}.
1. Destroy two void tempests anywhere on Norrath and collect their [[Void Storm Shred]].
1. Return to Researcher Aillena.

## Rewards

- 3 [[D.I.R.T.Y. Money]]
