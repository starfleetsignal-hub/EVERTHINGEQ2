## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key.
   - Kill 15 [[A sand scourge|sand scourges]] around {{waypoint -192.84, 84.92, -534.68}} in [[Sandstone Delta]] at [[Saltscorch Steppes]].
1. I need to find the Falydia Music Box key elsewhere.
1. Go to [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]].
1. Find the Falydia Music Box key clue from [[A cacti abhorrence|cacti abhorrences]] around {{waypoint -46.81, -0.03, 133.95}} in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]] at [[Sunscar Vale]] and [[Desert of Woe]]. It can also drop from mantraps, though the quest itself is for abhorrences exclusively.
1. Examine the clue to receive the quest [[The Falydia Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the **Falydia Music Box** in [[Sandstone Delta]].
