## Steps

1. Enter [[The Willow Wood: Besieged Hamlet]] and near {{waypoint 810, -21, -563}} and...
   - Kill 8 attacking Rockpaw gnolls ([[A Rockpaw destroyer|Rockpaw destroyers]] and [[A Rockpaw scavenger|scavengers]] update)
   - Extinguish 5 fires that have been set (just left-click them)
   - Free Shireen's parents from their captors - kill the two [[A Rockpaw captor|Rockpaw captors]]
1. Return to Shireen Faren-Delnara

## Rewards

- At least 2g 20s 65c (at level 46)
