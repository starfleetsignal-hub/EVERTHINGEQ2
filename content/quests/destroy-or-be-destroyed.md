An arrow with a message reads: Should this reach anyone, please, by all that you hold dear, I bid you stand! Do what must be done, destroy the Scourge destructors, before all is lost!

## Notes

- *This is a 5 minute timed quest.*
- Found in all versions of Zek, the Scourge Wastes:
  - [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Solo)]]
  - [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Heroic)]]

## Steps

1. Kill 20 (solo) or 35 (heroic) [[A Scourge destructor|Scourge destructors]], located throughout [[Siege Valley]], before time runs out.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +150 faction with **The Green Hoods**
- [[Encrusted iron key]]
