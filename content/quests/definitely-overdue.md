## Description

During a recent raid of the League of Freethinkers' hideout, several Compendiums of knowledge were stolen by agents of the Libant. These tomes are currently stashed away in the vaults of Ravenscale Repository. I have been tasked with recovering these Compendiums.

## Steps

1. Find all 6 of the League of Freethinker Compendiums within [[Ravenscale Repository]]
   - See the talk page for known locations. The books no longer respawn.
   - The books are orange-red in color, standing right-side-up on their spines and spawn on tables and bookshelves.
1. Return to [[Tanya Alvernon]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
