## Steps

1. Go to [[Esoterra Gardens]] {{waypoint -624, 42, 785}} and engage a sempiternal stoneripper.
   - You cannot defeat it. Run away and return to [[Hetii]].
1. Collect 5 [[Burning ore]] from the [[Aetherscar]] ![](images/Burning-ore-aetherscar.jpg)
   - {{waypoint 672, -34, -81}} {{waypoint 715, -26, -175}}   {{waypoint 657, -39, -222}}x2  {{waypoint 644, -39, -188}} .
1. Return to [[Hetii]].
1. Confront the stoneripper once more with the [[A Glowing Flute]].
1. Return to [[Hetii]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 93g 34s 70c
- [[Singing Cincture]]
