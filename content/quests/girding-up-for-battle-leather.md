## Steps

1. Examine your [[A fine leather pattern]] and accept the quest
1. Visit the City Quartermaster in Qeynos or Freeport depending on your home town.  They are located near the guild registrars.  Turn in your armor mold and choose which piece you want.

## Rewards

One of the following:

- [[Dupione Silkworm Hood]]
- [[Dupione Silkworm Fistwraps]]
- [[Dupione Silkworm Slippers]]
- [[Leafwrapped Circlet of Tranquility]]
- [[Leafwrapped Gloves of Tranquility]]
- [[Leafwrapped Boots of Tranquility]]
