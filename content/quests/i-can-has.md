## Steps

1. Head to the Ration Hut at {{waypoint 1327, 48, 2059}} and obtain the following items:
   - bread (on the table) ![All the food you need ...](images/I_Can_Has..._quest_-_food_table.jpg)
   - lettuce (on the table)
   - ground meat (in a bucket on the floor next to the table)
   - sliced cheese (on the table)
1. Return to Hitus Sharpclaw.

- At least 46g
