## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of Tunare has asked for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Gather the requested supplies
   - Collect 3 [[Cinder Bark|cinder bark]] found in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]]
     - *Note: drops in [[Chests|small chest]] from a scorched shambler (found in the Smeltfield Vale area)*
     - *1-2 drops per group member*
   - Gather or purchase [[Breadfruit]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas
     - *Common harvest from BoZ aether shrub*
     - *Can buy off broker*
     - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to [[Herald of the Mother of All]] {{waypoint 154, -23, 3}} in [[The Lesser Faydark|Lesser Faydark]]
