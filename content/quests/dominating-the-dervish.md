## Steps

1. Slay dervish in the Sprawl:
   - 2 [[A Dervish crook|Dervish crooks]] {{waypoint -101, -4, 112}}
   - 4 [[A Dervish hoodlum|Dervish hoodlums]] {{waypoint -81, -4, 53}}
   - 4 [[A Dervish hooligan|Dervish hooligans]] {{waypoint -86, 0, -12}}
1. Return to Enforcer Kurdek to receive your reward. {{waypoint -83.48, -4.74, -36.21}}

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
- One of the following:
  - [[Earring of Enforcement]]
  - [[Boots of Dominance (Level 1)|Boots of Dominance]]
  - [[Sleeves of Enforcement]]
  - [[Wand of Dominance]]
  - [[Kurdek's Fistwraps]]
- +300 faction with **The Dreadnaughts**
