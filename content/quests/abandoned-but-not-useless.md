1. Harvest 6 boughs of firewood from the ruins of the [[Abandoned Kejek Village]].
   - Some locations (reusble, after a short cooldown) include:
   - {{waypoint -1044, 534, 507}}
   - {{waypoint -1048, 532, 541}}
   - {{waypoint -1090, 533, 585}}
1. Light the campfires for visiting sojourners.
   - First campfire: {{waypoint -918, 481, 384}}
   - Second campfire: {{waypoint -905, 479, 348}}
   - Third campfire: {{waypoint -921, 482, 398}}
1. Return to [[Lionnaeus]].
