## Notes

I came across a small orb of light floating innocuously in the air within The Nexus Core.  I reached out to touch it, and noticed a wave of warmth come from the ball of energy.  It shuddered in the air for a moment, then darted off toward the entrance of the Nexus.  There may be more of them around the Nexus Core, if I want to find out more about them.
