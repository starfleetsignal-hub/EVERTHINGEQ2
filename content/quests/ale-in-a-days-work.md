## Note

- The set of items you need to find can be done in any order.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]] at {{waypoint -654, 132, 953}}.
1. Gather 6 **feysteel keg coupler**![feysteel keg coupler](images/Feysteel_keg_coupler.jpg) or **feysteel beer hose**![feysteel beer hose](images/Feysteel_beer_hose.jpg) in [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]].
   - *Note: These will be on the ground and inside buildings, and have a white particle effect.*
1. Gather 6 **incarnadine keg pumps**![incarnadine keg pump](images/Incarnadine_keg_pump.jpg) or**  incarnadine gauge regulators** ![incarnadine gauge regulator](images/Incarnadine_gauge_regulator.jpg) at [[Dropship Landing Zone]] in [[Moors of Ykesha]] at {{waypoint 1760, 455, 766}}.
   - *Note: They have a golden particle effect and spawn randomly on both levels of Dropship Landing Zone, on counter tops or metal floors.*
   - Note: *If you look at the Map, you will see this Quest showing a feather-icon in the [[Moors of Ykesha]] (Lower). You can ignore that. All of the Pumps/Regulators can be found lying around in various places on the landing-zone itself.*
1. Return to the [[Bar of Brell]] and repair [[Buzzed]].
1. Talk to [[Snoogle Blitzed]].
1. Go to [[Timorous Deep]] and collect 8 ***Haoaera peppers*** at [[Haoaera]] {{waypoint 1807, 127, -363}}.
   - *Note: They look like Ivy Plants growing on the sides of rocks, pillars, and trees in this area. They have faint green glow particle effects.*![Haoaera Pepper at the side of a tree](images/HaoaeraPepper.jpg)
1. Go to [[Enchanted Lands]] and 6 kill [[A defiled farmer|defiled farmers]] near [[Berrybrook Field]] {{waypoint 530, -4, -319}} for ***Seamist malt***.
   - *Note: Like in other events, defiled farmers are non-aggro until approached, then become con to your level.
1. Return to the [[Bar of Brell]] and refill [[Buzzed]] with ale ingredients.

## Rewards

- [[Gear Grease Porter (Alcoholic)]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Flying Kegerator]]
  - 8 [[Beer Token|Beer Tokens]]
