## Steps

1. Find and Destroy the enchanted ceremonial drum at {{waypoint -160, -408, -133}}. To get to this location, you will need to enter the caves at {{waypoint -277, -335, -78}}. You will come to a fork at {{waypoint -330, -357, -170}}, go left. At {{waypoint -306, -369, -245}}, there will be an entrance. Go in and down the rope and through the tunnels till you get to the drum {{waypoint -159, -407, -132}}.
1. Kill the [[An Enraged Tizmak|Enraged Tizmaks]] that spawn.
1. Speak with [[Zalyn D'Kilned]].

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- -10,000 faction with **Tizmak Clan**
- [[Tizmak Barrel]]
