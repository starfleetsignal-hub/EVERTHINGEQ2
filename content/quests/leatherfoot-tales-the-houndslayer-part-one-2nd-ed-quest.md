## Notes

- If you accidently destroyed the quest starter, speak with Deputy Stoutgut again and he will give you another copy.

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A carnivorous hound|carnivorous hounds]] in [[Rivervale]]
   - Known Locations: {{waypoint -24, 0, 57}}, {{waypoint -41, 0, -27}} and {{waypoint -123, 0, 120}}
1. Examine the book.
1. Slay 15 [[A rabid jaggedtooth|rabid jaggedteeth]] in Rivervale
   - Known Locations: {{waypoint 30, -22, 19}}, {{waypoint -182, -7, 66}} and {{waypoint -16, -14, 9}}
1. Examine the book.
1. Slay 20 [[A blightrat avenger|blightrat avengers]] in Rivervale. {{waypoint -103, -4, -164}}
1. Examine the book.
