## Steps

1. Speak to [[Erus Dal'viv]] {{waypoint 9.71, 0.57, 4.14}}in [[The Fortress of Drunder]]
1. Speak to [[Vilbrog Wrathwind]] in [[The Fortress of Drunder]]
1. Prove yourself in the [[Spire of Rage]]:
   1. Defeat 15 Sullonites
   1. Speak to [[Dalrinai Heartrage]], beyond the first doorway at {{waypoint -3, 102, 99}}
   1. Defeat the Daiku Warleader, [[Grolla Skullwielder]]
   1. Return to [[Dalrinai Heartrage]] in the [[Spire of Rage]]
1. Prove yourself in the [[Strategist's Stonghold]]:
   1. Speak with [[Ellrien Silverblade]] at {{waypoint 16, 0, 93}}
   1. Defeat 12 Avazeks and collect their feathers
   1. Defeat [[Warmaster Korok Hai]]
   1. Speak with [[Ellrien Silverblade]] another time
1. Finally, prove yourself in [[Tower of Tactics]]:
   1. Defeat [[Arch-Bishop Rektor]]
   1. Defeat the Daiku Wolf Mistress, [[Deynka Packlasher]]
1. Return to [[Erus Dal'viv|Erus]] in Drunder.
1. Speak with the Duality in Thurgadin {{waypoint 475.38, -211.64, 245.68}}.

## Rewards

- At least 48g 35s 24c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Campaigner's Drape of Victory]]
  - [[Cloak of Woven Brilliance]]
  - [[War Minister's Cloak of Glory]]
  - [[Silver-threaded Tactician's Drape]]

Also allows you to purchase Class specific red adornments from Maayla A'nadir in Thurgadin.
