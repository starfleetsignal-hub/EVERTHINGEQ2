## Notes

A repeatable quest for Faction. Repeatable up to 3 times and only if your Port Woe faction is less than 20,000. This quest requires:

- 4 [[Crowberry]]
- 4 [[Ptarmigan Meat]]
- 8 [[Red Lichen]]
- 8 [[Sedge Root]]
- 40 [[Zimaran Kindling]]
- [[Recipe Scroll: Appetizing Edibles|Recipe]] for [[Flavorful Food]] and [[Delectable Drink]] auto scribed upon accepting quest.

## Steps

1. Craft Food and Drink
   - 4 [[Flavorful Food|Flavorful Foods]]
   - 4 [[Delectable Drink|Delectable Drinks]]
1. Speak with [[Polonia]] at {{waypoint 17.06, 5.72, 373.41}}

## Rewards

- +1,000 faction with **Port Woe**
- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
