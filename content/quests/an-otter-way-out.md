## Steps

1. Find a way to free [[Miyau]] by doing the following"
   1. Speak with [[A Ry'Gorr guard]] right next to [[Miyau]]. He tell's you that he is hungry and could eat a small mammoth.
   1. Kill [[A woolly mammoth calf]] near {{waypoint -37, -350, 3279}} and loot [[Mammoth Calf Meat]] from the body.
   1. Return the [[A Ry'Gorr guard]] right next to [[Miyau]] and give him the [[Mammoth Calf Meat]].
1. Escort [[Miyau]] to [[Nipik's Haven]] {{waypoint -1205.62, -536.56, 3439.05}}in [[Eastern Wastes]].
   1. Speak with Miyau so she starts to follow you
   1. Run or fly to [[Nipik's Haven]] to update the quest
1. Speak with [[Miyau]] at {{waypoint -1205, -536, 3442}} She can take up to 5 minutes to spawn, just wait for her.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
