## Steps

1. Salvage and gather the following
   - Salvage old fishing net glass floaters from the water around the dock.
   - Obtain water piercer oil by killing [[A water piercer]] fish in the deeper water areas around {{waypoint -210, 3, 442}} and {{waypoint -293.81, 2.49, 859.95}}
1. Speak to [[Adris Spiritshine (Port Woe)|Adris Spiritshine]]
1. Place the hope orb light in the Dock Keeper's Hut at {{waypoint -90, 9.3, 348}}
   - Click the three yellow orbs on the wall. (These may sometimes be difficult to see from certain angles)
1. Speak with the dock keeper, [[Kober Botley]].
1. Place hope orb lights at the Port Pavilion at {{waypoint -13.03, 6.05, 380.24}}
   - The *three yellow orb placing location* is hanging from a rafter in front of a post.
   - The position for the Orb Light is visible on a post of the Port Pavilion and is visible after being placed.
1. Place hope orb lights at the Port Trades Building at {{waypoint 15.70, 5.74, 355.02}}
   - The location is on the inside wall to the right of the crafting station. ￼
1. Speak to [[Tristyl Ashwood]].
1. Place hope orb lights outside the port elder's hut at {{waypoint -12.08, 5.68, 404.72}}
1. Return to [[Adris Spiritshine (Port Woe)|Adris Spiritshine]].

## Rewards

- At least 4p 36g 48s 99c
- 3 x [[Hope Orb Lights]] house item
