## Steps

1. Pick 8 woodsun flowers in Willow Wood.
1. Return to Aelis, who will ask you to take the [[Tribute for Ulinir|completed tribute]] to [[Davyn Thornbranch]].
1. Speak with Davyn Thornbranch.

## Rewards

- At least 1s
- [[Feir'Dal Tribute Leaf]]
- [[A Bouquet of Woodsun Flowers]] - house item
