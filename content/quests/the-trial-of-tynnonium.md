## Steps

1. Enter [[Sebilis]] and go to the [[Training Hall and Study]]: {{waypoint 90, -11, 174}}
1. Complete the following tasks:
   - Defeat 4 [[A Broken Shackle initiate|Broken Shackle initiate]]s
   - Defeat the [[A Broken Shackle instructor|Broken Shackle instructor]] or [[Grand Master Sathaz]]
1. Speak with the [[Swifttail Trialmaster]] back in [[The Tabernacle of Pain]]. {{waypoint -73, -43, 160}}
1. Enter the ring and defeat [[Grandmaster Raakaz]]. {{waypoint -87, -42, 164}}
1. Return to Grandmaster Raakaz

## Rewards

- +7,500 faction with **Tabernacle of Pain**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Shackle of Tynnonium (Fighter)]]
  - [[Shackle of Tynnonium (Mage)]]
  - [[Shackle of Tynnonium (Priest)]]
  - [[Shackle of Tynnonium (Scout)]]
- Prefix [[Shackle Grandmaster|Title: "Shackle Grandmaster"]]
- Suffix [[Of the Tynnonium Shackle|Title: "of the Tynnonium Shackle"]]
