## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 50 [[Celestial Coal]]
- 36 [[Reef cluster]]
- 75 [[Strangler root]]
- (You can have these materials on you already or purchase them from the Broker)

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Temry Loneivy (Myrist)|Temry Loneivy]] at {{waypoint 27.36, -20.10, -73.71}} in the  [[Myrist Conservatory]].
1. Collect the following:
   - Destroy (attack) green vases and right-click to "Pick up the Pieces" from 3 vases for [[Shard of Broken Pottery|pottery components]]. ![green vase](images/Green_vase.jpg)
     - *Note: The vases can be found along the edges of the [[Myrist Conservatory]] and [[Myrist Foyer]].*
   - 3 [[Vial of Sticky Sap|vials of sap]] found in the [[Myrist Conservatory]]![vial of sap](images/Vial_of_sap.jpg)
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Box of Newly Formed Pottery]] and craft a [[Box of Newly Formed Pottery]] on a [[Work Bench]]
   - There is a [[Work Bench]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]]. Go to the second floor [[South Wing Catalog]], then the teleporter to the [[Smiths' Gallery]] at {{waypoint 388, 16, 170}}.
   - There is also a [[Work Bench]] just inside the zone [[Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wind]]
1. Speak to [[Alamiri Lorfaere]] at {{waypoint 59, -20, -92}} in the [[Myrist Conservatory]].
1. Go to the [[North Wing Stacks]] on the ground floor of the Library, then use the teleporter at {{waypoint -259, -20, -284}} to go to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]]. Enter [[Vegarlson, the Earthen Badlands]] through the portal at {{waypoint 775, 412, -335}} and speak with [[Temry Loneivy (Vegarlson)|Temry Loneivy]] at {{waypoint -356, 41, -386}}.

## Rewards

- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
