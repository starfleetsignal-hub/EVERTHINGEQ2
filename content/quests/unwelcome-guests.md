## Description

"Agarighus would like me to help him move the young shrumbler worms away from the proto-caplings in the geranariums to the east. I will need to capture the shrumblers using agarighus' shrumbler net. Once I've captured five, I should take them to their natural habitat with the large shrumblers in the north eastern section of the caves."   -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Use the [[Shrumbler Net]] on 5 [[Young shrumbler|young shrumblers]] in the [[Dyer Mycoria Germinarium: North East|Dyer Mycoria Germinariums]] (cave off [[Shroomba Subterrane]] at {{waypoint -134, -45, -192}}
1. Release them in the Northeast passages around {{waypoint -4.16, -38.37, -345.87}}
1. Return to [[Agarighus]]
