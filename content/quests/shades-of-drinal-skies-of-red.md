## Steps

1. I should seek out the creature known as Gulthga in the Fearcreep, and see if I can help.
   - Seek out the creature known as [[Gulthga (NPC)|Gulthga]] {{waypoint -1349, 218, 606}} in the Fearcreep
1. I have found Gulgutha, which turned out to be a creature of Fear - an amygdalian! However, he says he also needs help figuring out what has transpired here in Ethernere. I should do what I can to assist him for now.
   - Help Gulthga find what is happening in the Fearcreep and Complete Gulthga's questline:
     - [[Fear Itself]]
     - [[Thule vs. Thule]]
     - [[Fortunes Turned]]
     - [[And His Name Is...]]
   - Speak with the creature known as [[Yulsluu]], and see if you can assist him and complete his questline:
     - [[Open Gates]]
   - Help [[Alzilius]] with his questline:
     - [[Unexpected Ally]]
     - [[Lost Keys]]
     - [[Close It Behind You]]
1. Speak to [[Drinal's Steward]] near the second Tourbillion in Fearcreep near {{waypoint -2400, 201, 734}}
1. Speak to Gulthga.
1. Kill [[Gulthga (Mob)|Gulthga]].
1. Go to the Fear Gate, but don't enter, until you receive an update. The quest will then say "Place Gulthga's remains on the stone...".
1. Now enter the [[Throne of Fear: The Fear Gate]].
   - This is a forced Advanced-solo zone, group members beyond two will be given 5 seconds and kicked out.
1. Click the stone at {{waypoint 168, 72, 106}}. If duoing both must click the stone *before* the next step.
1. Kill [[The Effigy of Fear (Quest Instance Boss)|The Effigy of Fear]].
1. Return to [[Drinal's Steward]] near Tourbillion at {{waypoint -2401, 200, 734}}
