## Steps

1. Visit the following locations in [[Skyfire Mountains]] for signs of a Order of Rime presence:
   - [[The Temple of the Red Lord]] {{waypoint -2306, 283, 345}}
   - [[The Temple of the White Lady]] {{waypoint -2459, 225, 1395}}
1. Return to [[Zixka Slizzis]] at the [[Upper Command Camp]].

- one of the following
  - [[Warslick Pouch]]
  - [[Guardian's Ornate Bow]] (Paladin)
  - [[Wolftamer's Wand]]
  - [[Dragonslaying Healer's Staff of Scales]]
  - [[Bow of the Jarsath Stalker]]
- At least 41g 63s 17c
- +1000 faction with **Legion of Danak**
