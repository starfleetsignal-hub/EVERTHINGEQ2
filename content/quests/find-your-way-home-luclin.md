## Steps

1. Obtain the Seed of Water in the [[City of Shar Vahl]] lagoon underwater {{waypoint 284.32, 0.26, 58.75}}.
1. Obtain the Seed of Wind in[[Savage Weald]] on the [[Savage Glimpse]] {{waypoint -734.84, 298.35, -829.21}}.
1. Obtain the Seed of Fire in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] inside a log by [[Firefall Rim]] at {{waypoint 259.13, 68.10, 510.20}}.
1. Obtain the Seed of Earth in [[Echo Caverns]] at [[Sods and Bends]] {{waypoint 257.12, 16.35, -355.72}}.
1. Speak to [[Master Ward Singer Oye]] in [[City of Shar Vahl]].
1. Enter [[City of Shar Vahl: The Chorale]] {{waypoint 169.59, 70.44, 263.28}}.
1. Speak to Master Oye on the [[Tiger's Paw]] {{waypoint 197.37, 74.76, 124.45}}.
1. Click the four ward totems around Master Oye.
1. Click the soul chamber in the center of the [[Tiger's Paw]].
1. Speak to Master Oye again.
1. Return to [[City of Shar Vahl]] and speak to [[Animist Sanura]] {{waypoint -260.61, 34.81, -54.07}}.
