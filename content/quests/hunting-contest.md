This quest is repeatable., as long as the guide is there to give and complete it.

## Starting the Quest

Guide quests can start in any city or zone in the game.  For this quest, the guide will speak of the thrill of the hunt.  After a bit of roleplaying you will be offered the quest with the following Description text:

*I met a great hunter and collector, who asked for my assistance in meeting his quota of animal hides for trade.*

## Steps

- Kill furred beasts (bears, wolves, lions, etc.) for corpse dropped pelts.
- The pelts must be from hunting animals and not acquired by trapping. (Tag must be Uncommon instead of No-Value.)
- Return to the guide and trade at least 5 pelts with him to gain your reward.
