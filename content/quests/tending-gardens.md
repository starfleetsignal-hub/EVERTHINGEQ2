## Notes

- requires 34,000 [[House Yrzu (Faction)]]

## Steps

1. Collect the following
   - 5 suffused peat in the [[Drukyna Marsh]] near {{waypoint -653, -49, 51}} around the base of rocks.
     - Small piles of dirt.  Spark effect, but can't see it well from a distance. ![](images/Suffused_peat.jpg)
   - 5 enriched soil in the [[Esoterra Gardens]] near {{waypoint -610, 51, 707}} around the shores of the pools. ![](images/Enriched_soil.jpg)
     - Small glowing gold piles of dirt.
1. Return to [[Silvele]] at {{waypoint 244, 588, -679}}.

## Rewards

- At least 62g 37s
- -1250 faction with **House Vahla**
- +1250 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1250 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
