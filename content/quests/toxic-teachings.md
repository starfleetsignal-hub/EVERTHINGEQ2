## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A toxic leech|toxic leeches]] (which hatch from "a wretched egg" when you approach them) in [[Toxxulia's Reach]] {{waypoint 479, 145, 2967}}.
   - *Note: Killing all [[A monstrous shoot|a monstrous shoot]] (mantrap looking thing) in the area will spawn [[The Toxic Brood Mother]] and with her spawns live toxic leeches in the same places as the eggs.*
1. Return to [[Matron T'Akinu]].

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +2000 faction with **Hua Mein**
