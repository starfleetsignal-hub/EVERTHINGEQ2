## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[Drowned footsoldier|drowned footsoldiers]].
   - The Troopers and Lieutenants are placeholders for this mob.  All are ^^^ heroic lvl 21-24.
   - The most efficient method to spawn these monsters is to travel back and forth between {{waypoint 19, -21, -270}} and {{waypoint 422, -13, 27}}, killing all "drowned" skeletons.
     - Drowned skeletons can also be found around {{waypoint 1181, -24, 1052}}. level 24^^^ heroic and up.
   - **Note**: It's a level 22 quest, but most mobs are 22-25, and hit pretty hard when you're level 22. Also quite a few elites kicking around!

## Rewards

- At least 17s 43c
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Armband of the Centaur Apothecary]] (All Priests)
  - [[Armband of the Centaur Stormer]] (All Mages)
  - [[Armband of the Centaur Archer]] (All Scouts)
