## Steps

1. Kill [[Tairiza the Widow Mistress]] in [[Tomb of Thuuga]].
1. Kill [[Venril Sathir]] in [[Venril Sathir's Lair]].
1. Kill [[Druushk]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]] and then move towards body to receive "a charged spirit siphon". (Killing Leviathan is no longer needed to enter VP).
   - You actually have to go in the room where Druushk is to begin with (the room he is in before you pull him) to find his ghost for your update.
1. Kill [[Orinox]] in Skyfire (instance zone-in via a rock on the ground: {{waypoint -1921, 273, 220}}.
   - This is a solo (^-con) fight.
   - You can not be grouped with anyone but a Fury who is on the same step in order to zone in, or even see that the rock is clickable to zone in.
   - Solo Encounter NOTE: Use an Elemental or an Arcane Resist Potion taken prior to fight to assist in avoiding some of the stuns. Also re-spec'd Fury AA line to be DPS Mainly in INT WIS STA (STA for the resist Stifle). Root his pet while using Snare on him to kite him around the zone while dps/dot/melee help to kill.

## Rewards

- [[Wrath of Nature (Mythical)]]
