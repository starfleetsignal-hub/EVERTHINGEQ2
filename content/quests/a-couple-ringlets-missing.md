## Prerequisites

- You must know the [[Serilian]] language to progress in this quest.

## Steps

1. Examine the poster at {{waypoint -25, 28, -127}}. You will not be able to interact with it if you do not speak [[Serilian]].
   - Lure out [[Captain Bangboom]] by completing a ring event in the East Tower of the [[Bloodforge Gate]].
   - Lure out [[Captain Powboom]] by completing a ring event in the West Tower of the Bloodforge Gate.
     - Complete the ring events by wiping out all three floors. After this, more goblins will spawn on the 1st and 2nd floors. Once this wave is defeated, the Captain of that tower should spawn up on either the 2nd or top floor.
1. Go to the [[Boombottom Strongbox]] and insert the two rings.

## Rewards

- Your choice of one of the following:
  - [[Crundom Helm]]
  - [[Crundom Gloves]]
  - [[Steelguard hoop]]
