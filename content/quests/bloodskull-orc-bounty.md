*Attention all Freeportians! By order of the Overlord, all able bodied citizens must aid Freeport by removing the Bloodskull orcs from our outlying lands. It is your duty as a citizen of Freeport to stop them before they infest our land. Take down their clan before they organize against all Norrathians.*

## Steps

1. Kill [[Bloodskull warrior|Bloodskull warriors]] until you collect 20 of their special earrings {{waypoint -1084, -48, 594}}.
1. Take the earrings to [[Captain Vrondell]] at [[The Freeport Militia House]] in [[West Freeport]]. 141 .5 124.5

## Rewards

- At least 23s 16c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Polished Bracelet of the Buccaneer]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Polished Bracelet of Rage]] (All Priests)
  - [[Polished Bracelet of the Militia]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Polished Bracelet of the Academy]] (All Mages)
