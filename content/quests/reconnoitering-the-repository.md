## Description

The League of Freethinkers' scouts are having a difficult time investigating the vaults within Ravenscale Repository due to the presence of the ever-vigilant Repository Guardians. I've agreed to dispatch of all the guardians so that the scouts may reconnoiter the repository unhindered.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Ravenscale Repository]] and dispatch 8 of the [[A repository guardian|Repository Guardians]]
   - Guardians can be found at the following locations:
     - [[Reliquary of the Drachnid]] at {{waypoint -163.06, 14.24, -184.77}}
     - [[Reliquary of the Drelock]] at {{waypoint 163.57, 14.24, -193.50}}
     - [[Reliquary of the Libant]] at {{waypoint 129.92, 14.20, -242.75}}
     - [[Theatre of the Diabolist]] downstairs at {{waypoint 16.82, 4.14, -316.34}} and {{waypoint -27.38, 4.14, -263.99}}, on the gallery at {{waypoint -40.62, 16.12, -334.50}} and {{waypoint -43.10, 16.10, -333.81}}
     - [[Reliquary of the Lifeguide]] at {{waypoint -81.99, 18.20, -392.02}}
