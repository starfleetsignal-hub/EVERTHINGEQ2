## Steps

1. Kill animals for their hearts
   - 4 [[A sea angler|sea anglers]] or [[A striped sharpfin|striped sharpfin]]
   - 4 [[A Ghorkaal shadowswift|Ghorkaal shadowswifts]]
   - 4 [[A wood spider|wood spiders]]
1. Speak to [[Heathryn Icepike]] on [[Wanderer's Dock]]
1. Return to Waulon
