## Steps

![Avatar of Forgotten](images/Avatar_of_the_Forgotten.jpg)

1. Head towards the [[Isle of Mara]] and zone into [[The Mystic Lake]]. From there, head to [[The Forsaken City]] located at {{waypoint -263.97, -40.44, 48.86}}. There is a climbing wall between the two statues that leads you to the gateway to The Forsaken City, under water. Turn to the right and follow the stairs all the way down to the entrance, then the waypoint will lead you. Be aware, this is underwater, so you will need to have some form of underwater breathing item to get there. When you're inside the city, head to the Plunderer Base Camp and click the bookcase located at {{waypoint 51.51, -50.83, -26.52}}.
1. Now you have the book, head to the [[Loping Plains]] to start to kill vampires and ghouls.
   - Kill 12 vampires, such as those just inside [[Castle Mistmoore]]
   - Kill 12 ghouls, such as those found around {{waypoint -353.74, 30.75, 209.66}}, {{waypoint -99.32, 49.59, 142.45}}, or {{waypoint -23.32, 48.86, -197.67}}.
1. Once you have all 12 updates, head back to [[Sinking Sands]] and go to [[The Silent City]] backdoor inside a cave near the Bay of Anuk at {{waypoint -945.53, -228.76, -1047.90}} and zone into the instance called [[The Empowered Fountain of Life]]
   - Once inside, head towards what looks like a moonstone pool in the center of the zone at {{waypoint -0.21, -13.40, -100.88}}.
   - Right-click it to perform the rite.
1. Hail the [[Avatar of the Forgotten]] to update your quest. She will give you "a decanter of blessed Fyr'Un water" that you must administer to followers of Rodcet Nife outside Qeynos.
1. Go to [[Antonica]]. The followers can be found in the following locations:
   1. Murgen Stillwhil: {{waypoint -506.63, -14.26, 154.04}} *(just east of the middle qeynos tower, first person)*
   1. Liyah Relltis: {{waypoint -499.48, -0.96, 560.24}} *(near the claymore, second person)*
   1. Gerehmie Lasherig: {{waypoint 375.67, -12.26, 812.25}} *(near the lighthouse/docks, third person)*
   1. Jeizin Faelurn: {{waypoint 139.18, -17.17, 202.86}} *(around the merchants between the two Qeynos gates, fourth person)*
   1. Curlose Mulerin: {{waypoint -742.32, -16.25, 241.98}} *(behind Sayer's Outfitters, fifth person)*
1. Now you've turned those five people, head to [[North Qeynos]].
   - *Note: The heroic guards are 55, so just use the gate and be careful of epics.*
   - Head to {{waypoint 473.33, -20.92, -218.69}} and talk to [[Plumetor Dul'Sadma]].
   - Once done, head into the Temple of Life and use the portal to get into the heart of it.
   - Inside, click the small orb at {{waypoint 561, -76, -207}}.
     - 2 [[A defender of Nife|defenders of Nife]] (two 75^^H linked) will spawn kill these to update the Quest.
1. Return to [[Plumetor Dul'Sadma]] outside and talk to her.
1. The quest will update again and she will enter the temple with her *everliving* friends.
   - If multiple people are on this or the previous step, you may have to wait for Plumetor Dul'Sadma to finish her dialog within the Temple of Life before another person can hail her.
1. Now, return to [[The Sinking Sands]] and hail her there to finish the Quest.
