## Steps

1. Collect 5 relics from outside the entrance to Chelsith {{waypoint 559, -165, 1392}} (relics look like small half triangles of grey material standing up, typically right next to rocks on the sea floor, not tab-targettable)

- Underwater breathing highly recommoned.
- Be careful for 83^^^ ghost on road outside of the shipyards and 83^^^ shark just south of the quest area.

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- +2000 faction with **Legion of Danak**
