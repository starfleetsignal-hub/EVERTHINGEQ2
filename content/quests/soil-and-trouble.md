## Steps

![Soild and Trouble soil samples look similar to beast dens or rocks.](images/Soil-and-trouble-samples.jpg)

1. Gather the following
   - Nye'Caelonea Soil Sample from the [[Gardens of Nye'Caelona]] at {{waypoint -615, -73, -466}} - this trackable harvest looks similar to a rock or beast den. It's typically near a tree trunk.
   - Twark Soil Sample from Twark - this trackable harvest looks similar to a rock or beast den. It is near [[Growf]]  {{waypoint 81, -171, -681}}
1. Speak to [[Elder Lyraquel]]

## Rewards

- +2,000 faction with **Nye'Caelona**
- At least 1p 53g 9s
