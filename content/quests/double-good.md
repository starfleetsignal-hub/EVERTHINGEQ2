## Steps

1. Examine flags at the following locations (see the *Waypoint Strings* on the talk page):
   - Flag 1 "near the flittering fairy": {{waypoint -100, 2, -79}} ([[Dyrilisia]])
   - Flag 2 "near the warboars": {{waypoint -72.26, 1.14, -94.51}} (and an inspectable chest)
   - Flag 3 "nearby a mud pit": {{waypoint -13, 2, -87}} (the Muddite Pool)
   - Flag 4 "room with forges": {{waypoint 204, -40, -160}}
   - Flag 5 "room filled with junk": {{waypoint 175, -16, -47}}
   - Flag 6 "room filled with flags": {{waypoint 150, -15, 161.5}}
   - Flag 7 "room filled with flags": {{waypoint 145, -16, 166}}
   - Flag 8 "room filled with flags": {{waypoint 132.5, -14, 172}}
1. Examine an empty crate near the shackles that initiated the quest, at {{waypoint -92, 3, -96}}
1. Search the bodies of the hanging evil eyes (in the same room as The Runnyeye Chef {{waypoint 85, -16, -233}}) until you find an ***egg sac***.
1. Put the egg sacs in the empty crate at {{waypoint -92, 3, -96}}
1. Examine another crate on the other side of the same room at {{waypoint -66, 3, -88}} to complete the quest.
