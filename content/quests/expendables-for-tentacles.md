## Steps

1. kill and gather the following
   - Cull the fields of 12 Oogothl Lashers in [[Tentacle Fields]]. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 416, 34, -24}}, {{waypoint 534, 44, -61}}, {{waypoint 403, 29, -163}}
   - Pick up 8 harvests dropped by the netherbians. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 430, 32, -37}}, {{waypoint 464, 35, -27}}, {{waypoint 432, 32, -97}}
     - {{waypoint 419, 31, -113}}, {{waypoint 348, 32, -172}}, {{waypoint 349, 31, -220}}
     - {{waypoint 351, 28, -282}}, {{waypoint 310, 43, -363}}
1. Return to [[Nanru]].

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- [[Void Adventurer's Wage]]- unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
