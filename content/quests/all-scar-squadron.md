- The quests [[Origins of Disease]] and [[Origins of Pestilence]] must be completed for the book to be clickable.

1. Catalogue a number of Named inhabitants of [[Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands|Detroxxulous]]:
   - Catalogue the Cadaver Lord, [[Ribcracker]] {{waypoint -218.89, 239.65, -39.27}}
   - Catalogue the Contagion Lord, [[Blistersnag]] {{waypoint 176.57, 394.21, 63.46}}
   - Catalogue the Fleshclot Prime, [[Scabclot]] {{waypoint -169.75, 126.20, -260.84}}
   - Catalogue the Plaguebug, [[Malavork]] {{waypoint 14.93, 27.56, 301.19}}
   - Catalogue the Gaseous Pox, [[Vilepox]] {{waypoint -176.42, 21.00, 545.36}}
   - Catalogue the Flesh Tearer, [[Bloodclaw]] {{waypoint -238.72, 125.95, -223.47}}
   - Catalogue the Greatrat, [[Tabricky]] {{waypoint -497.87, 33.24, 158.63}}
   - Catalogue the Deadly Swarm, [[Potroxxious]] {{waypoint 220.34, 20.76, 455.62}}
1. Defeat a member of All-Scar Squadron.
