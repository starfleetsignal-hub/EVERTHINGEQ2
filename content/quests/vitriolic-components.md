## Steps

- Obtain 5 pale spitting spider venom sacs, by hunting [[A pale spitting spider|pale spitting spiders]] at {{waypoint -956, 133, 566}} this general area and in the cave reachable at {{waypoint -1087, 130, 572}}
- Obtain 5 yellow recluse venom sacs, by hunting [[A yellow recluse spider|yellow recluse spiders]] at {{waypoint -656, 86, 1210}}.
- Return to [[Hodgemottin]]

## Rewards

- At least 53s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Band of Flowing Rivers]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Crest of Flowing Rivers]] (All Priests)
  - [[Emblem of Flowing Rivers]] (All Mages)
  - [[Signet of Flowing Rivers]] (All Fighters)
