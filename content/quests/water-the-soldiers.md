## Notes

- You must be level 85 to obtain this repeatable quest.

## Steps

1. Gather 3 buckets of water by the streams (Around {{waypoint -266, 378, 709}}, {{waypoint -206, 376, 756}} more near {{waypoint -206, 376, 756}} if not enough near stream they will respawn shortly).
1. Bring water to the NPCs located on the raised area next to the launch pad.
   - Bring water to [[A resting Quel'ule solider]] {{waypoint -569, 377, 651}}.
   - Bring water to [[A resting Quel'ule vindicator]] {{waypoint -564, -373, 614}}.
   - Bring water to [[A resting Quel'ule defender]] {{waypoint -550, 377, 642}}.
   - Bring water to [[A resting Quel'ule protector]] {{waypoint -569, 377, 651}}.
   - Bring water to [[A resting Quel'ule watcher]] {{waypoint -550, 377, 642}}.
1. Return to [[Shareon Beliqeh|Shareon]].

## Rewards

- At least 10g 5s 35c
