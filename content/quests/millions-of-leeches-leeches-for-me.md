There's something about this dead immolated. I just can't put my hand on it...

## Notes

- You have 3 minutes to complete this quest.
- Entrance to [[The Deep Hollow]] is in the cavern ceiling at {{waypoint 106.7, 479.14, 100.21}}, a little northwest of the Arcanna'se Spire entrance.

## Steps

1. Survive the incoming slimmolation in the given time!
   - Kill 10 [[An immoleech|immoleeches]] as they attack you.
   - Next step after the 3 minutes count down is over and you survived.
1. Defeat [[Lord Slurm]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Dirt encrusted key]]
- +250 faction with **Shields of Maldura**

## Trivia

The title of this quest is a reference to the song "Peaches" by The Presidents of the United States of America.
