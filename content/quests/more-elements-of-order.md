## Steps

1. obtain the following
   - Obtain 16 **tempest coral** by killing [[A maelstrom amalgam|maelstrom amalgams]] underwater in the [[Mouth of Prexus]] around {{waypoint -241, -24, -66}} ![Tempest coral](images/Tempest_coral.png)
     - Each amalgam drops two tempest corals on the ground, which must then be harvested.
   - Obtain 10 **empyreal strands** by killing [[A desert hornet|desert hornets]] in the [[Sandscoured Coast]] around {{waypoint -289, 18, -369}}
     - Not a guaranteed update.
1. Return to [[Ehasbehas]].
