This quest starts the moment you speak to [[The Glademaster]] after completing [[Operation Green Hood]] and auto-completes the moment you move the dialog forward. The quest is not available if you have completed the quest [[Give the key to Captain Vulis]].

## Steps

1. Press "Accept" on the New Quest window
1. Progress the dialog to complete this quest and receive your reward.
   - BTW, [[The Glademaster]] tells you they have received other keys from other agents since you went out, and tells you to keep the key.

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Branch of the Treefolke]]
  - [[Mammoth (Weapon)|Mammoth]]
  - [[Sureshot Bow]]
  - [[The Moonlight Mace]]
  - [[The Stonepine Blade]]
