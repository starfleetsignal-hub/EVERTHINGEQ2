## Steps

1. Travel to [[Kylong Plains]] and use the [[Catalogue Creature]] ability to catalog:
   - Catalog [[An ember worker]] around {{waypoint 1559, 213, -1881}} near the [[Ember Hive]] in [[Highton]]
   - Catalog [[An ember soldier]] around {{waypoint 1559, 213, -1881}} near the [[Ember Hive]] in [[Highton]]
   - Catalog [[An arbor giant]] around {{waypoint 849, 213, -2185}} near [[Karatun Hold]] in [[Highton]]
   - Catalog [[A Broken Toe shaman (Kylong Plains)]] around {{waypoint 1965, 120, -2310}} near [[Camp Rotsnot]] in [[Highton]]
   - Catalog [[A Broken Toe raider]] around {{waypoint 1965, 120, -2310}} near [[Camp Rotsnot]] in [[Highton]]
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]

## Hints

The creatures are trackable.  If you don't have tracking, many ranger mercs can track, for instance [[Villax Sned]] from the VoV expansion can track.

Fastest route is via Jarsath Wastes. Use bell or druidring and evac when you land, then run/fly to Kylong Plains.
