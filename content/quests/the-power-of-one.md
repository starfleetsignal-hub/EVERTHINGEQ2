## Description

Marathan Allim wishes me to travel into the beach area and harvest blood from the creatures who invade his land! I must also collect a large crystal that he claims harnesses the powers of these void creatures! I am unsure what any of this is actually for!

## Steps

1. Kill 15 of [[An invading lacerator]], [[An invading ripper]], or [[An invading render]].
1. Find a large void crystal somewhere around the Void Anchor {{waypoint 104, -128, 510}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g
- +2250 faction with **Order of Flame**
