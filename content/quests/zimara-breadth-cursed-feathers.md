## Notes

- You MUST finish the [[Ballads of Zimara Timeline]] signature quest before the quest starter will drop for you.
- it can be done in any order, fastest travel route by discovery name going clockwise
  1. Step #2 {{waypoint -923.16, 244.15, 343.16}} to discover [[Authon's Gallery]],
  1. Step #3 {{waypoint 312.53, 212.12, 618.56}} to discover [[Ruined Gateway]]
  1. Step #1 {{waypoint 346.24, 193.58, 6.92}} to discover [[Halfatho's Final Rest]]
  1. Step #4 {{waypoint -36.68, 227.86, -333.51}} to discover [[Researcher's Overlook]]
  1. Step #5 {{waypoint 911.66, 179.52, -763.97}} to discover [[Edge of the Abyss]]
- unlocks the [[Hexes, Charms, and Protections]] achievement once this quest and the 5 subquests are done

## Steps

- ![Tef-Feather](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Tef-Feather.png)Travel to {{waypoint 346.24, 193.58, 6.92}} to discover [[Halfatho's Final Rest]]
  - inspect the **Tef-Feather** on the ground, in the middle of the memorial sculpture to start [[Zimara Breadth: Tef-Feather]]
- ![Kur-Feather](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Kur-Feather.png)Travel to {{waypoint -923.16, 244.15, 343.16}} to discover [[Authon's Gallery]]
  - inspect the **Kur-Feather** on the desk to your right as you enter the structure to start [[Zimara Breadth: Kur-Feather]]
- ![Dol-Feather](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Dol-Feather.png)Travel to {{waypoint 312.53, 212.12, 618.56}} to discover [[Ruined Gateway]]
  - inspect the **Dol-Feather** on one of the rubble pieces (hard to see - look close) to start [[Zimara Breadth: Dol-Feather]]
- ![Nab-Feather](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Nab-Feather.png)Travel to {{waypoint -36.68, 227.86, -333.51}} to discover [[Researcher's Overlook]]
  - inspect the **Nab-Feather** on top of the square crates to start [[Zimara Breadth: Nab-Feather]]
- ![Cip-Feather](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Cip-Feather.png)Travel to {{waypoint 911.66, 179.52, -763.97}} to discover [[Edge of the Abyss]]
  - inspect the **Cip-Feather** on top of the stone table to start [[Zimara Breadth: Cip-Feather]]
