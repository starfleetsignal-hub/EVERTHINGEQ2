## Steps

![a voidtide terror](images/A_voidtide_terror.png)

1. Vadreem requires gorehusk samples taken from the voidtide terrors along [[Fleshborer Beach]] around {{waypoint -1083, 22, 520}}
   - hunt voidtide terrors for meat samples x 8
   - hunt voidtide terrors for chitin samples x 6
   - hunt voidtide terrors for appendage samples x 4
1. Return to Vadreem at {{waypoint -920, -50, 945}}
1. He will shapeshift to lead you to [[Nightscar Steppes]]. Speak to him again when you are prepared for the journey. He will "hide" you in his shell and take you for a ride across [[Leech Scale Strand]].
1. Speak with Vadreem, now on [[Nightscar Steppes]] at {{waypoint -273, 25, -22}}.

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- One of the following:
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
  - [[Doomview Shortbow of Frenzy]]
  - [[Coral Crossbow of Saga]]
