## Steps

1. Speak to Candice and ask her to take you to [[Cheriweth's Confectory]].
1. Kill all the monsters on the first floor. Don't go downstairs nor upstairs yet.
1. Speak with Candice Cheriweth, who will have appeared in the kitchen on the first floor.
1. Loot four sacks of fresh gingerbread dough from the second floor.
1. Return to Candice Cheriweth.
1. Clear the monsters out of the basement. To aid you, Candice spawns a pet for you, Crumbles.
1. As soon as you get into the basement, you will see two Humbugs. Chase them through the portal!
1. Confront the Humbugs, [[Dilly Bittersweet|Dilly]] and [[Billy Bittersweet]].
   - If your pet Crumbles gets the kill shot on Billy or Dilly you won't receive credit and will have to redo the instance.
   - You'll have to run down the path and kill the gingerbread/dread men and gumdrops in your way. Alternately, you can jump off the cliff and hug the walls to skip as many of the fights as you can.
   - You can fight Billy and Dilly separately.
1. Return to Candice Cheriweth. There's a portal on the docks that takes you back to her house.
