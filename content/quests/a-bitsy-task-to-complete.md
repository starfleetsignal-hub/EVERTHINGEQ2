## Steps

1. Use the [[Frizzleclang Metalpickerupper-mabob IV]] received from Bitsy to pick up 10 metal scraps along the beach.
1. Swim out to the sunken ship at {{waypoint 525, 0, 256}}.  When you get close [[A sunken sailor]] of your level will spawn and attack.
   - Continue swimming around spawning skeletons until you get 6 updates.

- Experience only
