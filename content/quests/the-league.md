NOTE:  You have to be level 68 to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Scout the Blackrunner Camp. {{waypoint 215, 90, 89}}
1. Kill [[An Ahroun blackclaw|Ahroun blackclaw]] and [[An Ahroun blackstalker|Ahroun blackstalker]] werewolves in that camp until you take 15 of their pelts as proof of your deeds. 10 min respawn, 7 up at a time.  [[Vysra the Ever-Hungry]] works but her placeholder ([[A blackrunner warrior]]) will not update the quest.
1. Return to [[Chardasus Divok]] to receive a flute, [[Whistle of the Freethinker]].  If you lose the flute, you can buy another from him for 24g.
1. Go up the stairs outside [[Castle Mistmoore]] and use the flute at {{waypoint 682, 187, 105}}
1. Talk to [[Brand]].
1. Return to [[Chardasus Divok]].

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The League of Freethinkers**
- [[Whistle of the Freethinker]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Crest of the Haven]]
  - [[Emblem of the Haven]]
  - [[Ring of the Haven]]
  - [[Signet of the Haven]]
