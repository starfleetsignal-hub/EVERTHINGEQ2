## Steps

Create 2 each from the random list below:

- [[A pristine briarwood bookcase|briarwood bookcase]]
- [[A pristine briarwood dining chair|briarwood dining chair]]
- [[An elaborate briarwood chair|elaborate briarwood chair]]
- [[A twisted briarwood chair|twisted briarwood chair]]

Create 4 each from the random list below:

- [[A canopied briarwood king bed|canopied briarwood king bed]]
- [[A pristine briarwood king bed|briarwood king bed]]
- [[An ornate briarwood chair|ornate briarwood chair]]
- [[An ornate end table|ornate end table]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

## Rewards

- At Least 24s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
