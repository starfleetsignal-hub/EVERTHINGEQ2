***This quest is repeatable up to five times***

## Description

"The nibbler stag population is pretty low near Kelethin due to many different species in the area. Everything from predators to racers scaring the nibblers away is a potential threat to their population. There's only one way to help the nibblers--destroy some of the competition." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay 6-8 [[A river racer|river racers]]
1. Return to Zephyre Violethair

## Rewards

- At least 13s 23c
