## Steps

1. Search [[Slaver's Point]] {{waypoint -467, -160, -866}} to find 4 evidences on the slaver leader - *look for pages on the ground*![Torn Slaver Note](images/Torn_Slaver_Note.jpg)
1. look for more clues
   - Defeat 4 [[A Frillik Tide brigand]] at the shore near [[Twark]] found around {{waypoint 202, -239, -875}}
   - Search for further clues at the shore near [[Twark]] - click on the "a Frillik Tide Dinghy" at {{waypoint 443, -242, -833}}![a Frillik Tide Dinghy](images/A_Frillik_Tide_Dinghy.jpg)
1. Return to [[Celsyn Beileth]]
