## Steps

1. Speak with the following NPCs ![The bowl at the side of the shrine](images/Steps_to_Acceptance_quest_-_bowl.jpg)
   - Shaman [[Sana]] at {{waypoint -1480, -515, -2256}}
   - Banker [[Priddie]] at {{waypoint -1355, -533, -2133}}
   - Fishing lead [[Banik]] at {{waypoint -1406, -542, -2077}}
1. Return to [[Nipik]]
1. Find the bowl of evermelting ice at {{waypoint -1628, -532, -2358}} and cleanse yourself
1. Look up and pay your respects to Prexus at the shrine (click on it).
1. Return to [[Nipik]]

## Rewards

- [[Othmir Welcome Tray]]
- At least 1p 25g
- +300 faction with **Othmir of Velious**
