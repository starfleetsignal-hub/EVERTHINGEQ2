## Notes

- [[Malicious Obsession]] is the corresponding "evil" aligned quest.

## Steps

1. Kill 10 bone rot skeletons ([[A bone rot bard|bone rot bards]], [[A bone rot druid|druids]], [[A bone rot magician|magicians]] and [[A bone rot warrior|warriors]] all update)
1. Return to Eleden

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g 80s
- When you finish this quest, [[A malignant shadow|malignant shadows]] will no longer knock you out of the sky in the larger region BUT the [[A Ghorkaal cursespinner|Ghorkaal cursespinners]]  will still knock you out of the sky if you get too close to Ghorkaal.
