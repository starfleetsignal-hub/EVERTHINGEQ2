## Steps

1. Make your way to [[Oakmyst Forest]] (you'll have to take a mariner's bell)
1. Search Oakmyst Forest
   1. Head to {{waypoint 954, 6, -293}}
   1. Follow the trail to {{waypoint 960, 2, -222}}
   1. Take the uphill path to {{waypoint 920, 16, -191}}
1. Speak to Laharn
1. Enter the cave hidden behind the fern leaves
1. Kill the undead that emerge from the rift
1. Confront the heretic - speak to [[Taskla (NPC)|Taskla]].  You may have to target her and use the H key.
1. Defeat [[Taskla (Mob)|Taskla]]
1. Return to Lakosha in Qeynos Capitol District

## Rewards

- At least 5s 81c @ lvl 20
- [[Ring of the Kerra (A Master Among Masters)|Ring of the Kerra]]
