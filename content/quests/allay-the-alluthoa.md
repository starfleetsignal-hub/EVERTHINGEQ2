## Steps

Zone into [[Zavith'loa: The Hidden Caldera (Heroic)]].

1. Still the speaker - Kill [[High Priest Hamanu'akaloa (Heroic)]]] at (-3,385, 12, -83).
1. Stop the Allu'thoa leader - Kill the [[Fiery Effigy of Clotl'thoa (Heroic)]] at (-3,343, 11, 74).

- At least 2p 16g 20000 status
- 15 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
