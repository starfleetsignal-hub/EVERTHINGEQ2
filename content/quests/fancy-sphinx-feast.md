## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An odd creature named Kupriania seems to be acting as Sassarish’s handmaiden. She's made it very clear that Sassarish's food storage is running low, and could use some help gathering more for the enigmatic sphinx.

1. Pick up [[Fancy Sphinx Feast]] from [[Kupriania]] at {{waypoint 273, 164, -59}}
1. Examine the baskets at her feet and fill them.
1. I should be able to find the nourishment Sassarish wants without having to leave Zenith Helionae.
   - *Note: The quest will ask you to find 2 of the 4 items listed below.*
   - The basket needs between 6 and 8 frenzy watermaw meat  near {{waypoint 52.86, 133.34, -121.61}}
   - The basket needs between 6 and 8 Zenith Potatoes.  Near {{waypoint 243.21, 155.82, -118}}
   - The basket needs between 6 and 8 winged viper meat (kill winged vipers).![Zenith Potatoes](images/ZenithPotatoes.jpg)
   - The basket needs between 6 and 8 winged viper eggs.
     - ![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Winged_Viper_Eggs.png){{waypoint 199.73, 148.36, -130.11}}
     - {{waypoint 116.02, 149.99, -256.58}}
     - {{waypoint 97.19, 150.09, -230.72}}
     - {{waypoint 59.89, 143.99, -240.50}}
     - {{waypoint 60.50, 140.15, -212.93}}
     - {{waypoint 24.24, 141.05, -224.72}}
     - {{waypoint -1.75, 142.20, -228.08}}![](images/Winged_Viper.png)
     - {{waypoint -20.86, 146.42, -217.06}}
1. Deposit the food in the baskets near [[Kupriania]] at {{waypoint 273, 164, -59}}
