## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The avatar summoning rituals require the manipulation of immense power and are inherently dangerous. To reduce the chance of failure and provide an anchor for that power, the herald of Drinal has asked me to collect an item that may have once belonged to that god or one of their most loyal followers

- Collect [[Reliquary of Drinal|Reliquaries of Drinal]] from a powerful creature in [[Zimara Breadth: Razing the Razelands (Heroic I)|Zimara Breadth: Razing the Razelands]] heroic dungeon.

**This does not update in [Solo]. It needs to be Zimara Breadth: Razing the Razelands [Heroic I].**

- Return to the [[Herald of the Silver Reaper]] at {{waypoint 85, -3, 291}} in [[Loping Plains]]
