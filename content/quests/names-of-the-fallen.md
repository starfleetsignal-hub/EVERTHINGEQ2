## Note

- You must be at least level 87 to get this quest.

## Steps

1. Collect Mementos from Fallen Coldain
   - Click on the bodies of dead Coldain soldiers in Storm Gorge
1. Return to [[Ullvar Silverbeard]] {{waypoint 544, -288, 440}} in the [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain|Hall of Remembrance]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
