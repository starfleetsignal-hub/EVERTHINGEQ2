## Steps

1. Visit [[Duffy]] at {{waypoint 143, -16, 224}}.
1. Speak to [[Lord Quinn Clothspinner]] at [[The Keep of the Ardent Needle (POI)|The Keep of the Ardent Needle]] {{waypoint -556, -8, -312}}
1. He sends you to the [[Ruins of Caltorsis]] to find some missing needles {{waypoint -1407, -13.88, -739.53}}. Inspect a locked strongbox inside the ruins.
1. Return to Lord Quinn Clothspinner.
1. Return to Sighard Sayer.

## Rewards

- At least 4s 32c
