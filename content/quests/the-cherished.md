## Steps

![How the gem looks underwater](images/Cherished1.png)
![How the gem looks above water](images/Cherished2.png)

1. Find a triangle-shaped gem in the water, keeping in mind the following:
   - The gem spawns randomly. See the talk page for locations found by other people.
   - It is small and black, but emits shiny sparkles. Regardless, you may need to change your camera angle under the water in order to see it. If your character is too big for the camera to zoom-in underwater than use a self-shrinking item, such as [[Gnomish Gent's Mechanicized Facade]] or [[Mystic Moppet Billy]]. One shrunken to this size, you should be able to see underwater when in first-person.
   - One person in the group will need to find it and click on it (it lights up and has the hand icon), and then it will auto update for the other group members.
   - You can delete the quest and restart it to respawn the gem in a new location.
1. [[Mullok]] will attack, kill him to receive his 'Cherished'.
   - He is a 60^^^ and hits fairly hard so be ready. He will continue to grow in size (every 25% of health) until you kill him.

## Rewards

- [[Rhaondrilliis]]

## Notes

- The entire quest is a not-so-subtle homage to The Lord of the Rings (Gollum, losing his "precious", the One Ring).
