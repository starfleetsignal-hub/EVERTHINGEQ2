This quest is part of a 6-quest series; the first five can be done in any order.

## Steps

![The Rotting Stump Beside the Entrance to Underrot Caverns.](images/Rotting_Stump.jpg)

1. Kill [[Dusk leaper|dusk leapers]] until you obtain 8 legs. {{waypoint -671.71, 66.21, -12.41}} (uncommon update)
1. Kill [[An ash owlbear|ash owlbears]] until you obtain 20 feathers. {{waypoint -656, 62, 213}} (always update)
1. Visit a rotting stump near Timber Falls. Right next to the entrance to [[Underrot Caverns]] {{waypoint 349, 2, -1284}}
1. Return to [[Kleron Asana]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Acolyte's Wrought Plate Greaves]] (Clerics)
  - [[Bandit's Light Iron Chain Leggings]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Beguiler's Embroidered Leggings]] (All Mages)
  - [[Expert's Fine Cloth Leg Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Preserver's Cured Leather Pants]] (Druids)
  - [[Sentry's Hammered Legplates]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Soothsayer's Bound Chausses]] (Shamans)
