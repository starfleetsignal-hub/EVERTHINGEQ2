## Steps

1. Travel to [[Jarsath Wastes]] and head to {{waypoint -850, -121, -607}}(fastest way is to Druid Ring to Jarsath and then Sokokar to the Kylong Post), use your mask and speak with [[A wise rhino]] at {{waypoint -681, -128, -622}}.
1. Kill 10 razorfangs and 3 sick rhinos.  The razorfangs can be found scattered in the surrounding area, and the sick rhinos are lying down on the ground around the pond.
1. Right click 4 grindhove calves {{waypoint -624, -117, -540}}
   - Bring grindhove calves to {{waypoint 647, -45, 704}} (Mine updated at {{waypoint 536, -44, 458}}) They will help you fight as you go. If you have a flying mount, you can fly here without fighting anything.
1. Go back to [[Wise rhino]]
1. Kill [[Survival Instinct]] (83^^) spawn at {{waypoint -650, -128, -601}}
1. Harvest the rock. (Respawns every 5-10 mins)
1. Return to Darklight Wood and talk to the Thelia at {{waypoint 446.17, -7.43, -267.64}}
