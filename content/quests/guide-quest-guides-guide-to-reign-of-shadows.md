## Prerequisites

- You need access to the Reign of Shadows expansion to do this quest.
- You need to have unlocked the access to the overland zones by completing the adventure or crafter signature lines.
- You need to be at least level 120 adventurer or crafter to enter [[Luclin Nexus]]

## Starting the Quest

- The starting zone seems to be the Teren's Grasp Spire.
- The guide is not always there.
- The quest is offered to the whole group, but it can't be shared.
- You only need the guide to start the quest, there is no time limit to finish the quest.
- The guide announces in general chat!*
