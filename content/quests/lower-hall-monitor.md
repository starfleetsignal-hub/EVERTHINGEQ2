## Description

The Lower Corridors of the Ruins of Guk are likely brimming with victims of the Curse of Ykesha, long beyond any hope of release from its grasp. Now, the most merciful thing to do is simply put them to their final rest so that they might know some semblance of peace. I must destroy as many of the wretched creatures as I can find, which will hopefully weaken the grip of the curse on the Lower Corridors.

## Steps

1. Destroy the cursed creatures of [[The Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors]]
   - Dispatch 8 cursed residents of the Lower Corridors (most of the frogs in the first room fall into this category, all except the knights that are grouped)
   - Find and put down 6 other creatures that may be victims of the curse (the ykeshan trolls in the room with the ramp leading to where the chest might be)
1. * Return to [[Vegupa Marrsheart]]

- At least 1p 60g
