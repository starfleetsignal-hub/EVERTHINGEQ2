This quest is repeatable.

## Starting the Quest

Guide quests can start in any city or zone in the game.  Because this quest involves fungusmen it is usually held in [[Frostfang Sea]].

## Steps

1. Use the *fungoid trapper* in your inventory on a fungusman to capture it. In Frostfang Sea, fungoids can be found on [[Pilgrim's Landing]] around {{waypoint -177, 47, -838}}
1. Return to the guide

## Rewards

- [[Fungoid Souvenir]]
