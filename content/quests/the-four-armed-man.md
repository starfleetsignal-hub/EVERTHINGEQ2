## Steps

1. Go to [[Lost Khorr]] and click the green sparkles {{waypoint 469, 70, -950}}
1. Go northeast and click more green sparkles {{waypoint 399, 52, -1023}}
1. Go down the path to the beach {{waypoint 298, 8, -1005}} and kill [[A four-armed seeker]]
1. Speak to [[Overseer Clackwhinge]] on [[The Isle of Refuge]] {{waypoint 1246, 8, 1125}}
1. Return to Charr

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
- 3 [[Short Glowbulb Plant]]s
