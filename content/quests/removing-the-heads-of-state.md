## Prerequisites

- You need +7500 faction with [[The Gwalnax Brigade (Faction)|The Gwalnax Brigade]] to unlock this quest.

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Chardok Palace]] in [[Chardok]] and defeat the leaders of the Di'Zok:
   - [[Silren Di'Zok]] {{waypoint 957, -77, 613}}
     - Silren is located in [[The Library of Divination]]. To access the library, follow these directions:
       1. Look in the two upper rooms of [[The Hall of Initiative]] for three books: [[Encyclopedia of Mutagenica Vol I]], [[Encyclopedia of Mutagenica Vol II]], and [[Encyclopedia of Mutagenica Vol III]]. They can all spawn in the same room or in both. {{waypoint 868, -21, 135}} and {{waypoint 890, -21, 275}}
       1. Once you have acquired all three, click the lever behind [[Stalis Ra'Dal]], at {{waypoint 923, -45, 355}}.
       1. Click on the clay golem NPC [[Re'Adon]] and you will automatically give him the books.
       1. Stick close to Re'Adon as he slowly makes his was past the secret walls.
       1. Once you're inside the library, continue down all the steps and click the book titled, "Passage to Oblivion", at {{waypoint 929, -66, 556}}.
       1. Silren will spawn at {{waypoint 957, -77, 613}}.
   - [[Nortalz Di'Zok]] in [[The Hall of Righteous]] {{waypoint 1061, -47, 347}}
   - [[Kromikus Di'Zok]] in [[The Hall of Prudence]] {{waypoint 983, -50, 228}}
   - [[Queen Velazul Dizok]] in [[The Throne of Ascendence]]. {{waypoint 1310, -67, 230}}
