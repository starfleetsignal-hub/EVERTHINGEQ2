## Notes

- [[Steamfont Stew (Quest)|Steamfont Stew]] from [[Lola Punwicket]] takes you to the same area.

## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Investigate [[Camp Redcap]] {{waypoint -430, 92, 885}}
   - Investigate [[Camp Cobalt]] {{waypoint -398, 100, 1023}}
   - Investigate [[Camp Vink]] {{waypoint -307, 91, 1019}}
   - Investigate [[Camp Riridus]] {{waypoint -250, 90, 911}}
1. Return to [[Waynil Garwater]]

## Rewards

- At least 27s 67c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Aegis of Discarded Parts]] (All Priests)
  - [[Geyser Rusted Mace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Orb of Discarded Parts]] (All Priests)
  - [[Knuckles of Intuitive Mauling]] (Beastlord, Bruiser, Monk)
  - [[Tower of the Brutal]] (Beastlords, Bruiser, Monk)
  - [[Orcish Spellcaster]] (All Mages)
  - [[Symbol of Discarded Parts]] (All Mages)
  - [[Round Shield of Discarded Parts]] (All Scouts except Beastlord)
  - [[Sun Marked Scimitar]] (All Scouts except Beastlord)
  - [[Tower Shield of Discarded Parts]] (Berserker, Guardian, Paladin, Shadowknight)
  - [[Wicked Blade of Mastery]] (Berserker, Guardian, Paladin, Shadowknight)
