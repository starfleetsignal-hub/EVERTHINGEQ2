## Notes

You must be level 55 to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest a fresh [[Ebon cluster]] from a **flame licked ore** in [[Lavastorm]]. ![Get your ebon here ...](images/Tinkering_with_Toggery_quest_-_ore.jpg)
1. Return the ebon cluster to Toggery.
1. Get a fresh [[Fulginate cluster]] from a flame licked ore in Lavastorm. Needs to be freshly harvested, meaning the fulignate harvested whilst looking for the ebon cluster won't work.
1. Return the fulginate cluster to Toggery for your reward

## Rewards

Depending on your class :

- [[Toggery's Tinkered Excavation Cap]] - All [[Mage]]s
- [[Toggery's Tinkered Excavation Helm]] - All [[Scout]]s, [[Defiler]], [[Mystic]]
- [[Toggery's Tinkered Excavation Helmet]] - [[Berserker]], [[Guardian]], [[Inquisitor]], [[Paladin]], [[Shadowknight]], [[Templar]]
- [[Toggery's Tinkered Excavation Skullcap]] - [[Bruiser]], [[Fury]], [[Monk]], [[Warden]]
