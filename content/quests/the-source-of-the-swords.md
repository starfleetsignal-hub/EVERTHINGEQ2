## Description

"Serris needs me to track down the location of the Seafury Buccaneers' weapon stash.  Once I've found the arms, Serris wants me to surreptitiously steal the weapons so they won't find their way into the hands of anyone who could use them against Freeport.  Serris will be waiting for me at the dockside taver, the Seafarer's Roost, once I have completed my task."  *--Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Find 3 sparkling crates along the docks in East and South Freeport. {{waypoint -210.05, -56.07, -21.00}} or {{waypoint -251.28, -56.07, -73.60}}
1. Find 3 sparkling chests around [[Champion's Coliseum]] in West Freeport. {{waypoint 270, -3.49, -101}} or {{waypoint 145, -4, -82}}
1. Find 3 sparkling crates in [[The Jade Tiger's Den]]{{waypoint -73, -21, -72}}
1. Enter the [[Jade Tiger's Den Cellar]] {{waypoint -136, -24, -90}}.
1. Kill [[Elly Sharptalon]] and her two tipsy Buccaneers (outside and around back, click cellar door) {{waypoint -136.75, -24.37, -91.34}}
1. Collect the 3 crates around the room
1. Speak to [[Sanura Lithestripe]] This character can be found in "[[Armor by Ithkar]]" in East Freeport<br> {{waypoint -129, -37, -5}}
1. Speak to Serris behind [[Seafarer's Roost]] {{waypoint -258, -55, -11}}

## Conclusion

"I was able to find and then confiscate the Buccaneers' weapon stockpile, but the mission was anything but stealthy.  I had to kill several Seafury Buccaneers that interfered with my mission, and a pair of drunkards saw the whole thing and then escaped!" *--Everquest II Journal*
