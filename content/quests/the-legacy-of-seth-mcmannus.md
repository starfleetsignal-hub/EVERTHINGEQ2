## Steps

1. Slay [[Dreadwake]] (Lvl 42, Epic x2). {{waypoint 101, -26, 107}}
   - If Dreadwake hasn't been killed in a while, when you accept the quest he will spawn right in front of [[Seth McMannus]] and eat him. However, if Dreadwake has been killed recently, then he will eventually spawn in the water near Seth, but won't jump out and eat him.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Ring of Holy Runes]] (All Priests)
  - [[Ringlet of Holy Runes]] (All Mages)
  - [[Signet of Holy Runes]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Signet of Utter Chaos]] (All Fighters)
