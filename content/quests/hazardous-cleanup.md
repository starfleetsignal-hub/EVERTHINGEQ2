## Steps

![discarded machine part](images/Discarded_Nurgan_machine_part_node.jpg)

1. Collect 12 *discarded machine parts* from the camps within [[Nurgan Mining Camp]]. There are at least two machine parts per camp:
   - {{waypoint -493, -5, -1558}} & {{waypoint -485, -5, -1607}}
   - {{waypoint -412, 29, -1826}} & {{waypoint -393, 29, -1841}}
   - {{waypoint -322, 32, -1819}} & {{waypoint -302, 32, -1805}}
   - {{waypoint -228, 33, -1780}} & {{waypoint -222, 33, -1757}}
   - {{waypoint -424, 36, -1870}} & {{waypoint -332, 36, -1846}}
   - {{waypoint -249, 48, -1862}} & {{waypoint -267, 48, -1870}}
   - {{waypoint -425, 13, -1691}}
1. Return to [[Tinkerist Nalzie]].

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- +5000 faction with **Exiles of Droga**
- [[Glowing Gem of Critical Magnitude]]
