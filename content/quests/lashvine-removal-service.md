## Steps

1. Cull 12 [[A lashvine sprout|lashvine sprouts]] around {{waypoint 628, 60, 115}}![A Lashvine Sprout](images/Alashvinesprout.png)
1. Kill the [[A mature lashvine|mature lashvine]] that spawns directly after the last [[A lashvine sprout|lashvine sprout]] is killed.
   - Hint: If last one killed and no [[A mature lashvine|mature lashvine]] spawns, try killing [[A lashvine sprout|lashvine sprouts]] at {{waypoint 695, 75, 194}}
   - NOTE: The mature lasher spawed on the 11th and I killed it.  Killing another sprout (the 12th) didn't spawn another mature lashvine so maybe run and kill the 12th before killing the mature lashvine will help!
1. Talk to [[Bimse]]
