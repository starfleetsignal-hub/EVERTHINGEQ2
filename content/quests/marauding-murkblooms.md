## Steps

1. Kill a variety of Murkbloom Fungusmen found around {{waypoint -209, 1, -237}}.
   - 5 [[Murkbloom defender|murkbloom defenders]]
   - 3 [[Murkbloom feeder|murkbloom feeders]]
   - 4 [[Murkbloom forager|murkbloom foragers]]
1. Collect the [[Strange Idol]] that is making the murkblooms hostile. {{waypoint -229.94, 4.28, -262.24}}. It has a 1 min respawn time.
1. Return to Lieutenant Kaneth {{waypoint -288, 0, 139}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 59c
- +150 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Nektulos Defender's Wrap]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Nektulos Healer's Wrap]] (All Priests)
  - [[Nektulos Medium's Wrap]] (All Mages)
  - [[Nektulos Stalker's Wrap]] (All Scouts)
