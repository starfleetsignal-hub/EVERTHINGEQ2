This is part 2 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Qeynos]] and [[Kelethin]] who wear Chain and Plate Armor (with the exception of [[Shaman|shaman]] types).

- If you are from [[Freeport]], then your version of the quest is called [[Custom Fitted Armor - Part II]].

## Steps

1. You must collect the following items:
   - 12 Missing gems from [[Marine slug|marine slugs]] in the [[The Thundering Steppes]] {{waypoint -389, -8, -319}}. They are located next to the Thundering Steppes Dock.
   - 4 Missing Arrows from any Zombies in [[The Thundering Steppes]], e.g. [[Unearthed settler|unearthed settlers]].
   - Old Spellbook from [[Grolven Chiptooth]] in [[Antonica]] (wanders outside [[Blackburrow]])
1. Return to [[Ian Cathlan]] to receive your reward.
