Create 2 each of any 6 from the random list below:

- [[Carbonite Brigandine Boots|Forged Carbonite Brigandine Boots]]
- [[Carbonite Chainmail Boots|Forged Carbonite Chainmail Boots]]
- [[Carbonite Chainmail Coif|Forged Carbonite Chainmail Coif]]
- [[Carbonite Plate Sabatons|Forged Carbonite Plate Sabatons]]
- [[Carbonite Vanguard Sabatons|Forged Carbonite Vanguard Sabatons]]
- [[Carbonite Brigandine Coif|Forged Carbonite Brigandine Coif]]
- [[Carbonite Chainmail Mantle|Forged Carbonite Chainmail Mantle]]
- [[Carbonite Chainmail Bracers|Forged Carbonite Chainmail Bracers]]
- [[Carbonite Plate Barbute|Forged Carbonite Plate Barbute]]
- [[Carbonite Vanguard Barbute|Forged Carbonite Vanguard Barbute]]

Create 4 each of any 3 from the random list below:

- [[Carbonite Brigandine Mantle|Forged Carbonite Brigandine Mantle]]
- [[Carbonite Chainmail Coat|Forged Carbonite Chainmail Coat]]
- [[Carbonite Chainmail Gloves|Forged Carbonite Chainmail Gloves]]
- [[Carbonite Plate Spaulders|Forged Carbonite Plate Spaulders]]
- [[Carbonite Vanguard Spaulders|Forged Carbonite Vanguard Spaulders]]
- [[Carbonite Brigandine Bracers|Forged Carbonite Brigandine Bracers]]
- [[Carbonite Chainmail Leggings|Forged Carbonite Chainmail Leggings]]
- [[Carbonite Plate Gussets|Forged Carbonite Plate Gussets]]
- [[Carbonite Vanguard Gussets|Forged Carbonite Vanguard Gussets]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 6s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
