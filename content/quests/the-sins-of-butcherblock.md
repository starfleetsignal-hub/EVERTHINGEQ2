## Steps

1. Speak with [[Zha Gu'Lau (Butcherblock Mountains)|Zha Gu'Lau]], who wanders [[East Fort Irontoe]]. {{waypoint -350, 165, -240}}
1. Speak to [[Darvil Blackfist]] who is located in the pool of the [[Yarpsnarl Camp]] northeast of [[Danak Dhorentath]]. {{waypoint -600, 100, 610}}
1. Kill Darvil Blackfist, then talk to him again (Undead, ya know?).
1. Collect the three buried treasure caches:
   - You will get attacked by three disgraced members of the family (35vv) after picking up each treasure chest. You do NOT have to fight them for the update.
   - Brodor Heartmill's treasure: {{waypoint -950.00, 105.00, 60.00}}
   - Orram Lockson's treasure: {{waypoint -492, 132, 688}}
   - Jerym Frothbrew's treasure (a box on the ground in [[Darkfall Gorge]]): {{waypoint -20, 100, -330}}
1. Return to Zha Gu'Lau.
1. Place the treasures in the the following locations (click on the table):
   - [[East Fort Irontoe]] {{waypoint -367, 160, -177}}
   - [[West Fort Irontoe]] {{waypoint -272, 215, -156}}
   - [[West Fort Irontoe]] {{waypoint -232, 214, -190}}
1. Return to Zha Gu'Lau for your reward.

## Rewards

- +2500 faction with **Cazic-Thule**
- One of the following:
  - [[Thule's Runed Band]]
  - [[Thule's Polished Ring]]
- Unlocks [[Thule's Blessing]] and [[Id's Release]]
