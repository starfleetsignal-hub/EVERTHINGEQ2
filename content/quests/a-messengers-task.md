## Description

Kalmath Gusinga runs a path from the Entrance from Greater Faydark and through the stable master near Tunare's Grove.

## Prerequisites

- Your faction with [[The City of Kelethin (Faction)|City of Kelethin]] must be Indifferent or better to obtain this quest.

## Steps

![a lost message](images/A_lost_message.jpg)

1. Harvest **lost messages** on the ground near the entrance to [[Zone|Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint -660, 54, 190}}
1. Return to the Royal Messenger ([[Kalmath Gusinga]]) with the lost messages (somewhere around  {{waypoint 49, -36, 244}})
1. NOTE: The pages are near the Sharpthorn creatures, which you have to kill anyway for the ([[Fun with Hemotoxins]]) quest you get from the Nybright camp, so you may as well do them both together.

## Rewards

- At least 9g 13s 1c
- Faction: +100 The City of Kelethin
