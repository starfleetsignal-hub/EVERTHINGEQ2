## Notes

- This is a subquest of [[Bring Him Bax]].

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Kriinz Nax]] in [[The City of Jinisk]] to receive [[Spear for Bax]]. {{waypoint 625, 37, -123}}
1. Speak with [[Braz Terrick]], right next to Kriinz Nax, to receive [[Armor for Bax]].
1. Return to Bax.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- +2,000 faction with **Kunzar Jungle Villagers**
