## Steps

1. Kill the lesser ice shades and use the [[Awakening Incense]] on the gnolls.
   - Kill 6 [[A lesser ice shade|lesser ice shades]].
   - Use the Awakening Incense on the [[An enthralled Snowfang gnoll|enthralled Snowfang gnolls]].  This can only be done if the ice shade in the linked encounter has been killed.
1. Return to [[Mirna Greatfrost]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,100 faction with **Snowfang Gnolls**
- [[Nephrite Snowfang Dreamcatcher]]
