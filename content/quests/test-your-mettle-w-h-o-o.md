## Steps

1. finish the conversation with [[Hydona]]
1. Create [[Processed Norrathian Sample]]
   - *you'll need the debris sample from [[The Thundering Steppes]], 1 aerated mineral water, and 5 Zimaran coal.*
1. Speak with [[Hydona]].
1. Craft a [[W.H.O.O. Basic]] using the following materials:
   - [[Defunct W.H.O.O.]] (received earlier in the quest)
   - [[Processed Norrathian Sample]]
   - 5 x [[Zimaran Coal]]
1. Speak with [[Hydona]].

## Note

There's a fuel merchant a little further round the loop at {{waypoint 312.66, 276.23, 1068.17}} if needed.
