## Steps

1. Go to [[Nektulos Forest]] and kill:
   - 10 [[A mist grinnin savage|mist grinnin savages]] at {{waypoint -149, 1, -721}}
   - 12 [[An elder ash owlbear|elder ash owlbears]] - same location as the regular ash owlbears from previous step, at {{waypoint -650, 60, 215}}
   - 10 [[A bloated soul leech|bloated soul leeches]] {{waypoint -10, -3, -1000}}
1. [[Ikara V'Tahl]] in [[Fallen Gate]] - spawns in the middle island in [[The Pool of Percelia]] with two guards, at {{waypoint 33, 6, -140}}
1. Return to [[Kirsteh the Witch]]
