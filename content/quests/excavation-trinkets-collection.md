## A Note on the [[D.I.R.T.Y. Timeline|D.I.R.T.Y.]] Phases

Originally only the worn and broken trinkets were available during Phase 1 of the D.I.R.T.Y. project. The stained trinkets and silvered trinkets became available later. The silvered trinkets are markedly more rare than the other types.
