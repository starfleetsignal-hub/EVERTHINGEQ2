## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[Blackshield Pirates (Group)|Blackshield Pirates]] (any will do) on the beach north of Gorowyn Beach Outpost. {{waypoint 2349, 6, 870}}
1. Return to Arowon Myli'Tak.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 86c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Fighter's Earring]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Earring]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Earring]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Earring]] (All Mages)
