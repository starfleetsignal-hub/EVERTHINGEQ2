## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 20+

## Steps

1. Slay the three Bloodsaber ambushers that attack as you travel towards [[North Qeynos]]. {{waypoint 731, -22, -464}}
1. Deliver the canopic jars to [[Baron Kaedrin Ironforge]] at the [[Ironforge Exchange]]. {{waypoint 339, -16, -96}}
1. Return to [[Harkam Nubbytoe]].
1. Speak to Rinna {{waypoint 788, -22, -537}}. *Note: Rinna will no longer be here if you have already completed [[Law of the Land]]. In this event, the quest finishes with Harkam.
