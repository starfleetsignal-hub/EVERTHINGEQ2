## Steps

1. [[Researcher Tahar]] in what remains of [[Quel'ule]] has asked me to assist him by collecting 8 soil samples. ![soil sample](images/Soil_sample.jpg)
   - The soil samples appear as small piles of soil with a green glow, within and around the city by boulders and cliff faces. Click the pile for the update.
1. Return to [[Researcher Tahar]].

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- +3000 faction with **Researchers of Quel'ule**
