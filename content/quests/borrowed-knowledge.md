## Steps

- [[Transcribe Research Notes]] auto-scribes when you accept the quest
1. Click on the portal that the Duality opened to the [[Research Library of Mayong Mistmoore]]
1. Go up the steps to Mayong's research library
   - As soon as you enter the room [[A vigilant Mistmoore guardian]] (scaled 4-5 levels above you) spawns and begins patrolling the hallway, eventually entering the library.
1. ![Research Notes](images/Research_Notes.jpg)Gather 5 [[Mistmoore's Research Notes]] from around the room
1. Craft [[Transcript of Mistmoore's Research Notes]] (Transcribe Research Notes) at an [[Engraved Desk]]
   - There are desks in Mayong's library {{waypoint 105, 6, 88}} and in the Duality's Sanctum {{waypoint 525, 130, -61}}
1. Talk to the Duality in his sanctum

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
