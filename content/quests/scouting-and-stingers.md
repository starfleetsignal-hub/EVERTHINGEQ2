## Notes

- While this quest is the following quest in the [[Ghassan the Trader Timeline]], it may still be acquired without completing [[The Final Piece]].

## Steps

1. Visit the following in [[The Pillars of Flame]]:
   - [[Remnants of the Lifire]]: {{waypoint 145, -88, -757}}; just west out of Swiftrider's Caravan
   - [[Searing Gorge]]: {{waypoint 32, -88, -676}}; just south out of Swiftrider's Caravan
1. Kill 20 [[A searing scorpion|searing scorpions]] in Searing Gorge. {{waypoint 18, -93, -657}}
1. Return to [[Sergeant Tuskin]].

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Swiftrider Mercenaries**
