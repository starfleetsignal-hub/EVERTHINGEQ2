*This quest is part of the [[Freeport Outlying Areas Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Zaddar attempts to start the ritual but fails.
1. Speak with [[Custodian Zaddar Sullissia]] again.
1. Examine the door of the tomb of Sir Haligan {{waypoint -75.89, -15.53, 18.26}}. Select "Remove the wedge" in the pop-up dialogue window.
1. Return to [[Custodian Zaddar Sullissia]]. {{waypoint -72.71, -13.06, 106.38}}

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Book of the Dead (Shield)]]
  - [[Book of the Dead (Book)]]
- +300 faction with **The Academy of Arcane Science**
