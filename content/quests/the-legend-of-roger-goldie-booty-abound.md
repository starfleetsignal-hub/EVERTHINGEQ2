## Steps

1. Follow the water up from [[Ryjesium Lake]] to the waterfall. Behind the waterfall is a deeper section of riverbed. On the flat section of this deeper area of the riverbed is a small mound of dirt at {{waypoint 1150.0, 40.3, -401.1}}. It does not appear on Tracking and it may take many passes searching this deeper area of the riverbed for the mound of dirt to be sighted.
1. Return to [[Ole' Man Silvers]].
