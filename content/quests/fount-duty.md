## Notes

- requires 22,500 [[House Yrzu (Faction)]]
- Look directly below House Yrzu. 4 spawn points that refresh quickly near {{waypoint 157, 204, -611}} and further in the bluish area.
- Founts are trackable. (11/11/2018)

## Steps

1. collect and kill the following
   - Use the [[Black Mirror]] on 6 subversion founts ![a subversion fount](images/A_subversion_fount.jpg)
   - Kill 6 [[A fount remnant|fount remnants]] *which spawn after using the black mirror*
     - possible locations
       - {{waypoint 46, 111, -13}} {{waypoint 211, 111, -122}} {{waypoint 209, 114, 156}} {{waypoint 254, 89, -49}} {{waypoint 278, 74, -15}} {{waypoint -193, 13, 403}} {{waypoint -21, -11, 653}}
       - {{waypoint -249, 16, 525}} {{waypoint -144, 14, 584}} {{waypoint -96, -22, 662}} {{waypoint -33, -17, 692}} {{waypoint 74, 58, 683}} {{waypoint 113, 70, 767}}
       - {{waypoint 313, -73, 582}} {{waypoint 276, 63, 338}} {{waypoint 266, 61, 338}} {{waypoint 137, 52, 184}} {{waypoint 215, 47, 214}} {{waypoint 426, 55, 295}}
       - {{waypoint 115, 70, 766}} {{waypoint 550, 37, 315}} {{waypoint 592, 27, 280}} {{waypoint 665, 21, -869}}
1. Return to [[Ilarmna]] {{waypoint 101, 579, -622}}.
