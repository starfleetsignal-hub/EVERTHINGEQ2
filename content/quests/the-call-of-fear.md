## Steps

1. Seek out the disillusioned crusaders in Kunzar Jungle's [[Outer Sebilis]]:
   - Speak to [[Quartermaster Hedoral]] {{waypoint -261, -118, 1068}}
   - Speak to [[Hierophant Rodalia]] {{waypoint -346, -116, 1009}}
   - Speak to [[Captain Anguric]] {{waypoint -437, -122, 950}}
     - in order, outer to inner.
1. Venture into [[Sebilis]] (door at {{waypoint -581, -103, 949}} in [[Kunzar Jungle]]) and locate weapons, armor and supplies within the city.
   - ![Sebilis Supply Crate](images/Sebilis_Supply_Crate.jpg)Find 5 supply crates: Mercantile District around {{waypoint -171, -14, 171}} and the Training Hall and Study {{waypoint 87, -12, 172}}. For example, there might be 3 in the first and 2 in the second place.
   - ![Sebilis Weapon Rack](images/Sebilis_Weapon_Rack.jpg)Find 5 weapons on weapon racks: In The Enlisted Barracks {{waypoint 185, -8, 127}} and the Officer's Barracks  {{waypoint 110, -10, 220}}
   - ![Sebilis Pieces of Armor](images/Sebilis_Pieces_of_Armor.jpg)Find 5 pieces of armor (looks like Exquisite chests): Also in the Enlisted Barracks {{waypoint 185, -8, 127}} and the Officer's Barracks {{waypoint 110, -10, 220}}
1. Return to [[Xuurk]] (Lost Temple of Cabilis / Fens of Nathsar)
1. In [[Riliss (POI)|Riliss]] ([[Fens of Nathsar]]) locate [[Proctor Talnisk]] representative of Dominus Rile; speak to him about an alliance {{waypoint -800, 67, -88}}
   - **Note:** Do not enter the building, lest you aggro Dominus Rile (Epic 84 ^^^)
   - If he's bugged, you can fix it by using "/target proctor" and then "/hail"
1. In [[Charasis: Vault of Eternal Sleep]] (enter through the [[Jarsath Wastes]], {{waypoint 607, -126, 198}}) loot a jewelry box on the left just after you enter the room of the [[Praetor of the Phylactery]] {{waypoint 23, -4, -165}}
   - You will have to do the walkthrough for [[Charasis: Vault of Eternal Sleep]] before you can open the door to retrieve the music box.![Praetor of the Phylactery's jewelry box](images/Praetor_of_the_Phylactery's_jewelry_box.jpg)
1. Return to the [[Proctor Talnisk]] in [[Riliss (POI)|Riliss]] ([[Fens of Nathsar]]) with the necklace to complete the agreement.
1. Return to [[Xuurk]] (Lost Temple of Cabilis / Fens of Nathsar) {{waypoint -80, 23, -1053}}
