## Steps

1. Kill creatures that have been plaguing the trolls of Grobb
   - Kill 7 [[A blood gorger (Moors of Ykesha)|blood gorgers]]
   - Kill 7 [[An Innothule glider|Innothule swamp gliders]]
1. Return to [[Nurtza Skullcrusher]]

## Rewards

- +10,000 faction with **Clan Grobb**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Grobb Snakeskin Belt]]
  - [[Gorger Gut Bow]]
