## Steps

1. Go to [[Savage Weald]] to kill animals, then click the Spiritual Offering Bowl that appears. ![A Spiritual Offering Bowl](images/A_Spiritual_Offering_Bowl.png)
   - Hunt 3 [[A moor wolf|moor wolves]] near the [[Ruins of Shar Makhdi]] {{waypoint -332.27, 62.52, -563.75}}.
   - Hunt 3 owlbears in [[Grimling Forest]] around {{waypoint -728.84, 38.26, -372.86}}.
   - Hunt 3 [[Saurek darkclaw|saurek darkclaws]] in [[Grimling Forest]] around {{waypoint -792.36, 46.78, -537.86}}.
1. Inspect the **A Freshly Killed Darkclaw** carcass at {{waypoint -607, 60, -471}} ![A Freshly Killed Darkclaw](images/A_Freshly_Killed_Darkclaw.png)
   - Defeat [[A wounded tigress]] and return her soul to the empyrea.
1. Return to Jalid Mpenda in [[City of Shar Vahl]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 43g 24s 30c 69440 status
