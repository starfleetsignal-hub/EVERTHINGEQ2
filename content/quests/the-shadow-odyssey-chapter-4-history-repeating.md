## Steps

1. Go to [[East Freeport]] to [[Ignacius Fallon]] at {{waypoint -62, -38, 30}}
1. Head to [[The Obelisk of Lost Souls]], where people have reported seeing tapestries related to the Ethernauts
   1. Get to [[The Keeper of Nihility]]'s room 2nd level of the dungeon {{waypoint 47, 566, 453}}. You need to have completed the quest [[Summoning the Creator]] to get there.(You no longer have to do the access quest.)
   1. Determine whether the tapestries are connected to the Ethernauts by clicking any of them, once inside the Keeper's room
   1. Search for a tapestry representing Bayle. Walk near the one with a Claymore on it. {{waypoint 32, 568, 480}}
   1. Search for a tapestry representing Eylee. Walk near the one with a Mandolin on it. {{waypoint 65, 568, 494}}
   1. Search for a tapestry representing Fiddlewiz. Walk near the one with a Coverall on it. {{waypoint 58.5, 568, 498}}
   1. Search for a tapestry representing Kruzz. Walk near the one with a Fork on it. {{waypoint 58.5, 568, 445.5}}
   1. Search for a tapestry representing Asharae. Walk near the one with a Mage Robe on it. {{waypoint 64, 568, 451}}
   1. Search for a tapestry representing Kaltuk. Walk near the one with a Simple Robe on it. {{waypoint 32, 568, 492}}
   1. Search for a tapestry representing Nurgg. Walk near the one with a Shield on it. {{waypoint 65, 568, 479}}
   1. Locate any missing tapestries - After you run into each tapestry to update the named Ethernauts, find the blank spot on the wall where a tapestry could have been hanging. The blank spot is behind a broken stone pillar near the entrance door in the same room.   <s>Just have to keep running around the pillar.</s>  (**Note 1** : (*Steps 2/1 through 2/9*) Running in two complete counter clockwise circles, touching each wall with a tapestry will update the quest.) {{waypoint 50.5, 568, 502}} (**Note 2:** (*Step 2/10*)) I hopped up beside the broken pillar near the entrance at (51.79 568.10 501.30 204.68 0.00 0.00 ) and the quest updated. The broken pillar lies against the wall.)  (**Note 3:** This room is a good place to collect Glowing Shards Collectables from Shinys.)
1. Return to [[East Freeport]] to talk to [[Ignacius Fallon]] again about the missing tapestries
1. Break into the Nasin's home and retrieve the tapestries
   1. Go to [[North Freeport]], [[3 Compassion Road]] and run along the left side of the house {{waypoint -150, -58, -226}} to be automatically zoned into The Nasin Manor Library (Note: The person on this stage of the quest must be the one to pull their group in)
   1. Kill the lvl 83^^^ guard waiting for you inside (difficult but soloable by classes with root or mezz, easier if you bring a group.  Hits really hard, and has 247k hp.  (You have to kill the guard in order to pick up the tapestries to further the quest.)
   1. Take down the two tapestries inside the library.

![Tapestry of a Bow](images/Tapestry_of_a_Bow.jpg)![Tapestry of a Flying Ship](images/Tapestry_of_a_Flying_Ship.jpg)

1. Replace the tapestries in [[The Obelisk of Lost Souls]] by heading back to the 2nd level and click any of the tapestries on the walls
1. Return to [[Firmroot Moot]] and talk to [[The Great Morsley]]

## Rewards

At least 1p 15g 9500 status
