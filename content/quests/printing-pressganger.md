## Steps

1. Go up the lift and speak to [[Datti Corywocket]] {{waypoint 1165, -480, 430}}
1. Crank the printing  machine next to her SEVERAL times, untill it updates.
1. Speak to Datti
1. Collect 3 octopus ink from the octopi in the harbor. ([[An icebound shell-cracker]] and [[A lurking shell-cracker]] both update, 100%.
1. Return to Datti
1. Crank the printing machine 6 more times.  It works! You receive [[A Stack of Newly Printed Pamphlets]]
1. Spread the propaganda to 5 gnome pirate workers downstairs on the docks.  (Target the gnome pirates, use the item)
1. Return to Dort
