## Steps

1. Deliver the rugs to [[Lord Bennet]] in [[The Keep of the Gnollslayers]] at {{waypoint -2100, -44, 434}}.
1. Return to Hartok Woolyman for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Adept's Woven Gloves]] (All Mages)
  - [[Footman's Worked Gauntlets]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Initiate's Threaded Hand Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Naturalist's Sewn Leather Gloves]] (Druids)
  - [[Plunderer's Small Link Chain Gloves]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Seer's Rustic Handguards]] (Shamans)
  - [[Theist's Shaped Plate Gloves]] (Clerics)
