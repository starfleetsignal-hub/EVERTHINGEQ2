## Steps

1. Visit [[The Fools Gold]]. {{waypoint -70, -17, 2}}
1. Speak to [[Sheriff Anrey Scarfoot]]. {{waypoint -69, -13, -0}}
1. Speak to [[Deputy Slyfoot]]. {{waypoint -69, -18, 6}}
1. Read the book.
1. Hail the following deputies:
   - [[Deputy Bristletoe]] {{waypoint -55, -23, 40}}
   - [[Deputy Spurrfire]]; roams [[West Rivervale]] near the general stores
   - [[Deputy Shallowfoot]] {{waypoint -77, -19, 97}}
   - [[Deputy Guraffin]] {{waypoint -107, -26, -81}}
   - [[Deputy Hillview]]; runs around {{waypoint -438.82, -0.41, -106.37}}
   - [[Deputy Crookfellow]] {{waypoint -56, -24, -56}}
1. Read the book.
1. Slay 10 [[Rabid jaggedtooth|rabid jaggedteeth]].
1. Read the book to obtain your reward.
