## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 48 [[Basilisk meat]]
- 120 [[Reef cluster]]
- 120 [[Silvered fleck]]
- 172 [[Strangler root]]
- 2 [[Flitterfin tang]]
- 8 [[Nimbic hide]]
- 300 [[Celestial Coal]]
- 50 [[Celestial Kindling]]

## Steps

1. Go to the [[South Wing Stacks]], on the main floor of the Library, then take the teleporter at {{waypoint 214.75, -19.59, 242.51}} to the [[Prophecy Portal Gallery]]. Enter [[Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands]] through the portal at {{waypoint 712.39, 412.96, 217.04}}.
1. Speak with [[Baerin Thunderaxe]] at {{waypoint 631, 61, 99}}.
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Healing Stone]] and craft 12 [[Healing Stone]] on a [[Work Bench]].
   - Each combine makes 1-2 Healing Stones, so you should only need 6 combines.
   - There is a Workbench in [[Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands]] at {{waypoint 523, 85.3, 89}}.
1. Speak with [[Baerin Thunderaxe]] at {{waypoint 631, 61, 99}} in [[Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands]].
1. Scibe [[Recipe Scroll: roguefurt cheese sandwich]] and craft a [[Roguefurt Cheese Sandwich]] on a Stove and Keg.
   - You can use the fire pit next to [[Baerin Thunderaxe]] at {{waypoint 630, 61, 105}}
1. To obtain a new [[Inert Runestone]], speak to a Bubonian Runecarver at {{waypoint 74, 87, -371}}.![a Bubonian Runecarver](images/A_Bubonian_Runecarver.jpg)
1. Take the replacement runestone to [[Dylindra Morganna]] in the [[Myrist Librarian Quarters]] at {{waypoint 306, -35, 165}}.

## Rewards

- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
