## Steps

1. Click the camp fire near Tora at {{waypoint 107.70, -345.34, -600.27}}.
1. Click the axe on table near Tora.
1. Click the axe on table near Tora again; you will be zoned into Vision of the Past.
1. Speak back and forth with with Tora's ancestor ([[General Bragmur]]) and [[Tora]] until the quest completes.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
