## Steps

1. Scribe the [[Kerra Traditional Medicine]] recipe page you received.
1. Mix up some of the kerra herbal healing salve. (chemistry table)
   - Supplies are in inventory, but you'll need 1 [[Ethereal candle]] for fuel, obtainable from a tradeskill fuel vendor.  If you have that and a portable chemistry table you can make it on the spot.
1. Return to [[Chinook]]

## Rewards

- At least 20g 5s 35c
- +1,250 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +500 faction with **Kerra Isle Outfitters**

## Notes

- The Kerra Herbal Healing Salve is a house item (rent status reduction 100) which you will be able to keep after delivering it in the next quest.
