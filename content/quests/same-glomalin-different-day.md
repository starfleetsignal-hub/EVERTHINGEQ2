## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[A Stonebrunt stalagmaw|Stonebrunt stalagmaws]] east of Moonfield Hamlet {{waypoint -301, 381, 1240}}.
1. Return to Ebodold Tongofur.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Stonebrunt Pouch of Blunt Knives]] (Beastlords, Brawlers)
  - [[Ivory Cored Bow]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Reborn Legend's Focus]]
  - [[Ornate Ulteran Bow]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues, Channelers)
  - [[Ulteran Focus]] (All Mages, all Priests)
