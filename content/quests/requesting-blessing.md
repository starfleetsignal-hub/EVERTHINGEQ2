## Notes

- You need to be able to speak [[Goblish]] to speak with [[Glibnox]]

## Steps

1. Find the soothsayer [[Glibnox]] in [[Twark]] {{waypoint 132, -164, -768}}.
1. Glibnox needs 3 slug slime.![tame forest slug](images/Tame_forest_slug.jpg)
   - Find the tame forest slugs around Twark  {{waypoint 213, -183, -727}}. Left-click the slug, or Right click and 'reach for the slime'.
1. Return to Glibnox.

## Rewards

- +2000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
- [[Charm of Lost Things (QuestReward)|Charm of Lost Things]]
- [[Sovereign of Rile]] (faction currency)
- At least 1p 53g 9s
