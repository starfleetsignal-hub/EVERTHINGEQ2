## Steps

1. Take the portal to [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]] next to [[Queen Bunny]] and speak with [[Felonious Frostfae]]. {{waypoint -609, 175, 991}}
1. Speak with [[Gimlet Gigglegibber]], next to Felonious. {{waypoint -608, 175, 993}}
1. Speak with Felonious Frostfae again.
1. Speak to Gimlet Gigglegibber again. Give him 10s (the price of a lotto ticket) and select the option to 'pull down on his arm like a lever'.
1. Enter [[McScroogle Corp. HQ]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]] through the manhole cover. {{waypoint -653, 175, 1088}}![McScroogle Headquarters](images/McScroogle_Headquarters.jpg)
1. Inside McScroogle Corp. Headquarters, click the switch at {{waypoint 47, 2, -36}} and select the option [Pass code Up-Up-Down-Down-Left-Right-Left-Right]. It will then say 'Pass Code Accepted'. Press the switch once more and the gate will open.
1. Pick up twelve (12) [[Explosive Material]] from the *Crate of Explosive Materials*. {{waypoint 51, 1, -20}}![Crate of Explosive Materials](images/Crate_of_Explosive_Materials.jpg)
1. Pull the fire alarm switch. {{waypoint 52, 1, 15}}
   - Be warned, once you do this the building will fill with [[A security bot|security bots]].![Fire Alarm](images/Fire_Alarm.jpg)
1. Start torching the twelve (12) scribing desks:
   - {{waypoint 43, 0, 25}}
   - {{waypoint 39, 0, 25}}
   - {{waypoint 35, 0, 24}}
   - {{waypoint -44, 12, 11}}
   - {{waypoint 2, 15, 9}}
   - {{waypoint 26, 12, 36}}
   - {{waypoint 39, 12, 35}}
   - {{waypoint -7, 15, -2}}
   - {{waypoint -45, 12, -25}}
   - {{waypoint 6, 14, -4}}
   - {{waypoint 12, 12, -29}}
   - {{waypoint 29, 12, -29}}
1. Defeat [[Mr. McScroogle (McScroogle Corp. HQ)|Mr. McScroogle]]. {{waypoint 0, 3, 2}}
1. Hail Mr. McScroogle and select your rationale for blowing up the desks.
   - If you are defeated by him, he vanishes and you must start the entire quest over again!
1. Return to [[Queen Bunny]] in [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]].
   - There is a portal back to Frostfell Wonderland Village at {{waypoint -621, 176, 932}}.
