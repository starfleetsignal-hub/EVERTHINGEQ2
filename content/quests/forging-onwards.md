## Notes

Requires Artisan 100
Supply list:

- 1 blushing umbrite (Received from  Queen Alwenielle)
- 6 magic-infused zeabud pollen
- 5 [[Thaumic Coal]] -it can be purchased from [[Tybek]] (a fuel merchant) at {{waypoint -144, -4, -58}}.

## Steps

1. She wishes to create an Imitation Chaos Stone. She directs you to [[Elanluelle]] {{waypoint -481, 237, 349}} for help to do this. Receive quest.
   - Gather 6 pollen from ripe zeabuds around the glaufaye village around {{waypoint -537.00, 122.34, -65.36}} (it's not marked on the map, but it is situated in the ravine between the [[Abyssal Overlook]] and [[Luminous Steppe]]).  ![ripe zeabud](images/Ripe_zeabud.jpg)
   - *These come from the yellow pinecone looking plants. They are literally everywhere around the Glaufaye village.*
   - *Note: can be done while flying* Here is a very good one (infinite for me) : {{waypoint -310, 119, -50}}
1. Use the [[Forge of Brell]] in [[Maldura]] {{waypoint 74, 35, -172}}  to create the [[Imitation Chaos Stone]]. The recipe will scribe automatically. Either side of the Forge will work. Alternatively you can use the [[Forge of Brell (House Item)]]
1. Bring the stone back to [[Queen Alwenielle]] in Zou'Lidelas in Thalumbra. {{waypoint -333, 240, 380}}
1. Speak to [[Elanluelle]]. {{waypoint -486, 237, 350}}
1. Return to where you met [[Queen Alwenielle]]. She will **NOT** be there. Read a sparkling scribbled note on the floor of The Tylwyth's Hut. {{waypoint -333, 240, 380}}  ![a scribbled note](images/A_scribbled_note.jpg)
1. Tell [[Elanluelle]] the Queen is missing!
