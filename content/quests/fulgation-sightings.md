## Steps

- Release [[!Fluffy - Series 7]] near the minotaur caves southeast of the Great Gear
  - The Minotaur caves are at loc {{waypoint -1260, 94, 1054}}
- Speak with [[P.J. Dangsprocket]] at Fruglin Observatory.
  - Fruglin Observatory is up a path at {{waypoint -1029.50, 98.06, 792.99}} south side or {{waypoint -961, 172, 738}} north side.
- Retrieve the data cylinder from !Fluffy
  - You will find [[!Fluffy]] running around **outside the caves** somewhere, retrieve the cylinder.
- Go see [[Watchman Fleep (NPC)|Watchman Fleep]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 2g 99s 88c
