## Steps

1. Find an invaluble artifact in [[Ravenscale Repository]]
   - Loot a [[R'Jhere's Severed Hand]] from the corpse of [[Head Archivist R'Jhere]]. Use the hand on a braizer at {{waypoint 0, 7, -290}} to summon the ghostly Mayong Mistmoore.
   - Equip and use the [[Illusion of the Head Archivist]] and then talk to Mayong Mistmoore. This will unlock the chest further on in the zone.
     - *Note that anyone talking to the ghostly Mayong without the effect of the robe will summon a repository guardian.  Be sure to EQUIP the robe (it'll end up in a charm slot) and then use it, just using the robe does not work.*
   - Open the chest at {{waypoint -87, 17, -364}} to get the [[Ankh of Ydal (Quest Item)|Ankh of Ydal]] and complete this step. The previous two steps are silent updates, and only one person gets the hand or robe so they must coordinate to unlock the chest. The chest will update the quest for everyone in the group, however.
1. Return to [[Marshal Ralem Christof (LP)|Marshal Ralem Christof]] in Loping Plains

## Rewards

- At least 36g 79s 48c
- +20000 faction with **The League of Freethinkers**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Bracelet of Freethinking]]
  - [[Ralem's Wrist Chain]]
