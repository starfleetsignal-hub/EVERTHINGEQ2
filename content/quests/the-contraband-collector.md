## Steps

1. Defeat [[Dockmaster Jit'coel]] in [[Hold of Rime: The Fortress Spire]]
1. Click barrels on the docks (I believe each person with the quest has to click different barrels)
1. Carry the crates and barrels away from the docks
1. You will be ambushed by a group of Rime Guards - kill them
1. Return to [[Signy Silverbreaker]]

## Rewards

- At least 41g 63s 17c
