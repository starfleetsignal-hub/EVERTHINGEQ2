*The following quest, [[Karath the Forgotten]], is only available upon completion of all other quests offered by the Watcher of the Fields.*

## Steps

1. Kill [[A grassland beetle|grassland beetle]]s for 2 uncommon quest updates.
1. Kill [[A highland lion cub|highland lion cub]]s for 7 uncommon quest updates.
1. Kill 18 [[A shallows fish|shallows fish]], found just off [[Dead River Docks]] and near [[Shrine of the Sirens]].
1. Return to the [[Watcher of the Fields]]. Click the banner corresponding to Kuranam to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- A ring, based on class:
  - [[Ringlet of Captured Souls]] - (All Fighters)
  - [[Ring of Captured Souls]] - (All Mages)
  - [[Emblem of Captured Souls]] - (All Scouts)
  - [[Signet of Captured Souls]]
