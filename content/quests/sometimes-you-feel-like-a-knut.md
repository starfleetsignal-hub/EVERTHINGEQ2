## Steps

1. Equip [[Griz]] and head down to [[Demon's Delve]]. Griz will guide you to an empty sack {{waypoint 91, -67, 119}} it is in the back of the cave, on a little table.
1. Griz will guide you to the NW tunnel, where there are two carts in the cave {{waypoint 243, -53, 51}}. One of the carts contains a black crystal (move around the carts - when in the right area the dialog will begin, then click through Griz's chat dialog to receive the update).
1. Click on the cart to destroy the crystal
1. Return to [[Knut Orcbane]]

## Rewards

- At least 5s 81c
- [[Halasian high backed chair]]
- [[New Halasian Courser Whistle]]
