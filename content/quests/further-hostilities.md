## Prerequistes

You need to have -10k faction or better with the Defenders of the Seal to start. [[Amirah]] is located near the pool in the Defenders' portion of [[The Hole]]

## Steps

Defender Amirah has given me orders to capture elemental essences from within the Tower of Serilis and then unleash them at the marker in Old Paineel

1. I must capture 5 elemental essences.  These are any "small" elementals, such as the ones just inside the elemental area {{waypoint 68, 80, -533}}, .  Using the potion does not agro the group, and does not affect faction.
1. I must free the wisps at the designated marker in Old Paineel.  Go to {{waypoint -327, -40, 26}}, near the cannon, and target yourself.  Use the potion again to release the elementals.  This also does not affect faction or cause agro.
1. Return to Amirah in Dartain's Fortress within The Hole.

## Rewards

1. [[Mark of Manaar]]
1. At least 55g 70s 61c
1. +2500 faction with **Defenders of the Seal**
