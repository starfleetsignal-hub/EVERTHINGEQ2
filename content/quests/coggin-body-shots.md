## Steps

1. Speak to [[Bartyn Cogginshots]] at the top of the tower {{waypoint 1194, 35, 1136}}.
1. Collect items for Bartyn so that he can make a [[Ghorkaal Teir'Dal Illusion Belt|Teir'Dal illusion]]
   - Kill [[A Ghorkaal shadowknight]] for its blood {{waypoint 832, 36, 991}} NOTE: killing each Shadowknight will cause a level 103 Ghorkal poisonspinner to spawn nearby.
   - Collect 8 malefic quartz from [[Ghorkaal]] - these are red crystals found around the harbor in Ghorkaal.  Possible locs include:
     - {{waypoint 955, 36, 732}}
     - {{waypoint 1001, 11, 883}}
     - {{waypoint 936, 11, 883}}
     - {{waypoint 914, 11, 878}}
     - {{waypoint 1094, 14, 736}}
     - {{waypoint 1051, 22, 747}}
     - {{waypoint 857, 11, 796}}
     - There are also a few down by the docks in the same areas as the others.
     - Respawn time on the crystals seems to be about 5 minutes.
1. Return to Bartyn

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g 80s
