## Steps

1. Search the underwater shipwreck off the pier of [[Danak Shipyards]] for the following items:
   - Marshal Somers' Favorite Chair {{waypoint -360, -80, 1390}}
   - Marshal Somers' Gear Locker {{waypoint -422, -71, 1395}}
   - Marshal Somers' Practice Dummy  {{waypoint -402, -70, 1383}}
   - Marshal Somers' Crate of Personal Items {{waypoint -413, -64, 1356}}
   - Marshal Somers' Favorite Book {{waypoint -351, -69, 1350}}
1. Return to [[Somers the Hard (Npc)|Somers the Hard]].
