## Steps

1. Click on the Treasure Chest underwater. {{waypoint -66, -8, 510}}
1. Click on the Water Logged Scroll on the beach. {{waypoint -92, -3, 464}}
1. Place 5 fish on the basket of fish at {{waypoint 20, -3, 451}}. They are movable, and will be along the shore or around the edges of pools of water, but not actually in the water.![a fish](images/Clickable_fish.jpg)
1. After moving all five fishes to the basket, you will receive a messages "A deep sounding tone echoes along the coast as the smell of rotten flesh drifts in the air" and [[Fhoorl-Kurrat]] with rise from the depths. Kill him to get the key.
1. Return to and open the treasure chest to receive the [[The Hand of Glory (Item)|Hand of Glory]]. {{waypoint -66, -8, 510}}
1. Return to Havras. {{waypoint -310, 11, -49}}

## Rewards

- At least 16g 49s 60c
- +1000 faction with **The League of Freethinkers**
