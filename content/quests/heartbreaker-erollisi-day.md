## Notes

- This quest is repeatable once per year.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Florist Vhy]] in [[The Wards]]. {{waypoint 2807, 125, 1157}}
1. Travel to [[Sighing Willow Pond]] in [[The Village of Shin]] on the [[Isle of Mara]]. {{waypoint -181.24, 5.48, -154.31}}
1. Inspect the *Shard of Treachery*.
1. Defeat the *minion of treachery* that spawns, either [[A treacherous hag]] or [[A treacherous fiend]].
1. Destroy the *Shard of Treachery*,
1. Go to the area beneath [[Kelethin]] in [[Greater Faydark]], around {{waypoint 167, 5, 493}}, and do the following:
   - Destroy 8 *Shards of Treachery*
   - Slay 6 *minions of Treachery*
1. Return to Florist Vhy.

## Rewards

- 5 [[Erollisi coin|Erollisi coins]]
- One of the following:
  - 4 [[Copper Kiss Blooms]]
  - [[Treacherous Hag Plushie]]
