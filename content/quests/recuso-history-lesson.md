## Steps

1. Speak to [[Brannelle Lydden]] at {{waypoint -773.84, 52.08, 369.08}}.
1. Gather 8 prickly figs from the Marus Cacti on the [[Marus Plateaus]] near {{waypoint -48.71, 68.50, 224.98}}.
   - *Note: They are nodules on the tall aloe-looking plants (see picture).*![Prickly Fig](images/Prickly_Fig.png)
1. Go to {{waypoint -465.76, 59.75, 338.82}} to spawn [[Breanna Fisher]]
1. Speak to [[Breanna Fisher]]
1. Return to [[Brannelle Lydden]]  at {{waypoint -773.84, 52.08, 369.08}}.

## Rewards

- [[Antique Recuso Smuggler Ring]]
- 4 [[Prickly Fig]]
- At least 1p 53g 9s
