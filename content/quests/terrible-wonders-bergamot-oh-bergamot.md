## Steps

1. Speak with [[Frau Choraline Lent]] {{waypoint -1128.28, -10.85, -464.36}}
1. Speak with [[Quibble]] in Umbrahollow Wilds. {{waypoint 809.69, 117.31, -374.08}}
   - Find 5 bergamot fruit - Some possible locations:
   - {{waypoint 636.07, 105.66, -393.24}}
   - {{waypoint 610.05, 105.16, -381.06}}
   - {{waypoint 505.38, 114.27, -550.58}}
   - {{waypoint 362.13, 113.86, -586.07}}
   - {{waypoint 367.28, 109.24, -482.88}}
   - Find 5 tea leaves - Some possible locations:
   - {{waypoint 595.31, 110.96, -347.00}}
   - {{waypoint 526.48, 114.95, -352.21}}
   - {{waypoint 362.54, 106.85, -553.95}}
   - {{waypoint 531.83, 125.03, -512.90}}
   - {{waypoint 253.25, 107.40, -647.62}}
   - Harvest 40 deeppod beans.
1. Return to find Quibble's sack {{waypoint 808.88, 117.21, -375}}
1. Inspect the webbing. {{waypoint 820.68, 117.04, -373.86}}
1. Tell [[Frau Choraline Lent]] of Quibble's disappearance. Receive Recipe: Scroll: Bergamot Tea.
   - Craft some tea and bring it to [[Frau Choraline Lent]] at the campfire. Requires 5 Formless Kindling.
1. Find more info about the webbing. Speak with [[Deeg]] {{waypoint -217.34, 89.17, -1049.70}}
1. Receive Tontu Fish Surprise Recipe
   - Collect the fish to make Tontu Fish Surprise. Opens collection Tontu Fish Surprise. Receive No-Trade Collectible *Tontu Fish Surprise Recipe*.
   - Collection Reward: Tontu Fish Surprise. Fragment of Collected Depths.
1. I should speak to Deeg {{waypoint -217.34, 89.17, -1049.70}} at the tontu camp.
1. Speak with [[Frau Choraline Lent]] in Val'Zyroth Ness.
