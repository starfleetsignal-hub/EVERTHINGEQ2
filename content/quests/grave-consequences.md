## Steps

1. Hunt 12 [[A graveyard plunderer|graveyard plunderers]] around {{waypoint -57.51, 98.67, 268.01}}.
1. Defeat [[Sister Corpselia]].
   - *She spawns after killing 12 plunderers, then disappears before you can kill her.* ![Sister Corpselia](images/Sistercorpselia.png)
1. Return to [[Dabhiti]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- [[Ancient Forlorn Gravemarker]]
