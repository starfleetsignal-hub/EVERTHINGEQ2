## Steps

1. Kill 25 [[A Sabertooth lookout (Thundering Steppes)|Sabertooth lookouts]].
   - A good location is [[The Sabertooth Den]] {{waypoint 1319, 15, -315}}, where about eleven are up at once.
   - Another good spot is the crater at {{waypoint 957, -25, -126}}, near The Sabertooth Den.

## Rewards

- Experience
- One of the following based on class:
  - [[Antonican Bow]] (Predators, Rangers)
  - [[Studded Gnoll Skin Satchel]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Ornate Compound Bow of the Paladin]]
  - [[Rod of Heretical Judgement]]
  - [[Wand of High Hopes]]
