*This quest is repeatable up to 5 times..*

## Steps

1. Kill 10 of the nearby troglodytes for common updates.
1. Examine the barrel again to fill it up.
1. Hail [[Becka Snowsworn]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 20s 65c
