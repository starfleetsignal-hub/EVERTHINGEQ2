## Description

"Thekela Gillywump is running low on ingredients. Normally Brodo fetches food for her, but he's been busy lately. She has asked me to get her some Growzzat honey from the area east of Memorial Hill." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Inspect 3 "fallen Growzzat bixie"s. Right click on the corpse then select inspect. Around {{waypoint -222.95, 15.52, -54.27}}
1. Gather 3 [[Broken Growzzat hive|broken Growzzat hives]] from the trees around {{waypoint -286.80, 20.64, -150.03}}
1. Return to [[Thekela Gillywump]]

## Rewards

- At least 1s 91c
