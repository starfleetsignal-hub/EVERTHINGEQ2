## Steps

1. Apply the corrosive acid to the four chests of equipments in the Storeroom {{waypoint -672, 117, 331}}. There is two on the ground and two up the lift.
1. Place the six explosive barrels inside the six Kromzek shields in the Weapons Room {{waypoint -644, 117, 416}}.
1. Return to Boyd.

## Rewards

- At least 23g 64s 59c
