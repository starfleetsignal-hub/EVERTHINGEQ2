   - Eight [[A soulless assistant|Soulless assistants]] at a room {{waypoint -15, 594, 403}}, where [[Xychlzys]] is. Two more at {{waypoint 17, 589, 199}} [[The Cistern of Immortality]]. Respawn time around 15 to 20 minutes.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 soulless zombies.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 soulless zombies.
1. Read the book.
1. Slay 10 [[A disciple of shadow|disciples of shadows]].
1. Read the book..
1. Slay 10 disciples of shadow.
1. Read the book.
1. Slay 10 disciples of shadow.
1. Read the book.
