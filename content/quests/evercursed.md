## Steps

1. Find and speak to a Wayward who may have encountered the Everlings
   - Speak to the [[An awaiting Wayward|awaiting Wayward]] at {{waypoint 84, -6, 546}}
   - Speak to the [[An anxious Wayward|anxious Wayward]] at {{waypoint 135, -4, 472}}
   - Speak to [[A Wayward dockworker]] at {{waypoint 180, -3, 471}}
1. kill 5 [[An etherwisp|wisps]] for tentacles. Help the bard [[Fabian]] {{waypoint 23, -4, 372}} by gathering 5 etherwisp tentacles.

- At least 1p 30g
