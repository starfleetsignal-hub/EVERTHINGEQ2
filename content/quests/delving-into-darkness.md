## Steps

1. Read the book [[1001 Tales of Maj'Dul]], located right next to Antiquitor Kantus Mor'Tael, and write a new line in the book for an update.
1. Speak to [[Antiquitor Kantus Mor'Tael]].
1. Read the book again, this time scribe the title "City of the Everlasting".
1. Speak to Antiquitor Kantus Mor'Tael once more to get an update.
1. Speak to [[Seeker Abdeef]] near zone-in of Maj'Dul. {{waypoint -228, 160, -104}}
1. Return to the [[The Living Tombs|Living Tombs]] and gather 10 different flaming staffs from the [[Trade Court]] area:
   - [[Staff of 'Un]]: {{waypoint -593, -18, 317}} or {{waypoint -570, -10, 305}} or {{waypoint -645, -18, 322}}
   - [[Staff of Orus]]: {{waypoint -671, -17, 314}} or {{waypoint -695, -18, 267}} or {{waypoint -658, -18, 244}}
   - [[Staff of 'Adh]]: {{waypoint -679, -18, 137}} or {{waypoint -653, -15, 169}} or {{waypoint -658, -18, 199}}
   - [[Staff of Nief]]: {{waypoint -647, -18, 114}} or {{waypoint -628, -15, 150}} or {{waypoint -640, -15, 150}}
   - [[Staff of Sek]]: {{waypoint -622, -18, 117}} or {{waypoint -619.74, -3.27, 127.26}}
   - [[Staff of Sul]]: {{waypoint -625, -15, 168}} or {{waypoint -590, -18, 177}} or {{waypoint -615.65, -15.72, 198.12}}
   - [[Staff of Phar]]: {{waypoint -618, 8, 91}} or {{waypoint -593, -4, 117}} or {{waypoint -563, 4, 145}}
   - [[Staff of Fyr]]: {{waypoint -576, 8, 102}} or {{waypoint -600, 5, 205}} or {{waypoint -610, -15, 233}}
   - [[Staff of Kan]]: {{waypoint -658, -15, 226}} or {{waypoint -677, -18, 188}} or {{waypoint -659, -15, 213}}
   - [[Staff of Valas]] {{waypoint -582, -17, 262}} or {{waypoint -588, -18, 255}} or {{waypoint -610, -19, 244}}
1. Return to Seeker Abdeef.
