*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Enter the [[The Court of the Blades (Quest Instance)|Court of the Blades]] via the sewer grate at {{waypoint 8, 149, 92}}. Note that this instance can be completed with a duo.
1. Complete the following within 5.0 minutes: (Do NOT try to box this instance you do not have time to run with more than one toon at a time, also if one person in group is not on this step, no one will be able to enter through the backdoor)
   1. Inspect ![a glass frame](images/Image:A_Thief_in_the_House_of_Blades_Glass_Casel.jpg); 1st floor {{waypoint 18, 0, 5}}
   1. Inspect ![a book](images/Image:A_Thief_in_the_House_of_Blades_Book.jpg); 2nd floor {{waypoint -16, 6, 8}}
   1. Inspect ![a picture](images/Image:A_Thief_in_the_House_of_Blades_Picture.jpg); 2nd floor {{waypoint 16, 5, 12}}
   1. Inspect ![a scroll](images/Image:A_Thief_in_the_House_of_Blades_Scroll.jpg); 1st floor on top of the safe {{waypoint 23, 0, 6}}
   1. Leave the zone the way you came in - remember the exit is found by using the climbable walls positioned on either side of the southern wall on the 2nd floor.
1. Kill [[A Blades Historian]], roaming near the [[The Court of the Blades|Court of the Blades]].
1. Approach ![a skeleton](images/Image:a_skeleton.jpg) in the [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -997, -96, -1200}}. Kill the [[A Raj'Dur raider|Raj'Dur raiders]] that spawn for an uncommon update. You may have to wait ~10 minutes for their respawn if the first group doesn't drop Historian Ganish's banner key. ***NOTE**: the raiders don't spawn at the skeleton. Examine it and head to the ocean - they pop up down the hill from the POI - Toofs*
1. Kill any Alliz Raef Ew lizardman near {{waypoint -488, -130, -872}} until you get Historian Kaldara's key update.
1. Re-enter the [[The Court of the Blades (Quest Instance)|Court of the Blades]] via the sewer grate.
1. Inspect ![the banner](images/Image:A_Thief_in_the_Court_of_Blades_Banner.jpg); 1st floor, the room with the glass frame
1. Exit the way you came in (top floor - use the eastern stairs to get from the 1st floor to the 2nd floor and then use the eastern climby wall to get to the third - door is right there...says "To the servant's exit" when you mouse over it - quest completes upon clicking it)
