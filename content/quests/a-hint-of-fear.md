## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Thundering Steppes]] and speak to [[Zha Gu'Lau (Thundering Steppes)|Zha Gu'Lau]] in [[Village of Thundermist]]. {{waypoint 520, 10, -100}}
1. Kill the pig at {{waypoint 560, 2, -140}}, then gut the pig. Alternately, lead a [[A cow (Thundering Steppes)|cow]] into the barn to kill and gut it.
   - Right-click on the pig to kill it, then right-click again to gut it.
1. Deliver the guts to the northwest house of the village, first bed on the right as you walk in. {{waypoint 588.19, 2.65, -153.94}}
1. Speak with Zha Gu'Lau. After dialogue, will drop a [[The corpse of Jehro Trammul|body]] that you need to pick up.
1. Deliver the corpse to the barn you visited earlier. Use the item from your inventory. {{waypoint 565, 2, -140}}
1. Speak with Zha Gu'Lau.
1. Collect [[Animal Feed|animal feed]] from around near the road that's outside the village. The feed looks like a box and will mostly spawn further south. {{waypoint 830, -1, -125}}.
1. Speak with Zha Gu'Lau.
1. Place the animal feed in the barn-troughs of the village.
1. Collect 6 more animal guts from the farm animals of the village.
   - There are three pigs and three cows, but you will be unable to kill them if an NPC is nearby. If you are unable to kill one because of this, just wait for a respawn.
   - Click on the feeder in corner of barn to lure them and kill them.
1. Place the dead livestock in the beds around the village:
   - {{waypoint 595, 2, -145}}
   - {{waypoint 550, 1, -80}}
1. Speak with Zha Gu'Lau.
