## Notes

- If you try to tag the mobs while in combat with them, a notice comes up in your chat box: "There's no point in tagging them all obvious like in the middle of combat. What kind of spy are you, anyway?"
- The mobs are on a 3-4 minute respawn timer if accidently killed.

## Steps

1. Use [[Queue's Humanoid Tagger]] on the following:
   - [[Spudtop]] {{waypoint 849, 86, -2258}}
   - [[Chilzerrn the Sly]] {{waypoint 1417, 51, -2173}}
   - [[Poogs]] {{waypoint 2068, 163, -2150}}
1. Return [[Germa Prash]] in [[Highton]]. {{waypoint 1275, 159, -2074}}

## Rewards

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- +600 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- [[Broken Toe Belt of Blasting]]
