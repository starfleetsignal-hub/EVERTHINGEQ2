## Description

"Grimply Sniffwistle's cognatic bixie boppers were being exterminated by a large kobold called The Cogsmasher. I have defeated him, and collected his clockwork smashing wrench. Grimply will probably be interested in this." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Talk to [[Grimply Sniffwistle]] at [[Spire Shadow Outpost]].  He may not have the quest icon over his head, but talk to him anyway to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 8s 57c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

Return the Cogsmasher's Cudgel to Grimply.

- Grimply Sniffwistle: "Oh yes! It's wonderful! They've begun to rue the day, with a whole swarm of unstoppable cognatic bixie boppers on the attack!"
- Player Character: "Actually, they're not unstoppable. The kobolds brought in an exterminator who smashed them to bits with a wrench!"
- Grimply Sniffwistle: "What? Unacceptable! I'll have to upgrade their armor. That'll double the weight though. Hmmm, so she'll need stronger wings, upgraded power reserves, a new fandoogler..."
- Player Character: "There's no need. I already beat their exterminator. I have his wrench if you want it."
- Grimply Sniffwistle: "Well, would you look at that! an original Screewoggin S-105 sprocket wrench, and it's not too much worse for the wear. Thank you very much!"
- Player Character: "You're welcome!"

I delivered the cudgel.

Grimply Sniffwistle's was happy to learn that his cognatic bixie boppers won't be getting smashed up any more.
