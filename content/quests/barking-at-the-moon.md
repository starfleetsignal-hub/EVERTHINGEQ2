## Remaining Prerequisites

- You must speak [[To Speak as a Dragon|Draconic]], [[Sathirian]], [[Koada'Dal (Language)|Koada'Dal]], and [[Krombal]] languages.
- You must have done the [[Shades of Drinal Timeline|Chain of Eternity Timeline]] for a legitimate access to Harrow End ( the key doesn't work )
- You must be at least a level 2 crafter (to complete the Chains of Eternity Timeline)
- You must have completed the [[Dark Mail Gauntlets Timeline|Dark Mail Gauntlets]] Heritage Quest

## Steps

1. Return to the Paineel instance where you picked up the quest and retrieve a note on his desk
1. Go to Feerrott and speak with Chiss Elissk at {{waypoint -127, 13, 190}}
   - *must speak [[Sathirian]]*
   - *must have completed [[Shades of Drinal Timeline]] to advance quest*
1. Go to Harrows End and complete [[Harrow's End: On the Trail of Ryizz]]
   1. kill first name, then get to the water tube in the far north room of the level and swim down and kill second name, only that is required
      - *Note: Extremely difficult zone, will require at least a couple people to go with you*
      - The second name will teleport people out of the room.  If this happens to your tank, the boss will leave room and reset the encounter! Bring enough people to burn the boss down before this happens
      - Mercenaries are affected by the teleport aswell, hence duoing with a merc won't necessarily do, unless you have enough damage to just kill him before he teleports you (29/08/16 - Amily, Guk/Halls of Fate)
      - Speak with Melanie Everling after killing the 2nd construct
1. Return to the Paineel instance where you picked up the quest, kill the mob and retrieve the note
1. Go to Skyshrine, and speak with Jorlak {{waypoint 18, 1, -4}}
1. Head to the Lyceum section of contested Skyshrine and follow the blood trails as you did in Cobalt Scar:
   - The blood trail fades soon, so not much time to stop and fight.  Best to have friends keep the path clear of trash for you. If there are names up and in the way, it is best to simply clear the path of them. Then clear back to starting pool and send the group ahead of you a short ways. You have less then 2 minutes per spot so you do not have time to type the coordinates in, best to try and memorize the spots.
   1. Blood pool 1 at {{waypoint 76, 0, 44}} - *(you can see and feel the energy of Ryizz here.)*
   1. Blood Pool 2 at {{waypoint 82, -1, 128}} - *(Ryizz has been here.)*
   1. Blood Pool 3 at {{waypoint 140, 0, 149}} - *(I am so close now, he cannot escape.)*
   1. Blood Pool 4 at {{waypoint 207, 0, 42}} - *(Here, more evidence of his passing.)*
   1. Blood Pool 5 at {{waypoint 282, 0, 0}} - *(Ryizzs' power is strong here...)*
   1. Blood Pool 6 at {{waypoint 248, -1, 170}} - *(He was here just minutes ago.)*
   1. Blood Pool 7 at {{waypoint 294, -1, 170}} - *(Where is he, what are those runes over there.)*
1. Click the runes at the base of the pillar in the center of the room {{waypoint 281, -1, 154}}
1. Speak to Harla Dar in Vesspyr Isles at {{waypoint -107.85, 51, 747.13}}
