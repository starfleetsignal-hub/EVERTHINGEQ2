Bynn Cloingborn is one of the gnemlin mages who have been maintaining the magical defenses of Maldura while the Aberrants attacked. She told me that their stores of several spell components are low. I've volunteered to acquire more abyssal aquata spirit core essences and fathomlurker beaks.

## Steps

1. Hunt these components within the [[Abyssal Chasm]]:
   - Obtain 8 core essences from [[An abyssal aquata spirit|abyssal aquata spirits]] {{waypoint -13.79, -24.51, -261.95}}.
   - Obtain 8 beaks from [[An abyssal fathomlurker|abyssal fathomlurkers]] -29. 79 -194..
1. Return to [[Bynn Cloingborn|Bynn]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 12203 status
- +1000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
