## Steps

1. Enter [[Gerion: Ark of Ascension (Solo)|Gerion: Ark of Ascension [Solo]]]
1. Search for evidence of the Dismal Rage's involvement
   - Go to [[The Dismal Stage]] at {{waypoint -1, -1, -542}}
1. Destroy 8 Dismal Rage soul chambers![A Dismal Rage soul chamber](images/A_Dismal_Rage_soul_chamber.png)
   - Double click on a soul chamber to destroy it, or right click and select Destroy
   - *NOTE: Complete this step before killing the third named, then you can clear the whole area and destroy the soul chambers before Xelha spawns.*
1. Defeat [[Xelha Nevagon]] of the Dismal Rage
1. Return to [[Hiwaaya Al'Naderi]] in [[Gerion, Hold of D'Lere]]

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- 2 x [[Dismal Rage Soul Chamber]]
- One of the following
  - [[Rageful Earring of the Umbrith]]
  - [[Rageful Ear Stud of the Grathok]]
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]]
