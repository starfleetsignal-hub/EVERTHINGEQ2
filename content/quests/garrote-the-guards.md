## Description

I must slay the Tallonite kedons and Tallonite Maru-zonns aboard The Vigilant: Rescue to weaken the defenses of the Tallonites.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Vigilant: Rescue]]
   - Kill 8 [[A Tallonite kedon|Tallonite kedon]]s
   - Kill 5 [[A Tallonite Maru-zonn|Tallonite Maru-zonn]]s
1. Return to Ismat

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
