## Steps

1. Speak with [[Frau Choraline Lent]] {{waypoint -1128.28, -10.85, -464.36}}
   - Gather 50 Tonfu Tofu
   - Gather 50 Deeppod Bean
   - Gather 50 Cavebeast Meat
   - Gather 50 Clayroot Vine
1. Receive Recipe Scroll: Camp Supplies - Requires 100 Formless Kindling. Use the campfire:
   - Craft 10 Fermented Tofu Spirit
   - Craft 10 Roasted Cave Beast
1. Speak to [[Frau Choraline Lent]].
1. Speak to [[Grar]] at the tontu camp {{waypoint -227.67, 88.16, -1039.53}}
1. Receive Recipe Scroll: Heavily Reinforced Barricades
   - Gather 50 Stonebark Lumber
   - Gather 50 Glowstone Crystal
1. Craft 10 Heavily Reinforced Barricades. Requires 50 Formless Kindling
1. Speak to Grar
1. Tell [[Frau Choraline Lent]] of Quibble's situation
