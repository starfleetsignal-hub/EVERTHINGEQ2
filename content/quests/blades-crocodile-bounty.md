## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- Once +20,000 faction has been achieved with the Court of the Blades this quest will no longer be available.

## Steps

1. Kill fifteen crocodiles.
1. Destroy five crocodile nests.
   - These can be found inside the [[The Croc Caves|Croc Caves]]. When you get close to a nest 1-3 [[A crocodile hatchling|crocodile hatchlings]] will spawn. When you destroy the nest, [[A crocodile matron]] will spawn. The matron will attack you even if she is greyed out!
1. Kill two [[A bull crocodile|bull crocodiles]]. {{waypoint -1332, -227, -652}}
   - Kill the groups of [[A saltwater crocodile|saltwater crocodiles]] and [[A deepwater crocodile|deepwater crocodiles]] to spawn the bulls.
1. Return to [[Mahir Lu'ay]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- 2g 99s 47c
- +2500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -2500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -2500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
