This is the third of the Kerran racial quests in Freeport.

## Steps

1. Batarra sends you to talk to [[Barmaid Miala]], in *[[The Hole!]]*, the tavern at the other end of the zone.
1. Miala will send you to [[Armsdealer Melai]], near the North Freeport Gates.
1. Melai relays a terrible story of an Erudite attack upon his brother.  He explains that he can't take vengeance himself as he's watched non-stop, but that I should go talk to the Bowyer [[Thindel]], a friend of his that has vowed to avenge Melai's brother.
1. Bowyer Thindel sends you to listen in on the Erudites that killed Melai's brother.  They apparently meet nearby.
   - Head to the building across from [[The Scratching Post]].  Work from left to right in the broken down rooms until you hear [[A whispering Erudite]] talking.  You'll overhear them a bit, and the quest will update.
   - 2nd room from left, shift+C for crouch, jump the first bed and stay against wall, tricky update spot.
1. Return to Batarra.
