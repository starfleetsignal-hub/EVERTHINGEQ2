## Notes

- [[A soggy boot]] that starts this quest can be fished out from any standard fishing nodes in Norrath while you have your [[Oceansfull Fishing Rod]] activated.
- [[Oceansfull Festival]] event does not have to be active to get the quest starter, but you have to fish out recipe for fishing rod and ingredients to make it from Oceansfull dungeon [[Awuidor: Trench of Terror]] (Solo or Heroic versions which have fishing nodes "shoal of fish")
- [[Give 'em the Boot(s)!]] achievement comes from repeating this Overseer quest 10 times.
