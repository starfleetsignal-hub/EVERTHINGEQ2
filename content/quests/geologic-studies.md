## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[An ashengaze basilisk|ashengaze basilisks]] for blood samples
1. Kill 5 [[A nocturnal hunter|nocturnal hunters]] for skin samples
1. Kill 5 Earthen Manifestations for rock samples
1. Gather 5 bone shavings from larger bone structures.  These are clicky updates at the following waypoints:
   - {{waypoint -737, -455, 203}}
   - {{waypoint -843, -471, 94}}
   - {{waypoint -788, -450, 372}}
   - {{waypoint -667, -440, 433}}
   - {{waypoint -632, -450, 295}}

## Rewards

- At least 9g 85s 50c
- +700 faction with **Drednever Expedition**
