## Steps

1. Hunt and obtain the following
   - Hunt 10 [[A hippolisk|hippolisks]] for tusks at [[The Salt Mire]] {{waypoint 142.38, 0.00, -276.16}}
   - Hunt 8  [[A hippolisk|hippolisks]] for poisonous barbs
   - Obtain 6 biological salt crystals from [[A gully ensnarer|gully ensnarers]] at [[Dregseep Valley]] {{waypoint 32.07, 0.00, -344.60}}
1. Return to [[Ozraa]]
