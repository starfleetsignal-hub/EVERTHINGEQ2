## Steps

1. Check the Ruins of Karana in [[The Thundering Steppes]] {{waypoint -231, 0, 554}}.
1. Return to Amy.
1. Check Archer's Woods in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint -760, -3, 588}}.
1. Return to Amy.
1. Check Behemoth Pond in [[Nektulos Forest]] {{waypoint -213, 0, -104}}
1. Return to [[Amy Bhorgese]] for your class specific reward.

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
- A bracelet based on class:
  - [[Lion Charmer's Armlet]] (All Mages)
  - [[Lion Soother's Armlet]] (All Priests)
  - [[Lion Wrestler's Armlet]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Lion Stalker's Armlet]] (All Scouts)
