## Steps

1. Slay the following monsters
   - Slay 10 [[A Vornerus drone|Vornerus drones]] for [[Vegnit Snogwit]].
   - Slay 7 [[A Vornerus stalker|Vornerus Stalkers]], [[A Vornerus defender|Defenders]], or [[A Vornerus screamer|Screamers]] for [[Vegnit Snogwit]].
1. Return to [[Vegnit Snogwit|Vegnit]] at the [[Drednever Crash Site]].

## Rewards

- At least 10g 74s 80c
- +700 faction with **Drednever Expedition**
