## Steps

1. Find 6 missing coldain warriors
   - These are clickable spawns labeled as 'A Wriggling Dwarf-Sized Cocoon' that can be found amongst the mobs of [[A crystalline hunter]] and [[A crystalline watcher]] in the various crevasses.  Clicking on the cocoon will free the coldain dwarf inside, allowing you to hail him, giving you the update.  The cocoons seem to have a fairly fast respawn rate.
   - Reported locations:
     - Western crevasse:
       - {{waypoint -200, -375, 109}}this update is bugged you can however revisit another one to get your 6th update.
       - {{waypoint -276, -384, 199}}
       - {{waypoint -337, -380, 230}}
     - Eastern crevasse:
       - {{waypoint -314, -379, 382}}
       - {{waypoint -401, -380, 385}}
       - {{waypoint -403, -384, 426}}
1. Return to [[Ralo Bravehelm]]
