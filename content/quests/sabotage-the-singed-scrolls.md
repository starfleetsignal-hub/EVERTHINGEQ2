## Steps

1. Enter any of the main areas of the city of Qeynos (North Qeynos, South Qeynos, Qeynos Harbor, Elddar Grove).
1. **Within 30 minutes**,find and destroy 5 sets of scrolls. You may need to enter all areas of Qeynos to find enough scrolls.
1. Return to [[Gol M'Tun]].

## Rewards

- Faction Only:
  - +3000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
