## Notes

- requires 20,500 [[House Vahla (Faction)]]
- This is a repeatable quest.

## Steps

![Plumewit Egg](images/Plumewit_Egg.jpg)

1. Collect 10 plumewit eggs in the [[Metetherial Plains]] and [[Pakiat Bluffs]]
   - They look like pink and navy blue eggs and can be found around rocks where the plumewits roam.
   - {{waypoint 261.48, -26.32, 443.67}} {{waypoint 145.59, 53.90, 179.47}} {{waypoint 446.94, 63.47, 285.12}} {{waypoint -43.37, 39.34, 428.11}}  {{waypoint -560.35, 132.04, -342.46}} I found an abundance of them at {{waypoint 69, -1.89, 408}} and many also in Pakiat Bluffs at {{waypoint -453.69, 132.66, -624.18}}

1. Return to [[Koyame]] at {{waypoint -108, 575, -653}}

## Rewards

- At least 90g 72s
- +1250 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1250 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1250 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
