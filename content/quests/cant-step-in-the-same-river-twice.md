## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[Fishing stonestalker]] by the [[Myhst River]] near {{waypoint -232, 16, 219}}.
   - Receive [[A bunch of messy stelecat kidney stones]] after the last kill.
1. Return to [[Izle Tot]].
1. Right-click disturbance in water to "Wash the Kidney Stones" in the [[Drukyna Marsh]] near {{waypoint -852, -54, 33}} to recieve [[A bunch of washed stelecat kidney stones]]. ![Wash the Kidney Stones](images/Wash_the_Kidney_Stones.jpg)
1. Return to [[Izle Tot]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -5000 faction with **House Vahla**
- -5000 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +5000 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
