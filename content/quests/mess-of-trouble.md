## Note

If you have not defeated [[A Skullcracker smithy]] {{waypoint 48.95, 37.03, -245.28}} in your last run, you can use that "cleared" instance to do the quest.

## Steps

1. Search for [[Drylsa]].
   1. Click the Signpost at the entrance to the Skullsplitter camp to zone into [[Savage Weald: Fort Grim (Solo)|Savage Weald: Fort Grim [Solo]]] {{waypoint -83, 25, -242}}.
   1. Kill mobs in the center area, then speak to Drysla {{waypoint 8.27, 34.28, -328.89}}.
1. obtain the key to Drylsa's chains and Dyrlsa's Knapsack
   1. obtain the key to Drylsa's chains
      1. Progress through the zone and kill [[Ulosi and Ulcine Nacisroc]] {{waypoint -28, 43, -273}}.
         - *Joust out of the white circles the twins cast on the ground, and kill adds as they come in, with AOEs if possible.*
      1. Kill [[Chief Gadzuuks]] {{waypoint 111.10, 50.93, -212.25}}.
         - *Joust out of the white circles on the ground, and kill adds as they come in, with AOEs if possible.*
      1. Kill [[Felnir the Shadow Prowler]] and [[The Dire Pyre]] {{waypoint 70, 31, -285}}.
         - *Felnir cannot be targeted. Use AOEs, and target his [[A moor wolf|moor wolf]] adds and kill them, both deal damage to him.*
         - If you can't kill him, you can still finish the quest if you can get to the knapsack without aggroing him.
      1. When Felnir dies, the smithy becomes attackable and aggro.  Kill [[A Skullcracker smithy]] {{waypoint 48.95, 37.03, -245.28}} for the key to Drylsa's chains.
   1. Obtain Drylsa's knapsack![Dyrlsa's Knapsack](images/Dyrlsa's_Knapsack.jpg)
      - *Note: you may pick up the knapsack before or after killing the smithy - your choice.*
      - *There is no set place for the knapsack, it can spawn in any of the huts, and is reddish in color.*
      - Possible locations include: {{waypoint 84.13, 41.13, -330.69}} , {{waypoint 28.46, 55.46, -350.05}}, {{waypoint -34.68, 44.22, -271.73}}, {{waypoint 86, 47, -258}}, {{waypoint 65, 38, -319}}, {{waypoint 55, -239, -313}}, {{waypoint -58, 51, -294}}..
1. Free [[Drylsa]] from her chains.
1. Return to [[Maufoul]] in Savage Weald.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- [[Skullcracker Grimling Banner]]
