1. Gather some parts in [[Grobb]]:
   - Hammer - [[Blacksmith's hammer]] {{waypoint -255, -85, -1000}} (next to [[Pazzt]] <Weaponsmith>)
   - Wood - [[Broken wooden target]] {{waypoint -342, -78, -862}} (outside [[Grunty|Grunty's]] building)
   - Rope - [[Excess rope]] {{waypoint -400, -83, -805}} (at the edge of the alligator pen)
   - Jug - [[Golden jug]] {{waypoint -372, -65, -1022}} (next to [[Thomryp]] <Wholesaler> and [[Potabak]] <Provisioner>)
   - Lead metal - [[Extra lead weapons]] {{waypoint -280, -76, -858}} (weapon rack near [[Grunty]])
1. Return to [[Grunty]]
