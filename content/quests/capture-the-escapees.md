## Steps

1. Capture 8 [[A freed Krupp prisoner|freed Krupp prisoners]] by getting them down to 20% health and then using the [[Froglok Shackles]] on them. They can be found north of the [[Eastern Pens of Riliss]], around {{waypoint -1921, -17, 797}}.
1. Return to [[Keeper Slarn]].

## Rewards

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- +2000 faction with **Riliss**
- [[Resplendent Sash]]
