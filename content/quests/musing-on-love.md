## Steps

1. Speak with the following, in no particular order:
   - [[Qwih Gelim]] in [[The Sinking Sands]]: {{waypoint -1474, -232, -888}}
   - [[A forlorn specter]] in [[The Sinking Sands]]: {{waypoint -949, -224, -1439}}
     - This is a ghostly specter just northeast of [[The Bay of Anuk]].  He’s on a small island close to land. While you need to speak [[Death's Whisper]] to understand what he says, you'll still get the update by hailing (press H) him.
   - [[Aquim]]in [[Maj'Dul]]: {{waypoint -139, 150, -18}}
1. Return to [[The Muse]].
