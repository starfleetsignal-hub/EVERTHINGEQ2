## Notes

- *Timed quest: Shattered Seas solo daily, 6 minutes.*

## Steps

1. Slaughter 15 [[A risen Shralok ambusher|risen Shralok ambushers]] (102^).
   - The Shraloks appear within the pass on the trail, as well as on on the cliffs above the trail.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 67g 40s 5800 status
- 3 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- [[I Most Certainly Shall Pass]] achievement upon first completion.
