## Steps

1. Speak to the female [[An outcast of Maj'Dul|outcast of Maj'Dul]] in Maj'Dul under the tent near [[Antiquitor Kantus Mor'Tael]]. {{waypoint -252, 136, -12}}
1. Enter [[The Living Tombs]] and look for the blue vase, which is believed to spawn in random locations:
   - Check the [[Trade Court]]. One known location is {{waypoint -693, 4.2, 175}}
   - Check the [[Priestess' Court]]. One known location is {{waypoint -309, 30, 191}}
1. Return to the outcast in Maj'Dul.
1. In [[The Pillars of Flame]] go to the top of the djinns' pillar near [[The Giant's Field]] for an auto-update. {{waypoint 1589, -77, -63}}
1. Return to the outcast in Maj'Dul.
1. Return to [[Siyamak (NPC)|Siyamak]].

## Rewards

- A Level 54 Adept of your choice
