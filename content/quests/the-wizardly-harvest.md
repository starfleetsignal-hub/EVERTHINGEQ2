## Steps

1. Use [[Fahimah's Magic Reader V 2.3]] to collect magical readings from various locations around the temple site.
   - Take a reading of the magical energies at the first temple. {{waypoint -80, 2, -3}}
   - Take a reading of the magical energies at the second temple. {{waypoint -164, 22, -172}}
   - Take a reading of the magical energies at the final temple. {{waypoint -233, 19, 77}}
1. Return to [[Fahimah]].
