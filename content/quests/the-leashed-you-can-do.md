## Notes

Can be done at the same time as signature line quest [[Rage of Cthurath: Who's Zobboz?]]. Try not to kill voidcysts while killing other mobs. Requires the quest [[Sprawl Good Things]].

## Steps

1. Enter [[Zon Zobboz: The Graftwerk (Solo)|Zon Zobboz: The Graftwerk [Solo]]]
1. lead emergent voidcysts back to the related gestation chamber and then click on the chamber.
   - Lead 5 red emergent voidcysts to the red gestation chamber at {{waypoint 540, 13, 653}}
   - Lead 5 yellow emergent voidcysts to the yellow gestation chamber at {{waypoint 688, 18, 39}}
   - Lead 5 green emergent voidcysts to the green gestation chamber at {{waypoint 681, 40, 42}}
     - *Note: the voidcysts can be leashed one at a time, or in multiples. Leashed voidcysts will escape after 10 minutes.*
1. Return to [[Ozron]] in [[Zon Zobboz]]
