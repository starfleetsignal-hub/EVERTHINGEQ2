## Steps

1. Speak with the shaman [[Bekktrab]] {{waypoint 207, -208, -504}}, south of Twark
1. Collect the items familiar to the spirit:
   - A spear {{waypoint 243, -220, -401}}
   - A cornerstone {{waypoint 538, -108, -315}}
   - Bones {{waypoint 676, -129, -643}}
1. Return to [[Bekktrab]] {{waypoint 207, -208, -504}}.
1. Go back to the location of the ebbing spirit {{waypoint -35, -119, -100}}, and kill the attacking wandering remnants.
1. Return to [[Bekktrab]] {{waypoint 207, -208, -504}}
1. Kill The Kly  in [[Crypt of Dalnir: The Kly Stronghold (Raid)]].
1. Return to Bekktrab.
1. Find the Pure Spring in Gardens of Nye'Caelona at {{waypoint -618, -88, -373}}.
1. Click to place the gravestone.
1. Click on the gravestone to place the other items.
1. Click to summon the spirit.
1. Talk to the spirit.

## Rewards

- At least 28p 91g 70s
- 170240 status at level 100.
- [[Quickened Shrunken Goblin Skull Earring]]
