## Steps

Kill the following enemies:

- Execute Rathmana : Kill [[Rathmana Allin]] in [[The Sanctorium]]
- Murder the mad giant : Kill [[Cazel the Mad]] in [[Cazel's Mesa]]
- Exterminate the eye sore : Kill [[Aysor the Mighty]] in [[The Poets Palace]]

or

- Waylay the Warden: Kill the [[Warden of the Spirit]] in [[The Vault of Dust]]
- Annihilate the Ancient: Kill the [[Ancient Cyclops]] in [[Ancient's Table]]
- Tame the Tamer: Kill [[Darantar the Tamer]] in [[Cazel's Mesa]]

or

- Kill an elite mark in [[The Hidden Cache]]: Kill any named in this zone
- Skin the Snake : Kill [[That Which Relentlessly Waits]] in [[Scornfeather Roost]]
- Silence the Keeper: Kill the [[Keeper of Silence]] in [[Poet's Palace]]

## Notes

The required targets have changed for the current TLE server, and contested kills are no longer required. Reference the [[Everquest2:Talk:Hunting Dangerous Prey (Deserts of Flame)|Talk]] page for the list of targets required on previous servers.

For targets inside [[The Poets Palace|Poets Palace]], you will need to complete the [[Shimmering Citadel : Poets Palace : The First Mirror.|Mirror Quests]]. To access the upper levels of the [[The Clefts of Rujark|Clefts of Rujark]], at least one person must complete [[The Lift Key of Rujark]].

You can no longer use a pet to pull the [[Keeper of Silence|Keeper of Slience]] from the top floor down to the scriptorium on the first floor. This encounter was changed so that the Keeper is inactive until a player approaches him, preventing him from being pet-pulled.

The Hunter's missions can be shared with others in the group. There is no confirmation message when sharing the mission, all eligible members of the group will immediately receive the quest.

Mission updates for killing a required target are zone-wide. Individual group members do not need to be near the target when it is killed; the only requirement is that they must be in the same instance as other members of the group.
