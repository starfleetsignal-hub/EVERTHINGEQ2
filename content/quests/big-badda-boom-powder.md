## Steps

1. Locate and speak to [[Kedorn Flameforger]] in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain|Thurgadin]].  He's near the entrance to Great Divide, at {{waypoint 930, -299, 324}}.
1. Get the powder from the barrel right next to Kedorn.
1. Fire the cannons!  They're in Storm Gorge at {{waypoint 765, -393, 734}}
1. Return to Jubie
