## Steps

1. Kill the following in [[The Crypt of Betrayal]]:
   - 8 [[A crypt tempest|crypt tempests]] (The scarabs are placeholders. Clear them to spawn the crypt tempest minotaurs. )
   - 12 [[A tortured essence (Crypt of Betrayal)|tortured essence]]s
   - 12 Bloodsaber hoodlums (Any Bloodsaber in CoB will update)
   - 12 [[An ire minion|ire minions]]s

To find these mobs, use the northern entrance to [[The Crypt of Betrayal]] at {{waypoint 4, -1, -185}} in [[Vermin's Snye]]; all the required monsters are in the vicinity of this entrance.
