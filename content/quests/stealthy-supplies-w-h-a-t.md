## Notes

- **You must have finished the Tradeskill Signature [[Ballads of Zimara Crafting Timeline]] quests to start this questline**
- This quest requires:
  - 1x [[Aerated mineral water]]
  - 10x [[Zimaran Coal]]
- when you start the quest you'll receive [[W.H.A.T. Prototype Notes]] and 2 recipes that auto scribe from [[Ameela Ziad]].

## Steps

1. I must find a nearby rock sample to use for the WHAT prototype
   - [[Archipelago Sample]] - clickable sparkly rock {{waypoint -56, 6, 436}} on the beach, next to the barrel. (Respawn time 1minute)
1. Craft the [[Processed Archipelago Sample]], uses [[Archipelago Sample]] (from rock), 1 [[Aerated mineral water]] and 5 [[Zimaran Coal]]
   - *Fuel Merchant, [[Kassa]] next to [[Ameela Ziad]] at {{waypoint 30, 6, 361}}*
   - *"Grandmaster Artisan's Crafting Nook" next to [[Ameela Ziad]] at {{waypoint 24, 6, 354}}*
1. Craft the [[W.H.A.T. Prototype]]
1. Speak with [[Ameela Ziad]] at {{waypoint 30, 6, 353}}
1. Use the [[W.H.A.T. Prototype]] from your inventory and go to {{waypoint 81, 5, 308}}, you will be spotted by a nearby NPC [[Ismara]] and thrown out.
   - *At this stage the ([[W.H.A.T. Prototype]] is just an illusion*
1. Speak with [[Ameela Ziad]] at {{waypoint 30, 6, 353}}
