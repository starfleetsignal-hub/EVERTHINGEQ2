## Steps

1. Enter [[Deathfist Citadel]], and complete the following tasks in any order:
   - Burn the southeastern catapult
   - Burn the southwestern catapult
   - Burn the northwestern catapult
   - Burn the northeastern catapult
   - Burn the large southern catapult (over the gate)
   - Slay 8 [[A Deathfist siege operator|Deathfist siege operators]]
1. Return to [[Angler]].

## Rewards

- At least 2g 32s 57c
- +200 faction with **The Green Hoods**
