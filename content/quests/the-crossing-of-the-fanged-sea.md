## Notes

- This quest was formerly the access quest for [[Everfrost]], but it is no longer required for passage to the frozen lands.

## Steps

1. Seek out [[Biddy Bobick]] in the [[Enchanted Lands]]. {{waypoint 51, 2, 38}}
1. Complete the sub-quest Biddy offers you: [[Of Sea, Sails and Slumber (Quest)|Of Sea, Sails and Slumber]].
1. After completing the sub-quest, return to [[Rath'Adran (Zek)|Rath'Adran]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]]. {{waypoint 480, -40, 224}}
1. Get the pieces of the Tinmizer.
   - Part A is in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]], inside an orc tent at the orc outpost {{waypoint -196, -27, -95}}.
   - Part B is in [[Enchanted Lands]], inside the Tagglefoot Farm house {{waypoint -777, -1, -653}}.
1. Return to [[Rath'Adran (Zek)|Rath'Adran]] with the 2 pieces of the disruptor.
1. Enter and complete [[The Fanged Sea]] instance, accessed from the [[Globe of Swift Travel]] in Zek.
   - See Discussion if you're having difficulties entering the instance.
   - See the [[The Fanged Sea#Walkthrough|Fanged Sea Walkthrough]] for a guide on how to complete the instance.
   - The quest will complete as soon as you deal with [[Rath'Adran (Monster)|Rath'Adran]].
1. After the quest is complete, in order to receive your reward, go to [[The Thundering Steppes]] and speak to [[Scholar Milnik]] on the docks, at {{waypoint -550, -16.45, -430}}.

## Rewards

Nothing directly for completion, however, return to [[The Thundering Steppes]], and speak with [[Scholar Milnik]] and he will offer you a choice of one of the following:

- [[Bardiche of Warm Winds]]
- [[Stiletto of Warm Winds]]
- [[Wand of Warm Winds]]
