## Steps

1. Speak to Kith standing next to Horron at obelisk campsite and choose option to send Horron back to Narezi.
1. Find Xxorix Tower at {{waypoint 63, 79, 112}}
1. Attempt to gain entry to the tower by walking through the purple void blocking the entry. You will be ported back out and the quest will update.
1. Obtain infected tissue and parts to help you get past the barrier, hunt [[A husked voidmaw|husked voidmaw]]s in [[Writher Ruins]] {{waypoint -56, 58, 335}} until you have
   - 4 husked voidmaw horns
   - 4 husked voidmaw feet
   - 8 husked voidmaw flesh
1. Enter Xxorix Tower at {{waypoint 66, 79, 89}} Upon entering, you will be attacked. Defeat [[Slun'Xud]] (lvl 137 ^)
1. Free [[Kiafa]] {{waypoint 77, 80, 64}} by clicking his floating body.
1. Speak with freed Kiafa and instruct him to seek safety at the Void Obelisk. He will run off and a giant worm will spawn outside the tower.
1. Walk outside tower and the giant worm activates into [[Jhuhjaraz]], Writher Queen (lvl 137^). Fight her until she escapes.
1. Return to the Void Obelisk campsite
1. speak with Kiafa at {{waypoint -648, -1, 429}} to finish the quest.
