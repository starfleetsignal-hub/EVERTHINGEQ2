## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- This quest is one of several that you can do to earn the trust of the Splitpaw Gnolls and advance [[A Darkened Shard]].
- You may need to right-click the malfunctioning terraporter and choose 'attempt to activate' in order to enter [[Sundered Splitpaw: Alone in the Dark]].
- You can perform this quest solo; you will still get the update for 'meeting up with your companions' when stepping into the final chamber.

## Steps

1. There is only one route through the caves and barriers.
1. You will find a ledge that leads around a barrier.  Bring the explosive barrel to the ledge and then go back for the crates to climb up and skirt the barrier.
1. Use the explosive barrel to blow up a destructible wall at {{waypoint -74, -48, -242}}.
1. Enter the central cavern and move to approximately {{waypoint -34.38, -40.50, -128.05}} to trigger the update and spawn the boss.  If solo, you can engage [[Animator Mezmolar]] and complete the quest.  If in a group, wait or go help your fellow group-mates along their paths - and then engage together.

## Rewards

- Experience
- Counts toward earning Splitpaw Gnoll trust.
