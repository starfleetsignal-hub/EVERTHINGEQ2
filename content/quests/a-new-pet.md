## Steps

1. Travel to the [[Seeping Ruins]] and use the [[Thermascope]] on [[A ravenous leech]] when it has been damaged to below 30% health. Gather one of the baby leeches that spawn around its corpse. {{waypoint -1145, 11, -460}}
1. Return to [[Hraen Vutha]] in [[Kunzar's Edge (POI)|Kunzar's Edge]]. {{waypoint -1373.73, 24.43, 516.27}}

## Rewards

- At least 6g 88s 19c
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- [[Regimental Chain cap]]
