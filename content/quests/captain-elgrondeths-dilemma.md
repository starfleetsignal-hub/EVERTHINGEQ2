## Steps

- Kill 10 [[Sonic shrieker|sonic shriekers]] in [[Antonica]].
- Kill 5 [[Young brown bear|young brown bears]] in Antonica. -924, -23, 309 is a good location.
- Kill 10 [[Timber wolf|timber wolfs]] in Antonica. -924, -23, 309 is a good location.
- Return to [[Knight-Captain Elgrondeth|Elgrondeth]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s 13c
- One of the following:
  - [[Nettleville Guard Armband]]
  - [[Nettleville Guard Bracers]]
  - [[Nettleville Guard Chain Bracer]]
  - [[Nettleville Guard Heavy Bracer]]
