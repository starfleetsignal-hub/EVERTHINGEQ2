## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- You can not get this quest if you have done the [[Peacock Club Timeline|Peacock Club]] quest, [[Time for a Garden Party]].

## Steps

1. Kill 8-20 [[A Blackfang curator|Blackfang curators]]. {{waypoint 565, 6, 368}}, {{waypoint 575, -1, 458}}, and {{waypoint 568, 6, 515}}
1. Return to Thalbinous.

## Rewards

- At least 6g 25s 11c
