## Steps

1. Find a [[Nameless druid (Jarsath Wastes)|nameless druid]] on a rock in a lava river in [[Skyfire Mountains]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]] {{waypoint -1354, 214, 263}}
1. Use the provided [[Rock sampler]] to take samples from the two other magma rivers in the [[Skyfire Mountains]] region. Note that you only have to stand on the edge to use the sampler, you do not have to go into the lava.
   - The first river is at {{waypoint -1012, 231, 493}}.
   - The second river is near VP, around {{waypoint -1205, 230, 1036}}.
1. Return to the [[Nameless druid (Jarsath Wastes)|nameless druid]].
1. Return to [[Veryn P'Neru]].

## Note

This quest can be done at the same time as:

- [[Broken Barrier: Wounded of the Air]] in Kylong Plains.
- [[Broken Barrier: Wounded of the Water]] in Fens of Nathsar.
- [[Broken Barrier: Wounded of the Earth]] in Kunzar Jungle.
