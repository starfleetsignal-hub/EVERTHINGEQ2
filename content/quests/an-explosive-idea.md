## Steps

1. Harvest 15 powdered minerals. These are random nodes that spawn throughout the zone, but particularly in the lower cavern and spider area.
1. Return to Emma Torque for your reward.

## Rewards

- 11s 77c
- +150 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
