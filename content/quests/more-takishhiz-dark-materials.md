## Steps

Souhila has tasked me with obtaining some rather curious components found in the Chamber of Chronicles, Den of Dawn and the Wellspring of Renewal in Takish'Hiz, and has encouraged me to wreak a bit more havoc along the way.

- Deposit 10 runes of Undying
- Destroy any 10 icons of the Wards of Dawn
- Slays 12 subsistence of grown and verdant wildfire for planar scintilla
- Slay 12 sylvan protectors and rooted watchresses for corruptible icons of Growth.

Return to the encampment and fill the Reinforced Strongbox

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- [[Imperial Hero's Mint]] x2
