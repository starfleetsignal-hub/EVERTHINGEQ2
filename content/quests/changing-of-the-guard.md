## Steps

1. With the discovery that one of the great houses of Qeynos must be involved, the jurisdiction of this investigation passes to the Antonican Royal Guard. Report to [[Beregis Geridion]] at [[The Sovereign House of Qeynos]] in [[South Qeynos]] in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 560, -15, 186}}
1. Speak to [[Lord Drystan Ironforge]], upstairs at the [[Jewel Box]] in [[North Qeynos]] in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 385, -12, -235}}
1. Drystan suggests searching the [[Ironforge Storehouse]], just outside the [[Jewel Box]]. {{waypoint 392, -19, -247}}
   - Locate a crate marked "Armor" in Gnollish. {{waypoint 12, -6, 20}}
   - Locate a crate marked "Weapons" in Gnollish. {{waypoint -26, -6, 30}}
   - Locate a crate marked "Digging Tools" in Gnollish. {{waypoint -15, -6, -40}}
   - Slay any [[An Ironforge storehouse guard|Ironforge storehouse guards]] that try to obstruct your investigation.
1. Return to to [[Beregis Geridion]].
