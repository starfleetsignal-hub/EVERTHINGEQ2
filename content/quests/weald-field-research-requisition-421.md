## Required Materials

*In addition to the materials harvested for the Requisition, you will need:*

- 1 [[Zelniak Steak]] -- *rare*
- 50 [[Coruscating Kindling]]

## Steps

1. Harvest Materials:
   - 15 [[Rockhopper Meat]]
   - 18 [[Radiant Algae]]
   - 45 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
1. Craft [[Braized Zelniak Roast]].
1. Return to [[Erdani Ruilep]].

## Rewards

- [[Research Mission Reward Crate]]
- 8 [[Recuso Field Voucher]]
- At least 1p 25g 7,700 status
