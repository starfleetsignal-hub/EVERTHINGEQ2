## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- The first turn-in yields -2500 reputation with the Sootfoot Talent Society, but subsequent turn-ins yield only coin.

## Steps

1. Kill [[A hatchling volcanu]], [[A volcanu broodmother]] or [[A molting volcanu]] located in the area around {{waypoint 126, -6, -462}} or {{waypoint -121, 0, -371}} until you get 8 updates (fairly common).
1. Return to [[Smapty]] for turn in.

## Rewards

- At least 18g 29s 27c
- [[Cinder Drake Plushie]]
- -2500 faction with **Sootfoot Talent Society**
- Repeatable, but only yields coin on repeat
