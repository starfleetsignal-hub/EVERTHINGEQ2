## Steps

1. Speak to [[Kal'ril Nightstalker]] on the South Freeport north dock {{waypoint -291, -57, 141}}
1. Speak to [[Ulthus Greygore]] on [[Overlord's Perch]] {{waypoint -72, -7, 189}}; kill him (even con)
1. Speak to Kal'ril
1. Clean up The Valorous Companions; they aggro as you appraoch (even con)
   - Kill 5 [[A seafaring raider|seafaring raiders]] in South Freeport (upper levels)
   - Kill 5 [[A seafaring trader|seafaring traders]] in South and East Freeport (along the shore)
   - Kill 5 [[A seafaring slaver|seafaring slavers]] in West Freeport (from the bank and stable south to [[Dreadnaught's Plaza]])
   - Kill 5 [[A seafaring swashbuckler|seafaring swashbucklers]] in [[The Commonlands]] (cliff above the dock {{waypoint -961, -84, -640}})
1. Speak to Kal'ril in South Freeport

- At least 22g 11s 70c
