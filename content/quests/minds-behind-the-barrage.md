## Steps

1. Search north-western paths of Highpass: go to {{waypoint 1124, 286, -124}}
1. Slay the [[An Akhevan lifetaker|Akhevan lifetaker]] (102^H) and his [[A risen Shralok|risen Shralok]] (102vH)
1. Kill 4 [[An Akhevan attacker|Akhevan attackers]] on top of mountains just to the south.
1. Then locate Waulon Highpebble within the craggy peaks {{waypoint 657, 394, 259}}, he is being attacked by 2 Akhevans. Kill them
1. Speak to Waulon for reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Spinnaker Chain Coif]] (Chain Armor)
  - [[Spinnaker Hood]] (Cloth Armor)
  - [[Spinnaker Leather Helm]] (Leather Armor)
  - [[Spinnaker Plate Helm]] (Plate Armor)
