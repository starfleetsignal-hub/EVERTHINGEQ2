This quest is repeatable up to 50 times.<br>
You need to be able to speak gnomish for Mad Mattie to talk to you.  You need to do this quest at least 5 (five) times to get the next quest.

## Steps

*Mattie, the gnome responsible for setting up the fireworks displays for Tinkerfest, has a small problem with malfunctioning clockworks loaded with fireworks which are self-replicating themselves and threatening to overrun Steamfont. I have offered to help destroy some of them.*

1. Disable 10 **Erratic Tinkerfest Clockworks** before they cause harm!
   - Use the [[Remote Detonator]] that [[Mad Mattie|Mattie]] gave you on nearby [[Erratic Tinkerfest Clockwork|Erratic Tinkerfest Clockworks]].
   - *Note: you receive a fresh detonator each time, which has a decent range, and the clockworks respawn quickly (if there are multiple players "hunting" them at once).*
1. Return and let Mattie know you safely detonated some clockworks.

## Rewards

- [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog]] - rewarded upon every completion of the quest
- On the 5th completion of this quest, you receive one of the following:
  - [[Ruthenium Clockwork Drape]] (new for 2011)
  - [[Carbonite Clockwork Drape]] (new for 2011)
  - [[Cloak of Clockwork Dreams]]
- On the 25th completion of this quest, you receive:
  - [[Title: the Explosive]]
- On the 35th completion of this quest, you receive one of the following:
  - [[Carbonite Cloak of the Great Gear]] (new for 2012)
  - [[Ruthenium Cloak of the Great Gear]] (new for 2012)
- On the 40th completion of this quest, you receive one of the following:
  - [[Grand Tinkerer's Mask]] (new for 2013)
  - 5 [[Shiny Tinkerfest Cog|Shiny Tinkerfest Cogs]]
- On the 50th completion of this quest, you are able to buy from Myron:
  - Prefix title [[Watchman]]

## Thoughtful Tips for Top-Notch Tinkerfest Times!

![Sample "Great Balls of Fire" Macro](images/Great_Balls_of_Fire_Macro.png)

- Make a macro for targeting and detonating the erratic tinkerfest clockworks. You merely have to be near one, press the button, and BOOM!
  1. Press **O** to open the Macros window.
  1. Find a blank macro slot and click on **<click to edit>**
  1. Add a Command Step: **/target_nearest_npc**
  1. Add a Use Item step, then drag the [[Remote Detonator]] to it.
  1. Give your macro a title.
  1. Click **OK**
  1. Drag the new macro to a hotbar for easy access.
