## Steps

![Flowers to give to Sherai Nurwin](images/Maraflowers.jpg)

1. Find 3 components to complete your quest. These are:
   - Harvest a [[Pearl of Mara]] from a pearl oyster, found under the docks at the bell underwater.{{waypoint 9.65, -5.02, 115.78}}
   - [[Bolt of Imported Silk]], purchased from a [[Merchant Kaleb Farbring]] at {{waypoint -68, -2, 115}} (price is 70s).
   - Harvest a [[Blossom of Mara]] from **flowers**, they pop pretty much all over the place. Check along the river and near the bridge. {{waypoint -130.34, -1.59, -24.42}}
1. Now speak with:
   - [[Lurina Ventur]] {{waypoint 18, 0, -83}}
   - [[Arellia Augren]] {{waypoint 75, 8, -56}}
   - [[Sherai Nurwin]] who roams around the lake {{waypoint -186.30, 2.12, -136.55}} and stops on the bridge.
1. Return to [[Ensign Geredo]] at {{waypoint -29.16, 0, 130.86}} to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Everburning Gnomish Love]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Shroud of the Aeromancer]] (All Mages)
  - [[Shroud of the Sky Warrior]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Shroud of the Skystalker]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Shroud of the Twilight Priest]] (All Priests)
- At least 9g 85s 50c
- +200 faction with **The Far Seas Trading Company**
