## Steps

1. Show the shield to [[Thurr]] {{waypoint -944, -518, -2120}} on Snowfang Isle (also known as "Icy Fingers")
1. Show the shield to [[Noden Loadcrusher]] at Fina's Retreat {{waypoint -1397, -509, -2257}}
1. Take the griffin to Thurgadin Harbor
1. Speak to [[Bellik Coldthunder]] {{waypoint 1301, -540, 405}}

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +1,200 faction with **Snowfang Gnolls**
