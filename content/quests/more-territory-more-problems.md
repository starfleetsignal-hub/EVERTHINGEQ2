## Prerequisites

- You must speak [[Sathirian]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. The targets of this quest are randomized every time you accept it:
   - Kill 7-9 [[A wretched Cabilisian trooper|wretched Cabilisian troopers]], [[A forsaken Cabilisian spirit|forsaken Cabilisian spirit]], or [[A restless Cabilisian dragoon|restless Cabilisian dragoons]]; OR
   - Kill 7-9 Sarnshaks.
1. Return to [[Ziveth Vraeth]].

## Rewards

- +250 faction with **Riliss**
