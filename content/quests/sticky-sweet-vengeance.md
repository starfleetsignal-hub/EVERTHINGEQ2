## Notes

This quest, and the following 2 are needed for [[Ballads of Zimara: Song of the Maedjinn]]

## Steps

*Hooatio, one of the hooluk I had freed from the Zakir Rish, had been a part of a resistance group against the Sovereign. He was sabotaging Zakir Rish weapons with a concoction when he was captured. And now I'm going to help him complete this task. First, we've got to recreate his concoction.*

1. ![Strungstone Peaks memorial](images/Strungstone_Peaks_memorial.jpg)Find the memorial at {{waypoint 107.36, 379.14, 663.42}} on [[Strungstone Peaks]]
   - Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint -682.82, 144.92, -5.32}} to [[Strungstone Peaks]]
1. Gather and kill the following
   - Gather 4 bixie honey pots from the memorial. (group-wide update)
   - Kill Arcaneform Vanquishers {{waypoint 101, 378, 683}} for 6 fracture runelink. (uncommon update)
1. Return to [[Hooatio]] at {{waypoint -859.88, 189.67, 372.18}}
   - Note: Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint 215.02, 379.28, 652.45}} to [[Isles of Wisdom]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +2,000 faction with **Isles of Wisdom**
