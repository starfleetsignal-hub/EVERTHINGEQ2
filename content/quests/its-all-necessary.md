## Steps

1. Do the following in no particular order:
   - Get a pulsing crystal sphere from [[A ley lantern]].
   - Get condensed mist from a [[A steam hydronach]].
   - Get a living shard from a [[A raging geonach]].
1. Return to [[Klipp Plaxiphone]] for your reward.

## Reward

- At least 2g 69s 57c
