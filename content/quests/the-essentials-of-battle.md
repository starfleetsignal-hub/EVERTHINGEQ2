## Steps

1. Talk to:
   - Ask a Wayward magus what might help the effort
   - Ask a Wayward arcanist what might help the effort
   - Ask a Wayward priest what might help the effort
1. After speaking with the above listed individuals a paper appears in your inventory [[Notes on Wayward defenders' requests]] and the recipe "*Essential Items for the Wayward Defenders*" appears in your Recipe Book for the [[Engraved Desk]].
1. Using an [[Engraved Desk]], craft the [[Essential Items for the Wayward Defenders]] that was supplied earlier from [[Recipes for Wayward Defenders' Supplies]][[Engraved Desk|k]]. You will need the [[Notes on Wayward defenders' requests]] in your bag for this too.
1. The completed recipe will update the quest and reward you with the recipe book with the recipies from [[Essential Items for the Wayward Defenders]]. 3 new recipes which will appear in your craft window [[Amplification Crystal]], [[Arcane Orb]] and [[Refreshing Ale]].
1. Craft the following:
   - [[Amplification Crystal]] with the [[Work Bench]] at {{waypoint -333, 15, 6}}
   - [[Arcane Orb]] with the [[Work Bench]]
   - [[Refreshing Ale]] with the [[Stove & Keg]] at {{waypoint -300, 12, -38}}
1. Return to:
   - [[A Wayward magus]] and give him the Amplification Crystal.
   - [[A Wayward arcanist]] and give him the Arcane Orb.
   - [[A Wayward priest]] and give him the Refreshing Ale.
1. Return to [[Catha Firebolt]]
