## Steps

1. Examine a skeleton at {{waypoint 84, 7, -74}}. *(one of the [[An ire minion|ire minion]] alcoves)*
1. [[Irisor the Insane]] (Level 25^^) will spawn. Kill him.
1. Inspect a rock in [[Windstalker Village]] in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint -1911, -32, -519}}.
1. [[Irisor the Insane]] (now Level 27^) will spawn. Kill him.
1. Inspect a rock in [[The Thundering Steppes]] near the giants {{waypoint 389, 1, 890}}.
1. [[Irisor the Insane]] (now Level 28^^^) will spawn. Kill him.
1. Go to [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] and inspect the cork of a wine bottle in the cellar at {{waypoint 143, -4, 30}}.  It's at the bottom right side of the wine rack.
1. [[Irisor the Insane]] (now Level 29^^^) will spawn. Kill him to complete the quest.
