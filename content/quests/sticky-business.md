## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[A laved horror|laved horror]]s in either [[Veksar: The Invasion]] or [[Veksar: The Sunken Theater]]. These spawn and ambush the player when they or someone in their group touches water within the instance.
1. Return to Sestina Ral'shir.
