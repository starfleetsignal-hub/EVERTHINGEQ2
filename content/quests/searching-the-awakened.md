## Steps

1. Drive cloud to [[Isle of Awakening]]. Then use the lift at {{waypoint 80, 150, 210}} to cross over.Go to enter the [[Palace of the Awakened]]{{waypoint 110, 150, 124}}.
1. Examine the Book Binding to the Drednever in your inventory and add it to the Drednever journal pages collection. Collect the rest of the scattered pages from around the [[Palace of the Awakened|Palace]].
   - See the Discussion page for page locations. But locations looks random.
   - Journal pages are tradable, alternatively you can buy from broker.
1. Turn in the collection to any collector NPC to receive [[Drednever Torn Journal]].
   - For convenience, there is a collector NPC, [[Hoot'el Latti]], located within the Windgazer's Hideout (Whisperwind Isle).{{waypoint -610.42, 8.32, -574.70}}
1. Return to [[Frigui Togginscog|Frigui]]
