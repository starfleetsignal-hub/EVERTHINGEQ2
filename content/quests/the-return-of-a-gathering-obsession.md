## Prerequisites

- You need to have a level 90 tradeskill class.
- You must have finished [[A Gathering Obsession, Final Errand]].
- You must visit [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]] *after* you've reached level 90 in your tradeskill, even if you've already been there.
- If you've received the mail from Qho and he does not offer you the quest, delete his mail message and go to Thurgadin. You will receive another copy of the mail and when you return to Mara, the quest should be active.

## Helpful Hints

- To speed things up, before you leave Mara, go into the bar across from the South Sea tents and buy a **Return to Mara** (one use) stone for 6g from [[Koros Splinterlimb]], the South Sea merchant who is drinking at the bar.  Use it after you finish gathering the harvests to teleport back to Mara quickly. These can be purchased in stacks of 20.
- If you lose Qho during any of these steps, you can go back to [[Lian Augren]] and tell her you can't find him.
- See the discussion page for information on good spots for harvests.
- Characters with a low adventure level can access most needed harvestables by using the [[Druid Ring (SF)|druid ring in the Sundered Frontier]] and fishing is easiest on [[Kerra Isle]].
- Since water is scarce on giant floating islands, a good place to find **school of tiny fish** are in north [[The Stonebrunt Highlands|Stonebrunt Highlands]] in the lake around the entrance to [[The Deepwater Pavilion|The Deepwater Pavilion.]]
- Bountiful Harvest is available and advised. Compact Sumac Harvesting Kit (12%) + [[Gi of the Ashen Artisan]] (12%) + Ample Harvest Tradeskill AA (15%) + [[Mark of the Far Seas]] (10%) can get even a free player to nearly 50% with a further 27% available from the [[Blessed Coldain Prayer Shawl]] and more from Harvest Nail adornments.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]] and zone into the city. The entrance can be found near the world bell on the docks of [[Thurgadin Harbor]] in [[Great Divide]]. Take the elevator up from the docks and go left.
   - You will receive a mail message from [[Qho Augren]] when you first zone into [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain|Thurgadin]].
1. Talk to [[Qho Augren]] in the [[Village of Shin]].  He asks you to gather some harvests for him to study. These resources are harvested in the [[The Sundered Frontier]] or [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]].
   - Gather 50 [[Sumac lumber|sumac]] lumber from **Toxxulia timber**
   - Gather 50 [[Kejekan palmfruit|kejekan]] palmfruit from **perenial shrub**
   - Gather 50 [[Lapsang tea leaf|lapsang]] tea leaf from **perenial shrub**
   - Gather 50 [[Marr cherry|Marr]] cherries from **perenial shrub**
   - Gather 50 [[Caynar nut|caynar]] nuts from **perenial shrub**
   - Mine 50 [[Amber]] from **molten formation**
   - Mine 50 [[Quicksilver cluster|quicksilver]] clusters from **molten formation**
   - Gather 50 [[Bamboo shoot|bamboo]] shoots from **Toxxulia culm**
   - Fish up 50 [[Giant octopus meat|giant]] octopus meat from **school of tiny fish**
   - Fish up 50 [[Seahorse roe|seahorse]] roe from **school of tiny fish**
   - Gather 50 [[Mottled pelt|mottled]] pelts from **craggy lair**
   - Gather 50 [[Titanium ore|titanium]] ore from **infused deposit**
1. Bring the harvests back to Qho Augren.
1. Good news! Qho's gotten permission to leave the village. But you'd better talk to his mother [[Lian Augren]] {{waypoint -52, 11, -112}} first, who is next to him in a little hut with a pack pony.
1. Apparently you've volunteered to take Qho to [[Great Divide]] and [[Thurgadin]]. Hail Qho.
1. Hail Qho and he will follow you.
   - Take Qho to harvest flowers {{waypoint -1305.06, -510.48, -2120.80}} near [[Fina's Retreat]] within the [[Great Divide]] using:
     - A world bell and one of these routes:
       - Either travel to [[Thurgadin Harbor]] Then, using the Griffon at, {{waypoint 1330, -540, 405}} ride to [[Icy Fingers]]
         - Or just evac - you'll land in Icy Fingers
       - Or Travel to [[Sinking Sands]] and talk to the othmir at {{waypoint -1504.04, -214.29, -386.16}} to be transported to [[Velious]]
     - For characters that have been to [[Icy Fingers]] use an [[Heroic Stone of Return: Great Divide]]. These can be bought on [[Icy Fingers]] from Matthew {{waypoint -1635.85, -533.16, -2379.75}} who is a [[Far Seas Supply Division (Faction)|Far Seas Supply Division]] merchant.
   - Pick a [[Windswept Frostflower]] from the ridge around {{waypoint -1300, -511, -2124}}.
1. Hail Qho.
1. Take Qho to [[Remembrance Park]] in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]] {{waypoint 550, -288, 460}}
1. Hail Qho after you get the Remembrance Park update.
1. Take Qho to the [[Goahmari Village]].
   - If you've already been there you can take the griffin; otherwise, head for the city exit labeled "The Great Divide- Dain's Last Stand" at {{waypoint 673, -246, 56}} and head southeast to {{waypoint 351, -15, 533}}.
1. Harvest a [[Goahmari Herb]].
   - Possible locations:
     - {{waypoint 326.90, -14.93, 566.80}}
     - {{waypoint 309.68, -15.02, 514.54}}
     - {{waypoint 309.21, -12.64, 424.79}}
     - {{waypoint 286.71, -14.90, 505.363}}
1. Hail Qho.
1. Go back to [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain|Thurgadin]]. See [[Merchant Hagus]] at {{waypoint 338, -292, 245}} (He is in the first room on your right as soon as you enter Icewell Keep).
   - Purchase a [[Thurgadin Souvenir Snowglobe]] for 5p 83g 20s.
1. Hail Qho
1. Take Qho back to his mother on the Isle of Mara. Hail [[Lian Augren]].

## Rewards

- At least 35g 94s 8c
- [[Artisan's Pack Pony]] - The spell: [[Summon Artisan's Pack Pony]] is automatically placed in your knowledge book.  Drag the icon to a hotbar for easy summoning.
