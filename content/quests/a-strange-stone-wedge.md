## Steps

1. Kill five [[A sandstone giant|sandstone giants]], who spawn in the far western end of the [[Yapping Maze]]. {{waypoint 1470, -31, 125}}
1. Kill six [[A pirate of Gunthak|pirates of Gunthak]], who can be found underwater, north of the docks at the [[L.M.S. Retribution]]. {{waypoint -1100, -211, -850}} (NOTE:  The pirates are on the sunken ship at the sea floor.  I had to clear the area several times to get the pirates to spawn.)
1. Kill five [[An elephant (Commonlands)|elephants]], which can be found throughout the zone. A couple of locations are {{waypoint -337, -48, -551}} and {{waypoint -141, -48, -775}}.
