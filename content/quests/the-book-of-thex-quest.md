## Steps

1. Collect ten pages.  The ten pages spawn in semi hidden corners all over the zone. **The page numbers at each spawn point are random**.
   - First Spot - Entrance {{waypoint -10, -0, -1}}
   - Second Spot - First Room On Left {{waypoint 35, 1, 4}}
   - Third Spot - First Room On The Right, in pit, under Broken Table {{waypoint 14, -1, -55}}
   - Fourth Spot - Walk up beam to ledge Above The Well {{waypoint 73, 11, -33}}
   - Fifth Spot - Corridor In Lower Section {{waypoint 72, -60, -129}}
   - Sixth Spot - Corridor In Lower Section {{waypoint 56, -64, -171}}
   - Seventh Spot - Armoury {{waypoint 46, -62, -148}}
   - Eighth Spot - Final Boss Room {{waypoint 96, -64, -157}}
   - Ninth Spot - Final Boss Room {{waypoint 151, -72, -154}}
   - Tenth Spot - Cavern of Immolating Souls {{waypoint 32, -36, -109}}
   - Notes:
     - The pages respawn once all pages have been collected.
       - Confirmed to be one minute delay before full respawn after previous persons last harvest.
     - Those who already have pages will not see respawns.
     - Page pickup does NOT update group members. ![example of flipping collection page](images/A_Curious_Tome_page.jpg)
1. Seek out a Thexian scholar who can interpret the book for you.
   - The scholar's name is [[V'Aevic Eil'Dragis]], he's in his [[A Thexian Hideout|Secret Lair]] {{waypoint -513, -91, -678}} at the end of a small stream in the Grobb faction area of [[Firmroot Moot]].
   - Notes:
     - You may wish to consider putting down a rally banner if your guild has that amenity; there is a lot of back and forth to this instance with no efficient means of transportation.
     - Note: this is a SOLO-only, non-combat zone.  No need to ungroup, you will be zoned separately automatically.
1. Deliver a message to [[Vlakis D'Vanus]] in [[Lavastorm]] at the Thexian Camp. {{waypoint -85, -130, 330}} near [[Najena's Dungeon]].
1. Return to [[V'Aevic Eil'Dragis]] at the [[A Thexian Hideout|Secret Lair]] to determine if he was able to decipher the book.
1. Find a tomb in [[The Commonlands]]. The tomb is near the [[Darklight Wood]] entrance at {{waypoint 158, -49, -739}}.
1. Return to [[V'Aevic Eil'Dragis]] at the [[A Thexian Hideout|Secret Lair]] to tell him about the status of the tomb: it is locked.
1. Search [[Befallen: Halls of the Forsaken]] for someone who might have known [[Kizdean Gix (NPC Halls of the Forsaken)|Kizdean Gix]].
   - Kill [[The Eidolon of Depravity]].
1. Search [[Befallen: Necrotic Asylum]] for someone who might have known [[Kizdean Gix (NPC Necrotic Asylum)|Kizdean Gix]].
   - Click each bench twice at {{waypoint -94, 0, -45}} and talk to [[Cmdr Windstream]]. The bench is in the same room as the ladder to the lower floor.
1. The betrayer [[Cmdr Windstream]] spoke of should be somewhere within the [[Befallen: Cavern of the Afflicted]]
   - Examine a headstone in [[Befallen: Cavern of the Afflicted]] {{waypoint 79, -31, -2}} to spawn the skeleton [[Skeleton Lrodd]] Level 80 ^^^, is not aggro. Kill for update.
1. Inform [[V'Aevic Eil'Dragis]] of the new information I have learned.
1. Gather a Vial of pure sunlight particles used to fight against vampires in the [[Evernight Abbey]].
   - You get this update upon killing [[Lord Marcus Thex]], the last named in [[Evernight Abbey]].
1. Go to [[Obelisk of Ahkzul]] and kill the [[Proto-Ravager]] for the vial of emptyness update.
1. Return to [[V'Aevic Eil'Dragis]] and he will send you to [[The Crypt of the Imprisoned]] in [[The Commonlands]] {{waypoint 164, -45, -743}}.
1. [[Kizdean Gix]] is a 85^^^ Heroic. Kill him without killing **any** of the adds he spawns or he'll become invincible. Adds will spawn at every 20 %. A chanter is recommended. Zone-In at {{waypoint 159, -49, -739}}
1. Inform [[V'Aevic Eil'Dragis]] of the new information I have learned.
1. Go to Neriak and talk to [[N'Glorion M'Oriquendi]] (Just inside entrance from [[Darklight Wood]] at {{waypoint 47.66, 17.32, -60.78}}).![](images/V'Aevic_Eil'Dragis_-_DEAD.jpg)
1. Return to [[V'Aevic Eil'Dragis]] and loot your reward from his lifeless dark elf body.
