## Steps

1. Visit the bar in the [[Lost Village of Bobick]] {{waypoint 43.28, 1.52, 34.17}} (This step auto-updates when you go to the far end of the bar)
1. Examine the barrels at the [[Lookout Stump]] {{waypoint 316, 2, -376}}
   - NOTE: If you need to respawn the thief you will need to restart the quest.
1. Kill [[The Jum Jum Thief]] that spawns next to the barrels after examining them {{waypoint 306, 2, -373}}
1. Return to [[Deputy Kegie]]

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Apostle's Ordained Plate Barbute]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Marked Cowl]] (All Mages)
  - [[Hanshi's Balanced Skullcap]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Polished Helmet]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Oracle's Sacred Coif]] (Shamans)
  - [[Protector's Moon Etched Cap]] (Druids)
  - [[Raider's Thickened Chain Helmet]] (All Scouts)
