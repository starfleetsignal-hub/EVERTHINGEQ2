*This quest is part of the [[Freeport Outlying Areas Timeline]].<br>
*If you came here looking for the quest that rewards the house item version of [[Book of the Dead (House Item)]] or the equipable version of the [[Book of the Dead (Equipment)]], see the quest [[Repairing the Ward]]. However, the previous quests in the questline must be completed before [[Repairing the Ward]] becomes available.*

## Steps

1. Slay graverobbers in the zone until you get a quest update.
   - Graverobbers are only up at night (20:00 - 05:00) and will immediately despawn at 05:00. Their spawn locations are surrounding (especially behind) mausoleums at: {{waypoint -30, -8, 111}} {{waypoint -80, -11, 38}} {{waypoint -80, -11, -24}} {{waypoint 46, -3, -29}}. Their placeholders are the rats, skeletons and zombies immediately surrounding the mausoleums. [[A novice graverobber]], [[An amateur graverobber|an amateur graverobber]], or [[A graverobber|a graverobber]] all can give the quest update.
1. You receive (a temporary) [[The Book of the Dead (Quest Starter)|The Book of the Dead]].
1. Return to [[Custodian Zaddar Sullissia]]. {{waypoint -73, -13, 107}}

## Rewards

- +150 faction with **The Academy of Arcane Science**
