## Steps

1. Find the Blood Mask
1. Find the Shadowed Mask
1. Find the Dawn Mask
- *Note*: all locations are random and can be repeated.  Sometimes a search results in nothing, and there is a reset on each searched item of at least an hour.  Another trick seems to be to zone out and back in. This will reset the searchable locations. Some locations to find searchable crates are:
  - {{waypoint -34, 2, 41}} in a crate next to the entry to the zone.
  - {{waypoint -46, 2, -16}} in a crate on the island next to Lukur.
  - {{waypoint 3, 0, 13}} in a barrel next to a  pile of crates in middle of the room immediately west of Lukur.
  - {{waypoint 23, 0, 0}} at the edge of the room immediately west of Lukur.
  - {{waypoint 11.96, 1, 64.73}} in a crate south-west of Lukur.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 5c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Opaque Cap]]
  - [[Opaque Bandanna]]
