## Steps

1. Enter Castleview Hamlet via the [[Castleview Hamlet: Cloister of Purity]] from [[Qeynos Harbor]]. {{waypoint 723, -21, -118}}
1. Speak to Emissary [[Talthallas (NPC)|Talthallas]] at {{waypoint 746, -12, -96}}.
1. Return to [[Qeynos Harbor]] and speak to [[Captain Gerafaltis]]. {{waypoint 695, -21, -84}}
1. Find [[Eireneith Alannia]] on the docks and talk to her. {{waypoint 983, -26, 79}}
1. Defeat the attacking [[A Renda'Dal soldier (Qeynos Province District)|Renda'Dal soldiers]]!  There are 3 of them, heroic, one even two v.  If you're a lower level or undergeared, bring a merc.
1. Speak to Eireneith again

## Rewards

- At least 5s 86c @ lvl 20
