## Steps

1. Enter [[Maldura: District of Ash (Heroic)]].
1. As you kill names in the zone, keys will drop: a [[Cerulean Umbrite Key]] and a [[Pyre Umbrite Key]].
1. Take the keys to the vault {{waypoint -182.84, -2.80, -103.12}}, then right-click the vault to recover the **astral umbrite**.
1. Return to [[Bhoughbh Bhildur]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status (11,599 status at level 110)
- [[Mechnamagica Workshop Key]] -- this gives you access to [[Maldura: Bhoughbh's Folly (X2 Raid)]]
