## Steps

1. Find [[Maldyn's bow]]{{waypoint 233.78, 60.15, 892.29}} within the Ivy Etched Remnants
1. Return to Maldyn to return the bow to him. But he claims this isn't his bow and sends three rows of ghostly animals to attack you.
   1. 3 ghostly dire bats
   1. 3 ghostly dire wolfs
   1. 1 ghostly dire bear
1. Talk to [[Maldyn Greenburn]] and he becomes attackable.
1. Kill him to receive the quest reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- [[Bauble of the Unkempt]]
