## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Hanging Root Basket for my apprentice.
   - Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Hanging Root Basket]]
   - Craft [[A Sturdy Hanging Root Basket]] on a [[Woodworking Table]] using the following materials and fuel:
     - 2 [[Hanging root|hanging roots]]
     - 4 [[Scintillating sandpaper]]
1. I need to take this basket back to my apprentice.
