## Description

I found a scrap of paper that I believe is a piece from a journal. If I can find the rest of the pieces then I can read the entry.

## Steps

1. Look for the five missing pieces of the journal.
   - Most, but not all, monsters in the zone can update this. Scorpions, snakes, zombies, skeletons, rats and gelatinous cubes update - **bats and Bloodsabers do not**. Update rate seems to be about 20%.

## Rewards

- At least 4s 13c
