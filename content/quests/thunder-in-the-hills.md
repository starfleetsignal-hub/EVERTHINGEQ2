## Steps

1. Fly to [[The Fortress of Drunder]] entrance at {{waypoint 773, 424, 3652}}(Location update: not necessary to zone in)
1. Talk to [[Drumlin]] below at {{waypoint 640, -332, 3644}}in the Eastern Wastes
1. Enter the [[The Fortress of Drunder]] (You must be level 90 to enter, but tradeskill level 90 is sufficient)
   - You can talk to [[Brontis]] to get the quest [[Bindings of Brontis]] before returning to [[Livi]]
   - You can save some travel time by delaying the turn-in of this quest. Use your 'Call to Home' ability then take the mariner's bell to Thurgadin to complete Bindings of Brontis. After crafting the item for the quest after that in Thurgadin, use the housing menu to enter your home, place the Gift of Nipik's Haven you received earlier from Livi, and use it to travel to Livi to turn in this quest, then fly up to the fortress to continue the quest line.
1. Return to [[Livi]]

## Rewards

- [[Othmir Flute]] (house item)
- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +150 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
