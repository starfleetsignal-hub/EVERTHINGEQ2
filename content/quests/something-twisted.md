## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- The level of the quest you are offered is random (30, 32 or 34).
- You may delete the quest and re-acquire it if you like without any penalty. This is recommended if you are askew to kill one of the scarce mobs, such as [[A lesser mimic]].
- Completing the required kills immediately completes the quest, and [[Master Eldin Necrosis]] immediately offers it again.
- Only the level 34 version rewards armor. Also, the level 34 quest reward is listed as "You will receive a piece of Ravenring armor", but you also receive an unlisted coin reward. Therefore the actual minimum amount of coin is an estimate; no amounts less than the value shown have been recorded.
- Most of the monsters required for this quest do not respawn.

## Level 30

- Kill one of the following:
  - 10 [[A Thexian guard|Thexian Guards]]
  - 6 [[An obsidian gargoyle|Obsidian Gargoyles]]
  - 6 [[A book fiend|Book Fiends]]
  - 8 [[An Everling manservant|Everling Manservants]]
  - 8 [[An eidolon watchdog|Eidolon watchdogs]]
- Reward:
  - At least 69s 67c

## Level 32

- Kill one of the following:
  - 4 [[An apprentice chef|Apprentice chefs]]
  - 8 [[A lesser mimic|Lesser mimics]]
  - 10 [[A crour scavenger|Crour scavengers]]
  - 10 [[A patchwork soldier|Patchwork soldiers]]
- Reward:
  - At least 53s 57c

## Level 34

- Kill one of the following:
  - 10 [[A deathwatch beetle|deathwatch beetles]]
  - 10 [[A doll of Nyth|Nyth dolls]]
  - 10 [[A patchwork fleshripper (Nektropos Castle)|patchwork fleshrippers]]
- Reward:
  - At least 2g 7s (estimated).
  - A random piece of Ravenring chainmail armor:
    - [[Ravenring Chainmail Mantle]]
    - [[Ravenring Chainmail Boots]]
    - [[Ravenring Chainmail Gauntlets]]
    - [[Ravenring Chainmail Legplates]]
    - [[Ravenring Chainmail Chestplate]]
    - [[Ravenring Chainmail Coif]]
    - [[Ravenring Chainmail Bracers]]
