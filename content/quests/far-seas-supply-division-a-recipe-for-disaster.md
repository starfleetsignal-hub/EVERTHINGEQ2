## Notes

**You can get this quest once you reach level 50 in your tradeskill. To get this quest or one of the 3 weekly quests offered for the Far Seas Supply Division, travel to the [[Isle of Mara]] and click on the [[Priority Orders: For Immediate Assignment]] desk.**

- You have 7 days to complete this quest.
- You will need the following fuels:
  - 1 [[Aerated mineral water]]
  - 2 [[Lambent candle]]
  - 6 [[Lambent coal]]
  - 2 [[Lambent filament]]
  - 2 [[Lambent incense]]
  - 2 [[Lambent kindling]]
  - 4 [[Lambent sandpaper]]

- You will need the following raws:
  - 4 [[Beryllium cluster]]
  - 1 [[Conger eel]]
  - 6 [[Indium cluster]]
  - 2 [[Maj'dul coffee bean]]
  - 1 [[Prickly pear]]
  - 3 [[Rough Nacre]]
  - 2 [[Severed sandalwood]]
  - 3 [[Stonehide leather pelt]]
  - 4 [[Succulent root]]

## Steps

1. Click on the tome located on the desk where you got the quest to receive a recipe book
1. Scribe [[Far Seas Essential Supply Manual vol 2.0]]
1. Craft the following:
   1. 1 [[Utility pocket knife]] - [[Metalworking]] uses Forge
   1. 1 sheet of [[Protective metal plating]] - [[Metal Shaping]] uses Forge
   1. 10 [[Anti-sea-sickness potion|anti-sea-sickness potions]] - [[Chemistry]] (All 10 are created in one successful crafting.) uses Chemistry Table
   1. 1 [[Flask of potent rum]] - [[Artistry]] uses Stove and Keg
   1. 1 [[Lucky charm talisman]] - [[Artificing]] uses Work Bench
   1. 1 [[Spell of protective warding]] - [[Scribing]] uses Engraved Desk
   1. 1 [[Spare ship's sail]] - [[Tailoring]] uses Loom
   1. 1 [[Replacement hammock]] - [[Sculpting]] uses Woodworking Table
   1. 1 [[Spare boom]] - [[Fletching]] uses Woodworking Table
1. On crafting your final item the quest may auto-complete if you are not on the Isle of Mara. If the quest did not auto-complete then click on the **Supply Cupboard** to turn in the quest and receive your reward {{waypoint -94, 2.5, 107}}

## Rewards

- 1 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- At least: 54g 49s 68c
- +2,000 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
