## Steps

1. Enter [[Tombs of Night]] and inspect the sarcophagi within:
   1. Plunder an ornate sarcophagus {{waypoint 27, -23, 89}}. - Back of the main hall where the Chief spawns (bottom of the tunnel behind first breakable wall)
   1. Plunder an elegant sarcophagus {{waypoint -69, 0, 103}}. - Top Level (this spawns the vampire, so must be done second) -
      - *Note: This will only update one person's quest, however killing the Named will update everyone's quest.*
1. Kill [[Qaseid S'Ryd]] who spawns after both sarcophagi are plundered.
1. Rid the Tombs of some of the vampires within:
   - Kill 10 Neonate vampires (near the elegant sarcophagus).
   - Kill 5 Adherant vampires {{waypoint -3, -24, 97}}.
   - Kill 5 Bloodsworn vampires {{waypoint -78.94, -37.33, 89.71}}.
1. Return to [[Laren Jadefire]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 26s 57c
