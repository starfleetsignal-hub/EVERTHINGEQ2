## Steps

1. Seek out [[Bondrin Steelfist]] at East Fort Irontoe {{waypoint -358, 166, -271}} in [[Butcherblock Mountains]].
1. Hail [[Bondrin Steelfist|Bondrin]] and convince him to join the Anchorage.
   - This is the successful dialog:
     - **You:** Hail, Bondrin Steelfist
     - **Bondrin:** Oi there. Quickly now, I don't have all day.
     - **You:** Hello, are you Bondrin?
     - **Bondrin:** What can I do ye for?
     - **You:** Sorry, I think I have the wrong person... I am looking for a blacksmith.
     - **Bondrin:** What do ye think I am? See that grinder over there? Why else would I have one'a them? What do you need a blacksmith for?
     - **You:** I'm sorry, allow me to introduce myself. My name is _, and I am looking for a renowned blacksmith in this area. I didn't see you working on anything so I made an assumption. My apologies.
     - **Bondrin:** Well aren't ye proper. What can I do for you? I may not be the best but I know the basics. Did you need a sword sharpened or a staff repaired?
     - **You:** A staff? Isn't that a woodworker's job?
     - **Bondrin:** Aye, but I have taken time to learn all the little ins and outs of the different styles so that I have a better big picture idear about how things work. Eventually I want to make all new recipes for my customers. If I ever get any, that is.
     - **You:** Maybe I can help with that. In fact, I'm looking for someone to come do just that in a business venture I am a part of. Are you interested? If not, I won't bore you with the details.
     - **Bondrin:** You mean I would get to make all new recipes for customers?
     - **You:** Yup! And you won't even have to worry about attracting customers either.
     - **Bondrin:** Count me in!
     - **You:** Great, head to Cobalt Scar and I will meet you at the Anchorage.
1. Return to to [[Whilhelm Nam'terin]] in [[Cobalt Scar]] {{waypoint 3821.94, -1020.05, 1863.29}}
