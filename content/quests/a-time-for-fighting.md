## Steps

1. Pick a few fights around Graystone Yard to establish your presence. Pick a fight with:
   - [[Angus Fallowfur]] {{waypoint 917, -20, -138}} behind the armory
   - [[Marus Donoval]] {{waypoint 875, -21, -125}} inside the tavern
   - [[Dunda Frostgrip]] {{waypoint 848, -21, -93}} just to the left of the [[Qeynos Harbor]] gate
1. Return to Burk Stoneshatter for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s
- One of the following:
  - [[Ring of the Graystone Mystics]]
  - [[Ring of the Graystone Brawlers]]
