## Steps

1. Collect the bones of 5 slain Green Hoods in and around the burnt remains of the Green Hood buildings in [[Green Hood Embers]]. It's a group update.
   - {{waypoint 332.86, -19.98, -469.98}}
   - {{waypoint 340.57, -19.83, -474.21}}
   - {{waypoint 348.40, -19.83, -470.67}}
   - {{waypoint 325.29, -20.03, -479.91}}
   - {{waypoint 314.05, -19.88, -456.84}}
   - {{waypoint 306.23, -19.84, -457.82}}
   - {{waypoint 318.05, -19.14, -445.72}}
   - {{waypoint 322.60, -19.94, -438.93}}
1. Bury the slain Green Hoods under the ancient Three-Toes tree by clicking the burial mound {{waypoint 333.62, -19.55, -396.17}}. NOT a group update.
1. Speak with the ghost of [[Sashra Thaltalis (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Sashra Thaltalis]] {{waypoint 333.59, -19.84, -392.30}}.
   1. Search the region for any sign of the Chest of Ralem.
      - Pick up a **cord of braided potameid braids** {{waypoint 431.71, -18.29, -447.28}}.(Alternative Loc {{waypoint 400.57, -15.43, -433.83}}) Group update.
   1. Speak again with [[Sashra Thaltalis (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Sashra]].
   1. Click the **ancient rubble** in the tower ruins. NOT a group update.
      - {{waypoint 463.97, -20.33, -418.10}}
      - {{waypoint 452.58, -20.02, -430.26}}
   1. Click an **old chest** in the orc sawmill {{waypoint 497.69, -19.65, -294.16}}.
   1. Search for another possible location within the valley. This will be the [[Shadowed Shrine]], entrance at {{waypoint 692.45, -17.43, -325.42}}.
      1. Kill the group of [[A Derris trooper (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Derris troopers]] {{waypoint 719.01, -17.27, -288.09}}
      1. Search the plunder within the [[Shadowed Shrine]] for the chest {{waypoint 719.06, -16.32, -305.89}}.
      1. Also take a moment to find and pick up **Treherth's Acorn** {{waypoint 734.58, -15.38, -292.06}}.
1. Give the chest to [[Twothorn]].
