## Notes

- This is the last quest in this series available to characters of Good-alignment.

## Steps

1. Collect armadillo hides by killing 10 armadillos (any type), west up the road and north along the wall from  the gates.
1. Return to Gerun.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
