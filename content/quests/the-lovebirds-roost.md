## Steps

1. Ferink's friend, [[Jojo Karanja]], lives in [[The City of Freeport]] -- head there to find Jojo on porch behind [[Seafarer's Roost]] {{waypoint -259.54, -54.84, 6.86}} in East Freeport.
1. Jojo says to deliver a necklace to [[Riki]], Ferink's mother, and remind her of the kindness of a Kerran she once knew.  Return to Riki in the Dreg Village {{waypoint -303, -47, -5}}.
1. Go back to tell Ferink the good news -- his mother says he can see [[Amoora]].

- At least 4s 32c
