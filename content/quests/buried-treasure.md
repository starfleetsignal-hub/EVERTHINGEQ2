## Description

I've found a map that leads to some treasure buried on the beach in the Nektulos Forest. If I can figure out the clues given to me on the map, I just might find what was secreted away. If I get lost, I should head back to the last location I've confirmed on the map and try looking from there.

## Steps

![Size Comparison of the X with a forest leaper's corpse](images/X_Marks_The_Spot.jpg)
You have to visit several locations in Nektulos Forest:

1. *Where the sand ends and the rocks begin*: Go to the path leading into the forest from [[Port Naythex]] {{waypoint 654, 0, -301}}
1. Next stop by a tree near the camps: {{waypoint 605, 0, -230}}
1. For the rum:  {{waypoint 571.82, -0.16, -175.80}}
1. Campfire: {{waypoint 550, 0, -137}}
1. Loneliest tree: {{waypoint 548, -1, -25}}
1. Hilltop between camps (beware of aggro mobs on the top): {{waypoint 619, 7, -163}}
1. Large hilltop (behind [[Bruhn K'Viir]]): {{waypoint 682, 20, -260}}
1. Mysterious tent (the revive tent on the beach): {{waypoint 750, 0, -155}}
1. Flitteries (the fairies near the dock): {{waypoint 800, 0, -150}}
1. Treasure in camp: {{waypoint 810, 0, -265}}
1. Line of dead trees (walkway to [[The Cauldron Hollow]]): {{waypoint 880, 2, -400}}
1. Box that's not a box (X marks the spot, only clicking on the center stone of the X will give you the update): {{waypoint 978.16, -0.89, -385.33}}
