## Steps

1. Speak to [[Jackal]] in [[Val'Zyroth Ness]] {{waypoint 43.57, 200.01, -1579.47}}
1. Enter [[Neriondalon]]
1. Return to the Gutters and speak with [[Phileas Wobbleblok]] {{waypoint 841.85, 172.42, -1013.37}}
1. Speak with [[Bellfast]] (on the ground next to [[Phileas Wobbleblok]])
1. Speak with [[Phileas Wobbleblok]]
   - Hunt 12 steamworks for chassis plates - 'a Thexian ironcolossus'
   - Hunt 6 dreadknights for confiscated steamwork bolts - not an update on every mob
   - Break open 15 confiscated steamwork part boxes for gears - break small chests scattered around and harvest gears from the ground
1. Return to [[Phileas Wobbleblok]].
1. Use the *Clanky Steamwork Suit* to avoid dreadknights and cross V'Ghen Span {{waypoint 706.94, 196.43, -223.67}} - drachnids and orange guards can still see you.
1. [[Merchant Lythriss]] is bound in webbing. {{waypoint 717.09, 196.43, -1218.52}}
1. Break the webs. Get ported. The Weave Sisters are talking about me.
1. Speak with Lythriss at the Gutters {{waypoint 858.57, 158.97, -1073.02}}
   - Obtain 8 Ruuk glyph gems from scorn fiends - around area {{waypoint 446.49, 196.38, -1136.89}}
   - Collect 3 Heart offerings from altars to Innoruuk - several altars around Scornsworn Theatre {{waypoint 611.38, 166.13, -1071.53}}
1. Meet [[Merchant Lythriss]] at in infernalist's summoning circle at the Royal Court Tower {{waypoint 312.23, 220.20, -1012.15}}
1. Speak with the Voice of K'Lorn
1. Speak with [[Merchant Lythriss]]
1. Speak with [[Jackal]] at the Gutters
