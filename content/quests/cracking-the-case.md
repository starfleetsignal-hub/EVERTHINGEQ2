## Steps

1. Gather 8 fulgurite crystals from [[A takizite stone|takizite stones]]. {{waypoint -675, 149, -575}} ![A takizite stone & fulgurite crystal](images/A_takizite_stone_&_fulgurite_crystal.png)
   - Not a guaranteed update.
   - May spawn [[A residual sparklet]].
1. Return to [[Dramon Gontalor]].
1. Hunt 12 [[A petrified dreadwood|petrified dreadwoods]] for bark samples.
1. Return to [[Dramon Gontalor]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Cuff of Polished Fulgurite]]
  - [[Ring of Polished Fulgurite]]
