## Steps

*I finally earned the trust of Nisch Mal Polphar, the leader of the refugee gnolls, but it comes as bittersweet news as he informed me that he is sick and the wounds he sustained during their departure from Clan Splitpaw has left him in an extremely weakened state. Before it is too late Nisch Mal Polphar wants to pass on leadership of their pack to his daughter, Nisch Mas Nolphia, and asked for my assistance in gathering components for a staff which he will present to her as both a gift and a display of her status. The items he needs can be found throughout the Darkpaw Warrens.*

1. Collect components for Nisch Mal Polphar to use to create a staff for Nisch Mas Nolphia
   - Collect 5 [[Bones of the Fallen]] - drop from a small chest from skeletal gnolls, including
     - a deceased darkpaw in Culling Grounds
     - gnawed remains in Terramyte Crystalline Grotto
     - chewed up bones at {{waypoint 113, -98, -161}} (need a ladder or drop down from above to reach this area)
   - Collect 3 [[Mycelium Filament|Mycelium Filaments]]
     - Drops from a small chest from any zygomyd in the Terra Fungi area (one entrance is at {{waypoint -22, -77, -255}})
   - Collect the blood of a hero
     - Drops from the chest upon killing the 3 prophets around High Shaman Rakada Darkpaw. 1 update per group
     - Note: See [[Darkpaw Warrens (Solo)]] for notes on how to progress through the zone and activate this encounter
   - Kill Darkpaw and collect the shaman's staff head
     - Drops from the chest upon killing High Shaman Rakada Darkpaw (can by done by killing the Prophets 3 and then the High Shaman, and looting both chest drops.)
1. Return to Nisch Mal Polphar at the Splitpaw Burrow terraporter {{waypoint -58, 2, 69}} or at the entrance {{waypoint -27, -44, -241}}, or in Darkpaw Dugout.
1. Speak with Nisch Mas Nolphia in Darkpaw Dugout (note: her title may have already changed from Nisch Mas Nolphia to Nisch Mal Nophia.)
