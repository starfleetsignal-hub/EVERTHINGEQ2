You won't be able to decipher the notes on the box until you finished the quests in the Iceshard Keep.

## Steps

1. Enter the Throne of Storms
1. Kill Wolfmaster Oor. He should be found near the Royal Kennels.
1. Speak with Breenus McFaylinn, the Ragebourne tactician, to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- [[Challenge of King Tormax]]
