## Steps

1. Enter a [[Maj'Dul Residence]]
1. There will be 3 aggressive mobs and an NPC. Kill the 3 agro  mobs.
1. The NPC will be either [[A Maj'Dul Landlord]], [[A Maj'Dul citizen]], [[An exhausted citizen]] or [[A Maj'Dul resident]]
   - ***NOTE:** It appears that only the Landlord has the information, so you may need to zone out and in until you find a Landlord. This is NOT confirmed.*
   - Right-click on him and select a type of beating to administer.
     - Most of the time they will say they will not tell you anything, or they die.
     - With luck, they will cough up the information you need
   - If he died, zone out and back in and repeat the above steps until you get the info
1. Return to [[A Tears grifter]] for your reward.

## Reward

- At least3g 23s 29c
- One of the following:
  - 4-6 [[Blades insignia token|Blades insignia tokens]]
  - 4-6 [[Coin insignia token|Coin insignia tokens]]
  - 4-6 [[Truth insignia token|Truth insignia tokens]]
