## Notes

- The lava will kill you very quickly.
- You may want to pick up the Solo Mission Quests before entering the two zones

## Steps

#### Part I: The Obsidian Core

1. Zone in to [[Solusek Ro's Tower: The Obsidian Core (Solo)|Solusek Ro's Tower: The Obsidian Core [Solo]]] at {{waypoint 95, 3, -164}}
1. Progress through the zone as normal (see above link for walkthrough).
1. Kill the final Name ( [[Galermos]], on level 5) to receive a [[Pyrestone Rune]], and return to the [[Coliseum of Valor]].

1. Entrance: Go around the perimeter and kill the mobs until Cindrax {{waypoint 0.02, -4.8, -97.79}} is targetable.
   - Try to back against a wall to prevent being knocked into the lava, which will kill you.
   - Cindrax call in adds. You must kill the adds as they pop or Cindrax will not take damage.
   - Once Cindrax is dead, click on the door at {{waypoint -99, -5.63, -101.86}}.
1. Now that Cindrax is dead, the lift operator will spawn.
1. Take the lift to level 2.
   - Kill the Magma worms for Magmucus. Right click and use this to offer some protection against heat.
   - Even with the Magnucus, you will still take damage from the ground fires, so avoid them
   - Kill the named.
     - He has a kickback and will summon adds. You must kill the adds as them spawn or he will be immune to damage.
1. Take the lift to level 3.
   - Kill the scorched fiends to obtain the Obsidian Sun Disc. It is unlimited but only last for 10 seconds.
   - The named will disappear after a few hits. When this happens, run to his pedestal and look back into the room.
   - Two fiends will spawn. Make note of which pedestals they come from. Kill the first fiend. After you kill the second fiend, you must click on the pedestal he came from to respawn the named.
   - Repeat until the named is dead.
1. Take the lift to level 4.
   - Use the Obsidian Sun Disc to walk through the flames. (Remember  it only lasts 10 seconds.)
   - Defeat Verlixa. It is best to attack her on her pedestal so you can then have full view of the room. Follow her into the brazier.
   - In this new room, kill the Mirage and follow it to the next room.

#### Part II: The Monolith of Fire

1. Zone in to [[Solusek Ro's Tower: Monolith of Fire (Solo) (Instance)|Solusek Ro's Tower: Monolith of Fire [Solo]]] at {{waypoint 95, 3, -164}}.
1. Progress through the zone as normal (see above link for walkthrough) up to the final room.
1. Speak to [[Solusek Ro (Solo)|Solusek Ro]] (you will have to hop onto one of the "sun rays" to get close enough)
1. Speak to him again and he turns into an apparent epic x4.
1. DPS him down to about 90%, until he leaves, and the [[Avatar of the Sun]] appears. **This updates the quest**.
   - Note: Killing the Avatar is required for the zone mission, but not for the Signature Quest. You can use Escape to leave at this point if you wish.
1. Speak to [[Druzzil Ro]] in the [[Coliseum of Valor]]
