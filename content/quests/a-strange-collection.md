## Description

*[[Anniisi Illari]] at [[The Fae Court]] has asked me to obtain a few Thexian insignias for her grandfather's collection. She said that her Grandfather is a collector of various oddities and uncommon things from the world outside the continent of Faydwer, so these Thexian insignias should be a great addition to his collections.*

## Steps

1. Head to the Thexian camp in SE [[The Lesser Faydark]] {{waypoint -616, -60, 433}}  Obtain 2 insignias from each of the following types of Thexians:
   - [[A Thexian soldier|Thexian soldiers]]
   - [[A Thexian scribe|Thexian scribes]]
   - [[A Thexian agent|Thexian agents]]
   - [[A Thexian archon|Thexian archons]]
   - [[A Thexian dragoon (Lesser Faydark)|Thexian dragoons]]
1. Return to Anniisi

## Rewards

- at least 9g 27s 1c
- +100 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Trinket of the Faydark Druid]] (All Priests)
  - [[Trinket of the Fayhunter]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Trinket of the Forest Fist]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Trinket of the Natural Caster]] (All Mages)
