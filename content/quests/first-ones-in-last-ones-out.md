Peetriini Suulo's brother died not long ago in the Shadow Grove.  He wants you to find and bury his body and recover his personal effects.

## Steps

![](images/Fulassi_Suulo's_corpse.jpg)

1. Examine **Fulassi Suulo's corpse** near the Tower of Zet at {{waypoint 718.90, -33.31, 484.93}}.  It is located behind a rock and may be difficult to see because he is a Fae (and therefore small).
1. Return to [[Peetriini Suulo]].

## Rewards

- At least 23g 13s 75c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Earhoop of the Crocodile]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Earstud of the Crocodile]]
  - [[Hoop of the Crocodile]]
  - [[Stud of the Crocodile]] (All Scouts)

## Note

This quest is marked "heroic" but can be done easily solo. Use invisibility totem if needed.
