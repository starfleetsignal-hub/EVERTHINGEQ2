## Description

The Vigilant: Rescue has been overrun with blood deamons that came with the Tallonites. I must eliminate the blood daemons as well as the Tallonite necron-amahn aboard The Vigilant: Rescue.

## Steps

   - Kill [[A Tallonite necron-amahn]]
   - Kill 2 [[A lesser blood daemon of Zek|lesser blood daemon of Zek]]
   - Kill 4 [[A greater blood daemon|greater blood daemon]]s

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
