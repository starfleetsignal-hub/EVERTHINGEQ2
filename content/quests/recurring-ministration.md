This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Speak to Clamik to start the timer
   - There can be harvest nodes on the beds, interfering with targeting - try to clean the nodes out before starting the quest
1. Use [[Salycinth Antidote]] on numerous [[A poisoned othmir|poisoned othmir]] to keep them alive.  You have two minutes, and must not let more than 3 othmir die during that time. You can receive more of the antidote by clicking on the basket at Clamik's feet {{waypoint 3598, -1013, 1739}}
1. Speak to Clamik

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- If you did not complete the quest [[A Potent Potable]], the first completion of this quest will fill the [[Achievements|achievement]], [[Potent Potioner]], which grants the suffix title, "[[The Nullifier]]"
