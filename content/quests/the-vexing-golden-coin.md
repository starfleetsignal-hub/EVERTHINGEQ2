## Steps

1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (The Vexing Golden Coin)|Pakiat Divining Device]] from your inventory at {{waypoint 256, 40, 209}}. In the Metetherial Plains. ![A Golden chalice marks the divining spot](images/Golden_chalice.png)
1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (The Vexing Golden Coin)|Pakiat Divining Device]] at {{waypoint 358, 58, 310}}.
1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (The Vexing Golden Coin)|Pakiat Divining Device]] at {{waypoint 554, 58, 408}}.
1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (The Vexing Golden Coin)|Pakiat Divining Device]] at {{waypoint 741, 68, 468}}.
1. Click on the ![Golden Coin](images/Golden_Coin.jpg)Golden Coin at {{waypoint 848, 134, 371}}.
1. Fly to the floating island nearby {{waypoint 977, 110, 438}} and speak to [[An air elemental]] to receive [[A golden coin]].
1. Return to [[Ta-sche-ra-he]].
