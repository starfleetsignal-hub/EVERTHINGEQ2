## Prerequisites

- To receive this quest, the illusion form given to you during the previous quest must be active. Alternatively, you can receive this quest if you have at least -10,000 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]].

## Steps

1. Defeat the following enemies in the [[Fens of Nathsar]]:
   - [[A magma scolder]] in the [[Mines of Nurga]]: {{waypoint -218, 61, -2009}}
   - 3 [[An omensplinter clomper|omensplinter clompers]] around [[Omen's Call]]: {{waypoint 293, -122, 415}}
     - Clompers are on the same shared spawn as the crabs and fish in the lake. If you don't see enough clompers, clear the area and wait for respawn.
   - [[A whirling guster]]: {{waypoint 397, -88, -186}}
   - [[A docile water elemental]]: {{waypoint 422, -120, 408}}
1. Return to Rantiri Skelbre.

## Rewards

- At least 36g 79s 48c
- +1,800 faction with **The Order of Rime**
- [[An Order of Rime brazier]]
