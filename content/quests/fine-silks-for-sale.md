## Notes

- This quest is not repeatable so choose your quest destination wisely.
- If you accept the quest, you *can* cancel it, and choose another one.  However, there is a 30 minute to 1 hour delay before the silk roll will be clickable again.

## Steps

1. Pick up the silk roll in the Weavemaster's room, and select one of the three options which will determine your reward for the quest.
   - One of the oracles in Antonica might desire such a silk.
     - Speak with [[Oracle Liandrin]] {{waypoint -1033, 14, -629}} at the [[Oracle Tower]] in [[Antonica]]
     - Reward: [[Liandrin's Oracle Robe]]
   - There might be a tailor shop in Qeynos that could use it.
     - Speak with [[Danielle Clothspinner]] at {{waypoint 349, -22, -203}} in [[North Qeynos]]
     - Reward: [[Clothspinner Silken Slippers]]
   - Perhaps one of the Merchants down under Qeynos in the catacombs would be able to find a buyer.
     - Speak with [[Adair Barnes]] at {{waypoint 12, 0, -5}} in the [[The Down Below]], near the Nettleville entrance.
     - Reward: [[Dusty Earring of the Merchant]]

## Rewards

- At least 21s 24c
- Your choice (by selecting one of the options - see above) of:
  - [[Liandrin's Oracle Robe]]
  - [[Clothspinner Silken Slippers]]
  - [[Dusty Earring of the Merchant]]
