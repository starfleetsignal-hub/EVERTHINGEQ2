## Prerequisites

- You must have +10,000 faction with either the Court of Truth, the Court of the Blades, or the Court of the Coin to unlock the quest.

## Notes

- This quest is also part of the [[Court of the Blades Timeline]], [[Court of the Coin Timeline]] and the [[Court of Truth Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Kill [[A Rujarkian raider|Rujarkian raiders]] for 2 updates. {{waypoint -1149, -132, -358}}
1. Talk to [[Scout Irfam]] at the top of the gorge leading into the main area of Sinking Sands. {{waypoint -883, -132, -301}}
1. Follow the trade route along the [[Orc Highway]]. The update is around {{waypoint -495, -110, -204}}.
1. Kill [[Messenger D'Zok]] and his adds. {{waypoint -330, -110, -132}}
1. Return to [[Fahad Nuhayd]].

## Rewards

- At least 2g 99s 47c
