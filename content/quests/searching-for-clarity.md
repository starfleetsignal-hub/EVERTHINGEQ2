## Steps

1. Speak with [[Kssshi]] outside the fort {{waypoint 4350, -821, 301}} and take his [[A soldier's shield|shield]] and [[A soldier's sword|sword]].
1. Click on the stack of crates near the ammunition merchant {{waypoint 4354, -822, 417}}
1. Right-click and examine the [[Refined Adamant]] and/or [[Firestone (Searching for Clarity)|Firestone]] in your inventory. Carry out two examinations to get two recipes.
1. Upgrade the equipment at the forge. The forge is typical in appearance and is next to Gunter Olwulf who is behind the broker, outside the large crafting tent. {{waypoint 4334, -821, 455}}:
   - Scribe [[Notes: Upgrading a Common Sword]] and craft the [[Exceptional Sword]]. Requires 1 [[Effulgent coal]], the sword, and the mats from the crate
   - Scribe [[Notes: Upgrading a Common Shield]] and craft the [[Exceptional Shield]]. Requires 1 effulgent coal, the shield and the mats from the crate
1. Return to Kssshi and give him the new weapons.
1. Speak to [[Quartermaster Fahris]]
1. Speak with [[Quentin]] inside the main tent {{waypoint 4407, -822, 415}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
