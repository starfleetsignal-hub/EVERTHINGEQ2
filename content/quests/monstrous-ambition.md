## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 8 of the following *random from quest giver*![Extravasated Mana](images/Extravasated_mana.png)
     - [[A walking wraith|walking wraiths]] in [[Somber Acre]] around {{waypoint 147, 47, 354}},<br>or
     - [[A night howler|night howlers]] around {{waypoint -270.84, 14.39, -4.38}}.
   - Gather 6 of the following *random from quest giver*![Bitumen Embalming Goo](images/Embalming_goo.png)
     - **extravasated mana** around {{waypoint 304, 101, 359}}<br>or
     - **bitumen embalming goo** in [[Somber Acre]] around {{waypoint 147, 47, 354}}
       - *Both can be found near the spots with gravestones*
1. Return to [[Salizar Ethaz (Forlorn Gist)|Salizar Ethaz]].

  - [[Kamapor Chain Boots of Insight]]

  - [[Kamapor Leather Boots of Insight]]
  - [[Kamapor Plate Sabatons of Insight]]
