## Notes

This is a subquest of [[The Adventure of the Celestial Acolyte]].

## Introduction

While attempting to find the important Bayle relic that the acolyte had been sent to retrieve in the Enchanted Lands, I came to a farm on the banks of the Lazy Drain river. The Tagglefoots must be the Storm Reapers that Seeress Ealayna Ithis referred to.

## Steps

1. Grab 3 carrots from the field outside the house {{waypoint -745, 0, -660}}.
1. Lead 3 ![mini trotters](images/Image:a_startled_mini_trotter.jpg) back to the hay bale in Tagglefoot Farms {{waypoint -759, 0, -654}}. Note: right click them.
   1. Known locations for the mini trotters are:
      1. {{waypoint -870, 3, -600}}
      1. {{waypoint -767, 3, -725}}
      1. {{waypoint -824, 0, -700}}
      1. Note: They respawn within seconds.
1. Speak with Jubbs Taggelfoot.

- Experience only
