## Description

"The spider silk did not work for what Grimply needed it to do. My new task is to gather up some of the Crushbone trainer armor, so he can melt it down and use it to fuse something together." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay [[A Crushbone trainer (Greater Faydark)|Crushbone trainers]] at [[Camp Rutok]] around {{waypoint -919, 93, -169}} until you find 10 pieces of armor.
1. Return to [[Grimply Sniffwistle]].

## Rewards

- At least 92c
- Mini-Cogmatic Bixie Bopper Pet (summoned, cosmetic only)
