## Steps

1. Kill [[Opal Darkbriar (Darklight Wood)|Opal Darkbriar]] (85x2) in [[Darklight Wood]]. She travels from the [[Nektulos Forest]] zoneline to the [[Neriak, City of Hate]] zoneline. She is non-agro, and will only be attackable after an [[Assassin]] who needs the update hails her.
   - Return to [[Controller Kenjedeau]].
1. Kill [[Tairiza the Widow Mistress]] in [[The Tomb of Thuuga]].
   - Return to [[Controller Kenjedeau]].
1. Kill [[The Leviathan]] in the [[The Chamber of Destiny]].  NOTE: You are officially BANISHED from using Freeport during the duration of this step. Merchants will not sell, and the guards will attack you on site. This really means very little though, since most guards are well below level 70 for a level 80 assassin to worry about. Just use the travel bell and run back for the update when you're done.
   - Return to [[Controller Kenjedeau]].
1. Kill [[Druushk]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]].
   - *Note: **Very important!** Equip the [[Blackened Leviathin Heart]] and your [[Fang of Ichor]] for this kill, the quest actually tells it too.*
   - *Note: Bring backup weapon because right after the kill you will lose your fabled Fang of Ichor*
1. Return to [[Controller Kenjedeau]] and receive your reward.
