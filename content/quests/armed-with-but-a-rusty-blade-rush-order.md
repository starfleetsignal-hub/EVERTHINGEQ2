### Steps

Craft 6 items from the random list below:

- [[Feyiron Rapier (Level 30) (Crafted)|Feyiron Rapier]]
- [[Feyiron Battle Hammer (Level 30) (Crafted)|Feyiron Battle Hammer]]
- [[Feyiron Crescent Axe (Level 30) (Crafted)|Feyiron Crescent Axe]]
- [[Feyiron Javelin (Level 30) (Crafted)|Feyiron Javelin]]
- [[Feyiron Morning Star]]
- [[Feyiron Hand Axe (Level 30) (Crafted)|Feyiron Hand Axe]]
- [[Feyiron Parrying Blade]]
- [[Feyiron Flail]]
- [[Feyiron Shamshir]]
- [[Feyiron Tribal Spear (Level 30) (Crafted)|Feyiron Tribal Spear]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

### Rewards

- At Least 48s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
