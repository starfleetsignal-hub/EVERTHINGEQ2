I found an old lens that looked like it used to be part of some kind of telescope device. There are numerals on the edge of the device that appear to be in the gnomish language. There might be a gnome in the Steamfont Mountains who could tell me more about this strange object.

## Notes

- You must speak [[Serilian]] to advance in this quest.
- Good locations to farm kobolds for [[Old Telescopic Lens]]:
  - 'enslaved' kobolds in [[Mines of Meldrath (POI)]] {{waypoint -1260, 94, 1054}}
  - 40+ kobolds in [[Grikbar Crater]] {{waypoint -306, 90, 1168}}
  - 40+ kobolds in [[Mazkeen Crater]] {{waypoint -785, 117, 716}}

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Vhizz Frugrin]] inside the [[Frugrin Observatory]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]]. {{waypoint -957, 156, 740}}
1. Travel to [[Runnyeye]] in the [[Enchanted Lands]], enter [[The Chamber of Rulgax]] {{waypoint 180, -17, 191}} and kill [[Lord Rulgax]] for his unique eye.
1. Return to Vhizz Frugrin in Steamfont Mountains.
1. Harvest the following three catalysts:
   - ice crystals from the base of the statue in [[Icespire Summit]]. {{waypoint -52, 76, -623}}
     - Icespire Summit is in [[Everfrost]], at {{waypoint -91, 60, -900}}. To reach the entrance, enter from the [[Icespire Caverns]], at {{waypoint -157, 22, -526}}.
   - heavy mineral water from a geyser in Steamfont Mountains. {{waypoint -588, 70, 1589}}
   - molten crystal from [[Lavastorm]] near the Ulteran Spire. {{waypoint -412, -31, -580}}
1. Return to Vhizz Frugrin in Steamfont Mountains.
1. Speak with [[A Grikbar outcast]], a kobold in Steamfont Mountains, at {{waypoint -1195, 96, 1306}}.
1. Kill 3 named kobolds in Steamfont Mountains (Suggest Order):
   1. [[Digmaster Pechpooka]] - [[Grikbar Crater]] {{waypoint -109, 43, 1183}}
   1. [[Slave Pechmooka]] - [[Mines of Meldrath (POI)]] (not the instance) {{waypoint -1394, 85, 1059}}
   1. [[Scout Pechyooka]] - roams around {{waypoint -980, 71, 1270}}
1. Return to the Grikbar outcast. {{waypoint -1195, 96, 1306}}
1. Enter [[King Grikbar Caverns|King Grikbar's Caverns]] from the [[Lair of the Rex]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]]. {{waypoint -70, 80, 1294}}. The entrance is in behind the banner.
1. Kill [[King Grikbar, the Scourge of Steamfont]]. {{waypoint -4, -34, -65}}
   - WARNING: At 50% health, the king will call the zone to him, so clear before you engage. Update will occur with the King's death, so if you can kill quickly enough you can ignore the mobs being called and just finish off the king.
