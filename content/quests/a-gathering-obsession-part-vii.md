## Requirements

- <s>Recommended harvesting skills of 250, but no hard minimum.</s> There are no skill requirements at this time.

## Notes

- <s>Do Not start the next Part, till you are 400+ in all Harvesting Skills</s>

- **Barren Sky (Isle of Awakening)**
- Can complete all requirements

## Steps

1. Go harvest 30 of each of the following in any tier 7 zone ([[Bonemire]], [[Barren Sky]], [[Tenebrous Tangle]], or [[Loping Plains]]):
   - [[Rough lumbered rosewood]] on Ravasect Incursion Island, Fear Tainted Isle
   - [[Squash]] on Bixie Isle, Gazer Isle
   - [[Soaring coffee bean]] on Bixie Isle, Gazer Isle
   - [[Ravasect meat]] on Tenebrous Landing, Ravasect Incursion Island, Vultak Scavenging Site
   - [[Aviak meat]] on Tenebrous Landing, Ravasect Incursion Island, Vultak Scavenging Site
   - [[Xegonberry]] on Bixie Isle, Gazer Isle
   - [[Sweet chai tea leaf]] on Bixie Isle, Gazer Isle
   - [[Flying fish]] at Tenebrous Landing
   - [[Hanging root]] at Tenebrous Landing, Gazer Isle
   - [[Horned leather pelt]] on Tenebrous Landing, Ravasect Incursion Point, Vultak Scavenging Site
1. Return to Qho Augren.

- Tradeskill Experience
