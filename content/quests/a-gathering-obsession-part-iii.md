Before you begin: You may want to consider getting the quest [[Journeyman Service]]. If you do, turn in to Qho first. Qho will require the harvests whereas the Tradeskill Coordinators will not.

## Steps

1. Go harvest (buying does not work) (no longer a minimum harvesting requirement to Mine/Gather etc. any node in the game, but a skill of 90? in each is recommended so as not to fail too often)
   - From [[The Thundering Steppes]], 10 of each
     - [[Severed ash]] from a Felled High Plains Arbor
     - [[Steppes mountain bean]] from a High Plains Shrubbery
     - [[Belladonna root]] from a Mesquite Catch Weed
     - [[Rough agate]] from a Wind Swept Stones
     - [[Seafury mackerel]] from a Mob of Fish
     - [[Sweet onion]] from a High Plains Shrubbery
     - [[Gold cluster]] from a Wind Swept Stones
     - [[Pig meat]] from a Beast Den
     - [[Lion meat]] from a Beast Den
       - Can be achieved at Coldwind Point; bouncing between the centaur and gnoll areas; fishing can be achieved at the Docks
   - And the same from [[Nektulos Forest]], 10 of each
     - [[Severed ash]] from a Putrescent Duskwood Arbor
     - [[Steppes mountain bean]] from a Polluted Natural Garden
     - [[Belladonna root]] from Rancid Roots
     - [[Rough agate]] from an Eventide Stone
     - [[Seafury mackerel]] from a Swarm of Fish
     - [[Sweet onion]] from a Polluted Natural Garden
     - [[Gold cluster]] from an Eventide Stone
     - [[Pig meat]] from a Beast Den
     - [[Lion meat]] from a Beast Den
1. Return to Qho Augren.

NOTE: Some of the items will not update if you are also on the quest [[Tiffin for Duggin]] from the Thundering Steppes docks. Once it is complete, this quest will update with no problems. (11/23/08)  Updated note: this particular conflict between Duggin and the Gathering Obsession is sometimes seen in other areas as well, such as the Enchanted Forest.  It does not seem to occur in all areas, nor all the time. (06/24/17)

## Rewards

- At least 26g 52s 31c
- Tradeskill experience
