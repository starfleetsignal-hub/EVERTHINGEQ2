## Steps

1. Retrieve 8 sacred deino bones from the Allu'thoa.  Go northeast to the [[Lobha Peaks]].  The bones are click-collectable.  They glow on mouse-over, but are not tab targetable.  They also "respawn" quickly.
1. Return to [[Nuwat Pickletrade]] {{waypoint -1025, 98, -172}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 29g 60s
- [[The Deinodon's Rest]]
