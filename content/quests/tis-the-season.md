## Steps

1. Click the sleigh next to Jarith. {{waypoint -158, -7, -35}}
1. Enter the [[Mushroom Bar and Grill]], underneath the main hub, and click the main platform. {{waypoint -58, -8, 3}}
1. Click the giant mushroom to the north. {{waypoint -51, -10, -12}}
1. Click the giant mushroom across from the main platform. {{waypoint -37, -10, 0}}
1. Click the giant mushroom to the south. {{waypoint -30, -10, 12}}
1. Cross the canal and click the giant mushroom on the north side. {{waypoint -15, -9, -19}}
1. Click the musicians' platform .{{waypoint 9, -9, 17}}
1. Return to Jarith.

## Rewards

- 1 [[Frozen Token of E'ci]]
- The sleigh of Frostfell supplies can now take you back to the [[Frostfell Workshop]].
