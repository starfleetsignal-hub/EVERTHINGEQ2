## Description

*Jergo Wheybringer, a dying halfling I encountered on Admiral Grimgadget's Prison Island, gave me a copy of his book, "Bootstrutter's Trail Guide to Ro." While I was unable to rescue him, I would like to visit the locations in the book to honor his memory.*
