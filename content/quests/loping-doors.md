## Steps

1. Craft 3 [[Lit Doorbot|Lit Doorbots]]
1. View the mausoleum doors in [[Somborn Cemetery]] in the [[Loping Plains]] {{waypoint -423, 14, 58}}
1. View the [[D'Venhz Rectory]] door in the [[Village of Somborn]] {{waypoint -395, 11, -62}}
1. Scan [[Freethinker Hideout]] {{waypoint -491, 10, 1}} (Somborn Village Nightmare handler)
1. Scan [[Crypt of Valdoon]] {{waypoint 50, 61, 263}} (Timorous Moor Nightmare handler)
1. Scan [[Castle Mistmoore]] {{waypoint 757.79, 75.49, -124.47}} (Lesser Faydark Nightmare handler)
1. Return to [[Dora Wicket|Dora]].

## Notes

- The locations for the doorbots are all near the horse stations and safe for lower-level adventurers.

- At least 2p 9g 8s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- [[Delightful Doors]]
- Ability to purchase Advanced Tradeskill Recipes from [[Dora Wicket|Dora]]
