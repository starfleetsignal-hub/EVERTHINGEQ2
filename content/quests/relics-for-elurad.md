## Steps

1. Find the three objects Elurad mentioned:
   - [**Mace of Thunder**](http://maps.eq2interface.com/poi.php?id=513) in the Stormhold Armory on Level 3 of Stormhold {{waypoint 17, -47, -88}}
   - [**Vessel of Storms**](http://maps.eq2interface.com/poi.php?id=950) in the [[The Chapel|Chapel of Karana]] on Level 2 of Stormhold {{waypoint 91, -15, -8}}
   - [**Tome of Thunder**](http://maps.eq2interface.com/poi.php?id=17504) on the 2nd Level of [[The Library (Stormhold)|Stormhold Library]] {{waypoint -128, -31, -139}}. Entrance to the Stormhold Library is a grate on the floor next to [[Qelinatias the Scion of Pain]] at {{waypoint 3, -26, -129}}.
1. Return to Elurad near the entrance.

## Rewards

- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Orb of the Fallen]]
  - [[Bracer of Thunder]]
  - [[Boots of the Grave]]
  - [[Gauntlets of Storms]]
