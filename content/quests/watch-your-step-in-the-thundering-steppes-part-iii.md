## Steps

1. Deliver a package to [[Brianna]] in [[The Village of Thundermist]] at {{waypoint 596, 2, -119}}.
   - As you enter the village, two [[A leg biter|leg biter]]s will attack you. The quest will update to indicate that you have lost part of the package for Brianna.
   - Until you turn in this quest the leg biters will attack every time you enter the village.
1. Brianna gives you the sub-quest [[Supplies for Brianna]]. You must complete that quest to continue this one.
1. Return to Jacques.

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Steppes Medium's Wrap]]
  - [[Steppes Healer's Wrap]]
  - [[Steppes Stalker's Wrap]]
  - [[Steppes Defender's Wrap]]
