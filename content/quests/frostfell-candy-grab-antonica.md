## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- Upon completing the quest a fifth time, you will receive the title, "Candy Catcher".

## Steps

1. Step on the *Frostfell Candy Grab Starting Region*. {{waypoint 47, -19, -28}}
1. Collect as many candies as possible!
1. Speak with Snowball.

## Rewards

Your reward selection is based off the number of candy pieces you collect during the allotted time:

- A Small Prize (1-35):
  - One of the following:
    - [[Blue and White Frostfell Elf Cap]]
    - [[Snow Form]]
    - 1 [[Frozen Token of E'ci]]
    - [[Candycane Staff]]

- A Big Prize (36-49):

    - [[Candy Cane Grab 'n Tag Cape]]
