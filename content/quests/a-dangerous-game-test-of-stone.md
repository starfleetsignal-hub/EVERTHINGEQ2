## Steps

1. Hunt 10 cockatrice in [[Jarsath Wastes]]:
   - [[A deadly stoneglint|Deadly stoneglints]] are nearest the quest giver, at {{waypoint 709, -14, 528}}.  [[A vile stoneleer|Vile stoneleers]] and [[A dreaded stonegazer|dreaded stonegazers]] also update.
1. Return to [[Kror Stormshield]].
