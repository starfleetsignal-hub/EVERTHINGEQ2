## Steps

Nothing is free and building, maintaining, and supporting the herald's camp is expensive. That's why the herald of E'Ci has requested that we assist in offsetting the camp's cost by retrieving a valuable relic guarded by [[Miasmasula]]. I need to retrieve the relic.

1. Retrieve the relic guarded by [[Miasmasula]]
   - Travel to [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] and zone into [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma (Solo)]] {{waypoint 601, 86, 327}} and clear all nameds in this zone.
     - The chest with relic is underwater near {{waypoint 826, 41, 53}}
       - ![The Ancient Relic, as seen underwater when you angle your camera low to the ground](images/Ancient_Relic.png)It's under the surface of the water, you may need to change your camera angle to see it, especially if your character is tall
       - *Bug report (2024-03-04): If your inventory is full, you open the Relic chest and attempt to take the relic then the attempt to retrieve the relic will fail. The quest will fail to update and it is impossible to complete the quest. Another attempt to complete the quest by retrieving the relic will fail as the game reports you have already retrieved the relic. Logging out and/or leaving the zone will not correct the defect. Note the 90 minute dungeon lockout timer. When possible, clear the dungeon Lockout Timer and repeat the quest with available space in bags.*

     - *Note: [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma (Event Heroic I)]] works as well*
1. Return to [[Herald of the Wintry Guardian]] at {{waypoint 75, -35, -1687}} in [[Everfrost]]
