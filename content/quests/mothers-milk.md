## Steps

1. Capture and return a tigress to Ritualist Joyaren. ![A captured tigress](images/Mother's_Milk_quest_-_tigress.jpg)
   - Use [[A tigress leash]] on [[A boldstripe tigress|boldstripe tigresses]] found around {{waypoint 1460, 19, 2070}}.  It only works on ones with the cubs
1. Return to [[Ritualist Joyaren]].

- At least 46g
- +2000 faction with **Kerra Isle**
