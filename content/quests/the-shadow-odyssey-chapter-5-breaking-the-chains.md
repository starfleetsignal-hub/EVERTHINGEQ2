## Steps

1. Gather seven missing runes of the [[Staff of Theer]]
   - [[Befallen: Necrotic Asylum]] {{waypoint 570, -59, 916}} in [[The Commonlands]] *(moderate to hard)* update from killing last boss.
   - [[Najena's Hollow Tower]] {{waypoint -123, -130, 367}} in Lavastorm *(easy to moderate)* update from a box in the last boss room. {{waypoint -82, -349, 98}}
   - [[Veksar: The Sunken Theater]] {{waypoint 631, -179, 290}} in Fens of Nathsar *(easy to moderate)* update from killing [[Lord Sasil]]
   - [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Crucible]] in Everfrost *(moderate)* update from a box in the last boss room. {{waypoint -1, 50, 206}}
   - [[Ravenscale Repository]] in Loping Plains *(moderate to hard)* update from killing T'Lon the Powermonger ("I need to find the rune in Mistmoore's possession").
   - [[The Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors]] *(moderate to hard)* update from killing [[Hoptor Thagglor]] (The Ghoul Lord).
   - [[Emperor Atrebe's Laboratory: The Fabled City of Kor-sha]] in Fens of Nathsar *(moderate to hard)* update from a box in the last boss room. {{waypoint -255, -58, -182}} . Note:You can also harvest on  {{waypoint -23.83, 3.20, -107.87}}  to receive [[The Name of Fear]]([[Greenmist Timeline]]).
1. Return to Firmroot Moot and talk to [[The Great Morsley]]
1. The Great Morsley will give you another Staff of Theer and will send you back to [[Obelisk of Ahkzul]] to find an anchor.
   1. The anchor is in the last room of the Obelisk of Ahkzul. Examine the anchor for the update.
   1. After you get the update, you have to kill [[Executor Vark]]. This turns the anchor aggro. Equip the Staff of Theer and destroy the anchor (Will update everyone in group).
   1. After the anchor is destroyed (it will update for everyone in group) you have 3 minutes to escape the Obelisk. You HAVE to run back through the zone and exit from the entrance point (tip: click *ground* for zoning out). Calling out, dying, etc. will prevent you from receiving the update and this numbered step will reset.
1. You will then be sent to find the Anchor in the [[Anchor of Bazzul]].(loc lower moors firmroot level)
   1. Examine the anchor (via right click) in the final room.
   1. Kill [[Executor Zynos]] to turn the anchor attackable, then equip the Staff of Theer and destroy the anchor. If lvl 100 or higher turn off auto-attack or you may kill the Anchor before using the staff which will result in NO update and waiting for the zone to unlock and doing it all over again.
   1. After the anchor is destroyed (it will update for everyone in group) you have 3 minutes to escape the Obelisk. You HAVE to run back through the zone and exit from the entrance point. Calling out, dying, etc. will prevent you from receiving the update and this numbered step will reset.
1. Return to Morsely at Firmroot Moot.
