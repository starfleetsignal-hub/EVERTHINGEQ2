## Steps

1. Kill 9 Ironshell Snappers {{waypoint 462.97, 0.09, -391.35}}
1. Kill [[A giant Ironshell Snapper]] (spawns where you killed snapper #9)
1. Kill 9 [[A lumbering lava rock|Vicious lavafangs]]
1. Kill [[A matron lavafang]] (spawns where you killed lavafang #9)
1. Return to Tehes.

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1000 faction with **Isles of Wisdom**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Aviak Stud of Shaping]]
  - [[Vultak Earring of Skyward]]
