1. Kill 7 [[A giant venom flow leech|giant venom flow leeches]]. {{waypoint -22, -75, 217}}

- At least 1p 10g
- +6,000 faction with **Ry'zilk's Renegades**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Amulet of Skyfire]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Choker of Skyfire]] (All Mages)
  - [[Medallion of Skyfire]] (All Priests)
  - [[Pendant of Skyfire]] (All Scouts)
