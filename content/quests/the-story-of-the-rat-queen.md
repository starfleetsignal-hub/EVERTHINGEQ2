## Steps

1. Kill 7 [[A crumbling tomb guard|crumbling tomb guard]]s. {{waypoint 247, -6, -92}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 5 [[A festering zombie|festering zombie]]s. *(any alcove in the zone)*
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 crumbling tomb guards.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 7 [[A Bloodsaber proficient|Bloodsaber proficient]]s. {{waypoint 90, 0, -165}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 [[A Bloodsaber adept|Bloodsaber adept]]s. *(same as proficients)*
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 5 Bloodsaber proficients.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 8 [[A ravenous cube (Vermin's Snye)|ravenous cubes]]. {{waypoint 162, -7, -50}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 5 ravenous cubes.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 [[A large tomb vermin|large tomb vermin]]. {{waypoint 15, 1, -140}}
1. Read the book.
