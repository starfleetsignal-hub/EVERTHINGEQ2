An erudite alchemist named Layth requests a number of lion organs from the valley nearby. I'll first need to find this black-clad female hunter and get the traps from her, then place them around the fallen druid ring where I can lure the lions. Only after the traps are sprung upon these lions can I bypass their magical wards and slay them. And I better not forget to harvest their organs. After all, that's what Layth's paying for.

## Notes

- ***Note:** This is a 7 minute timed quest.*
- Found in all versions of Zek, the Scourge Wastes:
  - [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Solo)]]
  - [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Heroic)]]
- Even gathering skill is not needed to start harvesting, it's recommended to have harvesting skill of 450. otherwise you'll need to much time to harvest the lions

## Steps

1. Find the black-clad hunter and you'll find [[Layth's enhanced lion traps]]. Click the bag by her body {{waypoint 131.73, 15.99, -7.00}}.  ![Black-clad hunter with bag](images/Anatomy_of_a_Lion_quest_-_Black_hunter_with_bag.png)
1. Place a lion trap inside the Druid Ring {{waypoint 216, 15, 110}}.
   - Kill [[A hill lioness]] to attract the attention of [[A warded patriarch]].
     - *Note: "a hill lioness" is only visible while on the quest and non aggro*
     - *Note: "a warded patriarch" is not damageable*
   - Lead the angry lion to the trap, and after it has triggered the trap, it will be come [[An unwarded lion kill]] it.
   - Double-click **a dead lion** to gather organs. You can get at least three from each lion, harvesting bonuses give you more.
   - Gather 30 organs and return then to Layth before the timer runs out.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- [[Encrusted iron key]]
