## Steps

1. Inspect 8 traps in [[Hollowfrost Grove]] and [[Guardian's Gulch]]. Known locations include:
   - **[[Hollowfrost Grove]]:**
     - {{waypoint -325, -9, -320}}
     - {{waypoint -360, -7, -278}}
     - {{waypoint -455, 1, -355}}
     - {{waypoint -360, 6, -435}}
   - **[[Guardian's Gulch]]:**
     - {{waypoint -520, -37, -666}}
     - {{waypoint -710, -37, -680}}
     - {{waypoint -453, -37, -903}}
     - {{waypoint -425, -37, -905}}
1. Place the traps. Right-click and 'Use' the traps that are in your inventory while in each valley. If you lose either trap for any reason, you can get a replacement by talking to Stu Wart.
   - Use the [[Trap for Guardian's Gulch]] while in [[Guardian's Gulch]].
   - Use the [[Trap for Hollowfrost Grove]] while in [[Hollowfrost Grove]].
1. Return to Trapper Stu Wart.
