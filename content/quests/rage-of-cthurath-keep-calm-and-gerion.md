## Notes

1. Must be Level 132 to enter [[Gerion, Hold of D'Lere]] (portal in Yon Gorroth)
1. [[The Wraithguard Connection]] quest then the [[Void Energy Farmer]] quests can be done in parallel.
1. For [[The Wraithguard Connection]] quest:
   - The runes around the outer edge of the stage at {{waypoint 1, -1, -565}} must be destroyed first.
   - Only then should the fourth named boss [[Xelha Nevagon]] be killed.

## Steps

1. Use the portal behind Najena to enter [[Gerion, Hold of D'Lere]] quest hub. Once there, use the northern portal at {{waypoint 1, 1, -133}} to enter [[Gerion: Ark of Ascension (Solo)|Gerion: Ark of Ascension [Solo]]].
   - *If clearing the runes for [[The Wraithguard Connection]] quest then do that step while clearing mobs.*
   - *For the walkthrough see [[Gerion: Ark of Ascension (Solo)|Gerion: Ark of Ascension [Solo]]]*
1. Place the 3 cimmerott runic wards in the zone.
   - Place the cimmerott runic ward at the [[Southwest Kill Kraal]] at {{waypoint 142, 1, -298}}
   - Place the cimmerott runic ward at the [[Triforium of Servitude]] at {{waypoint -148, 23, -448}}.
     - *Cannot be placed until the [[Nazkun]] is dead. Use the teleporter at {{waypoint -108, 1, -468}}.*
   - Place the cimmerott runic ward on [[The Dismal Stage]] at {{waypoint 1, 0, -586}}
     - *Cannot be placed until the [[Xelha Nevagon]] is dead. Use the teleporter at {{waypoint 109, 1, -468}}.*
1. enter [[Gerion: Dominion of Pain (Solo)|Gerion: Dominion of Pain [Solo]]]
   - *For the walkthrough see [[Gerion: Dominion of Pain (Solo)|Gerion: Dominion of Pain [Solo]]]*
   1. *Use the **Leave the Ark of Ascension** portal at {{waypoint 11, -1, -455}} and return to [[Gerion, Hold of D'Lere]].*
      - *If completing [[The Wraithguard Connection]] quest then pickup [[Void Energy Farmer]] quest at this point.*
   1. *Use the **To Gerion: Dominion of Pain** portal at {{waypoint 121, 1, 66}} and enter [[Gerion: Dominion of Pain (Solo)|Gerion: Dominion of Pain [Solo]]]*
1. Place the 3 cimmerott runic wards in the zone.
   - Place the cimmerott runic ward at the [[Northern Feeding Pits]] overlook at {{waypoint 332, 1, 26}}
   - Place the cimmerott runic ward at the [[Gallows of Damnation]] at {{waypoint 337, 11, 370}}.
   - Place the cimmerott runic ward at the [[Pier of Judgment]] at {{waypoint 498, 12, 285}}.
1. Return to [[Lady Najena]] in [[Yon Gorroth]].
   1. *Use the **Leave the Dominion of Pain** portal at {{waypoint 504, 13, 291}}.*
   1. *Use the **Return to Yon Gorroth** portal at {{waypoint 2, 0, 113}}.*
   1. *Speak to [[Lady Najena]] in [[Yon Gorroth]]*
