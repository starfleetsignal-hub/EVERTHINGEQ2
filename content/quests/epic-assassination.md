## Steps

1. In [[Darklight Wood]] (use a Druid Portal), click a skull off the path near [[Smoldering Lake]] {{waypoint -107.41, -53.15, -15.29}}
1. Speak to [[An elven spirit]]*

If you select "You have no need of the body.  I now know who you seek, and those bones were too large to be hers.", then when asked to /say the name of the elf, say Opal Darkbriar.  You can select the other dialogue option instead to bypass this.*

1. Kill the elven spirit (even level, one up)
1. Speak to Xolantan in Freeport

*If you select "You have no need of the body.  I now know who you seek, and those bones were too large to be hers.", then when asked to /say the name of the elf, say Opal Darkbriar.  You can select the other dialogue option instead to bypass this.*

## Rewards

- At least 41g 63s 17c
- [[Impressive Powers within Freeport]]
