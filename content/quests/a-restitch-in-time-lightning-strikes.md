## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 50 [[Etherium]]
- 50 [[Plumewit hide]]
- 50 [[Gnarled entwood]]
- 100 [[Celestial Coal]]

## Steps

1. Enter [[Bastion of Thunder: Lightning Strikes (Tradeskill)|Bastion of Thunder: Lightning Strikes [Tradeskill]]] at {{waypoint -95, 3, -164}}.
1. Collect the following: *Note: Track Harvesting works on the nodes.*
   - 10 Aluminum Orbs
     - Found around the base of the Acolyte Tower (SE).
   - 10 Copper Orbs
     - Found around the base of The Glacial Tower (NW). *Note: The floor is slippery.*
   - 10 Torden Clay
     - Found around the base of The Sandstorm Floor tower (NE).
   - 10 Zinc Orbs
     - Found around the base of the Tower of Thunder (SW).
   - Grab [[A bucket]] then collect 5 Torden Water in the Pool by Karana accessed from the teleporter pad at {{waypoint -212, -19, 44}}
     - Before you can harvest the water, you need [[A bucket]] (The bucket is trackable and found around or at the top of the towers.).
1. Craft the Untempered Lighting Rods at {{waypoint 22, -2, 5}}
   - *Note: BE SURE to make the rods first.*
   - Requires:
     - 10 Aluminum Orbs
     - 10 Copper Orbs
     - 10 Zinc Orbs
     - 50 [[Etherium]]
     - 50 [[Celestial Coal]]
1. Craft the Heated Clay Temper
   - Requires:
     - 10 Torden Clay
     - 5 Torden Water
     - 50 [[Plumewit hide]]
     - 50 [[Gnarled entwood]]
     - 50 [[Celestial Coal]]
1. Examine the Heated Clay Temper in your inventory to apply it to the lightning rods.
1. Place the tempered lightning rods around the Tower of Thunder (SW) and collect the solidified lightning.
   - {{waypoint 585, -15, 717}} {{waypoint 769, -17, 680}} {{waypoint 765, -17, 590}} {{waypoint 605, -17, 538}}
   - Place the lightning rod. Back away (to avoid getting kicked). Wait for lighting to strike, then harvest the lightning rod.
