## Steps

1. Search for an old weapon in the Ruins of Old Paineel.
   - An old erudite weapon can be found close to the Ruins teleporter. {{waypoint 1714, -108, 3431}} ![an old erudite weapon](images/An_old_erudite_weapon.jpg)
1. Switch the weapon you found for the weapon in the [[Hall of Wizardry]]. {{waypoint 2000, -298, 3365}}![Unidentified Weapon](images/Unidentified_Weapon.jpg)
1. Speak to [[Safa]] in the [[Hall of the Fell Blade]] {{waypoint 1887, -299, 3424}} and convince her that searching the battlegrounds in [[Kejaan's Rill]] is a waste of time.
1. Return to the Hua Mein spirit in Kerra Village.

- At least 70g
- +5,000 faction with **Hua Mein**
