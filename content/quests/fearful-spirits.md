## Steps

1. Search for the [[Spirit of Restlessness]] again, somewhere in the maze. For example, it has been found at {{waypoint 17, 268, -24}}). Once found, the quest will update and the spirit will pop somewhere else.
1. Complete the quest by hailing the [[Spirit of Restlessness]] again in its new location. For example, the final location has been {{waypoint 50, 266, -2}}).

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g
- One of the following:
  - 4 [[Flames of Envy]]
  - 4 [[Flames of Fury]]
  - 4 [[Flames of Hatred]]
