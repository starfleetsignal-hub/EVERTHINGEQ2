## Steps

![an ancient drake spirit](images/An_ancient_drake_spirit.jpg)

1. Kill 5 [[A shambling shaper|shambling shapers]] in [[Dragonscale Hills]] to obtain [[Fleshy Strands]] (*Auto-Update*).
1. Use the strand at the dragon skeleton, also known as [[The Nameless Cage]] {{waypoint -908, 100, 1368}}.
1. Use the Metaphysic Propulsor on the 'ancient drake spirit' to receive [[An Ethereal Shard]].
   - The ancient drake spirit despawns, so before you use the strand you may want clear the hill. If the Drake does despawn before you get a chance to use the Propulsor on it, simply kill another shambling shaper and you will get a new strand to use.
1. Return to Eegar Friggderp with the shard for your reward.

## Reward

- At least 2g 69s 57c
