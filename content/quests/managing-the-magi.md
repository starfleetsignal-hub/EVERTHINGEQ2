This quest is repeatable up to 3 times..

## Notes

- [[Driving Back the Dervish]] must be completed three times before this quest will be offered.

## Steps

1. Kill 6 Black Magi of a specific type.  The type will be chosen randomly between [[A Black Magi anarchist|anarchists]], [[A Black Magi fanatic|fanatics]] and [[A Black Magi insurgent|insurgents]].
1. Return to Liege Aquila

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c (first time)
- At least 3s 14c (second time)
- At least 5s 12c and +200 faction with **The Freeport Militia** (3rd time)
