## Notes

- [[Catapult Ammo Suppliers]], [[Far Seas Supplier - Blood Ore Pick]], [[Utilitarian Desires of the Miners]] and Disrupting the Mining Operation all require you to kill [[A Vallon miner|Vallon miner]]s so it is advisable to do these quests together, if possible.

## Steps

1. Slay 10 [[A Vallon miner|Vallon miner]]s in or near [[The Deathfist Quarry]]. {{waypoint 342, -114, -114}}
1. Return to [[Hammer]].

- At least 44s 67c
- [[Bloodstained Armor Breaker (Level 30)|Bloodstained Armor Breaker]] - (Shadowknight)
- [[Cestus of the Veneniferous]] - (Beastlord, Bruiser, Monk)
- [[Mace of the Far Skies]] - (Defiler, Inquisitor, Mystic, Templar)
- [[Library Keeper's Wand]] - (All Mages)
- [[Gemmed Jambiya]] - (All Mages)
- [[Poisoned Jambiya]] - (Ranger)
- [[Forestcleaver]] - (All Scouts, Berserker, Fury, Guardian, Paladin, Shadowknight, Warden)

Note: Mages get a choice of one of either the Wand or Jambiya
