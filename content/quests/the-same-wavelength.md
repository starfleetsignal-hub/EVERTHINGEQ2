## Description

I was given a set of attuning rods to take with me into the Eternal Prism. I should look for items within the Phylactery that have a strong magical aura, and examine them to attune the rods to their type of magic. Once all of the rods have been attuned, I should return to Camp Wyndalsyd in Everfrost.

## Steps

Warning:  Not a group update.  Each person must click

1. ![Errant magic-type mobs have this appearance](images/The_Same_Wavelength.jpg)Use the received [[Attuning rod]] to attune them to the different types of magic found within [[Miragul's Phylactery: Scion of Ice]]
   - The essence of "Destructive Magic" {{waypoint -209.42, 15, 133}} Room with the void chest.
   - The essence of "Overbearing Magic" {{waypoint -190, -10, 88}} Final room on North wing.
   - The essence of "Empowering Magic" {{waypoint -140, 1, 92}} Room after Chaotic Manipulation.
   - Any essence of "Aiding Magic" {{waypoint -52, 2, -35}} First room on the left (North wing).
1. Destroy 5 of the errant magics within Scion of Ice.  Errant magics, which appear as moving clusters of shards/ice, include:
   - [[A blazing whim]]
   - [[A charged theory]]
   - [[A verdant notion]]
