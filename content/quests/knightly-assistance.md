## Steps

Note: No Mass Production

1. Speak to [[Safa]] in the Hall of the Fell Blade {{waypoint 1889.21, -298.99, 3421.41}}.
1. Scribe the recipe [[Armaments of Paineel]] and craft 3 of each:
   - 3 x traditional Paineel breastplates (forge)
   - 3 x traditional Paineel greatswords (forge)
   - 3 x traditional Paineel greaves (forge)
   - 3 x tabards of the Fell Blade (sewing table)
   - *components needed: 27 [[Titanium ore]], 24 [[Quicksilver cluster]], 9 [[Bamboo shoot]], 9 [[Ethereal coal]], 3 [[Ethereal filament]]*
   - Crafting stations are in the Paineel Commons at {{waypoint 1800, -299, 3492.5}} on both sides
1. Return to Safa for your reward.
1. Go to Adeela  {{waypoint 1729, -282, 3470}} for next update.

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- +1,000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +1,500 faction with **Fell Blade**
- +1,000 faction with **Craftkeepers**
