## Steps

1. Slay each of the identified spies lurking within the city of Qeynos.
   - Kill [[Bethaz]], spying on the docks behind the [[Lion's Mane Inn]] in [[South Qeynos]]. {{waypoint 569, -21, 107}}
   - Kill [[Hilirgo]], lurking under the aquaducts in [[North Qeynos]]. {{waypoint 390, -24, -122}}
   - Kill [[Ocron]], the spy near the underground harbor in [[Elddar Grove]]. {{waypoint 620, -20, -226}}
   - Kill [[Palax]], spying near the [[Grounds of Fate]] and the [[The Clock of Ak'Anon|Clock of Ak'Anon]] in [[Qeynos Harbor]]. {{waypoint 753, -21, 12}} **You must right-click to attack**
   - Kill [[Rarezut]], spying outside [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn]] in [[Qeynos Harbor]]. {{waypoint 797, -23, 74}}
1. Return to [[Marik McPherson III (Qeynos Capitol District)|Marik Mchpherson III]].

- [[Lion's Mane Corner Counter]]
