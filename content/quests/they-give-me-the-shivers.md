## Steps

1. Kill 12 [[An icy shiver|icy shivers]] in [[Hollowfrost Grove]].
1. Walk through [[Icewail Pass]], which is directly behind the [[Snowsworn's Hunter's Outpost]], and step into [[Icewail Caverns]] to get a quest update. {{waypoint -351, -22, 69}}
1. Return to Baldus.

- At least 2g 20s 65c

  - [[Ever Frozen Stalker's Mantle]]
