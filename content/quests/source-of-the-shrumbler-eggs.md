## Steps

1. Kill the [[The Shrumbler Breeder Queen]] (level 6 solo named) in the very bottom of [[Drippy Cave]] at {{waypoint 134.14, -51.00, -357.51}}
1. Speak to [[Captain Steelforge]] at {{waypoint 123.32, -31.04, -553.97}}

## Rewards

- At least 65c
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Dwarven Woodaxe]] - Ranger
  - [[Impaling Pike]]
  - [[Steel Linked Necklace]]
  - [[Faydark Scout Jerkin]]
