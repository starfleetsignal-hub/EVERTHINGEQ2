## Steps

The avatar summoning rituals require the manipulation of immense power and are inherently dangerous. To reduce the chance of failure and provide an anchor for that power, the herald of Bristlebane has asked me to collect an item that may have once belonged to that god or one of their most loyal followers.

- Collect [[Reliquary of Bristlebane|Reliquaries of Bristlebane]] from a powerful creature in [[Aether Wroughtlands: Tarnished Oasis (Heroic I)|Aether Wroughtlands: Tarnished Oasis]] heroic dungeon.
- Return to the [[Herald to the Grand Prankster]] at {{waypoint -832, 7, -203}} in [[Rivervale]]
