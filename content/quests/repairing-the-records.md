## Steps

1. Craft 5 [[Restored note]]s on an [[Engraved Desk]] (one is next to Skrit); the recipe [[Restoration of Damaged Notes]] is autoscribed.
   - *NOTE: Do not craft 5 notes at once. Update: As of 4/9/18, these notes stack when you're creating them.
   - *If, for whatever reason, you do not have enough [[Damaged note|Damaged notes]] in your inventory during this stage of the quest to craft all 5 [[Restored note|Restored notes]], you may harvest additional notes in the same area(s) you recovered them originally, but you will only be able to pick up x2 at a time during this stage of the quest.  You will need to pick up x2 extras, create a quest note, pick up x2 more extras, etc until you have the required x5 [[Restored note|Restored notes]]
1. Speak to Skrit

## Rewards

- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]]
- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
