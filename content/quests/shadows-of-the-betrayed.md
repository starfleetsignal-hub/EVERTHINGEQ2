## Notes

1. If you lost your damaged relic quest starter you can buy one from Shady  Swashbuckler XVIII in Nektulos Forest, or speak to Tahar in Quelule and ask him to mail it again.
1. You must complete [[Outfitting the Kerra: A Strange Spirit]] before reaching step 7 or [[Natasha]] will not speak to you for either quest line.
1. To craft the [[Repaired Armoring Tongs|Armoring Tongs]] for step 11 you will need:
   - 1 Kaborite Cluster (rare harvest, returned to you as part of quest reward)
   - 2 Quicksilver Cluster
   - 2 Amber
   - 1 Ethereal Coal
   - Access to a forge

## Steps

1. After inspecting the relic, speak to [[Dror Ironstein]] {{waypoint -232, 214, -191}} in [[West Fort Irontoe]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]].
1. Examine the [[A damaged relic|damaged relic]] again.
1. Go to [[The Village of Shin]] on the Isle of Mara and pick up the quest [[Emergency Portal Repairs]] from [[Madria Varas]] {{waypoint -87, 3, 105}}.
   - Every hour, Madria Varas offers 1 of 4 quests, so you may have to return every hour until she offers this one.  The 4 quests are:
     1. Supply Stocking +3 hours
     1. Shipyard Services +2 hours
     1. Clockwork Rescue +1 hour
     1. [[Emergency Portal Repairs]]
   - While at the Village of Shin, speak to the merchant [[Koros Splinterlimb]] on the floor below where Madria is located and buy a [[Mission Stone of the Void]] that will take you directly to The Overlook.
1. Use the [[Mission Stone of the Void]] to take you directly to The Overlook and speak with [[Urdu]], the balloon conductor {{waypoint -1079, -98, -214}}  at [[The Overlook]] in [[Moors of Ykesha]] to fly to the [[Palace of the Ancient One (tradeskill)]].
1. Speak to [[An injured raider]] at {{waypoint -11.28, 13.26, 31.11}}
   - **IMPORTANT:**  be sure to speak to the injured raider BEFORE speaking to Tomkin.
1. Speak to [[Tomkin Cogsbottom]], and select the **second** option "I have a quick question first...".
   - This is all you need to do to advance the Shadows of the Betrayed quest. **There is no need to complete the Portal Repairs mission unless you wish to.**
   - If you do decide to do the Emergency Portal Repairs mission note the following:
     - **IMPORTANT: Do not complete the mission until your Shadows of the Betrayed log says "Return to Natasha..."**
     - Tomkin's "What can I do?" option offers the Building a Better Portal subquest. Do not accept this until you're ready. It is vital that you not complete the Portal Repairs mission before asking your "quick question", because when the  mission completes the option disappears.
   - If you happen to complete the Mission Quest first and Tomkin won't speak to you about the Ethernauts, you will need to re-obtain the Emergency Portal Repairs quest from Madria in the Village of Shin, then repeat steps 4 & 6 (assuming you already spoke to the injured raider).  Be sure to choose the dialogue line with Tomkin that says "I have a quick question first..."
     - NOTE: **Reset your instance for the Palace of the Ancient One (tradeskill) before returning!**
1. Speak to [[Natasha]], the mystic on [[Kerra Isle]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]{{waypoint 1191, 42, 2244}}. After a brief conversation you can choose the option to go into a trance and get teleported into [[A Spiritual Pocket (Artisan)|A Spiritual Pocket]].
   - You must have at least 4000 faction with the Kerra Isle Outfitters before you can continue this quest.  You will need to have completed the tradeskill crafting quests given by [[Chinook]] to [[Outfitting the Kerra: A Strange Spirit]] before you can continue.
1. Speak to [[Kaltuk Ironstein]] at {{waypoint 39.39, 0.00, 19.08}}. You will be teleported back to [[Kerra Isle]] at the end of the conversation.
1. Speak to [[Researcher Tahar]] {{waypoint -200, 367, 590}} in [[Quel'ule]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]].
1. Enter [[Miragul's Planar Shard (tradeskill)]] in [[Everfrost]] {{waypoint -164, 10, -372}} (the same entrance as the shard zones, but the tradeskill zone will be shown on the list) and speak to [[A vigilant sentry]].
1. You have 3 minutes to find Miragul's Notes. They are clicky books on the table at {{waypoint -106, 0, 25}}
   - The book is buried under a pile of other books and can be hard to locate. The timer display disappears, but the timer does NOT stop. Be sure to leave before the mobs reaggro. The mobs scale to level, they were 138 when I went in at 130.  ![Ingredients](images/Shadows_of_the_Betrayed_quest_-_Repaired_Armoring_Tongs.png)
1. Leave the zone and scribe [[Miragul's collected notes on crafting]] and craft the [[Repaired Armoring Tongs|Armoring Tongs]]. This requires a rare [[Kaborite cluster]], which will be returned to you when you complete the quest (see the image to the right for all components required). There is no forge in Quel'ule so remember to craft the Armoring Tongs before returning to the Researcher, or simply bring along a portable forge. You must craft it to all four progress bars and counter all of the reactions or you will die and get tradeskill debt.  If you use a [[Potion of Innovation]] it will only take two rounds of crafting to complete.
1. Return to [[Researcher Tahar]] {{waypoint -200, 367, 590}} in [[Quel'ule]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]].
