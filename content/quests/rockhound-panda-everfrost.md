## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Everfrost]]

![](images/Everstorm_Bay_rock.png)
![](images/Icewail_Cavern_rock.png)
![](images/Icegill_Lagoon_rock.png)

1. Obtain 3 samples from Everstorm Bay- this is dock area. Rocks are on the  sea floor, sparkling brown rocks West of dock around {{waypoint 118, -56, 79}}
1. Obtain 3 samples from Icewail Caverns - around ice cavern {{waypoint -399, -5, -12}}, cavern is accessible from Hollowfrost Grove in the North and through underwater tunnel in the bay {{waypoint -350, -22, 69}}
1. Obtain 3 samples from the Icegill Lagoon - on ice floes around {{waypoint 186, -37, -1324}}
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]] in Sundered Frontier.
