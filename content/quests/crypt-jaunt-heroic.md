## Steps

*Dominus Rile had the Crypt of Dalnir built for his weaponsmith and sealed it shut, never intending for it to be disturbed, but then came The Kly, and with it, a rise of undead, armed with weapons produced by Dalnir. The Crusaders of Greenmist have requested my assistance in wiping out this threat, including Haggle Baron Dalnir.*

- Enter [[Crypt of Dalnir: Baron's Workshop (Heroic)]]

1. Burst the giant goo
1. Hunt down the frenzied devourer
1. Defeat the Enchanted Sword and Shield
1. Crush the Haggle Baron
