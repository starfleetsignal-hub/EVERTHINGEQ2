## Steps

1. ![bitter herb](images/Snowfang_bitter_herb.jpg)Gather 5 herbs nearby. These are scrawny bushes in a nearby crevice {{waypoint -1057, -532, -2223}}
1. Buy [[Far Seas Trading Company Recipe: Leather Pelt Curatives]] from [[Matthew]] for 4g 60s 80c
1. Return to [[Ruffin]] {{waypoint -918, -522, -2071}}
1. Scribe the recipe, then use the Snowfang Container next to [[Ruffin]] to make the [[Curing Solution]].
   - You'll need the [[Bitter herb (Level 70)|bitter herbs]] you just harvested, 1 [[Liquid]] and 1 [[Ethereal coal]]
1. Talk to [[Ruffin]] again.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
