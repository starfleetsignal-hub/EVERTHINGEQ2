## Description

"Seren Leafsway is worried that the delicate balance struck within the Faydark is going to be devastated by the foreign swampflitters that have shown up in Emerald Lake. She asked that I exterminate the prolific bugs and destroy their eggs." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. In [[Emerald Lake]], kill 6 [[A swampflitter|swampflitters]]
1. Destroy 4 swampflitter eggs around the shore of the lake
1. Return to Seren

## Rewards

- At least 27s 29c
- [[Hanging luminescent florets]]
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Fighter's Bracelet (Version 2)|Fighter's Bracelet]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Bracelet (Version 2)|Priest's Bracelet]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Bracelet (Version 2)|Scout's Bracelet]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Bracelet (Version 2)|Sorcerer's Bracelet]] (All Mages)
