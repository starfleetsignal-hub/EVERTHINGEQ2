## Steps

1. Travel to [[Camp Char'Gin]]. {{waypoint 1251, -139, -1530}}
1. Speak to [[Siraj al Din]]. {{waypoint 1252, -165, -1611}}
1. Travel to [[The Clefts of Rujark]] and collect 3 red crystal formations. You must right-click them to chip off a piece.
   - {{waypoint -90, -22, -282}} in ceiling
   - {{waypoint -146, -15, -202}} in ceiling
   - {{waypoint -194, -10, 39}}
1. Return to Siraj al Din.

  - [[Naturewalker's Cap of the Forestspeaker]] ([[Fury]], [[Warden]])
  - [[Sensei's Skullcap of the Fightmaster]] ([[Bruiser]], [[Monk]])
  - [[Spiritualist's Coif of the Spiritcaller]] ([[Defiler]], [[Mystic]])
  - [[Thaumaturge's Cowl of the Archcaster]] (All Mages)
  - [[Vagabond's Chain Helmet of the Bloodsinger]] (All Scouts)
