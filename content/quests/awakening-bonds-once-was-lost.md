## Prerequisites

- Must be level 8 of one [[Ascension]] class to start the quest

## Requirements

- The quest [[Tears of Veeshan: Falling Tears]] must be completed, in order to see the collection items.
- [[Epic Repercussions]] may be required

## Steps

1. See if the omen stones have any useful information.
   - Commune with the omen stones, on the altar next to Haylis.
1. Speak with Haylis again.
1. Face Skalmos, the Grozmok!
   - Use the portal nearby to enter [[Brokenskull Rock: Glory to the Grozmok]], and make your way through to Skalmos. You will not be able to kill him, just survive 60 seconds until Skalmos captures you. {{waypoint -1763, -968, 2638}}
   - After you're returned to the Sanctury of Divine Unity automatically, speak to [[Seeress Ealaynya Ithis]] in the Sanctuary
1. *The Seeress believes that there might be a way to repair the destroyed Vessel of Wisdom. She gave me a lead to follow, so I should see if I can gather what is necessary to repair the Vessel.*
   - Find the spiritual Threads that the Seeress mentioned.
   1. Find the collection items of the [[Gossamer Nexus Threads]] collection in the [[Vesspyr Isles]]
      - *Note: There's several spawn locations. All the shinies can be found on the main island, however they also spawn on others. Looks like standard yellow shiny.* It took about 10 minutes to get the whole collection, easiest ones were on Droumlunds. ***These are super common, if you don't get any then you are missing the [[Tears of Veeshan: Falling Tears]] prerequisite.***
      - [[Daarspire]]
        - {{waypoint -77.85, 200.37, 117.05}}
        - {{waypoint -110.62, 200.29, -6.75}}
        - {{waypoint 37.5, 175.13, -124.58}}
        - {{waypoint -44.59, 227.94, 144.4}}
        - {{waypoint -102.85, 206.97, 316.66}}
        - {{waypoint -163.98, 212.23, 374.15}} Check this region, many spawn here
        - {{waypoint -78.24, 200.42, 120.13}}
        - {{waypoint 249.98, 176.36, 71.28}}
      - [[Droumlunds]]
        - These spawn really fast and you can get them easily by jumping between the top 3 spawn points
        - {{waypoint 239, 313, -798}}
        - {{waypoint 331, 317, -806}}
        - {{waypoint 340, 320, -699}}
        - {{waypoint 219.39, 309.26, -666.04}}
        - {{waypoint 528.07, 320.05, -659.67}}

   1. Speak to a [[Collector]] to turn in the completed [[Gossamer Nexus Threads]] collection to receive the [[Nexus Gabion]].
1. Return to the [[Sanctuary of Divine Unity]] and collect the shattered pieces of the [[Vessel of Wisdom]] from the table near Haylis.
   - Click the broken vase on the table next to Haylis.
1. Use the Nexus Gabion to gather the essence from the Vessel of Wisdom.
1. Return to Haylis and inform him of what happened.
   - Speak to Haylis once more.

## Rewards

- [[Gossamer Thread Wand]] *(for [[Fury]], [[Warden]], [[Mystic]], [[Defiler]], [[Templar]] and [[Inquisitor]])*
- <s>[[Gossamer Thread Bow]] *(for [[Channeler]])*</s> Replaced with Blade. Petition to switch if you already have bow.
- [[Gossamer Blade]] *(for [[Channeler]])*
