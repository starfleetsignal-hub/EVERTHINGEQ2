## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Prexus has asked me to assist in gathering oceanbound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the oceanbound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Oceanbound Energy Reserves|oceanbound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect [[Oceanbound Energy Reserves|oceanbound energy]] by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Oceanlord]] {{waypoint -1765, -402, -2728}} in [[Fens of Nathsar]]
