## Steps

1. Gather and kill the following:
   - Gather 8 frost whip petals - they are white bushes in the snow near {{waypoint -783, -386, 2942}}
   - kill 5 [[A frostpaw direbear|frostpaw direbears]] or [[A sootpaw direbear|sootpaw direbears]] near {{waypoint -533, -380, 3149}}  ![frost whip petals](images/Swog's_Salutary_Salve_quest_-_frost_whip_petals.jpg)
1. Speak with [[Swog Bloodytusk]]
1. Use [[Swog's Pacifying Unction]] on 5 *an ill Ry'Gorr .....* found withing the Ry'Gorr Keep and outside
   - You can use the Unction on the same Ry'Gorr for all updates if you wait the 20-30 seconds till he is ill again.
   - Everytime you use the Unction successfully on [[An ill Ry'Gorr striker]] [[A Duhjalm attacker]] will spawn behind you after 3 seconds and attack you
1. Speak with [[Swog Bloodytusk]]
   - *Note: He will tell you at the end that [[Blor Greatbasher]] has a quest for you tomorrow*

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +200 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
