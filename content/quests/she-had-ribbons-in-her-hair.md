## Steps

1. Slay [[A highwayman (Antonica)|highwaymen]] until you get three quest updates.
   - These are found at the gorge at {{waypoint -1700, 0, 300}}. Higher level [[Highwayman|highwaymen]] (level 17) are found on the rocks above the gorge, but if you walk along the road inside the gorge, lower level highwaymen (level 10) will spawn in ambush at either end.
1. Return to [[Licha]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 17s 55c
