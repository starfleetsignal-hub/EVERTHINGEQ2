## Steps

1. Speak with [[Inquisitor Thorson]] in [[Sunken City]]. You can travel to [[Sunken City]] by using the mariner's bell on the docks.

Inquisitor Thorson is the starting point of the [[Freeport Districts Timeline]], beginning with [[A Flowing Conversation]].
