## Steps

1. Retrieve or re-create the Elemental Resonance Spectrometer
   - **Adventure way**: Retrieve the device by killing [[A Strifewing pilferer]] (even con to you, v) {{waypoint -135, -84, -268}} on [[The Isle of Discord]]
   - **Tradeskill way**: Craft the device from the recipe given at a Work Bench. Requires tradeskill 80, 1 titanium ore, 1 quicksilver cluster, 1 amber, and 1 Ethereal Coal
1. Return to Aillena

## Rewards

- At least 2g 7s 7c
