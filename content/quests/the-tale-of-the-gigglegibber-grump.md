## Notes

- You will need 5 [[Carbonite cluster|carbonite clusters]], 5 [[Gold cluster|gold clusters]], and 100 [[Basic Coal]] during this quest. It is recommended that you have them in your inventory before starting. Buy them off a broker or harvest them from [[The Thundering Steppes]] (*stonecrest ore* - carbonite, *wind-swept stones* - gold).

## Steps

1. Speak to a Gigglegibber goblin:
   - [[The Qeynos Exchange]] {{waypoint 687.62, -20.05, -42.90}}
   - [[The Freeport Reserve]] {{waypoint 198.80, -2.47, -117.36}}
   - [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]] {{waypoint 84.41, -4.09, -1184.61}}
1. Go to the [[Enchanted Lands]] and click on the sewer cover under the docks to be transported to [[Gigglegibber Hideout (Frostfell 2006)|The Gigglegibber Hideout (Frostfell)]]. {{waypoint 41, -30, 118}}
1. Speak with [[The Gigglegibber King]] {{waypoint 168, 11, -319}} and complete the subquest, [[Unlocking the Elfin Lord]].
1. Return to [[Pinchy Presentpeeler]] and complete the subquest, [[Removing The Grump]].

  - [[Frigid Snowmound]]
