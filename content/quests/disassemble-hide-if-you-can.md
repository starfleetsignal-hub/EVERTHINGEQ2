## Steps

1. Create [[Invisibility Potion for Burnish (Recipe Book)|Recipe for Invisibility Potion]] (auto scribed when you receive the quest) at nearby Bivouac Forge or at an [[Engraved Desk|engraved desk]].
1. Find [[Invisibility Potion for Burnish (Recipe Book)]] in inventory, scribe it and Craft [[Invisibility Potion for Burnish (Quest Item)|Invisibility Potion for Burnish]].
1. Return to [[Burnish (Aspiring Artisan)|Burnish]]

## Rewards

- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
- [[Status Coin (75,000)]]
