## Description

Skrit wants to keep an eye on the state of the magical corruption around the ruins of Malvonicus' tower. By examining the magical residue each week, she may be able to learn more about the cause, and whether the situation is improving or getting worse.

## Steps

1. Examine 5 dirt piles by the Malvonicus'tower {{waypoint 901.65, 0.00, 1429.78}}
1. Return to [[Skrit]]
