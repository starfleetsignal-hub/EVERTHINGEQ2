## Steps

1. Place the [[Beacon of Feral Attraction]] within the Thexian camp {{waypoint 521, -45, -16}}. You can hang around to watch the frenzied kodiak lay waste to the camp.
1. Speak to [[Malyl N'Vazin]] at [[Wanderlust Fair]] in [[Darklight Wood]] {{waypoint 276, -20, -3}}

- At least 17s 43c
