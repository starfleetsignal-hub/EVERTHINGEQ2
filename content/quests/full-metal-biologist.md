## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
- Must have complete [[Maedjinn Trouble in Ferric Fields]] quest to see Ornetta

## Steps

1. I've agreed to retrieve arcane components from different creatures that can be found across Aether Wroughtlands.
   - *Note: the quest will ask for 3 items listed below*
   - Hunt 8 ironshell snappers for iron carapace pieces {{waypoint 425, -8, -419}} or {{waypoint 703, 2, -310}}
   - Hunt 6 silvershell snappers for silver carapace pieces {{waypoint -550, 257, 570}} or {{waypoint -476, 296, 698}}
   - Hunt 6 coppershell snappers for copper carapace pieces {{waypoint -99, 120, 373}}
   - Hunt 6 golden vipers for gold leather near {{waypoint 593, 260, 264}}
   - Hunt 6 gold muck for fluidic gold near {{waypoint 565, 255, 661}}
   - Hunt 6 goldshell snapper {{waypoint 719, 255, 635}} or {{waypoint 611, 255, 634}}
1. Return to [[Ornetta]] at [[Turquoise Oasis Camp]] {{waypoint -27.75, 87.18, 476.85}}

## Rewards

- 4p 4g 15s 72c
- 5 [[Zimaran Ballad]]
- +1000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
