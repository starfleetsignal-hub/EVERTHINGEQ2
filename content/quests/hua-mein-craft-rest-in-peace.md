## Steps

1. Lay the remains of the hua mein to rest at the site of the old battle near [[Kejaan's Rill]].
   - Speak to the spirit about safe travel to the burial site and get teleported to the battlegrounds.
1. Bury the remains in the patch of loose dirt (shimmering with green particles). ![a patch of loose dirt](images/A_patch_of_loose_dirt.jpg){{waypoint 625, 192, 4029}}
1. Use the [[Hua Mein Charm of Escape]] to return to the spirit.

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +5,000 faction with **Hua Mein**
