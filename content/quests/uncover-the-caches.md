## Steps

1. There are 8 interactive bushes found throughout the zone. At each one, do the following:
   1. Interact with the bush.
   1. Slay the [[A tool guardian]] that spawns. (level 10)
   1. Right-click on the tool that appeared, and destroy it.
1. Return to Acting Lieutenant Germain for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
- +175 faction with **The Concordium**
