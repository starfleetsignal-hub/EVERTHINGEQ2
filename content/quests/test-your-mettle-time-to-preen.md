## Steps

1. Talk to [[Hydona]].
1. Craft the [[Preening Oil]]
   - *all materials supplied; crafting table is next to [[Hydona]] or any chemistry table will work.*
   - *Note: Next to [[Hydona]] is "Varig's Forge" which "may be used to craft most of the known tradeskill recipe"*
1. Talk to [[Hydona]] to apply the [[Preening Oil]]
1. Talk to [[Hydona]] to complete quest.

## Rewards

- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c 56000 status
- 5,487,787,399 TS exp.
- +4,000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
- [[Hooluk Preening Oil]] - *House item*
