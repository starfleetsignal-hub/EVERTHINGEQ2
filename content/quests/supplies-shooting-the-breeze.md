## Notes

Daily crafting mission. Reset timer 18 hours. Quest **expires in 2 days**.

Journal entry: In order to help support the continuing population of explorers in the area, I must help create supplies needed by the adventurers.

I need to harvest some resources to craft into ammunition.

When accepting quest you receive [[Recipe Scroll: Supply Mission Materials]], which auto scribes 4 recipes needed for crafting dailies.

Materials needed:

- 30 [[Larch Wood]]
- 12 [[Ilmenite Ore]]
- 18 [[Sedge Root]]
- 30 [[Zimaran Sandpaper]]

## Steps

- Harvest: 25 x Larch Wood (you will actually need 30 to craft all 6 items)
- Harvest 18 x Sedge Root
- Harvest 12 x Ilmenite Ore
- Craft  (on Woodworking Table) 6 x Bundled Wastes Ammunition - this is called Wastes Ammunition in recipe book, each one uses 5 Larch Wood, 2 Ilmenite Ore, 3 Sedge Root, 5 Zimaran Sandpaper

Quest auto completes upon crafting the last item.
