## Steps

![Stones to repair Hands of Kajak](images/Awakening_Stones_for_Hands_of_Kajak.jpg)
Note that while listed as Heroic, this quest is entirely soloable.

1. Speak with [[Ootuko]] {{waypoint 1280, 47, 2189}} on the .
1. Speak with [[Meelia]] {{waypoint 1264, 44, 2153}}.
1. Speak with [[Urix the Speaker]] {{waypoint 1318, 44, 2124}}.
1. Locate the four kerran ancestor spirits in the village and free them by speaking with Urix.
   - The lost spirits are floating balls located around the village area.  Right click, read text and lead back to Urix.  Speak to Urix once again.
1. Speak with [[Kulrah]] {{waypoint 1073, 6, 2268}}.
1. Find the stones necessary for repairing the Hands of Kajak around the island. They are at multiple locations.
   1. One possible location is {{waypoint 1051.60, 17.83, 2079.26}},
   1. Another possible location is {{waypoint 1295, 16, 1913}},
   1. Another location is {{waypoint 1446, 24, 1981}}
   1. Another location is (1205, 35, 2070)
1. Return to Kulrah.
1. Speak with [[Jakna]] {{waypoint 1357, 12, 2372}}.
1. Kill 12 [[A root glimmer|root glimmers]] in [[Toxxulia Forest]].
1. Return to Jakna.
1. Return to Ootuko.
