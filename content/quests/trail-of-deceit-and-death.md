## Description

*Something is causing Maldurans to go berserk within the Mushroom Bar and Grill, while the creatures of Kralet Penumbra continue to bring death to anything that stumbles into their Submerged Lair.*

## Steps

- Destroy enemy targets within Kralet Penumbra and Maldura.
1. Kill the submerged beast
   - [[Abyssral]] in [[Kralet Penumbra: Submerged Lair (Event Heroic)]]
1. Kill the vengeful elder
   - [[The Vengeful Elder (Event Heroic)|The Vengeful Elder]] in [[Maldura: Bar Brawl (Event Heroic)]]
