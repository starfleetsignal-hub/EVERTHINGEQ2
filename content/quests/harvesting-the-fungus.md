## Prerequisites

- Your faction with [[The City of Kelethin (Faction)|City of Kelethin]] must be Indifferent or better to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Go to the Myconid Caves of [[The Lesser Faydark]] and search out the nursery where the Lesser Amanita grow.
   - This is accessed either from a well in the [[Glade of the Unicorns]] {{waypoint 269, -24, -173}} or via the tunnel under the waterfall in the [[The Fugutr Tribe]] area. The well access places you closer to the nursery
   - If you use the well, be careful.  The pool you drop into is the spawn location of a named goblin [[Melanog]] (59^), that can see invis.
   - The nursery is fairly deep in the tunnel, heading west/south around {{waypoint 456, -22, -224}}.
   - Between the pool and the nursery a side tunnel leads to heroic ^^^ area. Keep LEFT as you pass.
   - The Nursery is the spawn area for [[Truffle Snuffler]]; 60^ boarfiend.  Clear the [[A myconid harvester|myconid harvesters]] first.
1. Harvest 12 ***Faydark Amanita*** (little mushrooms)
1. Return to [[Duchess Maareanna]]

## Rewards

- At least 12g 57s 51c
- +100 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Earhoop of Called Growth]]
  - [[Earring of Called Growth]]
  - [[Facet of the Frenzied Growth]]
  - [[Spike of the Frenzied Growth]]
