1. I must slay skygazing astronomers and their leader.  Enter Dartain's Fortress {{waypoint 116.76, 92.36, -238.99}}.  That location is a zone door beneath the Defenders of the Seal. Then head to {{waypoint 572.09, 122.17, -74.81}}.
