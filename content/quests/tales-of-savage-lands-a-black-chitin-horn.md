## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A cht'thk spiker|cht'thk spikers]] around the [[Ruins of Shar Makhdi]]  {{waypoint -186, 66, -662}} in [[Savage Weald]].
   - *The spikers elsewhere in the zone will not update this quest.*
