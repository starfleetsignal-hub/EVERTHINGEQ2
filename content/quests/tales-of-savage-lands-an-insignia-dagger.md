This quest is repeatable.

1. Kill 15 Shadel [[A Shadel crook|crooks]], [[A Shadel maurauder|maurauders]], [[A Shadel outlaw|outlaws]], [[A Shadel raider|raiders]], [[A Shadel thug|thugs]], or [[A Shadel trapper|trappers]] at the [[Ruins of Shar Makhdi]] {{waypoint -245.98, 66.54, -668.61}}  in [[Savage Weald]].
