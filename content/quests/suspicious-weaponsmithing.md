### Steps

Craft 12 items from the random list below:

- [[Fulginate Rapier (Level 40) (Crafted)|Fulginate Rapier]]
- [[Fulginate Battle Hammer (Crafted)|Fulginate Battle Hammer]]
- [[Fulginate Crescent Axe (Crafted)|Fulginate Crescent Axe]]
- [[Fulginate Javelin (Level 40) (Crafted)|Fulginate Javelin]]
- [[Fulginate Morning Star (Crafted)|Fulginate Morning Star]]
- [[Fulginate Hand Axe (Level 40) (Crafted)|Fulginate Hand Axe]]
- [[Fulginate Parrying Blade (Level 40) (Crafted)|Fulginate Parrying Blade]]
- [[Fulginate Flail (Crafted)|Fulginate Flail]]
- [[Fulginate Shamshir]]
- [[Fulginate Tribal Spear (Level 40) (Crafted)|Fulginate Tribal Spear]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

### Rewards

- At Least 54s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
