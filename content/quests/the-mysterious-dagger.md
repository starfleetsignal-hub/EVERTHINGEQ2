## Steps

1. Investigate [[Headstone Walkway]] {{waypoint -851, 0, -1350}} by walking across the bridge
1. Return to Chel Morgan.
1. Investigate the docks at [[Port Naythex]] {{waypoint 712, 0, -325}} (will update anywhere around west end of docks)
1. Return to Chel Morgan.
1. Search [[Black Water Lake]] for the missing dagger, which is very small and exactly at the water's edge. {{waypoint -492.41, -1.32, -909.96}} Make sure you right click the dagger and pick it up..
1. Return to [[Field Investigator Chel Morgan|Chel]] for your reward.

- At least 5s 67c
