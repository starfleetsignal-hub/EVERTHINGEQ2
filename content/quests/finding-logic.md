## Steps

1. Leave Gnomeland Security. From where Raffik is take the road west, then south at the fork.
1. Zone into [[Klak'Anon]]. {{waypoint -213, 85, 1401}}  Upon zoning in you should see [[A broken clockwork gnome]] west of your position. Right click for update.
1. Return to Grendlo Nimwitty for your reward.

## Reward

- At least 2g 69s 57c
- [[Grease Stained Sandwich]]
