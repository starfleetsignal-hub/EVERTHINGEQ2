## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Right click on the door next to the quest giver and select the option to go 'through the door'. You will enter the instance [[The Cove of Decay: A Heroic Confrontation]]
1. Kill [[Saltwater Saul]] {{waypoint -276, -21, 1176}}. In order to make him aggressive you need to first kill [[Navigator Nina]] and [[Brineblender]] at {{waypoint -193, -22, 1200}}.
1. Kill [[Kor'allaztaraka the Feared]]. He will rise from the watery depths after Saltwater Saul has been killed.
1. Kill [[Hairaza Sollen]]. She becomes aggressive after Kor'allaztaraka the Feared has been killed. {{waypoint -158, -22, 1172}}

## Rewards

- 5 [[City Token|City Tokens]]
