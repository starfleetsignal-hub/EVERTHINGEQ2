## Description

I've been commissioned to retrieve a number of historical artifacts from within Veksar.

## Steps

1. Retrieve five items from within [[Veksar: The Sunken Theater|Veksar]]
   - a painting on the walls {{waypoint -60, -1, -48}}
   - a statue from the halls {{waypoint 12, 6, -116}} - *see talk page for possible other locations
   - an urn {{waypoint 85, 7, -83}}
   - a tome {{waypoint 40, 6, -233}}(at the bottom of the climbing wall)
   - a chest of valuables {{waypoint -11, 0, -187}}
1. Return to [[Lieutenant Kresic]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
