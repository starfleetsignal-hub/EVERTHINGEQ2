## Note

You must mentor to level 50.  The mobs that drop the pieces of cork required to spawn the Queen must be at least green con.

## Steps

1. Defeat [[Queen Zynixia]] in the [[Tower of the Drafling]]
1. Return to the Chronomagi Tasker

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Chronomancers**
- Ability to purchase [[Replica: Queen Zynixia]] from [[Timeless Trader]] for 1p 8g
