## Steps

1. Use [[Szeth's Diaku Illusion Elixir]] to make the Daiku forces non-aggro and proceed toward {{waypoint 428.34, 51.95, -212.14}}.
   - *Note*: The illusion does not work on the heroic diakus.
1. You will be recognized and teleported. Witness the conversation. You will be teleported into a [[Shining Mesa Stronghold]]-Solo-instance.
   - *Note*: Be sure to resummon your mount.
   - *Note*: If you are in a group while you do the quest, every member gets their own instance.
1. Proceed towards {{waypoint 79.96, 115.15, -109.40}}.
1. You will be attacked by [[An Invoker of Flame]]. You will not be able to kill him. Eventually [[Khap]] will appear and defeat them.
1. Speak with [[Khap]].
1. Continue to {{waypoint 384.18, 65.15, -287.05}} and walk through the portal to return to [[Wracklands]].
1. Return to [[Szeth Hibis'kis]] at {{waypoint 541.13, 34.55, 150.72}}.
