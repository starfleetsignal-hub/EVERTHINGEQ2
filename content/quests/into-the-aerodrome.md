## Steps

1. Go to [[The Aerodrome]] in [[Chardok]] and complete the following tasks: {{waypoint -85, 6, -295}}
   - Destroy the *Totem of I'ar*: {{waypoint -74, -18, -319}}
   - Destroy the *Totem of I'ar Owt*: {{waypoint -94, -18, -307}}
   - Destroy the *Totem of I'ar Ethre*: {{waypoint -74, -18, -283}}
   - Slay 4 [[A Bathezid aerodrome sentry|Bathezid aerodrome sentries]].
   - Slay [[Master Skyrider Koonzid]].
1. Return to [[Warlord Gadar Gwalnax]] in [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]].

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Gwalnax Brigade**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Exarch's Solemn Ceremony Wristguard of Oration]]
  - [[Executioner's Cobalt Bracers of Dominance]]
  - [[Illuminate's Fallen Leaves Wrist Wraps of Overbearing]]
  - [[Magus's Ensorcelled Satin Cuffs of Blasting]]
  - [[Outrider's Blue Iron Chain Vambraces of Stealth]]
  - [[Stormcaller's Winter Weave Bands of Restoration]]
  - [[Witchdoctor's Ill Omen Chain Cuffs of Commanding]]

## Notes

- Continuing SOE's penchant for the backwards, the three totems in this quest are, as anagrams, "Air," "Air Two," and "Air Three."
