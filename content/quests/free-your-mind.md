## Steps

To start this quest, you must have accepted [[Overseer Clackwhinge]]'s quest, [[Falling Out]].

1. Retrieve supplies from Clackwhinge's Cellar - click the wooden trapdoor inside Malvonicus' Tower to enter, then click the sparkling treasure chest
1. Return the supplies to Shortspanner ![Chest of Clackwhinge's parts](images/Chest_of_parts.jpg)
1. Use the [[Entropic Retrogressor]] on 8 [[A twisted Far Seas crewmember|twisted Far Seas crewmembers]]
   - Most will turn into Far Seas survivors, but for some (roughly 25%) the game will emote in white 'This one is too far gone' and it will attack you.  Only uses of the Retrogressor that save crewmembers count as updates
1. Return to Shortspannner
