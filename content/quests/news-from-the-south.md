## Description

"Jenthis wants me to travel south to the Emerald River. There I am to meet Fhera Dyalla within the rocky area near Tunare's Sapling. I need to get her report and deliver it to Jenthis." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Fhera Dyalla]] {{waypoint -101, 7.5, 41}}
1. Collect 4 buckets of purified water (on the ground around Fhera)
1. Move near {{waypoint -80, 0, 12}} and use [[Bucket of Purified Water]] from  your inventory to put out the flames. After each bucket used, [[An orc arsonist]] will spawn.
1. Return to [[Jenthis Viridar]]

## Rewards

- At least 7s 54c
- [[Royal Kelethin bed]]

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Jenthis Viridar: "Did you get an order book?"
- Player Character: "Yes, I acquired several."
- Jenthis Viridar: "Good, good. While I work on figuring out their plans, I have another job for you."
- Player Character: "What job?"
- Jenthis Viridar: "South of here, where the Emerald River comes to rest about Tunare's Sapling, you'll find some rocks. That's where you'll meet Fhera Dyalla. Bring her report back to me."
- Player Character: "All right."

I need to collect Fhera Dyalla's report. I need to speak with Fhera Dyalla where the Emerald River meets Tunare's Sapling, south of Sapling Spur Outpost.

- Fhera Dyalla: "Help. Please help!"
- Player Character: "What's wrong?"
- Fhera Dyalla: "The orcs... They were just here minutes ago. I couldn't stop them from poisoning the Emerald Lake. They splashed some black goop on Tunare's Sapling, and now it's in the water."
- Player Character: "Jenthis sent me to gather your report. I don't think he expected this..."
- Fhera Dyalla: "I am wounded. But, my injuries must wait... the orcs are attempting to burn Tunare's Sapling to the ground. You've got to stop them!"
- Player Character: "What can I possibly do?"
- Fhera Dyalla: "I've purified some of the tainted water. Look for the buckets nearby, pick them up, and then use them on the burning tree. Enough purified water should douse the flames. And keep an eye out for orcs!"
- Player Character: "I will put out the fire!"

The orcs are trying to burn down Tunare's Sapling! I need to prevent the orcs from burning down Tunare's Sapling.

I helped put out the fire. I have saved Tunare's Sapling from the orc arsonists. I need to give Fhera's report to Jenthis. I need to return to Jenthis with news of the orc attack.

- Jenthis Viridar: "Do you have Fhera's report?"
- Player Character: "Yes, the orcs have attacked!"
- Jenthis Viridar: "There is orc activity in the south? Tell me what has happened!"
- Player Character: "The orcas attempted to burn down Tunare's Sapling, but I was able to stop them."

I have spoken with Jenthis. I have given Jenthis the report Fhera gave me.

I have given Jenthis the news from Fhera Dyalla.
