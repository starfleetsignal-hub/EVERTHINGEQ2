## Steps

![undead ward (clickable)](images/Caught_by_the_Tail_%28undead_ward%29.png)

1. Examine all the traps northeast of the [[New Combine Outrider Encampment]] to see which one was triggered.  It will be selected randomly.
1. Pick up the unconscious scout in the trap
1. Return to Outrider Storian
1. Speak to Outrider Storian
1. Gather undead wards (5) from the barrels in the [[Eerie Encampment]]
   - Attack the barrels.  They may not have a ward inside them - if they don't, you'll get an orange emote saying so.  If they do, the ward is left where the barrel was and can be collected via left click
1. Return to Outrider Storian
