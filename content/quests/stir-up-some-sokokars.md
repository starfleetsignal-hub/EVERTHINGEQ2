## Steps

1. Head to [[Murkdweller's River]] and complete the following tasks: {{waypoint 560, -78, 495}}
   - Kill 4 [[A trained war sokokar|trained war sokokar]]s
   - Release 4 [[Leashed sokokar|leashed sokokars]] by clicking their posts:
     - {{waypoint 535, -76, 577}}
     - {{waypoint 591, -77, 530}}
     - {{waypoint 552, -76, 436}}
     - {{waypoint 470, -76, 496}}
1. Return to Field Chief Ry'zilk.

- At least 14g 35s 64c
- +6,000 faction with **Ry'zilk's Renegades**
