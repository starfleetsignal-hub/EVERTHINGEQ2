## Steps

1. I must collect Caertaxian Legion necro-portal remnants for the Guardians of the Underfoot within The Hole.
   - Collect 5 Caertaxian Legion necro-portal remnants from frenzied necromancers and scavenging bone collectors. Also drops regularly from idle handlers in the area near the [[The Shattered Pillar]].
1. Return to Mourne.

## Rewards

- At least 20g 70s 61c
- +750 faction with **Guardians of the Underfoot**
