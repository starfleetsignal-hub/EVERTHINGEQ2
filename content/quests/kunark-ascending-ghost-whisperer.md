## Steps

1. Find [[A nameless spirit|a nameless iksar spirit]] {{waypoint 701.94, 222.03, 903.95}} at its first location close to the mouth of Crusader Cave. ![a nameless spirit](images/A_nameless_spirit.jpg)
1. Speak with the iksar spirit who will run off after conversation.
1. Find the iksar spirit again {{waypoint 465.87, 136.01, 724.13}} at his second location next to a lavapool.
1. Speak to him again and defeat the [[A magma elemental|magma elemental]] (level 102-103^^ golem), that will automatically attack you.
1. Continue your interrupted conversation with the spirit.
1. Kill more [[A magma elemental|magma elementals]] until you get the quest update receiving an [[Ancient Knife Hilt]].
   - *Note: Depending on your luck you'll need to kill between 8 and 15 elementals to receive the hilt*
   - *Note: [[A magma elemental|magma elementals]] can be found in pools of lava in the surrounding area
1. Return to the iksar spirit and speak with him yet again.
1. Return to [[Setri Lur'eth (Obulus Frontier)|Setri Lur'eth]] {{waypoint 620.24, 242.48, 1185.92}} in the [[Crusader's Cave]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 41g 75s 36181 status
