## Steps

1. Speak to Amoora's father, [[Jaharin]], in the [[Kerran Village]] across the river {{waypoint -190, -47, 139}}.
1. Now speak to Ferink's mother, [[Riki]], in the [[Ratongan Village]], back across the river {{waypoint -303, -47, -5}}.
1. Deliver the bad news to Ferink at his place by the river.  This completes the quest.

- At least 2s 67c
