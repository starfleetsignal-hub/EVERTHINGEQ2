## Preq's

1. MUST be of Freeblood Race
1. Must have Marketplace  'Cloud of Bats' ability
1. Must be at least Level 86

## Steps

1. Speak to Remmi, next to Drammind, so she will join you.
1. Defeat 8 Rime with Remmi. (NOTE: Remmi is considered a pet)
1. Return to Drammind to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
