## Steps

1. ![One of the "dragon images" around the vault](images/A_dragon_image.png)After you receive the quest, you will need to examine the "dragon image" Celtic knot symbols **seen on the four pillars** in [[The Lair of Scale]] (the platform with the vault).
   - The exact location where you can click to examine is a bit buggy, so just move your mouse all over each side of the columns until you see it flash 'a dragon image'. The interaction point should be in the middle part of the column on the side facing away from the vault.
     - Northeast column is at {{waypoint -6, 123, 300}}
     - Northwest column is at {{waypoint 29, 123, 300}}
     - Southeast column is at {{waypoint -6, 123, 339}}
     - Southwest column is at {{waypoint 28, 123, 339}}
1. Find four keys by killing the drakota on platforms within [[The Audience Chamber]]:
   - Copper Key: [[A furywing drakota|furywing drakota]] {{waypoint 46, 89, 250}}
   - Silver Key: [[A furywing drakota|furywing drakota]] {{waypoint -29, 89, 248}}
   - Black Key: [[A furywing drakota|furywing drakota]] {{waypoint -59, 89, 279}}
   - Gold Key: [[Koturah]] {{waypoint 25, 89, 255}}
     - The furywing drakota placeholder for Koturah will NOT give the update for this quest. Respawn seems to be about 20 minutes.
1. Return and examine the vault at {{waypoint 11, 121, 320}}. You are unable to turn the lock, but there is something written on the vault:
   - *"When the staves of the yore scales meet the vortex of scaleborn, when the four arms are raised, only then shall the maw of fortune release itself."*
   - This used to require the player to speak [[Draconic]], but this requirement has since been removed.
1. Search around the dungeon for fifteen dragon staves, also called 'ceremonial darslayers'. Like collectibles, they have a set amount of charges and then they despawn. If this happens, wait about 5 minutes and they will respawn. They are spread throughout the entire Sanctum:
   - ![a ceremonial darslayer](images/Ceremonial_darslayer.png)Follow the list below in reverse if you are starting from the previous step.
   - If you have a tinkered [[Gnomish Divining Rod]] or the [[Track Harvestables]] ability, you can track the staves.
   1. [[Ready Room]] {{waypoint 196, 158, -57}}
   1. [[The Oratorium]] {{waypoint -12, 136, 90}}
   1. The Oratorium {{waypoint 31, 136, 90}}
   1. [[Scaleborn Barracks]] {{waypoint 199, 136, 41}}
   1. [[Chamber of Animation]] {{waypoint -176, 136, 56}}
   1. [[Chamber of the Diviners]] {{waypoint -251, 84, 244}}
   1. [[Fount of Awakening]] {{waypoint -57, 78, 180}}
   1. [[Hall of Rites]] {{waypoint 8, 55, -46}}
   1. [[Pool of Spirits]] {{waypoint 157, 41, 144}} (in the water)
   1. Pool of Spirits {{waypoint 149, 41, 143}} (in the water)
   1. [[The Gorgs' Pit]] {{waypoint -14, 58, 303}}
   1. The Gorgs' Pit {{waypoint 36, 58, 302}}
   1. Ledge above the entrance/exit to The Gorgs' Pit {{waypoint -13, 74, 290}}
   1. Ledge above the entrance/exit to The Gorgs' Pit {{waypoint 29, 74, 290}}
   1. Ledge above the entrance/exit to The Gorgs' Pit {{waypoint 12, 74, 290}}
      - If the above three locations have you going in circles, go to {{waypoint 9.5, 87, 287}} and look down to get a visual on the ledge.
1. Run over the Scaleborn vortexes throughout the dungeon and you will automatically place a dragon staff at it. Scaleborn vortexes are the snail-shell-shaped symbols with purple draconic lettering located on the ground:
   1. [[Mouth of the Sanctum]] {{waypoint -16, 158, -67}}
   1. Mouth of the Sanctum {{waypoint 38, 158, -67}}
   1. Chamber of Animation {{waypoint -148, 134, 54}}
   1. Chamber of Animation {{waypoint -148, 134, 54}}
   1. Chamber of Animation {{waypoint -148, 134, 54}}
   1. Chamber of Animation {{waypoint -148, 134, 54}}
   1. Hall of Rites {{waypoint -19, 65, -2}}
   1. Hall of Rites {{waypoint 29, 65, 13}}
   1. [[Room of Recovery]] {{waypoint -145, 41, -157}}
   1. [[The Audience Chamber]] {{waypoint -54, 86, 339}}
   1. The Audience Chamber {{waypoint -37, 86, 295}}
   1. The Audience Chamber {{waypoint -10, 86, 255}}
   1. The Audience Chamber {{waypoint 43, 86, 267}}
   1. The Audience Chamber {{waypoint 50, 86, 382}}
   1. The Audience Chamber {{waypoint 33, 86, 377}}
1. Inspect the column at the middle of the Gorgs' Pit. {{waypoint 13, 55, 321}}
1. Within 3-minutes, click four polychromatic (**red**) blocks found along all three climbable walls of the Gorgs' Pit (**one** on the **east** wall, **one** on the **west** wall, and **two** on the **south** wall).
   - If you have trouble seeing the red blocks (balanced or lower settings may have trouble), go to Options --> Peformance --> Detail Range --> and set it as high as possible.
   - The blocks are called 'loose blocks', you can click them from relatively far away, and they are always in the same place.
1. Within 3-minutes, click four **gold** blocks found along the **south** climbable wall.
1. Within 3-minutes, click flour **green** blocks found along the **east** climbable wall.
1. Within 3-minutes, click flour **yellow** blocks found along the **west** climbable wall.
1. Examine the vault again in [[The Lair of Scale]] {{waypoint 11, 121, 320}}. Upon completion you will be ambushed by three [[A dreadknight|amygdalans]].
