It looks like my apprentice could use some new equipment. I should craft them a Sturdy Matoppie Basket to speed up their progress.

## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Matoppie Basket for my apprentice.
   - I need to craft a Sturdy Matoppie Basket for my apprentice.
     - Acquire the following ingredients and fuel:
       - 2 [[Matoppie roots]]
       - 4 [[Effulgent sandpaper]]
     - Using a [[Woodworking Table]], craft 1 [[A Sturdy Matoppie Basket (Item)|A Sturdy Matoppie Basket]]
1. I need to take this basket back to my apprentice.
   - I need to take this basket back to my apprentice.
