## Starting the Quest

1. Prior to examining the corpse (starter), the player must have completed the quest [[Arcana and Teleportation]] from [[Alim Dabir]] {{waypoint 1981, -298, 3338}} in Paineel. You won't be able to see the corpse without first having Paineel teleporter access to The Hole via this quest.
1. Locating the corpse is a challenge for many. You'll take the first teleporter to the East, run down the ramp and go to the South teleporter. From there, run due East to the cliff. Jump down and you will be at the corpse. You can also try looking for {{waypoint -378.52, -22.62, 318.97}} in the southern half of the map over the gorge. It's on the 2nd ledge down from the pyramid building. *April 2025 update/clarification: you need to kill [[Nayt the Grate Keeper]] to access the map where corpse is found. After you kill Nayt, there is a "wall" that can be attacked. Once the wall is down, there is a teleport ring that will take to a different part of the map--the pyramid building. You can just jump down to the second ledge at {{waypoint -378.52, -22.62, 318.97}}
1. Obtain [[The Last Will and Testament of Taglfitz Von Gearbender]] from the corpse of Taglfitz Von Gearbender.
   - Examine the will from your inventory to begin the quest.

## Steps

1. Find [[Toggery Von Gearbender]] at {{waypoint -246, -60, -657}} in the [[Temple of Solusek Ro]] in [[Lavastorm]].
1. He sends you out to "contain a spirit" for use by the Von Gearbenders.
   - Go to [[Spirit's Resonance]] and kill [[Nortlav the Scalekeeper]].
1. Speak to Toggery Von Gearbender again.
1. Speak to Deloris Von Gearbender. She will now be standing right next to Toggery.
1. Kill every named mob in [[The Outer Vault]] except the Epic at the end. (You can kill him--it just isn't needed for this quest.)
1. Speak to Deloris Von Gearbender again at {{waypoint -246, -60, -657}} in the [[Temple of Solusek Ro]] in [[Lavastorm]].
1. Go back to [[Demitrik's Bastion]], and click Taglfitz's corpse again. **NOTE: Taglfitz' corpse does not respawn in original zone.  You will need to RE-SET the dungeon and repeat all steps to get to Taglfitz' corpse.**
1. Speak to Deloris Von Gearbender again.
1. She hands you a letter that goes to your inventory
1. Click on the letter
1. Taglfitz Von Gearbender appears beside you.
1. Follow his dialogue to receive reward
