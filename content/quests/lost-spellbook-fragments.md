- ***Note**: Evil-aligned characters may not be able to complete this quest; see below.*

## Steps

- Take the fragment to [[Amren Talbot]] in the [[Concordium Tower]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District|South Qeynos]].

## Notes

- The monster [[Kinloc Flamepaw's Remains]] only spawns after dusk/night.
- He will not update this quest for evil characters.
  - *This note was left here almost a decade ago; unsure if the 'He' is [[Kinloc Flamepaw's Remains]] or [[Amren Talbot]].*
    - If the 'He' is Amren Talbot then Evil-aligned characters could start the quest, but be unable to complete it.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 68c
