## Steps

1. Travel to the [[Erudin Gardens]] and slay several void creatures. Use the teleporter next to [[Laila]] for easy travel.
   - Slay 6 [[A Theerian shadowblade|Theerian shadowblades]]. {{waypoint -259, 524, -52}}
   - Slay 6 [[A void destroyer (SH)|void destroyers]]. {{waypoint -98, 520, -5}}
1. Report back to [[Qutaybah]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +5,000 faction with **Scions of the Deepwater Knights**
- One of the following:
  - [[Deepwater Symbol of the Shoreman]]
  - [[Deepwater Symbol of the Voyager]]
  - [[Deepwater Symbol of the Ameliorant]] (caster choice)
  - [[Deepwater Symbol of the Elementalist]] (caster choice)
  - [[Deepwater Symbol of the Wavecrasher]]
