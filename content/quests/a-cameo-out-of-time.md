## Steps

1. Recover the other missing Antonia Bayle cameo piece. This drops in a chest from mobs in the zone.  2nd chest does not always drop in the same instance.
1. Speak to [[Vusal Zaminli]] in [[The Chronoweave]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- 1 [[Ancient Platinum Coin]]
- Experience
