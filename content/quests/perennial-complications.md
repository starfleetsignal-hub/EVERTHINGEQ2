## Steps

1. Gather 7 plants in the water areas in [[Esoterra Gardens]]  {{waypoint -659, 31, 574}}.![Garden Water Plant](images/Waterplant.jpg)
   - They have green foliage and white-tipped flower stalks.
1. Return to [[Karo-amat]].
1. Grind plants at {{waypoint -733, 123, -480}}.![The Grinder](images/The_Grinder.jpg)
1. Return to [[Karo-amat]].

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Piece of Sheet Music]]

- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
