This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Enter [[A Maj'Dul Residence]].
1. Search a wicker basket in the back room {{waypoint -30, 0, -3}} to spawn a scorpion or cobra.
1. Move the creature like a house item to the bed in the side room containing 'a Maj'Dul resident'. {{waypoint 0, 0, 25}}
1. Kill the djinn and two flame torrents that spawn.
1. Return to any Tears grifter for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least3g 23s 29c
- One of the following:
  - 7 [[Blades insignia token|Blades insignia tokens]]
  - 7 [[Coin insignia token|Coin insignia tokens]]
  - 7 [[Truth insignia token|Truth insignia tokens]]
