Academy scholars attempting to study ancient Teir'Dal monuments within Nektulos have been chased from the forest by Thexain loyalist. Archivist Jindlefog of the Arcane Scientists has asked me to clear a path for the researchers by killing the bandits.

## Steps

- Kill 16 [[A Thexian ruffian|Thexian ruffians]]/[[Everquest2:A Thexian scout|Thexian scouts]] in [[Nektulos Forest]].
- Look near the [[Darklight Wood]] Gate. In that area you will find the [[A Thexian ruffian|Thexian ruffians]] and [[Everquest2:A Thexian scout|Thexian scouts]].

## Rewards

- 2403 status
- +150 faction with **The Academy of Arcane Science**
