Once again, I have been called to the aid of Doctor Arcana. I am to enter a new realm in pursuit of information. I am to investigate the occurrence that has left the estate known as Unrest not only in total devastation, but also suspended in the alternate dimension of arcane power known as Ultera. Doctor Arcana has instructed me to brave the horrors that await and collect some of the crystals that appear to have formed in the presence of the massive metallic shards that appear to have brought on the destruction.
