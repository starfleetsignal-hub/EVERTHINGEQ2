## Note

You may want to have [[Overgrowth Seed Stealers]] in your journal before starting step 6, and [[Bringing Peace to the Eternal Sen-tree]] prior to step 8. The latter only obtainable after the former is complete.

## Steps

1. Search for [[Faezoth]] and [[Silrena]] at the [[Blinded Eye Camp]] {{waypoint -381.32, 250.75, 140.98}}
1. Speak to [[Faezoth]] .
1. Find the island [[Hu Haket]] {{waypoint 15.28, 13.15, 1026.56}}
1. Examine piles of **Clawed Bones** around the druid ring {{waypoint 244.57, 47.70, 1158.36}} on [[Hu Haket]].
1. Return to [[Faezoth]] at the [[Blinded Eye Camp]] {{waypoint -381.32, 250.75, 140.98}}
   - Obtain 8 **draconic essence** by killing [[An angler beast|angler beasts]] at [[Hu Haket]]![An angler beast](images/An_angler_beast.png)
   - Obtain 10 **entangler nettles** by killing [[A grove entangler|grove entanglers]] around {{waypoint -548, 89, -172}} or {{waypoint -226, 124, -387}} at [[Tera'Noth Peninsula]]![A grove entangler](images/A_grove_entangler.png)
1. Enter [[Takish Badlands: Overgrowth (Solo)]] in [[Firiona's Motte]] at {{waypoint 742.43, 58.08, 42.50}}
   - Obtain 8 **spell-shaped stones**. These are scattered throughout the zone. See talk page for possible locations.![Spell-shaped stone](images/Spell_shaped_stone.png)
   - Progress through the zone:
     1. Kill [[Florafang]] {{waypoint 739.01, 58.07, 60.30}}
     1. Kill [[The Gobbler]] {{waypoint 748.05, 59.17, -294.66}}
     1. Kill [[Cleavespine]] {{waypoint 469.57, 110.82, -605.45}}
     1. Kill [[Tawerek]] {{waypoint 180.86, 94.56, -483.89}} to obtain **versipink fungus**
        1. Note:  I was able to leave the zone and return to Faezoth at this point without killing Leafjaw.  My equipment was in bad shape, and I wanted to proceed with the quest. Go back to where you came in and use the zone portal there.
     1. Kill [[Leafjaw]] {{waypoint 82.20, 80.03, -314.23}} to access the zone exit portal.
1. After zoning OUT to Takish Badlands, seek [[Faezoth]] at [[Hu Haket]] {{waypoint 245.74, 48.83, 1166.67}}
   1. Watch the spell, then kill [[Kozurenth]].
   1. Speak to [[Faezoth]].
      - *Note: you can now use "a transport of Growth" at [[Hu Haket]] {{waypoint 78.13, 1.88, 974.46}} to travel between there and [[Blinded Eye Camp]] {{waypoint -391.65, 250.34, 125.33}}*
1. Enter [[Takish Badlands: Kigathor's Glade (Solo)]] just outside the [[Blinded Eye Camp]] at {{waypoint -394.33, 245.01, 213.48}}
   - Search for [[Zizzez]], the late Sultan's Jester
     - Kill [[Lopik Runtbottom]] {{waypoint -803.39, 181.02, 208.54}}
     - Kill [[Migral Strongfeet]] {{waypoint -852.93, 261.70, -156.32}}
     - Kill [[Chief Dunesea]] {{waypoint -529.34, 99.94, -141.35}}
   - Find [[Zizzez]] at {{waypoint -548.24, 80.76, -212.79}}.
     - Zizzez will run away. Find him somewhere else.
     - Kill [[The Harrower]] {{waypoint -428.34, 139.04, -499.09}}
     - Kill [[Zizzez]] at {{waypoint -546.58, 109.65, -523.81}}
     - Kill [[Kigathor Stonebark]] at {{waypoint -687, 149, -541}}
     - Exit to [[Takish Badlands]].
1. Return to [[Faezoth]] at [[Blinded Eye Camp]] {{waypoint -381.32, 250.75, 140.98}}
