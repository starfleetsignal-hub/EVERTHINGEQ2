## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Impressed by my success fighting the Zakir Rish, Riccaww has asked me to carry out further vengeance upon them for the mutilation and suffering brought upon his kin. He wants me to slay more maedjinn and to destroy their arcanum caches.

1. The gold and platinum maedjinn and their arcane caches can be found at their respective strongholds at the highest reaches of Aether Wroughtlands.
   - Hunt 6 Zakir Rish goldwatchers or Zakir Rish goldweavers {{waypoint 854.38, 329.13, 100.20}} or {{waypoint 759.62, 312.47, 49.89}}.
     - *Note: Quest asks either for Zakir Rish goldwatchers or Zakir Rish goldweavers*
     - *Note: Take the cloud at {{waypoint 726.80, 275.73, 205.55}} for acces to [[Albalatin Terrace]] because you can't fly up there directly*
   - Hunt 6 Zakir Rish platinum platinumancers or overguards {{waypoint -65.85, 650.36, -36.93}}
     - **You can find easier mobs to complete this at around {{waypoint 67, 662, -108}}**
   - Destroy either Albalatin arcanum caches or Am Dhahab arcanum caches
     - Destroy 8 Albalatin arcanum caches![](images/Ballads_of_Zimara_-_Ambalatin_Arcanum_Cache.png)
       - {{waypoint -56.32, 652.50, -65.04}}, {{waypoint -7.27, 651.59, -73.33}}, {{waypoint 14.24, 662.68, -121.11}}, {{waypoint 17.50, 662.74, -128.28}}
       - {{waypoint 34.78, 662.36, -184.58}}, {{waypoint 71.42, 663.35, -178.39}}, {{waypoint 65.32, 662.87, -122.98}}, {{waypoint 92.76, 650.08, -76.12}}
     - Destroy 8 Am Dhahab arcanum caches![](images/Am_Dhahab_arcanum_cache.png)
       - {{waypoint 825.90, 328.52, 235.31}}, {{waypoint 840.01, 328.51, 132.41}}, {{waypoint 927.79, 327.95, 81.31}}
       - {{waypoint 904.51, 320.49, -41.55}}, {{waypoint 809.14, 312.46, -40.07}}, {{waypoint 856.23, 312.45, -44.87}}
       - {{waypoint 788.36, 279.21, 555.50}}
1. return to [[Riccaww]] at [[Turquoise Oasis Camp]] {{waypoint -33.75, 87.18, 483.86}}
