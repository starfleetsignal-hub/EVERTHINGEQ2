## Steps

1. Go to [[Greater Faydark]] and use the [[Lense Manufactory]] to place [[Minute Reflection Amplifiers]] into your inventory.
1. ![](images/Minuscule_spider_web2.png)With the Minute Reflection Amplifiers equipped in your helm spot, collect 20 [[Spider silk|minuscule spider webs]].
   - They are trackable using [[Track Harvestables]].
   - They spawn throughout the zone, however some areas high concentration are:
     - [[Clan Crushbone]] {{waypoint -825, 80, -143}}
     - [[Valley of the Great Swarm]] {{waypoint -280, 22, -110}}
     - The area around [[Sapphire Pond]].
     - {{waypoint -451, 19, 109}}
1. Return to [[Juna Crankfizz]] and have her craft the legendary clock of the Fireknight!

## Rewards

- [[Cloak of Flames]]
