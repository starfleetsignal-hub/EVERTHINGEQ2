## Steps

1. Gather the following
   - 6 **lorpa vines** (sparkly non trackable plants on cliff shelves above village, can be harvested while flying)
   - 8 monitor toxins (auto-update from the [[A brackwater monitor|brackwater monitors]])
1. Speak with [[Dorl]] in hut near [[Guld]], {{waypoint -1131, 28, 1407}} to receive 3 x [[A pygmy spear-wall|pygmy spear-walls]].
1. Build the first piece of pygmy spear wall {{waypoint -1276, 25, 1457}} killing any Allu'thoans along the way.
1. Build the second piece of pygmy spear wall {{waypoint -1425, 49, 1364}}
1. Build the third piece of pygmy spear wall {{waypoint -1408, 93, 1100}}
1. Continue North on that path in search of [[Captain Greymast]] until you come across two [[An Allu'thoa landhunter|Allu'thoa landhunters]] (101 heroic) standing over [[Corrith Midner]] {{waypoint -1306, 135, 1040}}.  Defeat them.
1. Speak to Corrith.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- [[Brackish Pearl Necklace]]
