## Steps

1. Obtain some [[Spiced Chokidai]] by examining one of dead chokidai scattered in a cave on a cliff on the west end of [[The Green Overlook]]. {{waypoint 2849, 39, 893}}
   - This is not the same cave that [[Captain Redspit]] is in.
1. Use the Spiced Chokidai on [[Blacktalon]]. {{waypoint 2826, 63, 962}}
   - If you delete the Spiced Chokidai, you can return to the cave to gather some more.
1. Rein [[Blacktalon]] while it's distracted (right-click).
1. Return to [[Standard Ku'Uul]].

## Rewards

- At least 3s 62c
