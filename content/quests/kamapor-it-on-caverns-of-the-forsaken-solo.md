## Steps

Kill all bosses in [[Castle Vacrul: Caverns of the Forsaken (Solo)]] as you need the last 2 for this quest:

1. Shatter the Shackled.
1. Expel the Expelled.
1. Pummel the Priestess.
1. Slay the second born.
1. Thwart the Thirstborn.

## Rewards

- At least 3p 59g 25s 12c 107,799 status
- 3 [[Vetrovian Dres'Aag]]
- [[Vetrovia Crate (Version 3)|Vetrovia Crate]]
