## Steps

1. Travel to [[Butcherblock Mountains]] and gather the following in order:
   1. 3 Druid's moss -- around the druid ring {{waypoint -26.02, 181.54, 573.77}}
   1. Broken Fields charred wood -- around {{waypoint -261.39, 149.30, 414.69}}
   1. between 1 and 3 Irontoes elemental samples -- scattered around {{waypoint -481.75, 153.10, -271.40}};
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].<br>

## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable
