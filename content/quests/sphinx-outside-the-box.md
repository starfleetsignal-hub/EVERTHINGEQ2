## Steps

![Scrying Bowl](images/Scrying_Bowl.png)
![Arcane Scroll](images/Arcane_Scroll.png)
![Arcane Puzzleboxes](images/Arcane_Puzzleboxes.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Pannaxxun]]. She will cast a Sphinx illusion on you
1. Collect 10 stolen arcana from "Arcane Scrolls", "Arcane Puzzleboxes", and "Scrying Bowls" on the platforms in [[Pakiat Bluffs]].
1. "Speak to Axulob (description is probably bugged)" : Speak to a [[Pakiat artifact attendant]] on one of the platforms  {{waypoint -826.28, 130.84, -769.03}} In order to speak to the Pakiat artifact attendant, you need to do the following:
   - Hailing the other attendants will trigger the artifact attendant to become active. Hail each of the different types of attendants, Food, then Water, and then Artifact (In that order.)
   - *Hailing Food and Water attendants will NOT give any kind of notification, or change the quest at all.  You must hail a food attendant, water attendant, and an artifact attendant, <B>in that order</B>.  Once you have hailed the food and water attendants then head to an artifact attendant {{waypoint -808, 131, -412.27}} who will have the quest update book above his head.*
   - List of attendants:
     - food attendants: {{waypoint -802.60, 130.86, -357.13}},{{waypoint -695.27, 125.11, -467.86}}, {{waypoint -763.58, 170.85, -727.75}}, {{waypoint -451.55, 165.20, -832.90}}
     - water attendants: {{waypoint -934.75, 81.94, -370.21}},{{waypoint -727.09, 123.26, -460.90}}, {{waypoint -778.04, 173.02, -683.87}}, {{waypoint -475.38, 64.64, -837.49}}
     - artifact attendants: {{waypoint -805.91, 131.76, -409.34}},{{waypoint -820.40, 130.84, -769.96}}, {{waypoint -578.19, 133.96, -836.63}}
1. Return to [[Pannaxxun]] at {{waypoint -202, 573, -558}}.
