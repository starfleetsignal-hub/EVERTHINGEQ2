## Steps

1. Kill 10 chokidai ([[A feral chokidai scavenger|feral chokidai scavengers]] and [[A wild chokidai predator|wild chokidai predators]] update). {{waypoint 402, 68, -540}}
1. Mark 5 spots close to [[Ry'zilk's Base Camp]] with chokidai scent (locations appear as shinies). {{waypoint 795, 70, -470}}
1. Return to Quartermaster Bi'zin.

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- +6,000 faction with **Ry'zilk's Renegades**
- [[Glowing Gem of Fortitude]]
