This looks to be the corpse of a dog that belonged to one of the quarry guards. It has a collar and name tag, his name was Rexon. Needlites killed him and sucked him dry. I should reduce the population of needlites as well as avenge this poor pooch.

1. Kill 15 [[Limestone Needlite|limestone needlites]] in the [[Limestone Forest]].
