## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Avelly will send you to hunt varying numbers of local nuisance creatures, for example:
   - Hunt 8 [[An aggressive needlite|aggressive needlites]].
   - Return to [[Avelley Perryn]] in [[The Upper Quarry]]
   - Hunt 6 [[A bogling cultist|bogling cultists]] 676.38 179.73 274.75 249.92 for blood boil elixir gourds.
   - Return to [[Avelley Perryn]] in [[The Upper Quarry]]

- [[Cavern Needlite]]
