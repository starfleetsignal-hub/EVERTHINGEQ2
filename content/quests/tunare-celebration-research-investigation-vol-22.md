## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Tunare has asked me to assist in gathering naturebound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the naturebound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Naturebound Energy Reserves|naturebound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect energy by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to [[Herald of the Mother of All]] {{waypoint 154, -23, 3}} in [[The Lesser Faydark|Lesser Faydark]]
