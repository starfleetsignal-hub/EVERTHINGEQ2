Although I can now enter Vex Thal, my mortal flesh cannot naturally enter the Plane of Shadow, where Emperor Ssraeshza reigns. Thanks to his command of shadows, his people are no longer simply shissar, and the Akheva are no longer Akheva. He forces Akheva to be remade as he desires, with shissar traits and features. They are his mighty army in Vex Thal, his akhessar! This corruption of Luclin's creations is causing unrest and resistance amongst the Akheva. I hope to use this, to get Aten Ha Ra's assistance in safely entering the Plane of Shadows.

- See [[Vex Thal: Shadow Citadel (Solo)|Walkthrough Vex Thal: Shadow Citadel (Solo)]]

     - Speak with Aten Ha Ra
     - Face Aten Ha Ra
