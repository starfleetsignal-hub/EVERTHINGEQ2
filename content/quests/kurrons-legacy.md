## Steps

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -280.48, 180.72, 0.42}} to enter [[Sanctus Seru: Echelon of Divinity (Solo)|Sanctus Seru: Echelon of Divinity [Solo]]].
   - *Note: Requires completing [[Shattered Dawn: Execution of Order]] to access the instance.*
1. Speak to [[Myrna Goshul]] at {{waypoint -202.53, 179.78, 134.82}}
1. Defeat [[Divine Prophet Buffo II]] at {{waypoint -104.73, 177.88, 145.62}}
1. Defeat [[Grand Cruciator Typhenon]] near {{waypoint -33.01, 179.78, -213.96}} and loot [[Luminary Athenaeum key]] from his body.
1. Speak to [[The Tabularium]] at {{waypoint 7.08, 180.75, -170.14}}.
1. Collect the book in the same room.
   - *Note: Each person in the group must collect their own book. It will respawn in about 2 second. It may be in a slightly different position in the room.*
1. Read [[The Rise of the Luminaries]] from your inventory.
1. Return to [[Nelena Redember]] on the lower level of  [[Sanctus Seru (City)]] at {{waypoint -183.41, 81.87, 226.45}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Redember Heirloom Pearls]]
