This quest is part 2 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Erudite Series.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Sevri Il'Bethod]] who is standing next to the mailbox, outside the inn {{waypoint 2, 0, -36}}.
1. You receive [[Silver Tweezers]] in your inventory.
1. Target any Kerra and use the Silver Tweezers until you have collected 5 kerran hairs. **Note:** After you tweeze a Kerra he can be tweezed again after about one minute.
1. Return to [[Sevri Il'Bethod]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s
- One of the following:
  - [[Chain wristguard of the Abbatoir]]
  - [[Cloth cuff of the Abbatoir]]
  - [[Leather bracer of the Abbatoir]]
  - [[Plate bracer of the Abbatoir]]
