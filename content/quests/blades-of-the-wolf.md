## Steps

1. Kill [[An arctic behemoth|arctic behemoths]] in the [[Jagged Plains]] until you get both a long blade and a dagger that have been embedded in them. {{waypoint 83, 24, -448}}; uncommon updates
1. Bury the blades you receive at the gravestone of Lysbith McNaff.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Vale Carved Charmer's Bracelet]] (All Mages)
  - [[Vale Carved Deputy's Bracelet]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Vale Carved Purifier's Bracelet]] (All Priests)
  - [[Vale Carved Trickster's Bracelet]] (All Scouts)
