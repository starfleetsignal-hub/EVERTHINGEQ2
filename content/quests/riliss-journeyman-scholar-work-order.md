*The supply sergeants have requisitioned a large order to resupply their outposts. The shipment is leaving soon, so please get directly to work.*

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

- I need to create:
  - (6) [[Scale shining agent]]
  - (6) [[Adeste's Disruption (Crafted)|Adeste's Disruption]]
  - (6) [[Luck enchanted bracelet]]
  - (6) [[Ring of counterspell]]
  - (6) [[Zildrax's Stonescale|Zildrax's Stonescale (Journeyman)]]
  - (6) [[Srukin's Cynicism|Srukin's Cynicism (Journeyman)]]
- Turn the completed order into [[Preparations Officer Zekilius]]

## Rewards

- At least 43g 60s 85c
- +750 faction with **Riliss**
