## Steps

1. Head to [[Fens of Nathsar]], use Cockatrice mask and speak to [[A sharp stonebeak]] at {{waypoint 357, -59, 1012}}.
1. Kill [[A Nathsar stonebeak|Nathsar stonebeaks]] for juveniles (15). These will update the quest even if grey to your level.
1. Kill 3 pairs of beta stonebeaks (the paired cockatrices) i.e. [[Domineering cockatrice|domineering cockatrices]]
1. Go to nest at {{waypoint 417, -99, 867}} by Yha-lei fishmen and click it, kill [[Bluequill]] (80^) that pops
1. Go back to [[A sharp stonebeak]]
1. Kill [[Alpha instinct]] (83^^ Heroic)
   - (Note: Another tough fight if solo, same tactics as previous quest, but actually slightly easier than the Kylong Plains one as this one doesn't hit you with the dot).
1. Harvest the rock (Respawns every 5-10 minutes)
