## Steps

1. Kill 50 droags deep in the Fabled Sanctum

## Notes

- Scaleborn warriors and sentries will not update this quest. Only draogs found deeper in the dungeon, such as Scaleborn reavers and various named draogs will provide updates.
- The best place to complete this quest is in [[The Audience Chamber]] {{waypoint -52, 86, 320}}, the large area above the [[Gorg Pit]]. All of the numerous droag in this area will update the quest.
- As with most missions, this quest will automatically complete when you've killed 50 droag. There is no need to return to the entrance to turn in the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 55g
- 5 [[Draconic Etyma]]
