The Isle of Refuge is the only known place in Norrath where the piperacea plant grows. Pepper from this plant is very valuable for trade, and Skrit would appreciate my assistance in restocking her supplies.

## Steps

1. Harvest 10 [[Pepper (Uncommon)|peppers]] from the Piperacea plants
1. Return to [[Skrit]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
