This quest is part 5 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Erudite Series.

## Steps

- Speak to [[Custodian Zaddar Sullissia]] in [[The Graveyard]]. The entrance to The Graveyard is in the south end of Stonestair Byway at {{waypoint 8, -5, 12}}.

Zaddar is the starting point of the [[Freeport Districts Timeline]], beginning with [[The Door Knocker]].

At least 1s
