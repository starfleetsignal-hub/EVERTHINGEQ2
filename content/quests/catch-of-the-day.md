1. Catch 6 [[Kunzar Snapper|Kunzar Snappers]] from pools of fish bearing the same name along the [[Omen's Churn Shore]]. {{waypoint 61, 0, -918}}
1. Return to [[Basher Malgon]]. {{waypoint 209, 73, -957}}
