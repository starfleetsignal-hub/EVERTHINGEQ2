## Steps

1. Enter [[The Arbiter's Master]], which is a special instance of [[Nagafen's Lair]] within [[Solusek's Eye]].
1. Speak with the Arbiter.
1. Speak with [[Lord Nagafen (The Arbiter's Master)|Lord Nagafen]].
1. Kill the four leaders of the Awakened:
   - [[Harla Dar]], within [[The Temple of Scale: Pedestal of the Priestess]] *(accessed from [[The Temple of Scale]])*
   - [[Gorenaire]], within [[Ascent of the Awakened: Audience with the Guardian]]
   - [[Talendor]], within [[Ascent of the Awakened: Audience with the Guardian]]
   - [[Lord Vyemm]], within [[The Laboratory of Lord Vyemm]]
1. Return to Lord Nagafen.

## Rewards

- Access to [[Deathtoll]].
- Your choice of:
  - [[Amulet of the Forsworn]]
  - [[Amulet of the Resolute Servant]]
