## Notes

There are two additional quests in this zone that require you to kill [[Category:Honeybrood Bees (Group)|Honeybrood bees]]: [[Refilling the Stores]] from [[Lady Koivuu]] and [[It Pays To Bee Nice]] from [[Soneej Viivi]], both in [[The Fae Court]].
This is another quest that will not give you the "you did not find any quest items" message. Quest updates only when a venom sac is obtained. Killing the heroic bees, e.g. [[A honeybrood drone]] seems to have a better drop rate for the venom sacs.

## Steps

1. Kill [[Category:Honeybrood Bees (Group)|Honeybrood bees]] {{waypoint -181, -55, 430}} west of the Fae Court station, for their [[A Honeybrood bee venom sac|Honeybrood bee venom sacs]] until you have collected the sacs. Quest will not update with every kill.
1. Return to Uric.

## Rewards

- At least 9g 27s 1c
- 150 faction with **Nybright Family**
