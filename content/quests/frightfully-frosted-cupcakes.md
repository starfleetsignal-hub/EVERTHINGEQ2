To complete this collection quest you must buy [[Pumpkin Bomb|Pumpkin Bombs]] (2 candy corns each) from a Nights of the Dead merchant. These bombs can be launched at other players upon which several mysterious pumpkins will erupt around that PC. These pumpkins may only be gathered by other players, and will spawn random seasonal collectibles in their inventory. You cannot gather these pumpkins yourself, but you will be randomly rewarded with any one of the following items, including a member of this collection, each time you throw a bomb:

- [[Ancient Desert Urn]]
- A title

## Rewards

- one of the following:
  - [[Etched Cenotaph]]
  - [[Engraved Cenotaph]]
  - [[Inscribed Cenotaph]]
