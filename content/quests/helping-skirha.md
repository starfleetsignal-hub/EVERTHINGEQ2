## Steps

1. Obtain the following
   - Obtain 6 **Floral Devourer Seeds** in Burrower's Timber around {{waypoint 557.23, 83.82, 279.20}}
     - *Get these by killing a floral devourer near the trees then collecting the dropped seeds.*  ![](images/Helping_Skirha_quest_-_Floral_Devourer_Seeds.jpg)
   - Obtain 12 direbeak gizzards {{waypoint 616.77, 86.67, 127.75}}
1. Speak with [[Skirha]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
- [[Felfeather Focus Stone Shelf]] - *House Item*
