## Steps

![a Colossi Vacuity Tablet](images/ColossiVacuityTablets.jpg)

1. Obtain 8 Colossi Vacuity Tablets.
   - *Note: Upright stone tables with a blue bubble and rings around it.*
   - Click on tablet and defeat the [[A shissar stalker]] that attacks. Click on the tablet again to collect it. Tablets respawn in the same location within 2 minutes.
   - Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 697.96, 96.94, 262.20}}
     - {{waypoint 834.80, 88.24, 398.16}}
     - {{waypoint 829.45, 101.26, 593.18}}
     - {{waypoint 674.42, 95.63, 755.28}}
     - {{waypoint 493.09, 79.81, 718.98}}
     - {{waypoint 353.42, 93.26, 581.14}}
     - {{waypoint 355.99, 93.30, 397.44}}
     - {{waypoint 484.76, 95.87, 258.92}}
1. Return to [[Tegin The Curator]] at {{waypoint 546.95, 34.56, 134.20}}
