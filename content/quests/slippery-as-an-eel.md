## Steps

![Image of a glacier eel tunnel](images/A_glacier_eel_tunnel.png)

1. Kill four [[A glacier eel|glacier eels]]
   - Click on the eel burrows found on the ice sheets near [[Fina's Retreat]] {{waypoint -1311, -538, -2267}}. Roughly 1 out of 4 burrows will produce an eel after it has been clicked on.
   - The eels are aggro, but it is possible to get out of aggro range before they spawn.
1. Return to Runt

## Notes

- Although Runt says "Come back tomorrow and help me get some more!" there is no timer on this quest. You can repeat it as often as you wish.

## Rewards

- At least 11g 72s 32c
- +200 faction with **Othmir of Velious**
