## Steps

1. Enter [[Gerion: Realm of Conquest (Solo)]] (the gate near Lady Najena, at {{waypoint -117, 1, 71}})
1. Slay 20 members of the Gerion Royal Legion
   - In groups in the Lucanic Stronghold section (the left path.) One update per group.
1. Slay 20 resurrected and everliving foes
   - Found throughout the All that Remains section (the right path.)
1. Destroy 6 crystalline energy accumulators
1. Destroy the last crystalline energy accumulator
   - Found at {{waypoint -503, 8, 298}}, near the final named, Lazirah Darkmoore. The path is only accessible after the first four named have been defeated, and Lazirah must be defeated before the crystalline energy accumulator can be destroyed.
   - Sentry Dillius (4th named, near Claymore) casts Fear
1. Watch the scene (do not exit the instance; if you do exit before the quest updates, you can return and click the bones on the ground for the update.)
1. Return to [[Gerion, Hold of D'Lere]], and speak with Lady Najena near the new portal at {{waypoint -67, 0, 29}}
