## Steps

1. Kill [[A poisonous spinner|poisonous spinners]], [[A voracious spinner|voracious spinners]], and [[A bristly spinner|bristly spinners]] in and around Funnelweb Depths in [[Darklight Wood]] {{waypoint 672, -50, -124}}, until you have 2 to 5 (random each time you take the quest) auto-updates from each. The updates are semi-rare.
1. Collect several spider eggs from the same area.
1. Return to [[Drizas N'Ryt]]

## Rewards

- +5,000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- -5,000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -5,000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
