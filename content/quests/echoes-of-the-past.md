## Steps

1. Collect 3 ptarmigan livers
   1. Go to one of the pools and obtain [[An albino toad]]
      - {{waypoint -38, -384, -761}}
      - {{waypoint -515, -403, -751}}
   1. Place the toad on one of the altars to attract a ptarmigan at each location:
      - {{waypoint 60, -389, -761}}
      - {{waypoint -283, -395, -775}}
      - {{waypoint -573, -412, -693}}
      - {{waypoint 60, -389, -761}}
   1. Kill [[A ptarmigan]] that attacks the toad (this step updates the quest).
1. Speak with Hilgan Gravelheart (He's a ghost dwarf that runs around in circles after you complete the pre-requisite quest.)

Note: As of March 6, 2011 this quest will only update for summoners if the summoner delivers the killing blow, not the summoner's pet.

Note: A snowy cliff beak used to work for this quest but no longer does.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
