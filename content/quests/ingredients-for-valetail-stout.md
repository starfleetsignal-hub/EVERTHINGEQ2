## Steps

1. Go to [[Enchanted Lands]], follow [[The Lazy Drain]] until you get to the [[Rivergate Cascade|waterfall]] at {{waypoint -405.85, -4.92, -55.22}}.
1. Return to Bartender Berrystein at the Fool's Gold in Rivervale.
1. Head to the nearby ravine at {{waypoint -190.63, -6.97, 53.54}} and kill [[Wax worker|wax workers]] until you get 5 ingredient jars.
1. Return the honey to Bartender Berrystein.
1. Kill [[Shakey]] at [[Shakey's Farm]]. {{waypoint -318, -1, -273}}
1. Return to Bartender Berrystein.
1. Go downstairs in [[The Fools Gold]] and talk to [[Fredwyn Goldsyrup]] at {{waypoint -71.81, -24.95, -5.89}}. Tell him you need a mug for *Valetail Stout*. This will complete the current quest and offer the following quest.

- 3g 7s
- One of the following, based on Class:

  - [[Medium's Cincture of Enchanted Energy]] (Mages)
  - [[Defender's Cincture of Enchanted Energy]]
  - [[Healer's Cincture of Enchanted Energy]]
