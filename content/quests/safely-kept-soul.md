## Steps

1. Enter [[Charasis: Vault of Eternal Sleep]] and obtain the *Phylactery of the Dragonslayer, Emperor Ganak* in the [[Altar of the Dragonslayer]] after defeating [[Praetor of the Phylactery]]. {{waypoint 27, -3, -142}}
1. Return to [[Ignazina Thafni]].
1. Kill Ignazina Thafni when she attacks.

- At least 31g 73s 78c

A choice of the following:

- [[Archon's Sanctified Wristguard]] (Plate)
- [[Legionnaire's Rune Etched Bracers]] (Fighter)
- [[Rake's Red Fox Vambraces]] (All Scouts)
- [[Prophet's Blackened Bone Chain Cuffs]]
- [[Hierophant's Vine Wrapped Bands]] (Druids)
- [[Ascendant's Raging Tiger Wrist Wraps]] (Leather)
- [[Master's Illusionary Silk Cuffs]] (All Mages)
