## Steps

1. Speak to a Priestess of the Silver Reaper {{waypoint -467, 15, 78}} in the [[Somborn Cemetery]] in [[Loping Plains]].
1. Speak with [[Miracle Magnus]] {{waypoint -610, 175, 990}} at the [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]].
1. Miracle Magnus needs a few components to make the miracle potion that will allow me to speak with Drinal's Steward
   - Hunt burrowing sand gazers for their paralysis eyestalks, by disturbing dirt mounds between the [[Crossroads]] and [[Freeport]] in [[The Commonlands]]. Doing so will spawn an equal-level gazer which you must kill for the update. (The mounds have a blue particle effect highlighting them...)
   - Gather wild hemlock in the [[Nerian Highlands]], around {{waypoint -50, -30, -300}} in [[Darklight Wood]]. (They look like brown, dead bush nodes...)
1. Return to Miracle Magnus in the Gnomeland Security Headquarters with the components.
1. Consume the [[Miracle Pellet]], which you will find in your inventory after speaking with him..
1. Upon taking Magnus' Miracle Pellet, you will find yourself in the presence of Drinal's Steward. Speak with the Steward.
1. Return to [[Priest Aaronolis Swornlove]] in [[New Halas]].
