## Steps

1. Head to Sinking Sands to speak to Captain Shoreflow {{waypoint -2161, -228, 16}}. Turns out it's not his bird.
1. Then go to Mystic Lake off of the Isle of Mara and talk to Captain Mord {{waypoint 42, -36, 277}}. He doesn't want the bird either.
1. Next visit Greater Faydark and talk to [[Captain Z'Sxhitar]] {{waypoint -536, -28, -642}} at the Shores of Growth near the Nursery. It's not his bird either.
1. Finally go to the Enchanted Lands docks and talk to Captain Screewoggins {{waypoint 39, 4, 116}}. Turns out he's the rightful owner of the parrot, and will complete the quest. But you do get to keep your new pet.
