## Steps

1. Head to [[Trakaraptor Alley]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]] around {{waypoint -755, -52, 395}} and use [[Query Element]] then [[Dominate Element]] on [[A docile earth elemental]]. You will then be given a buff which augments your vision allowing you to see the elemental plane.
1. Head back to {{waypoint -755, -52, 395}} (if you had to wander off to find your elemental) and there should be an elemental with the title "The Wounded" under its name. It's a ^^^80 heroic mob.  Use [[Query Element]] on the wounded earth elemental. Attempting to use [[Dominate Element]] on the wounded earth elemental results in a message stating the "element is solid, and will not allow you to dominate it." Hail it to get it to aggro. (This is intended.)
1. Kill it and retrieve your item off the body.
1. Return to The Apprentice. He tells you why this elemental behaved differently.
