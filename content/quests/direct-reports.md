## Steps

1. Get the reports from the following NPCs:
   - [[Kilriss Zessch]] {{waypoint -254.50, -344.60, 1132.20}}
   - [[Master Mora (Npc)|Master Mora]] {{waypoint -109.80, -48.90, 1230.10}}
   - [[Field Master Selrahc]] {{waypoint -187, -41, -89}}
   - [[Supply Clerk Linxx]] {{waypoint -187.50, -33.50, 1079.00}}
   - [[Shoran Sirrom]] {{waypoint -293.80, -48.80, 1374.40}}
   - [[Vice-Commander Nathash]] {{waypoint -186, -41, -34}}
1. Gather 5 *Danak Supply Lists* from the following locations:
   - {{waypoint 155, 6, 809}}, (top floor on the tower railing)
   - {{waypoint 440, -12, 547}}, (top floor on the tower railing)
   - {{waypoint 10, 14, 121}}, (top floor on the tower railing)
   - {{waypoint -185, -41, -32}}, (behind [[Vice Commander Nathash]], on the table)
   - {{waypoint -150, 50, 338}}, (behind [[Sergeant Slithess]], on the table)
1. Return to [[Somers the Hard (Npc)|Somers the Hard]].
