## Steps

![](images/Riverbank_mushroom.jpg)

1. [[Hanif]], a *Springview Healer*, wants some help gathering medicinal supplies. Collect a number of items for Hanif:
   - 2 mandibles from [[A stone beetle (Commonlands)|stone beetles]] {{waypoint 172, -46, 262}}
   - 2 riverbank mushrooms from the banks of [[Dog Trapper Lake]] {{waypoint 31, -50, -695}}
   - 3 festering skins from [[A scourge rat (Commonlands)|scourge rats]] {{waypoint -20, -49, 38}}
1. Return to Hanif with the supplies.
1. Administer the first two doses to the worst-off here at the camp:
   - Take the medicine Hanif mixes to [[Durio Caepio]] standing by the fire in the center of the camp.
   - Take a dose of the medicine to [[Galla Lentula]] inside the southwestern-most tent.
1. Go back to Hanif and tell him of the visions the lepers have been having.
1. Hanif wishes to study this, but needs some material from [[Lucretias Domna]].  She can be found in [[Enlightenment Cemetery]] {{waypoint 113, -48, -195}}.
1. Kill the nearby zombies for Lucretias to obtain 7 sinews - [[A dusty cadaver|dusty cadavers]], [[A putrid cadaver|putrid cadavers]], [[A rotting cadaver|rotting cadavers]], [[A twitching cadaver|twitching cadavers]] all update
1. Return to Lucretias, who will hand over [[The Dissertation of Affliction]] and a piece of [[Pusling Flesh]] for Hanif.
1. Return to Hanif with the Dissertation and Flesh to finish the quest.

## Notes

- It seems likely that this quest is foreshadowing the return of [[Bertoxxulous]], as the quest-dialogue hints at the disease afflicting the lepers at [[West Nomad Camp]] originating from the [[Plane of Disease]].
