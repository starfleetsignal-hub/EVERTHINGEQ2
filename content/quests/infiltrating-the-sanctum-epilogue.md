## Steps

1. Kill [[Cyenadros, Lord of the Sanctum]] in [[The Lair of Scale]], the platform above [[The Gorgs' Pit]] and [[The Audience Chamber]]. {{waypoint 11, 121, 321}}
   - If he is not up, he can be spawned by first killing the worshippers standing on the ringed rugs on the floor below, which will spawn epic x2 drakotas on the floor above, killing those 4 drakotas will spawn the named.

## Rewards

- At least 27g 50s
- [[A Memento of Cyenadros]], a house item that gives 600 Rent Status Reduction
