## Steps

1. To replenish her supply of tracking powder, Tiff needs the dust from five [[A moon wisp|moon wisps]] (a common update). Moon wisps can be found around The Commonlands' [[Commonlands Spires|Ulteran Spires]] {{waypoint -12, -47, -417}}.
1. Return to Tiff with the wisp essences.
1. Tiff now wants to mark two crates with the tracking powder. These are located in the crates on [[Smuggler's Dock]] at {{waypoint -441, -91, -962}}.
1. Returning to Tiff, she now hands over a map. The map can be examined periodically to see where the crates are going. Follow the crates to their midway points, examining the map after each update. **The points are random from the following list:**
   - Riverbank west of the [[Ratongan Village]] {{waypoint -78, -46, 23}}
   - Road north of the [[Hidden Canyon Station]] {{waypoint 598, -50, 536}}
   - Slightly northeast of the [[Commonlands Druid Rings]] {{waypoint 654, -60, -86}}
   - Bridge south of the [[Kerran Village]] {{waypoint -155, -46, 220}}
   - [[Path of Tears]] {{waypoint 1097, -39, -359}}
1. Eventually, the crates will move to a 'location inside the [[Yapping Maze]]'. They can be found at {{waypoint 1325, -36, -188}} in the Thexian area of the maze.
1. After inspecting the crates to determine their contents, report back to Tiff.
1. By now, the crates have returned to the Blackshield docks. Return there, and hang out near the NPCs on the docks to eavesdrop.
1. Return to Tiff to let her know what the Blackshield smugglers are doing,
