## Steps

1. Collect the following items
   - Kill 4 [[A Stonebrunt stalagmaw|Stonebrunt stalagmaws]] {{waypoint -295, 378, 1258}} and loot the [[Stalagmaw Bits]] from their corpses.
   - Kill 4 [[A Stonebrunt scintilla|Stonebrunt scintillas]],near algthin pond north of [[Moonfield Hamlet (POI)|Moonfield Hamlet]], {{waypoint 185.34, 365.91, 857.33}}. Look Up as they are non-agro and hover. They look like shining balls of light. Loot their corpses for [[Scintilla Dust]].
1. Use the [[Gigglegibber Rock Polisher]] given to you by [[Gawdit Gigglegibber]] (it will be in your bags).
1. Return to [[Gawdit Gigglegibber]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
