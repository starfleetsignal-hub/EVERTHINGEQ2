## Steps

1. Collect 15 bone chips from the undead to the northwest of the observatory
   - These are auto updates from [[A petrified cackler|petrified cackler]]s around {{waypoint 1784, -27, 3813}}
1. Return the bone chips to Ishaq in the observatory at {{waypoint 1832, 6, 4365}}

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- [[Refulgent Gem of Striking]]
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
