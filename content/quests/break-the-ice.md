## Steps

1. After completing [[Steal Them Back!]], Beth will ask you to wait about a minute while she studies the weapons you just turned in before she will talk to you.
1. Sabotage the Order of Rime's *frostruned weapons* in [[Ru'Kaus]]. {{waypoint 179, 79, 227}}
   - Two [[A watchful sleetsaber|watchful sleetsabers]] and one [[A watchful nova-vicar|watchful nova-vicar]] will spawn while your sabotaging the weapons. It is not necessary to kill them.
1. Return to [[Beth Littlefort]].

## Rewards

- At least 36g 79s 48c
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
