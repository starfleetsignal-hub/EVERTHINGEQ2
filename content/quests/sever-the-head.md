## Steps

1. Kill [[Assault Captain Phezzryn]] at the end of the path leading north from Thulwun Station East. {{waypoint 434, 177, -177}}
1. Speak to [[Secondary Shai'Dn]] in [[Thulwun Station East]]. {{waypoint 720, 159, 177}}

## Rewards

- At least 3s 62c
- One of the following, depending on Class:
  - [[Tempered Dagger]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Tempered Knuckles]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Tempered Mace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Tempered Sword]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Tempered Wand]] (All Mages)
