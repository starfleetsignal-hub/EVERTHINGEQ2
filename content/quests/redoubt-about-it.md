## Steps

1. Scout out Mount Dshinn
   - The first area {{waypoint -886, 223, 1146}}
   - The second area {{waypoint -889, 143, 988}}
   - The third area {{waypoint -836, 139, 1105}}
1. Scout out the last area on Mount Dshinn {{waypoint -756, 95, 1033}}
1. Examine the ancient ruins (left-click the boulders)
1. Defeat the [[A disturbed Teir'Dal spirit|disturbed Teir'Dal spirits]] (one 100^H one 100H)
1. Return to Zaveta

- At least 1p 35g

  - [[Zaveta's Treasured Cloak]]
