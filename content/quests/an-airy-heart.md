## Steps

1. Head to {{waypoint 1473, 392, -326}} and use [[Query Element]] then [[Dominate Element]] on [[A docile air elemental]]. You will then be given a buff which augments your vision allowing you to see the elemental plane.
1. Head to {{waypoint 1456, 415, -536}} and there should be an elemental with the title "The Wounded" under its name. Its is a ^^^80 heroic mob. This mob can spawn anywhere in the lost valley. Use [[Query Element]] then [[Dominate Element]] and it will become aggro. Kill it and retrieve your [[An airy heart (Quest Item)|An airy heart]] off the body.
1. Return to The Apprentice
