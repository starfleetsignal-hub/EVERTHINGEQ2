## Notes

This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Kill 3 old dragoons.
   - The skulls on the shelves that line the walls of the instance become clickable. You will be given three names of random skulls to examine. Upon examination a named mob will spawns. Examples are: [[Dragoon B'Lavin]], [[Dragoon Nezzka]], [[Dragoon N'Xry]].
   - Make sure everyone in your group has completed this step before proceeding.
1. Return to the [[Keeper of the Chamber]]. He will [[Keeper of the Chamber (Monster)|become aggressive]]; kill him and slip through the door right away.
   - **Warning**: Immediately enter the door once it opens. If you wait too long to enter (~10 seconds), the door will close and lock preventing completion of the instance.
