## Steps

1. Equip [[Truth Extractor]] (helm)
1. [[Maldreya Darkheart]] patrols the front and back halls inside the [[Temple of War]]. Speak to her away from other NPCs; the stairs {{waypoint -72, -8, -174}} are a good spot. If you are spotted attacking her, then you are expelled from the building.
1. Reduce Maldreya's health to 25% or less and use the Truth Extractor on her.  If she dies too fast, chronomentor to lvl 60.
1. Speak to Rusty
1. Speak to [[Foci Tamara Paust]] in the basement of [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] {{waypoint 18, -34, -147}}
1. Return to where you killed Maldreya and speak to her.
1. The next quest (Agent of Rage) continues in the back room with the humming sigil in it.

## Rewards

- At least 11g 5s 35c
- [[The City of Freeport's Great Port]]
