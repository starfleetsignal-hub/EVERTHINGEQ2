## Steps

1. Inspect the book next to Antiquitor Kantus Mor'Tael titled [[1001 Tales of Maj'Dul]] {{waypoint -236, 135, -11}} (sitting on the ground to the left of the Box of Bones). Write your name, write the title "The Undead Fate of Dalgin B'Dynn" and then keep clicking next page until you update.
1. Go to [[Hullcrusher Rock]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] and slay the [[The Sage of Sea Winds|Sage of Sea Winds]]. {{waypoint -1870, -205, -322}}
1. Examine the gravestone in the [[The Croc Caves|Croc Caves]] and slay the [[The Sage of Shadows|Sage of Shadows]]. {{waypoint -1239, -234, -832}}
1. Locate the [[Aqueduct Ruins]] and slay the [[The Sage of Sandstorms|Sage of Sandstorms]]. {{waypoint -747, -108, -547}}
1. Locate the [[Spectre Tower]] and slay the [[The Sage of Whispering Death|Sage of Whispering Death]]. {{waypoint -1298, -223, -126}}
1. Locate 'Dalgin B'Dynn's Demise', a broken cage under the water at the ocean entrance of the Croc Caves, and slay [[Dalgin B'Dynn]]. {{waypoint -1260, -236, -1112}}
   1. NOTE: If Dalgin doesn't spawn run towards the back of the cave {{waypoint -1251, -234, -1113}} and then back out again. May have to wait a few minutes if he was spawned recently.
1. Return to [[Antiquitor Kantus Mor'Tael]].
