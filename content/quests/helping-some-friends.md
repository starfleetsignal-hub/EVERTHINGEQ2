## Steps

1. Rondo will give you a Prank Preparedness Kit, examine it.
1. Go speak with [[Drundo Parn]] in the tavern.
1. Next speak to [[Jayla Midhop]] outside the armorer.
1. Speak to [[Bolo Brassharp]] outside the Inn/Bank.
1. Return to Rondo.
