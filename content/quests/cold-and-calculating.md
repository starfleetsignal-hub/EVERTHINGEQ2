## Steps

1. Kill some enemies of the Frostfoot
   - Kill [[Tamer Nicoli]]
   - Kill 4 ice drakes, found in the room with [[Hoarfrost Pyreflyte]]
- Kill another enemy of the Frostfoot
   - Kill [[Debitino Coperilus]]
   - Kill 10 wolves, found around Debitino.
1. Return to [[Sergeant Snur Frostfoot]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p
- 16293 status at Level 90
