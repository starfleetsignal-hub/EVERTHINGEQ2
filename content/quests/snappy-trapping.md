Mira suggests that you check the traps you set a short while ago.  She'll need crab sinews, crab shells, and snow cougar pelts to make the armor she mentioned.

## Steps

1. Check the first trap, Northermost one (must be done in order).
1. Check the second trap, Southernmost.
1. Return to [[Mira]]

NOTE: The quest line may "split" once you've completed this one.  MIRA will offer you "Pelt Parlay", but if you are working on the Coldain Shawl quest series, you need to speak to MISA (who is nearby) in order to get the "Monster! Monster!" quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1500 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
- [[Trapped Iceclad Crab]]
