## Steps

1. [[Vikram]] gives you purification icons to be placed in [[Grumblugtin Village]].
1. The placement of each icon is shown by a brilliant shiny spot (shown at right), in order, as you continue to ascend the village.  Continue to cross bridges all the way to the top to place all five.  ![Placement Location for Purification Icon](images/Iconpurify.png)
   - purification icon nidana {{waypoint -410, 155, -680}}
   - purification icon zutra {{waypoint -431, 159, -572}}
   - purification icon cikitsa {{waypoint -515, 150, -632}}
   - purification icon indriya {{waypoint -586, 150, -738}}
   - purification icon sthana {{waypoint -568, 177, -612}}
1. Return to [[Vikram]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- [[Kinnahma Jar of Blessed Essence]]
