## Starting the quest

Players will speak to a different NPC to start the quest depending on whether they are Good or Evil aligned.  Evil characters speak with [[Harbinger Gloska]] {{waypoint 961, 7, 1466}}, Good characters speak with [[Murrar Shar]].  The only difference between the good and evil versions of the quest is a bit of flavor text.

## Steps

1. Find the boat at {{waypoint 1243, 5, -523}} in [[Tranquil Sea]]
1. Locate camp at {{waypoint 1062, 7, -553}} on [[Death Weave Isle]]
1. Inspect the camp equipment
   - a camp object {{waypoint 1054, 6, -562}}
   - another camp object {{waypoint 1044, 8, -556}}
   - a third camp object {{waypoint 1034, 7, -555}}
1. Find some sign of what happened to the Far Seas Traders - go to {{waypoint 1029, 11, -716}} and kill the two [[A Lost battlemaster|Lost battlemaster]]s attacking [[Kitkalla]], then speak with him.  He offers you the quest [[Escorting Kitkalla]] to start the Death Weave Isle solo quest series.
1. Continue to help Kitkalla - complete the quest [[Escorting Kitkalla]]
1. Continue to help on [[Death Weave Isle]]
   - Help the Gorir urzarach colony - complete the quests:
     1. [[Confronting the Lost]]
     1. [[Hand to Mouth]]
     1. [[Testing the Meat]]
     1. [[A Dragonfly, A Spider]]
     1. [[Karrabukk's Word]]
   - Continue to help the Gorir urzarach colony - complete the quests:
     1. [[Redeeming Qualities]]
     1. [[Ritualistic Tendencies]]
     1. [[Redemption's Folly]]
     1. [[The Four-Armed Man]]
1. Speak to [[Karrabukk]]
