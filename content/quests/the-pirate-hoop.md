## Steps

1. Visit Jeweler [[Girafer L`Un]] {{waypoint -137, -22, -88}} in [[North Freeport]]. She will give you another quest, [[The Pirate Hoop - Owner Found]].
1. Go to [[Beggar's Court]] and speak to [[Banwyn Sympronian]] {{waypoint 58, 5, -25}} to close both quests.

## Notes

- The North Freeport step can be skipped, and this quest closed by heading straight to Banwyn in Beggar's Court.  You will not receive the second quest nor credit for it, however.
- The text of the second quest states, in part, "...I should look for a Sympronian - either Banwyn or Gredwyn."  Gredwyn Sympronian's location is currently unknown (although since the earring is "an exact duplicate" of Banwyn's, it can be assumed that Gredwyn is dead).

## Rewards

- Experience (x 2 for both quests)
- At least 11s 40c
