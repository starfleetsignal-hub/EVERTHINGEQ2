## Steps

1. Collect 8 copper tablets from [[Ghastly Fate Point]], around {{waypoint -227, 105, 29}}.
   - Find white **Grimy Tablets** and click to uncover **Ancient Copper Tablet**.![Ancient Grimy Tablet + Copper Tablet Underneath](images/Updatedwrotttablets.png) Not guaranteed update.
1. Return to [[Wrott Havenmore]]

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- 2 [[Ancient Copper Tablet]]
