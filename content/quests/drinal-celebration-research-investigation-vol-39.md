## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of Drinal has asked for my assistance in gathering the needed supplies.

1. Gather the requested supplies
   - Collect 3 [[Crawler Legs|crawler legs]] found in [[Aether Wroughtlands]]
     - *Note: drops in [[Chests|small chest]] from [[A neonblight crawler]] Coral Edge area* {{waypoint 775, 207, -173}}
     - **[NOTE: this target is a heroic mob, as of 5/1/2024.]**
     - Also drops from an emerald devourer. {{waypoint -256.76, 102.92, 338.76}} Adja-Beryl Berms area
     - *1-2 drops per group member*
   - Gather or purchase [[Bittersweet Vineroot|bittersweet vineroot]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas
     - *Common harvest from BoZ root*
     - *Can buy off broker*
     - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to [[Herald of the Silver Reaper]] {{waypoint 85, -3, 291}} in [[Loping Plains]]
