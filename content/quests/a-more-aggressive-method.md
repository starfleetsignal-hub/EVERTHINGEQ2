## Steps

1. Speak with [[Madam Victra]] at [[Wanderlust Fair]] in [[Darklight Wood]] {{waypoint 243, -19, -2}} to procure a *hungry boarfiend*.
1. Return to Vornlin.
1. Take the *hungry boarfiend* to [[Nightshadow Bog]] and kill Nightmarsh Fungusman to spawn *shroomlings* that the boarfiend will eat until he explodes.
   - If your boarfiend vanishes without updating, return to [[Madam Victra]] for a new boarfiend. #***Possible bug*: The quest will not update even after getting near boarfiend. If this occurs, delete the quest and pick it up again.
   - The quest will also only update if you are in the correct area (as shown by the *RoI* on the map).
1. Return to Vornlin.

## Notes

- If you are having trouble getting the update, try killing the fungusmen one at a time. Killing them two or three at a time or being in combat when the boar explodes seems to mess up the quest, and you'll have to restart.
  - 7/8/2012: After having tried everything I've read online about how to get this update, I gave up on these tricks and received the update while killing swiftly (with a merc), while in combat, and after having gotten a new boar multiple times without deleting and restarting the quest. Just get a new boar and keep trying until you get the update.
  - 5/21/2015: The quest works! You MUST KILL 10 mushroom guys to get the update. Ensure you are in the correct area and single pull them to be sure.
  - 4/1/2018: Deleted the quest 3 times. On fourth attempt it worked. I only needed to kill 3 mushrooms when I got the update and I killed 2 of them with AoE. I got the update when I was stood at the POI loc of 350, -46, 260. (I had also just dinged lvl 15).
  - 1/30/2022: The problem with the Boar exploding and the quest not updating is still a thing, needed to drop the quest 4 times to work.
