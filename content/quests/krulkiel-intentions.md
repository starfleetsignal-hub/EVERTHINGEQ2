## Notes

This quest can be done as a group and leads to all the other prelude quests including the tradeskill quests.

## Steps

1. Ride "a geomanced transport" disc at {{waypoint 303, 188, 452}} in Butcherblock Mountains (Highlands Outpost)
1. Locate the purple/bluish portal at {{waypoint 131, 213, -179}}
1. Enter the portal selecting "Kamp Krulkiel: Ritual Sacrifices"
1. Save 12 spellbound captives and kill 18 Krulkiel
1. Defeat [[Grikrut]]
1. Leave the instance via the portal at {{waypoint 111, 249, -326}} and return to [[Physiker Tsaada Hopebringer]]

## Rewards

- [[Spellbind Stave]]
- At least 5g 36s 66c
- +1750 faction with **Open Hand**
