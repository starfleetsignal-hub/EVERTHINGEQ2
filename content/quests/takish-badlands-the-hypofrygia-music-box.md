## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key by killing 15 [[A floralhide gnawer|floralhide gnawer]] around {{waypoint 488.94, 0.00, -113.04}} in [[Takish Badlands]] at [[Deadly Shallows]].
1. I need to find the Hypofrygia Music Box key in [[Sandstone Delta]] from [[A garden pest|garden pests]] around {{waypoint 652.17, 118.92, -387.82}} at [[Feldar Farm]].
1. Examine the clue to receive the quest [[The Hypofrygia Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the **Hypofrygia Music Box** in [[Takish Badlands]].
