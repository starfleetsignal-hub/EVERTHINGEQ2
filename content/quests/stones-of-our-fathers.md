## Steps

![crumbled stonework](images/Crumbled_stonework.jpg)

1. Gather 5 **crumbled stonework** - located in a fairly wide area around the base of the [[Tower of Bone]] {{waypoint 615.04, -23.96, 222.36}}.
1. Return to Thadus Flintwrit.

## Rewards

- At least 16s
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Fighter's Necklace]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Necklace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Necklace]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Necklace]] (All Mages)
