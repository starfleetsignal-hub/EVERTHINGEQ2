The research into the Elemental Planes continues for the Great Library of Myrist. This time, I've been tasked with studying the elements of soil, so I am off to Vegarlson: Council's Stronghold.

## Steps

- Defeat targets within [[Vegarlson: Council's Stronghold (Solo)]]
1. Destroy The Great Gravelly One
1. Annihilate Aggregahn
1. Serve Escargore
1. Kill Lesha Needleleaf
1. Defeat the Council of Four
