## Description

The Quel'ule scholar Azira has requested assistance in delivering the latest reports from Quel'ule to Paineel.

## Steps

1. I must collect the report from Quel'ule to deliver to Paineel.
   - Click on the group of scrolls called *Status Report for Paineel* on the work desk right next to Azira. ![Status Report for Paineel](images/Status_Report_for_Paineel.jpg)
1. Deliver Azira's report to [[Aaliya]] {{waypoint 1993, -299, 3357}} in the [[Hall of Wizardry]] in Paineel.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,500 faction with **Researchers of Quel'ule**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Quel'ule Daily Supply Tasks
