## Prerequisite

This is part of the [[Sarnak]] racial quest line

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Trannin Steelsea]] {{waypoint -67, -37, 16}}
1. Head for [[The Freeport Militia House]] in West Freeport. When you get behind [[Overlord's Plaza]] {{waypoint 36, -19, 30}} you will be jumped by 6 [[Seafury Street Tough|Seafury Street Toughs]] (same level, mixed double- and triple down, grouped). Kill them
1. Collect [[A Hastily Written Note (with desc)|A Hastily Written Note]] from the chest
1. Speak to [[A mysterious sarnak]] {{waypoint 38, -20, 3}}
1. Speak to Trannin Steelsea
   - If you choose the survival/honor response and kill him (even con), it is bad faction with [[The Seafury Buccaneers (Faction)|The Seafury Buccaneers]] and good faction with [[The Blackshield Smugglers (Faction)|The Blackshield Smugglers]]
   - If you choose the Freeport laws,
1. Speak to the Ambassador in the Chancery

## Rewards

- At least 19g 93s 2c @ level 79
