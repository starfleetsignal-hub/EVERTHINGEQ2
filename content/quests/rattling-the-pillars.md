## Description

The scholars studying the Eternal Prism have reported that they have identified a few strong pillars of magical power within the area known as the Scion of Ice. They have theorized that these concentrations might be manifestations of some kind, and that it may also be possible to diminish the grip of the dark magic that holds the Eternal Prism by destroying these manifestations. The scholars have selected one for me to seek out and destroy, to test their theory.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Miragul's Phylactery: Scion of Ice]] and perform one of the following, based on the quest text you receive:
   - "Search for the guardians of magical knowledge, and destroy a pair of them": Kill [[Ankhef]] and [[Zaza]].
   - "Destroy one of the more powerful manifestations of power": Kill [[Arcanus]].
   - "I should look for the manifestation of the spirit of young Miragul himself": Kill [[Shade of Miragul]].
   - "I should search for the manifestation of pure magical manipulation.": Kill [[Arcanus]].
   - "Look for a manifestation that resonates with chaotic power": Kill [[Chaotic Manipulation]].
1. Return Ragheejb Yasir in [[Moors of Ykesha]].
