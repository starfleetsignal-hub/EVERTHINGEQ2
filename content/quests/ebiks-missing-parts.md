## Description

"Ebik Wobblecog is waiting for a ship to Qeynos. He just realized that he's missing some parts that his father asked him to collect. Ebik needs a size 7 clunker, a copper-coated springer, and a triangle spinner. I should look around the island for these devices. They could be anywhere!" -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Find a Copper Coated Springer {{waypoint -144.58, -14.70, 166.27}} underwater south southeast of Ebik.
1. Find a Triangle Spinner {{waypoint -79, 5, -154}} behind the treehouse at [[The Last Stand]].
1. Find a Size 7 Clunker {{waypoint 7, 2, -117}} underneath the bridge above [[Razortooth Falls]].
1. Return to Ebik for your reward {{waypoint -93, -8, 64}}.
