## Steps

The following steps are all done in [[The City of Mist]] {{waypoint -166.27, 134.40, -869.81}} by killing [[An iron construct|iron constructs]] and [[A jade construct|jade constructs]]:

1. Retrieve a pristine iron golem head
1. Retrieve two pristine jade golem hands
1. Retrieve two pristine iron golem pauldrons
1. Retrieve two pristine jade golem arms
1. Retrieve two pristine iron golem legs
1. Retrieve a pair of pristine jade golem boots
1. Return to Ebenezer Sprockbok.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 5g
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Rilissian Girdle of Blossoms]] - Priests
  - [[Rilissian Girdle of Fangs]] - Fighters
  - [[Rilissian Girdle of Poison Vines]] - Scouts
  - [[Rilissian Girdle of Scales]] - Mages
