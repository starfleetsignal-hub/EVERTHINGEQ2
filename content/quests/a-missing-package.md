## Steps

![Poor guy...](images/Dwarven_backpack.jpg)

1. Obtain the [[Dwarven backpack]] {{waypoint 58, 196, -324}}
   - ***Warning!*** Once the backpack is obtained, a soloable group of 4 Krukiel bugbears (2 [[A Krulkiel gatherer|Krulkiel gatherer]]s, 1 [[A Krulkiel scrapper|Krulkiel scrapper]] and 1 [[A Krulkiel berserker|Krulkiel berserker]]) will spawn and attack, but you can sprint and outrun them.
   - *Trivia:* If you pick up the backpack and then delete the quest, it remains in your backpack until you destroy it or complete the quest.  If you delete the quest from your journal and then reaccept it, you will immediately automatically complete the 'obtain the backpack' step of the quest.
1. Speak with [[Opus Froth]] at the [[Butcherblock Dock]] {{waypoint 695, 24, 603}}
1. Return to [[Verung Kae'Rush]]

## Rewards

- At least 1g 29s 17c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Irontoe Chaplain's Wristlet]] (Priests)
  - [[Irontoe Geomancer's Wristlet]] (Mages)
  - [[Irontoe Sentry's Wristlet]] (Fighters)
  - [[Irontoe Sapper's Wristlet]] (Scouts)
- +1000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
