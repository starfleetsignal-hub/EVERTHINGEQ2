## Steps

1. Fulfil the frogloks' work order as requested
   1. Click on the recipe book next to Reedip and scribe the ***Tupta Traditional Artisanry*** which gets placed in your inventory.
   1. Collect the items:
      - [[Fresh clay]] can be found along the narrow ledge at the end of the cannal northeast of Tupta.  (5 for the whole quest) {{waypoint -160.47, -91.79, 593.32}}
      - [[Ykeshan shellfish]] can be found along the shores of a canal northeast of Tupta.  (15 for the whole quest) {{waypoint -150, -95, 588}}
      - [[Tuptan fruit]] in open baskets about the settlement. (10 for the whole quest)
      - it also requires 5 [[Smoldering coal|smoldering coals]] for fuel (5 for the whole quest)
   1. Make 5 ***traditional froglok pottery*** using a forge
1. Place the items in the basket next to Reedip

## Rewards

- At least 47g 11s 70c
- +2000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
- -500 faction with **Clan Grobb**
