## Notes

- This is a subquest of [[The Stormhammer (Quest)|The Stormhammer]].

## Steps

1. Gather keys from the following named mobs in [[Kaladim]]:
   - [[Iron Lord Kraxxaxis]] or his placeholder, [[An Iron Lord]], on top of a tower in [[Hall of the Hammer]] {{waypoint -16, 43, -282}}
     - In order to reach him, climb the wall at {{waypoint -18, 26, -255}}
   - [[Chiselmaster Unekee]], who is part of the ring event in Hall of the Hammer, or [[A Grungetalon foreman]] {{waypoint 33, 26, -241}}
   - [[Picklord Ryjeka]], or [[A Grungetalon picklord]], found in [[Everhot Mine]] {{waypoint 235, -47, -552}}
   - [[Reverend Rutzkill]], who is part of the kobold ring event at the cliffs by the river, or [[A Deadwatch exorcist]] {{waypoint -153, 45, -236}}
   - [[Queen Varronik]] {{waypoint -214, 48, -85}}
1. Go to [[Underfoot Cathedral]], and click on the tomb behind the throne of [[King Caertex]]. {{waypoint 25, 30, -543}}
1. Kill [[The ghost of Blyle Bundin]].
1. Return to [[The Haunted Vaults]] and speak with [[General Hognuk Ventille]].

## Rewards

- Progress in [[The Stormhammer (Quest)|The Stormhammer]]
