## Steps

1. Go to [[Moors of Ykesha]] [[Dropship Landing Zone]] and talk to [[Gabblin Bittlegrip]] {{waypoint 1660, 450, 877}}, who gives you [[An uncharged power source]] (in your bags).
1. Take the balloon to Brokenskull Rock drop zone to reach the [[Obelisk of Ahkzul]] (or Anchor or Palace of Ferzhul) and use [[An uncharged power source]] near the zone in {{waypoint -1765.32, -968.00, 2643.05}} (One way to do this is to fly in through the hole in the cave ceiling and land under the funnel of void going up into the sky. {{waypoint -1753.90, -966.70, 2677.47}} This is the zone point for The Palace of Ferzhul. Use the uncharged power source to charge it.)
1. Return to Gabblin
1. Click the portal Gabblin creates to zone your entire group into [[Rilgor Plegnog's Chambers]]
1. Hail [[Rilgor Plegnog]] {{waypoint 16, 0, 2}}. At the end of the dialog he turns into a 82 ^^^ heroic aggro
1. Kill Rilgor.
   - You may return to Moors via a portal at the back of the room {{waypoint -24, 1, 4}}
   - NOTE: Killing Rilgor will cause everyone to get a lockout timer of 2 days, 20 hours. However, only the first person to go through the dialogue with Rilgor will get the update. It is recommended that each member who needs the update disbands and runs either solo or with others who do not need the update.
1. Enter [[A Clockwork Apocalypse]] in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint -978, 11, 714}} and speak to [[A time-locked enchantress]] {{waypoint 824, -20, 278}}
1. Destroy the portals, in order:
   1. Exit to [[Antonica]], then destroy the Antonica gear portal by right or double clicking on it.
   1. Destroy the [[Moors of Ykesha]] gear portal near Gabblin by right or double clicking on it.
1. Head to [[Lavastorm]] and use the note you got from the enchantress when standing next to any lava pool, such as the pool at {{waypoint -259.96, -127.94, 381.17}}
1. In your mailbox, open "Norrathian Express Delivery Service - A Sealed Package" and choose "Receive Gifts"
