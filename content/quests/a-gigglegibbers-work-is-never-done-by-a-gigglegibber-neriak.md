## Notes

- This quest scales to the adventure level tier of the character, i.e. level 21-30 characters will receive the level 25 version of the quest.
- This quest is repeatable up to 10 times. per adventure level tier.
- You may need to kill more than the listed number of monsters in order to retrieve the required drops for this quest.
- Players adventure level 121+ will need to mentor down to a lower level range in order to get these quests offered.

## Steps

1. Slay the following monsters (for your adventure level tier) until you retrieve 5 of the indicated quest item:
   - **Tier 1 (Level 1-10)**
     - [[An untamed spirit|untamed spirits]] in [[Darklight Wood]]
     - [[A fallen ranger|fallen rangers]] in [[Darklight Wood]]
   - **Tier 2 (Level 11-20)**
     - [[A rock lioness|rock lionesses]] in [[The Commonlands]]
     - [[A moon wisp|moon wisps]] in [[The Commonlands]]
   - **Tier 3 (Level 21-30)**
     - [[A mischievous mist grinnin|mischievous mist grinnins]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] ( -175, 3, -696 )
     - [[A dusk leaper|dusk leapers]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] ( -616, 31, 15 )
   - **Tier 4 (Level 31-40)**
     - [[A Vallon miner|Vallon miners]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]]
     - Kill 5 [[A Blackhook raider|Blackhook Raiders]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] {{waypoint 115, -42, 299}}
   - **Tier 5 (Level 41-50)**
     - [[A molten fragment|molten fragment]]s in [[Lavastorm]]
     - [[Sootfoot harvester|Sootfoot Harvesters]] in [[Lavastorm]]
   - **Tier 6 (Level 51-60)**
     - [[The Lesser Faydark]] (solo)
       - [[A grovespirit acolyte|Grovespirit acolytes]], [[A grovespirit caller|callers]] and [[A grovespirit defender|defenders]]
       - [[A honeybrood worker|honeybrood workers]]
     - [[The Sinking Sands]] (heroic)
       - [[An Anaz Mal veilguard|Anaz Mal veilguards]] in [[The Living Tombs]]
       - Mummies, such as [[A Sulite tjat (Silent City)|Sulite tjat]] in [[The Silent City]]
   - **Tier 7 (Level 61-70)**
     - [[The Barren Sky]] (solo)
       - [[A ridgestone defender|Ridgestone defenders]], [[A ridgestone guardian|guardians]], [[A ridgestone sentinel|sentinels]], and [[A ridgestone trampler|tramplers]]
       - [[A dryeye vehemence|Dryeye vehemences]] or [[A dryeye entangler]]
     - [[Faydwer (Continent)|Faydwer]] (heroic)
       - [[A Kragbak battler|Kragbak kobolds]] in [[Kaladim]]
       - [[An obsidian construct|Obsidian constructs]] in [[Mistmoore Catacombs]]
   - **Tier 8 (Level 71-80)**
     - [[A trained war sokokar|trained war sokokar]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]], {{waypoint 594, -89, 487}}
     - [[A kunzar tiger|Kunzar Tigers]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
   - **Tier 9 (Level 81-90)**
     - [[A bristlevine horror|Bristlevine Horrors]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]
     - [[A Stonebrunt stalagmaw|Stonebrunt Stalagmaws]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]]
   - **Tier 10 (Level 91-100)**
     - [[A feargaze creeper|Feargaze creepers]] in [[Eidolon Jungle]], {{waypoint -1522, 223, 444}}
     - [[An ethershade|Ethershades]] in [[Obol Plains]] {{waypoint -408, 27, 409}}
   - **Tier 11 (Level 100-110)**
     - [[Aranid bluffstocker|Aranid bluffstockers]] in [[Plane of Magic]]
     - [[A blazing ignus wisp|Blazing ignus wisps]] in [[Plane of Magic]]
1. Return to [[Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber (Neriak)|Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]].
