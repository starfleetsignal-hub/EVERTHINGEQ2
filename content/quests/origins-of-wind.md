## Notes

- May be repeated hourly.

## Steps

1. Catalogue a number of inhabitants of [[Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wind]]:
   - Catalogue [[An air torrent]] at the [[Misty Falls]] around {{waypoint 146.17, -226.64, -1163.86}}
   - Catalogue [[A servitor of air]] all over [[The Windglades]] and the [[Upper Glade]]
   - Catalogue [[A nilborien windraker]] all over [[The Windglades]] and the [[Upper Glade]]
   - Catalogue [[A bixel hiveguard]] in [[The Bixel Hive]] around the entrance and throughout the hive
   - Catalogue [[A bixel hiveminder]] at [[The Bixel Hive]] in the [[Lower Larvae Cells]] around {{waypoint -566.33, 502.47, -280.06}} and elsewhere in the hive
   - Catalogue [[A bixel honeytrader]] at [[The Bixel Hive]] in [[The Honey Larder]] around {{waypoint -671.57, 403.10, -168.05}}
   - Catalogue [[An electric stormrider]] at the [[Precipice of Wind]] around the tops of the arches in the center
   - Catalogue [[A greater efreeti windlord]] at the [[Precipice of Wind]] around {{waypoint -314.66, -490.71, 777.99}}
   - Catalogue [[A lesser efreeti windlord]] at the [[Precipice of Wind]] around {{waypoint -314.66, -490.71, 777.99}}
   - Catalogue [[A phoenix stratosurger]] at the [[Precipice of Wind]] around the edges of the island
   - Catalogue [[A prismatic widow]] in the [[The Aerie]] in the shrubbery all over the island
   - Catalogue [[A zephyren parrot]] in the [[The Aerie]] around {{waypoint 1360.26, 326.50, -94.80}} and other branches around the island
   - Catalogue [[A zephyren skycleaver]] in the [[The Aerie]] all over the island
   - Catalogue [[A zephyren winddancer]] in the [[The Aerie]] all over the island
1. Defeat a [[Wilder of Air]] (You do NOT have to catalogue it) - Possible locations:
   - {{waypoint -725.00, 513.05, -266.89}} at the edge of [[The Bixel Hive]]
   - {{waypoint -324.41, -325.06, 1015.45}} up the trunk of a huge tree at the [[Precipice of Wind]]
   - {{waypoint -94.32, -112.77, -1203.50}} or {{waypoint 258, 72, -1444}} in [[The Windglades]]
