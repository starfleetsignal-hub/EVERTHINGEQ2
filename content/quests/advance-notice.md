## Steps

1. Search the Rime side of the wall in Storm Gorge for the camp positions
   - Find the first camp near the South Wall {{waypoint 451, -378, 1278}}
   - Find the second camp closer to the ravine {{waypoint 327, -351, 1400}}
   - Find the third camp closer to the Hold of Rime {{waypoint 318, -318, 1176}}
1. Return to General Icemaul {{waypoint 723, -376, 861}}
