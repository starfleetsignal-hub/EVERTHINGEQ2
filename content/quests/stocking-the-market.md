## Prerequisites

You must be a level 60+ Scout to be offered this quest.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Beggar's Court: Sweat-Stained Cobbles]] {{waypoint -149, -50, -375}} and collect 6 sparkling crates in the lower area
1. Speak to Captain Siren in Freeport
1. Kill 3 [[A distracted Militia guard|distracted Militia guards]] (level 60, even con) throughout Freeport
1. Speak to [[A Seafury trader]] behind [[The Freeport Militia House]] {{waypoint 162, -16, 152}}
1. In [[Stonestair Byway: The Animus Corridors]] speak to [[Coalition Agent]] {{waypoint -50.30, -4.36, -20.05}}
1. Speak to Captain Siren in Freeport

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Seafury Buccaneers**
- Cloak based on level:
  - [[Freeport Cloak of Stealth]] (Level 60)
  - [[Freeport Seafury's Drape]] (Level 70)
  - [[Seafury Cloak of Greater Stealth]] (Level 80)
  - [[Lucanic Cloak of Swiftness]] (Level 90)
