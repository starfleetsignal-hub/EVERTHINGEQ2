## Steps

1. Harvest the following:
   - 18 [[Dewpalm lumber]] from [[Fallen dewpalm branch]]
   - 15 [[Strangler root]] from [[Strangler root break]]
   - 18 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Coalesced reef]]
   - 10 [[Basilisk meat]] from [[Basilisk lair]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Box of Grease Rags]].
1. Craft the [[Box of Grease Rags]] on a [[Work Bench]].
1. Speak to [[Jurie Gearshaper]] at {{waypoint 7.70, -14.04, -22.59}}.
