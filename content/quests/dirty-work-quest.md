## Steps

1. Purchase Fate's Whisper from Loamshallow {{waypoint 12, 3, 0}} for 1c.
1. Go to the North Freeport gate
1. Eeavesdrop on a conversation by hiding behind the western side of the gates
   - At {{waypoint 82, -7, 112}} you'll see a speech balloon.
   - Prompt the conversation to learn the location of the meeting.
1. Go poison the cups at the meeting place {{waypoint -17, -2, 83}} with the Fate's Whisper. Right click the two cups to poison them.
1. Return to Delcairn.
