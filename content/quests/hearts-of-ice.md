## Steps

1. You must go to [[Everfrost]] and kill the following
   - 3 [[An arctic behemoth|arctic behemoths]] (43^)
   - 2 [[A glacial construct|glacial construct]] (46^^^)
   - 2 [[A frozen tempest|frozen tempests]] (48-49^)
   - 2 [[The Kromise (Faction)|Kromise giants]] (any Kromise in zone, 46+)
1. Return to [[General Fandrak]] in Feerott.

## Rewards

- At least 3s 7c
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Earstud of the Enchanted]]
  - [[Hoop of the Enchanted]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Spike of the Enchanted]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Stud of the Enchanted]]
