## Steps

1. obtain the following
   - Obtain 8 blightmetal stalker claws {{waypoint -219.05, 13.61, -524.18}}
   - Procure 6 malwing ravager tongues {{waypoint -777.78, 68.12, -221.82}}
   - Acquire 8 copper maedjinn hearts {{waypoint -764.13, 54.28, -400.55}}
1. Return to [[Embri]]
