## Description

"Glienna was seeking out a special fruit, called eros berries, that grows in the grove of Sapphire Pond. She wishes to share them with Shimmer in hopes for a romantic evening. Unfortunately, she was attacked by decaying guardians before she could find any. She requests that you go in search of the berries for her." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. [[Glienna Lillypad]] wants you to collect 6 Eros Berries from the bushes near the [[Sapphire Pond]] around {{waypoint -891, 68, 482}}. They look like normal bushes, but with red mist rising from them.
1. Return to [[Glienna Lillypad]]

## Rewards

- At least 5s 81c
