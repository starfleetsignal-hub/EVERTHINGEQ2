## Prerequisites

- You must have at least -6,900 faction with Synod Reet.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Sebilis]] and hide secret provisions within 6 'Sathirian Crates' found in the rooms near the entrance:
   - Two in the entry foyer, one on each side on the upper platform.
   - One in the hallway leading out of the entry foyer.
   - One in the hallway leading to the Exit foyer.
   - Two in the exit foyer, one on each side.
1. Return to [[Special Agent Froak]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- +1,000 faction with **Synod Reet**
