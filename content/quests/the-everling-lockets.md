## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- This is a semi-access quest to unlock the different levels of the dungeon. At least one person in the group will need to have completed, or be actively completing, this quest in order for the group to access the second floor and basement. Furthermore, this quest is necessary in order to complete most of the quests in the dungeon since many of the examined-quests are found on the second level and basement.
- If this quest has been completed once then all levels of the dungeon will be unlocked; there is no need to complete this quest a second time to access the second floor or dungeon.

## Prerequisites

- Completion of the quests:
  - [[Visions of Hatred]] - access to the upstairs balcony
  - [[The Boar's Head]] - access to the barracks
  - [[The Red Marble]] - access to the library
- Complete [[Alexa (NPC)|Alexa's]] orders before being offered the quest:
  1. Speak with Alexa in [[The Chapel (Nektropos Castle)|The Chapel]] at {{waypoint -24, -1, 53}}, after slaying the [[Inquisitor of Ulkorruuk]].
  1. Alexa will send you to the [[Nektropos Castle Game Room|Game Room]] at {{waypoint 26, 0, 51}} to find a note left by the castle's architect. Start examining all possible objects in the room until you find the correct one with [[A hastily scrawled note]] stashed in it.
  1. Examine the note - not a right-click, actually read the note itself. It will instruct you to interact with the wall-sconce at {{waypoint 21.71, -0.07, 67.27}}, opening a secret passage.
  1. Take the secret passage upstairs to [[Elise's Bedroom]]. Click the book on the nightstand at {{waypoint 46, 13, 85}}. This will make Elise spawn on the middle walkway of the ramparts, important for later on.
  1. Travel up the stairs from Elise's Bedroom and exit from either doorway onto the ramparts. Alexa will re-appear, depending on which exit you took, at either {{waypoint 61, 31, 45}} or {{waypoint 8, 30, 82}}. Speak with her to receive the quest.

## Steps

1. Travel around the ramparts and slay all six of the Everling sisters to obtain their lockets. Going clockwise:
   - Enter the [[Sheila's Bedroom|west tower]] and go down the stairs, click on dresser to spawn and kill [[Sheila Everling]]. {{waypoint 70, 13, 0}}
     - After Sheila, a door may open at {{waypoint 60, 13.50, 0.43}} allowing access to the second floor and gather quests from places like [[The Library (Nektropos Castle)|The Library]].
   - Go back up and continue around to the [[Crysta's Bedroom|northwest tower]], down the stairs, and kill [[Crysta's Handmaiden]] at {{waypoint 45, 14, -73}}. Go near the bed to spawn [[A doll of Nyth|dolls of Nyth]]. After killing them, click on the bed again to read her journal, which tells you to go to [[Melanie Everling (Nektropos Castle)|Melanie Everling]]'s bedroom.
     - Note:  click the bed from far enough away that the Dolls of Nyth don't spawn.  You may need them later for other quests.
   - Go back up and continue around to the [[Melanie's Bedroom|northeast tower]] and down the stairs. Kill [[Melanie Everling (Nektropos Castle)|Melanie Everling]] and [[Crysta Everling]]. {{waypoint -51, 13, -84}}.
     - If you haven't already, read *Elise's Diary* in the [[Elise's Bedroom|southwest tower]] to spawn Elise on the roof.
   - Go back upstairs and go to the middle walkway.  Kill [[Elise Everling]] at {{waypoint -2, 30, -25}}.
   - Backtrack and continue clockwise around to the [[Jenni's Bedroom|east tower]]. Go down the stairs to the bedroom and kill [[Jenni Everling]] at {{waypoint -70, 13, -5}}.
   - Head back upstairs to the [[Deirdre's Bedroom|southeast tower]], at {{waypoint -54, 23, 87}}.
   - WARNING:  As soon as the first player of the group enters [[Deirdre's Bedroom]], [[Archfiend Izzoroth]] will spawn with a short delay before aggro, giving you time to hustle down the stairs into the basement where [[Deirdre Everling]] will spawn.  Archfiend Izzoroth can be killed by a small group or soloed by a self-mentored player. He is static.
   - Quickly descend through Deirdre's Bedroom into the basement below and kill [[Deirdre Everling]] at {{waypoint -46, 0, 80}}. After Deirdre is killed, interacting with the wall-sconce at the end of the nearby hallway will open a secret door similar to the one used back in [[Sheila's Bedroom|Sheila's basement]]. This door will take you back to the first level of the dungeon.
1. The journal should update to "***I need to find a way into the basement***" in order to progress.  Enter [[Maltus' Bedroom|Caretaker Room/Lord Everling's bedroom]] at {{waypoint -20, 13, -45}} and kill the [[An Everling caretaker|Everling caretakers]].  Then go to {{waypoint -17, 14.00, -51.00}} and kill the [[A decaying corpse|decaying corpse]].
1. The journal should now read: ***I need to figure out what this key goes to***.  Enter the [[Quarters of Lord Everling]] at {{waypoint 18, 14, -52}} and examine the book on the pedestal.  This will spawn [[Guard Captain Ga'vin]] - kill him. Then, ***right-click*** the stand to the right of the bookcase and select 'use' to open the secret door to the basement (left-click only inspects).
1. Proceed down the steps and click the dead-end wall to open the secret door.  Speak with [[Alexa (NPC)|Alexa]] at {{waypoint -39, -13, 42}}.
1. Backtrack to a wall sconce at {{waypoint -5, -14, -23}} and examine it. This will unlock a secret room to your immediate south. Enter and examine with the scroll on the desk at {{waypoint -12, -14, -12}}, which enables you to interact with objects found in the basement.
1. Exit the secret room by clicking on the candle stand in the corner of the room (left of the desk), and then use a wall-stone at {{waypoint -21, -13, -22}}. Go through and confront [[Alexa (Named Monster)|Alexa]] at {{waypoint -19, -13, 8}}. Upon her death, the quest will complete.  Then Ollix will appear and give a short speech before attacking.

## Rewards

After finishing this quest, on all future visits to Nektropos Castle, you will be able to:

- Enter the second floor from any tower
- Open the Quarters of Lord Everling
- Open the passage to the basement
- Open all secret doors in the basement
