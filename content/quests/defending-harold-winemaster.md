## Prerequisites

- Completion of [[The Case of the Poisoned Wine]] isn't necessary to pick up this quest, however the character must have it in their quest journal.

## Steps

1. Visit [[Pride Lake]] in [[The Commonlands]]. {{waypoint 634, -46, -446}}
1. Return to interview Harold Winemaster.
1. Visit [[Black Water Lake]] in Nektulos Forest. {{waypoint -462, -3, -993}}
1. Return to re-interview Harold Winemaster.
1. Visit [[Behemoth Pond]] in Nektulos Forest and find the discarded poison bottle at {{waypoint -195, 3, -258}}. You must right click it and choose to pick it up, left clicking does nothing.
1. Return to [[Harold Winemaster]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Coin
- AA Experience
