## Steps

1. Go to [[Hidden Valley|the entrance of Cauldron Hollow]] {{waypoint 886, 6, -465}}. Select [[Cauldron Hollow: The Witches' Cove]] and enter. The quest will automatically complete.
   - **Warning**: there are heroic mobs that will rush you upon entering. If you die, you will be unable to re-enter the instance.

## Rewards

- One-time access to the special instance, [[Cauldron Hollow: The Witches' Cove]].
