## Steps

1. Collect beans from a basket at {{waypoint 92, 154, 3}} (Inside [[Pilgrims' Hearth]])
1. Kill [[Bentor the Mighty]] at {{waypoint -95, 153, -132}} (Located in a pen near [[The Stone Hammer]])
1. Return to [[Barker Gordon]] at {{waypoint -345, 31, 205}} (He is on the [[Erollis Dock]])
1. Pick up a plate of ham and beans (Right behind him)

## Rewards

- 4 Ham and Beans
- At least 12s 2c
  - [[Forged Axe]] (Warriors)
  - [[Forged Blade]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Forged Cestus]] (Brawlers, Beastlord)
  - [[Forged Tower Shield]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Empowered Holy Symbol]] (All Priests)
  - [[Empowered Totem]] (All Mages)
