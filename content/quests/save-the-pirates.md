## Notes

- When you enter the zone, you will appear on a dock with the Smarmy Sprocket ship behind you and [[Heron Cogcarrier (The Prison of Admiral Grimgadget)|Heron Cogspinner]] with [[Sparrow Cogcarrier (The Prison of Admiral Grimgadget)|Sparrow Cogcarrier]] directly in front of you. If you don't move from this spot when you zone in, the pirates on the dock should not attack you. However, be aware that the pirates are flagged to see you through invisibility / sneak, even if the red highlight denoting aggro disappears. Therefore, you must fight a ways through before you will be able to begin the quest.
- Although the quest in your journal may scale numerically, the encounters do not. So it is best to chronomentor down to 30 if you have outleveled the zone so you can get experience and AA from the named.

## Starting the Quest

1. Fight your way through the pirates on the dock to the prison at the center of the island.
1. Right-click on the prison door and select 'Use'. Heron and Sparrow Cogcarrier will run up and blow open the door.
1. Fight your way down to the first cell where [[Admiral Grimgadget]] and [[Cap'n Cog]] are imprisoned at {{waypoint -1, -25, -26}}. Hail Cap'n Cog to receive the quest.

## Steps

1. Take the doorway to the left of the fireplace (north) into a deeper part of the prison. {{waypoint -2, -32, -73}} ![Dead Gnome](images/Ol'_Greasy.jpg)
1. Right-click [[Ol'Greasy]] at the end of the hallway of prison cells and take the keys you find. Be prepared to kill the jailed pirates immediately once you take the keys.
1. Return to Cap'n Cog's cell and speak with Admiral Grimgadget.
1. Take the doorway to the right of the fireplace (east) and kill [[Captain Jihannum]] in the first cell on your right in the hallway. Be prepared to kill the jailed pirates in the other cells once you start attacking the captain. You will have to click the first door on the right to be able to kill Captain Jihannum.
   - Hailing [[Jergo Wheybringer]] in the cell across from Captain Jihannum will allow you to get the quest starter book [[Bootstrutter's Trail Guide to the Desert of Ro (Quest Starter)|Bootstrutter's Trail Guide to the Desert of Ro]] while you're there.
1. Go back to the prison cell and hail Admiral Grimgadget again. Cap'n Cog will run to the ship. Follow him to the ship and **wait for the Admiral to arrive** (do not click the ladder yet - it will zone you out and you'll have to start over).
1. Once Admiral Grimgadget arrives, hail him again to complete the quest.
1. Hail Cap'n Cog and agree with him.
1. Click the small ladder on the side of the ship to zone out.
