The most riotous of goblin leaders are gathering within Runnyeye, and must be dispatched before they ruin our lands.

## Steps

*I must complete my mission in [[Fabled Runnyeye: The Gathering (Heroic)]].*

- Become the undisputed eye poking champion
- Down da Halfies Master
- Kapow the Broken Toe Captain
- Stop the Mighty Smith
- Slay the High Shaman
