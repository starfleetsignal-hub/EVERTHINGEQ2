## Steps

1. Kill and find the following
   - Kill icebone sentinels for 6 unremitting embers {{waypoint 483, 33, 148}}
     - *they drop 2 unremitting ember on the ground after you kill em*
   - Search sulfide deposits ( similar to ore nodes) for 8 zinc dust on the northern beaches beginning at {{waypoint 361, 18, 136}} or at {{waypoint 365, 13, 119}}  {{waypoint 556, 8, 238}}
   - Hunt 8 frosthide maulers for blubber oil at {{waypoint -180.37, 7.94, 48.36}}  These are an icebound minion {{waypoint -99, 6, -130}}
1. Return to Gwinres

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- [[Frosthide Mauler Plushie]]
