*Must be a Half Elf to receive this quest.*

## Steps

![](images/Campsite_remains.jpg)

1. Search campsite remains for clues as to who is setting them up. You may need to search several campsites before you find the information you need. You are looking for [[A page with scribbled gnollish]].
   - Possible locations for the campsite remains:
     - {{waypoint 999, -18, -735}}
1. Return the note you find to Elvic Garrett.
1. Speak with [[Acting Lieutenant Germain]] for your reward. {{waypoint 900, -15, -577}}

## Rewards

- At least 92c
