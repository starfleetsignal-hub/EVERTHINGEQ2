This is part 4 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Freeport]] who wear Leather and Chain Armor ([[Shaman|shaman]]).

## Steps

1. You must collect items from creatures in [[Edgewater Drains]].
   - Kill 12 [[Sewage viper|sewage vipers]] for their brains.
   - Kill 10 [[A drudge mucus|drudge mucus]] for their slime.
   - Kill 12 [[A contaminated rat|contaminated rats]] for their poisoned skin.
1. Return to Vassi.

- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Mantle of the Dreadnaught]] - ([[Bruiser]])
  - [[Runic Ceremonial Mantle]] - ([[Defiler]])
  - [[Shoulderpads of Nature's Fatebinder]] - ([[Fury]])
  - [[Mantle of the Silent Fist Initiate]] - ([[Monk]])
  - [[Blessed Ivy Engraved Shoulderpads]] - ([[Warden]])
