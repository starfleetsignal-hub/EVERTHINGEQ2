## Steps

1. Use the ***Ul'Alarum Oracle Stone*** (obtained during the previous quest) at one of the 3 following deadends of the moat outside [[Crushbone Keep]]:
   - {{waypoint -1067.23, 105.16, 235.69}}
   - {{waypoint -1188, 105, 5}}
   - {{waypoint -1351, 105, 38}}
1. Kill [[The Malgurt (Named)|the Malgurt]] when it is summoned. It can take a while before [[The Malgurt (Named)|the Malgurt]] arrives
1. Return to [[Blinik Belestro]]

## Rewards

- At least 17s 43c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Forged Axe]] (Warriors)
  - [[Forged Blade]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Forged Cestus]] (Brawlers, Beastlord)
  - [[Forged Tower Shield]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Empowered Holy Symbol]] (All Priests)
  - [[Empowered Totem]] (All Mages)
