## Starting the Quest

You should be actively working on the quest [[Repairing the Eternal Prism]].

## Steps

1. Observe the motives of [[Lucretia Nasin|Lucretia Nasin's]] organization
   - You should finish the quest [[Making Sense of Miragul, Part 1]]. Finishing that quest updates this quest automatically.
1. Return to [[Chiara Oscura]].

## Rewards

- At least 8g 61s 8c
