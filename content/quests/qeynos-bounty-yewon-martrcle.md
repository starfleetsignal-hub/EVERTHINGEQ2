## Steps

1. Travel to [[East Freeport]] via [[The Thieves' Way]] and kill [[Yewon Martrcle]] who will spawn inside the Gorowyn Chancery at {{waypoint -108, -41, -45}}
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- +3000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
