## Steps

1. Talk to Occultist L'Takk in the Temple of War in North Freeport {{waypoint -86, -10, -165}} to receive this City Task.
   - Slay the 11 [[Tormented wraith|tormented wraiths]] in the [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]] - found in the Reservoir of the Wraith {{waypoint 101, 37, -158}} and the adjacent hallways
1. Return to [[Occultist L'Takk]].

## Rewards

- 9221 status points
- +150 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
