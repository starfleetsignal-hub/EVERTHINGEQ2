## Notes

You need to be able to speak Cae'Dal by completing [[Examples of Cae'Dal Script]] to speak with [[Celsyn Beileth]]

## Steps

*Iksars have been giving trouble to the elves that venture into the woods. Deal with them.*

1. Kill 12 Iksar Abductors. Found throughout [[Warslik's Wood]] and [[Slaver's Point]].
1. Return to [[Celsyn Beileth]] {{waypoint -20, 18, -214}}.

## Rewards

- [[Cae'Dal Sage Bookcase]]
- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 15108 status
- +1400 faction with **Nye'Caelona**
