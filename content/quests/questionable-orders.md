## Steps

1. [[Harruk Ti'Gok (NPC)|Harruk Ti'Gok]] gives you orders to begin the final dismantling of [[Haoaera]], despite previous orders from Commander Vor'Ork to the contrary.  When you refuse to carry out the orders he will attack you.
1. Kill [[Harruk Ti'Gok (Monster)|Harruk Ti'Gok]].
1. Speak to [[Primary Tlecche Myli'Din]].

## Rewards

- At least 17s 43c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Archon's Greaves]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Pants]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Leggings]] (Druids)
  - [[Elder's Leggings]] (Shamans)
  - [[Enlightened Leggings]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Greaves]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Shade's Leggings]] (All Scouts)
