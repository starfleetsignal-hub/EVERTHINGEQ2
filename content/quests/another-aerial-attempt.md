## Prerequisites

- You must speak [[Volant]] by completing [[Words of a Feather]] in order to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Hoo'Loh]] on [[Strifewind Isle]] {{waypoint 62, -20, -420}}.
   - Note: If Hoo'Loh is not in his familiar location on the chain, check below it to the west on the ground (was on the chain 10/4/2020).
1. Return to [[Frigui Togginscog|Frigui]] on the [[Isle of Desolation]] {{waypoint 506, 301, -558}}.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 74s 85c
- [[Book Binding]], part of the [[Drednever Journal Pages]] collection
