## Steps

1. Enter [[Karnor's Castle]] and kill the following:
   - 20 drolvargs
   - [[An Undead Jailer]] {{waypoint -109, -71, 82}}
1. Return to [[Xvenloo Ghox]] outside Karnor's Castle.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 6s 7c
- +2000 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Chardok Defender's Neckguard]]
  - [[Karnor Invader's Choker]]
  - [[Omen Rejector's Medallion]]
  - [[Pendant of the Far Reach]]
