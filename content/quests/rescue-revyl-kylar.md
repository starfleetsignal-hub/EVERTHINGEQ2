*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Find and rescue [[Revyl Kylar]] at {{waypoint -490, 0, -183}}
   - You need to kill six level 20▼▼▼ gnolls to free him.
     - The revive point is fairly close so if you're having difficulty you can kill a few, die, run back, then kill the rest.
1. Escort Revyl Kylar back to his father.
   - Lord Kylar will recognize that his son is safe when his son is physically in front of him. You may have to move around a bit to get Revyl close enough to his father. Once Revyl gets close enough he should approach and face his father. Just running to the keep alone won't update the quest.
1. Talk to Lord Kylar.

## Rewards

- At least 16s
