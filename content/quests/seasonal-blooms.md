## Steps

1. Collect 8 *assorted seeds* from [[A hawk nest|hawk nests]].
   - One log ramp to the nests near the entrance at {{waypoint 525, 99, -808}} and one on the other side of the river at {{waypoint 752, 110, -513}}.
1. Examine the seeds in your inventory and plant 8 of them **anywhere**.
   - Placing the seeds on your hotbar speeds this process.
1. Return back to the Pixie Elder.
