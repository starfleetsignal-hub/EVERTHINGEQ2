## Steps

1. Go to [[Stonestair Byway: The Animus Corridors]] in North Freeport
1. Help your erudite kin:
   - Kill 8 kerrans. This can be kerran battlepriests, medics or citizens.
   - Destroy 6 kerran spirit totems. Target and attack them to destroy them.
1. Return to [[Azreana]] in Freeport to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 20s 65c
