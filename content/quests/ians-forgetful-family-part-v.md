This is part 5 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Qeynos]], [[Kelethin]] and [[New Halas]] who wear Chain and Plate Armor (with the exception of [[Shaman|shaman]] types).  The equivalent of this quest for evil-aligned characters is [[Custom Fitted Armor - Part V]].

## Steps

1. You must collect the following items in [[The Crypt of Betrayal]]:
   - 5 Poker Chips from [[A crypt tempest|crypt tempests]]
   - 10 Keys from any Undead in the zone
   - 12 Lucky Gold Coins from any Bloodsabers in the zone
   - Bonechipper from [[An ire minion|ire minions]]
1. Return to [[Ian Cathlan]] to receive your reward.
