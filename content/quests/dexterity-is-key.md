## Steps

Create 12 items from the random list below:

- [[Tranquil Canvas Robe]]
- [[Tranquil Canvas Mitts|Tailored Tranquil Canvas Mitts]]
- [[Tranquil Canvas Pantaloons|Tailored Tranquil Canvas Pantaloons]]
- [[Tranquil Canvas Slippers|Tailored Tranquil Canvas Slippers]]
- [[Woven Boiled Pants|Tailored Woven Boiled Pants]]
- [[Woven Boiled Vest|Tailored Woven Boiled Vest]]
- [[Woven Boiled Boots|Tailored Woven Boiled Boots]]
- [[Woven Boiled Gloves|Tailored Woven Boiled Gloves]]
- [[Dexterous Boiled Boots|Tailored Dexterous Boiled Boots]]
- [[Dexterous Boiled Gloves|Tailored Dexterous Boiled Gloves]]
- [[Dexterous Boiled Pants|Tailored Dexterous Boiled Pants]]
- [[Dexterous Boiled Vest|Tailored Dexterous Boiled Vest]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 6s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
