## Steps

Note: The ground sparkles where the [[Whispering Medallion]] is expected to be used.
![](images/Whispering_Medallion.jpg)

1. Speak with [[Dreadmage Sathrozz]] {{waypoint 552, 250, 1340}}.
1. Follow the [[Whispering Medallion]] to the Mouth of Crusaders' Cave
   - go to {{waypoint 713, 221, 877}} and use the [[Whispering Medallion]]
1. Continue following the trail "to the Pass"
   - go to {{waypoint -366, 52, 406}} and use the [[Whispering Medallion]]
1. Find the trace of Krartukus "to the woods"
   - go to {{waypoint -5, -173, 36}} and use the [[Whispering Medallion]]
1. Continue following the trail "Chalp. Find the Stone!"
   - Stone in Chalp {{waypoint 170, -123, 218}}. Inspect it.
1. Defeat the Kromdul defender. Spawns a little way back down the path.
1. Go back and take a piece of the stone.
1. Return to [[Dreadmage Sathrozz]] {{waypoint 553, 250, 1339}}.
1. Speak to [[Dreadmage Sathrozz]] again after he finished the ritual

## Rewards

- [[Armored Iksar Skeleton]]
- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 15108 status
