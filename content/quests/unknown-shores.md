## Steps

1. Click on the Dinghy at {{waypoint -18.09, -2.06, 100.25}} to travel to Skerry Hideaway.
1. Once on Skerry Hideaway, talk to [[Korrom Flintknuckle]] at {{waypoint -108, -3, -66}}.
1. Kill all of the [[A cursed scallywag]] on the beach. When you have killed them all, a message will broadcast : "The malevolent presence on this island has revealed itself!".
1. The force fields blocking the way into the building at {{waypoint 224.20, 1.57, 99.87}} will drop.
1. Kill [[Captain Rattlebones]].
1. Go back to [[Captain Douglan Wakerunner]] by clicking the rowboat at the entrance.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- 5 [[Bobbing Acorn]]
- [[Vicious Pterodon Plushie]]

## Note

After killing [[Captain Rattlebones]], you are now able to click on the chest near the boat to pick up the quest "[[For Goodness Sake]]".

After completing this quest, clicking on the dinghy presents you with multiple options, rather than zoning you directly into the Skerry Hideaway.
