## Steps

1. Enter [[Karnor's Castle]] and hail [[Xlenla Ghox]] in the back corner of [[Karnor's Jailroom]]. {{waypoint -109, -71, 82}}
   - **Warning**: Do not kill [[An Undead Jailer]] for he is the target of the following quest.
1. Return to [[Xvenloo Ghox]] outside of Karnor's Castle.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 6s 7c
- +1000 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Sokokar Skin Satchel]]
  - [[Vicious Bow of Beyond]]
  - [[Selene's Moonwand]]
  - [[Runic Longbow of Clavicle Piercing]]
  - [[Wand of Mysterious Ways]]
