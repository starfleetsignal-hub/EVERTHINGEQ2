## Steps

1. Speak with [[Planetologist Ahkar]] in [[Maj'Dul]]. {{waypoint -150, 177, -124}}
1. Speak to [[Harshaa]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] just north of [[The Twin Tears]]. {{waypoint -405, -155, -1331}}
1. Kill the [[A desert maniac|desert maniacs]] that spawn and attack you.
1. Speak to Harshaa again once the madmen have been defeated.
1. Slay [[The Prophet]] in the Sinking Sands, spawned by killing the four packs of desert lunatics around the altar at {{waypoint -651, -105, -514}}.
1. Examine the altar.
1. Speak to Harshaa again.
1. Return to Planetologist Ahkar, who will direct you to recover some artifacts from the Samiel Pirates.
1. Stow away on board the Samiel Pirate ship by hiding in a box in the Sinking Sands at {{waypoint -2164, -228, -24}}. This will zone you into the instance [[A Samiel Pirate Ship]].
1. Find the key you need by killing [[Lieutenant Kielmal]].
1. Open the chest aboard the ship and then use the ship's wheel to zone out to the docks in the Sinking Sands.
1. Return to Planetologist Ahkar.
1. Find more information about the Legend of Rahotep:
   - Evil-aligned players: Speak to [[Librarian Zabnolique]] within the [[Academy of Arcane Science]] in [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]]. {{waypoint 18, -7, -136}}
   - Good-aligned players: Speak to [[Librarian Dungil]] within [[The Concordium Tower]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 711, 33, 146}}, (blue portal)
1. Return to Planetologist Ahkar.
1. Destroy the Legions of Rahotep. This is a group encounter: a tank and healer of at least level 60 is recommended for this group. There are approximately 24 level 55^, ^^ and ^^^ undead creatures, followed by [[Rahotep]] himself (55 Epic x2).
   - Begin the encounter by approaching {{waypoint -281, -139, -1171}} in the Sinking Sands.
   - When Rahotep's health reaches 50%, he will heal back to 100%
   - **Warning:** The Rahotep event will begin immediately when someone on that step of the quest nears the listed location. The event will despawn relatively quickly and the respawn time after failure is approximately 20 minutes.
1. Return to [[Planetologist Ahkar]] to claim your reward!
