## Steps

1. Clear away 15 weeds that are growing near the shield amplifiers.![shield amplifier](images/Shield_amplifier.jpg)
   - Note: Best to go back and forth between the 2 totems near the cockatrices to get the 15 needed, the weed respawn is fast. {{waypoint 1076, 6, 2270}} and {{waypoint 1188, 14, 2375}}
   - It is also easy to harvest at a 3rd Totem (Shield Amplifier) nearby {{waypoint 1429, 15, 2359}} so you can rotate between the three.
   - All 3 locations spawns 2 weeds to clear.
1. Return to Chinook

## Rewards

- At least 20g 5s 35c
- +1,250 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +500 faction with **Kerra Isle Outfitters**
