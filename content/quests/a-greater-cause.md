- [[Greater Essence of Clarity]]
- [[Greater Elemental Remedy]]
- [[Greater Essence of Regeneration]]
- [[Greater Noxious Remedy]]
- [[Greater Essence of Power]]
- [[Moderate|Moderate (Journeyman)]]
- [[Death Cloud II|Death Cloud II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Assault II|Assault II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Holy Circle II|Holy Circle II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Bloodbath II|Bloodbath II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Mortal Embrace|Mortal Embrace (Journeyman)]]
- [[Amends|Amends (Journeyman)]]
- [[Death Cloud II|Death Cloud II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Crescent Strike II|Crescent Strike II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Righteousness II|Righteousness II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Blasphemy II|Blasphemy II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Enrage II|Enrage II (Journeyman)]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 6s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
