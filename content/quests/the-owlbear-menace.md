## Steps

1. Kill more [[An ash owlbear|ashen owlbears]] until you have found 8 owlbear feathers. Although the quest helper shows the greater spawn area for the ash owlbears, they are primarily concentrated around {{waypoint -563, 43, 139}}.  May be able to just buy at broker from other players.
1. Find [[Dannian Blackleaf]] {{waypoint 426, 26, -1510}} near the waterfall in Nektulos Forest.
   - He randomly spawns and despawns for days on end (game time). He does NOT appear on a regular cycle based on observing his location for 3 days game time.

## Rewards

- At least 17s 43c
