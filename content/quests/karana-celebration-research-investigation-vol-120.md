## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Karana has asked me to assist in gathering stormbound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the stormbound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Stormbound Energy Reserves|stormbound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect stormbound energy by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Stormlord]] {{waypoint -371, -1, -1091}} in the [[Enchanted Lands]]
