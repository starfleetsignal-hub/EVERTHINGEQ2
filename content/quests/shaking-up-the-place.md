## Steps

1. Frighten 6 Brokenskull Wenches {{waypoint -1309, -944, 564}} in the shanty town
   - All you need to do is run near them to frighten them.
   - You can frighten them while stealthed or invisible.
   - You can use the same wench more than once, just run out of sight and back.
1. Return to Rattamus.
