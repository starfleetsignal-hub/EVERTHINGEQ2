## Steps

1. Find the following in Grunt's Pass {{waypoint -70, -122, 87}} :
   - Interesting Artifact
   - Valuable Artifact
   - Sentimental Artifact
   - Grisly Artifact
     - *They are found within **An Unburied Relic** which looks like a piece of pottery on the ground near the devourers. There's 4 different ones, most are rusted and useless, and there's one of each which sparkles - that one gives the update. They are not trackable.*
     - *All 4 are found in the area between {{waypoint -228, 7, 368}} - {{waypoint -480, 84, 230}} down to {{waypoint -154, -82, 111}}*
     - *See the talk page for possible locations*
1. Return to Klok Grissuc.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 14622 status
- [[Iksar Coal Barrow]]
