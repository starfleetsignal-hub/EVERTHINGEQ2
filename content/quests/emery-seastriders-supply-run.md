*This quest is repeatable up to 4 times..*

## Steps

1. Kill 8-12 of either [[An arctic monitor|arctic monitors]] or [[A bighorn dasher|bighorn dashers]]. {{waypoint 83, 24, -414}}
   - The target monster and how many you are required to kill are selected randomly each time the quest is started.
1. Return to Emery for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 66s
