## Steps

Note: the fighters in this quest will scale to your level. If you are playing a class that has difficulty soloing, you might need help.

1. Patrol the area near the amphitheater and watch for street fights.  You'll find a fight going on at {{waypoint -49, -23, 136}}.
1. Hail one of the fighters, and they'll both attack you.  Kill them.
1. Patrol the area on the south dock.  You'll find another pair of fighters at {{waypoint -287, -56, 113}}.  Hail one of them and kill them.
1. Return to Sergeant Daimyo and he will send you to Clerk Brutus {{waypoint -125, -37, 41}} to finish your citizenship. He will charge you a fee of 1g for the privilege.
