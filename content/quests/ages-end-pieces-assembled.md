## Steps

1. Use the portal to the [[Vigilant Sanctum]] near the Duality
1. Speak with the Duality within the [[Vigilant Sanctum]] at {{waypoint 527, 130, -65}}
1. Examine the Shissar Calendar by clicking on the big stone behind the Duality
1. Speak to the Duality
   - *Note:a long series of statements - a flash of light and you need to head to Cobalt Scar at the place of Yelinak's Fall*
   - *Note:the first answers will get you through the conversation*
1. Enter [[Cobalt Scar]] and proceed to the far southwest on top of the mountains to a point near {{waypoint 5376, -686, 1491}}
1. You must investigate the events at [[Yelinak's Fall]]
   - *Note: <s>Solo-only zone, must be out of group to see portal.</s> One CAN have at least two in group and will be automatically zoned in upon arrival to way point.
1. Proceed to Firiona Vie who is standing beside Lendiniara at {{waypoint 24, 1, -18}}
1. Speak with Lendiniara
   - *Note:at the end of the conversation you will get knocked away from them and drakes will spawn*
1. Defeat 8 drakes
   - Note:A couple were located near the entrance/exit portal at {{waypoint -102, 4.6, 14.3}}*
1. Speak with Lendiniara
1. Speak with Jorlak
1. Return to your home city and speak with the monarch. Zone in at Overlord's Plaza {{waypoint 11, -19, 10}} in [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]] to speak with Lucan D'Lere. For Queen Antonia Bayle, the chambers of her castle at {{waypoint 490.5, 5, -23}} in [[Qeynos Capitol District]]
   - *Note:Examine the [[Skyshrine Tower Rune Stone (Quest item)‎|Skyshrine Tower Rune Stone]] you got to start the next quest*
