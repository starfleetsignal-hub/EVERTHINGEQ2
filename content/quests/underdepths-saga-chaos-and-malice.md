Queen Alwenielle's memories were restored along with her true form. She explained to me how she and her fellow glaufaye are ancient guardians of invaluable knowledge, after suffering a significant attack she locked it away within a gem and broke the gem into three shards. The Grand Artificer held one of the Chaos Rebirth Stone shards within his Exceptional Screwdriver. Now we know that the Aberrant siege was a diversion, while the Primordial Malice infiltrated the city in search of the gem shards!

## Steps

1. Talk to a queen in the [[Stoneheart Yards]] near the portal.
   - Good-aligned players speak to [[Queen Amree (Thalumbra)|Queen Amree]] {{waypoint 468, 96, 51}}
   - Evil-aligned players talk to [[Queen Cristanos (Thalumbra)|Queen Cristanos]] {{waypoint 472, 96, 67}}
1. Recover the [[Grand Artificer's Exceptional Screwdriver]] from [[Xoth, the Ever Rending]] at {{waypoint -63, -13, 255}} in [[Kralet Penumbra: Temple of the Ill-Seen (Advanced Solo)]]. (Note:  Xoth will not appear until all the named in the zone are killed.)
   - Access the instance from Thalumbra [[Penumbra Expansion]]; you want the red portal {{waypoint 456, 6, -760}}.
1. Approach [[Queen Alwenielle (Unveiled Palace)|Queen Alwenielle]] within [[Maldura: Unveiled Palace]] {{waypoint 203, 66, -2}}, located in Maldura royal wing.
   - *A strategy discussion may be found on the Talk page.*
   1. Slay [[Dragoon T'Xilkrin]] and [[Dragoon Vizarn]] in the wings of the palace. Each drops a [[Charged Malduran crystal]].  (Boss' must be at least green to gain the crystals from the small chest drop)
   1. Insert the [[Charged Malduran crystal|crystals]] in the power cells at {{waypoint 279.09, 69.19, 111}} and {{waypoint 279.58, 69.21, -113.32}}.
   1. Use the switches {{waypoint 351.50, 69.44, -1.05}} until you are ported.
   1. Kill the [[The Butcher of Hate|Butcher of Hate]] (click his name to read strat).
1. Speak with [[Queen Alwenielle (Unveiled Palace)|Queen Alwenielle]] again.
1. Speak with your faction representative in the [[Stoneheart Yards]] near the portal.
