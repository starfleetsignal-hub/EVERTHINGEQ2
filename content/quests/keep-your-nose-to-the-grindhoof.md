## Notes

- Consider doing [[101 Things to Do With a Dead Grindhoof]] at the same time as this quest since this quest also requires killing [[An ambushing grindhoof|ambushing grindhooves]].

## Steps

1. Kill 12 [[An ambushing grindhoof|ambushing grindhooves]] in the plains north of [[The Azure Cradle]]. {{waypoint 543, -41, 712}}
1. Return to [[Quartermaster Ulrothk]].

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **Clan Skleross**
- At least 9g 46s 1c
- [[Cuffs of the Terrible]]
