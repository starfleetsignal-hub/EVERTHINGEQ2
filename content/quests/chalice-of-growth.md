## Steps

1. Enter [[Buried Takish'Hiz: Terrene Threshold (Signature)|Buried Takish'Hiz: Terrene Threshold [Signature]]] at {{waypoint -552.43, 11.65, 429.51}} and kill the following
   - *See the walkthrough on the instance's page for detailed walkthrough steps for the dungeon.*
   1. Defeat the [[Guardian of New Bloom]] in the Petrified Cavern {{waypoint -869.63, -18.26, -156.59}}
   1. Defeat the [[Guardian of the Harvest]] in Tel Nev'Dorra, The Royal Hall {{waypoint -900.43, 44.55, 569.03}}.
   1. Defeat the [[Guardian of Waning Light]] in Tel Nev'Dorra, The Royal Hall {{waypoint -962.66, 44.61, 570.07}}.
   1. Defeat the [[Guardian of Dormant Life]] in the Core of Caedor {{waypoint -926.95, 22.98, 291.24}}.
   1. Stop at nothing until the Tarnished Arbo Talv is found: slay [[Anyena Terratha|Anyena]] and [[Yorven Terratha]] {{waypoint -931.87, 38.48, 472.00}}
1. Return to Shawah via the exit at {{waypoint -925, 2, -34}}

## Notes

1. Can be done concurrently with [[Renewal of Ro: Buried Truths]] if you were already able to complete [[Sealed With a Curse|Sealed with a Curse]].
1. [[Anyena Terratha|Anyena]] and [[Yorven Terratha]] are the 4th name (pair) in the instance, and you have to kill them to gain access to the last room. So while steps 1 to 4 can be completed in a cleared zone, the 5th step cannot.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Growth's Guardian Ring]]
  - [[Eye of the Great Mother]]
  - [[Towering Strength of the Elddar]]
