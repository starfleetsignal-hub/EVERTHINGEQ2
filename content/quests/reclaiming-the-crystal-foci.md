## Steps

- You have to find three crystals:
  - [[Green crystal focus]] - dropped from [[A Sathirian researcher|Sathirian researchers]] in [[Sebilis]] in the Lab and Studies Room.
  - [[Blue crystal focus]] - a harvest in the [[Chardok]] Secret Library second room in the Palace.  First 3 books must be harvested from the side rooms up the first stairs and then give the books to the golem bookepper so he will unlock the secret passages.  The blue crystal focus is on the shelf at {{waypoint 908, -67, 557}}.
  - [[Red crystal focus]] - Purchased from [[Gurdo Stonefist]] {{waypoint 2086, 520, -959}} in [[Teren's Grasp]] for 14cp.

The green crystal can also drop off of [[A Sathirian dreadmage|Sathirian dreadmages]] in [[Sebilis]] in the Blacksmith Room
