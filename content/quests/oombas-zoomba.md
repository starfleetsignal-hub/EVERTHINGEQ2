## Steps

1. Create a [[Zoomba]]
   - Oomba gives you a [[Blueprint: Zoomba Prototype]], scribe it in your book in order to make the prototype Zoomba. Requires one (1) each of Titanium Ore, Quicksilver cluster, Amber, and Ethereal Coal.
   - Use the Workbench by Fenwick {{waypoint 1291.32, -540.25, 472.13}}
1. After completion, goto the bar area above the Thurgadin docks {{waypoint 1212.92, -475.45, 645.44}} and release it.
1. Return to [[Oomba]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,000 faction with **Iceclad Pirates**
