## Steps

1. You are given 3 [[An othmir barricade kit]].
1. Travel to the [[Abandoned Snowfang Camp]] at {{waypoint -1476.18, -537.30, -2490.51}} and place a barricade kit at each of 3 green shinies.
1. Wander up the hill further into the gnoll camp until you find the source of the commotion. {{waypoint -1438, -525, -2574}}.
1. Return to [[Clicker]].
