## Steps

1. Speak to Rivra Prayercrystal
1. Speak with the Cryomancers standing around the encampment.
   - Speak to Cryomancer Addicus.
   - Speak to Cryomancer Dobba
   - Speak to Cryomancer Garn
   - Speak to Millety Snowblind.
1. Speak with Rivra Prayercrystal
1. Search for the Daggerflow camp. {{waypoint -117, -336, -20}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
