## Steps

1. Gather usable meat from the [[An adamantine karkata|adamantine karkata]] in Merinep Cove {{waypoint -824, 7, -437}}

- kill them until one of them drops a small chest with [[Adamantine karkata meat]] in it.

1. USING the meat, lure 3 [[A highland saliraptor|highland saliraptors]] to the valley path leading to Lobha Peak {{waypoint -1151, 62, -405}}.  They may be found nearby, around {{waypoint -1245, 46, -135}}.  Note: if you lure one while in combat you may find yourself bugged and unable to fly.
1. Return to Gowlin {{waypoint -567, 8, -582}}
