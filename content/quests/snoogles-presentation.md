Snoogle wants you to obtain four items from the lands of Norrath, and gives you several clues about how to find them, as well as ***Snoogle's Special Flask*** to aid you.

## Steps

1. Gather the following in any order (note that your character must be more than [[Alcohol Tolerance|inebriated]] to harvest these items -- examine ***Snoogle's Special Flask*** to get drunk):
   - The first object lies in the savanna, and follows its own kind though it is much smaller in size.  (Gather a ***Pink Elephant*** from the plains elephant area south of [[Pride Lake]] in the [[The Commonlands]] at {{waypoint 432, -47, -333}}
   - The second object flies in the sky above its home, and even the dead around cannot help but laugh when it oinks.  (Gather a ***Flying Pig*** from inside the barn in  the [[Village of Thundermist]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]] at {{waypoint 562, 2, -126}}
   - The third object is small and fast and has a tail, and was last seen on the beach by a jungle sunning its scales.  (Gather a ***Small Lizard Toy*** from the entrance to the tunnel just off the docks in [[The Feerrott]] at {{waypoint 73, 4, -92}}
   - The fourth item talks to you and should not.  It is good with brew and corned beef, and grows in a very enchanted place.  (Gather a ***Strange Cabbage*** from under the tree just off the docks in [[Enchanted Lands]] at {{waypoint 58, -1, 15}}
1. Return to [[Snoogle Blitzed|Snoogle]] at [[Bar of Brell]] and convince him to take all four items.

## Reward

- [[Snoogle's Party Keg]]
- Choice of:
  - [[Petamorph Wand: Talking Cabbage]]
  - [[A Strange Elephant Plushie]]
  - [[A Pig Plushie]]
  - [[A Small Lizard Plushie]]
  - [[A Strange Cabbage Plushie]]

## Notes

- The progression for intoxication is Tipsy, Inebriated, Drunk, Very Drunk. You may have to be Very Drunk to see the objects; luckily, with the brew you are given, one sip is all it takes.
- The Plushies were added as a reward starting in 2009.
- see [[Getting Sober]] for hints how to sober up
