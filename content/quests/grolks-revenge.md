## Notes

- On [[PvE]] servers, this is a guide or GM-related event. [[PvP]] and Exchange servers may have this quest as a constant fixture.

## Steps

1. Kill 20 [[Bloodskull priest|bloodskull priests]].
1. Speak to [[Grolk]] and inform him that you have killed 20 priests.
   - [[Grolk]] will inform you that you were followed and a group of [[Orc berserker|orc berserkers]] will spawn.
1. Kill the group of [[Orc berserker|orc berserkers]].
1. Speak to [[Grolk]] and receive your reward!
