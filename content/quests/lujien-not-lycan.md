## Steps

1. Speak to [[Venox Tarkog]] at {{waypoint 219, 80, -47}}.
1. Gather 7 moonstones in the Harrowing Horde valley {{waypoint 96, 41, -48}}. They are white gemstones with a blue sparkle.
   - Each time you gather one you will be attacked by a member of the Horde.
1. Return to [[Venox Tarkog|Venox]] who will then craft an [[Ethernere Moonstone Necklace]] to give to [[Deema Mertshak|Deema]].
1. Bring back the necklace to [[Deema Mertshak|Deema]] in the alcove.
1. The quest will auto-complete when she puts on the necklace.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
  - [[Rack of Cardinian Fabrics]]
