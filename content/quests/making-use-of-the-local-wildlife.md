## Steps

![A clump of sweet grass](images/Sweet_grass.jpg)

1. Use the harness that you received to harness three [[A wooly mammoth calf|wooly mammoth calves]] in [[Guardian's Gulch]] and return each one to Seamus McCoven. You must speak with Seamus to turn them in and they can only be brought in one at a time.
1. Harvest 8 sweet grass found throughout [[Guardian's Gulch]]. Some possible locations are: {{waypoint -656, -38, -740}}, {{waypoint -590, -38, -710}}, {{waypoint -493, -38, -760}}, {{waypoint -465, -38, -868}}, {{waypoint -365, -38, -960}}, {{waypoint -315, -38, -1100}}, {{waypoint -210, -38, -1065}}, {{waypoint -242, -38, -1008}}, {{waypoint -245, -38, -1010}}, {{waypoint -276, -38, -1023}}
1. Return to Seamus McCoven.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 20s 65c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Earhoop of Frozen Thoughts]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Earring of Frozen Thoughts]] (All Priests)
  - [[Facet of Frozen Thoughts]] (All Mages)
  - [[Spike of Frozen Thoughts]] (All Fighters)
