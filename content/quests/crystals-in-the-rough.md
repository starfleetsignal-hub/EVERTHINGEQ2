## Notes

- requires 31,500 [[House Yrzu (Faction)]]

## Steps

1. Collect the following ![a shard of Starfire Crystal](images/A_%28large%29_shard_of_Starfire_Crystal.jpg)
   - 3 Starfire Crystals in the [[Pakiat Bluffs]] *{{waypoint -945.17, 79, -91.18}}, {{waypoint -972.81, 69.03, -105.39}}, {{waypoint -991.23, 76.54, -83.18}}, {{waypoint -888.67, 131.37, -709.19}}, {{waypoint -892, 139, -675}}, {{waypoint -896, 140, -675.49}} or {{waypoint -902.08, 131.51, -643.14}}*
   - 6 deep bismuth by killing greatmoles.
     - *[[Cross greatmole]] near {{waypoint -874, 28, 254}} or [[Scavenging greatmole]] near {{waypoint 149, 105, -42}}*
1. Return to [[Ylseeda]] at {{waypoint 194, 584, -722}}.
