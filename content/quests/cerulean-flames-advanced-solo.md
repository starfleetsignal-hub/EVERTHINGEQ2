## Description

*Blue fires have erupted throughout the dhalgar district of Maldura. The Shields of Maldura could use my help in putting out the flames, and whoever started it.*

## Zone

Defeat targets within [[Maldura: District of Ash (Advanced Solo)]]

## Steps

- Kill the evil protégé
  - [[Gavitzle (District of Ash Solo)|Gavtizle]]
- Destroy the quarry golem
  - [[The Quarry Behemoth (District of Ash Solo)|The Quarry Behemoth]]
- Kill the master of the forge
  - [[Tothrak (District of Ash Solo)|Tothrak]]
- Slay the molten beast
  - [[The Fire Gazer (District of Ash Solo)|The Fire Gazer]]
- Defeat the leaders of ash
  - [[Cugdava the Changer (District of Ash Solo)|Cugdava]]
