## Prerequisites

- You must be at least level 66 to complete this quest.

## Steps

1. Talk to [[Tavish Dracinov]] in [[D'Venhz Rectory]] in the [[Village of Somborn]]. {{waypoint -395, 12, -53}}
1. Speak with [[Amares D'Venhz]] at the other end of the room. {{waypoint -373, 12, -33}}
1. Amares sends you to gather:
   - 20 chunks of raw meat from any bone golem in the [[Loping Plains]]/[[Mistmoore Catacombs]].
     - [[A carrion abhorrence]] in Loping Plains. {{waypoint -150, 7, 6}}.
     - [[A rictus gnawer]] in Mistmoore Catacombs.
   - 20 congealed blood drops from bats in Mistmoore Catacombs.
     - [[A bloodbeak gorer]], [[A borebeak sipper|borebeak sipper]], and [[A borebeak stabber|borebeak stabber]] update the quest. {{waypoint 34, -123, 135}}
1. Return to Amares D'Venhz.
1. Talk to [[Havras "Hack" Scutter]], nearby at {{waypoint -310, 11, -49}}.  Select "You know anyone that...".
1. He tells you to create a weapon suitable for slaying a powerful undead ghoul lord. This will launch **one of two sub-quests**, depending on whether or not you have completed the heritage quest [[Restoring Ghoulbane]] and obtained [[Ghoulbane (HQ Reward)|Ghoulbane]]:
   - If you have: [[Ghoulbane Empowered]]
   - If you have not: [[Gogas Afadin (Quest)|Gogas Afadin]]
   - While completion of only one of these subquests is necessary to advance this step, players that have already completed Restoring Ghoulbane can complete both.
1. Kill [[Mordiggian]] (68^^^ Heroic) in [[The Breathless Hollow]] of Loping Plains. To spawn him, click *twice* on a plate on a rock at {{waypoint -380, -13, 141}}.
1. Return to Amares D'Venhz.

- 77400 status
  - 79980 status @ lvl 68, 69, 88, 95, 110
