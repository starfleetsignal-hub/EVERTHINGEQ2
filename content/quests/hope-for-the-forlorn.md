## Description

The curse of Gynok Moltor taints every room inside the Halls of the Forsaken. To combat this evil I've been asked to put an end to a great number of the forlorn resident inside the cursed halls. Perhaps then the risen dead may finally achieve their eternal rest.

## Steps

1. Enter the [[Befallen: Halls of the Forsaken]] and slay 40 [[A forlorn denizen|forlorn denizens]] found inside
1. Return to Squire Hadley in the Commonlands

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
