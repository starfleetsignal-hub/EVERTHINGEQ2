## Steps

![](images/Snowy_mound.jpg)
![](images/Chunck_of_snow.jpg)
![Smash the snow into this bowl](images/A_Friendly_Splash_quest_-_bowl.jpg)

1. Break 9 snowy mounds around [[Fina's Retreat]] and gather chunks. Snowy Mounds are large piles of snow with particle effects.
   - Each large mound breaks down into three small chunks, so you will only need three large snow mounds.
1. Return to [[Diu]] -1568, -539, -2340
1. Smash your chunks in the bowl next to [[Diu]]. Receive [[Festival water]].
1. Splash 7 othmir villagers using the [[Festival water]]. Any othmir NPC that doesn't have a speech bubble when moused over will work.
1. Return to [[Diu]]
