## Steps

1. Find the brazier at {{waypoint 196, 411, 1251}} and burn the samples
   1. Burn the first sample, which emits a red flame.
   1. Burn the second sample, which gives off a blue flame.
   1. Burn the third sample, resulting in a yellow flame.
1. Extinguish the embers by clicking on the brazier again.
1. Return to Ebodold Tongofur and tell him the color of the flames during each burning. (Try: Red, Blue, Yellow. YMMV.)

## Rewards

- At least 46g
