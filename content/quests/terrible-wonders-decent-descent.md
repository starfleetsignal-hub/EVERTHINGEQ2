## Notes

- You receive in game mail from [[Quibble]] to point you where to go, but reading mail is not required to receive quest.
- Items: Glob of Webbing, Thick Rope are temporary no-zone items, if you leave the zone you will need to gather them again.

## Items Needed

Bring 50 [[Formless Coal]] with you.

## Steps

1. Quibble directs you to enter Corathus Descent [Tradeskill] - click blue crystal floating deeper in that tunnel at {{waypoint 342, -68, -276}}
1. Gather globs of webbing, small white puffballs on webs. Throw at spiders to incapacitate them. You can pick up more later as needed.
1. Gather Sword Webcarver {{waypoint -105.00, -5.94, 250}} and click on web walls blocking path.
1. Catch up with [[Quibble]] {{waypoint 29.56, -0.27, 137.56}}
1. Find a way to entice the pilbippi down. In order to do that:
   - gather 3 x Cave Snail from 'a cave snail' that wander around in that area
   - gather 2 x Corathus Root Sap from yellow blobs
   - gather 3 x Entangling Vine from 'a subtunarian botanic sprout' in water.
   - Gather recipe from the stone block at {{waypoint -40, -1, 124}}, find it in bags and scribe it. The stone block is a makeshift workbench to craft on.
1. Using  your 50 Formless Coal and gathered ingredients create Baited Pilbippi Trap. Place {{waypoint 22.10, -2.29, 153.34}}
1. Climb aboard the tethered pilbippi. Oops, Quibble took it. Find another way down to the bottom.
   - Pick up a number of Thick Rope laying around and use that as Rope ladder at {{waypoint -87.41, -7.02, 255.49}} (close to where you got the sword earlier) to climb down onto a lower path.
1. Use a boom barrel {{waypoint 52.46, 0.00, 0.00}} to break the rocks blocking the path, keep walking down.
1. Take the Teir'Dal Trow in search of Quibble. Go to {{waypoint -435.30, -166.84, 18.67}}, as you go use Globs of Webbing on Texians to incapacitate them. Use trow. (this zones you into Val'Zyroth Ness zone, there are lvl 137 aggro mobs around the area, watch out for them if your adventure level is low)
1. Find [[Quibble]] around Hate's Covenant: go up the ramp to {{waypoint -1125.89, -10.43, -465.26}}
1. Speak with [[Frau Choraline Lent]] {{waypoint -1128.28, -10.85, -464.36}}
