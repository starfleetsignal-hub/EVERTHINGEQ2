## Steps

1. Get some items:
   1. Get 8 blood gorger syringe beaks
   1. Get 6 crystals that grow around the bottom of some of the trees in Innothule Swamp, Each tree usually has several crystals.
      - Example locations:
        - {{waypoint -25, -77, -224}}
        - {{waypoint -14, -77, -253}}
        - {{waypoint 33, -87, -189}}
        - {{waypoint 45, -90, -195}}
        - {{waypoint -100, -78, -137}}
        - {{waypoint -181, -86, -142}}
1. Return to [[Hop Wrenchwit]]
