## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill [[Factory Overseer Klankbolt]] in the Clockwork Factory and take a sample of oil from it.
1. Kill 20 various clockwork in order to collect samples of oil for Agent Willsprock.
1. Return to [[Agent Willsprock]].

## Rewards

- At least 15g 15s 54c
- [[A Viscous Manifestation]] after 5 completions.
