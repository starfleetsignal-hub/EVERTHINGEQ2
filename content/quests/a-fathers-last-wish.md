## Steps

1. Find a a father.
   - Go to [[Cobalt Scar]] and speak with [[Eshkan]] just outside of [[Scarstone]] at either {{waypoint 4371, -821, 377}} or {{waypoint 4243, -819, 432}}
1. Find Eshkan's children and deliver the toy.
   - Head to [[Freeport]] and zone into [[Big Bend: Sanctum of Spirits]] {{waypoint 60, -12, 271}}.
  - Note: the zone scales to the level of the player, the aggressive trolls do not, however, see through Stealth/Invisibility.
   - Speak with [[Rodice]] inside a building around {{waypoint 11, 0, 31}}
1. Return to [[Eshkan]].
1. Examine the [[Disused manacles|disused manacles]] and speak with the [[Eidolon of Heroism]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
