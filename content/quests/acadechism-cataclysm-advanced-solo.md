## Steps

1. Kill 15 orcs before time runs out (you have 6 minutes).

Most of the orcs are in the first room and in the two ante-chambers on either side of it. However, there are a few more orcs deeper in zone, mixed with other creatures. Note that time starts ticking away immediately.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 67g 40s 6250 status
- 3 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
