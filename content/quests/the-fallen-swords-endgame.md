## Steps

1. Use the Swords of Destiny to slay Rallos Zek.
   - Zone into the Plane of War from the Fortress of Drunder.  Kill all the named except for the 2 Commanders.  They must be killed at the same time to continue the script.  After you kill them, any other mobs still alive in the zone will swarm you.  They all fear so if you don't have an anti-fear aug or something similar, kill all the ads before killing the Commanders.  After the Commanders are dead, head up the center of the zone to where Rallos Zek is.  There is a golden orb on his platform that should now be clickable.  Click it and it will transform to the Swords of Destiny.  Wait for all the dialogue.  General Teku will then spawn.  Kill his adds to make him attackable.  Kill him and Sullon, Tallon, and Vallon will tell you to take out Rallos.  Click the Swords of Destiny behind Rallos, wait for more text, and you'll get the update.
1. Return to [[Erus Dal'viv]] in Drunder
1. Return to [[The Duality]] in Thurgadin
