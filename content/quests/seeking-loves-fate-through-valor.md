## Steps

1. Speak with [[Graarp Muckflipper]] at the Temple of Life
1. Travel to the [[Graveyard]] and seek out six Artifacts of Valor from several [[Restless Knight of Truth|restless Knights of Truth]] (which spawn equal to your level). The artifact icons look like runes in your inventory and are named: Charity, Compassion, Devotion, Honor, Love, Truth.
1. Speak to [[Squire Glurrp]] at the tent outside of Befallen {{waypoint 641, -55, 745}}.
1. Search for the final two Artifacts of Valor near the entrance to Befallen. {{waypoint 645, -64, 826}} and {{waypoint 568, -61, 859}}
1. Go to {{waypoint 552, -70, 869}} to meditate... and have a conversation with the "Glorious Lord of Light"...
1. Return to [[Sister Katey Dalwynn]] at the Antonican Druid Rings.
