## Steps

1. Find the lost testimony of William Taros by clicking on the book at {{waypoint -12, -24, 246}} (at the bottom of the broken stairwell near the final mob)
1. Give the testimony to a reputable historian [[Riss Taskel]] {{waypoint -2, -7, -133}} in the [[Academy of Arcane Science]] in [[North Freeport]] or [[Ashtur Lawton]] in the [[Concordium Tower]] (blue transporter) in [[South Qeynos]].
1. Speak with [[High Lord William Taros]] at {{waypoint 607, -65, 781}} in [[The Commonlands]]. If he is not up, hail the statute of him that appears at the /loc.

## Rewards

- At least 25g 70s 61c
- One of the following:
  - [[Mithaniel Marr Figurine]]
  - [[Queen of Love Pewter]]
