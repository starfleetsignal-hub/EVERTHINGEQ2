## Background

The Grand Master at the Wu Encampment is worried that the Nybright Family has learned of Wu's hidden artifact and have begun seeking it.

## Notes

- This quest requires 0 faction with the Disciples of Wu and NOT having completed the [[Lock and Key]] quest.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Grand Master Lu Sun]] in the [[Camp of the Legendary Wu]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]] at {{waypoint 781, 111, -281}}.
1. Locate the main provision tent {{waypoint -586, -61, 485}} at the Thexian camp in Lesser Faydark.  You will get an auto-update.
1. Click on each of the three barrels in the same provision tent and receive [[A Thexian beverage sample]] from each.
1. Return to [[Grand Master Lu Sun]] with the drink samples.

## Rewards

- +100 faction with **Disciples of Wu**
- At least 15g 15s 54c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Gemmed Chain of the Sandmage]] (All Mages)
  - [[Gemmed Chain of the Dunestalker]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Gemmed Chain of the Desert Sword]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Gemmed Chain of the Sun Priest]] (All Priests)
