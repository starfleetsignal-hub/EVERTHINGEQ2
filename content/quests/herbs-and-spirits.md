## Notes

- Given on Wednesdays and gives the 2 different bushes as house items as well.

## Steps

1. Vanesta asked me to gather some 6 fresh herbs for making healing poultices.
   - Gather *Healing Herbs*. These are pink and blue bushes in the area around the Tourbillon just outside Cardin Ward, and Track Harvestables will list them.
