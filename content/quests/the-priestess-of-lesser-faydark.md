## Prerequisites

- You must be at least adventure level 50.

## Steps

1. Go to the [[Camp of the Legendary Wu]] in [[The Lesser Faydark]] and speak with [[Vergo Stormherald]]. {{waypoint 797, 120, -393}}.
1. Find clues to the priestess's whereabouts (click on the interactable stones):
   - some fabric and leaf under stone {{waypoint 691, 62, -253}}
   - hair ribbon under stone {{waypoint 619, 29, -35}}
   - dried river mud {{waypoint 337, -20, 148}}
   - torn page {{waypoint 239, -21, -52}}
1. Speak with [[Tuilla Laarivera]] at [[The Fae Court]]. {{waypoint -267, -56, 405}}
1. Enter the [[The Hidden Grotto]] {{waypoint -4, -57, 224}} and speak with [[Esthella Evenheart]]. 69, -94 327.
1. Return to [[Kurista (Greater Faydark)|Kurista, High Priestess of Growth]].

## Rewards

- +7500 faction with **Tunare**
- [[Eva's Altar of Tunare]]
