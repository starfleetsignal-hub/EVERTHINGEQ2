## Steps

It's time to give chase to those who would threaten peace and prosperity on the light side of Luclin! There's nowhere for them to hide!

- Shed Light on the shadow within Maiden's Eye.
- Close the book on the librarian of Wayward Manor.
- Oust the lord within Echelon of Divinity.
- Vanquish the shade within the vault of Ssraeshza.
