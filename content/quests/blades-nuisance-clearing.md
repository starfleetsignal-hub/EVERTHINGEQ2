## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- Once +20,000 faction has been achieved with the Court of the Blades this quest will no longer be available.

## Steps

1. Kill a random number (usually between 7 to 10) of two random sets of mobs. Some of these may include:
   - [[A desert madman|desert madmen]]
   - [[A dune scarab|dune scarabs]]
   - [[A dune spiderling|dune spiderlings]]
   - [[A toxic cobra|toxic cobras]]
   - [[A Rujarkian protector|Rujarkian protectors]]
   - [[A Rujarkian brute|Rujarkian brutes]]
   - [[A Rujarkian spiritist|Rujarkian spiritists]]
   - [[Rujarkian highwayman]]
1. Return to [[Mahir Lu'ay]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 23s 29c
- +2500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -2500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -2500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
