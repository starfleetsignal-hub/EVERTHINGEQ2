## Steps

1. Zone into the [[The Commonlands]] then to [[Befallen: The Spread of Undeath]] {{waypoint 677.91, -67.27, 805.35}}. Investigate the Befallen Caverns upper levels for ties to Anashti Sul.  Talk to "a wandering entity" at {{waypoint 12.91, -0.27, -1.35}}
   - *Note: This instance doesn't scale and is populated with level 57 heroic undead*
   - *The quest can also be done in  [[Befallen: Cavern of the Afflicted]] , which will auto-scale to the player's level (up to level 100)*
1. Investigate the Befallen Caverns' cave areas for ties to Anashti Sul.  Talk to another "wandering entity" at {{waypoint 110.69, -55.69, -118.65}} (in the caves after falling down the well and going down the hill pass the water heading East at the Arch).
1. Investigate the Befallen Caverns' lower levels for ties to Anashsti Sul.  Speak to a third "wandering entity" at {{waypoint 140.71, -71.55, -142.13}} (Last ghost is in the room with final boss).
1. Return to Prophet Bainyn Fairwind.
