## Steps

1. Speak with [[Brun]] {{waypoint -1134.01, -11.70, -463.46}}
   - Find 40 Cavernhide Leather
   - Find 40 Deepvein Ore
   - Find 40 Clayroot Vine
   - Find 40 Stonebark Lumber
1. Bring the ingredients to [[Brun]]. Recipe Scroll: Brun's Elemental Disguise auto-scribes to recipe book. Requires 1 Formless Kindling. Use the small stool near the campfire (workbench).
1. Speak with [[Brun]].
1. Find the [[Dunamis Fens]] - Portal to [[Keramos Caverns]] {{waypoint -925.76, -55.91, 353.12}} Zone inside to Portal to [[Kermos Caverns: Dunamis Fens]] {{waypoint 12.61, 183.04, -26.94}}
1. I should don the disguise.
1. I need to find [[Quibble]]. I should look for 5 clues.
   - {{waypoint -205.26, 171.20, 13.47}}
   - {{waypoint -297.66, 164.18, 80.41}}
   - {{waypoint -206.95, 191.51, 197.56}}
   - {{waypoint -388.81, 220.20, 93.71}}
   - {{waypoint -507.59, 245.19, 158.17}}
1. I should speak to [[Quibble]] {{waypoint -605.54, 246.84, 260.87}}
   - Receive Recipe Scroll: Earth-Bound Hammer. Requires 1 Formless Kindling.
   - earth-bound handle {{waypoint -379.59, 213.81, 5.16}}
   - earth-bound hammer head {{waypoint -138.20, 211.71, 293.05}}
   - hammer pin {{waypoint -308.63, 216.76, 374.45}}
1. Find the crafting station next to [[Quibble]] {{waypoint -593.80, 246.48, 263.09}}
1. Shatter the cage and speak to [[Quibble]]
1. Find [[Frau Choraline Lent]] in [[Val'Zyroth Ness]] {{waypoint -1128.28, -10.85, -464.36}}
