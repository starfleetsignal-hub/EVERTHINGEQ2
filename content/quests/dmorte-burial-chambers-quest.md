## Notes

- This is the introductory quest for the Bloodline Chronicles [[Adventure Packs|adventure pack]].
- This instance, as with many of the other Bloodline Chronicles instances, include breakable walls which open access to areas after you damage them.
- To interact with the trapdoor, right-click and select "use".

## Steps

This quest used to be journal level 30 but now requires level 50 to obtain it.

1. Enter the [[D'Morte Burial Chambers (Zone)|D'Morte Burial Chambers]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] at {{waypoint 678, 2, -617}}. The instance will be called [[Unearthing]]. To update the first step, locating the D'Morte burial chamber, you may need to move towards the center of the crypt and then back towards the entrance (but do not exit the instance).
1. Find the coffin in center of the crypt at {{waypoint -53, -11, 42}} and approach it. You may need to move around the area near the coffin to update the quest. The second step of the quest will then update with "I was unable to open T'Haen's coffin, something is keeping it sealed."
1. In order to break the seal of T'Haen's coffin you must examine and 'disturb the grave' then kill the ghost that spawns at each gravesite around the zone. Each of the ghosts are worth AA experience. Their locations are as follows:
   - [[Alicon D'Morte]] {{waypoint -57, -9, 16}}
   - [[Yillandris D'Morte]] {{waypoint -69, -7, 96}}
   - [[Zendra D'Morte]] {{waypoint -77, -7, 89}}
   - [[Sinege D'Morte]] {{waypoint -68, -6, 75}}
   - [[Cahl D'Morte]] {{waypoint -62, -6, 78}}
   - [[Hval D'Morte]] {{waypoint -76, -7, 93}}
   - [[Lucin D'Morte]] {{waypoint -47, -6, 99}}
   - [[Omartin D'Morte]] {{waypoint -52, -6, 104}}
   - [[Erila D'Morte]] {{waypoint -46, -6, 95}}
   - [[Blensiir D'Morte]] {{waypoint 3, -3, 87}}
   - [[Jergaeth D'Morte]] {{waypoint -9, 3, 97}}
   - [[Kalten D'Morte]] {{waypoint 1, -3, 75}}
   - [[Vander D'Morte]] {{waypoint -19, -3, 74}}
   - [[Narjin D'Morte]] {{waypoint -19, -3, 74}}
1. Once all of the graves have been disturbed and the ghosts defeated, the quest will update with "I need to open T'Haen's coffin". Right-click on the coffin to examine it and this will complete the quest.

## Rewards

- [[Crest of D'Morte]]
  - Examine this item to initiate the following quest.
