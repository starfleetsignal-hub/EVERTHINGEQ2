## Steps

To prove that I am a well-rounded crafter, I must show off my harvesting prowess in any zone or zones of my choice.  **THIS YEAR OF DARKPAW QUEST MUST BE COMPLETED BY DECEMBER 31, 2024**

1. I must harvest from anywhere in the lands before December 31, 2024
   1. Sucessfully harvest 3,000 times before December 31
1. Return to [[Vare Smothers]]
