A page pulled from a magical whirlwind of paper appears to be an old bounty calling for hte apprehension, dead or alive, of high-ranking officials within the Fabled Vaults of El'Arad. I may just fulfill it... without keeping them alive.

## Steps

1. Defeat the [[The Arcane Librarian (Fabled)|Arcane Librarian]]
1. Dispatch the [[The Messenger of El'Arad (Fabled)|Messenger of El'Arad]]
1. Kill [[Korgo the Vault Keeper (Fabled)|Korgo the Vault Keeper]]

## Notes

- This quest is only available in the Fabled version of the instance. You must be at least level 95 to enter.

## Rewards

- 5 [[Draconic Etyma]]
- At least 1p 55g
