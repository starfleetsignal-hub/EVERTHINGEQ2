## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
  - Quest becomes repeatable after initial completion. No Scarred Dinars reward on repeats.

## Steps

1. Gather and hunt the following
   - Gather either
     - 8x permafrost potatoes (similar to appearance to harvest  shrubs) or
     - 8x Dry Wood (small wood log)
       - Found below camp and along path to the South
   - Hunt either
     - 12 mammoths for steaks or
     - 12 snowfeather hawks for meat
       - Found below camp and just in camp
1. Return to [[Zorie]] at Hoarfang Ridge {{waypoint -475, 123, -338}}

## Rewards

- [[Cold Othmir Lantern]] house item
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- 50 x [[Scarred Dinar]] -- *Only the first time*
