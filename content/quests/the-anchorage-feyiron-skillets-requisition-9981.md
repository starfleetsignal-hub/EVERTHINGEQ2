## Steps

1. Harvest the following:
   - 18 [[Dewpalm lumber]] from [[Fallen dewpalm branch]]
   - 15 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Coalesced reef]]
   - 15 [[Nimbic hide]] from [[Basilisk lair]]
   - 10 [[Strangler root]] from [[Strangler root break]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Wooden Container]].
1. Craft the [[Wooden Container]] on a [[Work Bench]].
1. Speak to [[Hibbie Feltfoot]] at {{waypoint 28.65, -14.04, -69.05}}.
