## Steps

1. Search piles of sand near [[Sentry Vylkren]] for an odd feather.
1. Speak with [[Prime Researcher Mak'bin]] at [[The Grand Athenaeum]] within [[Gorowyn]]. {{waypoint 2646, 63, 1346}}

## Rewards

- At least 12s 2c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Forged Axe]] (Warriors)
  - [[Forged Blade]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Forged Cestus]] (Brawlers, Beastlord)
  - [[Forged Tower Shield]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Empowered Holy Symbol]] (All Priests)
  - [[Empowered Totem]] (All Mages)
