## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Tunare has informed me that summoning an Avatar requires the land to be attuned to their element. To achieve this, altars have been set up around the region, but they need to be supplied with power. I have been asked to assist with this by collecting objects infused with the essence of slain foes and offer them to an altar.

1. Collect 3 infused [[Bramble Seeds|bramble seeds]]
   1. Dropped in [[Zimara Breadth]] by a mystic grappler (found in Auric Palisades at {{waypoint -624, 189, -636}}
      1. **WARNING 14 March:**  these mobs disappear on death instead of leaving a corpse to loot - you must click them quickly in order to loot the item
   1. Also dropped by metal-infected daggerclaw and scorched direfangs  near {{waypoint -333.26, 98.95, -288.45}}
1. Afterwards you take those shards to a shrine in [[The Lesser Faydark|Lesser Faydark]] - the shrine spawns in random spot in that zone.
   1. This is NOT a group update.
   1. One person clicking the altar will cause the altar to disappear and reappear in another location
   1. It is not clear if there is only ever one altar in the zone that is moved by other groups
   1. It may be possible to click simultaneously to get a group update
   1. You may spend as much as 30 minutes tracking down an altar to click
   1. Possible Locations include:
      1. {{waypoint 63, -27, 237}} south of the bridge near The Snake's Head
      1. {{waypoint -299, -53, 390}} across the river from The Fae Court
      1. {{waypoint -282, -49, 195}}
      1. {{waypoint -110.05, -53.47, -166.61}} near Lower Grove Stable
      1. {{waypoint -264, -52, 147}}
      1. {{waypoint 317.16, -24.06, -52.81}}Northwest of the Herald camp
      1. {{waypoint 336, 38, 341}}
      1. {{waypoint -467, -57, -181}}
      1. {{waypoint -25, -47, 471}}
1. Return to [[Herald of the Mother of All]] {{waypoint 154, -23, 3}} in [[The Lesser Faydark|Lesser Faydark]]
