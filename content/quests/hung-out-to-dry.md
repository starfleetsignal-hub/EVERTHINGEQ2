## Notes

The Fish Drying Rack recipe auto-scribes. You do *not* need to locate it in inventory and scribe it manually.

## Steps

1. Gather the following
   - Gather some wood - *from [[Driftwood]] on the beach shore. Trackable.*![driftwood](images/Driftwood_visible.jpg)
   - Gather some [[Rusty nail|metal]] - *from "broken barrels" all over the beach shore. Trackable.*![broken barrel](images/Broken_barrel.jpg)
1. Construct a [[Fish Drying Rack]].
   - Use the [[Goblin Workbench]] {{waypoint 18, -241, -875}}. ![Goblin Workbench](images/Goblin_Workbench.jpg)
   - Requires 100 Artisan, 1 [[Rusty nail]], 2 [[Driftwood]], and 1 [[Thaumic Coal]].
1. Place the drying rack for Kreeg (17, -242, -868).
1. Speak to [[Kreeg (Goblin)|Kreeg]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +2000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
