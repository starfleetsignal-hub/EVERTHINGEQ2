## Steps

1. Help the 3 Combine Caravan defenders in the camp.
   - Stand near defender and mobs will spawn
1. Defeat 6 [[A holgresh attacker|holgresh attackers]]
   - You must land the killing blow for an update.
1. Report back to [[Captain Haliran]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- One of the following, depending on Class:
  - [[Combine Shield of the March]] (Berserker, Guardian, Paladin, Shadowknight)
  - [[Withering Skull Fist Wraps]] (Bruiser, Monk)
  - [[Withered Bark Buckler]] (All Priests)
  - [[Withered Totem of Mourning]] (All Mages)
  - [[Withered Bark Branch Dagger]] (All Scouts)
