## Description

"Sajas Goldbeam, a fae stationed at Green Knoll, has asked that I scout out several locations beneath the city of Kelethin to determine the extent of the Crushbone attacks upon the city." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Talk to the following NPCs:
   - Visit [[Aquamarine Pond]] {{waypoint 503, -1, 205}}
   - Visit [[Joleena's Restaurant Acorn Lift]] {{waypoint 606, 0, 315}}
   - Visit [[Granite Hills Acorn Lift]] {{waypoint 73, 34, 414}}
   - Visit [[Kelethin Central Stump]] {{waypoint 366, 20, 343}}
   - Visit [[Kelethin Outer Stump]] {{waypoint 595, 30, 497}}
1. Return to Sajas

## Rewards

- Al least 2s 57c
- [[Detailed map of the Faydark]]
