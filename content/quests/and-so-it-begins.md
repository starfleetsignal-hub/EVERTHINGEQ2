## Notes

- The islands that [[Harun Clearsky]] will send you to are in the same order as the islands for [[Criggle Cloudpuff]]'s quests. It is convenient to run both questlines in synch.

## Steps

I need to search the last known location of Clarissa Cloudpuff. Her suspicious brother Criggle thinks it was on Discord Isle.

1. Go to [[The Isle of Discord (POI)|The Isle of Discord]] via the cloud station to receive a quest update.
1. Collect the missing pages of Clarissa's Journal (118, 119, 121, 122, 124 and 125). Slay [[A Blacktalon curate|Blacktalon curates]] and [[A Blacktalon grunt|Blacktalon grunts]] around the isle. {{waypoint 8, -123, -147}}
1. Kill [[A Blacktalon conscript]] to collect a bloodstained page.
1. Return the pages to [[Criggle Cloudpuff|Criggle]] on [[Whisperwind Isle]].
