## Notes

- This is a limited repeatable quest. The quest gives random # of mobs and types of mobs to kill.
- The first time you complete the quest, you will get your choice of reward as well as experience. Subsequent completions yield only experience.

## Steps

1. Slay 4-8 randomly assigned mobs. Possible mobs/locations include:
   - [[A lesser mummy|lesser mummies]] or [[A crypt mummy|crypt mummies]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] near [[The Bay of Anuk]] {{waypoint -769, -155, -1170}}.
   - [[The Spectre Tower]] {{waypoint -1281, -210, -94}}
   - ghouls near [[The Chimney]] -1 199 -118 -755
1. Return to [[Aquim]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Choice of One of the Following:
  - [[Ring of Elemental Command (Level 40)|Ring of Elemental Command]]
  - [[Shimmering Gauntlets of Force]]
  - [[Djinn Prayer Doll]]
