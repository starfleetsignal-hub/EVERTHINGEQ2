## Notes

- Previously, this was the only known quest for learning [[Thexian]]. Eventually, however, the [[Thexian Language Primer|language primer]] was made available for purchase.

## Steps

1. You must find four symbols inside [[The Tombs of Night: Retribution]]:
   - *Symbol of Raet*: Kill [[Rhayn T'Dax]] found in the passageway connecting the first level to [[The Maw of Mortality]], at {{waypoint 54, -23, -101}}.
   - *Symbol of Veth*: Open the coffin at the very back of The Maw of Mortality at {{waypoint -84, -22, -62}}.
   - *Symbol of Ythe*: Examine a coffin slightly north of [[Nilith T'Feyd]] at {{waypoint -102, -24, -27}}
   - *Symbol of Trev*: Spawn and kill [[Dvith N`Zur]] at the base of [[The Forgotten Tears (The Tombs of Night: Retribution)|The Forgotten Tears]] at {{waypoint 17, -41, 88}}. To do this you must kill **all** the Neonate vampire found on this bottom level. Once every last one is dead, Dvith will spawn as an NPC hypnotized by [[A nightmare of Dvith|nightmares of Dvith]]. Kill them and in a few moments Dvith will become an attackable mob.
1. Return to [[Ytheri Coldtouch]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- A least 1g 26s 57c
- [[Thexian|Thexian Language Primer]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Potion of Marr]]
  - [[Salve of Marr]]
- Ability to purchase the [[Ceremonial Armor of Marr (Armor Set)]] and crafting books released with the [[Bloodline Chronicles]] adventure pack from the camp's faction merchant, the [[Quartermaster of the Hand]].
