## Description

I've been asked by Bergna Veliumsmiter to make my way into the depths of the Forgotten Pools and bring back samples of what is now living there.

## Steps

- Bring Back 6 reptilian samples in [[Forgotten Pools]]
  - reptilian samples are collected by killing [[A murk viper]]
- Bring Back 6 wooden samples in [[Forgotten Pools]]
  - wodden samples are collected by killing [[A petrified ancient]] or [[A wicker walker]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 80g
- [[Primal Velium Shard]] x1
- +350 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
