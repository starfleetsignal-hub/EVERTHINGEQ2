## Steps

1. Free the spirit of [[Graalg Skirmisher]]
   - Approach the statue at {{waypoint 0, 0, 99}}
   - Subdue the spirit of [[Graalg Skirmisher]] that spawns
   - Click the statue
1. Free the spirit of [[Gwomp Boarspearer]]
   - Approach the statue at {{waypoint 2, 68, -116}}
   - Subdue the spirit of [[Gwomp Boarspearer]] that spawns
   - Click the statue
1. Attack the statue at the end of the way. A light appears, wandering down and opens the barrier you passed on your way to [[Gwomp Boarspearer]] .
1. Free the spirit of [[Krohnak Beardripper]]
   - Approach the statue at {{waypoint 0, 102, 98}}
   - Subdue the spirit of [[Kroshnak Beardripper]] that spawns
   - Click the statue
1. Free the spirit of [[Soothsayer Groirk]]
   - Approach the statue at {{waypoint 0, 300, 98}}
   - Subdue the spirit of [[Soothsayer Groirk]] that spawns
   - Click the statue

## Rewards

- At least 1p 1g
