## Notes

- If you complete [[Chew the Fat]] before receiving this quest, you will be unable to get this quest.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Beckavar]] at {{waypoint -1372, -509, -2252}} (next to the griffawn pad)
1. Beckavar asks you to speak to a Snowfang gnoll.  He states that you must know the Gnollish language.
1. Speak to [[Thurr]] at {{waypoint -944, -518, -2120}}
   - Note: if you are on the quest [[Chew the Fat]] he will not respond until it is completed.

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +150 faction with **Othmir of Velious**
