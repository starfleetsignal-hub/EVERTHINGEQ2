## Steps

1. Kill any Bloodsworn sect vampire in [[The Tombs of Night]] or [[The Crypt of T'haen]] until you receive 5 amulet updates.
1. Find and examine a dark altar in [[The Crypt of T'haen]] to obtain a piece of it.
   - The altar looks like a lamp post at {{waypoint -108, 26, -9}}
1. Defeat the [[Guardian of the Altar]] and his companions that spawn when you break off a piece of the altar.
1. Return to [[Aleeshi Monala]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 26s 57c
- [[Silver charm (Version 2)|silver charm]] (a vampire illusion charm that uses any [[Vampiric incisor]] as a catalyst).
- 4 [[A vampiric incisor (Level 70)|vampiric incisors]]
