## Description

*If I'm going to stop the goblin assault, I'll need to defeat those that lead them. Find the leaders of the Pickclaw in both High Keep and Pickclaw Depths, and defeat them.*

## Steps

- Zone into [[High Keep (Heroic)]]:
  - Defeat [[General Graknus (Heroic)|General Graknus]]
- Zone into [[High Keep: The Pickclaw Depths (Heroic)]]
  - Defeat [[Bolgin Serilis (Heroic)|Bolgin Serilis]]

## Rewards

At least 5p

- 15 [[Draconic Etyma]]
- [[Sparkling Crate]]
