## Steps

1. Gather some more healing components outside the village, they can be found around [[The Gnarls]], [[Rot Reek Bog]] and [[Bog'ta Beach]] regions of Yon Gorroth.
   - gather gnarltree durians for their pulp x14 ![gnarltree durian](images/Gnarltree_Durian_%28pickup%29.png)
     - (not trackable) on the ground in [[The Gnarls]] - the area East of Darou village, stretching from {{waypoint 311, 74, -578}} to {{waypoint 10, 38, -499}}
   - pop boil nests for boil flies to harvest x8 ![boil fly nest](images/Boil_Fly_Nest.png)
     - (not trackable) look around [[Rot Reek Bog]] {{waypoint -253, 18, -130}} and whole area with pink pools, and also on [[Bog'ta Beach]] along shore from {{waypoint -317, 1, -524}} to {{waypoint -511, -2, -144}}
     - look around rocks and on edges of main paths, kick nests and some will release a swarm of flies you need to click on for update. Not guaranteed from every nest.
   - hunt gloomeye scuttlers for their medicinal purple blood x12 ![gloomeye scuttler](images/A_gloomeye_scuttler.png)
     - kill crabs on [[Bog'ta Beach]], along shore from {{waypoint -317, 1, -524}} to {{waypoint -511, -2, -144}}
   - hunt mirefang corruptors for pest-repellant fur x8 ![mirefang corruptor](images/A_mirefang_corruptor.png)
     - kill big cats in [[Rot Reek Bog]] area, {{waypoint -253, 18, -130}} and whole area with pink pools
1. Return to [[Narezi]]
