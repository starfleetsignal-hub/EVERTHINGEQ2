This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Enter [[A Maj'Dul Residence]].
1. Kill all the mobs.
1. Free the hostage.
1. Return to any Tears grifter for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 23s 29c
- One of the following:
  - 4 [[Blades insignia token|Blades insignia tokens]]
  - 4 [[Coin insignia token|Coin insignia tokens]]
  - 4 [[Truth insignia token|Truth insignia tokens]]
