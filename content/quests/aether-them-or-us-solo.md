## Steps

1. Kill 50 Zakir Rish and their cohorts in areas such as:
   - [[Am Dhahab]] at {{waypoint 854.61, 329.14, 93.26}}
1. Destroy a solo boss. For example:
   - [[Balimon]] in [[Fiddtan Crest]] at {{waypoint -450.59, 277.41, 616.19}}
   - [[Firethrone]] in [[Iron Flats]] at {{waypoint -323.30, -41.37, -441.81}}
   - [[Zox]] in [[Iron Flats]] moving around {{waypoint 110.15, -44.57, -374.35}}

- At least 4p 31g 10s 14c 67200 status
- 3 [[Zimaran Ballad]]
