## Steps

1. Enter [[New Tunaria]].
1. Kill [[Thurindor Felltree]] (65^^^). He will spawn when you get close to {{waypoint -388, 78, -50}} (east of Thex Monument, south of the graveyard) if not already up and wandering around.
1. Return to [[Rooleean Felodaan]] {{waypoint -393, 15, 184}} at the [[Spire Shadow Outpost]] in [[Greater Faydark]].
