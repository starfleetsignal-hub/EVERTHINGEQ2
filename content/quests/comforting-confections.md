## Steps

1. Gather the ingredients for making the candied mushroom tart.
   - Gather 4 *mushrooms* found on the ground.
   - Harvest 4 *sugar crystals* found on the ground.
   - ***Note:** Must be at least Artisan level 2 to scrbe recipe.
1. Cook 2 of the candied mushroom tart.
   - Use the fireplace next to Shasta.
1. Return to Shasta.

## Rewards

- Elemental Token
