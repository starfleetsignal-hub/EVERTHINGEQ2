## Steps

![A dimensional rift in the Wu Camp arena.](images/Wucampdimensionalrift.jpg)

1. Head to the Wu camp by BB zone in. {{waypoint 815, 136, -462}} Go up the hill to the arena, where a light forms in the center.
1. Right click on the light and inspect. Four monks will spawn at the corners, only one is targetable (non - aggro ).
1. Defeat all the monks when they become targetable to spawn the main mob. All are 60^^^ heroics. Each of these monks are based on an element, and have differing damage.
   - [[Fist of Earth]] - Extra Physical Damage
   - [[Fist of Water]] - Cold Damage
   - [[Fist of Air]]   - Divine Damage
   - [[Fist of Fire]]  - Fire Damage
1. Defeat the main mob, [[Zetavorash]]. The main mob has bonus Magic Damage which is a knockback, and has the Physical Damage of Earth, bring two healers.
1. Return to Sharina Nybright.

## Faction

- +500 faction with **Nybright Family**
- -500 faction with **Disciples of Wu**
