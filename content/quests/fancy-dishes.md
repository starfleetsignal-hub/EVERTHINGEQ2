## Steps

1. Gather eight slate beetle shells for Mashi Su'Wah of the Hua Mein.
   - Kill 8 [[A slate beetle|slate beetles]] in Kejaan's Rill along the river bed. {{waypoint -185, 235, 3360}}
1. Return to [[Mashi Su'Wah|Mashi]].

- At least 46g
- [[Hua mein cooking pot]] (house item)
- +2,000 faction with **Hua Mein**
