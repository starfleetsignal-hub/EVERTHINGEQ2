## Steps

1. Wash away impurities in the middle wading pool in the village. Click on swirls in the water at {{waypoint 78, 8, 428}}.
1. Return to [[Kaystha]]
1. Enter the healer's ahbat. Speak to [[Healer Jaanti]] {{waypoint 25, 12, 410}}
   - Gather 6 **cardamomum** (a small leafy shrub) in [[Natim Marshlands]]. Some possible locations:![Cardamomum Plant](images/Cardamom.png)
     - {{waypoint 333, 2, 236}}
     - {{waypoint 254, 3, 335}}
     - {{waypoint 332, 3, 289}}
     - {{waypoint 362, 2, 227}}
     - {{waypoint 371, 2, 242}}
     - {{waypoint 262, 2, 201}}
     - {{waypoint 251, 2, 188}}
   - Gather 7 **bhasma zinc** (small green crystals) along the the cliffs of [[Stone Spine Peninsula]] - along the base, and at the cliff-edges up-top. Some possible locations:![Bhasma Zinc](images/Bhasmazinc.png)
     - {{waypoint 407, 5, 110}}
     - {{waypoint 385, 7, 81}}
     - {{waypoint 453, 37, 95}}
     - {{waypoint 441, 62, 55}}
     - {{waypoint 493, 57, 117}}
     - {{waypoint 245, 54, 35}}
     - {{waypoint 271, 52, 34}}
1. Return to [[Healer Jaanti]]
