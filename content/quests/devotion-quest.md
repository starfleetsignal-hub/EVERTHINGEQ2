## Steps

1. Pray twice a day, once during game-day (6:00 am) and once during game-night (8:00 pm), at an urn inside the [[Temple of Growth]]. {{waypoint -449, 49, -857}}
   - The praying urns will only spawn when the guards in the temple are present. This means that you must either sneak inside and pray quickly or pray while fighting. **Do not kill the guards!**
1. Return to the the altar in the [[Tower of Devotion]]. {{waypoint -126, 73, -757}}
1. Inspect four tombs within the cemetery. {{waypoint -447, 69, -150}}
1. Return to the the altar in the Tower of Devotion.
1. Harvest a flower outside the Temple of Growth. {{waypoint -450, 47, -876}}
1. Use one of the three vases at the base of the [[Thex Castle]] stairs. {{waypoint -86, 66, -314}}

## Rewards

- At least 9g 85s 50c

  - [[Signet of the Devoted]]
  - [[Bracelet of Devotion]]
