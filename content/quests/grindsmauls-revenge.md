This quest is repeatable., as long as the guide is there to give and complete it.

## Starting the Quest

![Event as it appears in the quest journal](images/Grindsmaul's_Revenge!.jpg)
For this quest, the guide (disguised as a ghostly Orc named Grindsmaul, without the [[Guides of the Shattered Land]] guild tag) will roleplay how he was cast out of the [[Crushbone sentry|Crushbone sentries]] for something he did not do, and now he wants revenge.  He will then offer you the quest.

## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[Crushbone sentry|Crushbone sentries]] on [[Orc Hill]].
   - *Although this quest is marked 'Heroic' in your journal, the mobs are solo.*
1. Return to Grindsmaul.
