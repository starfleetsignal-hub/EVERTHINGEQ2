Uko gives you another kill quest that he was told to do by his masters.  This time he hints about some sort of reward. Uko can be found by taking one of the horse stations to the Lower Grove Station.

## Steps

1. Kill 10 Grovespirits.  They can be found in and around [[Tunare's Grove]] {{waypoint -92, -53, -230}}.
1. Return to [[Uko]]

## Rewards

- At least 13g 59s 50c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Cloak of the Faydark Druid]] (All Priests)
  - [[Cloak of the Fayhunter]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Cloak of the Forest Fist]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Cloak of the Natural Caster]] (All Mages)
