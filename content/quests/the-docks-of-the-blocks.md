## Prerequisite

- You must be at least level 20 to start this quest.

## Steps

1. Speak with the griffon trainer, [[Chelzyk Gi'rul]] {{waypoint 2351, 6, 1407}} on the Gorowyn docks and fly to the [[Butcherblock Mountains]]. Or just just the nearby [[Mariner's Bell]].
1. Speak with [[Hulis Glettlegear]] {{waypoint 633, 26, 600}} on the [[Butcherblock Docks]].

## Rewards

- At least 6s 77c
