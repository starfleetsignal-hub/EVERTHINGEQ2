## Steps

1. Speak with [[Elder Su'Linh]] {{waypoint -517, 294, 3083}} about how to dispose of the body.
1. Find and burn the body. {{waypoint 268, 126, 2749}} The Corpse is  on the path Just in front of Tox's Lair.
   - *This is timed.* You have five minutes to complete this step. The location of the corpse is shown as blue dot on the map.
     - You might choose to evac and fly to the [[Toxxulia Forest]] station and then run to the body.
     - Or take your fastest mount and head down the ramp from the caves and go northwest to {{waypoint -220, 252, 2811}} where you can run straight up the cliff wall and then jump west down into [[Toxxulia Forest]] pretty close to the body location. When you jump off the cliff, it would be a good idea to be wearing some type of featherfall cloak, so that you can land in the forest and not fall into open air.
     - Or head towards north end of Hua Mein village, jump up on the rocks and over the cliff, be there in 30 seconds.
     - Or jump off the flying transporter near to where the body is marked on the map. Try that one. ![Burn the Hua Mein body](images/A_Visual_Emergency_quest_-_body.jpg)
1. Inform [[Bai'Yun]] that the deed was done.

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +2000 faction with **Hua Mein**
