## Steps

1. Go to [[The Barracks of Bathezid]] in [[Chardok]] and obtain the following: {{waypoint -330, -18, -520}}
   - 3 [[Bathezid Brigade parade cape|Bathezid Brigade parade capes]] from Bathezid Brigade troops (chest drop).
   - 3 [[Bathezid Brigade parade tunic|Bathezid Brigade parade tunics]] from Bathezid Brigade troops (chest drop).
   - 3 [[Bathezid Brigade parade lance|Bathezid Brigade parade lances]] from Bathezid Brigade troops (chest drop).
   - *Bathezid Brigade patrol manifest* by killing [[Warlord Shrez'Nid]], who spawns in the middle of the barracks. {{waypoint -331, -21, -538}}
   - [[A Bathezid gate crystal]] from [[A Bathezid gatekeeper]].
1. Return to [[Warlord Gadar Gwalnax]] in [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]].

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Gwalnax Brigade**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Bathezid Brigade Helm]]
  - [[Sokohide Cuirass]]
  - [[Tsinisite Robes]]
