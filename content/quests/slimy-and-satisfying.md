## Steps

1. Hunt the following
   - Hunt Prickly Oozelings all along the southern beaches from  around {{waypoint 35, 4, 700}} to {{waypoint 310, 6, 768}}
   - Hunt Octo-wanderers in the water in Erud's Crossing south of where you kill the Oozelings, around {{waypoint 75, 3, 833}}
1. Return to [[Dhibba Mossflock]] at {{waypoint -7, 6, 371}}

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +3,000 faction with **Port Woe**
