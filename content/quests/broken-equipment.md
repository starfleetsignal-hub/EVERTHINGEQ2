*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Collect an essence injector by examining one of the crates outside the houses south of [[The Ruins of Val`marr]]{{waypoint 473, -56, 409}}.
1. Collect 6 [[Undying Essence]] from various skeletons around the [[The Ruins of Val`marr]] ([[A fallen guardian|fallen guardians]], [[A fallen crusader|crusaders]], [[A fallen apostle|apostles]], [[A fallen diplomat|diplomats]] and [[A fallen disciple|disciples]] all drop essences).
1. Activate the sentries, which appear as ghostly blue pieces of equipment in various locations around the outskirts of the ruins.
   - western {{waypoint 593, -48, 290}}
   - south-west {{waypoint 557, -56, 368}}
   - south-east {{waypoint 357, -47, 377}}
   - eastern {{waypoint 357, -46, 319}}
   - north-east {{waypoint 341, -47, 176}}
   - north-west {{waypoint 590, -47, 162}}
1. Return to Tecera.

## Rewards

- At least 10s 18c
- +100 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
