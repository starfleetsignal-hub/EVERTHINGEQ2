"The spirit of Sir Valinayle beckoned me as I began to enter the halls of Stormhold. He mentioned that he was waiting for me. With the promise of treasure, I've agreed to seek out the vengeance Sir Valinayle is unable to distribute."  *--Everquest II Journal*

1. Slay 12 [[A scorched skeleton|scorched skeletons]] found on the first level at  {{waypoint 83, 5, -150}}
