## Steps

![River Camellias!(Northfield Corn)(images/Northfield_Corn.png)!(Hilltop Walnuts)(images/Hilltop_Walnuts.png)](images/River_Camellias.png)

1. Travel to [[Rivervale]] and gather
   1. River camellias along the [[Valeflow River]] -- around {{waypoint -618.98, 0.00, -314.55}} or {{waypoint -504.25, 0.0, -254.55}}
   1. Northfield corn -- bag on the ground at [[Shakey's Farm]] {{waypoint -331.51, 0.15, -317.21}}
   1. Hilltop walnuts -- scattered around [[West Rivervale]] {{waypoint -34.79, -10.79, -182.50}}
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].
