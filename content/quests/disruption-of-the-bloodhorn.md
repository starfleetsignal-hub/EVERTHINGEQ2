## Description

[[Watchman Fleep (NPC)|Watchman Fleep]] would like me to take care of some Bloodhorn Minotaurs.  The Bloodhorn Minotaurs are located east of the Gnomeland Security HQ, towards [[The Mines of Meldrath]].

## Steps

1. Kill the following around {{waypoint -787.75, 92.33, 1094.91}}in no particular order:
   - Kill 6 [[A Bloodhorn enslaver|Bloodhorn enslaver]]s
   - Kill 6 [[A Bloodhorn hunter|Bloodhorn hunter]]s
1. Return to [[Watchman Fleep (NPC)|Watchman Fleep]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Pulsing Gem of Fortitude]]
- At least 2g 69s 57c
