## Steps

1. Collect 3 pieces of spirit essence (random updates from kills):
   - One from the marsh elementals in the Tears of Tunare
   - One from any of the corrupted sifaye in the Tears of Tunare
   - One from any of the holgresh in the Valley of the Fallen
1. Return to the strange spirit
1. Defeat the spawned [[An unstable spirit|unstable spirit]]
1. Speak to the now rejuvenated spirit

## Rewards

- At least 32g 89s 85c
