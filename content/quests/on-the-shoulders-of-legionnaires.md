## Steps

1. Slay [[A Crushbone legionnaire|Crushbone legionnaires]] in [[Crushbone Keep]] until you find 10 shoulderpads. (*See notes below*)
1. Return to [[Elonca Nusback]]

## Rewards

- Experience
- +100 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
- One of the following:
  - [[Crushbone Legionnaire Mantle]] *(Chain Armor)*
  - [[Crushbone Legionnaire Pauldrons]] *(Plate Armor)*
  - [[Crushbone Legionnaire Shoulderpads]] *(Leather Armor)*
  - [[Crushbone Legionnaire Sleeves]] *(Cloth Armor)*
  - [[Crushbone Legionnaire Wraps]] *(Leather Armor)*

## Notes

- The legionnaires in [[The D'Vinnian Throne]] won't work for this quest.
- None found in cellar or ground floor. (Edit: as of 2026 july 13, most of the legionnaires are found near the torture chambers in the basement of Crushbone Keep. I got 2 shoulderpads killing all the NPC on the high floors and the rest of the required items in a couple of very fast spawn cycles downstairs. Tracking helps a lot).
- Found two in room at {{waypoint 12, 25, -43}} [[Hall of Tactics]] .
- Two in the corridor at {{waypoint 17, 235, 64}}
- One at {{waypoint 16, 25, 46}} and four in the room behind him.
