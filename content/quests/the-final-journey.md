## Steps

1. Zone into the [[Sundered Splitpaw: Upper Tunnels]] from the Thundering Stepps.
1. Follow the path to the “T” at the cliff. Turn left.
1. From the cliff, use feather fall to drop down into the pit below.
1. Examine the pile of bones ( 6, -44, -55 ) to accept the quest.
1. Exit the area using the tunnel at ( -55, -44, -84 )
1. Jump up onto the crate at ( -120, -9, -127 ) and grab an exploring barrel. Place it at the next crumbling wall ( -65, -15, -163 ) and detonate it.
1. Enter the next room and head to the movable plank ( -1.90, -3.92, -181.54 ). Pick it up and place it across the chasm. DO NOT cross yet.
1. Go to ( -2, -3, -165 ) and pick up an explosive barrel from off the platform. Carry this barrel with you as you cross the plant and jump down.
1. Place the explosive barrel at the crumbling wall ( 40, -5, -192 ) and detonate it.
1. Enter the room and turn right. Follow the path to the Lower Tunnels. ( 40, -31, -253 ).
1. Click to enter the lower tunnels to enter the **Splitpaw Den**.
1. Place the bones at the shrine ( 21, -68, -177 )

NOTE:  You can get back to the Upper Tunnels OR The Thundering Stepps at ( -17, -69, -156 )

## Rewards
