## Steps

1. Enter the Throne of Storms and spy on the meeting between King Tormax and his subordinates without getting caught. (You get a hint message ingame when you found a good spot, stay and listen until you receive a quest update)
1. Kill Allectus Prime Karsgard.
1. Report back to Cameron Barclay.
