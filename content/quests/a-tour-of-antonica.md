## Notes

- Does not count towards the [[Agent of Antonica]] achievement.

## Steps

Visit the following locations in [[Antonica]] (in no particular order):

- [[Crater Pond]]:  {{waypoint 85, -24, 502}}
- [[Coldwind Point]]: {{waypoint 339, -16, 761}}
- [[Fippy's Hill]]: {{waypoint -100, 7, -245}}
- [[Forbidden Isle]]: {{waypoint 281, -28, -655}}; stand directly in front of the bridge.
- [[Keep of the Ardent Needle (POI)|Keep of the Ardent Needle]]: {{waypoint -554, -14, -346}}
- [[The Tower of the Oracles]]: {{waypoint -1033, 14, -629}}
- [[Claymore Monument]]: {{waypoint -469, -1, 540}}; step up to or on the monument.
- [[Archer's Wood]]: {{waypoint -659, -8, 509}}; stand by a large rock on the western side of the largest tree.
- [[The Bells of Vhalen]]: {{waypoint -1300, 38, 45}}; stand near the large bell on the floor of the tower.
- [[Keep of the Gnollslayers]]: {{waypoint -2121, -46, 415}}
- [[Fangbreaker Keep]]: {{waypoint -2036, -14, -488}}
- [[Ruins of Caltorsis]]: {{waypoint -1454, -15, -759}}
- [[Grave of Holly Windstalker]]: {{waypoint -1854, -3, -622}}
- [[Hidden Vale (Antonica)|Hidden Vale]]: {{waypoint -2054, -47, 959}}; go to the other side of the pass.
- [[Stormhold]]: {{waypoint -2350, -20, 230}}; near the gates, not inside.
- Speak with [[Librarian Dalmas]] at [[The Concordium Tower]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District|Qeynos]] at {{waypoint 713, 41, 101}}. Take the red teleporter and Dalmas is located underneath the stairs.

## Rewards

- [[Bootstrutter's Adventure Pack]]
- At least 9s
