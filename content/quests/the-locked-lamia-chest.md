## Steps

1. Hunt for the creatures that may be carrying one of three keys needed to open the lamia chest.
   - Keys are dropped by killing [[A lamia carver|lamia carvers]] on the coast just outside [[The Glittering Mine]].
1. Once you have the keys, return to the chest.

## Rewards

- At least 99s 67c
