## Steps

1. Kill 12 drakes - [[An azure drake|azure drakes]], [[A squalburst drake|squalburst drakes]] and [[A thundermaw drake|thundermaw drakes]] update but [[An azure drakeling|azure drakelings]] don't.
1. Return to Raxi Ananta for your reward

## Rewards

- At least 9g 85s 29c
- [[Flickering Gem of Criticals]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Gem of the Heavenly Mage]] (All Mages)
  - [[Gem of the Sky Warrior]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Gem of the Skystalker]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Gem of the Winds]] (All Priests)
