## Steps

1. I need to put together a recipe for a quick, lethal, painless poison from the [[Specification: Plex's Poison]], I received from [[Plex]].
   1. Create and scribe [[Recipe: Plex's Poison]] at an [[Engraved Desk]] (one is in a corner of the main tent in Scarstone)
      - Requires 1 [[White peony tea leaf]]
      - Requires 1 [[Effulgent incense]]
      - Requires 1 [[Parchment (Lethal Contingency)|Parchment]] provided by Plex.
1. I need to brew seven doses of poison for Plex
   1. Scribe and create 7 [[Plex's Poison]] at a [[Chemistry Table]] (one is in a corner of the main tent in Scarstone, another in a smaller sidetend {{waypoint 4313, -821, 443}})
      - Requires 7 [[Matoppie roots]]
      - Requires 7 [[Iridium cluster]]
      - Requires 7 [[Amethyst]]
      - Requires 7 [[Effulgent candle]]
1. I need to give the poison to Plex

## Rewards

- 10 [[A Mysterious Blue Coin]]
- At least 1p 30g
