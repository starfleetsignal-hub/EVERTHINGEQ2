## Steps

1. Create the komodo saddle
   - Scribe the [[Recipe: Wind Komodo Saddle]]
   - Craft a [[Wind Komodo Saddle]] on the loom on Shio's platform with the following materials and fuel:
     - 6 [[Horned leather pelt]]
     - 3 [[Hanging root]]
     - 1 [[Rough lumbered rosewood]]
     - 5 [[Scintillating filament]]
1. Return to Shio
1. Test the saddle on the komodo {{waypoint 698, -164, 262}}

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- [[Bridled Wind Komodo]]
- 6 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- At least 2g 16s 26c
