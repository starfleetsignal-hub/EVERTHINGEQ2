## Steps

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 727.46, 77.35, 696.05}} to enter [[The Vault of Ssraeshza (Solo)|The Vault of Ssraeshza [Solo]]].
1. Proceed through the zone.
   - *Note: Do not kill [[Shade of Vyzh'dra]] before step #3 or the quest will not update.*
   - *Note: See [[The Vault of Ssraeshza (Solo)|The Vault of Ssraeshza [Solo]]] for help proceeding through the zone.*
1. Speak to [[Zictirris]] at {{waypoint -318.77, -161.96, 387.97}}.
1. Move into the room at {{waypoint -263.16, -161.44, 348.15}} for an update
1. Kill [[Shade of Vyzh'dra]]
1. Use* [[A Vigilant Sanctum Key]] to enter [[The Duality's Vigilant Sanctum (Mythic Passage)|The Duality's Vigilant Sanctum [Mythic Passage]]]. (** Examine the key in your inventory for the option to be teleported immediately.*)
   - *Alternately: Travel to [[The Sundered Frontier]] and use the door at {{waypoint 1850.97, -371.87, 3450.79}}.*
1. Speak to [[The Duality]] at {{waypoint 526.30, 130.09, -85.11}}.

## Rewards

- Ability to fly in Blood of Luclin overland zones
- At least 2p 10g 49s 87c 56000 status
- One of the following:
  - [[Disciple of Rhag Axe]]
  - [[Disciple of Rhag Wand]]
  - [[Ssraeshzian Defiler's Strike]]
  - [[Fist of the Ssraeshzian Tormentor]]
  - [[Ssraeshzian Taskmaster's Mace]]
