## Steps

![](images/Seed_Sampling_loose_soil.png)![](images/Seed_Sampling_nut.png)

1. Scribe the [[Othmir Gardening Tools]] recipe and craft the [[Gardener's Rake]] on a Forge , using the following materials:
   - 3 [[Sumac lumber]]
   - 1 [[Titanium ore]]
   - 1 [[Ethereal coal]]
     - It is not entirely obvious, but the firepit next to Livi at {{waypoint -1147, -532, 3390}} is a Forge station.
     - There is now a fuel merchant also next to the forge
     - Remember that the T9 materials needed are not local harvests to DoV, so BYO or buy at Broker NPC around the corner
1. Gather 5 [[Nut|nuts]] from under trees in the wurm area (*Remember: You can use your flute from the last quest to render them inert if you are low level*)
   - Click on the green sparkle patches and wait for a nut to appear, then click the nut
   - Note: The demi-god avatar, Tallon Zek now spawns in this area, so keep an eye out. If spawned, a quick shout to your server's top tier channel should remedy this ;)
1. Return to [[Livi]]

## Rewards

- [[Gardener's Rake]] (house item)
- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +150 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
