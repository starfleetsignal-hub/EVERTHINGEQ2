## Prerequsites

You need to have 2250 faction with the Order of Flame to get this quest offered. (Only need to finish the Crystal Replacement quest)

## Steps

1. Kill 2 [[An invading render|invading renders]] that guard the entrance to the golden skull caves.
1. Investigate void activity inside the following skull caves. They can all be reached through the entrance at {{waypoint 49, -105, 244}}
   - Goto the [[Cave of Molten Cores]] {{waypoint 59, -101, 164}}
   - Goto the [[Cave of Pure Flame]] {{waypoint 23, -120, 196}}
   - Goto the [[Cave of Deepest Lava]] {{waypoint 95, -112, 215}}
1. Return to [[Pyrissa Flamecog]]

## Rewards

- +2250 faction with **Order of Flame**
- At least 1p 40g
- One the following, based on class:
  - [[Rake's Vambraces]]
  - [[Legionnaire's Bracers]]
  - [[Archon's Wristguard]]
  - [[Master's Cuffs]]
  - [[Hierophant's Bands]]
  - [[Prophet's Chain Cuffs]]
  - [[Ascendant's Wrist Wraps]]
