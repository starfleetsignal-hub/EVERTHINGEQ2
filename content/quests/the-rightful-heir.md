## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 80+<br>
You will need to finish your level 80 archetype quest before this quest will be offered to you.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Lachlyn Cauldthorn]] in [[Elddar Grove]], [[Qeynos Province District]]. He is aloft on the platform of [[Arbos, the Elddar Tree]]. {{waypoint 664, 12, -374}}

## Rewards

- At least 25g 70s 61c (at level 92)
- [[Painting: Claymore Plaza Remembered]]

## Notes

- If you have safefall or a "floaty cloak", when you get to the top of Arbos, send the elevator back down, and just jump off when you head out on your next task.  You will be coming back here several times, and having the platform ready speeds things up considerably.
