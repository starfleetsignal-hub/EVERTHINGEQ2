## Steps

1. The gehein need help defending Ashen Tower. I agreed to help slay the ogres assaulting them, as well as repair the spiritual attuners that have been damaged by the ogres.
   - Slay Oggok invaders (8)
   - Collect broken pieces of the spiritual attuners (6) ![](images/Spiriual_attuner.jpg)
1. I need to use the attuner pieces I've collected to repair the spiritual attuners around Ashen Tower.
   - Repair the spiritual attuners at Ashen Tower (4). They are the floating blue orbs at the base of the tower's pillars.
   - {{waypoint -360, -6, 863}}
1. I should report my success to Zeeil.
   - Speak to [[Zeeil]] {{waypoint -331, -7, 839}} at Ashen Tower

- At least 1p 25g
