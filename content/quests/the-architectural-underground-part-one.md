## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[A rabid shriller (Vermin's Snye)|rabid shrillers]]. *([[A rabid shriller (Down Below)|rabid shrillers]] in [[The Down Below]] also work)*
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 6 [[A tomb vermin|tomb vermin]].
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 7 [[A crumbling tomb sentry|crumbling tomb sentries]].
1. Read the book.

## Rewards

- Experience only
- Upon completing all three quests in [[Architecture of the Vermin's Snye (Quest Starter)|Architecture of the Vermin's Snye]], in any order:
