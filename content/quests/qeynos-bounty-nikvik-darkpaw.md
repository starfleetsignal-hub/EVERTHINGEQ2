## Steps

1. Travel to [[Antonica]] and kill [[NikVik Darkpaw]] who will spawn when you approach the entrance of [[Blackburrow]] at {{waypoint -1412, -4, 827}}.
   - If you have trouble getting him to spawn, try running to the west of the Blackburrow entrance close to the wall.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- AA Experience
- +3000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
