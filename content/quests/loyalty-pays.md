## Steps

1. Kill fifteen [[A Blades guard|Blades guards]].
1. Kill fifteen [[A Truth guard|Truth guards]].
1. Head back to [[The Court of the Coin]] and click on the note on a stool next to [[Suliman Abid]].
1. Take the test. The answers are: 1, 4, 2, 2, 1, 3, 4, 3, false, true.

## Rewards

- Suffix title [[Vindicator of the Coin]]
