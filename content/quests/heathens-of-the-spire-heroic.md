## Steps

*The Crusaders of the Greenmist have recently learned there is little to no mention of their god, Cazic Thule, within the Arcanna'se Spire, and have tasked me with bringing fear upon the creatures that guard it. I have accepted!*

- Defeat targets within [[Arcanna'se Spire: Forgotten Sanctum (Heroic)]]
1. Slay the Keeper of Passions
1. Vanquish the Keeper of Pestilence
1. Extinguish the Keeper of Fire
1. Destroy the Keeper of Growth
1. Defeat the Defender of Magics
