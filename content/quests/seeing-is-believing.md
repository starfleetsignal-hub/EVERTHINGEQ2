## Steps

1. Talk to [[Hamish]] {{waypoint 4427, -823, 493}} to get 7 [[Alchemical Lens|Alchemical Lenses]]
1. Create 7 [[Magesight Overlay|Magesight Overlays]] at a [[Work Bench]] {{waypoint 4414, -821, 361}}) with the recipe automatically scribed from Plex
   - Requires 7 [[Effulgent coal]] and 7 [[Iridium cluster|iridium clusters]].
1. Create 7 [[Magesight Spotting Scope|Magesight Spotting Scopes]] at a [[Work Bench]] using the [[Recipe: Magesight Spotting Scope|recipe]] automatically scribed from Plex
   - Requires 7 [[Effulgent coal]] and 7 [[Amethyst|amethysts]].
1. Return the Magesight Spotting Scopes to Plex

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
