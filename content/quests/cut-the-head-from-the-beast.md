## Steps

1. Kill [[Urth Klavik]] in [[The City of Jinisk]]. {{waypoint 648, 36, -124}}
   - -100 faction with **The City of Jinisk**.
1. Return to Sonjaz Manx.

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- +2,000 faction with **Kunzar Jungle Villagers**
- [[Glowing Gem of Criticals]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Haunted Idol of Breathing Sounds]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Orb of Ghostly Cowardice]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Meteoric Orb of Confusion]] (All Priests)
  - [[Traitor's Gem of the Deadly Thinker]] (All Mages)
