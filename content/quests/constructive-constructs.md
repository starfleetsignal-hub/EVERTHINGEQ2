## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[An ice fright|ice frights]] in [[Icewail Caverns]]. {{waypoint -378, -8, -90}}
1. Kill [[A glacial construct|glacial constructs]] in [[Icewail Caverns]] until you have obtained the following quest updates: {{waypoint -378, -8, -90}}
   - glacial construct core
   - glacial construct appendage
   - glacial construct head
1. Return to Baldus.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 20s 65c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Shattered Lands Stalker's Shroud]] (all Scouts)
  - [[Shattered Lands Medium's Shroud]] (all Mages)
  - [[Shattered Lands Healer's Shroud]] (all Priests)
  - [[Shattered Lands Defender's Shroud]] (all Fighters)
