## Steps

1. Researcher Ma'Jok gives you [[An Underfoot elemental leash]] and asks you to obtain a live sample for study.
1. Choose any nearby [[An Underfoot hardstomper|Underfoot hardstomper]], then return to Researcher Ma'Jok.

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Fell Blade**
