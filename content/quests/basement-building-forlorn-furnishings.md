Crafting furniture out of local scavenged materials results in items that frequently need to be replaced or repaired, but Renfry likes them. Scavenge more materials and replace rickety furniture with yet more rickety furniture!

## Note

Upon accepting this quest, you will receive the following:

- [[Illusion: Werewolf]]
- [[Recipe Scroll: Shabby Furniture]] which will automatically scribe the [[Shabby Furniture]] recipe to your recipe book

## Steps

1. I must gather items for Renfry's task.
   - Gather [[Wooden Scrap]] (15)
   - Gather [[Animated Roots]] (30)
   - Gather [[Sturdy Spiderweb]] (30)
     - *Note: To progress this quest, click on the door {{waypoint 0, 0, 29}} in Renfry's Basement to return to [[Forlorn Gist]], then use the [[Illusion: Werewolf]] you received upon accepting the quest to make the items visible and make all mobs non-hostile.  Should you lose or dismiss the illusion, another can be purchased from inside [[Renfry's Basement]]; [[Ziggy]] sells them for 1p.*
1. I must craft the requested items.
   - Create [[Shabby Furniture]] (3)
     - *Note: There is a [[Far Seas Crafting Station]] inside [[Renfry's Basement]] at {{waypoint -363, 16, 78}}*

## Rewards

- At least 9p 30g 45s 97c
- 56000 status at tradeskill level 125
- 2 [[Vetrovian Dres'Aag]]
- [[Vetrovia Crafting Crate]]
