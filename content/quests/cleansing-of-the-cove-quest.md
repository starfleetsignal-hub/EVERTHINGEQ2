## Steps

1. Enter the instance [[Cleansing of the Cove (Zone)|Cleansing of the Cove]], an instance of [[The Trembling Lagoon]], at {{waypoint -902, -9, 315}}.
1. Slay [[The Spectre of the Feerrott]] (level 39-40). He is spawned by proceeding to the final area of the instance and finishing an event at the stone altar. Clear all of the skeletons worshiping at the altar to initiate the event. Note that the normal creatures do respawn, so you must plan around them. If the worshipers are not attackable then proceed to kill every skeleton in the proximity.
1. Return to Mashara or Tarin to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- Unlimited access to the [[Cleansing of the Cove (Zone)|Cleansing of the Cove]] solo instance
- At least 2g 69s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Cunning Mind Effigy]] (All Mages)
  - [[Jagged Ceremonial Dagger]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Orb of Assassin's Greed]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Totem of Cowardice]] (All Priests)
