## Steps

Construct Mage [[Gozzinak]] has asked for heroes, like myself, recruited by [[Yelinak The Ancient|Lord Yelinak]], to bring an end to [[Sevalak of Storms]]'s relentless attack upon Dracur Prime.

- Kill [[Sevalak of Storms]]

## Rewards

- 23g 64s 59c
- Depending on your class, one of:
  - [[Azure Hammer of the Dracurians]]
  - [[Radiant Dracurian Heart Piercer]]
  - [[Radiant Sculpted Mace of the Dracurians]]
  - [[Radiant Dracurian Axe]]
  - [[Dagger of the Dragonkin Generals]]
