## Steps

![the ferocious elemental's body](images/Ferocious_elemental's_body.jpg)

1. Return to [[Kunzar Jungle]] and find a trinket owned by the Wounded of the Earth.
   - The trinket can be found on the [[Ferocious elemental|ferocious elemental's body]] where you killed them. {{waypoint -752, -53, 398}}
1. Return to [[Veryn P'Neru]].
1. Speak with [[Tilzax V'Reryn]] to receive the [[Bite of the Wolf (Fabled)|Bite of the Wolf]] and the next quest (which will upgrade it to the mythical version).

STOP!
If your goal is to have the Item for the [[Epic Conversion Timeline]], you do NOT need to do the RAID version. You can begin that timeline now.
