## Steps

1. Obtain the glowing shard from the crater in [[The Broken Fields]] {{waypoint -495, 120, 362}}
   - [[A Yarpsnarl brute]] will spawn on top of shard as you approach.
   - You need to get into the crater to kill the brute before taking the shard. Otherwise, a brute will spawn after you kill the previous one.
   - It is not necessary to kill the brute.
1. Return to [[Wyrwynne Shadhe]]

## Rewards

- At least 1g 59s 57c
- [[Serilian Coldstone]]
- +4000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
