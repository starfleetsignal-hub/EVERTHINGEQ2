## Notes

- Can be done at the same time as signature line quest [[Rage of Cthurath: Who's Zobboz?]]

## Steps

1. Enter [[Zon Zobboz: The Chimeric Chain (Solo)|Zon Zobboz: The Chimeric Chain [Solo]]]
   - *For the walkthrough see [[Zon Zobboz: The Chimeric Chain (Solo)|Zon Zobboz: The Chimeric Chain [Solo]]]*
1. find and recover the following
   - Find and recover 8 ancient Scrykin Farseer's Stones![Scrykin Farseer's Stone](images/Scrykin_Farseer's_Stone.png)
   - Find and recover 13 Codex Artiface pages![Codex Artiface page](images/Page_of_the_Codex_Artiface_page.png)
   - *Note: Both items spawn on the ground, starting in The Skyvarium (take the teleport up after defeating the second named)*
1. Return to Ozron

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following:
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
  - [[Rageful Symbol of the Umbrith]]
  - [[Rageful Wand of the Umbrith]]
