## Steps

1. Inspect a hammer near the entrance to [[New Tunaria]], in front of the NPC [[Commander Marcus Traeis]]. {{waypoint -1005, 83, 656}}
1. Return to [[Teriniu Featherlight]]. {{waypoint -370, 14, 183}}
1. Enter [[New Tunaria]] and pick up a tiny ring on the ground. {{waypoint -41, 25, -795}}
1. Return to Teriniu Featherlight.
1. Enter New Tunaria and pick up a piece of armor on the ground. {{waypoint -535, 69, -1078}}
1. Return to Teriniu Featherlight.
1. Enter New Tunaria and hail the elf corpse on a rock in the middle of the water. {{waypoint -765, -2, -1202}}
1. Return to Teriniu Featherlight for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 9g 85s 50c
