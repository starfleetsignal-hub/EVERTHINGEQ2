## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The avatar summoning rituals require the manipulation of immense power and are inherently dangerous. To reduce the chance of failure and provide an anchor for that power, the herald of Anashti Sul has asked me to collect an item that may have once belonged to that god or one of their most loyal followers

- Collect [[Reliquary of Anashti Sul|Reliquaries of Anashti Sul]] from a powerful creature in [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma (Event Heroic I)|Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma]] heroic dungeon.

**This does not update in [Solo]. It needs to be Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma [Event Heroic I].**

- Return to the [[Herald of Undeath]] at {{waypoint -2063, -130, -35}} in [[The Sinking Sands]]
