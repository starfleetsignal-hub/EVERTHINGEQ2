## Steps

1. Use the chrono portal {{waypoint 9.45, 1.24, 14.86}} to zone into [[Covenant District]]
1. Acquire the Draconic Disc of Binding Runes. You must complete two subquests from [[Yendilor]] at the entrance within the zone:
   1. [[Proving Your Worth (SkyShrine)|Proving Your Worth]]
   1. [[Marketplace Mayhem]] - this quest will reward the disc
1. Speak to [[Jorlak]] to complete the quest.
