## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[An elder graniteback|elder granitebacks]]{{waypoint -556, 102, 297}} gorillas south of The Quarry.
1. Return to [[Grimshaw Packwright]].
1. [[Grimshaw Packwright]] sends you to [[Paix Hormezyar]] in [[West Fort Irontoe]].

## Rewards

- At least 75s 48c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Amulet of Never-ending Skirmish]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Spiroc Feather Ritual Charm]] (All Priests)
  - [[Spry Spiroc Foot]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Trinket of Dwarven Tradition]] (All Mages)
