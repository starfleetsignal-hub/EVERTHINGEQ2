## Steps

1. Protect Boulden Smitestone. Keep the mobs away from him by slaying waves of [[An undying Rallosian soldier|undying Rallosian soldiers]], [[An undying Rallosian priests|undying Rallosian priests]] and [[An undying Rallosian magi|undying Rallosian magis]].
1. Eventually the [[A Rallosian Captain]] will spawn. Kill him to complete the quest.
1. To exit the boat in [[The Feerrott]] you must wait until [[Boulden Smitestone (The QSS Bootstrutter)|Boulden Smitestone]] is finished casting. Continue to kill the spawning soldiers until he is done, then speak with him to leave.
   - Alternatively, if you do not want to wait until Boulden is finished casting, you can attack him. Killing him will return you to [[The Thundering Steppes]] docks. Make sure you have completed the quest before doing this, however.
1. After the quest is complete, in order to receive your reward, go to [[The Thundering Steppes]] and speak to [[Scholar Milnik]] on the docks, at {{waypoint -550, -16.45, -430}}.

## Rewards

- At least 96s 67c
- Speak to [[Scholar Milnik]] on the [[Dead River Docks]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]] to receive a little more experience and one of the following:
  - [[Greatstaff of the Sightless]]
  - [[Rod of the Sightless]]
  - [[Shredder of the Sightless]]
  - [[War Axe of the Sightless]]
