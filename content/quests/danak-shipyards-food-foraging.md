## Steps

1. Harvest 25 cranberries, torsis tea leaves, cabilis cocoa beans, succulent petals, king prawns, or barracuda (fish or bush nodes)
   - There are several fishing nodes on the beach outside of quest givers' hut
1. Return to Assistant Sziths

## Rewards

- Tradeskill Experience
- At least 17g 22s 36c
- +2000 faction with **Legion of Danak**
