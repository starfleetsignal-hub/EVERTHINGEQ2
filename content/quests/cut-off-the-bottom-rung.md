## Notes

- You cannot obtain this quest if you have any active Tabernacle of Pain quests.

## Steps

1. Kill the novice monks of [[The Tabernacle of Pain]]: {{waypoint -101, -57, 111}}
   - 8 [[A Clay shackle monk|Clay shackle monk]]s
   - 8 [[A Stone shackle monk|Stone shackle monk]]s
1. Return to Havik Thyx.

- At least 1p 5g
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Band of the Kunzar Roamer]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Loop of the Kunzar Roamer]]
  - [[Ring of the Kunzar Roamer]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Ringlet of the Kunzar Roamer]]
