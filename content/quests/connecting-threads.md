The Quellithulians believe that there are breaks within the lines of mystical energy that thread together the Ulteran Spire network. I must seek out these breaks in the lines, and repair them using the device given to me.

## Starting the Quest

This quest remains the same regardless of the zone you start in. The only difference is the person you speak with to start the quest:

- Talk to [[Betanli Illya]] in [[Antonica]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (Ant)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -2077, 13, -625}}
- Talk to [[Dadeem Sam'dlu]] in [[The Commonlands]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (CL)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -7, -48, -427}}
- Talk to [[Zawfeli Aull'bin]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (TS)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint 1167, -26, 171}}
- Talk to [[Ialanian El'vadiv]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (Nek)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -349, 2, -641}}
- Talk to [[Dalahmia Ja'evl]] in [[Enchanted Lands]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (EL)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -394, -2, 85}}
- Talk to [[Krahl Olswain]] in [[The Feerrott]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (Feer)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -77, 13, 167}}

## Steps

![](images/EtherealThreads.jpg)

1. Connect the broken ethereal threads by using the [[Ethereal Mender]] on a torn and a severed thread.
   - There are four updates and for each you must have used the [[Ethereal Mender]] on both a torn ethereal thread and a severed ethereal thread.
   - See talk page for thread locations.
   - The threads respawn quickly and can be reused.
1. Return to one of the six NPCs for your reward.

## Rewards

- 5 [[Quellithulian Token of Appreciation]]
