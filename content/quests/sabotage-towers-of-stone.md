## Steps

1. Destroy 5 piles of rocks Freeport is using to rebuild their towers. Rocks look like piles of slate and are  located along the walls of Freeport.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -1000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -1000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
