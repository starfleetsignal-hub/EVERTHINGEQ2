## Steps

1. Enter the gate to the [[Baubbleshire: Vale of Shadow|Baubbleshire]] from [[Elddar Grove]]. {{waypoint 816, -23, -530}}
1. Rescue the trapped gnomes and halflings within the Baubbleshire.
   - [[Dondl Fuzzlecutter]] {{waypoint 883, -13, -440}}
   - [[Yola Sindlefop]] {{waypoint 809, -4, -467}}
   - [[Almin Jumroller]] {{waypoint 909, -17, -476}}
   - [[Algan Tinmizer]] {{waypoint 859, -12, -417}}
   - [[Lyza Bethan]] {{waypoint 895, -15, -531}}
1. Speak to [[Rohn Tinkerton]] just inside the Baubbleshire gate. {{waypoint 827, -19, -512}}
1. Take the new and improved [[Clockwork E.C.U.]] to 5 of the void portals and stay by each until it has absorbed the portal's energy.
1. Return to [[Rohn Tinkerton]].
1. Return to [[Elddar Grove]] and speak to [[Bungle Proudfoot]]. {{waypoint 693, -14, -420}}

## Rewards

- At least 22s 7c (at level 25)
- [[Ring of the Halfling]]

## Notes

Nothing in the zone sees invis, and you can do much of this quest while invisible.
