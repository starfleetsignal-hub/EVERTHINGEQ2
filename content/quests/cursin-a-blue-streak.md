## Steps

1. Enter [[Sebilis]] and use the [[Fulmination Scepter]] on 8 'sathirian scrolls' in the [[Laboratory and Study]]. {{waypoint -298, -22, 330}}
1. Return to [[Baron Terrask]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 5g
- +5,000 faction with **Outer Sebilis Residents**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Droag Slayer's Helm]]
  - [[Gloves of Thievery]]
  - [[Jade-inlaid Greaves]]
  - [[Smoldering Black Book]]
