## Notes

From the Great Library go to the North Stacks and {{waypoint 710, 412, -368}} to access the portal.

Crosswinds Isle at {{waypoint 352, 181, -261}} has portals to the four main islands and is useful for saving some travel time

- You can fly direct to the [[Precipice of Wind]], or use the portal to [[Crosswind Isle]] at {{waypoint 142.74, -178.80, -820.62}}, then take the portal to the [[Precipice of Wind]] from there {{waypoint 352.24, 180.62, -213.94}}

## Steps

1. Gather aethersteel keys on the [[Precipice of Wind]]  {{waypoint -441, -478.15, 953.07}}
   - Gather 4 aethersteel keys (they look like ghostly dragonfly wings)![Aethersteel Key](images/Aethersteel_Key.png)
   - Steal 6 aethersteel keys from efreeti windlords (uncommon update)
   - Steal 6 aethersteel keys from stormriders
1. Go to [[The Bixel Hive]] (take the portal back to [[Crosswind Isle]], then use the portal there at {{waypoint 316.88, 180.62, -249.09}}).
1. Open 7 aethersteel chests in search of the lamp.![Aethersteel Chest](images/Aethersteel_Chest.jpg)Note that the bixels don't see invisibililty. Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint -425, 435, -180}}
   - {{waypoint -339, 449, -175}}
   - {{waypoint -478, 436, -95}}
   - {{waypoint -589, 512, -97}}
   - {{waypoint -574, 583, -263}}
   - {{waypoint -648, 581, -108}}
   - {{waypoint -604, 574, -103}}
   - {{waypoint -463, 580, -46}}
   - {{waypoint -424, 641, -187}}
   - {{waypoint -432, 504, -238}}
   - {{waypoint -533, 525, -120}}
1. Open 1 more aethersteel chest and destroy the vase.
   - Open the aethersteel chest on to the Queen's Platform at {{waypoint -528, 642, -136}} and break a vase that comes out of the chest
1. Return to [[Nidinoff]] by the [[Misty Falls]] at {{waypoint 249, -222, -1187}}.
1. Return to [[Rouree]] in [[The Windglades]] at {{waypoint 505, -202, -1398}}.
1. Click on **Rouree's Urn**![Rouree's Urn](images/Rourees_Urn.jpg) at {{waypoint 501, -202, -1396}}.
