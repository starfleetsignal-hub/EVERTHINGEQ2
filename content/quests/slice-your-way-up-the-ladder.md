## Notes

- You cannot obtain this quest if you have any active Tabernacle of Pain quests.

## Steps

1. Kill the higher rank monks of [[The Tabernacle of Pain]]: {{waypoint -144, -77, 241}}
   - 4 [[A Rock shackle monk|Rock shackle monk]]s
   - 3 [[A Copper shackle monk|Copper shackle monk]]s
   - 2 [[A Bronze shackle monk|Bronze shackle monk]]s
   - 1 [[A Steel shackle monk|Steel shackle monk]]
1. Return to Havik Thyx.
