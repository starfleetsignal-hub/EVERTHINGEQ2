## Steps

1. Speak to each of the 6 chieftains at the surrounding camps. Just look for the quest books over their heads. You can see them all from where [[Bronlor Stormhammer]] stands.
   - [[Clink Fizzclang]] - Gnomish Chieftain
   - [[Seamus Rockpounder]] - Dwarven Chieftain
   - [[Liekvig the Defender]] - Skindancer Chieftain
   - [[Varnax the Destroyer]] - Underpaw Chieftain
   - [[Pleep Tesch Val]] - Splitpaw Chieftain
   - [[Gnawtoe the Battler]] - Darkpaw Chieftain
1. Collect 20 wolf or other canine meat for the Splitpaw Gnolls. Surprisingly, the Darkpaw gnolls ([[Darkpaw youth|a Darkpaw youth]], [[Sabretooth runt|a Sabretooth runt]], etc.) outside the camps work just fine for this. Even [[Grolven Chiptooth]] counted.
1. Give the meat to the Splitpaw chief, [[Pleep Tesch Val]], and talk to Bronlor Stormhammer again.
1. Find the thief that stole the ritual tablet!
   1. Enter [[Blackburrow]]. Just inside the door, release the [[Darkpaw Sniffer]] (look in your bags. It is a Gnoll's head. Use it)
   1. Follow the sniffer around. He will lose the scent several times. Pick it back up at 1) the tunnel down, 2) the bridge, 3) the ramp down past bridge, and 4) ramp down to spider area. He loses it for the final time at the water's edge.
   1. Swim to the [[Blackburrow|Blackburrow Brewery]] and, at the back of the room, find a ratonga named [[Pestario Norvegicus]]. Hail him and get the tablet and a written apology, then he ports.
1. Take the tablet and apology back to Bronlor Stormhammer for your reward.
