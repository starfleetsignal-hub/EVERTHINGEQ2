## Steps

1. Find 6 artifacts in [[Dire Hollow: Barrow Ruins]] {{waypoint 500, 2, -1140}}:
   - The Rubicund Disc drops from [[Feyd the Slayer]]. {{waypoint 79, -15, 30}}
   - Feyd guards the ***Pylon of N'Aeth***. {{waypoint 78, -15, 29}}
   - The Tenebrous Globe drops from [[Blaesra the Nightwalker]]. {{waypoint 76, -7, 98}}
   - Blaesra guards the ***Tablet of V'Hriz***. {{waypoint 79, -7, 101}}
   - The Brazier of Black Flame drops from [[Tufar the Mangler]]. {{waypoint 7, -16, 58}}
   - Tufar guards the ***Shattered Plinth***. {{waypoint 9, -14, 82}}
1. Kill [[Varo Z'Kaz]] {{waypoint 44, -10, 67}}-- Be careful not to kill before all the other updates! :)
   - If the zone is grey, go to zone entrance, but don't leave, then return to [[Varo Z'Kaz]] {{waypoint 44, -10, 67}} to spawn him and his companions.
1. Return to [[Aleeshi Monala]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 5g 69s 57c
- One of the following spells or skills:
  - [[Corpsecrush]] (fighters)
  - [[Sacred Shield]] (priests)
  - [[Sunbolt]] (mages)
  - [[Dreadfang]] (scouts)
