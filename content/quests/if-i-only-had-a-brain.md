## Notes

[[Sarkan]] appears in camp on [[Nightscar Steppes]] when you complete [[Ward Removal]] quest from [[Vadreem]]. This quest, Vadreem's [[Portal Seeker]] and Derlak's [[Sacrilege Remediation]] can be done at the same time in [[Oblivion's Bask]] area

## Steps

In search of Incursion Dominax on [[Oblivion's Bask]] plateau {{waypoint -49, 136, -234}} you must:

1. kill ebonlithe lamias until Incursion Dominax appears - this can take 10 or so kills before quest progresses.
1. find and slay the Incursion Dominax on [[Oblivion's Bask]] - this is Vasynea (trackable) at {{waypoint 41, 157, 344}}. She activates as you approach.
1. Return to Sarkan in [[Nightscar Steppes]] camp (he uses Vasynea's head that you brought, to take her shape)
