## Notes

- requires 50,000 [[Pride Pakiat (Faction)]]

## Steps

1. Travel to the west coastline of [[Aetherscar]] {{waypoint 941, 12, -148}}. Even though quest journal does not reference her, speak to [[Badalsonu]] *FIRST,* then with [[Kalisonu]]. Receive [[Kalisonu's Beacon]].
- note: neither NPC will have book or quest feather.
1. Return to [[Khat-Ra Pakiat]] and speak to her. She will offer to teleport you to the meeting area, when you are ready.
1. Place the beacon at {{waypoint -967, 73, -50}} and listen to the conversation.
1. Speak to [[Khat-Ra Pakiat]] after returning to the [[Plane of Magic]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Celestial Ascension]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Pride Pakiat Field Bow]]
  - [[Pride Pakiat Augury Wand]]
  - [[Pride Pakiat Field Buckler]]
  - [[Pride Pakiat Augury Symbol]]
  - [[Pride Pakiat Reinforced Towerguard]]
  - [[Pouch of Aetherscar Shards]]
