- +10,000 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**

## Steps

1. Kill 2 [[Cackling cannoneer|cackling cannoneers]]
1. Free 2 [[Cannoneer prisoner|cannoneer prisoners]] who are kept in the barrels near the cannons.
1. Return to [[Shulgi]].
