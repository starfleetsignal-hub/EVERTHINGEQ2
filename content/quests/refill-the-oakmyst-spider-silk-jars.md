## Steps

1. Speak to [[Varion Smitelin]] {{waypoint 716.89, 48.32, 102.31}} at [[The Concordium Tower]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]].
1. Travel to [[Oakmyst Forest]].
1. Slay ten [[An Oakmyst spider|Oakmyst spiders]] and collect their silk. {{waypoint 943, 3, -226}}
1. Return to [[Varion Smitelin]].

## Rewards

- [[Oakmyst spider silk gloves]]
- At least 3s 62c
- +65 faction with **The Concordium**
