1. Speak with [[Inayat Wijdani]] {{waypoint 1651, -297, 3161}} in the [[Hall of Necromancy]] in [[The City of Paineel]].
1. Return to Fazia.  Use the teleporter slightly NE to get to Paineel and back quickly.
