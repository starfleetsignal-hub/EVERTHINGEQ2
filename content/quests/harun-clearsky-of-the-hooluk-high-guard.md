## Notes

- The islands that [[Harun Clearsky|Harun]] will send you to are in the same order as the islands for [[Criggle Cloudpuff]]'s quests. It is convenient to run both questlines in synch.

## Steps

1. Go to [[Strifewind Pond]] and harvest the reeds there to receive [[Hardened Pipe Reed]]. {{waypoint 152, -21, -490}}
1. Go to {{waypoint -557.18, -46.50, -395.53}} on the [[The Whisperwind Isle]] and use the Hardened Reed Pipe from your inventory.
1. Speak to [[Harun Clearsky]] when he arrives after a short flight.

## Rewards

- +75 faction with **The Windgazer**
