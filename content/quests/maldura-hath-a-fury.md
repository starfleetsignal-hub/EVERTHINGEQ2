Tyshgwyn the Fury, a warrior of significant standing amongst the people of Maldura, and leader of a special force of fighters, has asked for some ore gorgelets from Stoneheart Yards, and some cepholex trophies. I can do that!

## Steps

1. Collect ore and tentacles
   - Collect 8 [[A cepholex|cepholex]] tentacles near any body of water connected to the [[The Silent Deep|Silent Deep]], for example around [[Blackanvil Bay]] {{waypoint 865.45, 44.73, -43.68}}.
     - Kill 8 [[A cepholex|cepholex]] for the updates.
   - Collect 12 **ore gorgelets** in [[Stoneheart Yards]] {{waypoint 582.11, 65.57, -148.95}}.
     - Kill [[An ore gorger|ore gorgers]] to spawn the gorgelets and collect those.
1. Return to [[Tyshgwyn the Fury|Tyshgwyn]].

![ore gorgelet](images/Ore_gorgelet.png)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 12854 status
- +1000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
