## Steps

1. Use the Enigmatic Portal Opening Device in the Alcove of Flame at {{waypoint 91, 3, -139}}.
1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 95, 3, -149}} to enter [[The Molten Throne]].
1. Speak to [[Druzzil Ro]].
1. Continue to the ramp to trigger the cut scene.
   - You can escape out of the cut scene early, but you'll still have to wait for the NPCs to get into position.
1. Kill all of the Sullonites.
1. Kill [[The Blood Primordial]]
   - Drag him into the bone cages that come out of the white circles to stun him, don't step into them yourself or you will be rooted for a short time.
   - He has a very short leash range. He breaks if you move too far back down the ramp.
   - Periodically through the fight you will get knocked back. Control your fall to stay on the ramp.
1. Go up the ramp and activate the Dresolik (red floating crystal) at {{waypoint -6, 26, 176}}.
1. Enjoy the fireworks and wait to be ported.
1. Speak to [[Druzzil Ro]].
