## Prerequirement

- Need to be able to speak [[Goblish]]

## Steps

1. Speak with Turklig at {{waypoint -95, -194, -568}}
1. Go to camp at {{waypoint -194, -146, -435}}
1. Search the Area for clues
   - click on "a goblin skull"
   - click on the "wrapped bloodstained bones"
   - click on "a set of strange vials"
1. Return to Turklig at {{waypoint -95, -194, -568}}
1. Travel to {{waypoint -5, -203, -427}} and click corpse for update
1. Kill [[A Kromdul scavenger]] that spawns to update the quest
1. Kill 3 [[A kromdul forest giant|kromdul forest giant]]s
1. Return to dead bodies {{waypoint -5, -203, -427}}  and talk to wounded gatherer
1. Talk to Turklig near the wounded gatherer at {{waypoint -0.38, -204.85, -430.02}}
1. Speak with someone in Twark: Talk to Mallic the Venerable across the Twark bridge at {{waypoint 170, -204, -522}} to complete quest

## Reward

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 166400 status
