## Notes

You only have to kill two for the quest and the respawn rates are very quick.

## Steps

1. Sergeant Daimyo sends you to gate to Longshadow Alley in North Freeport to eliminate street toughs at {{waypoint -56, -20, -325}} and {{waypoint -101, -33, -329}}
   - These [[NPC|NPCs]] will spawn on a ledge.
   - Hail the street toughs to begin battle.
1. Return to Sergeant Daimyo at {{waypoint -135, -37, -1}} and he will give you the next quest.
