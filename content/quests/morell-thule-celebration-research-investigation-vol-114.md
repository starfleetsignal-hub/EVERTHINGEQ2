## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Morell-Thule has informed me that summoning an Avatar requires the land to be attuned to their element. To achieve this, altars have been set up around the region, but they need to be supplied with power. I have been asked to assist with this by collecting objects infused with the essence of slain foes and offer them to an altar.

1. Collect 3 [[Infused Dream Shard|infused dream shards]] (random body drop from mobs in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]], confirmed drops from Floral Devourers and Beetle Crabs)
1. Use the [[Infused Dream Shard|infused dream shards]] and empower an altar.  Random spawn in The Thundering Steppes, its yellow sparkles visible far away, possible locations of altar
   - {{waypoint 376, 13, -534}} near [[Antonican Road]], next to one of the towers
   - {{waypoint 590, 0, -380}} up the road from [[The Mirror]]
   - {{waypoint 666, 1, -205}}
   - {{waypoint 184, -1, 606}}
   - {{waypoint 426, -1, 900}} east of [[Crater Mine]], near the start of the road
   - {{waypoint 120, 0, -90}} near [[Bridge Keep]]
   - {{waypoint 1212, -2, -20}}
   - {{waypoint -245, 0, 53}} along [[Dead River Road]]
   - {{waypoint -160, -1, 808}}
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Lord of Dreams]] {{waypoint 1097, -1, 646}} in [[The Thundering Steppes]]
