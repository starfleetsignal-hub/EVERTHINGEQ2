## Notes

- This questlist is needed for [[Scars of Destruction: Complications]], but can be started after [[Scars of Destruction: Turtle Power]]

## Steps

1. Hunt and collect the following
   - Hunt 12 x [[A damned ulthork]] {{waypoint 60, 60, 577}} on the beach and above Remnant Shores camp
   - Collect 6 small corrupted bones - these are rather large tusk-like bones on the beach among ulthorks
     - Possible locations include {{waypoint 141, 7, 613}} ,  {{waypoint 231, 7, 676}},  {{waypoint -345.26, 10.20, 785.62}},   {{waypoint -371.19, 9.84, 789.27}},   {{waypoint -307.86, 10.07, 784.51}}
     - Look along the beach outside camp and small island to the West, also along Southern beach. There are also many on the beach to the east.
1. Return to [[Prisa]]

## Reward

- [[Necro-blighted Bone]] house item
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
