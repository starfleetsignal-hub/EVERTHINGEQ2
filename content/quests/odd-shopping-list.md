## Steps

1. Collect the following items:
   - 6 bladders from [[A ferocious razorfang|ferocious razorfangs]]. {{waypoint 487, -42, 181}}
   - 6 quills from the [[A vile stoneleer|vile stoneleers]]. {{waypoint 504, -40, -100}}
   - A sample of pollen from a *budding succulent*. These are small, non-trackable cacti  that can be found at the following locations: {{waypoint -643, -23, -1253}}, {{waypoint 166, -40, 984}}, or {{waypoint -434, -65, -576}}.
1. Return to [[Vixna Slizzis]] at the [[Upper Command Camp]].

## Rewards

- At least 41g 63s 17c
- +1000 faction with **Legion of Danak**
