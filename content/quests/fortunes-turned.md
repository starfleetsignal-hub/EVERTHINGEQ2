## Steps

*At the beginning of this quest, Gulthga will grant you a blessing of Cazic Thule. This adds a proc against lizardmen to the Ethernere Affinity buff. After this quest is completed the proc will be removed.*
Note:  Take a druid portal to Obol Plains, then use The Turbullion for fast travel to this part of Eidolon Jungle.

1. Enter [[Temple of the Faceless: Imprisonment (Advanced Solo)]] at {{waypoint -2163, 222, 768}} in Fearcreep.  *(You can work on this quest at the same time as [[Unexpected Ally]] so that you won't have to wait for zone reset.  This quest can also be done in the Heroic version of this zone.)*
   - [[Temple of the Faceless: Imprisonment (Heroic)]] also allows for group-wide updates.
1. Kill 6 lizardmen.
1. Kill [[Sarinich]]. Destroy the two statues at {{waypoint -328, 14, 216}} and {{waypoint -482, 14, 216}} to remove his immunity buff "Fear Shield"
   - IMPORTANT: Read this on how to kill Sarinich ([[Sarinich (Advanced Solo)]])
1. Kill [[Baroddas]]. He will summon any apes not dead, so be sure to get them all.
1. Kill [[Phobis]] then swim down through the waterfall behind where Phobia spawned.
1. IF you are concurrently doing [[Unexpected Ally]], then collect the staffs. The staffs do not appear unless you are also doing the [[Unexpected Ally]] quest.
   - Staff of Fright: {{waypoint -417, 55, -33}}
     - Staff of Fright is to the right of the entrance of the waterfall.
   - Staff of the Wastes: {{waypoint -360.65, -13.10, -305.28}} - after you have to swum down through the waterfall behind where Phobia spawned, cross the open grass area to find the stair and find it there.
1. Reach the lower temple grounds. This update will occur at {{waypoint -404, -2, -105}}
1. Slay the leader of the lizardmen, [[Venekor]] (96^^). {{waypoint -375, 0, -404}} You have to kill enough of the trash mobs to spawn Kilkilarus.  Kill him and Venekor will spawn.  He's at the far north part of the zone. He will summon all of the unkilled Pained Wretches if you let him.
1. Speak to the [[Shade of Venekor]].
1. Return to [[Gulthga]] to complete the quest.
