## Steps

1. Talk to Occultist L'Takk in the Temple of War in North Freeport {{waypoint -86, -10, -165}} to receive this City Task.  Kill 1 of the follwing:
   - Kill 10-15 [[A Blackscale bonetalker|Blackscale Bonetalkers]] in the [[The Blackscale Sepulcher]].
   - Kill 12-15 [[A Blackscale guardian|Blackscale guardians]] in the [[The Bonemire]].
   - Kill 10-15 [[A Blackscale precursor|Blackscale precursors]] in [[The Bonemire]].
   - Kill 12-14 [[A Blackscale shadowmind|Blackscale Shadowminds]] in the [[The Blackscale Sepulcher]].
   - Kill 11-15 [[A Blackscale shadowspeaker|Blackscale Shadowspeakers]] in the [[The Blackscale Sepulcher]].
   - Kill 10-13 [[A Blackscale stalker|Blackscale Stalkers]] in the [[The Blackscale Sepulcher]].
1. Return to [[Occultist L'Takk]].

## Rewards

- 9332 status
- +500 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
