## Steps

1. Kill Obulus remnants to get 8 drops from them. They are located in Western Warslik's Woods at {{waypoint 583, 172, -467}}
   - *Note: They only spawn at 8pm game time; Despawn at 5am*
1. Return to Setri Lur'Eth {{waypoint 622, 242, 1186}}
1. Hail her, follow dialog, tell her you were given a stone by an ancient being
1. Hail Lanys T'Vyl
1. Enter the portal erected by Lanys T'Vyl within Crusader's Cave. *You can only do this solo or with a merc.*
1. Explore [[Vaedenmoor, Realm of Despair]]
   1. Kill a Kaniz ravager and a Nightmarish acolyte, the two unique mobs on either side of the central platform.
   1. Walk up to Lanys T'Vyl
1. Confront the Goddess of Nightmare {{waypoint 7, 313, 119}}.
   - *Note: Attack Terris-Thule (^^^108 Epic x 4), and fight till you are disarmed or dead*
1. Report back to [[Queen Cristanos (Neriak)]] in [[Neriak]] or [[Queen Amree]] in [[Kelethin]].
