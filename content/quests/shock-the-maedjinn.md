## Notes

- He will not give you this quest until you have reached level 128.
- Requires completion of [[Corrosion Commotion]] to be offered.

## Steps

Despite Oolfonso's antiviral potion, a few of defenders have returned from Fiddtan Crest showing symptoms of an aggressive metal-organic virus. Alarmed by this, Oolfonso has sent me to retrieve charged silver goos from the Chaotic Currents in Aether Wroughtlands.

1. The entrance to 'Aether Wroughtlands: Chaotic Currents’ can be found near Fiddtan Crest in [[Aether Wroughtlands]].
   - Enter [[Aether Wroughtlands: Chaotic Currents (Solo)|Aether Wroughtlands: Chaotic Currents [Solo]]] at {{waypoint -364, 139, 433}}'
1. Obtain 9 overcharged silver ick Oolfonso was referring to!
   1. *Follow the steps in this walkthrough for the zone:  [[Aether Wroughtlands: Chaotic Currents (Solo)|Aether Wroughtlands: Chaotic Currents [Solo]]] to obtain all 9 silver icks*
   1. *You don't have to kill the final named, but might as well since you are here.*
1. Return to [[Oolfonso]] at the camp in [[Minstrel's Cradle]] at {{waypoint 699.97, -43.69, -466.82}} in [[Aether Wroughtlands]].
