## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Bristlebane has informed me that summoning an Avatar requires the land to be attuned to their element. To achieve this, altars have been set up around the region, but they need to be supplied with power. I have been asked to assist with this by collecting objects infused with the essence of slain foes and offer them to an altar.

1. Collect 3 [[Misplaced Game Pieces|misplaced game pieces]] (random body drop from many mobs in [[Zimara Breadth]])
1. Use the [[Misplaced Game Pieces|misplaced game pieces]] and empower an altar.  Random spawn in Rivervale, its yellow sparkles visible far away, possible locations of altar:
   - {{waypoint -177, 11, -214}} near the northern bridge over the Valeflow River
   - {{waypoint -257, 2, 217}} near the southern bridge over the Valeflow River
   - {{waypoint -147, 7, 186}}
   - {{waypoint -113.16, -9.57, -14.82}} (on hill behind the huge Fool's Gold Bldg, when you zone in)
   - {{waypoint -580.90, 3.42, 106.60}}
   - (-85, -20, 115)
1. Return to the [[Herald to the Grand Prankster]] at {{waypoint -832, 7, -203}} in [[Rivervale]]
