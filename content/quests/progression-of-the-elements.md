## Steps

1. Follow an elemental to see where they actually come from
   1. Travel to [[The Lowland Basin]] section of The Sundered Frontier.
   1. Head toward the crack in the ground at {{waypoint 1846, -123, 3591}}.  An unsuspecting underfoot elemental will spawn a short distance away -- often to the NE.
   1. Follow it a short distance and it will despawn, leaving a sparkling green mound of dirt behind.
      - *Note: Approach the spawn location slowly and back away from the creature if you follow too closely, it will turn into [[An alarmed underfoot elemental]] (lvl 81) and attack. If it does become alarmed kill it and then re-approach the spawn location more carefully.*
   1. Harvest the pile of sparkling green dirt to update the quest ![Sparkling green mound](images/Progression_of_the_Elements_quest_-_sparkling_green_mound.jpg)
1. Return to Researcher Ma'jok at {{waypoint 2022, -123, 3424}}

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Fell Blade**
