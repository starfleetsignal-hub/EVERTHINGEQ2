## Steps

1. Talk to your new class trainer in which ever inner city zone of Freeport they are.
   - If you are a neutral class, then you can choose whether to stay your current class or switch to the other associated class.
   - If you are an alignment restricted class, then you must talk to the trainer for opposite alignment to your class. Note: If you are a Paladin on a PVP server and want to move to Freeport and remain a Paladin, Then do NOT talk to the Shadowknight trainer. He does not give you an option to remain a Paladin. To remain a Paladin, you will have to skip this step.
1. You will be asked several times if you are sure you want to be your class to confirm your choice.

NOTE: Your hotbars will reset after you confirm your choice, even if you do not change classes.  Before confirming, you may want to save your hotbar configuration, as described in the main [[Betrayal Timeline|Betrayal]] article.

- All Mage trainers are found in the [[Academy of Arcane Science]] in [[North Freeport (POI)|North Freeport]].
- All Priest trainers are found in the [[Temple of War]] in [[North Freeport (POI)|North Freeport]].
- All Scout trainers are found at the [[Seafarer's Roost]] in [[South Freeport (POI)|South Freeport]].
- All Fighter trainers are found at the [[Freeport Militia House]] in [[West Freeport (POI)|West Freeport]].

## Reward

- [[Call of the Overlord]] general ability - replaces your [[Call of Haven]]
- -100000 faction with **Exile**
- Finally rid of that blasted **the Exiled** suffix title
- Possible change of class
- All spells are kept at same grade as they were at previous (So any masters will be kept)
- Free Respec of all of your character traits, racial traditions, enemy masteries, specialized training (master 2)

## Notes

- If you are upgrading a spell through the knowledge tab your saved time may be lost even if staying the same class.

- When completing this quest (even if you stay the same class) it may take a long time for all of your abilities to reappear.
