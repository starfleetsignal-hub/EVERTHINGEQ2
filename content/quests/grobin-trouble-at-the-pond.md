## Description

"Sentry Trellis believes that the Grobins surrounding Amethyst Pond could be polluting the water. He has asked me to clear out some of the polluters and bring back a sample of the pond water." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Do the following...
   - Kill 4 [[A grobin polluter|grobin polluters]] around [[Amethyst Pond]] {{waypoint -19.14, -50.31, -668.54}}
   - Use [[Empty delicate vessel]] to collect water from Amethyst Pond
1. Return to Sentry Trillis

## Rewards

- At least 1s 16c
