## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Indium Knife for my apprentice.
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Indium Knife]]
   1. Craft [[A Sturdy Indium Knife]] on a [[Forge]] using the following materials and fuel:
      - 2 [[Indium cluster]]
      - 4 [[Lambent coal]]
1. I need to take this knife back to my apprentice.
