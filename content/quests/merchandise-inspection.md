## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- You must have +20,000 faction with the Court of the Coin to unlock this quest. Once +50,000 faction has been achieved, however, this quest will no longer be available.

## Steps

1. Inspect barrels in [[The Pillars of Flame]].  Possible locations include:
   - [[T'Narev]] {{waypoint 43, -56, -1175}} or {{waypoint 216, -81, -1409}}
   - [[Swiftrider's Caravan]] {{waypoint 63, -88, -798}} or {{waypoint 26, -86, -741}}
   - [[Camp Char'Gin]] (western Stinging Isle) {{waypoint 1247, -165, -1622}} or ( 1217, -191, -1710)
1. Inspect barrels in [[The Sinking Sands]].  Possible locations include:
   - Near [[The Undercity Arena]] carpet {{waypoint -304, -107, -259}} or {{waypoint -226, -104, -408}}
     - The quest helper calls this "Along the east side of the wall near the pitfighters" even though it's on the western side of the zone.
   - Dervish camp on the north side of [[The Oasis of Marr]] {{waypoint -1240, -215, -309}}
   - [[Croc Hunter Camp]] {{waypoint -1317, -226, -598}}
   - Between the two tents at the dock {{waypoint -1450, -224, -432}}

## Rewards

- At least 3g 23s 29c
- -500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- +500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
