## Steps

![](images/Unrefined_holiday_cheer_cluster.jpg)
![](images/Raw_holiday_spirit_cluster.jpg)
![](images/Elven_radiance_cluster.jpg)

1. Click on the snow pile next to Sugarbow to enter [[Frostfell Wonderland's Icy Mines]]. {{waypoint 33, -3, -1218}}
1. Gather the following items from the crystal formations:
   - 6 unrefined holiday cheer.
   - 6 raw holiday spirit.
   - 6 elven radiance.
1. Kill the following monsters to break the ice barriers barring access to the rest of the instance:
   - [[A tumult of Frostfell magic]] {{waypoint 580, 341, -1422}}
   - [[Iciclis]] {{waypoint 602, 370, -1233}}
1. Find and speak to Twinklecane. {{waypoint 604, 383, -1606}}
1. Slay [[Cryogias]]. {{waypoint 643, 387, -1589}}
   - To spawn the named, attack [[A glob of melting snow]] near Twinklecane. The remainder will combine together to form Cryogias.
   - For characters that cannot output a lot of DPS, you may want to bring a friend or use a DPS Merc. There are globs that spawn during the encounter which heal Cryogias.
1. Return to Sugarbow outside the instance.
