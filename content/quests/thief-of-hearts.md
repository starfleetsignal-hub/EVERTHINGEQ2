This quest is part of the World Event: [[A Goddess Risen]].

## Steps

1. Find where the Lady of Insurrection is hiding.
   1. Click on the Movable Rock at {{waypoint -87, 37, -281}}.  This will zone you into the [[Abysm of Treachery]]
1. Confront the [[Ullkorruuk|Lady of Insurrection]]
   1. Head to The Lady of Insurrection at {{waypoint 52, -1, -599}}
1. Defeat [[The Lady of Insurrection]]
   1. After some brief dialog, she will turn into an even level ^ attackable mob. After you do enough damage to her, she will cease being attackable.
   1. Speak to [[Erollisi Marr]]

## Rewards

You are rewarded the prefix title *[[Love's Rescuer]]*, three items, and coin. The items themselves and the coin amount may differ slightly per player, but they seem to be designed for levels ending in 5. You can mount and unmount them to make them usable for your level.
