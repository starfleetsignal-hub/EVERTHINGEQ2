## Steps

1. Use the [[Ethereal Mender (Ethereal Thread Restoration)|Ethereal Mender]] on severed or torn ethereal threads, black whirlwinds found in the area around the camp and spires.
   - You need to do this in pairs. Match one torn and one severed thread to get one update.
   - When you start a pair all of the same type will disappear.
   - When grouped everyone gets update for one person's activities
1. Return to [[Tal'il Amhenti (Obol Plains)|Tal'il Amhenti]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- Access to Obol Plains Spire
- At least 1p 30g
