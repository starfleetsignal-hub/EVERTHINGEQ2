## Notes

- This is the final [[Aerakyn Mentors in Norrath|Aerakyn mentor]] quest in [[Qeynos]].
- Mobs do **not** see through invisibility/stealth.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Croyrr]].
1. Leave The House of Endings. Enter [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum]] {{waypoint 692, -20, 267}}.
1. Attend the gathering {{waypoint 748, -24, 324}}. There will be an undead mob that needs to be killed in the meeting room.
1. Hail [[Ysergin]].
1. Defeat Ysergin, [[Grendyr]], [[Nvestra]], and [[Phel'Dak]].
   - If you kill Ysergin then die before killing the others, the quest will still update to the next objective. The remaining Aerakyn will no longer be hostile.
1. Address the remaining Aerakyn. (Click on the blue stars.}
1. Before leaving, gather 10 relics from [[A portal guardian (Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum)|portal guardians]], [[A ghastly knight (Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum)|ghastly knights]], and [[A ghastly specter (Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum)|ghastly specters]].
1. Return to [[Gren Bluesong]] in The House of Endings.
1. Address the gathered from within the ritual circle. (Click on the yellow stars.)
1. Defeat Gren.
1. After his death, continue to address the gathered. (Click on the yellow stars again.)
1. Speak to [[Sloish Jellysmots]].
1. Leave the House of Endings and find [[Lonce Jellysmots|Lonce]] {{waypoint 404, -17, -153}}. Listen to him speak to the crowd.
1. Speak to Lonce once he is done.
1. Check in with [[Averniss]].
