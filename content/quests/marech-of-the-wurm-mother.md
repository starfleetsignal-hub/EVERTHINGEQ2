## Steps

1. Capture discordant soullights on [[Veeshenna Tol]] {{waypoint -341.07, -15.62, 631.90}}.
   - Killing [[A harmonic soullight|harmonic soullights]] found thoughout the island will spawn a discordant soullight.
   - Use the [[Discordant Chalice]] to capture the discordant soullight.
1. Return to Marech.
