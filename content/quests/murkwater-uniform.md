## Steps

1. Gather pieces of armor from Murkwater ratonga:
   - 2 updates from [[Murkwater induct|Murkwater inducts]] {{waypoint -268, 0, -7}}
   - 2 updates from [[Murkwater steward|Murkwater stewards]] {{waypoint -250, 1, -12}}
   - 2 updates from [[Murkwater abettor|Murkwater abettors]] {{waypoint -224, 0, -9}}
     - *Note:The updates on the abettors took the most tries (about 10), and for all three types, both updates hit on the same mob.*
1. Return to [[Cicero Puer]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- at least 2s 81c
- [[Murkwater Ring]]
