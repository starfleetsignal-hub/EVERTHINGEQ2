## Notes

- This quest was part the former access quest to [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]].

## Steps

1. Stop supply runners in [[Antonica]].
   - For [[Rusty Sails|Rusty]] and [[Noa Sails]] to appear, you must pass by a spot in [[Thundering Gorge]] on the way down the path from [[The Thundering Steppes]] and get the text message, "The sound of wooden cart wheels can be heard on the road below." {{waypoint -2317, 5, 725}}
1. Speak to Rusty Sails at {{waypoint -2231, -43, 514}}.
   - When you hail Rusty, [[Gawar the Bad (Antonica) (25)|Gawar the Bad]] and a pair of *orc marauders* (all level 25, heroic) will spawn.
1. After killing the orcs return to [[Captain Abella Coranis]] in the The Thundering Steppes.
1. Talk with [[Engineer Ximmix Wobblecog]] on the docks in The Thundering Steppes at {{waypoint -481, -21, -383}}.
1. Go to the [[The Crypt of Betrayal]] and retrieve Wobblecog's parts in the coffin. The coffin is next to [[Torg Bramblehair]] at {{waypoint -30, 7, -9}}.
   - To get to The Crypt of Betrayal if you are good aligned, go to Qeynos Capital District and behind The Coldwind Coast Stables is a grate on the ground {{waypoint 371, -20, 175}}  Click the grate to enter The Down Below.
   - Follow the path to the entrance to the Vermin's Snye located at {{waypoint -70, -0, -109}}
   - From inside Vermin's Snye go to the entrance to the Crypt of Betrayal at {{waypoint 2, -0.48, -184.89}}
   - From inside the Crypt of Betrayal find the  parts are in the coffin {{waypoint -30, 7, -9}}
   - After clicking on the coffin, you will be attacked by [[Gawar the Bad (Crypt of Betrayal)|Gawar the Bad]] and 3 additional orcs. Gawar is level 25^^ Heroic and the three orcs are level 25v Heroic.
1. Return to Engineer Ximmix Wobblecog.
1. Gather *Distilled Spirits* from a jug in the [[Blackburrow]] brewery at {{waypoint 38, -40, 71}}. [[Gawar the Bad (Antonica) (26)|Gawar the Bad]] will again appear as you leave Blackburrow. He might even attack before you enter.
   - Travel to the Antonica Dock, then head East. Dive into the water and swim to {{waypoint -1017, -52, 1177}} and follow the tunnel to the cave to the hidden entrance to Blackburrow at {{waypoint 1106, -33, 1064}} Note that the 'hidden entrance' for Blackburrow leads to [[The Blackburrow Brewery]] on the bottom level and takes you straight to where you need to be.
   - You may need to right-click the jug {{waypoint 37, -39, 70}} to bring up the option to choose the *Distilled Spirits*
1. Return to Engineer Ximmix Wobblecog.
1. Hunt 5 [[A clay crawler|clay crawlers]] and 5 [[A living clay|living clays]] in [[The Ruins of Varsoon]]. You will once again be attacked by [[Gawar the Bad (Thundering Steppes)|Gawar the Bad]] and 5 additional orcs (all level 27 heroics) when you get near the entrance to [[The Ruins of Varsoon]].
1. Return to Engineer Ximmix Wobblecog.
1. Use a [[Mariner's Bell]] to zone into the [[Prize of Prexus]] instance (shown on the map between Antonica and Zek) and speak with [[Captain Abella Coranis]] upon arrival.
1. Defeat several waves of *Deathfist orcs*. Kill [[Captain Bloodstain]] and [[Gawar the Bad (Prize of Prexus)|Gawar the Bad]] once and for all in the final wave.
   - Anchors spawn on both sides of the deck, at the bow, and the stern; as long as the anchor is there it spawns pairs of Deathfist orcs. Destroy the anchors to slow the spawns.
1. Speak to Captain Abella Coranis again to end the quest and be transported to the dock in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]].
