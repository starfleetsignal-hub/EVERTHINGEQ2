## Steps

1. Meet her in Maldura {{waypoint 371, 82, 26}} where she tells you that the Queen would like to speak to you privately.
1. She arranges a meeting for you with [[Queen Alwenielle (Thalumbra, the Ever Deep)|Queen Alwenielle]] at [[The Tylwyth's Hut]] in [[Zou'Lidelas]] in Thalumbra {{waypoint -334, 240, 381}} where she hands over two pieces of [[Blushing umbrite]] for use in the next quest.
1. Speak with [[Elanluelle]] at {{waypoint -486, 237, 350}} to complete the quest and receive the next quest.
