## Steps

1. Complete the following around the Sathirian village along [[Omen's Churn Shore]]:
   - Kill 8 Sathirians.
   - Collect 5 crates:
     - 3 are at a set of tents {{waypoint 105, 12.5, -837}}
     - 4 are at a giant stump {{waypoint 125, 12, -740}}
     - 2 are in front of the hut on the shore at {{waypoint 35, 1, -888}}
     - 1 on the shore at {{waypoint 13, -0.6, -836.5}}
     - 2 around a set of tents at {{waypoint 32.5, 11, -796}}
     - 2 on the shore at {{waypoint -41.5, 0, -813}}
     - 3 around a tent at {{waypoint -49, 5.5, -772}}
     - 2 on the shore at {{waypoint -98, 0, -759}}
1. Return to [[Basher Malgon]]. {{waypoint 209, 73, -957}}

- At least 14g 81s 38c
- [[Radiating Gem of Criticals]]

  - [[Augur's Wristguard of Wellness]] (Clerics)
  - [[Gladiator's Bracers of Overpowering]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Grandmaster's Wrist Wraps of Redirection]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Pathfinder's Vambraces of Quickness]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Rainkeeper's Bands of Regrowth]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Ritualist's Chain Cuffs of Summons]] (Shamans)
  - [[Sage's Cuffs of Invoking]] (All Mages)
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
