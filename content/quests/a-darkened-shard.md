## Starting the Quest

Travel to the [[The Thundering Steppes|Thundering Steppes]] and click on a [[Terraporter]], one of the bone structures found near gnoll camps. You will receive a [[A darkened azure shard]] in your inventory and examining it will start the quest. You can find the terraporters in one of the following locations:

- {{waypoint 548, -1, -507}}
- {{waypoint 710, -2, -414}}
- {{waypoint 1023, -1, -247}}
- {{waypoint 1267, -3, -208}}

## Notes

- This is the first quest of the [[Splitpaw Saga Timeline|Splitpaw Saga]] timeline. This was originally an optional adventure pack which was purchased separately when the game originally released; however, it is now considered part of the base game and no additional purchase is needed to access the content.

- This content will become available to players on Time Locked Expansion (TLE) servers as part of the Shattered Lands mid-expansion content unlock. This occurs approximately halfway through the scheduled time for the original content (e.g: eight weeks after launch).

- If you are lower level and the gnolls are aggressive to you, do not allow yourself to be attacked by [[A Tesch Mal abductor]], one of the gnolls guarding a terraporter. If one of them hits you, you will be instantly teleported to a different instance called [[Sundered Splitpaw: Captive Audience]]. This was originally intended to be an introductory zone for players who had not purchased the expansion pack and will not progress this quest.

## Steps

1. After obtaining the quest, examine [[A darkened azure shard]] again and select **Examine the shard further**. This will cause a vision of a [[Terraporter]] cairn to appear in front of the character.
1. Approach one of the other terraporters found at the locations listed above or leave the area and then return back to the terraporter you used to obtain the shard. If you click on the same terraporter immediately after obtaining the quest, it will not update.
1. Click on the terraporter to enter the instance [[Sundered Splitpaw: Delving into the Darkness]].
   - This is intended to be a solo instance; however, it can be done as a group. Each person who has the quest must be close enough to the terraporter to get a quest update before being pulled into the zone.
   - None of the gnolls in the instance can see through stealth, and you can move objects without breaking stealth.
   - The instance is persistent, and you can exit the zone at {{waypoint -52.51, -64.76, -224.66}}. If you leave the zone, you will be teleported to Thundermist Village.

### Find the Gem

Once inside, you must find what the cave has to do with the gem. To do this, you need to obtain a key to open a chest located at the bottom of the dungeon.

1. Head to {{waypoint -126, -68, -99}} and kill  [[The Tesch Val Fanatic]]. He will drop a chest containing a copper key.
   - NOTE: The ribbon does not work well in the cave. Go to {{waypoint -28, -69, -209}} and then to to {{waypoint 26, -69, -173}}. Once there, put in the waypoint {{waypoint -126, -68, -99}}.
1. The [[A copper key|copper key]] unlocks the chest on top of a rock 'platform' at {{waypoint -116, -65, -83}}. To reach the chest you must pile up crates. Find the three crates lying nearby and stack them one on top of the other at the base of the pillar to build yourself a staircase up to the chest. There are other crates in the zone if needed.
   - You can invis all the way in and out except for the fight with the Fanatic and the roamer immediately in front of him.
   - The key drops even if The Tesch Val Fanatic is grayed out.
1. Climb to the top of the pillar and have the person who looted the [[A copper key|copper key]] open the chest. They will recieve [[A slightly glowing shard]].
   - Other group members will have to right click on the opened chest and choose to take *a slightly glowing shard*.
1. Examine *a slightly glowing shard* while still in the zone to make it fuse together with [[A darkened azure shard]]. *A slightly glowing shard* will disappear and your quest journal will be updated. Only the person that has the key and opens the chest will get the update automatically; everyone else in the group must right-click the chest to get their update.
   - If you obtain the shard from the chest but have not advanced the quest, you can exit the zone and re-enter it. When you leave, you will be teleported to Thundermist Village. Return to the terraporter at {{waypoint 708.23, -1.81, -410.36}} to update the quest and complete it by examining the slightly glowing azure shard.
1. You can now exit via the terraporter at the entrance: {{waypoint -54, -65, -226}}. You will end up in [[Village of Thundermist]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]].
1. Proceed to the entrance to [[Sundered Splitpaw: Upper Tunnels|Splitpaw Lair]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]]. Head to the Giant's Forge at {{waypoint 197, 1, 1081}} then enter the Sundered Splitpaw at {{waypoint 171, 5, 1053}}.

## Reward

- [[A slightly glowing shard]] - Examine it and it will merge with your [[A darkened azure shard]], letting you access the rest of the Splitpaw Adventure Pack. It will also grant you the [[Gnollish|Gnollish language]] if you do not already have it.
