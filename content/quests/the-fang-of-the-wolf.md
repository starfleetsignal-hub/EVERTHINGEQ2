## Description

"Leslie Afterstar has been sent into the forest to decrease the predator population that is eating all the good game. The population has become so dense that he is requesting your help to thin their numbers for him. Slay a few howlers and report back to Leslie." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

- Slay three [[A howler|howlers]] {{waypoint 336, 54, -40}} South West Side of Orc Hill. [[A prowler|Prowlers]] also update.
- Return to Leslie Afterstar

## Rewards

- At least 2s 13c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Leslie Afterstar: "This is more difficult than I was lead to believe."
- Player Character: "What is?"
- Leslie Afterstar: "I'm here to regulate the predator population. Our hunting has been very poor recently and so I'm here to help increase their numbers."
- Player Character: "By killing the animals that hunt them also?"
- Leslie Afterstar: "Exactly. However, there are more of them than I thought, and I cannot be out here all week. Perhaps you would like to make some coin and slay some for me, [Player Character]?"
- Player Character: "Perhaps. What does it entail?"
- Leslie Afterstar: "Firstly, you should slay howlers at the top of the cliff. They are a type of wolf."
- Player Character: "Howlers are easy. I will do it."
- Leslie Afterstar: "Use that tree north of me to climb to the top of the cliff. The one with the glowing pink flower."
- Player Character: "I climb things all the time."
