## Prerequisites

- You must have started the quest [[When Darkness Comes]] or [[Eliminate Geraufl]] in order to complete this quest.

## Steps

1. Observe the mining happening near [[Grrop the Foreman (Lost VoK)|Grrop the Foreman]] {{waypoint -37, 14, 189}}, watching the ground carefully at the miner's feet.
1. Pick up [[A dirt covered rock]] that spawns near the miner's feet. You may need to be a little patient.
1. Wash the rock at the water barrel behind Grepp's hut {{waypoint -63, 14, 154}}.
1. Bring the channeling stone ([[A blue channeling stone|blue]], [[A red channeling stone|red]] or [[A yellow channeling stone|yellow]]) back to Pikspoh.
