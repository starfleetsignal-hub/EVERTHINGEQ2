This quest is repeatable up to 5 times.

## Steps

1. Slay the vermin she specifies (chose randomly) in any order:
   - [[A forgotten soul|forgotten souls]] {{waypoint -146, -1, -84}}
   - [[A tortured soul (The Down Below)|tortured souls]] {{waypoint -192, 0, -27}}
   - [[A venomous watcher|venomous watchers]] {{waypoint -124, 0, -195.5}}
   - [[A frenzied watcher|frenzied watchers]] {{waypoint -136, 7, -209}}
   - [[A rabid shriller (Down Below)|rabid shrillers]] {{waypoint -192, 0, -61}}
   - [[A venomous keeper|venomous keepers]] {{waypoint -99.5, 0, -199.5}}
1. Return to [[Adair Barnes]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- At least 1s 51c - 1st completion.
- At least 3s 2c - 2nd completion.
- At least 4s 53c - 3rd completion.
- At least 6s 63c - 4th completion.
- At least 10s 25c - 5th completion.
