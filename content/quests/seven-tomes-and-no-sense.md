## Notes

- Requires at least -2,000 [[Pride Pakiat (Faction)]]

## Steps

1. Gather 6 books. They're mostly found on the bluffs below the plateaus near trees. ![stolen book](images/Stolen_book.jpg)
   1. Known locations: {{waypoint -868, 77, -251}} {{waypoint -940, 84, -339}} {{waypoint -741, 172, -701}} {{waypoint -414, 78, -882}} {{waypoint -425, 95, -776}}  {{waypoint -719, 127, -494}} {{waypoint -592, 136, -398}} {{waypoint -463, 128, -613}}, {{waypoint -573.06, 132, -579.2}}, see the talk page for more possible locations
   - *Note: most books seem located at the base of trees.
   1. Kill the [[A possessive pakiat siamang]] that appears when you pick up each book to update the quest.
   - *Note: the books are only visible when the camera is 29m or closer. do not zoom out fully or you can't see em*
   - *Note: Seems that if a book respawns you can click on it again to get another update.*
1. Return to [[Karo-amat]].
1. Approach the 7th book at {{waypoint 56, 62, 140}}, kill [[The monkey king]] and loot [[The seventh tome]].

(!!YOU MUST BE MENTORED FOR CHEST TO DROP!!) *edit: January 2020, not mentored, lvl 120 and body drop contained tome* ![Seventh Book](images/Seventh_Book.jpg)

1. Loot the chest for [[The seventh tome]].
1. Return to [[Karo-amat]] to receive [[Stack of tomes]].
1. Place the stack of books at {{waypoint -677, 124, -453}}. ![stack of books](images/Stack_of_books.jpg)
1. Return to [[Karo-amat]].
