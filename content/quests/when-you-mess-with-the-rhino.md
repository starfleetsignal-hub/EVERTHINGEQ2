## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
  - While this quest can technically be completed an infinite amount of times, it will stop being offered once your faction is greater than -10,000 (indifferent) with The Sel'Nok Brigade. Should your faction ever dip lower than that in the future, this quest will become available again.

## Steps

1. Kill 12 [[An aggressive grindhoof|aggressive grindhooves]] in the [[Stonehoof Badlands]]. {{waypoint -670, -130, -590}}
1. Return to Emissary Dih'ri.

## Rewards

- At least 16g 14s 34c
- +11,100 faction with **The Sel'Nok Brigade**
