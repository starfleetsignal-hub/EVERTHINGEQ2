## Steps

1. recover 3 stolen Gems
   - search in the barrels found at {{waypoint -106, -335, -29}}, {{waypoint -119, -335, -32}} and {{waypoint -112, -335, -38}}
     - searching the barrels behind the tent will spawn a group of two [[A D'Kilneld Company Guard]] that attack you.
1. Return to [[Tamke Mudhoof]]
