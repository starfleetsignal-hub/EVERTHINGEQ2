The previous batch of hide from chokidai pups has finished the tanning process and is ready to be crafted into leather armor for the goblins of The Bloody Tooth clan. I offered my assistance to create it.

## Note

Upon accepting this quest, you will receive the following:

- 4 [[Chokidai hide|chokidai hide]]
- [[Chokidai Hide Armor (Recipe Book)]] which will automatically scribe the [[Sturdy leather armor|Chokidai Hide Armor]] recipe to your recipe book.

The recipe for this quest can **only** be crafted at a [[Goblin Workbench]] in the area. One is found at {{waypoint 72, -164, -722}}, near Oogthor.

## Supply List

Because these must be crafted in [[Twark]], and the required materials cannot be harvested in [[Obulus Frontier]], bring the following (minimum) supplies:

- 4 [[Chokidai hide|chokidai hide]] (granted upon receiving the quest)
- 12 [[Splitiron ore]]
- 12 [[Thalumbral root]]
- 20 [[Thaumic Coal]]

## Steps

1. I can use a goblin workbench to make the armor
   - Craft [[Sturdy leather armor|sturdy leather armor]] (4)
     - The *Chokidai Hide Armor* recipe produces [[Sturdy leather armor|sturdy leather armor]]
1. Oogthor is in the main goblin encampment of Twark
   - Bring the armor back to [[Oogthor]] {{waypoint 74, -165, -725}}

## Rewards

- [[Sovereign of Rile]] currency
- [[Ancient Shadowstone Relic]] status token
- +2000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
- At least 2p 26g 80s
