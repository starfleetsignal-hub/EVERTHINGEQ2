## Steps

1. Free Phenocryst's mindstone from the sediments at {{waypoint 750, -456, 3746}}
1. Examine [[Phenocryst's Mindstone]] and speak with him
1. The Mindstone will guide you now to his parts (there seems to be some randomness to its location) ![missing part](images/Phenocryst_missing_part.png)
   1. Gather his first part in the crate or pile of rocks at {{waypoint 572, -492, 4022}} or {{waypoint 616, -493, 3984}} or {{waypoint 632.95, -489.19, 3948}}
   1. Gather his second part at the Shinking Pond at {{waypoint 815, -466, 3876}}
   1. Gather his third part in the [[Caverns of Living Stone]] at {{waypoint 360, -459, 3807}} {{waypoint 326, -459, 3861}} {{waypoint 268, -458, 3825}} {{waypoint 277, -453, 3797}} ( 380, -458, 3831 ) Note: Found 2 of them behind Rocks along the Cave Walls.
   1. Gather his last part in the [[Caverns of Living Stone]] at {{waypoint 348, -437, 3629}} or {{waypoint 353.87, -438.12, 3632.0}} or {{waypoint 339, -436, 3641.0}}
1. Finish the Conversation with the Headstone
1. Go to [[The Protolith Room]] in the [[Caverns of Living Stone]] at {{waypoint 434, -433, 3602}} and click on the stone at {{waypoint 410, -432, 3571}}
1. Wait till Phenocryst is reformed (takes a few seconds)
1. Speak with [[Phenocryst]]
