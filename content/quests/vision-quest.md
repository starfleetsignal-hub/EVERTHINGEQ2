## Steps

1. Acquire components for dream trance drops. They all can be found in Bile Stream valley in Oogothl Sprawl.  {{waypoint -161.60, 73.72, -312.05}}
   - Gather 8 x tohu belladonna
   - Hunt 12 x [[A keratite stalagmaw|keratite stalagmaw]]s for marrow chalk
   - Hunt 9 x [[A bristlepod ooze|bristlepod ooze]] for ooze pearls
     - *Note: the pearls spawn on top of the ooze corpse and must be clicked to update*
1. Return to [[Xhindar]]

## Rewards

- [[Lashling]] house pet
- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following
  - [[Brokenskull Medallion of Conceit]]
  - [[Doomview Amulet of Expedition]]
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
