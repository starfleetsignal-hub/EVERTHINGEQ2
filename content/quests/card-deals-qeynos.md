## Steps

1. Speak to [[Zoe Hertzflip (Qeynos Province District)|Zoe Hertzflip]] on the second floor of [[The Mermaid's Lure]] in [[Qeynos Harbor (POI)|Qeynos Harbor]], and complete her subquest [[Ogled Goggles (Qeynos)|Ogled Goggles]] in exchange for her deck of playing cards
   - To complete Ogled Goggles, you'll have to complete the subquest [[Wine for the Lady (Qeynos)|Wine for the Lady]] for [[Barb Islenet (Qeynos Capitol District)|Barb Islenet]]
     - To complete Wine for the Lady, you'll hve to complete the subquest [[Fribles's Fate (Qeynos)|Fribles's Fate]] for [[Abner Siblet (Qeynos Province District)|Abner Siblet]]
1. Return to Frethe Zizlop
