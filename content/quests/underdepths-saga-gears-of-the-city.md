The Grand Artificer of Maldura, Tobon Blingden, one half of Maldura's ruling seat, has requested my presence at his workstation in the Alcove of Reason. Hopefully, I can get a few answers from him about the Dark Gears, now that I have evidence that Black Bolt was connected with the immolated.

## Steps

![Crystallized Proximity Translocator](images/Crystallized_Proximity_Translocator.png)

1. Click the [[Alcove of Reason]] **Crystallized Proximity Translocator** {{waypoint -52.86, 14.90, 203.68}} to port over to [[Tobon Blingden]], the Grand Artificer, on the platform in the [[Infinite Caverns]] {{waypoint -52.09, 5.35, 248.98}}.
1. Speak to [[Queen Alwenielle]] in the [[Palace of Unity]] {{waypoint 376.47, 85.85, -0.03}}.
1. Find and slay the Primordial Malice members in the [[Stoneheart Yards]] in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]] {{waypoint 621.26, 106.00, 341.68}}.
   - This will be a group of linked foes, [[A Primordial Malice shadowknight]], [[A Primordial Malice cursespinner]], and [[A Primordial Malice cultist]].
1. Good aligned speak to [[Zeen Corblecog (Thalumbra)|Zeen Corblecog]] at {{waypoint 462, 96, 43}}. Evil aligned speak with [[Virun D'Xyafin (Thalumbra)|Virun D'Xyafin]] at {{waypoint 470, 95, 66}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g 33394 status
- +1000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
