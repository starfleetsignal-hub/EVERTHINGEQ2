## Steps

1. Speak to [[Keep Master Yonette Ironbar]] {{waypoint 682, 351, 79}} If you are flying in the NPC may disappear on you. Just land and relog and Yonette should appear again.
1. Help the Far Seas Traders fend off the attack on Highpass
   - Help [[Waulon Highpebble]] with his investigations - do the following quest line
     1. [[Clerical Error]]
     1. [[Minds Behind the Barrage]]
   - Continue to help [[Keep Master Yonette Ironbar]] - do the following quest line
     1. [[Bone Removal]]
     1. [[Battlefield Relief]]
     1. [[Over the Walls of Highhold]]
1. Report back to [[Keep Master Yonette Ironbar]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- One of the Following:
  - [[Electrified Adornment of Fading]]
  - [[Electrified Adornment of Aggressiveness]]
