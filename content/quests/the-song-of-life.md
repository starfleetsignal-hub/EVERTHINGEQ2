I met a ghost by the name of Thaneni, wandering along the river near Zou'Lidelas. He pointed out that for a people that revere passion and growth, the glaufaye seem to be lacking both. It's made me curious.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Arideva]] {{waypoint -453.63, 222.14, 337}}.
1. Kill [[A lashing petalcreeper|lashing petalcreepers]] {{waypoint -414.31, 131.31, -162.36}}, collecting 16 **petalcreeper seeds**.
1. Plant petalcreeper seeds within fresh locust corpses: Kill 8 [[A cave locust|cave locusts]] {{waypoint -145.22, 112.21, 327.87}}. Click the bodies afterwards to plant the seeds.
1. Return to [[Arideva]].
