## Steps

1. Find the Chetari Passages above the Narthex of the Necropolis
   - Use the "A geomanced transport" at {{waypoint -503.37, -4.02, 438.22}} to get to the zone .
   - Once you land the quest will update around  {{waypoint -600.21, 130.54, 411.38}}
1. Enter the [[Dragon Necropolis: Chetari Warrens (Solo)|Dragon Necropolis: Chetari Warrens [Solo]]] at {{waypoint -838, 129, 345}}
   - *For the Walkthrough see [[Dragon Necropolis: Chetari Warrens (Solo)|Dragon Necropolis: Chetari Warrens [Solo]]]*
1. Explore "[[Dragon Necropolis: Chetari Warrens (Solo)|Dragon Necropolis: Chetari Warrens [Solo]]]"
   1. Progress through the Chetari tunnels to reach the interior crypts.
      - Progress through the tunnel at {{waypoint 33.15, 160.27, -366.19}} till you reach the [[Heart of the Necropolis]] at {{waypoint 45, 99, -75}} or {{waypoint 18.52, 99.77, -94.13}}
   1. "Find and deactivate the tinkered barricade generator Mesmer said to expect on the inside."
      - tinkered barricade generator at {{waypoint -7.26, 98.82, -137.29}} (first cubbyhole to the left of the exit from the tunnel)
   1. once you killed [[Jaled Dar (Solo)]|Jaled Dar]] walk closer to the [[Tomb of Jaled Dar]] near {{waypoint 15.00, 106.69, 115.60}}
   1. examine the tombstone at {{waypoint 15.00, 106.69, 115.60}} *(click on it)*![Translation of the text of the plaque in the Tomb of Jaled Dar](images/The_Tomb_of_Jaled_Dar.png)
1. Return to [[Mesmer Gearloose]] at {{waypoint 64, 7, 638}} in the [[Western Wastes]]
1. Enter [[Dragon Necropolis: Tombs of Enlightenment (Solo)|Dragon Necropolis: Tombs of Enlightenment [Solo]]] at {{waypoint -748, 67, 282}}
   - *For the Walktrough see [[Dragon Necropolis: Chetari Warrens (Solo)|Dragon Necropolis: Chetari Warrens [Solo]]]*
   - *This entrance is at the ground-level Chetari cave in the overland zone, not the upper level area you used to enter Chetari Warrens for the first part of this quest.*
1. Speak with Jazirah Leganyn at the entrance.
1. Follow the homing drone to {{waypoint -370, 133, -347}}
   - *Kill [[Vaynon the Underdrake]] as you pass*
   - ***WARNING:** DO NOT PROGRESS IN THE ZONE PAST THE DRONE*
1. Defeat [[Urasi the Unvarnished]]
1. Speak with Jazirah.
1. Following the homing drone even further.
1. Speak with Jazirah at {{waypoint -507.07, 132.51, -340.36}} *(in front of [[Beresk the Bejeweled]])*
1. Follow the homing drone through the Chetari
   1. Kill [[Beresk the Bejeweled]] *(it is blocking the tunnel)*
      - *Run around the chamber while DPSing her, firing off HOs and completing them, and immediately restore any blackened crystals. All classes are able to run while casting spells for this fight.*
   1. Go down the Tunnel at {{waypoint -393, 132, -302}}
   1. Follow the homing drone through the Chetari tunnel at {{waypoint -386.18, 120.65, -332.64}} to update the quest
1. Catch up with Jazirah and the drone at {{waypoint -328.85, 113.47, -0.81}}
1. Defeat the [[Seket the Sightless]] <Chetari runemage>. He is linked with [[Seket's Sight]] <Chetari Rune Golem>
   - *dismiss your healer merc and don't cure arcane*
   - get an Arcane detriment by hitting Seket the Sightless then use a Heroic Opportunity on the golem, Seket's Sight.
   - Burn Seket down while he is summoning a new golem.  Still a dumb fight, is buggie.
1. Collect the claw rune the Chetari dropped on the ground.
   - *Note: You do not need to fight Rakirith the Reanimated*
1. Return to [[Mesmer Gearloose]] at {{waypoint 64, 7, 638}} in the [[Western Wastes]].
