## Prerequisites

- Your faction with [[The City of Kelethin (Faction)|City of Kelethin]] must be Indifferent or better to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Go to Tunare's Grove in The Lesser Faydark, around {{waypoint -114, -54, -246}} around the lower grove station
1. Kill various Grovespirit wisps for random updates of ***Wisp Essence***
   - NOTE: Only "grovespirit" wisps work - the essences of growth do not!
1. After receiving credit for 6 ***Wisp Essences*** (not all will update) return to [[Duchess Maareanna]]

## Rewards

- At least 12g 57s 51c
- +100 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
