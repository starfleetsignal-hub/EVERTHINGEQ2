## Steps

1. Obtain [[A tarnished old ring]], a semi-rare body drop from [[An elder skyfire wyvern|elder skyfire wyverns]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]]. Roams outside [[The Temple of the Red Lord]]. Spawn points include {{waypoint -2325.11, 242.51, 655.48}}, {{waypoint -2194.82, 232.30, 579.51}}, {{waypoint -2184.77, 250.70, 392.84}}, {{waypoint -2042.43, 237.69, 413.37}}, {{waypoint -1931.76, 222.58, 647.99}} and {{waypoint -1901.43, 201.27, 957.41}} with approximately 6 minutes respawn timer.
1. Examine the ring and clean it.  You will receive [[A dormant sorcerer's band]].
1. Speak with [[Cerennhir Aelindel]] in [[New Tunaria]].  You will receieve [[A weakened sorcerer's band]]
1. Speak with [[Maelius Irenaeus]] {{waypoint -335, -40, -642}} in [[Lavastorm]] at the [[Temple of Solusek Ro]] on the balcony above the dragon, [[Lord Xyfl]].
   - Go up the stairs and to the right, then right twice more.
   - If you are not a follower of [[Solusek Ro]], you must prove your worthiness by fighting Pyroklastanar, an 82^^^ Heroic summoned mob.
1. Return to [[Jaestin Chaldean]] with the [[Cor Gollor ed'Lhach]].

## Rewards

- Experience only
