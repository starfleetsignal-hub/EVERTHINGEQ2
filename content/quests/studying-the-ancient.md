## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[Briarstone ancient|Briarstone ancients]] at {{waypoint 607, -25, 230}}. These are found closer to [[The Tower of Bone]] and on the right hand side (northeast relative to the tower).
1. Return to Unlara.

## Rewards

- 5s 67c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Forged Axe]] (Warriors)
  - [[Forged Blade]] (Bards, Predators, Rangers, Rogues)
  - [[Forged Cestus]] (Brawlers, Beastlord)
  - [[Forged Tower Shield]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Empowered Holy Symbol]] (All Priests)
  - [[Empowered Totem]] (All Mages)
