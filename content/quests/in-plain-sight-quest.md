## Steps

1. Find the portal to the [[Research Hall of Mayong Mistmoore]] in the [[Somborn Cemetery]], at {{waypoint -379, 17, 59}} and search for Mayong's notes, which can be found on a small table near the southeast corner. Click the book to receive the [[Mistmoore Researcher's Logbook]]. {{waypoint 96.85, 7.30, 112.74}}
   - You will have to fight your way past various vampires ([[A vampire swordguard]], [[A vampire sentrymage]], [[A vampire runekeeper]] -- equal to your level) before you find the notes.
1. Return to [[The Duality]].
1. Search for the writings of the Deepwater Knights in Paineel's library. {{waypoint 2001.23, -297.81, 3352.06}}
   - This is a book on a table inside the [[Hall of Wizardry]] next to [[Scholar Dahlia Dominique]]. Click it to receive [[Writings of the Deepwater Knights]].
1. Return to The Duality.
