## Steps

1. Good idea to Chrono yourself down to 65 or 70 so everything will drop chests.
1. Get the book [[Teachings of Master Wu]] from the northern most part of the bottom level of [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]].
1. The pages drop off of random mobs in the zone in small chests.
   - Book **NO-TRADE** - clickable book in [[Exarch's Seclusion]] {{waypoint -17, 57, -224}}
1. Make sure you turn in the finished collection once you complete it.
