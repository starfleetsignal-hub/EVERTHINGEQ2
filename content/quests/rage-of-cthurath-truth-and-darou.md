## Notes

- Requires 50x [[Formless Coal]]

## Steps

![a bog gnasher](images/A_bog_gnasher.png) ![a bog wisp](images/A_bog_wisp.png) ![Cementoblast](images/Cementoblast.png) ![a toxic veilbloom](images/A_toxic_veilbloom.png)

1. Kill 12 [[A bog gnasher|bog gnashers]] in [[Rot Reek Bog]] for their tongues - around {{waypoint -231, 19, -330}}, more of them down at Bog'ta Beach around {{waypoint -539, 0, -240}}.
1. Return to [[Darou Village]]
   - Teleporting back to the village will not advance the quest. To complete this step you must use a [[Darou Village]] entrance e.g. {{waypoint 313, 73, -582}}.
1. Kill 4 attacking ebonblights and knightbloods at the edge of the village.
1. Return to Tiadra and Duusain.
   - Speak to Tiadra and receive the [[Runic Rejuvenation Wand]] recipe. Find it in your inventory and scribe it. Level 10 crafting required.
1. Gather the parts needed for the wand around {{waypoint -231, 19, -330}}.
   - Hunt 9 bog wisps around for fetid embers. Loot from bodies 2 at a time.
   - Gather 9 cementum from Cementoblasts, around {{waypoint -245, 20, -321}}. Trackable.
   - Hunt 6 toxic veilblooms around for veilbloom pollen. Loot from bodies, not a guaranteed drop.
1. Create the [[Runic Rejuvenation Wand]] (BooK)  in the tempered netherforge crucible at {{waypoint 497, 82, -645}}
   - Requires 50x [[Formless Coal]] :   (Recipe should be in your bag.)
1. Use the [[Runic Rejuvenation Wand]] to rejuvenate the inert cimmerott runic wards.
   - Cimmerott runic ward Fid at {{waypoint 404, 80, -600}}
   - Cimmerott runic ward Roh at {{waypoint 401, 73, -474}}
   - Cimmerott runic ward Keh at {{waypoint 537, 76, -483}}
1. Return to Tiadra
