## Steps

1. Solve Sassarish's newest riddle
   1. ![a thunderous cumulus](images/Thunderous_Cumulus.png)Head to [[Columnar Highgarden]] and kill [[A thunderous cumulus]] {{waypoint 650.12, 207.46, -463.12}} and collect the lightning bolt
      - *Note: The first thunderous cumulus will not have a feather*
   1. Kill 7 [[A thunderous cumulus|thunderous cumulus]] and loot the lightning bolts
1. Return to [[Sassarish]] on [[Zenith Helionae]] at {{waypoint 266.06, 160.41, -72.32}}
