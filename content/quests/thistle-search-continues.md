## Steps

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -280.48, 180.72, 0.42}} to enter [[Sanctus Seru: Echelon of Order (Solo)|Sanctus Seru: Echelon of Order [Solo]]].
   - *Note: Requires completing or being on [[Shattered Dawn: Execution of Order]] to access the instance.*
1. Find Thistle at {{waypoint -62.82, 88.05, 272.49}}.
1. Continue through the zone and defeat [[Lady Warglave]] at {{waypoint -267.78, 89.95, 2.87}}
1. Return to Thistle at {{waypoint -62.82, 88.05, 272.49}} and click on him.
1. Return to [[Sanctus Seru (City)]] and speak to Taldris Aelin at {{waypoint -139.06, 175.68, 206.53}}

  - [[Valorous Sanctus Gussets]]
