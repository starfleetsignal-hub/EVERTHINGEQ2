## Prerequisites

- You must have at least +17,800 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Obtain 8 Kromdek weapons by killing [[A Kromdek warmonger|Kromdek warmongers]] or [[A Kromdek stonelord|Kromdek stonelords]] in [[Camp Kromdek]]. {{waypoint -1123.60, -327.18, -1751.30}}
1. Go back to [[The Sheet]] and deposit the weapons on the rack. {{waypoint -2657, -412, -2706}}
1. Speak with [[Bragek Joll]]. {{waypoint -2656, -412, -2702}}

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- +1600 faction with **The Order of Rime**
