## Steps

1. Find [[Drukin Tellfizz|Drukin]] down on a ledge in the [[Shattered Weir]]. {{waypoint -767, -471, 173}}
1. Complete [[Drukin Tellfizz, Stuck Gnome]] and [[Drukin Tellfizz, More Than Just a Stuck Gnome]].
1. Return to [[Zastik Togginscog]].

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Dwarven Berserker's Sphere]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Elvish Sphere of Wanderlust]] (All Priests)
  - [[Fatality Sphere]] (All Mages)
  - [[Flaming Totem of Killing]] (All Scouts)
