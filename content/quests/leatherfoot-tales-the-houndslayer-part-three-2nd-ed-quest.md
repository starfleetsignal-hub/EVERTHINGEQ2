## Steps

1. Slay 20 [[A rabid hound|rabid hounds]].
1. Read the book.
1. Slay 20 [[A young blightrat|young blightrats]] {{waypoint -176, -9, -66}}.
   - It's possible that the [[A rabid pup|rabid pups]] around [[Brushfoot's General Store]] share the same spawn as the young blightrats.
1. Read the book.
1. Speak to the following NPC's:
   - [[Barmaid Rosaline]] {{waypoint -62, -17, -7}} - in [[The Fools Gold]] tavern in the great room
   - [[Beanna Inktoes]] {{waypoint -74, -16, -11}} - in [[The Fools Gold]] tavern in the great room
   - [[Bindo Halfbottom]] {{waypoint -79, -14, -22}}- in [[The Fools Gold]] tavern one room off of the great room
   - [[Banker Fargrove]] {{waypoint -67, -23, -52}} - behind the counter in [[The Pot of Gold]] bank.
1. Read the book to obtain your reward.
