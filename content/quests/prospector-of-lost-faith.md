## Steps

1. Use [[Stone Prayer Symbol]] at the following locations in the [[Butcherblock Mountains]]:
   - [[Glubbsunk Cove]] {{waypoint 674, 23, 253}}
   - [[Tidesylph Isle]] {{waypoint 567, 29, -119}}
   - [[Kragploom Mesa]]{{waypoint 479, 350, -165}}
   - Bugbear area [[Kamp Krulkiel]] {{waypoint 202, 261, -421}}
   - Entrance to [[Kaladim]] {{waypoint -171, 162, -515}}
   - [[Camp Yarpsnarl]] {{waypoint -793, 100, -323}}
   - [[Chessboard]] {{waypoint -836, 101, -21}}
1. Return to Bronlor.
1. Travel to [[Crushbone Keep]] in [[Greater Faydark]] and enter [[The D'Vinnian Throne]]. {{waypoint -59, 26, -42}}
1. Recover the *Chalice of Hope* in [[Throne of D'Vinn]]. {{waypoint 46, -8, -172}}
   - The chalice does not respawn, but if multiple group members have the quest all will get the update as soon as one gets the chalice.
1. Return to Bronlor Stormhammer.

## Rewards

- +2500 faction with **Brell Serilis**
- One of:
  - [[Obsidian Symbol of Serilis]]
  - [[Onyx Symbol of Serilis]]
