## Steps

1. Find [[Ferocious elemental]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]] at {{waypoint -751, -53, 408}}.
   - The elemental will turn into a 80^^^heroic, will attack upon getting close; kill it to update this step.
1. Return to [[Veryn P'Neru]].

## Note

This quest can be done at the same time as:

- [[Broken Barrier: Wounded of the Air]] in Kylong Plains.
- [[Broken Barrier: Wounded of the Water]] in Fens of Nathsar.
- [[Broken Barrier: Wounded of the Fire]] in Jarsath Wastes.
