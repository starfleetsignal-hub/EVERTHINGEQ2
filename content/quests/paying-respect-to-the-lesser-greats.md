Note: You must be at least level 85 to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Inspect all the gravestones just north of the Mausoleum of Scholars. {{waypoint 473, 234, -167}}
   - Inspect the first gravestone, that of Arch Wizard Intisar O'lothamar.
     - You will receive **Touch of Flame**, a one hour elemental mitigation buff.
   - Inspect the second gravestone, that of Accomplished Necromancer Azad Il'Matar.
     - Beware: [[A festering corpse]] will spawn and attack you when you do.
   - Inspect the third gravestone, where Master Illusionist Yathala Ja'Deey the Dreamweaver is buried.
     - Upon doing so, "Spirit of the Dreamweaver" is cast upon you - which can have multiple effects.  Each time you click the grave, a new illusion form will be cast.  If you have illusions hidden however, you may not see the effects, which are primarily cosmetic.  In a few cases, your maneuvering will be cosmetically adjusted regardless (i.e. if your illusion is zombie, you will lurch around whether or not you actually show the zombie illusion). Some possibilities: Gargoyle, Owlbear, Zombie.
   - Inspect the fourth gravestone, which has unfortunately been desecrated.
1. Return to [[Taysir Al'Babr]] with news of your findings.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
