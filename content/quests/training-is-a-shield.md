## Notes

- This quest is also part of the [[Heritage Quests Timeline]].
- You must complete a series of trials for Grozmag. All but the last take place in the instance, [[Grozmag's Trial]], located at the bottom of [[The Deathfist Quarry]]: {{waypoint 307, -114, -116.5}}

## Steps

1. Grozmag's First Trial:
   - Kill a single [[A Zek berserker|Zek berserker]].
   - Return to Grozmag. Respond with 2nd choice: You are exhilarated. If you don't, the quest will reset.
1. Grozmag's Second Trial:
   - Kill a mixture of [[A Zek berserker|Zek berserkers]], [[A Zek dark knight|Zek dark knights]] and [[A Zek defiler|Zek defilers]].
   - Return to Grozmag. Respond with 2nd choice: They performed poorly. If you don't, the quest may reset.
1. Grozmag's Third Trial:
   - Kill [[A Zek pummeler]] and [[A Zek warrior]].
   - Return to Grozmag.
1. Grozmag's Fourth Trial:
   - Kill a mixture of [[A Zek war wolf|Zek war wolves]] and [[A Zek destroyer|Zek destoyers]].
   - Return to Grozmag.
1. Grozmag's Fifth Trial:
   - Kill [[A Zek defiler]], [[A Zek berserker]], and [[A Zek war wolf|Zek war wolf]].
   - Return to Grozmag.
1. Grozmag's Sixth Trial:
   - Kill [[Grozmag's Champion]], [[A Zek inquisitor]], and two [[A Zek war wolf|Zek war wolves]].
   - Return to Grozmag. Response: You will do his bidding.
1. Kill [[Emperor Fyst (Named Monster)|Emperor Fyst]].
   - Enter [[Deathfist Citadel]].
   - Kill [[Chief Torturer Goortz]], [[High Priest Kuzbak]], [[Alchemist Yallessul]], [[Arena Champion Kraz'k]], [[Prince Gluntok]], and [[Prince Valtoris]] to spawn Emperor Fyst on the [[Emperor's Terrace]].
   - Emperor Fyst is also the last step of the quest, [[The Rescue of the Green Hoods]], so you might want to complete them together.
1. Return to Grozmag.
