Across Norrath they come by the thousands, gathering within the citadel known as Runnyeye. Eh, more meat for the grinder.

## Steps

*I must complete my mission in [[Fabled Runnyeye: The Gathering (Heroic)]]*

- Go on a goblin killing spree - Kill 100 goblins
- End the outraged evil eye
- Slay the champion eye poker
- Commit goblin regicide
- Deplete the Gorged
- Bushwhack the Boar Master
- Defy the Captain
- Flatten Da Gas Passer
- Pound down the Mighty Smith
- Bring the ruckus upon the Rowdy
- Annihilate the High Shaman
