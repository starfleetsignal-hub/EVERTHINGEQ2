## Steps

1. Find the area in the Lyceum of the Recondite where the Alchemical Draconic Conduit Gem should reside {{waypoint 201, -1, 81}}
1. Use [[Jorlak's Chrono Periapt (Dabbling in Draconic Alchemy)|Jorlak's Chrono Periapt]]
1. Pick up the scroll at {{waypoint 180, 0, 99}}
1. Scribe the [[Alchemical Draconic Conduit Gem (Recipe Book)|scroll]] and craft an [[Alchemical Draconic Conduit Gem (Quest Item)|Alchemical Draconic Conduit Gem]] (a level 2 crafter item). The Gem requires the following:![Flawless Drakiz Gem container](images/SkyshrineFLDGem.png)
   - 2 [[Flawless Drakiz Gem]] - Known locations: (Found as small pots on the ground in The Arcane Proving Ground (Southeastern most area). NB One gem is needed as the Primary Component and one gem is needed as fuel.)
     - {{waypoint 270, 0, 330}}
     - {{waypoint 211, -1, 324}}
     - {{waypoint 285, 0, 281}}
     - {{waypoint 144, -1, 352}}![Ground Ancient Drakiz Shell container](images/GADSSkyshrine.png)
   - 2 [[Ground Ancient Drakiz Shell|Ground Ancient Drakiz Shells]] - Known locations: (Found as tall pots on the ground in the outer central area)
     - {{waypoint 282, 0, 229}}
     - {{waypoint 289, -1, 167}}
   - 2 [[Undying Drakiz Blood]] - Known locations: (Found as small, brown and tan jugs with spigots in Effigy of Drakivess Mortis (Northwestern most area))
     - {{waypoint 336, 0, 46}}
     - {{waypoint 430, 0, -40}}
     - {{waypoint 405, -1, -110}}
     - {{waypoint 423, 0, 34}}![Undying Drakiz Blood container](images/UDBSkyshrine.png)
   - **NOTE: Harvesting some of the items above spawns adds. Make sure the group is together and you’re ready (or just run to a guard)**
1. Use [[Jorlak's Chrono Periapt (Dabbling in Draconic Alchemy)|Jorlak's Chrono Periapt]] to return to [[Jorlak]] and complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 50g
- +3,000 faction with **Claws of Veeshan**
- [[Counter of Draconic Alchemy Goods]] (house item)
