## Steps

- You receive [[Fully Trained Warwolf Mount Whistle]] ![The markers look like this ...](images/Upon_the_Wolves_of_War_quest_-_marker.jpg)
1. Go to Storm Gorge and summon your Warwolf! (use the whistle from your quest helper)
1. Ride your wolf to the first marker {{waypoint 1015, -428, 728}}
1. Ride your wolf to the second marker {{waypoint 752, -377, 853}}
1. Ride your wolf to the third marker {{waypoint 578, -381, 1092}}
1. Ride your wolf to the last marker {{waypoint 357, -361, 1336}}
1. Return to Lagur and Kagur

## Rewards

- [[Timber Wolf Mount Whistle]]
- At least 1p 15g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Howling Wolf Buckle]] (Mages & Priests)
  - [[Woven Warwolf Fur Belt]] (Fighters & Scouts)
  - [[Fangmarked Leather Belt]] (Mages & Priests)
  - [[Warwolf Studded Collar]] (Fighters & Scouts)
