## Notes

- This quest was originally the access quest for [[Fallen Gate]], but is not longer required.

## Steps

1. Talk to [[Tundis N'oxyle]] at the entrance to [[Fallen Gate]].
1. Tundis requires you to prove yourself by killing some of the [[A sandstone giant|sandstone giants]] that roam the [[Yapping Maze]] for five (auto-update) stones.
1. Return to Tundis who will tell you there will be a ceremony to grant you your key to Fallen Gate.
1. Return to [[Kanos X'aphon]] and inform him of your findings.
1. He will then inform you to gather a group, although this is not required. When you tell him you are ready he will port you to [[The Thexian Meeting]].
1. Inside the instance are level 12-14 enemies. The objective is to kill the two named Thexian leaders.
1. When you walk back down to the zone entrance, you will see Kanos waiting for you. Hail him for quest completion and zone out.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 10c
- One of the following:
  - [[Cap of the Betrayer]]
  - [[Circlet of the Betrayer]]
  - [[Heavy Circlet of the Betrayer]]
  - [[Hood of the Betrayer]]
