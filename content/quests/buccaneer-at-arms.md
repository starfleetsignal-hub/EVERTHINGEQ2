## Steps

1. Speak to [[Lieutenant Rhyslon]] outside [[Freeport Block & Tackle]] {{waypoint -209, -56, 139}}
1. Speak to [[Lazy Shae]] {{waypoint -255, -57, 131}}
   - **Stop mentoring**. Opponents will be at your full level, not your mentored one
1. Fight various even con opponents. Although Lazy Shae, [[Rayd Aerin]] and [[Groms Grollop]] will join the fight as even cons, they will not be worth AA. Beware of attacking a guard that walks through the area or you may have trouble with him.
1. Speak to Lieutenant Rhyson
1. Speak to three [[A Seafury recruit|a Seafury recruits]] wandering the dock around the Bait & Tackle
1. Speak to Kain Starkson

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c @ level 90
- [[Sunset Over Port Living]]
