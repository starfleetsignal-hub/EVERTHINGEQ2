## Steps

1. Zone in the [[Sleeper's Tomb: The Awakening]] a group heroic instance using the portal behind [[Jorlak]]
1. Find three ancient texts:
   - Kill [[Master of the Guard]] in the [[Vestibule of Peril]] {{waypoint 144, 11, -80}}
   - click scroll on the floor at the south end of [[Hall of Scrutiny]] {{waypoint 298, 21, 276}}
   - kill [[Ingolfa Arnar]] in [[Keening Undercroft]] {{waypoint 409, 21, -334}}
1. Enter the portal that opens in the center of the room; go all the way to the back of [[Warder's Dominion]] to kill [[The Final Arbiter]] {{waypoint -1364, -185, -87}}
1. Imprint the essence of the four Warders
   - Target the four epic dragons around the room and use the [[Essence Crystal]] on each
   - Although they are aggro they will not attack if you stay out of their circle (just run around the edge of their platforms)
