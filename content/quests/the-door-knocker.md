## Steps

1. Examine the plaques next to five tombs:
   - Tomb of Sir Penan {{waypoint -40, -9, 15}}
   - Tomb of Sir Breel {{waypoint -15, -8, 80}}
   - Tomb of Sir Haligan {{waypoint -80, -15, 20}} (upon examination you will be attacked by [[Sir Haligan, Captain of the Knights of Truth]]. Destroy him.
   - Tomb of Sir Arlin {{waypoint -15, -7, 90}}
   - Tomb of Sir Xantille {{waypoint -60, -11, 40}}
1. Return to Custodian Zaddar Sullissia. {{waypoint -73, -13, 107}}

## Rewards

- At least 1s 5c
- +150 faction with **The Academy of Arcane Science**
