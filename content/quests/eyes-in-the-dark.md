## Steps

1. You must find the 2 eyes of the dragon. These spawn underwater in the Drowned Caverns: Outer Grotto at the following locations:
   - {{waypoint -89.07, 5.96, 93.74}}
   - {{waypoint -13.84, 0.25, 53.6}}
1. Once you have both eyes you must return them to their master. This is the dragon skeleton in [[Sundered Splitpaw: Upper Tunnels]] at {{waypoint -49.36, -45.94, -68.38}}.
