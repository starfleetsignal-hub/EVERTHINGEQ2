## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[An arctic badger|arctic badgers]] near {{waypoint -1456, -526, -2307}} for their claws
1. Buy [[Miau's Foreign Herbs]]
   - These can be purchased (48s) from [[Chitter]] at {{waypoint -1598, -532, -2418}}
1. Add herbs to pot
   - Click on the pot right in front of Miau
1. Speak to [[Miau]]. She will grind the claws into powder (wait about 10 seconds)
1. Inhale the steam (Click the pot again)
1. Speak to [[Miau]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +300 faction with **Othmir of Velious**
