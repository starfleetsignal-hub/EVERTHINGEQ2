## Steps

1. Look for signs of the pale barbarian or his kin at {{waypoint -410, -338, 3492}}
1. Speak with [[An escaped Thrael'Gorr]]
1. Escort the [[An escaped Thrael'Gorr|escaped Thrael'Gorr]] to Ry'Gorr Keep {{waypoint -653, -342, 2691}}
   1. It is possible to just fly back to the Ry'Gorr.  As soon as you reach the indicated area, the quest will update.
1. Speak with [[Rogla Dainkiller]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +4,550 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
- [[Ry'gorr Chest]]
- Prefix Title: [[Clan Ry'Gorr Joiner]]
