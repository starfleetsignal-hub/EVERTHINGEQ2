Now that he has the kelp to help cleanse the waters of Spirit Lake, Bailin Grove, the Elder of the Green Hoods, requires my help in returning there. Not only must the Scourge orcs be cleared of the area, but they have to be stopped from coming back.

## Steps

1. Kill 9 Scourge orcs around [[Spirit Lake (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Spirit Lake]] {{waypoint -25, -4, -13}}. These include:
   - [[A Scourge warmage]]
   - [[A Scourge rager]]
   - [[A Scourge lookout]]
   - [[A Scourge invader]]
1. Speak with [[Bailin Grove the Elder (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Bailin Grove the Elder]] near [[Spirit Lake (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Spirit Lake]] {{waypoint -24.77, -5.76, 31.18}}
1. Set defense seed stones at each of the four entrances to [[Spirit Valley (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Spirit Valley]]. ![](images/Defense_stone_location.png)
   - Southeast {{waypoint -63.55, -13.38, 68.97}}
   - South {{waypoint 22.31, -8.34, 83.57}}
   - Southwest {{waypoint 49.58, -7.70, 41.17}}
   - Northwest {{waypoint 54.37, -4.74, -55.33}}
1. Speak with [[Bailin Grove the Elder (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Bailin Grove the Elder]] at the center of [[Spirit Lake (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Spirit Lake]] {{waypoint -63.92, -3.65, -91.86}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- +500 faction with **The Green Hoods**
- One of the following:
  - [[Blood Thorn]]
  - [[Eye of Battle]]
  - [[Portcullis of War]]
  - [[Fist of Skirmishing]]
