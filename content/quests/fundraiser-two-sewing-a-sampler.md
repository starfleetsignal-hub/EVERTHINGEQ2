Deputy Hopple doesn't have any interest in sewing, but I'll show him! The broadcloth cap he wants me to submit for the sampler sewing contest will be exquisite. If I'm not up to the task I can always buy my way out of this one, but I'm betting the prize will be something useful only to a skilled tailor. Either way, they'll be able to build the entire museum based on what folks will pay for my work. Dare I say, it might even make HIM look good?

## Steps

1. Make (or obtain) a [[Broadcloth Cap]].
   - Make them yourself, have someone else make them, or buy them from the broker.
   - After obtaining the necessary item, you may not get a quest update. If this happens, simply delete the quest from your journal. Upon accepting the quest again, it will give you the necessary update.
1. Return to [[Deputy Hopple]]
