## Steps

1. [[Researcher Tahar]] in what remains of [[Quel'ule]] has asked me to assist with his research by crafting an [[Amber lens]] for him.
   - Scribe the [[Directory of Mana Focusing Lenses]] in your inventory.
   - Craft an [[Amber lens]] at a Work Bench (requires 3 [[Amber]], 2 [[Quicksilver cluster]] and 1 [[Ethereal coal]]).
   - A Work Bench is just West of Researcher Tahar on the same wall.
1. Return to [[Researcher Tahar]].

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- +3000 faction with **Researchers of Quel'ule**
