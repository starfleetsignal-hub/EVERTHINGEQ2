## Prerequisite

You must be a level 40 or higher [[Fighter]] to receive this quest

## Steps

1. Enter [[Stonestair Byway: The Animus Corridors]] {{waypoint -6, -17, -328}}
1. Speak with [[Adiva Kariyya]] {{waypoint -6, -4, -83}}
1. Speak with [[Fateen Muhamin]] {{waypoint 46, -3, -20}}
1. Speak with [[Rasha Angorin]] {{waypoint 50, -2, -74}}
1. Speak with [[Nomala Pumarr]] {{waypoint -76, -4, -30}}
1. Walk near the Fountain of Anguish near {{waypoint 26, -4, -44}} and get attacked by 3 [[A stalking Kerran|stalking Kerrans]] (your level, double down, grouped)
1. Kill [[Ameer Ta'Reev]] {{waypoint 0, -3, -64}} near gate (even-con Guardian)
1. Kill [[Khalfani Tigirin]] {{waypoint -43, 0, -53}} near dock (even-con Guardian)
1. Speak to the Grand Duke in West Freeport

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
- [[A Freeport Militia Standard]]
