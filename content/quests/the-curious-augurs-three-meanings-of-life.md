*This quest is also part of the [[Enchanted Lands Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Answer [[The Curious Augur]]'s riddle correctly.
   - Choose the answer: *I have seven fear feeders and eighteen shoal serpents.*
1. Speak with [[The Curious Augur]] again.
1. Kill 7 [[Salt snapper|salt snappers]] on the beach near the [[Lost Village of Bobick]]. {{waypoint 102, -3, 39}}
1. Return to [[The Curious Augur]].
1. Speak with [[Sarma Singebellows]] just outside [[Rivervale]] near [[The Lazy Drain]] {{waypoint -193, -3, -260}}.
   - The repeatable quest Sarma offers does not need to be repeated if already done to advance this quest.
1. Return to [[The Curious Augur]].

- At least 17s 67c
