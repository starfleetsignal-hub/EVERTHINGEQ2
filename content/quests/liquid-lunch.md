## Notes

You need to be lvl 10 tradeskill to complete this quest. It will allow you to pick it up at a lower level and complete every other step but you will find yourself at the end unable to complete it at the stove due to the recipes not having autoscribed and there is no physical copy in your inventory so you don't have the option of quickly gaining levels to then complete either.

This quest is repeatable every 90 minutes from accepting, as long as Chronoportals event is available.

You receive a recipe book [[Liquefying and You]], which auto scribes.

Each ingredient for crafting the meal requires you to visit 8 different Portal to the Past instances and picking up one ground spawn, no need to fight anything. The items can spawn in multiple locations but very close to zone in.

This quest can be done at the same time as quest [[Hunt the Past]] in the same instances.

## Steps

1. "I must obtain one ingredient from each of the eight original Portals to the Past" Go back to [[The Chronoweave]] and visit other portals and pick up 1 item from each listed below.
   - Rat Meat from [[Portal to the Past: Qeynos Hills]] - dead rat in or around tents.
   - Karana Malt from [[Portal to the Past: Plains of Karana]] - blue and brown bottle close to "a ranger" NPC at the end of ravine.
   - Soda Water from [[Portal to the Past: Qeynos Ruins]] - green glass bottle in area in between stairs straight ahead from zone in. {{waypoint 1039.98, -19.01, -671.72}} or {{waypoint 1027, -19, -664}} *(Note: do not kill any bears or Holly will turn aggro and she is epic.)*
   - Corking Device from [[Portal to the Past: Steamfont]] - mechanical hand with faint sparkly effect, close to zone in, can be hard to see {{waypoint -12.8, -2.1, 7}}
   - Aged Cork from [[Portal to the Past: Nagafen's Lair]] - brown bottle near cages, can be hard to see.
   - Snake Meat from [[Portal to the Past: Southern Desert of Ro]] or [[Portal to the Past: Southern Desert of Ro (Public Quest)]] - dead snake around rocks by zone in.
   - Gator Meat from [[Portal to the Past: Ocean of Tears]] - dead alligator around pool of water at zone in
   - Ancient Cask from [[Portal to the Past: Guk]] - brown barrel in first room.
1. "I need to scavenge some nearby items" - go back to [[Portal to the Past: Grobb]] instance
   - harvest 3 x Mandrake Root - green wispy grass clumps
   - harvest 3 x Swamp Acid - yellow square bottles, look inside and around huts
1. Craft [[Liquidised Gator]], [[Liquidised Rat]] and [[Liquidised Snake]] using Grobb Brew Barrel near [[Grubthunk]]
1. Craft [[Meat Sludge]]
1. Craft [[Grobb Liquidised Meat]]
1. Speak with Grubthunk.
