## Walkthrough Summary

Assist [[Findink Topgallant]] in [[Karuupa Jungle: Heart of Conflict (Solo)|Karuupa Jungle: Heart of Conflict [Solo]]] by collecting his tools, using boom barrels to scare off Floraform creatures, and deploying mechano harpooners to aid his defense.
Defeat [[Ugweepai]] to complete the instance, then return to [[Shaman Bawiggi]].

## Steps

1. Return to [[Shaman Bawiggi]] at {{waypoint 730, 168, -395}}.
1. Enter [[Karuupa Jungle: Heart of Conflict (Solo)|Karuupa Jungle: Heart of Conflict [Solo]]] at {{waypoint 741, 168, -390}}.
1. Speak with [[Findink Topgallant]] just inside the entrance at {{waypoint 722, 106, -322}}.
1. Collect the following items beside Findink:
   - [[Findink's Boom Barrel|Findink's Boom Barrels]]
   - [[Findink's Mechano Harpooner|Findink's Mechano Harpooners]]
1. Help Findink complete his tasks:
   1. **Scare off 5 Floraform creatures**
      1. Place a Boom Barrel near each Floraform and **click to ignite** it for quest credit.
      1. Suggested placement locations:
         - {{waypoint 644, 94, -411}}
         - {{waypoint 515, 91, -345}}
         - {{waypoint 475, 91, -312}}
         - {{waypoint 607, 74, -269}}
         - {{waypoint 618, 87, -343}}

![Suggested boom barrel placement locations](images/Putstuffhere.png)

   1. **Deploy 3 Mechano Harpooners**
      1. Place a harpooner at each location, then **click to load** it for update.
      1. **Note:** You must load each harpooner after placement or this step will not update.
      1. Suggested placement locations:
         - {{waypoint 681, 66, -158}}
         - {{waypoint 706, 72, -67}}
         - {{waypoint 631, 71, -155}}
1. Defeat [[Ugweepai]] at the end of [[Karuupa Jungle: Heart of Conflict (Solo)|Karuupa Jungle: Heart of Conflict [Solo]]] to complete the instance objectives.
   - *See the [[Karuupa Jungle: Heart of Conflict (Solo)|instance page]] for a full encounter walkthrough.*
1. Return to [[Shaman Bawiggi]] to complete the quest.
