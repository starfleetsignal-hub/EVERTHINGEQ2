## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- This quest is one of several that you can do to earn the trust of the Splitpaw Gnolls and advance [[A Darkened Shard]].
- This instance is a zone consisting of multiple layers of underwater caverns. The water, as the zone name implies, is hot. Nodes called *a burning fissure* can cause one of two elemental based debuffs; bring either a priest or a lot of elemental cure potions to cure them. It isn't possible to completely avoid all the burning fissures.

## Steps

1. Zone into [[The Drowned Caverns: Outer Grotto]]: {{waypoint 43, -78, -228}}.
1. From there, zone into [[The Drowned Caverns: Hot Water]]: {{waypoint 21, 30, -42}}.
1. Your goal is to find and kill [[The Ghastly Rosch Mal]]:
   - Step into the upper submerged cavern at {{waypoint -84.56, 23.50, 86.94}}.
   - Swim down into the lower submerged cavern at {{waypoint -110.97, -0.35, 55.81}}.
   - Swim back up into the upper cavern at {{waypoint -71.85, -0.37, -1.45}}.
   - A hard-to-see passage leads into The Ghastly Rosch Mal's chamber. Go to {{waypoint -56.71, 20.84, -32.75}} and then head straight up.
   - The Ghastly Rosch Mal spawns at {{waypoint -94.14, 23.27, -52.73}}.
1. Return to Khol Mudflank in Splitpaw Den.

## Rewards

- Experience
- Does **NOT** count toward earning Splitpaw Gnoll trust.
