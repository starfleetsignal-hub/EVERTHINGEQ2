## Description

Extra effort will be needed in Sandstone Delta: Grave Grange to ensure the Hizites remain in relative safety throughout the southern deserts of Ro.

- I must protect the Hizites!
1. Enter [[Sandstone Delta: Grave Grange (Solo)]] {{waypoint 707, 119, -342}} near farm.
   1. Slay the scarecrow.
   1. Bleed the tick.
   1. Destroy the decomposer.
