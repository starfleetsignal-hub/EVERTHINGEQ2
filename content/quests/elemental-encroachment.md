## Steps

1. Kill 4-6 [[A Sablevein crumbler|Sablevein crumblers]]. {{waypoint -490, -61, 274}}
1. Return to [[Udiyitas X'Kilrae]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 24c
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Dark Bodyguard's Sandals]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Dark Evoker's Slippers]] (All Mages)
  - [[Dark Marshall's Boots]] (Warriors, Crusaders)
  - [[Dark Pathfinder's Boots]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Dark Spiritualist's Boots]] (Shamans)
  - [[Dark Vicar's Boots]] (Clerics)
  - [[Dark Windcaller's Boots]] (Druids, Channelers)
