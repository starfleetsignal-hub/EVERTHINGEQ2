## Steps

1. Slay 10 [[A freshwater crab|freshwater crabs]]. They are found at the mouth of the river, in the very southeast corner of The Commonlands {{waypoint -1287.13, -48.93, 596.03}}.
1. Return to [[Mooshga]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 11s 30c
- 10 [[Mooshga mush (QuestReward)]]
