## Steps

1. Collect a few specimens for Gildas in [[The Crypt of Betrayal]]:
   - Kill 15 [[A crypt tempest|crypt tempests]] - Minotaurs {{waypoint 83, 6, -20}}
   - Kill 12 [[A tortured essence|tortured essences]] {{waypoint 148, 7, -47}}
   - Kill 12 'Bloodsaber thugs* - no such mob as 'a Bloodsaber thug', but any of the Bloodsabers in the zone update. (Note: the quest actually states "recover arcane words from the...")
   - Kill 12 [[An ire minion|ire minions]] - these are in the alcoves (ire warlocks do not update quest).
1. Return to Gildas for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following (depending on class):
  - [[Ensorcelled Pantaloons of the Adept]] - ([[Conjuror]])
  - [[Leggings of the Befuddler]] - ([[Illusionist]])
  - [[Defiled Sanguine Pantaloons]] - ([[Warlock]])
  - [[Spellbound Cerise Pantaloons]] - ([[Wizard]])
