- [[Qeynos Capitol District|North Qeynos]] - near the castle drawbridge {{waypoint 390, -16, -38}}
- [[Greater Faydark]] - at the top of the ramp from [[The Green Knoll]] to Kelethin {{waypoint 456, 91, 166}}
- [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]] - at [[J'Narus Landing]] dock {{waypoint 16, 4, 309}}
- [[Timorous Deep]] - on the [[Faydedar Watch]] dock {{waypoint 2333, 2, 1443}}
