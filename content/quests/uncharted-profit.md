## Description

Lieutenant Kresic has come to Omen's Call to produce maps of the newly discovered locales for well paying patrons. I am to go transcribe a map for him of Veksar, and he will share with me the coin she earns from the transaction.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Veksar: The Sunken Theater]] and complete the following tasks:
   - Kill any 5 Yah-lei within the instance.
   - Examine the [[Unfinished Map of Veksar]] from your inventory while standing in the locations below:
     - [[The Lost Chancel]] {{waypoint 56, 0, -56}}
     - [[Lord Sasil's Encomium]] {{waypoint 34, 4, -86}}
     - [[The Campaign Chamber]] {{waypoint 77, 6, -95}}
     - [[The Golem Factatory]] {{waypoint 79, 6, -172}}
     - [[The Runearium]] {{waypoint 51, 6, -199}}
     - [[Cribblekeep's Treasureliquary]] {{waypoint -6, -1, -200}}
     - [[The Grand Sancloister]] {{waypoint -23, -1, -180}}
     - [[The Tomb of Lord Sasil]] {{waypoint -12, -3, -88}}
     - [[The Broken Theater]] {{waypoint -73, -3, -71}}
1. Return to Lieutenant Kresic.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
