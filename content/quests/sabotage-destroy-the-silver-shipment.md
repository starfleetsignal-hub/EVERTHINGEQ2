***This quest is only available to players under level 50***

## Steps

1. **Within 20 minutes**:
   - Gather a flask of chlorine in [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. Flasks can be found all over but the broker is a good place to start.
   - Use the chlorine to destroy 5 chests of silver in [[Qeynos Province District]] at [[Qeynos Harbor (POI)|Qeynos Harbor]]
1. Return to [[Gol M'Tun]].

## Rewards

- Faction Only:
  - +2000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
  - -2000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
  - -2000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**

## Notes

You can use the [[Mentoring]] system to perform this quest if you are too high level. You are not required to stay mentored once you have the quest active.
