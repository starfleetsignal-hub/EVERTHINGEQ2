## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Gather and hunt the following
   - Gather 12 of either
     - Elddar tablets in [[Ruins of Takish'Hiz]]  {{waypoint 83.52, 143.49, -733.28}}
     - boshglub orbs (little dark red balls) in [[Mhyt-moo Tidal Burrows]] {{waypoint 301.01, -0.95, -6.95}}.
   - Hunt 8 of either
     - Mhyt-moo zealots for runic shell strands in [[Mhyt-moo Tidal Burrows]] {{waypoint 301.01, -0.95, -6.95}}.
     - cursed Elddar for Geochantment Runes in [[Ruins of Takish'Hiz]] {{waypoint 109, 114, -615}}
1. Return to [[Kamyar]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
