## Steps

1. Investigate the icebergs to the south for the source of Lodizal's illness
   - Go to {{waypoint -1601, -541, -2076}}
1. Kill the undead and collect any clues they may drop
   - Kill 7 [[A defiling shadowbone|defiling shadowbones]]
   - Collect 3 clues (small harvestable stone statues that may spawn when a defiling shadowbone is killed)
1. Return to Nipik
