This mission is a delicate, and must be handled with great care and swiftness if I am to succeed. For the safety of those who inhabit the dark side of Luclin, I must not fail!

## Steps

- Dispatch the dark spirit in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket: Untamed Lands (Solo)|Shadeweaver's Thicket: Untamed Lands [Solo]]]
  - *entered from [[City of Shar Vahl]] at {{waypoint -131, 31, 242}}*
- Slaughter the serpent in [[Echo Caverns: Quarry Quandary (Solo)|Echo Caverns: Quarry Quandary [Solo]]]
  - *entered from [[City of Fordel Midst]] near the [[Luclin Nexus]] at {{waypoint 393, -36, 749}}*
- Hunt down the dark prowler in [[Savage Weald: Fort Grim (Solo)|Savage Weald: Fort Grim [Solo]]]
  - *entered near the skullsplitter camp at {{waypoint -33, 25, -242}}*

## Rewards

- At least 7p 48g 44s 138,599 status
- 35 [[Bazaar Crown]]
