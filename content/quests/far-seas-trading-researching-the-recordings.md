## Steps

- Speak to [[Jeremy]] at {{waypoint 142, 157, -168}}
- Gather the latest recordings from 5 [[A Daar sentinel|Daar sentinels]] on [[Daarspire]]:
  - Use the [[Sonic Tranquilizer (Item)]] to pacify [[A Daar sentinel]], then right click it and choose "Remove Recordings".
- Return to Jeremy.
- Assemble the recordings into a report book. The recipe is "Recording Transcript Record Book" and requires 5 Effulgent Incense and 2 rhenium ore at an Engraved Desk.
- Deliver the report to Ethan.
