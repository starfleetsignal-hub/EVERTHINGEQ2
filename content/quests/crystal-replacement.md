## Description

Pallasa Rargon, a member of the Order of Flame, a remnant of the ancient order that once inhabited the temple of Solusek Ro, welcomed my help in their ancient crusade against the invading Void army. They must first ensure that the invaders do not get closer to the Shrine of Thunder and the docks that bring new adventurers to Lavastorm.

## Steps

1. Replace 4 of the crystals in the wards along the beach leading into [[Najena's Cove]].  {{waypoint -50, -128, 456}}
   - *Note: These are to the left as you reach the shore. Just click on the rods. You can easily reach 4 of them without running afoul of the lacerators closer to the Cove, but beware of the slightly softer brutes!*
1. Defeat 2 of the foul creatures that may attack myself or the wards!  Kill [[An invading brute]].  Found at {{waypoint -73, -128, 454}}in the same area as the crystals.  These are spawned creatures lvl 80 which pop from the rocks between the wards so be prepared.
1. Return to [[Pallasa Rargon]].
