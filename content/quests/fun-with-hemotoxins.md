## Notes

- This quest can be done in conjunction with [[Tana's Task]] which also requires killing sharpthorns.

## Steps

1. Kill 10 sharpthorn shambling mounds which can be found just near the Greater Faydark station. {{waypoint -489, -54, 222}}
1. Return to Uric M'Ots to complete quest

## Rewards

- At least 9g 27s 1c
- +100 faction with **Nybright Family**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Canine's Shamanistic Tooth]] (All Priests)
  - [[Contained Rage of the Angry Goddess]] (All Mages)
  - [[Orb of Crystal Bolts]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sphere of Spirit Absorbtion]] (All Fighters)
