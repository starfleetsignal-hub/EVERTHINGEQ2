I found what looks like a wing from a giant insect of some form. It is shiny and has a spectrum of colors that gloss across it when held in the light. It could be used to paddle a boat. I feel oddly compelled to find out more about it, while on the dark side of this moon.

## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[Limestone needlite|limestone needlites]] within the [[Limestone Forest]] {{waypoint 454, 155, 458}} area of [[Echo Caverns]].

- [[Crown Receptacle (Tier 5) (Version 1)|Crown Receptacle (Tier 5)]]
- [[Large Insect Wing]] -- *collectible*
