## Steps

1. Gather the special ingredients for making swamp foot lotion:
   - Gather 10 tillage mushrooms from around the [[Tillage Patch Station]]. They do respawn in same places  so you can double back and collect some of the same ones.
   - Gather 10 pungent herbs. They appear as random harvests around the water reeds on the shores of [[Firmroot Moot]]. With standard graphics settings, these may be hard to see until you stand right next to them.
   - Harvest a bottle of gnomish grease sitting on the counter next to [[Hambert Cogpocket]] (Overseer of Operations) at the [[Dropship Landing Zone]] {{waypoint 1841.48, 452.75, 870.30}}.
1. Return to [[Hophip Valorfeet]] at Firmroot Moot and place the ingredients in the basket next to him {{waypoint 156, -89, -473}}.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2000 faction with **Survival Accord**
