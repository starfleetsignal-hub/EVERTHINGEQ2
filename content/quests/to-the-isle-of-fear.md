## Steps

1. Explore the [[Fear Tainted Isle]] for clues. Around {{waypoint 60, 248.54, 802}} you will get an update.
1. Continue north and [[A treasure guardian]] will spawn and attack. Kill it.
1. Return to [[Bilgeron Cogsworth IV|Bilgeron]].

## Rewards

- [[Aged Seeing Coin]] (examine this item to start the next quest)
  - ***Note:** If you lost it or didn't get it when you completted the quest talk to [[Bilgeron Cogsworth IV]] again.*
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Ornamental Sash of the Twilight Priest]] (All Priests)
  - [[Ornamental Sash of the Sky Warrior]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Ornamental Sash of the Aeromancer]] (All Mages)
  - [[Ornamental Sash of the Skystalker]] (All Scouts)
