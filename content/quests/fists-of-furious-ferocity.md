## Steps

1. Kill [[A disciple dissident|disciple dissident]]s, scattered around the zone near rock piles, until the key drops. (auto-update)
1. Open the chest all the way in the back of the instance. {{waypoint -240, 5, 315}}
1. Return to [[Feng Slow Fist]].

- At least 9g 20s
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Gemmed Mantle of the Desert Sword]] - fighters
