This is a subquest for [[The Word of the Harbingers - Qeynos]] and [[The Word of the Harbingers - Freeport]] and can only be attempted while you are on that quest.

## Steps

1. Enter the instance [[Poren's Trial (Zone)]].  The entrances are in [[Antonica]] for good-aligned or [[Commonlands]] for evil-aligned.
   - Antonica: Click on a boat near the lighthouse.  {{waypoint 411, -37, 791}}
   - Commonlands: Click on a boat near the docks. {{waypoint -1030, -146, -654}}
1. Defend the turtle nests
   - Kill the groups of aqua goblins around the sea turtle nests. Each time you kill a group the quest updates.
1. Return to Animist Poren.

## Rewards

- [[Medallion of Marisha Kur]]
