Clicking on the bottle to start the quest is a raid-wide update. All raiders are offered the quest simultaneously.

## Steps

1. Locate the stash of personal items mentioned in the note at {{waypoint 69.21, 7.32, 399.31}}.
1. Defeat [[Joggu the Steadfast]].
1. Retrieve the stash of personal items (this is a raid-wide update).
1. Defeat [[Sommumun the Frenzied]].
1. Read the book [[Journal of Tru'n Wyssker]] (auto-looted when opening the stash of personal items).
1. Defeat [[Bucko, Loyal Pet to Maferi]].
1. Defeat [[Chieftain Maferi]].
1. Speak to [[Tru'n Wyssker]] in [[Sodden Archipelago]] (overland, not the raid zone) at {{waypoint -574.50, 47.96, 854.49}}.

## Rewards

- At least 5p 1g 15s 50c
- Scars of Destruction Account Flag: Bonds of Mischief - Part 1
