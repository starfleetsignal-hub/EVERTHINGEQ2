## Prerequisites

- [[The Balance of Nature]]
- [[Mediate the Masters]]

## Steps

1. Zone into the [[The Forsaken City]] and speak to [[Saphis Once-Blessed]] {{waypoint 0, -45, 22}}
   1. Climb the wall at {{waypoint 34, -36, 58}}
   1. Take the stairs up {{waypoint 27, -24, 17}}
   1. Enter the cave {{waypoint 39, -17, 12}}
   1. Cave exits at {{waypoint 7, -6, -15}}
   1. Follow the rickety wood pathway to {{waypoint -244, -4, -97}}
   1. Swim to the Island {{waypoint -276, -7, 78}}
1. Gather and examine 4 clues from bookcases
   - [[Hall of Discourse]] {{waypoint -42, 15, -14}} a faded undelivered letter (the first clue)
   - [[Alchemic Alcove]] {{waypoint 40, 15, -24}} a yellowed message (the second clue)
   - [[Clerical Conclave]] {{waypoint -52, -70, 5}} a folded note (the third clue)
   - [[Esoteric Library]] {{waypoint 54, -58, 95}} a journal page (the fourth clue)
     - The pages must be examined in the Forsaken City
1. Return to Brother Lycabe.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 55s
- +200 faction with **The Whistling Fists Clan**
- One of the following:
  - [[Earhoop of the Droag Soldier]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earring of the Droag Soldier]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Earstud of the Droag Soldier]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Hoop of the Droag Soldier]] (All Priests)
- Completion of this quest, along with [[Brother Pang's Trial: Combating Corruption]], provides access to the third floor of the [[Tower of the Four Winds]].
