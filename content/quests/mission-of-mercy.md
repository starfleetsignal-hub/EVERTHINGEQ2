## Notes

The Goo Brew recipe auto-scribes. You do *not* need to locate it in inventory and scribe it manually.

## Steps

1. Mix the cure.
   - Use the Goblin Firepit next to him to create [[Goo Brew]].
   - Requires 100 Artisan, 3 slug slime, 5 [[Thaumic Coal]].
1. Help the injured goblin {{waypoint 485, -191, -507}}
   - Location may vary each time. Pay attention to where the charm shows you.
1. Return to Glibnox

## Rewards

- +4000 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
- At least 1p 53g 9s
