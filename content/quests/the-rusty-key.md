## Steps

1. Jump in the moat around [[The Deathfist Citadel]] and swim around until you get an update.  {{waypoint 44, -45, -790}}
1. Click the northernmost sewer grate and select [[Deathfist Citadel: Onslaught]]. The quest completes upon entry.
   - Every member of group must have the key to enter the instance.
     - It may be possible to enter as a group if one person has the key. That person needs to be alone, click on the grate, keep the selection box up, invite that person, then they can zone the group in.

## Rewards

- One-time access to [[Deathfist Citadel: Onslaught]], a group instance of [[Deathfist Citadel]]. The zone may be repeated, however, if you find an additional key. [[Magister Lugsnik]] resides in this instance, but does not award Alternate Advancement (AA) points or loot.  Several other named Heroic and Epic bosses exist. They do award AA but drop only generic 'Treasure Chest' loots appropriate to their level.
