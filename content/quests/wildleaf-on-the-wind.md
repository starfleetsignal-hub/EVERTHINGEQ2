## Steps

1. Search for [[A shadowed thicket hare]] near {{waypoint 584.80, 45.80, 257.43}} or {{waypoint 282.37, 45.61, 359.86}}
1. Use **Wildleaf's Figurine** on [[A shadowed thicket hare]].
1. Cleanse 12 [[A shadowed thicket hare]] using 'Wildleaf's Figurine'.
1. Find [[Julizia Windleaf]] at {{waypoint -254.21, 46.42, 348.80}} and defeat her when she attacks.
1. Return to [[Fedelar Wildleaf]] at {{waypoint 674.50, 57.68, -327.30}}.
