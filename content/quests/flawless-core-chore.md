## Steps

You must have completed [[Ry'Gorr Mining Operations]] from [[Thirin Veliumdelver]] to begin this quest.

1. Travel down to the [[Velinoid Catacombs]] and kill 6 [[A velinoid smasher|velinoid smasher]]s
1. Return to [[Knut Orcbane]]

## Rewards

- At least 5s 81c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Knight's Breastplate]] (Crusader, Warrior)
  - [[Channeler's Robe]] (All Mages)
  - [[Shade's Tunic]] (All Scouts)
