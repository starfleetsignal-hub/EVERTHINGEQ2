## Steps

1. Search [[Deino Shores]] for the missing hunting party {{waypoint 234, 6, 629}}![Hunting Party](images/Huntingparty.png)
1. Speak to [[Strug]] {{waypoint 236, 6, 632}}.
1. Search [[Savage Slough]] for Karuupa pygmies {{waypoint -535, 38, 158}} in Dedraka Village.  Then find the captured Karuupa pygmies {{waypoint -618, 125, -16}}.![Captured Hunter](images/Capturedhunter.png)
1. Free 6 *a captured Karuupa hunter*  by obtaining [[Dedraka Shackle Key|Dedraka Shackle Keys]] from small chest drops off Dedraka mobs. These are not guaranteed drops. Need 6 keys total -  one per pygmy.
   - {{waypoint -579, 44, 228}}
   - {{waypoint -587, 75, 133}}
   - {{waypoint -589, 75, 135}}
   - {{waypoint -674, 119, 112}}
   - {{waypoint -663, 119, 107}}
   - {{waypoint -612, 125, -20}}
   - Note: look for a tiny wooden chest, ONE(1) no-trade key will be contained within, which will open ONE(1) Pygmy's chains. (at least freed Pygmy is group-wide update)
1. Return to [[Strug]]
1. Search for [[Zaag]] - Find Zaag's pack {{waypoint -123, 3, -227}} ![Zaag's Pack](images/Zaag's_pack.png)
   - Follow the trail of feathers leading up the hill to the northeast to {{waypoint -252.15, 75.54, -509.16}}
1. Search for [[Zaag]] in [[Dead Dedraka Vale]] {{waypoint -332, 105, -620}}
   - Examine [[Zaag]]'s body, then slay the Libant vampire spawn.
1. Return to [[Chief Zubbluggib]] (Fast Way:  Evac to beginning of zone and head uphill!)
