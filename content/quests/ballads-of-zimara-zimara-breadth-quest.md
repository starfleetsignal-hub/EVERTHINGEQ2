## Notes

- The second quest in [[Ballads of Zimara Overseer Adventures]], claimable after purchasing [[Ballads of Zimara]] expansion.
- Once added to Overseer window the series stays there permanently and cycles through 3 quests:
  - [[Ballads of Zimara - Splendor Sky Aerie (Quest)|Ballads of Zimara - Splendor Sky Aerie]]
  - This quest
  - [[Ballads of Zimara - Aether Wroughtlands (Quest)|Ballads of Zimara - Aether Wroughtlands]]
  - then goes back to the first quest again.
- Any character on the account can start and complete any of the 3 quests if they show available.
