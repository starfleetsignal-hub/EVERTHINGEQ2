1. Obtain 12 *vials of blood* from devourers ([[A gnawing devourer|gnawing]], [[A gnashing devourer|gnashing]], and [[A feasting devourer|feasting]]) in the [[Skyfire Mountains]].
1. Return to [[Necromancer Corporiss]].
