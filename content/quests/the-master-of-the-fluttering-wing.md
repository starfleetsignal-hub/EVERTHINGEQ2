## Steps

1. Read through the book.
1. Go to [[Gunthak Pirate Camp]] in [[The Mystic Lake]] and interact with the bird cage hanging from the tree. {{waypoint 71, -37, 329}}
1. Find all 8 birdcages in the Mystic Lake and [[The Village of Shin]] within 20 minutes:
   - Mystic Lake
     - {{waypoint 145, -38, 76}}
     - {{waypoint 9, -2, -3}}
     - {{waypoint -285, 0, -106}} (rock surrounded by several pairs of undead mobs, 63^^ Heroic; clear or be sneaky; go to EAST side of tree and look due WEST; walk forward onto root, giving you some elevation; face due NORTH; jump onto rock)
     - {{waypoint -106, 9, 315}} (on ground)
   - Village of Shin
     - {{waypoint -251, 4, -71}}
     - {{waypoint -184, 7, -174}}
     - {{waypoint 38, 33, -156}}
     - {{waypoint 81, 13, -60}} (jump up on wall in front of the house, or the roof)
1. Return and examine the first birdcage in Mystic Lake (the one from Step 2) and release the bird spirit.
1. Follow the bird spirit to the entrance of [[Xux'laio's Roost]] at {{waypoint 149, -34, 38}}.
1. Enter the raid instance and defeat [[Xux'laio Master of the Fluttering Wing]].

## Rewards

- Permanent access to [[Xux'laio's Roost]]
- [[The Idol of The Soaring]] (House Item)
