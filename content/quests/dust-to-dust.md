## Steps

1. Use [[Pouch of Stunspore Powder|stunspore dust]] to kill 7 skeletons near [[Warpfont Spire]]. {{waypoint -150, -47, 200}}
   - The weaker skeletons near [[T'Vatar Post]] also update the quest.
1. Report your findings to [[Calnozz J'Melvirr]].
