1. Craft a [[Healing Poultice (Reviving the Ratonga)|Healing Poultice]] on the cooking pot nearby {{waypoint 1223, 19, 1213}} using the [[Healing Poultice (recipe for Reviving the Ratonga)|recipe]] which autoscribed at the completion of the previous quest.

1. Speak to [[Skrit]] {{waypoint 1211, 18, 1280}}
