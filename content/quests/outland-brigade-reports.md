- *This quest is also part of the [[Antonica Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Scout Dinardo is dead. You must collect his pieces of his report from the undead around [[Ruins of Karana]].
   - Kill skeletons that are at least level 27, either solo or heroic, for 5 uncommon updates, near the rear entrance to [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] at {{waypoint -486, 6, 1048}}.
     - Skeletons inside [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] also seem to work.
1. [[Scout Kaylinn]] can be found on the ledge just north of the [[Coldwind Shores Cemetery]], west of the gnoll towers overlooking the gnoll camp on the north edge of [[Thundermist Valley]] at {{waypoint 1342, 12, -226}}. Wait a few moments for her to appear.
   - *Note*: this is also the spawn point of [[Windfeather]], and it seems that if Windfeather has recently spawned then the scout will despawn, so try looking again in ~5 minutes.
1. [[Scout Deeryen]] can be found by interacting with a boulder at {{waypoint 902, 0, 1174}}.
   - This is the hilltop commonly referred to as *Goliath Island*, at the southernmost end of the left fork of the [[Dead River Basin]] where [[Obsid Boulderboom]] is located.
1. [[Scout Eldyum]] must be spawned. This is a 3-wave ring event. Stand near the griffon station end of the [[Village of Thundermist]]. Start the ring event by walking on the rock nearest [[Defender Bolaris]]/[[Defender Rowan]] at {{waypoint 604, 6, -163}}. After the third wave the scout will then appear among next to the rock you walked on to start the ring.
   - You no longer have to kill (or even attack) the third wave; you may allow the guards to take care of them. The scout will spawn after this mob is dead.
1. [[Scout Cendalya]] can be found on a rock ledge at {{waypoint 1410, 0, 308}}.

## Rewards

- At least 96s 67c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Steppes Strap of Defense]] (Fighters)
  - [[Steppes Strap of Destruction]] (Mages)
  - [[Steppes Strap of Restoration]] (Priests)
  - [[Steppes Strap of Stealth]] (Scouts)
