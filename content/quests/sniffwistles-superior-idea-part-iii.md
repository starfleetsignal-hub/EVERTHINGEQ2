## Description

"Grimply asked me to go get some huge spider silk for him this time. I guess he needs it to attach to the boppers cogspinner weebly." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay [[A huge spider|huge spiders]] at [[The Combine Spires]] around {{waypoint -499, 41, 244}} until you find a huge spider silk
1. Return to [[Grimply Sniffwistle]]

## Rewards

- At least 92c
- Experience
- Achievement Experience
