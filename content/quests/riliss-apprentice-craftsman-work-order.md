*Unexpected flooding near one of the temporary camps has destroyed many of their supplies. We need to restock so it is up to every skilled crafter to do their part.*

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

- I need to create:
  - (6) [[Waterproof sock storage cabinet|waterproof sock storage cabinet]]
  - (6) [[Spiked wall segment|spiked wall segment]]
  - (6) [[Standard issue ration pack|standard issue ration pack]]
  - (6) [[Hornet honey mead|hornet honey mead]]
  - (6) [[Shield reinforcing strut|shield reinforcing strut]]
  - (6) [[Longbow of accuracy|longbow of accuracy]]
- Turn the completed order into [[Preparations Officer Zekilius]]

## Rewards

- At least 41g 61s 68c
- +750 faction with **Riliss**
