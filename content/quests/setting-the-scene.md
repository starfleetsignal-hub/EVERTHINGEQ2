## Steps

1. Scribe the [[Othmir Folding Table (Recipe)|Othmir Folding Table]] recipe.
1. Craft an [[Othmir Folding Table (House Item)|Othmir Folding Table]]
   - You'll need 3 [[Sumac lumber]], 1 [[Titanium ore]], 1 [[Ethereal sandpaper]]
   - You can use the [[Woodworking Table]] located behind [[Matthew]] {{waypoint -1642, -533, -2380}}
1. Place the table on the dock {{waypoint -1409.18, -542.57, -2078.92}}
1. Return to Arjon {{waypoint -1378.59, -533.50, -2143.70}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
