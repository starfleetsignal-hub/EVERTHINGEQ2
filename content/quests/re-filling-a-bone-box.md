## Prerequisites

- You must have at least +17,800 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Obtain 8 *arcane enriched bones* by killing [[A dragonmage spellslinger|dragonmage spellslinger]]s around [[Jaled Dar's Remains]]. {{waypoint -1600, 0, -1550}}
1. Go back to [[The Sheet]] and deposit the bones in the crate. {{waypoint -2666, -412, -2694}}
1. Speak with [[Bragek Joll]]. {{waypoint -2656, -412, -2702}}

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- +1600 faction with **The Order of Rime**
