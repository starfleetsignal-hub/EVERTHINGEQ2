## Preparation

If at level with quest, no issue; However, if coming as/with Mentored, removing Auto-damage buffs will prevent one from "one-shotting" the golems. Bringing a Shaman and having their pet only attack will slow combat down enough to use device (read: SPAM the device) at 50% health before golem dies. If no pet assistance available, then equip previous-era gear which should prevent killing them too quickly.

Remember that you need to be lvl 90 to enter the Lyceum. Your Class vendor also sells spells called "Methodical Strikes" and "Dial it Back". You'll have problems with using Dial it Back since the mobs are quite strong. I dismissed my familiar (necro), used "Methodical Strikes" and managed to get them down 1 by 1. It's hard though - even Mentored. Might be the most challenging Quest i've done in quite some time.

## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[An Osseous Corruptor]]. When he’s under 50%, use the [[Nechronosync]] item to make his body split apart. Then finish the mob and click on the corpse to get the update
1. Kill [[Necranaut Dolk]] at {{waypoint 376, 1.77, 70.26}}
1. Speak to [[Jorlak]]
1. Speak to [[Tavoken]]
1. Speak to [[Jorlak]] to complete the quest.
