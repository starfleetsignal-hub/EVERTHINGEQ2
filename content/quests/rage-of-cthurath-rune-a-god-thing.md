## Steps

1. Enter [[Gerion, Hold of D'Lere]]. Use the Rift at {{waypoint 564, 94, -694}}
1. Use the spinning teleport close to Lady Najena at {{waypoint -119, 1, -68}} to enter [[Gerion: Rage of Cthurath (Signature)|Gerion: Rage of Cthurath [Signature]]].
1. Find Lucan D'Lere's throne room
   - Clear the room (you will see message that teleport spindle has been activated), then use the **Teleport to Throne Room** portal at {{waypoint -119, 1, -68}}
1. Lady Najena at {{waypoint 85.38, 955.84, 11.72}} will instruct you to place the final cimmerott runic ward.
   - Place the final commerott runic ward at {{waypoint -71, 969, 0}}
1. Face Lucan and defeat the Manifestation of The Consumer
   1. Clear the room first as the boss fight pulls the room when you attack him
   1. Defeat the [[Manifestation of The Consumer]]
      - Goes invulnerable at times
      - Power Drains
      - When Lady Najena says "Get behind the stone I summoned!" get behind the large stone that appears in front of the throne on the slightly lower platform and stay there until the stone is destroyed.
      - A portal will open on the ground in front of the named and waves of adds enter, kill them to re-engage the named and repeat. Ignore the named until each wave has been killed as you can't attack the named during this stage
1. Speak with [[Overlord Lucan D'Lere (Gerion Signature)|Lucan D'Lere]].
1. Speak with [[Lady Najena]].
