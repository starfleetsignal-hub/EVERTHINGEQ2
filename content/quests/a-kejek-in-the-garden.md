## Steps

1. Since you last found evidence of missing Kejek sojourners near [[The Marred Plateau]], Pantheera has asked you to narrow your search to the nearby [[Erudin Gardens]].
   - Head to the far North end of the Erudin Gardens, to the captured Kejekan sojourners at {{waypoint -160, 524, -481}} (The four Kejekan sojourners suspended in the air.)
1. Attempt to release the kejeks, which will spawn [[An alerted Theerian shadowblade]] which attacks you.
1. Try again to release the captured Kejekan sojourners.
1. They feel doomed to their capture. Return to [[Pantheera]] with this unfortunate news.

- At least 55g 70s 61c
