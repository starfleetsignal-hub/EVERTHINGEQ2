## Steps

1. Speak with a satyr by the name of [[Ignatios]], located behind a rock in the Thexian camp in Lesser Faydark. {{waypoint -527, -60, 413}}
1. Within 30 minutes, escort him to Hearth Cymenea in the [[The Fugutr Tribe|Fugutr tribe main camp]]. {{waypoint 109, 13, -460}}
   - You are able to use the horse to travel to the Upper Grove station, and not risk losing Ignatios.
   - If you have a flying mount Ignatios will completely disappear while you are in the air. This will allow you to fly the entire way to the drop off point without Ignatios ever being in danger.
1. Hail [[Ignatios]] to complete the quest, and he will offer you the next quest.
