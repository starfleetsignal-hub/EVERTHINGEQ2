## Notes

- This quest can be completed twice for two different sets of rewards.

## Steps

1. Go to the [[Fount of Awakening]] and inspect another chest. {{waypoint -57, 78, 170}}
1. Slay 12 [[A silent sentinel (heroic)|silent sentinels]] (minimum target level of 65) for common updates.
1. Return to the second chest and open it. {{waypoint -57, 78, 170}}
1. You are now on a timer of 10 minutes to return to the 1st chest at {{waypoint 29, 128, 203}}.
1. Open the first chest to receive your reward.

## Rewards

First time:

- [[Lockless Buckler]]
- [[Bonecleave Chakram]]

Second time:

- [[Lockless Orb]]
- [[Marrowgore Chakram]]
