This quest is part 13 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Read the book.
1. Kill 5 [[A motley kodiak|motley kodiaks]] - found anywhere between [[Mound Lake]] and [[The Funeral Pyre]]. Though most are north along the river near [[The Funeral Pyre]].
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 3 [[An Asilian vandal|Asilian vandals]] - found at [[Mound Lake]] {{waypoint -607.94, 0, -528.99}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 3 [[A deadly rumbleroot|deadly rumbleroots]] - found near the owlbear camp.{{waypoint -700, 63, 110}}
1. Read the book to obtain your reward and close the quest.
1. Return to [[Jaeta H'arn]] on the beach for the next quest.
