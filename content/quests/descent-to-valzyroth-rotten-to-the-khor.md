## Steps

1. Speak with [[Maygan Healhand]] {{waypoint 45.81, 200.16, -578.33}}
1. Enter [[Khor Boldir: Corrupted Burrow]] - zone is inside [[Khor Boldir]] - Portal from [[Val'Zyroth Ness]] is at {{waypoint 108.34, 89.37, -1194.53}} Zone door is inside at {{waypoint 31.35, 62.21, 171.61}}
1. Begin the search for the mining crew. {{waypoint 80.27, 43.72, 115.95}}
1. Explore Khor Boldir in search of the mining crew. {{waypoint 164.80, 43.70, 118.87}}
1. Examine a destroyed irontoller.
1. Cull (x) aggressive tontu. Receive Gnemlin Mining Tools.
1. Keep searching for the miners {{waypoint 306.98, 69.09, 64.82}}
1. Defeat 3 tontu threatening the miners.
1. Speak with the survivors.
1. Search for [[Jackal]]. {{waypoint 255.46, 43.81, 193.40}}
1. Examine [[Jackal]] and carry him to safety.
1. Return to escapee camp to get [[Jackal]] some help. In [[Val'Zygoth Ness]] {{waypoint 45.51, 200.15, -576.32}}
1. Speak with [[Maygan Healhand]].
1. Enter [[Khor Boldir: The Brellspring]] - inside [[Khor Boldir]] from Val'Zyroth Ness {{waypoint 108.34, 89.37, -1194.53}}. Zone inside to find door at {{waypoint 2.66, 66.64, 104.09}}
1. Search for [[Shaman Poha]] {{waypoint 192.84, 77.05, -91.95}}
1. [[Shaman Poha]] ran deeper into the Brellspring {{waypoint -110.12, 80.83, -47.43}}
   - Lift lever {{waypoint -119.36, 81.12, -17.91}}
1. [[Shaman Poha]] {{waypoint -96.89, 103.26, -23.55}}
1. Demand [[Shaman Poha]] return the dark reliquartz gem.
1. Follow after for the svik that stole the dark reliquartz gem
   - Use minecart lever {{waypoint -99.75, 103.56, -8.51}}
1. Made it up to Toiling Terrace but Rotscaled must have ran further
   - clear the temple area to spawn Draveth the Severed {{waypoint 10.51, 117.53, -95.63}}
1. Seek the Rotscaled {{waypoint 8.60, 83.87, -219.45}}
1. Claim the dark reliquartz gem from Rifki the Rotscaled {{waypoint 35.94, 77.66, -282.94}}
1. Return to Gnemlin Refuge where [[Shaman Poha]] waits for the reliquartz - in [[Val'Zyroth Ness]] {{waypoint 47.19, 200.04, -579.05}}
1. Speak with Jakal
