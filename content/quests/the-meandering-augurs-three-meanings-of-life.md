## Steps

1. Kill [[A Tallon grunt (Zek, the Orcish Wastes)|Tallon grunt]] for 3 updates. {{waypoint 645, -38, 225}}
1. Return to The Meandering Augur.
1. In [[The Thundering Steppes]] harvest [[Bush Nodes#T4|high plains shrubberies]] for 20 updates.
   - *Bountiful Harvests* count for updates.
1. Return to The Meandering Augur.
1. Visit the [[Grove of Stones]] in Zek. {{waypoint 210, 14, 108}}
1. Return to The Meandering Augur.

## Rewards

- At least 17s 67c
