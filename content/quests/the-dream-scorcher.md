## Requirements

- You must be a level 80 [[Defiler]] to begin the quest.
- You must know [[A Mysterious Green Tome|Froak]] the language of Froglok. this a quested language starting with the collection
- You must know [[Ykeshan]], the language of the trolls, for step 2.
- You must have at least +40K faction with the [[Synod Reet (Faction)]] for step 3 of the quest.
- Reet Faction questing may require Froak language.
- You must be at least a lvl 2 artisan to craft the medicine bag (step 3).

## Steps

1. **Obtaining the Quest:**
   1. Talking to [[Crowhood the Olden]] in [[Fens of Nathsar]] at {{waypoint -2631, -396, -2104}} (northeast of Bellywhumper Burrows, Dragon's Rest is the closest sokokar post to her location) reveals that she lost "it" (her medicine bag) to a monster.
   1. You can find said medicine bag on the body of [[Guagua]] north of her swimming near {{waypoint -2721, -475, -2265}}.
   1. Take the medicine bag to Crowhood and receive the actual Quest [[The Dream Scorcher]].
1. **Placing the Totem:**
   1. Crowhood sends you to place [[Crowhood's Totem]] at the following locations:
      - [[Fens of Nathsar]]: near [[Protectis]] near Cabilis at {{waypoint 331, 17, -1437}}
      - [[Kylong Plains]]: in the Yeti caves near Teren's Grasp at {{waypoint 1327, 410, -1040}}
      - [[Jarsath Wastes]]: in the Temple of the Red Lord at {{waypoint -2462, 315, 297}}
      - [[Kunzar Jungle]]: near the Iksar Village close to Jinisk, at the ledge with the grave (climb up to {{waypoint 180, 87, -417}})
   1. Inspect the nearby grave (you have to speak Ykeshan) and prepare for a fight with four nightbloods (level 84) that spawn almost immediately. Move them away from the grave, because after them, another nightblood named Incubaa (Level 84, Heroic, ^^^) spawns. He fears, knockbacks and hits pretty hard, so you should bring friends.
   1. Return to [[Crowhood the Olden]].
1. **Creating the Medicine Bag:**
   1. Crowhood sends you to speak to another shaman, namely [[Mizzog the Sighted]] in [[North Freeport]] near the Jade Tiger Den at {{waypoint -45, -24, -51}}.
   1. Mizzog tells you to create something to protect your mind (four items and a medicine bag to carry them) and provides you with a list of ingredients, a recipe and a sewing kit.  If you lose one of them, the box next to Mizzog can be harvested to receive those things again.
   1. You need (in any order though you will need Huntmaster clasp to make medicine bag):
      - [[Master of the Hunt Leash Clasp]] from [[Huntmaster Viswin]] inside (not in front of) [[Castle Mistmoore]] 2nd level North tower. LOC of entrance to Castle Mistmoore is {{waypoint 518, 209, 174}}
        - *Note: This is an EPIC x2 step! So bring friends*
        - *Note: only one update if you have multiple Defilers in your group!*
        - *Note: the PH will also drop quest item*
      - A [[Burnt Scripture of Fear]] from the [[The Priest of Fear]] in [[The Estate of Unrest]].
      - A [[Shissarian Dreamward]] from [[Majora Leviathora]] in [[Chelsith]] *Note: You must kill the other three Leviathan in the zone in order to break the barrier around Majora.*
      - An [[Fear Etched Idol]] from a skeleton in [[Snaggletooth]]'s room in [[Sebilis]] at {{waypoint -149, -133, 339}} on second level (via underwater tunnels).
      - A [[Quivering Sphere of Panic]] bought from [[Bulgug Urple]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]] at {{waypoint 474, -96, 909}} (need 40.000 faction with Synod Reet and 2P 40G to buy)
      - A **medicine bag**:
        - Scribe the recipe and go and find the following ingredients:
        - 20 [[Piece of obsidian]] dropped from docile fire elementals (use tinkered pets to pull if one-shotting them) in the lava rivers in the Skyfire Mountains range in [[Jarsath Wastes]] Less than common drop.  However a *magmatic destroyer* drops 2 updates at a time, spawn when you are in their "zone" of the river.
        - 1 hidebound pelt (harvested, from guildhall or from broker)
        - 100 lichenclover roots (harvested, from guildhall or from broker)
        - 10 smoldering filament (fuel from fuel merchant, tailor or wholesaler)
        - Return to the [[Fens of Nathsar]], proceed to the [[Field of Bone]] and use your "defiler sewing station" inside the giant ribcage close to the head {{waypoint -1519, -371, -1515}}.
        - Create your medicine bag - if you fail you only need more filaments, however you must be higher than an Unskilled artisan to craft it (as of 12/07/2009).
   1. Return to [[Mizzog the Sighted]]. She tells you to find an amulet and summon a powerful spirit.
1. **Subduing Spirits:**
   1. First, head to [[City of Mist]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]]. Killing MOBs here yields [[Shulatu's Talisman]] as a rare drop.  Any mob appears to be able to drop it, and the item drops from a wooden chest.
   1. Head back to the grave at {{waypoint 180, 87, -417}}, and talk to [[Shulatu Darkskar]], a ghost, who will ask you to subdue eight animal spirits.
      - The animal spirits are located all around Kunark and require you to use the spell Spirit Sight, (Defiler Spirit Sight), which Shulatu will give you. All of them should have a knockback and stun. They are single-player mobs but level 84, so it won't hurt to at least bring one other person with you. They roam in circles about the following locations:  Note:  The order listed here is for geographical convenience, not the actual order listed by the quest.  Animal spirits are trackable.  Using the Quick Access Teleport or "Fast Travel" on the EQ2 Map is extremely helpful!
      - Tiger - [[Kunzar Jungle]] around {{waypoint -202, 115, -336}}
      - Gorilla - [[Kunzar Jungle]] around {{waypoint -471, 12, 124}}
      - Chokidai - [[Kunzar Jungle]] around {{waypoint 652, 98, -646}}
      - Shark - [[Fens of Nathsar]] around -1,157.74 -445.15 -2,992.21, {{waypoint -2060, -550, -3069}}  {{waypoint -1402, -425, -3065}} {{waypoint -1777, -448, -2992}}
      - Cockatrice - [[Fens of Nathsar]] around {{waypoint -259, -83, 1372}}
      - Crocodile - [[Fens of Nathsar]] around {{waypoint -135, -408, 949}}
      - Rhino - [[Jarsath Wastes]] around {{waypoint 632, -45, 681}}
      - Wyvern - [[Jarsath Wastes]] around {{waypoint -2108, 175, 1373}}
   1. Return to the grave, start the ritual (click on the mound). Bring a group, because after Shulatu's speech, a series of heroics will spawn (2 waves: of 3 and 4, 83^^). Kill them and [[Shulatu Darkskar]]'s ghost (84^^^). Accept the rings.
   1. Return to Mizzog. She wants you to find a strong spirit.
   1. Kill [[Drusella Sathir]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]] [[Charasis: Maiden's Chamber]] entrance at {{waypoint 815.35, -128.06, 372.22}}.
   1. Return to Mizzog. She will hand you [[Mizzog's Dream Totem]] to enter the dream world ([[Astra, the Dreamscape]]).
   1. Use this item while in [[North Freeport]] to pull your group into the dream world.
   1. Clear the zone (don't forget the named nightblood INSIDE the ziggurat, under the stairs), and kill [[Kullaba Dreadpool]] to receive your epic.
