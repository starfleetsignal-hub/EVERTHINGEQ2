## Note

- Appears to level max out at Level 90 in quest journal

## Steps

- I must catalog a clockwork gnome scrapbot (within the [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]]). {{waypoint -583.01, 174.84, 927.94}}
- I must catalog a cargo clockwork spider (within the [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]]). {{waypoint -597.15, 173.95, 989.54}}
- I must catalog a wayward clockwork gnome in Steamfont Mountains (near the [[Steamfont Windmill]]).
- I must catalog a clockwork disassembler in Steamfont Mountains (near the [[Steamfont Windmill]]).
- I must catalog a clockwork collector in Steamfont Mountains (regularly sent from [[Klak'Anon]] to investigate the [[Smokehorn Basin]]).
- I must catalog a clockwork explorer in Steamfont Mountains (near Klak'Anon).
- I must catalog a glitched clockwork spider inside Klak'Anon (as soon as you zone in).
- I must catalog an impaired clockwork gnome inside Klak'Anon (as soon as you zone in).
