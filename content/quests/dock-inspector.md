Tusk, an orc member of the Green Hoods, was stationed at the Sullon mines outpost as an informant for the Green Hoods, but when the Scourge began wiping out the Deathfist orcs he abandoned his post. He made his way to Green Hood Landing in hopes of finding other Green Hood survivors.

## Steps

1. Explore the [[Warship Docks (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)]] at {{waypoint 503, -41, 304}}.
1. Search the east tower of the fort for survivors at {{waypoint 460, -27, 249}}.
1. Search the west tower of the fort for survivors at {{waypoint 515, -27, 222}}.
1. Search for the source of the boulder attack in [[Tallon Field]] at {{waypoint 439, -24, 146}}.
1. Speak to [[Shadow (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Shadow]] by the working catapult in [[Tallon Field]] at {{waypoint 439, -24, 146}}.
1. Speak again to [[Tusk (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Tusk]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 9,954 status
- +500 faction with **The Green Hoods**
