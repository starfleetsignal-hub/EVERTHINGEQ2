## Notes

- This quest requires:
  - 6x [[Formless Coal]] and a work bench.
  - There is no work bench and no fuel merchant in [[Gerion: Ark of Ascension (Tradeskill)]]. Bring tinkered portable work bench or be prepared to summon one from your AA abilities. You can bring fuels with you, use broker or any of the fuel merchant summoning items if you have them. You can also leave the zone, craft elsewhere and come back. If your account is already flagged and able to enter Yon Gorroth, you can buy fuels there and zone into Gerion from Yon Gorroth side.
  - When you start the quest you'll receive [[Rift Stabilization Device v1]] and a [[Recipe Scroll: Riftside Reapers|recipe]] from [[Flamebearer Denat]]. Do not craft anything until the quest asks for it.

## Steps

1. Enter the [[A violent rift|Rift]] by selecting the destination of [[Gerion: Ark of Ascension (Tradeskill)]] zone.
1. Place the [[Rift Stabilization Device]] at {{waypoint -61, 1, -158}} next to the Rift.
1. Speak with [[Flamebearer Denat]] at {{waypoint -49, 0, -147}} next to the Rift.
1. Scavenge 6 pieces of [[Discarded Lucanic Armor Piece|Discarded Armor]] which may be found at the following 2 locations: {{waypoint 14, 0, -219}}, {{waypoint -15, 0, -220}}.
1. Craft [[Avarice Lucanic Sentinel Illusion]].  ![](images/Destination_Unknown_Stabilization_quest_-_Discarded_Armor.jpg)
   - Requires 5 [[Formless Coal]] and a [[Work Bench]]
1. Don the [[Avarice Lucanic Sentinel Illusion]] disguise.
1. Collect 7 [[Oddly Resonating Shard]] pieces.  ![](images/Destination_Unknown_Stabilization_quest_-_Oddly_Resonating_Shard.jpg)
   - Some possible locations include:-
     - {{waypoint -29, 0, -376}}, {{waypoint -58, 0, -359}}
     - {{waypoint -101, 0, -502}}, {{waypoint 97, 0, -510}}
     - {{waypoint 140, 0, -437}}, {{waypoint 105, 0, -392}}
     - {{waypoint 77, 0, -339}}
1. Speak with [[Flamebearer Denat]] at {{waypoint -49, 0, -147}} next to the Rift.
   - Accept [[Rift Stabilization Device v1]]
1. Craft [[Rift Stabilization Device v2]]
   - Requires 1 [[Formless Coal]] and a [[Work Bench]]
1. Place the [[Rift Stabilization Device v2]] at {{waypoint -61, 1, -158}} next to the Rift.
1. Exit the Rift to [[Yon Gorroth]]. This completes the Quest.
