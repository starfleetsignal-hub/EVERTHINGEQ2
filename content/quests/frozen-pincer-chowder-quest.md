Olga Macleod wants to enter her recipe for frozen pincer chowder into the New Halas' City Festival cook off. In order to do so, she needs some specific ingredients that she asked me to collect.

## Steps

1. Collect **a sprig of halasian reeds** at {{waypoint -446, 22, 99}}
1. Kill 4 [[A frozen pincer]] around {{waypoint -430, 25, 79}}
1. Return to [[Olga Macleod]]
1. Pick up a bowl of frozen pincer chowder (Right next to her)

## Rewards

- At least 12s 2c
- 4 [[Frozen Pincer Chowder]]
