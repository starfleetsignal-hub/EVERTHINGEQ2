## Steps

1. Go to [[J'Rais Bridge]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] (The bridge that leads from [[The Commonlands]]). Inspect the skeleton on the bridge at {{waypoint -256.8, 4.91, 220.69}} to be offered the quest [[A Calling in the Forest]].
1. Speak to [[Theeral the nomad]] to be given the [[Flesh Bound Tome]].
   - Obtaining the book updates this quest.
1. Go to the door leading to [[Lord Everling's Secret Laboratory]] at {{waypoint 174, -13.5, 10}} in [[Nektropos Castle]].
1. Return to Rikantus in Antonica. Speak to him and then click the chest in the tent behind him.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 74s 63c
