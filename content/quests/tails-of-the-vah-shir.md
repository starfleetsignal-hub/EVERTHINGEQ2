## Steps

1. Speak to [[Junior Hymnist Wadakha]] in [[The Jharin Assemblage]] {{waypoint -76, 31, -392}}. Complete [[The Center of Spirit]] and return to Junior.
1. Speak to [[Jalid Mpenda]] in the [[Keep of The Khala Dun]] {{waypoint -285, 46, -320}}. Complete [[The Center of Heritage]] and return to Jalid.
1. Speak to [[Scout Amani Abasi]] at the end of [[Shar Vahl Pier]] {{waypoint -40, 36, -43}}. Complete [[The Center of Exploration]] and return to Amani.
1. Answer [[Scout Amani Abasi]]'s questions:
   - A good spot to see oncoming invaders down the road.
   - The owlbears would make quite a bit of noise if a large force was coming.
   - This would definitely be a rest stop for oncoming forces.
   - You can hear the hum of power coming from their barriers.
   - The activity on Pirates Path is easy to watch for anything suspicious.
1. Return to [[Junior Hymnist Wadakha]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 90g 2s 4c 69440 status
