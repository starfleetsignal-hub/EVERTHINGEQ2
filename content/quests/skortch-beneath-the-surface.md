## Steps

1. Investigate goblin activity in the northern section of [[Blackhook Territory]] at {{waypoint 145.51, 183.20, -524.52}}
1. Kill the following
   - Slay 5 [[A Blackhook zealots]]
   - Slay the [[A frenzied ulteran spikewolf]]
1. Speak to [[Eleana Stormalong.]]
1. Speak to [[Eleana Stormalong]] back at [[Blackhook Bastion]] {{waypoint 289, 283, -730}}
1. Return to Skortch
