## Steps

(Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 214, -20, 243}} to the [[Prophecy Portal Gallery]] and enter [[Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands]] through the portal at {{waypoint 714, 412, 217}})

1. Obtain the following:
   - 10 **pusling skins** from puslings in the [[Valley of Decay]] {{waypoint 1, 95, -185.47}}
   - 12 **decaying flesh** from fleshclots and putrid piles around [[Bileburn Clot (Detroxxulous)|Bileburn Clot]] {{waypoint 33, 86, -325}}
1. Speak to [[Kanlum]] at {{waypoint 497, 83, 47}}.
1. Erect the [[Disease icon]] in the middle of the [[Rotmere]] cesspool at {{waypoint 193, 394, -2}}
1. Speak to [[Kanlum]] at {{waypoint 497, 83, 47}}.
