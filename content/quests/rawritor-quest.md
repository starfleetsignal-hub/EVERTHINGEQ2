## Prerequisite

You need to complete [[Grassgalor (Quest)|Grassgalor]] in [[Timorous Deep]] in order for [[Gamila Haziiya]] to offer you this quest.

## Steps

1. I must capture [[Rawritor]]. {{waypoint -94, 373, 959}}
   - You can only use [[Chokéball]] on [[Rawritor]] when you have brought it to 30% life.
   - You must be CLOSE to [[Rawritor]] or using the [[Chokéball]] will fail.
   - You receive [[Chokéball: Rawritor]] after using the [[Chokéball]] on [[Rawritor]].
1. I need to speak with [[Gamila Haziiya]] now that I've captured [[Rawritor]].

## Rewards

- At least 35g 70s 61c
- Congratulations! You have collected all Chokémon on Norrath, for now.
