## Steps

1. Use a world bell to go to [[Tranquil Sea]]
1. Talk to [[A distraught Far Seas survivor]] just off the dock to the left {{waypoint 932, 7, 1480}}
1. Go to [[Malvonicus' Folly]] {{waypoint 907, 7, 1388}}
1. Go to [[Sapswill's Rest]] {{waypoint 1215, 17, 1267}}
1. Go to [[The D.I.R.T.Y. Dig]] {{waypoint 1082, 8, 1095}} (easiest to fly northeast over ridge to it)
1. Go back to the dock and talk to the distraught Far Seas survivor

## Rewards

- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]]
- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
