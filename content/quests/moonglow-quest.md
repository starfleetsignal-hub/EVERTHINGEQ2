## Steps

1. Go to [[Chelsith]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]]
1. Kill octopi until a [[Semi sentient water]] drops (body drop)![Examine of the ssw](images/SSWex.jpg)
   - Use the [[Semi sentient water]] item from your inventory to summon a pet water glob. ![View of Pet](images/SSWpet.jpg)Warning: the pet would not pop for me if you are to near the entrance. Walk slightly down the first slope.
   - The pet wont do anything but follow you around for a while, but eventually it will stop following you and stay in one spot.
     - The pet can and will most likely die if you try to do other things while it follows you. The safest way to do this is to simply sit at the entrance for 10 min till it will let you talk to it.)
   - When this happens, the pet becomes clickable, and says "[Look what the lil guy found]."
1. Click the pet to receive Moonglow.

## Notes

- [[Semi sentient water]] is **no-zone** and must be used to summon the water glob before leaving the zone.
