## Steps

1. [[Royal Librarian Brinn]] sends you to warn the Palace.  Head to North Qeynos and find [[Murrar Shar]] on the steps to the entrance and speak with him about what you know.
1. Murrar sends you back to Brinn, who will give you the book "Shadows in the Leaves" and ask you to speak to Saphronia in Kelethin.
1. Once you arrive in Kelethin, seek out [[Princess Saphronia]] {{waypoint 391, 145, 157}} who is unhappy with the message you deliver. *Note: Evil-aligned characters take careful heed of the Level 85 Epic x4 patrolling fae guard outside the acorn structure.*
1. Return to Librarian Brinn in Qeynos Harbor, but he isn't to be found in Fish's Alehouse anymore... Discover Brinn facedown in the alley behind the inn {{waypoint 795, -23, 51}}
