## Steps

1. Kill [[A fear feeder|fear feeder]]s for a rare update. {{waypoint 395, 29, -828}}
1. Harvest 20 [[Gemstone Nodes#T4|rhythmic stones]] in the Enchanted Lands (auto updates per every successful mining)
1. Kill goblins until you find one with some blackened metal (auto update - all Runnyeye Goblins work)
