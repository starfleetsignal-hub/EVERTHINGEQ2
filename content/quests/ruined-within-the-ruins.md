## Steps

1. Collect 12 artifacts from within the ruins inhabited by the [[A Thullosian ogre|Thullosian ogres]]{{waypoint 382.95, -73.19, -206.62}} for [[Lucius Vandermarr|Lucius]]. Be prepared to fight groups of 3-6 lvl 79. Artifacts are small, broken gray urns.
1. Return to [[Lucius Vandermarr]] in [[Firmroot Moot]].

### Strategy

1. Take balloon ride to Boarfiend Cavern.  Hop on your mount, if you have one.  Resume your merc, if you have one.
1. If you do not have a flying mount, walk to the edge of the rock at {{waypoint 657, 343, 241}} and jump down (NE) to stepped wall.
1. Walk on wall near {{waypoint 562, 156, 182}}, jump down (SE) to ground and retrieve No 1.
   - {{waypoint 510, 105, 181}} - this is close to spawn point for [[General Grummut Stomp]]
1. Go through the walkway at {{waypoint 510, 105, 181}} and retrieve No 2.
   - {{waypoint 504, 105, 258}}
1. Go (SE) to ramp {{waypoint 493, 105, 293}}, walk (or jump) to bottom of ramp and walk (NW) to retrieve No 3.
   - {{waypoint 449, 44, 228}}
1. Continue (NW), head down hill (NE) and retrieve No 4.
   - {{waypoint 453, 28, 140}}
1. Continue down hill and retrieve No 5.
   - {{waypoint 417, 20, 124}} or {{waypoint 421.83, 19.62, 147.27}} - artifact may spawn at both locations.
1. Walk (NW) and thru walkway to canal at {{waypoint 425, 20, 87}}.  Head (SW) and retrieve No 6.
   - {{waypoint 528, 16, 90}} - this is close to spawn point for [[The Incredible Lumberer]]
1. Go across canal and retrieve No 7.
   - {{waypoint 569, 16, 59}}
1. Follow canal (NE) to walkthru at {{waypoint 458, 16, 28}}.  Go thru walkway (N) wand retrieve No 8.
   - {{waypoint 499, 16, -58}}
1. Go (N) and retrieve No 9
   - {{waypoint 545, 16, -159}}
1. and No 10.
   - {{waypoint 569, 18, -141}}
1. Go back (S) to pond, around wall on right, up ramp and retrieve No 11.
   - {{waypoint 574, 20, -94}}
1. Continue up ramp (jump gap if not on a flying mount) and retrieve No 12.
   - {{waypoint 740, 111, -18}}

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +1200 faction with **Survival Accord**
