## Steps

1. Go to [[Barkrich Oakleaf]] at {{waypoint -747, 14, 1902}} in the [[Fens of Nathsar]], [[Barkrich Oakleaf|he]] will ask you to prove yourself.
   - Complete the Sub-Quest "[[For the Swamp]]".
1. Kill 4 Epic Named (in order). The last two are killed in the same fight in Veeshan's Peak:
   1. Kill [[Tairiza the Widow Mistress]] in the [[The Tomb of Thuuga]] for the [[Rune of Stamina!]].
   1. Kill [[Venril Sathir]] in [[Venril Sathir's Lair]] for the [[Rune of Focus!]]
   1. Kill [[Shade of Khalan Dar]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]] for the [[Rune of Accuracy!]] (both runes are received when examine Rune from kill of last dragon in encounter)
   1. Kill [[Phara Dar]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]] for the [[Rune of Control!]] (both runes are received when examine Rune from kill of last dragon in encounter)
1. You need to "use a blade to remove the runes from the scale". Examine the rune in your inventory.
   - Make sure you have [[A Bottle of Entwrite]], the reward from the quest [[For the Swamp]] in Fens of Nathsar.
1. Return with the original [[Eagle's Talon (Fabled)]] to [[Ryvenar Sal'Vara (Npc)|Ryvenar]].
   1. He will give you a "Powered Cloth". Use it to copy the runes onto the cloth. You receive "Rune of the Marksman".
   1. Click on [[Ryvenar Sal'Vara (Npc)|Ryvenar]] a few times as he goes through the next steps and finally get the bow!
