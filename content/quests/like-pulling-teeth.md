## Prerequisites

- The quest is only offered once your reputation with the Sootfoot Talent Society is at or below -5000, which will only be reached once you've completed all other quests with the Sootfoot Goblins.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- This is the only repeatable quest with the Sootfoot Talent Society that offers continual faction progress.

## Steps

1. Obtain 8 ichor laden teeth by killing [[A vulcanu slayer|vulcanu slayer]]s, [[A vulcanu verdant|vulcanu verdant]]s, [[A vulcanu gaper|vulcanu gaper]]s or [[A vulcanu executioner|vulcanu executioner]]s. These are all found in [[Hydra Pass]]. {{waypoint 60, -15, -40}}
1. Return to [[Magnurmal]].

## Rewards

- At least 20g 19s 93c
- [[Magma Sample (House Item)|Magma Sample]] (first turn-in only)
- -2500 faction with **Sootfoot Talent Society**
