## Steps

1. Visit the [[Orc Encampment]]. {{waypoint 496, -40, 208}}
1. Return to [[Ismena Cellus]].
1. Slay 10 [[A Tallon grunt|tallon grunts]] in the [[Orc Encampment]].
1. Return to Ismena Cellus.
1. Visit the [[Grove of Stones]], between the [[Splintered Plain]] and [[Spirit Valley]]. {{waypoint 210, 14, 120}}
1. Return to Ismena Cellus.

## Rewards

- At least 15s 26c
- [[Cellus Family Bookcase]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Blessed Earstud of Zek]] (All Priests)
  - [[Blessed Stud of the Hounds]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Zekian Earhoop of Disdain]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Zekian Hoop of Stature]] (All Mages)
