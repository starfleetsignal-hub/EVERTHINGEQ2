## Steps

1. Kill drolvarg around [[Challenge Rock (POI)|Challenge Rock]] for 30 challenge tokens. Multiple tokens can drop from the same mob. Once you have 30, click on the totem at {{waypoint -1786, 104, -653}}.
1. Kill [[Snarl Fangtooth]].
1. Return to [[Hilbraega Stoutmun]] in [[Kunzar's Edge (POI)|Kunzar's Edge]]. {{waypoint -1376, 10, 543}}

## Rewards

- At least 6g 88s 19c
- +1000 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- [[Tenacious Cap]]
