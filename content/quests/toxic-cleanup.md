## Description

*Highly acidic liquid has coalesced into pools within the Stygian Threshold, threatening to pollute Thalumbra. I'm to enter this part of the cavern and clean up this toxic mess, however I see fit.*

## Zone

- Defeat targets within [[Stygian Threshold: The Toxic Pools (Event Heroic)]]

## Steps

- Clean up the toxic puddle

- Clear out the toxic coagulation
  - [[Blobulous]]
- Destroy the cause of the toxic mess
  - [[Acinox]]
