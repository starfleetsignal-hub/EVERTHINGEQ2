## Prerequisites

- You need to be at least a level 5 adventurer *or* tradeskiller to receive this quest.
- You may receive an entire level of experience for each tradeskilling quest, but it may appear that you remain on the same level due to a cap on level gains per quest. On quest completion check your tradeskill experience. If your experience is at 100%, try crafting any item that will give you experience to bump you to the next level, and then do the next quest.
- To get to the Village of Shin, use any Bell and travel to the Isle of Mara.

## Important Note

- To save time, if you are running this quest as a level 20+ tradeskiller you can purchase a [[Return to Mara]] from [[Koros Splinterlimb]] in the tavern opposite the broker to instantly transport back to the Isle of Mara.

## Steps

1. Go harvest (buying does not work) in any tier 1 zone, 5 of each.
   - [[Severed elm]]
   - [[Jumjum]]
   - [[Black coffee bean]]
   - [[Rawhide leather pelt]]
   - [[Root (harvest)|root]]
   - [[Baubbleshire cabbage]]
   - [[Raw white tea leaf]]
   - [[Frog leg]]
   - [[Sunfish]]
     - *Note: Tier 1 zones are: Darklight Wood,Greater Faydark (Nursery, central area), The Forest Ruins, The Caves, Oakmyst Forest, Ruins, The Peat Bog, The Graveyard, Sunken City, The Sprawl, Timorous Deep (Chrykori isle and Gorowyn Isle) and Frostfang Sea. Murdunk oranges and carrots can be harvested in The Commonlands, where you can also fish for sunfish and frog legs in the river.
     - *Note: the Sprawl and the Graveyard have no fishable spots)*
     - *Note:* As of 3/21/2024 I can confirm that Frostfang Sea does in fact drop every single item on the list for this quest.
     - *Note:* As of 3/26/2025, can be complete in The Ruins (Freeport); pending your Luck, may have to clear every resource node to leverage random node spawn cycle fresh.
1. Return to Qho Augren.

## Rewards

- At least 24g 40s 27c
- Tradeskill experience
