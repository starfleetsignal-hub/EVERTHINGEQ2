*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Disrupt the orcs' plans:
   - Disrupt the northern Bloodskull offensive by killing [[A Bloodskull orc|a Bloodskull orc]] in the camp south-southeast of the Kerran and Ratonga villages {{waypoint -230, -48, 250}}.
   - Disrupt the southern Bloodskull offensive by killing [[A Bloodskull orc|a Bloodskull orc]] in the camp further south-southeast of the first camp. {{waypoint -273, -48, 341}}
   - Destroy 5 crates of supplies in either camp. Search Crate for explosives (right click). Receive explosives with 4 charges. Repeat if used up.
   - Examine the siege tower being constructed and destroy it with the last of the explosives you found. {{waypoint -273, -47, 344}}
1. Return to [[Rainus Canton]].
