## Steps

1. Help protect the New Combine Foothold by destroying the encroaching corrupted creatures and retrieving lost supplies
   - Kill 6 corrupted Drakkel Wolves
   - Kill 6 corrupted briarthorn plants
   - Retrieve the missing supplies.  These are crates with silver sparkles.  They can be found around the roots of the purple veined trees in Riven Plateau
1. Return to Field Marshall Vishra

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Withered Chainlink Boots]] (Shaman)
  - [[Haze Panther Skin Foot Wraps]]
  - [[Withering Treant Sabatons]]
  - [[Corrupting Cloth Shoes]] (Mage)
  - [[Mist Panther Skin Boots]]
  - [[Withering Combine Armor Sabatons]]
  - [[Corrupted Soul Chainlink Boots]]
