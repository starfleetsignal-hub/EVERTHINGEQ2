## Steps

1. Slay [[A dryland scavenger|dryland scavengers]] (level 63) until you get a quest update. They are found just around the corner from [[Ahnjun Sprywing]]. A 5-minute timer starts once you get the quest update.
   - [[A young dryland scavenger|Young dryland scavengers]] and [[A vicious dryland scavenger|vicious dryland scavengers]] also update.
1. Climb the nearest chain links to where the two NPCs named [[Orly]] and [[Yarly]] stand {{waypoint 382, 316, -656}}. Inspect the carcass that you received in the previous step.
1. You will be attacked by [[Soctoro]] (level 64). You must slay him before the 5-minute timer expires.
1. Return to Ahnjun for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Confessor's Chipped Dragon Bone Armlet]]
  - [[Hunter's Chipped Dragon Bone Armlet]]
  - [[Scholar's Chipped Dragon Bone Armlet]]
  - [[Soldier's Chipped Dragon Bone Armlet]]
- At Least 9g 85s 50c
- +75 faction with **The Windgazer**
