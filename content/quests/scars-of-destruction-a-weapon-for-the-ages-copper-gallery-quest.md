## Notes

- If you complete this quest once the next quest is unlocked
- This quest is repeatable., to get more [[Alpus' Copper Procurement Token]]

## Steps

1. Obtain the following
   - Velium-Alloyed Hilt
     - Dropped from final bosses in Heroic I instances
   - Velium Alloyed Steel Bar
     - Dropped from final bosses in Solo instances
   - 20 Vials of Condensed Deific Magic
     - Looted from killing Solo and Heroic bosses in instances

## Rewards

- [[Alpus' Copper Procurement Token]]
