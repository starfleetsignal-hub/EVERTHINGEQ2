## Steps

*Guard Mathews was given a crudely drawn map from a traveler that passed through the Darkpaw Dugout. He doesn't recognize the map's markings or locations and is unsure if the map is a potential threat to the camp. He asked me to investigate who the map belongs to and recommended that I speak with Ranger Alphin first.*

1. Speak with [[Ranger Alphin]] {{waypoint -11, -77, -249}} about the map
1. Speak with [[Nisch Mal Polphar]] {{waypoint 2, -75, -280}} about the map
1. Speak with [[Nisch Mas Nolphia]] {{waypoint 0, -77, -272}} about the map
