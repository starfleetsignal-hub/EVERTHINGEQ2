## Description

After allowing [[Frup Groaak]] to meditate on the death of [[Giidib]], Frup asks you to find the assassin responsible. He gives you a [[Shard of Marr]] to summon forth the [[Spirit of Giidib]].

## Notes

- As of an unknown update, it is no longer necessary to use the [[Shard of Marr]] and follow the [[Spirit of Giidib]] to [[The Bells of Vhalen]]. You may fly or ride there and the required named mob should spawn immediately. If it doesn't then follow the steps accordingly.

## Steps

1. Go to [[Antonica]] and travel to the Tower of Vhalen. {{waypoint -1300, 0, 45}}
1. Before you near the tower, activate the [[Shard of Marr]] to spawn the [[Spirit of Giidib]]. Once the Spirit of Giidib spawns, right click him, and select "Take me to the assassin."
1. Approach the Tower of Vhalen to spawn [[Viragos, the Assassin of the Hand]], then kill him.
1. Return to [[Frup Groaak]] for your reward.
