## Steps

1. Harvest the following:
   - 18 [[Reef cluster|reef clusters]]
   - 15 [[Strangler root|strangler roots]]
   - 15 [[Dewpalm lumber]]
   - 10 [[Basilisk meat]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Clockwork Grease 9940b]].
1. Craft the [[Clockwork Grease 9940b]] on a [[Work Bench]].
   - You will need 5 [[Scarred Coal]] for fuel, harvested in The Anchorage
1. Speak to [[Jurie Gearshaper]] at {{waypoint 7.70, -14.04, -22.59}}.
