## Description

"I found Scout Spelunkle within Drippy Cave. He told me that the grobins are keeping a shrumbler breeder queen further within the cave and using it to produce eggs. He suggested that I destroy the queen to stop the production of the eggs." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Do the following...
   - Kill 5 [[A grobin chipper|grobin chipper]]s
   - Kill [[The Shrumbler Breeder Queen]] {{waypoint 69.81, -48.08, -420.16}}
1. Speak to [[Captain Steelforge]]

- Scout Spelunkle: "I believe I've found the source of the shrumblers that have popped up in the Nursery. Strange, horrible sounds echo from further down the cave."
- Player Character: "Then I'll head down there and deal with her. Go ahead and report back to Captain Steelforge, and I'll see what I can do."
- Scout Spelunkle: "Watch for the tunnel guards, since they do not take kindly to outsiders. You might also want to dispatch some of the chippers. They are gathering ore for some reason, and I'm sure it isn't good for the Nursery, so clearing them out should slow down their plans significantly."
- Player Character: "I will. Farewell, and I will meet you back at Steelforge's camp."
