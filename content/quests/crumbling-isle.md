## Steps

![](images/Survivors.png)

1. Rescue 6 Far Seas crewmembers.  Just talk to them to update.  They can be found at the following locations.
   - {{waypoint 978, 10, 1357}}
   - {{waypoint 866, 20, 1305}}
   - {{waypoint 823, 22, 1382}}
   - {{waypoint 828, 16, 1430}}
   - {{waypoint 884, 10, 1378}}
   - {{waypoint 832, 21, 1462}}
1. Enter Malvonicus' Tower and click the wooden trap door at {{waypoint 910, 7, 1386}} to enter [[Clackwhinge's Cellar]], ![Clackwhinge's Cellar](images/Clackwhinge's_Cellar.jpg) speak to Overseer Clackwhinge![Overseer Clackwhinge](images/Overseer_Clackwhinge.jpg)
1. Return to [[Researcher Aillena Belzia (Tranquil Sea)|Aillena]]

- At least 1p 30g
  - 1p 40g 40s @ lvl 96
