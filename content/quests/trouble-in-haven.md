## Steps

1. Enter [[The Hearthfire]] in [[Shadow Haven]] and speak to [[Hapith Gracearms]] {{waypoint -801.14, -1.06, -69.62}}.
1. Speak to [[Cavin]] in [[The Hearthfire]] {{waypoint -824.29, -0.75, -28.78}}.
1. Go to [[The Bazaar Stables]] {{waypoint -60, -2, 176}}.
1. Find Driena's body (can appear in a variety of locations in the stables).
1. Right click and examine Driena's body.
1. Return to [[Thaddaeus Pryce]] in the [[House of Midst]] {{waypoint 776.28, -8.50, -732.81}}

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- [[Large Book Stack]]
