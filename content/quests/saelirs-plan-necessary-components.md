This is the starter quest for [[Kelethin Betrayal Timeline|Kelethin Betrayal]].

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Saelir Varryn]] in Kelethin at waypoint {{waypoint 224, 112, 407}}
1. He asks you to collect the following items:
   - Red-blooded Mushrooms from around waypoint {{waypoint 91.68, -4.87, 60.28}} near the Old Kelethin Acorn lift.
   - Yellow-blooded Mushrooms from around waypoint {{waypoint 85, -5, 54}} near the Old Kelethin Acorn lift.
   - Buy purple berries from Brizeyl Myrika in Joleena's Restaurant at waypoint {{waypoint 536, 71, 322}} for 5cp.
   - Reedmace Seeds from reeds located at waypoint {{waypoint 582, 46, 29}}.
1. Return to [[Saelir Varryn]] in Kelethin to complete the quest.

## Reward

- Experience and AA experience only
