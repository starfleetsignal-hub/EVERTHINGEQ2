## Steps

1. Gather 5 [[Far Windstomper food crate|Far Windstomper food crates]] in the lower level area of Zek. You can get the crate from any spawn location more than once if you wait for a re-spawn. See the talk page for a list of confirmed, *random* locations.
1. Return to Mess Sergeant Akseltz

## Rewards

- One of the following Neck-slot items, based on class:
  - [[Defender's Medallion of Orc Slaying]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Medium's Medallion of Orc Slaying]] (All Mages)
  - [[Healer's Medallion of Orc Slaying]] (All Priests)
  - [[Stalker's Medallion of Orc Slaying]] (All Scouts)
