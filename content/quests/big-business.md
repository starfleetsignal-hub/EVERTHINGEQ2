## Notes

- This quest is no longer necessary to use the city teleporters.
- None of the former business owners will attack you, although some are less thrilled than others to sign over their businesses for the greater glory of Neriak.

## Steps

1. Collect signatures.
   - [[Jhultyrr D'Vethiurden]]: [[Seloxia Stronghold]] {{waypoint -58, 15, 396}}
   - [[Tarven J'Oussana]]: [[Neriak Down Under]] {{waypoint -157, 3, 240}}
   - [[Quilafein H'Tyhaduis]]: outside [[Spires of Innoruuk]] {{waypoint -279, 26, 219}}
   - [[Kelryne V'Ziath]]: [[Hall of the Ebon Mask]] {{waypoint -481, 66, 100}}
     - Talk to him and at end of conversation he will give [[Kelryne's Recipe for Chocolate Chip Cookies of Seething Malice]].
   - [[Zezaghar D'Arkenth]]: [[Library of K'Lorn]] {{waypoint -693, 17, 245}}
   - [[Chalthara V'Zeraep]]:[[Death Grotto]] {{waypoint -599, 27, -47}}
1. Return to Divton T'Szelyl.

## Rewards

- At least 3s 62c
- Ability to use the Neriak teleporters
