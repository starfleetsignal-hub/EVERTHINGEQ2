## Steps

![](images/Highland_sandstone_deposit.png)

1. [[Felidael]] has asked that you seek out [[Chordatan]] for information about tombstones to erect in honor of the fallen Kejek whom you have put to rest.
   - Speak to [[Chordatan]] at {{waypoint -885, 483, 364}}
1. Chordatan needs 10 slabs of sandstone to complete a new batch of tombstones. Collect them from  around the [[Abandoned Kejek Village]]. Locations include:
   - {{waypoint -1063, 531, 606}}
   - {{waypoint -942, 520, 563}}
   - {{waypoint -925, 518, 559}}
   - {{waypoint -910, 518, 527}}
   - {{waypoint -965, 519, 529}}
   - {{waypoint -966, 517, 510}}
   - {{waypoint -977, 523, 570}}
   - {{waypoint -1037, 527, 630}}
   - {{waypoint -1027, 526, 629}}
     - *Note: It's a group update each time you get one of em*
1. Return to Chordatan with the sandstone
1. Take the finished tombstones to [[Felidael]].

## Rewards

- At least 45g 70s 61c
