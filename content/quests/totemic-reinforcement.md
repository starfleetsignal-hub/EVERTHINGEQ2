## Steps

1. I need to craft several ritual totems.
   - The recipe, [[Totemic Mastercrafting and You]] is automatically added to your recipe book.
   - Using an Engraved Desk, craft 4 [[A satyr ritual totem|satyr ritual totems]], using the following materials provided by Phaerus:
     - [[A slab of granite]]
     - [[Blessed water (Totemic Reinforcement)|blessed water]]
     - Bring a portable desk if at all possible.
1. I should take these [[A satyr ritual totem|totems]] back to Phaerus.  ![Totem Placement](images/Totem_Placement.jpg)
   - I need to place the first totem around the ritual area. {{waypoint -6, -632, -719}}
   - I need to place the second totem around the ritual area. {{waypoint 2, -632, -728}}
   - I need to place the third totem around the ritual area. {{waypoint -7, -632, -738}}
   - I need to place the fourth totem around the ritual area. {{waypoint -16, -632, -727}}
1. I shoud return to Phaerus once more.
