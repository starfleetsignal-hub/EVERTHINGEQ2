## Steps

1. Defeat [[Mistress Zhvari, Nizari'zhi]] in [[Nizara, City of the Nayad]]

You must kill her and BOTH of her guards (Nizari'ochu preator(s)) to get the quest update.

1. Return to the Chronomagi Tasker

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Chronomancers**
- Ability to purchase [[Replica: Aeri'ssth, the Magic Weaver]] for 1p 8g from [[Timeless Trader]]
- Ability to purchase [[Replica: Mistress Zhvari, Nizari'zhi]] for 1p 8g from [[Timeless Trader]]
