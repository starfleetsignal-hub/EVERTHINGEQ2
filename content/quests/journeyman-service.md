## Prerequisites

- L20+ Tradeskill Profession
- Some T3+ (skill 90+) harvesting skills

## Starting the Quest

Quest starters are Trades Coordinators:

- Gorowyn  - [[Grandmaster Jayak Lat'wasr]] in [[Gorowyn]], [[The Wards]] ( 2777, 123, 1183, 310 )
- Freeport - [[Grandmaster Kawayzer]] in [[West Freeport]]
- Kelethin - [[Grandmaster Feldmanus]] in [[Tunare's Pages]] in the area with the broker
- Qeynos   - [[Grandmaster Kimbialis]] at the [[The Ironforge Exchange]] in [[North Qeynos]]
- Neriak   - [[Grandmaster Zyrythius]] in the [[Dockside Markets]]
- New Halas - [[Grandmaster Cheri Glodsdottr]] in [[Frostfang Sea]], crafting area at {{waypoint -331, 176, -45}}

## Notes

- You may wish to complete [[A Gathering Obsession, Part III]] simultaneously. If you do, turn in to [[Qho Augren]] before starting this quest.
- It is recommended that persons starting this quest examine the content of the Advanced volumes offered as rewards and their average price and availability on the Broker. In particular, volumes at the high end of the Tier tend to be rarer, more expensive and contain key recipes, such as some imbued chest armor pieces. Likewise certain volumes are disproportionately valuable when obtained early, such as Tailor volumes with the recipes for Mastercrafted Hex Dolls (which can be equipped by characters at the very first level of each Tier), or those with certain Mastercrafted poisons.

## Steps

1. Harvest 50 raw materials from nodes in [[The Thundering Steppes]].
   - You will receive a quest update every time you successfully harvest a node, regardless of the type of node.
1. Return to a Trades Coordinator.
1. Make a level 20 recipe item from a list to pristine / apprentice IV level. Suggested options are listed below.
   - [[Crafted Vase]] ([[Carpenter]])
   - [[Ash Buckler (Crafted)|Ash Buckler]] ([[Woodworker]])
   - [[Defender of the Harvest Foliage]] ([[Tailor]])
   - [[Pristine forged carbonite short sword]] ([[Weaponsmith]])
   - [[Carbonite Kite Shield]] ([[Armorer]])
   - [[Oolong Tea (Crafted)|Oolong Tea]] x6 ([[Provisioner]])
   - [[Bria's Entrancing Sonnet]] ([[Jeweler]])
   - [[Iceshield]] ([[Sage]])
   - [[Intercept II]] ([[Alchemist]])

## Rewards

- For [[Provisioner|Provisioners]]
  - [[Provisioner Mastery volume 1]]
- For all other tradeskill classes you get to choose one advanced recipe book from the 20 to 29 range for your class only
- Tradeskill Experience
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +250 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +250 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +250 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +250 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +250 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +250 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
