## Notes

**To start the sub-quest:** you must speak to a cauldron in the forge room to at {{waypoint -9.55, 0.00, -26.51}}).

- This is a sub-quest, part of the [[Craftsman Errands]] quest (from the [[Artisan Epic Timeline]]).
  - This quest doesn't have a typical "completion" (no turn-in, no ding/reward). Once you build the traps, you return to what you were doing prior, but have traps to use!
- The rats within this special instance of [[Nektropos Castle]] will all be 10-20 level above you (the scale).
  - You must sneak past the first group of rats. You have to get quite close for them to attack (aggro), so it is possible to sneak by without too much worry. To get past them to get this sub-quest, keep close to the walls.
- You should already be in this zone if you have this quest, but if you must return:
  - Travel to Nektropos Castle in [[Nektulos Forest]] and enter the instance [[Nektropos Castle: Craftsman Errands]].
  - If you log out while inside the instance, you will be outside of the instance again.

## Steps

1. There are four items to be found.
   - The recipe for a **rat trap** is on the bookshelf in the forge room.
   - **Scrap metal** can be found in barrels in the forge room {{waypoint -3, 0, -28}}.
   - **Scrap wood** is found in the stables {{waypoint -16, 0, -9}}. Southwest corner.
   - **Something edible looking** is in the kitchen {{waypoint 13, 0, -26}} hanging above the fire in the pot.
   - A single harvest from each of the 3 sources will provide enough to produce 20 rat traps
1. Scribe the recipe and use the forge to build 20 traps. Drag the rat traps (a cage icon) to your hotbar for easy use.
   - Use the rat trap on the rats to maneuver around the castle and collect the items needed for [[Craftsman Errands]]. You can set the traps and pull the rats to them to speed things up, but each trap will only disable 1 rat.

## Rewards

- You are rewarded with [[AA]].
- (and the parts needed for the Errands quest)
