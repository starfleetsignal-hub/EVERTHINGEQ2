## Steps

1. Read the mail from Raffik, 'I gots lost.'
1. Find [[Raffik]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]] at {{waypoint -625, 90, 1218}}. Take the Travel Disc, it'll take you right to him.
1. Speak with [[Ginnie Hebblethwaite]] in [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] at {{waypoint -524, 178, 1063}} to receive a [[Clockwork Salvager Stun]] recipe.
1. Craft a [[Clockwork Salvager Stunner]] from the recipe. There is a workbench in the next building at {{waypoint -518, 175, 1038}}.
   - All the mats can be found within Steamfont Mountains:
     - 1 [[Gemstone Nodes#T4|velium cluster]]
     - 1 [[Gemstone Nodes#T4|rough opaline]]
     - 1 [[Ore Nodes#T4|feyiron cluster]]
     - 1 [[Glimmering Coal]] - *purchased from [[Fizza Cogsworth]] at {{waypoint -522, 175, 1035}}*
1. Use the stunner on [[A clockwork salvager]] east of Raffik at {{waypoint -646, 84, 1224}}. Right click it and remove the compass.
1. Speak with Raffik.

- At least 5p 13g
- Tradeskill experience
