## Note

This questline is only available to Ratonga.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Temple Street: Vault of Necromechanica]]
1. Find 3 gnomish equations on the walls of Temple Street:
   - {{waypoint 66, 3, 63}}
   - {{waypoint 24, 3, 57}}
   - {{waypoint 19, 3, 59}}
   - {{waypoint -2, 3, 117}}
   - {{waypoint -14, 3, 107}}
   - {{waypoint -17, 3, 100}}
   - {{waypoint -29, 3, 84}}
   - {{waypoint -4, 3, 53}}
1. Return to the Academy in North Freeport and click on sparkling books {{waypoint 26, -6, -130}}
1. Return to Temple Street and click on clockwork parts on the ground {{waypoint 30, 3, 17}}; fix the machine
1. Kill 6 [[A necromech|necromechs]] (even con)
1. Speak to Sneel in North Freeport
