## Steps

1. Eliminate the source of the skeletons by defeating [[Tralb the Mystic]]:
   1. Go to the inner chamber of [[Ganak's Old Crypt]]. {{waypoint 607, -103, 935}}
      - As you enter the inner chamber, look for the NPC version of Tralb on the far balcony. If you do not see him, the ring event will not start, and you will have to wait potentially 45 minutes for him to respawn.
   1. Clear the entire inner chamber (lower level and upper balconies) of the [[A risen brittlebone|risen brittlebones]]. As soon as you kill the first one, a green status update will flash on your screen.
   1. When you kill the last brittlebone, another green status update will flash on your screen. Clear the inner chamber (lower level and balconies) of the [[An invoked deathwrap|invoked deathwraps]] that have spawned.
   1. When the final deathwrap has been killed, a final green status update will flash on your screen, "The deathwraps have been defeated". [[Tralb the Mystic]] will become attackable after a few moments. Defeat him for the quest update.
1. Return to Taskmaster Scally.
