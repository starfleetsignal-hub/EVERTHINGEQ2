## Prerequisites

- You must have at least 5,500 faction with The Gwalnax Brigade to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Go to [[Nak'azar Cavern]] in [[Chardok]] and obtain the following items: {{waypoint -483, -66, -91}}
   - 4 [[Grime crawler roe]] from [[A grime crawler|grime crawlers]]
   - 2 [[Shrooldoll|shrooldolls]] from [[A Villisis dweller|Villisis dwellers]]
   - 6 [[Woozy pellets]] from Nak'azar Brigade troops
   - 2 [[Thrasher venom sac|thrasher venom sacs]] from [[A sokokar thrasher|sokokar thrashers]]
1. Return to [[Dragoon Slitek]] at the [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]].

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Gwalnax Brigade**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Emblem of the Bellywumper]]
  - [[Ring of the Bellywumper]]
  - [[Ringlet of the Bellywumper]]
  - [[Signet of the Bellywumper]]
