This quest can be completed once per day.

## Steps

1. Gather 10 fallen tree sap in [[Hawkeye Trails]], the valley east of Scarstone {{waypoint 3637, -867, 373}}
   - The sap looks like sparkling piles of dirt. They cannot be tabbed to or tracked, and frequently appear under trees and next to stumps and bushes
1. Examine the barrel in Scarstone again

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
