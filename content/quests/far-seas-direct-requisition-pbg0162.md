## Description

I have discovered a direct delivery Far Seas Requisition slip. The hunter charged with filling the order must have been slain. If I hunt down the creatures and return the resources I shall earn the payment for labor. This particular order has Peat Bog slugs listed for Tanaria. I'm sure she has some strange use for these slugs, I wonder what it could be. Should I leave the Peat Bog before completing this requisition, I may return via any number of mariner bells within the City of Qeynos.

## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A bog slug|bog slugs]]. {{waypoint 679, -35, 419}}
1. Speak to [[Tanaira]]. {{waypoint 759, -26, 365}}
