## Description

Something is amiss aboard the Vigilant! I should find my way aboard and explore the entire ship.

## Steps

1. I should find my way to the Navigation Bridge and defeat [[Vice Vigoth Sanad|the Tallonite leader]].
1. I should find my way to the Officer's Dojo and defeat [[Vigoth Rodnan|the Tallonite leader]].
1. I should find my way to the Captain's Deck and defeat [[Marus Xand|the Tallonite leader]].

## Reward

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x14
- At least 1p 80g
