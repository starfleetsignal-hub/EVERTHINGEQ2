## Notes

- To gain access to [[Trakanon's Lair]] you must complete the quest, [[Taking on Trakanon]], which starts in [[Veeshan's Peak]].
- You must have the [[The Chelsith Stone (Quest Item)|The Chelsith Stone]], taken from the [[Into the Bowels of the Beast|Leviathan's stomach]], in your inventory to progress in this quest.

## Steps

1. Complete the following:
   - Obtain the [[The Lost Medallion|Lost Medallion]] by defeating [[Trakanon]] within [[Trakanon's Lair]].
   - Examine the [[The Chelsith Stone (Quest Item)|Chelsith Stone]] from within your inventory and insert the Lost Medallion into it.
     - If someone in your raid performs this step, the whole raid will get the update.

1. Return to [[The Moppet Shoppe]] and speak with [[A stranger]].
   - For the text of his dialogue with the player, click [[The Secret of the Stone/Conversation|here]].
