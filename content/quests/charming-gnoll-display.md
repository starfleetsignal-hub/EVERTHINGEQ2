## Steps

*I found a handcrafted charm in one of the gnoll camps. It doesn't seem to be worth much but given its placement within the gnoll camp there may be meaning to them. I should collect more and bring them to the gnoll refugees back at the Darkpaw Dugout.*

1. Collect 15 gnoll charms in Darkpaw Warrens (the first charm will count, so 14 more are needed.)
   - Small bone statues in the Sabertooth Burrow area.  These are a solo update.
   - Gnoll Charms may respawn in a locked Darkpaw Warrens instance
1. Deliver the charms to Nisch Mas Nolphia in Darkpaw Warrens {{waypoint -26, -44, -240}}
