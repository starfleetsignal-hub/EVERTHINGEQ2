## Steps

1. Gather 3 [[Fabric Bundle|Fabric Bundles]] from outside the abandoned Kappa hut {{waypoint 88.38, 5.17, 312.11}}.
   - NOTE: The possessed harvester W.H.A.T. v2 will **not** pick the bundles up.
1. Harvest 25 [[Fox Pelt|Fox Pelts]] from animal dens.
1. Speak with [[Zolea]] at {{waypoint -59.17, 9.33, 374.12}}

## Rewards

- +2,000 faction with **Port Woe**
- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
