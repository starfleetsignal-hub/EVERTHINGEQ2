## Steps

1. Slay the following mobs:
   - 20 [[A shade prowler|shade prowlers]], found in [[The Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -1176, -216, -136}}
   - 20 [[A sunstrider gazelle|sunstrider gazelles]], found in [[The Pillars of Flame]] at {{waypoint 780, -107, -527}}
   - [[A Blades tower captain]]
   - [[A Truth tower captain]]
1. Return to [[Neriph, Caliph of the Coin]].
1. Complete the following tasks, in no particular order:
   - Speak to [[Ahzeem the Bartermaster]] in [[Maj'Dul]] at {{waypoint 67, 148, -143}}.
   - Speak to [[Gaazahl the Merchant]] in Maj'Dul at {{waypoint -235, 160, -106}}.
     - Defeat the thugs and speak to Gaazahl the Merchant again.
   - Speak to [[Honest Ali]] in [[The Sinking Sands]].
   - Kill crocodiles/caimen in the Sinking Sands for 20 uncommon updates.
1. Return to Neriph, Caliph of the Coin.
1. Hire a new preceptor and 'fire' the old one:
   - Speak to [[Preceptor Jazeel (Maj'Dul)|Preceptor Jazeel]] in Maj'Dul at {{waypoint 30, 137, 217}}.
   - Kill [[Preceptor Kamal]], who spawns at{{waypoint 28.39, 137.09, 187.55}}. He spawns with one add in front of the door to the [[Champions' Court]] shortly after the cheering fans spawn then travels up the stairs. Spawn time is around 40 minutes. Has been seen spawning at 4:15 a.m. and 9:25 p.m. EQ2 time. If you have trouble spawning him see the Discussion Page for more information.
     - If you are the only player in Maj'Dul, if you zone out of the city and then back in, the zone will repopulate - spawning both the cheering fans and Preceptor Jazeel as you approach his spawn location.
1. Return to Neriph, Caliph of the Coin.
