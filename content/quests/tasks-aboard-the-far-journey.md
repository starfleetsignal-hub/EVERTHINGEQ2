## Description

"After recovering from being rescued from the seas, you find yourself aboard the Far Journey and have been given tasks to complete in return for your rescue." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Notes

- This quest is only available if you chose [[Queen's Colony]] (Good-aligned) or [[Outpost of the Overlord]] (Evil-aligned) as your starting city.
- After completing the quest, talk to [[Captain Varlos (The Far Journey)|Captain Varlos]] to leave [[The Far Journey]] and travel to Queen's Colony or Outpost of the Overlord.
- Click [here](https://youtu.be/JSyBp_xk43k) for a video walkthrough of this quest.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Waulon (The Far Journey)|Waulon]] at {{waypoint -3, 1, -20}} - he asks you to retrieve his hat.
1. Open the three treasure chests near {{waypoint -3, -2, 10}} on the boat by clicking them.  Two of them are empty, one has a [[Small bag (Version 2)|small bag]], a [[Threadbare tunic]], and [[Waulon's Hat]].  Loot the items.
1. Talk to Waulon and exchange the hat for [[Mariner's Charm]].
1. Speak to [[Ingrid (The Far Journey)|Ingrid]] at {{waypoint 2, -2, -4}} - she asks you to buy a [[Shard of Luclin]] from [[Vim (The Far Journey)|Vim]] at {{waypoint -2, 1, 14}}.
1. Sell your [[Mariner's Charm]] to [[Vim (The Far Journey)|Vim]], and buy the [[Shard of Luclin]].
1. Return to [[Ingrid (The Far Journey)|Ingrid]].
1. Speak to [[Captain Varlos (The Far Journey)|Captain Varlos]] again - he asks you to kill the rats on board the ship and gives you a [[Splintered club]].
1. Kill two [[A rat (Far Journey)|rats]].
1. Return to Captain Varlos - a drakota will attack the boat and free [[A goblin (The Far Journey)|a goblin]] that was locked in a wooden cage.
1. Wait for Captain Varlos to ask you to kill the [[A goblin (Far Journey)|goblin]].
1. Kill the [[A goblin (Far Journey)|goblin]].

## Rewards

- [[Mariner's Charm]]
