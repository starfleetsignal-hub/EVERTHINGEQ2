## Description

Aside from your vanilla pirate and lookout, Stromur's supposed to be out hunting other Brokenskull stragglers, but he's got a stomach that's lacking in ale, so I'm going to complete what the Far Seas Trading Company hired him to do.

## Steps

Hunt down Brokenskull pirate slurs, drunkards, swabbies, wenches, bouncers, fishers and riggers

- Kill 30 of any of the following:
  - [[A Brokenskull bouncer]]
  - [[A Brokenskull drunkard]]
  - [[A Brokenskull pirate fisher]]
  - [[A Brokenskull pirate rigger]]
  - [[A Brokenskull pirate slur]]
  - [[A Brokenskull pirate wench]]
  - [[A Brokenskull swabby]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 16g 20,000 status
- 15 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
