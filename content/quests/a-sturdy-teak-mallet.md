## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Teak Mallet for my apprentice.
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Teak Mallet]]
   1. Craft [[A Sturdy Teak Mallet]] on a [[Woodworking Table]] using the following materials and fuel:
      - 2 [[Severed teak]]
      - 4 [[Luminous sandpaper]]
1. I need to take this mallet back to my apprentice.
