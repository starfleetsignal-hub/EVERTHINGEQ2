## Steps

1. Hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 8 [[A necrocraft sentinel|necrocraft sentinels]] around {{waypoint -31, 32, 268}} for **thrice-lived tissue**. ![thrice-lived tissue](images/THRICELIVEDTISSUE.png)
     - *This is an uncommon ground drop; look under the corpse for a sparkling hunk of meat.*
   - Gather 8 **goldandula** (gold leafed bush) around [[Nightmare Rise]] {{waypoint -319, 18, -217}}.
     - OR
   - ![Discordant suffused rubble](images/Discordant_suffused_rubble_X.jpg)Gather 12 discordant suffused rubble in Mahngavi Wastes  {{waypoint -301.27, 0, -681.99}}.

1. Return to [[Corpseolynne]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- 3 [[Goldandula]]
- Choice of:
  - [[Forlorn Dirk of Foresight]]
  - [[Forlorn Greatspear of Foresight]]
