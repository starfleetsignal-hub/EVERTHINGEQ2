## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 3 [[Golden Fleck]] (Rare)
- 12 [[Silvered fleck]]
- 24 [[Nimbic hide]]
- 25 [[Strangler root]]
- 50 [[Celestial Coal]]

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Recipes for Adventure]] at {{waypoint 454, -202, -1432}}.
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Offering to Xegony]].
   - There is a [[Work Bench]] near by at {{waypoint 479, -202, -1441}} to craft the [[Offering to Xegony]].
1. Travel to what looks like a teleport pad next to the portal at {{waypoint 122, -181, -819}} on the same island.
   - The location looks like a guild hall teleport pad.
1. Click the object that looks like a teleport pad to place the offering.
1. Speak with [[Recipes for Adventure]] at {{waypoint 454, -202, -1432}}.

## Rewards

- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
