## Prerequisites

- Neriak citizenship.

## Steps

1. Get items for potion
   - Get 5 scales from [[Gloom snake|gloom snakes]] in [[Fallen Gate]]
     - Soloable versions of these snakes (19^) are found in the entry corridor of the dungeon.
   - Harvest 4 [[Bloodwine root]] throughout [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
   - Harvest 4 [[Rancor quartz]] throughout [[Neriak, City of Hate]]
1. Return to Ssapnolu
1. Present a gift to the Queen
   - Go to Queen Cristanos' altar at the ground floor in [[Darklight Palace]] {{waypoint -578, 30, -180}}
     - You will get an update when you are next to the correct place
   - Place the gift upon the altar by examining it in your inventory. You will receive another update.
1. Return to Ssapnolu

## Rewards

- 3 [[Virile Frenzy]]
