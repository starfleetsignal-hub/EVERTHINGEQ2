## Steps

1. Leave Zalyn to the south and a Tizmak Hero (level 90^) will attack you.  Once he reaches 50% health, he changes into Toridar Mudhoof, then you can talk to him.
1. At 50% health you can speak to Toridar. Be careful of your dialogue choices. The correct responses are:
   - "I was tricked! Betrayed by a fellow adventurer that used my compassion against me!"
   - "It made sense at the time. Is there anyway I can prove to you that I mean no harm to the Tizmak?"
   - "Yes, I'll go free them at once!"
- *Note: 1 one Tizmak Hero spawns for each group member*

## Rewards

- At least 70g
