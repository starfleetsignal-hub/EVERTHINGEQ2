## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Tarew Marr has informed me that summoning an Avatar requires the land to be attuned to their element. To achieve this, altars have been set up around the region, but they need to be supplied with power. I have been asked to assist with this by collecting objects infused with the essence of slain foes and offer them to an altar.

1. Collect 3 infused [[Water Orb|water orbs]]
   - These are random drop from trash mobs in [[Aether Wroughtlands]] that will show quest indicator by their name. I received all 3 Orbs from a Dune Prowler in groups of three. They only had feathers by their names. Around {{waypoint -279, 87, 256}}
1. Use the infused [[Water Orb|water orbs]] and empower an altar.
   - *The shrine spawns in random spot in that zone. Possible locations include:*
   - {{waypoint -334.79, -5.75, -94.72}} ([[The Camp of Forgotten Souls]])
   - {{waypoint 14.25, -4.41, -91.92}}
   - {{waypoint 27, -7, 19}}
   - {{waypoint 98, -46, 189}}
   - {{waypoint 91, -40, 315}}
   - {{waypoint -290, -3, -130}} ([[The Camp of Forgotten Souls]])
1. Return to [[Herald of the Fathom Lord]] {{waypoint -275.14, -0.13, 51.21}} in [[The Mystic Lake]]
