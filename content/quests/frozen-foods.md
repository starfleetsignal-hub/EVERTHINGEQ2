## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- [[Gathering]] skill 190+ AND/OR [[Trapping]] skill 190+ required for this quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest 100 foodstuffs from shrubs and dens in [[Everfrost]].
1. Return to [[Duggin Brandywine|Duggin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill Experience
- At least 3s 7c
- One of the following:
  - [[Teir'dal shot glass]]
  - [[A dining plate]]
