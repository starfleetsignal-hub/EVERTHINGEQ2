## Prerequisites

To complete this quest, you must be able to speak [[Language of the Dust|Serilian]].

## Steps

Find the rest of the bow parts and see if you can put it together.

1. Obtain the other four parts of the mighty bow to go with Ritsy's lucky string
   - Obtain 2 mechanical servos (You need to find a key in the water in the begining of [[Spirit's Resonance]]. It is really hard to see, it is at the bottom of the pool (use gnomish diving goggles), on the side closest to the exit at {{waypoint 409, 116, -253}}. Left-click (double-click) the 2 tall golems in the room to turn them aggro, level 90^^^. They drop one servo each.)
   - Obtain a golden bow shaft (Dropped from [[The Gorgon Wrangler]] {{waypoint -260.00, -296.00, -612.00}} at the bottom of the Underfoot portion of [[The Hole]].)
   - Obtain some sort of binding, be observant (Green Shiny on ground in [[Skygazer Observatory]] area in "Dartain's Fortress" entrance within the bottom of the circular building in [[The Hole]].) {{waypoint 561, 121, -44}}
   - Obtain a finely crafted bow grip (listed as A Strong Bow Grip) from the Kerra Village Faction Merchant Purrak in Sundered Frontier at {{waypoint 1367, 47, 2155}}(30g, apparently no faction needed)
1. Go click the anvil at {{waypoint -26, 121, -239}} in [[The Hole]]. It is next to the daily faction quest giver.
1. Speak to [[Kardaxian]] {{waypoint 6, -102, -820}} in [[The Outer Vault]] (you need to speak [[Serilian]] to get the update). (The Outer Vault is marked No Merc, so unless you are a super character of some kind it is not likely you'll solo it.) ([[The Outer Vault]] is group accessible, so bring your friends.)
   - Kill [[Corrupted Guardian of the Seal]] in [[The Outer Vault]].
   - Kill [[Nortlav the Scalekeeper]] in [[Spirit's Resonance]].
1. Return to Kardaxian in [[The Outer Vault]] and he will begin to create the bow, after a few seconds hail him to claim your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 60g 50s 55c 85495 status
- [[The Bow of Underfoot (HQReward)|The Bow of Underfoot]]
