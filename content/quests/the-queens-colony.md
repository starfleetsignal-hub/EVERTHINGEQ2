The Queen's Colony guidebook mentions a lot of people who can be found within the outpost. I may learn something by speaking to these people.

## Note

This quest is initiated by reading the book "The Queen's Colony". This book is given as a reward for completing the "A Presence of Evil" quest. You must read all 12 pages to receive the quest. The quest is very simple. You just speak to various trainers related to your class. These will change depending on your class, but all of these are located within the fortress walls. Be sure to check up in the tower, as well.

Speak to the various NPCs, and then speak with Murrar Shar to compelte the quest.

## Steps

I need to visit each of the trainers mentioned in the guidebook.

- I should learn from Myrrin the Magnific in the top of Myrrin's Tower. (in The Queen's Colony)
- I should learn from Captain Warrik on the ground floor of Myrrin's Tower. (in The Queen's Colony)
- I should learn from Gregory Collins. He is outside Myrrin's Tower on its eastern edge. (in The Queen's Colony)
- I should learn from Cheye Seawind. She is outside Myrrin's Tower on its northwestern edge. (in The Queen's Colony)
- I should learn from Borda Gemseeker. She is southwest of Myrrin's Tower. (in The Queen's Colony)
- I should learn from Hayl McGuinness. He can be found northwest of Myrrin's Tower. (in The Queen's Colony)
- I should learn from Marla Gilliam. I can find her northwest of Myrrin's Tower. (in The Queen's Colony)
- I should learn from Verrin Glimmertide. I can find him northwest of Myrrin's Tower. (in The Queen's Colony)
- I should learn from Charlene Stoutmuffin. I can find her in the northwestern corner of the bank, north of Myrrin's Tower. (in The Queen's Colony)
- I should learn from Zidget Sprogrudder. I can find her in the northwestern corner of the bank, north of Myrrin's Tower. (in The Queen's Colony)
- I should learn from Gurga Trueheart. I can find him in the center of the bank, north of Myrrin's Tower. (in The Queen's Colony)
- I should tell Murrar Shar what I have done.

I should speak with Murrar Shar. (in The Queen's Colony)
