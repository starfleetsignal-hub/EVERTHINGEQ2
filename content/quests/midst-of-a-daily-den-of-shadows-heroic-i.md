The dark side of the moon is in the midst of dangerous times, no more so than today. And so I've been tasked with bringing down the enemies of Fordel within Vex Thal: Den of Shadows.

## Steps

See [[Vex Thal: Den of Shadows (Solo)]] for zone walkthrough.

- Complete my moon mission.
  - Kill the matriarch
  - Wreck the Warden
  - Vanquish the Xakra
  - Ixnay the Imperatrix
  - Nullify the Nemesis
  - Banish the Shade
