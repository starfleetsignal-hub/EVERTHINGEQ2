## Steps

*The long-forgotten Chamber of Stasis has not been seen by adventurers in ages, and is watched over by ancient creatures. Search through the chamber and confront the strongest creatures found there.*

- Enter the zone [[Ssrez'Ras: Chamber of Stasis (Heroic)]]:
  - Destroy [[Deviator Yothsra]]
  - Defeat the Twins [[Nemesis Arcanox]] & [[Nemesis Elementox]]
  - Defeat [[Ziskaya]]
  - Destroy [[The Guardian Icon]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- 5 [[Draconic Etyma]]
- [[Sparkling Crate]]
- a crate of heroic gear
