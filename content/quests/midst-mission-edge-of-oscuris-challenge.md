The precipice of the Plane of Shadows is home to a number of the most dangerous beings that threaten to spread their avarice for doom and gloom unless the most dangerous mortals band together to stop them. Myself and my comrades are armed and ready to invade Vex Thal: Edge of Oscuris, and banish the darkness, once and for all!

## Steps

- Slay the monstrosity
- Gouge the eye sore
- Terrorize the terror
- Expel the High Priestess

- At least 4p 49g 06s 40c
