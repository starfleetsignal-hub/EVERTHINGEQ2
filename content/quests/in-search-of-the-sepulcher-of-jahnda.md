## Steps

1. Talk to [[Mirf Guinders]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]] (North Qeynos) {{waypoint 295, -12, 86}}.
1. Kill any undead monsters in Vermin's Snye for 3 auto-updates
1. Return to Mirf
1. Slay [[Ayamia the Rat Queen]] in Vermin's Snye {{waypoint 45, 0, -140}}. She's a rare spawn, so you will probably need to clear the room multiple times to get her to spawn.
1. Visit the Central Stairwell {{waypoint 120, 0, -109}}.
1. Find the painting of Coldwind Cliffs {{waypoint 187, -7, -90}}.
1. Enter the Sepulcher of the Prime Healer {{waypoint 236, -7, -81}}. **WARNING**: you may be ambushed at this point if the undead in this area are not grey
1. Visit the Tomb of Lord Tonmerk {{waypoint 252, -6, -119}}. At this point, you can click the tomb to zone in.

## Rewards

- Access to the [[The Sepulcher of Jahnda]]
- At least 11s 40c
