## Steps

1. Speak to [[Rui]] {{waypoint -1573, -536, -2414}} # Buy [[Rui's Bird Nets]] (48s) from [[Chitter]] at {{waypoint -1596, -511, -2419}}
1. Find Rui and the birds {{waypoint -1316, -536, -2185}}
1. Use the net to catch three seagulls on the hill just south of Rui {{waypoint -1299, -520, -2128}}
1. Speak to Rui
