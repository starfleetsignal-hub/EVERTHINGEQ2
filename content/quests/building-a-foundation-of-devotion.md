## Steps

1. Prove your faith by harvesting sacred ore at the following locations:
   - [[Antonica]], inside the [[Gnoll Caves]]. {{waypoint -870, -28, -2}}
   - [[Blackburrow]], reached through the tiny pond in the spider area, near [[Geologist Fribden]]. {{waypoint 49, -41, -17}}
   - [[The Wailing Caves]], at the bottom of [[The Pit of Death]]. {{waypoint -55, -52, -169}}
   - [[Fallen Gate]] {{waypoint 44, 7, -122}}
1. Return to Bronlor Stormhammer.

## Rewards

- +1,250 faction with **Brell Serilis**
- [[Humble Altar of Brell Serilis]]
