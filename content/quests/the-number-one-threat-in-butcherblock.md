## Steps

1. Slay 10 bears in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
   - Any bear in the zone will update the quest, but the most convenient are the [[A slatepaw|slatepaws]] by [[The Druid Rings]] {{waypoint 20, 184, 494}}
1. Return to [[Trapper Coalbear]]

## Rewards

- at least 2s 91c

## Trivia

- This quest is an obvious cultural reference to Comedy Central's, [Stephen Colbert](http://www.colbertnation.com/home). He has a segment on his show called, "Threat Down", where he counts down the top 5 threats to America, the world, etc. There is / was a long running joke about bears being the number one threat to America (and Stephen Colbert hating bears).
  - He is also alluded to in a Lord of the Rings Online quest having to do with bears as well.
