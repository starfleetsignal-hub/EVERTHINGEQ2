## Steps

1. Travel to [[Blackwind Isle]], use your [[Hardened Pipe Reed]] at the listening staff at {{waypoint -293, 114, 259}}, and speak to [[Bedziine Redstone]] when he arrives.
1. Harvest 20 diamondjaw crawlers from 'diamondjaw crawler nests'. These are unique nodes found all over Blackwind Isle.
1. Place the crawlers on the cart at {{waypoint -755, 120, 263}}.
1. Return to Bedziine Redstone. Summon him with your pipe reed if necessary.
1. Return to [[Harun Clearsky]] on [[The Whisperwind Isle (POI)|The Whisperwind Isle]]. Summon him with your pipe reed if necessary.

## Rewards

- At least 9g 96s 54c
- +75 faction with **The Windgazer**
