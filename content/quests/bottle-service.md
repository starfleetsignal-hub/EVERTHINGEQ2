## Steps

![](images/Lost_bottle_of_wine.jpg)

1. Collect 6 *lost bottles of wine*.  They are located in [[The Main Ballroom]].  Fast respawn time.  The bottles are greyish transparent, so a bit hard to see, but once you found one, the others will be much easier to recognize.
1. Use the [[Empty Wine Crate]] in your inventory to get [[Crate of Wine]].
1. Return to [[Gaivin Sostner]].
1. Return to [[Taechun Welby]].

After you turn in the quest, he gives you directions to a secret passageway, but not a quest.

## Rewards

- At least 36g 79s 48c
- 3 [[Bottle of Ydalian Wine|Bottles of Ydalian Wine]]
