## Notes

- See the talk page for this quest or the page for the zone's patchcraft monster for detailed lists of spawn locations.

- [[Antonica]] and [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] both have [[A patchcraft thrasher|patchcraft thrashers]].
- [[Frostfang Sea]] and [[Greater Faydark]] both have [[A Patchcraft Growler|patchcraft growlers]].
- [[Darklight Wood]] and [[The Commonlands]] both have [[A patchcraft howler|patchcraft howlers]].
- [[Timorous Deep]], [[Obulus Frontier]], [[Karuupa Jungle]], [[Sodden Archipelago]], and [[Takish Badlands]] all have [[A patchcraft Snapper|patchcraft snappers]].
- Patchwork creatures are [[Tracking|trackable]].

## Steps

1. I must travel to a myriad of areas across Norrath in search of the patchcraft components that Rakshatta has requested.
   - Collect ensorcelled fabric from patchcraft creatures.
   - Collect magical ribbons from [[A patchcraft Snapper|patchcraft snappers]].
   - Collect enchanted zippers from [[A patchcraft thrasher|patchcraft thrashers]].
   - Collect mischievous fluff from patchcraft creatures.
   - Collect arcane yarn from [[A patchcraft howler|patchcraft howlers]].
   - Collect ensorcelled buttons from [[A Patchcraft Growler|patchcraft growlers]].
1. Return to [[Rakshatta]]

## Rewards

One of the following:

- [[Aged Patchcraft Keeper Helm]] - an [[Appearance|appearance]] helm that looks like a bucket.
- [[Patchcraft Keeper Helm]] - an [[Appearance|appearance]] helm that looks like a bucket.
- 5 [[Fool's Gold Coin]] - event currency you can spend at any [[A Bristlebane Day merchant|Bristlebane Day merchant]]
