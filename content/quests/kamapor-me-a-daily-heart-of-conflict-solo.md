## Steps

Defeat all encounters in  [[Karuupa Jungle: Heart of Conflict (Solo)]] :

1. Chop up some vines.
1. Defeat the deino duo.
1. Ixnay the invader
1. Ambush the ape.
1. Slaughter the shaman.

Note: on entering the  zone you will be automatically issued with the zone quest "Kamapor It On: Heart of Conflict (Solo)" (if it's not already in your journal). This extra quest requires just the last two bosses.

Completing the zone completes both quests.
