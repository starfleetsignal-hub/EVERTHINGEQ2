## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Mithaniel Marr has asked me to assist in gathering valorbound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with the collecting the valorbound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. I need to collect enough [[Valorbound Energy Reserves|valorbound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect [[Valorbound Energy Reserves|valorbound energy]] by defeating powerful foes 0/10
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Truthbringer]] {{waypoint -840, -48, 199}} in [[The Commonlands]]
