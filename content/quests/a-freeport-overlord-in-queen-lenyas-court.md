## Steps

1. Journey to the [[New Tunaria]] and enter [[The Throne of New Tunaria: Operation Overlord]], a special instance of [[Throne of New Tunaria]]. {{waypoint -72, 96, -205}}
1. Speak with [[A sergeant]] near the entrance. {{waypoint -70, 67, -809}}
1. Speak with [[Lucan D'Lere (The Throne of New Tunaria: Operation Overlord)|Lucan D'Lere]]. {{waypoint -76, 90, -611}}
1. Speak with [[Queen Lenya Thex (NPC)|Queen Lenya Thex]] in [[The Royal Sanctum (The Throne of New Tunaria: Operation Overlord)|The Royal Sanctum]] below. {{waypoint -75, 66, -554}}
1. Speak with [[The Palace Overseer]] next to Queen Lenya Thex. {{waypoint -77, 66, -553}}
1. Return to Lucan D'Lere and report your findings.
1. Leave the zone and the quest will automatically complete.
1. See [[#Unlocking Soulfire's True Power|below]] for how to unlock [[Soulfire]]'s true power.

## Unlocking Soulfire's True Power

When you were speaking with [[Lucan D'Lere (The Throne of New Tunaria: Operation Overlord)|Lucan D'Lere]], he left you with a final clue: "Unfortunately, your skills are not as grand as I was led to believe. However, you can redeem yourself. When my expedition is complete here, infiltrate this throne. Seek and destroy the one that keeps me from the key I seek. He has not made his presence known, but I can sense him cowering in the shadows of my overpowering radiance. Perhaps he will let himself be known when I am gone."

The 'one' you must seek out is none other than [[Mayong Mistmoore]]. Although the quest text makes it seem like you must defeat him in Throne of New Tunaria, you will get credit if you kill him in any of the three places he appears: [[Castle Mistmoore]], [[Mistmoore's Inner Sanctum]], or [[Throne of New Tunaria]]. There is no quest for this; simply slay Mayong and speak with [[Sir Trodonis]]. You will be able to upgrade your Lucanic weapon into one of the following:

- [[Soulfire Gladius (Mythical)]]
- [[Soulfire Hammer (Mythical)]]
- [[Soulfire Kama (Mythical)]]
- [[Soulfire Longsword (Mythical)]]
- [[Soulfire Sabre (Mythical)]]
- [[Soulfire Staff (Mythical)]]
