### Steps

Craft 12 items from the random list below:

- [[Indium Battle Hammer (Level 50) (Crafted)|Indium Battle Hammer]]
- [[Indium Crescent Axe (Level 50) (Crafted)|Indium Crescent Axe]]
- [[Indium Javelin (Level 50) (Crafted)|Indium Javelin]]
- [[Indium Morning Star (Level 50) (Crafted)|Indium Morning Star]]
- [[Indium Hand Axe (Level 50) (Crafted)|Indium Hand Axe]]
- [[Indium Parrying Blade (Level 50) (Crafted)|Indium Parrying Blade]]
- [[Indium Flail (Level 50) (Crafted)|Indium Flail]]
- [[Indium Shamshir]]
- [[Indium Tribal Spear (Level 50) (Crafted)|Indium Tribal Spear]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

### Rewards

- At Least 1g 21s 50c
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
