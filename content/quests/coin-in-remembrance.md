## Steps

1. Bury the ancient High Keep coin within the [[Blessed Knoll]] {{waypoint 418, 35, 719}}.
   - Click the sparkling pile of dirt in the middle of the druid ring. When you do, an NPC called [[Glade Guardian]] appears, throws you up in the air and says that "Lord Kithicor's rest is supposed to be eternal."
1. Recover the buried coin
   - Click the glowing green pile of dirt to reveal a dagger.  Then click the dagger to pick it up.
1. Return the dagger to Eleden Calmseas
