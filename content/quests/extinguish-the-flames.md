The flames of this ascension brazier call upon the Aberrants from below. I need to extinguish the flames somehow, and do so as quickly as I can!

## Notes

- You have 4 minutes to complete this quest.
- The quest starter is found in a cave up in the ceiling of [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]]. The cave entrance is at {{waypoint -83.17, 235.93, -778.77}}.

## Steps

1. Extinguish **three** flames in the **ascension brazier**.
   - Note the color of the brazier's flame. Your quest journal will also state the color. The flame colors are random each time.
1. Kill [[An Aberrant Ascender|Aberrant Ascenders]] near the top edge of the room, looting a colored ascension stone from each.
   - Each aberrant will drop a red, blue or yellow ascension stone.
     - Two of these stones will be combined to form a new stone that matches the color of the flame to be extinguished.
     - There is a subtle aura around each aberrant that will have the same color as the stone it drops, so you will only have to kill the two that you need.
   - Look for aberrants up high around the edges of the cave. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -52.99, 263.40, -686.80}}
     - {{waypoint -69.01, 298.90, -601.00}}
     - {{waypoint -106.04, 232.58, -691.72}}
     - {{waypoint -168.50, 311.97, -621.95}}
     - {{waypoint -204.87, 301.53, -720.78}}
1. Right-click and *Examine* one of the stones in order to combine it with another stone.
   - To extinguish the green flame, you must combine [[A blue ascension stone]] and [[A yellow ascension stone]] to create [[An emerald ascension stone]].
   - To extinguish the orange flame, you must combine [[A red ascension stone]] and [[A yellow ascension stone]] to create [[A topaz ascension stone]].
   - To extinguish the purple flame, you must combine [[A red ascension stone]] and [[A blue ascension stone]] to create [[An amethyst ascension stone]].
1. Click the brazier to extinguish the flame.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +250 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- [[Dirt encrusted key]]
