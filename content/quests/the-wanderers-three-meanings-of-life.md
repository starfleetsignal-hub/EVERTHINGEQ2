## Steps

1. Kill a number of monsters in [[The Thundering Steppes]], randomly assigned from the following:
   - 3 [[A plains serpent|plains serpent]]s around {{waypoint 184, -2, 1354}}.
   - 3 [[An elder hawk|elder hawk]]s around {{waypoint 76, 0, 1320}}.
   - 3 [[A carrion gorger|carrion gorger]]s around {{waypoint 66, -22, 777}}.
1. Return to [[The Wandering Augur]].
1. Kill a number of monsters in The Thundering Steppes, randomly assigned from the following:
   - 5 [[A large shore crab|large shore crab]]s. {{waypoint 1024, -21, 1164}}
   - 5 [[An irresistible siren|irresistible siren]]s. {{waypoint 1193, 0, 1066}}
1. Return to [[The Wandering Augur]].
1. He then asks you to visit one of the following places:
   - [[Coldwind Shores Cemetery]]: {{waypoint 1317, -6, -80}}
   - [[The Last Stand]]: {{waypoint -380, 6, 761}}
   - [[Shrine of Decay]]: {{waypoint 357, 5, 1189}}
1. Return to [[The Wandering Augur]].

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
