## starting the quest

This is a Guide Quest, as such it, is is held by a player volunteer who is not always there. Their presence will usually be announced in the Guide Forums or via an in-game message in General channel.

Speak to the [[Guides of the Shattered Lands|Guide]], who is typically in [[Kelethin]] near the Tinkered Portal-Gate to Thalumbra:{{waypoint 298.85, 127.45, 308.40}}
