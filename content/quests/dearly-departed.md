## Steps

1. Head to [[The Ethermist Shores]] {{waypoint 114, -41, -157}} for this quest.
   - Defeat Horde threatening wayward spirits
     - Find groups of [[A Skirth devourer|Skrith devourers]] and kill them
   - Rescue lost Wayward spirits
     - Every time you defeat a group of Skrith devourers, talk to the Wayward soul they were attacking.
1. Return to Riishu

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
