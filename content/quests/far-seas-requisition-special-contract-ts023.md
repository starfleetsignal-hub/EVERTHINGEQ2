- There are Four Sequential Quests

## 1

### Steps

1. Harvest 12 hardened Steppes stones from [[Ore Nodes#Tier 3|stonecrest ore]]
1. Collect a beating heart from [[A corpse feeder]]
1. Collect 6 antlers from [[A mature antelope|mature antelope]]s

## 2

### Steps

1. Grab a pint of Gatehouse brew from [[Bridge Keep]] {{waypoint 217, 0, -159}}
1. Collect 9 headdresses from [[An Amazon abbot|Amazon abbot]]s (drop rate 33%-40%)
   - Fly to [[Coldwind Station]], keep the Centaur camp clear; avoid isolating kills of just the abbot's
1. Collect 6 prayer shawls from [[A Steelhoof prophet|Steelhoof prophet]]s (drop rate 16%-20%)

### Notes

- [[An Amazon abbot|Amazon abbot]] seems to be the rarest spawn; at least while you need them for step #2
  - Had 1-3 spawn every cycle; clearing from the Station, to the Tents and west to the river-bed
  - Never had an abbot spawn along the river-bed in the grassy (green) area; only around the tents.
