## Notes

- This quest is the former access quest for [[Bloodskull Valley: The Excavation Site]], however it's completion is no longer required to access that instance.

## Steps

1. Kill the [[Bloodskull messenger]] in [[The Commonlands]]. He wanders in a small circular path in the area east of [[The Ruins of Val`marr]]. The southern end of his path takes him right by the tent and small graveyard that you spawn at when you die.
1. Gather 4 mineral deposits. These are clickable rocks not too far from the entrance of [[Bloodskull Valley]].
   - glowing piece of ore {{waypoint 289, -47, 657}}
   - jagged piece of ore  {{waypoint 164, -46, 607}}
   - shimmering piece of ore {{waypoint 94, -47, 604}}
   - chipped piece of ore {{waypoint -46, -44, 599}}
1. Return to the fallen blackguard.

## Rewards

- Formerly, access to [[Bloodskull Valley: The Excavation Site]]
