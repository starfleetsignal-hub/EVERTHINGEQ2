## Prerequisites

You must be at least level 15 to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Merchant Edmond]] at the [[Crossroads]]
1. Speak to [[V'tal Narin]] at the [[Crossroads]]
1. Complete the sub-quest, [[Narin Heirloom]]
1. After completing [[Narin Heirloom]], speak to [[Merchant Edmond]] at [[The Crossroads]]
1. Return to [[Inquisitor Irizan]].

- 29s
