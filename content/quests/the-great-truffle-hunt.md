## Notes

- *Timed quest: Shattered Seas solo daily, 3 minutes and 30 seconds.*

## Steps

1. Harvest 10 truffles within the time limit.
   - Click for dirt mounds at the foot of large trees. If you get a truffle, click it.
   - Sometimes instead of a truffle you will get a trap. Click the trap, then spam your "F" key while holding your cursor over the trap to break loose.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 67g 40s 5800 status
- 3 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- Achievement [[Truffle Hunter]] upon first completion
