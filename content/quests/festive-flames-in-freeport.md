This quest is repeatable.

## Notes

![The markers for placing event decorations is a green sparkle.](images/Heroes-fest-decorating-location.jpg)

- All of the decoration locations are marked with a green sparkle that looks similar to a [[Collection Quests|collection]] "shiny" from a distance.
- If working on this and placing the lanterns for the lost decorations quest, you may have to pass by the same general areas a few times, as the refresh between setting the Ro Candle and the paper lanterns may have a considerable time gap.

## Steps

1. Find 4 places to "celebrate" in Freeport by setting  a [[Case of Ro candles|Ro Candle]] off. The activation points look and act like green shinies.
   - Along the row of shops in West Freeport {{waypoint 161, -3, -93}} or {{waypoint 85, -10, -23}}
   - By the statue of the overlord in West Freeport {{waypoint 241, -4, 116}} or {{waypoint 220, -3, 102}}
   - Along Victory Road in [[South Freeport]] {{waypoint -90, -25, 154}} or {{waypoint -78, -25, 266}}
   - On the docks in South Freeport {{waypoint -267, -56, 204}} or {{waypoint -294, -56, 136}}
1. Return to Gandy Gearlotta

## Rewards

- Choice of:
  - [[Heroes' Festival Cobbler]] x2
  - [[Heroes' Festival Fizzer]] x2
  - [[Token of Heroism]] (event currency)

Repeating the quest for the fifth time offers an additional reward:

- [[Ro Candle]]
