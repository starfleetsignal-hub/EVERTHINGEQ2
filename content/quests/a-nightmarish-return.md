## Steps

1. Relive the tale by working through The Hedge Hollow and killing Altius Volfban.
- The steps in this quest are identical to [[A Nightmarish Illness]]; however you can also hail the gnome in the maze to get the [[Train to Zone!]] quest and, provided you've completed other requisite NoTD quests, you can locate and loot [[A Gleaming Chest]] in the maze.
- IMPORTANT: If you zone out before killing the last werewolf, Altius, you'll have to start over upon reentry.
- Note: If you are doing doing this for [[A lost necklace]], you need to loot Altius's body and then right-click on the statue to be placed closer to the chest, rather than at the start of the maze.
