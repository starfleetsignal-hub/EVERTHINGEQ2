## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.. However [[Trial of Sun Kai: Solo (Instance)]] has a minimum persistence of 2 days 20 hours, so no new terracotta warriors will spawn should you zone into the instance again after completing this quest.
- This quest scales with player level up to 70.
- You can receive additional [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]] as a rare body drop from the terracotta warriors. These can be turned into [[Brother Huang Mantian]] for titles.
- Mercenaries cannot be summoned to assist you in the solo version of this quest.

## Steps

- Destroy 5 vases within 30 minutes. Vases are [[Tracking|Trackable]] and may spawn at other locations than those listed below:
  - {{waypoint -52, -33, -36}}
  - {{waypoint 58, -50, -41}}
  - {{waypoint 83, -56, -4}}
  - {{waypoint 43, -89, -11}}
  - {{waypoint -66, -89, 8}}
  - {{waypoint 66, -46, -1}}
  - {{waypoint -27, -41, -15}}
  - {{waypoint 21, -41, -13}}
  - {{waypoint 28, -70, -40}}

## Rewards

- 35 [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]]
