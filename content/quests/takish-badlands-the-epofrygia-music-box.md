## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key by killing 15 [[A dustwing ravager|dustwing ravagers]] in [[Takish Badlands]] around {{waypoint -680.19, 197.20, 158.40}} at [[Takish Dunes]], [[Dunetooth Bluff]], or [[Hu Haket]].
1. I need to find the Epofrygia Music Box key in [[Sandstone Delta]] from [[A Tsmang Blightswarm drone|Tsmang Blightswarm drones]] around {{waypoint 530.35, 103.12, -693.28}} at [[Tsmang Blightswarm Hive]].
1. Examine the clue to receive the quest [[The Epofrygia Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the Epofrygia Music Box in [[Takish Badlands]].
