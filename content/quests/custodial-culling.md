## Description

I must enter Vasty Deep: The Conservatory and kill the custodians within its walls. There are two kinds of custodians, the gilded custodians and the forgotten custodians.

## Steps

1. Kill gilded custodians and forgotten custodians within the [[Vasty Deep: The Conservatory]].
   - Kill three [[A gilded custodian|gilded custodians]]
   - Kill three [[A forgotten custodian|forgotten custodians]]
1. Return to [[Alia]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 80g
- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
