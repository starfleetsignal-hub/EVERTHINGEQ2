## Steps

1. Help the Qeynos Guard by investigating reports of Frogloks being tricked into leaving [[Castleview Hamlet]].
   - Locate [[Fribit]] in front of [[Irontoes East]] in [[North Qeynos]] and ask why he felt the need to leave [[Castleview Hamlet]]. {{waypoint 360, -21, 131}}
   - Locate [[Klecker]] at the [[Temple of Life]] in [[North Qeynos]] and ask why he felt the need to leave [[Castleview Hamlet]].
1. Enter the [[Castleview Hamlet: Cloister of Purity|Castleview Hamlet]] gate from [[Qeynos Harbor]], [[Qeynos Province District]] and investigate where the pressure upon the Froglocks is originating. {{waypoint 722, -21, -119}}
1. Destroy 6 propaganda posters.
   - {{waypoint 761, -20, -133}}
   - {{waypoint 757, -20, -116}}
   - {{waypoint 747, -21, -107}}
   - {{waypoint 737, -21, -111}}
   - {{waypoint 801, -16, -116}}
   - {{waypoint 794, -21, -97}}
1. Defeat 5 of the Renda'Dal that are spreading the misinformation.
1. Return to to [[Recruitment Officer Brandit]]. You may be ambushed by Renda'Dal as you leave [[Castleview Hamlet: Cloister of Purity|Castleview Hamlet]].
1. Report to [[General Golias Sydwen]] near the castle. {{waypoint 476, 3, -2}}

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
