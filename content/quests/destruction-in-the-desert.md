## Steps

![A Sul'Dal mystical orb](images/A_Sul'Dal_mystical_orb.png)
Many things that would oppose the clergy in the Dismal Rage exists in the deserts baking under Ro's hot gaze. The Dismal Rage asks that I destroy them before they can taint their teachings.<br>

1. [[Fethar Lothki]] will randomly offer you one of the following tasks:
   - destroy 20 x [[A Sul'Dal mystical orb]] the in the caves {{waypoint -695, -233, -1200}} beneath the Eye of Anuk in [[The Sinking Sands]].
     - You can access the caves either by dropping down into the hole or by the sea. The orbs are found on the ground in the cave system, and you need to destroy 20 orbs. From time to time they disappear and shift around to different areas of the cave floor. There are only a few around the cave, but more spawn every minute or so.
   - destroy 20 x Rujarkian spiritists in [[The Sinking Sands]]
   - hunt down 20 x Anaz Mal diviners near the northern Tear of Anuk in [[The Sinking Sands]] ![](images/An_Anaz'Mal_incense_burner.png)
   - destroy 20 x Anaz Mal incense burners near northern Tear of Anuk ( around {{waypoint -465, -134, -1108}})  in [[The Sinking Sands]]
1. Return to [[Fethar Lothki]] at {{waypoint -92, -10, -163}} in [[The City of Freeport]]

## Rewards

- [[City Token]]
- At least 2595 status at level 55
- +500 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
