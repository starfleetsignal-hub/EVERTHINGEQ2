## Steps

1. Slay the liches found within the Vestigial Cella to free the tortured souls trapped within.
   - Slay ten liches within the Vestigial Cella.
   - Slay [[The Wight Specter]] within the Vestigial Cella.
   - Slay [[Ecto Malgum]] within the Vestigial Cella.
1. Return to [[Akram]].

## Rewards

- At least 90g 70s 61c
