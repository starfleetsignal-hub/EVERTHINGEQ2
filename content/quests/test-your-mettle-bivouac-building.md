## Notes

- The forge at {{waypoint 720.93, -43.60, -472.77}} is actually a multi-purpose craft table.
- Total materials required: 12 Aether Ore, 9 Bittersweet Vineroot, 9 Corymbia Wood, 9 Direbeak Hide, 15 Pepitas Seeds, 9 Trystine Gem, 6 Charger meat, and 6 Breadfruit
- Total fuel required: 15 Zimaran Candle, 30 Zimaran Sandpaper, and 15 Zimaran Kindling (plus 5 Zimaran coal for the next quest).

## Steps

1. "I must speak with the foreman of the bivouac area in the Aether Wroughtlands."
   - *Use the Splendor Sky Aether Breach portal at {{waypoint -1093, 181, 737}} to get to [[Zimara Breadth]], then turn around and click on the Zimara Breadth portal and go to [[Aether Wroughtlands]].*
   - *Flight should be enabled in both Splendor Sky and Zimara Breadth by this stage.*
     - Even Tishan's Fluffy Mount that looks like it's land only that you got at the start of the xpac can fly
1. Speak to [[Dargrim]] at {{waypoint 712.04, -43.65, -457.51}}
1. Craft 3 of each of the following:
   - [[Bivouac Healing Potions]]
   - [[Bivouac Repair Kits]]
   - [[Bivouac Barricades]]
   - [[Bivouac Provisions]]
   - *Note: All of em can be crafted at the "Bivuac Forge" in front of [[Gruduk]] (Mender) at {{waypoint 720.93, -43.60, -472.77}}*
1. Return to [[Dargrim]].

## Rewards

- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c 54250 status
- +4,000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
