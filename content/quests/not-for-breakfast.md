## Steps

1. Obtain these spell components in [[Drukyna Marsh]]:
   - 8 [[Wakingdew root ball|wakingdew root balls]] {{waypoint -634.77, -47.07, -2.20}} by killing the ones that spawn from killing the carnivorous [[Brackish wakingdew|brackish wakingdews]]![brackish wakingdew](images/Brackish_wakingdew.jpg) on the western land part of [[Drukyna Marsh]].![Cnidcara Egg Clutch(on rock)](images/Cnidcara_Egg_Clutch.jpg)![Cnidcara Egg Clutch(in water)](images/Cnidcara_Egg_Clutch%28w%29.jpg)
   - Gather 6 ***Cnidcara Egg Clutches*** in the eastern water part of [[Drukyna Marsh]].
     - *Note: The [[Cnidcara nester|cnidcara nesters]] will attack to defend the eggs if they are nearby. The eggs can be found near or on rocks for example in these locations:*
     - {{waypoint -777.42, -55.17, 228.43}}
     - {{waypoint -859.82, -55.17, 99.15}}
     - {{waypoint -843.71, -56.09, 65.48}}
     - {{waypoint -678.09, -55.17, 140.45}}
     - {{waypoint -667, -55, 130}}
     - -668.73. -55.17 153.76
1. Return to [[Shirada]] {{waypoint -151.42, 573.32, -551.04}} in [[Khali'Vahla]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Effect Scroll]] x3
- +5000 faction with **House Vahla**
- -5000 faction with **House Yrzu**
