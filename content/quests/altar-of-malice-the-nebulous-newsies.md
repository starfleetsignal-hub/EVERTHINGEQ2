## Notes

- The guide, Naylie NelANews, is not always there.
- The quest is offered to the whole group, but it can't be shared.
- You only need the guide to start the quest, there is no time limit to finish the quest.
- If low level, one **MUST** be mentored, or the kills will not update quest.

## Steps

1. Find [[Naylie DelANews]] in [[Phantom Sea]] {{waypoint -43, 30, 961}}
1. Kill undead rangers for vellum {{waypoint 192, 61, 852}}
1. Get ink from [[A savage entangler|savage entangler]]s, east coast off [[Kithicor Island]], around {{waypoint -145, 3, 205}}.
   - *Note: If you cannot find savage entanglers, kill the eels and snappers (non-aggro) in the water in this area as they are place holders, or switch instances of Phantom Seas*
   - *Note: It will be faster when you just fly above the water to spot them, instead of swimming around.*
1. Return to Naylie![](images/Magic_and_Potions!.JPG) {{waypoint -44, 31, 961}}
1. Visit the village of [[Ghorkaal]] on Kithicor Island {{waypoint 860, 33, 903}}
1. In one of the buildings in Ghorkaal, find a (possibly small) black book called "Magic and Potions!" on the right side of the bookcase at the back of the building {{waypoint 803, 46, 931}} (Group Update)![](images/A_vial_of_pure_ethereal_magic.JPG)
1. Find a vial of pure ethereal magic in Ghorkaal {{waypoint 821, 51, 858}} on table (purple bottle) in back of room (Group Update)
1. Return to Naylie {{waypoint -44, 31, 961}}
1. Locate [[Highpass]] {{waypoint 769, 328, 104}} or anywhere along that path to Castle Highhold
   - *Note: works best to fly along east coast and come in behind Castle Highhold to avoid being knocked down.*
   - *Note: you can get both updates "near" the weekly tradeskill npc*
1. Locate [[Castle Highhold]] {{waypoint 626, 437, 175}}
1. Use the printer in Castle Highhold {{waypoint 524, 352, -83}} (you must fly over the walls). Click 12 times for update. (Group Update)
1. Return to Naylie {{waypoint -44, 31, 961}}
1. Pick up **Residual Magic** residue from 11 spirits of the lost Newsies in major cities. After you release them, make sure **to click on the ground spawn and collect the residue.** (You can ask guards to locate “newsie”)  ![](images/Altar_of_Malice_-_The_Nebulous_Newsies_quest_-_Residual_Magic.jpg)
   - Pendair in [[Qeynos Capitol District|South Qeynos]] {{waypoint 568, -17, 273}}
   - Casiopiea Dorfwife in [[Qeynos Province District|Qeynos Harbor]] {{waypoint 787, -23, 104}}
   - Melenkurion the Mischevious in [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 178, 111, 94}}
   - Semiramis the Wise in [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 432, 138, 247}}
   - Shanta d'Elfie in [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 262, 127, 280}}
   - Mistigris the Enigmatic in [[New Halas]] {{waypoint -342, 182, -215}}
   - Ozymandius the Traveler in [[New Halas]] {{waypoint 117, 152, 14}}
   - Aezaar the Damned in [[The City of Freeport|North Freeport]] {{waypoint -102, -8, -166}}
   - Imaryn Yth'Dare in [[The City of Freeport|North Freeport]] {{waypoint 20, -20, -302}} via [[The Thieves' Way]] Freeport North Exit
   - Argail the Vile in [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]] {{waypoint -342, 26, 138}}
   - Misharu  in [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]]  {{waypoint -110, 9, 322}}
     - *Note: If you do this in a group and make sure no one clicks the dialog too fast you can get 1 update for each player from each npc.*
     - *Additional Note: You do not need to travel all over to release all of the spirits.  As of August, 2015, each repops within 90 minutes (may be quicker) and gives credit for being released again. (You can speed up the process with those that are clustered close together.)*
     - *If you're 115+, don't wait. Just hit all the locations and you can get this done in 15 minutes or so.*
1. Return to Naylie on the small island in the Phantom Sea {{waypoint -44, 31, 961}}
1. Speak to Naylie at Cardin Ward in [[Obol Plains]] {{waypoint -336, 15, 21}}
