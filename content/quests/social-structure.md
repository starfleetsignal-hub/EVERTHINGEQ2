## Steps

1. Find and kill the four tiers in the Huuptics' social structure. They are all located along the beach near to the quest giver:
   - [[Huuptic idler]]
   - [[Huuptic hunter]]
   - [[Huuptic fetcher]]
   - [[Huuptic elder]]
1. Return to [[Anrev Kaelyna]].

## Rewards

- At least 99s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Lizard Scale Arcane Bracelet]] (All Mages)
  - [[Lizard Scale Covert Bracelet]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Lizard Scale Devout Bracelet]] (All Priests)
  - [[Lizard Scale Martial Bracelet]] (All Fighters)
