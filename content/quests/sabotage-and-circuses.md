## Steps

1. Speak with [[Florin Gearcrank]] {{waypoint 10.70, -69.36, -307.54}}.
   - Receive the [[Darkpaw Sabotage For Beginners]] recipe scroll.
1. Enter [[Darkpaw: Sabotage (Tradeskill)]] instance at {{waypoint -6.92, -61.79, -323.70}}.
1. Destroy the [[Flea-Ridden Pelt]] on the ground at {{waypoint -68.47, -69.47, -253.21}}.
1. You received the [[Flea-Bitten Sabotage]] quest when you stepped into this instance,
   - Enemies in this instance are very high level e.g. Level 140, so don't proceed past the first room without wearing the [[Darkpaw Illusion]].
   1. Craft a [[Darkpaw Illusion]].
      1. Collect [[Darkpaw Leather]] scrap from the [[Darkpaw Leather|Darkpaw Leather Pelt]] at {{waypoint -52.08, -68.21, -240.86}}.
      1. Collect [[Darkpaw Fur]] from the small Darkpaw Fur pelt on the ground at {{waypoint -31.73, -69.28, -230.08}}.
      1. Mine [[Darkpaw Fuel]] {{waypoint -40.21, -68.36, -231.70}} or from other locations in the first room.
         - [[Darkpaw Fuel]] will be used in multiple recipes so additional fuel can be mined for this future use.
      1. Create a [[Darkpaw Illusion]] using the [[Darkpaw Mortar & Pestle]] on the ground at {{waypoint -60.99, -65.12, -218.32}}.
   1. Use the [[Darkpaw Illusion]].
      - A [[Darkpaw Illusion]] will not timeout but it will be deleted when you cancel it or leave the zone. It will have to be re-crafted every time you enter this Instance.
   1. Craft [[Darkpaw Flea Repellent]].
      1. Collect a [[Fleawort]] herb from the [[Fleawort|Fleawort plant]] at {{waypoint -42.39, -69.32, -206.09}}.
      1. Collect [[Darkpaw Herbs]] from the [[Darkpaw Herbs|Darkpaw Herbs plant]] at {{waypoint -70.58, -68.68, -230.02}}.
      1. Craft the [[Darkpaw Flea Repellent]] using the [[Darkpaw Mortar & Pestle]] on the ground at {{waypoint -60.99, -65.12, -218.32}}.
   1. Throw the [[Darkpaw Flea Repellent]] bombs by clicking on the purple sparkles in front of the 3 [[Itching Adventurers]] at {{waypoint -46.61, -65.16, -226.53}}.
   1. Craft [[Darkpaw Acid]].
      1. Collect [[Darkpaw Salt]] from the [[Darkpaw Salt|Darkpaw Salt pile]] at {{waypoint -92.78, -69.99, -205.97}}.
      1. Collect [[Mineralized Pond Water]] from the purple sparkles in the pond water at {{waypoint -89.28, -73.55, -164.65}}.
      1. Craft [[Darkpaw Acid]] using the nearby [[Darkpaw Vial]] at {{waypoint -88.46, -73.61, -167.94}}.
   1. Destroy all three bolts of Darkpaw fabric that are in the room.
      1. Click on the bolt of Green Design Fabric at {{waypoint -85.07, -70.62, -201.90}}.
      1. Click on the bolt of Blue Flowered Fabric at {{waypoint -87.48, -72.75, -184.25}}.
      1. Click on the bolt of Blue Geometric Fabric at {{waypoint -45.81, -73.20, -176.29}}.
   1. Find and release [[NPC|Glorin]] from the cell at {{waypoint -7.95, -63.25, -109.30}}.
      - Harvest two pieces of [[Metal Scrap]] plus a [[Darkpaw Fuel]] along the way.
      1. Craft [[Darkpaw Bolt Cutters]] on the [[Darkpaw Forge]] at {{waypoint -18.28, -62.89, -105.41}}.
      1. Click on the purple sparkles in front of the cell door at {{waypoint -18.20, -63.69, -108.60}} to use the Bolt Cutters to open it.
      1. Speak to [[NPC|Glorin]]. He will vanish as soon as the conversation ends.
   1. Craft a [[Darkpaw Bone Grinder]] to grind up nearby bones.
      1. Travel to {{waypoint -55.20, -68.48, -94.10}} area.
         1. Harvest two pieces of [[Darkpaw Stone]] from the Stone nodes.
         1. Harvest one pieces of [[Darkpaw Fuel]] from the fuel nodes.
      1. Craft a [[Darkpaw Bone Grinder]] using the [[Darkpaw Mortar & Pestle]] at {{waypoint -68.75, -70.29, -68.35}}.
   1. Click on 3 skulls with purple sparkles to grind them up them.
      1. Some possible locations are:-
         - {{waypoint -66.68, -70.47, -77.17}}.
         - {{waypoint -62.16, -70.22, -40.60}}.
         - {{waypoint -109.80, -70.30, -50.91}}.
   1. Check the dying mushroom
      1. Travel back to the first room {{waypoint -50.46, -64.84, -215.89}}.
      1. Click on the dying mushroom.
   1. Collect 2 [[Natural Antifungal]] from the plant at the base of the mushroom.
   1. Craft [[Slow-Acting Antifungal]] using the nearby [[Darkpaw Mortar & Pestle]].
   1. Travel to the Zygmoyd area {{waypoint 80.67, -80.22, -125.76}}
   1. Click on the purple sparkles to release the antifungal.
   1. Speak with [[Glorin Gearcran]] at {{waypoint 80, -80, -125}} in the Zygmoyd area to complete the Flea-Bitten Sabotage sub-quest.
   1. Return to [[Rinndle]] in the [[Darkpaw Dugout]] to finish the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 67,200 status
- 10 [[Sabotage Silver]]
- [[Darkpaw Adventuring Aids]]
- Recipes for the following items:-
  - [[Darkpaw Arcane Ark]]
  - [[Darkpaw gong mallet]]
  - [[Darkpaw ladder]] - places a ladder in front of you in [[Darkpaw Warrens (Solo)|Darkpaw Warrens]], to be able to climb onto ledges and other areas
  - [[Darkpaw powder keg]] - places a barrel that can be ignited to destroy nearby walls and break apart Terramite deposits for [[Raw terramite]]
  - [[Darkpaw shovel]] - used to dig up dirt mounds and mushroom patches
  - [[Darkpaw Sniffer]]
  - [[Darkpaw terraporter crystal]] - used to repair broken terraporters
  - [[Darkpaw wolf whistle]]
  - [[Darkpaw Seasoned Shank]]
