## Steps

1. Speak with [[Zha Gu'Lau (Feerrott)|Zha Gu'Lau]] at [[Murdunk's Fall]] in [[The Feerrott]]. {{waypoint -320, 3, 105}}
1. Retrieve the skulls of the following lizardmen leaders. They are found in chests at two lizardmen camps:
   - *Dar'Lek skull*: {{waypoint -1025, 6, 710}}
   - *Vul'Thos skull*: {{waypoint -1260, 4, 685}}
1. Go to the top of [[The Temple of Alliz]], where [[The Eye of Thule]] is, and use the skulls from your inventory to summon [[Keeper Dar'Lek]] and [[Keeper Vul'Thos]]. Loot the charms that they drop. {{waypoint -323, 9, 853}}
   - Only one charm drops per kill, so if in a group, the summoning must be done by each person individually.
1. Go to [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]] {{waypoint -1702, -8, 819}} then zone in and kill [[Thulian Arch-Guardian]]. To summon the guardian, place the charms in the statues at the entrance right when you zone in, at {{waypoint 7, 7, 145}} and {{waypoint -7, 7, 145}}. The guardian will spawn at the top of the nearby stairs and slowly walk down.
   - If you fail to kill him you must wait ~10 minutes before you can start the event again.
1. Kill [[Dunarin Theerego]] in the [[Cave of Fear (POI)|Cave of Fear]] in [[The Feerrott]]. He will spawn as you approach the [[Portal of Fear (POI)|Portal of Fear]]. {{waypoint -1830, 30, 450}}
1. Return to Zha Gu'Lau.

## Rewards

- Choice of:
  - [[Cape of Thule]]
  - [[Cloak of Thule]]
- At least 27g 50s
