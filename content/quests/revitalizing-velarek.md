## Steps

1. Summon and Speak to Manifestation of Vel'Arek.
   - *Target yourself, right-click on Vel'Arek and select Use. This can be done from any where at any time.*
1. Slay [[Imzok's Revenge]] in [[The Protector's Realm]].
1. Summon and Speak to Manifestation of Vel'Arek again.
1. Defeat a tactical genius: Slay [[Selrach Di'Zok]] in [[The Temple of Kor-Sha]] within [[Chardok]], or [[Venril Sathir]] in [[Venril Sathir's Lair]] within [[Sebilis]]. Either will update the quest.
1. Summon and Speak to Manifestation of Vel'Arek again.
1. Kill a powerful draconic general ([[Silverwing]]) in [[Veeshan's Peak]].
1. Return to the cave in Kunzar Jungle.
