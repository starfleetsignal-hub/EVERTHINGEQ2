## Steps

1. Go north to the [[The Boiling Springs]] {{waypoint -1138, 65, 554}} and do the following
   - Kill 6 [[A sulfuric viscid|sulfuric viscids]] for their cores
   - Gather 6 gripteria roots - these are thick green leafed plants, easy to spot
1. Return to Corrith

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g 80s
