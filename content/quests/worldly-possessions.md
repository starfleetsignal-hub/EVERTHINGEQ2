## Steps

1. I should locate the Wayward and see what I can do to help them.
   - I must locate [[Karm LTal]] {{waypoint -104, 7.8, -79}}
   - I must locate [[Dagmar McGlllivray]] {{waypoint -12, -0.5, 296}}
   - I must locate [[Blip Marrsquire]]{{waypoint 25, 7, -14}}
   - I must locate [[Dimble Sprockwobble]] {{waypoint 213, -4, 147}}
   - I must locate [[Ellora Whisperglen]] {{waypoint -92, 14, 126}}
1. I must collect the missing items. From what I gather, they are scattered around Ethermist Shores and Lightshadow Undergrowth.
   - I should find Karm's missing books {{waypoint 24, -3, 106}}
   - I should find Dagmar's missing love letters (on TsolEw camp pillar){{waypoint 189, -7, 185}}
   - I should find Dimble's missing wrench. Swim out from the beach a little way {{waypoint 157, -64, -234}}
   - I should find Blip's missing trophy of Valor (on beach){{waypoint 107, -51, -263}} [***Note: this trophy is difficult to spot from certain angles, regardless of settings. Refer to screenshot below***]
   - I should find Ellora's missing supply bag (on beach){{waypoint 227, -48, -162}}
1. I should return these items to their owners.
   - Return the books to Karm {{waypoint -104, 8, -80}}
   - Return the letters to Dagmar {{waypoint -11, 0, 298}}
   - Return the trophy to Blip {{waypoint 26, 7, -14}}
   - Return the wrench to Dimble {{waypoint 214, -4, 148}}
   - Return the supply bag to Ellora {{waypoint -93, 14, 126}}
1. I should return to [[Daliva Selumas]] {{waypoint -134, 14, 188}} in the Lightshadow Contingent.
   - I should return to Daliva and inform her of my success
