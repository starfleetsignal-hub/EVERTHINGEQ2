## Steps

1. Enter [[The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers]].
1. Obtain 5 vials of blood collected from dead grinnins. Roam the instance killing every pack of mist grinnin you see. Upon their death, the mist grinnin will occasionally spawn [[A dead grinnin]] on the location of the player. Click it to drain it's blood and receive a quest update.
1. Once five vials have been obtained return to [[Thraixe Il'ferrai]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- 27s 67c
- One of the following:
  - [[Charm of Night]] (Scout choice)
  - [[Scepter of Smiting]] (Priest choice)
  - [[Token of Night]] (Fighter choice)
  - [[Wand of Flames]] (Mage choice)
