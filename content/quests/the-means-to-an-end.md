## Steps

*Note: The mail system takes longer to notify you with the mail received event. Check your mail immediately anyway; you'll find the mail there before the notification.*

1. Check your in-game mail to receive your first task (this will take 5-10 minutes until you receive the message).
1. Speak to [[Cantile Digglefiz]] near the building chopping wood{{waypoint -1894, -32, -504}} in [[Windstalker Village]] in [[Antonica]]; kill her.
1. ![a Drop Barrel](images/A_Drop_Barrel.jpg)Drop off the report at the [[Keep of the Ardent Needle]] in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint -547, -13, -325}}, at a drop barrel to the right of the entrance (outside).
1. Check your in-game mail again for the next step. (Again, there will be a 5-10 minute delay until you receive the message).
1. Speak to [[Kerand D'Gamd]] hes outside the spider cave in [[Darklight Wood]]; kill him.  {{waypoint 695, -49, -139}}
1. Take the report to a drop barrel just outside of the tunnel heading to [[Nektulos Forest]] {{waypoint 711, -66, 75}}.
1. Check your in-game mail once more. (Again, there will be a 5-10 minute delay until you receive the message).
1. Speak to [[Norian Deathblow]] {{waypoint 2492, 26, 1321}} in [[The Timorous Maw]] in [[Timorous Deep]]; kill him.
1. Deposit the report in a drop barrel on the docks. {{waypoint 2392, 10, 1439}}
1. Check your in-game mail. (Again, there will be a 5-10 minute delay until you receive the message).
1. Speak to [[Kason Withershadow]] {{waypoint -375, -47, -256}} at [[The Crossroads]] in [[The Commonlands]]; kill him.
1. Talk to [[Zadkiel X'aphon]] behind you. You will be given the [[Barrel to Butcherblock]]
1. Zone into the [[Halls of the Unseen: The Takeover]]. This is a heroic instance and will zone your entire group in.
1. Fight through the zone, and kill [[Evana L'Alval]] {{waypoint 109, 2, 31}}
1. Kill [[Misa Ravensoul]] {{waypoint 175, -2, 59}}
1. Loot the chest Misa drops
1. Speak to [[Nyran Dudez]] {{waypoint 2, 2, 0}} to complete the quest

## Notes

- If you are completing this quest in your 80s, you will most likely need a group to defeat them.
- Only one person in your group needs to hail the contacts to start the encounters.
- Cantile, Kerand, Norian, and Kason all have a respawn time of 10 minutes. Actually funny that you have to make haste, but have to wait that much.
