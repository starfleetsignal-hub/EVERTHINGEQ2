## Steps

![A belchweed](images/Belchweed.jpg)

1. Harvest four belchweeds
   - These are harvest nodes found in front of the drawbridge to Deathfist Citadel.
   - Reported locations:
     - Around the tree at {{waypoint 182, -41, -588}}
     - In the passage to the moat {{waypoint 164, -40, -617}}
     - To the south of the first barricade {{waypoint -94, -33, -575}}
     - In front of the castle gate {{waypoint 48, -36, -566}}
1. Poison four slosh barrels:![An orc slosh barrel](images/Orc_Slosh_Barrel.jpg)
   - Derris {{waypoint 98, -36, -559}}
     - Found in tent around Deathfist Citadel
   - Drunder {{waypoint -67, -36, -739}}
     - Found in tent around Deathfist Citadel
   - Gorynn {{waypoint 95, -38, -835}}
     - Found in tent around Deathfist Citadel
   - Sullon {{waypoint -332, -28, -465}}
     - Found in tent southeast of [[Bridge of Zek]]
1. Return to Orca

- [[Borderland Pack]] (9 slot bag)
