## Steps

1. Collect the ingredients for a corrosive oil of vitrol all on [[Isles of Wisdom]]
   - Obtain 6 qalqadis motes from skylight spren  {{waypoint -759, 141, 32.26}}
   - Collect 12 Powdered Zootoxin dropped by Stratos fairies around the same area as the motes. (group-wide update; might be hard to find them, since they're non-aggressive, but if you tab through the enemies close by its easier.
   - NOTE: They usually drop more than one Powdered Zootoxin.
   - Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -636.68, 160.45, 192.16}}
     - {{waypoint -613.10, 157.64, 214.40}}
     - {{waypoint -862.57, 149.21, 21.64}}
     - {{waypoint -809.43, 152.60, -20.63}}
     - {{waypoint -819.82, 150.97, 47.79}}
     - {{waypoint -861.22, 148.19, 19.46}}
     - {{waypoint -798.98, 144.44, 0.40}}![Stratos Pyrite](images/Stratos_Pyrite.jpg)
   - Gather 8 Stratos Pyrite. (group-wide update) Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -721.96, 143.55, 3.26}}
     - {{waypoint -704.74, 143.44, -7.44}}
     - {{waypoint -707.09, 144.13, -21.51}}
     - {{waypoint -656.96, 143.11, 9.73}}
     - {{waypoint -694.76, 139.41, 34.77}}
     - {{waypoint -687.28, 138.53, 47.96}}
     - {{waypoint -689.08, 138.74, 63.67}}
     - {{waypoint -743.07, 140.52, 48.65}}
1. Return to [[Ecihoolo]]
