## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

*Hooatio was so impressed by my success with the last campaign that he asked me to return to Auric Palisades, to confront the maedjinn directly and dispose of more Zakir Rish weapons.*

1. Slay and destroy the following on [[Auric Palisades]]
   - Note: Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint -682.82, 144.92, -5.32}} to [[Auric Palisades]]
   - Slay 8 Maedjinn
     - Quest can vary between Maedjinn Suppressor, Maedjinn Occupiers and Maedjinn Aggressors
   - Destroy 6 Zakir Rish Weapons in the area using the Brittle Arcanum Concoction (auto-applies when you right-click the Weapons Rack).![Zakir Rish Weapons](images/Zakir_Rish_Weapons.jpg)
1. Return to [[Hooatio]] at {{waypoint -859.88, 189.67, 372.18}}
   - Note Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint -705.44, 145.19, -364.46}} to [[Isles of Wisdom]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1,000 faction with **Isles of Wisdom**
- 5 [[Zimaran Ballad]] coins
- One of the following:
  - [[Branded and Sealed Hooluk Bucket]]
  - [[Branded and Sealed Hooluk Pithos]]
