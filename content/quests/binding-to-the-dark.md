## Note

- The Quest starts when you unpack one of the required Shard items.
- If you have completed the [[Reign of Shadows Timeline]] signature quests, you can also purchase the Shards from Vashlyn, an Opportunistic vendor in [[Echo Caverns|Echo Cavern]][[Echo Caverns|s]] at {{waypoint -63.00, 177.41, -109.42}}  <nowiki/>in exchange for one of the following methods: 250 Bazaar Crowns, 100 Mark of Illumination, 250 Shadowmark, or 500 Mark of Twilight. At the top of the hour (real time) a random shard in the quest will become available for purchase.
