## Steps

1. Ariana says the deal with the orcs to not invade the nomads if she stayed in the caves as their prisoner was a lie. They are about to attack the nomad camp.
1. She asks you to kill [[Advisor Kre'lak]] {{waypoint -63, -39, -55}} to slow them down. He is the orc you kill to get access to the lower levels of the Wailing Caves.
1. You then have to warn her husband [[Orwen Neovra]] in the nomad camp {{waypoint 394, -47, -809}}, in the [[The Commonlands]].
