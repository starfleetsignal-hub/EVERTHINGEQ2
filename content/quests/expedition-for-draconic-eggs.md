## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Harvest 8 - 15 'petrified draconic eggs'. These are unique harvesting nodes found nestled amongst the tree root around [[Vicious Breeding Grounds]].
   - There is a direct flight route to there from Hidden Refuge. You will have to cope with the creatures on that island (level 62, some heroic).
   - It will take some time to find enough draconic bones. It is suggested that you work on all three draconic quests simultaneously.
   - Not a single egg "node" that works. They are here @ Vicious Breeding Gronds but not harvestable. Not sure if it's broken.
1. Return to [[Archaeologian Tydis]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- [[Ice Etched Stone]]
- First turn-in: good faction with **The Lost Children of Marr**
