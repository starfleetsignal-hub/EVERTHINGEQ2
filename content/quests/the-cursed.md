This quest is part 17 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

This is the second of several quests that take place in the [[Sanctorium of the Trials]]. This instanced zone is accessed via the doorway beside Szin. {{waypoint -350, 3, -1260}}

1. Kneel to the Sanctorium of the Trials torch (examine it) to activate the door.
1. Click the door to enter the *Chamber of the Unavailing* version of [[Sanctorium of the Trials]].
1. Slay two waves of zombies inside:
   - [[Master Gin'aste]] and [[Master H'arn]]
   - [[Mistress Val'intra]] and [[Grand Master V'thal]]
1. Exit the instance and talk to Szin.

## Rewards

- At least 36s 13c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Forest Pouch of Gloom]] ([[Beastlord]], [[Bruiser]], [[Monk]])
  - [[Gloombow]] ([[Channeler]], Plate Fighters)
  - [[Nektulos Carved Wand]] (All Priests)
  - [[Perfectly Strung Bow]] (All Scouts, Plate Fighters, [[Channeler]])
  - [[Stargazer's Focus Wand]] (All Mages)
