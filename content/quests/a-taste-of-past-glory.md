## Steps

1. Kill 200 sentient beings. They must be above level 80.
   - Mobs in [[Skyfire Mountains]]:
     - working: Droag, Drachnid, Wurms, and the [[A Dracurion keeper]] linked with [[A burning familiar]] near Wrothflame both count.
     - not working: Drakes, Gnawers and Wyvern.
   - Void mobs in [[Lavastorm]]
   - most mobs in TSO instances - fastest
1. Slay [[Pawbuster]] in the [[The Execution Throne Room]].
1. Return to [[Borga Rallok]].
1. Slay the ancient beast, [[The Leviathan]] in [[The Chamber of Destiny]].
1. Return to [[Borga Rallok]].
1. Slay the dragon, [[Silverwing]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]].
1. Return to [[Borga Rallok]]. (Must have Gorynn's Fist or Enervated Gorynn's Fist in your inventory.)

## Rewards

- [[Gorynn's Fist (Mythical)|Gorynn's Fist]]
