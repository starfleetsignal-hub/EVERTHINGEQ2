Note: if you leave the zone and need to re-enter.  Delete the quest and then click on the calibrating device to re-enter the zone.

## Materials Needed

- Materials must be collected in step 5 - note that, unlike the preceding quest, you cannot pre-possess these, the quest will not update unless you harvest them in-zone.

- 120 [[Hardened Zelniak Pelt]]
- 120 [[Humming Azurite]]
- 120 [[Luclizite Cluster]]
- 120 [[Slow Creeping Root]]
- 20 [[Coruscating Coal]]

## Steps

1. Blow the signaling horn to summon Nuniun and his troops.
1. Wait for the opportune moment to blow the horn again.
   - *Wait for the green text emote: Now's your chance, while he's distracted.*
1. You are freed, attempt to escape!
   - *Exit crystal at {{waypoint -2, 20, -254}}*
     - Note: You can fly now.
1. Speak to [[Nuniun Wolfbane (Shadeweaver's Thicket)|Nuniun Wolfbane]] {{waypoint -133, 221, 497}} back at the camp in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]].
1. Collect materials for explosives (these must be collected within [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]]): A good place to start harvesting is {{waypoint -8.34, 87.40, 236.39}} and  {{waypoint 6.49, 40.41, -193.24}}
   - 120 humming azurite
   - 120 slow creeping roots
   - 120 luclizite clusters
   - 120 hardened zelniak pelts
1. Craft 12 [[Nuniun's Special-Made Explosives]] (there's an omni-workstation at {{waypoint -121, 220, 447}}).
   - *Makes 6 per combine.*
   - As with many "high stakes" combines (such Forlorn Gist timeline, in Vetrovia, etc) be aware that the 'Progress' and 'Durability' button positions for this special combine have been swapped from your usual/saved/default UI positions for those buttons in normal crafting *(should you choose to 'twist' while attempting this 'high stakes' combine)*
   - ***Note:** Pay attention!! Failures may result not only in an unsuccessful combine, but death! There are 6 different counters. they spawn every round, and they kill you if you miss. my advice is, dont twist and just concentrate on hitting the counters.*
   - ***Note: A Progress Potion*** may seem like overkill, but the virtual instant completion with no counters relieves all death anxiety.
1. Carefully craft 12 more explosives, just in case!
1. Place the explosives on the device {{waypoint -73, 199, 508}}.
1. Have Nuniun Wolfbane trigger the explosives from a safe distance. *Follow the rope line up to where he's standing at the edge and hail him to blow the explosives. {{waypoint -93, 214, 479}}* *He may not have a quest icon over his head - just talk to him anyway.*
1. Speak with [[Nuniun Wolfbane (Shadeweaver's Thicket)|Nuniun]].
   - *You may need a wait a few seconds while he's glorying in the afterglow of blowing things up.*
1. Return to [[Hulda Flintbraid (The Bazaar)|Hulda Flintbraid]] in the [[Great Artisan Hall]] of [[The Bazaar]] {{waypoint -116.29, -1.45, 19.36}} and inform her of the happenings.
   - ***Note:** Return and speak to [[Nuniun Wolfbane (Shadeweaver's Thicket)|Nuniun Wolfbane]] {{waypoint -132, 220, 495}} back at the camp in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]], who will now be available for hire!*
