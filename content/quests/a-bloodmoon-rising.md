## Note

When you accept the quest you will get a [[Primordial Ritual Scroll]].

## Steps

1. Head to [[Castle Mistmoore]] in [[Loping Plains]] and in the lower jail area swim underwater to the middle room. On a desk {{waypoint -248, 4, -5}} you'll find a [[Laboratory Note]]. Gather the [[Laboratory Note]]
1. Examine the [[Laboratory Note]] to update the quest.
1. Next head to [[Mistmoore's Inner Sanctum]] to kill [[Mayong Mistmoore (Instanced)|Mayong Mistmoore]] and receive [[The Bloodmoon Gem]]
   - *Note: [[Mayong Mistmoore]] in [[Castle Mistmoore]] or in [[Throne of New Tunaria]] DO NOT work*
1. Head to [[The Temple of Kor-Sha]] in [[Chardok]] and kill [[Selrach Di'Zok]] and receive [[The Heart of the Slave King]]
1. Head to [[Veeshan's Peak]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]] and kill [[Phara Dar]] and receive [[The Blood of Phara'Dar]]
1. Examine the [[Primordial Ritual Scroll]] and *Speak the words* and receive [[The Self Empowered Bloodmoon Gem]]
1. Return to [[Atria X'aphon]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]] at [[City of Jinisk]] {{waypoint 647, 36, -146}} and give her the [[Sedition]] and [[The Self Empowered Bloodmoon Gem]]

## Rewards

- [[Sedition, Sword of the Bloodmoon]]
