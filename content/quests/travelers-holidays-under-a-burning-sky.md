Ultera has a very mild climate, and Mei Lan doesn't understand the concept behind Scorched Sky. I am headed to one of the hottest overland spots that I know, Lavastorm, to find suitable symbols of the event.

## Steps

1. *"I must collect items that symbolize Solusek Ro's purifying fires."* Travel to [[Lavastorm]], and collect the objects scattered around the zone
   - Gather 4 **Fennin's Pass Soot** in [[Najena's Valley]] near the [[Fennin's Pass]] at {{waypoint -443, -118, 475}}.![Fennin's Pass Soot](images/Fennin's_Pass_Soot.webp)
   - Gather 4 **Volcanic Rock**(s) near the Goblet of Ro Mount Station around {{waypoint -661.88, -67.12, -160.79}}.![Volcanic Rock](images/Volcanic_Rock.webp)
   - Gather 4 flaming **Mount Feed** very near the [[West Tube Pass|Western Pass]] Mount Station around {{waypoint 148, 0.26, -397}}.![Mount Feed](images/Mount_Feed.webp)
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]

## Reward

Unlock the Days of Summer 2021: Week 6 Holiday event rewards that you can purchase at [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2292.99, -199.41, 2505.71}}
