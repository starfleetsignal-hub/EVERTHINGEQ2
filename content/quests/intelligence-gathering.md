## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- You must have +20,000 faction with the Court of Truth to unlock this quest. Once +50,000 faction has been achieved, however, this quest will no longer be available.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Tower of the Moon]] {{waypoint -150, 176, -150}} in [[Maj'Dul]] and find the clickable book.  The book is a dark green colour - there are several locations that has a book with a similar aesthetic (most on the top floor, but one on the table in front of the bookbinder NPC). The book will always be on top of a stack. The book will be in a random location, observed locations include:
   - {{waypoint -4, 15, -1}}
   - {{waypoint -1, 27, -2}}
   - {{waypoint 5, 28, 14}} (top of the stack on top of the bookcase)
1. Speak to [[Copier Fuazi]] {{waypoint -207, 160, -72}} in Maj'Dul and tell him you need a book copied for the Court of Truth.
1. Harvest 10 pieces of [[Darjeeling tea leaf]], from Tier 6 bushes.
   - Harvest the bushes in [[The Sinking Sands]].
1. Return to Copier Fuazi and give him the Darjeeling tea leaves.
1. Return to Muyassar Zuhur.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 23s 29c
- -500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- +500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
