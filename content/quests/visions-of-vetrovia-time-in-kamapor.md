## Steps

1. Gather the following
   - Gather 10 **alabaster shells**. Small white shells located underwater with a bubble animation. Possible locations:![Alabaster Shell](images/Alabaster_shell.png)
     - {{waypoint 138, -5, 479}}
     - {{waypoint 332, -34, 502}}
     - {{waypoint 345, -35, 505}}
     - {{waypoint 346, -33, 492}}
     - {{waypoint 362, -33, 453}}
     - {{waypoint 383, -32, 455}}
     - {{waypoint 389, -30, 468}}
     - {{waypoint 406, -31, 467}}
     - {{waypoint 322, -34, 502}}
     - {{waypoint 322, -33, 445}}
   - Gather 6 **golden seaweed strands**. Possible locations:![Golden Seaweed](images/Goldenseasweed.png)
     - {{waypoint 170, -8, 364}}
     - {{waypoint 211, -24, 461}}
     - {{waypoint 161, -14, 471}}
     - {{waypoint 150, -8, 468}}
     - {{waypoint 215, -19, 498}}
     - {{waypoint 246, -28, 472}}
     - {{waypoint 264, -31, 482}}
     - {{waypoint 277, -31, 489}}
1. Return to [[Priestess Sayana]]
1. Place the offering at the Prexus altar {{waypoint 63, 11, 496}}  which is located immediately behind [[Priestess Sayana]] by clicking the base of the altar.![Click at the base of the Prexus Altar](images/Prexus_shrine.png)
1. Speak with [[Oracle Priti]] {{waypoint 54, 11, 508}}
1. Speak with [[Kasyapa]] {{waypoint 48, 11, 496}} He should now follow you.
1. Explore the [[Rolling Hills of Karda]] to find an unconscious [[Kyv]] {{waypoint -251, 67, 93}}, then kill 3 [[A sandrock lizard|sandrock lizards]] around him.
1. Speak with [[Kasyapa]]
1. Examine the unconscious [[A healing Kyv|Kyv]] at {{waypoint -251, 67, 93}}
1. Return to [[Kamapor]] and speak with [[Healer Jaanti]] {{waypoint 26, 12, 410}}, who will give you [[Sandrock Antivenin Potion]]
   - Must have completed the quest [[Head Over Heals]] before [[Healer Jaanti]] will speak to you.  You will need to harvest some items first for Healer Jaanti to make the Antimenin Potion.
   - Apply (right click) antivenin to [[A healing Kyv|Kyv]]
1. pick him up (right click).
1. Return to [[Healer Jaanti]]
1. Speak with [[Oracle Priti]] {{waypoint 54, 11, 508}}
