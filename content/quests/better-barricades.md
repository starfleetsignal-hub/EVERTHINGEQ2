## Steps

1. Speak with [[Arms Master Ryan]] {{waypoint 689, 352, 65}}
1. Click the crate at {{waypoint 638, 352, 178}} near [[Quartermaster Roberta Robbins]], where the boxes and barrel were in the previous quest. You will receive an [[Updated inventory list]] and [[Recipe for Makeshift Barricades]] will auto-scribe
1. Craft Recipe for Makeshift Barricades on [[Engraved Desk]]; [[Makeshift Barricade (Better Barricades)|Makeshift Barricade]] auto-scribes
   - Requires 2 [[Leonid root|leonid roots]], 1 [[Uncut tourmaline]] and 1 [[Effulgent incense]]
1. Craft a Makeshift Barricade on the [[Forge]] {{waypoint 623, 352, 181}}
   - Requires 1 [[Iodocus cluster]], 1 [[Larix lumber]], 1 [[Cadmium ore]] and 1 [[Effulgent coal]]
1. Speak to Arms Master Ryan
1. Speak to Quartermaster Robbins

## Rewards

- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]]
- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
