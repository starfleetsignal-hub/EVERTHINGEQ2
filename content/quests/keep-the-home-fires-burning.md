## Steps

1. Gather 6 firewood - *found in the disintegrating wooden cart, trackable harvest node*.![disintegrating wooden cart](images/Disintegrating_wooden_cart.jpg)
1. Stack the firewood in the goblin camp {{waypoint -212, 34, -1771}}.![firewood placement](images/Firewood_placement.jpg)
1. Speak with [[Rumbletum]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +3,000 faction with **Broken Toe Clan**
- +3,000 faction with **Goblins of Nurga**
