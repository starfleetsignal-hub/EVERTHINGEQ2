## Steps

The Yazdani occupying Strungstone Ascent in Zimara Breadth are amassing. What once posed little threat to the Sang'Huuu is now much more serious. The task they laid before me is simple - infiltrate the Aurai stronghold, and destroy them!

- Enter [[Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent (Solo)]]
1. Bash the Basalt Sentinel
1. Defeat the First Trial Guardian
1. Slay the Second Trial Guardian
1. Destroy the Third Trial Guardian
1. Thrash the Terror of the Four Winds
