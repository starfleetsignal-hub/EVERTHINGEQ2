## Steps

1. Deliver the parchment to Ishaq in the [[Paineel Council Chamber]] at {{waypoint 1858, -294, 3473}}
1. Return to Zafirah at {{waypoint 1659, -267, 3176}} and give her Ishaq's thanks for being of service to Paineel.
   - *Note:Go to Saihah Al'Lad {{waypoint 1872, -293, 3518}} to continue the quest line.*
