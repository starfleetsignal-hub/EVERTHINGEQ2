## Steps

1. Travel to [[Enchanted Lands]] and collect the following:
   - Collect 4 Seamist dice.
     - Located on [[The Fae Isle|Fae Isle]], near the Druid Ring on, around {{waypoint -615.36, 7.37, 57.85}} ![Seamist Dice](images/Seamist_Dice.webp)
   - Collect 4 Misty Ridge messes.
     - Located between [[The Runnyeye Ward]] and The Hole (poi in Enchanted Lands), around {{waypoint 133, -2.70, -490}}. Also near **Deputy Stoutgut** {{waypoint 171, -1.22, -484}}  ![Misty Ridge Mess](images/Misty_Ridge_Mess.webp)
   - Collect 4 Bramblefoot pies.
     - Located near the [[Bramblefoot Hills]] mount station, around {{waypoint -240.29, 25.29, -1008.15}} ![Bramblefoot Pie](images/Bramblefoot_Pie.webp)
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]]

## Reward

Unlock the Days of Summer 2021: Week 4 Holiday event rewards that you can purchase at [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2292.99, -199.41, 2505.71}}
