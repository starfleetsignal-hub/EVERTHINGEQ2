## Steps

1. Infuse the Bite of the Wolf with elemental power.
   - Kill [[Imzok's Revenge]] in [[The Protector's Realm]] to get a [[Chunk of Overflowing Elemental Strength]]. The item must be used before you leave the zone, as it is TEMPORARY & NO-ZONE, and using it will complete this step.
1. Use the magical whetstone owned by the Brood of Di'Zok on the Bite of the Wolf.
   - Kill [[Selrach Di'Zok]] in [[The Temple of Kor-Sha]] to get a [[Whetstone of Apotheosis]] Again this item is TEMPORARY & NO-ZONE and must be used before leaving the zone.
1. Seal the elemental power within the Bite of the Wolf with the secretion from a magmatic gland.
   - Kill the [[Cinder Wasp Queen]] who is part of the [[Qunard Ashenclaw]] encounter in [[Veeshan's Peak]] to get the [[Ancient Magmatic Gland]]. Again, you must use the TEMPORARY & NO-ZONE item before leaving to seal the elemental power within the blade and receive your reward.
