## Description

I must venture into the Outer Vault and kill stonehard rippers, smashers, and impalers as well as the two sentinels, The Sentinel of Stone and The Sentinel of Earth.

## Steps

- Kill stonehard rippers, smashers, impalers, and The Sentinel of Stone and The Sentinel of Earth in [[The Outer Vault]].
  - Kill 2 [[A stonehard ripper|stonehard ripper]]s
  - Kill 2 [[A stonehard smasher|stonehard smasher]]s
  - Kill 2 [[A stonehard impaler|stonehard impaler]]s
  - Kill [[The Sentinel of Stone]]
  - Kill [[The Sentinel of Earth]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 80g
- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
