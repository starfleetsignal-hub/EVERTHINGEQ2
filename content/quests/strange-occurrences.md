## Steps

1. Go to Antonica and find a graveyard {{waypoint -1839, -84, 825}}
1. Kill 5 undead that spawn. To get all five, you must move away from the graves and move back.
1. Return to Prophet Bainyn Fairwind.

## Rewards

- At least 2s
- +1250 faction with **Rodcet Nife**
- [[Humble Altar of Rodcet Nife]]
