## Steps

1. Collected the personal effects of the various ranks of Tallonites aboard The Vigilant.
   - Find [[A snowflake obsidian barb]]
   - Find [[An embroidered crimson sash]]
   - Find [[An exquisite obsidian mask]]
     - *Note: All 3 items drop from random mobs in the zone*
1. Return to [[Kamil Aziz]].

- At least 90g 70s 61c
