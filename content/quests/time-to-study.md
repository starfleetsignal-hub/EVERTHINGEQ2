## Steps

1. Use the attuner on [[A soullight]] in the Eidolon Undergrowth. *(They are mostly found near the leaves of trees near {{waypoint 106.86, -8.24, 158.73}}.  Also, they cast a self-buff called "Spirit Barrier" that mitigates approximately 95% of incoming damage.  It only lasts for six  seconds, but there is a chance that it will proc again with a successful hit.  Just pause for six seconds and the buff will expire.  Make sure the soullight is blue NOT white if you're level 95. If you're unable to attack the one you are targeting, then find another.)
1. Return to [[Hashu Whelegrind|Hashu]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
