## Steps

1. Use the bucked of meat just outside the camp. {{waypoint 464, -0.1, -182}}
1. Slay [[An ancient glacier bear]] that spawns.
1. Speak to Thala for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 65s 79c
- One of the following:
  - [[Boots of the Winterwarden]]
  - [[Tundra Runner's Mantle]]
  - [[Staff of Frozen Elements]]
  - [[Hallasian Battlepriest's Boots]]
