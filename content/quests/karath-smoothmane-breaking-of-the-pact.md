## Note

- *You can do this quest steps turn and turn about with [[Karath Smoothmane - Winter Comes]].
- *Both quest books can be bought by [[Indis Surion]], [[The Concordium Tower]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. Take the yellow spot.
- *The needed mobs are next to each other (here: the farming area southeast to the Anontican Station Griffon Tower {{waypoint -19, 0, -770}}).
- *This quest is also part of the [[Book Quests Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[An undead farmer|undead farmer]]s. {{waypoint -12, 0, -768}} After this, kill [[An undead explorer]] there. They are placeholders for farmers and settlers (step 4)
1. Kill 10 undead farmers.
1. Kill 10 undead farmers.
1. Kill 10 [[An undead settler|undead settler]]s. {{waypoint -12, 0, -768}}
1. Kill 10 undead settlers.
1. Kill 10 undead settlers.
1. Kill 10 drowned soldiers of any kind including [[A drowned trooper|drowned trooper]]s, [[A drowned footsoldier|drowned footsoldier]]s, [[A drowned captain|drowned captain]]s and [[A drowned lieutenant|drowned lieutenant]]s. All are found in [[Dead River Basin]] around {{waypoint 55, -20, -252}}.
1. Kill 10 drowned soldiers of any kind.
1. Kill 10 drowned soldiers of any kind.
