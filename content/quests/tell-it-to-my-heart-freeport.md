## Notes

Quest is repeatable annually.<br>
![Tholuxe Paells Lace](images/Tholuxe_Paells_Lace_Erollisi_Day.png)
![Pure Love Fluff](images/Pure_Love_Fluff_Erollisi_Day.png)
![Halasian Brandy](images/Halasian_Brandy_Erollisi_Day.png)
![Glittering Gold](images/Glittering_Gold_Erollisi_Day.png)
Upon accepting the quest, recipes from book *"Wooer's Picnic Setting"* are automatically scribed in your recipes book:

- [[Chocolate Brandy Truffle]] - makes 1 to 4 items depending on how many progress bars you complete
- [[Lovey Cushion]] - makes 1 to 2 items depending on how many progress bars you complete
- [[Gilded Lovers' Plate]] - makes 1 item
- [[Romantic Heart Blanket]] - makes 1 item

**Materials needed**

- **Common harvests** from any tiers needed: metals, roots, wood, leather pelts, food crops - game will pull from smallest eligible stack first in your bags and if none available then it pulls from Shared Tradeskill Bag, then from any house harvest depot or guild hall depot. You can click "change" button by each ingredient in crafting window and switch out the harvest the game chose for any others from the list. You may want to click and choose the one you have plenty of.

**Other materials** needed that are picked up from the ground in [[The City of Freeport]] - look around South Freeport and East Freeport docks, and inside buildings and Inns there.

- 1 x [[Tholuxe Paells Lace]]
- 2 to 4 x [[Pure Love Fluff]]
- 3 to 12 x [[Halasian Brandy]]
- 2 x [[Glittering Gold]]

**Fuels needed**

- 9 x [[Glowing Kindling]]
- 9 x [[Glowing Filament]]
- 6 x [[Glowing Coal]]

## Steps

*I've volunteered to craft the perfect wooer's picnic setting for Lothias*

1. Craft the following:
   - 1 x [[Romantic Heart Blanket]] on the Loom
   - 4 x [[Lovey Cushion]] on the Loom
   - 12 x [[Chocolate Brandy Truffle]] on Stove and Keg
   - 2 x [[Gilded Lovers' Plate]] on the Forge
1. Return to [[Lothias]] (in Freeport or Qeynos - both work)
