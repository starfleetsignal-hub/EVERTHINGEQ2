## Steps

1. Find 3 artistically shaped stones in the [[Metetherial Plains]]:
   - Click on ***a crescent-shaped formation*** at {{waypoint 68.32, 104.18, 17.34}}.
   - Click on ***a lagamorph-shaped formation*** at {{waypoint 153.38, 51.39, 179.15}}.
   - Click on ***a head-shaped formation*** at {{waypoint -82.92, 42.88, 369.13}}.
     - *Note: You get a scroll shaped cursor when you mouse over the stones.*
1. Return to [[Lemistka]] {{waypoint 176.30, 593.80, -528.77}} in the southern study area of [[Khali'Yrzu]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Khali'Yrzu Rubicite Chest]]
- -1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- +1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
