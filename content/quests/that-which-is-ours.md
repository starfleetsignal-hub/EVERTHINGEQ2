## Prerequisites

- Mages must be at least level 20 to be offered this quest.

## Steps

1. Enter Castleview Hamlet via the [[Castleview Hamlet: Cloister of Purity|Castleview Hamlet gate]] from [[Qeynos Harbor]]. {{waypoint 723, -21, -118}}
   - You must visit the stack of books down by the old bell inside {{waypoint 788.23, -36.31, -96.46}} before any books will update!
   - Confiscate 6 of The Concordium's missing tomes
   - Slay 6 [[A Renda'Dal activist|Renda'Dal activists]] or [[A Renda'Dal soldier|Renda'Dal soldiers]].
1. Return to [[Bellengere the Three (Qeynos Capitol District)|Bellengere the Three]].

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Concordium**
