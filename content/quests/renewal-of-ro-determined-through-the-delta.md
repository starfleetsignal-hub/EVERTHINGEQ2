Step 10 of 13

## Steps

1. Speak with the unfettered carpet keeper at [[Blinded Eye Camp]]. {{waypoint -440.21, 246.98, 184.21}}
1. Enter [[Sandstone Delta]].
1. Speak with [[Sasan]] at [[Sandstone Landing]]. {{waypoint 669.71, 51.26, 179.71}}
1. Speak with [[Briggs]] {{waypoint 594.31, 24.81, 170.43}} and then obtain the following at [[Lifire River]] {{waypoint 498.20, 4.51, 144.05}}
   - Obtain 10 **wild snapper float bladders** from the [[A wild snapper|wild snappers]] (fish) in the river.
   - Obtain 8 **dream spore mushrooms** along the river canyon walls.![Dream spore mushroom](images/Dream_spore_mushroom.png)
1. Return to [[Briggs]]. Receive [[Dream Time Dust Vial]].
   - Take the carpet ride {{waypoint 453.16, 5.94, 193.12}} up to [[Lokh Barnacoz]].
   - NOTE:  Scritches is in the Sandstone Delta, not at the Blind Eye Camp in the Badlands.
1. Attach [[Dream Time Dust Vial]] to [[Scritches]] {{waypoint 127.99, 121.12, 74.38}}
1. Follow [[Scritches]] to the **Relic Shard** {{waypoint -190.71, 9.50, 190.80}}
1. Defeat [[Gurjit the Aggressor]]. Receive [[Pirate's Brass Key]].
1. Enter [[Sandstone Delta: Eye of the Storm (Solo)|Sandstone Delta: Eye of the Storm [Solo]]] on [[Lokh Barnacoz]] at {{waypoint -47.46, 57.80, 160.50}}
   - *[[Sans Manifested Sands]] from [[Sathara]] at {{waypoint 535.14, 13.93, 222.52}} coincides with this instance.*
1. Search for the pirate's brass chest.
   1. Kill all the [[A hungry hippolisk|hungry hippolisks]] at {{waypoint 288.86, 117.08, 29.44}} to spawn [[Gallopagus]].![Buried Ruins](images/Buried_Ruins.png)
      - Before fighting [[Gallopagus]], , click on the **Buried Ruins** at {{waypoint 265.78, 113.57, -12.44}}, {{waypoint 246.75, 109.98, 40.23}}, and  {{waypoint 296.62, 117.53, 19.83}} to cause a pillar to rise from the ground.
      - When [[Gallopagus]] charges, hide behind a pillar to knock him out and clear the traumas.
   1. Click **a barrel of fresh fish** at {{waypoint 281, 114, 111}} to get the detriment *Lokh Mess*, a buff that protects you against certain attacks.![A barrel of fresh fish](images/A_barrel_of_fresh_fish.png)
   1. Kill [[Fushuga]] and [[Hargan]] {{waypoint 186.61, 91.70, 221.87}}
   1. Open chest at {{waypoint 169.39, 82.81, 405.49}} to release and kill [[Stormy Withers]].
      - Before attacking Stormy Withers, go to a nearby overlook and "kick" the thrashing thresher off the platform and kill the shark bait.
      - You will also notice a fresh fish rack nearby in case your Lokh Mess buff wears off, you can run over to "eat" more fish.
      - Watch for the "Gangway!" emote. Quickly run into and back out of the overlook to cure the detriment. If you stay too long you will see another emote, "Spurned...." that will buffet you around, possibly OFF into the depths. It will also remove your Lokh Mess, so you'll have to renew. This mob benefited from using ascensions and big hits, but the first five were still absorbed, so use them later.
   1. Click the dead Thrasher nearby to "Shark the Jump" to the next area.
   1. Kill [[Pri'ki'ori'ki]] and [[Wa'la'kra'na]] {{waypoint 67.05, 80.08, 337.59}}
      - Pull [[Wa'la'kra'na]] to the edge of the bubble (out of [[Pri'ki'ori'ki]]'s AOE range). Once he's dead, kill the remaining yha'lei from range (may be able to melee him up close if you are dps/tank).
   1. Find and open the brass chest at {{waypoint 55.98, 80.13, 325.61}}.
      - Receive [[The Rainkeeper's Storm Shield (Quest Item)|The Rainkeeper's Storm Shield]].
   1. Kill [[Agmork the Almighty Angler]] to access the zone exit.
      - Hide in one of the tents to avoid being knocked off the island by his summoned waves.
   1. Zone out to [[Sandstone Delta]].
1. go to the [[Sandscoured Coast]] {{waypoint -325.23, 17.04, -232.40}}
1. Speak to [[Selaera Taenaeris]] {{waypoint -19.82, 18.75, -169.85}} in [[Sandstone Delta]]
