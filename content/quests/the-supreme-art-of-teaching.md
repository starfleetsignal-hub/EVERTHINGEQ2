## Steps

![Yellowish Crystal](images/The_Supreme_Art_of_Teaching_crystal.jpg)

1. Collect 8 starfire crystals by clicking on yellowish crystals on the bluffs and plateaus.
   - Sometimes a [[Starfire defender]] will spawn, other times you'll get an update. If you fail, the crystal can be clicked after 5 minutes.
   - Some possible locations
     1. {{waypoint -437.71, 79.92, -906.11}}
     1. {{waypoint -498.56, 134.72, -842.75}}
     1. {{waypoint -448.19, 135.72, -596.44}}
     1. {{waypoint -519.47, 134.12, -608.31}}
     1. {{waypoint -542.96, 134.38, -796.68}}
     1. {{waypoint -742.91, 170.31, -683.71}}
     1. {{waypoint -743.27, 81.88, -808.62}}
     1. {{waypoint -777.08, 104.50, -591.72}}
     1. {{waypoint -807.73, 83.80, -811.38}}

![Starfire Defender appears ready to attack!](images/Starfire_defender.png)

1. Talk to [[Ta-hetu]] at {{waypoint -467, 162, -773}}
1. Click the Crystal Grinder at {{waypoint -437, 165, -809}}. ![Crystal Grinder](images/Crystal_Grinder.jpg)
1. Talk to [[Grodney]] at {{waypoint -475, 164, -845}}
1. Click on the Starfire Dust Storage at {{waypoint -441, 165, -806}}. ![Starfire Dust Storage](images/Starfire_Dust_Storage.jpg)
