## Prerequisites

- You must be at least level 20 and of Good-alignment.

## Steps

![Blessed Earth](images/Blessed_Earth.jpg)

1. Go to [[Vhalen's Tower]] in [[Antonica]] and place the [[Blessed Earth]] in the urn next to the tower. {{waypoint -1300.27, 37.92, 55.37}}
1. Go to [[Arbos, the Elddar Tree]] in [[Qeynos Province District]] and plant the earth by clicking the bush and following the dialogue. {{waypoint 663, -9, -376}}
1. Take the [[A sapling (Item)|sapling]] you received and place it in your home. Interact with the sapling and select the options in the following order:
   1. Give it water;
   1. Change the soil;
   1. Sing to it.
1. Pick up the sapling and take it back to [[Greater Faydark]] and plant it by [[Tunare's Sapling]]. The giant log serves as the host for the sapling {{waypoint 24, 5, 118}}
1. Return to [[Kurista (Greater Faydark)|Kurista]].

## Rewards

- [[Humble Altar of Tunare]]
- +1250 faction with **Tunare**
- [[A sapling (Item)|the sapling]] for your house
