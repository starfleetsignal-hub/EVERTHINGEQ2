## Steps

1. Use the [[Icemane cub leash]] on 4 [[An icemane cub|icemane cubs]] {{waypoint 410, 39, -395}}
1. Click on the icemane post {{waypoint 56, 27, -363}}
1. Return to [[Verien Skysigh]]

- At least 76c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Bodyguard's Tunic]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Evoker's Robe]] (All Mages)
  - [[Marshall's Breastplate (gold)|Marshall's Breastplate]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Pathfinder's Tunic]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Spiritualist's Tunic]] (Shamans)
  - [[Vicar's Breastplate]] (Clerics)
  - [[Windcaller's Tunic]] (Druids)
