## Notes

- This quest is repeatable. this is the repeatable version of the previous quest.
- To get to [[Nightscar Borne Lands]] area use the [[Longhowl]] next to [[Lynwyn]] at {{waypoint -282, 25, -5}}.
- To get back to [[Nightscar Steppes]] use [[Longhowl]] at {{waypoint -806, 12, -715}}.

## Steps

![Icon of The Consumer](images/Icon_of_The_Consumer.png)
![Altar to the Consumer](images/Altar_to_the_Consumer.png)

1. You will be asked to do 2 tasks from list below: 1 kill and 1 destroy task:
   - kill 8 ebonlithe lamias of the Nightscar Borne Lands
   - kill 8 obliviwrought nightbloods of the Nightscar Borne Lands
   - destroy 6 icons of the Consumer - they respawn, possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -645, 14, -849}}, {{waypoint -588, 26, -862}}, {{waypoint -602, 23, -831}}, {{waypoint -590, 13, -923}}, {{waypoint -822, 31, -1269}}, {{waypoint -824, 31, -1286}}
   - destroy 6 altars to the Consumer - they respawn, possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -871, 24, -852}}, {{waypoint -660, 11, -942}}, {{waypoint -593, 23, -831}}. {{waypoint -583, 13, -924}}, {{waypoint -857, 29, -1272}}, {{waypoint -1308, 51, -1133}}
1. Return to [[Lynwyn Singlespring]] at {{waypoint -293, 25, -11}}

NOTE: If you are trying to complete the [[By Anonymous]] achievement, you must have this repeatable quest active in your quest journal to mark the Unbreakable Icon.

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following:
  - 2 x [[Small Orange Void Dewdrop Plant]]
  - 2 x [[Orange Void Dewdrop Plant]]
  - 2 x [[Tall Orange Void Dewdrop Plant]]
