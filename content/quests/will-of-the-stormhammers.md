## Steps

1. Speak with [[Brelaine]] - she's outside the gate to The Willow Wood {{waypoint 809, -21, -551}}
1. Enter [[The Willow Wood: Besieged Hamlet]] and...
   - Kill 6 gnolls
   - Collapse 3 gnoll tunnels
1. Return to Brelain
1. Enter [[Graystone Yard: Herald of Rage]] and kill 8 brawlers ([[A Graystone barbarian|Graystone barbarians]] and [[A Graystone dwarf|dwarves]] update)
1. Approach the Stone of Kaladim
1. Watch Brelaine as she breaks the dwemer
1. Head to Brelaine's apartment in [[South Qeynos (POI)|South Qeynos]] - it's the door at {{waypoint 514, -17, 221}}
1. Enter [[Brelaine's Home]] and speak with Brelaine.
1. Watch the meeting
1. Return to Mav Boilfist

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g @ lvl 95
- [[The Stone of Kaladim]]
- [[Ring of the Dwarf]]
