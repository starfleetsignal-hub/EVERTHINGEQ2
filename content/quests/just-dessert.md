## Steps

1. Harvest from kunzar berrybushes (buying does not update):
   - 3 raw cranberries
   - 3 Cabilis cocoa beans
1. Kill 5 mistgoblins ([[A mistgoblin scout|scouts]], [[A mistgoblin shaman|shamans]] and [[A mistgoblin warrior|warriors]] all update). {{waypoint -199, 128, -721}}
1. Return to Ireka Nazan.

## Rewards

- 1p 10g
- +2,000 faction with **Kunzar Jungle Villagers**
