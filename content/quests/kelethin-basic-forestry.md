This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Gibrien gives you a [[Ringonometer]] and tells you to measure a single tree (random which you get, approach tree for auto-update):
   - tree at Emerald Lake {{waypoint -839, 61, 263}}
   - tree in Fae newbie area near the shore {{waypoint -204, -58, -739}}
   - tree on south side of Lower Emerald River near ancient combine spires {{waypoint -290, 7, 204}}
1. Return to Gibrien.

## Rewards

- -3000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- +3000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
- -3000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- Experience
