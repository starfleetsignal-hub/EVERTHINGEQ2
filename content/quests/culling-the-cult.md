## Description

In the depths of the dungeon known as Befallen, a new threat has emerged. A dark offshoot of Solusek Ro followers have formed a necromantic cult known as the Burning Dead and are attempting to harness the dark energies generated there from the curse of Gynok Moltor. To combat this threat I've agreed to slay a number of the necrotic cult members in an attempt to stop their heinous acts.

## Steps

Enter the [[Befallen: Necrotic Asylum]] and slay a number (30) of necrotic cult members

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- [[Void Shard]]
