## Steps

1. Kill the following monsters:
   - Kill 5 [[A moldering soldier|moldering soldiers]] {{waypoint -309, -51, -3}}
   - Kill 8 [[A purulent defender|purulent defenders]]
   - *Note*: These place-hold for each other so you may end up killing more than you need of one or the other.
1. Return to [[Laexyra Y'Barriath]].

## Rewards

- At least 92c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Dark Bodyguard's Shoulders]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Evoker's Shoulderpads (dark)|Evoker's Shoulderpads]] (All Mages)
  - [[Dark Marshall's Pauldrons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Dark Pathfinder's Shoulderpads]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Dark Spiritualist's Shoulderpads]] (Shamans)
  - [[Dark Vicar's Pauldrons]] (Clerics)
  - [[Dark Windcaller's Shoulderpads]] (Druids, Channelers)
