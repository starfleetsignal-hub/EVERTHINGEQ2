*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment*

## Steps

1. Visit the following [[The Feerrott|Feerrott]] locations in any order. At each location, you will receive the message that your [[Mapping beacon]] begins to glow. Inspect the beacon to get the quest update.
   - [[Murdunk's Fall]] {{waypoint -282, 4, 92}}
   - [[Restored Druid Ring]] {{waypoint -567, -14, 843}}
   - [[Tower of Vul]] {{waypoint -945, 3, 465}}
   - [[Sentinel Stones]] {{waypoint -710, 2, 543}}
   - [[Golgoth Bridge]] {{waypoint -317, -6, 506}}
   - [[Ogre Guard Tower]] {{waypoint -599, 6, 308}}
1. Return to [[Tarin Varinblade]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 53s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Apostle's Plate Gloves of the Divinehand]] ([[Inquisitor]], [[Templar]])
  - [[Channeler's Gloves of the Runemaster]] (All Mages)
  - [[Hanshi's Hand Wraps of the Whirlingfist]] ([[Bruiser]], [[Monk]])
  - [[Knight's Gauntlets of the Warleader]] (Plate Fighters)
  - [[Oracle's Handguards of the Fatebringer]] ([[Defiler]], [[Mystic]])
  - [[Protector's Leather Gloves of the Tempestwalker]] ([[Fury]], [[Warden]])
  - [[Raider's Chain Gloves of the Heartstriker]] (All Scouts)
