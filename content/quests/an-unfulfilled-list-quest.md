## Steps

![](images/Lost_relic_object.jpg)

1. Search around for any sign of possible relics that belonged to the spirits in a former life.
   - Gather 8 of the [[Lost relic|lost relics]] on the ground of [[The Ruins of Old Paineel]].
   - Can be tracked with Track Harvestables
   - 7 locations of the 8 lost relics:
     - {{waypoint 1606, -103.99, 3178.43}}
     - {{waypoint 1599.45, -103.48, 3164.53}}
     - {{waypoint 1636.14, -106.41, 3120.79}}
     - {{waypoint 1639.91, -107.63, 3160.52}}
     - {{waypoint 1672.96, -109.33, 3048.21}}
     - {{waypoint 1639.02, -110.40, 2985.44}}
     - {{waypoint 1654.78, -108.61, 2997.86}}
     - *(They re-spawn after a few minutes and are able to be recollected)*
1. Find [[A manifested Paineel spirit]] {{waypoint 1607.40, -101.21, 2930.11}} to give the relics to in [[The Ruins of Old Paineel|Ruins of Old Paineel]].

## Rewards

- At least 46g
