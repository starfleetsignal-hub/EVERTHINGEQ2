## Steps

1. Slay the following creatures. Any type will do. You can find level 11-13 versions of these creatures in the fields in front of [[Frontier Farm]].
   - 5 Wolves
   - 5 Bears
   - 5 Birds *(some [[A sparrow-hawk|sparrow-hawks]] can be found around [[Crater Pond]])*
1. Return to [[Matsy Rollingpin|Matsy]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Meat pie surprise]]
- At least 1s 51c
