## Steps

1. Hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 6 ulteran spikewolves around the fort areas for slobber {{waypoint 352, 251, -614}}
   - Gather 8 thornroot juice![Thornroot](images/Thornroot.png)
     - Thornroots can be found all over the lower portion of the fortress, around {{waypoint 63, 191, -476}}. They look like harvest nodes, but can not be tracked or targeted.
1. Search for goblin goo mix pit at {{waypoint -59.93, 188.51, -640.05}}
1. Defeat the 2 aridwing vultures that spawn as you get close.
1. Gather mix pit sand in mounds around the pit.
1. Examine the Scattered Bones.
1. Return to Skortch.

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
