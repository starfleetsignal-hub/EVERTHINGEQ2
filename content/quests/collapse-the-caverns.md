## Steps

1. Enter the [[Kromise Caves]] and use the [[Explo Powder SGL]] at {{waypoint -95.49, -23.79, -1097.39}}. You have to be standing in exactly the right spot.
   - Four monsters will spawn when the item is used: [[A giant berserker]], [[A giant brigand]], [[An icegill angler]], and [[An icegill lurker]]. They do not need to be killed for the quest.
1. Return to Areth.
