## Notes

You need to speak [[Gnomish]].

## Steps

1. [[Professor What]] is sending you on a mission, and every gnomish mission needs explosions. Pick up a **Boom Burble** (beetle), found wandering throughout [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]].
   - * Be quick, Boom Burbles will explode if you leave them too long, with a knockback and some damage!*
   - * The Boom Burble transforms into the safe(er) [[Pocketed Boom Burble (Steamfont Mountains)|Pocketed Boom Burble]] once collected.*
   - * You can collect extra Boom Burbles during your mission, which can be sent to kill mobs.*
   1. Click **Whatzzit's Hover Clank** {{waypoint -585, 174, 952}} to fly you to the exhaust port above the [[Klak'Anon]] entrance {{waypoint -297, 142, 1357}}.
   1. Click on the [[Pocketed Boom Burble (Steamfont Mountains)|Pocketed Boom Burble]] in your bag, sending him to explode the port. The explosion results in a pile of debris that you click to enter [[Klak'Anon: Ulterior Mechanica]].
      - * The [[Pocketed Boom Burble (Steamfont Mountains)|Pocketed Boom Burble]] will take several seconds to explode the exhaust port, be patient.*
      - Entrance will become active once ALL group members have released their [[Pocketed Boom Burble (Steamfont Mountains)|Pocketed Boom Burble]]; then is a group-wide zone-in.
   1. Enter [[Klak'Anon: Ulterior Mechanica]] by clicking on the heap of rubble left after the [[Pocketed Boom Burble (Steamfont Mountains)|Pocketed Boom Burble]] explodes the port.
1. Find a way to destroy the Ulterior Mechanica
   1. Follow ramps up, slaying clockworks as you find them, to reach the **Clockwork Gate Lever** {{waypoint -33, 14, 113}}. Activating the lever opens a gate at {{waypoint -2, 14, 118}}.
      - *Note: The fire grate does % based damage, avoid it.*
   1. Next, head downhill and continue clearing clockworks.
      - *Note: pick up Boom Burbles while clearing, 5 are needed at the end. Get more if you use them against mobs. They DO respawn, so backtrack if you run out.*
   1. Return to the gate and head uphill, once again clearing clockworks until ***"You hear the grinding of gears as massive doors are lifted!"***, indicating that the giant doors are open.  All clockworks must be killed for this to happen.  Check behind machinery.
   1. Head to {{waypoint -92, 42, 47}} and click through three sets of gates to get to the [[Central Contrapticus]] {{waypoint 330, 0, 0}}. Here you will face an encouner of heroic *(3 v)* clockworks announced by the message ***"A threat is detected!"***.
      - *Note: Destroy them, if needed using [[Pocketed Boom Burble (Ulterior Mechanica)|Pocketed Boom Burbles]]. There are three sparkling piles scattered around the room (possibly even behind the [[Central Contrapticus]]) that can be clicked to obtain more Boom Burbles*
   1. Approach the charged field and send [[Pocketed Boom Burble (Ulterior Mechanica)|Pocketed Boom Burbles]] to destroy all of the 5 components. Each time you destroy a component it spawns more clockworks for you to defeat.
1. Collect the destroyed component pieces.
1. DO NOT LEAVE ZONE UNLESS FINISHED. YOU WILL HAVE TO START ALL OVER AGAIN. oH jOY![Gather Burnt Gnomish Object](images/Gather_Burnt_Gnomish_Object.jpg)
   - group-wide update on pickup.
1. Exit the [[Klak'Anon: Ulterior Mechanica|Ulterior Mechanica]] instance and click **Whatzzit's Hover Clank** to return to [[Professor What]] in [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters|Gnomeland Security]].
