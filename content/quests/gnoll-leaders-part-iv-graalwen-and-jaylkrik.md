*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

All the following mobs are located on a steep hill around {{waypoint -1654, 7, -72}}:

1. Kill 4 [[A Sabertooth camp guard|Sabertooth camp guards]]
1. Kill [[Graalwen]]
1. Kill 4 [[A Sabertooth spear guard|Sabertooth spear guards]]
1. Kill [[Jayl'Krik]]
1. Return to [[Bulvar Rakgam]]

## Rewards

- At least 14s 17c
- +110 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
- One of the following:
  - [[Black Steel Choker]]
  - [[Heartland Signet]]
  - [[Lucanic Healer's Mantle]]
  - [[Verdant Antonican Chain]]
- You are now also able to finish the quest [[A Gnollish Trinket]]
