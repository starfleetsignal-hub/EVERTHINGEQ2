## Prerequisites

- You must have at least +3200 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Collect 8 *skyfire wurm skins* from [[A skyfire wurm|skyfire wurms]] located in [[Skyfire Mountains]].
1. Deposit the items in the crate. {{waypoint 1020.88, -39.28, -16.77}}
1. Speak with [[Pylemea Stoerki]].

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- +2400 faction with **The Order of Rime**
