## Steps

1. [[Lieutenant D'Raka]] will randomly offer you one of the following tasks:
   - Gather Rujarkian Alliance Notes in [[The Clefts of Rujark]] - Find 10 Rin'Dal tablets in [[Cavern of Training]] {{waypoint 115, 20, -155}}.
   - Slay Steelslave maulers in [[The Clefts of Rujark]].
   - Gather Rin'Dal history scrolls in [[The Living Tombs]].
   - Slay commerce sentinels in [[The Living Tombs]].
1. Return to [[Lieutenant D'Raka]] in the [[South Freeport]]

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Seafury Buccaneers**
- 7,476 status (@L62)
