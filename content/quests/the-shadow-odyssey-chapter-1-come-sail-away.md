## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Skystrider Launch-Dock]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] and speak with [[Professor Fondfate (Sinking Sands)|Professor Fondfate]]. {{waypoint -1355, -167, -752}}
   - During the conversation, Professor Fondfate gives you [[The Collected Tales of the Ethernauts]].
1. Travel to the [[Dropship Landing Zone]] in [[Moors of Ykesha]] and speak with [[Professor Fondfate (Moors of Ykesha)|Professor Fondfate]] when you arrive. {{waypoint 1740, 451, 846}}
   - You can take [[The Skystrider]], the flying ship that docks in the Sinking Sands, or you can simply use a [[Mariner's Bell]].
1. Seek out the missing clockwork, [[Z-bot XGO]], in the lizardman camp outside [[U.J.L. 1.0]]. {{waypoint 1149.5, 392.5, 860.5}}
   - **Good-Alignment**:
     - Hail [[Shaman Zaz'i'zaz]], who will give you the next quest.
   - **Evil-Alignment**:
     1. Kill one of the groups of Thalz'Iz'Zaz Lizardmen at the camp.
     1. Speak with [[Shaman Zaz'i'zaz]], who is upset with you for approaching the clockwork. This will finish the quest and open the next quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g 9500 status
