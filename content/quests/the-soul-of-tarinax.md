*This quest is also part of the [[Feerrott Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Enter [[The Obelisk of Lost Souls]].
1. Examine an urn in [[Troxlep]]'s room, upstairs at {{waypoint -22, 618, 332}}.
1. Kill the following:
   - [[A shadowed rift watcher (Obelisk of Lost Souls)|a shadowed rift watcher]] (a shadowman associated with an eye) - in the cavern near [[Soul Harvester Zej]] {{waypoint 122, 629, 381}}.
   - [[A shadowed evocator]] (a shadowman associated with an open mouth) - in the cavern near [[Soul Harvester Zej]].
   - [[A shadowed soulflayer]] (a shadowman associated with a stripped soul)
1. Return to the urn, and examine it.
1. Kill [[A shadowed acolyte]] (with an acolyte symbol). They spawn inside the Obelisk in a room with the book of Summoning {{waypoint -31, 590, 355}} or in a room near [[The Harbinger]] {{waypoint 27, 594, 334}}.
1. Return to the urn.
1. Return to General Fandrak in the Feerrott.

## Rewards

- 1g 59s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Accurate Bow of Lifetaking]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Cleric's Staff]] (All Priests)
  - [[Enchanted Longbow of the Mists]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Mist-Imbued Satchel]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Riddler's Staff of Tricks]] (All Mages)
