## Description

"Brodo Gillywump, a halfling at Green Knoll found that the local deer are becoming sick due to eating diseased pebbleberry bushes within Treant Grove. He would like me to destroy the cause of this sickness to stop it from spreading." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Destroy 3 diseased pebbleberry bushes {{waypoint 701, 8, -50}} (either side of the [[Butcherblock Canyon]])
1. Destroy one more bush
1. Kill 4 [[Crushbone corruptor|Crushbone corruptors]] {{waypoint 491.6, 5.3, -219.2}}
1. Return to Brodo

## Rewards

- At least 76c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Brawler's Bandoleer (Version 2)|Brawler's Bandoleer]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Hunter's Shortbow (Version 2)|Hunter's Shortbow]]  (All Scouts)

  - [[Thug's Bandoleer (Version 2)|Thug's Bandoleer]]  (All Scouts)
