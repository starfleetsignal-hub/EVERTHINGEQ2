## Steps

1. Purchase a recipe book from [[Khepri]] and craft any of the Enlivened weapon recipes found within.
   - [[Recipe Scroll: Enlivened Weapons I]]
   - [[Recipe Scroll: Enlivened Weapons II]]
   - [[Recipe Scroll: Enlivened Weapons III]]
   - Each recipe book is unlimited use and costs 200 [[Mira Miracula]].
   - Each recipe requires a [[Merely Sated Weapons|Merely Sated weapon]] as the primary component. Each Merely Sated weapon has three corresponding recipes, each with a different stat breakdown.
   - These recipes cannot be commissioned.
   - Each recipe requires a Renewal of Ro rare material plus [[Enlivened Growth]], a rare harvest from any harvesting node that can only be found if you are on this step of the quest.
1. Take the newly-crafted weapon back to [[Khepri]].
