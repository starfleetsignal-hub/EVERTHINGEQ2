## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.. However [[Trial of Qin Lo: Solo (Instance)]] has a minimum persistence of 2 days 20 hours, so no new terracotta warriors will spawn should you zone into the instance again after completing this quest.
- This quest scales with player level up to 70.
- You can receive additional [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]] as a rare body drop from the terracotta warriors. These can be turned into [[Brother Huang Mantian]] for titles.
- Mercenaries cannot be summoned to assist you in the solo version of this quest.

## Steps

1. Defeat 50 [[A terracotta warrior|terracotta warriors]] within 30 minutes.
   - Using health, power, and speed totems will help you keep a fast pace. There is more than enough time to complete the trial if you kill quickly and move fast, but losing track of an uncleared room or passage could cause you to spend five minutes or more hunting the last one or two terracotta warriors.

## Rewards

- 5 [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]]
