## Steps

1. Obtain 5 [[Tin Long Sword|Forged Tin Long Swords]] (Pristine grade swords do not work).
   - You can now craft these as you normally would and don't have to stop at the third row.
   - You may craft them yourself (level 1 item in [[Artisan essentials volume 1]]) or purchase off a broker.
1. Return to Brianna who then sends you back to Jacques.

## Rewards

- [[Brianna's Broken Crate]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Earring of Enlightened Paths]] (Conjurers, probably all Mages)
  - [[Earhoop of Enlightened Paths]]
  - [[Facet of Enlightened Paths]]
  - [[Hoop of Enlightened Paths]]
