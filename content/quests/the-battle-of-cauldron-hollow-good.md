## Notes

- This quest is for good-aligned characters only.
- Evil-aligned players should look at [[The Battle of Cauldron Hollow (Evil)]] instead.

## Steps

1. Upon zoning into the instance, [[Battlefield of Cauldron Hollow]], you receive a full set of Ivy Etched armor and appear as a Greenhood, Kithicor Ranger [[Gandari]], with a bow, arrows, and 8 spells:
   - **Armor & Weapons**
     - [[Ivy Etched Tunic (TBoCH Good)|Ivy Etched Tunic]]
     - [[Ivy Etched Gauntlets (TBoCH Good)|Ivy Etched Gauntlets]]
     - [[Ivy Etched Hood (TBoCH Good)|Ivy Etched Hood]]
     - [[Ivy Etched Sleeves (TBoCH Good)|Ivy Etched Sleeves]]
     - [[Ivy Etched Bracer (TBoCH Good)|Ivy Etched Bracer]]
     - [[Ivy Etched Leggings (TBoCH Good)|Ivy Etched Leggings]]
     - [[Jaelan's Katana (TBoCH Good)|Jaelan's Katana]]
     - [[Blood Orchid Katana (TBoCH Good)|Blood Orchid Katana]]
     - [[Stonewood Compound Bow (TBoCH Good)|Stonewood Compound Bow]]
     - [[Arrow (TBoCH Good)|arrow x 100]]
   - **Spells (these are NOT the normal spells!)**
     - **[[Immolate (TBoCH Good)|Immolate]]** - Cold DD
     - **[[Healing (TBoCH Good)|Healing]]** - Heal self ~3000 dmg, fast recast
     - **[[Careless Lightning (TBoCH Good)|Careless Lightning]]** - Heat DD ~13000 dmg, ~10 sec recast
     - **[[Trueshot (TBoCH Good)|Trueshot]]** - Increases DPS by 32%, Ranged Crit chance by 43%, Slashing and Piercing by 32, and Ranged by 64
     - **[[Call of Flame (TBoCH Good)|Call of Flame]]** - 1133-1888 heat dmg if bow equipped
     - **[[Ensnare (TBoCH Good)|Ensnare]]** - Snare for 24 sec, slow target 44%
     - **[[Swarm of Pain (TBoCH Good)|Swarm of Pain]]** - Divine DoT: 1121-1370 dmg instantly, 934-1141 dmg every 6 seconds, lasts 24 seconds
     - **[[Barbcoat (TBoCH Good)|Barbcoat]]** - 187-228 dmg to attacker when hit
1. Put on your armor, arm yourself, and cast your [[Trueshot (TBoCH Good)|Trueshot]] and [[Barbcoat (TBoCH Good)|Barbcoat]].
1. Talk to [[Leaf Falldim]].
1. Hail [[Pathfinder Rolan]] and send the elite greenhood troops into battle.
1. Find and kill [[Prince Talvus Thex]].
1. Once [[Prince Talvus Thex]] is dead, red con mobs will spawn a slaughter you and your troops.
1. When you die, you return to [[Antonica]] and [[Eva Corunno'thes (Antonica)]] rewards you. You do not get to try again.

## Rewards

- at least 1g 77s 51c
- [[Bowl of Growth]]
