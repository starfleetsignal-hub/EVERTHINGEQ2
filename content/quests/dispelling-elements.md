## Steps

1. To make a dispelling ward capable of freeing the captured Kejek sojourners, [[Yaqub]] needs you to gather some spell components.
   - Collect 10 void residues from [[A tainted scintilla|tainted scintillas]], pink flying orbs. {{waypoint 67, 246, -7}} and {{waypoint -121.01, 206.82, -301.53}}
   - Collect 10 petals from [[A carnivorous netherbloom|carnivorous netherblooms]].
     - Both mobs can be found in and around [[Deepwater Pavilion]].
1. Take the items back to Yaqub, who then creates the [[Dispelling Ward]]. ( Use your Quel'ule transport stone. )
1. Return to [[Pantheera]] with the ward. {{waypoint -878, 483, 371}}
