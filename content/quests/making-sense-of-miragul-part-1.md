## Steps

1. Seek information using the received item *Fragment of the Eternal Prism* regarding Miragul's findings within [[Miragul's Phylactery: Scion of Ice]]
   - Seek out Miragul's amassed library of magical tomes. Its a table at {{waypoint -217, 15, 117}} upstairs that requires a key (dropped from [[Arcanus]]).
   - Seek out facets of Miragul's magical experiments. These are clickable items found in the lower part of the zone. You need 5 items to complete the quest. Items include vials, books on the tables, bottles and chains on the wall.
     - *Scroll **and** Bottle at {{waypoint -212, -10, 95}}.*
     - *Large bong at {{waypoint -200, -10, 117}}.*
     - *Books at {{waypoint -217, -10, 108}}.*
     - *Hanging chains at {{waypoint -214, -10, 122}}*.
     - *Scroll at {{waypoint -212.76, -8.38, 98.38}}*.
1. Return to [[Lucretia Nasin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 31g 73s 78c
