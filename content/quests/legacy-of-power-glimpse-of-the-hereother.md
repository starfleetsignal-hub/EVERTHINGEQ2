## Steps

1. Speak to [[Druzzil Ro]].
1. Find [[Enchantress W.]] in [[Plane of Magic]] near the standing stones in [[The Stratafold]] at {{waypoint 598, 37, 544}}.![Enchantress W.](images/Enchantress_W..png)
1. Collect the items needed:
   - Kill a [[Planar earth elemental]] ![planar earth elemental](images/Planar_earth_elemental.jpg)*(Often located on small floating islands, but also on rocks in the water of the Auric Tide)* to obtain **"Mana Infused Lead"**. (Not a guaranteed drop, may take several tries!) Some possible locations (clockwise from the  [[Amphitheater of Song]]):
     - {{waypoint 973, 19, 1006}}  -- *Mages Pebble*
     - *{{waypoint -575.66, 168.50, 72.21}} -- Floating island west of Drukyna Marsh*
     - {{waypoint -1278, 356, -848}} -- *Floating island above the ocean rim northeast of [[Pakiat Bluffs]].*
     - {{waypoint -1174, 214, -672}} -- *Floating island east of Pakiat Bluffs*
     - {{waypoint 838.51, 14.01, 726.92}} {{waypoint 838.51, 14.01, 726.92}} -- A rock in the water off the Stratafold.
     - {{waypoint -890, 297, 990}} -- *Near [[Valor's Roost]]*
   - Collect 6 [[Song-Polished Sand]] near {{waypoint 306, -153, 930}} in the [[Amphitheater of Song]].
     - *These look like small mounds of dirt emitting musical notes.*![Song-Polished_Sand](images/Song-Polished_Sand.jpg)
   - Collect a [[Pendant of the Elder Scrykin]] from open full chests in the [[Pakiat Bluffs]]. Some possible locations:![Pendant of the Elder Skrykin](images/Pendant_of_the_Elder_Skrykin.jpg)
     - {{waypoint -681, 124, -457}}
     - {{waypoint -514, 140, -666}}
     - {{waypoint -473, 162, -769}}
     - {{waypoint -588, 132, -819}}
     - {{waypoint -786, 132, -422}}
     - *The pendant is a small glowing shiny on top of the chest or on the ground next to books or pillows.*
1. Speak to [[Enchantress W.]] at {{waypoint 598, 37, 544}}.
1. Kill 12 [[Arcstone miner golem]].
   - Appear around Enchantress W after you finish the dialog.
1. Speak to [[Enchantress W.]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 41g 75s 48,000 status
- [[Paradoxulars]]
