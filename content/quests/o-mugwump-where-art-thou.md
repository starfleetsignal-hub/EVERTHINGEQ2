## Steps

- Speak to [[Mugwump]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] {{waypoint -279, 0, 166}}. He is directly across the first bridge after you zone in.
  - Mugwump will also introduce you to the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]] series of quests. You can start those quests when you are close to level 20.
- Return to Mooshga for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 11s 13c
