## Steps

1. Speak to [[Natasha]], the mystic who made contact with the strange spirit {{waypoint 1191, 42, 2244}}
1. Scribe the [[Focus of the Spirits]] recipe to then craft a [[Kerra Spirit Totem]]. Requires 3 [[Sumac lumber]], 2 [[Quicksilver cluster]] and 1 [[Ethereal sandpaper]] which can be crafted on a [[Portable Woodworking Kit]] to save travel time.
1. Return to Natasha ![Speak to the spirit that appears ...](images/Outfitting_the_Kerra_A_Strange_Spirit_quest_-_spirit.jpg)
1. Interact with the small totem, which Natasha places on her right ... and then speak to the spirit that manifests

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +1,250 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +500 faction with **Kerra Isle Outfitters**
- Achievement [[Kerran Krafter]]
