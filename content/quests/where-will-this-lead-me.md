- *This quest is also part of [[The Thundering Steppes Timeline]].*
- *This is the former access quest for the instance, [[Chamber of Immortality]]. While completion of this quest is no longer required to enter the instance, adventurers may still obtain and complete it.*

## Prerequisites

- Speak [[Halasian]].

## Description

*In The Village of Thundermist I came across a very sick barbarian. The only relevant information I got out of him was something about a hermit. I think he thinks the hermit got him sick. Perhaps if I find this hermit I could help the ailing barbarian.*

## Steps

1. Find the [[Elderly Hermit|an elderly hermit]] in [[Ruins of Karana]]. One possible location is {{waypoint -675, -6, 683}}.
1. Go into [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] and kill the following named skeletons for the hermit:
   - [[Thimzem Ci'Re]]
   - [[Erbmys Sumyd]]
   - [[Et'sipe Ymeresh]]
   - Note: These named are spawned by slaying increasingly difficult waves of their followers. Example: Killing [[A Sumyd apprentice]] and [[A Sumyd guard]] will spawn [[A replacement Sumyd apprentice|replacement apprentices]] and [[A replacement Sumyd guard|replacement guards]]. Killing this wave causes [[An enraged Sumyd apprentice|enraged apprentices]] and [[An enraged Sumyd guard|enraged guards]] to spawn. On killing the wave of "enraged" mobs, the named will appear behind a curtain.

## Rewards

- Experience
- Access to the front door of the [[The Ruins of Varsoon]].
