This is part 1 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Freeport]] who wear Chain and Plate Armor (with the exception of [[Shaman|shaman]] types).

- You must be at least level 20 to receive this quest.
- If you are from [[Qeynos]], [[Kelethin]], or [[New Halas]], then your version of the quest is called [[Ian's Forgetful Family - Part I]].

## Steps

1. You must collect items from creatures in [[The Commonlands]].
   - Kill 18 [[A fallen guardian|fallen guardians]] in Forgotten Valley {{waypoint 579, -49, 298}}.
   - Kill [[Anders Blackhammer]] (located in **the crossroads**) {{waypoint -522, -44, -300}}.
1. Return to Dyric.
