## Steps

1. Collect 8 ravasect eggs from the new colony:
   - Jumping down the waterfall at {{waypoint 436, 179, -530}}, and swim through the underwater cave behind the waterfall.
   - Kill [[A Humator eggbearer|Humator eggbearers]] and they will drop harvestable egg goo.
1. Return to [[Aetryol Monsoon]].

## Rewards

- At least 9g 94s 79c
- After completing this quest as well as [[The Flame of the Corpse Candles]], hail [[Aetryol Monsoon]] again and you will be offered your choice of:
  - [[Speed of the Taloned Vigil]]
  - [[Knowledge of the Taloned Vigil]]
  - [[Wisdom of the Taloned Vigil]]
  - [[Strength of the Taloned Vigil]]
