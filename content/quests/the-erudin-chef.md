## Steps

1. Collect 10 madcap mushrooms, located at the entrance to the cave behind the [[A stonebrunt stalagmaw|stonebrunt stalagmaws]] {{waypoint -395, 382, 1227}}.
1. Collect 10 wizard cap mushrooms, located around [[Aglthin Pond]] {{waypoint 40, 363, 979}}.
1. Return to [[Chef Bahiyya]] and hail her.
1. Try the stew by clicking the cauldron beside [[Chef Bahiyya|Bahiyya]].
1. Speak again to [[Chef Bahiyya]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
