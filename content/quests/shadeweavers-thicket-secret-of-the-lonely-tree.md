High up in an area where a lonely tree stands, I found a strange magical apparatus. I can only guess that it is some form of monument to the killing of those whose unfortunate souls these skulls belong to. The skulls are blackened and have been burned by magic. There is only one form of hostile in this area that would kill in this manor.

## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Kill 15 Firefall tegi [[A firefall caller|callers]], [[A firefall guardian|guardians]], [[A firefall seer|seers]], or [[A firefall sentry|sentries]] around {{waypoint 153.94, 82.55, 341.20}} in [[Firefall Fringe]].
