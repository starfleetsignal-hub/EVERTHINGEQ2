## Steps

1. Speak to [[Stara Vincent]] at the [[The Concordium Tower|Concordium]] in South Qeynos (Qeynos Capital District).  Take the red portal and she's on the lower level, on the right as you head down the staircase.
1. Meet Stara at a lone tree in [[Qeynos Province District]] - head to {{waypoint 576, -20, -276}}
1. Kill the [[A crazed Freeblood|crazed Freebloods]] that attack you (two even heroics and one v heroic, no class).
1. Return to Talas in Qeynos Capitol District {{waypoint 648, -19, -250}}.

## Rewards

- At least 22s 73c @ level 23 (coin amount scales w/level)
