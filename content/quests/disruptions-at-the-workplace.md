## Steps

1. Travel to the mining pit below the [[Erudin Gardens]] and disrupt the mining operation occuring there.
   - Slay 6 [[A slobberjaw drudge|slobberjaw drudges]]. {{waypoint -403, 478, -278}}
   - Slay [[An Erudian slaver]]. 89^^ Heroic {{waypoint -398, 474, -311}}
1. Report back to [[Qutaybah]].
