## Steps

1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 214, -20, 243}} to the [[Prophecy Portal Gallery]] and enter [[Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands]] through the portal at {{waypoint 714, 412, 217}})
1. Obtain the following:
   - Gather 10 **fetid bones** in the water of [[Pus Flats]] near {{waypoint -9, 19, 573}}. Some bones show above the water, but many do not, so look closely.  ![Half-submerged fetid bones](images/It's_Not_Easy_Being_Gangrene_quest_-_Fetid_Bones.jpg)
   - Obtain 12 **undamaged necrotic poison sacs** (uncommon update) from [[An arachnae infestor|arachnae infestors]] on [[Canker Shores]] near {{waypoint -128, 27, 294}}
1. Speak to [[Kanlum]] at {{waypoint 497, 83, 47}} and receive [[Gangrenous Suit]]
1. Use the [[Gangrenous Suit]].
1. Gather 8 **unholy scrolls of The Last House**![example of an unholyScroll](images/UnholyScroll.jpg) from the crypts around [[Kanlum]].
   - The scrolls are on the ledges around the crypts at Kanlum's eye level.
   - You have to put the suit on before you'll be able to see the scrolls.
1. Place the **unholy scrolls** in the [[Traveling tome trunk]] at {{waypoint 540, 84, 70}}
1. Speak to [[Librarian Szeth]] on the second floor of the Library in the [[North Wing Catalog]] at {{waypoint 330, 15, -204}}.
