## Notes

- This is a subquest of [[Bring Him Bax]].

## Steps

1. Craft [[Yellowfang Mantle]]:
   - You must be a level 9 Artisan.
   - Tradeskill Station: [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]]
   - Recipe: [[Yellowfang Mantle Recipe]] (received automatically)
   - Materials:
     - [[Yellowfang Pelt and Fangs]] (received automatically)
     - 2 [[Deklium Girdle]]s
       - Crafted by a level 76 Jeweler.
       - Materials:
         - [[Deklium cluster]] x3
         - [[Ferrite cluster]] x1
         - [[Rough kunzite]] x2
         - [[Smoldering Coal]] x1
     - 2 [[Bristled pelt|bristled pelts]]
     - 5 [[Smoldering filament|smoldering filaments]]
   - There is a loom in [[Kunzar Jungle]] at {{waypoint 650, 38, -93}}.
   - The mantle's crafting can be commissioned.
1. Return to Bax.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- +2,000 faction with **Kunzar Jungle Villagers**
- Return to [[Kazid Nazan]] to complete [[Bring Him Bax]]
