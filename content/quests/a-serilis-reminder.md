## Steps

*Steinsister Taswynn, a Priest of Serilis, believes that the rising faith in Chirannis is being used to unify the gnoll packs, but that it can also be used to splinter them again. She's sent me to remind them of their creator, Brell Serilis.*

1. Hunt scout skilled gnolls for 12 leather satchels
   - Eligible gnolls include:
     - a Snowfang lightfoot
     - a Blotch Mot skirmisher
     - a Lleth Mal shadowclaw
     - a Rockpaw tracker
     - a Mudpaw pathfinder
     - a Dustpaw Skulker
     - a Sabertooth infiltrator
1. Gather 6 sacks of Blackburrow hops from Sabertooth Burrow
   - Sacks are NOT a group update, but they do respawn in a locked instance (after 1-2 hours)
   - Sabertooth Burrow locations:
     - {{waypoint -40.21, -43.72, -191.31}}  ![](images/Blackburrow_Hops.png)
     - {{waypoint -11.78, -42.79, -194.69}}
     - {{waypoint -65.63, -47.30, -221.79}}
     - {{waypoint -108.00, -50.38, -246.45}}
     - {{waypoint -14.92, -39.79, -255.73}}
     - {{waypoint -2.50, -37.99, -239.44}}
1. Swap out existing Chirannis devotion satchels in Dustpaw Burrow and in Sabertooth Burrow
   - Satchels are NOT a group update, and once they are clicked they cannot be used by another player
   - Satchels will respawn in a locked instance (after 1-2 hours?)
   - Splitpaw Burrow locations:
     - {{waypoint -17, 1, 1}} ![](images/Chirannis_Devotion_Satchel.png)
     - {{waypoint -33, 1, 62}}
     - {{waypoint -46, 1, 95}}
     - {{waypoint 8, -1, 139}}
   - Dustpaw Burrow locations:
     - {{waypoint -85, -17, -51}}
     - {{waypoint -36, -13, -116}}
     - {{waypoint -113, -10, -167}}
     - {{waypoint -87, -10, -153}}
     - {{waypoint 66, -12, -243}}

1. Return to Steinsister Taswynn in Darkpaw Dugout {{waypoint -22, -77, -269}}
