## Notes

- These steps must be performed in the order below
- You will probably need a group to get these updates.

## Steps

1. **The first update is in Deep [[Sebilis]]**: Kill an 80^^^ "a waterlogged wraith"{{waypoint 22, -132, 173}} which has about 120,000 HP's and does crushing dmg.
   - Jump into the water and swim through the underwater routes, see discussion page for information on getting there if you need it
1. **Second update is in [[Chardok]]**. Take the sokokar then backtrack to the water room. Kill an 80^^^ "a drenched zombie" {{waypoint -481, -85, -336}}
1. **Third update is in [[Charasis: Vault of Eternal Sleep]]**. Pick up the note located in Charasis:Vault of Eternal Sleep, {{waypoint -12, -7, -110}}. This is the clue to find the last pirate and is needed to spawn the next update
1. **Head to [[Chelsith]]**, When inside go left to {{waypoint 291, 7, 290}} here you will find The Pirate Jones (80^^^) *(about 144,000 hp and does crushing damage)*
   - Hail him and go through his dialog, at the end he will attack you. Kill him and get the map update.
