## Notes

- [[Assistant Volwon Zhi'dok]] will only offer one of his four quests daily.

## Steps

1. Fish up 25 [[King prawn|king prawns]] or [[Barracuda|barracudas]] in [[Fens of Nathsar]].
1. Return to Assistant Volwon Zhi'dok in Bathezid's Watch.

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- +2000 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
