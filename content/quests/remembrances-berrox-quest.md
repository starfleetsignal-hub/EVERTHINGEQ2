## Steps

1. Kill 10 shadowed creatures.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 shadowed creatures.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 shadowed creatures.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 soulless zombies.
   - [[A soulless assistant|Soulless assistants]] or [[A soulless resurrector|resurrectors]] will work.
   - Eight Soulless Assists at a room {{waypoint -15, 594, 403}}, where [[Xychlzys]] is. Two more at {{waypoint 17, 589, 199}} [[The Cistern of Immortality]]. Respawn time around 15 to 20 minutes.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 soulless zombies.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 soulless zombies.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 evil eyes.
   - [[Mitalla the Voice]], [[A spectral sentry|spectral sentries]], [[A paroxysmal observer|paroxysmal observers]], [[A ghastly sentry|ghastly sentries]] and [[Paroxroz]] will work.
   - You can find [[Evil Eye|evil eyes]] at {{waypoint -32, 600, 340}}.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 evil eyes.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 evil eyes.
1. Read the book.
