Charles Q Tinkersmith continues his attempts to establish a special research branch for gnemlin research. Unfortunately, all his prototypes were left behind when his research lab was overtaken by the immolated, He's asked me to go in and retrieve some prototype eyepieces and spare lenses.

## Steps

1. Find some spare lenses
   1. Go to the door at the south side of the [[Sublevel Access Antechamber]]  {{waypoint -106, 2, 46}}  within [[Maldura]].
   1. Zone into [[Maldura: Algorithm For Destruction (Heroic)]].
      - *Note: This can be done solo if you are careful, you won't need to fight.*
   1. Click the Box of Lenses {{waypoint -85.51, 13.66, 198.91}} to get a [[High quality lens]].
   1. Click 3 **A Dead Assistant** on the steps nearby.
      - The assistants appear in random spots. Some possible locations include:
        - {{waypoint 5, 24, 137}}
        - {{waypoint -34, 9.8, 158.5}}
        - {{waypoint -106, 5, 113}}
        - {{waypoint -115, 1, 106}}
1. Bring the lenses and prototypes back to [[Charles Q Tinkersmith]] in [[Maldura]].

## Rewards

- [[Tinkersmith's Prototype Eyepiece]]
- At least 1p 53g 9s 13200 status
- [[Coin of the Grand Artificer]]
- +1,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +5,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
