Thorrigg, a smithy apprentice who saw me speaking with Lasslyn, has told me of their need to refill their coffers of raw material. They could use whatever ore has been mined from within the Splitiron Mine as soon as possible.

## Steps

1. Search the [[Splitiron Mine]] for ore {{waypoint 299.96, 127.68, 222.64}}.
   1. Speak with [[Urmkam]], who is trapped under the boulders {{waypoint 271.81, 131.98, 207.63}}.
   1. Clear the cave-in rubble by destroying 6 boulders.<br>*Note: In order to make the boulders destroyable, you will need to go to {{waypoint 278, 128, 202}}. Once an NPC talks to you through the rubble, you will be able to destroy the boulders.*
   1. Kill 8 [[A fathomlurker|fathomlurkers]]. {{waypoint 139, 136, 249}}
1. Speak with [[Urmkam]] again.
1. Return to [[Thorrigg]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 41g 75s 12854 status
- +10,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +10,000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
- Clears the mine for easier access to East Thalumbra
- One of the Following:
  - [[Verdant Umbrite Post]]
  - [[Pyre Umbrite Post]]
  - [[Cerulean Umbrite Post]]
