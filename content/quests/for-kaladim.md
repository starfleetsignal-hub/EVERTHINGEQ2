## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Slay 8 from any of the following mob types in [[Kaladim]], most from Clan Kragbak:
   - [[A Kragbak soldier]]
   - [[A Kragbak gladiator]]
   - [[A Kragbak chanter]]
   - [[A Kragbak howler]]
   - [[A Quaketail vendor]]
1. Return to [[Gorn Stonearms]]

## Rewards

- At least 41s 65c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Blessed Clover Bracelet]]
  - [[Bound Clover Bracelet]]
  - [[Enchanted Clover Bracelet]]
  - [[Woven Clover Bracelet]] (All Scouts)
- +1000 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade**
