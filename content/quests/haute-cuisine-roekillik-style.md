## Prerequisites

- -10,000 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**

## Steps

1. Collect ingredients for the goulash.
   - Collect 2 brown rock salts - Appears as large brown rocks.  {{waypoint -71.23, 29.51, 30.58}}
   - Collect 3 handfuls of deathberries. - Appears as a bush. {{waypoint 48, 20, 212}}
   - Collect 3 baneshrooms - Appears as mushroom patches. {{waypoint -61.40, 37.96, -79.42}}
   - Collect 3 toxicorn - Appears as grass.  {{waypoint -420.29, 10.50, -24.46}} or {{waypoint -368.46, -10.40, 113.06}}
   - Collect a shank of erudite.  Kill erudites to get update. (NOTE: Got an from a Dartain forge lord in Dartain's Fortress 556.96, 89.87, -221.77).
1. Return to [[Sous Chef Puabi]].

## Rewards

- At least 55g 70s 61c
- +2,500 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**
