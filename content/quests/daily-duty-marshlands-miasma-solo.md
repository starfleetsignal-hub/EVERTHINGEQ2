## Steps

I've been tasked by the Sang'Huuu to clear the marshlands of the foul creatures that contaminate it, lest their disease spreads to outlying areas of Splendor Sky Aerie. I hope they have a plague doctor on hand upon my return!

1. Enter [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma (Solo)]] in [[Burrower's Timber]] at {{waypoint 604.51, 85.45, 325.17}}
1. Slaughter [[Droseraceae]] the Swamp Terror at {{waypoint 838.86, 60.10, 215.83}}
1. Chop down [[Nerjehl Khaneh]] the Auduborian which circulates around the lake at {{waypoint 793.86, 41.62, 66.23}}
1. Disinfect [[Miasmasula]] the Diseased Harbinger which circulates around the lake at {{waypoint 793.86, 41.62, 66.23}}
