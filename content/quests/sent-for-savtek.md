## Steps

1. Speak to [[Savtek]].  He's on the southeast corner of Daarspire near the edge of the island {{waypoint -300, 214, 257}}
1. Enter the cave that the Harrowing Horde have taken over.  The entrance to the cave is at {{waypoint -59, 201, -57}}
1. Slay 5 Harrowing Horde within the cave.
   - [[A charging skirth]], [[An Orafic soulscreecher]], [[A Skirth soulstalker]] count.  Be careful of [[A Harrowing Visitant]], which counts towards the quest but will probably kill you.
1. Return to Lepethida
