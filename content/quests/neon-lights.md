## Starting the Quest

[[GM Event|GM Events]] can start in any city or zone in the game.  After a bit of roleplaying the guide will offer you the quest with the following Description text:

*I met a gnome who is attempting to manufacture a better light source.  He requested my assistance in gathering some of the harvest material he needs to tinker up his experimental recipe.*

## Steps

1. Harvest tier 1 materials:
   - [[Frog leg]]
   - [[Jumjum]]
   - [[Lead cluster]]
   - [[Rawhide leather pelt]]
   - [[Root]]
   - [[Severed elm]]
   - [[Tin cluster]]
1. Return to the guide.

## Rewards

- [[Hanging light]]
