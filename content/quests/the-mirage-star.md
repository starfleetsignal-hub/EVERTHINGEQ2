## Steps

1. Kill [[Tairiza the Widow Mistress]] in [[The Tomb of Thuuga]].
   - Received [[Illusion: Drachnid]]
1. Return to [[Tibby Joopin]] in [[Teren's Grasp]].
1. Next is to test  [[The Leviathan]].
   - *There is a vicious and vile beast that threatens all sea going vessels in the northwestern waters of Kunark.  Some call it the Leviathan.  I'm not sure where its lair is, but if you can destroy this beast, the wand should grant you the ability to use another illusion.*
   - Received [[Illusion: Fishman]]
1. Return to [[Tibby Joopin]] in [[Teren's Grasp]].
1. Kill [[Silverwing]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]].
1. Return to [[Tibby Joopin]] in [[Teren's Grasp]].
