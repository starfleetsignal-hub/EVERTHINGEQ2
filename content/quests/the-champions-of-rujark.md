## Steps

1. Return to [[The Clefts of Rujark]] and kill the three Champions of Rujark:
   - [[Blademaster Thul]] {{waypoint 118, 20, -181}} Second Floor
   - [[Azhahkar the Gatecaller]] {{waypoint 25, 22, 2}} Second Floor
   - [[Far Seer Vokkal]] {{waypoint -16, 49, 107}} Top Floor - Upper Clefts
1. Return to [[Zahr Shabao]].

## Rewards

- At least 15g 15s 54c
- +5000 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
