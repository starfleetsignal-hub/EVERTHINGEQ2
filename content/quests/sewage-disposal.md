## Notes

- ***Note:** This is a 5 minute timed quest.*

## Steps

1. Destroy 15 [[A crocodile egg|crocodile eggs]]. Only kill the minimum number of crocs in the moat to spawn Viletooth. If you kill too many, the eggs do not appear. This is a group update.
   - The one you hit to start the quest counts as one of the 15.
   - Possible locations:
     - {{waypoint 36.23, -11.60, -15.55}}
     - {{waypoint 51.57, -11.44, -6.65}}
     - {{waypoint 47.14, -11.42, 8.34}}
     - {{waypoint 42.17, -11.47, 4.00}}
     - {{waypoint 14.22, -11.66, -20.25}}
     - {{waypoint 32.26, -11.60, 1.50}}
