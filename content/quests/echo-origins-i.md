Within the Midst Library, I discovered a tome entitled, Echo Origins I, with pages of notes and diagrams of the cavern's flora, specifically throughout the southern half, but little in the way of its fauna. As a scholar of some renown, be it with quill or sword, I feel the need to fill in the blanks and complete this tome.
