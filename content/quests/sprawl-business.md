## Steps

1. You will get assigned  2 random tasks from list below:
   - Hunt 8 nullbound dominators - golems in Coral Forest area {{waypoint -60, 3, 407}} they activate when someone walks by them.
   - Hunt 10 echo of sacrifices in Detritus Pantheon {{waypoint 371, 43, 356}}
   - Hunt 12 void snappers on the beaches around Oogothl Cove {{waypoint 26, 4, 415}}, {{waypoint -92, 4, 661}}
   - Destroy 10 scriptbound watchers in Vital Terrace around {{waypoint -86, 74, -342}} and on platforms above.
1. Return to Vegk

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following:
  - 2 x [[Void Dewdrop Plant]]
  - 2 x [[Tall Void Dewdrop Plant]]
