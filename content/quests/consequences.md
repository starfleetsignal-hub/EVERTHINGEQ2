## Steps

1. Speak to [[An outcast of Maj'Dul]] in the [[Midday Market]]. {{waypoint 87, 140, -16}}
1. Kill [[A deepwater caiman|deepwater caiman]]s in [[The Sinking Sands]] for a rare update. {{waypoint -1789, -222, -232}}, {{waypoint -1121, 203, 28}}
1. Return to the outcast.
1. Wait 70 minutes realtime and speak to the outcast again.
1. Return to [[Barakah (NPC)|Barakah]].
