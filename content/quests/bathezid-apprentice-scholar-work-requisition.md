## Steps

1. After receiving the [[Bathezid apprentice scholar recipes]], collect the requisition list from the Work Requisitions clipboard (next to [[Supply Sergeant Anuhadux]])
1. Create 6 of one of the following (randomly determined after clicking the clipboard):
   - [[Archonix's Calculation (Journeyman)]] (Engraved Desk - sacred incense)
   - [[Good luck charm (Item)|good luck charm]] (Work Bench - smoldering coal)
   - [[Potion of endurance]] (Chemistry Table - smoldering candle)
   - [[Ring of protection]] (Work Bench - smoldering coal)
   - [[Rust proofing]] (Chemistry Table - smoldering candle)
   - [[Saavedra's Instant Illusion (Journeyman)]] (Engraved Desk - sacred incense)
1. Return to [[Supply Sergeant Anuhadux]].

## Rewards

- At least 47g 79s 14c
- +750 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
