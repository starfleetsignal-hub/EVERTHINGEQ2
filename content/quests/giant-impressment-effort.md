## Steps

1. Collect the following:
   - 8 Succulent Needles. Body drop from the cacti around [[Giant's Walk]] area {{waypoint -470, 86, 175}}.
   - 8 Pricklyburr Leaves. Blue-green bushes with yellow-tipped fronds, found in [[Warslik's Wood]] area (along river path). The ubiquitous mundane, non-harvestable version of these plants have no yellow-fronds. The bushes are fairly abundant {{waypoint 401, -216, -353}}, {{waypoint 346.93, -197.08, -481.31}}.  {{waypoint 420.97, -204.66, -204.40}} They do not have white text like a node so you  need to mouse over them to see their brown text box. Not a group update.
1. Create a vaporous vessel by using the porous vessel you received.
1. Use the vessel on a Kromdul giant.
   - *Note: Turn ranged/focus off before you use it!*
1. Return to [[Arcanist Chaarex]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 15108 status
