## Description

I must enter The Vigilant: Rescue and slay the powerful entities that are loyal to Marus Xand. I must slay Prime-Kedon Kelv, Kerbiros Razorback, Freezerburn, and Amahn-Prime Nirel.

## Steps

   - Kill [[Prime-Kedon Kelv]]
   - Kill [[Kerbiros Razorback]]
   - Kill [[Freezerburn]]
   - Kill [[Amahn-Prime Nirel]]
