## Steps

1. Kill rats in Vermin's Snye for 10 auto-updates.
   - Very rare update - expect to kill hundreds of rats.
1. Kill [[A giant burrower|giant burrower]]s for 5 auto-updates. {{waypoint 151, -7, -101}}
   - Rare update - expect to kill ~25.
1. Kill Bloodsabers for 5 auto-updates.
   - Rare update - expect to kill ~30.
   - [[A crumbling tomb guard|crumbling tomb guard]]s and [[A festering zombie|festering zombie]]s also update this step.
