*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Kill the high priests of both sides of the conflict (Tae Ew and Rallosian)
   - [[High Priest of Thule]] is found at the head of the Anaconda River {{waypoint -1200, 0, 600}}. Talk with him first, then kill him.
   - [[Kromok the Acolyte (Named)|Kromok the Acolyte]] is found north of the Tower of Vul {{waypoint -822, 0, 381}}. Talk with him first, then kill him.
- Return to Malkin.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 69s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Ash Coated Acolyte's Armband]] (All Priests)
  - [[Ash Coated Researcher's Armband]] (All Mages)
  - [[Ash Coated Soldier's Armband]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Ash Coated Stalker's Armband]] (All Scouts
