## Notes

![](images/Negative-Charged_NF-Specs.jpg)![Skull object on the ground at the top of Raven Eye Spire.](images/Remmysskull.jpg)

- The [[Negative-Charged NF-Specs]] disappear after some time or after logging out and can be re-aquired from the NPC.

## Steps

1. Search Raven Eye Spire {{waypoint -980, 166, 596}} with the [[Negative-Charged NF-Specs]]
   - Go to the top of the Spire with the [[Negative-Charged NF-Specs]] equipped. On the way up, you will run into a named 39^ kobold that will aggro on you.
   - Right-click the skull that is bright orange on the ground & inspect it. You may have to switch to first-person perspective to see it.
1. Kill Mazkeen kobolds until you get the plot coordinator (rare update).
1. Return to [[Watchman Plarg]] {{waypoint -592, 129, 797}}.

## Rewards

At least 1g 26s 57c
