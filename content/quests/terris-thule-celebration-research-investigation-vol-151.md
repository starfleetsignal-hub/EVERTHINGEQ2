## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Terris-Thule has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items.

1. Purchase the following items
   - Purchase a [[Hood of Silent Screams|hood of silent screams]] from Rahlu for 5000 status
     - *in [[Darklight Wood]] wanders the path along {{waypoint -253, -56, 23}}*
   - Purchase an [[Polished Obsidian Mirror|polished obsidian mirror]] from Oliska for 5000 status
     - *in [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}*
     - *in [[Qeynos Province District]] at the [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}*
   - Purchase a [[Black Flame Candle|black flame candle]] from Kuutanya for 5000 status
     - *in [[Zimara Breadth]] wanders the path of the [[Isles of Wisdom]] near {{waypoint -750, 142, 24}}*
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Dream Scorcher]] {{waypoint -33, -37, 265}} in [[Darklight Wood]]
