## Steps

1. Examine [[Satchel of Iron Ration Ingredients]] in your inventory and choose *pull out some ingredients* which will provide the following 3 ingredients in your inventory:
   - [[Dried beef chunk]]
   - [[Miscellaneous grain]]
   - [[Salt and preservative]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe: Grant's Iron Rations]] and craft 5 [[Grant's Iron Rations]] at the stove {{waypoint 3633, -1017, 1656}}. Requires 5 [[Effulgent kindling]] as well as the contents of the satchel
1. Speak to Grant

## NOTE

Bulk Craft will ONLY result in 1 (ONE) item, so make 1 @ a time.
IF you bulk craft you will have to make more Cobalt Chiori Pastes, Which means you will need to harvest more Cobalt Chiori.

## Rewards

- 5 [[A Mysterious Blue Coin|A Mysterious Blue Coins]]
- At least 1p 30g
