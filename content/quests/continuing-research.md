## Steps

1. Collect the following undamaged drachnid parts (corpse-drops from drachnids near Dreads Landing):
   - an undamaged drachnid thorax
   - an undamaged drachnid abdomen
   - 8 undamaged drachnid legs
1. Return to [[Charleston Gnomegomery]] on [[Dreg's Landing]]. {{waypoint 869, 12, 144}}

## Rewards

- At least 6g 88s 19c
- [[Nurgan Foe-blaster's Cowl]]
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
