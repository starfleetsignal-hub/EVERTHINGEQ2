## Notes

1. Character must be a Fury and level 80+ to be offered quest.

## Steps

1. Place for "A Mutilated Deer" Fury epic starter, {{waypoint 193, -43, 169}} will trigger quest when you get close.
1. Head to the [[Wanderlust Fair]] and talk to [[Torbus Filnow]] {{waypoint 272, -20, -14}}
1. Go next to [[Trainer Drisace Do'Arkenarn]] {{waypoint -373, -50, 40}} and speak to her about the mutilated deer.
1. Head to the [[Shadow Oak]] in the northwest area of the zone.
1. Go up to the top of the Oak until you see a green barrier - then talk to [[Atalika]] {{waypoint 444, -24, -296}}.  He will give you access past the green barrier.
1. Pass through the barriers to reach the top, and speak to the Head Druid, [[Thelia N'Fyre]] {{waypoint 446, -7, -265}}. She will give you a [[Chokidai Mask]] and the quest [[The Pride of the Chokidai]].

## Rewards

- 4g 11s 9c
- [[Chokidai Mask]]
