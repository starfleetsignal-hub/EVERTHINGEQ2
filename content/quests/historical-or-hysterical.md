## Steps

1. Speak to [[Miltar Hyzill]] at {{waypoint -614.30, 35.43, 443.43}}.
1. Gather 16 carapaces from giant and massive lightcrawlers near {{waypoint 263, 120, 640}} or {{waypoint 185.05, 105.93, 465.44}}
1. Return to [[Miltar Hyzill]] at {{waypoint -614.30, 35.43, 443.43}}.
1. Return to [[Brannelle Lydden]] at {{waypoint -773.84, 52.08, 369.08}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- One of the following:
  - [[Righteous Sanctus Sleeves]]
  - [[Righteous Sanctus Leather Pauldrons]]
  - [[Righteous Sanctus Mantle]]
  - [[Platemail Righteous Sanctus Vambraces]]
