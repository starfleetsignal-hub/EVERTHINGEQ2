## Notes

- This quest scales to the adventure level tier of the character, i.e. level 21-30 characters will receive the level 25 version of the quest.
- This quest is repeatable up to 10 times. per adventure level tier.
- You may need to kill more than the listed number of monsters in order to retrieve the required drops for this quest.
- Players adventure level 121+ will need to mentor down to a lower level range in order to get these quests offered.

## Steps

1. Slay the following monsters (for your adventure level tier) until you retrieve 5 of the indicated quest item:
   - **Tier 1 (Level 1-10)**
     - [[A rolling stone|Rolling stones]] in [[Frostfang Sea]]{{waypoint -46, 24, -333}}
     - [[A blizzard grizzly|Blizzard grizzlies]] in [[Frostfang Sea]]{{waypoint 102, 29, -425}}
   - **Tier 2 (Level 11-20)**
     - [[A large frostbiter|Large frostbiters]] in [[Frostfang Sea]] {{waypoint 540, 43, 619}}
     - [[A Ry'Gorr grenadier|Ry'Gorr grenadier]] in [[Frostfang Sea]] {{waypoint 659, 59, 667}}
   - **Tier 3 (Level 21-30)**
     - [[A frenzied gorger|Frenzied gorgers]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] {{waypoint -195, 210, -63}}
     - [[A Shadesire defender|Shadesire defenders]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] {{waypoint -444, 161, -294}}
   - **Tier 4 (Level 31-40)**
     - [[A Runnyeye mercenary|Runnyeye mercenaries]] in [[Enchanted Lands]]
     - [[A threatening commoner|Threatening commoners]] in [[Enchanted Lands]]
   - **Tier 5 (Level 41-50)**
     - [[A primal frostfin|Primal frostfins]] in [[Everfrost]]
     - [[A wooly silk slinger|Wooly silk slingers]] in [[Everfrost]] {{waypoint -326.47, 0.19, -418.44}}
   - **Tier 6 (Level 51-60)**
     - [[The Lesser Faydark]] (solo)
       - [[A grovespirit acolyte|Grovespirit acolytes]], [[A grovespirit caller|callers]] and [[A grovespirit defender|defenders]]
       - [[A honeybrood worker|honeybrood workers]]
     - [[The Sinking Sands]] (heroic)
       - [[An Anaz Mal veilguard|Anaz Mal veilguards]] in [[The Living Tombs]]
       - Mummies, such as [[A Sulite tjat (Silent City)|Sulite tjat]] in [[The Silent City]]
   - **Tier 7 (Level 61-70)**
     - [[The Barren Sky]] (solo)
       - [[A ridgestone defender|Ridgestone defenders]], [[A ridgestone guardian|guardians]], [[A ridgestone sentinel|sentinels]], and [[A ridgestone trampler|tramplers]]
       - [[A dryeye vehemence|Dryeye vehemences]] or [[A dryeye entangler]]
     - [[Faydwer (Continent)|Faydwer]] (heroic)
       - [[A Kragbak battler|Kragbak kobolds]] in [[Kaladim]]
       - [[An obsidian construct|Obsidian constructs]] in [[Mistmoore Catacombs]]
   - **Tier 8 (Level 71-80)** - [[Kunzar Jungle]]
     - [[A trained war sokokar|trained war sokokar]]
     - [[A kunzar tiger|kunzar tigers]]
   - **Tier 9 (level 81-90)**
     - [[A Toxxulia guardian|Toxxulia guardians]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]
     - [[A slitherstrike mamba|Slitherstrike mambas]] in [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]]
   - **Tier 10 (Level 91-100)**
     - [[An etherveil planeshifter|Etherveil planeshifters]] in [[Eidolon Jungle]] {{waypoint -1195.53, 15.90, 429.13}}
     - [[A churning obol|Churning obols]] in [[Obol Plains]]
   - **Tier 10 (Level 101-110)**
     - [[Brackish wakingdew]] in [[Plane of Magic]]
     - [[Deep aethersalmon]] in [[Plane of Magic]]  (Waypoint -199.49, 2.73, -995.07)
   - **Tier 14 (Level 120)**
     - [[A toxic sunbloom|Toxic sunblooms]] in [[The Blinding]].
     - [[A greater sandgrabber|Greater sandgrabbers]] in [[The Blinding]].
   - **Tier 15 (Level 125)**
     - [[A highland prickler|Highland pricklers]] in [[Svarni Expanse]].
     - [[A sandrock lizard|Sandrock lizards]] in [[Svarni Expanse]].
1. Return to [[Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber (New Halas)|Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber]] in [[New Halas]].
