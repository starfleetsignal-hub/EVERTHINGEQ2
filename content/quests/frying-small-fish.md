## Steps

1. Eliminate the following, in any order. Unfortunately, none of these give AA experience or anything better than standard chests.
   - [[Pumtog]] near [[Gunthak's Shanty]] {{waypoint -1284, -1070, 1101}}
   - [[Sunza Bloodocean]] near the [[Salty Sail Port]] {{waypoint -898, -1087, 1006}}
   - [[Junka Bloodocean]] in the [[Brokenskull Farpost]] {{waypoint -577, -1080, 1362}}
1. Talk to [[Swaggerin' Grakk]] at the [[Salty Sail Port]]. {{waypoint -923, -1094, 1111}}

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +1,200 faction with **Survival Accord**
