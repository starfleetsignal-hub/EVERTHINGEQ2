## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Description

While battling some of the accursed Turmoil Toads in the Shard of Fear I found a Halasian Love Charm. On one side it says "Conall and Boand Lancebreaker" and on the other it says, "Windstalker Village or Halas, our eternal love shall keep us warm".

## Steps

1. Go to [[Windstalker Village]] in [[Antonica]] and speak with [[Conall Lancebreaker]]. {{waypoint -1896, -36, -616}}
   - It's possible that Conall will not be visible if the quest is grey or red to you. If this is the case, mentor to the appropriate level and he should appear.

## Rewards

- At least 17g 7s 97c
