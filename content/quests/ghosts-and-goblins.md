## Description

"The spiders in the Tainted Forest do not seem to be the source of the Island's evil, so Deianeira asked me to find the Forest Keeper, a treant. Apparently it will not speak to her because she betrayed Freeport for Qeynos, so she's been unable to determine what it knows about the changes in the lands. I hope the Forest Keeper has some suggestions." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[The Forest Keeper]] {{waypoint 129, 2, 31}} in the Tainted Forest.
1. Kill 3 [[A ghostly treant|ghostly treants]].
1. Return to The Forest Keeper.
1. Speak to [[A Sapswill soothsayer]] {{waypoint -71, 13, -163}} at the [[The Last Stand]].
1. Gather four items:
   - Water from [[Sapswill Falls]] {{waypoint -72, 0, -251}}
   - Water from [[Hope Falls]] {{waypoint -11, 0, -250}}
   - Water from [[Razortooth Falls]] {{waypoint 16, -10, -88}}
   - Harvest 3 roots from any root node in the colony (there is a small cluster near the Sapswill Falls, {{waypoint -76, 2, -216}}).
1. Return to a Sapswill soothsayer for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Sapswill swill]]
