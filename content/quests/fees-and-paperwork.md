## Steps

1. Pay a fee of 1 gold piece for paperwork to obtain your citizenship to [[Neriak, City of Hate]]. You will lose all access to [[Haven]] and all the privileges you have as an [[Exile]].
1. She will give you a follow-on quest, [[Exile to Neriak- Acceptance]].
