Korrom Flintknuckle, First Mate aboard The Swimming Oak, is curious about the shipwrecks around Svarni Gateway, and the fates of the crews they held. He's asked me to investigate some of the wrecks, while Captain Douglan Wakerunner is away.

## Notes

*Each location spawns [[A vigorous shark]] [126]. It is not needed for the updates.*

## Steps

1. There are several interesting ship wrecks in the waters surrounding Svarni Gateway, the initial landing spot in Svarni Expanse.
   - Inspect shipwreck to the northwest of Svarni Gateway at {{waypoint 804.77, 1.14, 301.86}}.
   - Inspect shipwreck to the north of Svarni Gateway at {{waypoint 682.10, 1.12, 393.90}}.
   - Inspect shipwreck to the south of Svarni Gateway at {{waypoint 629.89, 1.12, 639.09}}.
1. Return to [[Korrom Flintknuckle]] at {{waypoint 699, 5, 529}}.
1. Find [[Captain Douglan Wakerunner]] in Kamapor at {{waypoint 118, 8, 294}}.
1. speak with [[Captain Douglan Wakerunner]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
