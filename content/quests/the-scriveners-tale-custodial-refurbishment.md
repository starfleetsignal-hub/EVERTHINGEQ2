## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 30 [[Wandervine fruit]]
- 25 [[Reef cluster]]
- 24 [[Nimbic hide]]
- 50 [[Celestial Candle]]
- Chemistry Table (Optional)

## Steps

1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Divine Antibiotic Potion]] and craft [[Divine Antibiotic Potion]] on a [[Chemistry Table]].
   - There is a [[Chemistry Table]], located by going to the [[South Wing Catalog]] on the second floor of the Library, then using the teleporter to the [[Crafters' Gallery]] at {{waypoint 332, 16, 174}}.
1. Use the [[Divine Antibiotic Potion]] on 8 ill custodians located in the Myrist Librarian Quarters (313.27 -34.91 186.24 268.68 0.00 0.00).
1. Speak to [[Dylindra Morganna]] at {{waypoint 306, -35, 165}}.
1. Go to the [[South Wing Catalog]] on the second floor of the Library, use the teleporter to the [[Smiths' Gallery]] at {{waypoint 388, 16, 170}}, then speak with [[Ghida Borrell]] at {{waypoint 660, 412, 59}}.

## Rewards

- [[Suffix Title: the Runemaster]]
- [[Masque de Lorme Pattern]]
- [[Recipe Scroll: Divine Weapons of Sky]]
- At least 14p 21g 53s 73c 48000 status
