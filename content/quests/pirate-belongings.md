This is not a normal collection quest. The collection members are not obtained by harvesting shinies.<br>
Instead, the fifteen collection members are themselves all obtained from the completion of other collection quests. This is called a "meta-collection".<br>
Also note that whilst ten of the items needed are from Advanced Solo (AS) zone collections, the other five items are from Event Heroic (EH) zone collections. The distinction is given in the list above with AS or EH accordingly.
