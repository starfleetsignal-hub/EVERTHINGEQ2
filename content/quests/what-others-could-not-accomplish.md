## Steps

1. Speak to [[Jendan Greenthorn]] inside the Tunarian Alliance building on the platform. {{waypoint 653, 11, -386}}
1. Enter the [[The Forest Ruins|Forest Ruins]] via any of the Qeynos bells. The nearest bell is by the dojo. {{waypoint 731, -20, 373}}
1. Speak to [[Colonel Belaridonis]], a snotty high elf of the Qeynos Guard. {{waypoint 886, -12, -557}}
1. Investigate the ruins yourself. {{waypoint 1017, -19, -635}}
1. Check out the slaughtered Koada'dal guards. {{waypoint 1001, -20, -669}}
   - Slay the ambushing [[A Rockpaw scavenger|Rockpaw scavengers]] and [[A Rockpaw destroyer|Rockpaw destroyer]].
   - Slay [[Grrosh]], the Rockpaw digmaster.
1. Follow the [[A Rockpaw lookout|Rockpaw lookout]] that fled towards the coast while you were fighting, and kill him when you catch up to him. {{waypoint 905, -27, -867}}
1. Return to [[Colonel Belaridonis]], and enjoy putting him in his place.
1. Return to [[Jendan Greenthorn]].
1. Speak to [[Trelly Greenthorn]], near the [[Baubbleshire]] gate in the [[Elddar Grove]].

## Rewards

- At least 84s 37c (at level 36)
- [[Ring of the Wood Elf]]
