## Steps

1. Enter [[The Living Tombs]] and visit the following locations within:
   - [[Residence Court]]: {{waypoint -344, -9, 511}}, at the monolith
   - [[Priestess' Court]]: {{waypoint -110, 12, 120}}, at the priestess spawn
   - [[Statue Court]]: {{waypoint -65, 1, 390}}, by the wisps
1. Return to the entrance of the dungeon at {{waypoint -633, 3, 327}}. Slay the [[A tomb raider|tomb raiders]] that spawn within four minutes.
1. Speak to [[Barusha the Tradesman]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -1165, -151, -304}}. Slay the [[A dervish assassin|dervish assassins]] that spawn within 5 minutes.
1. [[Amina]] will run up. Speak to her for update.
1. Enter [[Nabil's Cave]] in [[The Pillars of Flame]]. {{waypoint 288, -87, -490}}
1. Speak to [[Nabil]]. [[A dervish assassin|Dervish assassins]] will spawn and attack. You have 5 minutes to kill them.
1. Read [[Assassination orders]] from your inventory to complete the quest.

- At least 18g 3s
- Choice of:
  - [[Brilliant Leggings of Worth]]
  - [[Durable Leggings of Worth]]
  - [[Flexible Leggings of Worth]]
  - [[Resilient Leggings of Worth]]
- +5000 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
- -5000 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -5000 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- Access to the instance, [[The Court of the Coin: Imminent Danger]]
