## Notes

- This quest is offered to any player, regardless of moral alignment.  This appears to have changed - now Master Irudan Ortai attacks the Ashen Order (good).

## Steps

1. Kill the following monsters in any order. {{waypoint 919, -130, -1220}}
   - 10 [[A Windsister jaggedfeather|Windsister jaggedfeathers]]
   - 10 [[A Windsister dustfeather|Windsister dustfeathers]]
1. Return to [[Master Irudan Ortai]].

## Rewards

- +5000 faction with **The Ashen Disciples**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Trinket of the Burning Arrow]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Trinket of the Fireball]] (All Mages)
  - [[Trinket of the Firesoul]] (All Priests)
  - [[Trinket of the Flaming Blade]] (All Fighters)
