## Steps

Nothing is free and building, maintaining, and supporting the herald's camp is expensive. That's why the herald of Terris-Thule has requested that we assist in offsetting the camp's cost by retrieving a valuable relic guarded by [[Miasmasula]]. I need to retrieve the relic.

1. Retrieve the relic guarded by [[Miasmasula]]
   - Travel to [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] and zone into [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma (Solo)]] {{waypoint 601, 86, 327}} and clean all named in this zone.
     - The chest with relic is near the portal {{waypoint 769, 52, -29}}
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Dream Scorcher]] {{waypoint -33, -37, 265}} in [[Darklight Wood]]
