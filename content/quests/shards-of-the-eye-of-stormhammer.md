## Notes

- This is a subquest of [[The Stormhammer (Quest)|The Stormhammer]].

## Steps

![](images/Stormhammer_Shard.png)

1. Collect the following shards in the [[Butcherblock Mountains]], all trackable:
   - In [[King's Rest]]:
     - Blossom {{waypoint 245, 159.5, 17.5}}
     - Forests {{waypoint 80, 168, -14}}
     - Branches {{waypoint 130, 160.5, 10.5}}
   - In [[Kamp Krulkiel]]:
     - Woodworld {{waypoint 183.5, 262.5, -349}}
     - Trunks {{waypoint 106, 269, -525}}
   - In [[Slagslore Peak]]:
     - Trees {{waypoint -14, 233, -193}}
   - In [[Darkfall Gorge]]:
     - Thorns {{waypoint -20, 96.5, -302}}
     - Seeds {{waypoint -13, 98.5, -387.5}}
   - In [[Kragploom Mesa]]
     - Fruit {{waypoint 459, 351, -133}}
     - Leaves {{waypoint 529, 350.5, -246.5}}
     - Groves {{waypoint 383.5, 353.5, -220}}
   - Near [[Kragploom Roost]]
     - Roots {{waypoint 603, 370, -368}}
     - Nature {{waypoint 560.5, 373, -347}}
1. Return to [[The Haunted Vaults]] and speak with [[General Beno Targnarle]].
