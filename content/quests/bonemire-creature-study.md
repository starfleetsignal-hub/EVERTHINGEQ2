## Notes

- This can be done at the same time as [[A Poisoned Gingus]].

## Steps

1. Perform the following:
   - Kill 3 [[A nocturnal hunter|nocturnal hunters]]
   - Harvest 3 spider eggs in the spider den at {{waypoint 251, 59, 642}}
1. Return to [[Weneb Tacklenogg]]

## Rewards

- At least 16g 81s 53c
- +500 faction with **Drednever Expedition**
