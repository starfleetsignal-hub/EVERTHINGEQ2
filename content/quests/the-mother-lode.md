## Steps

1. Use the [[Scar wurm pheromone]] that Ilmtar gives you on the [[Indigo Draw]] bridge over [[Innoruuk's Scar]] at {{waypoint -465, -50, -60}}.
1. Kill [[The wurm mother]] that spawns.
1. Return to [[Ilmtar D'Viervs]].

## Rewards

- At least 3s 62c
- [[Darklight Placard]]
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Archon's Nebulous Gauntlets]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Nebulous Gloves]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Nebulous Gloves]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Elder's Nebulous Gloves]] (Shamans)
  - [[Enlightened Nebulous Gloves]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Nebulous Gauntlets]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Shade's Nebulous Gloves]] (All Scouts)
