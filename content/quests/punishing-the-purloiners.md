## Description

The League of Freethinkers has learned of a special task force created by Mayong Mistmoore which must be stopped! These agents, known as the Ravenscale Purloiners, seem to be charged with searching the wide expanses of Norrath and collecting as many arcane objects as they can find and depositing them inside Ravenscale Repository. The Freethinkers have asked me to search the repository and eliminate any Purloiners I find.

## Steps

1. Eliminate 8 [[A Ravenscale Purloiner|Ravenscale Purloiner]]s within [[Ravenscale Repository]]
1. Return to Tanya Alvernon

- At least 1p 60g
