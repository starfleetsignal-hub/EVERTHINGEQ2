## Prerequisites

- You must have at least -4000 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Obtain 8 *flanks of meat* from [[A runewake impaler|runewake impalers]] located in the water off the southern coast of the zone. {{waypoint -190, -71, 1460}}
1. Deposit the items in the crate. {{waypoint 1015, -39, -25}}
1. Speak with [[Pylemea Stoerki]].

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- +2000 faction with **The Order of Rime**
