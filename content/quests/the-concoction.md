## Steps

1. Collect items for Atalika:
   - Collect 8 spider poison glands from the [[A voracious spinner|voracious]], [[A bristly spinner|bristly]] or [[A poisonous spinner|poisonous spinners]] to the west of [[Shadow Oak]] {{waypoint 651, -51, -221}}
   - Collect 5  around the Shadow Oak itself. {{waypoint 449.00, -30.26, -230.46}}
1. Return to [[Atalika]] in [[Shadow Oak]].

- At least 2s 67c

  - [[Elder's Shoulderpads]] (Shamans)

  - [[Shade's Shoulderpads]] (Assassin, Bard, Beastlord, Rogue)
