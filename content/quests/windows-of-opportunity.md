## Steps

1. Slay [[An Erudin voidcaller]] in one of the erudin camps north of [[Quel'ule]] {{waypoint -495, 448, 414}}, {{waypoint -334, 467, 251}}, or {{waypoint -457, 514, -55.67}}, or {{waypoint -478, 512, 112}}.  Be sure to loot the body!
1. Return to the [[Chief Strategist Jasim|Chief Strategist]].
