## Notes

After completing this quest, if you'd like more trees, you can buy more tickets from questgiver for 1p each.

## Steps

1. Decorate areas of [[Kelethin]] by clicking green shinies throughout the area:
   - Near [[Granite Hills Acorn Lift]] {{waypoint 126, 94, 411}}
   - Across from [[Kelethin First Regional Bank]] {{waypoint 345, 109, 138}}
   - [[A weathered memorial stone]] {{waypoint 322, 92, 335}}
1. Decorate [[Far Residential Aerie]] {{waypoint 413, 111, 501}}
1. Defeat [[Mr. McScroogle]].
1. Return to [[Gutten Gigglegibber (Kelethin)|Gutten Gigglegibber]].

## Rewards

- [[Frosty Frostfell Tree Ticket]]
  - To use the ticket double click the tree to the left with the blue star on top.
