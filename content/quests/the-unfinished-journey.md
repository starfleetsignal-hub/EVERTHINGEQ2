## Steps

1. Kill all of the bosses within the Molten Pools
   - Slay [[Teraradus the Gorer]]
   - Slay [[Kerridicus Searskin]]
   - Slay [[The Crumbling Icon]]
     - *once you killed those 3 you'll get the first reward*
1. Kill all of these bosses
   - Slay [[Morin the Swindler]] and [[Barin the Brute]] in *[[Brokenskull Bay: Fury of the Cursed (Raid)|Brokenskull Bay: Fury of the Cursed]]*
   - Slay [[Jessip Daggerheart]] in *[[Brokenskull Bay: Fury of the Cursed (Raid)|Brokenskull Bay: Fury of the Cursed]]*
   - Slay [[Grevog the Punisher]] in [[Castle Highhold: No Quarter (Raid)|*Castle Highhold: No Quarter*]]
   - Slay [[Zebrun the Torso]] in [[Castle Highhold: No Quarter (Raid)|*Castle Highhold: No Quarter*]]
   - Slay [[Gomrim]], [[Zwebek]], [[Tonnin]], and [[Yermon]] in *[[Ssraeshza Temple: Echoes of Time (Raid)|Ssraeshza Temple: Echoes of Time]]*
1. Kill all of these bosses
   - Slay [[Diabo Kela'Set]] in [[Ssraeshza Temple: Echoes of Time (Raid)|*Ssraeshza Temple: Echoes of Time*]]
   - Slay [[Farozth Ssravizh]] in [[Ssraeshza Temple: Echoes of Time (Raid)|*Ssraeshza Temple: Echoes of Time*]]
   - Slay [[Ka'Rah Ferun]] in [[Ssraeshza Temple: Echoes of Time (Raid)|*Ssraeshza Temple: Echoes of Time*]]
   - Slay [[Inquisitor Soronigus]] in *[[Ossuary: Cathedral of Bones (Raid)|Ossuary: Cathedral of Bones]]*
   - Slay [[Sacrificer Aevila D'Serin]] in  *[[Ossuary: Cathedral of Bones (Raid)|Ossuary: Cathedral of Bones]]*
   - Slay [[Virtuoso Edgar V'Zann]] in  *[[Ossuary: Cathedral of Bones (Raid)|Ossuary: Cathedral of Bones]]*
   - Slay [[Captain Krasnok the Immortal (Epic)|Captain Krasnok]] in [[Brokenskull Bay: Fury of the Cursed (Raid)|*Brokenskull Bay: Fury of the Cursed*]]
   - Slay [[Grethah the Frenzied]] in *[[Castle Highhold: No Quarter (Raid)|Castle Highhold: No Quarter]]*
1. Kill all of these bosses
   - Slay [[Ritual Keeper V'derin]] in *[[Ossuary: The Altar of Malice (Raid)|Ossuary: The Altar of Malice]]*
   - Slay [[Tserrina Syl'Tor (Ossuary)|Tserrina Syl'Tor]] in [[Ossuary: The Altar of Malice (Raid)|*Ossuary: The Altar of Malice*]]
   - Slay The [[Construct of Malice]] in [[Ossuary: The Altar of Malice (Raid)|*Ossuary: The Altar of Malice*]]
   - Slay [[Arch Lich Rhag'Zadune (Epic)|Arch Lich Rhag'Zadune]] in [[Ssraeshza Temple: Echoes of Time (Raid)|*Ssraeshza Temple: Echoes of Time*]]
   - Slay [[The Protector of Bones]] in [[Ossuary: Cathedral of Bones (Raid)|*Ossuary: Cathedral of Bones*]]
   - Slay [[Primordial Ritualist Villandre V'Zher]] in [[Ossuary: Cathedral of Bones (Raid)|*Ossuary: Cathedral of Bones*]]
