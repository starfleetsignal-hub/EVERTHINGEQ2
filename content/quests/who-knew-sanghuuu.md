## Steps

Little did I know there'd be yet another task waiting for me from the Sang’Huuu, who describe even more dangers threatening their way of life. What's another week?

- Crush the Grand Conjurer within Vale Invasion
  - zone [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] - Instance: [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion (Solo)|Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion [Solo]]] at {{waypoint -797, 174, 194}}
  - Confront the Grand Conjuror - Kusala'Din at {{waypoint -748, 63, 416}} He will summon Magmalatorr.
  - Confront the Grand Conjuror a second time at {{waypoint -557, 79, 678}} He will summon Tkesh'Tura
  - Use the teleporter at {{waypoint -790, 84, 620}}
  - Confront the Grand Conjuror for the last time.
- Destroy The Diseased Harbinger within Marshlands Miasma
  - zone [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] - Instance: [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma (Solo)|Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma [Solo]]] at {{waypoint 600, 85, 327}}
  - Confront The Diseased Harbinger - Miasmasula at {{waypoint 791, 41, 25}}
- Kill the Prime Invaders within Wisdom's Plight
  - Zone : [[Zimara Breadth]] - Instance : [[Zimara Breadth: Wisdom's Plight (Solo)|Zimara Breadth: Wisdom's Plight [Solo]]] at {{waypoint 310.27, 166.36, -82.20}}
- Massacre Ihe Maze Master within Every Which Way
  - Zone : [[Zimara Breadth]] - Instance : [[Vaashkaani: Every Which Way (Solo)|Vaashkaani: Every Which Way [Solo]]] at {{waypoint -490.57, 162.15, -603.38}}
- End the Electrifying within [[Aether Wroughtlands: Chaotic Currents (Solo)|Aether Wroughtlands: Chaotic Currents [Solo]]] at {{waypoint -363, 139, 438}}

## Rewards

- At least 10p 77g 75s 36c 138,599 status
- 30 [[Zimaran Ballad]]

On entry to each of these five instances it will trigger the associated **Firmament Hand:** daily quest once per day. Each of these quests will reward:

- At least 4p 31g 10s 14c 67,200 status
- 2 [[Zimaran Ballad]]

Therefore completion of this quest alone could deliver:

- At least 32p 33g 26s 94c 474,999 status
- 40 [[Zimaran Ballad]]
