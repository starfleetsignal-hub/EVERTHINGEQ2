## Notes

- If you have an evil character, you will have to right-click the NPC merchants and choose "hail" to continue/complete the quest.

## Steps

1. Answer [[Miella Maarsitotts]] questions.
1. Talk to [[Danaria Redvine]], [[Jysolin Nerala]], [[Paiva Kapanen]] and [[Zilender Pilmaartuseveral]] in [[Jysolin's Pub]]. {{waypoint 215, 109, 357}}
1. Offer the glass of Faerlie Ale that Jysolin gives you to Tunare at [[An old stone memorial]], at the base of [[Tunare's Sapling]]. {{waypoint -32, -10, 43}}
1. Return to Miella Maarsitotts in [[Kelethin]].

## Rewards

- Proficiency in [[Faerlie]].
