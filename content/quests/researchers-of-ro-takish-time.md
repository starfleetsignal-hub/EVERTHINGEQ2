*I must travel to the Takish Badlands to set up a research outpost to investigate changes in the local resources due to the massive infusion of Growth.*

## Materials Required

- You must harvest: (Already owning or buying from the broker does not count) [note: I brought the Echeveria Root with me.  The harvests listed @ step 2 below were sufficient for me.]
  - 1 [[Echeveria Root]] (rare)
  - 20 [[Apatite Gem]]
  - 20 [[Brittlebush Taproot]]
  - 35 [[Desert Willow Wood]]
  - 15 [[Dustmaw Hide]]
  - 15 [[Pallasite Ore]]
- Bring along this fuel (or use a summon fuel merchant item):
  - 15 [[Astral Coal]]
- Bring along these portable workstations (or use the summon workstation AAs):
  - [[Portable Work Bench v2.0]]

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Hamdi]]
   1. Zone into the [[Takish Badlands]] by using the red carpet from [[Goldami Reservoir Camp]] in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]] {{waypoint -533.28, 3.57, 472.58}}.
   1. Speak to [[Hamdi]] at {{waypoint 646, 155, -693}}
1. Harvest the following in [[Takish Badlands]] *or [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]]*:
   - 30 [[Apatite Gem]]
   - 30 [[Brittlebush Taproot]]
   - 30 [[Desert Willow Wood]]
   - 30 [[Dustmaw Hide]]
   - 30 [[Pallasite Ore]]
1. Return to [[Hamdi]], who gives you the auto-scribing [[Recipe Scroll: Madman's Rest Equipment]]
1. Make the Madman's Research Equipment on a summoned/portable work bench. You will need:
   - 1 [[Echeveria Root]] (rare)
   - 20 [[Apatite Gem]]
   - 15 [[Brittlebush Taproot]]
   - 5 [[Astral Coal]]
1. Make the Desert Loom on a summoned/portable work bench. You will need:
   - 5 [[Brittlebush Taproot]]
   - 15 [[Dustmaw Hide]]
   - 15 [[Desert Willow Wood]]
   - 5 [[Astral Coal]]
1. Make the Desert Woodworking Table on a summoned/portable work bench. You will need:
   - 20 [[Desert Willow Wood]]
   - 15 [[Pallasite Ore]]
   - 5 [[Astral Coal]]
1. Place the equipment by clicking the green sparkles next to Hamdi at {{waypoint 644, 155, -693}}  ![Samples of local flora](images/Researchers_of_Ro_Takish_Time_quest_-_Samples_of_local_flora.jpg)
1. Pick local flora:
   - {{waypoint 638, 155, -652}}
   - {{waypoint 540.59, 139.14, -688.80}}
   - {{waypoint 493.5, 140.91, -647.72}}
1. Speak to [[Hamdi]] to complete the quest. You will receive the auto-scribing recipe [[Recipe Scroll: Sandstone Delta Equipment]] at the end.
