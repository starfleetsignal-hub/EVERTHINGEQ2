## Steps

1. Vegk wants you to search through piles of slag and debris in [[Gardens of Industry]] first floor {{waypoint 285, 66, -54}}, second floor {{waypoint 256.05, 94.90, 187.56}} and third floor reachable with the jump pad at {{waypoint 279.50, 99.76, -46.08}} for some usable materials.
   - *Note: the piles of slag and mounds of debris are 2 separate objects to click on.*
   - ![A pile of slag containing abysium flakes](images/A_pile_of_slag.png)Search through piles of slag for 8 abysium flakes
   - ![A mound of debris containing oblivionite dross](images/Mound_of_debris_containing_oblivionite_dross.png)Search through mounds of debris for 7 oblivionite dross
1. Return to Vegk.

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
