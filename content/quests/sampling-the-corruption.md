## Steps

1. kill Nightblood invader (there's a solo invader that roams around this area) {{waypoint 704, -34, 418}}
1. Kill 10 [[Corrupted Growth essence]]s. {{waypoint 445, -38, 451}}
1. Return to [[Inara]]. {{waypoint 595, 66, -34}}

- At least 19g 40s 30c
