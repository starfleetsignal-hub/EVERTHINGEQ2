## Description

The elder Libant are amassing inside the walls of Evernight Abbey. Should they organize the younger members they might pose a significant threat to any major populous on Norrath. I've been asked to enter Evernight Abbey, find the Libant elder sweordwegend, slay them and return to Tanya Alvernon for a reward.

## Steps

1. Slay 10 Libant elder sweordwegends within [[Evernight Abbey]]
1. Return to [[Tanya Alvernon]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
