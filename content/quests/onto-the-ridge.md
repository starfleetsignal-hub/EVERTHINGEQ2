## Steps

1. Kill and collect the following:![Ancient Rune Stone](images/Ancient_Rune_Stone.png)
   - Kill 8 Snowhorn Mammoth around {{waypoint 96, 96, -305}}
   - Collect 9 Ancient Rune stones in the same area - very small flat iced over stones
1. Find Thekror who has now moved to Hoarfang Ridge camp at {{waypoint -472, 123, -325}}
1. Speak to Thekror
   - *You receive [[Wand of Vrakorr Elemental Destabilization]].*
1. Defeat 6 creatures in the Icespine area {{waypoint 429, 104, -477}} after weakening them with [[Wand of Vrakorr Elemental Destabilization]]
1. Speak to Thekror at  Hoarfang Ridge camp at {{waypoint -472, 123, -325}}

## Rewards

- [[Drakiz Runic Jar]] (house item)
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
