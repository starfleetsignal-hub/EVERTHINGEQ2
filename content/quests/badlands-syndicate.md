## Notes

- The quests [[Origins of Soil]] and [[Origins of Earth]] must be completed for the book to be clickable.

## Steps

1. Catalogue the Badlands Syndicate in Vegarlson, the Earthen Badlands.
   - Catalogue the Arborian Princess, [[Telrisa, of the Trees]] located at {{waypoint 1334, 465, 39}} or at {{waypoint 1428, 635, 59}}
   - Catalogue the Korascian Legend, [[Spinetongue]] {{waypoint -137, 12, 757}}
   - Catalogue the Protector of Earth, [[Gemmed Guardian]] {{waypoint -286.78, 37.28, -232.09}}
   - Catalogue the Elemental Queen, [[Glorpa the Ancient]] {{waypoint 416.23, 82.45, -92.33}}
   - Catalogue the Ancient Vekerchiki Champion, [[Jabloz]] {{waypoint -208.10, 109.90, 136.06}}
   - Catalogue the Muddite Builder, [[Glorok of the Mud]] {{waypoint 999, 63, 39}}
   - Catalogue the Dark Mover, [[Gharitsnel]] {{waypoint 640, 14, -450}}
   - Catalogue the Terrak King, [[Voltorak Razorclaw]] {{waypoint 255, 227, -98}}
1. Defeat a member of the Badlands Syndicate.
