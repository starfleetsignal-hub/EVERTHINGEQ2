## Steps

1. Pick up plants inside of the first cave area of Forgotten Pools
1. Click on the standing monuments near Terig The Lost {{waypoint -51, 16, -342}}
1. Speak with the spirit of Bjoron Axefall (in same room)
1. Return to Ragna Orcsmasher

## Rewards

- At least 45g
