## Steps

1. I need to learn about Tiding of Jing from [[Tai Wei]] {{waypoint 641, 334, 541}}.
   - Complete the subquest [[Smoke Gets in Your Eyes]].
1. I need to learn about Tiding of Hui from [[Bai Jin]] {{waypoint 522, 332, 521}}
   - Complete the subquests [[Walking the Central Path: Part One]] and [[Walking the Central Path: Part Two|Two]].
1. I need to learn about Tiding of Xue from [[Wen Wen]] {{waypoint 522, 334, 480}}.
   - Complete the subquests offered by [[Wen Wen]] and [[Stalking the Stalkers]] [[Xue Chao]] {{waypoint 591, 335, 530}}.
1. Return to [[Zhen Gao]].
