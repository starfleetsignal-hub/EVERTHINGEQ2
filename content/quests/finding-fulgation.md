## Steps

![A small rock, barely visible in the cleft of the cliff face. Right click it to "hook" the rock and climb the cliff.](images/Rocktohook.jpg)![Remmy's cooking pot at the base of the final cliff climb.](images/Remmyscookingpot.jpg)![Remmy Fulgation's Field Journal, to the left of the bedroll in his makeshift camp atop the cliff wall near Camp Cobalt.](images/Remmysfieldjournal.jpg)

1. Search for any sign of Remmy Fulgation near [[Camp Cobalt]] {{waypoint -388, 102, 1040}}
   - Inside Camp Cobalt you will find "An Ownerless Bag". Open and receive [[Day log of R.F.]] and [[Fulgation's Grappling Hook]] This will advance the quest.
   - Read the Day log of R.F. (right click it in your inventory and select 'read')
1. Find a way on top of the hill near [[Camp Cobalt]] where Remmy's field journal is.
   - There is a cliff at {{waypoint -379, 113, 986}} that has a stone at the top.
   - Right click the stone and hit "Hook It".
   - There is one more to hook, just above Remmy's cooking pot.
   - [[The Journal of Remy Fulgation]] is the small book right next to the bedroll, labeled Fulgation's Field Journal. Click it to pick it up (you might have to crouch and zoom in to see it).
   - To get down, right click the stone and hit "lower yourself down".
1. Return to [[Watchman Plarg]] for your reward.
