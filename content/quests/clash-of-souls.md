## Steps

1. Use the [[Shrunken Gazer Eye]] to absorb the essence of the fearstruck soullights (6)
   - *Note:Soullights must be under 50 percent health to absorb.*
1. The essence has been absorbed, and your weapon should be ready. Find the creature in the Fearcreep and destroy it!
   - [[Selgaad the Ruinous]] {{waypoint -1551, 224, 464}}
1. Return to [[Vylsith]] {{waypoint -1353, 218, 585}}

## Rewards

- [[Fear-Touched Umbra]]
- One of the Following:
  - [[Gossamer Ring of the Cavalier]]
  - [[Gossamer Ring of the Thaumaturge]]
  - [[Gossamer Ring of the Order]]
  - [[Gossamer Ring of the Sentry]]
