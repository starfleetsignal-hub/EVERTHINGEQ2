## Steps

1. Travel to [[Antonica]] and kill [[Meeglash Darkpaw]] who will spawn near [[Archer's Wood]] at {{waypoint -831, -8, 742}}.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- AA Experience
- +3000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -8000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
