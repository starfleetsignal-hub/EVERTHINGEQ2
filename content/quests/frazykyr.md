## Steps

1. Kill [[Assault Captain Frazykyr]] in a tower in [[Thulwun Station West]]. {{waypoint 1326, 198, 182}}
1. Return to [[Secondary Terkenil Niba'Xi]].

## Rewards

- At least 11s 40c
- [[Gorowyn Destrier Whistle]]
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Forged Dagger]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Forged Knuckles]] (Brawlers, Beastlord)
  - [[Forged Mace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Forged Staff]] (All Mages)
  - [[Forged Sword]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
