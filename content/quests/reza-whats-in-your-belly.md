- {{waypoint -56.29, 9.07, 65.28}}
- {{waypoint -58.23, 12.23, -92.41}}

- {{waypoint 103.76, 8.24, 12.18}}
- {{waypoint 131.53, 6.27, 28.61}}
- {{waypoint 188.27, 6.15, 108.37}}

## Steps

1. Kill 10 lizard type creatures
   - Updates include: [[A muck dreg]], [[Greenblood dreg|a greenblood dreg]], and [[A carrion dreg]]
1. Kill 10 dragonfly type creatures
   - Updates include: [[Wetland damselfly|a wetland damselfly]], [[A marsh damselfly]],and [[Marsh deathwing|a marsh deathwing]]
1. Kill 10 snake type creatures
   - Updates include: [[A swamp constrictor]], [[A brush nightstalker]], and [[A brush constrictor]]
1. Return to [[Reza]]

## Reward

- At least 1g 10s 97c
- [[Alternate Advancement]] Experience. (First Completion Only)
- 2x of [[Reza's Rations]] (Not on every completion)
