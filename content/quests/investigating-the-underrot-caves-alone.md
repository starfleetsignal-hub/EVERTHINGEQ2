## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- Grants access to the instance [[The Underrot Caverns: Moldy Crypt]]. You must get this quest each time you want to enter the solo (Alone) version of the instance.
- Your alignment does not matter. You can receive both quests and even have both versions in your journal at the same time.

## Steps

- There are no steps involved. Entering the instance closes the quest.
- The entrance is in [[Nektulos Forest]], located downstream from [[Soul Eater Falls]] on the east side of the river. {{waypoint 349.86, 3.69, -1274.87}}

## Rewards

- The reward is the loot in the instance. See [[The Underrot Caverns: Moldy Crypt]]
