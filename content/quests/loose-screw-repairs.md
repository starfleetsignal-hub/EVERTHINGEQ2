[[Triad of Elements (Solo)]] is available only during [[Summer Jubilee]] world event, while [[Tinkerfest]] or [[Scorched Sky]] or [[Oceansfull Festival]] events are active.

## Description

While battling the clockwork invasion of different planes in Triad of Elements, I found an incomplete clockwork repair kit. Clearly, someone is trying to aid this mechanical invasion! I should seek out the missing repair kit items in hopes of tracking down the culprit.

## Steps

1. Loot [[Incomplete Clockwork Repair Kit]] from small chests dropping from random mobs in [[Triad of Elements (Solo)]]
1. Loot necessary tools from additional chests. In order: [[Angled Electro-spanner]], a [[Propulsion Timing Augmentron]], [[Engraved Gizmatic Screwdriver]].
1. Return to [[Moors of Ykesha]] and look for VIXX at {{waypoint 1745, 452, 934}}.

## Rewards

One of the following:

- [[Clockwork Bronto Beetle Plushie]]
- 4 x [[Mithril Jubilation Medal]] (current year's currency)
