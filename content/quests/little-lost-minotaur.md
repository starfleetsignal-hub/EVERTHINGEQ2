## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A crypt tempest|crypt tempest]]s. {{waypoint 76, 0, -23}}
1. Return to Torg Bramblehair
   - **Note**: After you finish the conversation (and the quest) with Torg his pet minotaur [[Spike]], a level 27^^^ minotaur, will spawn behind you. It will aggro, but you don't need to kill it as a part of the quest. However, since you can't run without aggroing rooms full of level 21-25 mobs, this pretty much means you will die at the end of this quest if you don't have a group capable of taking down Spike. You can run to the nearest entrance and escape through it to avoid the wrath of Spike.
