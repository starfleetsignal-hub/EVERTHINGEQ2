## Notes

- [[Scarytale Ending]] needs the zone [[Mahngavi Wastes: Phantasmal Shades (Solo)|Mahngavi Wastes: Phantasmal Shades [Solo]]] as well

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Plibiik]] {{waypoint 2, 95, 580}} and receive the [[Muramite Emerald]]. ![Muramite Emerald](images/Muramiteemerald.png)
1. Enter [[Mahngavi Wastes: Phantasmal Shades (Solo)|Mahngavi Wastes: Phantasmal Shades [Solo]]] {{waypoint 639, 48, -449}} Run the Zone
   - after killing [[Esmerelda Everghast]] *final named of the zone* speak with Ballentree's ghost minion [[Devorra]] {{waypoint -546, 21, -413}}
1. Enter [[Akashic Neverthere: Ballentree Repository]] (Forced Solo Zone) in [[Mahngavi Wastes]] {{waypoint 373, 111, -32}}![Akashic Portal](images/Akashic_portal.png)
   - Recover [[Tavian Faust|Tavian Faust's]] stolen memory *(random blue shiny on ground.)*
   - Obtain the **Golden Skull** (usually at {{waypoint -208, 21.22, 169.6}} inside building).
1. Exit instance and Bury the golden skull in the cemetery in [[Mahngavi Wastes]] ![Blessed Dirt Marking Location for Skull Burial](images/Blessed_dirt.png)
   - *Look for upturned dirt with a gold sparkle effect.* Some possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -37, 97, 583}}
     - {{waypoint -31, 98, 677}}
     - {{waypoint -72, 96, 673}}
1. Return the memory to [[Tavian Faust]] at {{waypoint -184, 104, 687}}.
1. Speak with [[Dhusala]] at {{waypoint 18, 99, 569}}.
