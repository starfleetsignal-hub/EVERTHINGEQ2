## Steps

1. Talk to Corsair G`kex on the ship at the docks in  South Freeport to receive this City Task.
1. Kill [[Magma wormling|magma wormlings]] in [[Lavastorm]] on the beach.
1. Return to [[Corsair G`kex]].

## Rewards

- 5840 status at level 41
- +500 faction with **The Seafury Buccaneers**
