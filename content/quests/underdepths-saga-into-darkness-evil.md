## Steps

1. Speak with [[Viti Bipklang]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]] at {{waypoint 26.23, 3.68, 310.97}}.
1. Click the tinkered portal-gate next to him.
1. Investigate the nearby city.
   - Speak to [[Gwynnura]] at {{waypoint 616, 87, 75}}.
1. Help with the harvesting efforts at the [[Ironblood Fungus Farm]].
   - Speak to [[Grygrim the Tender]] {{waypoint 777, 56, -213}} and complete his quest, [[Not So Tender Touch]].
1. Return to [[Virun D'Xyafin (Thalumbra)|Virun D'Xyafin]] near the portal {{waypoint 470, 95, 66}} and speak with him.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g 33394 status
