## Description

"Priestess Xaliea, a dark elf near Ebb Tide's Alehouse, is unhappy that she is stuck mending wounds while she would rather be out adventuring. In order to live vicariously through my adventures, she has asked me to bring her three bone chips from some of the local skeletons." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill undead pirates ([[A shipwrecked explorer|shipwrecked explorers]] and [[A shipwrecked swashbuckler|shipwrecked swashbucklers]]) around {{waypoint -91.68, 0, -36.57}} to obtain 3 bone chips.
1. Return to Priestess Xaliea for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Bone Bracelet]]
