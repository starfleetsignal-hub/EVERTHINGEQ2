## Steps

1. Obtain 10 *golem samples* from [[A Rime icebound battler|Rime icebound battlers]].
   - These spawn as you run through [[Traitor's Pass]]. Do not fly through this area, you must be on the ground for them to spawn. Simply run along Traitor's Pass and you will be attacked by them every 20 meters or so. Some confirmed spawn locations are:
     - {{waypoint 29, 16, -949}}
     - {{waypoint -845, -108, -493}}
     - {{waypoint -634, -25, -1231}}
     - {{waypoint -45, 3, -1039}}
1. Return to [[Vixna Slizzis]] at the [[Upper Command Camp]].

## Rewards

- At least 41g 63s 17c
- +1,000 faction with **Legion of Danak**
