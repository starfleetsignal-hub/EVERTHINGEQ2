## Steps

1. Travel to [[Fens of Nathsar]] and use the [[Catalogue Creature]] ability to catalog:
   - Catalog [[A Yha-lei hydromancer]] around {{waypoint 416, -101, 835}}
   - Catalog [[A Yha-lei mindbender]] around {{waypoint 416, -101, 835}}
   - Catalog [[A Nathsarian frog-biter]] around {{waypoint -466, -118, 1295}}
   - Catalog [[A wild sokokar (Fens of Nathsar)]] around {{waypoint -1203, -133, 1445}}
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]

## Hints

The creatures are trackable.  If you don't have tracking, many ranger mercs can track, for instance [[Villax Sned]] from the VoV expansion can track.

- Unlock the Days of Summer 2022: Week 4 Holiday event rewards that you can purchase from [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2293, -199, 2506}}
