## Description

"Princess Zhyleen would like me to collect some glade shrubling samples from around Tunare's Glade. The guardians of the glade feed on these shrublings, and Princess Zhyleen would like them studied for impurities that may have led to the strange behavior by the guardian." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Harvest 8 glade shrublings in [[Tunare's Glade]]
1. Return to [[Princess Zhyleen]]

## Rewards

- At least 92c
- Experience

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Princess Zhyleen: "I am Zhyleen, welcome to Tunare's Glade."
- Player Character: "I am [Player Character]. Lexxiuna sent me."
- Princess Zhyleen: "Good. I am glad you have arrived. Not long ago one of the glade guardians turned on its caretaker. This is... well, I'm sure I don't need to tell you how concerning this is."
- Player Character: "What can I do to help?"
- Princess Zhyleen. "Well, we would like to figure out what has caused this corruption. There has been talk of this... this poison being given to the glade guardians through the food they ingest: the glade shrublings. If you could collect some, it would be helpful."
- Player Character: "I will collect some."
- Princes Zhyleen: "You can find them all around Tunare's Glade."
- Player Character: "Okay."

I need to collect some "glade shrubling" samples for Zhyleen.

I have collected the shrub samples. I need to bring these samples to Zhyleen.

- Princess Zhyleen: "Have you collected the shrublings?"
- Player Character: "Yes, here you go."

I have given Zhyleen the samples.
