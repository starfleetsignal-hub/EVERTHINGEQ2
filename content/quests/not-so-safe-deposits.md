## Notes

- *Completing* this quest voids the ability to pick up [[Freesia Lastchain|Freesia Lastchain's]] quest.
- To start [[Freesia Lastchain|Freesia Lastchain's]] quest you have to do parts of this one.
- When you started [[Crime Pays Someone]] but want to do [[Lend a Helping Hand]] instead, then you can delete the [[Crime Pays Someone]] quest and pickup either of them. You still can't do both but change your selection that way.

## Steps

1. Slip the bag of homing gold in the shipment of bank goods in the [[Luclin Nexus]] {{waypoint 253, -36, 702}}![Bank Lockbox in the Nexus](images/Bank_Lockbox_in_the_Nexus.jpg).
1. Deliver homing gold piece to [[Carth Quesnel]] at the [[Great Artisan Hall]] in [[The Bazaar]] {{waypoint -105, -1, 6}}.
1. Turn on Carth's homing drone {{waypoint -99, -1, 4}}![Quenel's Homing Drone II](images/Quenel's_Homing_Drone_II.jpg).
   - *Note: This is a small clockwork that will probably appear behind you, not the tinkered crafting station.*
1. Follow the homing drone to {{waypoint -272, -1, -40}}.
1. Confront and kill [[Luthaz L'Xak]].
1. Now you *can*, as an alternative, start the quest [[Crime Pays Someone]] at [[Freesia Lastchain]] {{waypoint -276, -1, -18}}. **WARNING:** As [[Freesia Lastchain]] says, you will not be able to finish the original quest line.<br>**OR**<br>Return to [[Principa Haleene Sirinae]] and finish this quest and continue in this quest line.

  - [[Garnet Balanzite Blade]]
