The name of this quest appears exactly as it does in game, misspelling and all.

This quest is part 4 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Erudite Series.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Sevri Il'Bethod]] who is standing next to the mailbox, outside the inn {{waypoint 2, 0, -36}}.
1. Go to the grate at the north side of the fountain plaza {{waypoint 40, -4, -59}}. When you approach this grate a dialogue window will appear.
1. Select "hold out the herbs and begin the chant".
1. Select the right words of the chant. They are: Kah, Shee-Jah, Ver-ata, Nik-Su, Gej-raz!

1. Return to [[Vess Al'Ishni]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[The Freeport Citizen's Guide to Opportunity - 2nd Edition]]
- [[Abbatoir Warding Amulet]]
- At least 1s
