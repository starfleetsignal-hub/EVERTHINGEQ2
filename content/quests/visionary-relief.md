This is not a normal collection quest. The collection members are not obtained by harvesting shinies.<br>
Instead, the collection members are themselves all obtained from the completion of other collection quests. This is called a "meta-collection".

## Rewards

- [[Small Stack of Draconic Etyma]]
- [[Dungeon Crawl Trinket]] this item is needed for the collection: [[Ethereal Explorer]]
- One of the following
  - [[Tainted High Keep Tower Shield]]
  - [[Vesspyr Warrior's Elaborate Bronze Sleeves]]
