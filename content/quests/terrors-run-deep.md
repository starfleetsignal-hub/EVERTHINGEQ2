## Notes

- For the **[Solo]** version of the zone players must be at least level 120 and wearing at least the free gear obtained from [[Tishan's Lockbox (Svarni Expanse)|Tishan's Lockbox]] from the latest expansion available to them, however it is recommended that you wear appropriate solo instance gear or better.
- As of 2023 there is now a **scaling zone available for players level 1 - 119** that updates this quest, named just [[Awuidor: Trench of Terrors (Solo)|Awuidor: Trench of Terrors [Solo]]], but the collection quest Fish Food is not available in this zone. The scaled zone has its own exclusive collection  [[Sounds of The Sea]] (2 pieces only per instance). Nothing special is required to  kill the bosses and no harvests exist there.
- Swimming up and down water columns is required and the ability to breathe underwater will assist. [[Totem of the Otter]], [[Fishbone Earring]] or an [[Oceansfull Fishing Rod (Recipe Book)|Oceansfull Fishing Rod]] may assist characters that don't have access to appropriate spells or racial ability.
- Two [[Gnomish Parachute Pack|Gnomish Parachute Packs]] or the [[Cloak of the Harvester]] can assist in avoiding some trash Mobs to provide a minor speed up for completion of the zone.
- Once this quest has been completed for the first time, it can be repeated for coins and five Collectables by entering the zone after resetting the timer.
- After completing the quest for the first time, it is automatically scribed into your journal whenever you enter the zone, but only once every 18 hours.
- You need to complete this quest six times to get the [[Awuidor's Aww'right]] achievement which unlocks [[Golden F'Aestival Bracers]] to increase their base 340 Resolve up by 30 to **370 Resolve**, plus increase Potency by 5,255.0%
- This walkthrough is written to optimize the zone for a Solo character or a group of two low level characters. Two high level characters may be able to run the two towers and collect Pearls in parallel.
- **Important!**  When all 5 nameds in the zone are defeated, evac to front and take the transport all the way to the end. There you will get the last POI for the "Depth Charge" achievement, and if you kill all three epic hidden creatures, a collectable will spawn in that back area.  (Note: the collectible appears on top of one of the large corals you can climb from one side.) This rare will complete the "Fish Food" collection. You find the epic creatures by running close to the coral and rock looking things that will surprise turn into the creatures. If you forget/miss this step, you can zone back into an instance that has been cleared to get the collectable, if the epics were not yet defeated. Reminder: the scaled zone does not have the Fish Food collection. However, you can still get the Achievement by going to the back of the zone for the POI discovery (you don't need to kill the epics).

## Steps

1. Go to [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]] in [[The Great Divide]].
1. Speak to [[Forgemaster Gert]] at {{waypoint 914.37, -274.46, 122.90}}.
1. Leave the city by [[Fast Travel]] to the [[Dragon Ring]] (druid ring) in [[The Great Divide]], or exit via the north door {{waypoint 672.67, -244.99, 53.53}}.
1. Acquire eight Ferrovelium Shards from [[Reeling icicles]] around druid ring. They become attackable when coming close ![celestial aperture creator](images/Terrors_run_Deep_quest_-_celestial_aperture_creator.jpg)
1. Apply the Ferrovelium Shards and received armor to the **celestial aperture creator** at {{waypoint 1750.55, 448.20, 731.72}} in the [[Dropship Landing Zone]] of [[Moors of Ykesha]].
1. Enter the [[Awuidor: Trench of Terrors]] *([Solo], standard, or [Heroic])* portal at {{waypoint 1732, 446, 720}}
1. Hail the [[An Awuidor Sentry|Awuidor Sentry]] {{waypoint -1122.46, 390.01, 773.52}}.
1. Walk up the tail or jump onto the [[A manta ray|Manta Ray]] {{waypoint -1118.87, 391.92, 746.04}} then hail the [[An awuidor manta wrangler|Awuidor Manta Wrangler]]. The preferred position is on the wings of the [[A mantr ray|Manta Ray]] so that you can step off the Mantar Ray onto a top platform of [[The Nautilus Ark]], the main structure.<br>Warning, you will have considerable forward momentum when leaving the Manta Ray.
1. Jump off at the 1<sup>st</sup> platform (Southern). Optionally clear this platform of the [[Terms#monster|Mobs]].
1. There are two harvestable nodes in this zone. Examining harvested rare items (or Pearls) can return a character to the centre of the bridge at the base of the two towers of [[The Nautilus Ark]]. When running with two Characters in this Solo zone each character should carry a rare harvest to enable them to use this ability.
   1. [[Marine sediment]]. This will be thrown at the named Bosses to make them vulnerable to attack for a limited period. Low DPS characters should harvest all they can find. Very low DPS characters with the ability to heal and dispell/cure may have to clear multiple platforms to gain enough [[Marine sediment]] for each Boss fight. A high 'Bountiful Harvest' skill will assist.
   1. [[Fish Nodes|shoal of fish]] which when harvested produces all the non-rare, level fish plus the following rare items:
      1. The [[Oceansfull Fishing Rod (Recipe Book)]] which will enable the player to craft the [[Oceansfull Fishing Rod (Spell Scroll)]] allowing the caster to breathe under water but **only when out of Combat**.
      1. [[Coral Reef Sponge]], five are required for an [[Oceansfull Fishing Rod (Recipe Book)|Oceansfull Fishing Rod]].
      1. [[Marid Shell]], five are required for an [[Oceansfull Fishing Rod (Recipe Book)|Oceansfull Fishing Rod]].
      1. [[Nereid Hair]], one is required for an [[Oceansfull Fishing Rod (Recipe Book)|Oceansfull Fishing Rod]].
      1. [[Scrap of Bulthar Hide]], one is required for an [[Oceansfull Fishing Rod (Recipe Book)|Oceansfull Fishing Rod]].
      1. [[Woodfall Timber]], two are required for an [[Oceansfull Fishing Rod (Recipe Book)|Oceansfull Fishing Rod]].
1. [[Marine sediment]] is available on this platform and at the next location. Enough must be collected from one or both these locations before the next Boss fight.

1. From the top platform of the 1<sup>st</sup> tower take the [[Shell of the Ocean Pearl]] portal {{waypoint -1.42, 668.39, 216.75}} for the [[Ocean Pearl of Awuidor]].
1. [[Marine sediment]] may spawn in the pool below the entry platform, harvest if required.
1. Place the [[Marine sediment]] onto a Hot Key so that it can be thrown during named Boss fights.
1. Kill two [[A regua pipefitter|Regua Pipefitters]] in the water column to swim up to the top level.  There are two ways to navigate the water columns:-
   1. If you hold the space bar to use standard swimming keystrokes (e.g. 'Space Bar' or 'End Key' for up and down) it will take approximately 28 seconds to swim from the bottom to the top of the water column.
   1. If you use the first person view with the standard movement keystrokes (e.g. 'w' or 's' for forward and backwards movement) it will take approximately 14 seconds to swim from the bottom to the top of the water column.
1. Kill four [[A regrua umbraclaw|Regrua Umbraclaws]].
1. Kill [[Ripperclaw]] {{waypoint 2.55, 630.33, 1194.68}}.
   1. Only throw one [[Marine sediment]] at a time at the named bosses to make them vulnerable to attack. Don't throw multiple as this appears to be a waste.
   1. After "A huge wave washes away the marine sediment" narrative text a wave will become visible and sweep across the platform. After the wave clears the platform throw more [[Marine sediment]] at named bosses to make them again vulnerable to attack.
   1. A vortex will form on the surface then adds spawn. Positioning a character on top of this vortex will stop adds from spawning. Solo characters have to kill the named before 10 adds stack and kill the whole party. The stack count appears in small characters with the displayed Name's buffs. Spending more time killing adds to keep their numbers under control may consume more [[Marine sediment]].
1. Take the [[Ocean Pearl of Awuidor]] {{waypoint -0.28, 630.99, 1178.66}}.
1. Pick up an [[Oceansfull Festival]] [[Fish Food]] collectable that most likely spawns on the lower or middle level platforms.
   1. If the Collectable does not appear on the [[Track Harvestables]] list on the top platform it should spawn on the lowest level platform.
   1. Wisps to Collectables can mislead in this zone and require re-establishment from different locations.
1. Eight Braziers have to be lit, four associated with each tower. Each tower has two on this base level with two on their middle platforms.
   1. *(First 4)*<br>Return to [[The Nautilus Ark]] by examining the Pearl. This returns you to the of centre of the bridge {{waypoint -1.04, 356.41, 3.77}} on the base level that is the only connection between the two towers. Immediately place the Pearl into the appropriate Pod. If you place every received Pearl into its pod, and only use Pearls for travel then it should prevent forgetting to pick up a Pearl.

      1. Move North to the base of the 2<sup>nd</sup> water column {{waypoint -0.32, 337.57, -127.57}} then West.
      1. Kill the [[A barnacle bastion|Barnacle Bastion]] {{waypoint 233.38, 321.85, -130.85}} to light a [[A barnacle brazier|Barnacle Brazier]] (1/4).

      1. Kill the [[A Briny Bulwark|Briny Bulwark]] {{waypoint -239.04, 321.85, -128.70}} at the opposite side of the water column (on the same level) to light a [[A briny brazier|Briny Brazier]] (1/4).

      1. Swim up the 2<sup>nd</sup> water column and take the exit approximately halfway up the column and move West.
      1. Kill the [[A barnacle bastion|Barnacle Bastion]] {{waypoint 157.81, 489.81, -129.56}}to light a [[A barnacle brazier|Barnacle Brazier]] (2/4).

      1. Kill the [[A Briny Bulwark|Briny Bulwark]] {{waypoint -170.04, 489.81, -129.06}} on the same level to light a [[A briny brazier|Briny Brazier]] (2/4).

      1. Return to the water column and swim up to the top of the 2<sup>nd</sup> tower {{waypoint -1.16, 666.86, -129.74}}.
      1. From the top platform of the 2<sup>nd</sup> tower take the [[Shell of the Kelp Pearl]] portal {{waypoint -0.85, 668.39, -212.05}} for the [[Kelp Pearl of Awuidor]].
      1. Kill the two [[A bulthar pipefitter|Bulthar Pipefitters]] in the water column to access the top level. Swim up the column of water.
      1. Kill the four [[An oceanic commander|Oceanic Commanders]].
      1. Kill [[The Brack Fish]] {{waypoint 1.54, 630.33, -1213.43}} (He doesn't take damage unless you stand in the middle of the swirls. Hit with dots or use pets.)  Take the [[Kelp Pearl of Awuidor]] {{waypoint 2.92, 630.33, -1190.09}}.
      1. Pick up an [[Oceansfull Festival]] [[Fish Food]] collectable from the lower or middle level platforms.
   1. *(Second 4)*<br>Return to to the centre of the bridge on [[The Nautilus Ark]] by examining the Pearl. Place the Pearl into the appropriate Pod.

      1. Move South to the base of the 1<sup>st</sup> water column {{waypoint -2.07, 338.55, 136.64}} then West.
      1. Kill the [[A barnacle bastion|Barnacle Bastion]] {{waypoint 233.65, 321.85, 136.29}} to light a [[A barnacle brazier|Barnacle Brazier]] (3/4).

      1. Kill the [[A Briny Bulwark|Briny Bulwark]] {{waypoint -238.24, 321.85, 135.86}} on the same level of the 1<sup>st</sup> tower to light a [[A briny brazier|Briny Brazier]] (3/4).
      1. Swim up the 1<sup>st</sup> water colum and take the exit approximately halfway up the column.

      1. Kill the [[A barnacle bastion|Barnacle Bastion]] {{waypoint 169.65, 489.81, 135.78}} to light a [[A barnacle brazier|Barnacle Brazier]] (4/4). One or more [[A barnacle brazier|Barnacle Braziers]] have been missed if you don't receive the Narative text "A portal forms in the waters below the four barnacle braziers!"

      1. Kill the [[A Briny Bulwark|Briny Bulwark]] {{waypoint -170.10, 489.81, 138.20}} on the same level of the 1<sup>st</sup> tower to light a [[A briny brazier|Briny Brazier]] (4/4). One or more [[A briny brazier|Briny Braziers]] have been missed if you don't receive the Narative text "A portal forms in the waters below the four briny braziers!"

1. From behind the brazier {{waypoint -205.12, 490.14, 124.18}} jump down to the East pool at the bottom of the towers.
   1. Use a [[Gnomish Parachute Pack]] to glide over the trash Mobs to the portal. It is possible to glide too far and miss landing in the pool. Using standard movement keys (e.g. 'w', 'e', etc.) can slow/stop movement above the portal which then bypasses the trash Mobs in the pool.
   1. The portals are too far away to be initially visible so there is no warning if you have missed lighting a Brazier
   1. If you do have to return to [[The Nautilus Tower]] from the pool examine a rare item, pearl or use a waterfall.
1. Take the [[Shell of the Reef Pearl]] portal {{waypoint -465.14, 99.70, 3.87}}.<br>**NOTE:** If you can't see the portal that means you failed to light all four Braziers.
   1. Kill two [[A marid pipefitter|Marid Pipefitters]] to access the top level. Swim up the column of water.
   1. Kill the four [[A marid tidalsounder|Marid Tidalsounders]].
   1. Kill [[Brenick of the Brine]] {{waypoint -1257.45, 630.33, 19.13}} and take the [[Reef Pearl of Awuidor]] {{waypoint -1238.85, 630.33, 21.04}}.
   1. Pick up an [[Oceansfull Festival]] [[Fish Food]] collectable from the lower or middle level platforms.
1. Return to to the centre of the bridge on [[The Nautilus Ark]] by examining the Pearl. Place the Pearl into the appropriate Pod. If you have a [[Gnomish Parachute Pack]] you can glide from the centre of the bridge to the portal in the East pool.

1. Move South to the base of the 1<sup>st</sup> water column {{waypoint -2.07, 338.55, 136.64}} then West.
1. Behind the Barnacle Brazier {{waypoint 267.81, 322.98, 125.63}} jump down to the West pool at the bottom of the towers.
   1. Use a [[Gnomish Parachute Pack]] to glide over the trash Mobs to the portal.
1. Take the [[Shell of the Coral Pearl]] portal {{waypoint 465.14, 99.70, 3.87}}.<br>**NOTE:** If you can't see the portal that means you failed to light all four Braziers.
   1. Kill two [[A nereid pipefitter|Nereid Pipefitters]] to access the top level. Swim up the column of water.
   1. Kill the four [[A nereid coralkeeper|Nereid Coralkeepers]].
   1. Kill [[Pirelia the Pale]] {{waypoint 1253.37, 630.33, 23.32}} and take the [[Coral Pearl of Awuidor]] {{waypoint 1235.60, 631.07, 21.79}}.
   1. Pick up an [[Oceansfull Festival]] [[Fish Food]] collectable from the lower or middle level platforms.
1. Return to [[The Nautilus Ark]] by examining the Pearl then place the Pearl into the appropriate Pod.
1. Placing the 4<sup>th</sup> pearl will activate the central portal to the deep. Step off the activated portal so you can target the portal
1. Enter the 'To The Deep' portal. Kill all four [[A sentinel of the deep|Sentinels of the deep]] in each group to be able to engage their [[A summoner of the deep|Summoner of the Deep]]. Kill all four [[A summoner of the deep|Summoners of the Deep]].
   1. Attack [[Esodeax the Entrenched]] if required. [[Marine sediment]] is not required.
   1. Pick up the fifth [[Oceansfull Festival]] [[Fish Food]] collectable item before leaving.
1. After completing the quest once it will now terminate at this step.
1. Return to [[Mimmi]] in [[Nipik's Haven]] {{waypoint -1131, -533, 3370}} in the [[Eastern Wastes]] to claim the initial quest completion rewards.
