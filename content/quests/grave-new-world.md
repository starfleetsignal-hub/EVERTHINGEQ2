## Steps

1. Search [[Warpwood Cairnlands]] around {{waypoint 50.23, 111.46, -41.05}} for unusual undead activity. ![Severed Crawler](images/Severed_crawler.png)
   - Slay 8 [[A severed crawler|severed crawlers]] and [[A necrocraft brute|necrocraft brutes]]. ![Necrocraft Brute](images/Necrocraft_brute.png)
1. Search for the necromancer responsible in [[Mahngavi Wastes]].
   1. Find [[Salizar Ethaz (Mahngavi Wastes)|Salizar Ethaz]] around {{waypoint 190.51, 35.91, -432.96}}.
   1. Fight [[Salizar Ethaz (Mob)|him]] until he stops fighting
   1. talk to him
1. Return to [[Sukanya]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- 3 [[Warpwood Sapling]]
