## Description

I've found a wooden staff that looks like it has seen better days. The markings on its burnt and charred surface seem to hint at some purpose beyond a normal walking stick.

## Steps

1. Kill [[A suspended watcher|suspended watchers]] in the tunnel leading to and inside [[The Hold of Malice]] from [[The Pool of Percelia]] {{waypoint 5, 7, -97}} for 3 updates.
1. Kill [[A gloom snake|gloom snakes]] in the entrance tunnel {{waypoint -1, 12, 129.5}} and {{waypoint 6.5, 5.5, 90.5}} or in the first cavern west and north of [[The First Gate]] for 4 updates.  Note: a murkwurm from the Pool will also work.
1. Kill [[A tormented weaponsmith|tormented weaponsmiths]] found deep in [[Fallen Gate]] in [[Hate's Forge]] {{waypoint -14.5, -18.5, -328}} for 2 updates.

## Reward

- [[Scorched wood staff]]
