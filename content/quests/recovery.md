## Steps

1. Harvest **Creeping Fortune** {{waypoint -635, 14, 236}}. ![Creeping Fortune](images/Creeping_Fortune.jpg)
   - The spider-repellent effect lasts 15 minutes.
   - For fastest travel, take the Nightmare mount to the Steamfont Mountain Station, the plant grows very near there.
1. Find and retrieve Phillip's experiment. Harvest the carcass at {{waypoint -206, 32, 57}}.
1. Return to [[Philip Glaslow]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 13g 79s 50c
