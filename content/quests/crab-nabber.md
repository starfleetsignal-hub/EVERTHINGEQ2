Too many of these poor helpless (unless you count their giant claws) crabs are being hunted for their delicious meat. The hunter appears to be a Scourge orc by the looks of the large footprints all over the beach. I can use this conch to take the form of a crab and use the crab's senses to detect where the crabber is hiding. And when found, he'll be witness to the full destructive power of my pinchers!

## Notes

- ***Note:** This is a 10 minute timed quest.*
- Found in all versions of Zek, the Scourge Wastes:
  - [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Solo)]]
  - [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Heroic)]]

## Steps

1. Kill [[A coastal snapper|coastal snappers]] to get **a crab brain**.
   - Combine the conch and the crab brain.
   - Use the conch.
1. Go to the island in the middle of Siren's Cove and find an empty crab shell {{waypoint 175.53, -42.42, 399.75}}. Fight off the flock of [[A hungry seagull|hungry seagulls]].
1. Check [[Deadwood Refuge]] for the crabber. Find an empty crab shell {{waypoint -107.34, -30.84, 184.70}}. Fight off the flock of [[A hungry seagull|hungry seagulls]].
1. Check [[Timberlands]] for the crabber. Find an empty crab shell {{waypoint -446.60, -37.03, -39.09}}. Fight off the flock of [[A hungry seagull|hungry seagulls]].
1. Check [[Hounds' Gorge]] for the crabber. Find an empty crab shell {{waypoint -547.27, -27.97, -181.91}}. Fight off the flock of [[A hungry seagull|hungry seagulls]].
1. Check [[Scourge Valley]] for the crabber. Find an empty crab shell {{waypoint -13, -5, -321}}. Fight off the flock of [[A hungry seagull|hungry seagulls]].
1. Go atop the towering rock {{waypoint -31, 71, -374}} and kill the crabber.
   - When the crabbers spawn they may use knock-back on you. Do not let them push you off.
   - If you die, the crabbers despawn and you fail, so feather fall may be useful.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Encrusted iron key]]
