## Steps

1. Enter [[Qeynos Capitol District]].
1. **Within 30 minutes**, steal 5 payroll shipments. They are in sparkling chests found in the South Qeynos area along the waterway that has the globe/bell.
1. Return to [[Gol M'Tun]].

## Rewards

- Faction Only:
  - +3000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
