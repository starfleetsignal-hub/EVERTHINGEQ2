## Steps

1. Talk to [[Sedrard Jithir]] at {{waypoint -30, 61, -72}} (after the key mob), who tells you where to find Mri'An Ba'Ay
1. Find corpse of Mri'An Ba'Ay at {{waypoint -16, 42, -75}} in Officer's Quarters, near the end mob
1. Click an open book book on the chair at {{waypoint -6, 42, -56}}
1. Speak to Flight Master Captain Sedrard Jithir at {{waypoint 14, 42, -66}}, just around the corner in the room West from where the book is
1. Speak to Flight Warden [[Daecon Joh'N]] in Paineel {{waypoint 1864, -225, 2972}}

## Rewards

- At Least 90g 70s 61c

Access to [[The Vigilant: Rescue]]
