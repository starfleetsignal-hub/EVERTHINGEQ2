## Description

The Kerra Isle outfitter has requested the assistance of a fellow Outfitter in crafting more equipment for the village. She has warned me that non-outfitters will not be able to complete this assignment.

## Required Class

You must be an [[Outfitter]] to complete this quest.

## Steps

1. I have been asked to make some equipment for the villagers of Kerra Isle.
   - Weaponsmiths: Craft 5 weapons. Known items requested are [[Titanium Long Sword|Titanium Long Swords]] and [[Titanium Claws]].
     - 20 [[Titanium ore]]
     - 10 [[Mottled pelt]]
     - 5 [[Amber]]
     - 25 [[Ethereal coal]]
     - [[Forge]]
   - Tailors: Craft 5 armor pieces. Known items requested are [[Woven Mottled Boots]].
     - 10 [[Bamboo shoot]]
     - 20 [[Mottled pelt]]
     - 5 [[Titanium ore]]
     - 25 [[Ethereal filament]]
     - [[Sewing table]]
   - Armorers: Craft 5 armor pieces. Known items requested are [[Titanium Kite Shield]].
     - 20 [[Titanium ore]]
     - 10 [[Mottled pelt]]
     - 5 [[Amber]]
     - 25 [[Ethereal coal]]
     - [[Forge]]
1. Return to [[Harriet]] on Kerra Isle.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,000 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +2,500 faction with **Kerra Isle Outfitters**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Kerra Isle Daily Supply Tasks
