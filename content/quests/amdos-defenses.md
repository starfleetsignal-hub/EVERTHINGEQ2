## Steps

1. Steal 8 Order of Rime weapons
   - These are clickable weapon racks in the Order of Rime camps, Use your Order of Rime Shardhammer Illusion, if you got it from [[Infiltration System]], of course.
1. Return to [[Amdo]]

## Rewards

- At least 14g 90s 39c
- One of the following (Class-based):
  - [[Petrified Sequoia Longbow]] (fighter class)
  - [[Unnaturally Tense Shortbow]] (scout class)
  - [[Deacon's Symbol]] (priest class)
  - [[Symbol of the Seer]] (mage class)
  - [[Mammoth Hide Pouch]] (bruiser/monk)
