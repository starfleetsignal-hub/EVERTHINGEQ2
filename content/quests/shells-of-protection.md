## Steps

1. Obtain 8 hard plates for Brink from the following creatures on the beach east of [[The Nursery]]:
   - Obtain 4 shells by killing [[A clawsnap|clawsnap crabs]].
   - Obtain 4 shells by killing [[A sand sifter|sand sifter turtles]].
1. Return to [[Brink Trudger]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 65c
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Brinks Spaulders]]
  - [[Brink's Stitched Footwraps]]
  - [[Brink's Linked Sleeves]]
  - [[Brink's Leather Boots]]
  - [[Brink's Shoulderguards]] - Ranger
