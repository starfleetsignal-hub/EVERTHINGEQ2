## Steps

1. Collect 5 mystical tea leaves from [[Toxxulia Forest]].
   - These are found from non-targetable bushes near the Druid Ring {{waypoint 880, -53, 3428}}. They look pretty much like regular bushes but sparkle when you get close to them. NOTE: Click them fast once you get one to get TWO from one Bush. ![Tea bush](images/Leaves_of_Green_quest_-_tea_bush.jpg)
1. Return to Mushien Lou.

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +2000 faction with **Hua Mein**
