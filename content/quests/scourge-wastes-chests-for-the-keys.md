[[Zek, the Scourge Wastes Timeline#Treasure Hunt Quests|Treasure Hunt Quests]] in [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Solo)]] and [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Heroic)]] reward a [[Encrusted iron key]]. Unpacking that key yields a status loot item, and one of the following:

- [[Etched wrought iron key]] unlocks "a wrought iron treasure chest", which rewards advanced solo quality gear.
- [[Polished pyrite key]] unlocks "a pyrite treasure chest", which rewards heroic quality gear.
- [[Etched blood iron key]] unlocks "a blood iron treasure chest", which rewards raid quality gear.
  - *[[Ancient iron scraps]] can only be sold for status.*<br><br>

These chests spawn randomly in various locations throughout [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Solo)]] and [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Heroic)]].

- The [[Tracking]] ability can be used to locate these chests.
- *If your class/race can't track, consider hiring a mercenary with tracking and have him track for you.*
- Chests show up in track as even (white) at level 100. You can also just filter for "chest".<br><br>
