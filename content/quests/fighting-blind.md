## Steps

![Awakened weapon rack for "Fighting Blind"](images/Awakened_weapon_rack.jpg)

1. Slay 10 blinded [[An aerakyn skywatcher|aerakyn skywatchers]] on [[Droumlunds]].
1. Destroy 8 Awakened weapon racks. They can be shot at from a distance and, if lucky, will stay to be shot again. Some possible locations:
   - {{waypoint 173, 308, -894}}
   - {{waypoint 199, 311, -899}}
   - {{waypoint 204, 312, -901}}
   - {{waypoint 296, 315, -879}}
   - {{waypoint 381, 312, -899}}
   - {{waypoint 380, 316, -878}}
   - {{waypoint 310, 330, -875}}
   - {{waypoint 471, 325, -816}}
   - {{waypoint 501, 323, -837}}
   - {{waypoint 611, 317, -784}}
1. Return to Ireatan
