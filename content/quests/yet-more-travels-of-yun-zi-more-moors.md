## Steps

1. Find the following objects in Moors of Ykesha in the following order:
   1. A small cannon in dropship landing zone around {{waypoint 1677.15, 452.56, 805.79}} or {{waypoint 1774.90, 452.56, 799.36}} or {{waypoint 1849, 452, 891}} {{waypoint 1829, 452, 974}} {{waypoint 1679, 452, 859}} {{waypoint 1630, 452, 828}} {{waypoint 1720, 452, 930}}
   1. A green wooden totem in Tupta (same height as the frogloks) around  {{waypoint -31.68, -55.87, 793.96}} or {{waypoint -43.51, -52.59, 835.73}} or {{waypoint -27, -51, 827}} or {{waypoint -83.80, -51.83, 805.14}} or {{waypoint -80, -43, 755}}
   1. A plate of rats on Brokenskull Rock around {{waypoint -1810.06, -968.00, 2676.87}} or {{waypoint -1731.80, -968.00, 2702.07}} or {{waypoint -1683, -968, 2692}}
1. Return to [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]].
