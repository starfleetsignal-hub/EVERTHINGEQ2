Sister Leela Prendyn doesn't know why Priest Aaronolis Swornlove has not yet been awarded the honorable station as Erollisi Marr's prophet, despite Her victorious return.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Priest Aaronolis Swornlove]]
   - Kill five followers of Ullkorruk within [[Nektulos Forest]] {{waypoint -399, 6, -383}}
   - Kill five followers of Ullkorruk within [[Nektropos Castle]] {{waypoint -29, -1, 54}}
   - Kill five followers of Ullkorruk within [[Everfrost]] {{waypoint -58, -9, 64}}
   - Kill five followers of Ullkorruk within a cave in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] {{waypoint 380, 91, -195}}
   - *Note: the mobs to be slain include [[A priestess of Ullkorruuk|priestesses of Ullkorruuk]], [[A traitorous wayfarer|traitorous wayfarers]], [[A duplicitous wayfarer|duplicitous wayfarer]], [[A deceitful pilgrim|deceitful pilgrims]], etc*
1. Return to Sister Leela Prendyn to receive your rewards.

## Rewards

- +2,500 faction with **Erollisi Marr**
- One of the following:
  - [[Amulet of True Sight]]
  - [[Bracer of the Lost Soul]]
