## Description

"The changes to the City of Freeport can be seen at every turn.  One of the biggest changes was the relocation of inn rooms from the slums and the Blood Haze Inn to the Jade Tiger's Den.  There are rumors of conflicts within the slums that forced the move. Regardless of the reason, I should visit the Jade Tiger's Den to see these inn rooms myself." *-- Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Locate the Jade Tiger's Den and find the door for the basic inn room {{waypoint -90, -21, -84}}.  This step updates automatically once you reach the area.
1. Access the room.  Buying a room, visiting someone, or touring all update.

## Conclusion

"I visited the Jade Tiger's Den to see for myself how the inn rooms have changed.  Nothing was amiss in the room.  Based on the migration of the rooms out of the neighborhoods, this is another example that life in Freeport has gone from slums to riches." *-- Everquest II Journal*
