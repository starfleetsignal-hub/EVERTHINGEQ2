## Steps

Nothing is free and building, maintaining, and supporting the herald's camp is expensive. That's why the herald of Mithaniel Marr has requested that we assist in offsetting the camp's cost by retrieving a valuable relic guarded by Hasira the Hawk. I need to retrieve the relic.

1. Retrieve the relic guarded by Hasira the Hawk
   - Go to [[Aether Wroughtlands]] and zone into [[Aether Wroughtlands: Tarnished Oasis (Solo)|Aether Wroughtlands: Tarnished Oasis [Solo]]] {{waypoint -337.44, 43.88, -359.34}} and clear all nameds in this zone.  The chest with relic is at {{waypoint -742, 55, -418}}
   - *Note: [[Aether Wroughtlands: Tarnished Oasis (Heroic I)|Aether Wroughtlands: Tarnished Oasis [Heroic I]]] works as well*
   - *Note: Must be Level 128 to enter Instance.*
1. return to [[Herald of the Truthbringer]] {{waypoint -840, -48, 199}} in [[The Commonlands]]
