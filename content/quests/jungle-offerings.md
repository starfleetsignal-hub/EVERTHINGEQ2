## Steps

1. Gather the following
   - Gather 8 **pygmy totems** in the village ![Pygmy Totem](images/Pygmytotem.png)
     - At present these are spawning along the path down to [[Zuzlub]].
     - Other possible locations: {{waypoint 574, 148, -760}}, {{waypoint 554, 133, -701}}, {{waypoint 503, 123, -693}}, {{waypoint 478, 119, -650}}, {{waypoint 444, 113, -628}}, {{waypoint 433, 114, -645}}, {{waypoint 560, 137, -671}}, {{waypoint 461, 118, -664}}
   - Kill 6 [[A glade strider|glade striders]] for deino feathers ![Glade Strider](images/Gladestrider.png)
     - *Many in the [[Scaleclaw Killing Fields]] around {{waypoint 596, 60, 73}} and [[Deino Shores]] around {{waypoint -59, 6, 547}}.*
   - Gather 4 **scaleclaw egg shells** in [[Scaleclaw Killing Fields]]. Some possible locations: ![Scaleclaw Egg Shells](images/Scaleclaweggs.png)
     - {{waypoint 511, 71, 45}}, {{waypoint 656, 64, 113}}, {{waypoint 591, 58, 175}}, {{waypoint 562, 25, 320}}
1. ![Semi-Circle of Stones](images/Semi-Circle_of_Stones.jpg)Place the items in the semi-circle of stones {{waypoint 570, 37, 497}}.
1. Speak with [[Jungle Quintessence]] that spawns.
   - Hunt 8 [[A glade strider|glade striders]] (see locations above).
   - Hunt 10 [[A deepwood ravager|deepwood ravagers]] in the [[Deepwood Wilds]] around {{waypoint -470, 18, 325}}.![Deepwood Ravager](images/Deepwoodravager.png)
1. Return to [[Jungle Quintessence]] {{waypoint 570, 37, 497}}

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- [[Karuupa Village Hut]]
