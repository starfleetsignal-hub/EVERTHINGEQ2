## Description

"Princess Zhyleen would like me to investigate the mushroom platform in the north eastern area of Tunare's Glade. I am to bring back any evidence I find to her." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Examine the chemistry set in [[Tunare's Glade]] at {{waypoint -250.41, 91.47, 493.24}}
1. Return to [[Princess Zhyleen]]

## Rewards

- At least 92c

- Princes Zhyleen: "Have you collected the shrublings?"
- Player Character: "Yes, here you go."
- Princess Zhyleen: "These will do nicely. Hm. I can already see something's not right, this greenish powder shouldn't be here."
- Player Character: "What is it?"
- Princess Zhyleen: "I do not know. It reeks of high-cap though."
- Player Character: "High-cap?"
- Princess Zhyleen: "Direct your gaze to the north-east. There upon the trunk of that tree you can see a mushroom platform; it is referred to by some as high-cap. Whatever this powder is, it was at one point on that high-cap, the smell gives it away."
- Player character: "What should we do?"
- Princess Zhyleen: "I'd like you to climb up there and investigate."
- Player Character: "All right.
- Princess Zhyleen: "Bring whatever you find back to me."
- Player Character: 'Okay."

Zhyleen would like me to investigate the "high-cap mushroom platform in northeastern Tunare's Glade.<blockquote>This is a makeshift alchemical lab. Zhyleen's theory was correct.

- Princess Zhyleen: "Did you find anything?"

- Princess Zhyleen: "Orcs... Why must they meddle? Hm, this missive is old. This was before Mentha's work in this area. These orcs may not even be around to be punished, and yet they still corrupt us. But we mustn't let that consume our thoughts. No, we must focus on righting this."

I have spoken with Zhyleen. I have told Zhyleen what I found on the mushroom platform.

I brought Zhyleen the Crushbone missive I found atop the mushroom platform.
