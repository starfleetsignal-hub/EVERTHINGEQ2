## Steps

Kill the following enemies

- Ice Gwarthlea, the Matriarch : Kill [[Queen Gwarthlea]] in [[Vasty Deep: The Vestigial Cella]]
- Vacate El'Arad : Kill [[El'Arad, Lord Primarch of Erudin]] in [[Royal Palace of Erudin]]
- Dispatch Demitrik : Kill [[Demitrik]] in [[The Hole: Demitrik's Bastion]]

## Notes

Mission updates for killing a required target are zone-wide. Individual group members do not need to be near the target when it is killed; the only requirement is that they must be in the same instance as other members of the group.

## Rewards

- Legend Hunting Crate
- At least 15600 status
