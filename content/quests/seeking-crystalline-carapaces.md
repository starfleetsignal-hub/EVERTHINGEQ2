## Steps

1. Talk to Occultist L'Takk in the Temple of War in North Freeport {{waypoint -86, -10, -165}} to receive this City Task.
   - Slay the 15 [[A crystalline burrower|crystalline burrowers]] in the Barren Sky on Blackwind Isle located near -495, 129, 422.
1. Return to [[Occultist L'Takk]]

## Rewards

- 8324 status
- +500 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
