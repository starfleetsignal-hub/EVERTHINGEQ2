## Steps

1. Collect evidence of corruption from the [[K'Val Ruins]]
   - Collect four spores from the plants.  These are red flowers that can be found around the larger patches of plants.
   - Search for a wolf carcasse {{waypoint -3172, -717, -1063}}
   - Search for a giants' carcasse {{waypoint -3583, -714, -1064}}
1. Return to Researcher Silviss.
