## Steps

1. Locate the Rockpaw digging operation - go to [[The Forest Ruins]] (take the mariner's bell) and head to the rocky dugout just inside the castle walls to the right {{waypoint 989.06, -18.94, -664.59}}
1. Kill the two [[A Rockpaw scavenger (Forest Ruins)|gnolls]] that ambush you
1. Kill the [[Grrosh|Rockpaw dig master]] - when he dies, you automatically receive a [[Gnoll Scrawled Note]]
1. Read the note
1. Find the Rockpaw provisions - they're on the beach at {{waypoint 1006, -26, -906}}, two barrels and two crates.  Attack them to destroy them.
1. Return to Milos Faren
