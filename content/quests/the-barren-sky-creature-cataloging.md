## Steps

1. Use the [[Catalogue Creature]] skill on the following creatures in no particular order.
   - **[[The Barren Sky]]**
     - [[A Whisperwind mystic|a Whisperwind mystic]]      Whisperwind Isle
     - [[A Windgazer lookout|a Windgazer lookout]]        Whisperwind Isle, by the porter from Nek and TS
     - [[A Windgazer refugee|a Windgazer refugee]]        Whisperwind Isle, up climb wall at Windgazer's Hideout
     - [[A Windgazer recluse|a Windgazer recluse]]        Whisperwind Isle, up climb wall at Windgazer's Hideout
     - [[A Strifewing poacher|a Strifewing poacher]]      Isle of Desolation {{waypoint 512, 285, -512}}
     - [[A dustwind shimmer|a dustwind shimmer]]          Isle of Desolation near the chain
     - [[A vicious dryland scavenger|a vicious dryland scavenger]]   Isle of Desolation {{waypoint 512, 296, -762}}
     - [[A Strifewing ransacker|a Strifewing ransacker]]  Isle of Desolation
     - [[A Blacktalon defender|a Blacktalon defender]]    Isle of the Guardians (can be scattered throughout the isle)
     - [[A Blacktalon observer|a Blacktalon observer]]    Isle of the Guardians (712.10, 385.28, -157.98)
     - [[Stonescale rumbler|a stonescale rumbler]]      Isle of Awakening, well south of the cloud station
     - [[A drifting cloudmist|a drifting cloudmist]]      Isle of Awakening, south of the cloud station
     - [[A Blacktalon grunt|a Blacktalon grunt]]          Blackwind Isle {{waypoint -426, 115, 302}}
     - [[A Blacktalon curate|a Blacktalon curate]]        Blackwind Isle {{waypoint -426, 115, 302}}
     - [[A mudstone ooze|a mudstone ooze]]                Blackwind Isle {{waypoint -267, 110, 426}}

- From Whisperwind Isle (evac point, and Spire from Nek/TS point) the easiest order to go between Isle's is as follows.
  - <b>[[Whisperwind Isle]]:</b>  Whisperwind Mystic, Windgazer Lookout, Windgazer Recluse, Windgazer Refugee
  - <b>[[Blackwind Isle]]:</b>  Blacktalon Grunt, Blacktalon Curate, Mudstone Ooze
  - <b>[[Isle of Awakening]]:</b>  Drifting Cloudmist, Stonescale Rumbler
  - <b>[[Isle of the Guardians]]:</b>  Blacktalon Defender, Blacktalon Observer
  - <b>[[Isle of Desolation]]:</b>  Dustwind Shimmer, Vicious Dryland Scavenger, Strifewing Ransacker, Strifewing Poacher
