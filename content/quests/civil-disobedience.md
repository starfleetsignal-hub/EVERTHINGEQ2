## Steps

1. Speak with the following in East Freeport:
   - [[Brand Narson]] outside [[The Harbor Exchange]] {{waypoint -303, -56, 70}}
   - [[Durog Malthese]] behind Seafarer's Roost {{waypoint -261, -54, -14}}
   - [[Tall Jim]] in [[The Emporium]] {{waypoint -81, -37, 54}}
