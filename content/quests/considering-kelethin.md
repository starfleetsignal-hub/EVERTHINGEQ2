## Steps

1. Speak with [[Nimess Sessi]] {{waypoint 416, 137, 233}} in the [[Fae Royal Hall]] in [[Kelethin]]. He only speaks the Fae language, so you may need to do the [[Learning Faerlie]] quest first. Edit: If your class doesnt have Invisibility, then bring a totem. The guards dont see invis. Else you will have to be a very sneaky exilist ;)

(I saw only one guard (95) on the royal platform. It was easy enough to jump over the ramp handrail, staying on the platform edge. Then just go into the court. Nimess is in the first room alone.)

1. Return to Gibrien.

## Rewards

- +2,000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
- -1,000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
