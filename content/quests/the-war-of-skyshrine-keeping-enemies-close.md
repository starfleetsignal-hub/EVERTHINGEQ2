## Note

- Dartain and Al'Kabor are the same person. Hence "Duality".
- The next signature quest timeline story wise starts with [[Unexpected Consequences]], but you don't need to finish this timeline to start the next one.

## Steps

1. Talk to [[Yelinak The Ancient]] {{waypoint 18, -242, 473}}
1. Then go back and talk to [[Al'Kabor]]. {{waypoint 477, -211, 248}}
1. Travel to [[The Thundering Steppes]] and talk to [[Murrar Shar]] at {{waypoint -118, -1, 831}}
   1. Enter [[Splitpaw: The Lost Missionaries]] at {{waypoint 174, 4, 1054}}
   1. Talk to [[Murrar Shar]] back outside.
   1. Talk to [[Queen Antonia Bayle (The Thundering Steppes)]] near the tent next to the tower.
1. Travel back to [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]] and talk to [[Al'Kabor]] at {{waypoint 477, -211, 248}}
1. Travel to [[The Commonlands]]
   1. Click the boulders to zone into the instance [[The Ambush of Lucan D'Lere]] at {{waypoint 675, -51, 451}}
   1. Kill the 4 Encounters to update the quest.
   1. Talk to [[Lucan D'Lere]] located within the instance.
1. Travel back to [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]] and talk to [[Al'Kabor]] {{waypoint 477, -211, 248}}
1. Go to [[Kylong Plains]], [[Teren's Grasp]] - and retrieve the book of Tsaph Katta from Olmyr Deepwell {{waypoint 1988, 487, -695}}
1. Travel to [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]] and zone into [[Library of Erudin]].
   1. Click the Page on the 3rd level (2nd floor), on a table next to the bookshelves where the Shard Chest is. {{waypoint 29, 37, -45}}
1. Travel to Everfrost [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Crucible]].
   1. Pickup the page on the table in the left room {{waypoint -104, 0, 24}}
1. Travel back to [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]] and talk to [[Al'Kabor]] {{waypoint 477, -211, 248}}
1. Talk to [[Yelinak The Ancient]] {{waypoint 18, -242, 473}} and then back [[Al'Kabor]] {{waypoint 477, -211, 248}}
1. Use the Portal that appears and take part in [[A Hidden Meeting]]
1. Talk to [[Al'Kabor]] at {{waypoint 477, -211, 248}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g 17,125 status
