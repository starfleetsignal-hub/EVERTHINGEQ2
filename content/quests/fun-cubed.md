## Steps

1. Speak to [[Librarian Szeth]] on the second floor of the Library in the [[North Wing Catalog]] at {{waypoint 331, 15, -202}} to receive 4 [[Aseptic petri cube|aseptic petri cubes]].
1. Place the petri cubes in the [[Myrist Librarian Quarters]] at the following places:
   - At the foot of the north-east bed {{waypoint 295, -34, 165}}
   - At the foot of the north-west bed {{waypoint 315, -34, 165}}
   - At the foot of the middle pair of beds {{waypoint 313, -34, 184}}
   - At the foot of the south-west beds {{waypoint 317, -34, 204}}
1. Return to [[Librarian Szeth]] at {{waypoint 331, 15, -202}}.
