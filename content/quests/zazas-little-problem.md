## Steps

1. Gather the items ZaZa needs:
   - Kill 2 [[A monitor behemoth|monitor behemoths]] {{waypoint 323.50, -48.04, -46.73}}
   - Kill 7 [[A giant vulrich|giant vulriches]] {{waypoint 862.9, -49.1, 152.65}}
   - Kill 4 [[A gloom snake|gloom snakes]], found in [[Fallen Gate]] in the entrance passageway.
1. Return to ZaZa

## Rewards

- At least 3s
- One of the following:
  - [[Fine link leggings]]
  - [[Strap sewn breeches]]
  - [[Weed woven breeches]]
