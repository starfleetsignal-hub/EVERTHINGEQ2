Lusiri A'Fahmi, a member of Deepwater Circle, told me spirits have been seen attacking different teleportation spires across Norrath. She is unsure if it's the work of Emperor Ssraeshza, or someone else, trying to stop us from reconnecting the spires to the Luclin Nexus or weaken its security. We mustn't let anything interfere or corrupt the work being done!

## Steps

1. Patrol the Ulteran Spires in [[Everfrost]] at {{waypoint 442, -33, -1347}} (at level 83, I was sent to patrol the Ulteran Spires in [[Eastern Wastes]] at {{waypoint -909, -333, 2461}})
1. Patrol the Ulteran Spires in [[Nektulos Forest]] at {{waypoint -341, 2, -622}}
   1. When you get there, you'll be attacked by a 120 ^^ [[A thrashing spirit]]. Kill 3 in total. (They aren't agro, just approach one and it'll turn on you.) **Update 9/23/2020: My 110 character was attacked by a 110 ^^ Heroic spirit. Perhaps they are player's level and not set at 120.
1. Confront the person behind the spirit attacks.
   1. This is [[Justan Cafferen]] at {{waypoint 294, 0, -679}}
1. Return to [[Lusiri A'Fahmi]] at {{waypoint 1990, 480, -859}} in [[Kylong Plains]]
