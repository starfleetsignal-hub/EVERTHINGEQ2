*This quest is also part of the [[Lavastorm Timeline]].*

## Steps

1. Scribe the [[Forge bellows diagram]] received when you accepted the quest.
1. Craft [[Repaired forge bellows]] at a sewing table:
   - [[Broken forge bellows]]; also received when you accepted the quest
   - 3 [[Ashen root]]
   - 2 [[Fulginate cluster]]
   - 3 [[Strengthened leather pelt]]
   - 6 [[Luminous filament]]
1. Return to [[Smakametta]].

## Rewards

- At least 55s 89c
- Tradeskill experience
