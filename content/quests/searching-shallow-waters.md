## Description

"Glienna lost her charm when she was near the Sapphire Pond. The decaying guardians attack whenever she ventures near, so she is requesting that you go in and find it for her." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Find Glienna's Charm near the [[Sapphire Pond]] around {{waypoint -891, 68, 482}} in a brown pond mud.
1. Return to [[Glienna Lillypad]]

## Rewards

- At least 5s 81c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Glienna Lillypad: "You are a very helpful person. Would it be terribly bothersome to ask you for assistance?"
- Player Character: "It depends on what you ask of me."
- Glienna Lillypad: "I lost my charm in the grove south of here when the decaying guardians attacked me. I am sure I dropped it in the Sapphire Pond as I was running away. But they attack whenever I try and retrieve it. Shimmer gave it to me and I cannot bear to lose it. Please retrieve it for me."
- Player Character: "I will gladly find your charm."
- Glienna Lillypad: "I thank you greatly. It must be in the water, so it might be hard to find."
- Player Character: "Thank you for the details. I will return."
