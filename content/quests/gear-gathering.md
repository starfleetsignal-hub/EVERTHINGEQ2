## Description

I need to collect Erudin Research Halls Cog Gears from within Erudin Research Halls so that they may not be used to repair the machines once they break. These Erudin Research Halls Cog Gears can be found scattered around the machines within the Halls.

## Steps

1. Collect 5 gears.
   - These are fairly large and highlight when you mouse over them. They can usually be found in any area where you also fight gnomes, such as the room with the first Beam Handler. They are usually found on the floor, but they can also be found on mechanical devices, such as the curved sides of the generator at -130, -15, -126.
1. Return to Rafiqa.

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
