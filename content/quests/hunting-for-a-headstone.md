## Steps

![The damaged headstone you need to repair.](images/Hunting_for_a_Headstone_Repair.jpg)

1. Find the missing headstone.
   - *Note: multiple locations are reported to exist, with quick respawn times. Among them are {{waypoint 295, 301, 184}} , {{waypoint 390, 265, 158}} , {{waypoint 243, 301, 165}} , {{waypoint 345, 278, 198}} , and {{waypoint 304.38, 318.49, 145.44}} . Also note that you are looking **outside** of the cave, not inside.  The headstone has also been found on top of the cave, up the ladders*
   - Track Harvestables works on these.
1. Repair the damaged headstone at the gravesite. {{waypoint 474, 234, -174}}
   - Bring the [[Fractured headstone]] over to the gravesite and click on the 4th headstone.  Click on it again after the repair for a free port over to the quest giver.
1. Speak with [[Taysir Al'Babr]].
