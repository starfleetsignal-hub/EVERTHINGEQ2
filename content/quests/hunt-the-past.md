## Steps

1. Kill mobs in each of the [[Chronoportal Phenomenon|Chronoportals]]. They are NPC until you approach then they spawn scaled to your mentored level. Excess NPCs despawn once you kill the required number.
   - 3 gnoll pups ([[Portal to the Past: Qeynos Hills|Qeynos Hills]]): {{waypoint 374, -16, 779}} (Antonica) these are around the outer edge of the zone, away from the normal mobs at:
     - {{waypoint -1104, -23, 415}}
     - {{waypoint -1193, -28, 450}}
     - {{waypoint -1265, -31, 518}}
   - 1 centaur courser ([[Portal to the Past: Plains of Karana|Plains of Karana]]): {{waypoint -470.33, -2.43, 63.03}} (Enchanted Lands) visible from the entry path
   - 2 whiskered bats ([[Portal to the Past: Qeynos Ruins|Qeynos Ruins]]): {{waypoint -401, 5, -309}} (Thundering Steppes) to the right from entry. Holly will not be bothered when you kill these animals
   - 2 minotaur guards ([[Portal to the Past: Steamfont|Steamfont]]): {{waypoint 26, 94, 1298}} (Steamfont Mountains) as you go down the corridor (note that these mobs are different than the normal [[Minotaur Guard|Minotaur Guards]])
   - 3 lava beetles ([[Portal to the Past: Nagafen's Lair|Nagafen's Lair]]): {{waypoint -130, -120, 517}} (Lavastorm) across the bottom floor (out of the lava)
   - 2 desert asps ([[Portal to the Past: Southern Desert of Ro|Southern Desert of Ro]]): {{waypoint -1444, -226, -407}} (Sinking Sands) these are invisible and untrackable; spawn them by walking near large rock piles to the right of the entrance
   - 2 isle goblin warriors ([[Portal to the Past: Ocean of Tears|Ocean of Tears]]): {{waypoint 128, -7, -85}} (The Feerrott) one to the right, two to the left along the wall the entry is on
   - 2 guk basilisks ([[Portal to the Past: Guk|Guk]]): {{waypoint 1883, 453, 950}} (Moors of Ykesha) three in the entry room: once two are killed normal guard mobs appear at door to next room
1. Return to Tazstra K'Rzheett

## Rewards

- One of the Following:
  - [[Guk basilisk]]
  - [[Lava beetle]]
  - 4 [[Ancient Platinum Coin|Ancient Platinum Coins]]
