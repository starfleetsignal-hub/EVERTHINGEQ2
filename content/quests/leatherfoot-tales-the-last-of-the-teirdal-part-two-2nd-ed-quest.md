## Steps

1. Hail [[Nillipuss]] {{waypoint -24, -8, 61}}
1. Slay 5 [[Wax worker|wax workers]]. {{waypoint -252, -12, 13}}
1. Read the book.
1. Slay any 12 blightrats. ([[A blightrat|blightrats]], [[A blightrat avenger|blightrat avengers]], etc.) {{waypoint -152, 2, -208}}
1. Slay 4 [[Vale nymph seedling|vale nymph seedlings]]. {{waypoint -170, -16, 20}}
1. Read the book.
1. Hail [[Merchant Timbertop]] {{waypoint -59, -22, 28}}
1. Hail [[Yania Wanderhill]] {{waypoint -100, -24, -94}}
1. Read the book to obtain your reward.
