## Materials Needed

- 8 [[Jumping Creeper]] *(rare)*
- 5 [[Primal Luclizite Shard]] *(rare)*
- 5 [[Wracked Wood Wedge]] *(rare)*
- 50 [[Coruscating Sandpaper]]

## Notes

- This quest will require harvesting OR possessing the above mentioned rares.  Due to a bug that has been reported, you cannot possess some and harvest others when you reach that step.  You must either possess ALL of them before reaching that step, or harvest ALL of them after reaching that step. If you want to take the easy route, be sure to have everything listed in your bags **before** you go talking to Nuniun. You may harvest them or purchase them, so long as you have them before you speak to Nuniun.
  - EDIT: **As of May 4th 2025, this bug is STILL in effect**. Make sure you have the rares you need in your inventory BEFORE you start Step 6 to avoid needing to restart this quest in the series. You can mail the items from another character and you get the updates.
  - On **July, 15th 2026** I was five creepers short, when I clicked on the dead shadeweaver. **The quest updated correctly**, checkmarking the shards and weges and saying "3/8 creepers", **but** only kept updating when I was harvesting the roots in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]]. (I went to Savage Weald, harvested a rare root and did not get an update. I went back to Shadeweaver's Thicket and did get updates again. So you might have to be in the zone to get updates correctly.)
- If you forget, just delete the quest and start over; if you have already harvested the mandible, it will stay in your inventory and will not make you repeat that step.
- **UPDATE: on 7th December 2024** I was able to buy rares from broker and harvest rare items at the same time and getting my update.
- Having [[Piercing the Darkness: The Sad Tale of Benosch Ironsprocket Part I|previously made an omelet for Nuniun]] *(you do not have to have completed the entire BoL TS sig timeline, only the this quest)* will open up additional dialogue choices and NPC responses throughout this quest, and also allow you to skip a step or two as well.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] at {{waypoint -355, 92, 178}}  in [[City of Shar Vahl]]. You can use one of 3 [[Spirit of the Vah Shir]] travel baubles you received from previous quest.
1. Speak to [[Enolyn Felwater]] of Nuniun's Scouting Party {{waypoint -448, 158, -798}}.  She will give you a travel bauble that will transport you to Nuniun.
1. Find [[Nuniun Wolfbane (Shadeweaver's Thicket)|Nuniun Wolfbane]] in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] atop a cliff to the east of [[Vex Thal]] {{waypoint -132, 220, 495}}
   - *Alternatively, travel overland through [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] then use the rope line at {{waypoint -287.20, 157.27, 477.20}} near [[Vex Thal]] to reach Nuniun's camp.*
1. Speak to [[Nuniun Wolfbane (Shadeweaver's Thicket)|Nuniun]] and listen to his story.
1. If you have not [[Piercing the Darkness: The Sad Tale of Benosch Ironsprocket Part I|previously made an omelet for Nuniun]] he will ask you to make him one Three-Rockhopper Egg Omelet now. You will be given the ingredients and can make it at the nearby campfire.
   - **Note:** If you have previously made Nunien an omelet, this step **will be skipped.**
1. Procure an intact shadeweaver mandible.
   - *Find **a recent shadeweaver kill** at {{waypoint 172, 91, 132}}. Not trackable.*
1. Obtain the remaining materials to make the signalling horn for Niniun.
   - [[Primal Luclizite Shard]] (5)
   - [[Wracked Wood Wedge]] (5)
   - [[Jumping Creeper]] (8)
1. Speak to [[Nuniun Wolfbane (Shadeweaver's Thicket)|Nuniun]] and receive the [[Nuniun's Signaling Horn]] recipe.
1. Craft [[Nuniun's Signaling Horn]] using a Work Bench
1. Return to [[Nuniun Wolfbane (Shadeweaver's Thicket)|Nuniun]].
1. Investigate a reading from a device near [[Vex Thal]] {{waypoint -75, 200, 508}}.
1. Attempt to calibrate the device.
1. Get ported to [[Vex Thal: (Tradeskill)]]

## Rewards

- At least 11p 21g 79s 83c
- [[Wall of Nope]]
- If you don't have flight capability, it will be available to you after this quest and you should be able to fly back to the top of the cliff instead of using the rope or needing a [[Call of Shade]] potion.
