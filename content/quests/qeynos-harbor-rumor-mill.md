## Steps

1. Find the Captain's log of the [[Leaky Drake]] in a chest on the docks right behind where Theyeurn is standing.
1. Speak with a religious individual, [[Peytro Ironside]], at {{waypoint 858, -26, 35}}
1. Find a theory about the origins of the attacks by talking to [[Betsie Fellhammer]] in [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn]]
1. Listen to some gossip about the attackers by talking with [[Aunika Cresh]] in [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn]] {{waypoint 810, -23, 60}}
1. Gather an eye witness account from a sailor on the [[Starfish]] by speaking with [[Aisha McCorn]] at {{waypoint 924, -25, 55}}
1. Gather a common man's opinion by speaking with [[Colin McEntire]] at {{waypoint 927, -26, 67}}
1. Return to Theyeurn for your reward.
