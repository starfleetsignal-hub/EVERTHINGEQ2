## Steps

1. Destroy 5 sacks of grain found at the [[Crossroads]], the Nomad camps, the Kerran camp, the Dervish camp or the Dregs Village.
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -1000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -1000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
