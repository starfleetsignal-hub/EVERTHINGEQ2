These stumps are infested with something. Not termites, though...

## Notes

- ***Note:** This is a 4 minute timed quest.*
- Found in all versions of Zek, the Scourge Wastes:
  - [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Solo)]]
  - [[Zek, the Scourge Wastes (Heroic)]]

## Steps

1. Bust 10 stumps before time expires.
   - Click any of the stumps nearby.
   - About 8 [[A stump eater|stump eaters]] will jump out of each stump and attack.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- [[Encrusted iron key]]
