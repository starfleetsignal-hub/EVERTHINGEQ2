## Description

"There is change unlike any before seen in the City of Qeynos. Not everyone embraces change and many seek to destroy what they don't understand. The protection of its citizens is the kingdom's primary focus; it is for this reason that the order to relocate all within the safety of the city walls was decreed. I should visit the Lion's Mane Inn and Tavern in South Qeynos to see the new rooms for myself." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Locate the [[Lion's Mane Inn and Tavern]] in [[South Qeynos]], and find a door for a basic inn room.
   - The Lion's Mane Inn is found in [[Qeynos Capitol District]] at {{waypoint 547, -9, 138}}.  Once inside the inn, the quest updates when you approach the staircase.
1. If you are good-aligned you need to buy the room for 0c and use the door to enter the inn room. If you are evil-aligned you can right-click a door and visit a room belonging to another character to complete the quest.
   - The doors are all on the second floor of the inn.  Your inn room previously located in the suburbs can be found if you click the proper door.

## Quest Steps and Dialogue

Locate the Lion's Mane Inn and Tavern, in South Qeynos, and find a door for a basic inn room.

I have located a door for the basic inn room. use the room door to enter the room. If you do not own one, click on the "Buy" button to get a room. You may also "Visit" or "Tour" the inn.

I visited the Lion's Mane Inn and Tavern to see my new accommodations. They are a pleasant change and the city is bursting with life. The City of Qeynos is a beacon of hope and a promise of justice to all. Everyone is doing their best to open their hearts to the winds of change.
