## Steps

1. Speak with [[Elder Fleesa]] {{waypoint -237, -56, -83}}
1. Seek the book "The Embrace of Luclin".  It is in [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] on a table at {{waypoint 23, -6, -121}} and looks like an open book with silver sparkles rising from it.  Left click it.
1. Return to Caltuur
1. Enter [[Stonestair Byway: The Animus Corridors]] {{waypoint -4, -18, -330}}
1. Once in Stonestair Byway, kill 8 Erudites ([[An erudite citizen]], [[An erudite shieldmage]], [[An erudite battlemage]] all update)
1. Return to Caltuur

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g @ lvl 95
