This droplet appears as if it is contained by magic. You can roll it around in the palm of your hand and it does not break or release the clear fluid. I feel oddly compelled to find out more about it, while on the dark side of this moon.

## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A cascading spout|cascading spouts]] at [[The Colossal Cascades]] {{waypoint -382, 176, 324}} in [[Echo Caverns]].

## Rewards

- [[Crown Receptacle (Tier 5) (Version 1)|Crown Receptacle (Tier 5)]]
- [[Contained Water Droplet]] -- *collectible*
