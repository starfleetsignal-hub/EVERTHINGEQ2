*This quest is unobtainable for characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Kill 10 undead mobs in [[The Graveyard]].
   - Including, but not necessarily limited to:  [[A crumbling skeleton]], [[A brittle skeleton]], [[A durable skeleton]], [[An undead battlepriest]], [[An undead fighter]], [[An undead pikeman]], [[An undead squire]], [[A rotting zombie]], [[An undead crusader]], [[An undead knight]], [[An undead lancer]], [[An undead priest]], [[A putrid zombie]], [[A fiendish ghoul]].
1. Return to [[Zekvila Dizelk]]. {{waypoint 86, -2, 90}}

## Rewards

- At least 2s 13c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Reinforced armband of service]]
  - [[Scaled armband of service]]
  - [[Scaled bracer of service]]
