## Steps

1. Destroy [[The Black Reaver]] in [[The City of Mist]]. {{waypoint -319, 149, -1011}}
1. Speak with the [[Swifttail Trialmaster]]. {{waypoint -73, -43, 160}}
1. Enter the trial ring and defeat [[Crush "The Icescale" Lizzard]]. {{waypoint -74, -43, 156}}
1. Return to the senior Steel shackle monk.
