## Steps

1. Find the [[Stormhold]] Library
   - The entrance to the Stormhold Library is a grate on the floor next to [[Qelinatias the Scion of Pain]] at {{waypoint 3, -26, -129}} in Stormhold, not Antonica coord's.
1. Return to Timothus Yelrow
