## Notes

- If for some reason you have any of the quest items acquired during this quest *before* starting it, making sure to destroy them otherwise you will not receive the necessary quest updates.
- If soloing: At a certain quest step you will need a specific chest drop. The chest will not drop if the mob is greyed out to you, therefore it is recommended that you mentor down to at least adventure level 80 before beginning this quest.

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Thrall's Quarters]] (top level) of [[Mistmoore Catacombs]] and speak with [[Zazoo|a dwarven thrall]]. {{waypoint -86, -31, 83}}
1. Enter the room to the right of the dwarf and click the scroll on the table titled, "Liberation Libation". {{waypoint -67, -31, 95}}
1. Obtain [[Dust of Recollection]] (chest drop) from either [[Head Maid Z'yra]] {{waypoint -97, -27, -66}} or [[Head Butler Z'ral]] {{waypoint -247, -21, -33}}.
1. Harvest 4 [[Blood of the Thrall]] from boxes named 'Libation Winery' found in the same room as Head Maid Z'yra. {{waypoint -78, -27, -69}} (*If you get the message "Can't see target", jump on top of the box to harvest.*)
1. Obtain 4 [[Vial of Evil Eye Bile]] (chest drop) from [[A Mistmoore bloodmage|Mistmoore bloodmages]] or [[A Mistmoore bloodoath|Mistmoore bloodoaths]] in [[The Blood Forge]]. {{waypoint -127, -28, -25}}
   - Warning: Do not kill [[Xicotl (Mistmoore Catacombs)|Xicotl]] or his placeholder, [[A Mistmoore bloodseeker|a Mistmoore bloodseeker]], if they are up. He is needed for a quest step further on.
1. Pick up the [[Stein of Cohesion]]. {{waypoint -63, -28, -93}}
1. Go to the [[Conversion Chamber]] and give [[The Cauldron of Uzun]] the ingredients to create the *Liberation Libation*. {{waypoint -166, -30, 77}}
1. Head back to the dwarven thrall and give him the potion. Follow him as he runs off. {{waypoint -86, -31, 83}}
1. Kill [[Xicotl (Mistmoore Catacombs)|Xicotl]] or his [[A Mistmoore bloodseeker|placeholder]]. {{waypoint -124, -28, -33}}
1. Kill [[Seneschal D'naraz]] or his [[A Mistmoore aide|placeholder]]. {{waypoint -210, -25, -13}}
1. Enter [[Castle Mistmoore]] and examine the *Chest of Zanne*. {{waypoint -90, 1, -26}}
1. In the same room as the chest, pick up the book lying on the coffin called, "Eternal Blood", and examine it from your inventory. {{waypoint -98, 1, -25}}.
1. Gather the following pages:
   - Page #2: {{waypoint -135, 0, -48}}
   - Page #3: {{waypoint -156, -4, -88}}
   - Page #5: {{waypoint -74, 5, -50}}
   - Page #7: {{waypoint -244, 6, -17}}
   - Page #8: {{waypoint -135, -5, 30}}
   - Page #10: {{waypoint -34, -4.73, 60}}(*The pages might not be at the exact coordinates. Look on other tables in the rooms that are indicated.*)
1. Click the *Skull of Rastin* in [[The Secret Stash]], at {{waypoint -87, 0, 8}}. To access the room, click on the vase at {{waypoint -59.8, 0, 2.8}}.
1. Obtain 10 [[Drop of Mistmoore rust|drops of Mistmoore rust]], a chest drop from [[A watchful Mistmoore protector|watchful Mistmoore protectors]].
   - The chest will not drop if the mob is grey. You must mentor down to at least adventure level 80 for the chest to drop.
1. Return to the *Skull of Rastin*.![Zanne, My Beloved](images/Zanne.jpg)
1. Click on the *Portrait of Zanne* on the wall near the entrance to Mistmoore Catacombs. {{waypoint -189, -9, -12}}
1. Kill 9 Castle Mistmoore Named Monsters or their placeholder(s) to obtain the necessary items to unlock the *Chest of Zanne*:
   - [[Guardian of the Bath]] {{waypoint -168, 1, -3}}
   - [[Ferox]] {{waypoint 18, 12, -90}}, or any [[A jeering gargoyle|jeering gargoyle]] found on the main floor {{waypoint -16, 38, -113}} or {{waypoint -16, 38, 110}}
   - [[Komornik Syncall]] {{waypoint -151, -3, -93}} or [[Vikomt D'Raethe]] {{waypoint -113, 0, 0}}
   - [[Deris L'Ibantcci]] {{waypoint 11, 42, -7}}
   - [[Vanquisher B'Riareos]] {{waypoint -70, 41, 0}}
   - [[Graf Koban]] {{waypoint -28, -5, 65}}
   - [[Pani Tanara Viswin]] {{waypoint -127.54, -4.18, -109.42}}
   - [[The Devoted Caitiff]] {{waypoint -9, 38, 128}}
   - [[Mynthi Davissi]] {{waypoint -10, 38, -135}}![Mayong Mistmoore](images/Mayong_mistmoore_bust.jpg)
1. Examine the *Bust of Mayong Mistmoore* and receive [[Blade of Mistmyr]]. {{waypoint -102, -5, 59}}
1. Return to the *Chest of Zanne* and use the Blade of Mistmyr to open it and collect [[Soulfire's blade]]. {{waypoint -90, 0, -20}}
1. Return to [[Sir Trodonis]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]. {{waypoint 866, 44, -280}}
