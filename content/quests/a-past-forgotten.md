## Steps

1. Speak with the Concordium librarian - head to [[The Concordium Tower]], take the red portal, and speak to [[Apprentice Librarian Cippin]] {{waypoint 709, 41, 105}}
1. Search [[The Down Below]] {{waypoint 441, -16, 85}} for a map to the Ratonga library - it's where the lowest level Bloodsabers congretate at {{waypoint 1, -2, -157}}.  Looks like a sparkling giant scroll.
1. Follow the path to the hidden library
1. Enter [[The Library of Vatheel]] {{waypoint -73.43, -0.13, -114}} - it's a sewer grate right next to the one leading to Vermin's Snye.
   - Defeat the Bloodsabers in Vatheel's Library
   - Defeat [[Sziss the Bookmongrel]] (scales ^ solo, no AA)
   - Find a library book of interest - it's in the back corner where Sziss was wandering
1. Return to Tilla in [[North Qeynos]] at {{waypoint 302, -12, 92}}
1. Search [[Antonica]] for evidence of ratonga scouts
   - Click on sparkly little mud pile looking things for updates - they're marked well on the in-game map.  Or some locations to try:
   - {{waypoint -63, 4, -212}}
   - {{waypoint -378, 5, -246}}
   - {{waypoint -392, -1, -482}}
   - {{waypoint -379, 16, -391}}
   - {{waypoint -358, 16, -391}}
   - {{waypoint -895, 1, -540}}
   - {{waypoint -136, 10, -239}}
   - {{waypoint -143, 10, -216}}
   - {{waypoint -499, 6, -273}}
   - {{waypoint -366, 16, -297}}
1. Return to Tilla in [[North Qeynos]] at {{waypoint 302, -12, 92}}
