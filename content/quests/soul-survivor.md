## Notes

- You will need the [[Disk of Bylze]] to respawn the [[Shade of Bylze]]. If you destroyed it you will need to reopen the chest that dropped it in the quest [[Burglars Afoot]]. Location of the chest is in Tenebrous Tangle at {{waypoint 563, -147, 641}}.

## Steps

1. Inspect [[The Anvil of Animatus]] in the [[Chamber of Animation]]. {{waypoint -167, 136, 41}}.
1. Speak with 'the tome of Animatus' who tells you to kill [[A Scaleborn animator]] and 3 [[A sentinel builder|sentinel builders]]. All of these spawn in the same room. The two named mobs in the Chamber of Animation also work in lieu of a Scaleborn animator.
1. Right click and examine the first blue pool {{waypoint -137, 136, 77}} in the Chamber of Animation. Then right click and examine the second pool. {{waypoint -167, 136, 55}}
1. Return to the [[Shade of Bylze]] {{waypoint 12, 157, -40}} for your reward and a very revealing twist regarding the shade's true identity.

## Rewards

- One of the following
  - [[Diaphanous Bulwark of Bylze]]
  - [[Essence Shield of Bylze]]
  - [[Ethereal Buckler of Bylze]]
  - [[Intangible Aegis of Bylze]]
