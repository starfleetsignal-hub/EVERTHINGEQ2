## Steps

1. Scribe [[Blueprint: Gnomish Pirate Distillery]]
1. Craft a [[Gnomish Pirate Spirits Distillery|Gnomish Spirits Distillery]] at the work bench. You will need:
   - 1 [[Titanium ore]]
   - 1 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
   - 1 [[Amber]]
   - 1 [[Ethereal coal]]
1. Place the distillery near Fenwick. There's a green shiny at {{waypoint 1292, -540, 478}}
1. Return to Fenwick.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +2,000 faction with **Iceclad Pirates**
