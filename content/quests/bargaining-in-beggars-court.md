## Steps

1. Zone into [[Beggar's Court: Sweat-Stained Cobbles]] and search for clues.
   - [[A barbarian citizen]] near the zone in will speak once you get near her and tell you that the "meeting" is in the northwest common domicile
1. Investigate the meeting - it's in the house at {{waypoint 51, 5, -32}}
1. Listen to [[Clan Chief Malachi Sleetsaber (Beggar's Court)|Clan Chief Malachi Sleetsaber's]] speech
1. Find Chief Sleetsaber's home - it's the house at {{waypoint -30, 2, -28}}
1. Threaten [[Hanne Sleetsaber]] - speak to her and then kill the two [[A Halasian wolf pup|Halasian wolf pups]] (scales, v)
1. Threaten Chief Sleetsaber - he's waiting right outside the door
1. Return to the meeting and listen to Chief Sleetsaber's new speech
1. Return to Selyse Brazenroot

- At least 10g 97c @ lvl 56
