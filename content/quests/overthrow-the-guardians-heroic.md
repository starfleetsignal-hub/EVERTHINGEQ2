## Steps

*The guardians of the Nexus Core do not allow outsiders to explore through its halls. Find the strongest of the Nexus Core guardians, and destroy them.*

- Enter the zone [[The Nexus Core (Heroic)]]:
  - Defeat [[Luminox Prime (Heroic)|Luminox]]
  - Destroy [[Amalgam of Energy (Heroic)|Amalgam]]
  - Destroy the [[Energy Delivery Module (Heroic)|Blaster Module]]
  - Destroy the [[Maligned Gyro (Heroic)|Maligned Gyro]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- 5 [[Draconic Etyma]]
- [[Sparkling Crate]]
- A crate of heroic gear
