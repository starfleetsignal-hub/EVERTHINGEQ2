## Steps

1. Enter [[Longshadow Alley: Daeneth Cloister]] {{waypoint -92.7, -34.6, -368.1}}
   - Obtain savage Freeblood death vapor. Kill (6) Freeblood savages
   - Gather Freeblood glowing arcane objects (6). These are found lying in rooms or streets
1. Return to Motius

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Academy of Arcane Science**

## Note:

For the next quest in this line you must seek out [[Foci Tamara Paust]] {{waypoint 17.69, -34.26, -148.16}} in the lower level of the Academy.
