## Steps

1. Speak to [[Atianoo Donicus]] near [[The Jade Tiger's Den]] {{waypoint -135, -24, -51}}
1. Click on a sewer cover in the water next to [[Seafarer's Roost]] in East Freeport {{waypoint -224, -75, 35}}
1. Speak to Lerenth {{waypoint 13, 0, 14}}
   - Accept his quest (especially if on Epic 2.0), [[Descent Into Madness]], as it follows the same zones as this quest
   - If you skip the quest, it will become unavailble FOREVER. (for that character)
1. Back in Freeport, use a city bell to enter [[The Graveyard]], then click a sewer cover {{waypoint -36, -10, 32}} to enter [[The Deadpools]]
1. Head to {{waypoint -3, 0, -21}} for auto-update
   - Click on occultists bodies for [[Descent Into Madness]] update
1. Return to North Freeport and click the sewer cover at {{waypoint -172, -58, -175}} and enter The Shadowhand Crypt.
1. Equip [[A white cowl]] (if you destroyed your first one, Adrianna gave you a new one at the start of this quest)
1. Speak to [[Doorman Janus]]
1. Head to around {{waypoint -32, -1, 0}} and listen to the meeting (auto-update) NOTE:the dialogue is triggered by proximity, if you have not talked to the doorman previous to aproaching the meeting the quest will not update. Simply go out of the zone and enter again to fix.
   - Talk to Distressed Occultist(s) for [[Descent Into Madness]]
1. Return to Adrianna in the North Freeport Temple

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- [[The Overlord's Watchful Eye]]
