## Background

McScroogle is tired of being pestered every [[Frostfell]] about changing his ways. He asks you to take over his body for the ordeal so he can get some uninterrupted sleep.

## Notes

- This quest is a homage to the classic holiday tale by Charles Dickens.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Mr. McScroogle (Frostfell Wonderland Village)|Mr. McScroogle]] to zone into [[Mr. McScroogle's Boudoir]].
1. Speak with [[Gwenda Gurgley Gigglegibber]], the Ghost of Frostfell Past, to be sent to [[McScroogle's Past]].
1. Speak with Gwenda Gurgley Gigglegibber once inside the instance.
1. Speak with [[Lani Cogspinner]] and get dumped.
1. Return to Gwenda Gurgley Gigglegibber to get teleported back to Mr. McScroogle's Boudoir.
1. Speak with [[Gergus Gigglegibber]], the Ghost of Frostfell Present, to be sent to [[Bobby Kritchat's House]].
1. Speak with Gergus Gigglegibber once inside the instance.
1. Move close to [[Tiny Jim]] to find out why he is sad.
1. Move close to [[Bobby Kritchat]] to find out why he is upset.
1. Move close to [[Grandpa Sean]] and [[Grandma Shawna]] to find out why they are upset.
1. Move close to [[Grandpa Jack]] to find out what's wrong.
1. Examine the [[A Package|package]] in your inventory to give presents Tiny Jim and his sister.
1. Clean the dirty dishes on the table for Bobby Kritchat by right-clicking on them.
1. Examine the [[A Pre-Cooked Frostfell Turkey|Pre-Cooked Frostfell Turkey]] in your inventory to give it to the grandparents.
1. Hover your mouse over the heater by [[Grandpa Jack]] and spam the 'F'-key.
1. Return to Gergus Gigglegibber to get teleported back to Mr. McScroogle's Boudoir.
1. Speak with [[Gilby Gigglegibber]], the Ghost of Frostfell Future, to be sent to [[The Future]].
1. Speak with Gilby Gigglegibber once inside the instance.
1. Navigate the maze until you arrive at the gravestone. {{waypoint -658.24, -17.83, -298.92}}
   - Follow the zone wall left and keep making left turns around obstacles so that you stay along the zone wall.
   - Within the maze you may come accross the following NPCs, which have interesting dialogue:
     - Mergott Mizzlefig McScroogle {{waypoint -540, -11, -338}}
     - Lani Cogspinner and [[Nightingale Brede]] {{waypoint -529, -11, -359}}
     - Tiny Jim {{waypoint -690, -15, -248}}
     - [[Harbey Furlman]] {{waypoint -678, -16, -319}}
     - [[Henry Cogsworth]] and [[William Rofterson]] {{waypoint -689, -14, -337}}
1. Click on the icicles to the right of gravestone to be teleported back to the start of the maze. Speak with Gilby Gigglegibber to be teleported back to [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]].
1. Speak with [[Mr. McScroogle (Frostfell Wonderland Village)|Mr. McScroogle]].
