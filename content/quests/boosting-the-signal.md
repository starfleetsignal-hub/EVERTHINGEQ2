## Steps

1. Use the [[Casket of Thuntonium]] to boost the signals at the following locations:
   - South signal {{waypoint 993, 385, 1017}}
   - North signal {{waypoint 1223, 424, 588}}
   - East signal {{waypoint 1206, 404, 768}}
   - West signal {{waypoint 1366, 408, 806}}
1. Return to [[Ember Tizzlespring]] at the [[Dropship Landing Zone]].

## Rewards

- At least 11g 5s 35c and
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Exarch's Final Communion Plate Barbute of Oration]] (Clerics)
  - [[Executioner's Adamantine Helmet of Dominance]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Illuminate's Crashing Waves Skullcap of Overbearing]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Magus's Spellbound Weave Cowl of Blasting]] (All Mages)
  - [[Outrider's Elven Chain Helmet of Stealth]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Stormcaller's Summer Weave Cap of Restoration]] (Druids)
  - [[Witchdoctor's Dark Awakening Coif of Commanding]] (Shamans)
