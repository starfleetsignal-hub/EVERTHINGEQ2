## Steps

1. Use the Transport at {{waypoint -676, 141, -20}} to go to the [[Claved Fate Islets]].
1. Gather 10 small pieces of reddish rock found on [[Claved Fate Islets]] on the winding paths to {{waypoint 431.50, 381.79, 562.99}}. Also near {{waypoint 103.13, 386.78, 747.95}}  ![](images/Stormy_Defenses_quest_-_Storm_Stone.jpg)
1. Return to speak [[Sqrauwk]]
1. Place storm stones around the perimeter on [[Isles of Wisdom]].
   - Locations are different each time, some examples (none in the camps)
     - {{waypoint -629.17, 139.92, 307.73}}, {{waypoint -476.49, 144.86, 255.06}}, {{waypoint -573.93, 148.69, 136.78}}
     - {{waypoint -648.03, 145.31, 93.27}}, {{waypoint -677.03, 143.66, -5.87}}, {{waypoint -674.12, 142.57, -28.42}}
     - {{waypoint -879.88, 142.61, -48.88}}, {{waypoint -891.21, 143.49, -28.79}}, {{waypoint -875.60, 156.94, 103.43}}
     - {{waypoint -887.33, 161.14, 164.11}}, {{waypoint -718, 161, 321}}
     - {{waypoint -479, 152, 308}}, {{waypoint -492, 152, 308}}, {{waypoint -459, 141, 273}}
     - {{waypoint -475, 145, 253}}, {{waypoint -573, 148, 136}}, {{waypoint -599, 148, 129}}
     - {{waypoint -645, 145, 93}}, {{waypoint -664, 140, 66}}, {{waypoint -675, 143, -5}}
- I was able to get all 10 stones at the very bottom of this area.
1. return to speak [[Sqrauwk]]
