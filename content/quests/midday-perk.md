## Steps

(This NPC is often bugged and partially stuck in the ground. To get around this type /target Alden and then type /hail Alden and he'll pop up onto the table so you can complete this step Ship Out)

1. Scribe the received recipe book [[Alden’s Axe-Strong Brew Recipe]] and craft 200 [[Barrel of Alden’s Axe-Strong Brew|Barrels of Alden’s Axe-Strong Brew]]
   - Level 70 tradeskill combine, geomancy skill, stove & keg
   - 1 [[Alden's strong swill]] (received from [[Alden Strongaxe|Alden]])
   - 2 [[Raw cranberry|raw cranberry]]
   - 2 [[Cabilis cocoa bean|cabilis cocoa beans]]
   - 5 [[Smoldering kindling]]
   - 10-40 [[Barrel of Alden's Axe-Strong Brew|barrels of brew]] per combine
1. Return to [[Alden Strongaxe|Alden]]

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- At least 81g 60s 18c
- +750 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
