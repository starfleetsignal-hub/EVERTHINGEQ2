## Requirements

- [[Bag of the Tinkerers]] from [[The Wondrous Inventions of a Crazed Gnome]]*  - confirmed*
- You need 5 [[Eucalyptus lumber]] *You can buy them from broker or harvest them*
- Need to speak Volant

## Steps

1. Click on the Cooking Pot, next to Warble, whom is laying on the ground, after completing [[One Door Closes, Another Door Opens]].
1. Pick up the Anaskar Stone
1. Pick up Warble's Body
1. go to the on slab in Stonebrunt Highlands {{waypoint -45.10, 310.73, 74.05}} (close to the dark cave)
1. Place Warble on slab in Stonebrunt Highlands {{waypoint -45.10, 310.73, 74.05}}
1. Build pyre on the slab
   - *Note:You need 5 [[Eucalyptus lumber]] (You can buy them from broker or harvest them, quest will update once you've built the pyre)*
1. "Singing the Magic words to restore the Anaskar Stone"
   1. Use Anaskar Stone and try to read the magic words
   1. Use the Pyre
   1. Use Anaskar Stone and sing the magic words
1. Use the stone again to port to The Village of Pothsi
1. In the center of the village, click the marked location to place the stone in its rightfull place.
1. Speak with Rashak
1. Head to the pool at location {{waypoint 1221, 39, 2244}}
1. Speak with [[Velaryyk]]
   - *Note:You need to speak [[Volant]] to talk to [[Velaryyk]]. As soon as you speak with him, Viax and his pet will spawn only a few feet away from Velaryyk. Remember, you are missing now 2 items in your equipped inventory, so hopefully you are carrying replacements.*
1. Kill [[Viax]]
   - *Note:This is a solo 98^^ fight. Click on [[Viax]] for info and 2 strats if you don't have ToV gear.*
1. Talk to [[Velaryyk]] again
1. Retrieve weapons floating above the pool.
