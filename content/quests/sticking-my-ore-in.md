## Steps

1. Scribe [[The Giant Book of Ore Imbuing]] and craft a [[Chest of imbued ore]]. The ingredients are:
   - 3 [[Ferrite cluster]]
   - 2 [[Redwood lumber]]
   - 5 [[Smoldering coal]]
1. Return to [[Jones]].

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience.
- At least 30g 37s 86c
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
