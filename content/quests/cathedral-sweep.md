The Cathedrals of the Master's Will and Whispers are infested with vermin. I must slay them all!

## Note

- You have 4 minutes to complete this quest.

## Steps

1. Clear 15 [[An accumulated consequent|accumulated consequents]] from the Cathedral of the Master's Will.
1. Clear 4 [[A dark-shadowed wretch|dark-shadowed wretches]] from the Cathedral of the Master's Whispers.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 51g 20s 11,300 status
- [[Coin of the Eternal Queen]]
