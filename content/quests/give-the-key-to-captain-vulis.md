## Notes

- This quest starts the moment you speak to [[Captain Vulis]] after completing [[Operation Green Hood]] and auto-completes the moment you move the dialog forward. The quest is not available if you have completed the now unobtainable quest [[Give the key to the Glademaster]].

## Steps

1. Simply progress the dialog to complete this quest and receive your reward.
   - [[Captain Vulis]] tells you he simply wanted to test you, and tells you to keep the key.

## Rewards

- Experience only
