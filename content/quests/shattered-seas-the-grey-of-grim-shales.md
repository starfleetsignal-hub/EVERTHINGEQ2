## Steps

1. Arrive on [[Grim Shales]] at {{waypoint -173, 7, -910}}
1. Clear the landing area
   - Kill 4 [[A curse crab|curse crabs]]
   - Remove 6 trees at the landing
1. Use the [[Emissary's Flare]]
1. Help with the search for [[Dargan Greymast]]
   - Help [[Captain Greymast]]
     1. [[Diving for Defenses]] (must be done first to unlock the other lines)
     1. [[Arming Greymast]]
     1. [[Deep in the Lost Shadows]]
   - Help [[Researcher Aillena Belzia]]
     1. [[From the Ruins]]
     1. [[Grim Reaping]]
1. Speak with [[Captain Greymast]]

## Rewards

- [[Simple Shissar Brazier]]
- At least 1p 35g
- One of the Following:
  - 2 [[Electrified Adornment of Endurance]]
  - 2 [[Electrified Adornment of Energy]]
