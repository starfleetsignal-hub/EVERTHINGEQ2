## Description

*I need to find out about the Roekillik. [[Pearl Honeywine]] suggested I find out what this race of ratonga-like creatures are surveying in the mines around [[Norrath]].*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Surveyor Menak]] in [[The Commonlands]]. {{waypoint 43, -24, -882}}
1. Speak to [[Engineer Klaaska]] east of [[Surveyor Menak]]. {{waypoint -19, -39, -889}}
1. Return to [[Pearl Honeywine]] (Good-alignment) or [[Mareva D'Iniv]] (Evil-alignment).
1. "Investigate the mines of Norrath":
   - If you are under level 30 go to [[Bloodskull Valley]] in [[The Commonlands]], quest updates at door.  {{waypoint -4, -48, 729}}
   - If you are level 35-40 go to [[The Mysterious Mine]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]]. {{waypoint -170, -16, -193}}
   - If you are level 46 and above go to the entrance of [[Permafrost]] in [[Everfrost]]. If you have problems getting the update you should zone in and then back out again to get it. {{waypoint 107, -4, -1143}}
1. Return to Pearl Honeywine or Mareva D'Iniv.
