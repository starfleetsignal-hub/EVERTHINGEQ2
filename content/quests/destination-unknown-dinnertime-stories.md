## Notes

1. Upon accepting the quest you get 3 recipes needed later auto scribed in your recipe book.
1. This quest requires:
   - 75x [[Formless Kindling]]
   - 1 x rare [[Flowfall Vines]] from [[Sturdy roots]] plus 10p for the research part.
1. Notes about research part of this quest:
   - Researching is estimated to take 7 days and 1 hour but it can be shortened by doing Fouran's missions. You can track your research by pressing K and going to Research tab, or by clicking on Researcher Fouran.
   - These missions reset at preset times every 3 hours. You can find the time until next reset in your Quest Journal, Mission timers tab (filter by Darou Village:Researcher Fouran Researching Requisitions).
   - After completing the last quest in crafting timeline [[Destination Unknown: Additional Tasks]] Fouran's quests will give bonus time reduction to alternate characters doing these quests (16 hours reduction each).

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Davrak]] at {{waypoint 490, 81, -567}}
1. Harvest:
   - 30x [[Perse Perch]] from [[Shoal of Fish]] nodes in the water on [[Bog'Ta Beach]]  {{waypoint -348, -1, -542}} and down south [[Writher Shore]] along east coast.
     - Note: The water will damage you, fish from the land.
     - Use TAB to locate the fish nodes efficiently.
   - 40x [[Darou Daikon]] from [[Sturdy Shrub]] nodes.
   - 45x [[Detritus Root]] from [[Sturdy Roots]] nodes.
   - 40x [[Gorroth Garbanzo]] from [[Sturdy Shrub]] nodes.
1. Craft 5x [[Hearty Darou Meal]]
   - Requires 25x [[Formless Kindling]]
1. Speak with [[Davrak]]
1. Craft 5x [[Restorative Darou Drink]]
   - Requires 25x [[Formless Kindling]]
1. Speak with [[Davrak]]
1. Craft 5x [[Delectable Darou Dessert]]
   - Requires 25x [[Formless Kindling]]
1. Speak with [[Davrak]]
1. Speak with [[Flamebearer Denat]] {{waypoint 546, 85, -643}}
1. Prove your worth to the Darou. This is achieved by working with [[Researcher Fouran]]
   1. Speak to [[Researcher Fouran]] at {{waypoint 509, 82, -654}}.
   1. Click [[Researcher Fouran]] and start research of [[Darou Badge of Trust]].
      - This requires a rare [[Flowfall Vines]] from a [[Sturdy Roots]] node and 10p.
      - Right-click and hail [[Researcher Fouran]] to receive one of 3 quests that will advance the research by 8 hours (*plus an additional 8 hours bonus time for players with the [[It's a Known Unknown|account flag]] for completing the RoC Tradeskill Sig on another character, for a total of 16hr reduction per 3hr quest if account flagged*)
        - [[Researcher Fouran: Requisition - Darou Delight]]
        - [[Researcher Fouran: Requisition - Grasslands Leather Vest]]
        - [[Researcher Fouran: Requisition - Oogothl Onyx Lock Mechanism]]
      - Keep doing these repeatable missions every 3 hours, or you can leave and let research complete on its own.
        - The 3 hour timer is server-wide.
        - Like many server-wide quest timers, you may check the status of the timer at any time by opening your Quest Journal and going to the* 'Mission Timers' *tab at the top.  You can filter by the drop-down, selecting* 'Rage of Cthurath**, or by searching* 'Dar' *in the search-box at the bottom of the tab.
        - Meaning, if you happen to catch the mission timer before it rolls over into a new 3 hour window, you can complete the mission twice in just a few minutes - once right before the roll-over, and then once more again immediately after the reset for the new 3 hour window. Just make sure you turn it in *before* the roll-over, otherwise it will count for the new window and you won't be able to get a new one.
      - You need to check the status of this research, he will not notify you it's completed.
1. After research is done collect [[Darou Badge of Trust]] and speak with [[Flamebearer Denat]]
