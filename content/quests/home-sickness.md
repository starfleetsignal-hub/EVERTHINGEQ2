## Starting the Quest

- Starts in your own house where you placed [[An Obulus Frontier Seedling]]

## Steps

1. Converse with the Gardening Goblin about how the plant is not looking healthy.
1. Take a soil sample to [[Obulus Frontier]] and ask [[Growf]] {{waypoint 81, -171, -681}} his advice.
1. Growf recommends that you gather splinters of the luclin rock near the crash site {{waypoint 390, -230, -261}} trackable harvest - [[Splinter of luclinite]].
1. Return the sample of luclin stone to the gardening goblin.
1. Finish conversation with goblin.
1. Goblin asks you to check back later and see how it does.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Gardener's Spade]]
- +1,500 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
