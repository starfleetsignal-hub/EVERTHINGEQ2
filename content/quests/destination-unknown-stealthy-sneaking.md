## Notes

Check your tradeskill vitality, refill if you can. Use your W.H.E.R.E. form if needed to avoid mobs.

You **CANNOT ride the trained nethernaught ray while in the W.H.E.R.E. form**

## Steps

![Likely rune](images/Likely_rune.png)
![a trained nethernaught ray just below quest hub in Oogothl Sprawl, will take you to far end of Coral Forest](images/A_trained_nethernaught_ray.png)

1. Collect items for Lady Najena to study. All items can be found in Oogothl Necropolis, Vital Terrace, Ver'Boten Atoll, and outside Zon Zobboz. Suggested sequence:
   - **(4) Terminus Tech Rune** {{waypoint -754, 144, 361}} in [[Ver'Bohten Atoll]]
     - Use [[A trained nethernaught ray]] at {{waypoint 193, 37, -201}} to get to Thralls Roost. (Note: The ray is actually *below*  the village in a small alcove. Jump down from {{waypoint 233, 78, -185}} ) .)
     - In Thralls Roost, use the [[Curious Mechanimagica]] pillar at {{waypoint -321.60, 14.73, 476.66}}  ) to teleport to  [[Ver'Bohten Atoll]], (NOT the spinning teleporter in front that's marked: To [[Oogothl Sprawl: Ver'Bohten Atoll (Solo)|Oogothl Sprawl: Ver'Bohten Atoll [Solo]]] )
     - Use the Curious Mechanimagica to return to Thralls Roost. Travel Southeast and use the ray at  {{waypoint -454, 23, 582}} to return to the Garden of Industry. Or use evac back to Obelisk.
   - **(1) Dark Sight Cabal Rune** {{waypoint 597, 173, -657}} in [[Oogothl Necropolis]].  Avoid [[Guorp]] by taking the path to the right at {{waypoint 587, 143, -578}}.
     - (If you have totem of escape, it will take you closer to the next one, **Sept of Ximor Rune)**
   - **(2) Sept of Ximor Rune** {{waypoint -40, 68, -437}} in [[Vital Terrace]], near Xhindar.
   - **(3) Zon Zobboz Syndicate Rune** {{waypoint -424, 79, 45}} OUTSIDE OF THE entrance [[Zon Zobboz Cattenate]].

1. Harvest 100x **non-food** resources in Oogothl Sprawl. Use any combination of nodes below:
   - Fallen Branch
   - Gem cluster
   - Rough Ore
   - Sturdy Roots
   - Animal Den (only the Grasslands Leather will update this step, not the meat)
1. Return to [[Flamebearer Denat]] at {{waypoint 169, 76, -164}} in the [[Oogothl Sprawl]].
   - NOTE:  The path that leads  back to [[Flamebearer Denat]] at {{waypoint 163.23, 47.95, -300.85}}
