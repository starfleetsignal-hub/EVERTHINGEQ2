## Steps

1. Destroy 5 piles of Ry'Gorr bones {{waypoint 582, 26, -224}}
   - Right-click to destroy the piles.  When you do, [[A newly risen Ry'Gorr]] will attack, but you do not have to kill it to update the quest.
1. Return to [[Dolur Axebeard]]

## Rewards

- At least 76c
- [[Ry'Gorr camp pelt]]
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Fighter's Ring]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Ring]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Ring]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Ring]] (All Mages)
