*The Scourge continue to expand throughout the wastes with no sign of slowing down, but if I kill some of their highest ranking leaders it may hinder their advance. I'll need to gather parts to throw into a cauldron somewhere within the keep to lure out Rinis or her old master - now in lich form - Yallessulich. That should eventually lead me to Sanctifier Yegigoth.*

## Steps

- Defeat targets within [[Scourge Keep (Heroic)]]
1. Kill [[Rinis the Everslaved (Heroic)|Rinis]] or [[Yallessulich (Heroic)|Yallessulich]].
1. Defeat [[Sanctifier Yegigoth (Heroic)|Yegigoth]].
