## Prerequisites

- You need at least +16,600 natural faction with The Order of Rime to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Inspect 'a dead shardfist' in front of the door to [[Emperor's Athenaeum]], at {{waypoint 858, -130, 159}}. Defeat the [[An ancient guardian|ancient guardian]] that spawns.
1. Inspect 'a dead shardfist' again to receive an 'Order of Rime satchel'.
1. Return to [[Delreri Feldrek]].

## Rewards

- [[An iksar bench]]
- [[Large Charasis pillar]]
- At least 41g 63s 17c
- +2600 faction with **The Order of Rime**
