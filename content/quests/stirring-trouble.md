## Steps

1. Hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 8 [[A murk slug|murk slugs]] in [[Savage Weald: Chaotic Caverns (Solo)]].
     - *Click the post outside [[Cadaverist Caves]] to enter the zone {{waypoint -910, 71, -414}}.*
   - Gather 10 **Manihot Root** in [[Savage Weald: Chaotic Caverns (Solo)]]. They kinda blend in with the rocks and found mostly in the hallways between rooms on the sides. If you are having trouble seeing them, try zooming FOCUS into first person OR turn on night vision as they easier to see. ![Manihot Root, blends into the rocks around it.](images/Manihot_Root.jpg)
     - Possible locations include:
       - {{waypoint -1087, 69, -417}}
       - {{waypoint -972, 67, -486}}
       - {{waypoint -932, 68, -494}}
       - {{waypoint -999, 53, -607}}
       - {{waypoint -1229, 71, -479}}
       - {{waypoint -1136, 70, -530}}
       - {{waypoint -1157, 71, -570}}
       - {{waypoint -1106, 70, -407}}
       - {{waypoint -1019, 67, -482}}
       - {{waypoint -997, 54, -587}}
1. Return to [[Maufoul]].
