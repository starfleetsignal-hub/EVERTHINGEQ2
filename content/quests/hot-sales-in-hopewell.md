## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 10 of the following (one of the two possible during the quest)
     - [[Arachnid sandstalker|arachnid sandstalkers]] for shells, for example around {{waypoint -273.95, -53.39, 652.02}}![An arachnid sandstalker](images/An_arachnid_sandstalker.png)
     - malicious dustmaw around {{waypoint -129, -4, 452}}
   - gather 6 of the following (one of the two possible during the quest) in [[Najami Oasis]] {{waypoint -45.38, 4.65, -18.18}}
     - mirage crystals ![Mirage Crystal](images/Mirage_crystal.png)
     - coconuts from palm trees
1. Return to [[Rahele]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- +1,000 faction with **Hizite Nomads of Ro**
- [[Mirage Crystal]]
