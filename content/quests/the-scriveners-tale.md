## Mail

Mail titled: Scrivener Requests an Audience.

I have been informed that the Scrivener has written your name into the tomes of accomplishments here in Myrist. This is a great honor and an audience has been requested with the Scrivener for you. Keep in mind he does not speak, so most of his communication will be through gestures or he may speak through someone else if that is a possibility. Perhaps, I can interpret for you.<br>Congratulations again and I hope to see you soon!<br>Signed,<br>Ghida Borrell

- Note: it may be possible to retrieve this quest from Jerol on Mara Island, should you happen to delete it or have an issue with the Norrathian Post (in-game mail).

## Steps

1. Speak with [[The Scrivener]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]] at {{waypoint 193, 15, -54}} on the [[Myrist Mezzanine]] (second floor) and listen to a translation of its speech from [[Ghida Borrell]].
1. Obtain an account of my deeds from following:
   - Go to the [[South Wing Catalog]] on the second floor of the Library, then take the teleporter at {{waypoint 388, 16, 170}} to the [[Smiths' Gallery]] and speak with [[Ramyn Matrick]] at {{waypoint 653, 411, 21}}.
   - Go to the [[South Wing Catalog]] on the second floor of the Library, then take the teleporter  at {{waypoint 332, 16, 174}} to the [[Crafters' Gallery]] and speak with [[Mourndax V'Zeaar (CG-NPC)|Mourndax V'Zeaar]] at {{waypoint 935, 412, 34}}.
   - Speak with [[Dylindra Morganna]] in the [[Myrist Librarian Quarters]] on the first floor of the Library at {{waypoint 306, -35, 165}}
   - Speak with [[Alamiri Lorfaere]] in the [[Myrist Conservatory]] at {{waypoint 59, -20, -92}}.
1. Speak with [[Ghida Borrell]] at {{waypoint 189, 15, -56}} 2nd Floor.
