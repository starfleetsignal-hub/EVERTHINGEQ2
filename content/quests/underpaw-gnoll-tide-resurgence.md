## Notes

- This is a repeatable quest.

## Steps

*Ranger Alphin is still concerned with the number of Underpaw gnolls deeper within the Darkpaw Warrens and wants me to return the to the Underpaw tunnels and thin out more of their number.*

1. Kill 15 Underpaw gnolls in Terramite Crystalline Grotto and Underpaw Stronghold
   - Terramite Crystalline Grotto: {{waypoint 72, 2, 210}} (past Splitpaw Burrow and up the rope ladder)
   - Underpaw Stronghold: {{waypoint 79, -25, 76}} (first right after entering Splitpaw Burrow)
   - Any larger Underpaw gnoll in either location will work (except for the Underpaw gnolls fighting Splitpaw gnolls)
1. Speak with Ranger Alphin in Darkpaw Dugout {{waypoint -12, -77, -250}}

## Rewards

- At least 3p 36g 79s 77c
- [[Underpaw Pitfighter Plushie]]
