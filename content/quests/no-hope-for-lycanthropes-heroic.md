## Steps

1. Kill 15 werewolves before time runs out (you have 3 minutes).

After picking up this quest, the antechamber to the west and the room to the west of that between them have more than enough werewolves. There are others in zone, but they are further away. Note that time starts ticking away immediately.

## Note

If your group doesn't have decent dps, you should consider pulling the vampire on the left past the door first and then start to dps on the werewolves. Start the quest before the first werewolf is dead.
