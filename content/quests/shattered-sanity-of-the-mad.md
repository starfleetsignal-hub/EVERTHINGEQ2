## Steps

1. To start the raid epic return to [[Neriak, City of Hate]] and talk to [[Dartain]] {{waypoint -708, 32, 233}}
1. You are then sent to kill
   - [[Imzok's Revenge]] in [[The Protector's Realm]]
   - [[The Leviathan]] in [[The Chamber of Destiny]]
   - [[Nexona]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]]
     - *Note: the updates can be done in any order.*
1. Return to [[Dartain]] for the last essence crystal.

[[Vazaelle, the Mad]] - Mythical version
