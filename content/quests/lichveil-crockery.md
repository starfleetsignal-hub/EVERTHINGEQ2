The Swimming Oak is depending on all of its crew members to ensure a financially successful sailing. I should help fill the plunder crate with ancient iksar pottery found on Lichveil Island.

## Steps

1. Collect ancient iksar pottery (6) ![Ancient Iksar Pottery](images/Lichveil_Crockery_quest_-_Iksar_Pottery.jpg)
1. Deposit the ancient iksar pottery in the plunder crate {{waypoint 747, 14, -228}} located near the cave entrance

## Rewards

- 3 [[Bobbing Acorn]]
