## Description

"Odo fears that the Crushbone orcs are turning the Faydark against itself. He would like me to travel to Treant Grove, west of Orc Hill, and search for coerced saplings. If I find them, he would like me to bring bark samples to Dhala Veera. I can find Dhala on Green Knoll, at the south end of Treant Grove." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Obtain 5 bark samples by killing [[Coerced sapling]]s just west of [[Orc Hill]]
1. Speak to [[Dhala Veera]] on [[Green Knoll]] at {{waypoint 614, 46, 51}}

## Rewards

- At least 2s 10c
- One of the following:
  - [[Cleansing Orb]]
  - [[Heavy Lead Scepter]]
  - [[Heavy Wristguards]]
