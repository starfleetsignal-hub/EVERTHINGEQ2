## Steps

1. You have been asked by [[Pantheera]] to exact revenge on those responsible for kidnapping and torturing the Kejek sojourners you have just rescued. Return to the [[Erudin Gardens]] and seek out some of the responsible parties.
   - Slay four [[A Theerian shadowblade|Theerian shadowblades]]
   - Slay six [[An Erudian neophyte|Erudian neophytes]]
   - Slay six [[An Erudian acolyte|Erudian acolytes]]
1. Return to Pantheera

## Rewards

- At least 55g 70s 61c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Deepwater Cape of Defense]]
  - [[Deepwater Cape of Destruction]]
  - [[Deepwater Cape of Stalking]]
  - [[Deepwater Cape of Rejuvenation]]

random drops can run this quest line without getting what you want in the drop.
