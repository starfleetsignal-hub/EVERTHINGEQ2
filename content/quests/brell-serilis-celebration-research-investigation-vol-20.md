## Steps

The herald of Brell Serilis has asked me to assist in gathering earthbound energy reserves for the avatar's summoning rituals. The energy needs to be pure for the ritual to succeed. I've been provided with a container to assist with collecting the earthbound energy, but it will only activate when powerful foes draw their last breath.

1. Collect 10 [[Earthbound Energy Reserves|earthbound energy]] to fill the container.  Collect [[Earthbound Energy Reserves|earthbound energy]] by defeating powerful foes
   - Overland bosses give one energy drop
   - Instance bosses give five energy drops
1. Return to the [[Herald to the Duke of Below]] {{waypoint -241, 212, -56}} in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
