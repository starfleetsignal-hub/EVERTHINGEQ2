## Prerequisites

- You must be level 80.
- You must have completed the quest [[The Broken Hand]].

## Steps

*Following the completion of [[The Broken Hand]] (Monk Group Epic), [[Master Makoto Shoda]] will instruct you to meditate in the shadows of the fangs.  This is the Area behind [[City of Mist]], where you start the Raid progression quest [[The Fate of Norrath]].*

1. In [[Kunzar Jungle]], you will find a rock formation at {{waypoint -276, 150, -1123}}. Click the rock and you will meditate. Following meditation you will receive the following update:
   - *The [[Pawbuster]] must be destroyed to ensure the safety of the people of Teren's Grasp.*
1. Kill the [[Pawbuster]].
1. Kill [[Venril Sathir]].
1. Kill the [[Shade of Khalan Dar]].
1. Return to [[Master Makoto Shoda]] and speak with him to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- [[The Restored Hand of Serenity]]
