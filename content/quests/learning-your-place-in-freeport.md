## Steps

1. Receive a [[Dog Whistle]] from Matthias. Use it to summon a pet dog.
1. Right-click the dog repeatedly and select either **pet** or **feed** untill you earn the dog's trust.
   - There is no pattern on what to pick, just sheer repetition.
1. Right-click the dog repeatedly again and select **sit** or **play dead** this time until you have taught the dog.
   - Again no pattern, just sheer repetition.
1. Talk to Matthias. He commands you to now kill the dog.
1. Right-click the dog one last time and select **attack**. You will swing a few times and kill the dog.
1. Talk to Matthias again.
