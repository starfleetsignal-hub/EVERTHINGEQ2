This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Collect the following items from [[Riven Plateau]]
   - Gather 6 withering wisp essence from the [[A withering wisp|withering wisps]].
   - Gather 6 patches of corrupted soil - sparkling clumps of dirt.  Know locations are as follows:
     - {{waypoint -3544, -757, -297}}
     - {{waypoint -3449, -757, -224}}
     - {{waypoint -3357, -754, -344}}
     - {{waypoint -3276, -760, -233}}
     - {{waypoint -3167, -757, -182}}
     - {{waypoint -3037, -758, -53}}
     - {{waypoint -2969, -754, -144}}
     - {{waypoint -2921, -752, -89}}
   - Collect 4 water samples from the large plants - look for stands of the large thistle-like flowers and then click the silver sparkles at their base.
1. Return to Researcher Grelga

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
