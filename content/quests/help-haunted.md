## Notes

- This quest does not show up in your journal.

## Steps

1. Help the ghosts
   - Help [[Tiki]] {{waypoint -94, -91, 443}} by completing the quest: [[The Spirit Sees All]]
   - Help [[Wik]] {{waypoint -158, -82, -531}} by completing the quest: [[Fallen But Not Forgotten]]
   - Help [[Zul]] {{waypoint 311, -91, -222}} by completing the quest: [[The Old Sting of Betrayal]]
   - Help [[Mog]] {{waypoint 136, -88, -500}} by completing the quest: [[Not Really Deep or Murky, Actually]]
1. Return to [[Tiptup Strongheart]].
   - *Note: Tiptup will not have a quest symbol over his head, but if you talk to him twice, he will give you your reward.*

## Rewards

- [[Little Lost Spirit]]
- At least 22g
- +2,000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
