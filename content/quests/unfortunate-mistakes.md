## Steps

1. Go to the soil under the tree near the [[The Peat Bog]] gate {{waypoint 581, -26, 372}}
   - You can visit the other 2 soil spots but they do not update the quest.
1. [[Ralkesh Orto]] walks up.  He speaks a dialog and then walks back to the the Peat Bog gates.  Go talk to him.
1. Return to Lakosha Maera.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 16c
- [[Pa'Rok Fetish]]
