## Steps

1. Listen to 3 other stories around City of Shar Vahl.
   - Find an empty pillow near the story tellers and wait for them to speak. Locations are around:
     - {{waypoint -225, 31, 160}}
     - {{waypoint -270, 34, 142}}
     - {{waypoint -245, 34, 101}}
1. Return to [[Shoraka Lano]].
   - During conversations with Shoraka, if you have previously [[Piercing the Darkness: The Sad Tale of Benosch Ironsprocket Part III|found their journal]] you are given dialogue options to return the jounral to them.  *You may safely return the journal in the dialogue, you will not "lose" it from your inventory.*
   - Before leaving it's a good idea to purchase [[Spirit of the Vah Shir]] port potions from within the Shar Vahl Kerrath Palace {{waypoint -48.83, 95.30, 344.69}} from the alchemist [[Chiallar]] at {{waypoint -184.22, 198.04, 594.20}}.
1. Return to [[Hulda Flintbraid]] with the news within [[The Bazaar]] {{waypoint -116, -1, 19}}.
   - You'll be granted flight after this step

## Rewards

- At least 3p 33g 49s 85c
- [[Vah Shir Pride Banner]]
- RoS Flag: Shadeweaver's Thicket Unlock
- 3 [[Spirit of the Vah Shir]] teleportation baubles
- Flight in Savage Weald unlocked
