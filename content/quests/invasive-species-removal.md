## Steps

1. Destroy creatures in the K'Val Ruins
   - Destroy 5 wildgrown Kromzek giants - found all through the ruins
   - Destroy 5 wildgrown wolves - found all through the ruins
   - Destroy 5 [[A briarthorn entraller|briarthorn entrallers]] - found in the throne room of the ruins
1. Return to Commander Bludeye
