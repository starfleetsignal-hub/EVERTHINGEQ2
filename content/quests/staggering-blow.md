## Steps

1. Kill 12 Rime soldiers
   - Any of the Order of Rime types count, except for Rime Field Commanders.  Their summoned pets do not.  Note: the Rime Advancers do not count; it seems that the Rime soldiers must be within the Storm Gorge gate.
1. Return to General Icemaul.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
