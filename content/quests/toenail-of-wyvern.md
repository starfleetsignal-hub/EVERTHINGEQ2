## Steps

1. Kill [[A young wyvern|young wyverns]] until one drops a [[Suitable Wyvern Toenail]] (body drop). {{waypoint -181, 38, -271}}
1. Use the [[Wyvern Toenail Grinder]]. It will convert the toenail to [[Ground Wyvern Toenail Dust]].
1. Return to [[Gillin Henry]] at [[Brakthyr's Post]]. {{waypoint 207, 71, -944}}

## Rewards

- At least 14g 81s 38c
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
