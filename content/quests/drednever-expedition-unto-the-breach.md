## Prerequisitea

- You need to be able to speak [[Gnomish]] to start the quest

## Steps

1. Slay the following monsters, whom are all found throughout the first level:
   - 14 [[A quetzsol|quetzols]]
   - 6 [[A lava crawler|lava crawlers]]
1. Return to Ognit.

## Rewards

- At least 8g 60s 47c
- +500 faction with **Drednever Expedition**
- One of the following:
  - [[Ring of the Drednever Archaeologist]]
  - [[Ring of the Drednever Explorer]]
