## Steps

![A Submerged Chest](images/A_Submerged_Chest.jpg)

1. Jump into 'Bridge Pond', the pond beneath the [[Bridge of Zek]]. {{waypoint -192, -29, -510}}
1. Slay monsters around [[The Deathfist Citadel|Deathfist Citadel]]:
   - 6 [[A Deathfist salvager|Deathfist salvager]]s (level 40). They are found all around Deathfist Citadel, particularly on the west side. {{waypoint 200, -36, -622}}
   - 4 [[A moatwater kelp horror|moatwater kelp horror]]s (level 39) in the moat around Deathfist Citadel. They can be pulled onto land. {{waypoint 151, -54, -686}}
1. Find four submerged chests in the moat around Deathfist Citadel. They are random spawns.
1. Return to Orca
