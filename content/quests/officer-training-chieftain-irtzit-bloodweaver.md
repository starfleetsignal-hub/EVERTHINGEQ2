## Note

*Make sure that your Brokenskull Officer Illusion is active while starting/completing the quest.*

## Steps

1. Speak with or kill [[Chieftain Irtzit Bloodweaver]] in [[Grobb]] {{waypoint -345, -75, -883}} to obtain his staff.
1. Return to [[Petty Officer Kimtiz]] in [[Brokenskull Loft]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
