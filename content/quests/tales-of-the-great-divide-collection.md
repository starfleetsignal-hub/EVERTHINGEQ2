This is not a normal collection quest. The collection members are not obtained by harvesting shinies.<br>Instead, the collection members are themselves all obtained from the completion of other collection quests. This is called a "meta-collection".

- The members of this collection are rewards from several other collections from the [[Great Divide]]
- The reward of this collection is needed for the collection [[The Chronicles of Velious]]
