Several chapters of Volume II of The Planar Chronicles detail what to expect from some of the elemental planes. I'll need to be fully prepared, with weapons drawn at all times, to make it out alive.

## Steps

- Complete this chapter on [Solo] missions.:
  - Slay Sterek Swiftwind in [[Eryslai: The Midnight Aerie (Solo)]]
  - Dethrone the King of Mud in [[Vegarlson: The Terrene Rift (Solo)]]
  - Defeat Ra-Sekjet, the Molten in [[Doomfire: The Enkindled Towers (Solo)]]
  - Kill Tethys All-Mother in [[Awuidor: Marr's Ascent (Solo)]]

## Rewards

- At least 5p 19g 75s 122,400 status
- 35 [[Archivist's Tremissis]]
