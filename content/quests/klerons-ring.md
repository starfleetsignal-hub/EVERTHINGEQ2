This quest is the last of a 6-quest series. You must have completed the first 5 quests from Kleron in order to recieve this one.

## Steps

1. Dig up a *vial of poison* hidden in mushrooms near the base of [[N'Mar's Ascent]] {{waypoint -412, 6, -1271}}.
1. Gather 6 stomachs from [[A Nerius crab|Nerius crabs]] on the beach of [[Port Naythex]].
1. Kill 3 [[Nerius dockhand|Nerius dockhands]] in the pirate camps near the docks.
   - All the differing Nerius pirates placehold for each other. If you don't see any dockhands, start killing the others.
1. Visit [[Hidden Beach Path]]. {{waypoint 649, 0, -307}}
1. Harvest a black sapphire by mining [[Gemstone Nodes#T3|eventide nodes]].
1. Kill 10 Nerius 'fools' in the camps by the dock.
   - These are any of the Nerius Pirates.
1. Return to [[Kleron Asana]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Crest of Displaced Time]] (All Priests)
  - [[Signet of Displaced Time]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Emblem of Displaced Time]] (All Mages)
  - [[Band of Displaced Time]] (All Scouts)
