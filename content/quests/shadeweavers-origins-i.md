Within the Midst Library, I discovered a tome entitled, Shadeweaver's Origins I, with pages of notes and diagrams of the thicket's environs, but little in the way of its inhabitants. This particular volume focuses on the eastern regions of Shadeweaver's Thicket, for which I feel is my duty to record its fauna, dead or alive, but preferably alive.
