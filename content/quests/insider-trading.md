Gain Amiable Faction with The City of Paineel by completing an extended series of subquests in service to the Craftkeepers.

## Steps

1. Enter [[The City of Paineel]].
1. Complete the subquests from [[Native Knowledge]] to [[News from Above]].
1. Return to [[Or'Iku]] in the Mercantile Court {{waypoint 1727, -282, 3481}}.
   - When visiting [[Or'Iku]] accept the [[Report to Quel'ule]] quest that is now available from [[Adeela]]
1. Return to [[Felice Adae]]

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
