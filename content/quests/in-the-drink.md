## Steps

1. Receive [[Location Buoys]] from the Shipping Coordinator.
1. Swim under water at the end of the docks and look for the crates on the sea floor. Examine them and attach a buoy to each one. When you surface from the water, you'll notice that barrels now appear to be floating in the water. These are the buoys.
1. Return to [[Shipping Coordinator Hando]] to tell him the buoys are in place.
1. Return to [[Burk Stoneshatter]], give him his missing shipment, and receive your reward.
