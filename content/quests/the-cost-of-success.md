## Steps

1. Return to your level-appropriate instance and complete the required steps, listed in the next section:
   - **[[#The Roekillik Excavation|The Roekillik Excavation]]: Up to level 30**; in the [[The Commonlands|Commonlands]] at {{waypoint 0, -48, 726}} (See Talk Discussion)
   - **[[#The Mysterious Mine|The Mysterious Mine]]: Levels 30-45**; in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] at {{waypoint -170, -18, -196}}
   - **[[#The Icy Dig|The Icy Dig]]: Levels 45+**; in [[Everfrost]] at {{waypoint 107, -4, -1143}}
1. Once all the steps for your respective instance are completed, return to Pearl Honeywine or Mareva D'Iniv.

## Instances

### [[The Roekillik Excavation]]

1. Head to the small arena near the north center of the zone. [[Gladion]], a 27 epic x2 minotaur, is in the middle of the arena, and three 27^^ [[Reverent Controller|Reverent Controllers]] are on the surrounding ledges. Gladion is not aggro by himself, nor is he linked with the Controllers, but attacking either Gladion or the Controllers causes both to attack you.
1. The cage is under a ledge at {{waypoint 135, -48, 749}}. Once you grab it, a roekillik sandbagger will appear, stun you and snatch the cage away.

### [[The Mysterious Mine]]

1. Upon entering, take the left path. It is guarded by the same groups of roekillik in the previous quest.
1. Once you reach the mine elevator room, take the north tunnel. Half way in is a group of two roekillik miners^ and a foreman^^. At the end of the tunnel is a room, guarded by an epicx2 greater rumbler. Behind and to the left of it is a small, antique cage. Grabbing it will summon a roekillik sandbagger, who snatches it away from you.

### [[The Icy Dig]]

1. Enter the citadel in the same manner as in the [[Revelation|previous quest]].
1. Head to the lower level via the ramp at the north end of the first floor. {{waypoint 34, 3, -416}}
1. Go through the doors at {{waypoint 77, -35, -265}}.
1. Slay [[The Tundra Queen]] and examine 'A small, unique cage' directly behind it at {{waypoint -100, -46, -335}}. A roekillik sandbagger will spawn and make off with the artifact before you have a chance to loot it.
   - The quest will update for everyone in the group when one person clicks the cage.
