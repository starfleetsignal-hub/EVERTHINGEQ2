- *This quest is unavailable for characters of Evil-alignment.*
- *Characters may need to have completed [[Bark Like a Gnoll]] to gain this quest.*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Bulvar Rakgam]] at [[Gnollslayer Keep]] {{waypoint -2114, -40, 435}}.
1. Speak to [[Barstan Rheyble]] at {{waypoint -2116, -46, 440}}. This will then give you the main part of the quest, which is to collect more trinkets.
1. Collect the following items: *(these mobs spawn super fast (1 to 2 min), and the items are body drops, so they can be looted even if the mob is gray)*
   - [[Trinket: A Miniature Scepter]] is obtained from [[Animator Z'Apha]] {{waypoint -2013, -6, 530}}, level 17 named with two or three [[A rotstuffed scarecrow|rotstuffed scarecrow]] (16vvv) adds.
   - [[Trinket: A Small Metal Figure]] is obtained from [[Bra'ak the Reptile]] {{waypoint -2176, 9, 94}}, level 18 named with one add.
   - [[Trinket: A Bone Carving]] is obtained from [[Tactician Mengs]] {{waypoint -1759, 0.5, 912}}, a level 18 named with two level 18 [[A Darkpaw brigand|Darkpaw brigand]] adds.
   - [[Trinket: A Petrified Bark]] is obtained from [[Graalwen]] {{waypoint -1654.7, 7.34, -69.8}}, a level 18v named.
   - [[Trinket: A Small Cube]] is obtained from [[Jayl'Krik]]  {{waypoint -1643.5, 14.0, 30.9}}, a level 18v named.
1. Once all the trinkets are collected, place them on the *Stone of Graw* located within the underwater cave at {{waypoint -1022, -47, 1178}}, which is near an alternate entrance to [[Blackburrow]]. {{waypoint -1092, -33, 1074}}
   - **Warning**: If you have the quest [[Passage to the Isle of Zek]] and haven't already killed [[Gawar the Bad]], he will appear in the cave with two adds.
1. Speak to the "elder Sabertooth spirit" that spawns (non-attackable).  You must be able to speak gnoll.

## Rewards

- Experience only
