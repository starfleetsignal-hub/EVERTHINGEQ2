## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[Manafin salamander]] near {{waypoint -920, 7, 453}} and receive a [[Filled Hemocapsule]] after the final kill.
1. Use the [[Filled Hemocapsule]] on 3 [[Tidal glider]] near {{waypoint 716, 10, 817}} or near {{waypoint -240.16, 5.20, -963.12}}
   - They will become agro. If you kill them after you apply the hemocapsule, it is fine, you still get the update
1. Return to [[Ta-hetu]].
1. Click on the Manafin Blood Vessel at {{waypoint -445, 165, -805}}. ![Manafine Blood Vessel](images/Manafine_Blood_Vessel.jpg)
