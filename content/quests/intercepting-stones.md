## Notes

- Good-aligned players start this series with the quest [[Sunwalker and Stones]].

## Steps

1. Examine an impaled skeleton near [[The Temple of Scale (POI)|The Temple of Scale]]. {{waypoint 39, 47, -146}}
   - Examining the impaled skeleton will break invis.
1. Return to [[A Freeport emissary (TT)|a Freeport emissary]].
1. Examine the following pillars, all situated near each other at a ruin in [[The Temple Grounds]]:
   - The locations below may not correspond to the numbers as listed. The pillars are examinable in any order. For many of the pillars you need to move the mouse pointer over a specific area to be able to right-click and examine the pillar **(the mouse pointer needs to be about halfway up the pillar)**. This is particularly true for the pillars still standing. Except the first one, two pillars are lying the ground.
   - {{waypoint 84, 57, -12}}
   - {{waypoint 101, 57, -9}}
   - {{waypoint 119, 57, 0}}
   - {{waypoint 136, 57, -2}}
   - {{waypoint 127, 57, -8}}
   - {{waypoint 128, 57, 3}}
   - {{waypoint 114, 57, 3}}
   - {{waypoint 109, 57, 4}}
   - {{waypoint 100, 57, 4}}
   - {{waypoint 92, 57, -2}}
1. Kill six [[A storm cell|storm cells]] floating above the ponds in the Temple Grounds for a common update. {{waypoint -22, 48, -112}}
1. Examine a pile of sacred stones at the bottom of the pond {{waypoint -12, 0, -134}}. [[Creature of the Black Pond]], a level 57-58 heroic ^^ mob, will spawn. You do not need to kill him to complete quest.
1. Return to [[A Freeport emissary (TT)|a Freeport emissary]] in [[Hidden Refuge (Tenebrous Tangle)|Hidden Refuge]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Flickering Gem of Criticals]]
