## Steps

1. Destroy 8 [[A fully-functioning Nurgan death-copter|fully-functioning Nurgan death-copters]] in the [[Mines of Nurga]]. {{waypoint -300, 53, -1961}}
   - The Nurgan death-copters start as [[A broken Nurgan machine|broken Nurgan machines]] lying on the ground with a [[A Nurgan minegineer|Nurgan minegineer]] right next to it. Kill the Nurgan minegineer as close as possible to the broken Nurgan machine and a fully-functioning Nurgan death-copter will spawn.
1. Return to Tinkerist Nalzie.

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- +5000 faction with **Exiles of Droga**
