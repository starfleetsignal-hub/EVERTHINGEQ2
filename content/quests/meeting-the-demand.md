1. Kill jungle cats for *fine pelts* (uncommon update):
   - 5 kunzar tigers: [[A kunzar tiger|Kunzar tigers]], [[A mature kunzar tiger|mature kunzar tigers]] or [[A bloodstripe kunzar bengal|bloodstripe kunzar bengals]] {{waypoint 363, -32, -49}}

1. Return to Kriinz Nax.
