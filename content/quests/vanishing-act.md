## Steps

1. Find three more pieces of equipment. Known locations:
   - {{waypoint -1831, 238, 302}}
   - {{waypoint -1816, 239, 295}}
   - {{waypoint -1804, 239, 295}}
1. Kill a visage of fear near {{waypoint -1816, 239, 295}}
1. Follow the trail of sparkles to {{waypoint -2399.80, 208.23, 821.31}}
1. Kill the three scarelings that pop.
1. Search for four pieces of evidence. These look like rocks with a white sparkle. Know locations:
   - {{waypoint -2420, 210, 819}}
   - {{waypoint -2420, 210, 797}}
   - {{waypoint -2401, 211, 837}}
   - {{waypoint -2386, 209, 833}}
1. Speak to [[Hul'Ylgith]] at {{waypoint -1327, 219, 607}}  in the amygdalan camp in Fearcreep to complete the quest.
