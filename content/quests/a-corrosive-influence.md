## Steps

1. Search for Dhakiy's fellows and try to see if their minds are still intact and worth saving
   - Find the lab workers. These are near the front of the zone. Kill them.
   - Find [[Kadara'Mola]] in the Labs. She's in the same room as the lab workers. Clear all of them first. Kill her.
   - Find the [[A phantasmic scientist|scientists]] (93^ heroic) that ran the experiments within the Lab. Kill them.
     - These are in [[The Dormatorium]]
   - Find [[Zaina Zumurrud]], the Head Librarian. Kill her. (read Zaina's page for the strategy to kill her)
1. Return to [[Dhakiy]]

## Rewards

- At least 90g 70s 61c
