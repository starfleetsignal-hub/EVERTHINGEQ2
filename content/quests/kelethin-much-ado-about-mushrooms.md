This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Visit 3 mushrooms and release the spores from them:
   1. mushroom near Tunare's Sapling {{waypoint 80, 3, -96}} {{waypoint -85.85, 7.97, -74.00}} {{waypoint -73, 5, -77}}
   1. mushroom near the Upper Emerald River {{waypoint -550, 48, 177}}
   1. mushroom east of the Crossing the River Road {{waypoint -449, 31, -74}} or {{waypoint -217, -1.5, 40}}
1. Return to Gibrien.

## Rewards

- -3000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- +3000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
- -3000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- Experience
