## Description

Upon inspecting the bust of Rallos Zek, I heard the voice of Rallos Zek and felt strongly compelled to carry out his bidding! Test the nobility of the Kromzek in the face of adversity! A great warrior shall show them their faults!

## Steps

Slay 15 Kromzek nobles in the Grand Kael area.

## Rewards

- At least 10p
- +50 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
- +50 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
- [[Primal Velium Shard]] x1
