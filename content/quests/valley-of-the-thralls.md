Thaldor, an accomplished dhalgar mage, hopes his stupefy potion will save anyone recently enthralled by the Aberrants, by knocking them unconscious.

## Steps

1. Free 8 [[A dhalgar thrall|dhalgar thralls]] or [[A gnemlin thrall|gnemlin thralls]] in [[Blightstone Barrens]] {{waypoint 622, 91, -611}} using the stupefy wand.
1. Return to [[Thaldhor]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 41g 75s
- +1000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Glinting Dagger of Maldura]]
  - [[Glinting Hammer of Maldura]]
  - [[Great Hammer of Maldura]]
  - [[Great Staff of Maldura]]
  - [[Great Cleaver of Maldura]]
  - [[Great Axe of Maldura]]
