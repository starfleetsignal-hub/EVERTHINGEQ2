*This quest is repeatable up to 5 times..*

## Notes

- [[Turgen Bremhurst]] at [[Bridge Keep]] asks you to kill many of the same mobs for [[Bridge Keep Threats]] so it is a good idea to work on the quests together.

## Steps

1. You are asked to kill between 9-12 of one of the following monster types (chosen randomly):
   - centaurs
   - giants
   - gnolls
   - skeletons  -  drowned protectors bridge keep are good
   - sirens
1. Return to Holly Rowan.

## Rewards

- At least 17s 13c
