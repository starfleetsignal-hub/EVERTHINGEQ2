## Steps

Quest becomes available after completing [[The Case of the Missing Fungus]].

Bring back 10 vile crystals from the water in the [[Corrupted Tillage Patch]].

- Glowing crystals are in the water
  - {{waypoint -698, -978, 295}}
  - {{waypoint -748, -978, 297}}
  - {{waypoint -945, -978, 373}}
  - {{waypoint -995, -978, 310}}
  - {{waypoint -1005, -978, 370}}
  - {{waypoint -1020, -978, 341}}
  - {{waypoint -1046, -978, 250}}
  - {{waypoint -1050, -978, 170}}
  - {{waypoint -1090, -978, 177}}
  - {{waypoint -1117, -978, 130}}
- They are located at eye level when underwater.  Turn your particle effects up and you will see them far away.  They are difficult to see with low graphics.
