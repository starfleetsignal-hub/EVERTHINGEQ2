**The Fourth Warrior - Making the New Cloak** isn't a quest in its own right, but a placeholder for several quests and subquests that your receive when speaking with Juna about the cloaks you found working on [[The Fourth Warrior - Finding the Cloaks]]. In speaking with her, you can receive all of the following quests:

- [[The Fourth Warrior - The Ardent Cowl]] (which requires the completion of four subquests)
- [[The Fourth Warrior - The Solusek Mining Co.]]
- [[The Fourth Warrior - Jhal'Dolek]]

The quests can be obtained all at once and completed at the same time as one another.
