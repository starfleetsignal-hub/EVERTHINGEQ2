## Steps

The [[An Everling manservant|Everling manservants]] must pay for what they've done!

1. Kill the manservants to receive [[Guestroom key 01]]. They are found at the following locations:
   - {{waypoint -31, 0, -50}}
   - {{waypoint -21, 0, -60}}
   - {{waypoint -30, 0, -46}}
1. Examine the key to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 53s 67c
- Access to the upstairs balcony
