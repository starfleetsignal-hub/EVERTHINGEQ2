This quest provides [[Alchemist|Alchemists]] with a recipe to distill dusts into higher level ones.

## Steps

1. Collect the following items from [[Everfrost]] and [[Lavastorm]]:
   - Find an **ice crystal** in [[Everfrost]], under water, under the docks at {{waypoint -56, -32, 157}}
   - Collect a **frostfin scale** in [[Everfrost]], found anywhere on the waterline, but up on the ice flows, not in the water.  {{waypoint -203, -20, 108}}
   - Find a **lava crystal** in [[Lavastorm]] on the beach {{waypoint -226.26, -129.21, 474.61}}
   - Find a **magma wormling scale** in [[Lavastorm]], near the rocks where the [[Magma wormling|magma wormlings]] bask on the hot beach. {{waypoint -112.27, -128.76, 483.61}}
1. Return the components to [[Alchemist Janicia]] for your reward.

## Notes

- You must be a level 70 [[Alchemist]] to get this quest.
- This quest can be completed as a level 10 adventurer with little difficulty, just be cautious of the sharks in Everfrost.*
- This quest cannot be shared with other players.
