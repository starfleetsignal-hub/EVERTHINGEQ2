## Steps

1. Collect three herbs northeast from Tora. They can be difficult to see against the dark rock. You can find them around:  ![Harvest this herb](images/Ritual_Gathering_quest_-_herb.jpg)
   - {{waypoint -12, -374, -690}}
   - {{waypoint -29, -373, -695}}
   - {{waypoint -52, -374, -697}}
   - Watch out for the aggro spiders that roam near [[Blisterrock Ridge]].
1. Return to [[Tora]] at {{waypoint 110, -346, -609}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
