This is the fourth of the Kerran racial quests in Freeport.

## Steps

1. Batarra sends you to creep up on the Erudite conspirators you had previously overheard and kill one of them if you are presented an opportunity.
1. Return to where you'd overheard the Erudites talking, and you'll be jumped from the shadows.  An Erudite conspirator (level 3-ish single mob) will attack.  Kill this erudite.
1. Return to Batarra to tell her of your success.

## Rewards

- [[Kejaan Stone Ring]] -  +2 agi, +2 wis, +5 health, +7 vs mental, applies Kejaan Aura when equipped, which increases max health of the wearer by 24
