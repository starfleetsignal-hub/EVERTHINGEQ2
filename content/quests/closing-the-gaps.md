## Description

Strange magic oddities, or 'tears', have been appearing in the area around the Eternal Prism. I must locate the source of these tears, and attempt to seal them shut using the Archaic Relic. Once they are sealed, I should return to Camp Wyndalsyd within Everfrost to report.

## Steps

1. Search within [[Miragul's Phylactery: Scion of Ice]] for 6 sources of the tears, then seal them using the received *[[Archaic relic|archaic relic]]*
1. Use the archaic relic on an arcane rift. They are random spawns and the group does get the updates at the same time.
1. Return for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]]
