## Description

It seems that strange fluctuations in the magical energies have been felt by the scholars studying the Eternal Prism. They believe that latent magical energies within the Prism itself might be responsible for the irregularities, and have asked that I quell these powers when I find them.

## Steps

1. Look for magical anomalies ( any mob ) within [[Miragul's Phylactery: Scion of Ice]] and destroy 12 of them. (any 12 will do)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
