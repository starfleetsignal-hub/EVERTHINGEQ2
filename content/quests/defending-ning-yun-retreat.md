This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Alternately repel either 8 Gruengachs, Overseers, or Slobberjaws.  The easier Slobberjaws are near {{waypoint 34, 298, 350}}, and overseers can be found around {{waypoint 374, 374, 659}}.
1. Report back to [[Wu Gang]].

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +2000 faction with **Disciples of the Circling Destiny**
