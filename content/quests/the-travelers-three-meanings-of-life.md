## Steps

1. Find [[N'Mar's Ascent]] at {{waypoint -369.36, 11.50, -1213.80}} or [[Behemoth Pond]] at {{waypoint -236, 3, -276}}
1. Speak with the Traveling Augur.
1. Harvest [[Root Nodes#T3|rancid roots]] for three rare updates.
1. Speak with the Traveling Augur.
1. Learn personal effort by hunting three [[An ash wolf|Ash wolves]] {{waypoint -1411, 18, -1123}}.
1. Speak with the Traveling Augur.

- At least 5s 67c

  - [[Augur's Bracelet of Sense]]
