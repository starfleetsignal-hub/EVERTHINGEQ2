## Prerequisites

- You must have started the quest [[When Darkness Comes]] or [[Eliminate Geraufl]] in order to complete this quest.

## Steps

1. Run up the wall at {{waypoint -100, 14, 184}}
1. Follow the wall until you are directly under a tree {{waypoint -88, 20, 216}} and look up.
1. Select the straight, light brown branch within the tree.
1. Speak with Fruppen for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
