## Steps

Create 12 items from the random list below:

- [[A pristine teak single bed|teak single bed]]
- [[A pristine standard teak bench|standard teak bench]]
- [[A pristine teak bookcase|teak bookcase]]
- [[A pristine comely teak chair|comely teak chair]]
- [[A pristine teak dining chair|teak dining chair]]
- [[A pristine standard teak chair|standard teak chair]]
- [[A pristine small ornate teak table|small ornate teak table]]
- [[A pristine sturdy teak chair|sturdy teak chair]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

## Rewards

- At Least 54s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
