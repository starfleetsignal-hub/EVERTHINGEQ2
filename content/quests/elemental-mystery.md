## Restriction

Unfortunately, completing this quest will also update the Cleansing quest. After you do 3 Cleansing quests, you are locked out from getting any more for 24 hours. There are two suggestions to avoid that:

First, DELETE the cleansing quest just after spawning the elemental lord to make sure you don't lose the possibility of the update. {14:34, 1st April 2009 (UTC)}

Second, considering this restriction and planning before doing this quest. Since this quest line requires only two Elemental Lords, while you can get three Cleansing quests before the lock-down, it wouldn't be much trouble. (2nd November 2009)

## Steps

1. I must find a way to reanimate Najena's Elemental Lord of Poison.
   - NOTE: You must be on the step "Find and destroy an Elemental Lord" of either the quest [[Cleansing the Elements]], [[Cleansing the Elements Again]], or [[The Last of the Elemental Cleansing]] to spawn the Elemental Lord of Poison.
   - Return to the three caves. Pick one of the 3 rooms and right-click on each of the 3 clickable pillars and choose Infuse with Poison.
   - Click the 4th pillar and choose Activate the Orbs.
1. Kill the Elemental Lord that spawns.
