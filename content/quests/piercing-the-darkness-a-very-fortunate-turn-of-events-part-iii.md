## Note

When you are in the right location to read [[Benosch Ironsprocket's Enchanted Field Guide to Luclin]], you will see a message in your chat window *"You have entered an area that could prove fruitful if examining the spelled book." *The areas listed below do NOT have to be followed in order*.*

## Steps

1. Examine/read [[Benosch Ironsprocket's Enchanted Field Guide to Luclin]] in the following places in [[The Blinding]]. If you can fly and do it in this order, it forms a loop leading back from the Tor to the Spire.
   - Entrance to Sanctus Seru near {{waypoint 700, 52, 684}}
   - [[Recuso Tor]] near {{waypoint -538, 60, 340}}
   - Teeming Caldera near {{waypoint -302, 7, -26}}
   - Marus Plateaus near {{waypoint -126, 82, 218}}
   - Grieg's Spire near {{waypoint 602, 429, -583}}
- Otherwise, run it in this order and take the tamed Shik'Nar from the Tor to the Spire at the end. You can also fast travel back to Grieg's Spire and do the travel in opposite order.
  - Entrance to Sanctus Seru near {{waypoint 700, 52, 684}}
  - Marus Plateaus near {{waypoint -126, 82, 218}}
  - Teeming Caldera near {{waypoint -302, 7, -26}}
  - [[Recuso Tor]] near {{waypoint -538, 60, 340}}
  - Grieg's Spire near {{waypoint 602, 429, -583}}
1. Return to [[Hulda Flintbraid]] in [[Sanctus Seru: Fabrication Hall]] to receive a [[Sealed Letter to Daini the Wright]].
1. Return to [[The Blinding]]
1. once again examine/read the book in the following places in the zone and deliver the letter
   - Read the book at 5 places
     - Entrance to Sanctus Seru near {{waypoint 700, 52, 684}}
     - Marus Plateaus near {{waypoint -126, 82, 218}}
     - Teeming Caldera near {{waypoint -302, 7, -26}}
     - [[Recuso Tor]] near {{waypoint -538, 60, 340}}
     - Grieg's Spire near {{waypoint 602, 429, -583}}
1. Speak to [[Daini the Wright]] inside a building in [[Recuso Tor]] near {{waypoint -603, 35, 446}}
1. Enter the [[Aurelian Coast]]
   - (The easiest way to do this is to zone into Sanctus Seru, then turn around and click on the zone out, selecting Aurelian Coast, this places you at the following location).
   - Then travel to the [[Sanctus Seru's Dawnshroud Gate]] near {{waypoint 468, 132, -480}}.
1. Read the book to complete the quest. To get the next quest click on the option to read the faint text.
   - *Note: Once you complete this step in the TS line, you can purchase travel baubles in Sanctus Seru.  They are [[Will of the Blinding]], [[Will of the Coast]], [[Will of Seru]] and [[Will of the Wracklands]]. Purchase from bartender at {{waypoint 11, 181, 176}}. These make travel throughout Luclin much more efficient. You will not be able to use them until you are level 120, either TS or Adventurer.*
