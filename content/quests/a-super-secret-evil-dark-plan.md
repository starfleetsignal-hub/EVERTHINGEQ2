## Prerequisites

- Neriak citizenship.

## Steps

![bloodwine root](images/Bloodwine_root.jpg)

1. Collect some items:
   - Harvest 8 [[Bloodwine root]] from roots around Neriak
   - Get the [[Sacrificial Urn of Bile]] from one of the warehouses (behind the counter with the General Goods seller) in the [[New Foreign Quarter]]  {{waypoint 142, 22, 114}}
1. Return to Mur'ss Z'Hel'Viiryn
1. Take the Sacrificial Urn of Bile to [[Tsabrar De'Kenmtor]] in [[Neriak Down Under]] {{waypoint -135, -5, 265}}. He will become aggro and attack you (level 10). Kill him.
1. Return to Mur'ss Z'Hel'Viiryn

## Rewards

![Touch of Arasai](images/Touch_of_Arasai_%28displayed%29.jpg)

- At least 2s 10c
- [[Touch of Arasai]]
