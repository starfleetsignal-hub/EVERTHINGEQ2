## Steps

1. Retrieve the four pieces of the recipe from [[Graystone Yard: Herald of Rage]]
   - On a barrel near the docks {{waypoint 893, -23, -143}}
   - Inside the former bank and armory {{waypoint 914, -19, -126}}
   - Also inside the former bank and armory {{waypoint 909, -19, -131}}
   - Inside the former provisioner's {{waypoint 826, -20, -133}}
1. Return to Mav Boilfist
1. Seek [[Bronlor Stormhammer (Qeynos Capitol District)|Bronlor Stormhammer]] near the Temple of Life, North Qeynos {{waypoint 504, -31, -181}}
1. Search the Concordium Tower for the book "Stormhammer Legacy" - take the blue portal, the book is at {{waypoint 709, 35, 142}}
1. Return to Mav
