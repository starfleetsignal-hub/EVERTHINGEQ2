*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Vashazdar Manoisti]], who is standing next to Gretchen.
1. Find the Toppled Hand of Thule {{waypoint -1000, -0, 840}}. Note that there are more than one - this is in the south-east.
1. Slay the [[Left Eye of Thule]] and his two friends, [[Eye of Thule subservient]], who spawn when you approach the toppled hand.{{waypoint -1017, -0, 829}} (All are roughly level 42)
1. Return to [[Vashazdar Manoisti]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 99s 37c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Cavalier's Pauldrons (44)|Cavalier's Pauldrons]] (Plate Fighters)
  - [[Mentor's Arm Wraps]] ([[Bruiser]], [[Monk]])
  - [[Primalist's Spaulders]] ([[Defiler]], [[Mystic]])
  - [[Scholar's Epaulets]] (All Mages)
  - [[Thief's Chain Spaulders]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Vicar's Plate Pauldrons]] ([[Inquisitor]], [[Templar]])
  - [[Wanderer's Shoulder Pads]] ([[Fury]], [[Warden]])
