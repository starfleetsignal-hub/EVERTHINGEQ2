## Steps

1. Enter the [[The Willow Wood: Besieged Hamlet]] from the Willow Wood gate in [[The Elddar Grove (POI)|The Elddar Grove]], [[Qeynos Province District]]. {{waypoint 810, -21, -563}}
1. Slay 8 invading gnolls.
1. Heal 6 injured defenders with Akina's holy focus (a symbol of purity).
   - On a hill. {{waypoint 808, -15, -588}}
   - Behind houses. {{waypoint 807, -20, -617}}
   - On the docks. {{waypoint 830, -21, -762}}
1. Return to [[Akina]].

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Celestial Watch**
