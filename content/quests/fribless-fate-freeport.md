## Steps

1. Kill [[Belazzor Fribles, the Inept (Commonlands)|Belazzor Fribles]], a Ratonga Pirate, just West of the [[Crossroads]].  He spawns when you are around {{waypoint -313, -47, -301}}
1. Return to [[Abner Siblet]] in [[North Freeport]] for your bottle of Thundermist Merlot.
1. This is where you start backtracking.
   - Return to [[Barb Islenet (North Freeport)|Barb Islenet]] and give her the [[Thundermist Merlot]]. She will then give you the Pretty Pink Googles.
   - Return to [[Zoe Hertzflip (West Freeport)|Zoe Hertzflip]] and give her the [[Pretty Pink Goggles]]. She will then give you Zoe's Wild Deck of cards.
   - Return to [[Frethe Zizlop (City of Freeport)|Frethe Zizlop]] and give him [[Zoe's Wild Deck of Playing Cards]]. He will give you the [[Tin Metal Helm]].
   - Return to [[Blat Berisen (Commonlands)|Blat Berisen]] and give him the [[Tin Metal Helm]]. Blat Berisen then turns into [[The Grand Prankster]].
