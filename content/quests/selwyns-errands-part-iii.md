## Steps

1. Kill the following in [[The Thundering Steppes]]:
   - 8 skeletons
     - One excellent location is {{waypoint 55.69, -20.71, -252.56}}, as they are near the writhers.
   - 8 [[A rock writher|rock writhers]]s
     - These can be found near {{waypoint 45.22, -22.60, -278.40}}
1. Return to Selwyn.
