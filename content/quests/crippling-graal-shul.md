## Steps

1. Perform the following tasks in the [[Gra'al Shul Temple]] {{waypoint -1755, 56, -198}}:
   - Kill 3 [[A Gra'al Shul alchemist|Gra'al Shul alchemist]]s.
   - Taint an alchemy lab (click on one of the tables next to where the alchemists are standing.)
1. Return to [[Hroar Kalacious]] in [[Kunzar's Edge (POI)|Kunzar's Edge]]. {{waypoint -1374, 36, 515}}

- +2000 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- At least 23g 10s 05c
