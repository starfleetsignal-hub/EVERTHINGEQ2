## Steps

1. Speak with [[Enchanter Glowwp]]. {{waypoint 463.03, -97.98, 1034.92}}
1. Enter [[Sebilis]] and find the following items (all are [[Track Harvestables|trackable]]):
   - *Scale of Venril* from [[Jail and Torture Chamber]]: {{waypoint 36.35, -131.75, 214.66}}
     - Drop into the water near the zone entrance  and swim to the underwater spout at {{waypoint -44.02, -52.50, 140.92}}. Once inside [[Ruins of Old Sebilis]], drop down into the rectangular pool and keep swimming until the waypoint wisp activates. The item looks like a regular shiny.
   - *Extirpating Powder* from [[Kotiz's Hidden Labs]]: {{waypoint -303.37, -25.75, 370.99}}
     - Go to the [[Laboratory and Study]] {{waypoint -259.59, -22.75, 337.38}} and click a small candle-like object on a table, at {{waypoint -307.55, -21.00, 324.58}}. This will open the hidden door.
   - 2 *karyotic algaes* from the water beneath the [[Mezzanine]]: {{waypoint 65.79, -96.00, 139.85}}
     - Just drop into the water near the zone entrance.
1. Return to [[Enchanter Glowwp]].
