## Steps

1. Travel to the [[The Thundering Steppes]] and kill [[Clint Gilcrush]] who is already standing on the left platform of the [[Dead River Docks]] at {{waypoint -442, -18, -449}}.
1. Return to [[Gol M'Tun]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- AA Experience
  - +3000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
  - -4000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
