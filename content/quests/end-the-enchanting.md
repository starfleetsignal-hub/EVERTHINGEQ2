## Steps

This area resonates strongly with enchantment magic. Perhaps I can begin attuning the shard by releasing some of the power found here.

1. Find a number of the enchantment medallions in the Frozen Menagerie.
   - *Note: 5 in total, automatic update from mobs in the zone (updated for me downstairs).*
1. Combining the medallions with the foci around the room, I may be able to disrupt them
   - *Note: all of them are in the room where Reverie is. Look like small columns with shining ball above. The last update is that ball in the center of the room*
   1. I should disrupt the focus in the north end of the Aerie.
   1. I should disrupt the focus in the west end of the Aerie.
   1. I should disrupt the focus in the south end of the Aerie.
   1. I should disrupt the focus in the east end of the Aerie.
   1. I should disrupt the focus in the center of the Aerie.
