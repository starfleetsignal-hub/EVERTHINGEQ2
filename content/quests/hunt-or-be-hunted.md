## Steps

Zone into [[Zavith'loa: The Hunt (Event Heroic)]]

1. Click the carcass at (-3,943, -87, 90).
1. Kill the ring event to spawn the Land Before Time dinosaurs.  Weep as you kill them.  Kill [[Sharptooth]] and [[Maw]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 16g 20,000 status
- 10 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
