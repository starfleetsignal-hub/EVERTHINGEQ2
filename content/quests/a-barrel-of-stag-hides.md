*This quest is repeatable up to 5 times..*

## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[A northern stag|northern stags]] for common updates.
1. Kill 5 [[A northern great stag|northern great stags]] for common updates.
1. Examine the barrel again to fill it up.
1. Hail [[Becka Snowsworn]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 22s 70c
