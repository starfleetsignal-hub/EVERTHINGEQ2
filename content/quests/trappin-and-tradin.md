## Steps

Keden McFurkins, a local Halasian trapper, has offered to share half of his profits along with a nice warm pair of gloves if you bring him back several quality yeti pelts.

1. You need to collect ten yeti pelts from the snow beasts in the [[Icy Keep: Retribution]]. These are one-per-person chest drops from yeti encounters in the zone.
1. Return to Keden.

## Rewards

- At least 4p
- 16293 status
- [[A Warm Pair of Gloves]], used to activate switches in [[Icy Keep: Retribution]].
