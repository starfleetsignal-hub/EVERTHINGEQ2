## Steps

1. Slay [[Vallon lugger|Vallon luggers]] for 8 casks of mead - common update.
   - ![](images/Vallon_Luggers.png)There are three routes you can find these mobs (see image):
     - Western Route (3 luggers). This runs from the tent at  {{waypoint 568, -16, 13}} along the path northwest to the Lumber Mill in Three Toes Valley {{waypoint 535, -19, -268}}
     - Central Route (1 lugger). This runs from the Three Toes Lumber Mill  {{waypoint 535, -19, -268}} to the tent in Blood Ore Valley {{waypoint 537, 11, -163}} (cuts through via Three Toes Stable Master).
     - Eastern Route (3 luggers). This runs from the Storage House at Pinewhisper Lookout {{waypoint 233, -39, 206}}, through Valley of Sacrifice and Spirit Valley to a Depot Tent east of Blood Ore Valley {{waypoint 182, 12, -121}}
1. Return to Mess Sergeant Akseltz
