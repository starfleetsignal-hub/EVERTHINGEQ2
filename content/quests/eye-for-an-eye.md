## Steps

![a mechaneyez assassin](images/A_mechaneyez_assassin.png)
![curious mechanimagica](images/Curious_mechanimagica.png)

1. search [[Coral Forest]] and [[Thrall Roost]] to find 3 remaining [[Mechaneyez assassins]].
   - Trackable. Possible locations for the first 2 assasins include:
   - {{waypoint -317, 12, 528}}, {{waypoint -50, 4, 467}}, {{waypoint -167, 1, 347}}
1. After killing 2 you need to find the last assassin:
   - Examine the mechaneyez assassin on the ground at {{waypoint -319.97, 11.72, 462.16}}
1. Examine
   - mechaneyez assassin on the ground at {{waypoint -319.97, 11.72, 462.16}}
   - the curious mechanimagica (round structure) around the spinning portal
     - *Note: **DO NOT** click the spinning portal to Ver'bohten Atoll*
1. Return to [[Xhol'Rog]].

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following:
  - [[Doomview Gauntlets of Descent]]
  - [[Star Blood Gage of Parables]]
