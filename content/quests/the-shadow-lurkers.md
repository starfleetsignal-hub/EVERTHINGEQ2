## Notes

- *Timed quest: Shattered Seas solo daily, 10 minutes.*

## Steps

1. Kill 15 [[A shadowed Senshali]]s before the timer runs out
   - Shadow Senshalis are 103 ^^ Heroics. They hide all over Grim Shales and must be triggered by walking near them. They cannot be tracked, even after they have spawned.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 67g 40s 5950 status
- 3 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- [[Out of the Shadows, Into the Light]] Achievement upon first completion
