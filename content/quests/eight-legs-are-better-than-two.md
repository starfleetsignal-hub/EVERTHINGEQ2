## Notes

- This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.
- This quest is also part of the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].
- This is one of the few quests in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]] that doesn't require Neriak citizenship. <—- This seems incorrect to me.
- As they take place in the same area and at the same level, it is recommended that you also get the quest [[Finding Millie]] and do them together.
- If you speak to Zintra again after the quest is complete she will ask if you want her to show you the results of the quest. If you say yes, she will apply a drachnid illusion on you. The effect has no duration and can be reapplied whenever you want!

## Steps

1. Find "the building of dusty pages and moldy leathers" in Neriak and get scrolls on transformations.
   - Speak with [[Va'Idax K'Tyriep]] on the 3rd floor of [[Library of K'Lorn]]  {{waypoint -683, 17, 216}} and get two scrolls, [[Amalgamation for Fun and Profit]] and [[Transmogrification of the Flesh]].
1. Return the scrolls to Zintra
1. Kill the following in [[Nektulos Forest]]:
   - 6 [[Dusk leaper]] spiders (23-24)
     - From the main road between [[J'Rais Bridge]] and [[N'Mar's Ascent]], take the path east that leads to the owlbears. The spiders will be all along the road next to rocks, nearish {{waypoint -615, 30, 40}}.
   - 4 [[A shade spinner|shade spinner]] spiders (22-23)
     - These can be found along the main road, mixed in with the [[Ash viper]]s, [[Ash shriller]]s, and [[A haze stalker|haze stalkers]].  Also, NE of the Commonlands Bridge griffon tower, at {{waypoint -297, -1, 61}}.
   - 6 [[A bloated soul leech|bloated soul leeches]] (24-25)
     - The bloated soul leeches can be found the length of the eastern river, from [[Bone Lake]] all the way south past [[The Funeral Pyre]].
1. Return to [[Zintra De'Oziana]]
