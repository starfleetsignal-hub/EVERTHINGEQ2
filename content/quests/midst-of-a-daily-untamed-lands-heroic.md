The dark side of the moon is in the midst of dangerous times, no more so than today. And so I've been tasked with bringing down the enemies of Fordel within Shadeweaver's Thicket: Untamed Lands.

## Steps

- See [[Shadeweaver's Thicket: Untamed Lands (Solo)]] for zone walkthrough:
1. Take down the alpha: [[Goretilla]] on [[Alphaweaver Hill]] {{waypoint -79.60, 65.98, -414.93}}.
1. Pummel the protector: [[Sotek]] at [[Whisperling Falls (Untamed Lands)]] {{waypoint -247.02, 39.03, -130.97}}.
1. Bring the heat: [[Nok Hakka]] in the [[Gor Taku Burn Out]] {{waypoint 50.10, 51.44, -155.29}}.
1. Shatter the spirit: [[Spiritist Karina]] on the [[Plateau of Broken Spirits]] {{waypoint 175.86, 57.69, -554.19}}.
1. Dispatch the phantom: [[The Desolator]] on [[The Desolate Grounds (Untamed Lands)]] {{waypoint 376.99, 17.74, -550.60}}.
