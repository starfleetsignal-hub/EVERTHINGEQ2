## Steps

1. Speak to [[Aaliya]] in the Bastion of the Tranquil {{waypoint 1993, -298, 3357}}.
1. Scribe the [[Notes on Refining]] to receive the recipe *block of refined quicksilver*. This is made at a **forge**, there's one at {{waypoint 1760, -298, 3476}}.
1. Craft 4 blocks of quicksilver. You will need...
   - 12 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
   - 8 [[Sumac lumber]]
   - 4 [[Ethereal coal]]
   - Crafting stations are in the Paineel Commons at {{waypoint 1800.00, -299.00, 3492.50}} on both sides
1. Return to [[Aaliya]] for your reward.
1. Then go to [[Adeela]] {{waypoint 1727, -283, 3473}} for next part of Quest line.

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- +1000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +1500 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**
- +1000 faction with **Craftkeepers**
