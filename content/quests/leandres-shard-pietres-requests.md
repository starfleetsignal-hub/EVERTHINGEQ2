## Steps

1. Find 3 items in any order from different zones:
   - Find a [[Firestone (Leandre's Shard)|firestone]] cooled in an elemental pool.
     - *In [[Karnor's Castle]] near brittle watchers {{waypoint -45, -56, 108}}.*
     - *It can be done by  soloing. The basic method is: Charm, send pet to hit another mob -> Root mob before pet die -> then Charm the mob to hit another mob. You can use this method to get accross the first bridge. Don't try to walk directly through the guard post to the second bridge. Instead, turn your right and go up to 2nd floor. Remember to use this method to clear your way to the back of the 2nd floor's outside way . You can jump from there to the second bridge after you clear the guards that stand near the bridge. Then just before you get to the rhino pen, order your pet stay and then go there to relog for safe charm-break (remember to save a good distance away from pet). Then get invisible and walk to the room with the pool in. Wait outside the room and mind the roamers. Recommend to charm the skeleton in right corner directly right of the entry, that one did not had a line of sight. (sometimes those are 2 though). This way you can sneak in while invis and wait for a good moment when the roamers in the room are sufficiently out of the way to get the stone from the pool. Good Luck !!*
     - At level 85 there were only a few green skeletons between the gate and the objective.  Everything else was gray. Bring a mercenary if you like, walk in, kill a few skeletons near the pool of scrying, search the pool, and done.
   - Find a fear-imbued coolstone.
     - *Labs in [[Sebilis]] roughly at {{waypoint -300, -22, 345}}, the fear-imbued coolstone ([[Coldstone]]) is a body drop from the [[A Sathirian researcher|Sathirian researchers]] there. When entering labs you might be lucky finding 2 linked mobs away from the others when passing the doorway right of the steps.*
   - Find a Hingestone
     - *It's a clicky update in [[Chardok]]. You have to clear all the way down to the palace and have green/blue/red portal access (or someone with). If you are lucky you can buy the different gate crystals from the broker too. Once you get down to the palace you will notice it looks just like Kor-Sha zone in. The hingestone is up to the left staircase in the room there with 3 ^^^ Sarnak's. Its sitting on the bookcase thing at {{waypoint 899, -21, 264}}. (*The following statement may be outdated and needs clarification: *"Probably not soloable or only when extremely lucky, especially if you need the gate crystals from the gatekeepers.")*
1. Return to [[Pietre V'Noquett]] in Neriak.
