## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- This quest is one of several that you can do to earn the trust of the Splitpaw Gnolls and advance [[A Darkened Shard]].
- If you die in the middle of the related instance, you will respawn in [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den|Splitpaw Den]] and can immediately reattempt the instance with all the monsters respawned.

## Steps

1. Zone into [[The Drowned Caverns: Outer Grotto]]: {{waypoint 45, -78, -226}}
1. Kill Underpaw Gnolls until you receive four uncommon updates for furs.
1. Zone back into [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den]].
1. Talk to [[Fral Fipfoot]] again. He will give you [[A gnoll disguise]].
1. Zone back into [[The Drowned Caverns: Outer Grotto]].
1. Kill ten Underpaw Gnolls for common updates for fur.
1. Zone into [[The Drowned Caverns: Flammable Fur]]: {{waypoint -17.55, 31, 11.88}}
1. Find three pieces of Underpaw information.
   1. First piece: {{waypoint -137.5, 0, -129.5}}
   1. Second piece: {{waypoint -89, -0.5, -81.5}}
   1. Third piece: {{waypoint -41, -1, -132}}
1. Kill [[Garanim Brewgullet]] and his four adds at {{waypoint -48, 5, -147.5}}.
1. Return to the Lower Tunnels and speak to  [[Fral Fipfoot]] .

## Rewards

- Experience
- [[A gnoll disguise]] (This is only a reward if you do not use it in the quest)
- Does **NOT** count toward earning Splitpaw Gnoll trust.
