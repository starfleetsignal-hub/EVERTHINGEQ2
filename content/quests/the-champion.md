This quest is part 18 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

This is the third of several quests that take place in the [[Sanctorium of the Trials]]. This instanced zone is accessed via the doorway beside Szin. {{waypoint -350, 3, -1260}}

1. Bow to the Sanctorium of the Trials torch (examine it) to activate the door.
1. Click the door to enter the *Hold of the Fallen* version of [[Sanctorium of the Trials]].
1. Slay [[Champion Ze'fien]].
   - Partway through the fight, 3 [[A virulent creeper|virulent creepers]] will spawn and attack. (If positioned in the middle, you can avoid aggroing the spiders)
1. Exit the instance and talk to Szin.
