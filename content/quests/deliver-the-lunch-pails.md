## Steps

1. Deliver a [[Lunch Pail (Version 1)|lunch pail]] to the worker on the north end of [[The City of Mist]]: {{waypoint -255, 148, -993}}
1. Deliver a [[Lunch Pail (Version 2)|lunch pail]] to the worker on the west end of The City of Mist: {{waypoint -133, 146, -908}}
1. Deliver a [[Lunch Pail (Version 3)|lunch pail]] to the worker on the south end of The City of Mist: {{waypoint -243, 135, -881}}
1. Deliver a [[Lunch Pail (Version 4)|lunch pail]] to the worker on the east end of The City of Mist: {{waypoint -340, 147, -998}}
   - This worker is dead, so examine the body for the update.
1. Return to Ireka Nazan.
