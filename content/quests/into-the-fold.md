## Steps

1. Speak with [[Velric Torch]] underneath the aqueduct in eastern [[Antonica]]. {{waypoint -1495.51, -12.40, -367.93}}
1. Speak with [[Vilma Abatro]] outside [[The Keep of the Ardent Needle (POI)|The Keep of the Ardent Needle]]. {{waypoint -575.06, -13.79, -304.77}}
1. Return to Velric Torch.
1. Collect 5 ![salty tears](images/Image:Salty_Tear.jpg) (harvestable flowers) along the banks of the river near to Velric.
1. Return to Velric Torch.
1. Speak with [[Henry Maxwell Denalis]] in the middle of [[Windstalker Village]]. {{waypoint -1862.60, -38.48, -535.00}}
1. Speak with [[Perinius Abatro]] in [[Keep of the Gnollslayers]] in Antonica. {{waypoint -2112.97, -39.88, 439.73}}
1. Return to Velric Torch.
1. Read [[Perinius' Log]] that Velric gives you (book in your inventory).
1. Speak with Velric Torch.
1. Speak with Perinius Abatro, now beside a tree in Windstalker Village. This will trigger a scripted NPC event. {{waypoint -1930.00, -32.47, -526.55}}
   - You have only a split second after completing the dialogue with Perinius before the scripted event triggers and he becomes un-hailable. If you have multiple group members requiring the update, it is recommended that you coordinate clicks through the dialogue to ensure you all get the update simultaneously. Otherwise, Perinius will respawn at the tree after several minutes if people requiring the quest update remain nearby.
1. Return to Velric Torch.
