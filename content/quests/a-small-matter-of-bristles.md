## Steps

1. Go to the [[The Tower of the Drafling]] in [[Rivervale]].
1. Kill 6 [[A bixie protector|bixie protectors]].
1. Kill 4 [[A bixie sentry|bixie sentries]].
1. Kill 2 [[A bixie venomguard|bixie venomguards]].
1. Kill 1 [[A royal bixie sentinel|royal bixie sentinel]].
