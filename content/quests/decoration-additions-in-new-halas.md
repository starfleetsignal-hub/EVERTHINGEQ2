## Steps

1. Decorate [[New Halas]]. The locations have been marked by Gigglegibber goblins with green sparkles:
   - Travel to {{waypoint -42, 151, -85}}, near Hammer's Rest.
   - Travel to {{waypoint 77, 151, -119}}, in front of Erronson's Furs and Leathers.
   - Travel to {{waypoint -50, 137, 268}}, next to White Lodge.
1. One more spot in [[New Halas]], marked by a green sparkle:
   - Travel to {{waypoint -101, 148, -13}}, a small mound with some tree stumps and a torch.
   - Defeat [[Mr. McScroogle]] (scales).
1. Return to [[Gutten Gigglegibber (New Halas)|Gutten Gigglegibber]].

## Rewards

- [[Frosty Frostfell Tree Ticket]]
  - To use the ticket double click the tree to the left with the blue star on top.
- If you talk to Gutten Gigglegibber again he will tell you that you can decorate the tree you just received by talking to [[Mr. McScroogle]] (see [[A Frostfell Favor]]). If you already did that quest, he will know that and tell you so.
