## Steps

1. Gather the [[Waterlogged crate]] in the water by the Mariner's Bell {{waypoint 669, -43, 300}}
1. Gather the [[Waterproof barrel]] in the water nearby {{waypoint 667, -43, 310}}
1. Return to Johfrit Gythell
1. Talk to [[Amazu Kharliko]] near the bank {{waypoint 660, -24, 372}}
1. Talk to [[Laudine Shardalow]] inside the bank {{waypoint 660, -24, 346}}
1. Return to Johfrit Gythell

## Rewards

- At least 1s 16c
- [[Antonican Signet]]
