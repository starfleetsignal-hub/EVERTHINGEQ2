## Steps

1. Speak with [[Atlyta]] {{waypoint 459, -153, 904}}
1. Click 3 [[Hymnwood roots]] around {{waypoint 317, -154, 923}}. The bottom parts of the floating trees are clickable.
1. Return to [[A pealing ignus|the pealing ignus]] {{waypoint 368, -153, 1034}}
1. Speak with [[A pealing ignus|the pealing ignus]] in [[Esoterra Gardens]] {{waypoint -684, 40, 647}}. You get teleported to [[Plane of Magic: Esoterra Gardens]]
1. Defeat the beast (an entranced stoneripper) at {{waypoint -622, 41, 715}} and then speak with the beast's body.
1. Speak with [[A pealing ignus|the pealing ignus]], and don't port back yet, answer "Let me deal with this".
1. Listen to the dialogue between the 2 ignus and the beast - W*ait for the beast's body and [[A pealing ignus|the pealing ignus]] complete their conversation, after you have hailed them.*
1. Speak with [[A pealing ignus|the pealing ignus]] one last time and port back.![an entranced stoneripper](images/Entranced_stoneripper.jpg)

- At least 1p 53g 9s
