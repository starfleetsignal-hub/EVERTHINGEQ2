## Steps

1. Travel to {{waypoint 692, 51, 673}} and click on the portal to enter [[Sanctus Seru (City)|Sanctus Seru [City]]].
   - if you don't wanna run all the way use [[A tamed Shik'Nar drone]] at {{waypoint -582, 33, 362}} and ride the Shik'Nar to Seru's Ascent
1. Take portal at {{waypoint -201, 87, 284}} to the upper part of [[Sanctus Seru (City)|Sanctus Seru [City]]] and use the door at {{waypoint -181, 180, 95}} to enter [[Sanctus Seru: Fabrication Hall]].
1. Speak to [[Olfud Gearsnoogle]] at {{waypoint 76.43, -511.81, 20.24}}.
1. Gather 10 Seru Honeycombs from trees. ![Seru Bee Hive](images/Seru_Bee_Hive.jpg)
   - *Note: Seru Bee Hives are NOT trackable.  However, the trees on which they are situated will be surrounded by a small number of green sparkles, thereby making it a lot easier, as the hives are not always visible unless you are zoomed right in. They can also be found on the lower level and on THE TRUNKS of the trees, not up in the leaves.*
   - Possible Locations include: {{waypoint -224.91, 93.15, -169.45}}, {{waypoint -254.87, 88.51, -262.22}}, {{waypoint -67.53, 88.51, -356.63}}, {{waypoint -268.03, 93.15, -85.89}}, {{waypoint -263.21, 94.61, 118.81}}, {{waypoint -320.25, 88.70, 160.16}}, {{waypoint -253.44, 88.51, 262.43}}, {{waypoint -168.84, 83.06, 238.17}}, {{waypoint -23.48, 88.56, 281.77}}
1. Return to [[Olfud Gearsnoogle]] in [[Sanctus Seru: Fabrication Hall]].
1. Collect Twilight Oak Leaves at  {{waypoint 148.12, -510.72, 4.23}} on the floor inside the Hall. ![Twilight Oak Leaves](images/Twilight_Oak_Leaves.jpg)
1. Return to [[Sanctus Seru (City)]], take the portal at {{waypoint -241, 180, 79}}, and then travel near {{waypoint -93.43, 81.88, 307.74}}.
1. Defeat the [[An ensorcelled twilight oak]] that attacks. (update might not occur if grouped with player when killing tree) ![an ensorcelled twilight oak](images/An_ensorcelled_twilight_oak.jpg)
   - WARNING!! Epic x4 **ATTACKABLE** [[A Seru knight|a Seru knights]] are sadistically placed in area. Be Aware of the environment where you engage this tree.
1. Speak to [[Hulda Flintbraid]] at {{waypoint -72.44, 88.88, 257.57}}. She is crouching behind a tree.![Hulda Flintbraid](images/Hulda_Flintbraid.jpg)
1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -332, 88, -169}}, then speak to [[Lamplighter Adrun]] at {{waypoint -127.62, 175.69, -132.54}}.
1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -241, 180, 79}}, and then collect 10 dawnshroud adamantite around Forum Meridium between locations {{waypoint -276.29, 89.80, 36.88}} and {{waypoint -335.83, 89.62, 61.03}} mostly found by the mender npc.![Dawnshroud Adamantite](images/Dawnshroud_Adamantite.png)
1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -332, 88, -169}}, and return to [[Lamplighter Adrun]] at {{waypoint -131.96, 175.69, -128.12}}.
1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -241, 180, 79}}, and return to [[Hulda Flintbraid]] at {{waypoint -72.44, 88.88, 257.57}} (back at the tree).
