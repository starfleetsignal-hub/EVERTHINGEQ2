## Steps

*Clan Underpaw gnolls have been attacking gnolls from other clans deeper in the Darkpaw Warrens, forcing them to congregate closer to the surface. While this infighting is seen as a good thing, Ranger Alphin is concerned that this will force the displaced gnolls out of the warrens. This is why Ranger Alphin has asked me to locate and eliminate some of the Underpaw gnolls, giving the other clans more of a fighting chance.*

1. Kill 15 Underpaw gnolls in Terramite Crystalline Grotto and Underpaw Stronghold
   - Terramite Crystalline Grotto: {{waypoint 72, 2, 210}} (past Splitpaw Burrow and up the rope ladder)
   - Underpaw Stronghold: {{waypoint 79, -25, 76}} (first right after entering Splitpaw Burrow)
   - Any larger Underpaw gnoll in either location will work (except for the Underpaw gnolls fighting Splitpaw gnolls)
1. Speak with Ranger Alphin at the Splitpaw Burrow terraporter {{waypoint -51, 2, 63}} or near the entrance {{waypoint -28, -43, -253}}

## Rewards

- At least 3p 36g 79s 77c
