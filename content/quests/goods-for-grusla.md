## Steps

1. Destroy and hunt the following![](images/Blood_Salt_visible.png)
   - Destroy 11 piles of harvested blood salt in the [[Coral Forest]].In areas around {{waypoint -294, 39, 564}}, {{waypoint -338, 47, 430}}, {{waypoint -104, 24, 522}}
   - Hunt 12 bloodshot wardens in [[Coral Forest]] and [[Thrall Roost]] {{waypoint -382, 46, 445}} more wardens above blue mushroom caps
1. Return to Grusla

- [[Blood Salt]]
- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following
  - [[Gerion Mantle of Frenzy]]
  - [[Void Obelisk Pauldrons of Incursions]]
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
