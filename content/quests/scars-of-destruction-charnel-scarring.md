## Notes

- [[Honoring the Youngest Victims]] needs the same zone

## Steps

1. Enter [[Western Wastes: Charnel Scar (Solo)|Western Wastes: Charnel Scar [Solo]]] zone in at {{waypoint 217, 65, 402}}
   - *Note: see [[Western Wastes: Charnel Scar (Solo)|Western Wastes: Charnel Scar [Solo]]] for the walkthrough*
   - *TLDR: use stun rune and progress through and click portal behind final named*
1. Search for the entrance to the Dragon Necropolis at {{waypoint -542.98, -4.02, 447.03}}
1. Continue searching the length of [[Chanel Scar]] of the entrance to the Dragon Necropolis up to {{waypoint -544.37, -3.84, 447.13}} *at 4. Named [[The Bonepicker]]*
1. Follow the tinkered relay posts leading into the cave at {{waypoint -654.69, 56.62, 370.83}}
1. Examine the barrier in front of the entrance to the Dragon Necropolis at {{waypoint -714.31, 67.25, 289.58}}
1. Return to [[Elna Tsernin (Western Wastes)|Elna Tsernin]]
