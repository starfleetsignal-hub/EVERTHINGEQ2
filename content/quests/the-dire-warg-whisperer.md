## Steps

1. Capture a [[Dire warg]] using the [[Dire Warg Muzzle]]. {{waypoint 378, 60, -206}}, {{waypoint 335.13, 28.32, -288.54}}
1. Return to [[Wolfmaster Heinrich]].

## Rewards

- At least 18g 87s 32c
- Ability to buy a Warg mount. Right-click [[Wolfmaster Heinrich]] and select 'Buy From Merchant'.

Choice Of:

- [[Tattered Cloak of the Immortal Castle]]
- [[Tattered Cloak of the Ghoul Summoner]]
- [[Tattered Cloak of the Plainstalker]]
- [[Tattered Cloak of the Lupine Warrior]]
