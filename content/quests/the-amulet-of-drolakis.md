## Steps

1. Kill one of the following despots of Kunark:
   - [[Venril Sathir|Emperor Venril Sathir]] in [[Venril Sathir's Lair]]; or
   - [[Selrach Di'Zok|Overking Selrach Di'Zok]] in [[The Temple of Kor-Sha]].
1. Return to [[Fiddy Bobick]] in [[The Moppet Shoppe]].
