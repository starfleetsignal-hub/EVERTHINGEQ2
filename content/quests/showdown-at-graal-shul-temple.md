## Notes

- This quest will not unlock until you have completed the quest [[The Test (Kylong Plains)|The Test]] from [[Hroar Kalacious]].

## Steps

1. Go to [[Gra'al Shul Temple]] {{waypoint -1747, 56, -196}} and perform the following tasks:
   - Use the canister of [[Fillzer's Formula: Coerced Fishman "Antidote"]] near the coerced mudfins. {{waypoint -1968, 93, -177}}
   - Kill [[Velga Thenn]]. {{waypoint -2030, 93, -200}}
1. Return to [[Hilbraega Stoutmun]] in [[Kunzar's Edge (POI)|Kunzar's Edge]]. {{waypoint -1376, 10, 543}}

## Rewards

- At least 14g 81s 38c
- +1500 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- One of the following:
  - [[Traveller's Mark]] (if Mage)
  - [[Esser Hawk Feathers]] (if Fighter)
  - [[A Lost Badge of Honor]] (if Priest)
  - [[A Lost Badge of Loyalty (Level 72)|A Lost Badge of Loyalty]] (if Scouts)
