## Description

I have discovered a direct delivery Far Seas Requisition slip. The hunter charged with filling the order must have been slain. If I hunt down the creatures and return the resources I shall earn the payment for labor. This particular order calls for six pairs of muck toad legs and one jar of silt sucker jelly. I wonder if this order is for jelly blasted toad legs? Should I leave the Peat Bog before completing this requisition, I may return via any number of mariner bells within the City of Qeynos.

## Steps

1. Hunt down creatures in The Peat Bog to fill the requisition:
   - Collect 6 pairs of [[A muck toad|muck toad]] legs. {{waypoint 653, -33, 504}}
   - Kill a [[A silt sucker (Peat Bog)|silt sucker]]. {{waypoint 653, -33, 504}}
     - The [[A silt sucker (Queen's Colony)|ones]] in [[Queen's Colony]] will also work for some reason.
1. Speak to [[Tanaira]]. {{waypoint 759, -26, 365}}
