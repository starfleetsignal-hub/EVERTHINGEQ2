## Steps

1. Talk to [[Drinal's Steward]] at the druid ring on Daarspire {{waypoint -129, 214, 349}}
1. Capture the spirits of the pickclaw goblins in [[High Keep]] on Karak Peak {{waypoint -317, 166, -581}}:
   - Capture 6 escaped goblin spirits in [[High Keep]] (advanced solo or heroic).
     - *from [[A Pickclaw varmit|Pickclaw varmits]], [[A Pickclaw rascal|Pickclaw rascals]], [[A Pickclaw rouser|Pickclaw rousers]], [[A Pickclaw grafter|Pickclaw grafters]], [[A Pickclaw gorger|Pickclaw gorgers]], [[A Pickclaw bookslingers|Pickclaw bookslingers]], etc.*
   - Capture the spirit of [[Raknag]] {{waypoint -60, -76, 87}} in [[High Keep]] on the McCabe Archive level.
   - Capture 6 escaped goblin spirits in [[High Keep: The Pickclaw Depths]].
   - Capture the spirit of [[Stompus]] in [[High Keep: The Pickclaw Depths]]
1. Return to Drinal's Steward.
1. Speak with [[Zal'Urid]] in Cardin Ward in [[Obol Plains]]. {{waypoint -316, 15, 10}}
