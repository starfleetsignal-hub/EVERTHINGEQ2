## Steps

1. Collect rocks from Blisterrock Ridge.
   - Search for odd-shaped rocks on the dirt ground around the Blistrrock camp. These are black rock piles, usually found near millipedes at  {{waypoint -597, -368, -433}}.
1. Go back to Felnon for update.
1. Collect stomach contents from sea creatures.
   - Any sea creature will update the quest
   - *Note:One way is to fly to [[Icy Fingers]] and jump into the ocean to kill a sea creature there*
   - *Note:Another way is to fly to [[Thurgadin Harbor]] and jump into the ocean to kill a sea creature there*
   - First Octopus kill in Thurgadin harbor updated, neither sea urchins or crabs updated for me.  Barracuda in Thurg harbor also work.
1. Speak with Felnon.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
