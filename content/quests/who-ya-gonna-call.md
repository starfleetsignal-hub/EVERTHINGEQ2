## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- The first turn-in yields -2500 reputation with the Sootfoot Talent Society, but subsequent turn-ins yield only coin.

## Steps

1. Kill 8 ghosts in the Temple of Solusek Ro. [[A soulbound Sol Ro remnant|Soulbound Sol Ro remnants]] and [[A soulbound Sol Ro seeker|seekers]] all update.
1. Return to Kipukas.

## Rewards

- At least 18g 29s 27c
- -2500 faction with **Sootfoot Talent Society**
- Repeatable, but only yields coin
