## Steps

1. Go to [[Skyfire Mountains]] and kill [[Enraged Spirit of Talendor]] {{waypoint -1504, 250, 1520}}
1. Hail the [[Spirit of Talendor]] that spawns for reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 90g 70s 61c
- One of the following:
  - [[Bound Essence Earring]]
  - [[Girdle of Bound Souls]]
  - [[Nortlav's Ring of the Scale]]
  - [[Trapped Souls Earhoop]]
  - [[Scalekeeper's Headguard]]
  - [[Soulbound Headguard]]

## Notes

The Enraged Spirit of Talendor is on an approximate respawn of 15-20 minutes.
