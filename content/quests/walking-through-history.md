## Steps

1. Speak to Hilgan {{waypoint 83, -333, -533}}when you are ready
1. Escort him to the Ring of War field {{waypoint 162, -338, -477}} till he wants to speak with you
1. Escort him to eastern front {{waypoint 63, -346, -456}} till he wants to speak with you
1. Escort Hilgan to the western front {{waypoint 311, -369, -595}} till he wants to speak with you
1. Escort Hilgan to the Cryomancers {{waypoint 310, -326, -384}}.
