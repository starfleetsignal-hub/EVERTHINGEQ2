## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 50 [[Celestial Candle]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Ghida Borrell]] at {{waypoint 660, 412, 59}}.
1. Take the teleporter to the [[South Wing Catalog]], and then the teleporter to the [[Crafters' Gallery]] at {{waypoint 332, 16, 174}}. Speak with [[Mourndax V'Zeaar (CG-NPC)|Mourndax V'Zeaar]] at {{waypoint 935, 412, 34}}.
1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 259, -20, -284}} from the [[North Wing Stacks]] on the first (ground) floor to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]]. Enter [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]] through the portal at {{waypoint 728, 412, -336}}.
1. Harvest the following within [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]]:
   - 18 [[Basilisk meat]] from [[Basilisk lair]]
   - 18 [[Nimbic hide]] from [[Basilisk lair]]
   - 18 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Coalesced reef]]
   - 18 [[Dewpalm lumber]] from [[Fallen dewpalm branch]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Thermal Dissipation Potion]] and craft [[Thermal Dissipation Potion]] at any [[Chemistry Table]].
   - *A [[Chemistry Table]] can be found at the front of [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]] at {{waypoint 14, 7, 103}}.*
1. Speak to [[Mourndax V'Zeaar (DF-NPC)|Mourndax V'Zeaar]] in [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]] at {{waypoint 16, 7, 100}}.

## Rewards

- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
- [[Archivist's Tradeskill Tack]] *Crate containing a choice of primary components for tradeskill related mount equipment*
