## Steps

Zafirah has asked that you venture into the caves and once again look for anything strange.

1. Look for anything strange within the vermin caves.
   - Head to {{waypoint 1315, -168, 3198}} and right click on the glowing stones with scribblings on them.
   - *This will complete the quest and offer the next quest [[Scribblings on the Wall]]*
