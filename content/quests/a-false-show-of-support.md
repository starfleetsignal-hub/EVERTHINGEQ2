## Steps

1. Kill the following creatures in [[Icegill Lagoon]]:
   - 3 [[An icegill angler|icegill angler]]s
   - 3 [[An icegill oracle|icegill oracle]]s
   - 3 [[An icegill shifter|icegill shifter]]s
   - 3 [[An icegill lurker|icegill lurker]]s
   - a named icegill goblin: either [[Master Mistmaw]] {{waypoint 66.02, -35.15, -1519.10}}, [[Master Sleetskin]] {{waypoint 179, -36, -1580}}, [[Master Windstrike]] {{waypoint 81, -35, -1417}}, or [[Master Stormgill]] {{waypoint 353.48, -36.38, -1529.32}}
     - **Warning:** DO NOT kill [[Master Webclaw]]! He does not update this quest and is needed for the next one!
     - To spawn one of these named goblins, you must find an iceflow that has five static encounters of goblins on it. Four such iceflows exist.
     - After clearing all 5 encounters on the same iceflow, a new encounter will spawn that has a chance of containing one of these named mobs. You must clear all the groups on the iceflow before the groups will spawn the instant repop and that instant repop may contain the named or a placeholder. If the placeholder pops you must repeat killing all the encounters on the iceflow.
1. Return to Rainscour.
