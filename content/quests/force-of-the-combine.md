## Steps

1. Speak with [[Captain Zum'guur]] at {{waypoint 4538, -824, 473}}
1. Head to the [[Drakelands]] and fire the [[Battalion Flare]] Zum'guur gives you (use it from inventory) at or near {{waypoint 5218, -919, 1461}}
1. Slay 8 drakes or wyverns in the zone (any update)
1. Return to Field Marshal Vishra

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
