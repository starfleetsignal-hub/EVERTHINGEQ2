This quest is repeatable.

## Starting the Quest

Speak to one of the following NPCs and pay them 5s:

- [[Gigglegibber Scalper]] on the front steps of the [[The Ironforge Estate]] in [[North Qeynos]] {{waypoint 474, -20, -341}}
- [[Gigglegibber Scalper]] inside [[The Freeport Reserve]] Bank in [[West Freeport]] {{waypoint 226, -2, -130}}

## Notes

In the dining room, on the table near the entrance, is a skull.  When clicked, you'll receive [[A skeleton mask]].

Killing all the rats and spiders is optional.

Tip for the dancing step: it's easier to watch the chat where the dance moves are announced. The task is not about speed but more about accuracy, you may find remembering 2 or 4 moves in a row and pressing buttons for that sequence easier over the length of the dance (50 moves in random order). If you mess up do not try to catch up on correct order, just wait  a moment for next sequence.

## Steps

1. You must find and release the following spirits.
   - I will need to find and release the butler's spirit. There may be a note or some other clue nearby to point me in the right direction.
     1. Examine the skeleton on the floor in the entry room by the Butler's ghost
     1. Examine the note on the desk
        1. Sweep the Entryway floor.
        1. Dust the Entryway furniture.
        1. Realign the Entryway paintings.
        - Click on 5 small dust piles
        - Click on the 3 vases, 3 bookshelves, 2 statues, desk, chair, chest, and canoe
        - Click on each of the 4 paintings, placing them on the wall
   - I need to complete the lord's to do list to release his spirit from the house.
     1. Break the 2 boards blocking the archway
     1. Examine the skeleton by the Lord's ghost
     1. Examine the note on the table
        1. Learn to dance with Helin. She hired instructors, they will all be in the cellar.
        1. Spend time with the kids.
        1. Pay the bartender.
     1. Examine the plate of spoiled food at {{waypoint -3, -5, -11}} in the basement, which will activate a waypoint wisp to follow
     1. Head upstairs to the 2nd floor and down along the ledge/walkway to the little hidden door in the wall at the end of the ledge
     1. Open the door and kill [[A large rat]], which may reward you with a [[Rat Plushie]]
     1. Examine the remains in the small room to find a key
     1. Return to the basement, and open the door behind the tapestry
     1. Free the children's ghosts by standing near them
   - I will need to find and release the lady's spirit. I should attempt to speak with her in the cellar.
     1. Go to the basement, and hail [[Lady Halcovian]] {{waypoint -6, -5, -9}}, after which you will receive 4 dance moves, "Back", "Down", "Spin", and "Sway"
     1. Place the dance moves in an easy-to-reach location on your hotbar, and read the directions carefully
     1. Continue speaking with Lady Halcovian to begin
     1. Watch the skeletons closely, and use the dance moves in the appropriate order.
   - I need to find the lord's coin to pay the bartender. It should be in the house somewhere.
     1. Pick up the coin on the table in the room with the children
     1. Click the cup on a table near the entrance to the dining room
1. The house is still haunted for some reason, I must stop this at once.
   1. I must find out what is truly haunting this house and release it from its curse. I should take a look around the attic.
      1. Go upstairs and use the candle on the bannister to burn the spider web
      1. You can kill [[A large black spider]] on your way to the attic, which may reward you with a [[Black Spider Plushie]]
      1. Go to the attic and examine the book at the base of the "T"-shaped cross near the window
      1. Ward the Undead by clicking on the cross. Now, kill all the zombies while clicking on the cross as often as you can! (Warning! waves...)
         - *Note: Instead of clicking the cross you can highlight the cross with your cursor and click F (use button)* repeatedly.
         - *Note: Clicking on the cross isn't necessary. Just slaughter each wave of zombies.*
   1. I will need to speak to the Gigglegibber Goblin again and let him know that I survived the Haunted House
