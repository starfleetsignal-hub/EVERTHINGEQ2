## Description

*If I'm going to stop the goblin assault, I'll need to defeat those that lead them. Find the leaders of the Pickclaw in both High Keep and Pickclaw Depths, and defeat them.*

## Steps

- Zone into [[High Keep (Advanced Solo)]]:
  - Defeat [[General Graknus (Advanced Solo)|General Graknus]]
- Zone into [[High Keep: The Pickclaw Depths (Advanced Solo)]]
  - Defeat [[Bolgin Serilis (Advanced Solo)|Bolgin Serilis]]

At least 5p

- 10 [[Draconic Etyma]]
