## Prerequisites

- Your faction with [[The City of Kelethin (Faction)|City of Kelethin]] must be Indifferent or better to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. [[Count Jooliin]] gives you **[[A codex addendum]]**
1. Find Royal Messenger [[Kalmath Gusinga]] who travels between [[The Fae Court]] and [[The Village of Panderpana]] and give him the *codex*.
   - [[Kalmath Gusinga]] will offer the optional quest [[A Messenger's Task]].
1. Return to [[Count Jooliin]]

## Rewards

- At least 9g 27s 1c
- +100 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
