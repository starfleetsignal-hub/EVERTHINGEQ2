  - [[Magus's Ensorcelled Satin Leggings]] (All Mages)
  - [[Outrider's Blue Iron Chain Leggings]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Stormcaller's Winter Weave Leather Pants]] (Channelers, Druids)
  - [[Witchdoctor's Ill Omen Chausses]] (Shamans)
