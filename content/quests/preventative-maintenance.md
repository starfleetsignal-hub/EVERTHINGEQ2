This quest is part 3 of a 6-quest series in the [[Isle of Refuge Timeline]] for Freeport-Aligned characters.

## Description

"As our first step in driving the Tunarians on the island, we are going to try a subtle approach. We will weaken their resources so when the attack does come, they will be helpless against us. I need to seek out Assassin Vamir at a camp to the west in Seaside Glade. He should know what to do to start our subtle invasion." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Assassin Vamir]] {{waypoint 192, -3, 142}} at the far end of [[Seaside Glade]].
1. Kill 4 [[A wilderbear|wilderbears]], [[A wilderbear cub|wilderbear cubs]] or [[A giant wilderbear|giant wilderbears]] and return to Vamir.
1. Kill three [[A cliffdiver hawk|cliffdiver hawks]] using the [[Hawk Bait]] given to you by Vamir:
   - Look for a cliffdiver hawk in the cliffs above [[Cliffdiver Canyon]], {{waypoint 234.55, -5.05, 142.21}}.![a Cliffdiver Hawks flying over the cliff](images/CliffdiverHawksOverTheCliff.jpg)
   - Stand underneath one near the cliff edge and examine the Hawk Bait in your inventory.
   - Wait for the hawk to fly down, and while it is eating the bait, attack it before it flies away.
1. Return to to Tayil N'Velex for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 15c
- [[A Note from Tayil]]

- One of the following, depending on your class:
  - [[Freeportian Cloth Hood]]
  - [[Freeportian Leather Cap]]
  - [[Freeportian Plated Coif]]
  - [[Freeportian Studded Helm]]
