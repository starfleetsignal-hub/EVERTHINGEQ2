## Steps

1. Visit four guard towers and click their reports. The reports are clipboards on the door-frame just inside the towers on the bottom floor.
   - [[Qeynos Tower 1]]: {{waypoint 164, -2, -790}}
   - [[Qeynos Tower 2]]: {{waypoint 388, 14, -517}}
   - [[Qeynos Tower 3]]: {{waypoint -229, 0, 35}}
   - [[Qeynos Tower 4]]: {{waypoint -194, -1, 489}}
1. Return the reports to Turgen Bremhurst.
1. Click sofa at {{waypoint 213, 1, -279}} to get the coin-bait.
1. Lay the bait around the hill at {{waypoint -181, 14, 119}} to make [[A highwayman (Thundering Steppes)|a highwayman]] spawn. Kill him (he scales to the player's level).
1. Return to [[Turgen Bremhurst]].

## Rewards

- Access through the [[Bridge Keep]] gates at night.
- At least 3s
- [[A crate of coin]]
