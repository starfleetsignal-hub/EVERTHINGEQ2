## Steps

![smelting pot](images/Smelting_pot.png)

1. Taint 3 arcane smelting pots on the bottom level of [[The Deathfist Quarry]]:
   - Taint the Smelting Pot of Ikuris in the northwestern tunnel: {{waypoint 396, -119, -159}}
   - Taint the Smelting Pot of Gurkis in the southern tunnel: {{waypoint 361, -125, -43}}
   - Taint the Smelting Pot of Horkul in the eastern tunnel: {{waypoint 245, -122, -120}}
1. Acquire 4 mining axes from [[A Vallon miner|Vallon miners]].
1. Slay named gnome [[Nixam Dugdiggle]] on the third level of the quarry, in the northern tunnel. {{waypoint 345.93, -22.20, -188.04}}
1. Return to the [[Glademaster]].
