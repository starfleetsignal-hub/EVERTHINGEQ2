## Steps

Kill the following enemies

- Assail the amphibian : Kill [[The Blue Baron]] in [[Chelsith]] (Jarsath Wastes)
- Impale the Impaler : Kill [[Impaler Tzilug]] in [[Charasis: Maiden's Chamber]] (Jarsath Wastes)
- Butcher the praetor : Kill [[Praetor of the Phylactery]] in [[Charasis: Vault of Eternal Sleep]] (Jarsath Wastes)

Or

- Defeat the empress : Kill [[Drusella Sathir]] in [[Charasis: Maiden's Chamber]] (Jarsath Wastes)
- Slice up the giant tentacle : Kill [[Majora Leviathora]] in [[Chelsith]] (Jarsath Wastes)
- Eliminate the spirit lord : Kill [[Lord Vizaroth]] in [[The Crypt of Agony]] (Kunzar Jungle/Sebilis)

Or

- Kill the nightmare keeper : Kill [[The Keeper of Nightmares]] in [[Charasis: Vault of Eternal Sleep]] (Jarsath Wastes)
- Fell the fallen emperor : Kill [[Fallen Emperor Vekin]] in [[Zone|The Crypt of Agony]] (Kunzar Jungle/Sebilis)
- Butcher the Betrayer of Chardok : Kill [[Korucust]] in [[Court of Korucust]] (Kylong Plains/Chardok)
