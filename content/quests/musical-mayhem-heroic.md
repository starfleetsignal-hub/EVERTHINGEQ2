## Description

Something resonates within the Ossuary, a sound so vastly evil and full of contempt that only death could be foreseen of the surrounding lands should whatever conducts the music emerge. I must stop it before that happens.

## Steps

Zone into [[Ossuary: Resonance of Malice (Heroic)]]

- Kill the keeper
- End the elder
- Defeat the ushers
- Kill the priestess
- Hush the choir, for good

## Rewards

- At least 2p 16g 20,000 status
- 15 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- One of the following:
  - Crate of Armor - Chain/Cloth/Leather/Plate based on class
