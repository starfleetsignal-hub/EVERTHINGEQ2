## Prerequisites

- You must have at least -4000 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Collect 8 *terrorspine steaks* from [[A terrorspine shark|terrorspine sharks]] located in the water off the southern coast of the zone. {{waypoint -190, -71, 1460}}
1. Deposit the items in the barrel. {{waypoint 1019.15, -40.34, -24.78}}
1. Speak with [[Pylemea Stoerki]].

## Rewards

- At least 15g
- +2000 faction with **The Order of Rime**
