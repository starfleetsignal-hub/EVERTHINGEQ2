## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
- This quest becomes an unlimited repeatable after initial completion.

## Steps

1. kill and gather the following
   - Cull 12 eyeshroud shrooms in [[Eerie Ridges]] - if you don't fly, entrance to that area is at {{waypoint -292, 87, -166}}![an eyeshroud shroom](images/An_eyeshroud_shroom.png)
     - *They can be dormant and come alive as you get close.*
   - Gather 18 pinhead eyeshroud spawn shrooms in [[Eerie Ridges]].![Mushroom cap - click to spawn pinhead spawn shrooms](images/A_Growing_Threat_-_Mushroom_cap.png)
     - *Click on a mushroom cap to spawn 1-3 pinhead spawn shrooms*
1. Return to [[Xhindar]]

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- [[Icon of Xktkez]] - house item
