## Steps

1. Ask about Teir'dal village at the docks: Speak to [[Heathryn Icepike]] and accept the quest [[Coggin Body Shots]]
1. Speak with [[Bartyn Cogginshots]] and complete the quest [[Coggin Body Shots]]. You receive [[Ghorkaal Teir'Dal Illusion Belt]]
1. Continue to do quests
   - Inquire about the Teir'Dal village and help the Far Seas Traders - complete the quest [[Touch of the Undead]]
   - Investigate the Teir'Dal village - complete the quests [[Gangrenous Treatment]], [[Plagued With Questions]], [[Down to the Bone]], [[Body of Work]]
1. Return to your emissary (Murrar Shar or [[Opal Darkbriar (Phantom Sea)|Opal Darkbriar]])

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- [[Electrified Adornment of Prismatic Resilience]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Electrified Adornment of Arcane Resilience]]
  - [[Electrified Adornment of Elemental Resilience]]
  - [[Electrified Adornment of Noxious Resilience]]
