## Description

"I have found a sandy bone shard in the claws of a crab here on the Island of Refuge. After looking closely at it I could see some designs etched on the surface. If I could find the other pieces and fix them I might have something to take from the island when I leave." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill crabs on the beach - [[A small coastal crab]], [[A coastal crab]], [[A large coastal crab]] until you acquire 5 scrimshaw tokens.
1. Kill [[A triggerfish (Queen's Colony)|triggerfish]], [[A shoal glider (Queen's Colony)|shoal gliders]], [[A sawtooth shark (Queen's Colony)|sawtooth sharks]], [[A razortooth shark|razortooth sharks]], [[A needlefang (Queen's Colony)|needlefangs]] or any other sea creature off the beach until you find 6 pieces of glue.
1. Kill [[A tainted Sapswill invader|tainted Sapswill invaders]], [[A tainted Sapswill tapper|tainted Sapswill tappers]], [[A tainted Sapswill hunter (Queen's Colony)|tainted Sapswill hunters]], [[A tainted Sapswill warrior (Queen's Colony)|tainted Sapswill warriors]], [[A tainted Sapswill ambusher|tainted Sapswill ambushers]], [[A tainted Sapswill scout|tainted Sapswill scouts]] or [[A tainted Sapswill fighter|tainted Sapswill fighters]] in the [[The Tainted Forest]] until you find a replacement cord on which to string the sandy bone shard.

## Rewards

- [[Scrimshaw Token]]
