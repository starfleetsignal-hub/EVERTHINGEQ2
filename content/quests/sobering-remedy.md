## Prerequisites

- You must know the [[Erudian]] language to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Gather the following components from [[The Peat Bog]]
   - Pieces from [[A bog slug]]. {{waypoint 679, -35, 419}}
   - Whiskers of [[A large scavenger]]. {{waypoint 674, -33, 474}}
   - Bile from [[A bog sludge]]. {{waypoint 661, -35, 449}}
1. Return to [[Vondorinsarnoo]].

## Rewards

- [[Pox ward ring]]
- At least 1s 5c
