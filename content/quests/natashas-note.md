## Notes

- Graverobbers only spawn during night (20:00 - 05:00) and immediately despawn at 05:00.
- Graverobber spawn points must be empty of other mobs (rats, skeletons, zombies); kill the placeholders just before 20:00 game time so the next spawn will be a graverobber:
  - {{waypoint -85, -11, 28}}
  - {{waypoint -75, -15, 19}}
  - {{waypoint -61, -11, -32}}
  - {{waypoint 47, -3, -30}}
- Possible mobs are [[A novice graverobber]], [[An amateur graverobber]] and [[A graverobber]]; they can spawn as singles, duos or trios; and are all trackable.

## Steps

1. Examine [[A dirty graverobber note]].
1. Speak with [[Tarakh]] in [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]]. {{waypoint -258, -55, 11}}  for Anashti Sul server update is in Temple Street (93, -4, 99)

## Rewards

- At least 4s 82c
