## Notes

Requires being level 130 crafter and completion of [[Scars of Destruction Crafting Timeline|Scars of Destruction crafting timeline]]. You will receive in-game mail titled "Help for the Downtrodden" from [[Ameela Ziad]], quest will be offered upon opening that mail. If for some reason mail gets deleted you can speak to [[Ameela Ziad]] in [[Sodden Archipelago]] directly to get quest.

Upon accepting quest a [[Recipe Scroll: Goblin Assistance and Other Silliness]] is auto scribed into your recipe book, which contains:

- [[Glarb's Lockpick]]
- [[Glarb's Mining Pick]]
- [[Gooey Glue]]
- [[Machete Bowl]]
- [[More Realistic Paper Mache Boulder]]
- [[Basic Paper Mache Boulder]]
- [[WHEE]]

Do not craft anything until requested.

**The zone has no regular harvests, bring with you:**

- 2 x [[Ilmenite Ore|Ilmenite ore]]
- 1 x [[Aerated mineral water]]
- 55 x [[Zimaran Coal]]

Other items used in crafting you will be asked to harvest in the zone. You can harvest with your WHEE harvester form (or not if you can handle the regular lvl 130 mobs in the zone).

## Steps

1. *I must travel to Spiral of Vul and find a goblin named Glarb*
   1. go to Sodden Archipelago and use door at {{waypoint 218, 287, -804}}
      - NOTE: Low adventurers will only see ***"Enter the Blackhook Spiral"*** but will be zoned into the [[Spiral of Vul (Contested)|Spiral of Vul [Contested]]]
   1. Speak to [[Glarb]] at {{waypoint 237, 238, 292}} (use main path  with some mobs along the way or run along cliff to the right to get to him) You are tasked with helping him find and rescue his brother.
1. Collect a void-infused rock from the area - these are larger, slightly sparkling blue rocks along the cliffs, around {{waypoint 181, 238, 345}}
1. I must craft a [[WHEE]]
   - *you can use Goblin Workbench near [[Glarb]] to craft [[WHEE]] using 1 void-infused rock you just picked up, 2 x Ilmenite Ore, 1 aerated mineral water and 5 x Zimaran Coal.*
1. Speak with [[Glarb]]
1. he wants you to craft him a pick
   1. use your [[WHEE]] to collect 12 x [[Blackhook Scrap Metal]] (nodes along the paths deeper into the zone).
   1. craft [[Glarb's Mining Pick]] using 2 x [[Blackhook Scrap Metal]] and 5 x [[Zimaran Coal]].
1. Speak with [[Glarb]]
1. craft 5 x [[Glarb's Lockpick|Glarb's Lockpicks]] (each one uses 2 [[Blackhook Scrap Metal]] and 5 x [[Zimaran Coal]])
1. Speak with [[Glarb]]
1. he wants you to make him a "paper machete" boulder
   - gather 8 x [[Scraggly Roots|Scraggly Root]] - along paths
   - collect 10 x [[Broad Leaf]] - deeper into the zone  starting past first gate at {{waypoint 439, 239, 258}} and {{waypoint 459.70, 243.39, 312.67}}, look around edges of green area among other shrubbery, Broad Leaf are labelled, they are trees.  I found a few around {{waypoint 483, 229, 64}}.
     - {{waypoint 462, 241, 277}},
     - {{waypoint 483, 245, 204}},
     - {{waypoint 490, 245, 188}},
     - {{waypoint 498, 245, 133}},
   - scrounge  [[Ground Charcoal|Ground Charcoa]]<nowiki/>l - from the pile next to mender by zone in {{waypoint 70, 239, 453}}
   - Upon harvesting last thing for this recipe you are also awarded  2 x [[Mystery Water]] ( DO NOT LOSE IT, THERE IS NO WAY TO GET MORE)
1. *Craft 2 x [[Gooey Glue]]* - each one uses 1 Mystery Water, 4 Scraggly Roots and 5 Zimaran Coal
1. *Craft [[Basic Paper Mache Boulder]]* - uses 1 x Gooey Glue, 10 x Broad Leaf and 5 x Zimaran Coal
1. Speak to [[Glarb]]
1. Craft [[More Realistic Paper Mache Boulder]] -* uses Basic Paper Mache Boulder,  1 Ground Charcoal, 1 Gooey Glue and 5 x Zimaran Coal*
1. Chat with [[Glarb]]
1. Speak with [[Ameela Ziad]] in [[Sodden Archipelago]]

## Rewards

- At least 11p 16g 55s 16c
- [[Crag Irongrip]]
- unlocks tradeskill researcher [[Blargh Critterkeeper]] in [[Spiral of Vul (Contested)]].
- unlocks daily tradeskill missions from [[Glarb]] (18 hours cycle)

[[Jovial Patchcraft Chomper]] has a 16.3% rare harvest chance which is slightly higher than [[Crag Irongrip]] 15.7% rare harvest chance.
