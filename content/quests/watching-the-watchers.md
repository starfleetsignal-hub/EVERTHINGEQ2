## Steps

1. Enter [[D'Morte Burial Chambers: Veiled Hollow]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] at {{waypoint 678, 2, -618}}.
1. Find the first sarcophagus {{waypoint -64, -6, 79}} and defeat [[Master Slayer Varash]] to obtain Varash's Journal.
1. Find the second sarcophagus {{waypoint -51, -6, 98}} and defeat [[Archkeeper Feras D'Lorn]] to obtain the strange looking censer.
1. Find the third sarcophagus {{waypoint -5, -4, 77}} and defeat [[Nightcaptain Revelaz]].
1. Defeat [[Ruenok G'Koz, the Rook Lord]] {{waypoint -52, -11, 44}} as fast as you can to prevent the continuous spawn of additional vampires.
1. Loot and read [[An important missive]] from Ruenok G'koz, the Rook Lord. (He will drop 6 total)
1. Exit the instance at ( -2, 01, 1 )
1. Speak to [[K'shar Haelbreng]] in Nektulos Forest on the east side of Timber Falls. {{waypoint 499, 26, -1454}}

## Rewards

- At least 5g 69s 57c
- Vampire is added to your Master Strike/Smite/Rage &#42;
- One of the following spells or skills:
  - [[Vicious Embrace]] (fighters)
  - [[Theft of Vitality]] (priests)
  - [[Soulsieve]] (mages)
  - [[Thirsting Veil]] (scouts).
