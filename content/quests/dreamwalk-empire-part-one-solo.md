Alternates with [[Dreamwalk Empire, Part Two (Solo)]].

**Note**: There is essential information missing for components of this Quest narrative. Despite having the quest up, the [[Forlorn Gist: Nightmares of Old (Solo)]] was *not* available at the entrance in Forlorn Grist, but that quest does not say what/if there is a precursor, unlike [[Karuupa Jungle: Heart of Conflict (Solo)]] which notes that it requires at-least starting [[Rumble in the Jungle]].

## Steps

1. End the nightmare: [[Aga-Yagaba]] in [[Forlorn Gist: Nightmares of Old (Solo)]].
1. Slay the shaman: [[Ugweepai]] in [[Karuupa Jungle: Heart of Conflict (Solo)]].
1. Banish the summoner. [[Esmerelda Everghast]] in [[Mahngavi Wastes: Phantasmal Shades (Solo)]]
1. Bury the horticulturists: [[Roseblood]] and [[Thornblood]] in [[Castle Vacrul: Rosy Reverie (Solo)]].
1. Slaughter the seneschal: [[Bloodmaiden Syvanti]] in [[Castle Vacrul: Suite of Screams (Solo)]].
