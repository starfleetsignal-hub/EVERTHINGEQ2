## Steps

1. Defeat the [[A Yha-lei attacker]] at {{waypoint -1185, -531, 3562}} to free the pups
   - The Yha-lei attacker is non-attackable until you approach, when he turns into a 92^.  The groups of Yah-lei near him do not need to be cleared, however may make getting to him a little easier.
1. Return to [[Piep]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +300 faction with **Othmir of Velious**
