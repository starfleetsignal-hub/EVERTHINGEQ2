## Steps

1. Head to [[Flamespout Fields]] {{waypoint -936, -470, 3718}}
1. Catalogue [[A Duhjalm deforester|a Duhjalm deforester]]
1. Return to [[Livi]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +150 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
- [[Gift of Nipik's Haven]] (House item to port to NH)

For those who do not know how to catalogue, hit k on your keyboard and look for the catalogue creatures in abilities. If you don't have this on your hotbar, you can drag it down.Then go and click on the deforester and click on catalogue creatures.
