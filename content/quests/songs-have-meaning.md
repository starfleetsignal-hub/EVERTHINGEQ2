## Steps

1. Speak with [[Rillo]] {{waypoint 294, -15, 463}}, the Culture Keeper
1. Listen to each of his songs. The quest updates when "The Lost Sister" has been listened to.
1. Speak with [[Delafar Nelmaise]]
1. Find an old coldain terrain map -- a clickable rolled scroll on a table in the Banker's area of Icewell Keep at {{waypoint 259.04, -292.11, 256.88}} within [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain]].
1. Return to [[Delafar Nelmaise]]

## Rewards

- At least 25g 70s 61c
- [[Deepwater Circle Leggings]]
