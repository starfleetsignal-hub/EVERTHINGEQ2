## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[Aqua goblin runt|aqua goblin runts]].
1. Return to Daarwyn.

## Rewards

- At least 12s 2c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Blackened Irontoe Manacle]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Engraved Irontoe Manacle]] (All Mages)
  - [[Hammered Irontoe Manacle]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Polished Irontoe Manacle]] (All Priests)
