## Steps

1. Touch the first path marker {{waypoint 1025, 18, 1259}}
1. Touch the second path marker {{waypoint 885, 25, 1217}}
1. Touch the third path marker {{waypoint 786, 33, 1198}}
1. Touch the fourth path marker {{waypoint 767, 66, 1265}}
1. Slay 11 undead Teir'Dal
1. Slay [[V'hallis En]] then loot [[An assassin's toolbox]] from the chest he drops
1. Return to Joreye
