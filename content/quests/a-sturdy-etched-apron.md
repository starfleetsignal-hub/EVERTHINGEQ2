## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Etched Apron for my apprentice.
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Etched Apron]]
   1. Craft [[A Sturdy Etched Apron]] on a [[Loom]] using the following materials and fuel:
      - 2 [[Etched leather pelt]]
      - 4 [[Glimmering filament]]
1. I need to take this apron back to my apprentice.
