## Steps

You are asked to kill 10-15 (random):

- [[A Strifewing poacher|Strifewing poachers]] in [[The Barren Sky]];
- [[A Strifewing stalker|Strifewing stalkers]] in [[The Barren Sky]];
- [[A Strifewing prowler|Strifewing prowlers]] in [[The Barren Sky]]; or
- [[A Strifewing ravager|Strifewing ravagers]] in [[The Barren Sky]]

## Rewards

- 9722 status at level 71
- +500 faction with **The Seafury Buccaneers**
