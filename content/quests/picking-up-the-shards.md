## Description

It's possible that the shards of the Eternal Prism are present within the phylactery created by Miragul. Removing the will likely prove quite helpful to purifying the Prism's pure essence. I should search within the place known as The Crucible to find the pieces I am looking for.

## Steps

1. Search within [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Crucible]] for 10 pieces of the Eternal Prism. The fragments look like small black bricks on the ground (sparkle effect if you have graphics turned up) spread throughout the zone. (NOTE: Do NOT leave your group before collecting all the shards. Disbanding will disallow you to harvest the shards resulting in a gated quest.)
1. Fragment Locations:
   - {{waypoint 36, 0, 4}} SW corner of corridor to a Reflection of Exile room (bodies trapped in ice)
   - {{waypoint 54, 2, 5}} North wall of a Reflection of Exile room
   - {{waypoint -114, 0, 37}} East wall of a Reflection of Silence room (library), right side of bookshelf
   - {{waypoint -86, 0, 9}} NW wall of a Reflection of Silence room (library), to the left of the door as you enter the room
   - {{waypoint 5, 15, 111}} NW wall of Halls of Ascension (2nd floor circular connecting chamber) to the left of a lamppost
   - {{waypoint -8, 15, 156}} South wall of Halls of Ascension to the right of a lamppost
   - {{waypoint -56, 15, 133}} NE wall of Halls of Ascension to the right as you approach the corridor to a Reflection of Arrogance (room with 3 doors inside)
   - {{waypoint -103, 15, 84}} NW corner of a Reflection of Arrogance room
   - {{waypoint -37, 50, 114}} NE wall of the circular room at the top of the spiral stairs (on the bookshelf)
   - {{waypoint -3, 50, 191}} along NW wall of a Reflection of Mastery (final room)
