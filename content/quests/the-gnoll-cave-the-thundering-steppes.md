## Steps

1. Tish asks you to acquire some research materials in The Thundering Stppes:
   - 5 Cursed Bone Marrow from [[A skeletal trooper|skeletal trooper]]s at {{waypoint -546, -11, 441}}.
   - 3 Spirit Essences from [[A spirited orb|spirited orb]]s at  {{waypoint -609, -5, 527}}.
   - 5 Imbued Bones from [[A Varsoon apprentice (Thundering Steppes)|Varsoon apprentices]] at {{waypoint -672, -3, 708}}).
1. Return to Tish Tickers.

## Rewards

- Permanent access to the instance, [[A Gnoll Cave]], located at {{waypoint -206, 14, 182}}.
- Shoulder slot armor based on class
