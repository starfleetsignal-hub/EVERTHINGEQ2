## Steps

![Anaz'Mal ceremonial vase](images/Anazmal_vase.jpg)

1. Collect or kill of the following sets of items or mobs:
   - Collect 20 **Anaz'Mal ceremonial vases** in the northern Tear of Anuk.
   - The vases seem to spawn at random locations around the Tear of Anuk. You can find them at the water's edge or on the ledge that surrounds the pond.
   - Kill 20 [[A Sandscrawler magus|Sandscrawler magi]]
   - Collect 20 **Sul'Dal relics** in the caves beneath the Eye of Anuk
   - Kill 20 [[A dervish cutthroat (Sinking Sands)|dervish cutthroats]] near the Undercity Arena

1. Return to [[Vhret Retharo]] in South Qeynos.

## Rewards

- 8902 status
- +500 faction with **The Concordium**
