## Steps

1. Gather and hunt the following![Skur'rax Webbing Pile](images/Skur'rax_Webbing_Pile.png)
   - Gather 10 glowing stone from Skur'rax webbing piles. You will be unable to progress collecting webbing piles for the [[Webbin' Feverishly]] quest until you collected these 10 webbing piles. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 211, 29, 435}}, {{waypoint 262, 28, 426}}, {{waypoint 352, 46, 455}}
     - {{waypoint 395, 61, 498}}, {{waypoint 449, 81, 398}}, {{waypoint 429, 80, 384}}
     - {{waypoint 399, 79, 438}}, {{waypoint 311, 96, 312}}
   - Gather glowing stone from hanging Skur'rax webbing clumps. Possible locations include:![Skur'rax Webbing Clump](images/Skur'rax_Webbing_Clump.png)
     - {{waypoint 182, 30, 488}}, {{waypoint 262, 28, 426}}, {{waypoint 328, 50, -483}}, {{waypoint 469, 74, 459}}
     - {{waypoint 449, 81, 398}}, {{waypoint 429, 80, 384}}, {{waypoint 72, 46, -500}}
   - Slay 12 Skur'rax droneguards.
1. Return to [[Muurak]].

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- [[Refined Oblivionite Dagger]] - item required to continue [[Rage of Cthurath: Distant Wastes and Dark Places]] quest.
- One of the following:
  - [[Parasitic Helm of Parables]]
  - [[Coral Barbute of Delusion]]
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
