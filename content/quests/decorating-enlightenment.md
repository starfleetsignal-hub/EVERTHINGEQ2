- Scribe [[Frostfell Lair Decoration]], received when the quest is accepted, if you haven't already learned the recipes.

## Steps

1. Craft 6 [[Frostfell Candle|Frostfell Candles]]:
   - 12 [[Frostfell Wax]], from one of the boxes near the entrance {{waypoint 1, 0, -569}}
   - 12 [[Elven Magic]], from [[Ginneldarf Gigglegibber]] {{waypoint 36, 0, -573}}
   - [[Chemistry Table]] {{waypoint 33, 0, -574}}
1. Return to Dux Frostfoot.
