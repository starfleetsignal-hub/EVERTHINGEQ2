## Prerequisites

- You must be at least level 40

## Notes

- This quest is also part of the [[Rivervale Timeline]].
- [[The Mysterious Drafling|The former access quest]] for the associated instance, [[The Tower of the Drafling]], is no longer required.
- Monsters in The Tower of the Drafling can see through invisibility spells.
- You may wish to pick up the book quest [[Learning About Nightbloods the Hard Way]], as this quest also requires you to kill many nightbloods.

## Steps

1. Kill 100 nightbloods. Many are found around [[Grubbin's Fishing Retreat]] at {{waypoint -684, 1, -308}} and the surrounding area.
1. Return to [[Rukir Pineleaf|Rukir]].
1. Get a pint of jumjum from a tap in [[The Tower of the Drafling]] at {{waypoint 381, 22, 116}}.
1. Bring the jumjum back to Rukir.
1. Rukir sends you back into [[The Tower of the Drafling]] to talk to the Drafling himself. He is not there, but you get a quest update as you enter [[The Queen's View]] at {{waypoint 478, 34, -28}}.
1. Return to Rukir to inform him that the Drafling wasn't in his tower.
1. Kill [[Asajj An'duuth]]'s and her entourage outside Rukir's tree stump.  {{waypoint -805, -7, -257}}
   - Asajj is a 44^^^-46^^^ inquisitor that heals her encounter. Her entourage includes two [[A wicked assassin|wicked assassin]]s (44-46H assassins), and two [[A corrupted attendant|corrupted attendant]]s (44-46H defilers) that also heal.
1. Report back to Rukir, who will send you to deliver some vegetables to [[Franny]] in the back room of the second floor of [[The Fools Gold]] at {{waypoint -81, -14, -25}}. (Turn around when you go through the door at the top of the stairs)
   - You have six minutes to complete this step.
   - As of an unknown patch this step became a group update.
1. After the poisoned vegetables cause [[Franny]] to fall ill, go question Rukir.
1. Rukir will turn [[Rukir Pineleaf (Named)|hostile]] and attack you. Kill him to complete the quest.
