## Steps

1. Visit Peeking Point at {{waypoint -250, -25, -490}}; this is the southern end of the [[Bridge of Zek]].
1. Follow [[The Emperor's runner]]. He will drop marching orders at four tents. Pick up each note after he visits each tent.
   - [[Cutlass marching orders|Cutlass Division marching orders]] {{waypoint -321, -27, -480}}
   - [[Voulge marching orders|Voulge Division marching orders]] {{waypoint -329, -28, -388}}
   - [[Battleaxe marching orders|Battleaxe Division marching orders]] {{waypoint -387, -27, -260}}
   - [[Maul marching orders|Maul Division marching orders]] {{waypoint -245, -27, -230}}
1. Return to Lute

- Experience only
