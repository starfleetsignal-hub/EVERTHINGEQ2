## Steps

1. Do the following in any order
   - Kill 12 Allu'thoa
   - Search for Lurgz and Gurgz
     - [[Lurgz]] is in the Allu'thoa village [[Gazanti]], on a wood platform overlooking the pond {{waypoint -1121, 101, 942}}. Kill the Allu'thoa menacing him.
   - Search for [[Toril Ledskipper]]
     - He's dead at {{waypoint -1117, 92, 886}}
   - Search for [[Zaveta Stickyfingers]] {{waypoint -1187, 121, 903}}
     - Zaveta is in a hut on a hill in the Allu'thoa village [[Gazanti]].  Kill the Allu'thoa menacing him, then spoeak to Zaveta.
1. Return to Corrith
