## Steps

1. Kill [[A mistgoblin shaman|mistgoblin shaman]]s for 10 uncommon updates. {{waypoint -68, 104, -569}}
1. Speak with the [[Swifttail Trialmaster]]. {{waypoint -73, -43, 160}}
1. Enter the trial ring and defeat [[Durasik]]. {{waypoint -74, -43, 156}}
1. Speak to the senior Copper shackle monk.
