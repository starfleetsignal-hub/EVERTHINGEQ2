## Notes

The Sokokar Leash recipe auto-scribes. You do *not* need to locate it in inventory and scribe it manually.

## Steps

1. Craft a [[Sokokar leash (Crafted)|sokokar leash]].
   - Requires 100 Artisan, 1 [[Splitiron ore]], 3 [[Abyssal pelt|Abyssal pelts]], 5 [[Thaumic Coal]], and Forge {{waypoint 437, 245, 1494}}.
1. I need to leash a stray sokokar and return it to someone called Zharss {{waypoint 452, 245, 1456}} in the crafting area.
   - Stray sokokars are hopping around the cave and the pathway to the mouth of the cave.
1. Return to Sokszar.

## Rewards

- +5000 faction with **Crusaders of Greenmist**
- At least 1p 53g 9s
