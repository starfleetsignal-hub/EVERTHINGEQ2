## Steps

1. Speak to [[Toro Smokehorn]] at 'an abandoned residence' in [[Maj'Dul]]. {{waypoint -170, 150, 118}}
1. Speak to [[Asadel Yasurbah]] at his residence. {{waypoint 124, 143, 101}}
1. Return to Toro Smokehorn.
1. Speak to [[The Saracen]] who should have spawned inside the apartment.
1. Kill Sandcrawler goblins in [[The Pillars of Flame]] for 30 updates. {{waypoint 535, -108, -830}}
1. Speak to The Saracen outside [[The Arena (Instance)|The Arena]] in Maj'Dul. {{waypoint -30, 178, 38}}
   - This will start a 15-minute timer.
   - You must disband if you are in a group.
1. Speak to [[The Terrorgore (The Arena)|The Terrorgore]] once inside the instance.
1. When the doors to the arena open, enter and assist The Terrorgore in killing [[Assault (The Arena)|Assault]] and [[Battery]].
   - If Terrorgore dies, zone out and zone back in to reset.
1. Return to 'an abandoned residence' at {{waypoint -170, 150, 118}} and speak to Toro Smokehorn.
1. Gather (or puchase) the following herbs from [[Bush Nodes#Tier 6 (level 50-59)|Tier 6 bush nodes]] in either [[The Pillars of Flame]] or [[The Sinking Sands]]:
   - 10 [[Prickly pear|prickly pears]]
   - 10 [[Darjeeling tea leaf]]
   - 10 [[Artichoke|artichokes]]
- Return 'an abandoned residence' in Maj'Dul {{waypoint -170, 150, 118}} and speak to [[Toro Smokehorn]].
