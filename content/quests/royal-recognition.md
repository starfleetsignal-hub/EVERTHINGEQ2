## Steps

1. Purchase 6 [[Rock salt (Quest Item)|salt]] (10c each) from Alchemist [[Khalil'mun]] at [[The Herb Jar]] in [[Qeynos Capitol District]]
1. Sprinkle the salt with water from the pool at the Temple of Life - walk down the stairs to the water and click the sparkly spot at {{waypoint 533, -35, -184}}. It will auto-update 6 times.
1. Place the [[Blessed salt]] around the entrance to Starcrest Commune - click the sparkles
1. Defeat the [[Spirit of Krys'lo|vengeful spirit]]
1. Speak to [[Logan Camerus|Guard Camerus]] near the Starcrest gate {{waypoint 675, -18, 217}}
1. Speak to Rupert Valorbound {{waypoint 654, -16, 184}}
1. Defend yourself from Guard Camerus

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g @ lvl 90
- [[Ring of the Froglok]]
