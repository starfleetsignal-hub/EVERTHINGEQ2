## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key.
   - Kill 15 [[A malignant fulgurite|malignant fulgurites]] around {{waypoint -329.77, 247.54, -672.54}} in [[Takish Badlands]] at [[Petrous Woods]].
1. I need to find the Gepofrygia Music Box key elsewhere.
1. Go to [[Sandstone Delta]].
1. Find the Gepofrygia Music Box key clue from [[A Swordfury follower|Swordfury followers]], [[A Swordfury worshiper|Swordfury worshippers]], or [[A Swordfury devotee|Swordfury devotees]] around {{waypoint 37.45, 40.48, -447.30}} in [[Sandstone Delta]] at [[Kren Rommol]].
1. Examine the clue for [[The Gepofrygia Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the **Gepofrygia Music Box** in [[Takish Badlands]].
