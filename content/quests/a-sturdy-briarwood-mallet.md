## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Briarwood Mallet for my apprentice.
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Briarwood Mallet]]
   1. Craft [[A Sturdy Briarwood Mallet]] on a [[Woodworking Table]] using the following materials and fuel:
      - 2 [[Severed briarwood]]
      - 4 [[Glimmering sandpaper]]
1. I need to take this mallet back to my apprentice.
