## Steps

1. Izzay gives you a [[Voice Modulator]] and asks you to eavesdrop on some conversations in [[West Freeport]] **at night** which begins promptly at 8:00 PM Norrath time (one minute Norrath time is equal to 3 seconds earth time).  You should stand near, but not too near the people having the conversation, and examine the [[Voice Modulator]]. You will see a message indicating when you are in the right location.
   1. Listen in on [[Commandant Dominicus]]'s conversation outside [[The Freeport Militia House]] {{waypoint 114, -16, 135}}.
   1. Listen in on [[Commandant Arkadios]]'s conversation outside the Coalition of Tradesfolke, between the gates to [[East Freeport]] and [[North Freeport]] {{waypoint 53, -20, 54}}.
   1. Listen in on [[Man-at-Arms Faustus]]'s conversation at the gate to [[The Commonlands]] {{waypoint 266, -3.6, -69}}.
   1. Listen in on [[Commandant Placida]]'s conversation at the entrance to [[The Thieves' Way]] {{waypoint 292, -3.5, -9}}.
   1. Listen in on [[Commandant Tor'Val]]'s conversation in the basement dungeon of [[The Freeport Militia House]] {{waypoint 145, -26, 125}}.
1. Return to [[Izzay Meestere]] at the Jade Tiger's Den /waypoint -117, -21, -72
1. He sends you to speak with [[Mehina]] at the [[Seafarer's Roost]] in [[East Freeport]] {{waypoint -236, -55, -26}}.  Ask her about scheduling a tour of famous houses.

## Rewards

- A little experience
