## Steps

1. Visit [[Blood Ore Valley]] {{waypoint 397, 6, -132}}
1. Visit the [[Valley of Sacrifice]] (the area with the [[Grove of Stones]]) {{waypoint 201, 10, 8}}

1. Visit Spirit Valley again
1. Visit [[Spirit Lake]], in the northeast area of [[Spirit Valley]] {{waypoint -57, -3, -73}}
1. Return to The Angry Augur
