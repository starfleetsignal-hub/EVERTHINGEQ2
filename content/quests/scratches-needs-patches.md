## Steps

1. Gather 20 roots (can be [[Tuber strand]], [[Belladonna root]] or [[Tussah root]])
1. Gather 10 tea leaves (can be [[Raw black tea leaf]], [[Oolong tea leaf]]  or [[Green tea leaf]])
1. Kill scarecrows for 5 [[A piece of jute cloth]], looted from their corpses. Mob suggestions:
   - In Thundering Steppes in the area from around {{waypoint 689, 0, -689}} and heading east towards the Antonica Station griffon tower.
   - [[A rotstuffed scarecrow]] at [[Scarecrow Fields]] or [[Northern Farmlands]], [[Antonica]]
   - [[Witherstraw scarecrow|a witherstraw scarecrow]] at [[Central Farmlands]], [[Antonica]]
1. Leave the material for delivery in Jacques' mail chest in [[The Thundering Steppes]] {{waypoint 238, 33, -1024}}. This is near [[Jacques]] at the Antonica Gate.
