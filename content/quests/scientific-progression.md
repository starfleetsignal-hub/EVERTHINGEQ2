## Steps

1. Collect two core samples from the calcified malefic extrusions within [[The Outer Vault]].
   - These are updates obtained by using [[Mineral Core Sampler]] from your bags while targeting the extrusions, located in the water between [[Crag]] and the [[Corrupted Guardian of the Seal]].
1. Return and speak to Almas.

## Rewards

- At least 90g 70s 61c
