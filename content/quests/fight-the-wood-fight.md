## Steps

1. Obtain the following:
   - Obtain 12 arbor vine-ligaments from arborians near {{waypoint 31, 163, -24}}.
   - Destroy **Rathe Blessed Urn** to gather 8 **Living Vegarlson Resin** in the [[Sanctum of Stone]] near {{waypoint 872, 62, 38}}. ![A Rathe Blessed Urn](images/Fight_the_Wood_Fight_quest_-_Rathe_Blessed_Urn.jpg) ![](images/Fight_the_Wood_Fight_quest_-_Living_Vegarlson_Resin.jpg)
     - Uncommon update. Check all levels, inside the pyramid.  You *could* avoid fighting by checking every level for vases not near the mobs.  Check the room again on the way out - respawn *can* be fast.
     - Respawn time for the vases is random (apparently few seconds to several minutes).
1. Speak to Assistant Tussle at {{waypoint -352, 42, -419}}.
