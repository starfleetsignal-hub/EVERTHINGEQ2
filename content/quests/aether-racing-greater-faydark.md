## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- For more racing tips see the [[Aether Racing]] page.

## Steps

1. Step on the the teleport pad to start the race. You must be dismounted.
1. Complete the course in the designated time period.
   - While racing, aim to fly through the blue and green spots floating in the air to increase your speed, but dodge the brown leafy swirls as they will slow you down.
1. Return to the Aether Racing Referee.

## Rewards

- 1 [[City Token]]
- Prefix Title: [[Bixie Barnstormer]] - if you completed the race in under 95 seconds
