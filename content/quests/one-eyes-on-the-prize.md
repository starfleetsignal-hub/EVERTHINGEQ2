## Steps

1. Poison and slay the following  ![](images/One-Eyes_on_the_Prize_quest_-_Barrel_of_Fish.jpg)
   - Poison 6 **barrels of fresh fish** in [[Lokh Barnacoz]] {{waypoint -68, 91, 168}}
   - Slay 12 [[A Stormfury roughneck|Stormfury roughnecks]] in the same area.
1. Return to [[Ulgaf]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- One of the following:
  - [[Resilient Dendromail Boots]]
  - [[Resilient Viridsilk Shoes]]
  - [[Resilient Florahide Boots]]
  - [[Resilient Chloroplate Sabatons]]
