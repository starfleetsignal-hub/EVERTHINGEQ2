## Steps

Create 6 each of any random 2 CA's below:

- [[Arcane Symphony|Arcane Symphony (Journeyman)]]
- [[Death Mark (Spell)|Death Mark (Journeyman)]]
- [[Bria's Entrancing Sonnet|Bria's Entrancing Sonnet (Journeyman)]]
- [[Jugular Slice|Jugular Slice (Journeyman)]]
- [[Dashing Swathe|Dashing Swathe (Journeyman)]]
- [[Makeshift Arrows|Makeshift Arrows (Journeyman)]]
- [[Shenanigans|Shenanigans (Journeyman)]]
- [[Swarthy Deception|Swarthy Deception (Journeyman)]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

## Rewards

- At Least 3s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
