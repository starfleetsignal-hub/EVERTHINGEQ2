## Description

I have been asked to collect the residuum of summoned magic from within the Eternal Prism, which I can find in an area known as The Anathema. The crystal mounted on the staff I was given should enable me to absorb the summoning residuum. Once the crystal is saturated with magical essence, I should return to Camp Wyndalsyd to report my success.

## Steps

1. Locate [[A summoning residuum]] within [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Anathema]] and use the [[A crystal-tipped rod|crystal-tipped rod]] on 5 of them to absorb it.  They are semi-random spawns but seem to reliably appear at the following locations as well as random spawn points throughout the zone:
   - {{waypoint 9, 1, -25}} behind a pillar at zone in [[The Halls of Contemplation]]
   - {{waypoint 38, 2, -71}} [[The Crypt of Disdain]]
   - {{waypoint 169.05, 4.51, -182.74}} [[The Dismal Hollow]] or
   - {{waypoint 206.59, 18.14, -158.32}} [[The Dismal Hollow]]
   - {{waypoint 263.81, 18.14, -92.82}} [[The Sepulcher of Corruption]]
   - {{waypoint -73, -14, 141.5}} [[The Pool of Reflection]]
   - {{waypoint -242, -4, 196}} [[The Fundament of Sorcery]]
1. Return to [[Chief Scholar Lakewind]]

## Notes

As well as getting updates from the locations listed above, you can spawn [[A summoning residuum]] by killing [[A hostile malevolence]], [[A dark malevolence]] or [[An envious malevolence]].  However, this is a random event (approximately one residuum for every two kills) and only one residuum will pop at a time: you need to use the crystal-tipped rod on the residuum before killing the next mob if you want another one to pop.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
