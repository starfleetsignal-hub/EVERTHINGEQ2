This quest is part 10 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Kill [[Foulfeather]], a named owlbear. (usually level 25^, sometimes 24^)
   - She is found with the other owlbears, south of the rumbleroots, east of [[The Commonlands]] entrance. {{waypoint -556.20, 40.85, 132.38}}
1. Return to Torq for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 11s 40c
- Neck slot item based on class:
  - [[Nektulos Defender's Chain]] (Fighters)
  - [[Nektulos Healer's Chain]] (Priests)
  - [[Nektulos Medium's Chain]] (Mages)
  - [[Nektulos Stalker's Chain]] (Scouts)
