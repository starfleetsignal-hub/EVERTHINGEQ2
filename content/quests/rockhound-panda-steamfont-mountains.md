## Steps

1. Travel to [[Steamfont Mountains]]

![](images/Screenshot_2025-10-11_203157.png)

1. Obtain 3 samples from Goldsteam Geyser {{waypoint -1018, 99, 816}} - mine 3 blue crystals
1. Obtain 3 samples from Anchorite Geyser {{waypoint -851, 131, 569}} - mine 3 blue crystals
1. Obtain 3 samples from Old Gargantua {{waypoint -573, 61, 1647}} - mine 3 blue crystals
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]
