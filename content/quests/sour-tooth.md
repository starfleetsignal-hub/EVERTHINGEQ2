## Description

Skrit has asked if I can make some more pickles for trade with the pygmies.

## Ingredients Needed

- 5 kith peppers
- 5 leonid root
- 10 hylocereus fruit
- 25 Effulgent Candle (fuel merchant near forge)

## Steps

1. Craft 5 [[Jar of Pickles]]
1. Return to [[Skrit]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
