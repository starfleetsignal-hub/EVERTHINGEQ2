## Requirements

- You must have completed the quest [[Handbook for the Recently Deceased]], given by [[Bai'Yun]] in the Hua Mein village, which is at the end of the Sundered Frontier timeline.
- **You must be level 90**.

## Steps

1. Complete the three "diplomatic tasks" for Paineel, Kerra Isle, and the Hua Mein.
   - For Paineel, kill any combination of 10 [[A Toxxulia guardian|Toxxulia guardians]], [[A Toxxulia winged sentinel|Toxxulia winged sentinels]] or [[A Toxxulia winged avenger|Toxxulia winged avengers]] around {{waypoint 275, 125, 2748}}
   - Then head to Kerra Isle and speak with [[Keyta Wicker]] at {{waypoint 1374, 49, 2196}}. Complete her quest, [[A Message with Spirit]], to obtain the [[Kerra Shaman's Token]].
   - Finally, go to the Hua Mein village and speak with [[Shi'jan Sensou]] {{waypoint -413, 278, 3212}}. Complete his quest, [[Into the Arena]], to obtain the [[Arena Master's Token]].
1. Return to [[Elder Shahib Zaim]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- 1 [[Mark of Manaar]]
- At least 30g 10s 55c
- +5,000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +5,000 faction with **Fell Blade**
- +5,000 faction with **Bastion of the Tranquil**
- +5,000 faction with **Seekers of the Dark Truth**
- +5,000 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +5,000 faction with **Hua Mein**
