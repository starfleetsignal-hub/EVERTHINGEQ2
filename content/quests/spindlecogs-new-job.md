This quest is a handy addition to the level 20 [[Armor Quest Timeline|armor quests]] since they don't give a helm as a reward.

## Steps

- Kill the following in the [[The Thundering Steppes]]:
  - 10 [[A fledgling antelope|fledgling antelope]]s
  - 5 [[A griffawn|griffawn]]s
  - 10 [[A tide octopus|tide octopi]]

## Rewards

- At least 22s 51c
- One of the following:
  - [[Spindlecog Auto-stitched Cowl]]
  - [[Spindlecog Hammer-studded Headband]]
  - [[Spindlecog Tinkerhelm]]
  - [[Spindlecog Tri-link Coif]]
