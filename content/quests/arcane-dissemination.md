## Steps

1. Kill an Accumulation of Knowledge.
1. Kill An aggresive Projection (three mobs, linked) outside of {{waypoint -111, 3, -50}}.
1. Enter the room at that location. Kill the three linked projection mobs.
1. Click on each of the six weapon racks around the room and kill the mob that spawns.
1. Click on the center weapon rack and kill the two mobs that spawned, which will update the quest 2/4.
1. Go down the hallway to the end by the doors to the next section and kill An aggressive Projection (three mobs, linked). {{waypoint -22, 8, -153}}
1. Return halfway down the hall to the side room {{waypoint -88, 3, -128}}.
1. Kill the three linked projection mobs in the room.
1. Click on each of the six weapon racks around the room and kill the mob that spawns. **Important note**: these weapon racks will NOT become clickable unless you kill the mobs at the end of the hallway by the door first.
1. Click on the center weapon rack and kill the two mobs that spawned, which will update and complete the quest.
