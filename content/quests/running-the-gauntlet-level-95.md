## Steps

Create 12 items from the random list below:

- [[Rhenium Vanguard Spaulders]]
- [[Rhenium Reverent Coif]]
- [[Rhenium Reverent Leggings]]
- [[Rhenium Righteous Cuirass]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 9g 37s 67c
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
