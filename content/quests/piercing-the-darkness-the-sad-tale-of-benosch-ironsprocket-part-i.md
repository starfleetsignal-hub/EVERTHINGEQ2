## Materials Required

At least:

- 15 [[Rockhopper Meat]]
- 15 [[Slow Creeping Root]]
- 15 [[Radiant Algae]]
- 15 [[Coruscating Kindling]]

### Suggested

[[Portable Cooking Set]] or Summon Crafting Stations AA

## Steps

1. Collect 3 undamaged [[Rockhopper egg|rockhopper eggs]].![](images/Rockhopper_egg.jpg)
   - The eggs are along the paths to the east of the spire, near the rockhoppers {{waypoint 205, 280, -210}}.  Most will "break" when you try to harvest them; just keep harvesting. [[Track Harvestables]] works once in range of an egg.
   - *It is absolutely **not** necessary to kill any rockhoppers as eggs self-spawn and are sparkling.*
   - Another suggestion: If you don't want to do a lot of back and forth, stand in one spot since the eggs respawn fairly quickly
   - *Note: The entrance to the passage down from the tower is at {{waypoint 702, 429, -600}}.*
   - If you're a lowbie, don't harvest the egg while there's a Rockhopper near by, It will agro and kill you.
1. Craft [[Three Rockhopper Egg Omelet]] on a [[Stove & Keg]].  *Note: The recipe is auto-scribed.*
   - The nearest [[Stove & Keg]] can be found in [[Recuso Tor]] at {{waypoint -621, 35, 439}}.
1. Return to [[Nuniun Wolfbane (The Blinding)|Nuniun Wolfbane]] at {{waypoint 637, 428, -646}}.
