## Steps

1. Use the [[Void Obelisk]] at {{waypoint -645, -1, 437}} to enter [[Oogothl Sprawl]].
1. Speak to [[Tomian Keeneye (Oogothl Sprawl)|Tomian Keeneye]].
1. Speak to [[Uglah]]
1. Gather 3 containers of goods {{waypoint 127, 65, -497}} at the obelisk and bring them to [[Sturg]] at {{waypoint 222, 80, -191}} in the [[Gardens of Industry]]
1. Finish the quest by talking to [[Sturg]].
   - *Note: Begin the next quest by talking to [[Tomian Keeneye (Oogothl Sprawl)|Tomian Keeneye]], who is now at {{waypoint 232, 80, -214}}*
