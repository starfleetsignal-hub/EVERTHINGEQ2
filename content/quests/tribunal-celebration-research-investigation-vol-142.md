## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The avatar summoning rituals require the manipulation of immense power and are inherently dangerous. To reduce the chance of failure and provide an anchor for that power, the herald of the Tribunal has asked me to collect an item that may have once belonged to that god or one of their most loyal followers

- Collect [[Reliquary of the Tribunal|Reliquaries of the Tribunal]] from a powerful creature in [[Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields (Heroic I)|Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields]] heroic dungeon.

**This does not update in [Solo]. It needs to be Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields [Heroic I]**

- Return to the [[Herald of the Seventh Hammer]] at {{waypoint 274, 180, -357}} in [[Timorous Deep]]
