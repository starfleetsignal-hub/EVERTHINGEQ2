## Note

- this quest is only available to Ogres

## Steps

1. Enter Big Bend at {{waypoint 64, -12, 267}}
1. Slay Crazed Ogres and Crazed Trolls
1. Collect Rallosian Relics. These will be altars or small boxes with a particle effect, located behind the closed doors of small rooms throughout the zone.
1. Return to Kroota Gukbutcher

## Rewards

- At least 6s 82c
