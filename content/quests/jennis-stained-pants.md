## Steps

1. Gather 5 small flower bulbs
   1. Go up the ramp towards the Sanctuary of the Devoted to get water from the flowers growing there. *(These are the same ones you used for cleaning the robes for the Sanctuary quests.)*
1. Return to [[Jenni Everling (Obol Plains)|Jenni Everling]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
