## Starting the Quest

1. Read the ingame mail from "Justinian Theo" - Offical Summons of Justice or (speak to [[Maelin Starpyre]] at {{waypoint 187, -5, -45}} who has no quest feather at this point) and examine the "Offical Summons of Justice" in your inventory
1. examine the [[Coin of Eternal Scales]] and "Answer the summons."
1. you get zoned to the [[Plane of Justice: Eye of the Tribunal]]
1. Speak with [[The Tribunal]]
1. Watch the conversation
1. you'll recieve [[Book of The Plane of Knowledge]] and get zoned into [[Myrist, the Great Library]]
1. speak to [[Maelin Starpyre]] at {{waypoint 187, -5, -45}} to start the quest

## Steps

![A Testimony of Tranquility](images/A_Testimony_of_Tranquility_Pickup.png)

1. Collect [[A Testimony of Tranquility]] at {{waypoint 194, -20, 233}} in the [[South Wing Stacks]] .
1. Read [[A Testimony of Tranquility]] from your inventory.
1. Speak with [[Maelin Starpyre]] at {{waypoint 190, -5, -41}}
1. Meditate upon the threat of nothingness in the [[North Wing Stacks]] {{waypoint 234, -20, -286}}. ![Meditation Location](images/Meditation_Location.jpg)
1. Kill [[Bookworm]] that attacks.
1. Pick up the gnawed book it drops at {{waypoint 234, -20, -286}} ![A gnawed book](images/A_gnawed_book_Pickup.png)
1. Speak with [[Maelin Starpyre]] again at {{waypoint 190, -5, -41}}
1. Gather 6 Dried Bark from the [[Myrist Conservatory]] {{waypoint 56, -20, -92}}![Dried Bark](images/Dried_Bark.jpg)
1. Deposit the dried bark in two piles in the [[North Wing Stacks]] {{waypoint 275, -20, -257}}![Dried Bark location](images/Dried_Bark_location.jpg)
1. Use the lift at {{waypoint 419, -5, -13}} to get to the [[Grand Librarian's Office]] at {{waypoint 414, 15, 245}} and meditate. ![Meditation Location in Office](images/Meditation_Location_in_Office.jpg)
1. Speak with [[Quellious]] at {{waypoint 435, 15, 233}}![Quellious](images/Quellious.jpg)
