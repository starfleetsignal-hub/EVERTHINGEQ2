## Prerequisite

You must be a level 20 or higher [[Fighter]] to receive this quest

## Steps

1. Enter [[Scale Yard: Traitors' End]] {{waypoint -65, -25, 283}}
1. Kill 8 [[A prison escapee]]
   - {{waypoint 24, -4, 9}}, {{waypoint 28, -4, 26}}, {{waypoint 6, -4, 66}} and {{waypoint 18, -4, 82}} on land
   - {{waypoint 10, -7, 85}}, {{waypoint 17, -7, 120}}, {{waypoint 0, -7, 111}} and {{waypoint -7, -7, 77}} in the water off the dock
1. Repair the three barricades at {{waypoint 42, -4, -16}} by clicking the wood piles
1. Return to Corporal Sergius Mordanticus in West Freeport

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
