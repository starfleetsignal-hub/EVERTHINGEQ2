Anmera, a dhalgar tender at the Ironblood Fungus Farm in Thalumbra, pointed out that the bits of crystal within the farmland soil, umbrite, is highly sought after. Apparently, there are many different types of it within the under depths, all with different qualities.

## Steps

1. Gather 12 **low quality umbrite** {{waypoint 921.10, 35.15, -273.11}}.![low quality umbrite](images/Low_quality_umbrite.jpg)
   - *Note: uncommon update, there's a knockback on a failed harvest. (Not Trackable)*
1. Return to [[Anmera]].
1. Speak with [[Lasslyn]] in [[Outer Maldura]] {{waypoint 531.91, 120.34, 335.08}} area of Thalumbia just outside Maldura's front-door.
1. Click on the cart next to her.![umbrite cart](images/Umbrite_cart.jpg)
