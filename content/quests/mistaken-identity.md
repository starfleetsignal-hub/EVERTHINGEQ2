## Steps

1. Defeat [[A Tsol Ew scarhunter|Tsol Ew scarhunters]] to find evidence of their activities (6) {{waypoint -1083, 0, 574}}
   - You need to collect 6 [[A scrap of Tsol Ew writing|scraps of Tsol Ew writing]], an uncommon small chest drop from the Tsol Ew scarhunters roaming the central area of The Eidolon Jungle.
1. Put the etchings together
   - You can do this by right-clicking and examining the stack of [[A scrap of Tsol Ew writing]] in your inventory.
1. It looks like a summoning ritual of some kind. Return to [[Kuule Vilehammer]] {{waypoint -726, 3, 551}} to tell her what you discovered
1. Find the ritual site {{waypoint -1450, -4, 310}}. **It's important to get the site update before killing the mobs.**
1. Kill the [[A Tsol Ew dreadcaller|Tsol Ew dreadcallers]]
1. Kill the summoned creature
   - [[Opulocht]] {{waypoint -1478, -5, 321}}, level 93^ evil eye
1. Return to Kuule
