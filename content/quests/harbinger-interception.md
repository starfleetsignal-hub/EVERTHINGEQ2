## Steps

Even in Death, the power contained in the gold-filigreed urn carries a message and a command. Remove the scorn harbingers from this sacred place - their scorn is unearned, and the future they herald will not come to pass!

1. Destroy [[A scorn harbinger]] from the Sleeper's Tomb.
