This quest is part 3 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Erudite Series.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Vess Al'Ishni]] at the docks.
1. Vess will teach you some magic words, and asks you to repeat them.
1. They are: Kah, Shee-Jah, Ver-ata, Nik-Su, Gej-raz!

## Rewards

- [[Abbatoir's seeing orb]]
- At least 1s
