## Steps

1. I need to free the captured scouts and ensure their safe return to the new camp.
   - Talk to [[Ely Havensaft]] at {{waypoint -113, 7, 756}}
   - Free the captured scouts (3)
     - [[Scout Geraldine]] {{waypoint -64, 6, 749}}
     - [[Scout Copperhair]] {{waypoint -30, 12, 743}}
     - [[Scout Mortimer]] {{waypoint -20, 13, 776}}
1. Scout Mortimer has been injured too badly to return to camp on his own. I need to get him some medical help quickly!
   - Escort the injured scout back to camp
     - At this point, you are given a 4 minute and 20 second timer to return to the camp with Scout Mortimer. You must hail Scout Mortimer, and then he will get up and slowly make his way back to the camp.  Mobs may spawn along the way, and you must defeat these mobs without letting Scout Mortimer die, and get him back to the camp before the timer expires, or you must start over with finding all three scouts again.
1. Now that I've rescued Veris' scouts, I should report back to him and find out what they discovered about the Tourbillion.
   - Speak to [[Veris Windcall]] {{waypoint 17, -5, 500}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
