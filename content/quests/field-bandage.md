## Steps

1. Kill 8 Allu'thoa for scraps of clean cloth (100% auto-update off any Allu'thoa on [[North Dshinn]])
1. Speak to [[Corrith Midner]]
1. Help get Corrith back to camp. Speak to him again and tell him you're ready, to lead him to the [[Hidden Beach]] {{waypoint -1408, 9, 924}}
1. Speak to [[Corrith Midner]] to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g 80s
