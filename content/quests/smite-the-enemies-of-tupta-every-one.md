## Steps

- Kill 6 Brokenskull pirates. You'll find them in Innothule Swamp, SE of Firmroot, general location {{waypoint -172, -82, -98}} or  {{waypoint -262, 24, 320}}.
- Kill 6 Ykeshan frogloks and trolls {{waypoint 612, 102, -460}}
- Kill 6 Thullosians {{waypoint 405, -50, -170}}

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +1000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
- -250 faction with **Clan Grobb**
