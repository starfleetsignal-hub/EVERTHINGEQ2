## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- Upon completing the quest a fifth time, you will receive the title, "Dessert Dasher".

## Steps

1. Step on the *Frostfell Candy Grab Starting Region*. {{waypoint -1092, -58, 21}}
1. Collect as many candies as possible!
1. Speak with Crumpet.

## Rewards

Your reward selection is based off the number of candy pieces you collect during the time allotted:

- A Small Prize (around 1-35):
  - One of the Following:
    - [[Blue and White Frostfell Elf Cap]]
    - [[Snow Form]]
    - 1 [[Frozen Token of E'ci]]
    - [[Candycane Staff]]

- A Big Prize (around 36-49):
  - One of the Following:
    - [[Candy Cane Grab 'n Tag Cape]]
    - [[Blue and White Frostfell Elf Cap]]
    - [[Snow Form]]
    - 2 [[Frozen Token of E'ci]]
    - [[Candycane Staff]]

- A Huge Prize (around 50-74):
  - One of the Following:
    - [[Candy Cane Grab 'n Tag Cape]]
    - [[Blue and White Frostfell Elf Cap]]
    - [[Snow Form]]
    - [[Candy Cane Grab 'n Tag Tapestry]]
    - 3 [[Frozen Token of E'ci]]
    - [[Candycane Staff]]

- An Enormous Prize (around 75-91):
  - One of the Following:
    - [[Candy Cane Grab 'n Tag Cape]]
    - [[Peppermint Candy Crown]]
    - [[Frost Elk Antlers]]
    - [[Blue and White Frostfell Elf Cap]]
    - [[Snow Form]]
    - [[Candy Cane Grab 'n Tag Tapestry]]
    - [[Frostfell Flame Andiron]]
    - 4 [[Frozen Token of E'ci]]
    - [[Candycane Staff]]
