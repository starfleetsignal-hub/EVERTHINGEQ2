I discovered an empty crate near the shore of The Silent Deep. In fact, I can see the fading light of the mushroom caps scattered about the ocean floor. Someone must have dumped them all out! I better collect them all before they're ruined!

![A mushroom cap](images/A_mushroom_cap.png)

## Steps

1. Harvest 10 **mushroom caps** from the ocean floor and return them to the cart.
   - You have 4 minutes to complete this quest.
   - Some of the caps may spawn [[An ironblood spore]] that will attack you.
   - Look before you leap! There's a school of hungry fish in the waters nearest the cart.
   - Can be done with group but, no group updates.
   - You receive [[A soggy mushroom cap]] when you click on them, quest does not update but you see a tracker in your detrimental effects window, when you get 10 mushroom caps in your bags just go deposit them in the cart to update quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +250 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
- [[Dirt encrusted key]]
