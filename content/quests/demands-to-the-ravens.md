## Steps

1. Speak to  [[Nikolas MacCraifft]] at {{waypoint -327, 177, -31}} in the trade-skilling area of Frost Fang.
1. Return to [[Commissioner Varla Z'Velran|Varla]].

## Rewards

- One of the following, as applicable to your home city:
  - +250 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +250 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
  - +250 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**

## Notes

You will need the following harvests to complete this series:

- 16 [[Iron cluster]]
- 15 [[Severed maple]]
- 1 [[Tanned leather pelt]]
- 4 [[Electrum cluster]]

You will need the following fuels to complete this series:

- 16 [[Glowing Coal]]
- 8 [[Glowing Candle]]
