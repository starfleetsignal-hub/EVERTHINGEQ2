## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A savage wyrm|savage wyrms]]
1. Return to Kalz Spikewing for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 10g 74s 80c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Blackened Sphere of Greed]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Deathly Wand of Timelessness]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Gem of the Great Caretaker]]
  - [[Sphere of Sound Mind]]
