## Steps

Kill void creatures in [[The Shadowed Grove]] area of [[The Lesser Faydark]] for 3 auto-updates for pieces of Tuurho's father's necklace.

- First piece = [[A broken clasp]]
- Second piece = [[A necklace chain]]
- Third piece = [[A brooch]]

Return to [[Tuurho Tuiijah|Tuurho]] for your reward.

## Reward

- At least 13g 79s 50c
- +100 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Weapon Belt of the Faydark Druid]] (All Priests)
  - [[Weapon Belt of the Fayhunter]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Weapon Belt of the Forest Fist]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Weapon Belt of the Natural Caster]] (All Mages)
