## Steps

![The Staff of the Lamp of Orllis.](images/One_if_by_Sea_Base.jpg)
![The Base of the Lamp of Orllis.](images/One_if_by_Sea_Staff.jpg)
![The Basin of the Lamp of Orllis.](images/One_if_by_Sea_Basin.jpg)

1. Visit each mess tent surrounding [[Deathfist Citadel]].  Examine the poster hanging in each tent. One of them will update the quest.
1. Collect the following items from each of the three tents, laying on the ground:
   - Base of the Lamp of Orllis
   - Staff of the Lamp of Orllis
   - Basin of the Lamp of Orllis
1. Visit the top of [[Titan's Tower]] {{waypoint -23, 75, -365}}
   - Three level 40 [[A Deathfist equite|Deathfist equite]]s will attack. Slay them or run away.
1. Go to [[Blackhook Landing]] {{waypoint -20, 0, 400}}.  Two [[Greenmist rower|Greenmist rowers]] will spawn attack. Slay them.
1. Click the middle rowboat to enter [[The Greenmist]], a boat instance.
1. Slay 3 orcs (Level 40) (2 [[A Greenmist raider|Greenmist raider]]s and [[A Rujarkian emissary]])
1. Click on the ship's wheel to return to Zek
   - Talking to [[Grumbumble the great|Grumbumble]], [[Nibnoggin the sensational|Nibnoggin]], and [[Witgizzle the brain|Witgizzle]] is optional.
1. Return to Lute

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Blood-Stained Diviner's Armband]] (All Mages)
  - [[Blood-Stained Guard's Armband]] (All Fighter)
  - [[Blood-Stained Outrider's Armband]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Blood-Stained Surgeon's Armband]] (All Priests)
