## Steps

1. Scribe [[Unmarked Weapons and Shields]]. Each shield needs 2 [[Maple lumber]], 1 [[Iron cluster]] and 2 [[Glowing Coal|glowing coal]]. Each spear needs 2 iron cluster, 1 maple lumber and 2 Glowing coal.
   - Craft 4 Ry'Gorr style iron spears.
   - Craft 4 Coalition round shields.
1. Bring the shields and spears to [[Commissioner Varla Z'Velran|Varla]].
1. Deliver the spears to the location [[Commissioner Varla Z'Velran|Varla]] has marked down the hill to the west {{waypoint 92, 71, 269}}.
1. Report back to [[Commissioner Varla Z'Velran|Varla]].
