## Steps

1. Slay [[A Tallon field cook|a Tallon field cooks]] around {{waypoint 497, -18, -10}} for:
   - [[A windmill garlic shaker]]
   - [[A kobold pepper shaker]]
   - [[A geyser salt shaker]]
     - The Tallon troopers are place holders for the Tallon field cooks and you may (or may not) get one of the three items on each kill.
1. Return to Mess Sergeant Akseltz

## Rewards

- Hand armor based on class
  - [[Knight's Gauntlets of the Stalwart]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Channeler's Gloves of Focus]] (All Mages)
  - [[Apostle's Plate Gloves of Resolve]] (Clerics)
  - [[Oracle's Handguards of Acumen]] (Shamans)
  - [[Raider's Chain Gloves of Pure Darkness]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Protector's Leather Gloves of the Winds]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Hanshi's Hand Wraps of the Tiger]] (Brawlers)
