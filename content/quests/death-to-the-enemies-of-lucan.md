## Steps

At random, you will be asked to complete either of these:

- Destroy 20 Rujarkian War banners in [[The Sinking Sands]]. These are randomly found along The Orc Highway {{waypoint -464, -110, -189}}

- Kill 20 [[A Sandscrawler grunt|Sandscrawler grunts]] {{waypoint -180, -153, -1279}} (far NW near raptors) in [[The Sinking Sands]]
- Destroy 20 Dervish weapons racks {{waypoint -168, -111, -447}}. They have fairly quick respawn timer
- Kill 20 [[Sul'Dal bladefury|Sul'Dal bladefuries]] found in [[The Eye of Anuk]] {{waypoint -650, -208, -1200}}

## Rewards

- 9120 status
  - 8693 status @ 57
  - 4176 status @ 95
- +500 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
