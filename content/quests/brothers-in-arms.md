You must be a good-aligned Kerran of at least Level 20 to start this quest. **Be warned** - you may be *offered* the quest as a Vah Shir, but the quest giver (Lakosha) will suddenly be unable to recognise you at step 8.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Ajarn]] in the [[Lion's Mane Inn and Tavern]] {{waypoint 552, -10, 136}}
1. Speak to [[Laharn Rahai]] near [[The Fountain of Storms]] {{waypoint 488, -11.5, 137}}
1. Return to Ajarn
1. Meet Ajarn at the [[Silent Fist Dojo]] in Eldarr Grove, [[Qeynos Province District]]
1. Speak to Ajarn again.  He will zone you into a special instance of [[Silent Fist Dojo (instance)|Silent Fist Dojo]]
1. Defeat the 3 Masters.
   - The Masters ([[Ajarn (Mob)|Ajarn]], [[Laharn Rahai (Mob)|Laharn Rahai]] and [[Amazu Kharliko (Mob)|Amazu Kharliko]]) all scale to your level and are even con solo.  They are not linked, and it may be possible to get in a corner of the dojo so you don't have to fight all 3 at once.  Note that you can take a mercenary into the zone if you're having difficulty.
1. Speak to Ajarn
1. Return to Lakosha in South Qeynos, Qeynos Capitol District

## Rewards

- At least 5s 81c @ lvl 20
