I met Lothaire Stormgazer, an Ayr'Dal who has personal reasons to be celebrating Heroes' Festival; he was one of the souls who had been taken to Ethernere before their time, and who had been returned to the mortal realm due to the actions of the great heroes. I have taken up his offer to show me a glimpse of his memories. In return, he has asked me to obtain items found within.

## Steps

1. I am to enter a Memory of Fear in Ethernere, and obtain swatches of cryosilk. The portal into Lothaire Stormgazer's memory should present itself near him.
   1. Enter A Memory of Fear in Ethernere
      - The portal is a little way behind Lothaire on the hill. {{waypoint 368.71, -18.60, -117.53}}
   1. Gather swatches of cryosilk (3)
      - Proceed through the zone, killing mobs to open the doors.  Click on the sparkling bolts of cloth on the ground.  Possible locations:
        - {{waypoint -220, 0, 106}}
        - {{waypoint -152, -9, 124}}
        - {{waypoint -157, -9, -108}}
1. I should return to Lothaire Stormgazer as soon as possible, now that I have the swatches of cryosilk he requested.
   - Return to Lothaire Stormgazer
     - There is portal near the last swatch of cryosilk, at {{waypoint -100, 0, -102}}, so that you don't have to run all the way back to the beginning
1. I am to enter a Memory of Harrow's End, and gather etherfused shards. The portal into Lothaire Stormgazer's memory should present itself near him.
   1. Enter A Memory of Harrow's End
   1. Gather etherfused shards (2)
      - Gather 2 etherfused shards: {{waypoint -149, 20, -30}} first floor, and {{waypoint -42, -59, -133}} basement.
1. I should return to Lothaire Stormgazer as soon as possible, now that I have the etherfused shards he requested.
   - Return to Lothaire Stormgazer
     - There is portal near the last etherfused shard, at {{waypoint -22, -60, -85}}, so that you don't have to run all the way back to the beginning
1. I am to enter a Memory of Vesspyr Isles, in search of a divine transcendence filament. The portal to Lothaire Stormgazer's memory should present itself near him.
   1. Enter A Memory of Vesspyr Isles
   1. Gather divine transcendence filament
      - Located at {{waypoint 658, 373, 186}}
1. I should return to Lothaire Stormgazer as soon as possible, now that I have the divine transcendence filament he requested.
   1. Return to Lothaire Stormgazer
      - As you approach the location where Lothaire was, the quest will update
   1. Speak with The Lord of Dreams

- One of the following:
  - [[Cryosilk Dream Cloak]]
  - [[Replica: The Lord of Dreams]]
