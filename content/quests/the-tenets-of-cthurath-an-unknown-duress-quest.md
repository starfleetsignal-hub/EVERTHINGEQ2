## How to start

Completing [[Rage of Cthurath: Wrest of the Unknown]] (main signature adventure questline for this expansion) flags the whole account to start getting these dropped quest starters.<br>
Quest starter is found in treasure chest dropped by:

- [[A voidcrust ravager]] on [[Leech Scale Shore]] at {{waypoint -638, 19, 905}} in [[The Unknown]]
- [[A xaephian subjugator]] at [[Gates to Oblivion]] {{waypoint -427, 17, 1295}} in [[The Unknown]]

## Notes

- This is a POI quest to find other secluded quest giving locations.
  - *Some locations require flight*
- Players are tasked with finding the five locations within [[The Unknown]]. At each location there is a hidden 'Xebrith Cognizance' which will reveal itself once the player is close enough. Clicking on them will offer 'Kill X number of Y mob' type quests that can be completed separately from this quest.
- Players do not have to interact with these Cognizances, merely to discover them, to get credit for *this* quest. (But they will still need to interact with them if they want the individual quests given by them)
- All 5 of the locations are also part of the larger list of locations needed for The Unknown exploration achievement [[Exploration: The Unknown]]

## Steps

![Xebrith Cognizance location indicator](images/Xebrith_Cognizance_location_indicator.png)

1. Explore/Discover these 5 locations within [[The Unknown]] (in any order)
   - Reach [[Overflow Overlook]] {{waypoint -1295, 58, 950}} to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Meaninglessness|Xebrith Cognizance: Meaninglesness]]
   - Reach [[Oblivion's Perch]] {{waypoint -878, 63, 1175}} to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Disorder]]
   - Reach [[Shadowfang's Leap]] {{waypoint 43, 48, -536}} to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Helplessness]]
   - Reach [[Bloodweaver Dais]] {{waypoint -695, 140, -920}} (floating platform above the island) to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Hunger]]
   - Reach [[Shadowbane's Guilt]] {{waypoint -1242, 194, -956}} (floating platform above the island) to reveal [[Xebrith Cognizance: Destruction]]
