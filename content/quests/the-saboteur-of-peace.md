## Prerequisites

- You must be at least adventure level 58.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Trader Darmlin]] at [[Butcherblock Docks]]. {{waypoint 673, 24, 589}}
1. Speak with [[Helper Tjek]] in [[Felderham's Folly]]. {{waypoint -313, 150, 193}}
1. Find 5 pieces of *good fruit* by clicking on the trees around Felderham's Folly. You will either get the quest item or [[Rotten mountain fruit]]. Do not delete the rotten mountain fruit since you need at least one for the the next step.
1. Give the *good fruit* to Trader Darmlin.
1. Return to Pacificator Merrek and give him the rotten fruit. {{waypoint 332, 194, 374}}![What "a totem" looks like.](images/Totemlocation.jpg)
1. Harvest [[A totem]], which spawns somewhere in between [[The Quarry (POI)|The Quarry]] and [[Dagnor's Pass]]. Use [[Track Harvestables]] or a [[Gnomish Divining Rod]] and look for "a totem".
   - See the talk page for a list of known locations.
1. Return the totem to Pacificator Merrek.
1. Go to the [[Village of Somborn]] in [[Loping Plains]] and click on the dirt patch. {{waypoint -405, 12, 5}}
1. Return to Pacificator Merrek.
1. Swim in the pool at the base of [[Tunare's Sapling]] in [[Greater Faydark]]. You must **dive to the bottom** because swimming on the surface does not update the quest {{waypoint -48, -11, 68}}.
1. Return to the dirt patch in the Village of Somborn in Loping Plains and click on it.

## Rewards

- +7500 faction with **Quellious**
- [[Merrek's Altar of Quellious]]
