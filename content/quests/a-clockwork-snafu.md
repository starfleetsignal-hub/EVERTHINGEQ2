## Notes

![Tinkered Hoverclank - this is an automated flying mount that takes you to/from the location of the first clockwork you must collect for Snafu.](images/A_tinkered_hoverclank.png)

- Low-level players and players without a flying mount can use the **Tinkered Hoverclank** on the [[The Skystrider|Skystrider]] dirigible (the boat/ balloon) docks in Sinking Sands to fly to/from the first clockwork location.

## Steps

1. Go to the [[Moors of Ykesha]]. The NPC to start this is [[Snafu Fizzbrip]] {{waypoint 1753, 472, 911}}. He is on top of a ramp, not far off the travel globe and the platform of the [[The Skystrider|Skystrider]] (the boat) that travels between the Moors and Sinking Sands. The *bottom* of the ramp to climb up to him is at {{waypoint 1727, 453, 943}}.
1. You need to go to [[The Sinking Sands]]. Low level players can simply take the travel globe at the dock or the [[The Skystrider|Skystrider]] to [[The Sinking Sands]]. Once in the Sinking Sands, you need to be at the [[The Skystrider Launch-Dock|Skystrider Launch-Dock]]. On the platform right next to the boat there is a **Tinkered Hoverclank** {{waypoint -1449, -162, -764}} to take you to the next step. Locate it and click on it. You will fly to a high, rocky ledge on an island in Sinking Sands.
1. Locate the clockwork and click it to "Recover Clockwork". Possible locations include:![Recover Clockwork](images/Recover_Clockwork.jpg)
   - {{waypoint -2192, -164, 425}}
   - {{waypoint -2186, -164, 392}}
   - {{waypoint -2257, -163, 396}}
   - Recovering Clockwork must be done individually by each group member, and the clockworks vary on how soon they respawn.
1. Defeat 8 [[A bleachbone siren|bleachbone sirens]]. These were not aggro when you landed but will now attack when you get close to them.
1. Fly back to [[The Skystrider Launch-Dock|Skystrider Launch-Dock]] on the **Tinkered Hoverclank**.
1. Return to [[Snafu Fizzbrip]] in the [[Moors of Ykesha]].
1. Speak with [[Hansel Zalben]] in [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]] {{waypoint -632, 174, 1099}}
   - There's a portal to get back to Gnomeland at {{waypoint 1781, 453, 838}}, near the celebrating Gnomes.
1. Return to [[Snafu Fizzbrip|Snafu]] in the [[Moors of Ykesha]]. When you hail him, he will tell you to go to [[Timorous Deep]].
1. Collect the clockworks fishing not far from the [[Mok Rent]] station in [[Timorous Deep]], south-west of the bridge at {{waypoint 2102, 10, -183}}. They are down on the rocky outcrops above the river, not within it.
1. Defeat 8 [[A Yha-lei attacker|Yha-lei attackers]]. These were not aggro when you arrived but will now attack when you get close to them.
1. Return to [[Snafu Fizzbrip|Snafu]] in the [[Moors of Ykesha]] to complete the quest.
