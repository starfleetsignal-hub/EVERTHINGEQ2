## Notes

- *If you have lost your original mail, go to Jerol {{waypoint -90.14, -2.52, 81.59}} in [[The Village of Shin]] to get a new one.*

## Starting the Quest

1. Read the Mail from [[Queen Alwenielle (Thalumbra, the Ever Deep)|Queen Alwenielle]].
1. She would like me to assist her with hiding the last [[Chaos Stone]] and asks to meet her at [[The Tylwyth's Hut]].

## Steps

1. Speak with either [[Queen Cristanos]] or [[Queen Amree (Greater Faydark)|Queen Amree]] depending on your alignment:
   - If you're good aligned speak with [[Queen Amree (Greater Faydark)|Queen Amree]] {{waypoint 422.3, 136.62, 262.79}} in the [[Fae Royal Hall]] in the [[Kelethin]] in [[Greater Faydark]].
   - If you're evil aligned speak with [[Queen Cristanos]] {{waypoint -577.86, 32.58, -193.24}} in [[Darklight Palace|the Darklight Palace]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]].
1. Travel to [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]] by clicking the **tinkered portal-gate** {{waypoint 298.85, 127.45, 308.4}} in [[Kelethin]] or {{waypoint 29.77, 3.68, 317.64}} in [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]].
1. Speak with [[Queen Alwenielle (Thalumbra, the Ever Deep)|Queen Alwenielle]] {{waypoint -333.79, 241.77, 383.79}} at [[The Tylwyth's Hut]] in the [[Zou'Lidelas]] area of [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]].<br> -- *Note: The Queen's hut is suspended in the branches about 50 meters up, if you arrive on the map to an empty grove, look up.*
1. Gather 8 [[Blushing umbrite]] around [[Penumbra Expansion]] {{waypoint 567.30, 55.69, -687.68}} or [[Aberrant Isle]] {{waypoint 127.21, 33.41, -815.2}}. ![Blushing umbrite](images/Blushing_umbrite2.png)
   - *Note: Aberrant Isle is full of ^^^ mobs. Blushing umbrite is a small white geode with a red hue radiating off it and is not trackable. There are 2 nodes that respawn quickly and are near each other by the waypoint posted above.*
1. Return to [[Queen Alwenielle (Thalumbra, the Ever Deep)|Queen Alwenielle]] {{waypoint -333.79, 241.77, 383.79}}.
1. Queen Alwenielle has sent me into the [[Arcanna'se Spire: Revealed|Arcanna'se Spire]] to switch out the last [[Chaos Stone]] with an [[Imitation Chaos Stone]]. Her plan must work!
   1. Enter [[Arcanna'se Spire: Revealed]] {{waypoint -61.95, 18.9, 160.85}} through the portal in the [[Abyssal Chasm]].
   1. Collect an [[Arcane Door Unseal Spell|an Arcane Gate Seal spell]] somewhere behind the second door to the left after zone in. ![An Arcane Gate Seal spell looks like a blowing sheet of paper.](images/An_Arcane_Gate_Seal_spell.jpg)
      - *Note: The pages can be found in either the library with the podium {{waypoint 140.89, 2.93, -21.22}}, or the binding room {{waypoint 159.66, 6.37, -62.81}}. It looks like a sheet of paper that is flapping in a breeze.*
   1. Click the **scribe's arcane podium** {{waypoint 147.67, 2.93, 14.99}} twice, first to put the scroll on it and then to activate the spell to open the doors.
      - *Note: Return to the center room at {{waypoint 0, 0, -46}} then through the first door to the right after the zone in door.*
   1. Find the [[Portal Gem|portal crystal]] for the damaged portal anchor at a random location in the room at {{waypoint -44.01, 4.27, 20}}. ![The portal crystal is a blue orb somewhere on the floor](images/Portal_crystal.jpg)
   1. Retrieve the [[Portal Gem]] and repair the **damaged portal anchor** at {{waypoint 56.25, 1.26, 26.84}} through the first door to the left of the zone-in door. ![a damaged portal anchor](images/A_damaged_portal_anchor.jpg)
   1. Go through the **portal to the upper floor** {{waypoint 51.29, 1.26, 18.62}} that has opened behind you.
   1. Queen Alwenielle appears as you walk down the steps and you should follow her to the Zou Crystalline.
   1. Speak with Queen Alwenielle to receive an [[Imitation Chaos Stone]].
   1. Switch the Chaos Stone for the imitation by clicking the **Zou Crystalline** {{waypoint -470.8, 30.38, -56.71}} in the center of the terrace.
   1. Listen to the dialogue between Queen Alwenielle, [[Tserrina Syl'Tor]] and [[Lanys T'Vyl]].
   1. Queen Alwenielle must get you and the crystal to safety! You will be teleported away by Queen Alwenielle with the [[Chaos Stone]] to a beach {{waypoint 108.00, -242.61, -918.64}} near [[Twark]] in [[Obulus Frontier]].
      - *Warning: The screen will become completely bright white!!!*
   1. Where you are captured by a group of Crusaders of Greenmist. When you awake, the quest completes with an auto-update and you find yourself in chains and dragged next to [[Trooper Sotek]] {{waypoint 733.44, 248.99, 1334.61}} in the [[Crusader's Cave]].
      - *Note: Make sure you recast your mount*

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g 35,200 status
- [[Scribe's Arcane Podium]] (a [[Housing|house item]])
- **[[Obulus Frontier Access]]** - The first completion unlocks this for the whole account.
