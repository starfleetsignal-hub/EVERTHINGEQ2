## Description

"Amber Walker, a provisioner at Green Knoll, would like me to slay several corrupted spiders for their tender meat so she can prepare a delicious meal for Kelethin's defenders. The spiders can be found wandering within Treant Grove." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[A corrupted spider|corrupted spiders]] around {{waypoint 442.18, 3.6, -1.38}}
1. Return to [[Amber Walker]]{{waypoint 595, 45, 70}}

## Rewards

- At least 2s 57c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Amber Walker: "Thank you for starting the fire, [Player Character]. The warmth feels so nice, and I am much more rested now. I really needed that! Could I convince you to gather meat for me so that I can cook something for the defenders?"
- Player Character: "Sure, I can gather you some meat."
- Amber Walker: "Corrupted spider meat is surprisingly delicious and should be easy to obtain. They can be found within Treant Grove."
- Player character: "I will track them and return."

I am to kill the corrupted spiders that crawl within Treant Grove, for their tender meat to be used for cooking. Slay the corrupted spiders.

I have enough tender spider meat for Amber's cooking. I should speak to Amber Walker at Green Knoll, now that I have the meat she wanted. Speak to Amber.

- Amber Walker: "Were you able to get the spider meat, [Player Character]?"
- Player Character: "I was, though it was unpleasant to do so."
- Amber Walker: "But it is very tasty, especially with cedar star nuts. Too bad I don't have any. I know how a delicious meal can reinvigorate the troops."

I spoke to Amber. Amber appreciated the spider meat I provided.

Amber Walker, a provisioner at Green Knoll, was thankful for the spider meat that I provided for her. She is going to be able to make a big meal for Kelethin's defenders out of it.
