## Notes

- Completion of this quest opens quest [[For the Good of the Herd]] from [[Herd Chief Turrak Greatstep]].
- You will be unable to progress collecting webbing piles for the [[Webbin' Feverishly]] quest until you have gathered the stones in Step 1 of [[Skur'rax and Stones]] quest

## Steps

![Skur'rax webbing mound](images/Skur'rax_webbing_mound.png)
![an ichorbeak scourge to use the web on](images/An_ichorbeak_scourge.png)
Narezi requires several living ichorbeaks, you will need to make nets out of Skur'rax webbing to catch them.

- Travel through The Gnarls and along western edge of Writher Ruins to  Skur'rrax Spire area around {{waypoint 103, 31, 455}}
1. Gather Skur'rax webbing mounds for webbing x14
   - look for white lacy nests to harvest (not trackable), along paths in Skur'rax Spires. Fast respawn.
   - when you gather 14 of them you receive [[Skur'rax Web Net]]
     - Possible locations: {{waypoint 216, 29, 466}}, {{waypoint 329, 43, 463}}, {{waypoint 328, 50, 534}}, {{waypoint 381, 55, 552}}, {{waypoint 396, 49, 640}}, {{waypoint 521, 40, 623}}, {{waypoint 585, 39, 580}}, {{waypoint 634, 42, 562}}
1. catch 7 ichorbeaks by using the [[Skur'rax Web Net]] on them - they can't be in combat in order to use the net, the net will aggro them but they will die shortly after and give you an update. You might need to keep your pet or merc on passive so they don't accidentally pull them.
1. Return to [[Narezi]]
