## Steps

1. Destroy creatures in the K'Val Ruins
   - Destroy 5 wildgrown Kromzek giants ([[A wildgrown Kromzek basher|bashers]], [[A wildgrown Kromzek entangler|entanglers]], [[A wildgrown Kromzek handler|handlers]], [[A wildgrown Kromzek lasher|lashers]]) - found all through the ruins
   - Destroy 5 [[A wildgrown Drakkel dasher]] wolves - found all through the ruins
   - Destroy 5 [[A briarthorn entraller|briarthorn entrallers]] - found in the throne room of the ruins
1. Return to Commander Bludeye

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Witherstone Handled Mace]]
  - [[Mace of Witherstone]] (All Priests)
  - [[Sacrificial Satyr's Dagger]] (All Mages)
  - [[Dagger of Withering Hopes]] (All Scouts)
