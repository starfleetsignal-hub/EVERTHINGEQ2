## Steps

1. Seek out evidence left behind by the vampires.  You're looking for bodies with white sparkles.
1. Found at:
   - {{waypoint 32, -8, -94}}
   - {{waypoint 7, -18, -279}}
   - {{waypoint -81, -33, -327}}
1. Return to Glaria.
1. Seduce a bar patron, and convince them to meet you in the alley. The bar patrons are the [[A lonely citizen]] in the bar of the [[The Jade Tiger's Den]].  Follow the one you talk to outside and down to one end of [[The Jade Tiger's Den]] {{waypoint -43, -24, -55}} or {{waypoint -142, -24, -94}}. Two [[A vampiric ambusher]] will spawn.  Kill them quick, as they despawn fairly quickly.  If they despawn, go back to the bar and chat up another [[A lonely citizen]] and follow them.
1. Return to Glaria again.
1. She'll give you a [[Sun Crystal]] and ask you to search out the strange vampires at night. (I did it between 0800-1000 Norrath time and it still worked fine. The blow locs are perfect.)
1. Your looking for [[A wandering vampire]]. They can be found at:
   - {{waypoint -98, -11, -82}}
   - {{waypoint 177, -16, 120}}
   - {{waypoint 305, -4, 98}}
   - {{waypoint 297, -3, 10}}
   - They randomly spawn as you move about the city
1. Return to Glaria yet again for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 25g 22s 61c
