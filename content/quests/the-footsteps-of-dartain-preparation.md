## Steps

![Basket of Resin for Step 2](images/Preparation_Resin.jpg)

1. Speak to Beziz Monommin in [[Deepwater Pavilion]] {{waypoint -163, 181, -405}}.
1. Go to the [[Abandoned Kejek Village]] via the cave entrance {{waypoint -580, 467, 346}} to collect the Resin at one of the following locations:
   - {{waypoint -1042, 534, 580}}
   - {{waypoint -929, 518, 570}}
   - {{waypoint -998.79, 521.58, 656.78}}
   - {{waypoint -983.17, 531.29, 541.61}}
1. Descend into the Nexus in [[Quel'ule]] {{waypoint -257, 326, 601}} and right click the Resin to charge it.
1. Return to Beziz.
1. See Kallana Esshar in [[Deepwater Pavilion]] {{waypoint -110, 188, -438}}.
1. Go to [[Vasty Deep: The Abandoned Labs]]. Click the console in the Librarium {{waypoint -97, -60, -351}}. (You can use a cleared instance to get this update.)
1. Return to Kallana.
