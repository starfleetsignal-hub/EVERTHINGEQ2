This quest is part 1 of a 4-part quest series involving [[Nektulos Forest]].

## Steps

*NOTE: Targok will not even offer the quest until you are at least level 20.*

1. Collect hides from several animals in [[Nektulos Forest]].
   - 8 hides from [[A firelight shriller|firelight shrillers]] (uncommon update)
   - 8 hides from [[An ash owlbear|ash owlbears]] (uncommon update)
1. Return to Targok.
