This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Enter [[A Maj'Dul Residence]].
1. Kill all mobs.
1. [[A Maj'Dul homeowner]] will spawn, kill him.
1. Return to any Tears grifter for your reward.

## Rewards

- 3g 23s 29c
- One of the following:
  - 6 [[Blades insignia token]]
  - 6 [[Coin insignia token]]
  - 6 [[Truth insignia token]]
