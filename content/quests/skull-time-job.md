## Steps

*Blortch of Clan Mucktongue was alarmed to hear the eldritch spellstone staffs were not just raising fallen Blackhook orcs, but that slain goblins have also been raised in the Spiral of Vul.*

1. Hunt 8 Vuldor Minions
1. Return to [[Blortch]]
1. Deposit goblin skulls into Blortch's Bone Barrel

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- One of the following:

  - [[Blackhook Rhodium Woven Armguards]]
