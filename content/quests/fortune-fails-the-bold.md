## Notes

- You must do [[Fleshless Tongue Untied]] to be able to talk Ga'zoka

## Steps

1. Hunt 8 [[An enslaved grimling|enslaved grimlings]]
   - *Linked to [[An akheva enthraller|akheva enthrallers]] in [[Umbral Plains]] around {{waypoint -307.61, 162.48, 380.94}} or {{waypoint 59, 151, 508}}.*
1. Click the **Sacred Treasure** at the bottom of [[Whispering Falls]] {{waypoint -317.70, 34.89, -136.91}}.
1. Kill [[A mutated netherbian]].
1. Click the **Sacred Treasure** again.
1. Return to [[Ga'zoka]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- One of the following:
  - [[Shadow-Touched Pride Cape]]
  - [[Shadow-Touched Pride Cloak]]
