## Steps

1. Loot 10 [[Transference Directive|Transference Directives]] from the corpses of shadowed men.
   - Good locations for shadowed men include [[The Obelisk of Lost Souls]] and near the docks of [[Nektulos Forest]].

## Rewards

- The ability to understand and speak [[Words of Shade]], the common language of all Shadowed Men.
