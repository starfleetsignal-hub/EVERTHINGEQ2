**Origin server**: Frup Groaak is in Castleview Hamlet on Origin

## Steps

1. Track down [[Giidib of the First Circle|Giidib]] to his last known location, on the beach in southern [[Antonica]]. {{waypoint -630, -34, 800}}
1. Follow Giidib's footprints and examine:
   1. a log {{waypoint -816, -5, 715}}
   1. a rock {{waypoint -697, -5, 590}}
   1. a stump {{waypoint -841, -5, 542}}
   1. around a tree {{waypoint -885, -10, 408}}
   1. a rock in the pond within [[Archer's Woods]] at {{waypoint -764, -7, 456}}. This will spawn [[Giidib of the First Circle|Giidib]].
1. Right-click Giidib and select 'Lead him to the nearest guard tower'. Run to [[Southern Qeynos Tower]] at {{waypoint -388, 4, 285}}. Giidib will drop dead just before reaching the guards posted outside.
1. When Giidib dies, return to [[Frup Groaak]] in Qeynos Harbor and give him the bad news.
