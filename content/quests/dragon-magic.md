## Steps

1. Examine the shinies in the mistgoblin area. A few confirmed locations are:
   - {{waypoint -104, 132, -666}}
   - {{waypoint -67, 144, -75}}
   - {{waypoint -58, 144, -708}}
   - {{waypoint -3, 144, -75}}
   - {{waypoint 20, 144, -781}}
   - {{waypoint -108, 131, -661}}
1. Approach a [[A mistgoblin shaman]] and speak with it.
   - Dismiss your [[Mercenaries|mercenary]] if you have one active.
1. Go to {{waypoint 24, 152, -840}} for an auto-update.
1. Return to Sirris Xanx.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- +2,000 faction with **Kunzar Jungle Villagers**
- [[Glowing Gem of Prowess]]
