## Steps

1. I must kill ten wisps and collect their dust. Kill the [[A grove wisp|grove wisps]] or [[A moon wisp|moon wisps]] at {{waypoint -598.64, -50.95, -610.87}}.
1. Go back to [[Arconicus]].
1. I must kill ten dangerous zombies ([[A dusty cadaver|dusty]], [[A putrid cadaver|putrid]], [[A rotting cadaver|rotting]] and [[A twitching cadaver|twitching cadavers]]) found in [[Enlightenment Cemetery]] {{waypoint 117.96, -47.95, -221.99}}.
1. Go back to [[Arconicus]].
