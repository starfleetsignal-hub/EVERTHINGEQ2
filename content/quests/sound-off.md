This quest is part 2 of a 5-quest series in the [[Freeport Racial Timeline]] Barbarian Series.

Test your fierce war cry on the residents of the Scale Yard.

## Steps

1. Yell at [[Vleske Vylkle]], near the dock {{waypoint 14, -4, 63}}.
1. Yell at [[Caleb Brazenroot]], near the inn {{waypoint 29, -4, 26}}.
1. Yell at [[Jondy Cobbleblork]], near the bank {{waypoint 75, -4, -36}}.
1. Return to Malachi.

- Coin: 1s 8c
- [[Halasian-Seasoned Beef Jerky]]
- [[Sleetspear Clan Brew]]
