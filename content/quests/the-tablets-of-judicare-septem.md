## Steps

1. Gather the following *Tablets of Judicare Septem*. They are [[Track Harvestables|trackable]] and must be collected in order:
   1. Tablet 1: Near [[Trythec Loft]] {{waypoint -18, 50, 423}}
   1. Tablet 2: Next to a tree near [[Gorowyn Beach Outpost]] {{waypoint 2382, 60, 1002}}
   1. Tablet 3: Underwater, beneath the [[Faydedar Watch|Gorowyn Docks]] {{waypoint 2311, -10, 1396}}
   1. Tablet 4: In an alcove in [[The Bloodworks]], near [[Mok Rent]] {{waypoint 1964, 45, 386}}
      - The flight master on the Gorowyn Docks cannot take you to Mok Rent. Fly back to the Gorowyn Beach Outpost and use the flight master there to reach Mok Rent.
   1. Tablet 5: Top of the waterfall at the mouth of the Mok Rent cave {{waypoint 1914, 47, -230}}
   1. Tablet 6: On the northernmost tip on [[Chrykori Isle]]  {{waypoint 260, 5, -714}}
      - As you approach, you will be attacked by [[A furtive goblin]]. Killing him is not necessary.
      - Killing the goblin decreases your faction with [[Bristlebane]] by -150.
1. Return to Justinian Theo. {{waypoint -290.70, 1.53, -155.28}}

## Rewards

- At least 2s
- +1250 faction with **The Tribunal**
- [[Humble Altar of The Tribunal]]
