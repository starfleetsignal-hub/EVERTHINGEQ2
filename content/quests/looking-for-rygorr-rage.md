## Steps

1. Search for [[Ulart Koshgak]] at {{waypoint -1015, -344, 2406}}
1. Kill 7 [[A blizzard dervish|blizzard dervishes]]
1. Kill 1 more [[A blizzard dervish|blizzard dervish]]
1. Speak with [[Orolr Throatgrinder]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +5,000 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
