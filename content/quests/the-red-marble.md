## Steps

1. Interact with the boar's head you got the black marble from at {{waypoint -14, 0, 40}}.
   - Place the red eye in the hollow socket.
   - The quest will auto-update and complete.
   - [[A swine fiend]] will spawn behind you in the hallway. Killing him is not required to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- Access to [[The Library (Nektropos Castle)|The Library]].
