## Steps

![The Blackwater Mask, as worn by Hack.](images/Blackwatermask_worn.jpg)

1. Retrieve the Blackwater Mask from the [[Freethinker Hideout]].
   1. Bring the barrel at {{waypoint 59, 0, 12}} to the breakable wall at {{waypoint 65, 4, 49}}, right-click it and select ignite.
      - When the wall is broken, one werewolf will appear at the wall opening and three more will appear further down the other corridors. They start pathing within a couple minutes and traverse the entire corridor system including the room hidden by the broken wall.
   1. Click on the middle chest in the hidden room. {{waypoint -560, -20, 140}}
1. Return to [[Havras "Hack" Scutter]].
