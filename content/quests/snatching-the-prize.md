## Steps

1. Follow the waypoint to Iarog's room {{waypoint 45, 3, 64}}.
1. Search for The Forest Spider, located on the shelf in the northeast corner of the room.  Right-click on The Forest Spider to take him.  Iarog will attack you.
1. Slay Iarog.
1. Right-click on The Forest Spider and take him.
1. Inspect the spider in your inventory.
1. Return to [[Fedul]].
