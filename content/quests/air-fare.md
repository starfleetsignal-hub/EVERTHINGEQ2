## Steps

1. Find [[Raffik]] in [[Paineel]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]] at {{waypoint 1987, -298, 3339}}
1. Craft a [[Potion of Nimble Fingers (Quest Item)|Potion of Nimble Fingers]] from the recipe given.
   - 1 [[Marr cherry]]
   - 1 [[Lapsang tea leaf]]
   - 1 [[Caynar nut]]
   - 1 [[Ethereal Candle]]
     - Note: Chemistry Table at {{waypoint 1755, -298, 3495}}
1. Speak to [[Aalithiana]] at {{waypoint 1813, -298, 3459}}
1. Return to [[Raffik]]

## Rewards

- At least 8p 10g
- [[Potion of Nimble Fingers (QuestReward)|Potion of Nimble Fingers]] recipe book
