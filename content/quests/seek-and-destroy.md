## Steps

1. Complete the following actions, in no particular order:
   - Slay 8 members of The Order of Rime.
   - Locate The Order of Rime's first secret camp: {{waypoint -395.40, -102, 1156}}
   - Locate The Order of Rime's second secret camp: {{waypoint -358, -89, 1382}}
   - Locate The Order of Rime's third secret camp: {{waypoint -217.70, -399.32, 870.27}} (in the gulch)
1. Return to [[Revliss Dru'shyv]] in [[Sathir's Span].
