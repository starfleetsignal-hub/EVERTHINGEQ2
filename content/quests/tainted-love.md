## Notes

- This quest is mutually exclusive with [[Heal the Pain]], you must chose one or the other. Either [[Heal the Pain]] or [[Tainted Love]] can be completed once per year, so you can get the one you missed later.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Shard of Love: Seeding Corruption]] from the portal on the docks in [[Everfrost]] and complete the following tasks: {{waypoint -55, -9, 57}}
   - Kill 20 Shard of Love inhabitants
   - Plant 8 *Seeds of Distrust* in vases.
1. Return to the Lady of Insurrection.

## Rewards

- 5 [[Erollisi coin|Erollisi coins]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Cloak of the Sisterhood]] (available only if you do not already have one)
  - [[Cowl of the Sisterhood]]
  - [[Gilded Vase of Devotion]]
  - [[Fires of anguish]]
