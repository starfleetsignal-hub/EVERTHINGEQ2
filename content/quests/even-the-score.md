## Steps

1. Kill [[Venox Tarkog (Named)|Venox Tarkog]] in Cardin Ward at {{waypoint -315, 13, -34}}.
1. Return to the beach at {{waypoint -100, -3, 431}} and kill [[Dalyana Pinecrest (Named)|Dalyana Pinecrest]]. She's a 95heroic berserker accompanied by two [[A follower of Innoruuk|followers of Innoruuk]] (95v heroic).  The quest will auto-complete once all are dead.

At least 1p 30g
