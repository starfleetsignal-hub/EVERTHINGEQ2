## Steps

1. Receive an [[Empty Jug]] from Tuathil.
1. Do the following steps (in no particular order):
   - Slay [[A rotting guardian|rotting guardians]] near the [[Upper Emerald River]] around {{waypoint -627, 47, 91}} until you have gathered 6 rock samples
   - Use the Jug in [[Emerald Lake]] at {{waypoint -795, 37, 301}}
1. Return to [[Tuathil Laeds]]

## Rewards

- At least 10s 18c
- [[Kelethin Courser Whistle]] (25% run speed)
- One of the following:
  - [[Cured Wraps]]
  - [[Belt of Woven Growth]]
  - [[Dewtrap Vest]]

## Note

Prior to its removal, this quest came between [[Corruptor Thurizil (Quest)]] and [[The Plan]] in the [[Greater Faydark Timeline]]
