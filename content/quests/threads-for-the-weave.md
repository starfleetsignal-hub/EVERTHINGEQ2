## Steps

1. Go to the Order of Rime camp near the [[Lake of Dismay]] {{waypoint 400, 72, -555}} and search for six spell scrolls. You can harvest the same spot more than once. Respawn is pretty short. These are small glowing scrolls (you may have to zoom in to see them) at the following locations:
   - {{waypoint 364, 99, -626}}
   - {{waypoint 400, 72, -554}}
   - {{waypoint 429, 65, -559}}
   - {{waypoint 363, 69, -599}}
   - {{waypoint 411, 99, -620}}
   - {{waypoint 425, 70, -587}}
1. Kill [[An elite flurryburst (Kunzar Jungle)|elite flurrybursts]] or [[An elite nova-vicar (Kunzar Jungle)|elite nova-vicars]] for one last scroll.
1. Return to Kerss Rekssok.

## Rewards

- At Least 36g 79s 48c
- +500 faction with **The City of Jinisk**
