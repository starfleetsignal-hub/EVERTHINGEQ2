## Steps

1. Gather and hunt the following:
   - Hunt 10 [[A wild snapper|wild snappers]] from the [[Lifire River]] around {{waypoint 508.36, 2.18, 66.04}} ![Sorghum](images/Sorghum.png)
   - Gather 8 sorghum from the river banks. See talk page for locations.
1. Return to [[Sathara]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Spellwoven Dendromail Hauberk]] -- *Chain*
  - [[Spellwoven Viridsilk Robe]] -- *Cloth*
  - [[Spellwoven Florahide Curiass]] -- *Leather*
  - [[Spellwoven Chloroplate Breastplate]] -- *Plate*
