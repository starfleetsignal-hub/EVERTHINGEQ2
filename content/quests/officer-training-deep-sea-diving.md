## Note

*make sure that you only have your brokenskull Illusion up, otherwise you can't get the update from Fip Marrsquire.*

## Steps

1. I have been asked to farm resources from the bottom of the sea.
   - ![A coral chunk](images/Coral_chunk.png)I need to retrieve coral chunks from the sea bottom.  The items are permanently-spawned nodes that look like small bushes; swim in large circle around {{waypoint -851, -1108, 1385}} and {{waypoint -753, -1110, 1255}}. See the talk page for locations found by other players.
   - I need to collect crab legs for the pirates. Auto-updates are obtained from [[A Ykeshan snapper|Ykeshan snappers]] all along coastline.{{waypoint -1255, -1090, 1521}}
   - I need to gather metal and wood from the wrecked vessels.
     - Metal and Wood from the wrecked vessels found on the harbor floor near Salty Sail Port and on the west side of the peninsula. These have been reported to be found around the following locations: {{waypoint -1102, -1110, 1501}} {{waypoint -1168, -1110, 1590}} {{waypoint -1462, -1112, 1529}} and {{waypoint -935, -1108, 1588}}
     - Alternatively try the talk page for exact spawns found by other players.
1. I need to speak with [[Petty Officer Kimtiz]] in [[Brokenskull Loft]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 5g
