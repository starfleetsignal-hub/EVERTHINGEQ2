## Steps

- You receive [[Drallvin's Lead]]
1. Use Drallvin's Lead on any of the corrupted wolves in the area.
1. Return to Researcher Drallvin
1. The wolf you just captured turns aggro and attacks.  Kill it.
1. Speak to Researcher Drallvin

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
