## Steps

1. I need to locate the Gehein prisoner, somewhere within the ogre city of Oggok. Being such a valuable detainee, they've probably stowed him well behind their frontal defenses.
   - Locate the missing Gehein within Oggok
     - [[Ruriix]] {{waypoint -348, 7, 128}}
1. Ruriix informed me that the enchantment on his bone cage can be broken with a special powder carried by ogre shamans. I doubt they will give them up without a fight. The shamans can be found within the stone buildings about Oggok.
   - Slay [[An Oggok spiritbinder|Oggok spiritbinders]] for their [[A pile of bone powder|magical powder]] (5)
1. I should return to Ruriix's cage and free him before the ogres take notice of their dead spiritual leaders.
   1. Slay the high shaman guarding Ruriix's cage
   1. Use the bone powder to disenchant Ruriix's cage
   1. Speak to Ruriix
1. I need to hurry back to Czilk and see if Ruriix returned safely.
   - Return to Czilk
