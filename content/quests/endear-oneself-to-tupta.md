## Steps

1. Kill a number of Thullosian ogres found throughout Ykesha's Spine in the [[Moors of Ykesha]] at {{waypoint 579, 16, -177}}:
   - Kill 6 [[Thullosian poker]]s
   - Kill 5 [[Thullosian barsher]]s
   - Kill 5 [[Thullosian spearer]]s, found mostly in the river at {{waypoint 682, 109, 29}} and {{waypoint 796, 124, 36}}.
1. Return to [[Urp Brightblade]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 5g
- +10,000 faction with **Guktan Guard**

One of the following:

- [[Void Ringlet of Power]]
- [[Void Signet of Power]]
- [[Void Ring of Power]]
- [[Void Emblem of Power]]
