## Steps

1. Gather and hunt the following
   - Gather 8x permafrost potatoes - small  green and red leafy plant found on cliffs West of camp, usually around rocks.![Potato Plant](images/PotatoPlant.jpg)
     - Possible locations: :{{waypoint -317, 75, -368}}, {{waypoint -331, 75, -379}}, {{waypoint -223, 75, -426}}, {{waypoint -179, 76, -337}}
   - Hunt 12x mammoths for steaks.
     - Found below camp and just in camp
1. Return to [[Zorie]] at Hoarfang Ridge {{waypoint -475, 123, -338}} in [[Western Wastes]]
