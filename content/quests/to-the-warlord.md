## Notes

- While the other quests offered from [[Scout Nan'Dor]] can be completed without having done the rest of the [[Jarsath Wastes Timeline|Jarsath Wastes Sel'Nok Brigade Timeline]], provided you have enough faction with The Sel'Nok Brigade to get the quests he offers in the first place, this quest will not be offered until the other quests in the timeline have been completed.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Warlord Sel'Nok]] in the [[Sel'Nok Cave]]. {{waypoint -996, -67, -980}}

## Rewards

- +50000 faction with **The Sel'Nok Brigade**
- At least 3g 37s 19c
- One of the following:
  - [[Mottled Sel'Nok Helm]]
  - [[Shined Sel'Nok Helm]]
  - [[Stitched Sel'Nok Helm]]
  - [[Tanned Sel'Nok Helm]]
