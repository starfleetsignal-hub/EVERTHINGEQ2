## Steps

1. Mine 8 deposits of *Pi'ter Sahlt* (trackable) from around [[Camp Kromdek]] {{waypoint -1123, -327, -1751}}. Each node can be harvested twice. See the talk page for a list of known spawns.
1. Return the Tinkerist Glazid.

## Rewards

- At least 17g 22s 36c
- +5000 faction with **Exiles of Droga**
