## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Bristlebane has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items

1. Purchase the [[Pilfered Pyrite Ore|pilfered pyrite ore]] from Toolie for 5000 status
   - Wanders the road behind The Fools Gold in [[Rivervale]] (from {{waypoint -59, -18, -31}} to Cask's General Store at {{waypoint -84, -19, 99}})
1. Purchase the [[Dictated Sound of Silence|dictated sound of silence]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the cunning fox tail from Kuutanya for 5000 status
   - Wanders the path on Isle of Wisdom in [[Zimara Breadth]])
1. Return to the [[Herald to the Grand Prankster]] at {{waypoint -832, 7, -203}} in [[Rivervale]]
