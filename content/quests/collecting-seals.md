## Description

I must collect Erudin Palace Royal Seals. I can find these seals on any erudite within Erudin Palace. These include the palace intimidators, palace haruspices, palace philosophers, or palace chevaliers.

## Steps

1. Collect 5 Erudin Royal Palace Seals: 4 from the bottom floor / 5th from random mage body on Gloom Chevalier floor.
1. Return to Rafiqa

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
