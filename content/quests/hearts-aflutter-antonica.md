## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- Some of the reward items are available from [[Douglas]] or [[Steven]], so check what they have if you want to choose the rewards that are unique to this quest.
- If you score at least 110, you receive the achievement, [[On Wings of Love]].
- Upon completing the quest a fifth time, you receive an additional reward, one of two prefix titles:
  - [[Heart Racer]]
  - [[Love Chaser]]
  - If you complete the quest a sixth time, you receive the second title you didn't pick.

## Steps

1. Step on the *Hearts a'Flutter Starting Pad*. {{waypoint 47, -19, -28}}
1. Withing 3 minutes, collect as many *Essences of Erollisi* as possible!
1. Speak with Goldie Hammerfall.
