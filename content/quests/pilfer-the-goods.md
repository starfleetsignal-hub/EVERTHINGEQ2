## Steps

1. Kill the [[A Jinisk caravan guard leader|Jinisk caravan guard leader]] and the [[A Jinisk caravan guard|Jinisk caravan guards]] at {{waypoint 406, -42, 36}}.
1. Right-click the cart and pilfer the contents.
1. Right-click the cart again to burn it.
1. Return to Field Chief Ry'zilk.

## Rewards

- At least 10g 20s 71c
- +6,000 faction with **Ry'zilk's Renegades**
