## Steps

1. Kill select mobs in Kunzar Jungle till you have 30 [[Impure Kunzar Essence]]
   - Mobs that drop the essence include: mushroom people near [[Sebilis]], Kunzar Swampflies & Vespid Hornets near [[The City of Jinisk]], kunzar tigers, burynai rabble rousers, an undergrowth sabrecat, iksar villager
1. once you have 30 right click the stack of them in your inventory and pick purify to create the Pure Kunzar Extract
1. Speak to [[Leandre]] by using the [[An incandescent wand]] or by targeting yourself and hail (press H by default)
