## Steps

1. Scribe [[Grant's Cobalt Chiori Paste Recipe]] and craft 6 [[Cobalt Chiori Paste]]s at the stove {{waypoint 3633, -1017, 1656}}. Requires 6 [[Aerated mineral water]], 6 [[Effulgent kindling]], and the [[Cobalt Chiori]] from the previous quest
1. Speak to Grant

NOTE:  If you can't find the recipe, make sure "show hidden" is checked in your recipe list.
