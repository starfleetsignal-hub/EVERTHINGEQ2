## Steps

1. Speak with Tomian Keeneye at {{waypoint 435, 81, -578}}
1. Find Focus Pillar Avi at {{waypoint -117, 24, -570}}
1. Click the pillar to collapse it.
1. Find Focus Pillar Lith at {{waypoint 479, 66, -251}}  (Note: this is in the safe harvesting area at the village.)
1. Kill a thorn flutter harrier attacking you
1. Click the pillar to collapse it.
1. Return to Kikos

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- Select one of the following:
  - [[Void Obelisk Gauntlets of Unreality]]
  - [[Void Obelisk Gage of Unreality]]
  - 15 [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
