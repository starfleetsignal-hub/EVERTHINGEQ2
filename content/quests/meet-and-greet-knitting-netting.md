## Notes

A repeatable quest for Faction. Repeatable up to 3 times and only if your Port Woe faction is less than 20,000. This quest requires:

- 9 [[Larch Wood]]
- 3 [[Whittling Knife]] (Purchased from the Fuel Merchant)
- 15 [[Zimaran Sandpaper]]
- [[Recipe Scroll: Netting Needles|Recipe]] for [[Netting Needles]] auto scribed upon accepting quest

## Steps

1. Craft 3 [[Netting Needles]]
1. Speak with [[Ismara]] at {{waypoint 87.68, 5.34, 307.82}}

## Rewards

- +1,000 faction with **Port Woe**
- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
