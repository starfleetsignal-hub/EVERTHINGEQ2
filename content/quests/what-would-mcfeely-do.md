## Steps

1. Speak to [[Parser Scrum McFeely]] in [[Gorowyn Beach Outpost]] next to [[Primary Sha'Ki]]. {{waypoint 2311, 37, 1014}}
1. Use the [[MF-47 "Wildfire"]], given to you by Parser Scrum McFeely, on 5 of the *Blackshield landing craft* on the beach north of Gorowyn Beach Outpost {{waypoint 2339, 0, 806}}.
1. Return to [[Primary Sha'Ki]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s 86c
