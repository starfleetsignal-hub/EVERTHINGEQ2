## Steps

1. Use the candle, [[Cierge of Solusek]], to summon the pet, Solusek's Reprisal. The "candle" is no-zone, but you can get a replacement by rehailing Spahzlyk
1. Recover 10 flame-glyph necklaces by killing [[A necrotic acolyte|necrotic acolytes]] and [[A necrotic initiate|necrotic initiates]].
1. Return to [[Spahztyk Hathookine]]

## Rewards

- At least 18g 39s 23c
- [[Flame-Flecked Orb]]
