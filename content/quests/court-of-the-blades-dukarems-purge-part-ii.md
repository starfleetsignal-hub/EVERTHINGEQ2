## Notes

- There was a bug involving a step in this quest that prevented its completion. If you encounter an issue regarding the caravan you're supposed to escort, see the talk page for help.

## Steps

1. Eavesdrop on Blades locations throughout [[Maj'Dul]]. Remain 10-15 seconds in each of the following areas to receive updates:
   - {{waypoint -87, 151, 128}}
   - {{waypoint -244, 135, -1}}
   - {{waypoint -142, 138, 150}}
   - {{waypoint -181, 150, -13}}
   - {{waypoint 148, 138, -23}}
   - {{waypoint 85, 148, -141}}
1. Talk to [[Ahzeem the Bartermaster]]. {{waypoint 65, 148, -144}}
1. Talk to [[Dukaris]] in [[The Court of the Blades]].
1. Return to Dukarem.
1. He orders you to find Hajid and his caravan. Go to [[The Pillars of Flame]] near [[T'Narev]] at {{waypoint 285, -141, -1416}}.
1. Escort Hajid along the road to [[Camp Char'Gin]]. Mobs will spawn along the route but will not appear until Hajid gets within a certain range - so do not get too far ahead. You can ignore regular mobs since they do not attack the caravan and you can still get the update at the end if a few camels die.
   - Mobs spawn at the following locations:
     - {{waypoint 394, -135, -1369}} - 4 bandits
     - {{waypoint 570, -126, -1158}} - 3 [[A Sandscrawler raider|Sandscrawler raiders]]
     - {{waypoint 779, -127, -1039}} - 3 mad disciples
     - {{waypoint 912, -129, -937}} - 3 hungry sandstalker sabertooths
     - {{waypoint 1004, -136, -1076}} - 4 [[A Windsister raider|Windsister raiders]]
   - Escort ends at {{waypoint 1133, -143, -1350}}
1. Return to Dukarem.
1. Return to the Pillars of Flame and go to the *Anvil of Ro*, which the djinn-infested pillar to the southwest. The book is at {{waypoint 1570, -97, -75}} and respawns after 1-minute.
1. Return to Dukarem.
