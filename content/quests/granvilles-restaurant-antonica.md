## Steps

1. Collect 15 shells of [[A large shore crab|large shore crabs]]. {{waypoint 1047, -20, 1089}}
1. Return to Tobias Granville.
1. Fish [[Fish Nodes#T3|a mob of fish]] (20).
1. Return to Tobias Granville.
1. Visit [[Shrine of the Sirens]] at {{waypoint 1481, -20, 239}}.
1. Return to Tobias Granville.
