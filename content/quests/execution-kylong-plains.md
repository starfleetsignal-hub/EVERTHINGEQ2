## Steps

1. Target a Sathirian near [[Varis]] and use the [[Hraen's Sticky Beacon]] on him/her. {{waypoint -503, 169, -167}}
   - You can use [[Thisk's Varis Beacon|Thisk's Varis Beacons]] to port yourself to and from Varis. If you port to Varis, you will land in a group of aggressive mobs.
1. Return to [[Hraen Vutha]] in [[Kunzar's Edge (POI)|Kunzar's Edge]]. {{waypoint -1373.73, 24.43, 516.27}}

## Rewards

- At least 14g 81s 38c
- +2000 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Emblem of the Drolvarg Warrior]]
  - [[Ring of the Drolvarg Warrior]] (All Mages)
  - [[Ringlet of the Drolvarg Warrior]]
  - [[Signet of the Drolvarg Warrior]] (All Priests)
