## Notes

All the [[A mosquito (Enchanted Naiad Grotto)|mosquitos]] in the grotto will respawn until the quest is finished and turned in.

   - -63.72, -14.85, 28.98

   - -42.53, -14.83, -24.93
   - -82.20, -26.02, -22.69
   - -101.21, -25.70, -4.28
   - -95.91, -25.87, 23.57

   - -89.95, -12.13, 36.25
