## Steps

1. Kill and gather the following
   - Kill 6 [[Fishing stonestalker]] or [[Shadowed stelecat]] on the [[Metetherial Plains]] near {{waypoint 511, 62, 291}}.
   - Kill 6 [[Plains ceffyl]] or [[Heights ceffyl]] on the [[Metetherial Plains]] near {{waypoint 719, 63, 474}}.
   - Gather 8 therial pollen spren on the [[Metetherial Plains]] near {{waypoint 266, 82, -18}} or [[The Stratafold]] {{waypoint 578.10, 43.49, 652.39}}
     - They are small white floating wisps.
     - They are not grouped or clustered and can be found anywhere in the area.
     - They do not have quest markers on them and they are hard to see on the light blue ground.
     - They are trackable (regular skill, not harvesting track) and targetable. But if you don't have the tracking facility you may easily see them from ground level looking to the horizon. Their shining whiteness is obvious.

![](images/Pollen1.png)
![](images/Pollen2.png)

1. Speak to [[Himaduri]] at {{waypoint -918, 34, 206}}
1. Kill the [[Rampaging stelecat]] that spawns
1. right-click to revive [[Himaduri]]
1. speak to her again.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Mite of Memoir]]
- +1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
