## Steps

1. During your rescue of [[Trelly Greenthorn|Trelly Greenthorn's]] brother, you were poisoned by the gnolls. Seek Trelly's help in curing the poison!
1. The cure makes you woozy, and you enter [[An Elddar Dream]].
   - Speak to [[A dreamlike figure|a dreamlike figure]].  She points behind you, indicating three bushes: the Rosemary of Growth, the Rosemary of Health, and the Rosemary of Lucidity.
     - Harvest the Rosemary of Growth.
     - If you harvest the other rosemary bushes, [[A gnoll nightmare]] will appear and attack you.
   - Speak again to [[A dreamlike figure|the dreamlike figure]].  She points behind you, indicating three basil plants: the Basil of Purity, the Basil of Spirit, and the Basil of Growth.
     - Harvest the Basil of Growth.
     - If you harvest the other basil plants, [[A gnoll nightmare]] will appear and attack you.
   - For a third time, to [[A dreamlike figure|the dreamlike figure]].  She points behind you once again, indicating three Sage Bushes: the Sage of Ages, the Sage of Growth, and the Sage of Time.
     - Harvest the Sage of Growth.
     - If you harvest the other sage bushes, [[A gnoll nightmare]] will appear and attack you.
   - Speak a final time to [[A dreamlike figure|the dreamlike figure]]. Open the door behind her to awaken from the dream.
1. Speak to [[Trelly Greenthorn]].

## Rewards

- At least 84s 37c (at level 36)
