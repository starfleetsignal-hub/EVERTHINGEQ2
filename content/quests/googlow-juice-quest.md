## Notes

The [[Recipe for Googlow Juice]] auto-scribes. You do *not* need to locate it in inventory and scribe it manually.

- Note: Player camera (or player) needs to be **<**27m from goocap for it to render.
- Goocap is trackable

## Steps

![Goocap Mushrooms for the Googlow Juice quest](images/Goosecap-mushroom.jpg)

1. Pick 10 fresh [[Goocap mushroom|goocap mushrooms]] - *found a bit further down the road along the wall, example: {{waypoint -385, 2, -1564}}; trackable, multi-harvest node*
1. Make 5 [[Googlow Juice (Quest Item)|Googlow Juice]] at the Goblin Firepit at [[Rumbletum]].  Each combine requires 1 [[Thaumic Coal]].
1. Speak with [[Rumbletum]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +3,000 faction with **Broken Toe Clan**
- +2,000 faction with **Goblins of Nurga**
