## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A crystal whisperer|crystal whisperers]] or [[A crystal tender|crystal tenders]] in [[The Crystal Grove]]. {{waypoint -663, -36, 256}}
1. Return to [[Merchant Vix]].

## Rewards

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- +5000 faction with **Riliss**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Exarch's Blessed Sacrament Plate Gloves]] (Clerics)
  - [[Executioner's Truesteel Gauntlets]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Illuminate's Still Water Hand Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Magus's Glowing Gossamer Gloves]] (All Mages)
  - [[Outrider's Blacksteel Chain Gloves]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Stormcaller's Spring Weave Leather Gloves]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Witchdoctor's Primal Wrath Handguards]] (Shamans)
