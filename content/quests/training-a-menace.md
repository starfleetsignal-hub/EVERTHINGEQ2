## Steps

1. Find the [[Sokokar Roost]]. {{waypoint 571, -76, 520}}
1. Destroy 6 *sokokar training supplies* (dark brown boxes).
1. Return to Speaker Rup.

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- +1,000 faction with **Synod Reet**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Dreamcrusher]]
  - [[Enslaver's Wand]] (All Mages)
  - [[Kunzar Native's Focus Wand]] (All Priests)
  - [[Pristine Swifttail Satchel]]
  - [[Runic Bow of the Jungle Ranger]]
