## Steps

1. Venture to [[The Thundering Steppes]].
1. Visit [[Coldwind Shores Cemetery]] at {{waypoint 1349, -2, -71}}, and receive an auto-update.
1. Return to Tanen for your reward.

## Notes

- Although the presence of two responses in the final conversation with Tanen give an impression that there will be a follow-up quest, sadly there is none.
- A probable member of Clan McMarrin is located on [[Dead River Docks]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]]: [[Kerath McMarrin]].

## Rewards

- At Least: 24s 71c
