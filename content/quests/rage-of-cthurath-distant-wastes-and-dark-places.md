## Steps

1. Speak to Lady Najena nearby {{waypoint 561, 94, -681}}
1. Investigate a possible connection between the Darou and the nightbloods by completing the quests:
   - [[Focus Elsewhere]]
   - [[Gnarls Gone Wild]]
   - [[Highland Anxiety]]
1. Speak to Tomian Keeneye in Darou Village at {{waypoint 435, 81, -578}}
1. Search for signs of nightblood and ebonblights near the Void obelisk in Writher Ruins at {{waypoint -640, 2, 424}} (Tip: if you've unlocked [[Kith (in Yon Gorroth)|Kith]] use him to teleport you)
1. Examine any of the items in the empty camp.
1. Click on the cocoon at {{waypoint -620, -1, 460}}. You will be knocked unconscious.
1. Speak with Tomian Keeneye next to you by Yon Gorroth Obelisk when you regain consciousness.
1. Search the [[Skur'rax Spires]] for the cocooned obelisk keyholder. This is a solidified cocoon at {{waypoint 429, 63, 500}} but you need a special blade to cut it open.
   - To complete this step you need to pick up and complete following sub-quests from [[Muurak]] standing by Yon Gorroth Obelisk at {{waypoint -602, 1, 460}}:
     - [[Wards With Friends]]
     - [[Skur'rax and Stones]]. This second quest rewards you with [[Refined Oblivionite Dagger]] needed to open the cocoon.
1. Cut open the solidified cocoon at [[Skur'rax Spires]] at {{waypoint 429, 63, 500}} and search the keyholder's corpse. (must have [[Refined Oblivionite Dagger]] from [[Skur'rax and Stones]] quest)
   - You receive [[Void Obelisk Key]].
1. Return to Lady Najena in the Darou Village at {{waypoint 561, 94, -681}}
