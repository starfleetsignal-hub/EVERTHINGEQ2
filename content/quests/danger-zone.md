## Steps

1. Kill the following creatures within [[The City of Mist]]: {{waypoint -224, 146, -913}}
   - 8 [[A Torsis phantom|Torsis phantom]]s
   - 8 [[A Torsis spectre|Torsis spectre]]s
   - 10 [[A spurbone skeleton|spurbone skeleton]]s
   - 5 [[A jade construct]]s
   - 5 [[An iron construct|iron construct]]s
1. Target and hail any 5 [[A Sathirian oathsworn sentry|Sathirian oathsworn sentries]], [[A Sathirian patroller|Sathirian patrollers]], or [[A Sathirian tracker|Sathirian trackers]]. This can be done while invisible. {{waypoint 710, 68, -370}}
1. Return to Ireka Nazan.
