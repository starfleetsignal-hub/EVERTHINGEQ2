## Prerequisites

- [[Access to Tower of the Four Winds]]

## Steps

1. After hailing Brother Pharad in the tower, zone into [[The Mediation Chamber]], a solo instance behind the tower {{waypoint 23, 33, -179}}, and speak with [[Brother Pharad (Mediation)|Brother Pharad]]
1. Mediate between the potenial allies. **You must select the correct responses to get the updates**
   - Speak with [[Master Luris (Mediation)|Master Luris]]. Responses:
     1. The fracture within your order may cause someone to question your purpose.
     1. You should not rely upon others to make up for the resolve you lack on your own.
     1. How can you hope to benefit the world when you need someone else to solve your internal conflicts.
     1. You can not hope to be worthy of the legacy of Zan Fi until you can mend that which has been broken within your order.
   - Speak with [[Sister Shoren (Mediation)|Sister Shoren]]. Responses:
     1. There is more to greatness than avoiding conflict.
     1. What has the Silent Fist Clan done to take a greater role in world events?
     1. To be truly worthy of this clan, your order needs to prove it can aid all of Norrath.
     1. You shouldn't need to join with them to fulfill your duty to Norrath. That should already be your goal.
   - Speak with [[Disciple Varlox (Mediation)|Disciple Varlox]]. Responses:
     1. A union with the Whistling Fists could diminish the purity of your order.
     1. Why would you risk the strength of your order by tainting it with these humans?
     1. Acquiring knowledge at the price of losing your identity does not seem a wise trade.
     1. The strength is already within you to accomplish what you seek.
1. Speak with Brother Pharad
