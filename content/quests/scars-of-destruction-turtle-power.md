## Steps

1. Speak with [[Armae Mistgazer]] who shows up next to [[Liwia]].
1. Speak with [[Wakuni]], high spirittalker at {{waypoint 87.55, 7.18, 400.37}}
1. Speak with [[Stissa]], Emissary of the Speaker, in [[The Sinking Sands]] {{waypoint -1502.57, -214.35, -386.93}}
1. Speak with [[Stissa]], Emissary of the Speaker again to embark to Fina's Retreat. This zones you into [[Great Divide: Fina's Retreat]] instance.
1. Speak with Osh at {{waypoint -1631, -535, -2427}}
1. Kill the following
   - Destroy 12 [[A risen gnoll|risen gnolls]]
   - 10 [[An undead ulthork (Great Divide: Fina's Retreat)|undead ulthorks]] attacking the village
   - the freeblood attacker [[Raelinn Darkmoore]] at {{waypoint -1387, -507, -2251}}
1. Speak with Osh.
1. Speak with Stissa at {{waypoint -1633, -530, -2399}}. *you will be sent to Western Wastes*
1. Speak with Elna Tsernin at {{waypoint 77, 7, 648}}.
