## Steps

1. Defeat [[Garanel Rucksif, the Cursed]] in [[The Estate of Unrest]]
1. Return to the Chronomagi Tasker

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Chronomancers**
- Ability to buy [[Replica: Garanel Rucksif]] from [[Timeless Trader]] for 1p 8g

## Notes

- **This quest cannot be solo'd** (regardless of level), due to specific mechanics within [[The Estate of Unrest]] requiring separate but simultaneous player interactions to access any of the dungeon floors beyond the first.  Fighting the boss can be done solo; accessing the boss cannot.
