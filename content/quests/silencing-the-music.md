## Description

The League of Freethinkers have learned of a most unusual sort of security device being employed by the agents of Mayong Mistmoore. The device is a music box which, when activated, spews forth a melancholic song that prevents even the most powerful of our soothsayers from investigating the affairs that transpire within Mistmyr Manor. The Freethinkers need me to find these music boxes and shut them down immediately! To ensure that no more of these music boxes are created I must also kill the Phantom of Symphonia.

## Steps

   - Kill the [[Phantom of Symphonia]] in [[Mistmyr Manor]] {{waypoint 175, 10, -190}}
   - Switch off all 6 of the music boxes within Mistmyr Manor:
     - Vestibule of the Regent {{waypoint 270, 22, -180}}
     - Parlor of Quiescence {{waypoint 207, 29, -264}}
     - Galleria of Remembrance {{waypoint 39, 23, -79}}
     - Southern upper room in the library {{waypoint 88, 37, -159}}
     - Exalted Refectory {{waypoint 33, 25, -347}}
     - Escritoire of Potentate {{waypoint 29, 26, -400}}
1. Return to Tanya in Loping Plains
