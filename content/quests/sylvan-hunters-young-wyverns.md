## Steps

This is a Kelethin city task for The House of Falling Stars.  House of Falling Stars have urged you to eliminate [[Young Wyverns]] in the [[Kylong Plains]].  Completeing this taks will earn you some favor within the organization.

- Kill 13 [[Young Wyverns]] in the [[Kylong Plains]].  Located at {{waypoint -138.91, 43.85, -448.47}}

## Rewards

- 13570 status
- +500 faction with **House of Falling Stars**
