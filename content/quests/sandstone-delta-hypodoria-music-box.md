## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key by killing 15 [[A sandscorned prowler|sandscorned prowlers]] around   {{waypoint -407, 6, -85}}  [[Sandstone Delta]] at [[Sandscoured Coast]].
1. I need to find the Hypodoria Music Box Key in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]] from [[A vengeful ekimmu|vengeful ekimmus]]  {{waypoint 483.38, 29.23, -226.67}} at [[Mordant Spires]].
1. Examine the clue for [[The Hypodoria Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the Hypodoria Music Box in [[Sandstone Delta]].
