## Notes

- If your adventure level is below 50 or above (100+), [[Captain Ingrid]] will not have the typical feather icon above her head to indicate that she offers a quest, but you can still hail her to get her quest.
  - This requires a tradeskill level of minimum 50
- This is one of three quests that must be complete before you can do the weekly unlimited, repeatable quest, [[Far Seas Supply Division - Rescuing the Relics]].
- Completion of this quest opens a tradeskill class-specific quest:
  - **[[Alchemist]]**: [[Spark's Aflame]]
  - **[[Armorer]]**: [[The Honor in Armor]]
  - **[[Carpenter]]** : [[Death's Table]]
  - **[[Jeweler]]**: [[The Jeweler's Mystery]]
  - **[[Provisioner]]**: [[Providing for an Exotic Feast]]
  - **[[Sage]]**: [[The Legendary Paper]]
  - **[[Tailor]]**: [[The Villagers' New Clothes]]
  - **[[Weaponsmith]]**: [[The Silken Sword]]
  - **[[Woodworker]]**: [[Kim's Totem Class]]

## Steps

1. You must speak to the leaders of Houses in order:
   1. Speak to the leader of the House of Nurwin, [[Zhen-hao Nurwin]]. {{waypoint -164, 7, -185}}.
   1. Speak to the leader of the House of Augren, [[Xin-xu Augren]]. She wanders near {{waypoint 47, 8, -37}}.
   1. Speak to the leader of the House of Ventur, [[Tumao Ventur]]. {{waypoint 17, 14, -63}}
1. Return to [[Captain Ingrid]] to claim your reward.

## Reward

- [[An unfinished Wantia armoire]]
- +200 faction with **The Far Seas Trading Company**
