## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- [[Vasty Deep: The Conservatory]] chrono dungeon is a group dungeon.

## Steps

The avatar summoning rituals require the manipulation of immense power and are inherently dangerous. To reduce the chance of failure and provide an anchor for that power, the herald of Tarew Marr has asked me to collect an item that may have once belonged to that god or one of their most loyal followers.

1. Collect [[Reliquary of Tarew Marr]] from a powerful creature in [[Vasty Deep: The Conservatory]] chrono dungeon
1. Return to [[Herald of the Fathom Lord]] {{waypoint -275.14, -0.13, 51.21}} in [[The Mystic Lake]]
