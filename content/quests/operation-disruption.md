## Steps

1. Spoil the distillation:
   - Kill [[Grogmaga]] to loot [[Grogmaga's key]].
   - Use the [[Grogmaga's key|key]] to open the chest at the foot of Grogmaga's bed, to loot [[Grogmaga's Fragrance]].
   - Go to the [[Upper Distillery]] and click on the seals of the six copper stills to add the perfume to the rum.
1. Jam the water pump:  ![](images/Operationn_Disruption_quest_-_a_broken_pipe.jpg)
   - There's [[A broken pipe (perfect for jamming the water pump nearby)|a broken pipe]] at {{waypoint 123, -124, 161}} in [[The Fermentation Field]]; take it to the Pumping Station and use it (click on the blue hologram) to jam the big water wheel.
1. Kill the last sugar cane press mechanic: [[Yipnik]] at {{waypoint 1, -100, -2}}
1. Destroy the rum master's book of recipes: {{waypoint -53.17, -97.44, 81.89}}

## Rewards

- At least 2p 16g 20000 status
- 5 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
