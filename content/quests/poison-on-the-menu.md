## Steps

1. Kill 2 [[A poison slimfish|poison slimfish]] in the water near the Thurgardin Harbor docks {{waypoint 1321, -551, 483}}
1. Click any two of the crates in the dining hall in Thurgadin Harbor {{waypoint 1234, -475, 639}}
1. Return to Feyda

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- +800 faction with **The Order of Rime**
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Fedya's Idol of Kraytoc]] (Fighter)
  - [[Fedya's Elemental Rime Runestone]] (Mage)
  - [[Fedya's Lucky Coldain Toe]] (Priest)
  - [[Fedya's Ticking Trinket]] (Scout)
