## Steps

1. Kill the following mobs in the [[Lake of Dismay]] or surrounding streams: {{waypoint 585, 63, -496}}
   - 5 [[A venom flow leech|venom flow leeches]]. These mobs can be pulled out of the water
   - 5 [[A kunzar scaleripper|kunzar scaleripper]]s. These mobs have a very wide agro radius and it's easy to get overwhelmed, so stand near the water's edge to pull them.
1. Return to Yarvis Urx.

## Rewards

- At least 13g 31s 29c
- +10,000 faction with **Kunzar Jungle Villagers**
- [[Bonedigger Ring of Excellence]]
