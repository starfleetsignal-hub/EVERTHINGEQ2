## Notes

- You must use the [[Crusaders of Greenmist Disguise]] (from the [[Scrying Eyes]] quest) to speak to Selsik to start the quest, even if you have sufficient faction to be non-hostile.  Make sure the "Hide my Illusion Form" box is UNCHECKED on Options page of Character window.

## Steps

1. Equip [[Crusaders of Greenmist Disguise]]
1. Right click and "Use" it from your character page to complete the disguise.
1. Speak to [[Trooper Selisk]] {{waypoint 735, 224, 896}}.  He tells you to speak to [[Trooper Sokszar]] {{waypoint 633, 247, 1243}}

## Rewards

- +5000 faction with **Crusaders of Greenmist**
- 1p 53g 9s at level 100.
