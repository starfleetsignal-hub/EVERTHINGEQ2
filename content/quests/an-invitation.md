## Steps

1. Meet the informant in [[Longshadow Alley: Daeneth Cloister|Longshadow Alley]]
   1. Speak to [[Kora N'Vaxin]] at {{waypoint 12, 3, 51}}.
1. Speak to [[Ryltrin V'Zival]] at {{waypoint 24, 0, -15}}
1. Speak to [[Miral K'Thaoth]] at {{waypoint -32, 1, 7}}
   1. After speaking to Miral, you are given the choice of letting her live or attacking her.  If you attack her, she will leave behind a small chest containing [[A Speech Announcement]]  If you choose to let her live, she will hand you the announcement.
1. Return to [[Ambassador Anttel V'Xeath|Ambassador Anttel]] at {{waypoint -123, -38, 23}}

## Rewards

- At level 24 24s 75c
