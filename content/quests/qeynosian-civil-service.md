This quest is repeatable up to 5 times.

## Steps

1. Slay 10 [[A moat rat|moat rats]]. {{waypoint 61, -17, 91}}
1. Slay 10 [[A lowland viper|lowland vipers]]. {{waypoint 350, -22, 618}}
1. Slay 10 beetles ([[A klicnik mite]], [[A klicnik warrior]], [[A klicnik worker]]). {{waypoint -122, -17, 82}}
1. Return to [[Knight-Lieutenant Alesso]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c - 1st completion
- At least 3s 2c - 2nd completion
- At least 5s 12c - 3rd completion
- At least 9s 33c - 4th completion
- At least 13s 54c - 5th completion
