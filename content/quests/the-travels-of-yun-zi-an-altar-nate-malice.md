## Notes

- Check your inventory for [[Malvonicus' Haste]], or buy one from [[T'Kaela]] {{waypoint 1226.00, 18, 1264}}, the leveling merchant in Tranquil Sea, for a boost to your flight speed in these zones.

## Steps

1. Visit the following locations:
   1. Isle of Refuge {{waypoint 1002, 3, 1409}} in [[Tranquil Sea]]
   1. Death Weave {{waypoint 894, 7, -440}} in [[Tranquil Sea]]
   1. Bawgava, South Dshinn {{waypoint -624, 12, 275}} in [[Tranquil Sea]]
   1. North Dshinn {{waypoint -1134, 28, 1374}} in [[Phantom Sea]]. You may get to the Phantom sea by continuing to fly north until you zone.
   1. Wanderer's Dock, Kithicor {{waypoint 1270, 23, 1190}} in [[Phantom Sea]]
   1. Peril Point, Grim Shales {{waypoint -231, 41, -933}} in [[Phantom Sea]]
1. Return to [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]].
