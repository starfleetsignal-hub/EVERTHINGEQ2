*This was the former access quest for [[Lair of the Necromancer]], but its completion is no longer required to enter the instance.*

## Steps

1. Slay some Thexians. They are found in [[Hidden Gorge]], by the gate to [[Darklight Wood]]. {{waypoint -1360, 0, -1360}}
   - 1 [[A Thexian ruffian|Thexian ruffian]]
   - 1 [[Thexian scout]]
   - 1 [[Thexian Vin guard]]
   - 2 [[A Thexian Vin chosen|Thexian Vin chosen]]
1. Slay [[Kixizor Z'Nast]], who wanders around the same area.
1. Return to [[Vanquisher Sharhan]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 29s 7c
