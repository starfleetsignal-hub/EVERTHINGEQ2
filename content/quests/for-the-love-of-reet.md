## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
  - While this quest can technically be completed an infinite amount of times, it will stop being offered once your faction passes -6900 with Synod Reet. Should your faction ever dip lower than that in the future, this quest will become available again.

## Steps

1. Kill any 10 Sathirians in [[Kunzar Jungle]]. The closest to the quest giver are the [[A Sathirian milite|Sathirian milites]]. {{waypoint 432, -112, 707}}
1. Return to Assistant Pubbit.

## Rewards

- At least 8g 11s 18c
- +35000 faction with **Synod Reet** on the first completion.
- +1000 faction with **Synod Reet** every completion after the first.
