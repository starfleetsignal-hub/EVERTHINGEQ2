## Requirements

- You must be at least level 55 to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Slay 12 droags in the [[Tenebrous Tangle]] for cogs. Any droag will give this update.
1. Return to [[Bilgeron Cogsworth IV|Bilgeron]].

## Rewards

- [[Flickering Gem of Mental Fortitude]]
- [[Droag Hide Tapestry]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Rugged Ornamental Spear]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Spectral Orb of Conjuration]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sea Serpent's Orb of Control]]
  - [[Snake Headed Token]]
