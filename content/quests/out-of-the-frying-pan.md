## Notes

- Go to the [[North Wing Stacks]] on the ground floor of the Library, then take the teleporter at {{waypoint 258, -20, -289}} to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]] and enter [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]] through the portal at {{waypoint 728, 412, -336}}.

## Steps

1. Obtain the following from [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]]
   - Steal 8 **Scrolls of the Corliac Temple**.
     - Found in the small tower structures with flaming tops. Possible locations include:
       - {{waypoint -78, 78, 419}}   ![A Corliac Temple Scroll](images/Out_of_the_Frying_Pan..._quest_-_scroll.jpg)
       - {{waypoint -236, 88, 396}}
       - {{waypoint -442, 59, 286}}
       - {{waypoint -362, 45, 388}}
       - {{waypoint -366, 42, 111}}
       - {{waypoint -174, 6, -107}}
       - {{waypoint -171, 21, -419}}
       - {{waypoint 91, -1, -155}}
       - {{waypoint 135, 12, -401}}
   - Obtain 16 jopal wings by hunting jopal.
1. Examine the [[Arcane Tome Paper]] in your inventory and choose **Concentrate on the paper**.
1. Speak with [[The Unbound Maladjusted Codex]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Doomfire Seal]] - *collection item*
