Tyshgwyn the Fury explained to me that Lasslyn had purposely sent me to her, in order for her to put me and my skills to better use! Tyshgwyn wants me to accompany her as she takes the fight to the Aberrants out in the fields of Blightstone Barrens and Penumbra Expansion.

## Steps

1. Follow [[Tyshgwyn the Fury|Tyshgwyn]]. She will vanish after a while on the road, but you'll find her battling Aberrants in [[Blightstone Barrens]] {{waypoint 667.36, 65.29, -336.85}}.
1. Kill 16 [[An Aberrant|aberrants]] in [[Blightstone Barrens]] and [[Penumbra Expansion]].
1. Return to [[Tyshgwyn the Fury|Tyshgwyn]] in [[Blightstone Barrens]].
1. Free [[Tyshgwyn the Fury|Tyshgwyn]] of her enchantment by engaging her in combat until she becomes unconscious.
1. Talk to [[Tyshgwyn the Fury|Tyshgwyn]].
