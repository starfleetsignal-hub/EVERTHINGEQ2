## Steps

1. Walk over to [[The Jade Tiger's Den]].
1. Use the [[Call of the Kerran Terror]] scroll that [[Azreana]] gave you near [[Shungi]], who is standing around near the Mailbox outside the inn.
1. Return to Azreana after Shungi defeats the skeleton.
1. Travel to [[Stonestair Byway: The Animus Corridors]] and use the scroll she gave you near the Fountain of Anguish {{waypoint 32, -4, -49}} .
1. Next move up the stairs towards {{waypoint 8.56, 0.00, -29.78}} and use the scroll again on the Fighting Platform.
1. Next move ahead, down the stairs, to the main Kerran road, and use the scroll again.
1. Next go into the house on the south side of the road {{waypoint -49, 0.27, -3}} and use the scroll once more.
1. Finally, use the scroll one last time on the docks {{waypoint -76, -4, -26}}.
1. Return to [[Azreana]].
