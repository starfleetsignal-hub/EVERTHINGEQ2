## Steps

1. Enter the [[City of Shar Vahl]].
1. Speak with [[Scout Kiira]] in Shar Vhal -110.41 29.85 -273.26 167.68 0.00 0.00.
   1. Obtain the blessing of [[The Dar Khura]]...
      - *requires completion subquest-serie started at [[Khazla Aradir]] at {{waypoint 4, 9, -215}}:
        1. [[Aquatic Ailments Encountered]]
        1. [[Song of Healing]]
   1. ...and obtain the support of [[The Jharin]]. (-91, 30, -391)
      - *Requires completion of [[Vahl That Remains]], started at [[Dahnji Hujara]] {{waypoint -275, 35, 130}}*
1. Return to [[Scout Kiira]] in Shar Vhal and speak to her again.
1. Click the palace door {{waypoint -48.21, 94.88, 342.30}} to enter [[Kerrath Palace: Royal Audience]].
1. Speak with [[King Bhusla Savarah]] and other pride elders.
   - NOTE: Zoom the camera out so that all NPCs are on screen at the same time to complete the dialogue.
1. Leave the palace and speak to [[Hymnist Wadakha]] at [[The Jharin Assemblage]] {{waypoint -84.25, 31.12, -410.88}}.
1. Continue the conversation with [[Hymnist Wadakha]] in the [[Secret Scriptorium]].
1. Exit the [[Secret Scriptorium]], then obtain a [[Sahteb Mahlni Gem Fetish]] from [[Sakhar Niridri]] in the [[Keep of The Khala Dun]] {{waypoint -286.97, 46.88, -310.51}}.
1. Return to [[Hymnist Wadakha]] at [[The Jharin Assemblage]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c 56,000 status
- [[Shar Vahl Acrylia Crate]]
