## Steps

You will have to kill goblins of a specific type inside the [[The Condemned Catacomb]]. The type of goblin you need to kill will be listed in your [[Quest Journal]], but will be selected randomly.

1. Read the book.
1. Kill 5 [[A Necrosis shrew (Condemned Catacomb)|Necrosis shrews]], [[A Necrosis conscript (Condemned Catacomb)|Necrosis conscripts]], or [[A Necrosis auger (Condemned Catacomb)|Necrosis augers]].
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 5 [[A Necrosis thaumaturge|Necrosis thaumaturges]], [[A Necrosis guard|Necrosis guards]], or [[A Necrosis crone|Necrosis crones]].
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 5 [[A Necrosis beldame|Necrosis beldames]], [[A Necrosis elite guard|Necrosis elite guards]], or [[A Necrosis seer|Necrosis seers]].
1. Read the book.
