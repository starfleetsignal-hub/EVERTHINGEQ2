## Steps

1. Speak to [[Shio Laugerwithe]] on the tree platform {{waypoint 727, -143, 248}}
1. Make the komodo egg warmers (5)
   - Scribe the [[Recipe: Komodo Egg Warmer]]
   - Craft 5 [[Komodo Egg Warmer]] on a [[Woodworking Table]] (on the same platform as Shio) with the following materials and fuel:
     - 5 [[Horned leather pelt]]
     - 10 [[Rough lumbered rosewood]]
     - 5 [[Scintillating sandpaper]]
     - Note: if you're already a 125 level crafter with Blueprint ability - after you make the first one, you can use the Blueprint feature to insta-complete the remaining four.
1. Collect the wind komodo eggs (5)
1. Return to Shio

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- At least 2g 16s 26c
