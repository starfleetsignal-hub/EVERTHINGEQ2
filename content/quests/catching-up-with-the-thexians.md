## Steps

1. Gather [[A Thexian field ledger]] in [[The Forward Post]] {{waypoint -97, -17, 5}}.  The ledger is a clickable page lying on a table inside the tent.  It is guarded by [[High Priestess Y`Lask]], 57^.
1. Return to [[Sharina Nybright]]

## Rewards

- +100 faction with **Nybright Family**
- At least 9g 13s 1c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Archaic Spiritualist's Coif]] (Shamans)
  - [[Boundless Naturewalker's Cap]] (Druids)
  - [[Defiant Vagabond's Chain Helmet]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Flowing Sensei's Skullcap]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Gallant Dragoon's Helmet]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Resplendent Thaumaturge's Cowl]] (All Mages)
  - [[Reverent Curate's Plate Barbute]] (Clerics)
