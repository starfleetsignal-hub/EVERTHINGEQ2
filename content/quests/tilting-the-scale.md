## Steps

1. Slay 8 [[A harnessed Spur stalker]]s around {{waypoint -225, 11, -1}}
1. Return to Modo Gillywump

- At least 90c
- One of the following (based on class):

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Modo Gillywump: "Hello there! I'm Modo. Welcome to Sapling Spur Outpost!"
- Player Character: "I'm [Player Character]."
- Modo Gillywump: "Well, a pleasure to meet ya. I'd like to wish you a pleasant stay, but the orc pawns off to the east are making absolutely certain that it won't be."
- Player Character: "What do you mean?"
- Modo Gillywump: "The orc pawns have tamed the local wildlife: the Spur stalkers. No, tame is not the right word--the stalkers are wild as ever--the orcs have harnessed them."
- Player Character: "What are the orcs doing with the Spur stalkers?"
- Modo Gillywump: "They're using the wolves to protect their new outpost and to harry travelers. You know, time was that these creatures were merely a nuisance. Now they're threatening our very existence. I think it may be time to lessen their numbers. Would you be willing to help?"
- Player Character: "I'd be happy to help."
- Modo Gillywump: "Good to hear! Head to the east and look for the stalkers around the outpost. And... well... I'd feel bad if we just left 'em to the forest after you kill 'em. One or two is fine for the forest to handle, but we should clean up the mess we make. Besides, there's no sense wasting their bodies!"
- Player Character: "Sounds good."
- Modo Gillywump: "There's a lot I could learn from studying them. Please, bring the bodies back with you when you're done."
- Player Character: "All right."

I need to put down harnessed Spur stalkers.

I have slain the Spur stalkers. I need to bring these Spur stalker bodies back to Modo at the Sapling Spur Outpost.

- Modo Gillywump: "Do you have the carcasses?"
- Player Character: "Yes, here you go."
- Modo Gillywump: "Ah, good work. Good work. These will--eugh, this fellow doesn't look too happy."
- Player Character: "What do you mean?"
- Modo Gillywump: "His teeth are discolored. I wonder what he's been eating? Let me just..."
- Player Character: [wait]
- Modo Gillywump: "There we go... hmmm... well, I can't be positive, but I think this is deer meat. Was the deer sick, I wonder?"
- Player Character: "I'm not sure."
- Modo Gillywump: "This seems like it's something that would interest my brother, Brodo. If you see him, maybe you could lend him a hand."
- Player Character: "I'll keep that in mind."

I have given Modo the Spur stalker bodies.

I have given Modo the harnessed Spur stalker bodies.
