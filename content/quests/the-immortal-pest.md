## Steps

1. Enter the [[Forgotten Pools]] zone at {{waypoint 374, -337, 1108}}
   - [[Bergna Veliumsmitter]] at the zone in will offer optional quests within:
     - [[Hold of Rime: Forgotten Pools - To Honor the Dead]] (Heroic)
     - [[Hold of Rime: Fortress Spire - Spying Spiders]] (Heroic)
     - [[Hold of Rime: The Ascent - Threat Assessment]] (Heroic)
1. Kill [[Aeri'ondi the Infectious]] who will appear when you approach {{waypoint -32, 10, -47}}
1. Speak with [[Terig the Lost]] {{waypoint -46.53, 15.93, -340.71}} in the [[Forgotten Pools]]
1. Kill all the Ragegrove Keepers in the [[The Shapery]] then kill [[Styrax Fluorescene]]
1. Kill [[Gerg Teae]] at {{waypoint 88.52, 67.69, -520.83}}
   - Pick up a speckled egg to receive the optional [[Egg-cellent]] quest
1. Ride the nesting ice gryphon
1. Journey to the top of the [[Forgotten Pools]] and defeat [[Lord Doljonijiarnimorinar]].
1. Return on the nesting ice gryphon.
1. Attack walls to clear path
1. Within 20 minutes place Lord Doljonijiarnimorinar's Essence in the Mausoleum of the Lost Ones {{waypoint -46.53, 15.93, -340.71}} in the [[Forgotten Pools]].
1. Speak with [[Terig the Lost]] in the [[Forgotten Pools]].
1. Bring Veeshan's Claw Tip to [[Viglundr Doomforge]] {{waypoint 516.96, -357.46, 827.91}} at the [[Storm Gorge Staging Camp]].

## Rewards

- At least 20g 31s 56c
- All of the following:
  - [[Frozen Adornment of Raw Power]]
  - [[Frosted Adornment of Critical Chance]]
  - [[Frozen Adornment of Increased Criticals]]
  - [[Icy Adornment of Heightened Power]]
  - [[Icy Adornment of Parrying]]
  - [[Shrine of Prexus]]

- Grants Access to [[Hold of Rime: The Ascent]]

(Following Quest Grants Access to [[Hold of Rime: The Fortress Spire]] *Needed for Beastlord Epic)
