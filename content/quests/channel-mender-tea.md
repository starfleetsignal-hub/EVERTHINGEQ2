This quest is repeatable. as long as you can get the starter.

## Description

After a bout with one of the crazed channel menders I recovered some tea leaves he had been carrying. The leaves had a complex, flowery aroma and a lively flavor. I've decided to acquire more of these leaves for my personal use.

## Steps

- Kill [[A crazed channel mender|crazed channel menders]] {{waypoint -25, 1, -75}} for five auto-updates.

## Rewards

- [[Oro brew]]
