## Steps

1. Kill  iksar found all over Warslik's Woods {{waypoint 270, -218, -395}} and the shore for the following updates (random drops):
   1. Collect a Vrassath Chest Plate From a Vrassath Iksar
   1. Collect a Vrassath Pauldron From a Vrassath Iksar
   1. Collect a Vrassath Gauntlets From a Vrassath Iksar
   1. Collect a Vrassath Leggings From a Vrassath Iksar
   1. Collect a Vrassath Boots Plate From a Vrassath Iksar
   1. Collect a Vrassath Helm Plate From a Vrassath Iksar
1. Return to Krellsis in the Crusader's Cave
1. Click the chest next to him for the **Vrassath Guard Suit**. Awards a charm to your inventory that when used applies an Iksar illusion and adjusts the faction for Vrassath to +40k.
