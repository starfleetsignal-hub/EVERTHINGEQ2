## Steps

1. Go to the far northeastern corner of the zone and examine a mound of dirt underneath a boulder. 1080..61 178.83 -2971.05
1. Kill the [[A Di'Zok underhand|Di'Zok underhand]] that spawns.
   - If in a group, each person should inspect the dirt pile to spawn the Di'Zok underhand.
1. Return to [[Germa Prash]] in [[Highton]]. {{waypoint 1275, 159, -2074}}
