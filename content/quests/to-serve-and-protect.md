## Notes

- You must understand the [[Orcish]] language
- This is a repeatable quest.
- You can delete & repeat the quest to get closer mobs like the Draconie Guardians)

## Steps

1. One the following **depending on your quest**
   - 6 sabertooths in Dragon's Head Drifts for their pelts
   - 5 frostpaw direbears in Dragon's Head Drifts for their pelts
   - 6 mammoths for meat
   - 7 Draconine Guardians
   - 7 Sinister Sentinels (found NE of the camp)
1. Return to Korgak Rotstomper

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +500 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
