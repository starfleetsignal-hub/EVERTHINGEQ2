Gelik Thwaxby, a gnemlin within Maldura's Alcove of Reason, knows a great deal of mechanamagica. He has recruited me to recharge the arcane defense pillars within Stoneheart Yards. They helped defend the city from the Aberrants' arcane attacks.

## Steps

![an arcane defense pillar](images/Arcane_defense_pillars.png)

1. Recharge 5 an arcane defense pillars in the [[Stoneheart Yards]] in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]]. These are *"obelisk-shaped armatures of gnomish design, as tall as a Teir'Dal, complete with spinning gears and a glowing umbrite focus gem".* Possible locations:
   - {{waypoint 656.65, 70.92, -99.33}}
   - {{waypoint 566, 81, -54}}
   - {{waypoint 646, 81, 30}}
   - {{waypoint 616, 68, -139}}
   - {{waypoint 539, 67, -133}}
1. Click each pillar.
1. Kill spawning mobs at each pillar.  Mobs have a high damage trauma AoE DoT.
1. Return to [[Gelik Thwaxby]]

- At least 1p 41g 75s
