- *See [[Aether Racing]] for more information.*

## Notes

- You must dismount before stepping on the pad to start the race.
- While racing: pass through lit green hoops and aim to fly through the blue and green spots floating in the air to increase your speed, but dodge the spots with red lightning around them, because they will slow you down.
- There is no level limit for flight on the Unstable version of the mounts, making it a good flight solution for characters under the 85 adventure or tradeskill level required for other mounts.
- If you click the big machine next to the quest-giver, you can see race results.

## Steps

1. Speak to the [[Aether Racing Referee]] located on the docks at {{waypoint 351, -9, 811}}.
1. Step on the the teleport pad to start
1. Complete the course in the designated time period
1. Return to [[Aether Racing Referee]]

## Rewards

**The rewards for completing the races varies depending on the number of times it is completed.**

- The rewards build when you repeat the races in the same zone.

- Title: [[Test Pilot]]

One of the following per time completed:

- [[Unstable Red C.R.A.S.H. Pad]]
- [[Unstable Dark C.R.A.S.H. Pad]]
- [[Unstable Blue C.R.A.S.H. Pad]]
