## Steps

1. Get the crabs from the crab traps. {{waypoint 54, -243, -908}} {{waypoint 58, -243, -909}} {{waypoint 61, -243, -909}}
   - You can click each trap individually or can use the same trap more than once. The crabs will appear in your inventory and you will click them to use them.
1. Use the crabs to catch 6 crawlers.
   - ![Crab-caught Crawlers](images/Crab-caught_crawlers.jpg) quest helper button to **Place Crab** on the blue-ish dirt near {{waypoint 74, -241, -884}} and wait for the crawlers (small worms) to surface. Click on crawler(s) to catch.
1. Bring the crawlers to [[Kreeg (Goblin)|Kreeg]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- +2500 faction with **Bloody Tooth Clan**
- 5 [[Thaumic Coal]]
