## Steps

[[The Great Morsley]] believes that the best way to breakup Brokenskull should be to begin a war between the Ykeshans and the Brokenskull.

1. Speak with [[Captain Lip Marrsquire]] {{waypoint 115, -88, -500}} about the Grozmok Stone. Lip suggests assisting Gurlig in the efforts to obtain the Stone.
   - Complete [[Gurlig Valorheart]]'s  {{waypoint 118, -88, -475}} sub-quests in order to retrieve the stone.
     - You must complete [[Heroes Honored]] to progress
     - You must complete [[Legacy of the Curse]] to progress
     - You must complete [[The Stone of Legend]] to progress
1. After you finish assisting Gurlig, return to [[Captain Lip Marrsquire]]. He will send you to retrieve the stone from the tent next to Gurlig at {{waypoint 116, -88, -479}} (clickable rock near npc)
1. Kill [[Captain Wavesmasher]] at the top of [[Brokenskull Rock]] {{waypoint -1762, -966, 2642}}
1. Complete these tasks in any order:
   - Place the Grozmok Stone you collected on the platform right next to the Captain you just killed. (Use it from your inventory)
   - Destroy the furthest west Catapult. {{waypoint -1554, -1007, 2739}}
   - Destroy the furthest east Catapult. {{waypoint -1762, -1019, 2488}}
   - Destroy the Catapult at the top of the rocks. {{waypoint -1737, -975, 2558}}
   - Destroy the Catapult at the bottom of the rocks. {{waypoint -1879, -1094, 2729}}
1. Return to [[Professor Fondfate]] at [[Firmroot Moot]]
   - Note that [[Professor Fondfate]] will immediately offer you the Chapter 7 quest, which is timed, and the timer starts as soon as you accept the quest.  If you accept it and are not ready to start, you can delete it or just let the timer run out.
