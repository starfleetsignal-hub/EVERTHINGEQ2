## Note

*make sure that you only have your brokenskull disguise  up, otherwise you can't get the update from Scabby.  If you die during the delivery down to the shanty, you will need to re-cast your disguise to get the quest update.*

## Steps

1. Deliver the eggs to [[Deckhand Vixil]] in Pirate Town ([[Gunthak's Shanty]]) {{waypoint -1343, -937, 540}}
1. Return to [[Scabby Gabby]]

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
