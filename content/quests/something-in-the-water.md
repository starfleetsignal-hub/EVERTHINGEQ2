## Steps

1. Take the balloon to Brokenskull Rock then swim to any of the void-touched saltshells or snappers and target them for a update. (one location at {{waypoint -1766, -1090, 2539}})
   - Kill 6 [[A void-touched saltshell|void-touched saltshells]]
   - Kill 6 [[A void-touched snapper|void-touched snappers]]
1. Return to Klip Marrsquire.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +1200 faction with **Survival Accord**
- Guktan Vase (House Item - Rent Status Reduction 250)
