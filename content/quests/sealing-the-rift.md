## Steps

1. Seal one of the Sablevaien rifts by kicking one of the stones that are in the crater's outer-ring. {{waypoint -389.07, -60.03, 290.53}}
1. Return to [[Pellandra D'Xatyl]].

## Rewards

- At least 24c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Bodyguard's Gloves]] (Bruiser, Monk)
  - [[Dark Pathfinder's Gloves]] (Ranger)
  - [[Evoker's Gloves]] (All Mages)
  - [[Marshall's Gauntlets]] (Paladin, Shadowknight, Warrior)
  - [[Pathfinder's Gloves]] (Bard, Rogue, Beastlord, Assassin)
  - [[Spiritualist's Gloves]] (Defiler, Mystic)
  - [[Vicar's Gauntlets]] (Inquisitor, Templar)
  - [[Windcaller's Gloves]] (Fury, Warden)
