## Steps

1. Obtain the names of the following people in [[Neriak, City of Hate]]:
   - [[Katenka]] {{waypoint -136, -4, 259}} - under the stairs near the Broker/Tradeskilling area.
   - [[Myrvyll T'Verret]] {{waypoint -473, 38, 119}} - second level of the "Hall of the Ebon Mask", outside on the porch overlooking the tunnel into the area
   - [[House Z'Xiett slave]] {{waypoint -558, 21, 191}} - outside of "The Bauble" in Cristanos Plaza.
   - [[House V'Freervs slave]] {{waypoint -666, 20, 180}} - on the path to the K'Lorn Library past Cristanos Plaza.
   - [[Xunaun H'Texiate]] {{waypoint -545, 28, -197}} - behind Darklight Palace.
   - [[Lathiss Dektate]] {{waypoint 121, 22, 147}} - near cheaper houses/first broker/stable area.
1. Return to G'shugaplum Gigglegibber. Make sure to have six open slots in your inventory!
1. Hand out the gifts:
   - **WARNING: You must give the correct gift to the correct person, or you will have to start the entire quest over!**
   - Katenka: [[Velvet painting of Queen Cristanos]] {{waypoint -136, -4, 259}}
   - Myrvyll T'Verret: [[Rallosian war club]] {{waypoint -473, 38, 119}}
   - House Z'Xiett Slave: [[Stinky cheese]] {{waypoint -558, 21, 191}}
   - House V'Freervs Slave: [[Wingclipper 2000]] {{waypoint -666, 20, 180}}
   - Xunaun H'Texiate: [[Backfiring wand]] {{waypoint -545, 28, -197}}
   - Lathiss Dektate: [[Teir'Dal Cloak of Flame]] {{waypoint 121, 22, 147}}
1. Return to G'Shugahplum Gigglegibber

## Rewards

- Lighting options for your [[Frostfell Tree]].
