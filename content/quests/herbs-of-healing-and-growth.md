## Description

"The spirit blooms were more damaged than the Matron expected. I've volunteered to gather the faeicia herbs she needs to heal the spirit blooms." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

![a faecia herb](images/A_faeicia_herb.jpg)

1. Harvest 4 herbs (a faeicia leaf herb) for the Matron. These are found to the west of [[The Nursery]] . Around (-75.61, -34.65, -826.22)
1. Return to the [[Matron of the Nursery]] for your reward.

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Matron of the Nursery: "Ah, wonderful! We will need to begin healing the flowers as soon as possible, then. I'm glad you returned quickly, for I have terrible news!"
- Player Character: "Oh, no! What happened?"
- Matron of the Nursery: "I'm hearing that the flowers themselves are more damaged than I had imagined. We need to make a mulch to help protect and heal the flowers before it's too late! Even with the shrumblers taken care of, the flowers could still die and the unborn fae with them!"
- Player Character: "Is this something I can help with? What can I do?"
- Matron of the Nursery: "An herb named faeicia grows here in the Nursery. When used as mulch, this herb's nutrients can repair injured flowers. Please, find several sprigs of faeicia and bring them to me!"
- Player Character: "I'll find the faeicia for you."

Collect faeicia herbs.

Return the faeicia herbs to the Matron of the Nursery.

- Matron of the Nursery: "Ah! These faeicia plants are exactly what I needed! You have done wonderfully, [Player Character], and have helped save the lives of many unborn fae. However, there is still much to be done."

I have returned the herbs to the Matron of the Nursery.

The Matron of the Nursery was able to heal the spirit blooms with the faeicia herbs I gathered for her.
