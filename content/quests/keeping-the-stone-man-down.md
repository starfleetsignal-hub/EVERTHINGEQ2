## Steps

1. Go to [[Hole from Skyfire to Danak Shipyards]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]] and speak with [[Krag]]. {{waypoint -857, -54, 1291}}
1. Kill [[An ashfire drake|ashfire drakes]] in [[Skyfire Mountains]] for 9 updates. {{waypoint -2463, 282, 509}}
1. Return to Krag to receive [[Diamond powder]].
1. Return to [[Skyrider Tosuj]] in [[Kylong Plains]]. {{waypoint 1474, 165, -2903}}
1. Enter [[Chardok]] and apply the diamond powder to the *Nak'azar titan*. {{waypoint -568, -65, -37}}
1. Go to [[The Water Chamber]] and pick up [[A Di'Zokian blasting keg]] at {{waypoint -480, -84, -333}}. Then head back to the *Nak'azar titan* and click on it. Destroy the [[A titan shard|titan shards]] that spawn.
   - All group members must do this at the same time in order to get the next update. If you don't, you'll need to go back and get another keg.
1. Return to Skyrider Tosuj in [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]].

- +500 faction with **The Gwalnax Brigade**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Rilissian Mantle of Blossoms]]
  - [[Rilissian Mantle of Fangs]]
  - [[Rilissian Mantle of Poison Vines]]
  - [[Rilissian Mantle of Scales]]
