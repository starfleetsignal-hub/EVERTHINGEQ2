## Steps

1. Hunt wolves in [[Antonica]] until you find 5 sleek wolf hides. The following wolves will provide updates:
   - [[A timber wolf]] {{waypoint -1350, -17, 128}}
   - [[A young timber wolf]] {{waypoint -250, -13, 154}}
   - [[A feral timber wolf]] {{waypoint -1739, -17, 460}}
   - [[A prowler]] in [[Greater Faydark]] {{waypoint -174, 6, 187}}
   - [[A ruins prowler]] in [[The Forest Ruins]]
   - [[A black wolf]] in [[The Commonlands]]
1. Return to Erwin for you reward.

## Rewards

- Coin
- [[Canine tooth earring]]
