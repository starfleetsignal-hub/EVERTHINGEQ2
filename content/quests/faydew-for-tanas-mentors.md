Tana would like me to collect some Faydew for her. The Faydew can be found near mushrooms in the area of [[The Lesser Faydark]] that Tana is in.

## Steps

![](images/Faydew_%28Visible%29.jpg)

1. Gather 12 [[Faydew]] around the GFay zoneline.  They are a sparkling groundspawn. (a lot can be found around the area at {{waypoint -337.34, -34.39, 39.89}} Tab picks them up, and I find it easiest to stay on the road and cycle through. However, they are visible from quite a distance, so you could just run through the zone :)
1. Return to Tana

## Rewards

- At least 6g 19s 1c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Archaic Spiritualist's Chain Cuffs]] (Shamans)
  - [[Boundless Naturewalker's Bands]] (Druids)
  - [[Defiant Vagabond's Vambraces]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Flowing Sensei's Wrist Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Gallant Dragoon's Bracers]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Resplendent Thaumaturge's Cuffs]] (All Mages)
  - [[Reverent Curate's Wristguard]] (Clerics)
