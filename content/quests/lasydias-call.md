## Description

![Lasydia, mermaid of Queen's Colony.](images/Lasydiasstatue.jpg)
"I found a statue under the waters of the isle that appears to be a lesser deity named Lasydia. After reading the inscription chiseled on it I feel a great need to make the waters around me safer. My immediate thought is to remove the threat to others that the razortooth sharks pose." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill 5 [[A razortooth shark|razortooth sharks]].
   - Examining the statue provides you with an automatic 12 minute buff that allows you to breath underwater.

## Rewards

- Experience
- [[Castaway Sash]]

## Journal Steps and Notes

- "Lasydia - as divinely merciful as she is beautiful. Deliverer of the drowned and caretaker of the castaway." A feeling of purpose surrounds and penetrates you. You feel a sudden desire to make the waters around you a safer place.

It is Lasydia's desire that I slay 5 razortooth sharks.

Lasydia has comforted me and the feeling to slay sharks left me after I had slain a number of them.

A mysterious sash floated to me out of the water after my desire to slay the vicious sharks left me. I can only guess if it was by accident or by design that the sash found me.
