## Steps

1. Speak with [[Finbert Lintzig]], at Gnomeland Security Headquarters {{waypoint -584, 175, 959}}
1. Slay the [[A clockwork reclaimer|clockwork reclaimer]] that spawns and attacks.
   - Note:  You must be high enough level or this step does not appear to trigger (did not trigger at level 20).
1. Speak with [[Finbert Lintzig]].
1. Obtain 5 more [[Little Black Box|Little Black Boxes]], by slaying [[A collection prognosticator|collection prognosticators]] near the [[Great Gear]] or [[Klak'Anon]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]].
1. Return to Finbert Lintzig.
