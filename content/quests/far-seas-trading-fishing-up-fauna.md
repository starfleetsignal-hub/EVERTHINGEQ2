## Steps

1. [[Kate]] is in the tower on [[Daarspire]] in the [[Vesspyr Isles]].
   - Speak to [[Kate]] in the upper level of the tower. {{waypoint 173, 188, -143}}
1. I need fish caught fresh in [[Vesspyr Isles]] for [[Kate|Kate's]] research.
   - Catch 5 fish in [[Vesspyr Isles]]. (This can be done at low levels by going to {{waypoint -78.64, 198.67, 66.89}} as the fishing seems to have a longer range)
1. [[Kate]] is in the tower on [[Daarspire]] in the [[Vesspyr Isles]].
   - Bring the fish back to [[Kate]]. {{waypoint 173, 188, -143}}
1. [[Kate]] asked me to draw diagrams of the fish so they could keep a record of any changes or differences
   - Draw the diagrams of each fish.
   - Scribe [[How to Study Fish]]
   - Craft 5 [[Diagram of a simple fish]] (These are normal crafts)
     - You need:
       - 5 Vesspyr Isles Fish Samples (These don't actually appear in your inventory)
       - 5 [[Matoppie roots]]
       - 25 [[Effulgent Incense]]
1. [[Kate]] gave me instructions on how to bind the diagrams into a report
   - Bind the diagrams into a wildlife sampling report
   - Craft the [[Wildlife Sampling Report]] (Please note that this is an exceedingly long craft)
   - You Need:
     - 2 Vesspyr fish diagrams
     - 2 [[Matoppie roots]]
     - 5 [[Effulgent Incense]]
1. [[Ethan]] is in the tower on [[Daarspire]] in the [[Vesspyr Isles]].
   - Deliver the Wildlife Report to [[Ethan]]
