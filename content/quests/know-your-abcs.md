## Description

The misguided abecedarians and bibliothecaries have erroneously maligned their interests to serve the powers of the void. Their research must be halted immediately!

## Steps

1. I must enter the [[Library of Erudin]] and put an end to the research.
   - Put an end to the abecedarian's research.
     - Kill eight [[An Erudin abecedarian]]
   - Put an end to the bibliothecary's research.
     - Kill eight [[An Erudin bibliothecary]]

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
