## Steps

this is the 3rd solo mission on Skyshrine.

- Zone into Dracur Prime [Advanced Solo].

1. kill in any order:
   - kill [[Nenlarn]]
   - kill [[Xerxis Mal'Fyrm]] (Supreme General)
     - and his 2 henchmen:
       - [[Mardonn Myr'Valak]] (Prime Sorcerer)
       - [[Ariorb Vel'Kera]] (Arch Priest, kill him first)
   - kill Hammilkor Barka
     - clear area first - knockbacks!
   - kill Valagas
     - get out of circles on the ground
1. Once the four names are down, Tigloth will spawn
   - kill Tigloth the Conqueror (98^^ Heroic)
     - knockback
     - drains energy
   - Tigloth normally drops an [[Ornate Crate of Endless Goodies]] just like the quest reward, except the contents will be lootable with whatever loot rules you normally work with, if you are not alone in the instance.
   - Tigloth may also drop a random Master level spell book.

## Rewards

- At least *29g 85s 79c
- An [[Ornate Crate of Endless Goodies]]
