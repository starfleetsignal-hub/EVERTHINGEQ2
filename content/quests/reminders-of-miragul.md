## Steps

1. Retrieve Myragul's Prized Possessions from the room where you killed [[Patriae Vykel]]
1. Pick up Miragul's wedding band、Miragul's baby boots、Miragul's locket、Miragul's looking glass in the room.
1. Return these to [[Lucretia Nasin]] by the shard giver outside.

- 34g 73s 79c
