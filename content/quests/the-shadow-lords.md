## Steps

1. Return to the [[Tomb of the Shadow Lords]] {{waypoint 1949, 41, 160}} and click on the sarcophagi to spawn the different shadow lords. Drop them down to <25% health and then use the [[Meta-Siphon]] on them:
   - Left sarcophagus: [[Shadow Lord Raellok]]
   - Center sarcophagus: [[Shadow Lord Vor]]
   - Right sarcophagus: [[Shadow Lord Durlak]]
1. Return to [[Meileimeileemtem Jeliimtieoileom]].

## Notes

Some time after completing this quest, you get a mail message from [[Meileimeileemtem Jeliimtieoileom]]:

M.J. in Mok Rent wrote:

*********************************************

Brunch,

I am uncertain exactly how interested in my work you were, but in case it
was of any interest at all, I have decided to update you on my findings.

From what I can tell, Verelisk was masquerading as a Sathirian outpost.
However, beyond control of the intended government, there was a council of
three, who truly pulled the strings. The phrase "Shadow Lords" often
evokes a strong response from the spirits of Verelisk.

Though loyal to the Sathirian empire, these Shadow Lords were unhappy with
the ruling ability of those in power. The Shadow Lords secretly worked for
the benefit of Verelisk, but were looked upon by members of Verelisk's
"real" government with hatred.

There are still many mysteries within Verelisk, but I have uncovered
something rather big here, and I have you to thank for that.

May your travels keep you safe.

*********************************************
