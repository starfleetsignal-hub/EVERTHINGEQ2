*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Enter the cave {{waypoint -158, -13, 574}} in the hills northeast of Tyllia and inspect the scarecrow on the table.
1. Inspect the haystack to the right.
1. Inspect the worktable to the right of the hay.
1. Return to [[Tyllia Navar]].

## Rewards

- At least 10s 18c
- +85 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Polished Band of the Alliance]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Polished Band of the Concordium]] (All Mages)
  - [[Polished Band of the Guard]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Polished Band of the Watch]] (All Priests)
