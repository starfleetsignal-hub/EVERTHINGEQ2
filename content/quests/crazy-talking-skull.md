## Steps

- The skeleton will ask you to find his missing body.
  - You can find his body at {{waypoint -128, 0, -147}}. Examine it and bring it back to him.
- Go back to the skeleton (quest starter) and re-examine it.
  - **NOTE:** The [[Skeleton of Gord Smith]] will attack you. Kill him to finish the quest.

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Gord's Bone Dirk]]
  - [[Gord's Bone Fists]]
  - [[Gord's Bone Half-Staff]]
  - [[Gord's Bone Sceptre]]
