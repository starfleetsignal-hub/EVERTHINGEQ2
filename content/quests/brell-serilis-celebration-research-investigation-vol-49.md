## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The avatar summoning rituals require the manipulation of immense power and are inherently dangerous. To reduce the chance of failure and provide an anchor for that power, the herald of Brell Serilis has asked me to collect an item that may have once belonged to that god or one of their most loyal followers

1. Collect [[Reliquary of Brell Serilis|Reliquaries of Brell Serilis]] from a powerful creature in [[Vaashkaani: Golden Rule (Heroic I)|Vaashkaani: Golden Rule]] heroic dungeon.

**This does not update in [Solo]. It needs to be Vaashkaani: Every Which Way [Event Heroic I]**

1. Return to the [[Herald to the Duke of Below]] {{waypoint -241, 212, -56}} in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
