This is part 6 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Freeport]] who wear Chain and Plate Armor (with the exception of [[Shaman|shaman]] types).

- If you are from [[Qeynos]], [[Kelethin]], or [[New Halas]], then your version of the quest is called [[Ian's Forgetful Family - Part VI]].

## Steps

1. Kill a variety of creatures within [[Nektulos Forest]] for materials:
   - Kill 18 [[A mist grinnin savage (Nektulos Forest)|mist grinnin savages]] ({{waypoint -145, 0, -777}}, just north of [[D'Abth Bridge]])
   - 12 [[An elder ash owlbear|elder ash owlbears]] (around {{waypoint -840, 91, 82}})
   - 14 [[A bloated soul leech|bloated soul leeches]] (around {{waypoint -560, 5, -400}}, by [[Mound Lake]]).
     - There are only 10 bloated soul leeches at mound lake and they are on a 5 minute respawn timer.
     - However, there are plenty of bloated soul leaches in the river starting at the bridge near the Pot ({{waypoint -972, 0, -572}}) and proceeding north.
   - Kill [[Obere Warrain]] (Heroic lvl 25^ with two lvl 25vvv [[A bodyguard (Nektulos Forest)|bodyguards]]) - he auto-spawns and attacks in the southernmost camp on the beach when you get close {{waypoint 568, -1, -129}}.
     - NOTE:  You do not need to kill the entire encounter to get the quest update.  You only need to kill Obere Warrain.
1. Return to [[Dyric Pire]].
