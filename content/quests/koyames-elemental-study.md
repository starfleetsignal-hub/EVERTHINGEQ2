## Notes

- The elemental samples are body drops and require you to loot the mob after it is dead. Not every elemental will drop an update.
- The various elementals spawn randomly on the little floating islands and a few other locations.
- Fire and earth elementals may be found on [[Mage's Pebble]] around {{waypoint 759, 53, 968}}.
- Additional elemental locations may be found on the Talk page.

## Steps

1. Obtain the following :
   - Obtain 3 [[Planar earth elemental]] samples of [[Shifting stratium]]. Some known locations:
     - {{waypoint 738, 55, 964}}
     - {{waypoint 974, 19, 1004}}
     - {{waypoint 838, 14, 730}}
     - {{waypoint 962, 109, 435}}
     - [[Zivier's Reach]]
   - Obtain 3 [[Planar water elemental]] samples of [[Fused brinidine (Version 2)|fused brinidine]] around {{waypoint 705.92, 8.78, 316.57}}
   - Obtain 3 [[Planar fire elemental]] samples of [[Glowing ignasium]]. Some known locations:
     - {{waypoint 960, 110, 433}}
     - {{waypoint 798, 26, 1007}}
     - {{waypoint 958, 109, 437}} these have a 3 minute respawn timer.
   - Obtain 3 [[Planar air elemental]] samples of [[Solidified aurorite]] ...
     - The shadowed air elementals around the stone circle in the [[The Stratafold|Stratafold]] {{waypoint 534, 30, 516}} respawn quickly.
1. Return to [[Koyame]] at {{waypoint -108, 575, -653}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Metetherial Crystal]] x 2
- +1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
