## Steps

1. Touch the Drinalian crystal next to [[Darathar (NPC)|Darathar]] to start the incantation.
   - Ripped from your mortal body!
   - Confront Darathar.
1. Return to [[Daarspire]] and speak with [[Yelinak the Ancient (Vesspyr Isles)|Lord Yelinak]].
1. Interact with the Drinalian column in [[Falinpol]] {{waypoint 14, 195, -1}}.
1. Return to Darathar.
1. Watch the interaction.
