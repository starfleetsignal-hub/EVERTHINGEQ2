*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Enter the [[The Court of the Coin (Quest Instance)|Court of the Coin]] instance via the back door at {{waypoint 197, 139, -3}}.
1. Complete the following within 5 minutes:
   1. Inspect a broken frame at {{waypoint 12, 0, 5}}.
   1. Kill the [[Terrorantula thief|Terrorantula thieves]] that spawn near the zone-in.
1. Zone out via the back door to receive your reward.
