## Steps

1. Speak with [[Khalhan al-Azhar]] then proceed to the second room.
1. Speak again with Khalhan al-Azhar in the second room. He directs you to step onto the rug in the center of the room to start the first trial.
   - From here on, after every trial is completed you must speak again with Khalhan al-Azhar and return to the rug afterwards in order to trigger the waves.
1. Defeat the first trial, (3 [[A sand scorpion|sand scorpions]])
1. Defeat the second trial ([[Charra'zon]])
1. Defeat the third trial ([[Uphian]] and [[Zathos]])
1. Defeat the fourth trial ([[King Vach]])
1. Speak to Khalhan al-Azbar to receive your reward and exit the instance.

## Rewards

One of the Following:

  - [[Bracelet of the Black Isles]]
  - [[Cobra's Coil]]
  - [[Ring of Delights]]
