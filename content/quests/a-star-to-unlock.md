## Notes

- [[Shady Swashbuckler XVIII]] can provide a replacement for a [[Dissassembled Star Key]], should you have deleted it.

## Steps

1. Go up to [[The Lyceum of Naar'Yora]] on the third floor and enter the north alcove, which is protected by a magical barrier, and click on the middle bookcase, second shelf from the bottom {{waypoint 0, 57, -45}}. To remove the barrier, kill the 1-2 [[A temperamental tome|temperamental tomes]] that are flying around the room.
   - The barrier can be finicky, and may take several minutes to de-spawn after the tomes are killed. Furthermore, it has been noted that if the barrier does not de-spawn at all, killing all mobs present in the Lyceum may trigger the de-spawn.
   - If the barrier respawns while the player is still inside the alcove, they will become trapped and the only option is to wait for the tomes to be killed by another player, or teleport out of the dungeon.
   - This is also the same bookcase used in the quest [[A Broken Predicament]].
1. Go to [[The Workshop of Naar'Yora]] {{waypoint 167, 57, -31}} on the third floor and obtain the following items:
   - 6 star sprockets, from piles of sprockets everywhere around the workshop.
   - 2 tiny star gears, common updates from killing the [[A Blacktalon tinker|Blacktalon tinkers]] in the workshop. You may have to clear the room multiple times for the tinkers to spawn.
   - 1 copper star band from [[A Blacktalon taskmaster]].
   - 1 special star key tool from [[Warden Skalarr]] or his placeholder, [[A Blacktalon workshop warden]].
1. Use the workbench at {{waypoint 177, 57, -31}} to make 'the Star Key'.
1. Examine 'the Star Key' from your inventory.
1. Go to [[The Circle of the Maidens]] on the second floor and examine the 'A Maiden' statue at {{waypoint 30, 28, 6}}.
1. Examine the other 'A Maiden' statue at {{waypoint 31, 28, -7}} and select "Pry the visor" to get an "A" dragon key.
1. Use this "A" key to open 'The Tome of Aaryonar' in the alcove that housed the book for step one. {{waypoint -6.88, 57.18, -43.23}}
1. Gather four *shipwrecked* clear crystals that can spawn in the six large birdbaths throughout the zone. They are small, but have a bright particle effect that can be seen from a distance. Birdbath locations are as follows:
   - Third floor, between the Lyceum and Workshop: {{waypoint 91, 57, 7}}
   - Fourth floor, back of [[The Gymnasium]]: {{waypoint -96, 87, 0}} and {{waypoint -139, 87, 0}}
   - First floor, [[Nest of the East Wing]]: {{waypoint 55, 1, 156}}, {{waypoint 100, 1, 112}} and {{waypoint 55, 1, 66}}
   - When harvested, these crystals will respawn in a different birdbath. Visiting all six birdbaths will get you four crystals.
1. Fill the crystals by:
   - Killing [[The Ironfang Vanguard]] in [[The Refectory]] on the fourth floor. {{waypoint 26, 86, 1}}
   - Killing 4 [[A Naar'Yora defender|Naar'Yora defenders]] in [[The Great Aviary]] on the sixth floor. {{waypoint 13, 141, 55}}
1. Go to the [[Deliberation Chamber]] on the fourth floor and place the filled crystals around the circle at {{waypoint -178, 86, -9}}. The crystals may be automatically placed upon entering the circle.
1. Kill [[Advisor Soliddar]], or his placeholder, [[An Advisor's assistant]].
1. [[The First Shard of Aaryonar]], a small ghostly dragon, will spawn after killing the Advisor. Speak with it to complete the quest.

## Reward

- [[Dragonscale Sash]]
