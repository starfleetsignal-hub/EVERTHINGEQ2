*Please note that you need to be able to speak Gymy, the pygmy language, to speak to Nuwat Pickletrade and complete the quest. Complete the quest [[Handle With Care (Tranquil Sea)|Handle With Care]] to receive the Gymy language primer.*

## Steps

1. Kill 20 Allu'thoa (any kind)
1. Seek out [[Nuwat Pickletrade]] at the Merinep output {{waypoint -1025, 98, -172}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 29g 60s
- One of the following
  - [[Electrified Adornment of Endurance]]
  - [[Electrified Adornment of Energy]]
