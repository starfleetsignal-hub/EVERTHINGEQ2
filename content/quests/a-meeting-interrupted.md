## Notes

- The item [[A twine bound package]], which offers [[A Lost Package (Commonlands)|A Lost Package]], can only be obtained while on this quest.

## Steps

1. During the nighttime (any time after 8 PM game time), head to the mausoleum at {{waypoint 150, -48, -726}}. (near the gate to Darklight Wood)
   - You can type /time to see the current game time or open the ingame clock window. It takes 72 minutes for a full game day.
1. When you are present at the mausoleum camp after 8 PM, you will trigger the following event:
   1. Slay the four [[A Bloodskull negotiator|Bloodskull negotiators]] that spawn on the south side of the camp.
   1. Slay the four [[A dervish negotiator|Dervish negotiators]] that spawn on the north side of the camp.
   1. Slay [[Kivas Levine]] (level 15), who spawns between the mausoleums.
1. Return to [[Brandus Levine|Brandus]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 16s
- One of the following:
  - [[Worn Militia Chain Coat]]
  - [[Worn Militia Leather Tunic]]
  - [[Worn Militia Tabard]]
