## Steps

1. Meet with [[Jin'tu]] and get proof of his identity {{waypoint 565, 196, 4000}}.
1. Return the [[Dituri Flower|flower]] to [[Bai'Yun]].

  - [[Vambraces of Clerical Precision]] (Crusader choice)
