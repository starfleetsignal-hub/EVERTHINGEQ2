## Steps

1. Harvest 20 wood from [[Wood Nodes#T5|corrupted arbors]].
1. Kill root horrors in the zone for 6 common auto-updates. A root horror is a creature like [[A bramble beast]]; they are found in the southwest section of the zone.
1. Kill [[The Oak Matriarch]]. {{waypoint -746, 0, 37}}
