## Notes

- You cannot get this quest if have any active quests from [[Havik Thyx]].

## Steps

1. Kill the following:
   - 10 [[A trakanasaur|trakanasaur]]s east of [[Snake Eye's Hunting Camp]]. {{waypoint -690, 26, -150}}
   - 14 [[A jungle quatcha|jungle quatcha]]s or [[An elder jungle quatcha|elder jungle quatcha]]s around [[The Shatterhorn]]. {{waypoint -392, 40, -179}}
1. Return to Emissary Tranz.

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- +10,000 faction with **Tabernacle of Pain**
