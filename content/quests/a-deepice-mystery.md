## Notes

- Since 2020 this quest is no longer repeatable, thus making this version of the quest the last time a character must do it to unlock the other quests in the zone.

## Steps

1. Defeat the following enemies:
   - [[A living blaze]] in [[Lavastorm]]. {{waypoint -236, -128, 488}} or {{waypoint -110.47, -110.34, 428.68}}
   - [[A living snow flurry]] in [[Everfrost]]. {{waypoint 41, -18, 10}}
1. Return to Jingle
1. Use [[Jingle's Lasting Thaw Potion]] on [[Candice Cheriweth]]. {{waypoint 62, -35, -1626}} (northeastern island)
1. Speak with Candice Cheriweth.
1. Use Jingle's Lasting Thaw Potion on [[Gardy Ex-Giftgiver]]. {{waypoint 225, -29, -1403}} (central island)
1. Speak with Gardy Ex-Giftgiver.
1. Use Jingle's Lasting Thaw Potion on [[Mr. McScroogle (Frostfell Wonderland Village)|Mr. McScroogle]] {{waypoint 342, -30, -1396}} (island northwest of Gardy).
1. Speak with Mr. McScroogle.
1. Use Jingle's Lasting Thaw Potion on [[Queen Bunny]]. {{waypoint 204, -29, -1372}} (central island)
1. Speak with Queen Bunny.
1. Use Jingle's Lasting Thaw Potion on [[Snarf Frostfoot (Frostfell)|Snarf Frostfoot]]. {{waypoint 175, -37, -1313}} (island southeast of Queen Bunny)
1. Speak with Snarf Frostfoot.
1. Return to Jingle.
1. Hunt for the mysterious ice creature on the northern islands. {{waypoint 207, -35, -1625}}
   - Defeat the [[A Frost Fae Drake|Frost Fae Drake]] and the [[A hoarfrost attacker|hoarfrost attacker]].
1. Hunt for the mysterious ice creature on the western islands. {{waypoint 438.61, -36.64, -1424.53}}
   - Defeat the [[A Frost Fae Drake|Frost Fae Drake]] and the [[An iceclaw scrapper|iceclaw scrapper]].
1. Hunt for the mysterious ice creature on the southern islands. {{waypoint 247, -35, -1239}}
   - Defeat the [[An icetooth snarler|icetooth snarler]].
1. Fight [[Jarok Frostpetal]] and [[Frostbite (Frostfell Wonderland Village)|Frostbite]].
1. Speak with Jarok Frostpetal, just northwest of the fight. {{waypoint 290.31, -36.08, -1268.31}}

  - 5 x [[Frozen Token of E'ci]]
  - unlocked [[Petamorph Wand: Frost Fae Drake]] to buy from Gerbi Frostfoot.
  - unlocked [[Frost Fae Drake Plushie]] to buy from Gerbi Frostfoot.
