## Steps

1. Speak with [[Augurer Dahimat]] in [[The Concordium Tower]] {{waypoint 705, 41, 107}} (take the red portal, he's on the lower level of the library)
1. Obtain the following
   - 6 [[Castle moat perch]] from the moat in Qeynos Capitol District
   - 4 [[Arbos moss]] from around [[Arbos, the Elddar Tree]] in The Elddar Grove (they look like gold sparkling tall grass, not moss)
   - A [[Scroll of Hallowed Zeal]] - purchase from [[Meredeth]] at the Temple of Life in North Qeynos for 12s
1. Return to Augurer Dahimat
1. Return to Maareona Ludimintium

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g @ lvl 95
