## Steps

1. Speak with [[Master Ward Singer Juma]] in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] just outside the magical barrier on the path to [[Vex Thal]] {{waypoint 6.44, 97.68, 262.12}}.
1. Create a focus for the magic song.
   1. Obtain a [[Hollow geode]] by clicking a **Crystal Formation** at {{waypoint -109.84, 44.12, -560.01}}.
   1. Click **Clear Clean Water** in [[Amelia Pond]] at {{waypoint -252.84, 36.85, -89.93}}.
   1. Click *Clear Clean Sand** on [[Needlespine Beach]] {{waypoint 584.07, 13.53, 128.82}}.
   1. Examine the [[Hollow geode]] from your inventory, then add the water and sand to obtain the [[Dar Khura Focus]].
1. Return to the magical barrier. Click the center of the **Spirit Portal** then use the [[Dar Khura Focus]].
1. Speak with [[Master Ward Singer Juma]] {{waypoint 6.44, 97.68, 262.12}}.
1. Speak to [[Scout Amani Abasi]] in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] beside the gate to [[City of Shar Vahl]] {{waypoint -546.81, 160.50, -729.44}}.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 90g 2s 69,440 status
