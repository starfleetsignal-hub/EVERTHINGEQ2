## Steps

1. Hail [[A skygazer]] in [[Maj'Dul]] to ask about good omens for Darzi's shop. {{waypoint -173, 177, -144}}
1. Check the wares of other vendors in [[Midday Market]]. {{waypoint 38, 139, -55}}
1. Check the wares in the [[Golden Scepter]]. {{waypoint 90, 147, -123}}
1. Speak to [[Ghassan the Trader]] at the docks in [[The Sinking Sands]].
1. Collect 6 cobra skins from [[A sunvenom cobra|sunvenom cobras]] in [[The Pillars of Flame]]. {{waypoint 950, -125, -1103}}
1. Return to [[Darzi Sendoa]].

## Rewards

- At least 6g 23s 20c
