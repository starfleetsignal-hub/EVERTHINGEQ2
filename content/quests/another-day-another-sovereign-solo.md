## Description

*The threats are never ending. The Crusaders of Greenmist have given me the pleasure of wiping out the leaders of some of the greater threats spread across Kunark, and I will not stop until that threat is vanquished.*

## Steps

- Defeat targets throughout Kunark

1. Kill [[Neh'Ashiir (Solo)|Neh'Ashiir]] in the [[Lost City of Torsis: Reaver's Remnants (Solo)]]
1. Kill [[NPC|The Kly (Solo)]] in [[Crypt of Dalnir: Ritual Chamber (Solo)]]
1. Kill [[Over-General Vihgoh]] in [[Kaesora: Xalgozian Stronghold (Solo)]]
1. Kill [[Lachina Protector of Magics|Lachina]] in [[Arcanna'se Spire: Forgotten Sanctum (Solo)]]
