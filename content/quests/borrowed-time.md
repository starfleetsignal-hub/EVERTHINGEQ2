Old Man Pomeranke sends you on a quest to find his dictionary in a 'crypt with flashing lights' in [[Kunark]].

1. Head to [[Sebilis]], as this is the location he described.
1. Gather [[Sul'Dal to Common for Idiots, Fourth Edition]] from a skeleton at {{waypoint -152, -23, 311}}.
1. Once you have the tome, and you've confirmed the quest updated, head back to [[Old Man Pomerenke]]. Make sure you complete his entire dialogue at this point or you will not receive the update.
