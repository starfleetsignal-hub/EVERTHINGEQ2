## Steps

1. Go back to where you found Gip {{waypoint 808, 163, -685}}.  Near his body you will find 3 clickable objects:
   - "residue coated rocks" {{waypoint 809.5, 163, -685.5}}
     - *This will give you a quest called [[Evidence? Odd Plant Residue]]", which asks you to return to [[Captain Lip Marrsquire]] at [[Firmroot Moot]].*
   - "shreds of cloth" {{waypoint 810, 162, -689}}
     - *This will give you a quest called [[Evidence? Torn Piece of Clothing]], which asks you to return to [[Captain Lip Marrsquire]] at [[Firmroot Moot]].*
     - *Note: Very hard to see for me, had to zoom in.*
   - "broken axe head" {{waypoint 824, 163, -677}}
     - *This will give you a quest called [[Evidence? Broken Axe Head]], which asks you to return to [[Captain Lip Marrsquire]] at [[Firmroot Moot]].*
1. Return to [[Captain Lip Marrsquire]] to update all sub-quests.
1. Bring the plant remains to [[Kilip the Green]] at {{waypoint 19, -77, -265}}.
1. Return to [[Captain Lip Marrsquire]] one more time for final turn in.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +1200 faction with **Survival Accord**
