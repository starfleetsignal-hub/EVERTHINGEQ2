## Steps

1. Destroy 6 tourbillion augmentations at the spires. {{waypoint 102, 38, -329}} Target and attack the gems attached to the spires.
1. Kill 8 Gehein at the spires.
1. Kill [[Zeeil (Named)|Zeeil]] at {{waypoint 72, 36, -338}} to receive [[Indecipherable Scroll (Quest Item)|Indecipherable Scroll]].
1. Return to [[Tal'il Amhenti]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- One of the following, depending on class:
  - [[Harrowed Belt of the Orafik]] (mage)
  - [[Harrowed Belt of the Tirun]]  (tank)
  - [[Harrowed Belt of the Gehein]] (healer)
  - [[Harrowed Belt of the Skirth]] (scout)
  - [[Harrowed Belt of the Orafik]] (brawler)
