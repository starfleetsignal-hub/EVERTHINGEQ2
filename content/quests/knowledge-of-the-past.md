## Notes

- This quest is unavailable if you already speak [[Koada'Dal (Language)|Koada'Dal]].
- This is a subquest for [[Wrapping It All Up]]; you may not obtain this quest if you are not currently on that quest.

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Tower of the Moon]] in [[Maj'Dul]]. {{waypoint -150, 176, -150}}
1. Examine the book on the table. {{waypoint 1, 13, 16}}
1. Go to [[The Halls of Learning]] in [[The Silent City]]  and kill the following monster types, in no specific order: {{waypoint 409, 30, 494}}
   - Tomes in the general area for 5 updates (Uncommon)
   - Mummies in the general area for 10 updates (Uncommon)

## Rewards

- Experience
- Language learned: [[Koada'Dal]]
