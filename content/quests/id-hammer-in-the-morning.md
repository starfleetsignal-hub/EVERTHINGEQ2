## Steps

1. Purchase the following items:
   - [[Legendary Hammer Foot]] from [[Eviss Ryiss]], Legion of Danak faction merchant, in [[Danak Shipyards]]: {{waypoint -100, -40, 1108}}
   - [[Legendary Hammer Head]] from [[Mirg Nel'Ron]], The Sel'Nok Brigade faction merchant, in [[Sel'Nok Cave]]: {{waypoint -986, -67, -988}}
   - [[Legendary Hammer Claw]] from [[Tragen Gorschk]], Clan Skleross faction merchant, in [[Skleross Encampment]]: {{waypoint -865, -19, -1161}}
   - Each piece costs 1g 20s.
   - The quest automatically updates if you already have these items in your inventory.
1. Return to [[Utandar Rizndown]] in the Danak Shipyards.

## Rewards

- At least 37g
- [[Worker Sledgemallet]]
