## Steps

*After having confirmed the mysterious illness affecting some of the Cult of Vul is being spread by the dark matter emerging from the void seeping fissures on the eastern mesas and islands, Hiwaaya Al'Naderi has asked that I place protective runes around the fissures in an effort to cleanse them.*

- Place protective runes at strategic places on [[The Floating Steps]] (0/8).
- Remove Vulwrought sentinels (0/5).
- Return to [[Hiwaaya Al'Naderi (Spiral of Vul)|Hiwaaya Al'Naderi]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- [[Void Rune Stave]]
