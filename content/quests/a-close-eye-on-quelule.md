## Description

The Kerra Isle outfitter asked me to travel to Moonfield Hamlet and collect the latest surveillance reports from Soren.

## Steps

1. You have been asked to travel to [[Moonfield Hamlet]] and collect the latest surveillance reports from [[Soren]].
   - Travel from [[Kerra Isle]] to [[Toxxulia Forest]] and speak to the Temporal pathfinder and go to [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]]. Once there, travel to the [[Moonfield Hamlet (POI)|Moonfield Hamlet]].
1. Speak with [[Soren]] {{waypoint 247, 412, 1213}} to collect the reports.
1. Return to [[Harriet]] on Kerra Isle to receive your rewards.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,000 faction with **Kerra Isle**
- +2,500 faction with **Kerra Isle Outfitters**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Kerra Isle Daily Supply Tasks
