## Steps

1. Recover 6 totems from feral lujien, found north of [[Venox Tarkog|Venox]] (uncommon auto-update).
1. Return to [[Venox Tarkog|Venox]].
1. Speak to [[Elder Direfur]] to the south.
1. Speak to [[Elder Brokenfang]] at {{waypoint 254, 88, 52}} .
1. Speak to [[Lunar Sky]] at {{waypoint 248, 92, 97}} .
1. Kill the now-feral [[A feral Lunar Sky|Lunar Sky]]. You'll get [[A broken lujien totem]].
1. Return to [[Venox Tarkog]] to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
