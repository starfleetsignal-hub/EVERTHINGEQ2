## Note

You may want to have [[Sand By Me]] in your journal before starting step 6.

## Steps

1. Attempt to light the holy flames above [[Kren Rommol]] by clicking on the **Holy Ortallian Brazier Oil** {{waypoint -186.48, 99.88, -477.66}}.![Holy Ortallian Brazier Oil](images/Holy_Ortallian_Brazier_Oil.png)
   - Defeat the sandstone walker.
   - Light the flame again. (May take several attempts.)
1. Return to [[Selaera Taenaeris]] {{waypoint -20, 18, -168}}
1. Gather 8 **Sandscoured driftwood** to burn around {{waypoint -475.02, -6.95, -62.25}}![Sandscoured driftwood](images/Sandscoured_driftwood.png)
1. Return to [[Selaera Taenaeris]]
1. Hunt 6 [[A Tsmang Blightswarm grub|**Tsmang Blightswarm grubs**]] in the [[Tsmang Blightswarm Hive]] around {{waypoint 384.56, 90.88, -705.63}}
   - The hive entrance is at {{waypoint 176.65, 78.31, -691.51}}
1. Enter [[Sandstone Delta: Eye of Night (Solo)|Sandstone Delta: Eye of Night [Solo]]] at {{waypoint 589.24, 112.99, -631.62}}![Ortallian Chest](images/Ortallian_Chest.png)
   - *Note for a walktrough of the zone see [[Sandstone Delta: Eye of Night (Solo)|Sandstone Delta: Eye of Night [Solo]]]*
1. obtain the following within [[Sandstone Delta: Eye of Night (Solo)|Sandstone Delta: Eye of Night [Solo]]]
   - Obtain **an Ortallian emblem belt** from the Ortallian Chest. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 410, 137, -387}}
     - {{waypoint 346, 137, -373}}
   - Obtain **an Ortallian mask** by killing mobs
   - Gather 5 **blazed broadcloth** in area around last name. Possible locations include:![Blazed broadcloth](images/Blazed_broadcloth.png)
     - {{waypoint -99.07, 79.66, -513.28}}
     - {{waypoint -113.09, 80.85, -532.62}}
     - {{waypoint -222.51, 97.82, -508.74}}
     - {{waypoint -248.59, 96.97, -487.46}}
     - {{waypoint -115.10, 79.10, -485.85}}
     - {{waypoint -161.05, 76.48, -561.94}}
   - Gather 12 **night metal** by breaking **Eye of Night wards**, then pick up night metal![Eye of night ward and night metal](images/Eye_of_night_ward_and_night_metal.png)
1. Return to [[Selaera Taenaeris]] at [[Sandscoured Coast]] {{waypoint -20.28, 18.77, -169.94}}
1. [[Ulgaf]] {{waypoint -254.35, 16.70, -257.21}} will give you [[A gem of deific fervor]] After you have completed his subquests:
   - [[One-Eyes of War]]
   - [[Swordfury Rising]]
   - [[Blind with Stormfury]]
   - [[One-Eyes on the Prize]]
   - [[In the Names of War]]
   - [[Take to the Skies]]
1. Return to [[Selaera Taenaeris]]
