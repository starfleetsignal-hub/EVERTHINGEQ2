## Prerequisites

![](images/A_rocksnake.jpg)![](images/A_boneshroom.jpg)

- You must speak [[Serilian]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Collect the following items from the [[Field of Bone]], chosen randomly every time you accept the quest. See the talk page for known spawn locations:
   - 6 to 8 *rocksnakes*
   - 5 to 7 *boneshrooms*
1. Return to Gribs McDibbles.

## Rewards

- +1,000 faction with **The Bellywhumpers**
