## Steps

1. Kill [[Arcanus]].
1. In the area where Arcanus popped there are several books lying around on the ground. Move these books in clockwise order like a house item into the portal. A message will appear when you have destroyed them all wanting you to close the portal.
1. Simply click the portal to close it.

## Rewards

- Only XP and AA
