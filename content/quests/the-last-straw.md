## Steps

1. Examine "Squire Rustbucket", or what's left of him, outside [[Bog Bottom Mill]] {{waypoint 10, -1, -605}}.
1. Return to Sir Tatters.
1. Examine a chest in the *Boar Room* in [[Runnyeye]]. {{waypoint -74.02, 1.26, -91.28}}
1. Obtain the key for the chest from [[An enslaved warboar|enslaved warboars]]. These can be found in the same room as the chest, and more can be found in the spiral passage near the entrance, e.g. {{waypoint -84, -27, 118}}.
1. Examine chest again to unlock it.
1. Return to [[Sir Tatters]].

## Rewards

- At least 1g 59s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Sludge Crest of Nastiness]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sludge Emblem of Nastiness]] (All Priests)
  - [[Sludge Ring of Nastiness]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Sludge Signet of Nastiness]] (All Mages)
