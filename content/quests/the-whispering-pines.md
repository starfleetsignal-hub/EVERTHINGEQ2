## Steps

1. First stage:
   - Kill 12 [[A desecrated vale oak|desecrated vale oak]]s or [[A desecrated vale sapling|desecrated vale sapling]]s in the southeast corner of [[Rivervale]] {{waypoint -630, -5, 138}}
   - Kill 12 [[A bramble beast|bramble beast]]s, [[A bramble terror|bramble terror]]s or [[A withered bramble|withered bramble]]s in Rivervale {{waypoint -447, 0, 147}}
   - Kill bixies in [[The Tower of the Drafling]] for 6 common updates {{waypoint -489, 24, 50}}
   - Harvest 25 [[Severed briarwood]] from [[Wood Nodes#T4|wretched arbors]] in [[Enchanted Lands]]
1. Second stage:
   - Go to [[Spirit Lake]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] {{waypoint -58, -3, -74}}
   - Kill 18 [[A Gorehorn hulk|Gorehorn hulk]]s in [[Runnyeye]] {{waypoint -22, -6, -203}}
   - Kill 25 lamias in Rivervale - /waypoint -562, 7, -152
1. Third stage (all in Rivervale):
   - Kill nightbloods in Rivervale for an update {{waypoint -684, 1, -308}}
   - Kill 8 dryads (i.e., [[A tainted maiden|tainted maiden]]s, [[A tainted nymph seedling|tainted nymph seedling]]s, etc.) {{waypoint -334, 15, 80}}
   - Kill fairies for 8 updates (i.e., [[A Darkflight reaper|Darkflight reaper]]s, [[A Darkflight witch|Darkflight witch]]es, etc.) {{waypoint -330, 0, 260}}
   - Kill 8 [[A field fright|field fright]]s or [[Shakey]] (will need to wait for respawn) {{waypoint -332, -1, -280}}
   - Kill 8 [[A wax worker|wax worker]]s {{waypoint -223, -14, 44}}

## Reward

- [[Pinewhisper]]
