## Steps

1. Search the [[Droumlunds]] (all updates are gotten in the air on a flying mount)
   - Search the smallest island {{waypoint 691, 408, -424}}
   - Investigate the western end of the larger island {{waypoint 693, 316, -598}}
   - Explore the highest plateau {{waypoint 444, 367, -729}}
   - Search the southern side {{waypoint 459, 300, -533}}
   - Search the northeastern tip {{waypoint 246, 314, -861}}
1. Defeat [[Alzid Prime (Vesspyr Isles)|Alzid Prime]] {{waypoint 156, 305, -858}}. You automatically receive an [[Icon of the Awakened]].
1. Return to [[Lepethida]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Nexite Tol Bangles]]
