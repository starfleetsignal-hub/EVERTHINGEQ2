## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key.
   - Kill 15 [[A verdant bullfrog|verdant bullfrogs]] around {{waypoint 74, 114, -231}} in [[Takish Badlands]] at [[Renewal Pond]].
1. I need to find the Fypolydia music box key elsewhere.
1. Go to [[Sandstone Delta]]
1. Find the Fypolydia Music Box key clue from [[A deceased marauder|deceased marauders]] or [[A fallen pirate|fallen pirates]] around {{waypoint -315, 3, 115}} in [[Sandstone Delta]] at the [[Relic Shard]].
1. Examine the clue for [[The Fypolydia Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the **Fypolydia Music Box** in [[Takish Badlands]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 52s 84c
- One of the following:
  - [[Renewal Crate]]
  - [[Fypolydia Music Box]] - Collectible
