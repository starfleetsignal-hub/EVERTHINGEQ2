## Description

In the depths of the dungeon known as Befallen, a new threat has emerged. A dark offshoot of Solusek Ro followers have formed a necromantic cult known as the Burning Dead and are attempting to harness the dark energies generated there from the curse of Gynok Moltor. To combat this threat I've agreed to slay a number of the bone sifters who tirelessly pick and sort at the remains of adventurers unlucky enough to fall victim to the Burning Dead.

## Steps

1. Enter the [[Befallen: Necrotic Asylum]] and slay 10 [[A bone sifter|bone sifters]] located in [[The Bone Ditch]] {{waypoint -389.10, -29.25, 65.84}}
1. Return to [[Squire Hadley]] for reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
