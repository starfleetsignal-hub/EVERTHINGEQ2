## Steps

1. Collect 6 [[Virulid acid]] from virulent conquerors
1. Pour the acid on 6 sparkling patches on ground to uncover the minerals
1. Click twice to pick up minerals
1. Return minerals to Armsmaster Veyx

## Rewards

- At least 70g
