## Steps

1. Talk to [[Marcus Puer]] {{waypoint -37, 0, -7}}
1. Marcus asks you to collect some items:
   - 3 snake skins from [[Filthy moccasin|filthy moccasins]] {{waypoint -213, 0, -111}}
   - 2 globs of goo from [[Drudge globule|drudge globules]] {{waypoint -117, 0, -123}}
   - 1 hollow cog shaft from [[A crazed channel mender]] {{waypoint -25, 1, -75}}
1. Return to Marcus.

- [[Stenchfang Rapier]]
