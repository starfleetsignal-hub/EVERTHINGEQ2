## Notes

- This is one of five "training sessions" you must complete in order to complete the quest [[Dream Weaving]] to receive a [[Gryphon Call]].
- You can only complete one of these five training sessions every 18 hours.

## Steps

1. Receive a [[Gryphon Fur Conditioning Recipe]].
1. Using a [[Chemistry Table]], craft the conditioning recipe, with the following items:
   - 1 [[Brontotherium Meat]] (Harvested from Den node or Broker).
   - 2 [[Goahmari Herb]] (Harvested in [[Goahmari Village]] or Broker).
   - 1 [[Aerated mineral water]]
   - 1 [[Ethereal candle]]
1. Click your gryphon. Quest auto-updates and auto-finishes.   Keep rubbing until the quest auto-completes
   - **NOTE** - If rubbing polish on its scales doesn't work, simply hail your gryphon to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- Choice of [[Frost Gryphon]] or [[Boreal Gryphon]] on completion of the final of the 5 quests.
