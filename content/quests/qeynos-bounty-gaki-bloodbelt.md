## Steps

1. Travel to [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]] and kill [[Gaki Bloodbelt]] who will spawn on the road just outside the [[Orc Encampment]] at {{waypoint 365, -42, 231}}
1. Return to [[Gil McMartin]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- +3000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
