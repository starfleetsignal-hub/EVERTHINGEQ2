## Steps

1. Enter [[Chelsith]] and obtain the following ingredients:
   - *Perfectly marinated Yha-lei belly meat* from any Yha-lei;
   - *Supple abyss lurker tentacles* from [[An abyss lurker]];
   - *Large accumulation of ghastly excretion sludge* from [[A ghastly excretion]];
   - [[Frog legs of the Blue Baron]] from the corpse of the [[The Blue Baron]].
     - You must examine the legs from your inventory to receive the update. You can use previously obtained legs if you have the in your inventory from a previous run.
1. Return the ingredients to [[Goddin Ratsachak]] in [[Kylong Plains]], who makes you a [[Veeshan's Feast (Quest Starter)|Veeshan's Feast]].
1. Deliver Veeshan's Feast to [[Tszuij Adawhi]] at [[Dreg's Landing]]. {{waypoint 912, 12, 215}}
1. Return to Goddin Ratsachak.

## Rewards

- At least 36g 79s 48c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Veil of Flowing Lava]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Veil of Liquid Hot Magma]] (All Mages)
  - [[Veil of Molten Rocks]] (All Priests)
  - [[Veil of Volcanic Ash]] (All Scouts)
