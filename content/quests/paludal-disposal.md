## Steps

1. Get rid of invasive creatures and plants in [[The Palestra Memoria]].
   - Exterminate 6 [[A netherbian skulker|netherbian skulkers]].
   - Click 10 **Paludal Thorns** to destroy them with fire.
1. Return to [[Vire Freebrooke]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- [[Defender of the Haven Cuff]]
