## Steps

1. Enter [[Lost City of Torsis: Reaver's Remnants (Solo)]]
1. Pick up one scroll in the room where you kill Tzirathk at {{waypoint 0, -5, -681}}
1. Pick up another scroll in the same room, on the floor at {{waypoint 34, -5, -694}}.
1. follow the Advisor Vrishril's spirit in the Eastern corridor and speak to Advisor Vrizsril's spirit
1. Defeat the spirit of Advisor Vrizsril (level 100^)
1. Go into the room where Neh'Ashir is (Last boss of the zone) and to the right by the wall at {{waypoint -50, -5, -1100}} is an ancient Fear altar. Click it.
1. head back to [[Kerss Rekssok (Mist)|Kerss Rekssok]]
