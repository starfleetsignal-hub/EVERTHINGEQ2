## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A sentry of Jinisk|sentries of Jinisk]] and 5 [[A Jinisk citizen|citizens of Jinisk]].
   - -100 faction with **The City of Jinisk** for each kill (-1500 total).
   - Pet kills don't give faction hit.
1. Burn both carts in the courtyard. {{waypoint 656, 37, -107}} and {{waypoint 628, 37, -137}}
   - The carts disappear for a while after being burnt, so if they are missing you may have to wait a few minutes.
1. Return to Sonjaz Manx.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- +2,000 faction with **Kunzar Jungle Villagers**
