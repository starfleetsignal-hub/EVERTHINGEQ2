## Notes

![](images/Inert_Awuidor_Purifying_Rune.png)

- You may not receive all of the [[Onzolan crystal|onzolan crystals]] needed for this quest on one run through the zone.
- The quest is listed as heroic but can be soloed
- You received an [[Inert Awuidor Purifying Rune]] you click on the conflagration of water to start the quest.

## Steps

![](images/Onzolan_crystal.png)

- Note:  You may want to do the Solo Mission Quest on each of the zones at the same time and sign up for the Weekly Solo Mission Quest at the same time.
1. Take the [[North Wing Stacks]] teleporter at {{waypoint 260, -20, -285}} to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]]. Use the water portal at {{waypoint 789, 412, -380}} to enter [[Awuidor: Marr's Ascent (Solo)|Awuidor: Marr's Ascent [Solo]]].
1. Progress through the zone. (See [[Awuidor: Marr's Ascent (Solo)|Awuidor: Marr's Ascent [Solo]]] for strategies).
1. Kill sirens and Yha-Lei to obtain 19 [[Onzolan crystal|onzolan crystals]].
   - The drops are uncommon. One is dropped for each party member.
1. Use the [[Inert Awuidor Purifying Rune]] from the quest helper to recieve the [[Imbued Awuidor Purifying Rune]].
1. Click on [[The Qisalis Tripearl]] at {{waypoint -1115, 393, 806}}.
   - Use the portal to the Elemental Tower at {{waypoint -47.77, 349.79, 5.20}} to reach the Tripearl
1. Take the portal at {{waypoint -47.95, 349.78, 29.28}} to return to [[Myrist, the Great Library]].

## Rewards

- [[Qisalis Champion Accolade]]
- [[Seal of Endurance]]
