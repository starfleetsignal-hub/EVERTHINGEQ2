*This quest is repeatable up to 3 times..*

## Steps

1. Hunt a random number of one of the following randomly chosen mobs:
   - 11 [[A northern stag|northern stags]], found in [[Hollowfrost Grove]]. {{waypoint -263, 6, -230}}
   - 10 [[A glacier bear|glacier bears]], found in [[Guardian's Gulch]]. {{waypoint -484, -38, -651}}
1. Return to [[Marta Terrilon]]

## Rewards

- First time at least 66s 19c
- Second time at least 3g 35s 76c
- Third time at least 5g 2s 26c
