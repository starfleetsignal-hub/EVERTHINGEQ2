## Steps

1. To Gain Entry: In [[The Pillars of Flame]] you need to clear the entire area of mobs around the door's location {{waypoint 93.58, -60.25, -394.1}} including around a rock until you get a message saying the Ancient Table Door has activated. Then click on the door knob that shows up in the middle of the door.
1. Defeat the [[Ancient Cyclops]] in [[Ancient's Table]]
1. Return to the Chronomagi Tasker

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Chronomancers**
- Ability to purchse [[Replica: Ancient Cyclops]] for 1p 8g from [[Timeless Trader]]
