## Steps

*The long-forgotten Chamber of Stasis has not been seen by adventurers in ages, and is watched over by ancient creatures. Search through the chamber and confront the strongest creatures found there.*

- Enter the zone [[Ssrez'Ras: Chamber of Stasis (Advanced Solo)]]:
  - Destroy [[Deviator Yothsra (Advanced Solo)|Deviator Yothsra]]
  - Defeat the Twins, [[Nemesis Arcanox (Advanced Solo)|Nemesis Arcanox]] & [[Nemesis Elementox (Advanced Solo)|Nemesis Elementox]]
  - Defeat [[Ziskaya (Advanced Solo)|Ziskaya]]
  - Destroy [[The Guardian Icon (Advanced Solo)|the Guardian]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- [[Draconic Etyma]]
