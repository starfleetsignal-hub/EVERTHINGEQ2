## Steps

1. Scribe the [[Blueprint: Loomba Prototype]] recipe given to you by Oomba.
1. Craft a [[Loomba]] on the Work Bench at {{waypoint 1290, -540.25, 473}}, next to the fuel merchant. Requires one (1) each of Titanium Ore, Quicksilver cluster, Amber, and Ethereal Coal.
1. Activate the [[Loomba]] **in front of** the Sewing Loom near Oomba. {{waypoint 1254.20, -540.25, 429.41}} "looks like a good location".
1. Talk to Oomba.  ![Loomba after it has failed](images/Oomba's_Loomba_quest_-_Failed_Loomba.jpg)

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,000 faction with **Iceclad Pirates**
