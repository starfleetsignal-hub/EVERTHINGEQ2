## Overview

[[Coin of the Eternal Queen]] are part of a new reward system introduced with the launch of [[Terrors of Thalumbra]]. They are given as rewards to various missions.

## How to Earn [[Coin of the Eternal Queen]]

### Requirement

- Level 100? is needed to get a *Normal mission*, a *Daily mission* or a *Weekly mission*
- The 3 mission types exist in solo, advanced solo and heroic versions. They work like independent mission systems.
- You can only have 1 *Weekly mission*.
- You can only have 1 *Daily mission*.
- You can only have 1 *Normal mission* for a given zone.
- You can have a *normal mission* and a *daily mission* for the same zone.
- It is possible to share missions if the above listed requirements are fulfilled and the mission is available from the quest giver at that time.

### Daily Missions

#### [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]] Underland Daily Solo Missions

*These quests are available every 18 hours. If you fail to complete one of these quests, you can try again in one hour. Each Quest is timed, rewards 5000 status and 3 [[Coin of the Eternal Queen]] in addition for doing them the first time you get an achievement and around 30000 status*

#### Instance Daily Missions

- Obtain from:
  - [[Holstin Coresprock]] at {{waypoint 440.15, 134.82, 304.57}} within [[Outer Maldura]] in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]]
- The Daily Missions are offered for an [[Terrors of Thalumbra]] instance.
- Daily Missions reward 3 (advanced solo) or 5 (heroic) [[Coin of the Eternal Queen]] and at least 1p 51g 20s 11300c, depending on the mission. (This quest will likely take the "Daily Double" nickname).
- The weekly quest changes every Wednesday night.<br>
