## Steps

1. Fly to [[Veeshenna Tol]] The southernmost islands in the Vesspyr Isles group {{waypoint -101, 51, 730}} and collect 12 aarumantle
   - Aarumantle looks like sparkling grey stones that twitch slightly
     - **Note:** These are all ground spawn clickies, however some will turn into [[An animated aarumantle|an animated aarumantle]] which will then attack! If this happens, killing it updates the quest as well.
1. Return to Savtek

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
