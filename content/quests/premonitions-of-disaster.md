## Steps

1. Speak to [[Sister Thog MacGilly]] at {{waypoint 52, 148, -57}}.
1. Speak to [[Gert Wolfbane]] at {{waypoint 125, 158, -145}}.
1. Retun to [[Envoy Tami Swifthammer|Tami]].

## Rewards

- One of the following, as applicable to your home city:
  - +250 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +250 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +250 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +250 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
  - +250 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +250 faction with **Ravens of the North**
- One of the Following rare harvestables:
  - [[Alkaline loam]]
  - [[Cured leather pelt]]
  - [[Blackened iron cluster]]
  - [[Rough coral]]
  - [[Severed bone]]
  - [[Silver cluster]]
  - [[Sisal root]]

Note: You can also pick up [[Ironforge Exchange Basic Recipes]] from the table next to Tami now at ( 61, 154, -131 ).
