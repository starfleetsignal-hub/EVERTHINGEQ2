## Steps

1. Kill [[A Tallon grunt|Tallon grunt]]s for an update. {{waypoint 645, -38, 225}}
1. Harvest 20 wild apples from [[Bush Nodes#T4|flattened bed of thistle]] in Zek.
   - Wild apples harvested from the nodes in the [[Enchanted Lands]] also count.
   - You can receive multiple items from a single harvest.
   - Alternatively, buy the apples from the broker.
   - Any previously-harvested apples will do, simply put them on the broker without setting a sales price, then remove them one by one.
1. Visit [[Refuge]] {{waypoint 40, -10, 100}}. To get there, follow the east road past the [[Grove of Stones]], keeping to the right.
1. Speak to Division Supplier Gaertarn.

## Rewards

- Experience only
