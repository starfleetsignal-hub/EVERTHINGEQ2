## Description

Many of the elementals pouring through the fractured Underfoot Seal into The Outer Vault have been corrupted and are unstable and deadly. The most powerful of these is the Corrupted Guardian of the Seal and it is him that I must stop.

## Steps

- I should seek out the [[Corrupted Guardian of the Seal]].
