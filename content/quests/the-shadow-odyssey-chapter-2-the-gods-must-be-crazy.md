## Steps

1. Pursue the missing piece of the clockwork in order to earn the regard of the lizardfolk and hopefully get a chance to examine it
   1. Kill Brokenskull pirates {{waypoint 988, 386, 1058}} until you loot a [[Clockwork Transmission Regulator]]
   1. Hail [[Z-bot XGO]] {{waypoint 1149, 392, 860}}
   1. Approach the entrance to the [[Obelisk of Ahkzul]] {{waypoint 855, 508, 1831}} for the update. DON'T zone in yet! You can be zoned in remotely after getting the update.
   1. Go back and hail [[Professor Fondfate]] {{waypoint 1740, 451, 846}} regarding what you have learned. Evac takes you right to him.
1. I should seek out what I can in the Void Pit. Go back and enter the [[Obelisk of Ahkzul]] and kill the last named mob in the zone, [[Executor Vark]].  You will receive a [[Staff of Theer (Fabled) (Version 2)|Staff of Theer]].
1. Return to [[Dropship Landing Zone]] and hail [[Professor Fondfate]]
