## Steps

1. Hunt the following
   - Hunt frostmane hunters for 8  water-repellent furs
     - *bears on ice shelves above [[Flooded Scar]] around {{waypoint -8, 59, 19}} or on other side around {{waypoint 22, 48, -142}}*
   - Hunt snowmane howlers for 12  sinew - white wolves just
     - *West and below the camp.  Look around {{waypoint -135, 119, -208}}*
1. Return to [[Zorie]] at [[Hoarfang Ridge]] {{waypoint -475, 123, -338}}
