## Prerequisites

This quest series opens up after you've completed "Snappy Trapping" and is needed for the Coldain Shawl quest.

## Notes

The shopping list for the 12 quests in the quest line beginning with this quest is:

- 1 mottled pelt
- 2 quicksilver cluster
- 8 sumac lumber
- 3 titanium ore
- 2 bamboo shoots
- 1 ethereal filament
- 4 ethereal sandpaper

## Steps

1. Search the area for clues by running along the ridge at {{waypoint -1319, -510, -2108}}
1. Return to [[Misa]] {{waypoint -1379.73, -533.55, -2141.43}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
