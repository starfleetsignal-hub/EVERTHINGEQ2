## Starting the Quest

The Quest can be started in both versions of the instance

- [[Maldura: District of Ash (Advanced Solo)|Maldura: District of Ash [Advanced Solo]]]
- [[Maldura: District of Ash (Heroic)|Maldura: District of Ash [Heroic]]]

## Steps

1. Get the Bowl of Water first from inside the District of Ash.
1. Right click to untie [[Shuck]], who joins your group {{waypoint 31.49, 31.62, -149.83}}.
1. Bring the wolf back to her owner, [[Bhardain]], in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]].  Don't let the dog die on the way out! {{waypoint 500.73, 119.57, 256.89}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- +1000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
