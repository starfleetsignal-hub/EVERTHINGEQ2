## Steps

1. Kill [[Princess Cherista]] in [[Castle Mistmoore]]. {{waypoint -20, 81, 124}}
   - Clear the entire room of all mobs (two waves) and there is a chance that Princess Cherista will spawn, or [[A Mistmoore princess]].
1. Click on the wardrobe to complete the quest. {{waypoint -28, 79, 131}}

## Rewards

- [[Flowing Cape of the Dark Lord]] - *The cloak is short, black and red and has a white Mistmoore emblem on it.*
- 58456 status @ level 84 status
- 54077 status @ level 94 status
