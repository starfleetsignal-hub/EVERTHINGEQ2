## Notes

- Faction can be obtained either from tradeskill quests or adventure quests. When you do adventure quests you will reach the required 50,000 faster. However when you reach 50,000 Port Woe faction and still have any not completed Stocking the Wanderer quests in your journal, you will no longer be able to turn them in and can just delete them.
- When accepting quest you receive auto scribed recipes for [[Salted Grayling]] and [[Replacement Crates]]. They will be needed in later faction quests.

## Steps

1. Obtain the Vital Supply Crates
   - purchase [[Vital Supplies]] crate from fuel merchant [[Kassa]] {{waypoint 30.11, 6.27, 361.19}}
     - *This requires +50,000 faction with Port Woe and 5p.*
1. speak with [[Captain Raffik]] at {{waypoint -118.19, 6.61, 399.39}} in the [[Sodden Archipelago]]

## How to achieve +50,000 Faction standing with Port Woe

- **Crafting way**
  - Five new repeatable quests open up to gain faction, from some of the same NPCs as Meet and Greet series. If you are a high level adventurer you can also gain more of that faction by doing adventure signature line quests.
  - Complete the following quests which can be done up to 3 times and give +2,000 Port Woe each.
    - [[Stocking the Wanderer: Crating it Up]] - [[Barsten]] {{waypoint -76.01, 6.69, 362.53}}
    - [[Stocking the Wanderer: Shelter Supplies]] - [[Dunstad]] {{waypoint -75.31, 6.45, 334.38}}
    - [[Stocking the Wanderer: Bundles and Bedding]] - [[Zolea]] {{waypoint -59.67, 9.33, 374.48}}
    - [[Stocking the Wanderer: Harvested Bounty]] - [[Grimar]] {{waypoint -30.84, 5.00, 370.03}}
    - [[Stocking the Wanderer: Salted Fish]] - [[Garron]] {{waypoint -31.17, 5.35, 357.47}}
- **Adventure way**
  - [[Scars of Destruction: Getting to the Gate]] needs 50k faction as well
