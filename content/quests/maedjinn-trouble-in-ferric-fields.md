## Steps

1. The entrance to [[Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields (Solo)|Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields [Solo]]]  can be found on the eastern end of Iron Flats in [[Aether Wroughtlands]].
   - Enter [[Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields (Solo)|Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields [Solo]]] {{waypoint -286.41, 18.51, -574.12}}
1. I must attack the amassing Zakir Rish forces while they remain within Ferric Fields, or else they are certain to wipe out Ihe camp of heroes' in [[Minstrel's Cradle]].
   - *See walkthrough for specific named tips: [[Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields (Solo)|Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields [Solo]]]*
   - Eliminate [[Katakir the Cruel]]
   - Eliminate [[Theya Shen'Safa]]
   - Eliminate [[Metalloid]] (stifles)
   - Eliminate [[Syadun]]
   - Eliminate [[Shanrazad the Spared]]
1. An exit portal will appear on the same platform where you teleported in. Click it to exit [[Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields (Solo)|Aether Wroughtlands: Ferric Fields [Solo]]] and Return to [[Embri]] at the camp in [[Minstrel's Cradle]] at {{waypoint 700, -44, -467}}
