## Notes

This is a sidequest of [[The Adventure of the Celestial Acolyte]].

## Introduction

Bog Valorheart, a scribe and old map collector in North Qeynos, sold one of her old maps to Acolyte Squallrider, just before he skipped town.

## Steps

1. Hunt down 8 [[A Coldwind octopus|Coldwind octopi]] for their inc sacs. You can find them in the waters between the [[Qeynos]] Gates and the [[Oracle Tower]] in [[Antonica]]. Consider starting to look around {{waypoint -1025, -47, -796}}.  There are several under the Norh Qeynos Gate bridge.
1. Return to [[Bog Valorheart]] at the [[Ironforge Exchange]] in [[North Qeynos]].
