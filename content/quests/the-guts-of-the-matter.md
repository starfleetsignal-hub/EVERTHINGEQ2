## Steps

1. Visit locations in the [[Forsaken Othmir Village]]:
   - the ruins of the marketplace {{waypoint 4234, -1087, 1941}}
   - the othmir graveyard {{waypoint 4323, -1087, 2198}}
   - the western alcove {{waypoint 4307, -1081, 1763}}
   - the village overlook {{waypoint 4161, -1057, 2014}}
   - the tunnel entrance {{waypoint 4115, -1082, 2227}}
1. Speak to Merrik
1. Speak to [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)|Osh]] {{waypoint 4267, -1094, 2039}}
1. Kill undead othmir further from camp and loot their bodies for 6 [[Foul entrails]]
1. Smear the 6 entrails around the camp
   - The locations are immediately in front of Yeppa's Last Stand and have a red glow
1. Speak to Merrik

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Othmir Village Bone Brazier]]
