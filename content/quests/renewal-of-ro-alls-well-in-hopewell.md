## Notes

This quest requires completion of [[Seeking Mystic Solutions]] and [[Fight or Fright]] from [[Jawdah the Mystic]]

## Steps

1. Speak with one of the Qayyid workhands, [[Anisha]] at {{waypoint 191.29, -60.10, 571.74}} ![Hakim al-Imam](images/Hakim_al-Imam.png)
1. Search for the Qayyid.
   1. Find [[Hakim al-Imam]] at [[Scorched Shores]] {{waypoint 551.54, -25.09, 53.01}} face down being attacked by [[A vengeful ekimmu]].
   1. Kill the [[A vengeful ekimmu]]
   1. right-click to examine the body.
1. Return to [[Silrena]] at [[Hopewell Harbor]]
1. Find a way to help the Qayyid recover.
   1. Speak to [[Jawdah the Mystic]] {{waypoint 177.44, -60.15, 458.47}}. This step requires completion of his quests (see [[Renewal of Ro: All's Well in Hopewell#Notes|Notes]], above).
   1. Pluck the petals of **Habibti's Kiss** in [[Najami Oasis]]. Possible locations include: ![Habibti's Kiss](images/Habibti's_Kiss.png)
      - {{waypoint -101.84, 24.81, -209.34}}
      - {{waypoint -75.26, 9.51, -214.71}}
      - {{waypoint -74.23, 10.08, -165.19}}
      - {{waypoint -91.22, 20.61, -107.50}}
      - {{waypoint -83.23, 29.22, -103.31}}
      - {{waypoint -82.66, 28.85, -88.12}}
      - {{waypoint -103.23, 29.28, -39.01}}
   1. Speak to [[Azizah the Smith]] {{waypoint 233.08, -59.91, 467.82}} to get the sanctified lead bangles made.
   1. Return to [[Hakim al-Imam]] at {{waypoint 551.54, -25.09, 53.01}} and right-click to fit the bangle on the unconscious Qayyid's wrist.
1. Return to [[Jawdah the Mystic]]
1. Obtain 8 vicious thrasher oil from [[A vicious thrasher|vicious thrashers]] - fish in the waters around [[Scorched Shores]]. Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint 433, -62, 853}}
   - {{waypoint 543.98, -62.69, 484.69}}
   - {{waypoint 688.49, -67.96, 572.50}}
   - {{waypoint 799.87, -71.53, 513.21}}
1. Return to [[Jawdah the Mystic]]
1. Go to [[Goldami Reservoir Camp]] {{waypoint -446.76, -17.00, 501.76}}
1. Go to [[Nerive Reservoir Camp]] {{waypoint -318.97, -16.63, 139.17}}
1. Defeat 12 attacking [[A Raj'Dur ambusher|Raj'Dur ambushers]]
1. Speak with [[A recent victim]] {{waypoint -400.45, -6.62, 179.13}}
1. Return to [[Jawdah the Mystic]]
