## Steps

1. Gather some seedlings from the mud right next to [[Marblefist]]
1. Plant some seedlings, use the [[Wakening Lands Seedling]] to plant em
   1. Plant a seedling at the Drying riverbed near [[Kael Drakkel]], for example at {{waypoint 608, -476, 3789}}
   1. Plant a seedling in the Thrael'Gorr mining pit, for example at {{waypoint 576, -494, 3914}}
   1. Plant a seedling near the old tree of growth, for example at {{waypoint 637, -445, 4123}}
   1. Plant a seedling at the southern most end of the region, for example at {{waypoint 610, -482, 4260}}
1. return to [[Marblefist]]
