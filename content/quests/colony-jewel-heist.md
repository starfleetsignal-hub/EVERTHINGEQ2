## Steps

1. Hunt [[A frenzied sandcrawler]] (8) fire ants on the Blister Ant Flats {{waypoint 236, 157, -202}} until guard ants appear
1. Hunt [[A sandcrawler guard]] ants (5) until royal ants appear
1. Hunt [[A sandcrawler royal guard]] ants (3) until a young sandcrawler queen ant appears
1. Hunt [[A young sandcrawler queen]] ant
1. Return to [[Eleana Stormalong]] at {{waypoint 289.67, 283.12, -734.58}}

## Rewards

- [[Ulteran Spikewolf Plushie]]
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
