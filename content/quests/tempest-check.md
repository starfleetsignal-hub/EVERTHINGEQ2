## Prerequisites

- This is a subquest to [[The Adventure of the Undercover Disciple]].

## Introduction

Kaarina Nightbloom, an old fae mystic in Greater Faydark's Spire Shadow Outpost, very cryptically told me that there is something happening with or around the Void Tempests in Norrath.

## Steps

1. Find a dead Concordium Pupil next to a Void Storm, the nearest is in [[Steamfont Mountains]] near the [[Hookable Rock]]. {{waypoint -350, 100, 945}} Lower level players can check the Void Storm at [[The Nursery]] in [[Greater Faydark]].
1. Return to [[Kaarina Nightbloom]] at the [[Spire Shadow Outpost]] in [[Greater Faydark]].
