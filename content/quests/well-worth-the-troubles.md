## Steps

1. Search for [[Nita Sparkswain]] in [[Cardin Ward]] {{waypoint -319.51, 14.69, 10.25}}
1. Click the book on the floor or any of the knocked-over furniture in the vicinity at {{waypoint -319.42, 14.69, 12.88}}
1. Rescue [[Nita Sparkswain]] from the Wayward camp at {{waypoint -383, -8, -251}}
1. Kill 12 [[A Wayward thaumaturge|Wayward thaumaturges]] and [[A Wayward heretic|heretics]].
1. Return to [[K'rin Valdim]] to complete the quest.

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Large Wayward Chest]]
- [[Thurible of Siphoning Flow]]
