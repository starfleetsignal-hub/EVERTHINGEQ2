## Note

You must be level 87 to get this quest.

## Steps

- Dran wants you to place false barrels of gunpowder to fool suspected saboteurs.
1. Place the fake barrels of gunpowder
   - Click the stacks of barrels at {{waypoint 762, -392, 722}}, {{waypoint 788, -398, 753}}, and {{waypoint 1050, -425, 764}}
1. Return to Dran Thurgadin docks.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +500 faction with **The City of Thurgadin**
