## Steps

1. Use [[Embri's Maedjinn Illusion]]
1. The magical mirrors stationed at the different Zakir Rish strongholds in [[Aether Wroughtlands]] are easier to access with the use of [[Embri's Maedjinn Illusion]]
   - Destroy 6 [[Iron Zakir Rish mirrors]] SOME possible locs include:
     - {{waypoint 4.69, -13.81, -454.29}}
     - {{waypoint 59.90, 37.81, -666.23}}
     - {{waypoint 43.75, 37.82, -656.13}}
     - {{waypoint 21.71, 37.54, -731.75}}
     - {{waypoint 39.00, 37.54, -755.32}}
     - {{waypoint -29.82, 43.48, -705.32}}
   - Destroy 6 [[Copper Zakir Rish mirrors]] SOME possible locs include:
     - {{waypoint -715.95, 58.67, -441.29}}
     - {{waypoint -786.38, 54.57, -483.57}}
     - {{waypoint -826.87, 54.07, -485.77}}
     - {{waypoint -819.62, 53.99, -441.79}}
     - {{waypoint -814.87, 53.98, -378.09}}
     - {{waypoint -779.77, 54.12, -333.87}}
   - Steal [[A silver Zakir Rish mirror]]
     - {{waypoint -721.29, 137.74, 339.69}}
1. Return to speak [[Embri]] at [[Minstrel's Cradle]] {{waypoint 699.89, -43.70, -466.97}}
