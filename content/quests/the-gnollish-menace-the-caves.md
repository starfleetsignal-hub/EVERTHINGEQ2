## Steps

1. Kill the following monster within The Caves
   - 30 Gnolls
   - 2 [[A Dustpaw armorer|Dustpaw armorers]] {{waypoint 55, 13, -75}}
   - 5 [[An alabaster behemoth|alabaster behemoths]] {{waypoint -42, 39, -127}}
   - 5 [[A Rockpaw guard|Rockpaw guards]] {{waypoint 56, 12, -85}}
     - **Note:** The Dustpaw armorers and Rockpaw guards count towards the 30 gnolls.
1. Return to [[Lieutenant Delsun]] for your reward
