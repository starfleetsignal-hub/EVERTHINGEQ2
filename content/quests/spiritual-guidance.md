## Steps

**NOTE:** the 'How to Start' information regarding the 'diary next to Madyl D'Lareth's feet' is the trigger for a different quest - The Writings of an Unkempt Druid.  Spiritual Guidance is started, as expected, by clicking on the NPC with the feather.

1. Swim out to sea to {{waypoint -96, -7, 742}}. A platform appears under you with Elementals around.
1. Kill elementals until one will talk to you.
   - It's better to single pull them, AoE pulling can cause them to become invisible and continue attacking you.
   - The update after killing the elementals is group-wide, **HOWEVER, the teleportation is NOT**.  Make sure each group member clicks the elemental at the same time, or the straggler(s) will need to delete and re-kick the quest.
1. You will be teleported to {{waypoint 225.55, 73.00, 1140.74}}. Click on the large jaw floating in the sky.
1. Return to [[Madyl D'Lareth]].
