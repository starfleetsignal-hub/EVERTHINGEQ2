After killing an ice maiden you notice that the feathers adorning her lithe wings are soft and downy. The feathers would make a rather comfortable pillow. Perhaps if you collected enough to fill a sack you could trade the bulk lot to an interested party.

## Steps

1. You should pluck 20 feathers off of ice maidens. These are one-per-person chest drops from ice maiden encounters in the zone.
1. Turn in is Kaan Frostfoot {{waypoint 70, 19.5, -114}}

## Rewards

- At least 8p
- 21440 status
- 10 tokens [[Epic Token of E'ci]]
- One of the Following:
  - [[Debitino's Flask]]
  - [[Ice Maiden Statuette]]
  - [[Corrival's Gambling Dice]]
  - [[Nicoli's Toothpick]]
