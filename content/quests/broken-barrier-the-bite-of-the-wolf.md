## Steps

1. Locate the materials required to reforge the [[Bite of the Wolf (Fabled)|Bite of the Wolf]]:
   - woven natural sinew - purchased [[Bulgup Urple]] {{waypoint 474, -97, 906}}, the Synod Reet faction merchant, in [[Kunzar Jungle]]. Requires Ally (+40k) faction and 20 loose natural sinew to buy. The loose natural sinew is a random drop off [[Kunzar Jungle]] mobs and can drop in increments from 0 up to 8 on a single mob.
   - a mechanically precise balancing core - drops from [[Gear of the Ancients]] in [[Charasis: Maiden's Chamber]]
   - a copy of the ritual scroll - drops from [[Leviathor'Consuma]] (one of the wurms) in [[Chelsith]]
1. Return to [[Tilzax V'Reryn]].
