## Steps

1. Travel to [[Everfrost]] and collect the following:
   - Collect 4 Love's Blooms on an ice-floe in [[Everstorm Bay]] {{waypoint 31.90, -16.79, 13.23}}![Love's Blooms](images/Love's_Blooms.webp)
   - Collect 4 Bitterwind Sweets at [[Bitterwind Pioneer's Encampment]] {{waypoint 455.30, -0.69, -200.02}}![Bitterwind Sweets](images/Bitterwind_Sweets.webp)* Located West of Bitterwind Trench Station.
   - Collect 4 Snowsworn Pillow at the [[Snowsworn's Hunter's Outpost]] {{waypoint -274.92, 3.98, -253.16}}![Snowsworn Pillow](images/Snowsworn_Pillow.webp)* Located South-East of Hollowfrost Grove Station.
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]]
