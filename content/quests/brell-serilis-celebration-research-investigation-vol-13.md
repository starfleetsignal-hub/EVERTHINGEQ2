## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Brell Serilis has informed me that summoning an Avatar requires the land to be attuned to their element. To achieve this, altars have been set up around the region, but they need to be supplied with power. I have been asked to assist with this by collecting objects infused with the essence of slain foes and offer them to an altar.

1. Collect 3 [[Infused Sable Clay|infused sable clay]] (random body drop from a lot of mobs in [[Aether Wroughtlands]] - confirmed from vicious lavafang, firespine gnawer, a dune prowler)
1. Use the [[Infused Sable Clay|infused sable clay]] and empower an altar.  Random spawn in Butcherblock, its yellow sparkles visible far away, possible locations of altar:
   - {{waypoint -254, 147, 303}} near The Broken Fields
   - {{waypoint -584, 110, 281}} near the Three Roads Outpost and griffon station
   - {{waypoint 242, 189, 430}} in The Highlands, close to the camps and griffon station
   - {{waypoint -390, 151, 42}} in The Quarry, south of the hole
   - {{waypoint -762, 105, 24}} next to the southern throne at The Chessboard
   - ( -995, 100, -450)  next to Greater Faydark Station
   - ( -490, 150, -440) on the path east from Kaladim.
   - ( -465, 138, 607) next to the sign "The Estate of Unrest"
   - (-145, 208, -109) Just west of Herald.
1. Return to [[Herald to the Duke of Below]] {{waypoint -241, 212, 56}} in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
