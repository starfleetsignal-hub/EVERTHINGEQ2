## Prerequisites

- You must have at least +3200 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

1. Gather 8 *magmatic crystals* from along the lava flows in [[Skyfire Mountains]]. Known locations are:
   - {{waypoint -794, 235, 437}}
   - {{waypoint -784, 235, 433}}
   - {{waypoint -846, 233, 445}}
   - {{waypoint -1530, 187, 1222}}
   - {{waypoint -1585, 185, 1198}}
   - {{waypoint -1907, 170, 1075}}
   - {{waypoint -1920, 168, 1061}}
   - {{waypoint -2008, 174, 1058}}
   - {{waypoint -2024, 171, 1090}}
   - {{waypoint -1258, 229, 1210}}
   - {{waypoint -1214, 222, 1446}}
   - {{waypoint -1918, 167, 1124}}
1. Deposit the items in the cart. {{waypoint 1023, -40, -25}}
1. Speak with [[Pylemea Stoerki]].

- At least 15g
- +2400 faction with **The Order of Rime**
