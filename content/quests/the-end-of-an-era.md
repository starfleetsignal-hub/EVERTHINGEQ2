## Steps

1. Buy [[Osh's Bonfire Wood]] from [[Chitter]] {{waypoint -1598, -532, -2418}}
1. Place the wood in the sacred circle at {{waypoint -1593, -539, -2366}}
1. Light the bonfire
1. Return to Osh
1. Place Nipik's Shrine at {{waypoint -1576, -536, -2384}}
1. Speak to [[Osh]] once more

- At least 1p 15g
- +300 faction with **Othmir of Velious**
- One of the Following:

  - [[Sacred Shawl of the Othmir Shaman]]
  - [[Gallant Scarf of the Othmir Patroller]]
