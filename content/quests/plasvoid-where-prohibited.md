## Notes

- Warning: Water in Drekkna's Deluge area does damage<br>
- **Kill all trash around named mobs before you engage them** or they will all come to aid. With each add that's not killed, charmed, feared, mesmerized or stunned the named mob gets 1 increment on a buff, that at 10 increments instantly kills you.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Zon Zobboz: The Oculeum (Solo)]] portal near [[Grusla]]
1. [[Grusla]] has entrusted me with the destruction of plasvoid energy cases and the disposal of the unnatural netherspawn scavengers throughout the Oculeum:
- destroy 8 x plasvoid energy cases
  - possible locations: {{waypoint 212, -168, 668}}, {{waypoint 245, -171, 659}}, {{waypoint 78, -228, 352}}, {{waypoint -10, -223, 362}}, {{waypoint 205, -212, 303}}, {{waypoint 176, -212, 276}}, {{waypoint -78, -190, 172}}, {{waypoint -126, -185, 140}}
- dispose of 8 x netherspawn scavengers - these come out of the ground, hang around a few seconds and go underground again.
- make sure you pick up all plasvoid energy cases and kill 8 scavengers **BEFORE** you teleport to next area and kill the 3 gazers.
1. Reach the belly  of the Oculeum - click the eye at {{waypoint -126, -174, 7}} to teleport to the next area.
1. Slay Fydoon, Pruul and Cootatu, three Zon Zobboz Syndicate masters.

Quest automatically completes at this point, if you wish you can keep going to complete the zone for daily mission or loot.
