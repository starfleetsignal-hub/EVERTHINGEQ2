## Starting the Quest

1. Right click the [[Disused manacles]] in your bags and concentrate on them to spawn [[Eiodolon of Heroism]]
1. Speak with [[Eidolon of Heroism]] to receive next quest.

## Steps

1. Find the "pretender of the throne."
   - Kill [[Queen Meacidaris]] in [[The Fractured Hive (Heroic)]]. ([[Vesspyr Isles]])  {{waypoint -407, 1, 644}}
1. Use [[Disused manacles]] in your inventory to summon [[Eidolon of Heroism]] to complete the quest and receive the next one.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
