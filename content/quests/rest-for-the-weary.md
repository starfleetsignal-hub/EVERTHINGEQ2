## Steps

1. Hail Quinn and click on the chest that appears next to her to get the the special supplies for this quest.
1. Scribe the received recipe book, [[Sturdy Bunk Bed Assembly Directions]]
1. Craft 20 [[Sturdy bunk beds]] for the people of [[Shadowed Cleft]] at the woodworking table, located in the same room as Quinn.
   - 1 [[Metal joint|metal joints]] (received from [[Quinn Wobble|Quinn]] or if you run out from a box at her feet)
   - 2 [[Redwood lumber]]
   - 2 [[Ferrite cluster|ferrite clusters]]
   - 5 [[Smoldering sandpaper]]
   - 1-4 [[Sturdy bunk beds|beds]] per combine
1. Return to [[Quinn Wobble|Quinn]]

- Note: Though this is a level 70 tradeskill combine, using geomancy skill (at a woodworking table), the reaction arts needed to successfully complete this are scaled lower, to allow level 50 crafters of any tradeskill class to complete the task.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- At least 80g 48s 18c
- +750 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
