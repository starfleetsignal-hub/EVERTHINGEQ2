## Steps

1. Plant 10 pieces of armor to confuse the droags around {{waypoint -1573, 205, 332}}.
   - When you are in the right location, you will receive a red status message saying, "I should look around for the location to cast aside the incriminating droag armor." The specific location to plant the armor is represented by a green shiny.
1. Return to [[Scout Nan'Dor]].

## Rewards

- At least 12g 82s 30c
- [[Steelbark Wristwrap]]
