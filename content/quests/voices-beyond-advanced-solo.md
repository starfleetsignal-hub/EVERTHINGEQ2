## Description

*Faint voices can be heard emerging from the deepest recesses of Kralet Penumbra, promising those who would listen power beyond imagining. Unfortunately, those who fall prey are never seen from again. Follow the voices, and bring death to the speaker.*

## Zone

- Defeat targets within [[Kralet Penumbra: Rise to Power (Advanced Solo)]]

## Steps

- Eradicate the temple protector
- Kill the protector
- Destroy the guardian
- Kill the listener
- Silence the one who speaks

## Rewards

- At least 1p 51g 20s 11,300 status
- 3 [[Coin of the Eternal Queen]]
