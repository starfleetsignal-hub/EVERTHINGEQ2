   - Volume II +123 -11 -13  (on shelf in Dracoratory, on ledge of top floor, where the Frankenstein-droag is)
   - Volume III -10 -69 +284  (first floor, Sothis' room, behind Sothis)
   - Volume IV +6 -69 +360  (1st floor on the way out of sothis' room, SW corner)
   - Volume V {{waypoint -52, -58, -112}}  (In the back of the room with Ishi-Kurat)
   - Volume VI -129 -67 +446  (INSIDE Septimus' room; fight him in room or you will be locked out - GET Volume IX at the same time!)
   - Volume VII -15 -68 +463  (in couldron room)

   - Volume IX -92 -68 +475  (INSIDE Septimus' room; fight him in room or you will be locked out - GET Volume VI at the same time!)
   - Volume X -4 -69 +274  (Sothis' room, in alcove behind sothis
1. Find 10 prismatic lexicons in [[The Laboratory of Lord Vyemm]]:
   - Volume XI -173 -81 +520  (Down the stairs in the 2nd small room after zoning in)
   - Volume XII -85 -93 +541  (In SW corner of [[The Lower Garrison]], near [[The Slavering Alzid]])
   - Volume XIII -97 -93 +688  (Down south passage from [[The Lower Garrison]], same room as Doom trio)
   - Volume XIV -6 -106 +703  (South corner of anteroom of chamber with [[The Uncaged Alzid]])
   - Volume XV +74 -91 +677  (At top of ramp leaving Uncaged's room, just over the edge)
   - Volume XVI +81 -107 +652  (North corner of antechamber on other side of the Uncaged's room)
   - Volume XVII +182 -134 +342  (On north side of Vyemm's chamber, lower level, near Alzid Prime)
   - Volume XVIII +211 -116 +293  (North side of last room, top level)
   - Volume XIX +206 -141 +438  (On right side under stairs leading to Vyemm's room, with Euktrzkai and Rallia)
   - Volume XX +58 -107 +693  (west side of [[The Uncaged Alzid]] chamber)
