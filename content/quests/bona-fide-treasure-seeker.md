## Note

- You can combine this quest with [[Visions of Vetrovia: Evil Dedraka]], They both require Dedraka's Descent Solo. *(if you haven't already completed it)*

## Steps

1. Enter [[Karuupa Jungle: Dedraka's Descent (Solo)|Karuupa Jungle: Dedraka's Descent [Solo]]] at {{waypoint -228, 109, -679}} and speak with Findink. He's just inside the zone-in at {{waypoint -606, 162, -586}}.
1. Gather the following
   - 11 bones of Findink's departed shipmates. Some possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -476, 192, -192}}, {{waypoint -497, 188, -151}}, {{waypoint -589, 176, -93}}, {{waypoint -555, 180, -97}}, {{waypoint -429, 113, -556}}
     - {{waypoint -537, 157, -562}}, {{waypoint -501, 157, -582}}, {{waypoint -502, 157, -553}}, {{waypoint -546, 186, -293}}, {{waypoint -405, 130, -317}}
     - {{waypoint -318, 119, -401}}, {{waypoint -249, 119, -343}}, {{waypoint -537, 156, -563}}, {{waypoint -541, 186, -250}}, {{waypoint -298, 119, -396}}
     - {{waypoint -378, 116, -460}}
   - Find the skull of Ole Swilly
     - Kill [[Cannibrea]] (auto update, skull drops off named). **((Do not leave the zone yet!))**
1. Return to [[Findink Topgallant]] {{waypoint -606, 162, -586}} in [[Karuupa Jungle: Dedraka's Descent (Solo)|Karuupa Jungle: Dedraka's Descent [Solo]]].
   - **He has moved to a platform just above the entrance**.
   - ***alternative: kill the last named of the zone, zone out at the end of the instance and back in to speak with him***

  - [[Cohort's Cloth Slippers of Foresight]]
  - [[Cohort's Leather Boots of Foresight]]
