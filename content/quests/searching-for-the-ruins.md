*This quest is part of the [[Freeport Outlying Areas Timeline]]*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Captain Arellius]] in the Ruins on the dock at {{waypoint -160, 0, 80}} to finish the quest.

## Rewards

- +150 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
- +150 faction with **The Dismal Rage**

## Notes

- If you continue the conversation with Captain Arellius you will eventually get 2 choices. It is possible to receive both quests by talking to him again after receiving the first one.
  1. **Yes, I would like to travel to the Commonlands**
     - The Captain sends to you [[Mooshga]] in the [[Commonlands Timeline]] at the Freeport Gates
  1. **No, I'll stay here**
     - The Captain sends you to speak with [[Lieutenant Argosian]] just inside [[The Ruins]]. See [[Reporting for Duty]] in the [[Freeport Districts Timeline#The Ruins series|The Ruins series of the Freeport Districts Timeline]].
