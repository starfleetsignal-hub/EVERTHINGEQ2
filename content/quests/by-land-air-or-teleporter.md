## Steps

1. Go to the City of Paineel
1. Visit the following locations
   - Visit the western lift, behind the Hall of Wizardry. {{waypoint 2050, -300, 3269}}
   - Visit the eastern lift, near the Mercantile Court. {{waypoint 1734, -283, 3337}}
   - Visit the launch pad. {{waypoint 2020, -190, 3136}}
   - Visit the airship dock. {{waypoint 1870, -225, 3003}}
1. Return to [[Felice Adae]] on [[Spire Island]].

## Rewards

- At least 70g
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
