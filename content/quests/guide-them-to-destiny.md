## Steps

1. [[Felidael]] has asked me to battle Kejekan spirits once more. He has asked me to use the [[Feral Spirit Talisman]] upon their spirits before I completely banish them from the world. Near  {{waypoint -1050, 535, 584}}
   - **Use** the Talisman on the spirits once they have reached 25% health.
   - *Note: you need to stand within melee range.*
1. Return to Felidael.
