## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- You can initiate this quest without doing the rest in the series, but you will receive no reward unless you have done the previous quests.
- If you have completed this quest before progressing to it in the [[Claymore Timeline]], you can repeat it for credit.
- Another location to find chest is -75.71, 157.84, -89.74

## Steps

1. Kill [[Orate Bozar]] and his two [[An Awakened spiritualist|Awakened spiritualists]]. {{waypoint 176, 157, -80}}
   - Orate Bozar's placeholder does not give a quest update.
1. Find the Apparition Chest again and open it to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- [[Tome of Animatus]] - examine to start next quest, [[Soul Survivor]]
