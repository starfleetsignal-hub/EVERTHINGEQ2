## Steps

1. Click the bone behind Yeppa {{waypoint 4262, -1087, 2038}}
1. Investigate the scrapings:
   - Examine a [[Corrupted Bone Shard]] in your inventory.
   - Scribe [[Notes: Bone Shard Investigation]].
   - Craft [[Investigated Bone Shard]] at the fire {{waypoint 4270, -1092, 2033}}. Requires 1 [[Amethyst]], 1 [[Effulgent coal]] and the bone shard.
1. Speak to Yeppa.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
