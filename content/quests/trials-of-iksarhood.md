## Notes

- This is a subquest of [[Bring Him Bax]].

## Steps

1. Right-click Bax and select "Signal to Bax".
1. Lead Bax to [[Yellowfang]] and kill it when it aggros. {{waypoint 224, 61, -569}}
1. Speak to Bax.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 10g
- +2,000 faction with **Kunzar Jungle Villagers**
- [[Glowing Gem of Casting Haste]]
