## Steps

![](images/A_follower_of_Wu_%28Wood_Elf%29.png)

1. Go to the Wu camp and find a gray-haired, female [[A follower of Wu (Wood Elf)|follower of Wu]] {{waypoint 802, 110, -302}} - see picture to the right. Hail her and tell her "Friendship is an opportunity for manipulation".
1. Finish the dialog.  She will change into [[Lynn Q'Tentu]] (60^^^ Heroic).
   - Note: If you select the wrong choice, she will despawn and respawn within 2 minutes.
1. Kill her.  You will automatically receive [[A strange looking rune]].
1. Return to Sharina and show her the rune.

- +500 faction with **Nybright Family**
