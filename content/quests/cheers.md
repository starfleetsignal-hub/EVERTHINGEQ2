## Steps

1. Speak to [[Mav Boilfist]]
1. Collect Mav's brew from the keg next to her.
1. Give sampels to an number of people around [[Graystone Yard]]
   - [[Gnor Farven]] found in the tavern.
   - [[Gurna Hadel]] found in the tavern.
   - [[Dunda Frostgrip]] found southwest of the scribe's shop.
1. Return to [[Mav Boilfist]]
