## Description

The Libant researchers have taken on the macabre feat of attempting to create a distilled libation from human and elf vitae. The League of Freethinkers has asked that I contaminate all the prototype libation distilleries found inside Ravenscale Repository. Additionally, I must destroy the creator of the distilleries.

## Steps

![A vitae distillery](images/Vitae_distillery.jpg)

1. Contaminate 6 vitae distilleries found inside [[Ravenscale Repository]]; they look like small chemistry sets on the floor, tables or work benches. Possible locations:
   - [[Laborum of the Coalescent]] (west)
     - {{waypoint 37, 24, -202}}
     - {{waypoint 60, 24, -182}}
     - {{waypoint 60, 24, -125}}
   - Laborum hallway ([[Librarum of Secrets]])
     - {{waypoint -2, 24, -133}}
     - {{waypoint -7, 24, -133}}
   - Laborum of the Coalescent (east)
     - {{waypoint -65, 24, -211}}
     - {{waypoint -40, 24, -212}}
   - [[Reliquary of the Drachnid]]
     - {{waypoint -153, 14, -175}}
     - {{waypoint -164, 14, -191}}
   - [[Reliquary of the Hunter]]
     - {{waypoint -127, 14, -220}}
     - {{waypoint -131, 14, -246}}
   - [[Reliquary of the Drelock]]
     - {{waypoint 163, 14, -184}}
   - [[Reliquary of the Libant]]
     - {{waypoint 109, 14, -223}}
     - {{waypoint 120, 14, -225}}
   - [[Theatre of the Diabolist]]
     - {{waypoint 15, 4, -301}}
     - {{waypoint 17, 16, -343}}
1. Kill the creator of the distilleries, [[Noyle the Essence-Weaver]], in [[Reliquary of the Drelock]] {{waypoint 158, 16, -199}}
1. Return to Tanya Alvernon in Somborn
