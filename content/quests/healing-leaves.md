## Prerequisites

- You must be a level 85+ crafter in order to begin this crafting questline.

## Steps

1. Exit [[Thurgadin]] via the back door at {{waypoint 674, -246, 57}}.
1. Collect 4 [[Spiceleaf Herb|Spiceleaf Herbs]] from fern-like plants (Spiceleaf Bush) just outside the rear entrance of [[Thurgadin]] along the river banks.
1. Return to Eirik {{waypoint 866, -274, 135}} inside [[Thurgadin]] to complete the quest.
- NOTE: You may be able to harvest the Spiceleaf Bush without having this quest but it won't count.

## Rewards

- [[Coldain Chalice]]
- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
