## Steps

1. Fly to [[The Isle of Awakening]] and visit the [[Pool of the Awakened]]. {{waypoint 207, 199, 451}}
1. Fly to [[The Isle of Desolation]] and speak to [[Ohzo Dreadful]]. {{waypoint 330, 0, -690}}
1. Go to [[Tenebrous Tangle]] and jump to the base of the falls in the [[Hidden Refuge (Tenebrous Tangle)|Hidden Refuge]]. You will get a quest update in the water at the bottom. {{waypoint 100, -216, 161}}
1. Return to Ohzo Dreadful for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 4g 80s 10c
- [[A loose feather]] - *inspect this to get the next quest*
