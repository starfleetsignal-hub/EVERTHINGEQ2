## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 60+

This quest begins a series of quests starting with [[Lilly Ironforge]], found at [[The Jewel Box]] in North Qeynos. At the same time, you will receive a letter in the mail from Queen Antonia, summoning you to the castle. After you speak with the Queen, it begin the quest [[The Price of the Crown]]. These two quest lines run in parallel with one another, and eventually converge with the quest [[The Eyes Have It]].

It does not matter who's quests you do first, Lilly Ironforge's or Queen Antonia's, but you will need to complete both series of quests to proceed further into the quest timeline.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Lilly Ironforge]] at [[The Jewel Box]] in [[North Qeynos]] in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 386, -19, -243}}.

- At least 18g 82s 38c (at level 61)
- At least 28g 71s 62c (at level 92)
