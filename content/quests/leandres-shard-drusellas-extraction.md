1. Kill [[Drusella Sathir]] in [[Charasis: Maiden's Chamber]]
1. Speak to [[Leandre]] by using the [[An incandescent wand]] or by targeting yourself and hail (press H by default)

STOP!
If your goal is to have the Item for the [[Epic Conversion Timeline]], you do NOT need to do the RAID version. You can begin that timeline now.
