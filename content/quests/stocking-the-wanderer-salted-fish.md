## Notes

This is a faction quest, it's repeatable up to 3 times and available **only when you have between 20,000 and 50,000 [[Port Woe]] faction** and have started [[Captain Raffik]]'s quest [[Stealthy Supplies: The Crafty Wanderer]]. If your faction with Port Woe is already at 50,000 you cannot pick it up nor turn it in anymore. You can go buy Vital Supplies from [[Kassa]] to progress main crafting questline.

This quest requires:

- 6 [[Rock Salt]] harvested during quest
- 12 [[Speckled Grayling]]
- 30 [[Zimaran Kindling]]

## Steps

1. Harvest materials
   - 6 [[Rock Salt]] {{waypoint -39.52, 4.74, 549.67}}
   - 6 [[Speckled Grayling]] from fish nodes
   - Bug Warning?: Recipe requires 12 [[Speckled Grayling]]
1. Craft 6 [[Salted Grayling]], recipe auto scribed earlier after accepting quest from Captain Raffik (Mass production does not work)
1. Speak with [[Garron]] at {{waypoint -31.74, 5.00, 358.16}}

## Rewards

- +2,000 faction with **Port Woe**
- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
