## Steps

1. Speak with [[Maareona Ludimintium]], she can be found at the top of the stairs leading from the docks.
1. Speak with [[Cayan Sable]], click the tipped bench {{waypoint 767, -24, 319}} behind the north western corner of the armor shop to be ported to his location.
1. Collect 10 Red Sun Berries from bushes around [[Starcrest Commune]].
1. Return to [[Cayan Sable]].

## Rewards

- At least 1s
- [[Courier's pouch]]
