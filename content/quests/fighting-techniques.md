## Steps

1. Take the nearby elevator up to the [[Erudition Aviary]] and examine 'a loose scroll' at the following locations:
   - "Talon to Talon" {{waypoint 132.85, 103.17, 9.56}}
   - "Wind Exploitation" {{waypoint 177.89, 103.17, 34.88}}
   - "Wings, Flights and Formations" {{waypoint 186.72, 103.17, 31.01}}
   - "Land Hopping and Bypassing Future Objectives" {{waypoint 186.15, 103.17, -32.44}}
1. Bring the information to [[Gaer Torrent]] in [[The Barren Sky]] on [[The Whisperwind Isle]], near the teleporter to Norrath. {{waypoint -578.37, -46.41, -386.06}}

## Rewards

- At least 13g 85s 15c
- +50 faction with **The Windgazer**
