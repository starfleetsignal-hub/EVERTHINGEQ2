## Notes

*A new tradeskill mission is available every 18 hours.*

## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 15 [[Celestial Filament]]
- Note: You will harvest the other needed materials as part of the quest.

## Steps

1. Harvest the following within [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]]:
   - 25 [[Nimbic hide]] from [[Basilisk lair]]
   - 10 [[Strangler root]] from [[Strangler root break]]
   - 10 [[Reef cluster]] from [[Coalesced reef]]
1. Craft an [[Explorer's Fanny Pack]] at a [[Sewing Table & Mannequin|Sewing Table]]
   - There is a [[Sewing Table & Mannequin|Sewing Table]] in the [[Crafter's Gallery]] of the Library at {{waypoint 900.90, 411.68, 52.30}}.
1. Speak with [[Temry Loneivy]] at {{waypoint 25.57, -20.10, -71.06}} in the [[Myrist Conservatory]]

## Rewards

- At least 48000 status
- [[Elemental Mission Reward Crate]]
