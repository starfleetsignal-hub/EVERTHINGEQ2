## Steps

1. Speak with [[Gurb the Master Weaponsmith]] in [[The City of Freeport]] at [[West Freeport (POI)|West Freeport]] {{waypoint 214.81, -3.66, 100.51}}.
   - Get 50 [[Ferrite cluster|ferrite clusters]] before moving on to the next step, otherwise you will have to redo it when you have the clusters
1. Speak with [[A Dervish craftsman]] in the Dervish camp in [[The Commonlands]] {{waypoint 1410, -17, 280}}
1. Head back to [[Juggernaut Gymnasium]] in [[West Freeport (POI)|West Freeport]] {{waypoint 253, -3, 76}} and speak with [[The Big Boss (Juggernaut Gymnasium)|The Big Boss]].
1. Head to [[Jarsath Wastes]] and speak with [[Borga Rallok]] in the [[Skyfire Mountains]] {{waypoint -2345, 230, 1130}} near the Temple of the White Lady post
   1. Speak with him again when you are ready to fight and he will spawn three level 83 heroic mobs ([[An iksar spirit soldier]]) that are a single group and all come together.
   1. Kill the spirit soldiers then speak to [[Borga Rallok]] again
1. Head to [[Chardok]] and make your way down deep into the Chardok Palace to find the four giant stones![](images/BruiserGiantStone.jpg)
   1. First stone: {{waypoint 906, -66, 545}} (on the floor)
      - The first three stones are found in the hidden library section of the Palace.  To get into this section, you need to collect three encyclopedia books from the two rooms in the upstairs sections of the palace. {{waypoint 857, 0, 244}}.  They are located on top of the bookshelves in the rooms on either side of the hallway. There are 3 non Agroible books that attack if the A Gorowyn Hierophant attacks you. Once you have the three books, head downstairs.  Open the hidden wall by clicking on the lever behind a Gorowyn palace adjutant (or it's named equivalent).  Hail [[Re'Adon]].  He will open up a hidden section of wall.  Follow him down past two hidden walls then continue until you enter a circular room with no exit.  This is the location of the first giant stone.
   1. Second stone: {{waypoint 870, -78, 602}} (Left room)
      - Next, look for a clickable book on the first shelf called Passage to Oblivion.  It can appear on any of the bookshelves.  Click it to open another hidden passage.  At the bottom of this passage there will be a room to the left and right.  The second and third giant stones are found in these rooms on the first shelf of the bookshelves, lying flat.
   1. Third stone: {{waypoint 971, -78, 605}} (Right room)
      - The third giant stone is in the right room, across from the second stone.
   1. Fourth stone: {{waypoint 1083, -50, 80}} (standing against the wall)
      - The last stone is found in the room to the north of the Queen's Chamber behind a golem.  Make you way out of the library and head west into a room with no exit.  At the back of the room will be a lever to pull, which opens a section of wall to the north.  Head north past the Queen's Chamber (which is to the west).  In the northernmost room, you will find the giant stone.
1. Next, track down the Guardians of the Four Arms of Gorynn and take their amulets. (*can be done in any order*)
   - **"a powerful man that lives around furry elephants"**
     - You will need 5 Fiery Magma Infusions for this step. These can be bought on the broker for a reasonable amount of gold.  If none are for sale you can follow this step below:
       - These are made by provisioners through a recipe sold at the [[Danak Shipyard]] by [[Eviss Ryiss]] <Danak Quarter Master> at location:{{waypoint -102, -41, 1108}}.  <s>The recipe is no trade, so the provisioner must purchase it themselves.</s>  (As of 10/09/2024 the "Sathirian Magma Recipes" scroll is < Heirloom Novalue > and NOT Notrade.) The recipe is a Level 75 Provisioner Recipe. If you are not a Provisioner, you will need one and they will need to obtain the recipe themselves.  *An ALT character who is a provisioner can be given the recipe since it is heirloom.* Appropriate faction with The Legion of Danak is needed to buy from Eviss Ryiss the Quarter Master.  The Legion of Danak Faction Questline begins with "Head or Tales" given by Agent Zhatia {{waypoint 386, 5, -350}} in the Jarsath Wastes.
       - The primary ingredient of a Fiery Magma Infusion is a Magma Rock Fish. This is not a normal fish. You will need a [[Collapsible Fishing Pole]] and a fishing skill of 400 to catch these fish which only spawn in the lava pits in the Skyfire Mountains. It has a special use for Provisioners to create Fiery Magma Infusion and Magma Fish Reduction.  One place Molten Schools of fish can be found is in a crater at {{waypoint -1553.78, 207.72, 367.69}} The Collapsible Fishing Pole needs to be equipped in one of your charm slots.  The schools can be hard to see, and a combination of harvest tracking and tabbing can help.
     - Head to [[Everfrost]] and start heading to [[Permafrost]].  In the sea outside of [[Permafrost]], head to the island at location: {{waypoint 162, -38, -1696}}.  When you step onto the island, [[Einar McPherson]] <The Drunken Arm> will appear.  Hail Einar McPherson with your 5 drinks and he will spawn as a 81^^^ to 85^^^.  Kill him.
   - **"an orc standing on a cliff"**
     - Craft the [[Claws of Fyst]] (requires an ebon cluster). The recipe may be commissioned.
       - To get the recipe, head to [[Sebilis]] and kill [[A Broken Shackle Instructor]] or his named counterpart [[Grand Master Sathaz]] {{waypoint 81, -11, 199}}. The recipe [[The Legends of Fyst]] is a guaranteed body drop so long as there is a Bruiser on this quest in the group when he is killed. (make the claws before you head to clefts of rujark)
     - Go to the Sinking Sands and enter the [[Clefts of Rujark]]. (Head up to the third level. (You will need the lift key for the elevators.  Click on the operating button for the first floor to second floor lift to receive the quest. Find the Overseer and kill him for the key. Click on the operating button of the elevator again to complete the quest.)) Halfway up a cliff on a ledge at location: {{waypoint -68, 38, -66}} will be [[Champion Gorlok]] <The Arena Arm>. Drop down from the third level to hail him, you will need to be able to speak [[Orcish]].
     - He tells you that he wants the claws and recipe of a Sathirian lizard monk master.
     - If you have the [[Claws of Fyst]] with you, he will respawn as a 84^^^.  Kill him for the update.
   - **"the gluttonous giant"**
     - In [[Fens of Nathsar]], head to the beach north of the [[Field of Bone]].  Follow the shore line to the north west until you see a large bucket on the ground {{waypoint -985, -400, -2904}}.
     - Click this bucket to receive the sub-quest [[A Big Fish Bowl]].
       - Head to [[Jarsath Wastes]], and hunt the sharks and swordfish in the harbor to complete this quest (need 50 of each).  Suggestions: Make sure you have a way to breathe water.  This is also a good time to practice your fishing harvesting skill if you need to improve it. This gives you something to do while waiting on the sharks and swordfish to spawn.
     - Once you've completed the quest, head back to Fens and click on the bucket once again.
     - A giant ([[Granitegrip]]) will walk down the beach very slowly towards the full bucket and attack you.  The giant can be between 80^^^ and 85^^^.  Kill the giant for your update.
   - **"the ogre gladiator"**
     - Head to The [[Feerrott]] back near [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]].  Around the location: {{waypoint -1801, -10, 759}}, you will find [[Ogg Kryn'Rok]] <The Stone Arm> wandering around.
     - Hail and challenge her for the amulet - she will tell you a story of how she lost it to another.  This updates this portion of the quest (*no fight needed*).
1. Go to [[Kunzar Jungle]] and head up the river to the top of the first waterfall.  There will be an empty building at {{waypoint 421, 46, -297}} where [[The Big Boss (Kunzar Jungle)|The Big Boss]] will appear when you get near.  Hail and go through his dialogue. *He's on a 15 minute timer.*
   - You must be able to speak [[Halasian]] for this step.
   - The Big Boss will tell you that before he will fight you, you must become *Grandmaster in the Art of Fist and Tail*.  That quest line begins with [[Prove Your Mettle]]. (To start [[Prove Your Mettle]] speak to [[Emissary Tranz]] near Tabernacle of Pain {{waypoint 37, -69, 228}}) **and** [[The Trial of Clay]] (To start speak to **a senior Clay shackle monk** at [[Tabernacle of Pain]] {{waypoint -30, -50, 166}})  This quest line ends with the [[The Trial of Tynnonium]] resulting in *Grandmaster Status in the Art of Fist and Tail*. **You must have completed [[The Trial of Tynnonium]] or [[The Big Boss (Kunzar Jungle)|the Big Boss]] will not allow you to fight him**.
   - Once you are *Shackle Grandmaster of the Arts of Fist and Tail*, return to the big boss, hail him, respond accordingly, and then kill him. He will spawn as a 81^^^.
1. Speak to [[Borga Rallok]] in the [[Skyfire Mountains]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]] at {{waypoint -2345, 230, 1130}}
1. Go to [[Chelsith]]
   - kill [[Majora Leviathora]]
   - Click the chest in the water in the wall of Majora's pit.  Open the chest with the Four Arms of Gorynn to receive [[Gorynn's Fist (Fabled)|Gorynn's Fist]]. The chest looks like a drawer in the wall of the pool/pit.

*Now you can talk to [[Borga Rallok]] to start the quest for the Mythical version of the Epic.  Note that the Mythical version requires raiding.*

STOP!
If your goal is to have the Item for the [[Epic Conversion Timeline]], you do NOT need to do the RAID version. You can begin that timeline now.
