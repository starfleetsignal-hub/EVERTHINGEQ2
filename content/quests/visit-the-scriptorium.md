## Steps

1. Speak to [[Scriptorium Assistant Amro]]. {{waypoint 221, 225, -48}}
   - Click on the door at {{waypoint 186, 146, -57}}
   - Take the lift to the second floor {{waypoint 214, 146, -67}}
   - Take the lift to the third floor {{waypoint 215, 185, -48}}
   - Speakt o [[Scriptorium Assistant Amro]]. {{waypoint 221, 225, -48}}t
1. Click on the following objects at the top of the following towers:
   - 'Cage of Ur'the Larn': North tower {{waypoint -20, 225, -222}}
     - Go to the tower at {{waypoint -31, 146, -237}} and step on the lift to the next floor up.
     - Go the second lift at {{waypoint -11, 185, -236}}
     - Collect the 'Cage of Ur'the Larn'
   - 'The Elixir of Jehr Gh'ez': East tower {{waypoint -273, 225, -57}}
     - Go to the East Tower {{waypoint -237, 146, -58}}
     - Take the lift at {{waypoint -259, 146, -48}}
     - Take the second lift at {{waypoint -259, 184, -69}}
       - An alternate location may be the top floor of the West tower (the one with the quest givers).
   - 'Orb of Kai'ell Vaili': South tower {{waypoint -23, 225, 106}}
     - Go to the South Tower {{waypoint -22, 146, 92}}
     - Take the lift at {{waypoint -12, 146, 119}}
     - Take the second lift at {{waypoint -31, 185, 119}}
1. Return to [[Scriptorium Assistant Amro]]. {{waypoint 221, 225, -48}}
1. Speak with [[Kedar the Curator]].
1. Return to Scriptorium Assistant Amro for your reward.
