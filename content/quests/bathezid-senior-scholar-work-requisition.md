## Steps

1. Receive quest from [[Supply Sergeant Anuhadux]]
1. Check clipboard behind him for your task
   - Scribe recipe book [[Bathezid senior scholar recipes]] (This is erased after each turn in).
1. Create one of the following from the recipe book as randomly requested by the work requisition:
   - 6 [[Gore's Challenge (Journeyman)]]
   - 6 [[Lockeye's Hideous Laughter (Journeyman)]]
   - 6 [[Tabben's Restoration (with effect)]]
   - 6 [[Amulet of undead command]]
   - 6 [[Emergency healing potion]]
   - 6 [[Lucky nose ring]]
1. Return to Anuhadux for your reward

## Rewards

- At least 40g 4s 31c
- +750 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
