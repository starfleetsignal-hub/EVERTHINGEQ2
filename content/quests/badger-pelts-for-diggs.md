## Steps

1. Hunt [[A woodland badger|woodland badgers]] in the [[The Forest Ruins]] until you have acquired ten badger pelts. {{waypoint 959, -23, -560}}
1. Return to [[Diggin Diggs]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 5c
- +55 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
- One of the following:
  - [[Badger Pelt Armguards]]
  - [[Badger Pelt Bracers]]
  - [[Reinforced Badger Pelt Armguards]]
