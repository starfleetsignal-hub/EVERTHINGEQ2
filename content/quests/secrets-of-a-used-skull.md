## Steps

![Ward Locations](images/Secrets_of_a_Used_Skull_map.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Antiquitor Kantus Mor'Tael]] in [[Maj'Dul]]. {{waypoint -235, 135, -8}}
1. Find the wards in the [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]]. The ![wards](images/Image:A_ward.jpg) are shaped like large dark Ys and spawn randomly. See the talk page for random locations found by other people:
   - *Demise* (in the tarantula & chimney area){{waypoint -1002, -103, -722}}
   - *Oblivion* (around lizard oasis) {{waypoint -506, -111, -665}}{{waypoint -498, -102, -981}}
   - *Ruin* ([[Broog the Banished|Broog]] area, giants and Brutes near orc-highway)
     - Waypoint:  -216.42 -111.58 -298.60 204.18 0.00 0.00
   - *Ethernere* (near [[The Bay of Anuk]])
     - Waypoint:  -691.51 -131.05 -1094.17 60.63 0.00 0.00
     - Waypoint:  -825.10 -158.88 -1171.09 6.70 0.00 0.00
     - Waypoint:  -663.65 -136.32 -1154.06 35.15 0.00 0.00
     - Waypoint:   -1056.27 -135.94 -1308.56 46.08 0.00 0.00
     - Waypoint:   -972.89 -111.46 -1207.26 194.65 0.00 0.00
   - *Dissolution* (around undead tower)
     - Waypoint:  -1112.05 -99.10 -934.06 187.55 0.00 0.00
     - Waypoint:  -1158.51 -106.58 -891.70 69.17 0.00 0.00
     - Waypoint:  -1057.24 -99.12 -821.78 212.28 0.00 0.00
   - *Necrosis* (around gnoll oasis)
     - Waypoint:  -221.95 -165.22 -1377.58 316.74 0.00 0.00
     - Waypoint:  -254.54 -158.69 -1308.52 66.65 0.00 0.00
     - Waypoint:  -129.52 -130.81 -1149.88 227.23 0.00 0.00
1. Return to Antiquitor Kantus Mor'Tael.
1. Examine ![the pyre](images/Image:An_ancient_pyre.jpg) in the Sinking Sands at {{waypoint -603, -130, -1143}}. **Warning:** You are now on a 3 minute timer to complete next two steps.
1. Kill 5 level 50+ skeletons. There a four immediately around the pyre and more packs slightly to the north.
1. Return to the pyre and examine it to spawn [[The Ghost of Hadal]] before the timer ends.
1. Talk to the Ghost of Hadal to complete the quest.

- One of the following:
