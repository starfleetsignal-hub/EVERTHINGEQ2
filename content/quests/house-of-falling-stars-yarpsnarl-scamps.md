Note: This writ replaces [[House of Falling Stars: Yarpsnarl Reaver]]

## Steps

1. Slay 12 [[A Yarpsnarl scamp|Yarpsnarl Scamps]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]]
   - Take the Griffin from the [[Butcherblock Docks Station]] or the [[Greater Faydark Station]] (at the Zone in from [[Greater Faydark]]) to the Lessor Faydark Station located in the [[Three Roads Outpost]] of [[Butcherblock Mountains]].
   - The [[A Yarpsnarl scamp|Yarpsnarl Scamps]] are only found in the area of the Yarpsnarl Camp {{waypoint -705, 100, 222}} located South of the [[The Chessboard]].

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **House of Falling Stars**
- +1,200 faction with **The Irontoe Brigade** (+100 per [[A Yarpsnarl scamp|Yarpsnarl scamp]])
- 1 [[City Token]]
- 4160 status
