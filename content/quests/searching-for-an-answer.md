## Steps

1. Craft the [[Necromantic Detector]] at the fire {{waypoint 4277, -1090, 2025}}. Requires 1 [[Rhenium ore]], 1 [[Iridium cluster]] and 1 [[Effulgent coal]] ([[Recipe: Necromantic Detector|recipe]] auto-scribes)
1. Use the detector then go to {{waypoint 4288, -1096, 2048}}
1. Use the detector again then go to {{waypoint 4422, -1091, 2002}}
1. Use the detector again then go to {{waypoint 4307, -1085, 1998}}
1. Use the detector a final time then go to {{waypoint 4263, -1079, 1968}}
1. Return to Yeppa

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
