## Prerequisites

- You must have completed the quests, [[Guard Posts]] and [[Sarnak Interlopers]], to unlock this quest.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Seerbassador Chylik]] in [[Drogan Exile Camp]]. {{waypoint -557, -202, -1316}}
1. Attain a +10,000 faction with the Exiles of Droga.
1. Return to Seerbassador Chylik.
1. Return to Consol Klinsen.

## Rewards

- At least 2g 60s 83c
- [[Resplendent Necklace]] - *Sept.30, 2018 - Only received Exp., AA, and Gold. No Necklace.*
