## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Sandalwood Mallet for my apprentice.
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Sandalwood Mallet]]
   1. Craft [[A Sturdy Sandalwood Mallet]] on a [[Woodworking Table]] using the following materials and fuel:
      - 2 [[Severed sandalwood]]
      - 4 [[Lambent sandpaper]]
1. I need to take this apron back to my apprentice.
