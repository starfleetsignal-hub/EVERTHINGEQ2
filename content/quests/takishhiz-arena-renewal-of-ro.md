## Steps

1. Speak with Firiona Vie in [[Takish'Hiz Arena: Renewal of Ro (Solo)]].
1. After the emote *"You feel a flood of heroism..."*, adds will start spawning around the room. Use Heroic Opportunity to banish the adds during the fight.
1. There are floating green shards that spawn around the edges of the room as the fight progresses. Click each set of shards when they spawn.   You will be asked random questions with 3 possible answers.  The answers are randomly placed in position 1, 2, or 3 each time.
   - **Q1**: A score of events carried out, orchestrated, or utilized by Nagafen culminated in this momentous day to bring about his beloved's resurrection. Tell me an event in which he did NOT have his claws...<br>**A1:** *Providing the Coalescence of Life as an offering to the Feral Spirit on Luclin*<br><br>
   - **Q2:** Firiona Vie has been known by several names, and worn a great many titles during her long life, rightful Queen of Felwithe, High Priestess of Growth, Eva Corunno'thes, and now Demigod of Renewal. What was her most recent title, just prior to her ascension?"<br>**A2:** *Chosen of Growth*<br><br>
   - **Q3:** For defying ancient draconian law Lady Vox and Lord Nagafen were judged and imprisoned in separate dungeons for centuries. Later, during the Age of Cataclysms Lady Vox would be slain by drakota, sent by what faction?<br>**A3:** *The Ring of Scale*<br><br>
   - **Q4:** After the return of Tunare's sacred gift to all, Firiona Vie planted an item, the last vestige of the Bloom of Growth, in the Takish'Hiz Arena. What was the item?<br>**A4:** *The Seed of Growth*<br><br>
   - **Q5:** Firiona Vie realized Tunar's chose land, the Elddar, and all their descendants did not need to go on bearing the scars of the wanton acts of a petty god. What was the destructive act she was hoping to overcome?<br>**A5:** *Turning Elddar Forest into desert*.
