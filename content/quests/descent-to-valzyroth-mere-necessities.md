## Steps

1. Kill fungrove fungusmen in the vicinity of {{waypoint -115, 12, 32}}
1. Return to Baeddan.
1. Speak with Gletha Billowchalk at {{waypoint -543, -0, -216}}
1. Use the Rejuvination Potion on sickly slaves at the camp and around {{waypoint -467, -18, -80}}
1. Return to Baeddan.
1. Do the following:
   - 12 Hunt Fungusmen for dhalgar items.
   - Search 10 fungoid rubbish. Locations include:
     - {{waypoint -449, -19, -111}} {{waypoint -423, -18, -96}} {{waypoint -390, -18, -111}} {{waypoint -383, -19, -128}} {{waypoint -300, -11, -48}} {{waypoint -282, -12, -18}} {{waypoint -283, -12, -35}} {{waypoint -266, -9, -1}} {{waypoint -204, 14, 78}} {{waypoint -171, 18, 52}}
   - Search 6 fungoid trinket baskets. Locations include:
     - {{waypoint -464, -13, -133}} {{waypoint -486, -15, -109}} {{waypoint -440, -19, -109}} {{waypoint -415, -18, -120}}  {{waypoint -386, -18, -109}} {{waypoint -371, -19, -134}}
1. Return to Baeddan.
1. Speak to Kelly Gnocktonic.
1. Do the following:
   - Kill Fungalrot Slugs around {{waypoint -537, -8, -15}}
   - Kill veiled Shroomfloats around
   - Gather 8 veiled Shroomfloat Eggsacs. Locations include
     - {{waypoint -405, -19, -47}} {{waypoint -330, -12, -61}} {{waypoint -322, -12, -57}} {{waypoint -279, -12, -35}} {{waypoint -239, -5, 11}} {{waypoint -303, -7, 19}} {{waypoint -265, 13, 82}} {{waypoint -279, 11, 83}}
1. Return to Kelly.
