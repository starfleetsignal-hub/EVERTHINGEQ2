## Steps

1. Make [[Heavy Gnomish Cannon]] using the provided [[Blueprint: Gnomish Cannon (no-value no-trade lore)|Blueprint: Gnomish Cannon]] which requires:
   - [[Work Bench]]
   - 1 [[Titanium ore]]
   - 1 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
   - 1 [[Amber]]
   - 1 [[Ethereal coal]]
   - There is a [[Work Bench]] located at {{waypoint 911, -274, 152}} in [[Thurgadin]].
1. Exit Thurgadin into the *Great Divide*. Once outside, head to the upper-level area of the outer walls. Then go to {{waypoint 734, -375, 864}}
1. Look for a shiny spot on the ground. Click it to place the cannon next to the barricade facing the Rime.
1. Return to [[Aggi Stonefist]] {{waypoint 698, -312, 361}} in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain|Thurgadin]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
