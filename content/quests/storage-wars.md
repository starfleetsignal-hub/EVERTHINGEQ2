## Notes

- [[Vikana|Vikana's]] quests may be done in [[The Merchant's Den (Solo)]], [[The Merchant's Den (Heroic I)]], or [[The Merchant's Den (Heroic II)]].
- I rescued all the captives.  Apparently I should NOT have done this because the next quest, "Competitive Market Strategies" requires you to rescue the captives. So, I had to redo the zone after the times expired.

## Steps

1. Take the road out of [[Little Town]] towards the [[Mahngavi Wastes]] gate.
1. Click on a **Temple Street Cart** {{waypoint -411.18, 0.09, -174.42}}
1. Click on two of the nearby open chests. Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint -404.66, -0.15, -169.05}}
   - {{waypoint -392.82, -0.13, -182.50}}
   - {{waypoint -395.97, 0.05, -196.68}}
1. Return to [[Vikana]] at {{waypoint -249.12, 13.58, 127.97}}
1. Search [[Nightmare Rise]] for clues {{waypoint -286.80, 53.54, -344.77}}
1. Enter The Merchant's Den ([[The Merchant's Den (Solo)|Solo]], [[The Merchant's Den (Heroic I)|Heroic I]], or [[The Merchant's Den (Heroic II)|Heroic 2]]) - {{waypoint -288.37, 53.76, -347.10}}
1. Kill [[A shady supplier]] in the first room {{waypoint -132.92, 1.40, -62.85}}
   - *Click the corpse to check its pockets and read the note to obtain a password, which you need to remember.*
1. Hail [[The Gateman]] behind the locked gate at {{waypoint -114.94, 0.00, -66.19}}
   - *Select the top option and enter the password.*
1. [[Captain Twoshanks]] {{waypoint -163.41, -52.65, 57.60}}
   - *Do not let the 'bilge haunt' adds get to ten total or you wipe.*
1. Click the false wall at {{waypoint -165.99, 0.10, -60.59}} to progress
1. [[Grimalda Goodhand]] {{waypoint -201.41, -0.47, -51.76}}
   - *Grimalda roams around her room and hall*
1. [[Gelda Glintswift]] {{waypoint -242, 0, -48}}
   - *Gelda will cast a buff that spawns **bubbles** in the next room. Double-click the bubbles to "kill" them and remove her damage shield.
1. Go up the stairs and destroy the cracked wall at {{waypoint -235.43, 8.55, -32.90}} to move forward.
1. Kill [[Elder Furdock]] {{waypoint -240, 0, 16}}
1. Free **captive Vikana associates**.
   - *You may be able to get these updates in a cleared instance of the zone.*
   - Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -219.37, 0.16, 17.51}}
     - {{waypoint -253.22, -4.45, 63.77}}
     - {{waypoint -229.43, -4.45, 71.77}}
     - {{waypoint -210.85, -4.45, 50.05}}
     - {{waypoint -181.31, -4.40, 58.60}}
     - {{waypoint -180.90, -4.44, 50.49}}
1. Return to [[Vikana]] in Forlorn Gist {{waypoint -249.12, 13.58, 127.97}}
   - *The portal to zone out spawns after killing [[Arachlord Dyrraga]], see steps below to clear the zone and zone out.*
1. Kill [[Liegess Lavalle]] {{waypoint -291.88, -4.44, 49.38}}
   - *When Liegess Lavalle calls for reinforcements, pull the lever on the wall that matches the shape of the portal, preventing additional mobs from coming through.*
1. [[Beefcake]]
   - *Clear the side areas around Beefcake*
   - *Hail Beefcake to start the fight.*
   - *Use Heroic Opportunities to clear Beefcake's stacks.*
1. [[Arachlord Dyrraga]] {{waypoint -236.49, -4.45, 90.05}}
   - *Every ~25%, Dyrraga will cast "Hide from Him" and disappear, then Mayong's image will appear and portals will spawn adds.*
   - *After a few seconds Mayong will leave, the adds despawn, and Dyrraga returns.*
