## Steps

1. Kill 12 Fragmented Geonids within [[Crystalline Breaks]]
   - *Note:They respawn in the zone.  While waiting for respawn, the named Crysallis advances the quest, as well as the four guards in the room.*
1. Speak with [[Rongot Wyrmrippa]] at {{waypoint -751, -343, 2786}} in [[Eastern Wastes]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +4,550 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
