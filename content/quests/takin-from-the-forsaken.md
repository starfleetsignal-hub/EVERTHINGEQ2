## Steps

1. Enter [[Sodden Archipelago: The Forsaken Isle (Solo)|Sodden Archipelago: The Forsaken Isle [Solo]]] at {{waypoint -136.44, 2.77, 403.29}}   ***clickable boat at dock***
1. Gather 11 kerra totems.
   - Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint -690.97, 151.72, -591.28}}
     - {{waypoint -705.84, 153.77, -501.41}}
     - {{waypoint -762.39, 151.53, -456.81}}
     - {{waypoint -594, 146, -374}}
     - {{waypoint -718.81, 164.82, -247.99}}
     - {{waypoint -633.80, 142.06, -211.84}}
     - {{waypoint -719.80, 172.96, -210.77}}
     - {{waypoint -707.28, 141.49, -178.68}}
     - {{waypoint -757.84, 137.57, -144.03}}
     - {{waypoint -681.72, 142.86, -72.85}}
     - {{waypoint -788.89, 139.01, -95.76}}
1. Gather 1 more kerra totem.
1. Defeat the risen Kerra shaman.
1. Return to Kober Botley.

## NOTE

You do **not** have to kill the named: Prolovok to complete this quest.

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
