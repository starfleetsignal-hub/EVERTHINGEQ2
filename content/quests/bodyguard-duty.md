## Steps

1. Escort Makarios's courier from [[Neriak, City of Hate]] to [[Darklight Wood]] and then through [[The Commonlands]] to the gates of [[Freeport]] where [[Importer Strathcona]] awaits your arrival {{waypoint -1226, -83, 34}}.
   - Note: You can quickly and easily do this by using the inner-city travel to the Dockside Markets and then quick-traveling to Commonlands.
1. Return to [[Exporter Makarios]] in Neriak.
