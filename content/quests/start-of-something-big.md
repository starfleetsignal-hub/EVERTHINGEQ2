## Steps

1. Collect the following auto-updated pieces from [[A mischievous maiden|mischievous maidens]] or [[A very mischievous maiden|very mischievous maidens]] found northeast of the docks around {{waypoint -130, -1, -14}} and [[A Mistcaller mischiefmaker|Mistcaller mischiefmakers]] found underwater around {{waypoint -293, -15, 164}}
   - An old ranger's mask (only from mistcaller mischiefmakers)
   - An ivy-etched gladius (only from mischievous maidens)
   - A Proudfoot platemail breastplate (only from very mischievous maidens)
   - An ancient Bravefoot helm (only from mistcaller mischiefmakers)
   - A collectible jumjum flask (from mischievous maidens)
   - An animal cookie mold (from mischievous maidens)
1. Return to [[Deputy Nettlebrine]]

## Rewards

- Experience
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Enchanted Earstud of the Leatherfoots]] (All Mages)
  - [[Enchanted Hoop of the Leatherfoots]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Enchanted Spike of the Leatherfoots]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Enchanted Stud of the Leatherfoots]]  (All Priests)
