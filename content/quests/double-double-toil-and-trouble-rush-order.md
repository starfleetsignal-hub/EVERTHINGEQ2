## Steps

Create 1 each of any 6 from the random list below:

- [[Exceptional Essence of Turgur]]
- [[Exceptional Mental Breach]]
- [[Exceptional Vitality Breach]]
- [[Exceptional Elemental Reprieve]]
- [[Exceptional Elixir of Intellect]]
- [[Exceptional Noxious Reprieve]]
- [[Exceptional Arcane Reprieve]]
- [[Exceptional Caustic Poison]]
- [[Exceptional Hemotoxin]]

Create 1 each of any 6 from the random list below:

- [[Dragonfire|Dragonfire (Journeyman)]]
- [[Unholy Hunger V|Unholy Hunger V (Journeyman)]]
- [[Brutality V|Brutality V (Journeyman)]]
- [[Bloodlust V|Bloodlust V (Journeyman)]]
- [[Calm Tranquility V|Calm Tranquility V (Journeyman)]]
- [[Divine Inspiration V|Divine Inspiration V (Journeyman)]]
- [[Call to Arms V|Call to Arms V (Journeyman)]]
- [[Call of Shielding IV|Call of Shielding IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Merciless Stomp V|Merciless Stomp V (Journeyman)]]
- [[Striking Cobra V|Striking Cobra V (Journeyman)]]
- [[Intimidate IV|Intimidate IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Taunting Blow VI|Taunting Blow VI (Journeyman)]]
- [[Raging Blow VI|Raging Blow VI (Journeyman)]]
- [[Blaze Kick V|Blaze Kick V (Journeyman)]]
- [[Divine Vengeance V|Divine Vengeance V (Journeyman)]]
- [[Siphon Strike V|Siphon Strike V (Journeyman)]]

Report back to the Rush Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 6g 64s 30c
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +300 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +300 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
