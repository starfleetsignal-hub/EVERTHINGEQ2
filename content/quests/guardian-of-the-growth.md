## Steps

1. Approach [[Wuoshi (NPC)|Wuoshi]] on [[Aeterna Gardens]] {{waypoint -605, 379, 635}}.
1. Find a way to get past Wuoshi's defenses.
1. Attack [[A root tender]]. After trying to kill it, it will mask you and you will be able to approach Wuoshi!
   - *Note: If you are having trouble **NOT** 1 shotting the root tender lower your level to 95 at the Chronomancer, or unequip your weapons and melee him*   "Getting completely naked and taking damage while pulling them toward Woushi helps with getting the mask applied to you"
1. Talk to Wuoshi, then return to Harla Dar.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Veeshenna Tol Altar Table]]
