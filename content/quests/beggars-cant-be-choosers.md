## Steps

1. Enter [[Beggar's Court: Sweat-Stained Cobbles]] {{waypoint -150, -50, -375}}
1. Speak to [[Enforcer Moxog]] {{waypoint 53, -7, 112}}
1. Target and use [[Beggar's Court Relocation Orders]] on 6 [[A hapless residents|hapless residents]]
   - two at {{waypoint 34, 0, 75}}
   - {{waypoint -13, -2, 80}}
   - {{waypoint -32, 0, 44}}
   - two at {{waypoint -34, 0, 2}}
1. Speak to Enforcer Moxog
1. Create 6 barricades by clicking green glowing ones
   - {{waypoint 52, -7, 68}}
   - {{waypoint -17, 2, 0}}
   - two at {{waypoint 49, 2, 19}}
   - two at {{waypoint 2, 2, 11}}
1. Speak to Enforcer Moxog
1. Run to {{waypoint 13, 0, 75}}
1. Run to {{waypoint -18, -7, 62}}. You will be attacked by 2 [[A rioting captive|rioting captives]] (your level, one down, grouped)
1. Run to {{waypoint -29, -5, 27}}
1. Run to {{waypoint 54, -6, 45}}. You will be attacked by 2 [[A rioting captive|rioting captives]] (your level, one down, grouped)
1. Collect 6 sparkling crates
   - {{waypoint 51, -5, 34}}
   - {{waypoint 43, -5, 40}}
   - {{waypoint 23, -6, 43}}
   - {{waypoint 13, -6, 38}}
   - {{waypoint 6, -6, 34}}
   - {{waypoint 7, -5, 24}}
   - {{waypoint -3, -6, 27}}
   - {{waypoint -21, -7, 59}}
   - {{waypoint -2, -7, 72}}
1. Speak to the Grand Duke in West Freeport
