## Steps

1. Speak with [[Foci Tamara Paust]] in the basement of [[The Academy of Arcane Science]] {{waypoint 18, -34, -147}}. Note: there is a claw like switch to activate the elevator pad to basement.
1. Speak to [[Rusty Cogsprocket]] near Longshadow Alley {{waypoint -95, -34, -358}}
1. Enter [[Longshadow Alley: Daeneth Cloister]] and kill [[A Nerian questioner]] (even level and con)
   - All Nerians you approach, including gate sentries, will attack you
1. Use [[Illusory Arasai Effigy]] and equip [[Cultist Robes]], and speak to a Nerian questioner (several wander near the gate). If the zone bugs out and you cannot approach a questioner to hail them, zone out and back in and then use the effigy immediately and they should now no longer be hostile.
1. Speak to a solo Nerian questioner (may have to try several, usually outside a room.
1. Watch a Thexian being interrogated (a cornered citizen) {{waypoint 28, 1, 26}} or {{waypoint 0, 0, -31}} or {{waypoint -27, 1, 10}}
1. Watch another interrogation at a different location listed above
1. Use [[Questioner's Gem]] on different dark elves at least 4 times or more. {{waypoint -33, 1, 8}}
1. Speak to Rusty in North Freeport
