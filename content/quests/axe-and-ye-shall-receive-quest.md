## Notes

On servers without the guide program this quest can be acquired from [[Cronyn Stormhammer]] near [[Kaladim]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] {{waypoint -216, 153, -427}}.

## Steps

1. I should search for 6 axes in the old halls of [[Kaladim]] {{waypoint -170.60, 165.01, -568.13}}.
   - Ceremonial Dwarven Pickaxe {{waypoint -8, 2, 53}}
   - Runed Cresent Axe {{waypoint -8, 2, 36}}
   - Dwarven Battle Axe {{waypoint -8, 2, 24}}
   - Runed Executioner's Axe {{waypoint 14, 2, 52}}
   - Cleaver Axe {{waypoint 14, 2, 38}}
   - Ceremonial Hatchet {{waypoint 14, 2, 26}}
1. Search for axes in the building of [[West Fort Irontoe]] {{waypoint -228, 210, -105}}.
   - Runed Battle Axe {{waypoint -274, 216, -155}}
   - Executioner's Axe {{waypoint -219, 211, -145}}
1. Search for axes in the in the buildings of [[East Fort Irontoe]] {{waypoint -386, 162, -247}}
   - Square-headed Axe {{waypoint -365, 162, -178}}
1. Go to Butcherblock docks and contact [[Dockworker Brawley]]  {{waypoint 673, 24, 609}} to deliver the notes on axes and complete the quest.  He roams this area.
1. Optional: Return to the [[Guides of the Shattered Lands|Guide]] and potentially receive the bonus title. This reward is at the discretion of the guide and is generally given for being in-character. NPC Cronyn does not grant this reward

## Rewards

- [[Decorating With Axes]] (from Dockworker Brawley)
- Optionally, from the guide
  - Title: [[The Axe Master]]
