Before you begin: Consider getting the [[Tradesman Service]] quest to complete simultaneously. Ensure you compensate for the additional severed briarwood you need for both quests.

You need to be either level 36 adventurer or tradeskiller  (no longer a minimum harvesting requirement for any node in the game, but a skill of 140? is recommended so as not to come up empty handed too often).

## Steps

1. Go harvest (buying does not work)
   - From [[Enchanted Lands]], 15 of each
     - [[Severed briarwood]] from Wretched Arbor
     - [[Green tea leaf]] from a Mystical Natural Garden
     - [[Tussah root]] from Velvety Roots
     - [[Rough opaline]] from a Rhythmic Stone
     - [[Shark fin]] from a Throng of Fish
     - [[Cucumber]] from a Mystical Natural Garden
     - [[Velium cluster]] from a Rhythmic Stone
     - [[Griffon meat]] from an Animal Den
     - [[Bear meat]] from an Animal Den
   - And the same from [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]], 15 of each
     - [[Severed briarwood]] from a Marred Arbor
     - [[Green tea leaf]] from a Flattened Bed of Thistle
     - [[Tussah root]] from Vestigal Roots
     - [[Rough opaline]] from a Mud Stone
     - [[Shark fin]] from a Fury of Fish
     - [[Cucumber]] from a Flattened Bed of Thistle
     - [[Velium cluster]] from a Mud Stone
     - [[Griffon meat]] from an Animal Den
     - [[Bear meat]] from an Animal Den
1. Return to Qho Augren.

## Rewards

- At least 36g 41s 30c
- Tradeskill experience
