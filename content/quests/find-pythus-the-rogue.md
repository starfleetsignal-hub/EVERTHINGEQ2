## Steps

1. Speak with [[Kaeso Polluvius]] on the South Freeport docks. {{waypoint -303, -57, 137}}
1. Speak with [[Aldrevia Metilla]] in the Jade Tiger's Den. {{waypoint -125.37, -21.43, -69.12}}
1. Aldrevia says you will find [[Pythus]] in the Commonlands near the Crossroads. {{waypoint -755, -47, -141}}
   - This is by a couple trees on the road to the Crossroads from Freeport. Run around the area for a little while and Pythus will appear. You may have to wander as far as rocks at {{waypoint -841, -46, -95}} to trigger Pythus.
1. Take Pythus' cap back to Sergeant Nominus.

- At least 9s
