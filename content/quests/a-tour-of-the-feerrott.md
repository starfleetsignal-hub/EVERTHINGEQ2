## Notes

- This quest is also part of the [[Feerrott Timeline]].
- [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Feerrott]] will be removed from your inventory when the quest is turned in. There is no known way to get it back afterwards.

## Steps

1. Read [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Feerrott]]
1. Visit the following locations in [[The Feerrott]]:
   1. [[The Drowning Causeway]]; this is the zone-in from the [[Globe of Swift Travel]]
   1. [[The Hidden Camp|Explorer's Haven]] {{waypoint -76, 13, 158}}
   1. [[Thule Camp|Thulian Glade]] {{waypoint 151, 8, 183}}
   1. [[Rallosian Mage Shrine|Crescent Shrine]] {{waypoint -177, -6, 435}}
   1. [[Murdunk's Fall]] {{waypoint -332, 2, 130}}
   1. [[Temple of Alliz|Pyramid of Fear]] {{waypoint -321, -1, 834}}
   1. [[Restored Druid Ring|Ring of the Amatra]] {{waypoint -565, -15, 841}}
   1. [[The Sentinel Stones|The Secret Meeting]] {{waypoint -724, 2, 549}}
   1. [[Altar of the Four Canons]] {{waypoint -730, 8, 220}}
   1. [[The Tower of Vul|Ruins of Rallos]] {{waypoint -870, -1, 502}}
   1. [[Gulch of Thule|Stone Gullet]] {{waypoint -1171, -9, 791}}; the stone entrance to the gulch
   1. First Intact Hand of Thule {{waypoint -620, -9, 586}}
   1. Second Intact Hand of Thule {{waypoint -18, 26, 922}}
1. Return to Hoggle Bogstrutter in Zek, the Orcish Wastes.

## Rewards

- At least 6g 5c
