## Steps

1. Gather one of a variety of items.
   - Gather fesmiarium scraps in Bloodtribe Ruins. Small piles of scraps around  {{waypoint 330, -7, 226}}*
   - Gather Runestones of Golden Rays in Sunset Pillars. Broken tablets around {{waypoint 423, 23, -71}}
1. Return to [[Tegin The Curator]] in [[Wracklands]] at {{waypoint 546.95, 34.56, 134.20}}.

## Rewards

- At least 90g 72s
- One of the following: *Note: These are only for the first completion.*
  - [[Shadow-Gripped Ssraeshzian Scepter]]
  - [[Shadow-Gripped Ssraeshzian Cleaver]]
  - [[Shadow-Gripped Disciple of Rhag Staff]]
