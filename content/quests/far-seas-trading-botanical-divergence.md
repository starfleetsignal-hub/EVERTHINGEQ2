Ethan would like to get this week's report from Claudia on the flora of Ethernere.

## Steps

![](images/Vesspyr_Flora_Leaf_Sample.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Claudia (Vesspur Isles)|Claudia]] at {{waypoint 154, 157, -161}}
1. Gather 5 plant leaf samples from "Vesspyr flora" on the very edges of three islands: Aeterna Gardens*, Veeshenna Tol, and Sothshae. Track Harvestables will list them. **Note: Gather as many as you like during the quest, and the next time this is the Weekly, this step will instant-update if you have 5 or more in your inventory. Unlike the Vesspyr Willows in a previous quest, these are quite easy to find. You can find all you need just by going along the very edge of one island.
1. Return to Claudia
   - (Claudia will put 2 recipes in your recipe book to be used at an Engraved Desk - "Plant Collection Record Sheet" and "Bound Plant Collection Record Book".)
1. "Fill out a collection sheet for each leaf sample" - Craft five Plant Collection Record Sheets (scribed earlier). There is an engraved desk near Ethan {{waypoint 163.76, 187.90, -158.55}}.
1. "Create a bound plant collection report from the record sheets" - Craft a Bound Plant Collection Record Books (ditto)
   - Note: this takes a considerable amount of time to craft!
1. Deliver the botanical report to Ethan.

## Materials Needed

- 17 [[Matoppie roots]]
- 30 [[Effulgent incense]]
