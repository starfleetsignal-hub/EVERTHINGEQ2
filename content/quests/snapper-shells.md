## Steps

1. Kill six [[A leathershell snapper|leathershell snappers]] and six [[A stoneshell snapper|stoneshell snappers]] for their shells. Both can be found at the pond near Therven. {{waypoint 1127, -23, -683}}
1. Return to [[Therven Senshun]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s 13c
- One of the following:
  - [[Small leather bag]]
  - [[An oaken bench]]
