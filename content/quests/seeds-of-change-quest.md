## Steps

1. Obtain the following ![wakingdew walker](images/Wakingdew_walker.jpg)![Gnarlroot Seedling](images/Gnarlroot_Seedlings.jpg)
   - Obtain 12 wakingdew walker stems by killing [[Wakingdew walkers]] near {{waypoint -707, 29, 589}}.
   - Gather 8 gnarlroot seedlings from [[Esoterra Gardens]]
     - *Purple fern-like plants near the pools under trees. A lot are around {{waypoint -707, 29, 589}} and {{waypoint -640.26, 29.23, 463.46}}.*
1. Return to [[Himaduri]] at {{waypoint -918, 34, 206}}.
