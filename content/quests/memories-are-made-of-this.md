## Steps

1. Hunt 8 [[A moor soul|moor souls]] for grimling ritual acrylia carving chisels in [[Hollowshade Moor]] {{waypoint -261, 10, -173}}.
   - *Kill all the other mobs in the area and the moor souls will spawn.*
   - ![a moor soul](images/A_moor_soul.jpg)
1. Return to the [[A grimling memory|grimling memory]] {{waypoint -136, 85, -691}}.
1. hunt and gather the following
   - Hunt 6 [[A saurek darkclaw rockhopper|saurek darkclaw rockhoppers]] for weapon parts around {{waypoint -738, 40, -285}} and {{waypoint -741, 41, -481}} .
     - ![a saurek darkclaw](images/A_saurek_darkclaw.jpg)
   - Gather 8 **Scalebark Sludge** for poison. Not trackable. They are found at the base of trees. Some possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -762, 41, -488}}
     - {{waypoint -689, 41, -501}}
     - {{waypoint -695, 42, -485}}
     - {{waypoint -701, 46, -450}}
     - {{waypoint -732, 47, -436}}
     - {{waypoint -720, 44, -414}}
     - {{waypoint -755, 43, -392}}
     - {{waypoint -753, 41, -354}}
     - ![Scalebark Sludge](images/Scalebark_Sludge.jpg)
1. Return to the [[A grimling memory|grimling memory]] {{waypoint -136, 85, -691}}.
1. Return to [[Grelas the Old]] {{waypoint -837, 43, -800}}.
   - *Note: If you are also on the quest: [[Reign of Shadows: Facing the Savage Beast]] turn to your left and harvest [[Khati Sha's third phylactery]] you are now able to advance that quest since completion of this quest was a pre-requisite.*

## Rewards

- [[Saurek Darkclaw Rockhopper]]
- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
