## Notes

Upon accepting this quest you receive [[Narezi's Scroll]]

## Steps

1. Read the scroll from [[Narezi]] - find it in inventory, right click and choose Use to open a book-like window, continue reading until quest updates.
1. Hunt [[A blightcoil revenant|blightcoil revenant]]s in [[Writher Ruins]] for  12 venom sacs {{waypoint -392, 61, 465}} - kill update from flying snakes in ruins above crevasse.
1. Return to Horron at the Obelisk and administer the venom to him. {{waypoint -628, -1, 445}}
1. Speak with Horron

## Rewards

- 3 x [[Spiked Void Crystal]]
- 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following:
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
  - [[Doomview Longbow of Fortitude]]
  - [[Coral Crossbow of Invasions]]
