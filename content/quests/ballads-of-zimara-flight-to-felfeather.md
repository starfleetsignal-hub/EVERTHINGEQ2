## Steps

1. Speak to [[Ryarme]] in Sanctuary at {{waypoint 335.36, 252.06, 729.13}}
1. Encourage Halebeak to take flight again
   - Complete [[A Real Whose Hoos]] from [[Viamme]] at {{waypoint 210.62, 274.96, 815.29}}.
   - If you have already completed Viamme's quest then Ryarme won't have the quest book over his head, hail [[Viamme]] for the update.
1. Return to [[Ryarme]].
1. Take flight on [[Halebeak]] at {{waypoint 347.05, 252.94, 730.48}} to [[Felfeather Isle]].
1. Kill and save the following
   - ![shards of steel](images/Shards_of_steel.jpg)Kill 5 shards of steel.
   - ![an injured Felfeather](images/An_injured_Felfeather.jpg)Save 3 injured Felfeathers by clicking on them. (group-wide update) Some possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 973.71, 167.27, 775.95}},
     - {{waypoint 989.84, 169.62, 817.03}}
     - {{waypoint 999.66, 169.19, 770.78}}
1. Search out [[Crestcry]] on [[Felfeather Isle]] at {{waypoint 1069, 179, 753}}
   - Briefly wait close to him, while he kills the shards of steel at which point he will update the quest.
1. Speak to [[Crestcry]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 36g 48s 99c
