## Description

The Quel'ule scholar Azira has requested assistance in applying a recording tag to a void creature to gather information for study.

## Steps

1. I have been asked to place a magical tag on one of the void creatures so that information about it can be recorded by the researchers.
   - Use the provided [[Tagging Device]] on a void creature. The void assaulters that attack the city every few minutes are a good source of void creature.
1. Return to [[Researcher Azira]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2500 faction with **Researchers of Quel'ule**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Quel'ule Daily Supply Tasks
