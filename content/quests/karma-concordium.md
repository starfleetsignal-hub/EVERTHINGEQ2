## Steps

1. [[Akina]] wants you to speak to [[Bellengere the Three (Qeynos Capitol District)|Bellengere the Three]] in [[The Concordium Tower]] (use the yellow teleporter). {{waypoint 701, 87, 130}}
1. Bellengere is unavailable, so speak to [[Assistant Rystan]].
1. Go to the entrance of the Tower, speak to [[Crexisz Lepidus]], who requests that you obtain incriminating evidence regarding Bellengere's illicit use of necromancy. {{waypoint 710, -20, 118}}
   - Enter the personal quarters of Bellengere the Three in [[The Concordium Tower]] (use the yellow teleporter). The entrance is a swirling purple portal. {{waypoint 701, 87, 130}}
   - Collect 10 incriminating documents.
     - The documents are trapped and must be clicked a second time to get the update.
     - Some of the trapped documents may even be fatal, so prehealing, warding, etc. is a good idea.
1. Return to Crexisz Lepidus. To provide him an alibi, he must attack you very publicly. Fight!
1. Return to Akina.

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Celestial Watch**
