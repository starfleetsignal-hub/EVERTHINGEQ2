## Note

- This quest is repeatable.
- Skirha's Preserving Prowess is a repeatable quest.

## Steps

1. Obtain Skirha's needed spell components from [[Burrower's Timber]]:
   1. Kill
      - 8 direbeaks{{waypoint 617, 87, 128}}  **OR**
      - 8 Beetle Crab {{waypoint 763.67, 58.01, 216.49}}
   1. Obtain
      - 10 floral devourer seeds around {{waypoint 557.23, 83.82, 279.20}}.
        - Get these by killing a floral devourer near the trees.  **OR**
      - 10 beetle larvae near {{waypoint 735, 77, 397}}
1. Return to [[Skirha]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- 5 [[Zimaran Ballad]]
- +1000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
