## Steps

1. You need 3 body drops from raid mobs in any order
   - [[Varig Crafted Ring]] from [[The Leviathan]] in [[The Chamber of Destiny]](T3)
   - [[Eternal Bloodlust Charm]] from [[Tairiza the Widow Mistress]] in [[Tomb of Thuuga]] (T1)
   - [[Dragon Tongue Tongs]] from [[Qunard Ashenclaw]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]] (T4)
1. Go with these items, your weapon and 100 [[Smoldering coal]] (T8 coal) to the The Infernal Forge of Ages in [[Solusek's Eye]] (Zone is at {{waypoint -69, -627, -123}} in Sol Eye (sub level 7)). Inside you'll find a recipe next to the forge (Knowledge of Honing the Dragon's Temper). Scribe it and create your mythical weapon. The level 2 recipe is Honing the Dragon's Temper.
