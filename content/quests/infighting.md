## Steps

1. I should search the Fearcreep to find the lost amygdalans.
   - Find [[Gal'hu]] {{waypoint -1840, 225, 1017}}
   - Find [[Vylet]] {{waypoint -2106, 216, 1014}}
   - Find [[Xagriin]] {{waypoint -2118, 221, 1153}}
1. I should return to the amygdalan camp and inform R'yleth of my success.
   - Return to R'yleth

## Notes

- Gal'hu and Vylet will appear on your map with quest icons, Xagriin will not because he is dead.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- [[Exquisite Sconce of the Faceless]]
