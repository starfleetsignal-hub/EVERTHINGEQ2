Mei Lan wants to know more about Oceansfull, as Ultera has no oceans.  I will head to the Great Divide and see if I can get her some suitable symbols of the event.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Great Divide]] starting in [[Thurgadin Harbor]]
   - In [[Thurgadin Harbor]] collect 4 Harbor Shells near {{waypoint 1370, -540, 486}}![Harbor Shells](images/Harbor_Shell.jpg)
1. Travel to Iceclad using Sinking Sands (this could be improved)
   - Collect 4 Iceclad Shrine Candles on Iceclad Island in Fina's Retreat merchant area, near {{waypoint -1398, -536, -2111}}
   - Collect 4 Sea Urchin Spines, North West of (in front of) **Lodizal** in Fina's Retreat on floating ice among the armorclad ice urchin(s)  near {{waypoint -1622, -542, -2543}}![Sea Urchin Spines](images/Sea_Urchin_Spine.jpg)
1. Return to [[Mei Lan]] in [[The Sundered Frontier]]

## Reward

Unlock the Days of Summer 2021: Week 5 Holiday event rewards that you can purchase at [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2292.99, -199.41, 2505.71}}
