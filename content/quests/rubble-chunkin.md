Shadow, a Teir'Dal Green Hood agent, busy bombarding the Scourge orcs using a catapult in Tallon Field, has encouraged me to lend a hand and cause some chaos, like himself.

## Steps

1. Kill 4 orcs near the broken catapult west of Shadow {{waypoint 599.49, -25.72, 139.36}}.
1. Inspect the catapult for damage.
1. Scavenge a large counter weight chain {{waypoint 538.33, -39.99, 236.61}}.
1. Scavenge a large metal arm pin {{waypoint 399.22, -35.69, 173.29}}.
1. Repair the damaged catapult. You will be attacked by a ^ 104 "Scourge Invader" Orc upon completing the repair.
1. Fire the repaired catapult 3 times.
1. Return to [[Shadow (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Shadow]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- +500 faction with **The Green Hoods**
- [[Scourge Catapult]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Iron Branch Ring]]
  - [[Iron Root Ring]]
  - [[Iron Leaf Ring]]
