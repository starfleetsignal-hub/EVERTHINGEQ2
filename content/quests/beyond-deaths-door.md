## Steps

1. Enter [[Deathtoll]] on [[Carrion Briar]] within [[The Bonemire]]. {{waypoint -297, -74, -515}}
   - It is no longer required that you complete the access quest and [[Marked Flameshield of Scale]] has been removed. On TLE servers, however, the Flameshield is still required.
1. Visit the following locations:
   - **Important:** Make sure that you leave [[Fitzpitzle (Deathtoll)|Fitzpitzle]] and [[Tarinax the Destroyer]] alive, as you will need them for the quest steps after this one.
   - [[Pit of Barbs]]: {{waypoint -340, 115, -111}}
   - [[Archives of the Wyrm]]: {{waypoint -584, 119, -274}}
   - [[The Flensing Chamber]]: {{waypoint -682, 122, -371}}
   - [[The Altar of Ending]]: {{waypoint -257.83, 60.61, -327.50}}
   - [[The Tears of Rage]]: {{waypoint 134, -151, -374}}
   - [[Lair of the Soulless]]: {{waypoint 144, 4, -136}}
1. Kill [[Fitzpitzle (Deathtoll)|Fitzpitzle]]. {{waypoint -574.52, 119.85, -265.63}}
1. Kill [[Tarinax the Destroyer]]. {{waypoint 130, -2, -114}}

## Reward

- Your choice of:
  - [[Fitzpitzle's Earcog]]
  - [[Fitzpitzle's Misty Protector]]
