## Steps

1. Find something edible to offer
   1. Obtain 2 [[Juicy cave fish|juicy cave fishes]] in the pool near {{waypoint 1061, -291, 3360}}
      - harvested from a **School of Juicy Fish**![School of Juicy Fish](images/School_of_Juicy_Fish.jpg)
   1. Obtain 2 [[Umami fungus|umami fungi]] behind the crystals near {{waypoint 1069, -289, 3350}}
      - harvested from a **Cluster of Fungi**![Cluster of Fungi](images/Cluster_of_Fungi.jpg)
   1. Obtain 1 [[Salty kelp]] in the pool near {{waypoint 1050, -291, 3367}} It is in the bubbles.
      - *Note: These are **inside the Den behind the Elder**. Track Harvestables does work on these. You can find all three ingredients in the pools that are behind the elder. The bunch of fungus is near the pool.*
      - harvested from a T**angle of Kelp**![Tangle of Kelp](images/Tangle_of_Kelp.jpg)
   1. Prepare a [[Juicy Fish Roll]] at the **Cooking Pot** at {{waypoint 1095.04, -295.19, 3377.81}} as an offering to show your respect to the elders.
      - ![Roekillik Cooking Pot](images/Roekillik_Cooking_Pot.jpg)
1. Return to the elder and give him the fish roll, then speak with him further.
1. Gather paper for the elder from the roll of [[Blank Parchment]], on the table at {{waypoint 1110.48, -292.63, 3377.90}}.  Look for a small tube on one of the tables near him.
   - ![Blank Parchment](images/Roekillik_Blank_Parchment.jpg)
1. Give the paper to him so he can draw you a map to the dwarven mines.
1. Return to The Duality.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
