I've taken on one last job for Lavic. He needs to have all of the witnesses of his assassination eliminated. I think this will be the last time I'll work for this guy.

## Steps

1. I need to travel into the [[Nektulos Forest]] and find the [[Citadel of Gul'thex]] and start from there. I'm to make it look like my tracks started around that keep.
   - Visit the [[Citadel of Gul'thex]] in [[Nektulos Forest]]
1. Collect 5 poison glands from [[Immense tree snake]]s
1. At [[Port Naythex]], kill:
   - 5-8 [[Nerius marauder]]s
   - 5-8 [[Nerius foreman|Nerius foremen]]
   - 5-8 [[Nerius seer]]s
1. Return to Lavic for your reward

## Rewards

- [[Lavic's Contact Ring]]
