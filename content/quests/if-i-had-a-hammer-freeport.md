## Description

"The local mender has offered to show me how to use some of the harvests I gathered to craft a small lucky charm using his anvil." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Obtain [[Recipe: Lucky Wolf Paw Charm]] from the table.
1. Obtain coal from the sack next to the table.
1. Read the recipe to scribe it and craft a [[Lucky Wolf Paw]] charm. It requires 1 [[Tin cluster]] and 1 [[Lead cluster]].
1. Visit [[Vissa D`Dbth]], the tradeskill tutor in [[West Freeport]] {{waypoint 31.14, -18.43, 111.68}} to learn more about crafting.

## Rewards

- At least 8s 3c
- [[Handbook of the Coalition of Tradesfolke]]
