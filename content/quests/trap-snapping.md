## Steps

1. Break 8 crocodile traps in the [[The Croc Caves|Croc Caves]] in the [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]]. The entrance to the caves is at {{waypoint -1205, -235, -1117}}.
   - First crocodile trap {{waypoint -1281, -233, -765}}
   - Second crocodile trap {{waypoint -1250, -233, -809}}
   - Third crocodile trap {{waypoint -1270, -237, -845}}
   - Fourth crocodile trap {{waypoint -1285, -237, -894}}
   - Fifth crocodile trap {{waypoint -1262, -237, -894}}
   - Sixth crocodile trap {{waypoint -1243, -237, -937}}
   - Seventh crocodile trap {{waypoint -1195, -237, -975}}
   - Eigth crocodile trap {{waypoint -1173, -237, -1095}}
     - Two [[Trap setter|trap setters]] may spawn and attack as you leave the caves, but killing them is not necessary.
1. Return to [[Barter Lord Feth Ardala|Feth]].
