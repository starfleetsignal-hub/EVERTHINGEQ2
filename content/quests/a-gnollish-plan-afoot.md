## Steps

1. The General has sent me to enter [[The Willow Wood: Besieged Hamlet|the Willow Wood]] via the gate in [[Elddar Grove]], [[Qeynos Province District]] in order to investigate reports of gnolls burrowing in under the walls. {{waypoint 810, -21, -564}}
1. Speak with a Willow Wood resident, [[Keterin Bowkite]], on the docks and ask her about the gnoll attacks. {{waypoint 836, -20, -759}}
1. Investigate the gnoll burrows at the southwestern end of the Willow Wood. {{waypoint 877, -16, -601}}
1. Click the gnoll burrow to enter the [[Forest Ruins: the Burrows|Forest Ruins: Burrows]]. Search the area for any clues as to how the gnolls obtained access.
   - The [[A Rockpaw destroyer|Rockpaw destroyers]] near the instance will not become aggro until you move closer to them.
1. On a crate, look for a glowing green parchment and collect it as evidence.
1. Exit the gates of the ruined fortress and locate the Qeynos Travel bell to leave. {{waypoint 893, -12, -559}}
1. Return to [[General Golias Sydwen]].
