## Description

*Flows of acid have washed out into Thalumbra from within Stygian Threshold, and flows of mental magic emanate from somewhere within Kralet Penumbra. I've been tasked with investigating the two sources, and putting a stop them both of them.*

## Steps

- Destroy enemy targets within Stygian Threshold and Kralet Penumbra.
1. Clear the toxic lair
   - [[Acinox]] in [[Stygian Threshold: The Toxic Pools (Event Heroic)]]
1. Defeat the mentalist
   - [[Urzzra-Uzulu]] in [[Kralet Penumbra: Uzulu Deep (Event Heroic)]]
