## Notes

- This quest is repeatable once per year.
- If you already received an [[Erollisi Day Cloak]] from previous years it will not be offered as a reward again since it is a Lore item.

## Steps

1. You will be sent to speak with several people in West [[Freeport]] and one in North Freeport.
   - [[Zak Silvanus (Freeport)|Zak Silvanus]] {{waypoint 296, -4, 16}} is a hardworking guy, with a sense of humor.  He prizes inner beauty. (Near the Champion's Coliseum, in the back)
   - [[Vixxi Starguide (Freeport)|Vixxi Starguide]] {{waypoint -100, -21, -83}} is attracted to tall broad-shouldered men.  Brains aren't everything! (Inside the Jade Tiger Inn, in North Freeport)
   - [[Marjorie Falconius (Freeport)|Marjorie Falconius]] {{waypoint 202, -3, 69}} is a very good provisioner, and is quite proud of the culinary masterpieces she makes. (Near the Champion's Coliseum, facing the Dreadnaught Plaza)
   - [[Amund MacRae (Freeport)|Amund MacRae]] {{waypoint 155, 0, 123}} interests include cards, ale and women. (Inside the Freeport Militia House, third floor)
   - [[Lysndra (Freeport)|Lysndra]] {{waypoint 34, -12, 91}} is a very active Kerra.  She finds hard work rewarding, and has several hobbies to keep herself busy even when she is not working. (Inside the Coalition of Tradesfolk, second floor)
   - [[Beldrin Findlebolt (Freeport)|Beldrin Findlebolt]] {{waypoint 27, -18, 82}} is a male that excels at tinkering and enjoys good food. (Inside the Coalition of Tradesfolk, first floor)
   - [[Colt Longpier (Freeport)|Colt Longpier]] {{waypoint 133, -4, -84}} is a lighthearted guy.  He is an outdoor enthusiast, and has a soft spot for animals. (Inside the Eldar Stables and Livery)
   - [[Tyluna Whittlebit (Freeport)|Tyluna Whittlebit]] {{waypoint 199, -2, -118}} enjoys carving figurines and experiencing nature.  Cats hold a special place in her heart. (Inside the Freeport Reserve)
1. After speaking to each, return to Diana Heartstrings who will then ask you how to match them all.<br>**WARNING:** *If you don't answer the matches right you will have to do the whole Quest again!*<br>  The pairs are:
   - Zak and **Lysndra **both appreciate industry and hard work.
   - Tyluna and *Colt *share a love for animals.
   - Amund and *Vixxi *each admire fine physical specimens.
   - Marjorie will please *Beldrin *with her cooking.
   - (Alternate pairings are Zak and Colt, Tyluna and Lysndra.)
1. Speak with [[Diana Heartstrings]] again to finish the quest
