## Starting the Quest

1. Purchase [[Spectacles of Owlsight]] from [[Merchant Thryst]] in [[The Barren Sky]] at {{waypoint -601.15, -46.25, -434.45}} for 3 [[Prismatic Pike|Prismatic Pikes]] (harvested from fishing nodes or purchased from Broker). Thryst will only offer the spectacles if all of the following have been completed:
   - Reaching level 130
   - Greater than -20,000 faction with [[The Windgazer (Faction)]]
   - Completing the achievement [[Live Free or Sky Hard]]
   - Completing the signature quest [[Blood of the Brood]]
   - Completing the quest [[Culture of the Aviaks]]
   - Completing the quest [[The Hatchery]]
1. Harvest a "dropped note" in [[Isle of Mara]] (found mine in the Village of Shin).  **MUST** have purchased the spectacles to see the note. The note is trackable and has a respawn of roughly 4 minutes.
1. Examine the harvested note called [[An Urgent Letter]] to start the quest.

## Notes

1. Step 7 can be done in advance by completing the repeatable Prelude quest [[Imprinted Memory|Imprinting History Lesson]]  at least once.

## Steps

1. Seek out Irko and Eriki by talking to locals on Felfeather Isle (In the Splendor Sky Aerie).
   - Speak to Juati Felfeather at {{waypoint 996.48, 168.05, 723.16}}
   - Speak with Matron Potoo Felfeather at {{waypoint 1067.31, 181.80, 812.96}} (southernmost hut on Felfeather Isle)
   - Speak with Rejjen at {{waypoint 954.42, 166.15, 819.03}}
1. Seek out Erki in the Elders' area behind the main huts. Erki is at {{waypoint 1105.51, 177.06, 697.11}}, and the guards will let you through now.
1. Speak to Matro Potoo Felfeather at {{waypoint 1067.50, 181.80, 814.55}} to receive access to the Felfeather Family Album.
1. Pick up the book "[[History of the Felfeathers Family Album]]" at {{waypoint 1088.89, 178.53, 718.03}} and examine it to read the history of the Felfeathers' power. The quest updates when you read the final entry and close the book.
1. Again examine the "[[History of the Felfeathers Family Album]]" in your inventory. You will now get the option to observe the first ritual between Erki and Jentis. This takes you to [[Lineage of the Felfeathers: The Ritual (Heritage)]], a non-combat zone where you watch the NPCs perform the ritual. Click on the book podium at {{waypoint 433, -13, 154}} to exit back to Splendor Sky Aerie.
1. Pick up and examine the album from your inventory yet again. The third option is now to observe the most recent ritual between Erki and Jentis. This takes you to [[Lineage of the Felfeathers: Ritual Chamber (Heritage)]], a non-combat zone where you watch the NPCs' failed attempt to perform the ritual. Exit via the book podium again.
1. Pick up and examine the album from your inventory once more to observe the original ritual between Jeefa and Parat. This takes you to  [[Imprinted Memory: Origins of the Felfeather (Event Solo)]]. Complete the instance and exit.
   - **Note**: The quest automatically updates to the next step if you have previously completed this dungeon.
   - *(A previous note suggests that the auto-update may not work if you have the associated repeatable quest [[Imprinted Memory|Imprinting History Lesson]]  in your journal).*
1. Place the album back on the shelf where you found it.
1. Speak to Erki Felfeather at {{waypoint 1105.51, 177.06, 697.11}}.
1. In search of Irko I am tasked to look for clues as to his whereabouts.
   - *The clues appear as "green shineys" and can be acquired in any order.*
   - Seek out the whereabouts of Irko Felfeather within less difficult areas to hide. Clear any three of the zones listed below and then look for the green shinies at the waypoints provided.
     - Within [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion (Solo)|Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion [Solo]]] at {{waypoint -981.28, 169.84, 635.64}}. Requires defeating [[Kusala'Din]].
     - Within [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma (Solo)|Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma [Solo]]] at {{waypoint 816.42, 43.88, 10.11}}. Requires defeating [[Miasmasula]].
     - Within [[Zimara Breadth: Razing the Razelands (Solo)|Zimara Breadth: Razing the Razelands [Solo]]] at {{waypoint 120.68, 158.32, 31.97}}. Requires defeating [[Queen Era'selka]].
     - Within [[Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent (Solo)|Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent [Solo]]] at {{waypoint -226.94, 433.05, 863.68}}. Requires defeating [[Fuejenyrus]].
   - Seek out the whereabouts of Irko Felfeather within more difficult areas to hide. Any three of the following locations complete this step.
     - *You can be zoned into another person's cleared instance to get the updates, provided you have previously killed the zone boss at least once.*
     - Within [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion (Event Heroic I)|Splendor Sky Aerie: Vale Invasion [Event Heroic I]]] at {{waypoint -914.81, 128.57, 644.29}}. Requires defeating [[Kusala'Din]].
     - Within [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma (Event Heroic I)|Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma [Event Heroic I]]] at {{waypoint 833.00, 44.44, 29.25}}. Requires defeating [[Miasmula]].
     - Within [[Zimara Breadth: Razing the Razelands (Heroic I)|Zimara Breadth: Razing the Razelands [Heroic I]]] at {{waypoint 146.36, 157.18, -33.85}}. Requires defeating [[Queen Era'selka]].
     - Within [[Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent (Heroic I)|Zimara Breadth: Strungstone Ascent [Heroic I]]] at {{waypoint -240.06, 393.68, 830.53}}. Requires defeating [[Fuejenyrus]].
   - Seek out the whereabouts of Irko Felfeather within the [[Splendor Sky Aerie|Aerie]]
     - Speak to [[Fupi Felfeather]] at {{waypoint -555.84, 80.94, 370.51}}
     - Examine Irko's Favorite Perch at {{waypoint -662.35, 279.41, -59.93}} (Peg sticking out of the rock, above [[Ragehorn]]). Must land on the rock above it for it to become clickable.
     - Examine Irko's Favorite Resting Spot at {{waypoint 304.87, 291.96, 130.02}}   (above [[Gloomclaw]]). Must land on the Limb  {{waypoint 305, 291, 129}}.
1. Speak to [[Winston the Wise]] within the [[Splendor Sky Aerie|Aerie]] at {{waypoint 421.07, 282.96, 115.89}}.
1. Speak with [[Matron Potoo Felfeather]] at {{waypoint 1067.50, 181.80, 814.55}} to gain access to the family crypts. Examine the Felfeather Family Crypt Key.

## Rewards

- [[Felfeather Family Crypt Key]] (examine to begin the next quest)
- Unlocks the [[Of Duty and Fate: Lineage of the Felfeathers (Achievement)]]
