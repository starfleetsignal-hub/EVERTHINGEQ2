## Steps

1. Gather 8 **splendorzite crystals** at [[Wishbone Beck]] around {{waypoint -89.27, 43.32, 482.35}}.  Some possible locations:![Splendorzite Crystal](images/Splendorzite_Crystal.png)
   - {{waypoint -169.01, 40.24, 614.12}}, {{waypoint -157.90, 37.63, 636.34}}
   - {{waypoint -138.32, 39.37, 646.20}}, {{waypoint -128.56, 41.93, 629.30}}
   - {{waypoint -105.76, 40.72, 592.63}}, {{waypoint -116.65, 39.41, 542.70}}
   - {{waypoint -85.18, 41.68, 516.35}}, {{waypoint -90.78, 41.64, 483.07}}
   - You may be attacked by [[A blade storm]] while gathering crystals.
1. Return to [[Coursequill]] and speak to him.
1. ![Hoot totem](images/Hoot_totem.png)Perform a couple of tasks:
   - Erect 6 **hoot totems** along [[Wishbone Beck]]. Some possible locations:
     - {{waypoint -180.56, 41.81, 601.90}}, {{waypoint -153.77, 42.81, 523.75}}
     - {{waypoint -121.59, 41.67, 455.66}}, {{waypoint -92.97, 39.65, 402.85}}
     - {{waypoint -50.17, 39.33, 338.10}}, {{waypoint -20.33, 40.64, 295.58}}
   - Kill 8 [[A blade storm|blade storms]] along the river.
1. Return to [[Coursequill]] and speak to him.
1. Go to [[Smeltfield Vale]] {{waypoint -594.25, 60.52, 477.84}} to carry out Coursequill's tasks:
   - ![Metallic crystal](images/Metallic_crystals.png)Slay 8 [[A metalmorphic besieger|metalmorphic besiegers]] around {{waypoint -722.91, 77.59, 600.63}}
   - Obtain 6 greenwood pinecones by slaying [[A fire-ravaged greenwood|fire-ravaged greenwoods]].
   - Destroy 6 **metallic crystals**. Some possible locations include":
     - {{waypoint -655.63, 63.06, 546.37}}, {{waypoint -698.57, 63.44, 555.59}}
     - {{waypoint -728.72, 58.87, 522.64}}, {{waypoint -772.29, 58.45, 502.61}}
     - {{waypoint -777.58, 60.52, 547.30}}, {{waypoint -753.90, 67.45, 568.78}}
1. Return to [[Coursequill]] and speak to him.
