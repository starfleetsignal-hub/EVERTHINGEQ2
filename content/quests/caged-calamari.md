## Steps

1. Check the 4 fish traps
   - first {{waypoint -1297, -546, 3458}}
   - second {{waypoint -1337, -546, 3402}}
   - third {{waypoint -1293, -546, 3417}}
   - fourth {{waypoint -1284, -546, 3402}}
1. Speak with [[Kian]]
1. Kill 8 [[A shell pincher]], [[A shell piercer]] or [[A shell crusher]] in the SW on the beach at {{waypoint -1037, -536, 3932}}
1. apply repellent on the 4 fish traps
   - first {{waypoint -1297, -546, 3458}}
   - second {{waypoint -1337, -546, 3402}}
   - third {{waypoint -1293, -546, 3417}}
   - fourth {{waypoint -1284, -546, 3402}}
1. Return to [[Kian]]
