## Prerequisites

- +30,000 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**

## Steps

1. Collect a vial of poison from one of the bottles in front of [[Master Poisoner Kammani]].
1. Enter [[Dartain's Fortress]] and dump the poison into the food supplies at {{waypoint 524, 121, -243}}.
1. Return to [[Master Poisoner Kammani]].

## Rewards

- At least 55g 70s 61c
- +2500 faction with **Caertaxian Legion**
- 1 [[Mark of Manaar]]
