This quest is part 6 of a 6-quest series in the [[Qeynos Timeline]].

## Description

"The scouts were able to push through and around the Abandoned Village, setting up camp in an alcove on the eastern side. I need to contact Sorcerer Oofala, in charge of protecting the camp. He has set up some staves that should ward off the evil temporarily, so once I make it around the village it should be safe. To get to the camp I need to head past Dead Canyon and then east down the southern wall of the village." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Report to [[Sorcerer Oofala]] {{waypoint 48, -3, -168}} at the far end of [[The Abandoned Village (Queen's Colony)|The Abandoned Village]].
1. ![Totems look like this.](images/Source_of_Evil_Quest_Totem.png)Destroy 3 totems, scattered about the abandoned village. They look like giant wooden hands sticking out of the ground.
1. Return to Oofala.
1. Kill [[The Dark Blademaster]] {{waypoint 160, 0, -177}} near the fog on [[Sapswill Hill]].
1. Return to Oofala.
1. Seek out [[High Chieftain Grexx]] {{waypoint -67, 13, -165}} at the [[The Last Stand]], a tree house east of the village.
1. ![A Dark Doorway, the entrance to The Source of Evil.](images/A_Dark_Doorway.png)Enter [[The Source of Evil (Zone)|The Source of Evil]] {{waypoint -112, 4, -172}} a cave to the northeast of the tree house (interact with the Dark Doorway).
1. Kill [[Vith L'Tar]] and any other non-pet monsters that spawn.
   - You need to destroy the 4 totems in the cave.
   - You may need to kill groups of tainted goblins to get to the totems.
   - As soon as you near Vith L'Tar, you will begin to spawn waves of undead. The only way to stop the waves from continuing is to destroy all the totems.
   - Once you destroy the 4th totem, Vith L'Tar becomes attackable.
1. Return to High Chieftain Grexx to report your success.
1. Return to [[Murrar Shar (Queen's Colony)|Murrar Shar]] {{waypoint -15, -5, 182}} in front of [[Myrrin's Tower]] for your reward.
