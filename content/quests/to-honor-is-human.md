## Steps

1. Enter the [[Starcrest Commune: Shattered Sanctum|Starcrest Commune]] gate from [[South Qeynos]] in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]]. {{waypoint 693.26, -20.46, 267.73}}
1. Speak to the Kerra [[Jeretis]]. {{waypoint 708, -20, 260}}
1. Return to [[Patricia Benson]].
1. Investigate the events surrounding the closure of the [[Nettleville: Strange Tidings|Nettleville]] gate by speaking to Guard [[Rendyl]],who is blocking access to the gate. {{waypoint 681, -21, 271}}
1. Return to [[Patricia Benson]].
