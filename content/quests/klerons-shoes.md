This quest is part of a 6-quest series; the first five can be done in any order.

## Steps

1. Kill [[A motley kodiak|motley kodiaks]] until you obtain 18 *snazzy bearskins*.  They're found between [[Mound Lake]] and [[The Funeral Pyre]]. There is also some [[Grizzled shadowmaw kodiak|Grizzled Shadowmaw Kodiaks]] around {{waypoint -1622, 17, -791}}.
1. Kill [[A shadowed man (Nektulos Forest)|shadowed men]] until you obtain 2 updates. Find shadowed men south of [[Citadel of Gul'thex]], behind the beach path.  {{waypoint 600, 0, -516}}
1. Rub the shadowed men items on a mushroom behind the [[Citadel of Gul'thex]]. {{waypoint 708, 0, -709}}
1. Return to [[Kleron Asana]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Acolyte's Wrought Plate Boots]] (Clerics)
  - [[Bandit's Light Iron Chain Shoes]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Beguiler's Embroidered Shoes]] (All Mages)
  - [[Expert's Fine Cloth Foot Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Preserver's Cured Leather Boots]] (Druids)
  - [[Sentry's Hammered Sabatons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Soothsayer's Bound Chain Boots]] (Shamans)
