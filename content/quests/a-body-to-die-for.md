## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 60+

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Lilly Ironforge]] at [[The Jewel Box]] in [[North Qeynos]]. {{waypoint 385, -19, -243}}
1. Travel to [[The Ironforge Estate]] and locate the entrance to the [[Ironforge Catacombs]] behind the manor off the North West corner. {{waypoint 511, -20, -351}}
1. Enter the catacombs.
   - Slay [[A ceremony guard|ceremony guards]] and [[A ceremony attendee|ceremony attendees]].
   - [[Kyle Antihilus Bayle]] will root you before you can interrupt the ceremony.
   - Bayle will successfully take over Gavin's body and knock you unconscious.
1. You will awaken in a cell within the catacombs.
   - Speak to [[Lilly Ironforge]] and tell her of her brother's fate.
   - Click the bars to open the cell and escape back to the [[Qeynos Capitol District]].
