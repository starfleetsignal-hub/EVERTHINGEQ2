## Description

The spirits of Castle Valmarr haunt their former outpost in hopes that someone will release them from the curse of Gynok Moltor. To this end I've been tasked to find the destroyed statues symbolizing the might of their former glory. It is hoped that once the statues are repaired, they might assist in the sanctification of the tainted keep.

## Steps

1. Repair 6 broken statues dedicated to Mithaniel Marr within [[Befallen: Cavern of the Afflicted]]
   - {{waypoint 38, 0, -70}} in the top floor room with Maledictive Terror
   - {{waypoint 135, -50, -34}} in Carrion Nest
   - {{waypoint 118, -56, -117}} in the Grotto
   - {{waypoint 96, -64, -155}} at the entrance to Bonegrinder Lair
   - {{waypoint 148, -72, -150}} inside Bonegrinder Lair
   - {{waypoint 25, -64, -164}} at the east end of hallway, opposite Bonegrinder Lair
1. Return to [[Squire Hadley]]
