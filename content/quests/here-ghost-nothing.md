## Notes

- The 'Argent Sanctuary' half of [[Ballads of Zimara: Vaashkaani Crescendo]] quest can be done concurrently with this quest.
- This quest can be completed in a cleared zone and can also be done in the Heroic I and Heroic II zones.
- Click range on trapped ghosts is about 34 meters.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Vaashkaani: Argent Sanctuary (Solo)|Vaashkaani: Argent Sanctuary [Solo]]] {{waypoint -491, 162, -609}}
1. Use the [[Blessed Stratos Censer]] on 8 different trapped souls around the zone.
1. Return to [[Priest Oo'Rah]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1000 faction with **Isles of Wisdom**
- [[Conjured Enforcer Plushie]]
