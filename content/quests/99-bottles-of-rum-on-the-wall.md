## Notes

- This quest cannot be deleted from the [[Quest Journal]]. To delete it, travel to the [[The Village of Shin|Village of Shin]] in Mara and speak to [[An escaped fabled spirit|an escaped fabled spirit]] {{waypoint -84.41, -2.52, 82.88}}.

## Steps

1. Find 99 bottles of rum on the wall in any of the F.S. Distillery zones
   - *Bottles that count for [[11 Bottles of Rum on the Wall]] or [[22 Bottles of Rum on the Wall]] will update this quest as well*
