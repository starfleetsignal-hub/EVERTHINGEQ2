## Notes

- To save time, complete [[Eneek Needs a Snack!]] at the same time, since it also requires Slagslore ettins.

## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[A Slagslore ettin|slagslore ettins]] {{waypoint -1, 226, -223}} across the rock bridge, east of [[Kamp Krulkiel]].
1. Return to [[Paix Hormezyar]].
1. Speak to [[Holdyn Mackendrick]], also in [[West Fort Irontoe]].
