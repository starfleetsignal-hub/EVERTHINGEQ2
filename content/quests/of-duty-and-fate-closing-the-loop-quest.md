## Steps

1. Find a way to learn the secrets of the Labyrinth of Colors
1. Once in the raid zone, at Vaashkani Teleporter, choose Sovereign Requirements Room.
1. Hail Furisa the Swift.
1. Once in the maze, look for the exit that has your name on it when you click.  Looks like a pink slit mirror.
