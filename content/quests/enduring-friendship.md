## Steps

1. Collect the ghostly dwarf's belongings.
   - Locate the dwarf's broken axe. {{waypoint -107, -16, -104}}
   - Search the halls to find the shield at {{waypoint -66, -62, -270}}, in the same room as the statue of honor
   - Locate the dwarf's lost mug as well. {{waypoint -203, -31, -248}}
1. Hail [[A lost dwarven spirit]] near the beginning of the zone {{waypoint -96, -1, 35}}.
1. Find the dwarf's lost companion, [[A lost barbarian spirit]] in the room containing the mug {{waypoint -186, -36, -196}}.
1. Find lost pieces of the barbarian's spirit by killing four [[A daz spiriteater|daz spiriteaters]].
   - These mobs will keep spawning as long as someone is on this part of the quest.
   - Location of the room where I found three of the four [[A daz spiriteater|daz spiriteaters]] {{waypoint -166.43, -33.69, -220.52}}. The fourth was at {{waypoint -209.49, -13.69, -210.88}}. All are trackable.
1. Speak with the barbarian.
1. Return to the dwarf and guide him to the barbarian.
   - This is a group update, and everyone in the group must hail the dwarf at the front of the zone at the same time.
   - The dwarf will stop three times and ask for directions.  Guide him as follows: left, straight, right.
1. Hail the dwarf once he reaches the barbarian to complete the quest.
