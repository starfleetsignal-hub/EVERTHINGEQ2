## Notes

- Fouran's missions serve as a way to reduce time needed to complete research of [[Darou Badge of Trust]] in main crafting timeline.
- These missions reset every 3 hours. You can find the time until next reset in your Quest Journal, Mission timers tab (filter by Darou Village:Researcher Fouran Researching Requisitions).
- After completing the full Destination Unknown crafting timeline once, this quest will give bonus time reduction to alternate characters doing these quests (16 hours each).
- A recipe for [[Darou Delight]] is scribed in your recipe book when you accept the quest.
- This quest requires:
  - 25x [[Formless Kindling]]

## Steps

1. Harvest
   - 10x [[Gorroth Garbanzo]] from sturdy shrubs
   - 18x [[Darou Daikon]] from sturdy shrubs
   - 25x [[Detritus Root]] from sturdy roots
1. Craft [[Darou Delight]]      (Requires a Workbench)
   - Requires 25x [[Formless Kindling]]
1. Hail [[Researcher Fouran]]

## Rewards

- [[Status Coin (75,000)]]
- At least 5p 46g 55s 16c
