I have seen the glaufaye, and come to realize their suffering is due to a preservation spell that has kept them alive, and outside the cycle of life. Thaneni, the elven ghost I found wandering near Zou'Lidelas, has given me his arcane staff, which he believes is a threat to the spell they weary of.

## Steps

1. Pick up [[Thaneni's Staff]] {{waypoint -365.4, 164.23, 148.96}} and equip it.
1. Attack [[Arideva]] {{waypoint -453.63, 222.14, 337}}.
1. You get pulled away by a force before you can kill Arideva completely.
1. He is sending you to an instance to kill the spell from within.
1. After finishing the instance you get send back automatically to Thulumbra.
1. Talk to Arideva again.
1. Return to [[Thaneni]].
