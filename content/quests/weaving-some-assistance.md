## Notes

- This is a continously repeatable quest without a lockout timer.
- With sufficient faction there are house items, plushies and mounts to be purchased in the [[The Sinking Sands]] from the [[Nomads of Ro]] merchant [[Banti]] {{waypoint -1232.78, -214.69, -299.69}}.
- Faction can be tracked under the 'Deserts of Ro' as 'Nomads of Ro'.

## Steps

1. Click the woodworkers' crate at {{waypoint -1212, -214, -291}} beside Khinir to receive the recipe (auto-scribes).
1. Sunshard Quartz is gathered from Sand Mounds (not trackable) on the beach in Sinking Sands. Not every mound will have a shard, but when they do, they yield 3 sunshards. You may have to zoom in your camera view tighter to see the shards. Check for mounds around:
   - {{waypoint -1331.12, -299, -222.06}}  ![](images/World_event_-_Sand_Mound.jpg)
   - {{waypoint -1378.22, -227.94, -95.48}}
   - 1=-792 2=-115 3=-267 (along orc highway)
1. Craft 6 of the requested item (either rattan cots or sturdy reed baskets). The top tier of the combine yields two, so three combines will finish this step. There is a woodworking table near the Sinking Sands dock at {{waypoint -1455, -225, -427}}. Each combine will use:
   - 1 [[Sunshard Quartz]]
   - 2 common harvested wood (any tier)
   - 3 common harvested roots (any tier)
   - 2 common harvested metal (any tier)
   - 3 [[Glowing Sandpaper|glowing sandpaper]]
1. Return to Khinir to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- 5s 54c
- +1,000 faction with **Hizite Nomads of Ro**
