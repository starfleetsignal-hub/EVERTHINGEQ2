- [[Tranquil Windcloth Slippers]]
- [[Tranquil Windcloth Mitts]]
- [[Tranquil Windcloth Pantaloons]]
- [[Woven Horned Leather Bracers]]
- [[Woven Horned Leather Gloves]]
- [[Woven Horned Leather Boots]]
- [[Woven Horned Leather Pants]]

  - +300 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +300 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +300 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +300 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
