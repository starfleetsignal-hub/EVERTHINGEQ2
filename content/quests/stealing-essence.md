## Steps

1. Enter [[The Crypt of T'haen]]. The entrance is within [[The Tombs of Night]] at {{waypoint -107, -32, 102}}.
1. Kill [[Vaera L'Far]]: {{waypoint 35, 0, -21}}
1. Kill [[Deiga Q'Rin]]: {{waypoint -15, 0, -8}}
1. Kill [[Ashokh the Slayer]], who spawns immediately after killing Vaera L'Far or Deiga Q'Rin.
   - See the talk page for a discussion of the possible problems you might have with the order of killing.
1. Return to [[K'shar Haelbreng]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 59s 57c
