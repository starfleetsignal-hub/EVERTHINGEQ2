## NOTE

The corrupt brethren you are looking for during this quest are actually up on Marred Plateau near the Erudin Gardens, not down with the other Terrorfangs. They all have "coerced" in their name, and will only spawn as you approach the large boulders around {{waypoint -307, 519, -119}}.

They will aggro if you get too close, so be careful as you cannot use the stones for the quest updates while in combat. Respawn time is fairly quick.

## Steps

1. Use the three stones to perform rituals on the coerced Terrorfangs, located on the [[Marred Plateau]] {{waypoint -361, 526, 33}}. The coerced Terrorfangs will attack after you use a stone.
   - Attempt three rituals of singing winds.
   - Attempt three rituals of fiery sun.
   - Attempt three rituals of healing flora.
1. Return to [[Imba Shiro|Imba]].
