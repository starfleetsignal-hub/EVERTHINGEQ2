## Steps

1. Kill 4 [[A Tizmak augur|Tizmak augurs]].Left side of cave tunnel,1 in a group of 3 {{waypoint -290, -398, -87}}{{waypoint -214, -411, -38}}
   - **WARNING** - **Killing the 4th Augur** will make you aggro to Tizmak, so use invisibility to leave the caves.  Depending on which quest line you choose to follow you can later regain it.
1. Speak with [[Zalyn D'Kilned]].

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- -10,000 faction with **Tizmak Clan**
