## Description

"Sentry Talvee has asked that I speak with Sentry Trillis, who can be found to the west, near Amethyst Pond. Trillis has encountered a threat to the Nursery and he may require assistance." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Sentry Trillis]] {{waypoint -27, -37, -734}}
1. Speak to [[Scout Moranne]] {{waypoint -11, -7, -729}} (up the tree behind Trillis)
1. Return to Sentry Trillis

## Rewards

- At least 1s 15c
- [[Faydark flower torch]]

## Notes

- This quest was reworked for [[LU57]].  The old version of the quest can be viewed [here](https://eq2.fandom.com/wiki/Sentry_Trillis%27_Report?oldid=216876).

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Sentry Talvee "One of my fellow Sentries, Trillis, sent me a report that said he saw a Tuwasp conversing with a grobin not too far from here. I think there might be an even bigger threat behind the scenes. Can you imagine what would happen if the grobins and the bixies were working together against us?"
- Player Character: "Where is Trillis? I should speak with him."
- Sentry Talvee: "Follow the road west, and you won't be able to miss him. Sentry Trillis is at the base of a scouting post that's positioned high on one of the trees, which overlooks the Amethyst Pond. Best of luck to you. I hope the two of you can figure this mystery out!"
- Player Character: "I'll do my best. Farewell to you."

I should report to Sentry Trillis. Report to Sentry Trillis.

- Sentry Trillis: "Shh... Be as quiet as a butterfly. We don't want those grobins to find our lookout post."
- Player Character: "I will be stealthy. Sentry Talvee sent me to speak with you."
- Sentry Trillis: "Ah, I'm glad he sent help! An alliance between the Tuwasp and grobins is highly suspicious. We need to find out what the grobins are up to, and if it will affect the Nursery."
- Player Character: "How would we do that?"
- Sentry Trillis: "First, we'll need to get Scout Moranne's report. She's stationed on the mushroom cap, high above us. It's the perfect vantage point to keep an eye on those scheming grobins."
- Player character: "How do I get up there?"
- Sentry Trillis: "You can climb the bark behind me. Any bark that looks like this has very good hand and footholds for you to climb. Keep a lookout for other climbable surfaces!"
- Player Character: "I will! Thank you, Sentry Trillis."

Sentry Trillis has asked me to climb the tree and check in with Scout Moranne. I should climb the tree and check in with Scout Moranne. Speak to Scout Moranne.

- Scout Moranne: "Did Sentry Trillis send you up here? There are grobins swarming around the Amethys Pond. From this distance, I can't see exactly what they're doing, but it's a safe bet that they're up to no good."
- Player Character: "Got it."

Scout Moranne has spotted grobins all over the Amethyst Pond! I should tell Sentry Trillis about the grobins. Return to Sentry Trillis.

- Sentry Trillis: "So, the Amethyst Pond is crawling with grobins. That pond is a natural spring that feeds the blooms of the Nursery. We need to clear out their scouts patrolling around the pond first, so that our sentries can get in there with as little resistance as possible."

Sentry Trillis needs my assistance.

I have met with Sentry Trillis, and I am ready to lend him my assistance.
