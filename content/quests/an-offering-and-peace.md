## Notes

- If you happen to lose [[A medallion of urns]] you can acquire a new one from the mailbox at the entrance to the Peacock Club in Maj'Dul. {{waypoint -225, 146, 82}}

## Steps

1. Find clues in the [[The Halls of War (The Silent City)|Halls of War]]. Inspect both of the following:
   - ![A tablet of runes](images/Image:A_tablet_of_runes.jpg): {{waypoint 224, 30, 344}}
   - ![A tablet of runes](images/Image:A_tablet_of_runes2.jpg): {{waypoint 82, 31, 504}}
1. Kill 6 [[An Anuk' Sul chancellor (Silent City)|Anuk' Sul chancellors]] located all around the Halls of War.
1. Go to the 'Serpent of Rhonen'Fyr'. {{waypoint 141, 15, 299}}
1. Inspect a ![ceremonial weapons rack](images/Image:Ceremonial_weapons_rack.jpg). {{waypoint 214, 0, 395}}
1. Slay the following monsters, in any order:
   - 3 [[A consul of war|consuls of war]]
   - 5 [[An emissary of war|emissaries of war]] {{waypoint 70, 7, 414}}. Unfortunately, there are only three of this mob and they are on a 5-10 minute respawn timer.
1. Reinspect the ceremonial weapons rack.
1. Inspect the ![Rune of Arbo](images/Image:Rune_of_Arbo.jpg) (5 minute respawn) at {{waypoint 149, 0, 365}}. If Rune Master Arbolis is not up, click on the weapon rack again to spawn him and he will place the Rune of Arbo on the ground.

- [[Bangle of Divine Knowledge]]
- [[Entropic Vine Covered Leggings]]
- [[Gloves of Swift Strike]]
- [[Rhoen'Fyr's Gloves of Command]]
- [[Skullcap of Inner Focus]]
