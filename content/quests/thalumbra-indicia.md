This is the final collection quest at the top of a hierarchy of collection quests covering the whole Terrors of Thalumbra expansion.<br>
This is not a normal collection quest. The collection members are not obtained by harvesting shinies.<br>
Seven of the collection members are obtained from the completion of other collection quests, making this collection a "meta-collection". However, in this case, all of those earlier collection quests are themselves "meta-collections", making this more of a "meta-meta-collection"!
Note that the eighth collection member listed above (Indicium of the Ill-Seen One) is obtained by clicking the brazier in the Contested zone. This item is NO TRADE.
