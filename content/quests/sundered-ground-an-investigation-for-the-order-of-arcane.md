*The evil version of this quest is [[Sundered Ground, an Investigation for The Spurned]]*

## Steps

1. Defeat [[An undergolem|undergolems]] "that are a challenge to you" in search of clues.
   - Undergolem areas can be seen from quite a distance, as there are rocks and dirt being thrown up in a big cloud. (see this talk page for locations)
   - Loot all the bodies. The [[Arcane Umbra Gravel]] you will want to save for later.
1. When you encounter [[An undertok]] clockwork, slay it and loot [[A clockwork mechanism]] from its corpse.
1. Return to [[Mathal Dewpetal]].
1. Go talk to [[Zeen Corblecog]] in [[Tunare's Pages]] {{waypoint 463.12, 91.27, 343.18}}.
1. Get 8 more undertok clockwork mechanisms.
1. Return to Zeen Corblecog.
1. Speak to [[Queen Amree]] in the [[Fae Royal Hall]] {{waypoint 422.45, 136.62, 262.62}}.
