## Steps

1. Find the 4 other elders in the swamp.
   - Find Elder Piunt at {{waypoint 39, -745, -294}}
   - Find Elder Teiut at {{waypoint 150, -745, -42}}
   - Find Elder Pultin at  {{waypoint -285, -745, 109}}
   - Find Elder Darcfi at  {{waypoint -524, -744, -131}}
