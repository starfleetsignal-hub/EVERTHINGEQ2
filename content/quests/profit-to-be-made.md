## Steps

1. Enter the [[Temple Street: Vault of Necromechanica]] in South Freeport {{waypoint -174, -36, 326}}
1. Defeat 6 clockwork machines within Temple Street
   - [[A necrotik]] (wanders with [[A necromechanist]])
   - [[A necrotok]]
   - [[A necromech]]
1. Collect 6 blueprints from within Temple Street
   - on top of barrel {{waypoint 52, 4, 60}}
   - on top of barrel {{waypoint 41, 4, 106}}
   - on ground {{waypoint 11, 3, 108}}
   - on floor inside building {{waypoint -18, 3, 87}}
   - on crate on docks {{waypoint -42, 4, 104}} (note: this is outside the highlighted quest area, but it is accurate)
   - on table {{waypoint 87, -4, 118}}
   - on table inside building {{waypoint 8, 4, 77}}(note: this location is not accurate?)
- Return to [[Val'il]]

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Seafury Buccaneers**
