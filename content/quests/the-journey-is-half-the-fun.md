## Prerequisites

- This Heritage Quest can be obtained once you become level 15, however you must be at least level 30 to complete the Feerrott run.

## Steps

### The Antonica Race

![Antonica race route](images/Antonica.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Selinda Whispersong]] in Antonica to begin the Antonican race. {{waypoint -380, 0, 210}}
1. Run the Antonica race within the time limit of 9:00 (the route is shown at right).
   1. [[The Bells of Vhalen]]: {{waypoint -1300, 38, 45}}
   1. [[The Keep of the Gnollslayers]]: {{waypoint -2121, -46, 415}}
   1. [[The Ruins of Caltorsis]]: Near the shores, surrounded by undead. {{waypoint -1383, -16, -720}}
   1. [[The Tower of the Oracles]]: {{waypoint -1024, 13, -614}}
   1. [[Keep of the Ardent Needle (POI)|Keep of the Ardent Needle]]: {{waypoint -554, -14, -344}} near [[Knight Grel Stoneshearer]]
   1. Back to the start line between the two guard towers: {{waypoint -380, 0, 210}}
1. Speak again to [[Selinda Whispersong|Selinda]] to update the quest.

### The Commonlands Race

![Commonlands race route](images/Commonlands.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Sian D'ior]] in [[The Commonlands]] to begin the Commonlands race. {{waypoint -946, -49, -56}}
1. Run the Commonlands race within the time limit of 10:30. The route is shown at right. Make sure you plan your route here, this race will be tight!
   1. [[The Crossroads]]: {{waypoint -488, -45, -317}}
   1. [[Lucan's Mount]]: {{waypoint 10, -15, -741}}
      - ([[Terrathud]] may be at the top of the mount, beware he has a knockback attack, but you can fall safely back into the water.
   1. [[The Tower of Zarvonn]]: {{waypoint 896, -45, -518}}
   1. [[Ring of Nature]]: {{waypoint 686, -60, -95}}
   1. [[The Ruins of Val`marr]]: {{waypoint 378, -47, 338}}
   1. [[Turmoil Cemetary]]: {{waypoint -1296, -69, 339}}
   1. Run through the towers near Sian: {{waypoint -930, -48, -14}}
1. Speak again to Sian to update the quest and get your first reward.

### The Thundering Steppes Race

![Thundering Steppes race route](images/Thunderingsteppes.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Lessa Gentare]] on the dock in the [[The Thundering Steppes]] to begin the Thundering Steppes race. {{waypoint -431, -21, -396}}
1. Run the Thundering Steppes race within the time limit of 13:30 minutes.
   1. [[Village of Thundermist]]: {{waypoint 550, -10, -27}}
      - Take the griffin to Village of Thundermist
   1. [[Deadview Pass]]: {{waypoint 919, 8, 603}}
   1. [[Shrine of the Sirens]]: {{waypoint 1481, -20, 239}}
      - About 50m out from beach as far west as you can go on the map - the shrine is a square building under the water. You must SWIM DOWN TO IT!!
   1. [[Shrine of Decay]]: {{waypoint 391, 1, 1188}}
   1. [[Coldwind Cove]]: {{waypoint 235, -22, 1350}}
   1. [[The Grave Pool]]: {{waypoint 130, -22, 753}}
   1. Through posts on docks {{waypoint -494, -21, -400}}.
      - If you have evac, you can use this to return to the dock quickly. Otherwise, you can take the griffon as shown on the map (green line).
1. Speak again to [[Lessa Gentare|Lessa]] to update the quest.

### The Nektulos Forest Race

![Nektulos Forest race route](images/Nektulosforest.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Stro D'Proi]] at the Commonlands gate in [[Nektulos Forest]] to begin the Nektulos Forest race. {{waypoint -210, 12, 254}}
1. Run the Nektulos Forest race within the time limit of 13:30.
   1. [[Behemoth Pond]]: {{waypoint -209, 0, -247}}
   1. [[Gargoyle Gorge]]: {{waypoint -926, 79, -1605}}
      - You can take the griffon at the Commonlands station-230, 1, 150to [[N'Mar's Ascent]], then to [[Bone Lake]], jumping off near the Gorge.
   1. [[Nektropos Castle]]: {{waypoint -1402, 121, -1856}}
   1. [[N'Mar's Ascent]]: {{waypoint -344, 1, -1264}}
      - You can head west from Nektropos Castle to the griffon at [[Bone Lake]]-777, 142, -1878 and take it to [[N'Mar's Ascent]].
   1. [[D'Abth Bridge]]: {{waypoint -258, 0, -665}}
      - From N'Mar's you can take the griffon-438, 61, -1305 back to the Docks station, jumping off when the griffon flies over the bridge.
   1. [[Port Naythex]]: {{waypoint 673, 0, -273}}
   1. Run across the bridge near the Commonlands entrance. -284.16 -.33 198.15
      - If you have an Evac ability, you can use this to get to the Commonlands entrance, run across the bridge, and run back to talk to Stro.
        - UPDATE: Attempted using Ranger evac on 05-12-23 Zarakon server and evac took me to Docks, not the CL entrance.
      - If you don't have Evac, you can take the griffon from the Docks to Commonlands as the quickest route.
1. Speak again to Stro to update the quest and get your second reward.

### The Zek Race

![Zek race route](images/Jboots_Zek.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Soci Esign]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]] In guard tower to the right of the main gate, to begin the Zek race. {{waypoint 483, -40, 223}}
1. Run the Zek race within the time limit of 11:25 minutes (15:30 on Origins). This race can be made easier by using the provided horse stations.
   1. [[Grove of Stones]]: {{waypoint 228, 14, 93}}
      - Take the horse from the Warship Docks to Refuge.  Run north west through the passage to the druid ring.  Optional:  Druid classes can simply open a portal to this location now from the start.
   1. [[The Charred Vale]]: {{waypoint -422, -35, -387}}
      - Take the Refuge horse to the Bridge of Zek, Jump off at the darkened area on the map.
      - You must step in the scorched area to get the update.
   1. [[Deathfist Lookout]]: {{waypoint -24, 75, -364}}
      - Run west from the Charred Vale.  You must climb to the top of the big winding rock.  Make sure you walk over to the skeleton.  If you have feather fall, jump off the top and turn to face south east while moving forward.
   1. [[Bridge of Zek]]: {{waypoint -192, -25, -553}}
   1. [[Deathfist Citadel]]: {{waypoint -43.66, -35.72, -642.51}}
   1. [[Spirit Lake]]:  {{waypoint -59, -4, -72}}
      - Take the bridge of zek horse to Refuge.  Run north through the passage.  Try to avoid touching the water as this will cause additional mobs to spawn and attack you.  Optional:  Again, Druid classes can port to the now active druid ring in this zone and run to the NNE.
      - You can run straight over the hills to the south and then through the shortcut Southwest of Deathfist Lookout. {{waypoint 100, 22, -300}} (**especially useful on Origins**)
   1. [[The Deathfist Quarry]]: {{waypoint 343, -114, -119}}
      - Take the Refuge horse to Three Toes Valley.  Run NE to the elevator shaft.  You must go all the way to the bottom and get off the elevator mechanism and rail tracks.  **Note:**  After you have received the update you may now evac if you can back to the zone in.  If you cannot:  After getting to the top, take the Three Toes Valley horse to the Warship Docks.
   1. Run through the gates near the start {{waypoint 495, -40, 219}} (a few steps from Soci)
1. Speak again to Soci to end the route.

### The Enchanted Lands Race

![Enchanted Lands race route](images/Enchantedlands.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Banoo Corest]] at [[Granary Hill]] in [[Enchanted Lands]] to begin the Enchanted Lands race. {{waypoint 213, 9, -246}}  Utilizing the horses that have been added to this zone can make this race much easier.  Take the Bobick Village Station horse to the Great Guard Station, and jump off just prior to your destination at the villa house at which you will find the NPC to start this leg of the race.
1. Run the Enchanted Lands race within the time limit of 6:53.
   1. [[The Runnyeye Ward]]: {{waypoint 165, 0, -429}}
      - Run North and very slightly east from Granary Hill crossing the midwater bridge, and just north of the bridge is your update.
   1. [[Tagglefoot Farms]]: {{waypoint -724, 0, -683}}
      - You may either head towards the Rivervale entrance to take the horse to Chompers Pond, or you can simply run past chomper's Pond to the east following the lazy drain stream to the update.
   1. [[Camp Ghobber]]: {{waypoint -790, 0, -939}}
      - Quickly run north of Tagglefoot farms to the tower where the shadowed men are.  Run near one of the defensive spike posts for update.
   1. [[Goblin Pass]]: {{waypoint 220, 0, -750}}
      - Run SW from Camp Ghobber and take this horse to Bramblefoot Hills.  Take the Bramblefoot Hills horse to the Goblin Pass.  Jump off just a few meters prior and run towards the tower to get the update.
   1. [[Lookout Stump]]: {{waypoint 331, 0, -420}}
      - Take the Goblin Pass horse to the Great Guard Station.  Quickly run north of that horse to Lookout Stump.
   1. Return to the Granary.  {{waypoint 213, 9, -246}}
      - Run south back to the horse station, through the opening in the wooden wall, and head NE.
1. Speak again to Banoo to update the quest and get your third reward.
   - If done correctly, utilizing the horses will save you an extra 2 minutes approximately.  You will also receive the neck item [[Amulet of the Far-runner]] for completing this leg.

### The Feerrott Race

![Feerrott race route](images/Feerrott.jpg)

1. Speak to [[Chon Ar'edij]] in [[The Feerrott]] to start the Feerrott race. {{waypoint -118, 7, -74}}
   - *Note: You must be level 30 or higher to start this race.*
   - Utilizing the horses that have been added to this zone can make this final leg much easier.
1. Run the Feerrott race within the time limit of 7:10.
   1. [[Temple of Alliz]]: {{waypoint -301, -7, 819}}
      - Run out to the horse at the entrance, and take it to the Greenblood River Station. Run across the bridge for update.
   1. [[The Ruins of Thule]]: {{waypoint -729, -3, 823}}
      - Either continue running east past the druid rings(currently under construction as of this post) or Run back to the Greenblood River Station horse and take it to Tae Ew station and head east. (Once the druid rings are complete druid classes may port to this ring, but any way you do it takes about the same amount of time.)
   1. [[Alliz Tae Outpost]]: {{waypoint -1221, 3, 688}}
      - Take the Tae Ew Station horse to the Alliz Tae Station.  Jump off the horse when you pass through the stone wall to receive the update.
   1. [[Ulguth Bridge]]: {{waypoint -1198, -4, 430}}
      - Run North West cutting across the lake, and cross the bridge just before the Tower of Vul station.
   1. [[Tower of Vul]]: {{waypoint -1018, 0, 488}}
      - Head South West from the Tower of Vul station toward the tower until update.
   1. [[Altar of the Four Canons]]: {{waypoint -719, 6, 195}}
      - Continue to head West until you can turn North.  Head in this direction until you come across an altar surrounded by manticores for the update.
   1. [[Murdunk's Fall]]: {{waypoint -265, 5, 140}}
      - Head West by North West to the pool of water, and at the North end is Murdunk Falls, the source of the rivers in the Feerott, and your update.
   1. Run to the area near Chon, approaching from the south. {{waypoint -114, 7, -71}}
      - Head North West to the path to approach the wizard spire, but take the North junction on the path to get to Chon.
1. Speak to [[Chon Ar'edij|Chon]] for your final reward.

## Reward

- 62,000 status
- [[Sian's Bracelet of Adroitness]] - after Commonlands race
- [[Runner's Ring]] - after Nektulos race
- [[Amulet of the Far-Runner]] - after Enchanted Lands race
- One of the following, auto-selected based on your class
  - [[Legendary Journeyman's Boots (cloth)]]
  - [[Legendary Journeyman's Boots (leather)]]
  - [[Legendary Journeyman's Boots (chain)]]
  - [[Legendary Journeyman's Boots (plate)]]

## Notes

- If you want a different version of the reward than the one given to you, you can purchase it from the [[Shady Swashbuckler XVIII]] on the Nektulos Forest docks.*
- It is recommended that you have one or two higher level characters along, either within or without the group, to run mob interference and train mobs for lower level characters and to act as guides.
- If you don't have a way to evacuate yourself to a safe place, the Nektulos Forest and the Thundering Steppes Griffins towers can be used, provided you have done the associated quests.
- It is also recommended that you have at least a 50% run speed or higher buff/mount.
