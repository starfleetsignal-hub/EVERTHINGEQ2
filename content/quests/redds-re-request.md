## Notes

- Quest [[The Reticent Tinkerer]] must be completed first to get this mission.
- This is a daily mission continuing helping Redd with clockworks attacking his abode.
  - This mission is on a set timer of 18 hours. Find when the next reset is by going to your quest journal, then mission timers tab and Live Events there. Search for Redd.
- While this is not a timed event, once you pull lever the script will go on even if you  don't do anything. And if you don't kill the waves of mobs they will come to you and attack you (Epic ^^^). This is not the time to go AFK.

## Steps

1. Help Redd upgrade and empower his cannon. Gather the following items found around his adobe:
   - 6 x heavy rhodium bars
   - 3 x mana batteries
   - 6 x non-incendiflaming wires - click on dismantled clockworks laying around
1. Click on Heavy Gnomish Cannon at {{waypoint -55, 58, -18}} to upgrade it.
1. Click on Heavy Gnomish Cannon again to empower it.
1. Speak with Redd
   - He can give you instructions on operating gate and cannon or you can click on second option in his dialogue if you already know what to do.
1. Destroy 10 waves of invading clockworks with the upgraded cannon.
   - Click on lever at {{waypoint -46, 59, -35}} to open the gates.
   - move to the cannon and click the pile of cannonballs, then click on cannon to load it.
   - wait for clockworks to walk in (they will be in groups of 1-3 but 1 cannonball takes care of the group as 1 wave), when you see in chat "target acquired" click cannon to fire.
   - repeat clicking on cannonballs, cannon, then cannon again until you complete 10 waves.
1. Click lever to close gate.
1. Destroy the Clockwork Menasour who managed to get in before the gate closed.
   - Load up the cannon and hit him once (no need to wait for "target aquired" prompt), that will take out half his health. After that he swipes at and destroys your cannon, so you need to go down to the lower level and finish the job in regular combat. He will heal up to full if you delay. He goes invulnerable occasionally, while he casts his frontal fire AE Menacing Flames, wait this out and kill him.
1. Return to Redd by his desk on upper level.
