## How to start quest

- **This is the crafting way of starting prelude event.** Right at the first conversation you have to choose if you want to go the crafting way, or the adventure way.
- Speak with [[Salira Freecreek]] in The Commonlands at Crossroads {{waypoint -526, -44, -314}}
  - When given a choice of answers choose the option: *"I'll stay here and prepare whatever you need.*"

## Notes

- **Even though it's possible to do this quest at lower level, it's much easier to do it closer to level 10 crafting or higher.**.
- Bring with you any common materials (at least 27 roots, 12 pelts, 12 ore, 9 gemstones, 6 wood) of any tier, plus 9 glowing candles, 9 glowing filaments and 9 glowing sandpapers as fuels.
- Bringing a portable sewing table, woodworking table and chemistry table is also recommended. There are no crafting stations at Crossroads.
- Recipes are auto scribed for you upon accepting quest. They make 1 - 2 items. ![](images/Savanna_Resin.png)
- The recipes use [[Savanna resin]] which you should pick up around Crossroads, they are small, non trackable, harvestable red bottles on the ground, on tables, barrels and in general area of Crossroads. Each bottle will give 3 resins, you will need at least 10 for this and next quest. There will be more resins needed later for repeatable quests, but they can also be picked up in quest hub zone [[Block and Tackle Storerooms]] after you get access to it.

## Steps

1. Craft:
   - 6 x D.I.R.T.Y. Artifact Cleansing Solutions (3 common roots, 3 gemstones and 2 leather pelts needed + 9 glowing candles)
   - 6 x D.I.R.T.Y. Magical Containment Crates (3 common roots, 2 metal and 2 wood needed + 9 glowing sandpapers)
   - 6 x D.I.R.T.Y. Arcane Stabilizing Straps (3 common roots,2 metal and 2 leather pelts needed + 9 glowing filaments)
1. Return to [[Salira Freecreek]] in Crossroads in [[The Commonlands]].

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- [[Sewage Slop]] (House pet)
- At least 11s 8c
