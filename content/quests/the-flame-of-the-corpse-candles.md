## Steps

1. Obtain a [[Droag Carcass]] from any living droag. After killing one, you will automatically be given a carcass.
1. Throw the Droag Carcass near [[A corpse candle]] (this can be done with stealth/invis).
   - If you place the droag carcass too far away from the corpse candle and the candle does not respond, simply attack the corpse candle and it will then move towards where you are standing and transform itself.
1. Wait a moment for the corpse candle to turn into a 'sated corpse candle', then use [[Catalogue Creature]] on it.
1. Return to [[Aetryol Monsoon]].

## Rewards

- At least 10g 4s 88c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Band of the Realm]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Crest of the Realm]]
  - [[Emblem of the Realm]]
  - [[Signet of the Realm]] (All Fighters)
