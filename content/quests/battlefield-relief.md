## Steps

1. Save 5 Highhold defenders from undead attackers
   - The defenders are scattered along the paths leading to Highpass.  They're being attacked by mixed groups of [[A risen Shralok|risen Shraloks]] and [[A risen Mucktail|risen Mucktails]], one 102^Heroic two 102Heroic. Kill the entire encounter to save the defender.
1. Return to Yonette

## Rewards

- At least 1p 45g 80s
- [[Saddle Rack]]
