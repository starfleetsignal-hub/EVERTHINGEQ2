## Steps

1. Speak with [[Eitharbor (NPC)|Eitharbor]] in [[The Nursery]] of [[Greater Faydark]], at {{waypoint -357, -28, -560}}.
   - He casts a spell on you, called 'Safe Passage', that he tells you will grant total invisibility so long as you do not cast or attack. It will also suspends movement buffs. However, it is NOT actually offer total invisibility. Some creatures see through it (certain aviaks and kobolds outside Kaladim for example) and it will break if you are attacked or if you fall and get hurt. You can still complete this part of the quest even if you no longer have the spell active. The spell will take effect the moment you zone into [[Butcherblock Mountains]]. Leave and re-enter zone to re-cast spell if lost.
1. Pick up 'Aviak Eggs' in [[Kragploom Roost]] within [[Butcherblock Mountains]]. {{waypoint 508, 399, -554}}
   - WARNING!!! When you take the egg you become visible and be attacked by a 30 or 31^ aviak.
1. Return to Eitharbor in Greater Faydark. {{waypoint -357, -28, -560}}
1. Kill [[Eitharbor (Named)|Eitharbor]] to complete the quest.

## Notes

- The [[A Kragploom scavenger|Kragploom scavengers]] can see invis.
- [[Eitharbor]] must be hailed and fought separately for each person doing the quest.
- Eitharbor has a 3 minute respawn.
- If the quest gets bugged and Eitharbor won't attack you, **DO NOT** delete the quest. There is no way to get it back without GM help.
