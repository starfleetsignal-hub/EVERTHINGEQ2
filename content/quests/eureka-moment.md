## Steps

1. Meet [[Uelmondi]] at {{waypoint 330, 52, 201}}.
   - Quest states use **at** the spot designated and perform ritual. One has to **TARGET** the circle, then **USE** the potion.
1. Use [[Uelmondi's Phial]] on the small arcane upwelling near Uelmondi {{waypoint 338, 54, 204}}.![a small arcane upwelling](images/A_small_arcane_upwelling.jpg)
1. Speak to [[Uelmondi]].
1. Meet [[Uelmondi]] at {{waypoint 536, 61, 342}}.
1. Use [[Uelmondi's Empowered Phial]] on the small arcane upwelling at {{waypoint 543, 55, 356}}.
1. Kill the [[Frenzied cnidcara]] that attacks then Speak to [[Uelmondi]] again.
1. Return to [[Uelmondi]] at {{waypoint 418, 36, 39}}.
