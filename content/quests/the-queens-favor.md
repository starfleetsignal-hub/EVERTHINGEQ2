## Steps

1. Travel to [[Khali'Yrzu]] and speak with [[Malusia]] {{waypoint 95.47, 579.56, -665.49}} again in her home town.
1. Speak to [[Thegnahex Lasmayta]] {{waypoint 257.53, 591.17, -633.07}} near the [[Majestrix Sangeeta|Majestrix]].
1. Talk to the following:
   - Speak to [[Samtayna]] {{waypoint 240.13, 588.21, -653.77}}
   - Speak to [[Aylekee]] {{waypoint 245.83, 591.56, -702.89}}
   - Speak to [[Yshaidin]] {{waypoint 192.65, 588.61, -621.47}}
1. Return to [[Thegnahex Lasmayta]] at {{waypoint 257.53, 591.17, -633.07}}.
1. Collect the following:
   - ***A chunk of yrzucite*** {{waypoint -105.54, 9.86, 66.49}} from the banks of the [[Myhst River]]. ![](images/Yrzucite.jpg)
     - *Note: a really tiny dark blue stone with a purple glow.*
   - 4 ***pieces of crystallized aether essence*** {{waypoint -189.21, 83.28, -47.26}} or {{waypoint -155, 76, -37}}in the northern part of [[Kandra Uplands]]. ![](images/Crystallised_Essence.jpg)
     - *Note: Essences appear as small floating light blue crystals atop the blue veins in this area*.
1. Return again to [[Thegnahex Lasmayta]] {{waypoint 257.53, 591.17, -633.07}} in [[Khali'Yrzu]].
1. Use the knowledge you gained in your audience with [[Majestrix Sangeeta]] {{waypoint 290.36, 597.36, -628.36}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -5000 faction with **House Vahla**
- +5000 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -5000 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Stoneweave Headgear]] (Cloth)
  - [[Shaped Stonelink Coif]] (Chain)
  - [[Infused Softstone Cap]] (Leather)
  - [[Resolute Runeplate Greathelm]] (Plate)
