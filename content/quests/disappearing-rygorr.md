## Steps

1. Find Rosk Gragdush's camp at {{waypoint -919, -370, 2926}}
1. Inspect the camp, either you use the fur, the rock or the wood to update it
1. Return to [[Blor Greatbasher]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g
- +4,550 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
- One of the Following (Class-based):
  - [[Arctic Grisly Bone Chip Band]] (mage & priest)
  - [[Arctic Ry'Gorr Band of Bone]] (mage & priest)
  - [[Arctic Foul Bone Carved Ring]] (fighter & scout)
  - [[Arctic Orc's Bone Gnawed Ring]] (fighter & scout)
