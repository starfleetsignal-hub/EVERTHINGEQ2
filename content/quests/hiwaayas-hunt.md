## Steps

*Hiwaaya Al'Naderi admitted to being at odds with most of the Cult of Vul, as she remains devoted to the renowned archmage, Bellengere the Three, and his promotions of a controlled and studious approach towards the inscrutable energies of the Void. The majority of cultists have been swept up in the excitement of the activated anchor and are being led by others with questionable intentions. Hiwaaya refuses to let this impede the current opportunity in the Spiral of Vul. She has asked that I procure arcana-biologic samples from Vulteran darkclaws and winged umbravores for comparisons and study.*

- Hunt Vulteran darkclaw for arcana-biologic samples (0/12).
- Hunt winged umbravore for arcana-biologic samples (0/6).
- Return to [[Hiwaaya Al'Naderi (Spiral of Vul)|Hiwaaya Al'Naderi]]
