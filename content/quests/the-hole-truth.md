1. Port down to The Hole and examine any of the three doors. To get to "The Hole", right click on the teleporter at {{waypoint 1923, 301, 3318}}.
1. Return to Saihah Al'Lad

- At least 70g
- +900 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +100 faction with **Seekers of the Dark Truth**
