*Note: This quest replaced [[The Stout Merchant Manifest]] when Qeynos was revamped in [[LU64]]

## Notes

If you want to get the parallel quest, [[Evidence for Constable Arathur]], at the same time as this quest, follow the following steps.

1. When zoning into Crow's Resting Place, go straight to the back room and talk to Fippy Darkpaw to receive this quest.
1. After doing so, return to the first room and kill [[An Unseen Hand taskmaster]].
1. After killing it, [[Rollus the Sly (NPC)]] will turn into an attackable mob, [[Rollus the Sly (12)]]. Kill him and loot [[Motley Merchant Manifest (Quest Starter)]] from his body.
1. Examine it to start the quest immediately.

## Steps

1. Listen to Fippy Darkpaw's instructions.
1. Read [[Motley Merchant Manifest (Quest Item)|Motley Merchant Manifest]] you receive from Fippy Darkpaw.
1. Talk to [[Gornit Penwiggle]] "G.P." in "The Qeynos Lingustics" in [[Qeynos Province District]] (In Qeynos Harbor) {{waypoint 831, -23, 125}}.
1. Talk to [[Proprietor Blagard]] "P.B." on the top floor of "Collector's Cove" in [[Qeynos Province District]] (In Qeynos Harbor) {{waypoint 827, -13, 33}}.
1. Talk to [[Karg Icemoore]] "K.I." on the second floor of "Fish's Ale House and Inn" in [[Qeynos Province District]] (In Qeynos Harbor) {{waypoint 818, -17, 54}}.
1. Talk to [[Borden Mannus]] "B.M." at "The Mermaid's Lure Tavern" in [[Qeynos Province District]] (In Qeynos Harbor) {{waypoint 935, -19, 9}}.
1. Talk to [[P.T. Irontoe (Qeynos Capitol District)|P.T. Irontoe]] "P.I." at "Irontoe's East Tavern" in [[Qeynos Capitol District]] (In North Qeynos) {{waypoint 338, -20, 155}}.
1. Return to [[Fippy Darkpaw, the 4th]].
