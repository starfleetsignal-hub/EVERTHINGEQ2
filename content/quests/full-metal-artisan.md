## Description

The Craftkeepers Task Coordinator reports the Fell Blade faction has requisitioned more essential supplies.

## Steps

1. Craft 5 traditional Paineel greaves. This is an Artisan recipe received from an earlier quest. Requires a total of 15 [[Titanium ore]], 10 [[Quicksilver cluster]] and 5 [[Ethereal coal]]. (note: traditional Paineel greatsword or traditional Paineel breastplate can also be requested.)
1. Return to [[Oolioomon]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2000 faction with **City of Paineel**
- +2500 faction with **Craftkeepers**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Craftkeepers Daily Supply Tasks
