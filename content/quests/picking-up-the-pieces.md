## Steps

1. Look in and around [[The Quarry]] {{waypoint -434, 145, -53}} in Butcherblock for the missing caravan crates.
1. Pick up 6 crates.  ![](images/A_supply_crate.jpg)
1. Return to [[Grimshaw Packwright]]

## Rewards

- At least 94s 55c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Earhoop of Tribal Ceremonies]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Earring of Tribal Ceremonies]] (All Priests)
  - [[Facet of Tribal Ceremonies]] (All Mages)
  - [[Spike of Tribal Bones]] (All Fighters)
