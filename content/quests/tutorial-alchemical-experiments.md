## Description

"The tradeskill tutor has asked me to try my hand at alchemy." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Craft an essence of [[Intercept|Intercept (Journeyman)]] using your [[Alchemist]] skills
   - buy a [[Basic Candle]] to fuel the [[Chemistry Table]]
   - you will need 2 [[Leaded loam]], 1 [[Rough malachite (Crate Reward)|rough malachite]], and 1 [[Root (harvest)|root]].
1. Return to the instructor

## Rewards

- At least 16c
- [[Artisan essentials volume 7 (Uncommon)|artisan essentials volume 7]]

Alchemists also make combat art upgrades for fighter types to improve their abilities in battle. Make me an Essence of Intercept to practice these skills."
