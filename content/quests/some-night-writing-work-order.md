## Steps

Create (2) each of any 6 random assigned spells below:

- [[Bane of Warding II|Bane of Warding II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Primal Fury II|Primal Fury II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Skeletal Grasp|Skeletal Grasp (Journeyman)]]
- [[Photosynthesis IV|Photosynthesis IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Regalia|Regalia (Journeyman)]]
- [[Geotic Rune III|Geotic Rune III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Vital Intercession IV|Vital Intercession IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Tyrant's Pact III|Tyrant's Pact III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Heretic's Doom II|Heretic's Doom II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Echoes of the Ancients II|Echoes of the Ancients II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Ancient Shroud IV|Ancient Shroud IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Virtue II|Virtue II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Instinct II|Instinct II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Rending|Rending (Journeyman)]]
- [[Signet of Intellect III|Signet of Intellect III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Teachings of the Underworld III|Teachings of the Underworld III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Vehement Skin|Vehement Skin (Journeyman)]]
- [[Regrowth IV|Regrowth IV (Journeyman)]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward.

## Rewards

- At Least 24s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
