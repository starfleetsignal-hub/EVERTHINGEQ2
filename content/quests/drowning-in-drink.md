## Notes

- Pay attention to what Nibbles has to say.
- Avoid all of the Highkeep guards. Sometimes this means waiting for one or more to path away from you.
- If you are spotted by a guard, you'll be detained and taken to [[The Storeroom Office]] {{waypoint 148, -124, 122}}, and have to work your way back after killing the guard.
- Gnomish Stilts and a Feather Fall Cloak may be useful.

## Steps

After obtaining the **Sugarcane Press Lift Key** from Kildiun:

1. Return to the **Lift to Highhold** {{waypoint -115, -160, -61}} and go all the way to the bottom.
1. Go to the **Lift to Sugarcane Press** {{waypoint -12, -159, -29}} and take the lift to the top.
1. Nibbles tells you to "kill Bull from above".
   1. Move around the back of the sugarcane press, avoiding [[Yipnik]].
   1. Wait until [[Bull McCleran]] is right below you, walking beneath the exhaust pipes, then pull the lever {{waypoint -2, -101, 11}}.
1. After the steam causes Bull to jump off the edge, take the **Lift to Sugarcane Press** down one level.
1. Click the **Misplaced Crate of Rum** {{waypoint 50, -124, 85}}.![Yeah, he's gonna be out for awhile... better go ahead and take this now.](images/Kildun's_Bag_Reward.png)
1. Return to Kildiun to complete the quest. Finish the dialog and then click **Kildiun's Bag** on the floor for your reward.
