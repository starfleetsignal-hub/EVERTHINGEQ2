   - Seek out Inkling of Strength in [[Whisperling Forest]] {{waypoint -536, 113, 73}}
   - Seek out Inkling of Vision {{waypoint -36.43, 142.82, 143.93}}
   - Seek out Inkling of Family near [[Firefall Fringe]] {{waypoint 183, 93, 127}}

1. Seek out the final spiritual tenet, the Inkling of Tenacity, on the edge of the chasm {{waypoint -78, 65, -526}}
