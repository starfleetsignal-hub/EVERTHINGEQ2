## Steps

1. Return to [[Rendyl]] at the [[Nettleville: Strange Tidings|Nettleville]] gate with the forged authorization form, and zone in once he has read your paperwork. {{waypoint 681, -21, 271}}
1. Look for evidence of what the Circle of Ten is concealing.
   - Enter the old apothecary shop to collect a [[Torn diary page]] and examine it. {{waypoint 638, -20, 284}} If you can invis or stealth, you may not need to fight any of the [[A crazed Freeblood|crazed Freebloods]].
   - Enter the old inn, which has been converted to a jail for imprisoning Freebloods.  Collect another [[Torn diary page]] and examine it. {{waypoint 651, -24, 356}}
   - Speak to [[Investigator Rashlas]]. {{waypoint 657, -25, 352}}
   - The Investigator has called the guards to kill you! Escape back to [[South Qeynos]]!
1. Return to [[Patricia Benson]].
