## Steps

1. Speak with [[Hakim al-Imam]] in the Harbor area {{waypoint 188.55, -59.82, 573.75}}
1. Obtain **Akar, the Sigil of Wilting** in [[Mordant Spires]]. ![Akar, Sigil of Wilting, Mordant Spires](images/Akar,_Sigil_of_Wilting,_Mordant_Spires.png)Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint 572.59, 29.71, -260.86}}
   - {{waypoint 485.57, 29.86, -139.78}}
   - {{waypoint 476.65, 27.82, -307.89}}
   - {{waypoint 552.26, 29.46, -229.54}}
   - {{waypoint 551.49, 29.62, -299.63}}
   - {{waypoint 526.38, 29.28, -211.71}}
   - {{waypoint 510.39, 31.03, -158.29}}
   - {{waypoint 541.56, 28.96, -341.57}}
1. Obtain **Iruka, the Sigil of Nurturing** ![Iruka, Sigil of Nurturing, Najami Oasis](images/Iruka,_Sigil_of_Nurturing,_Najami_Oasis.png) . Mounted on the verticle stone.
   1. Obtain **Iruka, the Sigil of Nurturing** in [[Najami Oasis]] at {{waypoint -117.66, 28.85, -34.16}}
   1. Defeat the 3 [[A desert madman|desert madmen]] attackers that spawm.
1. Enter [[Raj'Dur Plateaus: The Sultan's Dagger (Signature)|Raj'Dur Plateaus: The Sultan's Dagger [Signature]]] {{waypoint 60.28, 87.70, -349.85}}
   - *Can be run concurrently with [[Takish Tablets for the Taking]]*
1. Confront [[Aldys, Sultan of the Daggers]]
   - See: [[Raj'Dur Plateaus: The Sultan's Dagger (Signature)]]
1. Pick up the **Sigil of Thii** {{waypoint -297, 117, -120}}
1. return to [[Hakim al-Imam]] in the Harbor area {{waypoint 188.55, -59.82, 573.75}}
