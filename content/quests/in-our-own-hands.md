## Steps

1. Speak to [[Hisska Rakepaw]] in North Freeport outside the gate to Stonestair Byway {{waypoint -13, -20, -302}}
1. Enter [[Stonestair Byway: The Animus Corridors]] and do the following
   - Defeat 5 [[An erudite portalcaster|portalcasters]]
     - Portalcasters spawn and attack automatically.  Just run in circles near the zone-in to find them.
   - Find evidence regarding the portalcasters' plans
     - The plans are in a building on a table at {{waypoint 0, -2, -66}}.  They look like a loose page with a silver sparkly effect.
1. Return to Hisska in Freeport
1. Enter the Erudite Planner's hideout - [[Melzeim's Hideout]] is accessed via a plain wooden door at {{waypoint -72, -34, -359}}, just outside the gate to Longshadow Alley
1. Defeat the organizers - kill [[Melzeim a'Galwe]] and two [[An erudite mastermind|erudite mastermind]]s - the quest updates once all 3 are dead.
1. Return to Hisska

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g @ lvl 95
