## Steps

1. Collect 6 *spider egg samples* (ground spawn) from near the [[Funnelweb Depths]] around {{waypoint 672.20, -50.84, -123.92}}.
1. Kill 6 Funnelweb Spiders around {{waypoint 672.20, -50.84, -123.92}} to collect their poison glands.
1. Return to Ghaunatar.

- At least 2s 91c
