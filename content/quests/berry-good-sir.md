## Notes

if one is considerably below level 80, and has done no adventuring or faction quests in Moors, the Froglok Guards will be aggressive, and the Merchants will "not want your business."

To *easily* resolve, and gain **positive** faction with ALL three camps within the Moors, spend time on the island Brokenskull Rock cleaning out the already aggressive pirates; After a time, one will max out faction to Ally with the three "important" factions in the Moors. (island is also a good place to practice the [[Safe Fall]] skill)

## Steps

1. ![Tupta berries](images/Tupta_berries.jpg)Collect 6 [[Dried Tupta berries]] from each of the 6 buckets in [[Tupta]].  Highlights when you move your mouse pointer over.
   - 2 at {{waypoint -35, -55, 788}}
   - 1 at {{waypoint -97, -42, 729}}
   - 2 at {{waypoint -132, -44, 676}}
   - 1 at {{waypoint -123, -45, 683}}
   - 1 at {{waypoint -110, -44, 669}}
1. Return to [[Varick Stoutheart|Varick]]

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience
- At least 64g 81s 38c
- +750 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- Opens up the quest [[Termite Troubles, Part I]] when [[Ship Out]] is turned in
