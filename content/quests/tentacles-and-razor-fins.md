Zintra De'Oziana, an eccentric Teir'Dal sent as Cristanos' proxy from Neriak into the underdepths, has shown a great appreciation for the beasts of Thalumbra, particularly the cepholex.

## Steps

   - Hunt [[A cepholex|cepholex]]. There are a few around the [[Abandoned Fungus Farm]] as well as along the coast. Need to Kill 16

## Rewards

- At least 62g 37s
- One of the following:
  - [[Astral Umbrite Buckle Sash]]
  - [[Bluster Umbrite Buckle Sash]]
  - [[Cerulean Umbrite Studded Belt]]
  - [[Pyre Umbrite Studded Belt]]
  - [[Verdant Umbrite Buckle Sash]]
