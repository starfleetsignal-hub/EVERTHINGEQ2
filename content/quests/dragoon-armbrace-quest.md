## Steps

1. Kill undead for a rare update.
1. Harvest 20 pieces of wood from [[Wood Nodes#T3|putrescent duskwood arbors]], at 1 update per successful harvest.
1. Kill [[An ashland wisp|ashland wisp]]s for an update ([[An ash wraith|ash wraith]]s do not count).
