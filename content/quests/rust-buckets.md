## Steps

1. Use the received [[Thalz'Iz'Zaz All-Purpose Cleaner]] and clean 5 [[Assessment bot|assessment bots]] to receive [[Strange powder]].
   - *If nothing happens the [[Assessment bot]] has probably been recently cleaned by someone else.*
1. Return to [[Ranger Zim'za'ai]]
1. Then take the [[Strange powder|powder]] to Blacksmith [[Zi'za'zir]] {{waypoint 1324, 431, 922}}.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
