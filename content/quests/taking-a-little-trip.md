## Steps

Speak with Nyran Dudez at {{waypoint 110, 2, 33}}.
"Look, your new uniform is on order, and I have been recruiting like mad! Nazar is helping as much as he possibly can as well!"
What do you want me to do?
"Well untill you decide there is someone we can trust, you're going to need to collect the reports from the other new cell leaders!"
[[Nyran Dudez|Nyran]] asks you to collect the cell reports again. (These are in the same locations that you visited while performing [[Menial Tasks...]])

- [[Neriak]]: {{waypoint -109, 21.6, -11.75}} in [[Indigo Hollow]] behind [[Ulsurus Outfitters]].
  - If the Neriak guards are hostile, use invis. The guards do not see invis, not even the epics.
  - ***Invisible works fine, as long as you take the right path.**   The first two guards at the Darklight Woods gate cannot see invis.  There are two guards in cubbies on either side of the interior of the gate that do see invis and a roamer further in Indigo Hollow.  To avoid all: go straight in and stop right in front of the fountain that you can see from the doorway.  Wait until the roamer (a level 108) roams away from the left path and off you go. Otherwise, you can group with an evil friend, have them go to the Ulsurus Outfitters and then call you to them, or if you have the ability, you can port yourself to them.*
- [[Gorowyn]]: {{waypoint 2594, 87, 1333}} within the [[Timorous Deep]]. Travel up the ramps to [[The Dragon's Anchor]], where the chest is behind some crates in back of the bar.
- [[North Qeynos]]: {{waypoint 480, -21, -378}} behind [[The Ironforge Estate]] in the [[Qeynos Capitol District]].
  - If the Qeynos guards are hostile, you will need to go to [[Antonica]] and use the Antonica bell to enter [[The Caves]]. Behind a waterfall there is a door to the [[The Down Below|Down Below]]. {{waypoint -28, 1, -76}} From there, take the **Qeynos Capitol District - North Qeynos** sewer grate. {{waypoint -101, 1, -97}}
- [[South Freeport]]: {{waypoint -186, -30, 279}} Behind the 10 Freedom Road house.
  - If the Freeport guards are hostile, you will need to go to the [[Commonlands]] and take the sewer hatch {{waypoint -1252, -84, 120}} to enter the [[The Thieves' Way|Thieves' Way]], then go through the **City of Freeport - South** sewer grate. {{waypoint -111, 1, 104}}

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Circle of the Unseen Hand**
- After completing this quest 10 times, the player is able to purchase a set of style armor in the Halls of the Unseen instance from "a dark talon merchant" (in the deepest room of the instance). You have to complete it 10 times, because you will be at 40000 faction after completing all previous steps and there is no other way to increase this faction. To be able to buy the other set pieces you need a total faction of 50000. Scouts also need to do this 10 times to be at 50k faction as a pre-requisite for their Epic 2.0 questline.
