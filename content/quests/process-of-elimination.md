## Steps

![Top to bottom: the plant to sample, a slug, and the fungus](images/Proccess-elmitaion-items.jpg)

1. Collect the following from [[Twark]] {{waypoint 271, -180, -658}}
   - a plant sample - any bush behind [[Growf]] {{waypoint 81, -171, -681}}
   - a sample from animals - right click on any Tame Forest Slug to collect the sample
   - a sample of fungi - the shelf fungus found on the tree trunks in the area
1. Speak to [[Elder Lyraquel]]

## Rewards

- +2000 faction with **Nye'Caelona**
- At least 1p 53g 9s
