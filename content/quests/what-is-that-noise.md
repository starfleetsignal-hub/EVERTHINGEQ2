## Notes

Those who have difficulty with drunk effects (blurred screen makes some get a headache) may want to bring [[Pot of Wake-up Juice]] or [[Hair of the Dog]] along. The quest from Sam, which is easier to do immediately following this quest, is one of the worst for the drunk effect blur.<br>
You can also just lose a duel against another player --  equipment takes no damage -- and you are sober right away.

## Steps

1. Go behind the curtain at Loc {{waypoint 329, -21, 145}} and take the stairs down. Then click on the trap door at Loc {{waypoint 339, -27, 144}} to zone into the [[Serilian Bar]].
1. Speak to [[Sam]] the ratonga bartender for the quest update.
   - Sam will offer you the next quest at this point also.
1. Return to [[Mraugl Stonecrusher]].

## Rewards

5 x [[Beer Token]]
