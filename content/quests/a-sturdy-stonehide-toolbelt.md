## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Stonehide Toolbelt for my apprentice.
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Stonehide Toolbelt]]
   1. Craft [[A Sturdy Stonehide Toolbelt]] on a [[Loom]] using the following materials and fuel:
      - 2 [[Stonehide leather pelt]]
      - 4 [[Lambent filament]]
1. I need to take this toolbelt back to my apprentice.
