*This quest is repeatable..*

## Steps

1. Find and learn more about the Memorial of Darkness. Read the inscription on the NE monolith {{waypoint -9, 0, 3}} in [[The First Gate]].
1. Kill zombies in Fallen Gate to collect 8 eyeballs. (Uncommon update)
1. Drop the offering into the urn at the foot of the Memorial of Darkness.

## Reward

- [[Spectral Dragoon Band]]
