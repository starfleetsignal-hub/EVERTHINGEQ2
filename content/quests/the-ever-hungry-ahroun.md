## Description

The Lyrech are fiercely opposed to Mistmoore and do everything in their power to stay independent. Oranthus, leader of this clan, asked for my help in showing Mistmoore that they aren't intimidated by his threats or his groveling lap dogs, the Ahroun. As a display of this power, I offered to bring back the head of the pack leader of the rival clan loyal to Lord Mistmoore.

## Steps

1. Kill the following mobs, all found at the [[Copse of the Ahroun]] {{waypoint 187, 87, 81}}:
   - 6 [[An Ahroun blackclaw|Ahroun blackclaws]]
   - 6 [[An Ahroun blackstalker|Ahroun blackstalkers]]
   - [[Vysra the Ever-Hungry]]. [[A blackrunner warrior]] is the placeholder and does not update the quest.
   - 15-25 min respawn on named, ~10 min respawn on the regular Ahroun Werewolves.
1. Return to [[Ormanthus]] during the day. If you attempt to speak with him at night he will be in his [[Ormanthus the Beast|werewolf form]] and attack.

## Reward

- +1000 faction with **The Lyrech**
- One of the following:
  - [[Tribal Twine Earring]] (caster item)
