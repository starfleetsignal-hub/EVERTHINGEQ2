**If your character is evil aligned, see the [[Blat Berisen]] page for location to start and complete this quest.**

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Frethe Zizlop (Kelethin)|Frethe Zizlop]] in [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 267, 125, 272}}(inside Gearheart's Forge)
   - This will start the subquest [[Card Deals (Kelethin)|Card Deals]]
1. Speak with [[Zoe Hertzflip (Kelethin)|Zoe Hertzflip]] at {{waypoint 198, 107, 343}}(inside Jysolin's Pub)
   - This will start the subquest [[Ogled Goggles (Kelethin)|Ogled Goggles]]
1. Speak with [[Barb Islenet (Kelethin)|Barb Islenet]] at {{waypoint 136, 108, 177}}(Inside Kelethin Research Library)
   - This will start the subquest [[Wine for the Lady (Kelethin)|Wine for the Lady]]
1. Speak with [[Abner Siblet (Kelethin)|Abner Siblet]] at {{waypoint 539, 77, 320}}(Inside Joleena's Restaurant on the top floor)
   - This will start the subquest [[Fribles's Fate (Kelethin)|Fribles's Fate]]
1. Go to {{waypoint 710, 15, 407}} in [[Greater Faydark]], [[Belazzor Fribles, the Inept (Greater Faydark)|Belazzor Fribles, the Inept]] will spawn and attack you; his level scales to yours. This loc is South West of Joleena's Acorn Lift and close to the entrance to Lesser Faydark.
   - Kill [[Belazzor Fribles, the Inept (Greater Faydark)|Fribles]].
1. Go back to [[Abner Siblet (Kelethin)|Abner Siblet]] in [[Kelethin]].
1. Return to the remaining NPCs in reverse order to complete each of the subquests. You will get Exp/AA exp for all 4 of them.
1. Return finally to [[Blat Berisen (Greater Faydark)|Blat Berisen]] in Greater Faydark for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Zoe's Wild Deck of Playing Cards]]
- [[Tin Metal Helm of Protection]]
