## Notes

- This quest is for evil aligned characters and will only be available for a short time till Wednesday, September 16, 2015.
- *good aligned characters see [[Malice in the Glade]]*

## Lore

The Disciples of Innoruuk have detected new activity at the [[Tower of Bone]] in [[Darklight Wood]], and the Seraph wants and investigation.

## Steps

1. Investigate the new activity at the [[Tower of Bone]] in [[Darklight Wood]].
   - Defeat three Primordial Malice zealots {{waypoint 452.06, -28.37, 221.71}}. These include a [[A Primordial Malice cursespinner|Primordial Malice cursespinner]], a [[A Primordial Malice cultist|Primordial Malice cultist]], and a [[A Primordial Malice shadowknight|Primordial Malice shadowknight]].
1. Speak to [[Virun D'Xyafin]] in [[Neriak]] within the [[Spires of Innoruuk]] {{waypoint -284.53, 29.20, 184.99}}.
1. Investigate the activities of the cultists across the Shattered Lands.
   1. Defeat three members of the Primordial Malice behind the cemetery east of the [[The Ruins of Val`marr|Ruins of Val`Marr]] in the [[Commonlands]] {{waypoint 203.93, -48.04, 253.51}}. You will receive a [[Cultist augmentation bone]].
   1. Defeat three members of the Primordial Malice in the [[Enchanted Lands]] {{waypoint -1003.34, 3.71, -297.99}}. You will receive a [[Cultist augmentation bone]].
   1. In [[Everfrost]], collect **a mysterious bauble** {{waypoint -636.25, -20.92, -313.53}} near the [[World Tree Root]].
1. Return to [[Virun D'Xyafin]] in [[Neriak]].
1. Take a [[Cultist augmentation bone]] and the **bauble** to [[Lanxena T'Xith]] outside of the [[Library of K'Lorn]] {{waypoint -697.61, 16.61, 211.29}} in [[Neriak]].

## Note

Upon completion of this quest, [[Lanxena T'Xith]] instructs you to hunt down and kill as many Primordial Malice as you find. Turn in any more **arcane Malice baubles** you obtain to merchant [[Omarin T'Zaana]], nearby at {{waypoint -688.66, 16.58, 201.44}}. The baubles can be exchanged for a number of house and appearance items. See [EQ2 Trader's Corner](http://eq2.eqtraders.com/articles/article_page.php?article=q169&menustr=080000000000) for screenshots of the various items available.
