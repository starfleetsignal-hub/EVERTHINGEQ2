## Description

"I found a research notebook that was apparently left behind by a hireling that was to collect specimens for Sythor the All-Seeing. I decided I could finish the work and collect the bracelet reward. It looks like these are all animals that would live in the water. Looks like it is time for a swim!" -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Complete the following, in no particular order:
   - Kill [[A sandstalker]]
   - Kill a [[A needlefang (Outpost of the Overlord)|needlefang]]
   - Kill a [[A shoal glider (Outpost of the Overlord)|shoal glider]]
   - Kill [[A reef turtle (Outpost of the Overlord)|a reef turtle]]
1. Take the specimens to [[Trainer Sythor the All-Seeing]] {{waypoint -12, 10, 163}} inside Sythor's Spire.

## Rewards

- [[Bracer of the Apprentice]]

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

I need to hunt and collect the four different types of animals mentioned in the notebook. I need to hunt a shoal glider. I need to hunt a needlefang. I need to hunt a reef turtle. I need to hunt a sandstalker.

I have found the body of a shoal glider. I have found the body of a needlefang. I have found the body of a reef turtle. I have found the body of a sandstalker. I have found the four types of animals listed in the notebook. I have completed the research and need to take these specimens to Trainer Sythor the All-Seeing.

- Trainer Sythor the All-Seeing: "I knew I shouldn't have sent such a tasty looking gnome into dangerous waters. Ludz will be slightly missed... Anyways, here is the reward I was going to give him, but I guess it's yours now."
- Player Character: "Thanks."

I delivered the four animal specimens to Sythor the All-Seeing.

I talked to Sythor the All-Seeing at the top of his tower at the outpost. Apparently his apprentice, Ludz, was an irresistable treat for the sandstalkers. He gave me the reward that he had originally planned to give to Ludz.
