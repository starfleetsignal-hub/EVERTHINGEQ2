## Steps

1. Go to {{waypoint -9, 670, 190}} and click on the book on the ceiling.
1. Defeat [[Necretia Widowmaker]] (behind the secret door).

## Rewards

- [[A mirror (The Palindrome)|a mirror]] (only awarded if currently working on the quest [[Finding the Dain]] and need this update).
- One of the Following:
  - [[Banner of Despair]]
  - [[Shadowed Indigo Loveseat]]
  - [[Shadowed Rock Bench]]
  - [[Shadowed Stone Table]]

## Notes

1. Simply picking up this quest (after the floor flips upside down) can update the quest [[The Tower of Frozen Shadow]] for the relevant step. For this to work, you must not already have the quest "Puzzled" in your journal, this quest must not already have been completed by anyone in this zone instance, and of course you must be on this step of the [[The Tower of Frozen Shadow]] quest. Note that the update is not obtained if you accept the quest being shared to you.
1. This quest is repeatable (although it is not marked in the journal as such), but will only give XP (and AA XP) once.
1. At the current time (30 Aug 2015), although after completion this quest is added to the "Tower of Frozen Shadow" section in the Completed Quests section of the Quest Journal, after subsequently defeating Tserrina and zoning out, the quest is then removed from the journal (so is not listed amongst the completed quests). So you may have completed this and not have it listed.
