## Notes

- You must be able to speak [[Draconic]] to complete this quest!

## Steps

1. Go the [[The Emerald Gulch]] within [[The Lesser Faydark]] and click on the rock to zone into the instance [[Emerald Halls: Guardian of Growth]]. {{waypoint 177, 0, -602}}
1. Speak with [[Kaarla Teofaunta]]. {{waypoint -58.54, -8.18, 14.28}}
1. Pick up [[A damaged hollow acorn]] and examine it. {{waypoint -448, -13, -149}}
1. Return to Kaarla.
1. Speak with [[Wuoshi, the Guardian of Growth (NPC)|Wuoshi, the Guardian of Growth]]. At the end of his dialogue, you will be automatically booted out of the instance.
1. Return to [[Kurista (Greater Faydark)|Kurista]].

## Rewards

- [[Ivy-shroud of Tunare]]
- +10000 faction with **Tunare**
- [[Tunarian Wolfsbane]]
