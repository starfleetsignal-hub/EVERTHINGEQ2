## Steps

1. Craft 4 [[Healing Poultice (Blessed Healing)|Healing Poultices]].
1. Apply the Healing Poultice on the 4 *an injured Far Seas defender* at {{waypoint 675, 351, 58}}.
1. Return to Quartermaster Robbins at {{waypoint 625, 351, 178}}.

## Materials

- 4  [[Tunarian Trumpetleaf]] - can be found at the druid ring in [[Phantom Sea]] around {{waypoint 429, 34, 745}}
- 4 [[Leonid root]]
- 8 [[Handama leaf]]
- 20 [[Effulgent Candle]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +2000 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- 15 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
