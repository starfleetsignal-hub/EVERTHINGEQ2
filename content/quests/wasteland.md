**NOTE:** Although this quest is flagged as 'solo' it requires killing Heroic mobs

## Steps

1. Kill 11-13 [[A rot zombie|rot zombies]] in [[Stormhold]].
   - They can be found in a small cavern south of [[The Chessboard (Stormhold)|The Chessboard]], near {{waypoint 73, 3, -63}}.  Four are up at a time, respawn is 10 minutes real time.

## Rewards

- 1467 status @L19
- +500 faction with **The Celestial Watch**
- 1 [[City Token]]
