## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
- Quest starter and updates needed for it have a chance to drop from any mob in [[Zon Zobboz: The Oculeum (Solo)]]. It may require to resetting and running the zone again if you don't get all updates.

*While fighting  within The Oculeum, I looted a curious key made of voidmetal, with three empty gem mounts on it. I'm going to search for the missing gems and see what the repaired key can unlock.*

1. Search for a voidskrit emerald.
1. Search for a voidskrit ruby.
1. Search for a voidskrit diamond.
1. Use the repaired voidmetal key to unlock the voidmetal chest in [[Zon Zobboz]] at {{waypoint 114, 12, 745}}

## Rewards

- [[Gestation Guard Plushie]]
