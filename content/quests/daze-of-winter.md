## Prerequisites

- You must have at least +25,000 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest.

## Steps

1. Use the [[Bonechill Wand]] to daze 8 Bellywhumper or Bonedigger burynai when they only have 20% health left. {{waypoint -1527, -369, -1748}}
1. Return to Rantiri Skelbre.

## Rewards

- At least 36g 79s 48c
- +2200 faction with **The Order of Rime**
