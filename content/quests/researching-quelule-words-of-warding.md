## Steps

1. [[Researcher Tahar]] in what remains of [[Quel'ule]] has asked me to assist by creating some warding spells
   - Scribe the [[Traditional Warding Spells]] recipe book in your inventory.
   - Craft 5 warding spells at an Engraved Desk. Requires a total of 15 [[Bamboo shoot]], 10 [[Sumac lumber]] and 5 [[Ethereal incense]].
     - *Note: The Engraved Desk is just west of [[Researcher Tahar]] along the same wall.*
1. Return to [[Researcher Tahar]].

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- +3000 faction with **Researchers of Quel'ule**
