## Steps

1. Speak to [[Dain Kalus Frostreaver]] in [[The Great Divide]] at the [[Blisterrock Ridge]] camp {{waypoint 109.04, -345.66, -612.60}}
1. Defeat  [[Narandi the Enraged]] in the [[Echoes of the Ring War]] public quest. The quest will update from either an Epic X4 or an Epic X2 version of Narandi.  (8-15-15 Quest updated as a Narandi x2 spawned from an ice rock around Ring War.  Ring War was not going on.)
1. Return to the Dain to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- [[The Ten Coldain Insignia Rings]]
- One of the following, by archetype:
  - [[Ring of Dain Frostreaver VI (Mage)]]
  - [[Ring of Dain Frostreaver VI (Priest)]]
  - [[Ring of Dain Frostreaver VI (Fighter)]]
  - [[Ring of Dain Frostreaver VI (Scout)]]
