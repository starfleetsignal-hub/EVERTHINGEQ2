## Steps

1. Gather 5 **Pure Soil** from any **patch of pure soil** in kobold camp {{waypoint 155, 190, 548}} next to Highlands.
1. If you are having trouble, press "TAB" repeatedly until you target 'patch of pure soil'
1. Return to [[Geldrani A'Zhi'Tel|Geldrani]].
