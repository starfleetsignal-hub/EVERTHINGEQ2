## Steps

1. Speak to Emissary [[Talthallas (NPC)|Talthallas]] in [[Castleview Hamlet: Cloister of Purity]].
   - It may be wise to clear your way, even though the monsters aren't aggressive.  They will be later.
1. Talthallas' guards attack!  Kill the [[A Renda'Dal Guardian]] (scales, even con) and [[A Renda'Dal guardian]] (scales, v)
1. Follow Talthallas to the Castleview Hamlet waterway
   - **WARNING:** The previously benign [[A Renda'Dal soldier (Castleview Hamlet)|Renda'Dal soldiers]] and [[A Renda'Dal activist|activists]] are now aggro!
   - However, if you die during this step, they'll revert to being non-aggro.
1. Head down into the waterway.  Enter the waterway, swim left and click on the grate to enter a sub-instance, [[Talthallas's Hidden Chamber]]
1. Confront Talthallas!  He's in the south corner of the room, on the ceiling, upside down.  Turns out he's a vampire.  Talk with him a while and he'll fly away.
1. Defeat the vampire infestation!
   - Four [[A New Tunarian vampire|New Tunarian vampires]] will spawn, first two individually (scales, even) and then a group of two (scales, one even one v).  Kill them all to update.
1. Destroy [[Talthallas (Named)|Talthallas]] (Scales, ^ solo)
1. Return to Eireneith in Qeynos Province District.
