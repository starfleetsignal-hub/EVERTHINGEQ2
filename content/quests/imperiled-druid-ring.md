## Prerequisites

- You must be at least level 50 to obtain this quest.
- Make sure you have [[Tunare's field altar]] in your inventory before setting off to Antonica. If you no longer have it, get a new one from [[Kurista (Greater Faydark)|Kurista]] otherwise you won't be able to finish the quest and you'll have to start again.

## Steps

1. Go to [[Antonica]] and enter the [[Imperiled Druid Ring (Instance)|Imperiled Druid Ring]] instance by click on the rock at {{waypoint -2186, 60, 935}}. **Note:** **the rock is behind the tomb marker for Sir Edwin Motte near The Den Mother**. From [[Antonica]] dock, if  you cannot fly, the fastest route is Griffin to Thundering Steppes and then hoof it west to the draw filled with Haze Bears.
1. Destroy the amplifiers at:
   1. Southern {{waypoint -2454, 18, 35}}
   1. Eastern {{waypoint -2595, 19, -30}}
   1. Northern {{waypoint -2511, 16, -112}}
1. Harvest the nearby [[Basil of Growth]], [[Rosemary of Growth]] and [[Sage of Growth]]. (These are Trackable Harvests.)
1. Go to the center of the druid ring.
1. Move the nearby stone table to the center of the ring.
1. Use [[Tunare's field altar]] from your inventory.
1. Return to [[Kurista (Greater Faydark)|Kurista]].

## Rewards

- +5000 faction with **Tunare**
- [[Friend of Growth]]
