This is a [[Mikk Eishammer#Related Quests|Forgemasters of Thurgadin Supply Task]]

## Steps

*Note: If you're a member, use fast travel to Sinking Sands, then talk to the Othmir on the docks to get to Fina's Retreat, this is WAY faster than the griffons*  ![Piece of driftwood](images/Stoking_the_Flames_quest_-_driftwood.jpg)

1. Gather 10 pieces of Driftwood to fuel the signal fires, harvested at or near [[Fina's Retreat]] in [[Great Divide]] (take griffon to Icy Fingers):
   - {{waypoint -1625, -542, -2547}}
   - {{waypoint -1241, -541, -2072}}
   - {{waypoint -1243, -542, -2170}}
   - {{waypoint -1364, -541, -2070}}
1. Return to [[Mikk Eishammer]]

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +1,500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
