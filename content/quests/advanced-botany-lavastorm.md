## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable

## Steps

![lava rose!(passion flower)(images/Passion_flower.png)!(burning orchid)(images/Burning_orchid.png)](images/Lava_rose.png)

1. Travel to [[Lavastorm]] and gather
   - 3 lava roses. Located in [[Lava Field]], multiple spawns around  {{waypoint -528.39, -23.97, -372.14}}
   - 3 passion flowers.  Located in [[The Wounds]], multiple spawns around {{waypoint -644.38, -76.76, 0.08}}
   - a burning orchid. Located near [[Temple of Solusek Ro]], multiple possible spawns around {{waypoint -337.85, 1.34, -466.18}}, as well as up the path to the temple. They do not sparkle, so look carefully.
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].

## Rewards:

- Unlock the [[Days of Summer: 2024|Days of Summer 2024: Week 5]] "account flag"
