## Steps

1. Capture 5 [[An emerald weaver|emerald weavers]] and bring them to Yurriar - target them and use the [[Urzarach Spider Charm]], then walk them back to Yurriar. Updates automatically when you get near Yurriar.
   - *Note: You can charm and bring back multiple emerald weavers at once*
1. Speak to Yurriar

## Rewards

- At least 1p 29g 60s
- One of the following:
  - [[The Urzarach Cinch]]
  - [[The Belt of Eight Eyes]]
