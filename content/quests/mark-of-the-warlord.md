## Description

The trolls of Firmroot have asked that I collect fetishes from the creatures within the Outer Stronghold of Ykesha for trophies and rituals. I should be able to find them on either the Ykeshan soldiers, or the froglok captives being held inside the stronghold itself. Once I have collected several of these, I am to return them to Firmroot.

## Steps

1. Collect 12 Ykeshan fetishes from the trolls and frogloks in [[The Ruins of Guk: Ykesha's Outer Stronghold]].
1. Return to Vegupa Marrsheart to report your success.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
