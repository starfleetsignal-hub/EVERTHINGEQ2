## Steps

1. Scribe the [[Kerra Traditional Outfitting]] recipe page you were given
   - Note: No Mass Production
1. Craft 5 of each
   - [[Wide-fletched bodkin arrow|wide-fletched bodkin arrows]] (woodworking table)
   - [[Woven reed quiver|woven reed quivers]] (sewing table)
   - [[Traditional kerra dagger sheath|traditional kerra dagger sheaths]] (sewing table)
1. Return to [[Chinook]]

## NOTE

*To complete this quest faster, bring*

- *10 bamboo shoots*

- *17 quicksilver clusters*
- 48 sumac lumber
- 15 titanium ore

- 1 chemistry table
- 2 woodworking tables
