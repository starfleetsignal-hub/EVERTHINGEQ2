## Notes

- Each time you complete this quest, you are rewarded a unique title, and automatically acquire the quest again. However, each time you reacquire the quest, the number of players you have to hand out roses to increases, all the way up to 1000.
- You can hand out roses year-round, thereby enabling you to progress and complete this quest throughout the year, however *Erollisi Rose* nodes only appear during [[Erollisi Day]]. If the festival is no longer active, you'll have to purchase [[Fresh-cut rose|fresh-cut roses]] from the broker.
- Your handed-out rose count no longer resets when [[Erollisi Day]] ends.

## Steps

1. Present other players with [[Fresh-cut rose|fresh-cut roses]].
   - The roses are harvested from *Erollisi Rose* nodes in either [[Enchanted Lands]] or [[Frostfang Sea]]. Three roses are harvested at a time.
   - Unlike handing out roses for the quest, [[Bouquet of Trust]], this quest requires you to hand out roses to different players, i.e. you can't just spam [[Fresh-cut rose]] on the same player. There is an approximate 1-hour cooldown before a rose can be used on the same player again.
   - The number of roses you are required to hand out changes each time you complete the quest.

## Rewards
