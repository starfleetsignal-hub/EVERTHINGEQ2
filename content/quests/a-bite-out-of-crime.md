## Steps

*Outlander Deston, a member of the Qeynos Guard, has heard reports of some stolen gnomish digging equipment hidden within the Darkpaw Warrens. It should all be recovered.*

1. Locate stolen gnomish digging equipment in Darkpaw Warrens
   - Gather 10 automated rockpickers
     - Some possible locations:
       - {{waypoint -10, -4, -170}}
       - {{waypoint -27, -13, -116}}
       - {{waypoint -20, 0, 87}}
       - {{waypoint 25, -17, 192}}
       - {{waypoint 46, -17, 211}}
       - (70,-16, 207)
   - Gather 10 mechanical components
     - Some possible locations:
       - {{waypoint 65, -2, 181}}
       - {{waypoint -25, -12, -140}}
       - {{waypoint 4, -1, 10}}
       - {{waypoint -15, 3, -12}}
       - {{waypoint -6, 1, 148}}
       - {{waypoint 60, -16, 199}}
       - {{waypoint 89, 3, 173}}
       - {{waypoint 14, 3, 206}}
   - Found in Sabertooth Burrow, Mudpaw Burrow(-50,-64,-224), Dustpaw Burrow (-105,-9,-156), and Rockpaw Burrow (42, 3, 187)
   - These items may also respawn in a locked instance after some time (1-2 hours?)
1. Return to Darkpaw Dugout, and deposit the equipment in one of the crates near Outlander Deston {{waypoint 6, -77, -235}}

## Reward

- At least 3p 36g 79s 77c
- [[Serilian Hammered Plate Cuirass]]
