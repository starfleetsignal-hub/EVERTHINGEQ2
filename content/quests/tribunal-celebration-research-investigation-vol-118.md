## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of the Tribunal has informed me that summoning an Avatar requires the land to be attuned to their element. To achieve this, altars have been set up around the region, but they need to be supplied with power. I have been asked to assist with this by collecting objects infused with the essence of slain foes and offer them to an altar.

1. Collect 3 [[Infused Gavel Fragments|infused gavel fragments]] (random body drop from mobs in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]]) Body Drops near {{waypoint -574, 78, 679}}
1. Use the [[Infused Gavel Fragments|infused gavel fragments]] and empower an altar.  Random spawn in Timorous Deep, its yellow sparkles visible far away, possible locations of altar
   - {{waypoint 727, 161, 150}} near [[Chrykori Isle]] griffon tower
   - {{waypoint -3, 31, 60}} west of [[Chrykori Village]]
   - {{waypoint 1170, 199, 232}} near the western end of the broken bridge
   - {{waypoint 1900, 75, -67}} south of the bridge to [[Mok Rent]]
   - {{waypoint 1989, 66, 211}} on a hill near the [[Tomb of the Shadow Lords]]
   - {{waypoint 1853, 120, -290}} near [[Haoaera]], by the aqueduct
   - {{waypoint 35, 20, -220}} near Excavation Site
   - {{waypoint 1340, 160, 120}} near [[Thulwun Station West]]
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Seventh Hammer]] at {{waypoint 274, 180, -357}} in [[Timorous Deep]]
