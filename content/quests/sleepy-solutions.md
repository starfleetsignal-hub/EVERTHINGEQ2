## Steps

1. Acquire the following  ![](images/Sleepy_Solutions_quest_-_Splendor_Spearmint.jpg)  ![](images/Sleepy_Solutions_quest_-_Hawberry.jpg)
   - Gather 10 **Splendor Spearmint** on [[Rootland Rise]] found around {{waypoint 40, 105, 475}}, {{waypoint 107, 108, 449}} or {{waypoint 221, 59, 336}} (can be harvested while flying)
   - Gather 6 **Hawberry** on [[Rootland Rise]] at {{waypoint 36.02, 134.46, 506.31}} (can be harvested while flying)
   - Purchase [[Powdered Melatonin]] from the alchemist [[Kiawk]] at {{waypoint 273, 282, 1057}}
     - requires +20k faction with [[Sky Watcher Sodality (Faction)]] and 10g
1. Return to [[Kudurri]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +1000 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality**
  - [[Ruddy Nesting Cushion]]
