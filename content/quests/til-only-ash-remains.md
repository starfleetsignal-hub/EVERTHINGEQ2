## Steps

1. Use the received *Songs of Fire and Ash* (a book in your inventory) and find and burn 10 froglok bodies within [[The Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen|Upper Guk]].  These are spread throughout the zone and will over time return to be burnable. So theoretically you could burn the same corpse over and over.
1. I need to burn the remains of Hopton Braveheart {{waypoint 7, -32, -117}}
1. Return to [[Gopton Braveheart]] at Firmroot Moot {{waypoint 112, -86, -428}}

## Rewards

- At least 15g 83s 87c
- [[The Valorous Tragedy of the Frogloks of Guk]]
