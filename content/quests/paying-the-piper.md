## Materials Required

- 1 [[Wracked Wood Wedge]] -- *rare*
- 12 [[Hardened Zelniak Pelt]]
- 50 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
- 15 [[Slow Creeping Root]]
- 40 [[Coruscating Coal]]

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Piccolo]] in the [[Luclin Nexus]] to receive a recipe: [[Recipe Scroll: Taggerbow]]
   - *He walks in a circular path around the [[Luclin Nexus]]*
1. Craft a [[Taggerbow]] on a Work Bench (Tradeskill benches are located in the Artisan's Hall {{waypoint -56, -1, 5}}
1. Use the [[Taggerbow]] on the following creatures in [[Echo Caverns]]:
   - *If needed, use the calming recipe item on the wildlife to get where you need to be. Alternatively, use [[Totem of the Chameleon]] if you don't have invisibility.*
   - [[A rock gnawer]] in [[The Upper Quarry]] {{waypoint 114, 108, 44}} - *Eats through profits in the quarry*
   - [[Limestone arachnid]] in [[Limestone Forest]] {{waypoint 453, 139, 39}} - *Has glowing green webs*
   - [[A needlite pupa]] in [[Limestone Forest]] - *Bloodsucker in miniature proportions {{waypoint 423.93, 143.64, 537.76}}*
   - [[A giant salted sea slug]] in [[Limestone Forest]] - *Surprisingly salty creature {{waypoint 645.70, 135.98, 678.78}}*
   - [[A chloro-fly]] in [[The Chlorochamber]] {{waypoint 189, 159, 460}} - *Winged and emits a humming noise when near*
   - [[Cavern beetle]] in [[The Chlorochamber]] {{waypoint 170, 157, 400}} - *One-horned and polka dots*
   - [[A falls tuatara]]  in [[The Colossal Cascades]] {{waypoint -311, 177, 335}} - *Easily excited spikey-lizards*
   - [[A trace sparkle]] (not to be confused with a trace mist) in [[The Colossal Cascades]]  {{waypoint -327, 173, 335}} - *Emits prismatic light*
   - ***Note:** I could not fly, but I stood at {{waypoint -444, 190, 326}}   and was able to target one of the sparkles hoovering above me. Also cross the river to {{waypoint -189, 178, 349}}  and follow the path north, then east.*
1. Deliver the catalog to [[Piccolo]] in [[Echo Caverns]] {{waypoint -65, 177, -102}} near the zone-in.
1. Speak to [[Foreman Gibbons]] in the quarry {{waypoint 19, 159, -99}}.

## Rewards

- At least 6p 73g 90s 7c
- [[Sheen Green Lumi Fungi]] (house item)
