## Steps

1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (The Bloody Brutal Truth)|Pakiat Divining Device]] *from your inventory* at {{waypoint -375, 16, 867}}, South East of Zivier's Reach teleport. ![first & subsequent locations](images/The_Bloody_Brutal_Truth-first_location.jpg)
1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (The Bloody Brutal Truth)|Pakiat Divining Device]] at {{waypoint -188, 14, 956}}.
1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (The Bloody Brutal Truth)|Pakiat Divining Device]] at {{waypoint -307, -7, 1158}}.
1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (The Bloody Brutal Truth)|Pakiat Divining Device]] at {{waypoint -456, 14, 1210}}.
1. Approach the blood wisp at {{waypoint -584, 16, 1234}}.![blood wisp](images/Blood_wisp.jpg)
1. Kill the [[A feral stelecat|feral stelecat]].
1. Click on the Blood Wisp.
1. Return to [[Ta-sche-ra-he]].
1. Click on Blood Wisp Storage at {{waypoint -569, 131, -885}}![Blood Wisp Storage](images/Blood_Wisp_Storage.jpg)
