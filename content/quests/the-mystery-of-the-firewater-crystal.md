## Notes

- You must be able to speak Ratongan for this part. Also, you need to walk backwards while looking at her when she tells you to go away, about 3m. At that point you will need to pay her 1g to get info and the sub-quest.
- [[The Commonlands]] > [[The Sprawl]] > [[The Serpent Sewer]] > [[Edgewater Drains]] Lvl 1(Take SE zonein from Sewers)

## Steps

1. Vasiliy, the Ratonga alchemist that resides within the Edgewater Drains needs particular ingredients to create the firewater crystal.
   - Obtain a flame emerald (Purchased from City of Jinisk Faction Merchant)
   - Obtain some kedge gills (The gills drop from any mob in Chardok that is over level 80.)
1. Return to Vasiliy in Edgewater Drains.

## Rewards

You can now buy the crystal from Vasiliy for 1g 96s.
