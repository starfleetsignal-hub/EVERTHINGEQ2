## Steps

1. Speak with [[Eralok Riz'rok]] {{waypoint 2486, 34, 1345}}.
1. Speak with [[Parser Talakla Dih'dl]] {{waypoint 2606, 119, 1169}}.
1. Speak with [[Parser Voldik Myli'sok]] {{waypoint 2705, 106, 1191}}.
1. Pick up the book, *Grazyk's Anatomy of the Sarnak Body*, on a table in [[The Grand Athenaeum]] {{waypoint 2662, 84, 1357}}.
1. Speak with [[Prime Parser Tolok Ku'ele]] {{waypoint 2599, 119, 1178}}.
1. Speak to and kill [[A parser disciple]], beneath [[Faydedar Watch]]. Loot his corpse afterwards for [[A sarnak cadaver]] {{waypoint 2407, 3, 1374}}.
1. Return to [[Hrath Everstill]].

## Rewards

- +1250 faction with **Bertoxxulous**
- [[Humble Altar of Bertoxxulous]]
