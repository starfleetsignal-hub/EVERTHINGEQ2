## Description

The Quel'ule scholar Azira has requested assistance in obtaining more samples of the strangely altered minerals in the region.

## Steps

![strange mineral deposit](images/Strange_mineral.png)

1. The strangely altered minerals can be found near the rocks and cliffs around Quel'ule.
   - Obtain 10 samples of the strange minerals (these look like an infused deposit with green sparklies, and can only be seen when you need to harvest them).
1. Return to [[Researcher Azira]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +2,500 faction with **Researchers of Quel'ule**
