## Steps

1. Slay [[Raschaud Tuthosols]] outside [[Riliss (POI)|Riliss]] in [[Fens of Nathsar]]. {{waypoint -104, -26, -152}}
1. Mount [[Burkmaiir]], Raschaud Tuthosols' nearby sokokar, and spread the fermenting agent over Riliss. You do not have to actually perform any action on the sokokar and the quest will update as soon as you land.
1. Slay [[Invigilator Warwyn Al'Xi]] outside [[Bathezid's Watch]]. {{waypoint 1300, 200, 1087}}
1. Mount [[Zaloga]], Invigilator Warwyn Al'Xi's nearby sokokar, and spread the fermenting agent over Bathezid's Watch. You do not have to actually perform any action on the sokokar and the quest will update as soon as you land.
1. Head to [[Jaled Dar's Remains]] and use [[Scroll: Call of the Plaguebringer]] to summon Hrath Everstill. {{waypoint -1545, -369, -1524}}
1. Speak to Hrath Everstill to begin the summoning of [[Bertoxxulous]]!
   - This is a ring event. The first wave consists of several [[A Priest of Life|Priests of Life]], followed by two waves of [[A Vindicator of Life|Vindicators of Life]], and then finally [[High Priestess Jahnda]].
   - If at any point the ring event bugs, either the successive wave not spawning or the quest not updating after killing High Priestess Jahnda, speak with Hrath to force the next wave to spawn. Hrath will despawn after several minutes, so if you have not completed this step in-time, use the scroll to summon him again.
1. Speak with [[The Avatar of Plagues]].

## Rewards

- [[Cloak of Pestilence]]
- At least 27g 35s 22c
- 10000 faction with **Bertoxxulous**
