## Steps

1. Enter [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Crucible]] and locate the notes detailing the trials of Miragul
   - Find notes of the peers research about Miragul's work {{waypoint -104.96, 0.08, 22.72}}
   - Find notes relating to Miragul's travels to Everfrost {{waypoint 49, 1, 45}}
   - Find the court minutes from the tribunal of Miragul {{waypoint -120, 14, 102}}
   - Search for notes detailing Miragul's travels to other planes {{waypoint 42.56, 16.81, 100.41}}
   - Find notes regarding Miragul's life after his exile {{waypoint -39, 51, 211}}
1. Return to the Moors of Ykesha and report your success.

## Rewards

- At least 1p
- 2 [[Void Shard]]
