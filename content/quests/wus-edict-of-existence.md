## Steps

1. Find Kyau's mat at {{waypoint 730, 90, -294}} and right click to begin meditation. It is a green mat next to [[A follower of Wu]]. You will be stunned for about 30 seconds with Wu's Contemplation while you are forced to meditate.
1. Return to [[Master Kyau]]

## Rewards

- At least 9g 27s 1c
- +100 faction with **Disciples of Wu**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Loop of Unwelcome Dreams]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Ring of Unwelcomed Dreams]]
  - [[Ringlet of Unwelcomed Dreams]]
  - [[Signet of Unwelcomed Dreams]] (All Scouts)
