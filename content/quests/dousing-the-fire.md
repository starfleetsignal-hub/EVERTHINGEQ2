## Description

The Dragoons of Thex have sent me into the Deep Forge to thin the residents so they are able to send in scouts to investigate the threat rising within.

## Steps

1. Thin the Fire Ogre population (kill 20) within [[The Deep Forge]]. There are 14 around the walkway area, 6 behind the lava waterfall, and 5 in the forge room.
1. Return to [[Isxot D'Zyath]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]]
