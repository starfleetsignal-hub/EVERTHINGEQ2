## Notes

- You can start the quest only before the first named got killed

## Steps

1. find various things
   - kill [[Bhoughbh Model XVII]] and acquire a Flux Diode
   - kill [[Short-Circuited Construct Bot]] and Acquire a Clockwork Fastener
   - kill [[Sentinel XXI]] and Acquire a Sentinel Gear
   - kill [[Electroshock Grinder VIII]] Find and Acquire a Shock Capacitor
   - kill [[The Malfunctioning Slaver]] and Acquire a Slaver Servo
   - kill [[MCP-Powered Pulsar]] (epicx4) and Acquire an MCP Unit
   - kill [[The Tinkered Abomination]] and Acquire a Clockwork Thing-a-ma-jig
   - find and Acquire a Bottle of Clockwork Grease
     - *clickable item at {{waypoint 104.57, 48.43, -74.99}}*
   - find and Acquire a Gnomish Tools
     - *clickable item at {{waypoint 69.86, 33.47, -148.16}}*
   - kill [[Hovercopter Hingebot]] and acquire a Optical Eye
1. Speak with [[Bhoughbh Bhildur]] at the top of the zone
1. kill [[Bhoughbh Nova-Prime]]

## Rewards

- At least 10p
- [[Bhoughbh's Tinkerering Manual for Dummies]]
