## Steps

1. Deliver a message to [[Tish Tickers]] on the top floor of [[Qeynos Tower 3]] at {{waypoint -227, 9.77, 31}}
   - Note:  Take the bird to **Qeynos Tower 4**, then follow the road North and it will take you to  [[Qeynos Tower 3]]. Go inside the tower and climb to the third level. Tish is there.
1. Tish gives you the sub-quest [[A Scrying Shame]]. You must complete that quest to continue this one.
1. Return to Jacques.

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
- Head slot armor based on class.
