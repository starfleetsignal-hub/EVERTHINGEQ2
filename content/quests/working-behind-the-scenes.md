The Quellithulians say that there has been damage done to the essence of the spires themselves, and that it must be repaired. I am to travel into the portal breach opened by the Quellithulians and use the planar workbenches to create the parts necessary to repair the damage.

## Starting the Quest

This quest remains the same regardless of the zone you start in. The only difference is the person you speak with to start the quest:

- Talk to [[Ajeem Dul'zar]] in [[Antonica]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (Ant)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -2059, 12, -639}}
- Talk to [[Ferra Al'darr]] in [[The Commonlands]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (CL)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint 0, -48, -424}}
- Talk to [[Sueril Omapelle]] in [[The Thundering Steppes]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (TS)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint 1170, -26, 151}}
- Talk to [[Yval T'laviv]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (Nek)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -329, 2, -643}}
- Talk to [[Eruzin Fah'rel]] in [[Enchanted Lands]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (EL)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -385, 0, 64}}
- Talk to [[Dalah D'arzin]] in [[The Feerrott]] by the [[Ulteran Spires (Feer)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -60, 13, 158}}

## Steps

1. I must create six of one of the following items to help repair the damage to the spire.
   - [[Silver Thread Mesh]]
   - [[Phased Support Beam]]
   - [[Rune Engraved Planeshard]]
1. Turn in the completed items to [[A Spire Essence Engineer]].
1. Zone back out and speak to one of the six NPCs to finish the quest.

## Rewards

- 6 [[Quellithulian Token of Appreciation|Quellithulian Tokens of Appreciation]]
- At least 55s 40c

## Notes

Some materials, including fuel, may be purchased from [[A Quellithulian supply chandler]] within [[The Spire's Essence]]. You will need common Tier 2 (levels 10-19) crafting ingredients.

- Silver Thread Mesh
  - 1 pure silver thread (purchase inside instance, 4s 80c)
  - 1 Raw Electrum
  - 1 Raw Turqoise
  - 1 Raw Tuber Strands
  - 3 thread filament(can purchase inside instance, 24c each)
- Phased Support Beam
  - 1 shimmering alloy (purchase inside instance, 4s 80c)
  - 1 Raw Iron
  - 1 Raw Tanned Leather
  - 1 Raw Electrum
  - 3 brown coal (can purchase inside instance, 24c each)
- Rune Engraved Planeshard
  - 1 shimmering alloy (purchase inside instance, 4s 80c)
  - 1 Raw Electrum
  - 1 Raw Turqoise
  - 1 Raw Tanned Leather
  - 3 brown coal (can purchase inside instance, 24c each)
