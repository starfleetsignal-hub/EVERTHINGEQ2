## Steps

1. Speak with [[Ulhira Grissh]] at {{waypoint -100, -9, -123}}
1. Kill [[A Thexian rebel|a Thexian rebels]] found around {{waypoint -30, 0, -22}} for 8 updates in [[Longshadow Alley: Daeneth Cloister]] {{waypoint -93, -34, -364}}
1. Speak with [[Kuzik Zozin]] at {{waypoint 26, -12, 97}} in [[The City of Freeport]]
1. Enter [[Big Bend: Sanctum of Spirits]] for the following updates
   - place 5 idols
     - {{waypoint -62, 5, -3}} *possible that you can place all idols at the same place*
     - The term 'place' is misleading as you have no idols in your inventory to place
     - other locs possible
   - kill [[Rossk]] spawns near {{waypoint -14, 0, 8}}
1. Speak with [[Zulk Veskk]] at {{waypoint -85, -25, 247}} in [[The City of Freeport]]

## Rewards

- At least 25g 70s 61c
