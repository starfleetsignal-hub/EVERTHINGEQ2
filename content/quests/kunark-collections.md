Expert Recognition collections, first introduced in the [[Echoes of Faydwer]] expansion, are comprised of the rarest collectables from most of the other collection quests introduced in the expansion. The reward, [[Kunark Expert Adventurer Goggles]], is an item that, when worn, will allow a character to see otherwise invisible Kunark and Faydwer collectible harvesting nodes ('blue shinies'), opening an entirely new set of collection quests in Rise of Kunark and Echoes of Faydwer zones.
*For a list of collections and blue shiny spawn locations, see [[Expert Collection Item Known Locations#Rise of Kunark Blue Shinies|Here]]*.
