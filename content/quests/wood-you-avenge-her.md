## Steps

1. Slay 20 [[A Sullon lumberjack|Sullon lumberjack]]s. {{waypoint -382, -28, -300}}
1. Slay [[A Sullon carpenter]]. He spawns when you walk close to the back wall of the Sullon mill {{waypoint -373, -28, -295}}. You can trigger him from outside the mill. He is level 38, but quite easy for his level.
1. Return to Birchbark
