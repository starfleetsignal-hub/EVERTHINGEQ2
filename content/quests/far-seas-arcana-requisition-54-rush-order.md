## Notes

**Need to complete the "Ship Out" questline in order to receive requisitions.**

**August 23, 2009: If you get a requisition that requires you to make Holy Strike V you will need to delete the quest and try again, as this spell cannot be made by a Sage.**

- You have 8 minutes 30 seconds to complete this quest.
- You will need the following fuels:
  - 48 masala incense
- You will need the following raws:
  - 24 succulent roots
  - 24 severed sandalwood
  - 0-6 beryllium clusters
  - 0-6 rough nacre

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Kaisha Swifteasel]] to receive the quest.
1. Click on the Requisition Order Desk next to [[Kaisha Swifteasel]] to receive your requisition.
1. Craft six random low level 50's Journeyman spells.
1. Speak with [[Kaisha Swifteasel]] to finish the quest.

- Known Requisition combinations
  - Requisition A (cannot be completed due to the Holy Strike V spell)
    - [[Ball of Fire III]] (level 51)
    - [[Atrophy V]] (level 52)
    - [[Condemn V]] (level 52)
    - [[Thorncoat V]] (level 52)
    - [[Holy Strike V]] **This spell cannot be completed, as it is a level 80 Paladin spell made by an Alchemist**
    - [[Aneurysm IV]] (level 53)
  - Requisition B
    - [[Hostage III]] (level 51)
    - [[Entrance V]] (level 52)
    - [[Enraging Demeanor III]] (level 52)
    - [[Thorncoat V]] (level 52)
    - [[Haze V]] (level 52)
    - [[Ego Shock IV]] (level 53)

## Rewards

- At least: 2g 43s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
