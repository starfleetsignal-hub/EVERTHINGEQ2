## Steps

1. Head west of Scarstone to look for undead
1. No sign of undead, but something "beautiful" - keep heading west and search the small islands
1. Speak to [[A distressed siren]] on the small island at {{waypoint 5260, -1067, 507}}
1. Return to Head Scholar Nabihan

- Direct Access to Scarstone via Navigation Globe
