## Steps

1. Svyi asks you to collect more advanced weapons for the training he is providing from within the [[Caves of the Great Tiger Spirit|caves]].
   - Retrieve the weapons from the first weapon rack. {{waypoint -507, 295, 3156}}  ![One of the weapon racks](images/Training_the_Young_quest_-_weapon_rack.jpg)
   - Retrieve the weapons from the second weapon rack. {{waypoint -551, 295, 3142}}
   - Retrieve the weapons from the third weapon rack. {{waypoint -474, 294, 3147}}
1. Return to Svyi.

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +2000 faction with **Hua Mein**
