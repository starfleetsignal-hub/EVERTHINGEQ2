## Steps

1. Go to [[The Thundering Steppes]] and:
   - Kill 16 [[A drowned trooper|drowned troopers]] skeletons.
   - Kill 6 [[A marine slug|marine slugs]] near the docks.
   - Kill 20 Undead Creatures (The 16 drowned troopers count towards these 20 kills).
1. Return to Selwyn.
