## Description

"Princess Zhyleen would like me to let Lexxiuna know what has occurred in Tunare's Glade. She is back at Spire Shadow Outpost." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Lexxiuna]] at [[Spire Shadow Outpost]]

## Rewards

- At least 4s 82c
- [[Cylinder of Superior Growth]]

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Princess Zhyleen: "Have you cured the saplings?"
- Player Character: "Yes, I have."
- Princess Zhyleen: "That's a relief. Thank you, [Player Character], this is much appreciated. I'm glad you arrived when you did! This news will be of interested to Lexxiuna, I am certain. You should tell her."
- Player Character: "I will let her know."
- Princess Zhyleen: "Good. Thank you again for your help."
- Player Character: "You are welcome."

Speak with Lexxiuna.

- Lexxiuna: "You return, [Player Character]! I knew you were the correct one to answer Princess Zhyleen's summons."
- Player character: "I helped Zhyleen clean up what the orcs had left behind."
- Lexxiuna: "From the last time we had cleared their ilk from the glade. That is good news. I had hoped that they were not threatening the grove, once more. Thank you for the news."
- Player Character: "You're welcome."

I have spoken with Lexxiuna.

I let Lexxiuna know what has occurred in Tunare's Glade.
