## Steps

1. Speak with [[The Forgotten (The City of Freeport)|The Forgotten]] in [[West Freeport (POI)|West Freeport]] {{waypoint 196, -3, -34}}.
1. Enter [[A Stinking Stormdrain]] ("a maintenance sewer grate") at {{waypoint 66, -10, -16}}
1. Deal with Lerenth
   1. Wait till he has killed the npc
   1. "Kill" Lerenth (level plus one): get his health to 0% so be comes unattackable
   1. Speak with Adrianna or Micha Longtaill within the zone
      - Adrianna will give you a scroll to kill Lerenth
      - Micha Longtail will give you a wand to restore Lerenth
   1. Use the scroll or wand on Lerenth while he is not attackable
1. Zone out and speak with [[The Forgotten (The City of Freeport)|The Forgotten]]

- At least 22g 12s 70c (@ level 83)
