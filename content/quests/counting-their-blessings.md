## Steps

1. Use the [[Elemental Resonance Spectrometer]] on the following mobs to study them:
   - Study a [[A nebulous haze]] on Cloudmist Isle around {{waypoint -605, 248, -150}}
   - Study a [[A drifting cloudmist]] on the Isle of Awakening around {{waypoint 100.72, 188.52, 400.63}}
   - Study [[A Strifewing prowler]] on the Isle of Desolation around {{waypoint 400.58, 272.83, -459.61}}
   - Study [[A Blacktalon conscript]] on Blackwind Isle around {{waypoint -382.76, 112.80, 291.74}}
   - Study a [[A Windgazer lookout]] on Whisperwind Isle around {{waypoint -609.44, -31.56, -424.47}}
1. Return to [[Researcher Aillena|Aillena]]

## Rewards

- At least 2g 7s 7c
