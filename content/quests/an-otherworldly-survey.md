## Description

Researchers from The Dead have commissioned the retrieval of a number of essences of the spirits within the Anathema for their studies.

## Steps

1. Retrieve the essence of 10 spirits from within [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Anathema]] by double-clicking a [[A tortured essence (Anathema)|a tortured essence]].
   - There are 11 spirits in the zone, in the following locations:
     - In the same room where you have to clear the flying skulls and urns to drop the force field
       - {{waypoint 60.99, 2.79, -66.95}}
     - Behind the spider boss's crypt
       - {{waypoint 171.48, 18.14, -152.12}}
     - Behind the tree at the end of the graveyard
       - {{waypoint 253.38, 18.14, -109.25}}
     - In the bottom of the Blood Sucker's crypt
       - {{waypoint 189.97, 3.25, -209.90}}
     - In the bottom of the Spider Boss's crypt
       - {{waypoint 180.24, 3.19, 0.13}}
     - In the frozen hallway
       - {{waypoint -88.88, -2.01, 8.22}}
     - Around the lake, in three places
       - {{waypoint -170.92, -12.73, 185.71}}
       - {{waypoint -99.64, -15.69, 208.72}}
       - {{waypoint -180.40, -12.23, 223.14}}
     - As you pass up to the puzzle room
       - {{waypoint -265.81, -2.27, 180.66}}
     - Boss at the bottom of the lake
       - {{waypoint -122.27, -32.21, 133.54}}
1. Return to [[Chief Scholar Lakewind]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
