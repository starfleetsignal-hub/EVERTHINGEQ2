## Steps

1. Pick up and examine *a list of chores* from the couch behind [[Taechun Welby]] at {{waypoint 218.5, 12.5, 50}}.
   - *Note: This provides you with "Portrait of Mayong Mistmoore".
1. Click on 6 statues of Mayong Mistmoore to dust them off.
   - {{waypoint 124.5, 11, -20.5}} in [[Foyer of the Visitant]]
   - {{waypoint 124.5, 11, 20.5}} in [[Foyer of the Visitant]]
   - {{waypoint 216, 23, -151.5}} in [[The Grand Ballroom]]
   - {{waypoint 132.5, 23, -152}} in [[The Grand Ballroom]]
   - {{waypoint 101.5, 23, -204.5}} in [[Master's Private Library]]
   - {{waypoint 257.5, 30, -207}} in the room up the stairs from [[Vestibule of the Regent]]
1. Polish all the suits of armor in the [[Galleria of Remembrance]] at {{waypoint 36, 23, -107}}
   - One of these suits will spawn [[A Possessed Suit of Armor]]
1. Hang the painting of Mayong Mistmoore on the wall at {{waypoint 55, 27.5, -204.5}}
   - *Note: When you get close to this area you will get a screen message saying 'This looks like a good place to hang the painting.' Use the "Portrait of Mayong Mistmoore" in your inventory.*
1. Return to [[Taechun Welby|Taechun]]

## Rewards

- At least: 3g 77s 52c
- One of the following:
  - [[Large Black-Framed Painting of Mayong Mistmoore]]
  - [[Large Red-Framed Painting of Mayong Mistmoore]]
