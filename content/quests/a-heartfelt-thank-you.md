## Steps

1. Pick [[Windswept Frostflower]] on the ridge to the west from {{waypoint -1344, -516, -2108}} to {{waypoint -1256, -530, -2213}}.
1. Deliver the flowers to [[Finnegan]] at {{waypoint -1252.69, -538.60, -2197.02}}.
1. Return to [[Arjon]].

- At least 1p 25g
