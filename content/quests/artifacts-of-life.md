## Note

- You must speak [[Sathirian]] to get the quest.

## Steps

![](images/A_refuse_pile_in_Twark.jpg)

1. Collect 10 Twark artifacts
   - Click on "A Refuse Pile" at random locations in the relatively large area around [[Twark]]. They dont all validate.
   - *Note: see talk page for more location details*
1. Return to Trader Gorakris

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 15108 status
- [[Middle Shard of an Obulus Medallion]] - needed for [[Kunark Ascending: Resurrection Machination]]
