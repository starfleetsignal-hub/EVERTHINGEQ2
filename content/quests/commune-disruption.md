## Steps

Zone into [[Ssraeshza Temple: Inner Sanctum (Heroic)]]:

- Overthrow the Overseer
- Destroy the temple's guardian
- Breach the gallery
- Stop the watcher
- Disrupt the commune

## Rewards

- At least 2p 16g 20,000 status
- 15 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- At least one of the following
  - Crate of Armor (Chain/Cloth/Leather/Plate) depending on class
