## Steps

1. Search for the remains of the Necromonstrosity.
   - Travel to [[Deepwater Pavilion]] and you'll find the bones at {{waypoint 9, 207, -170}} at the base of [[Ghanlin's Descent]]. ![Remains of the Necromonstrosity](images/Recycle_Your_Minions_Part_Three_quest_-_bones.jpg)
   - NOTE: if you're having trouble locating the bones, travel to the Erudin Gardens, then put the waypoint in, and follow it to the bones. You need to be on this level, not above, or below.
   - NOTE: you can also go to {{waypoint 9.35, 248.91, -148.57}} and jump straight down.
1. Examine the bones to spawn [[The Necromonstrosity]] (88^). Kill it for its [[Necromonstrosity Bone Chips|bone chip]].
   - *After you've slain the monster, examine the sole remaining skull on the ground where you spawned it to receive the quest starter item [[Skeletal Erudite Remains]].*
1. Return to [[Damodar]] {{waypoint 470, 244, -43}}.
