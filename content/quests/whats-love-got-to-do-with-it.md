## Notes

- This quest in not repeatable.
- This is a subquest of [[Unforgettable]].

## Description

I met up with Priestess Annalisa Swornlove in the city of New Halas, along with her brother, Aaronolis, and three Sisters of Erollisi.  She has been shaken by the apparent power of hate, but she is determined to continue to carry out the works of Erollisi!

## Steps

1. Enter the special instance, [[Nektropos Castle: Love's Errand]], and enter the courtyard. {{waypoint 0, 0, 26}}
1. Pick up [[A curious scroll]] from [[The Chapel (Nektropos Castle)|The Chapel]]. {{waypoint -20, 0, 55}}
1. Examine the scroll from your inventory.
1. Summon and speak with [[Kirstiana X'aphon]].
   - Click on the stone in front of the statue, titled *The Lady of Insurrection*, to dust off the cobwebs. Click on the stone again to summon Kirstiana X'aphon.
1. Locate and enter the Everling library.
   1. Click the brick wall at {{waypoint -20, 0, 68}} to open a secret door leading to the rest of the instance.
   1. Kill the two [[A restless lich|restless liches]] at {{waypoint 35, 13, 30}} and {{waypoint 35, 13, 16}}. After you killed the final lich, a green status message will flash on your screen telling you that a door has opened.
   1. Enter the Everling library. {{waypoint -27.5, 13, 63.5}}
1. Recover the stolen tome, [[The Enemy Within]], a big blue book on a shelf at {{waypoint -20, 14, 48}}.
1. Return to [[Priestess Annalisa Swornlove (Frostfang Sea)|Priestess Annalisa Swornlove]]

## Rewards

- [[Intricate Tapestry]]
