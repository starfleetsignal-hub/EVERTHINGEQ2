## Steps

The Sang'Huuu are doing all they can to hold back the Zakir Rish, and wish to take the fight to the djinn in their home territory. They've sought me out, knowing of my reputation, and I have agreed. The djinn will never know what hit them!

- Enter [[Vaashkaani: Argent Sanctuary (Solo)]]
1. Slay the Silver Sardar
1. Slaughter the sphinxes
1. Fell the Fallen
1. Hunt the serpent
1. Defeat the General
