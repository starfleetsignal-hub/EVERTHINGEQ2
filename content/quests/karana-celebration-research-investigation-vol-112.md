## Notes

This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

The herald of Karana has informed me that summoning an Avatar requires the land to be attuned to their element. To achieve this, altars have been set up around the region, but they need to be supplied with power. I have been asked to assist with this by collecting objects infused with the essence of slain foes and offer them to an altar.

1. Collect 3 [[Infused Storm Stone|infused storm stones]] (random body drop from a lot of mobs in [[Aether Wroughtlands]], various mobs such as viscious lavafangs and firespine gnawers update this quest)
1. Use the [[Infused Storm Stone|infused storm stones]] and empower an altar.  **Random spawn in The Enchanted Lands**, it's yellow sparkles are visible far away, possible locations of altar:
   - {{waypoint 90, 0, -45}}
   - {{waypoint 503, 1, -326}} in Berrybrook Field
   - {{waypoint -148, -1, -377}}  next to the bridge over the Lazy Drain
   - {{waypoint 135, 7, -540}} at "The Hole"
   - {{waypoint 310, 25, -565}}
   - {{waypoint -119, 29, -963}} northeast of the The Foul Stench pond
   - {{waypoint -106, 32, -1097}} in Bramblefoot Hills, east of the Lamia Cave
   - {{waypoint -343, -0, -585}}
   - {{waypoint -761, 3, -702}} on the hill north of Tagglefoot Farms
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Stormlord]] {{waypoint -371, -1, -1091}} in the [[Enchanted Lands]]
