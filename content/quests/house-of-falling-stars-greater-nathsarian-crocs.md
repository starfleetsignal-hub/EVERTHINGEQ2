## Steps

1. Kill 13 [[A greater Nathsarian croc|greater Nathsarian crocs]] in [[Fens of Nathsar]].
   - They're found in the river gorge that runs across the bottom of the zone.  Take a lift down to the water level at {{waypoint -537, 145, 973}} (or float, or use a jumper or glider mount...).  Note that plain [[A Nathsarian croc|Nathsarian croc]]s don't update the quest.
