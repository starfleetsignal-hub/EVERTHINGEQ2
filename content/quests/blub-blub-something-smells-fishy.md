## Steps

1. Slay 10 Coldwind fish offshore in [[Antonica]] around {{waypoint 61, -42, 853}}. Here are a few of the fish you can choose from:
   - [[A Coldwind perch]] (level 11)
   - [[A Coldwind pike]] (level 12)
   - [[A Coldwind barracuda]] (level 14)
1. Return to [[Matsy Rollingpin|Matsy]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Coldwind Chowder]]
- At least 92c
