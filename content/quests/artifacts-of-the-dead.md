## Description

The initial parties that the Coalitions sent into the Phylactery were almost entirely wiped out, and physical artifacts of them have been left behind that may have been touched by the corrupt power there. I should collect those artifacts so that they can be studied for how they have been affected by magic.

## Steps

1. Search among the beasts within [[Miragul's Phylactery: The Anathema]] for heirlooms belonging to those who died inside
   - An unfortunate's sword, dropped by [[A mordant penitence]].
   - An unfortunate's shield, dropped by [[Blood Sucker]].
   - An unfortunate's breast plate, dropped by [[A deathly Malice]].
   - An unfortunate's head, dropped by [[A deepened sadness]].
   - An unfortunate's hand, dropped by [[A dark Malevolence]].
   - An unfortunate's leg, dropped by [[Patriae Vykel]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
