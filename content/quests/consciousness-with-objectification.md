## Steps

1. Equip the [[Glasses of Awareness]]
1. Go to {{waypoint 748, 153, 201}} on top of the northernmost peak of the stratafold, and click on the 4 visions that appear.
   - Note: Graphics need to be at least Balanced to be able to see visions.![](images/Visions.jpg) Also, be aware that the placement of the icons was poorly programmed in and these can be impossible to click even tho you can see them easily.
   - the eye is what you see
   - the mist is what you smell
   - the "shiny" is what you feel
   - the red musical notes are what you hear
1. Go to {{waypoint 1127, 797, -1485}} on the rocky outcrop above the teleporter on the zone in island (an evac will get you there quickly), and click on the 4 visions that appear.  ![](images/Other_Visions.jpg)
   - the eye is what you see
   - the mist is what you smell
   - the "shiny" is what you feel
   - the red musical notes are what you hear
1. Examine the [[Unattuned Blessing of Consciousness]] you got to receive [[Attuned Blessing of Consciousness]]
1. Return to [[Khat-Ra Pakiat]] at {{waypoint -789, 171, -724}}
   - *Note: Don't forget to re-equip your normal helm!*
