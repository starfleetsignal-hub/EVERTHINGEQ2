## Notes

This quest is repeatable. You will be asked to do 2 random tasks for more coin.

## Steps

![boil flies](images/Boil_flies.png)
![a gloomeye scuttler](images/A_gloomeye_scuttler.png)
![a mirefang corruptor](images/A_mirefang_corruptor.png)

1. Gather some more healing components outside the village, around [[The Gnarls]], [[Rot Reek Bog]] and [[Bog'ta Beach]]:
   - gather gnarltree durians for their pulp x 12
     - (not trackable) on the ground in [[The Gnarls]], the area East of Darou village   {{waypoint -24.12, 49.61, -496.06}}
   - pop boil nests for boil flies to harvest x 12  {{waypoint -371, 1, -513}}
     - (not trackable) look around [[Rot Reet Bog]] and on [[Bog'ta Beach]] - around rocks and on edges of main paths, kick nests and some will release a swarm of flies to click on. Not all nests will release flies.
     - *NOTE:  Once you kick the nest, you must collect the flies to get credit.*
     - {{waypoint -343, 24, -358}}, {{waypoint -401, 21, -298}}
     - {{waypoint -467, 18, -275}}, {{waypoint -377, 17, -358}}
   - hunt [[A gloomeye scuttler]] for their medicinal purple blood x 10
     - kill crabs on [[Bog'ta Beach]] {{waypoint -334, -0.88, -542}}
   - hunt [[A mirefang corruptor]] for pest-repellant fur x 10
     - kill big cats in [[Rot Reet Bog]] area
1. Return to [[Narezi]]

## Rewards

- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- 2 x [[Gnarltree Durian]]
