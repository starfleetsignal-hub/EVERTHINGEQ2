- [[Woven Etched Boots (Level 30) (Crafted)|Tailored Woven Etched Boots]]
- [[Woven Etched Gloves (Level 30) (Crafted)|Tailored Woven Etched Gloves]]
- [[Woven Etched Pants (Level 30) (Crafted)|Tailored Woven Etched Pants]]
- [[Woven Etched Shoulder Pads|Tailored Woven Etched Shoulder Pads]]
- [[Woven Etched Tunic (Level 30) (Crafted)|Tailored Woven Etched Tunic]]

## Rewards

- At Least 24s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
