## Steps

1. Slay [[A Samiel outfitter|Samiel outfitters]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] for 8 uncommon updates. {{waypoint -1848, -228, 634}}
1. Read [[A Samiel outfitter note]] from your inventory.
1. Atop of [[Brigand's Boneyard]] (climb point at {{waypoint -1916, -234, -543}}) slay the group of [[A Samiel plunder guard|Samiel plunder guard]]s.  Note:  You may have to actually climb the wall to get the guards to spawn.  Just landing on top of the rock on a flying mount won't trigger it.
1. Inspect the crate labeled 'Far Sands cargo' to receive [[A crate, Far Sands cargo]].
1. Return to Barter Lord Tum Jal'Daris.

## Rewards
