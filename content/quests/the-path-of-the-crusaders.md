## Steps

I must make my way past the Temporal Chamber, defeating all of the Awakened Legion that I encounter along the way.

1. Defeat [[Balor the Primeval]] in battle.
1. Defeat [[Conflagrator Belkreiz Blazeclaw]] in battle.
1. Defeat [[Ikatiar]] in battle.
1. Investigate the Temporal Chamber for clues as to what happened to the mighty Crusaders
   - Gather Amulet at {{waypoint -62, -419, -12}}
1. Use the portal located beyond the Temporal Chamber to be transported
1. Return to [[High Commander Vyrin]] to complete the quest.
