## Description

*I've decided to take on another task for the Royal family on behalf of Count Jooliin in the Lesser Faydark. He's tasked me with taking a blessed seed into the Shadowed Grove, planting it and then collecting the plant that sprouts. This seed must be very valuable if Queen Amree was keeping this in her private vault. For her to give up this seed in such a fashion only tells me that the void influence is greatly troubling her.*

## Prerequisites

- Your faction with [[The City of Kelethin (Faction)|City of Kelethin]] must be Indifferent or better to obtain this quest.

## Steps

![Grows after planting seed...](images/A_Mystical_Revelation.jpg)

1. Take the [[A blessed seed of growth|seed of growth]] to the Shadowed Grove and plant it there. There are two locations (right or left of the entrance). Need to stand still a moment on the rocky surface at the entrance before the stubs that show where to plant appear. ***Note:** In graphics settings from High Performance and better, you should see a swirling green circle that shoots mystical runes into the air in the two spots!*
   - Right click the stub to plant the seed
     - Location 1: {{waypoint 390, -35, 350}}
     - Location 2: {{waypoint 315, -35, 400}}
1. Right click the stub to retrieve the [[A tainted sprout|tainted sprout]].
1. Return the tainted sprout to Count Jooliin

## Rewards

- At least 9g 27s 1c
- +200 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
