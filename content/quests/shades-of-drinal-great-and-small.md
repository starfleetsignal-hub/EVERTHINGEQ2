## Steps

1. Meet with [[Drinal's Steward]] at the Ashen Tower at {{waypoint -307.03, -7.13, 840.16}}
1. Speak with Zeeil {{waypoint -331, -7, 840}} and Nrik {{waypoint -312, -7, 833}}
   - Speak with Zeeil and complete the following quests
   1. [[Last of Our Kind]] (93)
   1. [[War Machines]] (93)
   1. [[Fallen Idol]] (93)
   1. [[Fearful Lands]] (93)
   1. [[Power to the Tower]] (93) rewards range
   - Help Nrik
   1. Complete the quest: [[Circumstantial Evidence]] (92) (this will not be available until [[Last of Our Kind]] is started)
   1. Speak to [[Kuule Vilehammer]] near the Tower of Umber and complete the following quests:
      1. [[Mistaken Identity]] (93)
      1. [[Guilty as Charged]] (94)
1. Return to [[Drinal's Steward]] at the Ashen Tower {{waypoint -307.03, -7.13, 840.16}}
1. Locate eight pieces of Rallos Zek's essence within Oggok (glowing white pieces of armor).  Some known locations:

![A piece of Zek essence](images/Rallos_Zeks_essence.jpg)

   - {{waypoint -350, 3, 301}}
   - {{waypoint -302, 7, 258}}
   - {{waypoint -381, -4, 448}}
   - {{waypoint -401, 0.34, 140}}
   - {{waypoint -424, 0.23, 299}}
   - {{waypoint -375, -3, 422}}
   - {{waypoint -387, -45, 541}}
   - {{waypoint -421, -4, 493}}
1. Click on the Helm of Rallos Zek at {{waypoint -346, 4, 164}} in Oggok
1. Defeat the Remnant of Zek at {{waypoint -383, 69, 60}} (on top of the tower)
1. Return to [[Drinal's Steward]] at the Ashen Tower {{waypoint -307.03, -7.13, 840.16}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
