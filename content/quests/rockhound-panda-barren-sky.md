## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable
- If you cannot fly you can take the cloud transport: for step 2 from Whisperwind Isle to Isle of Awakening then to Isle of Eaglewatch. For step 3 take cloud back to Isle of Awakening, then to Blackwind Isle. For step 4 take cloud back to Whisperwind Isle and from there go to Cloudmist Isle.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[The Barren Sky]]<gallery>

File:Isle of Eaglewatch rock.png|rock from Isle of Eaglewatch
File:Blackwind Isle rock.png|Blackwind Isle rock
File:Cloudmist Isle rock.png|Cloudmist Isle crystal
</gallery>

1. Collect 3 samples of "interesting rock" from Isle of Eaglewatch - go to {{waypoint -239, 306, 618}} and mine white flat rocks, multiple spawns along rock walls.
1. Collect 3 samples of rock from Blackwind Isle - go to {{waypoint -309, 115, 242}} and mine small rocks along rock walls. Near the closest rock wall (-473.37, 129.71, 316.82).
1. Collect 3 samples of rock from Cloudmist Isle - go to {{waypoint -587, 247, -188}} and mine crystals spawning along rock walls.
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]] in Sundered Frontier.
