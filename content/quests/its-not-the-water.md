## Steps

1. Slay 10 bats ([[A void shrieker|void shriekers]] and [[A void shriller|void shrillers]]) around [[Deputy Huckfar]]'s wagon {{waypoint -46, -2, -414}}
1. Return to [[Deputy Huckfar]]
1. Slay 10 more bats outside [[Deputy Huckfar]]'s wagon {{waypoint -46, -2, -414}}
1. Return to [[Deputy Huckfar]]
