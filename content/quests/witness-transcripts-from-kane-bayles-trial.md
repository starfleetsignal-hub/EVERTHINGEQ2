## Steps

Go to the Crypt of Betrayal and:

1. Kill 7 [[A restless corpse|restless corpse]]s - halls and rooms from [[Cryptkeeper Maglus]] to [[Vegalys Keldrane]] {{waypoint 126, 7, -102}} and onwards towards [[Vargon]].
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 8 restless corpses.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 8 [[A diseased corpse|diseased corpse]]s - same as restless corpses.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 [[A tortured essence (Crypt of Betrayal)|tortured essences]] - rooms between [[Vargon]] and [[Adun]] {{waypoint 152, 7, -6}}.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 9 [[A tortured essence|tortured essences]].
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 7 [[A crypt tempest|crypt tempest]]s - near Bloodsaber entrance {{waypoint 76, 0, -23}}; shares spawn points with beetles.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 7 crypt tempests.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 [[An agonized essence|agonized essence]]s - lower crypt {{waypoint -10, 0, -95}}.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 15 [[An agonized essence|agonized essences]].
1. Read the book.
