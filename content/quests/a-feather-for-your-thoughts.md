## Steps

1. Kill 7-9 vultaks. Any vultaks will do, including the lower level ones in [[Tenebrous Tangle]].
1. Jump into [[Strifewind Pond]]. {{waypoint 174, -20, -494}}
1. Go to [[The Whisperwind Isle (POI)|The Whisperwind Isle]] and kill 5 [[A Whisperwind mystic|Whisperwind mystics]] (level 60-64) to get 5 wings.
   - You will lose 100 Windgazer faction per kill. The mystics and aviaks are part of the same faction. If your faction isn't high enough, you may become "kill-on-sight" to the Windgazer aviaks by doing this step.
