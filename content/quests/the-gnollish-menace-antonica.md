## Notes

- For a discussion on quest availability please visit the talk page.
- On [[PvE]] servers, this is a guide or GM-related event. [[PvP]] and Exchange servers may have this quest as a constant fixture. The quest introductory information reads as follows:
  - *The gnolls have killed many innocent travelers in Norrath; the depths of [[Blackburrow]] are filled with their smouldering skeletons. My allies and I will help set the spirits of the dead to rest, and make Norrath safer for travelers in the future.*

## Steps

1. Kill 50 gnolls within [[Blackburrow]].

## Rewards

Select one of the following:

- [[A tortured elf skeleton]]
- [[A tortured halfling skeleton]]
- [[A tortured ogre skeleton]]
