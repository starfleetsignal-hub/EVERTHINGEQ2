## Steps

1. Obtain 8 Far Wanderer Cargo Crates. These are underwater near [[Warship Dock]]. {{waypoint 492, -51, 361}} and have a 20 minute respawn.
1. Return to [[Mess Sergeant Akseltz]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 17s 67c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Apostle's Wristguard of Resolve]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Cuffs of Focus]] (All Mages)
  - [[Hanshi's Wrist Wraps of the Tiger]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Bracers of the Stalwart]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Oracle's Chain Cuffs of Acumen]] (Shamans)
  - [[Protector's Bands of the Winds]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Raider's Vambraces of Pure Darkness]] (All Scouts)

## Notes

- This quest would appear to scale downward with level (confirmed as having been done as a level 14 quest by a level 14 character)
- The crates that have been picked up eventually reappear - doing the quest in two separate runs allows for avoiding to have to pick up the crates that are dangerously close to hostile creatures.
- Be prepared to deal with almost constant fights with the Kelp Horrors which swarm.
