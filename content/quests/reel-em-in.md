## Notes

- *Timed quest: Shattered Seas solo daily, 6 minutes.*

## Steps

1. Catch ? fish from the nearby pool.
   - Move your cursor around over the water while spamming the "F" key to catch fish.
   - You may get different results each time, such as attacks by aggro crabs, a knockback, fish slips the hook, etc.
1. Run the fish to the fish baskets in the pygmy village at {{waypoint -651, 7, 267}}

## Rewards

- At least 1p 67g 40s 5800 status
- 3 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
- Achievement [[Friendly Fishmonger]] upon first completion
