## Steps

1. Enter [[Kurn's Tower: Breaching the Void]] from the ropes at {{waypoint -1841, -376, -1694}} in [[Fens of Nathsar]]. Once inside the instance, climb the ladder and enter [[The Expanse of Bone]]. {{waypoint -58, -5, -74}}
1. Defeat [[Jennre Warsinger]] and use [[The Great Lift]] {{waypoint 84, 1, 0}}. Once at the top, step onto the pink teleportation pad.
1. While standing on the pink disk, right-click the disc controller and select the option to go up. Step off the disc once it has stopped and you will enter the [[Atrium of Silence]]. {{waypoint 10, 289, -9}}
1. Go through the west doorway and enter [[The Aurora Chamber]]. {{waypoint 57, 289, 0}}
1. Return to [[Chief Llump the Wise]] in [[Fens of Nathsar]].
