## Steps

1. Speak to [[Taliseda]] and [[Salindis]] at {{waypoint 206, 594, -498}}.
1. Travel to {{waypoint 147, 205, -581}} where you will spot a suspicious Allyrian, who changes into [[A savage stoneripper]] as you approach. Kill the stoneripper when it attacks.
   - *NOT a group update. Those not updated can move away and back to spawn their own stoneripper for an update.* ![](images/A_suspicious_Allyrian.jpg)
1. Return to {{waypoint 206, 593, -499}} to find [[Taliseda]] missing.
1. Speak to [[Karylindi]] at {{waypoint 208, 593, -520}}.
