## Steps

![](images/Gendri_Stalk.jpg)

1. Gather tar for a trap - go to the [[Tar Pits of Inevitability]] at {{waypoint -892, 30, 296}}, Mouse over the tar pit and you will get a hand icon to harvest the tar. There is no need to kill anything to retrieve the tar as originally stated.
1. Gather deino bones - left-click the bones sticking out of the tar pits
1. Return to Guldri at the docks
1. Speak to [[Yurwri]] at {{waypoint -712, 14, 259}}
1. Use the [[Deinodon Tar Trap]] on a rampaging deino.  Once you do it will fall over and Yurwri will examine it.
1. Take Yurwri to [[Finze]] {{waypoint -689, 27, 151}}
1. Gather 5 Gendri Stalks from the [[Bawgava Tidepools]] - they're easy to spot, look for tall green reeds growing in the pools of water.
1. Return to Finze

## Rewards

- At least 5p 50g
- [[Pygmy Bone Rack]]
