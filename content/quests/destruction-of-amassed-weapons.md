## Steps

*With the components I provided Hooatio, he was able to make enough brittle arcanum concoction to sabotage any Zakir Rish weapons I am likely to find near the Zakir Rish forces themselves, on Auric Palisades.*

1. Slay and destroy the following on [[Auric Palisades]]
   - Note: Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint -682.82, 144.92, -5.32}} to [[Auric Palisades]]
   - Slay 8 Maedjinn Aggressors
   - ![Zakir Rish Weapons](images/Zakir_Rish_Weapons.jpg)Destroy 10 **Zakir Rish Weapons** in the area. (group-wide update)
1. Return to [[Hooatio]] at {{waypoint -859.88, 189.67, 372.18}}
   - Note: Take to the Skies cloud at {{waypoint -705.44, 145.19, -364.46}} to [[Isles of Wisdom]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +2,000 faction with **Isles of Wisdom**
