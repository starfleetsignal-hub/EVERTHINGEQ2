## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- You must have +20,000 faction with the Court of the Blades to unlock this quest. Once +50,000 faction has been achieved, however, this quest will no longer be available.

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Twin Tears]] {{waypoint -452, -75, -993}} in [[The Sinking Sands|the Sinking Sands]] and kill:
   - any [[The Alliz Raef Ew (Faction)|Alliz Raef Ew lizardmen]] for 10 updates.
   - [[An Alliz Raef Ew seer|Alliz Raef Ew seers]] for 2 rare updates.
   - any [[The Anaz Mal Gnolls (Faction)|Anaz Mal gnolls]] for 10 updates.
   - [[An Anaz Mal scrapper|Anaz Mal scrappers]] for 2 rare updates.
1. Return to Dirar Muti.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 23s 29c
- +500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
