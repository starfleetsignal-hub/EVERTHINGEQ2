## Notes about starting the bag quest

Step close and wait for them to offer you the quest. You don't have to hail them - guides are a person, not an NPC, so they have to offer each player the quest manually. Please **be patient**, unsummon or **hide pets**, **get off your mount** and **step back** once you've gotten the quest. If you're in a group, everyone in the group will be offered the quest if they're in the same zone.

Note: To enter Antonica 1 you often must enter Antonica 2 and join a group with someone in Antonica 1. After the group is formed right click their name and select Antonica 1. Popular servers may have in excess of 200 people in zone during the event. If you stay to help people zone in please do it from the docks or across the bridge to make it easier on the player giving out the quest.
![Sample visual settings to aid in seeing the collectibles. Visible at 200m.](images/Screenshot_%2827%29.png)
**To aid in seeing the green shinies you must run at least BALANCED on the display.** To increase range farther under particle detail set Particle level of detail (Far) to "Very High". To reduce clutter set particle quality to minimum, max spell results to character to 0, and disable flora. This allows you to see them at 200+m as shown in the example on the right.

## Steps

1. I should search for Inspirations in Antonica.![Antonica Shiny Area](images/Map_antonica_Shinny_path.jpg)
   - Finish and hand in the [[Inspirations of Self (Collection)]] collection.
   - *Antonica: Start at the docks. Nothing here will attack you at level 20+. Be careful with the gnolls on the south portion, bears and wolfs on the east portion.*
1. I should look for Evocations in the Commonlands.![Commonlands Shiny Area](images/Map_commonlands_shinny_2.jpg)
   - Finish and hand in the [[Evocations of Grasp (Collection)]] collection.
   - *Commonlands: Start at the docks. The orc skeletons have a few mobs among them that will still be aggro at level 20, but they're easily avoidable. Don't get too close to Crossroads if you're good-aligned - the guards are level 30 KoS Heroics. Watch the spiders and scorpions on the southern area.*
1. I should look for Ordinations in Thundering Steppes.![Thundering Steppes Shiny Area](images/Map_steppes_shinny_path.jpg)
   - Finish and hand in the [[Ordinations of Mind (Collection)]] collection.
   - *Thundering Steppes: Easiest to get to via the docks, but the path from the Antonica gates is safe. Don't take the Spire. Be swift, be careful. The whole riverbed is full of aggro heroic skeletons. Also note that some of the crabs near the beach are aggro. The area in the south is full of heroic skeletons and giants. Lower level players need an escort to complete Thundering Stepps effectively.*
1. I discovered Conjurations in the Feerrott.![Feerrott Shiny Area](images/Map_feerrott_shiny_path.jpg)
   - Finish and hand in the [[Conjurations of Being (Collection)]] collection.
   - *Feerrott: Start at the docks or the Spire, but not the Druid Ring. This one is pretty easy, stick to the area north of the road and avoid the Bitter Blooms.*
1. I should search for Ministrations in Lavastorm.![Lavastorm Shiny Area](images/Map_lavastorm_shinny_path2.jpg)
   - Finish and hand in the [[Ministrations of Breath (Collection)]] collection.
   - *Lavastorm: Start at the docks and go straight to the area past Fennin's Pass by jumping up to the rocks at the red cross on the map and running counter-clockwise until you can jump down in front of the bridge (green spot). You will have to be very careful, since there's roaming fire imps and magma worms, as well as static groups of aggro goblins. The roaming goblin guards and the black lizards aren't aggro.*
1. I should search for Noctivagances in Everfrost.![Everfrost Shiny Area](images/Everfrost_Assemblage_of_Magics.png)
   - Finish and hand in the [[Noctivagance of Pain (Collection)]] collection
   - *Everfrost: Start anywhere die and re-spawn is The Icy Fjord @ the start of the ravine. Just run down the ravine, avoiding the aggro wolves at the end. The Jagged Plains have a mix of aggro and friendly mobs, so tread carefully. There's quite a few spawn points in the ravine, so you can also search them just there.*
1. Finish the collection [[Assemblage of Magics]].
1. Speak to Historian Gretta Helsiph in Antonica to finish the quest. {{waypoint 126, -16, -187}}
