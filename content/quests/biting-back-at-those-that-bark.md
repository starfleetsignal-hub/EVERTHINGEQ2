## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Kill 4 [[A Grikbar warrior|Grikbar warriors]]
   - Kill 4 [[A Grikbar battler|Grikbar battlers]]
   - Kill 4 [[A Grikbar scamp|Grikbar scamps]]
1. Return to [[Waynil Garwater]]

## Rewards

- At least 53s 57c
- [[Pulsing Gem of Striking]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Defender's Strap of the Geyser]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Healer's Strap of the Geyser]] (All Priest)
  - [[Medium's Strap of the Geyser]] (All Mages)
  - [[Stalker's Strap of the Geyser]] (All Scouts)
