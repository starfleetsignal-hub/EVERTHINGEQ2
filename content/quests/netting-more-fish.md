## Description

The Hua Mein Craftsman has requested assistance in fishing for the Hua Mein provision stores.

## Steps

1. Catch 25 fresh octopus (giant octopus meat)
1. Catch 25 seahorse roe
1. Return to [[Xi Lan]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,000 faction with **Hua Mein**
- +2,500 faction with **Hua Mein Craftsmen**

## Notes

- This quest is one of the Hua Mein Daily Supply Tasks and is available on Thursdays.
- If you already have the required amount of giant octopus meat and seahorse roe in your inventory, this quest will update to complete as soon as you receive it. Just speak with [[Xi Lan]] to finish it.
