## Steps

1. Collect the following
   - Dig in piles of snow to search for draconic items x 10
     - *click snow piles in Icespine area around {{waypoint 429, 103, -477}}  pick up Draconic Claw or Draconic Potion Vessel (a lot of piles yield nothing, keep trying)*
   - Obtain draconic phantasm residue x 6 - from [[A frostbitten chaosbone]]
1. Return to [[Nythruth]] at {{waypoint -480, 123, -325}} [[Hoarfang Ridge]] in [[Western Wastes]]

## Reward

- [[Draconic Potion Vessel]] house item
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- 50 x [[Scarred Dinar]]
- 5 x [[Velium Drachma]]
