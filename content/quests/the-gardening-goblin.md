## Starting the Quest

- Starts in your own house where you placed [[An Obulus Frontier Seedling]]

## Steps

1. Converse with the Gardening Goblin about how the plant is sparkling.
1. Click on the plant to receive, [[A strange harvest]].
1. Converse with the goblin about the plant producing a rare harvest when it sparkles.
1. Pick up the plant from your house to return it to your inventory.
1. Return to [[Growf]] in the Obulus Frontier and let him know about the plant's magical properties.
1. Complete the conversation with Growf.

Note: The Gardening Goblin is now standing beside Growf and is hireable as a Brigand Mercenary.
