This quest is repeatable.

## Description

"Grimply Sniffwistle, a tinkering gnome at Spire Shadow Outpost, is determined to see his clockwork bixie creation fly off and battle the threatening kobolds south of The Combine Spires. the only component he requires now is an energy source! The vinebloom seedpods within Tunare's Glade should provide the needed energy." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Gather 5 vinebloom seedpods in [[Tunare's Glade]] {{waypoint -198, 44, 467}}
   - [[An angered Fayfly]] may spawn when a pod is harvested. The Faeflies are aggro, but not needed to advance the quest
1. Return to Grimply

## Rewards

- At least 27s 29c
- [[Summon Mini-Cognatic Bixie Bopper|mini-cognatic bixie bopper]]
- [[A cognatic bixie bopper]] will fly up to the Steamfont kobold camp and start slaying kobolds.  Repeat this quest until there are 3 boppers  in the camp, and [[The Cogsmasher]] will spawn and destroy the boppers. The Cogsmasher can then be killed for the quest starter [[Cogsmasher's Wrench]]
