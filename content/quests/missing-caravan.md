## Description

*Lieutenant Masts is concerned about a caravan that is two days overdue and has asked me to search for any clues or indication of its whereabouts.*

## Notes

- You can complete this quest or [[Caravan Lost!]]. It is not possible to complete both.

## Steps

1. Find the caravan. {{waypoint 850, -125, -900}}
1. Return to [[Lieutenant Masts]].

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Swiftrider Mercenaries**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Disciple Woven Armband of Cunning]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Disciple Woven Armband of Faith]] (All Priests)
  - [[Disciple Woven Armband of Reason]] (All Mages)
  - [[Disciple Woven Armband of Resolve]] (All Fighters)
