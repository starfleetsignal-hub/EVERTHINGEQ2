## Description

"Shimmer Moonfair has been trying to navigate his way to his friend Glienna next to the Emerald Lake. However, he has been injured and pleads that you speak with her immediately so that she doesn't get the wrong idea about his tardiness." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Bring a message from Shimmer to his love interest [[Glienna Lillypad]] at [[Emerald Lake]] {{waypoint -712.49, 45.96, 319.07}}.

## Rewards

- At least 9s 94c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Shimmer Moonfair: "Oh, you there. Please help me."
- Player Character: "You look injured. what happened?"
- Shimmer Moonfair: "I am due at Emerald Lake to meet my friend Glienna, but these guardians attacked me. now I am injured and it will take some time for me to limp my way to her. It is important that you run ahead of me to inform her of my tardiness."
- Player Character: "Isn't it more important that I tend to your wounds?"
- Shimmer Moonfair: "No! She is waiting for me for too long and I must send word to her immediately. I do not want her to think I have forgotten her or do not care for her. Please, send her my message with great speed."
- Player Character: "It sounds like you really care for this girl. I will tell her."
- Shimmer Moonfair: "I thank you for your help. Please don't tell her that I am injured, it will only make her worry. She is just down the path south of here."
- Player Character: "Very well then."

Speak to Glienna Lillypad near Emerald Lake.

- Glienna Lillypad: "You are not Shimmer. He is so late. I wonder what could have happened to him."
- Player Character: "I bear a message from Shimmer. He is resting a ways down and will be here shortly. He asks that you do not worry."
- Glienna Lillypad: "Oh, that Shimmer. Resting like that when he is so close? How strange. But knowing that he has not forgotten about me brings me great joy. Thank you for delivering the message."
- Player Character: "I am just helping a stranger in need."

You have spoken to Glienna Lillypad near Emerald Lake.

You have spoken to Glienna Lillypad for Shimmer Moonfair.
