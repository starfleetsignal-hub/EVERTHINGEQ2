## Notes

- Public Quests spawn in zones all across Norrath.
- Public Quests show up as blue-tinted circles on your map, which disappear once all the foes for that PQ have been slain.
- You may need to chronomentor to an appropriate level so that the mobs con to you.
- There is a walkthrough for the mechanics of the Kunark Ascending PQs on the forums here: https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/a-guide-for-kunark-ascending-pqs.576939/
- The beginning of a walkthrough for the Planes of Prophecy PQs is here:  https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/anyone-be-super-nice-and-say-how-public-quests-work-in-new-expansion.582723/

## Difficulties

The public quests spawn in the following difficulties:

- Solo (no arrow up)
- Heroic
- Epic X2
- Epic X4

## Rewards

Completing a public quest rewards you with one of two types of coinage, which may be exchanged with certain merchants:

- [[Crusader's Medal]]
- [[Defender's Medal]]
- [[Planar Quintessence]]  (PoP PQs)
