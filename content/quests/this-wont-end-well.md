## Starting the Quest

Speak to one of the following NPCs:

- [[Skeer Deekat]] at {{waypoint 288.67, -20.88, -51.34}} a tree near the [[The Claymore Plaza]] in [[North Qeynos]]
- [[Skeer Deekat]] at {{waypoint 284, 106, 135}} opposite of the bank in [[Kelethin]]
- [[Skeer Deekat]] at {{waypoint -103, 147, -13}} near the Chronomage in [[New Halas]]
- [[Freid Deekat]] in [[West Freeport]] {{waypoint 183, -3, -80}} by the Freeport Reserve
- [[Freid Deekat]] in [[Neriak]] Near the Docks
- [[Freid Deekat]] in [[Gorowyn]] outside the Wards.

## Notes

- This quest scales from level 10 to highest level.
- Up to, but no more than THREE(3) players may enter Haunted Mansion and complete quest together.
- If you've done this quest in the past, you need to watch for the Toy Chest in the children's room for [[They won't miss it...]] Only one person can loot it, and if you miss it, it won't be available for another year.

## Introduction

A Gigglegibber Goblin called Skeer Deekat in the City has commissioned you to deliver him a special food item for his master. You aren't exactly sure what all this is about but your curiosity has gotten the best of you and you decided to go on the errand despite your better judgment.

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Somborn Cemetery]] in [[Loping Plains]] to meet someone about a meal.
1. As you enter the cemetery, [[Sarkon D'Ryil]] thinks you are the meal and bites you. You'll awake inside a Inn's bed in Somborn. You received a nearly lethal bite.
1. Find out what happened... Talk to [[Proctor Eiolie]] at the [[Haunted Mansion]] at {{waypoint -375, 15, 0}}.
1. Find a way to help Proctor Eiolie. Enter the Haunted Mansion through the door next to you.
1. Accept and complete the quest, [[There Will Be Ghosts]]. Watch for the Toy Chest in the Children's Room for [[They won't miss it...]]
1. Return to Proctor Eiolie
