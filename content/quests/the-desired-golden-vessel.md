## Steps

1. Click on Starfire Dust Storage at {{waypoint -441, 165, -807}}.
1. Click on Manafin Blood Vessel at {{waypoint -445, 165, -806}}.
1. Click on Starfire Crystal Storage at {{waypoint -450, 165, -805}}.
1. Right click on the [[Component filled Allyrian Flask]] in your inventory, choose examine and then Seal the flask tightly to receive [[Sealed component filled Allyrian Flask]]
1. Click on the Charge Device at {{waypoint -585, 131, -892}} to receive [[Unenchanted Divining Device]]. ![Charge Device](images/Charge_Device.jpg)
1. Speak to [[Ta-sche-ra-he]] to receive [[Pakiat Divining Device (The Desired Golden Vessel)|Pakiat Divining Device]]
1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (The Desired Golden Vessel)|Pakiat Divining Device]] at {{waypoint -743, -13, -1099}}
   - Get close to the glowing chalice effect and use the [[Pakiat Divining Device]]. ![A glowing chalice marks the divining spot](images/Glowing_chalice.png)
1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (The Desired Golden Vessel)|Pakiat Divining Device]] at {{waypoint -647, -13, -1142}}.
1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (The Desired Golden Vessel)|Pakiat Divining Device]] at {{waypoint -434, -15, -1145}}.
1. Use [[Pakiat Divining Device (The Desired Golden Vessel)|Pakiat Divining Device]] at {{waypoint -169, -20, -1127}}.
1. Click on the Golden Vessel at {{waypoint -109, -22, -1213}}. ![Golden Vessel](images/Golden_Vessel.jpg)
1. Return to [[Ta-sche-ra-he]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- -1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- +1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
