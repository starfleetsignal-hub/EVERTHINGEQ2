## Steps

1. Go to the [[Hall of Rites]] and inspect the chest at {{waypoint 10, 65, 1}}.
   - You can inspect the chest while standing on the ledge above.
1. Obtain 4 eye gems to open the chest; these are uncommon quest updates from the [[An eternal aegis|eternal aegis]] that surround the walls of the Hall of Rites and [[Banisher Preparatory]], west of Hall of Rites.
1. Once you have all 4 gems, return to the chest and retrieve scrolls.
1. Take the scrolls back to [[Xhorroz]] {{waypoint -129, 77, 204}} for your reward.

## Rewards

- Your choice of:
  - [[Blood-Drenched Splint of Xhorroz]]
  - [[Red Bands of Xhorroz]]
  - [[Red Bangle of Xhorroz]]
  - [[Soaked Wraps of Xhorroz]]
