## Steps

1. Find and click 3 sickened citizens:
   - [[Temple of War]] {{waypoint -111, -8, -129}}
   - [[The Jade Tiger's Den]] {{waypoint -84, -21, -70}}
   - [[Citizen's Vista]] {{waypoint -20, -19, -275}}
1. Speak to Sneel
1. Search for ratonga behaving strangely in South Freeport. This is a group of [[A crazed ratonga|crazed ratonga]] (3 double downs, grouped) {{waypoint -5, -8, 148}}, near the Tier 1 guild hall. Kill them. *(NOTE: Before I got the update to be able to open the door, I had to move away and then return to the door. The group respawned and I killed them again. Then I got the update and was able to open the door to the hidden room.)*
1. Click the door to enter the [[Hideout of the Roekillik]]{{waypoint -5, -8, 148}}
1. Kill 2 [[A roekillik infiltrator|roekillik infiltrators]] (one down, grouped)
1. Speak to Sneel in North Freeport
