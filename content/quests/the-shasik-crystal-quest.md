## Steps

1. Enter [[Nu'roga]] from the [[Mines of Nu'roga]]. {{waypoint -189, -74, -1965}}
1. Kill the two [[A Drogan soldier|Drogan soldier]]s guarding [[Witch Doctor Bzegone]]. {{waypoint 2, 15, -3}}
1. Kill the [[A Drogan horn blower|Drogan horn blower]]. {{waypoint -61, 6, 24}}
1. Go to the second floor, [[Chanting and Ritual Chamber]]. {{waypoint 5, 64, -11}}
1. Kill [[Gzaxr]] {{waypoint 21, 68, 0}} and [[Rbumk]] {{waypoint -19, 68, 0}}.
   - They each have a 100% damage preventing shield on them. To dispel it, you must have [[Bilthor]] breathe his fire on them. You will know when it was done correctly when you receive a green status message saying, "Bilthor has dispelled [Gzaxr or Rbumk]'s defenses".
1. Go to the third floor, [[Arcane Practice and Study]]. {{waypoint 40, 102, 19}}
1. Kill [[Crusader of Rok Nilok]]. To spawn him, attack the [[A crazed Drogan ritualist|crazed Drogan ritualists]] and Witch Doctor Bzegone will summon him after a few moments. {{waypoint -1, 101, 0}} **NOTE:** Or you kill him really fast (1 second after he spawns) or he will turn in gray colour. You will have to wait until he finished the fight so you can kill him. If you kill before he finihes the fight with Witch Doctor Bzegone, then it won´t update.
1. Go to the fourth floor ([[Blood Altar]]), kill [[Tuz'Rak]], and loot the [[The Shasik Crystal (Quest Starter)|Shasik Crystal]] from his chest. {{waypoint 0, 176, 37}}
1. Return to Fauske Kadjers.

## Rewards

- One of the following:
  - [[Band of the Greenmist Crusader]]
  - [[Drogan Stone Loop]]
  - [[Nurogan Ring of Rites]]
  - [[Shasik Crystal Ring]]
