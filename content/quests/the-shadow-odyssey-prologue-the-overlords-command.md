## Note

This is the evil-aligned starting quest of this Signature.  If you're good-aligned, please look on [[The Shadow Odyssey, Prologue: A Missive from the Queen]]

Must be lvl 75 to start

## Steps

1. Speak with Vimminy Crix {{waypoint -212, -56, 39}} who tells you to locate the Overlord.
1. Head to [[Overlord's Plaza]] and then enter the portal to the Citadel {{waypoint 10, -20, 10}} in the center of [[The City of Freeport]] to speak with Overlord D'Lere. You may enter the "The Overlord's Office" instance solo or grouped with another person on the same step.
1. Find [[Lucan D'Lere]] and hail him.
1. Finish the conversation to finish the quest (and get the next).

## Rewards

- At least 1p 15g 15000 status
