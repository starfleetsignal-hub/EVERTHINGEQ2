## Description

Within the Ossuary of Malevolence awaits an enemy the Far Seas Trading Company cannot hope to vanquish alone. They know, however, that I walk among heroes, and that no task, no matter the danger, is too great for us to complete. Within the Ossuary of Malevolence lies our enemy, and therein they will fall.

## Steps

1. Zone into [[Ossuary of Malevolence (Contested)]]
1. Kill 10 Named mobs in the zone.
