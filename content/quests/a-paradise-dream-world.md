## Steps

1. Read the [[Bogstrutter Isle Guide]] given to you by [[Hoggle Bogstrutter]] for completing the quest, [[Scattered Notes]]
1. Visit the location noted as Bogstrutter Cove {{waypoint 503, 0, 262}}
   - This is the [[Warship Dock]] Note:  I received the update BETWEEN the large censors(torches), heading north out of the Docks. -Reigning, Kithicor
1. Visit the location noted as Greenspire Wood Inn {{waypoint 639, -18, 30}}
   - This is the area just north of the [[Orc Encampment]] on the western edge of the [[Splintered Plain]]
1. Visit the location noted as Rustic Pond Resort {{waypoint 273, -21, -350}}
   - This is the [[Three Toe's Watering Hole]] east of [[Three Toes Valley]]
1. Visit the location noted as Rodcet Steam Hall {{waypoint 723, -17, -298}}
   - This is the cave in [[Three Toes Valley]] to the far west of [[Three Toe's Watering Hole]] known as [[Shadowed Shrine]]
1. Visit the location noted as the Celestial Abyss {{waypoint 341, -113, -113}}
   - This is [[The Deathfist Quarry]] in [[Blood Ore Valley]], you must travel down the elevator to get update
1. Visit the location noted as Windspirit Round {{waypoint 207, 14, 108}}
   - This is the [[Grove of Stones]], in the [[Valley of Sacrifice]] between [[Splintered Plain]] and [[Spirit Valley]]
1. Visit the location noted as Spiralball Arena {{waypoint -57, -3, -73}}
   - This is [[Spirit Lake]], northeast of [[Spirit Valley]]
1. Visit the location noted as Pirate Bay {{waypoint 146, -44, 396}}
   - This is [[Siren's Cove]], a small island near the beach south of the [[Refuge]]
1. Visit the location noted as Titan's Tower {{waypoint -23, 75, -367}}
   - This is [[Deathfist Lookout]] on top of the corkscrew looking spire
1. Visit the location noted as Castle Bogstrutter {{waypoint -174, -23, -612}}
   - This is just past the [[Bridge of Zek]] at the head of [[Citadel Road]] leading toward [[The Deathfist Citadel]]
1. Return to Hoggle Bogstrutter

## Rewards

- At least 2g 69s 57c
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Deathfist Bone Spike]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Earhoop of the Deathfist Orcs]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Earring of Deathfist Rank]] (All Priests)
  - [[Faceted Deathfist Emblem]] (All Mages)
