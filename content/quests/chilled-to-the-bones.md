## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..

## Steps

1. Slay [[A fractured shardbone|fractured shardbones]] until you find a *flawless rune bone* (uncommon update).
   - Fractured shardbones are found in greatest numbers around the [[Monolith of Woe]] {{waypoint 1152, 208, -1612}}, but can also be found in the northwest at {{waypoint 1341.58, 135.26, -2579.73}} or {{waypoint 1113, 188, -1736}}.
1. Return to [[Beth Littlefort]].

## Reward

- At least 15g
- +400 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- [[An Order of Rime barrel|an Order of Rime barrel]] (house item)
