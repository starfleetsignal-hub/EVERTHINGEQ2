## Steps

1. Kill 10-15 gorgs on the [[Temple Grounds]] in the [[Tenebrous Tangle]].
   - The quest may specify [[A twilight gorg|twilight gorgs]], or 'any gorg on Temple Grounds'.  If the latter, [[A territorial gorg|territorial gorgs]], [[A hulking gorg|hulking gorgs]], [[A tempermental gorg]] and [[Grubgullet]] all update.

## Rewards

- 8760 status @ lvl 61
- +500 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
- 1 [[City Token]]
