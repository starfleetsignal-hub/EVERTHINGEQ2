## Notes

***Must be at least lvl 50 crafter to start the questline***

***To finish the entire quest line you need:***

- 6 indium cluster
- 3 severed sandalwood
- 6 stonehide leather pelt
- 12 succulent roots
- 9 lambent coal
- 9 lambent filament
- 9 lambent sandpaper
- *OPTIONAL* [[Portable Woodworking Kit]],[[Portable Sewing Table]] and [[Portable Work Bench]] for fast crafting. Available in broker or crafted by a Tinkerer.

## Steps

![Likely Plant sample in Lesser Faydark](images/LikelyPlantsampleLF.png)

1. Harvest:
   - 6 [[Waxy Leaves]] from a **Likely Plant Sample** in [[The Lesser Faydark]] {{waypoint -632.68, -52.83, 101.76}} near the gate to [[Greater Faydark]].
   - 12 [[Succulent root|succulent roots]]
     - *This step will autoupdate if you have the roots in your bags.*
1. Speak to [[Ewart Nefird]] back in [[Sinking Sands]] {{waypoint -522.77, -86.31, -1053.10}}.

## Rewards

- At least 3g 7s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
- [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
