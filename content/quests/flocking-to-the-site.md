## Steps

1. Defeat 10 mobs in a certain zone (based on character level):
   - T1 - [[A scourge bat|Scourge bats]] in the [[Graveyard]] OR [[A glowmyst beetle|glowmyst beetles]] in [[Oakmyst Forest]]
   - T2 - [[A Klicnik warrior|Klicnik warrior]]s in [[Antonica]] OR [[A moon wisp|moon wisps]] in [[The Commonlands]]
   - T3 - [[A Nek wasp|Nek wasp]]s in [[Nektulos Forest]] OR sirens in [[The Thundering Steppes]]
   - T4 - [[A Seamist warden|Seamist warden]]s or [[A Seamist maiden|maidens]] in [[Enchanted Lands]]
   - T5 - [[A fire imp|Fire imps]] in [[Lavastorm]] OR [[An ebon drake|ebon drakes]] in [[Steamfont Mountains]]
   - T6 - [[A golden sunfeather falcon|Golden sunfeather falcons]]s in the [[The Pillars of Flame]]
   - T7 - Blacktalon aviaks in [[The Barren Sky]] OR [[A ravenous blood gorger|ravenous blood gorgers]] in [[Loping Plains]]
   - T8 - [[A trained war sokokar|Trained war sokokar]]s in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
1. Return to Researcher Aillena.
