Lasslyn was convinced by my assistance at the Splitiron Mines and the ore that I provided her, that I may be the perfect person to obtain some tok oil she needs for edge quenching blades.

## Steps

1. Enter the city of [[Maldura]].
1. Speak to [[Alaric Clangzback]] in the [[Stone Mercantile District]] {{waypoint -187.19, -3.26, 116.58}} and complete his quest, [[Ooze Cruise]].
1. [[Alaric Clangzback|Alaric]] will now allow you to purchase the [[Tok Oil]] for 48g
1. Return to [[Lasslyn]].
1. Give [[Defenders' Renewed Weaponry]] to 6 [[A Maldura defender|Maldura defenders]] on the walls around [[Outer Maldura]] {{waypoint 461.51, 135.24, 223.50}}.
1. Speak to [[Tyshgwyn the Fury]] {{waypoint 451.52, 123.34, 250.14}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 41g 75s 12854 status
- +10,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +10,000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Defenders' Renewed Throwing Dagger]]
  - [[Defenders' Renewed Wand]]
  - [[Defenders' Renewed Throwing Hammer]]
  - [[Defenders' Renewed Bow]]
