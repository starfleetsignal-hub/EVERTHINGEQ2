## Steps

1. Visit the following locations in [[Moors of Ykesha]] in order:
   1. [[Dropship Landing Zone]] {{waypoint 1750, 450, 793}} Simply zone in via bell, spires, or druid ring
   1. [[Firmroot Moot]] {{waypoint 133, -86, -525}} Take the balloon ride to Firmroot Moot and walk north a bit
   1. [[Grobb]] {{waypoint -222, -82, -907}} Move through the cave to the northeast to get to Grobb
   1. [[Tupta]] {{waypoint -105, -45, 670}} Go back to the balloon ride in Firmroot Moot and take the balloon to Tupta, then walk east a little bit along the large root
   1. [[The Shadowed Cleft]] {{waypoint -1270, -991, -26}} Take the balloon ride to The Shadowed Cleft. The update happens when you land
   1. [[Brokenskull Rock]] {{waypoint -1807, -1094, 2773}} Find the balloon ride nearby and take it to Brokenskull Rock, then fly or swim south a bit for the update
1. Return to [[Yun Zi (Merchant)|Yun Zi]] on [[Spire Island]] of [[The Sundered Frontier]] at {{waypoint 2297, -199, 2506}}
