Tana thanks me for my help and continues to look for her bugs. In the meantime, she has suggested I find her companion, Uko, and offer my help to him. Uko can be found by following the path west and going north at the fork.

## Steps

1. Take the Stablemaster to Lower Grove.
1. Speak with [[Uko]], at {{waypoint -99, -55, -195}} at the Lower Grove Station.

## Rewards

- Experience only
