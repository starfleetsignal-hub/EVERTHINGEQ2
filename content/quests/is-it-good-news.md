## Notes

- Previously, players had to have [[A young sokokar (Item)|a young sokokar]] in their inventory to use the [[Sokokar|Sokokar Posts]] in Kunark, however this was changed in the global travel revamp. Now, completion of this quest opens up in-zone travel within Kunark, and the young sokokar is an appearance pet. Unlocking the posts by discovering them is no longer required.
- If you lose or destroy the young sokokar, talk to [[Stewlin Vincent]] to get another one.

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Stewlin Vincent]] in [[Teren's Grasp]]. {{waypoint 1976.76, 480.02, -882.66}}
1. Speak with Stewlin Vincent again to be ported to [[Kunzar's Edge]]. Talk to [[Hroar Kalacious]] when you arrive.

## Rewards

- Tradeskill experience.
- About 11g 67s 45c
- [[A young sokokar (Item)|A young sokokar]] (if you previously received one from the adventure [[Sokokar Timeline]], you will not receive another).
- +800 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
