You must be a Half-Elf of Evil alignment and at least level 20 to receive this quest.

## Steps

1. Zone into [[Beggar's Court: Sweat-Stained Cobbles]] and do the following
   - Kill 8 [[A Militia guard (Beggar's Court)|Militia guards]] loyal to the late Captain Lusarius Rommuls.
     - These are the non-interactable Militia guards with the subtitle <Rommuls Loyalist> under their name.  Left click them and they become aggro and attack.
   - Place contraband provisions inside the barrels - the barrels look like normal barrels with a sparkly silver effect.  Possible locations include...
     - {{waypoint 62, 5, -72}}
     - {{waypoint 78, 5, -14}}
     - {{waypoint 87, 5, -8}}
     - {{waypoint 80, 5, -31}}
     - {{waypoint 39, 6, -24}}
     - {{waypoint 66, 5, -33}}
1. Return to Thavian Destrius

## Rewards

- At least 1p 20g @ lvl 95
