## Steps

1. Kill 7 [[An Allu'thoa watcher|Allu'thoa watchers]]
1. Kill [[An Allu'thoa dispatcher|the Allu'thoa dispatcher]]
1. He runs from you.  Chase him north to {{waypoint -856, 174, -95}}
1. Kill the 3 Allu'thoa dispatchers (98vv)
1. Return to Zedj

## Rewards

- At least 1p 29g 60s
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Spinnaker Chain Vambraces]] (Chain Armor)
  - [[Spinnaker Leather Armguards]] (Leather Armor)
  - [[Spinnaker Bracers]] (Cloth Armor)
  - [[Spinnaker Plate Gussets]] (Plate Armor)
