## Steps

1. Kill eight uthork attackers. (Located on the shore across from Kippy).
1. Repair three othmir Prexus shrines. (Broken shells and candles on beach around Kippy).
1. Return to Kippy.

## Rewards

- One of the Following:
  - [[Behemoth Clamshell]]
  - [[Behemoth Clamshell Broken]]
  - [[Behemoth Clamshell Shattered]]
