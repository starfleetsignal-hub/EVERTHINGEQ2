## Steps

1. Zone into [[Longshadow Alley]].  There is a special instance for this quest.
1. You need to kill Thexians and click on idols, all in the turquoise area highlighted on your map.  Idols may not be in the places indicated when searching the map, I found mine by checking each idol indicated map location.
   - If you can't find enough idols, go back again later.  The zone will reset and you can get the rest that you need.
1. Return to Belimee Viperwing.

## Rewards

- +500 faction with **The Dismal Rage**
