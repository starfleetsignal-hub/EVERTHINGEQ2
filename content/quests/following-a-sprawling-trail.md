## Steps

1. Speak with [[Ekatos]] at {{waypoint 194, 114, 216}}
   1. He is hard to find. If you go to the zone in for the Coral Roost {{waypoint 250, 82, 321}} then {{waypoint 273, 95, 128}} go up the ramp. He is on the platform just above.
   1. **Note:**  I found him by going to  {{waypoint 250, 82, 321}} , then flying straight up until I could see the platform in front of me outlined in red vines. Fly North and he is beside the crates on the ground.
1. Search [[Detrius Pantheon]] for the delivery at {{waypoint 321, 104, 636}}
1. Examine
   - the rider's corpse
   - the ray's corpse
1. Hunt 8 altarbound sacrificers and altarclaw subjugators to recover items.
   - Not guaranteed updates, uncommon
1. Return to [[Ekatos]].
1. Return to [[Nanru]].

## Rewards

- 2x [[Wide Filled Darou Pot]]
- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
- One of the following:
  - [[Gerion Symbol of Force]]
  - [[Doomview Wand of Descent]]
  - [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
