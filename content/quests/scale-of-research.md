1. Talk to [[Sethus]] in [[Teren's Grasp]] in [[Kylong Plains]] {{waypoint 2241, 524, -976}}
1. In [[The Sundered Frontier]], go to left door in [[The Hole]] {{waypoint 1851, -371, 3449}} (same door as Demitriks Bastion) and enter [[The Duality's Vigilant Sanctum]]
1. Speak to [[The Duality]] {{waypoint 537, 130, -84}}
1. ![Notes on the Ssraeshza era](images/Notes_on_the_Ssraeshza_era.jpg)Gather 5 notes around his sanctum (trackable)
1. Speak to Duality again
