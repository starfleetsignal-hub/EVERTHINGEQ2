## Steps

1. Follow the trail of feathers (pink glowies).
1. Examine the final feather on the trail. {{waypoint 245, 186, 334}}
1. Defeat the ambush (2 [[A Haoaeran assaulter|Haoaeran assaulters]] and 2 [[A Kragploom ambusher|Kragploom ambushers]].) Four linked, 21vv-22vv.
   - If you die you'll have to re-obtain the quest from Bredik.
1. Return to Bredik.

## Rewards

- At least 12s 2c
