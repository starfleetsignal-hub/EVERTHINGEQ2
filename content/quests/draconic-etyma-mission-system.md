## Overview

[[Draconic Etyma]] are part of a new reward system introduced with the launch of [[Tears of Veeshan]] (ToV). They are given as rewards to various missions.  They are also awarded for completing Dungeon Finder instances, in this case all of the ToV requirements are bypassed, you can start earning them from level 20 (when the Dungeon Finder first becomes available).

## Requirement

- Level 95 is needed to get a *Normal mission*, a *Daily mission* or a *Weekly mission*
- The 3 mission types exist in solo and heroic versions. They work like independent mission systems.
- You can only have 1 *Weekly mission*.
- You can only have 1 *Daily mission*.
- You can only have 1 *Normal mission* for a given zone.
- You can have a *normal mission* and a *daily mission* for the same zone.
- It is possible to share missions if the above listed requirements are fulfilled and the mission is available from the quest giver at that time.
- [[Tears of Veeshan: Falling Tears]] must be finished to get the Mission [[Champions from Beyond]]
