## Steps

Deyrina Alderwatcher has asked me to search Sanctum Arcanum for some more ancient druidic potions and components.

- Gather 10 Thifling brambleknot bags
- Gather 10 Elddar arcane phials
- Slay 12 corrupted sylvans for wilted weed roots
- Slay 12 lumberhead guardians or living briarroots for burls of wilting

## Rewards

At least 2p 33g 88s 74c

- [[Imperial Hero's Mint]] x2
