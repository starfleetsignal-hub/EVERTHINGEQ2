## Steps

*Blortch is adamant there are still too many undead orcs ravaging Vul Valley and Sullen Sanctuary in the Spiral of Vul. He refuses to leave until their numbers are reduced and he has enough sustenance for all of his kin to make their trip back to Clan Mucktongue.*
This quest is repeatable.

1. Repeatable quest - must be done twice
1. Destroy 9 eldritch spellstone staffs **or** gather 9 squat spiketus for water
1. Slay 14 Vuldor skeletal orcs **or** Obtain 14 Stonebrunt centipede meat  - click on rocks for a chance at centipede - note it is a group update but if grouped the centipedes do not seem to spawn
1. Return to [[Blortch]]

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- One of following:
  - [[Stonebrunt Rock]] (House item)
  - [[Squat Spiketus]] (House item)
