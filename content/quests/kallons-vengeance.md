## Notes

- This is an introductory quest that will show you the nearby areas of the Sinking Sands and where you will find more quests.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Jaza]] in the [[Croc Hunter Camp]]. {{waypoint -1340, -227, -600}}
1. Go to the bottom of [[Sandslide Passage]]. Speak to Jaza who will have spawned next to the nearest tree. {{waypoint -1140, -212, -235}}
   - If you cannot see him there, this is just a graphical bug. Try looking at the tree from various angles and distances.
1. Kill [[Fasul]] and his cronies who will spawn after a few moments.
1. Speak to Jaza again.
1. Return to Kallon for your reward.

## Reward

- At least 5g 48s 39c
- [[Shimmering Gem of Prowess]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Barbaric Charm of Alteration]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Unspeakable Orb of Diamonds]]
  - [[Villainous Slayers' Barb of Terror]]
  - [[Viper Headed Totem]]
