## Steps

1. Take the cloud near [[Enge]] {{waypoint -463.05, -70.97, -439.15}} to [[Splendor Sky Aerie]].
   - *If required [[Tishan's Lockbox (Splendor Sky Aerie)|Tishan's Lockbox <Sky Explorer's Gear>]] {{waypoint -326.66, 114.23, 567.35}} is available for gear upgrades.*
1. Speak to [[Whoella]] {{waypoint -326.17, 113.77, 569.88}} and receive the [[In Need of Reeds]] quest.
1. Speak to [[Coursequill]] at {{waypoint -344.28, 118.00, 601.86}} and receive the [[Ethereal Hook]].
1. Use the [[Ethereal Hook]] on 12 pairs of **torn wayfarer whisperers** and **severed wayfarer whisperers** at [[Splendour Wellspring]] around {{waypoint -440.26, 63.36, 338.20}}. Some possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint -333.08, 88.89, 381.65}}, {{waypoint -364.94, 81.80, 381.88}}
   - {{waypoint -406.67, 65.06, 337.08}}, {{waypoint -398.47, 61.95, 311.13}}
   - {{waypoint -443.66, 63.10, 336.74}}, {{waypoint -345.30, 75.48, 280.89}}
   - {{waypoint -328.92, 85.88, 249.41}}, {{waypoint -324.47, 87.20, 296.02}}
   - {{waypoint -316.59, 93.81, 332.28}}, {{waypoint -345.02, 79.22, 322.73}}
   - {{waypoint -385.54, 73.48, 344.71}}, {{waypoint -504.35, 73.82, 346.24}}
1. Return to [[Coursequill]] and speak to him.
