## Steps

![](images/Shambler_seedling.jpg)
![Eidoloite node](images/Eidolite.jpg)

1. Gather the following components:
   - Obtain 6 ethereal tendrils from [[An etherspark|ethersparks]].
   - Gather 6 shambler seedlings from [[An obol shambler|obol shamblers]].
   - Gather 6 eidolite. *(These look like blue gem-nodes but do not require any harvesting skill.)*
1. Return to [[K'rin Valdim]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
- One of the Following, depending on class:
  - [[Aetherplate Sabatons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Boots of the Lost Soul]] (Shamans)
  - [[Slippers of Sorrow's End]] (All Mages)
  - [[Viridian Boots of Autumn]] (Druids)
  - [[Sandals of the Harrowing Sirocco]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Greaves of Mourning]] (Clerics)
  - [[Emerald Flame Boots]] (All Scouts)
