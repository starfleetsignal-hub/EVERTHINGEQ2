## Notes

- This quest is part 19 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]] and forms a bridge to the [[Enchanted Lands Timeline]].

## Steps

- Speak to [[D'Niz]] in [[Enchanted Lands]]. He is located on the beach west of the docks. {{waypoint 115, 0, 30}}  When you speak to him, the quest completes.
- [[D'Niz]] tells you to speak to [[Deputy Nettlebrine]] at the docks to start the [[Enchanted Lands Timeline]].

## Rewards

- At least 17s 67c
