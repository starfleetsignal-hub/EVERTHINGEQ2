## Prerequisites

- You need to be a level 80 or higher [[Warlock]] to start this quest.
- You will need the ability to enter the library inside Nektropos Castle (either by yourself or with the help of someone). See [[The Boar's Head]] and [[The Red Marble]].
- At least 15K faction with [[Residents of Teren's Grasp (Faction)|Residents of Teren's Grasp]].  See also [[Kylong Plains Timeline]].
- (Optional) Enough gathering skill to harvest tier 8 roots/bushes: Minimum 340 gathering skill without the use of skill-increasing equipment. This requirement can be avoided by buying the required items.

- Ability to speak the [[Froak]] language.  See [[A Mysterious Green Tome]].
- Ability to get through the green gate ([[A Bathezid gate crystal]] or can be bypassed with the Sokokar post), blue gate ([[A Nak'azar gate crystal]]), and red gate ([[A Caroeth gate crystal]]) inside [[Chardok]], either by having them yourself or someone in or outside the group opening the gates. Various call and transportation spells works too.

## Steps

1. Find out what happened to Ritter Shortshank:
   - Find [[Shortshank's grave]] in the [[Coldwind Shores Cemetery]] in the [[Thundering Steppes]] {{waypoint 1362, -1, -70}}. Dig up the grave, take the note from the coffin and read "[[Ritter's Confession]]" in your inventory.
1. Find more about Everling Village by locating [[The Journal of Samantha Rageshree]] in the [[The Library (Nektropos Castle)|The Library]] of [[Nektropos Castle]] {{waypoint -19, 14, 52}}. This appears as a glowing book on the center shelves.
1. Find [[Dalmat Rageshree]], an Everling village descendant, in the [[Tome of Death]] room of [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] {{waypoint 237.39, -7.97, 54.95}}
   - *Note: You must have [[The Journal of Samantha Rageshree]] in your inventory, or [[Dalmat Rageshree]] will not continue the conversation and update the quest.  [[The Journal of Samantha Rageshree]] will disappear from inventory when the quest updates.*
1. Retrieve a research tome that [[Dalmat Rageshree]] claims was stolen from him by a Qeynosian named Abelard Raleigh.
   1. Find and speak with [[Abelard Raleigh]] inside the [[Lion's Mane Inn and Tavern]] within [[South Qeynos]] {{waypoint 516, -9, 132}}
   1. Continue to talk to [[Abelard Raleigh]]. He will refuse to give up Dalmat's tome and attack as a level 80^.
   1. Attempt to kill [[Abelard Raleigh]] to take the tome by force. When he is nearly dead, he will give up, and you will receive [[On Achieving the Quality Of Unending Life]]. If he disappears after you kill him and you haven't gotten the update, wait for him to respawn and kill him again until you get the update. (He will not become active for the update right when you hail him, so if you have a merc, dismiss him, as he will be killed too quickly.)
   1. Return to [[Dalmat Rageshree]] in [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] and speak with him.  He will give you [[The Tale of Alexander Simond]].
1. Find the grave of Alexander Simond's wife, as indicated in [[The Tale of Alexander Simond]]:
   1. Inspect the grave of Yasinia Simond in [[Nektulos Forest]] behind [[Nektropos Castle (POI)|Nektropos Castle]] {{waypoint -1627, 117, -1852}}.
   1. Defeat [[A guardian apparition]] that attacks you (level 81^)
   1. Loot and read the journal entry ("[[A torn out journal page]]") from the chest of [[A guardian apparition]].
1. Find out how to create the poison known as [[Siv'ajauhar's Kiss]] by speaking with [[Tahd Feek]] at [[Stealth and Skullduggery]] inside [[Teren's Grasp]] within [[Kylong Plains]] {{waypoint 1734, 487, -1042}}.
   - *Note: This step requires 15,000 Faction with The Residents of [[Teren's Grasp]] in order to speak with [[Tahd Feek]].*
1. Gather the materials needed to create Siv'ajauhar's Kiss:
   - Collect 80 skorpikis venom sacks (from [[A Durus decapitator|Durus decapitators]], [[A Durus scar guardian|Durus scar guardians]], etc).
     - *Note: [[A Durus decapitator|Durus Decapitators]], [[A Durus scar guardian|Durus scar guardians]], etc are located in [[Jarsath Wastes]] {{waypoint 562.87, -60.42, -37.27}} There are approximately 42 of them in the Howling Stones chasm. Chrono to level 80 and run through with a merc to get the updates quickly. Two runs shouid yield enough for the quest.*
   - Collect 6 [[Mantrap root|Mantrap roots]]. These can be bought or harvested and come from either root or shrub nodes. Raw mats already in inventory will also auto update.
   - Collect 10 pieces of [[Smoldering Material|smoldering material]]. These also can now be bought or harvested and come from any T8 node.
     - *Note: You can save a lot of money on these steps by buying ONE mantrap root or smoldering material, putting it on your broker, and taking it back out until you have all of the updates. (For bronze/silver player on the EQX server, you can just put them in to your guild storage box and remove them to get the update, as well.)*
1. [[Tahd Feek]] will be able to make the poison with these ingredients.
   1. Return and speak to [[Tahd Feek]] in [[Teren's Grasp]] until he finishes the poison to get [[Siv'ajauhar's Kiss]].
1. Place the bottle of [[Siv'ajauhar's Kiss]] in the safe place mentioned in Alexander Simond's journal:
   1. Examine Yasmina Simond's grave behind [[Nektropos Castle (POI)|Nektropos Castle]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] {{waypoint -1627, 117, -1852}}.
   1. Examine the gravestone, look closer, and pour the bottle down the cylinder hole. You'll receive [[A second torn out journal page]] and [[The Idol of Siv'ajauhar]].
      - *Note: While [[A second torn out journal page]] will show up in your inventory, [[The Idol of Siv'ajauhar]] will not.*
1. Find out more about Kyrtoxxulous and the idol:
   1. Head to [[Concordium Tower|South Qeynos mage tower]], and go through the red portal. Down the stairs, turn right and around, then there will be a glowing book behind one of the pillars on the left hand side, {{waypoint 707, 42, 104}}. Take it.
   1. Examine [[Kyrtoxxulous, Father of Pestilence]] in your inventory and read the passage.
1. Examine the shrine built to honor Kyrtoxxulous in the ravine in the [[Fens of Nathsar]], near the [[Sokokar Post Eastern Pens|Eastern Pens sokokar post]] {{waypoint -1687, -401, 1103}},
1. Locate [[A pox-ridden reet slave]] in the [[Trakaraptor Alley]] north of [[Outer Sebilis]], {{waypoint -634, -68, 483}} in the [[Kunzar Jungle]]. You must understand [[Froak]] in order speak to him.
1. Acquire [[The Idol of Sakpat'azif]] by looting it from the corpse of [[Xalgoz (Karnor's Castle)‎|Xalgoz]] {{waypoint -140, -90, -4}} or the nearby [[Mistmoore Emissary]], who are both in the basement of [[Karnor's Castle]].
1. Recover the [[Idol of Sital'athoth]]:
   1. Speak to [[A scorned reet slave]] behind the [[Myconid Spore King]] in Lower Sebilis {{waypoint -57, -110, 287}}.  He will tell you the Idol was taken from him.
   1. Kill [[Taskmaster Kulrak]] in the Sebilis Slave Quarters {{waypoint -243, -25, 423}}. Everybody who needs the idol in the group gets it (body drop).  Placeholder is "a foreman" with about roughly 10–20 minutes respawn.  The placeholder will not give an update.
1. Recover the Idol of Yam'alogaeth
   1. Speak to [[A liberated reet slave]] in the [[Reet Camp]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]] {{waypoint 497.31, -97.32, 915.15}}.  He will tell you he told the Di'Zok sarnak where the Idol was.
   1. Kill either the [[Queen Velazul Dizok]] or her placeholder, "a Gorowyn royal delegate", deep in [[Chardok]] palace area {{waypoint 1313, -67, 228}} and loot the Idol from the chest she drops.  Everybody who needs it in the group gets the idol. (To get the Queen to spawn, all 4 of her kneeling guards that surround the throne must be killed. Once done the queen will spawn within 30 minutes.)
      - *Note: I killed everything in the room she spawns in, was standing on her throne for 1 min.*
1. Complete the ritual started by the reet slaves.
   1. Place the idols around the makeshift shrine down in the ravine in eastern [[Fens of Nathsar]] {{waypoint -1687, -401, 1103}} and be ready for a fight.
   1. The spirit of [[Gherzhas]] (level 86^^^) will spawn and attack once you place the idols.  Kill him to receive your quest reward.

STOP!
If your goal is to have the Item for the [[Epic Conversion Timeline]], you do NOT need to do the RAID version. You can begin that timeline now.
