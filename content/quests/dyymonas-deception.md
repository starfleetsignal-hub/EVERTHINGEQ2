## Steps

1. Enter [[Longshadow Alley: Daeneth Cloister]] {{waypoint -92.7, -34.6, -368.1}}
1. Put on the Thexian Disguise you received from Dyymona (inventory) in a safe place
   - some Thexians may see through your disguise and become hostile
1. Deposit Arasai Torture Instruments (6) (green particle effects mark the spots where to place them)
   - {{waypoint -59, 4, 19}}
   - {{waypoint -46, 1, -8}}
   - {{waypoint -48, 0, -28}}
   - {{waypoint -50, -5, -62}}
   - {{waypoint -34, -5, -68}}
   - {{waypoint -24, -2, -30}}
1. Kill Arasai zealots (6)
1. Be extra cautious while returning to Dyymona. You will be attacked by  a group of 3 mobs. Kill them to receive an unsealed note. Read the note in your inventory
1. Confront then kill Dyymona K'Vexx to receive a key for the chest on floor next to Dyymona

## Rewards

- At least 84s 37c
- [[Ring of the Teir'Dal]]
