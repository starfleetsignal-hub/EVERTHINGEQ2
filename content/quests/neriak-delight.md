## Steps

1. Gather 14 baby snakes from the serpents near the [[Searing Tendril]]
   - You can find the baby snakes by killing the [[Docile slither]]s or [[Agitated slither]]s {{waypoint -153.30, -56.73, 70.43}}.  This will spawn baby snakes.
   - TIP: if you are too young to kill the snakes, smack one and run back to [[T'Vatar Post]]. After the guards kill it, harvest the baby snakes!
1. Return to [[Calnozz J'Melvirr]]

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
