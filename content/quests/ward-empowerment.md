## Steps

1. Capture 6 stink scarabs on caiman beach near {{waypoint -1400, -227, -490}}. They can also be found at the oasis near Twin Tears on the grassy patches near {{waypoint -400, -120, -676}} and {{waypoint -1138, -211, -237}}.
1. Return to Researcher Aillena.
1. Destroy two void tempests anywhere on Norrath and collect their [[Void Storm Shred]].  You must defeat the 5 enemies before the Void Tempest will zap you back and go invisible.  Click on the base of the void tempest to get it selected and kill it.  See the list of [[Tempest affected Creatures List|void tempests locations]] for ideas on where to fight the void tempests and collect the shreds.
1. Return to Researcher Aillena.
