## Notes

To receive this quest you are required to have 20,000 (kindly) faction with [[Port Woe]]. If your faction is lower continue doing the Meet and Greet repeatables or  other adventure quests that improve Port Woe faction.

- 3 x [[Ilmenite Ore]]
- 3 x [[Chalcedony]]
- 2 x [[Sedge Root]]
- 5 x [[Zimaran Coal]]

## Steps

1. Find the captured adventurers
   1. They are found at {{waypoint -7, 201, -456}}
      - *You can use and possess your [[W.H.A.T. V1]] to get all the way to captives if your adventure level is low **or** if you are high enough level just kill your way through.*
      - *In case you completed the adventure timeline already you can just fly there and use the [[W.H.A.T. V1]] when you are nearby*
      1. Go West through the swamp to the path {{waypoint 550, 34, 465}} leading up to [[Sodden Uplands]]. Then travel all the way East to Blackhood Gate at {{waypoint 127, 137, 80}}
      1. Use the [[W.H.A.T. V1]], possess your magic harvester and travel to {{waypoint -7, 201, -456}} quest updates by Captured Adventurer in a cage.
      1. Take the right branches of the path up the hill until you reach the cages of Captured Adventurers.
         - *NOTE: You must remain in W.H.A.T. form in order to see the diversion, and then remain in W.H.A.T. form to travel back to the prisoners to release them. Watch for the update. (I had to do it three times.)*
   1. Cause a diversion
      - click the Potential Diversion at {{waypoint -70, 196, -490}}
   1. Quickly free 3 prisoners
      - a caged prisoners up the ramp at {{waypoint -51, 204, -554}} and 2 of them just where you found Captured Adventurer {{waypoint -7, 201, -456}}
1. Speak with [[Ameela Ziad]] at {{waypoint 29, 5, 360}}
   - *You'll receive autoscribed [[Recipe Scroll: Arcane Tuning Fork|recipe]] for [[Arcane Tuning Fork]]*
1. Create [[Arcane Tuning Fork]] - uses 3 [[Ilmenite Ore]], 3 [[Chalcedony]], 2 [[Sedge Root]] and 5 [[Zimaran Coal]]
1. Speak with [[Ameela Ziad]] at {{waypoint 29, 5, 360}}
   - She finishes this quest and tells you to go meet the new ship's captain on the dock.
