## Prerequisites

You need to know how to speak [[Gnollish]] to start or complete this quest.

## Steps

1. Gather 10 discarded weapons in the undead areas to the northeast and southeast of Snowfang Isle
   - These are axes, spears and shields laying all over the ground, between the areas of {{waypoint -1031, -537, -2163}} and {{waypoint -1139, -539, -2298}}
1. Return to Thurr to complete the quest

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,100 faction with **Snowfang Gnolls**
- [[Snowfang Clan Banner]]
