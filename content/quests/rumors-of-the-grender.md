## Description

"Apparently, there are rumors around Kelethin that the disease the creatures on Green Knoll suffer was caused by a half-man, half-beast known as the Grender. If I can prove or disprove these rumors, I will be able to remove the suspicions hanging over the Outsiders." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

![One of the Grender's focus totems](images/Grender_focus_totem.jpg)

1. Speak with [[Kalamar Vesinger]] inside [[Joleena's Restaurant]] at {{waypoint 539, 71, 324}}
1. Examine [[A Parchment Scroll (Right click this item to examine it)]] in your inventory
1. Visit the following places (in no particular order):
   - [[Tunare's Sapling Alcove]] {{waypoint -86, -10, 18}}
   - [[Opal Pond]] {{waypoint 167, 2, 92}}
   - [[Aquamarine Pond]] {{waypoint 494, -1, 205}}
   - [[Granite Hills]] {{waypoint 129, 50, 375}}
1. Return to [[Kalamar Vesinger]]
1. Destroy 3 totems that spawn around the [[Kelethin Central Stump]] {{waypoint 344, 9, 388}}
   - The totems are at {{waypoint 366, 20, 343}}, {{waypoint 341, 37, 311}}, {{waypoint 267, 27, 409}}, {{waypoint 344, 9, 388}} or {{waypoint 253, 48, 336}}
1. Slay [[The Grender Guardian]] in the hidden room in the [[Kelethin Central Stump]]
   - The ramp to the entrance is at {{waypoint 344, 9, 388}} and [[The Grender Guardian]] is at {{waypoint 324, 44, 339}}
1. Return to [[Kalamar Vesinger]]
1. Return to [[Lady Katri Eloranta]]
