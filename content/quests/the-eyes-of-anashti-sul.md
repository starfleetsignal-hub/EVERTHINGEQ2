## Notes

- This was formerly the access quest for the inner temple of the Silent City, called [[The Fountain of Life]]. It is no longer necessary to complete this quest to gain access to The Fountain of Life as of [[LU48]].
- There are 3 versions of this quest, each picked up randomly when you inspect the Stone Tablet. The only difference between versions is the order in which the monoliths are inspected.

## Starting the Quest

1. Move to the West section of The Silent City to [[The Altar of the Eyes]]. {{waypoint 903, 35, 192}}
1. Inspect the 'Rhoen' rune on [[The Altar of the Eyes]].
1. Speak to the [[The Steward of Fyr'Un]]. He will give you [[A stone tablet]].
1. Examine the tablet to start the quest.

## Steps

1. Visit various monoliths:
   - Zul {{waypoint 864, 0, 226}} middle building, east side
   - Yuex {{waypoint 918, -1, 204}} mid-way up the center structure, west side
   - Paro {{waypoint 1025, -8, 549}} ground level, west corner from A Jin'Tat Battlepriest
   - Fett {{waypoint 815, -16, 267}} ground level, east side from Spiregaze
   - Luna {{waypoint 985, -26, 440}} ground level, southwest corner
   - Adu {{waypoint 791, -2, 100}} ground level, northeast corner
   - Myr {{waypoint 1072, 16, 420}} third tier up, west side
   - Uzu {{waypoint 1020, -1, 315}} middle tier, west side
   - Rhoen {{waypoint 851, -26, 502}} ground Floor, east side
1. Enter the [[The Fountain of Life]]. {{waypoint 895, -1, 245}}
   - **Warning**: You will zone-in within aggro range of multiple (4) 62^^^ Heroic gnolls, so be prepared to fight. There is also a patrol of 3 more (Heroic) gnolls that goes as far as the entrance.
1. Turn around, open the door and enter the foyer.
   - Since the access quest was removed, you no longer need to place any runes into the sockets on the floor, the quest completes immediately. Although the sockets remain active, interacting will tell you that you don't seem to have any runes to fit into the puzzle.

## Rewards

- At least 18g
