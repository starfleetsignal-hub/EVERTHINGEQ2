## Steps

1. Collect a few specimens for Gildas in [[The Crypt of Betrayal]]:
   - Kill 12 [[A deathly scarab (Crypt of Betrayal)|deathly scarabs]]
   - Kill 8 [[A forsaken shriller|forsaken shrillers]] for their eyes
   - Kill 12 [[A restless corpse|restless corpses]] {{waypoint 119, 7, -95}}, may also be found at   {{waypoint 148, 7, -69}} and {{waypoint 171, 7, -174}}
1. Return to Gildas for your reward.

## Rewards

- One of the following (depending on class):
  - [[Ensorcelled Shawl of the Adept]] - ([[Conjuror]])
  - [[Shawl of the Befuddler]] - ([[Illusionist]])
  - [[Defiled Sanguine Shawl]] - ([[Warlock]])
  - [[Spellbound Cerise Shawl]] - ([[Wizard]])
  - [[Shawl of Allurement]] - ([[Coercer]])
