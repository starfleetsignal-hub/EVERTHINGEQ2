## Prerequisites

- You must be at least adventure level 14 to obtain this quest.

## Starting the Quest

Speak with [[Captain R.K. Irontoe]] in [[Kelethin]], at {{waypoint 136, 112, 98}}. He offers a great deal of lore, and many different conversation paths. After introducing yourself and exchanging common pleasantries, the responses that get you to the quest are as follows:

1. What did [[Kaladim]] do during the War of Fay?
1. What were trolls and ogres doing in [[The Lesser Faydark]]?
1. Did the trolls and ogres stop the dwarven brigades?
1. What happened at the Battle of Brute Hill?
1. So they managed to reach Kelethin and rescue the city?
1. The dark elves burned the forest?
1. What about [[Felwithe (City)|Felwithe]], home of the high elves?
1. So the gnomes and their machines freed Felwithe.
1. What else occurred in the Siege of Felwithe?
1. Were there any significant dwarven battles in [[Greater Faydark]]?
1. Please forgive my wording. I just wondered if there were any dwarven historical sites nearby.
1. Who is the Sisterhood of Erollisi?
1. Where was this place?
1. These elven maidens returned to aid the ones that once suppressed their right to fight?
1. How do you know all this?
1. Let's get back to the story of [[The Battle at Burning Roots]].
1. Tell me more.
1. So death was certain. How sad.
1. Were they defeated?
1. Did the amazons stop the bonfire platoon?
1. What happened to the Bloodforge Brigade?
1. Where can I go to honor those that fought at The Battle at Burning Roots?
1. I will honor and serve.

## Steps

1. Interact with the monument, [[The Battle at Burning Roots]] in [[Greater Faydark]]. {{waypoint -299, 18, -54}}
1. [[Gathering|Gather]] 10 dwarven war artifacts found around the memorial. This is ground spawn and does not actually go into your inventory. *(<s>Note : as of 09/16/24 it appears you only need 1 artifact</s>).*
1. Return to [[Captain R.K. Irontoe]] {{waypoint 138.63, 111.44, 98.22}} in [[Kelethin]] and give him the artifact.
1. Inspect a strongbox in one of the caves within [[Dyer Mycoria (POI)|Dyer Mycoria]]. {{waypoint 3, -43, -295}}
1. Inspect another strongbox (which looks like an attackable creature strongbox) in a tent on [[Orc Hill]]. {{waypoint 284, 61, -143}}
1. Examine a scroll in another tent on Orc Hill. {{waypoint 254, 67, -182}}
1. Kill [[A Crushbone invader|Crushbone invaders]] for 3 uncommon auto-updates.
1. Ring the gong on Orc Hill. {{waypoint 287, 61, -154}}
1. Find and defeat [[A Crushbone spy|Crushbone spies]]:
   - Look for the reddish sparkles and the mouseover infobox, ***orc scribbling***, which mark the spawn. See the discussion page for more info.
   - By the passageway to [[Butcherblock Mountains]], at {{waypoint 745.99, 13.99, -70.57}}. He spawns on top of the root above your head, so look up if you can't find him. You may receive the message "The orc spy has not reached his post yet", which means you may need to wait a few minutes.
   - Along a branch leading off of [[Old Kelethin]] platform, at {{waypoint 154.98, 110.85, 79.11}}.
   - Underneath [[Memorial Hill]], at {{waypoint -177.74, 3.71, -15.06}}.
1. ![a Brook Patch toadstool](images/A_Brook_Patch_toadstool.jpg) Speak to [[The Mushroom King]], at {{waypoint -144.78, -9.38, 141.77}}.
1. Go to [[Tunare's Glade]] {{waypoint -252, 36, 363}} and:
   - Collect 10 [[A Brook Patch toadstool|Brook Patch toadstools]] (they do NOT show up using [[Track Harvestables]] but do show up on [[Tracking]]).
   - Kill 20 [[A Fayfly|Fayflies]].
1. Retun to The Mushroom King and he will give you the *shrool dust*.
1. Sprinkle the dust on the strongbox on Orc Hill. {{waypoint 284, 61, -143}}
1. Examine the [[Dwarven ringmail plans]].
1. Find the nine copies by killing various orcish military leaders and their personnel:
   - First: [[A Sullon Brigade warsmith]] {{waypoint -655, 80, -257}}
   - Second: [[A Vallon Brigade warsmith]] {{waypoint -750, 70, -75}}
   - Third: [[A Derris Brigade warsmith]] {{waypoint -840, 97, 10}}
   - Fourth: [[A Tallon Brigade warsmith]] {{waypoint -1080, 115, 205}}
   - Fifth: [[A Gullon Brigade warsmith]] {{waypoint -1392, 116, 175}}
   - Sixth: [[Crushbone Expedition Leader]] {{waypoint -741, 79.72, -194}}
   - Seventh: [[Crushbone Expedition Chef]] {{waypoint -778, 90, -262}}
   - Eighth: [[Crushbone Expedition Weaponsmith]] {{waypoint -790, 88, -216}}
   - Ninth: [[Crushbone Expedition Planner]] {{waypoint -730, 96, -262}}
1. Enter [[Crushbone Keep]] and inspect four chests inside the first room. If you're looking forward from the zone-in, two chests are on the right, and two chests are the left.
1. Return to [[Captain R.K. Irontoe]] in [[Kelethin]]. {{waypoint 136, 112, 98}}
