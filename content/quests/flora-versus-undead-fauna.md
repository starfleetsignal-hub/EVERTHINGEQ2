## Steps

You receive [[Rune of Aggressive Growth]] at the beginning of the quest

1. Use the Rune of Aggressive Growth to summon a force of nature near the undead, 5 times
   - Get near any undead around the walls of Highkeep, then use the rune to summon [[A force of nature]].  Then run - you don't need to kill the monster to update the quest, the forces of nature won't actually attack the undead, but they *will* attack you if you AOE around them and they hit pretty hard.
1. Return to Mibton

- At least 1p 45g 80s
