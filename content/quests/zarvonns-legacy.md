## Steps

*Having been told an interesting story about Zarvonn the Mad Mage, I've decided to investigate it further. Perhaps I may be able to solve the mystery as to his centuries-old disappearance.*

1. Speak with the old nomad, [[Gierasa Kulnovich]] at {{waypoint 409, -47, -805}}.
1. Find a woman in [[The Tower of Zarvonn]] at the northern part of [[The Commonlands]].
   - Speak with [[Assistant D`Verin]] upstairs at {{waypoint 909, -21, -521}}
1. Gather the skulls from the orc skeletons that appear to be leading the other undead.
   - Obtain 4 skulls by killing the [[A fallen orc lieutenant|fallen orc lieutenant]]s at {{waypoint -1188, -48, -460}}
1. Speak with D`Verin.
1. Speak with [[Overseer Melicinn]], D`Verin's mistress, at the top of the tower.
1. Find the lost soul and recover ectoplasmic goo from its remains.
   - Kill a [[A lost soul (Commonlands)|lost soul]] wandering in [[Turmoil Cemetery]]. {{waypoint -1333, -68, 333}}
1. Return to Overseer Melicin to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- Formerly, access [[The Eternal Gorge]].
