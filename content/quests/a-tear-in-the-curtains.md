## Steps

1. Speak to [[Rohn Tinkerton]] near the [[Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]].{{waypoint 658, -19, 151}}
1. Meet [[Rohn Tinkerton]] inside the [[Everquest2:Category:The Baubbleshire: Vale's Shadow Quest NPCs|Baubbleshire]]: Vale's Shadow. {{waypoint 827, -19, -512}}
1. Take the [[Clockwork E.C.U.]] to one of the void portals and stay by the portal until it has absorbed the portal's energy.
1. Return to [[Rohn Tinkerton]].
1. The Clockwork E.C.U. explodes, releasing [[An escaped nightblood]]! Slay it.
1. Speak to [[Rohn Tinkerton]].
1. Return to [[Elddar Grove]] and speak to [[Martha Picklenose|Martha]]. {{waypoint 809, -21, -528}}

## Rewards

- At least 22s 7c (at level 24)
