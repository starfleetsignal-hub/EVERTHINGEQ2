The goblins are trying to establish a trade relationship with the Cae'dal elves in Nye'Caelona. It seems the elves have noticed some of the local rabbits nibbling at unprotected seedlings in the garden, and wish to trade for some of Growf's rabbit repellent spray.

## Note

Upon accepting this quest, you will receive [[Go-Away Rabbit Spray (Recipe Book)]] which will automatically scribe the [[Go-Away Rabbit Spray]] recipe to your recipe book.

The recipe for this quest can **only** be crafted at a [[Goblin Firepit]] in the area. One is found at {{waypoint 132, -164, -768}}, near [[Glibnox]].

## Supply List

Because these must be crafted in [[Twark]], and the required materials cannot be harvested in [[Obulus Frontier]], bring the following (minimum) supplies:

- 4 Astringent Herb (gathered in the zone)
- 12 [[Mycelial tendrils]]
- 12 [[Fruiting sporangium]]
- 20 [[Thaumic Coal]]

## Steps

1. Astringent herbs can be found to the south
   - Gather astringent herbs (4)
     - They are trackable small green bushes, and can be found in the area south of [[Twark]] from {{waypoint 215, -215, -455}} to {{waypoint 248, -225, -374}}
1. I can brew this up in a goblin fire
   - Craft the [[Rabbit repellent spray|pest spray]] (4)
     - The [[Go-Away Rabbit Spray]] recipe produces [[Rabbit repellent spray|rabbit repellent spray]], which is the pest spray requested by the quest
1. Lyraquel can be found in Nye'Caelona
   - Deliver the spray to [[Elder Lyraquel|Lyraquel]] {{waypoint -204, 91, -209}}
1. Oogthor is in the main gobline encampment of Twark
   - Deliver the payment back to [[Oogthor]]
