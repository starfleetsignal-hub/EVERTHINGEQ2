## Steps

1. Retrieve the okoute stones from the 3 caves. Find a little blue glowing stone next to one of the four orbs and click it to retrieve it. You CAN do this stealthed, but you will become visible when you try to take the stone.  To avoid aggro, get directly behind the orb next to the stone, and back as far from the center of the room as possible (without dropping into the lava).
1. Return to [[Pyrissa Flamecog]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 40g
- +2250 faction with **Order of Flame**
- [[Girdle of Molten Rocks]]
