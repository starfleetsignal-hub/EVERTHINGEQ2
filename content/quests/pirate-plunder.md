## Steps

Zone into [[Brokenskull Bay: Bilgewater Falls (Solo)]]

- Silence the smooth talker - kill [[Smooth-Talkin Duncan Togglecog (Solo)]], at {{waypoint -27, 120, -249}}
  - *Note: he spawns after the other 3 nameds (excluding Captain Berlon) in the zone are killed*
- Plunder the life from the Brokenskull Pirate captain - kill [[Captain Berlon Bilgewater (solo)]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 67g 40s 5,500 status
- 1 [[Far Seas Ferrin]]
