This is part 3 of 6 in the  for residents of [[Freeport]] who wear Chain and Plate Armor (with the exception of [[Shaman|shaman]] types).

- If you are from [[Qeynos]], [[Kelethin]], or [[New Halas]], then your version of the quest is called [[Ian's Forgetful Family - Part III]].

- Note as of LU32/33, this is a solo quest.

## Steps

1. You must collect items from creatures in [[Nektulos Forest]].
   - Kill 10 [[Ash viper|ash vipers]] for their unbeating hearts.
   - Kill 12 [[Ash shriller|ash shrillers]] for their wing membranes.
1. Return to Dyric.
