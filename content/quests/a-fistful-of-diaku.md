## Steps

1. Search [[Revenant's Vista]] {{waypoint 153.58, 63.57, -181.69}}.
1. Go down the path to the docks and pick up a [[Small curious bag]] on the dock {{waypoint 45.60, -15.03, -228.27}}.
1. Return to [[Bridon Kizzlebash]] and talk to him.
1. Enter [[Wracklands: Diaku Corral (Solo)|Wracklands: Diaku Corral [Solo]]] or [[Wracklands: Diaku Corral (Heroic)|Wracklands: Diaku Corral [Heroic]]].
   - Note: If you are stuck visit: [[Wracklands: Diaku Corral (Solo)|Wracklands: Diaku Corral [Solo]]] for a walkthrough (solo).
   1. Search for 6 [[Discarded camellia leaves]] on  the path between the dock and the first bridge.
      - These are a swirling leaf particle effect, you may need to turn up your graphics to be able to see them.
   1. Advance through Diaku Corral in search of Gattley.
      - First update just across first bridge {{waypoint -711.92, 30.99, -410.60}}.
   1. Next update just inside the  gate to [[Diakaroo Bonanza]] {{waypoint -731.22, 61.53, -532.25}}.
   1. Gather Gattley's wrench after defeating the Joro siblings and their sister{{waypoint -542.95, 61.56, -650.52}}.  **Check Your Quest for updates!**  If you missed a [[Discarded camellia leaves]], you can jump down to the dock and get the update, then run through for the next two updates.  If you are not on this step when the Joro's die, the wrench will not spawn, and you'll have to reset the zone and start again.
   1. Defeat [[Vleecan Fele]], Professor Gattley's captor, in [[Bossman's Redoubt]] {{waypoint -637.37, 61.19, -496.36}}.
1. Return to [[Bridon Kizzlebash]] and talk to him.
