## Steps

1. Obtain a sample of Fire within the Plane of Fire.
   1. Go to the [[North Wing Stacks]] on the ground floor of the Library, then take the teleporter at {{waypoint 260, -20, -285}} to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]]. Use the portal at {{waypoint 728, 412, -337}} to enter [[Doomfire: The Enkindled Towers (Solo)|Doomfire: The Enkindled Towers [Solo]]].
   1. Progress through the zone. (See [[Doomfire: The Enkindled Towers (Solo)|Doomfire: The Enkindled Towers [Solo]]] for strategies).
   1. Loot a [[Thermite briquette]] from the final name, [[Ra-Sekjet]].
1. Return to [[Myrist, the Great Library]] and speak with [[Maelin Starpyre]] in the [[Myrist Main Stacks]] at {{waypoint 246, -7, -26}}.
1. Obtain a pure sample of Air within the Plane of Air.
   1. Go to the [[North Wing Stacks]] on the ground floor of the Library, then take the teleporter at {{waypoint 260, -20, -285}} to the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]] and use the portal [[Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wind]] at {{waypoint 712, 412, -380}} to enter [[Eryslai: The Bixel Hive (Solo)|Eryslai: The Bixel Hive [Solo]]].
   1. Progress through the zone. (See [[Eryslai: The Bixel Hive (Solo)|Eryslai: The Bixel Hive [Solo]]] for strategies).
   1. Loot an [[Ephemeral gust (Pure Adventure)|ephemeral gust]] from the final named, [[Aurorax]] the Bixel Queen. You will be teleported to {{waypoint -543, -169, -9076}}.
      - NOTE: If you are not teleported, or if you revive during the fight, take the portal on Honeycomb Landing {{waypoint -579, 358, -415}}
   1. Kill the attacking [[An icecrete rumbler|icecrete rumbler]].
   1. Take the portal at {{waypoint -642, -176, -964}} to return to [[The Bixel Hive]].
   1. Use the **Pollen Launcher** at {{waypoint -508, 356, -436}} or evac to return to the starting island.
   1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 494, -202, -1470}} to return to [[Myrist, the Great Library]].
1. Speak with [[Maelin Starpyre]] in the [[Myrist Main Stacks]] at {{waypoint 246, -7, -26}}
