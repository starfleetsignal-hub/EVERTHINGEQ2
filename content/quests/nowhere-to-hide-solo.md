## Steps

*Everywhere the enemy of the Crusaders of Greenmist exist, but nowhere can they hide. I have taken up their mission to destroy their enemies wherever I might find them, and destroy them I shall!*

- Destroy enemy targets for the Crusaders of Greenmist.
1. Defeat [[Ongnissim the Unseen (Solo)|Ongnissim]] within the [[Lost City of Torsis: The Spectral Market (Solo)]]
1. Kill [[Neh'Ashiir (Solo)|Neh'Ashiir]] withing the [[Lost City of Torsis: Reaver's Remnants (Solo)]]
1. Kill [[Dalnir]] within [[Crypt of Dalnir: Baron's Workshop (Solo)]]
1. Defeat [[The Kly (Solo)|The Kly]] within the [[Crypt of Dalnir: Ritual Chamber (Solo)]]
1. Defeat [[Grand Master Glox]] within [[The Ruins of Cabilis (Solo)]]
1. Kill [[Over-General Vihgoh]] within [[Kaesora: Xalgozian Stronghold (Solo)]]
1. Kill [[Xerxes'Kade]] within [[Arcanna'se Spire: Repository of Secrets (Solo)]]
1. Defeat [[Lachina]] within [[Arcanna'se Spire: Forgotten Sanctum (Solo)]]
