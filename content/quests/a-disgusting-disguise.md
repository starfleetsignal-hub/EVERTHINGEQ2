## Steps

*I need to prove my cleverness to the pack and Nisch Mal Polphar by destroying the trust between the various gnoll clans that can be found in the Darkpaw Burrow. Nisch Mas Nolphia gave me an item that when combined with scraps of clothing and armor from felled gnolls will allow me to take on the appearance of a Sabertooth gnoll. This will give me the opportunity to infiltrate the other gnoll camps and burn their banners, breeding discontent between the clans.*

1. Craft a gnoll disguise
   - Collect 3 [[Gnoll Armor Scraps]] from a small chest from any gnoll group in Darkpaw Warrens (1 update per group member)
   - Examine the [[Gnoll Disguise Kit]] in your inventory and select "Combine gnoll armor scraps with the contents of this box" to receive [[Clever Sabertooth Gnoll Disguise]]
1. Burn 5 gnoll banners while disguised
   - Use the Clever Sabretooth Gnoll Disguise (NOTE: this illusion lasts less than 30 seconds, but it can be reused.)
   - While the disguise is active, click on Darkpaw Banner on the wall in the Sabertooth Burrow area to burn them
   - These are solo updates, but the banners do respawn in a locked instance
1. Return to Nisch Mas Nolphia in Darkpaw Warrens {{waypoint -26, -44, -240}}
