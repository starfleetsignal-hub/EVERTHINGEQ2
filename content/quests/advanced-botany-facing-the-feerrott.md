## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable

## Steps

![Feerroot!(Overgrown Pitcher)(images/Screenshot_2024-09-28_104800.jpg)!(Dreadleaf Seed)(images/Dreadleaf_Seeds.png)](images/Feeroot.png)

1. Travel to [[The Feerrott]] and gather
   1. 3 bundles of "feerroot" -- multiple spawns around Fear Camp  {{waypoint -991.73, -4.62, 744.09}}
   1. an "overgrown pitcher" -- these spawn along the Greenblood River
      - possible locations {{waypoint -224.14, 4.53, 264.67}}; {{waypoint -266.68, 3.79, 225.23}}; {{waypoint -247.90, 5.68, 295.79}}
   1. 3 "dreadleaf seeds" -- multiple spawns in the Dread Basin around {{waypoint -1856.30, -21.69, 645.70}}
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].

## Rewards:

- Unlock the [[Days of Summer: 2024|Days of Summer 2024: Week 2]] "account flag"
