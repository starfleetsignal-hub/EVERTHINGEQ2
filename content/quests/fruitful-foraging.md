## Description

The Far Seas traders constantly need supplies of fresh fruit and vegetables to sustain the encampment.  Skrit would like me to harvest some freshly picked hylocereus fruit, Kith pepper, and/or sulfur fruit to replenish the stores.

## Steps

1. Gather fresh-picked fruits and vegetables to replenish the isle's supplies (50)
   - Harvest 50 fresh fruit/vegetables *in [[Tranquil Sea]] or [[Phantom Sea]]*
1. Return the provisions to [[Skrit]]

## Rewards

- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token]]
- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
