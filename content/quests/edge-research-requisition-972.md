## Required Materials

*In addition to the materials harvested for the Requisition, you will need:*

- 25 [[Coruscating Coal]]

## Steps

1. Harvest Materials:
   - 18 [[Humming Azurite]]
   - 15 [[Radiant Algae]]
   - 45 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
1. Obtain 1 [[Humming Sapphire Gem]] -- *rare* (this can be obtained from a harvest depot or broker)
1. Craft a [[Research Magnification Lens]].
1. Return to [[Puhrihni "The Edge" Fordel III]].

## Rewards

- [[Research Mission Reward Crate]]
- 8 [[Recuso Field Voucher]]
- At least 1p 25g 7,700 status
