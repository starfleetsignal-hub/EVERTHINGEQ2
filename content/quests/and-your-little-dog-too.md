## Steps

- Kill 5 spiders ([[A Mazkeen war spider]], [[A pale spitting spider]], [[A yellow recluse spider]], [[A yellow recluse spiderling]])
- Kill 5 [[A mountain lion|mountain lions]]
- Kill 5 coyotes ([[A feral coyote]], [[A Grikbar hound]], [[A mountain coyote]]...)
- Return to Watchman Plarg for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 53s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Earhoop of Gnomish Spirit]] (All Priests)
  - [[Earring of Gnomish Spirit]] (All Mages)
  - [[Facet of Gnomish Spirit]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Hoop of Gnomish Spirit]] (All Scouts)
