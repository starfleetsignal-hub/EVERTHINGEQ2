## Steps

1. Gather 10 of each of the followinng materials from crates around the tent:
   - [[Flash Initiator]]
   - [[Amber]]
   - [[Cobalt Powder]]
1. Craft 10 [[Amber Storm Fulminant]] at a [[Chemistry Table]] at {{waypoint 4313, -821, 444}} using the gathered materials.
   - Requires 10 [[Effulgent candle]]
1. Return the 10 Amber Storm Fulminant to Plex

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
