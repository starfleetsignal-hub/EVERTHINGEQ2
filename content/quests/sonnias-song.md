## Steps

1. Kill [[Venril Sathir]] and harvest the harp to the right when you enter the throne room.
1. Kill [[The Leviathan]] and harvest the harp under one of the tentacles on the right hand side as you enter the room. {{waypoint -25, -24, -110}}
1. Kill [[Druushk]] and harvest the harp in the room he spawns in. {{waypoint 1672, -364, 254}}
1. Return to [[Son'Nia]] in [[The Barren Sky]] for your reward.

- [[Ayonic Axe (Mythical)|Ayonic Axe]]
