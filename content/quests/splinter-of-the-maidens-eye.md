## Steps

1. Search for buried Akheven artifacts by clicking on Brittle Rocks.  *Note: Flatish grey stones. Once you click on them and they break apart, some will yield a much smaller whitish piece. Click on that to update your artifact count.*
   - Possible locations include:
   - {{waypoint -347.78, 2.58, -308.49}} {{waypoint -343.13, 11.49, -192.40}} {{waypoint -428.67, 14.84, -155.87}} {{waypoint -373.60, 14.82, -164.03}}
   - {{waypoint -378.32, 15.58, -201.82}} {{waypoint -356.48, 12.14, -237.31}} {{waypoint -436.72, 27.71, -259.84}} {{waypoint -497.84, 39.41, -91.81}}
   - {{waypoint -613.35, 62.30, -139.24}} {{waypoint -581.08, 62.95, -177.08}} {{waypoint -552.92, 66.74, -203.43}} {{waypoint -701.53, 63.57, -210.82}}
   - *Note: Be sure to click on the small silver square that is the ancient artifact. Not all rocks hide artifacts.*
1. Return to [[Zalle Dessimda]] at {{waypoint -166.23, 3.89, 209.66}}.
1. Obtain 12 relics from Akhevans near {{waypoint -700.34, 4.16, -697.98}}. *Note: Not a guranteed update.*
1. Return to [[Zalle Dessimda]] at {{waypoint -166.23, 3.89, 209.66}}.
