This shouldn't be too difficult at all. If I'm not up to the task I can always buy my way out of this one, but I'm betting the prize will be something useful only to a skilled furniture builder. Either way, I almost feel sorry for anyone else entering this contest, even if the goal is to raise museum-building funds. There's only one possible outcome: I will take first place.

## Steps

1. Make (or obtain) a [[A pristine oval ash table]]
   - Make it yourself, have someone else make them, or buy it from the broker.
1. Return to [[Deputy Hopple]]

## Rewards

- [[Vale Briarwood Bookcase Plans]]  - recipe for [[A pristine vale briarwood bookcase]]

## Notes

- A version of this item without books is also available as [[A pristine briarwood bookcase]]
