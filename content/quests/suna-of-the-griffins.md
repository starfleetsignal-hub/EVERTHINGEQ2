## Note

You need to fly for this quest.

## Steps

1. Find Suna. She is located on top of a snowy mountain at {{waypoint -874, -176, 3306}}  The nearby tundra griffons are not aggressive but will attack you if you get too close to them or their young.
1. When you hail Suna, five tundra wind griffins will attack. You cannot win this fight!
1. Fight the griffins until Suna calls them off (Note:  You can die). Hail her to complete the quest.
