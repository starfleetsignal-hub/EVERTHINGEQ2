## Notes

- Crazy Peepers is a 20^^ Heroic, with four [[A crazed mender|crazed menders]] joining him.
- This quest previously granted access to [[The Serpent's Lair]]. It is no longer required.

## Steps

1. Kill [[Crazy Peepers]] at {{waypoint -13, 0, 66}}
1. Return to Cicero
1. Deliver the letter to Merchant [[Tula Mawh]] {{waypoint -113, 0, 7}}
1. Return to Cicero again
