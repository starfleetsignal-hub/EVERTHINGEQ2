## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A gliding wave|Gliding Waves]]
   - These are found in the water near the Tower of Serilis in the Guardians of the Underfoot portion
1. Return to Sami (next to Labib).

## Rewards

- At least 20g 70s 61c
- +500 faction with **Defenders of the Seal**
