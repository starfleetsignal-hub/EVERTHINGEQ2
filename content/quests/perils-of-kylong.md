## Steps

1. Find the Shade of the Great Chokidai near Karnor's Castle.{{waypoint 484, 28, -156}}
1. Defeat 6 Drolvarg warriors outside [[Karnor's Castle]].
1. Meet the Shade of the Great Chokidai near the Brute Gathering.{{waypoint 438, 25, -640}}.
1. Defeat 6 [[A hillock crusher|Hillock crushers]] within the Dreadlands. {{waypoint 591, 70, -799}}
1. Seek out the Shade of the Great Chokidai near the Seeping Ruins. {{waypoint -875, 28, -458}}.
1. Defeat 5 "members of the Cult of the Arisen" in the area east of the nearby river.
   - *Note: Any of the Sathirian Iksars in that area will provide updates.*
1. Return to [[Shalih Mar]] at Dreg's Landing. {{waypoint 801, 10, 137}}

## **Reward**

- at least 9g 48s 1c
