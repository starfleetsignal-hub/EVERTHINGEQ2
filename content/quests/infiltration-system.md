## Steps

1. Use the class-specific illusion item given to you when you accepted the quest and talk to [[Geldryy Alvek]] on [[The Sheet]]. {{waypoint -2652, -412.34, -2673.50}}
   - The illusion item differs based on your class archetype:
     - A [[Scout]] will receive [[Order of Rime Sleetsaber Illusion]].
     - A [[Fighter]] will receive [[Order of Rime Shardhammer Illusion]].
     - A [[Mage]] will receive [[Order of Rime Flurryburst Illusion]].
     - A [[Priest]] will receive [[Order of Rime Nova-vicar Illusion]].
   - While here, and still in disguise, pick up the next quest in the series, [[Elemental Task]]. {{waypoint -2655, -412, -2667}}
1. Head to {{waypoint -2526.75, -412, -2859}} and attempt to pick up the piece of paper. ![The paper (arrowed) is flapping on the wooden chest lid, and difficult to see...](images/Infiltration_System_piece_of_paper.jpg)
1. Kill [[An observant shardhammer guard]] who will spawn.
1. Grab the piece of paper again.
1. Return to Sliza Xarezia in Riliss.

## Rewards

- At least 36g 79s 48c
- +1000 faction with **Riliss**
- Both of the following:
  - [[Rilissian Soldier's Rhino]] - *Mount*
  - [[An iksar bookcase]] - *House Item*
- The class specific illusion item is not removed and serves as an additional reward.
