## Steps

1. Speak to [[Artimus Klangfangle]] {{waypoint 4426, -823, 493}}
1. Klangfangle wants spell components, but doesn't tell you what they are!  Click the golden page behind him to get the list.
1. Collect the following
   - 6 illumines glands from [[A ripper-snapper|ripper-snapper fish]].
   - 4 ancient hardwood - sparkling board clickables, found among the shipwrecks underneath the ocean in Howlers Cove.
1. Return to Artimus Klangfangle
