**NOTE*** You MUST strictly adhere to these steps, or you will end up repeating the quest to get a completion. You must not leave the first room until you have killed** [[A Luminary bargainer]]

## Steps

1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -280.48, 180.72, 0.42}} to enter [[Sanctus Seru: Echelon of Order (Solo)|Sanctus Seru: Echelon of Order [Solo]]].
1. Click on the barrier to bring it down.![Barrier to Sanctus Seru](images/Barrier_to_Sanctus_Seru.jpg)
1. Speak to [[A knight of the Echelon]]. He will attack and the mobs in the zone become hostile.
1. Kill [[A Luminary bargainer]] at {{waypoint -251.87, 89.62, 19.89}} to 'Learn more about the luminaries'. **If any other MOBS from the next step are killed before this step, you will have to repeat the quest.**
1. Defeat the following:
   - 8 [[A Luminary conciliator|Luminary conciliators]]
   - 9 [[A Luminary animaster|Luminary animasters]]
   - 11 [[A Luminary bard|Luminary bards]]
   - 16 [[A Luminary ranger|Luminary rangers]]
   - 11 [[A Luminary bargainer|Luminary bargainers]]
   - *Note: No names need to be killed to complete the quest.*
1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -408, 88, 3}} to return to Sanctus Seru (City).
1. Return to [[Hulda Flintbraid]] at {{waypoint -72.44, 88.88, 257.57}}.

## Rewards

- At least 2p 10g 49s 87c 46453 status
- [[Recuso Artisan's Braided Belt]]
- [[Golden Coin of the Combine Inquisition]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Luminary Animaster's Staff]]
  - [[Knight of the Echelon Bardiche]]
