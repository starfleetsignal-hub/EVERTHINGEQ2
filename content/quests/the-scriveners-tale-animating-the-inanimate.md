## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 50 [[Celestial Sandpaper]]

**Caution: if the crafting process is interrupted, you lose the materials you have gathered and they do not repopulate.  You will need to drop the quest and start over.**

For the entire quest line:

## Steps

1. Collect the [[Recipe Scroll: Myrist Trunk|**recipe scroll**]] in the [[Smiths' Gallery]] at {{waypoint 635, 411, 41}} in [[Myrist, the Great Library]] (accessed from a transporter in the [[South Wing Catalog]], which is down a hallway to the south on the second floor of the Library {{waypoint 388, 15, 169}} ![recipe scroll](images/Recipe_scroll_for_Animating_the_Inanimate.jpg)
1. Collect the [[High Quality Firing Sheet]] in the [[Smiths' Gallery]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]].
   - Note: Spawns nearby after the recipe is collected.
1. Buy [[Prepared Malachite Shards]] for 12p from [[Oleene Wanderweaver]] in the [[Smiths' Gallery]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]].
1. To find the [[Planar Binding Kit]] and the [[Latch Bindings]] take the main library 2nd floor teleporter at {{waypoint 332, 15, 173}} to the [[Crafters' Gallery]]. These are chests with green sparkles.
1. Speak with [[Ghida Borrell]] in the Smith's Gallery at {{waypoint 660, 412, 59}}. She gives you [[Embolden Spellbook]].
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Myrist Trunk]] that you collected earlier, craft a [[Non-operational Myrist Trunk]] on the [[Woodworking Table]] in the Smith's Gallery and place it at {{waypoint 654, 411, 55}}
1. Use the [[Embolden Spellbook]] [[Ghida Borrell]] gave you on the newly placed [[Non-operational Myrist Trunk]].
1. Speak with [[Ghida Borrell]]  at {{waypoint 660, 412, 59}}.
