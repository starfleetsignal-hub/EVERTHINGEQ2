## Notes

- You will be required to scribe and craft a level 10 item. Bring 50 Formless Coals as fuel.

## Steps

1. Speak to Jackal if needed.
1. Enter Corathus Descent [Signature] using the concatenated umbrite at {{waypoint 344, -67, -276}}
1. Kill all the drachnid in the area, then click on the sword at {{waypoint -104, -5, 250}}
1. Go down to the end of the room and click on the webbing.
1. Speak to Jackal at {{waypoint 29, -0, 138}}
1. Craft a Baited Pilbippi Trap at the slab at {{waypoint -38, -0, 123}} which acts as a makeshift work bench. The level 10 recipe will be in your bag.
   - Gather 3 Cave Snails. These are trackable.
   - Gather 3 Entangled Vines. These are looted from subtunarian botanic devourer. Locations include:
     - {{waypoint 21, -8, 63}} {{waypoint 15, -8, 16}} {{waypoint 1, -8, -12}} {{waypoint -11, -8, 32}} {{waypoint -20, -8, 83}}
   - Gather 2 Corathus Root Sap. Locations include:
     - {{waypoint -18, 04, 154}} {{waypoint -15, -6, 150}} {{waypoint 7, -6, 78}} {{waypoint 20, -7, 28}} {{waypoint 29, -3, 26}}
   - 50 Formless Coal.
1. Click on the yellow sparkles at {{waypoint 21, -2, 153}} to place the trap.
1. Click on the tethered pilbippi.
1. Collect 2 Thick Ropes around  {{waypoint 35, -2, 148}}.  Then go to {{waypoint -94, -5, 256}} and click the yellow sparkles, then climb down the ladder.
1. Click on the boom barrel at {{waypoint -111, -54, 248}}, then head down the hill.
1. Kill the Teir'Dal at the bottom.
1. Investigate a Teir'Dal corpse.
1. Click on the Teri'Dal Trow.
1. Approach Jackal at {{waypoint -1198, -35, -550}}
