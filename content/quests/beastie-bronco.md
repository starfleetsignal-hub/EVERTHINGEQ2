## Steps

1. Return to [[Najena's Dungeon]] and use the [[Sootfoot Bitey-Belt]] to leash a stunned Void attacker.
1. Return the leashed Void attacker to [[Blisterzonk]].
1. As you cross [[Fennin's Pass]] and near Blisterzonk, the Void attacker will break the leash becoming [[A raging Void attacker]]. Kill him and then speak to Blisterzonk.
   - If you do not kill him or return him too slow, he will rampage around briefly and return to Najena's Dungeon.
