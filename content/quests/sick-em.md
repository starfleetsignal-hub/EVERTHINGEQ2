## Notes

Sickly and regular nullfang subjugators can share spawn spots, if you don't see many sickly ones kill the regular ones and wait for respawns.

## Steps

![a sickly nullfang subjugator](images/A_sickly_nullfang_subjugator.png)
![a nullfang subjugator](images/A_nullfang_subjugator.png)
Vegk suggested a devious plan  to reduce the nullfang population in Coral Forest and Thrall Roost areas of Oogothl Sprawl.

1. Hunt sickly nullfang subjugators for 8 sickly spores - several of them around {{waypoint 105, 4, 522}}, {{waypoint -132, 25, 566}}, {{waypoint -100, 5, 657}}
   - on the last update you receive [[Sickly Spores]].
1. Infect 8 lone [[A nullfang subjugator|nullfang subjugator]]s with the [[Sickly Spores]] (they cannot be in combat)
   - this only works on lone [[A nullfang subjugator]], cannot use [[Sickly Spores]] on wandering encounters of 2 or 3 nullfang subjugators.
1. Return to [[Vegk]]
