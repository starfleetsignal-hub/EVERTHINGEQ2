## Notes

- This quest is repeatable..
- There are several possible objectives for this quest, and you will get 2 randomly chosen combinations among those. The steps above is simply an example. First objective will always be killing 10 of some mob. Second objective is usually gathering 5-8 items, like coins or teeth. Below are some possible objectives:
  - I need to collect ten [[A deepwater crocodile|deepwater crocodile]] skins.
  - I need to collect eight coins from [[A Samiel corsair|Samiel corsairs]].

## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks, in no particular order:
   - Kill 10 of a certain mob
   - Collect 5-8 of an item off of a certain mob
1. Return to [[Thara Bashirah]]

## Rewards

- At least 3g 23s 29c
- -2500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -2500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- +2500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
