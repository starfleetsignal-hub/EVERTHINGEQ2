## Steps

1. Place six scent pods outside the northern hive area:
   - {{waypoint -2003.01, -530.49, -1490.42}}
   - {{waypoint -2166.98, -522.62, -1647.45}}
   - {{waypoint -2097.72, -529.97, -1433.57}}
   - {{waypoint -1931.31, -520.80, -1704.58}}
   - {{waypoint -1836.69, -529.03, -1689.82}}
   - {{waypoint -1917.18, -528.05, -1592.95}}
1. Go to the hive entrance at {{waypoint -2109.23, -527.13, -1516.69}} and return to [[Adephala|Queen Adephala]] at {{waypoint -2622, -607, -1654}}.

## Notes

- You are not actually given items in your inventory to perform this task with. Simply click the green sparkling effect.
- Although this quest is listed as heroic in your journal, it can be completed solo.
- If the queen won't talk to you, zoom out to insure you click OK, when she tell you to stop harming her offsprings. .

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
