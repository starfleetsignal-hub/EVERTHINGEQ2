## Steps

1. Receive [[A Letter From Lieutenant Kaneth]] after agreeing to go to the militia outpost at [[N'Mar's Ascent]].
1. Deliver the letter to [[Sergeant Cornelius]] at the militia outpost at the top of [[N'Mar's Ascent]]. {{waypoint -420, 60, -1322}}
   - Take the griffin ride to get there the fastest.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 59c
- +150 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
- 5% of 1 AA point if the quest is greyed out
