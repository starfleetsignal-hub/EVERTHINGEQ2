## Steps

1. Travel to the [[Isle of Refuge]] in the [[Tranquil Sea]] zone (use any Mariner's Bell and select [[Tranquil Sea]] - it's directly right from Antonica)
   - NOTE: This is a different zone from the original Isle of Refuge zones (Queen's Colony or Outpost of the Overlord).
1. Survey the damage around [[Malvonicus' Tower]] for the missing emissary
   - East of the tower {{waypoint 849, 16, 1390}}
   - North of the tower {{waypoint 914, 18, 1313}}
   - West of the tower {{waypoint 943, 13, 1368}}
1. Find a survivor on the Isle of Refuge - [[Researcher Aillena Belzia]] is at {{waypoint 979, 11, 1386}}, click on her.![Researcher Aillena Belzia](images/Researcher_Aillena_Belzia.jpg)
1. Talk to Aillena and accept the quest she offers you
   - NOTE: If you have already completed some of Aillena's quests, she will simply disappear after you click on her. In this case, continue completing her quests listed below. After the completion of [[The Bell Tolls Four]] speak with her again and update this step of "Shattered Seas: Refuge Return"
1. Continue to assist survivors - complete the solo quest lines for the following NPCs
   - [[Researcher Aillena Belzia]]: Complete the quests:
     1. [[Crumbling Isle]]
     1. [[Running for Safety]]
     1. [[Stragglers]]
     1. [[Building a New Future]]
     1. [[A Place to Hang Your Hat]]
     1. [[Pushing Ahead]]
     1. [[The Bell Tolls Four]]
   - [[Overseer Clackwhinge]]: (click on trap door in tower) Complete the quests:
     1. [[Up Against the Wall]]
     1. [[Research and Recovery]]
     1. [[Falling Out]]
     1. [[Captain Greymast]]
1. Search the [[Isle of Refuge]] for additional information - find [[Waulon Highpebble]] near [[Sapswill's Rest]] and talk to him. {{waypoint 1189, 7, 1252}}
1. Search the northern beach for clues...
   - Search the beach at [[Crestrider's Strand]] - click the oar on the sand at {{waypoint 1030, 7, 708}}
   - Search the shore at [[Crestrider's Strand]] - click the sparkling spot in the water at {{waypoint 1016, 3, 685}}
1. Return to [[Waulon Highpebble]]
1. Speak to the representative of your city - evil characters will see [[Harbinger Gloska]] on the docks at {{waypoint 961, 7, 1466}}, good characters will see [[Murrar Shar]] at {{waypoint 973, 7, 1431}}

- At least 1p 35g
- One of the following:
  - 2 [[Electrified Adornment of Extra Attacks]]
  - 2 [[Electrified Adornment of Damaging]]
  - 2 [[Electrified Adornment of Haste]]
  - 2 [[Electrified Adornment of Heightened Power]]
