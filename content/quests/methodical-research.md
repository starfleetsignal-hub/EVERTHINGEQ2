## Steps

1. Obtain 5 more crystal samples found near the caves you've been researching. {{waypoint 1372, 5, 3944}}
1. Deliver the samples to Ishaq's pupil in Paineel: [[Inglorit Y'tiuri]]. {{waypoint 1857.80, -294.86, 3474.93}}
1. Return to Ishaq in [[The Eye of Dartain]]. {{waypoint 1832, 6, 4366}}

- At least 46g
