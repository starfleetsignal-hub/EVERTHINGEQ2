## Steps

1. Scribe the [[Schematic: Spiritshatter Drill]] recipe that Lumzig gave you, and craft a [[Spiritshatter Drill]] at a [[Forge]], [[Oversized Drillbit|using]] [[A Pair of Hand Grips|the]] [[Gnomish Trigger Array|provided]] [[Canister Holster|materials]].
1. Talk to [[Mergus Stonesledge]] {{waypoint 533, -212, 230}}in the [[The Temple of Brell|Temple of Brell]] in [[Thurgadin, City of the Coldain|Thurgadin]]. Speaking with him will complete the quest and offer the next one in the series.

## Rewards

- At least 30g 10s 55c
- [[Blessed Spiritshatter Drill]]
