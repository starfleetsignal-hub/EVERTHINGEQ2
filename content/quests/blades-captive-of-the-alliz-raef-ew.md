## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- You must have +10,000 faction with the Court of the Blades to unlock this quest. Once +20,000 faction has been achieved, this quest will no longer be available.

## Steps

1. Head to {{waypoint -240, -112, -350}} near the southwest corner of [[The Sinking Sands]] and click the cage there. You may need to kill the guards for the cage to become clickable.
1. Discover the Main Camp  {{waypoint -375.85, -117.00, -653.85}} for update
1. Rescue the Captive Merchant on the island in the Alliz Raef Oasis (near [[Twin Tears]] {{waypoint -508, -133, -790}}).
1. When you hail him four solo Alliz Raef will spawn. Kill them and hail him again.
1. Escort the Captive Merchant to [[Mahir Lu'ay]] for coin and faction.

## Rewards

- 8g 57s
- +7,500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -7,500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -7,500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**
