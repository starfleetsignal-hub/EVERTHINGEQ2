## Steps

- This is a subquest for [[The Word of the Harbingers - Qeynos]] and [[The Word of the Harbingers - Freeport]] and can only be attempted while you are on that quest.

1. Enter the instance [[Keven's Trial (Zone)]].  The entrances are in [[Antonica]] for good-aligned or [[Commonlands]] for evil-aligned.
   - Antonica:  Click on a boulder up against the mountains north of [[Archer's Wood]].  {{waypoint -902, -5, 440}}
   - Commonlands: Click on a boulder up against the mountains south of [[Southern Crossroads Savanna]].  {{waypoint -848, -47, 686}}
1. Find the 6 starving wolf pups and lead them to food.
   - Kill the starving deer (even con, vv).  The corpses will not disappear.  Speak to one of the wolf pups, and it will follow you like a pet.  Lead the wolf pup to the deer corpse.  You'll have to position yourself so that the wolf pup ends up standing inside the corpse.  If positioned right the quest will update and the pup and deer corpse will both disappear.
1. Return to Field Priest Keven.

## Rewards

- [[Medallion of Kaldrahir]]
