## Notes

- This quest is available after completing Narezi's quest [[Yon with the Wild]]. Completion of this quest opens Narezi's repeatable quest [[More Healthy Pursuits]].
- Completing this quest unlocks Kith giving rides to Yon Gorroth Obelisk.

## Steps

1. Speak to Narezi in next hut {{waypoint 465, 81, -568}}
1. **Right-click Kith** next to her and "Scratch Head" to see if he will help you find missing hunters.
   - Follow him, until at {{waypoint 455, 80, -592}} he warps to The Gnarls.
1. Catch up with Kith in The Gnarls - go to {{waypoint 180, 63, -515}}.
1. Speak to Kith.
   - Follow him until at {{waypoint 152, 59, -520}} he warps again.
1. Catch up with Kith in Rot Reek Bog - go to {{waypoint -394, 18, -81}}.
1. Speak to Kith in Rot Reek Bog
   - After he warps yet again at {{waypoint -371, 19, -48}}, catch up to him in Writher Ruins at {{waypoint -301, 51, 172}} You will be attacked at this spot by a ravaging husked voidmaw. Kill him and accept [[Grathok Decaying Pincer]] from loot window that opens.
1. Speak to Kith at the attack site and he will port with you back to Darou village.
1. Speak to Narezi at {{waypoint 465, 81, -568}}

## Rewards

- 3 x [[Small Spiked Void Crystal]]
- [[Void Adventurer's Wage]] - unpacks into 15x [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
- At least 5p 86g 23s 58c
