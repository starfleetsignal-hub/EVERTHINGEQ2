## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

Building and maintaining a camp requires a great deal of work and materials. The herald of Tarew Marr has asked for my assistance in gathering Ihe needed supplies.

1. Gather the requested supplies
   - Collect 3 crimson [[Direbeak Feather|direbeak feathers]] from [[A crimson direbeak|crimson direbeaks]] found in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] {{waypoint 504.66, 162.19, 0.32}}
     - *Note: Drops in [[Treasure Chest]] of [[A crimson direbeak]]*
   - Gather or purchase [[Trystine Gem|trystine gems]] found in [[Ballads of Zimara]] areas
     - *Common harvest from gem cluster*
     - *Can buy off broker*
     - *Just having them in inventory when you get the quest will count*
1. Return to [[Herald of the Fathom Lord]] {{waypoint -275.14, -0.13, 51.21}} in [[The Mystic Lake]]
