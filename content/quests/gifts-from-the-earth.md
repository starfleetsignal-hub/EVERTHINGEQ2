*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Head to the [[Nektulos Forest Station]] (ding arrival at the station to make mounds active).
1. Harvest the mounds of disturbed soil around the griffin station until you find an [[Unearthed Ree chest]]. Note: Ignore the instruction in the quest journal to search south of the station.
1. Return to Ventar.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 59c
- +70 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
