## Steps

1. Speak with [[Phaerdriira Z'Zea'Val]] in [[Darklight Wood]]
1. Slay [[Elghinyrress Do'Texith]] at Little Neriak behind the pavilion tent in [[Fallen Gate]], at {{waypoint 203, 18, -94}}
1. Return to Phaerdriira Z'Zea'Val

## Rewards

- +3000 faction with **The City of Neriak**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Qeynos**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Freeport**
- -4000 faction with **The City of Kelethin**
