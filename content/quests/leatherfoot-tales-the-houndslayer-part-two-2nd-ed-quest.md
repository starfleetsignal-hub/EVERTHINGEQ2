## Steps

1. Slay 25 [[A rabid pup|rabid pups]]. {{waypoint -19, -10, 44}}
1. Read the book.
1. Visit the following locations:
   - [[Brushfoot's General Store]] {{waypoint -46, -10, -170}}
   - [[Roundbelly's General Store]] {{waypoint -193, -8, 107}}
   - [[Middlehill's General Store]] {{waypoint -207, -11, -124}}
1. Read the book.
1. Slay 20 [[A deceptive thief|deceptive thieves]] or [[A crazed thief|crazed thieves]]. Although the quest text does not say it, killing [[A corrupted thief|corrupted thieves]] and [[A corrupted brigand|corrupted brigands]] will also give quest updates. {{waypoint -201, -9, -76}}
1. Read the book to obtain your rewards.
