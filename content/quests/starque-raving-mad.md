## Steps

1. Rescue Starque, the deino rider - he's tied to a stake at {{waypoint -1204, 120, -575}}. Left click him.
1. Escort Starque back to Bawgava.  The quest updates next to Zedj.
1. Speak to Zedj

## Rewards

- At least 1p 29g 60s
- One of the following:
  - [[The Fierce Hook]]
  - [[Earthshaker's Spine]]
  - [[Zedj's Overwhelming Response]]
  - [[Starque's Mad Dasher]]
  - [[Starque Bloodletter]]
