## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
  - Quest becomes repeatable after initial completion. No Scarred Dinars reward on repeats.
  - ** Note this quest is not always after the same items.**

## Steps

![Veeshan worship relic](images/Veeshan_worship_relic.png)
![](images/Veeshan_Prayer_Tablet.png)

1. Collect and hunt the following
   - Collect either
     - 10 Veeshan worship relics or
       - *golden dragon busts around [[Warming Warfields]], that whole green area to the North and Northwest of camp. This area is full of them {{waypoint -419, 35, -587}}*
     - 10 Prayer Tablets
       - *they can be found near {{waypoint -332, 31, -664}}. They are Light Blue rectangles.*
   - Cull/Kill either
     - 12 [[A mystvine flutterer]] in the same area, or
     - 12 [[Featherwing|Featherwings]]
1. Speak to [[Thekror]] at [[Hoarfang Ridge]] camp

## Rewards

- 40 x [[Scarred Dinar]] -- *only the first time*
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- One of the following:
  - [[Veeshan Prayer Tablet]] house item
  - [[Veeshan Worship Relic]] house item
