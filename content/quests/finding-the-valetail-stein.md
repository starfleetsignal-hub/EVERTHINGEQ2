## Steps

1. Fredwyn Goldsyrup has asked me to retrieve a great stein that was lost to a drunken goblin in Runnyeye.  I'll do my best to retrieve it, but I hope it is worth the effort.
1. Go to [[Runnyeye]], and kill [[The Runnyeye Brewmeister]] {{waypoint 45, -16, -177}}. (His placeholder is [[A Runnyeye brewer]].)
1. Speak with Fredwyn Goldsyrup again.
1. Go retrieve his tools inside of his house at {{waypoint -655, -4, 195}}. They are in a cask in the back of the house interior.
1. Return to Fredwyn to finish the quest.

- At least 2g 69s 57c

  - [[Harnessed Soul Sapphire]]
