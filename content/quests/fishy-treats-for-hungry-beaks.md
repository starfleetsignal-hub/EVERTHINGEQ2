## Notes

- This is one of five "training sessions" you must complete in order to complete the quest [[Dream Weaving]] to receive a [[Gryphon Call]].
- You can only complete one of these five training sessions every 18 hours.

## Steps

1. Do one of the following:
   - Fish for 8 [[Iceclad Cutlassfish]] in the waters around [[Lodizal's Dock in Icy Fingers]] or near the Remnants of Ring War.
   - Buy 8 cutlassfish from a broker. If you have them in your pack when the quest is selected, it will auto-update as well. The cutlassfish are consumed in the process.
1. The quest auto-updates and auto-completes.

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- Choice of [[Frost Gryphon]] or [[Boreal Gryphon]] on completion of the final of the 5 quests.
