## Notes

- This quest is repeatable., however the quest journal does not indicate this.

## Steps

![The Bough of Tenderness looks like a log.](images/Rekindle_the_Love_quest_-_bough_of_tenderness.jpg)

1. Collect a *Bough of Tenderness*, a harvestable log in [[The Boughs of Tenderness]]. Known spawn locations are:
   - {{waypoint 181, 12, -139}}
   - {{waypoint 210, 15, -197}}
1. Loot [[Spark of passion]], a corpse drop from [[Sprite of passion]], an [[Underconned]] non-heroic mob, which spawns when you walk by the trees near [[The Pool of Devotion]]. {{waypoint 91, 2, -155}}
   - All players in the group get the update after spark of passion is dropped, assuming they have harvested the log previously.
1. Interact with the *Flame of Love* in [[Love's Eternal Flame]]. {{waypoint -105, -6, -105}}

## Rewards

- [[Chalice of Loving Incense]]
