## Steps

1. Kill 15 skeletal orcs located in [[The Wailing Caves]].
   - The easiest to kill are the [[A Shin`Ree apprentice|Shin`Ree apprentices]] and [[A Shin`Ree pawn|pawns]] (level 13) just inside the zone, but every type of orc skeleton in the Wailing Caves updates.
1. Return to Ronam Olansk

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Apprentice Magi's Wand]] (All Mages)
  - [[Enchanter's Dirk]] (All Mages)
  - [[Crossroad Worker's Dagger]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Fencer's Guard Blade]] (Crusaders, Warriors}}
  - [[Aspiring Priest's Mace]] (All Priests)
  - [[Chakram of Exaggerated Motion]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Forest Protector's Long Blade]] (Druids, Channelers)
