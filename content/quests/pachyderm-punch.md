## Steps

1. Slay [[A plains elephant|plains elephants]] (level 14-15) for 5 slightly uncommon updates.  They are found on the south/southeastern shores of [[Pride Lake]] {{waypoint 435, 0, -315}}. Only plains elephants will count, the regular elephants everywhere else will not.
- Return to Mooshga.

## Rewards

- At least 4s 32c
