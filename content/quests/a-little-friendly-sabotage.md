## Steps

1. I must break the first spinning wheel in [[Erronson's Furs and Leathers]] at {{waypoint 71, 153, -127}}.
1. I must break the second spinning wheel in Erronson's.
1. I must break the third spinning wheel in Erronson's.
1. I must return to [[Commissioner Varla Z'Velran|Varla]] to report my progress.

  - +250 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
