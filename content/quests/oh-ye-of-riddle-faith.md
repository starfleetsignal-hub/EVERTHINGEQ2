## Steps

![](images/Sphinx_Feathers.jpg)

1. Gather and claim the following
   - Gather 6 sphinx feathers from the rugs {{waypoint -477, 162, -780}}, {{waypoint -585, 131, -822}}, {{waypoint -752, 170, -737}}, on the platforms in [[Pakiat Bluffs]]
   - Gather 4 tuffs of sphinx fur from sphinx brushes on pillows on platforms in [[Pakiat Bluffs]].![](images/SphinxBrush.png)
     - The brushes respawn quickly. Just camp a safe one. There are 3 non guarded brushes near {{waypoint -480, 165, -850}}
   - Claim the voice of a sphinx - Speak to [[Meskhenar]] at {{waypoint -878, 82, -453}}.
1. Return to [[Pannaxxun]] at {{waypoint -196.60, 573.57, -549.72}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- [[Ash of Equinox]]
- +1500 faction with **House Vahla**
- -1500 faction with **House Yrzu**
- -1500 faction with **Pride Pakiat**
