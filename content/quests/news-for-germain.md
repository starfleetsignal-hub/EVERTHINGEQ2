## Steps

1. Harvest a pack on the edge of the pond. {{waypoint 1120, 0, -660}}
1. Examine the pack in your inventory.
1. Right-click and search bushes until you get a leaf sample. {{waypoint 1162, -22, -740}}
1. Return to Acting Lieutenant Germain for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 92c
- +125 faction with **The Concordium**
