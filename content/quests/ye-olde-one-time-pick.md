## Notes

This quest has not been discovered in the last 3 server creations and is presumed removed from the game.

## Steps

1. Find the handle of the military pick, a random update from any type of corporeal undead (skeletons, zombies, etc.) in [[Fallen Gate]].
1. Find the ore used in the construction of the weapon, four random updates from any type of undead in [[Fallen Gate]].
1. Find the original haft or a replacement for this pick, again a random update from any type of undead in [[Fallen Gate]].
