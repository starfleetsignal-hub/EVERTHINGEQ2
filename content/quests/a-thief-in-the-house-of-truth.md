## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- Reading the story "The Tale of Mal'Ahkt" in the [[1001 Tales of Maj'Dul]], found  next to [[Antiquitor Kantus Mor'Tael]] in [[Maj'Dul]] at {{waypoint -239, 135, -7}}, will allow you to skip the [[The Guardian of Ank'Nief|Guardian of Ank'Nief]] during the final step of this quest.
- This cannot be done in a group as the keys are unique and only one set is given per instance. You need all three keys in your inventory to open the case. Make sure you disband and go in separately or you'll have to do it again!

## Steps

1. Enter the [[The Court of Truth (Quest Instance)|Court of Truth]] via the sewer grate at {{waypoint -147, 150, 19}}.
1. Complete the following within 10 minutes:
   1. Kill and loot [[Historian Da'ran]]: {{waypoint -0.06, -0.14, 2.32}}
   1. Kill and loot [[Historian Mandala]]: {{waypoint 17.33, -0.14, 5.16}}
   1. Kill and loot [[Historian Fa'zin]]: {{waypoint -33.90, -18.33, 47.13}}
   1. Examine the glass casing in the large room on the bottom floor. {{waypoint -62, -18, 38}}
   1. Slay [[The Guardian of Ank'Nief]] to exit, unless you chose to 'speak the name of the guardian' while inspecting the glass casing. This option is only made available if the directions in the 'Notes' section above were followed.
      - If you have trouble with this step, please see the Talk page for more ideas.
1. Exit through the door you came in (/way 7,1,33) to complete the quest. Do NOT exit through the main door. The knocker of the right door says "Servant's Entrance" when you mouse over it. The WRONG door says "Maj'Dul"
