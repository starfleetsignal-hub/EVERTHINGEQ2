This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Enter a [[Maj'Dul Residence]].
1. Open the chest next to the bed in the third room. {{waypoint 42, 0, 5}}
1. Kill the four [[An ensorcelled defender|ensorcelled defenders]] that spawn.
1. Kill [[A spellbound guardian]] in the second room.
1. Kill one of the [[A fascinated sentinel|fascinated sentinels]] that has spawned in the first room.
1. Return to any Tears grifter for your reward.

## Reward

- At least 3g 23s 29c
- One of the following:
  - 6 [[Blades insignia token|Blades insignia tokens]]
  - 6 [[Coin insignia token|Coin insignia tokens]]
  - 6 [[Truth insignia token|Truth insignia tokens]]
