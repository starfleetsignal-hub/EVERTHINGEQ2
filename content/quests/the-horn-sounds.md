## Steps

The four giants spawn immediately after blowing the horn.

   - [[Curn]]
   - [[Ikro]]
   - [[Rem]]
   - [[Vihr]]

Accept the quest quickly (the giants attack you after a few seconds).
If you killed the giants before accepting the quest, you need to decline or delete the quest and blow the horn again.

## Rewards

- At least 12g 57s 51c
- Choice of the following:
  - [[Curn Eye Necklace]]
  - [[Ikro Eye Necklace]]
  - [[Rem Eye Necklace]]
  - [[Vihr Eye Necklace]]
