## Steps

I've promised Talthenis to do what I can to help preserve the wondrous ancient ruins of Takish'Hiz and the history it contains and honor those whose fates were sealed here.

- Destroy 10 inert core fragments in the Geomancer's Stronghold
- Collect 10 historical scrolls found within the Chamber of Chronicles
- Dispatch 12 pillaging Renda'Dal from the Elddar Exchange
- Dispatch 12 pillaging Wards of Dawn from the Den of Dawn
