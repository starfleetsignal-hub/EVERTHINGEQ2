## Steps

1. Slay [[Commander Ralio]] at {{waypoint 1486, -14, 218}}. The heroic dervishes there are placeholders, so kill them until Commander Ralio shows up.
1. Return to Captain Vertas for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 91c
- [[Vertas Lieutenant's Insignia]] - replaces your [[Vertas Corporal's Insignia]]
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Band of Theoretic Design]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Loop of Theoretic Design]]
  - [[Ring of Theoretic Design]]
  - [[Ringlet of Theoretic Design]]
