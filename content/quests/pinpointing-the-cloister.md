## Description

Although the leaders of the League of Freethinkers have successfully created a portal which enables them to enter Evernight Abbey, they still have not yet determined the abbey's precise location. I've been tasked to placer a descrying beacon deep inside the halls of the abbey so that the Freethinkers' diviners may attempt to ascertain the true location of the Libant's domicile.

## Steps

1. Place the received [[Descrying Beacon]] in the center of the [[Cloister of Liturgy]] within [[Evernight Abbey]].  Loc is {{waypoint 1.63, -17.86, -361.27}}
1. Return to [[Tanya Alvernon]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- 1 [[Void Shard]] x1
