## Requisite

Must be Aerakyn: part 2 of the 3-part Aerakyn racial questline (evil version)

## Steps

1. Enter [[Temple Street: Vault of Necromechanica]] from South Freeport {{waypoint -176, -37, 322}} and speak to [[Psellic]] {{waypoint -6, 3, 108}}
1. Enter [[Beggar's Court: Sweat-Stained Cobbles]] from [[North Freeport (POI)|North Freeport]] {{waypoint -149, -50, -376}} and listen to Psellic give a speech in [[The Work Yard]] {{waypoint -17, -5, 18}}
1. Click the blue sparkles and give your own speech
1. Enter [[Scale Yard: Traitors' End]]  from South Freeport {{waypoint -65, -25, 285}} and speak to Psellic at the end of the dock {{waypoint -1, -4, 108}}. Choose whether to free him or fight him (Wizard, your level, solo)
1. Speak to Plex in West Freeport

## Rewards

- At least 10g 97c @ lvl 55
