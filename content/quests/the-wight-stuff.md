## Steps

1. Harvest 6 Zek scrub roots.
   - Zek scrub roots can occur throughout the wastes. See the discussion page for suggested locations. They are a group update. They look like small dark green bushes that can be seen from the air if you are not too high.
1. Hunt 8 toxic pine grubs.
   - Click tree stumps in [[Tallon Field]], then slay the [[A toxic pine grub]] that pops.
1. Speak to [[Oarwille Wight]].
1. Kill the [[A Scourge orc|Scourge orc]] that has wandered into the barn.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- +500 faction with **The Green Hoods**
