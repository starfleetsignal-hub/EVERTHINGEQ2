## Steps

1. Obtain the following
   - a Velium-Infused Petrified Driftwood from [[Vitoth of the Gnarled Roots]].
   - a Velium-Studded Exquisite Walrus Hide from [[Chieftain Maferi]] or [[Grand Shaman Grungizt]].
   - 30 Blessed Prayer Tablets. These are guaranteed drops from the following bosses:
     - [[Joggu the Steadfast]]
     - [[Sommumun the Frenzied]]
     - [[Bucko, Loyal Pet to Maferi]]
     - [[Barlsbit the Scavenger]]
     - [[Viterol the Voracious]]
     - [[Monuseru, Titan of the Depths]]

## Notes

All of the above quest items also have a chance at dropping from T1 trash mobs.
