## Steps

- You have 12 minutes to catalogue the following:
1. Catalogue [[A magmitre sludge]] in [[Wurmscale Barrens]]  {{waypoint -1.76, 102.73, 739.33}}
1. Catalogue [[A pyrocrux]] in [[Wurmscale Barrens]] {{waypoint 29.16, 123.85, 717.92}} (In the same lava pool as the Magmitre Sludge, I could target it from higher up)
1. Catalogue [[A magma elemental]] in [[Wurmscale Barrens]] {{waypoint -1.76, 102.73, 739.33}} (Under the lava)
1. Catalogue [[An ashblaze drake]] in [[Wurmscale Barrens]]  {{waypoint -1.76, 102.73, 739.33}} (Near the Magmitre Sludge)
1. Catalogue [[An Obulus sokokar hatchling]] between [[Wurmscale Barrens]] and [[Grunt's Pass]] {{waypoint -132.40, 123.18, 499.67}}
1. Catalogue [[A toothed gnasher]] in [[Grunt's Pass]] {{waypoint -340.96, 49.79, 495.35}}
1. Catalogue [[A deadly stoonleer]] in [[Giant's Walk]] {{waypoint -603.06, 54.06, 343.78}}
1. Catalogue [[A deadthly spined succulent]] in [[Giant's Walk]] {{waypoint -512.05, 83.12, 242.73}}
1. Catalogue [[A drake of the grove]] in [[Garden's of Nye'Caelona]] {{waypoint -498.18, -65.90, -294.02}}
1. Catalogue [[A garden geode]] in [[Garden's of Nye'Caelona]]  {{waypoint -617.46, -85.34, -400.08}}
1. Catalogue [[An Obulus garden trap]] in [[Garden's of Nye'Caelona]] {{waypoint -569.27, -78, -346.53}}
1. Catalogue [[A forest slug succulent]] in [[Warslik's Woods]] {{waypoint -222, -180.12, -564.80}}
1. Catalogue [[An Obulus jibsnapper]] near the [[Ocean of Tears]] {{waypoint 8.43, -242.27, -877.79}}
1. Catalogue [[An Obulus leatherback]] near the [[Ocean of Tears]] {{waypoint 49.30, -241.98, -898.92}} (On the same beach as the Jibsnappers)
1. Catalogue [[A frontier riverfly]] near [[Warslik's River]] {{waypoint 148.90, -242.88, -501.97}}
1. Catalogue [[A luclinite cluster]] near [[The Luclinite Chunk]] {{waypoint 232.56, -239.83, -279.58}}
1. Catalogue [[An Obulus vapor entity]] at [[Frontier Falls]] {{waypoint 328.22, -228.13, -81.47}}
   - *Note: You really want to end at the Frontier Falls because next you must kill 4 named mobs.  They are non-agro mobs until they are catalogued.  The area they spawn and move around in is in blue on your map.*
1. Catalogue and then kill [[Globulus]] at [[Frontier Falls]]
1. Catalogue and then kill [[Chalpdog]] at [[Chalp]]
1. Catalogue and then kill [[Stonemaw]] at [[Grunt's Pass]]
1. Catalogue and then kill [[Goldwing]] at [[Giant's Walk]]  had to fly way up to a ledge

## Rewards

- 5 [[Encrusted metal key]]
- At least 1p 53g 9s
