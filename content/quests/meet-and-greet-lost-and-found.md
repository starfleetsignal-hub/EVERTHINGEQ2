## Notes

Non repeatable.

## Steps

1. Speak with  [[Grimar]] at {{waypoint -32, 5, 372}}
1. Find the missing child while using the W.H.A.T.   Cast your W.H.A.T. V1, click the hand (a magic harvester) that spawns next to you and possess it. You will only see the child when you are transformed into that hand form. Go to {{waypoint 123, 14, 466}}  quest updates when you get close to "lost teen".
1. You can cancel [[W.H.A.T. V1]]  or walk back to Port and talk to  [[Grimar]] {{waypoint -32, 5, 372}}

## Rewards

- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
- +2000 faction with **Port Woe**
