## Description

*Both the exile of Stygian Threshold and the speaker of Kralet Penumbra stand sentry against anyone who dares enter their domains. I dare, and I shall overcome these foes!*

## Steps

- Destroy enemy targets within Stygian Threshold and Kralet Penumbra.
1. Slay the exiled one
   - [[Mawz Harak]] in [[Stygian Threshold (Heroic)]]
1. Defeat the one who speaks
   - [[Xacx-Kahda]] in [[Kralet Penumbra: Rise to Power (Heroic)]]
