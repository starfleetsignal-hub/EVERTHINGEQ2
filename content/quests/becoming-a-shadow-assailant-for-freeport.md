## Notes

The good version of this quest is entitled [[Becoming a Shadow Assailant for Qeynos]].

## Steps

1. I must take arms up against the challenging Void beasts that are using the Voide Tempests as portals into Norrath.

1. Go to a zone of appropriate level for you and find a Void Tempest
   - You can not mentor for this quest.
   - A list of storm locations can be found under [[Tempest affected Creatures List]]
1. Kill 4 [[Dark seepage]] and/or [[Dark horror]], no dings for updates, just kill.
   - Spawn one at a time.
1. Kill the [[Void Tempest]] and collect the [[Void Storm Shred]].
1. Return to [[Librarian Motius Feldarn]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s 40c
- 1 [[Academy of Arcane Science's Note of Service]]
- Title: Shadow Assailant
