## Materials Required

- 4 [[Jumping Creeper]]  *(rare)*
- 4 [[Primal Luclizite Shard]] *(rare)*
- 228 [[Hornbeam Spruce Log]]
- 36 [[Luclizite Cluster]]
- 64 [[Radiant Algae]]
- 104 [[Slow Creeping Root]]
- 120 [[Coruscating Coal]]
- 100 [[Coruscating Sandpaper]]

## Steps

1. Enter [[Echo Caverns]] and fly up to the tunnel entrance {{waypoint -101, 250, -42}}.
1. Take left fork in tunnel {{waypoint -178.47, 274.39, -66.47}}, to the Chasm Fiend Cliffs.
1. Look slightly up for the circular dark grey structure. Fly under it and slightly to the right to  {{waypoint -336.79, 193.87, -414.87}}.
   - Note:  The ribbon will mislead you. Ignore the ribbon and fly straight Northeast, then when you get to the cave wall, fly Northwest and slightly down. The ribbon will catch up and take you to the cave entrance for the Savage Weald.
1. The entrance for [[Savage Weald]] is at {{waypoint -504, 195, -429}}
1. Head to the transport pad at {{waypoint -583, 269, -813}}  The little guys here are non-aggro. Use the transport pad to fly down to the forest floor.
1. Find [[Erdani Ruilep]] in the [[Savage Weald]].
   - Make your way to the river and cross at {{waypoint -381, 59, -575}}
   - Follow the  path up and to the left to find [[Erdani Ruilep]] at  {{waypoint -419, 96, -316}}.
1. Obtain 3 bioluminescent fungus.
   - *Trackable harvest, **dormant emanating mushroom*** ![Dormant Emanating Mushrooms](images/Dormant_Emanating_Mushrooms.jpg).
1. Return the fungus to Erdani.
   - *You will be given 3 **Will of the Weald** travel potions, which allow you to return directly to Erdani.*
   - *You can (and should) buy more travel potions from the nearby merchant Vuchoz.*
1. Either collect a large number (50) of the fungus or make a deal with [[Piccolo]] {{waypoint -65, 177, -105}} in the [[Echo Caverns]] area. Note: All you have to do is talk to Piccolo and you will get all fifty, then use Will of the Weald to return.
1. Return to Erdani.
1. Collect 10 corpsecreeper flower seeds from **grimling corpses** in [[Savage Weald]] ![Grimling Corpse](images/Grimling_Corpse.jpg) (trackable harvest) around [[Hollowshade Moor]] {{waypoint -433, 11, -111}}.
   - *You can get 2 updates off each corpse.*
   - For low level adventurers try {{waypoint -84.57, 20.31, -244.57}}
   - Found one at
     - {{waypoint -292.79, 61.05, -279.29}}
     - {{waypoint -276.25, 76.82, -332.50}}
     - {{waypoint -270.56, 60.92, -386.46}}
     - {{waypoint -305, 12, -234}}
     - {{waypoint -258, 63, -458}}
     - {{waypoint -339, 10, -200}}
1. Return the seeds to [[Erdani Ruilep]].
1. Returne to [[Echo Caverns]].
1. Speak to [[Piccolo]] {{waypoint -65, 177, -105}} in the [[Echo Caverns]] area about potential light sources.
1. Return to [[City of Fordel Midst]].
1. Speak to [[Serisia Darling]] in the [[Bank of the Bazaar]] {{waypoint -278, -1, 68}}.
1. Create 4 [[Dimly-Lit Firefly Lamp|Dimly-Lit Firefly Lamps]] using a [[Work Bench]].
   - If required, there is a fuel Merchant and multiple tables in the [[Great Artisan Hall]] at {{waypoint -111.41, -1.64, 5.27}}.
1. Return to [[Erdani Ruilep]] in the Savage Weald with the disappointing news and offer the lamps.
   - Use one of the [[Will of the Weald]] obtained at Step 2 of this quest..
1. Wait for an opportunity to find out more about light in the [[Grimling Forest]] area.
   - *Check mail for summons from [[Serisia Darling|Serisia]] (may need to wait a while, or zone, or both), and collect the attached [[Letter from Serisia]]:*<br>*From: Serisia Darling<br>Subject: Away from prying eyes<br>My apologies for what I can only assume was a very awkward impression of our last meeting. I do have knowledge of the plant you seek, but cannot speak of it near any sort of local ears. Meet me in the Bazaar above the arena and I can answer any questions you have. Please make haste, as I can only monitor the area for so long before I will need to return to my assigned location in the Moors.<br>I await our meeting.<br>Serisia*
1. Meet [[Serisia Darling]] at the [[The Palaestra Memoria|Palaestra Memoria]] in the [[Bazaar]] {{waypoint 248, 0, 132}}.  You will receive [[Tightly wrapped plant seeds and clippings]].
1. Return the plants to [[Erdani Ruilep]].
1. Create 4 x [[Luclizite Hanging Vine Lantern|Luclizite Hanging Vine Lanterns]]. Crafting Station next to Erdani.
1. Return the lanterns to [[Erdani Ruilep]].
1. Place the lanterns around the edges of the farm area by clicking the corner vine sprouts.
   - {{waypoint -422, 96, -340}}
   - {{waypoint -427, 96, -331}}
   - {{waypoint -415, 96, -320}}
   - {{waypoint -408, 94, -328}}
1. Return to Erdani.
1. Travel to [[City of Shar Vahl]] via the entrance at {{waypoint -825, 56, 137}} and deliver the gift to [[Shoraka Lano]] {{waypoint -215, 32, 57}} near [[Celebration House]].
