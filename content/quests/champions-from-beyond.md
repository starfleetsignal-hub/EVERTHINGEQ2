- Players can only be offered this mission from Anex after they have completed the final signature quest [[Age's End: Shattered Fate]] (Raid). *A new 'third option' of dialogue referring to Kerafrym will become available.*

- Kill any of the following Ancestral Spirits at the end of any [[Chains of Eternity]] and [[Tears of Veeshan]] heroic dungeons.
  - [[Arch Confessor Yonbry]]
  - [[Baddi Waca]]
  - [[Challenger Aknos]]
  - [[Grand Armsmith Korin]]
  - [[Seta Bakindo]]
  - [[Sentinel Anderin]]
  - [[Zordakalicus Ragefire (Heroic)|Zordakalicus Ragefire]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- 5 [[Draconic Etyma]]

## Notes

A random champion will be chosen from the list.
