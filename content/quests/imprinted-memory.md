## Notes

1. You will need to speak [[Volant]] to speak to Tento.
1. If doing this in the Heroic version have one groupmember ONLY click through the dialogue and lead the brothers.

## Steps

1. Speak to Tento Felfeather to receive the repeatable quest [[Imprinting History Lesson]]. ![](images/Quest_Giver_and_Entrance_Book_Portal.png)
1. Click the podium to zone into [[Imprinted Memory: Origins of the Felfeather (Event Solo)|Imprinted Memory: Origins of the Felfeather [Event Solo]]].
   - Can also be done in the Heroic version of this zone.
1. Complete the instance (see [[Imprinted Memory: Origins of the Felfeather (Event Solo)]] for walkthrough)
1. Speak to Tento Felfeather.
