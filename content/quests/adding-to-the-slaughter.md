## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[A Haoaeran skyscreecher|Haoaeran skyscreechers]] in and around [[Thulwun Station West]]. {{waypoint 1267, 189, 215}}
1. Return to [[Secondary Terkenil Niba'Xi]].

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Symbol of Strength]] (Fighters)
  - [[Symbol of Intelligence]] (Mages)
  - [[Symbol of Wisdom]] (Priests)
  - [[Mark of Agility]] (Scouts)
