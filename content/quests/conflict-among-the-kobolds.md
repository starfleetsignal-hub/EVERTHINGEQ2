## Steps

![](images/A_strange_concotion.jpg)

1. Around [[Grikbar Crater]] in camps {{waypoint -421, 92, 892}}:
   - Kill 3 [[A Grikbar battler|Grikbar battlers]]
   - Kill 3 [[A Grikbar scamp|Grikbar scamps]]
   - Kill 3 [[A Grikbar warrior|Grikbar warriors]]
   - Sample of the Grikbar concoction located at one of their camps (will be an bottle looking harvestable labeled "A Strange Concoction" {{waypoint -423, 92, 879}})
1. Around [[Mazkeen Crater]] in camps {{waypoint -706, 129, 629}}
   - Kill 3 [[A Mazkeen raider|Mazkeen raiders]]
   - Kill 3 [[A Mazkeen savage|Mazkeen savages]]
   - Kill 3 [[A Mazkeen warrior|Mazkeen warriors]]
   - Sample of the Mazkeen concoction located at one of their camps (will be an bottle looking harvestable labelled "A Strange Concoction" {{waypoint -697.42, 130.10, 608.73}} )
1. Return to [[Watchman Plarg]] for your reward.
