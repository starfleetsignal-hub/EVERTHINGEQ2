## Notes

- The 'Argent Sanctuary' half of this quest can be done concurrently with side-quest [[Here Ghost Nothing]].
- [[Storm's A Brewin']] can be done after [[Vaashkaani: Golden Rule (Solo)|Vaashkaani: Golden Rule [Solo]]], you will have to return to Priest Oo'Rah after the instance the first time.
- Fear, Mesmerize, Stifle and Stun runes (Tier 0 e.g. [[Tishan's Zimaran Rune: Adamant Defiance (Tier 0)|Tishan's Zimaran Rune: Adamant Defiance [Tier 0]]]) can be taken from [[Tishan's Lockbox (Splendor Sky Aerie)|Tishan's Lockbox]] which is located in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] at {{waypoint -328, 114, 567}}.
- All 'Vaashkaani' instances are entered via the portal in [[Auric Palisades]] at {{waypoint -485.76, 160.97, -603.85}}.
- As of 1/14/24, it appears General Ra'Zaal encounter has an AOE stun, rather than a stifle.
- On 1/14/24 was able to kill Isos without addressing the adds, just had to diligently switch targets back to Isos.

## Steps

1. On [[Auric Palisades]] enter [[Vaashkaani: Argent Sanctuary (Solo)|Vaashkaani: Argent Sanctuary [Solo]]].
   1. Search for a sign of the imprisoned virtuosa
      - *Begin with a [[Tishan's Zimaran Rune: Adamant Defiance (Tier 0)|Tishan's Zimaran Rune: Adamant Defiance [Tier 0]]] (Stun rune) in the Waist.*
      - *Killing MOBs gives faction negative Vaashkaani and positive Isles of Wisdom.*
      1. Clear the first room of all metalbound behemoths
         - [[An unwell wisher|Unwell wishers]] at {{waypoint -37.82, 2.96, 52.10}} and {{waypoint -16.06, 2.96, -52.55}}.
         - You could use a dispel [Save]
         - You look tasty [Attack]
      1. Kill [[Tazir Tanziri]] roams around {{waypoint 111.31, 3.47, 2.69}}
         - *[[Tazir Tanziri]] becomes invulnerable at 50% until shards are attacked.*
         - Silver shards give him 25% damage taken reduction each.![a silver shard](images/A_silver_shard.jpg)*Shards to be destroyed form in the long corridor behind/in front of [[Tazir Tanziri]]*
      1. Make your way through the zone to the room at {{waypoint 212.01, 3.43, 38.44}} to update the quest
   1. Continue searching Vaashkaani for signs of the imprisoned virtuosa
      1. Kill [[Sansobog]] first, then [[Akharys]], use Stun immunity rune for this fight
         - [[An unwell wisher|Unwell wishers]] at {{waypoint 225.13, 3.43, -63.83}} and {{waypoint 274.76, 3.43, 61.51}} . Save or Attack.
         - There are two chromaclouds floating overhead in the room.  Killing them before the named can preclude unnecessary dirt naps.
      1. Make your way through the zone to the room at {{waypoint 265.88, 3.43, -59.63}}.
      1. Kill [[Uah'Lu the Unhallowed]]
      1. Click the portal to the Aviary {{waypoint 296.69, 3.65, 0.33}}
   1. Don't give up searching Vaashkaani for signs of the imprisoned virtuosa
      1. Clear the mobs in the Aviary.
      1. Kill [[Xuxuquaxul]]  {{waypoint 89.74, 11.43, -182.00}}
         - *Casts Fear*
      1. Hail and kill [[General Ra'Zaal]] {{waypoint 96.42, 7.94, -311.63}}
         - *Casts AOE <s>Stifle</s> Stun*
         - Use H.O.s on the  two Ghoul guards to debuff
         - *Summons adds that provide stacks. Sixth stack causes insta-kill*
         - Use your heroic opportunities on the adds or they can't be killed OR just  DPS the General before the stacks get to 6.
      1. once you killed [[General Ra'Zaal]] you'll recieve [[Golden Key of Vaashkaani]]
         - *Click the portal {{waypoint 105.13, 7.94, -319.53}} to return to [[Zimara Breadth]].*
1. Enter [[Vaashkaani: Golden Rule (Solo)|Vaashkaani: Golden Rule [Solo]]]
   - Click on the Vaashkaani, Alcazar of Zimara portal and select [[Vaashkaani: Golden Rule (Solo)|Vaashkaani: Golden Rule [Solo]]].
   - **Note**: *[[Storm's A Brewin']] is not offered until this quest is complete so cannot be run concurrently.*
   - Note to the Note:  As of Jan 19th 2024, *[[Storm's A Brewin']] is offered after the [[Here Ghost Nothing]] and [[Vaashkaani: Golden Rule (Solo)|Vaashkaani: Golden Rule [Solo]]] are completed.*
   1. Venture through Vaaskhaani in search of the palace throne.
      - [[An unwell wisher|Unwell wishers]] at {{waypoint 15.14, 3.86, 49.06}} and {{waypoint 14.37, 3.86, -47.29}}. Save or Attack.
      1. Clear the mobs including the two magic carpets on the ground floor at {{waypoint 8.98, 2.96, 41.36}}, {{waypoint 7.04, 2.96, -40.05}}.
      1. Go up a ramp to the platform and clear the mobs plus the third magic carpet at {{waypoint 7.82, 19.92, -0.25}}.
      1. Go down the hallway at the back of the platform.
      1. Kill [[Nezri En'Sallef]] - use Heroic Opportunities (HO's) to dispel her gold shield if it gets to 25 you die.
         - **Note:** Her golden shield stifles you, plan accordingly with adornments.
   1. continue heading toward the Vaaskhaani throne room
      1. Kill trash and remove the orbs from the first rooms on the right and left.
         - [[Orb of Gales]] {{waypoint 147.75, 20.36, 0.05}}
         - [[Orb of Cyclones]] {{waypoint 227.24, 32.43, 43.95}}
         - [[Orb of Rains]] {{waypoint 226.84, 32.42, -44.32}}
         - [[Orb of Hail]] {{waypoint 199.90, 32.42, -44.88}}
      1. Continue down the hallway and clear the trash in the room on the right, then kill [[Isos]] in the room on the left
         - He stifles, plan accordingly with adornments.
         - Must kill adds to kill Isos, use HOs to knock them out. {{waypoint 262.24, 31.18, 58.37}}
           - Remove the [[Orb of Thunder]] {{waypoint 261.51, 32.42, 66.95}}
      1. Go back to the room on the right and kill [[The Storm Mistress]] {{waypoint 262, 31, -25}}
         - *She stuns, so plan accordingly with adorns*
         - *Use Heroic Opportunities on Lightning Balls a couple of spawns, then  focus on The Storm Mistress.*
         - *Kill her before the three Indestructible Lightning Balls complete their circuit.*
      1. Remove the [[Orb of Lightning]] {{waypoint 262.69, 32.45, -67.23}}
      1. Click the portal [[To the Forum]] {{waypoint 281.21, 31.61, 0.16}}
         - Clear the room and then engage [[Hezodhan]]
         - Summons adds, too many will insta-kill
      1. Take the magic carpet he was sitting on, now a transport, to the upper level
      1. Go up the ramp, click on the glass portal to Teleport (left or right doesn't matter).
      1. go futher till you are in the [[Djinn Hoard]]
   1. The Vaaskhaani throne room can't be far now
      1. Go to the [[Sovereign's Reflection]] (second room on the right, aka Mirror room) at {{waypoint 604, 71, -44}}
         - Fear rune needed.
         - Click mirror to summon the named Zakir-sar-ussur (Sovereign)
   1. Survive the confrontation with [[Zakir-Sar-Ussur]], the Sovereign!
      - Keep [[Zakir-Sar-Ussur]] (Sovereign) in line of sight of the mirror (in front) and then use a Heroic Opportunity to trap him in the mirror. (This was very hard to do with a tank mercenary, due to needing to place the boss directly in front of the mirror. It's easier to dismiss your merc and solo it if you're a healer).  Once he's in front of the mirror, move back so you can see him AND his reflection.
      - DPS the Hand while throwing another Heroic Opportunity on the Sovereign when he struggles to break free to continue to trap him into the mirror if needed.
      - DPS the Hand again until it is dead.
1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 591, 71, -55}} to return to [[Miranna Ameema]] on the [[Isles of Wisdom]].
