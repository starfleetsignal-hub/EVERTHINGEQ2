## Steps

- Once you have examined [[A mud encrusted key]] from your inventory, you will 'cleanse it' to receive a cleansed key.  You will receive a 'sub-quest' 'A Muddy Key' for you to clean the key.
- The cleansed key will randomly give you one of the following:
  - [[A burnished bronze key]] - gives the reward quest [[Bronze Key of Fabled Rujark]] when opening the chest.
  - [[A fabled platinum key]] - gives the reward quest [[Platinum Key of Fabled Rujark]] when opening the chest.
  - [[A gold engraved key]] - gives the reward quest [[Gold Key of Fabled Rujark]] when opening the chest.
  - [[An ancient silver key]] - gives the reward quest [[Silver Key of Fabled Rujark]] when opening the chest.
   1. Open the door at {{waypoint -4, 10, -195}} to finish [[The Key of Coins (Fabled)|The Key of Coins]]
1. Open the chest at {{waypoint 2.83, 10.25, -222.35}} for your reward

## Rewards

Depends on which key you receive above.

## Notes

- To access [[The Treasure Chamber]] you must have completed [[The Key of Coins (Fabled)|The Key of Coins]] quest or be accompanied by someone who has.
