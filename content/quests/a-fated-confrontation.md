## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 80+

## Steps

1. Travel to the [[The Tin Soldier]] in [[South Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]] and go upstairs to enter the [[Tin Soldier Practice Rooms]] to try and win back the allegiance of [[Marik McPherson III (Tin Soldier Practice Rooms)|Marik McPherson III]] to his rightful ruler, [[Queen Antonia Bayle|Queen Antonia]]. {{waypoint 577, -12, 264}}.
1. In the upstairs room, you will find both Marik and [[Kyle Antihilus Bayle]]! Bayle makes threats, then kicks you out of the training rooms.
1. Return to [[Lachlyn Cauldthorn]].

- At least 29g 85s 79c (at level 92)
- At least 1p 25g (at level 98)
