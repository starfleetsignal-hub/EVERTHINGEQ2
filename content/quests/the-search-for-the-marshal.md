## Steps

![The remains of Marshal Cristof](images/The_Marshalls_remains.jpg)

1. Examine a skeleton chained to the wall in [[The Deathfist Jail]]. {{waypoint -15, -10, 7}}
   - You will receive two items, [[A tarnished bracelet]] and [[A dusty arrowhead necklace]]. These once belonged to a Green Hood, Marshal Cristof. The inscriptions confirm this. (And yes this is the same one that also shows up in the peacock club, spooky isn't it?)
1. Return to [[Freeboard]] and show him the items.

## Rewards

- At least 5g 69s 57c
- +500 faction with **The Green Hoods**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Tainted Armlet of Rapidity]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Tainted Armlet of Reason]] (All Mages)
  - [[Tainted Armlet of Resolution]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Tainted Armlet of Reverence]] (All Priests)
