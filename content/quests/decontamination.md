## Steps

1. Go to the bottom of [[Ryjesium Lake]] and examine the corpse of a large drachnid. {{waypoint 573, -16, 46}}
1. Return to [[Grush McSearrin]].

- At least 14g 81s 38c
- +1000 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Sash of Chardok]] (All Priests)
  - [[Sash of Karnor's Castle]] (All Mages)
  - [[Sash of Teren's Grasp]]
  - [[Sash of the Reach (69)|Sash of the Reach]] (All classes)
