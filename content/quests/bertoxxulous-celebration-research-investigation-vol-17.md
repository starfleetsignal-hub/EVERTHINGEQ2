## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Collect 3 [[Infused Pox Powder|infused pox powders]]
   - These are rare body drops from trash mobs in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] that will show quest indicator by their name. (bears have a decent drop rate)
   - These are group updates
   - You will spend on average 15 minutes getting to and collecting shards
1. Afterwards you take those shards to a shrine in Antonica shrine spawns in random spot in that zone.
   - **Notes:**
     - This is NOT a group update.
     - One person clicking the altar will cause the altar to disappear and reappear in another location
     - It is not clear if there is only ever one altar in the zone that is moved by other groups
     - It may be possible to click simultaneously to get a group update
     - You may spend as much as 30 minutes tracking down an altar to click
     - ![](images/Screenshot_2024-08-14_173137.png)
   - **Possible Locations include:**
     - {{waypoint -500, 7, -297}} (hill above the [[Keep of the Ardent Needle]])
     - {{waypoint -1072, -7, -372}}
     - {{waypoint -895.80, -21.97, -593.38}} (right next to the [[Oracle Tower Station]])
     - {{waypoint -562.03, -15.14, 658.29}} (Western edge of [[Archer's Wood]])
     - {{waypoint -864.73, -25.67, 311.50}} ([[Qeynos Plains]] North-West of Bertoxxulous)
     - {{waypoint -1291.83, -27.77, 579.01}} ([[The Firemyst Gully]] in the alcove behind Bertoxxulous)
     - {{waypoint -1270, -15, 97}} (A tree below [[The Bells of Vhalen|Vhalen's Tower]])
     - {{waypoint -1441.15, -14.27, -433.11}} (Near where the aqueduct passes over the river)
     - {{waypoint -1698.15, -14.69, 265.68}} (just north of [[Sentry Acton]] at the southern Qeynos Highway at the river)
     - {{waypoint -2044.66, 6.77, 244.38}} (Hill above [[The Keep of the Gnollslayers]], at the tower)
     - {{waypoint -1715, -24, -544}} (under viaduct near [[Windstalker Village]])
     - {{waypoint -2049, 14, -505}}  [[Windstalker Highlands]]
1. Return to [[Herald of the Plaguelord]] {{waypoint -1157.19, -33.96, 525.38}} in [[Antonica]]
