**If your character is good aligned, see the [[Blat Berisen]] page for locations you can access.**

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Frethe Zizlop (Neriak)|Frethe Zizlop]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]]. {{waypoint -35, 13, -5}}
   - This will start the subquest [[Card Deals (Neriak)|Card Deals]].
1. Speak with [[Zoe Hertzflip (Neriak)|Zoe Hertzflip]] on the docks in [[Neriak, City of Hate]]. {{waypoint 16, 3, 286}}
   - This will start the subquest [[Ogled Goggles (Neriak)|Ogled Goggles]].
1. Speak with [[Barb Islenet (Neriak)|Barb Islenet]] in The New Foreign Quarter, [[Neriak, City of Hate]] {{waypoint 82, 23, 138}}
   - This will start the subquest [[Wine for the Lady (Neriak)|Wine for the Lady]].
1. Speak with [[Abner Siblet (Neriak)|Abner Siblet]] in The Down Under (crafting area), [[Neriak, City of Hate]] {{waypoint -156, 2, 243}}
   - This will start the subquest [[Fribles's Fate (Neriak)|Fribles's Fate]].
1. Go to {{waypoint -564, -52, 23}} in [[Darklight Wood]]. [[Belazzor Fribles, the Inept (Darklight Wood)|Belazzor Fribles, the Inept]] will spawn and attack you; his level scales to yours.
   - Kill [[Belazzor Fribles, the Inept (Darklight Wood)|Fribles]].
1. Go back to [[Abner Siblet (Neriak)|Abner Siblet]] in [[Neriak, City of Hate]].
1. Return to the remaining NPCs in reverse order to complete each of the subquests. You will get Exp/AA exp for all 4 of them.
1. Return finally to [[Blat Berisen (Darklight Wood)|Blat Berisen]] in Darklight Wood for your reward..

## Rewards

- [[Zoe's Wild Deck of Playing Cards]]
- [[Tin Metal Helm of Protection]]
