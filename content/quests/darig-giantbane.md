*The following quest, [[Karath the Forgotten]], is only available upon completion of all other quests offered by the Watcher of the Fields.*

## Steps

1. Kill 12 [[A highland stalker|highland stalker]]s. These are found roaming [[Thundermist Valley]] beside the Watcher. Kill the other types of lions to make more stalkers spawn.
1. Visit [[Deadview Pass]], which is on the southern boundary of [[Thundermist Valley]] at {{waypoint 930, 0, 570}}
1. Kill one giant. The weakest are [[Firerock scout|Firerock scouts]] (level 24), found at the south end of [[Thundermist Valley]].
1. Return to the [[Watcher of the Fields]]. Click the banner corresponding to Darig to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- Hand slot armor based on class:
  - [[Sentry's Gauntlets of Strength]] - Shadowknight
  - [[Bandit's Chain Gloves of Agility]] - Beastlord
  - [[Beguiler's Gloves of Intelligence]] - Wizard
