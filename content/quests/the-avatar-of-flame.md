## Steps

1. Meet Civean Il'Pernod at the [[Torch of Ro]] in [[Lavastorm]]. {{waypoint -118, 18, 66}}
1. Rescue [[Marathan Allim]] from the clutches of The Thyr:
   - Enter [[Solusek's Eye]], navigate to the entrance of [[The Oratorium of Thyr]] and enter the unique instance, [[Oratorium of Thyr: Allim's Fate]]. {{waypoint -201, -618, -201}}
1. Slay all the Thyr in the instance until [[Lord Thygal]] becomes active, at which point engage him and spill his guts onto the molten floor.
1. Once Thygal is dead, the [[Avatar of Flames]] will appear and after a brief dialogue, give you your reward.
   - The Avatar may bug out if you hail him while he is floating upwards. If he does, zone out and then back in.

## Rewards

- Item Reward: [[Cloak of the Burning Prince (Level 67)|Cloak of the Burning Prince]]
- +10000 faction with **Solusek Ro**
