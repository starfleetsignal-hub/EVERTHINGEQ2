This is a [[Mikk Eishammer#Related Quests|Forgemasters of Thurgadin Supply Task]]

## Steps

1. Make 5 batches of powder to fire the gnomish cannons
   - The Explosive Gnomish Cannon Powder recipe can be found within the [[Thurgadin Supply Manifesto|Thurgadin Supply Manifestorecipe]] book, which can be obtained by clicking on the **Forgemasters Recipe Notes** on the counter in front of [[Mikk Eishammer]]
   - The materials required for the recipe can be obtained by clicking on the jar of **Gnomish Powder Supplies** on the counter in front of [[Mikk Eishammer]]
     - Required materials:
       - [[Chemistry Table]]
       - 5 [[Nitrate]] (from the jar mentioned above)
       - 5 [[Sulfur]] (from the jar mentioned above)
       - 5 [[Charcoal]] (from the jar mentioned above)
       - 5 [[Ethereal candle]]
1. Return to [[Mikk Eishammer]]

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +1,500 faction with **Forgemasters of Thurgadin**
