## Steps

1. Speak with [[Zaveta Stickyfingers]] and tell her that you're ready to show her the ruins (Zaveta will follow you there)
1. Travel to the ruins {{waypoint -756, 95, 1033}}
1. Speak with Zaveta Stickyfingers
1. [[Zaveta Stickyfingers (Mob)|Zaveta]] will attack you. Kill her and the quest auto-completes

## Rewards

- At least 1p 35g
- One of the Following:
  - [[Electrified Adornment of Swift Casting]]
  - [[Electrified Adornment of Reuse]]
