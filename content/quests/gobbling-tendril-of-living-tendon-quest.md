## Notes

- Commonly found while on [[Xebrith Cognizance: Dread]] quest.

## Steps

1. kill 15 [[A grave creeper|Grave creepers]] (encounters) in [[Oogothl Sprawl]]
   - [[A grave creeper|Grave creepers]] can be found in the Tentacle Fields and Detritus Rise in Oogothl Sprawl zone.
   - *As with many 'Seek & Destroy' missions in Rage of Cthurath expansion, this step only counts the number of 'encounters' defeated, not individual numbers of mobs themselves.*

## Rewards

- x100 [[Aethermatic Alloy]]
- [[Status Coin (75,000)]]
