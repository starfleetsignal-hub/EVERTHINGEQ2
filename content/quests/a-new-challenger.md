## Steps

1. Blow the [[Horn of Challenge]] while on the natural rock bridge.
   - The horn will begin to glow around {{waypoint 75, 243, -255}}
1. Defeat the [[The Krulkiel Champion]]. He is level 33^.
1. Return to Trozusk and inform him that his honor is safe.

## Rewards

- At least 27s 67c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Metalic Sliver of Magical Energy]] (All Priests)
  - [[Totem of Sustained Wounding]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Illuminated Wand Shard]] (All Mages)
  - [[Divine Embossed Orb]] (All Scouts)
