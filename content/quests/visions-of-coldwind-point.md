## Notes

- After completing [[Once Upon an Isle Dreary]], [[Quallium]] will give you his book, [[Visions of Coldwind Point (Quest Starter)|Visions of Coldwind Point]], and beg you to read it. Sift through the terrible poetry to gain clues as to the four places you need to visit roughly along the [[Coldwind Coastline]].

## Steps

1. Read through the book after examining it.
1. Visit in any order:
   - "The vigilant giant": [[Coldwind Point|Coldwind Point Lighthouse]] {{waypoint 347, -9, 812}}.
   - "The Peeking Tree": [[The Peeking Tree|Solitary Tree]] {{waypoint -25, -5, 671}}
   - "The Sword like no other": [[Claymore Monument]] {{waypoint -435, 1, 553}}
   - "The King of the Thicket": [[Archer's Wood|The Great Oak]] {{waypoint -698, -3, 596}}

## Rewards

- Experience Only
