## Steps

1. Collect (kill) 8 essences of Greenmist from the Thulosians in Moors of Ykesha {{waypoint 393.64, -62.16, -187.53}}
1. Look for a meal being prepared by the chef within [[The Ruins of Guk: Ykesha's Outer Stronghold]], {{waypoint 2, -8, -241}}
1. Pour the poison into 8 dishes within [[The Ruins of Guk: Ykesha's Outer Stronghold]], found throughout the zone at tables and the floor
   - {{waypoint 18, -6, -227}}, table in Cook's room
   - {{waypoint 44, -8, -193}} and {{waypoint 88, -8, -176}}, on the floor below the ramps
   - {{waypoint 97, -8, -245}}, floor in the Forge room
   - {{waypoint 52, -18, -85}}, floor in room with troll statues
   - {{waypoint 48, -14, -0.5}}, floor behind a fire
   - {{waypoint 32.6, -7, -32}}, floor behind a fire
   - {{waypoint 49, 13, -158}}, on a bench at top of ramp
   - {{waypoint 62, -8, -253}}, {{waypoint 75, -8, -262}}, {{waypoint 44, -8, -322}} and {{waypoint 24, -8, -323}}, on the floor in the hallway leading up to Zraxth
1. Return to Bulvariz in The Moors of Ykesha
