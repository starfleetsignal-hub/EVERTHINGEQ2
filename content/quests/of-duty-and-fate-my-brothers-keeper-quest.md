## Note

The reward from  [[Fireflies in the Sky]] will be needed for step 12 of this quest. Speak to [[Arkemitus Spiritwell]] in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] at {{waypoint 1016, 169, 691}}

## Steps

1. Examine [[Felfeather Family Crypt Key]] (from previous quest) to start the quest.
1. Enter the Felfeather Family Crypt at {{waypoint 1130, 177, 733}} in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]].
1. Look for Irko within the crypt at {{waypoint 52.94, -5.83, 141.78}}.
1. Locate some sort of keystone to break the hold on Irko. It is a clickable "a Spirit Entombment Keystone" at {{waypoint 11.97, 3.19, 72.16}} in the crypt.
1. Speak to Irko while having the keystone, back at {{waypoint 52.94, -5.83, 141.78}}.
   - During your interaction with him, he will attack. Fight him until he becomes able to be talked to again.
     - Combat Tip: Avoid the frontal - 'With the little power I have left, begone!' (He will lock in place so it is possible to get behind him without him turning to face you)
1. Seek out someone wise from your previous adventures and get their advice.
   - This is [[Winston the Wise]] in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]], at {{waypoint 420, 282, 117}}.
1. Speak with Erki back at {{waypoint 1105.51, 177.06, 697.11}} and present the idea from Winston.
1. Go back to Irko within the Felfeather Family Crypt and convince them to negotiate.
1. The reward from  [[Fireflies in the Sky]] will be needed now before you proceed. Speak to [[Arkemitus Spiritwell]] in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] at {{waypoint 1016, 169, 691}}
1. Travel to Zimara Breadth, and enter the Felfeather Ritual Room: Chromatic Chamber at {{waypoint -691.50, 219.40, -612.06}}.
1. Inside the zone, find the brothers arguing at {{waypoint 462.41, -13.45, 147.34}}.
1. Perform the dialogue with Erki.
1. Search the maze for keystones. Finding a keystone will release a brother's spirit for you to find. Clicking a spirit will make it follow. Only one spirit can follow at a time.
   - **NOTE**: The maze is easy to traverse. Whichever section you are in, go to the hallway of the direction you want to go. You will find a green rift portal. Click at the bottom corner of the rift to teleport.
1. Return the spirits **one at a time** to the central area. They will be bound, and have a countdown of 30 seconds. **Right click** the spirit to capture. Repeat for second spirit. (see Note, and step 9)
1. The elder and brothers are there when you exit the instance.  click each brother to finish at {{waypoint 1111.35, 176.80, 696.14}}.

## Rewards

- unlocks the [[Of Duty and Fate: My Brother's Keeper (Achievement)]]
