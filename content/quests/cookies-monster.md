## Steps

![Cookie's dump pile](images/Cookie's_dump_pile.JPG)

1. Approach Cookie's dump pile during in-game night, which begins at 8pm. {{waypoint -109, -125, 630}}
1. Examine the dump pile and defeat [[Cookie's Monster (Named)|Cookie's Monster]] that spawns.
1. Loot the [[Handfuls of Old Food]] from the chest that it drops.
   - Unless everyone in your group approaches the dump pile at night, you will not get the update that enables you to examine it. Once everyone receives the update, have one person examine the pile to spawn the monster. Once it is dead, everyone take turns looting the food from the chest it drops.
1. Return to Cookie Yevek.

## Reward

- At least 1p
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Chardok Defender's Cloak]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Karnor Invader's Mantle]] (All Mages)
  - [[Omen Rejector's Cape]] (All Priests)
  - [[Wrap of the Far Reach]] (All Scouts)
- +5000 faction with **Dalnir's Wheel Taskmasters**
