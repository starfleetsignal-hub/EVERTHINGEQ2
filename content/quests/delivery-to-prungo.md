## Steps

- Talk to [[Prungo Fizzler Styles]] at near the Steamfont Mountains zone {{waypoint -800.94, 11.35, 279.79}}
- Return to [[Philip Glaslow]]

## Rewards

- At least 4g 80s 10c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Arcane Sage's Gloves]] (All Mages)
  - [[Dauntless Gladiator's Gauntlets]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Fearless Pathfinder's Chain Gloves]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Feral Rainkeeper's Leather Gloves]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Otherworldly Ritualist's Handguards]] (Shamans)
  - [[Resilient Grandmaster's Hand Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Righteous Augur's Plate Gloves]] (Clerics)
