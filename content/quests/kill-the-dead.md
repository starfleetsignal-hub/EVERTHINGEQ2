This quest is part 11 of a 19-quest series in the [[Nektulos Forest Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Slay 10 [[A Dragoon sentry|dragoon sentries]]. The easiest place to find them (level 25) is at [[The Funeral Pyre]] {{waypoint -960, 12, -715}}. Higher level dragoon sentries (27 non-heroics) can be found north of [[N'Mar's Ascent]].
1. Return to Jaeta for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 5s 56c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Imbued Sceptre of the Spellpriest]] (All Priests)
  - [[Kris of the Dark Mage]] (All Mages)
  - [[Wizard's Training Wand]] (All Mages)
  - [[Seafarer's Blade]] (Paladin, Shadowknight, Warrior)
  - [[Shortblade of the Nature Defender]] (Fury, Warden)
  - [[Thexian Kris]] (Assassin, Bard, Ranger, Rogue)
  - [[Voracious Ulak]] (Beastlord, Bruiser, Monk)
