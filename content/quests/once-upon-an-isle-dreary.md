## Steps

1. Slay [[A dankfur gnoll|dankfur gnolls]] until you recover four scrolls. The gnolls are located on the islands just south of Quallium, [[Qeynos Castaway's Isle (POI)|Qeynos Castaway's Isle]] and [[Qeynos Shepherd's Isle]].
1. Return to [[Quallium]] for your rewards.

## Rewards

- [[Wordsmith's Earring of Insight]]
- [[Visions of Coldwind Point (Quest Starter)|Visions of Coldwind Point]] - quest starter for followup quest.
- At least 4s 77c

## Notes

- The name of the quest is a reference to the famous poem by Edgar Allen Poe, "The Raven".
