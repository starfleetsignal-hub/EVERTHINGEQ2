*The following quest, [[Karath the Forgotten]], is only available upon completion of all other quests offered by the Watcher of the Fields.*

## Steps

1. Kill 16 Thundering Steppes' hawks of any type; [[A juvenile hawk]], [[A puerile hawk]],[[An elder hawk]] or [[A mature hawk]].
1. Kill [[Griffawn|griffawns]] until you collect a golden feather. This is an uncommon quest update.
1. Harvest 18 [[Belladonna root|belladonna roots]] from [[Root Nodes#T3|mesquite catch weed]], found throughout the zone. You will need a gathering skill of 90.
   - You need 18 *harvests* not 18 pieces.
1. Return to the [[Watcher of the Fields]]. Click the banner corresponding to Duranar to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- Leg slot item based on class:
  - [[Sentry's Sabatons of Strength]] - Shadowknight
  - [[Bandit's Chain Shoes of Agility]] - Beastlord
