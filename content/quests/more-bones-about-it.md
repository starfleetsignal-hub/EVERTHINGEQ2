## Steps

*There are still more undead goblins roaming the streams and ponds in Sullen Sanctuary of the Spiral of Vul. I have chosen to provide more of their skulls for a proper burial upon Blotch's return to Clan Mucktongue.*

- Hunt 14 Vuldor minions
- Return to [[Blortch]]
- Deposit the goblin skulls in Blortch's bone barrel

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
