## Steps

1. Go to [[Castle Highhold (Poi)|Castle Highhold]] {{waypoint 672, 352, 67}}
   - If flying, Low-level toons should swing south to get past [[The Blessed Knoll]], to avoid falling from flying mount
   - The purchasable item [[Return to Highhold]] will take you there
1. Speak to [[Arms Master Ryan]] {{waypoint 687, 352, 64}}
1. Speak to [[Quartermaster Roberta Robbins]] {{waypoint 625, 352, 179}}

## Rewards

- 5 [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]]
- At least 1p 40g 40s
- +500 faction with **Far Seas Supply Division**
