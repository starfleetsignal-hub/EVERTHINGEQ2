## Notes

This  is a repeatable version of [[Rend Control Now]]. Speak with an NPC called a Flamebearer standing close to some elemental rifts to receive this quest.

This quest asks for challenging mobs, NOT gray conning. Rifts spawn in 41 zones across Norrath and Luclin in several spots in each zone, however they will only produce mobs of level appropriate to the zone, they do not scale to player level. Mentoring down or chrono mentoring will work if you'd like to lessen the challenge. Otherwise look for rifts in zones appropriate to your adventure level.

When you kill elementals from rifts they leave behind 2 - 3 spawns of [[Chaotic Elemental Filament]]. Pick them up, they are a primary component in repeatable tradeskill quests for this event, tradable to others if you don't craft. They also sometimes give a piece for [[Rarefied Chaos Materials]] collection.

## Steps

1. Slay waves of challenging elementals emerging from planar rifts 0/5 - each rift will spawn a small ring event: few small elementals you need to kill and one final powerful elemental that will update this step.
1. Use the [[Etheric Plication Rod]] to close unguarded planar rifts 0/5 - use the item given to close "a planar rift" left after you killed the wave of mobs.
1. Report back to  [[A Flamebearer]] (does not have to be the same zone you got the quest from)

## Rewards

- [[AA]] experience (only on first completion)
- 3 x [[Brass Lambent]]
