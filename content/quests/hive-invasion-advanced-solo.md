## Steps

*A swarm of bixies have taken over part of Guk, and need to be driven out. Seek out the strongest of the creatures lurking within the Fractured Hive, and destroy them.*

- Enter the zone [[The Fractured Hive (Advanced Solo)]]:
  - Defeat the [[Furious Honey Elemental (Advanced Solo)|Furious Honey Elemental]]
  - Defeat [[Ziza Cerata (Advanced Solo)|Ziza Cerata]]
  - Defeat [[Bazziz Zz (Advanced Solo)|Bassiz Zz]]
  - Defeat the [[Monstrous Roc (Advanced Solo)|Monstrous Roc]]
