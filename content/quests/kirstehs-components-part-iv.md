## Steps

1. Go to the [[Edgewater Drains]] and collect:
   - 12 [[A drudge mucus|drudge mucus]] samples; Edgewater Drains top level
   - 10 [[Sewage viper]] poison sacs; Edgewater Drains bottom level
   - 14 [[A contaminated rat|contaminated rat]] pelts; Edgewater Drains middle level
1. Return to Kirsteh
