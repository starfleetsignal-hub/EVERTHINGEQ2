## Starting the Quest

NOTE: at NO point of the Starting Quest steps will the Scout be given a Quest Journal listing or update; Only *after* such time as they have spoken with  [[Chancellor Thanial Vylardin]] {{waypoint -219.26, 57.34, -224.04}} in [[Nye'Caelona]] (crafting area) with there be Quest Journal entry.

1. Inspect the below items, in order (all trackable) (not with Track Harvestables, with just Tracking)
   1. [[The Sinking Sands]]
      1. Single Gold Coin {{waypoint -372, -183, -1529}}
      1. an empty canteen {{waypoint -2037, -158, 58}}
      1. hoof prints {{waypoint -1885, -230, 1081}} on [[Onerock Isle]]
      1. chest {{waypoint -1966, -229, 1020}} on [[Onerock Isle]]
   1. [[Moors of Ykesha]] (all at Lower Moors)
      1. a dead brokenskull: {{waypoint -1317, -1059, 1352}}
      1. deep hoof prints: {{waypoint -1778, -1096, 1564}}
      1. a newly extinguished fire: {{waypoint -2206, -1062, 812}}
      1. a finely crafted satchel: {{waypoint -2092, -1075, 150}}
   1. [[The Feerrott]]
      1. a slain king manticore: {{waypoint -652, 9, 82}} (nearest port is the spire)
      1. fresh footprints: {{waypoint -382, -0, 262}}
      1. head SW to The Horse Queen: {{waypoint -227, -6, 452}}
      1. Witness an elf getting attacked, track and pick up a torn half of a map at {{waypoint -133, -5, 461}}
1. Read the torn half of the map
1. Head to [[Obulus Frontier]], talk to [[Chancellor Thanial Vylardin]] {{waypoint -219.26, 57.34, -224.04}} in [[Nye'Caelona]] (crafting area).

## Steps

1. Return to [[The Feerrott]] for four trackable clues. (nearest port is the druid ring)
   1. Tracks: {{waypoint -576, -7, 585}}
   1. Tracks: {{waypoint -552, 4, 310}}
   1. Arrow: {{waypoint -346, 9, 148}}
   1. Dagger: {{waypoint -58, 13, 177}}
1. I need to pick up the trail again. Head to the [[Barren Sky]] (use the Spire next to you after the step above).
   1. a slain aviak: {{waypoint -337, 300, 680}} on Isle of Eaglewatch
   1. a berry-stripped bush: {{waypoint -628, 243, -152}} on Cloudmist Isle
   1. flattened earth: {{waypoint 548, 299, -685}} on Isle of Desolation
   1. find and speak with Dolas: {{waypoint 536, 308, -645}} on Isle of Desolation
1. Dolas sends you to find his crown in [[Nye'Caelona]]. Head there and find a Nye'Caelona Custodian {{waypoint -173, 91, -163}} wandering on the top level of the city. In **stealth**, pickpocket a key (via-right click). Must be directly behind.
1. Dismount and USE (Right Click) the key in your inventory to teleport to the chancellor's throne room.
1. In **stealth**, pickpocket the display key from the wandering guard captain.
1. ![Dolas' Crown (once you have key from guard)](images/Dolas'_Crown.jpg)Use the display key to unlock the display and obtain the crown.
1. Return to Dolas' former location in [[Barren Sky]]: {{waypoint 536, 308, -645}} on Isle of Desolation. Examine the blood you find.
1. I need to pick up on Dolas' trail again. He appears to be bleeding. Head to the [[Bonemire]] (click on the Spires at {{waypoint -477.77, 292.74, -538.70}}.
   1. more blood: {{waypoint -834, -437, 360}} on [[Shattered Weir]].
   1. a blood smear: {{waypoint 199, 183, -387}} on [[Cacotoxic Stain]].
   1. fresh blood: {{waypoint -97, -74, -582}} on [[Carrion Briar]].
   1. Speak to Dolas to the east of the last update at {{waypoint -253, -73, -596}}
1. Dolas sends you to collect four items.
   1. [[Blessed Mineral Water]] from [[New Halas]] in [[Frostfang Sea]]) at {{waypoint 71, 147, -51}}
   1. [[Purple calendula]] from [[Cobalt Scar]] at {{waypoint 5628, -1015, 738}} a plant atop a cliff.
   1. [[A marble mortar and pestle]] from [[Tranquil Sea]] at {{waypoint -677, 8, 289}} in a pygmy hut.
   1. [[Desert limestone]] from [[Pillars of Flame]] at  {{waypoint -46, -43, -542}}
      - Note: This spawns a L107 heroic golem. Come prepared. Assure you loot tiny chest that drops desert limestone.
1. Return to Dolas in [[The Bonemire]] on [[Carrion Briar]] at {{waypoint -253, -73, -596}}
1. Craft the Item
   1. Scribe the [[Cae'Dal Poultice]] recipe he gives you.
      - Requires a [[Shadeweave root]]
      - level 100 in [[Artisan]].
   1. Place the mortar on the ground near Dolas
   1. craft the combine
   1. Give [[A Cae'Dal Poison Poultice|a Cae'Dal Poison Poultice]] to Dolas.
1. Find a dark rider in [[Phantom Sea]] at {{waypoint 271, 0, 1012}}. Approach him so that he despawns.
1. Open track and find "Inquisitor Foust" nearby. {{waypoint 283, 51, 1062}}
   - *Note: to update the quest you must have completed the entire [[Shattered Seas Timeline]]*
1. Find my black spot - *This is obtained by completing a class-specific [[Collection Quests|collection quest]]:*
   - [[Assassin]] - [[Assassin's Self-Realization]]![](images/Black_spot.png)
   - [[Beastlord]] - [[Beastlord's Self-Realization]]
   - [[Brigand]] - [[Brigand's Self-Realization]]
   - [[Dirge]] - [[Dirge's Self-Realization]]
   - [[Ranger]] - [[Ranger's Self-Realization]]
   - [[Swashbuckler]] - [[Swashbuckler's Self-Realization]]
   - [[Troubadour]] - [[Troubador's Self-Realization]]
   - *Note: The locations vary by class, and are semi-random. The shinies are purple and trackable. Only one spawns at a time on approximately a 5 minute timer. Spawn points are shared by collection members.*
1. With your [[Infamous deed]] in hand, return to Inquisitor Foust in [[Phantom Sea]] at {{waypoint 283, 51, 1063}}.
1. Inquisitor Foust sends you into [[Ossuary of Malevolence (Contested)]] in the [[Phantom Sea]] at {{waypoint 696.22, 53.01, 769.57}} to obtain five uncommon treasure chest drops from trash mobs.
   - I need the [[Bones of a Primordial Malice cultist]].
     - From [[Primordial Malice cultist|Primordial Malice cultists]] in the west wing.
   - I need the [[Blood of a Primordial Malice cultist]].
     - From [[Primordial Malice cultist|Primordial Malice cultists]] in the west wing.
   - I need a lump of [[Rotting flesh]].
     - One drop from a [[Flesh construct]] in the west wing.
   - I need [[A death shroud]].
     - One drop from a [[Skeletal terror]] in the east wing.
   - I need [[A runed skull]].
     - One drop from a [[Skeletal obscenity]] in the east wing.
1. Return to Inquisitor Foust in [[Phantom Sea]] at {{waypoint 283, 51, 1063}}. He will give you a [[Visage of Malice (Recipe Boob)|Visage of Malice]] recipe that you must scribe, then head to the dark elf village [[Ghorkaal]] to the west of his location. Use a cauldron {{waypoint 806, 45, 927}} to craft the [[Visage of Malice (Item)|Visage of Malice]].  This will require a [[Shadowstone ore]] rare.
   - *Note: If you have lost any of the components, a chest will appear nearby the cauldron. Click it to be given replacements.*
1. ![The Grand Inquisitor's Chapel Pillow](images/The_Grand_Inquisitor's_Chapel_Pillow.jpg)Head to the [[Cathedral of Bone: The Grand Inquisitor's Chapel]] at {{waypoint 730, 48, 786}} in order to slay the Grand Inquisitor.
   1. Use the [[Visage of Malice (Item)|Visage of Malice]] as soon as you zone in. Head down the second short flight of stairs and to the left to find a skull on a pillow at {{waypoint 12, 2, 141}} to despawn the golems.
      - As of nov2023, quest updates even with entire zone grey.
      - *Note: If they are not removed, they join the Grand Inquisitor in combat.*
   1. Kill one name in each of the west and east wings.
      - [[Castigator Falkus]] {{waypoint 495, 0, -22}}
      - [[Organist Deherin]] {{waypoint -505, 0, -24}}
   1. Kill the Grand Inquisitor in the middle wing.
      - *Note: This zone is relatively difficult, but mercenaries are allowed and you can zone in other classes via [[Call of the Veteran]]. **Not a group zone in for scouts, each scout must click on the coffin to zone in** (or use Call of the Veteran). With five scouts the two epic x4 red golems were manageable and killable even without despawning them.*
1. Return to Inquisitor Foust in [[Phantom Sea]] at {{waypoint 283, 51, 1063}}. He will give you [[Foust's Magical Leash]].
1. Head to [[Nektulos Forest]] and find a dark rider just south of [[N'Mar's Ascent]] {{waypoint -369, 12, -1214}}, on the road. **Dismount**, run up to him and get aggro, cast the leash and some Ca's, until the duration of the leash runs out or he ports you to [[Fens of Nathsar]] outside of [[Kurn's Tower]].
1. Using the cave entrance at {{waypoint -1847, -375, -1689}}, locate and speak to [[Foreman Fluff]] {{waypoint -1855, -387, -1813}} near the zone-in location to [[Kurn's Tower]], in the tunnel just below [[Kurn's Tower]].
   - *Note: Requires [[Kurn's Tower Access Timeline]] until you get access in order to see the foreman.*
1. Foreman Fluff agrees to help you gain entry into Kurn's Tower in exchange for some shinies:
   1. [[Golden Belt of the Unseen]]: Sold by [[A dark talon merchant]] in [[Halls of the Unseen]] at {{waypoint 169, -4, 59}} for 66p 3c.
      - *Note: Requires [[The Means to an End...|The Means to an End]] and 50,000 faction with [[The Circle of the Unseen Hand (Faction)|The Circle of the Unseen Hand]]*. Even if you have the bauble you need the HQ for the merchant to appear.
   1. [[Golden Necklace of Rime (Item)|Golden Necklace of Rime]]: Sold by [[Vassava Kilka]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]] at {{waypoint -737, -46, 1910}} for 504p 20c.
      - *Note: Requires [[The Order of Rime Timeline]] and 50,000 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]]*. With the [[Guise of Pursuit]] you do NOT need to do the Rime Timeline.
      - Talk to [[Brind]] {{waypoint -313, -46, 1401}} on the Jarsath Wastes docks, and choose the 1st option to be sent to the Deepwater Circle boat, the merchant will be on the boat.
   1. [[Gold Encrusted Pearl Ring]]: Clickable bucket in [[Cobalt Scar]] at {{waypoint 4258, -1094, 2050}}.
      - *Note: Requires [[Cobalt Scar Timeline]] through [[High Tide]].
   1. [[Gold Laced Cloak of Nighmarish Depths]]: Uncommon chest drop from [[Abyssral]] in [[Kralet Penumbra: Submerged Lair (Event Heroic)]].
   1. [[Golden Shackle of Mist]]: Common chest drop from [[The Mist Reaver]] in [[Lost City of Torsis: The Shrouded Temple (Event Heroic)]].
1. Return to Foreman Fluff. He will give you an explosive barrel.
1. Place the barrel at the marked location at the large rock {{waypoint -2006, -353, -1801}} just east of [[Kurn's Tower]]. An entrance will appear leading to [[Kurn's Tower: The Locket]].
1. Enter [[Kurn's Tower: The Locket]]. Proceed through the tunnel and climb up the wall. Head to the right, avoiding the guards, and find the gold piles. The chest is behind the throne chair {{waypoint 94, 3, 13}} [[Dola's Mother's Locket|Dolas' Mother's Locket]]. Loot it. Note: the guards can be avoided but will one shot you if seen.
1. Return to the [[Bonemire]]. Dolas has left, but he has left a small bag behind at {{waypoint -253, -73, -597}}. Click the bag to finish the quest.
