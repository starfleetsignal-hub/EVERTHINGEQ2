## Steps

1. Begin your investigation of the Council of Ten's secretive behavior happening within [[Nettleville]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]], by speaking to [[Guard Rendyl]] just outside the gates to [[Nettleville: Strange Tidings|Nettleville]]. {{waypoint 680, -21, 270}}
1. Enter [[Nettleville: Strange Tidings|Nettleville]] and find out what's happening in the district.
   - As you wander, [[A crazed Freeblood]] will attack you, requiring you to slay it. {{waypoint 647, -25, 365}}
   - Talk to a human resident to find out what happened here. {{waypoint 616, -26, 384}}
1. Locate 5 peaceful Freeblood and help them escape.
   - {{waypoint 642, -20, 289}}
   - {{waypoint 657, -20, 283}}
   - {{waypoint 646, -25, 357}}
   - {{waypoint 576, -19, 351}}
   - {{waypoint 599, -19, 372}}
1. Return to [[General Golias Sydwen]].
