## Steps

1. Go to [[North Freeport]] and speak to [[Researcher Kendril]] {{waypoint 2, -7, -132}} in the [[Academy of Arcane Science]].
1. Complete Kendril's quest, [[Traces of Teleportation]].
   - Once Kendril's quests are complete, you will have the items you need to return to [[Sir Tallen Yevix]].
1. Speak with [[Sir Tallen Yevix]], who will tell you to examine the artifact.
1. Right click on the Dimensional Tranducer (looks like a tinkered mender bot) behind Tallen to update the quest.
1. Next she will send you to [[The Baubbleshire]] to kidnap [[Algan Tinmizer]] {{waypoint 863, -12, -414}} (right click and Abduct him)
   - Note: You can use the bell on the [[Antonica]] docks to get into the zone. The guards are level 16 heroics. Also, if you are a Master Tinker, you will not have to do this step, and instead will be able to repair the bot by yourself!
1. Return with Algan to Sir Tallen Yevix
   - Wait until the device is repaired.  You will see the conversation between Sir Tallen Yevix and Algan.
   - Algan will run away once he repairs the Dimensional Transducer.
   - Speak with Sir Tallen Yevix again.
   - You will have to click through some dialog between Sir Tallen Yevix and [[Lucan D'Lere]], once that conversation is over.
