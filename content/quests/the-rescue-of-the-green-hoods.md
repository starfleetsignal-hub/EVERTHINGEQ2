## Steps

1. Find the mine lift at {{waypoint 329, 0, -114}}.
1. Return to [[Sashra Thaltalis]].
1. Collect 5 scalps each from [[A Sullon centurion|Sullon centurions]], [[A Tallon raider|Tallon raiders]] and [[A Vallon grunt|Vallon grunts]]. You have 60 minutes to complete this step.
   - [[A Vallon grunt|Vallon grunts]] are near the [[Vallon Lumber Mill]] {{waypoint 577, -21, -242}}
   - [[A Tallon raider|Tallon raiders]] are in the camp east of the docks with the Warpriests. Also there are some south of [[Three Toes Valley]]. {{waypoint 287, -40, 257}}
   - [[A Sullon centurion|Sullon centurions]] are along the road to [[Deathfist Citadel]]. You could find some on the road in [[Mythical Forest]] {{waypoint -340, -35, 96}} also.
1. Return to Sashra Thaltalis.
1. Kill [[Sentry Goorlux]] {{waypoint -214, -29, -274}}, {{waypoint -71, -9, -307}}, {{waypoint 140.88, 0.00, 0.00}}. As of [[LU53]], Goorlux's placeholder was removed, and Goorlux is almost always up.  He's on a 6-8 minute respawn timer.
1. Return to Sashra Thaltalis.
1. Go to [[Deathfist Citadel]], you can enter through the sewers or the front door.
1. Kill all the named in the courtyard, then go to the ramparts. (Needed for spawning Fyst later)
1. Find [[Infiltrator Stryjin]] {{waypoint 31.49, 8.66, 59.52}} in the SW tower, and talk to him. He wants you to rescue 3 trapped Green Hood women.
   - To get to him you must drop in from above, as there is no ladder up from the courtyard in the SW tower.
   - either of the middle towers (left and right) have ladders that take you to the top  example:{{waypoint 39.79, 10.31, 89.08}}
1. The women are at the following respective locations:
   - [[Rinis Nightsong]] {{waypoint -9.64, 8.00, 30.51}}
   - [[Wensa Neldora]] {{waypoint -21.49, -10.00, -10.13}}
   - [[Shelmay Wavecrest]] {{waypoint -29.39, -10.00, 11.61}} - For Shelmay's update, at least one person in group will need to obtain the key from Chief Torturer Goortz {{waypoint -16, -10, -15}} first. (Copied from eq2zam)
1. Return to [[Infiltrator Stryjin]].
1. After returning to [[Infiltrator Stryjin]], you must kill [[Emperor Fyst]].
