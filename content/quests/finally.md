## Steps

1. Zap 10 Nightmarsh Fungusmen at {{waypoint 356.35, -47, 248}} with [[Vornlin's Mushroom Blaster]].
1. Return to Vornlin.

- At least 41s 2c
- [[Lucent Gem of Fortitude]]
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Elder's Nebulous Tunic]] (Shamans)
  - [[Shade's Nebulous Tunic]] (Assassin, Bard, Beastlord, Rogue, Ranger)
  - [[Channeler's Nebulous Robe]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Nebulous Tunic]] (Druids)
  - [[Enlightened Nebulous Tunic]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Archon's Nebulous Breastplate]] (Clerics)
  - [[Knight's Nebulous Breastplate]] (Crusaders & Warriors)
