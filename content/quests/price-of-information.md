## Notes

Upon completion of this quest [[Corpseolynne]] becomes a merchant who sells [[Crone-cast Focal Gem]] for 7g 20s for [[Visions of Vetrovia: A Smashing Success]]

## Steps

1. Gather the following
   - Gather 8 **Phantasmyl Gossamer** in [[Acrid Loft]] around:
     - -109.87 48.48 171.68 45.79
     - {{waypoint -94.94, 47.40, 173.97}}
     - {{waypoint -86.39, 47.91, 243.50}}
     - or [[Forlorn Steps]] around {{waypoint -104.41, 58.08, 424.70}}
     - {{waypoint -63.92, 32.72, 275.74}}
     - {{waypoint -87.64, 18.40, 327.54}}
     - {{waypoint -77.51, 19.31, 310.14}}
     - {{waypoint -54.25, 33.10, 263.99}}![Phantasmyl Gossamer](images/PHANTASMYLGOSSAMER.png)
       - *Note that the local inhabitants have blue spectral familiars. If it isn't a swirl, it's not the gossamer.*
       - Note: Turn on Particle Effects to max under Display options otherwise they can be almost invisible.
   - Gather 6 **Crimson Nihilite** from [[Somber Acre]] around:
     - {{waypoint 112, 46, 337}}.
     - {{waypoint 189.39, 57.05, 363.18}} ![Crimson Nihilite](images/Crystal_nihilite.png)
1. Return to [[Corpseolynne]].

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
