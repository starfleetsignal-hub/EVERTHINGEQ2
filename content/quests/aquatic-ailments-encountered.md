## Steps

1. Find items in the waters of the lagoon {{waypoint 53, -24, -102}} (you may need water breathing for this):
   - Gather 8 **Serrated Seagrass**![Brackish Clay](images/Brackish_Clay.jpg).
   - Gather 10 **Brackish Clay** -- in the shallowest water, mostly east of Khazla; **can** be seen from the surface.![Serrated Seagrass](images/Serrated_Seagrass.jpg)![Brackish Clay(seen from surface)](images/Brackish_Clay%28seen_from_surface%29.jpg).
1. Return to [[Khazla Aradir]].
1. Touch the sacred manta spirit {{waypoint 5, 9, -205}}, then follow it to where [[A diseased pike]] attacks {{waypoint 210, 7, -283}}.
   - Kill 8 more [[A diseased pike|diseased pike]].
1. Return to [[Khazla Aradir]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 52g 59s 84c
- [[Petamorph Wand: Sacred Manta]]
