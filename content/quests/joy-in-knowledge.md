## Steps

1. Talk to [[Grodney]] at {{waypoint -477, 165, -847}}.
1. Talk to [[Badalsonu]] at {{waypoint 671, -37, -299}} in [[Aetherscar]].![Badalsonu](images/Badalsonu%28injured%29.jpg)
1. Return to [[Grodney]] in [[Pakiat Bluffs]].
1. Return to [[Badalsonu]] in [[Aetherscar]] with [[An Allyrian poultice]].
1. Place [[Badalsonu's Escape Beacon]] at {{waypoint -440, 177, -523}}. ![Place Badalsonu's Beacon](images/Place_Badalsonu's_Beacon.jpg)
1. Speak to [[Badalsonu]] to receive [[An Allyrian flask]].
1. Return to [[Ta-hetu]].
