## Steps

1. Speak with "the Undercloud dweller", [[Miranna Ameema]] at {{waypoint -326.43, 118.56, 641.55}}
1. Find the entrance to the [[Sang'Huuu Parliament Chambers]] in [[Sanctuary (POI)|Sanctuary]] at {{waypoint 314.61, 305.08, 997.50}}.
1. Find a way to access the [[Sang'Huuu Parliament Chambers]] by completing the following quests from [[Jabber Longwind (Splendor Sky Aerie)|Jabber Longwind]] {{waypoint 211.60, 274.96, 821.76}}:
   1. [[Junior Hootie in the Making]]
   1. [[Earning Your Talons]]
   1. [[Screeching with Sang'Huuu Scouts]]
   - NOTE: If you completed the prerequisite quests before being on this step of Parliamentary Procedures, look for a green sparkly near Jabber to gather.
1. Enter the [[Sang'Huuu Parliament Chambers]] {{waypoint 312.84, 305.13, 997.11}}
1. Speak to [[Kindellan the Elder]] {{waypoint -834.55, 71.58, 541.64}} and [[Fullplume the Elder]].
1. Exit the [[Sang'Huuu Parliament Chambers]].
1. Return to [[Miranna Ameema]].
