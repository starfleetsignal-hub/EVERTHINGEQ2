## Steps

1. Use the [[Reet Engraved Knife]] to free 10 [[A reet slave|reet slaves]] at [[Dalnir's Wheel]]. {{waypoint 4, -124, 757}}
1. Return to Duz Heronimous.

  - [[Executioner's Truesteel Helmet of Dominance]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Illuminate's Still Water Skullcap of Overbearing]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Magus's Glowing Gossamer Cowl of Blasting]] (All Mages)
  - [[Outrider's Blacksteel Chain Helmet of Stealth]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Stormcaller's Spring Weave Cap of Restoration]] (Channelers, Druids)
  - [[Witchdoctor's Primal Wrath Coif of Commanding]] (Shamans)
