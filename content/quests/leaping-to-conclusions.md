## Steps

1. Collect 6 scattered fossils in the area. They look like piles of bones with bright white sparkles rising from them.
1. Find Saldas' Egg. It's by a small altar up against the mountainside. {{waypoint 73, 193, 646}}
1. Return to [[Saldas Rubbleground]].
1. Speak to [[Rell Ver'nief]], by the griffon tower at the [[Highland Outposts]]. {{waypoint 331, 189, 493}}
1. Collect items for Rell:
   - 5 bear fur from [[A slatepaw|slatepaws]]. {{waypoint 58, 187, 479}}
   - 5 drake blood samples from [[An emerald drake|emerald drakes]] {{waypoint -137, 165, -321}} or [[A mature emerald drake|mature emerald drakes]] {{waypoint -175, 153, 358}}.
   - Nest materials from [[Kragploom Mesa]]. {{waypoint 362, 373, -151}}
     - Kragploom Mesa is reached by climbing the wall at {{waypoint 495, 327, -127}}.
     - The nest you need to click on is in a wooden structure towards the back of the area.
1. Retun to Rell.
1. Collect 6 life essence from from [[A pulsing essence|pulsing essences]] {{waypoint 0, 178, 568}} or [[A wandering essence|wandering essences]] {{waypoint 164, 190, 40}}.
1. Speak with Saldas. {{waypoint 327, 194, 554}}
1. Fuse the mountain saliraptor essence by clicking on the sparkling bones next to Saldas.
1. Speak to Rell.
1. Apply the essence to the saliraptor egg by clicking on the sparkling egg next to Rell.
1. Speak to Rell.
1. Meet with the mountain saliraptor by clicking it and selecting 'reach out and pet the creature'.

## Rewards

- At least 21s 49c
- [[Scaled Mountain Saliraptor]]
