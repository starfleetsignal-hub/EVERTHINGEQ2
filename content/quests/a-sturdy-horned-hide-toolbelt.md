## Starting the Quest

- In the players' home of any Norrathian city

## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Horned Hide Toolbelt for my apprentice.
   1. Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Horned Hide Toolbelt]]
   1. Craft [[A Sturdy Horned Hide Toolbelt]] on a [[Loom]] using the following materials and fuel:
      - 2 [[Horned leather pelt|horned leather pelts]]
      - 4 [[Scintillating filament]]
1. I need to take this toolbelt back to my apprentice.
