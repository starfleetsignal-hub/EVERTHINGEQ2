*This quest is unavailable to characters of Good-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Slay the following creatures in Feerrott:
   - 3 [[A bitter bloom|bitter blooms]] {{waypoint 13, 4, -31}}
   - 3 [[A marsh damselfly|marsh damselflies]] or a [[Wetland damselfly|wetland dragonflies]] {{waypoint -46, -7, 473}}
   - 3 [[A muck dreg]] or [[A carrion dreg]] {{waypoint 85, -1, 79}}
1. Return to Krozk to receive your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 53s 57c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Bouncer's Earhoop of Force]] (All Mages)
  - [[Bouncer's Earring of Force]]
  - [[Bouncer's Earstud of Force]]
  - [[Bouncer's Hoop of Force]] (All Priests)
