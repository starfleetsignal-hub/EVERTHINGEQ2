## Steps

1. Obtain the following
   - Velium Crystalline Shard - Looted from the final boss of any Western Wastes or Blackhook Spiral Heroic II dungeon.
   - Velium Flux - Looted from the final boss of any Sodden Archipelago or Dragon Necropolis Heroic II dungeon.
   - 25 Scrolls of Lost Deific Magic - Looted from any non-final boss of any Heroic II dungeon.
