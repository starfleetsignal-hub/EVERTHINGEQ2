## Note

In order to complete this quest, a character on your account must have reached a certain amount of progress in the [[Visions of Vetrovia Timeline|Visions of Vetrovia Signature Adventure Questline]] or the [[Visions of Vetrovia Crafting Timeline|Visions of Vetrovia Signature Tradeskill Questline]] in order to access all of the zones.

The quest does not need to be turned in to the guides; you will receive the quest rewards once the last POI has been visited.

The POIs within a single zone can be visited in any order, however you must complete all the POIs in the current zone before the quest will update to proceed to the next zone.
