## Steps

1. Slay 8 [[A Numbfoot hunter|Numbfoot hunters]] in [[Everfrost]] to receive the [[The Spirit of Frigid Pine|spirit's]] blessing.
1. Return to the [[The Spirit of Frigid Pine|spirit]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- Experience
- The blessing of the [[The Spirit of Frigid Pine|spirit]] for a step in the quest [[In Search of a Menagerie]].
