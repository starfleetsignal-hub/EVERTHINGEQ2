Lepethida has sent me to retrieve some waters of the etherflow within an amphora.

## Steps

![Amphora](images/Vesspyr_Amphora.png)

1. Get an amphora within Falinpol
   - The [[A Falinpol Amphora|amphora]] is a blue-colored vase in the merchant's area around {{waypoint 25, 195, -5}}. It may appear in other [[Falinpol]] locations, including inside the tower near Nexus Core entrance {{waypoint 148, 188, -114}}.
1. Head to the etherflow pool {{waypoint -121, 198, 117}} and use the [[A Falinpol Amphora|amphora]] as an item to fill it.
1. Return to Lepethida

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
