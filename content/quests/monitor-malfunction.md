Brumley reported some strange readings from one of the monitoring devices I placed earlier. He asked me to take a look and make sure it's not malfunctioning.

## Steps

1. Check the sealed door near the [[Mushroom Bar and Grill]] around {{waypoint -45, -10, 34}}.
1. Click on one of the two bodies near the door {{waypoint -40.09, -3.92, 43.03}}.
1. Defeat the Solo micro-**RAID** [[Maldura: Unveiled Palace]] to continue.
   - *Note that at level-125/125, this step didn't even come-up and the quest skipped to informing Brumley and finding Brytthel.*
   - This step appears to no longer be included as it doesn't appear for a 100/100 either.
1. Return to [[Brumley Langboom|Brumley]].
1. Find [[Brytthel]] in the [[Palace of Unity]] {{waypoint 370.65, 82.35, 28.72}} and tell her the news.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13,200 status
- [[Coin of the Great Artificer]]
- +1,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +5,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
