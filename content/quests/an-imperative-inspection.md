## Steps

1. Talk to [[Anism]] {{waypoint -212, -87, -992}} on the way to the [[Serilian Spiral - Top|Serilian Spiral]]
1. Find five anchors:
   - First location by the tower at {{waypoint -61, 41, -849}}
   - Second location is at the [[Serilian Spiral - Top]] {{waypoint -164, 132, -969}}
   - Third location is down South ramp from elevator past Haldor end of the cave on top right. {{waypoint -117, -101, -790}}
   - Fourth is located on the right side of  the great door at the bottom on the guardians side {{waypoint -533, -300, -568}}
   - Fifth and final one is on the left side of the great door at the bottom on the guardians side {{waypoint -532, -300, -603}}.
1. Return to [[Filakt]]

1. +2500 faction with **Guardians of the Underfoot**
1. At least 55g 70s 61c
1. [[Mark of Manaar]]
