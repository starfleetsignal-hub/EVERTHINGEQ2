## Notes & Prerequisites

- [[Holly Rowan]] at the [[Village of Thundermist]] asks you to kill many of the same mobs for [[Helping Holly Rowan]] so it is a good idea to work the quests together.
- Turgen speaks only [[Halasian]] (the language of the [[Barbarian|Barbarians]]), therefore [[Halasian]] is required to obtain this quest.

## Steps

Turgen askes you to kill a varying number of any one of several types of mobs. These include:

- Sirens, such as those near {{waypoint 1481, -15, 250}}
- Skeletons (these are usually easiest to solo as there are enough solo skeletons just below the bridge where Turgen stands)
- Gnolls
- Giants
- Centaurs, such as those near {{waypoint -285, -62, -413}}

You can delete the quest and request a new one from Turgen if he offers you a choice you don't like.

## Rewards

- after several of these quests (usually 3), access to open the [[Bridge Keep]] gates during nighttime
