## Steps

1. Kill [[Gulgar the Usurper]], a giant albino molerat, in [[Darklight Wood]] {{waypoint 169, -62, -229}}.
- NOTE: You are not actually given an item in your inventory.
1. Feed the molerat blood to the wolf pups in the smaller cave just south west at {{waypoint 258, -65, -190}}.
- NOTE: Right click the wolf pups to feed.
1. Return to [[Nemain]].

## Rewards

- At least 4s 32c
- One of the following (based on class):
  - [[Archon's Barbute]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Hat]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Helm]] (Druids)
  - [[Elder's Helm]] (Shamans)
  - [[Enlightened Kasa]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Barbute]] (Crusaders & Warriors)
  - [[Shade's Cowl]] (All Scouts)
