## Steps

1. Destroy and slay the following
   - Destroy 8 **Eye of Night wards** in [[Kren Rommol]] {{waypoint -73.34, 23.23, -448.79}}. They're scattered throughout the area.  ![](images/One-Eyes_of_War_quest_-_Eye_of_Night_Ward.png)
   - Slay 12 [[A Swordfury worshipper|Swordfury worshippers]], also in [[Kren Rommol]].
1. Return to [[Ulgaf]].

## Rewards

- At least 2p 33g 88s 74c
- [[Eye of Night Ward]]
- One of the following:
  - [[Band of the Hounds' Devotion]]
  - [[Ring of the Hounds' Blessing]]
