## Notes

After completing this quest, if you'd like more trees, you can buy more tickets from questgiver for 1p each.

## Steps

1. Decorate areas of [[Gorowyn]] by clicking on green shinies in certain areas:
   - Near entrance to [[The Grand Athenaeum]] {{waypoint 2657, 83, 1317}}
   - [[Gorowyn City Services Complex]] {{waypoint 2648, 66, 1174}}
   - At the end of the outside dock by the Kylong Plains bell {{waypoint 2321, 1, 1455}}
1. On the platform near the foreign broker in [[The Timorous Maw]] {{waypoint 2489, 33, 1343}}
   - Defeat [[Mr. McScroogle]].
1. Return to [[Gutten Gigglegibber (Gorowyn)|Gutten Gigglegibber]].

## Rewards

- [[Frosty Frostfell Tree Ticket]]
  - To use the ticket double click the tree to the left with the blue star on top.
