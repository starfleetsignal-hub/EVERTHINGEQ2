## Notes

This is a Public Quest that is automatically inserted into your quest journal upon entering the designated area.

The quest begins every 60 minutes, at the top of the hour.  [[Researcher Kirat]] will shout to the zone at the top of the hour, "Calling all who are willing! I have been informed that an attack has been launched against the Combine Wizard Spires... We require your assistance! My sources say that the assault will land in 5 minutes!"

Throughout the waves, and immediately afterward, purple harvestable shinys will spawn in the vicinity of the Wizard Spire.  Each shiny will contain one [[Token of Greater Teleportation]].

## Steps

1. Protect the researchers
   - Waves of Ry'Gor orcs will spawn around the Wizard Spire area, attacking from two fronts:
     - {{waypoint -976, -343, 2255}}
     - {{waypoint -795, -340, 2387}}

## Rewards

- 25 [[Token of Greater Teleportation]]
