## Steps

1. You need to find 5 water barrels. Update comes from killing [[A holgresh heister]].(You can get the same spawn multiple times.)
   - Some spawn locs are:
     - water barrel {{waypoint -797, -651, -851}}
     - water barrel {{waypoint -938, -636, -1020}}
     - water barrel {{waypoint -1143, -636, -1004}}
     - water barrel {{waypoint -1295, -603, -1017}}
     - water barrel {{waypoint -1259, -601, -1156}}
     - water barrel {{waypoint -1082, -635, -973}}
1. Give the water to 3 dehydrated Soldiers in the camp.
   - a dehydrated Soldier {{waypoint -1716, -598, -1220}}
   - a dehydrated Soldier {{waypoint -1716, -598, -1213}}
   - a dehydrated Soldier {{waypoint -1704, -600, -1200}}
