## Steps

1. Destroy 6 snake cages and kill the snakes within. The cages can be found at the Bummer Gang camps in the Fog Hills{{waypoint -400, -8, -240}}. There are usually two cages per camp.  Possible snake types are [[A crimson death adder|crimson death adder]], [[An emerald asp|emerald asp]], [[A mottled rattleback|mottled rattleback]].
1. Discover the identity of the snake dealer. You can do this by examining a receipt lying on crates/barrels at the same camps. {{waypoint -379, -7.48, -249}}
1. Go to [[Shinspitter Camp]] at {{waypoint -661.77, -4.40, -223.70}}. [[Crumb Shinspitter]] ( Gives AA for kill) will attack you after you finish speaking with him.
1. Return to [[Marshal Samson Winnfield]]

- at least 9g 85s 50c

  - [[Fistmallet of the Cursed Plains]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Ghoulcrusher]] (All Priests)
  - [[Runic Foil of the Ethermage]] (All Mages)
  - [[Rusted Sceptre of the Undying Necromancer]] (All Mages)
  - [[Werewolf Piercer]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Windcaller's Sword]] (Druids, Channelers)
