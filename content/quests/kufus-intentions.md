## Notes

- This quest is only available to characters of evil alignment. Good aligned characters should complete [[A Split in the Ashen Order]] instead.
- You must have at least 30,000 faction with the Ashen Disciples to obtain this quest. It is not necessary to complete the preceding quests in this timeline such as [[Diplomatic Liaisons (Ashen Disciples)|Diplomatic Liaisons]].
- To obtain the required faction without doing the quest line, you can kill [[An Ashen Order monk|Ashen Order]] monks around [[T'Narev]]. If you are in a group, killing groups of Ashen Order monks will raise your faction with the Ashen Disciples much faster than completing the repeatable quests offered by [[Shing Ho]].

## Steps

1. Go to [[The Cave of Knowledge]] {{waypoint 94, -220, -1208}} and examine the scroll, [[An Observation on Quellious and The Disciples]], on the table next to [[Kufu, The Keeper of Knowledge]] {{waypoint 237, 2, 289}}. The entrance is found inside a cave at sea level, below [[T'Narev]].
   - When you are inside the Cave of Knowledge, you can find [[Kufu, The Keeper of Knowledge]] by always taking paths to the right when given a choice.
   - You can use stealth or invisibility to avoid the encounters inside the Cave of Knowledge and the camp where Kufu is located is safe.
1. Return to Shen Rai.
