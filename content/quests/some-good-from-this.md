## Steps

1. Collect 6 [[Virulid acid]] from virulent conquerors in the [[Hive of the Rogue]]. Respawn is fairly quick
1. Pour the acid on 6 sparkling patches on ground near Hive of the Rogue to uncover the minerals
1. Click twice to pick up minerals
1. Return minerals to [[Armsmaster Veyx]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Corrupted Chainlink Vambraces]] (Shamans)
  - [[Corrupted Combine Armor Pauldrons]] (Clerics)
  - [[Corrupted Treant Vambraces]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Withering Cloth Mantle]] (All Mages)
  - [[Withering Soul Chainlink Vambraces]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Corrupted Mist Panther Skin Shoulderguards]] (All Druids)
  - Brawler Shoulders
