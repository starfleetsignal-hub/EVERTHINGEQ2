## Steps

1. Gather reagents:
   - 8 [[Tormentil Leaf|tormentil leaves]] (found along the banks of [[The Long Draw]] {{waypoint -219, 7, -184}}.
   - 4 willowtip bear livers (from [[A willowtip grizzly|willowtip grizzlys]] and [[A willowtip mauler|maulers]]) - grizzlys are weaker and can be found around {{waypoint -600, -7, -140}}.
   - 4 mandrake roots (carried by [[A Bummer Gang gravecaller|Bummer Gang gravecallers]]) found at {{waypoint -364, 3, -264}}.

## Rewards

- At least 4g 80s 10c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Glorified Evoker's Sphere]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Humanity Sphere]] (All Mages)
  - [[Massive Sphere of Perfection Deflection]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Rust Runed Barb]] (All Priests)
