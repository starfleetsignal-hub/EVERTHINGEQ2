## Steps

1. Enter [[The Blackscale Sepulcher]] in [[The Bonemire]]. {{waypoint 106, 211, -648}}
1. Kill [[Oranti the Rizen]]. {{waypoint 163, 0, 183}}
1. Kill [[Grash]]. {{waypoint 165, -19, 17}}
   - To spawn, you must kill four [[A Steward of the Seal|Stewards of the Seal]] at the same time. The stewards have a 50% chance to spawn in place of [[A Blackscale Firebrand]], [[A Blackscale Razorwing]], or [[A Blackscale Plague Bearer]], found only in [[The Sealing Chamber]]. When a steward spawns, you must aggro but not kill it. Every minute or so the droags in the chamber will de-spawn and then respawn as another type, unless they are currently aggroed. Once you have aggro on four stewards, it is safe to kill them all and Grash will spawn.
1. Speak with [[Turadramin]]. {{waypoint 10, 0, 195}}
1. Retun to [[Arbiter Selek]].

## Rewards

- At least 13g 59s 50c
- [[Manacle of the Guilty]]
