## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of E'Ci has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items.

1. Purchase the [[Frozen Dryad Tear|frozen dryad tear]] from Kuutanya for 5000 status
   - Wanders the path on Isle of Wisdom in [[Zimara Breadth]])
1. Purchase the [[Cold Fire Ember|cold-fire ember]] from Buskin for 5000 status
   - Wanders in the [[Everfrost]] tunnel near the Permafrost entrance from {{waypoint 42.61, -4.90, -1185.80}} to {{waypoint 62.64, 22.83, -721.54}}
1. Purchase the [[Frost Touched Powder|frost-touched powder]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Return to [[Herald of the Wintry Guardian]] {{waypoint 75, -35, -1687}} in [[Everfrost]]
