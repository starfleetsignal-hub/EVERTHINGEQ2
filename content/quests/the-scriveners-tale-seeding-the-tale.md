## Required Materials

Bring at least:

- 3 [[Deadly terrorfruit]]
- 30 [[Reef cluster]]
- 9 [[Silvered fleck]]
- 12 [[Nimbic hide]]
- 50 [[Celestial Kindling]]

## Steps

1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 143, -179, -820}} to [[Crosswind Isle]].
1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint 384, 181, -250}} to [[The Aerie]] (portal with the purple feather).
1. Travel to the bird house at {{waypoint 890, 335, -8}} where [[Ajekz the Hungry]] is and receive [[Recipe Scroll: Satchel of Bird Seed]].
   - Note: Move into the small house after you get the update. There are aggro mobs that path in range of the update location.
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Satchel of Bird Seed]] and craft a [[Satchel of Bird Seed]] on a Stove.
   - There is a Stove nearby {{waypoint 932, 331, 23}} or there is a Stove in [[Myrist, the Great Library]] at {{waypoint 25, -20, 74}}.
1. Click on Ajekz the Hungry at {{waypoint 885, 335, -17}}.
1. Use the teleporter at {{waypoint 820, 357, 77}} to go to [[Crosswind Isle]] then take to [[The Windglades]] (portal with the tree) at {{waypoint 352, 180, -277}} - or Evac.
1. Go back to [[Recipes for Adventure (Eryslai)|Recipes for Adventure]] at {{waypoint 454, -202, -1432}} on [[The Windglades]] island.

## Rewards

- At least 3p 1g 92s 16c 48000 status
