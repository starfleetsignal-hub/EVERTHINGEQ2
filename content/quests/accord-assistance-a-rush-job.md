## Steps

1. Gather 20 swamp rushes to repair the netting on the transport balloons. They look like tall green grass and appear at random locations throughout the zone at the edge of water pools and streams.
1. Return to Hophip and put the swamp rushes in the delivery basket next to him.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2000 faction with **Survival Accord**
