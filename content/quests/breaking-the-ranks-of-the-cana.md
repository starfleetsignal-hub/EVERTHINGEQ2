## Description

I must enter Vasty Deep: The Vestigial Cella and kill the Ca'Na forces. These include the Ca'Na elitia, Ca'Na desecratia, Ca'Na oblitora, Ca'Na blasphem, and the Ca'Na ravagia.

## Steps

1. Kill the Ca'Na within [[Vasty Deep: The Vestigial Cella|the Vestigial Cella]].
   - Kill eight of any of the following: [[A Ca'Na elitia]], [[A Ca'Na desecratia]], [[A Ca'Na oblitora]], [[A Ca'Na blasphem]], [[A Ca'Na ravagia]].

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
