## Description

"Grimply Sniffwistle, a tinkering gnome at Spire Shadow Outpost, is determined to make a clockwork bixie to battle a pack of kobolds that have moved into the zone from Steamfont. I volunteered to obtain the underwood fae drake wings necessary to give the clockwork creation flight." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[Underwood fae drake|underwood fae drakes]], west along the river from the outpost near {{waypoint -160, 3, 173}}
1. Return to Grimply

## Rewards

- At least 4s 4c
- One of the following, based on class:
  - [[Fighter's Ring (Version 2)|Fighter's Ring]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Priest's Ring (Version 2)|Priest's Ring]] (All Priests)
  - [[Scout's Ring (Version 2)|Scout's Ring]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Sorcerer's Ring (Version 2)|Sorcerer's Ring]] (All Mages)
