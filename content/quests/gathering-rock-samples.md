## Steps

1. Collect the following items from [[The Caves]]:
   - A windspirit crystal sample (lower level around {{waypoint 51, 0, -112}}).
   - An elddar spark crystal sample (middle level in forge area {{waypoint 62, 12, -68}}).
   - A prexus tear crystal sample (behind the three alabaster behemoths {{waypoint -50, 16, -133}})
1. Return to Quardifle.

## Rewards

- At least 4s 21c
- One of the Following:
  - [[Crystal embedded earring]]
  - [[Crystal embedded ring]]
