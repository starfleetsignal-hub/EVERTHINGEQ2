- Thumore D'armer, a mysterious entity who I have assisted before within a dream plane, has appeared to me again! He told me of some troubled individuals, who cannot be woken from their dreams. He has sent me to enter their dreamscapes in an attempt to free them.

## Note

*In order to begin this quest you must first complete **both** [[Shattered Remains]] and [[Thumore's Absence]]
*This quest can be repeated annually, but [[Shattered Remains]] must be completed **every year** before you can do so.

## Steps

1. Thumore D'armer told me of Azhar Ellonialis, a troubled erudite within the city. I should do what I can to free him from his unnatural sleep.
   1. Enter Azhar Ellonialis' dream of Ultera
      - Find Azhar Ellonialis in Qeynos Harbor {{waypoint 688, -20, -84}}
      - Find Azhar Ellonialis in West Freeport in the Freeport Militia House (upstairs laying on a cot  {{waypoint 139, 1, 131}}, click on him to enter his dream.
   1. Find a way to wake Azhar Ellonialis from his dream
      - Kill **ALL** mobs (don't leave *any* or he won't talk to you) and make your way to Azhar {{waypoint -186, 12, -96}}. Speak to him to update quest.
      - Note: if Azhar is not standing in his expected spot, type: /target Azhar, then hit your "H" key to hail him.
1. I should return to [[Thumore D'armer]] within the city of Qeynos or Freeport.
     - In Freeport {{waypoint -67, -24, -42}}
     - In Qeynos {{waypoint 308, -22, -98}}

1. Thumore D'armer told me of Werri Stonebolt, a troubled coldain within the city of New Halas. I should do what I can to rescue her from her nightmare.
   1. Enter Werri Stonebolt's dream of Velious
      - Find Werri sleeping in New Halas {{waypoint -104, 148, 31}}. Click on her to enter her dream.
   1. Find a way to rescue Werri Stonebolt from her nightmare
      - Clear all mobs (same as above, kill them **ALL**) and then speak to Werri. {{waypoint -666, 117, 411}}
1. I should return to Thumore D'armer within the city of Qeynos or Freeport.
   - Return to [[Thumore D'armer]]
1. Thumore D'armer told me of Chullok Hir'touz, a troubled sarnak within the city of Gorowyn. I should do what I can to free him from his nightmare.
   1. Enter Chullock Hir'touz's dream of wurms
      - Go to Gorowyn and find Chullok Hir'touz. {{waypoint 2740, 130, 1190}}. Click on Chullok to enter his dream of wurms.
   1. Find a way to free Chullok Hir'touz from his nightmare
      - Clear all (That's right! **ALL**) mobs and then speak to Chullok. {{waypoint -30, -415, 13}}
1. I should return to Thumore D'armer within the city of Qeynos or Freeport.
   - Return to [[Thumore D'armer]]
