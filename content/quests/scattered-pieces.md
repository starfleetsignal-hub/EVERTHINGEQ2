Apparently, pieces of the shattered spires are still scattered across the lands, and in order to repair their damage, these pieces must be collected. I should be able to find the shards within any zone that contains one of the Ulteran Spires.

## Starting the Quest

- Talk to [[Salu Vel'nen]] in [[Lavastorm]] by the [[Ulteran Spire (LS)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -396, -31, -571}}
- Talk to [[Iwarii Blegath]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] by the [[Ulteran Spire (Zek)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint -37, -33, 355}}
- Talk to [[Talnya Umeni]] in [[Everfrost]] by the [[Ulteran Spire (EF)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint 448, -35, -1362}}
- Talk to [[Havann Elwaudi]] in [[Loping Plains]] by the [[Ulteran Spire (LP)|Ulteran Spire]] at {{waypoint 78, 37, -308}}

## Steps

![](images/Ulteran_Spire_Shard.jpg)

1. Collect 6 broken Ulteran Spire Shards.
1. Return to one of the four NPCs for your reward.

## Rewards

- 3 [[Quellithulian Token of Appreciation]]
