## Prerequisites

You need to have at least +10k faction with the Guardians of the Underfoot

## Steps

1. Use the potion Artyom gives to you on any elemental in the Underfoot section of the Hole
- Note: it works best on the blobs right in the beginning of the underfoot section.
1. Speak to Artyom

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]]
- At least 55g 70s 61c
- +2500 faction with **Guardians of the Underfoot**
