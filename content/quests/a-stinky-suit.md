## Steps

1. Collect materials to make a boarfiend costume:
   - 7 tufts of [[Boarfiend fur]], looted from the corpses of [[A Nuknok screamer|Nuknok screamers]]. {{waypoint 1110, 433, 574}}
   - Some garbage from inside the [[Boarfiend Caves]]. {{waypoint 1359, 456, -234}}
1. Bring the materials to [[Tandy Sprinklecog]] at [[Dropship Landing Zone]] to receive a [[Boarfiend Costume]]. {{waypoint 1782, 453, 943}}
1. Return to Nesbit.
