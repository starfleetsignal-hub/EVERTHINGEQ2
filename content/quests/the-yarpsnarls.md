## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks in no particular order:
   - Kill 7 [[A Yarpsnarl lookout|Yarpsnarl lookouts]] in the [[Yarpsnarl Camp]]
   - Kill 7 [[A Yarpsnarl ravager|Yarpsnarl ravagers]] in the [[Yarpsnarl Camp]]
1. Return to [[Argro Durthor]]

## Rewards

- At least 5s 67c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Brutal Expert's Arm Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Holy Acolyte's Plate Pauldrons]] (Clerics)
  - [[Infused Beguiler's Epaulets]] (All Mages)
  - [[Keen Bandit's Chain Spaulders]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Peerless Sentry's Pauldrons]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Pure Preserver's Shoulderpads]] (Druids, Channelers)
  - [[Wracked Soothsayer's Spaulders]] (Shamans)

Note that the quest [[Straight and to the Point]] gives the same armor rewards as this quest. This is probably a bug with this quest since the rewards are level 27 and this is a level 23 quest.
