## Steps

1. Gather 10 [[Abalone]] under the water line at {{waypoint -1391, -545, -2210}}.
   - From Arjon go north over the hill to crab gathering at bottom of hill.
   - Just walk into the water. You don't have to go underwater.
   - Abalones are on ice shelf where fish trapper is standing.
1. Return to Arjon {{waypoint -1378.59, -533.50, -2143.70}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +1,500 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
