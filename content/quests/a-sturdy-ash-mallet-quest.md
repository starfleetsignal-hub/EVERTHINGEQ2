## Steps

1. I need to craft a Sturdy Maple Mallet for my apprentice.
   - Scribe the [[Recipe: A Sturdy Ash Mallet]]
   - Craft [[A Sturdy Ash Mallet (Item)|A Sturdy Maple Mallet]] on a [[Woodworking Table]] using the following materials and fuel:
     - 2 [[Severed ash]]
     - 4 [[Sparkling Sandpaper]]
1. I need to take this mallet back to my apprentice.
