## Prerequisites

- You must speak the [[Goblish]] language to receive this quest.

## Notes

- This quest is also part of the [[Lavastorm Timeline]].

## Steps

1. Slay the following:
   - Slay 50 crawlers. These are centipedes, such as [[A juvenile lava crawler|juvenile lava crawlers]]. {{waypoint -428, -117, 343}}
   - Slay 50 drakes or wyrms. A nearby mob are the [[A magma wormling|magma wormlings]]. {{waypoint -148, -129, 477}}
1. Return to [[Charly Ashlash]] and examine the [[Old Worn Left Boot]] he gives you.
1. Find the corpse of a smelly fish to use as bait.
   - This is obtained from [[Foulgill the Rotten]] or [[A slightly smelly fish]]. {{waypoint -176, -138, 544}}
1. You will then be given two hours to bait the trap, which is located at {{waypoint -566, -113, 410}}.
   - *Be sure everyone in the group right-clicks the nest on the ground to get the update.*
1. A hungry goblin will run to the trap and immediately be consumed by [[Big Squiggly]]. Kill the monster to receive an [[Old Worn Right Boot]].
1. To make sure the boots are truly Efreeti made, Enter the [[Temple of Solusek Ro]] at {{waypoint -342.21, 1.25, -459.94}} and seek out [[Tazgar]] at    {{waypoint -329, -48, -680}}. When you speak to him 4-5 mobs will spawn. Kill them and speak to him again. Note: Exit from the Temple is {{waypoint -329, 3.58, -516.35}}.
1. Enter [[Solusek's Eye]] and kill Defenders of Thyr and Pyredukes of Thyr until you receive a key, which is a semi-rare auto-updating drop. You can find them on the fifth level around around  {{waypoint -109, -373, 39}} and you can get to them quickly taking the portal to [[The Speaker of Fates]] room if you've already unlocked it.
   - *Note: Navigating Solusek's Eye can be overwhelming. Refer to the [[Solusek's Eye#Geographic Walkthrough|walkthrough]] for directions to the third level.*
1. Once you have the key, use it to disable the spires back in Lavastorm. The following order is recommended for minimal backtracking:
   - **Spire One:** {{waypoint -634, -111, 301}}
   - **Spire Four**: {{waypoint -440, -99, 119}}
   - **Spire Five:** {{waypoint -567, -28, -410}}
   - **Spire Two:** {{waypoint 120, -5, -465}}
   - **Spire Three:** {{waypoint -115, 19, 75}}
1. When the spires have all been disabled, you must defeat [[The Magolemus]] at {{waypoint -247, 86, -225}}. He attacks with a knockback, so be sure to put your back to a wall.
1. After killing [[The Magolemus]], hail [[A Projection of Najena]] who spawns directly on top of the Magolemus's corpse.
   - **Warning:** The Projection of Najena will despawn very quickly.
   - *Note: If you cannot see the dialogue box for the Projection of Najena, lower your resolution setting to Extreme Performance.*
   - Note: You do not need to kill the Magolemus. You can hail the projection and get the update even if others have killed him.
1. Reactivate the spires that you disabled in Step 8.
1. Return to [[Tazgar]]. Enter the [[Temple of Solusek Ro]] at {{waypoint -342.21, 1.25, -459.94}} and seek him out at {{waypoint -329, -48, -680}}. Note: Exit from the Temple is {{waypoint -329, 3.58, -516.35}}.
1. Enter [[The Bastion of Flames]] within [[Solusek's Eye]] and kill [[Efreeti Lord Djarn]]. The entrance to this instance is on the fifth level at {{waypoint -152, -370, -288}}. The fastest way there is to take the teleporter to [[The Speaker of Fates]] room if you've unlocked it. Refer to the [[The Bastion of Flames|instance article]] for a walkthrough.
1. Return to [[Tazgar]] for your reward.
