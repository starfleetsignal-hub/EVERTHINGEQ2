   - Gather 12 **boshglub orbs** near {{waypoint 29.57, 1.15, -48.92}}. Possible locations include:![Boshglub Orb](images/Boshglub_orb.jpg)**Best place to look is near fishmen tents and lean-tos.
     - {{waypoint 48, 0, -23}}
     - {{waypoint 28, 1, -48}}
     - {{waypoint 15, 0, 5}}
     - {{waypoint 62, 0, -114}}
     - {{waypoint 55, 0, -119}}
     - {{waypoint 15, 0, -80}}
     - {{waypoint 97, 0, -34}}
     - {{waypoint 15, 0, -95}}
   - Hunt 8 [[A Mhyt-moo zealot|Mhyt-moo zealots]] for **runic shell strands** near {{waypoint 301.01, -0.95, -6.95}}.![a Mhyt-moo zealot](images/A_Mhyt-moo_zealot.jpg)
