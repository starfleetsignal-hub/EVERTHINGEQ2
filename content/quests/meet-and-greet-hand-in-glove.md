## Notes

A repeatable quest for Faction. Repeatable up to 3 times and only if your Port Woe faction is less than 20,000.  This quest requires:

- 9 [[Fox Pelt|Fox Pelts]]
- 3 [[Sedge Root]]
- 15 [[Zimaran Filament]]
- [[Recipe Scroll: Sturdy Work Gloves|Recipe]] for [[Sturdy Work Gloves]] auto scribed upon accepting quest

## Steps

1. Pick up quest with Garron first.
1. Craft 3 [[Sturdy Work Gloves]]
1. Speak with [[Garron]] at {{waypoint -31.37, 5.22, 357.79}}

## Rewards

- +1,000 faction with **Port Woe**
- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
