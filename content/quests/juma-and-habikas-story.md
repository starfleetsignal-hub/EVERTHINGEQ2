## Steps

1. Talk with [[Habika]] across and upstairs.
1. Go back and talk to [[Juma]] again.
1. Kill the [[Murkwater Villain]] in the Murkwater room down in [[The Serpent Sewer]] below [[Freeport]].
1. Return to Juma.
