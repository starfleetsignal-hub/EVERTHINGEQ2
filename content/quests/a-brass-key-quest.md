## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable.*
- Grants access to the instance: [[The Buccaneer's Rest]].
- You no longer need to be in a group of two or more in order to complete the quest; just enter the zone. However, if you are in a group, every person in the group must have a key and have examined it in order for the group to zone in.

## Steps

1. Examine the key and notice the skull and crossbones on the handle.
1. Find the entrance to [[The Buccaneer's Rest]] by clicking on the basement door at the base of the *Coldwind Lighthouse* at {{waypoint 343, -9, 830}}.

## Rewards

- One-time access to [[The Buccaneer's Rest]].
