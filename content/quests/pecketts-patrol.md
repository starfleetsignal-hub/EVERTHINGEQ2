## Notes

- Do NOT use a flying mount during this quest.

## Steps

1. Talk to [[Corporal Peckett]] at [[Coldwind Point]]. {{waypoint 337, -17, 736}}
1. Walk across the lighthouse bridge and follow the *Coldwind Trail*. {{waypoint 405, -24, 668}}
1. Continue along the trail. {{waypoint 421, -26, 590}}
1. Continue along the trail. {{waypoint 296, -21, 471}}
1. Continue to the trail end. {{waypoint 319, -19, 404}}
1. Speak with [[Corporal Batton]] near the gate to South Qeynos. {{waypoint 412, -21, 300}}
1. Go east toward [[Crater Pond]].
   - Patrol the north bank of Crater Pond. {{waypoint 149.11, -23.79, 392.05}}
   - Patrol the south bank of Crater Pond. {{waypoint 161.69, -20.67, 541.72}}
1. Go to where *Black Glade Trail* meets [[Qeynos Highway]]. {{waypoint -31.31, -8.77, 231.46}}
1. Follow the trail. {{waypoint -78.29, -25.90, 366.60}}
1. Follow the trail. {{waypoint -70.11, -26.71, 477.36}}
1. Follow the trail. {{waypoint -66.03, -24.25, 551.75}}
1. Talk to [[Knight-Captain Antillin]] at {{waypoint -107, -27, 600}}.
1. Return to [[Corporal Peckett]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 51c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Alliance's Circlet of Agility]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Concordium's Circlet of Concentration]] (All Mages)
  - [[Guard's Circlet of Brawn]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Watch's Circlet of Conviction]] (All Priests)
