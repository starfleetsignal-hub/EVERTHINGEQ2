## Steps

1. Click on a book at {{waypoint -125, -77, 71}}. The book is guarded by Thrott in the globe room.
   - Collect 20 Spectral Essences from the ghosts in the area.
   - As in the previous quest, you will need to enter the jail several times so you will probably need to kill an "undead warden" again several times to get "a skeleton key".
   - Retrieve the shank that you placed in the jail cell earlier during Paranoia at {{waypoint -190, -147, -237}}
1. Perform the ritual by clicking on the sparkly floor at {{waypoint -176.13, -149.36, -196.42}} then talk to [[Bronin Higginsbot]]
   - Sometimes there is no shiny or other indicator, just click around at {{waypoint -179.39, -149.19, -197.83}}
1. Collect the evidence page on the ground by the casket where you got the "details of Captain Boshinko's service amongst the books" in the last quest at {{waypoint -77.75, -126.91, -154.29}}
1. Go back to the jail and talk to Captain Boshinko in the Iron Maiden {{waypoint 178, 149, 197}}
1. Kill Chaperon OR Shade of Nightmares {{waypoint -89.67, -180.24, -274.49}}
1. Go back to the jail and talk to Captain Boshinko in the Iron Maiden {{waypoint 178, 149, 197}} to complete quest.
