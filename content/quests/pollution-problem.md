## Steps

- Travel to Antonica's [[Archer's Woods]] and enter this event-only zone through "An Enchanted Mushroom Ring" at {{waypoint -696.00, -4.00, 621.00}}.
1. Clean up the pollution by killing 5 [[Manifestation of pollution]] found in the lower water pool.
1. Gather cleansing water from [[A water sprite|a water sprite]] (left or right-click).  Do this from 5 times from different water sprites.
   - NOTE: You can only *click* each water sprite one time.
1. Use the cleansing water and cleanse 5 [[A tainted treespirit|tainted treespirits]] (left or right-click).
   - They will turn in to a [[A cleansed treespirit|cleansed treespirit]]
   - One tainted treespirit is at {{waypoint -79, -67, -319}} the others are around {{waypoint -55, -63, -361}}
1. Return back to the Treespirit Elder
