## Steps

1. Travel to [[Antonica]] and gather the following in order:
   1. Take 1 Acorn Stash in Archer's Woods at {{waypoint -711.17, -3.08, 608.08}}
   1. Pick up 3 pine cones at the base of the pine tree at the top of the waterfall around {{waypoint -1792, 63, 938}}
   1. 3 Fresh Produce around {{waypoint -1926, 18, -101}}
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].

## Hints

- Gathered items are not trackable
- Acorn Stash is not a group update, but spawns instantly after clicked

## Rewards

- Unlock the [[Days of Summer: 2023|Days of Summer 2023: Week 1]] Holiday event rewards that you can purchase from [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2293, -199, 2506}}
