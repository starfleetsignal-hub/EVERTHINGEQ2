## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Mithaniel Marr has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items.

1. Purchase the [[Amazonite Heart|amazonite heart]] from Tolinvar for 5000 status
   - In The Commonlands, wanders along the road called Shiv Lane north of The Crossroads, from {{waypoint -243, -46, -420}} to {{waypoint -89, -48, -642}}
1. Purchase the [[Oathbound Blade Fragments|oathbound blade fragments]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the [[Truth Speaker Braid|truth speaker braid]] from Laparnuu for 5000 status
   - in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]], near Petalcrown Perch and the Roost Warrens.  Laparnuu wanders the path from {{waypoint 338, 238, 7}}  all the way to {{waypoint 31, 236, -13}}. The guards at Petalcrown Perch can give you Laparnuu's exact location.
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Truthbringer]] at {{waypoint -840, -48, 199}} in [[The Commonlands]]
