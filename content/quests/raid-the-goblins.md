## Steps

1. Click the Pillar of Screaming Skulls to lure out the Warclerk. {{waypoint 170, -100, -970}}
   - If you have more than one person in your party with this quest they each will need to examine the pillar.
1. Kill [[The goblin Warclerk]] (lvl 55vv defiler plus mob of about 6 lvl 50v)
1. Examine the scroll in front of the tent. {{waypoint 254.8, -106, -1035.5}}
1. Return to [[Captain Sterling]].

## Rewards

- +1000 faction with **The Swiftrider Mercenaries**
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Damned Effigy of a Lost God]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Glimmering Stone of Energy]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Totem of the Acidic Storm]] (All Mages)
  - [[Undertaker's Clawed Fist]] (???)
