## Steps

1. Cure the trees by using the received ***Merza's Sick Tree Ointment*** while standing between the roots in the greyish mist.
   - Use the ointment on the tree near Ykesha’s Spine {{waypoint 143, -87, -189}}
   - Use the ointment on the tree near the entrance to Tupta {{waypoint 49, -78, 110}}
   - Use the ointment on the tree between the Anchor of Bazzul and Grobb {{waypoint -138, -84, -211}}
   - Use the ointment on the tree behind Firmroot Moot {{waypoint 221, -85, -567}}
1. Return to Merza.

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2,000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
