## Steps

1. Harvest a small glowing point on the road as you start to pass the north end of [[Behemoth Pond]] island. {{waypoint -341, 0, -348}}
   - The small glowing point looks like a collection quest shiny but without the** ?** above it.
1. Swim to the west side of the river and talk to [[A lost sentry]]. {{waypoint -125, 0, -376}}
   - *Note*: [[A lost sentry]] will despawn after you talk to him.
   - Three [[A corpse beetle (Nektulos Forest)|corpse beetles]] will burst from the sentry. Kill them if you want but they are not needed for the quest.
1. Return to Lieutenant Kaneth.

## Rewards

- At least 1s 59c
- +150 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
