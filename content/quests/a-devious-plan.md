## Steps

1. Repeat the following 5 times:
   - In [[The Drake Breeding Ground|The Drake Breeding Grounds]] on [[The Isle of Awakening]] there are piles of broken egg shells. At these locations there may be unique harvesting nodes called 'a mature egg'.
   - Target the egg and harvest it.
   - While standing close to an egg shell pile, use the [[Organo-Synthetic Egg Diagnosticator]] and the egg will be transformed.
   - If the Diagnosticator disappears, talk to [[Taer Felwind (NPC)|Taer Felwind]] and ask him for a new one.
1. Return to Taer.
1. Return to Harun on Whisperwind Isle. Summon him with your pipe reed if necessary. Speak to him to complete the quest.
