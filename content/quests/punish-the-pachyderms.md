## Steps

1. Kill 9-11 [[An elephant (Commonlands)|elephants]] in the [[The Commonlands]].  These can be found {{waypoint -349, 0, -499}} just northwest of the [[Crossroads]].
   - the [[A plains elephant|plains elephants]] near [[Pride Lake]] do *NOT* satisfy this quest.

- 1211 status @ lvl 11
- 1000 status @ lvl 15

- +500 faction with **The Freeport Militia**
