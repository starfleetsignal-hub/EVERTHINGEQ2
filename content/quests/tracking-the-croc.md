## Steps

1. Click the ship wheel next to [[Captain Azhub]] to zone into the instance, [[Azhub's Ship]], and speak with Captain Azhub once inside.
   - Once Captain Azhub is spoken to inside the instance, a script is started. If you delete this quest past this point, you will have to zone out and reset the instance.
1. Kill the [[Big old croc]] when he appears.
1. Kill 3 [[A furlong froglok|furlong frogloks]] when they become attackable after defeating the trolls.
   - -100 faction with **Guktan Guard** per froglock killed.
1. Kill [[Pipi Proudblade]], the leader of the frogloks (level 80^).
   - -100 faction with **Guktan Guard**.
1. Return to the [[Moors of Ykesha]] and speak with Captain Azhub. Click the ship wheel to be zoned out back to [[Salty Sail Port]].

## Rewards

- At least 12g 5s 35c
- [[Seafarer's Orbs of Tranquility]]
