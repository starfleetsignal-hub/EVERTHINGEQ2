## Notes

Timed mini-quest

- About 7 min 40 sec to complete.

## Steps

1. Clear all mobs before starting to avoid delays.
1. Click the second **rune foundation** at {{waypoint -90.75, -38.95, -268.20}}.
1. Click the third **rune foundation** at {{waypoint 89.63, -38.45, -269.17}}.
1. Run past the final **rune foundation** and around the bend in the corridor beyond to {{waypoint 262.25, -38.73, -264.39}}.
   - Slay [[Izzak Sira]]. If you can't simply burn him down, then lead Izzak Sira to the each of the other three rune foundations. There will be a different colored circle on the ground next to each foundation. Pass him through the circles until you receive the message that he can take damage now, then kill him.
   - Loot the [[Rune of Awakening]].
1. Click the final **rune foundation** at {{waypoint 122.79, -38.54, -400.77}}.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
