## Steps

1. Leaving the docks, turn right and get an auto-update under the first clothsline at {{waypoint -37, 0, 36}}.
1. Continue north along the east Big Bend for auto-update at {{waypoint -49, 4, -5}}.
1. Continue on to the intersection near the entrance to [[South Freeport]] for auto-update at {{waypoint -4, 3, -30}}.
1. Head to [[The Torch of Oggok]] at {{waypoint 11, 0, 9}}.  A halfling named [[Bushy Swashbottom]] will spawn and attack you - slay Bushy.
1. Return to Braz Gutpounder.

## Rewards

- At least 1s
- [[Braz's Stool]]
