## Steps

1. Enter [[Karnor's Castle]] and swim in the moat around {{waypoint -163, -63, 174}} to overhear the conversation.
1. Return to [[Ebet Corscrag]] outside Karnor's Castle.

  - [[Executioner's Cobalt Gauntlets]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Illuminate's Fallen Leaves Hand Wraps]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Magus's Ensorcelled Satin Gloves]] (All Mages)
  - [[Outrider's Blue Iron Chain Gloves]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Stormcaller's Winter Weave Leather Gloves]] (Channelers, Druids)
  - [[Witchdoctor's Ill Omen Handguards]] (Shamans)
