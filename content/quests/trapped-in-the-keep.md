Grassblade was aghast to hear how the life energies of his Green Hood comrades who fell when Deathfist Citadel was retaken by the Deathfist forces, have somehow been stopped from rejoining the life flow. Being an accomplished burglar, he has volunteered to not only show me how to gain access to Scourge Keep, but to also help me solve this mystery.

## Steps

1. Enter Scourge Keep by clicking the sewer entrance beneath the [[Bridge of Zek (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Bridge of Zek]] {{waypoint -210, -25, -538}}.![To Scourge Keep](images/Scourge_Keep.jpg)
   - The entrance is underwater at {{waypoint -192.61, -36.12, -579.35}}
   - The [[A needler|needler]] fish do not see invis.
1. Speak with [[Grassblade (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)‎|Grassblade]] once inside the Keep {{waypoint -7.05, -17.03, -124.93}}. ![Grassblade](images/Grassblade.jpg)
   - He is 180° around the moat from where you came in.
   - Note: [Heroic] instance has orcs that can see invis.
   - Note: Ramp to Grassblade's right has orc patrolling to water's edge and will agro any who happen to be speaking with the Halfling.
   - Tip: Don't kill any (or as few as possible) Orcs prior to getting to Grassblade. Right by Grassblade is a skeleton on a pike. Clicking that will start a timed quest to kill 100 Orcs in 10 minutes.![skeleton on a pike](images/Skeleton_on_a_pike.jpg)
   - Tip: After zoning in, pick up the item [[A blood sap-tipped arrowhead]] off the guy pinned to the tree with arrows. {{waypoint 140.03, -12.64, 138.75}}
1. Search for a missing Green Hood.
   - Speak to a [[A Green Hood spirit]] {{waypoint 45.38, -9.62, -102.98}}
   - In order to gain entrance into the Keep, you will need to do one of 2 things:<br>**(a)** Kill the crocodiles in the moat until you get a brief cinematic, then kill [[Viletooth]] at the north end of the moat. Or<br>**(b)** Kill orcs around the outside of the moat, be sure to get the timed quest to kill 100 orcs.  Then you can access the sewers.
   - Speak to another lingering [[A Green Hood spirit (Supplier's Hoard)|Green Hood spirit]] in [[The Supplier's Hoard]] {{waypoint 23.40, 0.07, 19.46}}.
     - This spirit becomes aggro and attacks.
   - You now need to enter the Keep by killing another named. To see how to do that, see the page on [[Scourge Keep (Advanced Solo)]] [[Onok (Advanced Solo)|Onok]] is usually recommended.
   - A convenient way to enter the Keep from here, is by jumping down the well at {{waypoint 26, 0, -27}}. (You can use the rope to climb back up if needed.) Then, a short swim, head for a lower door at {{waypoint 17, -10, 22}} passing through a mine along the way.
   - Speak to another [[A Green Hood spirit (Depleted Armory)|Green Hood spirit]] in [[The Depleted Armory]] {{waypoint -11, 1, 20}}.
   - Speak to another [[A Green Hood spirit (Qulkor's Rest)|Green Hood spirit]] in the jail ([[Qulkor's Rest]]) {{waypoint -15, -10, 7}}.
1. Rendezvous with Grassblade in the sewers under Scourge Keep.
   - Jump down the well into the sewers.  Grassblade is right there at {{waypoint 34, -11, -28}}.
   - Talk to him to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s 13200 status
- +500 faction with **The Green Hoods**
