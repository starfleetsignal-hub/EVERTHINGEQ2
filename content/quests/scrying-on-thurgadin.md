## Steps

1. Place a Rime scrying stone in Thurgadin Harbor on the top scaffold {{waypoint 1214, -520, 359}}
1. Place a Rime scrying stone in Thurgadin, City of the Coldain, Throne Room {{waypoint 124, -278, 258}}
   - You will be attacked by 3 [[A Thurgadin highwatch|Thurgadin highwatch]]es (two 90v, one 90vv) when you try to place this one.  The quest updates when they are all dead
1. Return to Bargbae

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
- +800 faction with **The Order of Rime**
