This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Collect a sample from the meat platter in the [[Refectory Hall]] {{waypoint -3, 42, -153}}.
1. Collect a sample from the dessert platter in the [[Refectory Hall]]. (Immediately next to the buffet with the meat platter.) {{waypoint 2, 42, -153}}
1. Take note of the arrangement of the fruit and vegetable platters (in the [[Hall of Mana]] just past the first two updates in the Refectory). {{waypoint 10, 44, -209}}
1. Return to [[Johara Najjar]].
