## Steps

1. Do the following in any order:
   - Slay [[A dune tarantula|dune tarantulas]] for ten of their silk. {{waypoint -794, -96.52, -781.71}}; common update
   - Slay [[A sentinel of Anuk|sentinels of Anuk]] for an ethereal fragment. {{waypoint -625, -165, -1375}}; uncommon update
   - Slay [[An exiled Kromtorr|exiled Kromtorr]] for a thread. {{waypoint -466, -95, -394}}; uncommon update
   - Slay [[A dustwhip scorpion|dustwhip scorpions]] for a perfectly shaped chitinous needle. {{waypoint -178.91, -100.15, -696.29}}; uncommon update
   - Harvest 'an exotic plant' at the northern oasis of the [[The Twin Tears|Twin Tears]].{{waypoint -380, -146, -1159}} or {{waypoint -380.02, -149.08, -1099.37}}![The exotic plant, for the dye](images/An_exotic_plant.jpg)
   - Find 'a Wisp of Air from the highest dune'. Go to {{waypoint -945, -44, -846}}. The spot you're looking for is not visible. Move your mouse over the area until a hand icon appears & harvest your wisp.
1. Return to [[El'Khazi, Purveyor of Fine Carpets]] to receive your reward.
