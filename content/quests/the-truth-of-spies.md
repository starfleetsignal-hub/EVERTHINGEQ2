## Notes

Scales with player level, requires level 80+

## Steps

1. Check information drop-off locations. These may be done in any order:
   - Examine a brown vase on the stairs leading down to the underground harbor in the [[Elddar Grove]], [[Qeynos Province District]] to find a message left for the dead spy [[Ocron]]. {{waypoint 602, -31, -241}}
   - Inspect an upside-down basket along the side of the [[Harbor Cache]] building in [[Qeynos Harbor]], [[Qeynos Province District]] to find a message left for the dead spy [[Palax]]. {{waypoint 779, -20, -8}}
   - Check the dartboard under the stairs in [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn]] in [[Qeynos Harbor]], [[Qeynos Province District]] to find a message left for the dead spy [[Rarezut]]. {{waypoint 802, -22, 68}}
   - Inspect a bucket of fish behind the [[Lion's Mane Inn]] in [[South Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]] to find a message left for the dead spy [[Bethaz]]. {{waypoint 531, -9, 124}}
   - Search a small box under the aquaducts almost all the way to the northeast wall in [[North Qeynos]], [[Qeynos Capitol District]] to find a message left for the dead spy [[Hilirgo]]. {{waypoint 284, -21, -187}}
1. Return to [[Lachlyn Cauldthorn]].

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c (at level 92)
- At least 1p 25g (at level 98)
