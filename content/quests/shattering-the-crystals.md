## Notes

- This quest unlocks [[The Crypt of T'haen: Endless Twilight]], a sub-instance of [[The Crypt of T'haen]]. This quest is completed inside the instance.

## Steps

1. Enter the [[The Tombs of Night]] instance to access the [[The Crypt of T'haen: Endless Twilight]]. The entrance is only active in the [[The Tombs of Night]] instance at {{waypoint -107, -32, 102}} a location near where you previously killed [[Dryxl T'Rath]].  Receive a quest location update after zoning into [[The Crypt of T'haen: Endless Twilight]] at {{waypoint 32.16, 5.70, 92.12}}.
1. Find and destroy the crystals located around the crypt (this is the quickest order):
   - {{waypoint -60, 0, -48}}  (Second crystal, lower floor)
   - {{waypoint 49, 16, -71}}  (First crystal, middle floor)
   - {{waypoint -29, 24, -24}}  (Fourth crystal, middle floor)
   - {{waypoint -93, 24, -30}}  (Third crystal, middle floor)
1. Return to [[Arthial the Protector]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 1g 21s
- One of the following:
  - 2 [[Potion of Marr|Potions of Marr]]
  - 2 [[Salve of Marr|Salves of Marr]]
- The ability to buy the previous quest rewards.
