## Steps

1. Scribe [[Learning To Weave Fine Fabrics]].
1. Craft eight [[Simple Roll of Fabric|Simple Rolls of Fabric]]. (You can find a Sewing Table & Mannequin at {{waypoint -312, 177, -54}} )
   - Materials needed to craft
     - 16 tuber strands
     - 8 severed maples (recipe specifies maple lumber)
     - 16 glowing filaments
1. Replace the first roll at {{waypoint 64, 153, -126}}.
1. Replace the second roll at {{waypoint 60, 153, -125}}.
1. Replace the third roll at {{waypoint 56, 153, -131}}.
1. Replace the fourth roll at {{waypoint 55, 153, -122}}.
1. Replace the fifth roll at {{waypoint 53, 153, -130}}, top.
1. Replace the sixth roll at {{waypoint 53, 153, -130}}, one down.
1. Replace the seventh roll at {{waypoint 53, 153, -130}}, two down.
1. Replace the eighth roll at {{waypoint 53, 153, -130}}, bottom.
1. Return to [[Envoy Tami Swifthammer|Tami]].
