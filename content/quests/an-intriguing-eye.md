## Notes

- Access quest for [[Crow's Resting Place]].

## Steps

1. Kill [[A Bloodsaber meddler|Bloodsaber meddlers]] around {{waypoint 6, 0, -109}} until you receive [[A Bloodsaber meddler note]].
1. Examine the note.
1. Speak to [[Gornit Penwiggle]] at The Qeynos Linguistics (near the South Qeynos gate) in [[Qeynos Province District]] {{waypoint 829.37, -23.55, 124.54}}.
1. Slay monsters in The Down Below for Gornit
   - Three bladders of [[A coiled burrower|coiled burrowers]] {{waypoint -66, 0, -154.5}}
   - Four froths from [[A rabid shriller (Down Below)]] {{waypoint -192, 0, -61}} *([[A rabid shriller (Vermin's Snye)]] also updates)*
1. Speak to [[Gornit Penwiggle]] in [[Qeynos Province District]] {{waypoint 829.37, -23.55, 124.54}}.
1. Speak to [[Proprietor Blagard]] upstairs in The Collector's Cove (north of the Inn) in [[Qeynos Province District]] {{waypoint 827.30, -13.99, 33.58}}.
1. Speak to [[Constable Arathur]] outside The Harbor Cache (near the Ak'Anon clock) in [[Qeynos Province District]] {{waypoint 779.01, -20, -14}}.
1. Kill [[A fallen noble]] in the Down Below. {{waypoint -172, 10, -5}}
1. Approach the hand statue at {{waypoint -143, -0, -74}}. It is also the entrance to [[Crow's Resting Place]] after the quest update.

## Rewards

- Access to [[Crow's Resting Place]]
- At least 5s 67c
