## Steps

Create 12 items from the random list below:

- [[Battery and Assault IV|Battery and Assault IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Head Shot II|Head Shot II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Breathtaking Bellow|Breathtaking Bellow (Journeyman)]]
- [[Bloody Reminder III|Bloody Reminder III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Disarming Grin II|Disarming Grin II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Thunderous Overture II|Thunderous Overture II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Perfect Shrill IV|Perfect Shrill IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Hidden Shot II|Hidden Shot II (Journeyman)]]
- [[Luda's Nefarious Wail IV|Luda's Nefarious Wail IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Deadly Focus|Deadly Focus (Journeyman)]]
- [[Gushing Wound IV|Gushing Wound IV (Journeyman)]]
- [[Snap of the Wrist III|Snap of the Wrist III (Journeyman)]]
- [[Hymn of Horror|Hymn of Horror (Journeyman)]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

## Rewards

- At Least 36s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
