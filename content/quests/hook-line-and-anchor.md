- Examine the [[Earring of Void Channeling]] (can be any zone)

1. Enter the [[Munzok's Material Bastion]]
1. Defeat [[Yzlak]], Master of all Forms
1. Defeat [[Ozyk]], Master of Blades
1. Defeat [[Umzok]], Senior Adjutant
1. I must slay [[Gozak]], Destroyer of the Kazar!
