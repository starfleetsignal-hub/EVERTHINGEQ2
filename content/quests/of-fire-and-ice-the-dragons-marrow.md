## Steps

1. Examine the Dragon's Marrow in your inventory.  This starts the quest.
1. Speak to [[Jaestin Chaldean]] back in [[The Tower of the Moon]].  He gives you [[Arendahr's Notes]]
1. Obtain the following updates:
   - Loot a frosted covered pommel gem from a chest dropped by [[Imzok's Revenge]] in [[The Protector's Realm]].
   - Loot a blessed scroll of ochliac from a chest dropped by [[The Leviathan]] in [[The Chamber of Destiny]].
   - Kill [[Druushk]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]].
1. Talk to [[Jaestin Chaldean]] back in the Tower of the Moon twice to obtain the mythical weapon.
