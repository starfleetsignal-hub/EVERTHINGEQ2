## Steps

1. Use your abilities to query and dominate [[A docile fire elemental]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]]'s [[Skyfire Mountains]] at the same location you previously did {{waypoint -1365, 207, 271}} .
   - A level 80^ [[Fire elemental]] will spawn in its place and attack you.
   - You are looking for an uncommon body drop called [[Lesser Element: Fire]]. You need to collect 10 of these.
     - When you have all 10, right-click on the stack in your inventory and select 'Use' to receive the update.

- Experience Only
