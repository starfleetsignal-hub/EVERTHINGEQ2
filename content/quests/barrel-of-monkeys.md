## Notes

- 13 Minutes Total time, repeatable on failure 10-15? mins, 3? hours on success, ease of quest tricky train aggro and knocked out of flight due to combat may slow things down.

## Steps

1. Click barrel to start.
1. Search [[Esoterra Gardens]] for an escaped siamang, the lowland siamang do not count.
1. Kill/knock out the escaped monkey. Pick it up only after you get rid of any trained additional aggro you may have picked up while moving around.
1. Do not drop the unconscious monkey on the ground it will escape and you will have to find another, place the unconscious monkey on top of the barrel, like you would a house item.
1. Repeat above steps 14 more times to complete.
1. Monkeys in the barrel start to escape at 15 placed, spawns a name, kill name, complete quest, name spawns at the barrel, looks like a mini King Kong.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 68g 39s
- 5 encrusted planar key
