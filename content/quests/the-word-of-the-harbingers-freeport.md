This quest is from Part 2 of the Beastlord Prelude and is expected to only be available until the release of the Age of Discovery expansion.

## Steps

Speak to Olgen at {{waypoint -74, -8, -113}} outside the [[Temple of War]] in [[North Freeport]] to begin this quest. You must have completed the quest Mutual Adversaries to receive this one.

1. Enter the spiritual pocket in [[The Commonlands]]. This is found east of the Crossroads at {{waypoint -716, -31, -276}}.
1. Click near the bottom of the portal to enter the zone Gathering of the Harbingers.
1. Speak to Elder Animist Sahdi at {{waypoint 34, 0, 12}}.
1. Speak to the other members of the order:
   - Elder Patriarch Vuzx - gives the subquest [[Vuzx's Trial]]
   - Field Priest Keven - gives the subquest [[Keven's Trial]]
   - Gardunk - gives the subquest [[Gardunk's Trial]]
   - Animist Poren - gives the subquest [[Poren's Trial]]
   - Bordag - gives the subquest [[Bordag's Trial]]
1. Collect the 5 medallions by completing the Harbinger's trials.
1. Speak to Elder Animist Sahdi.
1. Return to Olgen in [[North Freeport]] to complete the quest.
