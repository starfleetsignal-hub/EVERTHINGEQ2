## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
  - Quest becomes repeatable after initial completion. No Scarred Dinars reward on repeats.

## Steps

1. Gather and Hunt the following
   - Gather either
     - 8 Temple Gold (Yellow rocks) from [[The Venerable Absence]] around {{waypoint 18.08, -23.60, -670.55}} or
     - 8 Vrakorr-soaked velium (Blue Rocks) from [[Icespine]] around {{waypoint 514.25, 135.01, -400.81}}
   - Hunt either
     - 12 briargloom menacers for scales around {{waypoint 18.08, -23.60, -670.55}} or
     - 12 ice-formed dragonkin for frozen arcanaum on [[Icespine]]
1. Return to [[Mergus Stonesledge]]

## Rewards

- [[Vrakorr-soaked Velium]] house item
- 50 x [[Scarred Dinar]] -- *Only the first time*
- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
