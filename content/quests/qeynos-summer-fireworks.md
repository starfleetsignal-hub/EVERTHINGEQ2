## Notes

- It's suggested that you turn off Flora in Options -> Display, as it makes it much easier to see the fireworks launchers.
- 2011: This quest only lasts through midnight Pacific time, July 4th.
- The Summer Fireworks quests can be found in Gorowyn, Kelethin, Neriak, New Halas, Qeynos, and Freeport.

## Steps

![Image of a celebration fireworks launcher](images/Celebration_fireworks.png)

1. The local fireworks coordinator noticed that I have a festive sparkler, and asked if I would like to help set off some of the fireworks in the city.
   - Light celebration fireworks in North Qeynos (0/10).
   - These look like bottles that you can interact with by clicking on. They are found outside all over North Qeynos.
   - Known locations include:
     - {{waypoint 329, -21, -26}}
     - {{waypoint 360, -21, -27}}
     - {{waypoint 392, -18, -162}}
     - {{waypoint 432, -21, -253}}
     - {{waypoint 440, -21, -280}}
     - {{waypoint 431, -21, -287}}
     - {{waypoint 460, -20, -346}}
     - {{waypoint 481, -20, -334}}
     - {{waypoint 454, -19, -322}}
     - {{waypoint 463, -19, -317}}
1. After you get all 10 the quest will automatically complete. The quest is repeatable.
