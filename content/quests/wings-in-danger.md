## Steps

1. Check on the sokokar herd
   - Check on the crusaders {{waypoint -70, 81, 564}} (left click either of the bodies)
   - Inspect the Herds {{waypoint -129, 122, 494}} (auto update)
   - Clear out 12 Poachers ([[A Barrens bandit|Barrens bandits]] and [[A Barrens poacher|poachers]]. Found up and down the path and in the bluffs around herd area.
1. Speak with [[Herdsman Sivik]] {{waypoint -172, 126, 535}} (He won't actually give you anything, the beacon torches are clickable objects on the bluff.)
1. Place the Beacon Torches:
   - {{waypoint -176, 134, 427}}
   - {{waypoint -165, 133, 432}}
   - {{waypoint -123, 134, 430}}
   - {{waypoint -124, 134, 418}}
   - {{waypoint -113, 134, 418}}
1. Return to Herdsman Sivik.
1. Return to Captain Tazthas

## Rewards

- At least 1p 53g 9s
- 14622 status
- [[Sokokar Tooth Earcharm]]
