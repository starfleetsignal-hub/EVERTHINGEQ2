## Steps

1. Identify key locations in [[Klak'Anon]]
   - Locate [[The Clockwork Factory]]. {{waypoint 163, -12, 3}}
   - Locate [[The Clockwork Assembly Plant]]. {{waypoint -141, 21, 54}}
   - Locate the [[Clockwork Hydroelectric Dam]]. {{waypoint -47, -5, -122}}
   - Locate the [[Mechanamagica Retrofitting]].
     - Location is {{waypoint -223, -11, -93}}, down a tunnel next to Clockwork Administrator Shadowbolt at {{waypoint -240, 7, -44}} in [[The Clockwork Commons and Maintenance]] that can be easy to miss.
1. Report back to [[Agent Blenkoweeble]] in Gnomeland Security HQ.
1. Eliminate some key clockwork targets inside Klak'Anon to disrupt their operations
   - Kill four [[Clockwork engineer|clockwork engineers]] in the Clockwork Assembly Plant
   - Kill [[Factory Overseer Klankbolt]] in the Clockwork Factory
   - Kill [[Waterwork Operator Malscrew]] in the Clockwork Hydroelectric Dam
   - Kill [[Master Magus Thornbolt]] in the Mechanamagica Retrofitting
1. Report back to Agent Blenkoweeble in Gnomeland Security HQ.

## Notes for Mechanamagica Retrofitting

Unless the mobs are gray to you when you arrive in this room, you will very likely discover that there is a seemingly endless supply of the fast-moving, aggressive and see-through-invis [[A mechnamagica hunter-seeker|mechnamagica hunter-seekers]] flying overhead.  Peeking in the door is enough to pull anywhere from one to a swarm of these critters, and no matter how many you kill, you never seem to get rid of them.

The reason that there seems to be an endless supply... is because there **is** an endless supply.  They respawn very, very quickly.  How, then, to discover the room, much less get through and around and up to Master Magus Thornbolt, without an inelegant death?

There's a shutoff switch.  One for each side of the room, cleverly hidden underneath an activated clockwork worker in the center of the room.  See the picture at left.  Flipping the switch won't remove the hunter-seekers, but it will prevent their respawn for a time; long enough, at least, to finish the tasks in that room.
