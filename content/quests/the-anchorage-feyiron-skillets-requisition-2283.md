## Steps

1. Harvest the following:
   - 18 [[Deadly terrorfruit]] from [[Celestial nursery]]
   - 15 [[Wandervine fruit]] from [[Celestial nursery]]
   - 15 [[Basilisk meat]] from [[Basilisk lair]]
   - 10 [[Strangler root]] from [[Strangler root break]]
1. Scribe [[Recipe Scroll: Vegetable Stock]].
1. Craft the [[Vegetable Stock]] on a [[Work Bench]].
1. Speak to [[Hibbie Feltfoot]] at {{waypoint 28.65, -14.04, -69.05}}.
