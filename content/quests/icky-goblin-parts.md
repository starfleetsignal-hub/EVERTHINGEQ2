## Description

Lieutenant Kresic has commissioned me to collect various Drogan items within Nu'roga.

## Steps

1. Collect various Drogan Goblin Parts within [[Nu'roga]]
   - Collect 25 [[A Drogan soldier|Drogan Soldier]] Tonques
   - Collect 10 [[A Drogan blightcaller|Drogan Blightcaller]] Staves
   - Collect 15 [[A Drogan slinker|Drogan Slinker]] Eyes
1. Return to Lieutenant Kresic

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
