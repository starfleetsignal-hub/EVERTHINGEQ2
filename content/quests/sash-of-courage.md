## Description

I must collect Tallonite war sashes from aboard The Vigilant: Incursion to prove that I have killed Tallonites in combat.

## Steps

1. I must collect Tallonite war sashes.
- Kill Tallonite [[A Tallonite amahn|amahns]], [[A Tallonite kage-zonn|kage-zonns]], [[A Tallonite lozonn|lozonns]] and [[A Tallonite zonn|zonns]] and loot 10 [[A Tallonite war sash|Tallonite war sashes]] from the corpses.

## Rewards

- [[Mark of Manaar]] x1
- At least 1p 80g
