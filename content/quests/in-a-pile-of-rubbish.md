## Steps

![Suspicious looking pile](images/Suspicious_looking_pile.png)

1. Find and search 10 suspicious piles of rubbish. They look like smashed pottery, and spawn randomly around the island, however mostly on or near the edge of the island.
1. Return to Fonwick.
