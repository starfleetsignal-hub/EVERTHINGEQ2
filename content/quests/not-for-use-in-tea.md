## Steps

1. Craft the [[Entrancing Othmir Flute]], from [[Notes on Wurm Control]] recipe book you received from [[Livi]], on a [[Forge]] {{waypoint -1147, -532, 3390}}, using the following materials:
   - 1 [[Sumac lumber]]
   - 1 [[Titanium ore]]
   - 2 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
   - 1 [[Ethereal coal]]
1. Use the flute on 3 wurms and take the saliva. An easy way to do this is to drag the icon of the flute from your bag to a hotbar, then target the wurm and click the flute. While the wurm is mezzed, right click it and collect the saliva.
1. Return to [[Livi]]

## Rewards

- At least 29g 85s 79c
- +150 faction with **Othmir Artisans**
