## Description

"Serris has the names of a handful of Freeport arms vendors who are selling their wares indiscriminately.  She has asked me to confront the merchants named Jan MacGetz and Met Sleeth and acquire their ledgers, by any means necessary." *--Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Met Sleeth]] on the south side of [[Champion's Coliseum]] {{waypoint 237, -3, 78}}
   - If you accept his gift ([[An ornate globe of Norrath]]), hail him again
1. Speak to [[Jan MacGetz]] near the Commonlands gate {{waypoint 267, -3, -111}}; kill her (even con)
1. Speak to Serris

## Conclusion

"I was able to "Convince" Jan MacGetz and Met Sleeth to give me their ledgers.  New the Freeport Militia will be able to find out who they've been supplying with weapons." *--Everquest II Journal*
