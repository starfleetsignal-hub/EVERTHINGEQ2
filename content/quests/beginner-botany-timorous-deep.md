## Steps

1. Travel to [[Timorous Deep]] and gather the following in order:
   1. 3 Bloodworks fern - around  {{waypoint 2010.31, 35.85, 448.78}}
   1. 1 to 3 Haoera jungle vine - around {{waypoint 1771.15, 116.23, -308.85}}
      - *Note: The amount of Haoera jungle vine that is needed is different for each toon*
   1. 3 Chrykori Village driftwood - on the beach {{waypoint -379.59, 0.00, 19.34}}
1. Return to [[Bao Bao]].

## Notes

- Gathered items are not trackable

## Rewards

- Unlock the [[Days of Summer: 2023|Days of Summer 2023: Week 6]] Holiday event rewards that you can purchase from [[Pas Yu]] at {{waypoint 2293, -199, 2506}}
