## Description

"Mentha Haora, a member of Kelethin's Order of the Arcane investigating the undead near the Combine Spires, lost all of her notes when kobolds attacked her original camp south of the spires, near Steamfont. I should return to the area and gather all of them that I can find." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Gather 5 fluttering pages at the kobold camp in front of [[Steamfont Alley]] {{waypoint -427, 123, 485}}
1. Return to Mentha

## Rewards

- At least 18s 7c

## Journal Steps and Dialogue

- Mentha Haora: "Oh, no! Where did they go? I could have sworn..."
- Player Character: "What is wrong, Mentha?"
- Mentha Haora: "The last time I had them was... oh, no! My papers! All of my notes, all of my work, it's gone!"
- Player Character: "You mean the scroll I brought back to you?"
- Mentha Haora: "No, [Player Character]. I still have that. I was just about to compare your findings with previous observations and tests, but I seem to have lost them while fleeing."
- Player Character: "While fleeing?'
- Mentha Haora: "Yes, we had originally set camp on the southern side of the Combine Spires, close to Steamfont, but then a pack of kobolds moved in. They caught us off guard. I must have dropped my notes up there while fighting them off and running away."
- Player Character: "I can gather them for you."

Collect Mentha's lost notes.

I collected all Mentha's lost notes. Return to Mentha Haora.

- Mentha Haora: "You found my lost notes, [Player Character]! That is such a relief. I was afraid I would be required to start my investigation all over again."
- Player Character: "Me too."

I have spoken with Mentha. I collected all of Mentha's lost notes.

Mentha Haora, a member of Kelethin's Order of the Arcane was relieved when I returned with all of her lost notes.
