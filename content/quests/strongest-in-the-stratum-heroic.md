*The Stratum of the Protectors has been lost for ages, but is still watched over by a group of nayad that now call it home. In order to explore this lost temple, I'll need to destroy the strongest creatures found here.*

- Enter the zone [[Izshrash'Khar: Stratum of the Protectors (Heroic)]]:

  - Destroy the [[A Warrior Construct|Warrior Construct]]
  - Destroy the [[A Mystic Construct|Mystic Construct]]
