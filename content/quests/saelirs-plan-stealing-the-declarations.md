This is the 2nd quest of [[Kelethin Betrayal Timeline|Kelethin Betrayal]].

## Steps

1. Speak with [[Saelir Varryn]] {{waypoint 224, 112, 400}} in Kelethin.
1. Search the bookcases {{waypoint 396, 145, 155}} for documents in the Fae Royal Court in Kelethin.
1. Return to [[Saelir Varryn]] {{waypoint 224, 112, 400}} to complete the quest.

## Rewards

- Exp and AA
