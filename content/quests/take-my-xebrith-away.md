Expires after 14 days

This is the quest for the Public Quest Zone of the Unkown called The Unknown: Void Inkursion. You can enter next to Dr. Arcana via a portal.The portalis just visible, shortly before Public Quest is starting.

## Steps

*Legions of the Xebrith Tendril are amassing in The Unknown, and I must stop them before the mortal and planar realms succumb to the void incursion. Meanwhile, none other than Doctor Arcana has requested I collect a curiosity from the infernal general of the void armies, for which I have agreed.*

- I must destroy the Legions of Xebrith Tendril.
  - Obtain an Infernal Nightblood Horn
- Return to Doctor Arcana
