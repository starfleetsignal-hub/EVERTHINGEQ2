## Steps

1. Rearm the traps; these are located under small snow mounds which must be cleared away first. Only unarmed (closed) traps will be usable to update these steps.
   1. Rearm the 2 traps at eastern front. {{waypoint 0, -341, -480}}
   1. Rearm the 2 traps at western front. {{waypoint 264, -369, -608}}
1. Return to [[Cala Giantender]] {{waypoint 70, -331, -522}}
