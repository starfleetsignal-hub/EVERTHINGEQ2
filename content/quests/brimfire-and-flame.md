Quest is active only during annual [[Scorched Sky]] event July.

## Starting the Quest

The "a random Scorched Sky fiery elemental" can be found in

- Butcherblock Mountains
- zones with Scorched Sky elementals

## Steps

1. Examine [[Shattered Brimfire Tablet]]  dropped by Scorched Sky event fire elementals' reward chest (uncommon drop)
1. Kill more event elementals for 3 more brimfire tablet pieces. (also uncommon drops)
1. Speak to one of the following NPCs randomly selected by your quest:

- [[Yamdalin Grumblebrow]] at {{waypoint -600, 102.5, 343.5}} in Butcherblock Mountains (next to the Lesser Faydark griffon tower).
- [[Jahinda Tojosoa]] at {{waypoint 17, -90, -910}} in Sinking Sands (at Camp D'Sas)
- [[Dirvond Chaeydark]] at {{waypoint 65, -89, -806}}  in Pillars of Flame just inside the zone entrance to the Swiftrider's Caravan.

## Rewards

- 3 x [[Mithril Jubilation Medal]] (in 2026)

One of the following:

- [[Suppressing Rain Cinders]]
- 5 [[Coin of Ember]]
