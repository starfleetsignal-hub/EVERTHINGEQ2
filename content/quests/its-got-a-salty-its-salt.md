## Steps

1. Kill 8 [[Blackshield Pirates (Group)|Blackshield Pirates]] (any level 12 will do) on the west side of The Green Overlook {{waypoint 2795, 65, 914}}.
1. Return to Tizyk.

## Rewards

- At least 3s 62c
- One of the following (depends on class):
  - [[Archon's Boots]] (Clerics)
  - [[Channeler's Slippers]] (All Mages)
  - [[Earthkeeper's Boots]] (Druids)
  - [[Elder's Boots]] (Shamans)
  - [[Enlightened Sandals]] (Brawlers)
  - [[Knight's Boots]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Shade's Boots]] (All Scouts)
