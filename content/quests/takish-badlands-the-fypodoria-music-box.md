## Notes

- This quest expires in 5 days, no matter how much progress you have or have not made on the quest!

## Steps

1. I should search for clues to the whereabouts of the key by killing 15 [[A vicious leafcutter|vicious leafcutters]] in [[Takish Badlands]] around {{waypoint 594.51, 45.59, -284.67}} at [[Firiona's Motte]].
1. I need to find the Fypodoria Music Box key in [[Sandstone Delta]] from [[A savannah stalker|savannah stalkers]] around {{waypoint 812.97, 196.53, 45.19}} at [[Sandstone Escarpment]].
1. Examine the clue to receive the quest [[The Fypodoria Music Box Key]].
1. Finish the subquest and return to the **Fypodoria Music Box** in [[Takish Badlands]].
