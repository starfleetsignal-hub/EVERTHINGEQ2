When asked about the branch of the last Glademaster, Birchbark Oakleaf, an ancient treant rooted deep within the soil of Deadwood Refuge, said it grew upon another treant by the name of Knotbark, but he has not seen Knotbark since the orcs were expelled from the refuge.

## Steps

1. Hunt 12 Deadwood treants ([[A deadwood harbinger|deadwood harbingers]], [[A deadwood reanimator|deadwood reanimators]], [[A deadwood timbersong|deadwood timbersongs]]).
1. Slay [[Knotwood (Solo)|Knotwood]]. (Knotwood should appear after you kill one more treant.)
1. Return to [[Birchbark Oakleaf (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)‎|Birchbark Oakleaf]].
