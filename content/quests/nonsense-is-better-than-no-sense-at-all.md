- [[Fallen Dynasty Timeline]] up to and including [[The Rift]], for access to the fourth floor of [[Tower of the Four Winds]].
- [[Vesspyr Isles Timeline]] up to [[Vyemm's Vengeance]], for access to the cave under [[Droumlunds]].
  - In Vesspyr Isles Timeline, you only have to complete Lepethida's quests through Waters of Strife.  Then go directly to Harla Dar's quests and complete through Vyemm's Vengeance!
