## Steps

- Head southeast from [[Emissary Glochk]], to find plenty of [[A fractured shardbone|fractured shardbones]] {{waypoint 1163.17, 186.32, -1736.80}}. Bring 15 skulls back to [[Emissary Glochk]] {{waypoint 1125, 151, -1899}}.
- The lower level [[A crumbling spurbone|crumbling spurbones]] by Dreg's Landing also count for this quest.

## Rewards

- The [[Di'Zokian]] Language
- [[An Emissary Glochk Effigy]]
- At least 31g 7s 16c
