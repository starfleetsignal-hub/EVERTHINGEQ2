## Notes

- When you accept this quest, [[Hrath Everstill]] has a nasty habit of not giving you the required quest items. So to avoid an excessively long journey back, before you set off for [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]] from [[Gorowyn]], be sure to check you have the following items. If you have not, hail Hrath Everstill again:
  - [[Hrath Everstill's Orcish Obfuscation]]
  - [[Antidote Prototype]]
  - [[Hrath's Gem of the Simulacre]]
  - [[Decaying Mist Prototype]]

## Steps

1. Travel to [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]], use [[Hrath Everstill's Orcish Obfuscation]] to gain an orc illusion, and use the [[Decaying Mist Prototype (Quest Item)|Decaying Mist Prototype]] on 4 Tallon Orcs. {{waypoint 621, -40, 209}}
1. Use the [[Antidote Prototype]] on any nearby orc, then use the Decaying Mist Prototype on the same orc. Do this on 3 different orcs.
1. Use [[Hrath's Gem of the Simulacre]] to summon a projection of Harth Everstill and speak with it.
1. Speak with [[Katrien Bogmoir]] inside the barn in [[Three Toes Valley]] to receive an [[Efflorescent serum]]. {{waypoint 344, -20, -466}}
1. Examine the efflorescent serum from your inventory to receive [[Effloresecent Serum-Infused Antidote Prototype]].
1. Use Effloresecent Serum-Infused Antidote Prototype on a nearby orc, then use the Decaying Mist Prototype on him. {{waypoint 528, -22, -421}}
1. Return to Hrath Everstill.

## Rewards

- +2500 faction with **Bertoxxulous**
- One of the following:
  - [[Bertoxxulous' Malignance]]
  - [[Bertoxxulous' Putrescent Rosary]]
