## Info

The quest description for this quest is bugged. You are not supposed to look for the Bixies north of the spires, but rather near the Opal Pond near the Old Kelethin Acorn.

## Description

"Master Alesmith, a hard working Dwarf that bases his operation at Spire Shadow Outpost, has offered me some part time work. My assignment is to hunt Trebuz bixies and gather their stingers for Matstyr. Their hives are located in the valley of the Great Swarm." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Slay Trebuz bixies around {{waypoint 192.37, 26.40, 219.19}} until you find 20 stingers
   - Seems any Trebuz bixie will do ( a Trebuz stinger, a Trebuz soldier, etc...)
1. Return to [[Matstyr Alesmith]]

## Rewards

- At least 2s 86c
- Experience
