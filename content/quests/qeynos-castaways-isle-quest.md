*This quest is unavailable to characters of Evil-alignment.*

## Steps

1. Kill 12 Dankfur gnolls on [[Qeynos Castaway's Isle (POI)|Qeynos Castaway's Isle]] at {{waypoint 161, -25, 882}}.
1. Return to [[Tyllia Navar]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 92c
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Darkpaw Champions Pelt]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Poisoned Skeleton Finger]] (All Mages)
  - [[Rotten Hawk Egg]] (All Priests)
  - [[Vigorus Darkpaw Talisman]] (All Scouts)
- +85 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
