## Notes

- **WARNING**:  This quest will result in massive negative faction with the Everfrost valkyries, also known as the Order of the Blade. This quest was part of the original Everfrost timeline, back when it was impossible to gain faction with the valkyries, and it does not take into account that the [[Update:48|LU48]] Everfrost revamp made it convenient to have decent faction with them. The faction hits can be avoided by allowing your mercenary to solo the valkyries.
- Shamus Felligan was one of the Guildmasters of The Shamen of Justice, the original guild for Barbarian Shamans in [[EverQuest]].

## Steps

1. Slay 12 valkyries in Everfrost: {{waypoint -196, 28, -566}}
   - [[A Cloudsong maiden (Everfrost)|Cloudsong maidens]], [[A Galebreaker maiden (Everfrost)|Galebreaker maidens]], [[A Skyshield maiden (Everfrost)|Skyshield maidens]], or [[A Windrider maiden (Everfrost)|Windrider maidens]] all update.
1. Retrieve the Gavel of Justice near the entrance to [[Icespire Summit]]. {{waypoint -101, 60, -908}}
1. Bury the Gavel of Justice at the gravestone of Shamus Felligan. {{waypoint 184, -19, 114}}
