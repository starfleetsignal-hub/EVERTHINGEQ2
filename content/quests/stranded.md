## Prerequisites

- You need to be at least level 62 to receive this quest.

1. Retrieve 15 pages from [[The Vaults of El'Arad]] on [[Isle of Awakening]] {{waypoint 308, 193, 376}} (note: these are little books, not pages)
   - *To proceed to the next floor, you will need to clear your current floor of all monsters first.* you'll have to defeat 2-65th heroic sentry before entering.

     - {{waypoint -50, 1, 113}}

     - {{waypoint 38, 1, 129}}
     - {{waypoint 39, 1, 130}}
     - {{waypoint 73, 1, 124}}
     - {{waypoint 39, 1, 100}}
   - Second Floor
     - {{waypoint -35, 29, 27}}
     - {{waypoint -35, 29, -34}}
     - {{waypoint 28, 29, -36}}
   - Third Floor
     - {{waypoint -1, 58, 50}}
     - {{waypoint 160, 58, -7}}
     - {{waypoint 172, 59, -35}}
1. Kill [[Korgo the Vault Keeper]] on the third floor {{waypoint 201, 57, -1}} for the tome update
1. Return to Arcanist Nazoor
