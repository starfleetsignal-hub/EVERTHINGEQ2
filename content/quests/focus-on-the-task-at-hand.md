## Steps

![velium crystal](images/Velium_crystal.jpg)

1. Collect 8 **velium crystals** from the geodes
   - [[A rumbling geode|A rumbling geodes]] spawns a crystal with a 50% chance after you kill them.  Make sure that you loot the corpse even if there is nothing there as the crystal can be hidden under the corpse.
1. Talk to [[Gritstone]]

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
