This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

- Open a rounded chest in the mid-west wall of [[The Treasure Chamber]] {{waypoint 10, 10, -232}}

## Rewards

- At least 2g 28s 78c
- 1 ~ 3 x [[Fragmented Nacre]]
- 1 ~ 3 x [[Flawless Nacre]]
- 1 ~ 3 x [[Rough Nacre]]

## Notes

- To access [[The Treasure Chamber]] you must have completed [[The Key of Coins]] quest or be accompanied by someone who has.
