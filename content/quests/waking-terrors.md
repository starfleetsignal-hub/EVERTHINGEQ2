## Steps

1. Search for someone who can help remove the curse
   - Speak with [[Geloka]] {{waypoint -819, 11, 544}}
1. Defeat [[A Tsol Ew scarhunter|Tsol Ew scarhunters]] and collect their idols (6)
1. Return to the idol {{waypoint -1077, 4, 921}} with the totems
1. Defeat [[A darkened spirit|the darkened spirit]] (level 93^ hand)
