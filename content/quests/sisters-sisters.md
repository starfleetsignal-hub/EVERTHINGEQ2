## Description

"Merchant Melilah asked me to deliver a message to her sister Fallulah. Fallulah is also a merchant with a shop near Kelethin First Regional Bank. This should be pretty simple." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Receive [[A Parchment Note (with a broken wax seal)]] and take it to [[Merchant Fallulah]] {{waypoint 294, 109, 137}}.
1. When she refuses to read it, return to [[Merchant Melilah]] and tell her Fallulah wants the clock.
1. Read the note that [[Merchant Melilah]] asked you to deliver.
1. Receive [[A Parchment Note (with no seal)]] and take it to [[Merchant Fallulah]].

## Rewards

- At least 50c

I've spoken with Melilah. Merchant Melilah told me to read the note she's sent to Fallulah so I could understand what was going on.<blockquote>The note is written in bold, dark strokes on a thick piece of parchment. The wax seal is broken, evidently from the pressure of a very heavy hand.
