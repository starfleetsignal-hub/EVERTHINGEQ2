This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

You must have completed [[Ry'Gorr Mining Operations]] from [[Thirin Veliumdelver]] to begin this quest.

1. Travel to the [[Velinoid Catacombs]] and kill [[A rumbling geode|rumbling geode]]s around {{waypoint -220, -35, 118}} - It will be a toss up as to if you will get one or not, if it is sparkling with red then wait a moment for it to shrink, then obtain it. When they die they have a chance to leave behind crystals. Collect 6 crystals.
1. Return to [[Hillfred Kinloch]]

## Rewards

- At least 12s 2c
- [[Wall mounted Halasian weapon rack]]
