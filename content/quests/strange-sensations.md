## Notes

- All instances are solo instances.
- This quest is only available until the start of the next expansion.

## Starting the Quest

1. Check ingame mail from [[Astrynia Burnwynd]] (Good aligned) or Unknown (Evil aligned)
   - Evil aligned: Speak to [[Keliki]] just outside the [[The Academy of Arcane Science|Academy of Arcane Science]] in [[North Freeport]] at {{waypoint -11, -8, -145}}
   - Good aligned: Speak to [[Palurien]] in the [[The Concordium Tower|Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos]] at {{waypoint 712, 41, 96}} *(Red Portal)*.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Researcher T'dylis]] in the cave at {{waypoint -140, -117, 626}} in Lavastorm. A portal to [[Runnyeye: Mad Experimentation]] opens next to him.
   1. Find the following goblish items (clickable): ![Goblish Items](images/Goblish_Items.png)
      - Collect **a runed goblish fetish** {{waypoint 62, -16, 2}}.
      - Collect **a set of hastily scribbled notes** {{waypoint 89, -15, -22}}.
      - Collect **an unfinished spellbook** {{waypoint 192, -16, -48}}.
   1. Exit the zone by using the return portal {{waypoint 63.03, -16.76, -82.61}} to [[Lavastorm]].
1. Speak to [[Researcher T'dylis]].  ![A runed scroll](images/Lendiniara's_Runed_Scroll.png)
   1. Use the portal to [[Crushbone: The Wracked]].
      - Kill 15 enraged orc casters ([[A Crushbone thaumaturgist|Crushbone thaumaturgists]]).
      - Destroy 6 corrupted shrines by clicking on them.
   1. Exit the zone.{{waypoint -533, -0.52, -202}}
1. Speak to [[Researcher T'dylis]].  ![An ancient iksar tome](images/An_ancient_iksar_tome.png)
   1. Use the portal to [[Skyshrine: Lendiniara's Wisdom]].
   1. Speak to [[Lendiniara]] *(the big white dragon)*.
   1. Click on **a runed scroll** next to her at {{waypoint -22, 1, 3}}.
   1. Exit the zone.
1. Speak to [[Researcher T'dylis]]. ![Half an ancient tome](images/Half_an_ancient_tome.png)
   1. Use the portal to [[Kaesora: Forgotten Atrium]].
   1. Click **an ancient iksar tome** at {{waypoint -441, 112, -506}}.
   1. Exit the zone.
1. Speak to [[Researcher T'dylis]]. ![An ancient scroll on a pedestal](images/An_ancient_scroll_on_a_pedestal.png)
   1. Use the portal to [[Silent City: Tomb of Seers]].
   1. Take the climbable wall (at the SE corner of the map) and click the **half an ancient tome** at {{waypoint 67, 31, 446}}.  Jump down from the climbable wall, there is an exit portal nearby to Lavastorm.
   1. Exit the zone by using the return portal to [[Lavastorm]].
1. Travel to the [[The Commonlands|Commonlands]] and meet [[Researcher T'dylis]] at {{waypoint 619, -47, 259}} *(The druid ring is the closest travel portal)*.
1. Enter [[A Crumbling Cavern]] at {{waypoint 962, -20, 558}}.
1. Speak to [[Researcher T'dylis]], then follow him to a pedestal at {{waypoint 236, -26, 43}}. Click **an ancient scroll** upon it.
1. Speak to [[Researcher T'dylis]].
1. Use the portal to [[Greater Faydark: The Sleeping Spires]].
1. Speak to [[Researcher T'dylis]] at {{waypoint -437, 74, 284}} in the middle of the non-active spires.
1. Defend T'dylis while he is preforming the ritual *(5-6 undercon mobs will come)*.
1. Speak to [[Researcher T'dylis]].

## Rewards

- 1p 35g 33440 status
- 8800 Experience
- [[Mysteries of the Keeper]] - *Allows you to increase a single ascension class from 10 to 11. asks you which one you want to increase*
- 3x [[Insight of the Keeper]] - *25day research time reduction. Can get the one ascension spell you got from apprentice to expert*
