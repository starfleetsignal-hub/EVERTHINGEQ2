Volume II of the Planar Chronicler has some interesting writings on the denizens of the elemental planes. My curiosity is piqued, to say the least.

## Steps

- Complete this chapter on [Event Heroic] missions.:
  - Defeat the Bearer of Winds in [[Eryslai: Trials of Air (Event Heroic)]]
  - Usurp the Mud King in [[Vegarlson: The Terrene Rift (Event Heroic)]]
  - Slay the Summoned Reprisal in [[Doomfire: Vengeance of Ro (Event Heroic)]]
  - Vanquish the Veiled Avenger in [[Awuidor: The Veiled Precipice (Event Heroic)]]

## Rewards

- At least 7p 79g 62s 50c 122,400 status
- 3 [[Planar Stones (Chaos Descending)|Planar Stones]]
- 65 [[Archivist's Tremissis]]
- [[Weekly Elemental Mission Reward Crate (Version 2)|Weekly Elemental Mission Reward Crate]]
- Choice of one bag of the following:
  - 15 [[Celestial Alexandrite]]
  - 15 [[Celestial Citrine]]
  - 15 [[Celestial Garnet]]
  - 15 [[Celestial Morganite]]
  - 15 [[Celestial Onyx]]
  - 15 [[Celestial Spinel]]
  - 15 [[Celestial Zircon]]

## Note

This quest, along with others, is required to complete the [[Celestial Doctrine]] quest given by [[Vorai]], located in [[Myrist, the Great Library|Myrist, The Great Library]] in the [[Elemental Portal Gallery]].
