## Steps

1. Click scroll at {{waypoint 162, 593, -526}} to be offered the quest. When you accept the quest, the quest timer is set to 1 minute.
1. Fly to the platform at {{waypoint 331, 623, -622.4}} (In front of the floating shard between the peaks behind Majestrix Sangeeta). The quest timer is reset to 9 minutes.
1. Face east
1. Repeat five times:
   1. Click the platform and 2-6 of the six floating rock pillars will light up in an order. Remember the order.
   1. A triangle will form in front of each pillar that lit up.
   1. Click the triangles in the order the pillars lit up. (You must be very close to the center of the triangle to click it.)
1. Defeat The Cursed One that spawns.

## Notes

- Easiest key quest
- 1 minute to start plus 9 minutes total time; can be completed in less than 5 minutes
- repeatable on failure 10-15? mins, 3? hours on success
- Appears to require decent Yrzu faction

## Rewards

- At least 1p 68g 39s
- 5 encrusted planar key
