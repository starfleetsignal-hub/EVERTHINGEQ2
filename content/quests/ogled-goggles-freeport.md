## Steps

I have been hired to get a helm for [[Blat Berisen|Blat Berison]] from [[Frethe Zizlop]]. Frethe wants a deck of cards from [[Zoe Hertzflip]]. Zoe seeks [[Barb Islenet|Barb Islenets]]' pink goggles. This adventure can't go on for too long, can it?

1. Obtain the [[Pretty Pink Goggles]] from [[Barb Islenet]] in [[North Freeport]], by the guild hall entrance {{waypoint -28, -20, -272}}, and return them to [[Zoe Hertzflip]] in [[West Freeport]].
