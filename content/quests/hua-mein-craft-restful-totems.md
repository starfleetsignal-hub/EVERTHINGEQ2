## Steps

1. Craft 10 more [[Kerra Spirit Totem|kerra spirit totems]] on a woodworking table for the Hua Mein villagers to use. Multicrafts give a single update. (Mass production does not work)
   - 30 [[Sumac lumber]]
   - 20 [[Quicksilver cluster]]
   - 10 [[Ethereal sandpaper]]
   - [[Portable Woodworking Kit]]
1. Talk to Li Sing.

## Rewards

- At least 46g
- +1,500 faction with **Hua Mein**
- +1,000 faction with **Hua Mein Craftsmen**
