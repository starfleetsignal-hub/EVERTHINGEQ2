**NOTES**

You must be level 16 to obtain the quest.![The bookcase in Windstalker Village from which you examine "The Case of the Windstalker Rumbler" to begin the quest.](images/Windstalkerbookcase.jpg)

## Steps

- Kill [[The Windstalker Rumbler]] in the [[Gnoll Caves]] at {{waypoint -802, -29, -64}}.

## Rewards

- At least 29s
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Bandit's Greataxe]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Blocker of Everlasting Trials]] (All Priests)
  - [[Fawnstare, Shield of Betrayed Eyes]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Heavy Great Flail of Splintered Skulls]] (All Priests)
  - [[Hex Doll of Welcoming Blight]] (All Mages)
  - [[Knuckles of Sublime Reality]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Pillar of the Drubber]] (Brawlers, Beastlords)
  - [[Sharpened Katana]] (Bards, Predators, Rogues)
  - [[Shield of Industrious Slamming]] (Crusaders, Warriors)
  - [[Yestrak, Magi's Bo]] (All Mages)
