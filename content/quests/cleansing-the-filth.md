*This quest is repeatable up to 4 times..*

## Description

"The soul of Quartermaster Berlon, a former knight within the ruined halls, has beseeched me to cleanse the filth so that his soul may begin to rest."  *--Everquest II Journal*

## Notes

- If your level is too high, Quartermaster Berlon will not offer his quests. [[Mentoring#Self-Mentoring|Mentor]] down to an appropriate level.

## Steps

1. Dispatch 5 centipedes ([[A dust crawler (Stormhold)|a dust cralwer]] {{waypoint -104, 5, -110}}, or [[A dirt crawler]] {{waypoint 73, -43, -68}})
1. Dispatch 5 oozes ([[A small life leech]], [[A life leech]], [[A fetid goo]]) {{waypoint 64, 5, -97}}
1. Dispatch 5 kohlrats ([[A crazed kohlrat]] {{waypoint -133, 5, -109}}, [[A cornered kohlrat]] {{waypoint -117, 2, -85}})
   - Only two known spawns are available; one of each types (00:12, 15 March 2025 (UTC))
