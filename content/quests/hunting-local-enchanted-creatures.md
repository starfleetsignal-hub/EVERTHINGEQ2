## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable up to 5 times..*
- Although this quest is repeatable, it will only reward achievement experience upon the first completion.
- Although these quests are listed in order, the actual order you receive them in is random.

## Quest One:

**Steps**

1. Kill 10 wasps near the [[Lost Village of Bobick]] {{waypoint 178, 1, -102}}
1. Return to [[Kelbri Mossborn]]

**Rewards**

- Experience
- At least 17s 67c

## Quest Two:

**Steps**

1. Kill 8 bears near the [[Lost Village of Bobick]] {{waypoint 34, 18, -225}}
1. Return to [[Kelbri Mossborn]]

**Rewards**

- Experience
- At least 35s 34c

## Quest Three:

**Steps**

1. Kill 10 beetles near the [[Lost Village of Bobick]] {{waypoint -112, 1, -114}}
1. Return to [[Kelbri Mossborn]]

**Rewards**

- Experience
- At least 63s 1c

## Quest Four:

**Steps**

1. Kill 11 turtles near the [[Lost Village of Bobick]] {{waypoint 125, -3, 37}}
1. Return to [[Kelbri Mossborn]]

**Rewards**

- Experience
- At least 90s 68c

## Quest Five:

**Steps**

1. Kill 12 wasps near the [[Lost Village of Bobick]] {{waypoint 178, 1, -102}}
1. Return to [[Kelbri Mossborn]]

**Rewards**

- Experience
- At least 1g 35s 35c
