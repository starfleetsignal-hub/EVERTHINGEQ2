## Prerequisites

- You must speak [[Krombral]], the language of Giants, in order to complete this quest.

## Starting the Quest

1. Approach the ravine in [[Solusek Valley]] within [[Lavastorm]] and speak to [[Sebir the Historian]] at {{waypoint -168, -10, -546}} - this is close to the Solusek Valley station or northern Ulteran Spire if you are map-travelling. He will tell you about a sage trapped within the nearby [[Maiden's Gulch]] instance that is need of rescuing.
1. Enter [[Maiden's Gulch: Rescue of the Sage]]. {{waypoint -193.76, -1.65, -351.19}}
   - You must be minimum level 50 to bring your group into the zone. Click the door and choose the correct instance to enter. (Level 45 minimum to enter the zone)
1. Kill the [[A Drakota inquisitor|Drakota inquisitor]] and [[Vyr'drais the Vicious]].
1. Hail the [[The Sage of Ages|Sage of Ages]] on the platform above the lava pit to start the quest.

## Steps

1. Enter [[Solusek's Eye]] and travel to [[Nagafen's Lair]] at the very bottom of the dungeon. {{waypoint -202, -617, -198}}
   - See the [[Solusek's Eye#Geographic Walkthrough|Solusek's Eye Walkthrough]] or the Guide at the Bottom of the [[Nagafen's Lair|Nagafen's Lair Page]] for help on reaching the Instance.
1. Speak to [[Majordomo Inferinus]] who tells you that you need to learn the Elder Dragon language to be able to speak with [[Lord Nagafen (Nagafen's Lair)|Lord Nagafen]].
1. Return to the [[The Sage of Ages|Sage of Ages]] in [[Antonica]] on the top floor of the [[Tower of the Oracles]] at {{waypoint -1030, 14, -618}}. He tells you that you need to find 26 translated runes of Elder Dragon to learn the [[Draconic|Draconic language]].
   - If the Sage of Ages disappears or is not there, leave and re-enter the tower again.
1. Gather the 26 runes.
   - If this is your first character (on a given account) completing this quest, you must gather the 26 runes from their locations in the world, as listed [[#Rune Locations|below]].
   - If you have already completed this quest on at least one character, you can transfer the [[Tablet of Draconic Runes]] to any character on your account, as it is an heirloom. Examine the [[Tablet of Draconic Runes]], Study the Runes, Commit the runes to memory then the quest will advance through all 26 runes. If you deleted the tablet, it can be re-acquired from the [[Shady Swashbuckler XVIII]] on the docks of [[Port Naythex]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] at {{waypoint 804, 6, -98}}.
   - If you are running to get the 26 runes, pick-up the Solusek's Eye runes while you are in the vicinity and the route to the Glassworker's Room is familiar - otherwise you'll be out to get the others, then back to get those in the Glassworker's Room, then out to the Sage again, and then back again to Nagafen.
1. Return to the Sage of Ages for your reward. Afterwards, go back to Nagafen's Lair and speak with Lord Nagafen to acquire the following quest.

*Instructions for getting good toons into Freeport safely for the Fen rune.*  There are four guards along the route, all level 45 Heroic, but invis worked on all of them.

1. From the Commonlands, head to the entrance of the Thieves Way just south of the main Freeport gate. {{waypoint -1245, -82, 121}}
1. Enter the Thieves Way.  The entrance to Freeport North is across the room. Find a click-able grate in the wall.  All four portals to the different districts of Freeport are in this room, so make sure you enter North Freeport. {{waypoint -123, 1, 50}}
1. Enter North Freeport.  Follow the wall away from the sewer entrance until you see a ramp going upward to your right. Take the ramp. A guard stands at the bottom of the ramp.
1. The Academy of Arcane Science is at the top of ramp. There are two guards at the door of the academy, but no other potential attackers.
1. Enter the academy, turn left, and take the left elevator to the upper balcony.
1. There is one last skeleton guard standing watch up here, but if you keep to the ledge, you can get to the book without aggro. If using invis, it will break when you click on the book.
1. Get your rune from the book (green book standing on edge on the table) and port out.
