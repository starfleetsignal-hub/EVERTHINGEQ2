*Well, before I can send you off you must discover the routes for yourself. There are stations near the Old Kelethin Lift, the Butcherblock Mountains, Crushbone Keep, Steamfont Mountains and New Tunaria. Once you map the location of each you can borrow one of our horses. We have to be sure you won't get lost out there.*

## Steps

1. Visit each of the five [[Horse Stable|Horse Stables]] in Greater Faydark. You do not have to speak to the [[A stable master (Greater Faydark)|stable master]], just walk around the station to get the update.
   - Old Kelethin Lift - actually at the foot of [[Underwood Road]] at {{waypoint 137, 20, 157}} where the log bridge goes above the Trebuz Bixie nest.
   - Butcherblock Mountains - in the middle of the [[Treant Grove]], at {{waypoint 667, 9, -61}}.
   - Crushbone Keep - actually this station is just at the southwest end of the [[Road to Crushbone]], at {{waypoint -579, 67, -92}}.
   - Steamfont - right at the entrance to Steamfont Mountains  {{waypoint -419, 130, -523}}.
   - New Tunaria - to the right after you run through the narrow canyon along stream at {{waypoint -706, 45, 323}}.

## Rewards

- Access to the horse stable network in [[Greater Faydark]].
- Experience
