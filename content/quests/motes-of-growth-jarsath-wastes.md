## Steps

1. Harvest 6 magiliths in Jarsath Wastes.

## Rewards

- 3 [[A Floret of Growth|Florets of Growth]]

## Notes

A single player can harvest a "magilith".<br>
*Two players* are required to simultaneously harvest the "massive magiliths" in order to receive Motes of Growth.<br>
**Four players** are required to simultaneously harvest "mega-magiliths" in order to receive Motes of Growth.
