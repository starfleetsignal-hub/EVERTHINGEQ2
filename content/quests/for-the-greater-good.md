## Notes

- In addition to the items supplied by this quest the following Tier 0 (zero) runes should be taken from [[Tishan's Lockbox (Splendor Sky Aerie)|Tishan's Lockbox]] which is located at {{waypoint -327.80, 114.22, 566.55}}
  - [[Tishan's Zimaran Rune: Adamant Defiance (Tier 0)|Tishan's Zimaran Rune: Adamant Defiance [Tier 0]]] - *Immunity to Stun effects*
  - [[Tishan's Zimaran Rune: Adamant Resolve (Tier 0)|Tishan's Zimaran Rune: Adamant Resolve [Tier 0]]] - *Immunity to Stifle effects*
  - [[Tishan's Zimaran Rune: Astral Dominion (Tier 0)|Tishan's Zimaran Rune: Astral Dominion [Tier 0]]] - *Immunity to Mesmerize effects*
  - [[Tishan's Zimaran Rune: Blinding Gleam (Tier 0)|Tishan's Zimaran Rune: Blinding Gleam [Tier 0]]] - *Immunity to Fear effects*

## Steps

1. Talk to [[Quartermaster Arkymedus]] by the Ulteran Spire at {{waypoint -322.94, 114.23, 565.33}}
1. He will ask if you are familiar with how to gear yourself. He will give a tutorial on how-to if unfamiliar, and if you are the quest will be completed.
