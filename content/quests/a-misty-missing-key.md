1. Fly to [[The Cloudmist Isle]] {{waypoint -597, 250, -132}} and search 'suspicious piles of dirt' for Minikin's key.
   - This is a rare update, and it can take dozens of cycles around the island waiting for the piles to repop to get the update.
   - took about 40mins to finally get the update - Woushi TLE
1. Return To Minnikin Folnor.
