This quest is repeatable and may be shared.

Members of Clan Ragebourne have taken on the arduous endeavor of undermining the Kromzek war effort.  To accomplish this goal they have asked me to hunt several types of Kromzek soldiers and their conscripts found within the monumental halls of Kael Drakkel.

## Steps

1. Eliminate Kromzek fabers (10)
   - The Kromzek fabers can be found in Old Kael

## Rewards

- At least 1p 80g
- [[Primal Velium Shard]]
- +350 faction with **Clan Ry'Gorr**
