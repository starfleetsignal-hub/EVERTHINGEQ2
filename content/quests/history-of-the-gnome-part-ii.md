## Steps

1. Visit [[The Siege Lands]] in [[The Commonlands]]. {{waypoint -1300, -77, -253}}
1. Read the book.
1. Visit [[The Hidden Vale]] in The Commonlands. {{waypoint -298, -48, 782}}
1. Read the book.
1. Visit [[The Burial Pit]] in [[The Wailing Caves]]. {{waypoint -175, -55, -12}}
   - In order to reach the Burial Pit in The Wailing Caves you must first kill [[Advisor Kre'lak]] {{waypoint -63, -38, -49}} in the *orcish church*. Once he is dead a secret door opens behind the church podium which leads down to the Burial Pit.
1. Read the book.

## Rewards

- Experience only
- Upon completion of all three chapters: [[The History of Gnomes (House Item)]]
