## Steps

1. Head east out of the retreat toward Maven's Reach.
1. Climb the first ladder at {{waypoint 79, 385, 592}}.
1. Climb the second ladder at {{waypoint 27, 427, 627}}.
1. Use the [[Ning Yun Prayer Beads]] that [[Xue Chao]] gave you at {{waypoint -12, 481, 620}}.
1. Return to [[Xue Chao]].
1. Speak with [[Wen Wen]]
   - *After finishing this quest, you should be ready to head over to [[Zhen Gao]] in the [[Ning Yun Retreat]] at {{waypoint 508, 331, 450}} and turn in your quest [[The Three Tidings]].
