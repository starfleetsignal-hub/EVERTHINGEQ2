## Steps

1. Kill more fleshgoyles in [[The Tombs of Night]] to obtain 6 ideal hides.
1. Kill [[A mist grinnin fisher (The Tombs of Night)|mist grinnin fishers]] to get 10 hooks.
1. Kill all of the [[A mist grinnin netslinger (The Tombs of Night)|mist grinnin netslingers]] at the bottom of [[The Forgotten Tears (The Tombs of Night)|The Forgotten Tears]] to spawn *a grinnin cooking fire* in the alcove at {{waypoint 64, -40, 60}}.
   - The cooking fire does not spawn every time (It can take over 10 attempts until the campfire is there)

![](images/EQ2_grinnin_cooking_fire.jpg)

## Attention !

- Killing anything (or harvesting a shiny) starts a 90 minute timer; the zone cannot be reset during that time

## Rewards

- [[Fleshgoyle Hide Tunic (Level 30)]]
