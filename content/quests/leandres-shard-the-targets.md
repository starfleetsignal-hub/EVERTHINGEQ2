## Steps

1. Kill the 3 following Epic named (in any order):
   - The Dark Matron ([[Tairiza the Widow Mistress]] in [[The Tomb of Thuuga]])
   - The Ancient Overseer ([[Venril Sathir]] in [[Venril Sathir's Lair]])
   - Drudge's Tormentor ([[Taskmaster Nichok]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]])
1. Speak to [[Leandre]] by using one of the 3 following ways:
   - the [[An incandescent wand]]
   - by using [[Eye of the Siren (Fabled)]]
   - by targeting yourself and hail (press H by default)
