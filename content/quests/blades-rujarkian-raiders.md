## Notes

- *This quest is repeatable..*
- Once +20,000 faction has been achieved with the Court of the Blades this quest will no longer be available.

## Steps

1. Complete the following tasks, in no particular order:
   - Kill a random number (between 7 and 10) of [[A Rujarkian raider|Rujarkian raiders]]. {{waypoint -1053, -138, -406}}
   - Kill a random number of [[A burdened Rujarkian raider|burdened Rujarkian raiders]]. {{waypoint -1158, -135, -417}}
   - Collect 5 of the Rujarkian raider camp standards:
     - {{waypoint -1223, -143, -434}}
     - {{waypoint -964.26, -126.45, -288.41}}
     - {{waypoint -1001.65, -125.10, -329.42}}
     - {{waypoint -1044.68, -127.10, -270.55}}
     - {{waypoint -1109.19, -131.37, -242.51}}
1. Return to [[Mahir Lu'ay]].

## Rewards

- At least 2g 99s 47c
- +2500 faction with **The Court of the Blades**
- -2500 faction with **The Court of Truth**
- -2500 faction with **The Court of the Coin**

## Notes

- Can also be done alongside Questionable Merchandise from the Ghassan the Trader timeline.
