## Notes

- The combines for this timeline are VERY long. Without potions they are taking 3-5 minutes EACH. Plan accordingly.
- The first part for [[Essence of Hate|Essences of Hate]] can be done either in [[The Molten Throne: Hate's Essences (Tradeskill)|The Molten Throne: Hate's Essences [Tradeskill]]] **or** [[The Molten Throne: Portals to Nowhere (Tradeskill)|The Molten Throne: Portals to Nowhere [Tradeskill]]].
- Bring an evac if you don't naturally have one (eg, totem of escape).
- The flaming path/bridge can be hard to see. Turn up graphics quality, [Balanced] will be fine.
- Equip and activate as much bountiful harvest gear as you can (the Essences can bountiful harvest).
- Many of the 'harvests' in both part 1 and part 2 are on inclines or sloped terrain.  For whatever reason these particular harvests are much more sensitive to Z-plane differences than most other harvest nodes in-game.  If you're standing next to a node and keep getting a 'cannot see it' message, you are likely below the Z-plane for that harvest node - simply stand on the higher end of the slope, looking down towards the object, and you should be able to harvest.  *(Again, this is similar to normal harvesting in-game, but the thresholds for detection on these specific nodes are much higher than normal, and you will get this message quite often while on this quest if not paying attention.)*

### Additional notes for Molten Throne: Hates Essences

- Above all, making use of the [[Cloak of the Harvester]] with its "Featherfall while equiped" (or any other Featherfall-type item) will allow one to more-easily jump gaps by slowing one's decent.
  - *[of note regarding jumping: the published house on Maj'Dul called **Jfai's Jump Trainer**<sub>(just Filter Hall of Fame for Jfai)</sub> will allow one, in a less-stressful environment, to learn the subtleties of jumping with and without accessories/augments, and to recognize when either is needed.]*
- *In this zone the the in-zone-crafted item [[Fragment of Ro]] is used to "fill" breaks in the path. It is not a quest-update, so if you're brave you can do without it, and simply jump the gaps.*
- *Third-person perspective, and looking DOWN at yourself may help. Also, try setting your movement speed to **walk** rather than run.*
- *The EPICx4 [[Construct of fire]] do not attack while you are on the flaming paths.*

## Required Materials

Bring at least 225 [[Celestial Coal]].

The final combine (see step 16) also requires six rares. Ensure they are in your inventory before moving to step 16:

- 2 [[Darkstone]]
- 2 [[Rubicite ore]]
- 2 [[Strand of ether]]

For the optional Fragment of Ro combine you'll need:

- 50 [[Etherium]]
- 50 [[Gnarled entwood]]
- 50 [[Golden ember]]
- 25 [[Celestial Coal]]

## Steps

Decide which instance you want to do. Both Instances can be entered in [[Coliseum of Valor]] at {{waypoint 94, 3, -149}}. The initial steps are similar in the two zones; just the mechanics of getting to the essences differs.

- *NOTE: If you selected  [[The Molten Throne: Hate's Essences (Tradeskill)|The Molten Throne: Hate's Essences [Tradeskill]]] **and you are planning to jump the gaps** you can skip the Fragment of Ro step.*

1. Enter your chosen Molten Throne zone
   - Collect 50 [[Molten shard|molten shards]] from  "Molten Throne debris" nodes.![Molten Throne debris](images/Molten_Throne_debris.jpg)
     - *After harvesting  "Molten Throne debris" nodes three times the recipe [[Fragment of Ro Diagram]] will auto-scribe.*
   - Craft a [[Fragment of Ro]] on the extinguished construct at {{waypoint 1811, 695, 795}}.
     - ***Warning**: If you leave the zone you lose your crafted [[Fragment of Ro]].*
   - Collect an [[Essence of Hate]] at {{waypoint 1808, 709, 960}}. ![Essence of Hate](images/Essence_of_Hate.jpg)
   - After you harvest it a flaming green ball spawns. Follow it up the spur to {{waypoint 1877, 726, 910}}.

**The next steps differ slightly between the two zones.**

If you entered [[The Molten Throne: Hate's Essences (Tradeskill)|The Molten Throne: Hate's Essences [Tradeskill]]]:

- Collect the next essence at {{waypoint 1877, 734, 892}}.
- A flaming path will appear - step onto it and proceed slowly as it extends before you.
- Periodically a blue-white sparkly block will appear in the path. Either jump it, or use your [[Fragment of Ro]] to "fill in" the blue-ish block with flame and continue on.
  - *If you're jumping, you'll need to edge very carefully toward the gap until you see the path beyond it. Jump OVER the block onto the red path beyond.*
- Use the white balls (portals) to step to new paths and collect [[Essence of Hate|Essences of Hate]] as you come across them.

If you entered [[The Molten Throne: Portals to Nowhere (Tradeskill)|The Molten Throne: Portals to Nowhere [Tradeskill]]]:

- A "square of flame" will appear. Step onto it and use the [[Fragment of Ro]] to make two white balls (portals) visible.
  - *The flaming green ball will swing between its spawning point and the first "flaming bridge"; it can be ignored if you have already found the flaming bridge.*
  - *If you cannot click on the white portal balls or fragments try adjusting your camera angle to above and behind your char.*
- Use one of the portals. You will be teleported to another "square of flame".
- If there is an [[Essence of Hate]] there collect it.
- Use [[Fragment of Ro]] again to make the next two to four portals visible and use one of the portals.
  - *You can use Track Harvestables to pick a likely direction.*
  - *You will end up with a network of portals you can 'follow' forward and backwards.*
- Continue until you have 9 [[Essence of Hate|Essences of Hate]].

**Whichever zone you have chosen:**

1. Continue as above until you have 9 [[Essence of Hate|Essences of Hate]] (they are LORE(9), so you can't harvest more than 9)![Essence of Hate LORE(9)](images/Essence_of_Hate_LORE%289%29.jpg)
1. **Before leaving the zone:** **use** the [[Inert Deific Vessel]] from your inventory to fill it.
   - *You will gain experience here.*
     - *7,665,790 XP + bonuses at level 110 (30/4/2025)*
     - *Some people have reported not receiving XP at this point?*
   - *To leave the zone: use evac, fast travel, or take the 'suicide express' by leaping off the path.*
1. Speak to [[Druzzil Ro]] in the [[Coliseum of Valor]] at {{waypoint -4, 6, 5}}.
1. Collect the following in the [[Plane of Magic]]:  from the Khali'Yrzu and the Khali'Vahla platforms.  *Note: These nodes are trackable except for 'metetherial fibre'.*
   - 8 [[Raw yrzucite ore|yrzucite ore]]
     - Mined from largish flat blue-veined grey rocks called yrzucite vein on the Khali'Yrzu platform near {{waypoint 197, 588, -636}}. ![yrzucite vein](images/Yrzucite_vein.jpg)
   - 14 [[Metetherial fibre strand|metetherial fibre]]
     - Collected from short blue vases called Full Metetherial Ewer on the Khali'Vahla platform near {{waypoint -143, 576, -630}}.![Full Metetherial Ewer](images/Full_Metetherial_Ewer.jpg) <s>*Note: If you click fast you can get more than one harvest before it disappears*</s> *The animation 'runs' twice if you're fast, but the item itself can only be harvested once.*
   - 16 [[Aetherite crystal shard|aetherite shards]]
     - Mined from tall red crystal formations called an aetherite crystal in the [[Aetherscar]] around {{waypoint 676, -37, -175}}![an aetherite crystal](images/An_aetherite_crystal.jpg)
1. Craft the [[Metetherial Shield Anchor]] on any [[Elaborate Work Bench]]. A [[Portable Work Bench v2.0]] also works. Long combine
   - Requires:
     - 16 [[Aetherite crystal shard|aetherite crystal shards]]
     - 14 [[Metetherial fibre strand|metetherial fibre strands]]
     - 1 [[Metetherial Core]]
     - 8 [[Raw yrzucite ore]]
     - 100 [[Celestial Coal]]
1. Talk to the wandering hermit in [[Nektulos Forest]] near {{waypoint -1347, 17, -1404}}.
   - This will zone you into [[Nektulos Forest: Apotheosis (Tradeskill)|Nektulos Forest: Apotheosis [Tradeskill]]].
1. Talk to a wandering hermit at {{waypoint -1360, 21, -1390}}.
1. Release the seal (blue circle on the ground) at {{waypoint -1393, 30, -1345}}.![Release Seal](images/Release_Seal.jpg)
   - This causes a giant blue bubble to form around [[Innoruuk]].
1. Protect [[Innoruuk]].
   - A large horde of epic mobs will spawn and begin assaulting the shield.<br>*  - They will aggro and attack you. Happily you are shielded and take no damage; however you can (and will) be occasionally knocked back or rooted by Ullkorruuk or Saryrn.  If you are knocked out of range, the horde will break off and resume their attack on the shield*
   1. Periodically during the fight the message 'You must seal the crack that has formed in the shield!' will appear.![Barrier Weakness - notice orange lightning on ground.](images/Barrier_Weakness.jpg)
      - Look for the orange lightning on the ground - this is the focus for the "weakness".

   1. Run to the seal at {{waypoint -1393, 30, -1345}} and draw in hatred by clicking on the seal.![Hatred Source](images/Hatred_Source.jpg)<br>*  - You will gain a 20 second debuff [[Endless Loathing]], which slows your speed by 50% and causes your character to zig-zag while moving. This debuff is required to properly seal each crack.*
   1. Run back to the shield where the creatures of hate are congregating. Find a 'hand' icon through the crowd of enemies, then (while still carrying the 20s debuff) click to repair the shield.<br>*  - With the number of larger hitbox enemies gathering at the forming crack, it is sometimes difficult to get the 'hand' icon needed to interact with the crack. Move the cursor around the edges of the pack or zoom out in 3rd person view from the side of the pack of enemies may help.*
      - Ullkoruuk and Saryrn will occasionally attack you directly, you're still shielded, but it will disrupt repair attempts or delay you while running.<br> - Be mindful of the 20 sec timer. If you have no debuff and try to repair the shield, you will fail and get a different message.<br>*  - Throughout the encounter, you may choose to stand near at the Seal of Hate if you wish and simply wait for new cracks to appear each time. Once one appears, simply grab another 'loathing' debuff and run back to repair the shield; returning to the seal each time to await a new crack.*
   1. Rinse and repeat until Innoruuk is restored.
1. Use the spawned portal at {{waypoint -1361, 21, -1388}} to enter [[Shard of Hate: New Ownership (Tradeskill)|Shard of Hate: New Ownership [Tradeskill]]].
1. Speak to [[Innoruuk]].
1. Speak to the Projection of Druzzil Ro at {{waypoint -216, 19, 3}}.![Projection of Druzzil Ro](images/Projection_of_Druzzil_Ro.jpg)
   - She will port you to {{waypoint -14, 31, -173}}.
1. Take the seal at {{waypoint -14, 28, -133}}.![Take Seal](images/Take_Seal.jpg)
   - You will be teleported to the [[Coliseum of Valor]].
1. Speak to [[Varig Ro]].
1. Enter [[Plane of Innovation: Nick of Time (Tradeskill)|Plane of Innovation: Nick of Time [Tradeskill]]] through the portal at {{waypoint -94, 3, 164}}.
1. Craft [[Repair and seal Meldrath's time portal]] at Meldrath's Malfunctioning Time Portal at {{waypoint 25, -5, -374}}. Requires:
   - 1 [[Chronoseal]]
   - 2 [[Darkstone]]
   - 2 [[Rubicite ore]]
   - 2 [[Strand of ether]]
   - 100 [[Celestial Coal]]
1. Use any of the 5 door exits to return to [[Coliseum of Valor]] then speak to [[Druzzil Ro]] at {{waypoint -4, 6, 5}} .
