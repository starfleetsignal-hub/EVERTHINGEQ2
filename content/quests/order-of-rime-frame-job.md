## Steps

1. Freeze 8 Ry'Gorr orcs with the Gatling Ice Wand - found at {{waypoint -369.45, -356.89, 105.46}}
   - The wand must be equipped in order to be used
     - Note: You must be at least level 87 to equip the wand.
   - You must bring the mob down to 30% health in order for the wand to work
1. Return to Borotar Pickbeard

- At least 1p 15g
- [[Frozen Adornment of Raw Power]]
- [[Frosted Adornment of Critical Chance]]
- [[Frozen Adornment of Increased Criticals]]
