## Notes

This is one of the tradeskill dailies.<br>
Requires 10 x Zimaran Coal.
Nodes are trackable with Harvest tracking.

## Steps

Glerb wants you to make him supplies for creating his own "rock"

1. Gather 2x Mystery water
1. Gather 5 x Broad Leaf (you can pull more than 1 from each bush)
1. Gather 8 x Scraggly Root (you can get more than one from each root node)
1. Craft 2 x Gooey Glue
