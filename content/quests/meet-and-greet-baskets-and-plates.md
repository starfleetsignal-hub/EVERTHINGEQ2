## Notes

A repeatable quest for Faction. Repeatable up to 3 times and only if your Port Woe faction is less than 20,000. This quest requires:

- 14 [[Sodden Reeds]] - Located around  {{waypoint 69, 4, 626}}
- 30 [[Zimaran Sandpaper]]
- 8 [[Sedge Root]]
- [[Recipe Scroll: Woven Reed Dishes|Recipes]] for [[Woven Reed Basket]] and [[Woven Reed Plate]] auto scribed upon accepting quest.

## Steps

1. Collect 14 [[Sodden Reeds]] around {{waypoint 85, 4, 612}} or {{waypoint 84, 4, 611}}.
   - Note: You can harvest more reeds than required, if necessary.**
1. Craft 2 [[Woven Reed Basket|Woven Reed Baskets]]
1. Create 4 [[Woven Reed Plate|Woven Reed Plates]]
1. Speak with [[Zolea]] at {{waypoint -59.42, 9.33, 373.68}}

## Rewards

- +1,000 faction with **Port Woe**
- At least 6p 26g 6s 44c
