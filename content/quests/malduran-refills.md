Once a week, Gwynvyn checks with the guards to find out what supplies they're running short of, and arranges for someone to deliver the extras they need.

## Steps

1. Deliver the supplies to the appropriate **chests** in the guards' storage area near [[Quartermaster Lysslyn]]![Malduran Refill chests](images/Malduran_Refills_chests.jpg)![Malduran Refills chest](images/Malduran_Refills_chest.jpg)
  - As you come up the ramp to the raised platform, in order on the left are the chests encountered:
   - Deliver the extra bucklers {{waypoint -190.72, -3.65, -39.85}}. Behind Lysslyn.
   - Deliver the fresh trail mix {{waypoint -193.54, -3.65, -37.77}}. Behind Lysslyn.
   - Deliver the fresh water flasks {{waypoint -202.29, -3.41, -29.69}}. to left/rear of Lysslyn.
   - Deliver the spare whetstones {{waypoint -207.33, -3.49, -28.23}}. to left/rear of Lysslyn.

1. Return to [[Gwynvyn]]

## Rewards

- [[Coin of the Grand Artificer]]
- [[Ancient Arcannium Coin]]
- At least 2p 26g 80s
- +2,000 faction with **Deepforge Founders**
- +1,000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
