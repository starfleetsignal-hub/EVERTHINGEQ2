## Steps

1. Kill 10 [[Vale nymph|vale nymphs]] near {{waypoint -152, -22, 36}}. Any similar dryad will give a quest update as well.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 [[Vale nymph|vale nymphs]].
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 [[Vale nymph|vale nymphs]].
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 [[A corrupted bandit|corrupted bandits]], [[A corrupted brigand|brigands]] and/or [[A corrupted thief|thieves]]. {{waypoint -201, -9, -76}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 [[A corrupted bandit|corrupted bandits]], [[A corrupted brigand|brigands]] and/or [[A corrupted thief|thieves]].
1. Read the book.
1. Kill 10 [[A corrupted bandit|corrupted bandits]], [[A corrupted brigand|brigands]] and/or [[A corrupted thief|thieves]].
1. Read the book.
1. Kill any 10 lamia in the zone. {{waypoint -350, 1, -343}}
1. Read the book.
1. Kill any 10 lamia in the zone.
1. Read the book.
1. Kill any 10 lamia in the zone.
1. Read the book to finish the quest.
