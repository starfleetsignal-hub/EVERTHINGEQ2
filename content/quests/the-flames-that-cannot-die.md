## Steps

1. Talk to [[Scholar Dahlia Dominique]] {{waypoint 1999, -298, 3352}} in [[The City of Paineel]]
1. Talk to [[Vima Madrigan]] at the [[Shrine of Thunder]] in [[Lavastorm]]  {{waypoint -140.89, -117.37, 632.92}}
1. Travel into the underfoot side of [[The Hole]] and kill the following in order:
   1. Recover a spike shard from [[A shimmering guardian (The Hole)|a shimmering guardian]] found at {{waypoint -379, -301, -662}}.  Note: the shimmering guardians are placed around the Gorgon Wrangler, so it can be difficult not to kill him at this step.
   1. Recover a crackling shard from [[A crackling stormlord]] found at {{waypoint -393, -296, -625}}
   1. Kill [[The Gorgon Wrangler]]
1. Now Collect Flames
   1. Get an uncommon update off the [[An ancient guardian wurm|ancient guardian wurm]]s just outside of [[Veeshan's Peak]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]]. Note: When trying to get this flame, you cannot be grouped with someone that has already got the flame from the wurm and is still on this quest. You will not get the update if they are in the group.
   1. Kill [[The Reformed Magolemus]] in [[Najena's Hollow Tower]]
   1. Kill [[Grodinor]], who is part of the final encounter in [[Vasty Deep: The Abandoned Labs]].
1. Now off to [[Library of Erudin]] where you have to kill any one of the Scholar Primarchs
   - *Note: originally you had to kill all 8 of them, but it was fixed recently to only need one. if your journal still needs all 8 of them, file a petition to fix it.*
1. Collect materials to bind the globe
   - Kill [[Blacksmith Lava-eye]] in [[The Hole]]. He is in the Dartain's Fortress area in The Hole.
   - Get 4 uncommon updates off of [[A temple sentinel|temple sentinel]]s (part of the ring event at [[The Temple of the White Lady]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]])
   - Must kill [[Queen Gwarthlea]] in [[Vasty Deep: The Vestigial Cella]]
1. Now you must venture into [[Deep Forge]]
   - [[Everburning Coals]] by killing [[Firelord Kaern]]. Only one drop per kill and you must be on the step as the coals are temporary and no trade
   - Then click on the forge by the first named
     - *Note: these appear at the same time but you must have the [[Everburning Coals]] in your inventory to be able to update this step.*
1. Return to [[Scholar Dahlia Dominique]] in [[The City of Paineel]] your reward.

## Rewards

- [[Globe of Everburning Flame]]
- 83200 status at level 90
