## Steps

1. Leap from the highest point in [[Norrath]].
   - After leaping off many, many locations (higher than this) in [[Tenebrous Tangle]], the spot found to work was at {{waypoint 967, 462, 172}} ([[Gazer Isle]] & dismount before leaping).
1. You'll be sent to an instance. This instance functions like a solo, you can bring a merc or a second player.
   - Note: You have to CoV the other player into your instance to help, and they will not receive an update if they are on the same step.
   - Click the Eidolon in the center and prepare to fight. These are solo ^ named, however mercenaries do not have the usual ToV buff it seems so bring a friend if you think heals will be an issue.
   - There are five named that spawn as soon as the previous is killed. The final named has a small knockback. If you get knocked off the side, you'll be zoned back to the center of the platform.
1. When you are finished fighting, speak to the [[Eidolon of Heroism]] and you'll be ported to an inn in [[Freeport]] ([[The Jade Tiger's Den]]) or [[Qeynos]] ([[Fish's Alehouse and Inn]]), depending on your alignment.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 25g
