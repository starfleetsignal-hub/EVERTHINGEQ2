## Steps

1. Collect 5 [[Alligator eggs]] in pools in [[Innothule Swamp]]
   - *Note: Eggs spawn at the base of the water reeds around the swamp*
   - *See the the talk page for random spawns where other players have found the eggs.*
1. Return to [[Groluk]]

## Rewards

- At least 22g 11s 70c
- +2,000 faction with **Clan Grobb**
