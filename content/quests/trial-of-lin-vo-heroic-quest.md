## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.. However [[Trial of Lin Vo: Heroic (Instance)]] has a minimum persistence of 2 days 20 hours, so no new terracotta warriors will spawn should you zone into the instance again after completing this quest.
- This quest scales with player level up to 70.
- You can receive additional [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]] as a rare body drop from the terracotta warriors. These can be turned into [[Brother Huang Mantian]] for titles.
- Mercenaries can be summoned to assist you in the solo version of this quest.

## Steps

1. Defeat 75 [[A terracotta warrior|terracotta warriors]] within 30 minutes.

## Rewards

- 30 [[Monk pebble|monk pebbles]]
