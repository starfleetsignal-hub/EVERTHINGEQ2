This is the second of the Kerran racial quests in Freeport.

## Steps

1. Batarra sends you to talk to [[Nashii]] who gives you a history lesson.  After the lesson, return to Batarra for the test.
1. Batarra will ask you 3 questions about what Nashii told you.
   1. That the Erudites were hiding a base of concentrated arcane power.
   1. That our anscestors fled to Kerra Isle.
   1. They heard a loud noise, followed by a cloud of mist that blanketed Odus.
1. After answering her questions, you'll be given a lamp for your house, and told to seek out [[Barmaid Miala]], in *[[The Hole!]]* as part of the third quest.
