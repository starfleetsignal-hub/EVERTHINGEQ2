## Steps

1. Gather 8 stolen goods in [[Dankmar Swamp]]
   1. Possible locations include {{waypoint 129, 5, 605}} {{waypoint 194.40, 4.49, 457.23}} {{waypoint 215.22, 4.49, 476.28}} {{waypoint 174.73, 4.73, 511.55}} {{waypoint 240.73, 4.24, 614.90}}  on a hill around {{waypoint 206, 22, 717}} (all are general locations)
1. Return to [[Gragir Sharpe]]
1. Check the shoreline west of Port Woe
   1. Looking for a boat at {{waypoint 305.37, 6.54, 788.44}}
1. Examine the beached boat. Right click and select Examine, or you will have to come back and do it again.
1. Return to [[Gragir Sharpe]]

## Rewards

- At least 4p 4g 15s 72c
- +5,000 faction with **Port Woe**
