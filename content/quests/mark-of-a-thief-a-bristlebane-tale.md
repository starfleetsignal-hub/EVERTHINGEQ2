## Starting the Quest

**Must be level 128 or higher adventurer to enter the instance.**

Click a large blue book around the locations listed below and enter Mark of a Thief (Solo) or (Heroic) zone

- In the [[Qeynos Plains]] in [[Antonica]], just outside the [[South Qeynos]]-Gate {{waypoint 279.82, -14.37, 384.80}}.
- In the [[Indigo Draw]] in [[Darklight Wood]], just outside the [[K'Rais Tunnel]] leading into [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]] {{waypoint -622.36, -55.32, -64.91}}.
- In [[Frostfang Sea]] just north of [[Erollis Dock]]. {{waypoint -402.77, 24.76, 147.01}}.
- At the [[Green Knoll]] in [[Greater Faydark]], just down the ramp that leads into [[Kelethin]] {{waypoint 554.97, 44.58, 45.02}}.
- In [[The Commonlands|the Commonlands]] just outside the [[West Freeport]]-Gate {{waypoint -1104.53, -59.94, 54.98}}.
- In [[Timorous Deep]] on the path leading down to [[Gorowyn Beach]] {{waypoint 2336.36, 20.34, 947.97}}.

## Steps

Speak to various forms of Bristlebane inside the Mark of a Thief instance and enter an open book portal leading "To a not so distant past"

- Kill all mobs inside including [[Canta Slug]], exit  and speak to [[Maybe Bristlebane]].
- Enter the next portal, kill all including [[Killer Quack]], exit and speak to [[Probably Bristlebane]]
  - Killer Quack fight in Solo requires using Heroic Opportunity when prompted
- Enter the next portal, kill all including [[Jopal Darkbriar]], exit and speak to [[Likely Bristlebane]]
  - 3 torches around her desk allow you to see adds she spawns, fight also requires completing Heroic Opportunities to damage her.
