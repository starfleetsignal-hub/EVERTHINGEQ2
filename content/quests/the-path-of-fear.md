## Steps

![Ganak's Battlement Notice](images/Ganak's_Battlement_Notice.jpg)
![Sathir's Span Notice](images/Sathir's_Span_Notice.jpg)
![City of Jinisk Notice](images/City_of_Jinisk_Notice.jpg)

1. speak with [[Lieutenant Vakrisan Sakulvik]] at [[Ganak's Battlement]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]] {{waypoint -170, -40, -77}}
1. Speak with [[Captain Delshirrs]] at the Danak docks {{waypoint -272, -45, 1192}}
   1. Speak with Captain Delshirrs
   1. Run with the Captain down the dock to the far ship and aggro the far seas crew
      - *Note: When she doesn't want to walk down the docks (and hailing her does nothing), you can try running down to the end of the docks and back (this sometimes triggers the script) or you will have to group with someone else on this step so that you can get the update.*
   1. Defeat the Far Seas crewmen - linked 3 [[A Far Seas deckhand|Far Seas deckhands]] and 1 [[A Far Seas shipping captain|Far Seas shipping captain]], all 80^Heroic Brigands. If you don't kill the crewmen quickly enough they will respawn, and you will have to start the quest over again
   1. Speak to Captain Delshirrs again. You do NOT need to return to [[Xuurk]] to move to the next step, even though Delshirrs tells you to.
1. Place the notices of [[Xuurk]]'s announcement (look for a small clipboard):
   - [[Jarsath Wastes]] - Ganak's Battlement on the front gate (look for small clipboard) {{waypoint -219, -42, -56}}
   - [[Fens of Nathsar]] - [[Sathir's Span]] on the wall near Revliss Dru'shyv (look for small clipboard) -149 -7 1,004
   - [[Kunzar Jungle]] - [[City of Jinisk]] on the back of the pillar in front of the broker {{waypoint 634, 36, -125}}
     - *Note: The order above is the ingame order of the 3 locations. They can be completed in any order.*
1. Return to [[Xuurk]] ([[Fens of Nathsar]])
