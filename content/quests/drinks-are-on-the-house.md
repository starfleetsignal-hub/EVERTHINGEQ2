This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

1. Speak to Sam to receive several hearty drinks, as well as a free shot.
1. There will be five NPCs in the bar, highlighted in red or yellow. Click on them repeatedly to give them beer and make them drunk.  When you run out of drinks, return to Sam for a free refill.
   - This is a timed quest, although the timing is not very strict. Once you get all five patrons drunk, return to Sam before they sober up, or else you will have to repeat the steps again.
1. Speak to Sam.
1. For the next round, repeat exactly what you did before.  Sam gives you another drink, so you will be even more drunk than before.
1. Return to Sam.

## Rewards

- Your choice of:
  - [[Bugbear Plushie]]
  - 5 x [[Beer Token]]

## Notes

If at any stage of the quest you fail to serve drinks fast enough, simply talk to [[Sam]] again to redo that part of the quest. You don't have to start over from scratch either because the quest keeps track of how many satisfied customers you had from previous attempts.

- A good tactic is to just serve as many drinks as you can to one customer at a time. This seems to make it update faster.
