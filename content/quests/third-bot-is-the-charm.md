## Steps

1. Fly to [[Blackwind Isle]]
   - When you arrive on [[Blackwind Isle]], head to the East and follow the edge (on your left) of the island to quickly get to the ramp {{waypoint -544.74, 134.00, 252.76}} that will lead you to the Blacktalon Camp and the [[Treasury of Claw]].
1. Enter the [[Blackwind Isle]], [[Treasury of Claw]] {{waypoint -540.41, 173.68, 324.08}}. Once inside, climb up and kill [[Crags Blacktalon]] (65^) then pick up the bot located behind where Crags stands.
1. Return to [[Frigui Togginscog|Frigui]] on the [[Isle of Desolation]].

- [[Treasury of Claw Hanging Lantern]]
