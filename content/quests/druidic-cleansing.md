## Steps

1. Fly to the Druid Ring in Obol Plains.
1. Click on the freshly dug soil at  {{waypoint -445, 27, 445}}.
1. Talk to [[A Spiritual Being]] which spawns in front of you.
1. Click on the freshly dug soil at {{waypoint -445, 27, 445}} again to retrieve a wand.
1. Return to [[Wegadas]] and use the [[Blessed Wand of the Wild]] on him.
1. Speak to [[Wegadas]] and give him the wand.

## Rewards

- At least 1p 30g
