## Steps

1. Fly to [[Mok Rent]].  Head over the bridge towards the [[Ruins of Verelisk]] {{waypoint 1918, 39, 267}}.  Look for piles of bones.
1. Disturb a pile to spawn [[A brittle Sathirian guard]] (level will scale to match yours) and kill it.  Click the skeleton remains to get a bone chip.
1. Repeat until you have 10 bone chips.
1. Return to Evdokia for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 0c (tier 1 players)
- At least 90c (tier 2 players)
- At least 11s 36c (tier 3 players)
- At least 41s 65c (tier 4 players)
- At least 55s 35c (tier 5 players)
- At least 3g 61s 87c (tier 6 players)
- At least 6g 88s 21c (tier 7 players)
- At least 9g 46s 1c (tier 8 players)
