## Steps

Nothing is free and building, maintaining, and supporting the herald's camp is expensive. That's why the herald of the Tribunal has requested that we assist in offsetting the camp's cost by retrieving a valuable relic guarded by Glisterfist Prime. I need to retrieve the relic.

1. Retrieve the relic guarded by Glisterfist Prime
   - Travel to [[Zimara Breadth]] and zone in to [[Zimara Breadth: Wisdom's Plight (Solo)|Zimara Breadth: Wisdom's Plight]] and clear all named in the zone.
     - The chest with the relic is at {{waypoint -784, 190, 228}}
1. Return to the [[Herald of the Seventh Hammer]] at {{waypoint 274, 180, -357}} in [[Timorous Deep]]
