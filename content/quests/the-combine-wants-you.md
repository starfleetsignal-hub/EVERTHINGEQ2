## Steps

1. Map 6 locations within Tears of Tunare:
   - The 6 locations are marked with blue POIs on your map and can be done in any order.
     - Map the following locations in the swamp:
       - First: {{waypoint 532, -741, -163}}
       - Second: {{waypoint 739, -752, -427}}
       - Third: {{waypoint 718, -751, -227}}
       - Fourth: {{waypoint 577, -714, 53}}
       - Fifth: {{waypoint 681, -679, 229}}
       - Sixth: {{waypoint 742, -664, 174}}
   - Just fly low over the ground at the spot, no need to land! The guardian beetles see through invisible so be careful in their areas, you'll have to fight your way in the first, second, third, and fourth numbered locations, so be prepared.
