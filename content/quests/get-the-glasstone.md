Zeen Corblecog, Knight of Gearheart, who accompanied me from Kelethin into the underdepths, is trying to maintain the tinkered portal-gate that allows us instant transport to Maldura. She has asked for consumed glasstone to replace the warped focus gems.

## Steps

1. Hunt 16 [[A crag gorger|crag gorgers]] at the [[Abyssal Overlook]] {{waypoint -212, 72, 111}}.
1. Return to [[Zeen Corblecog (Maldura)|Zeen Corblecog]].

## Rewards

- At least 62g 37s
- One of the following:
  - [[Astral Umbrite Buckle Sash]]
  - [[Verdant Umbrite Buckle Sash]]
  - [[Pyre Umbrite Studded Belt]]

## Notes

This quest is repeatable.<br>Repeats do not include armor as a reward.
