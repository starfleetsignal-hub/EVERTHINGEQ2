## Steps

1. Kill 6 [[A drudge mucus|drudge mucus]] - throughout the halls of the first level
1. Read the book
1. Kill 5 drudge mucus
1. Read the book
1. Kill 3 [[A rotting globule|rotting globule]]s - throughout the halls of the first level
1. Read the book
1. Kill 3 rotting globules
1. Read the book
1. Kill 3 rotting globules
1. Read the book
1. Kill 3 rotting globules
1. Read the book
1. Kill 10 drudge mucus
1. Read the book
1. Kill 5 drudge mucus
1. Read the book
1. Kill 3 rotting globules
1. Read the book a final time
