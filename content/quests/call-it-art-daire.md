## Steps

Create 12 items from the random list below:

- [[Pristine adamantine table|adamantine table]]
- [[Pristine rosewood table|rosewood table]]
- [[Pristine adamantine lamp|adamantine lamp]]
- [[Pristine short adamantine table|short adamantine table]]
- [[Pristine azurite entwined sculpture|azurite entwined sculpture]]
- [[Pristine azurite tall entwined sculpture|azurite tall entwined sculpture]]
- [[Pristine azurite rotating sculpture|azurite rotating sculpture]]
- [[Pristine azurite tall rotating sculpture|azurite tall rotating sculpture]]

Report back to the Work Order Foreman to turn in the order and collect your reward

## Rewards

- At Least 2g 46s
- [[City Token]]
- One of the following, based on [[Tradeskilling Factions]]:
  - +200 faction with **The Ironforge Exchange**
  - +200 faction with **Ravens of the North**
  - +200 faction with **The Coalition of Tradesfolke**
  - +200 faction with **Tunare's Pages**
  - +200 faction with **The Dark Bargainers**
  - +200 faction with **Academy of Logistics**
