## Notes

- This is a mandatory sub-quest of [[A Mark of Awakening]].

## Steps

1. Go to the [[Nest of the West Wing]] on the first floor and harvest 'a shiny key'. {{waypoint 44, 0, -101}}
   - One minute respawn timer if more than one character needs the key.
1. Return to the Book of Power on the third floor at {{waypoint -43, 57, -7}}.
