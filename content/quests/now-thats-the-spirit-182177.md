## Steps

1. Use the [[Shamanic Spirit Fetish]] on 5 'rampaging' deinodons near death (health in the red).
1. Return to Finze

- At least 1p 29g 60s
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Fathom Chain Helmet]] (Chain Armor)
  - [[Fathom Cowl (QuestReward)|Fathom Cowl]] (Cloth Armor)
  - [[Fathom Leather Skullcap]] (Leather Armor)
  - [[Fathom Plate Helm]] (Plate Armour)
