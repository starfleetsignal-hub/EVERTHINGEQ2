## Description

"Agarighus would like me to go into the western germinarium. It has been taken over by hostile grobins, but there are still proto-caplings stuck inside. I need to rescue the proto-caplings before the grobins have a chance to steal or eat them." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

1. Harvest 5 [[Shroomba proto-capling]] spores around {{waypoint 77, -47, -195}}
1. Return to [[Agarighus]]

## Rewards

- At least 8s 43c
