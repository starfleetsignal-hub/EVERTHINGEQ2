## Steps

1. Spray 10 [[A Tupta termite|Tupta termites]] with the [[Potion sprayer]] obtained from Varick.
   - **Note**: If  you have difficulty finding the last few, they are trackable. The ones you have already sprayed will be white in the track window and the ones you have not will be grey. They generally will  be on the wood branches and roots that make up Tupta rather than the mud bases.
1. Report back to [[Varick Stoutheart|Varick]] for your reward.

## Rewards

- +5000 faction with **Guktan Guard**
- One of the following:
  - [[Blue termite]]
  - [[Orange termite]]
  - [[Green termite]]
