## Notes

- You must have completed [[Nanru|Nanru's]] quest [[Rays the Roost]] in the [[Expendables for Tentacles]] questline to access this quest.
- You receive [[Voidwell Vial]] when you accept the quest.
- Don't stand in the pink water while fighting. The "Zobboz Ichor" will do damage, sometimes enough to one-shot you.
- Quest [[The Leashed You Can Do]] from Ozron can also be completed while on this quest.

## Steps

1. Use the portal at <s>{{waypoint -434, 83, 52}}</s>   {{waypoint -463, 82, 61}}  in [[Oogothl Sprawl]] to zone into [[Zon Zobboz]]
1. enter [[Zon Zobboz: The Graftwerk (Solo)|Zon Zobboz: The Graftwerk [Solo]]] using the portal on the left at {{waypoint 128, 12, 820}}. ![scrykin](images/Scrykin.png) ![vistaar prana from dead scrykin](images/Vistaar_prana_from_dead_scrykin.png)
   - *for the walkthrough of the instance see [[Zon Zobboz: The Graftwerk (Solo)|Zon Zobboz: The Graftwerk [Solo]]]*
1. Fill the Voidwell Vial by killing 14 Scrykin on the second island (after killing the second named), then using the [[Voidwell Vial]] on the vistaar prana.
   - * Use the "**Teleport up**" portal at {{waypoint 631, 52, 691}} then Use the "**To the other side**" portal at {{waypoint 659, 104, 581}}*
   - *NOTE: Scrykin is a mob type, not a mob name (a gangly xxx). When they die they spawn a wisp called a vistaar prana.*
   - *NOTE: as soon as you gather 14 vistaar prana you can leave the zone (evac) or finish the zone and exit through portal at the end.*
1. Speak with [[Tomian Keeneye (Oogothl Sprawl)|Tomian Keeneye]] at {{waypoint 231, 80, -214}} back at the [[Gardens of Industry]] in [[Oogothl Sprawl]].
1. Reenter Zon Zobboz *(portal at {{waypoint -434, 83, 52}} in [[Oogothl Sprawl]])* and use the right teleporter at {{waypoint 2, 15, 816}} to enter [[Zon Zobboz: The Chimeric Chain (Solo)|Zon Zobboz: The Chimeric Chain [Solo]]]
1. Obtain the cimmerott runes ![visual of Yemi, Gara, Hirc and Olunt rune](images/Yemi,_Gara,_Hirc,_Olunt_rune.png)
   - yemi cimmerott rune
   - gara cimmerott rune
   - seq cimmerott rune
   - theng cimmerott rune
   - hirc cimmerott rune
   - voma cimmerott rune
   - yuru cimmerott rune
   - olunt cimmerott rune
   - dokt cimmerott rune
   - thiet cimmerott rune
   - The **seq**, **voma**, **dokt** and **thiet** runes are dropped by **non-aggro many-eyed snappers** or **Nullites** (small chest drop). Not an actual item, just a quest update.
     1. Swim across to The [[Land of the Nullites]] island.
     1. Kill [[Lord Shomp]] on a shelf just above {{waypoint -253, 15, 911}}.
        - *Shomp has knockback.*
        - During the fight, he will periodically become invincible and you will see this message: *The island Despot begins to ride the voidstream!*
        - *A black storm cloud is then created somewhere in the Nulltown area, and it must be dispersed either by having your body touch the storm cloud or by right-clicking the cloud and clicking "Scatter".*
        - *There is no point targeting the storm cloud and trying to attack it with spells or weapons.*
     1. Search the bodies of the inkubus mobs on this and subsequent islands to loot [[Eyes of an Inkubus]]  (a Lore appearance item). Equip this to see the mobs that drop the **theng** and **yuru** runes further in this zone. [[An inkubus]] is a non aggressive heroic, but not tough mob. ![an inkubus in Zon Zobboz: The Chimeric Chain](images/An_inkubus.png)
        - possible location of [[An inkubus]] at:
        - {{waypoint -301, 11, 978}}, {{waypoint -478, 10, 766}}, {{waypoint -579, 11, 811}}.
     1. Swim across to the [[Ichor Grounds]] island. Kill [[Vadrak the Vile]] at {{waypoint -422, 11, 849}}.
        - Periodically you will see a pop-up warning that *Vadrak prepares an irradiated frontal assault*. Move behind him to avoid this attack.![](images/Yemi_rune.png)
          - The **yemi** rune may be found in the vicinity of [[Vadrak the Vile]] or the "**Teleport up**" portal. Possible locations include:
            - {{waypoint -444, 15, 797}}, {{waypoint -531, 12, 863}}, {{waypoint -568, 12, 758}}
            - {{waypoint -509, 12, 902}}, {{waypoint -213, 19, 921}}
     1. Use the "**Teleport up**" portal at {{waypoint -581, 13, 772}} to [[The Skyvarium]].
     1. Kill [[Xigothid]] at {{waypoint -800, 104, 598}}. Has an attack that knocks you up in the air.
        - The **gara** rune can be found by this named (on the first platform you port to). Possible locations include:
          - {{waypoint -775, 104, 631}}, {{waypoint -702, 101, 566}}, {{waypoint -765, 104, 672}}
     1. Use the "**Teleport across**" portal at {{waypoint -760, 103, 540}}.
        - The **hirc* rune can be found on the platform. Possible locations include:
          - {{waypoint -775, 137, 393}}
          - {{waypoint -689, 136, 384}}
          - {{waypoint -715, 137, 417}}
     1. Jump down to the pool from {{waypoint -725, 136, 367}} to [[Isle of the Vatborne]].
     1. Kill [[Kur'Granox]] at {{waypoint -791, 48, 215}}.
        - *Has a knockback and calls for many adds. Kill trash on the way to him. Will follow you down if you get knocked off the platform.*
        - The **olunt** rune can be found by this named. Possible locations include:
          - {{waypoint -734, 49, 220}}, {{waypoint -804, 37, 144}}, {{waypoint -807, 18, 209}}
          - {{waypoint -787, 49, 233}}, {{waypoint -741.96, 50.24, 197.48}} ![a xaephian fugitive, equip Eyes of an Inkubus to see them](images/A_xaephian_fugitive.png)
     1. The **theng** and **yuru** runes can only be found by killing the xaephian fugitive. Equip the [[Eyes of an Inkubus]] because this mob can only be seen if you have it equipped. There are multiple fugitives, and you may need to kill several to get the update.
        - The [[Eyes of an Inkubus]] drops on bodies of the inkubus mobs on previous islands.
        - I found the xaephian fugitive at {{waypoint -709, 13, 387}}, a second one at {{waypoint -691, 10, 249}}
     1. Once all the the runes have been collected the quest updates with 'Return to Tomian'.
     1. Swim across to the [[Eye of Mozal]].
     1. Kill [[Mozal]] at {{waypoint -568, 9, -15}}.
        - Mozal calls in a few adds, *eyes without a face*, during the fight.
        - When an omni focus (a small, rune-covered pillar) arises from a portal, click it to drain it. It weakens the named and prevents more adds.
     1. Use the "**Leave The Chimeric Chain**" Portal at {{waypoint -537, 10, -59}}.
1. Leave [[Zon Zobboz]] and return to [[Tomian Keeneye (Oogothl Sprawl)|Tomian Keeneye]] at {{waypoint 231, 80, -214}} back at the [[Gardens of Industry]] in [[Oogothl Sprawl]].
