## Steps

![Book of Gnollish Orders as found near the tent of the Dustpaw foolish enough to leave it unattended.](images/Gnollishordersfound.jpg)

1. Slay [[A Dustpaw guard]] near the tent at {{waypoint 20, 5, -70}}.
1. Pick up the [[Book of Gnollish Orders]] {{waypoint 21, 5, -68}} from the tent.
1. Examine [[Book of Gnollish Orders]].
1. Slay more Dustpaw guards until you get a quest update.
1. Return to Consul Bree for your reward.

## Rewards

- [[A Book of Gnollish Orders]]
- +150 faction with **The Qeynos Guard**
- At least 7s 55c
