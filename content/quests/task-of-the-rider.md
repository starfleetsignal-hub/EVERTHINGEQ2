## Prerequisites

- You need at least -1900 faction with [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|The Order of Rime]] to obtain this quest without the illusion.

## Steps

1. Speak to [[Kyena the Eastern Lord]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]]:
   - Her route starts from the bridge at {{waypoint 338, -114, 640}} and continues north along the central path of the jungle.
1. Collect 10 *Rubble-rabble pointy-twigs* from [[A Rubble-rabble burrower|Rubble-rabble burrowers]] and [[A Rubble-rabble detector|detectors]]. {{waypoint -77.33, -31.85, -81.59}}
1. Return to Kyena the Eastern Lord.

## Rewards

- At least 38g 82s 52c
- +1,800 faction with **The Order of Rime**
- [[An Order of Rime stone long box]]
