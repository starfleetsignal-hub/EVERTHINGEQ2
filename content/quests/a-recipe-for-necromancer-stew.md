## Prerequisites

- You must be at least adventure level 77.
- You must have at least 40,000 faction with Clan Skleross, Legion of Danak, and The Sel'Nok Brigade.

## Steps

1. Obtain the following items:
   - 6 *unearthed bones*, from Iksar skeletons near [[Venril's Crown]] {{waypoint 9, -138, -244}}
   - 8 *shards of agony*, from Iksar spirits near [[Tylix's Fist]] {{waypoint -832, -115, -750}}
1. Return to [[Necromancer Corporiss]].
