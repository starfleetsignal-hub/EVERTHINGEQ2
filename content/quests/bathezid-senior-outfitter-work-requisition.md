## Steps

1. Receive quest from [[Supply Sergeant Anuhadux]]
1. Check clipboard behind him for your task
   - Scribe recipe book (These are erased after each turn in).
1. Create 6 of the required items
1. Return to Anuhadux for your reward

This quest raises faction with [[Bathezid's Watch]].

## Rewards

- At least 30g 11s 19c
- +750 faction with **Bathezid's Watch**
