## Steps

1. Slay the following mobs, which are all found south of the quest giver - around [[Pool of the Awakened]], for quest updates:
   - A miniscule tail spike from [[A thornspine devourer]].
   - An undamaged frigithermeosis gland from [[A drifting cloudmist]].
   - An undamaged pit sensor organ from a [[Stonescale rumbler]].
1. Return to Taer for your reward.

## Rewards

- At least 10g 4s 59c
