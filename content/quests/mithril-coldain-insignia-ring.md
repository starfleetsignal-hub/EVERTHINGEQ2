## Steps

1. Take the chipper you made to the Ring War Battlefield.
1. There are six ice egg-like objects you can interact with. Click one and you'll have to kill a level 90^^^ giant, after the giant is dead click the ice again to receive your update. Do this 6 times, there are 6 click-able ice eggs on the Ring War Battlefield, you must do each one.
- Note: The giants hit really hard, are CC immune, and have stifling detrimentals.  The giants have an AE knockback effect with moderate damage. Be prepared with a sturdy tank and good heals.  Knockdown and other interrupt effects do work and make the fights smoother as you can entirely avoid the giant's AE knockback.
- Note: With careful click timing, the entire group can get the update when clicking the ice tomb after killing.  Click immediately after killing the giant spirit, otherwise it resets.

   - {{waypoint 269, -333, -447}} (next to quest starter)
   - {{waypoint 238, -339, -493}}
   - {{waypoint 171, -330, -438}}
   - {{waypoint 214, -321, -386}}
   - {{waypoint 295, -321, -366}}
   - {{waypoint 307, -350, -509}}
1. Return to Malidain, who completes the quest and moves to the Throne of the Dains in Thurgadin
