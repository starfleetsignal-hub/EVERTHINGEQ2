## Description

It might be possible to study residual magics from the Eternal Prism, if I can collect crystalline shards of magic. I should search through the Scion of Ice, and if I can collect pieces of these shards, and return them to camp Wyndalsyd in Everfrost.

## Steps

1. Collect 12 crystalline shards (clickies) of magic from within [[Miragul's Phylactery: Scion of Ice]]
1. Return to Chief Scholar Lakewind outside Miragul's Phylactery for your Reward

## Rewards

- At least 1p 60g
- [[Void Shard]] x1
