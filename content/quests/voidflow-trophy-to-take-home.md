## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.
- Quest starter and updates needed for it have a chance to drop from any mob in [[The Unknown: The Obliviwrought Conclusion (Solo)]]. It may require resetting and running the zone again if you don't get all updates.

## Steps

*I've challenged myself to find the other parts of this impressive voidflow spear...*

1. Search for a voidflow spear shaft
1. Search for a jeweled spear heel
1. Search for a worn leather spear grip
1. Return the Spear to its base at the Hidden Camp in Nightscar Steppes in [[The Unknown]].

## Rewards

- [[Voidflow War Lance]]
