## Notes

- Make sure that your Brokenskull Illusion is applied, otherwise you can't get the update from Fip Marrsquire.
- After completing the Officer Training quests you should consider holding off completing this quest in order to retain the [[Brokenskull Officer Disguise]], as the illusion reward from this quest doesn't act as a disguise. Quests in and around [[Gunthak's Shanty]] will be much easier with disguise still available for you to use.

## Steps

1. Use the [[Brokenskull Officer Disguise]].  You may have to cancel the illusion and re-equip it after talking to [[Fip Marrsquire|Fip]] to get [[Petty Officer Kimtiz]] to talk to you.
1. Hail [[Petty Officer Kimtiz]] in the Brokenscale Loft {{waypoint 919, 29, 832}}, and complete her subquests to earn the Brokenskull rank of [[Petty Officer]]:
   - [[Officer Training - Chieftain Irtzit Bloodweaver]]
   - [[Officer Training - Deliver to Brokenskull Rock]]
   - [[Officer Training - Sir Vipwi Marrswatch]]
   - [[Officer Training - Deep Sea Diving]]
1. Raid the plans by approaching the area at {{waypoint 857, 21, 968}} and getting the two updates from clickable tables and two from scrolls on other tables.
1. Return to [[Fip Marrsquire]].
