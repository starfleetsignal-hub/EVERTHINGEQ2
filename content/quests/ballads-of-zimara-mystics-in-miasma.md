## Note

- Side-quest [[Fear the Cleaner]] also requires [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma (Solo)|Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma [Solo]]], and can be done at the same time.
  - ***Note:** If you do not see the quest feather above the NPC who gives this quest, then finish this part of the signature quest and then come back again.*
- It is not a requirement of this quest to kill the Names which appear in the following order:
  1. [[Droseraceae]] at {{waypoint 841.42, 60.10, 211.53}}
  1. [[Nerjehl Klaneh]] circulates around the lake at {{waypoint 797.29, 41.62, 67.43}}
  1. [[Miasmasula]] circulates around the lake at {{waypoint 797.29, 41.62, 67.43}}

## Steps

1. Enter [[Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma (Solo)|Splendor Sky Aerie: Marshlands Miasma [Solo]]] {{waypoint 600.95, 85.67, 326.65}}
1. Speak to [[Miranna Ameema]] just inside, at {{waypoint 668.50, 76.11, 320.07}} who gives you a [[Blinding Conviction Gem]].
1. Kill the following
   - Defeat 8 Fear-Affected Marshland Creatures
   - Destroy 7 Metallic Shards. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 687.13, 75.04, 367.18}}
     - {{waypoint 743.65, 71.77, 417.51}}
     - {{waypoint 817.31, 71.60, 342.93}}
     - {{waypoint 885.18, 74.98, 281.16}}
     - {{waypoint 887.45, 64.16, 202.54}}
     - {{waypoint 747.19, 44.15, 119.07}}
     - {{waypoint 789.68, 58.99, 270.21}}
   - Save 4 Felfeather Apprentices. Possible locations include:
     - {{waypoint 775.18, 71.95, 414.35}}
     - {{waypoint 807.78, 73.33, 380.62}}
     - {{waypoint 841.07, 43.54, 132.27}}
     - {{waypoint 768.95, 51.79, -35.53}}
1. Locate and save [[Sqokri]] at {{waypoint 933.46, 32.43, 226.08}} NOTE: to get to [[Sqokri]] , go to {{waypoint 862, 58, 153}} and follow the path southwest, it will take you directly to him.
   1. Kill the fearful miasma
   1. Target [[Sqokri]] and Use the green [[Blinding Conviction Gem]]. Do not attack [[Sqokri]].
   1. Lead [[Sqokri]] to zone-in near [[Miranna Ameema]] at {{waypoint 668.50, 76.11, 320.07}}
1. Return to [[Archmystic Crestcry]]
