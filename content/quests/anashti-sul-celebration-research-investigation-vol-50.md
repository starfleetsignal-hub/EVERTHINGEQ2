## Notes

- This quest is repeatable.

## Steps

An endeavor of this scale requires precise components made from the highest quality reagents. The herald of Anashti Sul has identified merchants that sell these reagents, and they need my assistance purchasing the items

1. Purchase the [[Banshee's Breath|banshee's breath]] from Melista for 5000 status
   - Wanders along Caiman Beach, from {{waypoint -1458, -228, -774}} to {{waypoint -1308, -229, -198}}
1. Purchase the [[Stoneskin Scale|stoneskin scale]] from Oliska for 5000 status
   - [[Freeport]] at {{waypoint -174, -56, -77}}
   - [[Qeynos Harbor]] at {{waypoint 863, -25, 24}}
1. Purchase the [[Ethereal Hair|ethereal hair]] from Nushoon for 5000 status
   - In [[Aether Wroughtlands]] this NPC wanders along a path from the Bivouac Camp {{waypoint 754, -45, -446}} to Minstrel's Cradle {{waypoint 344, -59, -183}}
1. Return to the [[Herald of Undeath]] {{waypoint -2063, -130, -35}} in [[The Sinking Sands]]
