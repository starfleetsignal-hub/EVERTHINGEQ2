## Steps

1. Go to the [[Den of the Widow Mistress]] {{waypoint 416, 64, 194}}, use the [[Drachnid Suit]] to avoid aggroing mobs, and recover the *mission case* from the body on the ground at {{waypoint 424, 33, 344}}.
1. Return to [[Charleston Gnomegomery]] on [[Dreg's Landing]].

## Rewards

- At least 14g 81s 38c
- +1000 faction with **Residents of Teren's Grasp**
- One of the Following, based on Class:
  - [[Abyssimal Slime]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Barbarian's Insanity]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Control Gem of the Clouds]] (All Priests)
  - [[Devious Archmagi Spellbook]] (All Mages)
