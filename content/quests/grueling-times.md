## Notes

- You must have completed the quest [[The Final Piece]] or Disciple Shula will not offer you this quest.

## Steps

![Gemstone in the Grueling Pits](images/Grueling_Times_Gemstone.jpg)

1. Travel to the [[Grueling Pits]] in the goblin camp north of the Swiftrider's Camp. {{waypoint 255, -106, -1035}}
1. Examine 6 gemstones being mined by [[A Sandscrawler miner|Sandscawler miners]] strewn about the POI.
   - One gemstone is outside the marked area, at {{waypoint 144, -100, -1087}}.
1. Return to [[Disciple Shula]].

## Rewards

- At least 1g
- One of the following, based on Class:
  - [[Emblem of Ancestral Passing]] (All Scouts)
  - [[Ring of Ancestral Passing]] (Wizard)
  - [[Ringlet of Ancestral Passing]] (All Fighters)
  - [[Signet of Ancestral Passing]] (???)
