## Notes

- This quest used to be the access quest for [[The Sanctum of Fear]], but it's completion is no longer required to enter the instance.
- Later in the quest, you will need four [[A clay golem heart|clay golem hearts]], which drop from the corpses of [[A clay golem|clay golems]] wandering the zone. You may loot them prior to being on the quest.

## Steps

1. Collect weapons from the Tae Ew Lizardmen:
   - 3 spears from [[A Tae Ew hunter|Tae Ew hunters]]
   - 5 runed swords from [[A Tae Ew defender|Tae Ew defenders]]
   - 5 maces from [[A Tae Ew warder|Tae Ew warders]]
1. Return to [[Nemar Gadgetglow]].
1. Find three auto-updating items from any lizardman in the dungeon:
   - Sliver of Thulzite
   - Shard of Thulzite
   - Piece of Thulzite
1. Return to Nemar Gadgetglow.
1. Find [[The Runes of Terror]]. {{waypoint 0, 61, -178}}
1. Kill [[The Acidic Mass]] at {{waypoint 0, 44, -214}}. It is the last spawn of a ring event started at the pool down the back stairs of the central pyramid on the upper terrace.
1. Receive a Broken Key, and take this to Nemar.
1. Speak to [[Ux Uval]] in the [[Codex of Cadavers]]. {{waypoint -2, -13, -76}}
1. Find [[The Pool of Dread]]. {{waypoint 0, 4, -8}}
1. Enter [[The Sanctum of Fear]]. {{waypoint -16.64, -10, -172.80}}
   - Make sure you have collected at least four [[A clay golem heart|clay golem hearts]] before proceeding to this step.
1. Kill [[Fright]] at {{waypoint 0, 35, -177}}. See the [[The Sanctum of Fear#Walkthrough|Sanctum of Fear Walkthrough]] for a detailed explanation on how to summon it.

## Rewards

- Formerly, access to [[The Sanctum of Fear]]
- At least 2g 69s 57c
- One of the following:
  - [[Blood Fire]]
  - [[Bone Razor (Level 40)|Bone Razor]]
  - [[Monsoon, Sword of the Swiftwind]]
  - [[Staff of Undead Legions]]
  - [[Withered Totem of Widdershins]]
