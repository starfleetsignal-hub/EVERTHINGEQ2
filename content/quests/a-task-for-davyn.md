## Steps

1. Speak with [[Taval Zane]], an inn patron, in the inn.
1. Speak with [[Jana Windstream]] outside the scribe shop.
1. Search the cart near Jana (right click on the crate and follow the wolf tracks).
1. Slay a rabid wolf that will spawn.
1. Return to Davyn Thornbranch.
1. Speak with [[Aelis]].

## Rewards

- At least 2s 40c
