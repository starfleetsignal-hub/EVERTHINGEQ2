## Notes

- The first quest in [[Overseer Velious Prelude Pack]], one of rewards from [[Destiny of Velious Crate]] (separate purchase directly from EQ2 website).
- [[Destiny of Velious Crate]] was introduced in June 2021 on Everquest2.com (Shop-->Packs). It is still available to purchase as of April 2026.
- Once added to Overseer window the series stays there permanently and cycles through 3 quests:
  - This quest.
  - [[Destiny of Velious - Velketor's Labyrinth (Quest)|Destiny of Velious - Velketor's Labyrinth]]
  - [[Destiny of Velious - Kael Drakkel (Quest)|Destiny of Velious - Kael Drakkel]]
  - then goes back to the first quest again.
- Any character on the account can start and complete any of the 3 quests if they show available.
