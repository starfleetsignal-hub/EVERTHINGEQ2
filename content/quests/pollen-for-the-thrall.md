## Steps

![Waterlace Leaves](images/Waterlace.png)

1. Gather fresh **waterlace pollen** from around [[Blackanvil Bay]] {{waypoint 829, 32, -17}}.
   - These look like lily pads floating atop the water around the edge of the bay. Note: The lily pads will not be visible if quest is gray
1. Return to [[Thaldhor]].

## Rewards

- At least 1p 41g 75s 13,200 status
- +1000 faction with **Shields of Maldura**
- +1000 faction with **Citizens of Maldura**
- One of the Following:
  - [[Evoker's Gearific Chain]]
  - [[Zaphardt's Invocation Cable]]
