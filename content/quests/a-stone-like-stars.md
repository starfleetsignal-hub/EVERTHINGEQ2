## Description

"Seth Hammerstone has a family reputation for creating beautiful stone carvings. He has come to this forest in search of a limestone that sparkles like the stars, even during the day. Gather several large samples of these stones and return to him." -- *Everquest II Journal*

## Steps

![Limestone](images/AStoneLikeStarsQuestObject.JPG)

1. Find 4 limestone samples at {{waypoint 370, 31, 40}}
   - More reported locations can be found on the Talk page
1. Return to [[Seth Hammerstone]]

- NOTE: Loc is general area of where the stones are ... they are found all over the valley where the orcs and treants are.

## Rewards

- At least 2s 78c
