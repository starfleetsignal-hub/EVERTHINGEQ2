## Steps

1. Enter the [[The Condemned Catacomb]] in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint -1135, -25, -1050}} (on a small isle near the [[Oracle Tower]]).
   - Speak to [[A Disciple of Disease Teleportation Wizard]] {{waypoint 2308, 33, 887}} near [[Gorowyn Beach]] to be sent to the woods southwest of [[Windstalker Village]] in Antonica.
   - Go to the Condemned Catacombs and choose the second instance on the list.
1. Speak to [[A Devoted Disciple of Decay]] at {{waypoint -174, 0, -30}}.
1. Kill [[A Concordium sorcerer]] (level 11▼▼-12▼▼) and loot [[Tattered Concordium Robe]] from the corpse.
1. Speak to the Devoted Disciple.  She asks you to lead her to safety. Use the [[Tether to the Disciple]] to get her to follow you.  Follow the catacombs to {{waypoint -82, 0, -81}}, being attacked at intervals by by several mobs that spawn at your level as well as the remaining original mobs (even if they are gray).
1. Once you reach the exit the Disciple will vanish, leaving behind a bag on the floor.  Search it and receive [[Odd seedpod]].
1. Return to Gorowyn (there is another Teleportation Wizard where you arrived in Antonica {{waypoint -1464, -12, -343}}); find [[Bucky Withywindle]] {{waypoint 2716, 136, 1360}} inside [[The Conservatory of the Reborn]].  Receive the sub-quest, [[A succulent Time to Live In]].
   1. Collect 3 seedpods (same as good version):
      1. [[The Sinking Sands]] - near the [[Croc Hunter Camp]] north of the dock
      1. [[The Commonlands]] - near the [[Nektulos Forest Station]], along the north zone wall
      1. [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] - through the front gates past the wood spikes
   1. Return to Bucky.
1. Head to the Sinking Sands again. Find ![a discarded carpet](images/Image:A_discarded_carpet.jpg) next to the usual carpet. Use it. It will bring you to the [[Twin Tears]]. Head west along the ridge to the huge rock to south-west. You'll find the body of ![a Devoted Disciple of Disease](images/Image:A_Devoted_Disciple_of_Disease_%28Object%29.jpg) at the rock {{waypoint -250, -90, -875}}.  The quest will update as you near the body.
1. Return to Evdokia in Gorowyn
   - The carpet at the Sinking Sands dock will return you to Gorowyn.
1. Head to [[North Freeport]].  [[Librarian Motius Feldarn]] gives the sub-quest, [[Arcane Apprentice Roll Call]].
   - Find an Academy of Arcane Science Apprentice; return to Motius
1. Go to [[Hate's Envy]] in [[Darklight Wood]] and speak to [[Lesaxle T'Zaflor]] **OR** [[Maj'Dul]] for [[Salyil]] next to the [[Library of Light]] {{waypoint -183, 161, -145}}.
1. Go to the [[Crossroads]] in the [[The Commonlands]] and talk to [[Luzmog Pusguzzler]]
1. Head east to {{waypoint -1070, -50, -240}}, where [[Meldrida Slate (NPC)|Meldrida Slate]] will spawn; follow her to a crumbling tower where she will attack you {{waypoint -1250, -65, -195}}.  [[Meldrida Slate (Monster)|Kill her]] and loot a [[Mysterious rune stone]].
1. Enter [[The Graveyard]] {{waypoint -1390, -90, -330}}. When you enter there is a crypt to the right {{waypoint 29, -10, 23}}; right click on the handle of the door and select "Enter using the rune stone as a key" to enter [[A Defiled Mausoleum]]. The quest updates.
1. Pick up the ![passage rune stone](images/Image:Passage_rune_stone_%28Tomb%29.jpg) on top of Sir Glimmon's sarcophagus {{waypoint -38, -45, -45}}.  There is no need to kill anything, although if you walk down the middle of the room or stand there, [[A disturbed soul]] at your level will spawn and attack.
1. Return to Evdokia in Gorowyn
