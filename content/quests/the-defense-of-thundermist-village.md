- *This quest is repeatable up to 5 times., with each turn-in giving a larger coin reward.*

## Steps

1. Kill a set number number of the following monsters in The Thundering Steppes. All mobs are found within [[Thundermist Valley]], to the west of The Village of Thundermist:
   - Kill 9 griffons.
   - Kill 9 badgers.
   - Kill 8 wolves.
   - Kill 8 bears.
   - Kill 12 lions.
1. Return to Tobias Vreldig.

## Rewards

1. At least 2s 91c
1. At least 5s 82c
1. At least 11s 49c
1. At least 17s 16c
1. At least 28s 56c
