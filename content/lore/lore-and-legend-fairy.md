- The quest level shown in your journal will scale with your character level, up to level 50.
- You must complete the two Boar's Head Quests to gain access to the librarian room.
- Level 60 fairies may be found on [[Gazer Isle]] in [[Tenebrous Tangle]]
- Level 30 fairies (medium respawn rate) around {{waypoint -241, 12, -5}} in [[Enchanted Lands]]
- There are level 6-11 fairies in the [[The Peat Bog]], a Qeynos beginner zone that is easily accessible for all via Antonica
- There are level 6-12 fairies in [[Oakmyst Forest]], a Qeynos beginner zone that is accessible via mariner's bell or from Graystone Yard.
- Sullivan Faries in Darklight Woods
