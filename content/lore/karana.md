![](images/Karana_-_The_Rainkeeper.jpg)
Karana is one of the nature [[Gods|deities]], the other being [[Tunare (Deity)|Tunare]], but is the most accepting of the two. He rules over the [[Plane of Storms]]. He controls the weather of the world, permitting it to be mostly calm and life-giving, but can quickly snap it into a frenzy of torment and destruction. All the rains that fall upon [[Norrath]] do so under his guidance and direction. It for this reason that he often goes by the name Rainkeeper, in addition to Stormfather. The continent of [[Karan]] is named in his honor.

Followers of Karana worship the power of the rain and respect the destructive force of hurricanes. Typical followers of Karana are rural humanoids - farmers, ranchers, hunters, and the like. They will often offer shelter to travelers trapped outside during a storm. Many of Karana's followers are also nomads, travelling where the winds take them. Karana's faithful are usually humble and generous, valuing strength, honesty and will not tolerate any disrespect of Karana, or of his wonders, for they know it is only through his mercy that all of Norrath is not consumed in an eternal tempest.

Although a neutral deity, Karana is allied with [[Tunare (Deity)|Tunare]] and [[Mithaniel Marr (God)|Mithaniel Marr]], and eternal enemy of [[Bertoxxulous]].
