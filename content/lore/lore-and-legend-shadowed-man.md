The book, [[On The Origins Of Shadowed Men]], can be found at the following locations:

- In [[South Qeynos]], a book on a small table beyond the red portal in the [[Concordium Tower]], at {{waypoint 711, 41, 107}}
- In [[North Freeport]], examine a blue book on a table on the second level of the [[Academy of Arcane Science]], at {{waypoint 5.83, 8.33, -118.24}}

Note that the quest level shown in your journal will scale with your character level, up to level 50.

You can find Shadowed men in [[Enchanted Lands]], [[Obelisk of Lost Souls]] and [[Nektulos Forest]]

- Note: In Nektulos Forest, Shadowed Men can be found immediately after the Hidden Trail and before entering Nektulos Forest proper. Make a left-hand turn before the brown mushroom and go straight ahead. Levels 24 - 25.
