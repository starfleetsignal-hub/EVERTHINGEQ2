- The book is also available during [[Nights of the Dead]]. You MUST complete [[There Will Be Ghosts]] in order to access the room the book is in. Once you have completed the quest, head back up from the basement and walk through the painting at the top of the stairs.
- With the arrival of [[Update:38|LU38]], the [[Master's Smite]], [[Master's Strike]], and [[Master's Rage]] skills were expanded to include  vampires. Provided players had completed: [[Enemy's Weakness]] (good-aligned) or [[Watching the Watchers]] (evil-aligned), the two final quests in the [[Bloodline Chronicles Timeline]].
- With the arrival of [[Update:54|LU54]], you can acquire the Vampire Lore and Legend quest, from examining any [[A vampiric incisor|vampiric incisors]].  A good place to get the incisor(s) is from the vampire encampment {{waypoint -239, 5, -310}} in Dark Light Woods (just past the Fairy and Werewolf encampments).
