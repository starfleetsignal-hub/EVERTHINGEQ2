![](images/Mithaniel_Marr_-_The_Truthbringer.jpg)
Mithaniel Marr, the Truthbringer, governs the [[Plane of Valor]]. He is the epitome of a paladin, and embodies the concepts of honor, courage, nobility, and loyalty. Mithaniel Marr is the son of [[Tarew Marr]], the god of water, and the twin brother of [[Erollisi Marr]], the goddess of love. He is the creator of the Frogloks and, along with his sister, is jointly responsible for the creation of the Barbarians. Therefore, by extension, he is also responsible for the creation of the Humans and Erudites. Mithaniel Marr is a martial deity, but unlike [[Rallos Zek (Deity)|Rallos Zek]], who concerns himself primarily with war itself and the most effective way to gain victory in war, the Truthbringer deals more with the moral and ethical aspects of combat, such as determining whether a given motivation for going to war is just or unjust.

His followers believe they are distinguished from the beasts of the world by their valor and honor. Followers of Marr live a life of a high moral standard and devote much of their lives to spreading the word of truth and selflessly giving to charity. They fight for the sake of all that is good in the world and will do so without fear of death. Many would say that Marr's followers take their cause a bit too seriously, having short patience for humor and mischief.

Mithaniel Marr is allied with [[Erollisi Marr]] and [[Karana]], and enemies with [[Innoruuk]], [[Cazic-Thule]], and [[Bertoxxulous]]. He has a friendly rivalry with [[Solusek Ro]].
