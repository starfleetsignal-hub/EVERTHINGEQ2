The starter book,[[Skeletons (QuestReward)|Skeletons]], can be found in the following locations:

- In [[Stormhold]], in [[The Library (SH)|The Library]], at {{waypoint -117, -32, -149}}.
- In [[Kelethin]] at  {{waypoint 292, 109, 90}} (click on bookcase in building North of the bank).
- In [[Darklight Wood]], examine a bookcase at {{waypoint 628.5, -24, 208}} on the first floor of [[The Tower of Bone]]

Note that the quest level shown in your journal will scale with your character level, up to level 50.

## Description

"I am searching for skeleton body parts in order to gain knowledge about their kind." -- *Everquest II Journal*
