- From the entrance you need to travel round the pit, jump across into a tunnel, travel through the round room with the goos, through a room with some Servants of Varsoon, into corridors with some Golems, through a room past the undead tailors in the deep area of [[The Ruins of Varsoon|Ruins of Varsoon]]. A blue book on the short, tilted book shelf in the back of the room.{{waypoint 9.70, 4.00, 69.05}}

- Note that the quest level shown in your journal will scale with your character level, up to level 50.
- If you're just mopping-up old L&L quests and don't care about the XP, then there are abundant Golems in the following locations:
  - The [[The Ruins of Varsoon|Ruins of Varsoon]] - Go to the [[The Thundering Steppes|Thundering Steppes]], then get a Griffen to [[Ruins of Karana|The Ruins of Karana]], and enter through the southern entrance at the bottom of [[Ruins of Karana]]. Lots of [[A clay guardian|Clay Guardian]] golems roaming the halls of half the zone. Also, in the room {{waypoint 160.43, 7.60, 65.61}} you can find some good drops among the alcoved Guardians.
