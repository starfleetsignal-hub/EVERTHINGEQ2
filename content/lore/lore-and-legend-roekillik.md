The quest is started by examining any of the dropped Roekillik items involved in the quest.

Note that the quest level shown in your journal will scale with your character level, up to level 100.

You can find Roekillik in [[The Sundered Frontier]].<br>
The items and updates may also come from the Roekillik in [[Enchanted Lands]] and [[The Thundering Steppes]], but have not confirmed this yet. *And Hero's Festival instances also.*
