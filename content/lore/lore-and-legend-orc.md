This book can be found in the following places:

- In [[The Commonlands]], A shield in the tower at [[The Crossroads]] {{waypoint -433.32, -44.09, -281.82}}
- In [[Crushbone Keep]], a weapon stand at {{waypoint 14, 0, 54}}

for the language

- In [[New Halas]] (specifically [[Pilgrim's Landing]]), kill Orcs to receive [[Orc Smuggler Requisition]] drops. These will offer the quest [[An Order of Orc Tongue]].

Note that the quest level shown in your journal will scale with your character level, up to level 50.

## Tips

In [[New Halas]] the Orcs in [[Pilgrim's Landing]] are very low level and can be killed en masse. Higher level are found in the Clefts of Rujark.
