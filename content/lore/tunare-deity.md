Tunare, Mother of All, Goddess of Life and Growth, resides upon the [[Plane of Growth]] from where she watches over all of her creations. Her blessings surround all living things. She is protective of what she considers to be her finest achievements, the good elf: High Elves and Wood Elves, whom she created as part of an agreement with [[Brell Serilis]] and [[Prexus]], as well as in response to the arrogance of [[Veeshan]]. She rewards faith and patience, and scorns those who foul the soil and destroy nature's bounty in the name of 'progress'. The only true form of progress is that of Growth, a force that maintains and empowers all living things.

Tunare is allied with [[Karana]], [[Erollisi Marr]] and [[Rodcet Nife]], and enemies with [[Bertoxxulous]].

## Accepting Tunare

By accepting Tunare, you devote yourself to confronting those who would destroy the living world. You will honor Tunare by preventing senseless destruction of nature whenever possible.
