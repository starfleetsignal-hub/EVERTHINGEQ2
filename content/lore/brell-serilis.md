​Brell Serilis, commonly referred to as the Duke of Below, was the first to come to [[Norrath]] during [[The Age of Scale (Lore)|The Age of Scale]]. He rules over [[The Underfoot]], a dark realm of vast caves and endless tunnels, at the heart of Norrath. From this underground nest, Brell seeded the depths of the world with all manner of creatures. During [[The Elder Age]], he created the Dwarves as part of the [[Gods]]' plan to keep watch over the dominant Dragonkind. Later, Brell created the Gnomes, resembling Dwarves to some extent, yet more wiry and gnarled, consumed with tinkering with devices more so than their cousins. The Gnomes refer to Brell as 'The Great Engineer', and gifted them a [[The Anguished Children of the Duke|manual]] on how to properly revere him.

Followers of Brell Serilis believe that the surface world is a waste of space. They find true happiness in the caves, caverns, and tunnels that perforate the belly of Norrath. But this is one of few points that the followers of Brell can agree upon. In addition to Dwarves and Gnomes, Brell is venerated amongst Ratonga, Gnolls, Kobolds, Bugbears, Centaurs, Minotaurs, Boarfiends, Burynai, Dhalgar, and many others.

The Duke does not view his children as good or evil, he only sees obedience and disobedience. He has gifted his creations with free will so that he can observe the choices they make.

Brell Serilis is an ally of [[Bristlebane]], and an enemy of [[Veeshan]].
