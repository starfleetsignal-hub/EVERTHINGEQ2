![](images/Quellious_-_The_Tranquil.jpg)
Quellious the Tranquil is the god of peace, patience, and inner strength. The ability to remain calm and collected, even during the most stressful events, is the gift that Quellious offers the conscience beings of [[Norrath]]. Obtaining inner peace and enlightenment are the most important things to followers of Quellious, for if all beings were able to know their inner selves, there would be no need for war. A simple rivalry can turn into a brawl, and a brawl can turn into a riot, and a riot into a war. What starts out as simple misunderstanding can escalate if those involved do not remain calm and patient with their emotions.

Followers of Quellious are not strict pacifists, however, and will fight to defend themselves and their loved ones. They often follow a nomadic lifestyle, constantly seeking what there is to know and hoping to find themselves along the way.

Quellious is allied with [[Rodcet Nife]] and [[Erollisi Marr]], and enemies with [[Rallos Zek (Deity)|Rallos Zek]] and [[Innoruuk]].

## Accepting Quellious

Do you have quarrels with your friends? Are you able to let disagreements go to the wayside when necessary? If you believe so, then begin the path of inner searching with Quellious as your guide.
