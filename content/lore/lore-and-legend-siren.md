Sirens may be found in the following zones:

- Individual level 20 solo [[Entrancing siren]] can be found off the coast of [[Thundering Steppes]] at {{waypoint -193, -29, -673}} and groups of level 23 solo [[Entrancing siren]] can be found farther out at {{waypoint -423, -78, -944}}
- Group of level 37 Heroic can be found at siren isle {{waypoint 174.27, 0.00, 360.80}} in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]]
- [[Butcherblock Mountains]], [[Tidesylph Isle]] at level 22.
