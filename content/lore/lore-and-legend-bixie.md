The book, [[Know Your Bixies]], can be found in the following locations:

- On a bookshelf on the top floor of [[The Tower of the Drafling]], at {{waypoint -123, 92, 68}}
- In a crate in a building in [[Clan Crushbone]] west of [[Crushbone Keep]], at {{waypoint -1172, 125, 47}} in [[Greater Faydark]]

Note that the quest level shown in your journal will scale with your character level, up to level 50.

1. You can find level 5 bixies (high respawn rate) at {{waypoint -156, -42, -563}} in [[Greater Faydark]].
1. You can find level 10 bixies (medium respawn rate) at {{waypoint -430, 4, -158}} in [[Greater Faydark]].
1. You can find level 41-45 bixies in [[The Tower of the Drafling]].
1. You can find level 60-65 bixies in [[Tenebrous Tangle]].
