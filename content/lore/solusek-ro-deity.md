God of the Sun, Lord of Fire, patron to all wizards, Solusek Ro rules over the [[Plane of Sun]] - a burning fortress hardened in the flame of the very stars themselves! His eyes burn with the fury of the Sun, his fists hold the power of an inferno! Known as the Burning Prince to mortals, he is the manifested consciousness of flame and the primal power of the Sun. He is the son of [[Fennin Ro]], Tyrant of Fire, who rules the greater elemental [[Plane of Fire]], from which the Plane of Sun derives its power.

Followers of Solusek Ro believe in the raw and unbridled power of fire. Fire is both a giver and taker of life. Fire is chaotic, but fair, as it consumes all in its path. Fire birthed the world and in fire shall it be consumed.

Solusek Ro rewards those devoted to the acquisition of power. Power is gained by superior force. True aggressive action is the only way for one to obtain what is desired. Followers of Ro have little fear. They are bold and brash, say what they mean, and do what they say. Those who seek true elemental power follow Solusek Ro, and thus many wizards turn to his burning embrace.

Solusek Ro is neither an enemy, nor ally, to any other [[Gods|deity]]. Although, he has a friendly rivalry with [[Mithaniel Marr (God)|Mithaniel Marr]].
