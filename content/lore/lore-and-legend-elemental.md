The book, [[Summoning an Elemental]], can be found at the following locations:

- In [[Qeynos Capitol District]] *([[South Qeynos]])*, examine a stack of books in the red portal room of the [[Concordium Tower]], at {{waypoint 712, 41, 99}}
- In [[The City of Freeport]] *([[North Freeport]])*, examine a blue book on a table on the ground level of the [[Academy of Arcane Science]], at {{waypoint 21, -5, -122}}
  - *Previously, one had to be level 25 in order to receive this quest. The book is now clickable at level 10, and possibly lower.*
  - *Note that the quest level shown in your journal will scale with your character level, up to level 50.*

- It was possible to get through this quest in maybe fifteen minutes in [[The Withered Lands]] *(accessed via griffen at [[Thurgadin Harbor]] in the [[Great Divide]] )*, in the marsh at the [[Tears of Tunare]], killing [[A marsh elemental|marsh elemental]] and [[A bloated marsh muck|bloated marsh muck]].
