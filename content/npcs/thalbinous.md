## Notes

- To spawn Thalbinous you must clear the three packs of [[A fiendish weedling|fiendish weedlings]] strewn about [[The Halls of Learning]]. There are two packs on the floor level, one on each side, and one pack near the top of the wall of ice. Once all three packs have been cleared, after about 30 seconds Thalbinous will spawn directly to the left of the ice wall, at the top.
  - To make the wall of ice climbable you must've completed the quest [[Punja's Progression]].
- [[Arbo'Akh]] states that Thalbinous is the last treant of the [[Elddar Forest]].
