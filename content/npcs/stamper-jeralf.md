## Description

Being from a clan within Temple Street, when the gnomes started their undisciplined tinkering into Necromecanica... Stamper jumped on board since he was a worshiper of Bertoxxulous. After all, only actions that please him will come from tinkering with plagues and the like! Stamper will use his powers in healing to protect his allies rather than attacking enemies by default.
