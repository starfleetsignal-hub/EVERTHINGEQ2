## Notes

This is a ghostly Di'zok that wanders around [[Dragon Drool Lake]], occasionally venturing near [[The Crypt Grounds]]. You can press H to hail him, and he will rotate through four different speech options:

- "Venril! I will see you gone from this world! May your torment be a thousand times what mine has been!"
- "Kirkata... my love... I am sorry."
- "Love that would last an eternity binds me to this world. My punishment."
- "What have I done... what have I done..."

Based on these statements, it is safe to assume that this is the ghost of Orthiss, from [[Orthiss and Kirkata]]. This is supported by the information we are given about Orthiss's fate at the end of the book.
