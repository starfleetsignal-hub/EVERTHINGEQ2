## Related Quests

In the patch notes for [[LU13]], there was the following:

- Landing in [[Everfrost]] quite by accident, [[Dovan Farfall]] finds himself interested in the peculiar history of these frozen lands. Perhaps you can help him uncover more information.

Each of these quests are started by examining a page found on the corpse of some creature in [[Everfrost]]:

- [[Student's Path: Ice Fright]] (40)
- [[Student's Path: Way of the Wolf]] (40)
- [[Student's Path: Arctic Monitor]] (40)
- [[Student's Path: Numbfoot Hunters]] (42)
- [[Student's Path: Icefin]] (42)
- [[Student's Path: Frostfin Sharks]] (42)
- [[Student's Path: Way of the Bear]] (43)

Then, in December of 2005, he vanished for a time and none could find him! Seems he was visiting home before preparing for his next big adventure!

In the patch notes for [[LU21]], there was the following:

- [[Dovan Farfall]] is once more in [[Everfrost]], looking for those who once offered to share their knowledge with him.

His current Dialog goes as follows:

Dovan Farfall says to you, "Ah, hello there. My studies have taken me to many places across Norrath and I have learned much. Before I venture into the Overrealm, I wanted to come back here and gather any additional information I could."

You say, "I've tried to expand my knowledge as well."

Dovan Farfall says to you, "That's great to hear! I've been gone for some time, so you must forgive my poor memory. Tell me, did we have dealings in the past?"

You say, "No, we did not."

This is apparently all he will say to you unless you have found one of the missing "Student's Path" pages.
