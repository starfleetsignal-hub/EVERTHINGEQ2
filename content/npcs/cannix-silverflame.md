## Lore

Although it's not explicitly stated, it's likely that Cannix is a member of the Order of Marr. Throughout the [[The Return of the Light]] quest he refers to valor and truth, attributes the deity [[Mithaniel Marr (God)|Mithaniel Marr]] values most. He also expresses dismay over ultimately lying to the player, although to his mind, it was for a greater good.
