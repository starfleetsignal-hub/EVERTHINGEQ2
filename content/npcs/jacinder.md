## Description

Created in Doomfire by the Fabrician of Flames to be a mindless minion to carry out her wishes, Jacinder was later granted sapience by one of the very objects they had been sent to steal -- Ayonae Ro's cithara.  Confused and weighted by guilt, Jacinder tried to run and hide only to be hunted and torn to shreds.  Thankfully, they could be sewn back together following their mistress' defeat.  Jacinder is now free to do as they wish, to go where they desire, and earn a little coin along the way.
