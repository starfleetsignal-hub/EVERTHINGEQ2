## Notes

Fhara Hanford was originally the North Qeynos furniture merchant prior to the joint edits by [[Antonia Bayle]] and [[Lucan D'Lere]] stating that all furniture crafted in the cities would be citizen made. She then became, for a brief time, the local seller of ranged weapon ammunition. As of [[LU43|game update 43]] she now participates as a trusted merchant of [[The Ironforge Exchange (Faction)|the Ironforge Exchange]].
