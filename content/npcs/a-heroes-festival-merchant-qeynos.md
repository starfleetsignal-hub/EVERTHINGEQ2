## Related Quests

In order to buy items players need two types of special currency from the annual [[Heroes' Festival Timeline|Heroes' Festival]].

## Items

This NPC sells special [[Heroes' Festival]] rewards in exchange for [[Token of Heroism|Tokens of Heroism]] and [[Mischeva's Tribute|Mischeva's Tributes]].

Offerings include a variety of [[Housing|house]] items, [[Appearance|appearance gear]], and weapons and armor with stats can be purchased at either the Freeport or Qeynos merchants.

- See the [[Heroes' Festival Merchant Items]] page for a full list.
