**Distressed merchants** are the placeholders for [[A gratified merchant|gratified merchants]]. They spawn in semi-random locations in the following zones:

- [[Antonica]] - usually near groups of gnolls or their tents
- [[The Commonlands]] - usually near groups of orcs
- [[The Thundering Steppes]] - usually near groups of gnolls or zombies
- [[Nektulos Forest]] - usually near groups of pirates
- [[Enchanted Lands]] - usually near groups of fairies
- [[The Feerrott]] - usually near groups of lizardmen
- [[The Sinking Sands]] - usually near groups of undead

Distressed merchants can spawn in any area of the zone where there are several groups of mobs close to each other. For example, in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]],  several groups of orc mobs gather on the path leading to [[Deathfist Citadel]]. Killing all the mobs (usually 4 groups) repeatedly has a chance of spawning a distressed merchant. In [[Antonica]], the same would be true of groups of [[Gnoll|gnolls]] (again usually 4 groups around a tent, fire, or tree).

Once a distressed merchant appears (or if they've already appeared and you're kind enough to stop and help them), clear all groups of mobs around the distressed merchant and wait for a moment. The distressed merchant will disappear and [[A gratified merchant]] will appear in their place and offer to buy items from you (at their maximum value) or sell special items to you. For more information on the benefits of saving a distressed merchant, see [[A gratified merchant]].
