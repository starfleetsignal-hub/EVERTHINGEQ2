## Backstory

Lanys T'Vyl is known as the Daughter of Hate and the goddess of envy. She was born to a priestess in Neriak, fathered by the Prince of Hate, Innoruuk during the Age of Turmoil. His plans were to use her as an avatar to strengthen the influence of hate throughout Norrath.

## Firiona vs Lanys

For the most part, Lanys has been a success in that respect. She has been responsible for countless vile acts when not thwarted by her rival, the Champion of Tunare, Firiona Vie. Lanys was successful in sacking the Elven outpost of Kunark, leaving the good-natured races of the world without a safe haven on that volatile continent. Years earlier, she and her mentor, Laarthik K'Shin, led an assault on the fort of High Pass. Their goal was to claim the highly important northern trade route between Qeynos and Freeport. The attempt was unsuccessful and Lanys would have perished in the war, which became known as Bloody Kithicor, but her father intervened. Innoruuk opened a portal from his realm in the Plane of Hate and abducted Lanys, saving her from certain death. This act is said to have raised her to demigod status. Over the years, Lanys traveled back and forth from the Plane of Hate to Norrath many times. Some tales tell of a time when she sought to seal her father from the mortal realm. This had led some to believe that she is, in fact, no longer a demigod, having fallen from the favor of Innoruuk.

## Kunark Ascending

Inside her newly formed Vaedenmoore, the Plane of Malice, Innoruuk arrives before her to punish her for being brought back to life. Innoruuk is immediately struck down, with Lanys absorbing his godly power. It is only through mortal intervention that her Chaos Rebirth Stone is destroyed and her plans for malice and destruction are foiled.

## Planes of Prophecy

With the Chaos Rebirth Stone destroyed, Lanys turns her attention to the planar realms of the other gods for her next scheme. The Council of the Gods discuss steps to stop her, but never come to agree upon directly intervening. Lanys uses an unknown artifact that allows her to steal other artifacts from different timelines, including the unexploded Dresolisk crystal from the Prime Norrath timeline from EverQuest 1. Thanks to adventurers stepping in, they are able to get one step ahead of Lanys and delay her ritual to detonate the Dresolisk Crystal inside the Plane of Fire -- an act that would've destabilized the planes and took out many other gods in the process. The Council of Gods take the fight to her inside the Molten Throne. Once again, thanks to mortal intervention, the idea is sparked to detonate the Dresolisk Crystal on Lanys herself. Fennin Ro, Solusek Ro, and Druzzil Ro are able to shield everyone from the blast, while Lanys is atomized into nothingness.

## Chaos Descending

The death of Lanys T'Vyl serves as the launching point of the storyline of the Chaos Descending questline. Despite overwhelming evidence that you did it in self preservation and first hand witnesses from numerous Gods, [[The Tribunal]] accuses you of deicide, using the Dresolisk Crystal to kill Lanys T'Vyl. Druzzil Ro steps in to take your punishment of being locked up inside the Plane of Justice for all of eternity.
