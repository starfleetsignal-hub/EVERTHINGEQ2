- At 10,000 faction - advanced [[Tradeskill|tradeskill]] recipes
- At 20,000 faction - [[Bathezid Craftmanship Volume I]]
- At 30,000 faction - [[Bathezid Craftmanship Volume II]]
- At 40,000 faction - various adventure equipment themed for the zone and this race's culture.
