## Researcher

- Researches the following:
  - [[Crystalline Diamond]] -  5 days and 5 hours to research
  - [[Crystalline Opal]] - 5 days and 5 hours to research
  - [[Crystalline Turquoise]] - 4 days and 16 hours to research
  - [[Crystalline Ice Shard]] - 5 days and 5 hours to research
  - [[Crystalline Seawater]] - 4 days and 16 hours to research

- Named mobs can drop a research time reduction item that is only temporary, make sure you use em when they drop

  - [[Worn Prayer Token]]
  - [[Worn Prayer Beads]]
