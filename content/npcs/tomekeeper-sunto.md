## Notes

- Tomekeeper Sunto is an NPC ghost used in the Lost Legends of Lavastorm Heritage Quest, he can be found in the library of the Temple of Solusek Ro and to the left along the wall from the library entrance. He is not far from the Book of King Tranix, caution should be used in speaking to him as there are undead mobs in the area around him.
