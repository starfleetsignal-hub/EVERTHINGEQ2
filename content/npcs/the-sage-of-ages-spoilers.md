## Lore

- Unknown to the player at the time they first encounter the Sage of Ages, he is actually [[Darathar]], a dragon in human disguise. Darathar is an enforcer for the Ring of Scale, sent to kill [[Lord Nagafen]] and [[Vox, the White Dragon|Lady Vox]] for defying Veeshan's Law which prohibits two dragons of opposing elements from mating and creating a prismatic dragon. He manipulates the player into assisting him in stealing one the prismatic eggs created by the union of Nagafen and Vox.
- When the player "rescues" him from Maiden's Gulch, he is not actually in any danger. Because a dragon is forbidden to kill another dragon, Darathar was given an artifact called the Harness, which enables him to control the drakota. Darathar was successful using the them to kill Lady Vox, but was unable to kill Lord Nagafen.
- Captain [[Captain Varlos (Queen's Colony)|Draik Varlos]], who rescues the player when they first enter the game aboard the [[Far Journey]], is also later revealed to be Darathar in human form.
