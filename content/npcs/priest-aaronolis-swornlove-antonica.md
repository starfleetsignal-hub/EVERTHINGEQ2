## Related Quests

A Priest of [[Erollisi Marr]], Aaronolis was the quest giver for the first [[Erollisi Day]] celebration in 2005. The quest was called [[Love of Ages Past (Antonica)|Love of Ages Past]]. In 2008, he offered the quest [[Find 'em a Find, Catch 'em a Catch! (Qeynos)|Find 'em a Find, Catch 'em a Catch!]] to players willing to play matchmaker.
