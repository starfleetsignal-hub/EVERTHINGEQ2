## Crystalline Spiderling Silk

During and after the quest [[Dream Weaving]], you can right click Ktlk and "gather silk" to receive 2 [[Crystalline Spiderling Silk|Crystalline Spiderling Silks]]. You can repeat this action every 24 hours (real time).

## Trivia

**K't'lk** was a Hamalki scientist, a central character in the Star Trek (TOS) novel *The Wounded Sky*. The Hamalki are described as glass or crystalline spiders.
