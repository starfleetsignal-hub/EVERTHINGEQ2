## Dialogue

- Trainer Kaali Saarinen: "I can tell you all about mending and banking! Banking and mending! Which do you want to hear about?"
- Player Character: "Tell me about banking."
- Trainer Kaali Saarinen: "You may already have an account with the Kelethin First Regional Bank. Go see Banker Athinae in Kelethin to access your account. You can access your Kelethin bank account from Qeynos, but not from Freeport."
- Player Character: "How do I open my bank?"
- Trainer Kaali Saarinen: "Inside the bank, your mouse pointer will turn into a dial icon when hovered over a banker. Just click on the banker to open your bank window. You'll see eight general slots and four "shared" slots. you can put bags or containers in your bank to increase the number of available slots."
- Player Character: "How do I make deposits and withdrawals?"
- Trainer Kaali Saarinen: "Coins and items can be dragged from your inventory to the bank window. To drag one item from a stack, hold down the Ctrl key as you drag it. To drag a certain number of coins or items, hold down the Shift key and drag, then enter the quantity."
- Player Character: "What are "shared" bank slots?"
- Trainer Kaali Saarinen: "Items (but not coin) placed in your shared slots can be accessed by other characters on your account that are also citizens of Kelethin. Lore and No-Trade items cannot be put into the shared slots. Characters that are not Kelethin citizens cannot access a Kelethin bank. So, you could not access a bank in Qeynos or Freeport."
- Player character: "Any other banking facts I should know?"
- Trainer Kaali Saarinen: "If you choose to join a guild made up of your fellow players one day, you can access your guild bank by right-clicking on a banker and selecting "Guild Bank.""
- Player Character: "I want to learn about mending items."
- Trainer Kaali Saarinen: "Your weapons and armor wear down when you die during combat. When they are down to zero durability, you no longer receive any benefit from them. You can visit a mender, such as Mender Kaarlo Karpela at Gearheart's Forge in Kelethin, to get them repaired. Speak to Trainer Leianna Teiampa if you wish to learn more about the effects of death."
- Player Character: "I'll return for training later."
