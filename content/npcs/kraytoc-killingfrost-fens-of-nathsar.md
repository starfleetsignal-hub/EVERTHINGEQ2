Kraytoc Killingfrost, often referred to by his order simply as "Killingfrost," is the leader of the Order of Rime, a mysterious and warlike sect from the continent of [[Velious]] that landed on the northern coast of the [[Fens of Nathsar]] on the continent of [[Kunark]] around the year 3820.
