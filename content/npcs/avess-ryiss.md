## Sells

Avess Ryiss sells the fabled set armor of every class.  To buy a piece of the armor, one must first loot a pattern and three gems from named and trash (respectively) in [[Veeshan's Peak]].  The pattern and gems are used to buy each piece.  The necessary items are different for almost every class. He will not offer to sell you anything that you can't equip.

## Notes

- Avess will not want your business unless your standing with Legion of Danak is greater than -20K. If you've done all the Jarsath Wastes solo quests your faction should be well above; if you don't like questing your best bet is to grind for faction by killing Dracurion droags.  At only +25 faction for each kill, questing may be faster even if you don't like doing it.
- [[RoK Fabled Sets]] - the Level 78 and 80 Class Set's
