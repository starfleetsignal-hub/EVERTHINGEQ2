Sensei Makoto Shoda originally served as the head of the dojo in [[The Elddar Grove]]. He was originally called to become the prophet for [[Quellious]] during the [[Return of the Gods Timeline|Return of the Gods]] live events leading upto the release of [[EoF]]. Unlike the other pre-release prophets, who moved on to lead players through their deity's [[Deity Quests|quest line]], this one was replaced once the events were over. NPC [[Pacificator Merrek]] took over the role of prophet of [[Quellious]]. Since that time, Makoto has returned and is now known as [[Master Makoto Shoda]]. Master Makoto can be found wandering the beach in [[Fens of Nathsar]] and is now involved in [[Monk Epic Weapon Timeline|Monk Epic weapon]] quests.
