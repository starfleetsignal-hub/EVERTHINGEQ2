## Description

Jazbella Cantile is a powerful siren that decided the world of the Grotto was not where she desired to be. Her battle prowess is undeniable and her ability to entice is incapable of being satisfied.

## Rarity

All mercenaries from [[Mercenary Crates|mercenary crates]] are granted randomly. Rarity describes how likely or unlikely a player is to get this mercenary from a crate and, in turn, how much they will sell to [[Sneerio Backlash]] for [[Status points|status points]].

- Jazbella Cantile is uncommon and sells for 3,000,000 status.
