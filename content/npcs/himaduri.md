## NOTES

As with some of the other NPC's here, Himaduri appears in 2 different places within the [[Plane of Magic]] dependent upon which stage of the timeline the player is on. Initially, she appears at the [[House Vahla]] platform to offer quests, but later moves to [[Manacross Island]], to practice her magic arts, and this is where the above screenshot was taken.
