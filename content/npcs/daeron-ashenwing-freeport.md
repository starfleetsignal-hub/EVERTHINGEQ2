## Description

Martial Mercenary "Daeron Ashenwing" once served in the army of Kerafym before risking his life to escape across the frozen wastes of Velious. In freedom, he continues a life of battle, all he has ever known, but uses his skills only for those he chooses.

- [[Calm Tranquility]]
- [[Charging Tiger]]
- [[Five Rings]]
- [[Lightning Palm]]
- [[Mend]]
- [[Rising Dragon]]
- [[Rising Phoenix]]
- [[Roundhouse Kick]]
- [[Striking Cobra]]
- [[Waking Dragon]]

## Rarity

This mercenary does not have a common-to-ultra rare classification because it can **not** be sold to [[Sneerio Backlash]].

## Notes

- Available as a /claim item only for those who have purchased the collectors edition of the Tears of Veeshan expansion.
- Daeron will appear in the Mercenary Den of each city only after you activate the token in your inventory that appears upon claiming it.
