## Homage

Both the name of this NPC and the quest offered is an homage to the television show, *[CSI: Crime Scene Investigation](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSI:_Crime_Scene_Investigation) *(a.k.a. *CSI: Las Vegas*):

- The NPC shares the name of [Catherine Willows](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Willows)
- Season 1 of *CSI* included an episode, *Law of Gravity*: a case solved by Willows' expertise in blood splatter
