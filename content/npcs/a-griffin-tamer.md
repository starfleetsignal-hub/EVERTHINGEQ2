## Notes

This NPCs type found all over Norrath in many zones. They are typically on top of [[Griffin Tower|Griffin Towers]], though they can also be found at ground level. In the past, most of them would not allow you to travel until you completed a quest. In most cases, the quests are now optional. In addition the towers in *most* zones are now linked directly. If you are looking for one in a zone, see [[Griffin Tower|page that lists locations]] of most towers.
