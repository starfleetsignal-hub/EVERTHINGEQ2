## Reaching her as an Evil Character

Make sure to hug the wall of the Fae Court acorn she's in until you get inside it.  There is a level 95^^^ Epic x4 guard who will kill you quite quickly otherwise.  This guard can See Invis/Stealth, but without a better route to take this may be the best (albeit lackluster) suggestion for the time being.  There's also a level 95^^^ Heroic guard--who can also See Invis/Stealth--immediately inside the door, so it's uncertain that Evil characters can reach her at all without help.

It turns out that killing a level 95^^^ heroic guard incurs no faction penalty, even for good characters, and is, according to the one who helped me, "good exp", so your best bet may be asking for help and bribing them with the kill :-).
