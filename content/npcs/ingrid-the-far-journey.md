### Lore

**Ingrid** is a crewmember aboard [[The Far Journey]], under the direction of [[Captain Varlos (The Far Journey)|Captain Varlos]]. She has a self-professed love of the sea, and seems to enjoy uttering seafaring tropes such as "yo ho ho and a bottle of rum".

Although her position aboard the ship is not directly communicated, it can be surmised by the orders barked at her by the Captain ("secure those chests", "fix that yard-arm") that she may be (or training to become) the ships Boatswain.

She seems fascinated by the arrival of [[Geredo (The Far Journey)|Geredo]], and prone to gawking at him rather than working. Despite this, she has been putting in more than her usual share of the work in an attempt to secure the coin needed to buy a piece of moon rock ([[Shard of Luclin]]) from Merchant [[Vim (The Far Journey)|Vim]].
