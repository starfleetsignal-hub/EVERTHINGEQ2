## Description

The 'starter' mercenary for players intitially entering [[Scars of Destruction]] content.  If not hired manually, players will be 'guided' to do so via [[Sodden Archipelago: For The Greater Good!]], the SoD Tishan's Lockbox quest.

## NOTES

- While the zone itself is level/flag restricted for normal teleportation options, players of any level can hire him if they find 'alternate means' to enter the zone. *('alternate means' usually involves making use of a guild's Rally Banner and entering via Guild Hall [[Guild Strategist|strategist]], but other methods also exist)*
- Hangar Rotstomper used to be a Coldain (as seen in the "original appearance" image), but was later changed to a standard Dwarf, matching [[Mercenary: Hangar Voidstomper|Hangar Voidstomper]]'s appearance.
