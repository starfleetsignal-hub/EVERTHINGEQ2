## Notes

- One of 3 new plants that show up in [[Nektulos Forest]] after [[LU24]].
- These plants are, for the most part, non-interact-able.  They can be targeted, but not attacked and they do not respond to the player.
- Wandering too close to them triggers the detrimental **Hate Blossom**
  - Decreases STR, WIS, STA, AGI, and INT of target by 5.0
  - Duration: 5 minutes
