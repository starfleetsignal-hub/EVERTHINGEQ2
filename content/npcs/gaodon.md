## Notes

- There are two versions of this character. When you first encounter him at {{waypoint -52, -43, 55}}, he is an un-hailable NPC. You must kill his other form, at {{waypoint -47, -54, 50}}, which is keeping the NPC form 'shackled'. Once dead, right-click the NPC version to hail him.
- From his dialogue he reveals that he was known as Gaodon Foi'zarth, brother to [[Silzok Foi'zarth]]. Together they mastered the secrets of clay but Silzok grew jealous. One day Silzok beat Gaodon and fed him to the chokidai. Insead of leaving him for dead, he bound his soul to the heart of a construct of clay. He did this with the [[Hearts of Gaodon]], the only golem hearts strong enough to pull a soul from [[Ethernere]] and bind it within a clay golem.
- He also claims that "A sarnark heart can beat for days after being removed."
