### Lore

**Draik Varlos** is Captain of the ship [[The Far Journey]]. His crew consists of [[Waulon (The Far Journey)|First Mate Waulon Highpebble]], [[Helmsman Nyles]], and [[Ingrid (The Far Journey)|Ingrid]]. The crew recently rescued you as well as several other adventurers who were floating adrift in the sea. In exchange for your passage to The Isle of Refuge (see [[Queen's Colony]] or [[Outpost of the Overlord]]), the Captain issues you a series of chores.
