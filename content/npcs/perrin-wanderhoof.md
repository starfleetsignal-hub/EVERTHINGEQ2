## Description

The magic in his homelands has been corrupted, from what we do not know. The plants and wild life have all felt the affects of this corruption. The magic has not reach throughout yet, and we can only hope it stays that way. I weep for the loss of the satyrs happiness, dance, and song. Perrin uses this magic of the corrupted forest to heal group as well as cure ailments. He is also strong in offensive magical arts. Perrin will use his powers in healing to protect his allies rather than attacking enemies by default.

## Notes

- Mercenary discovery:
  - non-Scout(scroll) Tracking **DOES** work, set track to [All].
  - Scout tracking **DOES** work, set to track [All].
  - Using **/target Perrin** does not work.
  - Asking a guard to "find" does not work.
  - Using Mercenary-track (ala [[Claz'zakla]]) **DOES** work.
- Perrin cycles through his various spawn points while he is up.
