Ecology NPCs added for [[Nights of the Dead]] 2008 were puns of [[Nights of the Dead At The Movies|scary movie characters]], and spawned in random locations throughout the starting cities.  This one was a spoof of **Jason Vorhees** of "Friday the 13<sup>th</sup>."
