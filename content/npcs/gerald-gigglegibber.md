Part of the 2007 April Fool's Day celebrations.

If you die and do NOT revive, this guy appears and ...

If he says **"Bristly Bane loves you!"**, you will be resurrected.

If he says **"You make Bristly Bane sad"** he will die of pain and suffering, and you are still dead.
