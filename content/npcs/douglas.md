## Notes

- Douglas appears only during the [[Erollisi Day]] festival and sells holiday-themed items for [[Erollisi coin|Erollisi coins]] and regular game coins, plus older recipe books.
- Some items may be duplicates of those you can chose from the [[Hearts a'Flutter]] races or items from retired quests.
- The other [[Erollisi Day]] merchant, who since 2026 sells only NEW items, is [[Steven]].

## Merchandise

- Click [[Erollisi Day Merchant Items (Douglas)|here]] for a long list of the items this NPC sells.

### Fun Fact

Steven and Douglas are based on Steve and Doug Butabi, two brothers from recurring Saturday Night Live sketch "the Roxbury Guys" and the movie "A Night at the Roxbury" about their nightclub-hopping antics.
