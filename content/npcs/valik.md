## Lore

**Valik** is a rescued passenger aboard [[The Far Journey]]. He is looking forward to getting off the ship and finding work. He makes mention of [[The Far Seas Trading Company (Faction)]] an organization that has established The Isle of Refuge (see [[Queen's Colony]] or [[Outpost of the Overlord]]).
