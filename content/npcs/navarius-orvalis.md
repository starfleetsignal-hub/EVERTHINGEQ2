## Notes

Navarius is the Sage for the city of [[Freeport]], selling many creature catalog books, racial history books, zone-specific kill task books, some lore and legend books, and a few house item books for quests where you did not get to keep the book. You also talk to Navarius for your heritage quest suffix titles.
