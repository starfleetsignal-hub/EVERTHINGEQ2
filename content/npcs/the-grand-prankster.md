The Grand Prankster is one of the planar manifestations of the god [[Bristlebane]]. He also appears in game as the [[Avatar of Mischief]].<br>
He was first encountered during the celebrations of [[Bristlebane Day]] as [[Blat Berisen]] (this name is an anagram of Bristlebane) just outside any of the 6 major cities offering the quest [[Tin Metal Protection]].<br>
In the final update for this quest he is revealed to be the Prankster himself.<br>
Afterwards he will offer the quest [[Rescuing the Princess - A Bristlebane Tale]] and offer to turn your [[Gigglegibber Mini-Comfort Crate]] into the [[Blessed Gigglegibber Mini-Comfort Crate]].
