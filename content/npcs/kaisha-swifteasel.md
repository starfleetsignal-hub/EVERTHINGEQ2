In order to get these quests, you must first complete [[Ship Out]] and you must be at least artisan level 65. There is no limit on the number of times these quests can be completed to earn faction with the [[Far Seas Supply Division (Faction)]], coin and [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]]. Like the [[Tradeskill Writs]] that grant [[Tradeskill Faction]] for your city, there is no time limit on when you can begin another one of these Rush Orders after you complete one and turn it in.

These are timed Rush Orders. If you do not complete all of the items within the time limit of 8 minutes and 30 seconds, you will have to start these tradeskill tasks over from the beginning and make all of the items again. In order to start the timer and find out which items you will need to complete, click the desk in front of Kaisha Swifteasel.

To complete the tasks, you will also need to bring common Harvestable (no rares, no special harvestables) and fuel appropriate for the Tier of the quest. The Rush Orders will ask you to make items appropriate for your tradeskill that are found in the Essentials recipe books for your class.

The room you go to get these quests has an Engraved Desk that is used by [[Sage|Sages]]. The Isle of Mara has the tradeskill workstations (or tables) in various locations for all classes, however you can save additional time by bringing the portable workstation appropriate for your tradeskill.

**Important**: If you are playing on a time-locked server, these quests will not be available until [[The Shadow Odyssey]] expansion is unlocked. The prerequisite quest [[Ship Out]] is only available from the Moors of Ykesha.
