## Description

Brother to [[Krivok Honorclaw]] and loyal member of the Lujien clan Krivix has been known to hire his services out to defend those who need protection. He only charges those he protects in order to repair his armor and further his studies.

NOTE: The ribbon doesn't work very well in Freeport. Go to the Seafarer's Roost, go out onto the deck, then go downstairs and click on the door for the mercenaries for hire, you will find them on the right-hand side.
