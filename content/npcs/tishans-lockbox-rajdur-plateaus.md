## Notes

- Provides a free set of level 125 equipment based on class.
- Provides a free flying mount, the [[Threadbare Buddy]]
- You can obtain an additional set of armor for your mercenary.
- The gear has 385 resolve, though with less potency than equivalent gear from [[Visions of Vetrovia]].
- All equipment has one or more adornments attached to it. These adornments are not visible until the item is purchased.
- The [[Ascension Mastery Primer]] can be obtained multiple times to upgrade all 4 Ascension classes to 20, if the char is at least level 120
