## Notes

Since 2026 this version of Erben in The Chronoweave sells **ONLY NEW** items added in the current year. For all older items check [[Distun Delki]] nearby.<br>
After the event ends this Chronoportals merchant moves outside to [[The Commonlands]] for limited time. During that time he will sell **ALL** Chronoportal merchandise, not just the new items.

## Merchandise

- This merchant only sells items that can be purchased with the event currency, [[Ancient Platinum Coin|Ancient Platinum Coins]].
