## Description

*"Nok is smaller than most Kromzek due to being a descendant of a human blood line. He was cast out of Kael Drakkel due to his defense of a lesser being that he had grown to care for. This is not the Kromzek way. He now sells his might and magic to those who would need his protection"*.

## Rarity

All mercenaries from [[Mercenary Crates|mercenary crates]] are granted randomly. Rarity describes how likely or unlikely a player is to get this mercenary from a crate and, in turn, how much they will sell to [[Sneerio Backlash]] for [[Status points|status points]].

- Decanus Nok Brinloy is common and sells for 1,000,000 status.
