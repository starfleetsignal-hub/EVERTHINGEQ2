## History

Sir Trodonis was once a street rat stealing from those he could. He found faith in the Overlord, and believes [[Overlord Lucan D'Lere]] to be kept on [[Norrath]] by the planes, an arisen being greater than the gods. He holds true to [[Freeport]], noting that "monsters" like [[Ogre|Ogres]], [[Troll|Trolls]] and [[Iksar]] are given safe haven, allowing them to be civilized members of society. He is a crusader of [[The Lucanic Knights (Faction)]] and a defender of the Overlord of Freeport.
