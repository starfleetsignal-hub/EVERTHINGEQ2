## Notes

![Alfred, during his tenure in Qeynos Harbor's shipyards.](images/Alfred_Ironforge.jpg)
Alfred Ironforge was originally located in [[Qeynos Harbor]] in the shipyard and was the crafting trainer for all up and coming crafters. With [[LU43]], however, he relocated to the old furniture store in North Qeynos when [[The Ironforge Exchange (Faction)|The Ironforge Exchange]] took over and he now hands out work orders to eager crafters.
