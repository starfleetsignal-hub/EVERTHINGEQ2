## Description

Dwarven Templar, on a pilgrimage in the name of Brell, he is destined to become a dwarf of rank within the order of the Church of Brell. Before he can do so, he must prove his worth my offering protection to anyone who might be in need. However, no one said he had to do it for free. Bdorn will use his powers in healing to protect his allies rather than attacking enemies by default.
