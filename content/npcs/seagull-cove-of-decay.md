Spawns once the zone is completely emptied of other mobs.  Four of them appear on the beach rocks at approximately:

- (-270, -13, 1,165), near chest at water's edge
- (-145, -19, 1,155), near the tents by the water
- (-120, -12, 1,045), semi-near the zone entrance
- (-195, -16, 1,085), also semi-near the zone entrance

The seagulls appear as NPCs; they have no "consider" information and are unattackable.  Hailing them causes them to fly to the water's edge, where each one spawns [[An assassin snake]] that eats the bird.
