## Notes

- The Sarge was one of the halfling leaders of the coordinated assault on [[The First Gate|Neriak's First Gate]] by the *Kithikor Rangers* and elite members of the *Leatherfoot Brigade*. Part of his involvement is chronicled in the Leatherfoot Tales.
