These traders sell rewards for those assisting [[The Chronomancers (Faction)|The Chronomancers]] by performing the quests described in [[Path of the Past Timeline]].<br>

## Location

They can be found in the starting cities of Norrath:

- [[Qeynos Harbor]] - opposite the docks {{waypoint 891, -25, 20}}
- [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]] - [[East Freeport]], south pier, behind [[The Harbor Exchange]]  {{waypoint -276, -56, 84}}
- [[Kelethin]] - ramp down to [[The Green Knoll]] {{waypoint 443, 92, 181}}
- [[Timorous Deep|Gorowyn]] - porch of [[Gorowyn Heights]], [[The Wards]] {{waypoint 2824, 134, 1154}}
- [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]] does not have a trader, only a [[Timeless Chronomage (Neriak)|Timeless Chronomage]]
- [[New Halas]] - first building on the right coming up from the docks {{waypoint -117, 147, -36}}
