She's a bard that sings a parody of the theme song of The Beverly Hillbillies, an American sitcom that ran from 1962 to 1971 on CBS.

Lyrics:

"Ooooh, come and listen to a story 'bout an adventurer named, 'Led'..."<br>
"...a poor necromancer, barely kept his party fed..."<br>
"...well then one day he was castin' at some brood..."<br>
"...and out from the critter came a bubblin' ooze."<br>

"Well, the next thing you know ol' Leds a runnin' scared..."<br>
"...partyfolk said, Led get away from there..."<br>
"...they said gettin' a resurrection is the place you're gonna be..."<br>
"...so Led casted up a spell called, 'home is where I oughta be'..."
