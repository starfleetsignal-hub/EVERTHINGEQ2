## History

Xhorroz is a thulian agent from [[Modinthule]], marooned upon [[Norrath]] with the closing of the [[Fear Gate]].
He serves [[Cazic-Thule]], and seeks to reawaken the [[Fear Gate]] to allow the forces of fear to drive forth upon this [[Norrath]].

He seeks knowledge held in the Overrealm by the dragon empire.
