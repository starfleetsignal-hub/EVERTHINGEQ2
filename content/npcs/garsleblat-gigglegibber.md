## Notes

See the [[Frostfell Vendor Items]] for a list of items sold by this NPC. This NPC sells the "smaller" event items like tradeskill recipes, seasonal decor (wrapped packages, basic ornaments), and snowglobes.

**Additional Merchants:**

- [[Gerbi Frostfoot (Frostfell)|Gerbi Frostfoot]] sells most of the "big" event items like the holiday themed mounts, ice-themed house items, all of the event armor and other gear.
- [[Ginneldarf Gigglegibber]] can be found *only* inside the [[The Icy Keep]]. He primarily sells the event fuel, but also sells the keys needed to locate chests and craft the year-round [[Frostfell Crafting Stations]].
