## Notes

- Provides a free set of level 130 equipment based on class.
  - You can obtain an additional set of armor for your mercenary.
  - The gear has 600 resolve.
- All equipment has one or more adornments attached to it. These adornments are not visible until the item is purchased.
  - All of these adornments (and more!) can also be purchased directly.
- The [[Ascension Mastery Primer]] can be obtained multiple times to upgrade all 4 Ascension classes to 20, if the character is at least level 120
