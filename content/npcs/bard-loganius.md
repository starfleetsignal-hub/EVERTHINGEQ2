## Notes

Some interesting information about this NPC and his compatriot, [[Entertainer Zagat]]. Clicking on the donation mug that stands between them will remove 1 silver from your inventory and start them singing (words only, not music) a folk song that was originally posted to the SoE forums () and subsequently placed in game.
