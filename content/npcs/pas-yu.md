## Notes

Pas Yu sells items related to the [[Panda, Panda, Panda!]] "Days of Summer" timeline.  Complete more quests on a character to earn account-wide flags to purchase additional items from this merchant.

## Item visibility

- ***Many non-cosmetic (aka 'stats') items on this merchant are not visible until the character viewing the merchandise is the appropriate level to equip or otherwise make use of the stats on the item***
- Most cosmetic items on this merchant are not bound by this visibility restriction
