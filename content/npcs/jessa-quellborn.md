**Jessa Quellborn** is the mother of [[Spirit of Jessa Quellborn-Rucksif|Jessa Quellborn-Rucksif]] and wife of [[Nicadius Quellborn]]. She died on the voyage from [[Erudin]] to [[Faydwer]] and was buried where the gazebo now stands. Thanks to her husband, a priest of [[Quellious]], and his blessing, the ground here is Hallowed ground and safe from the undead that roam the estate.
