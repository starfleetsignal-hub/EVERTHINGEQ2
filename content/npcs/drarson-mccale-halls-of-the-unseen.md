## Notes

Drarson is first encountered in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] standing behind the docks at {{waypoint 658, 24, 621}} while on the quest for the [[Mysterious Mark]]. After speaking to Drarson and moving to the next step of the quest, he will then appear inside the [[Halls of the Unseen]] at his new location at {{waypoint 35, 0, 3}}. Later on in the [[Dark Mail Gauntlets Timeline]], you will face Drarson as an 84 ^^^ Heroic mob, as you fight to take over in [[Halls of the Unseen: The Takeover]].
