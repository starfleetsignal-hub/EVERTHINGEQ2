## Notes

- The wikia mapref location has been bugged and led off into walls, or the opposite direction, but it was updated 12/25/23 and should work now. The right location is ( 333.2, -23.78, -1510.67)
- In 2015, only those who purchased the [[Terrors of Thalumbra patch notes|Terrors of Thalumbra expansion]] could get quests from this NPC, as the storyline revolves around the city of [[Maldura]], which was added with that expansion.
- This NPC may or may not be a fae; he looks like a fae from distance, but up close he looks more like the inhabitants of those in the Maldura region.
- This NPC is close to the Workbenches in the zone and he is quite small, so look closely!
