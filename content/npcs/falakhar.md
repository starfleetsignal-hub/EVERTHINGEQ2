## Notes

This NPC sells armour in exchange for [[Mark of Manaar|Marks of Manaar]] (gained through the [[Mark of Manaar Mission System]]).

The only combat-effective armor sold is for the feet, shoulders, and forearms slots, and the price ranges from 15 to 25 [[Mark of Manaar|Marks of Manaar]]. Appearance-only armour is sold for the head, chest, legs (except cloth armor), and hands slots at a price of 10 [[Mark of Manaar|Marks of Manaar]] per piece.
