## Notes

Grabby Gigglegibber only appears during the annual [[Nights of the Dead]] festival. He will exchange [[Nights of the Dead#Crafting Materials|treats]] dropped by monsters during the festival for [[Candy Corn]]

[[Candy Corn]] can be used as a holiday currency while shopping at [[A Nights of the Dead merchant|Nights of the Dead merchants]] for special holiday items.

Candy Corn can also be used as a crafting material when making items from the various Nights of the Dead [[Crafting|crafting]] recipes, like [[Eerie Skull Candle]].
