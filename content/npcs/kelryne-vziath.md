If you ask him why he is here, then inquire about what career he wants to move into, then ask what his specialty is, he will confide he is best known for his [[Kelryne's Recipe for Chocolate Chip Cookies of Seething Malice|Chocolate Chip Cookies of Seething Malice]]! Proceed all the way through the dialog as he tells you how to make them, and you will receive the recipe scroll!

He also tells you that all the ingredients you need can be bought at [[Cuisine Excelsior]] in the [[Fourth Gate]].
