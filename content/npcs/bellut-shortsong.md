## Description

Born and raised in Rivervale among the halfling folk, where he was known to be quite a percussionist. Made to leave after an "incident" involving a stolen pie... He fled to Freeport. He has since spent his time mingling among the other gnomes in Temple Street learning the "street" ways of surviving. Now he has the last laugh when they come asking him for his services as a mercenary!
