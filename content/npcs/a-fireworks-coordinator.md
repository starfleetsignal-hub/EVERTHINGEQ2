## Notes

This NPC is found in the major cities players can call home. To get the quest you must purchase [[Festive Sparkler]] for 10c from any of the [[A Sparkler Merchant]] **or** [[Ember Merchant Assistant]] found celebrating [[Scorched Sky]] every year in late June. It is not necessary to equip the sparkler to get quest, but you must have it in your inventory.

## Quests

- [[Festive Fireworks]] - 6 quests (1 for every major city) but the steps remain the same.
