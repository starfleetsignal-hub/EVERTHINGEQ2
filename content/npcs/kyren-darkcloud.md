## Description

So, there I was! surrounded by Blackwater pirates... Outnumbered and outmatched! Each enemy wanting my head on a platter... I reached into my satchel and threw down a smoke bomb and disappeared in a cloud of dark smoke. That was the intention at least. What actually happened was when I threw the smoke bomb, I slipped off the pier and woke up on the beach a few hours later. Nevertheless, the name "Darkcloud" stuck.
