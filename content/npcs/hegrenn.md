## Notes

Hegrenn was moved from her location in South Qeynos to this location during the LU64 Qeynos revamp.

- She also changed professions from blacksmith to furniture merchant.

### Fun facts

- Many items unlocked sold by this NPC were original to the game when housing was in its infancy, removed for many years, and returned with the revamp.
- Some items were also once associated only with various racial quests (e.g. a [[A Bouquet of Woodsun Flowers]]).
- Many of the paintings depict Qeynos in either it's *before* or *after* revamp states. For example, one shows [[Painting: Baubbleshire|The Baubbleshire]], which was once a housing location before the revamp and two others, the gates of Qeynos before the revamp in one image, and the new look in a second image. Most *before* images have the word, remembered, in the titles.
- Some items are also sold near [[Standard Housing|standard player housing]] (as of 2016), while others were once retired but returned from the former merchants that existed only when the old "hovels" or "suburb" zones had player housing, like [[Castleview Hamlet]].
