## Notes

Roslyn Mischeva  is an NPC that offers [[The Play's the Thing]] quest as apart of the  [[Heroes' Festival|Heroes' Festival Timeline]].  She is only available during the Heroes Festival.  After the world event ends, this NPC despawns and will not respawn again until the next Heroes Festival.

**Locations**
It's far easier to hail her and take the quest near the event hubs

- West Freeport: Standing North of Seafarer's Roost {{waypoint 59, -19, 84}}  (After accepting her quest, she disappears from this location)
- Qeynos: near the [[The Claymore Plaza]] near the vicinity of {{waypoint 297, -15, -21}} (After accepting her quest, she disappears from this location)

You can also hail her at the locations with the monsters that you must battle for the quest and achievements.
