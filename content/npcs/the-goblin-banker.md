## Directions

1. Go past [[The Muddite Pool]].
1. Left and down the spiral path.
1. Left at the **Y** were the [[Evil Eye]] mobs spawn.
1. Follow winding path to a room with two apposing archways ([[Riklin the Forgemaster]] also spawns in this room), take the archway on the left.
