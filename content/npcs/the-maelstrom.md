## Notes

- When you click on The Malestrom, you'll be asked "Are you prepared to weather the storm?" If you answer "Yes", a ring event will begin with a series of elementals being summoned. The event concludes with [[Magnimaclus]] spawning. If you answer "No", nothing happens.
- During the event, the door to the room will lock. After you have defeated [[Magnimaclus]], click on the sword again to get the message "The power of the storm has subsided" and the door can be opened again.
- If the event is on cooldown, the sword will say, "The power of the storm has subsided".
- **Known Bug**: The mobs associated with the ring event can sometimes spawn beneath the ground, above the ceiling, or outside the walls, putting them out of range of attacks (even AoE). If this happens then you must wait until the event can be started again. The event respawn is approximately 40 minutes.
