## Summer Ethereal Items are here!

During the event players will be able to pick up Ethereal missions from the mission NPC’s in Thalumbra, the Ever Deep. These missions will grant you [[Umbrite Ethereal Coin|Umbrite Ethereal Coins]], and a chance at the current accessory reward or an Ethereal Rune.

[[Umbrite Ethereal Coin|Umbrite Ethereal Coins]] may be used to purchase any of the available rewards from the Llyleia, the Ethereal Merchant.
