## Dialogue

When talking to Trainer Gurga Trueheart:

- Trainer Gurga Trueheart: "Hello there, [Player Character]. You look like you keep both your body and mind in good shape. Even so, I bet you could use more information on your physical attributes: Strength, Agility, Stamina, Intelligence, and Wisdom. That's what I'm here to teach you."
- Player Character: "Tell me what my physical attributes do."
- Trainer Gurga Trueheart: "Strength determines how much weight you can carry. The more Strength you have, the more physical damage you can do in melee combat. Strength also determines the maximum amount of power Guardians, Berserkers, Monks, and Paladins have."
- Player Character: "Tell me about Agility."
- Trainer Gurga Trueheart: "Agility helps you avoid physical attacks. The more Agility you have, the higher your Avoidance ability. Agility also determines the maximum amount of power Swashbucklers, Rangers, Troubadors, and Dirges have."
- Player Character: "Tell me about Stamina."
- Trainer Gurga Trueheart: "Stamina gives you more Health points. The more Health you have, the longer you can survive in battle. Stamina is a useful attribute for every class."
- Player Character: "Tell me about Intelligence."
- Trainer Gurga Trueheart: "Intelligence is more than just a sign of how smart you are. The more Intelligence you have, the more damage you do with spells in combat. Intelligence also determines the maximum amount of power Wizards, Warlocks, Illusionists, Conjurors, Troubadors, and Dirges have."
- Player Character: "Tell me about Wisdom."
- Trainer Gurga Trueheart: "Wisdom increases your resistances to magical types of attacks: Heat, Cold, Disease, Poison, Magic, Mental and Divine. Wisdom also determines the maximum amount of power Templars, Mystics, Furies, Wardens, and Paladins have."
- Player Character: "How do I view my physical attributes?"
- Trainer Gurga Trueheart: "You can see your physical attributes, as well as other important aspects of your character, in your persona window. Just press the P key to open your persona window."
- Player Character: "How do I improve my physical attributes?"
- Trainer Gurga Trueheart: "Your physical attributes, as well as other aspects of your character can be improved in a number of ways. Many items add points to your attributes, resistances, Health, or Power. Hold your mouse pointer over your items to see what benefits they provide."
- Player Character: "Are there other ways?"
- Trainer Gurga Trueheart: "Many spells and combat arts enhance your attributes. Some of these can be cast on yourself, while others can improve other player characters that you group with. Grouping with other classes provides a wider variety of ways to improve your ability."
- Player Character: "I will ask you about this later."
