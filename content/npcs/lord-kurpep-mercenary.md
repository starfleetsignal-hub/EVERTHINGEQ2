## Abilities/spells

- Feral Defense VII (Master) - Grants the berserker spell resistance, and a chance to counter attack with increased hate towards any opponent that damages the beserker in melee.
- Gorir Rage VIII (Master) - Builds rage within the berserker, granting a chance at becoming berserk while in combat. While berserk, attack speed and Damage Per Second is increased.
- Rage of the Lost VI (Master) - When the berserker receives melee damage in combat they have a chance to be healed for a small amount.
- Return of the Lost VI (Master) - Increase the Damage Per Second of all the Berserker's allies.
- War Growl VI (Master) - Grants a chance for the berserker's group to berserk in combat for a short duration.
- Total Domination - Threat management
- Rending Claws - Melee damage
- Bathe in Blood - Knockdown/melee damage
- Frenzy of the Lost - Melee damage x3
- Feral Mauling - Melee damage/Debuff
- Intercept - Threat management
- Enrage - Threat management
- Mock - Interrupt

## Description

This form is not the actual Shin Lord, it is an echo, a memory, that has its own designs.

## Notes

- This treasured Berserker Mercenary is available for hire in the [[Moors of Ykesha]].  Hiring fee is free.  Salary is 4p 50g every 30 minutes. [[Mercenary Battalion]]
