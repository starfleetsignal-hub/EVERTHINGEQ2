## Description

When you see this mercenary *before* you hire them, they will look like a nebulous spirit. Once hired, this mercenary will be your character's [**doppelgänger**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doppelg%C3%A4nger).

(All abilities/spells are at **Master**)
