## Notes

- Players formerly had to have one unit each of [[Cauldron blowfish]] and [[Thicket crayfish]] in order to open dialogue with him, however this is no longer necessary. His role in the collection quest, [[The Lore of Fauna: The Behemoth]], has been nullified.
- Jeroel is also a collection collector. Make sure you have your 'Amount of Experience' slider bar designated to how much XP or AA you want before you turn in The Lore of Fauna: The Behemoth. He will cash in ALL your ready-to-be-turned-in collections quests!
