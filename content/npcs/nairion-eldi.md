## Notes

First appears [[Goldami Reservoir Camp]] as part of the adventure party with [[Firiona Vie]] as a representative of the Chosen of Growth. He travels to Buried Takish'Hiz, where he's kidnapped and must be rescued, and appears later in [[Takish Badlands]] at [[Madman's Rest]] {{waypoint 630.63, 155.29, -727.98}}. He relocates to [[Hopewell Harbor]] {{waypoint 176.75, -60.15, 459.95}} for the conclusion of the story.
