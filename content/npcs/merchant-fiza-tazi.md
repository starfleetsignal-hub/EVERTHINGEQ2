Turn in sets of fertilizer to this merchant to receive a random collectible from Renewal of Ro expansion. Each "set" requires:

- one [[Chest of scorpion exoskeletons]]
- one [[Desert charger horns]]
- one [[Red desert sand]]

There is a 20% chance of receiving a [[Manaspark Lotus Flower]] instead of a collectible.
