## Note

If your doing Fighter Epic 2.0 and need to get this guy to spawn start doing the quests at [[Ry'Gorr Keep]]. Starting with [[Orolr Throatgrinder]] until you can do the quests for [[Tragg Sharptongue]].  You no longer need a full 50K Ry'Gorr faction, but you'll need to do enough of the Eastern Waste timeline to spawn the Oracle at the mountain camp.

## Steps

1. Starting with [[Orolr Throatgrinder]]
1. Keep doing quests and advance the timeline.
1. Talk with [[Tragg Sharptongue]] and do his quests until [[Rise of Thrael'Gorr]].  The Grand Oracle Hoop'Ak will show up now in the mountain camp in Eastern Wastes at ( 344, -63, 2658 ) .
