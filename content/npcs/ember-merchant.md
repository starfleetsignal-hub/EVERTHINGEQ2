## Notes

Items available for purchase during Scorched Sky event are split between two merchants:

- [[Ember Merchant]] who sells only new items introduced in the current year
- [[A Devotee of Flame (Ember Merchant Assistant)|Ember Merchant Assistant]] who sells items from previous years, also [[Festive Sparkler]] needed for [[Festive Fireworks]] quests.

The merchants will have a slightly different appearance depending on the location, but will be among a group of Devotee's of Flame that are casting spells to set off a fireworks effect.

[[Coin of Ember]] currency needed to buy most of the items from the [[Scorched Sky]] event can be obtained from flame-themed enemies (all elementals) near the Devotee's of Flame celebration sites and from event quests.
