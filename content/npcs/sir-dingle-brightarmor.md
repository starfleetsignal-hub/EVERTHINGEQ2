## Rarity

All mercenaries from [[Mercenary Crates|mercenary crates]] are granted randomly. Rarity describes how likely or unlikely a player is to get this mercenary from a crate and, in turn, how much they will sell to [[Sneerio Backlash]] for [[Status points|status points]].

- Sir Dingle Brightarmor is common and sells for 1,000,000 status.

## Notes

- This mercenary was added in 2016 and is one of several crusaders. Except for the ultra-rare (celestial) mercenary, [[Calyptor Athta, Sentinel of Dawn]], who is a guardian, all of the crusaders mercenaries are paladins.
