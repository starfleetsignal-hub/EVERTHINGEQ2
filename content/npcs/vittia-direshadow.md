## Description

Once a Teir'Dal striving to be a member of the elite shadowguard within Neriak, Vittia is now driven by the heat of battle. Her bloodlust keeps her fierce in combat and strong against her foes. This has shown to be a powerful skill for a mercenary to possess and she is taking full advantage of it.
