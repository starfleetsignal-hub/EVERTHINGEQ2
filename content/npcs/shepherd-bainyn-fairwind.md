## Notes

During the events of [[LU48]], the Shepherd was located outside the Temple. Once the details of the [[Seeress Ealaynya Ithis]]'s death were revealed, the Shepherd and several others took up residence back inside the Temple.  He is now the current [[Prophet Bainyn Fairwind|Prophet of Rodcet Nife]].
