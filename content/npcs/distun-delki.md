## Notes

Since 2026 this version of Distun in [[The Chronoweave]] sells **only older** items. (For NEW items see [[Erben Delki]]) nearby.<br>
After the event ends this Chronoportals merchant moves outside to [[Antonica]] for limited time. During that time he will sell **ALL** Chronoportal merchandise, not just the old items.

## Merchandise

- The NPCs only sell items that can be purchased with the event currency, [[Ancient Platinum Coin|Ancient Platinum Coins]].
- Some items are books with lore, some are "plushies" that look like miniature monsters (MOBs) one fights in battle, some are other house  items.
  - The paintings hail the game's historic connection to the original Everquest (EQ) game and, as such, have a look that mimics the graphics of the older game in most cases.
- This merchant also sells recipes - for year round crafting of the lesser versions of event plumes for your equipment's event slot.
