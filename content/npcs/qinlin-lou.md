## Description

A gentle soul who sells his protection to benefit his family back in the Hua Mein village. Qinlin is a master of the Seven Winds style, an ancient Hua Mein art of fighting. Few are more formidable.

## Rarity

All mercenaries from [[Mercenary Crates|mercenary crates]] are granted randomly. Rarity describes how likely or unlikely a player is to get this mercenary from a crate and, in turn, how much they will sell to [[Sneerio Backlash]] for [[Status points|status points]].

- Qinlin Lou is uncommon and sells for 3,000,000 status.
