New Halas is populated with many ecology NPCs, all either Barbarians or Coldain dwarves.  Unlike in older content, these NPCs are completely un-interactible. Furthermore, their names change with every game reset, although their behavior remains static.  For example there will always be 3 coldain performing ice magic at {{waypoint -124, 147, -70}}, but their names will keep changing.

Names appear to be generated out of predefined parts-of-names.  Barbarian NPCs will often have vaguely scottish or celtic sounding names, while Coldain NPCs often have stereotypically dwarflike descriptive last names (e.g. Coldfoot, Thundermaul, Firebeard).

For a partial list of possible names, see Whatlinkshere/Frostfang Sea ecology NPCs.
