## Notes

- Heartgar MacInnes appears during the [[Erollisi Day]] festival and allows players to exchange [[Erollisi Day Hard Candy and Love Notes]] for [[Barbarian love note|barbarian love notes]]. This allows players to keep their inventory from becoming cluttered with a multitude of various hard candies.
- [[Liannya Heartswell]] is another Erollisi Day NPC that exchanges Erollisi Day Hard Candy and Love Notes, but for for [[Erollisi coin|Erollisi coins]].
