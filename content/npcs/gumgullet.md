## Notes

- Gumgullet is primary vendor in Splitpaw Den. He also functions as a mender.
- In addition to selling basic food and water, he also sells the following special items (but only to those that have earned the trust of the Splitpaw Gnolls!):
  - [[Word of Force]] - Mage spell scroll
  - [[Bounty of the Virtuous]] - Priest spell scroll
  - [[Goading Gesture]] - Fighter combat art distillation
  - [[Trick of the Hunter]] - Scout combat art rune
  - [[A glowing azure shard]] - in case you lose yours somewhere
