### Lore

Vim is a merchant aboard [[The Far Journey]]. It can be inferred from your conversation with [[Ingrid (The Far Journey)|Ingrid]] that she was one of several adventurers rescued along with yourself, [[Geredo (The Far Journey)|Geredo]], [[Valik]] and [[Anikra]]. She is in possesion of a rare moon rock ([[Shard of Luclin]]) and does not seem shy about using the opportunity onboard to sell her wares.
