Formerly known as [[Lyssia Delnara]], during the LU64 Qeynos revamp Lysia moved from the Willow Wood to the Elddar Grove.  She also married [[Milo Faren]] and adopted his last name.

## Notes

- Lyssia won't appear unless a player has completed the (Half Elf only) quest [[Fire and Bite]]
