Ecology NPCs added for [[Nights of the Dead]] 2008 were puns of [[Nights of the Dead At The Movies|scary movie characters]], and spawned in random locations throughout the starting cities.  This one was a spoof of **Shaun** of "Shaun of the Dead" (romzomcom stands for **rom**antic **com**edy with **zom**bies).
