## Dialogue

- Trainer Leianna Teiampa: "Even the bravest defender of Queen Amree will meet an untimely end on occasion. Let me teach you the consequences of death and how you can recover properly.
- Player Character: "What can cause my death?"
- Trainer Leianna Teiampa: "Death can come in many ways: being bested on the field of battle, falling from great heights, drowning in water, falling into lava, standing too close to an exploding chest, and so on. It's a dangerous world out there."
- Player Character: "How will I know when I am going to die?"
- Trainer Leianna Teiampa: "Just keep an eye on your health bar, the green line located beneath your name in the upper left of your screen. At least...you hope it's green."
- Player Character: "Why green?"
- Trainer Leianna Teiampa: "Green means you're healthy. As your health diminishes, the bar turns yellow, then orange, and finally red. If the bar is empty, you die. You pay the debt, which cancels all others, if you get my meaning."
- Player Character: "What happens then?"
- Trainer Leianna Teiampa: "Your spirit lays trapped in your fallen body, unable to move. At this point, you have a choice: wait for a nearby ally to revive you, or choose a location from the window in the center of your screen at which to reappear. In some cases, you'll have a single choice, in others multiple choices."
- Player Character: "So death is not the end?"
- Trainer Leianna Teiampa: "Fortunately for all of us, death in Norrath is a temporary thing. If another adventurer revives you, you reappear at the spot of your death. If you choose a location to revive, you will appear at that spot. There are consequences to death, however."
- Player Character: "What are the consequences?"
- Trainer Leianna Teiampa: "There are three results of death. First, you incur some experience debt which can be paid off by earning more XP (experience points). Second, you suffer a revive sickness that lowers your abilities for a short time, which leaves you temporarily vulnerable and less effective. Finally, all your equipped items will take damage that will eventually have to be repaired."
- Player Character: "What is experience debt?"
- Trainer Leianna Teiampa: "Experience debt is indicated by a red line on your XP bar. As you earn experience going forward, part of your XP will go toward paying off your debt while the rest will continue to advance you toward your next level. Mousing over your XP bar also tells how much debt you have."
- Player Character: "What is revive sickness like?"
- Trainer Leianna Teiampa: "You'll see an icon for revive sickness in your spell effects window. Mousing over the icon will tell you how long it will remain. The type of sickness you have depends on how you were revived, but no matter what there will be a negative effect. Don't worry, it passes soon enough."
- Player Character: "What are the results of item damage?"
- Trainer Leianna Teiampa: "Your equipped items usually take about 10% wear with each death. When they are down to zero durability, you no longer receive any benefit from them. don't let your equipment ever get into such poor condition, [Player Character]!"
- Player Character: "What can I do about item damage?"
- Trainer Leianna Teiampa: "You will need to see a mender, someone who is able to repair your items back to 100% durability. Speak to Trainer Kaali to learn more about repairing your gear. Should your equipment take damage, Mender Kaarlo at Gearheart's Forge in Kelethin can repair things for you."
- Player Character: "Thank you for your advice."
