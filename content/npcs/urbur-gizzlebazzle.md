## Notes

Urbur Gizzlebazzle is actually part of a small ring event.  He will be surrounded by four [[An extractor of truth|extractors of truth]] (29^^-30^^ assassins).  When they're all dead, he'll become hailable.  Once he's hailed, [[Vendra T'Nath]] will spawn as an NPC, kill Urbur, and then become agro.
