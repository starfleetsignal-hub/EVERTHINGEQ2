## Locations

1. He first appears at the mouth of the [[Crusader's Cave]] for the quest [[Kunark Ascending: Ghost Whisperer]] {{waypoint 701.94, 222.03, 903.95}}.
1. After your first conversation he moves to a nearby lavapool {{waypoint 465.87, 136.01, 724.13}}.
1. After your last conversation during [[Kunark Ascending: Ghost Whisperer|Ghost Whisperer]] he moves yet again to his final location {{waypoint 93.36, -208.15, -462.30}} at the bridge over [[Warslik's River]] leading towards [[Twark]]. Here he'll then also offer the quest [[A Vicious Tongue]].
