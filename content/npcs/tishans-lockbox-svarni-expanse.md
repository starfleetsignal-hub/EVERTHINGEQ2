## Notes

- Provides a complete set of free level 120 equipment based on class.
- Provides a free flying mount, the [[Displaced Harbinger]].
- You can obtain an additional set of armor for your mercenary.
- The gear has 275 resolve, slightly more stamina than 275 gear from [[Reign of Shadows]], but 2-3k less potency than equivalent RoS gear.
- All equipment has one or more adornments attached to it. These adornments are not visible until the item is purchased.
