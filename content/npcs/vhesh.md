## Related Quests

- This NPC will have a green feather and moving toward him will update the tradeskill quest, [[Into The Spire]], which is required to complete that quest and start the Kunark series of the tradeskill timeline. If the green feather does not appear, return to [[Arcanna'se Spire: Into The Spire (Tradeskill)]] and have the Queen send you again.
- This NPC starts the Tradeskill timeline with [[Not Dead Yet]] and offers several quests in the [[Kunark Ascending Crafting Timeline]].
- The other goblins in the regions above will attack until you complete the initial quest, so tread carefully in the region above him.
