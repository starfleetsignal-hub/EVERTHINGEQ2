## Notes

- To quickly locate Santa Glug look for throngs of players on one island, getting their daily [[Frostfell Gifts|Frostfell gifts]]!
- Each character can get one gift from Santa Glug every 18 real-world hours. Each year new gifts are added and some cycled out. Gifts can be non-combat pets, appearance gear, house items, and house pets.

## Dropped gifts to pick up

![one of presents dropped by Santa Glug in Freeport or Qeynos Capitol](images/One_of_presents_dropped_by_Santa_Glug.png)
![Santa Glug running through Qeynos dropping gifts](images/Santa_Glug_in_Qeynos.png)

1. [[Qeynos Capitol District]] and [[Freeport]] have a visiting Santa Glug running around dropping presents about every 2 - 2.5 real time hours.
   - This is announced by audible sleigh bells ringing and a message in chat *"The ringing of sleigh bells fills the air. Are you listening?"*
   - He drops 10 presents in randomized locations and anyone passing by can pick them up.
   - The gifts show on track as **To: You**. Asking a city guard to find NPC named **To: You** will create a wisp to a nearest gift.
   - picking up a present named To: You <From: Santa Glug> gives a random item. Possible items are:
     - [[A cask of Santa's Secret Cider]]
       - opens into 5 x [[Santa's Secret Cider]] - putting those in drink slot and letting them slowly **auto-consume** gives a title when you drink the 5th one.
     - [[A sack of pre-diamonds]]
       - opens into [[Sack of Coal]] house item plus Suffix Title: [[The Naughty|, the Naughty]] or Suffix Title: [[Says bah, humbug!|, says bah, humbug!]] if you don't have those titles yet.
     - [[Blizzard Blue Gumdrop (House Item)]]
     - [[Bucket of Icy Snowballs]] - interactive house item
     - [[Frostfell Snow Cloud (Mount)]]
     - [[Large Snow Man]] - house item
     - [[Large Snow Woman]] - house item
     - [[Red Festive Antlers]] - appearance head gear
     - [[Red-Striped Sentient Present]] - vanity pet scroll
     - [[Shroud of Holiday Gloom]] - cloak
     - [[Snowflake Sentient Present]]
     - [[Tiny Frostfell Dragon]] - vanity pet scroll
     - [[Traditional Holiday Mantle]] - cloak
     - pieces for [[Frostfell Hot Drinks]] collection
     - pieces for [[Frostfell Sweets]] collection

1. Players can buy a [[Gifty Bag]] from Marketplace for 300DB and open it by Santa sleigh in [[Qeynos Capitol District]] or [[Freeport]]. This results in a reward [[The Spirit of Giving]] for the player  who opened the bag and a separate Santa Glug's Lil' Helper who runs around the city and drops 10 presents for others to pick up. Those presents will have a name of a player who opened the [[Gifty Bag]] in their name ( Xxxx's Generous Gift).
