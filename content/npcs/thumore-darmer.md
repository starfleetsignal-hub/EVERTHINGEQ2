## Location

- Prior to completing [[Shattered Remains]], Thumore's sleeping body can be found in [[North Qeynos]] at {{waypoint 410, -21, -189}} or in [[North Freeport]] at {{waypoint -73, -21, -76}}
- During the quests [[Thumore's Absence]] and [[Shattered Remains]], you can interact with the NPC Thumore inside [[A Collection of Dreams]]
- After completing [[Shattered Remains]], NPC Thumore can be found in [[North Qeynos]] at {{waypoint 308, -22, -98}} or [[North Freeport]] at {{waypoint -67, -24, -42}}
