## Background

Gixen only appears during the [[Frostfell]] holiday, which is held approximately mid December through early January each year. He will trade players [[Frozen Token of E'ci]] for leftover items picked up in [[Gigglegibber Gifty Storehouse]] during the repeatable quest [[Raiding the Gifty Storehouse]]. The names of the items are:

- [[Green Gifty Box]]
- [[Red Gifty Box]]
- [[Clockwonky Toy Gnome]]
- [[Clockwonky Toy Monster]]

## Gift Conversion

He will accept trades in the amounts of of 1 Frozen Token for 2 gifts, or 10 Frozen Tokens for 20 gifts. You get approximately 34-37 gifts per run, which is reliably 17-19 tokens. You do NOT get double tokens for members.
