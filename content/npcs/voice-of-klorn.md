## Notes

- This NPC is surrounded by small ghosts with blank (no character) names. They do, however, appear to follow a track. The NPC talks to the player as they approach it:
  - "Let Anger be your guide and you shall serve Innoruuk with all your Hate!"
  - "Search your feelings! You know you want nothing but to be despised!"
  - "In this realm, to Hate is to embrace everything that is of Innoruuk."
  - "Let your soul fill with rage and feel the power of malice like you never thought possible."
