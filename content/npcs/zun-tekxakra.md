## Description

- Once subjugated as the other Akhevans,Zun awoke from her spell with the desire of vengeance in her songs.Her only desire is to deliver death on those who held her mind captive for so long.

- Dance of Arrogance V (Master): Group(AE) Increase Combat Health Regen. Increase Max Health (AE)
- Dance of Four Blades VII(Master): Self. Increase AGI. Adds 25% base avoidance.Increase Mitigation vs elemental,noxious and arcane
- Luclin's Serenade IV(Master): Group(AE) Decrease Hate Gain 41.7 if not fighter. When damaged Decrease Threat to target if not fighter
- Songs of Shadow VI(Master): On hostile spell hit 30% inflicts x mental damage
- Zun Tek'Xakra's Allegretto VII(Master): Group(AE) Increase Hast
- Dutiful Avoidance VI(Master): Group(AE) Increase Defense of X
- Selo's Accelerando VI: Group(AE) increase speed 46%
- Painful Lamentations: Interrupts target encounter.Inflicts X mental damage
- Thunderous Overture: Max AOE 12,Radius 15m, inflicts X mental damage
- Chaos Anthem: Dot enemy every 6.Decrease WIS by X
- Zun Tek'Xakra's Choral Rebuff: Decrease enemy Mitigation vs elemental,noxious and arcane
- Jealousy of Luclin: Group(AE) Increase INT x. On a hostile spell damage enemy
- Gift of Shadows: Raid or Group Friend. Increase Reuse Speed of target by 40%
- Ceremonial Blade: Mental damage enemy
