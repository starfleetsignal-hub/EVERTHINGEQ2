## Description

Denva has been a bit of an outcast when it comes to fitting in with the rest of the Gorowyn populace. He does not believe that one must be overly loyal to the city, but instead, that the city be loyal to their citizens. Because of these beliefs, he has turned to helping adventurers in battle in order to make this way in the world.
