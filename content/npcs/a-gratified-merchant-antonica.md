**Gratified merchants** spawn in those locations where [[A distressed merchant|distressed merchants]] have been successfully rescued from the monsters that surround them. Gratified merchants appear in semi-random locations (again, only after you've rescued them) throughout the zone. In Antonica, these locations typically involve groups of gnolls and sometimes their tents. For more information on spawning gratified merchants, read the information about their placeholders, [[A distressed merchant|distressed merchants]] for the appropriate zone.
