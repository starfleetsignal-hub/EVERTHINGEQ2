## Description

*A member of the ensorceled glaufaye, Delidrae has had eons within Thalumbra to hone her fighting skills and augment them with arcane arts. She longs to face new foes and face the challenge of a battle, once more!*

## Abilities / Spells

- **Mercenaries (sic) Resolve** (this ability mistakenly uses a plural instead of a possessive in the name; in game /bug submitted) - self-target buff
- **[[Battery and Assault VIII]] (Master)** - damage, mele
- **[[Desperate Thrust IV]] (Master)** damage, debuff
- **[[Umbral Splash]] (Master)** debuff
- **[[Cloaked Assault]]** - damage, passive group heal
- **[[Immortalis]]** - damage, passive group heal
- **[[Thalumbral Rake VI]]** - damage, passive group heal
- **[[Force of the Glaufaye]] (Master)** damage, group heal, AOE
- **[[Touch of the Glaufaye]] (Master)** heal

## Notes

Available as a /claim item only for those who have purchased the Collector's Edition or Premium Edition of the Terrors of Thalumbra expansion.

- This mercenary uses Wizard-like and Brigand-like abilities as well as having some healing potential.
- Delidrae will appear in the Mercenary Den of each city **only after** you activate the token in your inventory that appears upon claiming it.
- **Alternate location:** {{waypoint -474, 245, 345}} in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]], near the glaufaye NPCs for the signature tradeskill quests associated [[Terrors of Thalumbra Crafting Timeline]].
