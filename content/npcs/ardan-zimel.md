## Lore

Ardan Zimel is a descendent of [[Ariska Zimel]]. His family departed from [[Freeport]] during the Age of Turmoil, attempting to escape judgement for a crime against the state.(Source: Quest dialog from Sir Trondonis during the quest [[The Stone Behind the Ankh]])

The Zimels were a fine family of smithies until one of them got the hunger for blood. Ardan Zimel lost his soul to the demons. He became a vampire and the first to find this out were his unfortunate family. (Source: Quest dialog from Horatio Mercuria during the quest [[The Stone Behind the Ankh]])

Ardan Zimel now wishes to live in peace amongst his new family of Mistmoore. (Source: Dialog from Ardan Zimel during the quest [[The Stone Behind the Ankh]])
