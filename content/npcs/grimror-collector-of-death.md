## Description

- A powerful defiler who has knowledge of some unknown magics. He is obsessed with death and decay.
- Same one in [[Plane of Disease: Infested Mesa (Event Heroic)|Plane of Disease: Infested Mesa]] final named

## Abilities / Spells

All spells are Master level. He doesn't have any death prevention but work harder than [[Kenny]]

- Gift of Malic: Increase damage of spell 5%. On a hostile spell cast heals caster for X. Trigger off disease,poison,magic,divine,mental abilities
- [[Noxious Efflux]]: Increase Max Health instead of STA
- [[Shroud of Armor]]
- [[Vehemence]]
- Bolstering Malice: Group(AE) Increases Al Attributes 32.8%
- Foreboding Sloth: Damage enemy. Decrease enemy Haste X
- [[Wild Accretion]] : Add Increase group Max Health(AE)
- Preemptive Protection: Wards (AE) against noxious damage for 80% - 240% target's max hp. Must not be in combat
- Sphere of Malice: Group(AE) Wards against X of all damage. Increase Mitigation (AE) vs elemental damage
- [[Carrion Warding]]
- [[Reanimate]]
- [[Cure]]
- [[Dire Balm]] : Add Increase Max Health of target X
- [[Ancient Shroud]]
