## Trivia and lore

[[Rikantus Everling]] is a divided soul. He exists in game as the ghost of Nektropos, but parts of his soul also exist as [[Rikantus]] (who lives at the cemetery in [[Antonica]]) and [[Antiquitor Kantus Mor'Tael]] (who lives in [[Maj'Dul]]). Once you have completed [[Nektropos Castle: Tribulation]], Antiquitor Kantus will have some new things to say to you.
