## Related Quests

This NPC is a vendor inside of the [[Frostfell Decoration Committee: Permafrost!]] version of [[The Icy Keep]]. In addition to the Frostfell fuels, he also sells the recipes needed to craft a key and loot the year-round Frostfell stations
The recipes:

- [[A Merry Key (Recipe)]]
- [[A Cheery Key (Recipe)]]
- [[A Festive Key (Recipe)]]

See the [[Frostfell Crafting Stations]] page for details on how the keys are looted and who can make them.

**Additional Merchants:**

- [[Gerbi Frostfoot (Frostfell)|Gerbi Frostfoot]] sells most of the "big" event items like the holiday themed mounts, ice-themed house items, all of the event armor and other gear.
- Two goblins in the zone also sell their own collection of "smaller" [[Frostfell Vendor Items]] like the tradeskill books, decorative gifts, and snowglobes.
