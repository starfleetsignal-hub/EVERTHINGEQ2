- Assigns quest [[Lands' Rend]]

- Accompanied by [[Apprentice Tavari]]

## Interaction

*You say, "Hail, Abdur Hamdaan"*
Abdur Hamdaan says to you, "Come to observe the anomaly? It's astounding."
*You say to Abdur Hamdaan, "Did you create it?"*
Abdur Hamdaan says to you, "Such power is beyond my reach, I assure you. No, I and Apprentice Tavari have come to investigate it, to learn of the magics that created it and possible intentions behind it. The last time something similar occurred, it was due to corruption of the elemental Planes of Power."
*You say to Abdur Hamdaan, "Similar, but not the same?"*
Abdur Hamdaan says to you, "Contaminated elementals spewed forth from those unstable rifts and raged against anything and anyone they found. This portal appears to be more stable, and nothing has emerged from it. Hmm... yet."
*You say to Abdur Hamdaan, "So then, why is it here? Who or what opened it?"*
Abdur Hamdaan says to you, "And where does it lead to? All good questions. But I fear if we stand around gawking too long, we will be caught unprepared when the answers reveal themselves. We need to act before the situation escalates."
*You say to Abdur Hamdaan, "But before we can act, we need to know what we are dealing with."*
Abdur Hamdaan says to you, "Then let us divide the tasks. I have a few spells Tavari and I can perform that should provide some insight. Meanwhile, I need some components that will allow us to nullify the magics of the anomaly and close the rift."
*You say to Abdur Hamdaan, "Are you sure? You don't know the magic that opened it, yet."*
Abdur Hamdaan says to you, "No, but I am confident I will have that answer by the time you return with the violet velium crystals and empyreal scintilla."
*You say to Abdur Hamdaan, "Velium crystals? Are they found in Velious?"*
Abdur Hamdaan says to you, "You won't have to go that far. The violet velium can be found in Frostfang Sea, while empyreal scintilla tends to congregate in The Village of Shin."
**Abdur offers quest [[Lands' Rend]]**

(If accepted) *You say to Abdur Hamdaan, "Then to Frostfang Sea and the Village of Shin, I go!"*
