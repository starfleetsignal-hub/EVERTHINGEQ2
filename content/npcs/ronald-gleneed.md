## Notes

This NPC is found on the Kael Drakkel server. He offers a selection of mounts, decorative house items, cosmetic armor and weapons, and potions which can be purchased using [[Lore and Legend Token|Lore and Legend Tokens]] awarded for completing achievements on the server.

Alternate versions of Ronald Gleneed can also be found in Qeynos and Freeport on the standard and Time-Locked Expansion (TLE) servers. There, he has a limited selection of items which can be purchased after completing all of the [[Legend Hunting]] quests on the Kael Drakkel server, including the [[Spirit of Drakkel]] green adornment.

On servers other than Kael Drakkel, you can find this NPC at the following locations:

- East Freeport {{waypoint -108, -38, 66}}
- Qeynos Harbor {{waypoint 905, -25, 78}}

You must have an [[All Access Membership]] to create a character on the Kael Drakkel server.
