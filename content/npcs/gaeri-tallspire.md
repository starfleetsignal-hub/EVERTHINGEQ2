## Description

Once a citizen of Paineel, Gaeri has done much research into the ulteran spires network. During this research she accidentally teleported a young boy into a hostile group of kobolds. She saved the boy but the ordeal changed her priorities and she now devotes her extra time into the protection of others. On the side she still dabbles in teleportation experiments and they are costly so she must charge for her protection services.
