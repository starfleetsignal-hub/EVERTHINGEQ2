## Lore

Druzzil Ro is the goddess of magic. Her relationship to Solusek Ro, who shares her name, is unclear. The Allyrian people native to the Plane of Magic are one of her creations.

Although she is the goddess of magic, there is magic older than her tenure. For example, the spell of undeath upon the glaufaye predates her. That particular magic was the result of a stave bequeathed by Godking Anuk.
