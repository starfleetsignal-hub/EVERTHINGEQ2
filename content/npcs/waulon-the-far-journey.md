### Lore

**Waulon** is First Mate of the ship [[The Far Journey]], working under the direction of [[Captain Varlos (The Far Journey)|Captain Varlos]]. His prized possession is his lucky hat, which he has misplaced and recruits you to find. Among the duties Waulon has on board, it is mentioned that he charts the course of the ship.
