## Description

Bordis was raised as a conjuror within the coldain ranks on the isle of Erollis, however he quickly saw that he was not to control elementals and instead dedicated his life toward learning about the magic behind the elementals and how to control it. He now calls himself an "Elementalist" instead of a conjuror and has become a very powerful elemental magic practitioner.
