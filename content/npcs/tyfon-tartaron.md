## Description

*"Tyfon Tartaron is one of the few surviving dracurions who were there the day Skyshrine fell to the Awakened. Many of the most important scholars who's (sic) jobs where (sic) the keeping of their history were spirited away for protection. Many healers and warriors were left behind to die."*

Note: the description above is as it appears in game, which includes an erroneous sentence fragment and the incorrect usage of the contraction, "who's", instead of the possessive, "whose".

## Rarity

All mercenaries from [[Mercenary Crates|mercenary crates]] are granted randomly. Rarity describes how likely or unlikely a player is to get this mercenary from a crate and, in turn, how much they will sell to [[Sneerio Backlash]] for [[Status points|status points]].
