## Description

Calyptor Atha is a champion of the Aviak kind. Atha heralds from the realm of dawn and has long taken it as his duty to protect those in need. There is no Aviak in the realms more powerful that Calyptor.

## Rarity

All mercenaries from [[Mercenary Crates|mercenary crates]] are granted randomly. Rarity describes how likely or unlikely a player is to get this mercenary from a crate and, in turn, how much they will sell to [[Sneerio Backlash]] for [[Status points|status points]].

- Calyptor Atha, Sentinel of Dawn is ultrarare and sells for 10,000,000 status.
