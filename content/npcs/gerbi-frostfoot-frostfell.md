## Notes

- Gerbi won't show you what he has to sell until you have completed a quest that takes you into the [[The Icy Keep]]. This can be [[Mission Improbable...]], or the tradeskill quest [[Frostfell Decoration Committee: Permafrost!]].
- If you are level 19 or below, he will only sell house items. Once you are level 20 and above, he will sell you armor, weapons, and other stat gear that is close to your level.

## Items

See the [[Frostfell Merchant Items (Gerbi)]] page for a list of items sold by this NPC.

## Additional Merchants

- Two goblins in the zone also sell their own collection of items like the tradeskill books, decorative gifts, and snowglobes. See the [[Frostfell Merchant Items]] page for a list of their items.
- [[Ginneldarf Gigglegibber]] can be found *only* inside the [[The Icy Keep]]. He primarily sells the event fuel, but also sells the keys needed to locate chests and craft the year-round [[Frostfell Crafting Stations]].
