## Description

- Doctor Arcana is something of a artifact seeker. He is an enemy of the sphinxes of the Pride Pakiat for that reason. He will stop at nothing to seek objects of power. He wields a powerful crossbow that fires arcane bolts, this is how he came to be known as Doctor Arcane. No one is sure if he is actually a doctor though.
