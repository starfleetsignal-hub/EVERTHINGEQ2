## Requirement

Only those who purchased the Collectors or Premium Edition of the Terrors of Thalumbra expansion can access this mercenary.

- If you purchased one of the upgrades, type /claim and look for the mercenary token that bears her name in the tab for Terrors of Thalumbra.

## Hiring Locations

- In Freeport, this mercenary is in the area *under* the back deck of the [[Seafarer's Roost]]. You must click on the door at the bottom of the stairs and enter the door labeled "Mercenaries for Hire!" to enter. Only after you have used the claimed token will you find her at {{waypoint -220, -273, -3}}.
- In Qeynos, mercenaries can be found in the bar area of [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn]]
