## Notes

This NPC is found in the major cities players can call home, near the [[A Fireworks Coordinator]] NPC. This appearance of this NPC varies by location, but they are all gnomes.

## Items

- This NPC sells the [[Festive Sparkler]] needed for the [[Festive Fireworks]] quests in all [[Player City|player cities]]. The Festive Sparkler must be purchased before you can get the quest.
- **Important:** this NPC sells *limited* of [[Scorched Sky Merchant Items]]. This NPC was designed to suit the limitations set for [[Server Types|TLE servers]]. Another event-NPC found in various locations, [[Ember Merchant]], sells *all* of the items (including the [[Festive Sparkler]]). Therefore, players on live servers will want to visit the Ember Merchant to spend [[Coin of Ember]].
