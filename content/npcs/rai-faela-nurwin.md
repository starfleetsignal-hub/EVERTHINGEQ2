With the advent of the ability to change tradeskill professions, some profession choices need a way to be re-done.

By talking to this npc, you can exchange your old Village of Shin crafting charm, for a new one for your class.

*Not sure if you just exchange it, or you turn your old one in, and get unflagged and have to do the quest for your new profession*
