## Notes

- Will only aggro when a person with the quest [[Freeport Bounty: Clint Gilcrush]] or the quest [[Qeynos Bounty: Clint Gilcrush]] talks to him.
  - When he becomes attackable, he is same level as the character, solo ^.
- This NPC is actually an in-game representation of Red Sox pitcher Curt Schilling. His name is actually just an anagram of Curt's name as well.
