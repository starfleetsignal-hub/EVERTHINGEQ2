## Selling

You have to attain the proper title in order to buy the matching boots - for example, having the *Hunter of Orcs* title allows you to buy the "... Boots of the Orc Hunter" but not the other two; if you have attained *Destroyer of Orcs* you can buy all three.

- Cloth
  - [[Silken Boots of the Orc Hunter]] for 52s
  - [[Silken Boots of the Orc Slayer]] for 2g 8s
  - [[Silken Boots of the Orc Destroyer]] for 13g 31s 20c

- Chain ([[Scout]] only)
  - [[Chained Boots of the Orc Hunter]] for 52s
  - [[Chained Boots of the Orc Slayer]] for 2g 8s
  - [[Chained Boots of the Orc Destroyer]] for 13g 31s 20c

- Leather
  - [[Hardened Leather Boots of the Orc Hunter]] for 52s
  - [[Hardened Leather Boots of the Orc Slayer]] for 2g 8s
  - [[Hardened Leather Boots of the Orc Destroyer]] for 13g 31s 20c

- Plate
  - [[Heavy Sabatons of the Orc Hunter]] for 52s
  - [[Heavy Sabatons of the Orc Slayer]] for 2g 8s
  - [[Heavy Sabatons of the Orc Destroyer]] for 13g 31s 20c
