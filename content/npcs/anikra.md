## Lore

**Anikra** is a recused passenger aboard [[The Far Journey]] similar to yourself. She mentions that her husband is missing after pirates threw him overboard (presumably how you and the other adventurers ended up in the water before the events of The Far Journey). She briefly exhibits an aptitude for magic when attacked by the escaped goblin aboard the ship.
