## Description

*"Once an Arch-Priestess of Zek, Klodae, along with her kin, were driven from the halls of Drunder after the death of Lady Vorticia V'uul. Cast out into the natural world of Norrath, only Klodae's resilience and strength of will has allowed her to survive, qualities her four sisters lacked"*.

## Rarity

All mercenaries from [[Mercenary Crates|mercenary crates]] are granted randomly. Rarity describes how likely or unlikely a player is to get this mercenary from a crate and, in turn, how much they will sell to [[Sneerio Backlash]] for [[Status points|status points]].

- Priestess Klodae Cer'Ule is uncommon and sells for 3,000,000 status.
