## Notes

- Kander was added to the game when [[Outpost of the Overlord]] and [[Queen's Colony]] were reactivated.  He appreciates heavy metal and nostalgia.
- There is also a rare mercenary version of Kander, [[Mindblaster Kander]], that players have a chance to get when opening [[Mercenary Crates|mercenary crates]].
- This NPC is named after one of the game developers (using his screen name) who is known for his love of heavy metal and his signature rock-star-style hair.
