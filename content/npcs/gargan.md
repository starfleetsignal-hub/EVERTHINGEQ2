## Info

He's a relic seeker who will reward you with +500 Faction for the City of Thurgadin in exchange for each [[Cloudy Velium Jewel]]. These Jewels can be earned by participating in the Ring War or the Storm Gorge events, or by doing Quests that you can also pick up at the Harbor somewhere.<br>
Once you have 40k Faction on one character you can also buy an Heirloom item to trade with your Alts, if you want them to get instant maximum Faction.
