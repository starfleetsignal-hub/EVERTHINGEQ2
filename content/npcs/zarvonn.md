Zarvonn appears at the the end of the progression through [[The Eternal Gorge]]. He activates [[Zarvonn's creation]] to become attackable, makes comments during the fight, and then runs off yelling threats when his creation loses the battle. He spawns and remains a non-attackable NPC character throughout his appearance.
