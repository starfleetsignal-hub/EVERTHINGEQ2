## Lore

**Geredo** is a rescued passenger aboard [[The Far Journey]]. Like many Gnomes of the region, he seems to have an interest in [[Tinkering]] and inventing. He mentions both mechanical leg extenders and an idea for a mechanized boat. By his own admission, he did not help rescue his fellow passengers on account of his inability to swim. He also has elbow pain which acts up in the presence of water.
