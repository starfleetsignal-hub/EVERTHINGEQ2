## Lore

If you speak with Daylia the Vigil before you begin her part of the City of Qeynos questline, she will explain to you why the Assassin class has become a necessary evil for the greater good of Qeynos. Qeynos has always had an intelligence network in its long history. The Bayle line of rulers recognize that certain political opponents may become an actual threat to Qeynos if given the chance to amass enough power, so it becomes the Eye of Bayle intelligence network's job to keep an eye on them, and if necessary, kill them before they can become an actual problem.

A comparative example in pop culture would be like James Bond working for MI6-- 007 does what is necessary for the greater good of England.
