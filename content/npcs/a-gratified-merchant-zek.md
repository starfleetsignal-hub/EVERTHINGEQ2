## Steps

1. Kill all mobs surrounding [[A Broken Skull]] near [[Deathfist Citadel]].
   - There are at least 3 locations:
     - {{waypoint -91, -36, -604}} - east of the Citadel
     - {{waypoint 169, -40, -582}} - to the southwest of the Citadel gate.
     - {{waypoint -69, -39, -799}} - to the northeast of the Citadel.
   - After each clearing, you'll get either a chest that gives a nice buff, a group of ^^ or higher mobs, or [[A distressed merchant]].  The skull will disappear and reappear a short time later.
1. When the distressed merchant appears, clear the area again and he'll turn into [[A gratified merchant]], who will sell you various items.
