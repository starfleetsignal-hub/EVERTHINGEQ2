## Description

Toggs the Enforcer was once a general in the Duhjalm armies that supported Kael Drakkel. The cruel ways of the storm giants resulted in the death of his son Morgo. Toggs left Kael Drakkel, never to return. Toggs now sells his powerful service to the highest bidder.

## Rarity

All mercenaries from [[Mercenary Crates|mercenary crates]] are granted randomly. Rarity describes how likely or unlikely a player is to get this mercenary from a crate and, in turn, how much they will sell to [[Sneerio Backlash]] for [[Status points|status points]].

- Toggs the Enforcer is uncommon and sells for 3,000,000 status.
