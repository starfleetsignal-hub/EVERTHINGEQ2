## General Info

This NPC is the quest-giver for year 2020 and [[Pas Yu‎]] is the merchant. In order to buy items from [[Pas Yu‎]] you must complete the quests in [[The "Travels" of Yun Zi Timeline]].

- Each quest unlocks new items to purchase for zero copper.
- You only need to complete the quests once per account to unlock the items for all of your characters.
