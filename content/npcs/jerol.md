If you go near him (about the distance from the door to the building with [[Koros Splinterlimb]]) and he says "<player>, come here!" that means he has a letter for you. Depending on certain conditions, he'll offer the following:

[[A letter with bottle attachment]] to begin [[Shipwrecked!]].<br>
[[Requesting your assistance once more]] to begin the [[Altar of Malice Crafting Timeline]].<br>
[[An Urgent Plea]] to begin the [[Kunark Ascending Crafting Timeline]].<br>
[[The Coalition of Tradesfolke requires your assistance]] (if evil aligned) or [[The Ironforge Exchange requests your assistance]] (if good aligned) for where to get the [[Tradeskill Apprentices|Tradeskill Apprentice]].<br>
[[Answer the Spirit]].<br>
[[Meeting in Freeport]] (if evil aligned) or [[Meeting in Qeynos]] (if good aligned) for starting [[The Fallen Swords Timeline]].<br>
[[Requested Presence]].<br>
[[Your Attention Requested]].

## Notes

- If you have accidentally deleted a quest [[The Norrathian Express|mail]] item, or if you never received a quest mail item at all, this NPC can provide them to you. Spam click him to receive all the outstanding mail items that you are supposed to have received.
  - When a new expansion is released, commonly a letter is sent to the player. Jerol can provide these letters.
  - If you delete the letters that you received from him, just click him again to receive new copies.
