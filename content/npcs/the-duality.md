## Notes

**The Duality** is *simultaneously* Dartain and Al'Kabor.

Dartain, recognizing that his power alone was insufficient to stop Odus from being torn between The Void and The Underfoot, sought to assume the spirit of Al'Kabor by blending erudite meditation techniques, Kerran spiritual shamanry, and a long-lost ritual of Cazic-Thule. He found Al'Kabor much stronger than expected, and instead of consuming his spirit, the two became The Duality.
