## Description

Captured from the Moors of Ykesha as a child, Maala has spent most of her time in behind bars in one way or another.  There are few mercenaries that are tougher than she is and even fewer more deadly.

## Rarity

All mercenaries from [[Mercenary Crates|mercenary crates]] are granted randomly. Rarity describes how likely or unlikely a player is to get this mercenary from a crate and, in turn, how much they will sell to [[Sneerio Backlash]] for [[Status points|status points]].

- Maala Sawtooth is common and sells for 1,000,000 status.
