## Notes

- Madria will offer her quests those of tradeskill level 50 and above.
- You no longer need to complete the [[Ship Out]] quest for her to offer you quests.
- She offers one of the quests listed below every hour; however you can only complete each one once per day.

All quests can be completed multiple times a day, except [[Shipyard Services]] which *still* has an **18 hour** reset timer, the other three have 90 minute resets (via Dungeon Finder/Persistent Instance tab) with a new quest being offered by Madria every hour on the hour.

- The quests are repeatedly offered on the following schedule, add/subtract for your current time:
  - 1500est/1200pst [[Supply Stocking]]
  - 1600est/1300pst [[Shipyard Services]]
  - 1700est/1400pst [[Clockwork Rescue]]
  - 1800est/1500pst [[Emergency Portal Repairs]]
  - 1900est/1600pst [[Supply Stocking]]
- The quests she offers can be completed in a group or solo.
- Completing her quests rewards both [[Far Seas Trading Company Token|Far Seas Trading Company Tokens]] and positive faction with [[Far Seas Supply Division (Faction)]], in addition to coin  and various items that are valuable to all tradeskill classes.
