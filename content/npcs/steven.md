## Notes

- Steven appears only during the [[Erollisi Day]] festival and since 2026 sells ONLY NEW items introduced in the current year, including the newest recipe book.
- All the older items, including older recipe books, can be found on the other Erollisi Day merchant [[Douglas]].

### Fun Fact

Steven and Douglas are based on Steve and Doug Butabi, two brothers from recurring Saturday Night Live sketch "the Roxbury Guys" and the movie "A Night at the Roxbury" about their nightclub-hopping antics.
