## Notes

![Grum before his demise](images/Grum.jpg)
During [[LU57]], Kobolds from the Steamfont Mountains entered the Greater Faydark and destroyed the old Shadow Spire Outpost.  Grum was tragically killed in the attack, and apparently his corpse was too big to move.

Before his passing, he offered the following quests.
