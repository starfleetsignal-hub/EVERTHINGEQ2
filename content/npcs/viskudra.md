## Notes

If you see and feel the rain, it's your warning to land somewhere, FAST! Otherwise she'll push you to the ground with her curse and if you're weak, you'll die! Otherwise you'll sustain damage based on the height you were at when you were cursed and forced out of the sky.

## Strategy

Go cannot target or engage this NPC. She just flies around creating rain and forces you out of the sky with her curse, HARD! There are only three known strategies:

- 1. Complete the adventure questline through to [[The Call Home]]. Start this line by speaking with [[Captain Val'kirr]]. This breaks the curse.

- 2. Complete the tradeskill quest [[Totemic Reinforcement]]. This is the end quest for [[Phaerus Thunderhorn]]. This doesn't break the curse but instead rewards you with the [[Shroud of Hidden Harvests]] which you can equip to make you invisible to Viskudra. If you aren't wearing this, you will be susceptible to the curse.

- 3. STAY ON THE GROUND!
