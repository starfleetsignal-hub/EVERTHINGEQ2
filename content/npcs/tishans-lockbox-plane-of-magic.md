## Notes

- Provides a free flying mount, the [[Displaced Harbinger]].
- You can obtain an additional set of armor for your mercenary.
- The gear pieces are slightly better than KA expert heroic and T1 raid gear. (*15 resolve, and more health than the expert heroic gear*)
- Orange adorn slots have taken the place of old purple adorn slots.
- Provides the following complete set of free level 100 equipment based on class:
