## Dialogue

When talking to Trainer Myrrin the Magnific:

- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "I am here to answer your questions on Spells and Combat Arts as well as other abilities. How can I help you?"
- Player Character: "Let us begin."
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "I am here to answer your questions. How can I help you?"
- Player Character: "Tell me about Spells."
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "A Spell is a magical ability. Spells have a variety of uses. Some allow you to damage an enemy, while others might provide benefits to yourself or an ally. You can hold your mouse pointer over a Spell's icon to see a description of what it does, or right-click on it and select "Examine.""
- Player Character: "Who uses Spells?"
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "Mages, priests, paladins, shadowknights, troubadors, and dirges all cast Spells. Each class has a list of Spells that is unique. While other classes may have similar spells, no one has capabilities exactly like another class. Also, certain magical items may be imbued with Spell effects that can be cast or that activate on their own."
- Player Character: "I have more questions."
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "I am here to answer your questions. How can I help you?"
- Player Character: "Tell me about Combat Arts."
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "Combat Arts are a lot like Spells, but they usually take less time to cast and cannot be interrupted. As their name implies, many Combat Arts focus on some aspect of battle."
- Player Character: "Who uses Combat Arts?"
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "All fighters and scouts use Combat Arts. Each of these classes has unique Combat Arts that help distinguish them from other classes. Combat Arts have names and effects that fit the class that uses them."
- Player Character: "I have more questions."
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "I am here to answer your questions. How can I help you?"
- Player Character: "Tell me about Abilities."
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "Abilities are general things your character can do, along with certain capabilities specific to your race. Some of your abilities are extremely useful, such as Sprint, which allows you to run very fast for a short period of time. You can also Call for Help when you are in danger, or launch a Ranged Attack if you have a ranged weapon and ammo."
- Player Character: "How are abilities different from Spells and Combat Arts?"
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "Unlike Spells and Combat Arts, Abilities cannot be upgraded. Most of your Abilities are received when you first begin your adventures in Norrath, although certain new ones can be granted as you become more experienced."
- Player Character: "I have more questions."
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "I am here to answer your questions. How can I help you?"
- Player Character: "Where do I find my Spells, Combat Arts, and Abilities?"
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "All your spells and Combat Arts, as well as other Abilities, are found in your Knowledge Book. Press the K key to open it at any time, and click the name of the tab you wish to view: Abilities, Spells, or Combat Arts."
- Player Character: "How do I use my Spells, Combat Arts, and Abilities?"
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "To use a Spell, Combat Art, or Ability, drag its icon from your Knowledge Book onto your hotbar. Some have already been placed there for you. To use one, simply click its icon on your hotbar or press the key assigned to it. You can open additional hotbars by right-clicking and selecting "Open New Hotbar.""
- Player Character: "I have more questions."
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "I am here to answer your questions. How can I help you?"
- Player Character: "How do I obtain and improve my Spells and Combat Arts?"
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "Most of your Spells or Combat Arts are given to you as you advance in levels and will be automatically scribed in your Knowledge Book. Certain special Spells or Combat Arts can be obtained by doing quests."
- Player Character: "How can I judge the effectiveness of my Spells and Combat Arts?"
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "There are six ranks of Spells and Combat Arts: Apprentice, Journeyman, Adept, Expert, Master and Grandmaster. Spells and Combat Arts that are automatically granted to you are usually given at the Apprentice rank. You should always try to have the highest rank of your Spells and Combat Arts because they gain effectiveness as you improve them."
- Player Character: "How can I improve my Spells and Combat Arts?"
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "Spells gained through quests or racial abilities are usually at their highest level and cannot be upgraded. Grandmaster spells are only available from your class training choices every ten levels starting at 14. you can see the choices by selecting the L key on your keyboard."
- Player Character: "What about other ranks of Spells and Arts?"
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "You will also find more potent ranks of spells and arts as you defeat opponents in battle. Many enemies will carry Adept versions of Spells and Combat Arts, while conquering some extremely powerful foes may result in the extremely rare Master versions."
- Player Character: "Are there other ways to improve my Spells and Combat Arts?"
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "In addition, certain crafters make upgrades to your Spells and Combat Arts using harvested components. They can create Journeyman quality spells, as well as valuable Expert scrolls using rare components. Buying from or trading with other players is an excellent way to upgrade your Spells and Combat Arts."
- Player Character: "Are all my Spells and Combat Arts unique?"
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "As you advance in level, you will obtain many completely new abilities as well as more effective versions of Spells or Combat Arts you have already learned. The background color of the spell icon tells you its type. Beneficial spells have a yellow, orange, or purple color, while attack spells have a red, green, or blue color."
- Player Character: "How can I keep all these Spells and Abilities straight in my Knowledge Book?"
- Trainer Myrrin the Magnific: "Your Spells and Combat Arts can easily be organized according to your personal taste. Just open your Knowledge Book and click the "Sort" button in the upper right corner. this allows you to organize your Spells and Arts for easy access to the abilities you wish to use."
- Player Character: "I will return for further instruction later."
