## Sells

Each Realm Crasher crate will give

- 1 piece of resolve equipment and
- *(More importantly!)* 1 random blue adorn
  - The random adorn is from the short list of [[Cthurath Armor (Rage of Cthurath)#'Free' Blue Adorns|dungeon-drop exclusive adorns]].  Because of this, it is the primary means to **randomly** acquire [[Evie the Astrologer]] and [[Viol the Botanist]] if you do not wish to farm them for hours/days at a time from [Solo] and [Heroic] Cthurath dungeons.

| Barters | Cost | Price |
|---|---|---|
| [[Realm Crasher Crate I]]<br>*(Resolve 630 equipment)* | x5 [[Essence of Displacement]]<br>x2000 [[Aethermatic Alloy]] | 15p 50g 25000 status |
| [[Realm Crasher Crate II]]<br>*(Resolve 640 equipment)* | x50 [[Essence of Displacement]]<br>x5000 [[Aethermatic Alloy]] | 45p 50g 35000 status |

## Notes

*If your primary motivation for purchasing these crates is for a [[Evie the Astrologer]] and/or [[Viol the Botanist]] adorn, the first crate (I) is widely considered the most effciient means to repeat-purchase to acquire those otherwise-rare adorns.*
