## Notes

The merchant will sell the dress only when you are on the quest, [[A New Dress, NOW!]], and only once. If you wish to obtain a dress to wear AND complete the quest, you can get the quest, buy the dress, then delete the quest. Go back to [[Licha]], accept the quest again, and then go back and buy the dress again.
