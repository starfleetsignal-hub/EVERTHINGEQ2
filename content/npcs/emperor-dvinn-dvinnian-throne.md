## Note

- The Emperor must be approached to start the ring event ending in [[Vindicator Crush]].  He will talk, then four 35 ▲ [[A D'Vinnian guardian|D'Vinnian guardians]] spawn and attack you.  He will talk some more, then Crush walks into the room as an NPC.  Halfway to the throne Crush becomes a 35▲▲▲ mob and attacks you.  Assuming you defeat Crush, the Emperor says some more nasty things and teleports away.
