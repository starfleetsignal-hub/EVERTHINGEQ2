## Description

- *Once one of the hand maidens of Tserrina Syl'Tor.  She was far more beautiful, so Tserrina took a knife and destroyed her face. She has since been released from the Tower by heroes from within Norrath.  No longer living with the eternal curse, she is forever doomed to wear the mask that hides her hideous wounds.*

## Rarity

All mercenaries from [[Mercenary Crates|mercenary crates]] are granted randomly. Rarity describes how likely or unlikely a player is to get this mercenary from a crate and, in turn, how much they will sell to [[Sneerio Backlash]] for [[Status points]].

- Cara Omica (Mercenary) is a Cursed Archon and sells for 6 Million status.
