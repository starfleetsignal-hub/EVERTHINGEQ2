## Notes

[[Zizzez]] is the Jester and right-hand of [[Aldys, the Sultan of the Daggers]]. It first appears next to [[Ismail]] in Raj'Durabad to extend an invitation to the Sultan's tent. It later appears to foil quite a few attempts to locate and retrieve a crystal arca that is of interest to Aerakyns who wish to return to their native lands.
