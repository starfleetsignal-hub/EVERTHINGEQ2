## NOTES

- One of 3 new plants that show up in [[Nektulos Forest]] after [[LU24]].
- Will trigger the following effect when you click on it or get too close:
  - **Hate Spores**
    - Decreases power of target by 6.3% instantly and every second
    - Duration: 10 seconds
  - Power drain can be partially resisted each second it triggers.
