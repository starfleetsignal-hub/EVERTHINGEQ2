## Notes

Cheris Alton is a friend of [[Joren Kalth]], and appears with him in the six main cities (Freeport, Gorowyn, Neriak, Qeynos, Kelethin, New Halas) as well as in The Withered Lands.  He disappears when Joren's quest, [[Journey to the Scar]], is completed.
