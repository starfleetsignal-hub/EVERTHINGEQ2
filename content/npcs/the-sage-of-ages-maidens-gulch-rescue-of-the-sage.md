## Description

*I am known as the Sage of Ages, a neutral historian charged with scribing the events of history so that their lessons will not be forgotten, or repeated. It surprises me that these creatures would be sent to attack me. Drakota rarely act of their own initiative. They are weapons used by Dragonkind to fulfill their own designs. I do not know what I could have done to anger the Scalelords. I only scribe the truth and have done so for many centuries.*

## Notes

The Sage of Ages is a central character in the epic quests [[Fire and Ice]] and [[Deception (Quest)|Deception]] which ultimately lead the player into a confrontation with the dragon [[Darathar]]. After you rescue him from the drakota in Maiden's Gulch, you will meet him again at the [[The Tower of the Oracles]] in Antonica.
