## Notes

Many items sold by Scribbleclaw require adequate faction with the [[Bloody Tooth Clan (Faction)|Bloody Tooth Clan]] and are purcahsed with [[Sovereign of Rile]] (faction) currency.

- [[Oogthor]] gives weekly and daily tradeskill quests to increase faction and earn currency.
  - Completing the "artisan epic 2.0" and doing one round of the quests from Oogthor earns enough faction to buy the Essentials tradeskill books (e.g. [[Bloody Tooth Carpentry Essentials]])
