## Notes

This merchant is one of 2 [[Scorched Sky]] NPCs, stands usually close to [[Ember Merchant]] and is designated to sell items from previous years, including [[Festive Sparkler]] needed for a few quests.

- For NEW items introduced in current year check [[Ember Merchant]] nearby.

[[Coin of Ember]] currency is needed to buy most of the items from the [[Scorched Sky]] event. It can be obtained from flame-themed enemies (all elementals) near the Devotee's of Flame celebration sites and from event quests.
