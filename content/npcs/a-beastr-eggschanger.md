## Notes

- This NPC is in various locations, only during the [[Beast'r Eggstravaganza]] event in mid-to-late April.
- The appearance of this NPC will vary, based on the location of the celebration. In [[Freeport]], [[Darklight Wood]], and [[Timorous Deep]] the NPC is cloaked and wearing a mask to look like a faun with large horns. In [[Qeynos Province District]], [[Frostfang Sea]], and [[Greater Faydark]] the NPC is a white faun.
- The NPC will exchange [[Beast'r Egg Hunting Guide|captured eggs]] that can be used as [[Pets|fluff pets]] for for house pets and sells a some Spring-themed items.
- This NPC may not appear on [[Server Types|TLE servers]], as the content unlocks do not always include [[Live Events]].
