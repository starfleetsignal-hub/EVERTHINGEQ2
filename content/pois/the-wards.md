This is an outdoor crafting area, an outdoors version of a [[Tradeskill Instance]].

## Reaching The Wards

To reach The Wards {{waypoint 2713, 123, 1141}}, you can either:

1. Walk from north without using any lifts:
   - through the pathway leading to Gorowyn North Entrance in Timorous Deep {{waypoint 2520, 108, 1006}}
   - through [[The Breathing Pools]] {{waypoint 2551, 114, 1057}}
   - through [[Gorowyn North Entrance]] {{waypoint 2593, 119, 1134}}
   - after going through the doorways, go the right (northwest)
   - enter another pathway {{waypoint 2689, 123, 1137}}.
   - proceed through the pathway to the The Wards.
1. From [[Faydedar Watch]] (around [[Chelzyk Gi'rul]], the Griffon Trainer and your transport to [[Butcherblock Mountains]]):
   - up the stairs through the holes on the wall to [[The Timorous Maw]] {{waypoint 2387, 21, 1352}}
   - go forward and right (northwest) through the circular pathway at {{waypoint 2462, 16, 1264}}
   - as you arrive in [[Gorowyn]], right in front of you there is a lift {{waypoint 2557, 9, 1237}}
   - from here, you can:
     1. go up with the lift to the **middle** level {{waypoint 2549, 89, 1242}}
     1. to the right (southwest), a ramp goes up {{waypoint 2557, 89, 1285}}
     1. climb the ramp up to [[The Upper Concourse]] {{waypoint 2528, 117, 1209}}
     1. proceed straight forward (west) on a ramp {{waypoint 2547, 118, 1171}} - you're now close to the northern entrance to Gorowyn.
   - *or*
     1. go up with the lift to the **top** level [[The Academy of Warfare]]
     1. to the right (south), a ramp goes down at {{waypoint 2566, 161, 1294}}
     1. go down and west a metal ramp to a horizontal (south-west to north-east) lift {{waypoint 2663, 137, 1282}}.
     1. take the lift to {{waypoint 2585, 120, 1178}} - you're now close to the northern entrance to Gorowyn.
   - make one more climb up the metal bridge at {{waypoint 2638, 120, 1143}} and proceed to the doorway at {{waypoint 2689, 123, 1137}}.
   - proceed through the pathway to the The Wards.
