Neriak Down Under is a crafting area that is built into a network of caves and tunnels.

## Reaching Neriak Down Under

You can enter either from three different doorways:

- a doorway at {{waypoint -162, 8, 321}}, at the southeastern end of [[Dockside Markets]]
- at a small cave end of [[Dockside Markets]]:
  1. take a small tunnel down at {{waypoint -142, 6, 249}}, a little north and below the tunnel between the markets and the [[Spires of Innoruuk]]
  1. take the ramp down at {{waypoint -154, 2, 254}}
  1. take one of the doorways, at {{waypoint -171, -5, 267}} or at {{waypoint -181, -5, 245}}
