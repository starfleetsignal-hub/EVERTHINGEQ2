## Background

> Few know of the Amatra, but many know of the [[Druid Ring|druid rings]]. Rings of megalithic stones holding druidic runes of nature and forming a circle.  They appear in many of the greater forests of [[Norrath]], but why here in this jungle? Some studies have placed the existence of a tiny arm or branch of the majestic [[Elddar Forest]] here within [[The Feerrott]]. That arm is called the Amatra Forest to those who believe and these are its stones.
>
> — [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Feerrott]]

## Notes

- The Restored Druid Ring is also known as the 'Ring of the Amatra' and formerly as the [[Destroyed Druid Ring]] before the druid rings were restored in LU58. The in-game map still displays this druid ring as 'destroyed'.
