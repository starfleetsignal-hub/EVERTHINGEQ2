## Background

> Just when you think you've run out of room to trudge forward, [[The Feerrott]] surprises you. Here, where a small pond meets a reed covered wall of stone, there is a hidden fissure. This tight tunnel is not for the claustrophobic. Tons and tons of stone press in on each other and offer just a tiny crack of a tunnel to pass through. Beyond this tiny fissure rests a sacred ground hidden from [[Norrath|Norrathian]] eyes for centuries, the [[Dread Basin|Valley of Fear]].
>
> — [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Feerrott]]
