## Notes

The Moonlight Tree spawns only at night, 10pm game time until dawn.  Use [[Foresting]] to gather [[Bough of Moonlight Wood|moonlight bough]].

- You need Foresting skill of 245 or higher to be able to harvest the [[Bough of Moonlight Wood|moonlight bough]].
