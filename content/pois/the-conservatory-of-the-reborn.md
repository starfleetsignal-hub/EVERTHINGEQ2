## Reaching the Conservatory

To reach the Conservatory, you can use either the Faydedar Watch (docks) entrance or the North Entrance to Gorowyn.

### From the Faydedar Watch (docks)

1. Go to [[Faydedar Watch]] (the docks area around [[Chelzyk Gi'rul]], the Griffon Trainer and your transport to [[Butcherblock Mountains]]).
1. Go up the stairs through the holes on the wall to [[The Timorous Maw]] {{waypoint 2387, 21, 1352}}.
1. Go forward and right (northwest) through the circular pathway at {{waypoint 2462, 16, 1264}}.
1. As you arrive in [[Gorowyn]], right in front of you there is a lift {{waypoint 2557, 9, 1237}}. Go up with the lift to the **top** level ([[The Academy of Warfare]])
1. To the right (south), a ramp goes down at {{waypoint 2566, 161, 1294}}. Take the ramp down and enter a pathway to your left (southwest) at {{waypoint 2673, 137, 1335}}. The Conservatory is on the other end of the pathway.

### From the North Entrance

1. Go through the pathway leading to Gorowyn North Entrance in Timorous Deep {{waypoint 2520, 108, 1006}}.
1. Go through [[The Breathing Pools]] {{waypoint 2551, 114, 1057}}.
1. Go through the [[Gorowyn North Entrance]] {{waypoint 2593, 119, 1134}}.
1. Take the horizontal (north-east to south-west) lift from {{waypoint 2585, 120, 1178}}.
1. The lift will take you to {{waypoint 2663, 137, 1282}}.
1. Enter a pathway to your right (south) at {{waypoint 2673, 137, 1335}}. The Conservatory is on the other end of the pathway.
