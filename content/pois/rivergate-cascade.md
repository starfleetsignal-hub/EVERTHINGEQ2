## Notes

*"This is a grand waterfall that falls from an [[Rivergate|outlet]] high in the steep foothills of [[Rivervale]]. The water trickles out from an outlet that links with the mysterious grotto workshop of the legendary [[Fiddy Bobick]]. A [[Valeflow River|river]] within Rivervale runs into [[Rivergate]] to power the machines of the great sipwright. The water then runs from the outlet to create the wondrous cascade." - (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Misty Thicket]])*
