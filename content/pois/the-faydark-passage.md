## Notes

The Faydark Passage is a path that starts near the zoneline to Greater Faydark and leads west and north towards [[Tunare's Grove]].  Although the path is clear, the giant vines that grow throughout LFay cover so much of it that it's almost like a tunnel.  It runs very near [[The Village of Panderpana]], and the area near there is patrolled by hostile brownies.
