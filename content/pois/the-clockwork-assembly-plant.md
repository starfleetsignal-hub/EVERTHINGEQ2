The individual parts of the various kinds of clockworks - heads, torsos, sawblades, etc - are made in the Clockwork Factory, and sent to the Assembly Plant in order to turn them into various kinds of clockworks.

## Mobs found here

The Clockwork Assembly plant is a ring event.  Players must clear all the groups of [[A clockwork engineer|clockwork engineers]].  This will spawn a wave of [[An activated klakdroid|activated klakdroids]]. Clear these to spawn [[The Klak'Anon Warhulk]] and some [[An activated klakdyne|activated klakdynes]].

There are also [[A clockwork assembler|clockwork assembler]]s and [[A clockwork metalsmith|clockwork metalsmith]]s found here; they are not involved in the ring event.
