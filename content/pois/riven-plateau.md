Overlooking the New Combine Foothold, the Riven Plateau is grown up in strange plants.  The spiky, purple-veined trees seem to be releasing a corruption directly into the soil, and other plants are hostile and aggressive towards adventurers.  The plateau also teems with wolves afflicted by the same corruption as the plants.
