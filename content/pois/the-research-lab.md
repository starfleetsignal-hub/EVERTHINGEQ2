## Lore

The Research Labs are the most horrifying place in Klak'Anon.  The clockworks, under the supervision of [[Bloodscrew the Surgeon]], perform cruel experiments on the beings, animal and sentient alike, that they've captured from the Steamfont Mountains.  Most of these "experiments" involve cutting them up alive with circular saws.
