## Notes

The book on the table notes that a creature named [[The Guurok]] can be killed by feeding it skulls. This isn't a true quest but a way to discover how to trigger an event in game. What the book is telling you is to collect 6 pirate skulls (dropped by the Fallen Pirates, Fallen Brigands and Fallen Assassins near this area) and drop them on the big rock on the middle of the stream, beside The Guurok. It will gradually get larger and eventually become attackable.
