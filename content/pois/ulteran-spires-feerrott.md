## Notes

Functioning [[Ulteran Spires]] appear in six zones on the ground and numerous places in [[Kingdom of Sky|The Overrealm]]. Ruins of the old [[Ulteran Spires|Ulteran Spire network]] can still be found in many other places around the Shattered Lands.

The spires will teleport anyone holding a shard to [[The Bonemire]] in the [[Kingdom of Sky|Overrealm]] every 5 minutes. Speak to the scion near the spires to receive a shard.
