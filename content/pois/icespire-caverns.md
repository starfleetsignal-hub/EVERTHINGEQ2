## Notes

The Icespire Caverns are actually a tunnel that runs north from the Jagged Plains to the door of [[Icespire Summit]].  Its most notable features are the gigantic ice crystals that bloom from the walls and floor of the cavern, and the Order of the Blade valkyries that patrol it.
