## Notes

- "[The] Crossroads is the the point where the two of the major roadways come to meet. In times past it has been an agricultural community and guard post. The [[Rallos Zek|Rallosian]] forces razed all structures here during [[The Age of War]]. Since then the [[Overlord Lucan D'Lere|Overlord]] has commanded the clearing of [The] Crossroads for use by travelers who cannot afford the traveler's tax in [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]], but can pay for the guardian tax required here where The Freeport Militia maintains a guard post." *- (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Commonlands]])*
- The guards at The Crossroads will attempt to kill non-Evil players.
  - This occurs on [[PvE]] servers as well
- There is a resurrection point inside of The Crossroads for all players.
