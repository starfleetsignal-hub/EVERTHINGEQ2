## Notes

This is the zone connection from [[Greater Faydark]] to [[Steamfont Mountains]].

The very last few meters of this path, just before you zone into [[Steamfont Mountains]], a new POI name appears; [[The Creeping Roots]]. To date, no quests or other information are connected to this POI.
