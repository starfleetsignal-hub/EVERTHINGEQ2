## Notes

- Mobs in [[The Bonemire]], [[The Halls of Fate]] and [[The Laboratory of Lord Vyemm]] have a small chance to drop items that can be used at the Mysterious Cube to summon Epic x4 named monsters.
- The location of the [[A monolithic phylactery|monolithic phylacteries]] surrounding the Cube represent what bosses are available for summoning.
- Each boss drops a specific 'Essence of Ghazi'. Once a player has all six in their inventory, they can use them at the Cube to summon [[Shade of Ghazi]].

|---|---|---|---|
| [[Adamantine Heart]] | West | [[Bearer of the Crux]] | [[Essence of Ghazi's Crux\|Crux]] |
| [[Ebony Draconic Wing]] | Northeast | [[Bearer of the Carpus]] | [[Essence of Ghazi's Carpus\|Carpus]] |

| [[Umbral Circlet]] | Southwest | [[The Umbral Keeper]] | [[Essence of Ghazi's Spirit\|Spirit]] |
