## Notes

The Jagged Plains got their name from the way shelves of ice jut out at odd angles from the permafrost, most likely due to [[The Rending]].  The Jagged Plains team with animals both aggressive and non, including ice wolves, ice lizards, bighorn sheep and arctic wyrms.  The Frosthorn minotaurs live in caves under the ice shelves, and are at war with the nobler but not much friendlier valkyries that call themselves The Order of the Blade.
