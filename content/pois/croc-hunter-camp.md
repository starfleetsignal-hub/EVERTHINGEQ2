## Notes

- This area is sometimes called the "North Croc Hunter Camp" to distinguish it from the [[South Croc Hunter Camp]] on the northern edge of [[The Oasis of Marr]]. Both camps have NPCs that will give quests but only the northern camp gives discovery experience.
- This is an excellent place to harvest [[Root Nodes#T6|sun bleached roots]], [[Bush Nodes#T6|parched shrubbery]] and [[Wood Nodes#T6|arid timber]].
