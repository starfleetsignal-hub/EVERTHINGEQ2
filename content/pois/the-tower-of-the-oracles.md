## Notes

- The residence of the *Coldwind Oracles*, a secretive group of mages.
- The Ulteranis, a large arcane orb, stands in the center of the top floor. Mouseover information reads: "The Ulteranis. Indentions of five rings encircle the smooth crystal of the Ulteranis."
