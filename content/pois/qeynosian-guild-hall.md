## Notes

When the game originally launched, this building was called the "Hall of Thunder" and had a sign posted above the main entrance that read "THIS BUILDING IS HEREBY CLOSED BY ORDER OF QUEEN ANTONIA!" The building was reappropriated for guild hall use with the release of [[LU49]].
