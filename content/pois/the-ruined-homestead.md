## Notes

The McCoven family tried, ill-advisedly, to set up a homestead in [[Guardian's Gulch]], and it was destroyed by elementals.  The surviving McCovens are enlisting the help of adventurers in picking up the pieces.

## NPCs

This is the fourth quest hub in Everfrost.  The following quest NPCs are located here:

- [[Inon McCoven]]
- [[Seamus McCoven]]
- [[Areth Windvell]]
