The Protectors of Growth set up a magical obelisk to repel the corruption, and in a small area around it the Wakening Lands are still as they once were.  The obelisk's power is waning, however, and the corruption encroaches further each day.

## Notes

- Once you've clicked on the horse at {{waypoint -2278, -672, -173}}, the Sanctuary of Waning is connected to the horse transportation system.
