## Notes

To reach this POI, jump down to the dragon's head where the epic [[Empowered Xuun'vok the Death Reveler]] spawns. Hop over the spikes over the dragon's eyes to land on its snout. Drop down to a whisker/moustache then drop/jump into the dragon's mouth.
