The area where the [[Ocean of Tears]] meets [[Faydwer]] is a deep bay that teams with aquatic life.  Less pleasantly, it also teems with aqua goblins and sirens who are waging war on each other.  There are several locations to visit in the bay - [[Glubbsunk Cove]], [[Tidesylph Isle]], and [[Sigan's Peril]].
