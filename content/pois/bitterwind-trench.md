## Notes

The Bitterwind Trench is a winding canyon that opens on Everstorm Bay and snakes its way northeast to the Jagged Plains.  The narrow canyon channels the bitter Everfrost wind particularly strongly, often kicking up gusts of snow that reduce visibility.  This bitter wind is the namesake of the canyon.

A group of Barbarians and Humans known as the Bitterwind Pioneers have made an [[Bitterwind Pioneer's Encampment|encampment]] in a relatively sheltered bend of the trench.  In addition to the roaming Bitterwinds, there are also wolves to contend with.
