## Notes

Unlike many of the sets of Druid Rings in Norrath, the ones in Butcherblock Mountains were always protected and maintained.  They have a unique feature - a shallow pool of water in the middle of the rings.  This water is blessed by [[Tunare]] and the powers of growth.  It has attracted wisps and - more problematically - bears.
