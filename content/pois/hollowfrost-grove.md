## Notes

Hollowfrost Grove is the remnants of a forest called the Icepine Forest, which grew up around Tunarbos, the World Tree.  Strangely, the Icepine Forest did not exist at all in the days of *EverQuest 1* - it grew some time between then and [[The Rending]].  After The Rending, the increased cold in Everfrost killed most of the forest, Tunarbos included.

Today, the area is, relatively speaking, one of the safer areas in Everfrost.  It is full of curiously non-aggressive skeletons with bones of ice, as well as harmless stags and not-so-harmless leopards.  Troglodytes of the Numbfoot clan also frequent the area, attacking the stags and leopards.  Worse, they seem to be performing some kind of shamanistic experiments with the [[World Tree Root]]...

Adventurers are best advised to head to [[Snowsworn's Hunter's Outpost]], where Beckah Snowsworn is overseeing the delegation of tasks that adventurers might perform in exchange for money and gear.
