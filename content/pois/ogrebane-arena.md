## Information

In the days of *EverQuest*, Ogrebane Arena was a small area inside Kaladim where players could participate in PVP.

The Serilian Horde continues to use it as an arena.  A ring event built into the arena allows adventurers to fight multiple named monsters.

### Ring event

1. Clear all the various kinds of spectators - [[A Boombottom spectator|Boombottom spectator]]s, [[A Grungetalon spectator|Grungetalon spectator]]s, [[A Kragbak spectator|Kragbak spectator]]s and [[A Terrok spectator|Terrok spectator]]s.  You do not need to kill the [[A Quaketail vendor|Quaketail vendor]]s.
1. Once the spectators are all dead, go to the bottom of the arena where the [[A Kragbak gladiator|Kragbak]] and [[A Terrok gladiator|Terrok gladiator]]s will have become attackable.
1. After all the gladiators have dropped, either [[The Klaw]] or [[The Master Miner]]  will spawn.
1. After beating whichever namer spawns, [[The Ringmaster]] will run down into the arena floor and become attackable.
