## Notes

To get this POI disco, you have to jump off the northern edge of the platform where [[The Hole]] teleportation pad is located. This will give you the update and then zone you into the [[The Hole]]. Be sure to wear a featherfall cloak, because you will fall quite a distance after you zone into The Hole!
