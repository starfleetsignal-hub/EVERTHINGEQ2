## Notes

- Crater Pond was created during the meteoric bombardment that ensued following [[The Shattering|the shattering of Norrath's moon, Luclin]]. The black and blue chunks of rock strewn about the pond are remnants of meteor and are referred to as *luclinite*. Although the water seems inviting, it is quite toxic and only [[A pond glider|pond gliders]] are known to have successfully adapted to its ingestion. *(Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to Antonica]])*
- [[Dog Pond]] in [[The Commonlands]] formed in the same manner.
