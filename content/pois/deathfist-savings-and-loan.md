## Notes

Inside the room there are 5 clickable chests. Examining them gets almost the same message, with each chest displaying a different symbol:

- The Deathfist Pawn - *for [[A Burglar Alert: Pawn]]*
- The Deathfist Praetorian Guard - *for [[A Burglar Alert: Praetorian]]*
- The Deathfist Centurion Guard - *for [[A Burglar Alert: Centurion]]*
- The Deathfist Legionnaire Guard - *for [[A Burglar Alert: Legionaire]]*
- The Deathfist Emperor - *for [[A Burglar Alert: Emperor]]*
