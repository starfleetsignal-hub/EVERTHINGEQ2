The K'val Ruins is an old palace of Kromzek design.  It has grown up with the corrupted plants and fallen into disrepair.  Giants enthralled by the corrupted plants still roam its halls.

## Notes

Inside the ruins, the room at {{waypoint -3367, -713, -911}} has an elevator to the top floor.
