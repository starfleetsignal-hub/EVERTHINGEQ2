## Background

> Emerging from the coast a person may find themselves being chased from the ancient ogre roadway and deeper southward. Here is where, in the dense walls of the rising foothills, a path exists leading towards a hidden vale. Here in this vale few lizardmen venture and here is where explorers may find a place for retreat. But remember to post watch! There is but one way into this vale and one way out. If you get trapped in here there is nowhere to run.
>
> — [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Feerrott]]
