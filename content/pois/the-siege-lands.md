## Notes

- Also referred to as *Barrage Meadow*.
- During [[The Age of War]], it was here that elephant-drawn orc trebuchets placed [[The City of Freeport]] under siege. It is said that the grass grows green here from the large amounts of orc blood spilt when the forces of Freeport finally broke the siege. The [[A ghostly orc|ghostly orcs]] roaming the field are remnants of that siege. *- (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Commonlands]])*
