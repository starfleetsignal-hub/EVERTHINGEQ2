## Notes

- [[A lost necklace]] has 2 quite different purposes -- see link.

You must have completed the following quests in order for the gleaming chest to appear in The Hedge Hollow.

- [[This Won't End Well...]] -- Talk to npc in a starter city then head to [[Loping Plains]].
- [[A Nightmarish Illness]] -- Kill the boss of [[The Hedge Hollow]] and receive [[A pair of gleaming lockpicks]] (body drop). The gleaming lockpicks will not show up in your inventory but the game remembers them.
- [[Train to Zone!]] -- Not available on your first run through [[The Hedge Hollow]].

Only AFTER you have completed those three steps will the gleaming chest appear.

There is no lockout timer on [[The Hedge Hollow]] so you can re-enter the zone right away.
