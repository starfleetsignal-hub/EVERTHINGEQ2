## Notes

This location actually consists of three rooms:

1. The Crushbone orc guests {{waypoint -116.5, -32, 92}}
   - Eight [[A Crushbone guest|Crushbone guests]] (66^^ Heroic - 67^^^ Heroic)
   - [[Darlek the Exterminator]] (67^^^ Heroic)
1. The Named Tunarian guests {{waypoint -116.5, -32, 67.5}}
   - Four [[A Tunarian guest|Tunarian guests]] (67^^ Heroic - 67^^^ Heroic)
   - [[Lenetian Leafvein]] (67^^^ Heroic)
1. The Unnamed Tunarian guests {{waypoint -116.5, -32, 50}}
   - Eight [[A Tunarian guest|Tunarian guests]] (66^^ Heroic - 67^^^ Heroic)

Both Tunarian rooms have [[A Mistmoore guard]] (66^^^ Heroic) stationed out front and [[A Mistmoore incarcerator]] (68^^^ Heroic) will wander through occasionally.  Also, [[A Mistmoore guard]] (67^^^ Heroic) wanders the hall outside these rooms.
