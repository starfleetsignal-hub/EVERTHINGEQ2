## Notes

- The 3rd floor can be reached two ways:
  1. Go down the ramp from the 1st floor, through the 2nd floor, and destroy a breakable wall at {{waypoint 43, -24, 3}}.
  1. Jump down the waterfall to the 4th floor, then go up the ramp from there.
