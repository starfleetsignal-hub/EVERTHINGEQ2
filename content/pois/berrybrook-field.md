## Notes

Nestled between the shores of the coast and the banks of [[The Lazy Drain]] is Berrybrook Field, a smal halfling shire. These halflings spent most of their days hunting and foraging for goods that would be traded amongst the stout folk, but being so close to the valuable resources also brought them close [to] the foul stench of the goblins of Runnyeye. - (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Misty Thicket]])
