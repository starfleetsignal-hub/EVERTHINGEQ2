## Background

*This is a small island just off the east coastline near [[Tagglefoot Farms]]. On this island sit three towering pines, sentient, the druids say. The massive trees are known as the The Three Pines to the halflings. They say that they can also attack using great magic and large roots that rise from the ground. The roots once formed a bridge to the mainland, but all those magical powers have been subdued by some other force of nature or unature. - (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Misty Thicket]])*
