## Notes

The Fae Drake Breeding Grounds are, as the name would indicate, the section of Lesser Faydark where the fae drakes breed.  The area is littered with nests and absolutely teems with various kinds of fae drakes.  Near the center of the area, some monks have established The [[Camp of the Legendary Wu]].

The more dangerous drakes congregate towards the northern section of the zone.  There, the [[Nest of the Patriarch]] and the patriarch of the drakes himself, [[Old Dimshimmer]], are found.

In the southwest corner of the area, [[Captain Trueshot]], an elf of interest to adventurers on the quest [[Casualties of the War of the Fay]], can be found.
