## Notes

The Lightshadow Undergrowth is the jungle covering most of the eastern half of The Eidolon Jungle.  The monsters found throughout it are weakest in the northwest, and get stronger the further east and south you travel.

North of the Lightshadow Undergrowth are [[The Ethermist Shores]], and to the south are [[The Greyfall]] and [[The Greyring]].  The eastern edge of the Lightshadow Undergrowth is marked by an incredibly high sheer cliff.
