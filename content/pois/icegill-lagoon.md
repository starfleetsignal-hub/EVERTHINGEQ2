During [[The Shattering]], a rock from [[Luclin]] landed directly on Halas, destroying the city and the earth beneath it, causing the area to become flooded under the ocean.  Now, the area is named after the goblin tribe that lives on the ice floes, the Icegills.  Under the water are ferocious sharks, water elementals, and the ghosts of the inhabitants of the old Halas.

There is an Ulteran spire here, built on one of the largest ice floes.

## Notes

- Called 'Icegill Lake' on the stable master map.
- Referred to as 'Eci's Cove' during the quest, [[In Search of a Menagerie]]
