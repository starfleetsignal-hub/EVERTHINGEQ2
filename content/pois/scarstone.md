## Notes

Scarstone is a fortress the New Combine established in the Cobalt Scar to fight the dracurion legions of Kerafyrm attacking out of the Drakelands.  In building Scarstone, they cut down many trees in the surrounding area, and are now suffering attacks from the wood elementals as well.
