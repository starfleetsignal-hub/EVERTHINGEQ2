## Notes

**Three Roads Outpost** is named because it is at the junction of three roads - one leading east to [[The Lesser Faydark]], one leading north to [[The Chessboard]], and the other west towards the [[Butcherblock Docks]].  Because of its proximity to the Lesser Faydark, it is informally known as *Lesser Faydark Station*.

There is a griffon tower there, as well as a few individuals with favors to ask of adventurers.
