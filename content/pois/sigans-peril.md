## Notes

Sigan's Peril is the name of a sunken ship in Butcherblock Bay.  It is haunted by the vengeful ghosts of its crew.

Honestly, a ship that *wasn't* haunted by the vengeful ghosts (or skeletons, or zombies) of its crew would be more unusual on Norrath than one that is.
