## Background

*This is a spot in the forest where a giant depression formed in [[The Age of Turmoil (Lore)|The Age of Turmoil]]. From that depression rose a gift from [[Tunare]], an [[Arbos, the Elddar Tree|Eldarr tree]], one of the few left on this world. It is rumored that all forests have the power to sprout an Elddar sapling, but few are capable in these ages of turbulent powers. What gave birth to this Elddar sapling is unknown, but there are forces on [[Norrath]] that would pay highly to obtain or control the sapling. - (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Misty Thicket]])*
