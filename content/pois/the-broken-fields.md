## Notes

A foreboding region of fire-scorched tree trunks and angry elementals and drakes.  Further south lies a massive camp of Kobolds.

Along the southwest mountain wall of the area, [[Dagnor's Pass]] cuts through the mountains towards the Estate of Unrest.
