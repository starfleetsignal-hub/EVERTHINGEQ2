In the days of Ak'Anon, this room was simply a mostly empty underground cavern with a large waterfall that served as a sort of buffer between the gnomes who worshipped evil deities. The Clockworks saw opportunity and built a hydroelectric dam that they use to power all of Klak'Anon.
