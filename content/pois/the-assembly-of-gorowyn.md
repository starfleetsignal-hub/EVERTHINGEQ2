## Notes

To reach the Assembly,

- **Jump** to a lonely ledge at {{waypoint 2705, 85, 1226}} from the [[The Shadowed Seminary]].
  - **Note:** *Prepare for 100+ HP falling damage.*
- Walking straight forward (southwest) to a pathway. The Assembly is on the pathway and beyond it.

Easy way is to walk west from the Dragon's Ancher. You take no damage.
