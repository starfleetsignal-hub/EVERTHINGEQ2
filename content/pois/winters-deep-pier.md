## Background

*This pier is a monument to Old Antonica. It once acted as a dock to small fishing boats of the Vale folk. This shoreline caressed the mysterious body of water called Winter's Deep. The center of this lake was veiled in an icy mist. What lies beyond was the inhospitable land called the Frigid Plains. Only the magical blessings from the powers that be kept the [[Enchanted Lands|Misty Thicket]] from being overtaken by the deathly chill of the north. - (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Misty Thicket]])*
