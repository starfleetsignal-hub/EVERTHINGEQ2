## Notes

> Caltorsis was once a small port and keep established to service the watch of the northern coastline. Mere seasons after the beginning of the [[Age of Destiny]], the knights of Caltorsis were all slain in a battle with pirates invading the shoreline. All that remains is the crumbling keep.
>
> — [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to Antonica]]
