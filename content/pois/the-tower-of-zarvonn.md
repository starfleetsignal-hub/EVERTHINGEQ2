## Notes

- "The Tower of [[Zarvonn]] was constructed near the latter half of [[The Age of Turmoil (Lore)|The Age of Turmoil]] and was a mage sanctum built for the then Foci of the Arcane Scientists, Professor Romiak Jusathorn. Records told of two of these towers being built, [but the second has never been found]." *- (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Commonlands]])*
  - During the quest [[Zarvonn's Legacy]], the location of the second tower is revealed as being the tower in [[The Eternal Gorge]].
- On the in-game map, this [[POI]] is called 'Zarvonn's Tower', however when you enter the tower the Discovery is called 'The Tower of Zarvonn'.
