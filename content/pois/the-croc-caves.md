## Background

> Several chimney spires in the sea attract one's attention with their distinctive columnar shapes. At the base of one set of spires, several crocodiles lay still in the water, their eyes following every movement. Several boulders near the entry appear clawed and scratched, as though something large had moved them at some point while passing through. Could [[Lockjaw the Ancient|Lockjaw]] still be alive after all these years? While no direct evidence of his presence was seen, it is said that crocodiles are extremely long-lived.
>
> — [[Bootstrutter's Trail Guide to the Desert of Ro (House Item)|Bootstrutter's Trail Guide to the Desert of Ro]]

## Notes

- **The Croc Caves** are a series of tunnels and caves beneath [[The Stilled Sea]]. There are 4 entrances to the caves.
  - [[The Oasis of Marr]]: at {{waypoint -1222, -211, -331}}
  - [[The Oasis of Marr]]: at {{waypoint -1106, -203, -179}}
  - [[Croc Hunter Camp]]: at {{waypoint -1330, -226, -636}}
  - Water entrance: at {{waypoint -1948, -229, -1006}}
- This is also an excellent place to mine [[Eroded ore]] and [[Sand scoured stone]] if you can beat the farmers to it.
