This is a crafting area, a version of a [[Tradeskill Instance]] that is not a separate instance but part of another zone (in this case, [[Haven]]).

## Features

The crafting area contains the following amenities:

- [[Sanice Castavon]], the Rush Order foreman {{waypoint 55.32, -31.97, -192.48}}
- [[Tabatha Woncroft]], the Work Order foreman {{waypoint 60.39, -31.59, -189.13}}
- [[Work Order Clipboard]] - a desk beside the Rush Order and Work Order foremen {{waypoint 61.7, -30.19, -187.06}}
- [[Ragil]], the Fuel Merchant {{waypoint 44.86, -32.18, -197.23}}
- [[Meglese Legarl]], the Fuel Merchant {{waypoint 44.81, -32.49, -209.27}}
- [[Jaurl Simetral]], the Fuel Merchant {{waypoint 27.04, -32.97, -183.45}}
- [[Logan]], the Broker (Black Market) {{waypoint -39.11, -68.89, -65.02}}
- [[Fizzbang Coghandler]], the Crafting Trainer {{waypoint 21.55, -33.01, -145.44}}
  - Sells essential volumes for levels 1 through 100 and advanced volumes for levels 1 through 10
  - Gives you level 2 Artisan and the first two Artisan Essentials books for free (by speaking to him)
  - Lets you choose your tradeskill archetype and tradeskill at level 9 Artisan and 19 Craftsman/Outfiter/Scholar
- [[Kit Breanut]], the Tradeskill Career Counsellor {{waypoint 37.91, -34.08, -223.39}}
- [[Chemistry Table]] x2 {{waypoint 55.55, -32.59, -208.97}}
- [[Engraved Desk]] {{waypoint 49.23, -33.25, -222.05}}
- [[Forge]] x3 {{waypoint 39.23, -32.57, -194.92}}
- [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]] x2  {{waypoint 60.84, -25.17, -176.65}}
- [[Stove & Keg]] x2  {{waypoint 37.63, -33.17, -175.35}} {{waypoint 39.79, -32.53, -155.48}}
- [[Woodworking Table]] x2  {{waypoint 16.6, -32.06, -167.58}}
- [[Work Bench]] {{waypoint 26.04, -32.28, -201.49}}
- The crafting area **does NOT** have a [[Bank]] (but one is located nearby in [[Haven]] at {{waypoint -39.11, -68.89, -65.02}})
- The crafting area **does NOT** have a [[Broker]] (but there is a Black Market one located nearby in [[Haven]] at {{waypoint -126.12, -68.68, -99.96}})

The crafting area **does NOT** have an Adorning and Tinkering Task NPC, a Trades Coordinator (grandmaster), a [[Tradeskill Tutorial|Tradeskill Tutor]], or a faction merchant.
