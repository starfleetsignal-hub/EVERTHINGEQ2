## Notes

- To enter this temple, you'll need to get to almost the northernmost part of west Lavastorm, and enter an archway. It's tricky to get to, but once you're in, the censer is easy to find. Just go left once inside past the skeleton guards, and it's sitting on a desk down the hallway on your left.
