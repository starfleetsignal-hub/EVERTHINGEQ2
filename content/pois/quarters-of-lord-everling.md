## Notes

- The hidden door will open only if you have completed the proper steps in [[The Everling Lockets]]. To open the door, right-click on the stand to the right of the bookcase and select 'Use.' You must right-click as left-clicking will just cause the character to inspect it.
- Contains the secret entrance to the basement
