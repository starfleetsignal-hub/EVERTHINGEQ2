![A close-up view of the glow from within the dagger](images/Everfrost_-_Titan's_Dagger_%28night%29.png)
The Titan's Dagger the last remaining piece of the Everfrost Peaks of old.  It is a massive piece of rock, almost like a hollow stalagmite or icicle, jutting out of the middle of Icegill Lagoon.  The Dagger is actually hollow and, if viewed from the north side, a luminescence can be seen welling up from inside it.

Once flying mounts were introduced, adventurers who attempted to look inside the Titan's Dagger for the source of the luminescence found that it came from nowhere and there was nothing else interesting inside the Dagger.  One of Norrath's more pointless (but prettier!) mysteries.

## Notes

- There is no yellow indicator on the in-game map for this POI, but it does award discovery experience when approached. The location is a bit fussy, and players may have to circle around the base of it to get a discovery point.
