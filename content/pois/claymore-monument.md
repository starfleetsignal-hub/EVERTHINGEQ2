## Notes

- A replica of the [[The Claymore Plaza|Qeynos Claymore monument]] found in [[Qeynos Capitol District|North Qeynos]].
- Legend holds that [[The Qeynos Claymore]] was wielded here to repel an *evil avatar* in the [[War of Plagues]]. In symbolic remembrance of this and in honor of past battles, Qeynosians often visit here to praise the fallen.
