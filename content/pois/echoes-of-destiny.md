## Notes

![Exterior of Echoes of Destiny](images/Echoes_of_Destiny_exterior.png) Echoes of Destiny is an alchemist's shop.  It stocks assorted potions for adventurers of levels 1 through 70, and a few items that can be paid for with Station Cash.
