## Reaching Smuggled Goods

To reach Smuggled Goods, enter a tunnel at {{waypoint -70, -68, -94}} next to the sign that says *Smuggled Goods* and follow the tunnel almost to the far end.

## Notes

Includes class, portal and teleport trainers [[Rantus Harbinger]], [[Kreznal]], [[Xan Del'Hra]], [[Kray Swiftmore]], [[Lance Dra'Kol]] and [[Annette Windsong]].
