One strange thing about Ak'Anon has always been the underground river that fed the large lakes, as despite the thermal activity in the Steamfont Mountains, there is no aboveground evidence that the river exists.  Although [[The Shattering]] did change the river's course slightly, it still flows into Klak'Anon as a waterfall to this day.
