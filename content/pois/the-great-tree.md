## Notes

**The Great Tree** is an immense Elddar Tree growing in [[Tunare's Grove]].  [[Arloona Delyssa]], a Priestess of Tunare offering a [[The Ivy Shrouded Timeline|signature quest series]], is meditating under the tree.
