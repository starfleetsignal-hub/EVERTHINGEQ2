## Notes

> This ruin is all that remains of the keep that once held the *Order of Gnollslayers*, a band of knights dedicated to patrolling the borders of [[Qeynos]] and slaying all [[Gnoll|gnolls]]. It is rumored that their leader, *Lord Hefax Tinmar*, buried the magical Sabertooth sword near here.
>
> — [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to Antonica]]
