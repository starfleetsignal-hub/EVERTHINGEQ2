## Notes

Amelia Pond is a reference to [Amelia "Amy" Pond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Pond), the first companion the [Eleventh Doctor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eleventh_Doctor) meets after his regeneration in the British sci-fi program *[Doctor Who](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Who)*.
