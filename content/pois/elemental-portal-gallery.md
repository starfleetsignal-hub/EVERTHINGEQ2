## Notes

- Portals to the four elemental realms are located in this area.
  - Northeast: Portal to [[Eryslai, the Kingdom of Wind]] at {{waypoint 715.69, 412.40, -379.63}}
  - Southeast: Portal to [[Doomfire, the Burning Lands]] at {{waypoint 728.63, 412.40, -336.86}}
  - Southwest: Portal to [[Vegarlson, the Earthen Badlands]] at {{waypoint 773.55, 412.40, -336.65}}
  - Northwest: Portal to [[Awuidor, the Unresting Waters]] at {{waypoint 786.50, 412.40, -379.79}}
- Mission questgivers are located in the center of this area.
