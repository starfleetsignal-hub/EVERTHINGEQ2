## Description

The Guard Towers in Maj'Dul are where many battles between [[The Court of the Coin (Faction)|Coin]], [[The Court of Truth (Faction)|Truth]] and [[The Court of the Blades (Faction)|Blades]] factions take place. The current holder of a tower displays their banner on the outside; their guards and captains can be found within
