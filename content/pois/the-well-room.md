## Notes

- As you near the well, in-game text appears in your chat box stating: *Peering down the shaft of what looks like an old well, you're not sure if you can see the bottom or if there is one.*
- If you jump down, [[Tumarinan the Scion of Suffering]] will aggro and attack immediately upon landing. It's a long way back up, so gird yourself for danger!
