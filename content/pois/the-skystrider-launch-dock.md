## Notes

- During the events of [[LU49]], this location was called A D.I.R.T.Y. Construction Project. Its sister camp, [[A D.I.R.T.Y. Excavation Site]] used to be accessible via a carpet formerly in this area, but was abandoned with the re-discovery of the technology used to create [[The Skystrider]], the new balloon transport introduced with [[The Shadow Odyssey]].
- Purple shinies can still be found here with the Excavation Helmet.
