## Background

*The Bogbottom family built this great mill during the end days of [[The Age of Turmoil (Lore)|The Age of Turmoil]]. Their love of revelry and song brought them to river's edge to erect this mill. Here they would spend day and night producing some of the finest lutes and flutes the world has ever seen. Unfortunately the Bogbottom's vanished while researching the legendary bard Kelkarn. Their absence has given the goblins a new outpost within the Misty Thicket. - (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Misty Thicket]])*
