## Notes

- Spire Shadow Outpost was formerly located right in front of [[Steamfont Alley]], the passage to the Steamfont Mountains. As of [[LU57]], kobolds from Steamfont destroyed the old Shadow Spire Outpost. Most of the inhabitants survived, but [[Grum]] was killed and [[Kresp]] disappeared. They relocated to the bottom of the Spire hill and set up some tents.
