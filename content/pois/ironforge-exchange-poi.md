The Ironforge Exchange is the crafting hub of [[Qeynos]] and the workshop of the The Ironforge Exchange. In addition to the tradeskill instance (see [[The Ironforge Exchange]] {{waypoint 331, -20, -99}}), there are various crafting amenities available in the Ironforge Exchange building.

## Features

The crafting area outside the instance contains the following amenities:

- [[Alfred Ironforge]], the Rush Order foreman {{waypoint 341.64, -20.63, -105.89}}
- [[Executor Adept Sal'ynelle]], the Work Order foreman {{waypoint 342.28, -20.63, -105.46}}
- [[Work Order Clipboard]] x3, one upstairs {{waypoint 337.39, -15.88, -97.40}}, one by the stairs {{waypoint 342.87, -20.63, -98.19}}, and one by the [[Stove & Keg]] {{waypoint 331.84, -20.63, -92.88}}
- [[Hal]], the Fuel Merchant {{waypoint 344.87, -20.63, -103.91}}
- [[Londiar Inygad (Qeynos)|Londiar Inygad]], who gives Adorning and Tinkering daily tasks {{waypoint 345.24, -17.39, -87.86}}
- [[Grandmaster Kimbialis]], the Trades Coordinator {{waypoint 336.47, -20.63, -101.28}}
- [[Jason Merrytoe]], the Tradeskill Tutor {{waypoint 342.12, -20.63, -95.4}}
- [[Devona Ironforge]], the Crafting Trainer {{waypoint 346.52, -21.13, -92.18}}
  - Sells essential volumes for levels 1 through 100 and advanced volumes for levels 1 through 10
  - Gives you level 2 Artisan and the first two Artisan Essentials books for free (by speaking to him)
  - Lets you choose your tradeskill archetype and tradeskill at level 9 Artisan and 19 Craftsman/Outfiter/Scholar
- [[Fidelia Recolo]], the Tradeskill Career Counsellor {{waypoint 346.44, -21.13, -86.03}}
- [[Fhara Hanford]], the faction merchant 1=339.49 2=-20.63 3=-107.78
  - Sells house items, including house tradeskill stations for faction, status and coin
- [[Chemistry Table]] {{waypoint 350.85, -21.13, -93.89}}
- [[Engraved Desk]] {{waypoint 353.51, -17.88, -95.97}}
- [[Forge]] {{waypoint 349.53, -21.13, -86.17}}
- [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]] {{waypoint 333.09, -15.88, -93.43}}
- [[Stove & Keg]] x1 {{waypoint 331.72, -20.63, -93.75}}
- [[Woodworking Table]] {{waypoint 337.19, -15.88, -91.21}}
- [[Work Bench]] {{waypoint 348.12, -17.39, -87.25}}

The crafting area outside the instance does not contain a [[Broker]], or a [[Bank]], but a Bank is located just across the street at Qeynos Hold {{waypoint 274, -18, -120}}, while the Broker is next door in Sneed Galliway's Trading Post {{waypoint 276.94, -19.26, -73.51}}. Another Broker can be found inside the tradeskill instance {{waypoint 331, -20, -99}}.
