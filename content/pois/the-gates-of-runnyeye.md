## Background

*If one is so brave or foolish to delve along [[Bog Mountain]] they may just happen upon a dark tunnel. This tunnel is the pathway to a mighty door leading into the goblin fortress [[Runnyeye|Runnyeye Citadel]]. The door is one of great beauty and of mysterious design. The designs are so alluring that they seem out of place amongst the crude makeshift world of the goblins. - (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Misty Thicket]])*
