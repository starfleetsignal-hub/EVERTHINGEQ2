## Background

*This tunnel was carved into the steep ridge of the vale foothills of Kithicor by halfling engineers. It was a great feat that no doubt employed the direction of a dwarven miner's guild. The Gullet was one of two great tunnels leading into the halfling city [[Rivervale]]. The natural defense of the steep hills kept the isolated vale safe from the dangerous wilds beyond. - (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Misty Thicket]])*
