## Notes

**The Sharpthorn Thicket** is a section of the Lesser Faydark inhabited by wood golems called Sharpthorns.  There are also hostile wood elementals.  The Sharpthorn Thicket overlooks [[The Hand of Reclamation]] where the Thexians make their camp.  A hill with a druid ring atop it called [[Crown Point]] can also be found here.
