## Notes

Fort Irontoe consists mainly of fortifying walls and a few small towers surrounding the [[Mortartoe Mines]].  The mines run through a mountain range, with [[West Fort Irontoe]] and East Fort Irontoe on opposite ends.

East Fort Irontoe sees somewhat less action from the Serilian Horde than the west does, but it has its own problems.  The dwarves tried to harvest trees in the area to help build their fortifications, infuriating the Shadesire treants.  The Treants conjured up wood elementals from the stumps of fallen trees and now wage their own small war against the dwarves in the fort.
