## Background

> Deep off the beaten path and nestled between rocky hills is a pass that holds three monuments of war, each with runes and visages of the gods of war. The three monuments appear to be having some form of secret meeting far from the ears of the city. Could this be the location of such a meeting, a location of buried generals or even the arrival point for a god's return?
>
> — [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Feerrott]]

## Notes

- The Sentinel Stones are also referred to as 'The Secret Meeting'.
