## Notes

- The [[Inventor's Threshold]] is a series of metal platforms covered with machines, gears, conveyor belts, and various engines that was constructed during the [[Update:64|LU64]] Qeynos Revamp.
- Several gnome quest NPCs and a tinkering trainer can be found here.
