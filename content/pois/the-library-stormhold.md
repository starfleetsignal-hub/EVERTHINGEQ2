- **Entrance from the rest of [[Stormhold]] at**: {{waypoint 4, -26, -128}}
- **Entrance from [[Antonica]] at**: {{waypoint -2480, -39, -233}}

## Notes

- The Library can only be reached by "teleporting" through a sewer from [[Stormhold]] on level 2.
- After entering Stormhold, you pass straight through the entrance hall, through the set of double doors and into a chamber with four doors (two either side).
- Make a left at the first 'T' intersection, {{waypoint -54.30, 5.00, -110.69}}.
- Make a right at the second 'T' intersection, {{waypoint -55.20, 5.00, -118.62}}.
- Cross over the damaged section of hallway at {{waypoint -76.79, 5.00, -119.14}}.
- Turn right at the third 'T' intersection, {{waypoint -103.55, 4.99, -122.23}} . The wooden door on the left leads to the Barracks and Captina Guard Hess' room, which is a dead-end for this venture.
- Turn left at the fourth 'T' intersection, {{waypoint -103.80, 4.99, -111.11}}. The right is a blocked tunnel.
- Going forward, the tunnel will curve around to the right.
- At {{waypoint -132.89, 5.00, -96.08}}, turn left and go through the wooden door.
- This is the entrance to the Claymore stairway - go down the stair to the bottom which is now the second level, the floor where the sewer-grate is.
- The sewer grate is at {{waypoint 5, -26, -121}} and you can now reliably follow the Waypoint wisp all the way to sewer-grate room without getting lost. It's a big room with a triple-fish statue, in the northwest of the second level, that contains zombies, white oozes, root horrors and [[Qelinatias the Scion of Pain]].
- The [maps](https://maps.eq2interface.com/map.php?zone_id=10) provided by [[EQ2MAP]] for this part of the dungeon are confusing, as the red bordered hallways don't connect to the black bordered ones as they are underneath those.
- After you get into the library, you'll also find there is a door at {{waypoint -118, -32, -182}}. This door leads out to the northeastern beach in [[Antonica]]. After you unlock and exit this door for the first time you can forever use it as entrance to Stormhold and as an exit to Antonica.
