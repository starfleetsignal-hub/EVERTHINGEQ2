## Notes

The Combine Spires that connect to the [[Ulteran Spires|Ulteran Spire Network]] are located on Spire Island. If you enter the Sentinel's Fate zones via the [[Ulteran Spires|Ulteran Spire Network]], you will automatically get this discovery ding.
