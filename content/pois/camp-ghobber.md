## Background

*Named after a great halfling marshal, this defiant halfling fort was placed in the wilds of the [[Enchanted Lands|Misty Thicket]]. Here the deputies would stand guard and watch over the activities of the goblins and offer protection and safe haven to those vale folk caught behind the great guard of the Misty Thicket. - (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Misty Thicket]])*

## Notes

- An entrance to [[The Obelisk of Lost Souls]] is found here.
