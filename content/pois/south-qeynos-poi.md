- South Qeynos was a zone by itself in the past and many quests still refer to it by name. [[South Qeynos|South Qeynos (Zone)]] and [[North Qeynos|North Qeynos (Zone)]] were both merged into [[Qeynos Capitol District]] with the release of [[Update:64|Game Update 64]].
- All the housing in Qeynos is found here.
