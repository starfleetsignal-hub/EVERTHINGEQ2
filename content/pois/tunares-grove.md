## Notes

**Tunare's Grove** is a section of The Lesser Faydark where [[Tunare]]'s influence is especially powerful.  The grove is home to [[The Great Tree]], an immense Elddar Tree sacred to Tunare and her followers.  The tree and grove are protected by aggressive faeries and dryads
