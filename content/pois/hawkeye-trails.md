## Notes

This trail is the main overland passage to the city of [[Scarstone]]. It is filled with [[A cobalt blackpaw|cobalt blackpaws]], [[An uprooted protector|uprooted protectors]], [[A protective forest watcher|protective forest watchers]] and [[A forest retaliator|forest retaliators]]. For those of you flying, watch the trees for [[A treetop entangler|treetop entanglers]] and [[A forest avenger|forest avengers]].
