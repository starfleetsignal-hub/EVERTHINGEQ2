## Background

> When the 2nd Fist of the [[Rallosian Empire]] reached its height they erected a grand city that was said to stretch from the foothills of [[Oggok]] to the greater Anaconda Valley of the Feerrott. When that empire was swallowed up by [[The Greenmist]] their grand capital was swallowed up by [[The Feerrott]]. But here can be found an extension of that great city still standing as a monument to one of the most feared empires on [[Norrath]].
>
> — [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Feerrott]]

## Notes

- The surrounding ruins are referred to as the 'Ruins of Rallos'.
- In the center of the tower is a portal to the [[The Obelisk of Lost Souls]].
