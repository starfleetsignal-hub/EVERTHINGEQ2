## Notes

- This is home of The Celestial Watch, one of the five major factions in [[Qeynos]]. The city's priests (templars, inquisitors, furies, wardens, and mystics) come here for training.
- You must pass a loading screen when using the blue teleporter at {{waypoint 532, -31, -230}} to zone inside the temple itself.
