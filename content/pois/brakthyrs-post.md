## Notes

- Must be Kindly (20,000) faction with [[Residents of Teren's Grasp (Faction)|Residents of Teren's Grasp]] to use the mender or to buy/sell from the merchant here.
- Merchant has a 46% tax - charging 46% more and paying 46% less for items you buy or sell
- Mender has a 27% tax - charging 27% more to repair your items
