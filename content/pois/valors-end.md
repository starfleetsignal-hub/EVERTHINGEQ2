## Notes

- "Valor's End is the resting place of four 'Cavalier' statues. These were some of the first statues ever commissioned to the legendary artist, *C.M. Brushwuttle*. It is said that only one of the statues actually existed here while the others were dragged with great effort and secrecy to this location. Why? Speculation has arisen that this may be the exact location of the former *Temple of Marr*, razed by the destructive wrath of the [[Overlord Lucan D'Lere|Overlord]] over 100 quatrains ago. Valor's End must surely be a sanctum to the righteous." *- (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Commonlands]])*
