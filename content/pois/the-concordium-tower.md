## Notes

![The top of The Concordium Tower, showing the three pinnacles that gave the tower its name.](images/The_Concordium_Tower_%28top%29.png)
The Concordium Tower is sometimes referred to as *The Three Towers*, or *The Tower of Three* due to the three separate pinnacles of the tower. It is also informally referred to as *The Mage Tower*. The Concordium Tower is the home of The Concordium.
