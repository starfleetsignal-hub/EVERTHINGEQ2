## Notes

**The Tower of Zet** is an obelisk of sorts found in the western end of [[The Shadowed Grove]].  It serves as a conduit between Norrath and the dimension that the shadowed men call home.

[[The Obelisk of Blight]], a 60-70 group scaled instance, can be accessed from the Tower of Zet.
