## Notes

In the northeast corner of [[Tunare's Grove]], there is a ring of fluorescent multicolor mushrooms known as the **Ring of the Fayllian Queen**.  [[Wenjii Glittermoon]], Queen of the Fayllian faeries, resides here, accompanied by the strongest of the fayllians.
