## Background

*The Guppy was once the stout sturdy sailing vessel ever engineered by the legendary [[Fiddy Bobick]]. The Guppy was built to sail the far seas and carry Fiddy and his adventuring friends to wondrous lands. Eventually Fiddy deconstructed and rebuilt The Guppy inside of [[Rivervale]] to use it as his dwelling. Ages past and after the cataclysms the halflings attempted to reconstruct the boat to cross the Sea of Mist. Unfortunately they were not shipwrights. Many halflings drowned as The Guppy sank barely a stones throw from the dock. - (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Misty Thicket]])*

The Guppy's history is, at present, unknown. It is suspected of being a pre-[[The Shattering|shattering]] ship, but it is currently populated by ghosts, presumably its crew. Rumor has it that one of the ship's crew haunts the wreckage as well, but no official sighting of this crew member has been confirmed.
