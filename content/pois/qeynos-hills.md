## Notes

- This POI was formerly synonymous with the whole of [[Antonica]], and perhaps more. But in the aftermath of [[The Rending]] and [[The Shattering]] during [[The Age of Cataclysms]], the name *Qeynos Hills* has been reduced to denote this ordinary piece of Antonican landscape.
