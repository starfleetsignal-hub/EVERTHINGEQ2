## Notes

- To reach the Aethenaeum while you're on the third floor, take a pathway from {{waypoint -502, 410, 268}}. The Aethenaeum is directly below the teleporter to the bottom floor.
- There are six scroll cases found here that provide lore concerning the Obelisk and the intentions of its inhabitants.
