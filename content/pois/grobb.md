## Notes

- Grobb is the hideout of the Clan Grobb. With sufficient faction points one can make use of the different NPCs:
  - **Mender** named [[Sewdak]] is located at {{waypoint -239, -85, -999}}. Another Mender [[Feexzak]] is at {{waypoint -466, -73, -931}}.
  - **Broker** named [[Kull]] is located at {{waypoint -478, -64, -1031}}
  - **Banker** NPCs [[Katak]] and [[Lucrzzt]] are located at {{waypoint -466, -64, -1018}}. You can access your bank with less faction (tested with -2200 for [[Clan Grobb (Faction)|Clan Grobb]])
  - **Faction Merchant** [[Lizz]] at {{waypoint -466, -64, -1005}}
  - **Crafting Sites**
    - [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]], [[Engraved Desk]], [[Stove & Keg]] at {{waypoint -471, -73, -927}}
    - [[Work Bench]], [[Woodworking Table]], [[Forge]], [[Chemistry Table]] at {{waypoint -384, -65, -1024}}
  - **Repeatable Quests** to raise faction are offered by
    - [[Warmonger Urziton]]
    - [[Belinza Skullsoup]]
    - [[Broosha Gatormarrow]]
    - [[Yazz Guttswig]]
