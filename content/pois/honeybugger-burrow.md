## Notes

*Honeyjum was known in ages past as a valuable elixir created through manipulation of jumjum, a bixie building material. The secret of honeyjum was kept by the Honeyjum family of the [[Enchanted Lands|Misty Thicket]]. Their burrow still sits in the far shire, abandoned and waiting for the return of the vale folk. - (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Misty Thicket]])*
