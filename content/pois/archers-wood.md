## Notes

- Once the training ground of early bowmen. Rumor has it that the legendary [[Holly Windstalker]] honed her skills here, marking one tree with her secret symbol. This place is now an occasional training ground for the elite division of The Qeynos Guard called The Green Hoods. *(Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to Antonica]])*
- The large tree at the center of the wood is referred to as either: *The Great Oak* or *Lighthouse Oak Tree*.
