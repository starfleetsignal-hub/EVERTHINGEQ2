## Notes

- Several quests will take Good-aligned characters to this farm, but it is also a major location for a battle during the [[Heroes' Festival Timeline|Heroes' Festival]] annual [[Live Events|live event]].
  - For a couple of weeks during the early part of November the event NPC [[Roslyn Mischeva]] will appear near here periodically and invite players to battle the "patchwork" version of [[Darathar (Heroes' Festival)|Darathar]].
