## Notes

The Village of Shin is located on the [[Isle of Mara]]. Anyone can travel to this zone by using a [[Mariner's Bell]]. To gain positive faction with the [[Far Seas Supply Division (Faction)|Far Seas Supply Division]], characters with at least level 50 in their tradeskill can visit 2 different NPCs and one clickable desk for daily quests, rush orders, and weekly tradeskill quests.

## NPCs that Offer Quests

- [[Kaisha Swifteasel]] offers unlimited repeatable quests. There is no limit on the number of these you can complete daily.
- [[Madria Varas]] offers daily quests (see her page for details).
- A desk on the upper level of the building can be clicked for a weekly quest. On mouse over, it is called [[Priority Orders: For Immediate Assignment]].
