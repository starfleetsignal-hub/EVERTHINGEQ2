## Background

> The former Deserts of Ro are not empty. Aside from the various crocodiles, caimans and sand giants, there are also a variety of small camp sites. Some of these are occupied at all seasons, while others seem to be intended for travellers. One would be wise to refrain from assuming that an empty camp is an abandoned one. The presence of various camps can be beneficial, as some of their residents are traders, willing to exchange goods for coin.
>
> — [[Bootstrutter's Trail Guide to the Desert of Ro (House Item)|Bootstrutter's Trail Guide to the Desert of Ro]]
