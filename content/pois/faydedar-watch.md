## Notes

- Faydedar Watch is named after [Faydedar](https://wiki.project1999.com/Faydedar), a water dragon who roamed the waters of [[Timorous Deep]] in the original [[EverQuest]].
- Under the docks, near the coastline, you can find many [[Harvestables (Tier 1)|Tier 1 harvest nodes]]. This is one of the fully safe spots to harvest on Tier 1 - no monsters are around to attack you.
- Carpet to [[The Sinking Sands]] can be found here, at {{waypoint 2398, 19, 1419}}.
