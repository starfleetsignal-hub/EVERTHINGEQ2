*This article is about the crafting area and Point of Interest in West Freeport. .*

The Coalition of Tradesfolke is the crafting hub of [[Freeport]] and the workshop of [[The Coalition of Tradesfolke (Faction)|The Coalition of Tradesfolke]], Freeport's tradeskill faction. In addition to the tradeskill instance (see [[The Coalition of Tradesfolke Workshop]] {{waypoint 19, -19, 85}}), there are various crafting amenities available in the Coalition of Tradesfolke building.

## Features

The crafting area outside the instance contains the following amenities:

- [[Alethea Augustine]], the Rush Order foreman {{waypoint 28.32, -12.43, 89.85}}
- [[Caius Callidus]], the Work Order foreman {{waypoint 29.48, -12.43, 89.36}}
- [[Work Order Clipboard]] x4, one in each room with crafting stations
- [[Aelia Catus]], the Fuel Merchant {{waypoint 46.53, -12.07, 89.01}}
- [[Jules Jumsinger]], the Banker {{waypoint 29.28, -18.44, 75.81}}
- [[Profallia]], the Broker {{waypoint 45.77, -12.07, 86.75}}
- [[Londiar Inygad (Qeynos)|Londiar Inygad]], who gives Adorning and Tinkering daily tasks {{waypoint 23.38, -12.43, 92.16}}
- [[Grandmaster Kawayzer]], the Trades Coordinator {{waypoint 25.61, -12.4, 81.41}}
- [[Vissa D`Dbth]], the Tradeskill Tutor {{waypoint 31.41, -18.43, 111.95}}
- [[Matthias Zoe]], the Crafting Trainer {{waypoint 30.88, -18.44, 77.45}}
  - Sells essential volumes for levels 1 through 100 and advanced volumes for levels 1 through 10
  - Gives you level 2 Artisan and the first two Artisan Essentials books for free (by speaking to him)
  - Lets you choose your tradeskill archetype and tradeskill at level 9 Artisan and 19 Craftsman/Outfiter/Scholar
- [[Jonak Harvester]], the Tradeskill Career Counsellor {{waypoint 33.92, -12.4, 104.65}}
- [[Juvena]], the faction merchant (Coalition of Tradesfolke Merchant) 1=36.36 2=-18.43 3=105.06
  - Sells house items, including house tradeskill stations for faction, status and coin
