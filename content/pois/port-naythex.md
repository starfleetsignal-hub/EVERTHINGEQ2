## Notes

- Boat goes to [[Butcherblock Mountains]].

## NPCs

- [[A Gigglegibber Goblin Gamblin' Game Vendor|Gigglegibber Goblin Gamblin' Game Vendor]] is located at the beginning and end of the docks near [[Inaill Icoryk]] and [[Harbormaster T'Krit]], respectively.
- [[Burvich|Mender Burvich]] is located about halfway down the docks
- [[Shady Swashbuckler XVIII]] sells [[Heritage Quests|Heritage Quest]] Rewards
- [[Inaill Icoryk]] sells [[A scrying stone|scrying stones]] to Evil aligned characters only
- [[The Norrathian Express]] is between Dyrina Springfyre (general goods) and [[Harold Winemaster]], east of the [[Griffon Tower]]
- NPCs related to the obsolete quests [[Maid for the Mist - Part I]] and [[Maid for the Mist - Part II|Part II]]
  - [[Captain Corrigan]] (Part II)
  - [[Daisy Winterhope]] is at the beginning of the docks (Part I)
  - [[Fisherman Aurvill]] and [[Fisherman Djaani]] are located in the tent south of the docks on the beach (Part II)
  - [[Seamist Fairy Nerina]] (and other fairies) are located east of the [[Griffon Tower]] (Part II)
