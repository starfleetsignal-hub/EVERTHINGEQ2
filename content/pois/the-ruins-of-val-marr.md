## Notes

- "The Ruins of Val'marr are all that is left of *Castle Val'marr*, built during [[The Age of Enlightenment]] to house the various orders of [[Erollisi Marr|Erollisi]] and [[Mithaniel Marr (God)|Mithaniel Marr]]. From this castle sprang the *Church of the Marr Twins*, *The Order of Marr's Fist*, and others. Much of the ruins has been stripped for its resources, but you can still see the shape of the mighty nest of valor." *- (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Commonlands]])*
