## Notes

Everstorm Bay was named after the perpetual storm clouds that hang over it.  The bay is too treacherous for boats to come any closer than Stormedge Isle, and adventurers must brave the shark-infested icy waters to reach the Everfrost mainland.  They must be particularly wary of one shark, the ancient and monstrous [[Dreadwake]].

The southern mouth of the [[Bitterwind Trench]], the main route into Everfrost's interior, can be found in the northwest edge of the bay. In the southwest is a small island with gravestones for some of the heroes of Halas' past.  In the northeast, a hard to find underwater cave{{waypoint -351, -22, 69}} provides a back door of sorts into Everfrost's interior.
