## Notes

- One of the many stomping grounds of The Sabertooth Gnolls.
- The western exit to [[Antonica]] is located here. It enters into the middle of Antonica, just east of [[Blackburrow]].
- As gnolls do not build towers, the two located here are must have been built by another race, presumably [[Qeynos|Qeynosians]].
