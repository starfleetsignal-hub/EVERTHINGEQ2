## Notes

The Bitterwind explorers have set up a small camp in a relatively sheltered bend in the Bitterwind Trench.  Quests and some minor amenities are available to adventurers passing by.

## NPCs

This is the second quest hub in Everfrost.  The following quest NPCs are located here:

- [[Thala Rohquilin]]
- [[Sarissa Icechyld]]
- [[Thyr Stormhammer]]
