## Background

*This was the humble abode of the halfing naturalist family of farmers and druids, the Tagglefoots. They were also members of the druidic guild called the Stormreapers, worshipers of [[Karana]]. They once existed inside the safety of [[Rivervale]], but decided to migrate to the banks of the Trickledown to provide protection for the [[The Isle of Three Pines|Three Pines]], sentient trees that have existed since the dawn of the vale. - (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Misty Thicket]])*
