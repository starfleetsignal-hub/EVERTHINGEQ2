Despite their name, the Mourning Lowlands are actually north and uphill from the Waning Forest.  Mist panthers have replaced the raptors and wolves as the primary predator, and are thick throughout the area.  The corrupted saytrs have built strange glyph-marked tunnels into the surrounding mountains.
