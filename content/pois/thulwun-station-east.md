## Notes

- The station is the eastern end of a destroyed bridge that used to connect to [[Thulwun Station West]] on the [[Isle of Mok]]. The eastern part of the station is an active battleground.
- The station has a griffin tower that will take you to [[Gorowyn Beach]] Outpost and [[Mok Rent]].
