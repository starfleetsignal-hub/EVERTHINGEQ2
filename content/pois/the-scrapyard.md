The Scrapyard is where old machines that the clockworks of Klak'Anon have decided are no longer useful are sent to be taken apart and salvaged for scrap.  It is a common fate for the older clockwork gnome models, and they live (so to speak) in fear of the Scrapyard's overseer [[Dreadscrew the Scrapper]].
