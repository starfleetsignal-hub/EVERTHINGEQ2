## Notes

This location is tied to *both* the spire network and the [[Druid Ring|Druid Rings]]. You can travel *to* it by the spire network or druid rings. Leaving only transports to the druid rings though.
To leave this location, click the back wall of the spire enclosure.

- For a complete list of updated locations and a map shows points that can be accessed by spires see [[Ulteran Spires]].

Ulteran Spires are typically located deeper within zones (often away from water or ports) than the [[Globe of Swift Travel]], though some zones accessible by globe can be reached by the spire.
