## Notes

- A former signal device designed by the legendary bard [[Ghost of Vhalen (Antonica)|Vhalen]]. The tower was destroyed during the [[War of Plagues]] when Vhalen used it to not only warn the entire region of invasion but also to play some of the largest [[Bard|bard]] spells ever composed. Vhalen died still tugging on the bells. *(Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to Antonica]])*
