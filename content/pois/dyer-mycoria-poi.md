## Notes

The pathways out of this chamber lead to:

- NW: first right, then left leads out; straight leads to [[Pengo]] (left at T) and Turkirk (right at T)
- E: Dyer Mycoria Germanium: South East
- W: Dyer Mycoria Germanium: West, and [[A grobin digger|grobin diggers]]

## Pronunciation

The native Fungusmen of Dyer Mycoria pronounce "dyer" as "jeyr."
