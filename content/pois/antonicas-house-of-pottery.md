## Notes

![Antonica's House of Pottery (outside)](images/Antonica's_House_of_Pottery_exterior.png) Even though the building identifies itself as a pottery store, it houses amenities for The Elddar Grove rather than pottery.  A broker, a provisioner and city merchants can be found here, as well as an alchemy table, a stove & keg, and a sewing table.
