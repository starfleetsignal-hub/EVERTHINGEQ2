## Notes

Qeynos Harbor was a zone by itself in the past and many quests still refer to it by name. [[The Elddar Grove|The Elddar Grove (Zone)]] and [[Qeynos Harbor|Qeynos Harbor (Zone)]] were both merged into [[Qeynos Province District]] with the release of [[Update:64|Game Update 64]].
