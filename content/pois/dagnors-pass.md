## Lore

Dagnor's Pass is named after Dagnor's Cauldron, a mountainous wasteland with a lake in the middle that contained two dungeons - Kedge Keep and [[The Estate of Unrest]].  During [[The Shattering]], Dagnor's Cauldron and Kedge Keep were destroyed and the lake drained into the [[Ocean of Tears]].  However, some unknown magic protected the Estate of Unrest, and it now sits on a floating island above the ocean.

Dagnor's Pass is a barren trail that leads to Unrest.  It is devoid of most life, but [[Danak Dhorentath]], prophet of [[Cazic-Thule]], has set his dreadful altar here.
