## Notes

- Enlightenment Cemetery is the olden known cemetery of the region and dates back to [[The Age of Enlightenment]], hence the name. The cemetery was constructed by residents of The Commonlands rather than the [[Republic of Freeport]], which was in its infancy at the time. The bulk of the dead were victims of a great plague recorded in the age. Some rumor that the cause of the great plague was related to the emergence of [[Innoruuk]] disciples within [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]]." *- (Source: [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Commonlands]])*
