Klak'Anon Central is the central cavern of Klak'Anon.  It consists mostly of an underground lake fed by a waterfall with a rocky shore that wraps most of the way around it. The [[King's Clockwork Castle]] is built over the middle of the lake.  Tunnels branching off from Klak'Anon Central lead to most other parts of the city.

'Klak'Anon Central' refers to the lakeshore.
