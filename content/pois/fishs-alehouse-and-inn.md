## Notes

- Fish's Alehouse and Inn is an inn in Qeynos Harbor.  It provides three-room inn rooms for adventurers both guilded and without.
- When [[Age of Discovery]] arrived, Fish's was turned into a gathering point for mercenaries seeking employment and adventurers seeking swords for hire.
