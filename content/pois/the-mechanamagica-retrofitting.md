The clockworks of Klak'Anon have been learning how to channel the forces of magic and, although it was thought to be impossible, succeeded.  Fortunately it isn't well refined - yet.  The Mechanamagica Retrofitting is where clockworks are outfitted with - well no one besides the Clockworks know exactly how the process works and they aren't telling, but the Mechanamagica Retrofitting is where the process takes place.  It is overseen by [[Master Magus Thornbolt]] and [[The Mechnomancer]]

## Notes

The Mechanamagica Retrofitting is the most difficult part of Klak'Anon.  The area is swarmed with fast spawning, wide-agro-range-having, [[A mechnamagica hunter-seeker (Klak'Anon)|mechnamagica hunter-seekers]].  Supposedly there is a switch at  that stops them from rapidly respawning, but it doesn't seem to work well.

The area is also a ring event.  There are groups of linked [[A klakdyne eliminator DCCC|klakdyne eliminator DCCC]]s and [[A mechnamagica battler|mechnamagica battler]]s.  Clear them to spawn waves of [[An activated clockwork worker|activated clockwork worker]]s, plus if the hunter-seekers currently aren't spawning this will start them up.  Once the clockworkers are disposed of, several [[A klakdyne eliminator M|klakdyne eliminator M]]s and [[The Mechnomancer]] will spawn.
