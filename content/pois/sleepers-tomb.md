The big gate down in the pit leads to:

- [[Sleeper's Tomb (Contested)]]
- [[Sleeper's Tomb: The Forgotten Catacombs (Advanced Solo)]]
- [[Sleeper's Tomb: The Forgotten Catacombs (Heroic)]]

The Portal up on the ridge leads to:

- [[Sleeper's Tomb: A Temporal Leap]] (98-100 Epic X4)
- [[Sleeper's Tomb: The Awakening]] (Heroic)
