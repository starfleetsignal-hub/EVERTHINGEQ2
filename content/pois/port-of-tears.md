## Notes

- You can reach this location by taking a [[Globe of Swift Travel]]
- Once you have reached this dock area, you can take a [[Magic Carpet]] to many other regions within Sinking Sands.
- Travel to [[Great Divide]] is also possible here if you hail the othmir emissary on the dock, though it is not the only means by which you can travel to that zone.

## Related Quests

This entire zone is laden with quests. If you explore the regions in and around the docks, you'll discover many hubs not far from this entry point.
