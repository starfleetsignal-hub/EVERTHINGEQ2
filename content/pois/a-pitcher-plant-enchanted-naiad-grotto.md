## Notes

These highlight when you *mouse over* them and they are only selectable **once**. Even though the entire plant highlights, the quest will only update if you click on the bottom of the plant as shown in the screenshot.

The [[A mosquito (Enchanted Naiad Grotto)|mosquitoes]] you encountered earlier will continue to respawn until you complete this quest and turn it in.
