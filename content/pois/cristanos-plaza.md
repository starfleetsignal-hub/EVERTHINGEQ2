## Notes

![](images/Cristanos_Plaza_Map.jpg)
**Cristanos Plaza** is also known as the **Fourth Gate**

- A. [[Hall of the Ebon Mask]] - Assassin's Guild
- B. [[Cristanos Hall]] - Citizenship Registrar
- C. [[Fiendish Familiars]] - House Pets merchant
- D. [[The Bauble]] - [[Jeweler]] supplies and [[Work Bench]]
- E. [[The Dashing Form]] - [[Tailor]] supplies and [[Sewing Table and Mannequin|Sewing Table & Mannequin]]
- F. [[Cuisine Excelsior]] - [[Provisioner]] supplies and [[Stove and Keg|Stove & Keg]]
- G. [[The Rack]] - Tavern with Food and Drink merchants
- H. [[Hex Tower]]
- I. [[Library of K'Lorn]] - The Spurned (Mage's Guild)
- J. tunnel to [[K'Nex Falls]] and [[Spires of Innoruuk]]
- K. tunnel to [[Death Grotto]] and [[Darklight Palace]]
