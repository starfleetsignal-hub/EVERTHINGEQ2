## Notes

The Royal Guardpost is one of the highest level areas in Crushbone Keep, with the mobs being mostly ^^^ Heroic and level 29-30. A named monster, [[Highguard T'Rexx]], can be found here, as can the door leading to [[The D'Vinnian Throne]].
