## Notes

This [[Griffon Tower]] is the closest to the [[The Commonlands]]. It has flights to [[Port Naythex Station]] and [[N'Marr's Ascent Station]]. To get to [[Bone Lake Station]] you must fly to [[N'Marr's Ascent Station]] and change griffons.
