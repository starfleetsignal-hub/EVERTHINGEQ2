Go to the spire and click the portal to enter. Kill all mobs until Quozz spawns. Kill the named and return to the spires to speak to [[Firiona Vie]].

## Note

- The exit from the zone may become invisible sometime during the action. If you use <code>/loc clipboard</code> to record your zone-in point then return to this point after destroying the interstice and wave your "hand" around you should be able to find it.
  - *Zone-in in my instance was at {{waypoint -32, 0, -3.3}}.*
- Alternatively, camping to character select then logging back in will effectively exit you from the zone.
- If all else fails, "call home" and then return to [[Obol Plains]] to complete the quest.
