## Notes

- NPC [[Taelonar]] wander this region.
- The area can be spotted from a distance by the pale, but vibrant blues of the trees which are a stark contrast to the washed-out pink tints and brown of the landscape in the surrounding areas. Small, purple, mystical spots of light can also be seen floating in the air from a distance.
- This area has [[Nye'Caelona (Faction)|Nye'Caelona]] flying guards; those working on the [[Kunark Ascending Crafting Timeline]] who have not completed [[The Gardens Are In Bloom]] for faction should be mindful of the surroundings, lest they get ejected from the region.
