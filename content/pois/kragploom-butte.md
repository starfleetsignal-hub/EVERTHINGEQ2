## Notes

This POI can be a little tricky to find. If you have a hard time go to {{waypoint 88, 195, 205}} and run straight north. You will be facing mountain wall and begin to climb up it. This POI is NW of the [[Druid Ring (BBM)|Druid Ring]] and SW of the yellow [[King's Rest]] dot on your map. Pretty much if you drew a straight line between those two POI you would intersect Kragploom Butte.
