## Notes

The candy for this achievement can come from the following sources:

1. Looted after killing any spooky creatures, generally associated with the American version of [Halloween](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halloween) (e.g. skeletons, werewolves, spiders, ghosts, etc.)
1. Returned to those who have the [[Artisan's Fully Trained Pack Pony]]
1. The collection, [[A Swinging Wake]], has shinies that can be *either* the needed collectibles *or* the candy needed to complete this achievement too.
- Buying candy from a broker or trading for the candy will **not** count for credit.

### Using Candy

Extra candy can be used as an event-only currency or as a crafting resource.

- See the [[Nights of the Dead Treats (Crafting Materials)]] page for details.
