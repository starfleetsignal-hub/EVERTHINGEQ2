## Notes

- Kill monsters in the [[Vesspyr Isles]], [[Obol Plains]], and [[Kunzar Jungle]] that drop a variety of [[Erollisi Day Hard Candy and Love Notes]].
- Evileye hard candy can be found in the [[Enchanted Lands]] and [[Runnyeye]] amongst [[A void watcher|void watchers]].

- You'll gather a lot of candy and notes, so check the [[Erollisi Day]] page for info on what they can be used for later.
