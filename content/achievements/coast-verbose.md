## Note

The "toppled pillar" at {{waypoint -831.17, 2.51, 794.97}} and signed by the infamous Green Hood scout "Grassblade" is a permanently marked object. It is not part of this achievement. A "fake-out" signed by Grassblade has been present in every expansion that had an Achievement for markable objects.
