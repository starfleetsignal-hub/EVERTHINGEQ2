## Notes

- The title is awarded when you have thrown and "pulped" **500** [[Pumpkin Bomb|Pumpkin Bombs]].
- Oddly enough, it starts the count over again and you get the achievement *again* when you hit the next 500.
- Pumpkin Bombs can only be obtained during the annual [[Nights of the Dead]] event.
- 1000 Pickups to max out achivement confirmed 27.10.2018 (Killeroy Thurgadin server :)
