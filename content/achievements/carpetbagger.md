## Notes

- This achievement, Part of the [[Triumph: High Keep: The Bloodless Incursion]] achievement
- Waypoints retrieved from EQ2 Furniture and some were verified 3/10/2019.
- If the carpets have been recently plundered, they will need to respawn. Respawn time is unknown. You cannot plunder a carpet if one is in your possession, either in your inventory or your house. All four must be plundered and in your possession. The carpets have been found at these waypoints.

Runner: {{waypoint -51, -78, 70}}  {{waypoint -151, -110, -32}} {{waypoint -117, -93, -30}}
Dining Rug: {{waypoint -98, -110, 50}}
Detailed: {{waypoint -124, -78, 37}}  {{waypoint -126, -128, 97}}
Round Segmented: {{waypoint -125, -110, -32}}  {{waypoint -128, -110, 88}}

The carpet found at any particular waypoint seems to vary. Occasionally, there is no carpet at a waypoint, or an unclickable carpet. The Diamond-weave High Keep Runner seems to be the rare carpet. These plunderable carpets seem to spawn somewhat like shinies - randomly and occasionally not at all. This means repeated trips into High Keep with chances decreased when other players are looting them, since the carpets are as contested as the mobs. The only upside to searching for them is that a player with a carpet in their possession cannot plunder it again.
