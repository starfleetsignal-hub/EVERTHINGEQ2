## Notes

![The ability Sober Up is granted during The Great  Pub Crawl to cancel drunk effects.](images/Sober-up-ability.jpg)

- You will get a temporary ability (can only be used during this event/this quest) called Sober Up. If you have a clear space on your hotbar it will appear. (See the main [[Brew Day]] page for more tips and tricks for sobering up during this event.)
- Since some of the bars are near the crew members for the quest [[Pressed into the Brewcrew]], you may want to also do that quest at the same time.
