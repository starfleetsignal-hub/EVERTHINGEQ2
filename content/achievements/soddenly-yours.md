- loose shingle {{waypoint -96, 16, 341}} on roof of dock keeper's hut in Port Woe
- ![Sleeping Simian markable, hard to see due to coloration](images/Sleeping_Simian_markable.png) simian {{waypoint 549, 79, 635}}  on tree limb near Y where two branches meet, light green color on light green tree limb, hard to see, look for the reddish-brown colored hands and feet
- ![Coconut location among lower browned fronds](images/Coconut_location_among_lower_browned_fronds.png) {{waypoint 166, 143, 332}} it can be on any of the same looking palm trees on that long ridge in the Sodden Uplands and moves on it's own every 10 minutes
