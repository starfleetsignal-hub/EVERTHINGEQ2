## Notes

- Kill monsters in [[Butcherblock Mountains]], [[The Lesser Faydark]], [[Greater Faydark]], just outside of [[Kelethin]], [[Loping Plains]], and [[Steamfont Mountains]] to collect a variety of [[Erollisi Day Hard Candy and Love Notes]].

- You'll gather a lot of candy and notes, so check the [[Erollisi Day]] page for info on what they can be used for later.
