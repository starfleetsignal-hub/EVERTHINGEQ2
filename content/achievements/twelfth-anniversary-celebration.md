  - [[Festive Flames in Freeport]]/[[Festive Flames in Qeynos]]
  - [[Missing Decorations in Freeport]]/[[Missing Decorations in Qeynos]]
  - [[Aggressive Advertising in Freeport]]/[[Aggressive Advertising in Qeynos]]
  - [[Heroes' Feast in Freeport!]]/[[Heroes' Feast in Qeynos!]]
- [[The Play's the Thing]] does not count towards this achievement; neither does killing the clothwork dragons.
