## Notes

This achievement is earned by completing the FULL story script within [[Freethinker Hideout]], thus activating the Fireguard and engaging [[Malkonis D'Morte (Freethinker Hideout)|Malkonis D'Morte]]. It is possible to earn this achievement within [[The Fabled Freethinker Hideout (Raid)]]. Other zones require further confirmation.
