You get the achievement after crafting all 16 items. Items are made at the crafting stations in [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]]:

- **Frostfell Stove and Keg** at Loc {{waypoint 73, -35, -1627}}
- **Frostfell Workbench** at Loc {{waypoint 333, -23, -1500}}.

You can also craft in your personal house if you own the personal house version of the [[Frostfell#Crafting Stations|Frostfell Crafting Stations]]
