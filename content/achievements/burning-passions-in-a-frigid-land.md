## Notes

- Kill monsters in [[Great Divide]], [[Eastern Wastes]], [[The Withered Lands]], and [[Cobalt Scar]] to collect a variety of [[Erollisi Day Hard Candy and Love Notes]].

- You'll gather a lot of candy and notes, so check the [[Erollisi Day]] page for info on what they can be used for later.

## Try hunt here

- Drolvarg near to [[Tower of Frozen Shadow]] at [[Icy Fingers]], Minotaur are Tizmak in [[Great Divide]]
- Ettin and Giants in [[Eastern Wastes]]
- Satyr in [[The Withered Lands]]
- Othmir and Ulthork on the beach in [[Cobalt Scar]]
