## Notes

NEW in 2025!

This achievement updates when you mark **one** holiday themed markable object per holiday. It is not necessary to mark all of the objects during each holiday, there is no additional achievement, nor reward for finding and marking them all.

These special markable objects **are visible** **only during their holiday,** in zones and areas related to that holiday's quests and activities, usually easy to reach even for lower level adventurers. They refresh 15 minutes after previous marking and are always in the same spots, there is more than one spot in each zone.
