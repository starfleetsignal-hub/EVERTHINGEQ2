This is the meta-achievement of the 4 sub-achievements listed above.  The 4 sub-achievements are all hidden and won't show up by default.

There are 3 possible ways to acquire these:

1. The final boss from the Planes of Prophecy heroic zones listed below will drop one at random
1. The Weekly PoP heroic quest AND the Weekly Fabled Guk quest each reward a [[A prized particularly potent planar part.|A prized particularly potent planar part]] allowing you to choose one of the items.  You should hold off on opening these as some of the items are rarer than others.
1. According to [this post](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/guide-planar-crafting-completionist.583192/), there may be clickable items inside the zones.  I wasn't aware of this until after I completed it, so I can't confirm if this still works.

To get to these zones, travel to the Plane of Magic on the world map.  Then take the teleportation platform to "Valor's Roost" in the south-east corner of the zone.  From there, zone into the "Coliseum of Valor".  All heroic zones listed below can be accessed from within the Coliseum of Valor.  You can also pick up the 2 weekly quests in the North-East section.  The flying book "**a Planar Chronicler**" will give you "**The Planes: Chapters 1 through 5 [Heroic I]**" and the froglok [[Griv, of The Knights of Marr|Griv, of the Knights of Marr]] will give you "**Run Amok in Fabled Guk [Heroic]**" for the Fabled Guk zones.  The zone-in for the Guk zones is back outside on Valor's Roost.  And it should be noted that the Guk zones don't drop any of the parts, but the weekly quest will, so it's still worth quickly running them.

This is the list of heroic zones and potential drops.  Of each zone, 1 of the 3 items seems to be an uncommon drop.  As of 2026, as a level 135 (mentored to 125) you can solo these zones pretty quickly (with the exception of Monolith of Fire due to zone mechanics).  They each have a zone reuse timer of 1 hour 30 mins.  You should farm these zones for the drops, then use the weekly quests to supplement the ones you're short of.
