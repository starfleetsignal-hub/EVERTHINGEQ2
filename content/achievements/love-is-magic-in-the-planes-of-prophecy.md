### NOTES

Can be found in various sections of the [[Plane of Magic]].

- Aluxob love note - found amongst [[Alux bluffwalker|alux bluffwalkers]] in the [[Pakiat Bluffs]] region.
- Djinn love note - found amongst [[An Uruvan djinn|Uruvan djinn]] in [[The Shimmering Citadel]]. Found some at /waypoint -51.70, 146.22, 56.20. You get to this waypoint via an elevator in [[The Pillars of Flame]] located at /waypoint -35.42, 92.03, 120.14.
- Pusling love note - found in [[Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands|Detroxxulous, The Plaguelands]] accessible through [[Myrist, the Great Library|Myrist, The Great Library]]. Puslings wandering about the pathway at /waypoint -54.26, 97.54, -3.42 drop these.
- Bubonian hard candy - dropped by Bubonian Infectors, Bubonian Fetors, (mobs with "bubonian" in their titles) found around the bones at /waypoint 178.33, 65.10, -378.27 in [[Detroxxulous, the Plaguelands|Detroxxulous]].<br>
