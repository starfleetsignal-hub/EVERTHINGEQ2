## Background

Long ago a noble family, the Quellborns, left Erudin to make a new life in Faydwer. [[Nicadius Quellborn]], the husband, was a priest of [[Quellious]] as well as a musician and composer. He was accompanied by his pregnant wife, [[Jessa Quellborn]]. Tragedy struck on the voyage, and Jessa, ill and near death, delivered a healthy baby girl but died soon after. Mixed with grief and joy, Nicadius named the girl [[Spirit of Jessa Quellborn-Ruksif|Jessa]], after his deceased wife. They reached Faydwer safely and established their home in the remote southern mountain range of [[Butcherblock Mountains]] known as Dagnor's Cauldron. The body of Lady Jessa was buried on the grounds, her grave consecrated to Quellious, and a gazebo was later built on the spot. They named their new home *The Estate of Rest*, establishing it as a place of rest and recovery for those in need of respite from the dangers of the Mountains, and those weary of the world.

Jessa grew in peace and beauty, nurtured by her father, the senior servants Hemstall and Ryline, and above all, Quellious. In time, she reached maturity, then eventually met, and was courted by and wed to a dwarf of [[Kaladim]], [[Ghost of Kyll Ruksif|Kyll Ruksif]]. He loved her greatly, and she him, and soon she was with child. Much happiness was brought to the mansion as servants readied the new nursery in anticipation. In his happiness, Nicadius was composing a lullaby for his first grandchild, but he never got to finish it...

One dark night while Kyll was away from the castle, [[Garanel Rucksif, the Cursed|Garanel Rucksif]] came to put an end to them all. Fueled by envy, hatred, and a liberal helping of strong dwarvish spirits, he crept into the mansion and slaughtered everyone. The last to die was Nicadius himself, who had been standing vigil that night in the chapel above the conservatory. Perhaps Nicadius cried out in that final moment, for as the madman struck him down Quellious came, and in Her ire cursed Garanel to forever haunt the scene of his evil, and Garanel dropped dead on the spot.

Someone must have buried the victims behind the mansion, but there were no blessings placed on the cursed ground. Perhaps Garanel drove them off before they could finish. In any event, Kyll never made it home, at least in life, but his ghost stands forever in the yard trying to get to Jessa to save her despite his wounds.

Recently, drawn by the dark energies of this haunted mansion, a priest of [[Cazic-Thule]] has come to torture the eternal residents, and after making a few threats to Garanel to allow him to, the priest defiled the chapel and made Fear supreme there. It is unclear if he even knows that Garanel needs little help to twist forever in the evil winds of his own deeds. Deep beneath *The Estate of Unrest* he waits, and watches...

## Notes

- The Estate of Unrest zone went live with LU32 as a free content addition to Echoes of Faydwer, and became the second ever [[Persistent Instance]] with [[LU38]].
- Unlike most zones where quests exist in the zone, this zone IS the quest, after a fashion. It is like an old Zork or Myst game, where various items related to the storyline lead you through unlocking the path until you reach the final encounter.
- You will have a debuff called ***Living Nightmare*** on you at all times while in the instance, which slows your movement speed by 20%.
- If soloing, you will require the help of at least one other person to gain access to the second floor of the mansion. Two levers have to be clicked at the same time, and they are too far away for one player to reach the other in time. Additionally, if your class does not have a way to teleport forward (or on top of an enemy), then you may get stuck on a [[The Festering Hag|particular encounter]].
- There is one exceedingly rare quest that can be found here, [[Reinforcing a Relic]], started from [[Leather Insoles|an item]] that drops off the final boss.
- For an (overly) detailed guide to the zone, please click [[The Estate of Unrest/Verbose|here]]. If further interested in the lore of the zone, click [[The Estate of Unrest/Lore|here]].

## Walkthrough

1. Pick up [[King's Field Pawn]] in the fountain. {{waypoint 8, 1, 2}}
1. Recover the two halves of the keys that will grant you entrance in to the mansion. One half will spawn in each of the two hedge mazes. The spawns are semi-random, but confirmed spawns are:
   - {{waypoint -64, 0, 23}}
   - {{waypoint 110, 0, 45}}
1. Defend [[Ghost of Kyll Rucksif]] by killing the [[A relentless bonegrasp|relentless bonegrasps]] that spawn around him and slowly walk towards him. After several have been killed, he will ask for you to speak to him and will give you [[Kyll's Spectral Hammer]]. {{waypoint -78.88, -0.62, 129.29}}
   - If you accidently kill him, he will respawn after several minutes. Alternatively, you can speak with [[Jessa Quellborn]] in [[The Hallowed Gazebo]] after killing the Ghost of Kyll Rucksif and she will give you the hammer. {{waypoint 109, 3, 95}}
1. Enter [[The Graves of Unrest]], kill [[Irritated Chef Demme]] and loot [[Chef Demme's Soothing Supper]]. {{waypoint -78, 0, 244}}
1. Kill the [[A hungering hellhog|hungering hellhogs]] and loot the [[Cursed Swine Meat]] from their corpses. {{waypoint 89, 0, 234}}
1. Pick up [[King's Field Pawn]] from the moat. {{waypoint 100, -10, 138}}
1. Enter the mansion. {{waypoint 8, 4, 102}}
1. Pick up [[King's Field Pawn]] from the kitchen (inside a glass). {{waypoint 1, 4, 160}}
1. Loot [[Rotted Vegetables]] from the kitchen cupboard. {{waypoint 31, 4, 161}}
1. Craft [[Soothing Supper]], using the rotted vegetables and cursed swine meat, at the stove in the kitchen. {{waypoint 6, 4, 160}}
1. Deposit Soothing Supper in the dumbwaiter. {{waypoint 31, 4, 171}}
1. Go to the bar area and kill all the [[A doomed visitant|doomed visitants]]. {{waypoint -9, 4, 165}}
1. Kill [[The Sadistic Bartender]] when he spawns and pick up the [[Very Watered Down Cocktail]] that spawns on the bar counter. Use it on the nearby blue fire and [[Unrest Backdoor Key]] will spawn on the ground nearby. {{waypoint -10, 4, 186}}
1. Pick up [[King's Field Pawn]] from the bar counter (inside a glass). {{waypoint -13, 4, 163}}
1. Leave the mansion and enter the *Game Room* through the backdoor. {{waypoint 27, 4, 197}}
1. Place the four chess pieces on the chessboard. {{waypoint 31, 4, 180}}
1. Kill [[Screwloose the Mad Tinkerer]] and loot the two [[Screwloose's Lever Screws]] that drop in his chest.
1. Add the lever screws to the levers at {{waypoint -16, 23, 104}} and {{waypoint 32, 23, 106}}.
1. Together with another player, activate the levers at the same time.
1. Go to the foyer and kill [[Torklar Battlemaster]]. {{waypoint 8, 8, 120}}
1. Pick up the [[Oil Can]]. {{waypoint -18, 12, 117}}
1. Use Kyll's Spectral Hammer on the door at {{waypoint -10, 12, 157}}.
1. Clear the room of all mobs and then speak with [[Spirit of Jessa Quellborn-Rucksif]]. {{waypoint -21, 12, 178}}
1. Speak with [[Nicadius Quellborn]]. {{waypoint 10, 24, 133}}
1. Use the oil can on the lever and pull it. {{waypoint 14, 24, 179}}
1. Speak with [[Jessa Quellborn]] to receive [[Jessa's Locket]]. {{waypoint 109, 3, 95}}
1. Return to Spirit of Jessa Quellborn-Rucksif and give her the locket. You will receive [[Nursery Door Key]].
1. Unlock the nursery door and speak with *a spirit child*. {{waypoint -17, 12, 138}}
1. Kill [[Billy (Unrest)|Billy]], who spawns after killing the [[A billy doll|billy dolls]].
1. Loot [[Incomplete Hymn of Tranquility]] from the bookcase in the nursery.
1. Return to Nicadius Quellborn and he will give you [[Completed Hymn of Tranquility]].
1. Play the Completed Hymn of Tranquility on the piano. {{waypoint 5, 12, 174}}
1. Kill [[The Priest of Fear]] in [[The Defiled Chapel]] and loot [[Polished Symbol of the Faceless]] from his corpse. {{waypoint 32, 24, 193}}
1. Use the Polished Symbol of the Faceless on the bookcase back on the first floor. {{waypoint -17, 4, 139}}
1. Kill all the [[A mangled mass of corpses|mangled massess of corpses]] in [[The Torture Chamber of the Damned]]. {{waypoint 52, -23, 207}}
1. Kill [[The Festering Hag]].
1. Kill [[The Hemogoblin]]. {{waypoint 2, -23, 234}}
1. Enter [[The Heart of Unrest]], slay all [[A shard of Garanel|shards of Garanel]] in the chamber and loot the bones of Garanel from the chests that they drop (seven in total). {{waypoint -24, -32, 67}}
1. Add the bones to the corpse of Garanel. {{waypoint -10, -30, 68}}
1. Slay [[Garanel Rucksif, the Cursed]], and bring him to justice in the name of the [[#Background|Quellborn family]]!
