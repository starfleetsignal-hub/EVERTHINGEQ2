## Notes

Originally, all new players started on the same island, *Isle of Refuge*, but depending on which city you chose to start in, the Isle inhabitants of the Isle reacted differently to you. As the game was subscription only in the beginning the Isle also functioned as a trial area for everyone, if you wanted to leave you had to pay for a subscription. In [[LU19]] they made these two distinct islands:

- [[Queen's Colony]], for good-aligned characters
- [[Outpost of the Overlord]], for evil-aligned characters

With the release of [[Echoes of Faydwer]], players could choose [[Kelethin]] and start in the [[The Nursery]] area of [[Greater Faydark]] in addition to the two islands and as of [[LU35]], the city of [[Neriak, City of Hate]] was added to the list.

The original starting quests remain in the completed section of the journal for players who completed them. Those quests which were moved to Queen's Colony or Outpost of the Overlord also moved journal sections, and are listed on those pages.
