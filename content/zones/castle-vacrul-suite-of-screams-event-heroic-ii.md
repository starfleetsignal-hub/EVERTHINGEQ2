## Prerequisites

*To enter, you must have completed [[Visions of Vetrovia: Vacrul Intentions]] or have earned the [[VoV Flag: Castle Vacrul Dungeons Unlock]].

## Walkthrough

1. 1st named: have group members drink as called, keep moving every 10 seconds or memwipe shuffle (doesn't apply to tanks)
1. 2nd named: click proper bookcases, use fighter intercept on cursed player, stop all dots on reflect.
1. 3rd named: there will be 4 circles, when you get a curse, there will be an emote stating that one of the races are screaming, you need to go into the correct circle to get curse cured otherwise the curse can bounce to another player. Kamapor is the centaurs, pygmies are karuupa jungle, the kyv archers are svarni, and the witches are mahngavi. There will be an add that spawns near the back of the room at the purple curtain, if the add is not killed in time, the dog will wake up and wipe the group. Do not use any implosion or hook shots on the adds, as this will end in the same result. Keep interrupts on the boss as it will full heal itself (confirmation of spell name needed).
