## Notes

This zone is only accessible to players on certain steps of specific quests.  NPC and monster population varies based on which quest is being used to access it.

It is strictly a solo zone.  Players cannot take mercenaries into it.

The zone teems with aggressive undead.  NPC Erudites near the gate will attack any undead dragged towards them; players in over their heads can exploit this.
