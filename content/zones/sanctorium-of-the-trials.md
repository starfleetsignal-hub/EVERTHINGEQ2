![Chamber of the Unavailing](images/Sanc2.png)

- **Chamber of the Unavailing** for the quest, [[The Cursed]]

![Hold of the Fallen](images/Sanc3.png)

- **Hold of the Fallen** for the quest, [[The Champion]]
