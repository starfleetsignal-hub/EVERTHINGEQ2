## Geography

### Getting to the zone

- To reach the zone for the **first time** you have to complete the first Quest [[Kunark Ascending: Beyond the Veil]]
- use the [[Cae'Dal Star]] from the preorder
  - to teleport to the Crusader Cave as long as you don't have +10k Faction with [[Nye'Caelona (Faction)]]
  - to teleport to [[Nye'Caelona]], once you have +10k Faction with [[Nye'Caelona (Faction)]]
- use a Spire to teleport to the Crusader Cave
- Once the quest [[Kunark Ascending: A Nightmare Resolved]] is completed you can use the teleporter in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]] at {{waypoint -66.81, 11.07, 184.95}} near the entrance to Arcanna'se Spire Instances to travel to [[Nye'Caelona]]
- In [[Kylong Plains]] is a cave on the right before you get to [[Jarsath Wastes]] at {{waypoint 2325.21, 161.17, -2926.67}}
- In [[Jarsath Wastes]] is a cave on the left before you get to [[Kylong Plains]] at {{waypoint -1258.84, -12.29, -708.24}}
- Tradeskillers can use the tunnel in [[Fens of Nathsar]] at
- Tradeskillers can use the [[Twark Transport Totem]] to teleport to Twark *(the Goblin area)*

### Getting out of the zone

- Once the quest [[Kunark Ascending: A Nightmare Resolved]] is completed you can use the teleporter in [[Nye'Caelona]] at {{waypoint -213, 90, -166}} to travel to [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep]]
- When you go the Crusader cave at {{waypoint 808.02, 249.84, 1314.52}}
  - to the South (left) you can zone to [[Kylong Plains]]
  - to the North (right) you can zone to [[Jarsath Wastes]]
- Tradeskillers can use the tunnel at {{waypoint -318, 48, 604}} to travel to [[Fens of Nathsar]]
- Talk to [[Raekis]] in the Crusader Cave for a Spire

### Traveling within the Zone

- There is a teleporter connection between the center of the tower at  and the inner City
- There is a teleporter connection between the first outer platform of the City  and the Gardens
- Tradeskiller's can use the [[Twark Transport Totem]] to teleport to Twark *(the Goblin area)*

### Travel to Connected Zones

The [[Crusader's Cave]] connects to two zones that can be used while working on Kunark Ascending quests.

At the far end of the cave, a tunnel splits in two directions to access the following:

- [[Jarsath Wastes]] - zone-in point at 826 262 1,244, which is useful for the [[The Gardens Are In Bloom]] quest when traveling to [[Danak Shipyards]].
- [[Kylong Plains]] - zone-in point at 809 247 1,357
