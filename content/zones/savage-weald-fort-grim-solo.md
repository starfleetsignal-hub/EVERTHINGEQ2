## Walkthrough

1. Kill [[Ulosi and Ulcine Nacisroc]] {{waypoint -28, 43, -273}}.
   - *Joust out of the white circles the twins cast on the ground, and kill adds as they come in, with AOEs if possible.*
1. Kill [[Chief Gadzuuks]] {{waypoint 111.10, 50.93, -212.25}}.
   - *Joust out of the white circles the twins cast on the ground, and kill adds as they come in, with AOEs if possible.*
1. Kill [[Felnir the Shadow Prowler]] and [[The Dire Pyre]] {{waypoint 70, 31, -285}}.
   - *Felnir cannot be targeted. Use AOEs, and target his [[A moor wolf|moor wolf]] adds and kill them, both deal damage to him.*
   - **GUIDE for missunderstanders!** 1. Slay adds near campfire - it will burn harder and harder. 2. Damage main wolf.
