## Steps

1. Defeat [[Inquisitor Soronigus]] at {{waypoint -3, 13, -12}}
   - Simply burn Mob
1. Defeat [[Sacrificer Aevila D'Serin]] at {{waypoint 428, -11, -23}}
   - During fight you get a curse. After 10 ticks instant death. To prevent enter the blood pool at {{waypoint 503.43, -1.78, -23.54}} around the [[Altar of Blood]] to survive the "last" tick. WARNING: If you stay to long in the blood pool you will explode...so in and out and in...
1. Defeat [[Virtuoso Edgar V'Zann]] at {{waypoint -511, 0, -23}}
   - During fight [[Virtuoso Edgar V'Zann]]  starts to play a melody. Now you have to play the bone organ within 12 sec (source: https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/virtuoso-edgar-vzann-ossuary-cathedral-of-bones-raid.582197/)
     - Fluidic Ballad - CDDFGGFF - Song of Fire.
     - Conflagration Ballad - AGAGABCB - Song of Ice.
     - Glacial Ballad - DDCBCDDF - Song of Water.
1. Defeat [[Protector of Bones]] {{waypoint 0, 0, -219}}
1. Defeat [[Rimordial Ritualist Villandre V'Zher|Primordial Ritualist Villandre V'Zher]] {{waypoint 0, 0, -220}}
1. A Portal opens ( i couldnt interact with it)
