Walkthrough

Entry level zone: you should be fine in most ToV gear or AoM quest gear.

Zone trash specifics: Raveners cast a reflective shield, so if they have it up in front of them, stop attacking them. Augurs have a heal, Ashen Cleansing, if low on dps try and interrupt this.

Run up the ramp to the left.  Cross the bridge to the right. Straight across next bridge. Take the right (NE) bridge.

### **Pterotrainer Yu'lua**

Located at: {{waypoint -3575.15, 11.62, -81.32}}  Cure curse, be mindful of knockback from adds. If you do get knocked down a level there is a teleporter you can take to put you back at the start of the zone.

Take the bridge to the West, into the tunnel.  If you go around the rock to the right you'll see the next bridge is blocked by a firewall.

Take the ramp to the right (that goes up steeply) and defile the statue at {{waypoint -3438.72, 66.75, -143.15}}   This removes the firewall.

### **High Priest Hamanu'akaloa**

Over the former firewall bridge, around to the right, located at: {{waypoint -3393.64, 12.11, -82.79}}  Kill adds as they spawn. Interrupt the names big heal, 'Magma for Blood'.

After killing the High Priest, head left, you'll see two ramps.  Take the left.

### **Fiery Effigy of Clotl'thoa**

Location: {{waypoint -3344.71, 10.52, 73.13}}  He has an incremental damage reduction buff on him called Lavatar. which starts at 4 increments. At about 8 increments (depending on your group set up) drag him out of the lava to let the increments drop back to 2. You cannot damage him at this point. While out of lava, adds slowly join the fight, kill the "fanatic Allu'thoa" or they will heal the named. (they get one-shotted by arrows). When he hits 2 increments again drag him back into the lava and dps. Rinse and repeat until he's dead.

As his increments increase so does his damage. Beyond 8 his pulse damage can 1 shot the group

To leave the zone, click on the pterodactyl.
