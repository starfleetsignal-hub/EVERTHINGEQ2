## Background

[[The Lady of Insurrection]], the demi-goddess of treachery, has tasked you with killing creatures that inhabit the [[Shard of Love: A Moment of Valor|Shard of Love]], and planting *Seeds of Distruct* within [[Erollisi Marr]]'s plane.

## Notes

- **Shard of Love: Seeding Corruption** was introduced as part of [[Erollisi Day]] 2013. It is accessible only by adventurers on the quest [[Tainted Love]].
