Home of the peaceful and reclusive Wantia people, the Village of Shin sits nestled on the southwestern shores of the [[Isle of Mara]]. Protected from the looming threat of the [[Pirates of Gunthak (Faction)|Pirates of Gunthak]] by the monks of the [[The Whistling Fists Clan (Faction)|Whistling Fists Clan]], the Wantia spend their days tending to their village and caring for the [[The Mystic Guardians (Faction)|native inhabitants]] of the island. Recently, outsiders from the [[Far Seas Supply Division (Faction)|Far Seas Supply Division]] have landed on the island seeking to establish trade relations with the Wantia.

## Background

The Village of Shin is the the starting zone of the Fallen Dynasty adventure pack. On regular servers, it is accessed via any [[Mariner's Bell]], listed as the [[Isle of Mara]]. On TLE Servers Shin is only accessible through the globes on the docks of [[Nektulos Forest]] and [[The Thundering Steppes]]. Previously, the quest [[The Far, Far Away Far Pelican]] was needed to access the Isle of Mara, but is no longer necessary. The outpost nearest the dock is operated by the Far Seas Company, and provides basic city services including brokers and a banker.
