## Background

You have laid siege to [[New Tunaria|Felwithe]] and all that stands before you is the [[The Royal Court|throne room]] of [[Queen Lenya Thex (Named)|Queen Lenya Thex]]. The most elite of the Renda'Dal can be found here, guarding their queen with great fervor.

## Notes

- If soloing, mentor down to adventure level 85.
- Accessible at adventure level 53.
