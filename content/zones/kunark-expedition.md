This instance is accessed from a ship's bell in [[East Freeport]] or [[Qeynos Harbor]], and is only available if you are on the quest [[Joining the Crew of the Bane of Prexus]] or [[Joining the Crew of the QSS Valiance]].

The zone is the deck of a ship, either the [[Bane of Prexus]] or the QSS [[Valiance]], at sea. The rolling waters on which the ship sails, visible from the decks, may cause discomfort to people susceptible to motion sickness.

This zone is part of the [[Sarnak Rumors Timeline]]
