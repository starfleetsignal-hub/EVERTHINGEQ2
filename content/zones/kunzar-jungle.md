Like all the zones of [[Kunark (Continent)|Kunark]], Kunzar Jungle was named after the pre-empire Iksar tribe of the same name. It is an immense and hostile jungle, filled with all manner of dangerous inhabitants. Since the fall of the [[Iksar Empire]], most of the outlying towns and cities have been reclaimed by the jungle. Only Torsis, now known as the [[The City of Mist|City of Mist]] remains largely untouched, although the living no longer dwell there. In events obscured by time, the city became cursed and all of its inhabitants fell dead without just cause.

Kunzar Jungle can be treacherous for an outdoor zone. There are many factions that will initially be hostile scattered throughout the zone.  In addition, Sathirians patrol the roads. More dangerous is [[Zelgad the Western Lord]], an Order of Rime affiliated Epic x2. [[The City of Jinisk]], a merchant town in the center west of the zone, is rather easy to build good faction with. To the north are the [[Ry'zilk's Renegades (Faction)|Ry'zilk's Renegades]], an army of Di'Zok, and the [[Hidden Plunderers' Camp (Faction)|Hidden Plunderers]], a group of Iksar working to interfere with [[Venril Sathir]]'s interests in the [[The City of Mist|City of Mist]] (and make a profit while doing so). Near the middle of the zone is [[The Tabernacle of Pain]] where the [[The Swifttail Caste (Faction)|Swifttail Monks]] continue to try and uphold their ancient traditions. Deep in the south lies the city of [[Sebilis]], the former seat of power of the Iksar Empire and domain of [[Venril Sathir]]. [[Dalnir's Wheel]] provides fresh water to the city and is worked by the enslaved frogloks of [[Synod Reet (Faction)|Synod Reet]].

Kunzar Jungle is the remnant of the [[EverQuest|EQ1]] zones of [Emerald Jungle](https://wiki.project1999.com/Emerald_Jungle), [Trakanon's Teeth](https://wiki.project1999.com/Trakanon%27s_Teeth), and [City of Mist](https://wiki.project1999.com/City_of_Mist).

### Travel

Kunzar Jungle can be accessed overland via the [[Fens of Nathsar]] in two different spots:

- The game-encouraged way for adventurers of the appropriate level is to follow the road in Fens of Nathsar that runs east from [[Riliss]]. Adventurers entering this way will arrive on the outskirts of [[The City of Jinisk]] where they can immediately begin working on the zone questlines.
- Alternatively, the zone can also be accessed via the river gorge in southern Fens of Nathsar. This route will lead adventurers to [[Murkdweller's River]], where the [[The Order of Rime (Faction)|Order of Rime]] has set up a camp.

Once inside Kunzar Jungle, there are [[Sokokar|Sokokar Posts]] that can you to every major location.

### Towns

Once sufficient faction is achieved, [[The City of Jinisk]] in the center-west and [[Outer Sebilis]] in the southeast have between them most amenities an adventurer would need, including brokers, bankers and menders.  Generally speaking, Jinisk will be the first of many factions that adventurers will reach good terms with and Outer Sebilis Residents will be the last.

## Quests

*Main article: [[Kunzar Jungle Timeline]]*
Kunzar Jungle has many questlines affiliated with the many factions:

- City of Jinisk timeline starts with [[Rug Deliveries]] for [[Trader Hisk Varn]].
- Ry'zilk's Renegades timeline starts with [[Simple Scouting Mission]] for [[Field Chief Ry'zilk]].
- Dalnir's Wheel Taskmasters timeline starts with [[Swatting the Pests]] for [[Foreman Vink]].
- Hidden Plunderers timeline starts with [[Prove Your Allegiance]] for [[Skulker Traz]].
- Synod Reet timeline starts with [[For the Love of Reet]] for [[Assistant Pubbit]].
- Adventurers will also find smaller questlines that raise faction with the Kunzar Jungle Villagers scattered throughout the zone.
