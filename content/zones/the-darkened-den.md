## Background

Rumor has it that there is a cave surrounding [[Titan's Tower]] that not even the nearby Sullon Orcs will enter. You must investigate.

## Notes

- The entrance is not always up if someone has cleared it recently. You must wait several minutes for it to respawn at one of the given locations.
- You may reenter this instance as many times as you like, until the door vanishes and you must find it again. The outside of the door will always be guarded by a [[A tainted scorpion (Zek)|tainted scorpion]]
- There is a [[Heroic: The Darkened Den|heroic]] version also.
