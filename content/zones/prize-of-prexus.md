## Background

The **Prize of Prexus**, was the first ship to make regular passage trips to the island of [[Zek]] after [[The Shattering]]. She required a major retrofit due to damage from Deathfist orc warships, requiring a retrofit spearheaded by [[Engineer Ximmix Wobblecog]]. Until recently, she regularly was attacked en route to [[Zek]].

## Notes

- Similar to the instance, [[The Maid for the Mist]], this instance can only be re-accessed via the [[Mariner's Bell]] in a guild hall once the quest has been completed.
