Every room is locked behind a barrier. These are lowered by continuing on the ramp until you find Zek-Idol. Destroy it, it'll spawn a whisp. Follow it and right click the barrier once its reached its respective room.

Ragebourne MacKulla<br>
Ring event.  Several waves of adds spawn before finally spawning the named.  Unknown strategy, one shot.

Rageburner , Ragefroster , Ragecrusher , Triad of Elements<br>Pull the Priests, making sure you DO NOT one-shot them on pull. When they gather in the center of the room, "bubbles" will spawn at each of the three pedestals and start floating towards the center. Kill the bubbles before they reach the center or the script will reset.  There will be three waves, so nine bubbles in total.

Move away from the white crystal that will form after each round of three bubbles. It will explode, causing trauma/arcane detriments.

Each bubble killed damages its respective named, so after the three rounds they'll die without you having to attack them.  They will then combine into the Triad of Elements.  **Do NOT kill anything before it has fully combined** - otherwise you'll run into the same bug as below.

Destroy the standards on the pedestals. Then you can kill the Triad.

<br><b>**WARNING** - Rage priests can be killed with one shot on pull, which will bug the encounter as the two remaining Rage priests will not be able to combine with the third.</b>
