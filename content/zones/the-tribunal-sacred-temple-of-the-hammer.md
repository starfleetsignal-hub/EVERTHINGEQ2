## Background

There is one last task before you to return [[The Tribunal]]'s guidance to [[Norrath]]. You must listen to the facts and determine the guilt or innocence of the accused party. Your actions up to this point have proven your faith in the Tribunal. Now, you must demonstrate that you've learned enough about justice.

## Notes

- If you accidently zone out, speak with [[Justinian Theo]] again to zone back in.
