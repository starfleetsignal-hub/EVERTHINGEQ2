## Background

[[Gomp Soilscratch]] has enlisted your help in eradicating a rock crawler infestation near to [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den|Splitpaw Den]]. If left unchecked, these spiders will overrun the Den before the Underpaw do!

## Walkthrough

*See:[[Crawlers Extermination (The Crawler Nest)]]*

## Notes

- This instance holds from 1 to 6 players.
- Like all Splitpaw Saga zones, the monsters scale to match your level, between 20 and 50.
- Stepping on the [[An egg sac|egg sacs]] strewn about the instance will spawn [[A rock crawler hatchling|rock crawler hatchlings]], which if enough are spawned can quickly overrun even a mentored down character.
- As soon as the ring-event starts, the passage back up to the [[Terraporter]] will be blocked off. When you finish the instance you must teleport out by your own means.
