## Prerequisites

*To enter, you must be running [[Visions of Vetrovia: Vacrul Intentions]] or have earned the [[VoV Flag: Castle Vacrul Dungeons Unlock]].*

### Note if doing the Signature quest

Before entering The Craw you will need to gather [[An allelopath blightsnail]] to lift the *weedwhip strangulation* curse. The snails spawn all along the tunnel to Skelegore and Bonewretch and can be tracked. They only appear to (and are required by) those doing the Signature quest [[Visions of Vetrovia: Vacrul Intentions]].

## Walkthrough

1. [[Sypheria the Shackled]] {{waypoint -154, -73, 265}}
   - Tank and spank + kill chain adds.
1. [[Skelegore the Regurgitated]] and [[Bonewretch the Expelled]] {{waypoint -291, -82, 253}}
   - Tank and spank.
   - cure debuffs to get released from the pools
1. [[Mazza Zanzeer]] (priestess) and [[Porzo Kel'Kanna]] {{waypoint 12, -15, 176}}
   - Enter The Craw at {{waypoint -41, -63, 196}} (straight across from where you originally entered the zone) and climb the stairs.
   - Tank and spank for the most part.  Mazza will not take damage until Porzo is dead. AOE the adds.
   - Exit The Craw at  {{waypoint 4.32, -53.31, 158.08}}  (lower exit door) to access the Royal Blood Halls and Cruor Keep.
     - *Note if doing the Signature quest: Vishra appears by the Royal Bloods Halls exit; check that your journal deems you to have "seen" him before continuing.*
1. [[Lysander Mistmoore]] {{waypoint -60, -45, -7}}.
   - Spawns once you've cleared the room. May take a moment to pop.
   - The statues aggro one-by-one during the fight; kill as they pop. If more than one is up you will die.
1. [[Vorigan Mistmoore]] {{waypoint -194, -60, -208}}
   - Hail the named to start fight. Interrupts can be used to stop his casting.
   - **Bloody Buffet** - Run over the blood boils that appear to remove his incremental stoneskin.
   - **Vampiric Hemorrhage** - Joust out of range or use an interrupt when he casts. If it hits you there is a knockback and a fast-acting trauma that will kill you if not cured very quickly.
     - Seems to be preceded by him leaping into the air. An interrupt at this point works nicely.
     - Staying near the far wall may help. However the knockback throws you in random directions, sometimes while in mid-air, so is very hard to correct for.
