The hooluk nest lands, previously shrouded in secrecy have been opened to adventurers who may help with the danger posed.

## Geography

### Travel

- In order to initially access Splendor Sky Aerie via Uleran Spire, Travel Globe, or Quick Travel, (regardless if over-level or not), players must complete the very brief Zimaran Timeline intro quest [[Ballads of Zimara: The Aerie Overture|The Aerie Overture]] from [[Baoko]] in [[Barren Sky]]. This will flag the player's entire account, allowing travel access to The Aerie for all characters after.
