## Walkthrough

### [[Embodiment of Fright]] (T1)

This boss is found in Feldar Farm, just north of the main entrance. To spawn the boss, all ten [[A Blightswarm swarmer|Blightswarm swarmers]] in the farmland must be killed. After the boss is killed, a Blightswarm mount appears at {{waypoint 697.97, 114.86, -468.27}} to carry the raid to the Saltscorch Steppes.

### [[Kandti]] (Bonus T1)

Kandti is summoned by using a [[Golden Whistle]] and can only be summoned once per instance.

### [[Tsmang Blightswarm Broodmaster]] (T2)

After defeating the Embodiment of Fright, [[Tsmang Blightswarm Broodmaster]] can be found inside the Tsmang Blightswarm Hive, not far from where the previous transport lands.

### [[Malformed Prophet]] (T2)

After defeating the Broodmaster, the [[Malformed Prophet]] can be found in or around Kren Rommol.

### [[Lockmaw]] (T2)

After defeating the Prophet, [[Lockmaw]] can be found in the Salt Mire.

### [[Beckut, the Mad Zealot]] (T3)

After defeating Lockmaw, [[Beckut, the Mad Zealot]] can be found roaming Dregseep Valley and Sandscoured Coast.

### [[Raaijs Viruniq]] (T3)

The high priest [[Raaijs Viruniq]] is protected by five power anchors (one for each previous boss in the zone). Each can be disabled only after their corresponding boss has been killed.

1. {{waypoint 611.92, 106.35, -623.20}} in Saltscorch Steppes ([[Embodiment of Fright]])
1. {{waypoint 442.55, 115.95, -470.69}} in Saltscorch Steppes ([[Tsmang Blightswarm Broodmaster]])
1. {{waypoint -245.15, 21.90, -379.28}} in Dregseep Valley ([[Malformed Prophet]])
1. {{waypoint 174.98, 5.60, -246.67}} in the Salt Mire ([[Lockmaw]])
1. {{waypoint -462.48, -1.44, 55.97}} in Sandscoured Coast ([[Beckut, the Mad Zealot]])

Once all five power anchors have been disabled, the carpet at Eye's Sacrifice {{waypoint -311.22, 10.71, -119.19}} becomes active and carries the raid to the Mouth of Prexus, where Raaijs can be found.
