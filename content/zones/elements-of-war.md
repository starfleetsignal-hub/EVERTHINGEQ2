## Walkthrough

Upon zoning in there will be three groups of wraiths spawned that are attackable and named to the north that cannot yet be agro'd having an audience with General Teku and Rallos Zek.

Kill the three groups of wraiths, avoiding getting too close to ANY of the named as it will begin the script to spawn the first named in the cycle.

### "Your presence is no longer a secret! Prepare to be attacked."

Once you see this text across the screen, General Teku starts the script for the series of ring event encounters.

[[Glykus Meldron]]

- During the fight occasionally [[A petrified crusher]] will spawn which can proc a debuff to force you to target them instead of Glykus. We had enough DPS to just focus fire on Glykus and have the tank hold agro on the adds. They despawn on Glykus' death.

[[Terrinon Bloodclaw]], [[Janus Furywings]], [[Meldin Talonmender]]

- Burn down Janus and Meldin first, then focus on Terrinon.
- During the fight, a large wind crystal will spawn that the group should move away from while continuing DPS on the named. Groups of [[A winged assaulter]] and [[A winged aggressor]] will also spawn. If you have enough DPS you can focus fire Terrinon down as the tank holds agro of the adds.

[[Hinvat Deathbringer]]

- Tank and spank. Occasionally ghostly [[A cloaked corruption]] will spawn and come to aid Hinvat. They heal to full health if you try to switch over to kill them so keep focus on Hinvat, curing as necessary.

The General seethes with anger, calling on his last champion to fight you.

[[Decorin Bonewing]]

- Endurance fight mixed with the floor is lava.
- During the fight text will pop across the screen stating ***massive steam rift opens at the feet of PLAYER!*** - the group needs to move away from that spot and be mindful that the "steam rift" will not despawn, even once the boss is dead. There will likely be more than one of these throughout the course of the fight so suggest the group huddles together until the prompt, then everyone move slightly into the same cluster - rinse and repeating each time.
- Decorin will split multiple times as he is "killed". The first time his health reaches zero he will split into two new slightly smaller versions, one with "Fire Wraith" under his name, the other with "Flame Wraith". Focus on killing one at a time, because once you kill one of these two, another two slightly smaller ones will spawn, that will then need to be killed.
- Once you kill the two smaller Decorin's with the same "Fire Wraith", or "Flame Wraith" title you can focus down the first of the original split, and then the final two that spawn from that. In total you will kill 7 variants of Decorin. This is the last named for the regular version of the zone.
