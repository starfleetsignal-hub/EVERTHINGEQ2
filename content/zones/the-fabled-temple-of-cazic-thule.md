This is a higher level version of the original [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule|Temple of Cazic-Thule]]. It has the exact same design as the original, with the same types of monsters and heroic named, most of which range in level from 103 to 107.

As with the original, this is a public dungeon and is not instanced. [[The Fabled Sanctum of Fear]] instance is available, with the entrances at the same location in the the Inner Temple at {{waypoint -16.25, -10.00, -173.24}} and {{waypoint 16.25, -10.00, -172.91}}.

## Notes

- You must be at least level 100 to enter this dungeon.
- All loot is level 100 and the same tier of gear found with the Terrors of Thalumbra expansion.
- Golems do not drop [[A clay golem heart|clay golem hearts]] and they are not required for [[The Fabled Sanctum of Fear]].
- The Fabled version of [[Xiggalg the Fearfiend (Fabled)|Xiggalg the Fearfiend]] {{waypoint 0.00, -11.00, -155.00}} is a level 108 Epic x2.
