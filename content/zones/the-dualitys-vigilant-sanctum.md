From the lowest level of [[Paineel]] where the three doors for [[The Hole]] are located, it is the left-most door as you walk in facing the crystal in the middle of the room. You won't get an option for [[The Duality's Vigilant Sanctum]] until you click the door, then a pop-up option box will allow you to select the zone you like.
