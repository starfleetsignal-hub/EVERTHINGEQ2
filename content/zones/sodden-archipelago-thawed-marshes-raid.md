All bosses within this dungeon are Tier 1 raid mobs with 10795 resolve.

This dungeon has two main paths, each containing four raid bosses. The two paths may be attempted in any order, but the bosses within each path must be defeated consecutively. After defeating the first eight bosses, a ninth boss becomes available.

## Thawing Rivalry Path

The bosses in this path correspond to the bosses required by the quest [[Scars of Destruction: Thawing Rivalry - Part 1]].

1. [[Barlsbit the Scavenger]]
1. [[Viterol the Voracious]]
1. [[Monuseru, Titan of the Depths]]
1. [[Grand Shaman Grungizt]]

## Bonds of Mischief Path

The bosses in this path correspond to the bosses required by the quest [[Scars of Destruction: Bonds of Mischief - Part 1]].

1. [[Joggu the Steadfast]]
1. [[Sommumun the Frenzied]]
1. [[Bucko, Loyal Pet to Maferi]]
1. [[Chieftain Maferi]]

## Final Boss

After defeating all eight of the previous bosses (four from each path), then the final boss, [[Vitoth of the Gnarled Roots]], becomes available.
