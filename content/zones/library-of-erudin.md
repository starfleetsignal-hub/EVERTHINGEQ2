## Getting there

In [[The Stonebrunt Highlands]], use the Temporal pathfinder to fly to Marred Plateau and then click on the column {{waypoint -554, 530, -137}} to teleport to the Library of Erudin.

## Notes

![Erudin Glyph](images/Erudin_Glyph.png)

- You will need to click on the Erudin glyphs to access certain areas of the dungeon.
- Click the bookshelf at {{waypoint -64, 37, -70}} to open a secret passageway.
- The weapon you pick up is also a collectible. The collection is [[Library of Erudin Murder Weapons]].
- The Mark of Manaar chest is located in [[The Erudin Stacks]] room next to [[Custodian Tajiff]]
- The key mob is [[An ancient enchanted tome]] located on the second floor.
- There is an [[Agnostic Dungeons|agnostic]] version of this dungeon available to players level 10 through 95.
