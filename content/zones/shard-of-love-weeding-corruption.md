## Background

Something is amiss within the [[Shard of Love: A Moment of Valor|Shard of Love]]! [[Sister Katey Dalwynn]], of the Sisterhood of [[Erollisi Marr|Erollisi]], has tasked you with disposing of the tainted creatures that inhabit the holy realm, and destroying the *Seeds of Distrust* that have been planted within vases in the Shard.

## Notes

- **Shard of Love: Weeding Corruption** was introduced as part of [[Erollisi Day]] 2013. It is accessible only by adventurers on the quest [[Heal the Pain]].
