## Background

Within this cave hold the leaders of The Tunarian Alliance on the island, destroy them!

This instance is where the [[Isle of Refuge Timeline#Outpost of the Overlord|Outpost of the Overlord Timeline]] ends. Killing the [[A Tunarian elder|Tunarian elders]] will cause the three named animals to aggro one-by-one. Once they're all dead, [[Tayil N'Velex (Outpost of the Overlord)|Tayil N'Velex]] will spawn at the entrance, walk towards [[Kayl of the Circle]], and kill him - completing the questline.

## Notes

- You can complete this instance as many times as you like.
