## Notes

- It may also be possible to access this instance by completing [[A Darkened Shard, Part 3]].

## Entrance

This zone is accessed from inside [[The Drowned Caverns: Outer Grotto|The Outer Grotto]] within the [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den|Splitpaw Den]]. Use the terraporter at {{waypoint 42.88, -78.54, -227.83}} to enter the Outer Grotto. Once inside, the entrance to the raid zone is not readily visible from the water and getting to it will require jumping between several of the pillars. Note that waypoints do not work well if you're in the water, only when standing on one of the islands or pillars.

- Once you enter the Outer Grotto, go northeast towards the entrance to [[The Drowned Caverns: Flammable Fur|Flammable Fur]] terraporter. The pillar spirals around, go to the top at {{waypoint -23.76, 31.45, 13.70}}.
- From the top of the pillar, jump to the neighboring pillar to the south at {{waypoint -23.12, 33.16, 21.14}}.
- Jump down to the pillar to the southwest at {{waypoint -8.93, 26.84, 27.47}}.
- From there, jump to the next pillar to the west at {{waypoint 1.93, 26.26, 21.69}} and go up and around to the top.
- Jump across to the next pillar to the northwest and you'll see the terraporter entrance at {{waypoint 9.95, 35.85, -6.67}}.

There are aggressive fish in the water, as well as Underpaw anglers who swim there. Around the islands and pillars there are gnolls, zygomids and bats. The entrance to the zone is guarded by Underpaw gatekeepers.
