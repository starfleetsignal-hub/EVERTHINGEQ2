## Walkthrough

1. Click the switch on the bridge at {{waypoint 18, 20, 95}}. This will activate a shield which prevents additional clockwork fliers from spawning and attacking.
1. Click the switch at {{waypoint -31, 14, 113}} to open the nearby door.
1. Once through the door, kill [[Court Guardian Chromtok (Court of Innovation)|Court Guardian Chromtok]].
1. Go to [[The Power Plant]] {{waypoint -51, 13, 282}}. You must deactivate the large yellow power cells that are on your left as you enter. To deactivate them, place e the levers located at {{waypoint 9, 30, 277}} in the correct orientation:
   - Power Cell A = down, down, up, up
   - Power Cell B = down, up, down, up
   - Power Cell C = up, down, up, down
   - Power Cell D = up, up, down, down
   - After you destroy each power cell a group of clockworks will enter the room from the hall. The order is:
     - First power cell destroyed: 1 flying clockwork, 1 walking clockwork
     - Second power cell destroyed: 2 flying clockworks, 1 walking clockworks
     - Third power cell destroyed: 2 flying clockworks, 2 walking clockworks
     - Fourth power cell destroyed: 2 flying clockworks, 2 walking clockworks & [[The Crimson Vanquisher (Court of Innovation)|The Crimson Vanquisher]]
   - Some people recommend fighting them from the back of the room as the flying clockworks will separate from the walking clockworks. If your group is strong enough you can just fight them in the center of the room.
   - The Crimson Vanquisher is particularly hard - possibly the hardest mob in the instance. He has a fairly long (15-20 sec?) AE stifle debuff. The debuff is elemental damage based (heat/cold) so use any buffs you have to help resist it.
1. Go to [[The Master Clockwork Domain]] {{waypoint -70, 43, 9}} and face [[King Klak'Anon (Court of Innovation)|King Klak'Anon]].
1. At low health, he will stop fighting and turn into [[King Klak'Anon the Mechnamaximus]]. Defeat him and pick up the [[Energized Clockwork Gear]] that spawns at his corpse.
1. [[Master Clockwork Protocol (original)|Master Clockwork Protocol]] will activate upon the death of King Klak'Anon the Mechnamaximus. Send this bucket of bolts to the junkyard and claim victory!
   - At around 10-20% health remaining, he will begin taunting you which activates his shield. Use the Energized Clockwork Gear to deactivate his shield.

## Quests

There is a sub-quest to [[Casualties of the War of the Fay]], called [[Saphronia's Request]], which requires you to kill [[Master Clockwork Protocol (original)|Master Clockwork Protocol]].
