## Note:

- This is the second Raid area
- Once you kill all the Tier 1 names in the [[Plane of Innovation: The Wasteyards (Raid)|Wasteyards]](first 4 names), you will be able to enter here.
- You cannot stand on the hills in the area, because deadly worms will crawl out of the hills and attack you till you dead
- To complete this area, an on-level team needs to have all 4 Ascension levels.

## Strategy

### **[[Dyspertitia]](T1)**

- 4 adds can't be killed. 4 adds resummon from time to time. They are hate linked to named. 4 tril health
- Everyone except **tank** should move behind named. It add trauma(cure able) to those who front and death touch it 10 sec later(not often)
- It use a cure able elemental debuff to everyone. Expiring wipe out entire raid team. Be sure hire a healer mercenary in your team to auto **cure or** use AE cure in time
- Sometimes a curse appear to a player in front named. This curse increase damage overtime. About 110,000,000(110M) last tick
- Use tank and spank

### **[[Resnak and Rythrak]](T1)**

- Unlock(Not white) when **Dyspertitia** is dead
- They are white name and turn to aggro when approach
- "The Brothers Flea" They swap their targets with each other.
- They can AOE deathtouch certain classes based on distance to each named. Diff class have diff distances.
  - Not sure what it is. But I keep two named 0 distance and it wipe out all **fighters** and **scouts** in hp approximately 70% 40% 15%
  - two named 10m away each wipe out all **Mage** and **Priest**
- 2.9Tril health each named

### **[[Rankle]](T1)**

- Unlock(Not white) when **Resnak and Rythrak** are dead
- at least clear half trash of this lake to pull named. About 6.9 Tril Heath. Because of the mechanics below, this is the tank-est named in T1 Raid
- It go rampage when increment to 10. When rampage it use AOE "Boil Mud" around it to kill whatever close to it. When rampage it deals 100M per hit
- this named **move in a very slow pace** and it gives you movement **prevent interrupt** persistent buff so everyone should **hit and run**

### [[Corpulus]]**(T2)**

- Requirements: Dispel charms or potions and any kind of movement speed boosts
- Try to keep moving he will drop dark puddles on ground near largest clusters of players which you need to dispel and move out of asap as they slow you down heavily and do deadly damage.
- Burn em down.

### **[[Everquest2:Grummus|Grummus]](T3)**

Every 10% spawns a bubble someone has to dps down has 3 locations, 2 are on a hill both near each other and other is on island. Has a Tank swap which has 30 secs to do in. Easiest way to do this is hold aggro til almost 40 secs left swap hold for 10 then swap back and repeat. Keep ranged 20-25meters if your not the tank he does a aoe that makes adds spawn based on how many aoe hits and adds are stronger based on number hit. At 40% hp he unroots. You can skip curing gut check if your going for flawless otherwise try to cure it because anyone who dies with it will cause raid wipe.

### [[Pox]]**(T3)**

- Requirements: Balanced number of Ascension classes of each type (Ex: 1 geo 1 eth 1 ele 1 tham) need 1 of each.
- Everyone goto proper totem before fight (note:arcane for is both elem & ether) geo is red and thaum is green totem.
- Upon pull of the named everyone right click the totem and attune failure to do so will cause death of unattuned person.
- The named will at certain %'s hp emote about a deadly breath if your not by your assigned totem (does this 3 to 5 times). Run back to the totem to prevent death and banish.
- Around 50% hp the named will check to see if there are equal ascension classes for all 4, Failure will wipe and is needed to kill the mob.
- After 50% just burn him down following the totem joust like before until it's dead.
- MML: Gently tap him with a pet while standing next to the correct totem for your ascension class, click totem, burn him hard.
