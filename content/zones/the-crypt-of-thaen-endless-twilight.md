## Access

This instance is available only during two quests in the [[Bloodline Chronicles Timeline]]: [[Shattering the Crystals]] and [[A Vicious Twist]] for *The Hand of Marr* or [[Exploring the Crypt]] and [[Crushing Betrayal]] for *The Brethren*. After turning in A Vicious Twist or Crushing Betrayal this instance will not show up as an option when attempting to zone into The Crypt of T'haen.

- **Crucial Note**: As such, it is vital that all named monsters are killed for AA experience and discovery locations are explored for discovery experience before you turn in the quests. Once you turn in the quests listed above there is no going back!

## Walkthrough

This instance is designed around the quests pertaining to it. Complete the quest steps and explore all over the instance to complete it. The final boss is [[T'Rath the Usurper]].
