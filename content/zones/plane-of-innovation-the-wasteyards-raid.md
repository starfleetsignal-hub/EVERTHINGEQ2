From Gninja on the old EQ2 forum, Nov 2017:

**Raids: Progression**

Raid progression within Planes of Prophecy will work differently than expansions in the past. In order to progress in raiding there is a specific order that bosses must be defeated in to open up access to other bosses. These bosses are broken into 4 tiers with tier 1 being the easiest/least tricks, whereas tier 4s have more complicated mechanics. You will not be able to engage a tier of bosses until the previous tier has been defeated. This means if you have killed all tier 1 bosses in one zone but not the tier 1 bosses in the other zones you will not be able to engage the tier 2 bosses in the original zone. This will require some zone hopping, but once they have been defeated you will be able to run each zone from start to finish. Most zones have bosses of all tiers, and most have a large number of bosses; there are 49 in total. You will be able to pull bosses in progression as long as 75% of your raid force has the previous bosses defeated, though those that have not defeated them may find the encounter more challenging. Bosses in each zone will put a marker on the map showing their number in progression order. [Changed to only one person needs to have defeated the required previous bosses to zone raid in.]

**Raids: Zone Access**

Gaining access to raids will work as follows: Once 75% of your raid force has completed the signature questline you will gain access to the Plane of Innovation: Wasteyards [Raid]. Upon defeating all tier 1 bosses within the zone you will gain access to Plane of Disease: Virulent Incursion [Raid]. Defeating all tier 1 bosses there will give you access to the next zone, and so on. All the raid zones are set to allow you to enter them as long as 75% of the raid has unlocked access. [Changed to only one person needs to have defeated the required previous bosses to zone raid in.]

**Raids: Alternate Characters**

To make access easier for alternate characters, once you have defeated all bosses of a tier you will get a key for your keyring (/keys) that will allow all characters on your account access to the same bosses your main character has access to. Armor from drops will work in a similar way: if you loot a pattern and turn it in for your shiny new breastplate, you then get access to buy more breastplates of that pattern’s type, which can be given to your alternate characters via the shared bank.

**Raids: Mechanics**

Many of the bosses in the Planes have the ability to deathtouch their main target if that target is not a fighter. This isn’t always the case and it doesn’t happen instantly in most cases, but the majority are set up this way. This makes threat management all that much more important, so you may want to go easy on the huge ascension abilities on pull or even large spikes in damage without letting your tank know its coming. Unless you want a date with death; just a suggestion!

Almost all named have some sort of mem wipe also for their target. Try to group 2 tank or 2 summoner tank pet

### **Karnah of the Source (T2)**

- Possible to tank and spank, but may require a bit of DPS. At 25%, it'll refill its health while increasing its offensive capabilities. With enough of a DPS push, you can burn through after 3 or 4 times before he starts to wipe you out.

### **Gearbox the Energy Siphon (T2)**

- Burn it, no big deal.

### **Construct Automaton (T2)**

- Burn it, no big deal.

### **Junk Beast (T2)**

- Every so often, a glowing exquisite chest will spawn containing a no-trade wrench that goes into your inventory. Throw the wrench to make it vulnerable again. Repeat until dead.

### **Meledrath the Mechanized (v2) (T3)**

- hp: 17,200,000,000,000 (17.2 tril)
- combat mit: ?
- Strat: Stay grouped together touching when cursed move out until cured, then return to group. Summons a robot you jump in to fight and hold the adds every 25% hp. Big enough DPS you can skip to end part where he stoneskins; just cure curse like earlier and it'll be gone and you kill em and can skip robot part but may not result in flawless.

Must clear all t3 to open the door

### **Operator Figl (T3)**

- hp: 17,200,000,000,000 (17.2 tril)
- combat mit: ?
- Strat: 95% hp he curses a player, then every 10% hp (85/75/65/55/45/35/25/15/5). You have to do jousting and curse curing. Getting the curse again before it is cured is death. Can skip script if you have enough dead players and big DPS soloing it: the curses goes on the dead and falls off, but still chance you get it and die.

### **Junkyard Mawg (T3)**

- hp: 17,200,000,000,000 (17.2 tril)(presumed)
- combat mit: ?
- Strat: everyone fight in front of him, he then curses 1-3 players; those three have to stay together and joust back to wall making sure you all are touching. When the curse is removed, move back to group.

### **Meldrath the Malignant (T3)**

- Literally impossible to solo. You can burn him all you want, but the encounter recognizes that you don't have enough players present to challenge him and resets. One player has to hop into his clockwork to fight against him, but it can't initially be the main tank. When the clockwork assassins spawn, another player (including the main tank) can jump into the clockwork while others finish him off.

### **Manaetic Behemoth (T4)**

Freeze, teleport to control room, adds, buzz-saws, energy overloads, but worst of all - stoneskin at 5% with twenty stacks to dispel. Can be solo'ed MML if enough DoTs land with perfect timing to hit him for more than 5% damage when he is just above 5% health so as to skip the stoneskin (you will be a frozen spectator while this happens); this is unlikely, but possible.
