## Background

These are the living quarters of [[Queen Antonia Bayle]], ruler of [[Qeynos]]. It can first be entered during the level 60+ quests in the [[The City of Qeynos Timeline#Level 60|City of Qeynos Timeline]], and can be revisited anytime thereafter.
