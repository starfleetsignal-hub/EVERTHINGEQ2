Entrance location in [[Obol Plains]] {{waypoint 447, 33, 509}}

## Note

You must finish the Signature line and receive the Shroud of Eternity before you will be allowed to enter this zone.

## Walkthrough

1. Defeat [[Caerina the Lost (Heroic)]] near the entrance and unlock the first ***Soulwell Teleporter*** {{waypoint 0.99, 0.20, 62.12}}, then clear the way to the ***Soulwell Tube*** {{waypoint 0.95, 16.24, -139.24}}
1. Enter and dive to the basement to unlock another soulwell. Follow [[Melanie Everling (Harrow's End) (Heroic)]] {{waypoint 4.33, -61.01, -100.95}} through the 3 rooms wherein you'll be ambushed by bound manifestations. In the end kill her once she becomes aggro to free her {{waypoint -43.04, -59.87, -137.89}}.
1. Go back to the *Soulwell Tube* {{waypoint 0.95, -59.86, -139.24}} and swim up past the first entrance to the next teleporter unlock in the [[Balistrade of Souls]]. Clear the area and kill the wandering [[Construct of Souls (Heroic)|Construct of Souls]].
1. Go back to the *Soulwell Tube* {{waypoint 0.95, 62.40, -139.24}} and swim up to the top of this tube by [[Bastion's Charge]]. Kill [[Bastion (Heroic)|Bastion]] {{waypoint 1.00, 106.71, -224.00}} to open the barrier blocking the way to [[Fitzpitzle's Rest]]. Kill [[Fitzpitzle (Harrow's End) (Heroic)|Fitzpitzle]] {{waypoint -110.40, 110.08, -298.70}} by following him through his 3 studies and beating him further each time.
1. Use the second ***Soulwell Tube*** {{waypoint 0.95, 108.69, -299.15}} and swim up to the top floor to kill [[Oligar of the Dead (Heroic)]]
1. Use the portal that spawns upon his death at {{waypoint 10, 19, 3}} to go back down into the base of [[The Deathtoll Tower]]. Kill [[Drinal (Heroic)|Drinal]] {{waypoint 0.91, 19.12, -22.86}} who stands on the lift going all the way to the top of the Tower.
   - *Note: Defeating Drinal before the lift has ascended will bug the lift out forcing you to call or evac out*
     - Note2: If the lift doesn't lift, you can right click on the Soulwell teleporter to be ported back to the entrance.
