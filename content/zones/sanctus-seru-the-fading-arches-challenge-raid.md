## Names

- [[Luminary Percontorius Felvin]]
  - Strat: Everyone in corner he has a knockback. Use Fear-Rune. "Tank'n spank."

- [[Luminary Cohortis Emon]]
  - Strat: Everyone fight in front of the mob, very close e.g. 4m. If the red cone comes, joust out of it and run on mob's back (4m distance again!) and continue your fight here. The mob will turn around. Note: Sometimes mob randomly ports. Follow him, distance 4m and fight in front of mob.

- [[Luminary Hertu Asundri]]
  - Strat: stay 25m from named, burn first earth golems, then the rest of adds before named. After adds and summoners go away dps named.
- [[Corrupted Lord Commander Seru]]

Must clear all trash on the level to open the interior door in his room.

- (LvL 129, Resolve 2935): use Stun-rune;
- Tank has to click "the heart" to ward the complete raid party vs. the deadly AE ;
- MT has to take the sword to defeat the gladiators... if the MT is too slow defeating gladiators, then... wipe
- golems have to be defeated
- "Castolien" have to be asap defeated as they stun your party members.
- 90%, 65%, 40%, 15% hp, Seru drops his sword.
- Stoneskin tank when he does "Devout Charges" or the emote about listening to his sword, and make sure you joust out 11+m.
