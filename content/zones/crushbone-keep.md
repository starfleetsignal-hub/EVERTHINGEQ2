## Background

This is the primary fortress of the [[Clan Crushbone (Faction)|Crushbone Orcs]]. Built during the [[War of Fay: Death|War of Fay]], the orcs use this as a base from which to launch continuous attacks on [[Kelethin]] and [[Greater Faydark]]. [[Morodag Greenbow|Recently]], elven reports are indicating that the orcs are practicing necromancy using the bones of deceased [[Feir'Dal]] and [[Koada'Dal]]. Such atrocity cannot be allowed to continue!
