## Strategy

1. Progress west to the first intersection, down the south stairs and then west again to the room with the first named, [[Polyph]]. {{waypoint 150, -30, 114}}
1. Return to the first intersection, this time down the north stairs and west again to the room with the next named, [[Cephelon of Many Tentacles]]. {{waypoint 162, -31, 59}}
1. Exit the room and head east past the first intersection and descend until you reach the next intersection. Take the north stairs down.
1. Continue to descend the stairs all the way until you get to a long hall that leads to the next named, [[Kith-Lu]]. {{waypoint 160, -58, 121}}
   - Shortcut - you can head outside and jump east down to the lower intersection into the abberant group to skip ahead to Step 7.
1. Head back outside up the stairs to the first four way intersection, this time taking the south stairs down.
1. Continue to descend the stairs until you get the the next three way intersection.
1. Head south to another intersection, this time going east to where the next named, [[Marakai, Hand of Mrwar]], roams.
   - Named spawns at {{waypoint 63, -83, 67}} and roams back and forth down the south hall.
1. Enter either the north or south room and drop down the hole to descend to the flooded area with the final named, [[Mrwar, the Destroyer]]. {{waypoint 60, -108, 88}}

## Notes

- Everyone except the tank should stay at least two meters away from the [[Aberrant spell-shield]]. They usually come in a group of three with an [[Aberrant mind-ripper]] and an [[Aberrant entwiner]] and have a high damage spell called Mental Detonation. If possible, mesmerize or stun the Aberrant spell-shield to reduce the frequency of them casting Mental Detonation.
- There is an [[Agnostic Dungeons|agnostic]] version of this dungeon available to players level 10 through 95.
