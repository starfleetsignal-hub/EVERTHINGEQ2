## Background

> This [Oakmyst Forest] woodland preserve is shaded by emerald green broad-leaf trees such as oak and maple. Beneath the trees, the undergrowth is scattered with trillium, bleeding hearts and red-tipped paintbrushes.
>
> — [[Pearl Honeywine]]

Forming a larger forested area with the Eldarr Grove region of [[The City of Qeynos|Qeynos]], Oakmyst Forest is a canopied refuge for animals and wildlife. No undeath, arcane magic, demonry or other twisted powers on [[Norrath]] have found their way onto this serene landscape. However, of late there have been whispers among the elves of [[The Tunarian Alliance (Faction)|The Tunarian Alliance]] and the native dryads of an unknown force attempting to corrupt this sanctuary.

## Geogrpahy

As of [[Update:64|LU64]], Oakmyst Forest can only be accessed from Qeynos [[Mariner's Bell|Mariner's Bells]].
