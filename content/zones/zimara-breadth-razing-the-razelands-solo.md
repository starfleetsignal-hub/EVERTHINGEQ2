## Walkthrough

1. Kill the following
   - Slay the Beast Master
   - Massacure the Matriarch
1. To get to the next area you must kill Eaglovok {{waypoint -361, 124, -650}}(has a knockback)
1. Click on the crystal at {{waypoint -333, 124, -646}} to go to the next area.
   - To return to the previous area, use the crystal at {{waypoint -228, 106, -459}}
   - To get to the Zenith Helionae use the path at {{waypoint -183, 110, -189}}
1. Kill Gilded Back Demolisher at {{waypoint -299.76, 95.01, -272.94}}
   - Demolisher has a knockback, it is recommended to clear the area of trash to limit adds.
1. Kill Sina A'rak at {{waypoint 77.61, 149.74, -322.56}}
   - Summons 2 adds at a time
1. Kill Doda K'Bael at {{waypoint -33.98, 156.84, 8.95}}
   - Has ability to teleport player around
   - Summons 1 add at a time
1. Kill Queen Era'selka at {{waypoint 145.23, 161.85, 42.84}}
   - Has knockback
   - Spawns two adds at a time
