## Notes

Zone in is from [[Karak Peak]]. {{waypoint -317, 166, -581}}

- It's recommended that you have 500 crit chance for this zone.
- The walk-through is much the same as the [[High Keep (Advanced Solo)]] version, with the exception that the bosses are 3-^, and something is different about Hammy.

##### Hammy - Great Hall

- In this version of the quest, [[Hammy (Heroic)]] is three-^, spawns an Escaped Swine add, and he poisons you with Swine Influenza.
- I died three times before I noticed, but there are (initially inert) Storage Crates literring the Great Hall where Hammy pops. After the encounter with Hammy starts. they become targetable and sparkle green. I can't be sure, but I think they were instrumental in solo'ing the encounter as a Level-100 Guardian : I lured Hammy over to one box, then attacked the box and smashed it. Inside were sacks of something that Hammy seemed to stop to inspect, while I kited-away hitting him with arrows. When he came-free, I lured him to another and did the same. By then the Escaped Swine had added, and I killed him. Suddenly, I was hitting Hammy for *X.Y* million damage hits at-a-time, and he went-down fast after that.

##### Sub-quests

I found the following sub-quests accidentally, and didn't get their locations:

- "Priorities" - there is no guide yet, and I was too busy solo'ing in the Heroic to take locations. You find a Guard with a sparkling note beside him. If you click on it, you will be offered this quest. If you accept, you have a list of people to locate/help. You will incidentally find these people as you fight through the dungeon killing-off the mobs to make the bosses spawn, but the mobs with the captives all have feather-quest icons on them. Some of them are in side-rooms that aren't on your path to the bosses, so you have to check every room. The quest will auto-update as you 'free' or find them (dead):
  - Find Samuel
  - Find Gelun
  - Find Denia
  - Find Halbert
  - Find S'dera
  - Find Linya
- [[Required Reading]] - updates unavailable until zone is reset and reentered
  - Note that there is *one* clickable book at {{waypoint -108.52, -77.78, 43.55}}, on the end of the domino-placed book line, but this is not one of them : Clicking that book knocks-over the domino-line of books and spawns a bunch of flying Unbound Tomes you'll then have to kill. Clearing these does not in any way have any relation to the quest either.
