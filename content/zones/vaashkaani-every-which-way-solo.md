## Walkthrough

1. Look for a big ball of energy and click on it to receive combat buff.  While you're looking for the ball(s) avoid the Master of the maze Abin'Ebi and second in command, Soku'Aos as they will put you at the start of the zone when they hit you.  With the right resolve the trash doesn't hurt much while your running through.  The smaller balls extend the sphere buff.  The large balls give a buff allowing mobs to take damage.  Kill first named Gazu, the slow roamer (can only be killed with sphere buff).
1. Progress through find another large sphere, get buffed and look for second in command avoiding Master of the Maze yet again.  Use Stifle Rune.
1. Find sphere, get buff.  Attack Master of the maze.  He will need a heroic opportunity done (pay attention to text)

## Walkthrough (more details)

1. Pick up the small balls to increase the duration of the "big ball" buff by 1 second each.
1. Pick up a big ball at {{waypoint 466.69, -10.61, 292.17}} or at {{waypoint 396.00, -10.61, 290.87}}
   - The big balls effect stack.  They only respawn when the time of each runs out.
1. Named have to die in the following order (they are not aggro).
   - *Note: You can only damage trash or named while under the effect of a big ball*
   1. [[Gazu'Taz the Gradual]].
   1. [[Soku'Aos the Sturdy]] *cannot be damaged while [[Gazu'Taz the Gradual]] is alive.*
   1. [[Abin'Ebi the Awkward]] - Master of the Maze.
      - *cannot be damaged while [[Gazu'Taz the Gradual]] and [[Soku'Aos the Sturdy]] are alive.*
      - Use your heroic opportunities or he will dispel the big ball effect
