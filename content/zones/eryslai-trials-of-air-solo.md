## Notes

Enter the zone Eryslai: Trials of Air {Solo}. Proceed to {{waypoint -200, -474, 621}} and listen to Xegony. Once she is done ranting, the Beacon of the Four Winds in the center of the platform will become active {{waypoint -312, -490, 774}}.  Right click the beacon to begin each trial.  If you leave the central area, Xegony will appear, kick you and drain power.

## Bosses

1. Trial 1: [[Cyclono]] is spawned by a ring event consisting of six waves of three [[A mist spren|mist sprens]] for 2 minutes. When he emotes about gathering debris, joust out (50m works) or crouch. [[A mist spren|Mist sprens]] continue to spawn and have an arcane detriment (Humidity) which lowers potency by 20,000%.
1. Trial 2: [[Quarez]] is spawned by a ring event consisting of waves of three [[A crackling stormrider|crackling stormriders]] for 2 minutes.  Lots of curing detriments as the crackling detriment lowers crit bonus by 20,000% making it difficult to damage him.  Movement needed to prevent him getting a buff from his flag which is in the middle of the area you fight him in.  When he emotes, you need to move him to the opposite side of the flag.
1. Trial 3: [[Kamara Zar]] is spawned by a ring event consisting of waves of three [[A Nilborian windraker|Nilborian windrakers]] for 2 minutes.

The portal to exit the zone is located at {{waypoint -442, -478, 952}}
