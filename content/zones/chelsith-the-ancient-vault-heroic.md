This is a heroic Instance! At least level 92 to enter.<br>For the solo (duo) version go there: [[Chelsith: The Ancient Vault (Advanced Solo)|Chelsith: The Ancient Vault [Advanced Solo]]]

## Walkthrough

please post strategies for boss fights!
<br>

[[Murmaz the Believer (Heroic)|Murmaz the Believer]]
We pulled him from far away and just tanked/spanked him.  No sense running into his room to kill the trash - they dont come along with him.

[[Overseer Kisiz (Heroic)|Overseer Kisiz]]
Adds come every few seconds - have tank grab them and heal through any damage.  Burn named entire time with any AE dmg on the adds.

[[Taskmaster Xatik (Heroic)|Taskmaster Xatik]] (including his 2 sidekicks [[Taskmaster Gurunak (Heroic)|Taskmaster Gurunak]] and [[Taskmistress Hegal (Heroic)|Taskmistress Hegal]]
Same as the advanced solo instance.  Mostly a tank and spank with multiple mobs.

[[Guardian of the Deep (Heroic)|Guardian of the Deep]]
Click the upper "Crystal Shapers", fight the adds
Click the lower "Crystal Shapers", fight the adds
Kill the main mob

[[Spirit of the Deep (Heroic)|Spirit of the Deep]]
Same as the Advanced Solo - attack him until he goes to one of the lampposts, take down the adds. Rinse and Repeat.
