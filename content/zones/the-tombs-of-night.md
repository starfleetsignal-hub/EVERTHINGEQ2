## Background

Two groups, the undead-cleansing *Hand of Marr* and the power-hungry *Brethren of Night*, have set up camps along the bank of [[Soul Eater Falls]] in [[Nektulos Forest]]. Should an adventurer be willing they can find plenty of work here as both groups need help in slaying multitudes of vampires and other foul beasts.

## Geography

The Tombs of Night is layered in levels. The first level is mostly where the mist grinnin goblins dwell, as well as native cave wildlife and a contingency of vampires. The second level is a passageway accessed directly in front of the zone entrance; and more of the same creatures that can be found on the first floor exist here. The third floor contains [[The Maw of Mortality (The Tombs of Night)|The Maw of Mortality]] and a stronger host of vampires. The Maw connects to the fourth floor, the 'blood-crystal pools' beneath [[The Forgotten Tears (The Tombs of Night)|The Forgotten Tears]], as well as to [[The Hand of the Crypt (The Tombs of Night)|The Hand of the Crypt]], where the most powerful vampires can be found as well as a [[The Crypt of T'haen|strange door]].

Throughout the zone there are many breakable walls. Some separate you from where you need to go, opening additional sections of the zone. Others are walled-up alcoves containing *a dusty coffin*, *a dusty vase*, or sometimes both. Destroying them may reveal nothing, a monster, or just some coin. *Dusty vases* can also appear in other places in plain sight.
