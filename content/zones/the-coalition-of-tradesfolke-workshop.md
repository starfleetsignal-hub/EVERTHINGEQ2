The Coalition Crafter's Association has both an instanced area and a crafting area in the building near the entrance to the instanced area. Between the two of these, crafters will find most amenities they might need.

## Entrance

Entrance at {{waypoint 19, -19, 85}} in West Freeport in [[The City of Freeport]].

## Features

### Inside the Instance

The tradeskill instance contains the following amenities:

- [[Gnaeus Ovidius]], the Work Order foreman {{waypoint -1.51, -0.48, -9.95}}
- [[Commissioner Venilos]], the Rush Order foreman {{waypoint -3.1, -0.19, -2.88}}
- [[Work Order Clipboard]] x26, one on a wall or partition near each crafting station
- [[Chrisana D'Abth]], the Fuel Merchant {{waypoint 1.52, -6.48, 11.46}}
- [[Jangles Zobblebok]], the Broker {{waypoint 10.23, -0.43, -9.01}}
- [[Chemistry Table]] x3 {{waypoint -11.53, -0.17, -8.44}}
- [[Engraved Desk]] x 4 {{waypoint -23.62, -19.65, 6.27}}
- [[Forge]] x4 {{waypoint -12.6, -19.78, 5.7}}
- [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]] x6 {{waypoint -6.98, -6.61, 1.82}}
- [[Stove & Keg]] x3 {{waypoint -14.53, -13.1, 13.57}}
- [[Woodworking Table]] x3 {{waypoint -12.16, -13.1, -1.4}}
- [[Work Bench]] x3 {{waypoint -14.85, -0.17, 8.05}}

The instance does not contain a [[Bank]], a [[Crafting Trainer]], a [[Tradeskill Tutorial]], a [[Tradeskill Career Counselor]], a Trades Coordinator (grandmaster), an NPC for Adorning and Tinkering daily tasks, or a faction merchant, however all of these can be found just outside the instance in the building.
