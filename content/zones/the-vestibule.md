## Background

You have discovered a [[List of acquisitions for the continuing of research.|troubling notice]] on one of the more powerful Void-creatures found in the [[The Obelisk of Lost Souls|Obelisk of Lost Souls]]. It speaks of 'a shifting' and the need to have 'suitable hosts prepared and ready'. You must infiltrate this 'vestibule' at the heart of the Obelisk and delay whatever scheme is at play here.

## Notes

- **Important Note:** In order to spawn the final boss of this instance, a player must have completed or be on the quest [[List of Acquisitions for the Continuing of Research]].

## Walkthrough

1. From the zone-in, go straight forward and up the stairs on your left. Enter the room and slay [[The Medium of Hykor]]. {{waypoint -477, 407, 396}}
1. Backtrack, go down the other passageway near the entrance, and enter the room at the end. {{waypoint -529, 371, 400}}
1. Interact with the pile of sacks at {{waypoint -532, 371, 408}} to receive [[An unconscious goblin]]. Left-click on one of the tables in the room to place the goblin on it. Do this for each of the six tables. Placing the item on one of the tables will spawn [[A shadowedman seed]].
1. After around one minute, the first shadowedman seed will die and spawn [[A goblin (The Vestibule)|a goblin]] with two adds. Kill them to make another shadowedman seed spawn at the next table. Repeat this process until all six possessed goblins are dead.
1. After the final goblin dies, [[Bovauk]] or [[Malisient]] will spawn at the back of the room.
