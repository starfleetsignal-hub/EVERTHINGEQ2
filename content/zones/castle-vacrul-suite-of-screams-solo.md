## Prerequisites

*To enter, you must have completed [[Visions of Vetrovia: Vacrul Intentions]] or have earned the [[VoV Flag: Castle Vacrul Dungeons Unlock]].

**CRITICAL NOTES**:

- The second encounter, Taskmistress Devioth, can be difficult to do solo because of the amount of game text you need read while trying to stay alive.
- During the fight, [[Taskmistress Devioth]] will call upon a race of 'chattel' servants to come to her aid. You click on the  bookcase appropriate to that race in order to PREVENT them coming to her aid.
- Be aware that [[Taskmistress Devioth]] will take damage for a while, but then switch which race she's trying to summon and she will suddenly seem to become immune, She'll summon a DIFFERENT race from the one you started with - so be ready to stem the new adds by clicking a NEW bookcase - if you re-click on the original one, you'll take a hard hit and be thrown around the room. Rinse and repeat until you manage to kill her.

## Walkthrough

1. [[Bloodlord Durogan]]
   - Click drinks on tables to keep from being one-shotted. He emotes: "Captain Durogan choose to feed upon (Player name). She/he must intoxicate her/his blood quickly!"  Hopefully you have a pet that can attack him while all you do is find bottles to  continuously drink, even while "intoxicated".
   - Leave the room via the bookcase door at {{waypoint 17.55, -50.19, 358.58}}
1. [[Taskmistress Devioth]]
   - Click the bookcase associated with the race called by the Named. Bookcases are in alphabetical order around the room (except kerra/iksar are swapped round). There's a handy map [here](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/castle-vacrul-suite-of-screams-removal-consideration.603394/page-2#post-6683344) that shows the layout. If too many of the adds spawn you will be killed.
     - Barbarian -> Halas Horrors -- southern end of room
     - Dark Elf -> Neriak Nightmares -- southern end
     - Dwarves -> Kaladim Killings -- southern end
     - Erudites -> Paineel Purgings -- western side, next to the door the adds run in through
     - Fae -> Faydark Frights -- western side (south)
     - Froglok -> Guktan Graves -- western side (south-center)
     - Gnome -> Ak'Anon Atrocities -- western side (north)
     - Halfling -> Rivervale Remonstrances -- western side (north)
     - High Elf -> Felwithe Fears -- western side (north)
     - Human -> Qeynos Quarrys -- northern end of room (NW corner)
     - Kerrans -> Kerra isle Hostiles -- northern end (NW corner)
     - Iksar -> Cabilis Curses -- northern end (NE corner)
     - Ogre -> Oggok Ordeals -- eastern side (north)
     - Ratonga -> Underfoot Unsavories -- eastern side (north)
     - Sarnaks -> Gorowyn Grievances -- eastern side (center, next to the door you entered by)
     - Troll -> Innothule Inedibles -- eastern side (south)
     - Vah Shir -> Shar Vahl Sacrifices -- eastern side (south)
     - Wood Elves -> Kelethin Cacophony -- eastern side (south)
   - If you click on the wrong bookcase you will be hit with an incurable detriment and unable to cast for a time.
   - If you die during the fight because you've been overwhelmed by adds make sure you stay down long enough for the encounter to reset, otherwise they will still be running into the room when you reenter!
   - Leave the room and continue down the corridor to the next bookcase door at {{waypoint 16.44, -50.16, 253.53}}.
1. [[Bloodmaiden Syvanti]]
   - Hail the Bloodmaiden to begin the fight.
   - You will feel like you are burning her down fast, and then suddenly that will stop and she will hover at 5% or 10% and *seem* to have become impervious. Keep burning.
   - She will periodically call [[A shrieking horror]] - you must kill this before it reaches [[Horacio]] and wakes him up, or you die.
   - She will also periodically curse you - this is preceded by (in-chat text) "The Bloodmaiden's victims from xxx scream uncontrollably" where "xxx" is one of four VoV locations. Run to the matching "circle of Vetrovian inhabitants" and use a cure pot as follows:
     - Karuupa Jungle (pygmies) - Cure Trauma
     - Mahngavi Wastes (witches) - Cure Arcane
     - Svarni Expanse (Kyv) - Cure Noxious
     - Kamapor (centaurs) - Cure Elemental
   - If you have a good healer you can skip the pot, but still need to be in the correct circle for their cure to take effect.
   - If doing this, only *one* of you will be cursed, and only that person should run to the circle and cure.
   - Attempting this with a healer merc seems to result in a cascade of curses; you will end up with all four on you in very short order and die.
