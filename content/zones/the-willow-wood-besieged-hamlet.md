## Notes

This zone is only accessible by characters who are instructed to enter it as part of a quest.

It is strictly a solo zone.  Other players and mercenaries cannot be taken into it.

The zone teems with both Rockpaw gnolls and guards.  The guards will attack any gnolls that get within their aggro radius.  In addition to the usual static spawns, gnolls will continually spawn in certain spots and race across the zone, attacking any players that cross their path.
