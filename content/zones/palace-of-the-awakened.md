## Background

Currently dominated by the [[The Blacktalon (Faction)|The Blacktalon]], fearsome Aviaks that dominate [[The Barren Sky]], and their Droag overlords, this former sanctuary of the [[Plane of Sky]] is in dire need of purification. Defeat these winged heathens, all while taking in the architectural beauty of the structure. As you roam these vaunted halls, you get a sense that you are [[Aaryonar the Watcher|being watched]].

### Layout

The Palace of the Awakened is separated into different levels by climbable walls.

- Level 1: Move forward until you reach [[The Fountain Room of Aeristok]]. In the four alcoves of this room there are four ethereal idols. Destroy these to unlock the side rooms as well as the ladder going to level two, which is at {{waypoint 42, 0, 0}}.
- Level 2: This floor consists of just room, [[The Circle of the Maidens]]. To reach level three, climb the wall at {{waypoint -27, 27, 0}}.
- Level 3: You enter [[The Lyceum of Naar'Yora]]. From here the dungeon experiences its first bifurcation. You can continue up to [[The Refectory]] by immediately turning around and heading up the ladder at {{waypoint -17, 56, -1}}. Or you can move forward to [[The Workshop of Naar'Yora]], and then move up to the Nest of the Sky Lords from the ladder at {{waypoint 188, 57, 0}}.
  - Level 4a: From the [[Nest of the Sky Lords]], take the elevator at {{waypoint 154, 82, 0}} up to the Erudition Aviary.
    - Level 5a: [[Erudition Aviary]], spawn place of [[Palace Adjutant Krilagarr]]. There are no more ladders up from here.
  - Level 4b: From [[The Refectory]], there is another bifurcation. You can go directly forward to the ladder at {{waypoint 55, 86, 0}} to proceed to the [[Oratory of Naar'Yora]], or you can turn around and head through [[The Gymnasium]]. At the end of the Gymnasium hallway, on the right there is [[The Hatchery (POI)|The Hatchery]], and on the left there is the [[Deliberation Chamber]]. From the Deliberation Chamber, take the ladder at {{waypoint -168, 86, -19}} to reach the Nest of the Overseers.
    - Level 5b: From the [[Nest of the Overseers]], take the elevator at {{waypoint -167, 111, 13}} up to the Enlightenment Aviary.
      - Level 6a: [[Enlightenment Aviary]], spawn place of [[Palace Adjutant Drakknair]]. There are no more ladders up from here.
    - Level 5c: From the [[Oratory of Naar'Yora]], take any of the elevators up to The Great Aviary.
      - Level 6b: From [[The Great Aviary]], take any of the elevators up The King's Roost, the highest level in the dungeon and spawn of [[Enmity of Naar'Yora]].
        - Level 7: [[The King's Roost]].
