## Notes

The Elddar Grove is so named because it is the home of Arbos, the Elddar tree. Arbos's branches are also home to the Tunarian Alliance. The Alliance are the [[Scout|Scouts]] of [[Qeynos]]. To the west of Arbos you will find the Dojo of the [[Silent Fist Clan]] of [[Monk|Monks]]. These devotees of the Martial Arts seek to turn their entire bodies into weapons of Peace.

## Quests

- [[Gildas Cedartree]] has [[Armor Quest Timeline|tasks]] that need doing, in exchange for Cloth Armor.
- The wardens of the Tunarian Alliance have many [[Qeynos City Tasks Timeline|writs for guilded adventurers]].
- [[Rask Helstot]], standing under a tree north of the [[Silent Fist Dojo]], may have found a way into the [[Shattered Vale Timeline|Shattered Vale]], but he needs your help!
