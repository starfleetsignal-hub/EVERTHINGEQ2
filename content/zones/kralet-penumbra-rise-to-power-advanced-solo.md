The two final bosses are up a lift.

As you walk into the northernmost room, in the middle of the floor is a round disc with a chain coming out of it.

The lift activation is via a small lever just to the south of the lift.  Make sure you're on the circle of the lift prior to pulling the lever.

On the 1st floor, kill the listener, then get back on the lift.  the lever is to the west of the lift.  Take it to the second floor, kill the final boss.
