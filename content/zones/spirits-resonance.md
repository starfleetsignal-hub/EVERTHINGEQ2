## Notes

- See [[Nortlav the Scalekeeper]] for strategy on the last named.
- Key mob is a gargantuan graveyard stinkbug.
- You have to clear all the bosses one by one to progress through the dungeon.
  - The Possessing Spirit of Kyrenna in  the Antechamber of Resonance
  - Upstairs into Artesian Basis to get Al'turr the Handyman
  - Then to the back of the room and  follow the snowy tunnel to the Saturnine of Hollows (Magicmelter the Summoner has NOT spawned yet) continue along the wall to the next archway to the next snowy tunnel
  - Follow through to The Vessel of Extent and dispatch Resercher Jal'Dea's Restless Spirit
  - Now return to the Saturnine Hollow and kill all the Graveyard Watchers
  - Magicmelter the Summoner will have spawned aggro nearby.  Kill him to open the doors in Antechamber of Resonance and give access to the Gallery of Soot.
  - Then go down to the Gallery of Soot and clear all the fire mobs. Once you do that, Tyrid Pyreflame will spawn. Kill him, and that opens the door to Norltav Scalekeeper.
