## Notes

- This instance is also refered to as 'Steelbone Family Crypt', since that is the name that appears on the in-game map.
- Since the [[The City of Freeport|Freeportian]] nobles of old used to intern their dead in [[Turmoil Cemetery]], it can be assumed that the Steelbone Family was a wealthy family of Freeport.
- The art-style in the instance is that of the [[Qeynos Catacombs Timeline|Qeynos Catacombs]], and the instance-lock is even titled, [[The Sepulcher of Jahnda]], an instance within the [[Vermin's Snye]].
