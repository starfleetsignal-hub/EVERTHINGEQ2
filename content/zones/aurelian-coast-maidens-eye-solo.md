## Walkthrough

1. Defeat [[The Drudge Lord]] at {{waypoint -307, 4, -220}}
   - Casts [[Shuffling Death]] (curse): decrease critical bonus by 5000 ends in fear.
   - Summons adds.
1. Defeat [[Xylox the Poisonous]] at {{waypoint -513, 32, -135}}
   - Casts a shield that causes no damage for a few seconds.
   - Nox damage
     - Solo:
     - Heroic: As the mob is rooted while it is using it's "deadly" poison cloud, go at 50m distance. To do that, clear your planned walk path of trash mobs.
   - Stuns
1. Defeat [[Shadowed Abomination]] at {{waypoint -545, 60, 57}}
   - Has a knockback
1. Defeat [[VaDyn Kar]] at {{waypoint -682, 59, -132}}
   - Small knockback with heavy mana drain: Cast with the words "Crush, Crush, Crush.". Then you have to avoid the red circles on the ground, e.g by running around [[VaDyn Kar]]
   - casts [[Clinging Shadows]] (curse): decrease potency by 10000
   - Summons adds.
1. Defeat [[The Shadow Overlord]] {{waypoint -664, 77, 84}}
   - Kill [[Shadowed Lord of Magic]] and [[Shadowed Lord of Might]] to remove damage reduction.
   - Casts "Whispers of Shadow" (stun + dot) combined with "Spectral Shadow" (dot)
