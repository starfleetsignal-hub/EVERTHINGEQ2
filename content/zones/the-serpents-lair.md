- The nest where [[Putrid]] doth coil.
- Entrance from [[The Serpent Sewer]] at: {{waypoint -86, 1.4, -89}}

## Notes

- Single room zone with two large groups on either side. Once they are cleared, [[Putrid]] and her broodlings immediately spawn, wandering all over the room.
- Although no longer required for entry, the original way to access this zone was to complete two quests for [[Cicero Puer]].
