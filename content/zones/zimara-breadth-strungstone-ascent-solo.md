## Notes

Don't forget your shinies after you killed a named.

## Walkthrough

1. Kill [[Rubblethrong]] {{waypoint 634, 206, -518}} to make the teleporter activate. (Summons adds)
1. Use the crystal shards at {{waypoint 647, 207, -461}} to teleport to Claved Fate Islets
   - The crystal shards deposit you at {{waypoint 709, 176, 268}}
1. Kill [[Bolagehera]] roams around {{waypoint 751, 186, 343}}
1. Kill [[Barlanka]] roams around {{waypoint 536, 282, 542}}
   - *Attacking a Trial Guardian will call all the MOBs in the immediate area and [[Barlanka]] to attack*
1. Use the crystal shards at {{waypoint 342, 402, 599}} to 'Traverse the expanse' to Sprungstone Peaks
   1. The shards deposit you at {{waypoint 217, 379, 651}}
1. Kill [[Crisj'Jen the Bold]] {{waypoint 88, 383, 727}}
   - This mob pulls you forward.  NO, this is knockback.
     - Possible Strat -- move around constantly
     - Put your back against a wall.
   - NOTE: My experience was that she knocked back and she knocked me over the edge. Suggest back against a rock to fight her. Also, once I killed the two dogs, she summoned two more.
   - Note 11/12/24:  She knocked me back 3 times.  After the first time I put my back to a rock, and she only kicked me a few feet (into the rock.)  After the third time she suddenly got behind me.  She could have kicked me away from the rock at that point, but she died.  There is a moment of "stuck" after you land from her knockback.  I inched forward each time so her next kick would just put me back against the rock again.  I saw no indication that she pulled me forward, but she may have spun me around that last time.
1. Kill [[Fuejenyrus]] {{waypoint -247, 434, 865}}
   - Does a knockback and teleports you. Make sure to stand against one of the pillars.
   - If you get kicked over the edge, it is possible to land on a ledge and continue the fight from there or run back up a rock -254.03 432.19 882.89 to the ruined gazebo.
