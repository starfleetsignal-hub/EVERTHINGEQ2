## Notes

- Scars of Destruction T4 Raid
- This raid-zone is a 1-Boss zone.  *([[Biethimut, Deepwater Titan]])*
  - Make sure you bring plenty of [[Pickleback (Alcoholic)|Pickleback]] to drink during the final boss encounter.  *(May be purchased in Sodden)*
- Two islands - Khaghar and Deepawater Watch
- Khaghar
  - The raid force starts out on Khaghar island
  - The raid must eliminate all of a specific undead monster  from the rest of the trash mobs on the island before being allowed to move to the next.
  - Once the final required target mob has been defeated, a travel vehicle will appear at the top outter-ledge of the sacrificial pit area.  Each raid member much click this balloon to travel to the next island.
- Deepwater Watch
  - Once on the beach, the raid must fight their way to the one (and only) boss, [[Biethimut, Deepwater Titan]] at the lighthouse.
  - Along the way, undead will drop special non-stacking skeletal parts, a large number of these parts are required to summon the boss.  Once the raid reaches the lighthouse, players with the various skeletal parts simply click on the pile of bones to add their own collected parts to it (one-click needed for each part added).  Once enough parts are added to the pile, the boss will spawn.
    - Occasionally, the fight up from the beach to the Lighthouse does not yield enough parts to spawn the boss.  Thankfully, the respawn timer for trash on this island is relatively short.  Simply make your way back towards the beach and the raid should encounter already-respawning trash.  Rinse & Repeat until enough parts are recovered and added to the pile.
    - **NOTE:** *If you leave the 2nd island **for any reason**  (zoning, dieing, etc) the collected parts in your own inventory will immediately disappear.*
      - Since releasing after death will send you back to the first island - if you have collected skeletal parts in your inventory, wait for resurrection each time you die, or the parts in your inventory will be lost.  It is generally considered a good idea to have designated looters for the skeletal parts, to better keep track of them, and also to minimize risk of loosing them to raid wipes, carelessness, etc.
