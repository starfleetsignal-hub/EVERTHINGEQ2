## Background

Behind the veil of beauty and simplicity that shrouds [[Antonica]], remnants of the [[War of Plagues]] can still be found corrupting the landscape. What was known as the Antonican Basin to older generations is known amongst younger generations as *The Firemyst Gully*, a cess-pit of death and decay.

## Notes

- This instance no longer requires an access quest to enter. The former access quest was [[Lord Grimrot's Scythe]].
- The other low-level instance is: [[Firemyst Gully: A Foul Wind]], which requires you to be on the quest [[A Foul Wind]].

## Walkthrough

![With An old dwarven shovel in your inventory, click on the dirt pile to force zombies to spawn.](images/Firemyst_Gully_graves.png)
![Right click and select the option to destroy the ward.](images/FiremystGully2.png)

- In a camp near the entrance, to the left, is [[Sir Thothur Dorarr (NPC)|Sir Thothur Dorarr]]. He gives the only Firemyst Gully quest, [[Cleansing of the Firemyst Gully]].
- Buy [[An old dwarven shovel]] from [[Brother Morrim]].<br>

**Your target is [[Galintari the Apocryphal]]**. He's located in the small skull-shaped hut in the middle of the zone.

- If you simply rush Galintari, dozens of zombies will pop out of the graves and *will* wipe you. You can use the shovel you bought to dig up the graves so you can fight the zombies a few at a time. They won't despawn if they kill you though, so if you run into trouble it may be faster to die, resurrect and clean them out afterwards. There's even a mender in the zone.
- Alternatively, if you destroy all the wards (impaled skeletons) throughout the instance, the large zombie pack will not spawn and you can engage Galintari safely.
