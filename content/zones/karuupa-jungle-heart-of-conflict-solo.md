## Notes

- The zone unlocks in one of two ways:
  - By starting the [[Rumble in the Jungle]] quest.
    - This leads into [[Bona Fide Treasure Seeker]], which also sends you to this zone — required for [[Visions of Vetrovia: Evil Dedraka]].
    - All of this can be completed before finishing [[Visions of Vetrovia: Evil Dedraka]].
  - By completing [[Visions of Vetrovia: Evil Dedraka]], granting the account flag **[[VoV Flag: Karuupa Jungle Unlock]]**.

## Walkthrough Summary

- [[Mean Green]] — Kill Thorny Lashvines to trigger the event; destroy roots and botanicals to drop his shield.
- [[Sumedorga]] & [[Agrodemus]] — Handle power-drain trauma and follow emote cues for sparkling bushes.
- [[Nakka Nakka]] — Summons adds frequently; defeat him to unlock the Pteranodon transport.
- [[Gharaka]] — “Donkey Kong” fight; dodge barrels and reach the top for the [[Barreling Basin Blue Ribbon]] achievement.
- [[Ugweepai]] — Possesses his adds in rotation; kill them first or the fight breaks. Updates [[Rumble in the Jungle]].

## Walkthrough

Each encounter follows a clear trigger. Read each phase before starting — several fights can break if steps are skipped or done out of order.

---

### [[Mean Green]]

1. **Trigger:** Kill a solo [[Thorny Lashvine]] at {{waypoint 469, 89, -286}} to start the ring event.
   - <br>
1. **Spawn:** Five [[Thorny Lashvine|thorny lashvines]] appear around {{waypoint 562, 81, -324}}. Defeating them spawns Mean Green inside the giant tree.
   - <br>
1. **Mechanic:** Mean Green is protected by five [[Maniacal Botanical]] “counters.”
   - → Destroy the **Latching Roots** next to each [[Maniacal Botanical]].
   - → Then kill the Botanical itself.
   - → Repeat until all five are dead — only then can Mean Green be damaged.
   - <br>
1. **Important:** Once you engage the first Botanical, your health will tick down whenever you are **not** engaged in combat.
   - <br>
1. **Suggested Route:** Start with the furthest Botanical (to the left/clockwise of Mean Green) at {{waypoint 628, 89, -427}}, and work around the tree to minimise downtime.

---

### [[Sumedorga]] and [[Agrodemus]]

1. **Trigger:** After defeating six waves of [[Ambushing Dramodon|ambushing dramodons]] at {{waypoint 640, 65, -214}}, the encounter begins.
   - <br>
1. **Mechanic:** When an emote says, *"One of the flower bushes sparkles with life!"*, move the named to that bush before resuming DPS.
   - <br>
1. **Detriments:**
   - → Both bosses inflict **Trauma** (power drain) and **Noxious** effects — cure Trauma immediately.
   - → Adds spawn during the fight; kill them quickly.
   - <br>
1. **If You Die:** The named hides in a bush at {{waypoint 582, 82, -189}}. Re-engage there.

---

### [[Nakka Nakka]]

1. **Location:** {{waypoint 631, 60, -41}}
   - <br>
1. **Mechanic:** Calls in adds throughout the fight.
   - <br>
1. **Next Step:** After defeating Nakka Nakka, use the [[Pteranodon Pterror]] at {{waypoint 559, 60, -54}} to reach the next area.

---

### [[Gharaka]] (“Donkey Kong” Fight)

1. **Location:** {{waypoint 388, 116, -104}}
   - <br>
1. **Mechanic:**
   - → Avoid the yellow root snare nodes that appear across the area.
   - → Gharaka throws barrels down the slope and spawns adds that patrol.
   - → Your goal is to reach the top of the hill (to the line of blue lights) within **3 minutes**.
   - → Collect hammers along the path to destroy barrels and defeat adds.
   - <br>
1. **Achievement:** Completing this climb during the fight awards **[[Barreling Basin Blue Ribbon]]**.
   - <br>
1. **Shortcut:** If you prefer to skip the barrel challenge, use the portal at {{waypoint 262, 48, -7}}.

---

### [[Ugweepai]]

1. **Location:** {{waypoint 480, 164, -140}}
   - <br>
1. **Mechanic:** Ugweepai possesses one of his adds at a time — [[Heshwani]], [[Veranir]], or [[Ceron]].
   - → Watch the emotes — they indicate which add is currently active and must be killed.
   - → **WARNING:** Do **not** kill [[Ugweepai]] before all three adds are defeated, or the encounter will break.
   - <br>
1. **Hazards:**
   - → Yellow root snares cause stuns and shift positions during the fight.
   - → Blue storm zones appear randomly; stepping inside causes knockback.
   - → Ugweepai applies a heavy **Trauma** debuff:
   - –80% Accuracy and Strikethrough
   - –100% Ability Cast Speed
   - –500 Haste
   - <br>
1. **Quest Update:** Killing Ugweepai updates [[Rumble in the Jungle]].
