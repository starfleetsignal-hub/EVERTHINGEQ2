## Walkthrough

- Run down to the other end, picking up all the constructs on the way, then AoE them down.  Timed quest completes.
- Head to the left (the door at the other end does not open), when you kill the first creature, new timed quest:  kill all the protectors, one of each type.  These are the adds/mobs, not the boss creatures.
- Kill named 1 at the end of this corridor, the nameds pop sequentially, so pulling the whole zone is not possible.
- Head for the far 2 corridors, turn toward the diseased skeletons, at end, kill named 2, head for the fire elementals, and now the fire protector should be present.  Kill named 3.
- There were two doorways locked by magic when you ran past them to gather the constructs (back toward the beginning of the zone).
- One doorway is now open.  Before you go down there, stand next to the closed doorway and AoE, you will be able to kill a magic protector this way without having to wait longer. Then go forward and go down the open corridor, killing named 4. (The timed quest now completes.)
- The second door is now open and you can complete the zone, killing [[Lachina Protector of Magics]].
