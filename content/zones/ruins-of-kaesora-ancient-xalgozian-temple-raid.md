To spawn Xalgoz you need to clear all other named mobs WITHOUT DYING. This includes mercs. Must also be done at color. Ive ran this zone over and over and at color without any deaths seems to be the only way he'll spawn. Killing the initial Named mobs this way will cause Sentinel Primatious to spawn at the end of the zone. Kill him to get to Xalgoz.

If there is an alternate way, someone chime in.
