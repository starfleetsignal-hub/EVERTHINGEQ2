## Walkthrough without Quest (copy from Solo)

*Note: Don't forget your shinies!*

1. Magmalatorr: Interrupts with a detriment (can be cured). Tank and spank.
1. Tkesh'Tura: Knockback, detriment (can be cured).
1. Once dead, take the teleport that's in the form of an orange crystal on the top of the little hill to the right.
1. Kusala;Din: You may want to DPS his hands first (but don't need to) as he knocks you around the area every few seconds. Once they're down, just burn him.

## Walkthrough with Quest (copy from Solo)

1. Speak to [[Miranna Ameema]] when you first zone in.
1. Speak with [[Kusala'Din]] at approximately {{waypoint -738, 63, 403}} he will summon a [[Magmalatorr]].
1. Kill the [[Magmalatorr]] using your Heroic Opportunities. **NOTE:** He only takes damage from Heroic Opportunities.
1. Continue to clear the zone until [[Kusala'Din]] appears again at {{waypoint -555, 79, 678}}
   - Talk to him and he will summon a creature from another realm.
1. Kill the [[Tkesh'Tura]] using your Heroic Opportunities.
1. A Zakir Rish Conduit will spawn. Defeat it.
1. You will receive the message: Continue doing what is necessary to stop the metal djinn incursion. (1)
1. Go to {{waypoint -776, 84, 619}}  and defeat the next Zakir Rish Conduit.
1. Continue doing what is necessary to stop the metal djinn incursion. (2)
1. Use the teleporter at {{waypoint -792, 84, 618}} to go to Molten Island.
1. Confront [[Kusala'Din]] again.
   - Defeat him using your Heroic Opportunities.
