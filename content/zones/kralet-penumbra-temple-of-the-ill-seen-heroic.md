## Strategy

1. Follow the hall south to the first platform, then west up the stairs where you will encounter the first named ahead, [[Grelikor (Heroic)|Grelikor]].
1. Head east up the stairs to the first intersection, then turn south and follow the path to the next named, [[Klandoth (Heroic)|Klandoth]].
1. Next head west up the stairs to the first intersection and take the stairs north into the room for the next roaming named, [[Tlat-a-tor (Heroic)|Tlat-a-tor]].
1. Exit the room due south and continue straight down the hallway until it turns east into a room with the next named [[Yagg-kythor (Heroic)|Yagg-kythor]].
1. Head out of the room and jump down to the bottom floor.
1. Make your way to the west side of the room and the hallway going south to the zone boss, [[Xoth, the Ever Rending (Heroic)|Xoth, the Ever Rending]].
