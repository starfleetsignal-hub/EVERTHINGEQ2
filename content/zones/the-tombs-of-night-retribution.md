## Background

This instance is a specialized version of [[The Tombs of Night]] for the quests [[Symbols of the Damned]] and [[A Far Greater Menace]] and for the *Hand of Marr* or the quests [[Mysterious Artifacts]] and [[Betrayal of Brotherhood]] for the *Brethren of Night*. These are the third and fourth quests in each series.
