## Walkthrough

1. [[Zabrina Selymes]]
   - Clear the room of mobs, including destroying (melee attacks) the restraining sigil and the enthralled morsel it releases,  to spawn [[Zabrina Selymes]] the Royal Butler.
   - Burn her down.
     - She will summon increasing numbers of enthralled werewolves and each time she does she will become invulnerable.
     - She will also emote in the direction of one of these adds in each group - "*taunts you as she eyes the sigil to the <x>*" - you need to kill that one to remove her invulnerability and continue your burn down.
1. Use the hidden bookcase doorway at {{waypoint 19, -50, 250}}.
1. Progress down the hallway to a second hidden door at {{waypoint 16, -50, 355}}  (It is a brown wall when seen from the passage, a bookcase when inside the room).
1. [[Veldahn Keyven]] {{waypoint 58, -50, 383}}
   - Kill the risen wretch and undying priest mobs in the room to activate [[Veldahn Keyven]]. He will periodically knock you back, then summon portals to raise mobs through. He progressively spawns more portals each time.
   - Each round gains a new add and, to prevent regeneration, must be killed in the correct order. Kill the adds in the order they are summoned; First wave mob first, second wave mob second, etc.
     - Example: If the first is a zombie, that mob type is always the first to be killed, then the second wave mob is a banshee, the kill order will always be zombie-banshee; then if the third wave is an aerakyn, the kill order will be zombie-banshee-aerakyn; etc.
1. [[Mayong Mistmoore]] and [[Vishra (VoV)|Vishra]]
   - Enter Mayong's throne room {{waypoint 43, -50, 439}}.
   - To begin, clear the room of all vampires, bat colonies, witches, werewolves, and restraining sigils with their restrained persons.
   - [[Mayong Mistmoore]] will yank you to him, and monologue, and then you must fight him.
   - Mayong has enthralled [[Vishra (VoV)|Vishra]] to fight with him. Burn Vishra down to ~10% and then pull him to the pool in the middle and 'kill' him once he emotes about breaking free and delivering a message. Don't stand in the pool - stand on the edge and attack Vishra until he dies.
   - [[Mayong Mistmoore]] yell out for his werewolves to start bringing him meals. Kill the werewolves to prevent them feeding their master. Repeat a few times until [[Mayong Mistmoore]] gets flustered at the 1% mark and flies away to end the encounter.
   - Do not use AOE's around the gargoyle-like statues. They will come to life and takes quite awhile to kill.
