This is a higher level version of the original [[Scornfeather Roost]] zone that was first introduced with the Desert of Flames expansion. You can select this instance by clicking on the entrance to the [[Clefts of Rujark]].

The progress through this instance is identical to the original version. You must kill all of the named bosses, and then [[Ibtinaya the Scorned (fabled)|Ibtinaya the Scorned]] will become attackable.

## Notes

- You must be adventure level 94 to enter this zone.
- The rewards are level 95 legendary and fabled items.
