## Notes

- Zone in using the portal in Myrist at {{waypoint 785, 412, -378}}
- Various portions of Awuidor are under water. To avoid drowning, use a waterbreathing spell, the [[Fishbone Earring]], [[Totem of the Otter]], a tinkered [[Rebreather]], etc.
- For the most part, if you wish, you can edge your way around the outside edges of the platforms to reach an emissary while avoiding aggroing most of the trash mobs.
- Throughout the zone you will occasionally get a red emote that says "A water spout has opened near you!" and you will be thrown up in the air.  It seems like a random occurrence. (It's Not 'throughout' the zone, it's only when you're fighting.  All the mobs have this ability)
- Throughout the zone, wherever possible, fight in the water since almost everything will knock-up or knock-back, and staying in the water, or pulling to the water neutralizes this.
- If something manages to kick you up and toward the edge of the platform, you can steer yourself somewhat, try to point back to the platform, or try to land in [[The Briny Waters]] at the bottom, where there are a couple of portals to return you to the platforms.

## Bosses

1. [[Hirpo the Frosted Spine]] is located at {{waypoint 152, 489, -129}} and is spawned by killing [[An emissary of Awuidor]] at {{waypoint -220, 321, -127}}.      **(Note: swim up the water tube.)**
   - Stun/stifle, a knockback and a power drain.
   - Cure detriments immediately to avoid the power drain.
   - Pull him back and put your back to the water tunnel to neutralize the knockback. He'll still knock you back, but you hit the water and stop.
   1. Note: The Barrier preventing upward progress is sadistically left in place until the NEXT emissary is killed.
1. [[Torrent of Awuidor]] is located at {{waypoint 1, 668, -187}} and is spawned by killing [[An emissary of Marr]] at {{waypoint -174, 489, -129}}.    **(Note: swim up the water tube.)**
   - Cure detriments immediately to avoid the power drain.
   - Pull him back and fight from the water in the center of the platform to neutralize the knockback.
   - At 0% health, he splits into two [[Raging Torrent of Awuidor]].
1. [[Etrigon Icefist]] is located at {{waypoint 263, 321, 140}} and is spawned by killing [[An emissary of the veiled]] at {{waypoint -239, 321, 137}}.  **Note: use the travel portal that spawns.**
   - Etrigon emotes *"How about we freeze you all and see what sticks?"* and starts spawning ice crystals around the platform.
   - Cure detriments immediately. He does have a power drain, also elemental and slashing damage.
1. [[Grobnor the Elder Orb]] is located at {{waypoint -154, 489, 135}} and is spawned by killing [[An emissary of vapor]] at {{waypoint 164, 489, 130}}.  **Note:  Travel up the water tube.**
   - Cure detriments immediately to avoid the power drain. He has four separate power drain detriments.
   - Grobnor has a bad fear. Crouch/walk will help to keep you from sprinting off the platform to your death.
   - Knockback.
1. [[Tethys All-Mother]] is located at {{waypoint 0, 356, 4}} and is spawned by killing [[An emissary of the deep]] at {{waypoint 1, 668, 200}}.
   1. **Note for the emissary, travel up the water tube.**
   1. **For [[Tethys All-Mother]], use the portal that opens up at  {{waypoint -173, 489, -138}}.**
      - Cure detriments immediately to avoid her two power drains.

## Walkthrough

Head east from the zone in.  The path branches south and loops around and meets back up, it doesn't matter which way you go.  Keep heading to the platform with all the mobs, and kill them all to clear the way to [[An emissary of Awuidor]] at {{waypoint -220, 321, -127}}.  You will see an emote, *"Massive footsteps ring out above you and to the west."*  Head back the way you came to the water tunnel at {{waypoint -7, 337, -129}} which is no longer blocked and is now accessible.

Swim up to the intersection where you can exit going east or west and head out the west exit.  Go down to the platform and confront the first boss in the zone, [[Hirpo the Frosted Spine]].

Head back and around the water tunnel to the platform on the other side and clear the mobs, including [[An emissary of Marr]] at {{waypoint -174, 489, -129}}.  Killing this emisssary triggers the emote *"Lightning and thunder sounds come from above you."* and spawns the next name.

Head back to the water tunnel and continue up, exiting at the very top.  Be prepared, you will most likely aggro a bunch of [[A chaotic torrent]] upon exiting the water.  You might want to try to exit on the south side, as the next named, the [[Torrent of Awuidor]] has set up camp on the north side.  When you are ready, kill the Torrent, which opens a portal at {{waypoint -2, 668, -53}}.  Use it.

This brings you back down onto that branched off path that looped around right at the beginning, only now the wall of water is gone.  Head south and then east and take note of another blocked water tunnel as you pass.  Head onto the platform and clear your way to [[An emissary of the veiled]] at {{waypoint -239, 321, 137}}.

Killing the emissary triggers an emote *"The air becomes significantly colder"* and spawns [[Etrigon Icefist]] at {{waypoint 263, 321, 140}} on the platform on the other side of the blocked water tunnel. Head there now and kill him, opening up access to the water tunnel. Swim up the water tunnel at {{waypoint 0, 337, 136}} and exit to the west at the intersection.

Head to the platform and kill everything, including [[An emissary of vapor]] at {{waypoint 164, 489, 130}}.  This triggers the emote *"The sound of teeth chomping together comes from the east"* and spawns [[Grobnor the Elder Orb]] on the platform on the other side of the water tunnel.  Head there and kill him.

Go back to the water tunnel and swim to the top.  Clear the mobs up top and kill [[An emissary of the deep]] at {{waypoint 1, 668, 200}}.  The final name will spawn with an emote of *"You sense a powerful presence"* and another portal will appear at {{waypoint -1, 668, 60}}.  Use it to travel back down to the center of the first floor, where [[Tethys All-Mother]] is waiting with 3 named adds.  Kill them to finish the zone.

The portal to exit the zone is located at {{waypoint -47, 349, 29}}.
