This is the new Hubzone for all Chronoportals during the [[Chronoportal Phenomenon]] event.

### Fabled Versions

- Fabled versions of portals do not scale at all.
  - Mobs will be level 116 in most Fabled options, with exception of [[Portal to the Past: Southern Desert of Ro (Public Quest)]] which is always current max adventure level.
- All named mobs have 587 bil hp and 37.5k combat mit
- All trash mobs in the zone have 38 bil hp and 32.3k combat mit

### Travel

As this replaced the old portals there are a lot of different ways to access this Hubzone, see the Adjacent Zones section..
