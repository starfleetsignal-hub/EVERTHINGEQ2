## Prerequisites

*To enter, you must have completed [[Visions of Vetrovia: Handle With Scare]] or have earned the [[VoV Flag: Mahngavi Wastes Unlock]].

## Walkthrough

Note: For trash-mobs: Have a eye on detrimental, some reduce your critical chance to 0.

1. Defeat [[Krel Korek Mal]] at {{waypoint -142, 101, 87}}: Has kickback.  Emotes "Swings a massive cudgel at your head", then has an insta kill frontal.
1. Defeat [[Warlock Ren Bolor]] at {{waypoint 343, 107, -16}}: Tank 'n spank; Adds have kickback. Additional adds spawn every 25%(?)
1. Defeat [[Oororr the Corrupted]] at {{waypoint 503, 89, 277}}: Tank'n spank - Spawns large number of ooze adds every 20%. If you fight at the edge of the pool, the adds will leash before they reach you.
1. Defeat [[Felazhor Khan]] at {{waypoint -79, 101, 206}}: Tank'n spank - Summons pairs of adds every 20%. Advised to clear all "blood of the dead" before initiating boss fight as they will be summoned at the start of the fight.
1. Defeat [[Lagrecia Vyl'Tayne]] and the sidekicks [[Drivazia]] and [[Chernala]] at  {{waypoint -54, 96, 614}}: Clear all Zombies on the graveyard, as they will summoned at named-pull. During the fight adds, [[The Dead Caller]], will summoned every 20%. Beside the first incomming high damage of the summoned zombies: Tank'n spank.
