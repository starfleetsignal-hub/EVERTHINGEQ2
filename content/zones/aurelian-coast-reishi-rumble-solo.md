## Notes

- Zone from [[Aurelian Coast]] using the portal at{{waypoint 113.79, 57.52, -656.59}}

## Walkthrough

1. Kill adds and epic x2 an ancient grove tender (yes, kill it).  Your merc will probably die if he gets in front of him during the fight.
1. First name: [[Nerobahan]] {{waypoint 639.24, 23.70, 588.11}}. If not cured, the curse turns in to incurable 10 sec fear.
1. Head to Spore gate at {{waypoint 513.71, 20.23, 504.97}}
   - Kill 5 waves, each bigger than the last.
   - After last wave, passage is granted.
1. Kill groups, kill 2nd epic.
1. Second name: [[Ghest Roppep]] at {{waypoint 352.00, 42.88, 558.65}}
   - Has adds, kill them.
   - Has a curse, not sure what it does.
1. Go to {{waypoint 471.86, 23.35, 564}}
1. Fight a 5 wave set of mobs
1. This should pop [[Ropscion Mindeye]]
   - has adds and nox detriment
   - Teleports you
1. Exit appears at {{waypoint 490.73, 31.96, 596.42}}
