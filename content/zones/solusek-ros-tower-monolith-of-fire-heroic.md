- As of 2026, this zone does not appear to be soloable.  To activate the first boss, it takes 3 group members standing on lit circles around the braziers to raise them.

## Strategy

### Jiva

- The platform he's standing on will disappear at 75% health.
- During the fight, gargoyle spirits will appear in the corners of the pool, and the name will become invulnerable. Each party member must stand inside one of the gargoyles to deal damage to the name.
- Use one of the braziers in the side rooms to get to the next level.
- Both braziers lead to one of the next names. In the corridor, kill everything that moves. If the door to the name is closed, then go back a little and you will notice that fire tornadoes appear near the torches in the corridor. Go to them, and ifrits will appear from them. Kill everyone until the door opens.

### Estryxia

- The name will have to be killed 10 times (in the expert zone 15 times). On the last life, the name will have a strong response.
- During the fight, a wall of fire will appear in the room - it does not cause damage to the group. The wall of fire does not cause damage.
- Use the brazier in the alcove to get to the next name.

### So'Valiz

- There are two columns with raised claws in the hall. During the fight, every 20% (80%, 60%, 40%, 20% and 1%), several claws will bend on one of the columns, and you need to bend the claws on the other column in a mirror image (the southern column is at the entrance). The number of bent claws increases each time (at 80%, 1 claw will be bent, at 60%, 2 claws, etc.). The time for bending the claws is limited - **30 seconds**. If you do not have time - it's a wipe.
- An incorrectly bent claw gives a strong, almost fatal blow.
- The columns alternate (if you first bend on the northern column, then you will bend on the southern one, and so on)
- During the fight, yellow circles will appear on the ground, from which you need to get out.
- Use the brazier in the alcove, and select "Central" to get to the next name.

### Xuzl

- During the fight, at about 80%, the name will become invulnerable.
- To remove the invulnerability, you need to jump high up with the help of the fiery pillars that will beat from the grates on the floor. There, under the ceiling, you will see fiery stripes in the shape of a star, you need to jump on them, and destroy five crystals in which swords are walled up. The swords will fall down.
- When all five crystals are destroyed, jump down and destroy the swords.
- During the fight, the name will summon hells.
- Use the brazier /waypoint 0, 102, 9 to go further.
- You will get into a looped corridor. You need to beat 8 hells that wander along the corridor and activate the petal-steps on the round platforms in the alcoves of the corridor. The number of steps you activate is the number of steps on the platform with the name. By and large, you do not need to activate all 8 steps, one is enough to jump onto the platform to the final name))
- When all 8 hells are destroyed, use the western or eastern brazier to get to the name.

### **Avatar of the Sun**

- Hail to Solusek Ro to activate him.
- During the fight, at about 89% Solusek Ro will disappear and the Avatar of the Sun will appear, an epic mob of lvl 117.
- **DPS check**. At least 6 players wearing RAID T1 gears if **2 player DPS.**  Expert heroic 32 resolve gears **if 4 player DPS**.
- Kill it before Solusek Ro return. A countdown 180 sec on named buff window
