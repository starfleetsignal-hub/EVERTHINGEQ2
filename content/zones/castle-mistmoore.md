## Lore

Towering over the [[Loping Plains]] looms the ancient haunt of vampire-kind, Castle Mistmoore. The castle is ruled by [[Mayong Mistmoore (Contested)|Mayong Mistmoore]], whose family has dwelt here since ages past. This is no mere [[The Tombs of Night|cave in the side of a cliff]], this is a fortress and a blood-sucking army lives within it. As such, Mayong requires a vast amount of blood. Fortunately for him and his cohorts, the native elf population of [[Faydwer (Continent)|Faydwer]] and a seemingly endless stream of witless adventurers make easy prey for these masters of undeath.
