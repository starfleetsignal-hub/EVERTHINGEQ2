## Background

Fiercely guarded by the valkyrie, Icespire Summit is the abode of the [[Spirit of the Fallen]]. Enter here to claim whatever treasure you may find and perhaps [[Karg's Gratitude|aid the soul]] of one trapped in limbo.

## Notes

- There are a few Heroic mobs to start you off, but then the hall is blocked by a 44 Epic x2.  The mob is a weak epic and can be trio'd, but still recommend taking a good group or a mentored down high level with you.
- Several quests in the [[Everfrost Timeline]] used to be the access quests for this zone. However their completion is no longer necessary to enter. They are listed in the Related Quests section below.
- If you examine the ice crystals in the center of the final room it is revealed that the ice statue above the crystals is the likeness of [[E'ci (God)|E'ci, Goddess of Ice]].
