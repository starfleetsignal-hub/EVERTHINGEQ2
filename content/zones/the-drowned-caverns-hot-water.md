## Background

Someone or something is funk-a-fying the potable water that sustains the Splitpaw Gnolls within [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den|Splitpaw Den]]. [[Khol Mudflank]] has asked for your assistance in solving this mess. If no solution is found in a timely manner then the entire Splitpaw Clan may have to migrate to the surface in search of water.
