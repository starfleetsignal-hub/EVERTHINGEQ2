## Prerequistites

You must have either the [[Spiritual Bedlam]] or [[Vexing Confrontation]] quest to enter this zone.

You can also gain access with the quest, **[[Reign of Shadows: Against Thal Odds]]** , the Signature quest.

## Walkthrough

1. Slay [[Rhag'Xiuhsiss]] in the [[Tower of Conscience]] {{waypoint 87.29, -30.63, -215.17}}.  On the way look for servitors, golems with blue aura and kill them to take away Rhags buff.
1. Find **blue beam of shadow** and step into it to get the "Clinging Shadows" buff {{waypoint 36.14, -19.04, -50.60}}![blue beam](images/BlueBeam_ReignofShadows-AgainstThalOdds.jpg).
1. Use the **teleporter** at {{waypoint -101.72, -3.27, 29.97}}![teleporter](images/Portal1_ReignofShadows-AgainstThalOdds.jpg) to bypass the wisps, which will "cancel" your "Clinging Shadows" buff.
1. Click on **the door** at {{waypoint 0.98, -40.81, -292.30}}![door](images/Door1_ReignofShadows-AgainstThalOdds.jpg) and use the **teleporter** at {{waypoint -10.41, -40.81, -316.19}}
1. Slay [[Enforcer Shaaho]] in the [[Chamber of Reincarnation]] at {{waypoint -82.67, 31.05, -421.38}}.
   - [[Enforcer Shaaho]] has a detrimental  with 10 increments. At 10 or 0 increments, Shaaho one-shots you, so be sure to leave one or two of the adds up (If you kill them all, step into a 'shadow circle' to spawn a new add).
1. Find another **blue beam of shadow** {{waypoint -67.29, 30.95, -196.05}}
1. Use the **teleporter** at {{waypoint -0.60, 20.05, -290.49}}.
1. Speak with [[Aten Ha Ra]] {{waypoint -85, 88, -400}} so that she becomes attackable.
1. Defeat [[Aten Ha Ra]] ![Aten Ha Ra (non-agro)](images/Aten_Ha_Ra-1.jpg)
   - Note: If there are ten [[A soldier of shadows|soldiers of shadows]] alive at once, you will instantly die.
   - Regularly, every ~20sec [[Aten Ha Ra]] summons one [[A soldier of shadows|soldier of shadows]], below 50% health she summons a group of two [[A soldier of shadows|soldiers of shadows]].
   - At every 10% loss of [[Aten Ha Ra|Aten Ha Ra's]] health:
     - She becomes damage-immune.
     - She creates two shadowy triangles: one triangle will be a **Furtive Shadows** (protection) and the other will be a **Disruptive Shadows** (death)
     - [[Aten Ha Ra]] will say "Umbral Detonation!" in chat, then begin casting "[[Umbral Detonation]]" (takes about 3 seconds, watch the casting bar). Protect yourself by clicking on the **Furtive Shadows** triangle. If you click the **Furtive Shadows** triangle too early, the protection will end too early and you will die. Click the **Furtive Shadows** triangle while [[Aten Ha Ra]] is casting [[Umbral Detonation]]. ![Furtive and Disruptive triangles](images/VexThalTriangles.png)
     - Each time you click a triangle, [[A soldier of shadows]] spawns, below 50% health a group of two [[A soldier of shadows|soldiers of shadows]] will add.
