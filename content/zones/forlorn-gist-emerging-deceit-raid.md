## Walkthrough

### [[Envoy to Bloodright]] (Tier 2)

- The Envoy occasionally "motions his arm towards" a certain class (fighters, mages, scouts, priests). Shortly after this emote, an incurable curse will land on everyone in the raid force. The curse is removed from characters of the indicated class that run out more than 20 meters. The curse is removed from characters of all other classes that run in less than 10 meters.
- The Envoy periodically casts a Barrage which can only be mitigated with [[Bulwark of Order]].

### [[G'dalt Spirit-stitcher]] (Tier 2)

- G'dalt occasionally casts an incurable curse on the person farthest from the water well. The curse is removed when that person clicks on the well's rope pully. Clicking the pully also adds one increment to G'dalt. Allowing the curse to expire kills the affected character.
- Two adds periodically spawn on the closest and farthest characters respectively. G'dalt heals based on how close the two adds are when they spawn (less distance results in a larger heal). The maximum range (for the minimum heal) is 30 meters. These two adds co-op strike with each other, but neither will co-op strike with G'dalt.
- G'dalt casts a power drain called **Power Bleed I** which can be mitigated with [[Raid Rune: Mana Bubble I]] or stronger.
- G'dalt periodically casts a Barrage which can only be mitigated with [[Bulwark of Order]].
- You will want to use the stifle cc rune.

### [[Hand of Lithania]] (Tier 3)

- The Hand alternates between delivering "a massive face slap" and "a way too hard slap to the wrist". These attacks are a powerful frontal and rear attack respectively.
- The Hand occasionally casts a curse on the person farthest from itself. This curse is only curable when the afflicted character targets itself.
- At 75%, 55%, and 25%, the Hand splits into smaller copies of itself. These copies have very low health. When they die, the original Hand returns. The alternating slaps may occur while the Hand is split.
- The Hand does not cast Barrage in any form; it is safe to use [[Bulwark of Order]] as a debuff.

### [[The Reanimated Horror]] (Tier 3)

- The Horror has a large frontal and a large rear attack that he switches between, emoting before performing either attack. The attacks do not alternate perfectly, and he usually favors the frontal attack. The frontal attack is accompanies by a loud belching noise.
- Two adds spawn throughout the fight, **a cursed doll jailer** and **a summoned cursed doll**. These adds alternate in perfect sequence. If two of the same type of doll are up at the same time, it is an instant raid wipe.
  - The **cursed doll jailer** can be pick-pocketed by a Swashbuckler or a Brigand to obtain a key to the nearby jail cell.
  - The **summoned cursed doll** does not move and does not attack.
- Occasionally, one of the tanks is ported into the nearby jail cell. The imprisoned player may be released by a Swashbuckler or Brigand who obtained a key from a **cursed doll jailer**. Each key may only be used once. The tank that is selected may or may not be the tank that currently has aggro.
- The Horror periodically casts a Barrage which can only be mitigated with [[Bulwark of Order]].

### [[Lithania Dyrmelia]] (Tier 3)

- At 90% and 40%, three adds spawn at the two doors in the north and the two doors in the south (the door that does not get an add is random each time). The adds do not leave the doorways and do not attack. Lithania takes less damage while these adds are alive.
- At 80%, 55%, and 30%, Lithania casts a powerful ward on herself, equal to 10% of her max health. While the ward is active, her health bar changes to purple, and she continues taking damage as normal. When the ward is complete, Lithania "heals" 10%, back to the health she had right before the ward was cast.
- Every 45 seconds, Lithania casts **Proximity Hex** (an incurable noxious) on a random player, which roots that player. Within the afflicted player's detriment window, the hex specifies the name of another character in the raid. The named character (and only the named character) can cure the hex by right-clicking on the afflicted character's name in the raid window and select "Rescue from the Hex". This action has a limited range and is greyed-out if the characters are too far apart. If the hex is not removed in time, the entire raid wipes.
- Lithania casts a power drain called **Power Bleed II** which can be mitigated with [[Raid Rune: Mana Bubble II]] or stronger.
- Lithania periodically casts a Barrage which can only be mitigated with [[Bulwark of Order]].
