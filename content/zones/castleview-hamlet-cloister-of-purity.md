## Notes

Players may only access Castleview Hamlet: Cloister of Purity if they are on a quest assigning them to enter.  The zone is solo only - other players and mercenaries may not enter.

## Lore

The Renda'Dal of [[New Tunaria]] are seeking to bolster their numbers.   They have made Castleview Hamlet their headquarters, and are attempting to recruit high elves to their cause while driving the frogloks away.
