## Walkthrough

**Palovina Vodlak**

- HP:36.5 tril Mit:265,802
- Cure curse (or person will die)
  - Kill werewolf adds to refresh curse cure
- Run to the test tube of the creature you transform into and self cure or be cured of the arcane detriment (you may need to look up at the ceiling to see who you match)
- 50%, 40%, 30%, 20%, and 10% health will teleport around the room to one of the corners and drag someone with him
- Under 50% health, no longer transmogifies allies but will perform a frontal with curable trauma

**Vulgrat Vilebones**

- Arcane detriment must be left to expire while the ally is not in the ring (go to a platform)
  - Curing the arcane detriment or letting it expire in the wrong place heals the named and dispels buffs from the ally
- Kill vilebone spiders to turn him back to normal, happens at 75%, 50%, and 25% health
  - Vilepone spiders left in the ring when he completes his 1.5 rotations will heal him
- Joust away 6 meters when he casts bone gore or he will heal per ally who fails to joust

**Wretched Francine**

- Interrupt the casting spell or she heals
- Three copies of her run away at 75%, 50%, and 25% health -- the one with the witch's hat is the one you should follow
  - Grab cauldron and place it in the room she runs to before the curse ends or the group wipes
- Stay within 9 meters of her and cure everything

**Viktor Vodlak**

- HP:36.5 tril   Mit:266,278
- Use Catalogue Creature on the adds and kill when they spawn at 80%, 60%, and 40% health
  - Panther add gives itself and the named a stacking damage buff that can be dispelled
  - Rock creature add gives itself and the named a stacking defensive buff that can be dispelled
  - Mushroom creature add heals the named for 5% every few seconds
- Kill transport book at 20% named health and click the portal it spawns when dead

**Nogrovska Vodlak**

- HP:68.5 tril  Mit:271,002
- 75%, 50%, and 25% health she gains title <negative> or <positive> then summons a golem
  - drag the golem through the OPPOSITE tornado to charge it, then kill it
  - blue=negative and red=positive
  - killing wrong polarity is a group wipe
  - you can attack a tornado to swap its polarity, which will put a debuff on you that only drops when you attack a tornado again
- Curses whole group in a random order with a curse that has a stack number, then has narrative text telling you which number person to curse cure. Have the group put their numbers in chat.
- Occasionally hits an ally with a curable noxious detriment that should be cured either "on land" or "in water"
  - Ally will die if it expires or cured in the wrong spot
  - Correct location is called in narrative text or they can look for a bubble detriment (water) or claw detriment (land)

**The Abandoned Labomination**

- HP:260 tril Mit:310,000
- Unless necessary, all group members should remain stacked at 0 meters distance from the named at all times
  - Group members lose a lot of potency and crit bonus for every meter they are away from the named
- Every 60 seconds will NPCSay "Nuuuummmm!" and then "Berrrrssss!"
  - All group members will be given a numbered curse which they should type in group chat
  - Healers should curse cure only the two people whose numbers equals the buff stack of "Wrath of the Math" the named
  - Ex: P1 has 23 and P3 has 24 and boss buff is 47: you would curse cure those two allies
  - Failure to do this within 30 seconds or curing wrong people will wipe the group
- 95%, 75%, 55%, 35%, and 15% health the named stoneskins to all but one damage type
  - You have to go to the proper slab in the room and click to summon the correct damage type add, then actually kill it gain ability to damage named again (through incurable detriment)
  - The named will emote which damage type to summon.
  - Using spells or combat arts on the named (including indirect AoE) when it is stoneskinned can dispel buffs from you
- 80%, 60%, 40%, and 20% health you will get a emote saying its safe to remove the incurable damage-type detriment via heroic opportunity
  - Start and finish a heroic opportunity to remove it (must be targeting the named)
  - You need all 4 archetypes to do this correctly (fighter, mage, priest, and scout).
  - Doing a heroic opportunity outside of the safety message will wipe the group if it is completed.
- Beginning at 70% health and every 60 seconds until its dead, it will NPCSay "Break yer limmmmmbsssss!"
  - Someone will receive an incurable elemental detriment called Limb Breaker with a broken white bone on a blue background
  - That person needs to stand on the circle ring it was on before the fight started and be cured of the elemental before returning to the fight
  - Bone Breaker causes the player to take huge spike damage until it's cured
  - If the player with Bone Breaker isn't within range of the group when a crawler queen is killed or during a heroic opportunity completion, it will not add the damage type incurable detriment or remove it, so you may have to do those steps twice for their benefit or wait until they cure Bone Breaker
- Beginning at 50% health and every 60 seconds until its dead, it will disarm a random ally of a random gear and create an add
  - The add will be an enchanted cloak or enchanted hands or enchanted shoulders, etc.
  - Dispel the add to make it disenchanted and then kill it to return the disarmed item back to the ally
  - Items that are disarmed do not provide any value (except resolve) to the ally, including removing adornment set bonuses, potency, etc.
- Beginning at 30% health and every 60 seconds until its dead, it will NPCSay "Controoool youuuuuu!"
  - It will begin to cast a short-casting spell called Thought Experiment which you have to interrupt
  - Failing to interrupt, one ally will receive a curable arcane detriment that must be cured within 3 seconds
    - Failing to cure will kill the ally
- If anyone dies with the incurable damage-changing detriment, multiple worm creature adds will spawn along with a pathologist add.  The pathologist will hit the group with curable traumas (cure quickly).  Once recovered, the damage-type add will have to be respawned and killed to replenish the incurable damage-changing detriment for the person who died. Failure to refresh the incurable damage-changing detriment and doing a heroic opportunity will kill the person without the incurable damage-changing detriment. Deaths prior to killing a damage add or right after a heroic opportunity will only spawn the pathologist.
- Damage-type-changing consumables will cause an ally to die in this fight (Flames of Yore, etc.)
