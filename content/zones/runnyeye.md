## Lore

Runnyeye, or Runnyeye Citadel, is the home of The Runnyeye Goblins who settled it while the [[Enchanted Lands]] were still the Misty Thicket. It was not built by goblins, as evidenced by the design of the tunnel systems and the intricacy of the many [[The Gates of Runnyeye|doors]] and pillars throughout the zone. Yet, as the goblins have been living here for centuries, Runnyeye is a mesh of these complex aesthetics and goblin trash-heaps.

[[The Age of Destiny (Lore)|Currently]], the goblins are the masters of Runnyeye. They exert total control of the borefiends, ostensibly over the evil eyes and therefore by extension over the Gorehorn Minotaurs as well. While [[Runnyeye Timeline|exploring the area]], it may be revealed onto the adventurer that the hierarchy within Runnyeye is not so linear, that perhaps the goblins are not so powerful as they claim to be.
