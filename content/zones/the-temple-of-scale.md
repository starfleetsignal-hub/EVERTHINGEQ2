## Background

The most fearsome arsenal of dragonkind dwell here, protecting the [[The Temple of Scale: Pedestal of the Priestess|inner sanctum]]. This is the temple of the Awakened, and the uninitiated will be torn limb from limb.

## Geography

This is a contested raid, meaning multiple, unaffiliated raid groups can enter here, potentially leading to disputes over boss encounters. The layout is identical to [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]].
