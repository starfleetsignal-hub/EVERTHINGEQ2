## Strategy

### [[Kiernun the Lyrical]]

- At least **one player [[Bard]]** is required
- When start fighting, bard gain a new spell called " ". Use it every time it cold down. It generates a **blue bubble**. Entire fight is surrounded around this bubble
- Pay attention to your chat window. about every 40 sec a player is chosen to enjoy deadly song and he/she needs to **quick in&out** that blue bubble to gain 5 sec immune to **Raid-Wipe**
- If more than 2 players get into bubble(or touch it simultaneously) make bubble go **broke** and do AOE damage to your team

### [[Bereth Mathias]]

You need to kill him before you've killed 24 opponents (it is his passive) or he won´t die, so you won't be able to progress. Will summon two adds at some point during the fight [[Keeper of the Ledge]] and [[Keeper of the Cave]] - these should be dealt with in a timely manner while keeping Bereth away from both. If he gets too close to proximity they will blow, wiping the raid. If the keepers are live for too long, he will use their power to wipe the raid. When you kill Bereth initially, open the "chest" and run away so the bomb doesn't kill you. When he revives, kill him quickly again.

### [[Kyrus of the Old Ways]]

Single-attempt named. Has an enrage ability that causes him to reflect spells until it is dispelled.

### [[Htardlem the Wizard|Htardlem]], [[Omzzem the Enchanter|Omzzem]], & [[Uigirf the Magician|Uigirf]]

Two of the three will decide to 'work together' and a warning will prompt that the third will be deadly if allowed to linger near the other two. Split the raid force and keep them away from each other.

### The Captains

- Choice A) [[Captain Graybeard]]
  - +10,000 faction with **The Ashenfell Guard** & -10,000 faction with **The Graybeard Guard**.
- Choice B) [[Captain Ashenfell]]
  - +10,000 faction with **The Graybeard Guard** & -10,000 faction with **The Ashenfell Guard**.

### [[The Forge Golem]]

It appears in the forge area when you kill **ALL** the mobs of the raid. Sea serpents appear from time to time, which you must kill. You can use tracking to help you clear the area completely of enemies. It seems that it only appears if you have killed Captain Ashenfell.
