## Background

![the castle gate](images/The_Forest_Ruins_-_gate.png)
![the castle foyer](images/The_Forest_Ruins_-_foyer.png)
![The Ruined Mage Tower](images/The_Ruined_Mage_Tower.png)

> The [Forest] Ruins are home to some unfortunate folk who have not yet earned their eternal sleep.
>
> — [[Pearl Honeywine]]

Under mysterious circumstances, the complex of the first mage society of [[The City of Qeynos|Qeynos]], The Order of Three, was destroyed. All that is left is a large dusty ruin filled with pirates by day and skeletons by night. The current Qeynosian mage society, [[The Concordium (Faction)|The Concordium]], in conjunction with The Qeynos Guard, look after the stability of the zone. While the recent incursion of Blackfurl Pirates and the constant presence of the Ruins' former inhabitants provides a constant threat to the safety of Qeynos, The Concordium (with help from adventurers) see that that The Forest Ruins remain accessible for study. Evidence that powerful latent energy is still present in the area are the elementals that linger motionlessly upon the perimeter of the central ruin. Further evidence is the great [[The Basalt Watcher|Basalt Watcher]] that appears to have a mind of its own. Recently, however, the threat is a much more tangible one of gnollish proportions...

The monsters inside the ruins change based on the time of day. At two times during the day, a zone-wide message will appear, indicating which mobs are currently occupying the inside of the ruins. At daybreak, when the Blackfurl Pirates occupy the central ruin, a message appears:

![](images/Undeadretreatforestruins.jpg)

## Geography

### Travel

After the Qeynos suburbs were blocked off during the [[Update:64|LU64]] Qeynos revamp, a Mariner's Bell was installed near the gate.  Currently the Mariner's Bell is the only way in or out of the zone.

- **Evil-alignment**: The bell is within aggro radius of the gate guards!
- **Evil on Fallen Gate server**: You must zone in from the Mariner's Bell in [[The Caves]]
