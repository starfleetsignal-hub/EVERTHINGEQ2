## Walkthrough

See [[Savage Weald: Chaotic Caverns (Solo)]].

1. **Brutal Bloodbath** (4,330 Resolve): Spawns when you kill the lookouts. But you have to kill the bloodpriests in the back of the room who are constantly healing the boss.  Once the bloodpriests are down, tank and spank, killing every bloodpriest add as it spawns.
1. **The Spelobanzeothitem** (4,330 Resolve): Has immunity from damage for the first 20 hits, then a simple turn and burn.
1. **Forblarg the Foul** (4,330 Resolve): Spawns adds throughout the fight, each bolstering him. Don't have more than 4 adds up at once.
   1. Click the bones in the center of the room to climb down to the next room.
1. **Morbigog** (4,330 Resolve): Setup in the center of the room. Pull the boss. He will spawn a crystal ("a balanzite eruption") atop someone. Kill it. After you kill the crystal, an add pops. Kill the add. Back to the boss. During the crystal/add phase, ground spawn rocks will appear. Joust out of those or you die. Cheetah or similar speed buff is helpful here.
   1. Climb up the rope to resume the dungeon run.
1. **Shred of Tashakhi** (4,345 Resolve): In reality, if you've done even just some of the main content leading you to this dungeon and are wearing 220-ish resolve gear, it's just a tank and spank with killing an occasionally spawned add. Otherwise to follow mechanics: you have to watch for the body of one of your group mates that spawns, and only cure that character. And when he tells you a color, you have to stand under that dome.
