## Walkthrough

This dungeon operates very similarly to a popular mobile game. You must protect the corn field from undead orcs who march towards the plants you grow in defense. Each plant has unique abilities and damages a different kind of enemy. At any point throughout the zone, you may remove an existing plant and replace it with another type.

- **Spore Shooter**: Shoots spores at a steady pace. Good against all types of skeletons with at least one leg.
- **Snow Spore Shooter**: Shoots snow spores that stop skeletons with at least one leg in their tracks.
- **Spore Popper**: Pop low-altitude spores that damage legless skeletons, but pass right under the leggy variants.
- **Mega Spore Shooter**: Shoots massive spores that explode on contact and inflict high damage to its target and smaller splash damage to others nearby.

Each plant that you cultivate adds one increment of **Green Thumb**, an incurable elemental that inflicts 0.5% damage to max health every 5 seconds, multiplied by increment stacks. See the Talk page for a discussion on planting strategies and which are more effective.

### [[Mungo the Misshapen (Solo)|Mungo the Misshapen]]

*You feel at home on the grange. Should you and all your allies lose this feeling, you will all buy the farm!*

When you first zone in, there is a single linked group of non-aggro [[A cornstalker|cornstalkers]].

*The disturbance has raised the dead, but the farm offers up its soil to help you defend it. Look towards the end of each row!*

After defeating this group, two rows of dirt mounds appear on the east side of the field. Each mound can cultivate either a **Spore Shooter** or a **Snow Spore Shooter** (at your discretion), and the plant will automatically attack the oncoming orcs. At the same time, one-legged [[Rujarkian bonehopper|Rujarkian bonehoppers]] and [[Armored Rujarkian bonehopper|armored Rujarkian bonehoppers]] will appear and slowly march towards the defending plants. If any orc reaches the east side, that orc will instantly kill one player. If all players are killed in this manner, it is an instant wipe, and the encounter resets.

*A Rujarkian scarecrow enters the cornfield to scare you off!*

After some time , [[Mungo the Misshapen (Solo)|Mungo the Misshapen]] will spawn and attack. Undead orcs will continue spawning and marching down the field during this fight. Mungo casts a curable noxious named **Foot Fungus** that slows the target and instantly kills the target on expiration. Mungo also casts a large knockback named **Shoo!** on all players in front of him and within 10 meters. This knockback can be avoided by jousting more than 10 meters or by standing behind Mungo.

### [[Mister Tickster (Solo)|Mister Tickster]]

*You win round one! The cornhusks all cheer and grin from ear to ear!*

After defeating the previous boss, another linked group of non-aggro [[A cornstalker|cornstalkers]] spawns, and you unlock the ability to plant **Spore Poppers**.

*More dead invade, and the farm answers. Another row of succulent soil is available for planting defenses.*

After defeating this group, a third row of dirt mounds appears. At the same time, a mix of one-legged [[Rujarkian bonehopper|Rujarkian bonehoppers]] and [[Armored Rujarkian bonehopper|armored Rujarkian bonehoppers]] and zero-legged [[Rujarkian remains]] and [[Armored Rujarkian remains]] will appear and slowly march towards the defending plants. If any orc reaches the east side, that orc will instantly kill one player. If all players are killed in this manner, it is an instant wipe, and the encounter resets.

*Something's really ticked off. Something BIG!*

After some time , [[Mister Tickster (Solo)|Mister Tickster]] will spawn and attack. Undead orcs will continue spawning and marching down the field during this fight. Tickster casts a curable noxious named **Tickborne Disease** that deals ticking disease damage.

### [[Dedroz the Decomposer (Solo)|Dedroz the Decomposer]]

*You survive the second round! And the corn go bananas!*

After defeating the previous boss, one final linked group of non-aggro [[A cornstalker|cornstalkers]] spawns, and you unlock the ability to plant **Mega Spore Shooters**.

*The deadliest of the undead orcs break through. It will take crops just as deadly to destroy them, which may now be planted. A fourth row of soil is now available!

After defeating this group, a fourth row of dirt mounds appears. At the same time, a mix of one-legged [[Rujarkian bonehopper|Rujarkian bonehoppers]] and [[Armored Rujarkian bonehopper|armored Rujarkian bonehoppers]], zero-legged [[Rujarkian remains]] and [[Armored Rujarkian remains]], and two-legged [[Rujarkian skeletal soldier|Rujarkian skeletal soldiers]] and [[Armored Rujarkian skeletal solder|armored Rujarkian skeletal soldiers]] will appear and slowly march towards the defending plants. If any orc reaches the east side, all players instantly die, and the encounter resets.

*The farmer of the grange has had enough of your grave stomping!*

After some time , [[Dedroz the Decomposer (Solo)|Dedroz the Decomposer]] will spawn and attack. Dedroz casts an incurable arcane named **Grave Visage** that shapeshifts the target into an orc skeleton and makes the target vulnerable to the spores of previously-friendly plant shooters. This effect can be removed by completing a [[Heroic Opportunity]]. Dedroz also casts a curable elemental named **Flaming Pumpkin** that inflicts ticking heat damage and destroys any spore shooters near the target. Finally, Dedroz casts a curable arcane named **Death Bind** which inflicts ticking magic damage and instantly kills the target on expiration.

After defeating the final boss, the wall of corn on the west side of the field disappears, allowing access to the barn and the small house.
