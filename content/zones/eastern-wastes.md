## Geography

To travel to this zone, use a Wizard Spire or go to [[Great Divide]] (Thurgadin Docks) using a [[Travel in Norrath|world-travel Globe]]. After you land, locate and hail the flight master with a griffon at {{waypoint 1333, -540, 405}}. You initially land in Scar Bridge but can add deeper locations later.

### Travel

Snow griffin stations are spread throughout the Eastern Waste to facilitate travel.

- [[Scar Bridge South]]
- [[Ry'Gorr Keep]]
- [[Nipik's Haven]]
- [[Caverns of Living Stone]]
- Kael Drakkel

In addition, a portal in Nipik's Haven leads to Fina's Retreat in Great Divide, but only after the quest [[Binding Nipik's Haven and Fina's Retreat]] is completed.
