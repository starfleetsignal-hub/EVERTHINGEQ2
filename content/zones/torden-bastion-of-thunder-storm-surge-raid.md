## Note:

- This is the third Raid area
- Once you kill all the Tier 1 names in [[Plane of Disease: Virulent Insurrection (Raid)|Plane of Disease]], you will be able to enter here.

## Strategy

### **Thangbrand  and Valbrand**(T1)

- Every 2 second "Raging Storm" dazes all including merc. Unable to use hostile spells, so use your group AE cure.
- Every 5 mins one named restores its hps.
- Make sure your raid survivors count more than 12 (including mercs). If your raid survivors are less than 12, names increase AOE dmg up to 150 M**,** and it triggers every second. So if you are less than a 12 player/merc raid team, you will fail the fight if fighting on-level.
- Make sure two names have their backs to the entire raid except for pet fighters or player fighters.
- Make sure two named are less than 15% HP differences. Huge HP gap makes that big one gain an offensive buff.
- Don't try to revive teammates cuz it is not allowed. You will immediately die after you revive.
- MML (mentored max level): Burn.

### **Wybjorn(T1)**

- Kill two dogs above to unlock this one (from an un-attackable white NPC to a usual named)
- Must be 12+ number player-type toons just like the two named dogs above.
- Kill named first, adds second. It seems killing adds will boost name's ability.
- If your raid survivors are less than 12, named does AOE damage of 45 M every sec.
- 3.2 Tril health for named and trash mobs.
- Killing the two dogs and this bird person gives you access to next raid zone.
- MML: Burn.

### **Eindride Icestorm(T2)**

- MML: Burn.

### **Brynju Thunderclap and Emmerik Skyfury(T2)**

- The red mob (Thunderclap) casts a red circle at 96% and becomes immune to damage. Standing in the red circle gives a buff to damage the blue mob (Skyfury). Blue mob creates a blue circle at 86% and becomes immune to damage unless one has the red buff. Standing in the blue circle gives a buff to damage red mob. Having both buffs at the same time is fatal. Having the mobs' health greater than 10% apart is a wipe. A couple of times during the fight they will repeat the circle casting.

- Cannot be MML solo'ed.

### **Kuanbyr Hailstorm(T3)**

- Three birds: Leistr, Hrani, & Iri: Three people must tank each of them separately before Hailstorm gets to 90%, or it is fatal.
- Get Hailstorm to 90% (he'll emote about dropping his protection from the birds), then immediately burn down the birds.
- Later when he stoneskins, kill the adds to get crossbows from the chests they drop to use on the Hailstorm to remove his stoneskin.

### **Agnarr the Stormlord(T4)**

- Need black adorn(s) to prevent deathtouch.
- At specific health thresholds—**86%**, **58%**, **39%**, and **6%**—the boss stoneskins. Simultaneously, multiple **Ball Lightning** orbs appear around the arena.
- The orbs will wander and then should arrange themselves into a triangle. If not, a couple of people should gently move them. This triangle will emit either a **blue** or **yellow** glow. **Blue triangle:** All players must position themselves **inside** the triangle. **Yellow triangle:** All players must position themselves **outside** the triangle. The triangle's color always alternates.
- Initially, the Ball Lightning orbs are in a **Charged** state. After they form a triangle, they will transition to an **Inert** state, at which point they must be destroyed promptly.
- The boss applies potent damage over time (DoT) effects throughout the encounter. It's imperative to cleanse these DoTs immediately to prevent overwhelming damage.
- MML: Follow the script. Stay out of Karana's protection bubble; Agnarr doesn't do any significant damage, and being close to it will kill you when the orbs do their thing.
