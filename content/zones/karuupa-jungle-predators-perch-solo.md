## Walkthrough

1. Defeat the group of [[A pygmy predator|pygmy predators]] at {{waypoint -761.83, 226.51, -357.96}}.
1. [[Pterrordax]]
   - Pygmies will drop from the sky when the howling sound starts. Avoid the targets on the ground, as they'll knock you back and heal him.
   - He will become progressively more tanky, which will require a heroic opportunity to clear.
1. Once a [[A juvenile plumedrake]] becomes attackable, kill it to initiate the next fight, and kill all the plumedrakes.
1. [[Ágilan]] and [[Háribon]]
   - Attack Háribon first (you need its detrimental to damage Agilan) -- **Disputed:** I found Háribon to be immune *until* I'd damaged Ágilan, and others have reported killing Ágilan first with no issue.
1. Kill [[Hinokawu]].
   - You must trigger a heroic opportunity to be able to hurt him.
   - Cure detrimental is helpful, as Devour will kill you if it's not cured.
