## Background

You've stumbled upon the entrance to a hidden lagoon. A peek inside reveals lizardmen involved in some bizarre ritual. Instantly, dread snuffs out any courage you may have had coming into this place. Do you retreat in fear or explore what more [[The Feerrott]] has to hide?

## Walkthrough

1. Begin killing every lizardman in the jungle/beachhead.
1. Once most are dead, kill the [[Alliz Tae Soultaker]] at {{waypoint -996, -8, 211}} and [[Asogwe Greths remains]], patrolling around that same location.
1. Kill the [[Alliz Tae Spiritcaller]] at {{waypoint -830, -7, 214}} and [[Soonogs remains]], patrolling around that same location.
1. Swim over to the isle at the center of the lagoon and slay all the [[A protector of fear|protectors of fear]].
1. Hail the Alliz Tae summoner NPC, It will ignore you but two in-game messages will appear in your chat box indicating that [[Alizasaur]] is spawning.
1. Slay [[Alizasaur]], who will spawn underwater at {{waypoint -974, -17, -16}}, make his way to the summoner NPC, kill him, and then return to his spawn location.
