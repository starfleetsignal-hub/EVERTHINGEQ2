## Walkthrough

Note: The air is poisonous! You're safe under the spider silk domes -> need to run from dome to dome to get around.

1. [[Blobule]]: Spawn him by killing the little spiders (web lurkers) -> "liquid toxins coagulate in one of the toxic pools".
1. [[Blobulous]]: Aggro *but do not kill* the the blobs in the acid pools. The aim is to get 4 of them together alive and under 50% health. They will merge to create Blobulus.
   - Try to damage them in their respective pools before moving on to the next one. When they grow in size and the text **a blob of acidic mass congeals, preparing to combine with other large massess...** you'll know you are progressing.
1. [[Acinox]]: Spawns after Blobulous is killed. Is invulnerable if there are any acid biters in the vicinity ("gets a thirst for acid") -> kill add then back to burning the named.
