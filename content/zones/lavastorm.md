Blessed by [[Solusek Ro (Deity)|Solusek Ro]], Lavastorm is a land riddled with ash and flame. Fearsome drakota, spirits of pure flame and void beasts of unimaginable power teem within the fiery earth. All throughout the zone lava and debris ooze through every crevice, making any unprepared adventure here come to an abrubt and painful end. The Order of Flame and the Sootfoot Goblins are the only two groups that may offer some [[Lavastorm Timeline|guidance]] in this unforgiving land.

## Lore

Lavastorm is a land of unrelenting volcanic activity. One look upon this island and one can clearly see it is a hellish environment that a weary soul could never hope to survive in. The heat in the air of Lavastorm is nearly unbearable, and the torrid coastal waters boil menacingly. Ash and magma billow from unpredictable vents, spewing darkness and fear from the very depths of the earth. For anything to survive in this harsh environment is astonishing, which speaks of the potential threat and power of Lavastorm's indigenous creatures.

This violent island was scene to great magical battles, so they say. It is said that this was the site of a great battle between drakota and fire giants. The drakota, as it is told, sought entrance to [[Nagafen's Lair]] to destroy him. It was on these magmatic steppes that a legendary dark elf sorceress known as [[Najena]] made a pact with [[Lord Nagafen (Nagafen's Lair)|Nagafen]] and the fire giants. She employed the help of the titanic elven golem, [[The Magolemus|Magolemus]], to help repel the drakota from Lavastorm.

The horns of battle are silent now, but the remnants of this conflict and [[Temple of Solusek Ro|citadels lost to the ages]] hide somewhere within the dark clefts of this brutal territory.* - (Source: [[Cogglesworth's Tour of Norrath: Part 2|Official EverQuest II Lore]])*

The land is like a great spiral staircase leading to Mount Solusek at its core. Steep volcanic mountains and sweltering rivers of lava form a natural barrier to the steppes of this spiraling staircase.* - (Source: [[Cogglesworth's Tour of Norrath: Part 2|Official EverQuest II Lore]])*
