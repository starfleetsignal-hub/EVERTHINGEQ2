[[The Age of Turmoil (Lore)|Formerly]], the Timorous Deep was the name of the ocean that lay around the continent of [[Kunark (Continent)|Kunark]], but was commonly used to refer to the waters north of Kunark, as here lay the primary routes for connecting the continent to the remainder of [[Norrath]]. During the [[The Age of Cataclysms (Lore)|Age of Cataclysms]], the landmasses shifted and large islands rose from these northern waters, most notably the volcano [[Gorowyn]], which after becoming dormant, emerged as the primary nest of the native Haoaeran. Eventually, in events obscured by time, the Haoaeran were cast out from Gorowyn by their former slaves, the lizard-like Sarnak, and the volcano became the capital city of the latter. [[The Age of Destiny (Lore)|Today]], the Sarnak rule over all the islands of Timorous Deep, yet their dominion is constantly threated by the Haoaeran who have set up their refuge in [[Haoaera]], high in the canopies of the central [[Isle of Mok]].

Timorous Deep is the starting area for Sarnak, and is an optional starting zone for [[Human (Character Race)|Humans]], [[Gnome (Character Race)|Gnomes]], [[Kerra (Character Race)|Kerrans]], [[Erudite (Character Race)|Erudites]], [[Half Elf (Character Race)|Half Elves]],[[Barbarian (Character Race)|Barbarians]], [[Ratonga (Character Race)|Ratonga]], and [[Dark Elf (Character Race)|Dark Elves]].

### Travel

The Timorous Deep is an ocean zone with three islands: [[Chrykori Isle]], [[Gorowyn]], and the [[Isle of Mok]]. It is possible to swim between the islands, but more practical to take the griffon routes instead. Unlike in other zones, it is not possible to jump off of a griffon mid-flight in this zone.

The [[Globe of Swift Travel]] is located on [[Faydedar Watch]], the docks at the foot of Gorowyn. {{waypoint 2322, 2, 1453}}
