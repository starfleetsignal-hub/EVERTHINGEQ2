## Aurum Outlaw :

kill everything. Start with one mob to get the HO chain started. adds with hats must be dispelled or you die when they do, it wont last so if the hat appears again you'll have to dispell again. Ho started fighter preferably if it a multi class ho it will not count, if a scout finishes it the scout will die if the window of opportunity is not up so its easier not to rely on the scout to finish the ho which is a blur icon in the detriment window . the window of opportunity is actually spawned by doing a ho

Ho on both Trash and named

Scouts honour must on in the detriment bar for grp to do damage

There is 5 metre range pulse damage all ranged try to stay at 5 or more away

Constantly keep pulling or will respawn so keep pulling all trash

The horses don’t respond only the people so horses will just come with them

The trash buff the named so 8 stacks you die

**Healers:** cures need to be done quick if you are fast enough it'll refresh the cure curse instantly once you catch them there will be a break but keep a close eye on the curses, make sure the heals and wards go in in advance of the curse and the ho running down so you don't make the ho a multi archtype ho.

**Mages**: dispell hat named to remove hat keep an eye on to when it returns. Hat must be off when mob dies.

**Scouts**: Do not do ho's unless Scouts honor detriment is up. Detriment is caused by a single archtype ho, it can not be scouts or scouts will die.

## Nugget:

Number everyone with the people who have highest mining skill as the highest numbers. Each player must harvest yellow orb for Aurum Ore to the same amount as their number. When get nugget is called it will be for 2 or 3 orbs. the person who started with 6 should harvest first then the person who has started with 5 harvests next and the person who started with four should harvest last when it's called Go harvest Unearthed Aurum Ore only one each so that no one in the group will ever have the same number of Ore. Have people not actively mining take slug's back to tank to stop interrupts. MUST have curse to harvest. Curing one curse resets the timer to 30 seconds so cure the number 1 person. During fight you will be called to grab a tree which should have been allocated before fight grab hold wait then return to fight he does a frontal that is a swipe on the tank and the tank runs through his legs or he does grab a tree and then everyone grabs a tree pick which one you're going to grab in advance

Kill adds fight. Rinse and repeat

**Healers:** only cure curse to extend the 30 second timer

You all get a number Bring the 2 outlying adds in with pets there will be the adds are called Celestial malaria in all 4 will end up with grp 2 that are there already and the 2 brought in. Do not kill them. Healer tanks named Calls out some stay inside circle some have to go out depending on the number called by the boss how far you have to go is in your detriment window you must be within 1 or everyone wipes. Interrupts are important do when you hear trigger Distance trigger is very important if outside circle you must be the exact distance form the named as the detriment says. Once it clear tell group you are clear has a buff on him called circle fire and the incriments tell you how many people will have to stay in the circle. There are two glowy circles if you have flying pets then they can get the balls otherwise. The priest grabs the named and stays with him, they can do a ho that will open portals so people can go get the orbs. Can't kill the orbs. Must get the interupt on the named or it will kill the orbs. Every ten percent he will get a buff called changed theorys and will change the number of the buff. There will be a tank that can hold the four orbs a little away from the named so they don't kill the orbs with aoes. Slow the dps down when the change over is happening to allow people to move around.

## Golden:

you'll see that there's platforms all around the dragon which all have different patterns on that are particular to your class, click a portal thing to get up to him and it'll put you on your platform. a scout has to perform a ho to start the encouter and will not be agro until then and it has to be a scout ho from start to end the mage jumps into the middle and an add will attach itself to the mage. the mages platform will disapear he will have to remain in the middle. once the mage has the add everyone else will have to jump to their platform. At a certain time something in chat or something will alert you that another add is about to apear this one is for a priest so you have two adds up and the group will jump back and forth killing the named. at some point something will alert you that a third name is coming so you dispell the priests add and kill it then the new add will attach to the priest. If you fall you can get back up by clicking the portal and selecting golden something and it'll put you on the middle platform with the dragon. if more than two adds are up then you will not be able to damage the named. There are times when you have to start a ho and immediatly cancel it except for the fighter ho wich must be started and finished by a figher. When the mage dispells the adds in preperation for killing them the fighter has five seconds to grab the agro the easiest way to do this is to target through the mage and maybe have everyone hold their agro. Can be no hesitation on jumps from platform to platform or you will get the add. adds will be based on who is on the platforms if you only have two healers on the platforms then the first add will be a healer you could then choose to make the second add a mage need two of the same archtype on the platforms to spawn the add

**Mages:** the mage jumps into the middle and an add will attach itself to the mage. the mages platform will disapear he will have to remain in the middle.
