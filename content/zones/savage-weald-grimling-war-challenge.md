## Note

Based on the sequence you defeat the named one different zone bosses is spawning.
Requires clearing of all heroic and event zones to access/unlock. Door to Savage Weald is zone in.
Each boss has a Damage type to use for extra damage (divine/poison/etc) use the damage type crystals to change accordingly.

## "Walkthrough"

Everyone has to adapt their walkthrough individually. Depending on which end boss you need. Trash mobs are accounted for in the usual way.

## Archetype Bosses

- [[Chieftain Yagnag]] (Fighter)
  - 91% 71% 51% 31% 11% he summons the gimmick adds (knee,scar,mend)
  - AOE dispels and dispels on adds and named to shrink them and debuff their strength/defense easier to kill.
  - During the encounter [[Chieftain Yagnag]] is calling for help. That called adds appear in groups of 2-4 (?):
    - [[A wealdling bonebreaker]]
    - [[A wealdling firechucker]]
    - [[A wealdling frontliner]]
    - [[A wealdling kneecapper]] (kills whoever hes engaged with if not killed fast)
    - [[A wealdling scarmender]] (heals boss)
    - [[A wealdling skinbinder]] (makes other adds and named tougher defense wise)

- [[Herbwitch Eepkie]] (Priest)
  - During the encounter [[Herbwitch Eepkie]] will summon 2 of [[An herbshambler]] dispel to make them easier to kill
  - Curse is curable when cursed player is below 50% power. Use sprint or ethershadow to drain yourself of power.
  - Target through priest to attack named.

- [[Patroller Goompa]] (Scout)
- hp: 47.3tril     mit: 252k
  - Target through scout to attack/target and they have to be stealthed
  - Have to cure curse in proper order
  - Joust out of goo/circles on ground
  - kill adds to keep stacks down

- [[Ugta Corpsecrone]] (Mage)
  - Stay 5m form each other for det
  - Drag him near the entrance to zone.
  - Target through mage.
  - Drag away any zombie add that spawn on player deaths they explode if timer hits 0 above them.
  - Use a mages tank pet to fight named if possible.

## Sub Bosses

- [[Boneblob]]
  - Location:The swamp marsh area
  - During the encounter in one of the three ponds [[A pile of bones]] will spawn and grow. Defeat is asap. This can happen more the once.
  - During the encounter you will be get a noxios curse. You must be more then 30 away from the rest of the group when you are cured. Otherwise you will be killed.
  - adds:
    - [[A murkmoor soldier]]
    - [[A pile of bones]]

- [[Falka Aza|Fazplaka Akka]]
  - Location: In the bandit ruins
  - 91% 71% 51% 31% 11% health he summons kestrels that have to be killed in order of highest to lowest height. 2/3/4/5/6 kestrels come per % round. You can hard burn at 11% and skip kestrels if you can.
  - Mez his gyrphon and dont kill it or you wipe.
  - kill the falcons adds he calls in to keep stacks down.
  - Cure Curse
  - Spread out 5m? apart from each other to avoid det.

- [[Hagnar the Horrible]]
  - Location: in the bandit ruins semi near falka aza loc
  - Destroy leash in bag
  - Cure curse
  - If enough DPS you can burn through with probably some deaths n dispels.

## Final Bosses

- [[Mejana Korvari]]
  - Location: In a grimling camp 9the one by where shadow hold raid is)
  - 91% 71% 51% 31% 11% vampire orb spawn someone with the det has to find and attack it for others to attack and kill it.
  - Power drain mob on pull and keep him at 0% use Ethermancy or deity spell, must have no power when you cure curse.
  - Keep 1 spider add up or you wipe if he has none up.
  - Cure elemental det in order it emotes. "Help your priest and mages" or "Help your mages and priests" single target cure and let the other know - they single target cure the same elemental det. This elemental det is the only script cure. All nox/trauma dets can be single target cured at other times.
  - Cure curse as soon as it is cureable.
  - Stay 5m apart from each other to avoid DoT det.

- [[Dreamscale]]
  - Location: The waterfall lake
  - Use heat damage (flames of yore)
  - Kill Sleep Eater add someone will get aqua lullaby det (head with lightning bolt icon) target through them to kill.
  - kill dream traps on yourself and other group members if it pops when det hits 60 they die. They repop when counter hits 0
  - Keep 1 fangler up or wipe if all dead
  - Use seaweed at start then cancel when he emotes "you have to drastically change form" then grab another to transform back. Repeat this as needed. Otter totems help with water breathing

- [[Grimling Zero]]
  - Location: A grimling camp on top of hill
  - Use mental damage (mindworms)
  - Every 10% 3 mercs pop up (on left right or middle) 2 of them have same weapons the other doesn't, kill those 2 first then the other one last EX: 2 double swords then sword & shield add.
  - Keep 1 owlbear up or you wipe if none are up.
  - Cure Noxious det (more info needed on specifics exactly)

## Spawning-Sequences

Kill the named bosses in this order to spawn these sub bosses and final bosses.

- Scout Fighter Mage Priest = Boneblob and Dreamscale
- Scout Fighter Priest Mage = Fakla Aza and Mejana
- Scout Mage Priest Fighter = Haglar the Horrible and Grimling Zero
