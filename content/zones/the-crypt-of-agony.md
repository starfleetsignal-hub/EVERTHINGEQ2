## Background

From behind this stone door you hear the slow shuffle of undead feet scraping aground the tiled floor. This is the holy crypt of [[Sebilis]], where those who remained loyal to [[Venril Sathir]] sleep quietly in their tombs, and those who cursed the emperor spend eternity in a cold undeath.

## Notes

- There is an [[Agnostic Dungeons|agnostic]] version of this dungeon available to players level 10 through 95.
