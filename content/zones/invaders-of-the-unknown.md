## Notes

- Invaders of The Unknown Public Quest and instance
- multiple possible spawn zones and entrances: see the [[#Locations|Locations]] section below
- Level 135 adventurer or level 133 crafter required to enter
- NOTE: You can only do 1 instance of a PQ *per zone* every 2 hours, even if the PQ respawns in the same zone prior to the 2 hour limit. (Same rule as The Unknown's static PQ)

## Walkthrough

1. Kill nightbloods and lamias in front of portals.
   - Watch out for knockbacks from mobs and void rays that come out of the portals.
   - Once dead, their corpses can be harvested for materials to deconstruct the portal by level 133 or higher tradeskillers.
     - Tradeskillers will need to have the fuel appropriate for their class in their bags.
     - Stand to the side of the portal to craft to avoid the void ray while crafting.
1. Once all portals are deconstructed the final boss can be killed.

## Locations

- Look for the PQ platform to be in the 'background' somewhere in the zone's sky (*looks like tentacles coming out of the sky*) - each zone has the same set place for the platform in the sky, even if the entrance is in a different location in the zone.
- If the PQ is up, the entrance will be at one of "**three**" locations within the zone. The entrance is visually identical to the spindle-like dungeon entrances of the rest of Cthurath expansion.
