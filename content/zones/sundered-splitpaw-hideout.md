## Background

[[Flitch Earmite]] want to play a rather destructive prank on his fellow Splitpaw Gnolls. Being the rambunctious adventurer that you are, you hastily agreed.

## Walkthrough

*See:[[Flitch's Prank (The Hideout)]]*

## Notes

- The instance can hold 1-6 players.
- The left-hand route is all heroic, while the right-hand route has a mixture of solo and heroic monsters.
- Like all Splitpaw Saga zones, the monsters scale to match your level, between 20 and 50.
- If you leave the instance, you will have to restart the quest from scratch. In other words, you must destroy all of the required supply crates in one visit.
- After destroying all the supply crates on a given route, a corresponding boss will spawn in the central chamber.
- Unlike some other Splitpaw Saga Zones, it's possible to enter this instance with a [[Mercenaries|merc]].
