Three players are required just to start the zone, because each of the three needs to stand in the swirling sand to spawn the first few waves of mobs, and you must stand in them to make them vulnerable.

Arkose: Pay attention to four of the eight runes that he paths between before you attack him. Use the arcane quartz rods on those runes when he starts to tap into his power.

Kill elementals until the winds appear to cross to the next area. Burn the named.

Kill the storm golems to access the final boss. Burn the named.
