1. First kill the [[An Allu'thoa rider|Allu'thoa riders]] at the entrance and use their mount to go next to [[Gra'ta the Mountain]].
1. Kill [[Gra'ta the Mountain]].
   - To make Gra'ta attackable, return along the same road you came in on and kill about four [[An armored ipsumodon|armored ipsumodon]].
   - Groups of three linked [[A compsognathus|compsognathus]] cause you to take more damage, try to clear them before attacking Gra'ta (look near {{waypoint -3750, 74, -299}} for an area with three nearby).
1. Kill [[Beastmaster Kau'mua]] first, and then his pet, [[Ceracuspis]].
   - Get back on the dinosaur mount to travel to the next named, Beastmaster Kau'mua.
   - Clear all nearby monsters, even those that are non-aggro, to prevent them from adding during the fight with the Beastmaster.
   - Ceracupis' run speed is much slower than that of the Beastmaster. Use this to your advantage.
   - From time to time, Ceracupis will drop a small red target ring on your location. Shortly after the target ring is dropped, he will charge that target ring, resulting in a lot of damage and a big knock-back. Get away from the circle to avoid the effects, but note that the effects extend beyond the target circle and diminishes with distance.
1. Kill [[Mo'iana the Ravenous (Advanced Solo)|Mo'iana the Ravenous]] and his followers.
   - Get back on the dinosaur mount to travel to the next named.
   - Kill the waves of [[Mo'iana Warder|Mo'iana Warders]] and [[Kindred of Mo'iana]] to spawn [[Mo'iana the Ravenous (Advanced Solo)|Mo'iana the Ravenous]].
   - Occasionally during this fight Mo'iana will RED Emote something like "A fully formed Parasite...." Immediately target the [[An adult parasite|adult parasite]] and burn it down before switching back to Mo'iana.
   - Finishing this step gives you the update to "search for the missing explorers" in the quest "The Missing Explorers".

If you go up the dirt ramp there are more dino mounts that will return you to the entrance.
