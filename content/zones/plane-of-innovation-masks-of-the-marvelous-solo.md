## Walkthrough

- Defeat the [[Ancient Clockwork Prototype]] that wanders near {{waypoint -61.47, 3.45, -10.38}}. Defeat the adds first!
  - Then loot the [[Ancient Clockwork Hand]] from its chest.
- Defeat the [[Clockwork Scrounger XVII]] near {{waypoint 48.11, 3.59, -18.77}}.
  - After defeating the first 2 nameds the door north of [[The Junkyard]] {{waypoint 70.81, 3.79, -86.80}} will open up.
- Defeat [[The Glitched Guardian 10101]] at [[Xanamech's Shell]] near {{waypoint 206.61, 3.42, -152.46}}. Joust out when you see the emote "The Gliched Guardian 10101 begins to overheat, a deadly explosion is imminent!" roughly every 25%.
  - This turns the [[Ancient Clockwork Hand]] into [[Electro-Charged Clockwork Hand]].
  - Equip [[Electro-Charged Clockwork Hand]] and open the door at {{waypoint 135.77, 3.49, -45.07}}.
  - **NOTE:** Remember to re-equip your normal weapon.
- Defeat [[An erratic clockwork]] near {{waypoint 155.71, 3.49, -57.75}}, then examine and speak to it to open the nearby waste duct grate.
  - Go through the now open grate at {{waypoint 177.08, 4.27, -66.42}} to enter the waste duct.
- Wait for the [[Security Sweeper]] to path from left to right then follow it down tunnel to the right. Then take the first exit to the right.
  - If it sees you it will port you to the front of the zone.
- Defeat the [[Glitched Cell Keeper]] at {{waypoint 124.58, 9.89, -218.67}}.
  - Joust out of the red circles. "Glitched Cell Keeper gathers power from his stores to repair himself."
  - The red circle will get bigger each time it appears.
  - This will unlock the door behind the cell keeper {{waypoint 114.22, 10.10, -218.81}}.
- Speak to [[Meldrath the Marvelous]] in the next room at {{waypoint 81.91, 10.04, -210.39}}.
  - This will unlock the northern exit of the cell {{waypoint 90.65, 10.11, -237.11}}
- Finally defeat the last named [[Gearclaw the Collector]] at {{waypoint -143.95, 3.42, -286.42}}.
  - Every ~25% the mob leans over and goes immune to damage. Right-click and choose Turn Key.
  - He will drop [[Muon's Planarwave Actuator]] needed for the quest [[Legacy of Power: An Innovative Approach]].
