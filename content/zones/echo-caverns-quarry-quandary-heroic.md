## Walkthrough

1. [[Undertaker Zemilox]] {{waypoint 136.03, 110.00, 8.35}}
   - Clear thought horror mobs until you get the message *"The Undertaker has arrived to consume your souls!"* signalling that the name has spawned.
   - During the fight, [[A shade horror|shade horror]] and [[A deepshade soulsapper|deepshade soulsapper]] adds spawn. Keep the group tight on the tank so adds can be taunted. Burn adds before focusing back on Zemilox.
   - Around 40%, the Undertaker will cast an AE Mez; it's recommended that everyone equip Astral Dominion belt runes or precast an avoid.
1. [[The Needlite Queen]] {{waypoint 713.08, 183.32, 309.00}}
   - Kill all the groups with [[A needlite bloodroyal|needlite bloodroyals]] until you get the message *"The deaths of her bloodroyals has awoken the queen of the needlites!"* signalling that the name has spawned. Loot the bodies to collect **needlight bloodsacs**.
   - Throughout the fight, the Queen will gain a stacking buff, Bloodthirst (red heart on a yellow background). Once it hits 10 stacks, she will begin one-shotting her current target. Remove stacks of this buff via dispelling or by using a bloodsac on the named.
   - [[A needlite bloodsapper|Needlite bloodsapper]] adds spawn throughout the fight and they also drop blood sacs; keep tight on tank so tank can taunt adds and burn them before focusing back on the Queen.
   - Has a reflect curse that you have to stop attacking when you get it.
1. [[Chief Broglyn]]-- Roams around {{waypoint 5.55, 157.15, 325.25}}
   - Move into [[The Chlorochamber]] and kill all the tegi boglings to spawn the next name. Be sure to kill the [[A bogling of Broglyn|bogling of Broglyn]] that pops after each group of three dies, or they will heal Broglyn during the fight
   - Will occasionally say *starts to go berzerk* in chat, which is a cue to quickly interrupt the Chief or he will go into a Tegi Tantrum, doing massive damage and debuffing the group.
   - Adds spawn every so often; keep tight on tank so tank can taunt adds and burn them before focusing back on the Chief.
1. [[Killmodo]] -- Roams around {{waypoint -246.77, 223.91, 48.90}}
   - Throughout the fight, [[A hungry tuatara|hungry tuatara]] adds will spawn, kill them, burn named.
   - Cure the curse which can be chain cast
1. [[Scyphodon]] {{waypoint -389.51, 176.07, 324.39}}
   - Scyphodon is weak to COLD damage.
   - Everyone in the group should preharvest **a colossal cane**, and consume the item. Magic Sugar (the buff gained from using colossal cane) requires 1 concentration, ensure that everyone is actually using it!
   - *"Scyphodon discharges electricity towards Charname!"* -- Stay out of the water or this will kill you.
   - *"lashes out at anyone too close with his tail!"* -- Big AOE, hits multiple times for up to 500m per hit.
   - Scyphodon warns: *"Those with a bit of sugar in their veins may soon be devoured!"* signalling that he is about to cast "Colossal Maw".
     - Before that cast ends, use a macro with **/cancel_maintained Magic Sugar** or* "Scyphodon's Colossal Maw devours you whole!"* and you die.
     - Once Magic Sugar is removed, move to a plant and pick up a new one.  Once Colossal Maw is done casting, reuse the cane to regain the buff, and the ability to target/damage the named.
