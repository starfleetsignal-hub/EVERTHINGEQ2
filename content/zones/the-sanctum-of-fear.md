## Background

The Sanctum of Fear was built in the [[Shard of Fear|Plane of Fear]] and transported by the Thulians before [[The Age of Turmoil (Lore)|the Age of Turmoil]] to [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]] in order to summon the horrid abominations onto [[Norrath]].* - (Source: [[Ux Uval]])*

## Notes

- Before entering you will need four [[A clay golem heart|clay golem hearts]] that drop from [[A clay golem|clay golems]] wandering [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]] in order to summon the [[Fright|final boss]].
  - [[A steel golem heart]], dropped by [[The Steel Golem]], can also be used.

## Walkthrough

When you zone into the Sanctum of Fear you will find yourself in a large room followed with a spiral ramp leading upward. The ramp has static spawns on each corner, and every time you kill a static spawn a wave consisting of 3-4 mobs will descend the stairs. At the top of the ramp there will be another chamber with [[Overlord Um Nix]] in wait. As soon as you approach, the Overlord and his adds will aggro.

![An image of the idol where you need to place the golem hearts. There are four of them.](images/The_Heart_of_Fear_-_idol.png)
Once they have been dealt with, look up to see you are in a large chamber with an inverted pyramid in the middle. Move up a level and slay all the lizardmen. At the inner corner of every quarter section of this level there will be a small idol. Right-click it and select 'Sacrifice a clay golem heart'. Left-clicking the idols will not work. After sacrificing a clay golem heart, four elite Thulians will spawn. Kill them as they spawn and once all four statues have been activated, proceed to the upper platform and slay [[Fright]].
