## Lore

![Entry view ahead south](images/Torden_Winds_of_Change_Entry_view.jpg)
The Bastion of Thunder is calling. Are you ready to brave Norrath's harshest storms?

Same lore to [[Torden, Bastion of Thunder: Tower Breach (Heroic)]]

## Preparations before fight

If you are first time try run PoP heroic dungeons as a F2P. Check this blog [[User blog:Tangstone/How to run Planes of Prophecy Heroic dungeons as a F2P in 2025|How to run Planes of Prophecy Heroic dungeons as a F2P in 2025]]

Group requirement: a player Mage([[Absorb Magic]]), a player priest, a player support who supply power(mana) and speed boost

Health requirement:45M for everyone. At least 40K Potency

## Walkthrough

### [[Elif Whitewind]]

- First named. Nothing same to [[Legacy of Power: Through Storms and Mists|solo version]]
- Take a look in your chat window when says "Elif Whitewind begins to wail in your direction". This effect last for 25 sec
- Use  [[Absorb Magic]]/[[Machined Energy Inverter]] to dispel "Wail"  to named otherwise he death touch one of your teammate(including merc)
- If all your teammate full set of wears Expert Heroic 1. You can ignore it and revive your man every 30s during battle

*Acolyte Tower:Arcanium* , this place is optional to final named. You can skip this place and head to **Torstien Stoneskin and Hreidar lynhillig**
![](images/Acolyte_Tower_Arcanium.jpg)

### [[The Hurricane (Heroic)|The Hurricane]]

- Optional boss fight.Kill it unlock the first blue bird mount to **Yveti Stormbrood**
- Basically same to [[Legacy of Power: Through Storms and Mists|solo version]]. Normal tank and spank deals it

### [[Yveti Stormbrood (Heroic)|Yveti Stormbrood]]

- Optional boss fight. Nothing same to [[Legacy of Power: Through Storms and Mists|solo version]]
- He summons "A Windcaller Golem" and immune to damage when HP reach 75%,50%,25%. Kill adds continue boss fight
- Only the first time  "A Windcaller Golem" generated randomly to one direction of entire tower. The second time will definitely be generated in the opposite direction. Therefore, the first and third add will appear in the same place.
- He deals heavy dot damage about 30M and drain your mana. Healer must go together with mana support
- You can pull named side to side so you can kill add faster

*The Tower of Thunder*
![](images/The_Tower_of_Thunder.jpg)

### [[Laef Windfall (Heroic)|Laef Windfall]]

- Kill him to unlock the teleport pad to final named. Basically same to [[Legacy of Power: Through Storms and Mists|solo version]]
- Keep an eye on your chat window when saying "Laef Windfall prepares to unleash a barrage of powerful wind in front of him". Named won't move and you need to run away and keep distance
- About 6 sec after he casts every one within gets a buff "Caught in the Storm". You can't do anything last about 20 sec. Run at least 30m away from him avoid getting caught.
- He deals heavy damage about 40M to a single target sometimes and it won't appear often

### [[Torstien Stoneskin and Hreidar lynhillig (Heroic)|Torstien Stoneskin and Hreidar lynhillig]]

- Final name. The most difficult boss in 421 resolve in this dungeon. Basically same to [[Legacy of Power: Through Storms and Mists|solo version]]
- Health check. Everyone in your team must above **45M** health.
- **Hreidar** must not be tanked by DPS class. If Hreidar's primary aggro target is a DPS, he casts "**Holy Hammer**" and hits you **315M** crushing damage. Recast time of "**Holy Hammer**" seems to be 2 mins. Use your Death prevention and Stoneskin if that case.
- Both of them can be damaged normally until one drops below 90% health.
- Keep an eye on named head when one of them **speak** "***I've got your back, friend!***".  3 things will happen following
  - The other one immune damage so you have to switch DPS to the **speaking** one. If you don't remember which one, open your UI settings show percentage health and mana to see which one is not immune to damage.
  - There is a red circle appears. It deals **45M** AOE damage in 50000m radius range when it lands. Normally run out of circle and avoid damage is **NOT** working in this one. Your entire teammate need 45M health or 45M ward to survive
  - The immune one heals 3% of his maximum health overtime

- Loot his key from body in the end
