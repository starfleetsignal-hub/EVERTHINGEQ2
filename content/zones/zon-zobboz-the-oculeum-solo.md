## Notes

- Warning: Water in Drekkna's Deluge area does damage<br>
- **Kill all trash around named mobs before you engage them** or they will all come to aid. With each add that's not killed, charmed, feared, mesmerized or stunned the named mob gets 1 increment on a buff, that at 10 increments instantly kills you.
