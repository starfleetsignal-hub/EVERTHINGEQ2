## Background

The crazed Guardians of Thyr have abducted [[Marathan Allim]], before [[Civean Il'Pernod]] and the faithful of [[Solusek Ro]] could complete the summoning ritual. Although tired from your journey through [[Solusek's Eye]], you must enter here and rescue the vessel of the Burning Prince!
