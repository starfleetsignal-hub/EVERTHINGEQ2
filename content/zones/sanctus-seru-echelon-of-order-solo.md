## Notes

- Zone from [[Sanctus Seru (City)]] using the portal at {{waypoint -280.48, 180.72, 0.42}}
- If you can't cure yourself, bring arcane cure potions.
- Need to be level 114 to enter.

## Walkthrough

1. Click on **Barrier to Sanctus Seru**
1. Speak to [[A knight of the Echelon]]. The NPCs in the zone will become hostile.
1. Kill the group of [[A luminary ranger]] near {{waypoint -241.16, 87.68, 136.52}} to activate [[Moggtu the Mad]] at {{waypoint -234.33, 87.68, 139.20}}
   - When he emotes 'Moggtu turns towards (name)!' he does a knockback towards that person. Put your back to a wall or joust behind
   - Knockback does an energy drain
1. Proceed to {{waypoint 3.03, 87.65, 350.31}}
1. Hail all [[An affirmation seeker]] ( I had to hail each one and kill all of them )  and kill them to activate [[Cerio Vallain]] at {{waypoint 1.64, 88.58, 363.50}}
   - He emotes descriptions of different types of 'voices' as an add comes in.
   - Defeat the adds in the order he emotes about them while beating on him
1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint -31.81, 87.64, 329.88}} to get back to entrance.
1. [[Raizyl Pajdu]] wanders from {{waypoint -214.69, 81.88, -280.78}} to {{waypoint -91.24, 81.88, -309.61}}
   - Clear trash between before pulling.
   - Says "the POWERLESS shall perish by my hand" and then casts an aoe.
   - "Enough Casting" arcane disarm, dispel to continue attacking
   - "Try to keep up!" teleports to {{waypoint -223.04, 88.04, -237.08}} or {{waypoint -61.01, 87.98, -319.53}}
1. Kill all [[A lumious anima]]. *Note: Every living one gives [[Dichromanus]] 10% damage reduction.*
1. Defeat [[Dichromanus]] at {{waypoint -15.57, 87.68, -326.26}}

   - [[Dichromanus]] has a knockback and occasional full power drain.
1. Defeat [[Lady Warglave]] at {{waypoint -267.78, 89.95, 2.87}}
   - Kill adds, put back to wall for knockbacks.
