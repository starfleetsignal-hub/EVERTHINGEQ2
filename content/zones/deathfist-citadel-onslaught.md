## Background

You've discovered [[A rusting key]] on the corpse of a fresh kill. It has the symbol of the Deathfist Orcs on it, but what could if unlock? If it could perhaps open a secret door of the [[The Deathfist Citadel|Deathfist Citadel]] then it may provide you an opportunity to catch the orcs inside unaware.

## Walkthrough

The objective of this one-time instance is just to slay everything in sight. Work your way up from the [[The Sewers|Sewers]] until you reach the [[The Emperor's Terrace|Emperor's Terrace]]. When you've sated your lust for orcish blood, you can leave the way you came or flee back to Zek from the main gain.

## Notes

- This is a one-time instance that can only be accessed if one, or possibly all, members of the group are on the quest, [[The rusty key]].  Repeat access requires hunting down a new rusty key.
- The quest automatically completes as soon as you click to enter from the 'North' sewer grate. (for whatever reason, entering from the 'West' sewer grate does not trigger quest completion).  It is not necessary for you to clear the instance.
- The named [[Magister Lugsnik]] resides in this instance, but does not award Alternate Advancement (AA) points or loot.
- Several named Heroic and Epic bosses exist in the zone. They do award AA but each only drop generic 'Treasure Chest' loots appropriate to their level.
