## General

Zone in from down stairs in the crypt {{waypoint -376, 16, 54}}accessed from the cemetery adjacent to Somborn Village.  This is the easiest difficulty of the 3 group zones in this area. **NOTE:** To enter the Cloister of Liturgy where Lord Marcus is found, **click** **The Libant Blood Stone** beside the shimmering curtain.   ***Make sure you are 100% Health before clicking the stone!***

To fight marcus you must use methodical strikes if they are grey or if not pull each named to the room behind em and kill em repeat until all are dead and then marcus will be fightable
