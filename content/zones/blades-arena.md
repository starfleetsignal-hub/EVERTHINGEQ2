## Background

This is the training ground for the Court of the Blades. There is no crowd and no monetary rewards - use this place to hone in your fighting skills.

## Notes

- You must have +20,000 faction with the Court of the Blades to enter this instance.
- [[A desert mummy|Desert mummies]] will instantly respawn when killed and count towards the Undead Slayer achievements, such as [[Undead Destroyer]], making this an ideal (yet boring) place to farm the achievements out.
