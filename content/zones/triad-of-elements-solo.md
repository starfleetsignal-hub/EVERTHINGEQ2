## Notes

Enter this dungeon during the [[Summer Jubilee]] event in the [[Moors of Ykesha]].

**Note:** If you miss looting any of the temporary boss-drop items mentioned below, look for "a fallen adventurer" corpse on the *next level* to get the item. If you crash or leave the instance for any reason remember to click the fallen adventurer's corpse to get all temporary drops again to help you finish the zone.

Completing this dungeon 10 times will award your character the "A Jubilee for You and Me" achievement. This will allow you to purchase the [[Plumes of Inspired Jubilation]] recipes and the Enduring tier of the plume items from [[A Jubilation merchant]]. Completing the dungeon 20 times will award you the "A Jubilee Spectacularly" achievement which allows you to purchase the Genuine tier of plumes from the merchant.

The encounters will automatically scale to the player's level. If you are level 125 and having difficulty completing the dungeon, you can use a [[Timeless Chronomage|Chronomage]] to lower your level. Due to how the scaling works, you will find bosses easier to defeat and will still receive level 125 rewards.

This instance is not available on TLE servers unless they have unlocked The Shadow Odyssey expansion.

## Walkthrough

1. Swim to the left platform at {{waypoint 1308, -86, -103}} and click the waterspray to port to the first area (Awuidor).
1. Take a look at the ends of the four platforms. You can see the blue siren (the first boss, Saian) at the end of one of them even from near the area you zoned into. You need only clear trash that's on your way to the boss and then to the transporter to the next area. When you approach the non-attackable trash mobs, one will kill the other, then turn to attack you.
1. Boss: [[Saian the Submariness]]
   - Take note of the vessel directly behind Saian. She cannot be damaged while its matching vessel is on the platform.
   - Destroy the vessel at the beginning of the fight. At 75%, 50%, and 25% health, Saian will pop back to her starting point, and a new vessel will appear. Destroy those immediately as well.
   - Loot [[Saian's Sea Shell]] from her body. **This is a TEMPORARY, NO-ZONE item!**
1. Click on the portal at {{waypoint 1352, -9, -142}}  to port to the next area (Solusek Ro's Tower).
1. Clear the trash as you move into the large room (The Solar Sanctum). Avoid the center area until you're ready, the boss mob is there. You'll be playing with levers (that look like giant claws) to spawn the boss in here, so clear anything in the way in this room first as well.
1. Boss: [[Ozani]]
   - Use [[Saian's Sea Shell]] to massively reduce the heat damage received during this fight.
   - Initially [[Ozani]] is immune to damage.
     - Move the claw-like levers at the base of a pillar, adjusting them until they're all facing outward instead of inward. When you adjust one claw, any adjacent claws will move as well.
     - When the claws are correctly positioned the rings at the top of the pillar will lower and a zone message will pop up stating that [[Ozani]] has lost some defense.
     - When levers at both pillars are correctly positioned a zone message will state all of her defenses have been lost. [[Ozani]] will now start taking damage.
   - At 50% health, Ozani will disengage and mephits will pop out of the cauldron in the center and attack. There are three mephits each wave, and about five total waves. The mephits will explode and attempt to inflict a fire DoT if not killed very quickly.
   - Loot [[Ozani's Solstice]] from his corpse. **This is a TEMPORARY, NO-ZONE item!**
1. Go through the now-open door on the western wall and use the portal at {{waypoint 200, 103, -93}}. It will return you to the entrance.
1. Cross the newly-formed ice-bridge across the water southeast from the entrance to the platform on the right at {{waypoint 1398, -86, -105}}. A bunch of snakes will attack along the way. Take the portal to the next area.
1. Clear trash and make your way to the platform across from the one you popped in on. There's a bridge at {{waypoint 1300, 30, -171}}.
1. Take the portal at {{waypoint 1403, 30, -104}} to reach the next boss in the Apex of the Abyss.
1. Hotkey your [[Ozani's Solstice]]. Clear the platform and cross the bridge to reach Tidalax.
1. Boss: [[Tidalax]]
   - Use [[Ozani's Solstice]] on Tidalax. This makes it so he can be damaged, and so that he cannot do a massive water AoE.
   - At 75%, 50%, and 25% health, a clam will open. Kill the pearl inside, then use Ozani's Solstice on Tidalax again to damage him more.
     - Whenever you destroy a pearl, Tidalax will do his water AoE. Stand inside the clam so that the shell will protect you, so that you can use your Ozani's Solstice again on Tidalax.
   - Loot [[Tidalax's Algae-Covered Scales]] from his corpse. **This is a TEMPORARY, NO-ZONE item!**
1. Take the portal at the back of Tidalax's platform at {{waypoint 1352, 84, -80}}.
1. The next boss, Baron Bromz, is straight ahead. Clear the trash on and around the central platform. Before you engage him, make sure you've used your [[Saian's Sea Shell]] to prevent his knockback and use [[Tidalax's Algae-Covered Scales]] to mitigate heat damage from the vents.
1. Boss: [[Baron Bromz]]
   - The vents will begin to glow right before they become active. Stand on top the vent to obtain a buff that will allow you to damage the Baron. You do not need to position the boss over the vent, you simply need to stand on it to receive the buff (it will also debuff your run speed).
   - You'll see a warning about his knockback, but it won't affect you as long as you have the buff from the seashell.
   - Gargoyles will spawn occasionally and attack during the fight.
   - Loot [[Bromz's Obsidian Crown]] from his corpse. **This is a TEMPORARY, NO-ZONE item!** Use the crown now, which consumes all the previous temporary items you picked up, to create the [[Elemental Mishmasher]].
1. Take the portal at {{waypoint 0.37, 103, -123}} to port to the Plane of Innovation section.
1. Clear both side hallways to kill the two gatewatcher mobs. This unlocks the center door.
1. As you enter Tinker Testworks, all of the inactive clockworks become activated and attackable. Clear the room. Eventually you will see a zone message stating that the barrier around the portal has dropped (this is the orb at the back of the room).
1. Boss: [[Sawclaw]]
   - Click the orb "to parts unknown" to port to Sawclaw in Sawclaw Chop Shop.
     - This should award the exploration achievement [[Elemental Foray]].
   - Clear the mobs in the Chop Shop. Approach the large bot in the back. Meldrath will appear and activate Sawclaw.
   - As soon as you engage, one of the batteries (the giant pillars in the room) begins overloading. This is indicated by a giant burst of energy around it. Head to the battery and use your Elemental Mishmasher to fully overload it. Now you can damage Sawclaw.
   - You will have to overload the other four batteries at 80%, 60%, 40%, and 20% health.
   - Watch out for the sawblades that move along the grooves in the floor. They might hurt a bit if you get hit by one.
1. Once Sawclaw is down, you'll get an exquisite end boss chest containing 5 Jubilation Medals from current year (Platinum Jubilation Medal in 2025) and other level-appropriate goodies. Click the portal at the back of the room to exit the zone.
