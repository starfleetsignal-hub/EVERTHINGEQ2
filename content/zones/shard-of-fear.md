## Background

The portal in the back of the [[Cave of Fear (POI)|Cave of Fear]] has finally opened up. Horrors from the [[Plane of Fear]] are pouring out into [[The Feerrott]]. As a fearless adventurer, you must team up with your most stalwart of allies to brave this hellscape of [[Cazic-Thule]]. Rumor has it that deep within the burning fields, a great [[Terror]] resides.

## Walkthrough

- To enter, click the stone monument that surrounds the pink mist.
- Feel free to roam the zone killing all the named monsters that you come across.
- There are three ring events, tied to the [[Shard of Fear Timeline]], that require you to defeat the skeletal guardians of the bone towers. To spawn the skeletal guardians, clear all the [[A fetid fiend|fetid fiends]] from around their corresponding towers.
- To spawn the [[Terror|final boss]], follow the guide found [[With a Heart of Fear|here]].
