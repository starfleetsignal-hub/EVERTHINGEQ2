## Named

1. First named is [[Master of the Guard]] at [[Vestibule of Peril]]. First kill all mobs before attacking him.
1. Second is the statue [[Eudoxxus]] at [[Forbidding Sanctuary]].
1. Third is [[Paldar Bladesoul]] at [[Depository of Discernment]].
1. Fourth kill [[Ingolfa Arnar]] at [[Keening Undercroft]].
1. use the portal in the middle of the room {{waypoint 408.96, 21.46, -351.00}}
1. kill the mob on the floor. And after a while [[Milas the Ancient]] attacks. He comes with the adds [[Vaaraak]], [[Uulthaax]] and [[Thaaladax]].

## Challenge Mode

The final boss [[Milas the Ancient]] has a challenge mode that can be triggered around 25%. At that point, red text will appear stating that he is trying to run away. Right-clicking the boss allows you to prevent this, causing you to fight both Milas and The Final Arbiter at the same time.
