## Notes

- Zone from [[Wracklands]] using the portal at {{waypoint 726.72, 77.96, 664.24}}
- Additional notes for the Heroic version are below the Solo walkthrough.

## Walkthrough

1. Proceed through the door to the east.
1. [[The Ruins of Ssraeshza]]
   - Kill [[A shissar dire lich]], [[A shissar ghoul lord]], [[A shissar grimreaver]] and [[A shissar soulstalker]].
     - *Note: Each wanders along wall of the room.*
   - Kill all the [[A temple sentry|temple sentries]].
     - *Note: They a single linked group of 4.*
   - Defeat [[The Architect of Ruin]] in north-east corner and loot the [[Artisan's Relic]] from his body.
     - Casts Moonlit Destruction. Curse damage detriment. You need to cure and have the toons not too packed (do a circle around him)
   - Click on the **Idol of the Artisan** at {{waypoint -80.43, -31.99, -79.11}}.
   - Use the activated portal at {{waypoint -34.72, -30.75, -33.28}}.
1. [[The Serpent's Maw]]
   - Approach the [[A warrior effigy|warrior effigies]] at the edge of the pit to activate them. Once the statues are dead, the tile each was standing upon becomes clickable.
   - Click the tiles until you find the one that displays a mural across the pit with two shissar facing each other. Walk across the pit toward that mural, there is an invisible bridge over the spikes.
     - Alternate explanation:
       - When you press a tile, look at the FAR wall.
       - So if you're standing at the first tile, press it and look at the wall on the far end that's facing you.
       - An image will show up.
       - You want to find the tile that displays on the far wall an image of two shissar facing each other .
       - Once found, walk in a straight line from that tile to the image. Straight! Don't walk left or right.
       - [**NB:** In Heroic, this works differently. One person must stay on the tile while the others cross.]
   - Approach {{waypoint -30.46, -169.88, -373.92}} to activate [[Vesshtri the Swordfang]].
   - Defeat [[Vesshtri the Swordfang]].
     - Periodically casts 'Slicing Fangs'. Joust behind him.
     - Periodically calls adds.
   - Collect **Warrior's Relic** at {{waypoint -30.87, -169.67, -388.27}} and use the portal to return to the central chamber.
   - Click on **Idol of the Warrior** in the SW corner at {{waypoint 74.30, -32.00, 77.01}}.
   - Follow the energy beam that links the statue to the portal and click on the portal.
1. [[The Aeon Shedding]]
   - Kill all of the disciples in the room.
   - Defeat [[Rath'Girosz and Rath'Argoth]].
     - Look at the clock on the ground. It starts at 6am, the sword is the hour hand, and the lance is the minute hand.  One of the 12 statues has a blue staff (the others are holding hammers), and that represent 12 on the clock.  Once you engage the bosses, the clock starts.
     - Rath'Girosz is stronger (offense and defense) between 6am and 6pm and Rath'Argoth is stronger during 6pm and 6am. The ambient light in the area indicates night/day.
   - Click on the center of the clock at {{waypoint 178.28, -160.73, -30.01}} and enter the time from the floor clock. *Note: The statue with the staff is 12.  The minute hand is very precise, so you could enter times like "8:03".  The hour hand is less accurate, so you might try adjusting the hour if it's not working.*
   - Collect **Mystic Relic** at {{waypoint 117.48, -161.81, -29.71}} and then use the portal to go back to the central chamber.
   - Click the **Idol of Mystics** in the NW corner at {{waypoint 75.33, -32.00, -78.06}}.
   - Use the activated portal at {{waypoint 32.47, -30.75, -34.34}}.
1. [[The Python's Coil]]
   - Kill all the shadelings.
     - *Note: Stay out of their blue frontal cone attack.*
   - Defeat [[Gorgorah the Shadowmanic]] in the center of the room.
     - Power drains - be prepared.
     - Periodically she will disappear. Go back into the shadow bubble to find her.
     - Periodic knockback.
   - Use the wheel at {{waypoint -230.21, -138.20, 64.36}} to rotate the ramp.
     - **Note for the Heroic version**: the ramp has to be rotated 8 times in a particular sequence: C (go press switch); C C (go press switch); A A C (go press switch); C C (go press switch); where C = clockwise, A = counterclockwise.
     - *Fun side note: When you click the wheel, if you are fast you can run and hop on the ramp while it moves.*
   - Go up the ramp and push the **Gate Activation Switch** at the top.
   - Repeat 2 more times to activate all 3 switches.
   - Note that the gate to the **Relic of Royalty** does NOT automatically open.
   - Rotate the ramp once more to align with the Relic's platform, and go up the ramp. Click on the gate itself to open it, then take the Relic.
   - Return to the ruins above by using the portal in the wall you arrived by.
   - Click the **Idol of Royalty** at {{waypoint -80.70, -31.95, 77.39}}
   - Use the **Underground Access** portal in the center of the room at {{waypoint -2.07, -30.59, 1.41}} and head down the stairs it reveals.
1. [[Sra'Veraz, the Shadowgate]]
   - **NOTE: The facets puzzle is a little different in the Heroic version -- see additional notes below.**
   - Kill Akhevans as needed to progress through the room.
   - Click the Facets at each landing and make a note of the pictogram on the panel that rises into view for each.  The options are: hammer, staff, sword, and symbol.
     - First landing: [[Boa's Facet]] at {{waypoint -66.68, -78.97, -98.15}}
     - Second landing: [[Python's facet]] at {{waypoint -97.21, -96.20, -65.61}}
     - Third landing: [[Viper's Facet]] at {{waypoint -64.49, -114.54, -33.91}}
     - Fourth landing: [[Cobra's Facet]] at {{waypoint -34.00, -132.32, -67.09}}
   - At the bottom of the ramp is a central room, with  four side rooms representing each of the Facets. In each room, click on the settings pedestal to enter the pictogram type for that Facet you learned on the way down.
     - [[Cobra's Setting]] at {{waypoint 33.04, -150.15, -65.93}}  -- enter the pictogram type you noted from the [[Cobra's Facet]].
     - [[Viper's Setting]] at {{waypoint -65.99, -150.15, 30.93}}  -- enter the pictogram type you noted from the [[Viper's Facet]].
     - [[Python's Setting]] at {{waypoint -162.62, -150.15, -66.26}}  -- enter the pictogram type you noted from the [[Python's Facet]].
     - [[Boa's Setting]] at {{waypoint -66.30, -150.15, -162.19}}  -- enter the pictogram type you noted from the [[Boa's Facet]].
       - *Note: If, for some reason, you did not note the symbols (like me) or you forgot, you can get the symbol settings by trial and error, since once a symbol setting is correct, you can no longer change it (once the statue is done rotating) and there's no penalty for getting it wrong.*
       - *Also note that the settings are not unique. A given setting can apply to 0 or more facets; eg, you might get one staff and 3 swords.*
   - Defeat [[Kor Va Xian]] at {{waypoint -64.88, -146.06, -67.14}}
     - Periodic knockback.
     - Periodically summons adds.
     - Will periodically port you to a dark room - you must get out of that room as quickly as possible or you will die.
     - *If you die during the final fight and have to revive at the entrance, camp on the steps that drop out; when you load back in, you will be on the spiral ramp going down and can get back to him.*
     - **Note for the Heroic version**: he may also give a luclinite bomb to someone: just destroy it before it explodes.

## Heroic Version - additional notes

Additional notes for the [[The Python's Coil]] and [[Sra'Veraz, the Shadowgate]] rooms:

1. [[The Python's Coil]] : rotating ramp puzzle:
   - the ramp has to be rotated 8 times in a particular sequence to get to each of the four switches:
     - Clockwise once, then go press switch
     - Clockwise **twice**, then go press switch
     - **Counter**clockwise **twice,** then Clockwise once, then go press switch
     - Clockwise **twice**, then go press switch.
1. [[Sra'Veraz, the Shadowgate]] :
   - The symbols don't appear when you click on the facets. Instead you must go all the way to the bottom, then stand in a certain spot on the bottom level (approximatively in front of each side room access) and look waaay up (you may have to "adjust" your POV to get it to appear). Then just run into the room you're standing in front of and type whatever it was  (hammer, staff, sword, or symbol) into the textbox that appears when you click on the pillar. ![Where to look for Heroic puzzle](images/The_Ruins_of_Ssraeshza_Heroic_Puzzle_Facets.png)
   - Defeat [[Kor Va Xian]] :
     - Strat notes as above, plus he may also give a luclinite bomb to someone: just destroy it before it explodes.
