## Notes

This instance is for the quests [[Missing Scouts]] and [[Luring Out the Evil]], from the [[Hand of Marr Camp]], and [[A Small Sampling]] and [[A Bloody Coup]], from the [[Brethren of Night Camp]]. These are the first two quest in each series.

Throughout the zone there are many breakable walls. Some separate you from where you need to go, opening additional sections of the zone. Others are walled-up alcoves containing **a dusty coffin**, **a dusty vase**, or sometimes both. Destroying them may reveal nothing, a monster, or just some coin. **Dusty vases** can also appear in other places in plain sight.

There are 4 classes of vampire underlings in the Tombs: Neonates, Adherents, Bloodsworn, and Elders. The Elders are only seen in [[The Crypt of T'haen]].
