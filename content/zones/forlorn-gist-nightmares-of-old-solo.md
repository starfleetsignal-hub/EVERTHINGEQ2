## Notes

- Optional: Clear all of the trash when you first zone in, it makes getting to all of the bosses easier
- Most of the mobs are linked in fairly large packs. Tag one and bring them in for aoe! This is true even if you see only 1 or 2 mobs - expect more when you pull one.

## Walkthrough

- [[Lavec Conun'Stah]] in a house in the [[District of Night]] at {{waypoint -330, 14.67, 292.36}}: Tank'n Spank.
  - Move out of giant red circle
- [[Krinas Bledso]] in the street at {{waypoint -126.53, 16, 319.85}}: Tank'n Spank
  - Back away when he says "I will cut you to the bone" as he does a series of high damage hits
- [[Stenkannreif, the Monster]] across the bridge in [[Acrid Loft]] at [[Stenkannreif House]] {{waypoint -238, 48, 362.85}}: Tank'n Spank
- [[Perisha, the Mistress of Flies]] in [[Somber Acre]] at {{waypoint 156.61, 50.12, 365.73}}: Tank'n Spank
  - Summons lots of [[A summoned forlorn resident|summoned forlorn resident]] adds.
- [[Aga-Yagaba]] at [[Horizon's End]] at {{waypoint 445.93, 108.74, 205.13}}: basically...Tank'n Spank beside some sideeffects:
  - She can trap you in a [[Mind Prism]].
  - Mind Prism prevents healing and lasts until the adds are dead
  - Summons lots of [[A summoned forlorn resident|summoned forlorn resident]] adds.
- Exit at {{waypoint 471.22, 109.22, 198.45}}
