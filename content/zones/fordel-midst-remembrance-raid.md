**Nhekrin:**

- Relatively slow burn. He spawns 2 adds throughout the fight, one light, one dark. Just choose one to kill, Nhekrin will turn into the opposite one. Be mindful with the damage, since you could break it and die if you go too quickly. Once the adds spawn, focus on them.

**Portabellious Shrieker:**

- Burn. Stay off of the green lines.

**Vestigial Broker:**

- Slow burn. His nameplate will change, as will the gem on his wrist, to a color. You must kill the matching colored cart. Do not over burn, less you miss a round of carts and die at the end. You won’t see it coming.

**Palomidiar Alakhaji:**

- Burn. Once he stops taking damage, pull him next to a bookshelf, then have everyone else go to the opposite side and click on it to push the shelf on him. Easiest if you keep the boss on the inside, and everyone else push from the outside.
