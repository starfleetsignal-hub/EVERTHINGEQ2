## Walkthrough

1. [[Danariun, the Crypt Keeper]] 117.21 -38.72 -399.31 91.42 0.00 0.00 Strat :
   - Kill shed of pile of glowing bones in priority
   - Max level note: burn it
1. [[Brutius the Skulk]] 304.10 -55.25 -93.02 6.25 0.00 0.00 Strat :
   - CURE detriments and curse NEEDED
   - Keep aggro on tank especially when it emotes "prepares himself to release a mighty smash!" and joust out. Kill all cave blooddrinkers as they pop.
   - Alternatively, everyone dive behind the waterfall and fight from there.
   - Max level note: burn it
1. The [[Lumpy Goo]] is rooming around {{waypoint 660.17, -46.28, 219.82}}.
   - Strat: Tank and spank, eliminate spawning goo - dispell detriments
   - Max level note: burn it
1. [[Gorius the Gray]] {{waypoint 665.00, -59.16, 66.84}}
   - Strategy: see dedicated page.
   - Max level note: burn it.
1. [[The Kly (Epic)]]
   - Strategy: see dedicated page.
   - At max level, one cannot ignore the script as he is usually damage capped to about 2% of his health per hit, so no script-avoiding fast-burn is possible. He can be solo'ed with patience (but not too much patience, the encounter has a fail condition timer). Avoid procs and DOTs that will tick his counter. Eliminate ripostes and damage shields. Only hit him when it is safe to do so. Work the script.
