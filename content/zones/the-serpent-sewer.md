## Background

The Serpent Sewer is the second of the three sewer zones underneath [[Freeport]]. It is a maze of interconnected rooms that owes its name more to confusing twists and turns than to its sparse snake population.  Adventurers seeking the treacherous [[Edgewater Drains]], or low-level risk-takers that stumble in from [[The Sprawl]] or [[The Thieves' Way]] meet great peril at the "hands" of The Serpent Sewer's neglected inhabitants.

## Notes

- The zone is referred to as: *Serpent Sewer* or *The Serpent Sewer* or even *The Serpent's Sewer*, all of which can be used interchangeably.
  - The zone itself is [[The Serpent Sewer]], while the journal category is just "Serpent Sewer".
- The only two quest-givers in this zone share the last name, 'Puer', despite being of different races: [[Cicero Puer]] *(a ratonga)* and [[Marcus Puer]] *(a gnome)*.
