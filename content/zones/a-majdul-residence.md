## Notes

- The instance is used for the [[A Tears grifter|Tears grifter]] quests, any of which is needed to enter the zone. There are as follows:
  - [[A Lesson in Gouging]]
  - [[Favor for a Sha'ir]]
  - [[Find and Free]]
  - [[Forced Into Retirement]]
  - [[From Riches to Rags]]
  - [[Hedging Our Bets]]
  - [[Hop On Outta Here]]
  - [[Knock Knock. Who's dead?]]
  - [[Send and Retrieve]]
  - [[Subtle Strife]]
  - [[Thieving from Thieves]]
  - [[Vile Dealings]]

- There are several entrances from [[Maj'Dul]]:
  - {{waypoint -260, 137, 48}} - [[Outcasts' Ledge]]
  - {{waypoint -161, 138, 109}} - near [[The Court of Tears]]
  - {{waypoint -143, 138, 134}} - near the Court of Tears
  - {{waypoint 114, 143, 130}} - [[The Gilded Twilight Terrace]]
  - {{waypoint 8, 139, -55}} - east of [[Midday Market]]
  - {{waypoint -45, 158, -192}} - next to the northern [[Guard Tower]], on top of a building

- There is a special version of this instance used for the quest [[Court of Truth: Ishara's Search]].
