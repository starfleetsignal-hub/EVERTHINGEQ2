## Walkthrough

1. Clear all the trash on ground level. When you have an arcane dot, clear it or you will be Feared (running in a random direction).
1. Kill the 4 up on the center building until the first [[A stone setting (Version 2)|stone setting]] drops. Grab the next [[A stone setting (Version 4)|stone setting]] from **a lost stone setting** in the SE corner of the lake behind the tall reeds {{waypoint -297, 345, -249}}. Place the two stone settings in each of the gazebos at the south end of the zone, on the ground {{waypoint -169, 4, -241}} and {{waypoint -189, 4, -240}}. ![A lost stone setting](images/A_lost_stone_setting.jpg)
1. [[The Meld of Vapor (Advanced Solo)]] - Joust when he emotes and casts his AOE (I just kited him around the zone ranging him, never hit me with it). Alternatively group with a healer (not a merc, they die fast) and tank him if you're undergeared, if you can take a 500k hit, and have the healer heal it, you can tank him. He drops [[A stone setting (Version 1)|a stone setting]].
1. Collect the final [[A stone setting (Version 3)|stone setting]] from the pile of rubble in the northeast corner of the zone, by clicking **a partially buried crate** {{waypoint -219, 6, -403}}. Place the two stone settings in each of the gazebos in the north end of the zone, on the ground {{waypoint -188.79, 4.43, -381.97}} and {{waypoint -168, 4, -382}}. ![A partially buried crate](images/A_partially_buried_crate.jpg)
1. [[The Meld of Haze (Advanced Solo)]] - You will receive a Curse that causes your combat arts and spells to cast longer and cost more power.  When he casts Haze, he will disappear and a visage of you, and anyone in zone with you, will appear somewhere in the zone.  You have to Kill your own visage of your self, killing someone else's results in a wipe. he will come running back at you once the visages are destroyed. Rinse and repeat.
1. Go to the top of the temple in the middle of the zone and click the two red outlined stones on the floor to spawn the last boss {{waypoint -172, 30, -338}} and {{waypoint -185, 30, -339}}.
1. [[The Mist Reaver (Advanced Solo)]] - Each of the 4 gazebos will have a clickable icon in them, Physical, Noxious, Arcane, and Elemental.  When he aligns himself with one of the types of damage, click ALL icons that represents the other types he is susceptible to in order to keep damaging him. (i.e. Aligned with Elemental, click Arcane, Physical, AND Noxious)
