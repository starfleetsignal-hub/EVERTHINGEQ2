## Notes

- *Make sure you maintain the buff from the a barrel of fresh fish or a rack of fresh fish to mitigate the damage from Poisoned Plates that the nameds have on them in Graceless.*

## Walkthrough

1. [[Gallopagus]]
   - Follow the path to where the [[A hungry hippolisk|hungry hippolisks]] are at {{waypoint 276.25, 117.01, 21.55}} and kill them to spawn [[Gallopagus]]
   - Clear any lizards and beetles from the area before you spawn Gallopagus (or at least use [[Singular Focus]]).
   - Notice around the area are clickable **buried ruins** - click to raise them into **excavated ruins**. Put these between yourself and the mob when he does his bull rush attack to remove the buff from him.
   - When you see the emote, *Gallopagus's calves bulge as he prepares to bull rush you!*, hide behind one of the three pillars you raised. He will hit it and be dazed for a few seconds. (*In Graceless mode, you want to clear all the mobs and also raise all the pillars before engaging*)*.*
1. [[Fushuga]] & [[Hargan]] at {{waypoint 187.37, 91.76, 223.89}}
   - The nameds' self buff, **Fresh Catch** will increase their potency. Each combat crab increases increments by 1. Increments can be reduced by killing adds, or by charm, fear, mesmerize, or stunning the crab adds, or you can click on them when they are at the edge of the platform and knock them off. Don't let the increments get to a stack of 10!
   - Kill Fushuga first.
   - Hargan will say, *"Haha! Here, crabby crabby!"* when he summons crab adds throughout the fight.
1. [[Stormy Withers]]
   - Take the bridge to the right and go down to the lower level of the next platform. Kill the [[A cliffside reeler|cliffside reelers]] and open the **fished treasure chest** {{waypoint 169.81, 82.81, 405.72}} to release [[Stormy Withers]]
   - When Stormy Withers says, *"Gangway!"* stand on either of the two Stormfury fishers' overlooks nearby at {{waypoint 131.28, 82.82, 377.73}} or {{waypoint 169.52, 82.82, 411.13}}
   - Watch his cast bar for **Bone Spurned**, you must move off the fishers' outlooks to avoid the huge knockback.
   - Text: Being on one of the Stormfury fishers' overlooks when Bone Spurned landed knocks you clear off!
1. [[Pri'ki'ori'ki]] and [[Wa'la'kra'na]]
   - To get to the next named, *"Shark the Jump"* at the giant shark carcass at {{waypoint 160.19, 86.52, 380.43}}
   - Upon entering the large colored dome around [[Pri'ki'ori'ki]] and [[Wa'la'kra'na]], you can't leave except by evac or killing the encounter.
   - [[Pri'ki'ori'ki]] stands rooted in the middle and will not take damage until [[Wa'la'kra'na]] dies.
   - There will be adds throughout the fight.
   - Clear detriments as they occur.
   - After [[Wa'la'kra'na]] dies, [[Pri'ki'ori'ki]] will constantly summon adds that will force target. She will not leave her spot in the middle of the platform, and the adds will force target and disarm until cured or killed.
1. [[Agmork the Almighty Angler]] {{waypoint -67.60, 118.31, 272.87}}
   - Spawns crab mobs consistently throughout the fight.
   - When Agmork says, "I'm outta here!" he leaps into the air and a big wave comes over the platform and drops a shark. The waves cast a huge knockback. Get under a tent to avoid being knocked off the platform. When the wave has passed, get behind a shark and kick it off the platform and continue kill the adds/kill the named.
