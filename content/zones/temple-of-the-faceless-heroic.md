## Strategy

- Venekor Hardmode
  - Tank in front of the pillar next to the entrance. Group on opposite site of circle.
  - On red text message, the tank should move under Venekor's feet to avoid the massive frontal attack, then return to their original position.
  - Bring a lot of DPS

## Notes

- Don't kill the Lizardman at the last Pavilion for hardmode
- There is an [[Agnostic Dungeons|agnostic]] version of this dungeon available to players level 10 through 95.
