## Background

[[Tarin Varinblade]], a servant of [[Qeynos]] stationed in [[The Feerrott]] states: "A great spirit is growing, being summoned from the dead that this forest has claimed. An army of the undead lead by a great specter will soon claim this land and then others." As the Feerottian jungle has claimed many [[Norrath|Norrathian]] souls throughout the years, this specter's undead army would be enough to threaten any of the sentient races of the world. It must be stopped!

## Notes

- While the overall map is the same as the [[The Trembling Lagoon]], impassable rocks have been added to turn the zone into something of a labyrinth, with a very specific progression.
- There is one spot where you can climb over the wall to reach the lagoon. This is in the altar area, at the far side of the wall from where you came in. Jump up the boulders and you can get to the other side. However, if you jump down to the beach there is no way back short of calling home or using an evac/portal.
  - If you use [[Escape]] inside the instance you will be transported back out to [[The Feerrott]].

## Monsters

![The Trembling Lagoon as it appears in this instance.](images/Cleansing_of_the_Cove.jpg)

- These are the monsters for the zone when you do it to complete the access quest. Some may be different on subsequent visits.
- Every single mob in this zone is Aggressive, though none of them appear to be Social.
- Monsters in this zone do not respawn.
- For some reason [[Disturbed mariner]] and [[Lost mariner]] award Achievement Experience the first time you kill one.
- A. [[Brush constrictor]] (36vv-36) snake
- B. [[Razortongue slinker]] (36v) monitor lizard
- C. [[Red moss snake]] (37vv) snake
  - [[Evol Ew skin eater]] (37vv) lizardman, paired with a [[Domesticated silkspinner]] (37vv) spider
- D. [[Evol Ew council member]] (37) lizardman
  - [[Evol Ew blood weaver]], [[Evol Ew spirit weaver]], [[Evol Ew jungle weaver]] (37vv) Lizardmen in the camp
- E. [[Evol Ew handler]] (38vv) lizardman paired with [[Razortongue stalker]] (38vv) monitor lizard
  - [[Razortongue stalker]] (38) monitor lizard, without handler
- F. [[Tormented water weaver]] (38v) skeleton mage
  - [[Disturbed mariner]] (39vv) skeleton pairs, singles are 39v
  - [[Lost mariner]] (39v) skeleton
  - [[Spiketail gnawer]] (39^) horned lizard
  - [[Enslaved spirit]] (39^) ghostly iksar roamer, Necro with grim thulian pet
  - [[Awakened mariner]] (39) skeleton
  - [[Arisen spiritshaper]] and [[Arisen deathbringer]](39vv) skeleton trios
- G. [[Vengeful acolyte]] (39^) ghostly skeletons, initially non-agro. Kill all surrounding mariners and spirits in Area F to agro.
  - Strategy:
    - Recommend you go clockwise and clear all the trios of skels first, then spiral your way to the altar.
    - Acolytes become agro one at a time, the first one after you kill the last undead outside the altar
    - **a spectre** appears after you kill the first acolyte. When you kill the second acolyte **a raised spectre** rises. When you kill the 3rd acolyte he becomes [[The Spectre of the Feerrott]]. ***He does NOT attack immediately as long as you are not in his range.
  - [[The Spectre of the Feerrott]] (39^) ghostly skeleton
    - Zone lockout timer (4 hours) starts when you kill the Spectre
    - Drops:
      - [[Long Sword of Undead Vengeance]]
