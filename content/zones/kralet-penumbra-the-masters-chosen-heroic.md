## Strategy

1. Proceed down the hall and follow the path until you get to first intersection, then head north.
1. At the next intersection head east and begin the counter-clockwise circuit of named, started with [[Golguth]]. {{waypoint -27, -42, 193}}
1. Exit the room and head due west to the next named, [[Servitor of Yothshaval]]. {{waypoint 83, -41, 193}}
1. Return to the first intersection due east, this time going north. A new door will be opened with a portal {{waypoint 7, -41, 162}}
1. Run down the hallway and go north at the intersection, following it counter-clockwise to the next named, [[Ogabo, Weapon of Yothshaval]]. {{waypoint 81, -2, 155}}
1. Leave the room and head south at the first intersection then follow the path counter-clockwise to the west side to the next named, [[Xal'Gilah]]. {{waypoint 88, -3, 231}}
1. Exit the room headed east, then take the ledge north up the next set of stairs. There will be a portal to the east near {{waypoint 14, -3, 192}}.
1. Head east and kill the mobs in the room. Return to the hallway and there will be another portal {{waypoint -4, 22, 162}}.
1. Head west and kill the mobs once again. There will be another portal in the hallway you just came through {{waypoint 66, 22, 262}}.
1. How about another portal for your portal... just kidding, turn around and head west to the final named [[Yothshaval, Acolyte of Penumbra]]. {{waypoint 109, 22, 229}}

- There is an [[Agnostic Dungeons|agnostic]] version of this dungeon available to players level 10 through 95.
