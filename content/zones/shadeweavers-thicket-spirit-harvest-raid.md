## Walkthrough

Quest: The turn in is [[Nelon Hes]] in the Bazzar in Fordel Midst.

### [[Nelon Hes]]

- Kill all hidden shadeweavers to spawn

- HP: 697,666,378,545,929
- Combat Mit:199,722
- Resolve: 5,085

Strat:

- Needs [[Glacial Freeze]] to lower his level.
- [[Nelon Hes]] will emotes "begins to siphon power from your allies". Joust out 25m.
- When ant adds spawn, burn them down as the named mem-wipes while they are up. The energy absorb joust will wipe raid so you have to kill ants ASAP so he doesn't run to raid while absorbing.

Raid leader clicks ant mounts after the fight to summon transport for the raid to the next area.

At the wooden platform, each person clicks on the backpack to be ported across the chasm. Raid should not move until everyone is across: it's possible to dump people to their deaths in the chasm if you engage too soon.

<br>

### [[Eternal Cinder]]

- Talk Nelon Hes and take the Ant ride over to next name

- HP: 645,153,452,114,083
- Combat Mit:199,722
- Resolve: 5,085

Strat:

- Needs [[Glacial Freeze]] to lower his level.
- The easiest way to do this fight is to wedge the raid in between the barrier and edge of the burnt tree up the hill. Avoid white circles, and kill adds if someone does step in the white circle.
- Every 15% he does the joust, emoting, "the Eternal Cinder sticks his tentacles into the ground". Joust out to the edge of the cliff

<br>

### [[Fershistha|Fenirek'tal]]

- HP: 620,147,471,720,748
- Combat Mit: 191,644
- Resolve: 5,085

Strat:

- Needs [[Glacial Freeze]] to lower his level.
- When [[Fershistha|Fenirek'tal]] emotes "prepares to spray a foul liquid" joust behind him.
- When he issues the emote "Fenirek'tal begins to crush" you need big heals along with detriment cures.

<br>

### [[Khati Sha]]

- HP(Form 1): 400,098,692,219,507  (his pet 150 Trillion)
- HP(Form 2): 699,965,839,839,45
- Combat Mit: 229,450
- Resolve: 5,190

1. Form 1 Strat:
   - Needs [[Glacial Freeze]] to lower his level.
   - Separate Kahti Sha and his pet.  Focus kill the pet.
   - Adds will occasionally spawn.  Taunt them from the named and kill quickly.
   - At 50%, Kahti Sha will full heal to 100% and turns into Kahti Sha the Twisted, which will stay up even if you wipe.
1. Form 2 Strat:
   - Needs [[Glacial Freeze]] to lower his level.
   - Joust 25 meters away from named to avoid his dreamscape.
   - Khati Sha the Twisted spawns two adds (at 80%, 70%, 60%, 40%, 20%, and 10% of HP) called "a haunted emotion" and "a tormented husk". The adds must be kept away from the named, separated from each other and killed quickly or they turn into an Amalgamation of Xuul, which wipes the raid.
