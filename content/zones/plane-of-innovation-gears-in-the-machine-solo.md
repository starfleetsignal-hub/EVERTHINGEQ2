## Walkthrough

<< Work in Progress>>
Remember to cast Fount of Power if not automatically done.

Avoid the furnaces with visible flames. They'll root you for a short time and throw fire at you.

## Bosses

1. **IMPORTANT**:  Do not talk to Meldrath the Marvelous (Clockwork) at {{waypoint -16, 4, -122}} until you have collected the "Magnetic Ether Compensator" at {{waypoint -128, 4, -35}} and "Maelin's Talismanic Whirlgurt" at {{waypoint -19, 12, -170}} or you will have to do the zone over again.
1. Kill [[Repair Bot 5000]] at {{waypoint 36, 4, -31}}.
   - The mob has a stiff power drain. Bring power potions and cure arcane pots if you don't have power regen or cures.
1. Kill the [[Powered Mechanization]] at {{waypoint -1, 4, -116}}.
1. Use the door opposite the [[Powered Mechanization]].
1. Kill [[Toa the Shiny]] near {{waypoint -214, 4, -186}}.
   - Periodically during the fight, Toa the Shiny will emote about an electric shock. Stopping all dps for ?? time or take massive damage.
1. Go to {{waypoint -48, 4, -186}} to spawn [[The Junk Beast]] and kill it.
1. Approach {{waypoint 26, 4, -185}} to spawn Meldrath the Malignant and listen to his rant.
1. Kill [[The Manaetic Behemoth]] when it becomes active.
   - Periodically it will put down red circles and send out missiles to the center of the circles. The missiles do damage if they hit and will explode a short time after landing.
