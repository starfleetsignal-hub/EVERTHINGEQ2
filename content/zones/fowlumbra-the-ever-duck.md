## Access

The zone is restricted via a portal that has a chance to spawn from the death of any mob in Thalumbra. The portal WILL despawn after some time, so make sure to click in if you see it spawn.

- There will be an emote in the chat window citing **In a burst of feathers, a portal appears!**

Note that this is a solo instance, and even if fighting in a group setting the player that gets the killing blow on the mob that prompts the portal is the one that is able to zone in.

The portal will only spawn once every 24 real time hours. When you see the zone wide message, take note of the time. Come back to the zone the same time the next day and begin killing any mobs in Thalumbra. The first mob that is killed in the zone after exactly 24 hours has elapsed should get the portal. Do not die in the zone, or you will only be able to revive outside and cannot re-enter.

## Strategy

Upon zoning in you will receive a buff that greatly increases your health and power, recommend waiting until you regain full health and power prior to starting the progression.

There will also be two npcs immediately to your right to mend your armor and give access to your bank.

There are initially a bunch of flocks of ducks all around the zone, as well as three named up at the time of zoning in [[Firequacker]], [[Skullquacker]], [[Wisecracker]]. No real strategy as the buff should allow you to survive them with ease.

- You will want to make sure you kill every flock of trash you see, as they all drop a small chest with [[A duck billed plat-apurse]] that contain anywhere from 120-180 platinum. The entire zones worth should add up to roughly 10,000 platinum.

Once the three initial named are defeated, the final named will spawn in the south portion of the zone - [[The Robber Ducky]], which will reward [[The Robber's Cache]], yielding a subclass specific hat.
