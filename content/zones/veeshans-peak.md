## Background

Inside the largest active volcano of the [[Skyfire Mountains]], Veeshan's Peak is the nigh-impenetrable ancient fortress of the Ring of Scale, the dragons that rebelled against the Claws of Veeshan and left with [[Jaled Dar's Shade|Jaled Dar]] to [[Kunark (Continent)|Kunark]]. This is a fiery abode built by dragons, for dragons. None but the most steadfast of heroes may hope to leave this place unburnt!

## Notes

- [[Trakanon's Lair]] is located deep within, but an [[Taking on Trakanon|access quest]] is required to enter.
- The Skyfire Mountain Post is the closest [[Sokokar|sokokar post]].

## Walkthrough

Make sure to get the [[Taking on Trakanon]] quest from [[Snyr'dok]] so you can take out [[Trakanon]] later.

Start heading to the Abysmal Chasm. As soon as you enter, a firedrake sentry and 3-4 frenzied racnor will come after you and your raid. Once you clear them, clear the rest of the trash mobs in the area so your raid isn't attacked later during the raid.

After that, go left from the where the drake attacked and move on to kill [[Kluzen the Protector]].

After you kill Kluzen, go through the tunnel and kill [[Nexona]].  Be careful not to go in the water; it will hurt you after a few seconds.

Go back and go up the bridge and follow the path towards [[Elder Ekron]].  Be careful when going across the second bridge, as meteors will try to hit the raid and will deal moderate elemental damage to those affected. To the left there is a staircase going down to [[Elder Ekron]].

After killing Ekron, go back up and through the tunnel and enter the Athenaeum of the Scale {{waypoint 1274, -370, 259}} . Clear all the trash mobs before solving the puzzle to kill [[Druushk]]. Now have 2 people go to either side of the room, both flip the first lever. Now go kill [[Druushk]].

After you've killed Druushk, go to his spawning room and teleport out. Go down the tunnel with draconian sentilels in it {{waypoint 23, -459, -342}}. Continue down until you reach [[Taskmaster Nichok]]. If you want to be able to mend your gear, go to the left and talk with Nixvisss  {{waypoint 7, -450, 67}} and he will ask you to kill the next few bosses. Now go to the right and collect a machine key by clicking on the bench at  {{waypoint 133, -459, -391}} ; anyone in the raid can get on of these. You will need it for the [[Silverwing]] fight later.

Go to the left and go up the path {{waypoint 14, -459, -433}}. (Note: The path will take a left turn, DO NOT turn left, keep straight and go up the wall {{waypoint -24, -449, -444}}.  Then get everyone on the elevator and press the button. It is a box with an glowing orange symbol on it.

Now go kill [[Milyex Vioren]] {{waypoint 197, -204, -1027}} . He has an enslaved engineer with him.

Go to the left and through the tunnel at  {{waypoint 165, -213, -708}} ).  There are two bosses in that direction: [[Qunard Ashenclaw]] and [[Hoshkar]].  When you enter the Clawed Vault {{waypoint 303, -148, -374}} you will come across a Hive on the floor of the path. Examine it, you will be attacked by Cinder Wasps. Kill Cinder Wasps until  [[Qunard Ashenclaw]] pops. Then, continue through the tunnel until it opens up and you see [[Hoshkar]] down below. Take the path to the left. There is a rock floating in the lava, when it comes by to you jump on it then to the platform Hoshkar is on. Then do the same on the way out.

Go back to the fork  180, -213, -750 ) and head west (the other path) to [[Xygoz]]. Hail him trigger the fight.

Use the teleporter to get back to the Chasm. Now head East through the passage {{waypoint -198, -451, 275}}. Continue until you come to the end. There will be two doors to the right guarded by Juggernaut Golems. Go through one of the doors. Travel south until you come to two more doors, take either one and follow the path to kill [[Travenro the Skygazer]].

Turn South and continue to the portal. Step through the portal and kill [[Silverwing]]. Reprogram the 8 turrets starting at {{waypoint -805, -557, 788}}. There are 2 turrents up on the ledge. Continue through a tunnel {{waypoint -665, -553, 727}} and past the teleporter to kill [[Shade of Khalan Dar]] and [[Phara Dar]].

Now go back to start, however you would like ( I used a totem of escape ) and talk to [[Snyr'dok]] again. Do the turn in and he will offer to take you to [[Trakanon's Lair]].  The portal to Trakanon's Lair that was once inactive, will now take you to him. Once there climb the stairs and kill [[Trakanon]]. Loot the trophy from his body.
