## Walkthrough (copy from Solo)

1. Kill the following
   - Slay the Beast Master
   - Massacure the Matriarch
1. To get to the next area you must kill Eaglovok {{waypoint -361, 124, -650}}
1. Click on the crystal at {{waypoint -333, 124, -646}} to go to the next area.
   1. Note: to return to the previous area, use the crystal at {{waypoint -228, 106, -459}}
   1. To get to the Zenith Helionae use the path at {{waypoint -183, 110, -189}}
