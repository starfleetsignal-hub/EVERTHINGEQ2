## Background

[[The Sage of Ages (Maiden's Gulch: Rescue of the Sage)|The Sage of Ages]], an important historian charged with scribing the events of history so that their lessons will not be forgotten or repeated, has been trapped by Drakota within [[Maiden's Gulch]]. Gather your allies and rescue this important [[Norrath|Norrathian]] figure from a horrid death!

## Access

To gain access to the instance, speak with [[Sebir the Historian]] in [[Lavastorm]] at {{waypoint -170, -10, -547}}. You must be at least level 50 to hail the Historian and gain access to the instance. However, other group members who are at least level 45 will be able to obtain the quest from the Sage of Ages.

## Walkthough

1. Engage and slay [[Vyr'drais the Vicious]].
1. Speak with [[The Sage of Ages (Maiden's Gulch: Rescue of the Sage)|The Sage of Ages]] once Vyr'drais is dead to pick up the quest, [[To Speak as a Dragon]].
