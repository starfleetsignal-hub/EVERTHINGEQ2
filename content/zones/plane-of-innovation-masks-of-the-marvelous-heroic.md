## Lore

![Entry view](images/Plane_of_Innoviation_Entry_view.jpg)Home of all things innovative and mechanical and watched over by the formless entity known as the Great Gear, the **Plane of Innovation** has thrived during the absence of the Gods through the ingenuity of Norrathians. It is this mortal power of creativity that the plane feeds upon. (Blame the gnomes if you want, as we all know the inquisitive minds of gnomes are limitless.)

Meanwhile, the clockworks under the Great Gear’s influence are unforgiving and calculating in defending their plane. Within the depths of the scrapyards, a massive foreboding building known as the **Manaetic Factory** looms.

In the factory, all of the evolving machines are fabricated and tested... on none other than YOU! Survive these "tests" and see what the Great Gear and his creations have lying in wait for those who attempt to defile the plane.

Another being has had some influence on the plane, who might be a recognizable name in Norrath’s history - Meldrath the Malignant has made the Plane of Innovation a home of sorts, using the advanced designs of the clockworks to figure out how to possess machinery at will.

Meldrath has started replacing his own body parts with superior mechanical devices, but is in need of fresh organic material to continue to research fusing biological tissue into mechanized bodies… That is where Norrathians come in!

Adventurers are sent to seek out Meldrath who might know of a way to gain access to further planes in the pursuit of the Tenets of Hate. Why he would choose to help Norrathians in this task remains a mystery.

Invading “fleshbags” will need to battle past all of the plane’s defenses to find out exactly what Meldrath is up to and how he has been able to hide away on the plane without being forced out by the native machines.

The denizens of Innovation will not hold back in defending their home, and they have no fear of death as they do not even grasp the concept. This makes them efficient killing machines that can easily dispatch the majority of any invading force.

## Walkthrough

### [[Ancient Clockwork Prototype (Heroic)|Ancient Clockwork Prototype]]

- First named. The most difficult boss in this dungeon
- He and his two adds "Clockwork Prototype XXVII"  each equals to a named from health to damage(15M). So you basically kill 3 bosses here.
- "Clockwork Protection" last for 10 second and immune all damage. Focus on healing and stoneskin stuff

### [[Clockwork Scrounger XVII (Heroic)|Clockwork Scrounger XVII]]

- Keep everyone 12m and above distances each to avoid "Short circuit" (chain lighting deals severe damage and eliminated entire group) during combat especially it says "*Short circuit detected ...* ".
- Named buff with stoneskin every time end of"Short circuit".
- It hit his target with "Sprocket Spray" dealing 33.4M damage(some times 293.1M) when changing target. Death prevention and stoneskin matters.

### [[The Glitched Guardian 10101 (Heroic)|The Glitched Guardian 10101]]

- It summons adds in 4 each time. maximum to 8 "a whirling gear"
- "Strange Ticking" deals 10M magic damage over time
- Ignore adds just focus on named. It is the easiest named in this dungeon

### [[Glitched Cell Keeper (Heroic)|Glitched Cell Keeper]]

- Same to the [[Everquest2:Plane of Innovation: Masks of the Marvelous (Solo)|solo version]]. about 5 seconds before red circle explode
- Only in Expert Version red circle extends every time

### [[Gearclaw the Collector (Heroic)|Gearclaw the Collector]]

- Same to the [[Everquest2:Plane of Innovation: Masks of the Marvelous (Solo)|solo version]]. But this one hit you harder.
- Everyone stop fighting every time it says *"Gearclaw the collector runs out of power..."* . And one of your teammate "Turn Key". Otherwise its spell "Bolt Buster" hits you 5.1M -> 10.7M->20M ... till you die.
- So don't hit it if "*runs out of power*". Instead you can revive all you teammates,heal them,mana restore them,get prepared then "Turn Key". There is a 600sec count down not sure why solo version shows normal but heroic and expert version disappear. After 600sec named reset and your team get wiped
- Your mana drain out when you are the one Right-click Gearclaw the Collector and choose "Turn Key".
- It gets buff "Energized!" increase its damage every time you "Turn Key". Dispel it with [[Absorb Magic]]/[[Machined Energy Inverter]]. You can ignore it if your team is strong enough
