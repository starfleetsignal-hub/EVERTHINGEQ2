This zone encapsulates what used to be [[Qeynos Harbor]] and [[The Elddar Grove]]. In [[Update:64|LU64]], [[Qeynos]] was combined into two zones, **Qeynos Province District** and [[Qeynos Capitol District]].

## Layout

### Landmarks

**Qeynos Province District** is the commercial and trading hub of [[Qeynos]]. With [[The Harbor Cache]] processing all goods brought into the city from every corner on [[Norrath]], the nearby taverns of [[The Mermaid's Lure]] and [[Fish's Alehouse and Inn|Fish's Alehouse]] are more than happy to receive the coin of Qeynos' many tourists and traders. The central plaza sitting at the feet of [[The Grounds of Fate]] and the entire road leading to the entrance to [[South Qeynos (POI)|South Qeynos]] form a grand market where an adventurer can find anything to suit his or her needs.

To the north, past the human dominated regions of the district, nests [[The Elddar Grove (POI)|The Elddar Grove]], home to [[Arbos, the Elddar Tree]] and the seat of elvendom in Qeynos. Elves have been essential in the defense and maintenance of the city, so in honor of their sacrifice they have been given civic control of an entire quarter of the city.

### Travel

Qeynos can be traversed by [[Mariner's Bell]], which are found mostly near waterways. For a list of all bell locations see [[Mariner's Bell#Qeynos Bell Locations|this article]].
