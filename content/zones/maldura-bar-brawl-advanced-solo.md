Enter the Zone.  Attack the brawlers, they will fall unconscious.

When you have KO'd enough, Dalkuhm will spawn.  "Kill" him.  This updates the "Free-For-Brawl" quest.

When you have KO'd enough, Barnum and Bailey (linked) will spawn.  Knock them out as well.

Another mob will spawn, and this mob will count for the "Free-For-Brawl" quest.

The Vengeful Elder will spawn.  This mob will count for the "Free-For-Brawl" quest.
