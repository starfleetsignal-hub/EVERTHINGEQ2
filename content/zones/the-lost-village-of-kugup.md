## Required Quest

- Good-alignment: [[When the Darkness Comes]]
- Evil-alignment: [[Eliminate Geraufl]]

## Walkthrough

This is the hidden village of the frogloks. You enter this zone by examining the [[Froglok Pondstone]] while standing on a stone in a pond in [[Antonica]] {{waypoint 95, -23, 488}} or [[The Commonlands]] {{waypoint -1013, -47, -242}}. As soon you enter, [[Uncovering the Froglok Secret]] will prompt. Complete it to complete this instance.

## Notes

- This is a one-time solo instance and cannot be completed with friends in a group.
- This is the quest version of [[The Hidden Village of Kugup]], containing only quest and ecology NPCs. Once you have earned the frogloks' trust, you will be able to enter Kugup freely from it's access point in [[The Feerrott]].
