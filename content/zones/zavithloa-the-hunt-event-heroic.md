You enter at [[Hunter's Lagoon]].

Go to {{waypoint -3944.23, -87.59, 89.45}} to examine a carcass. Little [[Compsognathus]] arrive. They eat the meat of the dead body and then start to attack you. Kill them all. Waves of different mobs arrive and need to be killed.

- Achievement Reward: [[Compy Stomp]]
