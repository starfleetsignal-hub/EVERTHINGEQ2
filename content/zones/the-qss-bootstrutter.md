## Background

![Location of The QSS Bootstrutter on the Mariner's Bell map.](images/The_QSS_Bootstrutter.jpg)
The route to [[The Feerrott]] has finally been discovered. You must board the QSS Bootstrutter and [[Protect the Priest's Ship! (Good)|assist Boulden Smitestone]] in defending the ship on it's perilous journey.

## Walkthrough

Speak to [[Boulden Smitestone (QSS Bootstrutter)|Boulden Smitestone]] and pick up the quest, [[Protect the Priest's Ship! (Good)|Protect the Priest's Ship!]]. Make sure to continue through all the quest dialogue, not just accepting the quest as soon as it pops up, in order to start the event. Slay the skeletal orcs as they spawn and eventually [[A Rallosian Captain]] will spawn. Kill him and continue slaying the skeletal orcs until no more spawn. This means that Boulden has finished his warding spell and it safe to leave the instance. Speak to him to do so.

## Notes

- This instance can only be re-accessed via the [[Globe of Swift Travel]] in [[The Feerrott]], [[The Thundering Steppes]], or [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]] once the quest has been completed.
