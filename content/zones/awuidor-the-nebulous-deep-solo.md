## Notes

- Various portions of Awuidor are under water. To avoid drowning, use a waterbreathing spell, the [[Fishbone Earring]], [[Totem of the Otter]], a tinkered [[Rebreather]], etc.
- For the most part, if you wish, you can edge your way around the outside edges of the platforms to reach an emissary while avoiding aggroing most of the trash mobs.

## Strategy

1. Use the teleporter at {{waypoint 756, 341, -1326}} to travel to the [[Tower of Awuidor]].
1. Swim up the water column to the top to clear the mobs up top.
1. Clear the mobs to spawn and Kill the [[Emissary of the sink]] on the second level of the tower to spawn Glaucos of the Sink at the top of the tower  {{waypoint 0.00, 630.33, 1217.87}}
1. Re-enter the water shaft and swim to the top of the tower at {{waypoint 2, 631, -1252}}, then kill [[Glaucos of the Sink]].
   - Glaucos has a trauma that causes a large power drain. Cure it quickly.
1. Use the teleporter at {{waypoint 2, 631, -1252}} to access the south water tower.
1. Clear the mobs to spawn and Kill the [[Emissary of frost]] on the second level of the tower to spawn [[Nehalenea Frostbringer]]
   - The [[Emissary of frost]] is a tiny white wisp located near the very back of the platform.
1. Swim up the tower to the top, then kill [[Nehalenea Frostbringer]].
   - Nehalenea has an elemental that causes a large power drain. Cure it quickly.
1. Use the teleporter at {{waypoint 2, 630, 1184}} to access the East water tower.
1. Kill the [[Emissary of the veil]] on the second level of the tower to spawn [[Pontis Aqueous]].
1. Kill [[Pontis Aqueous]] on the top level {{waypoint -1293, 631, 22}}.
   - Joust to about 30m when he emotes *"holds eerily still"* and stay out until the whirlwind visual effects disappear.
   - Also has a knockback and power drain.
1. Use the teleporter at {{waypoint -1241, 630, 22}} to access the West water tower.
1. Kill [[Oceanus the Titan]] at the base of the tower at {{waypoint 1307, 394, 21}}.
   - If you've got water breathing, fight him in the water tunnel to avoid being feared off the platform to your death.
1. Kill the [[Emissary of the fathoms]] on the second level of the tower to spawn [[Aegaeon, the Violent]]
1. Kill [[Aegaeon, the Violent]] on the top level {{waypoint 1318, 626, 21}}.
   - Aegaeon summons small waterspouts that cause a knockup, and periodically ports you.
1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 1265.93, 630.80, 48.67}} to return to [[Myrist, the Great Library]].
