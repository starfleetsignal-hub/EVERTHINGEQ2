Note: The entrance can be confusing. In Paineel, right-click the transporter and go to The Hole. In the room with three doors, choose the West door to Underfoot Depths. The doors to the east and south also say "the hole," but it is the West door you want.

## Strategy

**Hub area:**

### [[Regulus]] and [[Regulas]]

**South Wing:**

### [[Ritualist Squilak]]

- Will call Master Syfak if not already killed

### [[Master Syfak]]

- Invisible until triggered. Will auto add to [[Ritualist Squilak]] unless pulled first

### [[Rathgar]]

- It seems this one is replaced to [[Graden]].

### [[Vaclaz]]

- Comes with 4 roekillik beastmasters. Available in a challenge-mod is the final named of **South Wing**
- Kill him remove the barriers to **Upper North**

**Upper North:**

### [[Xaalax]],[[Yaalax]] and [[Zaalax]]

- 3 individual named but group together
- Drop one chest after kill them all

### [[Mosaasus]]

- Dragon roaming in water
- Take elevator to **Lower North**

**Lower North:**

### [[Saalax]]

- Group with two Simulacrum of Saalax and Inferno Elemental

### [[Master Yael]]

- Group with a lot of mobs and [[Theerax the Enforcer]]

**West Wing:** entrance near **Hub area** by clicking the door at {{waypoint 111.5, 92, -242.18}}

### [[Aereon]]

- Will call [[Haephaus]] if not already killed

### [[The Taehric Construct]]

- If you want to fight the normal version, right click it and select 'Attack the construct now'
- Wait it charge to activate challenge mode and it change name to [[Energized Taehric Construct]]
- Final named of **West Wing**
