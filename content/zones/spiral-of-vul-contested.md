From LU128 Lure of Darkness announcement:

A cult of Void zealots has made themselves known to Norrath, calling themselves the Cult of Vul. They claim to have been inspired by the old writings of a renegade shadowman named Ssynthi. Their numbers swelled recently after Bellengere the Three began influencing the cult's members, claiming power from the Void can be tapped by its members. His message cast a wide net, and gained the attention of the downtrodden, the gullible… and the greedy.

The revelation of the Void anchor constructed in the Sodden Archipelago, was met with elation by the cult, and answered in swift numbers.

You don’t have to be Lady Najena to know, their excitement could mean our downfall! It is up to you to put a stop to their plans and end the Cult of Vul! Do you have what it takes? Join us for the Lure of Darkness and find out!
