## Lore

Named after the famed dwarven king, [[Dagnor Butcherblock]], this rocky terrace is home to all manners of rugged creatures. Yet none are as infamous as the dwarves, who built their mighty kingdom of [[Kaladim]] from within the bowels of the nascent earth. When time and destiny eventually met, the dwarves spread onto the surface where they would come to dominate the surrounding area. They reigned supreme for centuries, serving ever-dutifully their god [[Brell Serilis]]. Unfortunately, Butcherblock would be heavily battered and tortured by the events of [[The Age of Cataclysms (Lore)|The Age of Cataclysms]], which left the landscape scarred. Coupled with a reduced dwarven presence in the wake of the [[Grand March]], Butcherblock would fall under the control of kobolds, bugbears, and aviaks. Recently, however, dwarves and adventures of all types have begun returning in droves to Butcherblock, most notably [[The Irontoe Brigade (Faction)|The Irontoe Brigade]], who seek to reclaim Kaladim.

## Geography

Butcherblock Mountains is divided into valleys by steep impassable cliffs. Some of the cliff faces can be climbed, and a few of the mountaintops can be explored. Dwarven mines honeycomb the hillsides, and many of the valleys are interconnected by tunnels.

### Travel

[[Griffon Tower|Griffon Towers]] have been erected at the top of the cliff above the docks, and at the passes to [[Greater Faydark]] and [[The Lesser Faydark]]. The [[Butcherblock Docks]] also has two [[Ships]], the [[Journey's End]] and [[Storm Breaker]], that provide transport to and from [[Nektulos Forest]] and [[The Thundering Steppes]], respectfully.
