## Steps

1. Climb up the wall.
1. Kill trash mobs.
1. Kill [[Jennre Warsinger]] 132 heroic (Achievement [[Victory: Jennre Warsinger (Solo)]] at first time). Wanders arround. Tank and spank for me, maybe it will be a good idea to pull him into the flames.
1. Kill [[The Ancient Void-Touched Wumpus]] 133 heroic (Achievement [[Victory: The Ancient Void-Touched Wumpus (Solo)]] at first time). Tank and spank.
1. Don't forget the shinies!
1. Take the lift.
1. Step on purple teleporter disk.
1. Stay at the arrival disc and activate the runes.
1. At next level clean the trash mobs in eastern, middle and western room. Be carefull: [[A Theerian stormbringer]] may have knock-back attack.
1. Boss mobs [[Telvorsinn]] in the north and [[Thovalakk]] in south room. They are linked (Achievement [[Victory: Telvorsinn and Thovalakk (Solo)]] at first time). Both 133 heroic. Tank and spank.
1. While searching shiny, in western room appeared [[Zevorg the Warmonger]], which is linked with [[Nexion the Warmind]] in eastern room. Both 133 heroic (Achievement [[Victory: Nexion the Warmind and Zevorg the Warmonger (Solo)]] at first time). Tank and spank.
1. Catch up the shinies ;)
1. Back to central disk, right click to activate the runes: Up.
1. Again: Kill trash...
1. ... then [[Yynzik the Scornridden]], which gives Achievement [[Victory: Yynzik the Scornridden (Solo)]] at first time. Again: Tank and spank the 133 heroic.
