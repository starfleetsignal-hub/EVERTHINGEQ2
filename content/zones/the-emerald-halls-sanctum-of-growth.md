## Notes

The Sanctum shares the same layout as the third floor of [[The Emerald Halls]]. Within the zone, you will find a great many non-attackable creatures which represent Tunare and Faydwer in general: wisps of growth, seedling drakes and seedling steeds (unicorns).<br>
There are, however, a few [[A wild bear|wild bears]] which roam the Sanctum's grounds, but they are well past where you will speak with [[Kurista]] or [[Firiona Vie|Eva Corunno'thes]].
