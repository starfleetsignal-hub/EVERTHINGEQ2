The Ironforge Exchange has both an instanced area and a crafting area in the building near the entrance to the instanced area. Between the two of these, crafters will find most amenities they might need.

## Entrance

Entrance is from {{waypoint 331, -20, -99}} in North Qeynos in [[Qeynos Capitol District]].

## Features

### Inside the Instance

The tradeskill instance contains the following amenities:

- [[Hawthorne Carris]], the Work Order foreman {{waypoint -0.69, -0.48, -1.32}}
- [[Crier Madlyn Treebright]], the Rush Order foreman {{waypoint -1.51, -0.48, -9.95}}
- [[Work Order Clipboard]] x24, one on a wall or shelf near each crafting station
- [[Elbow]], the Fuel Merchant {{waypoint 1.52, -6.48, 11.46}}
- [[Hyacinth Hagglefoot]], the Broker {{waypoint 10.23, -0.43, -9.01}}
- [[Chemistry Table]] x3 {{waypoint 2.82, -6.45, 23.31}}
- [[Engraved Desk]] x 3 {{waypoint -22.79, -6.44, 21.92}}
- [[Forge]] x6 {{waypoint -11.61, -6.41, -32.58}}
- [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]] x3 -1,86 -6.48 0.73
- [[Stove & Keg]] x3 {{waypoint -22.6, -6.44, 22.09}}
- [[Woodworking Table]] x3 {{waypoint -1.86, -6.48, 0.73}}
- [[Work Bench]] x3 {{waypoint 2.82, -6.45, 23.31}}

The instance does not contain a [[Bank]], a [[Crafting Trainer]], a [[Tradeskill Tutorial]], a [[Tradeskill Career Counselor]], a Trades Coordinator (grandmaster), an NPC for Adorning and Tinkering daily tasks, or a faction merchant.

## Notes

This tradeskill instance **does not** have a [[Bank]]. The nearest one is across the road from the outside crafting area at Qeynos Hold {{waypoint 274.44, -17.95, -120.02}}.
