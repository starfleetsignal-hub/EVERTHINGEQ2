## Notes

- The only boss that you'll probably need to be concerned with is Riyadh, who will silence you and strip your buffs. He also summons five treants and seems invulnerable as long as those are up.
- There is an [[Agnostic Dungeons|agnostic]] version of this dungeon available to players level 10 through 95.
