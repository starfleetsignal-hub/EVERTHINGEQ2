## Note

You must have finished [[Shattered Dawn: Execution of Order]] to have access, but you can be zoned in by your group leader.

## Walkthrough

1. Click the gate near {{waypoint -81.25, 78.12, 2.85}} to enter the main room
1. Defeat four sets of 3 mobs each in the second room, noting the column they are near. These are:
   - [[A frigid Luminary]],
   - [[An aqueous Luminary]],
   - [[A sanguinated Luminary]], and
   - [[A toxic Luminary]]
1. Once the luminnaries are dead, the [[Archon of Life]] spawns in the center of the room near {{waypoint -22.94, 78.12, 4.12}}
   - Pull him to each column, in the order which the luminaries spawned, to damage him
   - You can damage him 25% at each column before moving to the next
   - He periodically summons adds
1. Upon death, a group of [[A dire Luminary]] will spawn around each of the two columns in the center of the room.
   - Defeat them to spawn the [[Archon of Death]] at {{waypoint -22.94, 78.12, 4.12}}
   - Simply pull him between the two main columns in the center of the room to damage him, moving from one center column to the other when he stops taking damage.
1. Advance to the last room and engage [[Sanctus Eternus]] near {{waypoint 128.12, 79.25, 4.35}}
   - Fight him in the area where he spawned to damage him.
   - The [[The Embodiment of Life]] and [[The Embodiment of Death]] will join in
