## Background

The Bloodskull Orcs are expanding their base of operations, [[Bloodskull Valley]], and filling it with troops and massive war machines. This surely is a sign that the Bloodskull are planning to launch a massive attack on [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]] and/or settlements in [[The Commonlands]]. A brave adventurer is needed to ensure that this doesn't come to pass!

## Walkthrough

The objective of this instance is to burn the various war machines while slaying as many orcs and troglodytes as possible. If you haven't already completed it, make sure to pick up [[Terithal's Revenge]] from [[Terithal]] right next to the entrance.

The two named monsters are:

- [[Treasure Hunter Bakrok]], part of a ring event located at [[Bloodskull Treasure Rock]]. {{waypoint -105, -39, 959}}
- [[The Troglodyte Champion]], part of a ring event located at [[The Bloodskull Arena]]. {{waypoint 162, -48, 770}}
  - In order to unlock the doors of The Bloodskull Arena you must first burn every war machine. See [[Terithal's Revenge]] for a list of the war machines' locations.
  - **Warning**: Do not jump into The Bloodskull Arena before you have burnt all the war machines or the ring event that spawns The Troglodyte Champion won't activate!
