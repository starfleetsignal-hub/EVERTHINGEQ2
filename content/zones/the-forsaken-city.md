## Background

Once called Tanaan, the Forsaken City was inhabited by human worshipers of [[Zebuxoruk]], the god of knowledge. In an effort to gain the 'greatest doctrine' of their god, the Tanaanites opened a rift into the manatheric ether. Though they did not know the exact nature of his doctrine, for it was hidden to them, the Tanannites knew that it was being guarded by a creature known as [[Chel'Drak]]. Through this rift into the realm of Chel'Drak, they came into contact with the [[The Nizari (Faction)|Nizari]], a race of nayads, snake-like creatures with the torso and face of women. The Tanaanites made a deal with the Nizari, that they would allow them into Tanaan in exchange for the Nizari representing them to Chel'Drak. Sometimes the wisest are lacking in common sense.
