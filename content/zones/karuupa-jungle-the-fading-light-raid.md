## Walkthrough

The first three bosses can be killed in any order. The fourth boss can only be reached when all of the first three are dead, and the fifth boss can only be reached when the fourth is dead.

### [[Quilliclaw]] (Tier 1)

- Clear 8 [[A gargantuan Karuupa Stalker|gargantuan Karuupa Stalkers]] and [[A gargantuan Karuupa Pouncer|gargantuan Karuupa Pouncers]] (predators) to spawn the boss.
  - The named does not spawn immediately after killing the 8th predator; he spawns when someone walks within the circle of stones in the southwest.
- There is a tank swap when he emotes "burns his target's face into his memory"
- This mob Fears every so often.
- Quilliclaw does not cast any form of Barrage; Bulwark of Order may be used as a simple debuff.

### [[Glaur Xrzin]] (Tier 1)

- Kill [[An overgrown Karuupa Fennik buck|overgrown Karuupa Fennik bucks]] or [[An overgrown Karuupa Fennik doe|overgrown Karuupa Fennik does]] (prey) to spawn the boss.
- There is a knock-up, then Glaur ports to the furthest raid member.
  - First knock-up at 95%
- There is a curse, ***Sharpshot Technique***, which must be cured when the afflicted player is within 5m of the mob or else it wipes the whole archetype.
- Glaur heals himself slightly for each person that is in close to him.
- Glaur does not cast any form of Barrage; Bulwark of Order may be used as a simple debuff.

Alternate Technique:

- If you have enough DPS, you can position the whole raid within 5m of Glaur and just beat him down. This makes curing easier for the healers and stops the knock-up and port.

### [[The Vetrovian Mantrap]] (Tier 1)

- Kill 10 [[A vibrant swamp hopper|vibrant swamp hoppers]] to spawn the boss.
- When pulled, the Mantrap summons all surviving [[A noisy skeeter|pesky skeeters]].
- Periodically, a "pesky skeeter" spawns. This add co-op strikes with the Mantrap.
- The tank's equipment is constantly damaged in 1% increments.
- An incurable noxious lands on a random member of the raid. That player must cure it with [[An "Acquired" Witchdoctor's Potion Bag]] or the entire raidforce will wipe. Bags are looted from Dedraka pygmies in the southeast corner of the zone.

### [[Behmothrus]] and [[Gargantaur]] (Tier 2)

- The beasts cast an incurable curse called **In the Line of Danger**. This is removed by standing between the two bosses while they are both staring at the cursed player.
- Roughly every 50 seconds, the beasts cast a detriment that lands on all players within 16 meters and deals significant damage. This detriment cannot be cured by normal priest cures. Each detriment starts with 10 increments, and each cure removes 1 increment.
  - Gargantaur casts an elemental called **Overconfident Execution**.
  - Behmothrus casts an arcane called **Magical Minded**.
- At 80%, 50%, and 25%, the two beasts run towards each other and "high five", then they swap aggro. Behmothrus runs towards Gargantaur's target, and Gargantaur runs towards Behmothrus' target.
- At 80%, 60%, 40%, and 20%, the two beasts kill their respective targets immediately, unless the raid is spread out (5 meters from any other player).
- Neither beast casts any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] may be used as a simple debuff.

### [[Guardian of the Plumage]] (Tier 2)

- Boss has a frontal, tail swipe, and stifle. This fight also has a 20-minute time limit.
- Save all big hitting spells and combat arts for 50%.
- At 50%, the boss will go to sleep. This is the time for the raid to go all in on damage, you do not need to worry about aggro during this (lasts about a minute).
- The dps requirements are around 3.8-5 trillion raid-wide, the goal is to keep the boss from healing to 60%, if the raid fails to do so then when the boss hits 50% it will fall asleep and start healing.
- Once the boss stands up, there is a 25 meter joust.
