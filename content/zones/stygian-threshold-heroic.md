## Steps

1. Clear Treants.  While you're at it, walk by each of the purple/yellow wisps and kill the faes that spawn (see 3rd name).  Gather "a bundle of treant branches" (chest drop from each treant), take to {{waypoint 183, 31, -380}}. Place on Firepit, Continue placing till fire starts. This will spawn 1st Name [[Timbergrod]] {{waypoint 233, 32, -365}}
1. 2nd Name = [[Metamorphous]] {{waypoint 466, 66, -125}}. Tank, spank, occasional adds if you dont kill fast enough.
1. 3rd Names = [[Diurna]] Regent of Dawn, and [[Nocturna]] Regent of Dusk... in other words, the day queen & the night queen. To spawn them, you have to walk by all of the purple and yellow wisps around the forest area and kill the fae that spawns. Eventually you'll "anger" them and then it'll tell you that they popped. {{waypoint 185.50, 32.43, -458.32}}   If you dont kill them fast enough, you have to dispel their self-root or find a way to drag them in to the area opposite of what they are (drag night fae to the sun-lit area, or day fae to the dark area)
1. 4th name = [[Grozgar]] {{waypoint -288, 14, -42}} the kobold. Not sure if there was a strat cuz he died so fast.
1. 5th name = [[Heracyne]] the spider. {{waypoint -319.71, 50.41, -70.79}}. Kill most of the spiders, including the non-aggro [[A curious scurrier]] to make her attackable. Not sure if it was the number of spiders that triggered her or the scurrier. Just #KillEmAll.  Loot a Silk Gland from the body.
1. 6th name = [[Zaraxia]], another spider.  {{waypoint -237.9, 90.54, 178.86}}. Use the Silk Gland you looted from Heracyne's corpse to protect you from the toxic atmosphere in her den.
1. 7th name = [[The Ink Walker]]. To spawn him, walk along the "beach" and trigger the ones sitting with their tails in the air. Run behind them and herd them in groups of 3 toward the named. It'll say something like "an ink lurker leads you into the water."  Walk six-ish of them to the named's spawn point and you'll be able to kill him. You'll know when you got there because you'll get the message "an ink lurker has led you far enough (x2)."
1. 8th name = [[Mawz Harak]]. <**I could not burn him lower than 80% - why?**> He has around 7 billion HP and it just makes for a very slow fight.  He casts a 20 second stun every so often, which he telegraphs by saying a message to Brell/God/etc. When he does that, just walk around behind him and keep blasting. There's also a mild knockback...  I recommend pulling him in to the little dome area so the knockback isn't an issue. Bring him in den Crystal Show Room (on the lower terrace below where boss fight starts) and use the crystal there. It grants a temporary buff that allows you to smash the spirits of the kobolds around the arena. **When he becomes invulnerable to your attacks right click one of the circles with ghosts inside. This provides "divine power". Mawz's shield collapses, allowing you to burn him down.**
