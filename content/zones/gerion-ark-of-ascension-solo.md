## Walkthrough

1. Use the portal behind Najena to enter [[Gerion, Hold of D'Lere]] quest hub.
   - Once there, use the northern portal at {{waypoint 1.08, 0.89, -133.20}} to enter [[Gerion: Ark of Ascension (Solo)|Gerion: Ark of Ascension [Solo]]].
1. Kill [[Dogmadog]] at {{waypoint 0.07, -1.17, -429.74}}.
1. Use the [[To Triforium of Servitude]] portal set in the floor at {{waypoint -108.13, 1.32, -469.24}}.
1. Identify which end alchove has bright, flaring torches and which alcove has non-flaring torches.
1. Kill [[Nazkun]] at {{waypoint -149.63, 23.68, -469.39}}. ![Look for the purple flames!](images/Rage_of_Cthurath_-_Keep_Calm_and_Gerion_-_purple_flames.png)
   - Damage is slow unless take to the alcove with lights on i.e. lights must be on.
   - During combat the lights can turn off and you have to use the floor portals to move to an alcove with lit (flaring) torches, then cure detriment(s).
   - *Note there are 3 Teleporters in front of each alcove, Left, middle and right they can be used to teleport to the other alcove. Curing the deteriment will summon the named to you*
1. Use the [[To Transept of Servitude]] portal at {{waypoint -112.63, 24.06, -469.08}}.
1. Use the [[To Triforium of Pain]] portal at {{waypoint 109.74, 1.29, -469.69}}.
1. Kill [[Surinon]] at {{waypoint 149.35, 23.68, -469.76}}.
   - Damage is slow unless you fight in the alcove with no lights on i.e. lights must be off.
   - *Note there are 3 Teleporters in front of each alcove, Left, middle and right they can be used to teleport to the other alcove. Curing the deteriment will summon the named to you*
1. Kill [[Xelha Nevagon]] at {{waypoint 0.37, -0.62, -564.77}}.  Seems to be straight forward tank and spank.
1. Kill [[Paragon of the Overlord (Solo)|Paragon of the Overlord]] at {{waypoint 0.25, -1.20, -443.22}}.
1. Use the [[Leave the Ark of Ascension]] portal at {{waypoint 11.59, -1.16, -455.59}}
