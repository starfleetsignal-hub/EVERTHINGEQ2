## Access Quest

Complete two quests for [[Tish Tickers]]: *(see the [[Thundering Steppes Timeline]] for more details)*

1. [[A Scrying Shame]]
1. [[The Gnoll Cave (The Thundering Steppes)|The Gnoll Cave]]

## Walkthrough

Tish Tickers has lost her [[Scrying|scrying]] equipment and needs help retrieving it from the gnoll cave. However, some mysterious power within has even the gnolls on edge.

Upon zoning in, you will revive the quest: [[Stop the Elementals]]. Fight your way forward killing the pitiful Sabertooth Gnolls and any [[Released elemental|released elementals]] you come across. Eventually you will encounter [[A Rampaging Elemental|a Rampaging Elemental]] at {{waypoint -400, 0, -620}}, slay him to complete the instance.
