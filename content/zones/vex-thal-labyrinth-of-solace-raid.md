Level 1

   1. HP: 800.1 trillion
   1. Mit: 229,450
   1. Resolve: 5190
   1. Use one of the scepters to pull.
1. To open the light wall at {{waypoint -80, -40, -320}} you will need to set the statues at {{waypoint 87, -3, 29}}, once these are set then you will be able to turn off the lights and pass through to head to [[Xakra Fu'un]] .
   1. Icon of Conscience {{waypoint 78, -30, -214}}
   1. Icon of Absolving {{waypoint 0, -20, -80}}
   1. Icon of Empowerment {{waypoint -82, -61, -221}}
   1. Icon of Symmetry {{waypoint -42, -19, -149}}
   1. Icon of Artistry {{waypoint 41, -19, -160}}
   1. Icon of Wonderment {{waypoint -2, -19, -207}}
1. Defeat [[Xakra Fu'un]] at {{waypoint 0, -20, -540}}
   1. HP: 835,196,142,818,686
   1. Mit: 229,238
   1. Resolve: 5190
   1. Use the scepter of illumination on the darkness
1. Open "area" {{waypoint 80, -40, -320}} you will need to set the statues on the other side. Once they are set you'll be able to turn off the lights and pass through.
1. Use Switch at the wall in the room at {{waypoint 80, -20, -320}}
1. Use Teleporter-Gate at{{waypoint 30, -40, -320}}

Level 2

1. Use Azure Teleporter at {{waypoint 15.13, 88.03, -424.64}}to go to NW-dome<br>Note: The Betrayer can be made stronger by to sending him to the next location. You can do this 3 times. OR...you can fight him...
1. Activate [[Sezjin the Betrayer]] at {{waypoint 80, 90, -210}} by speaking to the unattackable verion and telling him to fight.<br>Note: As you come closer to the named to acitvate it, adds will spawn left and right to the named.
   - First (easiest) level of Difficulty
   - Second Level of Difficulty
     - Detrimentals during the encounter
       - Illuminating Emperment III: Resolve 5190, Flurry -45.0, Doublecast -122
       - Surge Create Chaos II: DOT
       - Traitor's Vassaglage: Cure it, as if you have still this curse active and you will hit by the second Traitor's curse, you will be tricked (unknow effect).
       - Traitor's Admission: Cure it, as if you have still this curse active and you will hit by the second Traitor's curse, you will be tricked (unknow effect).
       - Cloak of the Persistent
1. Use Azure Teleporter at {{waypoint 0, 150, -350}}to go to gold
1. Clear area to active portal to next area
1. Use other Gold teleporter at {{waypoint 50, 150, -230}}
1. Clear area to active portal to next area. (this is where the named will be if you sent him on you can send him on one more time from here)
1. Use portal at {{waypoint 85.36, 88.33, -251.63}} goto Emerald
1. Clear area to activate portal to next area
1. Use other Emerald teleporter at {{waypoint -14.32, 88.43, -208.04}}
1. flick the switch then go through the door
1. Defeat the triple [[Zun Thall Heral]] with [[Zun Diabo Xiun]] and [[Zun Liako Ferun]]
   - Effects in the encounter
     - "Mark of Many" will stun use stun-rune to counter
     - "Mantle of Mindlessness" will stifle use stifle-rune to counter
     - "One the clock": Encounter must be defeated within 15min
   - You have to split the encounter, so that none of the three named are to close to each other.
