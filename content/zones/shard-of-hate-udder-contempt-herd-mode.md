## Introduction

- This tongue-in-cheek level is accessible once everyone in your group has completed all the achievements for [[Shard of Hate: Utter Contempt (Heroic)]] (i.e., killed every possible name).  THIS IS NOT ACCURATE.  Players who have not completed the achievement, "[Heroic SoH] Triumph: Decimate Hate" can be pulled into the zone by a group member who has completed the achievement.
- Offical acknowledgement of this level was proffered by Bunji in the Test Update Notes for Tuesday June 22, 2018.

## Quest

Pick up daily quest from Bella, Concordium Experiment.  Located next to Daily Heroic quest giver in Valor {{waypoint -129.24, 0.00, -108.82}}.  As soon as you complete the Planes of Prophecy Triumph achievement, "[Heroic SoH] Triumph: Decimate Hate" then, you will be able to see this lovely bovine!
