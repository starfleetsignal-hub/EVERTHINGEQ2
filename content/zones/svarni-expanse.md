## Geography

### Travel

Travel within the Svarni Expanse is mostly by foot or mount.  Flying mounts cannot be utilized until unlocked by completing the quest, [[Visions of Vetrovia: Into The Keep]].

The Far Seas Trading Company has setup a makeshift raft {{waypoint 567, 3, 487}} at [[Svarni Gateway]] to transport you to [[Kamapor]], and a similar raft {{waypoint 202, 3, 445}} exists there to transport you back.

Travel to the adjacent zone, [[Karuupa Jungle]], is provided by plumedrake.  Speak with [[A plumedrake keeper]] {{waypoint -33, 53, 553}} in the hills Southeast of [[Kamapor]].
