## Background

[[Gnip Gnopp]], a fine constructor of gnomish toys and gizmos in [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters|Gnomeland Security]], would be glad to provide [[Gardy Ex-Giftgiver|Gardy]] with wonderful new mechanized gifts, but he is unable to meet the demand. His shop is closed up and is in a state of disarray. If I help him clean the place up and get things in working order then he would be able to help.
