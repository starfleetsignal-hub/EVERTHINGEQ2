## Background

This was once a place of ethereal beauty, but now it rots. it lies broken and in disrepair. Nothing is as it was when the [[Erollisi Marr|Queen]] reigned here. When she was here, the lands were tended, the waters flowed and the grounds were kept pristine. But sight of that has been lost. The sword resides where heart should be, and grief is held within its unshakable grip.

## Access

In the past, access to this zone required the completion of a world event access quest, which was dependent on your alignment. However, these quests were removed from the game in :

- [[Through the Gates of Bliss for Love]] for good-alighned characters
- [[Through the Gates of Bliss for Hate]] for evil-aligned characters
