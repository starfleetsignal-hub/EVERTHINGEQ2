## Walkthrough

1. Activate a Teleporter
   1. Attack the [[A volatlie current|Volatile Currents]] and drag them over to the inactive silver piles on the ground. After a [[A volatlie current|Volatile Current]] is dead, the silver pile will become an  [[An overcharged silverick|Overcharged Silverick]] and attackable.
   1. Drag the [[An overcharged silverick|Overcharged Silverick]] to the middle of the three large balls on the platform then kill it.
      - Repeat this until all three balls are electric. They are auto-updates and you need 3 to activate a teleporter.
      - **NOTE:**  Some of the silver piles took forever to defeat, when that happens, just to the middle of a different ball. For me, this worked and it went down faster.
1. Once the teleporter at {{waypoint -470.93, 252.95, 602.27}} is active, click it to teleport up.
1. Kill [[Satashi the Staggering]]
   - Summons adds and stuns. Plan adornments accordingly.
   - Cure arcane, and has a nasty knockback.
1. Once defeated, step off the platform to jump back down to the [[Maelstrom Mesa]] level.
1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint -453.21, 252.95, 636.52}} to the [[Havoc Hold]] area.
1. Activate the next teleporter which is located on the [[Havoc Hold]] level.
   - Follow the same steps to have 3 [[An overcharged silverick|Overcharged Silverick]] activate a teleporter. You should now have 6 silver ick.
1. Take the teleport at {{waypoint -614.99, 255.19, 742.02}} to the top.
1. Kill [[Vashtu the Volatile]]
   - Summons adds and fears. Plan adornments accordingly.
   - adds are called [[A cloud of chaos]] and each add gives the named 20% damage reduction and 5 adds up = instant wipe
   - Periodically he says "I'm starting to get angry!" or "I'm getting angry again!" and places a buff "Explosive Temper" on himself. That buff starts with a count 25, counting downwards. Dispell before it reaches 0 or you get knocked back.
1. Once defeated, step off the platform to jump back down to the [[Maelstrom Mesa]] level.
1. Take the teleporter at {{waypoint -631.33, 255.19, 776.87}} to the [[Conductor's Cliff]] area.
1. Activate the next teleporter which is located on the [[Conductor's Cliff]] level.
   - Follow the same steps to have 3 [[An overcharged silverick|Overcharged Silverick]] activate a teleporter. You should now have 9 silver ick.
   - *NOTE: You should now have the update to return to Oolfonson and don't need to kill the next named.*
   - If you decide to proceed, Take the teleport to the top.
1. Kill [[Etosh the Electrifying]]
   - Attack and DPS his two hands first. He has a knockback and a fear, but both can be cured before the debuff ends.

***Don't forget your shinies!***
