## Notes

- Zone in is from [[Falinpol]]. {{waypoint 297, 160, -195}}
- It's recommended that you have 465 crit chance for this zone.

## Walkthrough

**The Nexus Core [Center]** - [[Luminox (Heroic)]]

- Click the sphere in the center of the first room with the inactive automatons - this will engage the zones defenses and bring them to life. Kill them and the other aerial mobs in the area to spawn [[Luminox (Heroic)]].
- Luminox has two attacks to be mindful of - a knockback and missiles he fires at roughly every 20% health. Watch positioning for the knockback and either dodge or out-range the missiles. Burn him down the next 20%, rinse and repeat.
- Once defeated, click the sphere in the center of the room once again to active the second phase of commands that raises all the platforms to get to the other wings of the core. Move counter-clockwise from the zone in, headed West first.

**Chamber of Energy [West]** - [[Amalgam of Energy (Heroic)]]

- Kill all the adds in the room and then engage with the named. At 75%, 50% and 25% health, joust away from him as he does huge AE and will summons energy globes. Run around the room and try to kill them at a range to avoid them coming into contact with Amalgam and healing himself. He will summon more globes as his health gets lower.
- If he does absorb some of the globules he will not emote again if he happens to heal above whatever health threshold he was at, so just focus on killing them and burning him down once they're dead.

**Chamber of Energy [North]** - [[Energy Delivery Module (Heroic)]] and [[Force Absorption Module (Heroic)]]

- Kill the automaton's in the area and then engage with the Energy Delivery Module first. During the fight, he will emote and root himself, then fire a laser that must be avoided - just joust to the left or right of whatever direction he is facing. The Force Absorption Module will be attacking you during this phase and may knock group members around, be mindful of positioning.

- When The Energy Module is down, switch over to Force Absorption Module. Every 12-15 sec, he will cast a spell (Hammerhand) that must be interrupted. We had a bard so DPS'd him down for about 5 seconds after the Energy named went down and had him cast Bladedance while we burned him down. Seemed to work.

**Chamber of Oscillation [East]** - [[Maligned Gyro (Heroic)]]

- Common strategy is to clear the named blades in the hallway and jousting the floor blades until you enter the room, then pulling the named to the doorway avoiding the two swarms of smaller blades inside the room altogether. The swarms die fast enough so if you want to be safe just clear them out before engaging the named.
- During the fight, the named will emote and summon his blades that spawn on its location then slowly work outwards. Did not notice a lot of damage from them (if any) as they are relatively easy to joust out of the way from. Burn the named down and head back to the center for the final named.

**The Nexus Core [Center]** - [[Luminox Prime (Heroic)]]

- Once all the other named are defeated, Luminox the Prime will spawn in the center room where he was originally as Luminox and roam around it.
- This fight is similar to the original encounter in terms of health percentages and missiles, but with a few notable additions.
- Started around 80% as before, Luminox will emote. This time, he will spawn black and gold globules that float all around the center of the area in addition to firing the missiles as before, doing much more damage than the original encounter. Joust away from him and avoid the black globules, else you will take massive damage and in some cases die instantly.
- Rinse and repeat this until 60%. After the missiles at this 60% threshold, Luminox will imitate the Energy named from earlier and room himself to fire a laser. Joust left or right to avoid it and continue to burn him down. He was able to get two fired off before getting to the 40% threshold.
- The remainder of the fight is rinse and repeat avoiding missiles and black globules.
  - Strategy that seemed to work best was to start the fight on one of the ramps towards the prior named. Once Luminox would emote, the team would joust through the globes to the opposite side of the room to that ramp. There seemed to be a safe spot to be both away from the globules and out of range of the missiles.
