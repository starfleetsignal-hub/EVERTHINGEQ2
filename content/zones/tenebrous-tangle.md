## Background

Fractured islands floating in a void of eternal dusk is all that remains of the [[Plane of Sky]]. The twisted jungles that lie atop these islands are dominated by the Droag, humanoid servants of the dragons. Many other bizarre creatures dwell here that while generally neutral, are quick to judge an outsider when they enter into a disagreement with one of their own.

Tenebrous Tangle is one part of the [[Overrealm]], also called the Dragon Isles due to the presence of dragons and their underlings. The floating islands that make up the zone are chained in place above the 'Undercloud', which obscures the islands' locations to the land dwellers of [[Norrath]].

## Geography

![](images/Tenebrous_Tangle-final.png)
![](images/Tenebrous_Tangle_Zone_Connection_Map.jpg)
To reach Tenebrous Tangle, and the other zones of the Overrealm, you must use an [[Ulteran Spires|Ulteran Spire]]. Simply click on the portal at the center of any Spire in the world and a game map will open up, allowing you to teleport to the connected Spire within Tenebrous Tangle. There were originally six Ulteran Spires spread throughout the Shattered Lands, with the ones in [[Antonica]] and [[The Commonlands]] being the only ones that would teleport you directly into Tenebrous Tangle. However, post-[[Update:56|LU56]] all the Spires opened up to each other and since then Tenebrous Tangle can be accessed from any one.

### Travel

The zone is split into seven floating islands:

- [[The Temple Grounds]] - Center
- [[Fear Tainted Isle (POI)|Fear Tainted Isle]] - South
- [[Vicious Breeding Grounds]] - Southwest
- [[Gazer Isle (POI)|Gazer Isle]] - West
- [[Ravasect Incursion Point (POI)|Ravasect Incursion Point]] - Northwest
- [[Bixie Isle (POI)|Bixie Isle]] - Northeast
- [[Vultak Scavenging Site (POI)|Vultak Scavenging Site]] - East

Travel between these islands is facilitated through a network of rideable clouds. Unfortunately, not all cloud stations connect with each other, and you may have to take a couple intermediate clouds to reach your destination. See the maps on the right for a visual guide.

### Town

There is a small camp called [[Hidden Refuge (Tenebrous Tangle)|Hidden Refuge]] located near the zone-in. If you don't have a mount that can absorb the fall, it can be reached by jumping from the waterfall near the zone-in to a pool on the island below. A few town services are provided here, such as a merchant and mender.
