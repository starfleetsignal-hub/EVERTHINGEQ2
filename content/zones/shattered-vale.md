## Notes

- The access quest, [[Vale of the Shattering]], must be completed for access to this instance. Until completed, the zone-in coordinates will default only to the [[Invasion of the Vale]] instance.
- The zone is full of various types of Sabertooth gnolls.  Killing them *does* affect factions, damaging [[The Sabertooth Gnolls (Faction)]], but improving [[The Great Herd (Faction)]] and [[The Far Seas Trading Company (Faction)]].

## Walkthrough

There are a few low-level gnolls on the trail leading into the zone, and then packs of lvl 16-17 gnolls running around it.  The gnoll packs are on a 5 minutes real time respawn timer - you can ignore them but they'll likely run into you and complicate your fights if you don't.

The druid ring itself is surrounded by [[A Sabertooth oracle (Shattered Vale)|Sabertooth oracles]].  Clear all of them to spawn [[Ungruthar the Juggernaut]], [[Captain Vulgart]], [[Oracle Yagwai]], and [[Warlock Umnoz]].  All four of them are worth a bit of AA each.
