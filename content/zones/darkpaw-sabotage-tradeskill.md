This instance is a sub-section of the Darkpaw Warrens, with several paths blocked.  Enemies in this instance are very high level, so don't proceed past the first room without Darkpaw Illusion!

For the initial quest and daily mission, all components and fuel needed can be harvested in the instance (see Quest/Mission Steps below.)  Post-mission crafting does require some drops and collected items from Darkpaw Warrens.

## Crafting

Once you've completed the [[Sabotage and Circuses]] quest, you'll be able to craft new items from the [[Darkpaw Adventuring Aids]] recipe list (received automatically.)  The fuel and most of the components for these items can be harvested in the nearby Zygmoyd area, but some items are only found in the [[Darkpaw Warrens]] instance.  Note: all of the components, fuel, and drops are tradeable.

Crafting can be done at the Darkpaw Sawhorse and at the Darkpaw Campfire, both at {{waypoint 76, -80, -123}}.  All recipes use 1 Darkpaw fuel, in addition to other items.  All harvests return 1 item, but the harvest is subject to Bountiful Harvest.

Items that can be crafted at the Darkpaw Sawhorse:

- Darkpaw Arcane Ark
- Darkpaw Gong Mallet
- Darkpaw Ladder - places a ladder in front of you in Darkpaw Warrens, to be able to climb onto ledges and other areas
- Darkpaw Powder Keg - places a barrel that can be ignited to destroy nearby walls and break apart terramite deposits for raw terramite
- Darkpaw Shovel - used to dig up dirt mounds and mushroom patches
- Darkpaw Sniffer
- Darkpaw Terraporter Crystal - used to repair broken terraporters
- Darkpaw Wolf Whistle

Items that can be crafted at the Darkpaw Campfire:

- Darkpaw Seasoned Shank
- Zygmoyd Sporegasbord

## Quest/Mission Steps

These steps apply to the initial quest [[Sabotage and Circuses]], as well as the daily mission Flea-Bitten Sabotage.

1. I must destroy the flea-ridden pelt.
   1. Click on the pelt on the ground at {{waypoint -68, -70, -253}}
1. Create a Darkpaw Illusion.
   1. Click on the Darkpaw Leather pelt on the ground at {{waypoint -52, -68, -242}} to collect 1 darkpaw leather
   1. Click on the small Darkpaw Fur pelt on the ground at {{waypoint -32, -69, -230}} to collect 1 darkpaw fur
   1. Craft the illusion using the Darkpaw Mortar & Pestle at {{waypoint -60, -65, -219}}
1. Create and use a Darkpaw Flea Repellent.
   1. Click on the flower at {{waypoint -42, -69, -207}} to collect 1 fleawort
   1. Click on the plant at {{waypoint -71, -69, -230}} to collect 1 Darkpaw Herbs
   1. Craft the flea repellent using the Darkpaw Mortar & Pestle at {{waypoint -60, -65, -219}}
   1. Click on the purple sparkles in front of the 3 itchy adventurers at {{waypoint -47, -65, -227}} to throw
1. I need to locate the ingredients to craft Darkpaw Acid. NOTE: Don't forget to use the Darkpaw Illusion now, if you haven't already!
   1. Click on the salt pile at {{waypoint -92, -70, -205}} to collect 1 Darkpaw Salt
   1. Click on the purple sparkles in the water at {{waypoint -87, -74, -167}} to collect 1 Mineralized Pond Water
   1. Craft the Darkpaw Acid using the nearby Darkpaw Vial
1. Apply acid to bolts of Darkpaw fabric.  Note: All 3 bolts are in the same room.
   1. Click on the green design fabric at {{waypoint -85, -71, -201}}
   1. Click on the blue flowered fabric at {{waypoint -86, -73, -184}}
   1. Click on the blue geometric fabric at {{waypoint -47, -73, -175}}
1. Find the missing gnome.
   1. Glorin can be found in a cell at {{waypoint -20, -64, -107}} - stop to harvest any Metal Scrap along the way!
1. Craft some bolt cutters in order to free the gnome.
   1. Craft the Darkpaw Bolt Cutters at the nearby Darkpaw Forge
   1. Click on the purple sparkles in front of the cell door to open it
   1. Speak to Glorin.  He will vanish as soon as the conversation ends.
1. Craft a Darkpaw Bone Grinder and use it to grind up nearby bones
   1. Travel to {{waypoint -48, -69, -88}} and harvest Darkpaw Stone from the Stone nodes
   1. Craft a Darkpaw Bone Grinder using the Darkpaw Mortar & Pestle at {{waypoint -68, -70, -69}}
   1. Click on 3 of the nearby bones (the skulls with purple sparkles) to destroy them.  The bones can be found throughout the area with undead.
1. Investigate the dying mushroom and make use of it
   1. Travel to {{waypoint -52, -65, -215}} (in the first room) and click on the mushroom
   1. Click on the plant below the mushroom to collect 2 Natural Antifungal
   1. Craft Slow-Acting Antifungal using the nearby Darkpaw Mortar & Pestle
   1. Travel to {{waypoint 60, -82, -107}} and click on the purple sparkles to release the antifungal
1. Speak with Glorin in the Zygmoyd area.
   1. Talk to Glorin at {{waypoint 80, -80, -125}} (this will complete the daily Flea-Bitten Sabotage)
1. At this point, you can return to the entrance at {{waypoint -81, -59, -277}} or stay and harvest/craft.  The Darkpaw Illusion will persist until you cancel it or leave.
