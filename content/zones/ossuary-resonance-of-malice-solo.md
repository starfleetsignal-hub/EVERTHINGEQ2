## Praetor Pheris

- North alcove at {{waypoint -147.31, 6.68, -79.95}}
- South alcove at {{waypoint -147.24, 6.60, 33.30}}

Praetor Pheris walks the balcony to the east of the zone-in point and up the north or south stairs. The best strategy is to drag him into the middle of the walkway between the two alcoves. This is because at 75%, 50% and 25% he will port into either side and cast bonewall. If you are not in the alcove with him by the time he finishes casting you will be hit with around 19,000,000 crushing damage. Dragging him back into the middle after each port gives you ample time to make it into the alcove with him.

If you start the battle with Praetor Pheris in the north alcove get him down to about 77% health. At 75% health (and at 50% and 25% health) Praetor Pheris will port to the alternate alcove. Waiting until he ports will not give you enough time to get to the opposite alcove before he does crushing damage which will kill you. Start running to the opposite alcove at about 2% - 4% percent before he ports. This will give you enough time to get to the opposite alcove and start bringing his health down again.

This is my experience. Other experiences may differ or be more or less effective.

## Achiun K'dal, Peris K'dal, & Ursher K'dal

Fairly easy fight.  They have quite a few HP, and don't hit hard.  However...

Clear trash in the room (see why below)!

Achiun will port away from you and start casting "Bone Spear".  This hits for apparently random amounts of damage but it hits for 50k-150k lands about 5 times within seconds.  Easy to interrupt but if you don't clear the trash in the room you will have to fight through them to interrupt the casting.  The other two are easy tank n spanks.

Killing them one at a time does not cause any problems.  It gives a buff though:

Kill Achiun first and get:
-Essence of Achiun:

- Increases Potency of Target by 1,000.0%
- Increases Crit Chance of Target by 10,000.0
- Cannot be Modified except by direct means

Kill Peris first and get:
-Essence of Peris:

- Increases Crit Bonus of Target by 1,000.0%
- Increases Crit Chance of Target by 10,000.0
- Cannot be Modified except by direct means

## Valdimus V'Derun (and singing friends)

The fight was easy enough: Stayed on named, he/she ported to the organ and did who knows what, but no damage bonus was apparent.  I didn't kill any of the Malice "singers" though and I got bugged - stayed in combat.  I ran to the zone and exited with no issue.

Roughly every 20% he runs to the organ and becomes invulnerable. "kill" the 4 malice singers to drop his buff and make him damageable again. Rinse/repeat. Malice singers don't actually die until the end.
