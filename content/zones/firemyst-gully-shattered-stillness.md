## Background

An ancient necromancer named [[Arch Lich Udalan|Udalan]] has returned to the gully and regained much of his former strength. The Arch Lich has already begun summoning another army, one much greater than the last. Once he has amassed a large enough army he will unleash it upon his old and closest foe...the city of [[Qeynos]]. But the terror and wrath will not stop there, he will raise more dead as he goes, creating an army so vast and powerful that none on [[Norrath]], save the Old Gods, will be able to contend with him!
