## Background

[[Trakanon]] is a general of the Ring of Scale. After the death of [[Jaled Dar (NPC)|Jaled Dar]] at the hands of [[Dominus Ganak|Sebilisian Emperor Ganak]], Trakanon, with dragon allies, descended on what was left of [[Sebilis]] and burnt it to the ground. He now bides his time at the summit of [[Veeshan's Peak (POI)|Veeshan's Peak]], plotting his next move.

## Notes

- Added in [[Update:51|LU51]], players who have completed the quest, [[Taking on Trakanon]], can speak with [[Snyr'dok]] in [[Veeshan's Peak]] and teleport directly to the entrance teleporter.
