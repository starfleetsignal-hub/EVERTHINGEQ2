## Background

Deep within the [[Temple of Solusek Ro]] resides an efreeti of terrible power. Gather your allies and rid the world of this menace.

## Walkthrough

1. Progress through this small instance until you reach the final chamber where [[Noble Dojorn]] will be waiting for you.

## Notes

- This instance is listed as 'The Vault of Flames' under the Persistent Instances tab.
- The access quest is no longer needed.
