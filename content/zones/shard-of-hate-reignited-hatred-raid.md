## Introduction

In the wake of Lanys T'Vyl's demise, the throne of Hate was vacant. Saryrn the Mistress of Torment and Ullkorruuk the Archtraitor battled for supremacy, each attempting to claim the seat of power for themselves - a decision they would both soon regret. Much to their dismay, the rightful god of Hate, Innoruuk, was restored and regained his throne with little effort on his part. The vile god vows that they will pay the price for their insolence and arrogance!

Woe to any traveler who dares to explore the revitalized Shard of Hate, the celestial home to the aforementioned divine father of spite and malevolence. Your presence here is unwelcome, no matter your blood, your alignment, or your past actions. All will meet merciless resistance at the hands, claws, and teeth of the newly reunified minions of Hate! But the unique treasures and great power that await inside beckon the bravest and the boldest Norrathians, as they have in ages past.

Will fate see your name added to the ranks of victorious heroes or those of the defeated insurrectors?

- Round 5 – one Tier 1, two Tier 2, one Tier 3

## Tier 1

- [[The Arch Bonefiend]]
  - *Spawned by picking up enough "bones" (red sparkles).*
  - Spawns on the right (east side), walks towards entrance.
  - Severe power drain if dots are not cured quickly. More damage resist if people die with the detriment on them.
- [[The Culler of Bones]]
  - *Spawned by clicking on a note near the beginning.*
  - Raid stays 15 meters away from mob.
  - Adds may spawn on the raid; the off tank keeps adds back to kill the add, then back on the named.
- [[The Deathrot Knight]]
  - *Spawned by placing weapons into weapon racks.*
- [[Lord of Decay]]
  - *Spawned by clicking note near the beginning.*
- [[Lord of Ire]]
  - *Spawned by killing [[Effigy of Ire|Effigies of Ire]] (billy dolls) in the Abbey area.*
  - Every 25% of the fight, one person in the raid will get a curable curse. DO NOT CURE THE CURSE.
  - The person with the curse is the only one that can see and harvest **a sharp stitching knife** and use this item (from their bag or hotkey it) on the person in the raid with the incurable curse (Curse of Ire). You only have about 12 seconds to get the knife and use it or it will wipe the raid.
  - Slow down DPS when the curse switch is due in order to make the switch.
- [[Lord of Loathing]]
  - *Spawned by killing [[A scorn reaper|scorn reapers]], then placing the corpses of the scorn reapers at the top of the stairs of the Abbey.'
  - One Scout, Mage, Priest will be ported to one of the three pillar ledges. The ported player kills the add on the pillar solo.
  - Once the add is dead, the raid must destroy the shield generator at the base of the pillar to free the ported player.
  - Trash mobs will continue to respawn in the area, including the skeletal dragon with power drain DoT.
- [[Mistress of Scorn]]
  - *Spawned by killing [[A thought destroyer|thought destroyers]].*
  - Have the entire raid crouch and walk up on the stage together.
- [[Hoarder P'Lewt]]
  - *Spawned by killing trash chest mobs  until NPC spawns on west side of stairs. Pay the pay Emissary (NPC) 500p to spawn named across path.*
  - Lots of arcane DoTs that cannot be cured by priests – other toons should cure and priests should hotbar arcane cure pots (equip Tranquil Umbrite Orbs?).
  - DoT dramatically increases casting/reuse time.
- [[The Phantom Wraith]]
  - *Spawned by killing [[An ire wraith|ire wraiths]] within 10 min of the clock chime.*
- [[High Priest M'kari]]
  - *Spawned by getting hats from [[Cleric of Hate]] in NW plaza, then placing them on clicky. Ravens in cages become clicky. After a few (all 4?) are clicked a door opens to subzone.*
  - Curable curses (tank, and apparently a different one that kills priests in 1-2 ticks) on a few people at a time.
  - Incurable curses on lots of people; to clear incurable, check the color of the detriment in det window and go through matching alcove (green or purple), which ports you to the other side of the room.
- [[Coercer T'vala]]
  - *Spawned by using barrels to blow up cage where PQ fuel merchant is.*

## Tier 2

- [[Master of Spite]]
  - *Spawned by killing [[A spite golem|spite golems]] and placing parts on the red glowing mound in the right spots.*
- [[Bleeder of Ire]]
  - *Spawned by killing [[A blood ire|blood ires]] and clicking the needle in the Refectory to bloodlet.*
  - Joust reverse to where he sprays poison.
- [[Master P'Tasa (SoH: Reignited Hatred)|Master P'Tasa]]
  - *Spawned by draining blood from corpses strapped to tables after getting vial from vase by coin guy*
- [[Deathspinner K'dora]]
  - *Spawned by clicking the torch by the Anvil, equip the torch, then burn webs*
- [[Demetrius Crane (SoH: Reignited Hatred)|Demetrius Crane]]
  - *Spawned by clicking ravens in the cages.*
  - Break people out of cages when caged.
- [[Hand of Maestro]]
  - *Spawned by destroying music boxes.*
- [[Dreadlord D'Somni (SoH: Reignited Hatred)|Dreadlord D'Somni]]
  - *Spawned by collecting tools, then placing them in the weapon rack by the Anvil*
- [[Grandmaster R'Tal]]
  - *Spawned by killing Kiraikueis.*
