## Strategy

1. Clear all of [[A deep feeder]] to unlock the cages for [[Korphax Kurcan]] and [[Cephax Kurcan]].
1. Drop down into the toxic water and swim quickly to the south until you see a fallen pillar - climb up it into the next room.
1. Clear another round of [[A deep feeder]] to unlock the cages for [[Icethidae]] and [[Pyromorphida]].
1. Text will flash across the screen citing **The braziers have been extinguished. It is now safe to enter the watery depths!**
1. Click the flame from one of the braziers on the current floor {{waypoint 84, -84, 124}} or {{waypoint 85, -84, 48}} then jump down into the now safe water.
1. Get close to the brazier stand {{waypoint 65, -107, 124}} and place the flame into it. A shield will appear needed for the final named, [[Abyssral]].
