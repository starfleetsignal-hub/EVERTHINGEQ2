## Background

Similar to [[The Drowned Caverns: Arena of Heroes]], this instance is an arena-event that tests a player's might against increasingly difficult opponents. Should the player be triumphant against all challenges, they will be crowned champion and rewarded with new armor and unique vanity items.

## Walkthrough

Enter the zone and talk to [[Fip Slytail (Sundered Splitpaw: Gladiator's Triumph)|Fip Slytail]] to initiate the event.  This starts a ring-event.  After each victory, talk to Fip to start the next challenge.  If you are having trouble clicking on Fip Slytail then tab target him and /hail to initiate dialogue.

1. [[A giant plague rat]]
1. [[A famished grotto aranea]]
1. [[Old Yellowtooth]]
1. [[A virulent rock crawler]]
1. [[A dreadsnout ravager]]
1. [[Phosm Stonebend]]
1. [[Kateth Brawnstrong]] and [[Gidheon Pathweave]]
1. [[A hateful Teir'Dal slave]], [[A dwarven gladiator]], and [[A disillusioned gnome slave]]
1. [[The Pit Champion]]

## Rewards

Speak to Fip Slytail a final time and select the last dialogue option stating that you would like your prize and would like to return to [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den|Splitpaw Den]].  Kill the [[A treasure chest (Sundered Splitpaw: Gladiator's Triumph)|treasure chest]] mimic that spawns at the center of the arena and it will drop an actual [[Treasure Chest]] containing various equipment items and two guaranteed drops:

1. [[A mounted dreadsnout head]]
1. [[Summon Zygomyd Sporeling]]
1. One of the following equipment items:
   - [[Arcane Champion's Robes]]
   - [[Arcane Champion's Wristbands]]
   - [[Earring of the Champion]]
   - [[Champion's Glittering Orb]]

## Notes

- Once you have entered the instance, you may not exit without defeating at least one challenger. If you wish to leave after that, inform Fip and the [[Terraporter]] will become active.
- If anyone in the group is too high, they must [[Mentoring|mentor]] down BEFORE you engage the first challenger or you will have trivialized the Arena and no experience nor treasure will be awarded.
- Completion of this instance is one of the several ways to earn the trust of the Splitpaw Gnolls and advance [[A Darkened Shard]].
- If you choose 'difficult' upon entering, you'll get mobs 2 levels higher than normal. If you choose 'very difficult', 5 levels higher.
- Mobs do not scale with the players above level 50. If you are above level 50 and mobs are grey, you will receive no reward, but does count towards earning favor with the gnolls. After each challenger a note will display at the top of your screen telling you that you have trivialized the arena and no reward will be offered, and the you will not be given the final fight with the mimic treasure chest.
- Lockout to repeat the instance is 3 days.
