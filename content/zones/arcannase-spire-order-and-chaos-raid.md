- Strategy

- Shanaira Powermonger: Burn it down.

- Botanist Heridal: Burn him down. Might need to kill the plants he spawns around room.

- Guardian of Arc: Can be burned down if fast enough DPS; otherwise need people in each room to do the towers when he emotes or wipe happens.

- Memory of the Stolen: Burn it down

- Amalgams of Order/Chaos: Need Order and Chaos (NOT Good and Evil) tanks for each one. Chaos takes sk, bruiser, or beserker, Order takes pally, monk, or guardian. Need to keep 'em separated until one is dead - anyone who isn't the correct tank type will get death touched on a melee hit so maintain aggro. Can be solo'ed mentored max level (MML) if one has a 100% melee hit avoidance ability. Wait several seconds (maybe 10) for the script to catch up while 100% melee avoiding before AoE'ing.

- Shanaira the Prestigious(click statue at main room to summon): Requires all 4 ascension classes to do specific ascension combos when emoted, but can be burned down with huge dps before it emotes and wipes. Hp is 9 trillion and change with 13 k combat mit. Need at least 6 people with dps over 100 bil. MML: Hard burn.
