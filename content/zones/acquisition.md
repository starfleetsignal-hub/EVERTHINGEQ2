## Background

The [[Crest of D'Morte]] that you discovered when you [[Unearthing|first visited]] the [[D'Morte Burial Chambers (Zone)|D'Morte Burial Chambers]] led you away from the tomb briefly, only to drag you back there. However, this time something much more dark is brewing within the chamber halls.

## Walkthrough

Kill the trash mobs at your leisure. When you're ready, go up the ramp from the central chamber and engage [[Izinar Malkevantrii]]. Afterwards, defeat the ghost of [[A'Lyn D'Morte]], even though they aren't aggressive. Upon its death, the breakable wall to the southwest becomes interactable. Destroy it and the coffin behind it to spawn the final boss, [[V'Rin D'Morte]].
