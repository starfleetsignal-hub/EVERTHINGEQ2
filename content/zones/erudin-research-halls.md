## Strategy

1. Avoid [[Taranis Gazerra]] near the entrance. It's protected by a shield and attacking it will trigger massive damage called Magnified Beam of Disintegration. To disable its shield, you need to disconnect the four beam machines located throughout the instance.
1. Clear trash, take south-east path up.
1. Kill [[Beam Handler Val'Kinad]] in Control Chamber Alpha.
   - When he says "No! While my power still holds, I WILL NOT FALL!" he becomes invulnerable. Click the machine on the wall to "unplug the contraption". When it disconnects, it will begin emitting an energy beam. Drag the Beam Handler across the beam to remove his invulnerability. Note that you must move him through the beam, so it might take a bit of repositioning by the tank.
   - You will get a message which shows he is "casting a powerful spell". He will be rooted and starts casting Horrifying Channeling. Everyone, including the tank, needs to move as far away from him as possible. Getting hit with his AoE will curse you for about 100K damage per tick.
1. Continue to [[Technomage Grid Mechanic]] and kill him outside the laser room. The lasers hit for 300k each, so in order to pass safely you must use the 8 mirrors in the previous room and place one in each of the glowing blue boxes in the room.
1. Kill [[Beam Handler Pin'Tannil]] in Control Chamber Beta, as you did the first.
1. Jump into the air vent. Caution! control the character so you don't fall off while landing.
1. Next is [[V'lad Olkeen]], kill him outside the circle he spawns in. Do not tank him near the edge. Small clockworks will launch themselves at the group and they have a knockback when they explode.
1. Kill [[Beam Handler Ran]] in Control Chamber Delta, like the others.
1. In next room, press the large red button which reads "Warning! Do Not Press!". This will activate [[A Fully Functional Deathgazer]].
1. Kill [[Beam Handler Polla]] in Control Chamber Omega, like the others.
1. Return to the Antechamber of Gears, the main room.
1. Kill [[Taranis Gazerra]].
   - Start by pulling it next to one of the nearby pillars that are pulsing with energy.
   - It will start gaining buffs called Inexorable Energy Shield which reduces damage by 8% each.
   - After 5-6 of these buffs, the tank should drag him over to another pillar, which will strip the buffs.
   - Depending on the group's DPS, you may need to move it several times.
