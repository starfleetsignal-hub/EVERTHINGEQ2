## Walkthrough

1. Enter [[Blackhook Spiral: Corrupted Caldera (Solo)|Blackhook Spiral: Corrupted Caldera [Solo]]] {{waypoint 218, 287, -796}}
1. Explore the Corrupted Caldera
   - Progress through the zone and kill [[Ghaliga the Ingrate]] {{waypoint -154, 140, 242}}.
   - Cure or you die
1. Find a way to cross the chasm and enter Vuldor
   - Progress through the zone and kill [[Atreyes]] {{waypoint -559, 166, 222}}.
   - Hop on the *spirithound* {{waypoint 568, 168, 187}}.
   - Progress through the zone and kill [[Blackgut the Promised]] he is on top of the next gate {{waypoint -132.88, 175.81, -27.72}}
     - He has a knockback if you don't get out of the target rings that appear on the ground.
1. Delve further into the depths of Vuldor
   - Kill [[Blackgut the Rewarded]] at the next gate {{waypoint 155, 129, -13}}. He calls for adds, which come out of a glowing pile on the path behind you, you can click that pile and Banish before more come out.
1. Reach the Void anchor being constructed in Vuldor.
1. Put a stop to Tarinax's destructive plans
   - Kill [[Lord Tarinax]] {{waypoint 23, 93, -17}}
   - Lady Najena appears during the fight and helps hold Tarinax back while you are tasked with making sure each magi in several groups of 3 is beaten down into "drained magi" stage. Najena only holds Tarinax for a minute or 2 then needs to rest for a minute before she beacons you to bring Tarinax closer to her again. When all magi are drained Tarinax can be killed.
1. An exit portal appears at {{waypoint -24.89, 93.72, 8.01}}
   - Do not leave just yet, if this is the current Hot Zone. [[Charred Lemange]] plus one or more [[A Sizzleshell Slugger|Sizzleshell Sluggers]] at {{waypoint 22.09, 91.19, -15.90}}.
