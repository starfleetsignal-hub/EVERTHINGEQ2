## Background

Within lies [[Godking Anuk]], protector of the Fountain of Life, which is where the once life-giving waters of the Fyr'Un originate. In life, the Godking used the magic water of the Fyr'Un to bring eternal life to all beings within [[The Living Tombs|Ahket Aken]]. However, at some point the water became corrupted, granting its imbibers an unnatural life. Now, alongside the rest of his defiled city, the Godking has fallen into undeath. He lives out his immortality staring out from within this temple at the heart of his lost city, watching as the sullied waters of the Fyr'Un carry their curse to stone and creature alike.

## Notes

- As of [[LU48]] completion of the access quest, [[The Eyes of Anashti Sul]], is no longer required to enter.
- Touching the water anywhere in the zone will spawn [[A minion of the Ewer]].
- Respawn on the trash mobs is fairly short, about 15 in-game minutes.

## Walkthrough

1. Upon entering the zone, turn around, open the door to the foyer and enter.
1. Kill [[Captain Graul Anashite]] who will have spawned back down the ramp.
1. Proceed west (left from the entrance), around the outside of the temple and clear the trash mobs through to roughly the middle-left of the room.
1. Kill [[Eshara]] and [[Lladan Promanari]] repeatedly until their final versions spawn. (Note: There are three other Epic x2 named mobs that respawn like [[Eshara]] and have a final form like [[Eshara the Risen]]. All five will give AA XP as do their revived forms)
1. Kill [[Eshara the Risen]] and [[Lladan Promanari the Revived]].
1. Carefully move the raid to the northwest corner of the room.
1. Kill the the Godking and his power and armor manifestations in the following order:
   1. [[Power of Anuk]], to stop the Godking's fire AOE from triggering
   1. [[Armor of Anuk]], to significantly reduce the Godking's high resistance to spells and melee damage
   1. [[Godking Anuk]]
