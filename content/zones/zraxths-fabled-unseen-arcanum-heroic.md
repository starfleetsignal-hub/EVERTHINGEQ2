## Notes

- This is a 'Fabled' revamp of the original [[The Shadow Odyssey]] 'Ruins of Guk' zones.
- Levels are increased, but zone mechanics and strats are largely the same as the original.

See:
[[Zraxth's Unseen Arcanum]] (Original)
for relevant zone info.

## Access

The Fabled Guk zones are unlocked in sequential order. Succesfully completing one zone, unlocks the next zone in the sequence, starting with [[The Fabled Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen (Heroic)|Halls of the Fallen]]

- [[The Fabled Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen (Heroic)|The Fabled Ruins of Guk: Halls of the Fallen [Heroic]]]
- [[The Fabled Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors (Heroic)|The Fabled Ruins of Guk: The Lower Corridors [Heroic]]]
- [[The Fabled Ruins of Guk: Ykesha's Outer Stronghold (Heroic)|The Fabled Ruins of Guk: Ykesha's Outer Stronghold [Heroic]]]
- [[Zraxth's Fabled Unseen Arcanum (Heroic)|Zraxth's Fabled Unseen Arcanum [Heroic]]]
- [[The Fabled Ykesha's Inner Stronghold (Raid)|The Fabled Ykesha's Inner Stronghold [Raid]]]

## Dungeon Difficulty

Three optional difficulties exist for each zone, and are selected by the whole group at the begining of the dungeon. Completing the zone on one difficulty unlocks the next difficulty for subsequent runs.

- Heroic
- Fabled
- Frenzied

## Relevant Quests

Talk to [[Griv, of The Knights of Marr]] to access all daily/weekly missions involving this Fabled zone.
