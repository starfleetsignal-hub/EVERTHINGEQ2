## Background

Splitpaw Den functions as the town for the Splitpaw Saga adventure pack. The Splitpaw Gnolls found here are friendly to non-Splitpaw and therefore the Den acts as a safe haven, away from the more aggressive Splitpaw and the incensed Underpaw. Unfortunately for the few amiable Splitpaw left, the Den is under constant threat by all manner of dangers. A brave adventurer will find no shortage or work to be done.
