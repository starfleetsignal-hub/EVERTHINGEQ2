## Note

- This is fourth Raid zone. You can enter here after you kill all T1 named in [[Torden, Bastion of Thunder: Storm Surge (Raid)|Torden]]

## Strategy

### **Ferris**(T1)

- Make sure this named **back to** entire raid. It deals a huge damage about 80M per second to non-fighter class **in front** of it. This fire Komodo dragon is huge so non-fighter class do not step into its **target circle**. It damage you too.
- If you non-fighter happens die cuz you overlap with named **target circle,** do not use your self-revive spell like [[Census Inquisitor AA Glossary|Immaculate Revival]]. Let someone in safe zone revive you incase you get instant killed
- Just like all T1 names. It makes huge damage to tank about 5-10 sec about 200M damage add up which accruing about every 5 mins. Usually it deals 3M-6M damage. If you see tank(or pet tank) take a lot damage all of a sudden. Use death prevention and a lot instant heal

### **Zrexul and Veleroth**(T1)

- 2.9Tril Health each
- Try to use **one tank** to both name. "Die together and live alone" buff makes them every time switch target gain 1 increment buff count and it increases infinite. I try to gain this buff 100+ and kill one named but last one just immune to all damage.
- If you are in **fail script**, basically both names **immune** to all damage and your raid **power/mana** constantly get **removed**. In such case just jump to lava and reset this fight
- So try to keep main tank alive and tank both of them and keep **preventing** names **change target** you will get them eventually. They do not have much damage as others in T1 zones.

### **Bling**(T1)

- 3.6Tril Heath
- Heals and DPS.Burn em down stay alive till dead.
