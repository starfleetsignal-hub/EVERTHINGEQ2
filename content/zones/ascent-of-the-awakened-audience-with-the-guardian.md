## Background

Here at the highest chamber of the Ascent, dwell two leaders of the Awakened, [[Gorenaire]] and [[Talendor]]. You must obtain their marks using the gem that [[Lord Nagafen (The Arbiter's Master)|Lord Nagafen]] gave you, should you in earnest desire to enter [[Deathtoll]].

## Notes

- The person who zones in the raid must have completed the access quest [[Audience with the Guardian]].
- Click on the statue at {{waypoint -52, 142, 23}} to summon [[Gorenaire]].
- Click on the statue at {{waypoint -55, 141, -23}} to summon [[Talendor]].
