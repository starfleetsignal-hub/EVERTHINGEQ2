## Strategy

1. Kill [[A fiery steward]] near {{waypoint -166, 22, 208}}.
1. Kill [[Kreigor]] inside a tower at {{waypoint -198.45, 2.60, -87.38}}
   - Periodically during the fight Kriegor says, *"Time to heat up."* Shortly after there is a short duration no-offensive-abilities detriment on the character.
   - Bring power and cure trauma potions. Trauma DOT is a heavy power drain. (There was a power drain, but it was minimal in Solo, not true it is a big one for healing classes.)
1. Kill [[A scorched factor]] around {{waypoint -336, 25, -77}}
1. Kill [[Morgelon the Wilder]] inside a tower at {{waypoint -361, 38.59, 110.02}}
   - Summons trios of [[A greater flame wilder|greater flame wilders]] throughout the fight.
   - Emotes *Morgelon the Wilder glares in your direction* (frontal?)
   - Elemental cures needed
1. Cross the flame bridge and kill ([[A conflagrant emissary]]) in the tower at {{waypoint -443, 59, 288}}
1. Kill [[Moracar, the Fiend]] inside a tower at {{waypoint -366.53, 42.52, 371.70}}.
   - Kill [[An unstable spirit globe|unstable spirit globes]] as they spawn, otherwise they despawn and respawn as much more powerful adds. Multiple [[An unstable spirit globe|unstable spirit globes]] can spawn at the same time. For classes with few AoEs, save them for the spirits or you will likely run out of time before they despawn.
1. Kill [[A seared advocate]] in the tower at {{waypoint -86, 78, 418}} (there are other mobs in the tower as well).
1. Kill [[Pyrefist, Dark Guardian]] and his minions in the tower at {{waypoint -234.98, 88.13, 404.13}}
   - Periodically casts a fear.
   - It is possible to pet pull the adds out of the tower without the named, but far easier to clear the whole area before you spawn him.
1. Use the red glowing travel portal at {{waypoint -193, 98, 436}}.
1. Kill [[Ra-Sekjet]] near {{waypoint -364, 61, -342}}. He is grouped with two adds, [[Jel-Ekset]] and [[Jek-Setek]].
   - Periodically teleports character away. He mostly teleports you to {{waypoint -431, 65, -348}}, so if you fight him there, it's no inconvenience.
   - Adds periodically will taunt you off Ra-Sekjet.
