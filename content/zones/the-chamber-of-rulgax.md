## Background

![Pictographs of the evil eyes and their minions line the walls of the central pillar in the final room.](images/The_Chamber_of_Rulgax2.png)
This place, in the deepest and darkest part of [[Runnyeye]], is considered by The Runnyeye Goblins to be the prison of [[Lord Rulgax]], the leader of the evil eye invasion of the [[Enchanted Lands]]. However no goblin, or any other being, is allowed in or out by orders of [[Overlord Baylzuthak]]. If Baylzuthak defeated Rulgax, why would he keep it under so many lock and key? One look at the central pillar in the final room will leave you asking: Are Baylzuthak and the goblins of Runnyeye the slavemasters of the evil eyes - or is it the other way around?
