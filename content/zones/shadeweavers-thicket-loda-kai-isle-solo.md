## Walkthrough

1. Slay [[Killmaster Kaden|Killmaster Kayzen]] sitting on a rock at {{waypoint 828.98, 45.79, 313.19}}.
   - Fight near the central cage containing deer. From there you can more or less see all the cages containing epic shadeweavers.
   - When you get the message "You're Kayzen's next BIG GAME TARGET" take cover behind the shadeweaver that has been let loose. You may need to Sprint.
   - If you kill all the adds on the isle, or kill the shadeweaver that is loosed, you will stall the fight and he will go invul.
   - Hide behind the Shadeweaver until he kills it then start DPSing.
   - Rinse and repeat until he is dead.
1. Slay [[Nimble Rodderick]] {{waypoint 806.32, 48.16, 445.24}}.
   - You need to click a chest and adds come in.
   - Kill one of the adds and loot the **loot hoarder's key**.
   - Open the next chest now that you have the key.
   - Then you can DPS Rodderick until the line pops again about finding the chest. You run to, and open, the next chest and start DPSing him again. Rinse and repeat until Rodderick is dead.
1. Kill [[Taja Verath]] {{waypoint 906.11, 48.88, 371.39}}.
   - Kill [[A forest tiger|forest tigers]] and their [[A Loda Kai tiger handler|handlers]] to spawn Taja.
   - DO NOT pick off the [[A tamed forest tiger|tamed tigers]] before pulling the name: you have to kill them during the fight to get rid of one of his buffs.
