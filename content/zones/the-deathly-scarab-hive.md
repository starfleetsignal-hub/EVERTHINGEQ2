## Notes

- The four named guardian scarabs will not become attackable until you walk onto the raised circular area in the central area. Once all four are killed, [[Queen Ankhestara]] will spawn.
- The lockout starts as soon as one of the named guardian scarabs is killed.

## Layout

- The hive is laid out like a cross with the entry door in the south wing. The west, north, and east wings are recessed pit areas with stairs down into them. The central area has a slightly raised circular area.
- The south entry area had 4 non-linked [[A deathly scarab (Deathly Scarab Hive)|deathly scarabs]].
- The west, north, and east wings each had 3-5 linked [[Deathly scarab worker|deathly scarab workers]].
- The central area has 4 non-attackable named scarabs spread out in the corners:
  - [[Guardian Ankhemaufut]]
  - [[Guardian Ankhentaris]]
  - [[Guardian Ankhrenlai]]
  - [[Guardian Ankhumyal]]
