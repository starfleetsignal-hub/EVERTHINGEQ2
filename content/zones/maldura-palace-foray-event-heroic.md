## Strategy

1. Get the flute from the dwarf at the entrance.
1. Go up the stairs.
1. One side are boars, the other side are wolfs. Go to the wolfs.
1. Select one, play flute. You are the wolf now. (20 seconds, hurry up!)
1. Run towards alpha wolf, [[Dreadmaw (Event Heroic)|Dreadmaw]].
1. Three bad minions are there. Attack one.
1. You will leave the wolf's mind and go back to your body.
1. The attacked minion will hunt you. Kill him.
1. Repeat with the other two minion.
1. Dreadmaw will become attackable. Kill him and get boar horn.
1. Run across to the other hall with boars, repeat the same procedure for [[Dreadtusk (Event Heroic)|Dreadtusk]]
1. After this, [[Dragoon Z'Koz]] will come in main area.
1. Go up to him, he will talk. Several waves of adds will attack you.
1. After the last wave he will disapear. He has gone down (between the circled stairs).
1. Follow him. Kill the adds (dogs), then Z'Koz.

## Reward

Gave [[Victory: Dragoon Z'Koz]]
