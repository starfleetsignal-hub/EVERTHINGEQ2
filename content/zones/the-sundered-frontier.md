The first zone adventurers will encounter in Sentinel's Fate, it is designed for adventurers level 80-85. There are over 150 new quests to do while exploring this rich and beautiful zone. Odus has a long, colorful history. The [[Erudite|Erudites]] from the City of Erudin and the City of Paineel were at odds long before the breaking. Add to that the Kejak and the mysterious Hua Mein, long thought to be extinct.

This zone contains EQ1 zones [[Paineel]], [[Kerra Isle|Kerra Island]], and [[Toxxulia Forest]].

## Geography

### Travel

#### Wizard Spire

There is a Combine Spire located on Spire Island at {{waypoint 2218, -210, 2474}}, accessible via the Paineel Teleporter at {{waypoint 1917, -301, 3318}}. Unlike the [[Kylong Plains]] and [[The Combine Spires|Greater Faydark Combine Spires]], there is a teleportation nexus at the center of this spire that connects to any of the [[Ulteran Spires]] in Norrath.

#### Druid Ring

The [[Druid Ring (SF)|Toxxulia Forest Druid Ring]] is located at {{waypoint 988, -49, 3382}}.

#### Launch Pads

A flying disc can take you from any Launch Pad to any other Launch Pad.

- [[The City of Paineel]] Launch Pad {{waypoint 2021, -190, 3135}}
- [[The Eye of Dartain]] Launch Pad {{waypoint 1935, -22, 4305}}
- [[Stonebrunt Encampment]] Launch Pad {{waypoint 956, 121, 3670}}
- [[Kerra Isle]] Launch Pad {{waypoint 1427, -5, 2421}}
- [[Toxxulia Forest]] Launch Pad {{waypoint 371, 137, 2508}}

#### Teleportation

[[The City of Paineel]] has a teleportation network in-place that allows you to access parts of the city that are difficult or impossible to get to on foot. This network is centered around one teleporter in Paineel that connects to all of the others, rightclick to select, the default is the Launchpad. Each other teleporter can only be used to go to this central teleporter.

- Central Teleporter {{waypoint 1917, -301, 3318}}
  - [[The Airship Docking Platform]] {{waypoint 1869, -225, 2975}}
  - Launch Pad {{waypoint 2021, -190, 3135}}<br>([[The City of Paineel]]'s Launch Pad)
  - [[Spire Island]] {{waypoint 2058, -239, 2715}}
  - [[The Hole]] entrance {{waypoint 1894, -375, 3375}}<br>(Teleports you to the entrance area of [[The Hole]] and its associated instances. Requires having completed the quest [[Arcana and Teleportation]]).Otherwise must fly or float to reach this area.
  - [[The Ruins of Old Paineel]] {{waypoint 1683, -111, 3448}}<br>(Requires having completed the quest [[Ruined Teleporter]])
