## Notes

- You have to be mentored down (grey mobs wont drop the chests) to kill two adds that drop two torches to open further bosses up after kill the 4 linked named boss (4th boss). Click one of the big braziers at end of room and one of the multiple braziers with the purple flames by the door to unlock the door. Each side unlocks each door. The end bosses room requires you to have the full crafted psionist helm to open the door. You can do this all solo.
- See the discussion page for details about the crafted psionist helm mentionned above.
