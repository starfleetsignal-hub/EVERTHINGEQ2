## Background

The Ascent is a training ground for those who have sworn their lives to the guardians of the temple and into the service of the Awakened. Within these walls the devoted ones train, hoping to one day have the chance to be honored by an audience with the guardians of the temple. Once one of the devoted ones passes all the trials and learns all of the sacred attacks, they are presented with a symbol of their accomplishment. When one holds all of the symbols that represent the sacred attacks, they are allowed access to the chamber of the guardians. Once there, they can request an audience. - *([[Ritnoc Flyfeather]])*
