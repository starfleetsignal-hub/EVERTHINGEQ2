## Notes

- Resolve of Tashmobs: 3740
- Trashmobs are realy trash as they have very less HP.

## Named Mobs

1. [[The Enraged War Boar]]
   - Resolve 3740 (**Obsidian Guard I**)
   - directly at the bottom of the ramp at the zone-in
   - Strat: joust and kill adds.
1. [[Berik the Bloodfist]]
   - Resolve 3865 (**Obsidian Guard II**)
   - strat: tank keep rest ranged 50% he does tank swap and curse you have to run to ranged to be cured.
1. [[Eriak the Fetid]]
   - Resolve 3970 (**Obsidian Guard III**)
   - Strat: 25 meter joust on the blast. 15m joust and right click cancel curse. kill add when you get marked.
1. [[Glokus WindHelm]]
   - Strat: have 2 or 3 tanks. 1 tank holds named, other holds both named adds. Kill blue aura music boxes that pop along with the 2 nameds. after that move to pillar sync click the shaman with all 4 class archetypes on shaman to allow you to kill him and the adds while other group kills main named.
1. [[Tagrin Maldric]]
   - Strat: Kill special adds and keep ranged
