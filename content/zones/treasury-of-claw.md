## Overview

There are three version of this instance, all of which pertain to a quest in the [[Cloud Mount Timeline]]. The different versions can be entered whether or not the player in on an associated quest. The entrances of the instances and their associated quests are as follows:

- [[In Search of a Bot]]: [[The Isle of Desolation]] {{waypoint 534.12, 307.24, -634.84}}
- [[Another Broken Brain]]: [[The Strifewind Isle (POI)|The Strifewind Isle]] {{waypoint -60.15, -7.13, -549.85}}
- [[Third Bot is the Charm]]: [[The Blackwind Isle]] {{waypoint -38.50, 112.53, -0.99}}
