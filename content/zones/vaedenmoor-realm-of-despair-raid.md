## Entrance Requirement

- [[Kunark Ascending: A Nightmare Resolved]] - to see the zone in
- To get to the  (T3 named) of the zone you must have killed [[Shanaira the Prestigious]] in [[Arcanna'se Spire: Order and Chaos (Raid)|Arcanna'se Spire: Order and Chaos [Raid]]], or the teleporter isn't active.

## **Names**

Most fights use scripts.

At max level:

Anaheed the Dreamkeeper: Mentored Max Level (MML): Don't burn - will wipe the raid. Wait till the screen changes color, then kill the add (a waking nightmare), then burn.

Hobgoblin Anguish Lord: MML: Don't burn. Wait till a raid member gets caged, destroy the cage, then burn. Don't think this can be solo'ed, since he will wipe the raid if killed before a cage is destroyed, and he won't cage a solo character, or maybe he just won't cage fighter classes.

Bridge after Hobgoblin won't show up unless all Kunark Ascending T1 raid mobs have been killed - check your achievements. Also, it may be that the Hobgoblin won't spawn unless all prior trash is killed.

Raenha, Sister of Remorse: MML: Burn

Vhaksiz the Shade: Some strategy is required as he becomes very damage resistant during the fight, but can be burned through MML if one can't be bothered to pay attention to mob buffs and scripts.

Teleporter to final name won't be active unless all T1, T2, and nearly all T3 Kunark Ascending raid mobs have been killed (including The Kly).
