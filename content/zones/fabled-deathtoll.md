## Steps

1. First room
   - Run through. No tactic. Simple burn down.
1. Second room
   - Arrow will hit you.
   - Stay at the left side wall. At the end of the room is a ramp up. The same is at the right side but this is a dead end way.
   - Kill the archers on this side.
   - Epic [[Yitzik the Hurler (Fabled)]] on a platform in the middle. {{waypoint -359.10, 139.23, -95.26}}
   - Walk from here in the 90° left direction for next room.
1. Third room
   - Hidden adds on the floor to the room: [[A carrion worm]]
   - The two groups at the entrance seems to be linked. Be carefull about it. The rest is simple burn down.
1. Fourth room
   - Hidden adds on the floor to the room: [[A carrion worm]]
   - At the entrance turn right, climable wall / way down. {{waypoint -687, 122, -263}}
   - Back on the upper level (wall at {{waypoint -665, 99, -315}} turn right. Simple burn down. The mobs would be add for next part.
   - Go to the other side. Here's the next named [[Fitzpitzle]] 103 Epic x4. He will disapear and transport himself to the first part of this room. (You know, where you came up the wall and killed the "adds").
   - Climb down at {{waypoint -632, 129, -361}} and take the stairs {{waypoint -556, 99, -370}}. In the hallway are "invisible" [[A deathless gazer]].
1. Fifth room
   - Kill all the slime blobs a.k.a [[A writhing corpuscle]] to create the named. They will move to hole before the [[The Altar of Ending]] after killing.Have also a look in the water if the big boss [[Amorphous Drake]]  will not appear. He is a 103 Epic x4.
   - Be careful: Bring him down slowly. He will split into two enemies. These can be done fast. The barriers at {{waypoint -143, 77, -381}} and {{waypoint -142, 77, -299}} will get open only if [[Amorphous Drake]] has been split!!
1. Sixth room
   - Jump down into the water. You'll have to kill some trash before the named is spawning. Just run up the spiral ramp and kill everything.
   - The named was also simple burn down for me.
1. Seventh, last, room
   - [[Tarinax the Destroyer]] is here. Maybe there is some tactic or strategy needed. For me it was a one-shot. The other, not fabled, instance guide might give you additional hints.
   - *Note: One-shot only worked for me when I hit him before he engaged. Otherwise his script triggered and he didn't die even when at zero percent health.*
