## Walkthrough

1. Psamtic the Sour {{waypoint -691.28, 141.95, -19.31}}
   - Summons adds that do a huge amount of damage, even when just 1, so burn down ASAP
   - Disarms
   - switches your target to the adds
1. Khosrow Al'Raz {{waypoint -596.77, 138.92, 282.28}}
   - Summons adds
   - Has a knockback
1. Glisterfist Prime {{waypoint -801.86, 189.44, 255.37}}
   - Teleports you around.
   - Summons adds
   - Hide in tent as much as possible.

***Don't forget your shinies!***
