## Background

[[Dolur Axebeard]], a coldain shaman stationed at [[The Great Shelf]], has asked you to return to the [[Cave of Illboding Dark]] on the [[Forbidden Ossuary of Ice]]. He believes that by confronting the head necromancer, [[Orug Deathmaker]], and shattering the dark crystal you can bring an end to the Ry'Gorr's ability to regenerate their undead.
