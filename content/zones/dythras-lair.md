## Background

One of the drakota assassins that slew [[Vox, the White Dragon|Lady Vox]] is holed up in this remote part of the [[The Feerrott|Feerrott]]. Enter here and bring low this foul beast to claim Vox's [[Essence Shard of Mind]]!

## Walkthrough

1. Clear the beach/jungle of all adds.
1. Slay [[Dythra, the Gloombringer]] and loot her body for the shard.

## Strategy

- During the fight, Thulian's will come to the aid of Dythra, running to the island in waves starting around 60% health.
- Dythra appears to be leashed to the front of the idol.
- There will be serious mana drain from the mobs.
