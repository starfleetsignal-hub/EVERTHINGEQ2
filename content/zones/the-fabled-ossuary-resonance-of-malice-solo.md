## Praetor Pheris

**Use a belt with the Stifle rune.**

Praetor Pheris walks the balcony to the east of the zone-in point and up the north or south stairs. The key to this fight, easily the hardest in the zone, is to joust away from his bone wall the moment he starts to build it. Fight him in the middle of the balcony between the two alcoves with your back to one of them. When he starts to build the bone wall, joust backwards until it explodes and then get back after him. Rinse and repeat until he's dead. **Shout out to Modok and Malven on Maj'Dul for the joust tip!!**

## Elder Senistus Verish

Located at {{waypoint 203, -24, -25}}. Pure tank and spank. He will disarm you periodically. I just push through it.

## Achiun K'dal, Peris K'dal, & Ursher K'dal

Located at {{waypoint -352.66, -0.31, -23.46}}. Wear a belt with a Stifle rune on it. Pure tank and spank. Focus all of your attention on Ursher K'dal. The other two seem to die with no attention. He has a knockup that he uses every now and then but it's easy to deal with.

## Priestess Kelania D'zil

Roams the floor around {{waypoint -447.31, -11.45, -23.66}}. Wear a  belt with a Stifle rune on it. Easy tank n spank! Clear the alcoves and fight her in one of them.

## Valdimus V'Derun (and singing friends)

Located at {{waypoint -508.61, -0.08, -23.27}}. Also a tank and spank. Fight him by the organ and focus on him ignoring the adds.
