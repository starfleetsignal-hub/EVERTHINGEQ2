## Overview

Located in the NW of [[Moors of Ykesha]] {{waypoint 720, 146, -837}}, quests are available for the zone from Firmroot Moot, which also has a **balloon ride to the Guk Drop Point near the zone entrance** (doors of Guk).  One of the harder group zones in the expansion, it contains many social mobs in large groups near others, high speed wandering and teleporting mobs  - make pulls tricky.  The main damage type is noxious, and priests will soon get tired of curing all the frequently reapplied DoTs.<br><br>[[Why Does There Have To Be Frogs?|2 shard quest of the day]] clicky was at {{waypoint 169, -63, -255}}.

## Progression

#### [[An Ancient Fungoid]]

- Clear your way down to [[An Ancient Fungoid]] {{waypoint 108, -34, -193}} and slay him. **Note:  ALL** frogloks and fungoids must be killed *before* you use the **pure fungus** in the next step, else the gate will not open.

#### [[Rideepa the Prideful]]

- Near where you killed the [[An Ancient Fungoid|Ancient Fungoid]], there is a clicky mushroom hidden in a bush at {{waypoint 96, -35, -207}}. Click the mushroom to receive **pure fungus**.
- In the center of the dark pool in the first room of the zone {{waypoint 69, -40, -112}}, right click on the **small idol** to "Pour the fungus you gathered on the idol". Doing this will open the door at {{waypoint 105, -32, -116}}.
- [[Rideepa the Prideful|Rideepa]] is located in front of a squat statue {{waypoint 173, -62, -242}} that you need to move to the top of the **peculiar pedestal** back in the [[The Chamber of Afterlife]] {{waypoint 7, -32, -96}}.
- There are two adds  to start, [[A gaz devotee]] and [[A gaz fanatic]]; during the fight, [[Rideepa the Prideful|Rideepa]] will emote, summoning help.

#### [[Froppit the Everliving]]

- Move the totem from {{waypoint 172.00, -62.17, -246.03}} back to the "pool" room and place it on the on the pedestal at {{waypoint 8, -25, -100}}. (You may have just done this after reading the prior boss info).
- Head back to the now-opened gate at {{waypoint 39, -39, -169}}.
- [[Froppit the Everliving|Froppit]] is located on a platform at the back of the [[Font of the Everliving]] {{waypoint -75.50, -47.59, -246.44}}. He has a couple of adds.
- You may wish to bypass the stairs. Coming from Froppit's chamber are a continuous stream of respawning [[A reanimated unstable ghoul|unstable ghouls]]. These don't give you any xp. Go around the curved hall the long way.
- If you kill [[A crumbling ghoul knight]] before Froppit is slain, a large AOE is released.

#### [[Molinap the Destructor]]

- Move up to the puzzle at {{waypoint -54.88, -42.99, -258.12}}.  You must click the three blocks in order to open the door behind you.  The correct order being: upper left (spiral symbol), upper right (an eye with a slitted pupil symbol), lower (mouth symbol).
- Kill [[A deadly cadavodile|deadly cadavodiles]] in the pool at the bottom of the room, then retrieve a [[Shiny Golden Trinket]] from a chest {{waypoint -22, -72, -326}}. You can right or left click it to open.  The item will be in your inventory, there is no emote or loot for it.
- Head up the path (it circles the room a couple of times as it rises) and turn right at the intersection at the top {{waypoint -10.04, -32.21, -373.19}}.
- [[Molinap the Destructor|Molinap]] is a drake, sitting atop his treasure hoard at  {{waypoint -78.56, -31.14, -388.50}}. His major attack is "Incapacitating Screech," a heat AoE that stuns.
- Use the [[Shiny Golden Trinket]] in this fight when [[Molinap the Destructor|Molinap]] emotes.

#### [[A Ghoul Usurper]]

- Move through the gate at {{waypoint 8.88, -33.29, -323.41}} and jump down the big black hole. *There's a [[Ancient cadavodile]] on the lip of the pool at the bottom, you may wish to arrow or pet pull it before you jump down. There is no apparent way to climb back up, so you are stuck in the last part of the zone unless you evac.*
- Clear the assorted ghoul frogloks. *Watch out for the roaming froglok knights, they appear to walk a little then teleport, and they move fast. It's unfortunately possible to have one land right on you out of nowhere.*
- Move three **cadaverous body of a cursed Guk froglok** to the following locations:
  - {{waypoint -24.31, -58.59, -254.90}}
  - {{waypoint 107.68, -63.20, -250.61}}
  - {{waypoint 151.99, -63.45, -258.65}}
  - The named spawns right by the final pedestal as soon as you place the body upon it.

#### [[Lord Kurpep]]

![Lord Kurpep](images/Lord_Kurpep.jpg)

- [[Lord Kurpep]] is located at {{waypoint 124, -40, -386}}.
- He periodically summons several [[A summoned shin knight squire|summoned shin knight squires]] and [[A shin knight squire|shin knight squires]], and ports a member of the group to the ledges on either side of his spawn spot, or way up the top of the spire behind him. Kurpep spawns adds on those group members that will accompany them back down.
- [[Lord Kurpep|Kurpep]] will charm and has a nasty noxious stun/power drain that he will cast on the pull and every 20% thereafter. To avoid the big AOE, use stifles/stuns.
