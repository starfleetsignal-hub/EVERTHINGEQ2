## Notes

- It is not necessary to do the access quest [[Liberating the Cavaliers]] to gain access to this zone for the the heritage quest [[War and Wardrobe]].
- You must be ungrouped to enter this zone.
- The access quest [[Liberating the Cavaliers]] gives permanant access to the zone and allows you to do the [[Swords of Destiny Timeline]] and the [[Assassin Epic Weapon Timeline]].
