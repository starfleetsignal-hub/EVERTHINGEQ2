## Notes

- It is possible to walk straight to [[Fippy Darkpaw, the 4th|Fippy]] in the back room and get his quest [[The Motley Merchant Manifest]].
- You can kill all of the [[A circle initiate|circle initiates]] and still get Fippy's quest.
- If you kill [[An Unseen Hand taskmaster]], it will cause all of the circle initiates to attack you, Fippy will run out of the zone, and [[Rollus the Sly (NPC)|Rollus the Sly]] becomes [[Rollus the Sly (Monster)|a level 12▲▲▲ heroic aggro mob]]. Once you come under attack by Rollus, [[An Unseen Hand initiate|Unseen Hand initiates]] will spawn in the back room.
  - If you have killed everything in the zone without getting Fippy's quest, zoning back in is safe as long as you do NOT talk to the Unseen Hand taskmaster.  Doing so causes her and all the circle initiates to immediately attack you.
