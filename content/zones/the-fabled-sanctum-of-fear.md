This is a higher level version of the original [[The Sanctum of Fear|Sanctum of Fear]] within [[The Fabled Temple of Cazic-Thule]]. The monsters are heroic, in the level range of 103-107.

## Named

[[Overlord Um Nix (Fabled)|Overlord Um Nix]]

One of the three golems of Fear - [[Dread (Fabled)|Dread]], [[Fright (Fabled)|Fright]], or [[Terror (Fabled)|Terror]]

## Notes

- You must be at least level 100 to enter this dungeon instance.
- Unlike the original version of this dungeon, you will not get waves of lizardmen coming coming down the ramp with each group that you kill.
- Golems do not drop [[A clay golem heart|clay golem hearts]], so are not required for the idols. You click on the idol and it will spawn ancient Thulian dreadfiends and frightfiends. Note that the dreadfiends will be epic x2, although they are simply heroics with more health.
