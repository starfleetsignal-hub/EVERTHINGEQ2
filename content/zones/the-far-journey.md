## Background

**The Far Journey** is a zone that serves as a tutorial for navigating the world of [[EverQuest II]]. Newly created players who select [[Queen's Colony]] or [[Outpost of the Overlord]] as their starting areas will spawn in this zone. After completing the tutorial mission and arriving at one of the above zones, this instance will never again be accessible.

It was removed from the game for a long time, but became available again at some point in 2016 when Queen's Colony and Outpost of the Overlord were likewise readded to the game.

## Notes

- To leave The Far Journey, you must complete the quest [[Tasks aboard the Far Journey]], then talk to [[Captain Varlos (The Far Journey)|Captain Varlos]].
