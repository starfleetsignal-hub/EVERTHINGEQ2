**Remnant Ferahhal [128]**

Once all raid members are afflicted with the removable Sapped Motivation debuff (the figure in pink flames on a yellow background), everyone should move 26 meters away from the name. The closer you stand to the name, the greater your chance of getting a one-shot.

Challenge mode - Before the fight, you need to kill all trash, as it applies stacks of invulnerability to the name.

**Ssyre, the Fading Fire [128]**

Named is located in the center, under the pyramid.

During the fight, golems will spawn in the room, heating it up. At the same time, a stacking Heat Level buff will appear near the player's name. If the buff reaches 10 stacks, it's a wipe.

To remove the buff, flood the room and then drain the water.

When the buff reaches 3-4 stacks, four people should exit through the tunnels and stand on the platforms. Once all four platforms are occupied, the water will begin to rise. Players can leave their positions and return (they can also remain near the platforms for the entire fight). To drain the water, pull the levers inside the room; they're located on the walls (the platforms must be clear). Everything is done quickly—quickly flood, quickly drain.

Challenge mode - you need to run out and stand on platforms when the adds become huge and large fireballs appear in front of them (the third stage of add growth).

**Zeltheen of the Fang [128] and R'thessil of the Fang [128]**

To reach the names, you need to knock the green orbs out of the shadow guards guarding the bridge. For the bridge to the other side to appear, you need to kill 20 orbs. You can use the /target shadow of former inhabit macro to select targets. The names are kept at a distance from each other.

During the fight, Zeltheen of the Fang places a removable Set to Rot dot on the raid, which deals damage and drains mana from everyone standing closer than 28 meters.

During the fight, R'thessil of the Fang places a curse called Silent Contagion. This curse must be removed.

Challenge mode - Spread the names as far apart as possible (towards the opposite walls on the right and left, where Trash was standing). During the fight, a curse is cast, and only one party member can be removed (the "initially infected" part is unclear, but there must be some way to determine who it is). Improperly removing the curse will result in a wipe.

**The Timeless Golem [128]**

Casts a knockback attack during the fight, so it's best to stand against the statue.

**Kua, Watcher of Wanes [128]**

During the fight, a valve will appear in the center of the room, allowing you to move a platform. Using the platform, you can press three buttons. Each button pressed increases both incoming and outgoing damage, meaning you'll hit the name harder, but the name will hit you harder in return.

Return to the central hall and open the stairs leading down using the pedestal.

As you descend, activate the pedestals on the podiums and then, using the clues, select the correct values ​​for the statues in the rooms.

**Reactivated Blood of Ssraeshza [128]**

During combat, applies buffs to party members depending on their class (priests, fighters, mages, and scouts).

To remove the debuff, players must enter the appropriate room and use the button located in the far corner.

Priests - Mark of the Hammer II (hammer on a light brown background) - remove the dot in the hammer room.

Tanks - Mark of the Sword II - remove the dot in the sword room.

Mages - Mark of Staff II - remove the dot in the staff room.

Scouts - Mark of the Symbol II - remove the dot in the symbol room.

If you don't have time to remove your debuff, you'll be transported to a random room, from which you simply need to run out.

After killing the name, use the portal in the center to get to the final name.

**Vyzh'dra the Unleashed [128]**

A tank is essential for killing the name.

During the fight, the name will gain a stacking Strength of Shadows buff. If the stacks are not removed, the raid will be one-shot when the name reaches 20% HP.

To remove the stacks, complete the following event:

During the fight, after seeing the Yellow text *"Raid member sees something sparkle within Vyzh'dra's open satchel... They may be able to grab it!"* in chat, the specified players must pick up the Shadow-Stitch Needle from the name (right-click on the name near the waist and select the last option). Important: if you do not have the Shadow Prospecting ability, you will not be able to use the needles.

The obtained needles should be placed in a triangle (so that none of the three needles are in line with any other) opposite the statues with golems (the bottom row, which appear to be holding something in their hands). If the triangle is placed correctly, a dark cloud with a symbol will appear in the center.

The tank holding the name will be afflicted with a permanent False Target debuff (reticule icon). During this time, the tank identifies themselves, making this debuff available for cure. After successfully removing the debuff, a green message about this significant event appears in the chat. After this, you can place triangles.

When the first triangle is placed, the central column will become active. Click on it, and beams will appear, dividing the room into sectors. Stepping into the beam = death.

At the same time, adds will appear in the room. After killing one of them, the column will become active again; click on it to remove the beams. After this, you need to kill all the remaining adds and then kill the name.

During the fight, an Incendiary Spark Hoarder appears. It explodes if it gets close to the name or any raid member.

Challenge Mode- Maniacal Casting, a permanent DoT: The player affected by this DoT applies a debuff to their allies when casting beneficial spells.

During the fight, Spark adds (well, that's the name, not the appearance) appear. The person who kills the Sparks must not be under a permanent curse.

During the fight, the Name will have a stacking Strength of Shadows buff. If the stacks aren't removed, the raid will be one-shot when the Name reaches 20% HP.

When the hall is divided into sectors, the stacks on the Name are removed, so if there are many stacks, it's best to wait until they wear off before using the pillar and removing the sectors.

To kill the Name, you need to complete the following event four times:

1. The tank holding the name will be afflicted with an irreversible False Target curse (sight icon). During this time, the tank will highlight themselves, making this curse available for curing. Once successfully removed, a green message about this significant event appears in the chat.

2. During the fight, after the Yellow text *"Raid member sees something sparkle within Vyzh'dra's open satchel... They may be able to grab it!"* appears in the chat, the indicated players must pick up a Shadow-Stitch Needle from the named player (right-click on their name near their waist and select the last option). Important: If you don't have the Shadow Prospecting ability, you won't be able to use the needles.

Place the obtained needles in a triangle (so that none of the three needles are in line with any other) opposite the golem statues (the bottom row, which appear to be holding something). If the triangle is placed correctly, a dark cloud with a symbol will appear in the center.

Important: If any of the group/raid members still have needles in their inventory before the next circle, the name will not give them out again!

3. Triangles are placed around the room to determine the location of all four symbols (sword, staff, symbol and hammer).

At least one player must be at the location of each symbol.

4. After completing these steps, you can activate the column to create sectors.

Each sector where the symbols were will have an add appear after a short delay (about five seconds). While the adds are being killed, the name remains locked in place.

After killing the adds, players can teleport to each other.

Once all four add characters are killed, the column will reactivate, causing the sectors to disappear.

Important: Don't move until the name runs toward the tank. Otherwise, the phantom beams from the sectors will kill you.

Before each circle, you can remove up to 20% of the name (no more, otherwise wipe).

When the name has less than 20% HP, you can zerg.
