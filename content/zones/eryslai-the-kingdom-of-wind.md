## Geography

From the Great Library go to 710, 412, -368 to access the portal.

**Eryslai** consist of four main islands and a travel hub:

- [[The Aerie]]
- [[The Bixel Hive]]
- [[Precipice of Wind]]
- [[The Windglades]] - The portal to/from [[Myrist, the Great Library|Myrist]] is at {{waypoint 498.66, -201.67, -1425.77}}.
- [[Crosswind Isle]] - Travel hub to reach the other islands.

### Travel

- The zone exit to [[Myrist, the Great Library|Myrist]] portal on [[The Windglades]] is at {{waypoint 498.66, -201.67, -1425.77}}.
- [[The Aerie]] portal to [[Crosswind Isle]] is at {{waypoint 814.89, 357.86, 76.89}}.
- [[The Bixel Hive]] portal to [[Crosswind Isle]] is at {{waypoint -518.15, 356.53, -430.37}}.
- [[Precipice of Wind]] portal to [[Crosswind Isle]] is at {{waypoint -431.14, -480.61, 943.32}}.
- [[The Windglades]] portal to [[Crosswind Isle]] is at {{waypoint 145.06, -178.64, -827.39}}.

[[Crosswind Isle]] has portals to the four main islands:

- [[Crosswind Isle]] portal to [[The Aerie]] is at {{waypoint 388.78, 180.81, -250.14}}.
- [[Crosswind Isle]] portal to [[The Bixel Hive]] is at {{waypoint 316.26, 180.81, -250.71}}.
