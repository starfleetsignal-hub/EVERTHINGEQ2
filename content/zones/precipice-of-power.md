## Walkthrough

Finally, some light can be shed on this topic.

First, there are cruxes required to spawn the Avatars. Below is a chart for all the Avatars in the Precipice. The cruxes can be obtained in pairs from final bosses in Altar of Malice and Rum Cellar Campaign raid zones, or a single from three of the Precipice bosses.

| Isle | Location | Avatar | Cruxes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Perception | {{waypoint 129.30, 0.14, -168.97}} | [[Avatar of The Tribunal]] | [[Otherworldly Crux of Justice\|Justice]], [[Otherworldly Crux of Infliction\|Infliction]], [[Otherworldly Crux of Truth\|Truth]] |
| Fulmination | {{waypoint 61.38, -0.02, 66.46}} | [[The Avatar of Karana]] | [[Otherworldly Crux of Fulgimation\|Fulgimation]], [[Otherworldly Crux of Fulmination\|Fulmination]], [[Otherworldly Crux of Tempests\|Tempests]] |
| Consternation | {{waypoint -144.00, 0.01, -93.10}} | [[The Avatar of Cazic Thule]] | [[Otherworldly Crux of Dread\|Dread]], [[Otherworldly Crux of Horror\|Horror]], [[Otherworldly Crux of Despair\|Despair]] |
| Conflagration | {{waypoint -102.84, 2.28, 138.63}} | [[The Avatar of Fennin Ro]] | [[Unquestioning Crux of the Tyrant\|Unquestioning]], [[Squalling Crux of the Tyrant\|Squalling]], [[Frightening Crux of the Tyrant\|Frightening]] |
| Underfoot | {{waypoint -71.48, -3.76, -244.78}} | [[The Avatar of Brell]] | none |

Many say Fennin Ro can't be soloed. This may be true if you're under powered and end up in some type of scripted battle, but I smacked him with my biggest gun straight out of the gate and one shot him.

Here is a list for the creation of other cruxes. You can sacrifice two cruxes to create a new one. Just examine a crux to pull this same info up. Note that the cruxes Unquestioning, Squalling, and Frightening can not be created from other cruxes - the three Fennin cruxes can only be obtained from the other Avatars.

- Fulmination + Truth     = Despair
- Despair + Truth             = Fulmination
- Fulmination + Despair = Truth
- Tempests + Infliction   = Dread
- Dread + Infliction          = Tempests
- Tempests + Dread        = Infliction
- Horror + Fulgimation   = Justice
- Justice + Fulgimation = Horror
- Justice + Horror            = Fulgimation

**Edit: 11/12/2020:** So after months of farming these Cruxes, I'm hoping I can add a little help here.

You should  run the raid zones in AoM. Most of them drop cruxes, but every other raid. If you do a raid and you get a crux, then the next one you won't, then the next you will, and so on. **Edit 2025:** Don't think this is still the case.

Fulmination and Fulgimation seem to be the most common and can drop in any zone it seems. Dread only drops in F.S. Distillery. Most others can drop in two or three zones. **Edit 2025:** Don't think this is still the case.

Infliction and Horror drop from the Construct of Malice in Altar of Malice, but it's impossible to solo and not necessary due to combining.

Cruxes are lore and no-trade; they can't be stockpiled.

Cruxes may be found in the following zones:

- Brokenskull Bay: Fulmination & Truth
- Zavith'loa: none
- Castle Highhold: none
- Ssraeshza Temple: Fulgimation & Justice
- Ossuary: Tempests & Despair
- F.S. Distillery: Dread & Justice
- Altar of Malice: Infliction & Horror

**Edit 12/02/2022:** Crux drops no longer seem to be randomized; list updated to reflect acquisition.

So now that you know the cruxes required for the Avatars, let's start on what to do in the Precipice. The Dread Armoloch of Blight and Dread Armoloch of Corruption (x2 mobs) hold the several keys needed to enter the other isles. Respawn time is  very long: 30 mins to one hour or even more. These keys are needed to get to the isles where you can then spawn the Avatars. With the correct keys, click on an altar on the main island to travel to an avatar's isle. Each of these isles has an altar in the middle. Insert the correct cruxes into the altar to spawn the Avatar. Brell Serilis is already spawned in when you arrive.

**Edit: 11/12/2020:** The key mobs have taken up to two hours to respawn, so be prepared for some very long wait times. If you spend a day just farming these mobs, you will end up with all the keys. They're unlimited use keys, or so I think; if not, then I have a wicked amount of the same ones and just never realized it, but I doubt that's the case. Bronze and silver keys drop, I have yet to figure out the difference between them (**Edit 2025:** Both bronze and silver of each type are needed to unlock a travel altar). The keys don't show up in your inventory or anywhere else. You'll see a notification in chat that says you have them, that's all.

**Edit 4/28/2021:** The key mobs have taken up to 12 hours to respawn. The good news is that Brell does not require Underfoot keys, but he is contested; so he may not be up on the Isle of Underfoot when you check.
