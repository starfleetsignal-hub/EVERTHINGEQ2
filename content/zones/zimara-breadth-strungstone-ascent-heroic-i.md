## Walkthrough (copy from Solo)

1. Kill [[Rubblethrong]] {{waypoint 634, 206, -518}} to make the teleporter activate. (Summons adds)
1. Use the crystal shards at {{waypoint 647, 207, -461}} to teleport to Claved Fate Islets
   - The crystal shards deposit you at {{waypoint 709, 176, 268}}
1. Kill [[Bolagehera]] roams around {{waypoint 751, 186, 343}}
1. Kill [[Barlanka]] roams around {{waypoint 536, 282, 542}}
   - *Attacking a Trial Guardian will call all the MOBs in the immediate area and [[Barlanka]] to attack*
1. Use the crystal shards at {{waypoint 342, 402, 599}} to 'Traverse the expanse' to Sprungstone Peaks
   1. The shards deposit you at {{waypoint 217, 379, 651}}
1. Kill [[Crisj'Jen the Bold]] {{waypoint 88, 383, 727}}
   - This mob pulls you forward.
     - Possible Strat -- move around constantly
1. Kill [[Fuejenyrus]] {{waypoint -247, 434, 865}}
   - Does a knockback and teleports you. Make sure to stand against one of the pillars.
