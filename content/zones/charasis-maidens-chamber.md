## Background

This is the tomb of [[Drusella Sathir (Heroic)|Drusella Sathir]], former mate of [[Venril Sathir|Emperor Venril Sathir]]. When she died suddenly during his reign, Venril cast all citizens from Charasis and made the royal chamber into his wife's sacred crypt. Only the royal iskar guard and servants were allowed to remain. They were to serve their empress eternally - never again would they know the warmth of the sun nor bask in the moonlight. The haunting howls of the damned were to be heard echoing off the [[Howling Stones|stones of the canyon]] for eternity.

Venril would visit the cliff city frequently, but he had crafted a spell to hide the entrance of Charasis from raiders and those who dared to steal his knowledge or defile his beloved Drusella' grand crypt. What he would do inside is not known. What *is* known, however, is that he became increasingly possessed by the necromantic arts during this time, seeking within it a way to restore his mate's life. Learning of this dark corruption, Venril's eldest son, [[Dominus Rile Sathir]], went to confront him over the heresy, and interrupted one such dark ritual being performed by the Emperor, killing him.

Rile destroyed Venril's body, burying all evidence in the tomb of Charasis. And thus Rile succeeded his father through the same violent act which his father had performed to attain his first seat of power. *(Source: [[Elna Tsernin (Fens of Nathsar)|Elna Tsernin]])*

## Notes

- There is an [[Agnostic Dungeons|agnostic]] version of this dungeon available to players level 10 through 95.
- If you're doing this in level 80 gear with a group, you might want to take note of the location, near Bi'titu that has a climbing wall so you don't take damage and end up immediately in combat with the linked mob of devourers at the bottom.  When you see the three sisters spawn, burning them down in alphabetical order works, or you can burn the two from the side down together and handle the one from the center last, either way don't try to kill the lady whose name begins with X until last unless you're really OP for the zone. Drusella casts a bone shield which reflects damage.  If you're doing it on level, you will want to be sure to keep all mercs, pets and yourself fully passive while she is cocooned in bones. Joust the AoE,s he is rooted while in that state.
- If you're trying to find the **fastest path to Drusella** for the KA Signature quest, in the agnostic version, Kill Gear and then go directly to the three sisters and then Drusella.  Beware that in the agnostic version it's possible to have other people in the zone with you and not be aware of it, agnostic is basically a "contested" zone.  Use /who as you go.  If someone beats you to Drusella, just zone out and re-enter, it has no lockout or persistence time.

## Walkthrough

1. Jump through the painting to fall down. Follow the corridor out of the room. (Click the pillar to bounce back up to zone-in).
1. Kill [[Bi'lithu of the Old Ways]] in the center room, then fall down hidden trap in the floor.
1. Use the lever to unlock Sandstorm's room.
1. Go to the north east room and kill [[Sandstorm]] {{waypoint -83, -17, 97}}. Use the pillar next to his spawn to unlock Impaler Tzilug's prison.
1. Go to the room just south of here and kill [[Gear of the Ancients]] {{waypoint -90, -17, 180}}. Use the lever to raise the floor in the south east corridor, making it traversable.
1. Follow the corridor all the way to the raised prison door (it’s a long run) to the north west room and kill [[Impaler Tzilug]] {{waypoint 150, -17, 69}}.
1. Backtrack and enter the southern room.
1. Click the Sathirian scroll {{waypoint 14, -20, 275}}. Three named will spawn. Kill Thera'ta first. She has a buff that heals the other two sisters when they are about to die.
1. Walk up the stairs behind the scroll and continue south, use the portal to teleport to [[Drusella Sathir|Drusella Sathir's]] room. Kill her.
