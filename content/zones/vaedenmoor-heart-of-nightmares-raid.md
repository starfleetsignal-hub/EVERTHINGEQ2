## Entrance Requirement

- [[Kunark Ascending: A Nightmare Resolved]] - to see the zone in.
- Each character in the raid must have killed [[Setri Lureth]] in [[Vaedenmoor, Realm of Despair]] to enter the zone.
- Must dismiss mercs to get in, otherwise it will give you a message saying you aren't flagged even if you have completed the above two options.

## Names

Mentored Max Level (MML) solo: First two names, Warden of Nightmares and Territus the Deathbringer, burn. Third, Baliath, burn and every 10% kill the adds. Adds may one-shot you; don't let them get close. Note: Expect your weapons to be damaged and broken every fight - bring a repair bot or equip your epic 2.0. Next: Gatekeepers: There are two that must be fought at the same time inside of two different portals; therefore, cannot be solo'ed. Final two names can be solo'ed: I recommend starting with Saryn, as her strategy is more difficult to learn. Saryrn cannot be quick burned (both seem to heal to max if killed too quickly). Tap her very gently, let an add spawn on the platform; kill it, click the monument that appears to get a buff, dodge her walls, repeat. Burn her quickly and she throws walls up so fast that they are about impossible to avoid. Terris, just slow burn to prevent her healing to max at death. The color of the detriment she tosses on you indicates what color platform one should stand on; seems to be optional when MML. Waking Nightmares appear near the platforms in the final phase; since Terris is being slow burned, kill them; dunno what happens if they are left up.

Final names for a well geared group for RoS and beyond, if anyone knows the full raid strategies please input them here.

I recommend starting the fight with Terris-Thule (Nightmares)

Saryrn: The "wall lady" summons a wall while emoting "Let's limit the playing field, shall we?" you must joust 20 meters away from her. burn her down and work that script.

Terris-Thule: Mostly just a burn, as far as I have seen there is no extra scripts

Both: This is the part where things are NOT fully known, anything can change. Fact: Target ONLY Terris, and burn her down, Saryrn will not trigger her wall script if she is not being attacked. Kill the adds that spawn on the platforms to the left and right as they spawn. A no cast trigger will occur on individual people, the best way to avoid this is having said person target themselves.
