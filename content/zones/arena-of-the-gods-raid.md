## Notes

- This zone allows guilds to fight instanced versions of the 9 contested avatars.
- These versions drop a completely different, somewhat lower stat loot table.
- Only the first avatar killed per instance will drop loot, but others may be summoned and fought for practice.
- After the first (loot-giving) kill, there is a small chance that Bristlebane will spawn, offering a unique encounter with it's own higher-grade loot.
