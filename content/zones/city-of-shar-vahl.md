Shar Vahl is a city zone on the dark side of [[Luclin]].  It is the ancestral home of the [[Vah Shir]] player race.  The inhabitants are friendly to outsiders who have earned access.

### Travel

Shar Vahl is found at the south end of the outdoor zone called [[Savage Weald]].

To be allowed in, player characters must be level 120 in either adventuring or crafting, and either:

- complete the quest [[Reign of Shadows: Spirited Attacks]] as part of the [[Reign of Shadows Timeline]], or
- complete the tradeskill quest [[Tiptoe Through the Shadows]] as part of the [[Reign of Shadows Crafting Timeline]].
