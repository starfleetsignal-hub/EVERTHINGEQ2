## Walkthrough

1. Clear thought horror mobs ([[A deepshade lurker|deepshade lurkers]], [[A deepshade deathwarden|deepshade deathwardens]], [[A deepshade tormentor|deepshade tormentors]], etc.) until [[Undertaker Zemilox]] {{waypoint 133.18, 110.00, 9.12}} spawns. Kill him.
1. Proceed past the quarry into the [[Limestone Forest]] {{waypoint 459.89, 137.95, 57.90}}. Kill all the groups with [[A needlite bloodroyal|needlite bloodroyals]] to spawn [[The Needlite Queen]] {{waypoint 694, 180, 283}}. Kill her.
1. Move into [[The Chlorochamber]] and kill all the tegi boglings to spawn the next name. Be sure to kill the [[A bogling of Broglyn|bogling of Broglyn]] that pops after each group of three dies, or they will heal Broglyn during the fight
1. Defeat [[Chief Broglyn]] around {{waypoint -41.97, 173.86, 378.68}}) roaming on the left bank of the river.
   - During the fight, [[A corrupted bogling|corrupted boglings]] spawn and add.
   - When the emote *Interrupt his tantrum! HURRY!* appears, kill adds.
1. Go to [[Tuatara Rim]] {{waypoint -170.95, 217.75, 133.01}} and kill [[A tuatara cliffcoasterS|tuatara cliffcoasters]] and [[A tuatara preyculler|tuatara preycullers]], then slay [[Killmodo]] {{waypoint -296, 221, 49}}.
1. Go to [[The Colossal Cascades]] and defeat [[Scyphodon]] at {{waypoint -393, 176, 325}}.
   - Before the fight collect a stalk of **colossal cane**. Sometimes you cannot harvest it before the fight begins. Use the cane to be able to target [[Scyphodon]].
   - [[Scyphodon]] warns you: "Those with a bit of sugar in their veins may soon be devoured!": During the fight [[Scyphodon]] casts "Colossal Maw".
     - Before that cast ends, use a macro with **/cancel_maintained Magic Sugar** or "Scyphodon's Colossal Maw devours you whole!"...you die. (You can also drag the Magic sugar icon to a free hotkey, which works like an on/off switch)
     - Then collect and use **colossal cane**...and then [[Scyphodon]] will cast "Colossal Maw" again...repeat that until you defeat the named.
   - Note: In the water you will get more damage.
1. Exit zone at {{waypoint -425, 184, 315}}
