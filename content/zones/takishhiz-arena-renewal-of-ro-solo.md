- No shiny after defeat of the named.

## Walkthrough

1. Watch the "event" after that named will spawn.
1. Defeat [[Malacheil Caedor]]
   - During fight (at 90%, 70%, 50%, 30%, and 10%), a **Temporal Knowledge Shard** will appear.
   - Cure detriments quickly.
   - Kill adds quickly: using Heroic Opportunities will get rid of all adds currently up.
   - There is a knock up and away
   - Purple text will appear in the form of a question.  Choose the answer corresponding to the text below.
     - "After the return of..." --> "The Seed of Growth." (sw position)...
     - "Firiona Vie realized Tunare's chosen land..." --> "Turning the Elddar Forest into desert."
     - "A score of events..." --> "Providing the Coalescence....on Luclin."
     - "For defying ancient draconian law Lady Vox and Lord Nagafen... what faction?" --> "The Ring of Scale."
     - "Firiona Vie has been known by several names... What was her most recent title, just prior to her ascension?" --> "Chosen of Growth."
