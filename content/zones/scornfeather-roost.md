## Background

Harpies make their nest here and prey on the Rujarkian Orcs whom dwell in [[The Clefts of Rujark]] below. Somehow you've managed to make your way through the maze-like Clefts to this upper plateau. If treasure and exploration are your goals then sharpen your blade and begin hacking away at the harpy witches.
