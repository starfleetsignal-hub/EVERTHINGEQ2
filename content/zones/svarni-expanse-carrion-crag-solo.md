## Walkthrough

1. [[High Shikari Olyxa]] {{waypoint -487, 136, -740}}
   - When Olyxa ports, run into white circles to bring back.
1. [[Skulkor]] {{waypoint -443, 160, -570}}
   - Skulkor has stacks. When skellies pop off, the stacks decrease. If the stacks go to zero, you die.
   - click the skulls left by destroyed skeletal pygmies to increase the stacks, before they reach zero
1. [[Corpsetalon]] {{waypoint -570, 192, -677}}
   - 90% damage reduction while in air.
   - Run into the dirt circles to spawn a worm, which will grab [[Corpsetalon]] and bring her down, removing the damage reduction.
   - Warning: There is a kickback when worm spawns.
   - Warning: there is also a kickback when Corpsetalon takes flight.
1. [[Gangrerious]] and [[Necrodaverus]] roams area {{waypoint -466, 188, -543}}
   - Tank and spank.
   - When [[Gangrerious]] emotes "Fooooood." check buffs.
1. [[Grumblugtin]] {{waypoint -355, 193, -712}}
   - Take out adds to reduce stacks.
   - [[Grumblugtin]] calls more adds during the fight.
