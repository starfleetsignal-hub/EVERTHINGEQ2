Originally, **Outpost of the Overlord** was the starting zone for all [[Freeport]] characters. It became optional with the release of [[Echoes of Faydwer]], and was eventually removed from the game around the time [[Sentinel's Fate]] was released. However, it was restored with the launch of the Stormhold Time-Locked Server in 2015 as [[Isle of Refuge]]. Starting with [[LU19]], [[Isle of Refuge]] was split into Outpost of the Overlord for characters of Evil-alignment and [[Queen's Colony]] for characters of Good-alignment.

Outpost of the Overlord is accessed via any of the [[Globe of Swift Travel|Globes of Swift Travel]] within [[The City of Freeport]] or [[The Commonlands]].

## Background

As an aspiring citizen of [[The City of Freeport]], [[Overlord Lucan D'Lere]] demands that you assist [[Tayil N'Velex (Outpost of the Overlord)|Tayil N'Velex]] and The Freeport Militia in taking full control of the island. This is no simple undertaking as there are many wild beasts wandering the area and the [[Qeynos|Qeynosian]] elves of The Tunarian Alliance have a major presence on the island. You must quickly learn [[Your First Adventure|Norrathian survival skills]] if you are to survive the challenges found here and become a citizen of Freeport.

## Geography

The island is laid out rather like a clock, with [[Sandstalker Bay]] in the middle. You start in the south, at the actual outpost itself, and as you move clockwise the monsters gradually get stronger. The island is small enough that there is no fast travel or instant travel.
