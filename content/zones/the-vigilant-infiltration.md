## Key mob

Tallonite Key-Zonn: Access him from {{waypoint -35, 68, -92}} he is hiding in the ceiling as you head down the pathway.
The sneaky lil bugger is non-aggro, and hiding up in the scrollwork above the pathway that leads down to a chute you can't jump back up out of. If you stand at the edge of that narrow ramp down and pan your camera out away from the ship and look up, you can see him in the crevice at the top of the hook shape. You can climb part way up the hook shape hole in the wall and shoot him, maybe AOE him from below.![](images/Hook_shape_hole_in_wall.png)

## Walkthrough

Press the globe at {{waypoint 4, 80, 40}}then go back and clear the mobs past the zone-in area to spawn Znag the Obrut (93 ^^^) and a separate group of a LOT of ^^ adds. It's a straight fight, just annoying with so many adds.

When cleared, press the globe at {{waypoint 22, 80, -85}} and head down the hidden ramp behind the blue portal. Don't forget to get the key mob.

After speaking to the NPC and freeing the slain Erudite for various quests, work your way to kill King Cruszhem (93 ^^^) wandering upstairs. Be wary of his AOE knockbacks if you haven't cleared the room yet. Then take the teleporter (only one works) to Cargo Bay.

Arch-Amahn Wretchedcorpse (94 ^^^) will summon ^^ mobs during the fight. Just burn him down first and then the adds later.

Press the globe at {{waypoint 0, 0, 30}} to open a door up the ramp.  Head up, step on the teleporter, then take the trampoline sigil to the next level. Take the sigil portal to the room with the big balls flying overhead. Fall down the hole in the floor, then take the sigil portal at the end of the tunnel. Finally, head to the end and press the globe.

Double back to the now-opened door to kill Kage-Zonn Radas (94 ^^^ with 2 ^^^ adds). Grab the chest and press the globe.

Double back again to the Officer's Quarters and update several quests with the various NPCs, items on the walls, and book on the stool, then press the globe to kill Vigoth Rodnan (95 ^^^).
