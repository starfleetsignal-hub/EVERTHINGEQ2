## Notes

- Various portions of Awuidor are under water. To avoid drowning, use a waterbreathing spell, the [[Fishbone Earring]], [[Totem of the Otter]], a tinkered [[Rebreather]], etc.

## Bosses

1. First boss [[Champion of E'ci]] is located at {{waypoint -1.52, 668.39, -54.73}} and is spawned (with four ads) in two steps:
   1. Killing the wandering [[A veiled sentinal]] on the plattform.
   1. This starts ring event. Beware of the water sprouts, keep moving!
   - Casts sprouts, too. Beware of them!
1. Second boss [[Champion of Marr]] is located at {{waypoint -0.56, 668.39, 159.50}} and is spawned in two steps again:
   1. Killing the wandering [[A veiled sentinal]] on the plattform.
   1. This starts ring event. Beware of the water sprouts, keep moving!
   - Casts sprouts, too. Beware of them!
1. Third boss [[Champion of the Veiled One]] is located at {{waypoint -1.30, 668.39, 206.08}} spawns after killing boss 2.
   - Hits REALLY hard, summons a "Barrier of Mist".
