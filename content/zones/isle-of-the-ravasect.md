## The Mysterious Cube

Mobs in Bonemire, [[The Halls of Fate]] and the [[The Laboratory of Lord Vyemm]] have a tiny chance to drop six different Legendary Lore items. These items can be used in the Mysterious Cube near the cloud platform {{waypoint 941, -107, 408}} to spawn 70^^^ Epicx4 nameds when the corresponding [[A monolithic phylactery|monolithic phylactery]] is up. Each epic drops a specific "Essence of Ghazi"; use all six of the essences in the Cube to spawn [[Shade of Ghazi]] (72^^^ Epicx4) out of the dragon bones around the Cube.

You can use the six items in any order and at any time, even months apart (provided the correct monolith has spawned), but you **must** have all six Essences to spawn the Shade of Ghazi.

| Item | Monolith | Mob | Essence<br>dropped |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Adamantine Heart]] | West | [[Bearer of the Crux]] | [[Essence of Ghazi's Crux\|Crux]] |
| [[Ebony Draconic Wing]] | Northeast | [[Bearer of the Carpus]] | [[Essence of Ghazi's Carpus\|Carpus]] |
| [[Moonstone Talon]] | Southeast | [[The Barbed Duke]] | [[Essence of Ghazi's Claw\|Claw]] |
| [[Sanguinite Orb]] | North | [[Bearer of Gore]] | [[Essence of Ghazi's Gore\|Gore]] |
| [[Serpentil Ring]] | South | [[The Ravaging Maw]] | [[Essence of Ghazi's Maw\|Maw]] |
| [[Umbral Circlet]] | Southwest | [[The Umbral Keeper]] | [[Essence of Ghazi's Spirit\|Spirit]] |

NOTE: In TLE, this appears only to work in Bonemire 1, not any additional instances. The Phylacteries and the mysterious cube will spawn in additional instances, but will not summon the mobs.
