## Walkthrough

1. Clear invaders in front of [[Kerrath Palace]] to spawn [[Obscura Zun'Xakra]] {{waypoint -48, 95, 344}}. Kill him.
   - He will charm the palace guards at the door, turning them aggro. DO NOT pick off the guards before pulling the name: you have to kill them during the fight to get rid of one of his buffs.
1. Kill [[Do'om Calamitus]] on [[Ward Singer Span]] {{waypoint 194.38, 77.62, 223.63}}.
   - DO NOT kill the Zov Dyn Dabo mobs on the blue triangles before you pull the name: you have to kill them during the fight to get rid of one of his buffs.
   - Leave at least one of the Zov Dyn Dabo up, or else the name will kill you instantly.
1. Use the portal at {{waypoint 183.82, 74.58, 110.99}} to enter the palace, then head in and left.
1. [[An'Kaas Fer'Zethon]] ports around inside the room at {{waypoint -171.93, -197.77, 577.15}} in the [[Dar Khura Wing]].
   - He has 236 billion in health, continues porting during the fight (taking you with him).
   - When pulled, he gets one increment of the buff "Stance of Xaui'Fer", which among other things increases his Potency by 10,000.0%.
   - When he emotes, **"My summoned inferno will destroy you all!"** expect an insta-kill explosion that leaves you a charred corpse if you don't get out of the room ASAP.  Better yet, pull to just outside the doorway and fight in the hallway to avoid the insta-kill. When you get ported into the room, run back out the door to the hallway.
1. Slay [[Venomess Tshakez]] {{waypoint 92.17, -198.19, 571.55}}.
   - *During the fight, she spawns [[Shed of Tshakez|Sheds of Tshakez]] adds. Seems to have some kind of aoe at close range.*
1. Slay [[Enforcer Yisith]] {{waypoint -48.39, -179.79, 568.91}}.
   1. Wait through the villain monologue, then kill the 8 [[Eom'Centi Xin'Xakra]] shades.
   1. Yisith will turn aggro and you can attack.
   1. Kill the waves of [[A Shissar invader|Shissar invaders]] and [[A Zun Va'Liako|Zun Va'Liako]] akheva. Yisith gives you a limited time in which to kill the adds each wave.
