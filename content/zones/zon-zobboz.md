The home of the grotesque "R&D labs" for the various Cthurath evil eye factions (and their minions) to conduct unnatural experiments involving living subjects

## Geography

Similar to [[Gerion, Hold of D'Lere]], Zon Zobboz is a small 'hub area' to access its dungeon instances and related quest NPCs
