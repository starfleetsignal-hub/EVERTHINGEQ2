This is a higher level version of the [[Vaults of El'Arad]] which was originally introduced with Kingdom of Sky expansion.

## Notes

- You must be at least level 95 to enter this dungeon.
- [[Korgo the Vault Keeper (Fabled)|Korgo the Vault Keeper]] will drop a level 95 Fabled class helm. This can be upgraded with a [[Sparkling Fabled Gemstone]] which drops from [[Doom Lord Septimus (Fabled)|Doom Lord Septimus]] in [[The Fabled Halls of Fate]].
