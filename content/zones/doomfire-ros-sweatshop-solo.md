## Notes

- speak to [[Chandrima]] near Highland Outpost in Butcherblock Mountains at {{waypoint 266, 188, 435}} to get initial quest that unlocks the portal each year.
- **As soon as you zone in, there are two quest givers on the right.  Take their quests.**
- The lava in this zone will instantly kill you.
- There are numerous looms in the zone.  Only the one at the final boss is clickable, but <b>do not click it before the final boss is dead or you WILL die!</b>
- If currently on the quest [[Combusting at the Seams]], bring along 5 [[Glowing Filament]] to craft the doll.
- The recipe for [[Hot Hoppers]] also requires 5 [[Coruscating Filament]], a [[Magma Morsel]] harvested in this zone, [[Asbestos Warts]] (body drop from [[Magmus Hoptimus]]), and 50 [[Firesilk Thread]], although the recipe for it is not available on Ember Merchants to purchase until after completing this zone at least once. Hot Hoppers will be useful to reach collectibles placed in challenging and hard to reach spots in the zone.
- There are three portals if exiting the zone is necessary.  The first is at zone in {{waypoint 372, 112, 468}}, the second, on [[Phoenix Isle]], at {{waypoint -84.32, 78.70, 416.98}} leads back to zone in (this fiery portal does not inflict damage), and the final is on [[Fabrician's Flat]] at {{waypoint -422, 62, -330}} which spawns after the final boss is killed.

## Walkthrough

- Collect as much [[Firesilk Thread]] as possible, these will be necessary for the final three boss fights.
- Find and harvest a [[Magma Morsel]]. This is needed for the in order for the 'flame-branded stallion' to take you to the next island. If you do not have one, the stallion will kick you and not allow you to ride. (These Magma Morsels can be found with track harvestables in a nest.  There are many of them.)
- If on [[Combusting at the Seams]], hail [[Girunai]] standing at {{waypoint 337, 109, 445}}.
- Kill the loom weavers and collect the crates around the loom for the Corilac iron heddles. Not every crate will have heddles in them. *(I came up short one Corilac iron heddle after collecting all the crates. I had to exit and wait a few hours before the crates reappeared and I could collect the last one.)*
- Scribe the recipe received from him after turning in the 12 [[Corilac iron heddle]].
1. After clearing enough firesilk weavers, [[Elemy Elmonnier]] will spawn around {{waypoint 275, 68, 155}}.  Kill it. (See Bosses section below for more info.)
1. Once Elemy dies, the message "A gaggle of searing scavengers descend upon an arm ablaze upon the cliffs of Cinder Heights!" will appear, signifying the spawning of the first doll part. {{waypoint 223, 80, 35}}
1. If you can't see the doll part, continue collecting crates.  *(If you don't have enough crates, check that you got the weaver at {{waypoint 319, 113, 357}} and {{waypoint 402, 113, 337}}.*
1. Clear goblins, imps, and slugs to spawn [[Burnard Backbreaker]].  Kill him.
1. Once Burnard dies, the message "Another gaggle of searing scavengers descend upon a leg alighted near one of the burning looms!" appears for the second doll part. {{waypoint 318, 77, 125}}
1. Return to Girunai for the pattern/recipe to reconstruct the doll.
1. Assuming a magma morsel has been harvested, use [[A flame-branded stallion]] {{waypoint 209, 67, 64}} to travel to Lonely Isle. The magma morsel has a limited number of uses.
1. Kill [[Fireye]].  **Before** you start this fight, use the [[Firesilk Thread]] to cover your weapons.
1. Once Fireye dies, the message "A brood of blazewing scavengers descend upon a torrid torso behind the Lonely Isle tower!" appears for **the third doll part**. {{waypoint -463, 57, 315}}
1. If on one of the side quests, collect the wood on this island before you leave it.
1. The portal inside the tower will flare when you first approach it. Wait until the flare subsides. Click on the portal inside the tower to teleport to Phoenix Isle.  Getting in range of this portal causes damage.  Lingering = death. (I edged close, then ran in and clicked the portal. I did not take damage, or die.)
1. Clear all the trash on [[Phoenix Isle]] around  [[Inquez the Ignited]].
1. Kill [[Inquez the Ignited]].  This boss will cast a trauma detriment that must be cured before you can kill it.
   - Trauma Cure can be purchased off the broker if your character does not have the ability/spell.
   - Before the fight, cover your weapons in a [[Firesilk Thread]] or Inquez is immune to damage.
1. Once Inquez bites the dust, the message "Another brood of blazewing scavengers descend upon a hot head nestled within The Roasting Roost!" appears for the fourth and final doll part. {{waypoint -162.26, 78.94, 384.52}}<br><b>**WARNING**</b>: inch within range to obtain the doll head, the flames in the middle of the nest are deadly.
1. Take [[Inquez the Reignited]] {{waypoint -204.85, 87.92, 386.80}} across Blistering Bay to Fabrician's Flat.
   - Do not try to use the loom before she is killed, it will cause instant death.
   - If on the quest Thready for Anything, Collect the luminous embers (6). These are rolling rocks on the ground.
   - Clear mobs in the area.
   - Hail [[Savretta Seyzon]] to respawn her as [[Savretta Seyzon]].  Kill her.
1. If on [[Combusting at the Seams]], use the loom to create the doll.  Recall that you need 5 [[Glowing Filament]].  Hail [[Jacinder]], the mended scorched moppet that spawns nearby at {{waypoint -407, 60, -372}}.
1. Collect Ayonae's cithara in the tent on Fabrician's Flat at {{waypoint -365.76, 58.74, -284.90}}.
1. Turn in completed quests at the zone line inside the dungeon entrance.
1. Travel back to Butcherblock Mountains to complete that quest. Otherwise, use the portal that spawned after the death of [[Savretta Seyzon]] to travel back to Moors of Ykesha.

## Bosses

1. [[Elemy Elmonnier]], wanders through the [[Loom and Doom]] area {{waypoint 275, 68, 155}}. He will spawn after enough of the weavers have been killed.
   - Elemy's steed is about to trample the ground around him! Get back!
   - Elemy Elmonnier says, "I shall brand you with the flaming hooves of my steed!"
1. [[Burnard Backbreaker]], wanders through [[Cinder Heights]] area {{waypoint 350, 50, 72}}. He will spawn after enough of the trash mobs, namely the goblins, imps, and slugs in the area have been killed. Sizzlespit silk harvesters and silk couriers seem to be required before the boss will spawn.
   - The effulgent express crawls out from the molten below. Don't let them reach Burnard!
1. [[Magmus Hoptimus]], spawns after killing the surrounding frogs. {{waypoint 154, 57, 302}}
   - Optional boss, not required to advance through the zone.  Drops Asbestos Warts required to craft Hot Hoppers.
1. [[Fireye]], sitting and drinking at the base of the tower on [[Lonely Isle]] at {{waypoint -428, 59, 293}}.
   - Use a Firesilk Thread before attacking or he will be immune to damage.
1. [[Inquez the Ignited]], perched on the southern cliff edge just outside the tower into which one teleports on [[Phoenix Isle]] {{waypoint -196.65, 99.91, 436.19}}.
   - Trauma cure, [[Firesilk Thread]] both required for this encounter. Firesilk Thread is needed to remove its invulnerability.
   - Inquez erupts, spreading ignited feathers all around!
   - <b>THE TRAUMA DETRIMENT MUST BE CURED OR Inquez WILL NOT DIE!</b>
   - Crackling eggs erupt from the volatile surface!  Destroy them before whatever's contained hatches! (click on eggs to destroy)
1. [[Savretta Seyzon]], standing before the Luminous Loom at {{waypoint -417, 58, -376}}.
   - Hail to spawn.  Reappears as [[Savretta Seyzon]], at {{waypoint -363, 61, -341}}.
   - Note: She will use an ability called Searing Seam. You will need to cure this or you will quickly die.
