## Background

This is the headquarters for the Court of the Blades, [[The Courts of Maj'Dul]] which believes that they must lead the Dervin Empire through military force in order to expand it. Their primary responsibility for the city is the protection of it against the many hostile creatures found in the [[Desert of Ro|desert]], specifically the Rujarkian Orcs.

## Notes

This zone can be entered after you reach kindly faction (20,000+) with the Court of the Blades. See [[Court of the Blades Timeline]] for the repeatable quests which can be used to raise your faction. You can also obtain insignia tokens which can be exchanged in [[The Tower of the Moon]] for faction with the Court.

Insignia tokens can be attained either by doing [[A Tears grifter|Tears Grifter]] quests or by killing guards for the Court of Truth or the Court of the Coin. Inside the court you may gain more faction by completing repeatable quests. You must reach ally faction to complete the quest timeline. To earn the [[Vanguard of the Blade]] title. you must be level 60, have full ally faction (50,000) and complete the [[Blades Champion]] quest.

The most efficient method of increasing your faction with the Court of the Blades is to repeatedly clear the guard towers controlled by the opposing factions, but do not take control of the towers. Instead, collect the Coin and Truth insignia tokens and trade them in for faction. This method is much faster than completing quests, and once you have reached the maximum faction with the Court, you can begin the final series of quests starting with [[Court of the Blades: Dukaris, Hero of the People]].

Inside the headquarters there are:

- [[Mailbox|Norrathian Express mailbox]]
- Banker
- Both Freeport and Qeynos [[Broker|brokers]]
- Mender
- Faction merchant that will sell advanced Tier 6 crafting recipes ([[Heirloom]])
- Merchant that you can sell any status loot items to
- Merchants that sell Journeyman versions of Tier 6 combat arts and spells
- Court Treasures merchant that will sell you house pets, [[Creature Cataloging Timeline|catalogues]] for catalogue quests, special armor, [[Mounts]] and residency licenses that allow you to buy housing in Maj'Dul.
- Quest giver for [[Becoming a Resident of Ro]] that will give you [[Call of Ro]] to return to Maj'Dul.
