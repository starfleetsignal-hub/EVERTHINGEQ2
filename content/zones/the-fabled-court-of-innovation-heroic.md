## Notes

- The entrance is {{waypoint 724, 24, 581}} at the [[Butcherblock Mountains]] Docks
- All trash mobs have 300-500 million HP. Named mobs have 2-10 billion HP.
- The sequence to power down the energy cells is the same as the original version:
  - A = down, down, up, up
  - B = down, up, down, up
  - C = up, down, up, down
  - D = up, up, down, down

## Timed Quests

- [[Mechnamagica Interception (Heroic)|Mechnamagica Interception]]
  - You can pet pull the named out of the first room before killing any of the mechnamagica interceptors, which will start this timed quest.
  - The interceptors cast short-range AoE stifle which can avoided by staying more than 9m away from them.
  - A good place to pull them is up the ramp to avoid the other clockworks there.

- [[Power Cell Power Down (Heroic)|Power Cell Power Down]]
  - This timed quest starts as soon as you deactivate the first power cell.
  - Focus on deactivating each of the power cells, and kill the clockworks that come to investigate.
  - After powering down all four cells, you will need to kill The Crimson Vanquisher within three minutes.
