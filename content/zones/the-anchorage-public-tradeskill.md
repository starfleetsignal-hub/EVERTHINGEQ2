This is the Tradeskill Hub that was introduced with [[LU109]].

## Lore

After the failure of the [[New Combine]] a few tradeskillers led by [[Whilhelm Nam'terin]] set out to reproduce the spirit of achievement through combined forces. He thus found a suitable place in an old mine the [[Cobalt Scar]] that he cleared out with the help of some of the recruited tradeskillers to [[Founder of the Anchorage!|establish]] The Anchorage in it. A place where new ideas could freely be used to create new before unheard of achievements in crafting.

## Geography

An old mine in the southern part of [[Cobalt Scar]].
