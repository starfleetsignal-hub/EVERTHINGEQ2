## Notes

the discovery Locations [[Cragnok's Cubby]] and [[Hawk's Point]] are not part of the normal route through the zone

## Walkthrough

1. The trash mobs [[An ill-invoked idea]] have a 90% damage reduction that can be dispelled with Heroic Opportunities. **You can avoid many of them by running along the wall, but you need to kill the ones you agro.**
1. Kill the [[Forerunner's Blink Buoy]] before you attack the named and he won't have anywhere to teleport to (see below).
1. [[Farzun the Forerunner]] at {{waypoint -623.85, 35.80, -696.11}}
   - Summons adds
   - Also throws poison on you.
   - Teleports himself to [[Forerunner's Blink Buoy]]
   - Destroy the orbs around him and kill him.
1. [[Tarsisk the Tainted]] at {{waypoint -554.07, 52.88, -422.69}}
   - "buries his hands into the ground" = he curses you roots you with [[Corrupted roots]] they can be killed with damage or removed with Heroic Opportunities
1. [[Cragnok]] at {{waypoint -562.28, 53.48, -176.20}}
   - Named is permarooted.
   - Named does a knock-up/stun (~1 minute cooldown) if you use an HO.
   - Joust his frontal when he "sets up to swipe away everyone in front of it!"
1. *optional "climb down" at {{waypoint -541.40, 52.91, -169.17}} for the shiny from named and to discover [[Cragnok's Cubby]], then climb up again*
1. [[Derussah the Deceptive]] at {{waypoint -728.34, 58.37, -352.68}}
   - Puts an elemental debuff on you so you can't see him, just cure.
   - Calls [[A violent vortex]] to aid him, kill add then dmg named again.
1. [[Hasira the Hawk]] at {{waypoint -853.94, 53.46, -408.08}}
   - Summons a sphere called [[Hasira's Marker]] typically close by {{waypoint -798.99, 56.06, -408.23}}, knocks you down, calls 2 adds and [[Hasira the Hawk]] disappears. Kill [[Hasira's Marker]] before it explodes and insta kills you. If [[Hasira's Marker]] explodes and you immediately revive (e.g. Mercenary Healer revives character) to continue the fight, then [[Hasira the Hawk]] will be invunerable until the combat is reset (i.e death or evacuation to zone in). Kill the [[Hasira's Marker]] and adds and [[Hasira the Hawk]] comes back.
   - **Note:** As of 6/5/24, killing the [[Hasira's Marker]] causes [[Hasira the Hawk]] to come back, even if you don't kill the adds... therefore you can just burn the boss down without killing the adds first.
   - Adds and flies around, slowing you. Cure,
   - Keep burning her and curing the slow debuff.
1. *Optional: at {{waypoint -804.43, 54.67, -477.72}} you can discover [[Hawk's Point]]*
1. exit the zone at {{waypoint -780.66, 54.26, -403.06}}
