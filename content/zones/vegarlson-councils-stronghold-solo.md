## Notes

- There are snails you can activate all around the zone to quickly move along the outside of the building.  You must loot [[Magic mud]] from the [[Massive mudglop|massive mudglops]] all over the zone to feed to the snails to activate them.  Then just jump (spacebar) on top of the snail while it is stationary. (or... just walk up onto its  head.)
- Do not go in the water in this zone.  You will become stuck and a giant hand will emerge and kill you.

## Walkthrough

1. Kill the [[Guardian of Gravel]] pathing along the west side of the pyramid to obtain [[Ethereal Gravel]]. If not near skip to step two and kill it when you see it later.
1. Kill [[The Great Gravelly One]], located in front of the ground level doors to the pyramid at {{waypoint 870, 62, 39}}.
   - During the fight he emotes *"Tremendous heat and wind build up around The Great Gravelly One!"*, and if you don't joust you get a power draining incurable curse.
1. Place the [[Ethereal Gravel]] in the [[Test of Gravel]] at {{waypoint 941, 63, 39}}.
   - Three blobs will come out of the [[Test of Gravel]]. Keep track of the directions.
   - Go through doors in the direction the blobs took.
   - *Note: You will be teleported each time, so check your compass.* **Always mouse over the door to see what direction it is before you click!**
   - *If you aren't sure of the order, scroll up in your chat window and look for the colored text, which will give you the order.*
   - My message was North door, North door, North door. So I had to click on the North door (3X's) and each time I teleported.
1. Collect [[Severed Ethereal Gravel]] from the [[Test of Gravel]] at {{waypoint 941, 63, 39}}.
1. Take the portal in the center of the room to the Upper Level.
1. Kill the [[Guardian of Stones]] pathing along the west side of the pyramid to obtain [[Ethereal Stones]].
1. Kill [[Aggregahn]] at {{waypoint 1078, 275, 39}}
   - He cannot be pulled out of the hallway.
   - When he says *"I can destroy as easily as I can build"*, use the [[Ethereal Stones]] to build a wall between you and [[Aggregahn]].
1. Place the [[Ethereal Stones]] in the [[Test of Stone]] at {{waypoint 1108, 275, 40}}.
   - Kill the [[Soldier of stone|soldiers of stone]] that spawn.
   - Note the order in which they are killed, then click the doors in that order.
     - *If you aren't sure of the order, scroll up in your chat window and look for the colored text, which will give you the order.*
1. Collect [[Severed Ethereal Stones]] from the [[Test of Stones]] at {{waypoint 941, 63, 39}}.
1. Take the teleporter that spawns in the center of the room.
1. Travel to {{waypoint 1233, 466, -182}}. Kill [[A cursed guard]], a bridge will appear. Go to the end and jump down to the rock islands.
1. Kill the [[Guardian of Soil]] near {{waypoint 639, 23, -422}} to obtain [[Ethereal Soil]].
1. Kill [[Escargore]] (a snail). He paths around the island.
   - Joust to the side when it emotes *"gives the googly eye"*.
1. Take the portal to the third level at {{waypoint 643, 14, -453}}.
1. Place the [[Ethereal Soil]] in the [[Test of Soil]] at {{waypoint 1271, 465, 40}}.
   - *It may be helpful to use [[Singular Focus]] for this fight.*
   - *You may need to drop your merc if your level is too high.
   - * Don't use area of effect spells.*
   - Kill one [[Earthly podwalker]] atop each dirt mound in the room. Kill the first target, and then drag the remaining two to the next mound and finally drag the last target to the third mound.
   - Repeat for the 3 [[Waters of Awuidor]].
   - Repeat for the 3 [[Flit of radiance]].
   - Collect [[Ethereal Leaves]] from one of the plants that grows.
1. Collect [[Severed Ethereal Soil]] from the [[Test of Soil]] at {{waypoint 1271, 465, 40}}.
1. Take the portal that spawns at {{waypoint 1355, 466, 39}}.
1. Place the [[Ethereal Leaves]] in the [[Test of Leaves]] at {{waypoint 1358, 635, 40}}.
   - Kill all the [[An arborian tender|arborian tenders]] that spawn.
1. Kill [[Lesha Needleleaft]] at {{waypoint 1435, 635, 39}}.
1. Take the leaves back at {{waypoint 1358, 635, 40}}.
1. Take the portal that spawns at {{waypoint 1435, 635, 39}}.
1. Fight each of the Council of Four in turn.
   - Use the Severed Ethereal aspect that matches the council member you are currently fighting. The names come to you one at a time.
1. Take the portal at {{waypoint 1505, 784, 41}} to the Heart of Thudos.
   - 07/21/2024 - There was no portal and no EQ2Map icons for a portal or for the Heart of Thudos.
   - 11/14/2024 - Click on the rune in the quest journal entry and it will spawn the portal.
1. Imbue the Vegarlson Purifying Rune. (If on the Epic Spell Quest)
1. Click on the Heart of Thudos at {{waypoint -216, 113, 160}} to cleanse it and get the update.

The portal to exit the zone is at {{waypoint 1430, 784, 108}}.
