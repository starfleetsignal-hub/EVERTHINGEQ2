## Requirements

- Prior to Planes of Prophecy Expansion 14 at least one player in the raid needed to have the achievement from killing [[The Crumbling Emperor]] in order for the raid to zone in. This is no longer necessary.

## Notes

The zone consists of two wings: the Western wing: Halls of the Betrayer; and the Eastern wing: Laboratory of Mutation. The eastern wing will not be accessible until the western wing is cleared and Grendish killed.

### Bosses in Halls of the Betrayer

1. [[Sontalak]]
1. Essedara and Jalkhir
1. Caden and Keplin
1. Rarthek the Swiftclaw
1. [[Kigara the Blazewing|Kigara]] and Kelana - must destroy the crystals that spawn around them to be able to damage them
1. Derig Cinderaxe (Derig the Prime Executioner)
1. Tavekalem
1. [[Grendish the Betrayer|Grendish]] - spawns and becomes attackable when all the above are dead (look UP!)

### Bosses in the Laboratory of Mutation

1. The Aerakyn Commanders (welcoming committee ring event)
1. Gerid, Harin, and Merig
1. Jardin the Conqueror
1. Andreis the Culler - in the Necromancers Alcove - comes with two souleaters
1. Controller Ervin and Pyrelord Kullis - in the Chamber of Experiments. Clearing to this point opens the central passageway, so now can reach...
1. Klandicar - in Council of the Undying. The wall now collapses to reveal...
1. Zlandicar the Unnatural
