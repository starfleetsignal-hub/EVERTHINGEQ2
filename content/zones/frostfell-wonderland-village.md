## Overview

Frostfell Wonderland Village is the hub of [[Frostfell]] joy on [[Norrath]]! The Frostfell Elves, Gigglegibber Goblins maintain it throughout the year, but during [[Time|Deepice]] they put up their *Magic Closets* in the major cities and invite everyone to come in an enjoy fun and games! At the heart of the village, stands [[Santa Glug]], who hands out presents to players every 18 real-world hours. There are also buckets of snowballs spread around that you can pick up and lob at your friend! The snowballs remain useful year 'round, so you can pelt your friends with them in July if you choose!

Though the Frostfell annual world event first came to Norrath in 2005, the village was created in 2006, updated in 2008 and completely made-over in 2013. The layout of the zone is like [[Icegill Lagoon]] of [[Everfrost]], however rather than mean Icegill goblins, there are snow-covered islands connected via rainbow bridges. Prior to 2008, the islands were surrounded by water that you could swim around in. For the 2008 festivities and onward, the water has been frozen.

## Entering the Village

To enter Frostfell Wonderland Village, click on the *Magic Closet* located in every major city:

- [[Qeynos Province District]]: {{waypoint 736, -21, -41}}
- [[The City of Freeport]]: {{waypoint 231.32, -3.52, -41.00}}
- [[Kelethin]]: {{waypoint 224, 112, 399}}
- [[Neriak, City of Hate]]: {{waypoint -98.85, 10.19, 275.48}}
- [[Gorowyn]]: {{waypoint 2594, 6, 1192}}
- [[Haven]]: beside the mailbox
- [[New Halas]]: {{waypoint -69.28, 147.89, -35.53}}

## Amenities and Vendors

Only a few of the normal city amenities are available in Frostfell Wonderland Village:

- A [[Gigglegibber Goblins' Lotto|Gigglegibber Gaming Goblin]] is near the exit wardrobe {{waypoint 83, -4, -1185}}
- A [[Banker]] is near north of the entrance at the cliff edge {{waypoint 139, -9, -1230}}
- A [[Mender]] is near the Icy Keep zone-in {{waypoint 129, -3, -1155}}
- A mailbox on the island at the foot of the stairs {{waypoint 191, -36, -1259}} (look for the sign on the red candy cane).
- See [[Frostfell#Frostfell Merchants|Frostfell Vendors]] for a list of Frostfell-specific merchants.
