## Background

You met up with [[Priestess Annalisa Swornlove (Frostfang Sea)|Priestess Annalisa Swornlove]] in the city of [[New Halas]], along with her brother, [[Priest Aaronolis Swornlove]], and three Sisters of Erollisi. She has been shaken by the apparent power of hate, but she is determined to continue to carry out the works of [[Erollisi Marr|Erollisi]]! You have volunteered to enter [[Nektropos Castle]] in search of a stolen tome from the *Vaults of Truth and Love*.
