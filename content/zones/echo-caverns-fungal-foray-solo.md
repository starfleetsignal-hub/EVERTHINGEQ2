## Walkthrough

1. Use the zipline {{waypoint -42.78, 142.10, -258.11}} then move east to reach [[The Shattered Chasm]].
1. Kill constructs until the portal at {{waypoint -297, 140, -305}} lights up. Take the portal.
1. Follow around the right wall from the portal, then kill the [[Corrupted Construct]] {{waypoint -148, 57, -376}}.
1. Go back around the way you came, and go across the ramp to the portal at {{waypoint -339, 62, -216}}.
1. Kill chasm fiends to spawn [[Shadowphage]], a giant flying poxfiend. Fight him around {{waypoint -334, 247, -283}}.
   - Summons [[A grim feedling|grim feedlings]] which must be killed ASAP to drop the damage shield on the name.
   - The damage shield increases by 1 every second his adds are alive, then starts to decrease when they're dead.
1. Deactivate the portal guards.
   - Use the zipline at {{waypoint -252, 255, -315}}.
   - Kill the portal guards at the bottom.
   - Click **McGillicuddy's Booty Buddy** {{waypoint -186.80, 168.73, -364.12}} to take you back up to the next portal.
1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -271, 254, -327}},
1. Defeat [[Vinzerah Ral'Moor]] {{waypoint -190, 315, -333}}
   1. *The key is to Dispel* then click on portal where she stood. (This needs clarification; dispel named? not all classes have dispel and low dps toons will be swarmed rather quickly which makes it impossible to locate anything visually. Target macros work for both named and crystals, but they are of little help when there's 5+ adds up)
1. Use the portal on the wall behind Vinzerah {{waypoint -190.14, 313.60, -349.20}} to go to [[Fungal Grove]].
   - If you are also on the quest [[Save the Last Blast For Me]], ***stop here*** and continue where you left off: you must not do the next step yet or you will have to repeat the entire instance.
1. Travel to [[Sods and Bends]] {{waypoint 470, -0.80, -471}}.
   - Along the way you will receive a message, *The smell of your blood has attracted a voracious fungus fiend!* Wait until [[A voracious fungus fiend]] spawns, then kill it.
1. Continue to {{waypoint 301, 13, -472}}, slaying [[A voracious fungus fiend|voracious fungus fiends]] along the way until you get the message, *"A massive fungus fiend awakens from it's slumber in search of food!"*
1. Defeat [[Sporefiend Zugu]] around {{waypoint 266.69, 16.56, -463.71}}.
1. Shortly after Sporefiend Zugu is killed, some zelmie messengers approach and ask you to follow, then fly away.
1. Go up the log at {{waypoint 298, 67, -528}} to reach [[Bolete Bluff]].
1. Use the tamed dragonfly at {{waypoint 507, 110, -525}} and use it to fly to [[The King's Cap]].
1. Defeat [[Fungus King Cremini]] {{waypoint 451, 262, -453}}.
1. Exit zone at {{waypoint 457, 262, -454}}.
