## Background

The leader of The Runnyeye Goblins, [[Overlord Baylzuthak]], is holed up in here. You must fight through his guards in order to reach him and put an end to his malicious plans.

## Notes

- The former access quest, [[Secrets of Runnyeye]], is no longer required to enter.
- The entrance you arrived through will take you back to the docks of the [[Enchanted Lands]], as a convenience to save you the trip back through Runnyeye.  To return to [[Runnyeye]], use one of the man-hole covers (circular exits) on the floor.

## Walkthrough

1. Go left from the zone-in and clear the trash.  The two minotaurs near the vaults are linked with [[A rune covered chest]].  Kill the chest, and it drops [[A star shaped key]] that you need to access the next section of the zone.
1. Wait in that room for a bit for [[Vault Curator Ashuk]] and his assistants ([[Assistant Curator Skint]], [[A Gorehorn boar handler]] and three [[A warboar ravager|warboar ravagers]]), then kill them.
1. Head north and unlock the door.  All the monsters in this room are initially set as NPCs.  Walk in and wander around to trigger a ring event.
   1. First ring - four [[A Gorehorn elite guardsman|Gorehorn elite guardsmen]].
   1. Second ring - three [[A Runnyeye deathguard|Runnyeye deathguards]], [[An Oculus mender]], and [[Seneschal Piknin]]
   1. Third ring - [[Overlord Baylzuthak]]
1. After Overlord Baylzuthak is dropped, [[Duke Zoryll]] will say something about finding treasure in tunnels to the south - he's referring to [[The Chamber of Rulgax]].
