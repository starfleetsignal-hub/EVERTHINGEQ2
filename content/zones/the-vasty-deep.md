## Geography

The Vasty Deep is a non-combat zone filled with quest NPCs for the associated instances. From the teleport-in point, a ramp spirals upward to a structure on top of the island. Inside that structure is a ramp spiraling down to the three instance doors.

### Travel

From any telepad, ask any Temporal Guide to send you to Deepwater Pavilion station. Take the stairs behind the quest givers (S) down to double doors at {{waypoint -160, 174, -377}} Click the teleporter to enter The Vasty Deep.
