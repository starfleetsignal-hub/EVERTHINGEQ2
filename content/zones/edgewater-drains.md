## Background

Edgewater Drains is the lowest of the three [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]] sewer zones. It itself is made up of three levels, connected by stairs, at the north and south ends of the zone. Each stair is connected to a large room that then connects via a hallway to the large room at the opposite end of the level. The bottom level is almost entirely underwater, while the upper two levels are only ankle deep. This zone is NOT recommended for adventurers with fear of water and/or large sea creatures.
