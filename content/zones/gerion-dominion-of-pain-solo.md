## Walktrough

1. Kill [[Sir Rendal Cauldthorn]] at {{waypoint 352, 9, 204}}
   - He is chained and rooted. When you aggro him, above either Northern or Southern Feeding Pit a group of lights will spawn. Click on the light and a [[Knight of Truth]] will spawn. Kill the Knight. Repeat with other lights until [[Sir Rendal Cauldthorn]] decides that's enough (as little as 2) and summons you to him. Now you can kill him.
1. Kill [[The Soulbleeder]] at {{waypoint 482.58, 0.53, 151.73}}.
   - *Tank and spank*
1. Kill [[The Exhumed Executioner]] moves on a path around {{waypoint 367.07, 0.52, 347.30}}.
   - Has a Stay of Execution buff that counts down from 20. If it reaches 0 you die instantly.
   - Completion of a Heroic Opportunity (HO) adds 10 to the counter up to the maximum of 20.
   - Multiple, fast completing HOs required during this combat.
     - As an example of a fast HO would be a Social/Macro recording for a Wizard of:-
       - /cancel_spellcast
       - Arcane Augar
       - Cease
       - Then whatever fast cast spell is flashing on a Hot Bar to complete the HO.
   - *Heroic Opportunities extend the countdown of the insta-wipe.*
1. Kill [[Dominator Zerada]] at {{waypoint 367.07, 0.52, 347.30}}
   - when Enforcers or Fellreavers say "Your sentence: Death" a HO is required or you interrupt or stifle them
   - *Tank and spank*
1. Kill [[Raynax S'Vere]] at {{waypoint 486.76, 11.34, 280.49}}.( do HO when he heals to stop it)
   - *Use heroic opportunities to deny him buffs. DO NOT use heroic opportunities when he outlaws them.*
