## Notes

- Full of [[A Grikbar pawbuster|Grikbar pawbusters]].  Level 49-50, most ^^^ but occasionally in groups of 3 ^. All with a pretty decent amount of hit points each.
- Kill all of the mobs before engaging the king. Because if you don't (even at level 70) all of the remaining mobs will come and attack you when the king gets to 50% health. *To engage the king, click on the bowl full of food at the base of his bed.
