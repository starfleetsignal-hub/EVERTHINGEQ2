## Background

You stumbled upon a [[Klogg Bindgear III|peculiar gnome]] in the ashen crags of [[Lavastorm]]. He proposed a business deal with you to infiltrate a quarry where supposedly there is a group of dwarves under some time type of mind-control that causes them to mine day-in and day-out. If you are able to free them, the gnome could sneak in and take the mined ore for himself! Any other treasure would be yours for the taking, of course.

## Access

To gain access to the instance, progress through the dialogue with [[Klogg Bindgear III]] just outside the entrance in [[Lavastorm]] at {{waypoint -199.63, -4.44, -359.81}}.

## Walkthrough

1. Progress through the dialogue with [[Gustad Aleslammer]] to spawn [[K'Dal the Deceiver]].
   - Choose your responses wisely, don't just spam the top choice.
1. Slay K'Dal the Deceiver.
