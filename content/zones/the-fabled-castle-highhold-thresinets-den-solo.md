### Kladnog Shralok

No need to kill any trash MOBs before you engage him. You'll need the Mucktails for later. He's up, near {{waypoint 126.70, -109.40, 44.14}}, when you zone in. Has a knockback. Wear a belt with a Stun rune on it so you're not stunned after the knockbacks. Except for the knockback, pretty much a tank and spank.

### Wrek Shralok

You can go straight to him after dispatching Kladnog. Call him by clicking on the skeleton bones at {{waypoint 245.71, -77.87, -21.80}}. Pure tank and spank.

### Vexven Mucktail

He spawns on you after you kill enough Mucktail, and the Mucktail Spirits that spawn when they die, in the courtyard. There are some large groups of Mucktails. Try to avoid them because if he spawns when they die you have him and all of their Mucktail Spirits to deal with at once. Other than that, basically, a tank and spank,

### Qworux

Located at {{waypoint 96.03, -86.03, 13.44}}. I clear all of the spiders on the first floor, the stairs, and from in front of his alcove. Not sure if they add if they're not killed. Has a knockback so fight him in the corner with your back to the wall. AOE the adds, or ignore them if your DPS is good enough.

### Thresinet

Located at {{waypoint 95.77, 0.00, -187.60}}. Hardest fight in the zone by far. Don't rush in!! There are four 'a deathden' around her, two in the front left and right and two in the back left and right. They're "feeding" her something. Take out the two in the front from range. Then charge her and engage from behind her. Shortly after engaging, take out the last two deathdens from range (I use a target macro to make targeting them easier in the chaos). Once they're gone, beat her down. AOE the adds as, if, needed.
