## Walkthrough

1. Use the zipline {{waypoint -42.78, 142.10, -258.11}} then move east to reach [[The Shattered Chasm]].
1. Kill constructs until the portal at {{waypoint -297, 140, -305}} lights up. Take the portal.
1. [[Corrupted Construct (Heroic)|Corrupted Construct]] {{waypoint -148, 57, -376}}
   - Turn right (west) from the portal and follow the path to find the [[Corrupted Construct]] {{waypoint -148, 57, -376}}.
   - He will initially be fighting three [[A deepshade corruptor|deepshade corruptors]], but will kill them then become aggro.
   - Throughout the fight, the named will announce *"The Corrupted Construct prepares to unleash a 10 meter blast around itself!"*, joust out 10 meters to avoid knockback.
   - Periodically, you'll get an emote, *"Plasma Plague protects you against the Corrupted Construct's Infections Fists!"* Don't cure Plasma Plague while Infection Fist is up.
1. Go back around the way you came, and go south across the ramp to the next portal at {{waypoint -339, 62, -216}}. Teleport, then head north.
1. [[Shadowphage (Heroic)|Shadowphage]] around {{waypoint -340.15, 62.92, -209.23}}
   - Kill [[A chasm fiend|chasm fiends]] until you get the message *"A sinister presence pierces the shadows!"* to spawn [[Shadowphage (Heroic)|Shadowphage]], a giant flying poxfiend.
   - Throughout the fight, you'll get the emote *"Shadowphage stops to gather darkness around itself!"* Several [[A grim feedling|grim feedlings]] will spawn, and must be killed ASAP (for each one up the name gets a buff that reduces incoming damage). The damage reduction increases by 1 every second his adds are alive, then starts to decrease when they're dead.
   - Additionally, the mob will emote *"Shadowphage drops something you don't want to be near!"* This will spawn a pile on the ground. Move at least 10 meters away from it to avoid a nasty power drain.
1. Continue up the hill, use the zipline at {{waypoint -252, 255, -315}}, kill the portal guards at the bottom, then find and click **McGillicuddy's Booty Buddy** {{waypoint -186.80, 168.73, -364.12}} to take you back up to the portal on top Shadowphage's hill (which will now be active).
1. Use the portal at {{waypoint -271, 254, -327}},
1. [[Vinzerah Ral'Moor (Heroic)|Vinzerah Ral'Moor]] {{waypoint -190.05, 313.18, -333.05}}
   - Periodically you'll see an emote, *"Vinzerah Ral'Moor activates another balanzite crystal!"* This will cause a mob to spawn above one of the crystals around the named.  Each one left up buffs the named, so kill them as they spawn.
   - Additionally, the named will emote, *"Vinzerah Ral'Moor summons forth an anime of order!"* This will spawn a golem add, if left up for long enough, it will emote *"will soon turn to chaos if not killed swiftly!"* so kill them immediately (prioritize killing the golems over killing the crystals).
1. Use the portal on the wall behind Vinzerah {{waypoint -190.14, 313.60, -349.20}} to go to [[Fungus Grove]].
1. Make your way down to [[Sods and Bends]] {{waypoint 470, -0.80, -471}}. (There is a ramp down to the lower level at  {{waypoint 386.32, 33.33, -311.91}})
1. [[Sporefiend Zugu]] spawns around {{waypoint 266.69, 16.56, -463.71}}.
   - In order to spawn Zugu, you need to be drunk, which can be done by using the **pulpy puffballs** that drop from mobs in the area.
   - Along the way you will receive a message, *The smell of your blood has attracted a voracious fungus fiend!* Wait until [[A voracious fungus fiend]] spawns, then kill it.
   - Continue to {{waypoint 301, 13, -472}}, slaying [[A voracious fungus fiend|voracious fungus fiends]] along the way until you get the message, *"A massive fungus fiend awakens from it's slumber in search of food!"*
   - During the fight, [[Sporefiend Zugu]] will emote *"Zugu buries his fists into the ground, stimulating the growth of his favorite food!"* When he does this, multiple [[A spore-bearing bolete|spore-bearing boletes]] will spawn, which then spawn [[A mushroom spore|mushroom spores]]. If you allow 10 spores to stay alive at any time,that's a fail condition.
   - The tank should stay very drunk for this fight, because periodically *"Sporefiend Zugu goes after the most highly intoxicated of you all!"*
1. Go back up the ramp to Fungus Grove and up the big log at {{waypoint 298, 67, -528}} to reach [[Bolete Bluff]].
1. Find the tamed dragonfly at {{waypoint 507, 110, -525}} and use it to fly to [[The King's Cap]].
1. [[Fungus King Cremini]] {{waypoint 441.75, 256.85, -434.05}}.
   - This named is **weak to HEAT damage**.
   - You must be drunk to see the named, and stay drunk throughout the fight. Use the **pulpy puffballs** you looted earlier, alcohol will also work.
   - Throughout the fight, the named will cast Amatoxin Binding, which will reduce your level of drunkenness, so you will need to continually consume the puffballs looted throughout the zone.
   - Adds will spawn, which drop more puffballs, the named will get a stacking buff for each add left alive, so kill them asap and assign puffballs to whoever has the fewest.
   - The Fungus King spawns an add that will heal him , burn it down.
   - There will be an acid rain: run under the mushroom until it says its clear. King Cremini is untargetable while you're under the mushroom.
