## Strategy

1. [[Wrath of Ro]] is located at {{waypoint 459, -16, -373}} and is spawned approaching [[Krel-Ariak]] and letting him complete his chanting.  Once the Wrath is dead, he summons several, ever increasing waves of [[A summoned pyrelord|pyrelords]].
1. He then casts  "a massive summoning spell" to summon [[Retribution of Ro]].  Take the portal that appears where [[Krel-Ariak]] stood.
1. Move to the staging area at {{waypoint 385, 18, -237}} and kill the waves of Doomfire guardians to spawn [[Vengeance of Ro]]. Krel-Ariak appears at the side of the platform, and joins in when you attack [[Vengeance of Ro|Vengeance]], but he dies easily.  Beware the knockback in this fight which can punt you into the lava.

A portal to return to [[Myrist, the Great Library]] spawns after killing the last named and is located at {{waypoint 427.97, 19.66, -261.89}}
