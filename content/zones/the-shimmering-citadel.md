## Lore

The [[Shimmering Citadel]] is the home to the Djinn of the [[Desert of Ro]]. Ruled by [[The Djinn Master]] from his court above, the citadel holds the lower hierarchies of Djinn and lesser beings that serve the court. Originally pulled from the [[Plane of Sky]], the floating citadel rests upon a ragged chunk of rock from which several enormous chains dangle. - *[[Dungeons in the Desert]]*

## Geography

### Entrance

- From [[The Pillars of Flame]]: Click on the mirror at {{waypoint 884, -177, -1595}}.
- Use [[Samirah's mirror]] to teleport directly inside.

### Exit

- To [[The Pillars of Flame]]: {{waypoint 58, 98, -128}}

### Instances

- [[The Poets Palace]]: Take elevator up first floor of citadel, then go to {{waypoint 99, 147, -59}}
- [[The Poets Palace: Return]]: *same as above*
- [[The Pedestal of Sky]]: Take elevator up first floor of citadel, then go to the eastern tower {{waypoint -258, 146, -54}}, then take the elevators up to the top. Click on the mirror. Requires completion of an [[Capturing Day and Night|access quest]].
- [[The Djinn Master's Prism]]: Same as above, however click on the prism, not the mirror; requires [[Scale of the Master|access item]].
