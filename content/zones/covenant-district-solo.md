To start the instance, you must talk to [[Cek'Arctor]] and select the option for Mirrakar. He'll then give you the quest [[Marauding the Market]] and open a portal. The quest is timed for 1 hour 30 minutes.

## Bosses

1. [[Berserking Defender (Solo)]]
1. [[Velkron (Solo)]]
1. [[Captain Veezaran (Solo)]]
1. [[Zyerek (Solo)]]
1. [[Commander Mirrakar]]: must kill all four other bosses in order for him to spawn.
