## Names

- [[Luminary Percontorius Felvin]]
  - Strat: Everyone in corner he has a knockback. "Tank'n spank."

- [[Luminary Cohortis Emon]]
  - Strat: Spawns a red cone where the most of the raid stands. Everyone fight in front of him. If the red cone comes, joust out of it and run on his back and continue your fight here. The mob will turn around.

- [[Luminary Hertu Asundri]]
  - Strat: Two summoners spawn adds after killing 3 adds on each side they go away. stay 20m from named, burn adds before named. After adds and summoners go away dps named.
- [[Lord Triskian Seru (Epic x4)]]

Must clear all trash on the level to open the interior door in his room

- Strat: Must kill Golems before burning down Seru. First Golem becomes attackable after ~20 seconds. Second Golem becomes attackable when Seru reaches 75% HP. Third Golem becomes attackable when Seru reaches 50% HP. Final golem becomes attackable when Seru reaches 25% HP. If you overburn Seru while a Golem is up, the raid will wipe.
- Seru will periodically drop his sword (90%, 65%, 40%, 15% HP). Have one party member pick up the sword and use one of its skills on Lord Seru.
- Requires a minimum of two players to complete or he causes a wipe.
