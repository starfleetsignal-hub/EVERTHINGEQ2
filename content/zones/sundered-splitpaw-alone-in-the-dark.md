## Background

One of [[Terraporter|terraporters]] in [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den|Splitpaw Den]] is malfunctioning. You and your team were sent to investigate what was causing the malfunction. However, as soon as one of you touched the terraporter, your whole team was teleported to different locations around the instance. You must find a way to reunite with your group and escape this madness!

## Notes

- Like all Splitpaw Saga zones, the monsters scale to match your level, between 20 and 50.
- If you decide you don't want to finish the instance, log-out/log-in and you will be back in [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den|Splitpaw Den]].
