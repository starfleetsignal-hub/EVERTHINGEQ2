Raid quest: [[Ley of the Land: Blinded by Illusions]]

- Rockhopper Pounce r(T1) {{waypoint -732.47, 6.29, 675.98}}
  - Strat: Finds biggest grouping of players teleports around then spawns a target on floor joust out of it. It seems he does it around these hp %'s (88% 66% 36% and 12%) they could be entirely random based on speed of dps or just in general but these were what was noted on the fight could even be more or less jumps. Has barrage.

- Praetorian K'Tikrn (T1) {{waypoint 409.31, 97.50, 777.83}}
  - Strat: emotes he will vomit joust away as the bubble spawns grows pretty big, try to do it in a triangle pattern to make it easier in regards to moving him. You can try to judge the size of the bubble to when the next vomit will be to get ready for joust.If cursed aggro will go crazy. If anyone gets cursed and dies just dodge the bodys, the bubble will pop if it hits a corpse.

- Thought Horror Overfiend (T1) {{waypoint -531.51, 62.33, 259.86}}
  - Strat: alot of curing, have everyone stand in center of room tank drags it to new location when the eye (icon) det appears when it wears off the distance is then calculated for damage. Need alot of dps as his damage gets worse longer the fight goes.

- Shik'Nar Imperiatrix (T1)
  - Strat: Focus on the named adds come you can burn them or not or just focus on named if you got alot of dps. The target swaps off the queen so you can switch back to her if it does.
