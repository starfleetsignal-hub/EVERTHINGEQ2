## Notes

- This is the observatory of [[Maj'Dul]]. Inside you will find NPCs that can advance your [[The Courts of Maj'Dul|Court factions]] when you turn in tokens to them. Also there is a [[Abnar the Bookbinder|book binder]] that can re-bind the books and pages you find throughout Desert of Flames.
- Another version of this zone exists called [[Tower of the Moon:Infiltration]] and is used in the quest [[Be Careful What You Wish For]].
