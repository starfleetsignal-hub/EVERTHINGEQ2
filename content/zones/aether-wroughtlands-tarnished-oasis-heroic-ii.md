## Notes

the discovery Locations [[Cragnok's Cubby]] and [[Hawk's Point]] are not part of the normal route through the zone

## Walkthrough (copy from Solo)

1. The trash mobs [[An ill-invoked idea]] have a 90% damage reduction that can be dispelled with Heroic Opportunities
1. Kill the [[Forerunner's Blink Buoy]] before you attack the named
1. [[Farzun the Forerunner]] at {{waypoint -623.85, 35.80, -696.11}}
   - Summons adds
   - Also throws poison on you.
   - Teleports him self to [[Forerunner's Blink Buoy]]
   - Destroy the orbs around him and kill him.
1. [[Tarsisk the Tainted]] at {{waypoint -554.07, 52.88, -422.69}}
   - "buries his hands into the ground" = he curses you roots you with [[Corrupted roots]] they can be killed with damaged or removed with Heroic Opportunities
1. [[Cragnok]] at {{waypoint -562.28, 53.48, -176.20}}
   - named is rooted
   - Joust his frontal when he "sets up to swipe away everyone in front of it!"
1. *optional "climp down" at {{waypoint -541.40, 52.91, -169.17}} for the shiny from named and to discover [[Cragnok's Cubby]], then climp up again*
1. [[Derussah the Deceptive]] at {{waypoint -728.34, 58.37, -352.68}}
   - Puts an elemental debuff on you so you can't see him, just cure.
   - calles [[A violent vortex]] to aid him, kill add then dmg named again
1. [[Hasira the Hawk]] at {{waypoint -853.94, 53.46, -408.08}}
   - summons [[Hasira's Marker]] close by, knocks you down and calls 2 adds. Kill the adds and the marker and Named comes back
   - Adds and flies around, slowing you. Cure,
   - Destroy the ball thing in the middle that she summons, or you will die.
   - Keep burning her and curing the slow debuff.
1. *Optional: at {{waypoint -804.43, 54.67, -477.72}} you can discover [[Hawk's Point]]*
1. exit the zone at {{waypoint -780.66, 54.26, -403.06}}
