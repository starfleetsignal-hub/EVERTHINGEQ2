## Strategy

1. Head north until you get to the first room where the first named is roaming [[Dossvor]]. {{waypoint 0, 0, -63}}
1. From here you can see the next two named - [[Korlaadrec]] to the west and [[Drelaakor]] to the east.
1. Return to the center room and continue heading norths to the final room with multiple levels.
1. Move to the center of the room and pull the lever at {{waypoint -1, 16, -135}} to raise the platform to [[Xexon, the Listener]].
1. Once Xexon is defeated, return to the lift in center of the room and pull the lever at {{waypoint 9, 29, -150}} to ascend to the final named, [[Xacx-Kahda]].
