## Walkthrough

### [[Palovina Vodlak]]

During the first half of the fight, Palovina casts "Transmogrify" on a random person, transforming them into one of eight different animals (one of them is a human, so watch out for that). The transformed player needs to stand under the test tube containing the same type of creature and then cure the arcane detriment. The arcane is not curable until you are under the correct test tube.

Around 50%, Palovina transforms into a werewolf. She stops casting "Transmogrify" and has no special strategy for the rest of the fight. Just tank and spank!

### [[Vulgrat Vilebones]]

Around 75%, 50%, and 25%, Vulgrat turns into a cloud of bones and becomes immune to all damage. While he is running around in this state, he drops a vilebone spider. Killing the spider turns Vulgrat back into his normal (damageable) form. Standing too close to Vulgrat in his bone-cloud form causes a curable trauma that significantly debuffs offensive stats.

### [[Wretched Francine]]

Around 75%, 50%, and 25%, Francine goes immune and splits into three copies, each of which runs to a different room. One player needs to grab the cauldron and follow the copy wearing a witch hat and place the cauldron on the glowing green outline of a cauldron in that room. Francine pops up when the cauldron is in the correct room, once again damageable. If the cauldron is placed in the wrong room, or in the wrong position in the correct room, an add pops instead. The cauldron can be moved again once this add dies.

### [[Viktor Vodlak]]

Around 40%, Viktor goes immune to damage and flies up to a high ledge, leaving behind two books. Killing the "Study of Proximity Translocation" causes the second book to despawn and opens a portal to Viktor's ledge. Once through the portal, Viktor begins to take damage again and can be killed normally.

### [[Nogrovska Vodlak]]

Red and blue tornados spawn shortly after the fight begins. Directly targeting and attacking a tornado changes its color from red to blue (or vice versa) but also places a detriment on the caster that causes spells to consume health instead of mana. Attacking any tornado will remove the detriment.

Around 75%, 50%, and 25%, Nogrovska stops taking damage and becomes either "positive" or "negative" (as seen in chat or in her name tag). She summons a golem add that is initially "neutral". Dragging the golem through a red tornado makes it "positive", and dragging it through a blue tornado makes it "negative". If the golem is killed while in an opposite state to Nogrovska (negative<->positive), the witch starts taking damage again. If the golem is killed in any other state, Nogrovska will summon another neutral golem.

### [[The Abandoned Labomination]]

There are four platforms around the edge of the room that correspond to slashing, heat, poison, and mental damage. Clicking the pedestal at the foot of each platform will spawn a non-aggro add of that type. While the add is engaged in combat and alive, all players' damage becomes the damage type of the add. When the add is killed, the players' damage goes back to normal.

At four points throughout the fight (80, 60, 40, 20?), the Labomination becomes immune to all but one damage type: slashing, heat, poison, or mental. The four pedestals and adds are used to select the correct damage type and continue the fight.

N.B. having all 4 creatures alive and attacking you (use a bind or other non damaging spell if higher level to avoid killing them) will allow you to just dps the boss down without thinking about it)

The Labomination also casts several detriments which debuff offensive and defensive stats, all of which are cureable.
