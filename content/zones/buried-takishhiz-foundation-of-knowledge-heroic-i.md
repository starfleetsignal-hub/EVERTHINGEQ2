## Thread Baron

- Fight him up top rather than in the ditch or you will have continuously spawning bugs to deal with.

- He has a counter on him "Furiously Unfurled". When this counter reaches 20 it is a group wipe.<gallery>

File:Furiously Unfurled.jpg|Furiously Unfurled Effects
</gallery>

- Do not interrupt "Rug Hug"! When Rug Hug us up the counter on Furiously Unfurled with begin to count down. Do not dispel Rug Hug until the counter on Furiously Unfurled is cleared. When the counter reaches zero you can dispel Rug Hug. While Rug Hug is up Thread Barron cannot take damage and group members will take varying degrees of damage and power drain depending on how far they are from Thread Barron up to 20 meters.<gallery>

File:Rug Hug.jpg|Rug Hug Effects
</gallery>

- Periodically he will cast "Tassled" (curse) on someone in the group, making them unable to see the mob. This effect needs to be cured or it will kill the group member it is on when it expires.
