## Walkthrough

**Diabo Xi Va**

1. Requires the raid to have 12 living players with a Phantastic weapon buff in order to avoid a raidwide debuff.
1. Spawns adds at specific health percentages: 90, 85, 70, 65, 50, 45, 35, 30, 20, 10.
   - These adds have coop strike with the boss, and will spawn from a random chandelier in the room.
   - When they spawn, they will put a curable elemental on the raid that can blur vision and will stifle. Stifle immunity rune recommended.
1. Has a stacking buff that makes the boss tougher.
   - If the boss is near the "shadowed" chandelier / "power source", the buff will go down. If the boss is too far away from that chandelier, the buff will go up. The tick up or down will happen every 3 seconds.
   - If the boss reaches 0 stacks, a red ring will spawn that will kill anyone inside of it a few seconds later, and a new chandelier will be picked.
   - One approach to handle this is to fight the boss away from the "shadowed" chandelier until the boss reaches 7-10 stacks, then bring them near the "shadowed" chandelier until the stacks are down to about 2, then move them back out.
1. The boss will periodically put up a stoneskin buff that can be removed by the appropriate scepter.
   - Red buff is dispelled by using the Scepter of Illumination, blue buff is dispelled by using the Scepter of Shadows.
   - This occurs about a minute into the fight, and then every 90 seconds thereafter, with a fixed pattern (assuming no other scepter usage). One player can manage the rotation for this fight if they have both scepters.
   - If the same scepter is used on the boss twice (such as if two players try to debuff the boss around the same time), the second player will be put in a penalty box for a minute.
1. Once Diabo Xi Va is dead, the area in the center of a room becomes a portal to the next area of the zone. Activate the golems in this next area by approaching the Emperor, then burn the golems down. There will be four travel points activated to bring you towards the next four bosses, which can be fought in any order.

**Thall Va Xakra Fer**

1. One of four bosses that can be selected after killing Diabo Xi Va.
1. Requires the raid to have 18 living players with a Phantastic weapon buff in order to avoid a raidwide debuff.
1. 15 seconds into the fight, and every 50 seconds after that, the boss will put a ring on the ground that will be either green or red.
   - When the circle is red, the raid must joust out. Any player still inside will die.
   - When the circle is green, the raid must joust in. Any player outside of the green ring will die.
   - At the start of the fight, this will start as alternating red, green, red, green, etc.
   - After the boss has reached 50% health, this pattern may change/randomize.
   - Note: The boss will send tells to people who are *in the right spot* (outside of the red, or inside of the green) telling them to do the wrong thing - trying to convince people outside of the red to come inside, or people inside of the green to back away.
1. 10 seconds into the pull, and every 35 seconds after that, a curse called "Shade Crowned" will land on a player in the raid which lasts for 30 seconds.
   - This curse should not be cured immediately - it should only be cured after about 15+ seconds, so the curse will have less than 15 seconds remaining and be flashing. Curing too early will result in having an extra curse on the raid that will need to be cured after waiting.
   - If this curse expires, then the raid will wipe.
1. Just over a minute into the raid (around when the second circle / first green circle spawns on the raid), and every 45 seconds after, the boss will put a stoneskin buff up similar to Diabo Xi Va.
   - Red buff is dispelled by using the Scepter of Illumination, blue buff is dispelled by using the Scepter of Shadows.
   - This buff is not guaranteed to oscillate between the red and blue buffs - it may be the blue buff or red buff several times in a row, necessitating more players with scepters being involved in removing the stoneskin.
   - Using an incorrect scepter will turn the screen grey and apply significant damage to the raid.
1. There is another curse called "Grasping Hold" that is a root, and can be cured immediately.
1. There is also a trauma that lands on the raid periodically that can root, and it should be cured immediately to prevent issues with jousting.

**High Priest Verrkara**

1. One of four bosses that can be selected after killing Diabo Xi Va.
1. Requires the raid to have 18 living players with a Phantastic weapon buff in order to avoid a raidwide debuff.
1. This fight consists of three chambers - the west chamber, the center chamber, and the east chamber. They are relevant for two reasons - there are adds that are in fixed positions on a timer, and there are stacking buffs on the boss and detriments on players that start partway through the fight.
1. The Adds
   - Roughly 35 seconds into the fight, and every 50s or so after, a red ring will spawn in one of the hallways leading off of the center chamber.
   - At the same time, an add will spawn at the far end of the opposite chamber, locked in place.
   - One group in the raid should be sent to kill the add, and must be back in the main chamber before the next ring spawns.
   - This group should *always* return to the center chamber after killing the add, so they can be ready to go to either of the side chambers when the next add pops.
1. The stacking detriments/buffs
   - There are detriments on the raid and a buff on the boss that will start around 75% on the boss' health.
   - These stacking detriments/buffs are unique to each room.
     - While you stand in the center chamber, both you and the boss will get "center chamber stacks" every 3 seconds UNLESS you have stacks from the side rooms - in which case the center chamber will first decrease your "east" or "west" stacks every 3 seconds until you are at zero. The same is true when you are in side rooms and have "center" stacks on you - they will be removed first before you get "east" or "west" stacks.
   - When you reach zero stacks of any kind, the boss will temporarily become untargetable until the next stack lands on you.
   - If you reach 40 stacks of any one kind, the raid will wipe.
   - If you die, your stacks will be back at zero - which can be dangerous if the raid had just moved into a new room.
1. *Alternate strat for stacks / adds:*
   - *If there is sufficient DPS in the raid, one could consider keeping one group in one side chamber, and the other three groups in the opposite side chamber. Whichever set of folks has the add burns it down when it pops. Burn the named until stacks become high (20-30), kill whatever add is up, then the three-group and one-group parts of the raid swap positions to the opposite chamber.*
1. Ascension related debuffs
   - Periodically in the fight, there will be text indicating that "High Priest Verrkara's scales begin to give off" a glow. When these messages come up, the appropriate ascension class needs to cast a specific spell to avoid a debuff on the raid.
     - "an azure glow" = Siphoned Fervor (Etherealist)
     - "a crimson glow" = Obsidian Mind (Geomancer)
     - "an emerald glow" = Toxic Life (Thaumaturge)
     - "a golden glow" = Thermal Depletion (Elementalist)
   - It is unclear what effect these detriments truly have - some mention ignoring this, others mention there may be some interrupt-related behavior.
1. Scepters
   - There did not appear to be a specific scepter script on this boss.
   - There is no penalty / detriment for using the scepters freely, and they are an excellent source of damage. Ideally use them when the boss is low on stacks for maximum impact, though having a few available as the boss is ramping on stacks can be handy.

**Akhessa Va Liako Vess**

1. One of four bosses that can be selected after killing Diabo Xi Va.
1. Requires the raid to have 18 living players with a Phantastic weapon buff in order to avoid a raidwide debuff.
1. 13 seconds into the pull, and every ~63 seconds after, the boss will emote "Akhessa Va Liako Vess summons for a conduit to channel her magic... It must be desposed of quickly before she can use it to her advantage!"
   - When this happens, a crystal will spawn in the room (somewhere in the main track between where the named started and the door). The black veils in front of the six cubbies in the room will also raise up, revealing a red crystal in the room.
   - One person in the raid will need to go to the crystal, click it to start moving it like a house item, and then place it in the cubby that has the red crystal in it. It is recommended they go all the way into the room and place the crystal at their feet, then immediately back out.
   - Once that person has placed the crystal, they will not be able to see the crystal for the remainder of the pull.
1. Disconcerting Leech detriment (Arcane)
   - Every 45 seconds, there will be an arcane detriment that will land on the raid force called "Disconcerting Leech" which will need to be cured three times as it has three stacks.
   - The important thing here is performing at least one cure within 4-5 seconds of the last group cure - if this doesn't happen, there will be a message indicating that each player who didn't receive a cure is triggering a heal on Akhessa Va Liako Vess, due to having this detriment "applied to them for too long without being dispeled".
1. At every x9% starting at 89%, the boss will disappear and a dark shade of the boss will appear. These do not have many hitpoints and can be burned easily.
1. At {{waypoint 85, 65, 45}}, and 25, the boss will emote that they focus their "mental attacks into the mind of" a single person (tank) on the raid. During that time, the tank will be locked down. The raid needs to burn the boss down to the next x3% (83, 63, 43, 23) rapidly to get the tank loose.
1. Scepters
   - Starting at around 80%, the boss will have a buff that looks like three shields with a black background that starts gaining stacks.
   - This can be removed by using a scepter on the boss.
   - As a twist, the boss will periodically cast a buff called "Dance of the Damned" which will be a purple/black background with a silhouette of a person in it.
     - This detriment causes the boss to do increased damage, so it's ideal to dispel this quickly. It has a long cast time, so a well-timed dispel can make it almost never be on the boss.
     - If a scepter is used on the boss while this is up, it will reflect the damage back to the raid. Make sure this is dispelled before utilizing a scepter on the boss.

**Thall Xundraux Diabo**

1. One of four bosses that can be selected after killing Diabo Xi Va
1. Stifle rune is recommended
1. Three tank boss - one tank for the main named (Thall Xundraux Diabo), and one tank each for the "Va Dyn Shadow Striker" (green), "Va Dyn Shadow Scrapper" (red) additional mobs.
   - Fight the main named in the hallway.
   - The "striker" named (green) must be pulled to the green room (right doorway, facing into the room)
     - Heavy mana drain in green room
   - The "scrapper" named (red) must be pulled into the red room (left doorway, facing into the room)
1. During the fight, the "striker" and "scrapper" will swap names/colors - when this occurs, the tanks handling the adds will need to swap rooms.
   - This occurs around fixed percentages - 90, 75, 50, 25.
   - The tanks holding the adds will lose target when these changes occur, and should immediately start running to their new room.
   - The main named appears to stoneskin while this is occurring, and may heal up if the two extra nameds stay out of their rooms.
   - There's a message of "The battle resumes!" when the adds are properly in position.
1. Starting at 80%, on about a 55 second timer, the curse "Smoldering Stare" will be placed on the tank.
   - In order for this become curable, the tank must target themselves.
1. Scepters
   - No known scepter mechanics for this fight.

**Emperor Ssraeshza**

1. Final boss in the raid zone.
1. Positioning
   - Tanks should be in front, the rest of the raid should be directly behind and within 5 meters of the boss.
   - There are times (unclear on trigger) where the boss "looks in the direction of <tank> in preparation for an acidic spray!" which is the frontal acid spray. Definitely occurs at 95% as a fixed percentage.
1. Two different curable curses during the fight
   - Ghastly Strangle curse - cure immediately
   - Scatterstorm curse
     - At 90%, 75%, 55%, 35% - three people in the raid will get the curse "Scatterstorm" and must joust 15+ meters away *from all other characters including each other*, and wait for the curse to expire (15 seconds). Failure to get away from people = people nearby die.
1. Archetype Challenge Script / Adds
   - At 85%, 60%, 40%, and 20% - four symbols will appear in the room, near the "points" of the symbol on the ground. One character of each archetype must go to their respective symbol and click it, and they will be teleported to where they must each kill an add. Once the add is dead, run to the middle of the room to port back up.
     - Scout = Two Crossed Swords
     - Fighter = Shield
     - Healer = Green Plus
     - Mage = Lightning Bolt
   - At 85%/60%/40%, it is the raid's choice on which characters go for each archetype.
     - Accompanied by a message like "Portals have opened to the Emperor's challenges, he will allow you to choose your challengers… You have 45 seconds to claim and defeat all 4 challenges.
     - At 85% and 60%, you have 45 seconds to complete the challenge.
     - At 40% you have 35 seconds to complete the challenge.
   - At 20% the boss will put an uncurable curse on a set of characters who the boss chooses to do the adds. There is a message shortly before the uncurables land of "The challengers have been chosen and you have [25] seconds to complete all challenges or pay the price of failure."
1. Scepters
   - Between 60% and 50%, the boss will need to gain five stacks of scepter buffs. These are removed at 50% ("Emperor Ssraeshza sheds some of his transformation scars.").
     - Failure to get the boss to 5 stacks of "Transformation Scars" will result in a red message indicating "Emperor Ssraeshza has no need to shed transformation scars and instead focuses his energy into destroying your will... to live!"
1. Other notes / items that may need clarification.
   - There was mention of a stun/root curse at 35%, potentially with recommendations of using a stun rune, AoE blockers, and/or a signet of slippery feet. The stun/root curse at 35% would line up with the "Scatterstorm" curse at 35% as well, which would make scatterstorm extra deadly.
   - There was also mention of a "bubble"/blue fire of some kind around 60% and jousting opposite it (along with having the folks doing the archetype challenges not come back up until this is complete).
