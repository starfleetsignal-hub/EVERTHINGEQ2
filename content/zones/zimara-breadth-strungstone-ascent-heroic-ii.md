## Walkthrough

1. Kill [[Rubblethrong]] {{waypoint 634, 206, -518}} to make the teleporter activate. Have your tank stand at approximately {{waypoint 660.56, 207.66, -555.56}} and group stand with backs to the nook at approximately {{waypoint 668.22, 209.40, -545.15}}. These positions helps mitigate his knockback while keeping scouts in close enough range to backstab. He has rampant aggression stacks, so tank class must keep aggro at all times, or group will wipe when the stack gets too high. He summons adds, but otherwise is a tank and spank.

2. Use the  at {{waypoint 647, 207, -461}} to teleport to Claved Fate Islets. The crystal deposits you at {{waypoint 709, 176, 268}}

3. Kill [[Bolagehera]]. He roams around {{waypoint 751, 186, 343}} He summons adds, teleports players at random, and also has rampant aggression. Be sure to dispel Mortal Coil upon any player deaths.

4. Kill [[Barlanka]]. He roams around {{waypoint 536, 282, 542}}. Trial Guardian's are part of his encounter and will summon all mobs in area to him, so clear area first of trash. The adds have a frontal stun that stacks. Try to keep behind them or burn them down. Tier 2 stun rune or higher. He will place a detriment on a random group member called Test of Magic which must be intercepted by the tank or the player will die when the detriment wears off. He also has Rampant Aggression.

5. Use the crystal shard at {{waypoint 342, 402, 599}} to 'Traverse the expanse' to Sprungstone Peaks. The shard deposits you at {{waypoint 217, 379, 651}}

6. Kill [[Crisj'Jen the Bold]] {{waypoint 88, 383, 727}}. This mob knocks back and teleports group members randomly. Also has a stun/stiffle (T2 Runes didn't seem to work). Only the tank can target the adds, so group members must target through the tank to kill them. Burn the adds as she will continuously summon more making her take less damage. She also has Rampant Aggression.

7. Kill [[Fuejenyrus]] {{waypoint -247, 434, 865}}. Mob has both Knock back and teleports players at random. Fight with backs towards one of the pillars. Burn down the 4 cardinal winds (North, South, East, West). It will summon Sacred Frost adds which can only be targeted through the tank. Periodically it will cast a detriment on a group member called Titanic Frost which must be intercepted by the tank, or the player will die when timer when the detriment wears off.
