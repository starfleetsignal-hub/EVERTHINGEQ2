This zone is accessed during and after finishing the [[Skyshrine Timeline|Access quest series]]. It takes the form of a red and blue portal at -33.93, -0.41, -7.88 in the Skyshrine center area.

## Faction Merchant

[[Quartermaster Zrek]] in Covenant District, Marketplace

## Mercenaries

there are 3 Archon [[Mercenaries#Skyshrine Mercenaries (LU63)|Mercenaries]] for hire, one in each District: Inq, Brigand, Monk.

## Geography

Same layout to [[Skyshrine: The City of Dracur]].

The paths connecting the outer circles are divided into three sectors, each covering 120 degrees. Access to each sector is **ONLY** possible through the **central inner circle**. Those paths are blocked in outer circle.

### Travel

On foot and disable mount

Use [[Evac]] to return center faster
