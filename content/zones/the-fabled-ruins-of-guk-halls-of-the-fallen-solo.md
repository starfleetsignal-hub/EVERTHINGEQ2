## Progression

### [[An Ancient Fungoid (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|An Ancient Fungoid]]

- Clear your way down to [[An Ancient Fungoid (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|An Ancient Fungoid]] {{waypoint 108, -34, -193}} and slay him.

### [[Rideepa the Prideful (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|Rideepa the Prideful]]

- Near where you killed the [[An Ancient Fungoid (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|Ancient Fungoid]], there is a clicky mushroom hidden in a bush at {{waypoint 96, -35, -207}}. Click the mushroom to receive **pure fungus**.
- In the center of the dark pool in the first room of the zone {{waypoint 69, -40, -112}}, right click on the **small idol** to "Pour the fungus you gathered on the idol". Doing this will open the door at {{waypoint 105, -32, -116}}.  Look above the pool and kill the blob mobs there!
- [[Rideepa the Prideful (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|Rideepa]] is located in front of a squat statue {{waypoint 173, -62, -242}} that you need to move to the top of the **peculiar pedestal** back in the [[Chamber of Afterlife (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|Chamber of Afterlife]] {{waypoint 7, -32, -96}}.
- There are two adds  to start, [[A gaz devotee (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|a gaz devotee]] and [[A gaz fanatic (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|a gaz fanatic]]; during the fight, [[Rideepa the Prideful (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|Rideepa]] will emote, summoning help.

### [[Froppit the Everliving (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|Froppit the Everliving]]

- Move the totem from {{waypoint 172.00, -62.17, -246.03}} back to the "pool" room and place it on the on the pedestal at {{waypoint 8, -25, -100}}.
- Head back to the now-opened gate at {{waypoint 39, -39, -169}}
- [[Froppit the Everliving (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|Froppit]] is located on a platform at the back of the [[Font of the Everliving]] {{waypoint -75.50, -47.59, -246.44}}. He has a couple of adds.
- You may wish to bypass the stairs. Coming from Froppit's chamber are a continuous stream of respawning [[A reanimated unstable ghoul|unstable ghouls]]. Go around the curved hall the long way.

### [[Molinap the Destructor (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|Molinap the Destructor]]

- Move up to the puzzle at {{waypoint -54.88, -42.99, -258.12}}.  You must click the three blocks in order to open the door behind you. There is no set sequence.  When you push in a block and it stays pushed in, that was a correct block.  If they slide back out, try a different combo.
- [[Molinap the Destructor (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|Molinap]] is a drake, sitting atop his treasure hoard at  {{waypoint -78.56, -31.14, -388.50}}.
- Kill [[A deadly cadavodile (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|deadly cadavodiles]] in the pool at the bottom of the room, then retrieve a [[Shiny Golden Trinket]] from a chest {{waypoint -22, -72, -326}}.
- Head up the path (it circles the room a couple of times as it rises) and turn right at the intersection at the top {{waypoint -10.04, -32.21, -373.19}}.
- Use the [[Shiny Golden Trinket]] in this fight when [[Molinap the Destructor (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|Molinap]] emotes.

### [[A Ghoul Usurper (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|A Ghoul Usurper]]

- [[A Ghoul Usurper (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|A Ghoul Usurper]] is located at {{waypoint 152, -64, -256}}.
- Clear the assorted ghoul frogloks. *Watch out for the roaming froglok knights, they appear to walk a little then teleport, and they move fast. It's unfortunately possible to have one land right on you out of nowhere.*
- Move three **cadaverous body of a cursed Guk froglok** to the following locations:
  - {{waypoint -24.31, -58.59, -254.90}}
  - {{waypoint 107.68, -63.20, -250.61}}
  - {{waypoint 151.99, -63.45, -258.65}}
  - The named spawns right by the final pedestal as soon as you place the body upon it.

### [[Lord Kurpep (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|Lord Kurpep]]

- [[Lord Kurpep (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|Lord Kurpep]] is located at {{waypoint 124, -40, -386}}
- He periodically summons several [[A summoned shin knight squire (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|summoned shin knight squires]] and [[A shin knight squire (Fabled Guk Halls of the Fallen Solo)|shin knight squires]].
