## Background

The Underpaw Clan has awoken deep below [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den|Splitpaw Lair]]. [[Fral Fipfoot]] of the Splitpaw Clan has asked you to infiltrate one of their hideouts and steal any information that might help to understand their intent.

## Walkthough

*See: [[Fur Fetching for Fral (Flammable Fur)]]*

## Notes

- Like all Splitpaw Saga zones, the monsters scale to match your level, between 20 and 50.
- This instance has no [[Persistent Instances|persistence]] or success/fail lockout; if you die you will respawn in [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den|Splitpaw Den]] and can immediately reattempt the instance with all the monsters respawned.
