## Bosses

### [[Jaled Dar]]

#### Jaled Dar encounter strategies

*Currently (as of 2026) this fight cannot be solo'd by higher level players, due to the 'possession' mechanic required to damage the boss. 2-3 players minimum seems to be the currently accepted wisdom... this number may go down over time as raw player stats increase, affecting potential output of posessed dragons.  The fight, however, is still MUCH quicker if utilizing a full 5-6 player team.*

- To defeat this boss, players in the group must defeat then *"possess"* one of the sets of bones of various members of The First Brood stationed around the perimeter of the crematory pit.  There are 6 different First Brood members to choose from, each may only be possessed by one player at a time.
  - First Brood Warder (Priest)
  - First Brood Protector (Fighter)
  - First Brood Vanguard (Scout)
  - First Brood Sentry (Fighter)
  - First Brood Custodian (Mage)
  - First Brood Servitor (Priest)
- The class/archetype of the player has no bearing on which Dragon should be possessed. Feel free to pick any Dragon you wish. *(... and have fun trying out abilities your class would not normally have access to for this fight!)*
- Once bones are possesed, players' abilites/hotbars are temporarily replaced with abilities that can damage the boss directly or aid fellow 'Dragon-possessing' party members.
  - To 'exit' the possession at any time during the fight, simply 'cancel' the detriment related to the specific possession.
  - *IMPORTANT NOTE: Players outside of dragons cannot interact with possessed dragons via normal party mechanics.  So, no heals or buffs for dragons from external players, the reverse is also true for Dragons, they cannot interact with un-poessessed players. Dragons can only Heal/Buff other dragons, and the same for unpossessed players.*
  - *(You may also 're-possess' a dragon after exiting... which opens up interesting 'alternate strategies' for high-level players wishing to avoid their dragon getting killed during this encounter)*
- Key Abilities to this fight
  - **Rebuke Death** from the Sentry
    - Draconic version of 'Bulwark of Order' - required for this fight, since possessed dragons cannot currently defeat the boss quick enough to avoid the effect, regardless of how high the level/stats of the player doing the posessing is.
  - **Magic Marrow** (Stacking direct damage) From the Protector and Custodian
    - A direct damage spell with a stacking component. The more stacks on the target, the harder each subsequent cast hits for.
    - Fast recast, plus long duration (30s) detriment makes a 'spam til boss dies' a viable strategy, if enough supports in your Dragon party to survive long enough.
- The remaining Brood members form the support to keep the Sentry alive (for Bulwark) and the Magic Marrow stacks (from Protector and Custodian) up on the boss
- As with much of Scars of Destruction, fight-wide power drains are pretty significant during this encounter, both for the possessed players and unpossessed players alike. Several of the Dragons come with power feed abilities to help counter this.

- *Note about the adds:* When the dungeon was originally introduced, it was intended that the possessed dragons deal with the adds via their AoE abilities.  The possessed Dragons are only moderately powerful in relation to the adds, and are much more vulnerable to the hits/damage from adds.
  - In modern times, however, with much higher stats and survivability - having one player remain 'outside' of a dragon to defeat the adds, solo, protecting the remaining possessed dragons during the fight is now a viable strategy.
  - They still won't be able to damage the boss while outside of a dragon, but it could arguably be an easier method to keep the rest of the possessed party safe - so long as players 'outside' their dragon don't get too close to the boss.
  - Because of the mechanism for entering/exiting these dragon forms, a player with access to power regeneration (hearts, shards, power-feed mercs, etc) and/or heals could simply cancel their possession, exiting the dragon, defeat the adds, then re-enter their dragon before it wanders back to its respawn point.
