## Background

The frogloks of [[The Hidden Village of Kugup|Kugup]] have placed their trust in you. [[Chief Kaorf (Hidden VoK)|Chief Kaorf]] has informed you that a [[Spirit of Marr's Chosen|great froglok spirit]] has been trapped within [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]] by the vile dragon [[Venekor (Spirits of the Lost)|Venekor]]. You must figure out how to penetrate this nigh impregnable bastion of fear, liberate the tapped spirit within and bring [[Mithaniel Marr (God)|Marr's]] justice to the most viscous lizardmen and amygdalans in the service of [[Cazic-Thule]].

## Walkthrough

The objective of this raid instance is to slay everything in sight. Start by clearing the upper terrace with [[Atrius the Watcher]] atop the central pyramid. Once he has been killed clear the inner temple area. Upon the death of [[Qzyk the Conqueror]] at the deepest part of the instance, the entire zone will repopulate with new mobs. Re-clear the inner temple area and make your way back to the upper terrace where [[Venekor (Spirits of the Lost)|Venekor]] will be lying in wait. Slay the dragon and bring freedom to the [[Spirit of Marr's Chosen|spirit of Marr's champion]]!
