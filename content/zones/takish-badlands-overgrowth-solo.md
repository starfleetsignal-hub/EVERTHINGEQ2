## Walkthrough

1. [[Florafang]] {{waypoint 739.10, 58.07, 58.26}}
   - Florafang calls in adds, ports the player back to back sometimes, and has 2 curable noxious detriments.
1. Turn around and run towards the water. There is a giant root on the left side that goes towards ant mobs.  Follow the wall left to find the next named.
1. [[The Gobbler]] {{waypoint 763.45, 59.77, -295.99}}
   - The Gobbler periodically casts power drain and summons adds.
1. Turn back the way you came, and go straight to the vine bridge at {{waypoint 532.17, 46.28, -295.34}} Follow the path up the hill to the left towards the next named. <br>
1. [[Cleavespine]] {{waypoint 468.87, 110.89, -605.97}}
   - Casts lots of power drain abilities, all cureable.
1. Go to back down the hill to {{waypoint 241.03, 88.52, -463.05}} and clear all the [[An angry hippolisk|angry hippolisks]].
1. [[Tawerek]] {{waypoint 174.99, 92.84, -472.95}}
   - On incoming and ~ every 10% throughout the fight, Tawerek has a knockback + interrupt.
   - Spiny Temperament stifles and power drains the player. [[Bloodbound Adamant Resolve]] did not prevent the stifle. Suggested to cure quickly and bring mana pots.
1. Go down into the [[Pond of Renewal]] {{waypoint 140.56, 80.16, -371.31}} and kill all the [[An overgrowth gnawer|overgrowth gnawers]] to spawn the named.
1. [[Leafjaw]] {{waypoint 67.80, 80.17, -300.41}}
   - Leafjaw has a couple of curable power drains.
   - At 80%, 60%, 40%, 20%, and 10%, he will go immune, summon an add, and summon an Orb of Overgrowth that must be killed to remove his immunity.
     - Orbs can spawn at the front and back of the pool, at {{waypoint 141.22, 79.88, -254.37}} or {{waypoint 143.60, 80.31, -380.93}}
     - Emote: Leafjaw emits a strange and powerful magic and summons a powerful orb.
