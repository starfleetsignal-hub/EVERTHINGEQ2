## Background

While questing in [[The Barren Sky]] you came across a lost [[Arcanist Nazoor|Erudite]]. In exchange for revealing the location of the hidden Vaults of El'Arad, the Erudite has tasked you with recovering a precious tome of teleportation. Oddly enough, you must kill his kinfolk in order to recover them...

## Layout

1. Clear out the entire first floor, up to and including [[The Arcane Librarian]]. {{waypoint 101, 0, 111}}
1. Return to [[The Ulteran Spire Room]] {{waypoint -6, -2, 6}}, and kill the [[A Quellithulian portal mage|Quellithulian portal mages]] around the spire.
1. After defeating [[The Messenger of El'Arad]], step into the spire to be teleported to the second floor.
1. Clear out the second floor and then click on the teleporter at {{waypoint 1, 26, -20}}.
1. Clear out the third floor and defeat [[Korgo the Vault Keeper]]. {{waypoint 200, 57, -1}}
1. Teleport back to entrance: {{waypoint 186, 56, -1}}
