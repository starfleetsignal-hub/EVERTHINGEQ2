## Notes

- If [[A bedrock brute]] knocks you to the ground, simply press X to stand back up.

## Strategy

1. Go to the [[Toppled Terrene Terrace]] {{waypoint -292, 37, -222}} and kill 5 [[A living stone|living stones]] to spawn 5 [[Runic Stone|Runic Stones]] at {{waypoint -290, 37, -229}}.
1. Click each [[Runic Stone]] to spawn [[Glimmerstone]] at {{waypoint -289, 37, -226}}.
1. Make a note of where the [[Runic Stone|Runic Stones]] went, because you'll need them during the fight.
   - Glimmerstone cannot be damaged in the circle of stones where he spawns.
1. Attack [[Glimmerstone]] then go activate and kill a [[Runic Stone]], once the stone has been killed you can pull [[Glimmerstone]] out of the circle to damage him. Once he ports back into the stone ring kill another [[Runic Stone]], repeat until he's dead.
1. Climb down to the base of the [[Korascian Cliffs]] and kill [[Slurpgaloop]] at {{waypoint 372, 60, 240}}.
   - Periodically he casts *Leap o' Suction*. He ports away, sucks you to him and does a big damage hit. Try to interrupt?
1. Go to [[The Forest Barrens]] around {{waypoint 96, 55, 331}} and kill five mounds and the [[A vekerchiki drone|vekerchiki drones]] hovering over them over to spawn [[Heaper]] at the top of the ramp leading up to the [[Collapsed Mesa]] {{waypoint 254, 160, 103}}, then kill him.
   - Heaper flies off periodically. Follow him and kill his adds.
   - Heaper apparently gets a buff from the emerald dust from breaking crystals in the [[Forest Barrens]]. You may want to go around and break all the crystals yourself before fighting Heaper to prevent him using them. **If you destroy all of the crystals, Heaper doesn't fly away.**  When destroying a crystal you get a buff called Emerald Dusted Weapon which helps killing the mounds and drones.
   - Or you can dispel or apply a state effect on Heaper to remove his Emerald Dust buff.
1. Click Heaper's mound near {{waypoint 254, 160, 103}} to obtain **sticky vekerchiki mud**.
1. Kill Slurpgaloop at  {{waypoint 375.12, 60.00, 237.88}} or the Muddite lurchers won't spawn.
1. Head to the [[Muddy Lagoon]] {{waypoint -73, 21, 588}}.
1. Kill [[A muddite lurcher|muddite lurchers]], then click the corpses to apply the **sticky vekerchiki mud**. This spawns [[A minimud]], which you must kill.
   - Be sure to pull the [[A muddite lurcher|muddite lurchers]] out of the water before you kill them (the water tries to compost you).
1. Once you have killed enough [[A minimud|minimuds]], [[The Monstrous Mudwalker]] spawns in the center of the water near {{waypoint -88, 15, 695}}.
   - Periodically, the Mudwalker will summon [[A mud mold]] add. Kill the add, then go back to the Mudwalker.
1. Kill [[Koni Ferus]] and [[Pete Bogg]] in the [[Shade Arbour]] by the stone arch near {{waypoint -67, 80, 429}}.
   - Since you will be jousting away from the summoned trees and brambles, you may want to clear the nearby area before pulling the names so you don't aggro anything else.
   - Kill Koni first. Any time she summons a tree, pull both names away from it or it will give them massive heals.
   - Periodically, Pete will summon bramble roots on you. Move away from them. If you are caught by the brambles, target them and kill them to move again.
