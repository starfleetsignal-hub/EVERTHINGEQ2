- Zone in from [[Karak Peak]]. {{waypoint -317, 166, -581}}
- It's recommended that you have 500 crit chance for this zone.

## Walkthrough

Most of the zone is progression related before certain doorways and named are available.

1. Grab the pie in the kitchen {{waypoint -107, -130.7, -35}}, to throw it in [[Bloogly|Blooglys]] face {{waypoint -128, -126, -162}}, then fight him, to receive a safe key.
1. Open the safes in room {{waypoint -78, -128, -124}} to find the blue bar. Grab it to make [[Nurgal]] attackable - kill him.
1. Proceed forward to [[Bigz the Big (Heroic)]] at {{waypoint -61, -150, -235}} and you'll be captured. After the dialogue, break the makeshift wall behind you.
1. Retrieve your weapons from the chest located at {{waypoint -126, -145, -208}} before the hallway leading to [[Zorin the Spirit-taker]]. Kill him.
1. Double back down to the mines and pull the chain at {{waypoint 37.52, -153.51, -246.98}} to debuff [[Bigz the Big (Heroic)]]. Kill him.
1. Proceed down the [[The Pickclaw Mines]] to reach a crushing room with [[Stompus]]. Kill him and a new hole in the wall will appear to the south west.
1. Continue down the new path and you'll come to a small army of goblins inside the last room of the caves. Kill the four waves until just [[Bolgin Serilis (Heroic)]] remains.
1. Bolgin will have some dialogue but will eventually spawn adds and become aggressive. Defeat him to finish the instance.

## Lore and History

- Before [[The Shattering|the Shattering]], **High Keep**, located in Highpass in the tall [[Antonica|Antonican]] mountain range known as the Serpent's Spine, was also sometimes called ***Highkeep Castle, Highpass Hold***, or ***Highhold***. High Keep was established by Sean McCabe and became well known as a gambling resort and spa, equipped with natural bathhouses and mud pits.

- Carson McCabe, Sean's son, eventually came to dominate trade on most of Norrath.  High Keep was the seat of a former empire that spread its power over central [[Antonica]]. The inhabitants of High Keep used their wits and resources to form a thriving center of trade, both legal and illegal, passing through the center of the continent.

- During the rule of Carson McCabe, while adding additional floors under the keep, construction crews tunneled into the Pickclaw goblins, who remain a problem there to the present day.

- The post-Shattering fate of High Keep and its major passage in Antonica, [[Highpass Hold]], was previously unconfirmed; however, some rumors indicate that it was (or is) the secret headquarters of the powerful [[Far Seas Trading Company (Faction)|Far Seas Trading Company (FSTC)]].
