## Notes

At least ONE person must have defeated Primordial Ritualist Villandre V'Zher, Captain Krasnok, Grethah the Frenzied, and Arch Lich Rhag'Zadune to enter this zone. And that person MUST be the one to zone the others in. They cant zone in after said person.

As of 8-3-2023
**Tserrina Sy'Tor**: Just sloooow burn her and kill any adds she spawns first. Then kill her. Should be like 5 adds total that will have to die first, or else shell just keep fully healing.

**[[The Construct of Malice]]**: Requires a "raid" of THREE people in different Raid groups. ONE person has to be a tank. So the easiest way to do this, without any hiccups... From the steps heading into the room. have the tank stand in one of the "middle squares. Using the MAP DIRECTION. plant person 1, one square to the SOUTH and person 2, one square to the NORTH.

When the tank pulls, pull the mob straight back so hes standing in the square directly between the other two players.

Just beat on the construct until the ground bubbles spawn on the other two players and it should die instantly if your dps is high enough. You will NEED to kill the adds first or else he wont die.

Its that easy, just annoying with the script.
