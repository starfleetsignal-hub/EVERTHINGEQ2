## Info

Can be done Solo with a Mercenary, or with 2 players.

## Walkthrough

Destroy the two statues in Temple of Thulian Might and Temple of Thulian Power to be able to kill [[Sarinich (Advanced Solo)]].<br>
When Sarinich is killed the teleporters to the 2nd level open up, the zone reuse timer is set and the collection shinies spawn.<br>
Kill [[Baroddas (Advanced Solo)]] to open up the door to the 3rd level.<br>
Kill [[Phobis (Advanced Solo)]] to open up the door to the waterfall.<br>
Swim down the waterfall. Kill all "a pained wretch" mobs in forest before engaging Venekor.  He will summon all surviving "a pained wretch" mobs to him.
