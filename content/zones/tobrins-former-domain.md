## Background

[[A shade of Tobrin|Tobrin]] was an apprentice of the legendary mage [[Miragul]]. In order to study under Miragul, Tobrin had to remove his own eye as an act of fealty. To cover up the wound, Tobrin created an [[Tobrin's Mystical Eyepatch|eyepatch]] and imbued it with magical powers. [[Master T'Lys]], who you met when you first landed on [[Everfrost]], has entrusted you with this eyepatch's location and contracted you to uncover it. You must enter this domain of Tobrin and recover it.

## Walkthrough

Kill everything in sight. Once you clear one room, a door will open revealing another room with more mobs ripe for the slaughter. Once the final (northern) door is opened, confront [[A shade of Tobrin]] and loot the eyepatch from the chest in the middle of the room. Once you do this, [[Master T'Lys]] and his goons will appear at the exit of the room and attack you. T'Lys will escape, but [[Tobrin's Mystical Eyepatch]] will be yours for safekeeping!

## Notes

- This is a one-time instance associated with the required quest. It can only be reentered if you are grouped with another player on the quest.
