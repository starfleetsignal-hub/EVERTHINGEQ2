## Named Kill order to avoid Bug

Kill [[Maldormous Exeter]] before you kill the [[Mewaye|Mewaye Encounter]] then the portal that takes you to the top to [[Ferzhul]] is active.

Zone in is from [[Brokenskull Rock]] in the [[Moors of Ykesha]] at {{waypoint -1773, -967, 2667}}.
