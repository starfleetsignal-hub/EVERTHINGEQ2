## Background

The Arena of Heroes is a place where Splitpaw Gnolls and any would-be heroes come to test their might in combat against great beasts. Should a new champion arise, they would be rewarded with new armor, weapons and unique vanity items.

## Walkthrough

The zone is a controlled-gauntlet; simply talk to [[Fip Slytail (Drowned Caverns: Arena of Heroes)|Fip Slytail]] to initiate the event, and every time thereafter defeating a challenge. The list of challenges is as such:

1. [[A frenzied cavern lizard]]
1. [[A ravenous tunnel feeder]]
1. [[Grunk "Priest Eater" Smashsnout]]
1. [[A captured evil eye]]
1. [[A zygomyd decay sower]]
1. [[The Murk]]
1. [[Stormbrow]] and [[Stratus]]
1. [[An arena wolf]] x3
1. [[Banetooth Splitclaw]]

## Rewards

Speak to Fip Slytail a final time and select the last dialogue option stating that you would like your prize and would like to return to [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den|Splitpaw Den]]. Kill the [[A treasure chest (Drowned Caverns: Arena of Heroes)|treasure chest]] mimic that spawns at the center of the arena and it will drop an actual [[Treasure Chest]] containing various equipment items and two guaranteed drops:

1. [[Terramite Shard of Transformation]], an illusion which turns you into a Splitpaw Gnoll
1. [[Splitpaw Dreadsnout]], a house-pet

## Notes

- Once you have entered the Arena of Heroes, you may not exit without defeating at least one challenger. If you wish to leave after that, inform Fip and the [[Terraporter]] will become active.
- If anyone in the group is too high, they must [[Mentoring|mentor]] down BEFORE you engage the first challenger or you will have trivialized the Arena and no experience nor treasure will be awarded.
- Completion of this instance is one of the several ways to earn the trust of the Splitpaw Gnolls and advance [[A Darkened Shard]].
- If you're struggling with killing [[The Murk]], here are some suggestions:
  - Fast [[Terms|DPS]]
  - Endurance (wait for about 5 complete heals)
  - Killing the mini-Murks throughout the room, then killing him
    - The mini-Murks will run to the big one and sacrifice themselves when he is at about 20% HP.  He will then be at FULL Health & Power. They do it one at a time, so kill those mini-Murks fast.
