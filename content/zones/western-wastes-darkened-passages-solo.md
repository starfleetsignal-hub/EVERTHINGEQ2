## Walktrough

1. Kill [[Garlahk]] {{waypoint -776, 138, 260}}
   - has a knockback, summons 2 adds at a time
1. take ramp behind Garlahk up to {{waypoint -769, 168, 320}}
1. Kill [[Melacckk]]
   - *Has stun*
1. Kill [[Gorecarve the Flayer]]
   - on the platform next to Melackk; has knockback - stand with your back against wall, ducking helps too
1. use a blue crystal at {{waypoint -693.75, 167.38, 383.59}} to click to "Travel to the Rime Claw Caves"
1. [[Arkanak the Dubious]]
   - has knockback
   - If you are on the [[Delving the Darkened Passages]] quest it is **IMPORTANT** to make sure you blow the horn three times before you kill all the giants.
1. [[Krejian Krusher]]
   - up the ramp behind Arkanak, mind the gap between ledge and Krejian's platform; has knockback, put your back against a wall
