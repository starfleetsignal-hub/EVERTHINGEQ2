## Access

Go to an island off the coast of [[The Pillars of Flame]] at location {{waypoint 1708.68, -189.84, -842.59}} . Click the magic mirror.  There's an epic dragon level 65 near there [[Barakah]].
![Entrance of Fabled Djinn Master's Prism,a nd Flying Carpet Mount from Mythical Wrist item. Love that graphic, too bad the stats are outdated now.](images/Fableddjinnprism.jpg)

## Note:

Talk to the NPCs for lore and to get an idea of the trading system. If you're at max level right now (level 110 gear), this will not be a challenge, but the mobs are green and give loot.  You can see the original document for strategy if you are doing it on level:  [[The Djinn Master's Prism]]  -- however, it's not exactly the same.  You are given a music box and an object that nullifies elemental detriments (see loot below).  The 'trash mobs' don't seem to drop any musical instruments.

## Strategy

### [[Guardian Tyniss (Fabled)]]

- Attacks: Heat, Crushing

### [[Honorguard Maro Joavao]]

- With two [[An Uruvan spirit servant]]
- Attacks: Magic, Crushing

### [[Honorguard Taro Joavao]]

- With two  [[An Uruvan spirit servant]])
- Attacks: Cold, Crushing

### **[[The Djinn Master]]**

- Attacks:
- Rage of the Djinn - Mental (Knockback, Stun, Root AOE)
- Soul Rend - Divine

## Loot

The loot table is probably larger than this, but this is a sampling.

- Crate containing Djinn Master's Head (Guild Hall decoration)
- [[Superior Djinn Bracelet of the Sky]]  - Mythical, when activated summons a mount!
- Purple adornments such as [[Winds of Obol]]
- [[Energized Superior Netherworld Robe]] (traded with NPC for level 95 raid gear)
- [[Superior Argent Blade of Speed]] (random drop from named)

Always drops:  [[Scepter of Unchained Melodies]]  and [[An ornate musicbox]]  (not house items)
