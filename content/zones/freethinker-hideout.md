## Background

This is the former hideout of the vampire-hunting faction, the [[The League of Freethinkers (Faction)|League of Freethinkers]]. Ironically, it has been taken over by powerful vampires and werewolves led by none other than [[Malkonis D'Morte (Freethinker Hideout)|Malkonis D'Morte]]. Under the command of [[Mayong Mistmoore]], Malkonis is searching for the [[Ankh of Ydal (Quest Item)|Ankh of Ydal]], a legendary rune that permanently scarred the face of Mayong in ages past.

## Walkthrough

- **Crucial Warning**: To engage the final boss, [[Malkonis D'Morte (Freethinker Hideout)|Malkonis D'Morte]], you must talk to four NPCs hidden behind breakable walls. These NPCs must be spoken to in a precise order that is randomized with every zone reset. To break the walls, you must place and ignite a barrel next to the wall. There is a limited number of barrels and they do not respawn. If you ignite the barrel next to the incorrect breakable wall, no NPC will be there and you will not be able to engage the final boss.![Doodaddle's Remains](images/Doodaddles_remains.jpg) To know which breakable wall has the correct NPC behind it, you must use [[Doodaddle's Soul Sensor]] while standing next to it. Doodaddle's Soul Sensor is looted from a maroon-colored skeleton named, 'Doodaddle's Remains', which spawns randomly on the ground in either of the two passageways. While using Doodaddle's Soul Sensor in front of a breakable wall, if you get a notice saying 'The sensor is silent', that is the incorrect wall - do not ignite the barrel. If you get a message saying that you hear something on the other side, that is the correct breakable wall - it is safe to ignite the barrel. You will get a fresh barrel in a correctly chosen room. As a safety precaution, do not ever left-click the barrel. Only ever right-click the barrel and you can safely choose to either move it or ignite it.
  - **Furthermore**, this breakable-wall step must be done before you kill the third boss, [[Treyloth D'Kulvith]]. If you kill Treyloth before talking to the NPCs, Malkonis will laugh and fly away.
- Follow these directions exactly:
  1. Before killing any boss, search the passageways for 'Doodaddle's Remains'.
  1. Once found, use the Soul Sensor next to each breakable wall until you find the correct one and remember it.
  1. In the room with the first boss, [[Zylphax the Shredder]], you are given the first barrel. After killing Zylphax, right-click the barrel and move it the correct breakable wall and ignite it.
  1. Speak to the NPC in the room and click through all their dialogue.
  1. Search for the next correct breakable wall.
  1. When found, return to the previous room, pick up the barrel and move it to the correct breakable wall and ignite it.
  1. Repeat this process until all four walls have been broken in the correct order and all NPCs have been spoken to. Malkonis will no longer fly away after Treyloth has been killed.
  1. Kill [[Othysis Muravian]].
  1. Kill [[Treyloth D'Kulvith]].
  1. Click the painting at {{waypoint 114, 3, -52}}.
  1. Click the lever behind it.
  1. Kill [[Malkonis D'Morte (Freethinker Hideout)|Malkonis D'Morte]].

## Strategy Walkthrough

1. When you enter the zone there will be three werewolves directly ahead of you. Have two people click the bone piles on either side of the front room simultaneously to make some of the wolves go away and then pull what is left. If you don't do this you end up with 4 or 5 werewolves at one time.
1. [[Zylphax the Shredder]]: Pull to the corner as he has a frontal power drain. When he says "Oops I dropped something" you have about 4 seconds to be as far away as you can to avoid a nasty poison AOE called Stench of Death. This AOE hits very fast. IMPORTANT: leave the barrel alone until you locate Doodaddle's Remains (anywhere in the hallways) and loot the sensor.
1. Go to the south hallway, be prepared for trash to come in groups of 4 or 5 at one time. When you get to the last room stay far left when you enter to avoid aggro.
1. [[Othysis Muravian]]: Every 30 seconds or so she will summon a heroic add who will heal her. She will also cast Prone to Corruption on a member of the raid, if they don't die from the spell it will spawn an epic x2 add. Have OT pick these up and kill
1. Go to north hallway, same type of trash as south. Last named will not start dialogue or become aggro until everyone is in his room.
1. [[Treyloth D'Kulvith]]: If you pile into the close corner to the right as you enter he will not aggro until pulled. He will memwipe at 75%, 50%, and 25%. He casts Reaching Rot every 34 seconds or so which is an AOE that hits anything outside of 10m. He continuously casts Searing Rot which is an AOE that hits anything within 5m. Try to stay between those two to take minimum damage. He also puts an arcane detrimental on the raid which must be cured or your spells reflect.
1. [[Malkonis D'Morte (Freethinker Hideout)|Malkonis D'Morte]]: To spawn the final name, you must have followed the script for the zone (see above). Fight him next to the wall to avoid the knockup, which will spawn a vampire bat. Throughout the fight, Malkonis calls for help from his ancestors, and one of the six coffins in the room will begin to glow. Attack the glowing coffin (using an arrow or other ranged item) to avoid a vampire add. Beware that the coffin to explode when it is destroyed, doing damage to the group.
