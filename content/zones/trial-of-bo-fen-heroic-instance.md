## Notes

- Once you are inside you will automatically receive the quest [[Trial of Bo Fen: Heroic (Quest)]]. Upon it's completion or deletion, you will be teleported from this instance back to the [[Tower of the Four Winds]].
- Mercenaries can be summoned in this instance.
