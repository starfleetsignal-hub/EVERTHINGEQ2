Zone in is at {{waypoint 63.61, 13.37, 962.53}}

## Walkthrough

1. You will end up taking most trash as you head to the Names, but you don't have to CLEAR .
1. Kill Mugulg, the Quartermaster (96^^) near the Quarterdeck.
1. Kill [[Chef Blarghrot (Heroic)|Chef Blarghrot (96^^)]]. He has some moderate AoE-attacks that can kill squishy mercs if they are in range.
1. Kill [[Captain Goldjaw (Advanced Solo)|Captain Goldjaw (96^^)]].
   - if you're on the access quest: loot the [[Sea Chest Key]] from the Captain's body.
   - then loot the chest in the Captain's room at {{waypoint -13, 34, -102}}.
   - then loot the "shiney" downstairs starboard side in a cabin {{waypoint 10.42, 26.12, -92.71}}(another interesting object).
1. Kill [[The Tempest of Zek]] (96^^).

The Fights are all laughably easy, so no tactics tips needed. This is an opinion.
