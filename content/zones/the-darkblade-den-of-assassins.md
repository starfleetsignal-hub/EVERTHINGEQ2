## Notes

- When entering this very small instance you will notice two groups of [[A Darkblade assassin|Darkblade assassins]]. Destroy them. Afterwards, two named mobs will spawn: [[Zaynis V'rix]] and [[Sinyaz V'rix]]. Both are grouped together, level 10^^ heroic, and usually drop legendary gear.
- Players used to have to complete [[Darkblades and Dirty Rats]] to access this instance.
