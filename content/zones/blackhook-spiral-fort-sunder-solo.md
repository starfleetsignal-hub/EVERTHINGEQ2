## Walktrough

1. Clear trash mobs until a purple message appears and gate switches can be clicked at {{waypoint 310, 178, -448}}.
1. Continue to clear mobs until the next gate can be opened.
1. Click the gate switch at {{waypoint 312, 177, -390}}
1. Kill Argh the Anvil at {{waypoint 303, 179, -345}} Don't forget to collect your shiney.
1. Proceed to the Great lift and throw the lever at {{waypoint 272, 179, -437}} to lower the lift, get on and then raise the lift.
1. Kill Igarn the Insurmountable and Winx the Unmountable who patrol the path around {{waypoint 133, 225, -365}}
1. Kill Zeraxyl at {{waypoint 53, 211, -489}} and collect your shiney.
1. Ride the mount at {{waypoint 52, 211, -489}} to the Isle of the Forgotten.
1. Kill Tajara the Vengeful
   - *She spawns at around {{waypoint -480.67, 330.84, -709.74}} and goes counterclockwise around the outer part of the island and then to the middle*
1. Ride the mount at {{waypoint -369, 320, -652}} to Harmok HQ.
1. Kill Harmok the Hammer {{waypoint -21, 182, -421}}
   - Has a very powerful knock back, and adds. Hiding in the forge helps with knockback, but adds additional damage. There's a small nook under one of the containers full of purple items.
