## Info

Must be level 92 to enter.

Has 3 floors, you can move between them using the round teleporters.<br>
The Recall Obelisks teleport you to the entrance of the 1st floor (regardless of which floor you're on).<br>
There are clickable objects (Vases, chests, usually inside the larger rooms) and monster-drops that each give a quest. The reward for those is 1 Obol and 2 plat.
