## Notes

- [[Renfry's Basement]] is a tradeskill instance, the entrance is in [[Forlorn Gist]] at {{waypoint -359.67, 14.22, 74.06}}
- Quest givers [[Renfry]] (Daily Tradeskill Mission) and [[Igore]] (Weekly Tradeskill Mission) are just inside.
- Contains Fuel Merchant [[Ziggy]] in the far corner {{waypoint -20.89, 0.02, 14.37}}![](images/Ziggy.png)
- Furniture merchant [[Grimalda Goodhand]] is around the corner {{waypoint -12, 0, 25}}![](images/Grimalda_Goodhand.png)
- Contains *Rickty Crafting Table* {{waypoint -16.44, 0.02, 19.36}} that functions as an all in one craft station identical to Varig's Forge.  Once the [[Visions of Vetrovia Crafting Timeline|Visions of Vetrovia Tradeskill Signature Quests]] have been completed, it transforms into a [[Far Seas Crafting Station]].  ![](images/Rickty_craft_table.png) ![](images/Far_Seas_Crafting_Station.png)
