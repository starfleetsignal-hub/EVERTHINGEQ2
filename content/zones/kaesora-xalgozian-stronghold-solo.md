Notes:

- Named spawns in sequence (one does not "pop" until the previous is down).
- There are two timed quests:
  - Spirit Shepherd - click on the spirit to make it follow you - you might want to clear first because it will not follow you to an area where there is a named up. So you must clear as far as Janosz. (Spirit= -150, 33, 483; 1st Altar {Purple}= -329, -13, 520; 2nd Altar {Yellow}= -563, 3, 281; Return to start)
  - Putting Souls to Rest -- attack any "restless souls" (20-25 each) above the cavern with the broken bridge around -437.23 11.47 120.28 -- careful, they will fear you and you might fall off the edge.
- The final groups of mobs before the Over-General Vihgoh can chain-stun you, so if you're not one shotting them, take them a group at a time.
