## Notes

- Zone from [[Wracklands]] using the portal at {{waypoint 727.46, 77.35, 696.05}}
- What to bring: Water breathing
- Estimated time at level 120: 15-20 Minutes; three named mobs (trash-named-trash-named-named).

## Zone Walkthrough

1. Open the tunnels and clear the 4 golems [[A Ssraeshzian juggernaut]] at the end of each tunnel.
1. [[Rhag'Sekez]] rooms in the "corridor" at the border of the zone.
   - Is an easy tank and spank
   - During fight, the named summoning crystals nearby. Move to half way between them so effects of each blue and red crystal cancel out.
1. Clear the new spawned trashmobgroups of snakes ([[A Ssraeshzian king cobra]] and [[A Ssraeshzian predator]]) to trigger the second named.![Snake Group](images/ASsraeshzianKingCobraANDASsraeshzianPredatorGroup.jpg)
1. Defeat [[Rhag'Voreth]]
   - Easy tank and spank. Summons braziers with different colored orbs.  As the fight progresses, named mob's scales will turn a color (orange, green, purple, silver). Drag named between the two braziers that will make the corresponding color.  When the next color is called, braziers will turn into adds.  Can ignore or AoE down.
   - Note: **After** defeating [[Rhag'Voreth]] the room immediately fills up with water. You will have some time to get to the air bubble. It is suggested to have a water breathing buff.
1. [[Arch Nemesis Rhag'Nazza]] spawns at the top of the pyramide..
   - Typical tank and spank with no difficult script.
   - Note: It will be invulnerable on top of the platform or near the surface of the water. So take it under water and kill it there.
   - Note: There is a large air bubble you can enter if you don't have water breathing.
