## Background

You found a peculiar lamp on the shore of [[Pirate's Perch]] in the [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]] and a seemingly mad djinn appeared. After some inquiry about how he became trapped in such a place, the djinn admits that it must kill you yet can allow you a choice on how. You must enter his gauntlet and defeat the trial to be set free.

## Notes

- A player entering here for the first time will be offered the quest, [[The Gauntlet of al-Azhar]].
- After zoning in, speak again to [[Khalhan al-Azhar]] to begin the trial and advance the quest.
- This instance has no persistence or lockout and thus may be completed as many times as desired.
