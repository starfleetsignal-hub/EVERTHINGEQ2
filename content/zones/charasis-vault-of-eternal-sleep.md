## Background

This is the tomb of [[Emperor Ganak]], great-grandson of [[Venril Sathir]] and final emperor of the [[Iksar Empire]]. He met his fate over the [[Field of Bone]] where both he and [[Jaled Dar's Shade|Jaled Dar]], leader of the Ring of Scale, were killed in a final confrontation. Years after his death, his soul was cursed, placed in a phylactery, and locked away in a booby-trapped tomb filled with unliving servants to guard it for all eternity.

## Notes

- **Warning:** This instance has invisible pitfalls hidden all over the ground, indistinguishable from the stone floor. As you walk over them, you will fall into a pit where you will be attacked by [[A haunting spirit]]. Use the clickable platform to get out of them. If you fall through the same one that you just left, wait several seconds for the platform to descend before using it to get back out. Hugging the walls is a good way to avoid falling into these traps.
- The teleporter at {{waypoint 44, -5, -247}} will take you to outside the entrance of the instance.

## Walkthrough

1. Jump through the painting to fall down.
1. Go to the north-east room and kill [[The Keeper of Dreams]] {{waypoint -43, -9, -173}}
1. Kill [[The Keeper of Nightmares]] in the south-west room {{waypoint 151, -6, -69}}
1. Go to the north room {{waypoint 80, -5, -240}} and use the crank next to the door to get inside.
1. In this room {{waypoint 55, -5, -274}} are 4 golems. At 0% health they become invulnerable. One must click the scroll in the room that corresponds with the golem name in order to kill them.
1. The blue **Crystal of Dreams** and the purple **Crystal of Nightmares** can be found on the pillars next to the [[Guardian of Eternity]]. Defeating the guardian is optional.
1. Place the blue **Crystal of Dreams** on the star pedestal in the north-east room [[Lock of Dreams]]{{waypoint -43, -9, -173}}.
1. Place the purple **Crystal of Nightmares** on the star pedestal in the south-west room [[Lock of Nightmare]] {{waypoint 151, -6, -69}}.
1. This will unlock the door to the center room {{waypoint 27.33, -6.62, -183.04}} ([[Altar of the Dragonslayer]]), where [[Praetor of the Phylactery]] awaits.
   - Upon engaging the Praetor, it will spawn a few adds (coming from its left and right side). If the adds reach him, he heals 20%, so root them or otherwise engage them until the Named drops. The Praetor does have a leash point, so don't back too far off the main platform he stands upon.
