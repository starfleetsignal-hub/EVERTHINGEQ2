## Notes

- There are LOTS of detriments in this zone. If you can't cure frequently, bring a player or merc along that can.
- Cats are the "merchants" in this bazaar.
- Click on [[Bazaar rat|bazaar rats]] and [[Haven hopper|haven hoppers]] to pick them up whenever you see one.
- You will ultimately need 14 [[Bazaar rat|bazaar rats]] and 5 [[Haven hopper|haven hoppers]].
- The various "Hawkers" (hawks flying around the main room) will trade you a [[Bazaar rat]] for body-dropped loot from various creatures.
- Any time you engage [[A deadly darkbloom]], right-click and choose Feed to feed it a frog. Kill the plant and collect a [[Blackened frog leg]] from its corpse.
- You will ultimately need 5 [[Blackened frog leg|blackened frog legs]] from [[A deadly darkbloom]].
- [[A deadly darkbloom]] and netherbians respawn.
- [[Fordel Midst: Bizarre Bazaar (Solo)#Repeat Visits|Repeat Visits]]: After following the strategies and completing the zone successfully, you may skip buying the items from the cat merchants on subsequent runs by hailing [[Percy]], a cat that sits in front of you after zoning in.

## First Walkthrough

- Note: After your first walktrough you can shortcut (see below).
- Note: Most items are temporary flagged. So if you leave zone, you have to start from the beginning.
1. Speak to [[Sammy]] {{waypoint -277.90, 0.34, 114.69}} at [[The Bizarre Bazaar]]
   - Buy a [[Money Bag]] for a [[Bazaar rat]].
1. Defeat [[Financier Fendilix]] {{waypoint -272.45, -1.51, 33.07}} at [[Bizarre Bank & Loan]]
   - 'A bear market?' trauma feign death, 'x' to stand up
   - 'Let's divide the profits' splits: Kill add first
   - Loot a [[Ball of ethereal yarn]] from his corpse.
1. Speak to [[Boris]] at {{waypoint -189.97, -0.61, 102.22}} at [[The Bizarre Bazaar]]
   - Buy [[30 Grit Sand Rune]] for [[Ball of ethereal yarn]] and a [[Bazaar rat]].
   - Buy a [[Key of Articulation]] for a [[Bazaar rat]].
   - Right-click on [[30 Grit Sand Rune]] and choose 'auto consume'. (If you're doing this with another person, it's best to stand right next to the person and use the charm, then auto-consume it. It seems to not land on the person if they're more than 5m away, or if the charm is just auto-consumed without using first. Works the same with the upgraded version, and the same with both in heroics.)
1. Move the mannequins from {{waypoint -175.70, -2.75, 109.13}} and {{waypoint -200.84, -1.48, 46.68}} and place them near {{waypoint -119.79, -1.49, 60.60}}
   - The female mannequin goes on the left and the male on the right. You can only carry 1 at a time. Once correctly placed they turn into **a fixed mannequin**.
   - Click on each and 'Use key' to spawn [[Mandee Quin]] and [[Mannee Quin]].
1. Defeat [[Mandee Quin]] and [[Mannee Quin]].
   - Loot [[A rough mannequin leg]].
1. Speak to [[Motor]] at {{waypoint -117.26, -0.60, 103.62}}
   - Buy [[Lucky Horseshoe (Bauble)|Lucky Horseshoe]] for [[A rough mannequin leg]] and a [[Bazaar rat]].
1. Defeat [[Short Shift]] {{waypoint -60.67, -1.74, 218.81}} at [[Short Shift Stables]].
   - Right-click the [[Lucky Horseshoe (Bauble)|Lucky Horseshoe]] and choose 'use' whenever Short Shift's stoneskin is up (blue and purple detriment). Charm has a short range, and if you're with another person who used the charm, there is a notice in chat that says something like you no longer feel the luck of that person. Just move closer to them. You'll see a notice the effect is reapplied when you're within range of the horseshoe carrier. Works the same with the upgraded version, and the same with both charms in heroics.
   - 'The battle is making [[A ghostly carrier]] angsty. Soothe it somehow.' Pat it down to soothe it.
1. Speak to [[Trade Baroness Elsindir]] {{waypoint -41.91, -1.47, 49.08}} at [[The Bizarre Bazaar]]
   - Buy a [[Bazaar Trade Agreement]] for [[Lucky Horseshoe]] and [[Money Bag]].
1. Speak to [[Mausi]] {{waypoint -49.72, 0.35, 112.79}}
   - Buy [[Cat's Eye Agate]] for the [[Bizarre Trade Agreement]] and 2 [[Bazaar rat|bazaar rats]].
1. Clear all the creatures in the area in preparation for the next fight vs.[[Trade Baroness Elsindir]], which has become attackable.
1. Defeat [[Trade Baroness Elsindir]] {{waypoint -41.91, -1.47, 49.08}}.
   - Periodically 'Your Cat's Eye Agate begins to glow!' (as she casts "Monopoly of Souls"). Use it to interrupt her death touch "Monopoly of Souls"...**or you are dead**.
   - Periodically you get cursed by "Trade Route" ![Trade Route Curse](images/Effect_traderoute.jpg)Then you do **much less damage** to [[Trade Baroness Elsindir]] (Hint: Reactive damage spells e.g. [[Repentance]] still do damage in the normal way). You must search and click flags and pick them up to remove her damage immunity again. These flags can spawn between the wall next to the broken column all the way over to the area between [[Mausi]]'s and [[Motor]]'s huts.
   - Hint: Sometimes she'll cast "Monopoly of Souls" in the middle of an active "Trade Route". It is a good idea to re-target her between flags to catch her casting, and more importantly, to interrupt the "Monopoly of Souls" than to finish the Trade Route.
   - Collect [[Bazaar Trade Agreement]] from her corpse.
1. Locate [[Mia]] (trackable), a black cat wandering through the bazaar, and speak to her and buy:
   - [[9 Lives (Level 120) (Version 2)|9 Lives]] for 1 [[Blackened frog leg]] and 1 [[Bazaar rat]] ((9 trigger death save))
   - [[20 Cat's paw]] for 1 [[Bazaar rat]] ((launches into air))
   - [[Chase the Cat]] for 1 [[Bazaar rat]] ((teleport to Mia)
   - [[Lucky Horseshoe (Bauble)|Lucky Horseshoe]] for 1 [[Bazaar rat]]
   - [[Spirit of the Cat]] 1 rat ((shape change to cat)
   - [[Upgraded 30 Grit Sand Rune]] for 1 [[Blackened frog leg]] and 1 [[30 Grit Sand Rune]] ((short range limit on buff))
   - [[Upgraded Lucky Horseshoe]] for 1 [[Blackened frog leg]] and [[Lucky Horseshoe (Bauble)|Lucky Horseshoe]] ((short range limit on buff))
   - Note: If you duo, you need only on [[Spirit of the Cat]] as you can trade it.
1. Proceed to {{waypoint 108.07, -1.76, 113.88}} and use [[Spirit of the Cat]] to pass the ghostly orange giant.
1. Speak to [[Spirit]] at {{waypoint 264.10, 0.50, 119.64}}.
   - Buy [[Paludal Catnip]] for a [[Bazaar rat]]
     - [[Paludal Catnip]] dispels 1000 levels of any hostile effect on group members.
1. Right-click on [[Upgraded 30 Grit Sand Rune]] and choose 'auto consume'.
1. Proceed to Last Stand of Fordel Midst at {{waypoint 303, 0, -133}} and follow the hallway down to the arena.
1. Defeat [[Bazaar Baron Brixwald]], wandering the arena.

- Note: It's a good idea to either have hotbar spots or macro [[Paludal Catnip]], [[Upgraded Lucky Horseshoe]], and [[Cat's Eye Agate]].
- Use [[9 Lives (Level 120) (Version 2)|9 Lives]] charm before pull.
- He has a knockback; pull him against a wall.
- If you fall into one of the holes use [[Cat's Paw (Level 120)|20 Cat's Paw]] to get out or use the stone as a step.
- If 'Your [[Cat's Eye Agate (Level 120)|Cat's Eye Agate]] begins to glow!'. Use it to interrupt death touch. Spell "Soul Espionage."
- Use [[Paludal Catnip]] Dispels 1000 levels of any hostile effects on group members (AE). Spell "Public Scorn".
- Use [[Upgraded Lucky Horseshoe]] grants immunity to most Stun, Stifle effects, and knockbacks. (blue and purple detriment)

**Note**: He will sometimes cast Public Scorn right before Soul Espionage. Public Scorn must be cleared off to interrupt Soul Espionage.

## Repeat Visits

After following the strategies and completing the zone successfully, you can use the "shortcut method" to run the zone.

1. Immediately upon entering, hail [[Percy]], a cat just in front of the zone in. Accept the option **"I have no need of your pounce"** to receive a [[Bazaar rat]].
   - *Note: You must be able to speak [[Kerran]]*
1. Speak to [[Sammy]] at {{waypoint -277.90, 0.34, 114.69}}
   - Buy a [[Money Bag]] for a [[Bazaar rat]]
1. Defeat [[Financier Fendilix]] at {{waypoint -272.45, -1.51, 33.07}}
   - Drops [[30 Grit Sand Rune]] for use against next named
1. Defeat [[Mandee Quin]] and [[Mannee Quin]] at {{waypoint -119.79, -1.49, 60.60}}
   - *Note: Before starting the fight, right-click on [[30 Grit Sand Rune]] and choose 'auto consume'.*
   - Drops [[Lucky Horseshoe]] for use against next named
1. Defeat [[Short Shift]] at {{waypoint -60.67, -1.74, 218.81}}.
   - *Note: Remember to use the [[Lucky Horseshoe (Bauble)|Lucky Horseshoe]] whenever Short Shift's stoneskin is up.*
   - Drops [[Cat's Eye Agate]] for use against next named
1. Defeat  [[Trade Baroness Elsindir]] at {{waypoint -41.91, -1.47, 49.08}}
   - *Note: Remember to use the [[Cat's Eye Agate]].*
   - Drops all the special items that are sold by [[Mia]]
1. Defeat [[Bazaar Baron Brixwald]] around {{waypoint 303, 0, -133}}
   - *Note: Remember to autoconsume the [[Upgraded 30 Grit Sand Rune]], and use [[9 Lives (Level 120) (Version 2)|9 Lives]], [[Spirit of the Cat]], the [[Upgraded Lucky Horseshoe]], [[Paludal Catnip]], and the [[Cat's Eye Agate]].*
