## Requirements

- You must be at least level 110 in adventure or tradeskill class to receive an in-game letter granting you access to [[Myrist, the Great Library]]. The access is an account flag, meaning your alts can access Myrist too. (need confirmation on minimum requirement, but 100 tradeskill confirmed sufficient)
  - 7/14/19. I just took my level 81 Paladin with level 100 trade skill into Myrist, the Great Library. I was able to go to the Elemental Portal Gallery where I was able to hire Skando. Accessing the zones on that platform requires level 110.
  - Confirmed 19/07/2024 ("All eligible characters" can access Myrist once the account has been flagged by the token granted through mail, and the event in the Tribunal is optional)
- Initially, to get access you had to read the mail from Justinian Theo, examine the attached gift [[Coin of Eternal Scales]] and then survive a mini-event.
