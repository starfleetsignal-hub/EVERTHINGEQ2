## Notes

- Various portions of Awuidor are under water. To avoid drowning, use a waterbreathing spell, the [[Fishbone Earring]], [[Totem of the Otter]], a tinkered [[Rebreather]], etc.

## Strategy

1. [[Champion of E'ci]] {{waypoint -2.42, 668.39, -54.59}}
   1. Clear the area of four groups of [[A veiled assassin|veiled assassins]], [[A veiled guardian|guardians]], [[A veiled mystic|mystics]], [[A veiled warlock|warlocks]], and [[A veiled sentinel]]. The [[A veiled sentinel|veiled sentinel]] roams.  If you don't clear all four groups before killing the roaming mob, you may end up clearing them while you are combating the following waves.
   1. After killing [[A veiled sentinel]], you get the message, *Upon the death of the sentinel, horns sound below you,* signalling the start of attacks by waves of marids. There are five waves, consisting of 3, 3, 5, 8, then 9 merids.
   1. The name's spawn is announced by the message, *The air grows colder and you hear massive growls.*  This named has a significant power drain that I have not found a way to defeat.  He will also throw you in the air, but you can guide yourself back down to safety unless you were rooted.  If you don't have a way to avoid being rooted, this can be tricky.
   1. Once the [[Champion of E'Ci]] is slain, a message announces, *Upon the death of the champion, you notice a strange sort of bridge spawns between the two towers*. Cross the bridge to the south.
1. [[Champion of Marr]] {{waypoint -1.35, 668.39, 176.31}}
   1. Clear the area of four groups of [[A veiled assassin|veiled assassins]], [[A veiled guardian|guardians]], [[A veiled mystic|mystics]], [[A veiled warlock|warlocks]], and [[A veiled sentinel]]. The [[A veiled sentinel|veiled sentinel]] roams.
   1. After killing [[A veiled sentinel]], you get the message, *More horns sound from the deep,* signalling the start of attacks by waves of marids. There are five waves, consisting of 3, 3, 5, 8, then 9 merids.
   1. The name's spawn is announced by the message, *A new enemy appears.*  Significant power drain and he will teleport you around.  Sometimes you pop out right on the edge of the platform, so be careful.
   1. Once the [[Champion of Marr]] is slain, a message announces, *You hear a frigid voice in your minds, the only words it speaks are... So Be It!*
1. [[Champion of the Veiled One]] appears at {{waypoint 0.57, 668.39, 219.68}} Pretty much a tank and spank.
