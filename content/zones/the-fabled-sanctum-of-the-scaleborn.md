This is a higher level version of the original [[Sanctum of the Scaleborn]] which was first introduced with the Kingdom of Sky expansion. You can select this instance by clicking on the entrance in the [[Tenebrous Tangle]] at {{waypoint 212, 21, -185}}

Just as with the original version of the dungeon, if you go to [[The Audience Chamber]] and kill all of the [[A Scaleborn worshipper (Fabled)|Scaleborn worshipers]] and then kill all of the [[A drakota worshipper (Fabled)|drakota worshipers]] around the [[The Lair of Scale]], this will spawn the Fabled version of the dragon [[Cyenadros, Lord of the Sanctum (Fabled)|Cyenadros]].

## Notes

- The layout of this version of the zone is identical to the original, with the same bosses and ring events. Most named heroics are level 101 to 103.
- The rewards are level 95, and most of them are flagged as LORE, not LORE-EQUIP so you will need to transmute duplicates you don't need.
- There are five [[Collection Quests Timeline#Fabled KoS Instance Collections|new collections]] which can be completed in the Fabled version of this dungeon.
- Unlike the original, you can remain mounted in the Fabled version of this dungeon. If you want to retain your mount speed and buffs, but do not want to show your mount, you can select **Options** > **Display** > **Characters and Animation** from the menu and choose the option **Never Show**.
