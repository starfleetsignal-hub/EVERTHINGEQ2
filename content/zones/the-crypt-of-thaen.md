## Access

To enter you must have completed [[A Vicious Twist]] (The Hand of Marr) or [[Crushing Betrayal]] (The Brethren) in the [[Bloodline Chronicles Timeline]] to be granted the option to choose this instance or the raid instance, [[The Crypt of T'Haen: Vengeance]].

## Walkthrough

The objective is to kill everything in sight. There are several named bosses strewn about the bottom floor and more as you make your way up the top of the central pillar. There are also a couple unique quests to this zone and discovery locations. Lastly, there are two other exits that lead back to The Tombs of Night in addition to the way you came in that are located in the middle and upper parts of the instance.
