## Bosses

1. [[Matron of Mist]] is located at {{waypoint 168, -227, -1128}} and is spawned by clearing mist spren.
   - Knockup, fight in water.
1. [[Anzarion]] is located at {{waypoint 136.65, -180.77, -841.33}}
1. Use flower ("Pollen Launcher") at {{waypoint 111, -186, -797}} to [[Honeycomb Landing]]. [[Daishani]], an / the Efreeti Emir, is located at {{waypoint -675, 403, -162}}, the ramp up to the left in [[The Honey Larder]].
   - Tuck in corner, knock up.
1. Take bird at {{waypoint -324, 451, -149}}. Then destroy the Honeymonger Stash at {{waypoint -655, 525, -325}}, so [[Bzzkill the Honeymonger]] cames up {{waypoint -648, 523, -315}}
   - Interrupt when he calls for it.
1. The Bixel Queen, [[Aurorax]] in {{waypoint -508.00, 641.00, -175.00}} 3 ways to kill her:
   1. Kill the guards, watchout that you don't fall then kill the queen *(slowest way to do the fight)*
   1. Attack the queen directly. every x% one of the guards will protect her (absorbs 100% damage) means damaging the named kills the guard. *(mistaken often as the burn strat)*
   1. Attack the queen directly, but instead of waiting for the guard to protect her engage the guards right away and at least 2 at the time because each is absorbing 100% of the damage means you kill multiple guards at once in the time you would need to kill 1 guard. The named stays at 100% till all guards are dead then you the queen from 100% to dead without a guard taking over. *(the actual burn strat)*
