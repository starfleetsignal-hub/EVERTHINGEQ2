## Boss Fights

Lemme state that im writing this for end game back tracking epic mob head hunting. So in no way with you see any strategy here for defeating him while leveling up and challenging him as lore intended.

So im not gonna worry about the lesser Boss Fights but go straight to Dozekar the Cursed, more specifically how to change him into Dozekar the Usurper.

By now (assuming your back tracking) you should basically be a demi-god in strength which may or may not make this a bit difficult cuz you should be able to one shot him with ease, heck even a weapon proc can take him out (i know from experience from my head hunting). However, this is NOT what you want to do. You want to drag this one out. Read that last little sentence again. Now one more time for good measure.

First off, pull the mob. DONT HIT HIM. Anything that procs, take it off. Shut pets down, mercs down.. anyone or anything that can cause damage to Dozekar. Give it a bit, like 10-15 seconds and Jorlack will port in and run his digital yap. Hell tell you that Grendish is returning. Which is what we want to happen.

Note: you can still die, i did the first go around by a script of sort, insta death even with my demi-godlike power. So gotta be ready for that.

Anways, Jorlack will trap Grendish in a spell and run his mouth a bit. After a few youll be able to click on him and decide to let Grendish join the battle or not. LET HIM join.

By doing this Dozekar will change from Dozekar the Cursed to Dozekar the Usurper. Swat him and Grendish both and collect your Challenge mode Dozekar the Usurper trophy.

## About Research Prism

This raid includes the quest [[Etch the Stone]], which grants the crafter who completes it [[Yelnar's Research Prism]]. If the crafter intends to keep the [[Tradeskill Apprentices|tradeskill apprentice]] from that quest, they can consume the stone; otherwise, warn them not to use the stone, as it can be sold on the [[Broker|broker]].
