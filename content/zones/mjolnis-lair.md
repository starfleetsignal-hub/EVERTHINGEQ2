## Background

One of the drakota assassins that slew [[Vox, the White Dragon|Lady Vox]] is holed up in this remote part of the [[The Thundering Steppes|Thundering Steppes]]. Enter here and bring low this foul beast to claim Vox's [[Essence Shard of Soul]]!

## Walkthrough

1. Clear the beach of all adds.
1. Slay [[Mjolni, the Bringer of Storms]] and loot her body for the shard.
