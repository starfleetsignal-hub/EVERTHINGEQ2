## Background

Captive Dervish and Rujarkian Orcs have revolted against their gnome-pirate captors. Guided by your wits and your newfound gnome allies, break into here and liberate the captive gnome-pirate leaders.

## Notes

- *See discussion page for help soloing.*
- There is a quest inside called [[Save the Pirates!]], which must be completed to finish the zone the first time around.
- Click the ladder leaning on the boat at the zone-in to leave the instance. Note that the zone will reset itself if all group members leave.
- The instance is repeatable. Speak with [[Sparrow Cogcarrier]] or [[Heron Cogcarrier]] to re-activate the boat to the [[Aboard the Smarmy Sprocket]] and on to this instance.
