## Overview

After zoning in, go to the left and you will see an enchained spirit of Zan Fi. A chat between the named next to the spirit will start. Afterwards you will have to fight off four waves of terracotta warriors until [[Kudzua the Tormentor]] becomes targetable.

## Strategy

- This instance does not require a full group. However, everyone should stay ranged from Kudzua except for the tank. She will cast Rain of Thistles, a high-damage magic AoE which can kill anyone within 15 meters of her.
- CONSERVE POWER, you only get roughly 10-15 seconds between rounds, and they will auto agro regardless where you are in the zone.
- The only revive point is outside the instance, in Mystic Lake. If the group is killed by the boss, you will need to wait to reset the zone. Although it is a persistent instance, the boss will not be there if everyone revives or you exit the zone and return.
- The exit is at the right end of the large room leading you back to the [[The Mystic Lake]].
