## Background

A [[Tremblar the Behemoth|massive rumbler]] has been discovered living in a potentially lucrative mine in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]]. The Far Seas Trading Company is guarding the entrance, preventing anyone from getting in and anything from getting out. Should a group of adventurers feel brave enough to slay the beast, the Far Seas Trading Company would be more than happy to allow passage into the mine as well as first access to any riches within.

## Notes

- To enter this instance you must first speak to [[Gragak Stinkyfeet]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]], standing just outside the door at {{waypoint 76, 22, -424}}.
- **Important Note**: The named monsters will periodically damage your gear. If you are too slow about killing them your gear will eventually break. Make sure to bring backup gear, especially weapons if you're playing a class that relies on weapons to use most of their abilities.

## Walkthrough

1. Hail [[Shyzin the Coercer]] near to the zone-in and she will ask you to protect her while she casts a weakening spell on the final boss. This will start a long ring event that begins ~5 minutes after first speaking with Shyzin.
1. Slay the mobs quickly as they spawn, as the next set of mobs will spawn whether or not of the previous set has been killed.
1. Wave 1 contains [[Greater Symbiant]] and friends.  Wave 2 contains [[Symbiant Protector]] and friends.
1. Finally, once [[Symbiant Lord]] (Wave 3) has been killed, Shyzin will thank you and state that the weakening spell is complete. [[Tremblar the Behemoth]] will spawn a few minutes later in the eastern part of the zone at {{waypoint 92, 12, -186}}.
