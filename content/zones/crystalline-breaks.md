Crystalline Breaks shares the entrance door with [[Crystal Caverns: Collapse]] and can be accessed after completing [[Rongot Wyrmrippa]]'s quest [[Crystal Caver]]. There previously was a condition that you couldn't enter the zone anymore after finishing [[Fragmented Removal]], this restriction however was later lifted, so as long as you have completed or are on the quest [[Crystal Caver]] you can enter this zone. However in the same change the guaranteed 4 ornate chests you would recieve in this zone seem to have been reduced to a slim chance or replaced altogether by small chests which drop roughly 25g and the occasional icy adornment.
