This is a higher level version of [[The Den of the Devourer]] which was originally introduced with the Kingdom of Sky expansion.

## Notes

You must be at least level 95 to enter this dungeon. You will need to kill the bosses in this order:

- [[Svacklan (Fabled)]] {{waypoint -180.40, -18.90, 24.20}} is found in the Hall of Guardians and spawned by starting a ring event.  The first wave is four Vornerous High Guards (100^), second wave is four Vornerous Honorguards (100^^) and the third wave is Svacklan with two Vornerous Honorguards (103^^)
- [[Deepguardian Kvaklin (Fabled)]] {{waypoint -303.80, -36.09, 47.61}} is spawned by starting a ring event. From the Hall of Guardians, follow the north passage to The Hatching Chamber. There are four Vornerous hive guardians in each corner of the room. Clear the room four times, and then Kvaklin and two more guardians will spawn.
- [[Spirit of Water (Fabled)]] {{waypoint -158.78, -3.89, 211.56}} with four water elementals (100) spawns after killing [[Deepguardian Kvaklin (Fabled)]]. Take the south passage from the Hall of Guardians to The Waterfall Room. Note that if you range pull the Spirit of Water, you will be attacked by the ravasect which patrol around the pool.
- [[The Devourer (Fabled)]]{{waypoint -315, 2, 211}} is found in the Queen's Hive east of the Waterfall Room. There are two Queen's Guard (103^^) linked to her, and there are ravasect hatchlings which spawn from eggs in the center of the room. You can have someone destroy the eggs right before engaging her if the additional hatchlings make the fight difficult for the group.
