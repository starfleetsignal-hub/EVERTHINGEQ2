This instance is used in repeatable adventure dailies from [[Prelude Paths of Sedition Timeline]]

## Entrance

Entrance is granted when you have one of the 3 daily quests. Which quest you can get depends on your character's alignment:

- citizens of Freeport and Gorowyn get [[Dispatch Conspirators for Freeport]] from [[Nikodim]]
- citizens of Neriak get [[Dispatch Conspirators for Neriak]] from [[Tranessa Vexis]]
- citizens of Qeynos, Kelethin or New Halas get [[Dispatch Conspirators for Qeynos]] from [[Lachlyn Cauldthorn (Block and Tackle Storerooms)|Lachlyn Cauldthorn]]<br>

**After accepting quest you must hail your quest giver and let him know you are ready to be teleported inside.**

## Walkthrough

*Click on sparkling weapon crates as you go to satisfy your quest requirement. There is more than quests ask for, if you miss any you can go back through each door.*

1. Clear mobs including [[An Ebon Mask Spymaster]], who unlocks access to next area.
1. Enter Gallery of Honor door at {{waypoint -117, 0, -51}}
1. Clear mobs including [[A Dreadguard Captain]], who unlocks access to next area.
1. Enter Cloister of Charity door at {{waypoint -50, 0, -37}}
1. Clear mobs, next key mob [[A Spurned Archmage]] spawns at {{waypoint -56, -3, -122}} immediately after you kill all mobs in this area
1. Enter Chambers of Valor door at {{waypoint -52, -2, -136}}
1. Clear West, North and East rooms in any order. This should complete your quest.
   - Killing all mobs triggers spawn of additional non aggro mob: [[A befouled Lord Knight of Truth]] at {{waypoint -122, 0, 11}}. Kill him and last optional boss spawns in the East rooms at {{waypoint -144, -4, 0}}. For the Glory of Cristanos! Kill [[Mistress Vysessa of the Blood]] and exit portal to [[Block and Tackle Storerooms]] spawns behind where she stood.

## Loot

All key mobs and boss drop chests (small, treasure, legendary and exquisite) with level appropriate loot.<br>
Level 135 character also get bundles of 30 or 50 x [[Malicious Shard]].

## Collection

There is 2 purple shinies per instance for [[Ancient Teir'Dal Artifacts]] collection.
