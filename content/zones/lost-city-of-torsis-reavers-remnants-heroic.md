## Walkthrough

1. [[A Residual Slime (Heroic)]]
   - Tank and Spank™.
   - Drain his power constantly.
   - Spawns adds occasionally.
1. [[A Spectral Beguiler (Heroic)]]
   - Cure arcane detrimental quickly  or it causes death.
1. [[Tzirathk (Heroic)]]
   - Spawns reflections frequently. Can be overrun if you don't keep them down.
   - Tank him near one of the the braziers and run to the other one when he emotes (orange text).
1. [[Dread Wraith (Heroic)]]
   - Red Text emote about staring into your soul
     - Wait two seconds and joust out 15 meters. Do not joust back, a death causing sphere of influence remains on the ground.
1. [[Neh'Ashiir (Heroic)]]
   - Keep tank in the same spot (Where Neh is)
   - **Do not kill all of the black reavers**
   - There we be two curses. One is incurable, and one is.
     - The curable usually only lands on tank
     - If the tank gets the incurable, everyone jousts 20m from tank.
     - If group members get the incurable, they joust 20m from everyone.
     - *The curse can hit more than one person at a time*
     - *The curable curse hides the incurable on group window. Joust it just in case*
