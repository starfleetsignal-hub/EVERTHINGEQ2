## Boss Tactics

feel free to post yours

[[Pharinich]]

- A random group member will be affected by the plague.
- The group member needs to move away of from the group else it will spread.
- Plague victims suffer from massive power drain.
- Burn through the adds.

[[A Planar Manifestation]]

- Group should stay together at entrance or on one pillar - all on the same!
- Ranged DPS takes on the Boss, while the tank grabs all the adds
- melee is pretty useless except for taking down adds
- jumping onto the boss's platform will get you thrown back, and probably make a dive into the abyss.

[[The Enraged Imp (Heroic)|The Enraged Imp]]

- Tank and Spank

[[The Thoughtbleeder]]

- he will appear once the room is cleared of evil eyes.
- sometimes you will get red text "I shall faze into your soul!", which after 5 seconds will instantly kill the group unless everyone jousts away.
- when he says "I've got my eye on you!" he changes targets. Tanks prepare to snap.

[[Baelon of Thule (Heroic)|Baelon of Thule]]

- after you have slaughtered several waves of linked Lizardmen and Eyes, Baelon will come storming through the Fear Gate to destroy you.
- everybody needs to stay inside the circle marked on the floor, or you will get crushed.
- get out of green mist that occasionally appears, but remain inside circle.
- sometimes 3 adds join in, dunno if that is hardmode or whatever.
