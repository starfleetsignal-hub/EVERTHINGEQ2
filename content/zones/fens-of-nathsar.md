Comprised of humid swamps, tall forests, deep canyons, and countless ruins, the Fens of Nathsar is as vast as it is perilous. Monsters of all types roam the land, preventing safe passage to any traveler who strays off the heavily guarded [[Sathirian Highway]]. In the wake of the [[The Age of Cataclysms|Age of Cataclysms]], the inhabitants of this zone have banded into factions more out of a need for self-preservation than for complete dominion of the region. The Sarnak of Bathezid's Watch and the Iksar of Riliss are the two most powerful of these factions. To the north, the Exiles of Droga have constructed a patchwork community of Goblins and Giants. In the northeast, Burynai loyal to the baron of [[Kurn's Tower (POI)|Kurn's Tower]] have nestled into the [[Bellywhumper Burrows]], seeking treasures within the [[Field of Bone]] to appease their lord. And far to the cold north, the Order of Rime has set up an icy domain to protect their flagship, [[The Spinebreaker]].

Like the other zones of [[Kunark (Continent)|Kunark]], the Fens of Nathsar owes its name to one of the original Iksar tribes that predate the [[First Iksar Empire]]. Nothing original is left of this first community, short of legends and rumor, in the wake of the Empire's turbulent rise and the numerous wars that ensued. Most likely they were fully absorbed into the ranks of the empire, assimilating completely.

The Fens most poignant feature is the immense [[Lake of Ill Omen]]. While the lake borders three of the five mainland zones of Kunark, only in the Fens of Nathsar is the beachhead sturdy enough to support a [[Omen's Call|dock]]. The Lake of Ill Omen gets its name from a supposed event that occurred during the Iksar tribal era. After generations of using the lake as a source of fresh water, those who touched the water  began to suffer untimely and horrid deaths. Screams echoed through the coastal city of [[Veksar]], until after days all one could hear was a maddening chorus of the damned. The Iksar hastily laid plans to abandon the city and migrate eastward, but on the night prior to their exodus, a blood moon rose above the lake, and the moaning stopped. When dawn was about to break, the dead Iksar arose as mindless zombies. The sick and elderly were abandoned. Children too young to move on their own were left to be consumed by the zombie horde. Veksar was totally forsaken and the lake became a symbol of death and ill omen. Eventually, the entire city sank beneath the waters, taking with it the zombie multitudes and the horrors of that accursed time.

Another unique feature of this zone is the [[Swamp of No Hope]], named by a group of humans who tried in vain to establish a colony within its borders. It is home to many creatures, including frogloks and beautiful, but deadly, [[A Nathsarian frog-biter|carnivorous plants]]. The frogloks have long called this land their home, which put them at immediate odds with the growing Iksar empire. Their numbers would prove too few, and they were eventually subdued and enslaved by the Iksar. When the Iksar empire collapsed, however, the frogloks retook the swamp and rebuilt their home, building a massive town deep within the swamp. Following the Age of Cataclysms, the town was destroyed and the frogloks were unable to rebuild. Today, those frogloks who venture too far from their crevices can find themselves chained-up in the [[Western Pens of Riliss|Western]] or [[Eastern Pens of Riliss]] to be sold as slaves in the Iksar flesh markets.

Far beyond the protection of Bathezid's Watch and Riliss, lies the [[Field of Bone]]. This was once a lush and fertile field in the early days of the Iksar, but now lies as a desolate wasteland. Bones litter the ground, more abundant than rock. Ancient Iksar ruins continue to crumble and crack. This carpet of sun-bleached bones and rubble is the remnant of the many great wars fought here between the Iksar and their countless foes at the peak of their Empire. It is said that the [[Jaled Dar's Remains|remains]] of a great dragon, [[Jaled Dar's Shade|Jaled Dar]], lay in waste where the child of [[Veeshan]] fell at the hand of [[Ganak]], the last of the Iksar emperors.

Fens of Nathsar is the remnant of the [[EverQuest|EQ1]] zones of [Lake of Ill Omen](https://wiki.project1999.com/Lake_of_Ill_Omen), [Field of Bone](https://wiki.project1999.com/Field_of_Bone), and [Warsliks Woods](https://wiki.project1999.com/Warsliks_Woods), and [Swamp of No Hope](https://wiki.project1999.com/Swamp_of_No_Hope).
