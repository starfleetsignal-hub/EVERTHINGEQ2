## Zone Progression

- Zone in from the orb at the end of the Airship Dock, there is an access quest for all three zones, given consecutively by the flight master. Pick up all the quests on the dock to save time.

When you zone in, you will be in the cargo hold of The Vigilant. After grabbing and updating some quests from two NPCs at the zone-in point, pick off the few mobs and kill a bloated Zekling hiding behind the crates to the right for A Gnawed Key. Take either ramp up to Cargo Control. Enter either passageway to find the first named, Lozonn Nobuna (L93 ^^^).

Once he's dead, step onto the one-way teleporter. Grab 2 scrolls each from the two alcoves for the quest Swiping Scrolls, then take either doorway to a trampoline-like one-way portal, that springs you up to the next floor.

A ring event here will spawn around 4 waves of pairs of Tallonite lozonns, followed by Zonn Kogir  (L93 ^^^) who has an AOE knockback. Once he's killed, grab the chest at 40, 80, 60 then go up the ramp to the flight controls. All group members must each Use your Crew Commander's Control Crystal for the quest Crash Course.

Go back down the ramp to Navigation and take the glowing portal to Engine Core room aka Airship Disco. Be careful of pet pathing in this room. Kill mobs until Zonn Gamaz spawns (L93 ^^^). Take the portal to the next room immediately after killing Gamaz, as he zaps the room about a minute after death.

Back at Navigation Bridge, work your way up the ramp again and engage the final named Vice Vigoth Sanad (L94 ^^^). He has knockbacks and short-range single target ports. At around half health, he will port himself down the ramp. Once killed, you will have to zone out to Paineel and back into The Vigilant if you want to turn-in your quest Crash Course (or just evac).

Note - information on each named is available, click on Names above in the zone menu.
