## Walkthrough

1. [[Thread Baron]] {{waypoint -14.72, 26.14, -4.89}}
   - Walk up to the rolled carpet to start the fight.
   - Periodically, Thread Baron will disappear and you can't damage him. Cure the detriment to continue.
   - Don't jump the gap! You'll die every time.  The entrance to the Antechamber is found by going to the rubble where Thread Baron was, turn around and look slightyly up.  Go up the rubble and face back the way you came to get up on the ledge.
1. [[Edrigen the Empowered]] in the [[Antechamber of Antiquity]] at {{waypoint -13.47, 29.56, 42.52}}
   1. Clear the mobs in the four rooms around the Antechamber.
   1. Click the tablets on podiums and kill the three summoned idols from each. Their locations and types vary each time they are pulled, but they will call out "Could you have survived a walk through" followed by their plane of choice:
      - **Arcane** (magic)
      - **Breath of Water** (water)
      - **Bubonium Bacterium** (disease)
      - **Flight of the Aviaks** (air)
      - **In the Company of Misery** (hate)
      - **Of Sun and Flames** (fire)
      - **Survivors of the Storm** (storms)
      - **Roots of Rock** (earth)
1. Take the portal in the [[Antechamber of Antiquity]] at {{waypoint -13.47, 29.56, 42.52}}
1. [[The Key to Knowledge]] in [[Trevaluen, the Branching Gateway]] {{waypoint -264.76, 36.44, 142.31}}
   1. Kill the [[A watcher of the gates|watchers of the gates]] to make the central statue active.
   1. Click the large central statue to activate the eight [[Takish cylinder lock|Takish cylinder locks]].
   1. Kill the locks to make the central statue aggro.
1. Take the Sanctum Arcanum portal at {{waypoint -213.61, 39.70, 141.64}}
1. [[The Delving Departed]] in the [[Arcane Wing North]] at {{waypoint -111.04, 31.28, 157.98}}
   - He charms and fears. These are curable arcane detriments, use a potion or self-cure. To avoid being overwhelmed by fears, equip a [[Bloodbound Blinding Gleam]] or equivalent belt red rune.
   - Kill at least the **Researcher** in each room. If not they assist in the fight. They also respawn if the name fight takes too long.
1. Unlock **The Vault of Knowledge**. (Go through the door at {{waypoint -58, 33, 191}}
   - If you have completed the zone previously, simply click on the arch over any of the symbols. Otherwise, you have to solve the puzzle (see below).
   - Once the final symbol aligns the vault will unlock with all the layers dropping to floor level. Access to the new central floor area is via stepping stones.
1. [[Sanum Ordast]] {{waypoint 69.46, 34.11, 193.23}}
   - Sanum has a buff called The Elddar Rudiments that buffs him and reduces damage. Stepping on the pads in front of a spinning column halts its rotation and removes an increment of this buff.
   - Midway through the fight he summons four [[A psychic transferrence|psychic transferrences]]. At 20% health he becomes immune, kill all four of them, then return to hitting [[Sanum Ordast]].

## Unlocking the Vault of Knowledge

- You may need to adjust your graphics settings UP to see the symbols properly
- You must kill at least the mobs at the arches prior to starting to solve the puzzle.
- You may need to adjust your camera angle to see both the arch and the top layers of the puzzle.
- The aim is to get all the the symbols on the puzzle to match each arch.
- Once you gave solved the puzzle once, afterwards you can just click one of the arches after the mobs are cleared and it bypasses the puzzle.
1. Start at **The Symbol of Tal'Kesh** at {{waypoint 9.74, 33.20, 197.12}}.
   - Touching an Arch or its central symbol will confirm you have located the correct Arch and appropriate left control.
   - The left control rotates the whole stack clockwise.
   - Turn the left control until the bottom layer (the **Stratum of Tal'Kesh**) shows the same symbol as the arch with the Symbol of Tal'Kesh i.e. the first layer (1).
1. Move counterclockwise to **The Symbol of Ixil'Dor** at {{waypoint 23.87, 33.36, 152.79}}.
   - The left control rotates the top 7 layers clockwise.
   - Turn the left control until the symbol on the **Stratum of Ixil'Dor** shows the same symbol as the arch with the Symbol of Ixil'Dor i.e. the second layer (2).
   - As each layer is aligned the symbol should be the same from the first layer (1) to the current layer. A mistake has been made if the symbols do not align. As higher layers are completed the alignment of the symbols will become more obvious.
1. Move counterclockwise to **The Symbol of Oth'Onen** at {{waypoint 63.61, 33.15, 131.90}}.
   - The left control rotates the top 6 layers clockwise.
   - Turn the left control until the symbol on the **Stratum of Oth'Onen** shows the same symbol as the arch with the Symbol of Oth'Onen i.e. the third layer (3).
1. Move counterclockwise to **The Symbol of Zaket'Ien** at {{waypoint 107.86, 33.15, 145.12}}.
   - The left control rotates the top 5 layers clockwise.
   - Turn the left control until the symbol on the **Stratum of Zaket'Ien** shows the same symbol as the arch with the Symbol of Zaket'Ien i.e. the fourth layer (4).
1. Move counterclockwise to **The Symbol of Wan'Lieth** at {{waypoint 128.94, 33.29, 186.54}}.
   - The left control rotates the top 4 layers clockwise.
   - Turn the left control until the symbol on the **Stratum of Wan'Lieth** shows the same symbol as the arch with the Symbol of Wan'Lieth i.e. the fifth layer (5).
1. Move counterclockwise to **The Symbol of Tevex'Thez** at {{waypoint 115.22, 33.26, 230.05}}.
   - The left control rotates the top 3 layers clockwise.
   - Turn the left control until the symbol on the **Stratum of Tevex'Thez** shows the same symbol as the arch with the Symbol of Tevex'Thez i.e. the sixth layer (6).
1. Move counterclockwise to **The Symbol of Hazen'Ad** at {{waypoint 74.31, 33.27, 251.15}}.
   - The left control rotates the top 2 layers clockwise.
   - Turn the left control until the symbol on the **Stratum of Hazen'Ad** shows the same symbol as the arch with the Symbol of Hazen'Ad i.e. the seventh layer (7).
1. Move counterclockwise to **The Symbol of Vel'Naen** at {{waypoint 30.83, 33.25, 237.49}}.
   - The left control rotates the top layer clockwise.
   - Turn the left control until the symbol on the **Stratum of Vel'Naen** shows the same symbol as the arch with the Symbol of Vel'Naen i.e. the eighth layer (8).
   - Once the final symbol aligns the vault will unlock with all the layers dropping to floor level. Access to the new central floor area is via stepping stones.
