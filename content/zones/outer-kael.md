## Requirements

Everyone in group must be level 85+ to enter.

## Geography

![A dungeon portal in Outer Kael](images/Outer_Kael_dungeon_portal.png)
![The cavern containing Kael Drakkel](images/Outer_Kael_cavern.png)

Outer Kael consists of a large icy cave containing the home of the Storm Giants, Kael Drakkel.

### Travel

The zone is small enough that there are no transports.  A large gate leads back to the Eastern Wastes, and the dungeons are accessed via blue portals.

There are four portals in total.  One leads to [[Iceshard Keep]] {{waypoint -1679, 20, -528}}, one to [[The Temple of Rallos Zek]] {{waypoint -1509, 25, -688}}, and one to [[Throne of Storms]] {{waypoint -1397, 23, -782}}.  The fourth {{waypoint -1793, 18, -449}} is for all of the Kael raid zones.  A door at {{waypoint -1432, 72, -422}} leads to [[Kael Drakkel]], also known as Kael Contested.

NPCs in front of the portals offer quests for the dungeons. You have to start with [[Iceshard Keep]] first, after finishing the quest there you get offered quests for [[Throne of Storms]] and then [[The Temple of Rallos Zek]].
