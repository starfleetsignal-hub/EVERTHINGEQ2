An expanse of land with varying climates, from dense forest to snow covered mountains, the inhabitants of this region deal with many [[Kylong Plains Timeline|threats]]. Whether it be the [[Gra'al Shul Temple|Gra'al Shul Iskars]] near [[Kunzar's Edge (POI)|Kunzar's Edge]], the mysteries of the [[Teren's Grasp|Combine ruins]], or the menacing drolvarg roaming around [[Karnor's Castle]], there is always need for help from a brave adventurer.

Explore a thriving plain where the relentless encroachment of the trees have finally pushed their way up deep into [[The Dreadlands]]. Come to the aid of Residents of Teren's Grasp by helping them establish a secure dock at [[Dreg's Landing]] and fend of a pending invasion from the Order of Rime. Travel east through the [[Varis|ancient tunnel]] and across the massive chasm that was once only a river to what remains of the [[Waning Thicket|Elven outpost of Firiona Vie]] and here help the residents hold the fort at [[Kunzar's Edge]] against the Iksar and Mudfin. Westward of Dreg's Landing journey up into the [[Ryjesium Peaks|high mountains]] to the ancient and mysterious [[The Combine Spires|Combine Spires]] where the settlement of [[Teren's Grasp]] clings to the frozen tundra. Venture north from from the remnants of the [[Lost Valley]] and back down into a [[Stonewood|dense forest]] where the burned ash has finally been covered by the fall leaves.

Kylong Plains is the remnant of the [[EverQuest|EQ1]] zones of [Burning Woods](https://wiki.project1999.com/Burning_Wood), [Dreadlands](https://wiki.project1999.com/Dreadlands), and [Firiona Vie](https://wiki.project1999.com/Firiona_Vie).

## Getting to Kylong Plains

- There are two boats that offer direct travel to Kylong Plains and place you at [[Dreg's Landing]]:
  - [[Maiden's Grace]] from the docks in [[Antonica]]: {{waypoint 436, -38, 820}}
  - [[Overlord's Edge]] from the docks in [[The Commonlands]]: {{waypoint -1051, -145, -680}}
- [[Ulteran Spire (KP)|Ulteran Spire Network]] takes you to the center of [[Teren's Grasp]].
- [[Druid Ring (KP)|Druid Ring]] takes you to just outside the walls of Teren's Grasp.

Of course, you can always just use a [[Mariner's Bell]] to arrive at Dreg's Landing.

## Travel within Kylong Plains

[[Sokokar|Sokokar posts]] give travel between [[Dreg's Landing]], [[Teren's Grasp]], [[Highton]], [[Jarsath Wastes|Road to Jarsath Wastes]], [[Road to Fens of Nathsar]] and [[Kunzar's Edge (POI)|Kunzar's Edge]]. In order to gain access to the Sokokar network, players must complete a short [[Here in my Sokokar I Feel Safest of All|questline]] in Dreg's Landing. Previously, players had to have [[A young sokokar (Item)|a young sokokar]] in their inventory, rewarded from the final quest of that questline, to be able to use the Sokokar posts. Furthermore, each individual Sokokar post had to be traveled to on foot and examined before it could be flown to. This as well is no longer required and upon completion of the questline, all sokokar flight paths within Kylong Plains and the entirety of Kunark are unlocked.
