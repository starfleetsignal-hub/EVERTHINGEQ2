Like [[Zon Zobboz]], Gerion: Hold of D'Lere is a small 'hub' zone. It primarily functions to access related "Gerion:" instances, and also any related quest NPCs

## Geography

### Travel

Once unlocked, the zone is accessed from within [[Yon Gorroth]] via the same portal in [[Darou Village]] that that brought you to The Void in the first place.
