## Prerequisites

*To enter, you must have completed [[Visions of Vetrovia: Evil Dedraka]], <s>[[Bona Fide Treasure Seeker]]</s> or have earned the [[VoV Flag: Karuupa Jungle Unlock]].*

## Walkthrough

1. [[Zaag the Unburied]] {{waypoint -310, 105, -629}}
   - Teleports to adds. Chase him down, then tank and spank.Tip: clear all the adds leading up to this fight so the adds will not join.
   - note: if on [[Visions of Vetrovia: Evil Dedraka]]  quest, click the corruption that spawns behind him after he is dead, at ( -293, 105, -614 ).
   - note: Not 100% sure on this, but as a caster, I had to get up close and personal for him to take any damage,
1. [[Scarfeather]] Epic X3 {{waypoint -415, 115, -422}}
   - Has increments. To increase damage to him, pull Scarfeather to the bubbles in front of [[A grazing ceredon]] or he'll seem to be impervious - pulling him out of the water he doesn't take any damage. Note: need to approach the front of the ceredon or they may not knock him down.
1. [[Krelburn]] {{waypoint -480, 124, -289}}
   - Do not agro the [[A blazing drake fly|blazing drake flies]], they give him a buff (when you agro the flies they will fly to you and Krelburn). Krelburn will eat any flies near it to gain buffs. You will see a new icon appear on the first left row of buffs it has, and he will turn dark red and fiery. There is an area to the right of bridge {{waypoint -472, 125, -313}}  where there are fewer flies that will spawn. If Krelburn eats a fly, cast defensive or healing spells/Combat arts and wait for his buff to expire as his buff will reflect damage to you.  (You can try to damage Krelburn when it has its buff up but you need mitigation especially against Heat damage. This advice is for players with only starter gear as your DPS will be very low for this boss.)
   - Dance to stay out of the black spots which will spawn a graboid plant which is a 1-shot. He will emote  "**sends out more of its seeking vines underground**"
   - Joust when he emotes **"Krelburn reaches..."**
   - ***NOTE:*** Pulling Krelburn onto the bridge apparently prevents the black spot spawning graboid plants from spawning. But be sure to kill all the drake flies!
1. [[Cannibrea]] {{waypoint -625.86, 185.36, -75.44}} (climb up clickable vine by Krelburn {{waypoint -511.76, 133.25, -264.39}} to reach)
   - Kill **a protective [[A glade strider|glade strider]]** to get a [[Pristine pterodon feather]]. Right-click the feather in your inventory and **Use** so it will counter the detriment *When Flocks Together* (Damage multiplier when the fledglings are summoned).
   - When Cannibrea emotes, "Spreads her wings showing off her tremendous plumage! Nest!" she will unleash an attack which will kill you if you are not in a nest. You have to get into a nest to avoid this attack. This step is important unless you know how to block. For new players, its best to step in the nest and then step out of it. *Note*: Do not keep standing on the nest as it has a DOT which will kill you over time.
   - Do not step on any Bone on the ground as it will create Skeleton mob add.
1. [[Elder Gromekus]], [[Olipna]], and [[Ponshudu]] {{waypoint -641, 114, 89}} (climb down the clickable **a sturdy vine** {{waypoint -588.56, 180.10, -55.69}} to reach them)
   - Kill the surrounding Dedraka Wranglahs so that you can pull them away from the corruption source (stone) behind them, or they'll add when you pull.
   - Drag them down the hill: They have a buff next to the corruption.
   - Kill the non-elders twice to weaken the elder. Then kill the elder twice
   - Note: if on [[Visions of Vetrovia: Evil Dedraka]]  quest, click the corruption stone that is at their spawnpoint {{waypoint -647, 118, 126}} once they are dead.
   - Speak to Vaziri who spawns at ( -645, 117, 112 ) .
