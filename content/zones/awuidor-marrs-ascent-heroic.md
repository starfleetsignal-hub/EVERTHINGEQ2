## Notes

- Various portions of Awuidor are under water. To avoid drowning, use a waterbreathing spell, the [[Fishbone Earring]], [[Totem of the Otter]], a tinkered [[Rebreather]], etc.

## Walkthrough

1. Kill the mob on the other side of the entrance
1. swim up
1. Kill the named *not above the entrance*
1. Kill the mob on the other side
1. swim up
1. Kill the whirlwind trash mobs before you fight the named
1. named
   1. Kill the named once to make him split into two mobs
   1. damage the mobs equaly (keep within 10% health) or you get a 2 debuffs that reduce your potency by 20% each (which lowers your dps quite a bit)
1. use the portal
