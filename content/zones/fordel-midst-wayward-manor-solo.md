## Notes

- From the [[Aurelian Coast]] enter the cave at  {{waypoint 143.20, 85.21, -484.41}}
- Go to the Door  at  {{waypoint 168.48, 61.91, -679.58}} to zone into the instance.
- Do not kill ahead on the balcony in the dark area or you may be unable to complete the zone.

## Walkthrough

1. *If Vusor Umerus has a flag, you can combine Paludal Removal with this quest.*
   - *Dispose of 16 reishi*
   - *Eliminate 10 paludal muck*
   - *Remove 8 muck diggers*
1. Kill reishi
   - Kill reishi for **sensate spores**.
   - Kill enough reishi to spawn [[The Deadly Dapperling]].
1. Defeat [[The Deadly Dapperling]] wandering the room at {{waypoint 534.98, -22.11, -580.77}}. He has a knockback that throws you out of range and breaks the encounter, so place your back to the wall.
1. Muck diggers
   - Kill at least one muck digger for **moltenite muck**.
   - Kite muck diggers into the stone traps to spawn [[Eegut Stonegut]].
     - I had to suspend my mercenary to trap the muck diggers. Then recalled him when Eegut Stonegut popped.
   - Don't step in the stone traps or you will be temporarily trapped.
1. Defeat [[Eegut Stonegut]] at {{waypoint 724.32, -21.21, -580.72}}.
   - Has a large knock back. Put your back to a wall.
   - Periodically 'frees' muck diggers from traps to add to the fight.
1. Harvest **<s>sulfuric rags</s>** [[Bottle of Screaming Flames]] from <s>lich body</s> Research station at {{waypoint 725.29, -7.36, -521.09}}. Skip steps 6 and 7.
1. Search a Muck Digger Remains at {{waypoint 719, -7, -498}} for **moltenite muck**.
1. Use Research station at {{waypoint 724.52, -8.65, -519.87}} to combine **moltenite muck**, **sensate spores** and **sulfuric rags** into a [[Bottle of Screaming Flames]]. You can click on Remains on the floor at {{waypoint 723.02, -8.65, -504.55}} to get additional ingredients.
1. Defeat [[Yelloweyes]] at {{waypoint 634.79, -8.65, -509.73}}
   - Use [[Bottle of Screaming Flames]] to dispel the **Dark Affinity** damage immunity buff every time it's up (room goes dark).
   - Has a trauma disarm.
   - Power drains.
1. Kill [[Netherbian dreaders]]
   - Kill [[Netherbian dreaders]] for nether coils.
   - Kill enough [[Netherbian dreaders]] for pools of darkness to spawn on the floor. This also spawns [[Tog Voil]] wandering the balcony.
1. Defeat [[Tog Voil]] to open doors at {{waypoint 641.02, -8.39, -599.81}} and {{waypoint 691.23, -8.40, -632.03}}.
   - Periodically summons adds and ports.
1. Proceed to the House of Midst's Library at {{waypoint 741.13, -8.55, -707.60}}.
   1. *For Paludal Removal, speak to Vusor Umerus who is inside the door on the left.*
      - *Gather 'Creatures Of The Shadows'  {{waypoint 767.14, -8.55, -746.76}}.*
      - *Gather 'Theoretical Sorcerous Engineering'  {{waypoint 753.08, -8.55, -720.88}}.*
      - *Gather 'Ceremony Of The Stars'  {{waypoint 760.16, -8.55, -761.58}}.*
      - *Geometric Philosophy'  {{waypoint 788.42, -8.55, -744.88}}.*
      - *Gather 'Philosophy of Indefinite Studies'  {{waypoint 788.77, -8.55, -736.84}}.*
      - *Gather 'Ethical Popular Alteration'  {{waypoint 767.83, -8.55, -715.17}}.*
      - *Gather 'Chronology of Sorcerous Academics'  {{waypoint 774.12, -8.55, -751}}.*
      - *Gather 'Applied Alchemy'  {{waypoint 788.39, -8.55, -728.37}}.*
      - *Speak to Vusor Umerus for your reward.*
1. Defeat [[Famyra the Biblioghost]] near {{waypoint 759.71, -8.55, -735.43}}.
   - Part way through the fight she smooshes the apprentices under the bookcases and [[An ancient tome]] spawns above each. Each tome gives her 25% damage reduction.
   - Destroy the tomes, then finish her off.
