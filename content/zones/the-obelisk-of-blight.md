## Background

This is a tower of the [[The Void|Void]], similar to [[The Obelisk of Lost Souls]]. Inside, a shadowman ruler named [[Axxyk'Tuur]] has been tasked with infiltrating fae society in order to absorb them into the Void army, or if they should prove too resistant, simply burn their souls for energy. Through [[Into the Shadowed Grove|investigation]] it is revealed that Axxyk'Tuur and the Obelisk of Blight have another directive, once that involves the protection of a device capable of controlling a powerful Norrathian dragon.

## Notes

- There is only one re-pop in the whole zone, [[An advanced automaton]], circling the ceiling in the center chamber of the final encounter area. This is certainly the guardian mentioned in [[Tower of Zet Scroll No. 3]].
- This zone is also known as the [[Tower of Zet]]. For Lore about this zone, see Obelisk of Blight Lore and Shadowed Man Lore.
- It is worth noting that the name of this tower, Zet, is similar to [[The Tower of Vul]] in [[The Feerrott]], which is mentioned in [[Tower of Zet Scroll No. 1]].
- Zet is the name of one of the [[Words of Pure Magic|Druzaic Shrines]].
