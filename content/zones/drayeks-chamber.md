## Notes

- Completion of the former access quest, [[Drayek's Chamber (Quest)|Drayek's Chamber]] is no longer necessary.
- For the instance involved in the [[Prismatic Weapon Timeline]], see [[Drayek's Chamber: Throne of the Kromise King]].

## Walkththrough

1. Approach the NPC version of [[King Drayek]] to start the event.
1. Slay the golems that aggro.
1. Slay the two wolves, [[Frostbite (Named)|Frostbite]] and [[Misty]], that aggro several moments after the golems are killed.
1. Kill [[King Drayek]]
   - He will spawn two sets of [[A Kromise royal guard|royal guards]], which seem to make him go immune to damage, then a set of skeletons which don't seem to make him immune. Put your back to a wall to avoid being kicked across the room away from the healer. Not much trouble at level 55 with a mercenary but still a challenge.
