## Notes

To obtain [[Deshniak's Research Prism]] talk to Deshniak at {{waypoint -407, 0, 25}} to start a ring event.

- The prism you recieve can be used or you can sell it on the [[Broker|broker]].
- R click on Deshniak's Research Prism which drops at the end of the battle *only* if you want to keep the [[Tradeskill Apprentices|tradeskill apprentice]], [[Apprentice Overknight Deshniak]].
  - Using the prism will turn it into [[Deshniak's Teleportation Stone]], which is a *no-trade* item, that allows you to place Overknight Deshniak in your home to start researching his recipes.
