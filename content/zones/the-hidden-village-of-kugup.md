## Lore

After both the [[Second Rallosian War]] and [[The Rending]], those frogloks lucky enough to survive hid themselves away within the jungles of [[The Feerrott]]. Ever since then they have been seeking a way to escape the region, fearing the proximity and repression of the Tae Ew Lizardmen. [[Mithaniel Marr (God)|Marr]] gave them hope when [[The Age of Destiny (Lore)|recently]] adventurers from the other continents began arriving to the Feerrott. The frogloks of Kugup no longer wish to remain hidden, but will stand their ground together with any outside aid and fight back the Tae Ew.

## Entrance

To enter the Hidden Village of Kugup, the player must have completed the access quest, [[Uncovering the Froglok Secret]]. Afterwards the zone may be entered via a stone-pedestal in [[The Feerrott]] at {{waypoint -1039, 1.32, 856}}.

- Occasionally when the pedestal is used, the teleportation portal may not visually render. However, if you hover your mouse slightly above the pedestal, the invisible portal called 'A Swirling Vortex' can still be clicked-on.

## Notes

- Merchants are available in this zone, but no mender.
