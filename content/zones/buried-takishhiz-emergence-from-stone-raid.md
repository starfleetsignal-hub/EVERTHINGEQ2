[[Heroic Opportunities]] started within this zone activate Core Power or Core Synergy, similar to heroic opportunities in the heroic versions of [[Buried Takish'Hiz: Terrene Threshold (Heroic II)|Buried Takish'Hiz: Terrene Threshold]].

## Walkthrough

### [[Stonesong]] (T4)

Stonesong is found on a pedestal in Daeron Overlook at {{waypoint -0.01, 33.47, -73.96}}. After defeating Stonesong, the barrier blocking access to the Antechamber of Antiquity is dispelled.

### [[Rugrat the Thief]] (T4)

Rugrat the Thief is found in the southeast room of the Antechamber of Antiquity at {{waypoint -21.91, 28.69, 73.61}}. After defeating Rugrat, a portal appears in the center of the room that transports you to the Branching Gateway.

### [[The Monument of Stone]] (T4)

The Monument of Stone is found at the western end of Sanctum Arcanum at {{waypoint -58.14, 33.19, 191.53}}.
