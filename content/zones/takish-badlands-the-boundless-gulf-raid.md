## Walkthrough

### [[Huhmuhngus Fuhngus Amuhngus]] (T3)

The [[Huhmuhngus Fuhngus Amuhngus]] lives in the eastern side of Petrous Woods. It arrives when all 10 [[Animated Fungi explorer|animated Fungi explorers]] in Petrous Woods are defeated.

Defeating this colossal foe opens the path up the mountain to Blinded Eye Camp.

### [[Sleujess, Pride Guardian]] (T3)

[[Sleujess, Pride Guardian]], the Desert Wildcat Matron, lives in Blinded Eye Camp at the top of the mountain. She arrives when all 19 [[A desert wildcat|desert wildcats]] in Blinded Eye Camp and the surrounding regions are defeated.

After Sleujess is defeated, a carpet at {{waypoint -497.16, 207.35, 66.17}} appears to take travelers to Tera'Noth Peninsula. Additionally, a group of six [[A Hizite ivory hunter|Hizite ivory hunters]] activates and begins harassing a captured Hippolisk at {{waypoint -366.67, 20.64, 322.19}} in the rocky coastline just below Blinded Eye Camp.

### [[Hambalumi]] (T3)

[[Hambalumi]], the Mhyt-moo Migmu, lives in the Mhyt-moo Tidal Burrows.

After Hambalumi's defeat, a group of six [[A Mhyt-moo spearfisher|Mhyt-moo spearfishers]] activates and begins harassing a captured Hippolisk at {{waypoint 34.82, 1.15, -45.47}} on the shores within the Mhyt-moo Tidal Burrows.

### [[Ragtag the Despoiler]] (T3)

(Despite being listed as the fifth raid boss according to the achievement list, Ragtag must be killed to activate Kiori.)

[[Ragtag the Despoiler]] lives in the very back of Firiona's Motte.

After Ragtag's defeat, a group of six [[A sandcrawler ivory hunter|sandcrawler ivory hunters]] activates and begins harassing a captured Hippolisk at {{waypoint 632.15, 3.20, -73.67}}, just below Firiona's Motte on the shoreline. Additionally, a carpet at , near the captured Hippolisk, appears to take travelers to Hu Haket. However, riders are intercepted by Kiori in the middle of the Sea of Sand.

### [[Kiori, the Jellyfisher Ancient]] (T3)

[[Kiori, the Jellyfisher Ancient]] lives in the middle of the Sea of Sand. It arrives after all three captured Hippolisks have been released. Kiori then attacks immediately.

### [[Nirag the Boundless]] (T4)

[[Nirag the Boundless]] lives on the island of Hu Haket. He watches over his domain until someone enters the druid ring, when he flies down and activates.
