1. Venture through Vaaskhaani in search of the palace throne.
1. Go up a ramp to the platform to the back of the platform.
1. Kill [[Nezri En'Sallef]]  {{waypoint 149, 20, -0}}
   - *Heroic Opportunities (HO) to dispel her gold shield - instakill at 25 stacks.*
   - **Use Stifle rune**
1. Remove the orbs from both rooms
   - *Orb of Gales {{waypoint 148, 20, 0}}*
   - *Orb of Cyclones {{waypoint 227, 32, 44}}*
   - *Orb of Rains {{waypoint 227, 32, -44}}*
   - *Orb of Hail {{waypoint 200, 32, -45}}*
1. Continue down the hallway and kill [[Isos]]  {{waypoint 262, 31, 58}}
   - ***Stifles**, plan accordingly with adornments.*
   - *Must loot above four orbs to damage him*
   - *Must kill adds to kill [[Isos]], use HOs to knock them out.*
   - Remove the Orb of Thunder {{waypoint 262, 32, 67}}
1. Kill [[The Storm Mistress]] {{waypoint 262, 31, -25}}
   - *She **stuns**, so **apply your stun rune to your waist***
   - *Summons lightning anchors, **HO her to damage them***
   - *If she summons a third anchor she instakills you*
   - Remove the Orb of Lightning {{waypoint 263, 32, -67}}
1. Click the portal To the Forum {{waypoint 281, 32, 0}}
1. Clear a path and then engage [[Hezodhan]] {{waypoint 432, 8, 0}}
   - *Summons adds, too many will insta-kill*
   - *He stops taking damage when too many [[Sand squall]]<nowiki/>s are up, kill them to damage him*
1. Take the magic carpet he was sitting on, now a transport, to the upper level
1. Go up the ramp behind you and kill all the mobs in the hallway to activate the transport portals (can use either side, doesn't matter)
1. Go to the [[Sovereign's Reflection]] (second room on the right, aka Mirror room) at {{waypoint 604, 71, -44}}. Click mirror to spawn named.
   - ***Fears** - put a fear immunity rune in your waist slot gear*
   - *Ignore Ashnu (the hand mob) for now, he can't be damaged yet.  Keep* [[Zakir-Sar-Ussur]] *(Sovereign) in line of sight of the mirror (directly in front of it). This is easiest done if you put your back to the mirror as you pull him, let him come to you right in front of mirror. You will see blue text "An opportunity has presented itself as The Sovereign steps in front of his mirror!". This is the time to complete a Heroic Opportunity to trap him in the mirror.  **Beware! your pet or merc might cast root and keep him from going into mirror.***
   - *Ashnu now can be damaged - DPS Ashnu until you see message that  Zakir is trying to break free from the mirror - switch target to Zakir and perform another Heroic Opportunity to continue to trap him into the mirror if needed.*
   - *DPS Ashnu again until he is dead.*
