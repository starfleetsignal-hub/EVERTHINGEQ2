## Background

A blasphemous group of Yha-lei have taken up residence in this former holy city of the [[Iksar Empire]]. [[Yothe K'lohgk]] believes they are worshiping a god other than [[Prexus]], an act of severe heresy for the fish-people. You must enter here and ascertain why type of briny mischief is brewing...

## Notes

- The only bosses that require spawning are:
  - [[Leviathor'Consuma]] {{waypoint 192, -1, 216}}
    - Use a [[Large bluefin]], caught from a 'school of bluefins', while standing close to the pool. The bluefins can be found at {{waypoint 229.50, -2.30, 155.25}}.
  - [[Leviathor'Glamar]] {{waypoint 102, 1, -255}}
    - Kill all the [[A Yha-lei worshipper|Yha-lei worshippers]] surrounding the pool.
  - [[Leviathor'Tentar]] {{waypoint -207, 0, 216}}
    - Kill all the [[A Yha-lei enforcer|Yha-lei enforcers]] and [[A Yha-lei arbiter|Yha-lei arbiters]] surrounding the pool.
- [[Majora Leviathora]] {{waypoint -340, 30, -60}}
  - Spawns after killing the other three above listed behemoths, as well as the [[A Yha-lei prognosticator|Yha-lei prognosticators]] surrounding the pool.
