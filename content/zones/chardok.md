Chardok is the site of an ancient Sarnak military fortress that has existed for centuries since the time of [[Atrebe Sathir|Emperor Atrebe]] and is considered by the sarnaks to be their ancestral home. It is led by the Brood of Di'Zok, who follow [[Queen Velazul Dizok]] and her pureblood council. Queen Velazul, in turn, serves at the behest of [[Selrach Di'Zok|Overking Selrach Di'Zok]]. The Gwalnax Brigade is seeking adventures to purge this council of their pureblood obsession so they can redirect the sarnak empire to focus on expelling the dragons from Kunark.

### Sokokar Flight Path

There is a [[Sokokar]] flight path available from within the dungeon, that brings you from the dungeon zone-in to nearby the entrances to the two inner instances. The first one is located at {{waypoint 189.45, 11.61, -665.26}}, and the second is located further inside the dungeon, at {{waypoint -468.29, -66.74, -78.04}}. In the past, players had to complete a questline from [[Skyrider Tosuj]] in [[Gwalnax Brigade Tower]] to unlock the flight path, but now one just has to click each of the hitching posts once.

### Gate Crystals

There are three gates in Chardok, each requiring a unique gate crystal to pass. The relevant gate crystal drops from a 'gatekeeper' mob that is always found in a building near each gate. The crystals are chest-drops, so if you are soloing, mentor down to at least adventure level 90 so the gatekeeper mobs are not greyed out. The crystals/gates are as follows:

- [[A Bathezid gate crystal]] drops from [[A Bathezid gatekeeper]] {{waypoint -350, -18, -548}} and unlocks the green Bathezid Gate. {{waypoint -236, -37, -353}}
- [[A Nak'azar gate crystal]] drops from [[A Nak'azar gatekeeper]] {{waypoint -412, -66, 17}} and unlocks the blue Nak'azar Gate. {{waypoint -258, -65, 3}}
- [[A Caroeth gate crystal]] drops from [[A Caroeth gatekeeper]] {{waypoint 205, -72, 78}} and unlocks the red Caroeth Gate. {{waypoint 107, -48, 174}}

## Walkthrough

To understand the lore behind the zone, it's best to progress through the dungeon while completing the [[Kylong Plains Timeline#Chardok|Chardok Timeline]], stopping progression to turn in completed quests and pick up new ones. If you just want to move through the zone clearing mobs and taking on bosses, then see the following sections for locations and boss spawning that are not intuitive:

### [[Ra'Tsinis]]

Ra'Tsinis is the first boss of the dungeon. His summoning is a timed event, and if the horn-blowing step is not completed within five minutes, you will have to wait for up to 30 minutes to try again. Follow these steps to summon him:

1. Enter [[The Wind Chamber (Chardok)|The Wind Chamber]] {{waypoint 39, -23, -456}}
1. Kill [[The Malefic Formation]] that spawns after the horns are automatically sounded.
1. Kill the six [[A konikor trumpeteer|konikor trumpeteers]] that spawn at the mouthpieces of the horns.
1. Within ~5 minutes, blow the horns (by clicking the mouthpiece) in the following order:
   - Kab: {{waypoint 8, 4, -520}}
   - Vul: {{waypoint 61, 4, -395}}
   - Uzu: {{waypoint 7, 4, -395}}
   - Myr: {{waypoint -30, 4, -481}}
   - Zet: {{waypoint 91, 4, -432}}
   - Roehn: {{waypoint -30, 4, -431}}
1. Ra'Tsinis spawns in the center of the chamber and immediately aggros.

### [[The Chardok Palace]]

To continue deeper into the Chardok Palace, and eventually reach [[The Throne of Ascendence]], you must click wall-levers behind several bosses. The first lever is behind [[Stalis Ra'Dal]], at {{waypoint 923, -45, 355}}, and the second is behind [[Nortalz Di'Zok]], at {{waypoint 1043, -47, 347}}.

### [[Silren Di'Zok]]

Silren is located in [[The Library of Divination]]. To access the library, follow these directions:

1. When you get to [[The Hall of Initiative]] {{waypoint 841, -30, 206}}, look in the two upper rooms for three books: [[Encyclopedia of Mutagenica Vol I]], [[Encyclopedia of Mutagenica Vol II]], and [[Encyclopedia of Mutagenica Vol III]]. They can all spawn in the same room or in both: {{waypoint 868, -21, 135}} and {{waypoint 890, -21, 275}}.
1. Once you have acquired all three, click the lever behind [[Stalis Ra'Dal]], at {{waypoint 923, -45, 355}}.
1. Click on the clay golem NPC [[Re'Adon]] and you will automatically give him the books.
1. Stick close to Re'Adon as he slowly makes his was past the secret walls.
1. Once you're inside the library, continue down all the steps and click the book titled, "Passage to Oblivion", at {{waypoint 929, -66, 556}}.
1. Silren will spawn at {{waypoint 957, -77, 613}}.
