## Gaining Access

- You must be on or have completed [[Mission Improbable...]] to gain permanent access to this zone.
- You can gain access to this zone if you're currently on the tradeskill quest, [[Frostfell Decoration Committee: Permafrost!]], regardless if you completed Mission Improbable... or not.
- If you click on the door before you pick up a quest, nothing will happen. You will get text that says, "Shhh...it's a secret," instead.

## Notes

- There is an Easy and Hard version of this instance. Aside from the mobs being tougher, more [[Frozen Token of E'ci]] will drop in the Hard version. All quests can be completed in either version.
- You must complete at least one quest in this zone before [[Gerbi Frostfoot (Frostfell)|Gerbi Frostfoot]] in [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]] will show you what he has to sell.
- The heroic version of this instance is called [[The Icy Keep: The Return (Heroic)]].
