## Walkthrough

1. On [[The Exploded Entryway]] clear all trash MOBs to enable the travel disc at {{waypoint -44.25, -83.64, 5.77}}
1. Travel to [[The Fractured Foyer]]
   - Clear trash MOBs and [[Killclaw (Heroic II)|Killclaw]]/[[Ferroc (Heroic II)|Ferroc]] to enable the travel disc at {{waypoint -140.43, -56.51, -14.13}}
1. Travel to [[The Destroyed Dining Room]]
   - Clear trash MOBs to enable the travel disc at {{waypoint 46.89, -17.88, -86.76}}
1. Travel to [[The Crumbled Kitchen]]
   - Install appropriate Fear Rune in Waist before combat with [[Sinistrad (Heroic II)|Sinistrad]].
   - Clear trash MOBs and [[Croygn (Heroic II)|Croygn]]/[[Sinistrad (Heroic II)|Sinistrad]] to enable the travel disc at {{waypoint 23.29, -27.89, 101.90}}
1. Travel to [[The Sacked Scullery]]
   - Clear trash MOBs to enable the travel disc at {{waypoint -110.68, 8.48, 24.55}}
1. Travel to [[Warehouse Rubble]]
   - Clear trash MOBs and [[The Gooey Gobdrop (Heroic II)|The Gooey Gobdrop]]/[[Sytrax (Heroic II)|Sytrax]] to enable the travel disc at {{waypoint -31.54, 15.06, -85.34}}
1. Travel to [[The Splintered Study]]
   - Clear trash MOBs to enable the travel disc at {{waypoint -1.99, 54.87, 177.95}}
   - One MOB is found out of sight on a piece of furniture up the crystal shard at {{waypoint 20.10, 66.14, 213.44}}
1. Travel to [[Remnants of The Defiled Chapel]]
   - Clear trash MOBs and [[Malius Crum (Heroic II)|Malius Crum]]/[[Gildilisk (Heroic II)|Gildilist]] to enable the travel disc at {{waypoint -91.02, 30.28, 121.30}}
1. Travel to [[The Blasted Bedchamber]]
1. Clear trash MOBs to enable the travel disc at {{waypoint -61.91, 64.31, 174.23}}
1. Travel to [[The Mutilated Torture Chamber of the Damned]]
   - Kill the [[The Hemogorgon (Heroic II)|The Hemogorgon]]/[[The Bonecleaver (Heroic II)|The Bonecleaver]]
1. Exit the dungeon through the portal {{waypoint 40.91, 67.82, -18.48}}
