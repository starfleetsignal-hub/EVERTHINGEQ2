## Background

Recently, the Yha-lei have begun brazenly attacking [[Omen's Call]]. After finding the [[Down by the Lake|stone slab]], it seems plausible that they are searching for, or maybe even have found, this ancient city of Veksar. Regardless, something has stirred up in them a newfound aggression. An adventurer is needed to dive underwater and find out what exactly is going on beneath the waves...

## Notes

- Exit to [[Fens of Nathsar]]: {{waypoint 20, 1, 0}}
- If soloing, mentor down to at least adventure level 85, or the keys will not drop.

## Walkthrough

1. Go to [[Lord Sasil's Encomium]] and turn the statue left. {{waypoint 37, 3, -85}}
1. Go to [[The Lost Chancel]], defeat the [[Squallic Destroyer]] and loot the [[Heavy silver key]] from it's chest.
1. Return to Lord Sasil's Encomium and turn the statue all the way to the right.
1. Go to [[The Campaign Chamber]], defeat [[Himai Kah'Bum]], and loot the [[Tiny brass key]] from his chest.
1. Use the keys on the magical chest that will appear at {{waypoint 75.20, 6.01, -90.99}}.
1. Pick up the scepter from where the chest was and place in on the triangle at {{waypoint 37, 6, -117}}.
1. Go to [[The Runearium]] and read the book at {{waypoint 24, 6, -201}}.
1. Backtrack a bit and defeat [[Krakal Goh-Lum]]. {{waypoint 108, 6, -155}}
1. Continue forward through the rest of the instance and defeat [[Dmeng Bah'Dash]] in [[The Grand Sancloister]]. {{waypoint -22, -3, -157}}
