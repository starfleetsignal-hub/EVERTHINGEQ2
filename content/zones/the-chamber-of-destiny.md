## Background

In the lost shissar city of Chelsith rests a vault built to hide one of the greatest creations on Norrath, the Shissar Calendar. This ancient calendar was built to foretell the fate of Norrath. The shissar used the calendar to foretell the death of their empire. Their attempts to combat that fate and save the shissar race failed. However, one of their creations, a great stone machine, did succeed in containing a small portion of the deadly force that destroyed the Shissar Empire. A small portion of that evil force is more than enough to slay an entire kingdom. Fortunately, a device of great destruction has a guardian of great destruction. The creature that dwells here has done so for ages and for ages it has been transforming its environment, covering much of the secrets of the shissar including a portion of the Shissar Calendar.

## Notes

## Strategy

### Solo

- Strategy 1 (without mercenary):
  - You will need [[The Greenmist Orb]], from the quest [[The Fate of Norrath]].
  - [[The Leviathan]] has the following important abilities:
    - A buff that resists all damage if he is not taking melee damage.
    - Spews acid that quickly breaks your gear.
    - Becomes immune to all damage at 10% health and will quickly regen health if the immunity buff is not removed. To remove the immunity buff, you must use The Greenmist Orb on the *Greenmist Oubliette* (large orb on pedestal in the northern part of chamber) when he reaches 10% health. {{waypoint 5, -24, -102}}
  1. When the Leviathan appears, use any spell or combat ability to bring it down to 10% health. The Leviathan will gain an immunity buff and a green status message will flash across your screen. If you cannot land this hit, leave the instance and zone back in so you can land the shot when it is not in combat and does not have the damage-resist buff.
  1. Click on the *Greenmist Oubliette* to remove the immunity buff. The cast must complete when you are standing next to the oubliette. If the tentacles knock you away, run back to the oubliette.
  1. Melee the Leviathan and spam any damaging abilities. Eventually one will land when the damage-resist buff is not active and the Leviathan will be defeated.
- Strategy 2 (with mercenary):
  - Short version for over-leveled characters wishing to do this encounter solo : 1) Make sure to have The Greenmist Orb on you (from Imzok's Revenge). 2) Stand next to the Greenmist Oubliette (big ball on a pedestal, north side of main room). 3) Pull Leviathan with a (tank) mercenary who should massacre the named (you cannot tank Leviathan because you'll need to use the Greenmist Oubliette, and if Leviathan is not tanked, it will OS you). 4) When Leviathan reaches 10% health it becomes immune. Click on the Greenmist Oubliette that just became usable. Tentacles might bounce you around but once you start casting the spell on the Oubliette, it cannot be interrupted. 5) Once casting is done, immunity subsides and Leviathan will die in an instant.

### Raid

First of all, you'll need to clear [[The Protector's Realm]] and loot [[The Greenmist Orb]] from [[Imzok's Revenge]]. It would be wise to have this **NO-TRADE** item looted by a support (non-dps) class, i.e. a healer or mage -- someone who won't be directly involved with killing [[The Leviathan]].

After you zone in, there are eight or so 84 x4 [[A Yha-lei disciple (Epic)|Yha-lei disciples]] that must be cleared. These mobs each drop a [[Fishman Skin]], which you'll want to assign to high burst dps classes. Scouts are likely the best option here, as well as high spell crit chance, fast-casting mage classes.

Once the Skins have been assigned (from as many of the Yha-lei as you feel like clearing), take a minute to orient yourselves inside the zone. Ensure the person who has The Greenmist Orb knows where the large orb that needs to be clicked later is inside the zone.

You could choose to leave 1 fishman for last, pulling this trash mob up the long hallway (to the "rear" of the zone) and kill it there. This will stop the raid pulling aggro when The Leviathan spawns.

After the last trash mob is killed and The Leviathan pops up in the middle of the room, tentacles will appear around the room. These tentacles lash out from the walls and have an incredible knockback. Try to avoid them.

Once the MT group engages The Leviathan, everybody needs to run straight forward from the hall they are in and move all the way to the edge of the water and attack from there.

The Leviathan has a [[Reflective Aura|reflect]] that is often up ([[Druid|Druids]], [[Crusader|Crusaders]] and [[Brigand|Brigands]] can dispel the effect) that will reflect any spells and debuffs. When it is down, it will only be down for a couple of seconds, so get debuffs in as quickly as possible. Due to The Leviathan's reflect, it is more or less an auto-attack fight for a lot of classes.

As soon as The Leviathan is engaged, someone needs to use a fish skin ("suit up") and become a tasty looking Yha-Lei to be eaten. Pay attention to who has a fish skin on, because they cannot do anything while they have that suit on. They will need to be cured and healed constantly until they are eaten. After 20-50 seconds this person will be eaten. That person will be ported into The Leviathan's stomach with 1 to 6 devourers around them. They must kill the devourers and loot the [[Volatile Fluid|Volatile Fluids]] from their bodies. Kill and loot as many as possible before dying or being spat out!

If this is your first time, you might just "farm" fluids until you have 50 or so, leaving your few casters with 5 fish skin uses and 1 or 2 scouts with a few fish skin uses also. If you feel like doing so, don't fret about keeping a few of the Skins, the Yha-leis will respawn in about two days' time.

Once The Leviathan is down to approximately 15% health, the Greenmist Oubliette within The Chamber of Destiny can be deactivated. The raid member who has [[The Greenmist Orb]] from Protector's Realm needs to sprint straight back to the hallway and along the north wall to the Greenmist Oubliette at  {{waypoint 2.00, -24.00, -108.00}} to deactivate it. Make sure this person has heals with them, or is given Salve, and also has 4 or 5 Dedicated Noxious Potions so they can cure themselves. It would be wise for this player to start heading that way near 25% health.

Finish off The Leviathan and congratulations on attaining access to [[Veeshan's Peak]]!
