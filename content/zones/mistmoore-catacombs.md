## Background

This is the the dank underbelly beneath [[Castle Mistmoore]]. The section that is attached to the castle is occupied by [[Seneschal D'naraz]], whose primary responsibilities are to manage the servants, and look after the [[A Tunarian guest|Tunarian]] and [[A Crushbone guest|Crushbone]] guests. There are also a couple of experimental laboratories, one used for converting Myr'Dal into worthy vessels and the other for nefarious blood tests. Far below there is a enormous chasm, where [[Mayong Mistmoore]] and his vampires permit the Myr'Dal and werewolves to dwell, as they are excellent at scaring off any fool-hardy adventures that would stumble in from the [[Loping Plains]].
