## Background

This is the former court of the now-scorned Sarnak hero, [[Korucust]]. Long ago, he was a member of [[Overking Bathezid]]'s council, and was essential in breaking ties with the deceitful dragons who had sent a secret agent into the council. With his help, the former Overking began a campaign that would drive the dragons from [[Kunark (Continent)|Kunark]] and raise the Sarnak nation. But Korucust was deceived, and along with him the hopes of the Sarnak nation rising.

A band of powerful adventures invaded [[Chardok]] and battled all the way to the Great Throne Room and slew Overking Bathezid before the eyes of his council. After his death, the destitute councilmen blamed the failure of the defenses on Korocust and his elite caste, the [[An entrapped soul of Shai'din|Shai'din]]. They publicly claimed that he had conspired with [[Venril Sathir]], the supposed mastermind of the assault.

Korokust and his caste were imprisoned in his personal court and over time the place of former majesty was turned into a crypt of eternal dread and misery through black arts. Those who enter, never depart...

## Notes

- You must have an [[Ethernere Crystal]], looted from [[The Bridge Keeper]], in your inventory to access this instance.
- To summon [[Korucust]] you need to complete the quest [[The Bones of Korucust]].
