## Walkthrough

### [[The Butcher]] (Tier 4)

1. Kill the cage that traps a random player. Dispel often. Burn.

### [[The Returned Foe of Legend]] (Tier 4) (Zarrakon)

1. Hard burn.
1. Access: Make sure to pick up the bone from the pile for the Mayong fight.

### [[The Vampire Queen]] (Tier 4)

1. Near the beginning of the fight, an add will spawn. If it is the "Rage" add, it can be tanked near the boss by the off-tank and ignored. If it is the other add (which does proximity knock-ups), it can be tanked by the off-tank on the opposite side of the room and ignored. It will periodically cause an arcane detriment, but it's likely easier to deal with than spending DPS on the add, since it will just repop if it is killed.
1. Throughout the fight, the queen will activate statues around the room, which will turn them translucent/blue.
   - Someone who has not yet clicked a statue in the raid needs to go and click the statue she activates, which will place an uncurable curse on the clicker - they cannot click any further statues or they will wipe the raid. It's usually best to rotate through the raid in a specific order (top-to-bottom, bottom-to-top, etc.) to make it clear who needs to click next. The names of the statues, clockwise from the one to your right when you just walk in, are: Finality, Time, Wonder, Loyalty
   - If you run out of people to click statues, you have until she activates all 4 statues to kill her. Note that when enough statues are lit up, she will also get a damage shield (health bar turns purple).
1. The queen will also place a hex on one person periodically through the fight. The player selected needs to look at their detriment window for the "Dark Tidings" detriment. It will have an effect that states "Dark Tidings can be removed by PLAYER, if not removed properly, Lenya will gain the upper hand on your raid party!" - "PLAYER" in the above will be in yellow text, and will be someone in the raid. That player is the only person that can rescue the hexed player without causing a raid wipe. You have about 24 seconds for this to occur, or the failure to act will trigger a raid wipe.![Rescue Hex in Lenya Thex fight](images/LenyaThex_DarkTidings2.png)
   - ![Lenya Thex rescue dialogue in Castle Vacrul: Haunting Presence raid zone](images/LenyaThex_Rescue2.png)The "PLAYER" specified needs to right click the hexed player in the raid window, and choose "Rescue from the Hex!" which will appear as the 3rd-from-the-bottom choice (above "Who" and "Trade") in the context menu that pops up.

### [[Poppet the Thrasher]] (Tier 4)

1. Burn.

### [[Lord of the Castle]] (Tier 5)

1. There is a 32 minute timer for this encounter
1. Set up your raid with essentially 2 MT style groups and 1 off tank group.
   - One tank and off tank will handle Mayong and adds for the duration of the fight.
   - The other MT-style group (aka "Poppit Group") acts as its own entity during the fight from 90% onward.
1. You can pull the named almost anywhere for the first 85%, however to make later scripts easier, it's recommended to pull him to the top of the stairs, right beside the cubby where adds come from. Doing so funnels adds directly in and is where you will need to be for the final script.
1. Constantly dispell Mayong to keep his increments at zero.
1. Periodically will cast "Juxtaposed Hex" which requires each player to either ignore the hex or cancel it in the detriment window.
   1. You can tell which it is by mousing over the detriment in the detriment window - the yellow text will either be "be cancelled" if you should cancel it, or "expire naturally" if you should leave it alone.
1. Pull Mayong and burn to 90%.
1. At 95%, adds begin spawning two at a time from the cubby, KILL THEM, they are priority as their AOE hurts!
1. Mayong has a joust out script.
   - Key words for ACT trigger: *blood filled husk to a fight*
   - Everyone other than the tank with Mayong’s aggro needs to joust to 36+M
   - Joust back in key words for ACT trigger: *bites into all nearby and heals his wounds*
1. Mayong also will place a blanket (Dark purple black bubble) on 1 member of your raid, move out of this.
   - If the raid is stacked up a simple 10m shimmy to the side is the best way to handle the blanket.
   - Blanket can spawn on the "Poppit Group". They just need to get out of the effect area.
1. Mayong also requires 1 person of your raid to be 50+M away from him at all times or you get silenced.
   - For the initial burn, put anyone out. Once you reach the 90% script, the "Poppit Group" will take care of this.
1. Uncurable curse
   - The final thing Poppit does during the entire fight is an uncurable curse. IF it goes on a tank, they must tank swap (OT pick up aggro on Mayong)
   - MT run 50m away and your uncurable will be removed.
   - If the curse goes on someone other than a tank, they must run up to Mayong and the curse will be curable, cure it!
1. Poppit Group
   - This group's job is to stay alive and keep the archers dead.
   - The "Poppit Group" home location is in the middle of the area in front of the pool of blood so that they can quickly move to any of the the 6 locations where archers spawn (on top of the pedestals).
   - Tank must wear the red rune [[Were-Withall]]. *Do not cure* the curses off of the tank who is tanking Poppit.
   - To kill the archers, you must have the red rune on and be shapeshifted into a werewolf. Run up to the archer, right click and select the option to bite.
   - Constantly dispell Poppit to keep his increments at zero.
   - Members of this group will at times get the uncurable curse on them and have to run to Mayong to get it removed.
   - Poppit has a long-casting AOE that puts an uncurable elemental on you, most of this group should avoid this by jousting 16m from Poppit.
   - Sigils
   - One DPS (for instance, a dirge) can handle the Sigil.
     - At 60%, 45%, and 30%, a Sigil will spawn in one of 3 locations. This Sigil has a barrier that must be removed via masterstrike, then killed.
       - Back left on the round circle
       - Back right on the round circle
       - In the middle circle up by where the named is pulled
     - At 5% after the Sigil spawns, Mayong will attempt to feast off of it. he turns into a bat and flies to it. the Sigil must be dead before he does this!
   - At 15-16%, Mayong will go to his coffin under the bloodfall. The "Poppit Group" should move here at about 17% to be there waiting. Once Mayong arrives, interrupt his healing via right-click interrupt or spamming F key. Mayong will then return to the raid.
   - SLOWLY Bring Mayong down to about 12% health and wait for the next “Blanket” script. After this, move Mayong into the cubby so that his LOS is broken from the coffin. A member of the "Poppit Group" should have a bone (Zarrakon broken rib), which is found in the room where Zarrakon was. Once Mayong  is inside the cubby, place the bone into the coffin. MAKE SURE MAYONG does not get line of sight to this coffin from this point on. Burn Mayong to 10% and win!
