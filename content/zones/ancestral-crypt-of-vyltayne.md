## Notes

1. This Dungeon has a maximum duration of 3 days. The associated daily quest may be randomly reissued after 18 hours while an old, empty instance of this zone is still active. When entering this dungeon, if it is empty then leave the zone and use the Reset Button in the Dungeon Finder's Persistent Instances tab to reset the 'Ancestral Crypt of Vyl'Tayne' zone timer.
1. The dungeon is a Solo Instance zone therefore a mercenary can not be used nor can another player provide any assistance. If appropriate carry healing potions, remedies, etc.
