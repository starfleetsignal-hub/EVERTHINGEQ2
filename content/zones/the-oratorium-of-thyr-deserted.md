## Notes

- This instance is totally void of monsters, and can be used to quickly access [[Nagafen's Lair]] without having to defeat [[Flame Lord Thyr]] every time you wish to do so.
- You must have completed the quest, [[To Speak as a Dragon]], and have completed the instance [[The Oratorium of Thyr]] at least once, to be given an option to zone into this instance.
