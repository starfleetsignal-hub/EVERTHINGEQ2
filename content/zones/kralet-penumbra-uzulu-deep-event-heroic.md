## Notes

To spawn the named look at the orb above the statue for the color that is needed then get all 4 orbs with the same color.

1. You have to click the orbs until one corner only has the color that is needed, with all 3 other corners having the same, different color.
1. Once you managed to have that setup, click the diagonally opposite corner to the one with the correct color until you have all 4 corners of the same color.

For example:

- Lets say you need all 4 red. Click the orbs until you have only ONE red orb in a corner and 3 of the same color i.e. blue, purple or white. Now say you have one red and 3 blue for example, keep on clicking the blue orb on the diagonally OPPOSITE side to the red one until eventually you get all four red.

OR:

1. Puzzle 1 - Statue Orb is Purple, press orbs in the following order:
   - Bottom Left
   - Top Right
   - Top Left
   - Bottom Right.
   - Kill the 2 adds that spawn plus [[Kyrogna]].
1. Puzzle 2 - Statue Orb is Red, press orbs in the following order:
   - Bottom Left
   - Bottom Left
   - Bottom Left
   - Top Right
   - Kill the 2 adds that spawn plus attack [[Jinsky Sparehopper]] on the platform to update statue orb colour.
1. Puzzle 3 - Statue Orb is Blue, press orbs in  the following order:
   - Top Right > Bottom Left > Top Right > Bottom Left > Top Right > Top Left.
1. NOTE: Clicking the brazier at the top where the two gnomes were at will immolate them and activate the 'Challenge Mode' for the final named.
   - Kill [[Urzzra-Uzulu]] the named that spawns.

Note: Clicking the middle pillar just resets the puzzle back to the starting position for the current puzzle being attempted. This does not change the statue's orb colour. Each reset will spawn 2 adds.
