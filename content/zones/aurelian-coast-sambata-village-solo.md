## Note

- Enter the cave at  {{waypoint 143.31, 85.40, -485.52}}  Zone into "Sambata Village (Solo)"  from 112.64 58.26 -654.15 346.78
- This zone starts with a barricade. Double click it.
- You can bypass most trash mobs.

## Steps

1. Click "access" on the Barrier. (Note:  You can not fly in this instance.)
1. [[Ercel Bloodpaw]] at {{waypoint 100, 75, -268}}
   - Level 122 Heroic Wolf.
1. [[Grugnop, the Guard]] at {{waypoint 28, 71, -430}}
   - Level 122 Heroic grouped with 2 wolves
     - has a short leash
     - Will port you.
       - You have to get back to him ASAP or the encounter will reset.
       - Stay near the center; if you get ported up a wall, or go past the single large boulder, it will reset.
1. [[Grrrunk the Trunk]] {{waypoint 55, 78, -570}}
   - Summons wolf adds [[A Sambata wolf]] with target lock/curse. --> Defeat [[A Sambata wolf]] as you are not able to heal you as long as you are cursed.
1. [[Ryryrd of the Wind]] {{waypoint -24, 88, -627}}
   - Is a group
   - During fight the mob cast winds, which throw you up. Find a safer spot with no add risks.
   - Looks like the mob has a tiny mana-drain.
1. [[Purpyron the Massive]] and his add [[Mrokor, the Smartist]]{{waypoint -100, 82, -694}}
   - First defeat [[Mrokor, the Smartist]] as this mob is much easier.

## Reward

- At least 67,200 status
- [[Mark of Twilight]]
