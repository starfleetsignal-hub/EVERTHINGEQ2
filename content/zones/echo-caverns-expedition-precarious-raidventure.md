## Walkthrough

There is an access quest for Vex Thal, the last area of the expansion, found in Echo Caverns. Vashlyn sells Announcement: Pops’ Raidventure Tours for 250 Bazaar Crowns. The Crowns can be gotten from weekly and daily dungeon quests. Examining the announcement will offer you the quest [[A Bounty is in Order|A Bounty Is In Order]]. You will need the Scepter of Illumination to access the last boss in Vex 1. More details will be below. Savage Weald: Shadow’s Hold is not part of this questline. The trophies in this expansion are *crafted*. The Reflections of the Grandmaster Vol. IV can be purchased from Vashlyn for raid currency. There is a 1 use and a permanent version available.
