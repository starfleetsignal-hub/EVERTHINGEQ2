## Background

Although a crypt, the [[Spirit of Vox]] persists, giving hope to [[Lord Nagafen (Nagafen's Lair)|Lord Nagafen]] that one day his [[Lady Vox|former love]] can be successfully resurrected.

## Guide

- If your goal is to speak with [[Lady Vox]] for the quest, [[Fire and Ice]], then while in the instance, the quest-bearer must:
  1. Examine the three essence shards in the following order: Soul --> Heart --> Mind.
  1. Then, interact with the totem in the middle of the platform. This will cause the quest-bearer to die.
     - If soloing, you must find a way to resurrect yourself as there is no respawn inside of the instance. If you release, you will respawn outside of the instance and lose all of your essence shards. The easiest way is to use [[Daybreak Cash]] to resurrect where you died.
     - Known as of 7/1/26, there is the option to revive inside the Crypt and then proceed to run back to The Spirit of Vox (L55 x 4)
- If your goal is bring low the mighty [[Spirit of Vox]], then just have just have a hapless volunteer march up to the totem and click. They will die. Three groups of ice shards will spawn and attack, followed by the Spirit of Vox herself.
