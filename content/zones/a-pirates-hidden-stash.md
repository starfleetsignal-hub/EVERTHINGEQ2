## Walkthrough

Pirates have been seen digging along The Thundering Steppes' northeastern beach. Pirates digging can only mean one thing: treasure.

The instance is straightforward; make your way to the back of the tunnel at {{waypoint 836, -19, 1067}}, killing everything you encounter. If you're lucky, two named mobs will be up: [[Yukkoz]] (level 25, at the end of the tunnels) and/or [[Blark the Plank Pusher]] (level 25, in an alcove halfway down the tunnels at {{waypoint 914, -20, 1083}}).
