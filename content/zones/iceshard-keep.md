## Notes

- There is an [[Agnostic Dungeons|agnostic]] version of this dungeon available to players level 10 through 95.
- The aggro range for mobs is quite large, so keep your distance.
- Many of the trash mobs have a frontal or AoE that they use almost immediately upon pull, so keep your distance, use stoneskins, have wards, or throw up AoE blockers.

## Walkthrough

1. [[Krindem Sigmundr]] {{waypoint -564, 99, 107}}
   - Will engage almost immediately after you take a step inside the zone
1. If on the quest [[Rage Against the War Machine]], pause near the end of the long hallway before you reach the **Great Hall**, so that you can listen the discussion which will update the quest.
1. [[Veteran Gandrfinn]] {{waypoint -695, 117, 330}}
   - First room on the left, rare spawn
1. [[Sjurd Randskeggr]] {{waypoint -656, 148, 297}}
   - First room on the left, up the lift
1. [[Grundr Kolfrodr]] {{waypoint -473, 148, 280}}
   - First room on the right, up the lift
1. If on the quest [[A Prince Amongst Kromzeks]], clear the **Trophy Room** and knock over the statue at {{waypoint -460, 119, 436}} to distract the guards BEFORE taking on [[Halkon Tormax]].
1. [[Halkon Tormax]] {{waypoint -695, 118, 550}}
   - Second room on the left, has two linked adds
1. [[Vilmen Riggandr]] {{waypoint -452, 118, 569}}
   - Second room on the right
1. [[A Drakkel bedbug]] {{waypoint -441, 118, 504}}
   - Key mob skitters between the beds in the **Barracks**, kill and loot the key to open the chest at {{waypoint -649, 118, 279}}
1. [[Praefectus Kriegr]] {{waypoint -558, 121, 555}}
   - Center room in the back
