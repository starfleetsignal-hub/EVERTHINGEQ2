## Background

For some reason you decided to explore a sewer in the middle of the desert. Rather than landing in a pile of dried-out dervish fecal matter, you've discovered the hide-out of dervish thieves.

## Notes

- For the entrance: at the top of the [[Sandslide Passage]], make a sharp left and continue along this upper shelf past the Rujarkian raider camps to reach the sewer grate way at the end.
- Make sure to pick up the quest [[Hidden Cache (Quest)|Hidden Cache]] if this is your first time entering this instance.
- If you're going there for the Chrono-mission, you do not have to do the quest to complete the mission.
- If you're looking for loot drops / transmuting, do NOT kill Mullok until last, the nameds seem to de-pop once he is down.
