## Lore

[[The Duality]] provides the following background about the [[Research Library of Mayong Mistmoore]], and why it is important to visit:

"But there is one more source of knowledge about shissar artifacts, and if you truly need to learn the purpose of this codex, I fear you may need to consult it."

"As you may be aware, Mayong Mistmoore has also been studying the shissar prophecies extensively, hoping to gain some advantage himself. His library may contain some information I don't have."

"I have the ability to open a portal for one person into Mistmoore's study. You may have to evade or deal with any patrolling guards, but if you can make a transcript of Mistmoore's research without being apprehended, we should have all we need."

## Map

This zone is based on a portion of [[Mistmoore's Inner Sanctum]].

## Notes

As this is part of the [[Altar of Malice Crafting Timeline]], you are expected to avoid all encounters in this zone.  The mobs are generally scaled to a higher adventure level to discourage combat.
