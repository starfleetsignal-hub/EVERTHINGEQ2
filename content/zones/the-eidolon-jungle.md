## Geography

based on [[The Feerrott]] layout

### Travel

- First entry is via the green reality portal from [[The Feerrott]]. It is easiest to travel to Feerott using a [[Ulteran Spires|Wizard Spire]], as the Eidolon Jungle portal {{waypoint -86.30, 13.63, 176.64}} is right next to the Spire.
- The Tourbillion at the far far east side of zone links to [[Obol Plains]]
