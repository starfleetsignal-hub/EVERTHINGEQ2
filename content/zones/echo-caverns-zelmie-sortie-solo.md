## Walkthrough

1. Throughout the zone, kill [[A Zel Mannen spore|Zel Mannan spores]] to shrink yourself. To travel and damage the names, you will need to be small. The spores do not always respawn so you will have to evac at times and do the run all over again.
1. Quickly click **Mushroom Manor** while you are still small {{waypoint 658.62, 13.85, -319.20}}.
1. Kill spores to stay small until you spawn [[Kwillix Quickshot]] {{waypoint 717.39, 32.35, -346.77}}. Kill him.
   - Kill zoomie adds ASAP; have pet/merc on assist, retarget and take them down fast.
1. Quickly click **Mushroom Manor** while you are still small {{waypoint 736.60, 28.79, -341.36}}.
1. Kill [[Steena Dragonflyer]] {{waypoint 758.79, 11.84, -517.48}} and her waves of adds. Joust out for her conal "Buzz Off" ability - it follows your direction.
1. Quickly click **Mushroom Manor** while you are still small {{waypoint 729.26, 12.13, -520.50}}
1. Hail [[Frindel Sporemiser]] then kill him {{waypoint 675.23, 18.36, -440.31}}.
   - Get out of Frindel's Bolete Blast ASAP or it will kill you.
