## Notes

- There is an [[Agnostic Dungeons|agnostic]] version of this dungeon available to players level 10 through 95.

## Named

[[Gavitzle (Heroic)|Gavitzle]]

Hail him to begin the fight. After a little bit a dialog, he will summon a group of six or so skeletons to fight you. Once those are dead, he will transport away and attach a bomb to you that goes off in 15 seconds. Gaviztle is impervious to damage the entire fight. You must chase after him as he teleports all around, and the bomb latched to you will latch onto his slime-shield. The bomb will go off after 5 seconds with a nasty knockback. Be sure to secure yourself against a wall or in a corner. After you repeat this cycle a few times he will eventually say quits and separate from his slime-shield. Before he dies, however, Cugdava will immolate him and take him away. The slime-shield drops the loot.

[[The Quarry Behemoth (Heroic)|The Quarry Behemoth]]

Kill the group of undead quarriers around the Behemoth's body to start the fight. An immolated will appear and activate the Behemoth, in which it will rise up and begin the fight. The Behemoth has a buff that absorbs 100 attacks before taking damage. You can either burn him down with DPS or use one of the crushers to get his buff removed (the crusher switches are on the wall on each side of the crushers).

[[Tothrak (Heroic)|Tothrak]]

Kill the six armorsmiths around the two forges in order to damage Tothrak. Each kill depletes an aspect of him, such as cracking his helmet or shattering his hammer. Once all armorsmiths are dead, a simple tank and spank.

[[The Fire Gazer (Heroic)|The Fire Gazer]]

Stand next to the river of lava in order to attract the smell of the magma walkers. They will come in waves, two at a time, from each end of the lava river. Stay at the edge or else they will lose your scent. After 5 or so waves The Fire Gazer will appear at the beginning of the river and wade his way towards the center. Tank and spank.

[[Cugdava (Heroic)|Cugdava]]

Depending on where she pops up, she will transform into the boss for that area.

## Achievements

The achievement awarded depends on the order in which you kill the first four nameds. The following have been found to work, but are not the only possibilities.

1. Kill order for [[Victory: Cugdava and Gavitzle I]]:
   1. [[Gavitzle (Heroic)]]
   1. [[The Quarry Behemoth (Heroic)]]
   1. [[The Fire Gazer (Heroic)]]
   1. [[Tothrak (Heroic)]]
      - Final Kill is in [[Metal Mercantile District]]
1. Kill order for [[Victory: Cugdava and Gavitzle II]]:
   1. [[Gavitzle (Heroic)]]
   1. [[The Quarry Behemoth (Heroic)]]
   1. [[Tothrak (Heroic)]]
   1. [[The Fire Gazer (Heroic)]]
      - Final Kill is in [[The Deep Quarry]]
1. Kill order for [[Victory: Cugdava and Gavitzle III]]:
   1. [[Tothrak (Heroic)]]
   1. [[The Fire Gazer (Heroic)]]
   1. [[The Quarry Behemoth (Heroic)]]
   1. [[Gavitzle (Heroic)]]
      - Final Kill is in [[Forge of Brell]]
1. Kill order for [[Victory: Cugdava and Gavitzle IV]]:
   1. [[Tothrak (Heroic)]]
   1. [[The Fire Gazer (Heroic)]]
   1. [[Gavitzle (Heroic)]]
   1. [[The Quarry Behemoth (Heroic)]]
      - Final kill is in [[Warsmith's Lifeblood]]
