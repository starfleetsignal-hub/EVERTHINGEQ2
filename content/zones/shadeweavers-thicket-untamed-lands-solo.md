## Steps

1. Slay [[Goretilla]] on [[Alphaweaver Hill]] {{waypoint -79.60, 65.98, -414.93}}.
   - Kill all the nearby [[A shadeweaver matriarch|shadeweaver matriarchs]] and [[A young shadeweaver|young shadeweavers]] first, or they'll add during fight.
   - Kill the [[Shadeweaver silk strand|shadeweaver silk strands]] that spawn during fight to remove invulnerability.
   - Stifle Rune
1. Slay [[Sotek]] at [[Whisperling Falls (Untamed Lands)]] {{waypoint -247.02, 39.03, -130.97}}
   - Find the glowing red beacon {{waypoint -252.69, 37.63, -131.41}} on the edge of the island. Click to **Honor thy spirits**.
   - Cure and get off the island ASAP, pull Sotek to the land opposite the island. (Every 20% or so he will port you back to his island and 'root' you with chains. When you land, cure arcane then pull back to mainland). Somehow, he is harder to kill as long as he is on the island, but relatively easy once you get him to the dry land on the other side. Casters, make sure you have a healing mercenary up.
1. Slay [[Nok Hakka]] in the [[Gor Taku Burn Out]] {{waypoint 50.10, 51.44, -155.29}} (at first his name is *a Gor Taku raid leader*).
1. Slay [[Spiritist Karina]] on the [[Plateau of Broken Spirits]] {{waypoint 175.86, 57.69, -554.19}}, across the bridge.
   - At the top of the hill, click the **Vah Shir Bones**. {{waypoint 133, 64, -576}} Kill the [[A lesser shade|lesser shades]] around the edge of the plateau, then Karina.
   - Any [[A lesser shade|lesser shades]] that repop will heal Karina, so you have to keep burning them down.
1. Slay [[The Desolator]] on [[The Desolate Grounds (Untamed Lands)]] {{waypoint 376.99, 17.74, -550.60}}.
   - Head west into the obsidian shards to find the Desolator.
   - Clearing out the [[Centi Liako Xakra]], [[Diablo Thall Xakra]], [[Zun Zethon Xakra]], and [[Va Senshali Xakra]] is a good idea, as the Desolator will keep porting you into the lightning patches. Dispelling them is helpful. Pull them out of the lightning which buffs them.
   - Every 10% or so he will open two portals and have you follow him through the one he went through. Go through, DPS him, repeat.
