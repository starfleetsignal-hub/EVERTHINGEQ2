## Progression

Upon zoning in there is hallway to the northwest and a large unattackable 'an overwhelming resentment' directly to your right that will not allow you to pass at the moment. Take the northwest hallway.

[[The Crypt of Disdain]]

Kill the [[A furious spark]] and then the 'Urn of Emotions' that become attackable. This will eventually spawn [[Corpus Amygdalus]]. Killing him will remove the ice blocking the way to the room at the top of the ramp. Head that way now.

[[The Dismal Hollow]]

- The key mob [[A wandering experiment]] roams a small area behind the south crypt.
- [[A vexing fear]] are unattackable until you get within a few few, then turn into aggressive [[A tormenting anguish]].

Talk to the Shade of Miragul up the set of stairs to the west who tells you to fetch legs from the north crypt and arms from the south crypt. Head into the north crypt to kill [[Faenelae]], looting the chest behind them for the legs. Next head to the south crypt and kill [[Blood Sucker]], looting the chest behind them for the arms. Return to the Shade of Miragul and give the body parts, which will lead to the spawning of [[Atrocious Amalgam]] down the small ramp. Killing them will spawn a barrel back near the entrance of the zone and he also drops a [[Music Box Handle]] that you can be inserted into a music box in the previous room [[The Dismal Hollow]] to stop the [[A vexing fear]] from spawning. Head back to the entrance and right click the barrel to light the fuse. This will cause it to roll under and kill the resentment that was previously keeping you from going east. Head down the newly opened hallway.

[[The Pool of Reflection]]

Intersection room used to get to the last two areas of the map that contain named, [[The Fundament of Sorcery]] to the south-east and [[The Crucible of Woe]] under the frozen pond through an underwater tunnel. Head to the south-east first.

[[The Fundament of Sorcery]]

There are four pillars with different colored images on them around the room, all with 3 rotatable rows. All the way to the east is the platform that contains the image for each of the four colors on the floor for each pillar. Once you know the correct image to use, return to each of the pillars and you'll see an icicle at the base pointing up like an arrow. This is the row you want to align the correct image in for each of the pillars. Right click will allow you to turn the row in the stack left, right, or center. Once you have the image in the appropriate column and row, the corresponding color and pattern will light up on the platform to the west. Repeat this for the other three pillars. Upon finishing the last pillar, the [[Doppleganger Encounter]] will spawn up on the platform, which mirrors your current group layout. Kill them and proceed back to the prior room, this time going under the frozen pond.

[[The Crucible of Woe]]

Swim down under the pond through the tunnel and back up into this room. Kill [[Patriae Vykel]], who is the final named in the instance.
