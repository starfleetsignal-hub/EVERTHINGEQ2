The layout of this [[Tradeskill Instance]] is not the same as the others. It is a simpler model used only on the starter islands.

## Entrance

Entrance at {{waypoint -20, -3, 154}} in [[Queen's Colony]] and [[Outpost of the Overlord]]

## Features

### Queen's Colony

The tradeskill instance in Queen's Colony contains the following amenities (same layout as [[The Ironforge Exchange|Ironforge Exchange]], minus a few amenities):

- [[Work Order Clipboard]] x24, one on a wall or shelf near each crafting station
- [[Newman Hines]], the Fuel and Recipe Merchant (Wholesaler) {{waypoint -0.4, -0.48, -9.32}}
- [[Chemistry Table]] x3 {{waypoint 2.82, -6.45, 23.31}}
- [[Engraved Desk]] x 3 {{waypoint -22.79, -6.44, 21.92}}
- [[Forge]] x6 {{waypoint -11.61, -6.41, -32.58}}
- [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]] x3 -1,86 -6.48 0.73
- [[Stove & Keg]] x3 {{waypoint -22.6, -6.44, 22.09}}
- [[Woodworking Table]] x3 {{waypoint -1.86, -6.48, 0.73}}
- [[Work Bench]] x3 {{waypoint 2.82, -6.45, 23.31}}

The instance does not contain a Rush Order foreman, a Work Order foreman, a [[Broker]], a [[Bank]], a [[Crafting Trainer]], a [[Tradeskill Tutorial]], a [[Tradeskill Career Counselor]], a Trades Coordinator (grandmaster), an NPC for Adorning and Tinkering daily tasks, or a faction merchant.

### Outpost of the Overlord

The tradeskill instance in Outpost of the Overlord contains the following amenities (same layout as [[The Coalition of Tradesfolke Workshop]], minus a few amenities):

- [[Work Order Clipboard]] x26, one on a wall or partition near each crafting station
- [[Garfa Gillgrinder]], the Fuel and Recipe Merchant (Wholesaler) {{waypoint 1.52, -6.48, 11.46}}
- [[Chemistry Table]] x3 {{waypoint -11.53, -0.17, -8.44}}
- [[Engraved Desk]] x 4 {{waypoint -23.62, -19.65, 6.27}}
- [[Forge]] x4 {{waypoint -12.6, -19.78, 5.7}}
- [[Sewing Table & Mannequin]] x6 {{waypoint -6.98, -6.61, 1.82}}
- [[Stove & Keg]] x3 {{waypoint -14.53, -13.1, 13.57}}
- [[Woodworking Table]] x3 {{waypoint -12.16, -13.1, -1.4}}
- [[Work Bench]] x3 {{waypoint -14.85, -0.17, 8.05}}

The instance does not contain a Rush Order foreman, a Work Order foreman, a [[Broker]], a [[Bank]], a [[Crafting Trainer]], a [[Tradeskill Tutorial]], a [[Tradeskill Career Counselor]], a Trades Coordinator (grandmaster), an NPC for Adorning and Tinkering daily tasks, or a faction merchant.
