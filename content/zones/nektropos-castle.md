## Lore

Referred to also as *The House of Everling*, Nektropos Castle sits isolated in the northeastern corner of [[Nektulos Forest]]. It is unclear who first built the castle, however it was most likely the dark elves. Some features of the structure share a similarity with Teir'Dal design, yet it doesn't possess exactly the same aesthetic as the other dark elf buildings found in Nektulos. Regardless, at some point in the past, Nektropos came under the ownership of the Everling Family.

For generations, the Everlings amassed great wealth in the manufacture and discovery of toys and trinkets that they would then sell to the wealthiest citizens of [[Norrath]]. The Everling family reached its financial zenith under Vulrandis Everling, who was the Merchant Lord of [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]]. During one particularly long trinket-hunting expedition, Vularandis discovered his 'true commodity', the 'cursed curios'. These were evil trinkets that possessed dark and corrupting attributes. The most malign of these artifacts was *[[The Idol of Everling|The Idol of Mor'Tael]]*, which became the most precious keepsake of the Everling Family.

![An Everling Family portrait; showing Rikantus as the current 'Lord Everling', flanked on both sides by his daughters.](images/Everlingfamily2.png)
The Everlings have always been led by the current patriarch of the respective generation, referred to as 'Lord Everling'. When the great, great grandson of Vulrandis, [[Rikantus Everling|Rikantus]], became Lord Everling he continued his family's legacy of searching for and selling cursed trinkets. However, this Lord Everling would take the Family on an even darker path. Believing all his sons to be dead, he converted his own daughters into golems, proven by golem-specific items dropping from their corpses. Furthermore, Rikantus made it the purpose of the [[Lord Everling's Secret Laboratory|Everling Laboratory]] to 'animate golems.' *- (Source: [[The Precious Fairy Book]])*

However Rikantus' malice didn't stop there. He began to construct his own personal undead army:  "[I have] sewn into their leaves of flesh what little of the charms I have left, not nearly enough for my entire army I shall craft. Their numbers shall swell as I lure more components into my clutches. I shall deconstruct them and make them anew. They will enter into the ranks of my patchwork soldiers." *- (Source: [[The Charm of the Brotherhood]])*

Yet Rikantus' most noteworthy exploit was his involvement in the creation of the [[A doll of Nyth|dolls of Nyth]]. Being a true Everling businessman, when Rikantus was contracted by The Thexians to develop a weapon for them, he didn't hesitiate: "...with these entities I was able to control the actions of the dolls. These tall lanky beings formed from the [[Marionette of Nyth|husks of the vile ettins]] made great vessels for these entities. And along with the maggots and entrails that composed their insides I too could hide the twin snake...within the little ones." *- (Source: The Dolls of Nyth, quest starter for [[The Secrets Within]])*

At some point Rikantus left the castle and the title of Lord Everling was left abandoned. Recently, whispers around Nektulos Forest share that there could be a new [[Maltus Everling|Lord of Nektropos Castle]]. Peculiar [[A patchwork fleshripper (Nektulos Forest)|patchwork golems]] and other [[A decaying flesh being|undead beasts]] have been seen wandering the area surrounding the castle. Unfortunately for the few remaining innocent denizens of Nektulos, The Freeport Militia, local merchants and even the wildlife won't venture anywhere near the castle grounds. The evil contained in Nektropos is so unsettling that even the undead dragoons of Gul'Thex have placed a ward upon the walls of the castle. No unwelcome guest can enter, save for the bravest of adventurers.

*For more information on Nektropos Castle and the House of Everling, complete the [[Nektropos Castle Timeline]], read [[The Idol of Mor'Tael]], and visit this [ZAM article](http://eq2.zam.com/wiki/The_Everling_Saga).*

## Walkthrough

Nektropos Castle is a confusing labyrinth of darkened corridors and secret passageways. There are no dropped quests in Nektropos Castle, so the mobs can be killed without any need to see if they dropped a chest. There are a few examined quests, though, which can be found [[Nektropos Castle Timeline|here]]. Below is a step-by-step guide to unlock the different levels of the dungeon. Good luck!

### First Level

1. Zone into the [[Nektropos Castle Courtyard|main courtyard]]. All the paths leading off the courtyard, except for one, are connected by a passageway that snakes along the perimeter of the first level.
1. Stepping into the courtyard prompts the quest, [[Visions of Hatred]]. Accept it.
1. Taking the east door, go to [[The Chapel (Nektropos Castle)|The Chapel]] {{waypoint -36, 0, 53}}. Kill the four [[A priestess of Ullkorruuk|priestesses of Ullkorruuk]] to spawn the [[Inquisitor of Ulkorruuk]]. Kill the Inquisitor to spawn [[Alexa (NPC)|Alexa,]] who tells you to find a hidden note in the [[Nektropos Castle Game Room|Game Room]].
1. Leave The Chapel and turn right. Head down the passageway until you find a mounted boar's head at {{waypoint -12, 0, 41}}. Examine the boar's head to remove its marble black eye and initiate the quest, [[The Boar's Head]].
1. Go to the [[Nektropos Castle Kitchen|Guests Dining Room]] and inspect the mounted boar's head at {{waypoint 30, 0, -33}} to complete [[The Boar's Head]], granting you permanent access to the [[Nektropos Castle Barracks|barracks]].
1. Go to the [[The Servants' Quarters (Nektropos Castle)|Servants' Quarters]] at {{waypoint -31, 0, -38}} and slay [[An Everling manservant|Everling manservants]] until you find [[Guestroom key 01]]. Inspect the key to complete [[Visions of Hatred]], granting you permanent access to the balcony doors on the second level.
1. Go to the barracks, examine the locker-box at {{waypoint 17, 0, 32}} to initiate the quest, [[The Red Marble]].
1. With the red marble in hand, return to the original mounted boar's head at {{waypoint -12, 0, 41}}, examine it, and complete [[The Red Marble]], giving you permanent access to [[The Library (Nektropos Castle)|The Library]].
1. Go to the Game Room at {{waypoint 32, 0, 59}}. You will be attacked by [[A swine fiend|a swine fiend]] along the way.
1. Once in the Game Room, look for anything you can interact with (including the chessboard, dart board and two pool cue racks on the walls) and left-click to search them. Eventually you will receive [[A hastily scrawled note]].
1. With the note in hand, head to the western wall sconce at {{waypoint 23, 0, 67}}, just outside the Game Room. Examine it. Enter the hidden passageway, kill the enemies that attack, and go upstairs to the second level.

### Second Level

1. You enter the second level in [[Elise's Bedroom]]. Read the book on the dresser in this room to summon [[Elise Everling]], needed later.
1. Walk up the stairs from the bedroom, exit northward onto the ramparts, and you will be greeted again by [[Alexa (NPC)|Alexa]] around {{waypoint 60, 31, 48}}. She will offer the repeatable quest, [[The Everling Lockets]], requiring you to collect six lockets from Lord Everling's children. Once accepted, proceed in a clockwise fashion, going from tower to tower.
1. Enter [[Sheila's Bedroom]], and examine the dresser at {{waypoint 69, 13, 8}} to summon [[Sheila Everling]]. Kill her.
1. Continuing northward on the ramparts, take the path on the right. Kill Elise Everling at {{waypoint 2, 30, -25}}.
1. Backtrack and proceed clockwise to [[Crysta's Bedroom]] at {{waypoint 55, 13, -82}}. Kill [[Crysta's handmaiden]]. Move close to the bed to spawn several [[A doll of Nyth|dolls of Nyth]], kill them, and then inspect the bed. This must be done in this exact order or the required mobs won't spawn. **NOTE:** *If you are planning on doing the HQ*  [[A Missing Mask]], i**nspect the bed from a distance** *and* c*onsider AVOIDING the bed until you have accepted the HQ.*  *There are only about 30 Nyth Dolls in the castle in a single instance.*
1. Still going clockwise, go east on the ramparts towards [[Melanie's Bedroom]] at {{waypoint -43, 14, -73}}. If the previous step was done correctly then both [[Crysta Everling]] and [[Melanie Everling]] will spawn. Kill them both.
1. Head south to [[Jenni's Bedroom]] at {{waypoint -60, 14, 0}} and slay [[Jenni Everling]].
1. Lastly, continue south to [[Deirdre's Bedroom]]. Go down to the basement and kill [[Deirdre Everling]] at {{waypoint -47, 0, 82}}. Beware [[Archfiend Izzoroth]], who will spawn in [[Deirdre's Bedroom]] when the first player enters. He won't aggro immediately, but move quickly to the basement. Although an epic-boss, Archfiend Izzoroth can still be killed by a small group or soloed by a self-mentored player. *STAY AT RANGE* and you will take no damage.
1. After killing Deirdre, [[Alexa (NPC)|Alexa]] will spawn again and tell you that you need another locket, found on [[Ollix Everling]], at the bottom level of the castle.
1. Proceed to [[Maltus' Bedroom]] at {{waypoint -17, 14, -51}} (northeast tower) and kill [[A decaying corpse]] to update [[The Everling Lockets]].
1. Go to the [[Quarters of Lord Everling]] at {{waypoint 18, 14, -52}} and inspect the book on the stand. This will summon [[Guard Captain Ga'vin]] who will begin running up and down the hallway. Note that he will immediately attack anyone standing near the doorway.
1. There is an empty sword stand to the right of the bookcase at {{waypoint 29.95, 13.50, -43.30}}. You must **right-click** the stand and select the 'use' option (left-clicking on the stand will not open the bookcase). If done correctly, the Guard Captain's sword will be placed in the stand and the bookcase will swing open, revealing the entrance to the bottom level of the dungeon.

### Bottom Level

1. Proceed downstairs, interact with the seemingly dead-end wall and go speak with [[Alexa (NPC)|Alexa]] at {{waypoint -39, -13, 42}}. It can take a minute for her to appear.
1. Backtrack to a wall sconce at {{waypoint -5, -14, -23}} and examine it. This unlocks a secret room to your immediate south.
1. Enter the secret room and interact with the scroll on the desk at {{waypoint -12, -14, -12}}, enabling you to interact with objects found on this level that will lead you to the secret passages.
1. Exit the secret room by clicking the candlestick in the corner, on the right, and use the wall-stone at {{waypoint -21, -13, -22}}. Go through and confront [[Alexa (Named Monster)|Alexa]] at {{waypoint -19, -13, 8}}.
1. Kill her, then speak with [[Ollix Everling]] who becomes attackable after a short speech. Kill him and you will have all the required Everling lockets to unlock the door to [[Lord Everling's Secret Laboratory]].
1. To get to Everling's laboratory, pass the hidden door at {{waypoint -20, -13, -2}}, then the stone slab at {{waypoint -2, -13, -4}}. Interact with the candelabra at {{waypoint 10, -13, -11}} to unlock the nearby stone slabs, and then proceed past the stone slabs and the wall at {{waypoint 32, -13, -6}}.
1. Continue through the perilous hallway until you arrive at a door at {{waypoint 176, -13, 11}}. If every step has been done correctly then the door should open, Congratulations: you now have access to every part of Nektropos Castle!
