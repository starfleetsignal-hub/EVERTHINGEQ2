## Background

Although you have brought low [[Maltus Everling]], the spirit of [[Lord Everling]] lives on within the twisted halls of [[Nektropos Castle]]. [[Valten Darkcleanse]] has asked your assistance in penetrating the cold undeath lingering about in Nektropos and put an end to his [[Maid Maltena|sister's remains]] along with Lord Everling.

## Notes

- The layout is very similar to the parent instance, [[Nektropos Castle]]. However, you are forced to take a specific pathway due to blocked passages. Ultimately this makes for a very linear progression and it is hard to get lost.
- Mobs randomly drop three different keys: copper, iron, and brass. These keys open up correlating chests throughout the instance, which each have a chance at providing loot. For example, an iron key will open the medium sized wooden chests.
  - Tinkering recipes (ex. Blueprint: Safety Recaller) have been found in one of the chest as well as boots.
  - Only the person opening the chest can get the loot.
- Although this dungeon is level 50-56, the loot is mostly level 40 treasured items. Some bosses have broken loot tables which drop items such as the **Bright Ember Necklace** and **Maltena's Brooch**, which are no longer in the game. The exception is Lord Everling, who drops level 50 treasured items.

## Walkthrough

1. The courtyard is full of [[An eidolon watch dog|eidolon watch dogs]]. In the stables can be the first named : [[Demitry the Ravager]] (will set the zone lockout), around the forge can be [[Unargin the Blacksmith]] (will set the zone lockout). You need to kill 2 [[A flesh rampager]] 53 (severe undercons with mana drain and some sort of damage reduction) in order to unlock the door.
1. After a few turns in a corridor you arrive in a room with several ghosts in it, amongst them can be [[Butler Balthazen]]. Kill them and touch the door. [[The Burning Soul]] will spawn in the firepit. He sends you some [[Soul ember]] that you need to kill. After 4 waves of minions, he will attack you. Killing him will unlock the other door of the room.
1. You need to go on the corridors upto the end. You will meet [[The Spirit Sucker]] in a side way. [[Maid Maltena]] might be up in a side room as well. In the Prison [[Torturess Amanda]] can be up. [[Watchman Gantder]] can also be in the prison. In the Chapel you can find [[Archbishop Z'Latten]] or [[Bichop Cephus]] (that second named doesn't give any AA or loot). At the end of the corridors kill [[Chalandria the Queen of the Cursed]]. Killing her unlocks a secret passageway through the wall at {{waypoint -20.52, 0, 67.94}}; it will become highlightable and clickable. Go through the passageway.
1. Reach Sheila's room. A ghost sent by her father will arrive and kill her. She will respawn as [[The Cursed Remains of Sheila Everling]] along with puppets. Killing them will unlock the door of her room.
1. Head towards Jenni's room, which is at {{waypoint -68.2, 13.5, -2.8}}.  On the way, In the side room at {{waypoint -21.5, 13.9, 17.4}}, there is [[Groundskeeper Edgan]] for you to kill.
1. Reach Jenni's room.  The ghost that killed Sheila arrives and kill Jenni. Once again, [[The Cursed Remains of Jenni Everling]] will spawn with some dolls. Kill them.
1. Head towards Crysta's room, which is at {{waypoint 74.91, 13.5, 2}}. To get there, go to the room at {{waypoint -27.8, 13.5, -28.94}},where [[Captain Greeves]] sometimes spawns. Clear the room, then click on the teleporter at {{waypoint -20.69, 13.8, -31.4}}, which will take you to another corridor, on the Western side of the zone.
1. After teleporting, head towards Crysta's room (in a side room, you'll find [[Lady T'Kalin]]). In Crysta's room, the same event as happened with the previous 2 ghosts occurs. Kill [[The Cursed Remains of Crysta Everling]] and her dolls.
1. Go to Deirdra's room {{waypoint 52.69, 13.54, 84.52}}and kill [[The Cursed Remains of Deirdra Everling]] and its dolls.
1. Take the teleporter in Deirdra's room, which will take you to a corridor to the north.
1. Head to Alana's room {{waypoint -45.63, 13.5, -84.21}} and kill [[The Cursed Remains of Alana Everling]].  This will unlock the door to Melanie's room.  (Note: [[The Thexian Overlord]] is sometimes up at {{waypoint -17.39, 13.77, -40.29}}).
1. Head to Melanie's room {{waypoint 56.68, 13.42, -82.09}}. Kill [[The Cursed Remains of Melanie Everling]] after Melanie is killed by the guard. This will unlock the last door at {{waypoint 17.08, 13.48, -55.58}}.
1. In the now unlocked room you'll finally find [[Lord Everling]], the last event of the zone. He has knockback and many 56v spirit wisps with him when he becomes aggro, so be prepared.
