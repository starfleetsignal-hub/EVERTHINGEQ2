**General notes for a smooth experience**:

- Don't stand in blue beams.
- Don't let mobs stand in blue beams.
- Kill everything.
- When the lighting of the room changes, its telling you that the named's mitigation buffs are gone.
- There are probably better ways to do these fights, these are just the most efficient way I've found so far.
- If you can't cure, bring potions.

## Walkthrough

1. **Pall of Oograll** {{waypoint -110, -139, -148}}
   - Target him directly and wait for the statues to turn in to attackable mobs (about  30 seconds).  When they are active combatants, their dark flame thing is extinguished, and one counter of Pall's mitigation buff is removed from him for each of them.
   - When he does his swear script, just move around behind him to avoid his frontal cone.  After two rounds of that, both statues are in the fight.  Target him (singular focus is helpful here), but do not kill them.  If you do accidentally, just wait until the next swear script and you're back in action.
   - Once he is dead, take the shadow portal at {{waypoint 3.56, 150.39, -128.34}} to the jail.
1. **Umbral Warden** {{waypoint 79.33, 143.03, -314.75}}
   - Travel down each set of stairs and kill everything.  Loot the [[Umbral jailer's key (Version 1)|umbral jailer's key]] from the umbral jailer.  Open every single cell door and free all the grimlings; they are not grateful for the jail break, so be sure to wipe them out (**all** of them).  These are the key to removing the Umbral Warden's mitigation buff.  Don't just kill them until he spawns; get them all.
   - If the room he wanders in is lit up, then his mitigation buff is gone, and he is a simple tank and spank.
   - Occasionally, he will cast an elemental detriment on you that makes him invisible.  Cure it, and he becomes visible again.
   - Once he is dead, take the shadow portal at {{waypoint 106.72, 143.17, -317.53}}.  Clear the area and take the next portal.
1. **Eom Va Zethon** {{waypoint 0.65, 139.84, -443.04}}
   - To deactivate the black lightning from the floor where the named walks, you have to clear out the mini ring event in each of the side hallways.  Once the room is clear, "an Ebon Xakra" will spawn.  Kill both of them and you can enter the named hallway without dying.
   - If the room he wanders in is lit up, then his mitigation buff is gone, and he is a simple tank and spank.  There is a script involving shadows that move from portal to portal, but in the solo, they can be largely ignored.
   - Don't click any of the black triangles until after he's dead.
   - Click the one triangle that says "Shadow Portal" to be sent to the Crypt of the Imperiatrix.
1. **Gloomscale Ssyssyx** {{waypoint 0.71, 150.79, -209.16}}
   - Exactly like the previous area; clear out the ring event scripts down the east and west staircases, then kill the two [[An ebon bloodeye]] at the top of the stairs to spawn her.
   - The three blue beams are the key to her mitigation buff.  They also hurt you badly and give her a significant heal if she's standing in them.
   - One method is to aggro but not kill the "an ebon bloodeye" who keep the beams going, so the beam is disengaged before the fight (or early on).  Once you kill them, you have about 10 to 15 seconds before they repop in their original location.
   - If you find them hard to target, click on their faces in the middle of the triangle.  Careful not to click the Turbulent Shadows triangle, because it will kill you.
     - Aggro one, then stand between the far 2nd and 3rd beams, then set off a small aoe to get them all on me, then just go nuts.  Without her buff, she's also a wimp, just like her predecessors
   - When she is dead, click on the triangle labeled Shadow Portal to move to the next area.
1. **Vasszhin Zeom** {{waypoint -106.11, 150.48, -346.15}}
   1. Kill everything on the way in to his room, and when you get there, the lights are on and he has no mitigation buff up.
   1. He occasionally pulls a weak add to help him, but you can ignore it and just focus on him.
   1. If the fight goes on long enough, he will occasionally drop a darkmelded pearl in your inventory.  At that point, the buff comes on, and one of the pillars becomes clickable. Just walk over to it and click each pillar until you find the correct one(s) to cancel the buff. The light in the room will change from a dark bluish to a warm yellowish color when you have placed the crystal(s) on the correct pillar. The more adds, the more pearls.
   1. When he is dead, click on the Shadow Portal at {{waypoint -91.43, 150.42, -298.14}} to move to the next area.
1. **Shade of Ssraeshza** {{waypoint 201.24, 139.41, -301.40}}
   1. Follow the hallway around, killing everything in your path.  Do not click the triangle that looks like a portal but is clearly marked Turbulent Shadows.
   1. Clear out the [[An akhessar shadowguard]] from around the room and he will become attackable.  Keep him out of the blue beams, and kill his adds as they spawn.
   1. If your screen goes pink/purple, you have a short time to cure arcane or you will be killed.  Mercs have a hard time with this one, so bring potions.
   1. The pattern of beams will change during the fight; you may have to move him a few steps to the side, as the blue beams and black puddles heal him and hurt you
