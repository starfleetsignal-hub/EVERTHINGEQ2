## Background

There has been an influx of hatred into the world. Neighbors are turning hostile to one another, friends no longer care for each other's company, and couples no longer sleep in the same bed. Something is definitely amiss. Adventurers returning from [[Nektulos Forest]] have reported a new, dark energy emanating from near the entrance to [[Darklight Wood]]. Apparently, as someone approaches it, their thoughts begin to cloud with anger. Could this be source of the rise in hatred? Could the minions of [[Innoruuk]] at work here?

## Strategy

Very easy to farm the first four named for plat in 10 or 15 minutes mentored down to level 90 in COE end game gear.

There is no hard and fast rule for this zone, giving the raid a bit of choice in kill order.  The first two named mobs ([[Demetrius Crane]] and [[Dreadlord D'Somni]]) are considered to be comparatively straightforward kills and therefore good practice for guilds beginning to raid.  However, the difficulty level rises sharply after that point.

Different raids have their own preference on which one of these two named mobs to kill first.  However, the stategy for each involves clearing the trash mobs near the zone in and all trash leading up to and surrounding them.  This stage is fairly straightforward as long as you remain aware of three things:

- The trash mobs wander a fairly wide area so it is important to choose a fighting position where the raid can avoid adds; the [[A spite golem|spite golems]] move quite quickly, so be aware of their wandering radius.
- In some areas, there are pairs of [[An animus haunted chest|animus haunted chests]] very close to each other, making a potentially tricky pull.  Do not precast anything (heals, reactives etc) before the tank pulls or you will get both chests heading straight towards the culprit!
- Look above to make sure the flying mobs ([[A scorn fiend|scorn fiends]]) do not add.

Once the first two nameds are down, simply rinse and repeat: kill trash on the way to the named mobs and then take down the nameds themselves (see named info for the relevant strategy for each one).
