This is an instance of Firemyst Gully which is available when you have the [[A Foul Wind]] quest. There are a group of undead dwarves near the entrance, and at the center of the zone is [[The Blightwind Witch]]. There are skeletons throughout the zone, as well as [[A tainted glade deer|tainted glade deer]].

## Notes

- Simply entering the zone will complete [[A Foul Wind]]. You do not need to defeat any encounters.
- This is a slightly lower-level zone than its parent zone [[The Firemyst Gully|Firemyst Gully]].
- The named ghost dwarves will award AA experience, but they do not drop any loot.
- The harvesting nodes in the zone are Tier 3 (what you'd find in [[The Thundering Steppes|Thundering Steppes]] and [[Nektulos Forest]]).
