Crafting Station for Tradeskill Items is near Junkbeast

## Walkthrough

### Repair Bot 5000

- Most of them are implementing the "scatter-concentrate" combat strategy
- When the boss gets the Planar Bubble it will become invincible and prepare to start collecting materials.
- When you see the progress bar of the Boss casting the Gather Spare Parts move, if you are within the 9-meter radius, the closer you are to the 9-meter radius, the more serious the damage will be. So stay 10 meters away from the Boss before this move is actually released to avoid being hit...
- Each time you cast Gather Spare Parts, it also absorbs mana/sucks away the Beastmaster's savagry/sucks away the Channeler's dissonance and replenishes 25% of its HP.

### Powered Mechanization

- Avoid lazing every time it teleport itself. Easy tank and spank.
- It only has **two** fixed teleport point and **one** orientation

### Toa the Shiny

- The boss comes with two minions: Keewi the Shiner and Brasoh the Polisher.
- The strategy is to deal equal damage to these three monsters: Try to deal equal damage to them, and try to make their health the same HP%.
- Because if you beat the two adds to death first, the boss will say "*Due to his high health, Toa protects his minions from death!*" and the adds will not really die.
- During the fight, the boss will say "*Toa the shiny gains an electric charge through their chassis!*" in the dialogue bar and will then get the Planar Bubble, which makes the boss invincible for a short period of time.
- Then be careful: if you hit the boss while he still has the Short Circuit buff (whether it is auto attack or skill), it will cause him to take damage and absorb magic.
- Therefore, you have to wait until Toa's Short Circuit buff disappears before you can attack it.
- If you're soloing this in 2026, you can put your merc on passive then just autoattack Toa. Then once he's at about 5% health, start casting your heavy hitters and AOEs to kill everyone at once.  This takes a while but you don't seem to die from "Brass Flash" this way.

### The Junk Beast

- Tank and spank. No mechanics

### The Manaetic Behemoth

- Due to the **Ones and Zeros** buff, if it takes too much damage, it will heal itself and increase its resolve value.
- As the battle progresses, it will use **Opening Weaponry Doors** to pin itself down and reduce damage by 95%, and then several red circles will appear on the ground nearby.
- Get away from these circles immediately, because the boss will shoot missiles at them, and you have about five seconds to run away.
- Don't die, because once someone dies, Reconstruction of Innovation will be activated and the Boss's **HP** will be **restored by 10%**.
- In addition, a few members(1 or 2) of your group will be randomly selected and will be hit with multiple layers of trama debuff. If it is not removed within the countdown time, the group will be wiped out. About 10 sec and need to cure 3-5 times to remove entirely
