## Prerequisites

*To enter, you must be running [[Visions of Vetrovia: Vacrul Intentions]] or have earned the [[VoV Flag: Castle Vacrul Dungeons Unlock]].*

## Notes

*Killing [[A bloodrose bouquet|bloodrose bouquets]] removes the detriment **Attar of Bloodrose**.*

## Walkthrough

1. [[Kartur]] {{waypoint -28, 0, 47}}
   - Tank and Spank.
   - Has a knockback/daze trauma AOE effect and calls in thrallic footman adds.
   - Pick up items from around the zone for his display cases. Periodically during the fight he will check his master's inventory and call a specific item he's looking for. Place that item in the case to remove the offensive buff he gets. If it stacks to 5 he will kill the party.
     - Note that at really low graphics settings, the display case may be impossible to see through, however if you hover over the case and/or know the location of the items, you can still place them. ![](images/Karturbuff.png)
     - [[A red dead head]] {{waypoint -2.42, -0.75, 7.90}} to place at {{waypoint -32.75, -0.80, 50.26}}![](images/Kartur_emote.png)
     - [[A maneki-neko]] {{waypoint 6.47, 0.93, 15.97}}  to place at {{waypoint -2336, -0.77, 60.87}}
     - [[A ratonga nutcracker]] {{waypoint 70.98, 0.50, 3.60}} to place at {{waypoint -32.76, -0.80, 54.04}}
     - [[A rubber ducky]] {{waypoint -0.80, 0.31, 46.09}} to place at {{waypoint -18.38, -0.78, 49.56}}
     - [[A rocking horse]] {{waypoint 22.44, -0.79, 36.40}} to place at {{waypoint -32.75, -0.80, 52.21}}
     - [[A speedster]] {{waypoint 48.07, -0.75, 54.28}} to place at {{waypoint -18.38, -0.78, 52.63}}
1. [[T'valla Rilayne]] {{waypoint 72.81, -0.70, 7.46}}
   - Drag outside and kill bats.
1. [[Vhenex the Werefather]] with adds [[Sister Herex]] and [[Brother Shen]] {{waypoint 104, -15, 114}}
   - The gold platform lowers the red beam temporarily.  Step on it and run through when it goes down.
   - Tank and Spank. kill adds [[Sister Herex]] and [[Brother Shen]] first to reduce his combat mitigation.
   - They are immune to AOE damages. Use single damage Spells/CA.
1. Hail [[Lyrez O'Scholoran]] to make him attackable and kill him. {{waypoint 155, -15, 65}}
1. [[The Black King]]![](images/ChessMoves.png)
   - To spawn the King, move chess pieces. You must stay on the chessboard to maintain the encounter. If you do make a wrong move the board just resets to the starting position.
   1. Move the Black Rook (Castle) on A-4 straight ahead to H-4 (the furthest square opposite its position). White Rook takes Black Rook.
   1. Move the Black Bishop at B-2 to E-5 (move this on a white diagonal) to take the White Knight. White Queen takes Black Bishop.
   1. Move the Black Queen at F-1 to G-2 (ONE square on a diagonal) to checkmate the White King.
   1. Kill the Black Queen before the King. She only takes DPS when on a white tile.
   1. Kill the Black Bishop next, then Black Rook and then finally the pawns.
   1. Note: Killing a Pawn before killing the other three first will insta kill you. Watch your AOE's
   1. Kill the Black King last; he will not take damage until all other pieces are down.
1. [[Roseblood]] and [[Thornblood]] (Epic x2 linked encounter)
   - *Cast waterbreathing via spell, totem, [[Fishbone Earring]], etc. for this fight.
   - Tank and spank, but keep on moving and don't stand still.
   - Everybody can cast on the move for this fight.
