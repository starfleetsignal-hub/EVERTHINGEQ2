## Progression

This zone contains several groups of named mobs.  Each named in a group can be spawned in different ways, and once a named from a group has been spawned, the other named from that group will be unavailable until the instance is reset or expires.  See Bosses below for information on how to spawn named in each group.

Named from the first 4 named groups (A, B, C, and D) can be spawned in any order.  Once a named from all 4 groups has been killed, Group E will become attackable.  When Group E is killed, Group F (High Shaman) will become attackable.  The final group (Group G) will only spawn after the zone boss (High Shaman Rakada Darkpaw) has been killed.

Items made in the [[Darkpaw: Sabotage (Tradeskill)|Darkpaw: Sabotage [Tradeskill]]] instance are required to spawn some named and to collect some items (although the instance can be cleared without using any of these items.)  Some of these items depend on drops or collected items from this instance, including:

- [[Darkpaw shamanistic rune]] - dropped by any gnoll
- [[Darkpaw skull powder]] - collected from the ground after destroying "aged gnoll cranium" (skulls on the ground)
- [[Darkpaw gong mallet head]] and [[Darkpaw gong mallet handle]] - dropped by specific gnolls (Oracle of Opalla, Follower of Fippy, Herald of Harclave)
- [[Hamlet's raw shank]] - dropped by Hamlet, one of the Group A bosses
- [[Underpaw gnolfactory]] - dropped by any Underpaw gnoll
- [[Raw terramite]] - destroy the smaller terramite shards in Terramite Crystalline Grotto (labeled "potruding terramite shard") by placing a [[Darkpaw powder keg]] near it and igniting it, rarely found by digging up a dirt mound in Rockpaw Burrow using a [[Darkpaw shovel]]
- [[Broken Darkpaw wolf whistle]] - Treasure Chest drop from any gnoll group with a wolf
- [[Zygomyd bolete]] and [[Zygomyd morel]] - dropped by Zygomyds in Terra Fungi
- [[Zygomyd truffles]] - rarely found by digging up a mushroom mound in Terra Fungi using [[Darkpaw shovel]]

## Bosses

The bosses in this dungeon are divided into groups. Only one boss from each group may be spawned in a single instance. Plus only one Anima (gong) Boss may be spawned in a single instance.

### Group A

- [[Anima of Vixra]] - spawn by using Oracle's Gong, a [[Darkpaw gong mallet]] is required MUST Keep Heroic Opportunity on her
- [[Dustpaw Devil]] - spawn by dragging 3 [[A dust devil|dust devils]] to Totem, a [[Darkpaw ladder]] is required in inventory to see the dust devils
- [[Gorlon the Goner]] - spawn by killing all skeletal gnoll undead (a deceased Darkpaw) in Culling Grounds
- [[Hamlet]]
- [[Haska the Hacker]] - spawned by killing Sabertooth hatcheteers in Sabertooth Burrow
- [[Kinmaster Kipp]] - spawned by placing a [[Darkpaw Seasoned Shank]] in the plate in Dustpaw Burrow

### Group B

- [[Anima of Snarlip]] - spawn by using Follower's Gong, a [[Darkpaw gong mallet]] is required
- [[Brucko the Brutal]] - spawned by collecting bones from the skeletal gnolls and completing puzzle starting atop the small platform
- [[Frostok the Frigid and Iceclaw]] - spawned by collecting 4 drums in Snowfang burrow and placing them in the correct order
- [[Madpaw Mixl]] - spawned by looting Mudpaw gatekeeper's key from a Mudpaw gatekeeper, and opening either door to Madpaw's pen
- [[Sporky]] - Kill Zygomyd Sporists, [[Zygomyd Sporegasbord]] required to see
- [[Repungus]] - Harvest a stinky cap, click body to spawn, [[Zygomyd Sporegasbord]] required to see

### Group C

- [[Anima of Gashok]] - spawn by using Herald's Gong, a [[Darkpaw gong mallet]] is required
- [[Emberclaw]] - spawn by killing the crabs, loot their bodies and then click the crabpots
- [[Vmork the Voracious]] - spawned by killing the group outside of its pen (1 gnoll and 3 wolves)
- [[YipYap]] - destroy Splitpaw wooden arks to spawn
- [[Twotooth]] - Kill 2 named from other groups to spawn. He summons Holes stand on them to stop adds, too many adds insta death.
- [[Glag the Gut]] - Kill 2 named from other groups to spawn, Drag Twotooth onto him to activate

### Group D

- [[Brulk Skullcrusher]] - spawned by destroying some (10) aged gnoll cranium ground spawns
- [[Cracklebones]] - to spawn destroy the wall with a [[Darkpaw powder keg]]
- [[Grotglutton]] - to spawn stand in the water and do not cure the Trauma Detriment
- [[Pillowpaw]] - spawned by destroying 10 pillow ground spawns
- [[Sawmaw]] - to spawn kill "gnawed remains" mobs
- [[Klubber and Tundo]] - to spawn kill all "Garatch Mot Pitfighter" mobs

### Group E

There is only one boss in this group. You must always fight the Prophets Three.

- [[The Prophets Three]]: Garshok, Snarlip, and Vixra - Spawn by killing 1 named from groups A, B, C, D

### Group F

There is only one boss in this group. You must always fight the High Shaman.

- [[High Shaman Rakada Darkpaw]]

### Group G - Bonus

- [[Perilaxous]] - to spawn destroy the wall with a [[Darkpaw powder keg]], Light the candles that have a corresponding lit chandelier hanging below
- [[The Terranaught]] - spawned by clearing all Terramite Mobs, will need a [[Darkpaw powder keg]] plus a [[Darkpaw terraporter crystal]]
- [[Yorgen Stonechipper]] - to spawn kill "a rockpaw runt", harvest miners helm, click the table
- [[Ernst Boarg]] - to spawn kill 8 fireboars, , will need a [[Darkpaw powder keg]], plus a [[Darkpaw terraporter crystal]]
- [[Korrosk the Exiled]] - to spawn kill Ghostly Underpaw Gnolls, will need 2 [[Darkpaw powder keg]], plus 2 [[Darkpaw terraporter crystal]]
