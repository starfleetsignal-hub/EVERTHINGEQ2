## Background

[[Flame Lord Thyr]], ruler of the Guardians of Thyr leads his army of fire giants from this bastion of flame and metal. Adventurers seeking the council of [[Lord Nagafen (Nagafen's Lair)|Lord Nagafen]] must past through here. However, no one has made it past the fire giant king in over four centuries.

## Walkthrough

1. Clear all mobs in the instance, especially the [[A magma elemental|magma elementals]]. The [[A lava walker|lava walkers]] and the [[A captured Cinderfoot|captured Cinderfoot]] may be left alive.
1. Speak to [[Flame Lord Thyr]] to start his event. You must be standing close to him to get his conversation tree. And you must 'Hail' him, not right click. Choose the obvious answers to continue.
1. The two [[A steward of Thyr|efreeti]] NPCs at his side will aggro. Kill them and speak to Flame Lord Thyr again.
   - If doing this in a level-appropriate group, have a ranged class speak to him this time. The reason for this is that the speaker will immediately be banished to a cage near the cave-ceiling and aggro any remaining magma elementals. After dealing with them, he/she can assist the rest of the group in attacking the boss.
   - If soloing as a high-level, use any ranged attack or weapon to attack the boss from the cage.
1. Kill Flame Lord Thyr.
   - The person in the cage will immediately be teleported back down upon Flame Lord Thyr's death.
   - You may now exit through the back doors.

## Notes

- If you have unlocked [[The Oratorium of Thyr: Deserted]] and cleared this instance once, you can bypass the content by zoning into The Oratorium of Thyr: Deserted.
  - When returning to this instance do not have any mercenaries summoned before zoning in or you will be placed in the uncleared version.
