## Strategy

1. Clear the [[A Barkgut hunter|Barkgut hunters]] and [[A Barkgut gatherer|Barkgut gatherers]].
   - *Note: Killing all the hunters makes Hagrash attackable; killing all the gatherers makes Gagrash attackable.*
1. Slay [[Gagrash (Event Heroic)|Gagrash]] and [[Hagrash (Event Heroic)|Hagrash]]
1. Right-click to dissolve the two barriers {{waypoint 53.42, 80.42, 186.00}}. They must be done quickly, one right after the other.
1. Click the **Howling Seal** {{waypoint 61.94, 89.80, 222.27}}.
   - Note: if grouping with a friend, you must both click the seal.
1. Slay [[Bagarash (Event Heroic)|Bagarash]], the Ancestral Alpha Kobold.
