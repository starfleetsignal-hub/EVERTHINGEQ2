## Lore

![Entry view](images/Plane_of_Disease_Outbreak_Entry_View.jpg)
The Plane of Disease is perhaps the most vile land in all of Norrath - are you sure you're still willing to explore this foul plane?

Festering like a pustule in the celestial spheres, the **Plane of Disease** is the unfortunate embodiment of its ruler and the bringer of plagues, Bertoxxulous.

The air hangs with a sticky, stagnant mix of pestilence and decay, its vapors so deadly as to cause organs to rot within seconds of contact. Bile and bodily wastes fill the lakes, rivers, and sea, frothing at the surface and scarring the shoreline. Large boils cover the putrid land, where rank pus excretes from the surface with every step.

You'll come into unsavory contact with the Plaguebringer's most devout worshippers, the Springview Healers, and unravel the secrets of this land of degeneration in your pursuit of the Tenets of Hate.

Venture deep into the infected bowels of this ever-decaying plane where an ancient tomb of kings, the Crypt of Decay, awaits, and discover what Lanys T'vyl and her Primordial Malice have in store for Bertoxxulous.

It is with utmost urgency that you, the mortal heroes of Norrath, survive this festering realm and its infestation of creatures, and prevent the Tenets of Hate from fulfilling their prophecy of domination.

The Plane of Disease may prove to be your most vile challenge yet, adventurer... do you think you've got the stomach to face it?

## Preparations before fight

If you are first time try to run PoP heroic dungeons as a F2P. Check this blog [[User blog:Tangstone/How to run Planes of Prophecy Heroic dungeons as a F2P in 2025|How to run Planes of Prophecy Heroic dungeons as a F2P in 2025]]

Group requirement: a player **DPS**, a player **priest**([[Cure Curse|cure curse]]), player support who **supply power**(mana) and speed boost,a [[Mage|Mage Archetype]] ([[Absorb Magic]], use [[Machined Energy Inverter]] for replacement but F2P can't harvest its components in 2025)

## Walkthrough

### [[Felkruk (Heroic)|Felkruk]](**Optional)**

- **Optional:**You can skip this one if you have [[Springview Healer Mask]] in your bags. You may wanna get one from [[Legacy of Power: Realm of the Plaguebringer|solo version]]
- **Hailing Felkruk**: Just like [[Legacy of Power: Realm of the Plaguebringer|solo version]] hail him first. Or you can just walk pass by him. It always trigger dialog scripts as you normally run this dungeons
- **Activate Crypts:** Open 10 **[[Last House Crypt|Last House Crypts]]** to aggro him.Clear all mobs spawn from **[[Last House Crypt|Last House Crypts]].** They will attack you when Felkruk reaching 20% health
- **Damage Over Time (DoT):** He deals "Awash in Decay" Dot to everyone. Be sure cure it over time all the time
- The Core mechanic is curse "Awakend Affliction" and "Dormant Affliction". Every player **priest** [[Cure Curse|cure curse]] recast time is forced set to 2 seconds.
- **Curse Mechanics:** 5 seconds later when you successfully cure curse, Felkruk applies a curse to team. Curses must be cured in the exact order they were applied.Each **priest** player's curse cure ability has a fixed recast time of 2 seconds.Use a tagging system or remember the curse application order to ensure accurate curing.
- You may wanna keep [[Springview Healer Mask]] in your bags, if you wanna run here often.

### [[The Carrion Larva and The Malarian Larva (Heroic)|**The Carrion Larva** and **The Malarian Larva**]]

- Trash around can be tough than named. Same with [[Legacy of Power: Realm of the Plaguebringer|solo version]]
- This named gets adds for each. They enhance named damage when alive. You can ignore two adds with a player healer.
- Nothing special this one.  Remember to use [[Hirudin Extract]] so next named won't heal himself

### [[The Flesh Eater (Heroic)|The Flesh Eater]]

- Every 30 seconds he summons "a putrid pile of flesh" aggro adds prevent named get enhanced
- Named consume every "a fleshclot" and "Skin Graft" increments. Dispel it with [[Absorb Magic]]/[[Machined Energy Inverter]] every time it shows up
- Kill it spawns next named

### [[High Dragoon V'Aliar (Heroic)|High Dragoon V'Aliar]]

- He has 4 adds compares to 2 in [[Legacy of Power: Realm of the Plaguebringer|solo version]]
- Get away about 30m when he casts a 10 sec spell "Magnetic Charge". It deals 40m knockdown,huge damage,stun can't be cured.

### [[Rallius Rattican (Heoric)|Rallius Rattican]]

- Same as [[Legacy of Power: Realm of the Plaguebringer|solo version]]
- Try not aggro any bats. Every bats deals severe damage and wipe out entire group before you notice anything.
- "Dominion of Disease" prevents all damage to Rallius when he is in central island
- He dispels some of your maintain spells when too closer to him like this one 70lv dungeon "[[The Crypt of Agony]]"
