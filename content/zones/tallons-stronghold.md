BOSSES

[[General Zevitus (Epic)]]

Kill order: right, left, middle.

[[Lieutenant Klaatuus (Epic)]]

Tank him where he stands. Tortoise Shell / Bladedance on pull. The bird has a curse that needs to be cured. If there are not enough healers or cures are down, have the cursed person run to the other side of the room to die or the curse will spread throughout the raid, causing a wipe.

[[Mystikus Terrorwing (Epic)]]

Tank him at the side of the room or in the back.  He will port to the center of the room and make a bubble.  Stay outside the bubble and kill the add that spawns.  When the bubble is down, pull him to the side or back of the room again.

[[Tyrax Terrolus (Epic)]]

Kill the banners by archetype. Dying heals him up to 50%.

[[Tallon Zek (Epic)]]

Kill the adds when the spawn.  Cure curse.
