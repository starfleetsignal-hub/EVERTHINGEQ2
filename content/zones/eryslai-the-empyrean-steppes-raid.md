You can fly in this zone. You have to in order to reach the different islands.

## Bosses

1. Winston, Baltaldor the Cursed et Pookie {{waypoint 220.86, 59.64, -1213.21}}. At the upper level of the first island. When dead, the adds drop chests. They may be useful during the fight. (We burnt.)
1. Rinturion Windblade {{waypoint 1174, 218, -86}}. On the Aviak's Island.
1. Pherlondien Clawpike {{waypoint 1442.37, 275.19, -16.32}}. On the Aviak's Island.
1. The Avatars of Air (Dust, Smoke, Wind, Mist) {{waypoint -360.40, -490.71, 839.30}} At the Precipice of Wind. Kill the four of them to spawn the Elemental Masterpiece.
1. Guardian of Faal'Armanna, The Bee Queen {{waypoint -550.02, 647.87, -171.69}}. In the Hive.
