## Zone access

Zone access is by hidden achievement *[[Unlock: Brackish Vaults: Realm of the Triumvirate Access]]* which requires:

[[Triumph: Plundering the Planes (Tier 2)]]

- Operator Figl, Rizlona, Thundercall and Cyclone, Wavecrasher and Firestorm, Skal'sli the Wretched, Grummus, Meldrath the Malignant, Junkyard Mawg, Arlyxir, Stormtide and Sandstorm, Kuanbyr Hailstorm, Nightlure the Fleshfeaster, & Pox.

## Strategy

1st named: kill proper assassin adds but with enough dps you can straight burn.

2nd name: form triangle on generators slow no dps or he blows up. You need two tanks. Generators appear when one tank steals the name from another tank.

3rd name: grab water using /mine when you have the flashing brain det use the item at 50% and again at 10% to cure curse and remove stoneskin
