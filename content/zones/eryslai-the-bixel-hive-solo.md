## Notes

- This is the Plane of Air. Its creatures fly. So remember to keep checking for attacks from above you, as well as in your normal field of vision.

## Strategy

1. Kill [[A mist spren|mist sprens]] around [[Misty Falls]] {{waypoint 160, -228, -1171}} to spawn [[The Matron of Mists]] at {{waypoint 160, -228, -1171}}, then kill her.
   - The Matron will not leave the falls/water.
   - Joust out of the fog.
   - She has a detriment that can't be cured while you are in the water. It is possible to pull her to the very edge of the water, and fight with you on the bank and her in the water.
1. Kill [[Anzarion]], a large air elemental who paths around the edge of the entire island.
   - *He seems to roam near {{waypoint 93, -198, -968}}*
   - Anzarion has a nasty knock-up. Pull him into the water nearly to the waterfall to avoid the kick.
   - This is sort of a DPS check fight. If you don't kill him quickly, he starts summoning [[A servitor of air|servitors of air]] every few seconds.
1. Use the **Pollen Launcher** (a large blossom) at {{waypoint 112, -186, -799}} to jump to the [[Honeycomb Landing]] of [[The Bixel Hive]]. Follow the ramp inside and up to the left, spiraling clockwise upwards.
1. Kill [[Daishani]] in [[The Honey Larder]] at {{waypoint -675, 403, -148}}.
   - Kill his two adds first to lower his damage reduction.
   - Periodically through the fight, Daishani will reflect your spells. Any attacks that damage the mob will instead damage you. Any attacks that have a heal component will instead heal the mob.
1. Go back down the ramp to the entrance, then take the ramp that goes up to the right to the [[Wax Armory]]. Just outside is the **Emir's private phoenix** at {{waypoint -323, 453, -146}}. Ride the phoenix to [[The Northern Overlook]].
1. Destroy the **Honeymonger Stash** at {{waypoint -655, 525, -325}} to spawn [[Bzzkill the Honeymonger]] at {{waypoint -547, 440, -84}}, then slay her.
   - Periodically during the fight, Bzzkill will call for reinforcements by casting **Stormrider's Call**. Watch her casting bar for that spell to start casting, and then interrupt her. If you cast your interrupt at the emote and don't wait for the cast bar, your interrupt can go off too soon. If you fail to interrupt, she summons a set of 6 adds.
   - Bzzkill has a buff called **Honey Infusion** that, among other things, reduces all damage she takes by 25%. If you're a class with a dispel, use it.
1. Follow the ramp to the right and down through the [[Lower Larvae Cells]], then around and up through the [[Upper Larvae Cells]] to reach [[The Queen's Cell]]. The Bixel Queen, [[Aurorax]] is in the center at {{waypoint -555, 648, -172}} surrounded by her guard. Do not stand in the honey pools of each guard while you kill them, as soon as they die, the honey disappears and you fall through the hole to your death. Stand on the edge of their honey pool to avoid falling or move out of it just before they die.

Use the **Pollen Launcher** at {{waypoint -508, 356, -436}} to return to the starting island or evac or fast travel.
