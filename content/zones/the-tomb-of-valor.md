The quest [[A Key to the Past]] is no longer required to enter this zone.

To spawn [[Lord Chesgard]] (as per Origins Server)

- Open the first sarcophagus beside where you enter
- kill each spawned mob in turn and shut their sarcophagus
- sarcophagi will open again over time, so you need to be efficient / fast at putting them to rest and closing lids
- Once all lids are closed, [[Lord Chesgard]] will spawn.
