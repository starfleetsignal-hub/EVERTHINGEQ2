However, a player must be on a [[Gobblerock's Hideout#Quests|related quest]] to gain access to the instance, and if they desire permanent access then the former access quest must still be completed.*

## Background

[[Boss Gobblerock]] and his Gobblerock Clan have taken up residence in a newly unearthed section of the [[Freeport Sewers Timeline|Freeport Sewers]]. They have been credited with terrorizing the merchants and militia of [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]] and are becoming a thorn in the side of the [[Overlord Lucan D'Lere|Overlord]]. These dastardly goblins constantly sneak into the city and pilfer supplies, food, and weapons. In response to this ever-growing threat, The Freeport Militia has sent [[Captain Ista]] into [[The Commonlands]] to investigate the situation and recruit willing adventurers to assist in cleaning out the sewer system.
