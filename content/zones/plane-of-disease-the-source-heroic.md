## Strategy

### Blighthorn

- Tank and spank
- Will use Wild Stampede. This move will kick/stun you
- During the battle, a blighted festrus will be called out. This monster will use a small range (4 meters radius) AoE automatic attack, and will also stun you for a short period of time.
- After that, head southwest and kill about 12 puslin leapers until you see the following message appear on the screen: "A massive pusling spews forth from the rancid depths of Pus Flats!". This means that the Rancine has spawned on one of the small islands in the sea.

### Rancine

- During the battle, the boss will call the budding lesions growing on the ground nearby to rise up into a ball of light (you will see a red beam of light particle effect) and fly towards him.
- Before the light ball flies to the boss, he will get the Pus Bubble buff, which reduces the boss's damage by <s>75%</s> 100% and pins him down. At the same time, if you hit him during this time, the damage will be reflected back. So stop DPS and don't hit him.
- Send one or two players to cut off the connection between the Rafflesia and the Boss, that is, click on the flowers that emit red beams.
- If you fail to cut the connection before the light ball flies to the boss, an extra lesion of doom will be born.
- You can also do nothing but heal and wait for several mins till end immune damage

### Gryme

- During the fight , you will be hit by an irreversible debuff called Contaminated Air, which will cause moderate damage every four seconds.
- Pay attention to the dialog box to see the message “*You notice torn flesh in Gyrme’s Shin!*”
- Now assign one person in the team to **right-click** the Boss's leg and select “*Kick ’em in the shin”*
- This will cause the Boss to fall into the construct of decay, thus causing the film to disappear. It will also temporarily disable Contaminated Air.
- After a while you will notice another text saying “*Gyrme’s shin is ready for another kicking!”*. Keep right clicking to kill this boss.

### Darvol

- There is a Soul Devour curse, which stacks up. The effect is that each additional layer will halve your maximum HP again.
- When the boss is weakened enough, it will start to cast Devouring Souls, at which point it will become completely invincible and start to stack Devoured Souls counters
- At the same time, a random person in the party will be afflicted with the Affliction of the Twelfth debuff. This will last for 30 seconds. If you let it expire, that person will die.
- But you can't get rid of this right away! Only those who have obtained Affliction of the Twelfth can dive into the central pool and move the bucket without getting hurt.
- The person has 30 seconds to perform the task of moving the bucket. Move the bucket to Darvol's feet just like you would move furniture in your house.
- *** In 2026, you can "pre-stage" the barrels.  Before aggroing him, just watch where he's pathing and place 3 barrels or so to where you plan to start the fight.  It's much easier to get them out of the water beforehand so you can see better and aren't as slow from being in combat.
- Once the barrel is in place it will become a barrel of volatile pus that can be attacked. Kill the barrel immediately and it will explode, preventing Darvol from casting Devouring Souls.
- Repeatedly carry the barrel to break Darvol's invincibility.
- Finish him teleport you to Wavadozzik Adan

### Wavadozzik Adan

- This is a DPS check battle.
- You have to react quickly to avoid being wiped out.
- During the battle, an arachnae nest will spawn on one of the four pillars.
- These spider nests have very low HP, so kill them as quickly as you can.
- If you leave this nest alive for too long you will see a message saying "An arachnae nest begins to ooze toxic liquid into the pit!", and the toxic water level in the center will begin to rise.
- Once the poison water overflows, the group will be destroyed.
- Additionally, this boss gains the Armor of the 7th King buff during the fight, which significantly reduces its damage.
- To remove this damage reduction buff, you must drag the boss under the spider nest you want to attack and then kill the spider nest.
- If successful, the message "The destroyed nest releases the rest of it's toxins in an explosive spray!" will appear and the boss's damage reduction effect will disappear.
- There will be a Seven Fanged Curse, which will slowly increase the counter.
- If the curse expires naturally, the person will die immediately.
- At the same time, under the influence of this curse, your moves that originally consumed mana will now consume HP instead.
- Under the influence of this curse, all your moves have a chance to increase the curse count by one. Try to make the number reach seven before the time limit expires and you will be fine.
- Finish him teleport you to Darvol

### Bhaly Adan

- First, pull Adan to Manifestation.
- The four Infected Microbes black balls will continue to replenish the Genetic Mutation buff.
- One person needs to be assigned to circle and tap the four black balls.
- Each time you click on the black ball, the Manifestation of Disease will reduce the number of Microbial Cell layers by one.
- During the battle, Adan will get the Communion of Plagues buff, which will make him invincible if there are ten spores in the air.
- Manifestation of Disease produces spores that replenish during combat.
- You can't target or do anything with the spores, the only way to make them go away is to keep hitting the Manifestation of Disease tree.
- During the battle, a Primordial Malice cultist will come out and join the battle. **Don't kill this mob**, because as long as you kill a cultist, someone in your team will be randomly selected to die.
