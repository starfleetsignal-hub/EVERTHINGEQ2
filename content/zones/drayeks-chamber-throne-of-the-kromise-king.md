## Background

The leader of the Kromise, [[King Drayek (Throne of Kromise King)|King Drayek]], has abducted a prismatic egg born of the union of [[Lord Nagafen (Nagafen's Lair)|Lord Nagafen]] and [[Lady Vox]]. Get in here and do what's right!

## Notes

- Do not kill Drayek too fast, as this will break the encounter. If soloing, mentor down to 50, or 55.
- [[The Sage of Ages]] and consequently [[Darathar]] make an appearance at the end.
