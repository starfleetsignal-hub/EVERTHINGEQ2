## Walkthrough

### [[Lady of the Lute]] (Tier 3)

1. Burn.

### [[Legion Captain Bloodrite]] (Tier 3)

1. Access:  Click button in back corner of the Lady of the Lute's room, in the fireplace room, "grab bowl of liquid", then click "douse" on the fireplace, then get close to the right side of the fireplace and "push to open"
1. Cure curses. Hard burn if you ignore curses. Flawless will be lost if you kill him while someone is cursed though.

### [[Gidget]] and [[The Bloodtender]] (Tier 4)

1. Access: click the lever directly behind Legion Captain Bloodrite to disable the beam and cross the room.
1. Cure curses. Burn. Flawless will be lost if you kill him while someone is cursed.

### [[Lysander Mistmoore (Epic)|Lysander Mistmoore]] (Tier 4)

1. This fight gets easier the more healers you have in group. 3 sufficed for my run. Follow him as he ports around, stop damage when he is on the ledge. Cure curse by targeting yourself.

### [[Vorigan Mistmoore (Epic)|Vorigan Mistmoore]] (Tier 4)

1. Burn.
