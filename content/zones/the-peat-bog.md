## Background

> The [Peat] Bog in particular is an area where seasons of growth have compacted upon dense, moist soil and the scent of decay in that section would make living there disagreeable to say the least.
>
> — [[Pearl Honeywine]]

Dark and dank, this zone is small, yet broken into smaller sections by circular wooden walls that vaguely resemble the edges of rotten tree trunks.  The former [[The City of Qeynos|Qeynos]] suburbs of [[Starcrest Commune]] and [[Nettleville]] are connected by a path made of wooden boards running along the zone's northern edge; so that their citizens wouldn't have to get their feet wet when crossing the bog. A grate leading to the moat in [[Antonica]] is found near the middle of the top edge of the zone.

The interior of the zone is laid out in a clockwise spiral, with stronger monsters found deeper in.

## Travel

The Peat Bog can be accessed by the Qeynos [[Mariner's Bell|mariner's bell]]. Zoning in will place you at the bottom of the ramp that leads to Nettleville.
**Evil-alignment**:
*The bell is within aggro range of one of the guards (20^^^ Heroic).
*The grate to Antonica is safe to use.

To find the sewer grate leading down to [[Vermin's Snye]], go south to [[Two Logs Pond]] and then due east.  The sewer entrance is near the edge of the zone, hard to see but safe to access.

## Trivia

- The monsters of the Peat Bog weren't always so oversized. When LU19 revamped all the newbie zones, the monsters of The Peat Bog were all greatly enlarged.  No particular reason was ever given for this.
- [[Two Logs Pond]] originally had a unique, muddy texture to the water.  This texture was removed from the water surface during the LU19 revamp, but it can still be seen underwater.
- There are several large purple mushrooms strewn out across the zone. If you kill the fungusmen guarding them, you can interact with them and recieve a shroom-tastic surprise!
