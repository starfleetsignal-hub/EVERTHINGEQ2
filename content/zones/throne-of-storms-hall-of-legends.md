## Bosses

There are five total bosses in the zone.  The first four can be fought in any order; all of them must be defeated before King Tormax will activate.

### [[Primus Pilus Gunnr (Epic)|Primus Pilus Gunnr]]

During the fight, Gunnr will summon three different kinds of adds - a misty warrior, a misty crusader, and a misty brawler.  The add must *not* be tanked by the same tank class as it is named (e.g. the misty brawler must not be tanked by a bruiser or monk).  If the wrong player gets aggro, Gunnr will kill all raid members of that archetype.

### [[Legatus Prime Mikill]]

Mikill must be kept in motion.  If he's allowed to stay in place for more than five seconds he'll unleash an AOE that wipes the raid entirely.  Having root prevention on the tank is essential for this fight.  Mikill has no adds. Standard practice is to keep the main raid force stationary and have the tank run in a circle around them. It can be difficult for melee to do full dps due to latency issues.

### [[Imperator Kolskeggr (Epic)|Imperator Kolskeggr]]

Kolskeggr casts an incurable elemental on the entire raid that reduces range by 50%.  If anyone affected by the elemental moves (including by jumping), it sets off an AOE.  Kolskeggr also continually spawns adds.  Stack the raid right on Kolskeggr's butt.  This fight is essentially a DPS check, raids that can't get Kolskeggr down fast enough will be overwhelmed by adds.

### [[Arch-Magistor Modrfrost]]

Modrfrost spawns two different adds.  These adds drop items that must be looted immediately and used to cure an otherwise incurable arcane and elemental detrimental. Each add drops 6 items, of their respective type, with one charge each. The items are instant-cast with no cooldown.

Modfrost will periodically cast  incurable arcane and elemental detriments on 4 people in the raid, the items dropped from the adds must be used to cure these quickly. All 4 elemental detriments must be cured, but only two of the arcane. Although 4 people receive an incurable arcane only 2 of these are "real". Curing the wrong arcane is a fail condition. The name of the arcane that must be cured is called "Ghastly Pallor".

Modfrost also randomly curses people, if this curse is not cured fast enough an incurable elemental spreads to the entire raid. This elemental can be cured with the item dropped from the adds but it is unlikely you will have sufficient charges to cure everyone.

### [[King Tormax (Epic)|King Tormax]]

Tormax requires the scout debuff from the Tower of Frozen Shadow x2.  Tormax will cast an energy field on the raid.  The raid must then joust out of its range before the field blows up.  Players who get caught in the explosion are afflicted with a viral curse.  If you are cursed, remain down where you are and wait for the raid to joust back to you for your rez. Tormax also summons a pair of x2 adds called [[A diaku warmonger]].
