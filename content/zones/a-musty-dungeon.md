## Walkthrough

This is a small instance only available during the quest [[Eliminate Geraufl]], part of the [[Froglok Timeline]]. The entrance is located in the [[The Freeport Militia House]], on the bottom floor. Your only objective is to kill [[Geraufl]]. To get into his cage, examine the barrel in front of the jail-cell door and blow it up.

Geraufl must be able to stun you as part of a scripted event that completes the quest. If he is grayed out then he might not be able to hit you, preventing the completion of the quest. To remedy this, [[Mentoring#Self-mentoring|mentor down]] to level 35 and don't use any one-shot abilities. Just aggro him and he should stun you soon after.

Good-aligned players can still complete this quest, even after mentoring down to a lower level. Just make sure to kill all the guards from the Chronomages located on the Freeport Docks to the entrance of the instance.
