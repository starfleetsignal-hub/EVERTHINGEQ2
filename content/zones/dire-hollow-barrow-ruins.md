## Background

Your many trials against the vampires and their nefarious minions in [[D'Morte Burial Chambers (Zone)|D'Morte Burial Chambers]], [[The Tombs of Night]] and [[The Crypt of T'haen]] have led you to this point. While undoubtedly the undead nature of vampires will cause [[The Crypt of T'Haen: Vengeance|trouble to rise again]] somewhere beneath that accursed cave under [[Soul Eater Falls]], you and your closest allies have been tasked with this final quest by *The Hand of Marr*. Enter the Barrow Ruins, slay this renegade pack of vampires and transcribe any writings you see that pertain to vampiric existence.

## Walkthrough

Simply slay everything you see, and examine the interactable pillars. The hellhounds will respawn, however the vampires will not. Once all the steps of the required quest are fulfilled, [[Varo Z'Kaz]] should spawn at his location in the middle of the instance. However, if he does not, then walk towards the entrance and then back down. An in-game message should appear in you chat box indicating that Varo Z'Kaz has spawned. Once he is killed it's safe to leave the instance.
