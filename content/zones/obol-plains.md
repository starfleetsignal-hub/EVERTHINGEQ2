based on the layout of [[Loping Plains]]

### Travel

To Travel to Obol Plains you must first own the [[Chains of Eternity]] expansion and then use an [[Ulteran Spires|Ulteran Spire]] to transport to the zone. The [[Druid Rings]] may also be used to reach Obol Plains as well as a [[Wormhole Generator: Obol Plains]] which is crafted by [[Tinkering|Tinkerers]].

The [[Guild Hall Amenities|Guild Hall Amenity]] version of the ulteran spires or druid rings will not give you access to Obol Plains. You must use the druid rings or spires that are in a regular zone.
