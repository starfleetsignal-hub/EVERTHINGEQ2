## Progression

Upon zoning in head east to the first intersection - take the north path past The Icebound Sentinels until you reach [[The Alluring Aerie]].

[[The Alluring Aerie]]

Clear the roaming trash and then the 'forsaken' valkyries around the orb in the center of the room. Eventually [[Reverie]] will spawn - kill her and continue through the west door.

[[The Frozen Menagerie]]

There are two non-aggressive named in this room [[Ankhef]] and [[Zaza]]. Recommend pulling trash closer to the door to keep from accidentally hitting one of them with an AOE and agro'ing them as they are linked. Once you are ready, a good strategy is to have an enchanter mez one of the two while the tank pulls agro from the other. Group burns down the first, then switches to the mezzed afterwards. After they're dead, head back to the first intersection you encountered near the zone in and this time head south.

[[Halls of Errant Thought]]

Initially the [[Chaotic Manipulation]] will not be attackable and there are four non-aggressive suppressed 'magics' floating in the area. Once you have killed any three of the four, the final one will be absorbed by the named and cause him to become attackable. Kill it and continue south.

[[Arcane Collective]]

- The key mob [[A frozen thought]] is outside next to the northern building.
- There is a book in the south room that can be clicked to pick up the questline for [[The White Dragonscale Cloak]].

Hallway eventually opens up into a large outside area with clickable scroll stand directly in front of the hallway, a small room to the north and to the south. Kill the trash in the area, then head into north room and look for a fluttering 'Slip of Paper' on the ground. Pick it up and then head into the south room and look for the same paper in that room. Once you have both papers, return to the scroll stand outside of the hallway and click it to 'Restore the Scroll'. This will spawn [[Arcanus]] close to where the key mob was located. Kill him and then either run back to the entrance or evac for the final named. *Note: He is not aggressive so you can safely evac without fear of accidentally agro'ing him.

When you get to the entrance room [[Shade of Miragul]] will now be spawned on the western wall. He has an AOE that does more damage the further you are from him and will also occasionally knock group members back. Fighting directly on top of him is the recommended kill strategy.
