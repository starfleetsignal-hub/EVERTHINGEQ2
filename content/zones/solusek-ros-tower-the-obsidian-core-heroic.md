## Lore

Deep within a blazing landscape of smoke and fire a giant volcano erupts, as it has for millennia, showering the Plane of Sun with molten rock and smoldering embers.

At the center of this volcano's lava-filled crater rises a monolith of rock, flame and obsidian; home of The Burning Prince and Lord of the Sun and Flame, Solusek Ro.

The tower's foundation lies deep, with lava flowing steadily throughout, forever altering and adding to the massive structure and filling every space with unbearable heat, making it inhospitable to any foreign entities who are not fully prepared to venture to this treacherous place.

Within this tower an unseen power draws Lanys T'Vyl and the Tenets of Hate. History tells of Solusek Ro discovering a crystal, The Dresolik, near the core of the volcano. He used the crystal to leech power from The Plane of Fire, home to his father, Fennin Ro.

That was many years ago. Today, all that remains of the crystal are the settings that held it in place at the highest point of the top floor of the tower, as it has long been destroyed, but not forgotten...

Lanys T'Vyl will stop at nothing to try to reclaim this power. Whether it be through Solusek Ro's help or not remains to be seen, but their plans have The Pantheon worried. Help Druzzil Ro stop The Tenets of Hate, and save The Planes of Prophecy!

## Walkthrough

### [[Everquest2:Cindrax (Heroic)|Cindrax]]

- First Boss in **Level 1** (top/entry)
- Clear all trash and three "construction of flame" to spawn boss
- Pull him to a dead end in case he knocks you to lava
- When Named HP drop tp 60%,40%,20%, he seals a player to a gargoyle around elevator each hp threshold. That poor player with "Bind Sight" will die in 60 sec.
- When Named Hp near to 16% or lower. He summons all 4 gargoyles and immunes damage. If a player happens to seal in gargoyle  and die. Gargoyles disappear and he can be damaged again. If not, kill all 4 gargoyles and kill Named.

### [[The Molten Behemoth (Heroic)|The Molten Behemoth]]

- Second Boss in **Level 2**
- Get back when seeing "**The Molten Behemoth begins expelling molten rocks! Get back!**"
- Pick up all **molten stones** after boss cast "Magmatic Expulsion" and place them on the brazier. Otherwise each stone heal boss 5%. 3 stone maximum

### [[Balrezu (Heroic)|Balrezu]]

- Third Boss in **Level 3**
- Kill all scorched fiend in the room at the end of the tunnel to spawn Balrezu then kill him
- He summons adds each time. first 2, second 4, last time 6.(6 braziers in room)He is immune while summoning
- Use your "Tag system" tag a add then remember which **brazier he comes from** and kill it last.  Click the brazier you killed last, Balrezu returns to fight. If you click wrong one, you will be teleported to a chamber full of Epic X4 mobs and get killed.

### [[Verlixa (Heroic)|Verlixa]]

1. Fourth Boss in **Level 4.**
1. Kill all trash mobs with huge mana drain and heading to the Boss
1. Burn the boss till he summon adds "Shimmering Mirages"
1. Remember the order and position brazier "Shimmering Mirages" flying to
1. Click brazier ---> Kill this Mirage , Click next brazier ---> Kill next Mirage ... ---> return to boss room and repeat step 3 ,4, 5 till boss dies
1. It's easiest to stand at the back of the room (with your back to the picture frame) when he summons the adds that go to the braziers.  This way you get a full view of the room and who's going where.  There's a brazier on each side of the room, one of the ceiling, one in the middle of the room, and one at the back near the picture frame.  Just make a mental note such as "Left, Right, Up, Center" then click and kill in that order.

### [[Galremos (Heroic)|Galremos]]

- Final Boss in **Level 5**
- Before finish all trash,please try to stay close to NPC "Lift Attendance". Named won't spawn on that side
- Kill all the obsidian trash mobs about 4 waves to summon named
- Start fighting named. See link above for detail info
- Drag adds during fight away from boss around 10 meters away. Or kill adds within 50 sec before it self-destuction(it explodes and heals boss 10% hp if close to boss. And strong AOE knock back)
- Every 90 sec curse kill a teammate. Keep "Burning Mana" so cure curse can cure
- DPS keeps drag trash and burn them. Other teammates use ascension spell burn down boss
