## Background

It is said that the Awakened are blessed with unnatural power, grace and potential. Perhaps that is why a series of trials have been set up - in order to weed out the weak and unwilling. Four trials are set down by [[The Keeper]] in [[The Halls of Fate (POI)|The Halls of Fate]]. Those who are ready to attempt the trials will be sought out by the keeper on the Rim of Eternity. - *(Partial excerpt from [[Book of Trials]])*
