## Walkthrough

### [[Flora Maldehyde]] (Tier 1)

- Needs a Stifle belt rune.
- Flora casts a trauma detriment on some players, ***' that MUST NOT be cured until the whole raid gets an arcane detriment, **Antiseptic Splash** at which time ***' MUST BE CURED. Failing on the cure steps wipes the raid.
  - *NOTE: Healers, Crusaders, and anyone else who may have a CA, spell, or AA ability that has a cure component need to review their spells before the fight and make absolutely sure they don't cast any of these while fighting Flora!*
- When you get the emote *Flora Maldehyde points a little too hard and her hand rips clear off...*, she will spawn an add, [[Flora's Severed Hand]].

### [[Beulus the Claw]] (Tier 1)

- Needs a Stun belt rune.
- Joust out when Beulus casts **Flurry of Claws**. You can come back in when he finished casting **Haze**.
- **Curse of Claws** must be cured. If someone dies with the curse on them, it kills everyone in the raid of the same archetype.

### [[Odelia Wretch]] (Tier 3)

- Add Components from "Awakening Life Potion" by right clicking the cauldron:
  - Akashic Rosemary Tuft, then ingredients for Potion of Clarity
- Needs a Fear belt rune.
- When Odelia casts **Transformation Warts** on the raid, the curses must be cleared. Healers' cures reset immediately allowing them to cure everyone as needed.

### [[Sister Belladonna]] and [[Auntie Grimm]] (Tier 2)

- Belladonna: add components from "Sister's Potion of Protection"  by right clicking the cauldron:
  - Pinch of Akashic Sage, Akashic  Sandalwood Shaving, Akashic Angelic Root Strand, Hardened Akashic Clove, and Pinch of Akashic Salt
- Grim: add components from "Auntie's Potion of Good Health" by right clicking the cauldron:
  - Ground Akashic Thyme, Akashic Eucalyptus Stalk, Akashic Pine Heart, and Essence of Akashic Ginger.
- Burn.

### [[Cloaked Necromancer]] (Tier 3)

- Burn

### [[Mother Ballantree]] (Tier 3)

- Burn.
- Loot the Mother’s Will from the chest.

### [[Grandmother Deleria]] (Tier 4)

- Burn her Heart down, and keep it down. It will regenerate health. When an incurable arcane hits, use the Mother’s Will on the heart, then cure while away from the Heart. Burn.
