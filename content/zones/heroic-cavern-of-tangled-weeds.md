An instance for 1-6 players with no lockout timer. Choose [[Cavern of Tangled Weeds|Solo]] or Heroic when entering.

## Description

- A small instance of 4 caves, with several encounters of level 36-37.
- Most mobs are healers, and mostly come in groups, making it a unique solo challenge.
- Bosses are very rare in the solo version; roughly 1 in 5 chance for each boss.

## Entrance

- Located in the twisted nymph area in northeast Enchanted Lands. (-410,-800) or (-640,-720) or (-500,-870) or (-450,-830)
- A ***very small bump*** in the ground that can be highlighted, with the message "*a moss-covered mound*". Sometimes only a tiny corner of the bump is poking out of the ground. Can be very hard to find.
- The entrance moves to different parts of the nymph area each time it is used.

## Bosses

- [[The Tangle Weed]] (Level 36) - last room of instance - no AA
- [[A Corrupt Cavern Tangle]] (Level 36) - halfway in
