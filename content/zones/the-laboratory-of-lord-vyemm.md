## Background

Deep within the lower levels of [[The Halls of Fate]], [[Lord Vyemm]] and his host of vile creatures work to exploit the *wurndrom*, creating increasingly foul amalgamations of droags for an eventual assault on [[Norrath]]. Together with [[The Temple of Scale]], these hallowed halls hold the most powerful dragonkind of the [[Overrealm]].
