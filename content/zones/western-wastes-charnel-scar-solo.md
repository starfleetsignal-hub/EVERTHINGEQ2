## Walkthrough

- Kill [[Ilhona the Relentless]] at {{waypoint -45, 4, 321}} (Stun  Rune ) & Knockback
  - May be beneficial to get underneath her while burning her down...
- Kill [[Glutterous Klag]] at {{waypoint -186, -32, 371}}
  - Tank and spank, has adds (use AoE if any)
- Kill [[Kralalach]] at {{waypoint -289, -32, 420}}
  - Has a knockback, Stun, no need to kill the adds.
- *Note:killing him removes the bone barrier.*
- Kill [[The Bonepicker]] at {{waypoint -552, -5, 457}}
  - * Mob has an AE stun attack and hits hard*
- Kill [[Alkorahil the Slicer]] and the 2 [[A Chetari fleshcleaver]] at {{waypoint -730, 67, 300}}
  - *Has a knockback*
