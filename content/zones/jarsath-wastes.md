Jarsath Wastes is comprised of two distinct halves: the [[Skyfire Mountains]], a large volcanic plateau in the east, and the Overthere, a rugged plain in the west that gradually slopes down to the sea. Fortunatley for the denizes of the lower plains, the lava flows meandering through the Skyfire Moutains flow into the east, and there is virtually no spillover westward. The only spillover that does occur, is when the dragonkind inhabitants of the Skyfire Mountains send waves of droags to attack [[Ganak's Battlement]]. The battlement is the main defense of the southern region of the Overthere, where the Iksar have their stronghold of [[Danak Shipyards]] as well as their ancient cities of [[Charasis: Maiden's Chamber|Charasis]] and [[Chelsith]].

[[The Age of Destiny (Lore)|Recently]], the sentient factions of the Overthere have begun sending raiding parties into the Skyfire mountains, as well as test their influence over the western plain. Di'Zok of the Sel'Nok Brigade have set up encampments throughout Jarsath Wastes to establish themselves in the region, as well as to keep tabs on their Iksar enemies, the Legion of Danak, and their even greater enemies, the dragons. Clan Skleross, a group of skorpikis who desire to carve out a new future for themselves, have been seeking adventures to help them reclaim their homeland in the [[Howling Stones]] and [[Traitor's Pass]] regions of the zone. Without question, however, the most powerful faction in the Overthere is the Legion of Danak. They control the only active port in the zone, as well as the majority of the main comercial highway that connects the northern and southern sections of the Overthere. While they are mostly autonomous, they still swear fealty to [[Sebilis]] and are loyal to the remnants of the former Sebilisian Empire.

Jarsath Wastes is the remnant of the [[EverQuest|EQ1]] zones of [The Overthere](https://wiki.project1999.com/The_Overthere) and [Skyfire Mountains](https://wiki.project1999.com/Skyfire_mountains).

## Getting to Jarsath Wastes

- Northern entrance, from [[Kylong Plains]]: {{waypoint 2406, 154, -2886}}
- [[Mariner's Bell]] will place you at [[Danak Shipyards]], in the southern part of the zone.
  - If you do not have sufficient faction with the Legion of Danak, you will be attacked on sight. For players who have never quested in Jarsath Wastes before, it is recommended that you enter from Kylong Plains, in the north.
