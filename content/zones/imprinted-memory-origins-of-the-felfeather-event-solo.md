To enter click the pedestal next to Tento Felfeather {{waypoint -664, 5, -559}} in The Barren Sky

**Note**: In the Heroic version have one groupmember ONLY click through the dialogue and lead the brothers.

## Walkthrough

1. Move toward the The Flock until you see “You enter the area while some sort of ritual is taking place.”
1. Wait for the dialog to finish and Manifestation of Ilk and Manifestation of Nurturing to spawn
1. Kill each Manifestation and then right click on the wisps (can be done in either order) and lead them to the appropriate NPC. The wisps are color-coded to match the target brother.
   - *You may re-summon your Merc at this point, even though it was suspended to enter this instance.*
   - Lead the wisp from Manifestation of Ilk to Brother Parat Felfeather
   - Lead the wisp from Manifestation of Nurturing to Brother Jeefa Felfeather
1. Hail each of the brothers in turn then hail The Flock – wait for dialog to finish.
1. A bunch of summoned elementals will spawn along with Master of Elemental Dawn – kill the summoned elementals (they form in a ring around the rim of the island) or they will add.
1. Right click on either brother and lead them with you to kill the Master of Elemental Dawn.
   - *If you die you must set the brother to follow again or you will get the Boss down to 2% and no further. Even if you can't usually cast while running, try running and casting.*
1. Hail The Flock and the Elemental Dusk will spawn
1. Right click on the remaining brother, and take them with you to kill the Elemental Dusk
1. Hail The Flock and wait for the dialog to finish, he will become Ikarantis, Mouth of the Sovereign Will, and attack. Kill him.
   - *He has a nasty frontal - just duck behind him when he emotes "unleash an sweeping attack at everyone in front.."*
