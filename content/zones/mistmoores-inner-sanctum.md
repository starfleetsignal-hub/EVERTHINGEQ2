## Background

This is the heart of evil, where [[Mayong Mistmoore (Instanced)|Mayong Mistmoore]] broods in the dark. Secluded from the rest of the castle, the inner sanctum is where the most powerful vampires dwell. Listen to that voice in your head, and leave now or face his wrath!

## Overview

[[A sanctum chaperone|Sanctum chaperones]] wander throughout the instance and respawn. They will aggro even if greyed out. Once in their proximity they will run to nearby [[A stoneskin destroyer|stoneskin destroyers]] and activate them. They also have an auto-stun/silence on the first player they hit. The best way to deal with them is to attack them from afar so they won't aggro the gargoyles.
