## Walkthrough

### Notes

- When clearing the trash in the zone, there are increments on the group adds that make them harder to kill. Control effects cast on the adds in the group will reduce the stacks and make the trash much easier to kill.
- There are big power drains throughout the zone.

### [[Haggle Baron Klok (The Fabled Kurn's Tower)|Haggle Baron Klok]]

1. The Haggle Baron is active immediately upon entering the zone, and he wanders around the first floor.
1. Do NOT cure the trauma from **Shield Break**
   - Klok casts a curable trauma, Shield Break, which lands on all characters within 10m of him.
   - Shield Break instantly removes 50% durability from the target's equipped secondary item. This durability loss can break the item (if it reaches 0%), removing all useful stats from the item.
   - If the trauma expires naturally, the item will regain the lost durability. If the afflicted character is cured or dies, the durability loss is permanent.
   - This spell has a very short cast bar, but it is preceded by the emote "Klok breaks down the defense of <player> and those around them!"
1. MUST cure the elemental from **Light as a Feather, Dead as a Doornail**
   - Klok casts a knockback, Light as a Feather, Dead as a Doornail, which throws nearby characters high up into the air.
   - The spell also applies a curable elemental by the same name. If this detriment expires naturally, the afflicted character dies instantly.
   - This spell has a medium-length cast bar.
1. INTERRUPT the arcane **Draw Life** and dispel after it lands.
   - Klok casts a curable arcane, Draw Life, which lands on several nearby characters. This spell deals significant damage to the afflicted characters and simultaneously heals Klok by percentages.
   - Draw Life has a medium-length cast bar, and it can be interrupted to prevent both the damage and the heal effects.
   - The heal portion of this spell can be dispelled after Draw Life lands.
1. MUST cure the noxious **Corruption**, BUT ONLY when the characters are within 2 meters of one another.
   - Best if all players stand within 2m of one another to ensure cure lands properly.
   - Klok casts a curable noxious, Corruption, which lands on one random character in the raid.
   - The detriment states (in the text above the effect list) that "Corruption should only be cured within 2 meters of a <class>". If the afflicted character is not within 2 meters of another character who is of the specified class, and is cured, that character dies, and two other characters are hit with a curable curse named **Absolute Corruption**.
   - If **Corruption** expires naturally, then one or two other characters are hit with **Absolute Corruption**.
1. When hit with an incurable detriment, look at your detriment window to see whether it's **Cobalt Sense of Impending Doom** or **Crimson Sense of Impending Doom** and run to the dome of corresponding color.
   - Two large bubbles will appear at the north, south, east, or west ends of the room (the locations are chosen randomly each time).
   - All characters must run to the bubble which match the color of their detriment. Characters which do not enter their bubble quickly enough (or those that run to the wrong bubble) are hit with a charm called **Silvered Voice of Persuasion**. This charm causes the character to move and act under Klok's control, unable to cast any spells, and summons [[A charming persona]] mob in their place. The charm and mob remain until the mob is killed or until the charmed character is killed.
1. Around 10-15% , Klok "panics" and revives fallen allies, which immediately come to his aid.
   - The summoned mobs have a large knockback that roots the character upon landing (the root is a curable trauma).
   - During this time, character deaths do not increment **Mortal Coil** nor do they trigger a heal, as would normally happen in this fight.
   - Klok continues casting his trauma, arcane, noxious, and elemental spells, but he no longer performs the red and blue bubble script.
1. Klok does not cast any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] may be used as a simple debuff.

### [[Sir Rouland (The Fabled Kurn's Tower)|Sir Rouland]]

1. Sir Rouland has a massive kick. To avoid being thrown off the platform, everyone wedges underneath the arch in one of the vertical supports for the fight.
1. Equip stifle rune in belt.
1. Massive power drain.
1. When the elemental dot goes on the group along with an uncurable curse, the mage in the group gets a message to cure two people. This must be done quickly. The curses on the two people cured by the mage become curable by the priest.
1. The mob mem wipes the tank throughout the fight. The tank must snap it back quickly because the mob checks every second for who has aggro. The mob increments each time it checks and is not on a fighter. If the count gets to greater than 5, it wipes the group.

### [[Ilenee's Despair (The Fabled Kurn's Tower)|Ilenee's Despair]] and [[Ilenee's Betrayal (The Fabled Kurn's Tower)|Ilenee's Betrayal]]

1. Raid splits into two groups, half on Despair, half on Betrayal.
1. Mobs must be kept 30 meters apart or they stop taking damage.
1. Mobs' health must be kept within 10% of each other.
1. There are specific damage types for each mob. One requires divine (Divine Embrace), the other requires heat (Flames of Yore).
1. A curse can apply from the mob a player is damaging. It can be priest cured (limited w/ 1 min cooldown) or the player can clear it by damaging the opposite mob (25% chance to clear curse with each attack). The curse reduces stats but does not kill the player.
1. Intermittently, the mobs will port everyone, mobs will be in separate rooms. Regroup on your assigned mob.

### [[Ione the Lifebringer (The Fabled Kurn's Tower)|Ione the Lifebringer]]

1. Use Lord Kurn to kill Ione.
   - Kill the chains that are holding Lord Kurn.
   - One player at a time clicks on Lord Kurn to possess him and do lots of damage to Ione.
   - A new hotbar comes up, just spam the buttons as they refresh.
   - Once Ione rechains Lord Kurn, the player that went in no longer possesses and cannot go in until at least 1-2 other players possess Lord Kurn.
   - Re-kill the chains, rinse repeat until the mob is dead
1. A curse applies randomly to one player at a time. Do not cure the curse! It gives that player +300,000 potency.
1. Periodically, a pair of adds come in, kill them and return to Ione. Ione takes very little damage while the adds are up.

### [[Warlord Kurn Machta (The Fabled Kurn's Tower)|Warlord Kurn Machta]]

1. Early in the fight, Warlord Kurn Macha will drop a key.
   - Someone must pick the key up (not the tank because they are going to have to intercept whoever is using the key periodically).
   - The person uses the key on the tank, who then must intercept the key holder.
   - The effect is **Warlord Kurn Machta's Iron Maiden** .
   - The intercept mechanic happens periodically throughout the fight.
1. A group of [[A tortured spirit (The Fabled Kurn's Tower)|a tortured spirit]] adds spawn periodically during the fight.
   - Charm one of these adds then kill the rest. This will prevent the Warlord from continuing to spawn additional groups of adds throughout the fight.
   - Can use the [[Charm of Undead Allure]], which lasts 10 min., available from the Relic and Signet Peddler GH amenity.
   - Once the add is charmed, do not allow it to fight so that the charm has less chance of breaking early.
   - During the fight, may need three people to be able to charm so that a new charm can be applied before the previous charm expires, or if the player dies, someone else can charm it again. You must have three concentration slots open in order to charm an add.
   - When you kill all but the charmed add, they drop power-draining circles on the ground. Move a short distance to get out of the power drain.
1. During the first 50% of the fight: a trauma dot, **Induced Agony**, goes on the whole group and needs to be cured ASAP because it hits hard.
1. Periodically during the fight, a noxious dot applies to one person.
   - Whoever has the noxious can't see the Warlord but can instead see bones on the floor around the room. Someone else in the group/raid that can see the Warlord will need to call out the number in the Warlord's buff package of how many bones need to be aggroed by the person with the noxious dot.
   - The number of bones varies from maybe 4-12. At least the number bones in the buff must be killed within 20 seconds.
   - Have the group holds AOEs until at least that number of bones is on the group.
   - If you don't kill enough bones, whoever has the noxious detriment (it will pass to another person if cured) will have to gather another set of bones.
   - You get a good amount of time to do this mechanic so: come back for heals if needed, do not run away from the group unit trauma is cured (or pot cure your own trauma), do not run off if you have one of the two curses that go out in the 1st 50%.
1. In the first 50% of the fight, curses will go on two members of the group.
   - In the detriment window, there will be a counter that starts at 999 when the curse lands.
   - Call for a curse cure when the counter is 99 or less.
   - It is best to stay within 5 meters of Lord Kurn because the curse will tick down by 300 each tick, meaning you only have to wait for 3 ticks before cure.
   - If you are out 6-10m, the tick down is 200, 11-15m is 100, >15m is 50 per tick.
   - If you are caught out away from the mob when the curse lands and it ticks down a lesser amount, you will do math to figure out how far out you have to stay to not kill yourself by ticking from some number more than 99 to 0.
1. During the last 50% of the fight, the curse mechanic changes.
   - Everyone in the group will get a curse.
   - The tank and the person with the key stays with the mob and the other players joust out so that their combined distance from Lord Kurn Macha is at least 500m. Then the curse on any one player can be cured. Everyone is cured and ported back to the mob.
1. The orbs that spawn at the edges and move through the room de-target you if they hit you, and they power drain. Generally they can be ignored.
