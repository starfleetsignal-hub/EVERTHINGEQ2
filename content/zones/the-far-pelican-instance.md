## Background

You've been accused of stealing the goods of The Far Seas Trading Company from [[Dead River Docks]] in the [[The Thundering Steppes|Thundering Steppes]] by [[Quint Cerlius M.E. (TS)|Quint Cerlius M.E.]]. You must board the The Far Pelican and rescue the crew to prove your innocence. Hopefully after you accomplish this you can learn where this [[Isle of Mara]] that the packages on the docks were supposed to be shipped to is located.

## Notes

- This one-time instance is only accessed while on the quest [[The Far, Far Away Far Pelican]], which is the access quest for the [[Isle of Mara]] and the [[The Village of Shin|Village of Shin]].
