## Notes:

Introductory zone: Can enter with ToV gear or quest gear with little-to-no problem

### **Huntmaster Au'mari**

A basic 'turn and burn.'

### **The Swamp Mother**

Another basic 'turn and burn' with many adds.

### **Gra'ta the Mountain**

You *can *choose to clear out the ipsumodons in the area before pulling.  Gra'ta has a rather large awareness box and will attack you as you approach.  If you don't intend to pull, stand back.  When Gra'ta engages, all of the ipsumodons add.  There is a KB/stun/stiffle/CC [insert info here].   Also, be on the lookout for Tail Swipe.  It is blockable, or just joust out.

### **Beastmaster Kau'mua**

You have to kill Gra'ta the Mountain or the Beastmaster will not spawn!

If you do not clear the adds in the area, they will add to the encounter.

Focus your attention on the Beastmaster.  Ceracuspis moves rather slowly until he charges at an individual-- The person glows red and drops a red circle onto the charge point.  You *can* be smart with the charging... or you can let it ram into the group.  It's just a KB, so nothing major.

### **[[Mo'iana the Ravenous (Heroic)|Mo'iana the Ravenous]]**

There are two main phases 100-20% and sub 20%.  At 20%, Mo'iana gets a pretty hefty Damage Reduction buff.  Also, there's something about folks needing to stand apart from one another.  Bite-Sized Morsels is a Lifetap.  If you can't burn through it, stand away/apart.

Mo'iana has a rather common trauma AE, Stomping Grounds.  There is also the curse-- everyone gets a curse, only one of said curses turns into a parasite.  You can communicate with one another re: who needs the curse cured via voice chat or group chat..., or ignore it..., or there is an ACT trigger for it:
