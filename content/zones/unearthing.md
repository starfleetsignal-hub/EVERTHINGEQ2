## Background

You've discovered an ancient Teir'Dal ring bearing the insignia of the *D'Morte* family. Upon further examination of the ring, an image of the [[Citadel of Gul'Thex]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] became etched into your mind. As you made your way there you stumbled upon the very burial chamber of the D'Morte family itself!
