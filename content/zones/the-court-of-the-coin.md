## Background

This is the headquarters for the Court of the Coin, [[The Courts of Maj'Dul]] which controls the flow of money within the city. According to [[Kallon Ebbtide]], "they seek to lead and revive the Dervish Empire through commerce and trading."

## Notes

This zone can be entered after you reach kindly faction (20,000+) with the [[The Court of the Coin (Faction)|Court of the Coin]]. See [[Court of the Coin Timeline]] for the repeatable quests which can be used to raise your faction. You can also obtain insignia tokens which can be exchanged in [[The Tower of the Moon]] for faction with the Court.

Insignia tokens can be attained either by doing [[A Tears grifter|Tears Grifter]] quests or by killing guards for the [[The Court of the Blades (Faction)|Court of the Blades]] or [[The Court of Truth]]. Inside the court you may gain more faction by completing repeatable quests. You must reach ally faction (40,000+) to complete the quest timeline for the Court. To earn the [[Vindicator of the Coin]] title. you must be level 60, have full ally faction (50,000) and complete the [[Loyalty Pays]] quest.

The most efficient method of increasing your faction with the Court of the Coin is to repeatedly clear the guard towers controlled by the opposing factions, but do not take control of the towers. Instead, collect the Blades and Truth insignia tokens and trade them in for faction. This method is much faster than completing quests, and once you have reached the maximum faction with the Court, you can begin the final series of quests starting with [[Court of the Coin: Maimun's Gifts]].

Inside the headquarters there are the following:

- [[Mailbox|Norrathian Express mailbox]]
- Banker
- Freeport and Qeynos [[Broker|brokers]]
- Mender
- Faction merchant that will sell advanced Tier 6 crafting recipes ([[Heirloom]])
- A merchant that you can sell any status loot items to
- Merchants the sell Journeyman versions of Tier 6 combat arts and spells
- Court Treasures merchant that will sell you house pets, [[Creature Cataloging Timeline|catalogues]] for catalogue quests, special armor, [[Mounts]] and residency licenses that allow you to buy housing in Maj'Dul.
- A quest giver for [[Becoming a Resident of Ro]] that will give you [[Call of Ro]] to return to Maj'Dul.
