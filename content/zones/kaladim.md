## Lore

The Stormguard mustered during the War of The Fey to support the elves of Telethin and Felwithe. They were engaged by trolls and ogres of the Neriak Alliance in the Lesser Feydark. Ultimately, they were successful in pushing through to the Greater Feydark after King Kazon Stormhammer and the Ogrebane Brigade won the Battle of Brute Hill thanks to the Stormhammer, a relic of Brell. They supported the elves during the Teir'Dal's last spiteful assault: setting fire to the forest. This final battle was known as the Fires of Faydark.

Before [[The Age of Cataclysms (Lore)|The Age of Cataclysms]], a gnomish construct came to Kaladim, city of the Dwarves, with an image of The Vaults of Serilis, a temple built by [[Brell Serilis]] himself in the deepest reaches of [[Norrath]].  Most dwarves, including the army, left Kaladim on a pilgrimage to find the Vaults, leaving behind only civilians.

When the Cataclysm struck, Kaladim suffered a severe cave-in, trapping many dwarves inside.  The dwarves that had been outside mounted a rescue expedition, aided at first by the trapped dwarves working from the inside.  Before the cave-in was cleared, all sounds from within Kaladim had ceased.  Finally breaking in, the dwarves had found that the Cataclysm had not only caused the cave-in, but opened a path for the [[Horde of Serilis]] to leave the Underfoot and invade Norrath.  The Horde of Serilis slaughtered the dwarves remaining in Kaladim and set it up as their new home.

With the Stormguard still in search of the Vaults of Serilis, [[King Stormhammer]] recalled the [[The Irontoe Brigade (Faction)|Irontoe Brigade]].  They now camp in the [[Butcherblock Mountains]], planning to build an army and take their home back from the Horde.

## Notes

- Kaladim was revamped with the release of *[[Sentinel's Fate]]* to be a 30s dungeon.  It was formerly a 60s dungeon.

### [[Ogrebane Arena]]

1. Clear all the various kinds of spectators - [[A Boombottom spectator|Boombottom spectator]]s, [[A Grungetalon spectator|Grungetalon spectator]]s, [[A Kragbak spectator|Kragbak spectator]]s and [[A Terrok spectator|Terrok spectator]]s. 1. After beating whichever namer spawns, [[The Ringmaster]] will run down into the arena floor and become attackable.

1. Kill the monsters by the pool of water in the south room: four [[A Terrok bulwark|Terrok bulwark]]s, then [[A Grungetalon geochanter]] and two [[A crystal construct]].
1. In the middle room, kill the groups of monsters surrounding the inactive Avatar of Below.  One [[A Grungetalon chipper (39-40)|Grungetalon chipper]], one [[A Grungetalon infuser|Grungetalon infuser]], one [[A Grungetalon packer|Grungetalon packer]] and [[A Ratillik overseer]].
