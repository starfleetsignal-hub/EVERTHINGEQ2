## Background

The Mystic Lake is an outdoor adventure area attached to [[The Village of Shin]] on the [[Isle of Mara]].  It is suitable for soloers and groups around level 55-68.  Populated by the [[Pirates of Gunthak (Faction)|Pirates of Gunthak]], [[The Mystic Guardians (Faction)|forest creatures]], [[The Black Death|undead skeletons]], [[The Forsaken City|drowned temples]], a [[Mystic Lake|mountain lake]], and a particularly hard glade at the far end, this densely packed zone offers a fairly large amount of content in a small area.

## Geography

The zone is neatly divided into four major areas:

- [[Mystic Woods]]: levels 55-56, mostly solo
- [[Tomb of the Sensei]] and [[Shadow Woods]]: level 58-62, solo and heroic
- The Mystic Lake and [[The Rotting Ground]]: levels 60-64, solo and heroic
- [[Direvine Wood]]: levels 65-68, mostly heroic

### Travel

Despite being an outdoor zone, flying leaping and gliding mounts do not work in this zone. Adventurers will have to navigate it the old fashioned way - on foot.

The four sections of the zone are separated by dangerous passages.  It is necessary to deal with aggressive monsters in each of the connecting passages, or to have invisibility or stealth.  A one-way passage leads from the final area back close to the entrance.

In order to reach the dungeon [[The Forsaken City]] and the instance [[Nizara, City of the Nayad]] contained therein, it is necessary to have good swimming and breathing.  The entrance is located a considerable distance under the surface of the lake.
