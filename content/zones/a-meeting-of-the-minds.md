## Background

[[Taryn D'Vat]], an agent of the Circle of the Unseen Hand, warns you before entering this ominous lagoon: "Two ancient and powerful beings are planning on striking an alliance to claim [[Norrath]] as their own. This meeting must not be allowed to take place as it would put our future at their feet. If this alliance is struck and their powers combined, Norrath would reel in its wake." The results of your efforts might be beyond your understanding, but action must nonetheless be taken.

## Access

To enter this zone you need to speak to the [[Taryn D'Vat|NPC]] outside the entrance at {{waypoint -911, -5, 361}}. Every time you zone out from the instance you must speak with this NPC again.

## Notes

- Has around ~8,000,000 HP on Varsoon.
- Clear the adds he spawns (but not Pkzwk Tzkr).
- There is only one encounter, with no trash to clear, like most T5 instances.
- If you do not kill [[Overlord Oxulius]] before [[Pkzwk Tzkr]], you will receive messages in your chat box in which Oxulius asks for aid from other powerful evil eyes, [[Borxx (A Meeting of the Minds)|Borxx]], [[Broxx (A Meeting of the Minds)|Broxx]], [[Brixx (A Meeting of the Minds)|Brixx]], and [[Braxx (A Meeting of the Minds)|Braxx]]. Each is worth AA experience independent of the others.
