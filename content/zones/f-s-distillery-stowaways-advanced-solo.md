## Progression

Straightforward instance.  Most progress is gated by progressive boss kills.  Players enter at the bottom level.

Aquire the quest [[Private Property (Advanced Solo)]] by clicking on the sign at the zone-in
*(this quest and its thorough wiki entry has a more detailed [[Private Property (Advanced Solo)|walkthrough]] of the instance.)*

Quicklist (for those who don't want to read the entire walkthrough):

1. Enter zone at bottom level
   1. (Optional) Acquire the quest [[Private Property (Advanced Solo)|Private Property]] at zone-in.
   1. Kill [[Oofgoof Doof]] to loot the [[North lift lever]] and [[South lift lever]] which... activate the North and South lifts respectively.  Allowing players to...
1. Access the top level
   1. [[Sir Wharfie]] (non-hostile) is feeding on some cargo.  Eliminate him to flush out the 'Stowaways' aboard
   1. Kill x8 [[A stowaway lush|stowaways]] to spawn the final boss [[Zaxfalump|Captain Zaxfalump]] and his First Mate, [[Fwump]]
   1. (Optional) Gather 'collection shinys' that spawn after defeating final bosses.

## Notes

You can use any [[Globe of Swift Travel]] to reach the [[Far Seas Distillery]] dock, which is near the zone-in point for this quest and others associated with the Rum Cellar Campaign quests.
