## Entrance

1. Starting from [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den]], use the terraporter for [[The Drowned Caverns: Outer Grotto]] at {{waypoint 42.88, -78.54, -227.83}}.
1. Once inside, go northeast towards the entrance to [[The Drowned Caverns: Flammable Fur|Flammable Fur]] terraporter. The pillar spirals around, go to the top at {{waypoint -23.76, 31.45, 13.70}}.
1. From the top of the pillar, jump to the neighboring pillar to the south at {{waypoint -23.12, 33.16, 21.14}}.
1. Jump down to the pillar to the southwest at {{waypoint -8.93, 26.84, 27.47}}.
1. From there, jump to the next pillar to the west at {{waypoint 1.93, 26.26, 21.69}} and go up and around to the top.
1. Jump across to the next pillar to the northwest and you'll see the terraporter entrance at {{waypoint 9.95, 35.85, -6.67}}.
