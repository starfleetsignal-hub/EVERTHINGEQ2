### Walkthrough

Upon zoning in you will see [[Prime Marus Nhak]] and two tallonite hunter-seekers that are not yet able to be engaged. Approach them to trigger the script dialogue until they can eventually be targeted.

[[Prime Marus Nhak]]

- Nhak will occasionally use an ability to teleport a member of the group around the room and will eventually call for a swarm of zekling adds. They do not hit very hard, just have the tank pick up agro and burn them down.

Continue south to reach the first intersection and follow the ramp east to continue the ascent. Be mindful that there are avazeks perched above as you progress that will drop down and attack the group.

You'll eventually come to a door with a barrier blocking your way. To gain access, continue following the ramp up and there will be a group of arazek's surrounding a targetable brazier at the end of the ramp. Kill them and destroy the brazier then head back to the door with the barrier and there will be a cube now floating in front of it - right-click it and 'Deactivate Mystic Field' to gain access to the next area.

As soon as you pass into the doorway you will immediately agro two groups of avazeks, recommend the tank face them away from the group as they have some devastated frontal PBAE attacks. Clear the remaining trash mobs across the room and prepare for the next named.

[[Djuul Ripclaw]]

- Djuul has a few different abilities that are interruptable, only one that is of note is a hard hitting single target attack called Serrated Wound. Recommend using a death prevent on the tank and trying to burn him down as quickly as possible. This is a straight forward tank and burn fight.

After defeating Djuul, head up to the south platform where he was when you entered the room for the lift - right click and raise it to the next level.

This area contains some familiar tallonite's from the Sentinel's Fate era, including some that are in stealth. Recommend fighting them against a wall or obstacle as they have a knockback that could push you over the edge and result in death. Head west and begin your ascent up.

Once again you'll come upon a door with another barrier. Ignore it for now and continue up the ramp until you come across another brazier. Destroy it and head back down to the barrier for another cube that you can right-click to disable this shield and pass into the room. Head south in the room towards the set of stairs to agro the stealthed tallonites overlooking the ledge before engaging the next named on the north side of the room.

[[Shini-Zonn Livia]]

- During the fight Livia will trigger Spirit of the Darkwalker on the group which can hit pretty hard and continuously knockback the group members name that is emoted. Recommend having a bard or druid use an AE block if available after pulling as the effect seems to be shortly after engaging.

- At around 50% health Livia will become unattackable with a bubble around her and summon three familiar adds. There is a longer than expected delay for Livia to become attackable again even once the adds are dead, be patient.

- Burn Livia the rest of the way down. She may summon a floating stack of red rocks before she dies, if she does have the group move out of the area as they pulse some decent damage to anyone close to them.

Proceed up the stairs to the south, making sure to destroy the brazier at the top of the stairs before ascending the ramp east. Fight your way up the ramp until you get to another barrier. Right-clock the cube to open the barrier, walk forward and kill the trash that rushes you before ascending the ramp north.

Once at the top of the ramp there will be a door... WITH NO BARRIER? /gasp. Proceed inside where there will be a handful of trash encounters around the room and the next named on the north side. Be careful when engaging the two encounters on the far south side of the bottom level, the "masters" open with a large attack and have a huge knock-up effect.

[[Prime Shini-Zonn Hypatia]]

- During the fight different types of "souls" will splinter off from Hypatia. One will explode upon death and the other will let her heal herself if not killed in a timely manner. Around 60% she fortified herself with a buff to let her absorb 999 attacks.
- Not sure which performed which effect, so our strategy was to DPS her down until she went "immune", kill a soul and move away from where it died - rinsed and repeated until she the emote that she was attackable again appeared and burned her down.

Head up either ramp on the south side of the room and through the doorway to get to the next ramp - ascend it headed west.

There will be another barrier blocked door, ignore it and continue up the ramp until you get to the final brazier - destroy it and head back down to the door and disable the barrier. Tallon will begin his script, use this time to try to clear all of the trash in the area before the final boss becomes targetable.

[[Warmaster Korok Hai]]

- He begins the fight rooted at the top of the stairs and will continuously spawn adds that will attack you in 3 different waves in the following order:
  - (8) diaku slicer
  - (2) rallosian gorefiends
  - (1) rallosian blademaster
- Our strategy was to just focus DPS on Warmaster the entire fight and let the splash damage from the PBAE's kill the trash as it spawned. As long as the tank can maintain agro, nothing hit terribly hard it was just a long fight.
