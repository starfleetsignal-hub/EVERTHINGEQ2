## Background

[[Frup Groaak]] has entrusted you with helping yet another one of his haggered froglok companions that have gotten misplaced after arriving on the shores of [[Antonica]]. You must enter here and route the lizardmen and their human associates.

## Notes

- The door behind you as you zone in is interactable. If clicked on, a message will appear in your chat window stating, "The door here has fallen into disrepair and no longer opens. This place must have been buried long ago."
