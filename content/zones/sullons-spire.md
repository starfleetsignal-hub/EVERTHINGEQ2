## Bosses

### [[Ragebourne Gregor Haldane]]

For the initial pull, Gregor comes with some adds.  The adds are fairly tough and hard-hitting, but they don't respawn once down.

At 65% health, Gregor will split himself in two.  Both Gregor Haldanes will regularly memwipe, the hardest part to this fight is keeping them out of the middle of the raid.

### [[Hragdold the Frenzied]]

Hragdold starts the fight by casting Clawswipe, a major power drain (heavily nerfed as of LU61).  He'll cast it about 4 times in a row, then summon adds.  Of the adds, one is a priest, it must be burned down quickly or it will heal all the enemy mobs in the fight.  After about 2 minutes Hragdold repeats this pattern.  It is imperative to get the adds down before Hragdold starts casting Clawswipe again.

At 35% health Hragdold will stop summoning adds and start using an instant death attack on the main tank called DoomDeath.  This can be blocked with death prevents.

At 10% health Hragdold enrages and begins hitting much harder.

### [[Mrogr Bloodtaint]]

Mrogr summons idols during the fight, the entire raid must joust out of their range and bring Mrogr along.  Mrogr has no adds and is an unusually easy fight.

### [[Aaranae Acrimae]]

Aaranae summons adds.  She has an AOE, the longer the fight goes on the shorter the break in between AOEs is.  If the fight exceeds 7 minutes (possibly nerfed as of LU61) the AOE will become far more powerful and one-shot most of the raid.

### [[Sullon Zek]]

Tank her near the wall where she stands.  Kill her adds immediately or the raid will be one-shotted.  Use [[Raging Fire Crystal]] on the weapon rack when it spawns.
