This zone encapsulates what used to be [[North Qeynos]] and [[South Qeynos]]. In [[Update:64|LU64]], [[Qeynos]] was combined into two zones, Qeynos Capitol District and [[Qeynos Province District]].

## Layout

### Landmarks

Qeynos Capitol District is the administrative, cultural, and residential hub of [[Qeynos]]. [[The Sovereign House of Qeynos|The Sovereign House]], [[Qeynosian Guild Hall]] and entrance to the Citadel of Qeynos are all found here, representing the municipal government. The world-famous [[The Concordium Tower|Concordium Tower]] and [[The Temple of Life|Temple of Life]] stand here, home to magically-inclined elite. Many tradeskill shops also adorn the roads throughout the district, such as [[Clothspinners]], [[The Coldwind Crier]] and of course the renowned [[Ironforge Exchange (POI)|Ironforge Exchange]]. Lastly, the majority of the city's middle-class and affluent citizens make their homes here, in the housing neighborhood of [[South Qeynos (POI)|South Qeynos]]. The former suburbs that existed pre-LU64, such as [[The Baubbleshire]] and [[The Willow Wood]], have been reduced to quest-specific instances. They no longer have player housing and cannot be accessed freely.

### Travel

Qeynos can be traversed by [[Mariner's Bell]], which are found mostly near waterways. For a list of all bell locations see [[Mariner's Bell#Qeynos Bell Locations|this article]].

## Quests

- See the [[The City of Qeynos Timeline]] for quests that scale with your level.
- There are also [[Tradeskill Quests]] for every trade, offered inside [[Ironforge Exchange (POI)|Ironforge Exchange]].
- Those who want to earn [[Status points]] will find NPCs that give [[Writs|adventure writs]] near the Queen's castle.
