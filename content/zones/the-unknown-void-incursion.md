## Notes

- Entrance is in [[The Unknown]] at {{waypoint -761.13, 18.00, 975.04}}
- related weekly mission from [[Dr. Arcana]] [[Take My Xebrith Away]]
- must be level 135 adventurer to enter
- must be level 133 crafter to close portals
- there is a fuel merchant and mender in the zone
- Awards a class tome that can be traded along with 500 Adventurer's Void Shards (PQ completion reward) to the Currency Merchant in Yon Gorroth for a crate containing class celestial spells.

## Walkthrough

Like in [[Invaders of The Unknown]], there are portals which much be deconstructed by level 133 or higher crafters.  However, in this one, portals are specific to the tradeskill class.  Everyone can  still craft to deconstruct them, but it will be slower for crafters whose profession is different from the portal's guardian.

Portal deconstruction requires gazer conduit fluid (harvested from the corpse of the named artisan gazer in front of each portal), severed eyebulb (harvested in zone from eyebulb weed plants), and Xebrith chaospark (harvested in zone from floating chaosparks), plus the fuel appropriate to the class of the portal being closed.

Kill the big gazer with the artisan-specific name in front of each portal and harvest the corpse for *gazer conduit fluid*. Click the portal to begin the decontruction. Once the portal is down the associated named nightblood, lamia, etc. can be killed (see list below for which boss goes with which portal).  After the nine portal bosses are down the final boss can be killed.

#### Important!

- **named eyeballs must die before portals can be closed**
- **named nightblood, lamia, etc. cannot be killed until its portal is closed**
- as much as possible avoid the central area where the final Name stands. Deal with the 5 outer portals first.
