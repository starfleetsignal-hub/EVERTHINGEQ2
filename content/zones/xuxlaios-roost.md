## Access

At least one member of the raid must have had completed [[The Master of the Fluttering Wing]] to enter this instance. To obtain the access quest, you must be able to access to the third floor of the [[Tower of the Four Winds]].

## Named Monsters

It is completely random which named monsters are in the zone, except for [[Xux'laio Master of the Fluttering Wing]]. It also possible that no secondary named monsters will spawn at all. You can repeatedly re-enter the zone until you have an instance with the desired bosses. Use the tracking skill to determine which bosses are present.

- [[The Ancient Twisted Root]]
- [[The Ancient Twisted Fungus]]
- [[The Crusher]]
- [[Xux'laio Master of the Fluttering Wing]]
