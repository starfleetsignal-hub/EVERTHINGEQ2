## Walkthrough

1. Kill the [[Brutal Bloodbath]] in the [[Blood Chamber]] {{waypoint -1114, 70, -359}}. After push kill lying victims, then boss will spawn during the fight adds, which will heal him by 1%*stucks if their HP less then 25%.
1. Break [[A cracked wall]] in the [[Blood Chamber]] {{waypoint -1110, 69, -384}}.
1. Kill [[The Spelobanzeothitem]] in the [[Grim Grotto]] {{waypoint -944, 67, -490}}. After reaching number of stacks, he will summon add. Kill it to reset stacks.
1. Break [[A cracked wall]] in the [[Lost Crossing]] {{waypoint -1031, 66, -487}}.
1. Kill [[Forblarg the Foul]] in the [[Excoriation Excavation]] {{waypoint -1044, 53, -611}}. Kill adds during the fight to reset stacks.
1. Kill [[Morbigog]] in the [[Pit of Horror]] {{waypoint -1025, 51, -611}}. During the fight he will summon crystals with knockback. Crash crystals > kill adds > damage boss > win.
1. Break [[A cracked wall]] in the [[Excoriation Excavation]] {{waypoint -1063, 54, -576}}.
1. Kill the [[Shred of Tashakhi]] in the [[Grimling Ritual Lair]] {{waypoint -1190, 69, -520}}. During the fight he will summon adds (animals come from afar), which u need to kill as fast as you can.
