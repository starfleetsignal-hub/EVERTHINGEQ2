## Notes

- Used for quest updates for the [[Dark Mail Gauntlets Timeline]].
- This zone is first access by speaking with [[Drarson McCale]] when on the appropriate step of the [[Mysterious Mark]] quest.
- After completing the Mysterious Mark quest, this zone is accessed from a barrel behind the Butcherblock Docks at {{waypoint 658, 24, 621}}.
- To leave the Halls of the Unseen, click on the barrel along a wall just west of where you first enter the zone, at {{waypoint 60, 1, -4}}.
