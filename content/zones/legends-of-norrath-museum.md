## Overview

The card game Legends of Norrath got removed from the game, but this instance remained. The LoN Museum is a place that resembles a tier 1 good side guild hall. This is the place that SOE can show off some of the newest*Legends of Norrath* Loot Card items obtained from purchasing card packs, getting one from the free card packs you get every month, or by trading with other players through the LoN client (which can be accessed by typing the command /lon). This museum has paintings and tapestries on the walls, and shows a few of the appearance items you can get for your characters. It includes a mounts section which is located in the back of the room on your right.

A new NPC, [[Collector Rattelis]], and a new quest, [[Back in Fashion]], were introduced with [[LU53]], as well.
