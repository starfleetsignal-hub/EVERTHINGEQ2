## Entrance

![a sink hole](images/A_sink_hole.jpg)

- Located at the sharp turn in [[Dead River Basin]] around {{waypoint 577, -21, 180}}. The entrance moves around this location, so has no *exact* location. Interact with a pile of dirt that highlights with the words **a sink hole**.

## Walkthrough

*If you ever get lost, the exit is at: {{waypoint 957, -24, 1191}}*
Rumblers have always been a nuisance for [[Norrath|Norrathians]]. These stone-snakes have recently burrowed into Dead River Basin and their tunnels bode ill for any unskilled adventurer.

The cave system is packed with numerous encounters of level 23-26 rumblers. Dispose of them at your leisure. There is no objective in this instance other than to cull their numbers. If luck is on you side, [[The Under Rumbler]] will be up at {{waypoint 848, -32, 1068}}.
