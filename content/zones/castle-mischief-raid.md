## Notes

Min and Max length of the instance is indeed 3 days

- Bristlebane
- hp: 39.2 trillon 66.2k mit
- Strat: have to kill adds and do emotes based on det he gives you. adds spawn every 69secs in sets of 3.

- Steps were made with a 4 guys group level 129 / 130.
- Bring [[Totem of the Deadly Sabertooth]] will be very helpfull.

## Steps

1. First named enemy is [[Rougard the Jokster]]. Power drain!
   - Rotate trough, the ads must be held under 50%. Then the named can be done.
   - If one of the ads is dead, it comes back.
   - If one of the ads is to low, every mob will be healed!
1. Second is [[Maxima Kierran]]. Timer based and tricky.
   - Burn down.
   - A blue dome will come from time to time. This will port one or more players in random prison cell. The player has to be freed by another player!
   - No damage possible to named while someone is in prison cell.
   - As long you are in prison cell, there is a timer: The less HP the named has, the shorter the time in prison cell to instant death.
   - A complete overall-timer is started after beginning the fight. The group died completely instantly after about four minutes, so be fast!
1. The next named [[Itty Bitty]] is healing herself very fast.
   - She will fire bombs from time to time.
   - Simple burn down.
1. The access to the next named, [[Linneas the Stitcher]] is tricky. You have to run fast so use here the mentioned totems!
   - Touch the statue in [[Scary-Go-Round]] room. Be careful: The red light area will give you instant death!
   - After touching run out of the room and straight down the corridor. If you are fast enough, you can pass the wall. If not, you will run against the wall. Behind the wall is the [[Stitcher's Workshop]] where the named is in.
   - The named: Simple burn down.
1. Next room is [[Grand Jester's Floor Show]]. The named ones are here two [[Stringed Puppet]]. Simple burn them down.
1. After this the God Bristlebane comes and talk. He will disappear.
1. Take the LEFT corridor! (Right one is a pitfall! Maybe give the [[Achievement|Achievements]] [[Exit Stage Right]] and a [[Lemming]] Achievement. (Must be named correct, was on screen but it was not in log))
1. The endboss is [[Fizzlethorpe Bristlebane]] in [[Throne of Mieschief]].
   - Simple burn down.
