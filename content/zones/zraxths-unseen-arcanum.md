## Zone in

In Moors of Ykesha, near the Guk instances, head to {{waypoint 683, 147, -861}} and walk through the vine wall through the tunnel.

Note: If you take the druid portal to Moors of Ykesha there is a froglok (Zara Dreamhopper) at the Druid Spire, at the landing zone area that will gate you right to the outside of the zone, but you need to know the [[Froak]]  language. This will save you the long run. Once there, walk through the vines on the wall and there is a tunnel.

[[Raza Dreamhopper]] will offer you two quests, but you must speak Froak which you can learn by completing the [[Finicky Frogloks]] quest in [[Fens of Nathsar]].

## Hard Mode or Normal Mode Selection

Once you have zoned in, there is a named froglok, [[Rhee'Gor]], which will select hard mode or normal mode.  If you wish to begin a hard mode encounter, approach him but do not attack him.  He will perform a ritual, which makes the zone much harder.  If you kill this named, the zone will be set to normal difficulty.
