## Entrance

- Accessed via the basement door of the [[Coldwind Point]] lighthouse when completing [[A Brass Key]] quest.

## Notes

- Grouped members who do not hold [[A brass key (Item)]] cannot get in. All group members who have the [[A brass key (Item)|brass key]], and have examined it, will enter the same zone.

## Monsters

On zoning in, the zone will be filled with either Freeportians, Thexians, Broken Skull pirates, or Spicerunner Kerran. Whether a named monster is included among them seems to be random.

- Freeportians: [[A Freeport privateer]], [[A high seas lyricist]], [[A wind rider privateer]], [[A privateer recruit]], [[A Freeport privateer captain]]
- Thexians: [[A Thexian corsair]], [[A Thexian brine contaminator]], [[A Thexian wave crasher]], [[A Thexian corsair recruit]], [[A Thexian commander]], [[Commander N'Tryna]]
- Broken Skulls: [[A Broken Skull buccaneer]], [[A Broken Skull mystic]], [[A Broken Skull deathbringer]], [[A Broken Skull recruit]], [[A Broken Skull commander]], [[Commander Rallosar]]
- Spicerunners: [[A Spicerunner deck hand]], [[A Spicerunner trafficker]], [[A Spicerunner gale bringer]], [[A Spicerunner ember hurler]], [[A Spicerunner recruit]], [[A Spicerunner spicemistress]], [[Boatswain Kiambu]], [[Spicemistress Kejar]]

## Related Quests

- [[Meteor Chunk]] is a rare chest drop off any monster found in this instance.
