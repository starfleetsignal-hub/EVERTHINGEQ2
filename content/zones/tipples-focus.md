## NOTE

![The zone-in to Tipple's Focus](images/An_ornate_metal_lamp.png)

- This is a random djinn lamp spawn throughout [[The Sinking Sands]]. This is a very fast solo instance.
- You can get tea and cookies by reading the note on the table in the second room.
- Use the doorknocker in the second room to port back to Sinking Sands.

## Zoning in at Higher Levels

- Zoning in at higher levels may instead take you to a zone called **A djinn Treasure Cache**. To open the chest, the combination is **22, 35, 29, 10, 42, 23**. This rewards a mighty 3g then zones you back to the [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]] docks.
- The lamp leads to a different zone, [[Shimmering Citadel: Inside the Bottle]] for those working on the Mage Epic 2.0 quest [[Key to Curing the Spellpox]].
