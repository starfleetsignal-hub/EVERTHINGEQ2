## Background

At long last you've arrived at the place where the [[Screaming Mace]] is kept. Soaked in blood and armed with your [[Congealed Blood Sphere]], there is but one final step to take before the mace will be yours!

## Walkthrough

When you zone in you will notice four idols in the center of the room. Examining [[Congealed Blood Sphere]] will highlight the proper idol. Clicking the correct one will spawn [[Gorfuzod the Keeper]], while clicking any other one will spawn four [[A harbinger of Fear|harbingers of Fear]].  Clicking the wrong idols is OK and the order doesn't matter, Once the Keeper is dead, pick up the mace hovering above the table in the back of the room.

## Notes

- This zone is listed under the name "The Inner Sanctum" in the Persistent Instances tab, even though the parent instance is [[The Sanctum of Fear]]. Also, even though there is a persistence for this instance, it cannot be reentered unless you're grouped with another player that has the required quest, [[The Screaming Mace]].
