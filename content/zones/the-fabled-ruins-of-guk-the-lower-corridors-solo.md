## Notes

- Past the first name, almost all mobs have constant knockback. Try to put your back to a wall.
- There are traps that look like root nodes in this dungeon. Disarm them from maximum range or they punt you upwards and often pull additional groups. Note that the disarm is temporary: the traps will reset themselves.
- This is a persistent instance, however if you don't do certain things before you leave you cannot progress when you return. If you get the key and don't use it to go downstairs, you lose it. If you don't click the orb to open the door after the minotaur, the blocks stop moving and you cannot get up there.

## Progression

### [[Krell Vruup (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|Krell Vruup]]

- Before you pull, be sure to kill all trash near and uphill of him, as he will call them on the pull.
- Tank and spank. He hits somewhat hard, but nothing particularly fancy. Burn him down.
- Once dead, the first gate opens.

### [[A Basalt Gargoyle (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|A Basalt Gargoyle]]

- Tank and spank.  Note:  may not a spawn in Guk solo instance

### [[Aruze Vleese (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|Aruze Vleese]]

- This minotaur is located at {{waypoint -104.46, -16.31, -115.35}}.
- He drops a ghoulish key that's needed to open the side gate {{waypoint -125, -22, -85}}.

### [[Hoptor Thagglor (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|Hoptor Thagglor]]

- Use the key on a panel to the right of the gate at {{waypoint -125, -22, -85}}.
- [[Hoptor Thagglor (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|Hoptor]] is at {{waypoint -254, -54, -115}}.
- This mob spawns two animated hand adds, [[The Burner (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|The Burner]] and [[The Crusher (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|The Crusher]]. As long as they are up, he will heal, so burn them down quickly.

### [[Gorscha Gahh (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|Gorscha Gahh]]

- Go to the locked gate at {{waypoint -142.71, -22.17, -126.51}}. Jump on the nearest moving stone then keep jumping on higher moving stones. Jump on to the building . Now jump on the fourth moving stone to jump on the next building. Click the Orb to open the second gate.
  - You might try standing on the big rock at {{waypoint -154.08, -20.43, -116.24}} and jump from there directly to the third tile. It saves you a couple of hops.
- [[Gorscha Gahh (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|Gorscha]] is located at {{waypoint -206, -12, -219}}.

### [[A Reanimated Hand (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|A Reanimated Hand]]

- The [[A Reanimated Hand (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|Reanimated Hand]] is at {{waypoint -56, -34, -270}} where you swim UP the magical pool, kill him and swim back down, and will spawn [[A helping hand (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|helping hands]] adds.

### [[Furdip (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|Furdip]]

- [[Furdip (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|Furdip]] is located at {{waypoint -238, -19, -284}}.
- [[Furdip (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|Furdip]] is invulnerable. Harvest [[A glob of congealed mess!|a glob of congealed mess]] from the blood fountain in the center (click the bubbling areas), and put it on your hotbar. When [[Furdip (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|Furdip]] says "Rest, rest and then your death!" use the congealed mess on him to make him vulnerable for ten seconds.  DPS hard during this time.
- Rinse and repeat the harvest/click/10 seconds of DPS.

### [[King Vroak Vriipp (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|King Vroak Vriipp]]

- Open the door by clicking the totem at {{waypoint -145, -21, -213}} (clickable if all other named have been killed first). You climb up a broken beam to get here.
- The [[King Vroak Vriipp (Fabled Guk Lower Corridors Solo)|King]] is located at {{waypoint -144, -40, -362}}, up some steps at the far side of the shallow pool.
