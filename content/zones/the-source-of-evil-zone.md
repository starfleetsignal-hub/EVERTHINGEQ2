## Background

It has been revealed that source behind the dark fog that corrupted the normally peaceful Sapswill Goblins is none other than a necromancer! Enter this den of respite and slay her!

## Notes

![The entrance to the instance.](images/Adarkdooryway.jpg)

- Kill all attacking mobs, then interact with and destroy the four totems along the wall of the cave to turn [[Vith L'Tar]] into an attackable mob.
