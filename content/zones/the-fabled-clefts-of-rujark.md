This is a higher level version of the original [[The Clefts of Rujark|Clefts of Rujark]] which was first introduced with the Desert of Flames expansion. You can select this instance by clicking on the entrance to the [[Clefts of Rujark]].

The layout of this version of the zone is identical to the original, with three levels. First level is soloable, with the upper two levels containing group content.

## Notes

- You must be adventure level 94 to enter this zone.
- The rewards from named bosses are level 95 legendary and fabled items.
- If you have completed the [[The Lift Key of Rujark]] quest from the original Clefts of Rukjark, you will have access to the elevators to the second and third floors.
- The collectibles in this zone are for the newer Fabled collections, not collections from the Desert of Flames expansion.
