## Description

- A large solo instance, but groups are allowed, consisting of numerous caverns and tunnels containing dozens of multi-mob encounters. You must own the [[Splitpaw Saga]] Adventure Pack.
- The difficulty scales to match your level, between level 20 and 50.  The layout of gnoll classes is random; some combinations are harder than others.

## Entrance

There are four entrances, at various places along the Thundermist Road in Thundering Steppes. The entrances are called terraporters.

- To enter, click on one of the terraporters in the Thundering Steppes and you will receive a darkened shard. Inspect it to receive the quest [[A Darkened Shard]]. You may need to move away from the terraporter and return to complete the "I found the cairn" step of the quest. Click on the terraporter to enter the instance.
- Another version of this zone, called [[Sundered Splitpaw: Captive Audience|Captive Audience]], is triggered if you are hit by [[A Tesch Mal abductor]] (level 20) before you manage to click on the terraporter yourself. ***NOTE: The Captive Audience instance does not contribute toward your Splitpaw access quest.***
