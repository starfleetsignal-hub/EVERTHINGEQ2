[[Rwznak]], [[Nynzok]], [[Omzuk]], [[Iztak]] after killing them, [[Yzlak]] spawns. After killing it, Moosh, Vengeful Spirit (female Ogre)  spawns. Her dialouge:

- [Moosh] says, "The agony!  You are my only hope for redemption, but I am your only hope for victory over Munzok!"
- [Moosh] says to you, "I was tricked into betraying the only one who had ever showed me kindness."
- You say to Moosh, "What is it you propose?"
- [Moosh] says to you, "You think you can survive with what Najena has given you, but I know it's not enough.  That's all I can say right now, I can't stay any longer or it will detect me."
- You say to Moosh, "But you've told us nothing..."
- [Moosh] says to you, "I have no time...we'll meet later."
- You say to Moosh, "Goodbye I guess..."

[[Ozyk]]

- [Moosh] says to you, "In the next fight, the void creature will activate monsters for you to fight.  It's a trick.  Once you kill them, he gains great power.  But if you use this rock right before the creature dies, you will gain and he will get nothing!  He will NEVER figure it out and you will be able to destroy this place with ease!"

[[Umzok]]

There is an auxillary crystal at {{waypoint 272.59, 0.98, 719.11}} , damage it with a right click option, you'll get the narrative message:"The crystal begins to emit a loud hum as it emits acrid fumes.This crystal seems to be some alien energy source."

[[Munzok the Corruptor]] he flees instead of dying, and spawns [[Gozak]].

- [Munzok the Corruptor] says in Words of Shade, "How dare you attack one who you are not worthy to worship!"
- [Munzok the Corruptor] says in Words of Shade, "I have no time for such foolishness, Gozak destroy these insects!"
- [Gozak] says in Words of Shade, "It's been too long since I entertained guests."
- After you defeat him he says: [Gozak] says in Words of Shade, "Even if you defeat one of us, we shall always return."
- "The Earring of Void Channeling drew me in and gave me the power to enter the Void anchor as a part of its own hidden agenda. I crossed swords with Munzok the Corruptor, who I had thought it was leading me toward. I felt a power unlike anything I've ever encountered come from him! Only after surviving the battle with Munzok did I realize that it was not his blood the item desired, but that of Gozak. At that moment, the earring spoke to me! It revealed that Gozak was responsible for great pain and heinous crimes. Upon defeating Gozak, I noticed the earring had changed, as if its own destiny had been fulfilled. Fulfilled even though Munzok still lives! I believe I now have the will to defeat him and close this anchor for good! However, something deep inside tells me that even Munzok is not the final peg that needs to fall into place for this nightmare to be over!"

[[Munzok the Corruptor]] appears again in the next portal.
