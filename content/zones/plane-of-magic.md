## Nameds

Note: all nameds have 30,940 combat mitigation and 880-890 million hit points

## Lore

The esotheric sphere of magic and arcanum knowledge that attracts mages and adventurers alike with its inspiring looks and atmosphere, full of its fascinating creatures and elementals, created by Druzzil Ro The Matron Of Magicks.

### Travel

There are teleportation platforms all over the zone, you need to get there once by traveling to unlock it. Once you found two, the Teleportation network will unlock. Unlocking all seven grants the achievement [[High Planes Drifter]].<br>

## Free gear

In the [[Plane of Magic]] at {{waypoint 1170, 792, -1585}} is [[Thisan's Lockbox]] he sells crates of gear for level 100 characters based on the class. The crates are sold for Oc.

- The gear pieces are slightly better than KA expert heroic and T1 raid gear. (*15 resolve and more health than the expert heroic gear*)
