## Walkthrough

### [[Cewtie Irewater]] (Tier 1)

- Kill 5 [[A unihorn snapper|unihorn snappers]] in Irewater Pond to spawn Cewtie.
- Everyone needs to stay out 15m from the mob, then joust when Cewtie calls *"begins cascading damage"*.
- If you don't get the curse ***Felled Cascade*** cured, the joust distance will increase. Watch his emotes for the distance.
- Every so often Cewtie throws a shiny at the person furthest out. Keep one person out further than the raid and off to the side, because Cewtie will run to the person with the shiny and he can't be allowed to run through the raid.
- At least one person needs to stay more than 20m out from Cewtie. If nobody is more than 20m out, then the entire raid force is hit with a knockback when the shiny spawns.
- Cewtie does not cast Barrage in any form; it is safe to use Bulwark of Order as a debuff.

### [[Cliff Dweller Gadsin]] (Tier 1)

- Kill 5 pairs of [[A dwelling corpse|dwelling corpses]] to spawn Gadsin. He appears after somebody walks in front of the tent.
- Every 60 seconds, Gadsin casts an arcane called **Tactical Cheating**. It lands on everyone within 15 meters and deals increasing damage over time. It cannot be cured by normal priest cures.
- At 95%, 75%, 55%, 35%, and 20%, Gadsin enters a new phase with a specific damage immunity from the list below. The first four phases are chosen randomly (without repeats). The final phase is a repeat of whichever damage immunity was "the most effective" earlier in the fight. Damage immunities can be bypassed with any item that changes damage type. In addition, each phase has a secondary ability, as listed below:
  - Elemental: extra healing based on how many players are in(???)
  - Noxious: higher outgoing damage
  - Physical: ???
  - Magic: ???
- Gadsin does not cast Barrage in any form; it is safe to use [[Bulwark of Order]] as a debuff.

### [[Persepherator]] (Tier 1)

- Kill 11 [[A disembodied skulker|disembodied skulkers]] to spawn the Persepherator.
- Every 45 seconds, a [[Collection of Bone]] will spawn. This add will co-op strike with Persepherator. If two adds are up at the same time, it is an instant wipe.
- At 90%, 65%, 40%, and 15%, Persepherator will cast 4 curses simultaneously. He may also cast a 5th curse 2-3 seconds after, and he may even cast a 6th curse 2-3 seconds after that. The extra curses can be prevented. If even one curse expires naturally, the entire raid force will be stunned for a full minute (effectively causing a wipe).
- Persepherator periodically casts a Barrage which can only be mitigated with [[Bulwark of Order]].
- You have to be within 12 meters of the boss or you will be one shotted.

### [[Cewtie?]] (Tier 2)

- Kill all undead on Shattered Monolith Cay to spawn Cewtie? (yes there is a question mark in her name).
- This is the re-animated version of Cewtie Irewater, the first boss of the zone. All strategies and notes from her applies to this fight, except where noted.
- Cewtie? runs slower than she did in her living form, so even the main tank can joust when she begins her cascading damage.
- Every 10% starting at 90% and ending at 20%, [[A disturbed skeleton]] will spawn in the western half of the island and run towards the main tank. This add will co-op strike with Cewtie?. Starting at 50%, two skeletons will spawn at once.
- Starting at 50%, small fog clouds with skeletal hands will appear on the raid force. Standing in these clouds reduces movement speed and deals significant damage.

### The Legion Lords (Tier 2)

*Spawning the Legion Lords is a complex task requiring several steps*

1. Collect 4 [[Zemii Idols]] from the battlefields of the first four bosses.
   - [[Cewtie Irewater]] {{waypoint 542.92, 96.35, 165.64}} -- in a doorway slightly north of [[Irewater Pond]]. The doorway is the same used to access [[Ghoulinda's Lair]] in the overland zone.
   - [[Cliff Dweller Gadsin]] {{waypoint 647.06, 49.84, -445.83}} -- in a doorway in the northwest corner of [[Thornborne Wood]]. The doorway is the same used to access the solo and heroic dungeons in the overland zone.
   - [[Persepherator]] {{waypoint 79.10, 33.35, -314.01}} -- in a tent near where he spawned.
   - [[Cewtie?]] {{waypoint -301.70, 38.62, -753.03}} -- against the stone pillar where she spawned.
1. Travel to [[Accursed Chantry Island]] and kill all the coven members on the island. This opens the gates to the Chantry.
1. Kill the five linked mini-bosses within the chantry: [[Gorefus]], [[Memnotum]], [[Psyclis]], [[Vermidicus]], and [[A Cloaked Necromancer]]. This opens the Zemii Altars.
1. Place a [[Zemii Idol]] on each of the four **Zemii Altars**. This reveals a travel bell on the south end of the island, which takes you to the graveyard containing the reformed Ritual Lords. (A second travel bell appears at the raid entrance, which also takes you to the graveyard.)
1. Entering the graveyard starts a 3-wave ring-event of undead. Kill all undead to start the next wave. After the third wave is dead, the Ritual Lords will spawn at the north, south, east, and west entrances to the graveyard, and the [[Essence of Coagulation]] will spawn in the center.
1. Each of The Ritual Lords has unique abilities. Because of these unique abilities, it's important to tank them separately and kill them in a certain order as follows:
   1. [[Gorefus, Legion of Decay]]
      - Gorefus uses Berserker abilities.
      - Gorefus powers up if standing near [[Vermidicus, the Scroungling|Vermidicus]].
      - Gorefus has a powerful frontal attack.
      - Gorefus periodically casts a Barrage which can only be mitigated with [[Bulwark of Order]].
   1. [[Vermidicus, the Scroungling]]
      - Vermidicus uses Shadowknight abilities.
      - Vermidicus powers up if standing near [[Gorefus, Legion of Decay|Gorefus]].
      - Vermidicus casts a 35-meter incurable stifle.
      - Vermidicus casts a curable stun(?) called **Burn Gaze**. This spell has a long cast bar and is interruptible.
   1. [[Psyclis, the Undying Storm]]
      - Psyclis uses mage(?) abilities.
      - Psyclis casts a 35-meter incurable fear.
      - Psyclis casts a power drain called **Power Bleed I** which can be mitigated with [[Raid Rune: Mana Bubble I]] or stronger.
   1. [[Memnotum, the Everlasting Protector]]
      - Memnotum uses Guardian abilities.
      - Memnotum taunts his target onto himself, possibly pulling the main tank off of the other bosses.
      - Memnotum periodically casts a Barrage which can only be mitigated with [[Bulwark of Order]].
   1. [[Essence of Coagulation]]
      - The other bosses are the re-animated (and possibly cursed) form of the coven members found in [[The Accursed Chantry]].
      - The Essence casts a high-damage curse on those that stand too close to it. This curse stops once the four Ritual Lords are dead.
