- The objective is to kill the two named thexian leaders. Afterwards, speak with [[Kanos X'aphon]] who spawns near the front door only after the instance is cleared.
- The actual instance name is: 'the Thexian Meeting', with an uncapitalized 'the'. Most likely a typo.
