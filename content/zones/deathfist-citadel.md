## Background

Deathfist Citadel is the main fortress of the Deathfist Empire, and seat of it's power under the command of [[Emperor Fyst]]. From it's location in northern [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]], the Deathfist Citdael strikes fear into any fool-hardy adventurer that sets their gaze upon it.

## Geography

### Entrances

- Killing [[Vigo Cerebus]] at the front gate of [[The Deathfist Citadel]] in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]] {{waypoint 46, -39, -604}} unlocks the main door of the citadel at: {{waypoint 44, -28, -660}}
- Sewer grates in the moat around the citadel at: {{waypoint 43, -42, -781}} or {{waypoint 80, -43, -753}}

### Exits

- Main door: {{waypoint 0, 0, 53}}
- Sewer grates: {{waypoint 26, -9, -44}} or {{waypoint 43, -9, -28}}

## Notes

- This zone was completely revamped with [[LU33]], changing from a Raid x2 zone to a Heroic zone. It also introduced some new mastercrafter armor sets ([[Deathfist Armor]]). The recipes for these armor sets are split between Deathfist Common Recipes (for head, hands, and feet) and Deathfist Rare Recipes (for chest, legs, and shoulders). All recipes require [[Blood Iron Ore]], which drops in the instance as well as neighboring [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes|Zek]].
- If you enter by the front gate, from the moment you walk in you will be bombarded by [[A Deathfist archer|Deathfist archers]]. Alarms will sound and [[A Deathfist defender (Deathfist Citadel)|Deathfist defenders]] will appear in a nearly constant stream. Additionally, as soon as you reach the battlements you can expect one or more named to come to you! Even if the zone is gray to you, the constant attacks will kill you pretty fast. The only sane approach may be the sewers.
- All evac spells are disabled while in this zone!
- [[Deathfist Citadel: Onslaught]] uses Deathfist Citadel as it's parent zone and is not entered from within Deathfist Citadel.
