## Walkthrough

1. Slay [[Greeneyes]] {{waypoint -405, 132, -548}}.
   - Kill the [[A grizzly gloombeak|grizzly gloombeaks]] adds to debuff Greeneyes, then kill him.
1. Slay [[Hopper the Blood-Drenched]]
   1. Go to the [[Saurek Watering Hole]] {{waypoint -429.34, 119.02, -386.44}} and kill the large group of [[A saurek bloodtrackers|saurek bloodtrackers]].
   1. Pull hopper into the water, then kill [[An aqualing|aqualings]] on top of him to remove his detriments. he gains a buff that levels up as you fight him - the more "blood drenched" he gets. it stacks from 1-100 and is a percent of the strength of his buff which makes him hit harder, and also makes him harder to hit. pulling and killing an aqualing, that you can find, and that respawn, around the pond, clears this blood off and lowers the strength of his buff
1. Slay [[The Poached Poacher]] {{waypoint -350.23, 105.43, 70.52}}.
   - *Before you pull the Poacher, make sure you have [[A beast skull]] in your bags. These drop from other beasts in the zone.*
   1. Aggro [[A whisperling|whisperlings]] and drag them to [[A desecrated beast|desecrated beasts]]. When the beasts give a blue message about looking skyward for whisperlings, kill the whisperling. Repeat until the Poacher spawns.
   1. Fight [[The Poached Poacher]] on top of the new non-aggro beasts you spawned.
      - *At 49% the Poacher becomes invulnerable. Click on the non-aggro beast to offer it a **beast skull** from the earlier mobs and make him killable.
1. Follow the ants to the ant hill {{waypoint -359.98, 103.64, 219.22}} and click the hill to enter.
1. Slay [[Thraxinae]] {{waypoint -177, -400, -401}} on the ceiling of the room.
   - *At times, disappears and spawns adds. Kill the adds, kill the named. Rinse, repeat.*
1. Travel to {{waypoint -525, -328, -329}} and kill [[Vencine Kovari|Vincenz Kovari and Vencine Kovari]].
   - the vampires will periodically take on beastial forms and one will go invulnerable at various times. If both are able to take on this form, you will die. Orbs will spawn around the area. One of the m will be linked to whichever vampire is changing form. Destroy the orb. if your gfx settings are high enough you can identify this one via a red tether from it to the boss.  Kill the vampiric orbs around the room to remove invulnerability, kill the named. Do not cure the detriment before the named changes back to original form. Repeat for other named.
