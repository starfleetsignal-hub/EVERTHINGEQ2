## Background

You have earned the trust and respect of [[Sir Trodonis]] and The Lucanic Knights. He has informed you that [[Lucan D'Lere (The Throne of New Tunaria: Operation Overlord)|Overload Lucan D'Lere]] seeks your audience personally from within the [[Throne of New Tunaria]].

## Notes

- This is a special quest instance of [[Throne of New Tunaria]] and is only used for the quest [[A Freeport Overlord in Queen Lenya's Court]], which is the final quest in the [[Swords of Destiny Timeline]].
- There are no monsters that can't be fought in this instance.
- You can earn some fast experience if you hit all the discovery points in this instance. They count separately from the normal instance POIs.
