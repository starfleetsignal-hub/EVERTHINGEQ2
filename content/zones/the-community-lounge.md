![The Lounge as viewed from the zone in point](images/The_Community_Lounge.jpg)
This zone is used by the [[Guides of the Shattered Lands]] for special server-wide events around holidays like [[Frostfell]]. These events are scheduled and announced in advance on official guide forums and Discord channel.

Access to the zone is only via special portal placed by one of the guides somewhere in Norrath, Sinking Sands dock has been the most used location.

The instance allows no fighting to occur and offers a place for players to relax and enjoy the architecture, a few libations, and the companionship of other players.

Players are encouraged to mingle and explore, use merchants and amenities which include many interactive bottles (that will simply make you drunk or give you special effect) and pies in bar area, throwing pillows for pillow fights, piano that summons various dancers. Merchants sell a variety of alcoholic drinks.  You can also buy temporary appearance clothing, hats and circlets. Wishing pool in the middle allows fishing, although results are not fish, but various temporary foods and an occasional [[A coffee cup]] (cup is the only item that will stay with you when you log out of the game).

A doorkeeper named [[Scheherazade]] stands at the entrance of the instance. When you want to leave, she offers transportation to [[Kelethin]], [[South Qeynos]], [[Dreg's Landing]], [[The Thundering Steppes|Thundering Steppes dock]], [[Drednever Crash Site]], and [[The Sinking Sands|Sinking Sands]].
