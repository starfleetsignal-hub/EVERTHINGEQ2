## Background

The Smarmy Sprocket is a fast-paced gnome pirate ship that has been around since before the [[The Age of Cataclysms (Lore)|Age of Cataclysms]].* - (Source: [[Blendrap the Engineer]])* Board the ship and aid these wily gnomes in the rescue of their captain!
