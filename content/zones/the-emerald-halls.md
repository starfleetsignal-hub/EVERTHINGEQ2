## Background

Before the [[Gods]] departed [[Norrath]], the Goddess of Nature, [[Tunare (Deity)|Tunare]], in an act of mercy for her [[Dal|creations]], hid the *Bloom of Growth*, a link between Norrath and the [[Plane of Growth]], deep within [[The Lesser Faydark]] and commanded the dragon [[Wuoshi]] to guard it until she returned. For centuries, the *Bloom of Growth* has sat the head of [[The Loa'Sur]], feeding the life-giving powers of the river and transforming the Lesser Faydark into what it is today. Wuoshi has continued to feed off of the Bloom's power, growing ever-stronger. Should you be as bold as to attempt to reach the Bloom, you will first have to defeat it's guardian.

The Emerald Halls became the first ever [[Persistent Instance]] with [[LU37]].

## Notes

- To progress through the raid, kill all the named mobs in each section and the barrier blocking access to the next section will despawn.
- When having all four archetypes present (fighter, mage, priest, scout), you can skip some of the numerous trash mobs when beating the challenge of the NPC, [[Askadian Adakus]]. He will give this challenge to a random archetype on each floor. Not having a player of this archetype present, or failing to do the challenge, will result in the zone being more costly in terms of time.
- Names reward AA and are all soloable with mercenaries.
- [[Seed of Growth]] drops from trash mobs are still useful for making [[Scroll of Fire Seeds]].
