## NOTE

![The zone-in to in Shimmering Citadel: Inside the Bottle/Tipple's Focus](images/An_ornate_metal_lamp.png)

- This is a random djinn lamp spawn throughout [[The Sinking Sands]].
- Unless you're on the Mage Epic 2.0 quest [[Key to Curing the Spellpox]], the lamp leads to a different zone, [[Tipple's Focus]].  Do NOT be grouped.
- There is no exit from the zone. To leave, you will have to use [[Call to Guild Hall]] or some other type of port.

## Known Locations

*[[Key to Curing the Spellpox|Supposedly]], there are fifteen possible spawn locations.*
*The spawn does seem to rotate on its own, so you can camp a specific location.  I've done this twice and waited no more than an hour each time.*

***Near the [[The Bay of Anuk|Bay of Anuk]]***

- On an island in the middle of the [[The Bay of Anuk|Bay of Anuk]] {{waypoint -772.33, -223.94, -1355.79}}.
- On an island in the [[The Bay of Anuk|Bay of Anuk]] almost wedged between the two rocks {{waypoint -846.19, -223.91, -1380.68}}.
- On a small rock island on the east side of [[The Bay of Anuk|Bay of Anuk]] {{waypoint -970.77, -219.01, -1446.46}}.
- On the other side of a small rock island on the east side of [[The Bay of Anuk|Bay of Anuk]] {{waypoint -980.85, -233.26, -1447.41}}.
- On a different small island east of the [[The Bay of Anuk|Bay of Anuk]] {{waypoint -1252.83, -223.30, -1197.07}}.
- On the other side of a different small island east of the [[The Bay of Anuk|Bay of Anuk]] {{waypoint -1248.79, -232.03, -1211.71}}.
- On another small rock island near the [[The Bay of Anuk|Bay of Anuk]] {{waypoint -1388.48, -222.55, -1240.22}}
- On yet another small rock island east of the [[The Bay of Anuk|Bay of Anuk]] (just to the west of the climbable point to the top of the cliffs) {{waypoint -1346.00, -228.00, -1204.00}}.
- On another small rock island east of the [[The Bay of Anuk|Bay of Anuk]] {{waypoint -787.39, -230.65, -1284.74}}.
- Inside the inlet to the northern most end of the map, southern most rock, just below the other two locations, lower of two rocks closest to the wall, has 4 level 53 mobs that spawn [[The Bay of Anuk|Bay of Anuk]] {{waypoint -785.58, -231.08, -1284.98}}.

***Near [[Onerock Isle]]***

- Across the water from the [[Onerock Isle]] carpet station, sitting atop of a large rock {{waypoint -1834, -212, 621}}.
- On a small rock island off the north end of [[Onerock Isle]] {{waypoint -2109.33, -232.85, 386.56}}.
