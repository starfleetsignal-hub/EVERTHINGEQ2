## Lore

Made up of North Dshinn, Kithicor Island and Grim Shales.

## Geography

### Travel

Phantom Sea can be accessed by

- zoning to Tranquil Sea and using the bell at the dock
- flying north from Tranquil Sea until you zone
- using the [[Anchor of Wanderer's Dock]]
- using [[Return to High Hold]] tradeskill merchants sell them for 6g (need to be at least level 20 Tradeskiller to use them)

Sold by:

- [[Koros Splinterlimb]] - in [[The Village of Shin]] {{waypoint -93, -2, 10}}
- [[Shazra]] - in [[Sapswill's Rest]] in [[Tranquil Sea]] {{waypoint 1229, 19, 1261}}
