## Background

The Court of the Coin is in the midst of political turmoil between its current leader, [[Neriph, Caliph of the Coin]] and [[Maimun Kazuhla]]. You have been played by both sides and ultimately have decided that Neriph should remain in power. Enter here and put a stop to Maimuns plot to take power.

## Notes

- Make sure to loot [[Neriph's piece of a broken medallion]] off the corpse of the Master Assassin, as it is required for the quest [[The Courts of Maj'Dul: Of Fate and Destiny]].
- One person in the group must have completed [[Court of the Coin: Imminent Danger]] in order to be able to zone the entire group in. Negative faction with the Court of the Coin does not prohibit entrance for other group members, and once you have gained access by completing the court timeline, you can enter with any amount of negative or positive court faction.
- [[Neriph, Caliph of the Coin]] can be damaged by your area effect spells and abilities so watch where you position yourself.

## Walkthrough

1. Defend off the waves of [[A hired assassin (Court of the Coin: Imminent Danger)|hired assassins]] to prevent them from killing [[Neriph, Caliph of the Coin (Named)|Neriph, Caliph of the Coin]]. If Neriph dies you will fail the instance and need to wait at least 90 minutes to reset it.
