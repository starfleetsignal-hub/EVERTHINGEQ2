A short post pointing to a scan of the original 2004 Prima Official eGuide for EverQuest II on the Internet Archive (https://archive.org/details/EverQuest_II_Prima_Official_eGuide). It was shared for players on the Fallen Gate time-locked server.

Replies note that the book is a fun look back but describes the game as it was at launch, so it's of limited use on modern or progression servers.
