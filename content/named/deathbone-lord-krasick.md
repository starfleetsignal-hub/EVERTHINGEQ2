## Strategy

Lord Krasick will spawn as soon as you move out of the center.

At every 10% of Krasik's health, he will become unattackable and will spawn a portal. Everyone needs to keep the tank between them and the portal to make it easier for the tank to grab the adds. Skeletal fearstalkers ([[A defiled prowler]] and [[A plagueborn prowler]]) emerge from the portals, with more coming in each wave. Avoid the portals themselves, if you are standing on them they do about 5K focus damage per tick.

There is an AOE arcane fear (Terrible Gaze) which is curable and of short duration. There is also a close range noxious AOE.
