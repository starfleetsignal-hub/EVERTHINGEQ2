## Notes

- Spawns when you're on the quest [[The Hand of Glory|Hand of Glory]] and move five fishes to the basket in front of where he spawns. Game messages "A deep sounding tone echoes along the coast as the smell of rotten flesh drifts in the air" and "The Fathomless Horror voraciously devours the dead fish" will warn of his arrival.

## Rewards

- 300 XP at level 65.
