Ability and strategy information changes between stages (1-20/21-40/41+).

## Abilities

- **Noxious Resistance**
- A buff that reduces noxious damage done to caster by 90%.
- **Caustic Glands**
- A buff that has a 10% chance to cast Corrosive Saliva on a melee hit. Corrosive Saliva inflicts increasing disease damage over time, decreases power over time, and reduces healing received.
- **Sacrificial Lamb**
- An incurable elemental that inflicts heat damage over time and decreases power over time. It also procs Defensive Breach which deals significant magic damage.
- **Parasitic Bond**
- An incurable arcane that reduces fervor by 20%, increases noxious damage done to target by 25%, and slows target by 100%. The Parasitic Bond transfers to the next closest target from [[A digestive sucker]]. The target dies if a second bond is applied. If everyone is bonded, everyone dies.
- **Blighted Bite [21+]**
- An incurable noxious that inflicts piercing damage and inflicts disease damage over time. On termination, if the target has less health than when the spell was applied, the target is cursed with Blighted Wounds, which reduces max health by 5% per increment.
- **Hemorrhagic Mana [21+]**
- An incurable trauma that inflicts slashing damage over time, converts the target's ability cost from power to health, and fears the target if health is reduced below 50%.
- **Pathogen Vectors [21+]**
- A buff that summons 10 [[Swarm of blightflies|blightflies]], increasing combat mitigation, ability doublecast avoidance, and flurry avoidance per active swarm. It also increases flurry, flurry multiplier, and melee multiplier over time.
