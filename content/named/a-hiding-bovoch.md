## Strategy

This is a Beastlord Warder (offensive), and exotic-enchanted. It has very few hit points, making it very difficult to get to 25% health in order to tame. One effective strategy was to bring a healer, un-equip weapons and punch it down while healing whomever has agro. The low dps of a shaman pet may be helpful too.
