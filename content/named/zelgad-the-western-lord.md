## Factions

- -250 faction with **The Order of Rime**

## Notes

This Named will kill you on sight if you do not have -39999 or higher faction with The Order of Rime, he will also kill [[A ghostly hunter|a ghostly hunter]], [[A risen brittlebone|risen brittlebones]] and [[A fisher hand|fisher hands]] on sight (basically any potentially aggressive npc to yourself; useful as a guard with high faction through Order of Rime illusion mask or normal).

## Strategy

Tank and spank. no knock back, moderate ae. We tanked him by the tree. As high HP.
