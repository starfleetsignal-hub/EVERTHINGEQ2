## Notes

Lead Sapper Kar'Lob is a Rogue of some kind, and does high damage output for his level.

In solo version of the instance:

- Rare (roughly 1 in 4 chance per visit)
- Has no arrows, but comes with 1-2 friends who are down-arrow.

In heroic version of the instance:

- Common (roughly 2 in 3 chance per visit)
- Is ^^^ heroic, and comes with 3 friends who have no arrows.

Guaranteed drop of a loot chest.
