## Strategy

![A blood-stained shackle](images/A_blood-stained_shackle.png)
![Maiden's Scream](images/Maiden's_scream.png)
![Vile Essence](images/Vile_essence.png)
![Blood Sap comes from an arrow outside the Keep](images/An_arrow_through_the_heart.png)

- Rinis is on the first floor in the alchemist chamber, [[Lich's Brew]], SE tower {{waypoint -11, 8, 27}}
- To spawn Rinis, you will need to collect several ingredients.
  - **Blood Sap** (the arrow piercing the orc on tree outside the Keep) {{waypoint 139, -12, 138}}
  - **Deathrot Oil** {{waypoint -8, 1, 22}} (in the Depleted Armory, below the Lichs Brew)
  - **A blood-stained shackle** (by the cauldron) {{waypoint -11, 8, 27}}.
  - **Maiden's Scream** (across from cauldron) {{waypoint -16, 9, 41}}
  - **Vile Essence** (SW tower, top floor) in [[Old Deathfist Stockpile]] {{waypoint 19, 17, 28}}
    - Needed for the fight; she gets an invincibility that can hit through with it, however it heals her if you pop it before the invincibility pops. Make sure you save it until then.
- If you are on the signature quest [[The Siege of Zek: Sacrifice (Solo)]], you can kill Rinis and [[Yallessulich (Advanced Solo)|Yallessulich]] in the same run, although you won't get achievement or chest for the Yallessulich on the quest kill (you can come back later and kill him for credit).
