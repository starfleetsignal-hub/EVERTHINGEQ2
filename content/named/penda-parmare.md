### Curse of the Plagueborn

Effects:

- Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 45.0% instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Inflicts 45.0% of max health in poison damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Decreases Potency of targets in Area of Effect by 1000.0.
- Reduces Melee Multiplier of targets in Area of Effect by 1.50.
- Slows targets in Area of Effect by 167.5%.
- If you die while this curse is on you, it will spread to those closest to you.

### Garb of Duality

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 9% chance to cast Harmonious Energy on target's attacker. Lasts for 5.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 1,188 - 2,207 magic damage on target instantly and every 5 seconds.
  - Decreases power of target by 9.0% instantly and every 5 seconds.
  - Decreases Casting Speed of target by 50.0%.
  - Decreases Crit Chance of target by 45.0.
  - Reduces Melee Multiplier of target by 0.30.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means
