## Strategy

Start the fight standing directly under him and zoom out your camera so you can see the floor around him. A red highlight will light up the floor periodically where he is going to swing his mace. Move out of the red area A.S.A.P. or you will die.

- The trick is to run under his belly and stay there until he has almost no health.
- If the above belly trick does not work (failed for me 4 times)

stay under his left foot. You will take damage but not from the mace
and will win
