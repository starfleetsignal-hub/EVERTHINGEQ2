## Strategy

Agna is not one of Fortress Spires harder bosses, but the real difficultly with this fight is executing it without pulling the X4 epics below, or falling off!

Everyone must stay within melee range of her during this fight or be hit with a hard AOE. Based on a timer she will port around the platform multiple times, its critical that people run to where she ends up quickly as if you are caught outside of melee range you risk dying. Just before her final port she can be attacked, keep her targeted and turn on a ranged auto-attack. When she takes damage you know her next location will be final.
