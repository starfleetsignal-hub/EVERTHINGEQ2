## Lore

[[Mithaniel Marr (God)|Mithaniel Marr]] is the [[Deity|god]] or valor, and embodies the concepts of honor, courage, nobility, and loyalty. He is the twin brother of [[Erollisi Marr]], goddess of love.

## Strategy

- After you get Mithaniel Marr down to a little less than 50% of his health, he will turn into an unattackable NPC, drop [[A Lovely Treasure Trove]] chest and slowly walk towards his sisters' coffin. After a lengthy monologue he will shatter the coffin, update your quest (if you're on it) and earn you the achievement "[[Love's Avenger]]."
- If you are looking for specific item drops, be sure the instance level is at least the level of the desired drop (e.g., level 80 for Valorous Wings).
