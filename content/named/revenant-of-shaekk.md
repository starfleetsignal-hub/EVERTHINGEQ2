The Revenant of Shaekk is a Tier 4 raid boss. This fight is nearly identical to [[Grand Shaman Shaekk]].

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance: 575.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance 645.0
- Resolve: 11,365.0

## Abilities

- **Marrow Shroud**
- A buff that causes Shaekk to reflect damage from sources that are 10 meters or more away (5% chance), and cast Marrow Sap when damaged by sources that are less than 10 meters away (5% chance).
- **Swarm Mentality**
- A buff that decreases damage from all sources for each escaped creature in the fight.
- **Swarmlord**
- A buff that decreases damage received and increases outgoing physical damage for each increment. The spell increments when Shaekk changes targets and maintains the new target for at least 5 seconds. At 20 increments, or when the number of increments equals the number of players in raid, Shaekk instantly kills the entire raid.
- **Teamwork**: Leader
- A buff that casts Tag Team Strike on the current target, if that target already has the effect from Teamwork: Member.
- **Bolstered Spellfrenzy**
- A buff that increases outgoing damage and reduces damage taken from mages and priests.
- **Bolstered Frenzy**
- A buff that increases outgoing damage and reduces damage taken from fighters and scouts.
- **Entrancing Ether**
- A curable curse that kills the entire raid when it expires. When cured, it immediately recasts on a different player. Curing this spell does not reset Cure Curse.
- **Necrotic Touch II**
- A curable curse that debuffs offensive, defensive, and level while dealing disease damage to the target and healing Shaekk for each non-fighter target. The curse applies to up to 7 targets within 12 meters of Shaekk. Curing this curse does NOT reset the Cure Curse ability.
- **Pustule Presence**
- A curable noxious that deals growing disease damage to the target. When removed, it triggers Pustule Pop on all allies within 25 meters, which deals much faster disease damage and slows movement speed.
- **Ethereal Clawstrike**
- A curable detriment that deals piercing damage over time.
- **Poisonous Scratches**
- A curable detriment that deals fast-growing poison damage over time.
- **Lifeleecher**
- A curable noxious that deals disease damage over time. After 5 ticks of damage, it also heals Shaekk.
- **Gangrenous Infections**
- A curable detriment that deals fast-growing disease damage over time.
- **Piercing Wail**
- A curable trauma that deals increasing piercing damage the closer the target is. It also stuns the target.

## Strategy

Shaekk continuously drops black circles on the ground. Standing too close to one summons an add. Standing within the circle for too long kills the player. The summoned adds and the trash in the zone all have the Teamwork: Member buff.

Shaekk alternates between Bolstered Spellfrenzy and Bolstered Frenzy throughout the fight. These two buffs cannot be removed.
