Guard Imobiun is a Tier 3 raid boss.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance: 525.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance: 590.0
- Resolve: 11,195.0

## Abilities

- **Mental Vise I**
- A curable arcane that deals fast-growing damage over time.
- **Gaurded Misdirection [SIC]**
- A curable arcane that deals increasing arcane damage and increases physical damage from all sources.
- **Rimestrike**
- A curable elemental that deals fast ticking increasing elemental damage and increases physical damage from all sources.
- **Frozen Giantspike**
- A curable trauma that inflicts piercing damage over time.
- **Crushing Gasps**
- A curable trauma that deals fast ticking physical damage and increases arcane, elemental, and noxious damage from all sources.
- **Viral Freeze**
- A curable noxious that deals fast ticking poison damage and increases physical damage from all sources.
- **Stalwart Admonishment**
- A curable elemental that deals elemental damage and increases all other damage types. This effect only lands on fighters.
- **Teamwork**: Leader
- A buff that casts Tag Team Strike on the current target, if that target already has the effect from Teamwork: Member.
- **Doomed Familiarity**
- A curse that deals increasing damage over time. It is incurable while the target is within 35 meters of Imobiun. Curing this detriment instantly resets the caster's Cure Curse.
- **Barrage**
- An attack that can only be countered with [[Bulwark of Order]].

## Strategy

Imobiun continuously summons [[A Kromzek gigantfang|Kromzek gigantfangs]] which have the Teamwork: Member buff.
