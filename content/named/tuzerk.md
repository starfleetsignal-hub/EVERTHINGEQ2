## Buffs

### Confidence Building

Effects:

- When Tuzerk and his group have killed three people, a cowardly healer will join the fray and heal them.

### Greater Pirate Onslaught

Effects:

- Greater Pirate Onslaught grants attack power to wearer.

### Vein Tapper

Effects:

- Casts Tap Veins which hits all targets within a base radius of 35 meters and heals Tuzrek for 2% of his maximum health per player hit.
