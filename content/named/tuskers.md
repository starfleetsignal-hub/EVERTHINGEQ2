## Strategy

Linked with [[Haladan Moonrunner]] and will take damage once you bring Haladan's health down and he emotes that Tuskers' shield is down. He has a pretty nasty kickback so it's best to tank with back to the wall.

You dont have to actually kill them. Instead bring them both down to under 5% and they die
