## Strategy

- Strategy: Stay out of the blue circles he randomly spawns (3 spawn) they will kill you rest is dps it down as fast as possible. The entire fight is random not set spawn for the blue circles getting flawless is done by sheer rng or huge dps to kill it fast. Best spot I think to fight is on the bridge that way jousting is easier.

- (Rumor is standing in middle of the blue circle and clicking the staff removes the circle but not 100% sure if true or not)

- Note: After this fight go to bottom of elevator for first T4 fight the firewall isn't the next path that is after him to reach sol ro.
