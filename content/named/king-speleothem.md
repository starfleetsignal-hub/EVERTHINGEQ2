## Strategy

- Part of a small ring event.  First, kill the active [[A Stonebrunt stalagmaw|Stonebrunt stalagmaw]].  This will activate several other Stonebrunt stalagmaws.  Clear them, and four [[A Stonebrunt crusher|Stonebrunt crushers]] spawn. Clear them, and King Speleothem activates.
- Easily soloable with a merc
