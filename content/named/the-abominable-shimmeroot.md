## Strategy

Becomes invulnerable when the smaller treants root themselves, must be moving constantly or become a funny spectacle for the onlookers.

I've found that if you don't move (and therefore don't pull all the roamers in the area) it just takes a little longer to defeat him.  11:03, December 15, 2013 (UTC)
