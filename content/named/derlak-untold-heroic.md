Ability and strategy information changes between stages (1-20/21-40/41+).

## Abilities

- **Sacrificial Lamb**
- An incurable arcane that deals mental damage over time and decreases power over time. It also procs Defensive Breach which deals significant magic damage.
- **Bone Cleave**
- A curable trauma that inflicts increasing slashing damage over time and disarms Primary and Ranged slots.
- **Septic Ward**
- A buff that reduces all damage done to caster by 50%.
- **Steleslag Link**
- A buff that links Derlak to a particular type of steleslag. It can be dispelled, which forces Derlak to link to the closest type of steleslag.
- **Steleslag Singularity**
- A buff that forces steleslags to flow toward Derlak instead of their closest target.
- **Circle of Pain**
- An incurable trauma that wounds target's health by 10% per increment.
- **Betrayer's Bane**
- A curable curse that lands on all players at once. It inflicts disease damage over time, makes the targets immune to Betrayer's Bite, and allows the targets to bypass Septic Ward.
- **Betrayer's Flame**
- A curable curse that lands on all players at once. It inflicts heat damage over time, reduces crit bonus, and makes targets immune to Betrayer's Chill.

## Strategy

Derlak summons five types of steleslags on the outer square daises. While linked to a steleslag, only that type spawns and flows toward the battle. Each steleslag type has its own detriment and its own buff. If a player has 20 increments of the same detriment, or if the player has all 5 detriments simultaneously, the player is instantly killed. If Derlak has 20 increments of the same buff, the entire group is killed.

- Molten
  - Applies Molten Blood, a buff that has a chance to cast Betrayer's Flame when Derlak receives a hostile ability. It also increases outgoing heat damage and increases flurry, multiplied by increments.
  - Applies Steleslag Burnthrough, an incurable elemental that deals heat damage multiplied by increments.
- Prismatic
  - Applies Seteleslag Voidburst, an incurable arcane that deals magic damage and increases incoming arcane damage, multiplied by increments.
- Decaying
  - Applies Blightforged Steel, a buff that has a chance to cast Betrayer's Bane when Derlak casts a hostile ability. It also increases outgoing disease damage, increases combat mitigation, and increases dodge, multiplied by increments.
  - Applies Steleslag Rotgut, an incurable noxious that inflicts disease damage and increases incoming disease damage, multiplied by increments. It also allows the target bypass Septic Ward.
- Frigid
  - Applies Steleslag Frostbite, an incurable elemental that deals cold damage and increases incoming cold damage, multiplied by increments.
