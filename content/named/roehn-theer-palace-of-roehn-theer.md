## Buffs

### Protection of Theer

Effects:

- Reduces physical, elemental, noxious, arcane, heat, cold, magic, mental, divine, disease, poison, slash crush and pierce damage done to caster by 100%.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

## Special Attacks

### Awe

Effects:

- Inflicts 35,026 - 42,809 magic damage on target instantly and every second.
- Inflicts an additional 3,891 - 4,756 magic damage on target every tick.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Stifles target
- Dazes target

### Balance

Effects:

- Reduces physical, elemental, noxious, arcane, heat, cold, magic, mental, divine, poison, slash, crush and pierce damage done to target by 40%.
- Increases STR of target by 100.0.
- Increases Crit Bonus of target by 5.0.
- Increases Crit Chance of target by 5.0.
- When any damage is received this spell has a 5% chance to cast Backlash on target's attacker.
  - Inflicts 13,227 - 16, 166 heat damage on target.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Cone of Blades

Effects:

- Inflicts 66,548 - 81,337 slashing damage on caster.
  - Any distance up to 20 away with an arc of 90 degrees turned at 0 degrees from caster.
- Inflicts 66,548 - 81,337 slashing damage on caster.
  - Any distance up to 20 away with an arc of 90 degrees turned at 90 degrees from caster.
- Inflicts 33,802 - 50,703 slashing damage on caster.
  - Any distance up to 20 away and a rear arc of 90 degrees
- Inflicts 104,576 - 127,815 slashing damage on caster.
  - Any distance up to 40 away and a forward arc of 90 degrees
- Inflicts 9,507 - 11,620 slashing damage on caster every 2 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 40 away and a forward arc of 90 degrees

### Inevitable Annihilation

Effects:

- Inflicts 2,859 - 3,494 heat damage on target instantly and every 2 seconds.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

## Runes on the Floor

You can run over various colored tiles to gain temporary buffs/debuffs that offer pros and cons during the fight. These last ~3 seconds.

### Approach of Zou Kunnen

<i>Step on a tile with a blue rune.</i><br>
Effects:

- Decreases power of target by 1.0%.
- Increases Crit Bonus of target by 10.0.
- Increases base heal and ward amount by 10%.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Heart of Aniquilacion

<i>Step on a tile with a red rune.</i><br>
Effects:

- Increases all damage done to caster by 7.5%.
- Increases the crit bonus of combat arts and hostile spells by 10%.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Paixao's Grace

<i>Step on a tile with a purple rune.</i><br>
Effects:

- Inflicts 1.0% of max health in crushing damage on target instantly and every second.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Increases power of target by 1.0% instantly and every second.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Wreath of Pingyuan Diqu

<i>Step on a tile with a green rune.</i><br>
Effects:

- Reduces physical, elemental, noxious, arcane, heat, cold, magic, mental, divine, disease, poison, slash, crush and pierce damage done to target by 10%.
- Decreases Crit Bonus of target by 5.0.
- Increases Hate Gain of target by 5.0.
- Reduces damage of combat arts and spells by 5%.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

## [[Roehn Theer\Runes|Runes in the Corners]]

There are four runes that will appear during the battle against Theer. Once he reaches 90%, he will gain a buff called Protection of Theer that makes him immune to all damage. At least one must be killed to remove Protection of Theer, and as many as all four can be killed.  Each rune that the group kills gives Theer a new ability.  Referring to Theer as "One Rune Theer", "Two Rune Theer", etc... indicates how many runes were killed.  There are essentially 15 versions of Theer that can be fought - four One Rune versions, six Two Rune, four Three Rune and one Four Rune.

Four Rune Roehn Theer is known in the game as Chaos Theer.  Roehn Theer with three or fewer runes is Order Theer.
