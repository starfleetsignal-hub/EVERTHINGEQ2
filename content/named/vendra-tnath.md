## How to spawn

- Kill the 4 [[An extractor of truth|extractors of truth]] around [[Urbur Gizzlebazzle]]'s rack {{waypoint 25, -40, 37}}
- Hail Urbur
- Vendra spawns as an NPC and kills Urbur
- Vendra becomes a killable mob

(The location above is where she goes to stand if not killed)
