- Players will usually only encounter this mob during quest [[Drowning in Drink]], but should be avoided, as they (Like all other ^ ^ or higher mobs in the zone) will 'catch' players who get too close and [[The Storeroom Office|"send them to the office"]] interrupting quest progress.
