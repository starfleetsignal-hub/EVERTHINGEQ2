## Strategy

**Abyssral** has a resistance to damage while in the water, however refuses to fight out of the water.  To make Abyssral attackable, right-click and move an **abyss stone** {{waypoint 87, -83, 48}} to **an aquatic brazier** {{waypoint 65, -107, 49}}.  This will create a bubble underwater where he loses his protection, and can be defeated.
