## Strategy

A few seconds after you first pull Nortlav, a random group member will become disembodied, and Nortlav will take on the appearance of that player.<br>
While the rest of the group continues to fight with Nortlav, the affected player, who is now in spirit form, must go to the back of the room and kill 3 waves of books that spawn on the book shelves in the center of the room. <br>
If the books are not killed or any reach the rest of the group, they will summon a set of larger books on the rest of the party that have tons of hitpoints, and plenty of damage to boot, which will be very difficult to survive.<br>
Once 3 waves are killed, kill the enlarged group member and the possessed party member will return to their body. <br>
Be sure to give the possessed member enough time to kill the 3 waves of books before killing the enlarged encounter, or else the books will reach the named, and the adds will spawn. <br>
Nortlav will randomly possess group members 3 times and die when the 3rd enlarged member is killed.<br>
*NOTE:  If you get possessed and "killed", your Stun buff might not come off.  Simply cancel it from your Maintained Spells window.*<br>
*NOTE: If you are to slow the possession take place 4 times. Also after killing the 4th posessed member you still had to Kill Nortlav*<br>
*NOTE: If attempting to kill Nortlav with just yourself, or yourself and a mercenary, he will completely heal whenever he's attacked below 50% HP. This does not apply to effects already cast on him or passive damage caused by damage shields, though. A very high-level healer can eventually defeat him just by standing there.*<br>
The Damage Shield method no longer works as of August 2020- he just stands back up again after dying, full health and goes back to saying "Ah ah ah...I will not be that easy to defeat...Let us dance some more, shall we?"
