## Notes

- Tseralith is the being responsible for [[Searching the Depths of Fallen Gate|reanimating the dead]] within [[Fallen Gate]]. Possibly the entity [[The Lost Religion of Fallen Gate|worshiped]] by the ogres and trolls of this section of [[Neriak, City of Hate|Neriak]].

## Strategy

Keep her stunned/stifled! She likes to Lifetap and spam cast Blight, which knocks down HP rather quickly. If you pull her fast, you can kill her before her [[Hand of Tseralith|help]] spawns.
