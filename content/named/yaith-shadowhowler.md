## Strategy

Undead gnoll mob is a shadowknight and will use class spells (unknown if he'll Harmtouch).  Only other detail of note is this mob has a combination life/manatap he'll try to use on you called Withering Soulscream.  Hit for around 1500 health/900 mana, don't know what it ticks for as my healer cured it immediately.  Bring a little mana replenishment gear and the rest is tank 'n spank.  Counts as a gnoll for Master Strike purposes.
