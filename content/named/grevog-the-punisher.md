## Strategy

Agro Management, Positional Encounter, Targeted Joust

Grevog has three mechanics to be mindful of:

- Immunity to damage based on position to the fountains
- An attack that will kill tanks in a single hit when the emote appears
- An debuff that hits selected individuals and catches them on fire

The raid force should position themselves between the two fountains to remove the immunity buff from Grevog.

Depending on the healing capabilities of the raid, recommend tanks turn on Subtle Strikes and do not use abilities to draw threat towards them. Healers should heal through the named.

When the prompt appears that Grevog tosses something at certain individuals, anyone with the yellow debuff should run to the fountains and jump in the water to 'douse' themselves. They can return to the raid when the debuff is removed.
