Last boss in [[Crypt of Dalnir: The Kly Stronghold (Raid)]].

## Preparation

Before engaging:

- Check each of the four corner rooms and make a note of what color the orbs are.
  - The colors are nominally Azure, Emerald, Crimson, and Violet. However, be aware that an orb's apparent color may conflict with its name (for example, The Emerald Orb may appear yellow; the Azure Orb purple; while The Violet Orb looks black). Since you are trying to color-match with the 5th active orb (see below) it's probably best to ignore the names.
- A fifth orb (the "Active" one) will appear above the bed in the northernmost room. This orb will change colors during the battle (at 98%, 94%, 84%, 74%, 64%, 54%, 44%, 34%, 24%, 14%, and 4%). Designate someone to watch this orb.

## Strategy

- Position your designated orb-watcher in the bedroom; the rest of the raid engages the Kly.
- A fifth orb (the "Active" one) will appear above the bed in the northernmost room. When the "Active" orb changes colors, two things must happen:
  1. First, the raid must immediately stop ALL attacks on The Kly -- that includes mercs and pets. To be safe, everyone should turn their backs to The Kly to prevent riposte damage. Any damage done to The Kly while the 5th orb has a color-match in one of the 4 rooms will stack buffs on him that will eventually wipe the raid (15 stacks == wipe).
  1. Second, someone must go into the corner room whose orb matches the active orb's color and destroy that orb. When that happens, the person who attacked the orb will be teleported to the sacrificial table.
- The Kly will now begin absorbing health from the person on the table. The raid party should stand at the head of the table, facing The Kly, and resume damage. The orb-watcher keeps watch in the bedroom for the next color-change.
- Rinse and repeat until he's defeated.
- Note: **14% will be the last 'orb' phase.**  You are now free to use any and all damage sources for the rest of the encounter.  At 4%, instead of having to destroy an orb as previous, the stacks will begin to climb rapidly, regardless if you hit the boss or not.  This is the final 'burn' phase. Your raid must defeat the boss before the stacks hit 15. *(The more disciplined the raid has been during the entire encounter, the lower the starting stacks will be for the final burn phase, giving you a longer window to do that final 4% damage)*
