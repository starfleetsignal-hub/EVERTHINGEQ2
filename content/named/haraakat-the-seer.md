## Special Attacks

### Arcane Zephyr

Effects:

- Inflicts 90.0% of max health in magic damage on target instantly and every 5 seconds.
- Decreases power of target by 90.% instantly and every 5 seconds.
- Stuns target
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Buffeting Winds

Effects:

- Inflicts 64,584 - 150,696 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 54,965 - 82,448 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 47,636 - 71,454 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 45,346 - 55,423 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 45,346 - 55,423 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 34,353 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 85.6% instantly and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 149.8% instantly and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 200 away.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Increases power of targets in Area of Effect by 3.9%.
  - Any point not covered by the above effects.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
- Throws caster back
- If this spell expires naturally while mindblast or demoralizing gale are active you will be hit with arcane zephyr, a massive damage spell.

### Cloak of Arcane Knowledge

Effects:

- Increases spell damage of wearer by 35%.
- When damaged this spell has a 7% chance to cast Epiphanic Feedback on target's attacker. Lasts for 5.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 1,481 - 2,751 magic damage on target.
  - Inflicts 830 - 1,541 magic damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - Decreases power of target by 12.0%.
  - Decreases power of target by 4.0% every 5 seconds.
  - Decreases WIS and INT of target by 526.0.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Ritual of Forbidden Lore

Effects:

- Inflicts 110.0% of max health in magic damage on target instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Decreases power of target by 110.0% instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means
