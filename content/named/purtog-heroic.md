## Strategy

- [[Purtog's disorderly|Purtog's disorderlies]] adds will spawn during the fight, heralded by Purtog saying *"Gather 'round everyone! Gather 'round!"*.
- The disorderlies do a power drain that increases with range. Fight close to the adds: Purtog says, *"Don't let 'em get near ya disorderlies! Dese ones means bizness!"*
