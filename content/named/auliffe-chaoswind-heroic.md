## Strategy

### Trash mob Fight

- You may wanna clear those glacial Gargoyle over your head in boss room
- They get aggro when you close to 30m.

### **Auliffe Chaoswind**

- This boss is a little different to [[Solusek Ro's Tower: The Obsidian Core (Solo)|solo version]]. There is only one **"Red Circle"** in heroic
- Before fighting, make sure everyone in your team at the **center** of boss room
- Make sure pull him to the middle of of boss room ASAP when fighting. Note that "**Frost White Shield**" generates right in the **middle** every time you aggro him very first.
- ![](images/Frost_Shield.jpg)Do **NOT** step out the edge. It kills you instantly. Make sure everyone close to edge including tank. You may wanna pull named on **edge** too.
- ![](images/Red_Circle.jpg) Hit him hard till he below **55% HP** and he casts **"Red Circle"**. Step out edge immediately it only cast **3 second** before it damage. You can't get out if you are too far away from edge. Especially when horrible lag, you will see red circle pop up and you die at the same time.
- After the red circle disappears, move your team back to the **middle** and he will use "**Frost White Shield**" again when he drops to **10% HP**
- **Blizzard Conditions** deals about 9.1M poison damage to every one overtime. Group heal overtime deals it
- ![](images/Loot_Auliffe_Chaoswind_Key.jpg) Don't forget to loot his key and open his chest
