## Special Attacks

### Aura of Eternal Health

Effects:

- Reduces all damage done to caster by 25%.
- There must be some way to suspend this effect.
- When any damage is received this spell has a 10% chance to cast Ancient Feedback on target's attacker. Last 4.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 8,412 - 19,628 magic damage on target instantly and every4 seconds.
  - Stuns target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 12.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 1.3 seconds.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Aweinspiring Aura

Effects:

- Opponents have a 9% chance every 15 secs to be struck with awesome wonder, which will immobilize them for several seconds.

### Bolt from the Depths

Effects:

- Inflicts 150.0% of max health in magic damage on target instantly and every 6 seconds.
- Decreases power of target by 150.0% instantly and every 6 seconds.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Eternal Sphere of Despair

Effects:

- Decreases Crit Chance of Caster by 103.5.
- Decreases Multi Attack of target by 72.4.
- Decreases Haste of target by 61.5.
- Decreases Casting Speed of target by 51.7%.
- A sphere of magical origin that demoralizes and damages its target.
