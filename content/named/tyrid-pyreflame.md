## Strategy

DPS (without cooldowns) on the boss.  He will shapeshift into various things throughout the fight.

When he enters the "bull" stage, around 75% he will put up a damage reflection.  This doesn't last long.  (20 sec ish?)

Save all DPS cooldowns for the "dragon" phase.

He will spawn adds.  If you can do it, just kill the dragon and tank the adds, you will have to kill them after, but the chest drops once the name is dead, adds or not.

As soon as you start the encounter the adds will spawn inside the fires spread around the room.
These adds will stay in the flames and not attack unless you get to close, hit them with an AOE,or Tyrid turns into his final (Dragon) form (at which point they will all attack at once).
So, to make this fight easier, while Tyrid is in his first, easy, form. Just run around the room and kill all of these adds before he changes into a harder form.
