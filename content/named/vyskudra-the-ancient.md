## Special Attacks

### Elemental Aura

Effects:

- If target avoids any melee attack, target takes damage from Thunderclap.

### Shock of Lightning

Effects:

- Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 85.0%.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 220.0% every 6 seconds.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Inflicts 85.0% of max health in magic damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 220.0% of max health in magic damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Stormforce

Effects:

- Increases STR and INT of caster by 275.1.
- On a melee hit this spell will cast Stormslam on target of attack.
  - Inflicts 124,812 slashing damage on target.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Thunderclap

Effects:

- Inflicts 35,540 slashing damage on target
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- a powerful force damages you.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 340,262 - 510,393 cold damage on target.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.

  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
