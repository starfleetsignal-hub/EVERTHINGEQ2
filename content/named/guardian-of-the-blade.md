## Strategy

- When he turns aggro after his speech, he pops a stone shield that lasts about 20 seconds or so.
- He goes down fast once his stone shield is not up. On my attempt, he went through about 3 shields before he was down.
- I would suggest killing the three adds at the top of the stairs. And have the tank pull the Guardian against the right wall.
- Other than that, the fight is very straight forward, just tank and spank.
