## Factions

- good faction with **The Forsaken City**
- bad faction with **The Nizari**

## To Reach Her

To get to her, find your way to the top level and kill both K'hazid and K'huthas, the elemental fire and water lords, respectively. Doing so will cause the forcefield walls behind the other named to disappear, allowing you to jump down to the named.

## Strategy

Mesmerize one add and charm the second.
