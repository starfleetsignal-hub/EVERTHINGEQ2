## Spawning

Collect the two scrolls from the side rooms. Items will show in inventory as Slips of Paper.  Then click on the scroll case at {{waypoint -197, 14.3, 77.8}}

## Strategy

He spawns 2 small hands from time to time. These heal him. Kill them (AoE works well they have few HPs) to prevent the fight from lasting longer.
