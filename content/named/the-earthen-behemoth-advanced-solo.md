## Notes

1. Clear [[The Collapsed Sanctum]] the first time you enter it, including non-aggro elementals. These will add and debuff.
1. The [[The Earthen Behemoth (Advanced Solo)|Earthen Behemoth]] spawns in the [[The Collapsed Sanctum|Collapsed Sanctum]] {{waypoint 66, -12, -143}} after killing [[Unatu]] and [[Poxrata]].
   - During the fight, the [[The Earthen Behemoth (Advanced Solo)|Earthen Behemoth]] breaks into smaller [[A dislodged stone|dislodged stone]] adds.
   - If the [[A dislodged stone|dislodged stones]] aren't killed quickly, the Earthen Behemoth will suck them back up and they will heal him. Their health is low so they can be taken out easily.
   - The [[The Earthen Behemoth (Advanced Solo)|Earthen Behemoth]] has a knock-back that throws you up in the air.
