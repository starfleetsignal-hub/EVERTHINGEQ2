## Strategy

Pull him to a corner to tank him there and kill him.  He has an AE knockback that does a nice chunk of damage and he drops ROK legendary set boots. During the fight a non-attackable mini golem named, "a fragment trampler" will run around the room. If you are in its way it will knock you around and do focus damage to you (5742x2 on a 83 mage).
