## Notes

He's standing around a fire with a group of three 91v Heroic [[A Ry'Gorr striker|Ry'Gorr strikers]]. Standard AOE's will take them out if you concentrate on him. Even though the [[A Ry'Gorr shocktrooper|Ry'Gorr shocktroopers]] aren't grouped with them, don't be surprised if they decide to join in. It might be a good idea to deal with them beforehand.
