The Malformed Prophet is a Tier 2 raid boss.

## Location

- {{waypoint -107.24, 14.46, -427.93}} on the eastern ramp within Kren Rommol
- {{waypoint -271.10, 22.49, -360.77}} on a hillside southeast of Kren Rommol
- {{waypoint -23.83, 19.05, -168.10}} overlooking the northwest shore of the Mouth of Prexus

## Stats

- 113 Flurry Avoidance
- 330 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 9434 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a hard time limit of 60 minutes.
- Every 75 seconds, the Prophet "looks for someone to point his gnarled finger at" and selects random targets that are within 10 meters or outside of 35 meters. The number of selected targets increases as the health of the Prophet decreases. He heals slightly for each selected target. Each target receives a curable trauma named **Pointy Finger of Doom**. When this detriment expires or is cured, all players within 10 meters of the target (not including the afflicted player) are killed.
- The Prophet casts a curable arcane named **Prophetic Immobilization** on the entire raid at once which deals growing arcane damage, roots the target, and snares the target.
- The Prophet casts a curable arcane named **Appetite for Destruction** on multiple raid members simultaneously. If this detriment expires naturally, the Prophet gains one increment of **Strength Consumption** for each failed cure, which decreases the damage he takes from all sources by 2.5% per increment. These increments are removed the next time he casts **Appetite for Destruction**.
- The Prophet casts a curable curse named **Hex of Flames** on 3-8 raid members simultaneously that are within 35 meters or outside of 55 meters. The number of selected targets increases as the health of the Prophet decreases. If fewer players than the selected number are in the appropriate range, then all players within range (and only the players within range) receive the curse. If the curse expires naturally, the entire raid force is killed.
- At random intervals, the Prophet casts **Mana Burst I**, which drains power from the entire raid. This effect can be blocked with [[Raid Rune: Power Flex I]] or higher.
- The Prophet does not cast any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] can be used as a simple debuff.
