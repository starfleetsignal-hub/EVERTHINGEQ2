## Special Attacks

### Icy Tempest

Effects:

- Inflicts 151,243 - 280,880 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 111,442 - 206,965 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 9 meters
- Inflicts 90,973 - 136,460 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 81,876 - 122,814 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is between 9 and 15 meters away
- Inflicts 61,407 - 75,053 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 51,173 - 62,544 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is between 15 and 200 meters away

## Strategy

After running in like a mad man, I found that this named will hit you with magic damage. On an 83 Assassin, it was ~10-13k, but this was considerably less than the other two, who hit for ~20-24k.

This boss and Omzuk will randomly choose which prison to be in front of after a failed encounter (i.e, every time I ran up and got killed, they reset in different locations.) Rwznak will always be at the front. See [[Rwznak]]
