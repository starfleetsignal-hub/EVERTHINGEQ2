## Notes

1. Click the **Icons of Scorn** on the wall around the zone until there are no more clicks. Go to {{waypoint 212, 33, -61}} to find white/red orbs or "lanterns". These must all be red to open the door. This part is a puzzle, and you must find a way to align them all.
1. Clear the [[A cleric of hate (Heroic)|clerics of hate]] and [[An acolyte of malice (Heroic)|acolytes of malice]] to spawn.
