## Notes

- Spawned by gathering the armor pieces from the [[Hateful Plate (Heroic)|Hateful Plate]], then placing them in **a chest unlike the rest** at {{waypoint -202.52, 15.76, 150.07}}.
- There will be periodic spawns of items of clothing that can be right-clicked and destroyed to mitigate his damage.  (Mousing over the clothing and pressing F repeatedly destroys them much faster).
