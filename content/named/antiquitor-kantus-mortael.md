## Trivia & Lore

**Kantus** is one part of the divided soul of [[Rikantus Everling]]. Another part of the soul resides in [[Rikantus]] (who lives at the cemetery in [[Antonica]]). Once you have completed [[Nektropos Castle: Tribulation]], Antiquitor Kantus will have some new things to say to you. He has beside him a book called the [[1001 Tales of Maj'Dul]], which features in several quests.
