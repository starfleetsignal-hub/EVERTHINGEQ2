## Notes

Dominus Rile - Venril's son, Wielder of Greenmist and greatest of the Emperors is one of the four "casual contested" raid mobs introduced in [[LU43]].

## Strategy

Dominous Rile should be tanked inside the room, and your raid/group will want to use a Main assist for targeting the adds the Dominous Rile summons.  Every 10-15 seconds Dominous Rile will summon a skeleton add that can be burned down fairly fast and then refocus DPS on the named.
