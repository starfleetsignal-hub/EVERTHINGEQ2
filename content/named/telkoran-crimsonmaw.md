## Strategy

In this fight two adds will come with the boss, burn them down before DPSing the boss. If you kill the boss while adds are alive, he will cast Phoenix Recovery and heal back to full. Adds have around 12.2M hp each. Simple tank and spank otherwise. All of the dps from this mob is slash so keep the group away from the mob and outside of aoe and melee range. It is recommended to have a tank with shield and good mitigation. Having a tank that can do nice aoe will help burn all of the mob at the same time.

## Quests

Needed as first step for quest [[Mystery in the Underdepths]].
