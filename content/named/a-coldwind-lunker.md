## Notes

- To spawn, look for 'a grouping of seaweed' underwater off Mariner's Island, then clear all the [[A coldwind pike|coldwind pikes]] away from it.  A level 14 coldwind pike will spawn (excluding this placeholder, coldwind pikes are level 12-13).  After killing the lvl14 pike, A Coldwind Lunker has a chance to spawn.  The seaweed can be really hard to see, so killing coldwind pikes indiscriminately also works.
- 'a grouping of seaweed' has multiple spawn locations:   {{waypoint 258, -53, 821}}  {{waypoint 319, -49, 691}}
