## Strategy

Grouped Encounter with [[Barin the Brute]].

Slow and Alternating Burn

As the named both reach certain health thresholds, groups of pirate adds will spawn. Depending on the raid forces setup, may be able to have tanks just hold agro and continue to burn named and tank through the encounter. If the tanks are having a hard time keeping agro and staying alive, switch off and burn each set of adds down before returning focus back to the named.

There may be a forced focus component to this encounter as well.
