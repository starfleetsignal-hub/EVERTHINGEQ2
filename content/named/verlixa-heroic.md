## Strategy

### Trash mobs

- Kill all "**an** **efreeti fire lord"** and all the way to the room you can see Verlixa
- Heroic version trash mobs burn your mana very quickly. You have to increase team power over time

### [[Everquest2:Verlixa (Heroic)|Verlixa]]

- This boss shares basically same mechanics in [[Solusek Ro's Tower: Monolith of Fire (Solo) (Instance)|solo version]]. But way way more difficult. Fourth Named
- This is a **DPS Check** and a **Memory Test** unfortunately
- Verlixa deals more damage than solo version. Just like fire above those floors. about 15M heat damage a hit(auto-attack is little)
- If you wanna see details about this fight, Check [[Solusek Ro's Tower: Monolith of Fire (Solo) (Instance)|solo version]] Verlixa part. Following is an **example** of a typical fight and some tips. Note that every time you re-trigger this boss fight. Mirage run are different in directions from last time. So you can't cheat like [[Kralet Penumbra: Uzulu Deep (Event Heroic)|Uzulu Deep]]
  1. First ***Run***, 2 Mirage and 2 braziers. Time limited in 2 mins(**Path of Flames)**.
     - Every time I fight the real boss Verlixa. I choose to stand near to his side and **face to the door**(Just like 0 point of my axis) . Cuz door side can't see clearly. Now I am reducing boss HP to trigger his first mirage run.![Every time I fight the real boss Verlixa. I choose to stand near to his side. Cuz door side can't see clearly](images/First_Mirage_P1.jpg)
     - Now he reach HP threshold and release two mirages in order. This time **First** brazier is on the **right side** from my view. Heroic version of mirages move way faster than [[Solusek Ro's Tower: The Obsidian Core (Solo)|solo version]]. The left/right/Top side takes 0.8 **sec** and it gone.![](images/First_Mirage_P2.jpg)
     - Second mirage this time fly cross the middle-floor one![](images/First_Mirage_P3.jpg)
     - Next picture I immediately run to the **first** brazier and believe the **second** is **behind**![](images/First_Mirage_P4.jpg)
     - Ok I clicked the first correct brazier and I am teleported to here. First thing is heading to Mirage and find my position and turn my face to **door side**![](images/First_Mirage_P5.jpg)
     - Next picture I still heading to **door side** and kill this mirage.The second one is behind me so I turn around and click it ![](images/First_Mirage_P6.jpg)
     - Now same thing happened in the second mirage. **Door side** reveals the door. See that circle in picture. Time is short. Go go go. Click it and we return to Boss Room. End of the First **Run**![](images/First_Mirage_P7.jpg)
     - Summary: The brazier at the door is always not kindled and not clickable. The layout and direction of the other braziers are consistent with the room where the boss is,so I always stand at the position of monsters and boss in any room, facing the door, so that the relative positions of all the braziers are the same for me. BTW, door side brazier is closer to the middle one.
  1. Second ***Run***, 3 Mirage and 3 braziers. Time limited in 2 mins(**Path of Flames)**. Same thing but more to remember and kill faster
  1. Last ***Run***, 4 Mirage and 4 braziers. Time limited in 2 mins(**Path of Flames).** Same thing but more to remember and kill faster

- If you click wrong one, you will die immediately
  - If you get any other players group with you. One revive the dead and the other try next possible brazier.
  - If your other teammates are fast. Let them click different one till one of you get the right one before time run out.
  - **Path of Flames** still count down if you have not click that circle door. It matters for 6 players group
- Be sure let your Mercenaries and Pets fight with you
