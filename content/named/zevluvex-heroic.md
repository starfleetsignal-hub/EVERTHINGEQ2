## Notes

- Spawned by slaying the [[A loathsome lich|loathsome liches]] on either side of the [[Basilica of Hate (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Basilica of Hate]] around {{waypoint -81.92, 30.78, -250.67}} and {{waypoint 54.97, 30.62, -257.55}}; once the liches are dead, turn on Ultravision (a buff from coercers or necromancers, or use [[Gnomish Shades]]) then find and click on the unlit torches on the sides of the building.
- Adds will spawn during the fight that can only be seen with Ultravision, [[A trace of darkness]] and [[A shadow abhorrent]]. The next time adds spawn, there will be two of each. There may be more in each succeeding round.
