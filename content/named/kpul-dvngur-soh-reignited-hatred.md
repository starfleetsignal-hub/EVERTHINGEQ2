## Spawning

Find around six music pages scattered about the north section of the zone.

## Strategy

Hit him as hard as you can as quickly as you can, then be ready for a long fight for what's left of his health bar. The buffs on him and the hard to remove debuffs he puts on you are very descriptive: Read them (you'll have time, since you won't be doing much else). Don't bother with emotes if solo; these will only stack counters on you that lead to a fail condition. Level 130 stifle and stun runes were absolutely ineffective. Was immune to most non-slashing based damage, and even that had little effect. Extreme and complete mana drain, so be prepared. Long slog of a fight characterized by trying to stay alive and waiting for the few, brief seconds afforded for a chance to cast something with quick, melee-based slashing damage.
