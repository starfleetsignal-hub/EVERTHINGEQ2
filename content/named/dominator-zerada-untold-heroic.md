Ability and strategy information changes at stages (1-20/21-40/41+)

## Abilities

- **High Horse**
- A buff that has a chance to deal damage to the attacker when Zerada is damaged, which adds one increment. It also increases fervor, ability doublecast avoidance, flurry avoidance, and combat mitigation per increment. Once the buff has reached (20/20/30) increments, it can be removed by a fighter's [[Bulwark of Order|Overpowering Barrage]], which replaces the buff with Back on Her High Horse and summons 6 [[A phantom charger|phantom chargers]]. If it reaches 100 increments, the entire party is killed. At stage 41+, increments can only be removed by casting Overpowering Barrage while flanking or behind.
- **Back on Her High Horse**
- A buff that decrements by 1 per second. When it reaches 0 increments, if any phantom chargers remain, Zerada dispels all hostile effects and recasts High Horse.
- **Grave Gash**
- A noxious detriment that inflicts disease damage over time and decreases fervor. It can only be cured while Zerada is dismounted.
- **Captain's Cull**
- A curable curse that deals increasing slashing damage over time.
- **Rein of Terror**
- An incurable arcane that strikes targets in front of Zerada and fears them. The ability [[Fearless Morale]] does not block this effect.

## Strategy

At the start of combat, the grated floor on the eastern end of the room retracts, allowing players to fall into the pool.
