- To spawn him, clear all the [[A decaying Darkfathom|decaying Darkfathoms]] from around the Shrine. A few [[A cursed Darkfathom|cursed Darkfathoms]] will spawn. Kill them to spawn either [[Elwyn Aquel]] or [[The Revenant of Prexus]]. Accompanied by two decaying Darkfathoms (8vvv).
