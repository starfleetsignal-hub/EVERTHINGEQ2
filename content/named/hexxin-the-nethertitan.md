Hexxin the Nethertitan is a Tier 2 raid boss.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance: 470.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance: 540.0
- Resolve 11,015.0

## Abilities

- **Blackhook Voidspike**
- An incurable trauma that deals piercing damage over time.
- **Nethersurge**
- An incurable arcane that deals damage to health over time based on how far apart from each other the target and their allies are on average. The more enemies slain, the stronger the surge becomes!
- **Distraction**
- An incurable arcane that deals arcane damage over time. The effect also causes blurry vision and causes the target to weave while moving.
- **Netherslam**
- A curable trauma that deals fast ticking increasing physical damage and increases non-physical damage from all sources. Higher ranks of this spell increase the debuff for non-physical damage.
- **Spellweaved Admonishment**
- A curable elemental that deals elemental damage and increases all other damage types. This effect only lands on mages.
- **Distorted Thoughts**
- A curable arcane that deals fast-growing damage over time.
- **Absorb Power**
- A curable curse that heals Hexxin significantly if it expires naturally. This curse lands on three players simultaneously.
- **Barrage**
- An incurable curse that can only be countered with [[Bulwark of Order]].
