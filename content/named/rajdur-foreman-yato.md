Raj'Dur Foreman Yato is a Tier 1 raid boss.

## Stats

- 108 Flurry Avoidance
- 324 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 9350 Resolve

## Strategy

- Immediately on pull, Yato summons all roaming Raj'Dur trash in Lower Raj'Durabad. (The summoned trash may socially aggro other non-roaming trash.)
- Yato periodically "targets those nearby" and gains a heal for each player within 11 meters. Yato's current target (usually the main tank) does not count for this ability. After the heal, he "ends his targeted nearby attack".
- Yato periodically "looks for a strategic target" and places a black circle under a raid member. Players who stay within this circle too long receive a curable curse.
- Every 48 seconds, Yato "targets enemies farther away" and casts a curable elemental called **Pelting**. This detriment lands on the entire raid at once, dealing extra damage to those that are standing farther away.
- Yato periodically summons a [[Raj'Dur Foreman Apprentice]] to join the fight. Each apprentice that is active decreases the amount of damage that Yato takes.
- Yato does not cast any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] can be used as a simple debuff.
