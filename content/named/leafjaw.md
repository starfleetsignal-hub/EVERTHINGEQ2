## Strategy

1. DPS him down.
1. If text is shown "Target is immune", an add will come. Kill add first.
1. After this, there will be a purple sphere up. Defeat it down. After this, Leafjaw is not immune for a short period.

It will repeat.

The purple spheres are at pond entrance {{waypoint 140, 80, -377}} or in the right-end of the pond under the "bridge" at {{waypoint 133, 80, -266}}
