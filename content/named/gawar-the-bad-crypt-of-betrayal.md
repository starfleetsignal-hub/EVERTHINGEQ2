## Notes

- Gawar only spawns in *Crypt of Betrayal* on a certain step on the quest [[Passage to the Isle of Zek]]. When you click on the coffin at {{waypoint -29, 7, -9}}, Gawar and three [[A Deathfist marauder (Crypt of Betrayal)|Deathfist marauders]] will spawn and attack. They do not have to be killed to continue on the quest, but Gawar has loot and AA.
