## Notes

- To spawn, kill and loot [[A hateful breastplate]], [[A hateful helmet]], [[A hateful pair of gauntlets]], [[A hateful pair of greaves]], and [[A hateful set of pauldrons]], then delete the armor from your inventory.
- Armor pieces will add throughout the fight.  They're on a timer (45 sec, I think) and if you don't kill them before the timer expires, they heal him and give him a damage reduction and melee damage buff.  Low health, they die pretty fast.
