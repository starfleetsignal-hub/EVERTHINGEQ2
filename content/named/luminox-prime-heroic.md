## Strategy

- This fight is very similar to the basic [[Luminox]] battle.
- At around 80% and every 20% after, Luminox Prime emotes "Luminox rends the space around you, separating the light from the dark!", creating inimical black globules and neutral gold globules throughout the room. Run into the light globules to get a run speed boost. Avoid the black globules which will destroy your run speed.
- Like Luminox, Luminox Prime launches "Manaetic Missile's Manaetic Explosion", which does enormous damage. The missiles move slowly and explode after 10 seconds, run away around the edges of the room until they self destruct.
  - An easy strategy is to start the fight with Luminox at one of the ramps that leads to towards one of the previous named that was killed and burn him the 20% until he emotes. At this point, have the team joust and run to the opposite side of the rooms ramp in a spot not near the pathing globules. The missiles have a relatively short range and you do not need to worry about them being able to reach the team.
- After the emote at 60% Luminox will switch up a bit and emote to fire a straight beam of light like the Energy Delivery Module boss earlier in the zone. Happened twice between the 60% and 40% threshold, and then did not seem to happen again the remainder of the fight.
