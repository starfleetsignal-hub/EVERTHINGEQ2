### To Spawn

Requires Darkpaw Ladder in Inventory to see, Agro and Drag the 3 "a dust devil" mobs from surrounding rooms to the totem

When Non Agro Dustpaw Devil Spawns, Use ladder to climb to totem and then move it (needs to be far enough from spawn point to trigger)

During fight Move Totem to the Purple Shadow Totem Locations and Fight at Totem
