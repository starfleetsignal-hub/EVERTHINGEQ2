## Strategy

- Clear [[An ethereal automaton|ethereal automatons]] and [[An ethereal spark|ethereal sparks]] to reach the Amalgam.
- The Amalgam can be kited using ranged attacks.
- At 80%, 50%, and 25%, he emotes "The Amalgam of Energy's core begins to overload! You can feel energy being ripped from the very air around you!" before casting [[Energy Globule|Energy Globules]], progressively more at each %. The Amalgam takes no damage while these are up, and if they reach him he will heal so switch to them and kill them.
