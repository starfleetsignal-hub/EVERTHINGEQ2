## Special Attacks

### Flames of Instability

Effects:

- Interrupts target
- Decreases power of targets by 20.0%.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 45.0% of max health in heat damage on target.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
- If this spell expires naturally, you will be afflicted with an unstable aura, and those around you will be hit with flames of instability.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Flames of Silence

Effects:

- Inflicts 65,526 - 152,893 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 61,311 - 91,966 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 52,114 - 78,171 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 43,109 - 52,689 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 37,936 - 46,366 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 26,823 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
- Stifles targets in Area of Effect

### Sear

Effects:

- Inflicts 12,837 - 25,482 heat damage on target encounter.
- Inflicts 8,344 - 16,563 heat damage on target encounter every 5.3 seconds.

### Touch of Flames

Effects:

- Inflicts 25,003 - 41,672 heat damage on target
- Inflicts 17,953 heat damage on target every 6.7 seconds.
- Stifles target
- Resistibility increases against targets higher than level 117.

### Ward of Distance

Effects:

- Players doing damage more than 30 meters from the target are hit with ward of retaliation, a massive focus damage and stun spell.
