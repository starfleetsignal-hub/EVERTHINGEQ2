Thet-ki-dua is a Tier 2 raid boss. He gains an additive advantage for each player below 18 and has a soft time limit of 45 minutes.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance 400.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance 800.0
- Resolve 13,430.0

## Abilities

- **Conscription of Cinders II**
- A buff that has a chance of triggering Cinder Burst on the attacker, which deals heat damage.
- **Power of Thet**
- A dispellable buff that increases melee multiplier and potency, multiplied by increments. Thet attempts to pull power from stored increments to heal himself.
- **Melodic Ambition**
- A buff that decreases damage taken from all sources for each active [[A hiding reverent dancer|Reverent Dancer]]. Each additional Dancer beyond 2 heals Thet.
- **Surging Pulse**
- An incurable trauma that deals increasing physical and arcane damage over time.
- **Overbearing Influence II**
- An incurable trauma that makes power usage also cost health based on the amount of power used.
- **Channeled Fury I/II/III/IV**
- A curable trauma that deals increasing physical damage and increases non-physical damage from all sources.
- **Frigid Burst I**
- A curable elemental that deals increasing cold damage. This ability can be blocked with Area Effect blockers, and the reuse time is 60 seconds.
- **Barrage**
- An incurable curse that can only be blocked with [[Bulwark of Order]].

## Strategy

At the start of the fight, all remaining [[A gazerling aberration|gazerling aberrations]] join the fight.
