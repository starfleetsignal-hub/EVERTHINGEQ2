## Strategy

- Melee damage type: Crushing. Average hit = 5k (no crits)
- 44% of non-melee damage dealt by mob - "Fiery Protection - Damage". Resist Type: Focus. Hits for 2 - 4k dmg. (16% crits)
- 32% of non-melee damage dealt by mob - "Pyreskin". Resist Type: Heat. Hits for 2 - 4k dmg. (22% crits) (Seems to be a damage ward?)
- 22% of non-melee damage dealt by mob - "Call of the Icy Winds". Resist Type: Cold. Hits for 2 - 3.5k dmg. (29% crits) (Seems to be an AoE? - Cast twice in fight, but within 15 sec of one another)

Joust the named when the red message comes up on your screen. He does root himself like many other named with this ability.<br>
If you don't Joust, you will be cursed.

Adds spawn ONLY if you're next to the eggs they hatch from. Being in the water seems to counter their normal radius as well as the wingbeat style rear knockback that the name has.

At 10% and below, Hoarfrost starts to teleport the raid into the center of the room. There are 4 or 5 egs in that area, so your best bet is to keep the name at the teleport point and AE off-tank the adds in the water. Kill Hoarfrost then finish off any adds still left alive. The adds are ^^^ heroics.

One place that may work well is to pet pull him into the hallway where you entered the room. The MT stands at the door, raid in middle of hall, and OT at bottom, to cover any adds that do spawn (although in this location, there should be a couple adds on the pull and no more).
