## Strategy

Tank and spank, at least 1 hour respawn. He also summons an [[Aqueous Swarm]].

I've seen him at level 93 (as shown in the picture) and as high as 95. He's always a ^^ mob so I don't know if he scales. I was at level 93 when I encountered him.
