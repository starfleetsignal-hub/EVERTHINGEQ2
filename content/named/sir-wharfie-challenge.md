## Notes

During the fight there will be voices and eventually a white object thrown at Sir Wharfie that gives him a new look with some extra defensive and offensive abilities. Look nearby at some barrels with targetable [[A heavy crate]] atop them. Destroy the crates and have someone in the group click the barrel that can be moved like a house item. Click it on top of the names location to 'throw' it and remove the extra buffs.
