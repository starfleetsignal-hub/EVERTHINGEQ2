## Strategy

- He has a Damage reduction buff that goes away when purple missiles hit him
- The rippers at the outer rim will buff him 5 triggers of Damage reduction again
- At 81% he kneels at the start of phase 2 & shoots green missiles that will heal him
- At 61% starts phase 3 & he has the chance to shoot out a red missile in place of a green missle.
- At 40% he starts a single laser w/missiles.
- At X% he does a multi laser (think of a asterisk or star that shoots at all angles).
