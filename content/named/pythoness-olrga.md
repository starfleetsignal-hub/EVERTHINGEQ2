## Strategy

Run like heck

## Another Strategy

If you enter the room (even to the left) the entire room aggros and comes to aid Pythoness Olrga. There's no straight forward "enter the room" strategy unless you have a full (questionable) or raid group.

- Have your group stand outside in the hallway.
- Have a ranged character "pull" the groups one at a time that are not associated with Pythoness Olrga. Tank and spank or root and nuke (preferably all of these).
- Have a healer/warder (or both) target the "ranged" puller and only engage the group out in the hallway.
- After you've cleared all the unrelated groups, enter the room and engage.
  - **NOTE:** Furies, Wardens and Templars earn their stripes in this battle.
