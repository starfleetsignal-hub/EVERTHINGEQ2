Phyorn, Void-Borne Emergent is a Tier 3 raid boss. He gains an additive advantage for each player below 22 and has a hard time limit of 30 minutes.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance 550.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance 900.0
- Resolve 13,670

## Abilities

- **Voracious Protection**
- A buff that enhances Phyorn's ability to dodge while any [[Voracious protector|voracious protectors]] are active.
- **Oration of Power**
- A buff that decreases damage taken from all sources for each [[Emergent Orator]] engaged in the fight. For each additional orator more than 2, each orator heals Phyorn for 10%.
- **Conscription of Consequence III**
- A buff that gives a chance of triggering Bolt of Consequence, which deals cold damage and interrupts the target, and Forced Consequence, which increases offensive ability.
- **Surging Magics**
- An incurable trauma that deals increasing physical damage to health and power over time.
- **Mindwracking Influence III**
- An incurable arcane that causes all abilities to cost additional power.
- **Pulsing Noxious Waves I/II/III/IV**
- A curable noxious that deals disease damage over time, increases non-arcane damage, and prevents the removal of Emergent Suppression.
- **Emergent Suppresion I/II/III/IV**
- A curable arcane that deals increasing arcane damage and increases non-arcane damage from all sources. This effect cannot be removed if the target also has Pulsing Noxious Waves.
- **Elemental Emergence III**
- A curable elemental that deals increasing cold and heat damage and increases non-elemental damage from all sources.
- **Void-Borne Pox**
- A curable curse with 6 increments that roots the target, prevents the target from casting hostile abilities, and holds the target at the bottom of the hatelist. If it expires, the entire raid is killed.
- **Soluble Pox**
- A curse that is initially incurable and can be made curable by swimming in the nearby water. If it expires, the entire raid is killed.
- **Barrage**
- An incurable curse that can only be blocked with [[Bulwark of Order]].

## Strategy

Phyorn places a red dome around himself periodically. After a few seconds, every player within the dome is killed. Two domes may be active at the same time.

[[Emergent Orator|Emergent Orators]] join the fight periodically. The orators do not move from their initial locations. After a while, they begin healing Phyorn. The amount of the heal increases with the number of orators engaged in the fight.
