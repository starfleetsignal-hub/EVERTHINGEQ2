## Notes

- The Headless Horseman was introduced in October 2009 as an epicx2 mob and revamped in 2013.
- In 2020 the associated quest ([[Headless or Tails]]) was turned into a PQ (public quest), with a zone-wide announcement 10 minutes before his appearance. Players can participate at any level.
- He spawns as a non-aggro mob, and will path around the tree until offered [[A lost necklace]] (right-click the Horseman, then *Offer Necklace*). The offeree's necklace is consumed.
- Some posts have implied that the necklace need only be in your bank to be accepted; others claim that it must be in your bags.
- Less than max-level participants will want to get the event buff -- click the glowing skull inside the hollow tree as soon as you arrive in the PQ area.

## Strategy

The Horseman spawns at the base of The Hollow Tree, in front of its dark gaping hole.  To aggro him, someone must be holding [[A lost necklace]] and must offer it to the Horseman. Whoever has aggro **must then drag the Horseman back to the tree** and everyone piles onto him there. At-level adds will spawn in the area; stay close to the fight and they won't be a problem.

At 50% health, the Horseman dismounts from his horse.  The horse will aggro and the Horseman will heal to 75% health. Take the horse down first, as it seems to keep the Horseman's health boosted. There are no other tricks to this mob; just keep him by the tree and burn, burn, burn.

### Aside

Notes pertaining to the pre-PQ version of this encounter suggested the tank drag the Horseman off the strewn field of bones surrounding the Hollow Tree (to the road adjacent the tree) and *then* back to the tree to weaken him. It is not clear whether the "drag away" part of this is still relevant.

- Also noted at that time: *The Horseman has a serious knock back. Just back into the maw of the tree and fight inside the tree*. Probably also no longer relevant -- knockback  doesn't seem to be a feature of the PQ version.
