## Strategy

It's linked with [[Primogen Daishirou]]

She is rooted in place and whoever is closest to her will be taking the hits. So nontanks need to try and keep max melee distance as to not stress the healers. Keep far away from [[Primogen Daishirou]] or deadly AEs will wipe your group.
