## Notes

Does not spawn until after [[Charanda (Epic)|Charanda]] has been killed.

This is a one-time attempt encounter.

## Strategy

Selective Target Burn, Tank & Spank

- Raidforce should all charge in at the start of the encounter, tanking near where Kildiun is initially standing in the room.

- Starting at around 90% somewhere in the room [[A barrel roller]] will spawn and begin to slowly roll a barrel towards someone in the raid. Text will appear that "**PLAYER sees something incoming to their position! They might want to point them out for the rest of the raidforce!**". The raid should target through this person and burn down the barrel roller.
  - The barrel roller will stop moving towards the player once the player targets and begins attacking them.

- Kildiun has an ability to force players to target him, when this happens the player (or entire raid to be safe) should joust around the room until normal targeting returns to burn the roller down.

- At least **ONE** barrel roller needs to be killed before Kildiun dies, or the encounter will automatically fail. The rest of the barrel rollers could theoretically just be kited around the room as the raid burns down named, but they are relatively easy to kill and recommend just killing as they spawn.
