## Strategy

This is the challenge mode of the final boss [[The Final Arbiter]] in the instance.

To trigger the challenge condition, defeat the four waves of dragon-kin that come out of the portal and engage with [[The Final Arbiter]] until red text appears ***You must close the portal quickly before things get worse! [challenge].*** May need to back-off on damaging [[The Final Arbiter]] to get to this point, takes at least 30 seconds from the time the text appears for it to change red and turn into the epic.

This named has a very high critical mitigation, so even with decent dps classes if they do not have over 275 critical bonus it may take a bit longer than the other named in the zone.

Basically a tank and spank, but the portal remains open. At best adds spawn at 20% health that come through and again at 10% health. If dps is too low the spawns will start earlier, though it appears at some point the portal becomes interactable and you can 'disable' it like the standard [[The Final Arbiter]] fight.

He has a knockback, so recommend piling into the cubby directly behind the portal. Our general strategy is normal dps until about 22% then pop AOE blockers and start the burn. Soon as the adds spawn have the tank try to pick them up and keep focus on the named.
