## Lore

Vykel appears, from in-game dialogue, to be the father of Miragul. It also appears that Vykel drowned, possibly in the presence of Miragul, and his soul blames his son for failing to save him.

## Strategy

The tank should have their back against a wall the entire fight. The boss only does a knockback in melee range. Around 50% he spawns an add and only the tank needs to pick it up while everyone continues to dps the boss.
