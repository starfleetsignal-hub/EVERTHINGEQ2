## Strategy

During combat, Toa, Brasoh or Keewl may emote

> &lt;Name&gt; gains an electric charge through their chassis! You may want to avoid hitting them or you will be shocked!

Failure to heed this warning results in a power drain. A few seconds later you'll see

> &lt;Name&gt; is no longer electrically charged!
