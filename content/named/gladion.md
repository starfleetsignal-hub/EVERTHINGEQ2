## Strategy

After killing the three [[Reverent Controller]], Gladion becomes a non attackable NPC quoting the following text:
"My mind, it's clear ... finally. Those things had enslaved me for their own amusement, thank you for freeing me. I must take my leave now and return to my people, but I wish you more luck in this wasteland than I have been granted."
