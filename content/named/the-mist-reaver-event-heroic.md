## Strategy

**The Mist Reaver** has *Manaskin*, which absorbs all damage when its power is above 75%.  Using a mana drain, like [[Ethermancy]], will eliminate this.

A [[Smothering mist]] will spawn and slowly drain your primary attribute (AGI, INT, STR, WIS).  When your primary attribute reaches 0, you will die.  Killing the [[Smothering mist]] appears to reset your primary attribute.  The [[Smothering mist]] can respawn.
