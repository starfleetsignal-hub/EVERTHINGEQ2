## Strategy

### Trash mob Fight

- Try to fight mobs in ground where is not slippy in case you pull too much mobs or named accidentally.

### **Inquisitor Barol**

- This boss seems very different with [[Solusek Ro's Tower: The Obsidian Core (Solo)|solo version]]
- Pull him to where is not slippy and start to fight
- When start fighting. **Barol** uses "**Saving Power**" , "**High Power Level"**, "**Malicious Design Damage**"
- **Malicious Design Damage** deals about 10M damage to every player overtime(about 6 sec a tick) group heal overtime can deal with it
- Keep an eye on **chat window** closely. When says "*Inquisitor Barol begins saving his power to use it against you! You should attempt to keep this power drained*", he will restore mana 15% every 4 sec.  If he reaches above 79%,he deals 250M damage to a random player.
- It is not a curse so [[Everquest2:Category:Death Prevention (Spell Type)|death prevent]]/[[Everquest2:Assassin AAs#%22Dragon%22|AA tree Persistence]] in "Dragon" works fine with it.
- If any Mage players in your team, use **[[Absorb Magic]]** every time his mana above 50% and he kills no one else.
