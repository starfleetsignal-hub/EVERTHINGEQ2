## Notes

- The character who clicks on the totem will die.

## Strategy

- She has a 3k cold AE attack, and has an amazing health regen rate. Try to have the tank turn her away from the raid, her AE can be jousted, but if you're quick on the cures (Elemental) you should be ok.
