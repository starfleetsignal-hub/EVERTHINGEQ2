## Strategy

- Rampage Kar uses the ability Swifttail Bronze Rampage, which hits multiple times for roughly 250 health.  He also uses the Swifttail Copper Kick that Durasik used, which does around 2K hp damage and stuns. He also has a lot of health, and mage classes may have trouble burning him down.

## [[Easter Eggs]]

- As his fellow Swifttail monk [[Crush "The Icescale" Lizzard]] is a reference to UFC fighter Chuck "The Iceman" Liddell, Rampage Kar may be a reference to one of Liddel's UFC rivals, Quentin "Rampage" Jackson.
