## Strategy

- Three players are required, all in different raid groups, arranged in a line about 10 m apart (about the distance of the brown floor squares): left player (LP), middle player (MP), & right player (RP). LP & RP must not be fighters.
- LP + RP do not need to attack.
- The MP catches the Construct and centers him between the others => LP  --  mob & MP  --  RP.
- Shortly the Construct will make an emote, and two large bubbles will form.
- Quickly, the LP & RP have to run into the bubbles; however, with proper positioning, the bubbles will spawn right on them.
- If the Construct is right between the bubbles, it can now be damaged. He may need slight re-positioning for him to be considered properly centered. There will be an on-screen message when the Construct's immunity is gone: "The Construct of Malice is zapped by the interference between Malice and Spite leaving it able to be harmed!".
- I believe that the two little adds must be kept alive during this fight for the script to run correctly. Be gentle with them, they are delicate.

With this strategy, mentored at max level, it should now be a one-shot.

![Place the left player on the left arrow tile, place the right player on the right arrow tile. Middle player (the tank) should use a non-AOE agro ability like a Snap Taunt to position him between the two middle arrows.](images/ConstructOfMaliceStrategy.png)

- NOTE* the script will not always go as planned. It may take a few tries before both bubbles show up properly if you don't cure curse.
