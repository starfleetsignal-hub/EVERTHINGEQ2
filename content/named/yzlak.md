## Special Attacks

### Aura of Fluxing Energy

Effects:

- When any damage is received this spell has a 11% chance to cast Fluxing Energies on target's attacker. Lasts for 4.0 seconds.
  - Inflicts 3,625 - 5,437 magic damage on target.
  - Inflicts 3,625 - 5,437 cold damage on target.
  - Roots target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Root effects of 12.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 1.3 seconds.

### Divine Blast

Effects:

- Inflicts 5,095 - 7,642 divine damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4.5 seconds.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 10.0% instantly and every 4.5 seconds.
- Slows targets in Area of Effect by 70.0%.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Frustrating Madness

Effects:

- Forces target to change their selected target to caster.
- Prevents target from changing targets

### Icy Tempest

Effects:

- Inflicts 231,842 - 430,564 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 170,831 - 317,258 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 9 meters
- Inflicts 139,454 - 209,181 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 125,509 - 188,263 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is between 9 and 15 meters away
- Inflicts 94,131 - 115,049 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 78,443 - 95,875 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is between 15 and 200 meters away

### Infuriating Madness

Effects:

- Forces targets in Area of Effect to change their selected target to caster.
- Prevents targets in Area of Effect from changing targets
