## Strategy

Prince Urbaniok the Just - Is Paladin Class

Prince Strixus the Wise - Is Monk Class

Prince Kulkak the Ruthless - Is Berserker Class

The Princes must be tanked by a member of the same class. Perodically they change weapons during the fight to which time you must swap tanks to account for the change in damage types.
