## Buffs

### Greater Pirate Onslaught

Effects:

- Greater Pirate Onslaught grants attack power to wearer.

### Rallosian Balance

Effects:

- If Omugra the Visious, Vuzalg the Skullsmasher and Thazuruz the Limbcrusher are not kept within 6% health of each other, they will all heal for 12% of their maximum health. If they are not kept within 30% of each other, they heal to full.

### Reflection of War

Effects:

- When one of the pirate kings strikes a death blow on a player, all three pirate kings will gain 25% damage reduction per increment for a short duration. Increments up to 4 times.

## Special Attacks

### Crushing Torment

Effects:

- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 228,438 instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Inflicts 411,188 - 502,564 crushing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 411,188 - 502,563 crushing damage on targets in Area of Effect every 3 seconds.
