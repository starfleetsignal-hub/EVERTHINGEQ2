## Spawn

Strewn about [[The Maw of Mortality]], and occasionally the tunnel leading there, are six wisps called [[A watcher of Sheya|watchers of Sheya]]. Clicking on a wisp will cause it to fly to the center of the Maw and twirl around. Upon sending the sixth wisp to the center of the Maw they will begin to spin with greater intensity, creating a force-field that will spawn either Sheya T'Lorn or [[A Bloodsworn slayer]].
