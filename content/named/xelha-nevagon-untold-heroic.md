Ability and strategy information changes at stages (1-20/21+).

## Abilities

- **Overlord's Tithe**
- An incurable noxious that deals poison damage and drains power over time. It also procs Defensive Breach, which deals large amounts of magic damage.
- **Foundation of Rage**
- A buff that roots Xelha and reduces damage done by targets not occupying the same segment of the Dismal Stage.
- **Deadly Reminder**
- A curable trauma cast by [[Revenant remains]]. It deals slashing damage over time. When cured, it replaces Leap of Faith with Leap of Doubt and vice versa.
- **Leap of Faith**
- A trauma that teleports players who fall off of the Dismal Stage to Xelha's current location.
- **Leap of Doubt**
- A trauma that does nothing for players who fall off of the Dismal Stage (effectively killing them).
- **Unholy Union**
- A curable curse that lands on (2/3) players simultaneously. If one player dies while the curse is active, the other player also dies. If the curse is active when Xelha dies, the cursed player(s) die instead. Curing the curse on one player also cures the other player if they are both on the same segment of the Dismal Stage. If the players are on different segments, the not-cured player dies. Curing the curse resets the priest's Curse Cure if both afflicted players and the curing priest are on the same segment of the Dismal Stage.
- **Dismal Phage**
- An incurable noxious that deals disease damage over time. It can be transmitted to other players 10 seconds after first infection and every 20 seconds after that. Transmission to another player also removes it from the first target.

## Strategy

Every (25/20)%, Xelha teleports to a different segment of the Dismal Stage, leaving behind three [[Revenant remains]].

In stages 1-20, everyone starts with the same Leap of Faith/Doubt ability (randomly selected). In stages 21+, each player may have a different Leap ability.
