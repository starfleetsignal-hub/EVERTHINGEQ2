## Strategy

- To spawn, loot a [[Spite stone]] from [[A hate mongrel (Heroic)|a hate mongrel]], then use the stone on the golem {{waypoint 192.00, 33.23, -45.68}}.
- To dispel the curse, right click on the rotting meat he drops, or you will die.
