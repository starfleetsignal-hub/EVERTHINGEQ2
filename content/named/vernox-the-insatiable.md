## Special Attacks

### Feral Speed

Effects:

- Increases speed of caster by 30.0%.
- Increases Haste of caster by 48.0.

### Lethal Hide

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 7% chance to cast Poisonous Dander on target's attacker. Lasts for 0.8 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
- Inflicts 1,477 - 2,743 poison damage on target.
- Decreases power of target by 11.0%.
- Stuns target
- Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 2.2 seconds and duration is reduced to 0.3 seconds.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means
