## Strategy

Rather simple tank and spank fight on occasion you will see him emote "Feel the slice of my blade, as it cuts you in two!" run away when he starts casting he will stay rooted in place. Make sure to back off all mercs and pets. Only saw the AOE once during the fight as he went down fast.
