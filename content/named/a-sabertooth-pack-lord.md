## Notes

- To spawn one of these, clear any of the camps full of [[A Sabertooth battler|Sabertooth battlers]].  On clearing the camp, [[A Sabertooth elder]] will spawn.  Killing the Sabertooth elder has a chance to spawn the Sabertooth Pack Lord.
