## Strategy

joust every 10-20% hp when he emotes amount his breath back to your pillar. At 50% hp he checks to make sure you have equal number of ascensions in raid like(1/1/1/1) or wipe occurs. If you can get past 50% hp just keep jousting and dpsing til dead. Make sure you attune at start of fight with correct pillar or you die.
