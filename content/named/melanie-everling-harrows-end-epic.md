## Special Attacks

### Bound Hands

Effects:

- Inflicts 10.0% of max health in crushing damage on target instantly and every 2 seconds.
- Inflicts an additional 1.0% of max health in crushing damage on target every tick.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Decreases Potency of target by 100.0.
- This curse may spread to nearby allies!
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Chain Rake

Effects:

- A melee attack that reduces weapon damage and stuns.

## Strategy

Melanie is perma-rooted. She comes with several adds. Pull adds into closest room and kill them. Then move raid force in to kill Melanie. Adds mem-wipe. Cure the arcane.
