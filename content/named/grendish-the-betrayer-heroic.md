## Strategy

- This is an indirect fight encounter, you will never actually engage with Grendish.
- When entering the area where you can see Grendish on the far upper platform, DO NOT fly directly to him as he will instantly kill anyone that gets within his proximity.
- On the platforms throughout the area will be different groups of 'protector' dragonkin type mobs. The goal is to go platform by platform and defeat all of the protectors on each (Note that most are on different levels facing Grendish but there are two a bit higher up on the left and right from where you entered the auditorium.
- When you kill the last group of protectors, Grendish will send a swarm of his 'breath' down to where your group is at - defeating them will cause Grendish to flee.
- The chest will be on the platform he was originally resting on in the form of a 'Treasure Chest'
