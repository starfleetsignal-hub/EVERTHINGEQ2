## Notes

- According to [[Marshal Ralem Christof]], Fadid Ka'lin was a crypt raider who was turned into a zombie by [[The Peacock Club]] to stop him talking about finding an artifacthe found, the [[Ewer of Sul'Dae]]. He was married to a woman called [[Hakfsa Ka'lin]] (also called Hafsa), who took revenge on the Peacock Club by killing them with the help of an ex lover, [[Kahil Baldoras]].
