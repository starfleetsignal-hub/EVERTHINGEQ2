## Strategy

### Trash mob Fight

- First Boss on East. You can go alongside mid of the road and skip most trash mobs

Share most mechanics in [[Solusek Ro's Tower: The Obsidian Core (Solo)|solo version]]. But hits you harder.

### **Clockwork Prototype**

- Two add and respawn immediately when leaving aggro
- So you have to kill all of them in one fight
- Unfortunately,3 of them shares same abilities and statics just like duplicates

### **Ancient Clockwork Prototype**

- First named. The most difficult boss in this dungeon
- It doesn't matter which one you kill first. The key is preventing all of them changing target.
- "**Cog Block**" deals **574.7K** damage normally. Dealing **16.2M** when changing target.You can use stoneskin or death prevention in such case
- "**Clockwork Protection**" lasts for 10 second and immune all damage in **2** out of 3. So team just focus on healing and stoneskin stuff.
