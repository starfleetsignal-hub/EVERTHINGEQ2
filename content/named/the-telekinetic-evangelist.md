## Strategy

- Spawned by killing the [[A cleric of hate|clerics of hate]] and [[An acolyte of malice|acolytes of malice]] as well as [[Surrogate of Wrath]] in [[The Abbey (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|The Abbey]], then defiling the three statues. Match the color of the illuminated stage section.
- During the fight, the Evangelist hits you with an arcane DOT that renders you unable to target, slowed, and you are transported to one of the three platforms on the stage. The arcane must be cured while standing in the Dark Portal {{waypoint -202.38, 15.95, 33.57}} before you can resume attacking.
