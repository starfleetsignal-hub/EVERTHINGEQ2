## Strategy

Philuphii (pronounced "fluffy") is little more than an Epic X2 version of the Crag fight in Outer Vault.

Strat - (Best to do Pet Pull) MT outside his Cave in-between a small Rock against the wall, dps must be a good 10-15mtr from boss to reduce the effect of AOE damage the boss deals every 30 seconds or so. Blockers up at the start of the fight since he releases a 20-30k AOE that effects people within the small cave he is located at. When the adds spawn The OT needs to taunt them and lead a little ways down one of the two corridors and get the DPS to burn those down. Then swap back to boss. AOE triggers every 30 seconds or so (could be less). Boss does not have much HP so with the correct set up it wont take long.
