## Notes

At approximately 50% health Advux will spawn three [[A virulent swarm|virulent swarms]] (approx 3,800,000 HP), then at 20% he will spawn [[A virulent queen]] (approx 350,000,000 HP). It *appeared* that Advux regenerated some health while they were active.  If Advux is killed before the virulent queen, the queen immediately despawns.
