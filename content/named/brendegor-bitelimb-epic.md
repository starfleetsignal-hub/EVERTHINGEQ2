## Strategy

Brendegor comes grouped with [[Iilatus Frostbeard]], [[Zaashka Bradagan]] and [[Duncan Bradagan]].  To start the fight, there is a system where one player climbs onto the roof above the four and drops a chandelier on the head either Iilatus or Brendegor - be sure to pick the right chandelier.

The Bradagans must not be allowed to target the same player that is being targeted by Brendegor, or they will all use a "Cooperative Strike" AOE that will wipe the raid.  The Bradagans do not Cooperative Strike with each other and can be tanked by one tank.  Assign at least one group to Brendegor, and have the rest of the raid burn down the Bradagans.  Duncan Bradagan has somewhat less health than Zaashka and should be burned down first.  Once both Bradagans are down, both groups join in on Brendegor.

Brendegor has an AOE in which he will emote in red - players must joust out quickly.  He memwipes after the AOE, so give the tank time to regain aggro.  He has other less powerful AOEs that can still deal heavy damage to players without sufficient critmit.
