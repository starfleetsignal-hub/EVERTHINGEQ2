## Strategy

- Killing the four bixies under her will make her attackable.

- Every so often she will use an ability called 'Slam Dance' which will knock group members into the air and slam them down, which can be a one hit kill if it hits just right. The group should try to position themselves under the branch / leaves of the tree so the knockup will be partially blocked.

- Will occasionally trap a group mate in wax - switch damage over to the targetable wax prison to destroy it and free them.
