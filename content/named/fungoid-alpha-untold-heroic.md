Ability and strategy information changes between stages (1-20/21-40/40+).

## Abilities

- **pH Unbalanced**
- A buff that provides a 10% chance to cast Acidic Slime when damaged. Acidic Slime stuns the target, inflicts poison damage, and decreases power of target.
- **Sacrificial Lamb**
- An incurable arcane that inflicts magic damage over time and decreases power of targets over time. It also procs Defensive Breach, which deals large amounts of magic damage.
- **Murderous Maw**
- An incurable trauma that inflicts slashing damage over time. It deals increasing slashing damage over time to fighters, and significantly more increasing slashing damage over time to non-fighters.
- **Putrid Aura**
- A buff that increases ability doublecast avoidance, flurry avoidance, flurry, flurry multiplier, and fervor for each [[A fungoid dasher|fungoid dasher]] within 5 meters of the Alpha.
- **Clinging Spore**
- An incurable elemental that transfers to another player when it expires, if the original target is not within 5 meters of the Alpha.
- **Dasher Disaster [21+]**
- A curable curse that inflicts increasing heat damage over time. It can be cured with [[Cure Curse]] or by killing [[A fungoid dasher]].
- **Putrid Proxy [21+]**
- A buff on [[A fungoid dasher|fungoid dashers]] and [[A Fungoid Thrasher|Fungoid Thrashers]] that reduces all damage by 50% while within 5 meters of Fungoid Alpha, and increases the Alpha's increments of Putrid Aura.

## Strategy

If Fungoid Alpha takes too much damage while any fungoid allies are active, he summons [[A Fungoid Thrasher]].
