## Notes

- Kassar is a triggered spawn for the [[Ghoulbane Empowered]] or [[Gogas Afadin (Quest)|Gogas Afadin]] quests. "Rattle the bones" in the corner of  the room past the spawn point, near the uncontrollable drelocks, at {{waypoint -56, -28, -98}}. The bones can only be clicked if you don't have both quests.
