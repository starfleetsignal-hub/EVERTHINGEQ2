## Strategy

Clear the area, including the burning apparation djinn and the skeletons in the alcoves. If [[A Mistmoor emmissary]] is present, kill it first and then focus on Xalgoz. There are three green vats in the room with orbs under them. When Xalgoz gets low on heath, they will activate and the orbs become clickable. Have a person near each orb and click it to deactivate their healing effect.

## Notes

- On the standard servers, the healing effect is minimal and this mechanic can be ignored.
- Xalgoz has a knockback, so the tank should position against one of the pillars in the room.
- At some time in the past, the Named in Karnor's Castle have been rebalanced with x10 more HP than originally stated.
