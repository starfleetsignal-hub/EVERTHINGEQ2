## Strategy

- Halfway through the fight with [[Captain Wavewreck the Reckless]], he will briefly become invulnerable and then transform into Wavewreck the Abominable.
- Wavewreck the Abominable has a thornskin buff that actually does less damage than Wavewreck the Reckless' damage proc.
