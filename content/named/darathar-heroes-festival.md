## Notes

This is a 'patchwork puppet' version of Darathar appears *only* during the [[Heroes' Festival Timeline|Heroes' Festival]] annual [[Live Events|event]].

- This monster will only appear after [[Roslyn Mischeva]] appears and invites players to join forces and recreate a famous battle.
- If you defeat this monster you can buy the [[Darathar Plushie]] from the [[Heroes' Festival Merchant Items|Heroes' Festival Merchant]]

## Travel

Getting to this monster is easy.
He spawns in one of two locations, which can be accessed via the [[Travel in Norrath|world "bell"]] (globe) found on any dock.

- **[[The Commonlands]]**: near the [[The Crossroads]] at {{waypoint -588, -47, -340}}
- **[[Antonica]]**: near the [[Frontier Farm]] at {{waypoint -910, -32, 235}}

## Quests

- [[The Play's the Thing]] - an event-only quest given by Roslyn Mischeva at the location

## Achievements

- [[Mischeva's Champion]] - an achievement for which this monster is one of many to defeat.
- [[Darathar's Reprise]] - kill this specific monster

## Roslyn Mischeva's Story

Ah, our hero! Good to see you here in Antonica! The stage is set, and our tale begins! We find our heroes in the first year of our rebirth. Not one step into this beautiful new world, the great deceiver made himself known. The Sage of Ages lied to us as Lord Nagafen used us as his arrow of vengeance.

The dragon behind the mask was none other than Darathar! Darathar was the Sage of Ages, though his fearsome mien sent lesser men running for the hills, the heroes stepped in and put that dragon down!
