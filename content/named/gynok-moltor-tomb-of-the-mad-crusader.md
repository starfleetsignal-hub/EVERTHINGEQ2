## Special Attacks

### Raiment of the Gnollslayer

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 5% chance to cast Thunderous Bolt on target's attacker. Last's for 5.0 seconds
  - Interrupts target
  - Throws target back
  - Inflicts 15,900 magic damage on target.
  - Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.

### Touch of Death

Effects:

- Inflicts 436,393 - 1,309,180 - disease damage on target.
- Inflicts 332,490 - 997,471 - disease damage on target every 3.3 seconds.
- Decreases power of target by 100.0%.
- Decreases power of target by 15.0% every 3.3 seconds.
- Heals caster for 643,458.
- Decreases Effective Level of target by 1.0.

## Strategy

Activate the encounter using a switch (looks like an arm) in the side room.

He comes with 4 adds which have various abilities so it helps killing the adds first, you will have to kill Gynok and adds in order to win this encounter. He has a knockback so fight in a corner if possible. These mobs drain power like crazy so having some kind of power regen is a must or you won't be able to heal. It's critical to have high aoe dps or high dps in general to dispatch the mobs quickly. When you kill adds they just drop on the floor but Gynok is able to resurrect them and they will join the fight. We ended up killing all adds and then fought Gynok and he resurrected one of the adds. Once Gynok died, we killed the final add.

[[Marn Moltor]] - 8,500,000 HP

[[Vin Moltor]] - 5,700,000 HP

[[Zathra Kalystin]] - 10,900,000 HP

[[Nax Sorast]] - 5,300,000 HP

Total encounter including Gynok Moltor parses to 48,900,000 HP. I'm sure with a better worked out strat to deal with healers, this number will come down.

Marn (37k hps) and Zathra (55k hps) heal like a boss and Gynok heals as well (but not high hps). Vin and Marn dps and are in fact pretty close in dps to Gynok. Nax drains power like no tomorrow so it's imperative to kill him first (he drained about 45-85k per hit, approximately 30 sec into the fight). Gynok also does power drain and pretty often, hits about 4-32k per hit).
