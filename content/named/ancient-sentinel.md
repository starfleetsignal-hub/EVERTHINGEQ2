## Special Attacks

### Touch of Velium

- Inflicts 70% of max health in magic damage on target instantly and every 10 seconds.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Decreases haste of target by 41.2.
- Decreases Reuse Speed of target by 33.0%.
- Slows target by 215.0%.
- If this spell expires naturally you will be encased in a velium prison., if not you will be hit with explosive shards.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Velium Blast

Effects:

- Inflicts 140,063 - 326,815 slashing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
- Inflicts 142,287 - 213,430 slashing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
- Inflicts 133,394 - 200,091 slashing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
- Inflicts 105,048 - 128,391 slashing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
- Inflicts 55,025 - 67,253 slashing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
- Inflicts 38,906 slashing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
- Heals targets in Area of Effect for 13,552.
  - Any point not covered in the above effects.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 15.0%
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Targets in Area of Effect can not cast hostile spells.
