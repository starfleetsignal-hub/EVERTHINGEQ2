## Special Attacks

### Abyssal Flames

- Inflicts 41,213 - 68,689 heat damage on targets instantly and every 2 seconds.
- Dazes target
  - If Target is not Epic
- Resistibility increases against targets higher than level 109.

### Abyssal Frostbite

Effects:

- Inflicts 482 cold damage on target.
- Inflicts 14,460 cold damage on target every 5 seconds.
- Decreases power of target by 1.0%.
- Decreases power of target by 35.0% every 5 seconds.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Abyssal Rot

Effects:

- Inflicts 482 disease damage on target.
- Inflicts 14,460 disease damage on target every 5 seconds.
- Decreases power of target by 1.0%.
- Decreases power of target by 35.0% every 5 seconds.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Abyssal Wizardry

Effects:

- Inflicts 482 magic damage on target.
- Inflicts 14,460 magic damage on target every 5 seconds.
- Decreases power of target by 1.0%.
- Decreases power of target by 35.0% every 5 seconds.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Gozak's Aura

Effects:

- When damaged this spell will cast Nether Bolt on target's attacker. Lasts for 1.5 seconds.
  - Disarms Primary, Secondary and Ranged slots of target.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 12,306 - 22,853 magic damage on target.
  - Inflicts 4,871 - 9,046 magic damage on target every 1.5 seconds.
  - Target is immune to beneficial spells.
  - Stifles target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stifle effects of 4.5 seconds and duration is reduced to 0.5 seconds.
- On a melee hit this spell will cast Gozak's Strike on target of attack. Lasts for 1.5 seconds.
  - Inflicts 200.0% on max health in magic damage on target instantly and every 0.7 seconds.
    - If target is not (Defender of Kazar)
  - Inflicts 1.0% of max health in magic damage on target.
    - If target is Defender of Kazar
  - Inflicts 19,500 magic damage on target.
    - If target is Defender of Kazar
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Nether Cloud

Effects:

- Inflicts 158,090 - 416,784 poison damage on target.
- Inflicts 86,020 - 226,779 poison damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 10 meters
- Inflicts 85,808 - 159,359 poison damage on target.
- Inflicts 53,260 - 98,912 poison damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If target is between 10 and 15 meters away
- Inflicts 47,343 - 87,922 poison damage on target.
- Inflicts 29,589 - 54,951 poison damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If target is between 15 and 9,999 meters away
- Decreases Mitigation of target vs physical damage by 3,600.
- Your spells have reduced effectiveness and longer casting and reuse times.

### Ripping Voidclaws

- Inflicts 197,824 - 461,590 slashing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - If target is closer than 10 meters
- Inflicts 121,738 - 182,607 slashing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - If target is between 10 and 15 meters away
- Inflicts 91,304 - 136,955 slashing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - If target is between 15 and 100 meters away
- Decreases Crit Chance of targets in Area of Effect by 200.0.
- Decreases Potency of targets in Area of Effect by 90.0.
- Decreases DPS of targets in Area of Effect by 50.0.

### Stunning Blaze

Effects:

- Inflicts 216,085 - 264,104 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Stuns targets in Area of Effect
- Resistibility increases against targets higher than level 109.

## Strategy

The [[Channeling Crystal of the Sullonites]] is mandatory for melee fighters for this fight to avoid getting one-shotted by Gozak's Strike.

- get many sullonite crystal in your team (at least 2 or 3 in the group) in order to get morph in a "tank" that can hold aggro from Gozak away from the rest of the group
- each owner of the green shard will cast the shard and become tank asap the previous is no more morphed or maybe a litle sooner before...
