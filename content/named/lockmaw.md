Lockmaw is a Tier 2 raid boss.

## Stats

- 113 Flurry Avoidance
- 330 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 9434 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a hard time limit of 60 minutes.
- Shortly after pull, Lockmaw summons all remaining crocodiles from the Salt Mire.
- Blob-like monsters named [[A smelly chum puddle]] spawn throughout the Salt Mire, one at a time. Each chum puddle targets a random player and slowly moves towards that player. If the chum puddle reaches its target, it grants one increment of **Feeding Frenzied** to Lockmaw, which reduces Lockmaw's incoming damage by 15% per increment. The name of the chum puddle's target is listed in the tag below its name.
- Lockmaw casts an incurable curse named **Lockmawed** on a random player, which causes Lockmaw to target that player, regardless of threat levels. If the targeted player dies before the curse expires, Lockmaw gains an offensive and defensive buff.
- Lockmaw casts a curable trauma named **Snapping Maw** which deals growing piercing damage to all targets in front of or flanking Lockmaw.
- Lockmaw casts a curable trauma named **Tail Whack** which deals growing crushing damage to all targets behind Lockmaw.
- Lockmaw casts a Barrage which can only be blocked with [[Bulwark of Order]].
