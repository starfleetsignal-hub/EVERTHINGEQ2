## Notes

One of leading members of the *Knights of Thunder*. It was during one of his many adventures, a crusade into a cursed bunker of the hills in [[The Commonlands]], that Lord Chesgard would claim his greatest prize, [[Bone Bladed Claymore]]. Unfortunately for all of his order, the blade was corrupt and would eventually twist Chesgard's soul and bring ruin to [[Stormhold]] and the Knights of Thunder.
