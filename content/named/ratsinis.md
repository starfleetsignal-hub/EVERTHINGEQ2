## Notes

- As with many SOE / EQ2 names, Ra'Tsinis is a real-world word spelled backwards. In this case, the name of the mob is a thinly-veiled reference to the 1982 classic video game [Sinistar](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinistar).
