## Strategy

This is a ring event, with five waves of mobs before the name spawns.

### Easy mode

Every 25 seconds, Anathraxxis puts a huge, blockable, slashing/noxious/elemental AOE (Spew Bile) on members of the group that must be cured quickly to prevent massive damage. It can also be prevented/delayed by interrupting/stunning Anthraxxis, or by jousting beyond 25m.

Once below 80% health, and periodically thereafter, ooze will form under someone (announced in a red message). This will continually bounce anyone near it up into the air if they move at all, causing them to get interrupted. If not stunned or interrupted, an intermittent stifle will also hit the group.

To start, tank should stand just under Anathraxxis' original location with other members at 45 degrees near one edge.  As ooze spawns, group members near the ooze should move to the other side.  If ooze spawns on the tank, the tank should move to where the group is not.  Think of the positioning in this fight as a triangle: the tank at one point, the other group members at another point, and the ooze at the last point.

Block as many rounds of Spew Bile as you can, if no block is up then stay way out. Healers should try to joust out.

A good technique is to start the fight by mezzing Anathraxxis, and killing his adds. Then keep him stifled throughout the rest of the fight as much as possible.

Kill the adds first, in this order: [[A repairing mass]], [[A coagulating mass]], [[A regenerating mass]].

### Hard mode

Additional adds will spawn periodically once Anathraxxis Fetidspine is below 50% health.
Note that because Anathraxxis is an epic in hard mode, Spew Bile cannot be prevents solely by chain stunning due to epic immunities.
