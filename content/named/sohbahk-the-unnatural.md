## Notes

After killing him [[A blue glowing feather]] near the [[Pool of the Dead]] can now be clicked and it will grant the player the ability [[Blessing of A'Sul]]

## Strategy

When fighting him avoid standing near the edge, as he has a attack that will throw the player(s) off the side of the wall
