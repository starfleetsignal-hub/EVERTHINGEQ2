## Notes

- Yells out, "Someone ran out all of my customers!  Someone is going to pay the tabs... with their blood!  Hahaha!", when spawned.
- [[Very Watered Down Cocktail]] will spawn on the bar when this mob is killed.

## Strategy

He is a [[Bruiser]].  The Sadistic Bartender can spawn level 70 allies (Dry Ice Patch, Ether Cloud, Scorch). These allies are rooted in place and do continous damage as long as you stay on top of them. They are usually proceeded with a taunt, for example, the animation of yourself being hit with a snowball, followed by yourself apologizing. At that point you can move and avoid the summoned ally, but if you don't you'll be rooted and slowed and take damage until you move away.

A good strategy is to attack him at the fireplace and slowly move down the hall towards the entrance, avoiding the spawned allies at each others feet as you move together in your group.
