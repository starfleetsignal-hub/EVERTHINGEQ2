## Special Attacks

### Dread Aura

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 7% chance to cast Dread Lightning on target's attacker. Lasts for 12.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 5,735 - 10,650 mental damage on target.
  - Roots target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Root effects of 36.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 4.0 seconds.

## Notes

- This mob initially appears in his NPC form, accompanied by a named Erudite NPC (Varifor T'Sarn). They will become attackable only when approached/hailed by a sufficiently large group. Exactly how large is yet to be determined. Three players (with mercs) aren't enough, whether in a group, or split into a "raid" of 3 groups.
- To fight Klaaktuus in easy mode, do not kill Varifor T'Sarn. Killing him will trigger the hard mode version of this encounter, [[Klaaktuus Unbound|Klaaktuus Unbound.]]
