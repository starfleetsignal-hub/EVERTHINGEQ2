## Strategy

- AE poison DOT (like the one used by [[Venekor (Younger)|Venekor the Younger]] in [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]]
- High damage area-effect Divine damage with stun
- Summons players that die or are outside the room (Also, summons random players within the room to statue locations.)
- Multiple adds
- [[Crushing]] auto-attack
- If you can make it to the North West corner of the room there is a flower pot. Try to jump behind it. I did it with 4 characters; we all jumped in that pot and no one was ported or bounced around the room like ping pong balls.
