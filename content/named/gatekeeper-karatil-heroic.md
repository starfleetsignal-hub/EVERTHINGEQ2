## Strategy

### Trash mob Fight

- First Boss on North. You have to kill 4 "Primordial Malice" trash to make **Karatil** attackable
- Drag those trash away from **Karatil** about 20m in case **Karatil** get aggressive before you prepared

### **Karatil**

- This boss share most mechanics in [[Solusek Ro's Tower: The Obsidian Core (Solo)|solo version]]. But hit you harder.
- When start fighting. Karatil use "**Burning Flesh**" , "**Burning Sand"**, "**Sinking Feeling**" all of them are DoT(Damage over time). His target gets increments overtime.
- Healer keeps healing and you will get through it. If your team difficult healing that one, try to tank from one to another.
