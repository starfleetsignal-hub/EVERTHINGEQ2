## Strategy

Taaltak is the last boss and comes at the end of these three waves

1. Wave
   - [[Commander Klaktaz]] and four Barbaric Zealots, just tank the named and burn them down.
1. Wave
   - Second is [[Commander Yutzal]] and five Barbaric Zealots, Yutzal will do a large AOE that needs to be jousted (its announced in red)
1. Wave
   - Taaltak and his six Barbarian Zealots.
   - It is key here that Taaltak must die before all of his Zealots, so once you only have two or three of them left move onto Taaltak.  He has an AOE as well that must be jousted and hits rather hard.
   - *Note :* All the adds can memwipe, so it is advisable to have a tanking class that is good at AOE to handle these.
