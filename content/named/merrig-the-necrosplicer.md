## Strategy

- Turn on Singular Focus and avoid using any AOE abilities!!
- As soon as Merrig is agro'd he will spawn a small army of [[A skeletal cobra]] and [[Merrig's reanimated bones]]. They do not hit incredibly hard so have the tank AOE taunt to keep their attention as the group burns him down. Once he's dead, you can resume using AOE abilities to kill off the adds.
