## Strategy

- Position raid force on the stairs leading into the room.
- Ward and buff MT and have the raid charge the room behind the MT.
- Have the MT stop at the top of the small steps on the far side of the room from the entrance stairs.
- Kill off the adds through MA and then burn down Imperator Ignus.
- Has 6 [[A Darkmaul trainee]] and their HPs total to approximately 580,000
