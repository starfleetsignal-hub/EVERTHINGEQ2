## Notes

1. Click the four **hanging cages** in the [[Rectory of Confession (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Rectory of Confession]] and killing the [[A bloodsconce|bloodsconces]] that emerge from them.
1. Click the light at the front of the blood pool (marked **Drain the blood**) at {{waypoint 201.89, 33.36, -67.12}}.
1. Climb down the ladder that appears {{waypoint 179.10, 33.36, -78.80}}.
1. [[Nation of the Damned]] skeletons will add throughout the fight. The Damned starts the fight with 40 increments (percent) buff of damage resistance. Each Nation of the Damned that's alive reduces the damage resistance by one increment (percent).
