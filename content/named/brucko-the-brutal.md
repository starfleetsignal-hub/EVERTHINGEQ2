### To Spawn

- Kill the ‘grisly bone’ mobs, loot the cursed femurs.
- Use the movable box to jump up to the platform at {{waypoint -106.66, -67.59, -66.58}};
- A red outline will pop in front of you with a bunch of bones you can place.
- Directly across from you (see image) there will be a bone outline/shape on the wall of the next platform, you need to place the bones that match the shape/outline you got – EG, square bones for the square. [See images]
- Once you complete the first ‘puzzle’, you do the same on the second platform, with the shape you need to match appearing underneath Brucko on the ledge across the way. Completing the second puzzle will open a bridge across to where Brucko is laying, where you can ‘investigate’ to spawn him
