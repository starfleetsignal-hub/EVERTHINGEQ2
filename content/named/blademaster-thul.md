## Factions

- +50 faction with **Maj'Dul Citizens**
- -100 faction with **The Rujarkian Orcs**

## Respawn

Respawn time about 25 minutes real world.

## Strategy

Blademaster Thul has a self buff called *Orosu* which causes lots of damage (slashing around 400 to 700) for **any damage he receive from anybody in front of him**. It is preferred to fight him with only the tank in front and all others on his back.
