## Strategy

This one is HARD!  Two conjurors at level 38 (one mentored) with master pets and nice gear, got badly owned three times before we killed him.  A druid or shaman would probably have helped more than the second conjuror as a duo.

He had five 36v and 36vv fog grimmin companions in a group. He has wicked AOE, a stun/mez, and his direct damage is high as well.  With the conjuror duo, we finally succeeded with:

- make two tank pets
- root the chieftain, and kill the group when they run over to attack us
- die when the chieftain breaks root early and AOEs us by surprise
- recast tank pets and run back to take on the chieftain without his helpers
- send both tank pets over to chieftain, stay as far away as possible, and heal pets like crazy while throwing everything we have at him.
