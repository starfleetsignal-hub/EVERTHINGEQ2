## Strategy

Comes with a bunch of [[A knotted guardian]] adds. The adds are easy to kill and don't do much dps. If you have a lot of AoE, just focus on the named. Adds had a total of 790,000 HP. Make sure you less Wuoshi pass by so she doesn't aggro your raid if it takes too long to burn Treah down.
