## Strategy

It's linked with [[Primogen Laenzuo]]

Neither are mezzable.

[[Primogen Laenzuo]] is rooted in place.

Throughout the fight they will teleport/switch places, so as a group you have to run from fighter to mage. Keep them as far away from each other as possible or deadly AEs will wipe your group.

Kill Fighter first, and then [[Primogen Laenzuo]].

[[Primogen Daishirou]] has a knockback as well.
