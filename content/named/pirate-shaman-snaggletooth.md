## Strategy

Kite & Burn

- During the fight 'shambling corpse' adds will spawn that seek out specific members of the raid. If it reaches the person it will explode and wipe the raid.

- The corpses are fairly slow moving - recommend kiting the named around the outside of the room in a clockwise style loop, being mindful of where the corpses are at in relation to the raid body.
