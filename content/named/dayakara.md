## Strategy

Lots of DPS<br>
Spawns [[A burning demon]] adds right from the beginning (^^^heroic). Off tank takes the adds.  His grp burns the adds while main tank stays on the name.  At 20% he will spawn a group of adds.  Off tank takes those adds and his group and burns them out. Adds had approximately another 1,000,000 HP, but if you fight the named mob in a corner some adds will not aggro so you can deal with them later.

Has Volcanic Fury Buff

- Inflicts 3321 - 4982 Heat damage
- Inflicts 4429 - 6643 Slashing damage
- Inflicts 4429 - 6653 Heat Damage

Unsure on timings.

## General Notes for Boxing/Soloing

I were able to box this mob with a berserker (315aa average Velious gear), mystic (220aa poor Velious gear) and a pair of wizard mercenaries. The key was to rush this as fast as possible while wards are holding off the Volcanic Fury spell.
