## Notes

Every Named in Scars of Destruction has a Placeholder that spawns instead of the Boss and does not drop the same items as the bosses, neither do they update the quests [[What a Wastes]] and [[Sodden Slugfest]].<br>
Funfact: Reference to Breezeboot Swordrattler, a gnome rogue in the Western Wastes in the original Everquest.
