## Notes

- Spawned by slaying the [[A shadow animus|shadow animuses]] that spawn from portals that appear all over the zone.
- In order to see the [[A shadow animus|shadow animuses]], you must loot [[A skull with horns]] on one of the librarian platforms in [[Repugnance's Rectory (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Repugnance's Rectory]] or near [[Wrath's Anvil (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Wrath's Anvil]], then use the charm.
- Every 25%, Igidishi will shape-shift into an animal form.
  - There will be small animal totems that spawn in the areas with dirt, roots and trees around the [[Basilica of Hate (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Basilica of Hate]]. The totems are trackable.
  - Click the totem that represents the same animal as Igidishi's form. Clicking the wrong totem will spawn a 117^^^ x4 [[A raging animus|raging animus]]. Taking too long to get to the totem allows [[An animus tangle|animus tangles]] to spawn and heal Igidishi.
- Igidishi's race is unknown, and its gender is neuter.
