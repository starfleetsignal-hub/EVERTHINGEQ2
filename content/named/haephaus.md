## Special Attacks

### Elemental Fury

Effects:

- Forces targets in Area of Effect to clear their selected target instantly and every 4 seconds.
- Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
- Inflicts 2,091 - 3,883 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 45.0% instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Targets in Area of Effect can not cast hostile spells.
- Dazes targets in Area of Effect
- Roots targets in Area of Effect
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Flames of the Forge

Effects:

- Inflicts 52,599 - 122,730 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 55,786 - 83,680 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 47,817 - 71,725 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 43,035 - 52,599 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 32,276 - 39,449 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 27,893 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
- Throws caster back
- Decreases INT, WIS, STR and AGI of targets in Area of Effect by 429.0.

### Sear

Effects:

- Inflicts 13,349 - 26,499 heat damage on target encounter.
- Inflicts 8,677 - 17,224 heat damage on target encounter every 5.3 seconds.

### Touch of Flames

Effects:

- Inflicts 26,149 - 43,583 heat damage on target encounter.
- Inflicts 18,914 heat damage on target encounter every 6.7 seconds.
- Stifles target
- Resistibility increases against targets higher than level 117.
