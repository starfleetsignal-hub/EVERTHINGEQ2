## Strategy

DPS check fight.

Will spawn [[Aquatic malformation]] adds throughout the fight, placing a **Bruised Ego** debuff on the group. If four of them are allowed to be up at the same time, the named will wipe the party.

Switch to the adds as they spawn and kill them, then resume DPS on named.
