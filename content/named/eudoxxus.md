## Strategy

Clear the two prismatic servitors on the pedastals to the left and right of the doorway as you enter the room.

Eudoxxus himself is initially non-aggressive in the center of the room so you can setup and pull him at your leisure.

During the course of the fight, Eudoxxus will have an aura appear around him and blue text will appear in your chat window that states ***Eudoxxus hardens in place and ceases to move.***

When this appears all damage from players and pets should be turned off. Appears to last for about 15 seconds. Failure to stop damage will cause two adds named broken chip to spawn and you'll have to deal with them. These adds do not despawn on Eudoxxus' death so recommend killing them if they do happen to spawn.
