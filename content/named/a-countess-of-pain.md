## Spawn

A Countess of Pain has two separate locations where it can spawn - {{waypoint -170, 25, -667}} and {{waypoint -358, 3, -802}}.

They are both spawned the same way.  Clear all the faeries in the general area and a group of faeries will spawn at the location listed.  Clear this second group of faeries and there is a chance that a Countess of Pain will spawn in a third group.
