## Spawning method

- The Black Death can only be spawned if the [[A diseased tormentor|diseased tormentors]] and [[A loathsome avenger|loathsome avengers]] are up.  If they are, clear all of them to spawn The Black Death and several [[A diseased deathbringer|diseased deathbringers]] (not linked).
