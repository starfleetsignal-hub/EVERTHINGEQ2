## Strategy

- He has a 0-10% (easy) 0-50% (heroic) HP drain reactive on spell attacks.
- Kastus can be damaged by any means but casting any offensive spell that does damage will result in him returning a one-shot kill back at you called Frostbite. If you are a caster, you can only use melee, roots, fears... anything besides damaging spells. Necros and Conjurers will find that Kastus will also immediately delete any pets that attack him.
