## Notes

Valagas (Fire Wizard)

## Strategy

Has pretty high magic dps. Casts a spontaneous combustion spell which sometimes crits over 650k. Many of his spells also crit for over 100k. It's best to keep the group away from the mob.

He casts an uncurable arcane debuff on a single player, description just says 3 seconds and "Boom!".  I've found that you move that player away from others and when the elemental ae hits, it only hits once instead of twice.  After doing this, the mob was easy to take down.

## Quests

[[Gate Uncalling]] requires him dead. If questing, save this mob until on this quest/step.
