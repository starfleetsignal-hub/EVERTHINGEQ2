## Special Attacks

### Ectoplasmic Shield

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 8% chance to cast Spectral Feedback on target's attacker. Lasts for 5.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 1,062 - 1,972 magic damage on target instantly and every 5 seconds.
  - Decreases power of target by 8.0%.
  - Decreases power of target by 4.0% every 5 seconds.
  - Roots target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Root effects of 15.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 1.7 seconds.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means
