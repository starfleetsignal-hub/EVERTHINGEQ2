## Strategy

- Encounter includes 4 [[Ratonga]] adds, all named [[Slithis]], 33^ and 33 heroic Assassins.
- Mob is a [[Warlock]] who casts [[Corrosive Strike]] but also has a group mezz and root similar to the single-target [[Cowering Bind]].
- Can also spawn as a level 33^^ goblin imposter (shown in picture).

## Lore

> There must be one amongst the horde that displays the regal beauty and opulence of the new order. I appoint me!
>
> — Queen Varronik of the Ring of Ratillik

Queen Varronik is the haughty and snide consort to [[Caertax the Deceiver|King Caertex]], leader of the Serilian Horde. The Queen is paranoid of being assassinated like the 12 queens before her. She has gone to great lengths to hire the sly and eccentric assassins known as the We. Each member of the We are named Slithis, something never found in ratonga society. The Queen is also known to keep imposters that take her place on the throne from time to time.

*from the description of [[Queen Varronik's Shoulder Pads]], an armor piece she dropped before the re-levelling of Kaladim*
