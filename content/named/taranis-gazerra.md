## Strategy

Final Boss. Taranis slashes, does minor gazer tricks (roots, fears), and gets a stackable buff every five(?) seconds or so that reduces incoming damage by 8%.  Note the glowing pillars around it; drag Taranis to one of them and it will be fully debuffed (even if it has a stack of ten buffs on it) and the pillar will deactivate.  Tank/spank, let it accumulate a bunch of buffs, drag it to a pillar, DPS, drag it to the next, etc.
Approx HP = 2,603,000
