## Strategy

Hragdold starts the fight by casting Clawswipe, a major power drain. He'll cast it about 4 times in a row, then summon adds. Of the adds, one is a priest, it must be burned down quickly or it will heal all the enemy mobs in the fight. After about 2 minutes Hragdold repeats this pattern. It is imperative to get the adds down before Hragdold starts casting Clawswipe again.

At 35% health Hragdold will start using an instant death attack on the main tank called DoomDeath. This can be blocked with death prevents.

At 10% health Hragdold will enrage and begin hitting harder.
