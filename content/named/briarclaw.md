## Spawning method

- Briarclaw's placeholder is the particular [[A great briarpaw|great briarpaw]] that spawns in his location.  Briarclaw is pretty rare, so either be patient or decide the AA isn't worth waiting him out.

## Strategy

- Tank n' spank with a merc.
