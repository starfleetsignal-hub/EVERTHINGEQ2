## Spawning method

- There is no way to spawn The Dawn Maiden without taking some hits to your faction with [[The Guardians of the Vale (Faction)|The Guardians of the Vale]].  If she doesn't spawn, you can grind faction back up while waiting for the placeholders to repop.
- Kill the *groups* of [[A vale maiden|vale maidens]] that are static spawns under the trees.  The wandering ones don't seem to be part of the spawning process.
- If you're lucky, The Dawn Maiden will spawn with one of the static groups.

## Strategy

- Comes with four friends, same level v.
- Easily soloable with a merc.
