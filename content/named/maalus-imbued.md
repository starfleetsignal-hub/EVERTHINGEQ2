### Greater Curse of Devastation

Effects:

- Interrupts target
- Decreases power of target by 20.0%.
- Decreases power of target by 85.0% every 4 seconds.
- Inflicts 20.0% of max health in disease damage on target.
- Inflicts 85.0% of max health in disease damage on target every 4 seconds.
- If you die while this curse is on you, OR this curse expires naturally it will spread to those closest to you.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Living Death

Effects:

- Shapechanges target into a minion of Maalus
- Decreases Crit Chance of target by 2,000.
- Decreases Crit bonus of target by 2,000.
- Stuns target
- Stifles target
- Target can not cast beneficial spells.
- Target can not cast hostile spells.
- You are an undead minion of Maalus.
- On any combat art or spell hit this spell may cast Death's Hand on target of attack. Triggers about 1.8 times per minute.
  - Interrupts target
  - Decreases power of target by 10.0%.
  - Inflicts 18.0% of max health in divine damage on target.
- On death this spell will cast Immortality of the Grave on caster.
  - Increases power of target by 100.0%.
    - This effect cannot be critically applied.
  - Heals target for 100.0% of max health.
    - This effect cannot be critically applied.

### Whisper of Death

Effects:

- Target will lose 66% more power when power is consumed.
- 229% of target's power consumed will also be drained from target's health.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

- During the fight, [[A greater deathknight]] spawns.  This thing is 90^^^ epic x2, but has a nasty physical (crushing) AOE, Mighty Thrashing.
