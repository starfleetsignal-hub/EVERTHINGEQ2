Slither Rattlebones is a Tier 3 raid boss.

## Statistics

- 118 Flurry Avoidance
- 337 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 9539 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a hard time limit of 45 minutes.
- The surge damage on this fight increases the farther a player is from Rattlebones.
- Rattlebones can only be damaged when standing next to an upright, intact pillar of stone.
- Every 60 seconds, Rattlebones casts **Vicious Bite** on his current target (usually the main tank), which instantly kills the target, unless the target has 2,400,000 noxious resist or higher.
- Every 5% health, Rattlebones destroys the pillar nearest to him, releasing a noxious cloud of gas. Players within this cloud are affected by **Noxious Dusting**, an incurable noxious detriment that increments over time. Each increment increases the damage from all sources and decreases the player's fervor. Increments begin ticking down when the player leaves the cloud. The pillar also releases 4 [[A disturbed burrower|disturbed burrowers]], which are immune to indirect AoE abilities.
- Every 30 seconds, Rattlebones casts **Rattling Burst**, a frontal AoE with a powerful knockback and stun effect.
- Rattlebones casts a Barrage which can only be blocked with [[Bulwark of Order]].
