## Strategy

Either dogpile in a corner or have tank in one corner and rest of raid in adjacent corner. Fans in room will blow people around if you stand too far out in the room so stay by wall during fight.

Fairly straightforward tank & spank; hits hard.

Cold resists

As stated above a straight forward Tank and Spank, if you pre-position melee classes in range of where the named will be parked, so that NO ONE needs to move during the fight you will rarely see the elemental detrimental from the falling ice.  Elemental cure potions ASAP if you get the detrimental.
