## Spawn

- Clear all the [[A Rallosian ghost|Rallosian ghosts]] from the south shore area.  When they are all cleared, four [[A Rallosian lieutenant|Rallosian lieutenants]] will spawn by an underwater rock at {{waypoint 405, -69, 1402}}.  Upon clearing the lieutenants, A Rallosian General has a chance to spawn.  If he spawns, it will be almost immediately after the lieutenants go down; if he doesn't, wait for the ghosts to respawn and try again.

## Strategy

- Accompanied by four Rallosian ghosts, same level, vvv. Easily soloable by any class.

## Notes

- Despite being a named monster, does not drop a loot chest.
