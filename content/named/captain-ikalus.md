## Special Attacks

### Cloak of the Undead Captain

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 5% chance to cast Bolt From the Dead on target's attacker. Lasts for 3.5 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 10,946 magic damage on target.
  - Decreases Mitigation of target vs physical damage by 5,425.
  - Decreases Mitigation of target vs arcane damage by 5,216.
  - Stuns target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 10.5 seconds and duration is reduced to 1.2 seconds.

### Winds of Asyphyxia

Effects:

- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 25.0%.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 15.0% every 6 seconds.
- Inflicts 147,038 - 273,070 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 75,506 - 140,225 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 10 meters
- Inflicts 90,834 - 136,251 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 45,417 - 68,126 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is between 10 and 15 meters away
- Inflicts 68,126 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 28,386 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is between 15 and 200 meters away

## Strategy

Tank and spank fight, comes with two adds. Comes with 2 [[An eternal loyalist]] that total 5,300,000 HP and 2 [[A reanimated guard]] that total 2,200,000 HP.
