## Notes

- You must have completed [[A Vicious Twist]] (The Hand of Marr) or [[Crushing Betrayal]] (The Brethren) in the [[Bloodline Chronicles Timeline|Bloodline Chronicles]] timeline to access The Crypt of T'Haen. If you are in a group, only one person in the group will need access to the dungeon.
- Stealth and invisibility can be used to avoid most of the encounters in the instance. This can be useful if your goal is to defeat her to update the [[Hunting Dangerous Prey (Shattered Lands)|Hunting Dangerous Prey]] daily quest.
