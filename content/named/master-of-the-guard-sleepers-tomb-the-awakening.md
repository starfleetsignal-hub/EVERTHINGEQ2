## Strategy

Clear the sentry constructs below and the one that wanders north and south behind him. He will occasionally use Massive Slam that appears to be a frontal PBAOE.

Important Note: Avoid killing all of the drakes on the northern and southern ledges before killing Master of the Guard, as when the final drake dies [[The Overseer]] spawns in the vicinity of Master of the Guard and paths upwards towards the north ledge. High probability you end up having to fight both named if this occurs.
