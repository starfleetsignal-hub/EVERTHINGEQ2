## Strategy

- Spawned by slaying non-aggro [[An anarchic lurcher|anarchic lurchers]]  in the zone. These are found in groups of three or six near the following locations:
  - Just east of the organ in the [[Amphitheater of Rancor (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Amphitheater of Rancor]] {{waypoint -34.47, 6.67, -87.04}}
  - In the southeast of the [[Amphitheater of Rancor (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Amphitheater of Rancor]] {{waypoint -67.15, 11.74, 4.59}}
  - Between the [[Amphitheater of Rancor (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Amphitheater of Rancor]] and [[The Abbey (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|The Abbey]] at {{waypoint -128.36, 17.15, -34.79}}, {{waypoint -110.45, 15.53, 0.09}}, and {{waypoint -143.07, 14.76, 37.14}}.
