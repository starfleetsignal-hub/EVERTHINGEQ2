Upon pulling, Mayong will summon heroic adds that should be killed as quick as possible. Have an OT for this. He also has an arcane spell (Touch of Mayong) that will decrease your level by 10. This needs to be cured as fast as possible. If your main tank gets it he/she will most likely be one shotted by Mayong. If this happens, be sure to have someone pick him up.

He has elementals as well. Cure those but it is most important to cure his arcane.

## Buffs

### Brute Strength

Effects:

- Increases STR of caster by 60.5.
- Increases DPS of caster by 33.0.

### Hardened Constitution

Effects:

- Increases STA of caster by 60.5
- Increases Max Health of caster by 25.0%.

### Remarkable Agility

Effects:

- Increases AGI of caster by 60.5.
- Caster will Dodge 3.0% of incoming attacks.

## Special Attacks

### Blanket of Eternal Night

Effects:

- Heals caster for 2.0% of max health.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 13.0%.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 5.0% every 6 seconds.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Inflicts 46,579 - 108,684 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 3,941 - 9,196 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If Target is closer than 9 meters
- Inflicts 28,664 - 42,996 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 2,389 - 3,583 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If Target is between 9 and 14 meters away
- Inflicts 18,811 - 22,991 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 1,791 - 2,190 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If Target is between 14 and 75 meters away
- Decreases Casting Speed of targets in Area of Effect by 50.0%.
- Decreases Haste of targets in Area of Effect by 35.0.
- Slows targets in Area of Effect by 41.0%.
  - If Target is between 10 and 75 meters away
- Decreases Deflection, Defense and Parry of targets in Area of Effect by 69.0.
- Decreases Mitigation of targets in Area of Effect vs all damage by 2,411.
- Roots targets in Area of Effect
  - If target is closer than 10 meters

### Mayong's Touch

Effects:

- Inflicts 15.0% of max health in mental damage on target.
- Inflicts 5.0% of max health in mental damage on target instantly and every 6 seconds.
- Decreases power of target by 15.0%.
- Decreases power of target by 5.0% every 6 seconds.
- Decreases Effective Level of target by 10.0.

### Soul Paralysis

Effects:

- Inflicts 52,252 - 87,086 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Stuns targets in Area of Effect
- Stifles targets in Area of Effect
