## Notes

- This mob is one of three possible named that have a chance to spawn near the front entrance to the Fountain of Life. To attempt to spawn this mob, clear all 5 encounters on the first level by the doorway (this includes 3 in the center of the level and one to each side of the entrance). A second wave of monsters will spawn; if the second mob consists of ghost lions then Laith will spawn, while if the second wave consists of two groups of zombies then [[Arazul, Hand of the Godking]] will spawn or [[Izur the Aegis]] if the second wave is repulsers.
