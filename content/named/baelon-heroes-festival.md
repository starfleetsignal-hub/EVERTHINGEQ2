## Notes

This is a 'patchwork puppet' version of Baelon appears *only* during the [[Heroes' Festival Timeline|Heroes' Festival]] annual [[Live Events|event]].

- This monster will only appear after [[Roslyn Mischeva]] appears and invites players to join forces and recreate a famous battle.
- If you defeat this monster you can buy the [[Baelon's Plushie]] from the [[Heroes' Festival Merchant Items|Heroes' Festival Merchant]]

## Travel

Travel to this monster is the most difficult of those for the event if your character is a low level, because he is near [[The Fearcreep]].

- Go to [[The Feerrott]] and head to the area with the [[Travel in Norrath|Ulteran Spire]]. Near the spire is a rift that allows for to travel to the Eidolon Jungle. Once in the jungle then use a [[Mount|flying mount]] to head up to the Feercreep.
- Other options
- [[Call of the Veteran]] (CoV) - ask someone who is at the location to use CoV to bring you to them
- [[Guild Strategist]] - if your guild has this [[Guild Hall Amenities|amenity]] for the [[Guild Halls|guild hall]], a higher level member can travel and plant the flag for other members.
- Use the [[Travel in Norrath|Druid Ring]] to Obol Plains and run/leap to the Tourbillion which will transport you to the Eidolon Jungle. From there you can run/leap to the mob.

## Roslyn Mischeva's Story

To say that Ethernere is a strange place would be an understatement. It is only here that a being such as Baelon could come to power. Cazic-Thule had died, oh not for us, not here, but in another thread, another dimension. This not-our-Cazic began to merge with Ethernere and cause it to take on aspects of the Plane of Fear. From this came Baelon, a devout Cazicite hoping to ascend as the Avatar of Fear. He guarded the Temple of Fear zealously, and spread rumors that he was in fact Cazic-Thule in the flesh.

Playing the part of Cazic, Baelon sent out the loyal followers who had died with Cazic and gathered at the Temple out into Ethernere to spread fear and horror and dread throughout the land. At that, he was successful, and Cazic's followers were able to anchor the Plane of Fear to Ethernere itself. With their foothold secure, it was only a matter of time before Cazic would awaken and raise Baelon to the status of Avatar.

What Baelon didn't count on was the arrival of heroes from the living world. Piercing the barrier of death, they brought power to bear that the wayward could never hope to wield. In unraveling the Drinal problem, they found that Cazic-Thule's body arriving in Ethernere was a primary catalyst to Drinal's ascension. The heroes discovered the Plane of Fear slowly changing Eidolon Jungle to reflect the madness of Cazic and his followers, and so confronted the source in the Temple of Fear. Not Cazic, but Baelon did they find, a pretender to Cazic's throne. It was there in the Temple of Fear that the heroes took Baelon down, ending his reign over the wayward of Eidolon Jungle.
