## Strategy

Kodux and Zarda are linked and both ^^^x4.

This fight requires two tanks and you also need two others who can peel or stick. You need to fight these two mobs in the corners as far away from each other as possible tanks facing tword the middle and groups on the edge of waterfall to maintain LOS. If they come together they turn glowie and do about 9K damage and sap about 6K power. They can come together two ways. First if one of the two has more that 20ish% more damage than the other they will run to each other. Also they randomly port people to the center of the room. When the port someone they also target that person. The ported person needs to either bring the mob to the proper corner or stand their until the tank has regained control. (yes you will probably die but its better than a raid wipe) If you run to the wrong corner you will bring them together and make them glowie. The hard part is if one of the tanks is ported by the other tanks mob. That is when a monk can peel the mob back to the right corner or you can have the mob stuck to the correct tank. Keep your tanks alive and keep your dps up and this fight is not so bad.
