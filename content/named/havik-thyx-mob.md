## Strategy

- He's level one, and you'll be in your 70s.  To lose to him you would have to mentor down to level 5, unequip your gear, and even that might not be enough to lose to him.
- Start with the tank with his back against the nearby cliff, position your healers and ranged DPS behind the tents out of LOS. Melee DPS will need to attack in spurts of 5 seconds before needing to run into the river and cleanse the ticking un-curable debuff.
