Ability and strategy information changes between stages (1-20/21+).

## Abilities

- **Breathless Fealty**
- A curable elemental that kills the target if cured. It also causes the target to "drown", displaying the breath bar. When it expires, it is replaced with a curable arcane of the same name, which inflicts divine damage over time.
- **Thralls to the Wall**
- A buff that increases combat mitigation, ability doublecast avoidance, flurry avoidance, flurry, flurry multiplier, and fervor for each living thrall. At 10 increments, the entire party is killed instantly.
- **Blast Femur**
- A curable trauma that slows target by 100.0%, disarms the Legs slot, and reduces the range of all abilities. It kills the target if they are also hit with Forlorn Hope.
- **Despotic Diatribe**
- An incurable detriment that targets the player closest to the southeast red crystal, which converts ability costs from power to health.
- ****
- An incurable detriment that targets the player closest to the southwest yellow crystal, which prevents the target from casting beneficial abilities.
- **Forlorn Hope**
- An incurable curse that targets the player closest to the northwest black crystal, which deals direct damage.
- ****
- An incurable detriment that targets the player closest to the northeast blue crystal, which drains power.
- **Is Statue? (21+)**
- An incurable noxious that prevents the target from seeing the Paragon. It is removed by destroying a statue of themselves somewhere in the benches around the Paragon. While this effect is active, ALL players see flashes of black across their screens.
- **Paragon's Protection (21+)**
- A dispellable buff present on [[A fortified thrall]] that increases combat mitigation, ability doublecast avoidance, and flurry avoidance.
- **Unrelenting Attack (21+)**
- A buff present on [[An advancing thrall]] that reduces combat mitigation, but increases flurry, flurry multiplier, and strikethrough.

## Strategy

The Paragon summons [[A fortified thrall]], which can only be damaged when a fighter is NOT in defensive stance, and [[An advancing thrall]], which can only be damaged when a fighter IS in defensive stance. Thralls spawn (1/2) at a time.

At (54%/71%), the four crystals in the corners of the room activate, each applying a different effect to the player closest to it.
