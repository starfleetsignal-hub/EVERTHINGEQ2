## Strategy

Captain Mergin will be very close to the elevator on the third floor - recommend having the raid standby on the second level and having a puller go face pull all the trash from upstairs around the named down to the raid before the raid force proceeds up.

- There are (4) large vats up on this ledge that are critical to this fight. They will randomly start to emit steam during the fight, and the raid should run the named over to this vat until green text is seen for ***You hear a hissing sound as the nearby vat gets doused with cold water***. Rinse and repeat for this part of the fight.

- Additionally, the Captain will get a buff called **'The Rum is NOT Gone**' which will render him immune to damage - these can accumulate though they are easy to manage and remove as long as they are called out as soon as they appear. Have mages (and shamans if they're spec'd) dispel this as it appears.
