## Abilities

- **Zon Zobbonk!**
- An incurable arcane that deals magic damage and decrease power over time. It also procs Defensive Breach which deals large amounts of magic damage.
- **Void Conduit**
- A buff that increases Mozal's Potency and Fervor based on its current percent of power. It increases Mozal's power by 1% every 3 seconds for each [[Eyes without a face]] that isn't charmed, feared, mesmerized, or stunned. It decreases Mozal's power by 10% when an [[Eyes without a face]] dies.
- **Probing Defenses**
- A buff that has a chance to cast Breakthrough on Mozal's target, which deals massive damage to non-fighters.
- **Omni Orbit [buff]**
- A buff that reduces power damage, increases Ability Doublecast Avoidance, increases Flurry Avoidance, and increases Combat Mitigation per increment. It can be removed by a mage who clicks on an Eye of Mozal, a pillar that appears within the combat area. If all orbits  are active, the group instantly dies.
- **Omni Orbit [detriment]**
- An incurable detriment that increases all damage done to target by 70%.
- **Elemental Void**
- A buff on [[Eyes without a face]] that increments when receiving cold or heat damage. The buff reduces elemental damage by 70%. On death, it releases an Elemental Vortex which inflicts immitigable cold damage to health and power, multiplied by increments.
- **Sight Unseen**
- A curable noxious that inflicts disease over time and reduces healing received by 20%. It can only be cured when afflicted with Omni Orbit [spell text incorrectly says when NOT afflicted]. It also prevents the target from seeing Mozal or the eyes.
- **Ogle the Mind**
- A curable trauma that wounds the target's power pool by 60%. It can only be cured when afflicted with Ommi Orbit [spell text incorrectly says when NOT afflicted].
- **Frozen Stare**
- A curable arcane that mesmerizes the target and drains power. After 5 seconds, it also increases Mozal's power. It can only be cured by mages (no effect if cured by other classes).
- **Heat Projection**
- **Cold Projection**
- **Magic Projection**
- **Optical Overload [21+]**
- A buff that increases flurry, flurry multiplier, melee multiplier, and strikethrough multiplied by increments. It increments by 1 per second if any vein is not controlled by charm, fear, mesmerize, or stun. It decrements by 1 per second if all veins are controlled by charm, fear, mesmerize, or stun. The minimum number of increments equals the number of defeated veins. If all veins are killed, Mozal kills the entire group.

## Strategy

(Stage 21+) At 25%, Mozal summons 4 [[Vein of Mozal|Veins of Mozal]]. Dispelling a vein transforms it from living to undead and vice versa.
