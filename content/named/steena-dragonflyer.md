## Strategy

Steena Dragonflyer's Buzz hits you for 1020390064 poison damage. This is a Frontal. Quickly move out of the way as its only a 2-3 second casting. Burn the adds with AoE's as they don't take too much, but they can build up fast. He seems to call in adds every 10% or so.
