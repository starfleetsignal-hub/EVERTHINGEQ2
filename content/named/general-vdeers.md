## Lore

- In the days of *EverQuest*, when the Dark Elves controlled the shipyards, General V'Deers would lead a patrol of dark elf dragoons around The Overthere every night, ruthlessly killing everyone he encountered who did not have the favor of Neriak. Presumably killed in The Rending, he continues his nightly patrol, only now out to kill every living thing that crosses his path.

## Strategy

- Do not try to solo as a caster or ranger. He has a ranged poison attack that can stun/stifle/root you which also can do 2k damage on you.
