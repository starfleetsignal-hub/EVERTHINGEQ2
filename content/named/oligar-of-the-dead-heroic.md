## Strategy

- Oligar will at times "face" a groupmember (red text). This person must joust away sideways, the group must stay out of line-of-sight between the two and not be in front of him (he will turn towards the person).
- at ~70% 3 statues will pop. Kill the right one first (memwipe until down!), then the center statue (might remove a stoneskin effect?).
- Do not kill all 3 statues, i.e. leave the left one up or they'll instantly respawn, he is killable with the right and center ones dead, not sure about other combinations.
- DPS Oligar until (either you or him) are defeated.
