## Strategy

- There are four inactive golems in the room with her - two [[A shattered sculpture|shattered sculpture]]s, [[A frozen guardian]] and [[A wall of ice (Permafrost)|a wall of ice]].
- When Alangria reaches 80% health she activates two of the golems.
- At 20% life she activates the other two golems.
- Casts an AoE every 20 seconds, it can be avoided at max range.
