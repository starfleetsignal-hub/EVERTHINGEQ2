## Strategy

- Shahrivar has a mana drain, so it's best to fight him in the small room halfway up the stairs to where he is located, which has a machine in it that will replenish your mana.

- it seems he can also drain the power of the thing giving power to the group, if the tank stands in between the mob and the power feeder, the mob can't power tap the feeder.
