## Special Attacks

### Abyssal Link

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a chance to cast Abyssal Feedback on target's attacker. Lasts for 7.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Throws target back
  - Inflicts 8,058 - 14,965 magic damage on target.
  - Inflicts 3,189, - 5,924 magic damage on target every 7 seconds.

### Nether Cloud

Effects:

- Inflicts 101,075 - 266,471 poison damage on target.
- Inflicts 54,997 - 144,992 poison damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 10 meters
- Inflicts 54,862 - 101,886 poison damage on target.
- Inflicts 34,052 - 63,240 poison damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If target is between 10 and 15 meters away
- Inflicts 30,269 - 56,213 poison damage on target.
- Inflicts 18,918 - 35,133 poison damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If target is between 15 and 9,999 meters away
- Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
- Decreases Mitigation of target vs physical damage by 3,480.
- Your spells have reduced effectiveness and longer casting and reuse timers.

### Stunning Blaze

Effects:

- Inflicts 138,154 - 168,855 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Stuns targets in Area of Effect
- Resistibility increases against targets higher than level 106.
