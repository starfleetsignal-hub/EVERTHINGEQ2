Sleujess, Pride Guardian, the Desert Wildcat Matrion, is a Tier 3 raid boss.

## Stats

- 118 Flurry Avoidance
- 337 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 9194 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a 45 minute time limit.
- Sleujess casts a curable elemental named **Wildcat Wound** that deals increasing elemental damage and increases all incoming arcane damage.
- At 90%, 80%, 70%, 60%, 50%, 40%, 30%, and 20% (but not 10%), [[A wily wildcat kitten]] joins the fight, choosing to stalk the player who is furthest from Sleujess at that moment. If the kitten reaches its target, it heals Sleujess. If two kittens are up at the same time, the entire raid is killed.
- Sleujess casts a knockback that pushes all nearby players up and away from herself.
- Sleujess occasionally spins 180 degrees (putting her back to the main tank) and prepares to pounce. Her trajectory and landing spot are marked with a blue beam and a blue circle. When Sleujess lands on her target, all players standing within or near the blue beam or blue circle receive a permanent incurable detriment called **Mad Dashed** which increases damage taken from all sources by 25% per increment and decreases max health by 25% per increment.
- Sleujess casts **Mana Bleed I**, which drains power from the entire raid. This effect can be blocked with [[Raid Rune: Power Flex I]] or higher.
- Sleujess casts a Barrage which can only be blocked with [[Bulwark of Order]].
