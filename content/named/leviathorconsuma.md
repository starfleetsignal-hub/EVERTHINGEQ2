## Strategy

To spawn Consuma follow the non-aggro Yha-Lei feeder that runs between the pool and the water below (after clearing the other mobs) down to the water, fish out one of the bluefins near the Feeder and use it near the pool in the Feeding chamber to lure the Leviathan.
