Plants and mushrooms will spawn during the fight.

The tank has to keep the named away from them until the mushroom is dead, after that bring it close to the plant and kill that.

The plant isn’t damageable until the name is close, but if it takes damage while the mushroom is up, the name heals back up.

All players can cast on the move during this fight.
