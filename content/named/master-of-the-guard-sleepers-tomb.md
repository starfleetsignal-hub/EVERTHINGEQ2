## Strategy

- clear the room below him
- pet pull him
- MT grabs the boss, group is at max range for heals/dps
- OT needs to grab adds, group focuses on killing adds
- when you get the big warning message (don't remember exact text right now, you will also notice a circle on the floor, and "electrified air") the group needs to joust PARALLEL with the MT keeping same distance - like on a rail. (rinse and repeat).
