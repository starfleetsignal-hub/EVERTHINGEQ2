## Strategy

Syncall will frequently do short-range teleports during the fight, and will take whoever has aggro with him when he blinks.  Group members such as healers targeting the "passenger" will lose their targeting in the process.  Clear the hall and his room before pulling him to avoid being ported into unwanted adds nearby.
