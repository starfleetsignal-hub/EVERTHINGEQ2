Digger Grendok is a Tier 3 raid boss.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance: 525.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance: 590.0
- Resolve: 11,195.0

## Abilities

- **Digger's Strike**
- A curable trauma that deals crushing damage and increases elemental damage from all sources.
- **Clobberstorm**
- A curable trauma that deals health and power damage, dealing more damage to closer players. Grendok also shuffles his hate list when casting this spell.
- **Mind Razor's Slice I**
- A curable trauma that deals fast-growing damage over time.
- **Falling Rocks**
- A curable trauma that roots the target and decreases offensive and defensive capabilities. It also deals increasingly more damage the closer the player is to Grendok.
- **Destroy Will**
- A curable elemental that lands on players in front of Grendok. The spell stuns and knocks back the target, dealing more elemental damage if the target is farther away from Grendok.
- **Growing Influence**
- A buff that increases outgoing damage for each increment. This effect continues to increase throughout the fight. Increments can be dispelled. At 20 increments, Grendok immediately kills the entire raid.
- **Frenzied Clawspike**
- An incurable trauma that deals piercing damage over time.
- **Teamwork**: Leader
- A buff that casts Tag Team Strike on the current target, if that target already has the effect from Teamwork: Member.
- **Monitored Attention**
- A curse that is incurable while the player's target or implied target is Grendok.
- **Monitored Locale**
- A curse that is incurable while the player is within 35 meters of Grendok.
- **Spellweaved Admonishment**
- A curable elemental that deals elemental damage and increases all other damage types. This effect only lands on mages.
- **Barrage**
- An attack that can only be countered with [[Bulwark of Order]].

## Strategy

Grendok continuously summons [[An exhumed descendant]] which has the Teamwork: Member buff.
