Ability and strategy information changes between stages (1-20/21-40/41+).

## Abilities

- **Poison Pressure**
- A buff that increases fervor, flurry, and flurry multiplier multiplied by increments. Increases by one increment each time Senzu fails to inflict damage on his most hated target. Heroic opportunities initiated by scouts removes all increments. At 10 increments, it kills the entire party.
- **Toxic Glands**
- A buff that has a 10% chance (multiplied by increments) to deal damage, drain power, and drain wards. It gains one increment for each [[Eyes without a face]] that is not charmed, feared, mesmerized, stunned, or killed. At 10 increments, it kills the entire party.
- **Noxious Void**
- A buff on [[Eyes without a face]] that reduces noxious damage by 80% and increments when receiving a hostile disease or poison ability. On death, it casts Noxious Vortex, which inflicts immitigable disease damage multiplied by increments.
- **Dome of Toxicity**
- Makes Senzu immune to all damage unless (1/1/2) players maintain Eye to Eye.
- **Eye to Eye**
- An incurable detriment applied to players standing within the green circle. The detriment deactivates Senzu's buff from Dome of Toxicity, and it causes all attacks to hit the player (even if they are not the primary target).
- **Venomous Gaze**
- An incurable detriment that inflicts poison damage to targets in front of Senzu. It inflicts extra damage to targets that have Eye to Eye.
- **Grafting Gaze [21+]**
- An incurable curse that targets the two players who are furthest away from each other and any players who have Eye to Eye. All targets are teleported to Senzu. When the curse expires, if the target is in front of Senzu or within 28 meters of another cursed target, the target dies.
- **Sensory Overload [21+]**
- A buff that increases flurry, flurry multiplier, melee multipler, and ae autoattack, multiplied by increments. Defeating a vein adds 1 increment. Senzu cannot die until all veins are defeated.

## Strategy

[Stage 21+] At 25%, Senzu summons (NA/4/5) [[Vein of Senzu|Veins of Senzu]].
