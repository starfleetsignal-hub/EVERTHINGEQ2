## Notes

- Dominus Atrebe, First-hatched son of [[Dominus Rile]], Creator of the Sarnak and Sokokars is one of the four casual contested raid mobs introduced in [[LU43]].

## Strategy

Usually form the Raid or Group up near the ruins by the river where Dominous Atrebe spawns.  Clear the surrounding trash mobs as Dominous Atrebe randomly ports party members away from the party and can aggro surrounding mobs if left standing.  No real tricks other than the random party member porting, does a heavy magic attack (roughly 12-20K before mitigation) every 30 seconds or so.
