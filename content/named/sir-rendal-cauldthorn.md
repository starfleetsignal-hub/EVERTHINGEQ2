## Strategy

He is chained and rooted.

When you aggro him, above either Northern or Southern Feeding Pit a group of lights will spawn. Click on the light and a Knight of Truth will spawn. Kill the Knight.

Repeat with other lights until Sir Rendal Cauldthorn decides that's enough (as little as 2) and summons you to him. Now you can kill him.
