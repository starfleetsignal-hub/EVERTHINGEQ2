Taskmaster Peggs is a Tier 2 raid boss.

## Statistics

- Resolve: 11,015.0
- Flurry Avoidance: 470.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance: 540.0

## Abilities

- **Stalwart Admonishment**
- A curable elemental that only lands on fighters. It deals elemental damage and increases all other damage types. This effect cannot be blocked
- **Impending Storm**
- A curable elemental that deals increasing elemental damage and increases physical damage from all sources.
- **Emerging Storm**
- A curable elemental that deals increasing elemental damage and increases physical damage from all sources.
- **Cascade of Confounding**
- A curable curse that lands on Peggs' current target. The curse starts with four increments, and all increments must be removed before the curse expires, or else the entire raid is killed. While afflicted, the target has a red screen and cannot move or cast. The target is held at the bottom of the hatelist until the curse is fully cured.
- **Ranged Support**
- A buff that decreases damage taken from all sources for each [[Blackhook Voidshot]] that is active.
- **Blackhook Heartspike**
- An incurable trauma that deals piercing damage over time.

## Strategy

Throughout the fight, Peggs summons a [[Blackhook Voidshot]] to assist. These snipers can appear anywhere within Rocky Ridge. Though the spawn locations are random, they form a set rotation for the pull. The rotation is different each time Peggs is fought.

At 50%, Peggs dismounts and summons three additional mount-like animals. These adds die very quickly.
