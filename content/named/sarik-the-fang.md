## Strategy

Sarik is an endurance fight. The idea of the fight is to outlast the waves of level 74 ^^^ [[A fang pack member|fang pack members]] that Sarik will spawn periodically. Use your AOE DPS to take out the wolves while the main tank fights Sarik in a save position. When the raid DPS kills the adds they should focus back on Sarik. Our adds totaled to another 4,000,000 HP alone.

Repeat this process until the boss is defeated.
