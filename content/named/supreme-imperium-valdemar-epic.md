## Strategy

- Valdemar must be debuffed with the scout debuff, Slice of Despair, that is obtained for completing the Tower of Frozen Shadow x2.
- Valdemar spawns non-aggro adds called Flame of War.  It is very important to not use Area of Effect attacks so as not to aggro the Flames of War.  Over time, the Flame of Wars will turn into aggro mobs called Enraged Flames of War, these must be off-tanked and burned down.
