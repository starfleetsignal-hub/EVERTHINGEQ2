## Strategy

Slaying the [[An immolated armorsmith|immolated armorsmiths]] and [[An immolated weaponsmith|immolated weaponsmiths]] weakens and breaks Tothrak's armaments. These immolated are non-aggro, you can range them and they'll stay at the forge, hard at work.
