## Notes

- Normally an NPC, but becomes attackable during a step in the quest [[Saving Soles]]. If not engaged, the Magolemus will then remain for fifteen minutes, and then despawn for two hours (unconfirmed).
- [[A Projection of Najena]] spawns over its corpse immediately upon its death. All people on the quest [[Saving Soles]] should quickly hail her. The NPC does not despawn as a result of conversation options, but rather will despawn after roughly a 2-minute timer.
- Although visually a living statue, he drops giant related corpse-loot.

## Strategy

Has around ~1,000,000 HP on Varsoon TLE.

The largest concern is handling his knock-back. It is recommended that the nearby mobs be cleared and the tank quickly move the Magolemus near a rock or wall (without lava) so that the tank's back can rest against the wall. All other personnel should be behind the Magolemus the entire time.
