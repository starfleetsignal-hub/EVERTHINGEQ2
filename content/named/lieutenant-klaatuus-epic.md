### Strategy

Tank him where he stands.  Tortoise Shell / Bladedance on pull. The bird has a curse that needs to be cured.  If there are not enough healers or cures are down, have the cursed person run to the other side of the room to die or the curse will spread throughout the raid, causing a wipe.
