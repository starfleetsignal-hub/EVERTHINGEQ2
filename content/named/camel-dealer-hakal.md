## Notes

- Other spoilers mention he is aggressive towards the Court of the Blades, but some players have not experienced this.
- If he should spawn aggressive, the [[Templar]] spell, [[Soothe]], works but may not last long enough for you to read all the dialogue for a quest with him.
- If he continues to respawn as aggressive, training him away from his spawn point and then outrunning the agro should work as well. He should stand at the point where he loses agro and no longer be aggressive.
