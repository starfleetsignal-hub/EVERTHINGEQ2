#### Strategy

- Clear the room of all npcs, and keep spawning hammers cleared.
- Once room is clear, turn the Doomsmith where he stands.
- Assign one person to keep tabbing through targets to kill hammers as they spawn.

Must clear hammers, if you don't the boss will put a VERY painful heat/elemental dot on your entire party that is not curable.
