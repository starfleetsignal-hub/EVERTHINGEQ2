## Strategy

### Trash mob Fight

- You can ignore all trash along the way. Just keeping healing and warding to the boss room {{waypoint -1271.12, 294.87, -1356.32}}

### **Keeper of Past Lore**

- This boss is a little different to [[Solusek Ro's Tower: The Obsidian Core (Solo)|solo version]]. And he has a separate room(cave) too
- At least one player healer is required. Two player healers avoid the **RNG**(random number generator) curse mechanic
- When you start to fight, **"Spectral Aggrivation"**  deals damage about 10M over time to everyone. As time ticks, one of your player gets **"Ancient Curse"**. From now on, every **30s** one of your player gets it. Player healer **[[Cure Curse]]** recast time is 60 sec
- You need to cure curse within 12 sec other wise it wipes out your team like normal raid zones, including mercenaries.
- Luckily the first time **"Ancient Curse"** is completely random. Some cases 0 **"Ancient Curse"** even he dies. Some times **"Ancient Curse"**  appears when just start fighting his HP is **99%**
- Tips: ![](images/Cure_Curse_fast_way.jpg) Player priest uses your mouse click yellow curse icon in your teammate info window and cure curse. It is faster.
