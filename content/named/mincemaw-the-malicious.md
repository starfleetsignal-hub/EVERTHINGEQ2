## Strategy

- Mincemaw has a fairly nasty Noxious DoT called Venomous Drool.
- At 50% health, any [[A cobalt lizard|cobalt lizards]] in the area will agro on whomever is attacking Mincemaw.  Clear them first.
- Not *too* difficult for a Cobalt Scar named; most classes should be able to take him if they have gear and know what they're doing.
