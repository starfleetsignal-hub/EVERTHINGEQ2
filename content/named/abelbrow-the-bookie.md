## Strategy

Quanth the Coldblade and Abelbrow the Bookie are one linked encounter.

Initially Abelbrow is aggro, but he does not hit hard so can be tanked by most classes. Get aggro on him and stop all dps until you get an uncurable elemental, named Debilitating Aura, then he will take damage. At 80% Quanth the Coldblade becomes aggro, do the same thing here as you did with Adelbrow, hold off from hitting him until he places his debuff on you. Coldblade's debuff is named Amplifying Aura. Once you have both debuffs you can kill them. If a debuff drops then just stop attacking until it is reapplied.

It is key that **both** mobs must die within 10s of each other.
