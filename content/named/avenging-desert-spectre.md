The Avenging Desert Spectre is a Tier 2 raid boss.

## Stats

- 113 Flurry Avoidance
- 330 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 1,500% Max Health
- 9434 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a hard time limit of 60 minutes "unless otherwise overridden".
- The surge damage on this fight increases with distance; players who are farther away will get hit harder.
- The Spectre has a "learning experience" known as **Gangly Gash**, an incurable elemental that deals extra damage for each target hit by it. This detriment has a short range.
- The Spectre calls for characters to "come closer", then casts **Cursed Existence**, a curable noxious that deals disease damage to all targets within 26 meters.
- The Spectre attempts to fear targets for 20 continuous seconds. During this time, "anyone entering his nearby area will be potentially instilled with fear". Victims who succumb to **Fearful Fright** are feared. This effect cannot be avoided once the detriment lands.
- The Spectre does not cast any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] can be used as a simple debuff.
