## Strategy

- The Clockwork Assembly Plant is a ring event.  Clear all the [[A clockwork engineer|clockwork engineer]]s to spawn a wave of [[An activated klakdroid|activated klakdroid]]s.  Kill the klakdroids to spawn several [[An activated klakdyne|activated klakdyne]]s and [[The Klak'Anon Warhulk]].
- Once he's spawned, should be easily moloable.
