## Strategy

- She comes with two [[A blade geist (Acadechism)|blade geists]] (64^).
- Kelzaral herself has two abilities. Kinetic Push does magic damage, knockback (not up in the air, just straight back), and slows. Lightning Strike just does magic damage.
- She'll also periodically summon [[A pike geist (Acadechism)|pike geists]] (four 64even, linked).
- The various geists have a few abilities. Slice does melee damage and decreases mitigation vs physical. Deep Cut and Whirling Slice both do initial melee damage and then extra melee damage per tick.
