## Notes

- He can be hard to pick up, since he immediately aggros onto the attacking droag. If you kill all the droag and then run away, he will eventually reset, but will return to his NPC status and become un-attackable. To add insult to injury, if he despawns in this manner, you will have to wait for his cooldown to be up for him to spawn again. The ring event will spawn again in the meantime, but [[A Gorowyn royal knight]] will be in his place instead.
