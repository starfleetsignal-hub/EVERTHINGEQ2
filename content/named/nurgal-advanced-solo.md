## Strategy

- Obtain the safe key from defeating [[Bloogly (Advanced Solo)|Bloogly]]
- Return to The Trader's Reserve and find the glowing blue metal in one of the four safes in the corners of this room. Click it to turn Nurgal targetable and aggressive.
- Tank and Spank
