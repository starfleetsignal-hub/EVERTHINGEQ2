## Strategy

The Curator will leash if you fight him outside the door. During the battle, he will periodically (every 30 seconds) port himself back to his starting point in the center of the room and root there until he lets off a crushing AE called Earthquake. The AE is *line of sight* only, so fighting him in a corner with a pillar between where he ports to is safe. Earthquake will hit for 12-18k crushing damage, depending on gear.
