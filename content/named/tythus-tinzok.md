## Special Attacks

### Cloak of Negative Energy

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 8% chance to cast Negative Feedback on target's attacker. Last for 7.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 8,404 - 12,605 magic damage on target.
  - Inflicts 3,760 - 5,639 magic damage on target every 7 seconds.
  - Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
  - Decreases Accuracy of target by 41.4%.
  - Spells you cast have a greater than normal chance to be resisted.

### Dark Energy Flow

Effects:

- Inflicts 7,528 - 13,980 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 7,227 - 13,421 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect every 3 seconds.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 167.0% instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Inflicts 167.0% of max health in magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Decreases Weapon Damage of targets in Area of Effect by 500.0.
- Decreases Potency of targets in Area of Effect by 400.0.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Dark Flames of the Void

Effects:

- Inflicts 5,162 - 12,045 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 167.0% instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Inflicts 167.0% of max health in heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Decreases Crit Chance of targets in Area of Effect by 500.0.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Voidslide

Effects:

- Throws caster back
- Inflicts 10,599,199 - 31,797,619 crushing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 2,355,371 - 7,066,133 crushing damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 7 meters
- Inflicts 7,419,449 - 13,778,969 crushing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 2,060,957 - 3,827,500 crushing damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
  - If target is between 7 and 14 meters away
- Inflicts 4,504,662 - 6,094,537 crushing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 1,751,807 - 2,370,106 crushing damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
  - If target is between 14 and 200 meters away
- Targets in Area of Effect can not cast hostile spells.
