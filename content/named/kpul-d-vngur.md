## Strategy

- In order to beat him you need an item called Vhalen's Unchained Fork which you get from the mender. After finishing his first quest to give you access to his anvil, you need to do the second quest to get the fork. The fork is used to prevent the named get's damage imune. The big AOE .. The Aria he casts can't be prevented and needs to be jousted.
- If attempting to solo him at high level, dps him down quickly to avoid him casting his immunity buff.
