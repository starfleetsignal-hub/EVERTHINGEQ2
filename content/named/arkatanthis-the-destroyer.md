## Strategy

During the fight against [[Xilaxis The Explorer]], when Xilaxis is at 80% health he will start a ritual.  If you want to fight Arkatanthis, do *not* disrupt the ritual.  Arkatanthis and Xilaxis will attack your raid force together.
