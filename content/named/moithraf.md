## Strategy

You can body pull the mobs in the room into the hallway, leaving the named free for one on one. Be sure to wait for the aberrants to come out of the room, they tend to stand back casting.  Using singular focus will avoid some AoE procs.  He casts purple damage rings, they're hard to avoid, best to heal through if you can.  Kluuron lives longer than the other mercs due to his health being higher.  But even he tends to die before the end from the purple rings.

Repops about once an hour.
