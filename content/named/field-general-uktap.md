## Special Attacks

### Cloak of Combat

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 8% chance to cast Bolt of Combat on target's attacker. Lasts for 16.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 10,104 - 18,765 magic damage on target.
  - Inflicts 3,609 - 6,702 magic damage on target.
  - Decreases INT and STR of target by 464.5.
