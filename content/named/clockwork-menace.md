Entry to this zone is by killing The Mechnomancer - found in the next room. Kill guards on the lower floor until the ring event starts, at which point activated clockwork workers come down to protect 2 levers on the ground floor, Kill them four times, and the mechnomancer will spawn with a whole lot of his buddies - he's 55 ^^^  He body drops a power cell key, letting you access CMF
