## Strategy

- He will spawn adds every 20% health.  You cannot kill any of the adds or he will heal to full and become invincible.  A mezzer is recommended.

- He is control-immune (at least Stun, Root, Stifle, Daze) and has a large amount of HP, so you will most likely need a group.

- Snare does work.
