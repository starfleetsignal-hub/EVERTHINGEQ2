## Strategy

Setup in the hallway.

Has two curses to be mindful of:

- The Chosen Curse, where the affected should remain in close proximity of Golguth.
- Yothshaval Curse, where the affected should joust out of range at least 20m.

Will occasionally summon [[An aberrant Mindslurper]] to protect himself that also drains power.
