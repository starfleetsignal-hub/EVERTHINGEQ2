## Strategy

***Phase One: Slow and Alternating Burn***

- Start the encounter at a normal pace and have the raid be prepared for an emote to call out a specific member of the raid starting around 90-95% with the text ***Jessip Daggerheart is attempting to sneak up on PLAYER_NAME_HERE, but they haven't noticed.... You should warn them!*** Call out the player affected and they need to immediately joust well out of melee range to avoid being "assassinated".

- Note: If the named successfully assassinates (2) people, the raid will automatically wipe.

- Continue slowly damaging the named down until around 80% - if a second emote has note appeared yet, slowly damage the named until around 77-78%. If they are damaged too quickly and pass beyond the 75% threshold, it will sometimes cause an instant wipe as though it treats the second assassination attempt as an automatic failure.

***Phase Two: Wait and Burn***

- Once the second assassination attempt is foiled, Jessip will say "I can see I need to change my tactics...." which means the raid no longer needs to be concerned with assassination attempts.

- Around 70% Jessip will spawn two "clones" of herself, both should have around 30% health. These need to be burned down ASAP as the longer they are up the more damage that will be inflicted during part of the next phase.

***
***Phase Three: Burn, Burn, Burn***

- Once the clones are taken care of the raid should focus fire and try to get the named downed as fast as possible.
