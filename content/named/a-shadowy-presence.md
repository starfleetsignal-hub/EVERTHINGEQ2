## Notes

Defilers can cure 'Invalidation' with their cast while stunned cure. Use  mage that are at max range for attack spell to cure priest that are at max range for heal. Manastone does NOT work.  Tribal Spiritists hat DOES work. It is possible, but unconfirmed, that standing directly in front of the mob may not take the AE. Will need a couple more tests to see if it was just dumb luck or an actual safe spot.
