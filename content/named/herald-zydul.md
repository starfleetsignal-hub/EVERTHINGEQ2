## Strategy

- To spawn him, clear all the groups of [[An Alliz Raef Ew champion|Alliz Raef Ew champion]]s and [[An Alliz Raef Ew shaman|shamen]] to spawn groups of [[An Alliz Raef Ew prophet|Alliz Raef Ew prophet]]s and [[An Alliz Raef Ew zealot|zealots]].  Some will be on the islands in the lake, some will run around the lakeshore.  Clear them all to spawn Herald Zydul.
- Easily soloable.
