## Strategy

1. If Petyr aggros you he will teleport you to [[The Storeroom Office]]. Do not engage him directly.
1. Get the [[Supervisor's Boiler Wrench]] from the desk immediately in front of the lift, return to safety and equip it.
1. Click on the loose bolt to send a spray of molten molasses across Petyr's path.
   - Burn Petyr with the molasses four times to defeat him.
   - Once burned, Petyr will run to your right, around the boiler to tighten it. Once done, you won't be able to use this boiler again for this attempt.
   - If Petyr catches you, he and the boilers will have reset by the time you return.
