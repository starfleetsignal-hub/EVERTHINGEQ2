## Strategy

1. Clear [[An ethereal automaton|ethereal automatons]], [[An ether-charged automaton|ether-charged automatons]], and [[A reinforced automaton|reinforced automatons]].
1. Start with the [[Energy Delivery Module]]. It will stop every so often, root itself, and fire a straight-line beam attack. Move to the side to avoid this attack.
1. The Force Absorption Module is slow and doesn't do a lot of damage. It will, however, emote "The Tank Module manipulates its magnetic field, attempting to whip you toward its energized companion!" then toss you, so just move away from him as he approaches.
