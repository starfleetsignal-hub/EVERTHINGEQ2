Drops one of several 95 Legendary Attuneable weapons, or 96 Heirloom Waist or Cloak, or 98 [[Phantom Armor (Altar of Malice)|Yellow Malice]] Boots, Shoulders or Forearms.

- Ornate chest - drops one of the above items, plus chances for additional standard ornate chest loot (Adept books, etc)
- Exquisite chest - Drops above, plus x1 [[Yellow Malice Gemstone (Heirloom)]] and occasionally an additional x1 [[Blue Malice Gemstone (Heirloom)]]

Spawned by a ring event where [[A viscous tar elemental|viscous tar elementals]] merge continuously until they *become* the named.

- Any random tar elemental must be brought down to low health (orange or red health bar), at which point another nearby 'full health' tar elemental will rush in and merge with the injured elemental, restoring the health of the injured elemental and 'upgrading' it to the next type.
- The 'upgraded' elemental must also be brought to low health, thereby summoning *another* healthy elemental from nearby to merge with it, restoring to full health and upgrading again.
- Repeat this cycle of injuring/merging/upgrading, until your target elemental finally *becomes* the Primeval Ooze.  Once spawned, you can finally defeat the Primeval Ooze like any other named to earn its loot.
- Obviously, this event will be difficult if you are one-shotting oozes before they can merge/heal.  Dismissing your familiar will reduce your stats significantly enough that you should be able to damage oozes without killing them.  If you need additional damage reduction, dismount as well.  If that doesn't work, start removing pieces of armor (not weapons) until you can reasonably damage without accidentally killing - *beware of your own occasional fabled and mythical crits.*
- When fighting your target ooze, be close enough to the tar pits that nearby oozes will rush in to merge, but not so close that they aggro. (The 'merge' range is decently large, so shouldn't be a problem, so long as one is not reckless). Near the center of the three pits area should be fine.
  - While aggro'd oozes *can* merge with a low health ooze, oozes must be at full health to merge. Oozes in combat can easily take an accidental low damage ripotse or damage shield hit or an AoE melee/spell, all of which would then prevent them from merging.  Best to just keep the extras out of combat altogether for the sake of convenience.
- Once a Primeval Ooze has been formed, the area enters a 'cooldown' (1hr or more), preventing the merge mechanic from occurring for some time until a new boss ooze is ready to be spawned again.  If they're not merging, even after being brought down to orange or red health bar, then someone else has killed it too recently.  Come back and try again later.
