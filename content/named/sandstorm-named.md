## Strategy

His chamber has patches of sand scattered throughout. If everyone stays off the sand during the fight, he won't summon any adds. This mob does an AE knockback, so it is important to have everyone fight with their backs to the wall to prevent getting knocked into sand; otherwise follow the strat below to deal with the adds.

For this fight you will need a [[Coercer]], [[Illusionist]] or [[Troubador]].

Throughout the fight he will summon two adds. Simply mezz them, and continue burning the named- quickly. He will put stoneskin on himself, but it doesn't last that long. The adds are Monks and can cure Mez effects.

If your mezz class is a [[Troubador]], then you will need to mezz one of the adds and quickly burn the other.

If you do not have a mezz class, then you will need to root one add and burn the other.

If you have no class that can mezz or root, then you may need to rethink your group. Or, burn the adds as quickly as you possibly can.
