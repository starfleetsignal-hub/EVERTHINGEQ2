## Notes

- Resolve of the named: 3740
- As usual the first Named of a raid-zone also this is very easy to defeat.
- the scout-counter-spell  is required for this mob.

## Effects

The Named has following Effects on it:

- **Toxic Bristles**
- **Unbridled Fury of the Shadows**: This effect must be countered by the scout-counter-spell
- **Savage Strength**
- **Symbiotic Aura**
- **Obsidian Guard I**

## Strategy

- 2 Tanks-"tank and spank": MT holds the named, OT the other holds the spawning adds.
