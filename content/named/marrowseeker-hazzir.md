Marrowseeker Hazzir is a Tier 3 raid boss.

Hazzir spawns when a player walks over the spot that he (eventually) stands on. This summons a large winged beast, which flies down and gets attacked by several Chetari, lead by Hazzir. Once they have eaten the beast, all of the Chetari become attackable.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance: 525.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance: 590.0
- Resolve: 11,195.0

## Abilities

- **Piercing Thought Process I**
- A curable trauma that deals fast-growing damage over time.
- **Mind Pressure I**
- A curable trauma that deals fast-growing damage over time.
- **Frenzied Clawspike**
- An incurable trauma that deals piercing damage over time.
- **Marrowfeast I/II/III/IV**
- A curable trauma that deals fast ticking increasing physical damage and increases non-physical damage from all sources. Higher ranks increase the how much the non-physical damage is increased.
- **Teamwork**: Leader
- A buff that casts Tag Team Strike on the current target, if that target already has the effect from Teamwork: Member.
- **Stealthy Admonishment**
- A curable elemental that deals elemental damage and increases all other damage types. This effect only lands on scouts.
- **Devoured Marrow**
- A buff that decreases damage taken from all sources and increases offense for each marrow thister Hazzir absorbs.
- **Selfish Ambition**
- A curable curse that kills the entire raid when it expires. When cured, it resets the curse cure of the healer (unless the healer cured themselves).
- **Barrage**
- An attack that can only be countered with [[Bulwark of Order]].

## Strategy

Hazzir summons [[A marrow thirster]] continuously throughout the fight, which has Teamwork: Member. Shortly after the thirster arrives, Hazzir attempts to absorb it, if it is within 50 meters of Hazzir. If absorbed, Hazzir heals based on how close the thirster is.
