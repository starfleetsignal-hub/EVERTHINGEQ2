## Abilities

- **Mental Malpractice**
- A curable arcane that deals fast ticking arcane damage to targets within 25 meters and increases physical damage from all sources.
- **Frigid Drop**
- A curable elemental that deals fast ticking elemental damage to targets within 25 meters and increases physical damage from all sources.
- **Venom Injection**
- A curable noxious that deals fast ticking poison damage to targets within 45 meters and increases physical damage from all sources.
- **Crushing Grasp**
- A curable trauma that deals fast ticking physical damage to targets within 45 meters and increases arcane, elemental, and noxious damage from all sources.
- **Festering Pressure**
- A curable noxious that kills the affected player's group when it expires.
- **Tentacle Hold**
- An incurable curse that prevents the target from moving or casting.
- **Monitored Detection**
- A curable curse that kills the entire raid instantly when it expires. It only lands on scouts, and it can only be cured while the scout is stealthed. This curse lands at 85%, 70%, 55%, 45%, 30%, 15%, and 10%.
