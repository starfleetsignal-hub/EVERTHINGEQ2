## Strategy

- Clear the snakes in between the elevator you arrived in and the platform to Shazzak.
- Recommend setting up right outside the elevator room against the wall and pulling Shazzak to you.
- At every 20% health he will fracture off a half dozen or so versions of himself, burn them down and return focus to named.
- Shazzak may port to the 'center' of the chamber and cause boulders to fall down in the chamber. Recommend staying put and just waiting for him to return to the group.
