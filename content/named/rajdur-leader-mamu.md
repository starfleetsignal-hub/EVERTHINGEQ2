Raj'Dur Leader Mamu is a Tier 2 raid boss.

## Stats

- 113 Flurry Avoidance
- 330 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 1,500% Max Health
- 9434 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a hard time limit of 60 minutes "unless otherwise overridden".
- Shortly after pull, Mamu summons all roaming Raj'Dur trash in Upper Raj'Durabad. (The summoned trash may socially aggro other non-roaming trash.)
- [[Raj'Dur instructor|Raj'Dur instructors]] periodically join the fight, shouting "Huzzah, for the Sultan!" as they arrive. Each instructor that is up gives Mamu an increment of **Leadership Coordination**, which decreases the damage he takes from all sources.
- Mamu will shout "See how you like this one!" before placing a black circle underneath a raid member. After a few seconds, all nearby raid members will be pulled into the circle. Players who stay within this circle too long receive a curable curse.
- Mamu will shout "Give me your power to use against you!" before he and his instructors cast a curable trauma named **Burning Power** (or **Instructed Burning Power**) on all players within 18 meters of the caster. This trauma deals physical damage, causes the target to take growing physical damage from all sources, and heal the caster for 0.25% (Mamu) or 0.1% (instructor) of the caster's max health for each target that is hit. The caster's current target (usually the main tank) does not count for this ability.
- Mamu and his instructors each cast a short-range curable elemental called **Severe Pelting**. This detriment only lands on players close to the caster, and it deals extra damage to those that are standing farther away.
- Mamu does not cast any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] can be used as a simple debuff.
