## Strategy

You need to get the big guy smash into the smaller ones - about 10 of them to reduce the healing he gets from them. If he steps on them once, they will turn red and the screen will emote, **"The crushing sound of glass and metal meets your ears as the Eternal Sentinel collides with one of the lesser gargoyles!"** When he runs over it a second time it will emote, **"A lesser gargoyle collapses to the floor, broken!"** The gargoyle is down for the count and will no longer heal.. A good (and somewhat fun) way to handle this is let the MT get his attention and just run in circles, passing over the gargoyles. Then it's just tank-and-spank.

Note that if you get too close to the smaller sentinels they'll fling you across the room and blur your vision for a few seconds.

White Text will indicate when he gets ready to jump. All group members must move away from the Sentinel or else suffer big damage and knockback.
