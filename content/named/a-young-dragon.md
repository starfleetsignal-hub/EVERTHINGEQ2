## Strategy

- [[Undercon|Underconned]] (notoriously so). Be careful.
- He has a knockback that can knock you off the roof of Halls of Fate; get up against one of the small pillars to prevent getting knocked.
- For soloing, get to level 70 and use a tank mercenary with good DPS ([[Vittia Direshadow]] is probably the best choice).
