The Embodiment of Fright is a Tier 1 raid boss.

## Stats

- 108 Flurry Avoidance
- 324 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 9350 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a hard time limit of 60 minutes "unless otherwise overridden".
- The Embodiment of Fright summons [[Fearful frightling|fearful frightlings]] throughout the fight.
- Characters may occasionally feel "eyes watching from all directions", accompanied by an incurable elemental named **Looming Fright**. This detriment does nothing to the character which is afflicted, but all other characters within 8 meters will be struck by the incurable **Imposed Fright**, which is a fear that cannot be resisted.
- Throughout the fight, mandrake bulbs (which look like purple flowers on a long vine) will appear all across the farm. These bulbs can be harvested by any character. The bulbs have a limited lifespan and will eventually "suffocate" and disappear from inventory. If the Embodiment is brought too close to one of these bulbs before it is harvested, the bulb will instantly be destroyed.
- At 95%, 75%, 50%, 25%, and 10%, the Embodiment of Fright will apply **Straw Protection** to itself, which makes it entirely invulnerable to all damage. This protection can be removed by using a mandrake bulb against the Embodiment.
- A curable curse, **Fearful Cramp**, is occasionally applied to the Embodiment's current target (usually the main tank). This curse prevents the afflicted character from casting any beneficial or hostile spells. This effect is not a stun, stifle, or mesmerize, and thus, cannot be resisted.
- The Embodiment does not cast any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] can be used as a simple debuff.
