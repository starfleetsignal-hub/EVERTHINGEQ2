## Strategy

Acklok spawns at the end of a solo ring event.  First kill a group of [[A grimestone burrower|grimestone burrowers]] (85v non-aggro), then a group of [[A grimestone devourer|grimestone devourers]] (85 non aggro).

Acklok seems to be somewhat overconned.  He hits harder and faster than non-named mobs, but he doesn't have any special abilities.
