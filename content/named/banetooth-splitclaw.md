## Notes

- You will need to complete the first part of the quest [[A Darkened Shard]] from the [[Splitpaw Saga Timeline|Splitpaw Saga]] timeline to access the [[Sundered Splitpaw: Upper Tunnels|Upper Tunnels]]. Once you have completed the second part of the quest, you will receive [[A glowing azure shard]] which will allow you to teleport directly to the [[Sundered Splitpaw: Splitpaw Den|Splitpaw Den]] where the Arena entrance is located. If you do not have the shard, you can use stealth to easily move through the upper tunnels.
