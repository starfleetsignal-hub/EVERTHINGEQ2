Ability and strategy information changes with stages (1-20/21-40/41+).

## Abilities

- **Scrying for Fear**
- A buff that, while active, Lu'Gul attempts to fear its most hated target.
- **Scrying for Stun**
- A buff that, while active, Lu'Gul attempts to stun its most hated target.
- **Scrying for Stifle**
- A buff that, while active, Lu'Gul attempts to stifle its most hated target.
- **Scrying for Mesmerize**
- A buff that, while active, Lu'Gul attempts to mesmerize its least hated target.
- **Isolate**
- An attack that throws back all players.
- **Ring of Horrors**
- An ability that summons a wall around Lu'Gul that players cannot pass through. It casts this spell immediately after Isolate. Players within the walled ring receive heat damage over time.
- **Summon Horror**
- An interruptible ability that summons a powerful monster to fight alongside Lu'Gul. It casts this spell immediately after Ring of Horrors.
- **External Experimentation [21+]**
- An incurable curse that decrements by 1 per second. At 0 increments, if target is not the closest to Lu'Gul, the target dies. It transfers to the next further target on termination. [Stage 41+] Multiple curses are active simultaneously. If a player gets the curse twice, that player dies.

## Strategy

At 65%, Lu'Gul calls for any remaining hatchery managers to join the fight. At 36%, it calls for any remaining scrykin to join the fight.
