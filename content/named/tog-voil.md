Tog will port from one floor to the other through out the fight.  This seemed to be on a timer rather than at certain mob health levels.  You'll need to use the black floating clouds close to you to port to Tog when he ports.  To do this you will need to stand in the middle of the black circular cloud discs. and then your cursor turns into a hand over the black cloud.  You can double click or right click and select transfer to port directly to where Tog has moved to.  If you try to run to where Tog is located you don't get much time to DPS him before he ports again. Also look out for Blood of Luclin Divine damage while he ports.

  - Note I have a DPS pet for my class and a healer Merc. The Healer merc had a hard time keeping up with where the mob was and would run away after port and then run back to Tog.  You'll need to set the healer merc in passive mode to be sure it stays with you while you port. The healer merc will continue to heal even if not set to protect or assist you or your other group member.**

At about 50% Tog will summon netherbian swarmers. In the solo instance I just ignored them and continued to burn Tog down.  But I have a great healer merc your mileage may vary here and may want to burn down the adds.  Tog still ports during this part of the fight so be sure to keep up.

Tog has two different kinds of debuffs and dots that he'll put on you. Nether Tether which roots you in place and Paralytic Lick.  Both can be cured by healer if the healer merc is in range.

Big hit spells are Freeze and Ill Incarnate.
