## Strategy

- Spawns at the end of a small ring event.  First clear all the orcs from inside and around the ruined house, then kill the [[A Lonetusk champion|Lonetusk champions]] that spawn.  He'll spawn in the basement.  He appears not to have a placeholder anymore.

- -100 faction with **The Bloodskull Orcs**
