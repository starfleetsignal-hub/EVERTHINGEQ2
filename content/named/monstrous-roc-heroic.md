## Strategy

- Burn and Joust.
- Pull up to the outer 'square' path and fight up there. At every 10% health, red text may or may not appear but you'll see the Roc squat down and lay a giant egg. Joust away from it as it will explode and do some heavy damage to any in the vicinity. Rinse and repeat.
