## Effects of the runes if not destroyed

- Red = Frontal (Get behind)
- Blue = Elemental ( 4 members of raid will get the uncrurable elemental and must joust away from raid)
- Yellow = Noxious (Two members of raid will get an uncurable nox and must joust away from the raid AND one another)
- Green = Tank Swap (One tank will have to lose aggro in about 15 seconds or it wipes the raid)
