## Strategy

At 75, 50 and 25%, an add will pop. The Add must be eliminated before continuing on the book. Meanwhile, the book can be mezzed. The 25% add is able to make pop "Lame" adds. Interrupt him or anything to avoid this, otherwhile, down the Lames.
