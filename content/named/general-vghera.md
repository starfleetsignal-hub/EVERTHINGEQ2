## NOTES

A Dark Elf Zombie. From EQ1 Kithcor Forest, a part of the Rogue Epic quest. Full name was Rald V'hgera

Drops one of several 95 Legendary Attuneable weapons, or 96 Heirloom Waist or Cloak, or 98 Yellow Malice Boots, Shoulders or Forearms ([[Augur's Chain Bracers of Celerity]]). And has a chance to additionally drop a Yellow or Blue Malice Gem, heirloom.
