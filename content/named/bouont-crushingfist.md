## Strategy

This boss is linked with 3 worms.

First, let the tank pull the boss and mez the 3 worms. Then, kill each worm while mezzing the others. Sometime, the boss will teleport himself in the center of the room and reset aggro. All non tank should go farther than the tank to let him aggro the boss when he come back.

When the worms are down, everyone should go to the center of the room and fight beside the pedestal. Sometimes the boss will teleport on top of the pedestal and will start to regenerate.  There are 3 buttons around the pedestal which must be pushed at the same time to interrupt this process.

But be careful, you mustn't be on the first step to push button or you die as while he is rooted while regenerating.  This is because he still will attack those near to him if the highest aggro (the tank) member is not within melee range (the stand thing for rooted mobs), so the tank should ensure they stay in melee range here.
