## Buffs

### Abyssal Onslaught

Effects:

- Spiritual Onslaught grants attack power to wearer.

## Special Attacks

### Aura of the Depths

Effects:

- When any damage is received this spell has a 10% chance to cast Abyssal Feedback on target's attacker. Lasts for 2.0 seconds.
  - Inflicts 10,496 - 24,490 magic damage on target instantly and every 2 seconds.
    - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
  - Decreases power of target by 1.8 - 2.2% instantly and every 2 seconds.
    - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Slime-Covered Scales

Effects:

- Opponents have a 9% chance to be struck with awesome wonder, which will immobilize them for several seconds.

### Wing Flap
