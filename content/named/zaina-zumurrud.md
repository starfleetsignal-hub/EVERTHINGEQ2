## Strategy

- Zaina will spawn many adds. These will aggro on a scout, who needs to kite them around. Make sure the scout doesn't engage the named, so they don't lose run speed.
- If you plan to tank the adds (Zaina spawns massive amounts of heroic no arrow up adds), be prepared for another 6,500,000 HP worth of dps. The adds also stun the tank like crazy and drain power. Keep your healers and ranged dps at a distance.
