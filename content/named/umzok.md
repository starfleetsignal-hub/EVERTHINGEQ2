## Special Attacks

### Adjutant's Aura

Effects:

- When any damage is received this spell has a 11% chance to cast Void Beam on target's attacker. Lasts for 2.0 seconds.
  - Inflicts 9,303 - 21,707 magic damage on target.
  - Stuns target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 6.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 0.7 seconds.

### Channeled Void Energy

Effects:

- Increases Weapon Damage of caster by 10.0.
- Increases Potency of caster by 10.0.

### Piercing Darkness

Effects:

- Inflicts 13,657 - 31,868 piercing damage of targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 11,609 - 27,088 piercing damage of targets in Area of Effect every 4.5 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 7 meters
- Inflicts 5,178 - 9,617 piercing damage of targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 4,779 - 8,877 piercing damage of targets in Area of Effect every 4.5 seconds.
  - If target is between 7 and 15 meters away
- Inflicts 5,121 - 6,259 piercing damage of targets in Area of Effect every 4.5 seconds.
  - If target is between 15 and 200 meters away
- Blurs vision of targets in Area of Effect.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Toxic Mists

Effects:

- Inflicts 13,395 - 31,255 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 12,056 - 28,130 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 9 meters
- Inflicts 7,814 - 14,511 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 7,032 - 13,060 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
  - If target is between 9 and 15 meters away
- Inflicts 4,912 - 7,367 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
  - If target is between 15 and 200 meters away
- Blurs vision of targets in Area of Effect.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Void Slam

Effects:

- Throws target back
- Inflicts 14,735 - 34,381 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 12,725 - 29,693 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 9 meters
- Inflicts 8,595 - 15,963 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 7,814 - 14,511 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - If target is between 9 and 15 meters away
- Inflicts 5,805 - 8,707 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - If target is between 15 and 200 meters away
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

## Strategy

- Use the shard you just get in your inventory (or by speaking to moosh) on each add that will come aggro and join the fight in order to buff raid party and debuff named (only one shard may be obtained from Moosh at a time for the entire raid).
