## Strategy

1. **Zzalazziz and Zizzalazz**. Spawn [[Zzalazziz]] by walking up one of the two stairs.
   - This elemental will spawn a doppelganger with the name [[Zizzalazz]].
   - The doppelganger [[Zizzalazz]] just needs to be held away from the raid. It will die when [[Zzalazziz]] dies. There is no balancing of health nor do they have to be close in health.
1. **Tempest Mage Adds**. Five seconds into the fight, two [[A tempest mage|tempest mages]] spawn on the twin stair landings.
   - To pull the mage-adds, have a player walk up the stairs and into the NPC, no attack needed. Make sure the player starts on the deck at the bottom of the stairs, because the mage-add can bug if a player is standing on the landing when the add spawns.
   - Drag the tempest mages to Zzalazziz. If you have sufficient AOE to burn the mage-adds, that's great, if not, have the melee DPS clear them out so they don't overwhelm the tank.
   - The mage adds will continue to spawn every 30 seconds thereafter.
