## Special Attacks

### Dooming Depths

- Inflicts 21,851 - 36,419 cold damage on target.
- Inflicts 13,111 - 21,851 cold damage on target every 4.5 seconds.
- Stifles target
- Resistibility increases against targets higher than level 117.

### Sympathetic Power

Effects:

- Increases Strikethrough of target by 33.0%.
- Increases AE Autoattack of Target by 33.0%.
- Combat and spell damage increase by 50.0%.
- On any combat art or spell hit this spell may cast Sympathetic Energy of target of attack. Lasts for 4.0 seconds. Triggers about 1.8 times per minute.
  - Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
  - Inflicts 113,942 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
    - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means.
