## Buffs

### Confidence Building

Effects:

- When Tuzerk and his group have killed three people, a cowardly healer will join the fray and heal them.

### Focused Anger

Effects:

- Target randomly selects which enemy it wants to attack and cannot be taunted or target locked. Does not Melee. Passively regenerates power over time.

### Greater Pirate Onslaught

Effects:

- Greater Pirate Onslaught grants attack power to wearer.
