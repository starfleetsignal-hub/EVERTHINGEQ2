Thet-ki-dua <Challenge Mode> is a Tier 3 raid boss. He gains an additive advantage for each player below 22 and has a soft time limit of 45 minutes.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance 550.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance 900.0
- Resolve 13,640.0

## Abilities

- **Conscription of Cinders II**
- A buff that gives a chance of triggering Cinder Burst on the attacker, which deals heat damage.
- **Melodic Ambition**
- A buff that decreases damage taken for each [[A hiding reverent dancer|hiding reverent dancer]] in the fight. Each additional dancer more than 2 heals Thet-ki-dua.
- **Power of Thet**
- A buff that increases melee multiplier and potency, multiplied by increments. Thet-ki-dua attempts to pull power from stored increments to heal himself.
- **Channeled Protection**
- A buff that slowly heals Thet-ki-dua and protects him from all damage. If Thet reaches 95% health, the effect is removed.
- **Surging Pulse**
- An incurable trauma that deals increasing physical and arcane damage to health and power over time.
- **Overbearing Influence II**
- An incurable trauma that makes power usage also cost health based on the amount of power used.
- **Channeled Fury I/II/III/IV**
- A curable trauma that deals increasing physical damage and increases non-physical damage from all sources.
- **Barrage**
- An incurable curse that can only be avoided with [[Bulwark of Order]].

## Strategy

At 80%, 60%, 40%, and 20%, three crystals appear around the room. Clicking the blue, red, and yellow crystals applies an incurable arcane, noxious, and elemental respectively. All three detriments are named Channeling Into Crystal, fall off if the player moves more than 20 meters from the crystal, and can be manually cancelled. Each player can only have one crystal detriment at a time. Having all 24 players click on a crystal, evenly divided between the three colors, causes Thet-ki-dua to lose Channeled Protection.
