## Notes

- It is possible to farm the book from Agony multiple times by only killing Agony and NOT Doom or Despair. You can lose aggro by running and zoning into [[The Temple of Cazic-Thule]]] or by using Feign Death. You then need to wait only 5 minutes for a respawn of Agony.
