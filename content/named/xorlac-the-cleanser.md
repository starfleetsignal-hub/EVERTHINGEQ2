## Strategy

Xorlac has a ring around a portal in which you must fight him in. However, he has a very nasty AoE (appears unblockable) that typically 1 hit killed our casters every so often. Power-draining of the mob is key here as is interrupting his spells. This is an endurance fight.

- Xorlac will emote something roughly every 30 seconds, I forget exactly what he says but when he says it, knock him down to avoid the AOE.  A group with 2 or more classes that have knock downs should be able to time their kicks/shield bashes whatever so that he never gets the AOE off.
