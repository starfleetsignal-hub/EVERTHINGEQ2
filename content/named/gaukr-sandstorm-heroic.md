## Strategy

### Trash mob Fight

- You may wanna kill all sand caster giants around boss room first

### **Gaukr Sandstorm**

- This boss is very different from [[Solusek Ro's Tower: The Obsidian Core (Solo)|solo version]].

- Try to prevents boss from moving and turning. Everyone should stand in **front** of Gaukr Sandstorm around **1-5 meters.** He deals most of the damage on **angles** and **distances** from everyone individually

- Everyone in your team stay close together before pull **Gaukr**. When he gets aggro first time he casts "**Torrent of Rain"** and **teleport** one aggro player to a random places (pick from **4 fixed points**). That teleported player should **back to team ASAP**.. ![](images/Black_out_and_4_random_teleport_point.jpg)

- He cast "**Sandstorm**"(Dot) to your team same time and it can't be cured. So it is deadly keeping a player far from Gaukr. This spell from 5M to 50M heat damage based on angles and degrees from **Gaukr**. For example 1m in front takes **6.1M**. 15m in front takes **16M**.

- During "**Torrent of Rain"** (about 18 seconds)**.** Those players in front of him about 1 - 5 meters take a small damage from "**Torrent of Rain"**  (**6M** Heat damage)**.** Other angles and distances take more damage up to **12M** every 4 sec. Be sure keeping group healing over time.  Everyone tries to cast death prevents and [[Stoneskin]] to player who targeted by **Gaukr.** He will cast "Pelt with Sand" and hit his target player **250M** heat damage.(If not player target, he picks threat number highest player and use it)
- Everyone in team at least **31M health** is easier to get through "**Torrent of Rain" .**
- Once you make it from "**Torrent of Rain"** .  There 13 second to dps and he deals less damage if he is NOT moving or turning

- Repeat above and you will beat him. Loot his key and get gears from floor level ![](images/Gaukr_Sandstrom_Key.jpg)
