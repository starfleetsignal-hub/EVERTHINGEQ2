## Strategy

- At somewhere between 25% and 50% health, Jenni will summon [[Jenni's Teacher]], a 34v solo conjuror.
- With the current stat distribution, Jenni is easily soloable with a merc.

## Notes

- Like all the daughters of Lord Everling she is technically a golem, as golem-related loot can be picked up from her corpse.
