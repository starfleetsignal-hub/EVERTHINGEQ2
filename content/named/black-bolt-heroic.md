## Strategy

- When [[Black Bolt]] says "Stay Away", joust out.
- If [[Black Bolt]] flees through a pyramid-shaped portal, click the portal to follow him, but remember the color of the portal!
- If [[Black Bolt]] ports again, you will be surrounded by several portals; use the one that's the same color as the first one.
