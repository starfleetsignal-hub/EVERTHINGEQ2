## Strategy

She's got 54k hit point, and uses smaller poison dots. Her main attacks are melee and an ability to teleport people into the cages in her room. These cages cannot be opened from the inside, and some cages spawn doppelgangers which will aggro anyone near them. A very easy fight, should you clear the cages before attacking her.
