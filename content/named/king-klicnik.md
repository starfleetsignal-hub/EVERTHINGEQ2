## Notes

- To spawn, find 'a larvae-infested carcass' and clear all the [[A klicnik warrior|klicnik warriors]] from around it. This may cause [[A klicnik prince]] to spawn. Kill the klicnik prince for a chance to spawn the King.
- Easily soloable.
