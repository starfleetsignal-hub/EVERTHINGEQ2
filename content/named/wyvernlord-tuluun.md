## Special Attacks

### Blinding Poison

Effects:

- Interrupts target
- Decreases power of target by 20.0% instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Inflicts 50.0% of max health in poison damage on target instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
- Decreases Haste of target by 97.0.
- Slows target by 140.0%.
- You must find some way to wash this poison off!

### Hardened Scales

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 8% chance to cast Scaly Backlash on target's attacker. Lasts for 0.5 seconds.
  - Interrupts target.
  - Inflicts 1,052 - 1,953 heat damage on target.
  - Stuns target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 1.5 seconds and duration is reduced to 0.2 seconds.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means
