## Strategy

- Spawn by killing the [[Surrogate of Contempt (Heroic)|Surrogate of Contempt]], [[Surrogate of Rage (Heroic)|Surrogate of Rage]], [[Surrogate of Rancor (Heroic)|Surrogate of Rancor]], [[Surrogate of Venom (Heroic)|Surrogate of Venom]], and [[Surrogate of Wrath (Heroic)|Surrogate of Wrath]], then craft on the book/crafting station  they drop to destroy vessel  and prevent respawning.
- Adds spawn during the fight

  - [[Rancor (Shard of Hate Heroic)|Rancor]]
  - [[Venom (Heroic)|Venom]]
