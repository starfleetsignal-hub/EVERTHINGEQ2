## Strategy

Brutas will be standing near some vents and other pirates on the second level of the first elevator the raid comes across. Clear the pirates before engaging.

- The fight itself is pretty easy as it is mostly a tank and spank, however periodically Brutas will teleport a member of the raid over one of the 5 bubbling vents nearby. There is 'A bucket of cool water' located near the barrels by the nearby elevator {{waypoint -11, -123, -8}}

- Recommend assigning a primary and secondary person to be on 'bucket' duty, as you can only have one bucket in inventory at a time. Their job will be to click the bucket in their inventory on any member of the raid that is teleported above the vent holes. A green text will appear if they were successfully freed. If all of the vents become obstructed with raid members for a period of time, it will result in an instant wipe for all raid members.
