## Strategy

- To spawn, kill all the [[A pain bringer (Heroic)|pain bringers]] and [[An apprentice of pain (Heroic)|apprentice of pain]] around the [[Rectory of Confession (SoH: Utter Contempt Heroic)|Rectory of Confession]] to spawn [[A keeper of pain]]. Kill the keeper and it will drop one of four keys, ([[A circle-headed key]], [[A cross-headed key]], [[A square-headed key]], or [[A triangle-headed key]]. Under the four cages in the area are different shapes: matching the shape to the correct key, then using the key on that cage, will spawn another [[A keeper of pain]]. Repeat for all four cages.
