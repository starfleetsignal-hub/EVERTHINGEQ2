Boss-Mob of the zone.
Spawns after all other named of the zone are killed.

## Buffs/Debuffs

- **To the Depths!**

## Strategy

- Berlon has the same swashbuckler abilities that all the other nameds in this zone do.
- He kicks the tank. Stand back to the wall.
- Periodically he summons n (2 in heroic) [[A skeletal crew member]]. On solo difficulty, the skeletal crewmembers don't do anything besides attack
- He also has a nastier trick called 'Bilge Wave':
  - When he's about to cast that, he emotes various things, including "The deep, dark seas are singin yer name, matey!", "Ye be drownin in ye own tears!" or "To the Wretched abyss with ye!".
  - It appears to be a frontal, AoE dot, taking the exciting form of a small phantom ship sailing towards the group.
  - The tank should make sure he has aggro and the rest of the group they are behind Berlon. The dot is incurable and causes crushing dmg and a stifle(this is the solo-issue).
