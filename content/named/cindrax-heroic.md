## Strategy

### Trash mob Fight

- First Boss on Top Level. You have to kill 3 "construct of flame" to spawn Cindrax.
- Lava will kill you instantly, if you run out of dead prevents. It is very difficult for non-scout class to get out. If you die, let a priest revives you and you will be out.
- Try to pull "construct of flame" one by one cuz this mob hit double damage compare to "a sentry of Ro" or "a radiant guardian". It self-destruct when HP lower than 20%. Spell cast time about 10 sec and apply strong knock back. (Burst of flame)

### Cindrax

- This boss share most mechanics in [[Solusek Ro's Tower: The Obsidian Core (Solo)|solo version]]. But a little different cuz this dungeon design for 6 full players(or 3 + 3 merc).
- Pull him to a dead end in case he knocks you to lava (Stone Push/Stone Pull)
- When Named HP drop to 80%,60%,40%,20%, he seals a player to a gargoyle around elevator each hp threshold. That poor player with "Bind Sight" will die in 60 sec. (Solitude)
- When Named Hp near to 16% or lower. He summons all 4 gargoyles and immunes damage. If a player happens still to be sealing in gargoyle and die. Gargoyles disappear and he can be damaged again. If not, kill all 4 gargoyles and kill Named.(Gargoyles Attack)
- Mercenaries and AOE immune pets ignore all mechanics above
- Note: Almost all PoP dungeons drain your mana. This one same (Grotesque Gaze). This boss is very easy if you lack gears and wanna try some heroic loot. This one is second easiest, first one is in Torden:Tower Breach-Gate Keeper.
