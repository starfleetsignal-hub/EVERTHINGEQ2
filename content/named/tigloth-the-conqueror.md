## Strategy

Will spawn after you kill the other four named.

Recommended to pull him down one of the nearby dead ends for the engagement as he has multiple knock-back abilities.

There are two 10-point achievements related to killing him:

- [[Tigloth the Conqueror's Demise]]
- [[Tigloth the Conqueror's Demise (Sense of Urgency)]]
