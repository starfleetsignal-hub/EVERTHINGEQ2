## Special Attacks

### Aura of the Void Sorcerer

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 5% chance to cast Void Retribution on target's attacker. Lasts for 9.0 seconds.
  - Teleports target to a random location within 10 meters.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 13,883 - 20,825 magic damage on target.
  - Inflicts 7,456 - 11,184 magic damage on target every 4.5 seconds.
  - Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
  - Decreases Weapon Damage of target by 50.0.
  - Decreases Potency of target by 50.0.
  - Decreases Accuracy of target by 20.0%.

### Chaotic Energies

Effects:

- Applies Chaotic Explosion on termination. Lasts for 5.0 seconds.
  - Throws target back
  - Inflicts 900.0% of max health in heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every second.
  - Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 900.0% instantly and every second.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means
- Grants Ultravision to target
- There must be some way to remove this effect nearby, saving the raid from a chaotic explosion, perhaps some energy source could stabilize you.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

  - Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Elemental Barrage

Effects:

- Inflicts 4,474 - 10,439 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 5,263 - 12,281 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect every 3 seconds.
- Inflicts 167.0% of max health in cold damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 167.0% instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Decreases Mitigation of targets in Area of Effect vs elemental damage by 2,112.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Meteor Shower

Effects:

- Throws target back
- Inflicts 196,629 - 365,168 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 113,838 - 211,413 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.

## Strategy

When you get the purple screen, ground yourself to (click) the void lamp or you will wipe the raid.

C2C or have the yellow detrimental cured or else it might interrupt you when you click the lamp.
