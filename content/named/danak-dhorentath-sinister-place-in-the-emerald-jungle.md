## Strategy

- He hits a bit harder than a monster of his level normally would - nothing exceptional, but don't forget to keep an eye on your health.
- At 50% health and 11% health, Danak will become immune to damage and a bunch of different colored glowies (gold, green, and purple) will spawn.  Danak will bring up a magical field over his body.  Click a harvestable of the same color as his magic field to cause an explosion, do 10% damage and make him vulnerable again.  Clicking the wrong color spawns [[A summoned imp]].
