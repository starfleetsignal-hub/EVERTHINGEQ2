## Spawn strategy

- Just keep clearing the groups of corrupted brigands away from the tree house.  Eventually Korav will spawn.

- Accompanied by three [[A corrupted brigand|corrupted brigands]], same level v heroic.
- Easily soloable with a merc.
- Tank n' spank
