## Strategy

- Grouped with 2 [[A summoned shadow (Crushbone Keep)|summoned shadows]] or [[A summoned watcher (Crushbone Keep)|summoned watchers]]. Lv30 evencon, selected randomly between the two types.
- Easily moloable, although the room he's in is densely packed with mobs and he should be carefully bodypulled.
