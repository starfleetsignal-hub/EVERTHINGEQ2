## Strategy

This fight can be boiled down to Cure Curse. If you do not have a healer in your group with cure curse do not even attempt this fight. Note Mercs DO NOT have cure curse.

The mechanic is as follows: The fight starts, and all group members are cursed. If the curse is not cured, the member with the highest hate will be charmed. This charm stuns the character that is charmed, rendering them unable to cure themselves, so healers must self cure first. At certain intervals (around 75%, 50%, and 25%), three mud globes will spawn. They are quick kills, so knock them out when they spawn and return to killing to the boss.  It's a relatively easy fight if you have remove curse; if you don't, you cannot beat this boss without major major headaches.

A party of three characters without a healer was capable of defeating Dagarn. Varied from Lvl 96 Shadowknight to a Lvl 99 Wizard. Once the fight starts there was a short delay before the first curse was cast. This starts a curse timer. As there was no healer in the party, Dagarn must be killed before the first Curse timer expires. Our party did not have the DPS to kill Dagarn in the time. Mercs (two Kennys plus a Matri Dut)were added to the party and then the party could just burn down Dagarn in the available time.
