## Strategy

Fight Berik on the ramp near the top, because he ports people to the zone in and you don't want a long run-back. When a tank is called to the priest, they stop attacking and pause. the tank must drop aggro (or have another tank pick up aggro) then run to the raid, return back to the tank location and resume the fight.
