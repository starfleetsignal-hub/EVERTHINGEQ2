## Notes

- You will have 380 seconds from the beginning of this fight to end it. Avatar of the Sun will spawn when Solusek Ro reaches 90% health and de-pops. At the end of 380 seconds, Solusek Ro will re-pop and wipe the encounter.
- During [[Legacy of Power: Drawn to the Fire]] it is not necessary to kill this monster. By the time he spawns, your quest will have updated; you can escape.
