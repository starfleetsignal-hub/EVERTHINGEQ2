## Notes

- There is no placeholder for Stiletto.
- His spawn timer was brought down to 10 minutes as of [[LU58]].
- You will need to be LVL 25+
- His roaming range may be large (among the Skindancer gnolls and across the bridge to the giants area) or a very small circuit, stopping at roughly {{waypoint 227, -21, 1345}} and {{waypoint 203, -2, 1327}} and {{waypoint 195, 2, 1295}}.
  - Now only drops one copy of [[Stiletto's Orders]], so if in a group you will have to kill repeatedly.
- He drops only [[Stiletto's Orders]], but you will not be able to loot them if you have the [[Stiletto's Orders Intercepted]] quest active, or have completed it.
  - It also does not drop if you have the orders in your inventory or in the bank.
