## Strategy

This strategy requires a Pet-Class (we had a necro).
You will have to fight this named in different rooms, the first ones were easy, only the last one was difficult and required the following strategy for us:

When you enter the room, **Fitzpitzle** is standing at the back, move the whole group behind that place and the tank approximately where **Fitzpitzle** is standing. The whole group should stay like this throughout the fight!

**Fitzpitzle** will teleport a lot. We just put the Necro-Pet (caster) on him and let it slowly burn him down - It died a couple of times, so the necro might have to recast it.
Throughout the fight there will be clockwork adds. The tank has to grab aggro from those adds as soon as they pop, otherwise they one-shot group members. With the tank standing in front of the group, this should work and the whole group should focus on killing those adds.
If you are on top of killing adds (killing fast enough) and **Fitzpitzle** is currently at a spot where the group can reach him, you can burn him down a bit until he teleports away again.

Notes from another user:
- Bring him down to 75% in the first room, he will run away.  Kill any remaining adds in that room.  He resets in the other room and will wait for you - don't rush to follow him.
- Bring him down to 50% in the second room.  Same process.
- Bring him down to 25% in the third room.  Adds become more meaningful in each room.
- In the final room, you basically need to follow him around the room.  Add control is a must as they hit hard.  If an add pops, blow it up and as fast as you can.  Key is to manage the adds.  With the right dps and healer you can just burn him directy and not worry about adds.  If low dps/softer gear you might have to go named, add, named, add, etc.
