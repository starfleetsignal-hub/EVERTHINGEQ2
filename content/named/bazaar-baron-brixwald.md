[[Bazaar Baron Brixwald]], wandering the arena, in the [[Fordel Midst: Bizarre Bazaar (Solo)|Fordel Midst: Bizarre Bazaar]] instance.

Encounter notes from the instance walkthrough:

- Note: It's a good idea to either have hotbar spots or macro [[Paludal Catnip]], [[Upgraded Lucky Horseshoe]], and [[Cat's Eye Agate]].
- Use [[9 Lives (Level 120) (Version 2)|9 Lives]] charm before pull.
- He has a knockback; pull him against a wall.
- If you fall into one of the holes use [[Cat's Paw (Level 120)|20 Cat's Paw]] to get out or use the stone as a step.
- If 'Your [[Cat's Eye Agate (Level 120)|Cat's Eye Agate]] begins to glow!'. Use it to interrupt death touch. Spell "Soul Espionage."
- Use [[Paludal Catnip]] Dispels 1000 levels of any hostile effects on group members (AE). Spell "Public Scorn".
- Use [[Upgraded Lucky Horseshoe]] grants immunity to most Stun, Stifle effects, and knockbacks. (blue and purple detriment).

**Note**: He will sometimes cast Public Scorn right before Soul Espionage. Public Scorn must be cleared off to interrupt Soul Espionage.

**Additional tip**: Make sure to right-click on [[Upgraded 30 Grit Sand Rune]] and choose 'auto consume'. If you've died, at any time, make sure to turn off auto consume and reapply it. **This item will dispell Brixwald's stoneskin and make him attackable (take damage).**
