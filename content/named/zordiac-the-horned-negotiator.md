### Strategy

Grouped with three other named gruengach - Clefthoof Archmagus Manno, Praun the Interrogator, and Warlord Yuengtash.

As each one of these named are DPS'd down, at around 70% health they will call a group of various 'a palace gruengach' adds that will rush to join the fight. The general strategy is to kill a named, dps the adds down, kill the next named, dps adds down, rinse and repeat. If you have sufficient survivability and dps you can attempt to have the tank hold the agro on all the adds and try to DPS race them all down.

Recommend killing Zordiac first, as he appears to have some type of healing ability that he will use on the others from time to time.
