## Strategy

Start with a general tank and dps strategy (Note that he uses an ability called Whirling Avalanche that does crushing damage to nearby players in melee range).

Once he reaches a certain percentage, he will become unattackable and an emote will appear citing 'The Construct of Absolution is separating its energy'. Four smaller versions will spawn (Persistence of Absolution, Prevalence of Absolution, Resilience of Absolution, and Resistance of Absolution). Have your tank tag them and pull them into raid to AE DPS down as many of the four as you can. The more of them you kill during this time frame between the Harbinger being dormant, the less work you have to do when he eventually goes dormant again if any were left alive.

Whether you kill all four of them or not during the first phase, any remaining will despawn and the Harbinger will become targetable again and will return to 100% health.

Rinse and repeat, and you'll notice you're able to damage him a bit more than the first round.
