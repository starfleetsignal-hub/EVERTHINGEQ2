Stonesong is a Tier 4 Raid Boss

## Stats

- 123 Flurry Avoidance
- 426 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 9299 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a time limit of 45 minutes.
- Stonesong carries a permanent buff called **Melodic Malady** that has different effects when it is in the "Radiated" state or not.
  - When "Radiated", all scouts and fights within 15 meters of Stonesong receive an increment of **Radiated Notes**. Mages and priests also receive an increment if they are too far away during this time. This state is indicated by a <Radiated> tag underneath Stonesong's name.
  - When not radiated, all mages and priests within 15 meters of Stonesong receive an increment of **Radiated Notes**. Scouts and fighters also receive an increment if they are too far away during this time. This state is indicated by the lack of a tag underneath Stonesong's name.
  - **Radiated Notes** is an incurable arcane that increases damage from all sources and decreases the player's maximum health pool per increment.
- Stonesong casts **Falling Notes**, a curable arcane, on all players within 75 meters. It deals magic damage, and the damage increases based on how far away the players are.
- Stonesong casts **Sonnet of Sustenance**, a curable trauma, on all players within 18 meters. It deals physical damage, increases the amount of physical damage taken from all sources, and slightly heals Stonesong.
- [[A student of the arts]] joins the fight at regular intervals. Each student gives Stonesong one increment of **Staff Support**, which decreases the damage taken from all sources per increment.
- [[A forgotten Maestro]] joins the fight at regular intervals. The Maestro has the same "Radiated" effect as Stonesong with the same penalties. The two can be in the same state simultaneously, and they can also be in opposite states.
- Stonesong does not cast any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] can be used as a simple debuff.
