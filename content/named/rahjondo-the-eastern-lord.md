## Strategy

Dustan the Southern Lord and Rahjondo are one linked encounter, they need to be tanked facing away from the rest of the group with the tanks back to a wall to cope with knockback.

Periodically the tank will be knocked back quite a way, at the same time one of the two mobs will memwipe, so it is recommended to save your snap aggro's (or the dps to save their hate reduction moves) for these times.

Approximate HP = 6,145,000
