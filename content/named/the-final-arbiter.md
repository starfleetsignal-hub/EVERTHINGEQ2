### Challenge Option

To trigger the challenge condition, do the strategy below to activate [[The Final Arbiter]]. Fight it until red text appears ***You must close the portal quickly before things get worse! [challenge].*** Do not interact with the portal. You may need to back-off on damaging [[The Final Arbiter]] to get to this point, takes at least 30 seconds from the time the text appears for it to change red and turn into the epic [[The Enkindled Arbiter]].

## Strategy

Red text appears when you get in the vicinity ***Legions of Awakened flood through the massive chrono-portal!***. This is the trigger of an event where four groups of dragon-kin spill out from the portal, kill them as they come out. Eventually another line of red text appears ***The Arbiter crushes the Final Awakened and turns on you!*** and the named becomes attackable.

- Note: It appears if you get within very close proximity to the arbiter before the waves are dead he will activate prematurely. This may affect the timing order of the script above.

When the text ***You must close the portal quickly before things get worse! [challenge].*** appears, right click the portal to finish the fight in standard mode.

He has a knockback, so recommend piling into the cubby directly behind the portal.
