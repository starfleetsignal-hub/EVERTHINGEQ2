- There are different versions of this fight.  The strategy will vary according to the area in which the two spawn:<br><br>
  1. [[The Deep Quarry]]:  At certain points in the fight, [[Cugdava the Changer (District of Ash Solo)|Cugdava]] (golem form) becomes indestructible. You get a message that a controller for the crusher has become active.  Lead [[Cugdava the Changer (District of Ash Solo)|Cugdava]] under the crusher.  Some bits will spawn. Repeat as needed.<br><br>
  1. [[Forge of Brell (District of Ash Solo)|Forge of Brell]]: Killing [[Cugdava the Changer (District of Ash Solo)|Cugdava's]] [[An undead warsmith|undead warsmiths]] causes her armor to be destroyed or damaged. Use cautious body pulls to get just a couple of warsmiths at a time. When all the warsmiths are dead, kill [[Cugdava the Changer (District of Ash Solo)|Cugdava]], then [[Gavitzle the Charbroiled (District of Ash Solo)|Gavitzle]].
