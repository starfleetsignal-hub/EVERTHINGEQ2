## Notes

During the encounter Mangla *falls down* and spawns multiple [[Mangla the Repelled]] (approx 5,200,000 HP) and multiple [[Repelling Mangla]] (approx 8,200,000 HP). These are all 100^^^ adds that must be defeated before Mangla becomes attackable again. After that it becomes a straight burn down.
