## Strategy

First kill the [[An Allu'thoa rider|Allu'thoa riders]] at the entrance and use their mount to go next to [[Gra'ta the Mountain (Advanced Solo)|Gra'ta the Mountain]]. To make Gra'ta attackable, return along the same road you came in on and kill about four [[An armored ipsumodon|armored ipsumodon]]. Groups of three linked [[A compsognathus|compsognathus]] cause you to take more damage, try to clear them before attacking Gra'ta (look near {{waypoint -3750, 74, -299}} for an area with three nearby). Then go back to Gra'ta and just burn her.
