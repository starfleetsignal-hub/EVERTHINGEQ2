## Strategy

Thorncreep will spawn one or more [[A briarthorn enthraller|briarthorn enthrallers]]. As long as they're up he won't sustain any damage. It's like he's on steroid induced stoneskins. Concentrate on these instead of him and as soon as you kill them, he dies.

## Additional Notes

He constantly spams his primary physical and magical attacks as well as *"Spore Salve"*. Both *Spore Salve* and *Greater Brain Digestion* heal and cure him. Interrupts, roots, mez spells, etc should be used.
