## Debilitating Poison

- Cancels up to 100.0% of target's speed buffs
- Inflicts 635 poison damage on target instantly and every 3 seconds
- Inflicts an additional 1270 poison damage on target every tick
  - This effect cannot be critically applied
- Slows target by 10.0%
