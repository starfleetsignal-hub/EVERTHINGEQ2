## Strategy

![](images/Rage_of_the_Crusader.jpg)When you first enter Arcane Practice and Study, there will be a group of six [[A crazed Drogan ritualist|crazed Drogan ritualist]]s in the center of the room casting a spell at a maelstrom of bones.  Pull them to the edge of the room and kill them.  Have each group member loot a [[Ghostly Channeler's Focus]] off a crazed Drogan ritualist corpse.  The Ghostly Channeler's Focus' are useable from your inventory - drag it to your ability bar for ease of use.

By the time this is done, Crusader of Rok Nilok will have spawned in the middle of the room.  Try to get a quick burn on him.

After he's been up for about 30 seconds, Crusader will cast Rage of the Crusader (see right) on himself. Before he casts it, have one group member use their Ghostly Channeler's Focus.  It will remove Rage of the Crusader if it's on him, and prevent him from casting it for 20 seconds.

When he isn't buffed with Rage of the Crusader, he's a relatively easy tank n' spank - the difficulty is in keeping him unbuffed, because with Rage of the Crusader up he'll tear right through a level appropriate group.
