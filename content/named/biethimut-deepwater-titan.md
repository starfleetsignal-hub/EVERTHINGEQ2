## Strategy

- Final boss of the [Raid] zone, commonly referred to as ***"The Drunk Boss"*** *(For reasons that will soon become clear)*
- In order to spawn this boss, the raid will need to collect the No-Trade Temporary skeleton parts that occasionally drop from the trash Undead and Constructs on the 'Undead Island'.  The dropped parts will need to be 'added' to a small pile sitting inside the tower.  As more parts are added, the pile will grow larger and eventually once the final parts needed are added, the pile will spawn the boss.
  - Depending on RNG of drops, the trash mobs may need to be killed multiple times to acquire enough parts to add to the pile. (they have a relatively quick respawn time)
  - **NOTE: If you leave the current island for ANY reason, the bones currently in your inventory WILL disappear before you can add them to the pile, this includes respawning after death (it sends you to the main zone-in on the previous island).** So, if you're the designated 'looter' of bones for your raid, do NOT choose 'respawn' if you die, only accept resurrections from players on the island itself, otherwise bones in your inventory will poof and looting progress will be lost.
- Spawns [[A Monstrous Fiend]] and occasionally [[A frenzied Skulker]]. (Do not kill Skulkers immediately)
  - Periodically 1 or more members of the raid will be hit with a semi-curable curse (red icon) in the player's detriment window.
  - They will need move a specific distance away from a Skulker *then* move back to within 6m of the fiend for at least 2sec, this will allow that player(s) to be cured.  If the player is not cured (or dies) before the curse expires, it is an automatic raid wipe.
  - The Skulkers are occasionally invisible to players, any kind of see-invis spell/totem will be useful for at least the raid tank(s).
    - The raid as a whole does not necessarily 'need' to see it, as they don't necessarily need to be targetting/killing it.
    - It's important that Skulkers stay up during the fight and are accessible to raid members, due to needing to be at specific ranges for curse curing.
    - If a current Skulker is killed, additional ones spawn periodically outside the normal fight area, and a replacement will need to be pulled into the fight for curse-cure purposes.
- The boss casts, in a repeating sequence, a number of raid-wide detriments.
  - These detriments will need to be cured from the groups, regularly.  If a 2nd detriment of this cycle lands on any individual player before the previous one is cured, it will immediately kill the player.
- At periodic percentages (80%, 65%, etc) the boss will gain a stoneskin, making it immune to damage.
  - A player will need to click on illuminated grave markers/pylons that are scattered throughout the fight area to break the stoneskin.
  - The player that clicks the grave marker, breaking the stoneskin, will gain an uncurable curse for a significant portion of time.
    - If a player with this uncurable curse clicks an additional grave marker, it will wipe the raid.  So, the responsibility of 'breaking' the stoneskin will need to spread around to different members of the raid.
- At the same time that each stoneskin above happens, the boss will cast an AoE - this AoE will kill any member of the raid that is not currently afflicted by a specific uncurable trauma detriment *(Name?)*.
  - To gain this uncurable trauma, each player will need to click on any one of the several "decomposing corpse" that spawn throughout the area
  - Once clicked, the player gains the needed uncurable trauma, and the 'decomposing corpse' turns into a 'disturbed corpse'
    - Clicking on a 'disturbed corpse' is bad - other players will need to wait for it to despawn and new 'decomposing corpses' to spawn in the area.
  - Once the raid-wide AoE goes off (or players die) the trauma is cured, and players will need to regain a fresh application of the trauma, in anticipation of the next AoE.
- While ALL of this is going on - there will be a powerful raid-wide power drain.  Players who are NOT under the influence of alcohol will sustain severe power drains during the entire fight...  Hence the name.
  - Most players bring several stacks of [[Pickleback (Alcoholic)|Pickleback]] (purchased from the bartender near the docks in Sodden) to drink continuously (and/or) put into a /drink macro.
    - Currently, it is not enough to simply put the drink on your hotbar, that will only 'examine' it if you attempt to click from hotbar.  *If you want an easy method of 'click-consumption', you will need to add the item, itself, to an actual in-game [[Macros|macro]] and add that macro to your hotbar to click/consume directly.*
  - There are currently only varying degrees of success using any alcohols other than the Pickleback (it seems designed specifically for this fight).  It may be best to just stick with Pickleback for now.  It is both relatively cheap, stacks to 100 each, and available right outside in Sodden.  Depending on your character's alcohol tolerance skill, 1 stack may be sufficient for several pulls, or you may need several stacks to sustain multiple attempts over the course of a single raid session. You will also gain many levels of alcohol tolerance due to this fight, so you may end up having a high tolerance skill by the end of this.  *Your mileage may vary*
