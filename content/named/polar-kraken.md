## Buffs

### Kraken's Anger

Effects:

- Reduces all damage done to caster by 90%.
- Increases Strikethrough of caster by 50.0%.
- Caster will Riposte 100.0% of incoming frontal attacks. Attacks from other quadrants have the same chance of being Parried.

## Special Attacks

### Constrict

Effects:

- Decreases Crushing, Piercing Slashing, Ranged, Disruption, Subjugation, Ministration and Ordination of target by 63.6.
