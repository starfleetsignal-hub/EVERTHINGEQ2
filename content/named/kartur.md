## Strategy

Kartur becomes invulnerable and will kill you if you try to brute-force him by not placing any of the items by the time he reaches 0% health. Players with VoV raid gear levels of resolve and high DPS in general will need to take caution not to go too hard. As long as you place a couple of the items, you can kill him.
