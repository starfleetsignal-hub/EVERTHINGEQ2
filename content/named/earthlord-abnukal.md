## Spawning method

1. You will need one of each [[A drusy crystal]], [[An elestial crystal]], [[A rutilated crystal]] and [[A tabular crystal]].  Hunt the [[An earthen manifestation|earthen manifestation]]s and [[A lesser earthen manifestation|lesser earthen manifestation]]s for them.  They are [[Treasure Chest|small chest]] drops.  Earthen manifestations seem to drop them at a rate of about 75%, lessers drop them at maybe 25%-33%.  **Get all four crystals** before moving on.
1. Once you have all four crystals, go to the rock pillar with crystals growing from it at {{waypoint -791, -447, 279}}.  Hovering your mouse cursor over some of the crystal growths will show a popup window saying "a drusy crystal", "an elestial crystal"... etc.  Left click a crystal on the rock pillar and if you have a crystal of the corresponding type in your inventory, the crystal on the rock will begin glowing.
1. Once all four crystals are glowing, Earthlord Abnu'kal will spawn immediately.

## Strategy

- He hits fairly hard.  Soloable with a merc, but be careful.
