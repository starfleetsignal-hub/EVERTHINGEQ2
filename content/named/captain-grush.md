## Strategy

45 Second heavy damage aoe, you take less damage the farther away you are, he comes with add's that have significant HP's. Basically a tank and spank with the kicker of jousting the aoe.

Also has a curse that prevents you from using any Combat arts, or it stuns you, as well as disarming you permanently until removed. have to have a cure curse.
