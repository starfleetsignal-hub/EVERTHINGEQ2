Scythe Rotgut is a Tier 2 raid boss.

## Statistics

- 113 Flurry Avoidance
- 330 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 1,500% Max Health
- 9434 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a hard time limit of 60 minutes.
- Every 43 seconds, Rotgut "flaps his wings hard" to cast **Elevate Chances**, a knockback which is also a curable elemental that deals damage to the player. It deals extra damage to those who are farther away.
- Every 44 seconds, Rotgut casts **Rotgut's Rotting Gut**, a curable trauma that deals growing damage over time. If this detriment expires, or if the afflicted player dies, then the player will lose 25% of their armor's durability.
- Rotgut casts **Mana Bleed I**, an incurable trauma that rapidly drains power. This effect can be blocked with [[Power Flex I]] or higher.
- At 80%, 60%, 25%, and 10%, Rotgut flies high into the sky, and a [[Scythecat Scavenger]] will spawn. Rotgut remains in the sky until the Scavenger is defeated. During this time, Rotgut will target a random scout with an incurable curse. The scout will then be pulled into the sky and dropped. Scouts without a minimum Safe Fall skill are killed instantly. (The exact value is unknown but it is greater than 507.)
- When the Scythecat Scavenger is killed, Rotgut returns to the fight with three [[A starving Rotgut ruffian|starving Rotgut ruffians]], each of which casts **Slashdown I**, a curable trauma that deals growing damage over time.
- Rotgut casts a Barrage which can only be blocked with [[Bulwark of Order]]. The damage from this barrage is a curable curse.
