## Special Attacks

### Cloak of Negative Energy

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 8% chance to cast Negative Feedback on target's attacker. Lasts for 7.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 6,021 - 9,032 magic damage on target.
  - Inflicts 2,694 - 4,041 magic damage on target every 7 seconds.
  - Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
  - Decreases Accuracy of target by 41.4%.
  - Spells you cast have a greater than normal chance to be resisted.

### Negative Flux

Effects:

- When a combat art is used this spell will cast Negative Tempest on target.
  - Any spell of combat art you initiate will cause massive negative energies to be released, decimating your raiding party.
- On a spell cast this spell will cast Negative Tempest on target.
  - Any spell of combat art you initiate will cause massive negative energies to be released, decimating your raiding party.
- Interrupts target
- Inflicts 4,373 - 10,203 magic damage on target.
- Inflicts 2,308 - 5,385 magic damage on target every 15 seconds.
- Decreases power of target by 25.0%.
- Decreases power of target by 10.0% every 15 seconds.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Negative Fluxstorm

Effects:

- Inflicts 12,046 - 20,243 magic damage on target instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 7 meters
- Inflicts 6,377 - 11,842 magic damage on target instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - If target is between 7 and 15 meters away
- Inflicts 4,737 - 11,053 magic damage on target instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - If target is between 15 and 9,999 meters away
- Decreases Mitigation of target vs physical and arcane damage by 1,008.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Xythian Storm

Effects:

- Inflicts 76,387 - 158,651 magic damage on target.
- Inflicts 48,477 - 100,682 magic damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 10 meters
- Inflicts 41,584 - 62,376 magic damage on target.
- Inflicts 25,312 - 37,968 magic damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If target is between 10 and 15 meters away
- Inflicts 18,080 - 27,120 magic damage on target.
- Inflicts 10,848 - 16,272 magic damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If target is between 15 and 9,999 meters away
- Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
