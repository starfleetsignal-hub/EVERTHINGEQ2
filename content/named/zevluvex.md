## Notes

- Spawned by slaying the [[A loathsome lich|loathsome liches]] on either side of the [[Basilica of Hate (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Basilica of Hate]] around {{waypoint -81.92, 30.78, -250.67}} and {{waypoint 54.97, 30.62, -257.55}}.
- Periodically he says, "Into the black!" and you are surrounded by blackness for a few seconds.  At the same time he spawns [[Trace of darkness]] and [[A shadow abhorrent]].  These give him a large damage reduction, so kill them before continuing on him.
