## Strategy

Normal heroic adds will spawn periodically throughout the fight.

Mob will heal if not DPS'd down fast enough when he's under 25%.  So save your cooldowns until then.

Killing the mystical droplets will heal the mob. Focus on the main named only.

Easy fight.
