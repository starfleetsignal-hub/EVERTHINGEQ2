## Trivia

- If you check Lynn's active effects once she turns into a dark elf and attacks, you'll notice that the Dark Elf appearance is an illusion. While probably just a design necessity, it could also be inferred that Lynn is infiltrating the Thexians on behalf of an unknown faction, which would make her a *quadruple* agent (she's already infiltrating the monks of Wu for the Nybright Family while secretly working for the Thexians).
