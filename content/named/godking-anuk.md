## Strategy

To defeat the Godking you must also defeat the two mobs that are linked to assist him, [[Power of Anuk]] and [[Armor of Anuk]]. While three tanks are optimum for this fight, *RaidWiki* reports that it can be accomplished with two tanks, one high-aggro fighter such as a Berserker focusing on the [[Power of Anuk]] and [[Armor of Anuk]], and a main tank focusing on the Godking.

Most raiding guilds suggest pulling the [[Power of Anuk]] via a ranged weapon first which will bring the Godking and [[Armor of Anuk]]. Be sure the tank fighting the Godking moves him to a corner of the room away from the other fight. Kill [[Power of Anuk]] first, which will remove the ability of the Godking to cast [[Flame Nova]]. Kill [[Armor of Anuk]] second which will drastically reduce the Godking's defense. Finish off the Godking third.
