## Special Attacks

### Caerina's Agreement

Effects:

- Bound to Drinal's will, sadness fills Caerina's heart as she knows she will never see her beloved again. Deeper into sadness she has fallen finding that her beloved has all but forgotten what she gave up for him to live on.
- On a melee hit this spell will cast Caerina's Sadness on target of attack.
  - Inflicts 39,049 magic damage on target.
    - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Draw Soul

Effects:

- Inflicts 6.0% of max health in magic damage on target instantly and every second.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Decreases power of target by 3.0% instantly and every second.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- If not cured this spell may cause extra abilities to manifest!

### Lost Hopes

Through lost hope and failed dreams and attack on the soul is waging.<br>
<br>
Effects:

- Inflicts 259,998 - 317,775 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4.8 seconds.
- Decreases Casting Speed of targets in Area of Effect by 100.0%
- Cannot be modified except by direct means
