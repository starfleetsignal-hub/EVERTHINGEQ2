Xychintus the Voidcaller is a Tier 2 raid boss.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance: 470.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance: 540.0
- Resolve 11,015.0

## Abilities

- **Nethertides**
- A curable elemental that deals fast ticking increasing elemental damage and increases physical and noxious damage from all sources.
- **Crushing Darkness**
- A curable trauma that deals fast ticking physical damage and increases arcane, elemental, and noxious damage from all sources.
- **Blackhook Voidspike**
- An incurable trauma that deals piercing damage over time.
- **Voidcaller's Curse**
- A curse that significantly decreases the maximum health of the target. If the curse expires, or if the target dies, the entire raid is killed. This curse is initially incurable, and it becomes curable after a set amount of time, depending on the class of the target.
- Fighters: roughly 12 seconds before expiration
- Scouts: roughly 3 seconds before expiration
- Mages:
- Priests: . Curing a priest automatically resets Cure Curse.
- **Tag Team Strike**
- An incurable attack that inflicts 150.0% of max health in poison damage instantly and every second. This effect lands on any player that is attacked by Xychintus and a Void-Soaked Entity simultaneously.
- **Stalwart Admonishment**
- A curable elemental that deals elemental damage and increases all other damage types. This effect only lands on fighters.
- **Summoned Support**
- A buff that decreases damage taken from all sources for each [[Void-Soaked Entity]] engaged in the fight.
- **Void Banishment**
- An incurable arcane that deals massive amounts of magic damage over time to all players within 15 meters.
- **Static Voidfield**
- "Static Voidfield". A buff that appears at 50%.

## Strategy

Throughout the fight, Rift Portals appear on a random raid member. These portals can be targeted and killed. If the portal is not killed within 25 seconds, a [[Void-Soaked Entity]] arrives through the portal. When the Entity dies, another Rift Portal appears in its place.
