Hambalumi, the Mhyt-moo Migmu, is a Tier 3 raid boss.

## Stats

- 118 Flurry Avoidance
- 337 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 9194 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a time-limit of 45 minutes.
- Though Hambalumi has the **Teamwork: Leader** buff, there are no enemies in this fight that carry the corresponding Teamwork buff.
- Hambalumi casts a curable arcane called **Unchecked Devotion II** which deals increasing arcane damage and increases all incoming non-arcane damage.
- Hambalumi casts a curable curse called **Waveform** which, if allowed to expire, instantly kills the entire raid.
- Hambalumi alternates between "releasing a depth charge" and "drag[ing] you to the depths".
  - When releasing a depth charge, all players within 15 meters receive an incurable curse named **Depth Charge**. The curse does not directly affect the player it lands on, but any other players within 8 meters of the afflicted are instantly killed.
  - When dragging players to the depths, all players too far away receive an incurable curse. Similarly, this curse does not directly affect the player it lands on, but any other players within 8 meters of the afflicted are instantly killed.
- Hambalumi casts **Mana Bleed I**, which drains power from the entire raid. This effect can be blocked with [[Raid Rune: Power Flex I]] or higher.
- Hambalumi casts **Slashdown II**, a curable trauma that deals fast-growing damage over time.
- Additional [[Seafoam bubbles]] join the fight in groups of three.
- Hambalumi does not cast any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] can be used as a simple debuff.
