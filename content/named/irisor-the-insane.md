## Notes

Irisor is part of the quest [[Two Wrongs Do Not Make a Right]].  Each time you examine a specific object for the quest, he pops up to kill.  He gradually gets a bit tougher for each appearance, specifically:

- In [[The Crypt of Betrayal]] (loc 84, 7, -74), he's level 25^^
- In [[Antonica]] (loc -1911, -32, -519), he's level 27^
- In [[The Thundering Steppes]] (loc 389, 1, 890), he's 28^^^
- In [[The Ruins of Varsoon]] (loc 143, -4, 30), he's 29^^^

He doesn't have any amazing attacks, nor does he require a special strategy to kill.  He drops no loot, but each different version of him grants AA.
