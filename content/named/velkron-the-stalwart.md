## Strategy

- Highly recommend clearing the surrounding droags, drakes and patrolling osseous destroyer golems to prevent accidental agro during the encounter.

During the fight will periodically spawn and "absorb" [[An animated toxoid husk]], increasing Velkron's power. We just continued to focus DPS on Velkron in the nearby cubby to try to avoid any knockbacks.
