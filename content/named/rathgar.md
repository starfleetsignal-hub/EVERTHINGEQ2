## Strategy

- Rathgar will periodically one-shot the tank.  At least 2 are needed so one can grab aggro when the other dies.
- During the fight, Rathgar will spawn an earth elemental add.  This add must be burned down quickly or Rathgar will kill it and gain 2 levels.
- Every time a player dies, Rathgar gains a level.
  - This does not apply to the tanks getting one-shotted.
- If Rathgar reaches level 100, he unleashes an AOE that will wipe the raid.
