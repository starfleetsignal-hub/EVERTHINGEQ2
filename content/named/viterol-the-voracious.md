## Abilities

- **Noxious Claws**
- A curable noxious that deals fast ticking poison damage to targets within 45 meters and increases incoming physical damage from all sources.
- **Strategic Stab I**
- A curable trauma that deals fast-growing damage over time.
- **Blinding Talon**
- A curable elemental that deals growing noxious damage. When cured, it triggers a blind effect to allies within 8 meters.
- **Falling Skies**
- A curable trauma that interrupts targets, inflicts increasing crushing damage over time, drains power over time, decreases potency of targets, reduces casting range of targets, and roots targets.
- **Voracious Plumage**
- A curable trauma that deals light damage over time. If this spell is still active when Purge Plumage is cast, then Viterol heals for each affected player.
- **Purge Plumage**
- A curable arcane that deals light damage over time. Viterol then heals for each player with Voracious Plumage.
- **Elevate Body**
- A curable elemental that knocks the target back, dealing more elemental damage the farther the target is from the caster.

## Strategy

Viterol occasionally marks his target to be ignored for 30 seconds. This player is unable to hold Viterol's attention during this time.
