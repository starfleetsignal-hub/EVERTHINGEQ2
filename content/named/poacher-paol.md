Poacher Paol is a Tier 2 raid boss.

## Statistics

- 113 Flurry Avoidance
- 330 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 1,500% Max Health
- 9434 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a hard time limit of 60 minutes.
- Paol has a pet falcon named **Astra**.
  - Astra passively boosts the offense and defense abilities of Paol.
  - Astra casts a curable trauma named **Slashdown I** which deals growing physical damage over time.
  - Astra casts a curable detriment named **Piercing Sands** which deals damage over time and drains mana.
- Soon after the fight starts, a pride of six peafowl arrives, surrounding Paol in a circle.
  - Each peafowl has a Wounds counter and a Courage counter. The Wounds counter starts at 0 for each peafowl. The amount of Courage depends on the number of players in the raid force.
    - 24 players = 10 Courage
    - 16-18 players = 6 Courage
    - 15 players = 4 Courage
    - 12-13 players = 3 Courage
    - 1-11 players = 2 Courage
  - A peafowl is considered Healthy if it has a Wounds count of 0. A peafowl is considered disabled if it has a Wounds count greater than or equal to its Courage count.
  - A peafowl can be poked to make it raise its feathers, which can then be used as a shield against Paol's most powerful attack. After this attack, the peafowl gains one point of Wounds for each player that hid behind its features. If this damage exceeds the peafowl's Courage, a number of players equal to the excess will die anyway. A peafowl cannot be used for protection if it is disabled.
  - Killing Astra causes each peafowl to decrease its Wounds count, unless the peafowl is disabled. If the peafowl has not recently been used to block an attack, its Wounds count resets to 0. Otherwise, the decrease amount depends on the number of players in the raid force.
    - 18 players = 2 Wounds points
    - 12-16 players = 1 Wounds point
- Paol has a melee poison effect named **Hemotoxin**, which is a curable noxious detriment that deals low damage to Paol's current target.
- At 85%, 75%, 65%, 55%, 45%, 35%, 25%, and 15%, Paol re-summons Astra and "draws back on her bowstring" to deliver a powerful attack that instantly kills all players in sight. This can be avoided by hiding behind the feathers of a peafowl. This attack also deactivates the effect from [[Rune of Bloodbound Torment]].
- Paol does not cast any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] can be used as a simple debuff.
