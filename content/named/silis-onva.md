## Special Attacks

### Crushing Force

Effects:

- Inflicts 50.0% of max health in crushing damage on target instantly and every 3 seconds.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Makes target immune to Root effects
- Stuns target
- Dazes target
- Target can not cast beneficial spells.
- Target can not cast hostile spells.
- To remove this fatal effect you must first remove the Crushing Stomp curse.

### Crushing Stomp

Effects:

- Decreases Crit Chance of target by 50.0.
- Decreases Crit Bonus of target by 50.0.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means
