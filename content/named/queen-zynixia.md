## Spawning Queen Zynixia

1. Kill either [[The Refinery Overseer]] or [[A bixie retainer|bixie retainer]]s to get a chest containing [[A piece of cork]].
1. Jump into the jumjum pit under her location in [[The Queen's View]] and click the four pipes. The queen then turns into a mob and attacks.
   - The cork is not consumed when used on the pipes, and can be saved for future runs of the zone.
