## Strategy

### **Clockwork Scrounger XVII**

- Clear out trash mob nearby your next named door for some space
- Each player must be **12m** or above **distances** to avoid "Short Circuit"during combat, especially when it says "*Short circuit detected ...* ".
- Melee players like scouts and fighters try to use ascending spells during the fight
- "**Short circuit**" deals a lot damage enough to one shot a 60M HP player. It generates from 2 close player(within 12 m) and cause damage to others and jump across people just like "***Chain Lightning"*** in most of *DND* games
- Named buff with stoneskin every time end of "**Short circuit**".
- "**Sprocket Spray**" dealing 33.4M damage and drain 557k mana. Death prevention and stoneskin matters. It only appears when it changes target every time.
- "**Zap**!" deals 8.5 heat damage to those who close together when it below 50% hp
