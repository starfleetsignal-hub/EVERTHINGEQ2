Beckut, the Mad Zealot is a T3 raid boss.

## Stats

- 118 Flurry Avoidance
- 337 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 9539 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a hard time limit of 45 minutes.
- Beckut casts a curable curse named **Zealous Destruction** on a random healer. At the same time, a [[Fanatical Follower]] joins the fight. If the curse is cured while the Follower is still alive, or if the curse expires, the entire raid is killed.
- Beckut casts an incurable curse named **Practitioner of Pain** on his current target that kills the target and the target's group. The curse also heals Beckut for 15% of his max health. Both effects are prevented if a priest in the target's group casts their [[Renewal of Ro Epic Spells|Renewal of Ro Epic Spell]] before the curse expires, and if that spell is Master tier or better.
- At random intervals, Beckut casts **Mana Bleed I**, which drains power from the entire raid. This effect can be blocked with [[Raid Rune: Power Flex I]] or higher.
- Beckut does not cast any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] can be used as a simple debuff.
