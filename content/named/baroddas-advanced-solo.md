## Strategy

When Baroddas is engaged all a shiverback terror still alive will add.<br>
When Baroddas reaches 15% hit points he becomes invulnerable for a short amount of time and heals himself to 50% hit points. This is advertised in chat with "Baroddas has become angry!"
