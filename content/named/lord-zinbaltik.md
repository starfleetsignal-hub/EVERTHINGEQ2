## Strategy

Clear out all mobs around him - will be unable to clear the two behind. There is a good chance he will link the [[Tainted hill cat|tainted hill cats]] behind him to the left - so prepare to kill them as well.  This is a solo instance mobs so despite the ease of the zone, take your time preparing for him.  If you rely on a pet, be well aware he'll probably sleep it or you right off the bat.
