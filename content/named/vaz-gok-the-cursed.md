## Strategy

Vaz'Gok the Cursed himself has a nasty AE stun that can stun an entire raid for up to 15 seconds.  He also has a 360 degree AoE knockback, and attempts to heal himself every 25%.  The healing can be interrupted without too much trouble by spamming stuns and knockdowns (he can be knocked down), and an Inquisitor's Punishments may work as well.
