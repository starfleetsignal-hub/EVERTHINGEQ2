## Strategy

- Bloodchief Tzabos has two tricks up his sleeve. *Aura of Fright* causes 10 seconds of Fear, so be crouching.  Second, at about 40 second into the battle he'll teleport away and summon three [[An elemental blaze|elemental blazes]].  Once the blazes are down, he'll re-aggro on his own and run back to you.
