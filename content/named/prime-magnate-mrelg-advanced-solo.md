## Info

Prime Magnate Mrelg is located at his altar between two flaming wooden pillars. When he is between these two pillars he is not directly targetable, even when not engaged in battle.

He has two modes: Easy and Challenge Mode. If you want to enter Challenge Mode, then you can begin killing him at his altar. The strategy for this is the same as heroic and detailed on the Heroic Page. ([[Prime Magnate Mrelg (Heroic)|Prime Magnate Mrelg (Heroic))]]

If you want to avoid Challenge Mode, you will need to first pull him away from his alter. Agro him and pull him down one of the ramps, about 30% down. It is likely that in this time he will call snakes to help him. The snakes temporarily stun you.

When you are far enough away, you will see him become directly clickable. Now you can begin killing him. At 80% life (20% damage) he will say "The Prime Magnate begins to gather darkness around him [challenge]". Your cursor will turn into a hand and you will be able to click him. Alternatively, you can right click him and choose "Disrupt the ritual". Once disrupted, he does not seem to call anymore snakes and can be killed quite easily.
