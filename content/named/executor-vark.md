## Strategy

He has a rather annoying knockback, so just tank him against the wall and healers out of range of the AoE, and he is mostly a straight burn.

## Notes

- He hits you with a Nuclear Force area of attack that does **22,750** damage.
- <s>A group seems to be required for this mob. The reason for this is if you backup out of range while your pet attacks, then Kierax will disappear and the fight will end. A moment later he will reset.</s>
- (Shadow Odyssey Chapter 2: The Gods must be crazy) When you enter the area, there is an NPC named **Adjunct Zorkus** present.  If you attack him, he will *knock you back and reset*. He will make his way over to **Executor Vark** and begin a dialog.  Executor Vark *will kill* Adjunct Zorkus and then turn his attention to you.  You can now fight Executor Vark.
