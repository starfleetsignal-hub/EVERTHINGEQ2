## Strategy

- Appears running a path around the top of the pillars after the warlocks and the other 2 named *(?)* from the quest *(what quest?)* are killed *(dubious information: there is a mob looking very much like a placeholder : "a warlock groundskeeper", unique mob roaming in the graveyard zone).*
- The named is flying and non-aggro, keep an eye up.
- Casts a very nasty group poison dot that MUST be cured ASAP.  This thing does around 1.5K of Focus damage per tick, and ticks multiple times a second.
- Specialist's Cure Noxious potions should be used by each group member when dot appears.
