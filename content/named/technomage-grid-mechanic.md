## Note

One of the [[Sentinel's Fate Rare Named]] that does not spawn in every instance.

Spawns in the laser eye puzzle hallway.

Casts "Mandate":  You will not be able to cast or target the name for 10 minutes.  Also, any AoEs will just simply knock you away from him.  If you're doing this zone solo, send in your merc so it gets the debuff - and just cure it off them if you're a healer - or usually just let the merc do the work - sit back and relax.
