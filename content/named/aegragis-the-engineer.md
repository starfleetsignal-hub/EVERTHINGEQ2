## Strategy

- There are clusters of barrels in the vicinity - make sure not to fight near them initially.
- Aegragis will summon red circles throughout the fight - joust out from them to avoid taking damage.
- At 75% health Aegragis will stop taking damage. Run to the nearest set of barrels and tank him there until he casts a red circle. Joust out and he'll cause an explosion that allows him to be attacked again.
- Repeat the above steps until he gets to 25%, then take him to the remaining barrels to once again until he causes an explosion. Burn him down.
