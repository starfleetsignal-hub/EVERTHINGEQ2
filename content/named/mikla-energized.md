### Sage's Scorn

Effects:

- Target will lose 41% more power when power is consumed.
- 288% of target's power consumed will also be drained from target's health.
- When beginning a hostile spell cast this spell has a 5% chance to cast Triumvirate Bolt on target. Lasts for 0.5 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 1,300,577 - 2,701,196 magic damage on target.
  - Inflicts 406,427 - 844,129 magic damage on target every 0.5 seconds.
  - Stuns target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 1.5 seconds and duration is reduced to 0.2 seconds.
- When a combat art is used this spell has a 5% chance to cast Triumvirate Bolt on target. Lasts for 0.5 seconds.
  - Inflicts 1,300,577 - 2,701,196 magic damage on target.
  - Inflicts 406,427 - 844,129 magic damage on target every 0.5 seconds.
  - Stuns target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 1.5 seconds and duration is reduced to 0.2 seconds.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means
