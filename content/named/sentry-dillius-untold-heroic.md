Ability and strategy information changes between stages (1-20/21+).

## Abilities

- **Pillar of Life**
- A buff that increases combat mitigation. Dispelling this ability casts Listless Life on the caster.
- **Listless Life**
- A curable elemental that wounds the target's health by (25%/40%). Curing this ability reapplies Pillar of Life to Dillius.
- **War Torn**
- An incurable arcane that inflicts divine damage over time, decreases power over time, and has a chance to cast Defensive Breach, which inflicts crushing damage.
- **Ghostly Presence**
- An incurable elemental that reduces healing received by 40% and drains 40% of all wards every second while no priests are within 5 meters of Dillius.
- **Ghastly Swing**
- An incurable trauma that makes non-priest targets afraid, inflicts slashing damage, inflicts extra slashing damage over time to non-priests, and reduces fervor of non-priests.
- **Unholy Touch**
- An incurable detriment
- **Unyielding Pain**
- An incurable elemental that can be removed with [[Intercept]].
- **Allies of Truth [21+]**
- A buff that increases Dodge and reduces all damage done for each [[A Spirit of Truth|Spirit of Truth]] engaged in combat. If 5 Spirits are actively fighting, the entire group is killed.
- **Pillar of Truth [21+]**
- A buff that increases combat mitigation. Dispelling this ability casts Truthseer on the caster. If both Pillar of Truth and Pillar of Life are active, Pillar of Life is dispelled first.
- **Truthseer [21+]**
- A curable elemental that  and allows the target to see [[A Spirit of Truth]]. Curing this ability reapplies Pillar of Truth to Dillius.

## Strategy

[Stage 21+] Dillius summons [[A Spirit of Truth]]. Initially, this Spirit is only visible to the player with Truthseer. Once dispelled, the Spirit is visible to all players.
