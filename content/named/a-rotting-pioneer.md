## Notes

- Spawns with 3 [[An undead explorer|undead explorers]], 2 21v Heroic and 1 21 Heroic.
- There are several places that undead spawn in the Fallow Farmlands zone. The undead farmers, undead settlers, and undead explorers all share the same spawn table, and where the undead explorers spawn, the Rotting Pioneer can appear. It will spawn with several undead explorers.
