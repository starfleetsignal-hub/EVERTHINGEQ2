## Strategy

When you go up against this Mob have your group stand under the rocks to the left of Wumpus and your tank can pull him to the group.  This will prevent you from flying back when he uses his Knock Back.  Makes life a lot easier. Make sure everyones back is against the rocks, or you will end up flying over them.  Thank you guys for your help.

Phantomwolf of Permafrost.

The AOE is called Thunderous Stomp. They come 52 seconds apart.
