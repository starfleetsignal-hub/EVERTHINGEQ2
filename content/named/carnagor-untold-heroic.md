Ability and strategy information changes at stages (1-20/21-40/41+).

## Abilities

- **Infernal Reach**
- A buff that has a chance to cast Infernal Strike when receiving a hostile ability, which inflicts heat damage multiplied by increments. If the target is not a fighter, the 10th increment kills the target. Infernal Strike has a range of 3 meters.
- **Sledge of Oblivion**
- An incurable noxious that deals disease damage over time, decreases power over time, and procs Defensive Breach, which deals magic damage.
- **Boiling Point**
- An incurable elemental that deals more damage the closer the target is to Carnagor. This damage can only be mitigated by fighters. It kills the target if they are in the boiling water.
- **Boiling Bastion**
- A buff that prevents all damage from targets without Boiling Point.
- **Gore Galore**
- A curable curse that lands on (3/4/5) people initially. If anyone cursed dies, everyone else cursed dies. If only one or zero remain cursed, Carnagor curses up to (3/4/5) more targets.
- **Infernal Carnage [41+]**
- A buff that increases flurry, flurry multiplier, and fervor every 2 seconds. It can be dispelled by arcane means.
- **Phantom Flux [41+]**
- A buff that increases combat mitigation, ability doublecast avoidance, flurry avoidance, and dodge for each fractured phantasm that is killed. At 4 increments, the entire group is killed instantly.
- **Grav Influx [41+]**
- A buff that roots Carnagor in the water and increases his melee weapon range. It also pulls all players towards him continually.
- **Immortal Technique [41+]**
- A buff that applies to all players in group when [[An immortal phantasm]] dies. It increases melee weapon range, prevents caster from being interrupted, and makes the player immune to Grav Influx, the waters of Nightscar Basin, and from fractured phantasm knockbacks.

## Strategy

When the fight starts, a large red circle appears around the platform, partially extending into the basin. Standing inside this circle applies Boiling Point.

Carnagor continuously summons [[A fractured phantasm]], which knock back players, if at least 4 players are within 3 meters.
