## Abilities

- **Infernal Reach**
- A buff that has a chance to cast Infernal Strike when receiving a hostile ability, which inflicts heat damage multiplied by increments. Infernal Strike has a range of 3 meters.
- **Sledge of Oblivion**
- An incurable noxious that deals disease damage over time, decreases power over time, and procs Defensive Breach, which deals magic damage.
- **Boiling Point**
- An incurable elemental that deals more damage the closer the target is to Carnagor. This damage can only be mitigated by fighters. It kills the target if they are in the boiling water.
- **Boiling Bastion**
- A buff that prevents all damage from targets without Boiling Point.
- **Gore Galore**
- A curable curse that lands on 3 people initially. If anyone cursed dies, everyone else cursed dies. If only one or zero remain cursed, Carnagor curses up to 3 more targets.

## Strategy

When the fight starts, a large red circle appears around the platform, partially extending into the basin.

Carnagor continuously summons [[A fractured phantasm]], which knock back players, if at least 4 players are within 3 meters.
