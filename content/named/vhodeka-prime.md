## Buffs

### Lesser Rage of the Awakened

Effects:

- Lesser Rage of the Awakened grants power to the wearer.

## Special Attacks

### Battering Onset

Effects:

- Inflicts 249,280 - 304,675 crushing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases DPS of targets in Area of Effect by 270.0 - 330.0.
- Decreases Reuse Speed of targets in Area of Effect by 31.5 - 38.5%.
- Decreases Mitigation of targets in Area of Effect vs physical damage by 4,800.
- Decreases healing by up to -2200.
- Decreases Spell Damage by up to -2200.

### Crushing Weight

Effects:

- The crushing weight of debris is unavoidable.

### Levigating Assault

Effects:

- Inflicts 43,895 - 65,842 melee damage on targets in Area of Effect
- Reduces healing received by 30.00%.
- Roots targets in Area of Effect
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Manifested Zephyr

Effects:

- Inflicts 22.5 - 27.5% of max health in magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 5 seconds.
- Inflicts an additional 0.9 - 1.1% of max health on targets in Area of Effect every tick.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Decreases Mitigation of targets in Area of Effect vs physical damage by 3,564.

### Quell

Effects:

- A massive crushing strike that debuffs outgoing melee damage.

### Tumble

Effects:

- Inflicts 63.0 - 77.0% of max health in crushing damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases Casting Speed of targets in Area of Effect by 100.0%.
- Reduces healing received by 75.00%.

### Will of the Defender

Effects:

- Opponents have a 12% chance every 20 secs to be struck with Dissolving Bulwark.

- +750 faction with **Claws of Veeshan**
