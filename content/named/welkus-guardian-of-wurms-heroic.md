## Strategy

- Begin the engagement close to the 'center' of the Z shaped path if possible.
- Text will flash across the screen about a portal spawning nearby - Welkus will become immune to damage until it is destroyed. The portal will be at either one of the 2 corners of the 'Z' or at the dead ends nearby.
- Welkus will also spawn circles on the ground that can do massive damage if you linger in them for too long, so make sure to move out if you see it appear.
