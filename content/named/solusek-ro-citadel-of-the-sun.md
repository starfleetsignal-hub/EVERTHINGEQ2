## Strategy

He basically has 3 dps checks a curse cure and jumping from inside to outside and back. 96% 55% and 12% are the %'s where the dps check occurs its when he says "show me your BURN", the dps checks are a 4-5% range you have to get him down by to succeed Ex: 12%-7% which he'll says " impressive work" on success of passing it. you jump on the triangles to the outer ring when the flames are gone completely if you jump early instakill occurs good to wait maybe 1 or 2 secs after its gone to be sure usually flames appear after he ports to other side within 1-2 secs. The cure curse will happen on usually the tank fail to cure causes wipe. The adds do nothing and the contained flames don't either ignore them.

Quick Summary: You can straight burn him past first dps check at start of fight if good enough dps then jump over, cure curse, then dps til 57% wait til he ports to center, cure curse, jump back dps again til almsot 12%, then jump out, cure,wait til center then finish em. jumping and getting cures is hard part and maybe dps checks if not fast enough but very easy fight.
