## Notes

- There may be certain conditions you have to meet to spawn Akh'Mun Rhoen. See the talk page for more information.
- Before spawning a vampire (Malkonis D'Morte) will spawn and go into a long story. (My logging stopped working because of a client crash so I don't have his name).
- There is a climbable wall at loc {{waypoint -63, 1.5, 340}} which will take you to the top of the tower.
- Defeating Ahk'Mun Rhoen will reward you with the achievement [[Rhoen Rampager]] and your guild the same title if they meet the [[Guild Achievements]] requirements.

## Strategy

Ahk'Mun Rhoen possess a strong knock back effect and a long duration stun. It is highly recommended that you not fight him on the tower where he spawns. Choose one person to climb the tower and aggro Ahk'Mun Rhoen. Then pull him down and have your tank put their back against something to keep Ahk'Mun Rhoen from wandering around while you fight. It is entirely possible to wipe due too the long duration stun if the knock back is not controlled. Other than that it is a fairly straight forward fight. Keep healers unstunned, keep slow on the epic monster and, and keep tanks back to the wall.
