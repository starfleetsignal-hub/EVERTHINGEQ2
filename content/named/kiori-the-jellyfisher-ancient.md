Kiori, the Jellyfisher Ancient, is a Tier 3 raid boss.

## Stats

- 118 Flurry Avoidance
- 337 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 9194 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a time limit of 45 minutes.
- Kiori casts **Turning Tides**, a curable curse that lands on all members of the raid simultaneously. This curse transforms players into baitfish, making them a tasty snack for the nearby Hippolisks. Players eaten by Hippolisks are killed. Additionally, any damage dealt by a player who is afflicted with the curse will instantly be regenerated, effectively making Kiori immune to all attacks.
- Starting at 50%, jellyfisher eggs spawn throughout the ocean. The eggs tend to spawn in bursts of three.
  - If a player approaches an egg, it begins drifting towards them, following them as they swim around.
  - If a Hippolisk approaches an egg (including eggs following players), the Hippolisk instantly eats the egg, preventing it from hatching.
  - If allowed to hatch, [[A mature jellyfisher]] joins the fight. Kiori is protected from damage while the mature jellyfisher is alive, and the mature jellyfisher can trigger a cooperative strike against Kiori's target. When the mature jellyfisher dies, Kiori heals back to full health.
- Kiori does not cast any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] can be used as a simple debuff.
