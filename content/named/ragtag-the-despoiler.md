Ragtag the Despoiler is a Tier 3 raid boss.

## Stats

- 118 Flurry Avoidance
- 337 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 9194 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a 45 minute time limit.
- Ragtag casts **Mana Bleed II**, which drains power from the entire raid. This effect can be blocked with [[Raid Rune: Power Flex II]] or higher.
- Ragtag casts **Despoiling Touch**, a curable noxious that deals fast-growing poison damage over time.
- Ragtag casts **Despoiling Slam**, a curable trauma that deals increasing physical damage and increases all incoming non-physical damage.
- Ragtag casts **Cascade of Spoiling**, an incurable elemental, every 20 seconds on players within 30 meters. Each increment increases damage from all sources by 25% and reduces maximum health and maximum power by 25%. The detriment naturally expires (removing all increments simultaneously) while standing more than 30 meters away.
- Ragtag periodically "summons an intense storm" and drops a large yellow circle on the ground. Two (but no more than two) of these circles may exist at the same time.
- Ragtag does not cast any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] can be used as a simple debuff.
