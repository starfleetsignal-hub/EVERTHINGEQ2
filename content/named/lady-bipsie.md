## Strategy

- Having enough DPS (burned solo, no merc, Inquisitor LvL100, Startekit of PlaneOfMagic) or...
- At regular times she emotes and calls in 2 adds before teleporting to a nearby catwalk. The whole group must immediately run to her new location, bringing the 2 adds along, as she will start casting a deadly AoE as soon as she has ported. Kill the 2 adds and turn your attention back to her until the next emote occurs. The teleportation destinations seem random so she might gate to the same spot she was at, making it easier.

## Note

After you have kill [[Lady Bipsie]] 3 [[A Brokenskull bouncer]] spawn and attack you.
