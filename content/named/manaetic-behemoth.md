## Strategy

Dodge buzzsaws on floor they instakill. Gotta goto correct lever in halls to prevent adds if they get to the room/pillar you wipe. People in control room have to turn knobs to add up to door codes to open them(presumed). If under level 120 you'll need glacier freeze to lower his level to fight him. People will get hit with a glacier freeze everyone keep 25m away or it spreads and people die. At 5%/6% he will stoneskin you need "machined energy inverters" or "veilwalker energy inverters" to cast on his 20 stack buff along with normal dispel to remove this to kill him.
