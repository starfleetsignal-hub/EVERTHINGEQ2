## Notes

Bloodmoon (named) begins as [[Bloodmoon (NPC)]]. If you are on the quest [[Ascension Assistance]] you will need to talk to him. After a brief discussion the NPC will change into an aggressive named, which you must kill to receive [[The Horn of Ancient Howls]].
