## Notes

- Splits into [[A Virulent Corpuscle]] and [[A Healthy Corpuscle]] at 50% HP, which must be separated. If they are not, they will rejoin as a new Amorphous Drake at 100% hp. Kill Virulent Corpuscle first, then kill Healthy Corpuscle to end the encounter.
- Frontal cone heat AE
- Immune to piercing
