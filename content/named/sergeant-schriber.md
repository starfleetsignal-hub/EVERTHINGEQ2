## Strategy

- On engaging Sergeant Schriber, 5-6 [[A Trained Soldier]] (zombie) will spawn around the edges of the room and begin walking slowly toward the named. If the adds reach the named, they heal him.
- For each [[A Trained Soldier]] that is killed, a new one spawns at one of the edges of the room (and again, begins slowly walking to the named)
- [[A Trained Solder]] can be mezzed or rooted

- Having an off tank aggro all the zombies will not work, as the Sarge will "rescue" you from them by taunting the zombies back over to him.
- Place the main tank in the center of the room, keep the Sarge in place and burn him down with a healer nearby.  Split the rest of the group up with two on either side of the room torching the soldiers as they arise.  [[A Trained Soldier]] is very weak for its level, so they can be taken down repeatedly without much trouble.
