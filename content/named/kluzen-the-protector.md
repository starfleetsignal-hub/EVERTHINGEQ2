## Strategy

Kluzen is the easiest of the named monsters in [[Veeshan's Peak]]. Before the fight, make sure to clear all of the nearby trash mobs, as they are roamers- don't forget the [[Drakota]] flying overhead.

Kluzen is practically a straight burn. He does a large AOE called wingbeat (45 seconds) that must be announced and jousted, but that is all.
