## Notes

Coltang is spawned in a small ring event at the given location. Simply clear the tigers around the totem until he spawns.  It has been reported that he may spawn as a ^^ equal to your player level.  If Coltang is not spawning, have a player with the quest do the killing (Coltang seems to spawn as a check based on if the person getting the kill of a tiger has the quest).

## Related Quests

- [[Justice for JoJo]] - After killing Coltang, right-click and inspect his corpse to get the tiger claws needed to update this quest.
