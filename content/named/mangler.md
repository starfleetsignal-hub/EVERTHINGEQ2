## Trivia

- Mangler was originally an Epic x2. He was downgraded to ^^^Heroic some time before 2011, and for some reason was not effected by the great un-nerfing of 2020.
- In the days of EverQuest 1, Mangler was a live guarddog living in Rivervale.  He was notorious for attacking *anyone* who crossed his path regardless of level, race and faction, and for causing massive faction hits if he was killed.
- Two of his presumed descendants, [[Mangler XII]] and [[Mangler XIII]], lived in [[The Baubbleshire]] until [[LU64]], when The Enchanted Reapers attacked.  They are currently missing and presumed dead.
