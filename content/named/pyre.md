## Strategy

The screen will emote something about Pyre's body or mass annealing. When this happens, it is a good idea to kill the adds that spawn so that the buff he gets will go away. When it emotes "Pyre's fists harden!" it just means that he gets a buff in attack and defense. No adds spawn. Otherwise, pretty much a basic tank-and-spank.
