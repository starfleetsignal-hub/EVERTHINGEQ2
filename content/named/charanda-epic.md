## Strategy

Slow Burn

- Charanda herself has a low HP pool and does not hit overtly hard, so recommend having a tank run forward and keeping her contained near the desk where she resides at. Most of the DPS done to her will likely be from the tanks keeping her contained, and then short bursts from the raid when the three mechanics have been dealt with.

- There are three mechanics to this fight: Selective target adds, Portia zombie add, and the green gas cloud.

1. The three adds - 'an Undead Despoiler', 'an Undead Focuser', 'an Undead Ruiner'. When these spawn, have the person tanking Charanda look for a yellow buff icon that will cite one of the three adds as being under Charanda's protection. Whichever one is listed in the buff DO NOT KILL. Have the main assist target the other two adds and burn them down.
1. Portia Rumuffin's reanimated corpse add - Similar to the non raid instances, Portia will enter the fray and must be burned down repeatedly until she reaches 0, then you can repeat the other steps.
1. Green gas clouds will fill the air, the raid must "duck" to stay under the floating gas clouds, there will be some spam text on success that you are safe from the gas and then a yellow text will appear "**The noxious gas cloud dissipates and Charanda begins looking around the room to see who is left! Don't give away your strategy!** As soon as this message appears the raid needs to stop ducking - easiest way to do this is to just hit the jump key once.

Once the two correct adds have been dealt with as well as Portia, the raid can burn down Charanda until the next set of "3" adds spawns. Then repeat the above strategy.
