## Strategy

The [[An arcane disruptor|arcane disruptors]] are adds that spawn throughout the fight and must be burned down promptly. The add tells the raid "You dare not slay me, lest I curse all casters near!" and appears to cast a spell called Arcane Dampening. The arcane disruptors can only be damaged by melee, and the script includes a fail effect.
