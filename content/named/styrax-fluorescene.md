## Strategy

This fight consists of 2 bosses linked (Styrax Fluorescene the Treant, and Anthocyanin the Flower). After clearing the room, the Boss will become aggro.

- Under the Red Trees, your Potency will go up infinitely, but the higher it is, the harder you will be hit by a DoT.
- Under the Blue Trees, you'll have a buff that increases your healing, but drains your Potency. And the Pathway is Neutral (Neither buff)

Begin the Fight By pulling the boss into the Neutral and focus down Anthocyanin dealing as little damage as possible on Styrax. You want to kill the flower before getting Styrax to 75% otherwise the flower will change colour and you will need to drag it into it's opposite side that it emotes.<br>
When the Flower is dead, Styrax will go into a rage mode at that point drag Styrax into the blue trees so his rage becomes manageable. Then just burn from there.

Update: However this fight was originally designed, there no longer seems to be any reason to move the boss around. Pull them into the area with the red trees, get the Nature's Rage buff, kill Anthocyanin and then Styrax. Particularly if you're soloing this, you'll want the potency buff to overcome their healing which is annoying and just prolongs the fight. There isn't a significant damage increase when he enrages.
