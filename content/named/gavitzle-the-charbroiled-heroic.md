## Strategy

1. If you clear the  first, [[Cudgava (Heroic)|Cudgava]] and [[Gavitzle the Charbroiled (Heroic)|Gavitzle the Charbroiled]] will spawn in the [[Deep Quarry (Heroic)|Deep Quarry]]. You will have to slay [[Cudgava (Heroic)|Cudgava]] in golem form. This awarded the Dungeon Achievement .
1. If you clear the [[Deep Quarry (Heroic)|Deep Quarry]] first, [[Cudgava (Heroic)|Cudgava]] and [[Gavitzle the Charbroiled (Heroic)|Gavitzle the Charbroiled]] will spawn in the [[Forge of Brell (Heroic)|Forge of Brell]]. Carefully body pull and kill the armor- and weaponsmiths around the forges. As each one dies, [[Cudgava (Heroic)|Cudgava's]] armor breaks or is destroyed. After that, kill the names. This awarded the Dungeon Achievement [[Victory: Cugdava and Gavitzle III]].
1. If you clear the [[Forge of Brell (Heroic)|Forge of Brell]] first, [[Cudgava (Heroic)|Cudgava]] and [[Gavitzle the Charbroiled (Heroic)|Gavitzle the Charbroiled]] will spawn in the [[Warsmith's Lifeblood]]. [[Cudgava (Heroic)|Cudgava]] will eventually change into a giant fire basilisk. Kill both names. This awarded the Dungeon Achievement [[Victory: Cugdava and Gavitzle IV]].
