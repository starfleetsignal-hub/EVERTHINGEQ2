## Special Attacks

### Dark Energy of the Void

Effects:

- A dark ball of energy that grows in strength as it passes from player to player.

### Eternal Void Aura

Effects:

- When receiving a hostile spell this spell has a 5% chance to cast Arcane Void Dampening on target's victim. Lasts for 5.0 seconds.
  - Inflicts 4,305 - 10,046 magic damage on target.
  - Decreases power of target by 9.0%.
  - Decreases Potency of target by 2,000.0.
  - Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means
- When damaged with a melee weapon this spell has a 5% chance to cast Pacifying Void Strike on target's attacker. Lasts for 4.0 seconds.
  - Inflicts 6,915 - 16,135 piercing damage on target.
  - Decreases power or target by 9.0%.
  - Dazes target
  - Target can not cast hostile spells.
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Daze effects of 12.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 1.3 seconds.

### Eternal Void Presence

You feel the power of trickery at work.<br>
Effects:

- Target will lose 51% more power when power is consumed.
- When damaged with a melee weapon this spell has a 5% chance to cast Void Distortion on target. Lasts for 3.0 seconds.
  - Forces target to clear their selected target.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 3,143 - 17,812 magic damage on target.
  - Decreases power of target by 4.0%.
  - Decreases INT and STR of target by 323.3.
  - Decreases Potency of target by 828.0.
  - Decreases Weapon Damage of target by 414.0.
  - Decreases Multi Attack of target by 414.0.
  - Dazes target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Daze effects of 9.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 1.0 second.
- When damaged with a spell this spell has a 5% chance to cast Void Feedback on target. Lasts for 3.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 2,200 - 12,468 magic damage on target.
  - Decreases power of target by 4.0%.
  - Decreases INT and STR of target by 323.3.
  - Decreases Potency of target by 517.5.
  - Decreases Crit Chance of target by 414.0.

### Munzok's Sigil of Interference

Effects:

- When beginning a spell cast this spell may cast Dark Feedback on caster. Lasts for 0.5 seconds. Triggers about 1.8 times per minute.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 158 - 893 magic damage on target.
  - Decreases power of target by 9.0%
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means
- When a combat art is used this spell has a 7% chance to cast Dark Nullification on caster. Lasts for 1.5 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 223 - 1,265 magic damage on target.
  - Decreases power of target by 11.0%
  - Stuns target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 4.5 seconds and duration is reduced to 0.5 second.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Void Inferno

Effects:

- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 6.0% instantly and every 4.5 seconds.
- Inflicts 7,481 - 13,894 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4.5 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 10 meters
- Inflicts 4,664 - 6,996 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4.5 seconds.
  - If target is between 10 and 14 meters away
- Inflicts 4,154 - 5,077 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4.5 seconds.
  - If target is between 14 and 200 meters away
- Inflicts 7,481 - 13,894 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4.5 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 10 meters
- Inflicts 4,664 - 6,996 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4.5 seconds.
  - If target is between 10 and 14 meters away
- Inflicts 4,615 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4.5 seconds.
  - If target is between 14 and 200 meters away
- Inflicts 167.0% of max health in heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4.5 seconds.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 167.0% instantly and every 4.5 seconds.
- Slows target in Area of Effect by 290.6%
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

## Strategy

This fight is BUGGED and has been for years (verified 10/1/20).  The raid needs to dismiss ALL pets (mage pets, healer pets and channeler interceptors, warders, squires - anything that isn't a deity pet or familiar).  Do not put them on passive, dismiss them.  The bug causes the name to reset when pets get charmed.

Munzok has a power drain, a curse, and nox dots.  Bring pots and clickies.

This is a multi-tank fight, as Munzok charms/mem-wipes.  Bring at least three.

Pull Munzok anywhere you'd like.  A gray nightblood will spawn.  Killing him will create a blue-purple bubble that protects from Munzok's AOE.  Everyone should stand in that bubble unless you're running to click a post.

Munzok will call a gaggle of nightbloods periodically.  Have the OT grab them and burn them down.

There are four posts in the room labeled divine, protective, stealth and (mage).  The base of the post glows in order Healer - Fighter - Scout - Mage.  When they glow, someone of that archtype must run over and right click on the lower part of the post to disable it.  Fail to do so twice in a row resets the boss.

Munzok is a straight burn.  Be aware he has interrupts that can make for difficult heals/rezzes.
