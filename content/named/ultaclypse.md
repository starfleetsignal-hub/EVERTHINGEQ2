## Buffs

### Diminished Control

Effects:

- Increases Weapon Damage of caster by 25.0.
- Increases Potency of caster by 25.0.

### Loss of Control

Effects:

- Increases STR and INT of target by 374.0.
- Increases Weapon Damage of caster by 50.0.
- Increases Potency of caster by 50.0.

### Weakened Control

Effects:

- Increases Weapon Damage of caster by 10.0.
- Increases Potency of caster by 10.0.

## Special Attacks

### Nether Cloud

Effects:

- Inflicts 120,675 - 318,142 poison damage on target.
- Inflicts 65,661 - 173,107 poison damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 10 meters
- Inflicts 65,500 - 121,643 poison damage on target.
- Inflicts 40,655 - 75,502 poison damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If target is between 10 and 15 meters away
- Inflicts 36,138 - 67,113 poison damage on target.
- Inflicts 22,586 - 41,949 poison damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If target is between 15 and 9,999 meters away
- Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
- Decreases Mitigation of target vs physical damage by 3,520.
- Your spells have reduced effectiveness and longer casting and reuse timers.

### Nether Field

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 7% chance to cast Nether Bolt on target's attacker. Last for 1.5 seconds.
  - Disarms Primary, Secondary and Ranged slots of target.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 10,420 - 19,351 magic damage on target.
  - Inflicts 4,125 - 7,660 magic damage on target every 1.5 seconds.
  - Target is immune to beneficial spells.
  - Stifles target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stifle effects of 4.5 seconds and the duration is reduced to 0.5 seconds.

### Stunning Blaze

Effects:

- Inflicts 164,943 - 201,598 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Stuns targets in Area of Effect.
- Resistibility increases against targets higher than level 107.
