## Spawn Note

There are absolutely no spawn placeholders for this mob, despite many many reports that killing crabs, lions, cubs, skeletons, hawks or any combination thereof. This mob is on a spawn timer and that timer is not sped up or slowed down by killing mobs. Kill mobs if you wish to pass the time, but killing them will not make Windfeather spawn any faster than if you simply waited or ran up and down the beach.
