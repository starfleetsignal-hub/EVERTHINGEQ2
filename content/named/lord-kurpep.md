## Strategy

[[Lord Kurpep]] is the last and hardest named.  He periodically summons several [[A summoned shin knight squire]] and [[A shin knight squire]], and ports a member of the group to the ledges on either side of his spawn spot, or way up the top of the spire behind him. Kurpep spawns adds on those group members that will accompany them back down.  Kurpep will charm and has a nasty noxious stun/power drain that he will cast on the pull and every 20% thereafter. To avoid the big AOE, use stifles/stuns.
