## Strategy

To make it attackable you have to kill the 6 [[A void spark|void spark]]s in front of him.
Pull him back to the 3 "cells". As people are imprisoned have someone click the corresponding tower in front of the cell to release them.

If you take to long to kill this mob seems to heal itself we didn't have much dps it seems I would suggest bringing a melee dps class to burn him down faster cause if it takes to long it starts to heal about 4-6% every few seconds.

**Tip:** You can simply interupt it from healing itself, the heal is called Void Recovery. It seems that it has to be interupted erlie, within 30% or so of the casting.
