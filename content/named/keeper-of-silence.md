## Notes

- It used to be that you could use a pet to pull the Keeper of Silence down to the Scriptorium on the first floor. This would allow players to engage him without having to complete the [[Shimmering Citadel : Poets Palace : The First Mirror.|mirror quests]]. The encounter was changed so that now the Keeper is inactive and cannot be engaged until a player approaches him, preventing him from being pet-pulled.
