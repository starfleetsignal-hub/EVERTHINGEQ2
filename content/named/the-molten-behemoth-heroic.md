## Strategy

### The Molten Behemoth

- This boss shares same mechanics in [[Solusek Ro's Tower: Monolith of Fire (Solo) (Instance)|solo version]]. Second Named
- Killing non-aggro a Magma Slug is NOT recommended in heroic. Going through fires will stun for a 0.3 sec and deal 10M heat damage to you. If you are less than 21M health. You can't even kill the first boss in every PoP heroic dungeon
- Use player healer group heal over time spell and walk through to boss room. Notice end of the room there is a brazier near wall. You are going to use it later.
- Take a close look on your chat window when blue text comes out "**The Molten Behemoth begins expelling molten rocks! Get back!**" . Named will cast a 2 sec spell "Magmatic Expulsion" in 3 sec later when seeing blue text. Everyone should run out about 15 meters to avoid its spell
- Getting hit by its spell in 12 meter causes knockdown/stun (incurable, lasts several seconds) and damage over time(DOT) about 50M total.  If you get hit some how. Using Death Prevent(Group AE) can solve that problem. Use Healing over time when boss casting if you are fast can solve that too
- After each AOE spell boss drops **molten stones** that can be moved like house items into the brazier. Each stone heal him 5% HP if you put them on brazier
- First time he drops 1 **molten stone.**  Second time 2 stones. 3 stones maximum. So strongly recommend keeping a player busy moving stones to braz
- Keep your healer and range dps away from boss can be a good way to kill it
- Mercenaries and AOE immune pets ignore all mechanics above
