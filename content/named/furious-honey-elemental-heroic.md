## Strategy

- Attack one of the nearby [[A glob of tainted honey]] and they will all begin to move up the nearby ramp and congeal to form the Elemental.
- Will spawn [[A giant glob of elemental honey]] throughout the encounter that slowly moves towards the Elemental. Burn it down before it reaches it or the Elemental will be healed.
