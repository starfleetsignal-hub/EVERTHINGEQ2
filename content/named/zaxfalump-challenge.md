## Notes

Zaxfalump will go through some dialogue and run onto a crate in the center of the bridge on the upper level where he will be rooted but attackable.

Depending on the groups DPS, Zaxfalump may be able to get burned down and killed before the healing orbs reach his location and before [[Fwump]] joins the fight.

If the group does not have enough DPS to burn him down through the healing orbs, then someone in the group will need to run around the to find some targetable 'crates' to click to pick up and move to block the pads in the four corners of the zone. Additional brownies will spawn and try to destroy them.
