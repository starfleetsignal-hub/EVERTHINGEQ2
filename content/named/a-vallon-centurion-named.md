## Strategy

- Despite outwardly appearing to be a trash mob, this guy is worth AA the first time killed and has a drop table.
- He has three friends ([[A Vallon sentry|Vallon sentries]], two 34^ one 34even) who remain stationary on the path around {{waypoint 634, -23, -368}} - since he wanders far enough to get out of sight of them, the fight carries the risk of unexpected adds.
