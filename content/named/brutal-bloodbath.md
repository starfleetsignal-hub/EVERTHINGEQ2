## Strategy

The fight begins when you kill the last outlying grimling. Brutal Bloodbath will spawn from that glob of blood in the center of the priests and come to wherever you are. I found that in this fight you have to kill the priests that are kneeling or they will keep healing him. During the fight he will spawn priest adds, however, I only saw one during my fight. He also throws globs of blood but I have no idea what they do.
