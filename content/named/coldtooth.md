## Strategy

- Coldtooth is a giant albino molerat. He spawns with 3 [[A Greater Tunneller|Greater Tunneller]]s (smaller versions of himself). Suggest taking down adds first (they are also Epic x2, but go down very quickly).

- Coldtooth starts with Coldtooth's Screech, which may stun the raid.
