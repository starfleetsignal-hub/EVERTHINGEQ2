## Notes

This is a 'patchwork puppet' version of Trakanon appears *only* during the [[Heroes' Festival Timeline|Heroes' Festival]] annual [[Live Events|event]].

- This monster will only appear after [[Roslyn Mischeva]] appears and invites players to join forces and recreate a famous battle.
- If you defeat this monster you can buy the [[Trakanon Plushie]] from the [[Heroes' Festival Merchant Items|Heroes' Festival Merchant]]

## Travel

Travel to this monster is easy.

- Use the world "bell" (globe) to go to the [[Kylong Plains]] and head straight back from the docks. He will be on the left side of the waterway.

## Quests

- [[The Play's the Thing]] - an event-only quest given by Roslyn Mischeva at the location

## Achievements

- [[Mischeva's Champion]] - an achievement for which this monster is one of many to defeat.
- [[Trakanon's Reprise]]  - kill this specific monster

## Roslyn Mischeva's Story

The story of Trakanon starts long, long ago. There is much lost to slow grind of time, but I do know this! Venril Sathir, warrior-mage of the Iksar tribe rose up to unite his people under one banner. He swept across the continent of Kunark,  acquiring vast amounts of territory and bent the scattered tribes of Iksar to his will. Ultimately, in the War of Joining, all five Iksar Tribe-States pledged fealty to Venril, and a new empire was born.

Venril's son Rile eventually took the reins of the relentless empire, both consolidating his power and extending his reach. Even the dragons of the Ring of Scale grew nervous at the ruthlessness the Iksar were showing, though they were split on how to counter them. This is the part of the tale where Trakanon takes the lead.
