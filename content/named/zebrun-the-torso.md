## Strategy

Burn (And Joust)

Zebrun 'crawls' very slowly and accrues a yellow buff over time that will eventually allow him to single hit kill players if it reaches 10 increments.

- Note: Recommend clearing the entire area between zone in and the entire courtyard that Zebrun resides to give room for potential jousting.

The raid should burn down Zebrun as much as possible until around the 8th buff increment.

- If Zebrun is low enough on health to where he will be dead before he reaches 10 increments, continue the burn. If it is questionable, the entire raid force should joust out as far as possible before the 10th increment and then immediately stop movement.
- Any movement after the emote prompt on the screen will cause more damage based on the distance you move from the location you were at when the emote first appeared.
