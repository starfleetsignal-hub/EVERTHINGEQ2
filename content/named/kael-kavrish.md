## Strategy

Completely range this fight; he's rooted to the floor and has a nasty elemental-based AoE (Mutant strike) that does more damage the closer you are to him. Carry Elemental cure potions to clear the elemental dot that is left over.<br>
Healing automatically pulls aggro 100%, and he hits you with mega ranged nuke.<br>
Apparently you can try curing his elemental AOE, which may stop him from auto-targeting healers.

**For people who are too impatient and want to kill this mob asap**: Fighters and scouts may be able to fight right by him, but keep in mind there will be lots of damage taken in the process. It is a good idea to have healers with you but keep them back towards the tables/bookshelves. When Ka'El emotes something along the lines of, *"I unleash the power within!"* or *"Fear the ultimate weapon!"* **immediately** back away to the tables and switch to ranged mode. Do NOT touch the white orbs circling around him at this time as they insta-death. Once the orbs are gone you will be able to fight him again until he summons them back. Rinse and repeat.
