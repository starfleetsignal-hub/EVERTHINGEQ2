## Notes

- To spawn
  - Clear all the Deathbone skeletons from the Grave Pool.
  - Once cleared, a group of four Deathbones will spawn in the island of skulls in the middle of the Grave Pool. Kill them.
  - A totem is left behind on the ground afterwards.  Click it.
  - All the Deathbones in the surrounding area will respawn.  Kill them.
  - The group of four rises from the island of skulls again.  Kill them.
  - A Deathbone Savage and four friends (two [[A Deathbone berserker|berserkers]], one [[A Deathbone knight|knight]], one [[A Deathbone theurgist|theurgist]]) spawns.
