## Notes

- Spawns at the end of a ring event
  1. First ring is four [[A small clockwork cobra|small clockwork cobras]], 56 even
  1. Second ring is three small clockwork cobras, 56^
  1. Third ring is four different groups of clockwork cobras, either groups of six 56vv or two 56^^ - stand in a corner of the room to avoid aggroing all four groups
  1. Fourth ring is either A Malfunctioning Clockwork or [[A worn down clockwork]] (56^)
- The ring event restarts about 5 minutes after killing the Named or its placeholder.
