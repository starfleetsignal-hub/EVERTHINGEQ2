## Strategy

DPS check, Joust

Grethah has two mechanics to be mindful of:

- Random raid member targeted 'sacs' that will explode after a few seconds and kill anyone in the vicinity - recommend designating someone to call out every time the sac spawns.
- A group of four swarm adds that spawn at 90%, and every 20% after (70%, 50%, 30%, 10%) that need to be killed in x amount of time, otherwise another round spawns.

Recommend pulling Grethah outside the castle to the zone in courtyard for better mobility.

Raid should use slow and steady DPS to Grethah, saving big spike damaging abilities for the adds in addition to jousting away from the sacs as they spawn.

If the general burn dps is high enough to where the T1 DPS' big hitting spike abilities are not refreshed by the next 20% health threshold, dial back damage and give time for these abilities to refresh.
