## Strategy

Lharnc has LOTS of hit points, so extra dps will go a long way toward knocking him down.

Lharnc also uses a target-switch charm ability that causes the tank and Lharnc to target a party member and attack them until dead.  A second healer to rez the dead people during combat will be a great asset.
