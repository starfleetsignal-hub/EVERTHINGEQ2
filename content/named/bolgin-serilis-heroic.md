## Strategy

- Initially unattackable and behind a small army of goblins. Kill them to progress to the next wave - after the fourth wave he will have some dialogue and become attackable.
- Spawns adds initially and runs back to a safe distance. Kill the adds and continue fighting him.
- Throughout the fight he will emote that he pops a cork and drinks a potion. Eventually text will appear saying the potion dissolves all hostile spells from him and will follow-up with he drank to much of his potion. **STOP ATTACKING DURING THIS TIMEFRAME!** After that text he will slump over for a few seconds, any damage during this time frame will spawn additional adds that you will have to contend with - splash damage will deal with most of them but once he reaches 20% if you spawn adds much tougher ones will spawn that can easily wipe the group.
