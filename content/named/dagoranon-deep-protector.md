This mob will spawn as soon as the Beastlord clicks the shell for his quest update. If aggro is not pulled immediately, you will have a dead Beastlord in the bottom of the pool from the initial attack.

Once you have pulled aggro, get the squishy members of your group behind the mob. His frontal damage of 20,000 every 6 seconds will kill rapidly. The fight can be done without power regeneration, but it is very slow. Healers will need to use mana stones or dark hearts to get power for group heals. Otherwise the mob's reactive power drain will prevent any healing.
