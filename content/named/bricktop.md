## Strategy

Mob has a nasty kickback. Pull him down to the next lower level and stay in one of the two tents. Stoneskins 2x every (30?) seconds. Each absorbs 20 attacks, but can be dispelled one at a time. He also self heals about the time he stoneskins, and never runs out of mana. You might want to bring a friend, or 2.
