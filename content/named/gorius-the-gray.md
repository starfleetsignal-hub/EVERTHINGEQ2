Second-last boss in [[Crypt of Dalnir: The Kly Stronghold (Raid)]].

## Strategy

1. First notice in the room the 4 cylindrical tanks. Gorius will get in one of these to heal himself at 95%, 80%, 60%, 40% etc of health.
1. Second notice in the middle of the room the two benches facing each pair of tanks. Each bench has a pair of horizontal levers, one for each tank. In the center of each bench are also 4 vertical knife switches (can be a little hard to see). Each tank is controlled by the closest pair of vertical switches and the closest horizontal lever. All knife switches will start in the closed (handle down) position.
1. Assign one (or two if you can spare them) players to switches. After the named is engaged the people on switches must quickly click each knife switch to "open" it. In the open state they will stick out horizontally.
1. When the Named gets inside a cylinder, CLOSE the 2 associated knife switches by clicking on them, then click on the associated horizontal lever.
1. The Named will emote that he can't replenish his health if we open his door (which is exactly what we want). As soon as he's out, burn. Open the 2 switches you just closed ready for the next round. Rinse and repeat.
1. Be quick to kill any minor gooblin add, if there is more than 3, a major gooblin will appear and your raid will likely wipe.
