## Strategies

- The Monstrous Roc spawns in a square room.  There is a ledge around the outside edge of the room, the inside is filled with honey.  Pull the Roc out of the honey and pull him around the ledge.
- The Roc will lay exploding eggs during the fight - the game emotes in red when he lays one.  The eggs will explode after 5-7 seconds, doing about 100K damage if you're caught in the explosion.  The eggs can be prematurely detonated if you're careless with AOEs.
- The Monstrous Roc also has a joustable AOE called "Squawk!".  In Advanced Solo, Squawk! only seems to stun you, but being stunned can trap you in the eggsplosion radius.
