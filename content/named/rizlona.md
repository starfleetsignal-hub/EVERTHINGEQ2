Two ways to do it.

1. Grab the book in hallway use on named and massive DPS down the boss and skip the minotuars as you can probably kill it before 6 spawn to wipe raid might need to kill 1 or 2 to keep the number down while dpsing.
1. Grab the book in hallway and use it on named to turn it into a dragon and have people kill the class specific minotuars as intended while dps boss down.
