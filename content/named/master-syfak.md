Effects:

- When a combat art is used this spell will cast Toxic Bolt on target. Lasts for 3.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
  - Inflicts 62,392 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Stuns targets in Area of Effect.
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 9.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 1.0 second.

- Cannot be modified except by direct means
