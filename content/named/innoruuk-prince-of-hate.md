## Strategy

Pretty much a tank and spank outside of needing a stifle rune and something to get rid of fear. He spawns adds starting at 60%, then more every 20%(?). They can be ignored (the first dragon just slept on the wall) if you pull him into the center of the room; the adds, shouldn't be an issue, as they spawn in set locations it seems. Cure the curse, and that's it.

Note: Innoruuk notices that <Player> is not protected from deific magic, <Player> has been DEATHTOUCHED!

If you wish to avoid this, you will need a black rune (CD) or rune set (PoP) that grants Protection of the Voidwrought (and of course, armor that has the black rune slots needed).
