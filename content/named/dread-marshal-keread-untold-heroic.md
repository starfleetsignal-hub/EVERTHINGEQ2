Ability and strategy information changes between stages (1-20/21+).

## Abilities

- **Griffin Master**
- A buff that has a chance to cast Piercing Beak on a melee hit.
- **Griffin's Reflex**
- A buff that has a chance to cast Talon Reaction the the attacker.
- **Protective Flank**
- A buff that has a chance to reflect hostile abilities cast from a flanking position. On a successful reflect, Keread's fervor increases through Prismatic Plume.
- **Piercing Beak**
- An incurable noxious that inflicts increasing disease damage over time, decreases power over time, reduces healing received, and increases melee weapon range.
- **War Torn**
- An incurable arcane that inflicts divine damage over time, decreases power over time, and applies Defensive Breach, which inflicts magic damage.
- **Talon Reaction**
- An incurable trauma that inflicts slashing damage. If the target is not a fighter, it also disarms the target's secondary and primary slots. This spell only affects targets within 3 meters of the griffin.
- **Singled Out**
- A curable curse that inflicts increasing piercing damage over time. If it is cured while no allies are within (6/8) meters, the target dies. If it is cured while cursed allies are within (6/8) meters, those allies also die. When cured, each ally within (6/8) meters is also hit with Singled Out. If exactly one ally is within (6/8) meters, and that ally is a scout, the curse is cured, it does not transfer, and the priest's Cure Curse spell is reset.
- **Outmaneuvered [21+]**
- An incurable arcane that reduces Flurry and increases all damage done to targets. It can be removed with [[Escape]].

## Strategy

Keread summons [[A Lucanic Wraithwing]] and [[A Lucanic Wraithrider]], which cast Griffin Prey, which forces the player to target the guards.
