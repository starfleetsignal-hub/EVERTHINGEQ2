## Notes

- Dominus Ganak, the Darslayer, Slayer of [[Jaled Dar's Shade|Jaled Dar]], is one of the four casual contested raid mobs introduced in [[Update:43|LU43]].
- He is non-aggro to those with Legion of Danak faction over +40,000.
