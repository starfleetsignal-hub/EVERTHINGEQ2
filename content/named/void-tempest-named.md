## Notes

These creatures started appearing during LU46. They appear in nearly ever major zone in game and several minor zones as well, always at a level appropriate to the zone. (See [[Tempest affected Creatures List]] for locations.) Although they initially appear as non-attackable creatures, they eventually become attackable after killing a random series of [[Dark seepage|dark seepages]], [[Dark horror|dark horrors]], or [[Shadow brute|shadow brutes]].
