## Strategy

Pull him out of the camp with a ranged weapon, or body pull him when he's at the outskirts. If you happen to attract a bunch of his fellow Tribes-lizards, avoid killing them. When they die they gain a 60% mitigation, 25.0 flurry and a size increase buff that stacks. Otherwise pretty easy. Watch for his damage reflect blood barrier. Can kill you fast.
