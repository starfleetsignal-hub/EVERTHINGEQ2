## Strategy

- You should only use single-target abilities for this fight. Do not use AoE spells or combat arts.
- At half health, The Frozen Corrival will knock everyone back and turn into an icy whirlwind.
- [[A Dark Hearted Corrival]] and three [[A Light Hearted Corrival]] will spawn. While these are alive, the Corrival will be rooted and immune to damage.
- Focus on killing the [[A Dark Hearted Corrival|Dark Hearted Corrival]] without killing the others.
- If you kill one of the Light Hearted Corrivals, the Corrival will reform at full health and you'll have to start over.
- After killing a Dark Hearted Corrival, the Corrival will reform at half health and can be killed
- High level players who are mentored down can kill the Corrival in one hit, bypassing this mechanic.
