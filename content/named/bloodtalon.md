## Spawn

- If not up, kill the 2-3 [[A mighty griffin|mighty griffins]] around the spawn. This will cause either [[A majestic griffin|a majestic griffin]] or Bloodtalon to spawn. If the majestic griffin spawns, kill it to respawn the mighty griffins and repeat the pattern.
