## Strategy

- Recommend clearing all of the loitering groups of goblins before beginning this encounter, as you'll need the space.
- At the start of the encounter Hammy will be empowered by 'Toughened Hide'. To remove this, pull him across the boxes in the hall with a green glow. These boxes will either spawn a smaller swine add, a bag of feed, or a floor trap. You'll need to find (2) floor traps and run Hammy over them to drop the buff to 50, then to 0.
- Tank and Spank after the buff is removed.
