## Group

Comes with three other named mobs: Tetroxae, Nurotoxia and Hemotoxia.  You must keep them close to each other in life (see below).

## Strategy

Pull all 4 mobs into the overhanging corner to deal with knockback.  Have the MA cycle through all four targets until they are all at 10%.  Main mob will stop taking damage at 10% until the other three are dead.  Burn the 3 smaller mobs down together then kill the named.  Cures are very important in this fight.
