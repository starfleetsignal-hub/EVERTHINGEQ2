Grand Shaman Shaekk is a Tier 3 raid boss.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance: 525.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance: 590.0
- Resolve: 11,195.0

## Abilities

- **Poisonous Practices I**
- A curable noxious that deals fast-growing damage over time.
- **Stalwart Admonishment**
- A curable elemental that deals elemental damage and increases all other damage types. This effect only lands on fighters.
- **Frenzied Clawspike**
- An incurable trauma that deals piercing damage over time.
- **Piercing Wail**
- A curable trauma that deals increasing piercing damage the closer the target is. It also stuns the target.
- **Swarm Mentality**
- A buff that decreases damage from all sources for each escaped creature in the fight.
- **Bolstered Spellfrenzy**
- A buff that increases outgoing damage and reduces damage taken from mages and priests.
- **Bolstered Frenzy**
- A buff that increases outgoing damage and reduces damage taken from fighters and scouts.
- **Necrotic Touch I**
- A curable curse that debuffs offensive, defensive, and level while dealing disease damage to the target and healing Shaekk for each non-fighter target. The curse applies to up to 7 targets within 8 meters of Shaekk. Curing this curse does NOT reset the Cure Curse ability.
- **Marrow Shroud**
- A buff that has a 5% chance to trigger when taking damage from players 10 meters or more away. It reflects damage back to the attacker. It also has a 5% chance to trigger when taking damage from players closer than 10 meters, casting the Marrow Sap debuff instead.
- **Pustule Presence**
- A curable noxious that deals growing disease damage to the target. When removed, it triggers Pustule Pop on all allies within 25 meters, which deals much faster disease damage and slows movement speed.

## Strategy

Shaekk continuously drops black circles on the ground. Standing too close to one summons an add. Standing within the circle for too long kills the player.

Shaekk alternates between Bolstered Spellfrenzy and Bolstered Frenzy throughout the fight. These two buffs cannot be removed.
