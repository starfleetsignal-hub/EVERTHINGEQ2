## Special Attacks

### Cloak of the Reanimated Hero

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 10% chance to cast Deadbolt on target's attacker. Lasts for 8.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 751 - 1,126 divine damage on target.
  - Inflicts 411 - 617 divine damage on target every 8 seconds.
  - Decreases Potency of target by 400.0.
  - Increases Haste of target by 194.6.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Venomous Cloud

Effects:

- Inflicts 55,757 - 100,681 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Stifles targets in Area of Effect
