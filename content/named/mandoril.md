## Buffs

### Idolic Stone of War

Effects:

- Provides retributive protection against most fighter classes.

## Special Attacks

### Fog of Lethargy

Effects:

- Inflicts 46,900 - 109,433 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3.5 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 58,625 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3.5 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 30,485 - 71,132 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3.5 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 42,992 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3.5 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 35,175 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3.5 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 31,267 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3.5 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Interrupt target in Area of Effect
- Decreases Haste in Area of Effect by 19.8.
- Decreases Casting Speed of targets in Area of Effect by 10.0%.
- Slows targets in Area of Effect by 35.0%.
- If this spell expires naturally, and you have any AoE spell from Mikla or Medorius on you, you will be hit with the Trilateral Curse.

### Spiral of Disorientation

Effects:

- Inflicts 46,900 - 109,433 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3.5 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 58,625 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3.5 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 30,485 - 71,132 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3.5 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 42,992 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3.5 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 35,175 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3.5 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 31,267 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3.5 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Forces targets in Area of Effect to clear their selected target instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
- Blurs vision of targets in Area of Effect.
- If this spell expires naturally, and you have any AoE spell from Mikla or Medorius on you, you will be hit with the Trilateral Curse.

### Trilateral Curse

Effects:

- Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 15.0%.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 67.0% every 4 seconds.
- Inflicts 15.0% of max health in magic damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 67.0% of max health in magic damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
- If you die while this curse is on you, it will spread to those closest to you.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Trilateral Vestments

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 8% chance to cast Sagestrike on target's attacker. Lasts for 9.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 896 - 1,664 magic damage instantly and every 9 seconds.
  - Decreases power of target by 7.0% instantly and every 9 seconds.
  - Decreases Haste of target 19.8.
  - Decreases Casting Speed of target by 15.0%.
  - If this spell expires naturally, and you have Sagestrike on you from a different caster, Trilateral Judgment will strike you.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means
- On a melee hit this spell will cast Focused Bolt of Slaying on target of attack.
  - If target is not a fighter, this spell does 33% Health and Mana damage.

## Strategy

- Mandoril, [[Medorius]] and [[Mikla]] are collectively called The Three Sages.
- Mandoril must be tanked by a Brawler.
