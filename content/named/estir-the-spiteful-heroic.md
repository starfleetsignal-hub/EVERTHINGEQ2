## Notes

- Part of the **Gruesome Twosome**, along with [[Laxil'Vas the Torn (Heroic)|Laxil'Vas the Torn]].
- Part of the **Cantankerous Triumvirate**, along with [[Laxil'Vas the Torn (Heroic)|Laxil'Vas the Torn]] and [[Ragash'Ta the Insidious (Heroic)|Ragash'Ta the Insidious]].

## Strategy

- Spawned by slaying the groups of [[Blood of fury (Heroic)|blood of fury]], [[Blood of hatred (Heroic)|blood of hatred]], [[Blood of malevolence (Heroic)|blood of malevolence]], and [[Blood of vengeance (Heroic)|blood of vengeance]]
- Avoid yellow circles on the floor. If a bat catches you in a circle, it will fly you to the roof and drop you fatally to the floor.
- During the fight, groups of [[A lady of spite (Heroic)|ladies of spite]] and [[A lord of spite (Heroic)|lords of spite]] will add.
- [[Estir the Spiteful (Heroic)|Estir]] has a buff that increases every 25% from 1 to 4. She can only be damaged when the number of adds alive equals the number of this buff. Use Singular Focus and avoid unnecessarily killing the adds.
- Cure only the noxious on the class called out.
