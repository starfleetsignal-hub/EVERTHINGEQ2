## Strategy

Strategic jousting.

Periodically Kerridicus will gain a buff and become immune to damage, spawning [[A Lava Titan]] golem at his location.

Tanks should pull this golem out of range and have the raid range DPS it down, as upon its death there will be three [[An Unstable Lava Globule]] with very little hitpoints. When they die they explode and put a decent sized DOT onto anyone in the vicinity of their death. Once they are dead, raid can resume damage on Kerridicus.

Rinse and repeat every time the named becomes immune and spawns the adds.
