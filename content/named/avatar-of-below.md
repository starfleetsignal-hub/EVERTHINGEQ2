## Notes

### Free Buff

If you worship [[Brell Serilis]], going near the [[Avatar of Below]] results in receiving a buff called **Blessing of Brell Serilis**, which lasts for 5 hours or until death:
+100 Max Health
+100 Max Power
+3% more armor effectiveness against piercing damage

## Strategy

To pull the Avatar, it is suggested that you have your back against the wall as he has a nasty knockback.  He continually summons a group of 5 [[Battleworn bugbear|battleworn bugbears]] (74^^^ Heroic). When you kill them another group will come, this will continue throughout the entire battle.  Each time he is down 20%, he will summon a ^^^74 epic x2 and he will begin to heal himself.  From a spectator's point of view you may need to kill the epic, before you can attack the Avatar again.

## Brew Day

This avatar also appears as a non-attackable NPC during [[Brew Day]], and can be found wandering around [[Antonica]] in the fields outside the [[North Qeynos]] gate giving out the quest [[Beers for Brell!]]
