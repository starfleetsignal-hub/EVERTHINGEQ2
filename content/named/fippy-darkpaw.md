## Notes

A famous Darkpaw gnoll who would plan elaborate attacks on [[Qeynos]] during the [[The Age of Turmoil (Lore)|The Age of Turmoil]]. His descendant [[Fippy Darkpaw, the 4th]] is still plotting Qeynos' downfall to this day. [[Fippy's Hill]] in [[Antonica]] is named after him. Darkpaw gnolls will often use Fippy's name as a battle cry or allude to him when they see a fellow Darkpaw fall in combat, saying, "Go with Fippy, fallen one!"
