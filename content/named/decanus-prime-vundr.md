## Strategy

When you get him down in health, he will turn into Lion of Ages with 6M HP (hence his name as The Lion). Easy mob with not much to note in terms of spells. Didn't seem to have any devastating spells either. But it is this lion that needs to be defeated to continue progression of the zone.
