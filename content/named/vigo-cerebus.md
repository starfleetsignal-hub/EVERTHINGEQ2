## Factions

- +50 faction with **The Green Hoods**
- -100 faction with **The Deathfist Orcs**

## Notes

Killing Vigo will open the main gate for a period of time, allowing all who wish to to enter [[Deathfist Citadel]] by the front door to the courtyard. The original test server notes for [[LU33]] said that the gate would remain open for 3 hours, however on live servers the gate will close momentarily after Vigo's death.
