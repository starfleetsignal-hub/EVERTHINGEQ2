## Strategy

- Clear the two groups on either side of the room, as well as any ground level wanderers.
- Every so often Zarkol will port the group around and begin to channel some very large red circles on the ground. They appear to be random as to where they appear, and he can still be damaged during this phase. After the third circle is summoned and disappears, you can burn for another 20 or so seconds before more circles are summoned.
