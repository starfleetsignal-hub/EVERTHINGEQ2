## Buffs

### Godslayer's Fury

Effects:

- Increases STR and INT of caster by 334.4.
- Increases Accuracy of caster by 33.0%.
- Increases Strikethrough of caster by 50.0%.
- Increases Potency of caster by 20.0.
- Increases Haste of caster by 25.0.
- Increases Weapon Damage of caster by 50.0.
- Adds 75% to spell and melee damage.

## Special Attacks

### Cloak of Retribution

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 8% chance to cast Counterstrike on target's attacker. Lasts for 5.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Throws target back
  - Inflicts 953 - 1,770 heat damage on target.
  - Inflicts 683 - 1,268 heat damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - Decreases Crit Chance of target by 30.0.
  - Your damage and healing critical hit chances are reduced by approx 30%.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means
