## Special Attacks

### Bloodclaw

Effects:

- On a melee hit this spell will cast Bloodslice on target of attack.
  - Inflicts 38,343 slashing damage on target.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Cloud of Destruction

Effects:

- Inflicts 251,110 - 585,924 crushing damage on target.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
- Inflicts 255,096 - 382,644 crushing damage on target.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
- Inflicts 239,153 - 358,729 crushing damage on target.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
- Inflicts 188,333 - 230,184 crushing damage on target.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
- Inflicts 98,650 - 120,573 crushing damage on target.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
- Inflicts 69,753 crushing damage on target.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
- Decreases power of target by 20.0%.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- If this spell expires naturally you will be hit with lethal consequence.

### Sargon's Edict

Effects:

- Target will lose 310% more power when power is consumed.
- 413% of target's power consumed will also be drained from target's health.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

## Strategy

General tank and spank encounter.

During the fight he will spawn a group of four Malignon adds, have raid switch over DPS to burn these down and refocus attacks back onto Sargon.
