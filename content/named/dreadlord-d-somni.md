## Strategy

- Cloak of Revulsion is an undispellable skill that can stun and 1k DD everyone that damages D'Somni.
- Frequently Knockbacks (up to half the  zone length)
- Uses "Tempest of Disease", raid root and does about 95% of max health in disease damage if not cured
- He hates mages and will run toward them to kill them. Expect many death if you're a mage.
