## Strategy

Forblarg the Foul will spawn as soon as you kill the last of the grimlings in front of the altar so if you like to prebuff, do it before the pull. Not a terribly hard fight (solo) but when he spawns, he kills his worshipers whom he later will rez mid-fight, he emotes, "Forblarg sends his foul energy toward one of the falling grimlings". AE burn either during that part, or all fight; there does not seem to be any adverse outcome. During the fight there is one thing to watch for- Forblarg says "Feel how I feel! the shackles bring pain!" and you get (yellow) text: "You are shackled by pain! Get near the torchlight to cure it!".  I think this will affect you if you pull him, I just charged in and tanked him on the spot where he spawns.

If you run to the torches, you get the text "you have found the light. Shadow Shackles can now be cured!" Forblarg becomes untargetable while you stand under the torchlight, so cure the dot then run out of the light. If you move out from under the torches before curing Shadow Shackles, you get purple text "You left the light! Shadow Shackles is once again uncurable!" Then you may die.
