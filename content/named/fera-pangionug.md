Fera Pangionug is a Tier 1 raid boss. It gains an additive advantage for each player below 12 in the raid and beyond a soft time limit of 60 minutes.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance 250.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance 700.0
- Resolve 13,230.0

## Abilities

- **Impish Will**
- A buff that increases offensive ability for each [[An Impish Bonder|Impish Bonder]] active in the encounter.
- **Surging Stabs**
- An incurable trauma that deals increasing piercing and cold damage to health and power over time.
- **Stargazing Strike**
- A curable detriment that deals fast increasing piercing and cold damage.
- **Cold Lance**
- A incurable trauma that deals piercing and cold damage as well as debuffing fervor.
- **Pulsing Mindstrike**
- A curable trauma that deals piercing and cold damage to health and power over time and snares targets.
- **Piercing Gaze**
- A curable trauma that deals high piercing and cold damage and increases crushing and poison damage from all sources.
- **Frigid Stare**
- A curable trauma that deals increasing piercing and cold damage over time.
- **Side-Eyed**
- An incurable curse that increases incoming cold and piercing damage types. If it expires or the target dies, it triggers a failure in the encounter. The first failure summons a [[Shadowed Gaze of Pangionug]]. The second failure additionally kills the target. The third failure additionally kills the target's group. The fourth failure additionally kills the entire raid. The curse is removed if, during the next check, no players are standing between Irid and Fera.
- **Barrage**
- An incurable curse that can only be blocked with [[Bulwark of Order]]. Irid and Fera have separate Barrage abilities.
