## Strategy

- Spawned by placing three fishbowls in the fountain, found around the zone. Possible locations include  {{waypoint -107, 31, -180}},  {{waypoint -262, 19, -1}}, [[Basilica of Hate (SoH: Utter Contempt)|Basilica of Hate]] {{waypoint 75, 30, -200}}, and [[Rectory of Confession (SoH: Utter Contempt)|Rectory of Confession]] (click knife at {{waypoint 175, 33, -76}}).
- Drag him out of pools when he spawns them or he heals. Turns players into fishes, if not cured they die.
