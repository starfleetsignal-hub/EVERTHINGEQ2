## Notes

Spawned as part of the Challenge Mode encounter with [[Baroddas (Epic)|Baroddas]]. To summon, click on the idol by the portal/gate when Baroddas is at roughly 75%.

## Strategy

- Will periodically summon group-debuffs, cure quickly.
- Seems to have non-rapid power drain.

**Chronomentor strategy:** Use a deliberately underpowered mercenary, like a mystic with only one accolade from the Tishan box from Reign of Shadows or whatever. Mercenaries gain their power from accolades, so just equipping them with one will give them just enough DPS to whittle down without one shotting. Just let your mercenary whittle him down until the challenge idol spawns.
