## Special Attacks

### Dark Feast

Effects:

- Inflicts 6,417 - 10,696 disease damage on target.
- Inflicts 2,139 - 3,565 disease damage on target every 4.5 seconds.
- Heals caster for 1.6 - 2.4% of max health.
- Heals caster for 0.8 - 1.2% of max health every 4.5 seconds.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Disease Blast

Effects:

- Inflicts 211,647 - 493,843 disease damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 218,060 - 327,091 disease damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 173,166 - 259, disease damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 151,520 - 185,191 disease damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 158,735 - 194,010 disease damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 120,254 disease damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 6 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
- Decreases Casting Speed of targets in Area of Effect by 100.0%.
- Roots targets in Area of Effect
- If this spell expires naturally while poisonous wave or toxic torrent are active you will be hit with toxic concussion, a massive damage spell.

### Nauseating Stench

Effects:

- Opponents have a 10% chance every 15 secs to be struck with crippling nausea. Those suffering from toxic inertia take disease damage, and are severely impaired.
