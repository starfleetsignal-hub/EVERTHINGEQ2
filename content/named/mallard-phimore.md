## Notes

- Mallard Phimore has an intrinsic buff called Lesser Storm Strength that grants him 100% crit avoidance.
- He exists so that Beastlords can have a Duck as their Avian Warder.
- He's a rare spawn, and the spawning method is unknown.
- The Duck is a refferance to Mallard Fillmore found here: http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mallard_Fillmore
