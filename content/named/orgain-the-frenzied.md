## Abilities

- **Spellweaved Admonishment**
- A curable elemental that only lands on mages. It deals elemental damage and increases all other damage types. The effect cannot be blocked.
- **Overrun**
- A buff that increments when Orgain switches targets or when a player dies. Each increment increases Orgain's outgoing damage. These increments never expire.
- **Frenzied Rush**
- A buff that increments when players stand in the black circles for too long. Each increment decreases Orgain's incoming damage. These increments expire the next time a black circle appears.
- **Blackhook Heartspike**
- An incurable trauma that deals piercing damage over time.
- **Forceful Strikes**
- A curable trauma that knocks down the target and deals crushing damage.
- **Barrage**
- An attack that can only be countered with [[Bulwark of Order]].

## Strategy

Small black circles continuously appear underneath Orgain and any active loyalist. Players who stand within 3 meters of the circles too long are hit with Frenzied Slam, which temporarily increases Orgain's offensive and defensive capabilities. These effects expire the next time circles appear. Orgain temporarily roots himself whenever a circle appears. When the root breaks, his next auto-attack is significantly larger than normal.
