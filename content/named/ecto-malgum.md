## Strategy

Ecto Malgum doesn't spawn until you kill the [[The Wight Specter]], so if you clear all the wisp adds on Ecto's platform before killing [[The Wight Specter]] it will make it easier.

Drag him  into the cave against the wall (by the bone piles).  Clear any dots.  2 adds MAY spawn at 50%.  They're easy, just burn the boss.

## Alternate Strategy

Definitely clear the wisps first or else he will call them. His adds spawn fairly regularly. We didn't have the dps or healing to ignore all the adds that piled up so when he started to summon them we mezzed Ecto and then took out the adds one by one. It took a little while, but we eventually got him down.

When he starts to summon his adds, a pole shows up in one of 3 places on his platform. The pole, named "pile of bones" is attackable. We thought that killing the "pile of bones" might prevent the adds but it didn't seem to have any effect. We have no idea what it's supposed to do.
