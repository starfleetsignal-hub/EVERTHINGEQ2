## Strategy

Assign 2 players as color-switchers. One for each side of the ramp.
You can fight on top of the pedestal the whole time.
When the tank (or anyone) notices the Statue change to a new color (aura!), tell the switchers "we need RED", "we need BLUE" (always the opposite color of the Statue's aura).
ALL 4 pillars must have the same color to counter the aura. The Aura is basically a damage-shield for the statue.
After each successful counter, you can (should) turn one pillar off again (or change one color) to prevent it from overcharging. Supposedly it could create massive AOEs otherwise, or heal the Statue.
