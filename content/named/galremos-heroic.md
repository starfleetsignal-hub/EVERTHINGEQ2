## Strategy

### Trash mob Fight

- Final Boss in Level 5. You have to kill all the bosses one by one to lower the lift.
- Before finish all trash,please try to stay close to NPC "Lift Attendance". Named won't spawn on that side
- Try to avoid lift in case some players may get stuck underground using some teleport skills to enemies
- Kill all the obsidian trash mobs about 4 waves to summon named. Trash mobs hate-link each other

### [[Galremos (Heroic)|Galremos]]

- This is a DPS check
- When starting boss fight. DPS needs to save nuke for adds (volatile construct of flames) later
- During the boss fight. [[Galremos (Heroic)|Galremos]] summon his adds(volatile construct of flames). And permanent gain 25% damage reduction max to 3(see buff1 for details)
- Volatile construct of flames appear every 60 sec or shorter. They auto run close to boss and cast self-destruct every 50 sec. This self-destruct spell takes 10 sec to cast and heal boss 10% hp if it close to boss in the end. And adds apply a strong AOE knock back with a lot AOE damage when successfully casting before it dies.
- Make a teammate drag adds away from boss further than 10 meters away. Or kill adds very quick within 50 sec before it has done casting self-destruct.
- Every 90 sec [[Galremos (Heroic)|Galremos]] cast a curse last for 20 sec to a teammate(see Galermos curse).It kills instantly like most curse when it terminated. Revive ASAP if one teammate dies.  Only "mana burns" can this be cured.  Maintain your debuff "**Burning Mana**" so a player priest can cure curse on it.
- Always Keeping teammate full mana especially those who just revive and lack mana. Otherwise try not to dispel "**Burning Mana**"
- DPS keeps drag trash and burn them. Other teammates use ascension spell burn down boss.(I try to one shot every trash as quick as possible but [[Galremos (Heroic)|Galremos]] still gets 3 increments)
- When boss HP less than 50%, lava rain come down and deal damage to everyone. 1 healer maybe not enough.
- Mercenaries and AOE immune pets ignore all the mechanics above.
