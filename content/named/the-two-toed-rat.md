## Spawn

There are three rat nests at {{waypoint -16, -14, -7}}, {{waypoint 69, -17, 122}}, {{waypoint 125, -5, 290}}. Clear all the [[A moat rat|moat rats]] away from them, and [[A large moat rat]] spawns. Kill the large moat rat, and [[The Two-toed Rat]] has a chance to spawn.
