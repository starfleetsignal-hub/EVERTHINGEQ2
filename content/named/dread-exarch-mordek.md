All fight on the dais.  Go to the pool once per 60 secs to mitigate the effects of the permanent curse. Return to the dais after every trip to the pool. Kill the named last. Use an anti-death on the tank to prevent curse problems if you anticipate this fight will take your group longer than one minute. Cure fast, but the curse is incurable so don't worry about curing curse. standing outside the barrier is deadly.
