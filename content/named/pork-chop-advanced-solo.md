## Strategy

![The cleaver needed to kill Pork Chop](images/Gud_Eats_Cleaver.png)

- To spawn Pork Chop, kill the [[The butcher's guard|butcher's guard]].
- The butcher will see his moment and try to run, going around the north side of the keep and despawning at the north west tower. When he reaches the north east tower he becomes attackable and if killed will spawn Pork Chop.
- Grab the **cleaver** on the wall inside [[Gud Eats Butcher (Scourge Keep)|Gud EatsButcher]], use the cleaver on Pork Chop, but it is not required to kill [[Pork Chop (Advanced Solo)]]
