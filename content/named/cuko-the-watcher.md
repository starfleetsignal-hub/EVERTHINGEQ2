Cuko the Watcher is a Tier 1 raid boss. He gains an additive advantage for each player below 12 in the raid and beyond a soft time limit of 60 minutes.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance 250.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance 700.0
- Resolve 13,230.0

## Abilities

- **Conscription of Blight**
- A buff that gives a chance of triggering Ocular Blight on the attacker, which interrupts, deals disease damage, and debuffs offensive ability.
- **Spotted Advantage**
- A buff that reduces damage taken from all sources for each dead player in the encounter area.
- **Surging Disfigurement**
- An incurable elemental that deals increasing heat and divine damage to health and power over time.
- **Mindwracking Influence I**
- An incurable arcane that increases the power cost of all abilities.
- **Cuko's Determined Burn**
- A curable elemental that deals fast ticking increasing elemental damage and increases physical damage from all sources.
- **Cuko's Impending Storm**
- A curable elemental that deals fast ticking increasing elemental damage and increases physical damage from all sources. This effect can be blocked by area effect blockers.
- **Cuko's Incited Rage**
- A curable elemental that deals fast ticking increasing elemental damage and increases physical damage from all sources.
- **Cuko's Masterful Slice**
- A curable elemental that deals fast ticking increasing elemental damage and increases physical damage from all sources. This effect can be blocked by area effect blockers.
- **Gazed Upon**
- A curable arcane that deals divine damage and increases elemental and physical damage from all sources. This effect applies with 3 increments, all of which must be removed to remove the effect completely.
- **Watchful Strike**
- A curable arcane that deals increasing magic damage and increases all incoming elemental damage. This ability can be blocked by area effect blockers.
- **Void Shackled I**
- A curable curse that prevents the target from moving or casting spells. It also deals increasing crushing damage. The curse is initially incurable for 5 seconds. When cured, it propagates to any other player within 8 meters.
