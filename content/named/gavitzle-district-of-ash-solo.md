## Strategy

1. [[Gavitzle (District of Ash Solo)|Gavitzle]] is not aggro to begin with, but if you step close and talk to the NPC, it turns aggro and will spawn adds.
1. Initially [[Gavitzle (District of Ash Solo)|Gavitzle]] shields himself in a gelatinous cube and summons several linked [[Animated dhalgar bones]] and [[Animated gnemlin bones]] to attack you.
1. Once the adds have been killed, [[Gavitzle (District of Ash Solo)|Gavitzle]] summons a portal and uses it to throw **a trailing puss bomb** at you, then ports a short distance away. Run over to him and scrape the bomb off on his gelatinous cube, then back out to avoid the blast.
1. Eventually, the explosions weaken his shielding enough that he takes damage.
