## Notes

For an 85 ^ monster, this mob is **seriously** underconned!!

Scorching Breath of Undeath will hit for 1200-4000 roughly every 3s. Its range is less than usual spell range, so the AE can be avoided at max range
Paralyzing Bite usually hits for under 2000, but can hit for 5000+. This is no AE.

Duoed with Coercer and Necro (both 83), but should be soloable by Necro. Did not drop anything at all.
