- Must be separated from [[A Healthy Corpuscle]]. If not, they will rejoin as a new [[Amorphous Drake]] at 100% hp.
- Immune to slashing
- Withering Shroud, ST DD (high damage amount) + power drain (can be 100%)
- Stunning Cry: AE DD + short stun
