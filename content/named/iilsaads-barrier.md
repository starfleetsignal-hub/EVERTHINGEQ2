## Special Attacks

### Combustible Coating

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 9% chance to cast Combustible Sparks on target's attacker.
  - Interrupts target
  - Throws target back
  - Inflicts 7,468 - 13,869 heat damage on target.

### Iilsaad's Retort

Effects:

- Inflicts 35.0% of max health in magic damage on target instantly and every 2 seconds.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases power of target by 35.0% instantly and every 2 seconds.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Iilsaad's Transformation

Effects:

- Interrupts target
- Decreases power of target by 7.0%.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 7.0% of max health in crushing damage on target.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
- Decreases Casting Speed of target by 100.0%.
- Decreases Haste of target by 99.0.
- Slows target by 140.0%.
- If this spell expires naturally, you will turn to stone and those close to you will begin Iilsaad's Transformation.

### Rock Storm

Effects:

- Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
- Roots targets in Area of Effect
- Resistibility increases against targets higher than level 117.

## Strategy

- During this fight, Iilsaad's Barrier will cause a fairly large glowing circle to appear on the floor. Players have a short amount of time to joust out of it.
