## Notes

Othysis Muravian's auto attack does crushing and disease damage.  Every 34 seconds will emote, "Through the portal now", and summon a [[A D'Morte preserver|D'Morte preserver]], a heroic mob which will heal Othysis Muravian with Gift of Rejuvenation and Superior Gift of Rejuvenation for 12.3 million hp if it gets close enough.  Every 30 seconds, she emotes, "Time for some soul searching", and does a Disease DD (Prone to Corruption) to a random person in the raid which will spawn an add (Epic x2) if they DON'T die to it.
