## Notes

- This version of Gawar only spawns during a certain step on the quest [[Passage to the Isle of Zek]].  After going into Blackburrow and collecting a jar of gnoll spirits, shortly after you leave Gawar will attack.
  - It's possible he may spawn in other locations depending on which Blackburrow exit you use.
