## Special Attacks

### Curse of Devastation

Effects:

- Interrupts target
- Decreases power of target by 15.0%.
- Decreases power of target by 67.0% every 4 seconds.
- Inflicts 15.0% of max health in disease damage on target.
- Inflicts 67.0% of max health in disease damage on target every 4 seconds.
- If you die while this curse is on you, it will spread to those closest to you.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Curse of Lethargy

Effects:

- Decreases AGI and WIS of target by 85.8.
- Slows target by 41.9%.
- Slows recast and casting times are lengthened.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Grim Raiments

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 3% chance to cast Necrotic Presence on target's attacker. Lasts for 45.0 seconds.
  - Summons a limited pet to aid the caster

## Strategy

- At the beginning of the fight, several [[Fallen dead]] spawn and attack.  They drop [[Necrotic Flashpot|Necrotic Flashpots]] that must be farmed for the fight against The Three Sages.
- At 90%, Maalus will emote that he is beginning a ritual.  Right click him and prevent.  Failure to prevent causes him to become [[Maalus Imbued]].
- Maalus has many curses, but only one needs to be cured.  Curse of Devastation is a health and power drain that, if a cursed character dies, will spread to other raid members near him.
