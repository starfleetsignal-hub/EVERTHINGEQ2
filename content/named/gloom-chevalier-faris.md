#### Strategy

Clear all of the trash mobs from the floor in order to activate the lift. As soon as the lift activates, Faris will float down towards you and the lift will begin moving upwards counter clockwise around the room.

Faris cannot be damaged directly, instead as the lift approaches each cubby a set of flying books will spawn. Killing the books will damage a portion of Faris.

As the lift moves, be mindful of the cubby holes with the lightning towers as these will damage you. Best to try to have the group continue to move and stand as far away from the cubby on the opposite edge of the floating platform.

Once you near the top and Faris gets to around 25%, killing the last round of books will put him under 10% where he can now be outright burned down.
