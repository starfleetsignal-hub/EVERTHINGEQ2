The color combination for the first 3 fragments inserted does not matter. The dragon spawns are based on the color of the 4th fragment inserted. Once a dragon has been defeated, the option to use a fragment of that same color as the 4th insert will go away and you will no longer be able to spawn it again in that same instance. But you must insert 4 different colored crystals. You get enough crystals per zone to spawn 2 dragons.

Tukaarak: x-x-x-Black
