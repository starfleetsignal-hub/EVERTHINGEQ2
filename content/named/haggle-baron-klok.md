## Strategy

Clear all trash mobs before getting to Klok. There are two types of mobs to clear; the first is a single mob that has a nasty instakill AOE. Have casters stand at max range from the main tank. The second type is a group of five mobs, one heroic and four regular x2. Beat down the four lesser mobs and when the heroic is down to about 15-20%, he spawns a burynai that will charge the group. It seems that this add spawns from where the group of five was pulled. Make sure the MT grabs aggro and burn them down.

Once the area is cleared of all trash, pull Klok to the middle of the zone. Every 15% he will cast a detrimental on most of the raid. Check detrimental window to see what color it is, blue or red. At that time, there will be a red and a blue bubble that will emerge. You will need to go into the corresponding color bubble and wait the curse out. If you are not in the correct bubble when the curse runs out, you will be charmed and turn on your raid.

When Klok is down to about 15% he will spawn 6 adds to come to his aid. Mez or off-tank them while you finish Klok.
