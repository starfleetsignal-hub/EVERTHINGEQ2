## Strategy

- Burn and Joust

- Bazziz will be at the top of the spiral ramp with two [[A tamed grizzly]] flanking him. You can body pull him away from the grizzlies however he will occasionally call for them to 'run' the group down. When he does, joust away from where you were currently fighting him as the grizzlies will run to that location and if you're still in range will do massive damage.

- Recommend clearing all the bixies opposite of where Bazziz is to give the group room to joust away.
