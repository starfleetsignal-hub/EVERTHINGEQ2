## Strategy

The main part of the zone has a circular area to run around. Everyone should stay on the same side of the slime. When the slime NPC Say emotes, everyone run backwards (including tank) until the slime casts his detriment (green flashes), then return to killing. The minimum distance is 25m to avoid accumulation of detriment.
