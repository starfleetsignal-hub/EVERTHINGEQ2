This npc spawns with 12 drakes and is not attackable. To make him attackable you must kill all the drakes on the temple. As you kill the 4 on the bottom lvl, the middle lvl becomes targerable, then when those are dead, the top is targetable. Finally after all the drakes are dead, the guy on top wakes up.

There is a 2 hour spawn timer on this ring event. [[Herald of Dar]] does not spawn the ring event.
