## Buffs

### Greater Sky Rage

Effects:

- Greater Sky Rage grants power to the wearer.

## Special Attacks

### Charged Concealment

Effects:

- Inflicts 25.0% of max health in magic damage of target instantly and every 6.6 seconds.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Elemental Aura

Effects:

- Opponents have a 12% chance every 15 secs to be struck with Shock of Lightning, a spell that does damage and drains mana to the target and those near.

### Gloom Wound

Effects:

- Inflicts 276,565 - 645,318 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 304,222 - 456,332 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 239,690 - 359,535 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 134,825 - 164,787 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 145,197 - 177,463 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 18 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 115,235 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 4 seconds.
  - Any distance up to 18 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Increases power of targets in Area of Effect by 39,696.
  - Any point not covered in the above effects.
- Inflicts 576,177.0% of max health in heat damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4 seconds.
  - Any point not covered in the above effects.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases Casting Speed of targets in Area of Effect by 100.0%.
- Decreases Haste of targets in Area of Effect by 100.0.
  - If Target is closer than 19 meters

### Haze of Umbrage

Effects:

- Heals caster for 116,312.
- Inflicts 18,322 - 27,482 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 4,886 - 7,329 cold damage on targets in Area of Effect every 4.5 seconds.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 15.0%.
- Inflicts 55.0% of max health in divine damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Reduces Melee Multiplier of targets in Area of Effect by 2.00.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Selective Marker

Effects:

- You are able to damage your raid target.

### Shadowed Charge

Effects:

- When Kildrukaun takes damage, charged elementals will be released. Specific arcane enchantments can ground you, dampening their release.

### Stormforce

Effects:

- Increases STR and INT of caster by 1,275.1.
- On a melee hit this spell will cast Stormslam on target of attack.
  - Inflicts 118,751 slashing damage on target.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Thunderous Echo

Effects:

- If target successfully avoids any melee attack, target takes damage from Thunderclap.
