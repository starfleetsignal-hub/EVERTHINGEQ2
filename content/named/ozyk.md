## Special Attacks

### Blademaster's Boon

Effects:

- On a melee attack this spell will cast Blademaster's Melee on target of attack.
  - Multiple attacks to different targets will accompany each melee swing.

### Cloak of the Blademaster

Effects:

- When any damage is received this spell has a 11% chance to cast Blademaster's Rebuke on target's attacker. Last for 4.0 seconds.
  - Throws target back
  - Inflicts 6,041 - 9,062 slashing damage on target.

## Strategy

- Kill the adds that pops quickly and move away from the bodies to avoid a  10 meter blast
