## Prerequisites

You have to kill

- the level 128 epic x3 public quest mob [[Bloodletter (Monster)]] (Achievement: [[Leechy Keen]])

and

- the level 127 epic x3 public quest mob [[The Great Saprophyte]] (Achievement: [[Crimini!]])

first, **in the same run**!

## Notes

The Root Mother monster is wandering the pathway up and down from [[The Chlorochamber]] to [[Mossy Mists Standing]] after a few minutes of killing the both public quest monsters.
