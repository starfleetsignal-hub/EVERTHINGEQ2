## Strategy

- Obtain the pie from the kitchen @ {{waypoint -107, -130.7, -35}} to get the option to 'Throw pie!' at Bloogly to allow them to be attacked.
- Grouped with [[An offbeat drummer]] and [[A string-snapping guitarist]] - burn them down first then focus on Bloogly.
