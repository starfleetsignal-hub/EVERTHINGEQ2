- To make him attackable:
  1. Approach him and wait for him to summon [[A servant of nihility|servants of nihility]].
  1. Kill the servants.
  1. Kill the summoned [[Bondur Flayfist]] and [[Gorusk Flayfist]].
- Killing the Keeper will open the door to the third level at {{waypoint 48, 567, 442}}.
