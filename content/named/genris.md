## Special Attacks

### Anxiety

Effects:

- Inflicts 1,987 - 7,949 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 1,711 - 6,845 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect every 3 seconds.
- Inflicts an additional 55 - 210 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect every tick.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases Mitigation of targets in Area of Effect vs noxious damage by 4,480 - 6,720.

### Crushing Hopes

Effects:

- Inflicts 1,987 - 7,949 disease damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 1,711 - 6,845 disease damage on targets in Area of Effect every 3 seconds.
- Inflicts an additional 55 - 210 disease damage on targets in Area of Effect every tick.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases Mitigation of targets in Area of Effect vs noxious damage by 4,480 - 6,720.

### Putrid Breath

Effects:

- Inflicts 6,346 - 25,384 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Inflicts an additional 83 - 334 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect every tick.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 5,511 - 22,044 disease damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Inflicts an additional 83 - 334 disease damage on targets in Area of Effect every tick.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.

### Shell Shock

Effects:

- Inflicts 4,801 - 14,404 crushing damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 3 seconds.
- Inflicts an additional 208 - 626 crushing damage on caster every tick.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases Reuse Speed of caster by 25.0%.
- Decreases Casting Speed of caster by 25.0%.
