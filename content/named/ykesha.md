## Special Attacks

### Aura of Torpor

Effects:

- Interrupts target
- Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
- Decreases Haste of target by 116.7.
- Your spells and melee attacks have reduced effectiveness and longer cast times.

### Cloak of Retaliation

Effects:

- 50% of the time the wearer takes any damage from sources 10 or more units away, that damage is returned on the attacker. 8% of the time the wearer takes damage from sources closer than 10 units, the attacker will have the Aura of Torpor debuff cast on it.

### Ykesha's Touch

Effects:

- Inflicts 446,934 - 830,020 disease damage on target every 3 seconds.
- Heals caster for 826,082.
- Decreases Potency of target by 600.0.
- Decreases Multi Attack of target by 100.0.
- Decreases Accuracy of target by 50.0%.
- Decreases Effective Level of target by 1.0.
- Decreases Mitigation of target vs physical damage by 2,160.
- Your combat damage and healing effectiveness are greatly reduced.

### Ykesha's Warcurse

Effects:

- Applies Ykesha's Battlestorm on termination.
- Decreases Potency of target by 50.0.
- Grants Ultravision to target
- Removing this curse will spare the raid the horrors of Ykesha's Final Solution.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means
