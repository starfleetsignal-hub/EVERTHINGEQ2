## Strategy

- Tank with your back to the stump located on his peninsula.
- He has a melee AoE that has to be jousted, *Piercing Darkness*.
- He has an AoE knockback, so control your flight by holding forward while airborne.
- You'll have to cure *Noxious*.
- There are arcane/traumatic poxules that have to be killed by mages/priests and fighters/scouts (if the wrong class hits it first, you're SOL)
  - If the poxules are up too long, they cast an AoE stun on the classes that are not able to kill them, so make sure you kill them quickly.
- *Battle Weary* (raid-wide debuff) sets in after 6:30.
  - *Combat Fatigue*, the second stage of *Battle Weary* sets in a couple mins later. You probably wont live through *Battle Weary*, though, and you WILL die to the next *Piercing Darkness* melee AoE once *Combat Fatigue* has been applied.
- Avatar has *Consume Soul*, so stay out of range of his melee AoE because it'll heal him.
