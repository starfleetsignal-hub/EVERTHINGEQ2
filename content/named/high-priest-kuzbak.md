## Strategy

To defeat this guy, it's definitely easier with a group. This guy heals himself once his health is under 40% and it gets really frustrating to kill him, even when your level is over 70. If you can stun him, that's your best bet if you're going solo. Otherwise, good luck taking him out alone!
