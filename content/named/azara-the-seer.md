## Special Attacks

### Cloak of Inquiry

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 9% chance to cast Brutal Inquiry on target's attacker. Lasts for 6.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 1,117 - 2,074 magic damage on target instantly and every 6 seconds.
  - Decreases power of target by 8.0%.
  - Decreases power of target by 4.0% every 6 seconds.
  - Roots target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Root effects of 18.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 2.0 seconds.
  - Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Dark Wind

Effects:

- Inflicts 12,239 - 36,717 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every 9 seconds.
- Reduces slow effects on targets in Area of Effect by 140.0%.
