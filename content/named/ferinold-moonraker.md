## Buffs

### Planar Power

Effects:

- Wards caster against 1,832 - 3,637 points of damage from all damage
- Increases DPS of caster by 37.5 - 62.5.

### Spiritual Onslaught

Effects:

- Spiritual Onslaught grants attacks power to the wearer.

### Web of Protection

Effects:

- The spider king is protected by his summoned spiders.
- Will absorb all damage.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

## Special Attacks

### Destructive Rake

Effects:

- Inflicts 7,893 - 23,678 crushing damage on caster.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases Reuse Speed of caster by 25.0%.
- Increases Casting Speed of caster by 25.0%.

![](images/Ferinold_Moonraker.jpg)

## Strategy

Burn Ferinold until he summons spider adds. When the adds are up, Ferinold is invulnerable to damage. Burn the adds as fast as possible, then return to Ferinold. This is a DPS check boss.

Ferinold can be defeated solo, but depending on your DPS, it can be slow (30-60 minutes).
