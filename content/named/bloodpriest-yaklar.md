## Strategy

- Once he dies, a mob will pop in each of the blood pools next to the altar and attack you.
- During the fight he may teleport a member of your group to the blood altar, where he will be chained down.  You have to attack the chains to break them and free the captive member.

Would recommend a group feign death or evac after killing the bloodpriest to avoid the combat with the mobs in the blood pools. See strategy on Tuz'Rak for explanation.
