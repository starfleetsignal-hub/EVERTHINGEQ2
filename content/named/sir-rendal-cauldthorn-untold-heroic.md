Ability and strategy information changes at stages (1-20/21-40/41+).

## Abilities

- **Aura of Attrition**
- A curable curse that lands on all players at the start of combat. It increases all damage done to the target by 1% multiplied by increments. The number of increments rises as Cauldthorn loses health.
- **Penitence**
- An incurable elemental that deals heat damage and drains power over time. It also procs Defensive Breach, which deals large amounts of magic damage.
- **Truth Hurts**
- A buff that sets Cauldthorn's health to a minimum of 10% multiplied by increments. Cauldthorn can only die when the increment count is zero. Increments are reduced by killing [[A Knight of Truth]] in the Southern and Northern Feeding Pits.
- **Aura of Agony**
- A buff that has a 1% chance to cast Pain Reflection, multiplied by increments. The number of increments rises as Cauldthorn loses health. Pain Reflection deals magic damage, plus an additional 10% of max health in immitigable heat damage if not within 10 meters of a fighter. At stage 21+, it additional decreases power. At stage 41+, it additionally drains 10% of wards.
- **Pull of Remorse**
- An incurable detriment that allows the target to land a deathblow on Cauldthorn. It also roots the target and slows the target by 100.0%. This detriment is cast when the last [[A Knight of Truth|Knight of Truth]] dies and lasts for 15 seconds

## Strategy

When the last [[A Knight of Truth|Knight of Truth]] dies, the entire group is teleported into the air with Cauldthorn, standing on an invisible platform.
