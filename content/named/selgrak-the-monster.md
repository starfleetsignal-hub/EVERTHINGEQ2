## Strategy

Down in the main hall, Selgrak summons friends from the pods around the room.  They are the main problem as they have an aoe stifle. The method to defeat him is to clear the entire room and the hallway to the north of Selgrak.  Then follow one of the two strategies:

1. Have the main tank stand on the stairs and pull Selgrak.  The healers should be in the doorway between the hallway and Selgraks orginal room and your secondary tank or DPS tank shall intercept the adds when they come in at 85%, 65%, 45% 25% and are immune to mez and root.  If the adds get close to the hallway then a massive dmg aoe will occur.  Its best to keep the adds on the main square of Selgrak's room while keeping the ability of the healers to heal both parties.

2. Go full on balls to the wall and do all the damage you can possibly do to the named!!

NOTE:  If you get mobs running at you from the door to the south it means that you allowed the add to get too close to the named...
