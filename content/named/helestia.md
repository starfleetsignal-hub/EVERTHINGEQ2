## Notes

- Spawned by clearing the [[A fettered wraith|fettered wraiths]], then clicking the tombstone.
- Easy kill, but casts a noxious detrimental every 20 sec. that ***will kill you if not cured*** before it expires in about 15 seconds.  You're going to want a nice stack of nox cure potions if you aren't a healer.
