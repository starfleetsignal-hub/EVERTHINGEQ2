## Strategy

- Spawned by pulling [[An anarchic lurcher (Heroic)|anarchic lurkers]] to the black portal at {{waypoint -202, 16, 33}} and killing them in the center of the portal. The lurkers can be found between the [[Amphitheater of Rancor (SoH: Utter Contempt Heroic)|Amphitheater of Rancor]] and [[The Abbey (SoH: Utter Contempt Heroic)|The Abbey]] at {{waypoint -128.36, 17.15, -34.79}}, {{waypoint -110.45, 15.53, 0.09}}, and {{waypoint -143.07, 14.76, 37.14}}.
