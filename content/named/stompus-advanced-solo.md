## Strategy

- Surrounded by a group of [[A Pickclaw conveyor operator]] that will join him when attacked.
- Stompus will initially charge and then retreat back to the crusher saying he has to keep it running. Burn down the adds before approaching him.
- As you attack him, occasionally large pieces of boulders will fall from the ceiling - they did not appear to damage us as we stood on top of him. Burned him down where he stood.
