## Strategy

The Master summons groups of "a ripping terror" which look like dogs. Shortly after summoning the dogs, he will drop a 12k (unmitigated) heat based nuke on whoever has agro.  He also has a chance to reflect any spell cast on him to the group/caster. AE/EncounterAE spells will hit your whole group, single target spells will only hit the person who cast it. Stunning him or mezzing him can have a devastating effect because your healers can be stunned.

This guy is cake, simply get everyone in group to JUST use auto attack, let healers heal when they need to. He does almost no damage at all if no one uses any spells or arts.

After numerous attempts at this, we found that you can use Blue AOE on him, making life a little easier, these don't get reflected.  Best thing to do is review your combat logs to find out what you can and can't do with him.

**Just Burn him down fast, the above strategy no longer applies. Based upon 10 fights done as of 10/28/2009**

## Notes

- >>**Edit for high level mentoring down for Scout Epic 2.0 update**<<
  - Made use of **Abyssal Elixir of Thorns**, a L96 thorn potion, which does ~300,000 damage; each of the Named have ~900,000 hps. Merely face AWAY from Named, their hitting you will *slowly* damage them, drag near their color platform, rinse/repeat for remaining colors.
  - To clarify the above: the mobs must **lowered** to 50% health on or near the pads of their corresponding color and cannot be rooted if they are being *one shot* down to zero... that is why they will insta-heal and it will *appear* as though they are taking no damage. I had to strip **all** my gear, remove **all** spell buffs that proc damage and mentor down to level 75 and I was **then** able to **lower** their health to 50% and voilà...the fight proceeds as intended, they root, the "Master" appears and you may continue. Epic update a short walk up the stairs after you place the final crystal on the pedestal.
