## Strategy

- Spawns at the top of level of the room with a handful of non-agro frogloks wandering in the vicinity.

- Strategy 1: Have the group setup in an area with no wandering frogloks, such as outside the room in the cubby and pet pull Ziza to the corner as to not have to worry about accidentally AOE'ing a nearby froglok.

- Strategy 2: Fight him in the room surrounding by the frogloks but make sure to have no AOE attacks or abilities going off. As the adds spawn the nearby frogloks will assist you in attacking them.
