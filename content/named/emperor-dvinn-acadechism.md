## Strategy

This mob turns invisible and launches multiple shadows of himself towards the party at several intervals.  At the end of the fight you must fight him + his shadows at the same time.  This is in addition to the other orc bosses in the room so be ready for a hard fight.

## Faction

- bad faction with **Clan Crushbone**
