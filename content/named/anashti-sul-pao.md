## Special Attacks

### Abyssal Monitor

Effects:

- If your power reserves fall too low, it could spell disaster!

### Abyssal Tempest

Effects:

- Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 6.0% instantly and every 6 seconds.
- Inflicts 7,236 - 16,883 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 1,475 - 3,442 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 9 meters
- Inflicts 5,937 - 8,905 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 1,299 - 1,948 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is between 9 and 14 meters away
- Inflicts 3,966 - 4,847 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 417 - 510 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is between 14 and 100 meters away
- Targets in Area of Effect can not cast hostile spells.
- When beginning a beneficial spell cast this spell may cast Abyssal Shock on caster. Triggers about 1.8 times per minute.
  - Interrupts target
  - Stuns target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 27.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 3.0 seconds.
- When a combat art is used this spell may cast Abyssal Shock on target. Lasts for 9.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Stuns target
  - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 27.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 3.0 seconds.

### Cloak of Ancient Enchantments

Effects:

- When any damage is received this spell has a 8% chance to cast Abyssal Strike on target's attacker. Lasts for 10.0 seconds.
  - 15% chance to apply Abyssal Shock. Lasts for 9.0 seconds.
    - Interrupts target
    - Stuns target
    - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 27.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 3.0 seconds.
  - 20% chance to apply Abyssal Silence.
    - Interrupts target
    - Stifles target
    - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stifle effects of 54.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 6.0 seconds.
  - 20% chance to apply Abyssal Mandate.
    - Forces target to clear their selected target
    - Disarms Primary, Secondary and Ranged slots of target.
    - Interrupts target
    - Prevents target from changing targets
    - When a combat art is used this spell will cast Abyssal Shock on target. Lasts 9.0 seconds.
      - Interrupts target
      - Stuns target
      - Epic targets gain an immunity to Stun effects of 27.0 seconds and duration is reduced to 3.0 seconds.
    - When beginning a spell cast this spell will cast Abyssal Interruption on target.
      - Interrupts target
      - Throws target back
      - Inflicts 2,226 - 5,195 magic damage on target
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 6,401 - 14,935 magic damage on target instantly and every 5 seconds.
  - Decreases power of target by 6.0%.

- Decreases power of caster by 7.0% instantly and every 7.1 seconds.
- Inflicts 9,740 - 29,221 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 3,316 - 9,949 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect every 7.1 seconds.
  - If Target is closer than 9 meters
- Inflicts 6,818 - 12,662 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 3,084 - 5,728 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect every 7.1 seconds.
  - If Target is between 9 and 14 meters away
- Inflicts 5,380 - 8,071 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 1,484 - 2,226 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect every 7.1 seconds.
  - If Target is between 14 and 90 meters away
- Decreases Crit Chance of targets in Area of Effect by 207.0.
- Decreases Potency of targets in Area of Effect by 207.0.

### Nether Tide

Effect:

- Interrupts targets in Area of Effect
- Throws caster back
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect. by 11.0%
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 6.0% every 6 seconds.
- Inflicts 520 - 2,081 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 200 - 800 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is closer than 9 meters
- Inflicts 715 - 1,073 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 211 - 317 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is between 9 and 14 meters away
- Inflicts 567 - 693 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Inflicts 183 - 224 mental damage on targets in Area of Effect every 6 seconds.
  - If target is between 14 and 200 meters away
- Decreases Haste of targets in Area of Effect by 77.8.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Presence of the Void

You feel the power of trickery at work.<br>
Effects:

- Target will lose 43% more power when power is consumed.
- 279% of target's power consumed will also be drained from target's health.
- When beginning a spell cast this spell may cast Void Intercession on target. Lasts 1.8 times per minute.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 1,670 - 9,462 magic damage on target.
- When a combat art is used this spell has a 7% chance to cast Void Interdiction on target. Lasts for 7.0 seconds.
  - Forces target to clear their selected target.
  - Disarms Primary, Secondary and Ranged slots of target.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 2,783 - 15,770 magic damage on target.
  - Prevents target from changing target

### The Ancient's Curse

Effects:

- When a combat art is used this spell will cast Ancient Vortex on target.
  - Any spell or combat art you initiate will cause massive negative energies to be released, decimating your raiding party.
- On a spell cast this spell will cast Ancient Vortex on target.
  - Any spell or combat art you initiate will cause massive negative energies to be released, decimating your raiding party.
- Interrupts target
- Inflicts 67,214 - 156,832 magic damage on target.
- Inflicts 30,406 - 70,948 magic damage on target every 15 seconds.
