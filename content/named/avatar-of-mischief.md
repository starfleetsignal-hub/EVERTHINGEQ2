## Notes

The **Avatar of Mischief** also appears in game as [[The Grand Prankster]] during the celebrations of [[Bristlebane Day]].

## Blessing

If you are a follower of Bristlebane, when the avatar is up, you may approach it and receive a 5 hour buff called Blessing of Bristlebane.
