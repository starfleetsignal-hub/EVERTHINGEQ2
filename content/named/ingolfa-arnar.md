## Strategy

There are three aspects of this fight to be aware of for both the normal and "challenge" mode:

- ***Teleports:*** Ingolfa will occasionally teleport one of the group members outside of the room into the south hallway. They must hurry back inside the room to stop Ingolfa from potentially leaving the room and triggering the challenge mode.
- ***Disease Pools:*** Red text will appear about a pool forming around a member of the group. Move away from that spot to prevent getting hit by a small range AOE.
- ***Adds:*** 'Phantasmal echoes begin to coalesce around you' and two adds will spawn. In normal mode if your DPS is high enough, you can just power through and let the residual AOE splash damage take these out.
