1. You must defeat Yellow Eyes and then Tol to have access to Famyra the Biblioghost.
1. Part way through the fight she smooshes the apprentices under the bookcases and an ancient tome spawns above each. Each tome gives her 25% damage reduction.
1. Destroy the tomes, then finish her off.

- Famyra has a power drain.  Its not serious but something to watch out for.
- The adds 'enraged apprentices' and 'Vortex of Pages' make this fight a little challenging I died but my healer merc stayed up long enough to rez me. I had my mage pet up and he couldn't pull aggro from the adds in time before I died. You'll need good AoE's or good heals to get through this quickly.
- I suggest burn Famyra until the adds come in
- Burn down the adds.
- Then kill the books.
- Finish off Famyra.
