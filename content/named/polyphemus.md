## Strategy

- Massively underconned and immune to root and control effects.  Bring a group.
- Seems to be a tank and spank mob but he has lots of HP which makes it a tough fight for a small group. He does have a Savage Assault and Beatdown which appear to be AOE's. We killed him with a 90 Wizard, 90 Warden and 88 Necro; other than taking a few minutes to kill him, he didn't really have a chance.

## History

[Polyphemus](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyphemus) is a Cyclops from Homer's epic poem, *The Odyssey*. He is located on an island in a cave with sheep, just as recorded by the Greek poet.
