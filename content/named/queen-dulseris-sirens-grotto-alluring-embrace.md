This is a long fight as Queen Dulseris calls her heroic allies to protect her. Prepare for at least ten groups of mobs to protect the Queen Dulseris and use power items such as the brains found in the grotto to help keep your power up. Eventually the queen will run towards the doors and also send another Heroic mob to fight you.

After defeating the last Heroic mob, click onto the same doors where the Queen Dulseris ran into and follow her deeper in her Grotto chambers called Tavalan Abyss.<br>
