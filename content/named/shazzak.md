## Notes

- To access the final boss, in a grotto under the main chamber, you must activate a pedestal device on each landing as you climb to the top. There are a total of 5 (4 are guarded + 1 at the pinnacle. You may then jump to the bottom from the top (it IS water, afterall) or run back down.  There is an elevator in the giant statue, that is now exposed, that can be activated to bring you to Shazzak's chamber.
