## Strategy

Frozen Statue of the Ice Maiden

- 2.7 Million Damage Taken (Was this the last 10% when we could damage it? Probably.)
- 67% of melee damage caused by crushing (Her adds do slashing damage though)
- Roughly every 2 mins she seems to use a cold based melee attack "Freeze" which hits for 7-8k. This seems to "Ancestral Centry" (average 4-5 hits on it per cast)... a short range AoE melee proc ??? but why multi-hits on same target?
- 100% of non-melee damage: "Calling of Ice". Roughly every 30 seconds. Seems a larger range AoE as it was hitting mages/healers. Cold based hit between 2-8k, average seems 5k.

Click the statue to activate the mob.

She will summon two heroic ^^^ adds when she is pulled. The name cannot be damaged directly. To kill her you must "kill" her adds.

When an add dies, two more will pop in the center of the room to take it's place and this happens each time one is killed. In order to kill the name you must kill the adds which in turn removes health from the name.

By the end of the fight you will have many, many adds, so you need a tank who can take the damage and healers who can heal through it. It is possible to mez some of the adds.

When the name dies all adds will despawn.

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=09-j72O4pqk

After she is down, you will need to use the [[Icy Sledge]] to break down the ice wall and continue through the zone.

Also, the Ice Maiden must be tanked in her room, and cannot be pulled out of it.
