## Strategys

- You'll see Ziskaya channeling and The Chamber Warden kneeled down. Click on Ziskaya and the Warden will stand up and four beams of light will go to the four 'chambers' creating barriers.
- When ready, click the glowing gold aura in front of the Warden - the person that clicks it will lose 50% health and the event will start.
- The Warden will begin attacking and one of the four barriers will drop down and one of the four Icons (Force, Matter, Pestilence, Spirit) will now be able to be attacked.
- If the Icon is allowed to get within range of the Warden, it will force the group to automatically target the Warden and he will be immune to damage.
- The tactic our team used was to have the tank stay planted in the middle where the Warden is and holding agro on him, while the rest of the group rushed into the chambers and killed the Icon's one at a time. If the Icon managed to slip past and agro'd onto the tank, he'd kite around until there was a gap and we could regain threat of the Icon to peel it off.
