The main purpose of this NPC is as a quest giver.

Another (apparently identical) version of Nagafen exists in the instance [[The Arbiter's Master]]; in this instance, Nagafen will give quest 5 for [[Deathtoll Access]].

## Trivia

Nagafen is also used (in a slightly modified version) on the Test server. It's used as a way to remove players from areas that they shouldn't be, such as at bottomless pits that aren't properly scripted yet, or in areas that do not have working teleporters. This NPC may also be referred to as *Naggy*.

In the past, it was possible to kill Nagafen. However, he is now level 150 and effectively unkillable.
