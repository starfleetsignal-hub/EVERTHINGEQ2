## Special Attacks

### Physical Impairment

Effects:

- Inflicts 20,429 - 47,668 divine damage on target instantly and every 10 seconds.
- Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
- Decreases Accuracy of target by 25.0%.
- Decreases Haste of target by 18.8.
- Your combat arts have longer casting and reuse times, and your melee and combat art damage is compromised.
