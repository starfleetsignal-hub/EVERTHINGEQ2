## Strategy

Be sure to clear the trash mobs throughout the zone, otherwise he calls it.

At 80%, 60%, 40%, and 20% he will teleport and stun/stoneskin himself and everyone in the group must click on the Giant portal in the middle of the room to keep him from Mass AOE.
