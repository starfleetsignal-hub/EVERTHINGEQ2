## Strategy

- Master P`Tasa requires to have fighters in the raid, otherwise he will curse you and you will wipe. He will not accept Scouts as a tank as he deaggro's them and blurs off them faster. **[Read more on eq2zone](http://www.eq2zone.com/forums/showthread.php?p=14875#post14875)**
- Also bring some high divine resist with your (near or even above 12K can help).
- Don't use attacks that do less then 1K damage, because with every attack he has a 1% chance to cast a stonekin on him self that lasts for 2 seconds and absorbs the next 9999 attacks.
  - Do as much damage as possible with single attacks, avoid dots.
    - Any threat increase/decrease will cause the curse
