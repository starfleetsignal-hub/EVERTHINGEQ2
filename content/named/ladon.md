## Notes

- He and his minions each have a frontal AE, Fiery Breath. Hits for about 100 and ticks every 5 seconds for ~30-50.
- His two minions (4800 hp each) will heal him during the fight.
- For several years he was demoted from Epicx2 to Heroic, but as of mid-2017 he's Epic again!
- Sibling of [[Anguis]].
