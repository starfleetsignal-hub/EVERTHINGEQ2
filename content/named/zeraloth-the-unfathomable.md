Zeraloth the Unfathomable is a bonus raid boss that doesn't neatly fit into the [[Scars of Destruction]] raid progression. He has five difficulty modes, depending on the number of arms that are active in the fight. Each difficulty mode has a separate resolve check, achievement, and loot table. Each arm has its own abilities and strategy (listed on the individual arm pages). Each arm also enables arm-specific abilities on Zeraloth.

Virtually every mob in the zone is social with Zeraloth. Attacking any enemy results in Zeraloth joining the fight and beginning his early mechanics.

## Abilities

- **Long Arms of the Claws**
- A curable trauma that decreases the health and power of the target. The strength of the ability increases the closer a player is to Zeraloth.
- **Left Claw**
- A buff that increases Melee Weapon Range by 500.0% and raises melee damage as increments increase.
- **Right Claw**
- A buff that increases Melee Weapon Range by 500.0% and raises spell damage as increments increase.
- **Curse of Cure Curse**
- A curable curse that lands on a random priest in the raid. If the afflicted priest cures the curse of any player (including themself), they immediately die. When cured by another priest, the curse jumps to a different (random) priest.
- **Limbo**
- A curable elemental that deals heat damage over time and decreases power over time. This detriment automatically reapplies to the whole raid if the number of players affected by Limbo is less than the number of Unfathomable Arms that initially rose from the depths.

### Arm of Senzu

- **Arm of Senzu**
- A buff that reflects all hostile scout abilities while [[Arm of Senzu]] is alive.
- **Rearmament**: Senzu
- A buff that tracks the distance between two randomly-selected scouts (identifiable by an incurable trauma). If the scouts are more than 0 meters apart from each other when the buff expires, then [[Arm of Senzu]] revives at full health.

### Arm of Pruul

- **Arm of Pruul**
- A buff that reflects all hostile priest abilities while [[Arm of Pruul]] is alive.
- **Rearmament**: Pruul
- A buff that tracks the distance between two randomly-selected priests (identifiable by an incurable trauma). If the priests are more than 0 meters apart from each other when the buff expires, then [[Arm of Pruul]] revives at full health.

## Strategy

At the start of the fight, Zeraloth is accompanied by five [[A Fathomable Feeler|Fathomable Feelers]], each surrounded by a cohort of trash mobs. The number of feelers defeated before Zeraloth reaches 96% determines how many arms are active during the main battle.

Zeraloth continuously sends large waves throughout the zone. These waves are visible water effects that move across the zone, starting at Zeraloth and ending near the entrance. Tanks are naturally immune to the wave, and players standing near a tank are similarly protected. Players that are unprotected are knocked back significantly and stunned.
