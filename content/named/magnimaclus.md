## Notes

- Spawns as the final wave of the ring event.
- **Known Bug**: The mobs associated with the ring event can sometimes spawn beneath the ground, above the ceiling, or outside the walls, putting them out of range of attacks (even AoE). If this happens then you're SoL and must wait until the event can be started again. The event respawn is ~40 real-world minutes.
