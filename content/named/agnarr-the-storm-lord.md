Strat: 86% 58% 39% 6% hp he stoneskins and does a knockback then spawns orb adds which you may have to form into a triangle if it doesn't auto do it. Color of Agnars det determines if you stay in or outside of the triangle of orbs only two colors blue or yellow and seems to go with a in out in out pattern*(this may change). Each group has to cure det with last person curing after rest have cured. The adds you can burn down once they go from charged to inert.
