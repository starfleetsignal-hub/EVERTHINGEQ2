## Strategy

Morbigog is a tank and spank fight with no script that I noticed. He does spawn adds (a balanzite eruption and a shadow swarmer), however, they died quickly to my AE (swashy - 1.3 billion hp, 6.9k DPS, 299k pot, 796 fervor, 4150 resolve (unbuffed); you get a buff when you zone in that adds stats). There is a knockback (probably when he emotes, "Moribigog lifts its massive arms and brings them crashing down") but I only got it once since he died quickly. He tosses you in the direction you are forward facing. If you put your back to the wall, for example, he tosses you to the center of the room.
