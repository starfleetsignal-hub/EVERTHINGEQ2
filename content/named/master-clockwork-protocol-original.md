## Strategy

(After killing the King) Clear the mobs from the ground level. Move up the ramps, taking out the flyers too. Have all your dps run up into melee range (ontop of the little pyramid usually works (but carefully not to aggro one of the guards in the back rim), beat the snot out of him(?) until he starts talking about "analyzing" something or the other (<10-15%), he then puts up a force field of some sort. at this point, have who ever picked up that [[Energized Clockwork Gear|gear]] start going tron on his butt (if you dont understand that reference, i'm sorry. i mean use the gear charm, it will activate a ranged-like attack). couple uses, and he will go down.
