## Notes

- Part of a small ring event.  Clear all the [[A dust adder|dust adders]] in Spirit Valley, then clear the wave of [[Dust viper]]s that spawn around his rock. Either he will spawn, or a totem that gives a temporary buff will spawn.  If he doesn't spawn, wait for the dust adders to respawn and try again.

## Strategy

- Has a pretty damaging noxious detrimental, bring a few cure potions if you can't cure.  Otherwise tank n' spank.
