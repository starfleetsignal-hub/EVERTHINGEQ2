## Strategy

Delahnus is basically a tank and spank.  The main thing to watch out for is her tail attack, which will one-shot most non-tank classes.  The safe spot is directly in front of her.

To engage, run up the hill she sits on and stop right in front of her nose.  At intervals (10%?) she will fly around the room.  Use ranged attacks or just rest until she lands again.  Make sure to get in front of her, out of range of the tail AoE.

Once her health drops to 50%, she runs away.  The fight is won at that point.
