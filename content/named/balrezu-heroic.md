## Strategy

### Trash mobs

- Kill all scorched fiend in the room at the end of the tunnel to spawn Balrezu
- Heroic version seems to be more scorched fiend than solo version

### [[Balrezu (Heroic)|Balrezu]]

- This boss shares basically same mechanics in [[Solusek Ro's Tower: Monolith of Fire (Solo) (Instance)|solo version]]. But more difficult. Third Named
- He summons adds each time reaches a HP threshold.
  - 70% HP. First time 2 adds. They must be the **middle left** and **middle right** brazier.
  - 50% HP. Second time 4 adds .They must be the four corners braziers in room
  - 30% HP. Last time 6 (6 braziers in room). All braziers gets a add
  - He is immune while summoning
- Hold "Ctrl"+ Hold "Shift" + click "T" ---> Use your "Tag System" to TAG a add in your favorite position and remember which **brazier he comes from**. Save that for the last one.  Click the brazier you killed last one. Balrezu returns to fight.
- If you click wrong one, you will be teleported to a chamber full of Epic X4 mobs and get killed.
  - Epic X4 respawn immediately when you kill them. So you can't fight them actually.
  - If your other teammates are fast. Let them click different one till one of you get the right one before everyone dies out. It summons balrezu too.
- Be sure don't let your Mercenaries and Pets kill tagged mob too fast
