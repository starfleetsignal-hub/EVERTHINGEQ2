## Overview

The final boss (T5) of [[Renewal of Ro]] raiding.

## Strategy

- There are several scripted mechanics during the fight that must be completed at/before various health thresholds of the boss (75%, 50%, ..., 15% etc)
  - Mechanics involve at least 1 Bard and will require entering the portals to the sides of the platform during the fight, managing the adds on below platforms, and clicking specific things in specific orders .
  - Failure to complete these scripted mechanic *before* pushing the boss past each individual threshold in the sequence will result in an automatic raid wipe.  There will be a message indicating that the raid team was supposed to solve a 'puzzle'
- **NOTE:**  In the modern era, this 'puzzles' mechanic can be bypassed entirely ***iff*** the raid force can reduce the boss's HPs from 100% to 0% in 14 seconds or less from initial moment of aggro. *Any longer than that (15s or more), and the triggers that detect the 'thresholds crossed' kick in and wipe the raid automatically.*
