## Strategy

- To spawn, find [[A malformed sentry]] somewhere on the path between the Pedestal of Judgment and the [[Sentry Creation Chamber]].  Walk with the malformed sentry until it reaches the Pedestal of Judgment.  It will stop in the middle of the four [[A Sentry of Punishment|Sentries of Punishment]].  After a moment the Sentries of Punishment will begin attacking the malformed sentry.  The Defiled Sentry may spawn as soon as they attack it.
