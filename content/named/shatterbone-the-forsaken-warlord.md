## Strategy

To spawn, clear groups of skeletons around the big tree (the skeleton groups near the edge of the water may need to die too.) Next, clear the 5-6 new skeleton encounters that pop closer to the tents (battlepriests, soldiers, scouts.) Finally, Shatterbone pops with 6 heroic forgotten vanquishers (level 63, all separate heroic encounters.) Be careful of the roamers that continue to pop when you're doing this.
