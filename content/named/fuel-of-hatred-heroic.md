- To spawn, fight [[A bubble of hatred (Heroic)|bubbles of hatred]] until they're stunned, when you can click on them to gather a glob. next, go click on the door in [[Repugnance's Rectory (SoH: Utter Contempt Heroic)|Repugnance's Rectory]] {{waypoint 175.39, 10.40, 149.90}}. Repeat 5 times.
- [[A glob of malice (Heroic)|Globs of malice]] will add during the fight.
