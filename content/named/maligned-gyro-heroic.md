## Strategy

1. The hall is filled with spiral rippers, which cast an elemental detriment called Whirling Gale that hits for 20K-80K. It's a dodging game.
1. Clear the paired [[A bladed custodian|bladed custodians]] and totally avoid [[A swarming ripper|swarming rippers]] (two groups of eight each) to reach the Maligned Gyro.
1. Pull the Maligned Gyro to the door area so you can avoid the swarms of adds on either side. During the fight, he will summon a handful of [[A swarming ripper|swarming rippers]]. Move away from the summoned rippers if they get near you.
