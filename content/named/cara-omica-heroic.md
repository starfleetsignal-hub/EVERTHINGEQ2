## Heroic Strategy

1. Tank and Spank,  Run up to her then run away to make her active.
1. The 4 trash mobs around her will become active so kill them first.
1. After the trash is dead have your tank charge her and turn her away from the group.
1. During the encounter she will randomly port a group member to her when she does have them run away quickly.
