## Notes

![Fyst before LU33](images/Emperor_Fyst_%28Named_Monster%29.jpg)

- Prior to [[LU33]], Fyst was an Epic encounter. With the revamp of [[Deathfist Citadel]] he was demoted to Heroic, but later restored to Epic.
- If you ring the gong in [[Blood Bath Arena]], Emperor Fyst will spawn in NPC form and send [[Arena Champion Kraz'k]] against you.
