## Strategy

Clear the two groups of banshee's and spectre's in the area, careful to keep an eye out for Paldar as he is always pathing around.

On the west side of the center column there is a construct called an enkindled companion that is grouped with Paldar as part of the encounter. You can agro either one to begin the fight.

Like the banshee's and spectre's on the way to this room, Paldar will periodically knockback and port group members around in addition to "rooting" them. Recommended to rush into the cubby where the enkindled construct is at to try to mitigate how often and how far group members get tossed.
