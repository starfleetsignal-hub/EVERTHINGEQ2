## Notes

- Slaverjaw was a deadly opponent when *EverQuest II* was first released. At 16^^^ he was the baddest monster in The Caves. Now he's easily soloable with most [[Mercenaries|mercs]].
- He has a stun, does a small power drain with every hit, and has a heat (elemental) nuke.
