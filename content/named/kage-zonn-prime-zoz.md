## Strategy

The sixth named encounter in [[The Vigilant: Final Destruction]].

Named can be tanked about anywhere in the adjacent rooms, he has a few AOEs and at least one of them is a nasty root for 20 secs or so.  The named will turn black and upon doing so any new damage done to him will spawn adds that wipe the raid.

Have the tank pull the mob and seat at one end of a room, have melee DPS go in and once Kage-Zon ZOZ turns black, all melee classes move away and turn auto attack off, casters stop casting as well.  Watch for the buff, I don't recall the exact name but was easily distinguished once you found it on his buff bar.  It had a bluish icon, and "Cloak" in the name.
![Cloak of Devouring Shades](images/Cloak_spell.jpg)

Rinse and repeat the joust, simple fight if no one damages him during the "out" calls.
