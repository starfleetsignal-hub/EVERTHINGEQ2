## Strategy

- Rooted in the center of the room surrounding by pits.
- He will occasionally knockback which may cause you to fall into a pit with snakes.
- If you do get knocked down, kill the snake and either step on the yellow disc to get knocked back to the main level or click the white glow at the bottom of the ladder to climb back up.
- Will also continuously leash you back to him which can make getting out of the pits tedious if you're top of the agro list.
