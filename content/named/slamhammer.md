## Strategy

We basically burned him when the add's spawn'ed. Not too bad of a fight with the right set up.<br>
[[A magic crushing hammer|The Hammers (adds)]] will spawn in the room [[Slamhammer]] is pulled from, possibly best to pull him back into the tunnel as deep as you can, and keep the DPS closer to the room so they can switch off to the adds which will spawn ~2-3 times during the fight. He will shoot off a warning AOE on the group right before the adds spawn and rush the tank. <br>

The adds spawn at every 20% of health lost by Slamhammer.  He is immune to stuns and stifles.
