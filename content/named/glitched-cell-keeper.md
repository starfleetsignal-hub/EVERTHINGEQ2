## Note

Joust out of the red circles. "Glitched Cell Keeper gathers power from his stores to repair himself."

- The red circle is bigger each time it drops.
- Don't stand in one place too long or he will one-shot you.
- While the red circle is active/charging Glitched Cell Keeper does't take damage.
