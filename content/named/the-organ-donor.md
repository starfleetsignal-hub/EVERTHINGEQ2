## Strategy

- Spawning the [[The Organ Donor]]:
  1. Click on four different colored **musical notes** that appear in random locations, especially in the [[Rectory of Confession (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Rectory of Confession]] and around the organ in the [[Amphitheater of Rancor (SoH: Utter Contempt Solo)|Amphitheater of Rancor]].
  1. As you click each set of musical notes, you get a little message such as *Blue musical notes dissipate into the atmosphere. You hear the C key*. Remember the color of the FIRST note you clicked.
  1. Play the note that corresponds to the first musical note you clicked to pop the name. The key will be the same color and will show the note as you hover over it.

![](images/Organ_Keys.png)

- The fight:
  1. It is helpful to have the Threat List Window open (**/togglethreatlistwindow**) so you can quickly and accurately target the appropriate add.
  1. [[The Organ Donor]] has a damage shield which stops most damage she receives. You win this by killing the right adds at the right time.
  1. Pay attention to the order of the color of the notes that periodically spawn floating above the organ. From left to right, kill the dirge adds that match those notes (and only those adds, in that order, so fight in Singular Focus). This order is random for each spawn of the adds:
     - [[A dirge in A minor]]: **green**
     - [[A dirge in B minor]]: **red**
     - [[A dirge in C minor]]: **blue**
     - [[A dirge in D minor]]: **orange**
     - [[A dirge in E minor]]: **purple**
     - [[A dirge in F minor]]: **yellow**
     - [[A dirge in G minor]]: **white**
  1. Once you have killed the correct dirges, the rest disappear and you can damage the [[The Organ Donor]] until she calls for an "Encooooooooore!" heralding the next wave of adds.
