## Strategy

- Spawn Vergul by killing *only* the groups of eight [[A Scourge soldier|Scourge soldiers]] and each group's [[A Scourge battlemaster|battlemaster]] around the castle.
- You can kill the wandering [[A Scourge patroller|Scourge patrollers]] as well.
- **Note:** Killing the orcs riding boars seems to prevent Vergul's spawn and, if you do, you'll get [[Harkor Longdraw (Advanced Solo)|Harkor Longdraw]] instead.
