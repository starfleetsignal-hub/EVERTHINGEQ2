## Strategy

This is a ring event. Four waves of trolls composed of [[A Ykeshan wraithsplicer]], [[A Ykeshan wraithwarder]], and [[A Ykeshan wraithbuster]] will spawn immediately after the prior wave is killed.

### Easy mode

- Treskar has a mana drain ability called Drain Power (85 second timer) that effectively keeps your power from going above 50%.
- During the fight, Treskar will spawn an add called [[Mana soul]] repeatedly.  These adds must be burned down in approximately 20 seconds, or Treskar will absorb it and regain 20% of his health.
- He has a fairly nasty poison-based AOE.
- He also a fear effect, Talon of Terror, although it isn't a particularly long lasting one.
