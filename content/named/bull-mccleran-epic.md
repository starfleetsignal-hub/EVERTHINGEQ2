## Strategy

- Named will say one of two lines:
1. "Oi! Say that to me face!" - Raid needs to relocate behind him until an emote appears that he will is about to prepare a massive strike.
1. "You think hiding behind me will save you? Think again!" - Raid needs to stay in front of him and wait for the emote to go off.
- Recommend pulling pets off and keeping them with you based on the above positioning text.
- Bull's pathing is a bit buggy on the level he is located initially, recommend pulling him down to the vent level where [[Brutas the Imbiber]] was previously located.
