## Strategy

- Easily beat the crap out of both [[Daeron Ashenwing]] and [[Vittia Direshadow]] at level 29.  You'll need to at least 2-box (with mercs) to have a chance, and probably need a full group with at least 2 healers (merc or otherwise).
- Punja Rahab has an AOE, Strike of the Massive, that hits 4 times in rapid succession for roughly 10% of your health each hit, and also stuns.
