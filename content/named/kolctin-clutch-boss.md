## Strategy

This mob spawns adds [[An osseous serpent]] which pull aggro off the mob so you cannot target it. You either have to have really high dps and burn adds fast and target the named mob or just AOE. Named mob itself has around 12,000,000 HP. If you don't have good dps, this fight can take several minutes.

- +250 faction with **Claws of Veeshan**
