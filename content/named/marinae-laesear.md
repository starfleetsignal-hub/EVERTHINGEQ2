## Notes

- Upon completion of [[Narin Heirloom]] it is revealed that [[V'tal Narin]] is actually an agent of [[Qeynos]]. As she starts to run away, she changes from the dark elf V'tal Narin into the high elf Marinae Laesear. Unless the surrounding guards are killed, she will likely aggro one and die to it shortly after. If the guards are killed beforehand, she will run northward, jump off a cliff into the sea and despawn. Oddly enough, she will respawn as [[V'tal Narin]] back in [[The Crossroads]] shortly after she dies/despawns.
