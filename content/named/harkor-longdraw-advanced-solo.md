## Strategy

- To spawn Harkor, kill all the orcs and warboars around the keep. It's a good idea to first start the quest [[Orc Hunting]] by click the impaled skeleton at {{waypoint 5.10, -11.52, -137.59}}, since you have to kill all the orcs and boars for both.
- Harkor rides a boar, and he a 270° "frontal" doing ranged damage: Harkor himself is rooted.
- Keep all the squishies in his back quadrant and somehow avoid his red text.
