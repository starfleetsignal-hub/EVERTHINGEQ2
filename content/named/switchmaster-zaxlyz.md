## Buffs

### Offensive Aura of Agility

Effects:

- Increases STR, INT, AGI and WIS of caster by 233.1.
- Caster will Dodge 5.0% of incoming attacks.
- Increases Multi Attack of caster by 10.3.
- Increases Accuracy of caster by 14.5%.
- Increases Defense of caster by 40.0.

## Special Attacks

### Chaotic Infusion

Effect:

- Applies Elemental Implosion on termination. Lasts for 5.0 seconds.
  - Throws target back
  - Inflicts 900.0% of max health in magic damage on targets in Area of Effect instantly and every second.
  - Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 900.0% instantly and every second.
  - Cannot be modified by direct means
- Grants Sonic Vision to target
- There must be some energy field active near here that could cancel this impending disaster.

### Enthralling Flames

Effects:

- Inflicts 30,690 - 37,510 heat damage on targets in Area of Effect.
- Stuns targets in Area of Effect.
- 5% chance to dispel when target takes damage

### Unstable Stalwart Aura

- Increases STA of caster by 233.1.
- Increases Max Health of caster by 414.0%.
- When damaged this spell has a 2% chance to cast Unstable Feedback on target's attacker. Lasts for 10.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 10,678 - 24,915 magic damage on target.
  - Inflicts 7,364 - 17,183 magic damage on target every 10 seconds.
  - Decreases Crit Chance of target by 207.0.
  - Deecreases Multi Attack of target by 207.0.
  - Decreases Effective Level of target by 4.1.

### Voidfield

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 7% chance to cast Void Strike on target's attacker. Lasts for 10.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 9,328 - 13,992 magic damage on target.
  - Your spells and melee attacks are less effective than normal.
  - When beginning a spell cast this spell may cast Void Flash on target. Triggers about 1.8 times per minute.
    - Interrupts target

## Strategy

Hailing the switchmaster gives you a menu of choices to pick 3 sets from the following 4 mob combinations

- Offensive/Agility
- Unstable/Stalwart
- Voidfield
- Voidstorm

It's easiest to pick Offensive/Agility, Voidfield, and Unstable/Stalwart. It doesn't matter what order you pick them. Additionally, you may pick different choices as well varying on your capabilities.

Killing Voidstorm gives the Switchmaster the ability to do a large AE knockback with heavy damage, a stun, stifle.

Once the previous 3 encounters are dead Switchmaster will immediately spawn. This guy hits like a freight train so a tank with a decent amount of critical mitigation is a plus. Switchmaster lays down a few AEs.

Exothermic Explosion(elemental) triggers **As of an [Update](http://forums.station.sony.com/eq2/posts/list.m?topic_id=442105) on 1/28/09 Switchmaster more reliably casts this AE** every 24-30 (need exact number) seconds. It manifests on your screen as "blue vision" or "infrared".

Enthralling Flames (elemental)occurs on a relatively stable timer about every 24-30 seconds (need exact number) and is a short range AE.  It has a stun and an elemental damage component.

Every so often (roughly every 15 seconds) 2 people in the raid get "yellow vision" or "sonic vision." These 2 people become colored Yellow and their entire screen turns yellow.

![](images/Yellowvision.jpg)

Within 5-15 seconds, these 2 individuals must run to the South west portal in order to be "cured" of yellow vision.

![](images/SwitchmasterPortal.jpg)

Failing to cure yellow vision results in raid wipe. Jumping into the southwest portal also causes damage to you as you hop in; be careful. Yellow vision can affect anyone in the raid.

![](images/ElementalImplosion.jpg)

You can alter your strat for killing and positioning switchmaster however you want. Some raids never do any tank switches when their tank gets yellow vision. You can if it works for you. The basics are this:

1. Tank fights switchmaster
1. Range DPS only until first exothermic explosion "blue vision" triggers.
1. After blue vision triggers, you can send melee dps in.
1. Melee dps jousts out for next exothermic explosion in ~45seconds. You can make it 30-40seconds if it's triggering early.
1. Everyone in raid should have elemental pots to cure their own elemental detriment. At range it's doing minimal damage anyway.

Throughout fight 2 people are constantly getting yellow vision, they run to southwest portal to cure it or game over.

People get deleveled 3-4 levels by doing damage to switchmaster (arcane curable). Make sure you have someone (like mages) to cure arcane on a healer that has this OR the healers have potions to cure it. The delevel makes your cure ineffective as a priest and will not remove any detriments. If the tank gets this debuff, cure it immediately.

Voidfield Proc occurs by damaging switchmaster, this is annoying. This debuff makes combat abilities and spells do 0 or 1 damage. Either have a priest curing this or just wait until it expires before firing any spells or combat abilities to avoid wasting power on them when they have no effect.
