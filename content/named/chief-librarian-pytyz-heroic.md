## Notes

1. Kill the three Librarians:
   - [[Librarian Lin'Dyz (Heroic)|Librarian Lin'Dyz]]: click the transporter pad at {{waypoint -117.73, 9.88, 201.70}} to activate, then click again to port to the balcony. Hail the Librarian at {{waypoint -114.48, 19.86, 184.54}} to activate.
   - [[Librarian Ry'Zon (Heroic)|Librarian Ry'Zon]]: click the transporter pad at {{waypoint 165.69, 7.11, 123.10}} to activate, then click again to port to the balcony. Hail the Librarian at {{waypoint 189.38, 21.26, 109.43}} to activate.
   - [[Librarian Zu'Vod (Heroic)|Librarian Zu'Vod]]: click the transporter pad at {{waypoint -154.24, 12.43, 215.01}} to activate, then click again to port to the balcony. Hail the Librarian at {{waypoint -182.08, 25.28, 207.35}} to activate.
1. After you have killed two nameds in the zone and killed the librarians, click the transporter pad in the [[Amphitheater of Rancor (SoH: Utter Contempt Heroic)|Amphitheater of Rancor]] {{waypoint 26.46, 7.53, -47.09}} to access the balcony.
1. Click the **Podium of House K'Lorn** to spawn the Chief Librarian.
