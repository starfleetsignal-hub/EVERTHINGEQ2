## Strategy

- Clear the group of ooze as soon as you enter the chamber wing.
  - His agro range is large so he will likely agro you as you approach before the oozes directly beneath him agro.
- Have everyone stack on top of each other as close to the outside lip of the hall as your starting point.
- Every 15 seconds or so orange text will appear 'Infectious blood pools near the ground...' and a visible red mound will appear at the mutations feet.
- Joust out of the low range (10m or so) - rinse and repeat every 15 seconds. If your DPS is high enough you may not need all the space, but there is generally enough room to do at least 3 'passes' down the hall if you stagger the pools well enough.
