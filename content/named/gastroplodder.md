## Strategy

Notes:

- Preperation is everything, because you only get 1 chance each zone on this bonus named.
- To pop him you have to kill the final nameds ([[Pete Bog]] and [[Koni Fernus]]) but **not** a single snail in the whole zone.
- He spawns as soon as you killed [[Pete Bog]] and [[Koni Fernus]] and starts to make his way to the [[A vekerchiki drone|a vekerchiki drones]] and then to the exit portal.
- The fight is over when you wipe or when the named reaches the "exit" portal where you zone out.

Preperation **before** you start the fight with [[Pete Bog]] and [[Koni Fernus]].

1. kill all the [[A vekerchiki drone|a vekerchiki drones]]
1. Kill all the [[An arborian forest warden]] that are between the [[A vekerchiki drone|a vekerchiki drones]] and the nameds without pulling/killing a single snail. (Best to pull the mobs away from the trees)
1. Once you cleared all the mobs in the way you can pull [[Pete Bog]] and [[Koni Fernus]] the hill down to the lake where [[The Monstrous Mudwalker]]. Kill an "near" the big stone so you out of range of any snail.

*The clearing is not needed with high dps, but makes it easier*

The fight it self:

1. [[Gastroplodder]] starts to move as soon as he spawns, even in fight he follows his path. *(Stun doesn't work but maybe snare)*
1. Every few seconds of the fight he will call snails to him. focus on the named but try to kill the snails
