## Special Attacks

### Cloak of Negative Energy

Effects:

- When damaged this spell has a 8% chance to cast Negative Feedback on target's attacker. Lasts for 7.0 seconds.
  - Interrupts target
  - Inflicts 6,021 - 9,032 magic damage on target.
  - Inflicts 2,694 - 4,041 magic damage on target every 7 seconds.
  - Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
  - Decreases Accuracy of target by 41.4%.
  - Spells you cast have a greater than normal chance to be resisted.

### Xythian Storm

Effects:

- Inflicts 76,387 - 158,651 magic damage on target.
- Inflicts 48,477 - 100,682 magic damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If Target is closer than 10 meters
- Inflicts 41,584 - 62,376 magic damage on target.
- Inflicts 25,312 - 37,968 magic damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If Target is between 10 and 15 meters away
- Inflicts 18,080 - 27,120 magic damage on target.
- Inflicts 10,848 - 16,272 magic damage on target every 5 seconds.
  - If Target is between 15 and 9,999 meters away
- Decreases Crit Chance of target by 200.0.
