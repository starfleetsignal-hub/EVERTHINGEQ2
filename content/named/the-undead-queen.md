## Notes

[[The Undead Queen]] will cast stifles and mezzes. Her primary damage types are crushing and mental.

See also the following named mobs of the same theme that wander the same area: [[The Undead King]], [[The Undead Knight]], [[The Undead Bishop]]. All of them have been sighted at one time in the area, though that is a rare occurence. They typically spawn one at a time.
