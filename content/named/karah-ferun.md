## Strategy

This boss will become invulnerable until you use [[Energized Shard of Luclin]], which is dropped by [[A Disciple of Luclin]]. Disciple is not attackable. You will have to physically make contact with him to "kill" him. Use the portal panels to teleport to the corresponding platform to "catch" him. ( can be oneshot too, although it seems like only when theres at least 2 ppl in a group)
