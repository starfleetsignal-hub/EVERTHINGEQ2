## Strategy

Easily solo/molo-able
Surrounded by tuatara hatchlings, and spawns adds at various health %.

## Notes

1 hour respawn from time of kill. The special attack "Thrashing" not only crushes her opponents (you) but also her spawned hatchlings. The crushing drains power from you and the hatchlings and transfers it into her power pool while doing physical damage.
