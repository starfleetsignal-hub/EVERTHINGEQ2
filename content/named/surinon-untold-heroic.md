Ability and strategy information changes for stages (1-20/21+).

## Abilities

- **Sufferance**: Body
- An incurable detriment that wounds the caster's health by 10% per increment. Increments are gained by clicking the western altar on the main floor. Across the group, a combined (6/12) increments are required to see Surinon. This spell persists through death and can be canceled.
- **Overlord's Tithe**
- An incurable noxious that deals poison damage and drains power over time. It also procs Defensive Breach, which deals large amounts of magic damage.
- **Tenets of Torment**
- An incurable elemental that has a 25% chance to cast Pleasure from Pain when damaged. Completing a heroic opportunity cures both Tenets of Torment and Pleasure from Pain. Tenets of Torment then transfers to the closest ally.
- **Pleasure from Pain**
- An incurable trauma that increases all damage by 1%.
- **Pain Management**
- A buff that increases combat mitigation, ability doublecast avoidance, flurry avoidance, flurry, and fervor for each increment. At 10 increments, the entire party is killed. Increments are removed by snuffing out torches in the three alcoves. Torches are lit (1/2) at a time.
- **Barrage**: Unfathomable Foray
- A powerful attack that kills the entire party, unless blocked with [[Bulwark of Order]].
