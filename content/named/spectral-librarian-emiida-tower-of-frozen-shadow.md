## Strategy

1. Clear the zone of everything that aggros when you go near it. It will join the fight later if you don't.
1. [[Zorglim the Departed]] is up on the mezzanine in one of the corners. Killing him makes the Spectral Librarian attackable. The [[Shadowed Tome of Ages Past]] spawns with her.
1. The trick to the Spectral Librarian & Tome fight seems to be to make sure to keep their damage within a very narrow band of each other. This may not matter so much when they're green/yellow, but once they're below some (yet to be determined) threshold it is vital.
