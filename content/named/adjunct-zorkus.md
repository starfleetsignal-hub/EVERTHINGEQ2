## Notes

As players progress through the zone, Adjunct Zorkus is there to give dialogue at various points within. When you reach the end of the zone in [[Executor Vark's Chambers]] he starts more dialogue with [[Executor Vark]] and is killed by him immediately before you engage in the final fight with [[Executor Vark]].
