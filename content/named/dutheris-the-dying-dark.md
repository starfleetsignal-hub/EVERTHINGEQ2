## Strategy

Every once in a while he will curse everyone. In order to break the curse you have to kill all 4 adds.. OR cure it.
He also has a Mana Drain from the person furthest away from combat, so make sure that isn't the healer. Also he has a rough AE that kills everyone, best to sun/stifle or mana drain to prevent him from casting it.

## Factions

- Good faction with **The Forsaken City**
- Bad faction with **The Nizari**
