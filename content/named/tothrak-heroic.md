## Strategy

Carefully body pull the [[An immolated weaponsmith (Heroic)|immolated weaponsmiths]] and [[An immolated armorsmith (Heroic)|immolated armorsmiths]]. As you kill these, [[Tothrak (Heroic)|Tothrak's]] armor and weapons weaken and finally shatter, and then you kill the name.
