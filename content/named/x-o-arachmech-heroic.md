## Strategy

X-O Arachmech has a knockback which can be defeated by dragging him into the water; fight him there.

He will spawn adds.  These adds need to be killed and looted for their electro-charged batteries.

The electro-charged battery is used to shock X-O Arachmech, stunning him for several moments allowing you to damage him in safety during that time period.  To use the electro-charged battery you will get a hand icon over the water.  Click the water (when the hand icon appears there) to shock X-O Arachmech.
