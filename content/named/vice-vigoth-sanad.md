## Strategy

Tank and spank.

- Note: Randomly ports members of the group back a bit.

It is easier to pull him down below so when he knocks everyone back nobody falls off the ledge.

- 75% he'll port everyone away inflicting damage and an arcane slow debuff.
- Move everyone down below where you ported in at. He'll eventually port himself to that location.
- Spin him and kill him there.  During this portion of the fight he does extra bad things if nobody is in melee range with him so if the tank gets ported keep a melee dps in there close.  Also has a mana drain.
