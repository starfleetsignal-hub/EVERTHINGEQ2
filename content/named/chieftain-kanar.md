## Strategy

Clear his room, once it is cleared setup a little away from his tower. When everyone is ready run up the tower and use walk and crouch (shift+r  and the 'Z' key) Doing this will evade further problems once you are feared.

The Gassy Gas clouds on the bottom will not affect you unless on the ground, every 4 seconds they deal focus damage.

Chieftan will also have AoE melee attack so healers should groupward/reactive/HoT as needed.

This is one of the hardest encounters in the zone.
