Ability and strategy information changes with stages (1-20/21-40/41+).

## Abilities

- **Mouth of Needles**
- A buff that begins with 10 increments. Increments are added as The Orozorgon loses health. It has a 4% chance (multiplied by increments) to cast Puncturing Fangs, which increases all damage done to the target by 1%. Increments can be removed through stuns (1-20) or by a stun-dispell combo (21+).
- **Constricting Coils**
- An incurable trauma that roots the target and kills the target on expiration. It can be cured if The Orozorgon is pulled more than 10 meters away from the player.
- **Acidic Venom**
- A curable curse that kills the target on expiration. It also kills the target if they also have Constricting Coils.
- **Uncoiled Frenzy [21+]**
- A buff that lands around (NA/20%/25%). It increases flurry, flurry multiplier, melee multiplier, and strikethrough every second.
- **Scaly Protection [41+]**
- A buff that makes Orozorgon immune to stuns. This buff can be temporarily dispelled by casting a heroic opportunity.
