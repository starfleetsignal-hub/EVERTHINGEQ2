## Strategy

Clear centaurs around this mob or they will come help her. Comes with three [[A Hedon bloomseeker]] which have a total of 2,100,000 HP. She has a very mean focus spell that does a lot of damage. She has AOE and short stiffles as well. Buff up and put some wards and burn down her adds and then just ward/heal and burn Sariah down.
