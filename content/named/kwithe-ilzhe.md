## Strategy

Mob has been changed to Solo^ and is quite doable. However, with 60% elemental mitigation and a 500 points ward, i still got hit for 3.3k damage by his Ice Comet. Additionially, take a look around and watch out for the Sha'irs, despite the fact that Kwi'the Il'zhe will start the fight, they will come after you if nearby.
