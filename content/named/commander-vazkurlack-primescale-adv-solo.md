## Strategy

He does not hit very hard.<br>
However, every so often you will get a message saying "Commander Vazkurlack Primescale places a trap near you..." and then a floating mine, which will move around the room, will appear - these explode for around 25k damage if you get too close to them. So the main concern is to get him down while avoiding the mines.
