## Spawn

- There are conflicting reports regarding the spawn. Some reports indicate that he will spawn every 36 Earth minutes (half a [[Time|Norrathian day]]), others once a full Norrathian day (72 Earth minutes). However, other people have reported to have waited more than 72 minutes and still nothing spawned. I have waited more than 2 hours (Earth time) and it didn´t appear... (28th of September 2025).
- If you are trying to complete the related quest, [[Capturing Day and Night]], you can farm [[A Silver Dragon scale]] from [[Keeper of Silence]] in [[The Poets Palace]]. This will offer a quest that will allow you to spawn [[Siyamak (Maj'Dul)|Siyamak]] in [[Maj'Dul]]. The scale is a rare drop, however it allows you a guaranteed spawn of Siyamak.
