## Strategy

To make this mob active, you must kill the three named bixies, [[Trixie]], [[Mixie]], and [[Dixie]] at the same time. If you do not kill them within 10 seconds of each other, they will respawn. Pull all three at once, bring each down to 10% and then AoE. As soon as they die, the collector goes active and immediately aggoes with a few adds. She has a fairly large kickback so fight with your back to the walls. You can also pull them from left to right one by one when you burn one down enough, grab the next one. This helps if you're low on heals.
