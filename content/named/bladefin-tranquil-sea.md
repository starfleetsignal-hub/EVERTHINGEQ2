## NOTES

Drops one of several 95 Legendary Attuneable weapons, or 96 Heirloom Waist or Cloak ([[The Mangy Pillager]]), or 98 [[Phantom Armor (Altar of Malice)|Yellow Malice]] Boots, Shoulders or Forearms.

- Ornate chests will drop 1 of the armor/weapons above and have chances for normal ornate chest loot as well (Adept books, etc)
- Exquisite chests (in addition to above armor/weapons) will additionally drop x1 [[Yellow Malice Gemstone (Heirloom)]] and occasionally x1 [[Blue Malice Gemstone (Heirloom)]] as well.
