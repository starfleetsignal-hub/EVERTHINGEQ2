## Strategy

- after taking some damage, his buddy [[Putretor the Rotting]] will come to help him out.
- Tank needs to grab him, but group keeps DPS on the main boss always - except when a group-member gets trapped inside a 'vile prison' which you should take down fast (the trapped one can dps it also!) otherwise it will cause mass-fear!
- get out of yellow circles on the ground as usual
