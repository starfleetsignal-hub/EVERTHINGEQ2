## Notes

This dragon was added for the [[15th Anniversary Event]].

- Battle with this dragon will only be available from 11/07/2019 2:00am PST through 12/06/2019 1:59am PST.

## Travel

Before the dragons for this event appear, a zone announcement appears in chat for those near the spire. It begins with a ten minute warning and counts down.

- On most servers at least one players stands at the location and monitor the dragon's appearance, making an announcement in General chat to call other players to the location.
- Fayren Icefang is found near the [[Everfrost]] Ulteran Spire portal {{waypoint 461.18, -34.13, -1411.19}}.

## Rewards for Defeat

Players who participate in defeating this dragon will receive:

- +1500 [[Faction|faction]] with the [[Quellithulian Portal Scholars]].
- 1200 status
- This dragon is one of four that players must defeat in order to obtain the [[Reveal Inner Dragon]] illusion and mount.
