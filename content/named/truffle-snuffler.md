![The Truffle Snuffler as he appears after eating psychedelic mushrooms.](images/Trufflesnufflerstoned.jpg)

After consuming the mushrooms upon which he feeds while in the Emerald Halls Hollows, the Truffle Snuffler appears in a shimmer of psychedelic rainbow colors. This appears to be merely (though hilarious) a graphic effect; no discernible buff ensues upon examination of the creature's maintained spells.
