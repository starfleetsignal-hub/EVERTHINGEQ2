## Spawning

A Murkwater Infiltrator is a very rare shared spawn linked up with the netherot chanters and Darkblade scouts/brigands/furies/maidens/nobles.  It may spawn in the netherot chanter room at {{waypoint -76, 0, 106}}, or in the Marius Darkblade room at {{waypoint -128, 1, 148}}
