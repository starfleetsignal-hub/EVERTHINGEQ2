## Strategy

Solo :
a very easy kill, root Ubrak and make sure he stays rooted. Pull back and kill the mobs that spawn with him, very easy but watch your aoe. Then just nuke him down slowly, no dots! Easy loot.

Group :  Any class can tank the adds easily. So for a faster kill tank and healer hold ubrak, nuke the adds first. And then he's yours.
