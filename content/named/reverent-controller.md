## Strategy

- There are three of these in a group - the first one killed is worth AA.  They must be pulled outside the arena or [[Gladion]] will attack along with them.
- They drop Vampire L&L bits, but aren't flagged as vampires, so aren't vulnerable to mastery attacks.  They're not flagged as any other particular monster type either. They're flagged as "undead" and "cadaveric".
