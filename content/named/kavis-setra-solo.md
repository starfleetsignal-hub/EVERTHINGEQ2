## Strategy

Jump down and start the fight; soon Kavis will use and destroy the teleporter. The column is now black,  you can enter it from north, west or east to reach Kavis' shadows. Use your Heroic Opportunity to make the shadows attackable. After killing the 3 shadows Kavis himself appears down in the central room, now you can burn him down.
