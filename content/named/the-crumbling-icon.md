## Strategy

Divide and Conquer

Must fight him in the lava or else he will stoneskin and become immune to all damage.

As the fight progresses the raid members in close proximity to named will accrue an 'overheat' debuff that incrementally counts up. If it reaches 10 on any member of the raid, it will detonate and wipe the encounter.

Best strategy is to have a main tank and an off tank, splitting the raid into two groups on opposite ends of the lava pool. Have the main tank pull agro initially and burn down the named until around 5 ticks. The offtank should then run in and work on pulling agro on the named, and pulling it across the lava to the secondary group. Continue this process until around 30% when the Allu'thoa worshipper adds arrive.

Focus burn on the worshippers, otherwise they will be sacrificed and heal the named.

At this point the raid should be able to start burning down the named the rest of the way.
