## Strategy

1. Use Dispel Magic or similar to prevent challenge mode
1. Oligar will at times "face" a groupmember (red text). This person must joust away, group must stay out of line-of-sight between the two (he will turn towards the person).
1. at ~70% 3 statues will pop. Kill the right one first (memwipe until down!), then the center statue (immunity on Oligar).
1. dps Oligar until defeated (he or you).
