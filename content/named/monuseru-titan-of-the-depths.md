## Abilities

- **Surging Swells**
- Deals increasing elemental damage to health and power over time.
- **Coastal Tides**
- A curable elemental that deals fast ticking increasing elemental damage and increases physical and noxious damage from all sources to targets within 25 meters.
- **Spellweaved Admonishment**
- A curable elemental that only lands on mages and .
- **Wounded Pride**
- An incurable curse that reduces the target's maximum health by 25%. This effect lands on all players within 15 meters. If a player is hit with this spell a second time, before the first has expired, then it advances to Utterly Wounded Pride, which reduces the target's maximum health by 50%. The effect does not stack a third time.
- **Subterfuge**
- An incurable curse that clears the player's target and disorients them [unable to move or cast].
- **Barrage**
- An unnamed spell that can only be countered with [[Bulwark of Order]].

## Strategy

Monuseru continuously summons animated droplets in groups of six. Any droplets remaining when the next group is summoned instantly disappear.

Monuseru continuously summons whirlpools that appear on a random player. Any player that remains inside the whirlpool is killed.
