## Strategy

CLEAR THE ROOM BEFORE PULLING THIS BOSS. He will agro all living mobs on pull

This boss usually cast a cloud of poison that can cumulate with itself, and drain mana and life. When he cast the cloud, move him, otherwise, you could have easily +50 poisons on you.
