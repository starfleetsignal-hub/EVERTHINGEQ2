## Strategy

- At the start of the quest [[High Tide]], speak to [[Osh (Cobalt Scar)]] and receive a blessing from Prexus.  This blessing increases your stats to the point that Salton can be soloed. *If you die for any reason, you have to speak to Osh again to receive another blessing!*
- During the fight, Salton will emote that he "feasts on a nearby corpse".  The corpse will refill his health, and he'll emote that the corpse begins to glow. When this happens, back off a long way before a large AE damage effect (121000)
- Although he refills his health each time he feasts, his total health is decreased each time, making it easier to kill him before the next feast.
- Joust him and pull him deeper into the tunnels where he has nothing to feast on.
- He may be bugged, during one fight he continued to chase during the 'casting' phase of his feast making it impossible to reach a safe distance.  On the next encounter I did not attempt to snare him and it worked correctly... so 'snare' might be the problem.
