## Notes

- To access this instance, progress through the quests [[Symbols of the Damned]] and [[A Far Greater Menace]] for the *Hand of Marr* or the quests [[Mysterious Artifacts]] and [[Betrayal of Brotherhood]] for the *Brethren of Night*. These are the third and fourth quests in each series. If you are in a group, only one person in the group will need access to the dungeon.
- Stealth and invisibility can be used to avoid most of the encounters in the instance. This can be useful if your goal is to defeat him to update the [[Hunting Dangerous Prey (Shattered Lands)|Hunting Dangerous Prey]] daily quest.
