## Notes

![Dryxl T'(R)ath in wraith-form](images/Dryxl_T'rath2.png)

- After death, he will transform into an NPC spirit that floats toward the entrance to [[The Crypt of T'haen]].
- During the [[Bloodline Chronicles Timeline]] Dryxl T'Rath was revealed to be a former member of the *Brethren of Night* and is actually [[T'Rath the Usurper]], the one responsible for imprisoning the master vampire [[T'Haen the Lost]] and causing the events told in the Bloodline Chronicles.
