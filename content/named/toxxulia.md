## Special Attacks

### Ancient Winds

Effects:

- Inflicts 76,880 - 179,386 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 59,795 - 111,049 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 59,795 - 111,049 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 45,558 - 68,338 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 45,558 - 68,338 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 30,752 - 37,586 magic damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Throws caster back
- Decreases INT of targets in Area of Effect by 257.4.
- Decreases STR of targets in Area of Effect by 257.4.
- If this spell expires naturally, and you have either Fetid Torrent or Piercing Pestilence on you, you will be hit with Judgement of Venom (a powerful damage spell).

### Caustic Scales

Effects:

- Opponents have a 11% chance every 15 secs to be struck with corrosive feedback, an incurable 8 second debuff that does damage, damages your gear, and debuffs your casting time and critical hit percentage.

### Fetid Torrent

Effects:

- Inflicts 76,880 - 179,386 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 59,795 - 111,049 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 9 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 59,795 - 111,049 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 45,558 - 68,338 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 14 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 45,558 - 68,338 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a forward arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Inflicts 30,752 - 37,586 poison damage on targets in Area of Effect.
  - Any distance up to 200 away and a rear arc of 180 degrees
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
- Decreases power of targets in Area of Effect by 10.0%.
- Blurs vision of targets in Area of Effect.
- If this spell expires naturally, and you have either Ancient Winds or Piercing Pestilence on you, you will be hit with Judgement of Venom (a powerful damage spell).
