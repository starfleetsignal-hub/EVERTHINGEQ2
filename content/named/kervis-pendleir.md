## Strategy

[[Kervis Pendleir]] is linked to all the other mobs in the room including 2 more named mobs, [[Coraste Verisue]] and [[Talwyn Verisue]] but they only have 4,000,000 HP each. The fight is ye old tank and spank. May want to send your tank in first and have them drop a few AOEs and then good to go.
