## Strategy

- Toxic Infusion is a long-lasting (4 1/2 minutes real time) AOE noxious DoT. The DoT hits half as hard if a player is more than 14 meters (in-game) from Witchdoctor Hazar.  If you're soloing, hanging back for less damage isn't an option.  Players who can't cure should stock a few noxious cure potions. All this aside, with statflation being as it is players should be able to take him down easily, especially if they're also using a merc.
