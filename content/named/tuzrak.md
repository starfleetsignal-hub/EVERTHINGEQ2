## Strategy

When you first see Tuz'Rak he is a non-attackable mob at the bottom of stairs. In order for him to become attackable you'll need to kill Bloodpriest Yak'Lar and then the two mobs that spawn in the blood pools next to the altar (these pop after Bloodpriest Yak'Lar dies and attack right away). Once the two mobs in the blood pools are taken care of, Tuz'Rak becomes attackable and runs to the top of the stairs.

A good strategy would be to have a monk group feign death or have a scout evac after killing the Bloodpriest. This would allow you to kill the mob in the blood pool on one side, go around the altar and clear the guards off the stairs. Then return to the bottom of the stairs and kill the mob in the second blood pool. This way when Tuz'Rak runs to the top of the stairs, the guards have already been cleared.

Pull Tuz'Rak to the bottom of the stairs. During the fight he will randomly teleport one of your group members to the altar and chain them to it. Have the DPS in the group switch to the chains that are holding the person on the altar.
