## Notes

- This named is required for the [[Beastlord Epic Weapon Timeline]]
- He has a level 98^ warder pet
- This is a test of Endurance not DPS!

## Hints

- The quest strips you of your necklace, belt and cloak from the preceding quests. Be sure to have proper replacements.
- Be in spiritual stance and kill Viax first (pet cannot die while Viax is alive). Or be in Feral and use kite strat.
- He has 100% crit Avoidance, so you will need to have 200% crit chance to have full crits.
- Having the [[Scales of Justice]] blessing from [[The Tribunal]] seems to help. Same applies to the [[Final Judgement]] miracle.
- There is a [[Power root]] from the [[Carnivorous Plant/Care and Feeding|Carnivorous Plant]]
- People report having a grandmaster level 6 defensive warder helps.
- Once you are on this step you can zone out and come back with no penalty if you need to regear / respec your toon.

## Suggested Strats

1. Use bear warder, put points into his potency/heal/and mitigation, get bear's extra healing ability and healing primal. (did only this in spiritual, didnt use god spells and had did not have GM bovid)(Did have GM bear though I think)

## Alternative Strat

Easy kite strategy. (Viax hits hard!)

1. Pull [[Viax]]'s pet with your pet. (I used a grandmaster Feline pet and I was in Feral stance.)
1. As soon as you send your pet in, pull [[Viax]] onto yourself. Your Warder will tank his Warder and they will do hardly any damage to each other.
1. Immediately use your snare (Vine Net IX) to keep [[Viax]] snared and kite him in a circle using ranged attacks. This takes time, do not rush it. I recommend getting 1 or 2 Mending Adornments if you want to stay in Feral Stance. Use your stun if snare breaks early. Use your pet heal (I only had 1 point into it) to heal your warder if you notice it getting low on health.
1. After [[Viax]] dies go finish off his warder.
