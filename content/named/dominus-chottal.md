## Lore

Dominus Chottal was the last of the Iksar Emperors. He was slain in [[Sebilis]] when it was assaulted by the forces of dragonkind.

## Strategy

Stage your Raid Force/Group at the top of the tower where Dominus Chottal spawns.  Main Tank will pull the Dominus where he stands and keep him there during the fight.  During the fight Dominus Chottal will call out a party members name and it will appear in red letters at the top of the screen.  This party member must jump off the tower down below or cause the raid /group to wipe.  If the Main tank gets called out have an off tank or main assist grab Dominus Chottal until the Main tank can climb back up top.  Key to this fight is watching for the call outs and jumping off the tower before you AOE the raid/group.
