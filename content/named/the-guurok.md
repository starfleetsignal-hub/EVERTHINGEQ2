## Spawn

![A pirate skull](images/A_pirate_skull.png)
The Guurok is the toughest mob in the [[Queen's Colony]]. He can be found as an NPC sitting at his spawn location. You will need to collect six [[A pirate skull|pirate skulls]] that are dropped by the skeletons on the island ([[A fallen pirate]], [[A fallen brigand]], [[A fallen assassin]]) and drop them on the big rock on the middle of the stream, beside The Guurok. An in-game message will appear in your chat window if you have skulls in your inventory: "The Guurok lets out a hiss, almost as if it smells you. As you place the skulls it will gradually get larger and eventually become attackable.
