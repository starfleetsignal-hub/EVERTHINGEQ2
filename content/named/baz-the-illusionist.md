## Notes

- Final encounter to the zone once all other named are dead (Except for [[Kildiun the Drunkard]]).
- When the text **Nibbles makes noises that seem to come from the upper storage room...** that is the sign that the event can be triggered.
- To begin the event, have a member of the raid speak to Nibbles through his few lines of text prompts.

## Strategy

Scout Tank, Positional

- This can be a very tricky fight depending on how well your elected scout tank(s) can hold agro.
  - If Baz is allowed to target fighters he pulses an incredibly high damage effect that will likely cause a wipe.
- The encounter itself is a combination of the previous three fights in their spirit forms ([[Pirate Shaman Snaggletooth]], [[Danacio the Witchdoctor]], and [[Brunhildre the Wench]]), complete with all their abilities.
- Recommended kill order is the reverse of how they were killed in their original encounters, so Brunhildre first due to the curse, Danacio due to the summoned skulls, then Snaggletooth. Burn Baz last.
