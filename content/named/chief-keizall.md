## Strategy

- To spawn him, clear all the Coldwind zombies from the general area, and eventually a level 17 zombie (they're otherwise level 15-16) will spawn.  Kill it, and Chief Keizall may spawn.  As of May 2017 he is *still* heroic, so unless you're overleveled you'll probably need a merc.
