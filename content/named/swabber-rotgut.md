## Strategy

- During the course of the fight adds called 'a Brokenskull lackey' will spawn and shortly after 'A Ritual Shrine'. Burn down the lackey's as they spawn and the shrines can then be damaged. Return to named after each lackey and shrine are destroyed. Rinse and Repeat.
