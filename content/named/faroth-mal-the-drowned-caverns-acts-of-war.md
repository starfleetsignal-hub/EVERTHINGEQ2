## Notes

- Faroth Mal is the leader of the Underpaw Gnolls.* - (Source: [[The Fall of Faroth Mal]])*

## Solo Strategy

- Faroth Mal will cast an elemental stun effect, which will cause the encounter to reset if there is nothing else in your party with aggro. However, this ability can be ranged. The moment you see him beginning to cast it, run in the opposite direction. By the time he is finished casting, if you started running at or soon after he began casting, you will out-range the stun.
- Adds will continue to spawn during the encounter, with one of them casting a root ability that if not dispelled, will cause Faroth's Mal stun ability to hit you, resetting the encounter.
  - (04/10/2023): After dying during the first attempt and zoning back in, Faroth Mal had respawned in a side alcove and no adds spawned during the second attempt, making the battle much easier since the only mechanic to watch out for was his casting stun.
