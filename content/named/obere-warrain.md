## Notes

Obere is a quest-only spawn; when you arrive at the camp, so does he. He has two [[A bodyguard (Nektulos Forest)|bodyguards]] with him in a group, level 25vvv. Most players should be able to solo him by the time he cons green. If you break aggro and run, Obere and his bodyguards will wait in the camp rather than despawning.
