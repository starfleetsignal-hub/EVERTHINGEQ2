## Strategy

- Spawned by killing [[An apprentice of pain|apprentices of pain]] in the zone.
- The [[Lord of Pain]] only takes damage while you're standing in the Ring of Pain.  As soon as you step into the Ring of Pain, you're inflicted with **Exacting Punishment**.  It begins with 20 increments and counts down as long as you stay within the ring.  If it reaches 0, you die.  Once you leave the ring to remove **Exacting Punishment**, you receive **Lasting Pain**.  If you re-enter the Ring of Pain before **Lasting Pain** expires, you die.  While outside the ring, you'll build increments of **Brink of Death** which can be cleared by entering and leaving the Ring of Pain.  By staying outside the ring only as long as needed for **Lasting Pain** to expire, you can keep the increments of **Brink of Death** low.
