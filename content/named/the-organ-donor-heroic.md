## Strategy

- To spawn, pick up the [[Harmonic Conch]] from inside the fountain {{waypoint -12, 14, -15}}, then use the Conch to get the ability to see music notes for 30 seconds. You'll need to find and click four different colored music notes, noting the colors and the order in which you clicked them. Next, go to the organ and click four colored keys in the same order as the musical notes you found.
