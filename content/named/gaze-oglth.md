The Ogles, Three are a Tier 1 raid boss. They gain an additive advantage for each player below 12 in the raid and beyond a soft time limit of 60 minutes.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance 250.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance 700.0
- Resolve 13,230.0

## Abilities

- **Overbearing Influence I**
- An incurable trauma that causes power usage to also cost health based on the amount of power used.
- **Void Cloaked**
- A buff that gives a chance of triggering Negative Static on the attacker.
- **Negative Static**
- A curable arcane that interrupts the target, inflicts magic damage over time, decreases Fervor, and decreases Accuracy.
- **Surging Heat**
- An incurable elemental that deals increasing arcane damage to health and power over time.
- **Ultimate Awareness**
- A buff that increases outgoing damage to its main target for each increment. Increments are added when players are struck by Shockwave Glance. Gaze, Glare, and Gawk share an increment counter; all three become stronger when a player is struck.
- **Burning Gaze**
- An incurable elemental that increases slashing damage to all targets within 50 meters. If a target has all three of Burning Gaze, Piercing Glare, and Crushing Gawk, the curse Oglth Suppresion is applied to them.
- **Reverberating Boom**
- A curable trauma that deals slashing damage over time and knocks back all targets within 15 meters. Damage from this effect has 100% bleedthrough.
- **Barrage**
- An incurable curse that can only be blocked by [[Bulwark of Order]]. Gaze, Glare, and Gawk have separate Barrage abilities.
- **Eye Lash**
- A curable elemental that deals multiple strikes of slashing damage and fast ticking increasing heat damage. The heat damage has an increased bleedthrough.

## Strategy

If any of the Ogles, Three are not targeting a fighter, that Oglth shuffles targets. If any two Ogles are targeting the same player, that player is instantly killed. If any two Ogles are in close proximity to each other, both heal slightly. If all three are in close proximity, all three heal slightly.
