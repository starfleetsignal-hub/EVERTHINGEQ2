## Strategy

good luck soloing this guy if you're not a tank. I managed with a Ranger plus Templar-merc. Kill all the adds asap, and hope the merc heals you and doesn't pick flowers instead.

I Soloed him on a Defiler with no merc, just took 16 mins so I had to deal with the light blue mobs respawning. It's doable, just takes awhile. (Valencinox, Freeport Server)
