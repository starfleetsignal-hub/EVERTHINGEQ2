## Notes

- The Broodmatron is the final mob of a ring event.  To start the event, clear all the [[An azure drakeling|azure drakelings]].  When most of those are down, several [[A clutch guardian drake|clutch guardian drakes]] will spawn.  Clear all of those and The Broodmatron spawns.
- The Broodmatron [now grants AA](https://forums.daybreakgames.com/eq2/index.php?threads/update-notes-thursday-january-7-2016.567113/).
