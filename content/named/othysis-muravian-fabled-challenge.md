## Notes

Othysis Muravian's auto attack does crushing and disease damage.  Every 34 seconds will emote, "Through the portal now", and summon a [[D'Morte preserver]], a heroic mob which will heal Othysis Muravian for about 25% if it gets close enough.  Every 30 seconds, she emotes, "Time for some soul searching", and does a Disease DD (Prone to Corruption) to a random person in the raid which will spawn an add (Epic x2) if they DON'T die to it.
