## Notes

- Old Ok'Mar is a [[Defiler]] and will cast appropriate spells.
- In solo version of the instance:
  - Rare (roughly 1 in 4 chance per visit)
  - Has no arrows, but comes with 1 friend who is down-arrow.
- In heroic version of the instance:
  - Common (roughly 2 in 3 chance per visit)
  - Is ^^^ heroic, and comes with 2 friends who have no arrows.
