## Strategy

- Save your big hitting abilities initially when fighting the heroic [[Gangel the Undying]] - upon death a mimic 'Exquisite' chest appears. Kill any adds before clicking it.
- Gangel will respawn as epic x2. Kill the add when it's up and watch the cast bar for 'Necromantic Sphere'. STOP ATTACKING. It lasts about 8 or so seconds and has a purple slime graphic below him and a 'phantom' at his chest level.
- When it fades resume attacks and make sure to re-cast debuff abilities as he dispels every time he casts the sphere.
