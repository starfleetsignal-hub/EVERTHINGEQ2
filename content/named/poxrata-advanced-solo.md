1. [[Poxrata]] is grouped with her friends [[Brudu (Advanced Solo)|Brudu]], [[Grudu (Advanced Solo)|Grudu]], and [[Krudu (Advanced Solo)|Krudu]] in the [[The Tide Wall|Tide Wall]] room {{waypoint -38, -10, -148}}.
   - Make sure you have a water breathing spell or charm before starting this fight. The [[Fishbone Earring]] from the Heritage Quest [[Hadden's Earring]] works perfectly, although some other water breathing spells such as *Watery Respite* may not.
   - You may need to focus on [[Brudu (Advanced Solo)|Brudu]], [[Grudu (Advanced Solo)|Grudu]], and [[Krudu (Advanced Solo)|Krudu]], ignoring the red that Poxrata emits. Poxrata will be mostly dead by the time you have both companions taken out. Alternatively, if you have plenty of DPS, you can simply burn Poxrata, and her friends disappear when she dies.
