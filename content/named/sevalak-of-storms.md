## Special Attacks

### Burden of the Awakened

Effects:

- Burden of the Awakened debuffs critical chance by an additional 15%

### Power Discharge

Effects:

- Decreases power of target by 100.0% instantly and every 2 seconds.
  - Damage from this effect is harder than normal to mitigate.
  - This effect cannot be critically applied.
- Cannot be modified except by direct means

### Stormshield

- Opponents have a 7% chance every 15 secs to be struck with Shocking Jolt, a spell that does damage, drains mana and knocksback the target and those near.

### Thunderous Echo

- If target successfully avoids any melee attack, target takes damage from Thunderclap.
