Illicus, Zon Zobboz Swarmer is a Tier 1 raid boss. He gains an additive advantage for each player below 12 in the raid and beyond a soft time limit of 60 minutes.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance 250.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance 700.0
- Resolve 13,230.0

## Abilities

- **Conscription of Blight**
- A buff that gives a chance of triggering Ocular Blight on an attacker which interrupts and deals disease damage and debuffs offensive ability.
- **Swarm Factor**
- A buff that decreases damage taken from all sources for each active [[A Summoned Swarmguard|Summoned Swarmguard]]. For each additional Swarmguard more than 2, it heals Illicus slightly.
- **Teamwork**: Leader
- A buff that applies Tag Team Strike if the target is also the target of another mob with the buff Teamwork: Member.
- **Illicus' Threat!**
- A buff that increases outgoing damage and decreases incoming damage. If Illicus loses 5% of his max health while this is active, he gains a deathtouch ability.
- **Surging Stares**
- An incurable arcane that deals increasing arcane and crushing damage to health and power over time.
- **Overbearing Influence I**
- An incurable trauma that makes power usage also cost health.
- **Elemental Burst I**
- A curable elemental that
- **Elemental Storm**
- A curable elemental that deals fast ticking increasing elemental damage and increases physical damage from all sources.
- **Elemental Aftershock**
- A curable elemental that kills the target, if Elemental Storm is still applied.
- **Brain Burst I**
- A curable arcane that
- **Illicus' Affliction**
- A curable trauma that deals crushing damage and significantly increases arcane damage from all sources.
- **Swarming Fear**
- An incurable noxious that makes the target afraid and prevents casting beneficial spells. This can be blocked by area effect blockers.
- **Crowded Animosity**
- A curse that deals increasing physical damage for each other player within 8 meters. It cannot be cured while any other player is within 8 meters. If it expires or the target dies, a [[Manifested Protector]] joins the fight. Illicus does not take any damage while a Protector is active. A second failure additionally kills the target. A third failure additionally kills the target's group. A fourth failure additionally kills the entire raid. The Protector has the Teamwork: Member buff.
- **Barrage**
- An incurable curse that can only be blocked with [[Bulwark of Order]].

## Strategy

Illicus summons massive amounts of void energy through his "eyes": [[Illicus' Conjuring Eye]], [[Illicus' Commanding Eye]], and [[Illicus' Focused Eye]]. These three are all identical, except for their name. Each Eye begins a countdown which can be paused by mesmerizing it. If the countdown reaches zero, the entire raid is killed. If mesmerized long enough, the Eye despawns safely and is replaced with another Eye.

Illicus casts the buff Illicus' Threat! at 80%, 60%, 40%, and 20%.
