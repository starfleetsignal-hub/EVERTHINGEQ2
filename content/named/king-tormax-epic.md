## Strategy

- Tormax comes with a buff that gives him 100% crit bonus and 150% crit avoidance. To dispel this buff, a scout must complete the Tower of Frozen Shadow x2 zone and receive the attack "Demoralizing Swipe".  Having at least 4 scouts with the debuff is necessary. **IT IS NOT LONGER NECESSARY (25TH OF MAY 2026)**
- Tormax will spawn two [[A daiku warmonger]] periodically throughout the fight.  These must be burned down.
- Tormax will emote in red and cast an energy field on the battlefield.  The raid must get far away from this energy field within a set amount of time.  The field will detonate after 10 seconds, hitting anyone still in range with an uncurable viral curse.  If you get the curse, stay away from the raid and let it kill you.

Fabled Version

- After the first wave of diaku warmongers spawn when both are dead start the joust as the energy field will pop right after.
- Once the dragon spawns kill the orb the "planar anchor"? killing this spawns a mini dragon use class debuff on one kill it then other dragon debuff kill it then tormax can be killed. Old versions of the debuff work not the fabled.

## Notes

- The named in each of the 4 rooms inside [[Throne of Storms: Hall of Legends]] must be killed before King Tormax will become active.
