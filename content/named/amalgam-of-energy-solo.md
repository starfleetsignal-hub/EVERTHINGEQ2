## Strategy

   - Clear [[An ethereal automaton|ethereal automatons]] and [[An ethereal spark|ethereal sparks]] to reach the Amalgam.
   - The Amalgam can be kited using ranged attacks. At 80%, 50%, and 25%, he emotes "The Amalgam of Energy's core begins to overload! You can feel energy being ripped from the very air around you!" before casting one or more [[Energy Globule|Energy Globules]]. The Amalgam takes no damage while these are up, so switch to them and kill them.
