## Notes

- A Bloodthirsty Leopard has a very rare chance to spawn in place of any snow leopard.  However, the [[A Numbfoot hunter|Numbfoot hunters]] in the area will agro the snow leopards and usually kill them on their own.  Eventually a Bloodthirsty Leopard will spawn, and since it's strong enough to kill the Numbfoot hunters, it will stay up indefinitely.  Because of this, there may actually be 4+ Bloodthirsty Leopards up at a time despite it being a rare spawn.
- Was reduced from ^^^Heroic to ^solo in [[LU48]], and restored to ^^^Heroic some time in 2018.

## Strategy

- Easily moloable.
