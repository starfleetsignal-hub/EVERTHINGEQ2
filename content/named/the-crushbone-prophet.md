## Strategy

- The Crushbone Prophet is part of a ring event. The event seems to be on a ~25 minutes real time respawn.
  1. Clear the [[A frenzied worshipper|frenzied worshippers]] (three 24^^^ unlinked) to activate the fanatical devotees.
  1. Clear the [[A fanatical devotee|fanatical devotees]] (two groups of two 24^^ linked).
  1. [[The Crushbone Prophet]] (24^^^) and two [[A boar beast|boar beasts]] (24 evencon) activate. Kill them to complete the event.
