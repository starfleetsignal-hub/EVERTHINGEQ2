## Acidic Mass Ring Event

On the north side of the central pyramid, a single [[Swirling ooze]] sits. If you kill this ooze, you begin a heroic ring event that spawns a series of monsters resulting in a final named monster as follows:

1. 1 [[Swirling ooze]] (46^^^ Heroic)
1. 1 [[Bubbling ooze]] (47^^^ Heroic)
1. 2 [[Bubbling ooze]]s (47^^ Heroic)
1. 3 [[Bubbling ooze]]s (47^ Heroic)
1. 4 [[Bubbling ooze]]s (47^ Heroic)
1. 2 [[Boiling ooze]]s (48^ Heroic)
1. 3 [[Boiling ooze]]s (48^^ Heroic)
1. 4 [[Boiling ooze]]s (48^ Heroic)
1. [[The Acidic Mass]] (49^^^ Heroic)

This Ring Event is part of the quest, [[The Heart of Fear]], but can be completed without having the quest.
