## Spawn in The Tombs of Night: A Search For Answers

To spawn Lureclaw the Angler you must kill all the [[A mist grinnin netslinger|mist grinnin netslingers]] in the water area near the waterfall, not just below below the waterfall, then kill the second wave of 2 groups of assorted mist grinnins that spawns.
