## Strategy

Frostbeard comes grouped with [[Brendegor Bitelimb (Epic)|Brendegor Bitelimb]], [[Zaashka Bradagan (Epic)|Zaashka Bradagan]] and [[Duncan Bradagan (Epic)|Duncan Bradagan]].  To start the fight, there is a system where one player climbs onto the roof above the four and drops a chandelier on the head either Frostbeard or Bitelimb - be sure to pick the right chandelier.

The Bradagans may not target the same target as Iilatus or else they will all a "Cooperative Strike" AOE which will wipe the raid.  The Bradagans do not Cooperative Strike with each other and can be tanked by one tank. Assign at least one group to Iilatus, and have the rest of the raid burn down the Bradagans. Duncan Bradagan has somewhat less health than Zaashka and should be burned down first. Once both Bradagans are down, both groups join in on Iilatus.

Iilatus has an red message AoE which players must joust.

## Notes

- Frostbeard is the easier (requiring less critical mitigation) of the two giants.
