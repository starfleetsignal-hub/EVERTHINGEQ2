## Notes

- To spawn this mob, clear the three groups of mummies on the ledge.  The groups are all groups of six level 55v mummies, one [[A vassal of offering|vassal of offering]] per group and the rest a random mix of the Anuk' Sul decars/Faro' Nuk veterans/Sulite lunas found all through Priestess' Court.  For each group killed, a moveable orange and blue striped vase will spawn.  Move it to the triangle shaped area in front of the Rin Priestess' door.
- For each vase placed, there is a chance to either spawn two [[An offering retriever|offering retrievers]] (55^^) or the Rin Priestess of the Fyr'Un (55^^^) and two offering retrievers (55v).
