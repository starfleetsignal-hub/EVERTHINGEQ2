## Strategy

- Jump to the platform in the middle of the room.
- When you engage him a bunch of groups of 3 ^^s will spawn around the outside of the room.
- You don't have to fight those mobs.
- Some other adds will spawn that will path to the central platform, AE them down or just kill the named to taste.
- Has a knock back that can hit you into adds, so fighting down the bottom of the pit is safer.
- Deals crush/divine mostly.
- There is a ladder on the safe (near) side of the pit to climb out, or you can just evac.
