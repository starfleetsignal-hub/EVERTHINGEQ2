## Strategy

1. Jump down into the room to start the fight; soon Kavis will use and damage the teleporter.
1. A [[A Shade of Kavis|Shade of Kavis]] will appear on the first ledge {{waypoint 162, -21, -14}}, initially immune to damage.
   - Don't use the teleporter: it will give you a debuff in increments of 20 and caps at 80 that will decrease your total health. The debuffs can be dispelled, but there's an easier way.
   - In each corner of the room, [[A hooked brineterror|hooked brineterrors]] will spawn, initially non-aggro but soon zooming in for the kill. As each spawns, a waterspout is formed.
   - Enter the waterspout nearest to the ledge and swim up the water column. Swim higher than the ledge before angling in, you're going to fall a bit as you exit the water.
   - Use a Heroic Opportunity to make the [[A Shade of Kavis|Shade of Kavis]] attackable, then kill it.
1. The second [[A Shade of Kavis|Shade of Kavis]] appears on the ledge at {{waypoint 200, -21, -46}}. Repeat the steps from the first Shade.
1. The third [[A Shade of Kavis|Shade of Kavis]] appears on the ledge at . Repeat the steps from the first Shade.
1. Kavis will reappear on the floor at ~30% health. He has an uncurable AOE debuff, so hopefully you didn't use the damaged teleporter. Burn him down.
