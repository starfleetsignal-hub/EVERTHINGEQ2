Kandti is a bonus Tier 1 raid boss that can be summoned  using a [[Golden Whistle]] in certain raid zones.

## Location

### [[Raj'Dur Plateaus: The Hunt (Raid)]]

- {{waypoint -118.61, 39.86, -270.15}} on the eastern river banks below Kalickia Falls.
- {{waypoint 46.50, 20.12, -211.96}} in a rock cleft on the western river banks, facing towards the lift.
- {{waypoint -46.81, 12.65, -166.65}} in a rock cleft on the eastern river banks, facing away from the river.

### [[Sandstone Delta: The Standing Storm (Raid)]]

- {{waypoint 772.16, 134.52, -315.12}} among the rocks in the northwest corner of Sandstone Escarpment.
- {{waypoint 622.26, 116.44, -305.40}} among the rocks in the northeast corner of Sandstone Escarpment.
- {{waypoint 566.63, 95.26, -90.63}} among the rocks just south of the Blooming Rindle waterfall.
- {{waypoint 649.95, 116.22, -191.15}} on rocks overlooking the north banks of the Blooming Rindle river.
- {{waypoint 691.04, 103.66, -102.79}} between the two waterfalls of the Blooming Rindle river.

If the encounter with Kandti is broken or otherwise reset, Kandti will move to a new location.

## Stats

- 108 Flurry Avoidance
- 324 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 9350 Resolve

## Strategy

- Every 45 seconds, Kandti "unleashes a screech in the direction of" a raid member. The named player will become the target of the monkey, regardless of current threat levels.
- Kandti casts a Barrage which can only be blocked with [[Bulwark of Order]].
