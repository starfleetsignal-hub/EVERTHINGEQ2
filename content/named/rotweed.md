## Spawning Rotweed

- Step onto the island in the middle of [[Teardrop Cave]] and three [[Rotweed Thistle|Rotweed Thistles]] appear, linked.
- If you remain on the island and kill them **AND** are lower than level 16, Rotweed will spawn immediately. If you're level 16 or higher Rotweed may not spawn.

### Bug Fixes

- To avoid the potential bug, it is recommended that after stepping on to the island (regardless of your level, and especially if you are completing the quest grayed out) that you back up to the cave mouth once again, and kill the [[Rotweed Thistle|Rotweed Thistles]] one at a time. Standing back with the third and final Thistle defeated should spawn Rotweed on the island. This technique was confirmed 2 January 2009 using an 80 [[Bruiser]].
- Alternatively, or if the above method doesn't work, delete the quest and return to [[Empress Anassa]], who will re-issue you the quest.
