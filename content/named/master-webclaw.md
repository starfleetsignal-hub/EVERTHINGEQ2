## Spawn

On the island are 4 groups of 3-5 Icegills each.  When these groups die a 5th group will spawn on the northern part of the island, this group can spawn a placeholder or Webclaw in it. Upon killing the 5th group's placeholder, there is an additional chance that Webclaw will spawn in a 6th group on the same spot.
