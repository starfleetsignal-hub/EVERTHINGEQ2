## Spawning The Cogsmasher

Complete [[Sniffwistle's Superior Attacker]] {{waypoint -372, 15, 188}} until 3 [[A cognatic bixie bopper|cognatic bixie bopper]]s have spawned in the kobold camp at the Steamfont entrance. The Cogsmasher will spawn and destroyer the boppers. It may then be killed to loot [[Cogsmasher's Wrench]]
