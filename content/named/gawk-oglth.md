The Ogles, Three are a Tier 1 raid boss. They gain an additive advantage for each player below 12 in the raid and beyond a soft time limit of 60 minutes.

## Statistics

- Flurry Avoidance 250.0
- Ability Doublecast Avoidance 700.0
- Resolve 13,230.0

## Abilities

- **Overbearing Influence I**
- An incurable trauma that causes power usage to also cost health based on the amount of power used.
- **Void Cloaked**
- A buff that gives a chance of triggering Negative Static on the attacker.
- **Negative Static**
- A curable arcane that interrupts the target, inflicts magic damage over time, decreases Fervor, and decreases Accuracy.
- **Surging Thoughts**
- An incurable arcane that deals increasing mental damage to health and power over time.
- **Ultimate Awareness**
- A buff that increases outgoing damage to its main target for each increment. Increments are added when players are struck by Shockwave Glance. Gaze, Glare, and Gawk share an increment counter; all three become stronger when a player is struck.
- **Crushing Gawk**
- An incurable arcane that increases crushing damage to all targets within 50 meters. If a target has all three of Burning Gaze, Piercing Glare, and Crushing Gawk, the curse Oglth Suppresion is applied to them.
- **Mind Boom**
- A curable trauma that deals crushing damage over time and knocks back all targets within 15 meters. Damage from this effect has 100% bleedthrough.
- **Barrage**
- An incurable curse that can only be blocked by [[Bulwark of Order]]. Gaze, Glare, and Gawk have separate Barrage abilities.
- **Overbearing Lash**
- A curable arcane that deals multiple strikes of crushing damage and fast ticking increasing mental damage. The mental damage has an increased bleedthrough.
- **Crushing Probe**
- A curable arcane that dazes and roots targets, deals fast ticking increasing mental damage, and increases physical damage from all sources.
