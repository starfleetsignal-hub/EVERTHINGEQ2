Jagah the Ferocious is a Tier 2 raid boss. He spawns when all void portals in the dungeon have been closed. Void portals are closed by killing nearby trash mobs.

## Abilities

- **Stalwart Admonishment**
- A curable elemental that deals elemental damage and increases all other damage types. This effect only lands on fighters.
- **Ferocious Soul**
- A curable elemental that deals elemental damage and increases noxious damage from all sources.
- **Distorted Ferocity**
- A curable arcane that deals fast-growing damage over time.
- **Out of Sight, Out of Mind**
- An curse that is incurable while the target remains further than 5 meters from Jagah. When the curse expires, the entire raid is killed.
- **Ferocious Support**
- A buff that decreases damage from all sources for each summoned supporter in the encounter.
- **Teamwork**: Leader
- A buff that casts Tag Team Strike on the current target, if that target already has the effect from Teamwork: Member.
- **Barrage**
- An attack that can only be countered with [[Bulwark of Order]].

### Beast of Burden

- **Burdened Protection**
- A buff that protects the Beast from all damage until dispelled.
- **Teamwork**: Member
- A buff that casts Tag Team Strike on the current target, if that target already has the effect from Teamwork: Leader.

### Acolyte of Aggression

- **Focused Protection**
- A buff that protects the Acolyte from all damage while it has any increments. The buff starts with 8 increments, and 1 increment is lost every time the Acolyte switches targets.
- **Spellweaved Admonishment**
- A curable elemental that deals elemental damage and increases all other damage types. This effect only lands on mages.

### Sentinel of Solitude

- **Sentinel's Protection**
- A buff that protects the Sentinel from all damage while it is within 10 meters of Jagah or any of Jagah's summoned supports.
- **Teamwork**: Member
- A buff that casts Tag Team Strike on the current target, if that target already has the effect from Teamwork: Leader.

### Mimic of Jagah

- **Teamwork**: Member
- A buff that casts Tag Team Strike on the current target, if that target already has the effect from Teamwork: Leader.

## Strategy

Jagah continuously summons Beasts of Burden, Acolytes of Aggression, and Sentinels of Solitude throughout the fight. Each of these adds has its own protection buff with a unique way of dispelling the protection.

At 75% Jagah begins summoning Mimics of Jagah. A Mimic is summoned whenever a player casts [[Temporal Mimicry]]. If two Mimics are summoned at the same time, the entire raid is killed. If Jagah and one of his Mimics are both targeting the same player, that player is instantly killed. This is not the same as the Teamwork/Tag Team Strike effect.
