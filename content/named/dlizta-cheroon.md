## Notes

- At 10%, he says "Forces of Darkness..." in Thexian. Stun him with your enchanter and burn him down. If you don't stun him, he will AE lifetap both power and health. This includes a knockback. DANGEROUS!  If "Forces of Darkness..." is used once at 10%, he seems to use it again once he gets back down to 25%.
