## Faction

- -500 faction with **The Court of Al'Afaz**.

## Hints on Defeating

* First off, make sure that the melee dps within your raid has on the appropriate **Noxious Resist** gear. They can either choose to eat the AoE, or joust it.

* Keep all casters at max range to avoid the Nox AoE.

* **On the pull,** have the Tank turn the mob so that the Tank's back is toward the island that The Great Gardener was on. This will make sure that the Tank is facing the raid (towards the steps) and the mob is facing the opposite direction (towards the island).

* The Great Gardener will spawn up to 4 adds. A Main Assist (MA) can target the adds so that dps can burn them down, or you can AoE them. Keep in mind that it is probably best to have a well-geared MA to tank the adds due to aggro reasons.
