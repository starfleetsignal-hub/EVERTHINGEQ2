## Strategy

DPS Check.

Try to clear the room prior to engaging named as they will begin spawning an add [[A Disruptive Tutor]] shortly after initial agro and it will 'revive' continuously throughout the fight.

There will be some spike damage every time the tutor becomes attackable again, be mindful of for survival cooldowns.

If the fight progresses longer than 7 minutes, the named will begin much more powerful attacks.
