The Tsmang Blightswarm Broodmaster is a Tier 2 raid boss.

## Stats

- 113 Flurry Avoidance
- 330 Ability Doublecast Avoidance
- 9434 Resolve

## Strategy

- This fight has a hard time limit of 60 minutes.
- [[Blightswarm swarmer|Blightswarm swarmers]] join the fight every 30 seconds, on average. If two of these swarmers are active at the same time, the Broodmaster begins to heal.
- The Broodmaster casts an incurable trauma named **Piercing Screech** which deals ticking piercing damage and stifles the affected target.
- The Broodmaster casts a curable noxious named **Venomous Infection** which deals ticking poison damage.
- At 90%, the Broodmaster begins looking for a weakness within the raid force. This starts a hidden 10 minute timer. If the Broodmaster is reduced to 10% before this timer expires, the Broodmaster abandons this effort (success!). If the timer expires before the Broodmaster reaches 10%, then the entire raid dies at 10%. No matter how long it takes to reach 10%, the death penalty is applied at 10%, and not when the timer expires.
- The Broodmaster does not cast any form of Barrage; [[Bulwark of Order]] can be used as a simple debuff.
