## Lore

This is the dragon being worshiped by the mist goblins in [[Kunzar Jungle]] (as shown in the text for the quest [[Dragon Magic]]).

## Strategy

- When your raid or group gets killed by Severilous he takes off and flies over Kunzar Jungle. This takes about 30 minutes. As he flies back he will approach from the north. He can be pulled from the sky if you happen to find him flying.

This mob AoE fears causing the raid to scatter. The best place to fight him is at the base of the tree just south of his spawn point. Once the tank has his back to the trunk of the tree, the roots of the tree keep the raid in the same area and will prevent the healers from having line of sight issues once the fear wears off. Easy one group or less kill with modern gear. Drops an ornate chest that contains 15-24p making for a nice split.
