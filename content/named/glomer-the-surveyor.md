## Strategy

The 157,000,000 hp reported by ACT may be the result of **not** following the strategy below. We failed to realize that all our ranged attacks were actually healing him and therefore increasing his hit points. Conceivably, his hp could be infinite.

Mob is perma-rooted, and heals when hit with ranged attacks. Has small knock back to try and make you hit him with a ranged attack. Casts Orafik Mindtap that drains power and does crushing damage. Don't run out of power.

If you're using Mercs and/or pets, make sure their ranged attacks are off. We had a group of 3 with 3 Mercs and multiple pets (regular and dumbfire) and hammered him for a good long while (doing absolutely NO damage) until we shut down all ranged attacks. After that he went down fast. Even the casters went up to him with their wands and helped beat him down.
