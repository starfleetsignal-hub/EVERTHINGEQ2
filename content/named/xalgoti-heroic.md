## Strategy

Simply have any casters in your group Root Xalgoti constantly while the tank stays in the corner that Xalgoti is in, whereas the party stands across the room directly and burns him down. AE Blocks can be essential If the Tank loses control of the threat and should give him enough time to pull him back.
You can also interrupt and stun him constantly to avoid him to change target.
