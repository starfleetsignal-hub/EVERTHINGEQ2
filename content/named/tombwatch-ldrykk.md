## Strategy

- Memwipes
- Teleports player
- Curse with damage (curable) - while curse is active, only "non hostile" spells may be used
- relatively large aggro range

A well-geared duo (one healer and one mage, geared for Experts) can kill him.  It takes a long time, he has a lot of health. Watch for line of sight issues for heals, especially after a teleport.
