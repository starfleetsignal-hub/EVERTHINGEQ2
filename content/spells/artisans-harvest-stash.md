## Notes

1. NOTE: You will need to complete the quest "[[A Stitch in Time, Part V: Sealed With Hate]]" in order to receive this spell. Once the spell is cast, the 44 slot Harvest Bag will open every time you open your bags. Also be aware that there will be several prerequisites before you will be able to complete this quest.
- this harvest stash is basically an extra backpack, that doesn't use a backpack slot. instead you have to cast this spell to open it.
- everything you harvest will be in this harvest stash.
- Crafts can take raw materials from this stash.
