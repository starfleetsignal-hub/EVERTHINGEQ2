## Note

- Higher Spell Ranks of this pet spell will increase pet effectiveness.
- It's easy to miss that this pet also does AE disease damage in addition to it's regular attack as it is listed in the description but not in the effects.
- It **does** add mobs that are not part of the current encounter if they happen to wander by.
