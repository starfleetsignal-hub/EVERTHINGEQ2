## Note

This spell scales from levels 20 to 50. It uses the same recast timer as a fighter's normal Taunts. It can be purchased from [[Gumgullet]] in [[Splitpaw Den]] once you have earned the trust of the Splitpaw Gnolls.

## AA Influence

[[Executioner's Anger]] ([[Guardian]] and [[Berserker]]): Increases threat amount
