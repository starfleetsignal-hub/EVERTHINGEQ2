## Notes

- Levels of hostile effects dispelled can be roughly computed for any level as 1.08*Level+1. At L80, this dispels 88 levels.
- This spell is usable by all priests and automatically scales with character level. It has no designated spell quality and upgrades do not exist.
- As of [[LU45]] this spell replaced [[Cure Noxious]], [[Cure Trauma]], [[Cure Elemental]] and [[Cure Arcane (Priest)|Cure Arcane]].
