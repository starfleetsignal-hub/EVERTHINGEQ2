## Notes

- Upgrades of this spell increase the damage of the possessed essence.
- The abilities of your possessed essence will vary depending on the class of the mob you have possessed.
- See [[Coercer Pet Strategies]] for more on using this spell, including what different classes can do.
- can be cast while moving

## Enhance

- [[Eye of the Siren (Mythical)|Eye of the Siren]] - *Increases the max health of the coercer's Possess Essence*
- [[Coerced Protection]] - *reduces all damage done to the pet by 25%*
