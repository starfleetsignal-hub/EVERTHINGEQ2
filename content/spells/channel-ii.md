## Effects

Upgrades of this spell Increase the power gained when using Channel II.

## Notes

This spell collects the power of all group members, double it and shares it with each member.
This spell doesn't look at the % of the power it looks after the actual power that the group members have.

Example:

- Before channeling=
  - Member 1 = 2,000
  - Member 2 = 4,000
  - Member 3 = 1,000
  - Member 4 = 0
  - Member 5 = 5,000
  - Member 6 = 3,000
- Total power = 15,000
  - Guess with master channeling the power gets doubled => power that is shareable = 30,000
- After channel = 30,000/6 = 5,000 power each group member
