Crystallized Soul: Y / Y / Y / Y / Y / Y / Y / Y (Apprentice to Grandmaster)

## NOTES

Effect explanation:

- When a group member strikes the death blow on a monster, there is a chance that a [[Crystallized Soul]] will be summoned into their inventory.
- This is an activatable item. Its activation effect is an AE heal centered on the user.
  - (This is a "true" AE, equivalent to a "blue background" AE spell. i.e. The effect will heal any player characters in range, not just group or raid members.)
- This item inherits the spell quality of the buff used to summon them. Improved spell quality of the buff increases the heal amount of the activation effect on the summoned item.
- Spell quality does **not** affect the buff's chance to trigger. It only affects the heal effect on the summoned item.
