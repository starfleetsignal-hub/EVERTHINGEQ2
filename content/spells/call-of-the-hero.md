## Notes

- Expert version is crafted from the recipe found in [[Ancient Teachings of: Call of the Hero]]
- The veteran reward [[Call of the Veteran]] works similarly to this spell
- This spell used to require a reagent, but that requirement was removed.

Although there are Adept, Expert and Master versions of this spell, they are all identical in terms of functionality. Higher versions of the spell offer no reduction in recast time.
