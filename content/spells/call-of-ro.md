## Notes

**Call of Ro** is an ability like [[Call of Qeynos]] and [[Call of the Overlord]] which teleports you to [[Maj'Dul]].

It is obtained from the quest [[Becoming a Resident of Ro]] which requires access into one of the three court houses in [[Maj'Dul]]
