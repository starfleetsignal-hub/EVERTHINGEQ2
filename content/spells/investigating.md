## Notes

The **Investigating** skill is used during the [[Swords of Destiny Timeline]] to gather evidence. This skill is new, as of [[LU32]].

- You gain the ability either by:
  - after completeing a task for [[Inspector Berlok Beeglesnoop|Berlok]] in the [[A Crusade to Faydwer]] quest
  - you buy it from [[Juulika McDarid]] in [[Teren's Grasp]] at {{waypoint 1838, 487, -840}} for 8p 75g

You may also aquire access to this ability while on certain Epic Quests as of [[LU42]].
