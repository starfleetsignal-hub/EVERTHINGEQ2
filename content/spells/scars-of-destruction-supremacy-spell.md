## Notes

- Players initially earn a scribe-able scroll ([[Scars of Destruction: Supremacy (Spell Scroll)|of the same name]]) with this effect upon completion of the Scars of Destruction Signature Questline achievement [[Scars of Destruction (Achievement)|Scars of Destruction]]. After scribing the spell, it will increase in power as other achievements and requirements are completed.
- While multiple other achievements (and quest series) strengthen the spell, it can only be initially earned through the Adventuring [[Scars of Destruction Timeline|SoD Sig Line]] [Sorry, Tradeskillers... :-( ]
