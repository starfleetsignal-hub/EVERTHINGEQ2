**Mining** is the skill used when harvesting [[Gemstone Nodes|Gemstone]] or [[Ore Nodes|Metal Ore]] to obtain Ore Harvests or Gemstone Harvests
<br clear="all"/>

## Tools

![](images/Icon_Pick.jpg)
**Picks** can be used to improve mining skill or reduce the time it takes to harvest a rock.

| Tool | Useable<br> at Level | Obtain | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Basic mining pick]] | 1 | This item is no longer sold by vendors, although it may be available on the broker. | Increases Mining skill by 1.0 |
| [[Ash Pick]] | 20 | Crafted by [[Woodworker]] (level 27) | Reduces harvest time for mining by .5 seconds |
| [[Briarwood Pick]] | 30 | Crafted by [[Woodworker]] (level 37) | Reduces harvest time for mining by 1 second |
| [[Teak Pick]] | 40 | Crafted by [[Woodworker]] (level 47) | Reduces harvest time for mining by 1.5 seconds |
| [[Sandalwood Pick]] | 50 | Crafted by [[Woodworker]] (level 57) | Reduces harvest time for mining by 2 seconds |
| [[Redwood Pick]] | 70 | Crafted by [[Woodworker]] (level 77) | Reduces harvest time for mining by 2.5 seconds |
| [[Miscalibrated Automated Pickaxe]] | CL30 | Made with Tinkering (skill 160). | Reduces harvest time for mining by -1.0 seconds<br>Increases Mining skill by +10 |
| [[Calibrated Automated Pickaxe]] | CL46 | Made with Tinkering (skill 235). | Reduces harvest time for mining by -1.0 seconds<br>Increases Mining skill by +15 |
| [[Overclocked Automated Pickaxe]] | CL66 | Made with Tinkering (skill 335). | Reduces harvest time for mining by -2.5 seconds<br>Increases Mining skill by +20 |

## Skill Cap

The skill cap for mining is based on whichever is higher of your adventure level or tradeskill level.
