## Notes

- Received from the Spell Scroll [[Friendly Tribal Goblin Runt]]
- This fluff pet is reminiscent of Kunark goblins.  It has neither speech nor special abilities - except, like The Fonz, the ability to look cool all day.

This pet does **not** override your Deity pet, but may override other fluff pets.
