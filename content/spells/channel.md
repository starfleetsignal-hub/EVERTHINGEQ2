## Effects

Upgrades of this spell reduce the power loss when using Channel.

## Notes

This spell collects the power of all group members and shares it each member.
This spell doesn't look at the % of the power it looks after the actual power that the group members have.

Example:

- Before channel=
  - Member 1 = 2,000
  - Member 2 = 4,000
  - Member 3 = 1,000
  - Member 4 = 0
  - Member 5 = 5,000
  - Member 6 = 3,000
- Total power = 15,000
  - Guess with master channel you loose 1% power => Power that is shareable = 14,850
- After channel = 14,850/6 = 2,475 power each group member
