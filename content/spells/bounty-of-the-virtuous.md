## Notes

- This spell automatically scales with character level. It has no designated spell quality and upgrades do not exist.
- This spell can only be purchased from [[Gumgullet|Merchant Gumgullet]] in [[Splitpaw Den|the Splitpaw Den]] once you have earned the trust of the Splitpaw Gnolls.
- The power restored is greater than the power to cast the spell at sufficiently high level, resulting in a net increase in power if the proc occurs.
