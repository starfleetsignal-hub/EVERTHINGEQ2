## Notes

- This spell is a "passive" spell. When you enter a solo instance where you "need" it, it should get cast. You see a green emote like message -*A surge of divine power flows through you!*
  - If it's not cast you can cast the spell manually from your spellbook, if you see it has not applied to you.
- When you duo a solo instance the buff will be maintained by the char that got zoned in first, the other one needs to cast it manually again.
  - *Check the Health of each other to see if you were affected by the buff health-wise or not.*
