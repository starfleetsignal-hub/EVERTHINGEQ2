## Notes

- A buff from the [[Guild Hall Amenities|Guild Buff Banner]] that can be placed in a [[Guild Hall]].
  - The banner can purchased ahead of time but the buffs do not work until the guild reaches level 100.
- The banner only needs to be clicked once time by each member to learn the new buff, similar to attuning with the Guild Tanslocation Beacon to get the [[Call to Guild Hall]] spell.
- The buffs are cumulative as the guild levels.
  - For example, if your guild is level 120 you will have all of the buffs and bonuses below shown for 100, 101, 110, and 120.

## Effects

| Guild Level | Buff |
|---|---|
| 100 | Primary stat buff, depending on character level (418.5 at char level 100, 343.4 at char level 90) |
| 101 | Increase status contributed to the guild by 5% (quests, status item sales) |
| 110 | Increases Ground Mount, Flying and In Combat movement speed by 5% |
| 120 | Increases Crit Chance by 4% of the caster's base Crit Chance [On TLE this is crit bonus, but can't be seen until Destiny of Velious is unlocked on the server.] |
| 130 | Increases Max Health by 10% |
| 140 | Increases Potency by 1% of the caster's base Potency. [On TLE servers this buff is replaced by 1 fervor.] |
| 150 | Increases Fervor by 0.5%. This buff stacks among group members with like or higher guild level of 150. A group of 6 would each gain 3 fervor on each player. |
| 160 | Increase status contributed to the guild by 10% (quests, status item sales) |
| 170 | Increase tithe gain by 0.5% |
| 180 | Increases abmod based on character level. This buff stacks among group members with like or higher guild level of 180. A group of 6 would each gain (X*6) on each player. |
| 190 | Increases Max HP based on character level. This buff stacks among group members with like or higher guild level of 180. A group of 6 would each gain (X*6) on each player. Note: This replaces the level 130 buff. |
