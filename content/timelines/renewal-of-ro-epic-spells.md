## Obtaining your Apprentice Epic Spell

1. Complete the [[Renewal of Ro Timeline#Renewal of Ro Signature Quest|Renewal of Ro Signature Quest]] on at least one character.
1. Complete all the [[Renewal of Ro Timeline#Music Box Quests|Music Box Quests]] on the same character.
1. Now that character and any of your alts can speak to [[Merchant Fiza Tazi]] in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]] at [[Hopewell Harbor]] {{waypoint 121.13, -59.93, 500.76}} and turn in sets of the following fertilizer items. Repeat until you receive your class' [[Manaspark Lotus Flower]]:
   - [[Red desert sand]]
   - [[Chest of scorpion exoskeletons]]
   - [[Desert charger horns]]
1. Inspect the [[Manaspark Lotus Flower]] to start your epic spell quest and receive the Apprentice version of your Epic Spell. The quest and epic spell names are the following:

### Traveling Researchers

![](images/Glyphed_Rune_-_Crusader.jpg)
After getting your dropped mythical quest update, on inspecting the item it tells you to find one of four **Traveling Researchers** (by archetype) in one of the three overland zones:  Quest is still good, Balkot spawned at 12:01 AM Central Standard Time on 16-Feb-2023.  Update:  10:01 AM Central Standard Time, 20 Jan 2026, Angrus Silentfang spawned on Maj'Dul.

- Mage -- [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]], [[The Wanderer]] {{waypoint -419.85, -59.44, 848.07}}
- Healer -- [[Takish Badlands]], [[Filowen Curetouch]] {{waypoint -348.30, 217.24, -244.53}}
- Scout -- [[Takish Badlands]], [[Angrus Silentblade]] {{waypoint 667.25, 247.34, -475.82}}
- Fighter -- [[Sandstone Delta]], [[Balkot Stonefury]] {{waypoint 1.26, -5.74, 554.93}}

The Traveling Researchers spawn daily on **even** hours, Pacific Time - 0000, 0200, 0400, 0600, 0800, 1000, 1200, 1400, 1600, 1800, 2000, 2200. [Convert to your local time here](http://www.timebie.com/std/pst.php).  A list of the next spawn times (at least for Antonia Bayle) [may be available on tempus.flame.org](https://tempus.flame.org/npcSpawns).

It appears that each researcher spawns on a 22-hour cycle. A given researcher is up for 1 hour and down for 21 hours; if the spawn time is due during a patch or immediately after it will most likely skip a spawn.
