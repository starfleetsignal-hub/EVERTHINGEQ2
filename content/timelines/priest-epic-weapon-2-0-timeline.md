UPDATE with Blood of Luclin:
You may now purchase your epic 2.0 spells from [[Maesy Colmiter]] in [[Sanctus Seru (City)]].
UPDATE with Chaos Descending:
You may now purchase epic 2.0 spells from [[Alhuurn Khalen]] in [[Myrist, the Great Library]].

- Characters **must be** at least a level **110** adventure or tradeskill class to enter [[Myrist, the Great Library]].*
- If you don't yet have them, this quest line rewards you with class specific epic 2.0 spells and unlocks them at the merchant [[Alhuurn Khalen]] for your other chars on your account.
- <s>You need to read one or two letters automatically placed in your Mailbox, accept the gifts, and follow the instructions to gain entrance to the Library.</s> There is a letter for 110 adventurers and another letter for 110 crafters. No longer needed; you can now zone into the [[Myrist, the Great Library]] from any [[Ulteran Spires|Ulteran Spire]].

## Requirements

- **The first quest [[Awakening Bonds: The First Steps]] requires**:
  - To start the Quest you:
    - Must be a level 100 [[Priest]].
    - Must be level 5 of one [[Ascension]] class
    - <s>Must have completed the City Timeline</s> *Removed with LU105*
    - <s>Must have your [[Epic Weapons|Fabled Class Epic Weapon]] 1.0 Quest completed</s> *Removed with LU105*
    - <s>Must have completed the [[Kunark Ascending Timeline]]</s> *Removed with LU105, still needed for [[Witness to the Past]].*
  - At some point of the first quest you will also need:
    - Complete the Ning Yun Retreat portion of [[The Stonebrunt Highlands Timeline]] and have +50k with [[Disciples of the Circling Destiny (Faction)]]
    - Access to [[Hua Mein Village]]. *(You can fly over the wall without faction, just avoid the guards)*
    - 500p
  - "Fast Travel" is your **Best Friend** when doing the quests.
  - The "Guise of Pursuit" is your next "Best Friend" when meeting faction requirements.

- **The second quest [[Awakening Bonds: Forks in the Road]] requires**:
  - Must be level 6 of one [[Ascension]] class to start the quest
  - Completion of [[The White Dragonscale Cloak]] - *Requires +20,000 faction with Guktan Guard*
  - +50,000 faction with Othmir of Velious
  - [[Great Divide Timeline]] - Must complete the Fina's Retreat series.
  - [[Cobalt Scar Timeline]] - Must complete the two series below.
    - [[Cobalt Scar Timeline#Killing Fields series|Killing Fields series]]
    - [[Cobalt Scar Timeline#Forsaken Othmir Village series|Forsaken Othmir Village series]] through [[High Tide]]
  - 500p

- **The third Quest [[Awakening Bonds: Whispers of the Past]] requires**:
  - Must be level 7 of one [[Ascension]] class to start the quest
  - Must have [[Paw of Opolla]], this is the Reward from the Heritage Quest [[A Source of Malediction]]
  - Must have completed [[Fallen Dynasty Timeline]] through [[A Vision of the Future]]
  - Must speak [[Thulian]]
  - Must be level 100 [[Artisan]]

- **The fourth quest [[Awakening Bonds: Once Was Lost...]] requires**:
  - Must be level 8 of one [[Ascension]] class to start the quest
  - [[Tears of Veeshan: Falling Tears]]

- **The fifth quest [[Awakening Bonds: ...Is Whole Again]] requires**
  - Must be level 9 of one [[Ascension]] class to start the quest
