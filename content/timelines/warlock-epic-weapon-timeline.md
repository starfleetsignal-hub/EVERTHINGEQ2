## Requirements

- You must be a level 80 [[Warlock]].
- You will need the ability to enter the library inside Nektropos Castle (either by yourself or with the help of someone). See [[The Boar's Head]] and [[The Red Marble]].
- At least 15K faction with [[Residents of Teren's Grasp (Faction)|Residents of Teren's Grasp]]. See also [[Kylong Plains Timeline]].
- Ability to speak the [[Froak]] language.  See [[A Mysterious Green Tome]].
- Ability to get through the green gate ([[A Bathezid gate crystal]] or can be bypassed with the Sokokar post), blue gate ([[A Nak'azar gate crystal]]), and red gate ([[A Caroeth gate crystal]]) inside [[Chardok]], either by having them yourself or someone in or outside the group opening the gates. Various call and transportation spells works too.
- (Optional) Enough gathering skill to harvest tier 8 roots/bushes: Minimum 340 gathering skill without the use of skill-increasing equipment. This requirement can be avoided by buying the required items.
