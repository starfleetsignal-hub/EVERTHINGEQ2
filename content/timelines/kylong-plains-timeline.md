In this timeline you will aid the Residents of Teren's Grasp as they seek to gain a foothold in [[Kylong Plains]]. Starting from [[Dreg's Landing]], you will have to fight tooth-and-nail to carve safe passage to [[Teren's Grasp]]. From there you will set out to the distant corners of this relentless land to lend a hand to their poorly defended outposts. Outside their ramshackle walls you will need to contend with swathes of unfriendly beasts. Good luck!
