The Feeding Duggin Timeline is all about... well, feeding Duggin Brandywine! This hungry halfling is willing to trade you some tableware for all the delectables you can bring him. You can find this hungry little Halfling on the docks of [[The Thundering Steppes]] at {{waypoint -469, -21, -405}}.

## Quests

- [[Tiffin for Duggin]] - harvest food in [[The Thundering Steppes]] or [[Nektulos Forest]].
- [[Home Cooking]] - harvest food in [[Enchanted Lands]]
- [[Filling the Bottomless Belly]] - harvest food in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]]
- [[Frozen Foods]] - harvest food in [[Everfrost]]
- [[Hungry, Hungry Halflings]] - harvest food in [[The Feerrott]]
- [[Desserts from the Desert]] - harvest food and wood in [[The Sinking Sands]], [[The Pillars of Flame]] or [[The Lesser Faydark]]
