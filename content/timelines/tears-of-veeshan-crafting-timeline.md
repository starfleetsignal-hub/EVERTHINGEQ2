## Prerequisites

You need to be a lvl 93 crafter to start the quest. [[Captain Ethan]] of the Far Seas Trading Company kicks things off with mail to you. The company has been hearing reports of travelers going to Ethernere and is interested, as always, in learning more. After reading your mail, meet Captain Ethan in the [[Village of Shin]].

### Ingredients needed for the Quests

- To complete these quests, you will need the following ingredients:
  - 2 aduki bean
  - 4 amethyst
  - 2 cutthroat trout
  - 9 iridium cluster
  - 11 matoppie root
  - 1 panther meat
  - 2 plump haddock
  - 18 rhenium ore
  - 1 ripe mangosteen
  - 6 swamp ash lumber
  - 8 thick bear pelt
  - 3 dough
  - 7 effulgent candle
  - 33 effulgent coal
  - 20 effulgent filament
  - 4 effulgent kindling
