The bird races of [[The Barren Sky]] are at war! In this timeline you must crisscross the floating islands to assist the [[The Windgazer (Faction)|Windgazer Hooluks]] as they try to stunt the war efforts of their Aviak and Vultak cousins. There are other lost travelers here that will surely require your assistance, as well.

This zone was revamped in 2010 and is now a suitable place to quest in the mid-60's. It transitions smoothly to the [[Bonemire Timeline]] around level 66.
