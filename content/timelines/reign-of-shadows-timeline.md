## Requirements

- If you wish to do all quests and receive the achievement for doing so, you will be required to speak the Akhevan language, [[Fleshless Tongue]]. There is currently one side quest that requires this. However, the Signature quest may be completed without it. Learning the language requires:
  - Complete the [[Blood of Luclin Timeline|Blood of Luclin Signature Questline]] through [[Shattered Dawn: Burn the Midnight Oil]].
  - Complete the BoL collection [[Akheva Runes]] (the collection pieces will not be visible until you have completed [[Shattered Dawn: Burn the Midnight Oil]]).
  - Complete the BoL quest [[Fleshless Tongue Untied]].

### Travel

- Access the [[City of Fordel Midst]] via Wizard Spire.
- Flight is restricted until you have completed certain quests in each zone.

*Echo Caverns: The Grumbling*

- *It's helpful to have Featherfall/Safefall/Glide in the pre-flight part of your adventures, for example, the Jann Magi Illusion, or Cloak of the Harvester.*

#### Travel Baubles

*Travel Baubles will become available after completing the Adventure Signature Line. Along the Tradeskill Line, crafters receive travel baubles earlier and the ability to purchase more.*

- [[Spirit of the Vah Shir]] is available in [[City of Shar Vahl]] from Alchemist [[Chiallar]] inside [[Kerrath Palace]] {{waypoint -186.32, -198.04, 594.66}}.
- [[Will of the Weald]] is available in [[Savage Weald]] from [[Vuchoz]] {{waypoint -411, 95, -323}} -- *Tradeskill*.
- [[Call of Shade]] is available in [[Shadeweaver's Thicket]] from Recuso Wilds Fuel Merchant [[Fertis]] at Nuniun's camp {{waypoint -127.00, 219.31, 449.03}}.
- [[Will of Echo Caverns]], [[Will of the Weald]], and [[Will of Shadeweaver's Thicket]] are available in [[Shadow Haven]] from Alchemist [[Sarah Poison]] in the [[House of Midst]] {{waypoint 633, -8, -519}}.

### Other Travel Hints

- If you have a [[Stein of Recollection]] or got the [[Dhalgar's Last Call]] from Thalumbra, go speak to a bartender in the [[City of Fordel Midst]] or Tsarji in City of Shar Vahl to set your call point to that tavern.

- Fast travel also allows you to port directly to the nexus in the [[City of Fordel Midst]].
