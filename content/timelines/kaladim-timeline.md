[[Kaladim]] has been overrun by kobolds, bugbears, roekilliks, and other manner of foul creatures. As such, the dwarves have been unable to reclaim their ancestral kingdom. They will send you on hit-and-run missions, where you will have to get out before being overrun by a host of furry enemies.
