*To get to [[Obol Plains]], take the Tourbillion in [[The Eidolon Jungle]] at {{waypoint -2416, 201, 735}}*

## Signature Series

*Progression of the [[Obol Plains]] portion of the signature series requires prior completion of [[The Eidolon Jungle]] portion of the signature series.  [[Shades of Drinal: Puzzle Pieces]] is the quest that sends you to [[Obol Plains]].*

Speak to [[Drinal's Steward]] at {{waypoint -723, -4, 21}} in [[Obol Plains]].  The following quests are part of the [[Obol Plains]] portion of the signature series:

1. [96] [[Shades of Drinal: Cardin Protection]]
1. [96] [[Shades of Drinal: Incantatory Corruption]]
1. [96] [[Shades of Drinal: Devoted Knowledge]]
1. [96] [[Shades of Drinal: Dashed Upon the Shore]]
1. [96] [[Shades of Drinal: Dreadcutter at World's End]]
1. [96] [[Shades of Drinal: Fate's Crusade]]
