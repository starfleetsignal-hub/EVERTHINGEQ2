## Notes

- You need to be at least level 100 Adventurer to start the Signature Questline.
- You need to be at least a level 2 [[Artisan]] for [[Underdepths Saga: Zaphardt's Defenders]].

1. Starting quest

   - [[Underdepths Saga: Into Darkness (good)]] - *in [[Kelethin]] from [[Dangle Springbolt]]*
   - [[Underdepths Saga: Into Darkness (evil)]] - *in [[Neriak, City of Hate]] from [[Virun D'Xyafin]]*

### Notes

The signature quests have few subquests, but they don't give much faction with the [[Shields of Maldura (Faction)|Shields of Maldura]]. The side quests, however, offer substantial amounts of faction, making it easier to achieve enough faction to use the merchant and menders and so on in Maldura.

### Item Triggered Quests

These are still  semi-valuable in that the umbrite keys can give you a Mythical Altar

*These are repeatable timed quests. Each rewards a key to a particular type of chest somewhere in [[Thalumbra, the Ever Deep|Thalumbra]]. See [[Thalumbra Chests for the Keys]].  The quests are ordered so you can start at the great drill and work your way around to Zou'Lidelas.*

- [[The Lost Miner]] -- from **a drained minecrawler** at the base of the Great Drill in [[Outer Maldura]] {{waypoint 448.62, 85.38, 365.08}}.
- [[Unsealed Tombs]] -- from **a talking dhalgar skull** on the centermost upper-level tomb in [[Tranquil Halls]] {{waypoint 360.16, 177.86, 556.37}}.
- [[Crate Courier]] -- from **a fallen crate** in the [[Glittervein Depths]] {{waypoint 462.65, 19.23, 883.79}}.
- [[Swimming For Shrooms]] -- from **an empty mushroom cart** at the [[Abandoned Fungus Farm]] {{waypoint 950.31, 41.67, 195.40}}.
- [[Memory Bloom]] -- from **a luminous pitcher plant** in [[Stoneheart Yards]] {{waypoint 515.72, 67.52, -168.39}}.
- [[Rubble Rampage]] -- from **a gleaming pickaxe** on [[Bluefist Battlefield]] {{waypoint 184.25, 78.92, -440.46}}.
- [[Extinguish the Flames]] -- from **an ascension brazier** {{waypoint -104.30, 232.52, -694.61}}.
- [[Leech Liberator]] -- from **a dying grimleech** in the [[Drowned Delta]] {{waypoint -404.12, 31.44, -496.74}}.
- [[Millions of Leeches, Leeches For Me]] -- from **a dead immolated gnemlin** {{waypoint 342.60, 483.44, 99.87}}.
- [[Pox Raiding]] -- from the scroll **an order for poxfiendlings** in [[Zou'Lidelas]] {{waypoint -428.05, 258.56, 394.03}}.
