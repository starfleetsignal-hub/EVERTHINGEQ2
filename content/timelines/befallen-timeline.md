Befallen is in the southwest corner of the Commonlands, near the [[Hidden Canyon Station|Hidden Canyon]] griffon tower. Formerly a fortress for the Knights of Marr, it is now inhabited by their ghosts and other undead. It consists of three separate dungeons.
