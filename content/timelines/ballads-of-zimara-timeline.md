## Requirements

- Character Level 125
- Receive a mail from Baoko:
  - ***Recalled to The Overrealm:***
  - *I hope this missive finds you in fine feather, my Undercloud friend. Regrettably though, I cannot give such assurances for myself or my hooluk kin. Which brings me to the point of my writing; Our parliament of elders would like to have an audience with you. Meet me at the Ulteran Spires on the Whisperwind Isle. I can share a few more details, answer your questions, and provide you with safe passage to their perch. Your friend in the lofty lands above, Baoko*
