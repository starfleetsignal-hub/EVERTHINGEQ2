## Notes

**This event deactivated when Scars of Destruction expansion went live.**

If you earned maximum faction with Open Hand you can still visit their [[Merchant Macleod]] in Sodden Archipelago on dock {{waypoint -48, 9, 380}}. All the prelude event items are still available for purchase.

## Requirements

- Receive a mail from [[Physiker Tsaada Hopebringer|Physiker Hopebringer]] or talk to her on the Butcherblock dock.
- You **Must** start with [[Krulkiel Intentions]] quest in order to be able to get the others in this series.
- All quests in this timeline give Open Hand faction

1. [[Under the Cover of Roughspun Darkness]]
