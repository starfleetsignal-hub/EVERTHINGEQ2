[[Frostfell]] is upon us! The Frostfell Elves and Gigglegibber Goblins of [[Frostfell Wonderland Village]] are in need of our aid to ensure that this year's gifts are just as good if not better than last year!

All quests in this timeline scale to player adventure level. Even if you mentor down, the quest level in your journal will remain your actual adventure level. However, quest mobs will still reflect your mentored-down level.

## Annual Quests

*Quests in this section can only be completed once per year, unless indicated otherwise.*

## Frostfell Trees

Speak with [[Gh'lad Tydingz Gigglegibber]] within any major city and complete his task to be rewarded your very own [[Frostfell Tree (House Item)|Frostfell Tree]]! This quest scales to the adventure level tier of the character, i.e. level 21-30 characters will receive the level 25 version of the quest. The first time this quest is completed at a specific adventure level, the player is rewarded a Frostfell Tree. Subsequent completions offer a choice of a [[Frostfell Wreath]], [[Festive Frostfell Antlers]], [[Frozen Token of E'ci|Frozen Tokens of E'ci]], and decorations for your tree. You can receive multiple trees and wreathes if you complete the quest at a different adventure level tier. These quests were introduced in 2005:

1. [[A Gigglegibber's work is never done (by a Gigglegibber).]] - This quest is repeatable up to 15 times. per adventure level tier and per city.
1. [[#Feelin' Gifty|Feelin' Gifty]]

### Feelin' Gifty

Completing one of these quests rewards the player with the ability to light up their [[Frostfell Tree]]. Only one of the quests can be completed per character, i.e. if you completed 'Feelin' Gifty in Qeynos' you will not be able to complete any of the other quests as they will not be offered. Additionally, the completed quest does not appear in the World Events category of the quest journal, rather in the city in which it was completed:

- [[Feelin' Gifty in Qeynos!]]
- [[Feelin' Gifty in Freeport!]]
- [[Feelin' Gifty in Kelethin!]]
- [[Feelin' Gifty in Neriak!]]
- [[Feelin' Gifty in Gorowyn!]]
- [[Feelin' Gifty in New Halas!]]

## Tradeskill Quests

Though there's just one crafting quest, it's very rewarding! First added in 2011, this quest can be repeated daily and is a great way to gain tradeskill XP. The subquests will appear as completed in your quest journal while on the main quest, but will disappear as soon as you complete the main quest:

## Repeatable Quests

### Candy Grab Races

First appearing in 2006, the Candy Grab Races test your speed and mouse-control. Depending on how successful you are at gathering candy you can win frozen tokens, house items, and vanity gear. Those who *really* work at it can even gain a special title.

- [[Frostfell Candy Grab: Antonica]] (infinite)
- [[Frostfell Candy Grab: Commonlands]] (infinite)
- [[The Great Candy (Cane) Chase]] (2015) (can be completed once every 18 hours)
