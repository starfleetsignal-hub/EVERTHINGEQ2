## Notes

To progress various parts of the quest line, you need the following:

- Must be an ally (40k faction) with the [[The City of Jinisk (Faction)|The City of Jinisk]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]]
- Must speak [[Ratongan]]
- When you go to Dire Hollow you must be ungrouped and your mercenary must be dismissed or the door will not be clickable.  It does not give a warning, it simply is non-reactive when you have your mercenary with you.

## Heroic version

- To be able to receive the quest go to [[The Sinking Sands]] and find the Kerran ghost, [[Apprentice Neemari]] in a cave at {{waypoint -1026, -230, -1209}}, accessed through a tunnel at {{waypoint -896, -235, -1246}} in the Bay of Anuk. Talk to him and listen to his story. You may now start the following quest, depending on your alignment:
  - [[The Responsibilities of a Berserker's Rage]] - If Qeynos
    - [[The Mystery of the Firewater Crystal]] - (Subquest)
  - [[The Consequences of a Berserker's Rage]] - If Freeport
    - [[The Mystery of the Firewater Crystal]] - (Subquest)
