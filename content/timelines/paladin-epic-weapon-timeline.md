## Prerequisites

- You must be a level 80 Paladin.
- You will need to have Ally (+40,000) faction with the [[Synod Reet (Faction)|Synod Reet]].
- You will need to be able to speak the [[Froak|Froak language]].

## Starting

Hail [[Kaherdin Surechest]] in basement jail (Door is on your right as you enter the building) in [[West Freeport Militia Building]] {{waypoint 133, -26, 133}}.
