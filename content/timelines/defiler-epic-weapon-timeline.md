## Prerequisites

- You must be a level 80 [[Defiler]].
- You must know [[Ykeshan]], the language of the trolls, before you begin this quest.
- You must know [[Everquest2:Froak|Froak]]    the language of Froglok. this a quested language starting with the collection
- [[Everquest2:A Mysterious Green Tome|A Mysterious Green Tome]]

- *Note: You can purchase the language primer from one of the [[Language Sellers#Ykeshan|language sellers]] of this language.*

- You must have at least +40K faction with the [[Synod Reet (Faction)]]
