## Notes

- The rewards of this Quest lines are all Level 95 and some quests can only be started at level 95
- [[Vesspyr Isles]] can be accessed from any druid ring.
- For tradeskill quests see the [[Tears of Veeshan Crafting Timeline]].
- For collection quests, see the [[Collection Quests Timeline#Tears of Veeshan Collections|Tears of Veeshan Collections Timeline]]
- For the missons see [[Draconic Etyma Mission System]]

****You are not required to complete the quest "Tears of Veeshan: The Eternal Broodlands" before doing this time line.***
