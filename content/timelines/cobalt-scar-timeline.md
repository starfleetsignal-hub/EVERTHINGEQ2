## Notes

- You MUST BE LEVEL 92 or higher to begin this quest chain. Discovered item quests are possible but very few.
- Cobalt Scar can be accessed from the Thurgadin Harbor in Great Divide by speaking to the New Combine Flight Master at {{waypoint 1278, -540, 306}}. It's the same flight station used to access [[The Withered Lands]].
- For tradeskill related solo quests see the [[Scars of the Awakened Crafting Timeline]].
