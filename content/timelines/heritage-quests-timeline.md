*This is a list of Heritage Quests, sorted by level. For an alphabetic list, see the heritage quests category.*

These quests are all heroic or epic in nature, and are more difficult to complete than normal quests. The rewards are familiar items from [[EverQuest]]. [[Signature Quests Timeline|Signature Quests]], which are similar in length and difficulty to Heritage Quests but reward items unique to [[EverQuest II]], can be found [[Signature Quests Timeline|on a separate page]].

### Heritage Quest Notes

[*] The quests with the asterisk are confirmed to have house-item counterparts.<br>
[1]The quests *[[A Strange Black Rock]]*, *[[An Axe from the Past]]* and *[[Stiletto's Orders Intercepted]]* have steps in common and should, if possible, be done together.<br>
[2]The quests *[[Hadden's Earring]]*, *[[Restoring Ghoulbane]]*, *[[A Missing Mask]]* and *[[War and Wardrobe]]* have steps in common and should, if possible, be done together.<br>
[3]*[[Reaching Blade of the Assassin]]* and *[[The Teachings of Yoru]]* need certain mobs/npc in the Antonica, Enchanted Lands and Rivervale and can be completed together at times.<br>
[4]The quests *[[Training is a Shield]]* and *[[The Rescue of the Green Hoods]]* have steps in common and should, if possible, be done together.<br>
[5]If you have completed *[[Berik's Revenge]]* and possess [[Berik, Sword of Thunder]] you can use Berik on the Bone Bladed Claymore  on the final step of this HQ to create the [[Electrified Bone Bladed Claymore]]<br>
[6]Although it is a level 55 quest, [[A Thread of Hope]] involves [[Shadow Odyssey]] zones that scale to the player's level. For example, if you are a level 80 group, you will have to run a level 80+ instance of [[The Deep Forge]]. Also, it may be beneficial to work on the pre-quest for "[[A Thread of Hope]]", (which is "[[Tinkering with Toggery]]") at the same time as doing "[[The Lost Legend of Lavastorm]]" and/or "[[Saving Soles]]" as all three quests involve adventuring and harvesting in [[Lavastorm]].<br>
[7]The *[[The Symbol in the Flesh]]* Heritage Quest has a sub-quest, [[Ghoulbane Empowered]], but the latter does not itself count as a Heritage Quest.<br>
[8]The **' behaves like a Signature Quest series. There are 12 quests in the series starting with the Collection quest *[[An Old Memoir]]*. However, only the final quest in the series, *[[The Fourth Warrior - Unification]]*, counts as a Heritage Quest.<br>
[9]The *[[Trail to the Past]]* Heritage Quest behaves like a Signature Quest series. There are 3 quests in the series, starting with this one, followed by *[[The Broken Pen]]* and *[[A Gnomish Master Plan]]*. In this case, however, all three quests count as Heritage Quests.<br>
[10]The *[[Greenmist Timeline]]* Heritage Quest behaves like a Signature Quest series. There are 5 quests in the series, *[[The Name of Fear]], [[The Word of Fear]], [[The Call of Fear]], [[The Path of Fear]] and [[The Triumph of Fear]]*. In this case, however, all five quests count as Heritage Quests.<br>
[11]The **' behaves like a Signature Quest series. There are 6 quests in the series starting with the quest *[[Mysterious Mark]]*. However, only the final quest in the series, *[[The Means to an End...]]*, counts as a Heritage Quest.<br>
[12]The **' behaves like a Signature Quest series. There are 4 quests in the series starting with the quest *[[Ritsy Delivery]]*. However, only the final quest in the series, *[[Ritsy's Lucky String (quest)|Ritsy's Lucky String]]*, counts as a Heritage Quest.<br>
[13]The **' behaves like a Signature Quest series. There are 10 quests in the series starting with the quest *[[Copper Coldain Insignia Ring]]*. In this case, however, all ten quests count as Heritage Quests.<br>
[14]This is the first and only tradeskill Heritage Quest and requires to be level 90 in a tradeskill.<br>
[15]The *[[Beauty's Only Skin Deep]]* Heritage Quest has two sub-quests, [[Besought Baubles]] and [[Unhappily Ever After]]. In this case, all three quests count as Heritage Quests.<br>

## Rewards

![Mounting Instructions for Dwarven Work Boots](images/DWB_Mounting_Instructions.jpg)
When most Heritage Quest rewards are examined, you are given the choice to mount them. This turns the reward into a House Item. If you examine the House Item you are given the choice to unmount it. You must unmount it if you wish to use it or to sell it.

## Suffix Titles

There are suffix titles that may be won by completing Heritage Quests. To receive the title, speak to Sage [[Indis Surion]] in [[The Concordium Tower]] in [[South Qeynos]] (yellow portal), Sage [[Navarius Orvalis]] in the [[Academy of Arcane Science]] in [[North Freeport]], Sage [[Piritta Silvarri]] in [[Kelethin]] in [[Greater Faydark]], or Sage [[Ricka Stonehide]] in [[New Halas]] in [[Frostfang Sea]] after completing the required number of Heritage Quests.
