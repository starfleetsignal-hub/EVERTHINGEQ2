[[The Shimmering Citadel]] was ripped from the [[Plane of Sky]] by [[The Djinn Master]]. Through this timeline you will learn more about the mysterious Djinn and their role in the harsh [[Desert of Ro]], if any.

You are no longer required to complete an access quest to enter the Shimmering Citadel or the Poets Palace.

## Main Questline

1. **[52] [[The Name of My Forefathers]]**
1. **[55] [[Lady Naga]]** - *rewards [[Samirah's mirror]], which allows the player to teleport directly inside the Shimmering Citadel from any zone in the Desert of Flames Expansion*
1. **[55] [[Visit the Scriptorium]]**
1. **[55] [[The Master's Museum]]**
1. **[55] [[The Caretaker (Quest)]]**
1. **[57] [[Be Careful What You Wish For]]**
1. **[57] [[In the Djinn Master's Service]]**
