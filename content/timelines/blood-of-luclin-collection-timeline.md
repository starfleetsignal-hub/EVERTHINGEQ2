## Overview

This timeline gives details about the [[Collecting|collection]] quests introduced as part of the Blood of Luclin expansion. Some quests of the [[Blood of Luclin Timeline]] reward a collection item.

Note: You must complete the Blood of L:uclin Signature Questline through Shattered Dawn: Burn the Midnight Oil  to see these runes.
