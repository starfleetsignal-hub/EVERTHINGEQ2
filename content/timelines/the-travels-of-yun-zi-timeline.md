## Notes

*See the [[Panda, Panda, Panda!]] page for more details.*

- When these quests were introduced in the summer of 2017, a new quest was added each week for nine weeks.
- These quests are now available throughout the year and may be completed at any time.
- Each of the quests must be completed in the order they were released, starting with the quests from 2017. You cannot skip ahead to later points in the quest series. ***2023 can be completed without previous progress in this questline.**
- You have to do the quest line with only one character per account to unlock access to "buy" the items on all of your characters account-wide.
- The quests are not level bound, but take you to high-danger locations, so a character that is well-geared saves frustration. A character with a flying mount is almost a necessity when you travel to The Withered Lands.
- Some players have reported that entering Skyshrine does not update the quest [[The "Travels" of Yun Zi - Ice to See Velious]], even though the level requirement to enter that area was waved for this series. Using an appropriate adventure level character is advised.
- These quests are only available to [[All Access Membership|All Access]] members. Don't forget to take advantage of [[Fast Travel]], which is included as part of your membership. It can significantly reduce travel time in some cases.

## Rewards

For each week completed, you receive Days of Summer Week X "currency". Unlike other special event currency this is not accumulated, but acts as an unlock. To check which weeks you have unlocked, check the Currency tab on any of your [[Understanding Your Character|character window]]; because the unlocks are account wide, all characters will list which week/s you've finished.
