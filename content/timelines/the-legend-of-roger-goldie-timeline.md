This Hallmark quest series will move you through major zones of Norrath as you level up, following [[Ole' Man Silvers]] as he searches for word of his captain Roger Goldie. Not to mention the captain's treasure...

1. [32] [[The Legend of Roger Goldie: The Adventure Begins]]
1. [42] [[The Legend of Roger Goldie: Some Like It Hot]]
1. [52] [[The Legend of Roger Goldie: Silvers versus the Volcano]]
1. [57] [[The Legend of Roger Goldie: Sandblasted]]
1. [67] [[The Legend of Roger Goldie: Quality Time in the Overrealm]]
1. [65] [[The Legend of Roger Goldie: Booty Abound!]]

## Notes

This is a timeline you can start at level 32 and return to about every 10 levels or so up to level 67 and sends you across a half-dozen zones.

Zones:

- Butcherblock Mountains - first at the Highland Outposts, then at the road to Greater Faydark
- Steamfont Mountains - at the foot of the Gnomeland Security Headquarters
- Lavastorm - at the base of the stairs leading into the Shrine of Thunder
- The Sinking Sands
- Tenebrous Tangle
- Kylong Plains

*For more "lore" information see [[The Legend of Roger Goldie Timeline/The Golden Path|the Golden Path]]*
