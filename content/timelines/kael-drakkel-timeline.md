## Item Triggered Quests

These quests are started by inspecting a mob-dropped item. They are non-repeatable and each rewards a Primal Velium Shard.

1. [95] [[Blue Blood Belts]] - Examine a Kromzek mage's sash to begin this quest.
1. [95] [[Bristles of Conflict]] - Examine a storm giant's beard to begin this quest.
1. [95] [[Fires of War]] - Examine an idigo nihilite to begin this quest.
1. [95] [[It Isn't Fare]] - Examine a storm giant meat to begin this quest.
1. [95] [[Toe Be or Not Toe Be]] - Examine a storm giant toe to begin this quest.
1. [97] [[Sovereign Squall]] - Examine a shard of lightning to begin this quest.   <br>*After completing all 6 Item Triggered Quests an NPC will appear in the Hypogeum named Kelymir Vallek, upon hailing him and having completed the 6 Item Triggered Quests he gives you the title Challenger of Kael, 10p, and 5 Primal Velium Shards. Additionally, finishing all 6 Item Triggered Quests as well as all 6 Object Triggered Quests you will be rewarded with an additional title Ragebourne, 10p, and 10 Primal Velium Shards.

## Object Triggered Quests

These quests are started by interacting with an object in the zone. They are repeatable every 18 hours and each rewards a Primal Velium Shard.

1. [95] [[How Noble is Our Cause]] - Examine the bust of Rallos Zek in Grand Kael to begin this quest.
1. [95] [[Strength of an Empire]] - Examine a statue in New Kael to begin this quest.
1. [95] [[Test of Military]] - Examine the bust of Rallos Zek in Iceshard Keep to begin this quest.
1. [95] [[War Rules All]] - Examine a statue in the Throne of Storms to begin this quest.
1. [95] [[Will Over the Weak]] - Examine a statue in Old Kael to begin this quest.
1. [97] [[Kneel Before Zek]] - Examine the bust of Rallos Zek in the Temple of Rallos Zek to begin this quest.    <br>*After completing all 6 Object Triggered Quests an NPC will appear in the Hypogeum named Kelymir Vallek, upon hailing him and having completed the 6 Object Triggered Quests he gives you the title Kromzek Slayer, 10p, and 5 Primal Velium Shards. Additionally, finishing all 6 Item Triggered Quests as well as all 6 Object Triggered Quests you will be rewarded with an additional title Ragebourne, 10p, and 10 Primal Velium Shards.
