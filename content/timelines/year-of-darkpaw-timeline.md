## Notes

- Event in Everquest 2. part of Year of Darkpaw: An EverQuest® Celebration.
- Start date January 1st 2024 - planned to be a year long event in 2024 every month up until September
- [Year of Darkpaw starts NOW!](https://www.everquest2.com/news/eq2-year-of-darkpaw-january-2024)
- [[Year of Darkpaw Crafting Timeline]]

## General Information

- From  announcement on forums: "The Year of Darkpaw: An EverQuest® Celebration will start with Masquerades of Divinity where heralds to the Avatars of the Gods will make an appearance in various areas across Norrath.
- These heralds can be aided to help bring forth the Avatars themselves to be challenged as part of the celebration. These Avatars include familiar forms you have seen in the past, as well as some that have never been challenged.
- A new herald will appear at the beginning and at the middle of each month, two different heralds will appear every month up until September, and other surprises to be revealed.
- Completing tasks/missions will progress the Avatar’s camp, which needs to be max level to call forth the Avatar. Challenging these avatars can garner unique and powerful weapons to be earned.
- Don’t miss out on buying the ability to teleport to each celebration camp directly! This ability can be bought at each camp and is only available for the first month that Avatar’s camp is active.

Starting January 1 2024,  find the [[Herald of the Fathom Lord]] within Mystic Lake. Then in the middle of January find the [[Herald of the Wintry Guardian]] in Everfrost.

### Research

All Heralds have 5 items to research. These are not items a player can receive, these items just serve as a mechanism to unlock purchasing items on each camp's merchant and to unlock Avatar encounter.  Completing missions for each Herald slightly helps progress of the research. Once all items are researched the Heralds have an option to keep doing missions to maintain the camp or to challenge an Avatar. Avatar instances are a group and/or raid encounters, bring friends!

On the ground, they look like a small yellow/orange Darkpaw logo; on the [[Track Harvestables]] window, they show as** !**  (exclamation mark).
