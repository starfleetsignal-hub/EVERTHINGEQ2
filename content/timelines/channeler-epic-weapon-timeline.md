## Requirements

- You must at least be a Lvl 95 Channeler.
- You must speak [[To Speak as a Dragon|Draconic]], [[Sathirian]], [[Death's Whisper]], [[Koada'Dal]], and [[Krombral]] languages.
- You must have done the [[Deathtoll Access|Deathtoll]] Access Quest to receive the [[Marked Flameshield of Scale]].
- You must have the investigation skill to 9 or more (obtained by completing the first chapter of [[Sword of Destiny Timeline]])
- You must have done the [[Shades of Drinal Timeline|Chain of Eternity Timeline]] for a legitimate access to Harrow End ( the key doesn't work )
- You must have completed the [[Dark Mail Gauntlets Timeline|Dark Mail Gauntlets]] Heritage Quest

## Quest starter

1. Go to Paineel (Sundered Frontier). In the same room as Sa'ib Waseem (Epic Repercussions NPC), go to {{waypoint 1986.34, -298.22, 3378.24}} Click the 4th book from the right on the middle book case section, 4 shelves up.
1. This will open a portal behind the pillar to the back-left.
1. Click the portal to enter Ryizz'Eianshieoll's Sanctum
1. Inside, go to the bookshelf at the back-right. There is a small book at {{waypoint -17.83, -14.49, -358.81}} Click that to pick it up.
1. Read the book to start your quest!
