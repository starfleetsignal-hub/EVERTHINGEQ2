The [[Gods]] have returned to [[Norrath]]! As a conscious creation of the world, it is your innate ability to choose who you worship. Depending on your chosen race, some gods will turn favorably to your supplications, others will be indifferent yet appreciative, and other will disregard them entirely. In order to begin the questline for a particular deity, you must first speak with their chosen deity historian or prophet.
