## Starting the Quests

- All players will receive an in-game mail from [[Bailin Grove the Elder|Bailin Grove]] to start the new quests in Zek, the Scourge Wastes.

*From: Bailin Grove*
*Subject: Your Help Is Needed*<br><br>
*[Scrawled in some kind of red ink:]<br>Proven friend of the Treefolke, this burl comes to you out of desperation! Grasp it tightly, so that we may speak. Our very survival rests with you!*<br><br>
*~Bailin Grove*

- Right-click **a Glowing burl** that came attached to the message to summon [[Bailin Grove the Elder]], who gives you the quest.

## Notes

- You need to be at least level 100 Adventurer to start the Signature Questline.
- You do not need to be a paid AllAccess member in order to access this content. (This content went free to play with GU103 on May 9 2017.)
- Once you finish [[Balin Grove the Elder (Zek, the Scourge Wastes)|Balin's]] initial quest, other characters on your account will be able to bypass that first quest if you wish, and access thereafter to the zone is via the Ulteran spires.
- If you don't get the starting email from Bailin, or you  delete it, speak with Jerol <Norrathian Express> in the [[The Village of Shin|Village of Shin]] in Mara.
- You must speak [[Words of Pure Magic|Druzaic]] for the quest [[Diviners and Conquer]]
