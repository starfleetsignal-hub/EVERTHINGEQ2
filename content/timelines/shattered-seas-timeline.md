## Notes

- For **tradeskill** quests see the [[Altar of Malice Crafting Timeline]].
- For **collection** quests, see the [[Altar of Malice Collection Timeline]]
- For the **missions** see [[Far Seas Ferrin Mission System]]
  - *There are overland solo missions and solo/adv solo/heroic/epic missions for a daily and weekly duration available*

## About the questline

The main quests of the questline start with "Shattered Seas: ", during those signature quests you'll often have to complete some solo quests.<br>
There are also some other solo quests avalible that don't count for the "Shattered Seas: " signature, but they give gear/jewelry and may unlock items at the armor trading merchant in [[Phantom Sea]].
