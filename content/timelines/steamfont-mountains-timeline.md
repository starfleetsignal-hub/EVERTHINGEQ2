As [[Butcherblock Mountains]] is to the dwarves, [[Steamfont Mountains]] is to the gnomes. Here you will aid the Gnomeland Security Forces, primarily based out out [[Gnomeland Security Headquarters]], as they seek to stabilize the region in the wake of [[The Rending]], and the peculiar uprising of their [[Klak'Anon|robotic creations]].
