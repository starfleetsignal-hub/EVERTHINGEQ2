This short timeline is a series of four quests released in [[Update:12|LU12]]. They detail the reappearance of roekillik, pale-ratonga in the service of the drakota. Their motives are unknown, but they have been seen digging at three peculiar locations: [[Bloodskull Valley]], [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]], and [[Permafrost]]. [[Pearl Honeywine]], the famous [[Qeynos|Qeynosian]] anthropologist, fears that the drakota are searching for artifacts that will allow them to manipulate a spell called 'The Harness', which was used by the dragons in ages past to subdue them. Should the drakota master this spell, they could free themselves from their dragonic servitude and reek havoc upon the denizens of [[Norrath]].

To begin the quest series speak to one of these two NPCs, based on your moral-alignment:
***Good-alignment:*** [[Pearl Honeywine]] in [[Qeynos Province District]] at {{waypoint 695, -21, -59}}
***Evil-alignment:*** [[Mareva D'Iniv]] in [[The City of Freeport]] at {{waypoint 32, -7, -130}}
