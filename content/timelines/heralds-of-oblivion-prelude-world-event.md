### Elemental Rifts

Elemental rifts open in 41 zones, each rift will let out a mini wave of elementals.  x4 normal mobs culminating with a more powerful boss-like elemental, after which the rift itself becomes a mob that can be closed (and close it by using a tool received from quest, DO NOT engage in combat with the rift itself, you cannot kill it). Killing each normal elemental spawns x2 [[Chaotic Elemental Filament]], a wandering passive mob, (the 'mini-bosses' spawns x3) which can be 'picked up' and used as a crafting component. These wanderers occasionally give a piece of [[Rarefied Chaos Materials]] collection (from Phase I rifts) or [[From Beyond]] collection (from Phase II rifts) in addition to the normal filament. Rifts respawn about 4 - 5 minutes after closing. Some rifts have [[A Flamebearer]] NPC nearby, he gives repeatable quest [[Relentless Defender]].

Even more rifts opened in Phase II of event, in the same zones with different mobs, but mechanics are the same. They also update the repeatable quest from [[A Flamebearer|Flamebearers]]. Mobs from Phase II rifts occasionally drop Void appearance weapons.
*Word of caution: The mini-bosses will cast a decently strong knockback when they are defeated. If you happen to be near a cliff (or there are some red ^ ^ ^'s wandering nearby), you WILL be knocked in that direction if you're not paying attention to your position.*
