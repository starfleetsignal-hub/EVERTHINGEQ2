### Mercenaries

Each different mercenary you have can contribute to that seasons Mercenary Battalion Buff.  For each 5 points you level a mercenary it contributes a fixed amount based on quality of the mercenary.

- Treasured - 1 point
- Legendary - 2 points
- Fabled - 3 points
- Celestial - 4 points

When the sum total of all your mercenaries contribution points reaches 20, 40, 60, 80 or 100 the buff gets a significant improvement

The mercenaries listed below are part of the Season 8 Mercenary collection that either come with the expansion or are able to be researched.  Together, once each is leveled to 20, the buff will top out at 60 Contribution points. However, to level to the maximum contribution of 100 you will need to purchase Mercenary crates from the marketplace and then level some of them in increments of 5 as well.  Note since leveling a mercenary takes the same amount of potions no matter the quality of the mercenary, many people will buy a crate to get better quality mercenaries to level and they will never level the treasured ones.

- *Duru Tha (Celestial)*, *Inquisitor*, in Val'Zyroth Ness at {{waypoint -901, -0, -66}}
