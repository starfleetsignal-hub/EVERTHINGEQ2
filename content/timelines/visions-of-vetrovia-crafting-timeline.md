## Tradeskill Missions

Once the *Were is the Messenger* questline has been completed, daily and weekly tradeskill missions can be obtained from [[Igore]] and [[Renfry]] in [[Renfry's Basement]]. You can use your [[Renfry's Basement Bauble]] to get there quickly from anywhere. Each quest will require and grant an [[Illusion: Werewolf]], but should you require another, you can purchase one from [[Ziggy]] for 1p.
