A great fae hero who was slain by [[Emperor D'Vinn]] in the War of Fay during the defense of [[Greater Faydark]] had her spirit bound to her sword upon death. Rather than become a sapling or budding flower as most faes, her spirit refused, and became encased within her blade. Only a righteous defender of the fae will be able to allow her spirit to be born anew.

This signature quest series results in the [[Eitholi, Blade of the Fae]].
