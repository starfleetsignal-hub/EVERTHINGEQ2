## Overview

This timeline is for obtaining access to [[Sokokar|sokokar post network]] for travel throughout Kunark.

- Both quest lines start the [[Kylong Plains Timeline]].
- All of the quests are soloable and start at Dreg's Landing in [[Kylong Plains]].
- Both quest lines will appear in your Journal under the Hallmark category.

## Requirements

You can chose one of two versions of the timeline.

- Your adventure **or** tradeskill must be a minimum of 65 to get the quests for either version.
- If you are at or above 65 in both adventure *and* tradeskill, you can choose which to pursue by selecting the right NPC to start the quest series with and do not need to complete both series.

### Adventurer Version

Starts with [[Borbin Happens]] in the [[Kylong Plains Timeline]]

### Tradeskill Version

- If your adventurer level is below that of the zone, the crafting version of this quest is recommended before attempting to complete the [[Tradeskill Timeline|tradeskill epic quests]].
- Before traveling to Kylong Plains, see the first quest's page for a list of supplies to bring along.

Starts with [[Jones]] in the [[Kylong Plains Timeline]]

## Quest Series Rewards

- The ability to travel in Kunark by using the sokokars.
- You will also receive a "fluff" pet, [[A young sokokar]], that you can summon to follow you.
