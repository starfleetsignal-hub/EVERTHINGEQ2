## Note

- For tradeskill related solo quests see the [[The Withered Lands Crafting Timeline]].
- For Heroic quests in Skyshrine see [[Skyshrine Timeline]]
- You need to be at least level 90 adventurer to start this quest line. If you are close, you can use [[Loose Teeth Everywhere]] to grind to 90. At 85, you'll only be able to complete [[Field Marshall Vishra]]'s quests and the previously mentioned repeatable.
- There is a dragon that flies overhead and occasionally knocks you out of the sky if you leap, glide or fly in the zone.  The dragon is not attackable, but there are two ways to stop this: one is to complete [[The Call Home]] quest, and the other is to complete the crafting timeline.  The crafting timeline is much quicker, but requires that you be a 90 crafter.
- The Withered Lands can be accessed from the [[Thurgadin Harbor]] in [[Great Divide]] by speaking to the New Combine Flight Master at {{waypoint 1278, -540, 306}}
