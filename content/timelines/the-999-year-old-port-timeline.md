This quest series is begun by speaking to [[Sparrow Cogcarrier (Antonica)|Sparrow Cogcarrier]] [[Antonica]] (for good characters) or  [[Heron Cogcarrier (Commonlands)|Heron Cogcarrier]] [[The Commonlands]] (for evil characters). There are different versions of the first quest for each side, but the last two are the same for both.

1. [[The 999 year old port]] (20) *in [[Antonica]] or [[The Commonlands]]*
1. [[Getting the Smarmy Sprocket Underway]] (25) *takes you to the [[Aboard the Smarmy Sprocket]]*
1. [[Save the Pirates]] (34) *takes you to the [[Prison of Admiral Grimgadget]]*

While in the [[Prison of Admiral Grimgadget]], you can receive the quest [[Bootstrutter's Trail Guide to Ro]] (50) from [[Jergo Wheybringer]].
