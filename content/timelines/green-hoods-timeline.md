This Timeline involves solo and heroic quests, and one Heritage Quest, in [[Zek, the Orcish Wastes]]. These quests begin at the [[Warship Dock]] at level 39 and conclude with heroic forays into [[Deathfist Citadel]] at levels 40 to 42.

## The Green Hoods

1. [[Operation Green Hood]] (39) - *from [[Shadow (NPC)|Shadow]] at the [[Warship Docks]]*
   - [[Into the Vallon Foundry]] (34) - *from [[The Glademaster]]*
1. [[Give the key to the Glademaster]] (31) or [[Give the key to Captain Vulis]] (30) (see Notes, below)
1. [[The Rescue of the Green Hoods]] (40 Heroic) - *from [[Sashra Thaltalis]], [[Short Sword of the Ykesha]] HQ*

### [[Deathfist Citadel]]

These quests may all be done at the same time. I suggest you get them when you start [[The Rescue of the Green Hoods]]!

- [[The Search for the Marshall]] (40 Heroic) - *from [[Freeboard]] at [[Three Toes Valley]]*
- [[Crippling the Defenses]] (40 Heroic) - *from [[Angler]] at [[Three Toes Valley]]*
- [[Usurping an Emperor]] (42 Heroic) - *from [[Pebbles]] at [[Three Toes Valley]]*

## Notes

- After [[Operation Green Hood]] you have a choice to make. You can go talk to [[The Glademaster]], in which case you will get the quest [[Give the key to the Glademaster]]. Pressing "Accept" on the New Quest window confirms that you ARE giving him the key. If, however, you speak first with [[Captain Vulis]] you get a different quest, [[Give the key to Captain Vulis]]. Either way, pressing accept completes the quest, you choose your reward, and move on to [[Sashra Thaltalis]] and [[The Rescue of the Green Hoods]].
