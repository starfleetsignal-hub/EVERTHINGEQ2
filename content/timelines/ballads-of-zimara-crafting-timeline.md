## Advanced Tradeskill Books Research

The Tradeskill Researcher is [[Researcher Uutecka]], located in [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] at {{waypoint 327.51, 286.46, 965.90}}, who allows you to obtain the advanced recipe books. Talk to Uutecka to start any Advanced Research. Examining a Ballads of Zimara Tradeskill Research Material will reduce the time to finish researching.

## Tradeskill Researcher Missions

These become available upon completing [[Test Your Mettle: Aetheric Safety]].

1. [[Disassemble: No!]]
1. [[Disassemble: A Rocky Beginning]]
1. [[Disassemble: Hide if You Can]]
1. [[Disassemble: Tricking the Eyes]]
1. [[Disassemble: A Different Approach]]

The 'daily' research mission becomes available after completing [[Disassemble: A Different Approach]]. The mission timer resets **every 11 hours after completion,** allowing a character to complete it twice a day, and +500 faction with **Sky Watcher Sodality** and [[Ballads of Zimara Tradeskill Research Material (1 Day)]]. You also get Status rewarded but only the first time the quests are done.

- **Tradeskill Missions [[Burnish (Tradeskill Missions)|Burnish]]** is located in [[Aether Wroughtlands]] at {{waypoint 693.87, -46.35, -440.29}}, with the portal from [[Splendor Sky Aerie]] to [[Zimara Breadth]] on [[Molted Island]] at {{waypoint -1091, 181, 736}}, transporting the player by turning around and using the portal at Zimara Breadth once more to go to [[Aether Wroughtlands]]. Once at the bivouac, [[Merchant Orikoo]] {{waypoint 721, -44, -455}} sells the [[Will of the Wroughtlands]] baubles for 2p 40g each that instantly transports the player back to the bivouac.
  - [[Bivouac: Barricades]]
  - [[Bivouac: Healing]]
  - [[Bivouac: Provisions]]
  - [[Bivouac: Repairs]]

The daily research mission is on a timer that is universal for your server. To see how much time until the mission resets, go into your quest journal, click the "Mission Timers" tab at the top, filter for Ballads of Zimara, and type in the word "Tradeskill" in the filter by name bar at the bottom.

Although not physically in Zimara, as the entrance is in [[Antonica]] near [[Steppes Station]] at Darkpaw Dugout {{waypoint -2133.23, -30.47, 675.67}}, the "Darkpaw: Sabotage" Tradeskill Daily Mission shares the Ballads of Zimara  mission timer filter. While completing Sabotage and Circuses, the daily research mission, Flea-Bitten Sabotage, becomes available by simply entering the Darkpaw: Sabotage [Tradeskill] zone.

1. [[Sabotage and Circuses]]

The 'daily' research mission resets every 18 hours. Completing Sabotage and Circuses allows crafting special items helpful for adventuring in [[Darkpaw Warrens (Solo)]]. Completing the daily awards Sabotage Silver currency.

- **Saboteur [[Rinndle]]** inside Darkpaw Dugout {{waypoint -15.82, -69.69, -295.14}} gives you the Sabotage and Circuses quest the very first time. The nearby ladder allows entrance to [[Darkpaw: Sabotage (Tradeskill)]] {{waypoint -7.20, -61.82, -323.64}}. In Darkpaw Dugout, Blackburrow Bartender [[Bernard]] {{waypoint -6.45, -76.84, -243.01}} allows fast travel to Dawkpaw Dugout for players with the [[Stein of Recollection]] or [[Dhalgar's Last Call]].
  - Flea-Bitten Sabotage

For more information, see [[Darkpaw: Sabotage (Tradeskill)]] and [[Darkpaw Warrens Maps]].
