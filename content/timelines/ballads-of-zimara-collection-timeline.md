## Collection Researcher

[[Researcher Magpie]] in [[Sanctuary (POI)|Sanctuary]], [[Splendor Sky Aerie]], at {{waypoint 379.25, 286.72, 943.22}} provides the no-trade items to complete collections. Each no-trade Item requires:

- 2 days to research
- Cost 50p 325,000 status

Refer to the [[Researcher Magpie]] entry for details of the no-trade Items and the associated Collections.

## Shiny Trader

[[Merchant Ragoof]], in [[Sanctuary (POI)|Sanctuary]], [[Splendor Sky Aerie]], at {{waypoint 223, 287, 946}} will exchange fertilizer items from Ballads of Zimara for collection items. They want one of each of the following per shiny:

- [[A pristine aviak claw]]
- [[A pristine aviak wing]]
- [[A spotted hooluk egg]]
