Despite it's name, [[Jarsath Wastes]] is not a wasteland at all. In this timeline you will assist the The Sel'Nok Brigade establish a foothold, help Clan Skleross contend with their degenerate Durus brothers, establish trust with the Legion of Danak, and hunt all manners of creatures with Clan Stormshield.

Like most of the timelines in [[Kunark]], this timeline is split between the factions for whom you will be assisting. Each NPC listed indicates the starting place for a questline, not necessarily the only NPC involved in that questline. A logical place to begin is with the quest, [[Snooping in Jarsath]], given by [[Associate Tuz]] in [[Kunzar Jungle]].

## Worker Sledgemallet

The reward for completing this questline is the [[Worker Sledgemallet (Level 80)|Worker Sledgemallet]], a unique item that has a 50% chance to proc ***Overthere*** on a combat hit, which upon termination will teleport the player to [[Danak Shipyards]]. This is of particular use to classes that do not have an evac spell, since the teleportation can occur while in combat. To unlock this questline, you must have at least 40,000 faction with [[The Sel'Nok Brigade (Faction)|The Sel'Nok Brigade]], Clan Skleross and Legion of Danak.
