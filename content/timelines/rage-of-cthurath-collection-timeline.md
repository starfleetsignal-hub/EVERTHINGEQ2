## Overview

This timeline gives details about the [[Collection]] quests introduced as part of the Rage of Cthurath expansion.<br>
The first collection you turn in will reward you with adornment [[Spiritstone of the Void Collector]]. Once equipped this spiritstone will level up every time you turn in a Rage of Cthurath collection.

## Shiny trader

- [[Doreen Ercel]] at {{waypoint -851, -47, 945}} in [[The Unknown]] appears once you have completed [[All the Rage]] achievement.

Will trade a random collectible from overland, solo, or heroic collections for a set of 3 different plant fertilizers (body drops in Rage of Cthurath zones). You need to have in your inventory:

- 1 x [[Oogothlan Dahlia]]
- 1 x [[Oogothlan Poppy]]
- 1 x [[Oogothlan Dark Lotus]]

## Orange Collections

Every collectible in these collections is distinguished with an orange name (not an orange ground spawn), and each is looted from the chest of a different boss. Completing either [[Entropy and Madness (Collection)|Entropy and Madness]] or [[The Book of Cthurath]] is required to see these "shinies" within the boss' chest.

- [[Affix of the Infernal Tyrant (Collection)|Affix of the Infernal Tyrant]]
- [[Cloak of the Quintet (Collection)|Cloak of the Quintet]]
- [[Ring of Cosmic Horrors (Collection)|Ring of Cosmic Horrors]]

Beginning with [[Revelations of Malice]], these shinies are also available in a choice box from the GU solo and heroic weeklies.
