This timeline gives the details of the collection quests introduced as part of the Terrors of Thalumbra expansion. These collections listed here are part of the overall list of collections available in EverQuest II, which are listed at [[Collection Quests Timeline]].

## Summary

This is a summary of the main hierarchy of collection quests added as part of the Terrors of Thalumbra expansion.

(1) There are thirteen (standard yellow) shiny collections (numbers 1-13 below) completed from ground spawns in the Thalumbra outdoor zone. These allow the completion of the [[Banners of Thalumbra]] meta-collection, and earn the ***Indicium of the Masterful*** for the final collection.

(2) There are seven (blue) shiny collections (numbers 14-20 below) completed from ground spawns in the Thalumbra outdoor zone. These allow the completion of the [[Masterful Banners of Thalumbra]] meta-collection, and earn the ***Indicium of Thalumbra*** for the final collection. [Note that these shinies only become visible after completing the [[Banners of Thalumbra]] meta-collection described in (1) above, although they can still be acquired from other players without completing (1) above.]

(3) There are six shiny collections (numbers 21-26 below) completed in the AOM Advanced Solo zones. These allow the completion of the [[Medallions of Thalumbra]] meta-collection, and earn the ***Indicium of the Underfoot*** for the final collection.

(4) There are five shiny collections (numbers 27-31 below) completed in the Maldura Heroic Instances. These allow the completion of the [[Tokens of the Dhalgar]] meta-collection, and earn the ***Indicium of the Dhalgar*** for the final collection.

(5) There are five shiny collections (numbers 32-36 below) completed in the Stygian Threshold Heroic Instances. These allow the completion of the [[Stygian Threshold Keepsakes]] meta-collection, and earn the ***Indicium of the Stygian Threshold*** for the final collection.

(6) There are five shiny collections (numbers 37-41 below) completed in the Kralet Penumbra Heroic Instances. These allow the completion of the [[Aberrant Idols]] meta-collection, and earn the ***Indicium of the Aberrant*** for the final collection.

(7) There are six shiny collections (numbers 42-47 below) completed in the Kralet Penumbra Contested zone. These allow the completion of the [[Memories of Kralet]] meta-collection, and earn the ***Indicium of Kralet Penumbra*** for the final collection.

Each of the seven steps results in the completion of one meta-collection. These steps can be completed in any order. The seven meta collections (numbers 48-54 below) each give one item for the final collection (number 55 below).

Other than this main hierarchy of collections, there are few unrelated collections also available, listed after the first 55 below.
