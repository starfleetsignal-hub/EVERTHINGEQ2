## Notes

This series of quests will take a beginning artisan from level 1 through level 9, giving the artisan experience with the various crafting professions, the first 7 recipe books, a 10 slot harvesting bag, and a crafting tunic with bonuses. Optionally, you may also receive a 36 slot Harvest Storage Bank Box. When done, the artisan will be ready to talk to a Crafting Trainer to choose a crafting class.

The timeline is a part of the larger [[Tradeskill Timeline]].

## Starting Zones

You will need to visit a Tradeskill Tutorial to get your first assignment; they can be found in most of the [[Tradeskill Instance|tradeskill instances]] within the major cities. In Qeynos and Freeport proper, you can go to the outdoor crafting areas in North Qeynos or West Freeport. The separate Tradeskill Instance zones in North Qeynos and West Freeport do **not** have a Tradeskill Tutorial.

1. Speak with any Crafting Trainer to receive artisan level 2. This is optional.
1. Speak with [[Thrawk For'Ork]] in [[Gorowyn Beach]] for the quest [1] [[Becoming a Crafter]]. (Available to all characters.) This is optional.
1. Speak with Tradeskill Tutorial for the [[Tutorial: Learning to Harvest]] (adventurer level 2) before starting with the actual crafting. This is optional.
1. [5] [[If I Had A Hammer]] This is optional.
1. [3] [[Tutorial: Forging Ahead]]
1. [4] [[Tutorial: Countering Problems]]
1. [5] [[Tutorial: Learning to Cook]]
1. [6] [[Tutorial: The Art of Weapons]]
1. [7] [[Tutorial: Scribing Scrolls]]
1. [8] [[Tutorial: Alchemical Experiments]]
1. [9] [[Tutorial: Essential Outfitting]]

- 7 [[Tin cluster]]s
- 6 [[Roots]]
- 5 [[Severed elm]]
- 5 [[Lead cluster]]s
- 4 [[Jumjum]]
- 5 [[Rawhide leather pelt]]s
- 3 [[Rough malachite]]
- 2 [[Leaded loam]]

- 1 [[Aerated mineral water]]
- 1 [[Basic Kindling]]
- 2 [[Basic Coal]]
- 1 [[Basic Sandpaper]]
- 1 [[Basic Incense]]
- 1 [[Basic Candle]]
- 1 [[Basic Filament]]

Plus 1 [[Tin cluster]] and 1 [[Lead cluster]] if you plan on doing [[If I Had A Hammer]].
