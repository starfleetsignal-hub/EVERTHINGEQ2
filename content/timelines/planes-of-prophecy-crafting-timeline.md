### Requirements

- You need to be at least level 100 Crafter to start the questline.
- Level 1 Adventurer is enough to access the zones for the questline.
  - Previously, while level 1 adventurers could technically access the zones, the zones were still populated with *hostile* mobs of all kinds at or above level 110.  As of 2023 this is no longer the case, as nearly 'all' of the mobs you will encounter (especially trash mobs), while still high level, will be non-hostile by default (with even more mob hostilities 'turned off' in 2025). *Any walkthrough steps mentioning devices to craft or tactics needed to evade/bypass said hostiles are now obsolete* and can be safely ignored (so don't waste materials) - only a small number of notable exceptions still exist, and are entirely contained in Part V involving the Plane of Hate, but those walkthroughs have already been updated with the 'new' 2023/2025 sidenotes/hostility info, so you will be made aware of them if you're following the wiki walkthroughs. Environmental hazards DO still apply, however; so don't attempt to walk through toxic steam or bathe in lava - you know - common sense stuff ;-)

### Travel

![Teleport pad in the Plane of Magic](images/Planes-of-magic-teleport-pad.jpg)
To travel to the Plane of Magic, players must go to a [[Ulteran Spires|spire]] in another zone (e.g. [[Enchanted Lands]]) or if a player is in a guild that has it, use the amenity in the form of the spire or wizard that creates a portal in the [[Guild Halls|guild hall]]. The new zone's name will appear in the center of the map after interacting with the portal.

- Within the Plane of Magic, players can use a [[Flying Mounts|flying mount]] to go from island to island or a teleport pad, similar to those found in other zones (e.g. Neriak).
- There is no level requirement to enter the zone.
