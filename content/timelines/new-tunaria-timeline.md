[[New Tunaria]], or Felwithe as it was once called, is a city founded on reverence towards the god of the Renda'Dal, [[Tunare]]. While there are many followers to the goddess of Growth, the Renda'Dal have chosen devotion and purity as their means to express their love toward her. Their strict adherence to purity has led them to take on an anti-foreigner culture, where any outsider is deemed as unclean.
