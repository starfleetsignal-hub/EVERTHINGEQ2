## About

***Craftsman Timeline*** includes tradeskill quests at tradeskill levels 20, 35, 45, 55 and 60. They are all gained from your crafting guild's [[Tradeskilling Factions]] headquarters, from the Trades Coordinator NPCs (with *Grandmaster* in their names). The timeline is a part of [[Tradeskill Timeline]].

## Quests

1. [[Journeyman Service]] (20) - harvesting in the T3 zone [[The Thundering Steppes]]
1. [[Tradesman Service]] (35) - crafting
1. [[Advanced Tradesman Service]] (45) - crafting
1. [[Advanced Journeyman Tasks]] (55) - talking to a NPC in the T6 zone [[The Sinking Sands]] and crafting; harvestables can be bought.
1. [[Senior Crafter Service]] (60) - harvesting and collecting items and objects in the T7 zone [[Tenebrous Tangle]] and crafting

Starting from level 20 and all the way to level 80, you can also do City Tradeskill Tasks Quests (another article: Tradeskill Writs) that will earn you tradeskill experience, personal and guild [[Status points]] and money.

For more tradeskill quests, see Tradeskill Quests.
