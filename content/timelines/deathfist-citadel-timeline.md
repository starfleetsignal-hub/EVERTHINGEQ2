- *This is a master quest list that excludes any holiday/event related quests.*
- *All quests listed here, unless noted, can be started and completed as of June 2018.*

[[Deathfist Citadel]] is the main fortress of the Deathfist Empire. Here resides [[Emperor Fyst]] and the other elite members of his clan. It is advised that any adventurer brave enough to enter here first complete the [[Zek Timeline]] to pick up any relevant quests as well as understand the themes surrounding the dungeon better. Lastly, there are no dropped quests, so you don't need to check corpses or chests for quest starters.
