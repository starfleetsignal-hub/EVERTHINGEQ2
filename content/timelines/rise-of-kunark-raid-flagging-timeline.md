This quest series gives lore background for the Rise of Kunark Raid progression and sends you through the various zones of [[Rise of Kunark]].  Most quests are epic, but a few are soloable or have soloable components. You must be level 80 to begin this raid progression quest.

1. [[The Fate of Norrath]] (Kunzar Jungle)
1. [[The Amulet of Drolakis]] (The Moppet Shoppe)
1. [[Into the Bowels of the Beast]] (The Moppet Shoppe)
1. [[The Secret of the Stone]] (The Moppet Shoppe)
   1. [[Taking on Trakanon]] (Veeshan's Peak)
   1. [[Mending the Drudge]] (Veeshan's Peak)

Only the quest 4-1 [[Taking on Trakanon]] is an actual visible access quest.  The others are **not** needed to enter the different raid zones.  Instead the access to these zones are gained by a "secret" flag you get upon killing the mobs some of the quests do ask for. The quest [[Mending the Drudge]] gives access to the mender within Veeshan's Peak.

## Tier 1

Anyone can enter regardless of previous kills or finished quests.

- [[The Protector's Realm]] --> [[Imzok's Revenge]]
  - Gives you [[The Greenmist Orb]] which you need to successfully kill The Leviathan.
- [[The Tomb of Thuuga]] --> [[Tairiza the Widow Mistress]]
  - Gives you [[Mistmyrian Soulcube]] which you need to successfully kill Venril Sathir.
- [[The Execution Throne Room]] --> [[Pawbuster]]
  - Gives you [[Isydryl's Wand of Intervention]] which you need to successfully kill Overking Selrach Di'Zok.

## Tier 2

Anyone can enter regardless of previous kills or finished quests.

- [[The Temple of Kor-Sha]] --> Overking [[Selrach Di'Zok]]
- [[Venril Sathir's Lair]] --> [[Venril Sathir]]

## Tier 3

Need to have killed Overking Selrach Di'Zok ***and*** Venril Sathir to zone in by yourself.  Anyone can enter if zoned in by someone having done those two kills.

- [[The Chamber of Destiny]] --> [[The Leviathan]]

## Tier 4

<s>Need to have killed The Leviathan to enter.</s>

Access not needed due to an old update.

- [[Veeshan's Peak]]
  1. West wing:
     - [[Kluzen the Protector]], [[Nexona]], [[Elder Ekron]], and [[Druushk]]
     - Killing all the named mobs in the west wing opens the entrance to the north wing.
  1. North wing:
     - [[Taskmaster Nichok]], [[Milyex Vioren]], [[Xygoz]], [[Cinder Wasp Queen]] + [[Qunard Ashenclaw]], and [[Hoshkar]]
     - Killing all the named mobs in the north wing opens the entrance to the east wing.
  1. East wing:
     - [[Travenro the Skygazer]], [[Silverwing]], and [[Shade of Khalan Dar]] + [[Phara Dar]]

## Tier 5

Need to have finished [[Taking on Trakanon]] to enter.  This quest can be started without having done any of the previous quests.

- [[Trakanon's Lair]] --> [[Trakanon]]
