## Overview

This timeline gives details about the [[Collecting|collection]] quests introduced as part of the Scars of Destruction expansion.

## Adornment

Completing the first collection gives the green adornment [[Spiritstone of Collected Scars]], completing more collections increases it's spirit level.

## Research

[[Researcher Sprunglock]] at [[Port Woe]] in [[Sodden Archipelago]] can research the "no-trade" collection items of this collections

## Shiny Trader

[[Spelth Cruncher]] <Shiny Wanter> in Western Wastes at {{waypoint -615, 135, 419}} just inside the Chetari Passages cave.

Must complete adventure signature line, plus all dropped quest and all POI quests in both overland zones to see him.

Must have 1 of each foods below to exchange for one random collectible from Scars of Destruction

- [[A golden dankmar pear]]
- [[A melted-fields snow pear]]
- [[A wormy apple]]
