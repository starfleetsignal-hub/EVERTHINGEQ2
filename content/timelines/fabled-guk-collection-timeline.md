## Overview

This timeline gives details about the [[Collecting|collection]] quests introduced as part of the Fabled Guk zones part of the Planes of Prophecy expansion. Fabled Guk heroic zones may be run at three intensities: Heroic, Expert, or Frenzied, and different collections drop based on both zone and intensity.
