## Researcher Missions

It isn't listed as being in the crates or even as a quest reward possibility, however these missions do randomly reward Shadowscream Studies craft books in addition to the crate contents.

A few useful notes:

- [[Purihni "The Edge" Fordel III (The Bazaar)|Purihni "The Edge" Fordel III]], [[Gundeg "The Anvil" Midst (The Bazaar)|Gundeg "The Anvil" Midst]] and [[Erdani Ruilep]] reward with "[[Recuso Field Voucher]]" currency needed to buy the [[Recuso Specialized Reconnaissance Helmet]] which substantially boosts your chance of harvesting Rares, AND gave useful recipe books, some of which can contain recipes for "Grandmaster" rated spells/combat-arts.
- You can have more than one mission live at the same time, and each harvest will double-count against all of the active missions.
- There are delay timers between the completion on one mission for an NPC and when that same NPC will give you a new mission, and you can expect to wait about 30mins or-so before each one will light-up with a mission-feather and be willing to issue your next one.
- The missions are not sequential; you might be issued the same mission twice in a row by the same NPC, and never see others.
- While trying to earn [[Recuso Field Voucher|Recuso Field Vouchers]], a useful strategy to maximize your time is to have three missions running at once to get maximum harvest overlap. One possible sequence is:
  1. Fast travel to [[City of Fordel Midst]] then [[The Bazaar]] to the [[Great Artisan Hall]]
  1. Collect quests from:
     - [[Purihni "The Edge" Fordel III (The Bazaar)|Purihni "The Edge" Fordel III]] at  {{waypoint -51.83, -1.39, -9.15}}
     - [[Gundeg "The Anvil" Midst (The Bazaar)|Gundeg "The Anvil" Midst]] at {{waypoint -51.95, -1.39, 18.06}}
  1. Use the [[Will of the Weald]] (purchased from [[Vuchoz]]) to teleport to [[Savage Weald]] to collect a quest from [[Erdani Ruilep]] at {{waypoint -418.36, 97.06, -314.00}}.
  1. Gather materials. Craft the three items at the workstation next to [[Erdani Ruilep]].
  1. Complete quest with [[Erdani Ruilep]] then fast travel to [[City of Fordel Midst]] to complete the final two quests.
  1. Wait for timer to restart this sequence at Step 2.
