In this timeline you will discover the true intentions of the droag, and their dragon overlords. This is the final stop in the Kingdom of Sky expansion. It's high time to put an end to the [[The Awakened (Faction)|The Awakened]] and their allies.

This zone was revamped in 2010, and is now a suitable place for solo questing from levels 65 to 70. The questing is a mix of original quests from when Kingdom of Sky was released, and [[Update:55|LU55]] quests that often involve the same NPCs. As you move from island to island while completing the main questline, many secondary quests will become available.
