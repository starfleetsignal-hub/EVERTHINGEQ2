## Other Collections

- [[Corny Brigade]] - A rare shiny found in Grave Grange (all difficulties) that appears at the end of the instance. It may or may not appear. The reward is the fabled familiar, Colonel Cornelius Gold. This collection is not required for the final meta collection.

## Shiny Trader

[[Merchant Fiza Tazi]] in [[Raj'Dur Plateaus]] at [[Hopewell Harbor]] {{waypoint 121.13, -59.93, 500.76}} will exchange fertilizer items from Renewal of Ro for collection items. She wants one of each of the following per shiny:

- [[Red desert sand]]
- [[Chest of scorpion exoskeletons]]
- [[Desert charger horns]]

## Empire of Antiquity Collections, LU122

Unless otherwise noted, all LU122 collections are found in [[Buried Takish'Hiz: Empire of Antiquity (Contested)]].

### Yellow Collections

#### Solo

These collections are found in the Elddar Exchange region of the contested dungeon.

- [[Dunebug Organs]]
- [[Dunebug Parts]]
- [[Satyr Pipes]]

#### Heroic I

These collections are found in the Blooming Biome, Hallowed Hollow, and Den of Dawn regions of the contested dungeon.

- [[Renda'Dal Wands]]
- [[Renda'Dal Weapons]]
- [[Satyr Alcohol]]

#### Heroic II

These collections are found in the Blooming Biome, Den of Dawn, Wellspring of Renewal, and Chamber of Chronicles regions of the contested dungeon.

- [[Elddar Ghostly Vestments]]

#### Heroic III

These collections are found in the Sanctum Arcanum and Takish Archives regions of the contested dungeon.

- [[Bark of the Greenwood]]
- [[Elddar Ghostly Essence]]
- [[Werebear Teeth]]

#### Meta Collection

- [[Buried Takish'Hiz Treasures (Collection)|Buried Takish'Hiz Treasures]]

### Blue Collections

These collections can be found after completing [[Buried Takish'Hiz Treasures (Collection)|Buried Takish'Hiz Treasures]]. Each collectible spawns after defeating a boss in the contested dungeon, and it spawns near that boss's location.

- [[Blackspawn Trappings]]
- [[Darkmystic Trappings]]
- [[Shadowbane Trappings]]
- [[Watchress Trappings]]

- [[Duende Ancient Armaments]]
