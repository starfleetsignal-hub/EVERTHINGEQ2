## Requirements

- Receive a mail from Baniti:
  - ***Your invitation to Hopewell Harbor:***
  - *Tales of your accomplishments circulate, even amongst the nomad tribes of Ro. They bring hope to the hopeless and strength to the weary. That is why I write you. Following some discussions with my tribe's Qayyid, I have been given permission to extend to you an invitation to Hopewell Harbor, our tribe's refuge and caravanserai located along the southern most coast of the Desert of Ro in a land known as Raj'Dur Plateaus. The enchantment on this letter will allow you to see and use an unfettered carpet at the docks of Freeport or Qeynos. It will bring you to Hopewell Harbor. I hope you will join us, soon. Peace be upon you. --Baniti.*

## Music Box Quests

- Must have completed the [[Renewal of Ro Timeline#Renewal of Ro Signature Quest|Renewal of Ro Signature Quest]] line to receive these quests.
- The quest locations are music boxes, which may be found at high points in the various overland zones. The music boxes are NOT in a single fixed location, they may appear at various points.
- After completing a music box quest, going to another zone and killing specific creatures allows you to pick up the second, "key" quest. These are technically their own quests, but you CANNOT get them without being on the second phase of the music box quest.
- *All the names of the Music Boxes are various [Musical Modes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mode_(music)).*
- [[Music Box Quests At A Glance]]

### Basic Structure of Music Box Quests

1. Find the <X> Music Box in an overland zone. Music boxes are NOT in a fixed location, they move.
1. The quest will direct you to kill 15 of a certain mob to find a hint to where the clue for the location of the Music Box's key is.
   - *Note: This mob is in the same zone and will have a feather if you have the quest as active quest.*
   - *Note: Each correct encounter gives 1 Quest update => 1 Mob Encounter = 1 Quest update / 2-4 Mob Encounter = 1 Quest update*
1. Head to next zone. (Raj'Dur Plateus &rarr; Takish Badlands &rarr; Sandstone Delta &rarr; Raj'Dur Plateus).
1. Kill 'Clue Mob' until it drops the '<X> Music Box Key Clue' quest starter. (Clue Mobs do not have a feather.)
1. Inspect the quest starter and start the dropped quest.
1. Kill 15 more of the 'Clue Mob' to finish the dropped quest to get the key to music box and update POI quest. (Clue Mobs STILL don't have a feather, but now there is a counter in your quest helper window.)
   - *Note: Each correct encounter gives 1 Quest update => 1 Mob Encounter = 1 Quest update / 2-4 Mob Encounter = 1 Quest update*
1. Return to the previous zone and inspect <X> Music Box to finish the POI quest. Music boxes are NOT in a fixed location, they move.

## Empire of Antiquity, GU122

*A few months have passed since the buried Elddar city of Takish'Hiz was reopened, the defeat of Malachiel Caedor, the Terrene Tyrant, and the subsequent Renewal of Ro. The magical waters of Growth are flowing once more, revitalizing the flora within the buried city of lore. News of the events and the awe-inspiring city have spread across Norrath, whispers of power and potential found within have inspired treasure seekers, historians, and devoted worshipers to explore the sight for themselves. Unlock powerful artifacts and learn impressive arcanum as you explore the history of the Elddar Empire, battle aggressive aspects of Growth, and protect the site from terrors without and within!*
