This timeline covers the quests in the Bloodline Chronicles Adventure Pack. You will aid either *The Hand of Marr* or *The Brethren of Night* to accomplish their independent goals. *The Hand of Marr* remain dedicated to ideals of [[Mithaniel Marr (God)|Marr]] and seek to destroy the vampire abominations on moral grounds, while *The Brethren of Night* seek the source of the great power resonating from within the caves.

Regardless of which path you choose, the quest lines are nearly equivalent until the end of their respective series and can be done simultaneously by group mates.

## Main Quests

Started by clicking on the trapdoor (right-click and select "use") leading to the [[D'Morte Burial Chambers (Zone)|D'Morte Burial Chambers]] in [[Nektulos Forest]] at {{waypoint 678, 2, -617}} and receiving [[A D'Morte insignia band]]. You must be at least level 50 to receive the insignia. Examine it to begin the adventure!

## Standalone Adventures

The following are standalone quests, with no prerequisites. Each quest has a corresponding instance that is only available while the player is on the quest. To receive these quests, speak to [[Cryptomancer Nasrara]] or [[Investigator Dahno]] and choose either 'alone' or 'teamwork'. You will be randomly offered one of the two quests for the requested group size. If you are not offered the instance/quest you want, just decline the quest and speak to the NPC again. Although the quests from the different NPCs have the same name, they are different quests in your journal. There is a 2 hour cool-down timer after completing the quests before the NPC will offer you another quest. They are all unlimited repeatable.

## Notes

The minimum level requirement for Bloodline Chronicles was originally level 30, however it was changed to require a minimum of level 50 with the release of the Time-Locked Expansion (TLE) servers. Players under level 50 will not be able to obtain the D'Morte insignia band required to begin this quest timeline.

The monsters in this zone will range from level 51 to 56, most will be heroic and the zones are densely packed. If you are playing on a TLE server at level 50, this quest timeline will require a full group of players. If you are playing on a standard server, you can complete these quests at level 50 with a mercenary.

On TLE servers, Bloodline Chronicles and Splitpaw Saga are not immediately available when the server is first opened and the level cap is 50. The adventure packs are made available when the first mid-expansion content update is released for the server. The exact amount of time depends on what the interval is between expansions (they have varied from server to server over the years) but they are typically released midway to the scheduled release of the Desert of Flames expansion.

The Bloodline Chronicles adventure pack was originally purchased DLC, but it is now included in the base game and available to free-to-play players.
