## Prerequisites

Before you can complete this quest:

- You must have the [[Investigating]] skill. (You can purchase the investigation skill at [[Teren's Grasp]] from [[Juulika McDarid]] at {{waypoint 1838, 487, -840}} with 50,000 Faction to Terren's Grasp)
- You must have ~ 10 platinum and the following faction levels:
  - At least 40,000 faction with [[Riliss (Faction)|Riliss]] to buy the [[Grim Blade of Rile]].
  - At least 40,000 faction [[Synod Reet (Faction)|Synod Reet]] to buy the [[Krup Belt of Evasion]].
  - At least 50,000 faction with [[Residents of Teren's Grasp (Faction)|Residents of Teren's Grasp]] to buy [[Straightedge]].
- You must have completed [[A Mysterious Black Tome]] and the follow up quests to learn [[Sathirian]] in order to speak to the Shisik Translator.
- You must complete the Krombral language [[Words of a Giant]]
- You must have the ability to speak [[Draconic]].
- You need to be level 4 tradeskill, recommended level 9.
- [OPTIONAL] Recommended completing [[A Mysterious Green Tome (Collection)|A Mysterious Green Tome]] for a faster Synod Reet faction grind.
