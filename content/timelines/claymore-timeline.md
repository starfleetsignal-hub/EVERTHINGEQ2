The [[Qeynos Claymore (Weapon)|Qeynos Claymore]] has been abducted from the [[Qeynos Castle|palace vaults]]! For players of Good-alignment, it is your sworn duty as an agent of [[Queen Antonia Bayle]] to retrieve it. For players of Evil-aligment, [[Overlord Lucan D'Lere]] demands that you retrieve it, and who are you to argue with the will of the Overlord?

This is the main story timeline of the Kingdom of Sky expansion. It will take you across every major zone, dungeon, and raid.

## Warning about betraying

If you complete the first quest, [[On Her Majesty's Secret Service]]/[[On The Overlord's Secret Service]], and then betray to Qeynos or Freeport before completing [[Learning from the Crystal for Murrar]]/[[Learning from the Crystal]] you will be **UNABLE** to finish this quest as [[Murrar Shar (Tenebrous Tangle)|Murrar Shar]] or [[Tayil N'Velex (Tenebrous Tangle)|Tayil N'Velex]] will refuse to talk to you. Completing the first quest will flag you to talk to the NPC of the alignment that you cooperated with on the prior quest, and thus neither NPCs will update the latter quest for you.

- UPDATE & WARNING for Varsoon Server (2023-Jan): Petition will not work. The GM will tell you to betray back to finish this quest. You may be required to buy another Betrayal Token, or go through the whole betrayal process.
