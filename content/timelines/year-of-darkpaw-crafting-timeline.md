## Notes

- Event in EverQuest 2, part of Year of Darkpaw: An EverQuest® Celebration.
- Start date January 1st 2024 - planned to be a year long event in 2024
- [Year of Darkpaw starts NOW!](https://www.everquest2.com/news/eq2-year-of-darkpaw-january-2024)

## General Information

- The Year of the Darkpaw is from January 1st, 2024 to December 31st, 2024 and all required Achievements must be completed within this period.
- Progress can be tracked by inspection of each Character's **Journal**, **Achievements** tab, **Tradeskills** group, **Year of Darkpaw** Monthly entries.
- Each of the individual Achievement requirements for every Month can be completed in advance but each will only appear highlighted in Green Text when the Month entry is active. Completed individual achievements for future Months remain in Gray Text.
- Credit for a Month only comes live at the start of that month. For example, only 3 monthly Achievements will appear as credited in March 2024 even if all individual requirementsa for any/all future months have been completed.
- The following table has been constructed to assist a person to track progress e.g. tracking completion requirements and current progress status in a spreadsheet.

### Notes

- 5 is the minimum Tradeskill Level required to start the first quest in the Year of the Darkpaw Crafting Timeline. You have until the end of the year to upgrade your character to reach the minimum Tradeskill Level to complete all quests and associated Achievements within the Year of the Darkpaw.
- 125 is the minimum Tradeskill Level required to complete every quest in the Year of Darkpaw Crafting Timeline.
- The July requirement includes the [[Shadows of the Betrayed]] quest. There is a **fixed 7 day delay** between the completion of the previous quest in the series and the delivery of the mail message that contains the quest start for [[Shadows of the Betrayed]]. Completion of the [[Shadows of the Betrayed]] quest must occur within the Year of the Darkpaw to be credited.
