## Requirements

Tradeskill level 130 is required to start this timeline.

### Fuel

1. May require at least:
   - 300x [[Formless Candle]]
   - 21x [[Formless Coal]]
   - 130x [[Formless Kindling]]

### Rares

- 1x Blighted Ore
- 2x Flowfall Vines
- 1x Tiama Wood
- 1x Zobboz Gem

## Notes

**WARNING: almost all water in this expansion will damage you, it's possible to resist its "Lava Burn" for a while but crafters in W.H.E.R.E. form or low adventure level will most likely die very fast.  ALSO, Totem of the Otter, Racial Abilities, and Class Spells which give 'water breathing' do not work. Even with high resists, you may DROWN.**

### Ability to Fly

- Last quest in main crafting timeline [[Destination Unknown: Additional Tasks]] unlocks flight in all 3 overland zones.
  - As of 11/23/2025 the ability to fly in The Unknown (from the crafting line) is not unlocked until TS Level 135.  The quest line gets you to 133.
  - After you have reached TS level 135, you need to zone out of The Unknown, then back in again, to be able to fly in The Unknown.

### Travel

- Travel to the expansion using [[A violent rift]] in Antonica at {{waypoint -2077, 3, 108}}. Initial access is granted during either adventure quest [[Rage of Cthurath: Facing Avarice]] or tradeskill quest [[Destination Unknown: Stabilization]]
- Account flag Rage of Cthurath: Yon Gorroth Unlock grants access to Yon Gorroth to all eligible characters on your account.
- Travel between 3 overland zones using obelisks. Each obelisk has options to go to the other 2 zones after you unlock those zones while questing.
- Locations of travel obelisks:
  - In [[Yon Gorroth]] at {{waypoint -643, -1, 448}}
  - In [[Oogothl Sprawl]] at {{waypoint 134, 65, -514}}
  - In [[The Unknown]] at {{waypoint -847, -47, 1009}}
- To get back to [[Antonica]] from [[Yon Gorroth]] use [[A hijacked portal]] at {{waypoint 563, 94, -697}}
- [[Kith]] in [[Darou Village]] at {{waypoint 464, 81, -573}} teleports you to [[Yon Gorroth Obelisk]] and back (requires completing [[Destination Unknown: Kith and Kin]]
- In [[Oogothl Sprawl]] use [[A trained nethernaught ray]] at {{waypoint 193, 37, -201}} to get to South East corner of Coral Forest, close to [[Ver'Bohten Atoll]] portal (may require some progress in crafting timeline to use)

## Daily repeatable missions

[[Destination Unknown: Additional Tasks]] unlocks repeatable daily crafting missions. These missions reset every 18 hours. You can see the time to next reset by going to Quest Journal ( J ), then Mission Timers tab and filtering by Rage of Cthurath and look for **Rage of Cthurath [Tradeskill] Daily Mission** Reset Time. The reset time is calculated based on time of day and not when you completed your quest.

1. [[Elemental: Air Quotes]]
1. [[Elemental: Earth to Anyone]]
1. [[Elemental: Fire in the Hole]]
1. [[Elemental: Water Under the Bridge]]
