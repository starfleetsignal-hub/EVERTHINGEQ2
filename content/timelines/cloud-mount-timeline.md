This solo/heroic quest series was introduced with [[Update:55|LU55]], and carries the player through all the major Kingdom of Sky zones. It's quests run perfectly alongside the timelines for those zone. By the end of this timeline, you will have received a full class-specific armor set, and the [[Self Contained Cloud|cloud mount]].

To start this timeline, speak with [[Bilgeron Cogsworth IV]] in [[Tenebrous Tangle]], at {{waypoint 50, 22, 193}}.

## WARNING: Betrayal

The Cloud Mount Timeline rewards you with class-specific armor, as well as cyan adornments for the class you are playing with on each quest. Betraying AND changing your class (i.e. Conjuror to Necromancer) during the process, or afterwards may leave you stuck with un-equipable gears or adornments. For the Varsoon TLE server, it is possible to pull the adornment out via [[Adornment Reclamation]] and trade or sell/buy the adornment set with another player of your opposite class.
