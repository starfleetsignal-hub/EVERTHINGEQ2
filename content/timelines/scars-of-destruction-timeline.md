## How to achieve +20,000 Faction standing with Port Woe

- **Crafting way**
  - *7 crafting quests open up to reach that. Each quest for +1000 faction can be repeated only 3 times and **only if your faction is under 20,000.**  These quests will be enough to reach 20,000 faction.*
  - +1,000 [[Meet and Greet: Baskets and Plates]]
  - +1,000 [[Meet and Greet: Clamming Up]]
  - +1,000 [[Meet and Greet: Dockside Repairs]]
  - +1,000 [[Meet and Greet: Flavorful Food]]
  - +1,000 [[Meet and Greet: Hand in Glove]]
  - +1,000 [[Meet and Greet: Knitting? Netting?]]
  - +2,000 [[Meet and Greet: Lost and Found]]  (not repeatable)
- **Adventure way**
  - *To reach the required 20,000 faction with Port Woe you can also do many quests from the Adventure Timeline, for example:*
  - +3,000 [[Fighting the Biters Back]]
  - +3,000 [[Shelled Sustenance]]
  - +5,000 [[Case of the Pinched Pickling]]
  - +3,000 [[Walking on Broken Glass]]
  - +5,000 [[Leave the Thievin' to Professionals]]
  - +2,000 [[Getting the Goods Back]]
