### Daily Mission

- [[Primal Velium Shard Mission System]]
  - [[Danna Stoneaxe]], {{waypoint 1087, -456, 540}} in Thurgadin Harbor in [[Great Divide]], offers a daily mission for a [[Destiny of Velious]] instance. This mission rewards 2 [[Primal Velium Shard|Primal Velium Shards]] and at least 1p 5g. The mission will change once a day at midnight PST (01:00 PDT, 04:00 EDT, 08:00 GMT or 09:00 CET).
  - [[Brann Stoneaxe]], {{waypoint 1091, -457, 540}} in Thurgadin Harbor in [[Great Divide]], offers a more challenging mission every three days for [[Destiny of Velious]] instances. The mission rewards 8-14 [[Primal Velium Shard|Primal Velium Shards]] and at least 3p 25g.
