- *This is a master quest list that excludes any holiday/event related quests.*
- *All quests listed here, unless noted, can be started and completed as of September 2018.*

There is little in the way of questing in this terraced fortress. The Rujarkian Orcs treat all explorers with hostility and any would-be traders with suppression. As a result there is all but one quest giving NPC found within and the majority of quests come from uncommon drops. Keep your head low as you climb your way about this orc-infested canyon.
