The quests found in this timeline are those unique to [[Maj'Dul]]. However, there are many quests that involve Maj'Dul in other timelines. These other timelines must be experienced to better understand the lore behind Maj'Dul:

- [[Peacock Club Timeline]]
- [[Court of the Blades Timeline]]
- [[Court of the Coin Timeline]]
- [[Court of Truth Timeline]]
- [[Shimmering Citadel Timeline]]
