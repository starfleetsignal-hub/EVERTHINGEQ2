The Court of the Coin is a [[The Courts of Maj'Dul|Maj'Dul Court]] that seeks to lead and revive the Dervin Empire through commerce and trading. It is now lead by the young [[Neriph, Caliph of the Coin]], who is rumored to depend to be highly on his advisors following the untimely death of his father, Omren.

Completion of this timeline results in access to the instance [[The Court of the Coin: Imminent Danger]], and is necessary for the [[Signature Quest|signature quest]] [[The Courts of Maj'Dul: Of Fate and Destiny]].

## Gaining Entrance to the Court

You need to have kindly faction (20,000+) with the Court of the Coin to enter their [[The Court of the Coin|headquarters]] in [[Maj'Dul]]. To gain entry, you can earn faction with the Court in a few ways:

- In Maj'Dul, kill guards, bodyguards, patrollers, trainees or officers from an opposing Court.
  - Upon killing a rival court mob, loot their corpse to receive a token.
  - You can exchange these tokens with [[The Court of the Coin consul]] on the first floor of [[The Tower of the Moon]]. Each token is worth +250 faction for the court you turn it into, and -250 faction with the opposing courts.
  - Killing tower guards from the opposing factions and collecting their insignia tokens is the fastest method for raising your faction with a Court. It is not required for you to take control of the towers. Instead, you simply let the tower guards respawn and kill them again.
- Repeatable quests for [[Thara Bashirah]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -1460, -225, -430}}. These will no longer be available after +20,000 faction. With the exception of the one noted, they reward +2500 faction with the Court of the Coin and -2500 faction with the other two:
  - **[45] [[Coin: Beachcomber]]**
  - **[45] [[Coin: Manifest Error]]**
  - **[48] [[Coin: Search for the Merchant]]** (only becomes available at +10,000 faction, and disappears after +20,000; rewards +7500 faction with the Court of the Coin and -7500 with the other two)
  - **[50] [[Coin: Resource Gathering]]**
- Repeatable quest for [[Majah L'Asi]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -8, -94, -920}}. This quest only becomes available after +10,000 faction has been achieved, but is no longer available after 40,000 faction. It rewards +3000 faction with the Court of the Coin and -3000 faction with the other two:
  - **[52] [[Service of the Coin]]**
- Repeatable quests offered every 30 minutes by [[A Tears grifter]] around Maj'Dul. Upon completion of one of the randomized quests, you will be offered insignia tokens of your choice.

## Gaining Favor in the Court

Once you have achieved 20,000 faction you have access to [[The Court of the Coin]] in [[Maj'Dul]]. You may complete any of the following to continue earning faction:

- Continue killing rival Court members in Maj'Dul for their faction coins. ([[#Gaining Entrance to the Court|see above]])

- Repeatable quests obtained from inside [[The Court of the Coin]]. These reward +500 faction with the Court of the Coin and -500 faction with the other two:
  - **[50] [[Changing of the Guard - Coin]]** - examine a box labeled 'Court of the Coin insignia daggers' just inside the Court zone-in, at {{waypoint 4, 0, 2}}.
  - Speak with [[Fadi Miyaz]] at {{waypoint 18, 0, 29}}. These repeatable quests are not offered if you are at max faction (50,000):
    - **[52] [[Supply Replenishment]]**
    - **[52] [[Monkey Hunt]]**
    - **[52] [[Cleft Crystals]]**
    - **[52] [[Merchandise Inspection]]**
