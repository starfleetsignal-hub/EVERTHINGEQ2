The Firewalker once had a piece of armor that imbued her with the power and swiftness of flame. This artifact, more powerful than anything wielded by her allies and enemies alike, gave a mythical drive to her cause. The Fourth Warrior, as she was known to her companions, may have been killed, but her legend would be harder to destroy.

[[Juna Crankfizz]] in [[Butcherblock Mountains]] will guide you through this journey. {{waypoint 635.81, 26.26, 588.38}}

1. **[70]** [[An Old Memoir]] (Collection Quest)
   - You must have the book, [[Snuffing the Fireknight]] (awarded from the collection quest), in your inventory to continue this questline. If the book has already been placed in your house, no quests will not be offered. If you do not want to travel back to your home, speak with Juna to purchase another one.
1. Finding the cloak fragments:
   - **[70]** [[The Fourth Warrior - Silken Drape]]
   - **[70]** [[The Fourth Warrior - The Leather Cloak]]
   - **[70]** [[The Fourth Warrior - Woven Metal]]
1. Empowering the cloak fragments:
   - **[70]** [[The Fourth Warrior - Jhal'Dolek]]
   - **[70]** [[The Fourth Warrior - The Solusek Mining Co.]]
   - **[70]** [[The Fourth Warrior - The Ardent Cowl]]
     - **[70]** [[The Fourth Warrior, Task for the Ardent Cowl: Living Stone]]
     - **[70]** [[The Fourth Warrior, Task for the Ardent Cowl: Lyrech]]
     - **[70]** [[The Fourth Warrior, Task for the Ardent Cowl: Raem Stone]]
