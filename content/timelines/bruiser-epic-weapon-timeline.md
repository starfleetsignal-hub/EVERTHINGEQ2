## Requirements

- You must be a level 80 Bruiser.
- You will need the following languages: [[Oggish]], [[Halasian]], [[Orcish]].
- You will need 50 [[Ferrite cluster|Ferrite clusters]]. Needed for a step in [[The Broken Fist]] part of the group (Fabled) questline.
  - These can be purchased from the broker.
- You will need 5 [[Fiery Magma Infusion|Fiery Magma Infusions]]. Needed for a step in [[The Broken Fist]] part of the group (Fabled) questline.
  - These can be purchased from the broker without the need to craft.
  - These are made by provisioners through a recipe sold at the [[Danak Shipyard]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]] by [[Eviss Ryiss]] <Danak Quarter Master> at location: {{waypoint -102, -41, 1108}}. These recipes  were no trade, but are now 'HEIRLOOM' so the provisioner does not have to purchase it themselves but can be traded through shared bank from an alt as long as they have the faction with Danak to purchase.
- You will need an [[Ebon cluster]] for the recipe, [[The Legend of Fyst]]. Needed for a step in [[The Broken Fist]] part of the group (Fabled) questline.
  - These can be purchased from the broker for a few platinum.
  - To scribe the recipe, you must be at least a level 5 crafter.
- You will need to complete [[The Trial of Tynnonium]]. Needed for a step in [[The Broken Fist]] part of the group (Fabled) questline.
- While not strictly required, having access to the Sokokar posts is extremely helpful. The quest timeline to obtain this access starts with [[Cutting Your Teeth by Cutting Theirs]]
