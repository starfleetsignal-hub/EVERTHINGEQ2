## Quests From Instance Zones

To get these, you must be able to go to [[Skyshrine: The Forbidden City of Dracur]] via the portal in Skyshrine at {{waypoint -33, 0, -9}} (10,000 Claws of Veeshan faction required).  After you go through the portal, you can then go to each district to get six quests by clicking statues or fountains.

### Covenant District

- [[Font of Everlasting Solemnity]] {{waypoint -65, -1, -182}}

### Dracur Prime

## [[Zlexenar]]

[[Zlexenar]] **appears by obtaining the suffix** [[Title: "the Ebon Claw"]], **or the** [[Title: "the Crimson Fang"]]. **If the player has obtained both he will offer the following quest:**

- [[Dreadscale Invasion of Dracur Prime]] (XXX heroic) [copy button] [eq2 maps button]

This quest awards +1,000 faction with the Claws of Veeshan, the [[Title: "of the Sky"|Title: "Of the Sky"]], and a Statue of The Wurm Mother.  The quest requires that you kill 20 Dreadscale invaders in Dracur Prime. You also get the prefix [[Title: "Dreadscale"]] upon completion of this quest, from the achievement "Dracur or Bust".
