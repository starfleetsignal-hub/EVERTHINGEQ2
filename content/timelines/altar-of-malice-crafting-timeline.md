## Prerequisites

You need to be a lvl 95 crafter to start the quest.

You will get an in-game mail from Captain Ethan Darani. Read it to start the first quest.

## Needed Materials

This list is for the full questline.

- 3 [[Cadmium ore]]
- 15 [[Hylocereus fruit]]
- 6 [[Iodocus cluster]]
- 8 [[Larix lumber]]
- 11 [[Leonid root]]
- 17 [[Sulfur fruit]]
- 2 [[Uncut tourmaline]]
- 5 [[Effulgent Candle]]
- 1 [[Effulgent Coal]]
- 7 [[Effulgent Incense]]
- 6 [[Effulgent Kindling]]
- 5 [[Effulgent Sandpaper]]

Also very helpful, especially for crafters with a low adventure level, is a [[Return to Highhold]] teleportation stone, available for 2 = 6p from [[Koros Splinterlimb]] on the [[Isle of Mara]] or [[Shazra]] at the Far Seas tent in [[Sapswill's Rest]] on [[The Isle of Refuge]].

### Daily Tradeskill Missions

Acquired from [[Skrit]] at {{waypoint 1224, 17, 1263}} in [[Tranquil Sea]] after completing [[Return to Refuge]].<br>

- [[Fruitful Foraging]] - Saturday
- [[Sampling the Danger]] - Sunday
- [[Sour Tooth]] - Monday (craft five items on cooking pot)
- [[Grim Running]] - Tuesday (**get [[Rallic pack pattern]] for [[Rallic Pack]]**)
- [[Legendary Pepper]] - Wednesday
- [[Salve Supplies]] - Thursday (craft five items on chemistry table)
- [[Small Arms for Small Arms]] - Friday (craft five items on forge)

### Weekly Tradeskill Missions

Acquired from [[Quartermaster Roberta Robbins]] at {{waypoint 625, 351, 178}} in [[Phantom Sea]] after completing [[Return to Refuge]].<br>
The weekly quest changes every Wednesday night.

- [[Blessed Healing]]
- [[Feeling Defensive]]
- [[Weapon Crates Restock]] (craft 10 swords; bring 10 iodocus clusters, 10 cadmium ore, 20 larix lumber, and 50 effulgent coal)
- [[Weapons with Benefits]]
