## Overview

This quest series is long and involved, but offers a major sense of achievement to most tradeskill-enthusiasts. Completing the series also has some major rewards described in the last section of this timeline.

It involves building faction with [[Bathezid's Watch]] first, which accounts for most of the time invested in completing it. The whole process is made easier by completing strongly recommended quests too.

To distinguish the tasks and writs needed to gain faction and the strongly recommended complimentary quests, the quests that make up the actual Artisan Epic are referred to as core quests.

## Prerequisites

### General Requirements

1. Although your adventure level does not matter, it is strongly recommended you mentor down to 75 so the quest feathers will be visible to you.
1. **The adventure quest that starts you on the road to obtaining faction is [[Favor of the Di'Zok]]**
1. You must be a level 80 artisan to begin the core quests in the series, but can begin the (non-core), tradeskill faction writs earlier.
1. You must have at least -20K or better (positive) faction with [[Bathezid's Watch]] in [[Fens of Nathsar]] in order to start the core quest series.
  - Though the core quests cannot be done before reaching level 80, the tradeskill writs needed to gain faction can be done when you reach tradeskill level 72, so it is **not** necessary to wait until level 80 to begin increasing faction in Bathezid's!
  - The quest, [[New Lands, New Profits]], is the best way for both low and high adventure level characters to jump start their faction. This can be done at tradeskill level 70 even though the core quests won't "open up" until 80.
  - Low-level adventurers will *need* to complete New Lands, New Profits so they will not be killed on sight (KoS) by the residents of [[Bathezid's Watch]].

### Strongly Recommended

#### Access Quest

Obtain access to the [[Sokokar Timeline|sokokar posts]] prior to beginning this timeline.

Doing so helps low-level adventure characters avoid a frustrating series of deaths while traveling and (even if you have a [[Mounts|flying mount]]) using the sokokar to [[Travel in Norrath|travel]] is much faster than manual travel in the zone.

- See the [[Sokokar Timeline]] for an explanation of how to gain access as a low-level or high-level adventurer.

A general travel tip for low-adventure level characters and those without a flying mount: though you can use the [[Travel in Norrath|world travel Globe]] to head to the [[Fens of Nathsar]] to build faction and start the first core quest, it is *much* safer and faster to travel by going to [[Kylong Plains]] first, followed by a trip to the Fens by sokokar.

#### Tradeskill Community Help

Talk to your [[Guild|guild mates]] who have already completed the quest and/or reach out to those in the Crafting chat channel when you are nearing the end of the epic quest series. Asking in advance will speed up the time it takes to complete the last step in the series.

- The final quest in the series *requires* items that come from recipes obtained by those who have *already* completed this series for their trade. If other players aid you in this, you will need the rare materials required and be prepared to meet them in person, as the crafting commission system must be used to transfer the items directly to you.
- If you have several tradeskill characters on your account that have done this series, the required items are HEIRLOOM. This means you can craft the items on alts who've finished it and place them in a shared bank.

#### Preparing Supplies

The supply list needed for this quest series is enormous if you plan to tackle it from a tradekill-only approach. Supplies are addressed in greater detail in the Tradeskill Supplies section below, but those who have not done so yet may want to do the following to prepare:

- Go out to gather the rare materials needed for the [[The Proof of the Pudding]] quest, as they can be very costly on the [[Broker|broker]].
- Go out to gather common [[Harvesting Timeline|tier 8 materials]]. While the common materials may not be costly on the broker, the sheer number needed to achieve the faction requirements before you begin can add up if you try to rely on the broker.
  - There is aggro-free harvesting in [[Traitor's Pass]] in [[Jarsath Wastes]]. Take the sokokar to the Skleross Encampment Station, then harvest your way towards the Howling Stones station. The area nearest the Skleross Encampment is aggro-free, and has 2-3 hard ore nodes, 2-3 soft ore nodes, bushes, wood, and roots. As you get closer to Howling Stones, there are aggro mobs here and there to dodge, but the best harvesting for dens is near the rhinos at Howling Stones.
- Complete the [[A Gathering Obsession Timeline]]. Sending the [[Artisan's Pack Pony]] to gather materials reduces the prep time.
- Talk to the leader of your [[Guild|guild]] about using some supplies from the [[Guild Hall Amenities|harvest depot]] (if the guild has one), especially if your guild also has the gathering hirelings (NPCs).
- Ask your friends to lend a hand. Those who've completed this series understand the volume of materials it takes and they might be generous enough to help with supplies.

### Iksar-only

The residents of [[Bathezid's Watch]] hate the members of the [[Sathirian (Faction)|Sathirian Empire]].  They assume all Iksars are Sathirian and will not do business with them. This is not based on your faction status with them.  Completing the quest [[New Lands, New Profits]] will allow you to travel safely in their area, but will not allow you to get the crafting quests.

If your character is an Iksar, you need to use an illusion form. Though many can be tried, the easiest, sure-to-work option is to use a petrified eye, which is a reward from from completing *any* of the racial bone collections, like froglok, wood elf, troll and so on.

For example, you can complete the [[Ratonga bone fragment collection]] to get the petrified eye or (if they are available) you can buy an eye on the [[Broker|broker]]. After you have the [[Petrified Ratonga Eye]], bring and use extra collectible ratonga bones to keep the illusion up while interacting in Bathezid's residents. How many you need will vary, so it's advised to bring several.

Because there are so few sentient options, player-cast and other "fluff" illusions (various [[Nights of the Dead]] costumes) do not seem to work for this. No matter which illusion form you try: press C to open the Character window, click the Options tab; under Graphics and make sure the box is unchecked to hide Illusion Forms as this will allow the locals to see you as you really are, even with an illusion in place.

## Considerations Before You Begin

Faction in the zones tied to Kunark can be a complicated lot. Like so many other factions in Norrath, as you kill and anger some, you please others.

What makes this zone slightly more complicated for those with a trade, is that all of the factions have recipes you may want. Naturally, you have to please each enough to get them to interact and sell you those recipes. For this reason, a pure-crafting approach to faction may be easier to manage for some players than a battle-driven balancing act.

Because this content was released long ago, many of the adventure-themed faction items and recipes may or may not appeal to some tradeskills.

If you are a [[Carpenter]] it's highly likely you will want to maintain a minimum of positive faction with *all* factions in the Kunark zones, regardless of the time that has passed since the Rise of Kunark was released. This is because all carpenter-made items retain their appeal among [[Decorating Houses|decor enthusiasts]] indefinitely.

## Improving Bathezid's Watch Faction

The recommendations below this point all refer to activity within the [[Fens of Nathsar]] zone. Though you can go directly to the Fens using a [[Travel in Norrath|world-travel Globe]], it is faster to travel to [[Kylong Plains]], then fly to the Fens zone-in point. The direct, Globe travel to the Fens puts you much further away from the [[Bathezid's Watch]] hall.

- To check the progress of your faction gain (while in-game), press **C** to open the character window. Click the Factions tab. Click the drop down arrow and select Kunark. You will see it listed with the other factions of this region. If you haven't earned faction in the region yet, you will not have this option in the drop down until you complete [[New Lands, New Profits]] or other quests in the area.
- If your adventure level allows (68+), you can opt for a mix of both adventure recommendations and tradeskill writs described in the sections below.
- If your only aim is to buy the themed-recipes from this faction, they are [[Heirloom]], so you only need to complete this series for the actual quest rewards.

### Adventure-focused

Go to [[Kylong Plains]] and take the [[Sokokar]] to the [[Fens of Nathsar]].

The fastest way to gain faction through adventure is to do quests, rather than randomly killing the enemies of Bathezid's. If you can safely travel up the mountain to the Watch itself, there are several quest givers outside the fortress.

- For a full list of quests see the Bathezid's Watch Faction Quests to quickly gain faction. Many reward 5K faction.
- At 5K faction per adventure quest, completing four will allow you to start the core quests in the Artisan Epic timeline.

If you opted to skip [[New Lands, New Profits]] or prefer battle to gain faction slowly:

- Kill a few Yha-lei fishmen in the area around {{waypoint 336, -120, 781}}.
  - They improve your faction slowly with Bathezid, but you don't need to slay many to become non-KOS. Killing a single Yha-lei offers only +25 (not even 250) per kill, so those who can't take on several at onceor take them down very quickly, may find it easier to complete New Lands, New Profits.
- Kill Rilisian Advancers just inside the zone. This also improves faction with the Watch, but will *reduce* faction with the [[Riliss (Faction)|Rilisians]].

### Tradeskill-focused

Before you travel to work on the faction needed:

- Do the quest, [[New Lands, New Profits]] to get the basic faction so the NPCs will talk to you in Bathezid's Watch.
- Highly recommended to do the sokokar quest as well: [[Sokokar Timeline]].
- Stuff your bags with [[Tier|tier 8]] common materials and fuels for your subclass.
  - See the Tradeskill Supplies section below for **important** details.
- Gaining another 40k and 50k faction (putting you at +20,000 and +30,000 faction respectively) will give you access to special recipes, so those who wish to buy all of the recipes as well will want to bring enough supplies for a marathon crafting session.

Once you have all of the above in order:
Go to [[Kylong Plains]] and take the [[Sokokar]] to the [[Fens of Nathsar]].
Travel up the mountain and enter the Bathezid's Watch fortress (west of the zone-in entrance). Once inside the fortress, turn right and go all the way to the end of the hallway to the crafting room. You will find two NPCs who offer you two ways to improve your faction *without* combat.

#### **Unlimited, Repeatable Writs**

These writs are all given by [[Supply Sergeant Anuhadux]] at 1,175 203 1,430 and all grant **+750 faction** with Bathezid's per writ.

- You will be crafting items for your trade's *subclass*. This means you will use tradsekill stations for each subclass, materials for each, and fuels for each. See the Tradeskill Supplies section below for details.
- If you plan to do only these writs, it will take 27 of them to reach the requirement to begin the Artisan Epic quests.
- The quests are all similar but can be chosen based on the following minimum tradeskill levels:
- Apprentice - level 72 (minimum tradeskill)
- Journeyman - level 74 (minimum tradeskill)
- Senior - level 76 (minimum tradeskill)
- Master - level 78 (minimum tradeskill)
- Once you get the writ, click on the clipboard behind Supply Sergeant Anuhadux.
- Open your inventory and scribe the recipe book by double clicking on it. You must scribe *every* time you start a new writ.
- These are untimed writs, so no need to rush.
- Each can be repeated as often as you like all day/night, without pause if you choose to do so.

#### **Daily Missions**

See [[Assistant Volwon Zhi'dok]] at 1,185 204 1,427 for the daily quest. Each grants **+2,000 faction** per mission.

Technically, these are also unlimited, but can only be completed once per day.

- These are more like courier-style errands and the tasks that will vary by the day you get them.
  - For example, you may be tasked with fishing for [[King prawn|king prawn]], a common tradeskill material used by provisioners.
- The dailies were added in 2009 to ease the mind-numbingly slow faction grind in the Kunark zones and to ease up on the heavy use of [[Harvesting Timeline|gathered materials]] from this [[Tier|tier]].
- If you choose to do *only* these dailies to gain faction, it will take 19 days to reach the required faction amount. Most low-adventure level players do a mix of the dailies and writs.

Note: these will show up in your Journal (press J while in-game) under the heading of Missions.

#### **Tradeskill Supplies**

While many quests have a hard-number list of which materials you can bring, this is not really effective with the faction-gain lead-in for epic, because you can bring a mix of supplies based on your trade's subclass and you can mitigate the number of repeatable crafting writs you do by picking up the daily writs that require zero crafting supplies.

Why bring materials for your entire subclass? While it *might* sound easier for a woodworker to bring 1,000 redwood and similar supplies only, that's an awful lot of one supply. It is likely you would use up the supplies you normally keep on hand for *actual* crafted items. If you plan to compelete any and every writ given, you will be making items from all of the subclasses associated with your tradeskill. You don't need to bring materials for every single tradeskill though, only those for the subclass you belong to listed below.

The best general advice a veteran tradeskiller will give: bring as many stacks of gathered materials as you can for your subclass; all subclasses need a enormous supply of roots, bring 2x as many roots.

Should you run out of gathered materials while in Fens working on writs, you can delete and get a new quest for which you still have supplies. [[Free-To-Play|All Access members]] can access the [[Broker]] by clicking on the EQ2 button in the bottom left of the screen and choosing Broker from the menu.

**Often overlooked:** You CANNOT buy fuel from the fuel merchant there until you have -20,000 (Apprehensive) faction, so it's a must to bring T8 fuels of your subclass too.

- **Outfitter** (Armorer, Weaponsmith, Tailor)
  - [[Lichenclover root]], [[Bristled pelt]], [[Redwood lumber]], [[Ferrite cluster]], [[Rough kunzite]]
  - Fuel: [[Smoldering filament]] and [[Smoldering coal]]
- **Craftsman** (Carpenter, Woodworker, Provisioner)
  - [[Lichenclover root]], [[Raw succulent petal]], [[Raw cranberry]], [[Torsis tea leaf]], [[King prawn]], [[Cabilis cocoa bean]], [[Redwood lumber]], [[Deklium cluster]], [[Ferrite cluster]], [[Bristled pelt]]
  - Fuel: [[Aerated mineral water]], [[Dough]], [[Smoldering kindling]], [[Smoldering sandpaper]] and [[Smoldering coal]]
- **Scholar** (Alchemist, Jeweler, Sage)
  - [[Lichenclover root]], [[Mineral salt loam]], [[Rough kunzite]], [[Deklium cluster]], [[Redwood lumber]], [[Ferrite cluster]]
  - Fuel: [[Smoldering candle]], [[Smoldering coal]], [[Smoldering incense]]

**Supplies for the Core Quests**
The supplies above are those needed to get the minimum faction to start the core quests in the epic.

- In order to complete the the *final* quest you will also need a variety of rare materials, imbuing materials, and fuel.
- See the [[The Proof of the Pudding]] page for a materials list if you plan to gather those in advance of the final step.

## Summary of Rewards

In addition to any coin and XP (experience) you gain, the following rewards are also worth your time:

- After finishing [[The Proof of the Pudding]], you can go back to [[Bathezid's Watch]] to claim your cloak from [[Danelak Hosfoak]].
  - The cloak is themed for each class with an eye  catching style and offers bonuses specific to that trade.
- [[Earring of the Solstice]] - an earring with major tradeskill bonuses that also allows master crafters to see special red "shinies" for highly prized collections.
  - You'll receive a Master's Certification scroll to [[Housing|place in your home]] by in-game mail.
- A prefix title for you tradeskill. For example, Master Sage.
- Seven [[Harvesting Timeline|T8]] rare materials.
- The recipe used to make the item themed to your trade in the final step of this quest is one you keep forever.
  - Though the item can only be sold by using the commission system and though many tradeskill enthusiasts do not ask for direct compensation, it is customary to tip well for the time involved. This can be a nice way to pick up extra coin as a master crafter later on!
- It is not technically a reward, but undertaking this quest series makes it easier to finish off the remaining faction needed to buy the tradeskill recipes sold in by [[Tac Ci'Toruen]].
