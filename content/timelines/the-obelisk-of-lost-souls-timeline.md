- *This is a master quest list that excludes any holiday/event related quests.*
- *All quests listed here, unless noted, can be started and completed as of August 2018.*

Through this timeline you will infiltrate the [[The Obelisk of Lost Souls|Obelisk of Lost Souls]] and gain a basic understanding of the shadowed men. However the true intent of the shadowed men and the other creatures of the Void will remain a mystery.
