This was a world event introduced with [[LU48]] and which ran until [[LU49]]. Players helped construction crews build the guildhalls in [[Antonica]] and [[The Commonlands]] by harvesting the needed materials.

The quests were received from [[Director Jasmine]] in Antonica and [[Director Hezreal]] in the Commonlands.
