## Starting Zones

- Good-alignment: [[Qeynos Province District]]
- Evil-alignment: [[The City of Freeport]]

## Timeline

These set of quests were originally intended to enable the froglok race for your  account back in May 2005. Their completion is no longer necessary to play as a froglok, although they may still be undertaken to better understand froglok lore. There are different opening quests between Good-alignment and Evil-alignment, but they become homogenized as soon as the player reaches [[The Lost Village of Kugup]].

Once the appropriate above quests are completed, use the [[Froglok Pondstone]] to reach [[The Lost Village of Kugup]] instance and continue the timeline.

## The Hidden Village of Kugup

Once you've completed [[Uncovering the Froglok Secret]] you will have access to [[The Hidden Village of Kugup]]. Enter here for the final quest in the timeline.
