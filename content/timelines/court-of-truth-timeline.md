The Court of Truth is led by [[Ishara, Caliph of Truth]], are the caretakers of Dervish law. They seek to advance the Dervish Empire through a robust judicial system, learning and order. The Court of Truth has use for your talents, if you're willing to lend them.

Completion of this timeline results in access to the instance [[Court of Truth: Riddle of the Sphinx]], and is necessary for the [[Signature Quest|signature quest]] [[The Courts of Maj'Dul: Of Fate and Destiny]].

## Gaining Entrance to the Court

You need to have kindly faction (20,000+) with the Court of Truth to enter their [[The Court of the Coin|headquarters]] in [[Maj'Dul]]. To gain entry, you can earn faction with the Court in a few ways:

- In Maj'Dul, kill guards, bodyguards, patrollers, trainees or officers from an opposing Court.
  - Upon killing a rival court mob, loot their corpse to receive a token.
  - You can exchange these tokens with [[The Court of Truth consul]] on the first floor of [[The Tower of the Moon]]. Each token is worth +250 faction for the court you turn it into, and -250 faction with the opposing courts.
  - Killing tower guards from the opposing factions and collecting their insignia tokens is the fastest method for raising your faction with a Court. It is not required for you to take control of the towers. Instead, you simply let the tower guards respawn and kill them again.
- Repeatable quests for [[Taj As'ad]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -1460, -225, -430}}. These will no longer be available after +20,000 faction. With the exception of the one noted, they reward +2500 faction with the Court of Truth and -2500 faction with the other two:
  - **[45] [[Truth: Preceptors' Requests]]**
  - **[46] [[Samiel Investigations]]**
  - **[48] [[Seeking the Past]]** (only becomes available at +10,000 faction, and disappears after +20,000; rewards +7500 faction with the Court of Truth and -7500 with the other two)
  - **[50] [[Truth: In Service to the Court]]**
- Repeatable quest for [[Majah L'Asi]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -8, -94, -920}}. This quest only becomes available after +10,000 faction has been achieved, but is no longer available after 40,000 faction. It rewards +3000 faction with the Court of Truth and -3000 faction with the other two:
  - **[52] [[Service of Truth]]**
- Repeatable quests offered every 30 minutes by [[A Tears grifter]] around Maj'Dul. Upon completion of one of the randomized quests, you will be offered insignia tokens of your choice.

## Gaining Favor in the Court

Once you have achieved 20,000 faction you have access to [[The Court of Truth]] in [[Maj'Dul]]. You may complete any of the following to continue earning faction:

- Continue killing rival Court members in Maj'Dul for their faction coins. ([[#Gaining Entrance to the Court|see above]])

- Repeatable quests obtained from inside [[The Court of Truth]]. These reward +500 faction with the Court of Truth and -500 faction with the other two:
  - **[50] [[Changing of the Guard - Truth]]** - examine a box labeled 'Court of Truth insignia daggers' just inside the Court zone-in, at {{waypoint 0, 0, 3}}.
  - Speak with [[Muyassar Zuhur]]] at {{waypoint -5, 0, 3}}. These repeatable quests are not offered if you are at max faction (50,000):
    - **[52] [[Aqueduct Research]]**
    - **[52] [[Intelligence Gathering]]**
    - **[52] [[Map Verification]]**
    - **[52] [[Phylacter of Truth]]**
