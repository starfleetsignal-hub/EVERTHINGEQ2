The Court of the Blades is a [[The Courts of Maj'Dul|Maj'Dul Court]] which believes that they must lead the Dervin Empire through military force in order to expand it. Their primary responsibility for the city of [[Maj'Dul]] is the protection of it against the many hostile creatures found in the [[Desert of Ro]], specifically the Rujarkian Orcs. Through this timeline you aid them in their cause, as well as uncover the deep insanity of their ruler, [[Dukarem, Caliph of the Blades]].

Completion of this timeline results in access to the instance [[The Court of the Blades: Dukarem's Assault]], and is necessary for the [[Signature Quest|signature quest]] [[The Courts of Maj'Dul: Of Fate and Destiny]].

## Gaining Entrance to the Court

You need to have kindly faction (20,000+) with the Court of the Blades to enter their [[The Court of the Blades|headquarters]] in [[Maj'Dul]]. You can earn faction with the Court of the Blades in the following ways:

- In Maj'Dul, kill guards, bodyguards, patrollers, trainees or officers from an opposing Court.
  - Upon killing a rival court mob, loot their corpse to receive an insignia token.
  - You can exchange these tokens with [[The Court of the Blades consul]] on the first floor of [[The Tower of the Moon]]. Each token is worth +250 faction for the court you turn it into, and -250 faction with the opposing courts.
  - Killing tower guards from the opposing factions and collecting their insignia tokens is the fastest method for raising your faction with a Court. It is not required for you to take control of the towers. Instead, you simply let the tower guards respawn and kill them again.

- Repeatable quests for [[Mahir Lu'ay]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -1455, -226, -378}}. These will no longer be available after +20,000 faction. With the exception of the one noted, they reward +2500 faction with the Court of the Blades and -2500 faction with the other two:
  - **[45] [[Blades: Crocodile Bounty]]**
  - **[45] [[Blades: Rujarkian Raiders]]**
  - **[48] [[Blades: Captive of the Alliz Raef Ew]]** (only becomes available at +10,000 faction, and disappears after +20,000; rewards +7500 faction with the Court of the Blades and -7500 with the other two)
  - **[50] [[Blades: Nuisance Clearing]]**

- Repeatable quest for [[Majah L'Asi]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -8, -94, -920}}. This quest only becomes available after +10,000 faction has been achieved, but is no longer available after 40,000 faction. It rewards +3000 faction with the Court of the Blades and -3000 faction with the other two:
  - **[52] [[Service of the Blades]]**

- Repeatable quests offered every 30 minutes by [[A Tears grifter]] around Maj'Dul. Upon completion of one of the randomized quests, you will be offered insignia tokens of your choice.

## Clefts of Rujark Series

After you have completed [[Blades: Rujarkian Raiders]] one time, speak with [[Hadoth Al'Uzza]] in [[The Sinking Sands]] at {{waypoint -1457, -225, -370}} to start the following quest line. The Court of the Blades is the primary defense of [[Maj'Dul]] against the Rujarkian Orcs and as such you will not lose faction standing with the other courts for completing these quests.

1. **[50] [[Slaughtering the Steelslaves]]**
1. **[52] [[Further Assistance to the Court of the Blades]]**
1. **[52] [[The Ashes of Conquest]]**
1. **[53] [[The Champions of Rujark]]**
1. **[55] [[Bring the End]]**

## Gaining Favor in the Court

Once you have achieved 20,000 faction you have access to [[The Court of the Blades]] in [[Maj'Dul]]. You may complete any of the following to continue earning faction:

- Continue killing rival Court members in Maj'Dul for their faction coins. ([[#Gaining Entrance to the Court|see above]])

- Repeatable quests obtained from inside [[The Court of the Blades]]. These reward +500 faction with the Court of the Blades and -500 faction with the other two:
  - **[50] [[Changing of the Guard - Blades]]** - examine a box labeled 'Court of the Blades insignia daggers' just inside the Court zone-in, at {{waypoint 0, -1, 10}}.
  - Speak with [[Dirar Muti]] at {{waypoint -4, 0, 21}}. These repeatable quests are not offered if you are at max faction (50,000):
    - **[52] [[Training Arena]]**
      - **[52] [[The Blades' Training Arena]]** (This sub-quest does not award faction)
    - **[52] [[Cleft Scouting]]**
    - **[52] [[Sweeping the Tears of Anuk]]**
    - **[52] [[Harpy Elimination]]**
