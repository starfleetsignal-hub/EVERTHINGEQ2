## Tradeskill Missions

Available within [[The Anchorage (Public Tradeskill)|The Anchorage [Public Tradeskill]]] upon completion of the tradeskill signature quest line from [[Whilhelm Nam'terin]] in [[Cobalt Scar]], with the entrance at {{waypoint 3833.34, -1020.77, 1884.77}} in Cobalt Scar. These quests reset every hour. Once the researchers achieve Rank IV, they will no longer offer missions, but Whilhelm will still offer the quest "Anchorage: Researching Requisitions" which is a random selection of one of the quests below and awards 20 [[Anchorage Chit]] and an [[Anchorage Mission Reward Crate]]. Whilhelm's quest resets every 45 minutes.

There are two ways to quickly travel to the entrance to the zone. The first is to use any [[Globe of Swift Travel]] to fast travel to [[The Great Divide]], and then talk to the New Combine Flightmaster {{waypoint 1279.58, -540.25, 305.17}} (not a coldain griffin rider) to travel to [[Split Rock Pass]] in Cobalt Scar, which is very near the entrance. The second is to purchase [[A Mischievous Castle]] house item from the specialty merchant [[Barrius Nam'terin]] next to Whilhelm that provides instant transportation to the entrance to the zone after right clicking on the item on the floor of your house and choosing to touch the castle. While it is possible to use travel via spire or globe to Cobalt Scar directly, the zone in is at [[Scarstone]], which is quite far away.

Note: every recipe needs 5 [[Scarred Coal]] as fuel, which can be only found within [[The Anchorage (Public Tradeskill)|The Anchorage [Public Tradeskill]]].
