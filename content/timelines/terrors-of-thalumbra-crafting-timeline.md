- See the [[Tradeskill Timeline]] page for a complete list of *all* tradeskill quests from every expansion.

## Prerequisites

- You need to be a lvl 100 crafter to start the quest.
- You must have finished [[The Captain's Lament]] to receive the mail that starts the Quest
- You need to be able to fly

## Notes

Crafting during these quests **can kill you**, so pay attention to your reaction arts during the crafting process.
Signs a recipe is dangerous include:
*The NPC tells you that a crafting combine is dangerous or that you should be very careful
*Reaction names that start with words like "Lethal" and "Catastrophic" and "Critical Channelling Instability"
Merchants here will not sell you fuel until you reach at least -25,000 faction, nor can you use the banks or brokers until you reach adequate faction, so be sure to bring all supplies with you to save time. See the Needed Materials section for a list.

## Note About Recipe books

The merchant [[Thalnor]] in the [[Forge of Brell]] within [[Maldura]] sells recipe books. You will need various levels of faction with the [[Deepforge Founders (Faction)|Deepforge Founders]], as well as [[Coin of the Grand Artificer|Coins of the Grand Artificer]]. His items are available at 10K faction, 30K faction, and 40K faction.

- *Note: You can buy the class-based crafting books when you have finished [[Researching a Solution]] and [[For Your Eyes Only]]*
