## Timeline

The timeline is a part of the larger [[Tradeskill Timeline]].

This questline is intended to introduce new players to some new concepts, guide tradeskillers past areas where there are other tradeskill quests they may also want to pick up, and reward recipes for some useful-at-all-levels items.

For new players/characters this questline is intended to be spaced out through their whole career levelling up. One mail message will be received every 10 levels. Already-high-level tradeskillers will be able to do the quests all at once, with about a half hour delay between each mail, although zoning will instantly send the player a mail.

Completion of the quest [[On A Boat (Tradeskill)|On A Boat]] unlocks Raffik as a Fury mercenary who can be hired after about an hour.

### Post-"Shipwrecked!"

After the events of [[Pirates!]], the storyline itself ends, but the questline continues to open up additional [[Artisan's Amalgamation]] recipes for each sucessive expansion's resources.<br>
Speak to [[Rakira]] to continue the questline to earn additional Amalgamations for expansion resources after [[Kunark Ascending]].

The post-'Shipwrecked!' questline requires harvesting as part of the quests themselves - so you cannot 'stock up' ahead of time.

Each quest for a specific expansion's resources is generally added in the expansion after.  *i.e. the quest for 'Scars' resources was added during the subsequent Cthurath expansion.  'Cthurath' resources quest is expected some time after the "next expansion" is already launched.*
