## Overview

This timeline gives details about the [[Collecting|collection]] quests introduced as part of the Planes of Prophecy expansion.

To make tracking your progress on these easier, they have been split into sections below.

- Some quests of the [[Planes of Prophecy Timeline]] reward with a collection item that is then added to an expert collection of the meta collection.

## Meta-Collection

A meta collection is a collection comprised of more collection items rewarded only by completing *other* associated collections.

1. the "first" meta collections:
   1. [[Elements of Augury]] - *from the [[Planes of Prophecy Collection Timeline#Plane of Magic Collections|Plane of Magic Collections]] rewards with [[Planar Entropy Essence]] for [[Vestiges of Prophecy]] collection*
   1. Planes of Prophecy Solo Dungeon Collections
      - [[Planar Astrolabe (Collection)|Planar Astrolabe]] - *Rewards with [[Planar Astrolabe (Collection Item)|Planar Astrolabe]] for [[Vestiges of Prophecy]] collection*
      - [[Planar Comet Dust (Collection)|Planar Comet Dust]] - *Rewards with [[Planar Comet Dust (Item)|Planar Comet Dust]] for [[Vestiges of Prophecy]] collection*
      - [[Planar Disease Ward (Collection)|Planar Disease Ward]] - *Rewards with [[Planar Disease Ward (Item)|Planar Disease Ward]] for [[Vestiges of Prophecy]] collection*
      - [[Planar Storm Flux (Collection)|Planar Storm Flux]] - *Rewards with [[Planar Storm Flux (Item)|Planar Storm Flux]] for [[Vestiges of Prophecy]] collection*
   1. Planes of Prophecy Heroic Dungeon Collections
      - [[Astral Comet Dust (Collection)|Astral Comet Dust]] - *Rewards with [[Astral Comet Dust (Item)|Astral Comet Dust]] for [[Vestiges of Prophecy]] collection*
      - [[Astral Mapping Device (Collection)|Astral Mapping Device]] - *Rewards with [[Astral Mapping Device (Item)|Astral Mapping Device]] for [[Vestiges of Prophecy]] collection*
      - [[Astral Plague Wards]] - *Rewards with [[Astral Plague Ward]] for [[Vestiges of Prophecy]] collection*
      - [[Astral Storm Flux (Collection)|Astral Storm Flux]] - *Rewards with [[Astral Storm Flux (Item)|Astral Storm Flux]] for [[Vestiges of Prophecy]] collection*
1. [[Vestiges of Prophecy]] - the final meta collection
   - rewards with [[Eye of the Prophet]] -- *can be upgraded to [[Astral Eye of the Prophet]] at [[Amarat the Procurer]] for 5 [[Tears of Ro]] and 5,000p 500,000 status, after finishing the [[Vestiges of Prophecy]] collection and "obtaining" the "PoP: Planar Raid Key (Tier 3 Raid)"*
