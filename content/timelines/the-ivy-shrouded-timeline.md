Ages ago, [[The Void|void]] creatures crossed over onto our plane of existence and a crystal was placed in the [[The Lesser Faydark|forest]] that anchored them to [[Norrath]]. As fate would have it, this crystal would be abducted by bandits who shattered it into pieces, selling them off for profit. Little did they know that these void fragments were worth more than mere gold. These [[A void crystal fragment|void crystal fragments]] are so statured with evil, that a cunning mind can easily use them to manipulate and completely control weak-minded creatures.

Recently, the high priestess of [[Tunare]], [[Arloona Delyssa]], has been sensing the influence of these shards on [[The orb of Tunare]], a relic of great significance to the Koada'Dal. This orb was an elven gift to Tunare and symbolizes the bond they share between one another. Little by little, the void fragments are pulling on the power of the orb, draining it. Arloona needs your help to collect and destroy these fragments for the honor and safety of her people.

This Signature Quest series results in one of the following:

- [[Ivy-Shrouded Earring of Tunare]]
- [[Ivy-Shrouded Girdle of Tunare]]
- [[Ivy-Shrouded Club of Tunare]]
- [[Ivy-Shrouded Orb of Tunare]]
