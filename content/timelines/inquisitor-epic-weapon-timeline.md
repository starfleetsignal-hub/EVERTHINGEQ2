## Starting

Quest starter [[Defaced Leaflet]] drops off named Yha-lei inside [[Chelsith]] and off of Yha-lei along the coast in Jarsath Wastes. Will drop in any chest randomly. Examine quest starter to receive the quest [[Defender of the Faith]].

## Requirements

- You must be a level 80 [[Inquisitor]].
- Need to speak [[Goblish]] (for the 4th step of [[Defender of the Faith]]).
- Need to speak [[Sathirian]] (for the 1st step of [[The Saga of Yasva V'Alear]]).
- 5p
- -35,000 [[Outer Sebilis Residents (Faction)|Outer Sebilis Residents]] faction will also be needed to speak to the residents for step 1.1 - This is not necessary if they are grey con to you, meaning you are 88 or above
