- *This is a master quest list that excludes any holiday/event related quests.*
- *All quests listed here, unless noted, can be started and completed as of July 2018.*

[[Permafrost]] is the bastion of the Kromise Ice Giants. They are worshipers of [[Vox, the White Dragon]], and will protect her at all costs. There are no NPC Quests obtained inside the zone, and the main questline is very straight-forward. The majority of time questing here will involve farming the giants for their [[#Book Quests|book]] drops, which provide an excellent source of [[Norrath|Norrathian]] lore.

## Access Quest

*There is a former access quest for an instance within Permafrost, [[Drayek's Chamber]]. It's completion is no longer necessary to enter the instance, however it does off some back-story to the dungeon.*

- **[48] [[Drayek's Chamber (Quest)|Drayek's Chamber]]** - obtained from [[Krindal]] in [[Everfrost]] at {{waypoint 578, -38, -1631}}

## Main Series

*On the first level of the citadel, in the northwest corner, there is a circular room with eight ice statues lining the outer wall. Each statue will start a different quest in the series, which must be done in order (i.e. you can't pick them all up at once). Examine the statue to your immediate right as you enter the room to start the series: {{waypoint 134, 3, -364}}*

1. **[48] [[Prove Your Strength]]**
1. **[48] [[Defeat the Frostpaw Pack Hounds]]**
1. **[49] [[Incompetence Punished]]**
1. **[48] [[Rem's Task]]**
1. **[47] [[Another Challenge Issued]]**
1. **[46] [[Victor or Victim]]**
1. **[49] [[Challenge of the Savages]]**
1. **[50] [[The Final Challenge of the Kromise]]**
1. **[50] [[The Horn Sounds]]**
