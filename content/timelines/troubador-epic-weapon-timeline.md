## Requirements

- You must be a level 80 [[Troubador]].
- Ability to speak and understand the following languages:
  - [[Uruvanian]] from [[Words of Air]]
  - [[Death's Whisper]] from [[The Whispers of Death]]
  - [[Druzaic]] from [[Words of Pure Magic]]
- Complete [[The Poets Palace]] Access

In order to complete the upgrade to [[Ayonic Inspiration]]:

- You must own the [[Sentinel's Fate]] expansion.
- You must speak [[Draconic]].
- You must be at least level 85 [[Troubador]] to enter [[Vasty Deep: The Vestigial Cella]].
