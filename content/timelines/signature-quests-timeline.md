The following quests are similar in scope to [[Heritage Quests Timeline|Heritage Quests]], but the rewarded items are new to [[EverQuest II]] rather than being familiar items from [[EverQuest]].<br>
Signature Quests were first introduced in the [[Kingdom of Sky]] expansion, and commonly involve a series of quests ultimately rewarding a Fabled item. Many of the intermediate steps may also reward very fine items.

The *Epic Weapon Timelines* are not listed here, but can instead be found at the [[Epic Weapons]] page.

See also: an alphabetical list of all Signature Quests

## Non-Series Signature Quests

- Level 60 - [[The Courts of Maj'Dul: Of Fate and Destiny]] - *from [[Desert of Flames]]*
  - Reward: [[Ring of Fate]]
- Level 65 - [[Koada'dal Magi's Craft]] - *from [[Echoes of Faydwer]]*
  - Reward: [[Shield of the Magi]]*
- Level 70 - [[Blood of the Brood]] - *from [[Kingdom of Sky]]*
  - Reward: class-specific [[Blood of the Brood#Rewards|Hoo'Loh's Helm or Hat]]
- Level 70 - [[A Mark of Awakening]] - *from [[Kingdom of Sky]]*
  - Reward: [[A Mark of Awakening#Rewards|Mark of Awakened]] neck piece
- Level 90 Tradeskill - [[Shadows of the Betrayed]] - *from [[Sentinel's Fate]]*
  - Reward: Class-specific [[Shadows of the Betrayed#Rewards|tradeskill weapon]]
- Level 100 Tradeskill - [[What Lies Beneath]] - *from [[Terrors of Thalumbra]]*
  - Reward: class-specific [[What Lies Beneath#Rewards|Censer of Confinement]] both adventuring and tradeskill versions
