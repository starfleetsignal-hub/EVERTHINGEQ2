- [20-50] [[Sundered Splitpaw: Gladiator's Triumph]]
  - Solo only.
  - Can only be completed once.
  - This is a ring event, not a quest with a journal entry.
  - rewards includes a pet:  [[Summon Zygomyd Sporeling]].
  - Character must be, or lowered to, level 20-50 to get any rewards.
