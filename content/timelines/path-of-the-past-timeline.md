**Path of the Past** missions can be received from Chronomagi Taskers.  Each quest has the reward of +1,000 faction with [[The Chronomancers (Faction)|The Chronomancers]], and the ability to buy a replica of the mission's target mob from [[Timeless Trader|Timeless Traders]].  When you reach sufficient faction you may also buy armor.

These missions count towards the Misson Achievements under your [[Quests Achievements]] tab.

You can only do one mission a day, with the timer running from 11:01 PM to 10:59 PM PST (Pacific Standard Time).

This list is cyclic: when one day offers [[The Vaults of El'Arad]], the following day will offer [[Ancient's Table]]. This listing defaults to the order the zones are offered in, starting with "A".
