### Travel

- The [[Sodden Archipelago]] can be accessed by using a Globe of Swift Travel
- The [[Western Wastes]]  can be accessed by using a Globe of Swift Travel

## Prerequisites

- You must be level 130 crafter to start this questline
- **You must have finished [[Ballads of Zimara Crafting Timeline]] to start this questline - this is still confirmed to be the case as of 9/7/2026, [[Ameela Ziad]] will tell you that you need more knowledge from the Hooluks even at 130 TS if you haven't progressed far enough in the BoZ crafting timeline.**

## Materials Needed

**Rares**

- 1 x [[Manganese Ore]]
- 1 x [[Golden Sennegrass Root]]
- 1 x [[Mountain Blueberry]] (Craftsman)
- 1 x [[Ulthork Hide]] (Outfitter)
- 1 x [[Porphyry Stone]] (Scholar)

**Common Materials**

- 22 x [[Chalcedony]]
- 20 x [[Crowberry]]
- 12 x [[Fox Pelt]]
- 76 x [[Ilmenite Ore]]
- 12 x [[Ptarmigan Meat]]
- 8 x [[Red Lichen]]
- 10 x [[Sedge Root]]
- 16 x [[Speckled Grayling]]
- 27 x Larch wood (for the Meet and Greet Clamming up)

**Fuels**

- 1 x [[Aerated mineral water]]
- 20 x [[Zimaran Candle]]
- 105 x [[Zimaran Coal]]
- 20 x [[Zimaran Filament]]
- 40 x [[Zimaran Kindling]]

More common materials are needed for faction quests and profession specific side quests

## Other Quests

Only quests not required to advance the Signature Quest Line are to be listed here.

The quests in this section are not directly advancing main crafting timeline, however they are useful and needed to boost faction with Port Woe to the levels required to progress that timeline.

In addition to these quests the Port Woe faction can be boosted by several quests from [[Scars of Destruction Timeline]] for level 130 adventurers, but be aware of faction minimums and limitations of crafting quests.

**Meet and Greet** group of quests can be obtained **only if your Port Woe faction is between 0 and +20,000 (kindly)**

**Stocking the Wanderer** group of quests can be obtained **only if your faction is between +20,000 (kindly) and +50,000 (ally)**
