This Hallmark questline grants access to the [[Shattered Vale]], and is appropriate for players to solo from levels 13 to 16, or with a group as early as level 10.

[[Rask Helstot]] {{waypoint 786, -21, -420}} in [[The Elddar Grove (POI)|The Elddar Grove]], [[Qeynos Province District]] offers the access quest [[Vale of the Shattering]].  After accepting the quest, the player may choose whether they'd rather kill Gnolls or Bloodsabers for him.  Which choice they make influences the questline.

*If the player choose to kill gnolls*

- [[Vale of the Shattering]]
  1. [13] [[Vale of Shattering: Proof of Worth]]
  1. [13] [[Vale of the Shattering: Investigating the Trail]]
  1. [14] [[Vale of the Shattering: Dispersing the Unkempt]]

*If the player chooses to kill Bloodsabers*

- [[Vale of the Shattering]]
  1. [13] [[Vale of Shattering: Proof of Ability]]
  1. [13] [[Vale of the Shattering: Investigating the Trail]]
  1. [14] [[Vale of the Shattering: Visiting the Windstalker Grave]]
