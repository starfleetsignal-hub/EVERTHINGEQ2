> the Wailing Caves dates back to [[The Age of Turmoil (Lore)|The Age of Turmoil]] when an orc raiding unit used it as a secret bunker to spy upon [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]] and feed upon the merchant traffic of the nearby roadways. The Freeport Militia discovered this bunked and descended into its bowels to capture every orc raider. They then slaughtered them in a slow and merciless fashion. It is this event that leaves these tunnels haunted to this day.
>
> — [[Bootstrutter's Field Guide to the Commonlands]]
