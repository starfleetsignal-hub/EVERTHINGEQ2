Mysteries abound and rumors are swelling. Something has The Order of Marr and The Lucanic Knights scouring [[Norrath]] for a secret item. Leading speculation is that the sister sword to the [[The Qeynos Claymore]] has been discovered, for surely only a [[Soulfire|weapon]] of such power could cause [[Queen Antonia Bayle]] and [[Overlord Lucan D'Lere]] to muster their most fearsome knights into such widespread pursuit. However, adventures returning from [[Crushbone Keep]] have claimed that [[Emperor D'Vinn (Acadechism)|Emperor D'Vinn]] has been taking council from none other than [[Mayong Mistmoore]] from deep within his [[The Acadechism|chambers]]. Yet at the same time, mages of The Academy of Arcane Science in [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]] have been heard whispering about one of [[Zebuxoruk]]'s prophecies: "Cleft in twine at the dawn of time, the two shall stand once more in a united front against that which brings the end." One thing is for sure: all threads eventually tie together.

The Swords of Destiny Timeline was released update-by-update during the Echoes of Faydwer expansion. Each update revealed a new chapter. Although not categorizad as a Hallmark Quest in the quest journal, the quests in this timeline serve as the defining tale of the expansion, and will lead you throughout Norrath and [[Faydwer (Continent)|Faydwer]] in search of answers.
