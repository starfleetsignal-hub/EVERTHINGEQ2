These are introductory quests for newly created characters between levels 1-6. They take place on the starting islands: [[Queen's Colony]] for [[Qeynos]] and characters of Good-alignment, or [[Outpost of the Overlord]] for [[The City of Freeport|Freeport]] and characters of Evil-alignment. A character can only complete the quests on the island aligned with their ethical philosophy (i.e. A Good-aligned character cannot complete the quests in the Outpost of the Overlord as they cannot visit the island. But you can go there using guild hall strategist).  Before reaching Queen's Colony or Outpost of the Overlord, characters will find themselves aboard the Far Journey.  After completing Captain Varlos' quest, characters can move on to the island for their alignment by talking to Captain Varlos.

[[Isle of Refuge]] was the name of the original starting zone pre-[[LU19]].
