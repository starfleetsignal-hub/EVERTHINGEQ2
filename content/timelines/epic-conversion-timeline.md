## Requirements

- It is no longer necessary for you to complete your Epic 1.0 class quest.
- You must be level 85+ to complete the [[Epic Repercussions]] quest.
- You must be able to speak [[Draconic]].
  - *You must speak [[Krombral]] to finish the quest [[To Speak as a Dragon]].*
- These quests are not available to the [[Beastlord]] and [[Channeler]] classes.

## How to Start

1. Speak with [[Kinloch Skry'mir]] at {{waypoint -35, 24, 214}} in the [[Tenebrous Tangle]].
   - If you do not have your epic weapon, he will provide you with a [[Shattered Weapon]].
1. Go to [[Lavastorm]] and speak with [[Researcher Sel'Quar]] {{waypoint -150, -117, 634}} in the [[Shrine of Thunder]].

## Quests

1. [[Fiery Jewel of the Underfoot]] [85] - [[Tenebrous Tangle]]
1. [[Epic Repercussions]] [90] - [[The Sundered Frontier]]

## Rewards

- An enervated version of your Epic 1.0 weapon with no procs or effects.
- A house item trophy of your epic Weapon.
- One or more Grandmaster-quality spells that duplicate the effects of your Epic 1.0 weapon.
