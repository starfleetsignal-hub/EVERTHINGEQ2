Whether you are a young Sarnak or a seasoned adventurer seeking to understand this new land, [[Timorous Deep]] lies open beneath you. In this timeline you will begin to encounter the ancient mysteries of Kunark, highlighted by the Sarnak's outward struggle against their former masters, the Haoaerans, and their inner struggle to understand their place on [[Norrath]].
