This quest series gives lore background for the Rise of Kunark Raid progression and sends you through the various zones of Rise of Kunark. Most quests are epic, but a few are soloable or have soloable components. You must be level 80 to begin this raid progression quest.

Only the quest 4-1 Taking on Trakanon is an actual visible access quest. The others are not needed to enter the different raid zones. Instead the access to these zones are gained by a "secret" flag you get upon killing the mobs some of the quests do ask for. The quest Mending the Drudge gives access to the mender within Veeshan's Peak.
