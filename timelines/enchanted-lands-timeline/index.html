Welcome to the Enchanted Lands! Whether you're fresh from The Thundering Steppes or stumbling in from Nektulos Forest, there is plenty more adventure to be had here. This is land of the halflings, who had been the dominant sentient race in these parts until strange demonic invaders began to flood it in the aftermath of The Age of Cataclysms. The halflings need your help in defeating these demonic foes, as well as curbing the villainous intent of The Runnyeye Goblins and the trickery of the Darkflight Fairies.
