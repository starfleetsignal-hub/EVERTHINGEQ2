In this timeline you will aid pilgrims on their way to New Halas. The journey, starting on Pilgrims' Landing, is a perilous one filled with dangerous beasts and natural hazards. However, it is the Ry'Gorr Orc incursion that will prove to be the most challenging of roadblocks.

All the quests are completed in a very linear fashion, and as such you are unlikely to lose your place. However, there exists only one quest, Bearly Recognizable Item, that is easy to miss since it is obtained from a dropped-item. Also, make sure when you eventually visit New Halas at the end of the timeline to pick up these quests, which are strewn haphazardly around the city.
