The Qeynos Claymore has been abducted from the palace vaults! For players of Good-alignment, it is your sworn duty as an agent of Queen Antonia Bayle to retrieve it. For players of Evil-aligment, Overlord Lucan D'Lere demands that you retrieve it, and who are you to argue with the will of the Overlord?

This is the main story timeline of the Kingdom of Sky expansion. It will take you across every major zone, dungeon, and raid.
