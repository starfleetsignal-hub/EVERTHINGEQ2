Tunare (Deity)

Lore

Tunare, Mother of All, Goddess of Life and Growth, resides upon the Plane of Growth from where she watches over all of her creations. Her blessings surround all living things. She is protective of what she considers to be her finest achievements, the good elf: High Elves and Wood Elves, whom she created as part of an agreement with Brell Serilis and Prexus, as well as in response to the arrogance of Veeshan.
