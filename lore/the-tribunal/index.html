The Six Hammers

Lore

The Tribunal is composed of six judges, hooded, cloaked, and armed with perfectly balanced warhammers. This council of justice does not leave its court. Those who need to be judged are sent to them. The Seventh Hammer is sent to enforce their decisions. Together, the rule the Plane of Justice. It is rumored that the plane itself is inhabited only by beings imprisoned there by The Tribunal for crimes against the universe and by golems that The Tribunal constructed to serve as guards. (Source: Justinian Theo)

Followers of The Tribunal seek one thing above all else: justice. While some claim to express this desire by pursuing careers as guards or magistrates, true believers in The Tribunal do not recognize the rights of any court on Norrath and enforce the Tribunal’s sense of ultimate justice on the rest of the population. Retribution, vengeance, and punishment are sacred duties. Followers of The Tribunal are methodical, patient and just. They must be so, for it is their belief that if they punish an innocent, The Tribunal will pass judgement against them, and doom them eternity. (Source: fvproject.
