This is the starter Inn Room for all Freeport villages. They now have two rooms.

All of these inn rooms were moved to the The Jade Tiger's Den and have been renamed Jade Tiger's Den Basic Inn Room. Because all of the housing from the villages was moved to one door -89.32, -21.43, -84.14, the door now lists each inn room with the number 1-6.
