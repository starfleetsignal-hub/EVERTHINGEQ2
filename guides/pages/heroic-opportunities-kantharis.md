A launch-era reference to Heroic Opportunities (HOs), the group combo system where each archetype's abilities advance a shared chain for a bonus effect. It was written in December 2004 and the HO system has changed since, so treat specific combos as historical.

**What it covers**
- Each archetype has one ability that opens a starter chain: Fighting Chance (fighter), Divine Providence (priest), Arcane Augur (mage) and Lucky Break (scout).
- Archetype icon colors: fighters blue, priests yellow, mages red, scouts green. Abilities that can advance the current step blink on your hotbar.
- The two stages: the starter chain, which must be followed in order, and the combat wheel, which has about 10 seconds to finish. Scouts can reroll the wheel's effect with a coin-icon ability before anyone advances it.
- A full table of starter chains for solo, duo, trio and four-archetype groups, with every wheel and its effect (damage, heals, power, buffs, debuffs). HO buffs lasted 6 minutes and area effects reached 25 m.
- Group etiquette: agree who advances each step when two players share an archetype, and suggested rules for which archetype should open the chain in duos and trios.

The thread's replies add a few corrections and ask for it to be pinned. The original also offered PDF and Word copies.
