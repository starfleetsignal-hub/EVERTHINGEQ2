A community discussion on catching up the three secondary tradeskills (adorning, tinkering and transmuting), which players needed for Blood of Luclin. It started as Jaysuit's request for faster leveling than the daily quests, and the replies turned it into a set of practical tips.

**Tips from the thread**
- A level 110 tradeskill boost, from the marketplace or left in your /claim window from an expansion bundle, raised transmuting to 600 and adorning and tinkering to 550 (Khal of Dragons, confirmed by Jaysuit).
- Some players found older boosts from Planes of Prophecy or Chaos Descending leveled tinkering and adorning but skipped transmuting (Rosyposy, Drax99).
- A boost can cost you rare recipe books you've already scribed, so think twice if you collect faction or quest recipes (Marae).
- For tinkering, buy tinkering training kits from the dock vendor, trading old currencies at the nearby exchange NPC if needed. Then craft them on a basic workbench, ideally with a crafting speed potion (Gregore).
