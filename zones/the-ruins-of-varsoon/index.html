Regardless of race, faction or diety, every soul on Norrath has heard the name Varsoon. To some this merely denotes the dungeon, full of adventure and thrill to be had, but to others versed in history, the name denotes nothing but fear.

Valdoartus Varsoon grew up during the Combine Era and already by a young age was simultaneously breaking and creating whole fields of arcane study. He was the first mage in the history of the Archesian Order to solve all seven of the Ember Conundrums on his first attempt. Not content with this, he sought out to discover a previously unknown Conundrum. He soon developed a spell that could draw fire from pure stone, thereby discovering the Eighth Conundrum.
