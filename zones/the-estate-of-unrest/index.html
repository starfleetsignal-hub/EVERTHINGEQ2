Long ago a noble family, the Quellborns, left Erudin to make a new life in Faydwer. Nicadius Quellborn, the husband, was a priest of Quellious as well as a musician and composer. He was accompanied by his pregnant wife, Jessa Quellborn. Tragedy struck on the voyage, and Jessa, ill and near death, delivered a healthy baby girl but died soon after. Mixed with grief and joy, Nicadius named the girl Jessa, after his deceased wife. They reached Faydwer safely and established their home in the remote southern mountain range of Butcherblock Mountains known as Dagnor's Cauldron. The body of Lady Jessa

Jessa grew in peace and beauty, nurtured by her father, the senior servants Hemstall and Ryline, and above all, Quellious. In time, she reached maturity, then eventually met, and was courted by and wed to a dwarf of Kaladim, Kyll Ruksif. He loved her greatly, and she him, and soon she was with child. Much happiness was brought to the mansion as servants readied the new nursery in anticipation. In his happiness, Nicadius was composing a lullaby for his first grandchild, but he never got to finish it...
