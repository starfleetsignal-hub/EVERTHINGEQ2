Once the peaceful resting place of the early elves and men that helped build Qeynos at the time of Antonius Bayle I, Vermin's Snye fell into its current state sometime around the War of Plagues. Servants of Bertoxxulous, the Bloodsabers, began to infest all of the catacombs and the resplendent Vermin's Snye was corrupted along with the other levels of the catacombs. What was once a hallowed tomb honoring Qeynosian heroes of old has become a den of filth and decay.
