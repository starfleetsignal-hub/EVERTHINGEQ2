This is a higher level version of the original Temple of Cazic-Thule. It has the exact same design as the original, with the same types of monsters and heroic named, most of which range in level from 103 to 107.

As with the original, this is a public dungeon and is not instanced. The Fabled Sanctum of Fear instance is available, with the entrances at the same location in the the Inner Temple at -16.25, -10.00, -173.24 and 16.25, -10.00, -172.91.
