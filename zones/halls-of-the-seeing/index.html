Only the bravest of souls would dare to venture within here. Deep inside this hidden sanctum lurks the mysterious and malevolent entity known only as the Seeing. A hoard of treasure is guarded by mighty constructs, obedient servants that will let no one escape with their master's many secrets.

Go through the instance killing all named mobs. You will come across NPC versions of Pain and Suffering. These become aggro after speaking with Tolapmuj. Tolapmuj will spawn after inspecting the armor racks called, 'Weapons of the Fall', strewn about the entire zone, and finding the correct one - it will give a unique dialogue in your chatbox and upon subsequent inspects it will allow you to 'scrutinize' and the 'flip the switch'.
