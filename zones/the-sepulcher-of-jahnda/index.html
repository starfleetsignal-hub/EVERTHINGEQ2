When you zone in, there's nothing but some non-agro trash mobs. You can ignore the rats, but it's safer to kill the spiders so they don't get in your way later and you can get some AA for killing one.

Near the entrance, there are 6 graves. If you hover the mouse over them you get the name of the hero buried there, and you right click on them, you're given an option to either whisper a prayer, or defile the grave. Defiling a grave causes A desecrated remains to spawn, which must then be killed. Either way, you must interact with all of them. One of the six is Lord Tonmerk's.
