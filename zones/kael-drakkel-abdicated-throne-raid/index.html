The only boss in this dungeon is Frostking Jund'Erin, a Tier 4 raid boss. He is hiding in the treasure vault beneath the throne. To reach him, clear all of the trash mobs in the throne room (including the top of each staircase), which activates the four gold statues in the corners of the room. Click each statue to activate the throne, which descends into the floor, revealing the vault.
