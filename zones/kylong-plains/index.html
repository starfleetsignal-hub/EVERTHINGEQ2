An expanse of land with varying climates, from dense forest to snow covered mountains, the inhabitants of this region deal with many threats. Whether it be the Gra'al Shul Iskars near Kunzar's Edge, the mysteries of the Combine ruins, or the menacing drolvarg roaming around Karnor's Castle, there is always need for help from a brave adventurer.

Explore a thriving plain where the relentless encroachment of the trees have finally pushed their way up deep into The Dreadlands. Come to the aid of Residents of Teren's Grasp by helping them establish a secure dock at Dreg's Landing and fend of a pending invasion from the Order of Rime. Travel east through the ancient tunnel and across the massive chasm that was once only a river to what remains of the Elven outpost of Firiona Vie and here help the residents hold the fort at Kunzar's Edge against the Iksar and Mudfin.
