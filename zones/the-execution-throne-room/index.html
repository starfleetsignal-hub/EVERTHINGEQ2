Deep in the heart of Karnor's Castle lies the one of the most bloody and gruesome sites in all of Kunark. When the castle was still a fortress of the Sebilisian Empire, iksar generals would imprison enemies of the empire deep within the dungeons. Nobles and other important figures were given the special 'honor' of being invited into the deepest part of the dungeon, where they would be subjected to brutal torture, included being de-skinned while still alive, before finally be given the gift of death and tossed into a maw of whirling metal blades.
