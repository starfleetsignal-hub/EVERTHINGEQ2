From Gninja on the old EQ2 forum, Nov 2017:

Raid progression within Planes of Prophecy will work differently than expansions in the past. In order to progress in raiding there is a specific order that bosses must be defeated in to open up access to other bosses. These bosses are broken into 4 tiers with tier 1 being the easiest/least tricks, whereas tier 4s have more complicated mechanics. You will not be able to engage a tier of bosses until the previous tier has been defeated.
