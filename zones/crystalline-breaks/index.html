Crystalline Breaks shares the entrance door with Crystal Caverns: Collapse and can be accessed after completing Rongot Wyrmrippa's quest Crystal Caver. There previously was a condition that you couldn't enter the zone anymore after finishing Fragmented Removal, this restriction however was later lifted, so as long as you have completed or are on the quest Crystal Caver you can enter this zone.
