Named after the famed dwarven king, Dagnor Butcherblock, this rocky terrace is home to all manners of rugged creatures. Yet none are as infamous as the dwarves, who built their mighty kingdom of Kaladim from within the bowels of the nascent earth. When time and destiny eventually met, the dwarves spread onto the surface where they would come to dominate the surrounding area. They reigned supreme for centuries, serving ever-dutifully their god Brell Serilis. Unfortunately, Butcherblock would be heavily battered and tortured by the events of The Age of Cataclysms, which left the landscape scarre

Butcherblock Mountains is divided into valleys by steep impassable cliffs. Some of the cliff faces can be climbed, and a few of the mountaintops can be explored. Dwarven mines honeycomb the hillsides, and many of the valleys are interconnected by tunnels.
