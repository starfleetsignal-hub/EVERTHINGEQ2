This is the headquarters for the Court of the Coin, The Courts of Maj'Dul which controls the flow of money within the city. According to Kallon Ebbtide, "they seek to lead and revive the Dervish Empire through commerce and trading."

This zone can be entered after you reach kindly faction (20,000+) with the Court of the Coin. See Court of the Coin Timeline for the repeatable quests which can be used to raise your faction. You can also obtain insignia tokens which can be exchanged in The Tower of the Moon for faction with the Court.
