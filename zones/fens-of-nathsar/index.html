Comprised of humid swamps, tall forests, deep canyons, and countless ruins, the Fens of Nathsar is as vast as it is perilous. Monsters of all types roam the land, preventing safe passage to any traveler who strays off the heavily guarded Sathirian Highway. In the wake of the Age of Cataclysms, the inhabitants of this zone have banded into factions more out of a need for self-preservation than for complete dominion of the region. Like the other zones of Kunark, the Fens of Nathsar owes its name to one of the original Iksar tribes that predate the First Iksar Empire. Nothing original is left of this first community, short of legends and rumor, in the wake of the Empire's turbulent rise and the numerous wars that ensued. Most likely they were fully absorbed into the ranks of the empire, assimilating completely.
