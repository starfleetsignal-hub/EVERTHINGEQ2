This instance is the domain of Tseralith, the one responsible for the reanimation of the dead within Fallen Gate. Upon entry you are greeted with the ominous sight of hallways lined with the skulls of Teir'dal dragoons. Bats flutter about in the hallways and will respawn quickly, providing a nuisance as you make your way through the instance.

Find your way to the Keeper of the Chamber located at 58, -46, -92, who guards the door leading to Tseralith. He will offer Breaking the Seal to the Inner Sanctum, which is required to pass through the door. To complete the quest you must first locate the randomly chosen dragoon skull from one of the many that line the halls of the instance. Once you find the indicated skull, examine it and the correlated dragoon will spawn. Some examples of these monsters are: Dragoon B'Lavin, Dragoon Nezzka, and Dragoon N'Xry.
