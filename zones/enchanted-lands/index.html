The Enchanted Lands were once called the Misty Thicket, a fertile land surrounded by steep cliffs and rivers. It was an isolated haven that provided safety for halflings, fairies and many other creatures. But after The Rending tore apart the world, the area's natural defenses were lost and ever since it has been tormented by fiends the likes of which Norrath has never seen.

Back in the days of the Misty Thicket, the halflings lived a peaceful life in the region's beautiful glades and forests. They gladly shared the territory with the fey, who thrived in these lands more abundantly than anywhere else on Norrath, and had occasional skirmishes with The Goblins of Runnyeye, whom also are native to this land. Sadly, however, the Misty Thicket became separated from the rest of Old Antonica during The Rending and became a target for malevolent demonic beasts. The mist that once permeated the land protecting it from evil dissipated onto the newly formed surrounding ocean
