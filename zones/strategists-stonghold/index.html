Upon zoning in you will see Prime Marus Nhak and two tallonite hunter-seekers that are not yet able to be engaged. Approach them to trigger the script dialogue until they can eventually be targeted.

Continue south to reach the first intersection and follow the ramp east to continue the ascent. Be mindful that there are avazeks perched above as you progress that will drop down and attack the group.
