Greater Faydark is the homeland for the Feir'Dal, Koada'Dal and Fae, however, after the events of the War of Fay, only the latter remained in large numbers. During the war, countless elves lost their lives from the onslaught of the Thex Empire. Alongside their orc allies, Thex soldiers put Faydark to the torch and the landscape was heavily burnt. Fortunately, the forces of Tunare prevailed. Since the war, the fae have been working on growing Faydark to it's former majesty.

Greater Faydark is a zone steeped in nature. From towering trees, to mushroom forests, to rivers that crisscross the terrain, to wildlife that is often more friendly than aggressive, Faydark is the perfect location for players of Good-alignment to start their journey.
