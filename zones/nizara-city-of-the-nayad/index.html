Entrance
Drop down to pool at the bottom of the zone and go to the underwater hole at 20, -119, 34. Swim through the passage until you reach the other side and click the portal at 47.79, -89.40, -10.06. If the portal is not up then kill Zin'vra, the Portal Keeper who will be at the same location.

The device used by the Tanaanites to connect Norrath with Nizara was stolen by the Nizari and the Tanaanites were either killed or enslaved. You must enter here and reclaim it if you have any chance of reaching Chel'Drak.

Nizara is a very challenging zone designed for a full group at level 70-75. Most mobs cast a spell called Flameclaw that stuns and applies a very high dmg heat DOT which is now curable. Flameclaw will wipe a tank even with max fire resist in 2 seconds. Two healers and an enchanter type are highly recommended.
