Within lies Godking Anuk, protector of the Fountain of Life, which is where the once life-giving waters of the Fyr'Un originate. In life, the Godking used the magic water of the Fyr'Un to bring eternal life to all beings within Ahket Aken. However, at some point the water became corrupted, granting its imbibers an unnatural life. Now, alongside the rest of his defiled city, the Godking has fallen into undeath. He lives out his immortality staring out from within this temple at the heart of his lost city, watching as the sullied waters of the Fyr'Un carry their curse to stone and creature alike
