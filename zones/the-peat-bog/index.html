The Peat Bog

Zone

Release

5-10

Harvest tier
1

Dark and dank, this zone is small, yet broken into smaller sections by circular wooden walls that vaguely resemble the edges of rotten tree trunks. The former Qeynos suburbs of Starcrest Commune and Nettleville are connected by a path made of wooden boards running along the zone's northern edge; so that their citizens wouldn't have to get their feet wet when crossing the bog. A grate leading to the moat in Antonica is found near the middle of the top edge of the zone.

The interior of the zone is laid out in a clockwise spiral, with stronger monsters found deeper in.
