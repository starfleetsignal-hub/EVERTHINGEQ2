Frostfell Wonderland Village is the hub of Frostfell joy on Norrath! The Frostfell Elves, Gigglegibber Goblins maintain it throughout the year, but during Deepice they put up their Magic Closets in the major cities and invite everyone to come in an enjoy fun and games! At the heart of the village, stands Santa Glug, who hands out presents to players every 18 real-world hours. There are also buckets of snowballs spread around that you can pick up and lob at your friend! The snowballs remain useful year 'round, so you can pelt your friends with them in July if you choose!

Though the Frostfell annual world event first came to Norrath in 2005, the village was created in 2006, updated in 2008 and completely made-over in 2013. The layout of the zone is like Icegill Lagoon of Everfrost, however rather than mean Icegill goblins, there are snow-covered islands connected via rainbow bridges. Prior to 2008, the islands were surrounded by water that you could swim around in. For the 2008 festivities and onward, the water has been frozen.
