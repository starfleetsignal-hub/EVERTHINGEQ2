Long ago, the Protector's Realm was constructed as a tomb to honor the greatest champions that perished in the fight to unite the first iksar tribes into the Empire of Ik. It is believed that the original ruler of that empire, Emperor Venril Sathir, placed great enchantments upon the crypt in honor of the great champions that rest there. This would surely keep any crypt raider out. In truth, the mighty Venril did not enchant this realm in honor of the iksar heroes, but rather, to use the spirits of the champions to act as guardians of ancient secrets. The band of crypt raiders that adventurers encounter within this instance is mentioned in a couple other areas outside of the zone. The gnome bounty hunter, Colonel Leevan, from the quest For a Few Coins More, mentions that he is on the trail of a serial killer that was once a member of a band of crypt raiders.
